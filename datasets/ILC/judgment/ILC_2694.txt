It is relevant to note that after the 2015 Amendment to Section 34 the above position stands somewhat modified.
Pursuant to the insertion of Explanation 1 to Section 34(2) the scope of contravention of Indian public policy has been modified to the extent that it now means fraud or corruption in the making of the award violation of Section 75 or Section 81 of the Act contravention of the fundamental policy of Indian law and conflict with the most basic notions of justice or morality.
Additionally sub section has been inserted in Section 34 which provides that in case of domestic arbitrations violation of Indian public policy also includes patent illegality appearing on the face of the award.
The proviso to the same states that an award shall not be set aside merely on the ground of an erroneous application of the law or by reappreciation of evidence.
