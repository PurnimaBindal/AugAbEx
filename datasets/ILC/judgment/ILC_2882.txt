That this is a writ petition on behalf of the Petitioner for issuance of a writ of mandamus or writs in the nature thereof or any other appropriate writs orders directions for commanding the respondents for payment of following pre retirement legal monetary dues to the petitioner with interest: a) to make payment of arrears of monthly salary for the period of 3rd November 2005 to 16th November 2006 as well as the monthly salary of December 2012 and January 2013 b) to make payment of difference of arrears of salary from 142007 to 30.12.2008 as per the report of the 6th Pay Commission amounting to Rs. 11983 c) to make payment of arrears of difference of monthly salary to be calculated on the basis of pay scale of MACP to be commenced from 982009 under the new scheme of 2010 made applicable from d) to make payment of TA for the financial year 2010 11 and 2011 12 amounting to Rs. 15 400 only e) to make payment of interest 18 % pa. over illegally withheld amount of Rs. 1 83 450 which is arrears of salary from February 2005 to 2nd November 2005” 4.
