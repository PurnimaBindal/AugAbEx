The Deputy Range Forest Officer aggrieved by judgment of the High Court has come up in The facts of the case briefly are On 25072016 from the respondent Titty alias George Kurian a Turtle was seized by Rani Forest Flying Range Staff at Karumbanakulam.
The offence under Section 2 9 39A 49A and 51 of the Wild Life Protection) Act 1972 was registered.
A charge sheet was submitted by the Forest Officer.
After seizure of the Turtle the same was sent for identification to Veterinary Surgeon who by its letter dated 26072016 identified the Turtle on inspection as “Indian Flap Shell” the scientific name is “Lissemys Punctata”.
The Court on 27072016 directed the Turtle to be freed.
