The matter was heard by the Division Bench and by order dated 13032019 the same was disposed of.3.

Now the petitioner has named certain persons who according to the petitioner are errant brokers and alleges that SEBI has been selective in taking action and therefore seeks for appropriate direction to consider the representation.
A counter affidavit has been filed by SEBI wherein apart from pointing out the factual aspects has mentioned about the investigation that has been done at various levels and regarding the pendency of the appeal before the Appellate Board.
The petitioner has filed an elaborate re joinder with regard to the factual averments set out by the SEBI in the counter affidavit.
5.
In our considered view SEBI is a Regulatory Authority and they are the organization which is functioning under the provisions of the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act 1992.
The powers of the various Authorities under the Act have been clearly circumscribed.
This Court exercising jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution of India cannot issue any direction to interdict the investigation or enquiry process __________https: www.mhc.tn.gov.in judis W.P.(MD)No.96619which according to SEBI have already been commenced and certain orders have been passed.
Aggrieved individuals are before the Appellate Board.
6.
That apart though the petitioner has named five persons to be errant brokers and those five persons are said to be still involved in certain other entities those five persons are not before us as respondents.
7.
Be that as it may if those five persons are involved in any other organizations and those are separate legal entities the question would be as to whether a Writ of Mandamus can be issued to SEBI to take action against those individuals with whom those five persons are associated.
All these areas are well beyond the scope of Writ jurisdiction of this Court.
So the petitioner has to necessarily await the decision of the Appellate Board before whom the proceedings are pending and also await the investigation which has been commenced at very high levels.
