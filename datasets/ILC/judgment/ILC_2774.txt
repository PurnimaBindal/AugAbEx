This complaint is filed under Section 35 of the Consumer Protection Act 2019 with a prayer to direct the OPs to pay Rs16 63 000 to the Complainant with interest thereon at the rate of 18% per annum from the date of award till the date of realisation and costs.
The matter is set for Admission.
The Commission perused the records of the Complainant’s case thereby found satisfied to dispense with Notice on the OPs to avoid delay keeping in mind the object of the CP Act.
It is to be noted herein that Consumer Protection Act 2019 came into force w.e.f from 20072020 and in view of Section 47 of Consumer Protection Act 2019 the Pecuniary Jurisdiction of the State Commission stands enhanced from Rs one Crore to Rs.10 Crores.
It is therefore this complaint cannot be entertained as on the day before the State Commission when the amount in complaint is stated less than rupees One Crore.
However in respect of matter having value less than Rs 1 Crore the jurisdiction falls within the District Commission.
