THIS WRIT PETITION COMING ON FOR PRELIMINARY HEARING IN ‘B’ GROUP THIS DAY THE COURT MADE THE The petitioner in this writ petition has sought for a direction by issuance of a writ in the nature of 3 mandamus directing the respondents to permit the petitioner to report to duties and perform his work.
2.
Heard Sri Paul Frederick learned counsel for petitioner Sri BVMuralidhar learned counsel for first and second respondents Smt. Savitha TH learned High Court Government Pleader for third and fourth respondents Sri BSGautam learned counsel for fifth respondent and perused the material on record.
3.
Brief facts of the case leading to filing of the writ petition are that the petitioner was appointed on compassionate grounds on 05.07.2014 on the death of his father who died in harness in the cadre of Second Division Assistant and was posted to the Office of the Health Officer of Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (hereinafter referred to as ‘the BBMP’ for short).
The petitioner subsequently was transferred from the Health Department to the Revenue Department in the 4 month of May 2015 and subsequently to the fifth respondent BBMP Boys High School Austin Town Bengaluru on 06052015.
4.
It is the case of the respondents BBMP that though the petitioner reported to duties has remained intermittently unauthorised absent from 02122015 and later did not report to duties at all.
5.
Learned counsel for petitioner contend that the petitioner reported to duties in terms of the order dated 06052015 at the fifth respondent School but was not permitted to sign the Attendance Register or perform his duties on and from 02122015.
The grievance of the petitioner is that from 02122015 he was not allowed to perform the duties and allegation of the respondents BBMP is that he remained unauthorisedly absent.
5 6.
The fact remains that there is no order passed by the Respondents BBMP upon the said allegation and the petitioner continues to be in service.
In the light of the fact that the petitioner is still on probation I deem it appropriate to direct BBMP to forthwith take the petitioner back to duties as a probationer.
7.
The contention of the petitioner is that he has not been paid salary from December 2015 to which he is entitled to.
Since the BBMP has not issued a notice even to the petitioner on the allegation of unauthorized absence that is now put up in statement of objections the BBMP is at liberty to hold proceedings and arrive at a conclusion as to whether the petitioner was in fact not permitted to report to duties or he remained unauthorisedly absent by conducting an enquiry after affording reasonable opportunity to the petitioner.
6 8.
In the event respondents BBMP come to conclude after an enquiry that the petitioner who is still on probation had in fact remained unauthorisedly absent BBMP will be at liberty to pass appropriate orders in accordance with law and if the petitioner is able to prove that he was stopped from working from 02122015 he shall be paid arrears of salary and other benefits if any from 02122015 till the date of reinstatement in terms of this order.
9.
The aforesaid exercise shall be completed by the respondents BBMP within a period of three months from the date of receipt of a copy of the 10.
The petitioner shall report to duties without awaiting any further orders from the respondent before the Public Relation Officer at the head office of the BBMP on 02.12.2020 at 1030 am 11.
With the aforesaid observations the writ petition stands disposed.
