This Court cannot lose sight of the fact that many short service commission officers may have been selected to permanent commission on WP(C) 9633 2021 & connected the basis of the marks obtained by them in UAC appraisal system.
It cannot be that in the interim the UAC appraisal system is disregarded for petitioners and taken into account for promoting other candidates.
