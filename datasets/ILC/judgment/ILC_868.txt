This appeal takes exception to the judgment and order dated 28.02.2019 passed by the High Court of Judicature at Bombay at Aurangabad in First Appeal No 6919.
The limited issue involved in this appeal is about the liability to pay interest whether commences from the date of taking possession or only from the date of award.
The Court while issuing notice on 13012021 noted thus “Counsel for the petitioner(s) submits that the High Court has glossed over the crucial fact that in the present case urgency clause was invoked.
In that event in light of the exposition of this Court in RL Jainby LRs vs. DDA & Ors. reported in4 SCC 79 the interest ought to be payable from the date of taking Issue notice on the application for condonation of delay as also on the special leave petition returnable in four weeks.
