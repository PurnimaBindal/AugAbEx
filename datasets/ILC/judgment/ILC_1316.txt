7.I am not satisfied with the said submission.
As rightly pointed out by the learned Senior Counsel appearing for SEBI the order dated 04.06.2021 has been passed only on the merits of the matter.
Even though the official need not have referred to the Reserve Bank of India still a careful reading of the order indicates that they have not been dictated to by the Reserve Bank of India.
The order has been passed after an independent application of mind.
If the petitioner is aggrieved by the same it is open to the petitioner to avail the remedies open to them under law.
I consciously refrain from entering into the merits of the matter.
By passing the order dated 04.06.2021 I am satisfied that the direction issued by this Court has been complied with in letter and spirit.
