The policeman’s power to seize certain property- Any policeman may confiscate property that may or may be suspected of having been robbed or that could be found in circumstances that may give rise to any criminal suspicion.
Such policeman shall report the seizure immediately to that police officer if he is subordinate to the officer in charge of the police station, it was mentioned by Justice P Patnaik of the Orissa High Court in the matter of Maa Kuanri Transport and Ors.
