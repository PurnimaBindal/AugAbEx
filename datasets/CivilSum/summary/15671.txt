Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1969 - Dismissal - Whether an enquiry officer can use an enquiry report he has recorded to take action and dismiss a respondent from service- The punishing authority may not have bias in mind when he accepts the self-recorded enquiry report but there could be apprehension of bias - Quasi judicial authorities should also demonstrate they have done justice - Impugned order dismissing respondent deemed illegal and void.
[Paras 10, 15 and 16]
