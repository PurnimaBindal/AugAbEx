Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Samiti and Zila Parishad Act, 1961, Sections 18(1) and 19(1) - Resignation and Acceptance of resignation - The resignation submitted by the Chairman of the Samiti could only be effective after a resolution accepting the resignation was passed by the Samiti - Although there is no provision in the Act for the Chairman to withdraw the resignation, the petitioner continued to serve as the Chairman until the resolution was passed - The resolution accepting the previous withdrawal of the resignation cannot be upheld as it was not a valid agenda.
