Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4-A(3) - Default/delay in payment of compensation - Employers are liable to pay penalty and interest in case of delayed payment - Employers cannot avoid this liability just because they informed the Insurance Company about the accident on the next day - Insurance Companies cannot be held responsible for paying penalty and interest - The Commissioner's award of penalty and interest against the employer is upheld.
