The Constitution of India, Article 16(1) - Promotion - Rule of rotation - Roster point - One post of Secretary - The law allows the government to reserve a promotional post by rotating vacancies as per the roster point, even if there is only one post available.
As long as the rule of rotation and roster point are applied to the vacancies, and candidates from reserved categories are considered when eligible, this rule is not violating Article 16(1) of the Constitution.
[Para 9]

The Constitution of India, Article 16(1) - Promotion - Rule of Rotation - Roster point - Single post - In this case, the government adhered to the rule of rotation to a single post and applied the 40 point roster to the single post.
When vacancies arose, they were reserved for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, and candidates were considered when eligible.
The vacancy available at the time of point No. 4 of the Roster was reserved for the Scheduled Tribes.
The roster point No. 4 in the vacancy of the Secretary reserved for the Scheduled Tribes was valid and constitutional.
When the Officer was available and eligible for promotion, he was entitled to consideration in accordance with the rules and be promoted as Secretary.
The Tribunal was therefore wrong in directing that the rule of rotation to the single post could not be applied.
[Paras 10 and 11]
