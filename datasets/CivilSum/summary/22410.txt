Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 25-FFF - Compensation - Workmen whose services were terminated for their alleged refusal to join duties at a transferred location, and whose undertaking was closed, shall only be entitled to compensation - It is unclear whether the undertaking was closed or what the effect of termination was, based on the existing evidence - The Labor Court is directed to settle these questions in current Section 33-C(2) proceedings.
