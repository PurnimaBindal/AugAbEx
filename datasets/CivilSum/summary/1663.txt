Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(j) - Industry - Employment in relief works undertaken by the State is not regarded as employment in an 'industry' - According to Section 2(j) of the Industrial Disputes Act, scarcity relief work undertaken by the State does not fall under the definition of 'industry' - Providing livelihood to those impacted by natural calamities is the primary duty of the State and cannot be delegated to the private sector - Relief work is not a business or trade; rather, it is a casual activity carried out at various places depending on the nature of the disaster in that area.
