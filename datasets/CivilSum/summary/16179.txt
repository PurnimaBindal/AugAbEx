Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 300-A L.P.A.
Clause X - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 5.3.
- Retiral benefits - An appellant sought voluntary retirement from a corporation, which was treated as a resignation.
He challenged the validity of the order and also claimed payment of pension, gratuity and other retiral benefits.
Rule 5.3 provides that on absorption in the services of the corporation, a former government employee would be entitled to get pensionary benefits that had accrued to him for his service under the government, but payment would be postponed to the date of absorption or the date of voluntary retirement, whichever was later.
However, the rule only fixes the time for grant of retirement benefits to those already entitled, but does not by itself create any right to any retiral benefits.
The appellant who was a Manager in Industries Department, Punjab, accepted a fresh appointment in a similar capacity in the Corporation.
He cannot now turn around and say that he was not bound by his contract to service.
The appeal and writ petition were dismissed.
[Paras 1, 4, 5 & 6]
