Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Alternative remedy - Termination - Petitioner contests his termination on the grounds of Industrial Disputes Act violation and seeks reinstatement with back wages - Both parties require evidence for their pleas - Management can only refute pleas through evidence to the court - Petitioner must look to remedy under ID Act.
