Examination - Shortfall in attending the mandatory lectures - Withholding of result - Appropriateness of - Petitioner took BA Part II examination, also attended BA Part III Classes - Preventing him from improving his educational qualification due to a technical objection i.e. shortage of lectures would be very unfair and improper - College Authorities should be mindful before submitting his admission form to the University - University Authorities did not raise any objection and gave him a roll number - Decided, once the candidate is allowed to take the examination rightfully or wrongfully, he cannot be refused admission later for any deficiency that should have been taken into account beforehand - The Trial Court's order directing respondents to announce the petitioner's Part II Examination result is confirmed - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 39 Rule I and 2 combined with Section 151.
