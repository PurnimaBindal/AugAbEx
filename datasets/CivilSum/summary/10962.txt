The Pension Rules of 1974 of the Constitution of India, as well as Articles 14, 16, and 300-A, were used to determine whether or not a cut-off date was reasonable for pensioners.
The Executive Council of the university held a meeting on July 23, 1993, in which it allowed employees under the C.P.F.-cum-Gratuity Scheme to opt for G.P.F.-cum-Pension-Gratuity Scheme.
However, employees who retired prior to this date were not given the option.
A reasonable classification between retired and serving employees subscribing to CPF Scheme has been made, which does not infringe on the rights of respondents under Article 14 of the Constitution, as the Executive Council has the discretion to introduce a cut-off date.
