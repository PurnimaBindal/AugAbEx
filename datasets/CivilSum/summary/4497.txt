Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation - Ad hoc and temporary appointment - Project posts - Contractual appointments - Appointment of Senior Research Assistant in time-bound temporary projects undertaken by Indian Institute of Technology, New Delhi, a statutory body corporate constituted under the Institutes of Technology Act, 1961 - The institute advertised purely temporary/ad hoc posts likely to be continued till the completion of the project - The employees accepted the offer as per the terms and conditions in the offer for appointment purely on a temporary/ad hoc basis till the completion of the project - Appointments against project posts for a fixed period on a temporary basis will not give any right to claim regularisation on the post - The petitioners cannot be heard to say that they were appointed against regular posts and to be regularised against regular posts in any cadre posts in the Institute after accepting terms of appointment - Simply because they were given benefits as available to regular employees will not make them employees of the institute - Their services were no longer required after the completion of the project and the employees have to go with its closure.
