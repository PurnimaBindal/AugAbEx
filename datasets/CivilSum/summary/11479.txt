According to Section 71(7) of the Punjab Municipal Corporation Act of 1976, Safai Sewaks who were appointed on the condition that their appointment would be deemed cancelled if stayed by the State Government have had their appointments cancelled due to the State of Punjab staying the operation of the appointment order.
Although the recruitment was made prior to the instructions, the order of appointment itself outlined the consequences of a stay order granted by the government or court.
As a result, the appointment of the Petitioners is deemed to have been cancelled from the very beginning.
