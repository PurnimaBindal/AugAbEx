Employment Exchange (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959, Section 4 - Appointment - To fill up vacancy in class IV group - Despite the school publishing an advertisement for filling the vacancy and the petitioner applying in response, the school authorities did not take any further steps.
Instead, fresh names were called for from the Employment Exchange.
It was held that apart from names sponsored by the Employment Exchange, departments should call for the names of candidates in newspapers, display them on their notice boards, and announce them on the radio, television, and employment news bulletins.
The cases of all candidates who apply should be considered.
The order of the learned single judge directing the school authorities to consider the petitioner's case alongside other eligible candidates was not interfered with.
