According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India, an employee cannot be dismissed without a hearing, even if absent from duty.
In this case, a constable was dismissed for being absent for nearly 45 days, and an appeal/revision was rejected.
The punishing authority considered the constable's previous record and determined that the behavior was incorrigible.
The dismissal order was passed after the petitioner was given a full defense, making it justified and not warranting any interference.
