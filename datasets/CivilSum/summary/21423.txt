Constitution of India, Articles 39, 16 - Equal pay for Equal work - Direct Recruitment as District Munsif - Juniors promoted as District Munsif placed juniors but drawing higher pay - Direct recruits seeking the same pay as drawn by promotees - It was held that the higher pay drawn by juniors on account of their actual length of service in the cadre of District Munsif is justifiable and constitutes a legitimate reason that exempts its grant from the equality doctrine.
This means that equal pay for equal work does not entail that all members of a cadre should receive the same pay, regardless of their seniority, source of recruitment, academic qualification, and other service-related features - Seniors cannot invoke the equality doctrine if the grant of higher pay to a junior is justifiable.
The appeal was allowed.
