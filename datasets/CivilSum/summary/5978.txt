Articles 14 and 226 of the Indian Constitution and Sections 7 and 11 of the Public Premises (Eviction of Unauthorised Occupations) Act, 1971 - Void orders - Limitation - Res judicata - Jurisdiction - Nullity - An order passed by an authority without jurisdiction is a nullity - It cannot be relied on for any future proceedings and can be challenged at any time.
[Paras 9, 10, 18 and 20]
Sections 7 and 11 of the Public Premises (Eviction of Unauthorised Occupants) Act, 1971 - Penalty - Damage - Estate Officer's jurisdiction limited to ordering recovery of rent arrears and eviction - Penalty or damage orders can only be passed by a criminal court after trial - Estate Officer's penalty orders are null and void and cannot be enforced.
