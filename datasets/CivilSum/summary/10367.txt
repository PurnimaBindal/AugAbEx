According to Haryana Civil Medical Services (Class II) Rules, 1978, Rule 11, in-service HCMS doctors are eligible for admission to MD/MS courses.
A petitioner claimed to have topped in the HCMS reserved category, but was considered as a general candidate and sponsored under the open category because it was assumed he had not completed his probationary period.
However, the petitioner had worked for the maximum probationary period of 3 years and his performance was satisfactory.
As such, he should have been considered against the in-service HCMS doctors category and assigned a discipline based on merit in the entrance test.
The respondent has now directed that the petitioner's case be considered based on his merit in the entrance test for MD/MS courses at the earliest.
[Paras 11 & 12]
