Constitution of India, Article 226 - Domestic enquiry - Dismissal - Natural justice - The petitioner challenges the order of dismissal and cites lack of subsistence allowance during enquiry proceedings, denial of assistance from a legal practitioner, and failure to receive a list of witnesses and documents.
Additionally, a request to summon defense witnesses was rejected.
The petitioner argues that natural justice was not observed.
The respondents are ordered to reinstate the petitioner into service.
