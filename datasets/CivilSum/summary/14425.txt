Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 162 - Departmental enquiry - Dismissal - Petitioner was absent from duty from February 9, 1991 till November 21, 1991 - He argued his absence was due to illness - Since the petitioner was a constable, he was expected to maintain discipline and submit an application accompanied by a medical certificate for leave after getting sick - However, the fact that petitioner produced a medical certificate proves that his illness was not fabricated - The impugned order of dismissal was set aside citing a judgment of the Supreme Court, Malkiat Singh v. Punjab State etc. (JT 1996(2) SC 648) - The petitioner will not receive any back wages.
