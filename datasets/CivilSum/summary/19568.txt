Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Allotment of house - Eligibility - Pay scales - The Respondent was allotted a house despite having her pay scales revised - The application for house allotment was filed on 5101989 - It was held that her application could not be dismissed as invalid as it was filed on the date when she became eligible due to the revised pay scales which had a higher basic pay than the petitioner - The allotment order was deemed infirmity-free.
