Respondent advocates Soumen Bose, Sitaram Bhattacharyya, and AK Ghosal argue that under Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India and Section 9A of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, petitioners cannot claim parity of wage structure and other benefits without furnishing a comparison with the category of departmentalized workers.
The Court cannot consider the applicability of the principle of equal pay for equal work to the petitioners' case without knowing the existing difference in emoluments and other benefits available to them, as different types of jobs have been catalogued for different groups of workers, including the group the petitioners belong to.
