Army Rules, 1954, Rules 34, 36 and 54 - Summary court martial - Notice of 96 hours for arrangements of trial is necessary under Rule 36 - A declaration must be made by the delinquent with substantial compliance of Rule 34, or the proceedings of summary court martial must be quashed.
[Paras 18 and 26]
Army Rules, 1974, Rule 115(2) - Admission of Charge - Procedure for recording admission of guilt must be complied with - The Presiding Officer must not accept the guilty plea if there is evidence contradicting it - A circumstance contrary to the delinquent's plea of guilty was presented where the complainant had verbally abused him - Non-observance of Rule 115(2) nullifies the trial.
