The Self Financing Professional Colleges (Prohibition of Capitation Fees and Procedure for Admission and Fixation of Fees) Act, 2004 (Kerala), Sections 3 & 4 states that students admitted into self-financing colleges under the Government quota based on merit must pay a fee that is similar to those admitted under the management quota.
However, the act does not prohibit self-financing medical colleges from charging the fees set by the Justice KT Thomas Committee for students admitted under the 50% merit quota allocated to the Government.
It should be noted that the fee structure of Justice KT Thomas Committee is not applicable to the students who were admitted after 2003-04.
