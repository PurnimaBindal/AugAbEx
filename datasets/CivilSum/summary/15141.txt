In order to ensure natural justice, it is the employee's right to receive a copy of the enquiry report before facing termination.
Additionally, appellate authorities must provide an opportunity for the employee to be heard before passing a speaking order.
In this case, the employees were not given a chance to represent themselves before the order of punishment was passed, and the order of termination was not a speaking order.
As a result, the order of termination was cancelled, and the department may continue from the point of giving the employee a copy of the enquiry report.
However, the petitioner cannot claim reinstatement or any financial benefits due to the cancellation of the order.
