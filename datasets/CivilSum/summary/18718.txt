Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Adverse remarks - Quashing of - Petitioner argues that remarks were based on an anonymous complaint - After a regular departmental enquiry cleared the Petitioner's name, adverse remarks should not have been made - It is not appropriate to take anonymous complaints into consideration while writing an employee's ACR - Respondents should have confronted the Petitioner before recording such remarks - Adverse remarks quashed accordingly.
