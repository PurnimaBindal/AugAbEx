Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Regularisation - Pensionary benefits - PSEB policy instruction to regularize work charge staff who complete 10 years service - Petitioner expressed no interest in regularization - Continued as work charge until age 60 as regular employee retirement age is 58 - Claim held to be unsustainable as petitioner opted to continue past age 58 and relied on instructions issued after that age.
