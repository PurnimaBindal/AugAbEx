Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(b) - Railway Protection Force Rules, 1956, Rule 47 - Natural Justice - A departmental enquiry must be held before dismissing a delinquent from service, unless it is not reasonably practicable, and this exception to the normal rule must be based on sufficient material showing impossibility of joining the delinquent in enquiry proceedings or it being a futile exercise due to connivance with witnesses.
Simply because the witnesses are unwilling to testify is not enough justification to dispense with the enquiry.
Even if the witnesses may attempt to tamper with evidence, the delinquent must be given a reasonable opportunity to present their case.
[Paras 9, 10, 11]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 311(2)(b) - Railway Protection Force Rules, 1956, Rule 47 - Appeal - Natural Justice - An appellate authority must provide a speaking order that records sufficient reasons as to why the contentions of the appellant were not found acceptable.
Dismissing an appeal without proper consideration cannot be sustained.
[Paras 11, 12]
