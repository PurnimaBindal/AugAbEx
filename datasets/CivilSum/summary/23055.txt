The interpretation of a memorandum of settlement must take into account the context in which it was made, and a narrow reading would defeat its purpose.
Cut-off dates or limited benefits for certain employees may be included in the settlement, and courts may consider the source of the dispute to understand its terms.
[Para 15]
When a settlement refers to an employer's certified standing orders, the probationary period specified in those orders must be followed.
In this case, workmen would be entitled to confirmation after six months, in contrast to the three-month probation period provided in the standing orders themselves.
[Paras 18-20]
Once an establishment is required to contribute to the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, it must continue to do so even if it falls below the minimum number of workers specified by the Payment of Gratuity Act.
