Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary enquiry - Enquiry conducted by enquiry officer - Authority discovered flaws in the enquiry and overturned the results - Disciplinary authority chose to initiate a new enquiry by appointing a new Enquiry Officer and issuing a fresh charge-sheet for the same accusations - This is not allowed - The authority could have sent the matter back to the same enquiry officer to conduct a fresh enquiry from the point where the flaws were identified.
