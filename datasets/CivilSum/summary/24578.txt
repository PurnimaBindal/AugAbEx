Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 323 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 - U.P.
Public Services Tribunals Act, 1976 - Jurisdiction - The Uttar Pradesh Public Services Tribunal, governed by a separate State Act, cannot issue any interim orders, as it is specifically prohibited from doing so - The State may establish Tribunals under the Administrative Tribunals Act to settle service disputes for its employees - The court has ordered Uttar Pradesh to explore the possibilities of replacing the current Services Tribunal with a suitable tribunal under the Central Act, thereby reducing the burden of service litigation on the High Court and allowing for the Tribunal to function effectively with full powers.
