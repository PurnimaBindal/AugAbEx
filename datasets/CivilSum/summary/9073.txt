Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination - Enquiry - Simply being a temporary employee does not permit unceremonious termination via a simple termination order even if the alleged misconduct or misbehavior is aided by the management.
This is also applicable to probationers and ad hoc appointees.
Terminating without inquiry under Article 311(2) due to misconduct is illegal and camouflaging the order does not hide the true character from being challenged in court.
[Paras 11, 12, 13 and 14]
