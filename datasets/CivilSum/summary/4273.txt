[Para 7] C. Constitution of India, Article 266 - Relaxation of Rules - Court cannot exercise power to relax rules under Article 266 of the Constitution.
[Para 9] D. Constitution of India, Article 226 - Qualifications - Decrees and Diplomas - Equation of decrees and diplomas - Court cannot determine equivalence of qualifications of decree and diplomas as this is within the expertise of academicians.
