The Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16, and 226 stipulate that employees' claims for benefits outlined in State Government policies and instructions must be considered.
However, many claims have been unresolved despite prior directives from authorities resulting in wasted court time and employee harassment.
In response, the Haryana Government has been directed to publish their policies and establish a forum chaired by a high-ranking government officer to examine and decide employee claims based on these policies, issuing speaking orders within a set time frame.
Additionally, the State Government is advised to revise their policies based on previous binding decisions regarding regularisation, pay scales, revision, grant of increments, compassionate appointment, and associated issues.
