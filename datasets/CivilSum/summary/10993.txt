Andhra Pradesh Charitable and Religious Institutions and Endowments Act, Sections 39 and 8 - Transfer of employees of Religious Institutions - The Commissioner of Endowments may have the power to direct the transfer of employees from one institution to another under Section 8 of the Act, which allows the Commissioner to make necessary orders for proper administration.
The absence of transfer rules does not jeopardize the exercise of power or leave a vacuum in staff control for ensuring proper administration.
