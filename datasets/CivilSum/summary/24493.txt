Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) (Class I) Rules, 1976, Rules 21 and 18 - Seniority inter se the direct recruits and promotees - Seniority in Civil Services must follow the order of appointment as per the mandate of Rule 21, not the actual date of appointment - Appointments should adhere to the rota of posts reserved as prescribed in Rule 18 - The "order of appointment" means rotation/order/sequence of slots enumerated for various registers qua different sources - Rules 18 and 21 should be interpreted together - The order of appointment is deemed to be the order prescribed in Rule 18 - Seniority cannot be determined with reference to actual date of appointment, but must reflect the rota and quota in the seniority list.
[Paras 21, 46 and 47]
