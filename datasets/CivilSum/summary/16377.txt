As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, equal pay for equal work is mandated, including for the post of Shashtri teacher.
The petitioner, who has been performing the same duties and carrying the same responsibility as similarly situated persons for the past 30 years, cannot be deemed unqualified for the post.
Therefore, he must receive equal pay as his counterparts for the same work.
[Paras 6 and 7]
