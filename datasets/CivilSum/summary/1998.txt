The Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Sections 2(a), 2(c) & 12 - Definitions of Civil and Criminal Contempt - "Preferably" - Meaning of the term "preferable" is more desirable - The High Court was directed to dispose of the petitioner's representation relating to the grant of Lecturer's grade preferably within three months - Steps were taken to decide the representation after receiving the directions - The Court only desired to dispose of the representation within three months - However, the representation was not decided within three months - Non-compliance with the Court's orders was not wilful or deliberate - Respondent's reply before the Court explaining the circumstances of having received the Court's order did not amount to filing a false affidavit - Such action does not amount to civil or criminal contempt.
