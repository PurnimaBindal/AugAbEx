Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(k), 10 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 9 - Bar of jurisdiction of Civil Court over Industrial Disputes regarding promotion and transfer - Individual grievance of petitioner workman challenging promotion and transfer order not considered as "Industrial Disputes" unless espoused by Union - Union not supporting petitioner workman's cause - Therefore, suit filed by petitioner before Civil Court is maintainable.
