The Contempt of Courts Act, Sections 10, 11 & 12 were followed in an order by the Supreme Court regarding the determination of seniority, though it was done with delay.
An IAS Officer was condemned and later tendered an unconditional apology, citing administrative and procedural delays as the cause.
The apology was accepted and the contempt notice was discharged.
