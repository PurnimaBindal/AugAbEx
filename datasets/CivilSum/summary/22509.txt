Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Government Services (Temporary) Service Rules 1949, Rule 5 - Temporary employee - In accordance with the rules or terms of appointment, the employer has the right to terminate the services of a temporary employee, and can exercise any options based on criminal cases or acquittals until the temporary service becomes permanent.
The employee has no right to the post.

Civil Procedure Code, Order 27, Rule 1 - Proper parties - An appeal has been filed by the Secretary of the Department of Government, who is competent to do so.
Although the title was wrongly described in the name of the Secretary instead of the Government through its Secretary, the appeal is still competent if it is otherwise so.
The nomenclature in the title is not conclusive.
[Para 5]
Limitation Act, 1963, Section 5 - Sufficient Cause - If there is a delay in filing an appeal, and if the reason is because of negligence on the part of counsel in informing the Government in time, then the explanation is considered proper.
As such, in this case, the delay of 217 days is condoned.
