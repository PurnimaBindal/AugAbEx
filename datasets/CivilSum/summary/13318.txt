Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Regularisation - Government instructions dictate that ad hoc employees who complete two years are eligible for regularisation.
The petitioner was denied regularisation due to a three-month break in service, despite having worked from 20/12/79 to 12/12/82.
However, the court held that the petitioner was still entitled to regularisation as she had completed two years of service.
