State Bank of India Staff Association Rules, Rules 5, 6, 9 and 14 - Members of the Union - An elected General Secretary of the SBI Staff Association is required to be a member of the Union - However, a retired employee of the Bank was elected in a meeting called without prior approval of the Registrar - This individual was never elected as an honorary member of the Union and became a non-member upon retirement - Therefore, he was not entitled to represent the Union and negotiate with the management.

Trade Unions Act, 1926, Section 6(e) and Section 22 - State Bank of India Staff Association Rules, Rules 5, 6, 9 and 14 - Trade Union - Members of Union - Although the provisions of Section 6 and Section 22 of the Act allow for ordinary or temporary members to hold office, it is not specified that they have the right to negotiate with management on behalf of the union - The management is also not obligated to negotiate with an office-bearer who is no longer an employee - Provisions of Trade Unions Act, 1926 must be harmonised with the relevant provisions of Industrial Disputes Act - Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 3 and 36.

State Bank of India Staff Association Rules, Rules 38 (a) and 42 - Triennial meeting - An election of an individual as General Secretary cannot be considered valid if the meeting is held after the prescribed period of 9 months without prior approval of the Registrar - Therefore, the election of 'A' as General Secretary (who happened to be a retired employee) was not valid.
