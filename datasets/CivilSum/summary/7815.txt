According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, ad hoc employees are entitled to medical leave, maternity leave, encashment leave, medical/half pay leave as given to regular employees.
The Court has passed an order that is equally applicable to other employees.
Thus, it should be implemented for all without forcing them to approach the Court again and again.
This decision was made in "Rattan Lal and others v. State of Haryana and others", 1985(3) SLR 548.
