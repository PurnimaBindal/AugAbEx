Response: Regarding examination and education, Suresh Mange, DAG Har believes that the authority holding the exam and the paper setter have the discretion to provide or not provide any choice in the questions.
All students must answer the same set of questions, and the absence of choice questions does not constitute an unfair opportunity for students.
In terms of reappearing for an exam, it is up to the authorities to decide how many chances to provide.
Granting a student an extra chance does not mean that others are entitled to the same treatment.
Lastly, whether or not exam questions are within the prescribed syllabus is determined by subject experts, not the court.
In situations where students raise questions about the validity of questions in an exam, the authority must refer to subject experts rather than the paper setter who may have already made a mistake.
