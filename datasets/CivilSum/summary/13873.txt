Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Section 11(1) - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, Rule 29D - Disciplinary proceedings - Order of dismissal - Charge of indiscriminate and excessive firing - In the face of attack by militants, the disciplinary authority and IGP put undue importance on the number of rounds fired, leading to an unconscionable and almost perverse order, despite the enquiry report exonerating all the petitioners - The punishment is grossly disproportionate and set aside.
