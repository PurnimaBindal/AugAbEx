Constitution of India, Article 226 - Panjab University Calendar (Vol.
I) 1989, Regulation 119 - Extra ordinary leave without pay - A university lecturer in History, who was selected for PCS (Executive Branch), was granted extra-ordinary leave without pay by the university authorities initially for a period of 26 months expiring on June 22, 1988.
As no orders confirming the petitioner in the PCS (EB) passed, his leave was extended up to April 22, 1990.
However, when the petitioner was informed that the University had not acceded to his request for further extension of leave, he was advised either to join his duties or resign, failing which action would be taken under Regulation 119.
In the meantime, during the pendency of writ petition, the petitioner was confirmed in the PCS (EB) w.e.f.
April 23, 1988.
As a result, he was deemed to have severed his connections with the University w.e.f.
that date and is entitled to all benefits for having worked in the University up to April 23, 1988 as the orders passed by the university have become infructuous.
[Paras 4 and 5]
