Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - National Cadet Corps Act, 1948, Section 9 - National Cadet Corps Rules, 1948, Rule 39(1)(b) - Disciplinary action in terms of withdrawal of petitioner from attending course - Natural Justice - A show cause notice must be provided before passing impugned order, even when the case is to be disposed of summarily - The impugned order without giving a show cause notice was not in consonance with Rule 39(1)(b) of NCC Rules as well as principles of Natural Justice - Court held that the respondents are not barred from taking the same action after observing Natural Justice.
