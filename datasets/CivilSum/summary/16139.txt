Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Resignation - Executive Instructions governing withdrawal of resignation - A petitioner who had lost elections to State Legislative Assembly and sought permission to withdraw his resignation had served for 17 years and was only 38 years old, surpassing the age limit for entering Govt. service.
The petitioner's request for withdrawal of resignation and condonation of break was endorsed by Deputy Director Treasuries but denied by Director Treasuries and Accounts without assigned reason, which was found to be unfair and unjust.
In filing a reply to the writ petition, no specific answer was given to the petitioner's claim that persons in similar situations had been given requisite relief.
As a result, the impugned order was set aside, and the petition was allowed.
