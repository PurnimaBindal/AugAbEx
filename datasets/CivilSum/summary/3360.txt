Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Regulations 54, 56 & 58 - Disciplinary proceedings - The show cause notice for the major penalty must be issued by the competent authority, which is only the Zonal Manager, whereas the minor penalty can be imposed by the Regional Senior Manager who has issued the show cause notice.
As the Regional Senior Manager has not mentioned any penalty in the notice, the petitioner cannot file a writ petition with the High Court at this point.
The authority to impose a minor penalty is within the domain of the Regional Senior Manager.
If the authorities impose a minor penalty on the petitioner after taking his reply into consideration, he cannot challenge the show cause notice.
Therefore, the petition is dismissed.
