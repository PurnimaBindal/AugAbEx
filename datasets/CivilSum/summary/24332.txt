Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Quantum of punishment - Judicial interference scope - Interference is limited unless punishment shockingly disproportionate - Court needs to record sufficient reasons for shock, and cannot interfere by recording "ands" and "buts" without clear findings - Misappropriating public money is a misconduct for bank officers who withdraw unauthorizedly even if returned later with high-interest rates - Leniency is not compelled.
[Paras 11 to 18]  B Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Speaking order - Natural justice - Setting aside punishment order requires speaking order - Insufficient reasons make order incongruous - Affected person needs to know the reasons prevailed against him for an adverse order - Silent reasons are not valid.
