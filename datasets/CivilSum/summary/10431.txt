Rajasthan Class IV Services (Recruitment and Other Service Conditions) Rules, 1963 - Regular Pay scale - Regularisation of services of 82 part-time class IV employees - Respondents directed to provide regular pay scale with dearness allowance - However, the State Government did not consider other part-time employees working in the same department who were in similar circumstances and appointed on later dates - The petitioner being one of them was not given similar treatment - This lack of equal treatment constitutes discrimination against the petitioner and other part-time employees, violating Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - The State Government is under a constitutional obligation to provide similar treatment to other employees in a similar position.
