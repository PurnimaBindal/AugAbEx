Letters Patent Appeal, Clause X - CRPF Rules, 1955, Rule 90 - Absence from duty - Dismissal - The respondent failed to inform his superiors about his absence from duty, which effectively prevented the appellants from taking a second medical opinion, despite submitting a leave application on medical grounds - As a member of the armed forces, the respondent was expected to adhere to a higher degree of discipline and provide his contact address when he absented himself - The absence of the respondent can be regarded as grave misconduct - Therefore, the order of dismissal issued by the appellant is valid and should not be challenged.
[Paras 15, 18, 21 and 26]
