The age of superannuation of employees of an autonomous body is decided by administrative instructions and cannot be enforced by a writ.
Furthermore, any changes to the retirement policy due to financial constraints are not unreasonable or unrelated.

The pay scales of employees and directors of a corporation cannot be equated as directors come from different government services and have different service conditions.
[Para 6]
Pay scales of employees from different corporations cannot be equated as each corporation has to formulate its policy according to its own circumstances.
Thus, there is no discrimination between the employees of two different corporations.
