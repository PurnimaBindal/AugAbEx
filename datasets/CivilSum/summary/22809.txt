Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 12(5) - Subsequent Reference - Earlier reference adjudicated by Tribunal, on non-appearance of appellant, holding that no dispute remains between parties - Subsequent reference on same question adjudicated by Tribunal on merits - High Court holding second reference to be incompetent - According to the ruling, the second reference was maintainable because the matter had not been previously adjudicated on merits.
