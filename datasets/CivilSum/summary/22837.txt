According to the Constitution of India Article 254 and the Industrial Disputes Act, the Central Act is to prevail in case of conflict between state and central legislation.
However, if the State Act receives Presidential assent, it will prevail.
In this case, the UP Act requires 100 workers instead of the 300 required by the Central Act, and as it receives Presidential assent, the provisions of the UP Act will prevail.
B The Constitution of India Article 254 does not state that the later law will prevail in case of conflict.
It only refers to conflicts in existing laws.
C. In order for a State Act to prevail over a Central Act based on Presidential assent, there must be evidence to establish the meaningful assent of the President.
Such evidence cannot be presented for the first time in an appeal.
