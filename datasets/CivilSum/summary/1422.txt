Under Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) Rule, 1930, Rule 9 for Selection/Appointment/Recommendations, State Government approached the Commission for the vacancies of 1982.
Five additional candidates had to be recommended by the Commission for unforeseen vacancies as per the instructions.
Even though the Commission finished selection in 1985, recommended candidates should be appointed only to the posts related to the year 1982.
Candidates selected for 1982 vacancies cannot demand appointment for subsequent vacancies.
[Paras 8, 11 & 14] B.
In accordance with Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) Rules, 1930, Rule 9 for Selection/Interview, 123 candidates were summoned for an interview for nine vacancies.
There was no specific evidence found for Commission's unfair conduct.
Selection cannot be invalidated by calling more candidates for interviews.
[Para 8] C. According to Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) Rules, 1930, Rule 9 for Selection/Unforeseen vacancies, five more candidates' names to fill unforeseen vacancies or those which appointed candidates do not join should be forwarded by the Commission.
However, this instruction does not include anticipated vacancies.
