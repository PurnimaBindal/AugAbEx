Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 - Appointment - Post of Teacher Grade III - The petitioner's appointment was wrongly denied due to the degree obtained from Barkattullah Vishwa Vidhyalaya not being recognized - If the select list expires through no fault of the petitioner and they are still found suitable for the role, the expiration of the select list should not hinder the petitioner's appointment - The petitioners have the right to be appointed to the post of Teacher Grade III.
