Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 54(5) - Charge-sheet - The charge-sheet must contain details of the offence such as the place and date of the commission.
The absence of the time of occurrence does not violate Rule 54(5) of B.S.F.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Border Security Force Act, 1968, Section 117(2) - Border Security Rules, 1969, Rule 169 - Petition against confirmation of findings of Court of Enquiry - A petition against the confirmation of findings of Court of Enquiry was rejected because it was submitted through a counsel and not by the convict.
Directions were issued to decide the petition on its merit since the counsel represents the person who is aggrieved.
