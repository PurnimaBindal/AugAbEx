Constitution of India, Article 136 - Appointment - Regularisation - Seniority - Interim order - Appellants selected via 30th Combined Competitive Examination were appointed into different non-permanent schemes.
They obtained orders from the High Court directing the regularisation of their services.
While an interim order was passed in the writ petition restraining the state government from giving retrospective seniority to those who were regularised by notification dated 13199, it was held that a final opinion could not be expressed as the writ petition is still pending.
However, the interim order was modified to state that all officers successful in the 30th Competitive Examination and already absorbed in the regular cadre prior to 13699 should be treated as senior to those who came via the 31st Competitive Examination.
For others, the status quo will be maintained until the writ petition is disposed of finally.
[Paras 2, 3 and 4]
