Constitution of India, Article 311 - Departmental Enquiry/Removal - In a case where an Assistant Fitter challenged his removal from the service of Corporation, a charge-sheet was issued to him for his default, followed by a regular enquiry and an order of removal by the competent authority under the rules governing his service conditions.
However, the petitioner's appeal against this order was dismissed since no fault was found with the concerned authorities' impugned orders.
