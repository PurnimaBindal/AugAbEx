Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation - Post of Work Inspectors - Petitioners who were initially hired on an ad hoc basis are eligible for regularisation - Despite termination every 89 days, they have been serving in the same capacity for more than five years - Refusal to regularise justified on grounds of no sanctioned posts and insufficient service tenure - However, respondents failed to provide any reason for the need to keep them in service or for budgetary constraints - Their longevity of service, spanning over 8 years, supports the case for their continuance - Similar ad hoc employees have been regularised on court orders before, disregarding lack of sanctioned posts - The court has ordered for the consideration of the petitioners for regularisation.
[Paras 7 and 8]
