Under the Employees Provident Fund Act, 1952, Section 17, Employees Provident Fund Scheme, 1952, petitioners are not obligated to maintain Provident Fund accounts of employees of contractors once exemption is granted to them.
Respondents dispute this, arguing that employees of contractors are still covered under the Trust, citing Section 2 of the Act which includes employees of principle employer and contractor.
The court finds that the exemption indeed relieves the principal employer from maintaining Provident Fund accounts of employees of contractors.

The rule of construction of statutes is to read the statute literally, giving the words used by the legislature their ordinary, natural, and grammatical meaning.
Therefore, the literal construction of rules is regarded as the golden rule of construction since it does not lead to apparent absurdity but to the intent expressed by the legislature.
