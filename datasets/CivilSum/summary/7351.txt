Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, Section 45 - DRTA (Conditions of Appointment and Service) Regulations, 1952, Regulation 5 - Promotion - Deputy Chief General Manager (Traffic) post - Mechanical stream petitioners not eligible for promotion as they have not fulfilled the 5 year service requirement as Senior Manager (Traffic) in grade as per rules - Separate seniority lists maintained for traffic and mechanical officers - Petitioners cannot claim rights as temporary appointments in traffic branch do not create any - Writ filed by petitioners seeking promotion in mechanical branch denied as they do not meet the necessary qualifications as per rules.
[Paras 13 and 14]
