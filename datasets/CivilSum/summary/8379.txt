Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - ITI Conduct, Discipline and Appeal Rules, Rules 34-A - Disciplinary proceedings - The rules allow enquiry to continue even after retirement of the employee.
The court has previously declared enquiry proceedings valid and permissible in an earlier petition, therefore the continuation cannot be challenged in a collateral proceeding initiated for payment of gratuity.
[Paras 4 and 5]
Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 4 - Withholding of gratuity pending finalisation of disciplinary proceedings - Withholding gratuity until disciplinary proceedings are finalised is not illegal.
[Para 6]
