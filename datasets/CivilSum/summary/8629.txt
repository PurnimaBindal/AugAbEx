Civil Procedure Code, Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 - Interim stay - Seniority - Prima facie case - The court must consider public interest before granting interim orders in service matters like appointment, promotion, confirmation, seniority or retirement even if the petitioners have a prima facie case, since it cannot be said that they will suffer an irreparable injury in case the interim injunction is not granted during the appeal.
