According to Rule 42(ii) of the Pepsu Road Transport Corporation (Conditions of Appointment and Service) Regulations 1981, if an employee is absent without leave, they must be given an opportunity to provide an explanation for their absence.
However, in this case, no such chance was given to the delinquent.
Therefore, it was held that the order of termination cannot be deemed legal and sustained under these circumstances.
