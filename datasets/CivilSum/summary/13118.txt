When an employee resigns, their employer-employee relationship comes to an end and there is no need for them to seek permission from the employer before joining another employer.
If an employee is dismissed for joining another employer upon rejoining, this is not considered proper.
As a result, they are ordered to be reinstated with 50% of backwages.
Furthermore, their services should be considered as a fresh appointment from the date of rejoining.
[Paras 11 & 12]
