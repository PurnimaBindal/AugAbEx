The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(c) and Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, Section 2(ii) pertain to Payment of Bonus for Daily Wagers.
The High Court did not consider the applicability of Section 33-C(2) of the Act, nor the jurisdiction of the Labour Court to decide the entitlement matter.
The Bonus Act applicability to daily wagers was also not taken into account.
In light of these factors, the matter has been remitted back.
