According to Section 3(q) of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, the Tribunal has jurisdiction over the allotment of accommodation to Government employees.
However, out-of-turn allotment under discretionary quota to a government servant, de hors the rules, cannot be considered a matter relating to a condition of service.
The respondent was unable to identify any service rule that entitled him to accommodation under the discretionary quota.
Recovery of damages from the respondent was made in accordance with Supreme Court directions in the Shiv Sagar Tiwari case.
The Tribunal lacked jurisdiction to hear the respondent's application challenging his liability to pay damages.
