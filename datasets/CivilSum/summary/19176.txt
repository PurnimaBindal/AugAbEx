Constitution of India, Article 311 - All India Services (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1969, Rules 6 and 24(1) - Natural Justice - Disciplinary action - I.A.S.
Cadre Officer - The Central Government has directed the State Government to re-examine a case involving misconduct committed by an I.A.S.
Cadre Officer, stating that a definite finding should be given as to whether the officer is guilty of misconduct or not, and that a mere warning should not be awarded.
The State Government subsequently issued an order for a fresh enquiry, cancelling the previous enquiry report without notice to the delinquent.
This exercise of revisional power under section 24(1) of the Disciplinary Rules was deemed legal, without violating the principles of natural justice.
The order of the Central Administrative Tribunal upholding the said order was sustained.
