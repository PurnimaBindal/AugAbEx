Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion/Selection Grade/Length of service - Grant in aid - Rajasthan Govt. dated 10180 - It is certain that the petitioners' service before they were admitted to grant in aid must be considered for calculating their 10 years of service in order to qualify for Special Grade Posts.
Additionally, their earlier increments should be safeguarded.
