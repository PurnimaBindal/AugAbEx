Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 7-A - Home workers - The Act is applicable to home workers engaged through contractors and liability of employer arises automatically when the Act becomes applicable - Section 7-A is only for determining the amount of contribution liability - Employers who fail to deduct workmen's contribution from their wages despite notice are liable to pay arrears and cannot claim benefit of their own negligence - The directions issued in District Exhibitors Association Muzaffarnagar & others case by the Supreme Court do not apply to such employers.
