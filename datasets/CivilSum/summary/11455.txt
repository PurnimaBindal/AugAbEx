Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Regularisation of contractual employees - Contractual employees are not entitled to continue on their post nor can they claim to be regularised on it.
Those who were appointed to public posts without following the prescribed procedure or without being appointed by a competent authority have no right to be regularised.
However, contract employees cannot be replaced by another contract employee and are entitled to continue till a regular appointee or transfer replaces them.
They are also entitled to the minimum pay scale of the post.
