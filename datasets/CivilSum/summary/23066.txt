The University Grants Commission Act, 1956, Sections 8(1), 14, 26(1)(e) deals with part-time teachers' regularization claims.
In this case, the respondent's appointment was not authorized by the university, and he did not meet the required qualifications.
The eligibility criteria cannot be relaxed unless there is a specific provision for it, and the benefit of relaxation may only be availed if the person satisfies the specified conditions.
Moreover, the appointment of the respondent as a part-time teacher was not approved, and his breaks in service were not condoned.
Therefore, the decision of the University Managing Council cannot be questioned.
The High Court's judgment conferring full-time teacher status on the respondent from June 1999 cannot be sustained.
