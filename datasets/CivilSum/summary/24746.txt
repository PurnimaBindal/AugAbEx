Constitution of India, Article 16 - Abolition of Post - The transfer of the Centre of Post-graduate Studies from the appellant University to Manipur University may be regarded as resulting in the abolition of the post held by the respondent.
If the post is regarded as one of a group, the principle "last come, first go" will apply, and someone junior to the respondent must leave.
If the post held by him constitutes a class by itself, he is surplus to the requirements of the University and is liable to be retrenched.
[Para 8] B Constitution of India, Article 16 - Post - The respondent has been adjusted against a suitable post in the appellant University and has been working there without a break during the pendency of this litigation, so no retrenchment can be permitted.
