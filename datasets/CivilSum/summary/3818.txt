Constitution of India, Article 14 and 226 - Departmental action - Correction of mistake - Natural justice - The competent authority has a duty to correct a mistake in the seniority list issued at the final stage to protect the interests of the affected employee.
If the person aggrieved by the correction cannot show any prejudice caused to them, a mere complaint of breach of natural justice principles is not enough to undo the justice done by the authority.
