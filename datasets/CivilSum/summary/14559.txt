Sanctity of examination is of utmost importance and cancellation of examination must be based on valid grounds.
In a situation where two candidates with roll numbers from other examination centers were found taking the exam at a different center, and one student did not have a roll number, and a person alleged to be a lecturer was seen leaving the center, cancellation of the entire examination at that center is not justified without proper investigation.
While unfair means must be dealt with, it is unfair to punish all students at the center for the actions of a few.
The decision to cancel the examination at the center is quashed and the board is directed to declare the result.
