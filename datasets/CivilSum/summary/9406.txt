Delhi School Education Rules, 1973, Rule 98 - Teacher - Claim of salary - Appellant's reappointment was accepted with a clause that her salary would be paid only after school received aid from DOE - Rule 98 entitles the appointed person to salary if there is no disapproval received within 15 days of communication of appointment to DOE - A letter regarding the appellant's appointment was sent to DOE and no rejection was received - As per the rule's requirement being fulfilled, grant-in-aid should be released in favor of the school towards the appellant's salary.
