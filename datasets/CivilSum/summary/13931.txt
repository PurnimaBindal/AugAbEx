Under Section 4-A of the Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923, if an employee dies in an accident, the Commissioner must award compensation, with penalties for non-payment.
Employers and insurance companies who fail to pay jointly and severally will be liable for 6% per annum from the date of the accident, and a penalty of 50% will be imposed on the employer.
Additionally, insurance policies do not contain any clause indicating that the liability for interest will not be assumed by the insurance company.
