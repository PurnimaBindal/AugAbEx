The petitioner, who previously served in the Army and is now an ex-serviceman, requests that his previous service during the national emergency be considered for seniority in civil service under the Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, along with the service rendered in civil service.
The court explained that the Rule 2 covers military service and entitles the person for any benefit for the service rendered during the national emergency.
However, the rule applies to only military service rendered during the proclamation of emergency period and not other service in any wing of Armed Forces, holding that the ex-serviceman is entitled to the benefit of military service.
[Paras 5 and 6]
