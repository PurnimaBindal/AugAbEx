Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 311 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume-II, Rule 5.32-A(c) and Rule 3.26(d) of Vol-I, Part-I - Premature Retirement - Premature retirement cannot be used as punishment - Power of pre-mature retirement cannot be invoked merely on account of registration of an FIR in corruption cases unless there is other adverse material available on the employee's service record, which would hold the employee liable to be terminated or if it is in the public interest.
Court also has the power to investigate even if the order is innocuous and non-stigmatic - Premature Retirement can only be ordered on account of registration of FIR U/s 7/13 & 49 of Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 on the basis of a motivated complaint alleging demand of bribe by the charge-sheeted subordinate - No adverse entry in service record - `Good' entries in the last 10 years' service record - Premature Retirement held not justified - Power of Premature Retirement cannot be used arbitrarily as a short method of terminating on account of misconduct and without recording satisfaction of the authority that it was undesirable in the public interest keeping the employee any more in service - The only ground of criminal case extinguished on an honorable acquittal - No other material on record to be considered - Dismissal of writ petition merely observing that the authority record the service record held to be entirely incorrect.
