Constitution of India Articles 14 and 16 - Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfers of Undertakings) Act 1970 Section 9 - New Bank of India (Amalgamation and Transfer of Undertakings) Scheme 1993 Clause 5(4) - Placement Scheme of employees - The Central Government framed the Placement Scheme under Clause 5(4) of the Amalgamation Scheme on 8.12.1993 - An order passed by the Punjab National Bank regarding transfers and deployments of employees of New Bank of India on 1691993 before the enforcement of Placement Scheme is not sustainable - Deployment must be made on the basis of principles and criteria laid down in the Placement Scheme, which are different from those laid down by the Punjab National Bank - The Placement Scheme framed by the Government under Clause 5(4) of the Scheme is an administrative order that cannot have retrospective effect.
[Paras 4 to 11] B UP High Court Amalgamation Order 1948 Clause 14 - Special appeal - Jurisdiction - An appeal against an order passed by a judge of the High Court at Allahabad is not maintainable before the Lucknow Bench.
