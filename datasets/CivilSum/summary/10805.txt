Kerala Engineering Service (Civil and General Branch) Rules 1965, Rules 4(d), 5(b) - Degree/Diploma holders - Promotion - Degree holders cannot be promoted before any junior Diploma holder - Eligibility criteria of 3 and 7 years experience required for promotion of degree and diploma holder - The rule does not allow promotion based on seniority - Court cannot process an interpretation to add a new method to provision.

Kerala Engineering Service (Civil and General Branch) Rules 1965, Rules 4(d), 5(b) - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226 - Degree/Diploma holders - Promotion of junior diploma holder - Constitutional validity - Degree holders have 75% posts whereas Diploma holders have 20% - Provisions cannot be annulled as Degree holders have been given more than a fair share, being violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - Separate seniority lists and quota prescribed by rules.
[Paras 24, 25]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Amendment of rule - Power of government - Executive order has to be in conformity with the rule - Government cannot amend or replace a rule by an executive order.
