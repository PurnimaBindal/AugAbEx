Railway Services (Pension) Rules, 1993, Rule 21 - Re-employment - Dearness relief - Double Dearness Allowance - Family pension - Compassionate appointment of pensioner receiving family pension on account of death of her husband in harness - Since the petitioner is both an employee and pensioner due to being hired to substitute her husband and receiving family pension simultaneously, Rule 21 will apply and she will not be entitled to receive double dearness relief - Stoppage of dearness allowance with the pension is not illegal.
[Para 7] As for the recovery of excess payment, the petitioner voluntarily paid excess dearness relief with the pension prior to the amendment of Rule and may not be able to repay it now - Recovery of the excess payment is deemed unjustified.
