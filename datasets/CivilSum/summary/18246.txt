According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 226 and 300-A, and Rule 4 of the Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules of 1965, military service must be taken into account for civil employment benefits such as increments and seniority.
The petitioner in this case was entitled to these benefits but they were later withdrawn by the respondents.
However, the order to withdraw these benefits was set aside and the petitioner's claim was allowed partly.
