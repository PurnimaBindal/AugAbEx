The principle of res judicata in relation to matters under the Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, was applied in a case where a workman filed a civil suit, but it was dismissed due to lack of jurisdiction.
The industrial dispute was then raised, and the management filed an application seeking dismissal of the reference due to the principle of res judicata.
The Labour Court found that the reference was not bad under the principle of res judicata, and this finding was upheld by the Court.

The principle of election under Section 11 of the Civil Procedure Code, 1908, was applied in a case where a workman elected to file a civil suit and must face the consequences of that election.
The result of the election must be taken to its logical end.
