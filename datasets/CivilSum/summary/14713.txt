Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 9 - Departmental enquiry - A de novo inquiry on the same charges must be conducted by an Enquiry Officer other than the one who already submitted a report if the Punishing Authority has ordered it, and if the previous Enquiry Officer gave the petitioner the benefit of the doubt without explanation, according to Para 6,7, and 8.
The rule does not permit a de novo inquiry on the same charges without explanation, but it allows the Punishing Authority to request further inquiry by the same Enquiry Officer.
The Punishing Authority does not have the authority to issue an order for de novo enquiry.
The de novo enquiry was quashed.
