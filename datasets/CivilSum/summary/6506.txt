Constitution of India, Article 309 - Central Civil Services (Classification Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 29(2) - H.P.
Staff (Recruitment Promotion Condition of Service) Rules, 1997, Rule 19 - Punishment - Enhancement of - Revision - Notice given under revisional powers before expiry of time for filing Appeal - It has been held that the delinquent will be deprived of the right to file an appeal under the rules if revision proceedings are allowed, thus rendering them not maintainable.
