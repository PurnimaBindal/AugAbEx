The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Conditions of appointment - Selection - Doctrine of estoppel - Candidates who accepted the appointment letter with a condition of six months of training, and appointment only if they were found successful and suitable, cannot question this condition at a later stage.
[Paras 11 and 12] B The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Concealment of facts - Clean hands - Individuals who conceal material facts cannot expect any relief from the court and must approach it with transparency.
