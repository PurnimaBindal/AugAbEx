Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - An employee must be given reasonable time to serve at one place, unless administrative exigencies require their services elsewhere - Personal inconvenience of employee must also be taken into account, but the interests of administration should be considered paramount - Petitioner's representation was rejected for lack of disclosure of administrative reasons for their transfer - If the petitioner is at fault, an inquiry should be initiated and resolved quickly - However, the employer should not deal with the petitioner in a casual and cavalier manner without proper explanation.
[Paras 1 and 12]
