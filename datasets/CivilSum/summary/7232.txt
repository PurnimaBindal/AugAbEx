Constitution of India, Article 16 - Regularisation of service - Appellants were initially employed as teachers by Municipal Council on contractual tenures - The clarifications stated that the engagement would not give any right to claim regular appointment - Also, each time the contract was renewed, it was specified that the extension would continue for the agreed period or until the posts are filled on a regular basis, whichever comes first - Thus, the claim for regularisation cannot be considered.
