Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Sections 28 and 30(2) - Appellants - Complainants - The complainants have been granted interim relief, restraining the appellants from terminating their services until the disposal of the complaint.
However, the question as to whether the ‘employer-employee' relationship was unquestionable was not considered.
An application has been made for the grant of interim relief restored, and the order of the single judge has been set aside.
