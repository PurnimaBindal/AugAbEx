In accordance with Article 226/227 of the Constitution of India, the termination of a petitioner who had been serving as an Assistant Lineman was challenged in court.
Despite having suffered a 25% disability due to an accident, the petitioner fulfilled his official responsibilities to the satisfaction of his employer for a period of five years.
It was determined that the petitioner could not be terminated under these circumstances, and the order was therefore quashed.
