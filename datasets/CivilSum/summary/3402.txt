Constitution of India, Article 226 - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Rule 16 - Termination of Services - Regularisation - Petitioners were appointed on a daily wage basis for six months for election duty and placed in an auxiliary battalion.
Their services were extended from time to time until they were permanently absorbed in CRPF.
However, the petitioners refused to perform their duty as a token of their resentment to show discontentment about auxiliary employment for about four years.
On disbanding of the battalion, the services of petitioners were discharged/terminated.
While respondents used the word 'misconduct' in one of their communications, it is not considered punitive as it was used only by way of indicating petitioner's conduct in showing resentment and resorting to a high degree of indiscipline while remaining a member of the force.
Thus, the order of termination is upheld.
