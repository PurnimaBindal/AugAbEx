Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Family pension - Arrears - Withholding of - Interest - The petitioner's husband was involved in a criminal case, and as a result, their pension was stopped by authorities.
However, it was later restored, but arrears were not given from 1991 to 1993.
It was held that involvement in a criminal case does not forfeit an employee's right to receive pension.
The arrears were ordered to be given with a 10% interest.
