Punjab Civil Services (Punishment & Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rule 7(6) - Inquiry report - Disagreement of Punishing authority with part of the findings - In case of disagreements, the punishing authority must provide the Government servant with the point(s) of disagreement and the grounds for it, as well as a brief statement.
It has been found that if the charges against the delinquent are divisible and the first part of the charge is enough to justify a dismissal, then the dismissal order cannot be invalidated solely because reasons for disagreement with other charges were not communicated.
