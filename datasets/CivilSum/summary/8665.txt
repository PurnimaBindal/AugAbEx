Enquiry officers do not need to record the statements of passengers or count cash in departmental proceedings against Bus Conductors accused of failing to issue tickets after collecting fare - Punishment is valid as long as there is evidence on which the authority could have found the employee guilty - Authority considered evidence provided by checking staff and past conduct and service record of the employee before issuing the punishment order.
[Paras 1, 10 and 17] B.
Punishing authorities are allowed to take past service record and punishments into consideration when issuing punishment orders - Since the petitioner was given copies of his past record and punishment before receiving his punishment order, the order is not vitiated.
[Para 10] C. Orders of punishment are not invalidated by discrimination, even if other employees with similar past records were allowed to continue in service - The guilty should not be exonerated purely because others were not punished.
