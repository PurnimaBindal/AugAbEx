Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical Reimbursement - The denial of medical reimbursement based on the petitioner's failure to furnish contribution was unfounded - The petitioner's husband, an ESI Corporation employee, received a medical card a day before his passing, and at the time of his hospitalization, the card had been valid for over a month - Despite not being a cardholder or making the necessary payment, the petitioner's husband had a constitutional right to healthcare - Furthermore, as the respondent possessed the petitioner's husband's retiral benefits, any payable amount could have been informed to the petitioner - As such, the petitioner is entitled to receive medical reimbursement for the treatment provided to her deceased husband.
