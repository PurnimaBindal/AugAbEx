Rajasthan Compassionate Appointments of Dependents of Deceased Govt. Servant Rules, 1996, Rule 2(c) - Compassionate appointment - The definition of "Dependent" is limited to unmarried/widowed daughters and excludes married daughters - This distinction is based on logical reasoning and is connected to the objectives of the Rules - The exclusion of married daughters from the definition of "Dependent" in Rule 2(c) is constitutional and does not violate Article 14 of the Constitution.
