Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934 - Rule 13.18 - Confirmation - Officiating Head Constables can be confirmed with the benefit of their officiating service counted towards their probationary period.
Some petitioners argued that the state confirmed juniors with average service records before their requisite five years of officiating service, without considering similarly situated cases.
This court has now directed the state to give the same treatment to the petitioners as they have given to the respondents regarding confirmation.
For petitioners whose ACRs were not considered due to unavailability, the state has been ordered to trace them and consider their case from the date of others' consideration.
Petition allowed.
