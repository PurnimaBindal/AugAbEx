The power to give out-of-turn promotion to subordinates must be exercised with subjective consideration of the facts involved under Constitution of India, Article 226.
Reports should be submitted to officers immediately subordinate to the ultimate authority having the power to grant promotion and the power to grant promotion should not be exercised arbitrarily.
Any actions taken must reflect a deep consideration of the circumstances.
[Para 6]

The claim of a petitioner for out-of-turn promotion, who engaged in apprehending notorious culprits, was ignored by the Director General of Police without valid reasons, even though the Inspector-General of Police recommended the petitioner's case, along with others, for out-of-turn promotion under Constitution of India, Article 226.
As a result, the order is liable to be quashed.
