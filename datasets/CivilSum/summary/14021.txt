Constitution of India, Article 226 - Seniority - Promotion - Vigilance enquiry - Pendency of - The petitioner's promotion cannot be stopped on account of a pending vigilance case if there was no charge-sheet pending when the petitioner became eligible for promotion.
Therefore, the respondent is directed to grant assumed seniority to the petitioner from the date he became eligible for promotion.
