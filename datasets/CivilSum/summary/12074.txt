Constitution of India, Article 226 - Award - Payment of compensation in lieu of reinstatement - Challenge as to - The petitioner was unable to be reinstated due to not joining duty when an offer was made during conciliation proceedings - According to the court, the petitioner should have joined duty as a helper under protest if he believed he was entitled to a higher designation/salary, hence, the petitioner is to blame, and the court rightly granted compensation instead of reinstatement at their discretion.
[Paras 7 to 9]
