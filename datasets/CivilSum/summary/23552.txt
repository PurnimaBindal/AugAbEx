Constitution of India, Articles 16(4), 14 and 335 - Reservation - Reservations in promotions - Validity of the government circular dated 451974 providing reservations in accordance with a roster to be maintained and implemented in the form of a running account from year to year - Article 16(4) provides for adequate representation of Scheduled and Backward Classes in Government Services - However, this provision cannot be used to cause reverse discrimination so as to violate the guarantee of equality in Article 14 - Therefore, the roster will stop operating as and when reservation is complete as per quote rule - Those who receive accelerated promotions against reserved posts will be placed junior to those who were senior to them in the lower cadre and were promoted later only because of post reservation - For further promotions, they will not have the right of prior promotion against general category posts only because of their prior promotion - Accelerated promotion does not amount to supersession - They will have preferential right to be considered only against reserved posts out of turn in the further higher cadre, not against the general posts - Therefore, seniority of the candidates in the lower cadre must be maintained in the higher cadre despite prior promotions of juniors against reserved posts, and they have to compete with the general candidates on the basis of seniority-cum-merit as general candidates, except in the case of reserved posts.
