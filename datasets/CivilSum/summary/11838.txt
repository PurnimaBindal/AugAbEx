The Punjab Electrical Inspectorate (Class-I) Service Rules of 1992, Rule 8, governs promotion and qualifications from Class II to Class I service, and mandates that Executive Engineers must have a degree and eight years of experience to be eligible.
Changes in the rules altered the eligibility qualifications, but did not infringe on any prior vested rights to promotion.
The constitutionality of the eligibility requirements were called into question, but the court found that providing eligibility conditions for promotion is a matter of policy and does not warrant interference unless it results in a failure of justice.
The court also found that reservation in promotion is permissible if it is made on the basis of different qualifications.
Furthermore, certain articles of the Constitution of India allow govemments to classify employees differently for the purpose of scale of pay based on their qualifications and experience.
Finally, no prior permission of the government is required for framing new service rules for promotion to higher posts as long as no vested rights are taken away.
