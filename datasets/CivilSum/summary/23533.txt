The Chairman of an Administrative Tribunal has direct control over its Registry and is responsible for its proper functioning.
In the case of a contempt petition moved by members of the Bar, the Chairman has a duty to explain the actual position of the Registry to the High Court.
[Para 13]  B Using intemperate language and making disparaging remarks unless it is necessary for deciding the case is inconsistent with judicial behavior.
Making written orders creates a permanent record, so it is necessary for judges to practice self-restraint while making orders.
The Division Bench overlooked this aspect and made disparaging remarks against the appellant based on a non-existing foundation.
All these remarks are expunged from the impugned order.
