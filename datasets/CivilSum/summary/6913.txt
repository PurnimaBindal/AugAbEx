As per Section 2(j) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, whether a hospital is an industry or not is a mixed question of fact and law.
The employer cannot raise this question at the stage of hearing of writ petition for the first time, especially if there is no pleading or evidence on this aspect.
Even if the hospital is a charitable organisation, it would not be proper to hold that it is not an industry without proper evidence.
[Paras 8 to 11] B.
In a case where a respondent working as a pharmacist in a hospital seeks the grant of payscale as recommended by the IVth pay commission, even if the employer claims inability to pay, the respondent is entitled to benefit from the recommendation along with arrears.
This was established by a circular issued by the Commissioner.
