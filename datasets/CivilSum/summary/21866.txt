Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Section 10 - Misconduct - Termination - If an employer terminates a workman without initiating a domestic enquiry, the employer can provide justification for their action in front of the Tribunal by presenting evidence.
In the case of a defective domestic enquiry, it is treated the same as no enquiry and the employer would still have to satisfy the tribunal that the dismissal or discharge was justified.
However, if the employer leads evidence before the Labour Court and the court finds the charges to be proven, the termination order would relate back to the date of the original order of termination.
[Paras 5, 7, 10 & 16]
