Constitution of India, Article 226 - Selection - Eligibility - Appointment for the post of Art and Craft Teacher - The deadline for receipt of applications was February 15, 1996 - The petitioner submitted her application and indicated that she would take the final exam in May 1996 - Despite not assessing her eligibility, the Selection Committee issued an interview letter - The petitioner had not passed the required training qualification by February 15, 1996 - The court relied on JT 1993(1) SC 220 and concluded that the petitioner was not qualified for selection.
