Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Kith and Kin Policy of appointment - Jurisdiction of the Tribunal - The Tribunal should usually only instruct to consider applications for appointment, even if the individual is deemed qualified for a position under the Kith and Kin policy - The Tribunal cannot direct the appointment of an individual to a post or establish a supernumerary post for appointment - Appointing an individual in the absence of a post would be a misuse of public funds and a severe abuse of a public authority's powers.
