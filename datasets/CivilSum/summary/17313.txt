Constitution of India, Article 14(4) - Sub-reservation - Sub- categorisation within reservation for SCs in Block `A' and Block `B' inter- transferable in case of non-availability of suitable candidates in a particular Block - If the most suitable candidate for a single vacancy is in Block `B', the Block `A' precedence cannot be prioritized, and the selection of the more meritorious candidate in Block `B' does not violate the reservation policy.
