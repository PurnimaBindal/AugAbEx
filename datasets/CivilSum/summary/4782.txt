Sections 6 and 11(2) of the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 establish that arrears of Provident Fund have a special protection and can be considered "Crown Debt".
Amounts due under the 1952 Act are deemed to be the first charge and must be paid in priority to all debts, as per Section 11(2).
Thus, the plea of the decree holder to prioritize the execution of the decree, which is prior in time, is untenable.
The relevant date is the date on which the amount fell due.
RPFC is entitled to receive the amount based on the priority of "Crown Debts".
