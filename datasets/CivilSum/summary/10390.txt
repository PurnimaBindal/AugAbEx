Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Regularisation - Regularisation will be based on the decision of the Supreme Court and instructions of State Government - Petitioner was appointed on a daily wage basis - The services of the petitioner have not been regularised despite juniors being given the same - Petitioner has claimed to have served for 240 days or more in a year - The respondents have not opposed this claim - The petitioner has been directed to be considered for regularisation as per instructions - Petition allowed.
[Paras 7 and 8]
