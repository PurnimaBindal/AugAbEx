The Army Act, 1950, Sections 122, 123 are being challenged, particularly the territorial jurisdiction of the High Court.
It was held that the service of a summons on the petitioner within the jurisdiction of the High Court does not give rise to a cause of action to question the punishment imposed upon the petitioner.
This is especially true since the petitioner was not posted, charge-sheeted nor were any disciplinary proceedings held or any order of punishment passed or communicated within the territorial jurisdiction of the Court.
