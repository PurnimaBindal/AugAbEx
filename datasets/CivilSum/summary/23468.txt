Constitution of India, Article 14 - General Insurance (Emergency Provision) Act, 1971, Sections 15(e) and 16 - Termination of services under scheme - The power to terminate the services of existing employees by giving one month's notice is a valid rule.
If a post is not abolished and the employee's performance of duties is unsatisfactory, termination may occur.
However, in this case, the termination order was illegal and was subsequently quashed.
As a result, the appellant was granted a sum of Rs. 1,00,000/- as compensation instead of reinstatement, based on the facts and circumstances of the case.
