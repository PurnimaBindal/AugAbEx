The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Termination of contract with a stigmatic order - Regular enquiry and proper opportunity must be given before such an order is passed - Termination order quashed.
[Para 10] B.
Civil Procedure code, Section 100 - Finding of fact - Identifying if an order of termination is stigmatic is a matter of fact - Regular Second Appeal is not the place to interfere with this decision.
[Para 11] C. The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Contract of service - Probationer - Natural justice - Service on a contract basis cannot violate principles of natural justice - A person working on contract basis cannot be equated to a probationer.
