The Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 9(1) and Regulation 19(1) are subject to Section 23 of the Contract Act, 1872 and Article 14 of the Constitution While Regulation 19(1) allows the FCI to terminate the services of a permanent employee with just three months' notice or pay, it does not provide opportunity of hearing and is thus void.
The Court held that the regulation is against public policy and struck it down.
[Paras 13 and 14]
