The power of the Labour Court to reduce the sentence under Section 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, is not unlimited in scope.
Discretion to reduce punishment can be exercised if the punishment is disproportionate to the gravity of misconduct, or if there exist mitigating circumstances, or if the workman's past conduct warrants it.
However, the Labour Court cannot reduce the punishment by way of sympathy under Section 11-A [Para 18]
The use of abusive language against a superior officer in presence of subordinates cannot be termed as an indiscipline that calls for a lesser punishment than dismissal, in absence of any extenuating factor under Section 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
