Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226 - Transfer - Consent - Respondents claim `swecha se' was a mistake - Petitioner working at same place for 9 years with complaint against him from villagers - Transfer without employee consent, not mala fide or based on extraneous consideration, and based on administrative exigencies can't be improper.
[Paras 48, 49]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226 - Order of Transfer - Judicial review - Transfer power must be exercised honestly, bona fidely, reasonably, and in public interest.
If based on extraneous reasons, it would be mala fide and colourable exercise of power.
Frequent transfers expose lack of mind application, executive highhandedness, and become punitive and mala fide.
Court must investigate the operative explanation behind the transfer order.
[Paras 25, 28, 30, 32, 47]
Transfer - Principles of natural justice - Order of transfer does not have adverse civil effects, so compliance with the principles of natural justice is not necessary.
[Para 22]
Transfer - Misconduct - Transfers cannot be used as punishment for misconduct.
