Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation of casual illegal appointments - No appointment made illegally, without public notice and without following prescribed procedures, can be protected and ordered to be regularised - Continuance in service for some time does not create a right to hold the post - Such appointees must compete with others eligible and can at most be given age relaxation on account of their service rendered.
[Paras 4 to 6]
