Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equivalence of degree/diplomas - Recognition and derecognition of - The Punjab Government recognised the two-year JBT course of Jabalpur in 1960 but derecognised it in 1990.
The petitioner, who passed the course in 1985, cannot be held disqualified as the derecognition was not made retrospective.
The certificate issued when the course was recognised must be accepted.
[Paras 14 and 15]
