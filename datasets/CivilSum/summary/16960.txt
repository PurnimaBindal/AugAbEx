Panjab Panchayat Samities and Zila Parishad Services Rules, 1965, Rules 11(3)(a)(i)(ii) - Temporary - Adhoc appointment - Appointment must be made based on advertised posts and selection with medical examination before regularization.
Temporary appointees are subject to termination if their work and conduct is not satisfactory.
The assumption of an adhoc appointment loses its significance and cannot be replaced by another adhoc/temporary appointment.
Termination would be illegal if the post is still vacant and exists.
[Paras 2, 9 and 10]
