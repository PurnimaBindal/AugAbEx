Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Police Act, 1861, Section 7 - Increment - Withholding of - DSP issued a charge memo accusing an officer of perfunctory investigation in a crime involving the death of a woman in 1992, in 1998.
The officer was punished with stoppage of increment for two years without cumulative effect in 2001.
This action deprived the delinquent officer of a reasonable opportunity to effectively present their defence.
As a result, the punishment order was deemed improper and quashed.
