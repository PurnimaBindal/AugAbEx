Delhi Road Transport Authority (Conditions of Appointment and Service) Regulation, 1952 Regulation 9(b) - Termination-Natural Justice - Rearing - Regulation 9(b) of the Delhi Road Transport Authority is arbitrary, uncanalised, and unrestricted, and violates the principles of natural justice as well as Article 14 of the Constitution.
As regulated bodies, government companies or public corporations are considered state instrumentalities and therefore subject to the observance of fundamental rights embodied in Part III as well as to conform to the Directive Principles in Part IV of the Constitution.
Regulation 9(b) is illegal and void, as it is arbitrary, discriminatory, and without any guidelines for the exercise of power.
The power to terminate the service of a permanent employee cannot be passed by simply issuing one-month notice under Regulation 9(b) or pay in lieu thereof without recording any reason in the order and without giving any hearing to the employee to controvert the allegation on the basis of which the purported order is made.
Power of Judicial Review - Interpretation of Statutes - The doctrine of reading down or recasting the statute can be applied only in a rare case where the language of the statute is clear and unambiguous.
The court should not attempt to amend or bend a statute even for a good reason without the legislative amending it.
