The Constitution of India's Articles 226 and 311(2), along with the Central Industrial Security Force Rules of 2001's Rule 32(i) and Rules 34 & 39(ii), discuss the dismissal from service of an individual without the need for an enquiry.
In one specific case, a petitioner confessed in writing to the molestation of a Swiss lady employee of a hotel while on SPG duty in Switzerland.
The DIG, SPG also had to apologize to Swiss authorities for his action.
An order of dismissal was passed upon the petitioner, dispensing the need for an enquiry due to the incident not only being prejudicial to the general public and discipline of the force, but also having the potential of tarnishing the good name of the country.
Considering the possible international ramifications of the case would attract significant media attention, the decision for dispensation of enquiry is rational and relevant.
No interference is necessary.
