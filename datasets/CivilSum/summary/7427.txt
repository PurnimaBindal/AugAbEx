Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 2(4), 6, 7 - Claim of gratuity - Dispute between legal heirs - Adjudication by Controlling Authority - Permissibility - Controlling authority cannot decide the issue of rightful heirs and their shares as it is not a civil court with any legal background and therefore cannot decide the inter se claims of heirs of deceased.
[Para 8]
Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 2(4), 6, 7 - Claim of gratuity - Right of nominee - Holding nominee to have exclusive rights over the amount is not valid as the mere nomination will not confer any beneficial interest in nominee of the amount payable and legal heirs can claim amount as per law of succession applicable to them.
So, the nominee merely receives the amount and cannot be said to have exclusive rights over such money.
[Paras 7 and 9]
Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 2(4), 6, 7 - Claim of gratuity - Nomination in the name of the mother - A nomination made in favour of dependent parents is valid if there is no dispute raised regarding the dependency of the mother on the deceased.
Deceased already had a family at the time of making nomination in favour of the mother, which is considered within the term 'family'.
