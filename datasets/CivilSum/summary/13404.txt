Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300-A - Entitlement Rules, Rules 4 and 7(b) with Annexure-I and Appendix II - Disability pension - Petitioner, discharged from Air Force service due to "non-organic psychosis", an incurable disease, claimed disability pension which was declined - According to Annexure-I, this disease qualifies for a disability pension - Evidence showed that petitioner had been dealing with this problem since 1990, after his attestation - As a result, petitioner was granted disability pension.
