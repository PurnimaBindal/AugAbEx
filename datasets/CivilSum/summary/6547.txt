Jammu and Kashmir Compassionate Appointment Rules, 1994, Rule 3 - Compassionate appointment - A petitioner received appointment as ASI in police department on compassionate grounds but is seeking appointment as Inspector.
However, the fact that two other individuals were appointed as Inspectors does not necessarily mean that the petitioner is at the same level as them since their situations, facts, and circumstances differ from the petitioner's case.
The rules clearly state that dependents of family members who died in a military-related incident can only be appointed to non-gazetted service posts.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to any relief.
[Paras 5 and 6]
