According to Haryana Social Welfare and Relief Organisation Service Group `C' Rules, 1983, promotion is regulated by seniority.
In a case where a promotion was challenged by the respondents on the ground that they were eligible for the promotion in terms of Draft Rules, the appellant was reverted and the respondents were promoted in their place by the High Court.
However, the High Court was not justified in relying on the Draft Rules, which were not enforced.
As the rules had not been notified for over a decade, the promotion could only have been regulated on the basis of seniority.
As a result, the appeal was allowed with all consequential benefits.
