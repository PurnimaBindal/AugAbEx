Guru Nanak Dev University Calendar Volume III (1966 Edition), Ordinance 3.1 Chapter 11 - Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Act, 1974 (as amended in 1983) Section 2-A - Petitioners must undergo probation for one year following appointment to a post lasting over a year, with probation extendable up to two years at most - After two years, petitioners must be deemed confirmed under statutory rules and regulations - Any refusal of approval by the University would be arbitrary and without jurisdiction.

Guru Nanak Dev University Calendar Volume III (1966 Edition), Ordinance 3.1 Chapter 11 - Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Act, 1974 (as amended in 1983) Sections 2-A and 11 - The Act has overriding power over University ordinances - If an employee is appointed to a post likely lasting over a year, they must undergo one year of probation which can only be extended to two years at most.
[Para 9]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 - Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Act, 1974, Sections 2-A and 11 - Vacancies arising from the resignation or death of employees cannot be considered as purely temporary - Such vacancies lasting over a year must be deemed as substantive and the appointments protected under the Act.
[Paras 7 and 9]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Approval - Discrimination - University approval of appointments and confirmations for certain employees and not for the petitioners, arbitrarily and unjustly discriminates against them.
[Para 10]

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Increment - Stoppage of increment - An employee must earn their annual increment unless their performance is found to be unsatisfactory - Proper procedure must be followed to stop an employee's increment - No evidence found that the petitioners' performance was unsatisfactory, so the respondent's action is not sustainable.
