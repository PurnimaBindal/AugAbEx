The responsibility for payment of wages to teachers lies with the management, regardless of whether the post is covered by grant-in-aid or not.
An order issued by DPI(S) was upheld by the Tribunal, which directed the petitioner-society to release backwages, a decision that was found to be neither arbitrary nor illegal.
However, neither the DPI nor the Tribunal have jurisdiction to direct the State Government not to release grant-in-aid to the petitioner society.
In light of these findings, the matter has been remanded back to the State Government for a final decision on whether grant-in-aid should be released to the petitioner society.
AIR 1989 SC 1607 is referenced in support of this conclusion.
