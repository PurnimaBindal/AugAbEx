The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Successive proceedings - If proceedings against an employee are initiated and concluded or interdicted by the Court, the Court orders must be challenged as per the remedy available in law - Once the court orders attain finality, the authority cannot reopen the same on the same cause of action.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Contract Act, Section 73 - Negligence - Misconduct - Suit for recovery of damages - Negligence may or may not amount to misconduct - Acts of malfeasance, misfeasance, and nonfeasance can lead to a suit for recovery of damages if proved - Negligence could result in disciplinary proceedings, but not for recovery of damages in every case.
