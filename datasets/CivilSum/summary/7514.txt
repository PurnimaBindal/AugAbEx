Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(a)(ii), 25-B & 25-F - Retrenchment - Daily wage workers - Computation of 240 days - Sundays and holidays - The counting of working days for the purpose of Section 25F of the Act only includes days on which the worker has actually worked, and does not include unpaid holidays when the management is not obligated to pay the worker.
