The Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised Pay Scale) Rules of 1998, which reduced the pay scale of vice-principals who were promoted and had their pay scale fixed at Rs. 8000-13500 in 1996, was challenged as being unconstitutional under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
The court held that vested and accrued rights cannot be withdrawn retroactively through amendments to rules or through the enforcement of newly framed rules.
[Paras 17, 18 & 19]

An amendment to a rule that withdraws benefits available to a government servant from a particular scale of pay on the basis of their rights flowing from relevant rules cannot be made retroactively.
Such an amendment would be invalid under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 17]
