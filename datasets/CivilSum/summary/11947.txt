Punjab Civil Services Punishment and Appeal Rules, 1970, Rules 8(3) and (4) - Constitution of India, Article 14 - Natural justice/Preliminary enquiry report/Enquiry Findings of the Enquiry Officer based on the preliminary enquiry report - The charged official must be supplied with a copy of a document being relied upon against them to comply with the rules of natural justice.
[Para 5]

Punjab Civil Services Punishment and Appeal Rules, 1970, Rules 8(3) and (4) - Constitution of India, Article 14 - Natural Justice/Preliminary enquiry/Witnesses/Statements recorded in primary enquiry - The delinquent official should not be taken by surprise during an enquiry and needs to be aware of who the witnesses are and what documents will be produced to prove a particular charge.
Non-supplying copies of the statements of witnesses in the preliminary enquiry violates principles of natural justice.
[Paras 6 and 7]

Punjab Civil Services Punishment and Appeal Rules, 1970, Rules 8(3) and (4) - Constitution of India, Article 14 - Natural justice/Enquiry/List of witnesses/List of documents - A charged official needs to be informed of the witnesses and documents going to be produced against them to prepare a proper defense and obstructing this right violates natural justice.
[Para 6]

Punjab Civil Services Punishment and Appeal Rules, 1970, Rules 8 (3) and (4) - Constitution of India, Article 14- Natural justice/Preliminary enquiry/Statement of witnesses/Statements recorded during preliminary enquiry - Statements from a preliminary enquiry that aren't supplied to the charged official cannot be relied upon to prove guilt and inflict punishment.
[Para 7]

Punjab Civil Services Punishment and Appeal Rules, 1970, Rule 8(8) - Constitution of India, Article 14 - Natural justice/Enquiry - It is the responsibility of the authorities to let charged officials know about the assistance of their co-employee/next friend since they are entitled to proper opportunity to defend and violation is against the rules and natural justice.
[Para 8]

Punjab Civil Services Punishment and Appeal Rules, 1970, Rules 8(3) and (4) - Constitution of India, Article 14 - Natural justice/Hearing/ Appeal/Appellate Authority - Where the delinquent official requests for personal hearing, it should not be denied, since it is an opportunity to show disciplinary authority reasons why the enquiry report shouldn't be accepted.
Petitioner was not given an opportunity at the right time, and this violates the rules of natural justice.
[Para 9]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Civil Services Punishment and Appeal Rules, 1970/Charge-sheet/Findings on guilt/Contents of -charge-sheet - Where the charge-sheet itself contains the language that the charge against the delinquent stands proved, it is almost impossible for the Enquiry Officer to dissent such findings, and it violates rules of natural justice.
[Para 12]

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Natural justice/Preliminary enquiry report - A preliminary enquiry report which was the basis of findings of guilt against the petitioner had to be supplied so that they could prepare their defense properly.
Defense of the petitioner was prejudiced which violates natural justice, and the enquiry proceedings are liable to be set aside on this point alone.
