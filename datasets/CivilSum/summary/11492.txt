Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admissions - Reservation for handicapped - Interference in writ jurisdiction - Examination of the eligibility of a candidate suffering from a specific type of disability for the MBBS course - Expert opinion required to determine the capability of such candidates to perform clinical examinations/diagnosis/surgeries - Court not equipped to arrive at such a conclusion, and therefore, such provisions cannot be deemed arbitrary - However, 3% reservation for handicapped individuals must not go unused, even if it means reconsidering and reconstituting an expert committee for the examination of handicapped applicants.
[Paras 10 to 13]
