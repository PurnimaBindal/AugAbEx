The Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 4(1)(b) mandates a minimum qualifying period for payment of gratuity.
This provision is a reasonable restriction on the employer's right to conduct business and is part of a welfare legislation intended to secure social and economic justice.
The Act's provisions ensure that workmen receive decent standards of living in their old age, and it has provisions for payment of gratuity that must be provided regardless of the employer's financial capacity to bear the burden.
Therefore, the provisions of the Act are both sustainable and valid, and the decision of the Pb.
& Hy.
High Court to reject Section 4(1)(b) is set aside.
