Civil Procedure Code, Section 9 - Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, Sections 7(i) and 14-B - Specific Relief Act, 1963, Section 41(h) - Bar to civil Court jurisdiction - An implied bar on civil court jurisdiction exists due to the availability of an effective alternate remedy in the special Acts, even in absence of specific bar- EPF Act provides for an appeal against any order passed under it - Unless the order is null and void passed without jurisdiction, the civil Court cannot accept suit against such orders.
