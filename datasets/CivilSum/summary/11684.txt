Faridabad Complex Employees Pension and General Provident Fund Rules, 1993 - Scope and applicability - According to the Rules, only employees who were in service of Faridabad Complex Admn.
as on 16.4.1992 are eligible for pension - Employees who retired earlier are not entitled to pension under the Rules, as confirmed in the case of Dhan Singh v. Faridabad MC (DB).
