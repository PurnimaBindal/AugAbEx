Factories Act, 1948, Section 2(n) proviso (ii), 92 and 101 - Renewal of Registration licence - Occupier of the factory - The occupier of a factory owned or run by a company must be one of the directors for the purposes of the Act, according to proviso (ii) to Section 2(n), and cannot be nominated by the company to be any other employee with 'ultimate control over the affairs of the factory.'
If the company fails to nominate one of its directors as the occupier, the Inspector of Factories may treat any one of the directors as the deemed occupier of the factory for prosecution and punishment for any breach or contravention of the Act or offences committed under the Act.
