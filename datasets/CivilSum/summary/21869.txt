Andhra Pradesh Police Service Rules, 1966, Rules 6, 7(b) & (e) and 26 - Probation - Order of Confirmation - Relaxation of conditions for confirmation - Rule 7 mandates the satisfactory completion of one year probation as Deputy Superintendent of Police, as well as three years of satisfactory service before becoming eligible for further promotion to Superintendent of Police.
However, if the conditions of Rules 6(b), 7(b) and (e) and Rule 26 are specifically relaxed with regard to satisfactory completion of probation within the Deputy Superintendent of Police category 2, it cannot later be argued that the relaxation applied only to the initial one-year probation and not the subsequent three-year period.
No specific confirmation order is required after the successful completion of probation, and the officer is considered confirmed and eligible for promotion.
