Under Section 14 of the Administrative Tribunal Act, 1985, and Section 197 of the Railway Act, 1989, the question of regularisation arises for the appellants.
They work in Handicraft Centres under the Railway Department's scheme but were not appointed following established rules or orders.
Although they were given a meagre remuneration per month and commissions based on work done, their appointment order did not provide a pay scale.
The appellants were engaged on a part-time basis and are not railway employees.
As a result, the tribunal lacked jurisdiction to hear their application.
