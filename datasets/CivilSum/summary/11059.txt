Seniority - Seniority is both a tool kit of employment as well as a condition of service.
[Para 8]
According to Article 110-A of the Jammu and Kashmir Civil Service Regulations, promotion should not be denied to an employee due to the actions of the State, particularly if those actions have not been proven.
In this case, the petitioner was denied a promotion due to being suspended, which is covered under Article 110-A [Paras 9, 13, and 14]
Article 110-A of the Jammu and Kashmir Civil Service Regulations stipulates that a suspended employee who is not considered for promotion should be immediately reverted unless there is another vacancy to accommodate them.
However, the employee should still be eligible for increments in higher grade, but no arrears should be allowed.
In this case, the petitioner was entitled to relief as he was not promoted due to his suspension, and the regulations require that such cases be decided accordingly.
The petitioner was directed to be considered for the next higher promotional post if found fit under the rules and be ranked senior to Respondent No. 5 for promotional posts with consequential benefits.
