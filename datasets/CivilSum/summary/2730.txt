Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume 2 Part I Chapter IX, Rule 95 - Arrears of pension with interest - Claim of - The petitioner retired from service on 3132001 and had paid some amount in March 2004 and some during pendency of the petition.
However, an amount of Rs. 17,248 remained unpaid.
It was held that as per the mandate of Rule 95, it was the duty of the respondents to complete the pension case well before retirement.
There is no justification for the release of pension after three years.
According to the law laid down by the Full Bench of this Court in AS Randhawa's case, the petitioner is entitled to interest @ 12% on delayed payment.
