According to Section 2(k) & (j) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1974, the employees of the Managing Body that manages the properties of a Temple and conducts business activities would be considered workmen.
However, it is important to separate essential religious activities related to Pooja and rituals from industrial activities, as only the latter would fall under the purview of the Industrial Disputes Act.
