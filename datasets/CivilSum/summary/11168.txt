Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol. II, Rule 14.30 - Pension/Transfer/Policy decision - The petitioners, who were employees of Punjab Roadways, were transferred to Municipal Corporation, Amritsar in 1980.
In 1983, the Punjab Roadways Department declared pensionary benefits to its employees in a policy decision, but the court quashed the cut-off date of the applicability of the policy.
Consequently, the petitioners became eligible for the pensionary benefits and the court issued directions to release their pension.
