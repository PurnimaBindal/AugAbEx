According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, and Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Volume II, Rule 13.8, Head Constables on List 'C' are entitled to seniority and promotion.
Authorities must ensure that every Head Constable on the list undergoes the intermediate school course in order of seniority determined in accordance with Rule 138 of Punjab Police Rules.
The D.I.G.
Police must determine the seniority of Head Constables according to range, not state-wise basis.
