Constitution of India, Article 14 - Criminal contempt - Right to seek justice - Directing an employee to limit their claim to a certain amount and considering only that is an obstruction of justice and amounts to criminal contempt.
[Para 4]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Discrimination - Promotion - If juniors to the petitioner who were promoted to the higher position are maintained, the petitioner cannot be deprived of the same benefits and is entitled to receive them with arrears for the three years before filing the petition.
