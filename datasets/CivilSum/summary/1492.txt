Constitution of India, Article 16 - Transfer - Mala fides - If a government servant has been transferred due to political pressure exerted by another government servant, and there is no fault or reason for the transfer, approaching a political person for defense is not considered pressure tactics or an exercise of political influence for retaining a place of posting.
However, if two or more government servants fight for a posting and exercise their political influence and only one succeeds, the unsuccessful person cannot take shelter of the discretionary jurisdiction of the High Court.
