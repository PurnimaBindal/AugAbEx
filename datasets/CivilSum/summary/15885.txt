Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 21 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - Punjab Govt. instructions dated 2441989 on compassionate appointments of one of the dependants of deceased Govt. employee or retired on medical ground before attaining the age of 55 years - The purpose of the concession is to help families in financial distress who have lost their bread earner - As the concession results in unequal treatment, it must be strictly adhered to - Only cases with clear evidence of family penury should be considered and the court can intervene with Article 226 - The petitioner's father retired on medical grounds after reaching 55 years of age and was not a government employee at the time of death - Consequently, the petitioner is not entitled to priority appointment under the instructions.
