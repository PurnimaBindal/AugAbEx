The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 state that qualifications are necessary for Promotion, such as the Condition of Middle Pass for the post of Assistant Electrician.
This is to be filled through promotion from the post of unskilled mechanic, but it was never notified.
Instructions were given not to impose the condition until it is notified.
The petitioner's case was wrongly ignored while promoting his juniors having middle passed.
The court directed to consider the petitioner's case from a retrospective date and grant all benefits from that date by giving notional promotion.
[Paras 3 & 4]
