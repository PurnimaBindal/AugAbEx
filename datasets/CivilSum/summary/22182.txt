According to Section 79(C) of the Electricity Supply Act, 1948 and Section 33-C of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, technical staff of PSEB were subject to regulations that governed parity in renumeration for working on weekends.
As a result, they were not permitted to file applications under Section 33-C(2) of the 1947 Act.
The High Court's ruling in this matter was upheld.
