Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection/Interview marks/Promotion by way of selection - Allotting anything more than 12% marks for interview during direct recruitments may be arbitrary.
However, this is not applicable in case of promotions.
The ratio of 1985(3) SLR 200 cannot be taken into consideration when it comes to recruitment by promotion.
Various factors such as experience, improved qualifications, and high qualifications must be considered by authorities while filling up posts in high grades by promotion.
In cases of promotion by selection, the fixation of 15 interview marks out of 75 cannot be deemed arbitrary, primarily when a written test is not provided.
