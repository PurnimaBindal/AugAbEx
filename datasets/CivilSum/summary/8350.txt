Punjab State Electricity Board Employees Punishment and Appeal Regulations, 1971, Regulation 5 - Misconduct - Charge sheeted - Punishment - For minor penalty of stoppage of two annual increments with cumulative effect, employee must be given reasonable opportunity to make representation as per rule - Respondent given opportunity to file representation and see official record for filing reply, complying with Principles of Natural Justice - No further legal requirement necessary in case of minor punishment - Distt.
Judge's decision that plaintiff was not given opportunity to meet the case was in error - Appeal allowed.
