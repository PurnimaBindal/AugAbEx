Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1980, Section 19 - Andhra Bank (Officer's) Service Regulation, 1982, Regulation 19 - Age of Retirement - Petitioner, who was appointed as an officer on 19.12.75, falls under the category of officers who shall retire on completion of 58 years as benefit of age of superannuation is only available to an officer directly recruited prior to 1.7.73.
As a result, the petitioner is not entitled to the benefit of age of superannuation of 60 years.
