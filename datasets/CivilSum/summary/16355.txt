According to the Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 226, eligibility for appointment and selection processes should be taken into account.
In this case, the petitioner obtained their Master's Degree from Utkal University through a correspondence course but was deemed ineligible for the post.
It's important to note that degrees conferred by Utkal University through distant education or correspondence course are not recognized, and the Commission acted justly by considering the petitioner ineligible for the Headmaster position.
