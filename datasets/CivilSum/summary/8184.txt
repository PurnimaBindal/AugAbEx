Consitution of India, Article 311(2) - Jammu and Kashmir Constitution, Section 126(2)(b) - Withholding of the departmental enquiry - It is not enough to simply state that "due to prevailing circumstances in the valley a regular enquiry is dispensed with" to justify the decision - There should be enough evidence and assurance of the authority documented on the file indicating that conducting a regular enquiry was not practically viable - In the absence of such evidence on the file, termination of a State employee's job against the laid down process in Section 126 of the J&K Constitution is completely illegal - The order of termination is nullified and the authority is instructed to conclude the enquiry within six months.
Otherwise the petitioner shall be reinstated.
