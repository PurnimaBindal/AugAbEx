According to Rules 6(3) and 7(d) of the Rajasthan Engineering Subordinate Service (Irrigation Branch) Rules, promotions were given to JE's who held Diploma in Mechanical, whereas the petitioner who only possessed a Diploma in Electrical and worked in Mechanical side for 31 years was not considered for promotion.
In conclusion, just being appointed as JE(Mechanical) does not make a person eligible for promotion if they don't possess the necessary qualifications as required by the rules.
