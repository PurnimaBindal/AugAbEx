Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delhi Police (Punishment and Appeal Rules) 1980, Rule 16(xii) - Removal from service - Natural justice - The Disciplinary Authority must serve on the delinquent its reasons for disagreement along with a copy of the report and a show cause notice, as required by rule 16(xii) of the Rules, if it disagrees with the findings of the Enquiry Officer, before ordering a major punishment such as removal from service.
Failure to do so renders the order passed by the Disciplinary Authority invalid.
In this case, the Petitioner must be reinstated in service without any back wages.
However, the respondents can continue proceedings from the stage of disagreement of the Disciplinary Authority, and pass fresh appropriate orders within four months of satisfying the requirement of rule 16(xii) of Rules.
[Paras 9 and 10]
