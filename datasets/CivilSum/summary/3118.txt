Under Section 124 of the Major Port Trust Act, 1963, and Regulation 24 of the Madras Port Trust Employees (Appointment, Seniority and Promotion) Regulations, a Schedule Caste candidate was the only one eligible for promotion to the post of Assistant Director (P&R).
The necessary experience qualifications were relaxed in the respondent's case as he was the only Schedule Caste candidate, and filling the vacancy was in public interest.
