State and Subordinates Service Rules (Kerala) Part II, Rule 10(aaaa) Explanation (ab) - Constitution of India, Article 16 - Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules (Kerala), 1960, Rules 16(1) and 11 (1)(iii) - Qualifications - Experience - A post requiring a basic qualification of BA and two years of experience allows for eligibility if the petitioner acquires the required qualification after gaining experience.
The contention brought forth by the Public Service Commission, stating that the qualification should be acquired before the experience, is not valid.
