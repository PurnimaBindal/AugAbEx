The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, regarding Regularisation of Services/Recruitment states that employees working on projects of temporary nature cannot be directed to be regularised.
However, the government has directed that the claim of employees working on daily rate basis in different Public Sector Undertakings cannot be overlooked while filling up regular vacancies.
These employees are entitled to be regularised through the State Public Service Commission.

According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, regarding Equal pay for equal work and qualifications, Junior Engineers working on daily rate basis and Degree-holders working on daily rate basis must be paid equally to regular Degree-holders.
Diploma-holders working on daily rate basis are also entitled to the same pay as paid to regular Diploma-holders.
Claims cannot be refused only on the ground that no payment has been made for holidays to daily-rated employees.
