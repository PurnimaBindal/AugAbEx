Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume II, Rule 6.16 - The existing method of calculation of gratuity set out in government notifications can only be applied to those employees who retired after the order came into effect.
Earlier retirees remain governed by old rules.
The increase in consumer's price index level cannot be considered as a change in the method of calculation.
[Para 21-22]

Gratuity is a one-time payment made in recognition of successful services rendered by an employee.
It must be calculated using existing criteria on the date of retirement to avoid anomalous situations.
[Para 10, 23]

Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 616-A(3) - Dearness allowance is not covered under this rule.
Dearness allowance and ad-hoc dearness allowance became payable by incorporating them as part of emoluments in accordance with the Central Government Notification dated 1985, with the prices index level on 3131985 serving as a benchmark.
After that, dearness allowance sanctioned as per price index becomes dearness pay.
[Para 8, 9, 21]

Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 616-A - Executive instructions can complement statutory rules where the field is not covered.
The method of calculating gratuity is not covered by statutory rules, so change can be introduced by executive orders.
Therefore, the changes in the method of calculation of gratuity introduced should not apply to all retirees irrespective of the date of retirement.
[Para 7, 22, 23, 24 and 26]
