Punjab Financial (First Amendment) Rules, Volume II, 1990 Rule II - Pension - Qualifying service - Leave salary - The adoption and subsequent withdrawal of the amendment by the BSEB had no impact on the retirement benefits of the petitioner.
An employee can count previous service rendered in another department of Government, and the earlier department must contribute proportionately towards the employee's pension in their later department of retirement.
