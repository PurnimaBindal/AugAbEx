Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admissions - Disputed question of facts - Waiting list - In cases where both parties give conflicting versions, disputed facts cannot be examined in writ proceedings - However, official records indicating that the waiting list was posted on the notice board cannot be rejected on mere allegations by the petitioner - Such records are presumed to be true - Admission granted to those who deposited the fees within time from the waiting list, upheld.
