The order passed by the competent authority removing the respondent from service is restored as misconduct was satisfactorily proved and the punishment of removal from service was commensurate with the gravity of the proven misconduct.
[Para 10] B The High Court's jurisdiction to interfere with the quantum of punishment is limited and can only be exercised with sufficient reasons.
The punishment imposed by the Disciplinary Authority or the Appellate Authority cannot be subjected to judicial review unless it is shocking to the conscience of the Court.
