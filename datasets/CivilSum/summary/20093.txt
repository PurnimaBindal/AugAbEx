Constitution of India, Articles 162 and 226 - Writ jurisdiction - The appellants are not part of the same cadre or service and are not bound by the rules, meaning they cannot have any resemblance to statutory cadre officers - The decision for any policy lies upon the appropriate government - Tribunal cannot provide policy direction or issue directives on the creation of promotion avenues - Such orders would indirectly interfere with policymaking, which is under the exclusive purview of the appropriate government.
