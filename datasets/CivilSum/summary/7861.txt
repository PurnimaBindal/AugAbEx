Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - Qualifying Examination - Promotion from Non-Executive to Executive cadre - The petitioner was denied promotion despite already passing the qualifying examination in 1987 and being at the top of the promotion panel based on that test - Batchmates of the petitioner had already been promoted to E2 grade - This is a clear indication of a violation of the relevant promotion rules by the respondents - The petitioner is entitled to a legal right of promotion as per the rules and should have been given promotion - The court should intervene under Article 226 of the Constitution of India - A writ of mandamus should be issued upon the respondents ordering them to treat the petitioner as qualified for promotion to E2 grade and grant him promotion with effect from the date his immediate junior was promoted.
