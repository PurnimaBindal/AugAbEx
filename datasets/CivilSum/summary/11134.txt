Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punishment - Natural Justice - Challengers of punishment order passed without furnishing a copy of enquiry report to the delinquent official - Argued for non-compliance with principles of natural Justice, as interpreted by the Supreme Court, such as in Mohd.
Ramzan Khan's case AIR 1991 SC 471 - However, the court ruled that the ratio of Mohd.
Ramzan's case will not apply, as the punishment order was passed before the judgment of the Supreme Court in that case.
