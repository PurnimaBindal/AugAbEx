Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1988, Rule 5(1) - Revision of pay scales - The petitioner is entitled to the option of revised pay scales and may take a delay in exercising that option.
The pay scales of various other employees in similar situations to the petitioner were revised according to the revised pay rules.
The case of the petitioner has found favor at every stage, but the benefits of the revised pay scales were not granted to him until his retirement.
His retirement benefits were also computed in the lower pay scale, resulting in discrimination.
The respondent authorities are directed to release the arrears and retiral benefits that are computed in the revised pay scale to the petitioner within one month.
