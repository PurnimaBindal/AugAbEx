Regulations for Civil Services Vol. II, Schedule B - Transfer Policy of Government as per Circular No. 10 of 1964 dated 3171964 - Challenge to Transfer - Dismissed claims on ground of being contrary to transfer policy of Govt. that mandates tenure of two years - Government instructions are not mandatory, and Government servant can be transferred before the term ends - Also, transfer is an exigency of service, and no one has the right to serve at a particular place.
[Paras 9, 12] B.
Challenge to Transfer - Vague allegations cannot persuade the court to take cognizance - No specific claim of mala fide or ulterior motive - The fact that allegations of mala fide are unrebutted does not hold any weight.
