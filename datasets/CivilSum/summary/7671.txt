Constitution of India, Article 227 - Education - Decrees and Diplomas - The writ court should not attempt to determine the equivalence of degrees and diplomas/certificates issued by different educational institutions/universities - Specialists should determine the equivalence - The institute can set the standard of qualifications for its teachers in the interest of large public and this will not affect the ultimate aim of studies.
[Paras 8, 10 & 14] B All India Council for Technical Education Act, 1987, Section 10 - University Grants Commission Act, 1956, Section 10 - The All India Council for Technical Education has only advisory and recommendatory functions - It does not have the authority to issue any sanction - It can only submit reports to the UGC for appropriate action - However, the UGC may act in consultation with the council.

Constitution of India, Article 14 & 311 - Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology Staff Regulations, Regulations 14(xxvi) and 38(ii) - Termination during probation in terms of contract of service does not attract natural justice if the employee is discharged because they lacked the necessary qualifications without any aspersion or stigma on their career - Article 311 of the Constitution does not protect the employees of a deemed University - If an innocuous order during probation for not holding necessary qualifications terminates their service, it cannot be considered illegal.
[Paras 11, 12 & 14] D Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology Staff Regulations, Regulations 14(xxvi) and 38(ii) - Discharge during probation - If the Chairman and/or Registrar of the University have been delegated the powers of the Board of Governors in specific matters, their actions on behalf of the Board cannot be considered without jurisdiction.
