Central Civil Service (CCA) Rules, 1965, Rule 10(4) - A government servant who was suspended and received a termination order on 10.1.1978 by a simple order had their termination order overturned by a Tribunal.
The Tribunal held that the order was a punishment, which should not have been passed without holding an enquiry.
The government has initiated an enquiry.
According to Rule 10(5), the government servant cannot be suspended with retrospective effect (i.e. 10.1.1978).
The power of suspension with retrospective effect would be available to Authority if the original order of termination was made by way of penalty.
