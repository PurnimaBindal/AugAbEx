Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(g) - Employer - The other firm was registered firm and was working as a contractor for providing unfinished components for the petitioner firm.
- Petitioner had control over the workers' subsistence, skill and continued employment - The Labour Court concluded that the petitioner and the other firm are the same, and the other firm was only a camouflage for the petitioner - The Labour Court's decision was neither perverse nor unreasonable.

Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) & 2(k) - Retrenchment - Industrial dispute - An objection was raised that the workmen who have already received retrenchment compensation cannot claim benefits under the Act - It was held that such objections are merely technical and should not be entertained during industrial adjudication.
