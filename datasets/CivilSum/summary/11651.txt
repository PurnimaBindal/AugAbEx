Enquiry into allegations of theft must be held before terminating an employee, as per Sections 2(oo)(a) and 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act.
Confession of guilt cannot be relied upon without proper proof.
Workmen can claim coercion and threat in cases where their confession was obtained at a police station without proper inquiry.
Mere identification of signatures of the person who recorded the statement does not prove the authenticity of the confession.
The Labour Court's decision to award reinstatement with 50% back wages is upheld.
