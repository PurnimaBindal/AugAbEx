Constitution of India, Article 310 - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rules 38 and 49 - Pension - An employee was denied pensionary benefits for not completing 10 years of qualifying service due to being declared medically unfit for retention in the service of BSF due to HIV.
No steps were taken to provide an alternative job and no minimum qualifying service was prescribed.
The court ordered invalid pension payment from the date of termination with litigation costs of Rs. 5000 for the authority's callous and unsympathetic view.
