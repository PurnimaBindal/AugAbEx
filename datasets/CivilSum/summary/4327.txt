Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Suspension/Retirement - If an employee retires on their superannuation date during a suspension period with no objections, the order of suspension is automatically revoked.
After the employer-employee relationship has ended, the employer cannot issue orders of suspension against the retired employee.
The period of suspension is counted as duty period for all purposes.
An order of reinstatement does not void the order of suspension.
Punjab National Bank Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulation, 1977, Regulation 152.
