New Bank of India (Officers') Service Regulations, 1982, Regulation 7 - Promotion Policy of New Bank of India, Clause 7.1 - Fitment of Assistant Managers in the seniority of Accountants in the same scale in the Junior Management Grade Scale I and inter se seniority based on length of service for further promotion - Policy accounts for service rendered in the erstwhile bank and pay scales of employees - Assistant Managers in the same scale cannot claim a separate and higher cadre - Policy is non-discriminatory and legally sound - Different weightage for various factors considered in laying down the policy - High Court judgment upheld, except for those already promoted based on previous ruling who will remain in the same cadre and seniority.
