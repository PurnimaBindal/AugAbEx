The Payment of Gratuity Act did not apply to the petitioner, who resigned from his post as Probationary Officer after less than ten years of continuous service, and was later appointed directly as Industrial Relation Officer.
As such, he is not entitled to gratuity or provident fund benefits.
[Paras 10, 12 and 18]
The petitioner cannot claim coercion or threat in his resignation after having received benefits from his subsequent higher post and salary for seven years.
This would be considered an approbate - reprobate situation, and the petitioner is estopped from doing so.
[Para 18]
