The petitioner in regards to the Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rules 26 & 30, had their claim for additional benefit of past service denied, although they were granted additional qualifying service under Rule 30.
The petitioner's contention that an amendment in statutory rules cannot alter the terms and conditions of their employment to their prejudice is not tenable, as they retired after the amendment in 1992 and are therefore governed by it.
As a result, the petitioner is not entitled to the additional benefit of past service.
