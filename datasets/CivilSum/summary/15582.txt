According to Rule 532-A of the Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, qualifying service of 30 years or age of 55 years was required for pension on voluntary retirement.
The petitioner completed 30 years in 1975 and attained the age of 55 in 1974 and was allowed pension from January 1974.
The petitioner was permanently absorbed in Punjabi University with effect from December 1, 1968, and could have voluntarily retired on December 1, 1970, having joined service on December 1, 1945.
Accordingly, the petitioner is entitled to pension from a later date as mentioned in Rule Section 32-A from the Punjab Govt. [Paras 3, 8 and 10]
