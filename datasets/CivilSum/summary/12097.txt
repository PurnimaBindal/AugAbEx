Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Section 11(b) - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Rule 2 - Disciplinary proceedings - Right to defend - Natural justice - Petitioner was denied the opportunity to engage a Defence Assistant due to not being a retired Central Govt. servant, despite the allowance of such assistance for another employee from the same organization - This denied the petitioner a reasonable opportunity to defend their case and violated principles of natural justice - The order of dismissal was deemed improper.
