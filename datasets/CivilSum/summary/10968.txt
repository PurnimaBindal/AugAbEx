Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Tamil Nadu Private Colleges (Regulation) Act, 1976, Sections 2(10) and 17 - Retirement age - Fixation of - The petitioner, a librarian in an Aided Educational Institution, was denied the concession to retire at the end of the academic year on their superannuation by the respondents.
Such denial of concession to librarians is discriminatory.
The respondents were not justified in not extending the services of the petitioner till the end of the academic year.
Librarians are included in the definition of teacher, and consequently, they have to be treated as teachers.
As such, they are entitled to be retired at the end of the academic session.
