Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Transfer - Complaints - Transfer on the basis of complaint - An employer has the right to transfer an employee on receipt of a complaint, but if the allegations in the complaint are found to be false, the basis of the transfer is invalidated - If an employee has been transferred based on a complaint made in a fake name and an inquiry determines the complaint to be false, the employee is entitled to be posted back to their original position - The apprehension of the employee about the complaint being made mala fide can be considered valid in such a scenario.
