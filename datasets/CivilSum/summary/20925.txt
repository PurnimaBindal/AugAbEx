The Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16 and 226 and the Govt. of India's Deptt.
of Personnel & Training's Scheme dated 191993, Clauses 3 & 4 outline the scope and applicability of the Scheme - which grants `temporary status' and regularisation of services of casual labourers.
This applies to all casual labourers who have worked for at least 240 days in a year and are entitled to get temporary status.
Clause 4 of the scheme does not entail that an ongoing Scheme would cover all who are engaged, but it is at the discretion of the Union Government to formulate a scheme as and when necessary.
Nonetheless, once temporary status is granted, it cannot be taken away, nor can their services be terminated except under Clause 7 for misconduct.
