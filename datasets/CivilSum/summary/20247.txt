Constitution of India, Articles 141 and 142 - Review - Age of superannuation - The Supreme Court has ruled, in the case reported as 1995 Supp (1) SCC 470, that there is no need for reconsideration - It is appropriate for Class IV employees, whether skilled or semi-skilled, to retire at the age of 60 years, and for those in Class III and above to retire at the age of 58 years under the Rules.
