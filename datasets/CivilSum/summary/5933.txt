The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 Section 11A and Article 226 of India's Constitution pertains to the punishment of individuals for misconduct charges.
Employers are not required to provide additional evidence unless requested to do so by the employee.
The order of removal is considered fair if the fairness and propriety of the domestic inquiry are not questioned before the Labor Court ruling.
In the absence of any evidence, it is not the court's function to invite the employer to provide additional proof of misconduct.
No illegality exists in the Labor Court award, and no action is required of the court or tribunal to demand employers' additional evidence on the question of misconduct.

Even though the misconduct is established, if the tribunal determines that the punishment of dismissal or discharge is unjustified, it can interfere with it.
