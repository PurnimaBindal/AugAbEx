The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) allows workmen to receive disputed benefits.
However, before proceeding to compute benefits in terms of money, the Labour court has to determine whether the workman has the right to receive the benefits.
If the right to receive benefits was not adjudicated, then the Labour court could not pass award under section 33-C(2).
