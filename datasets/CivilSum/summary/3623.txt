The case of M.A.T.
2696 of 2002 involved a situation where the appellants were reverted to their previous posts after a writ was allowed by the High Court, without being given a chance to be heard.
The respondents in the case were represented by Mr. PK Panja, Advocate.
The matter was remanded back to the writ court for rehearing on merit.
