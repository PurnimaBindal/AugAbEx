According to Section 25G of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, retrenchment of junior-most employees who are Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe must be protected.
The government orders dated 2511990 and 381997 provide further protection.
As per the orders, the respondent cannot pass retrenchment orders against the petitioner.
Instead, they must create a supernumerary post and absorb the petitioner, in addition to bearing their costs.
[Paras 3, 5 and 6]
