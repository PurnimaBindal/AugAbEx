Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 - Judgment by Tribunal - Maintainability - If a Tribunal has granted a benefit that a previous Bench had not provided when a similar relief application was evaluated, that decision cannot be reconsidered by a Coordinate Bench of the Tribunal because it is constrained by the earlier ruling - In the case of a disagreement, the only option is to refer the matter to a larger Bench.
The case is remanded to the Tribunal for further review in accordance with this rule.
