Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation/Promotion on direct quota posts - Petitioner is within promotion zone but was not considered for promotion due to lack of dereservation - If a Scheduled Caste candidate is available for promotion on direct quota posts, there is no need for dereservation - The rule of reservation applies to promotions and must not be denied to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes - Posts intended for SC/ST candidates must be filled in the same manner as other posts and cannot be left vacant - Paragraph 9.2 of Chapter 9 of the brochure on Reservation for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes must be applied - No other instruction or order shall override the Presidential Directive.
