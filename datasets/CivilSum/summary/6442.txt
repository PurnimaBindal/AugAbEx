The Draft Rules for promotions under Article 309 of the Constitution of India should not be violated.
Minor breaches of these rules will not result in appointments being nullified if the rules have not been adopted or approved by the appropriate authorities.
[Para 9] B Under Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution, appointments and reservations for ex-servicemen and their dependents should be strictly interpreted.
The reserved category cannot claim the third post unless their reservation status is explicitly confirmed, as reservations are granted at the expense of merit.
Only two out of 15 posts are reserved for ex-serviceman category and must be adhered to.
