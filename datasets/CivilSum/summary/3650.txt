Central Reserve Police Force Rules, Rules 11, 27 - Removal from service - Proportionality of punishment - The petitioner allegedly intentionally concealed material information while applying for employment which would disqualify him from being employed - The court held that unless the punishment was found to be excessive and unreasonable, it should not be interfered with - Furthermore, the court found that the reasons given for the punishment were reasonable and not arbitrary or extraneous.
