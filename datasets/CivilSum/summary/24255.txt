Bihar Civil Services (Executive Branch) and Bihar Civil Services (Recruitment Rules, 1951, Rule 15-A - Examination - Unfair Means - While examining charges of adopting unfair means in Combined Competitive Examination, the Commission has jurisdiction to investigate malpractices and consider explanations provided by the respondents in detail.
The Commission acted in accordance with rules, regulations, and executive instructions, and its decision to cancel the examination of the respondent and debar him was warranted and should not be interfered with.
