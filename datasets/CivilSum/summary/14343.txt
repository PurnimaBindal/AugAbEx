Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab Police Rules, Rule 1226 - Transfer - Permission as to - According to the grievance of the petitioner, the Director General of Police must have given prior approval for transferring him from GRP to Commando Battalion.
However, since GRP and Commando Force constituted a range and a transfer from Railways to Commandos was similar to a transfer from one district to another, no concurrence of DGP was required.
In fact, DGP could be said to have given his tacit and prior approval to all transfers from the GRP to the Commando Battalions.
