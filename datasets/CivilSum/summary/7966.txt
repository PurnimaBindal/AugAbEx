The Central Vigilance Commission has jurisdiction over advising on cases involving Gazetted Officers of Central Government and Board level appointees in Public Sector Undertakings.
If a formal departmental enquiry with an appointed Enquiry Officer is instituted, the report and all documents must be sent to the Commission for their advice to the Disciplinary Authority.
The Vigilance Manual applies to employees of Public Enterprises or Boards.
[Paras 14, 16 and 21 to 23]
Show cause notice given after enquiry expresses a tentative opinion and is not final under Constitution of India Articles 14 and 311.
[Paras 24 and 25]
While suggestions from the Vigilance Commission are not binding on competent authorities, they must be referred to the Commission for reconsideration before proceeding with a course of action.
[Para 24]
The competent authority may correct a mistake by withdrawing a show cause notice proposing a major penalty without seeking prior advice from the Vigilance Commission.
[Para 25]
A delay in issuing charges after two years of events cannot be the sole reason for quashing proceedings, as the issue remains under review by the Competent Authority and the Vigilance Commission.
[Para 26]
When disciplinary proceedings are pending against an officer being considered for an appointment to manage a company, the Commission's advice must be taken into consideration.
