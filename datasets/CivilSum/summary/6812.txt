The Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I Part I, Rules 8.116 and 8117 - Leave encashment - Half pay leave - It is not possible to encash half pay leave if it is due at the time of retirement, only full pay earned leave can be encashed.
