Constitution of India, Articles 233 and 237 - District Judge - Additional District Judge - Judicial Magistrate - Members of UP Civil Service (Judicial Branch) - The Governor may exercise his powers under Article 237 of the Constitution to include any judicial service as a Magistrate for the purpose of Article 233, and limit their promotional avenues to the post of Additional District Judge only.
The appointment of District Judge is recognized by the Constitution on the basis of experience in Criminal and Civil law as they deal with the liberty and property of individuals in Criminal Courts, and should not be confused with that of Additional District Judge.
[Paras 11 and 12]

Constitution of India, Articles 233 and 237 - Magistrates - According to the Constitution, a parallel judicial service may be created to include magistrates of any kind.
The Magistracy performing judicial functions when trying offences under the Indian Penal Code and other Statutes are recognized by the Constitution.
However, the promotional avenues of Magistrates are only available up to the Additional Sessions Judge.
[Para 14]
