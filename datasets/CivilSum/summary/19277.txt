Constitution of India, 1950, Article 16 - Evidence Act, 1872, Section 115 - Transfer/Estoppel - The principle of promissory estoppel cannot be used by workmen to avoid impending transfer – If employees have been treated unfairly, they may express their dissatisfaction by making a representation, but it cannot be considered as a means to pressure management into overturning its retention decision.
