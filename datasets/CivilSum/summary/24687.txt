Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 9 - UP Public Services (Tribunal) Act, 1976, Section 3 - Regional Rural Banks Act, 1973, Section 3 - Jurisdiction - An employee of Prathama Bank, which is sponsored by Syndicate Bank, does not fall under the definition of public servant as used in the UP Act.
Therefore, Civil Court has the competence to try the suit.
