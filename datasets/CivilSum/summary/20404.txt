Tamil Nadu Civil Services (Disciplinary Proceedings Tribunal) Rules, 155, Rule 8 and Tamil Nadu Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, Rule 17 - Disciplinary Proceedings/Enquiry - Special Rules framed to conduct disciplinary proceedings will override the general Rule 17 of Civil Service Rules.
[Para 6] B Tamil Nadu Civil Services (Disciplinary Proceedings) Rules, 1955 - Natural justice - Preliminary enquiry - There is no requirement for a preliminary enquiry before holding a regular departmental enquiry.
Notice and opportunity is not necessary to be given before holding a regular departmental enquiry or to take a decision to hold such enquiry.
