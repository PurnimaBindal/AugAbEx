Constitution of India, Article 226 - Common order of transfer - Joint petition - Maintainability - Holding that entertaining a joint petition is not universal and must be confined to the facts and circumstances of the individual case only - Although a common order of transfer is based on a single order of government, the relief sought by each petitioner cannot be said to be based on the same cause of action, as each individual's posting is fundamentally different - Therefore, a joint petition cannot be entertained.
[Paras 13, 16 and 17]
