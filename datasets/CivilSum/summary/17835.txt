Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 311 - Retirement - Adverse remarks - Annual confidential report - Representation - After issuing notice of retirement to an employee who has received adverse remarks on their annual confidential report and has submitted a representation, the government can reconsider the matter and pass a fresh order for retirement - Even if the government has previously allowed an employee to continue beyond the age of 55 years, they still have the right to review and retire them before the age of 58 years.

Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 311 - Adverse remarks - Representation - Govt. instructions - Although government instructions may provide that representations against adverse remarks be disposed of within three months, there is no rule or law that states a representation not decided within this timeframe would be deemed accepted - Therefore, an employee has no vested right if their representation is not decided within three months.
