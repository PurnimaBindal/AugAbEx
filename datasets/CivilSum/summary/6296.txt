Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 states that if a demand notice is issued more than three years after termination, the claim may be considered time barred.
Even if the delay is not taken into account, the award of the labour court may still decline relief if the dispute has become too old.
B.
According to the High Court's observations in CWP No. 9580 of 1999, if no plea of delay is taken, then there is no consideration of time limitations.
However, if a plea of delay is taken, then the dispute may be considered stale if too much time has passed.
