Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Termination - Qualifications - A General Teacher was terminated for lack of required qualifications despite holding only a Trade Certificate, not considered equivalent to Basic School Training Course.
Termination based on a circular issued after a Supreme Court ruling, which allowed petitioners in similar cases to acquire necessary qualifications within three years.
Since the petitioner was no longer employed, no reinstatement orders were given; however, the respondents were instructed to reserve a post for the petitioner and enroll him in the course necessary to obtain the required qualifications.
