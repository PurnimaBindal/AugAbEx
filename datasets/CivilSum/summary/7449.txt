According to Section 56 of the Evidence Act, 1872 and Rule 16.1(iv) of the CBSE Examination Bye-laws, parentage is typically determined by the Board of Education's school record.
However, if there is a final decree of declaration regarding a student's parentage, the Board cannot disregard it or challenge the Civil Court's findings.
As such, the Board must respect the court's decision and update its records accordingly.
