According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, adhoc lecturers in Government colleges should be paid for their work during the summer vacation period.
Despite working on an adhoc basis as lecturers from 1976 to 1982, the petitioners were not paid for this period.
However, they were paid for summer vacation when they continued to serve on an adhoc basis after 1982.
Based on the Supreme Court's decision in Ram Avtar's case and the dismissal of the government's review application, the petitioners are entitled to payment for the 1976-1982 summer vacation period.
The government has been ordered to make this payment within four months.
