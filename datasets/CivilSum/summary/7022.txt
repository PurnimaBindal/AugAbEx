Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India - Selection - Reservation in appointments - The application date for an O.B.C.
certificate is not material as long as it is submitted at the proper time and stage of the selection process - Rejecting candidature on the ground of applying for an O.B.C.
certificate after the prescribed cut off date, which was later extended by the authority, is unjust and irrational.
