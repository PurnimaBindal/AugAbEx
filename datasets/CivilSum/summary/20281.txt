Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - Admission to Medical Course - Petitioner, who was included in the category reserved for bona fide residents of Punjab, appeared for Punjab Medical Entrance Test 2000 (PMET-2000) and was made to give an undertaking not to seek admission against the remaining 85% quota in any other States.
Petitioners secured a position in the merit list for getting admission, but after the declaration of the result, eligibility criteria changed.
Petitioners who had given undertaking not to seek admission under 85% quota in any other State were not able to find a place in medical colleges in Punjab or appear for entrance exams in any other State.
As a result, the court issued a show-cause notice to the State asking why relief should not be granted to the appellants.
