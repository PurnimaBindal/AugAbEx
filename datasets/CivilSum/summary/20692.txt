Constitution of India, Articles 136 and 226 - Promotion - Judicial Review - The High Court was not justified in directing the promotion of Respondent No. 1 to the post of Assistant Divisional Manager/Senior Branch Manager - This direction could not be given while exercising the power of judicial review - The appropriate direction should be given to the appellant based on the case of Respondent No. 1 and his promotion from the date his juniors were promoted - The direction of the learned single Judge was modified accordingly.
