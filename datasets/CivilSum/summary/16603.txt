According to the UT Chandigarh Employees Rules, the conditions of service for those in the corresponding posts of the Punjab Civil Service shall be governed by the same set of rules and orders that are currently applicable to the latter category.
Since the revised payscales adopted by the Chandigarh Administration on 101291 were overtaken by a subsequent notification, Superintendents Grade II were entitled to the benefit of the 1992 notification when their office was designated as the office of the Head of Department.
They were also entitled to the further pay revision in 1998, as per the 1998 notification.
