According to Circular No. 10/7/88/88- EPI/8014 dated 3081988 issued by the Department of Finance, Government of Punjab, the benefit of house rent allowance cannot be reduced by the State Government on account of any refixation of pay in the revised pay-scales.
The decision in Civil Appeal "Mohinder Singh and others v. State of Punjab" dated 21.4.1995 and CWP No. 10243 of 1998 "Hem Raj Gupta and 47 others v. State of Punjab and others" are binding and must be followed.
Additionally, the High Court cannot take up the same matter for reconsideration.
