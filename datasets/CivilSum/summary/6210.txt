The petitioner requested a writ of mandamus from the Constitution of India, Articles 226/227, to direct the respondents to allow her to be placed in compartment for English paper and provisionally admitted to IXth Class to avoid losing her academic year.
The Middle Standard Examination has a pass percentage of 33% for ordinary school students in all subjects instead of 25%, and these students can receive grace marks up to 15 to help them pass.
Students under the Model School Scheme can receive 1% grace marks.
There are no supplementary examinations for MSE, and students are either declared pass or fail.
The Registrar conveyed this decision to the DEO, Chandigarh Administration, and all Heads of Institutions in Chandigarh.
The petitioner knew that there would be no supplementary examination.
Therefore, she is not entitled to a compartment in the English paper.
The petition was dismissed.
[Para 7] B The Middle Standard Examination has no supplementary examinations, and students either pass or fail.
Experts in the Education Department will determine which policy will benefit the students more and apply it to all students.
The petitioner lost a year of studies because she was ill, but even otherwise, she might have passed in the English subject like she did in the other subjects.
Making an exception for her is not possible because doing so will set a precedent that contradicts the decision not to have supplementary examinations.
Therefore, the petition was dismissed.
