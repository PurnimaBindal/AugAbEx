Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Pay - Parity of pay - Tailor Masters working in Haryana Armed Police seeking parity in pay with those in other Departments of Haryana - Parity in pay limited to employees with same rank, functions, duties, and responsibilities - Petitioners primarily responsible for repairs while Tailor Masters in other departments stitched new clothes - Petitioners enjoying facilities not provided to counterparts in other departments - Petitioners constitute a different class from those working as Tailor Masters in other departments - Petitioners not entitled to parity in pay - Petition dismissed.
[Paras 10 to 13]
