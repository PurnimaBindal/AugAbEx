According to Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions Act, 1989, Section 18 Proviso (iii), the Removal from Service requires Approval of Director, and that approval must be granted in writing.
The Director cannot delegate their role, and there can be no deemed approval if the Director fails to give an order within the specified period.
Thus Termination orders passed by management cannot operate by deemed approval until the written approval of Director is received.
[Para 7]
In Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions Act, 1989, Section 18 Proviso (iii), the term "unanimous" mandates collective opinion of managing committee.
Thus the Secretary of the Committee's opinion alone cannot be substituted for the collective opinion of the Committee, and the Committee cannot delegate its power to the Secretary.
Hence, the order of removal passed solely on the opinion of Secretary is invalid.
[Paras 8 and 12]
Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions Act, 1989, Section 18 Proviso (iii)  violates Constitution of India, Article 14.
According to this Article, no one should be deprived of their right without giving them a fair opportunity to present their case.
Punitive order of termination is also not justified unless due decision-making process was followed.
