Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-F - Retrenchment - Compensation - Reinstatement - If a workman has obtained better employment after termination, they may not be reinstated with back wages, as stated in the Labour Court.
Only retrenchment compensation would be appropriate.
[Para 3]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay - The petitioner is barred from seeking relief in writ jurisdiction due to delay.
The petitioner was already employed in a better position and suspended after the award of the Labour Court, and the award was challenged after the suspension.
The court presumes that the workman agreed with the award, but filed the writ petition without good intent, thus, the petition is liable to be dismissed.
