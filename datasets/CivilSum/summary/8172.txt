Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab State Electricity Board Technical Service Class-III Rules, 1996, Rule 9 - Experience - Qualifications - Promotion from Plant Attendant to Auxillary Plant Attendant - If the rule does not specify the requirement for qualified experience, past experience on the post before acquiring prescribed qualifications must be considered for promotion to the higher post - Interpretation of the rules cannot go against clear language and no instructions can override the plain language of the rules - Eligibility for promotion includes non-ITI and non-matriculate Junior Plant Attendants who have six years of experience - Taking into consideration experience of direct recruits as Plant Attendant obtained prior to obtaining ITI qualifications is legal - Entire experience as Plant Attendants, no matter when it was acquired, will be taken into account for eligibility.
