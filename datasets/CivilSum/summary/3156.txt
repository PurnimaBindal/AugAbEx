The petitioner, who was an M.Sc in Computer Science, sought a job in the Transport Department on compassionate grounds following the death of his father, who was a Conductor in Haryana Roadways.
The government appointed him to a clerical post instead of a more suitable position based on his qualifications.
It was determined that heirs or wards of deceased employees do not have an automatic right to such appointments, and that the state is justified in having policies to provide for compassionate appointments.
Additionally, the petitioner's claim of discrimination was dismissed because he accepted the appointment without protest for a year before raising concerns.
The petition was dismissed.
