Under Section 6-A(2) of the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Clause 2(v), (vi), (xv) of the Employees Pension Scheme, 1995 - the benefit of the scheme cannot be denied to employees who are eligible to join it but not members of the Family Pension Fund and have already received pensionary benefits - it is not a ground for denial.
Clause 2 of the scheme deems eligible employees as members, and the petitioners are members of the Provident Fund Scheme by virtue of Clause 2, thus entitled to the benefit.
[Paras 14 and 15].
B.
The retroactive membership option given to employees under Section 6-A(2) of the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Clause 2(v), (vi), (xv) of the Employees Pension Scheme, 1995 means the retrospective date will be taken as the cut-off date and therefore, the benefit of the scheme will not be denied.
[Para 13].
