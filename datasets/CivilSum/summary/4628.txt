According to Karnataka Civil Service Rules, Rule 247(2) and the Karnataka Legal Aid Board Act, 1918, Section 13, deputationists in the Statutory Board of the State Government are entitled to count their service there towards their qualifying service for calculating their retiral benefits, including those who were permanently absorbed by the board and whose lien was not in the Government Department.
Since they never resigned from Government employment and were obliged to go on deputation, the responsibility for their retiral benefits lies with their parent department, who should arrange for contributions from the borrowing department.
Arrears should also be paid with 12% interest, with a total cost of Rs. 2,000.
