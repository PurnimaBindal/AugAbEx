Candidates who were on the waiting list for the post of Mandi Supervisors, with vacancies available and a waiting list validity period of one year, were entitled to be offered those vacancies after candidates on the main select list.
Those who were not on either list have been offered appointments, which violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
The respondents have been directed to offer appointment to petitioners as Mandi Supervisors against posts that were ordered to be kept reserved.
[Paras 6, 10, 12, and 14] B.
The respondent-Board has no justification to deny equal and fair treatment to petitioners who are better placed than those who were not on any list.
The Board cannot perpetuate illegality and cannot deprive rightful claims of persons who are better placed.
The denial of appointment to petitioners is not legally sustainable.
