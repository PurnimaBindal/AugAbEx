Constitution of India, Article 311 - Dismissal from service - The appellant was accused of losing his service revolver - The appellant defended himself by stating that he shared a room with two other colleagues and the revolver along with six cartridges were lost and unaccounted for - It is possible that the explanation is true considering the circumstances - Due to the appellant's history of unblemished service, dismissal from service is too harsh of a punishment - Authorities must change the penalty to compulsory retirement so that the appellant is eligible for retiral benefits.
