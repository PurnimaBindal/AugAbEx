According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution, employees who are absent from duty or overstay their leave may be subject to a presumption of abandonment.
The Bipartite Settlement between the Bank and its employees stipulates automatic retirement for employees who are absent from duty for 90 or more consecutive days beyond the leave granted without appropriate intimation to the management.
Although the Rule provides an opportunity for employees to explain their absence, if no explanation is given, the principles of natural justice cannot be examined in the absence of the employee.
The High Court was incorrect in interfering with the Bank's action, which was strictly in accordance with the agreement between the employees and the Bank and did not violate the principles of natural justice.
