University of Ajmer Act, 1987, Section 6 - Affiliation of colleges - All colleges specified in the University of Ajmer Act, 1987 and those within its jurisdiction will be affiliated to Ajmer University on the Act being passed, excluding those affiliated with University of Rajasthan, University of Jodhpur and Mohan Lal Sukhadia.
[Para 12]
Rajasthan Rules for Payment of Grant-in-Aid Rules, 1963, Rule 3 (16) - Age of superannuation for the teachers - Teachers of affiliated colleges cannot force the government to retain them beyond 58 years of age, as per the Rajasthan Rules for Payment of Grant-in-Aid Rules, 1963, Rule 3 (16) and the discretion of the Government for a possible extension after 58 years of age.
Teachers will be retained by the colleges themselves till the age of 60 due to contractual obligations.
[Paras 17, 19, 20 and 23]
Rajasthan Rules for Payment of Grant-in-aid Rules, 1963, Rule 3 (16) - Rajasthan Government Ordinance 67-A - The provisions of affiliation under University of Ajmer Act, 1987 apply to colleges that are not maintained by the Government and only receive 80% of maintenance expenditure reimbursement from the State.
Ordinance 67-A will not apply to such colleges.
[Para 22]
