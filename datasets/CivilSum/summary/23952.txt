The High Court declared that allocating 20% seats to Management for the admission of students to be against Articles 15(4) and 16(4) of the Constitution as it was favouritism towards Management and ordered the refund of fee of the students.
The Apex Court put on hold the directive regarding refund of fee as the students kept on pursuing the course and completed it.
Both parties came to an agreement to dismiss the directive by the High Court pertaining to the refund of fees to the students.
