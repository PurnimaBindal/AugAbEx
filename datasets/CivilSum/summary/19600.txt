Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Section 3 - Premature retirement - Promotion - Adverse report - The petitioner was able to cross the efficiency bar and receive ad hoc promotions despite having an adverse report - Following his promotion, there were no adverse reports on his service record - It is well-established in the law that adverse reports prior to promotion, especially when it is based on merit and not seniority alone, lose their significance - The promotion nullifies the impact of negative remarks against the government employee - The order of premature retirement is overturned.
[Paras 5-8]
