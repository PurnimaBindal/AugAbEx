Sections 6-A and 16 of the Employees Provident Fund & Misc.
Provisions Act, 1952 - An Electricity Board created a scheme to provide family pension benefits - Initially, the pension was meant to be given from March 1st, 1971, the date on which section 6-A was inserted - The Board later attempted to establish a cutoff date - However, this date was deemed to be bad due to various factors such as it being fixed after the creation of the scheme, having no rational with the purpose sought to be achieved, and the contribution required from employees being minimal and covered by the Board.
Consequently, the cutoff date was held to be invalid.
Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India - Pension - Pension is not a charitable benefit, it is given in exchange for work done - The introduction of a cutoff date for granting family pension to employees who joined after a specific date was deemed to be without any rationale and not connected with the intended objective - All employees were deemed to be eligible for family pension benefits.
