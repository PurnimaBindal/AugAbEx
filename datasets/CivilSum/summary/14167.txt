The Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Section 11 and the Road Transport Corporation Act, Section 45 discuss dismissal related to unauthorised employee absence.
While the employee was not a habitual absentee and provided a valid excuse of illness, the dismissal was deemed harsh and disproportionate.
The Labour Court decided that reinstatement was justified, but due to the serious consequences of unsanctioned leave, back wages were denied.
