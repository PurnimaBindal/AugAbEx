According to the Constitution of India's Article 311, as well as the Electricity Supply Act of 1948's Section 79, and the Classification and Recruitment Regulations of Maharashtra State Electricity Board of 1961's Regulation 16, if an employee is found guilty of misconduct or falsifying information regarding their caste in order to secure employment, their position may be terminated as a result of disciplinary proceedings.
A petitioner in this case was terminated for such reasons, and the termination order was deemed legal.
