Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Appointment - Seniority - Selection - Direct recruitment - A writ petition challenge was made against selection for direct recruitment - During the pendency of the petition, appointed candidates could not take their positions - They were not added as parties to the writ petition - Appointments were granted after the dismissal of the petition -The seniority of the candidates must be determined from the day they joined, rather than the day they were selected.
