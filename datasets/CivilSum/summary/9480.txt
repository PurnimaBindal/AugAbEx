Rule 8122 and 8.21A of Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part I pertaining to voluntary retirement and pensionary benefits were subject to a delay in payment.
An appellant's claim for payment was limited in an execution petition to leave encashment for 180 days alone, resulting in the dismissal of the execution application by the executing court due to Rule 8.21A being of no aid.
As the case fell within the scope of Rule 8122, the high court stated that the executing court's decision could not be challenged.
