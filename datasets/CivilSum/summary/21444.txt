Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation - Linked cases - A peon filed a writ petition for service regularisation, and his case was connected with a service appeal of Registration Clerks, resulting in the dismissal of his petition with theirs - The High Court's order was overturned, and the case was sent back to be judged on its own merit.
