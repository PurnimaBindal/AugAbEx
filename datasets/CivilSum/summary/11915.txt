After successfully completing the Common Entrance Test and Interview, the petitioner was ranked 19th in the sports category, with two candidates ahead of him giving up their admission rights, entitling the petitioner to the next available seat.
This decision is in accordance with Article 226 of the Indian Constitution.

As per the course structure, a candidate is considered to have passed the first year if they clear both the first and second semesters.
There is no regulation that mandates a candidate to clear all papers of the first semester before being promoted to the second semester.
Since a candidate higher in merit than the petitioner vacated a seat, the college authorities are obligated to offer the seat to the petitioner.
As a result, the petitioner should be admitted to the BE Course session of 1992-93's second semester, with the assumption that they failed in all the papers of the first semester, which is valid as per Article 226 of the Indian Constitution.
