Jammu and Kashmir Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 24 requires passing a departmental examination within three chances for promotion to Class I and II posts such as Senior Compilers that involve qualifications based on seniority commencement date.
Those passing the exam only in subsequent chances will not be granted seniority above those who passed in earlier chances.
