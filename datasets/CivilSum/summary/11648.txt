Haryana Co-operative Societies Act, 1984, Section 114 - Primary Cooperative Credit and Service Societies Staff Service Rules, 1992 - Appointment - Post of Clerk - A challenge was made before Deputy Registrar regarding the appointment of the petitioner to the post of Clerk.
The argument was that the society was running at a loss and appointing the petitioner was not in the financial interest of the society.
Additionally, the agenda for the meeting in which petitioner was appointed was not sent through registered post as required by the rules but by UPC repelled.
However, Additional grounds taken in Revision before Government and petitioner's appointment was cancelled absolutely on additional and new grounds, i.e. the petitioner was overage at the time of appointment.
It was held that as these questions were not raised before the Deputy Registrar where the evidence was led, the petitioner was not granted any opportunity to prove that he was eligible.
This has caused prejudice to the petitioner.
It has been categorically recorded that the petitioner fulfills all the requisite qualifications for appointment to the post of a clerk.
Therefore, the appointment of the petitioner could have not been set aside on the ground that he was overage, which was raised for the first time in the revision petition and a finding was recorded without permitting the petitioner to prove his eligibility at the time of evidence led before the Deputy Registrar.
The respondents are directed to take the petitioner back into service with all consequential benefits.
[Paras 24 and 25]
