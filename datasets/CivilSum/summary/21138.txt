Fundamental Rules, Rule 22-C - Stepping up of pay - To remove anomalies caused by the application of FR 22-C, pay in the identical pay scale in the higher post is fixed for seniors who, on promotion, draw less pay than their juniors - The petitioner opted for an ex-cadre post of UDC Cashier and was to be promoted to the post of UDC Incharge and then as Head Clerk - However, the petitioner never worked in these positions and the pay scale for UDC Cashier was entirely different - As a result, the petitioner is not eligible for the benefit of FR 22-C. [Paras 8 to 10]
