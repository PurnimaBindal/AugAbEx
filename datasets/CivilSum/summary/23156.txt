Constitution of India, Article 16 - Intermediate Education Act, 1921, Section 16-G - Appointment - Headmistress of a Junior Model Government Aided School upgraded to a Higher Secondary School was selected as Principal without fulfilling the qualifications required for the position - Competent authority did not approve her appointment and did not relax the qualifications - It was held that since her appointment was not approved, it was not necessary for the Managing Committee of the School to seek approval for the termination of her services as Principal.
