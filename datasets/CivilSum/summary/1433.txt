Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 311 UP Recruitment of Services (Determination of Date of Birth) Rules, 1974, Rule 1 (as amended in 1980) - Evidence Act, Section 3 - Date of Birth - The date given in the High School Certificate must be corroborated by the service book and other documents.
Despite a character roll containing a different date and the employee being only 16 years of age at the time of joining the service, the date given in the High School certificate could not be doubted or disbelieved.
