The Tamil Nadu Minority Schools (Recognition and Payment of Grants) Rules, 1977 lay down impugned conditions for recognition, which the High Court found did not infract Articles 14 and 30(i) of the Constitution.
The High Court correctly held that institutions operating on temporary recognitions must comply with recognition rules to earn recognition.
[Paras 6, 9, 11 and 13]
While Article 30(1) of the Constitution declares the right of minority educational institutions as absolute, it is subject to regulatory measures.
[Para 6]
Many institutions claiming to be minority institutions under Article 30(1) of the Constitution apply to the High Court or Supreme Court for a writ of mandamus to recognize their institutions.
Courts are hesitant to pass much interim relief, including allowing students of unrecognised institutions to appear at examinations or issuing certificates to such students who have passed examinations.
