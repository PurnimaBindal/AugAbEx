Constitution of India, Article 21 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10, 33-C(2) - Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, Section 10, Clause 28(c)(5) - Right of workman to receive subsistence allowance during enquiry - Adjudication by labour Court - Holding, the employer was harsh on the employee by not paying subsistence allowance, which resulted in the employee being unable to participate in the enquiry and ultimately resulting in his dismissal - Section 10(2) of the 1946 Act guarantees an employee the duty to pay subsistence allowance pending enquiry by the employer.
The dispute over subsistence allowance is required to be adjudicated upon by the Labour Court reading Section 33C(2) in light of Section10A(2) of the 1946 Act and no reference according to Section 10 of the 1947 Act is required - Therefore, the jurisdiction exercised by the Labour Court in issuing a payment award for a subsistence allowance cannot be faulted.
