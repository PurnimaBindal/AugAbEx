Constitution of India, Article 14 and 226 - Indian Air Force Services - Promotion - Adverse remarks - The Selection Board in 1987 postponed the case of promotion due to non availability of other report and considered various other aspects also - Adverse remarks in the appraisal report of 1986 expunged in 1989 - The Selection Board in 1989 promoted the petitioner - High Court held that petitioner cannot claim promotion from 1987 merely on the ground that adverse remarks have been expunged, as the Board had considered various other aspects.
[Paras 10 and 11]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Indian Air Force Services - Extension in Service beyond the date of retirement - Adverse remarks in the appraisal report of 1986 expunged in 1989 - Still in the report of 1987 the petitioner was graded below minimum standard fixed for grant of extension to such officers - High Court ruled that it cannot exercise the power of judicial review to assess and appraise the merit of grading of an officer - Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to extension in service beyond the date of retirement.
