All India Institute of Medical Sciences Regulations, 1999, Regulation 35 - Selection - Appointment - Selection and appointment to the post of Professor Anatomy must be made by the Standing Selection Committee that consists of professionals, including experts of the subject, after considering all competing claims.
The petitioner's name was not even recommended by the committee, and the selected candidate was found to be much more meritorious than the petitioner.
As such, the petitioner cannot be granted relief, and the court cannot issue a direction to appoint an unselected candidate, even if some faults are found in the selection process.
[Para 11]
Central Civil Services Joining Rules, 1979, Rules 1 and 4 - Joining time - Scope and applicability - These Rules apply to transfers made after their coming into force.
They do not apply to appointments made through selection.
[Para 12]
In case the competent authority extends an appointee's joining period in the public interest according to the rules and instructions, and the candidate joins within the extended time for joining, the candidate's seniority must be fixed without any depression.
[Para 12]
The President of the Institute sanctioned a two-year-long leave from the post of public importance, i.e., Professor Anatomy in the All India Institute of Medical Sciences, but it was found that such a decision must be taken by the Governing Body of the Institute or at least placed before it for approval.
This is because the interest of the Institute is likely to suffer since it cannot be deprived of services for such a critical post for an extended period.
