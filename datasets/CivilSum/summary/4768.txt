Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 16 - Appointment - Challenge - The petitioner cannot claim estoppel by acquiescence as neither the method of selection nor constitution of Selection Committee is challenged.
The plea that the Selection Committee didn't follow the laid criteria also cannot be considered under the doctrine of estoppel by conduct or acquiescence.

Manager (Finance) with all monetary benefits and seniority above respondents.
