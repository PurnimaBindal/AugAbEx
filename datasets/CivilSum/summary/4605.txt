Constitution of India, Articles 12, 14, 39(a) and 41 - Termination by providing one month notice or notice compensation - Any regulation allowing the 'State' to terminate services of its permanent employees by giving notice or notice compensation is against public policy and violates Article 14 and directive principles of the State - Being a "permanent" employee guarantees security of tenure.
[Paras 9, 11 and 12] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - 'State' - State Corporations - The Punjab Spinning & Weaving Mills Ltd. qualifies as a 'State' under Article 12 of the Constitution - A Govt. agency vested with statutory duties and functions where the terms of service of its employees are governed by Statutory Rules, writ petition can be filed against violation of Rules and Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - Mandamus won't hold against a private body with no public obligation if there is no similarly convenient remedy available - The shape of the body is irrelevant, what is crucial is the nature of duty imposed on it.
