The Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 14(4) (a) and 14A(4)(c) - Joining - A petitioner who previously held a local rank as a Commandant and was later promoted to and continues to serve as a Commandant does not need to give joining again and must be treated as having joined regularly from the date of promotion on the same post, thereby entitled to all the benefits on a regular basis.
[Para 17]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39(d) - Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 14(4)(a) and 14A(a)(c) - Fundamental Rule 17(1) - On the principle of equal pay for equal work, a petitioner who was already working under local rank as a Commandant and was later promoted as a Commandant, discharging all the duties and responsibilities of a Commandant under local rank and vested with the same powers, must not be treated differently from an officer holding a regular rank.
The State can only make classifications based on qualifications, duties, responsibilities, etc, when there is a reasonable nexus with the object sought to be achieved.
The Principle of equal pay for equal work would not apply unless there was no reasonable basis for the unequal classification.
In such cases, the petitioner should be entitled to all the benefits of the regular rank of a Commandant from the time he was holding the post under a local rank.
[Paras 18 and 21]
