Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 21 - Rajasthan (Regulation of Appointments to Public Service and Rationalisation of Staff) Act, 1999, Sections 9, 11 and 19 - The Act aims to restrict illegal appointments to government jobs and prohibits the regularisation of daily wage employees.
The provisions of the Act are repugnant to the Industrial Disputes Act and violate Articles 14, 16 and 21 of the Constitution.
The Act also violates the right to livelihood and the right to remedy in law against illegal and arbitrary refusal to regularise and consequent termination, and it nullifies the finality of judgments beyond the domain of legislation.
