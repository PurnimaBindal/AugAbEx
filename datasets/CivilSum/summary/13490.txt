According to Sections 12 and 2-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, any dispute regarding the dismissal of a workman is considered an industrial dispute regardless of whether or not the other workmen or union parties are involved in the dispute.
The Conciliation Officer must perform his duties upon being notified of such a dispute and report the proceedings with the outcome to the appropriate Government.
There are no time limitations provided by either Section 2-A or Section 12 of the ID Act for the Conciliation Officer to close proceedings due to delay or laches.
In this case, the workman presented a demand notice after having exhausted the remedy of appeal and representation against the order of dismissal, and the Conciliation Officer had no grounds to declare the claim as being belated.
