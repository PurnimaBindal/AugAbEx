The termination order in relation to embezzlement, based on the report of two Inspectors, carries the implication of punishment and casts a stigma, therefore attracting Article 311 of the Constitution.
[Para 5]
Termination order based on allegations of misconduct in the embezzlement investigation, without proper enquiry and denial of reasonable opportunity for defense, violates Article 311(2) of the Constitution.
