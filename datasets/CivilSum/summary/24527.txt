In cases of illegal inquiry, under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and the UP Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, permission to justify punishment must be given without the requirement of pleading or prayer, as long as the tribunal declares the inquiry illegal.
The award will not be vitiated for this reason.
[Para 8] B As the provisions in the UP Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and the Central Act are the same, judgments under both acts are applicable interchangeably.
[Paras 6 and 8] C. When a dismissal is found to be illegal under Sections 33 and 33A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and Section 6F of the UP Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the workman is entitled to full back wages from the date of dismissal.
The date of dismissal cannot relate back to the date of the employer's illegal order.
