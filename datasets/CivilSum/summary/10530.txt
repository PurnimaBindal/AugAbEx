Constitution of India, Article 16 - In the event of the death of a Tubewell Corporation employee, their dependents may be offered employment on compassionate grounds.
The corporation follows the administrative ex gratia scheme of the state government.
The scheme confers legal rights upon the dependents of the deceased employee.

Constitution of India, Article 16 - In the case of the death of a Tubewell Corporation employee, their dependents have the right to seek employment on compassionate grounds.
The corporation must offer a suitable position that matches their qualifications.
The corporation is required to issue an appointment order within six weeks as per AIR 1991 SC 459.
