Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Education - Grant of scholarship - Denial of Merit Scholarship based on non-residence in Punjab - Students who studied in the institutions affiliated with the Punjab School Education Board at Chandigarh cannot be deemed non-residents of Punjab - The Punjab Education Board's affiliated institutions in Chandigarh are considered part of Punjab - The decision to refuse the grant of merit Scholarship on the basis that the student attended institutions in Chandigarh and his or her parents were not Punjab Government Employees and did not live in Chandigarh has no reasonable connection to the aim of awarding scholarships - The purpose of the Punjab State Merit Scholarship is to recognize the achievements of students who pursue higher education in Punjab institutions and encourage them to pursue their studies vigorously - This distinction between equally situated individuals is unfair and violates Article 14 of the Constitution and is therefore invalid - The instructions are quashed - The State is directed to grant scholarships to the applicants on merits in accordance with the rules.
