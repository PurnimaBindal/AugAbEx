"According to Articles 226 and 311 of the Indian Constitution, a departmental enquiry for removal can be dispensed with.
In this case, the petitioner was convicted under Section 34 of the Police Act and had a history of repeated misconduct.
Considering the impact of his behavior on other employees, a penalty of removal was deemed appropriate, and thus the departmental enquiry was correctly waived.
[Paras 6 and 7]"
