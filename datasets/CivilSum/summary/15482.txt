Constitution of India, Article 226 - Payment of Gratuity Act - Public enterprise - Petitioners seek benefit of past service rendered in public sector enterprises while opting for Voluntary Retirement Scheme - However, benefit of past service can only be given if lending organization liquidates its liability by making lump sum payment - In this case, payment towards gratuity, earned leave and half pay leave not made by lending organization - Thus, past service of petitioners cannot be counted - Petition dismissed accordingly.
[Paras 16, 17 and 19]
