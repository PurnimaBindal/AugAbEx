Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Compulsory retirement - Sustainability - The absence of adverse remarks in ACR's for 15 years preceding the order mandates sustainability - The contention that adverse reports prior to promotion are deemed to be wiped out is rejected - The decision is held to be arbitrary and legally mala fide due to the petitioner's case not being referred to the Officers Committee - The decision is deemed ultra vires the instructions.
