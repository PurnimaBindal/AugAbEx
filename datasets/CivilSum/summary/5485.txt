According to Constitution of India, Article 311, the phrase "gravest act of misconduct" applies to cases where a constable consumes alcohol and is under the influence.
Therefore, if a constable consumes alcohol but is not under the influence of it, his case would not amount to "gravest act of misconduct" under the rules.
[Para 11] B.
The petitioner, who was found smelling of alcohol while not on duty, was not given the extreme penalty of dismissal as it was not warranted according to Constitution of India, Article 311.
