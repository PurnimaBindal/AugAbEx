Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Education - Admission - Maximum seats 100 - Instructions - Male and female candidates both must receive 50 seats - If seats fall vacant in different categories, they will be converted to General category - Five seats fell vacant - Ratio disturbed - 53 seats allocated to male candidate and 47 seats allocated to female candidates - Held, conversion of reserved category, to general category would change the character of the seat not only in its character but also in its limitations - Respondents were under obligation to maintain the ratio of equal distribution of seats between the male and female candidates - Respondents must maintain an equal distribution of seats.
