Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Reimbursement - Petitioner claiming reimbursement for outdoor treatment and regular dialysis for renal failure - The irreversible nature of this chronic disease and lack of facility for regular dialysis in Govt. hospitals means that it is not necessary for the patient to be admitted in hospital for undergoing the process of dialysis - The petitioner is held entitled to reimbursement at Govt. rates.
