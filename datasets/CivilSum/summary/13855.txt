Constitution of India, Article 226 - Claim to fix designation/seniority - Petitioner appointed lawfully to ex cadre post 17 years ago - Petitioner received revised pay scales extended to all employees in relevant scale - Court held that working in ex cadre post did not mean Petitioner must always be treated as such - Respondent wrongly concluded Petitioner received higher pay scale when it was just a revised one given uniformly - Respondents ordered to determine Petitioner's appointed post and fix her seniority and pay.
[Paras 10 to 13]
