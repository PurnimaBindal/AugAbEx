Constitution of India, Article 226 - Contractual appointment - Removal - Post of Sanskrit teacher - The petitioner was terminated from their contractual appointment on 3041998 when a list of 21 selected Sanskrit teachers belonging to the General Category was received by SSSB.
The principle of 'first come last go' was also applied.
No person junior to the petitioner was found to be in service.
It is permissible to relieve people appointed on a contract basis of their duties as per the contract.
Posts cannot be continued after their positions have been filled by regular appointments.
