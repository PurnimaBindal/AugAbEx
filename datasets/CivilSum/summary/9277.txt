Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sec. 11-A - Punishment/Termination/Reinstatement/Grave misconduct - A workman was charged with serious misconduct of physically assaulting his superior and using abusive language - The charges against him were proven, and the gravity of his misconduct was also acknowledged by the Labour Court - The Labour Court had no authority to interfere in exercising discretion under Section 11-A - The order of reinstatement with full back wages was overturned - Instead, the management was directed to pay Rs. 30,000/- and provide benefits under Section 17-B to the worker as compensation.
