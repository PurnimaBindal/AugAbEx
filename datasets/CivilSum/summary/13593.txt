Despite possessing an equivalent qualification to the required Master's Degree in Social Work, the petitioner was unjustly declined for consideration of the post of Child Development Project Officer.
This is in violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, as the advertisement specifically permits persons possessing equivalent qualifications, such as a Master's Degree in Sociology, to apply for the post.
The Commission declined to consider her application solely on the grounds that she did not possess the exact required qualification.
