Article 311 of the Constitution of India and Rule 1221 of the Punjab Police Rules, 1934 deal with discharge from service, which can only be approved if the police officer in question is deemed to be unable to perform their duties efficiently.
If deemed so, an order of discharge may be passed as long as it is based on objective data.
However, in cases where the order of discharge is passed solely on account of allegations made in an FIR, and these allegations do not constitute misconduct, the procedure prescribed under the Rules must be followed.
If not, the impugned order may be set aside.
The case of a discharged police constable was brought before the court for two separate incidents, one where he had a tiff with the employees of the Octroi Department and one where the petitioner was accused of having committed an offence.
In the latter case, he was discharged from service while still under suspension, without having his overall performance assessed or following the prescribed procedure, and the order was set aside.
