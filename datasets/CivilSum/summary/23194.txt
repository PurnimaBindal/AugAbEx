Constitution of India, Article 226 - Necessary party - Non- impleadment of - A Division Bench of the High Court made a decision on the dispute question without including the employers of the employees that belong to the Petitioner - It was held that the employers should have been impleaded before considering the disputes - Therefore, the matter was remanded with the permission given to the employers to seek impleadment before the High Court.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ appeal - New plea - Permissibility - It was held that ordinarily a contention that was not raised before a Single Judge should not be allowed to be argued before a Division Bench - However, in cases where the question is purely of law, the contention can be entertained and decided.
