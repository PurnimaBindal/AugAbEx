According to the Constitution of India's Articles 14 & 16 and Jammu & Kashmir SRO 43 of 1994, Compassionate Appointment serves to provide immediate relief for the deceased employee's family, rather than as a source of alternative employment.
Applicants cannot claim their preferred position, and once they accept a lower post, they cannot later claim a higher one.
Additionally, the issuing of a writ in favor of the petitioner based on a particular order passed by an Authority in a similar case is not possible.
