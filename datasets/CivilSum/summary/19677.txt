The post of Commandant (Selection Grade) is a Group A post under the Central Government and the age of superannuation for Group A posts is 58 years according to the Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Sections 43(a) and 104.
[Para 16]  B The Commandant (Selection Grade) post is higher than the Commandant post and only a Commandant is promoted to a Commandant (Selection Grade) position, and then a Commandant (Selection Grade) is promoted to the post of Addl.
D.I.G.
Promotion is always to a higher rank and not equal, according to the Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Section 104 and Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Rule 2(b).
[Paras 14 and 15] C. The promotion of the petitioner from Commandant to Commandant (Selection Grade) was subject to Vigilance clearance, which cannot later be considered as a mere grant of selection grade.
The grant of selection grade is never subject to a Vigilance clearance, but only promotion can be.
This is according to the Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Section 104.
