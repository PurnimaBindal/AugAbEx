Police Act, 1861, Section 7 - Delhi Police (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1980, Rule 15(2) - Disciplinary proceedings - A police officer who is the delinquent - The department must obtain the prior approval of the Additional Commissioner of Police before initiating a department enquiry, otherwise, it will compromise the validity of the proceedings - The termination of employment of the delinquent as a result of the departmental proceedings is considered improper.
