Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Constitution of India, Article 39(d) - Parity in wages - Workman claiming regular pay scale asserting that he attained temporary status upon completion of 6 months of continuous service - Computation under Section 33-C(2) can be made when there is an existing right or mandate of statute or per settlement or admission or as a service benefit - Workman had no existing right or adjudication of right prior to application - Issue requires adjudication to be crystallised - Forum approached was not the appropriate one - Order set aside.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Constitution of India, Article 39(d) - Parity of wages claimed for years 1983-84 - Application decided in year 1987 - Writ petition filed by management in year 1989 - Management did not pursue writ - Equity not in favour of management due to lack of diligence shown in prosecuting writ - Management not entitled to claim restitution of amount paid in terms of order of year 1989.
