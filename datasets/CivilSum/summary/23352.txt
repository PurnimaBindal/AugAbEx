Deputy Engineer (Electrical) Recruitment Rules, 1978 - Promotion - Quota rule - The High Court determined that the ratio of 2:1 was not justified and that the State Government could introduce a more reasonable rule - The High Court did not specify the reasoning behind its decision to strike down the 2:1 ratio for Junior Engineers and supervisors - The High Court did not substitute the appropriate ratio and instead left it to the government to establish a new one - The respondents must provide evidence to show that the legislative measure violates Article 14 of the Constitution and is unjust and arbitrary - The matter was referred back to the High Court for a fresh decision as no data were presented.
