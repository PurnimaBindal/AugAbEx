Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Contract of Service/Fixed term appointment/Extension of period of services - An appointed Dean of Kurukshetra University for a fixed term of three years, which could be further extended, was not granted an extension despite due consideration by competent authority - The employee does not have an indefeasible right to claim an extension based solely on the possibility of an extension, and there is no legal duty on the University to extend the term - The court cannot issue a mandamus to control discretion - The petition is dismissed.
The facts of AIR 1982 Punjab and Haryana 439 are not applicable.
