Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F -Termination of services - A respondent/claimant who was appointed on a contract basis and allowed to work for three years without contract renewal cannot have their services terminated without following the provisions of Section 25-F. An award of reinstatement with back wages is valid.
