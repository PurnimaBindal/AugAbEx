Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(3) - Approval - Approval is only required when there is a pending industrial dispute.
Once the dispute is settled, no permission is necessary and the ban placed on the common law is removed.
[Paras 4 to 6]  B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 and 11-B - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Misconduct - Reinstatement - Relief - If a worker is accused of serious misconduct such as riotous, violent and disorderly behavior, wilful insubordination, subversive acts, moral turpitude, instigation and intimidation of workers and murder, it is not in the interest of the establishment to reinstate such a worker.
Only compensation can be awarded.
