Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Department of Postal Superintendent/Post Masters Group-B Recruitment Rules, 1987 - Only 6% of postal service Group B grade posts are filled by promotion from clerical line.
Petitioner failed Departmental examination but challenged result claiming qualifying percentage is 45% in each paper and 50% in aggregate according to instruction from 2861975.
Respondents refer to letter from 5111997, in which qualifying standard was enhanced to 50% marks in each subject and in aggregate.
Held, criteria of 50% marks in each subject and in aggregate was existing from 1986 onwards and has been referred to in letter from 5111997.
Petitioner not entitled for promotion.
[Paras 3, 4, 13 and 14]
