Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 12 - Rajasthan State Pensioners Medical Concession Scheme, Rule 16 - Medical Reimbursement - Petitioners may require treatment out of state without prior approval from the approved hospital and authority - However, in situations such as sudden injury or illness requiring emergency treatment out of state, it cannot be expected of the patient to obtain prior approval at the risk of their life - The petitioner had suffered a serious head injury while in the State of Tamil Nadu and remained in a coma for days, resulting in admission to a local hospital by strangers - To reject the petitioner's claim for reimbursement due to lack of prior permission and reference to that hospital would be harsh and a violation of Articles 14 and 21 of the Constitution - Technicalities of Rule 16 need to be relaxed in such cases, and provisions must be read liberally.
