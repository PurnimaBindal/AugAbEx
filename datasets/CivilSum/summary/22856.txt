The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Differences between "contract of service" and "contract for service" - The master has greater power and control in "contract for service" while in "contract of service" there is lesser power - The degree of control decides legal quality of relationship, including selection, control of method of working, suspension & termination, responsibility of payment of wages.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Suspension and subsistence allowance - A government employee gets status only when he fulfills the conditions of a civil post as per CCS (CC&A) Rules - An employee on purely contract is not entitled to the same benefits and protection as available to government servants.
To claim those benefits, there must be a civil post available and the appointment should be made in accordance with the procedure prescribed for filling that post, and in accordance with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution i.e., by open selection.
