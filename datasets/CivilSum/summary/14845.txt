According to the Haryana State Education School Cadre (Group `C') Service Rules, 1998, academic qualifications of OT and Sanskrit are considered equivalent to B.T./B.Ed.
with Sanskrit as a teaching subject.
While a person who possesses a qualification of Shastri (Honours) with OT is not considered as suitable as a person possessing a BA, B.Ed.
with Sanskrit as one of the subjects in BA Classes.
Therefore, the petitioner in this case, who has a BA with Sanskrit as elective subject and B.Ed.
with Sanskrit as one of the teaching subjects, should have her services regularized.
