The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 state that in case of a dispute regarding the correctness of date of birth, the burden of proof lies with the employer.
If the employer fails to establish the correctness of the entry of date of birth, the contention of the petitioner regarding his date of birth, supported by school records, should be considered.
An order for retirement in the absence of proof regarding the correctness of the date of birth in the service book is illegal.
