Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 309 - Kerala Service Rules, 1959, Part I Rules 64 and 65, Part III, Rule 26 - Appellant requests leave without allowance on medical grounds - Government imposes the condition that leave will not count towards service benefits or pension - Court rules that altering the nature of the leave and imposing that condition is unlawful - Appellant's period of leave without allowance qualifies for pensionary benefits.
