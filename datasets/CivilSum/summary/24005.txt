Constitution of India, Article 191(1)(a) - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, Rule 48-A - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(k) - Nomination papers - Voluntary retirement for contesting election - Deeming acceptance on expiry of notice period - Validity of acceptance of nomination papers of a Govt. employee before acceptance or rejection of his request for voluntary retirement - The Rules that govern retirement clearly provide deeming acceptance of the request for voluntary retirement - Once the matter falls within the scope of Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, Rule 48-A, and the proviso to sub-rule (2) is clear, Fundamental Rules will have no bearing - Acceptance of a Govt. servant's nomination papers after the notice period of voluntary retirement was deemed perfectly valid.
