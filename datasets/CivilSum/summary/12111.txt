Constitution of India, Article 16 - Annual Confidential Report - Petitioner received adverse remark in Annual Confidential Report - Petitioner made exhaustive representation against adverse entry, but Authority did not pass a speaking order - Authority had an obligation to decide the representation by passing a speaking order - The order rejecting the representation was quashed due to mechanical decision-making and lack of thought - The Authority was instructed to re-decide the representation.
