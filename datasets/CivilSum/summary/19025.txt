Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 14 - Dock Workers (Regulation of Employment) Act, 1948, Section 5-A - Calcutta Dock Workers (Regulation of Employment) Scheme, 1970, Clause 20(5) - Instrumentality of State - Resolution No. 74 of 1992 issued by Calcutta Dock Labour Board (C.D.L.B.)
challenged - C.D.L.B.
and its functionaries are considered authorities under Article 12 of the Constitution, which makes them instrumentalities of State - Therefore, these authorities are required to discharge their statutory obligation objectively and fairly without acting arbitrarily - The appellant cannot insist that registered employers must requisition the service of Derrick Fitters and deprive them of their entitlement to supply of other Dock Labours - The passing of Resolution No. 74 and the insistence upon the respondent for paying the bill amount for notional and fictional engagement of Derrick Fitters is opposed to Article 14 of Constitution and cannot be sustained in eyes of law.
