Rajasthan Jail Service Rules, 1959, Rules 20, 23(3), 27(3) - Inter se seniority - Determination of - Petitioners are claiming seniority above promotees - Respondents, on the other hand, placed higher to Petitioners on the basis of date of joining - However, the appointment of Petitioners got delayed due to pending cases - Petitioners have argued that the fruits of selection cannot be prejudiced - The court held that as per the rules, a person becomes a member of the service only upon his appointment - Therefore, it is fair that seniority is fixed accordingly.
