Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution govern promotions and arrears of salary.
The Executive/State Government must comply with the rule of law, or it will jeopardise the entire administrative system.
If a civil servant is entitled to promotion but not given it, they and their dependants will suffer, and the court cannot overlook their grievances.
Once a civil servant is found entitled to promotion with retrospective effect, they are entitled to the benefits of arrears of salary accruing from the date of promotion.
The principle of 'No work no pay' does not apply when the employer does not offer the work.
B.
The concept of 'No work no pay' does not apply in situations where the employee was entitled to a certain job but not given it, according to the rule of law.
