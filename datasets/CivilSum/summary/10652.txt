The petitioner was enrolled into the BAMS course based on a domicile certificate issued by the SDO (Civil) in 1985.
However, there is no proof that any investigation was conducted to determine the genuineness or validity of the produced certificate at the time of admission in BAMS Once the petitioner finished the course, authorities requested that he clarify and provide evidence that he is a resident of Haryana State.
In 1992, the petitioner submitted an additional domicile certificate, which authorities deemed to be a false document.
Nonetheless, there is no evidence that the domicile certificate issued in 1985 was not genuine, and the enquiry conducted behind the petitioner's back infringes principles of natural justice.
The reports rendered from the said inquiry cannot be trusted, as no person should be condemned without being given an adequate opportunity to provide input.
As a result, the petitioner is eligible to receive a Bachelor's degree in Ayurvedacharya.
