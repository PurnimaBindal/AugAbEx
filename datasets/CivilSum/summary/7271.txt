Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 82 and 16.24(1)(ii) & (ix) - Absence from duty - Authorisation of earned leave is mandatory and cannot be taken at will without the prior permission of the competent authority, especially for police personnel who are disciplined force members.
[Para]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 16.24(1)(ii) & (ix) - Absence from duty - Disciplinary proceedings - The principles of natural justice are fully satisfied if the employee is given an opportunity to show cause, and his explanation is considered at every stage.
There is no scope for interference from the writ court if the employee's absence from duty is considered to be a grave misconduct, especially when repeated despite warnings and punishments.
[Paras 20 & 21]
Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 16.24(1)(ii) & (ix) - Pension - Absence from duty - Qualifying service - Only when the total service exceeds 10 years, will the right to pension of the delinquent be considered.
The rule will not be applicable if the employee does not have 10 years of qualifying service, excluding the forfeited period of service.
