Constitution of India, Article 226 - Mala fide - Personal allegations against respondents impleaded by name - Written statement filed by some other person/official - Failure to provide specific reply to the allegations would result in those personal allegations being deemed as proved.
[Para 6]
Transfer - Mala fide - Despite financial irregularities being proven against the Principal of the School, the Director School Education protected and retransferred the petitioner lecturer despite stay orders from the Government.
The transfer was motivated by private interests and not public interest.
[Paras 11 and 14]
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Criminal Procedure Code, Section 144 - Misconduct - Magistrates do not possess the jurisdiction to issue any order or direction under Section 144 Criminal Procedure Code to restrain any Govt. employee from discharging his duties due to their misconduct or to direct the authorities to appoint any other person in their place.
[Para 13]
Absence from duty - Wages for absence from duty - Transfer - Transferring a workman who has either been working or has not been allowed to work under the guise of an illegal transfer cannot be used as a basis to declare them as absent from duty thereby depriving them of their wages.
