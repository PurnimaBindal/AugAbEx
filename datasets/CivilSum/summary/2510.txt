Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 162 - Misconduct - Dismissal - A single act of corruption can lead to dismissal of an employee under the rule as gravest act of misconduct (A.I.R. 1992 S.C. 2188) [Para 11] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 162 - Misconduct - Dismissal - Forfeiture of all pensionary benefits does not depend on the length of service of an employee.
If the employee is guilty of gravest acts of misconduct, he can be dismissed from service without any consideration of pension or length of service.
[Para 11] C. Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 162 as amended by Punjab Police Rules (Haryana First Amendment) 1985 - Police Forces (Restriction of Rights) Act, 1966, Section 3 - Misconduct - Dismissal - Acts of causing disaffection and indiscipline among members of police force are the gravest acts of misconduct and are sufficient to warrant dismissal under the Rules.
