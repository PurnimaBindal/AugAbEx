On the basis of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, if an employee chooses to change his or her cadre, they will be granted a higher pay scale within the same cadre.
However, once the option is chosen, there can be no withdrawal.
In this case, the petitioner was allowed to move from clerical cadre to that of a Labour Inspector.
However, he was denied promotion in that new role as he was already on a higher pay scale.
The petitioner argued that he should not be deprived of a higher pay scale in the clerical cadre or promotion in the changed cadre.
Since tenure-based posts in the same cadre that offer a higher pay scale cannot be considered promotion posts that are filled by direct recruitment, the petitioner should be granted higher pay scales after five and ten years of service in the clerical cadre.
[Paras 6 and 7]
