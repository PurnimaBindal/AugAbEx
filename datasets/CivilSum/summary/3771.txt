Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - UP Election Department District Level (Clerical Grade) Service Rules, 1992, Rule 3 - Appointment - Retrenched employees - Amending the rules to eliminate preference for retrenched employees - Court held, amendment did not breach constitutionality as it ended reverse discrimination for eligible candidates and conferred preference on retrenched employees fulfilling necessary criteria.
