Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 - Selection - Appointment - Refusal of - Reverification - Petitioner chosen for appointment, but reverification report was adverse, resulting in appointment denial - The name of the Petitioner was mentioned in an FIR, however, the Petitioner was neither challaned nor chargesheeted in the criminal case - Complainant submitted an affidavit absolving Petitioner of charges - It was concluded that the naming of Petitioner was not the fault of the complainant but due to misunderstanding - Therefore, the re-verification report and order issued on the basis of the FIR were quashed - As a result, the petition was allowed.
