Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Civil Service (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970 - Delayed departmental enquiry - Irregularities in plot allotment - The petitioner was accused of not controlling his subordinates, resulting in embezzlement - However, the government cannot proceed with the enquiry after such a long delay without adequate explanation - With almost 24 years since the supposed irregularities, the petitioner has already suffered from the loss of full retiral benefits - Allegations of supervisory negligence cannot provide a basis for the inquiry after such a long period - Therefore, proceedings initiated against the petitioner must be quashed, and he should receive corresponding benefits.
