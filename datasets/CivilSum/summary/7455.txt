Rajasthan Subordinate Services (Recruitment to other Service conditions) Rules, 1960 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Change of pay scale - Violation of principles of natural justice - Petitioner's pay scale changed without notice or opportunity of hearing - Petitioner was qualified for the post - Audi alteram partem facet of natural justice required - Pay scale could not be withdrawn without notice or opportunity - Order quashed.
