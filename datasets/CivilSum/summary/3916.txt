For posts with eligibility qualifications, the relevant date for determining eligibility is the last date for filing applications.
Qualifications gained after this date will not make the candidate eligible for selection.
[Para 7]

Selecting agencies must maintain records of their selections for at least one year.
Claims that records are unavailable prior to that timeframe cannot be accepted.
[Para 9]

While it is not within the courts' purview to alter the decisions of selection committees, unequal allocation of marks may indicate favouritism.
A candidate related to a selection committee member was awarded 19 out of 20 marks for an interview, without which they would not have been selected.
The court must be informed of the basis for such high marks, or adverse inference may be drawn against the respondent.
[Para 10]

The government has not identified which posts are reserved for persons with disabilities under the J&K Persons with Disabilities Act.
The board conducting selection was not obliged to select candidates with disabilities, as the Act had not yet been published.
The selection process was not vitiated by the absence of reserved positions.
[Paras 11 and 12]

For positions requiring a degree or diploma in Electrical/Electronics, greater weight should be given to degrees, which are a superior qualification to diplomas.
The selection criteria must be amended accordingly.
[Paras 13 to 15, 20 and 22]

Selections may be set aside if the criteria for allocation of marks for degrees and diplomas was defective, even if candidates have already been appointed.
However, the appointed candidates are entitled to wages for the period they worked.
