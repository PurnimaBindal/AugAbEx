According to CCS (CCA) Rules, Rule 10(2) - Pay Scale - Benefits of revised pay scale, the Principal of the school who was placed under suspension during the pendency of criminal proceedings must have the benefits of pay revision postponed until the conclusion of judicial proceedings.
This approach is not arbitrary or perverse because in the event of acquittal, the respondent would have to pass a proper order on both the issue of revised payscale and the full pension admissible in light of final orders.
Therefore, there is no illegality in not releasing revised payscale benefits to the petitioner.
