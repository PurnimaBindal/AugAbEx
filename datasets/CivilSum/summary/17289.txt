Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Entrance test - The petitioner's merit for admission to the LL.B course was evaluated according to the prescribed procedure.
He was initially placed at serial No.194 in the merit list but was later placed at serial No.252 as per the criteria fixed for determining merit.
Although the petitioner challenged the determination of his merit and claimed that he was denied admission, part-(c)(i) of para 1 of the prospectus provided only 25% weightage to be given for academic achievement and added to the score of the entrance test.
The petitioner was awarded 9498 marks out of 200 which included 70 out of 150 for the entrance test and 2379 marks out of 50 for the qualifying examination and 119 marks as PU weightage.
Therefore, para (c)(i) has to be read along with the illustration which forms part of the scheme.
Ultimately, the petition was dismissed.
