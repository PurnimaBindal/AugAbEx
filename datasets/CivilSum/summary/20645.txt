Union Territory of Chandigarh Employees Rules, 1966, Rule 2 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39(d) - Equal pay for equal work - The respondent was appointed as Science Master on a temporary basis and was later transferred to the post of Science Supervisor on his own pay in the State Institute of Education.
The respondent was performing the same duties as a District Science Supervisor in the State of Punjab, but as the post on which he was appointed was an equivalent post and not higher than that of Science Master, he cannot claim salary higher than that of Science Master.
