Punjab State Electricity Board Services of Engineers (Electrical) Regulations, 1965, Regulation 15 - Claim of seniority - According to Regulation 15, inter se seniority should be determined based on relative seniority in the class from which promotions were made, unless supersession is due to inefficiency or ineligibility.
The petitioner was punished with stoppage of one increment.
The respondent argues that seniority was based on service records and that the petitioner's promotion case was rejected due to a departmental inquiry.
Allegations of delay and laches were made.
It is held that the respondents were not justified in relegating the petitioner to a lower place in the seniority list, as the minor penalty should not have affected seniority.
Since the petition has been brought for final hearing after 16 years, the plea of delay cannot be considered.
[Paras 4, 6, 9]
Written Statements - New reasons in written statement - Sustainability - The respondent did not provide any reasons in the impugned order to deny seniority to the petitioner as stated in the written statement.
This amounts to filling gaps or blanks in a grossly irrational way.
The validity of the order must be evaluated based on the reasons stated therein and not supplemented by new reasons in the form of an affidavit; otherwise, a bad order is validated when challenged.
[Paras 7, 8]
