Government Employees (Conduct) Rules, 1966, Rule 18(2) state that a government employee cannot buy or sell immovable property without informing their employer.
In this case, the petitioner's wife purchased the land with her own money and she is not a government employee.
Additionally, the petitioner did not pay for the transaction, nor was he acquiring the property in his name or on behalf of a family member.
Therefore, Rule 18(2) does not apply to this transaction.
