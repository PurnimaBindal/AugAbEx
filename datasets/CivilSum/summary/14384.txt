Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Absorption in service - Regular pay scale - The respondents who are Safaiwallas with the appellant in Brichgunj Cantonment area have approached the Court with a request for regular absorption into service as well as payment of regular pay scales equivalent to those received by Safaiwallas in other departments - A balance has to be struck by courts between conflicting interests of the employer and the employees - Articles 14 and 16 of Constitution of India guarantee a right to equality rather than employment - The respondents have the right to be treated equally in terms of their employment as compared to other Safaiwallas under similar circumstances - Negating this right of equality will mean the respondents are being denied their fundamental right.
