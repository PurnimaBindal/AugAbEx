The All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) Act of 1987 grants the AICTE power not only to grant approval for starting new technical institutions and introducing new courses and programs, but also to regulate the number of seats for admission in professional colleges.
Therefore, the issue of the number of students to be admitted in technical education institutions must be treated as occupied by Central legislation, and the State is not competent to legislate on that subject.

The denial of a prayer for the grant of affiliation for conducting a 2-year full-time MBA Course on the ground of non-production of NOC from State Government is illegal, as the 1987 Act and 1994 Regulations do not require such production.
The decision of the Vice-Chancellor to decline affiliation to the petitioner is declared illegal, and respondent No. 4 is directed to issue NOC to petitioner for conversion of the postgraduate diploma course into an MBA course with an intake capacity of 60 students, and the competent authority of respondent No. 3 shall grant affiliation to the institute.
