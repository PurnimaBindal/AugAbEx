Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation of services - After one year and six months of work for HUDA, the petitioner filed a writ petition for regularisation.
The petition was granted subject to appeal, in accordance with the judgment in Piara Singh's case.
However, since the Supreme Court reversed the decision in Piara Singh's case, the petitioner is no longer entitled to the benefits of the order passed in the writ petition.
Nonetheless, the authority has been instructed to consider the petitioner's case for regularisation based on subsequent policies and instructions.
