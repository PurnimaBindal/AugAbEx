 Constitution of India, Article 311 - Domestic enquiry - Termination - Allegations that the conductor charged for fare from passengers without issuing tickets cannot be substantiated without the statements of the passengers before the domestic tribunal or checking staff.
The statement of checking staff alone cannot form a basis for holding the plaintiff responsible for embezzlement.
[Para 15]

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Domestic Enquiry - Termination - Past service record - Principles of natural justice - Taking into consideration past record of delinquent where no charge-sheet was issued violates principles of natural justice.
A report beyond the charge-sheet cannot be taken into consideration.
[Paras 16 and 17]

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination - Back wages - No work no pay - After termination order employee has not worked in the department - The claim of back wages is denied.
[Para 18]
