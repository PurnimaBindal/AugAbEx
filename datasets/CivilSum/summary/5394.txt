The petitioner stood first in the examination and joined as Physical Inspector, but Respondent No. 8 filed a Writ Petition to challenge the appointment.
The District Inspector then directed the Managing Committee to recast the panel and the memo appointing the petitioner was withdrawn.
However, discrepancies were found in the Score sheet, and a criminal case was lodged against Respondent No. 4.
The matter was referred to the Director of School Education to take a decision and pass necessary orders.
The Writ Petition was dismissed, and the Criminal Court was at liberty to arrive at an in-depth conclusion.
The scope of interference by courts in the selection process has been held to be limited.
The authorities shall not ordinarily be interfered with by the courts, and the decision whether a candidate fulfills the requisite qualification or not should be left to academic bodies and the concerned selection committee, which consists of experts.
