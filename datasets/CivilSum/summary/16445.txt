The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 state that deputationists do not have the right to compete with department employees for promotion due to deputation being a temporary arrangement.
However, they can still claim consideration for promotion or benefits in their parent department if a junior employee is granted such benefits.
[Paras 9 and 10]
According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, general category employees cannot demand equality with appointees in evacuees quota as it is a separate classification.
