The petitioners, who are registered under the MP Societies Registration Act, 1973, are considered to be a commercial establishment under Section 2(4) of the MP Shops and Establishments Act.
As such, they fall under the provisions of the Payment of Gratuity Act as defined by Section 1(3)(b) and Section 2(8) of the MP Shops and Establishments Act.
