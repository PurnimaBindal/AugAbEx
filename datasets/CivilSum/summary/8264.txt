Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Pensionary Benefits - Delay - Interest - The petitioner, retired on 31787, faced a delay of more than one year and six months in payment of retiral benefit due to administrative procedure, without any explanation of the reasons behind the delay being provided.
The petitioner is entitled to receive interest at a rate of 12% per annum on the delayed payment, and the respondents must pay Rs. 8500/- as costs of the writ petition to petitioner.
