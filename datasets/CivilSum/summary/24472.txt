Constitution of India, Article 14 and Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 4 address discrimination and the doctrine of equal pay for equal work.
There is disparity in pay and age of superannuation between the Members of the Administrative Tribunal and Judges of the High Court or Vice-Chairman of the Tribunal, but this does not violate the equality clause.
The Tribunal is only equated with the High Court for adjudication of disputes relating to service matters, not for all purposes.
Therefore, the Chairman, Vice-Chairman, and Members of the Tribunal should not be equated with the Chief Justice and Judges of the High Court for all purposes.
