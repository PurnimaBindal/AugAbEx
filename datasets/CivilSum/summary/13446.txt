Constitution of India, Article 226 - Necessary parties - Seniority - In cases where a pure question of principle is involved, parties may not be relevant.
However, when a petitioner claims seniority over those appointed prior to them and seeks a declaration of prior appointment, the affected parties are necessary for the declaration to be valid.
