M.A.T.
2930 of 2002 : Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - Second wife of deceased employee seeks compassionate appointment for her daughter after the death of her husband while in service - Review application from son of first wife rejected - As per Clause 14 of Office Circular, the widow of the deceased employee must indicate the order of preference when there are multiple dependents - The Authority has been instructed to offer compassionate appointment to the eldest daughter of the deceased.
[Paras 9, 10 and 15]
