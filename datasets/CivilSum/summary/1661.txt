Constitution of India, Article 311 - Secondary Schools Code, Rule 7513 - Abandonment of service - Employee absenteeism for more than 7 days amounts to abandonment of service, but this rule doesn't apply to employees who have already been sanctioned leave.
[Para 5]
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Secondary Schools Code, Rule 7513 - Abandonment - Despite the presumption of abandonment of service after a certain period of absenteeism, a notice to the employee is necessary for compliance with the rule of natural justice - The plaintiff, having worked at the institution for more than a decade, should have been given an opportunity before terminating her services.
[Paras 6 and 7]
Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Compensation in lieu of reinstatement - The termination of services on account of absenteeism without giving the employee a chance to be heard violates the principles of natural justice - Since the institution cannot afford the arrears that would be due if full reinstatement was granted, the court awarded a compensation of Rs. 30,000/- with 6% interest.
