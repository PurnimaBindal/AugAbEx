The petitioner challenged the appointment process for the post of Lecturer under Section 29 of the Aligarh Muslim University Act.
The Executive Committee and the Selection Committee were accused of preparing a select list and keeping a reserve list for appointment without following the proper selection procedure.
The Division Bench of the High Court ruled that this violated Section 29(2)(a) and the resolution and action of the Executive Council were ultra vires.
The court found no reason to interfere with the High Court's decision and dismissed the petition.
