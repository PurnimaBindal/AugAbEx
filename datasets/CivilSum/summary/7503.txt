Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Termination simplicitor - Whether considered stigmatic - Argument made that order was based on allegation of misconduct and denial of regularization due to criminal case in which the petitioner was acquitted - However, since termination did not involve a full-scale inquiry into allegations of misconduct or guilt, it cannot be seen as a punishment aimed at circumventing the requirement of an inquiry under Article 311(2) of the Constitution according to the standard established by the Supreme Court in the case of Pavanendra Narayan Verma.
