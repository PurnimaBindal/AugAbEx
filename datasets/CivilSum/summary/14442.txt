As per the Constitution of India, specifically Article 226 and 311, if an employee's services are terminated without adhering to the principles of natural justice, the writ jurisdiction cannot be barred by an alternative remedy of appeal.
In a disciplinary enquiry, if the petitioner requests an adjournment due to illness, the Enquiry Officer and punishing authority cannot proceed ex parte and dismiss the petitioner from service on that basis.
Such an action would be considered invalid and dismissed.
[Paras 12 to 15]
