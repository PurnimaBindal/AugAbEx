A civil servant who is not considered for promotion at the appropriate time and is unable to work due to the authorities' failure to act cannot be denied the monetary benefits of the higher post to which they were promoted through deemed promotion, even if sealed cover procedure was not applied in the case of pending departmental proceedings.
[Para 7]
When a civil servant is entitled to promotion from retrospective effect, they cannot be deprived of the arrears of salary resulting from such promotion from the assigned promotion date.
The principle of no work no pay does not apply in cases where the employee was not provided the work they were entitled to.
