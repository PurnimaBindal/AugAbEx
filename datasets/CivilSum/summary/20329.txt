According to the Constitution of India, Article 16(4), as well as the Government of Pondicherry, Jail Department, Group C and Group D Posts (Non-Ministerial) Recruitment Rules, 1981, and the government-issued Brochure on "Reservation for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in Services" in Paragraph 11.3, Chapter 11 pertaining to Reservation in promotions, the carry forward of reserved vacancies must occur for three "recruitment years" in the event of non-availability of SC/ST candidate.
This recruitment year will begin in the year of actual recruitment, and the vacancy is required to be carried forward for three calendar years starting from the recruitment year in which it was initially carried forward.
An appellant who was given an ad hoc appointment as a general category candidate cannot request regularisation solely because he has completed the probation period satisfactorily and has served for three years.
As a result, his promotion only on ad hoc basis was deemed reasonable.
(Paras 2 to 6)
