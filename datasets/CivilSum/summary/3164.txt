Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Examination - Declaration of result - If a university withholds a candidate's result for an extended period without sufficient reason, the candidate cannot be deprived of the benefits of their qualifications.
The result will be deemed to be declared and the candidate will receive the benefits as if they passed the examination.
However, there may be exceptions to this general rule.
