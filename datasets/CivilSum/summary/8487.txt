The Appellant appointed as peon did not disclose he had passed S.S.L.C at the time of selection, but there is no information indicating that someone with higher qualifications shouldn't have been selected.
Therefore, it's not considered fraud.
Furthermore, when terminating the appellant, the provision of section 25-F of Industrial Disputes Act was not followed.
Due to these factors, the order of termination is being reversed.
