The Appropriate Government refers to the State Government, not the Central Government, for the appellant company challenging an order to retrench 40 workmen.
Appeal dismissed after a single Judge reconsidered the case.
[Para 9]
Employer's application for retrenchment of 40 workmen was not in triplicate and consequently did not comply with Rule 76-A(3).
The deemed grant of permission would only commence once the application was in order.
