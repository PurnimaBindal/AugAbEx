Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 20 - Disciplinary proceedings - The Administrative Tribunal cannot entertain an application challenging a departmental proceeding at the stage of a second show cause notice under Section 20(1) - Even if no reason has been recorded - Such an order of the Tribunal must be set aside.
