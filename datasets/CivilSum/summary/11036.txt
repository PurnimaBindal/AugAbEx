Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Rajasthan Famine Relief Works Employees (Exemption from Labour Laws) Act, 1964, Section 3 - Exemption from Labour Laws - Termination of service - Project based employees - Famine relief work is treated as a project - When the project is completed, the services of the project-based employees are automatically terminated - The provisions of the Industrial Disputes Act do not apply in this case.
