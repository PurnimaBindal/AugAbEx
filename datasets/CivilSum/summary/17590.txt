Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - Minor - Instructions - Limitation - The petitioner was a minor of three years when his father died in harness and was left at the mercy of others when his mother remarried - His two previous applications for compassionate appointment were rejected under the old limitation rules, but a fresh limitation was prescribed under new instructions that were not properly published - The petitioner's penurious condition and lack of means to have up-to-date knowledge of the government instructions justified granting him compassionate appointment without considering his earlier rejections - The petitioner was held entitled to compassionate appointment and directed to be appointed to any eligible and suitable position.
