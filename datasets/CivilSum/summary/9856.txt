According to Rule 1221 of the Punjab Police Rules, 1934, absence from duty during training can result in discharge from service due to unsatisfactory work and conduct.
Repeated absence during training despite advice and warning can be grounds for discharge on the recommendations of the Principal of the Training College.
Such discharge is not considered stigmatic or punitive.
If the recruit is not perceived to be an efficient and disciplined police personnel, discharge can occur within the first three years of service.
The details of leave and absence are taken into consideration in this evaluation, and it is not a form of punitive action.
Failure to maintain discipline during training and repeated absences indicate that the recruit is not likely to be a good disciplined police personnel.
Therefore, the order of discharge on those grounds is valid.
[Paras 5, 7 and 10]
