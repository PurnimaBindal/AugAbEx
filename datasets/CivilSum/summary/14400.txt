Constitution of India 1950, Article 311 - Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Section 9(2)(b) - Punishment - The employees of the petitioner union causing inconvenience to the public through work stoppage means the Corporation was justified in penalizing with wage cuts - The problematic and unreasonable behavior of the employees of the petitioner union amounted to indiscipline - Disciplinary authorities have jurisdiction and the right to impose punishments like wage cuts, even if the Payment of Wages Act does not apply - Therefore, the action taken by the authority was legal.
