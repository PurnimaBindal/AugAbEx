Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Termination - The petitioner's services were terminated but it was not clear with whom he was working at the time - Evidence presented showed that the petitioner did not report for duty and did not complete 240 days in the year - As the petitioner failed to provide proof for his working days, the petition was dismissed solely on this basis.
