Constitution of India, Article 226 - Haryana Service of Engineers Class-II, P.W.D.
(Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1970, Rule 9 - Promotion - Selection grade - Pension - Petitioner was promoted to AE/SDOs, but subsequent promotions to the post of SDOs became a matter of challenge in courts and the government reviewed various promotions on the basis of Apex Court's directions.
Petitioner's name did not figure in the final order regarding promotions, and while the promotion issue was being agitated before the court, petitioner was given selection grade on completion of 12 years.
However, the benefit was later withdrawn as his case was not approved by HPSC, and the petitioner's claims have been ignored.
The impugned order was quashed, and the petitioner is entitled to the benefits of the order passed in CWP No. 3219 of 1993 decided on 1781995, including placement in Ranking list, selection grade, and pension fixation.
