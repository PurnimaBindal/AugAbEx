Section 10(1)(c) of the Industrial Dispute Act, 1947 deals with reference of disputes to the government.
It has been held that the Joint Secretary's decision to make judgement on merits of the dispute instead of referring it to government is not proper and exceeds jurisdiction.
Such authority does not have the power to enter upon the merits of the dispute intended to be referred.
As a result, the order has been set aside.
