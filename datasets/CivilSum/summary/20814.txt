According to the UP State Roadways Organisation (Abolition of Posts and Absorption of Employees) Rules, 1982, Rule 8(ii) read with Rules 4(1) and 5, government employees on deputation to the corporation must choose to be absorbed into the corporation within three months.
If they fail to do so, they will be deemed to have been absorbed into the corporation.
None of the employees exercised their option and were deemed to have opted for absorption.
However, they were not entitled to count the period from the date of their deputation until the date of absorption as part of their government employment for the computation of pensionary benefits in their favour, as per Constitution of India, Article 309.
