The Maharasthra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Schedule IV, Items 5 and 9 state that an agreement that has failed to be implemented can lead to a complaint.
However, in this case, the respondent was not given the opportunity to be heard and the basic principles of natural justice were not followed.
The discretion claimed by the respondent was not on the basis of the grant or refusal of the increment, but on the basis of the workman's performance.
Since the adverse performance report was not brought to the respondent's attention, it was not permissible to deny or reduce the increments.

The cause of action for complaint being of recurring nature, the question of limitation does not arise as per the Maharasthra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Schedule IV, Items 5 and 9.
