Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation of services - Daily wagers - Petitioner not eligible for benefit of Supreme Court Judgment in Piara Singh's case as not having completed 10 years of service on 30.9.1988 - Later instructions prescribe for regularisation after five years of daily wage service with no break in service for 30 days or more in any year - Anand Kumar's case states any break due to action by the employer must be ignored - Worker-caused breaks in service will be covered by the instruction's condition - Unless the condition is successfully challenged, petitioner is not entitled to regularisation.
