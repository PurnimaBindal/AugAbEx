The Scheme for Regularisation of employment of Dock Workers and efficient performance of dock work incorporates a reserve pool of registered dock workers who receive a guaranteed minimum wage as attendance allowance and disappointment money by the Board.
Such reserve pool workers are utilised by vessels fitted with grab crane in loading and unloading of cargo, even if the owners of transhippers employ regular winchmen and pay them monthly salary.
Though imposition of higher levy in respect of loading done by ocean going vessels with grab cranes and recovery of cost of maintaining reserve pool gang workers aims at efficient performance of dock workers and regularity of employment of such workers, it is valid and does not violate Article 14 of the Constitution However, the scheme cannot be made effective with retrospective effect before its approval.
[Paras 14 to 17]
