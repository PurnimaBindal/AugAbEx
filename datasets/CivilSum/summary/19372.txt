Article 16 and 310 of the Constitution of India discuss age of retirement for work-charged employees.
Certified Standing Orders of the Corporation have set the age of retirement at 60 years for work-charged employees and 58 years for regular employees.
The Rule providing two different ages of retirement satisfies the purpose of reasonable classification since work-charged employees do not have the same job security as regular employees.
To account for uncertain job security, two years have been added to the retirement age of work-charged employees for their benefit.
