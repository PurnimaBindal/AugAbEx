Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Regularisation of services - The petitioner, a daily wager appointed in 1988, submitted a detailed representation which was considered and rejected by the respondent through a speaking order.
As per the view taken by the Hon'ble Supreme Court in the case of Umadevi, reported in 2006(3) SLR 1 (SC), no mandamus can be issued for regularization of services with all consequential benefits.
[Paras 1 and 3]
