According to the Constitution of India, Article 311, a delinquent official is entitled to a reasonable opportunity to provide documents during the enquiry.
If original documents are missing, transcripts prepared by witnesses can be supplied for evaluation and used as evidence.
The non-supply of original documents cannot efface the overwhelming direct and circumstantial evidence during the Departmental Enquiry.
There is enough evidence on record to return a finding of guilt against the respondent.
[Para]
According to the Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311, the High Court does not have the jurisdiction to re-appreciate evidence like sitting in appeal.
Evidence of a person with a doubtful reputation can be corroborated by independent evidence.
Evidence of a woman cannot be disbelieved solely because of her reputation or character.
