Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Central Civil Services (Fixation of Pay of Re-employed Persons) Orders, 1986, Rules 4, 5 and 16 - If the pay of a re-employed pensioner is fixed in accordance with Rule 4, he may be given normal increments within the time scale of the post, subject to the condition that his pay and gross pension and other benefits do not exceed Rs. 3,500/- per month- The pay of the petitioner was correctly fixed and did not exceed the limit of Rs. 3,500/- per month taking into consideration his 32 years qualifying Army service, including pension and other benefits- The order of refixation of the pay and recovery on that basis were quashed.
