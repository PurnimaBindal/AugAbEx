The Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 1(5) pertains to the applicability of the act.
The petitioner company claims to be a liaison office of the principal company and performs functions such as gathering information and inquiries for the principal.
In a civil judge's ruling, the petitioner was considered a 'shop' and therefore covered under the Act's provisions.
It is held that the petitioner company is an extension of the principal company and furthering the same objective of trading.
Thus, it is covered under the provisions of the Act.
