Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1958, Rules 17A, 31 and 39 - Promotion - Regularisation of ad hoc and temporary promotions already made with retrospective effect - Such power is not unknown in service jurispendence - Employee cannot be allowed to suffer for the fault of the authority and its inaction at the due date - Non-considering of seniority is immaterial when the ad hoc or temporary promotions were itself made after consideration of seniority and merit by the DPC and the challenge is by a person not eligible - Competent authority has such residuary power under Rules 31 and 39.
