The challenge of deduction of pension from refixed salary for reemployed ex-servicemen was held, with sub-clause (2) Clause 16 to be read along with Explanation (ii) stating that pension, defined in Order 3(1), should be deducted from pay fixed under the rule after ignoring Rs.15 and the net amount is payable.
It was made clear that pension up to Rs. 15, considered meager, should be disregarded, and the rest of the pension is not available.
As a result, the petition has been disposed of.
