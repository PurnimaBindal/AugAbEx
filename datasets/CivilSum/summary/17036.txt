Constitution of India, Article 226 - TN Recognised Private Schools (Regulation) Act, 1973, Section 14 - Non-payment of salary - Petitioners, who were teachers, claimed salary for a period when the aided primary school they worked at was defunct and had no students - Teaching grant was based on average pupil attendance - However, the petitioners did not work and were subsequently absorbed into other schools - Court held that the petitioners were not entitled to salary for the period they did not work and dismissed the petition.
