Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Market Committee Class III Service Rules, 1989 - Merger of cadre - Discrimination - While persons in similar positions were absorbed into the permanent clerk cadre, the petitioner electrician's claim was arbitrarily rejected.
The fact that the electrician post was not mentioned in the circular did not disqualify the petitioner from similar treatment.
Three employees initially appointed as Mechanical unit operator were absorbed into the clerk cadre, despite the same post not being mentioned in the circular.
The court held that petitioner is entitled to be absorbed into the permanent clerk cadre, from the date he began working in the position, provided he passes the Punjabi type test, which is an essential qualification, within six months.
