The granting of awards under the Scheme for Grant of Reward to Officers and Staff of Income Tax Department falls outside the purview of the Tribunal, as it is considered an ex-gratia payment and is not a condition of service.
[Para 9] B The final decision of the committee under Rules 2(b) and 2(c) of the scheme cannot be deemed illegal or arbitrary, as it is based on the appraisal report of the investigation wing and the assessment made on the basis of seized material.
Therefore, if the committee determines that an individual is not eligible for the grant of reward, their decision cannot be called into question.
