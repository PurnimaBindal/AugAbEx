As per the Constitution of India, namely Articles 14, 38, 226, there is a scheme for absorption of those serving as casual workers for more than a decade and working for more than a year, which is to be prepared on a rational basis.
However, casual labourers working for nominal wages have been repeatedly deprecated by the Supreme Court.
The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 38, 226 establishes that casual workers are entitled to the same wages as regular employees performing similar types of work.
Therefore, those who are still employed should receive wages at equivalent rates to those of regular Corporation employees with similar job responsibilities.
Payment is expected to be made within two months of this order.
