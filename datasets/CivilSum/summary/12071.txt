Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Overtime duty - Wages for working on holidays - Classification between employees based on their posting on counters - Workers at District Industries Centers governed by the same rules as those working in the office - No discrimination allowed based on posting - Extra wages to be paid to those working on holidays when counterparts in office do not work - Directions given to compute and pay their wages from dates worked on counters on holidays.
