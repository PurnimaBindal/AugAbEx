Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II Part I - Benefit of Selection Grade - Recomputation of Pension - Petitioner in a Writ Petition sought recomputation of retiral benefits after extending the benefit of Selection Grade according to a judgment in another civil writ petition - Court found petitioner entitled to arrears for a period of three years and four months preceding November, 1994, and salary arrears for a period from September 19, 1991 to Feburary 29, 1992 - Respondent directed to pay six months of salary arrears - Retiral Benefits should be recomputed on the basis of last salary drawn - Petition granted.
