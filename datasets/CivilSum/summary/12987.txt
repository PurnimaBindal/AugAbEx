According to Sections 10(1) and 33(2)(b) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, an Enquiry officer accused of bias may represent themselves during the hearing of an approval application.
However, if the same officer appears as an advocate for management during a reference against termination of service based on their own investigation, this would demonstrate bias, similar to a judge who convicts an accused and then advocates for prosecution in an appeal against conviction.
