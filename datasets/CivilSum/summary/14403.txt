The Constitution of India, comprising of Articles 14, 16 and 226, deals with Regularisation, Absorption in other departments and Surplus employees.
The issue at hand involves the Abolition of 12 posts of Labour Inspectors Grade-I that were lying vacant with no consideration being given to their absorption in other departments.
The State Government had issued policy instructions which required the authorities to consider the cases of employees working on ad hoc and temporary basis for regularisation and absorption in other departments.
Failure to adhere to these instructions resulted in discrimination and failure of justice.
The authorities cannot ignore the instructions of the Government as various other employees were adjusted within the department and in other departments on the basis of the same policy.
