Haryana Revenue Department Naib Tehsildar (Group C) Service Rules, 1988, Rule 9, as per Article 226 of the Indian Constitution - Promotion from Kanungo to Naib Tehsildar - Notification was amended to require Kanungo to have passed departmental examination for Naib Tehsildar; petitioner challenged notification as there was no such requirement for direct recruits; State Govt. has the power to prescribe different qualifications for direct recruits and promotees.

Section 82(6) of Punjab Re-organisation Act, 1966 - Haryana Revenue Department Naib Tehsildar (Group C) Service Rules, 1988, Rule 9 - Petitioner's claimed right to promotion was not available to him before appointed day, as the rules under which he claims the right to be promoted were not yet in existence during the reorganisation of the states of Punjab and Haryana - Thus, the State Govt. can establish different terms and conditions for promotion.
