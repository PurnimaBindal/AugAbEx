Constitution of India, Article 16 - Haryana State Agricultural Marketing Board Service Rules, 1974, Rules 7 and 10 - Challenge to promotion of Respondent No. 1 as Assistant Draftsman - A person must have a diploma in draftsmanship or 5 years' experience as Tracer and pass the test prescribed by the Board to be promoted to the post of Assistant Draftsman on the Civil side - In the case of architectural wing posts, a diploma in Architectural assistantship or 5 years of experience as Tracer/Ferroprinter is eligible for promotion - The nature of the duties of assistant draftsmen in the two branches are different- Respondent No. 2 is not eligible for promotion to the post of Assistant Draftsman (Architecture) as he does not have 5 years of experience as Tracer in the Architecture Branch- The promotion of respondent No. 2 to the post of Assistant Draftsman (Architecture) against the rules has been nullified - Respondents are required to fill up the post in accordance with the qualification of 5 years experience as Tracer in the branch concerned.
