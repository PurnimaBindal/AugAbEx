Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay scales - Gazetted status - Classification - Criteria for classifying posts into gazetted or non-gazetted cannot be based solely on pay scales, but must also consider administrative and procedural factors - The Railways have various categories of posts with equal pay scales, but some are classified as Group 'C' rather than gazetted Group 'B' - There is no discrimination against the Account Staff by not granting them gazetted status and facilities at par with those in the Indian Audit and Accounts Department - The Tribunal's decision to grant Accounts Staff in Group 'C' gazetted status in Group 'B' based solely on pay scale is not valid - Railways' appeal allowed and Tribunal's order is cancelled.
