Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural justice - Seniority - Ranking list - Any change in ranking list and exclusion of names without notice or opportunity as in this case is null and void for the affected parties - In the present case, ranking list is being quashed for violation of principles of natural justice - Authorities are directed to start anew, issue notices, consider objections and provide hearing to affected individuals.
