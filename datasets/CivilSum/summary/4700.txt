Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 173 - Disability Pension - An individual is discharged before becoming a soldier and later granted an invalidity gratuity of Rs. 50/- due to disability.
After more than three decades, the individual files a writ petition claiming disability pension.
However, the petition is liable to be dismissed due to delay, latches, and acquiescence.
