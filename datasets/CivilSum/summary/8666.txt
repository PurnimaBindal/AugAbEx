Army Act, 1950, Section 192 - Pension Regulations of the Army (Part I), (1961), Regulation 16(a) - Pensionary benefits - Payable from date of retirement or cessation of service after completion of qualifying service - Forfeiture in Court martial proceedings or under an order under pension regulations results in no payment.
[Para 12]

Army Act, 1950, Section 192 - Pension Regulations of the Army (Part I), (1961), Regulation 16(a) - Pensionary benefits - Proceedings for forfeiture must be undertaken within reasonable time - Forfeiture not valid if after filing of petition against dismissal and cashiering - Show cause notices were not quashed on ground of delay but same were proposed to be considered on merits.
[Para 15]

Army Act, 1950, Section 192 - Pension Regulations of the Army (Part I), (1961), Regulation 16(a) - Pensionary benefits - Forfeiture will take effect from date on which benefits accrued and become payable.
[Para 16]

Army Act, 1950, Section 192 - Pension Regulations of the Army (Part I) (1961), Regulation 16(a) - Pensionary benefits - Denying pension paid out to petitioners solely because of punishment imposed by Court martial not acceptable - Satisfactory prior service must also be taken into account - Unless the nature of the offence committed is so grave as to wipe out years of satisfactory service, pensionary benefits should be granted in full - Orders of forfeiture quashed.
