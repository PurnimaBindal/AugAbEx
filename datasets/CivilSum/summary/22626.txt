Recruitment - Enlargement of zone of consideration - The decision to enlarge the zone of consideration and lower cut off marks without reason smacks of arbitrariness and prejudiced the appellant.
The prevailing rules at the time of vacancies should ordinarily be followed, including eligibility criteria and procedures.
The appointing authority used its power for an unauthorized purpose, which would amount to malice in law.
The selection has been set aside.

Recruitment - Post of watchmen/Messenger/Attender - Allocation of 50% marks for interview was misused and demonstrated favoritism.
The duties of the post do not require a high level of intellectual ability or trait evaluation, and the allocation of interview marks contravened the ratio laid out in Ashok Kumar Yadav's case.
The selection has been set aside.
