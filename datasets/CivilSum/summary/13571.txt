Constitution of India, Article 226 - Departmental enquiry show cause notice - The finding of guilt recorded by the Disciplinary authority is not tentative or provisional.
However, if a tentative finding of guilt is arrived at, the Disciplinary Authority can consider the matter afresh and issue a second show cause notice.
The petitioner is entitled to make a representation against the finding of guilt as well as against the proposed penalty.
If any representation against the finding of guilt is made alongside the representation against the proposed penalty, the Disciplinary Authority is obliged to consider it dispassionately on merits while taking a decision on the question of penalty.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Departmental proceeding - Retirement - A new point cannot be entertained at this stage, involving a question of fact, namely, whether the petitioner has already retired or not.
The petitioner can, however, raise this point before the Disciplinary Authority.
