In cases where a Govt. servant is subject to a stoppage of increments that is passed contrary to the provisions of Rules and principles of natural justice, the order is deemed void and the Govt. servant has the right to claim arrears of pay and allowances that became due due to the void order.
[Para 5]
A Govt. servant's right to claim wages cannot be interrupted, even by a void order.
Claims for arrears of pay can be made up to the age of superannuation and are subject to a limitation period of three years.
[Para 5]
In order for a Govt. servant to be deprived of his right to claim salary, there must be a valid order; if the order is void, then the Govt. servant need not secure a declaration.
[Para 5]
Claims for arrears of pay and allowances that are filed within the limitation period can be decreed by ignoring the void order.
[Para 5]
If a void order imposes a stoppage of increments with cumulative effect, a Govt. servant is entitled to arrears of pay and allowances that were due three years prior to the date on which the suit was filed.
[Para 6]
If a notice under Section 80 of the Civil Procedure Code is given, the period for filing suit is extended and the respondent is entitled to two months' pay for the notice period.
Once the suit is instituted, the respondent is entitled to all arrears of pay and allowances.
[Para 7]
Orders imposing a penalty of stoppage of increments without conducting an enquiry cannot be sustained.
