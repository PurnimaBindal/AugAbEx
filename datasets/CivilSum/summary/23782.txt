Limitation Act, 1963, Article 113 - Constitution of India, Article 311 - Dismissal of employee - Suit against dismissal - The suit must be filed within three years - The time limit is determined by the residuary Article 113 of the Limitation Act - The argument that there is no limitation to sue for a declaration of dismissal as void or inoperative cannot be accepted.
