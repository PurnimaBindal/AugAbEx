Admission/Education - Admission to M.D./M.S.
Diploma courses and to MDS Courses - Allotment of seats - Under the existing system, All India quota candidates must specify eight preferred medical colleges and six subjects for admission - Admissions are based on merit, preference, and eligibility criteria using computer systems - A waiting list equivalent to 10% of the seats will be created to account for non-joining candidates - We support proposed modifications suggested by the Director General of Health Services, New Delhi.
[Para 4] These changes include: conducting the All India PG Entrance Examination on the second Sunday of January, results announced by the third week of February, the merit list containing candidates up to the number of available seats, a waiting list of successful candidates with more than 50% marks, in-person allotment from March 1-15, 15 days for candidates to join allotted institutions, and forfeiting claims for a seat under the All India Scheme if not appearing for allotment on the specified dates or not joining the allotted course and college by March 31.
