Constitution of India, Articles 226 - Replication - New plea - When a respondent has not had an opportunity to meet new pleas on facts, no new plea can be allowed to be raised in the replication - The petitioner can only rebut the new pleas raised in the written statement.
[Para 1] B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Cancellation of promotion - Natural justice - Stopgap arrangement - The petitioners were class IV employees who were requested to work against the posts of Laboratory Attendants as a stopgap arrangement temporarily - The orders of their adjustment were cancelled to effect regular promotions as per seniority - Persons who were more than ten years senior were promoted - As the petitioners were promoted just for a stopgap arrangement in the exigencies of service, they had no rights against the posts - They were reverted in terms of their appointment letters - There was no question of a violation of principles of natural justice - The order passed was to do justice to the seniors - There was no interference in writ jurisdiction.
