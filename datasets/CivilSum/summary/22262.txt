Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300-A - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 13 - Confirmation - Pension - Appellant was appointed Chief of Police in Dadra and Nagar Haveli in 1954 - Worked in various positions before retiring as Deputy Superintendent of Police - Although a permanent post of Chief of Police was declared in 1967, the appellant was not confirmed due to absence of Recruitment Rules - Despite having served for over twenty years, the appellant was denied pension as he had worked on officiating basis throughout his career - The court directed the respondents to treat him as a confirmed employee and grant pension and other post-retiral benefits.
