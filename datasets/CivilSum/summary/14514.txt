Constitution of India, Article 226 - Creation of posts - Filling of vacancies - The employer has the responsibility to decide the number of posts or vacancies needed for the work required - The court cannot intervene in the employer's administration until the employer's policy decision is deemed fair and just - Petitioners' argument that the number of posts cannot be decreased due to an increase in targets is not a valid reason for the court to issue a direction under Article 226 of the Constitution.
