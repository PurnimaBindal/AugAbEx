Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2(1) - Dismissal from service - Quantum of punishment - A constable in police was charged for consuming alcohol and abusing people in a drunken condition, resulting in a penalty of stoppage of four increments.
The appellate authority issued him a show cause notice as to why the penalty could not be enhanced to dismissal.
He was ultimately dismissed from service, despite no evidence indicating that he was on duty when he consumed alcohol.
Despite receiving 24 commendation certificates over 15 years of service, the DGP passed the order of dismissal without regard to his past service.
The penalty of dismissal is disproportionate, and the loss of salary for a period of 10 years would be an adequate punishment.
Dismissal was quashed, and reinstatement with continuity but without back wages was ordered.
