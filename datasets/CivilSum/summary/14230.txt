Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act was violated when a mass retrenchment was carried out without following the proper procedures.
The employer challenged a labour court's decision to award 25% back wages and reinstatement, claiming that the casual daily wage workers could not be considered workmen under the Act due to the absence of appointment letters.
However, the court deemed the workmen's claim as admitted as it remained unrebutted, leading to the dismissal of the employer's petition.
