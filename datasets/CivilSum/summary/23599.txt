Torts - Damages - Operation of patient in Govt. Medical Hospital - A mop was left in the patient's body, causing the formation of pus and subsequently leading to the patient's death - Negligence on the part of the hospital was established - The government is vicariously liable - The doctrine of re ipsa loquitur is applicable - The state is responsible for paying damages - It cannot be argued which doctor or staff member acted negligently.
