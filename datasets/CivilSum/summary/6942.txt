According to Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India, the withdrawal of a resignation is valid until the effective date.
If an authority accepts a resignation that has been withdrawn, it would be unjustified.
The order accepting the petitioner's resignation is quashed.
[Paras 6 and 8]
A petitioner who is under suspension when an order of resignation is passed can continue disciplinary proceedings against them through an interim order.
If the petitioner is ultimately removed from service and doesn't challenge the order, they must be treated as under suspension and entitled to subsistence allowance until the removal order is passed.
The court held the acceptance of resignation as illegal and quashed the order.
