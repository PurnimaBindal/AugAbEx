Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 173 - Disability Pension - Finality of decisions - Once the Medical Board has determined the disability is not service-related, and an appeal against this determination is rejected, the decision attains finality - Re-approaching the same authority which has already adjudicated the claim will not extend time limitations - A writ petition filed after an unreasonable delay subsequent to the finality of the authority's decisions, with no explanation for the delay, is subject to dismissal.
