Response: Charu Tuli, AAG, Punjab, refers to Constitution of India, Article 226 and Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Part I, Rule 2.2(c) regarding the withholding of retirement dues, such as pension, gratuity, and GPF, during the pendency of a departmental inquiry.
While withholding of gratuity during the inquiry is justifiable, non-payment of provisional pension cannot be.
The employer is obligated to pay provisional pension as per rule 2.2.(c).
The petitioner, who stands retired from service, has not received their pension, gratuity, and GPF due to the ongoing inquiry.
The respondent is directed to release the provisional pension to the petitioner and finalize the inquiry proceedings within six months.
