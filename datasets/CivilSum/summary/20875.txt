Under the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 77-A r/w Employees' State Insurance (General) Regulations, 1950, Regulation 26, the appellant was required to make contributions to the fund of the Corporation from 2811968 to 31.10.1979.
A demand was made on 1.12.1982 and it was found that the demand was not barred by limitation, as the bar of limitation was not applicable under the Act.
