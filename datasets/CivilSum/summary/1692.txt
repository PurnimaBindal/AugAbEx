Constitution of India, Articles 15(4) and 16(4) - Reservation - Appointment to the post of Forest Range Officer - Quota fixed for reserved as well as general category - Appointment of reserved category candidates against posts intended for general category - Reserved category candidates appointed on the basis of higher marks, against the instructions - General category candidates to be given the vacant posts due to appointments of reserved category candidates in the general quota seats, as per the issued directives.
