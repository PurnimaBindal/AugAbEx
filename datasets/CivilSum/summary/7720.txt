Under Section 79(c) of the Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948, the Board has the power to frame regulations regarding the condition of service.
Clause (C) specifically allows the Board to make regulations for charging penal rent for not vacating staff quarters.
This was deemed to be within the condition of service and within the Board's power to regulate.

According to Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, the court cannot strike down legislation or enactment as arbitrary or unreasonable unless it is proven that such actions violate the constitutionally protected rights of citizens.
[Para 8]
In regard to penal rent, relief granted to some ineligible persons does not entitle other similarly situated, ineligible persons to seek similar treatment.
