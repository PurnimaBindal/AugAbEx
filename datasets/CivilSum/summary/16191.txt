Punjab Civil Service (Revised Pay) Rules, 1988, Rule 3 - Condition of service - Pay fixation Rules do not lay down the condition of service nor they are issued under Article 309 of the Constitution of India - Any stipulation or embargo incorporated in such Rules affecting the rights of the members of service will not have any statutory effect - The rule that "Junior Draftsman shall be eligible for promotion as Draftsman after a minimum period of twelve years" does not pertain to the method of recruitment to the post of Draftsman as the rules are meant for fixation of salary only.
[Para 4 and 6] B.
In the absence of Service Rules, executive instructions can be issued to govern the recruitment to the service - If the appointing authority recommends a candidate's name for promotion after considering his eligibility, such action cannot be deemed illegal or violative of any Rule.
[Para 5] C. In case of promotion of a physically disabled person against a reserved post, if the competent authority finds his claim in order after due consideration and promotes him, such promotion cannot be undone without issuing him show-cause notice and complying with the principles of natural justice.
[Para 12] D Article 41 of the Constitution of India aims at providing fundamental rights such as the right to work and education and public assistance, and recognizes that all human beings are not created equally by God - Therefore, once a physically disabled person is considered and promoted in accordance with governmental policy and his work is satisfactory, he should be allowed to continue in that post until he is found unfit.
