The Punjab Civil Services (First Amendment) Rules, Vol-I, 1997, Rule 3.12(2) and the Punjab State District Consumer Disputes Redressal Forums Class-III and Class-IV Services Regulations, 1999 mention rules regarding appointment by transfer, deputation, lien on substantive post, and repatriation.
Following the 1997 Amendment in Punjab Civil Services Rules, the lien in the substantive post in the parent department should remain only for two years and will automatically terminate.
After this period, the deputationist cannot be repatriated on the alleged substantive post from which their lien stood terminated, as this would mean the termination of their services if the post is no longer available.
In a specific case, an order of repatriation was quashed, and the authority directed to take the petitioner back on the post of Distt.
Forum with all consequential benefits of continuity and back-wages.
