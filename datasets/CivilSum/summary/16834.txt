Under Section 25 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, regarding retrenchment, payment of wages through pay order is legitimate.
The section doesn't mention paying in cash and no excessive wages were given during the notice period as the succeeding month had fewer working days.
[Paras 6 and 7]
According to Section 25G of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the rule of 'last come first go' must be followed for retrenchment.
An employer complied with this by making a seniority list that listed employees by category and grade, posted 23 days before the retrenchment.
No objections were raised, thus it's impossible and too late to say Section 25G was violated.
