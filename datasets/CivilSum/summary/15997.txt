Response : The petitioner's claim for leave encashment was granted, but he was not entitled to seven days' earned leave as he had worked during the vacation period.
However, an employee is entitled to claim earned leave if he is prevented from availing himself of the full vacation.
The action of the respondents in ordering the recovery of honorarium was illegal and violative of principles of natural justice.
Similarly, the withholding of security without affording the petitioner an opportunity was considered as imposing a penalty on the petitioner, and hence his claim for refund of security is well-merited.
