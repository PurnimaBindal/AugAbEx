Trivandrum Port & Headload Workers' Co-op.
Society Ltd v. Dhanesh Kumar Workman Compensation Act, 1923, Section 2(i)(h) - Substitute Workman - Eligibility for compensation - It is legally established that being a substitute workman does not affect eligibility for compensation if the individual is engaged in the trade or business of the principal employer - The most important factor is whether the worker was hired for the service of the principal employer, and if so, any accidents that occur during the course of employment must be compensated - The appellants in this case cannot evade their responsibility, as they have already agreed to be liable in the event of an accident - The appeal has been dismissed.
