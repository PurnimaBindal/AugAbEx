Indian Administrative Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulation, 1955, Regulations 7 and 9 - Promotion - Requirement of no deterioration certificate - Departmental proceedings - Delay in promotion due to non-issuance of certificate by State Government - No junior promoted ahead of appellant during delay - Delay not without reason - Appellant not entitled to claim salary of higher post for period not worked - Seniority unaffected - No justification for interfering with Tribunal's decision.
