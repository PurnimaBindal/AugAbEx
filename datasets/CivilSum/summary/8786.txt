Section 36 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 only applies to proceedings under the Act, and thus cannot be invoked to give relief to a workman in a domestic enquiry.
A Labour Court erred in directing the petitioner to recast certified Standing Orders in light of Section 36.
[Para 7]
Standings Orders must make provision for termination of employment, including the right of the workman to defend himself effectively.
However, there is no right for a workman to be represented by a legal practitioner or person of his choice who is not employed by the employer.
The Labour Court's direction to recast the Standing Orders in light of Section 36 cannot be sustained and is quashed.
