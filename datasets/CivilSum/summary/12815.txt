Constitution of India, Article 311 - AP Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1961, Rule 40 - AP Small Scale Industrial Development Corporation Staff Regulations, Regulation 43 - Double jeopardy - Prohibition against double punishment for the same set of allegations - Additional penalty cannot be imposed after a disciplinary inquiry has concluded with one penalty already imposed - Power of revision does not allow for infliction of a second punishment for identical charges.
