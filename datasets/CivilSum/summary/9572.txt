Constitution of India Article, 311(2) (b) - Punjab Police Rules, Rule 1624 (ix) (b) - Dismissal - Petitioners' dismissal from service was conducted without an enquiry on the grounds that they were dangerous and desperate employees - However, it was held that the dismissal could not be justified and is therefore quashed - Petitioners are entitled to all consequential benefits - Respondents are permitted to proceed against the petitioners in accordance with the law.
