Employees Rules, 2006 - Compassionate appointment - Financial assistance - If a request for compassionate appointment under the 2003 Rules is declined because of the implementation of the 2006 Rules, which offer financial assistance instead of compassionate appointment, then the authorities must give the applicant the choice to accept the prescribed financial assistance amount.
