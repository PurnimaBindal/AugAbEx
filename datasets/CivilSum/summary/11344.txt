According to the Panjab University Regulations for B.A./B.Sc.
exams, Regulation 41(iii), admission to both B.Sc.
Part I and Part II is at the student's own risk and responsibility.
The petitioner in this case was found ineligible to appear in B.Sc.
Part I due to less than 40% marks in 10 + 2 exams.
As a result, his provisional admission to B.Sc.
Part II was cancelled and his name was struck off from the college rolls.
Despite the delay in informing him of the decision, the petitioner does not have the right to demand a declaration of his B.Sc.
Part I exam result or seek direction from the High Court to the University.
