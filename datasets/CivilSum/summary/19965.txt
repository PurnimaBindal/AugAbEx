Punjab Financial Corporation (Staff) Regulations 1961 - Regulation 41(1)(2) - Departmental Enquiry - The writ petitioner was cleared of all charges by the Enquiry officer, but the Managing Director remanded the matter back to him for clarification.
The delinquent employee did not protest and participated in the proceedings.
However, the inquiry officer found the delinquent guilty leading to dismissal from service.
Dismissal order was set aside by the High Court as prejudice was caused to the delinquent employee.
The impugned order of the High Court was set-aside and the matter was remitted to it to decide whether failure to supply the report of the Inquiry Officer had prejudiced the delinquent employee.
