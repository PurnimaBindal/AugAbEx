Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 9-A - Settlement - Conditions of service - Changes in - The writ court is not an appropriate forum or remedy for withholding of allowances and reduction of retirement age from 60 to 55 years.
- A dispute regarding the validity of notice can be raised under the Industrial Disputes Act and settled under the recognized and permissible settlement under the Act.
- The remedy under Article 226 is not available, and it is open for the workmen to agitate their claim before the conciliation officer under the Act on all such grounds, whether the settlement has been arrived at under pressure and compulsion is a question of dispute of facts that cannot be examined in writ proceedings, especially considering that more than 98% of employees have accepted the settlement, and conciliation proceedings are still not closed.
