Constitution of India, Article 226 - Departmental proceedings - Natural Justice - The petitioner was not provided with many relevant documents related to the charge-sheet.
Additionally, no personal hearing was given after material was collected behind his back, and he was not given the opportunity to cross-examine witnesses against him.
This violation of natural justice principles would render the inquiry invalid.
[Paras 16 and 17]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Departmental Enquiry - Although not binding, the judgment of a criminal case should be taken into consideration as a piece of evidence in a disciplinary proceeding.
