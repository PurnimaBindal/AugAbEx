The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Pay-scale - A merger of Hindi Typist and LDC cadres resulted in a reduction of the pay-scale - However, a tribunal order stipulated the scale should continue - Despite arguments for a pay-scale reduction to Rs. 3200-4900 and to effect recovery, the court ruled against this - Thus, the petition was dismissed.
