Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Reservation - Post of Assistant Professor - Reserved category candidates can compete for non-reserve posts - If a candidate from a reserved category is appointed based on seniority and merit, they are treated as promoted/appointed from the general category, even if they belong to a reserved category.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Court directions in writ petition for claim of petitioner as selected candidate for post - Direction not complied with - Order final - Once the petitioner is held as selected, there cannot be any deviation without valid justification - Court-issued directions must be strictly followed and are not subject to questioning by the commission or state government.
