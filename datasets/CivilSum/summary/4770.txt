Constitution of India, Article 226 - Casual labourers - Order granting temporary status - Setting aside of - Petitioners argued that temporary status was granted in accordance with scheme dated 10.9.1993; however, judgment in Dhani Rams case found that the scheme was not continuous - Petitioners did not meet the criteria of one year of service as of 1.9.93 - Setting aside impugned order would result in revival of order conferring temporary status, which petitioner are not entitled to - In such cases, principles of natural justice are not required to be followed.
