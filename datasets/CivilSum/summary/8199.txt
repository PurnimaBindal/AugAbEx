Constitution of India, Article 226 - Selection - Ad hoc Appointment - If a post is to be filled up on a regular basis, an ad hoc employee has the right to be considered for it - The petitioner in C.W.P.
773/99 was considered but not selected - No flaws in the selection process have been identified - The petitioner is solely claiming the right to be absorbed on a regular basis - The petitioner cannot grieve about their non-selection - However, if a vacancy arises on a regular basis in the future, the petitioner may be considered by ignoring the bar and giving age relaxation based on the period of their service with the respondent.
[Paras 7 and 9]
