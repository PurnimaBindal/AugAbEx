Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 122 - Seniority - Cadre - The seniority of police constables in the Telecommunication Cadre will be determined by Rule 122 of the Punjab Police Rules and will be based on their initial appointment in the Police - this method is fair, equitable, and excludes any discretion, arbitrariness, or favoritism.
[Para 6]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Natural justice - Notification of a hearing is not necessary to change a seniority list that has not been determined, issued, prepared, and circulated by a competent authority.
[Para 7]  C Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 122 - Seniority - Promotion - Guidelines from a senior officer can be sought by a competent authority for the promotion of constables to Head Constables - promotions must be granted purely on the basis of seniority-cum-merit, and persons with adverse records or pending departmental inquiries should not be promoted - this criteria is not illegal.
