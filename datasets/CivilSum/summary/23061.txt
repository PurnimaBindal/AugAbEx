The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 seeks to ensure fairness in the selection process, particularly when it comes to competition and reservation.
When the recruitment agency is left with only one eligible candidate in an open selection, the vacancy cannot be filled as there is no choice left.
The tribunal has the authority to nullify such a selection, particularly when the advertisement explicitly states that in such circumstances, the vacancy will be unreserved and offered to candidates from other reserved categories.

To ensure that the selection process adheres to the principle of fair play, justice, and equal opportunity enshrined in Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution, the concerned department must call eligible candidates from the open market through newspapers and radio and television announcements, in addition to the Employment Exchange.
This will provide a wider choice of the best talent to subserve the public interest better.
