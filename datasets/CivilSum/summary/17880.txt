Constitution of India, Article 14 - Adverse remarks/Review - The order expunging adverse remarks created a right for the petitioner that couldn't be taken away without notice or hearing.
The order was reviewed without notice to the petitioner, which could have prevented the petitioner from keeping the expunged remarks intact.
[Paras 3 and 4]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion - Authorities must present employee's case with required information for promotion consideration and reconsideration upon change of circumstances.
Inaction on the part of the authorities should not negatively affect the employee.
After the expungement of adverse remarks, the employee's case of promotion should have been considered.
[Para 5]
