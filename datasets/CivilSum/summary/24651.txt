The Haryana Service of Engineers Class I, P.W.D.
(Public Health Branch) Rules, 1961, Rules 22 and 6 - Promotion - Qualifications - Power to relax.
The government has the power to relax the condition in Rule 6(b) as to experience of 8 years not only in individual cases but also to meet particular situations, such as a large number of vacancies in Class I.
The judgment and order of the Division Bench is set aside and that of the single Judge recorded - Appeal allowed.
