Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 151 - Acquittal - Service benefits - Claim of - An order of dismissal based on conviction was quashed.
Monetary benefits were granted from the date of acquittal until the death of the employee while the petition was being processed.
The argument that the period between suspension and acquittal should be taken into account for pay determination was rejected.
It was held that acquittal would restore the employee to the same position as prior to the case being lodged.
Consequently, he is entitled to all previous arrears, including regular increments and allowances, as if he was never suspended.
