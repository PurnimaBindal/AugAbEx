Constitution of India, Article 226 - Backwages - After the Labour Court determined that the respondents did not provide sufficient evidence of misconduct, it was discovered that the departmental enquiry was conducted without following the rules of natural justice as evidence led by management was excluded without proper basis - Withholding backwages in such circumstances would be supporting the arbitrary decision of management - The withholding of backwages has been quashed in this award and the workman has been directed to be reinstated with full back wages.
