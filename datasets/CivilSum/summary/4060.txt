Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Domestic enquiry - Natural justice - The management did not offer the petitioner any chances to cross-examine the witness it presented - Moreover, the witness merely produced the file without proving any fact - Consequently, the file should not have been admitted as evidence without proof - The enquiry that followed lacked substance - The Award of Labour Court upholding it is thus null and void.
