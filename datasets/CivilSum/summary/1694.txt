As per the Karnataka Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 and the Constitution of India, Article 226 and 323-A, the dispute regarding service matters of civil servants is not within the jurisdiction of the High Court.
As for daily wage employees, the Tribunal has the exclusive jurisdiction in cases where the relationship between the State and the employee is a matter of service.
Hence, a writ in the High Court is not maintainable and the jurisdiction lies with the Tribunal.
[Paras 3, 4, 5, 8 and 10]
