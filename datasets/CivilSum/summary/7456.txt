Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 10 - Second reference - Maintainability - The Labour Court dismissed the second reference without considering the case's merits, declaring it illegal - The Supreme Court overturned the judgment relied upon by the Labour Court - The second reference is admissible because the Supreme Court ruled that the government could make a second reference to maintain industrial peace, rather than simply settling the dispute.
