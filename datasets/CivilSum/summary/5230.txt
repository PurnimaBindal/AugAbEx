As per Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(a) and 10(1)(c), Reinstatement with full back wages can be granted to workmen without inordinate delays, even if management withholds the best evidence.
Back wages cannot be denied based on speculation.
[Paras 4 & 5]

According to Constitution of India, Article 226 and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1)(c), if the best evidence is withheld, an adverse inference must be drawn.
