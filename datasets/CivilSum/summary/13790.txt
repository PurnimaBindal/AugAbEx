According to regulations governing army pensions, in the event of an Army personnel's death, the family pension should be granted.
If the widow remarries, she is still entitled to receive the pension as she falls under the first category.
However, the deceased's mother is not eligible to receive the same.
It is important to note that there is no provision in the law indicating that the widow loses her right to the pension upon remarriage.
