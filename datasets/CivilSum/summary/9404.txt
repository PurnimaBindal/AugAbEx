A Masters of Arts student's viva voce result was cancelled due to the belief that the viva marks have a correlation with written marks.
However, statistical analysis showed that there was no direct correlation between the two.
The argument that the viva marks were deliberately lowered also lacks a solid foundation.
It is common knowledge that a student may perform well in some areas but not in others.
The purpose of the viva and the written tests are distinct.
Even if there was unfair grading, it would not be justified to hold a new viva only for three students.
The examination occurred in 2004, so interference would be disruptive to what has already been settled.
