Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 10 and 11A - Reference - Scope of - Labour Court received dispute whether non-employment of union members was justified - Court ruled for reinstatement with backwages - However, the order is not sustainable as it is beyond the scope of reference without a finding on the justification of the non-employment - The matter is sent back to the Labour Court for reconsideration - Petition is granted accordingly.
