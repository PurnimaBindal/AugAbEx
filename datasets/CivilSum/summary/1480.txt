Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33C(2) - Claim for difference of wages - Petitioner claimed to have worked as an Artist despite being appointed as a Painter and sought the difference in wages - Employer contested the claim, stating that the petitioner was not qualified to be an Artist - The claim under Section 33C(2) required determination of the fact whether the petitioner was indeed performing the work of an Artist - The order of the Labour Court to pay the difference in wages was overturned.
