Constitution of India, Article 226 - Academic Regulations of College, Regulation 4 - Education - Attendance - Absence - Condonation of absence - If a student attended only 29 lectures instead of the prescribed minimum of 35, the regulation permits the principal to condone up to 10% of the absence.
However, if the absence exceeds 10%, the principal does not have the authority to condone it.
[Paras 8 to 10]
