Constitution of India, Article 14 - Family pension - To determine pensionary benefits or family pension for a deceased employee, the ad hoc service should be considered.
Denying family pension to the family members of a person who worked for almost nine years and was appointed on a regular basis prior to death, despite being eligible for a grant of family pension, is unreasonable.
