Industrial Disputes Act, Section 11-A - A Conductor has been accused of embezzling Rs. 190/- by charging passengers but not issuing tickets - After investigation and a personal hearing, the Conductor's services were terminated - A plea for leniency is rejected - The Court will not protect those who have misused funds and lost their moral way - The verdict stands, and the petition is dismissed.
