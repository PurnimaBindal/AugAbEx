Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Re-designation - The petitioner was selected and appointed as an Art and Craft Teacher, but was later re-designated as a Sewing Teacher after 30 years.
The order of re-designation is not acceptable, and the decision in CWP No. 6306 of 1991 Nirmal Kaur v. The State of Punjab & others on 2711992 has been followed.
