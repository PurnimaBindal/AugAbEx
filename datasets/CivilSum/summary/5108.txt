Constitution of India, Article 16 - UP Recruitment of Dependant of Government Servant Dying in Harness Rules, 1974, Rule 5 - Appointment on compassionate grounds - The petitioner's deceased spouse worked for over 15 years as a part-time Tubewell Operator.
While similarly situated individuals who worked with the petitioner's spouse were regularized, the petitioner's spouse passed away before regularization.
As a result, if the petitioner-wife possesses the necessary qualifications, the advantage of compassionate appointment may be extended to her.
