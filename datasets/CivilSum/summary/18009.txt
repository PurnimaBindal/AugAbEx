Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - salary/Equal pay for equal work/Pension and gratuity - The petitioner, who was appointed as a peon on a regular pay scale in 1963, was later given fixed wages at the rate fixed by the DC However, there was no order by the competent authority to reduce the salary.
Mere stoppage of increments is considered a penalty.
According to rules, the petitioner is entitled to full salary in a regular pay scale, increments, and other benefits.
The petitioner is also entitled to all retiral benefits treating him in regular service from 1963.
Additionally, he is owed arrears to be paid with 12% interest.
The cost for this comes to Rs. 5000/-.
