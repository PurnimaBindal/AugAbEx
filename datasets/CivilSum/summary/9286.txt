Punjab Agricultural Service (Class I) Rules, 1974, Rules 9 and 11 - Promotion by selection - Seniority is not the only factor - Promotees cannot solely claim promotion based on seniority - The worth of the selectee must be assessed based on annual confidential reports which is an objective basis - The promotion system aims to secure the best possible candidate.
[Paras 11, 12 and 13]

Constitution of India, Article 16 Promotion/Efficiency Bar/Adverse Confidential Reports - An adverse entry prior to crossing the efficiency bar is not completely erased and can be considered for promotion to the next rank - An adverse entry that does not prevent crossing the efficiency bar is still relevant for promotion to the higher rank.
[Paras 14 and 16]
