Punjab Medical Education Service (Class-I) Rules, 1978, Rule 7(2), Appendix 'C' - Criteria for promotion from Senior Lecturer (Surgery) to Assistant Professor (Surgery) - Teaching experience - Rule requires teaching experience at a medical college as Senior Lecturer, Lecturer/Registrar, Research Officer - Experience as Medical Officer (Post Partum) is not considered teaching experience - Ineligible persons without experience cannot be promoted over juniors who meet the criteria - Seniority begins from the date of promotion/appointment to the post.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Punjab Medical Education Service (Class-I) Rules, 1978, Rule 7(2), Appendix C - Promotion/Seniority/Experience/Officiating service - Officiating service on a promotional post counts towards experience on that post upon regularization - This experience is taken into consideration for promotion eligibility.
