The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Recruitment - Applications sent through proper channel that are delayed for receipt - Petitioners shall not be treated unfavorably due to default committed by departmental authorities in non-receipt of their applications within stipulated time.
[Para 4]
The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Selection - Necessary parties - Pleadings - The petitioner cannot be granted relief since they neither challenged the selection nor included the selected candidates in their petition and cannot amend their petition at this stage.
