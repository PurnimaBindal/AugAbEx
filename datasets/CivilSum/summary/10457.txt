Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Air Force Act, 1950, Section 27 - Promotion - Adverse remarks - Merely absent adverse remarks in the ARs is insufficient to determine fitness for advancement from Air Commodore to Air Vice Marshal - Evidence of the case of the petitioner considered by the Promotion Board of 13 Air Marshals as well as his statutory complaint being considered and later rejected by reasoned order - Judging the recommendations of the Promotion Board does not fall within the High Court's purview as an appellate Board.
