Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 Sections 4 and 19 (as inserted by Amendment Act 1984) - Loss of earning capacity - The Commissioner cannot evaluate the loss of earning capability without the aid of a qualified medical practitioner - In case of disagreement by any party, the Commissioner can rely on another qualified medical practitioner's evaluation to make a judgment.
[Paras 15, 16 and 25]  B. Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 Sections 2, 4 and 19 - Loss of earning capacity - The loss of earning capacity should be assessed with reference to "all the work" that the worker was capable of performing at the time of the accident, and not just to the work being performed at the time of the accident.
[Paras 23 and 25]  C. Interpretation of Statutes - When interpreting a statute, the primary concern is the language employed by the legislature - the plain and rational meaning of each sentence, word, and phrase should be interpreted in relation to the Act's general aim - It is the duty of the judicial interpreter to provide a fair and honest interpretation of the legislature's language.
