Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Result - Cancellation of - Change in Examination Centre - The petitioner's result was cancelled on the grounds that they had filled in the wrong centre on their own but the exam superintendent allowed them to appear at the wrong location.
The roll no. slip required filling in by the student and the change of centre needed permission from the superintendent.
Both the petitioner and the board were held liable as they displayed contributory negligence.
However, the board was directed to fill in the roll no. slip in its entirety in the future and declare the result of the petitioner.
