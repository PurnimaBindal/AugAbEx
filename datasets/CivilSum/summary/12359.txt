Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Approval of - Regularisation - Claim for - `Doctrine of Reasonable Expectation' - Petitioner became an Assistant teacher on an additional post, subject to the approval of D.I.S.
in 1982 - Approval was not granted until 1992 - However, a person who worked for 21 years as a teacher, even against an unapproved post, has a reasonable expectation to be regularized in due time - Therefore, the Doctrine of Reasonable Expectation cannot be disregarded - The Respondents are ordered to consider the case of an additional post of Assistant teacher and to approve and regularize the appointment - On such appointment, D.I.S.
is directed to give consent for the same.
