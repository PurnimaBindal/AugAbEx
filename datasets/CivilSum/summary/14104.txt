Compassionate appointment was offered to the petitioner on a Class-IV post, but the petitioner is claiming a Class-III post according to qualifications.
Circulars indicate that compassionate appointment can be made for either Class-III or Class-IV posts.
It was held that the provision is meant to assist the family of the deceased in coping with sudden crises and cannot be used to convert the appointment process.
