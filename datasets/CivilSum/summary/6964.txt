According to Section 9A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, an employee can retire at the age of 56 years.
However, Rule 11 allows for service benefits to be granted until the employee reaches the age of 58 years.
Despite this, the labour court's order did not explain how it arrived at the conclusion that the respondent's retirement age was 58 years.
The court also contended that changing the retirement age would violate Section 9A of the 1947 Act as it is a change in service conditions.
However, the court's finding was unwarranted and could not be sustained as the Apex Court in Harmohinder Singh's case held that fixing the retirement age does not fall under the ambit of the 4th Schedule mentioned in Section 9A.
