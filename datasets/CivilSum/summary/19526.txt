Punjab Agricultural Produce Markets Act, 1961, Section 20(2) Proviso - Promotion - Auction Recorder - The petitioner was promoted from Clerk to Auction Recorder with a pay scale of Rs.450-800, but kept in the scale of clerk of Rs. 400-600.
The committee approved the grant of pay scale of Rs. 450-800 from the date of promotion and passed a resolution to this effect.
The approval of the Board was only required in cases where the pay scale of the post was Rs. 500 or more.
As the committee had already approved the grant of pay scale of Rs. 450-800, no further approval of the Board was required, and the petitioner is entitled to that scale from the date of his promotion as Auction Recorder.
