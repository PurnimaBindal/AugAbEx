J&K Constitution, Sections 104 and 126 - J&K Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 33 - Termination of services of a Police Constable due to unauthorized absence from duty - The petitioner was not afforded any opportunity of a hearing or an inquiry before the impugned order was passed - The person charged with misconduct has a right to receive a statement of allegations - The petitioner had also taken the statutory remedy of appeal as soon as he became aware of the discharge order - Therefore, the petition cannot be dismissed solely on the grounds of delay - As a result, the impugned order is quashed and the respondents are directed to reinstate the petitioner into service.
