Kerala Toddy Welfare Fund Act, 1969, Section 2(c) - `Employer' - Liability to pay contribution to the fund - Definition of employer - The definition refers to a person who employs any person, whether directly or through any other person or whether on behalf of himself or any other person, as an employer.
The employment by any person can be for himself or for any other person.
Mere association with the conduct of the business of an establishment or shop does not mean that the person has employed the workers on his behalf.
In the absence of evidence to the contrary, the intermediary persons cannot be held liable as employers when the principal employer admits to employing the workers.
The law pre-supposes the conduct of legal business and cannot be interpreted to frustrate the objective of the Act and violate the statutory provision of law.
Holding the appellants as employers vis-a-vis the licensee's shop would facilitate the violation of the Kerala Abkari Shops Act and the Rules framed thereunder.
The High Court's judgment is not sustainable as it takes a general view of the matter without reference to the purpose and object of the Act and the law under which the licence to run the shop was granted.
