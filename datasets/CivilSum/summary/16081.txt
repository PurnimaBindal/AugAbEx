Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Chapter 23, Rule 23.9 - History sheet - quashing of - The mere fact that the petitioner has been involved in five criminal cases for which he was eventually acquitted does not authorize the respondents to use his history sheet.
To be considered habitually addicted to crime, one must have a conviction recorded against their name, not simply be accused of a crime.
The rule necessitates that a person be habitually addicted to crime, and not just accused of a crime.
Therefore, the history sheet of the petitioner drawn by respondents should be quashed according to Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India.
