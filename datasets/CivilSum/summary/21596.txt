Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Departmental proceedings/Natural Justice/Misconduct - Enquiry - The constable was discharged from service by the SP on the grounds of habitual absenteeism, negligence, and indiscipline, which cast a stigma on the constable's career.
The order for punishment could not be passed without giving the constable an opportunity to explain, and therefore the order of discharge is flawed due to a manifest error of law.
[Para 5]  B Discharge from service on the ground of misconduct of constable - The principles of natural justice were not followed, and the High Court quashed the order and directed the constable be reinstated.
The employer is at liberty to proceed afresh in accordance with the law and may pass any order after providing the employee with an opportunity to explain - the High Court's order was modified.
