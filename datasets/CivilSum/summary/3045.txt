The Employment Exchanges (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959 and related rules mandate that Employment Exchanges be notified of job openings.
Appellant failed to select candidates for unskilled labourer positions from a pool of six hundred candidates sponsored by the Employment Exchange.
Appellant then sat on the select list for years and failed to follow proper procedures.
Selected candidates are entitled to Rs. 10,000 in damages each due to appellant's actions, which were not justified.
