Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 36-A Award - The petitioner, a Clerk-cum-Cashier, was selected for the post of Probation Officer, but the bank offered appointment on the condition that he resign from his current post before accepting this promotion.
However, there were no rules in place requiring the resignation from the first post while accepting a promotional post through direct recruitment at the relevant time.
The Industrial Tribunal gave an award in favor of the petitioner, directing the management to offer the next available vacancy in the cadre of Officer's Grade.
A writ petition under Article 226 is maintainable, and the petitioner is entitled to promotion post with the protection of his pay that he was already drawing.
[Paras 7,8 and 9]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Non-implementation of award of Labour Tribunal is a controversy that the High Court has wide powers to give redress for where the employer is not even prepared to follow the award of Labour Tribunal.
Otherwise, it will reduce the decision of the award of a tribunal to mere waste paper, shattering the confidence of the workmen in semi-judicial institutions, which would have serious repercussions on the administration of justice - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 36-A [Para 10]
