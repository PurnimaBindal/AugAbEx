Constitution of India, Article 16 - Compulsory retirement - Overturning of order - The order of compulsory retirement against the petitioner was set aside by the Tribunal and upheld by the Supreme Court, with no court directing the denial of actual benefits like pay, pay fixation, promotion, etc. - Failure to provide benefits after the order of compulsory retirement was overturned was deemed improper.
