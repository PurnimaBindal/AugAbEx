Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Promotion/Representation - The representation of the petitioners has not been decided - Juniors were promoted without considering their cases favourably - Subsequent events may also be considered instead of the pending representation - The court has directed that the decision be made within 3 months and that the petitioners be considered for promotion to the post of Labour-cum-Conciliation Officer from the date their juniors were promoted, if their representation is found favourable.
