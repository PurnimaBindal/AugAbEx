Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 335 Admission - Reservation - Promissory estoppel Admission to MBBS Course - A petitioner declared himself as "forward class" then later as "Scheduled Tribe" - Commissioner Tribal Welfare is investigating whether he belongs to Scheduled Tribe - Petitioner got provisional admission for the first year pending verification of social status - Petitioner not cooperating to complete the enquiry - Social status must be cleared before petitioner can continue studies - Promissory Estoppel does not apply as it only covers cases where withdrawal of an assurance caused harm, which is not the situation in this case.
[Paras 29 to 33]
