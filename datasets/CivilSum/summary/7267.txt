The Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Sections 2 and 25-F make it clear that a workman engaged in the year 1994 cannot have his services terminated after six years.
Even though the workman had brief breaks in his employment, he had worked continuously for years as a laborer for the Housing Board.
Therefore, he is entitled to relief for his reinstatement.
The decision of the Labour Court directing his reinstatement with 50% back wages was upheld, and the cases of 2005(1) SCT 146 (SC) and JT 1997(5) 560 were distinguished.
