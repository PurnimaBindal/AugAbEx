Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Retrenchment compensation - Payment after termination - According to established precedent, retrenchment compensation and notice pay must be given prior to retrenchment in order to be considered valid.
Any subsequent payment will not fix an illegal or void retrenchment.
Workmen have the right to challenge the legality and validity of such orders for violating Section 25-F, and there can be no estoppel against the statute.
