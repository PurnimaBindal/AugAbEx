Sections 33-C(1) and 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, deal with reinstatement with back-wages and claims for money due.
An application under Section 33-C(1) is maintainable to claim the amount computable on the basis of award.
If the workman claims an amount beyond what is determined by the appropriate government under Section 33-C(1), Section 33-C(2) will come into play.
It was held that the appropriate government can direct the employee to file an application under Section 33-C(2), but the application will lie under Section 33-C(1) for amounts computable on the basis of award.
