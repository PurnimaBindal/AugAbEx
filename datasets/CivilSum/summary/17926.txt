Constitution of India, Article 14 - Uncommunicated adverse remarks/Promotion/Representation - While considering an official for promotion, any adverse report that is pending representation cannot be taken into account.
[Para 9] B Constitution of India, Article 14 - Efficiency bar/ Promotion/Adverse remarks - Crossing the efficiency bar only indicates that the official is capable of fulfilling the duties of their current position.
However, previous adverse remarks cannot be disregarded and must be considered when assessing promotion cases.
Individuals with a history of adverse remarks cannot be considered equal to those with good reports, and those with a better service record have priority for promotion.
