According to the Haryana Warehousing Employees Pension Regulations, 1996, which are deemed to have come into operation on 2761996, any employee who retired on or after that date is entitled to pension.
The Regulations replace the EPF and should be construed liberally as a piece of social welfare legislation.
The Cut off date for entitlement is clearly stated, and the amount of PF can be adjusted in the arrears of pension to be paid.
