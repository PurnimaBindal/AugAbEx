Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - High Court rejected a writ challenging the appellant's post of Officer on Special Duty (Special Component Plan) - The appellant argues that the State Govt. could not have abolished the post as it was in the cadre of the Directorate of Harijan and Social Welfare Dept.
- However, there is no evidence of the State Govt. extending the duration of the post or agreeing to its continuation - While a post created in the Secretariat establishment can be used in an attached or subordinate office, this does not give the office the right to hold on to that post - Appeal dismissed.
