Code of Civil Procedure 1908, Section 100 - Second Appeal - Superannuation - The plaintiff was retired from service as the authority believed he was over 55 years old, but the plaintiff failed to provide proof of his birthdate and there was no credible evidence that he was over 55.
The age of superannuation is 58 years, so the order retiring the plaintiff was premature.
The court directed the respondents to grant the plaintiff pecuniary benefits of his three years of service.
[Paras 13, 14 and 15]
