The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 226 and 227, covers the topic of compassionate appointment and the required consideration.
However, in a specific case where a petitioner was considered by a screening committee, but not given a position due to non-availability of vacancy, they were eventually given a one-year contract appointment.
Despite a previous contempt petition, it was ultimately decided that no violations had occurred warranting further legal action.
