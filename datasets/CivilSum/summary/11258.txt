Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Charge-sheet - Delay in issuing Charge-sheet - If the charge-sheet delay is long and likely to harm the defendant's defence, preventing the production and examination of important defence documents and witnesses, the sustained delay cannot be allowed - This would certainly prejudice the employee's right to defence - A charge-sheet issued 20 years after the FCI stock shortages allegations was quashed.
