Constitution of India, Article 226 - Family Pension - If an employee receives his own pension and family pension on the death of his/her spouse either as a Government servant or after retirement, then recovery of Dearness Allowance is not allowed, but an employee who is re-employed is not entitled to receive Dearness Allowance on family pension - There is no prohibition on receiving double Dearness Allowances on pension and family pension.
