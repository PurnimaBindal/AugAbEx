Constitution of India, Article 32 - Delay and laches/Retrenchment - Petitioners who have been retrenched after 15 years are challenging their retrenchment - A writ cannot be issued for a roving enquiry and delay deprives a person of his right - The court only issues a writ in favor of someone who has some right - A person who has lost his remedy by lapse of time may only approach the court if some fresh cause of action arises - The petition is likely to be dismissed.
