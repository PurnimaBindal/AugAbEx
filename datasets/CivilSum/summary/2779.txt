Punjab Police Rules, 1934 Rules 137, 139, 1310 and 121(4) - Police Act, 1861, Section 22 - Seniority and transfer of Police Constables and Officers - Rules strictly prohibit the transfer out of cadre of Police District and Police Range - Seniority and promotion lists are maintained for the posts of Head Constables at the district level, and for the post of Assistant Sub Inspectors at the range level - The cadre level varies for the posts of Constables, Head Constables, Asstt.
Sub Inspectors, and Sub Inspectors - Consent of the person concerned is mandatory for any transfer from one Police District or Range to another - Members of the whole State Police are not considered to be part of one single cadre.
