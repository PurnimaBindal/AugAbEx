Under the Administrative Tribunals Act of 1985, Section 19, back wages have a limitation on the amount of difference that can be granted, and repeated representations to appropriate authorities to claim relief over a long period of time will not excuse claiming the difference in back wages from the back date.
Additionally, the tribunal cannot grant the difference of back wages beyond the limitation and would not be justified in putting the clock back for more than the period of limitation.
