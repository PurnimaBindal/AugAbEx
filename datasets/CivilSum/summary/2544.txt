Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Termination/Disciplinary proceedings - Certificate - Petitioner's Matriculation certificate from an institution not recognised by the Govt. - No fraud or forgery involved - Enquiry Officer returned a finding against the petitioner for mere non-recognition of certificate - Employer expected to verify qualifications of employee before appointment - Petitioner allowed to work for over five years, gaining experience on the job - Enquiry proceedings and report quashed since petitioner cannot be thrown out of service for non-recognition of certificate by the Govt.
