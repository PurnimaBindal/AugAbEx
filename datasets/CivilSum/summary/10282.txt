Haryana Industrial Training and Vocational Education Department (Group A) Service Rules, 1989, Rule 9(1) states that the rank of Assistant Apprenticeship Advisor (Technical) Group-A Junior can be filled up with 50% promotion from amongst Assistant Director (Technical) Group-B and 50% by direct recruitment.
However, post of Assistant Director (Technical) Group B, a feeder post have the same scale as that of petitioner.
As per Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 309, it would be appropriate that claim of petitioner should be considered and granted a higher scale as may be deemed appropriate by the State Government.
[Paras 5 and 6]

According to the Constitution of India, Article 226, the court will not ordinarily interfere in the matter of deciding the scale that should be given to a particular employer.
This is typically the decision of an expert body.
However, if the scale given is considered discriminatory or unconscionable, interference will be called for.
[Para 6]
