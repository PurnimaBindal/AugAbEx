The Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, has been deemed constitutional and legal, with no part of the Act containing any issues.
Additionally, since the Act is welfare-based, it has been deemed as intra-vires with the Constitution of India.

Regulation 10-B of the 1950 Employees State Insurance (General) Regulations has also been deemed as constitutional and in line with the Act's provisions for the effective implementation of the welfare scheme.
It provides a means for the registration of factories and establishments and the correct information provision of employees while attaching responsibility to employers for inaccurate information.
