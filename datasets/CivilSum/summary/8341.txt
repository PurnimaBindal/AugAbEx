UP Temporary Government Servants (Termination of Services) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Fair treatment of temporary employees - Termination of temporary employee - Natural justice - The State must be a fair employer - It should not take advantage of temporary employee's vulnerability - Its actions must be fair and consistent with the requirements of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - If a temporary employee has been working satisfactorily for 25 years and his services are terminated without notice or opportunity, the State's actions cannot be upheld and are liable to be quashed - Such actions of the State render an employee ineligible for Govt. employment forever and affects his livelihood and family too - The authorities must reinstate the temporary employee with back wages and consider his case for regularisation.
