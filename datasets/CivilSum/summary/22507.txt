Sections 2(k), 2(A), 2(p), 10-B and 18 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Settlement - Joint effect of settlement on all workers - A settlement was reached for the reinstatement of all workers without back wages for the strike period, but 12 workers did not accept the settlement - The union acted on behalf of all 29 workmen and the settlement was duly signed by both parties - Unless shown to be ex-facie unfair, unjust or malafide, all workers would ordinarily be bound by the settlement entered into by their representative union - The Labour Court erred in taking the view that the 12 workers were not parties to the settlement, as they were represented by the union - Therefore, the company is directed to offer the benefits of the settlement to the workers if they opt to accept it.
