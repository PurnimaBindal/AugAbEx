Section 7-A of the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, covers orders under the Act.
The petitioner in this case has not come to court with clean hands.
The petitioner falsely stated that there was no relationship between partners of eight establishments and proprietors of firms and that they were not owners of premises.
The earlier order was obtained and passed in pursuance of ulterior motives, by playing fraud upon the department, and poor workmen were deprived of their legitimate dues.
The petition has been dismissed with the cost of Rs. 1 lac, and the Commissioner of provident fund has directed to initiate legal and departmental action against all those involved in this fraud.
