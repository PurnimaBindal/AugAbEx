Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(j), 25(F), 25(G) and 25(H) - Termination - The Labour Court has set aside the termination order of a respondent who was engaged as a `chowkidar' on daily wages by a training institute.
The services were terminated because it had been regularised in contravention of Govt. instructions.
The issue of whether the petitioner-Institute is an industry within the meaning of Section 2(j) of the Act or not is to be framed by the Labour Court.
