Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - Although petitioner was deemed medically fit upon enrolling into Army, there is no evidence to indicate that the disease he was suffering from was constitutional in nature.
Given these circumstances, it can reasonably be inferred that his disease was attributable to his Army service.
As a result, the respondents have been directed to release the disability pension of the petitioner, who is required to appear before the Re-survey Medical Board as and when summoned.
[Paras 6, 7 and 10]   B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - Despite being discharged from the Army in 1977 and having his appeal for disability pension rejected in 1981, a writ petition was filed by the petitioner in 1999 for grant of disability pension.
The petitioner is entitled to 38 months of disability pension retroactively, as well as future disability payments in accordance with applicable rates.
