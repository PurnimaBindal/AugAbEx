The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 were invoked in a case involving the termination of a workman and the award of reinstatement.
The challenge to the award cited delay in raising an industrial dispute, with the termination occurring in 1983 and the claim statement filed in 1998.
The court held that the workmen's claim had become stale.
Moreover, the appointment must be consistent with the rules and regulations, otherwise, it is liable to be terminated under Section 2(oo)(bb) of the Act and does not amount to retrenchment.
