According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 and the Guru Jambheshwar University, Hisar Act, 1995, Sections 3 and 4, a student cannot insist that the college where they are studying must remain affiliated to a particular university at the time of their admission.
It is permissible for the legislature to change the affiliation of the college from one university to another through an Act, and the state legislature is fully competent to legislate for technical education, medical education, and universities.
Therefore, the petition was dismissed.
