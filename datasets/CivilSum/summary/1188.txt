Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Career Advancement Scheme - Bank Sarkar - Although the selection process may vary, the post of Bank Sarkar is not a promotional one, and the holder of the post must be considered a direct recruit - Even if it were classified as a promotional post, the petitioner would still be entitled to the benefits of the career advancement scheme - The order revoking the earlier decision to grant the benefit was set aside after the contention was rejected.
