Rajasthan Service Rules, Rule 244(2) - Rajasthan State Govt. Circular dated March 24, 1977 - Pre-mature retirement - The respondent received a notice of pre-mature retirement and appealed to the administrative tribunal for his case to be evaluated in accordance with the Govt. Circular.
The tribunal erred in treating the case as an appeal against the impugned order and must reconsider the matter in line with the conditions specified in the circular.
