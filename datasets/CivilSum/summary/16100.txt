Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume II, Rules 511, 518 - Invalid pension - Delay - Pre-mature retirement - Invalid pension can be given to public servants who are permanently incapacitated due to physical or mental infirmity - If a medical certificate proves such incapacity, the person must be invalidated without delay - A petitioner who became blind due to an eye disease sought retirement on medical grounds in 1982 - Govt. took over 6 years to form a medical board to certify that petitioner had become invalid - Petitioner is entitled to an invalid pension from 191982.
