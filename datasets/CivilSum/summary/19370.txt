Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 19(1) - Govt. Orders issued vide OMNo.
38/16/Pension Unit/80 dt. 30.12.80 - Military service benefits - Pension - The petitioner retired from BSF prior to completing 20 years qualifying service, thus not entitled to the benefit of Army service towards pension, despite having voluntarily rendered service for the country.
However, the Government has been directed to reconsider the matter and make some provision for the benefit of such persons.
