Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 311 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 54(B) - Suspension/Minor penalty - To avoid putting an employee in a difficult financial situation, suspension should only be used when a major penalty is likely to be imposed.
If there are no proceedings for a major penalty, then suspension is unjustified.
[Para 27]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 311 Fundamental Rules, Rule 54(B) - Suspension/Prolonged suspension/Minor penalty - Although a minor penalty cannot be used to justify a suspension, the competent authority must make an order under Rule 55(B) regarding payment of pay and allowances for the suspension period.
The authority should consider the circumstances and amount to be paid, as outlined in the rule.
[Para 29]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 311 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 54(B) - Suspension/Punishment - While suspension is not a penalty, it can still be punishing for an employee and should be re-considered frequently.
[Para 29 and 30]
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Indian Penal Code, Section 409 - Prosecution/Criminal Proceedings/Sanction - Criminal proceedings cannot be initiated against a public servant without the permission of the competent authority.
[Para 30]
Constitution of India, Articles 14,16 and 311 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 54(B)/Suspension/Natural justice/Pay and allowances for suspension period - Indefinite suspension violates principles of natural justice, and the competent authority is required to make an order regarding pay and allowances for the suspension period at the time of revocation.
Denying pay and allowances for the suspension period is arbitrary and not justified.
