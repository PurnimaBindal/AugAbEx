In accordance with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, the power of age limit relaxation is only granted in certain cases in the public interest, such as if no other suitable candidates are available for the post or to mitigate hardship.
However, there cannot be a wholesale relaxation of age limits simply because an advertisement is delayed or a vacancy occurred earlier.
This kind of power would create uncertainty and be unfair to candidates who did not apply under the assumption that they were age barred.
[Para 11]

Rule 11(1) of the Rajasthan Medical Services (Collegiate Branch) Rules, 1962 establishes a cut-off date for age limits.
While there may be different cut-off dates for advertisement, receipt of applications, and selection, fixing the first day of the following January is not unreasonable as long as it is equally applicable to all.
The provision of the Rule is upheld.
