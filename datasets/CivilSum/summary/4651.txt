According to Section 8f of the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1972, a garnishee order can be issued to recover arrears of provident funds.
In this particular case, respondent No. 3 had availed a cash credit facility from the petitioner bank, but only a portion of it had been utilized.
Respondents 1 and 2 sought to recover the balance of the arrears of PF due from respondent No. 3, however it was held that the petitioner bank cannot be considered a garnishee with respect to the unutilized portion of the overdraft/cash credit facility under the given circumstances.
