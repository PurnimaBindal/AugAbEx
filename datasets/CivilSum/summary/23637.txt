Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Merger of Departments/Promotion - The decision to merge the Exploration Wing of Indian Bureau of Mines with Geological Survey of India was communicated through subsequent letters dated 28687 and 4/6 February, 1969, and included the sanction of merger and certain instructions regarding conditions of service and seniority in the amalgamated cadres of Class I and II posts.
The decision came into force from 4/6 February, 1969, and not from 1-1-1966.
Therefore, contesting respondents are unable to claim seniority and promotion during 1-1-66 and 6-2-1969 in GSI [Paras 8, 9, 12 and 13]
