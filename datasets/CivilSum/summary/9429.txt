Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Central Bank of India Employees (Pension) Regulations, 1995, Regulation 3(9) - Pensionary benefits - The bank treated the petitioner as superannuated from service on 3131986 and provided all pensionary benefits, including provident fund accumulations.
However, since the petitioner failed to refund the Provident Fund money with interest according to the 1955 Regulations, the validity of his option for the pension is dependent upon the refund of PF.
Even though the petitioner exercised the option during 1994, it would only become a lawfully exercised option on fulfilling the conditions under Regulation 3(9) of the 1995 Pension Regulations about the refund of PF contribution.
Thus, the court held that the petitioner wilfully neglected to comply with the imposed conditions.
