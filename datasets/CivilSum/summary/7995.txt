Constitution of India, Article 311 - Government Servant - Termination - Post of Kurk Amin - The Appellant was appointed as Kurk Amin on a commission basis to recover outstanding dues of co-operative societies - However, a preliminary inquiry on indecent behavior was conducted, and the Appellant was found guilty - The termination orders were passed, holding that a regular inquiry would be unnecessary due to the admission of guilt by the Appellant - The Court held that the Appellant is a government servant and, therefore, entitled to protection under Article 311 of the Constitution - In the absence of any regular departmental inquiry being conducted, the order based on the preliminary inquiry cannot be sustained - The appeal was allowed accordingly.
