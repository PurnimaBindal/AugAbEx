Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 122 and 1318 - Confirmation/Date of confirmation/Seniority/Natural justice/Officiating service - When seniority is based on the date of confirmation, this date is crucial for further promotions and career advancement within the service - The date of confirmation cannot be changed to the detriment of an employee's seniority without first allowing them a full opportunity to be heard - The employee may also argue for the inclusion of officiating periods towards their probation period and other grounds for defense - Any order made without providing a fair opportunity violates the principles of natural justice.
