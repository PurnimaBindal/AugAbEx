Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F(b) and 25-H - Petitioner appointed as Technical Assistant with special pay initially for a short duration - Services regularised, but later withdrawn and retrenched - Respondent Bank employed Respondent Nos. 5 and 6 without proper procedure and without considering petitioner's case as per Section 25-H - Court held that Respondents were appointed without advertisement and selection procedure and allowed the petition - Appointment orders of Respondent Nos. 5 and 6 quashed - Respondents directed to first give appointment to petitioner and then consider appointments for Respondent Nos. 5 and 6.
