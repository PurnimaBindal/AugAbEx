Sections 6(d) and 4 of the Payment of Bonus Act, 1965 deal with rebates and deductions from gross profit.
The employer's interest on bonds or government loans for the relevant accounting years is not deductible from gross profit for payment of bonus, as per Section 6.
However, the available surplus will consist of gross profits and deductions from that permitted under Section 6.
The computation of gross profits as per Section 4 has to consider the question of nexus with these items.
The Tribunal will determine the allocable surplus for relevant years by considering the net profit as per the Second Schedule.
