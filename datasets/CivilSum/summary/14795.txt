Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - West Bengal School Service Commission Act, 1997 - Recruitment process - Selection - In 1997, an order directed the School Managing Committee to allow certain individuals to apply for future vacancies and receive some weightage, however, this committee lost its power to appoint teachers with the Act of 1997.
As a result, if the petitioners are not eligible, they cannot participate in the selection process.
The petition was dismissed.
