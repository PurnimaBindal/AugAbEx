In accordance with Article 16 of the Constitution of India, seniority for appointed individuals will be counted from the date of their appointment and not retrospectively.
This applies even if the initial appointment is only ad hoc.
Officiation in such a post cannot be taken into account for considering seniority.
[Paras 7 and 8]  B.
According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India, once an employee of the State opts for the service of a Corporation, they shall cease to be in the service of the State.
It cannot be held that the post holder has been deputed to the Corporation holding their lien with the State Government.
[Paras 9 and 10] C. According to Articles 16 and 311 of the Constitution of India, an option was provided for employees to opt either to be absorbed into another department or to move to the Corporation.
After joining the Corporation, the period of time when the employee was appointed back to the service of the State Government shall not be counted towards their seniority and it shall be deemed that they were appointed afresh.
There is no evidence that the appellant ever exercised their option to remain with the State Government.
