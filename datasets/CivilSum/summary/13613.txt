Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 137 and 138 (as amended on 8.9.92) - Legality and constitutionality of amended provisions - Promotion from the post of Constable to Head Constable - Constables who pass Lower School Course are added to List 'C' to be considered for promotion and only selected on suitability - List 'A' constables do not automatically have the right to go for Lower School Course - After passing, constables are admitted to List 'C' under Rule 138 for eligibility for promotion - Unamended Rule 138 also provides for second selection for List 'B' - Grounds to challenge Rule validity cannot be based on speculation of arbitrariness in promotion - Only mala fide or other valid grounds can be used to challenge individual promotion - Rule cannot be declared ultra vires for this reason.
