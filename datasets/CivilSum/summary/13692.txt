I, Rule 3.26(D) - Premature Retirement - Good reports - Average reports/suspension period - The employee must have earned at least 70% good reports during the preceding 10 years to be retained - Order for premature retirement quashed - Petitioner recorded 6 good and 2 average reports out of 8, and was acquitted in criminal proceedings - Petitioner had been under suspension for two years.
