Constitution of India, Article 32 - Assurance given to the Court/Dismissal of writ petition on assurance given - Petitioner, a Trained Graduate Teacher in Rajasthan Atomic Power Project, was protected under existing designation, grade, pay, and allowances when the school merged with Atomic Energy Education Society and promised assurance to the Court - The AEE Society must honor its promise, regardless of subsequent dismissal of contempt petitions and other applications - The Court directed the AEE Society to absorb the petitioner with retrospective effect and all benefits, even if a supernumerary post needs to be created.
