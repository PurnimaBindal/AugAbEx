Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 17 - Termination order - Appeal - Limitation - The order dispensing with the services of the petitioner was passed on 741984, and before the order could be served on the petitioner, he filed an appeal on 971984, requesting a stay of the order dated 4.7.1984.
Rule 17 specifies the time limit for filing an appeal to be 45 days from the date a copy of the order appealed against is delivered to the appellant - indicating that if the information regarding the order was received by the person aggrieved, they can file an appeal.
With that in mind, the action of the respondents not deciding the appeal filed by the petitioner cannot be sustained.
