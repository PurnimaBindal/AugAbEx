Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Jammu and Kashmir Police SRO 126 of 1994 - Post of Prosecuting Officer - Reservation for children of Defence Personal/Ex-servicemen - Applicants who applied under the reserved category and were selected in the open category based on their own merit, cannot be counted in reserved category of Defence Personal/ex-servicemen - Reservation benefits should only be extended to those who were unable to make a mark in the open category.
