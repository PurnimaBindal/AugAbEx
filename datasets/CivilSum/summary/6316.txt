In accordance with Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Section 15 of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936, factual findings made by authorities cannot be disturbed in writ jurisdiction unless they are found to be based on misreading of evidence, absurd or erroneous.
[Para 7]
As per Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Section 2(ii)(f) of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936, if the issue of jurisdiction goes to the root of a court, it can be raised at any time.
However, if evidence is required, being a mixed question of law and facts, it cannot be dealt with initially in writ jurisdiction without any evidence regarding the activities carried out by the establishment.
