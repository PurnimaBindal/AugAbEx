Punjab Police Service Rules, 1959, Rules 8 and 10 - Respondent Nos. 4 and 5 were given appointment on directions of High Court being next in merit of general category candidates - Respondents filed writ seeking deemed date of appointment as date given to other candidates appointed pursuant to advertisement of year 1989 and not determining seniority as per actual date of appointment of year 1989 was allowed - Respondents were given deemed date of appointment despite holding substantive posts in other departments - They shall not be entitled to any salary or benefits but entitled to seniority - Deemed date of appointment and confirmation are given to provide parity to similarly situated candidates selected in pursuance to same selection process.
