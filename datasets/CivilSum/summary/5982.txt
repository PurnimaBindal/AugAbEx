According to the Land Acquisition Act, Sections 30 and 18, compensation must be apportioned between the tenant and landowner even if the tenant did not have occupancy rights at the time of notification.
It was determined that a tenant did not have occupancy rights in 1973, but obtained such rights through a 1974 declaration and a 1976 decree.
Therefore, compensation cannot be refused to the tenant solely because they obtained occupancy rights after the notification for acquisition.
The compensation must be paid to the rightful owner, regardless of who owned the property at the time of acquisition notification.
Once occupancy rights are declared, the Section 3 Punjab Occupancy Tenants (Vesting of Proprietary Rights) Act indicates that the tenant is deemed the owner of the property and is entitled to the entire compensation.
