Constitution of India, Article 309 - Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1958 Part II, Rule 9 - Temporary employee - Rule 9(a)(i) allows for appointment of a temporary employee against a vacancy to serve the public interest - Proviso to Rule 9(a)(iii) provides for replacement according to seniority - However, Rule 9(a)(iii) only applies when the temporary employee must be replaced by a regular employee during the term of the appointment - Therefore, the temporary employee has no legal right to continue beyond their appointed term.
