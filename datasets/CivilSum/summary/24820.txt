The Board of Arbitrators was directed to determine if the Botanical Survey of India's Scientific Assistant post was eligible for the revised scale of pay of Rs. 550-900.
The terms of reference provided no scope for two different levels of pay or minimum qualifications for each level.
The government could not be directed to frame proper Recruitment Rules for the Senior Scientific Assistants-Level-I and Scientific Assistants-Level-II positions.
According to Paragraph 8, the Board of Arbitrators acted beyond the limits of its terms of reference, rendering the Award illegal and non-binding on the parties.
