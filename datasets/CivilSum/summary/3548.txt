Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Quashing of appointment - The review-petitioner, who secured 158 marks along with 30 other candidates in the merit list for apprenticeship training, was not entitled to any preference solely because of his marks - The review-petitioner was placed at the top of the merit list because of his age, not his marks - The Division Bench was correct in not giving preference to the review-petitioner for appointment.
[Paras 4 and 5]
