According to the Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948, Section 79(c) and Assam State Electricity Board's Service Regulations, 1960, Regulation 12, promotion is based on "seniority-cum-merit", where seniority is considered important.
A person with sufficient merit cannot be promoted over those senior to him, unless they lack qualifications or are otherwise unfit.
The petitioner has been promoted as UDA.
