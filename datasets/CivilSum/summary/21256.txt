The inclusion or exclusion of non-practicing allowance for doctors, and their eligibility for allotment of Government accommodation, is currently pending before the Vth Pay Commission according to the Constitution of India, Article 32- Allotment of Government Residences (General Pool in Delhi) Rules, 1963, and no interference by the court is warranted at this stage.

While the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 21, protect the rights of employees, recommendations made by the Pay Commission do not automatically confer any rights upon them unless they are approved by the relevant Government body.
