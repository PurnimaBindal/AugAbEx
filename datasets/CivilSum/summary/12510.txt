The termination of services of workman after a proper enquiry can not lead to the government declining a reference under Sections 10(1) and 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act.
The court is responsible for adjudicating the propriety and illegality of the enquiry, and the government cannot exercise the power to adjudicate upon the dispute.
Additionally, if Section 11-A of the Act is applicable, the workman can bring forth evidence that the punishment was disproportionate to the misconduct, and the court has the authority to reduce the punishment.
Hence, in cases of termination, removal, and dismissal from service, the government does not have jurisdiction to decline the reference.
