Constitution of India, Article 311 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 5(3) - Madras City Municipal Corporation Act, 1919, Sections 85 and 86 - Voluntary retirement - Municipal Commissioner cannot approve voluntary retirement - Request for voluntary retirement must be made to the Council, subject to confirmation by State Government - Because the application is invalid, retiree cannot be deemed retired from service on expiration of 3 months notice as per clause (c) of Rule 56(3) of Fundamental Rules.
