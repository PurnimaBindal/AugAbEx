Constitution of India, Article 14 - Recruitment - Age limit - Cut off date for age limit - Recruitment through Employment Exchange - The cut off date for age limit when selecting candidates through Employment Exchange is the date on which the Employment Exchange provides the list of candidates.
Recommendations or demands made after the candidates have been sponsored will not change the eligibility criteria for those candidates.
Every group of candidates that is sponsored on a different date will have their own cut-off date as per the recommendation of the Employment Exchange.
Any other interpretation may have serious consequences that may affect the rights of those who have already been sponsored.
[Paras 6 & 7]
