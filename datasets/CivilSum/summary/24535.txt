According to Sections 2(s) and 2(g) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, engineers on Retrainers list of the company for the purpose of attending TV complaints are considered as contractual employees.
The Retainers are paid per complaint basis and are not employed as employees of the company.
Additionally, there are no specific posts of such engineers nor have any such posts ever been advertised or selection made.
The retainers need only visit the company office to collect complaints and are not required to attend any duties or mark their presence.
Therefore, it cannot be concluded that there exists any relationship of an employee and employer or of master and servant between the retainers and the company, nor can the retainers be termed as workmen.
