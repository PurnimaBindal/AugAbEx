Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - Eligibility criteria - The guidelines for promoting junior engineers from Class III to Class II mandate employees meet certain minimum criteria - The validity of these circulars has not been contested - Even promotions on seniority-cum-merit basis may have minimum eligibility criteria - Court has no basis to interfere under Article 226 of the Constitution - Petition dismissed.
