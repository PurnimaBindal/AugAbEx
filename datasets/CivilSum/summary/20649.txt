Constitution of India, Articles 136 - All India Services (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1969, Rule 3(3) - Suspension - Appellant was initially suspended under Rule 3(1) in anticipation of disciplinary proceedings against him, but later the suspension order was changed and he was informed that he was involved in a criminal case and was being suspended under Rule 3(3) instead.
No charge sheet has been filed yet and the matter is still under investigation.
The appellant was the only one placed under suspension, while several other senior officers were also involved in the criminal case.
The appellant argued that the government cannot selectively suspend individuals.
The suspension order was set aside, leaving it up to the government to decide whether to suspend the appellant if and when a charge sheet is filed.
[Paras 6 to 8]
