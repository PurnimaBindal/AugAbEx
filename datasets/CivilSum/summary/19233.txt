Constitution of India, Article 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(p) and 18(1) read with rule 58 of the Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules, 1957 - Settlement relating to Voluntary Cessation of Employment, Clause XVI - Regional Manager/Deputy General Manager may appoint clerical staff and pass orders of voluntary retirement - Petitioner was absent from duty and served notice to resume within 30 days or considered voluntarily retired under Clause XVI - Petitioner did not return to work and was treated as voluntarily retired by Regional Manager - Impugned order issued by Branch Manager but passed by Regional Manager - Writ petition dismissed on grounds that order was passed by competent authority.
