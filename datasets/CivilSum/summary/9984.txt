As per Article 14 of the Constitution of India, norms were fixed for gain in weight of wheat and loss of weight in paddy by inspectorate staff of stores.
These norms were based on expert and chemical reports.
The inspectorate staff must account for the gain in weight of wheat and loss of weight in paddy.
Petitioners challenged the norms based on various factors that may cause loss in weight of wheat, but the court determined that only the gain in weight of wheat due to moisture was to be considered.
Other factors such as transportation and pet animals should be raised separately before the authorities.
Since the matter regarding loss/gain of weight during storage is well-established, the petitioners must account for it.
Therefore, the petition was dismissed.
[Paras 15 to 18]
