In accordance with Section 30 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, an appeal can be made regarding a substantial question of law.
The Commissioner made an error in calculating the age of the deceased, which is vital in determining the amount of compensation.
As a result, the phrase 'substantial question of law' must be interpreted broadly to encompass incorrect conclusions based on a misinterpretation of significant evidence.
