Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Secretarial and Attached Offices Assistants Joint Cadre Act, 1989 - Petitioners argued that ministerial staff deserved revised pay scales given to Secretarial staff - Pay Commission experts must determine pay structure and equivalence of posts - Nature of duties and responsibilities attached to relevant post also to be considered - Court cannot issue a sweeping direction to Government without consideration of these aspects - Cases remanded to High Court.
