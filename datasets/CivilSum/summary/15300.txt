Kerala Service Rules, Part I, Rules 5(A), 60(C) - Kerala Education Rules, 1959, Chapter IXA, Rule 62 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 - Revised pay scale - Benefit of - Teachers in aided schools and civil servants are entitled to it, however, it is denied to teachers whose service is extended beyond 55 years of age of retirement - Such differential treatment of a section of employees for no justifiable reason is not acceptable - Therefore, teachers of Government schools are not debarred from receiving the benefits of the revised scale.
