Central Industrial Security Force Act, 1968, Section 22 - Central Industrial Security Force Rules, 2001, Rules 36 & 52(2)(c) - The validity of de novo enquiries - The disciplinary authority slapped a penalty on the petitioner, agreeing with the findings of the Enquiry Officer.
However, the Appellate Authority set the penalty aside and directed a de novo enquiry without stating reasons.
This action was not appropriate as Rule 52(2)(c) requires proper reasons if the appellate authority decides to remit a case, and such a decision cannot be arbitrary.
Therefore, the order of the appellate authority directing a de novo inquiry was set aside and directions were issued to hear the appeal on merit.
