MP Revision of Pay Rules, 1990, Rules 3(d), 7(1) - M.P.
Fundamental Rules, Rule 27 - Pay fixation in revised scale of pay - It has been ruled that an employee, whose pay was revised under sub-rule (1) of Rule 7 of the MP Revision of Pay Rules, 1990, is automatically entitled to benefits of advance increments for Family Planning Operations in his previous pay scale under the circular dated 2911979.
However, once the revised scale of pay is fixed, no further benefit of advance increments can be claimed in the event of promotion or higher pay-scale.
