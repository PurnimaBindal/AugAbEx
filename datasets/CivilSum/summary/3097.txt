According to Section 3(5) of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 and Section 16 of the Apprentices Act, 1961, an employer is liable to pay compensation if an apprentice is injured in the course of employment.
However, Section 16 does not exclude the jurisdiction of a Civil Court to try a suit for damages.
[Para 10]

In cases where death gives rise to a claim for compensation under common law as well as the Workmen's Compensation Act, a party can claim compensation under either of the laws and not under both.
Although a party cannot claim both common law damages and compensation under the Workmen's Compensation Act, simply receiving compensation under the Act does not exhaust their option to claim damages under common law.
