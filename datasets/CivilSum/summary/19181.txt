Posts allocated for the Special Protection Group are filled via appointment on deputation of members from central police organizations, Intelligence Bureau, and State Police Organizations under the Special Protection Group Act, 1988 and Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16, and 311.
The petitioner was nominated as a candidate for appointment by Border Security Force Headquarters, although his service record had an adverse red entry.
Although the Selection Board recommended the petitioner's case subject to verification of the red entry in his service record, when it was found that the punishment imposed on him remained valid, he was repatriated back to BSF.
The candidate must maintain an unblemished service record to be eligible for induction into the S.P.G.
Thus impugned order is valid as per Paras 7, 11, and 12.
