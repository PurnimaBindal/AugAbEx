Eligibility for the post of Primary Teacher is dependent on having a degree equivalent to BA from a recognized institute.
The petitioners did not hold such a degree and therefore were not eligible for the position.
[Para 15] B.
As per Circular, the degree of Shiksha Shastri is not recognized for employment purposes as a Primary Teacher.
Whether or not it is equivalent to a BA degree is a matter for the authorities to determine, and the Court should not interfere with executive domain.
The petitioner was not eligible for appointment as they did not possess a degree from a recognized institute.
