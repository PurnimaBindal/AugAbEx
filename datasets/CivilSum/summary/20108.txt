Constitution of India, Article 309 - Indian Police Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations (1955), Regn.
7(3), Proviso - Appointment to IPS cadre of members of State police force - The State Government failed to send the `no deterioration certificate' of a member before the notification of appointment to the IPS Cadre, which was a condition precedent - The direction to the Government to appoint the candidate was deemed illegal - Nevertheless, after the candidate's retirement, the State Government expressed that he was well deserving - The Union Government was directed to include the candidate's name in the notification.
