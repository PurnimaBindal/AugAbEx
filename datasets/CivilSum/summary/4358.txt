In accordance with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, a temporary employee cannot be discriminated against.
If a temporary employee is terminated in line with their contract or statutory regulations and their juniors are retained, this does not automatically make the termination unconstitutional.
The onus is on the employee to provide proof of discrimination based on malice or bad faith, compared to their juniors.
If this discrimination is proven, government action can be deemed unconstitutional under Articles 14 and 16.
