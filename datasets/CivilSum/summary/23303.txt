Constitution of India, Article 323-A - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 14(1) - Jurisdiction - The Central Administrative Tribunal has been vested with all the jurisdiction, powers, and authority exercisable by 'all courts' except the Supreme Court according to Article 323A Clause (2) (d) - The Act also covers casual typists employed on daily wages by the Union of India claiming the right to continue employment under its provisions - The remedy for termination lies in the Tribunal and not the High Court.
