Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 10(1) - Abolition of Contract Labour through notification under Section 10(1) of the Act - A Memorandum of settlement aimed to develop a scheme for departmentalisation of jobs and absorption of contractor workmen - Absorption of workmen has no age limit, but those above 45 years were excluded - Petitioners waited for 10 years after the notification of Section 10(1) - The court held that petitioners should be absorbed from the date of contract labour abolition and should be treated as direct employees - Petitioners entitled to wages from the date of petition filing.
