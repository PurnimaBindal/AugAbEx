Under Punjab Municipal Act, 1911, Sections 38(6) and 6-A, and Punjab Municipal Services (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1975, Rule 5, absorption in municipal service is based on consideration by the authority or screening committee on the basis of service record and qualifications.
Despite repeated reminders by the Director, the petitioner's service record was not made available to the screening committee, and the order dispensing with their services on the basis of not being recommended by the screening committee after almost 10 years was not justified.
Consequently, the order of removal was quashed, and all consequential benefits and pension for the LRs of the deceased petitioner were deemed entitled.
