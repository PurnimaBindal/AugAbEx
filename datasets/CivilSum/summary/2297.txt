The Haryana Food and Supply Department Office (Group C) Service Rules, 1982, Rule 9(1)(o) dictates the equation of pay scales of similar posts in State and Central Government Departments.
In order to determine similarities of positions and assign the appropriate pay scale, this is the job of the Pay Commission and cannot be claimed based solely on job titles.
As a result, the claim made by junior analysts of the Food and Supplies Department of Haryana for equation of pay scales assigned to similar posts in the Central Govt. Department is not valid.
