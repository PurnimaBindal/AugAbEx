Constitution of India, Article 226 - Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanam Employees Service Rules, Rule 9 (VIII) (inserted on 1021992) - Appointment - In-service candidate - Applicants for selection were called in 1991 when the post became empty - An interview was held in 1993 - The purpose of Rule 9(VIII), which is a statutory rule, is to give preference to in-service candidates - It was held that once priorities are decided by the amended statutory rule, the same has to be interpreted in favour of an in-service candidate by keeping in mind the principles of purposive construction of statutes - Therefore, the petitioner cannot claim on the ground that the vacancy occurred before the insertion of the rule.
