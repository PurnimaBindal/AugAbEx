Constitution of India, Article 136 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination - Back wages - Although appellant's termination was set aside by the Labour Court due to breach of Section 25-F on the part of respondent management, the appellant was denied back wages because he raised an industrial dispute more than 5 years after the termination - The appellant contends that during that time he had approached the Tribunal and the matter remained pending for five years - The Tribunal ultimately took the position that it lacked jurisdiction to handle the case - As a result, considering the peculiar facts of the situation and the fact that the respondent management was not to blame for the delay, the appellant should be granted 50% of the back wages from the date of termination until he was actually reinstated.
