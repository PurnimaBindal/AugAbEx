Constitution of India, Article 372 - Repealing of statute - All India Council for Technical Education Act, 1987 will replace Pharmacy Act, 1948 with parallel Provisions - Exclusion of earlier Act is implied - Admissions made in breach of the provisions of 1987 Act are invalid - Authorities under the 1987 Act are competent to conduct inspection, accord approval and lay down conditions on Pharmacy Colleges.
