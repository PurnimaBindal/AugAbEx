Constitution of India, Article 16(4A) - Non-selection post - Reservation in promotion - It is held that the 77th Constitutional Amendment Act 1995 permits the State to provide for reservation in promotion for scheduled caste or tribes in non-selection or selection posts if they are not adequately represented.
