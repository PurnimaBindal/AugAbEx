The employees of the previous District Boards/Zila Parishads/Panchayat Samitis in Haryana are entitled to receive the benefit of previous service towards pension and gratuity as specified in the States Reorganisation Act, 1956, Chapter X, and Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
This benefit was affirmed by a State Government memorandum issued on November 22, 1991.
