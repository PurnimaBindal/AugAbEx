The Karnataka Civil Services (Probation) Rules, 1977, Rule 9, along with the Karnataka Govt. Order No. GAD 110 SMC 66 dated 23.1.67 pertains to the cadre strength of Assistant Commissioners, both for regular and temporary posts.
While the Govt. Order dated 3.3.76 provides a clear picture of the permanent cadre strength and the separate 133 temporary posts, it cannot be deemed to have increased the strength of the cadre to 285 posts.
Rule 9 of 1977 Karnataka Civil Service (Probation) Rules only refers to substantive vacancy and confirmation of probationer, and the Govt. Order dated 3.3.76 cannot be interpreted in light of the Rule.
Additionally, the decisions made in Badami's case and Gonal Bhimappa's case hold binding implications for both direct recruits and promotees to the Assistant Commissioners cadre.
