Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 7 - Misconduct - Composite charges - The petitioner was accused of being in a drunken state and misbehaving with officials, but the charge of being drunk wasn't proven or disproven.
However, the charge of misbehaving with persons on duty was proven.
Even though the Enquiry Officer found the petitioner innocent of the charges, the disciplinary authority formed its own opinion on the matter and submitted a dissenting note to the petitioner for an effective representation.
The petitioner responded to the show cause notice with the dissenting note, received a personal hearing and no prejudice was caused to him.
The punishment of stoppage of three increments with the cumulative effect were upheld with categorical findings of the petitioner's misbehaviour.
