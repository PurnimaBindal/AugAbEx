Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Swatantra Sainik Samman Pension Scheme, 1980 - Sawatantarta Sainik - Evidence - The provisions of the Scheme must be interpreted generously for the purpose of granting freedom fighter pension - Once the applicant has already provided certificates of two co-prisoners and pension has been released to him, the authority cannot require the applicant to provide new affidavits after the death of those co-prisoners - It is often difficult, in several cases, to locate any co-prisoners after such a long period of time - Pension reinstated.
