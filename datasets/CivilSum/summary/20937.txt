Regional Director is authorized to exercise power under Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 94A - Order passed by him imposing damages and interest for delayed payment is valid.
[Para 2]
Imposition of damages and interest on delayed payment is required under Employees' State Insurance (General) Regulations (1950), Regn.31A - Employer must comply with Regn.
31 by producing duly stamped card indicating deposit of employer's and employee's contribution with the Corporation - Order imposing damages and interest on delayed payment is valid in case of non-compliance.
