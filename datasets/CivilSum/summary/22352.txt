Article 316(1) of the Constitution of India with proviso and Articles 317(3)(c) provide for appointment of disabled persons as members of the Public Service Commission and the respondent, who was a blind Ph.D holder and a recipient of national and international awards, was therefore appointed as a member, with blindness being held to not be an infirmity that would prevent him from performing his duties.
An infirmity of body or mind can only be taken into consideration if it arises after the appointment and not before.
[Para 15]

According to Article 316(1) proviso and 317(3), the Public Service Commission has seven members from the non-service category and four from the service category.
There was a contention that the ratio between service and non-service categories be 50% each, but the court rejected it.
"As normally as may" in Article 316(1) suggests that the proportion of 50% service members is only an approximation and not mandatory.
