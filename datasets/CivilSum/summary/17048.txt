According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, a government employee can join another department while retaining their lien on their post and taking leave without pay.
However, if the employee's leave salary and pension are not contributed to by the other department, they cannot be treated as being on leave without pay.
Even if there is a technical objection, the employee themselves is ready to contribute, and any objection is removed.
Therefore, the order treating the petitioner as on leave without pay is quashed.
