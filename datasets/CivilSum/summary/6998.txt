Sections 2-A and 7-A of the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 was applied to four units belonging to the same family, interconnected in money transactions, conducting business of Hire Purchase and in the same premises with unity of ownership, management and functional integrality.
The mere fact that one of the units was a partnership firm while the others were limited companies was not sufficient to exclude their coverage under Section 2-A of the Act.
As a result, the finding of clubbing all four units together under the Act should not be interfered with.
