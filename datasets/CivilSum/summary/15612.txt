Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - The petitioner, who joined Air Force service on 11582, was discharged from service on 17295 due to disease Neurosis.
The Medical Board assessed his disability at only 20%.
However, there is nothing on record to indicate that the petitioner was suffering from such a disease when he joined Air Force service.
It is worth noting that such diseases are often suffered by armed forces personnel given the strict discipline required in Air Force service.
As a result, the respondents were instructed to provide disability pension to the petitioner.
