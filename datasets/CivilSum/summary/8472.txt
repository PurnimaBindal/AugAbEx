Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 9(2) - Dismissal - Enquiry - Natural justice - The delinquent plaintiff was only furnished a copy of the report of Enquiry Officer along with the order of dismissal, and was not given reasons for the disciplinary authority's disagreement with the findings of Enquiry Officer - This lack of information caused serious prejudice to the defence of the plaintiff, leading to the quashing of the impugned order.

Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rules 8 and 9(1) - De novo enquiry - The Enquiry Officer found the plaintiff guilty of two charges but one charge was not proved - The disciplinary authority ordered a fresh enquiry to be conducted by an older Enquiry Officer - The plaintiff participated in the enquiry without protest, and it is held that appointing a new enquiry officer for the de novo enquiry would not vitiate the order of dismissal.
