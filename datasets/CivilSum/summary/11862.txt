The petitioner, who was promoted to the post of Gram Panchayat Supervisor, should not be denied the benefit of their promotion just because of an order of suspension that was passed against them during a departmental enquiry in which they were later found innocent.
The court assumes that the authorities had considered the suspension and disciplinary proceeding before granting the promotion, and once the promotion is granted, the authorities cannot deny its benefits to the petitioner.
