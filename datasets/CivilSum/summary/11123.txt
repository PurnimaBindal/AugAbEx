Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjabi University Calender, Vol. II, Ordinance 38 - Examination/Unfair means - Natural justice - Punishment - In order to adhere to principles of natural justice, the unfair means committee/Syndicate must provide the student with all material it wants to consider before administering punishment - Failure to do so will render the punishment order in violation of natural justice.
