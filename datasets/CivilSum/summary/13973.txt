The Employees Pension Scheme 1995, clause 2(1)(ix) and Paras 6-A, 10, 12 and 14, along with the Family Pension Scheme, 1971, require a minimum qualifying service of 10 years for pension claims.
However, a petitioner whose claim for pension was rejected due to having only about 9 years of service was a member of both the Provident Fund Scheme, 1952, and the Pension Scheme, 1971.
In 1991, his services were illegally terminated, so he withdrew his contributions from the fund due to financial constraints.
After being reinstated with continuity of service and all consequential benefits, his contribution towards the Provident Fund Scheme 1952, Pension Scheme 1971, and Employee Pension Scheme was deducted from the date he joined service until he retired.
Although the petitioner offered to deposit the withdrawn amount with interest, his request was rejected.
Nevertheless, he should not be denied the benefit of past service prior to 1991, and directives should be given to release his monthly pension under the scheme.
