Section 2(c) of the National Commission for Backward Classes Act, 1993 along with Sections 2(g), 3(iii), 5(1)(2) and 6 of the Central Educational Institutions (Reservation in Admission) Act, 2006 and Articles 15(4) and 16(4) of the Constitution of India consider 27% reservation for Other Backward Classes.
Cases must be examined in detail and it is desirable to hold the operation of the Act with respect to Section 6 for OBC category.
Reservation for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes candidates will not be affected.
[Para 25]

Backwardness related to reservations must be based on objective factors and inadequacy must exist factually.
[Para 15]

Equality of opportunity specified in Articles 15(4) and 16(4) of the Constitution of India depends not only on the absence of disabilities but also on the presence of abilities.
Differentiations or classifications for special preference must not be unfair for those left out of the groups that are favoured.
[Para 19]
