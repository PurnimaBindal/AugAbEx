Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Termination - The petitioner was appointed as a Night Watchman on a daily wage basis from the candidates sponsored by employment exchange for a clear vacancy.
However, the Principal Secretary terminated his services instead of approving his appointment.
Despite interim orders, the petitioner is continuing in service without receiving any salary.
Respondents have been instructed to regularize the petitioner's services and pay the time scale attached to the post.
[Paras 2 and 4]
