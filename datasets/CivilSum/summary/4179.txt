The regularisation of the petitioner's service was done with effect from 31.12.1990 per the instructions given in notifications dated 2821991.
However, the petitioner requested that his service be regularised from 30.9.1988 based on the instructions contained in Haryana Govt. letter dated 641990, but the request was refused.
The respondents argued that the instructions issued on 641990 were not notified by the government and although they may have been presented before the Supreme Court, the petitioner cannot claim any right based on that.
The instructions on 641990 have a wide scope that involves all categories of employees.
The notification issued under the proviso to Article 309 of the Constitution cannot override the instructions dated 641990.
Hence, the petitioner is eligible to receive the benefit of instructions dated 641990.
