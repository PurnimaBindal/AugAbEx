Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Coal Mines Provident Fund (Employees Recruitment Rules) 1982 Rules 7(1), 7(6) and 7(7) - Probation - Termination during probation - ExtenSion of probation - The termination during probation was challenged on the ground that probation could have been extended beyond three years.
However, there was no specific order of confirmation, and in case probation was extendable further, there could be no deemed confirmation after completion of the initial probation period.
The High Court was not right in declaring deemed confirmation and directing payment of compensation when the termination was in accordance with Rule 7(7) of the Rules.
