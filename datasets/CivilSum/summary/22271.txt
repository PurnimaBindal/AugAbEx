Articles 15(3) and 16 of the Constitution of India allow for the reservation of posts for women.
Making special provision for women in employment or posts under the State is an integral part of Article 15(3) and the power conferred under this article is unaffected by Article 16.
The special provision for improving women's participation can take the form of affirmative action or reservation.

Article 16(4) of the Constitution of India refers to a separate quota reserved for a special category of persons, allowing protection of the weak category against competition from open category candidates.
