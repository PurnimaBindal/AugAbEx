After receiving a failure report from the Conciliation Officer regarding an industrial dispute, the government declined to refer the dispute for adjudication.
The Act defines the function of the appropriate government under Section 10(1) as administrative rather than judicial or quasi-judicial.
Therefore, the government cannot determine the merits of the dispute and make a decision beyond the powers conferred upon them.
The court, therefore, directed the State government to refer the dispute immediately, and the petition was allowed accordingly.
