Constitution of India, Articles 309, 14 - Rajasthan Technical Education Rules (1963), Rule 2 - Appointment - Challenge as to - Necessary party - Appointment on post of Lecturer for beauty culture - A writ petition was filed challenging the appointment - Though the Single Judge did not quash the appointment, it was noted that the selection procedure was not fair and directed for a fresh selection - It was held that the candidates appointed earlier were necessary parties and the order passed by the Single Judge in their absence was liable to be set aside.
