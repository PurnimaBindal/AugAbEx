Food Corporation of India Staff Regulations 17, 66, 67, 68 - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Suspension - Change of headquarters - Petitioners challenged order of suspension and change of their headquarter during suspension - Regulations 17 and 66 allow for employee transfer and suspension - Sub-Regulation (6) of Regulation 66 allows for change of employee's headquarters during suspension - Validity of Regulations not in question - Petitioners have the option to appeal the decision - Thus, the petition is dismissed due to lack of merit.
