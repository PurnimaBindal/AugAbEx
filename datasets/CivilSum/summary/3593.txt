Madhya Pradesh Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1966, Rule 9(4) - Fundamental Rules, Rule 54-B - Detention - Suspension during detention - Rule 9(4) requires detention to be legal; illegal detention without case, order or proceedings against the employee will not attract the rule - Continued suspension after acquittal is illegal, against Rule 9(4), and entitles the employee to wages.
