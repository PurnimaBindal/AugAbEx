The punishment of pre-mature retirement, if based on proper inquiry and evidence and the delinquent intentionally abstained appearance in the inquiry proceedings, cannot be considered illegal, arbitrary or violative of principles of natural justice under Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
[Paras 17 and 18] B.
In departmental proceedings under Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, vague allegations of bald assertion cannot be considered for examining the allegations of mala fide or malice in absence of specific materials.
The burden is heavy on the petitioner and the court cannot make a fishing inquiry for that purpose.
