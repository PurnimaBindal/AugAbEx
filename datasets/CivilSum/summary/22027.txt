The liability for payment of wages to each employee engaged by a contractor as contract labour lies with the contractor as per the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Sections 21(4), 18, 19 and 20 and the Andhra Pradesh Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Rules, 1971, Rules 21 and 25(v)(a).
If the contractor fails to make payment of wages, then the Principal employer shall be liable to make payment of wages in full or the unpaid balance due to the workers.
However, the Principal employer is entitled to recover the amount so paid from the contractor.
Section 21(4) has no application to a situation where a contractor may have paid wages but has not complied with the condition imposed by Rule 25(v)(a) of Rules 1971.
The court is not called upon to pronounce on the rights of contract labour employed by contractor to recover these amounts.
The appellant, who is the principal employer, is not liable to pay an additional amount under Section 21(4).
Thus, the direction of the High Court to the appellant to pay additional wages as per the provisions of Rule 25(v)(a) of Rules, 1971, is set aside.
