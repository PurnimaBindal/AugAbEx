According to Section 4 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, liability for compensation rests with the employer if an employee is injured in an accident while on the job.
However, in a case where a fake driving license is used, the liability to pay compensation rests with the insurance company.
In the present case, as the driving licence of the truck driver was not found to be fake, the insurance company is thus liable to pay the compensation.
