Review/Recalling of orders - While the Supreme Court Orders and Rules, including Order XL, Rule 1 and Civil Procedure Code, Order 47, Rule 1, do not preclude the courts from recalling or reviewing their own orders, the court has the power to do so if it believes it is in the interest of justice.
