Admissions to Medical Colleges - Filling up of seats left vacant from the all India quota - If any seats remain unfilled under the all India quota, they will revert to the State quota - The directions given under Sandhya Kabra's case are applicable for the future.
[Para 10&19]

Admissions/Education - Admissions to Medical Colleges - Filling up of seats left vacant or future resultant vacancies - Candidates who are put in the waiting list have the right to be called for counselling against the resultant vacancies - Eligibility for this option is open - If seats fall vacant in Delhi after the first counselling session, they will remain in the Delhi quota - The option to be placed in waiting list must be in writing - Waiting list candidates should not be ignored for consideration just because they opted for it on their own.
[Paras 14 and 15]
