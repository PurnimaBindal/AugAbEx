Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 85(a) - Punishment for failure to pay contribution - The payment of contribution by an employer during a criminal proceeding cannot be used as a defense to show that the payment is not an offense under Section 85(a) of the Employees State Insurance Act.
[Para 7]

Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 85(a) - Failure to pay contribution - Punishment - The first respondent did not pay the required contribution but paid it during an ongoing court proceeding and requested mercy, which was granted by the trial court finding the respondent not guilty.
However, this approach was deemed illegal by the court, and the first respondent was found guilty under section 85(a).
As the first respondent's Bar license was cancelled and incurred heavy losses, the minimum sentence of imprisonment was waived, and the first respondent was instead ordered to pay a fine of Rs. 5000/-.
[Paras 7 and 8]
