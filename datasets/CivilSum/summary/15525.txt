Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination - To prevent termination, continuous service of 240 days is required.
- After assessing testimony from both parties, the court concluded that the employee did not provide evidence of working 240 days the year before his termination.
- The award upholding the termination order is not flawed on its face and should not be interfered with.
