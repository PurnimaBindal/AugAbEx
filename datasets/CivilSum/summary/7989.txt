Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Section 33(C)(2) - Claim of arrears - In a proceeding under section 33C(2), where applicants do not question the validity or legality of undertakings given in a settlement, promotees must receive one increment of higher responsibility.
Petitioners had given up all their claims, and their claim of arrears of salaries and dues made in application under section 33-C(2) of Industrial Disputes Act as well as the Writ stands dismissed.
It is held that a proceeding under Section 33-C(2) is in the nature of execution proceedings, and the rights and obligations of parties cannot be adjudicated therein.
The application under Industrial Dispute Act and Writ are rightly dismissed because they are not based on a pre-existing right, and the undertaking giving rise to the right of enforcement is itself in dispute.
