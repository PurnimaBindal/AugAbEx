Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 3 - Karnataka Contract Carriages Acquisition Act, 1976, Section 19(3), 19(8) - Gratuity - Eligibility - According to section 19(3) of 1972 Act, any person solely employed in connection with acquired property becomes an employee of Corporation - Respondent was employed as such - Therefore, Corporation is liable to pay gratuity.
[Para 5] B Interpretation of Statute - It is established that when two or more interpretations are possible, the Court should choose one that benefits the beneficiary under the Statute.
