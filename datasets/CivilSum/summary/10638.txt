Despite having suffered a disability during Army service, an individual discharged on their own request and not on account of medical disability is not entitled to disability pension under Army Headquarter Letter No. A/09381/AG/PS 4(d) dated 10/19th June, 1970.
In the case of the petitioner, although they were assessed to "medical category "B" Permanent" and had a disability attributable to or aggravated by military service, since they sought their discharge for personal reasons, they are not eligible for disability pension based on that basis, according to the rules outlined in paras 2, 8, and 9.
