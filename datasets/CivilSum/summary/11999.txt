Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Selection Process - Group D post - Employment exchange sponsorship- Respondent 7 filed writ petition alleging that she was prevented from participating in the selection process because her name was not sponsored by employment exchange - Pending the writ petition, R7 was allowed to participate in the selection process, and she occupied the first position in the panel prepared by the selection committee - The petitioner argued that Respondent 7 was ineligible for the selection process because her writ was dismissed - The institute's decision to allow Respondent 7 to participate in the selection process was found to be valid, and the petitioner's contention that Respondent 7 should not have been considered as an eligible candidate was rejected.
