Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Rule 173, Appendix II Para 7 - Disability pension - Requirement of disclosure of disease at enrolment - Petitioner discharged due to "CHRONIC SUPPURATIVE OTITIS MEDIA (BIL)" after 6 months and 16 days of service - No evidence of disease being caused or worsened by military service - Petitioner aware of illness before enlistment, but failed to disclose it to Medical Board - Board found disease to be pre-existing and not related to military service, rendering petitioner ineligible for disability pension.
