Civil Procedure Code, Order 41 Rule 4 - Appeal/Necessary parties - Any person who is aggrieved and party to a decree is entitled to appeal against it - Co-defendants or co-respondents are not necessary parties to be included in appeal if not affected by the decree - A decree in favour of an employee of a Corporation cannot be enforced against the State Government or the Chairman of the Corporation - The appeal by the Corporation can proceed without impleading co-defendants, Chairman and the State as either appellants or respondents - The appeal is not invalid due to non-joinder of necessary parties, and does not result in conflicting decrees.
