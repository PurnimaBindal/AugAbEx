Under Article 16 of the Constitution of India, a deceased petitioner's period of service is being claimed for pension, despite the District Board having taken a stand against this.
The deceased father was allowed to continue working beyond the date of superannuation by the Mines Board in anticipation of government sanction, but an extension proposal was rejected.
The Mines Board subsequently ordered a recovery of wages paid during the extension period from Death-cum-Retiral benefits, but there is no evidence of any wrongdoing on the part of the retired employee.
Based on the 1997(1) PLJR 876 case, no recovery can be made from Retiral Benefits.
