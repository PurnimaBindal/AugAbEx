The prayer for pay revision cannot be justified as a right under Article 21 of the Constitution of India and is not applicable or forceful to claim pay revision, according to the Constitution of India, Articles 16, 21 and 226.
[Para 23 and 24] B.
The maintainability of an appeal for revision of pay and opening of pension scheme is not permissible, according to Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 96(3) and Order 43, Rule 1A, which implies that the Judgment under appeal, which was a consent order, cannot be appealed.
[Para 35] C. The revision of pay and the opening of Pension Scheme do not come under any right or domain of concern.
The employees of the concerned bank, who are the petitioners in this case, are not Govt. employees, but employees of the Govt. company, which has a separate juristic entity.
Therefore, the central govt. cannot be held liable for payment of the additional amount due to the revision of pay.
