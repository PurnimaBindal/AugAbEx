The Rules regarding Promotion in Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 have been amended, stating that vacancies should be filled based on the Recruitment Rules prevalent at the time they arise.
Changes to the Rules under Article 309 of the Constitution can only apply to future vacancies, not ones already in existence.
When promotions were 100% for vacancies that then later allow some percentage of direct recruitment, amendment rules won't apply to vacancies existing prior to the amendment.
[Paras 9, 12 and 14]  B.
Ad hoc service which continued uninterrupted till regularisation must be counted for determining seniority when regular vacancies remained unfilled at that time in Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 on Promotion.
