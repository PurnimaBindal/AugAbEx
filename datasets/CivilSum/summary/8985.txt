Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 16 - Disciplinary enquiry - Criminal trial/proceedings - Dismissal - Appellant faced departmental enquiry simultaneously with criminal trial and proceedings, resulting in dismissal - Subsequently acquitted by criminal court - The acquittal did not affect the punishment previously imposed based on the enquiry - Authorities had no justification to reconsider the case after the enquiry had already concluded more than two years prior and a final verdict had been reached by the criminal court.
[Paras 6 and 8]
