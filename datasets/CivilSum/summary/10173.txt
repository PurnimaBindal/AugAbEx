According to Section 19 of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, the parameters for the Junior Accounts Officer post test were relaxed.
However, it was questioned whether this was permissible for candidates in the reserved category.
In a similar case involving reserved category employees who had also taken the test, the High Court dismissed the Tribunal's order of not granting the benefit of relaxed parameters.
Therefore, the petition was allowed.
