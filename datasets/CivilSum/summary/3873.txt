Constitution of India, Articles 226, and 14 - Benefit of pension - Petitioner wants parity with other Universities of State for grant of benefit of pension - Directions sought against respondents to grant benefit of pension in lieu of University Contributory Fund - Pension Scheme introduced at Kurukshetra University and MD University from 141995 - In Agricultural University, Hisar it was introduced from 1.1.1992 - Decision held that principles of parity cannot be applied when Universities follow different set of service conditions - Paying capacity is relevant while granting benefit of pension - Employees of other Universities cannot claim benefit as a matter of right when it has been granted with effect from different dates - Petition dismissed.
