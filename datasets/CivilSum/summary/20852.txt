According to Section 2(aa) of the Apprentices Act 1961, Section 12-A of the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, and Rule 3(1) of the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Central Rules, 1946, "employee" is defined to exclude apprentices engaged under the Apprentices Act or under the Standing Orders.
Therefore, "Apprentices" engaged under the "Standing Orders" of an establishment are not considered to be employees under Section 2(f).
