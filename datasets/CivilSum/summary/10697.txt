According to the Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923 and the Employees State Insurance Act of 1948, Sections 39, 40, 44, and 53, an employer is liable for compensation.
However, in a claim made under the 1923 Act, the employer has disputed the applicability of the 1948 Act.
The appellant has not secured the necessary employee and employer contributions as per the 1948 Act and has failed to comply with required book-keeping procedures.
Consequently, the provisions of Sections 39, 40, and 44 of the 1948 Act are deemed violated by the appellant, precluding them from benefiting under Section 53 of the Act.
This is considered a desperate attempt by the appellant to avoid liability under the 1923 Act.
