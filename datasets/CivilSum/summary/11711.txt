Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admissions - Education - Waiting list - If a merit candidate does not report by the last fixed date, the seat will be filled from the waiting list - Various candidates are not admitted upon reporting after the deadline - However, an applicant who reported late with incomplete documents and obtained their eligibility after the deadline was given admission without offering it to a waiting list candidate as per Rule - The authority's actions were found to be biased and arbitrary - Petition granted - Waiting candidate directed to be admitted and shortage of lectures to be condoned.
