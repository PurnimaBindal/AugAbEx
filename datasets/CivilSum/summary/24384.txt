Constitution of India, Article 16 - Probation - Appointment on probation for a specified period - If the probation period has expired and the employment has ended, employees cannot seek any compensation - It has been decided that an appointment made on a probationary/ad-hoc basis for a limited period of time comes to an end when that time has elapsed and the current incumbent has no claim to the position.
Decision based on 1992(3) SCT 742 (SC) [Para 5].
