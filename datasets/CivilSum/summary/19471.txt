Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Part-time job - Sanction - Termination for non-grant of sanction to continue the part-time post.
Despite admitting that the work of the part-time water carrier had continued, the competent authority failed to grant further sanction to continue the post, and no reason was given for the failure.
This resulted in the petitioner being deprived of his livelihood.
It was ruled that the petitioner must be reinstated and that the competent authority must grant sanction for the post in the same terms as it was going on.
