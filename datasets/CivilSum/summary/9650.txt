Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Reversion - Ground of not meeting promotion qualifications - Petitioner rose from Chemical and Metallurgical Assistant to Laboratory Supdt Grade II and then Grade I - Although the petitioner competed for promotion as Assistant Chemist and Metallurgical, objections were raised by other candidates - Respondents argue that the petitioner's promotion as Laboratory Supdt Grade II and Grade I was erroneous - Petitioner argues that their practical experience makes up for any lack in qualifications - However, non-science graduates can only be promoted up to the post of CMA per Railway Board Instructions, and the petitioner was given a relaxation of qualifications for promotion - Held, following Dharambir Singh, administrative experience cannot substitute for knowledge and accepting the petitioner's plea would mean relaxing the educational qualification rule.
