The claim by the Administration that the ASM/SM cadre was a combined one in South East Railway lacked substantiation in any document, letter or order.
A letter from the Additional District Pay Commissioner to the General Manager recognized the existence of a separate ASM/SM cadre, however, this cannot be considered satisfactory or accurate.
The ASM/SM cadres were separate and distinct prior to 1983, and have now become a single entity with the abolition of the option.
The Railway Board requires revised percentages for the category, depending on whether the existing cadre structure is a combined one or a structured one.
As the cadre in South Eastern Railway is a separate one, the Chief Personnel Office deviated from the scheme by applying an alternative that was to be adopted by a zone where combined cadre existed.
The SMs should have been designated as Deputy Station Superintendents automatically, and should not have been subjected to selection procedures if alternative 'II' was adopted.
The implementation of the Chief Personnel Officer letter is beneficial to the majority of the employees, both employee unions accept this.
However, all of the employees who had opted before 1983 must be entitled to the benefit that would have been available to them on their options.
