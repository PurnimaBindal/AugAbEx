According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, a fair and proper enquiry must be conducted by the employer before taking any action against an employee.
If no enquiry is held or if the enquiry is found to be defective, the employee can ask the Labour Court for permission to present evidence justifying the action.
In this particular case, the management requested an opportunity to present evidence in their written statement, should the decision be adverse.
However, the Labour Court ordered the workman's reinstatement without giving the management the chance to justify the termination.
As a result, the award is flawed and requires remand.
