Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Providing equal pay for equal work - The petitioner sustained a 40% disability while working as a conductor, rendering him unfit for the job.
He was then made a Booking Clerk but at the pay scale of a conductor - This is not permissible as no one must be forced to work at a higher rank and lower pay.
Respondents must pay the petitioner as a Booking Clerk.
