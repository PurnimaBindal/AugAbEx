Input: "In Bihar, there is a question of whether SC members who were promoted as Executive Engineers in excess of the quota should be pushed down to lower posts or in matters of seniority.
The petitioners, who are Assistant Engineers, are alleging that a number of Scheduled Castes members have been promoted in excess of their quota.
However, the respondents have stated that only 128 SC/ST members have been promoted to Class-I service out of a total of 675 B.E.S.
Class-I positions, of which 159 are reserved for SC/ST members.
Therefore, it cannot be said that promotions were granted in excess of the reserved quota (Paras 18-20).
Also, according to the Bihar Reservation of Vacancies in Posts and Services Act, 1991, Section 4(6)(a), if posts meant for ST category could not be filled for three consecutive years due to non-availability of eligible ST members, they can be filled up from amongst SC members, by exchange (Paras 17 and 19)."
