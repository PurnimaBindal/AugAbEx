Employment Exchange (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959, Section 4(2) - Violation of Provision - Minority Educational Institution - Whether an establishment under Section 4(2) of Act - Notification not issued by appropriate government as per Section 4(2) of Act - It has been determined that Minority educational institution is not considered an establishment within the meaning of the Act; therefore, provisions of Act cannot be violated.
