Constitution of India, Articles 115 and 226 - Judgments and presidents - Contract of service - Enforcing service contract specifically in writ jurisdiction - Single Judge Bench has to abide by Division Bench judgment - Order passed by Supreme Court or High Court presumed to consider relevant facts - Case remanded to single Bench to determine whether writ jurisdiction can enforce service contract, based on Division Bench judgment.
