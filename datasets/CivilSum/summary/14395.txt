Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection Grade - Criteria and prerequisites to grant of selection grade - Punjab Govt. directions issued on 331980 pertaining to grant of selection grade to 20% most-senior employees in the cadre, subject to completion of 15 years of service, in the absence of a promotion post - Prerequisites involving completion of 15 years service, seniority and availability of post in 20% posts of the cadre must be interpreted in coherence - A junior cannot receive a selection grade if a senior in the cadre hasn't been granted it yet, as a junior cannot receive a higher pay scale than his senior in the cadre unless it is his personal pay - Nevertheless, if a junior has already been granted selection grade in a cadre, seniors cannot be refused the same for any reason.
