Army Act, 1950, Sections 38 and 39 (a) and (b) - Court Martial - Over stayal of leave - Absence from duty - Reasonable cause - The petitioner claimed that he was injured badly after an accident while travelling during leave and was admitted to the hospital in unconsciousness by passers-by.
The petitioner argued that Section 38 was not applicable in his case, and Court Martial could not be held.
However, the petitioner's plea was not properly considered by the Army Authorities.
Consequently, the order of dismissal was set aside, and the authorities were directed to reconsider the petitioner's case with careful consideration as no such action was taken in similar cases.
