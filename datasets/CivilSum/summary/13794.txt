Constitution of India, Article 16 - Order of reversion - Interim order - Modification of interim order - No reason given in order - Sustainability - The court held that after an ex-parte ad-interim order is passed, a vested right starts accruing in favour of the person.
Therefore, the court must indicate its reasoning for taking away that right when vacating, modifying or varying the order.
The best way to do this is by assigning reasons and grounds in support thereof.
