According to Punjab Civil Service Rules in Vol. II, Rule 6.17(3) and the General Insurance (Employees) Pension Scheme of 1995, para 2(1) states that the definition of family for family pension entitlement must include both mother and father.
It is deemed unreasonable and unsustainable to exclude parents from the definition of family in Rule 6.17 and Para 2(I) of the GIC (Employees) Pension Scheme.
Hence, parents should not be deprived of the benefit of family pension.
