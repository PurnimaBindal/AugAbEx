In accordance with Section 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act, the authority responsible for granting admission to a group of 50 reserved seats for girls awarded admission to only 49.
A court ruling later ordered that the 50th seat be granted to the candidate first in line on the waiting list.
A girl candidate who was not next in line, nonetheless filed a contempt petition.
Unfortunately, the petition was subsequently dismissed.
