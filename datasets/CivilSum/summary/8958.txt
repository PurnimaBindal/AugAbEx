The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 (4-A) (as amended by Karnataka Amendment Act) - Refusal to refer a dispute - Merely refusing to refer a dispute to a Labor Court does not end the industrial dispute, and the government may reconsider and make a reference in appropriate cases to promote industrial peace.
Refusing to refer the dispute does not exhaust the government's power to make a reference.
[Para 12] B.
The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 (4-A) - Rejection of a dispute reference by the government does not mean that an application under Section 10 (4-A) is not valid, as it can still be filed before the Labor Court within the time prescribed.
The provisions contained in Section 10 (4-A) have an overriding effect over other provisions.
Refusing to reference earlier does not bar an application under Section 10 (4-A) under any of the provisions contained in Section 10.
