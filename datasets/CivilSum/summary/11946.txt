Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Regularisation - Petitioners employed as Sub-Assistant Engineers for four/five years - Being qualified and allowed to work for 4/5 years, it does not make sense to form a district level committee to screen their cases and deny their claim at the time of permanent appointment - Formation of the committee goes against the law - Petitioners' cases must be reviewed for permanent appointment.
