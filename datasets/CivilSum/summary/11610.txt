Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Selection proceedings - Quashing of - Petitioners employed in class IV posts - Challenging selection proceedings to post of EDP Assistant Grade III - Respondents filing affidavit that none of the petitioners have requisite qualification for the post - Petitioner contending that appointed candidates have neither come through employment exchange nor public advertisement - Respondent contending that persons appointed are meritorious candidates and were previously adhoc employees of respondent - Adhoc appointment of respondents never challenged by Petitioners - Petitioners requesting that the petition be considered in public interest - It was held that none of the Petitioners meet the minimum qualification requirement and the basic recruitment procedures for other persons have been followed, thus no relief can be granted - Accordingly, the petition is dismissed.
