Kurukshetra University Act, Section 11(g) - General Rules for Examination, Clause 9 - Kurukshetra University Calendar Volume II (G), Paragraph 186 - Examination - Reevaluation of Answer Sheets - The petitioner appeared for BCom.
part II examination, declared failed, and applied for re-evaluation.
The result was declared on 12.11.98, stating that the petitioner had passed and was given casual admission in BCom.
part III subject to the approval of the university.
However, the approval was declined by the university on the basis that the petitioner was admitted late in part III.
It was determined that the university was at fault for not declaring the result in a timely manner and that the petitioner should not suffer for the university's fault.
The university was directed to declare the petitioner's result for BCom.
part III for when she was given casual admission.
[Paras 7, 8 and 9]
