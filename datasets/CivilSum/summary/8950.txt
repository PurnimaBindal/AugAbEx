Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 Section 10 - Award given by the Labour Court - The award was ex parte in nature - The state of Madhya Pradesh was not brought on record through the appropriate Secretary or through the Collector - An appearance was made by some employee, but nothing on record indicates that the employee was duly authorized - Consequently, the ex parte award was set aside.
However, the concerned employee who stood reinstated was given a direction to continue in service until the Labour Court passes an order to the contrary.
[Para 3] B.
Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, Order 29 Rule 1 - Service on State - The proper course is that the State should be impleaded through the appropriate Secretary or through the Collector - However, in this case, such procedure was not adopted - As a result, the ex parte award was set aside.
