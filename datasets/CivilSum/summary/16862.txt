Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Selection - Ownership of property - The possession of a registered property ownership document cannot be a pre-condition for appointment as Extra Departmental Branch Post Master (EDBPM) - The ability to lawfully manage and use the property where post offices and public call offices can be established is enough - Complete ownership is not a requirement as per Clause 3(iii) of the Notification of qualifications and conditions for appointment of EDBPM.
