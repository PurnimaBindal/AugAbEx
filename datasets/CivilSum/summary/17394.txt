Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rule 2 (as amended and applicable in Haryana) - The definition of "military service" was amended in Haryana to reflect that it only includes service during the period of emergency - Those who joined the military prior to this amendment are not entitled to benefits available to all those who served during the emergency - The petitioner had joined the Army before the emergency and is therefore not entitled to any benefits of military service even for the emergency period.
