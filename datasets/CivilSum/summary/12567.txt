The Punjab State Legislature Members (Pension and Medical Facilities Regulation) Act, 1977, Section 2 - Rule providing pension and medical facilities to Ex-Members of the Punjab Legislative Assembly, the Punjab Legislative Council and the Legislative Assembly of erstwhile State of Patiala and East Punjab States Union, provided they have been members of either house for a period of atleast five years after the commencement of Constitution of India - Challenge to the rule alleging the same to be discriminatory qua those who remained the members of the Punjab Provincial Legislative Assembly elected under the Government of India Act, 1935 before the commencement of the Constitution of India - Allegation that the petitioner is not equal to those elected under the Constitution of India or under the Representation of Peoples Act - No discrimination or arbitrariness can be attributed to the action of the Government - Reasonable classification between unequals - Petitioner is not entitled to the benefit of pension and medical facilities granted to a distinct class.
[Paras 4 & 5] B.
In regards to reasonable classification - To pass the tests of permissible classification, two conditions must be fulfilled, namely(i) classification must be founded on an intelligible differentia distinguishing persons grouped together from others left out group and (ii) differentia must have a rational relation to the object sought to be achieved - The Legislature takes care to reasonably classify persons - If it deals equally with all persons belonging to a well defined class, it is not open to deny equal protection on the ground that the law does not apply to others.
