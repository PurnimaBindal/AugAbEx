State Planning Board (Class - III) Service Rules 1988, Rule 18, Appendix-B - Qualification/Relaxation of qualifications - The post of junior Scale Stenographer has specific educational qualifications, experience, and a qualifying test - The rules do not permit relaxation of educational qualifications and experience, but the condition of passing the test can be relaxed - An order issued by the state government to relax the condition of passing the test is not against the rules and can be referred to the rule.
