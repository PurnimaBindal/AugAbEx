Kerala Education Rules, 1959, Chapter XIV-A, Rule 43 (As amended in 1986) - Proviso - Promotion as High School Assistant - Circulars cannot equate subject ratio with subject requirement - The proviso can only be applied when there are enough teachers in the core subject for which vacancies are available - The proviso aims to prevent stagnation and provide more opportunities for promotion.
