Based on the evidence presented, the Court found that the primary duties of petitioners, who were recruited as Senior Engineers, were not manual or technical in nature and involved the supervision of employees subordinate to them.
As such, the Court below was correct in finding that the petitioners were not considered "workman" under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(s).
The Court sees no reason to interfere with these findings.
