Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission/Education/Admission in the Postgraduate Courses in the Medical College, Rohtak in specialities of Orthopaedics and Surgery in degree courses Group I and II - Candidates appearing for both diploma and degree course should only have one interview - Selection for both courses will be based on higher merit in two interviews for those who appeared twice - It is illogical to hold a separate interview for candidates applying for admission in the same specialty for both degree and diploma courses when they are required to appear before the same Committee for Group I and II.
