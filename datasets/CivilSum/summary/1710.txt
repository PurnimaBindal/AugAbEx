The petitioner was promoted to the Punjab Superior Judicial Service as a District and Session Judge.
However, the pay scales for the subordinate and superior services were fixed at Rs. 4100-5000 and Rs. 3200-4700, respectively, resulting in an anomaly and a violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
The court directed the government to create a new pay scale of Rs. 4500-5700 and protect the basic pay of the petitioner and similarly situated persons.
[Paras 2, 3, 6, 7, 12, and 13] B.
The rule providing a lower pay scale for promotional posts from the Subordinate Judicial Service to the Superior Judicial Service is unreasonable.
It results in junior persons retaining a higher pay scale in the subordinate service and bypassed individuals receiving a lower pay scale.
This violates the equality clause under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
