Constitution of India, Article 226 - CISF Rules, Rule 19 - Probation - Termination - The Petitioner was appointed as a constable in the Central Industrial Security force on probation for two years with a termination clause in case he is found unsuitable for retention.
The Petitioner was later terminated from service.
The order was challenged on the grounds that it was passed without initiating any departmental proceedings and was punitive.
However, it was found that the performance of the Petitioner was not satisfactory and a number of complaints were received regarding his conduct and punctuality.
Rule 19 provides for discharge or confirmation of a person after considering his record during the probation period.
The Respondent was of the opinion that the Petitioner was not likely to become an efficient member based on his overall performance and conduct.
Thus, the discharge order cannot be considered punitive as it was a termination simpliciter and not a punishment.
The employer has the absolute right to assess the suitability of a person before confirmation, therefore, the Petitioner was dismissed accordingly.
[Paras 7 to 9]
