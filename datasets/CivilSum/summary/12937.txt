The Constitution of India's Articles 21 and 38 and the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995's Section 47(1) prohibit the removal or compulsory retirement of disabled employees due to acquired disability during service.
Employers cannot terminate a disabled employee's job on the grounds of incapacity alone, as such people are entitled to equal treatment as able citizens under Section 47(1) of the Act.
Quashing the retirement order is thus justified.
