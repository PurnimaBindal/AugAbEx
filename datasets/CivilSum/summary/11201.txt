The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226 and 227 along with Army Rules, 1954, Rule 13(3)III (iii) and Pension Regulations for the Army, 1961, Regulations 173, 186(2) specify disability pension consisting of disability and service element.
When disability is assessed as nil after three years, there is a stoppage of disability pension, but the petitioner is entitled to service element of pension as per rates fixed by Central Government with arrears of 38 months preceding filing of writ as it was argued that the service element was contingent upon continuance of the disability element.
