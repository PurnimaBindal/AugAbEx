Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 12 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 33-C and 10(1) - Dispute between Workmen and Management - The award given by the Tribunal in terms of the settlement arrived at between the parties must be complied with - The Management failing to comply with the terms of the award does not amount to contempt - The award can be executed u/s 33-C of Industrial Disputes Act - Industrial Tribunal is not a court subordinate to High Court.
Civil Appeal No. 2050 of 1990, decided on 24.4.1990 (SC) relied.
