Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 21 - Haryana Compassionate Assistance to Dependents of Deceased Govt. Employees Rules, 2003, Rules 4 and 8 - Compassionate appointment - Compassionate appointment is only granted in exceptional circumstances where the family is in such dire financial straits that only a compassionate appointment can alleviate the crisis - The rule providing financial aid sufficiently meets the need - Granting compassionate appointment to someone who was not entitled to it is not grounds for discrimination to claim the same relief.
[Paras 13 and 14]
