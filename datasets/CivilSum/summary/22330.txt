Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Tort - Damages - Delay in declaration of result - The person aggrieved must prove violation of their fundamental rights resulting in great prejudice and actual injury caused, and not for every slight deviation from the rule by authorities - If the petitioner has not suffered any prejudice, loss, or injury despite proof of negligence by the authority, the court will not award damages - In this case, the petitioner's delay in result did not affect their future and they already got admission in another course despite failing, therefore, compensation cannot be awarded.
