Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - Factories Act, 1948, Section 46 - Andhra Pradesh Factories Rules, 1950, Rules 65 to 71 - Writ Jurisdiction/Authority - Article 226 of the Constitution's term 'Authority' is not restricted to the definition for the purpose of 'State' in Article 12 - The usage of 'any person or authority' is not confined only to statutory authorities and instrumentalities of the State - It covers any other person or body that performs a public duty, regardless of whether they are part of the State or not.
Public-interest rules impose a public duty on each management to provide a canteen, and the High Court may issue a writ directing the management to do so if they ignore this obligation.
