Section 2-A of the Employees' Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 states that different departments or branches of an establishment will be considered parts of the same establishment.
To determine if two units are distinct, the court must examine the functional integrality between them, whether one unit cannot exist without the other, and if the employer has integrated them in finance and employment.
