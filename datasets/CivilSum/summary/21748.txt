Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - The Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 47(2) - Railway Establishment Manual, Para 531(b) - Promotions - Physically disabled individuals cannot be excluded from holding a position in public employment after the 1995 Act's implementation, except for certain posts or departments, which can be exempted through appropriate government notification.
If no such notification exists, the Proviso to Section 47(2) of the Act is not enforceable, and physically disabled individuals can be promoted or appointed to any post or department.
[Paras 7 and 9] B. Proviso - The proviso's standard role is to exempt something out of the enactment or to qualify something enacted therein that would otherwise fall under the enactment's purview.
However, if the language of the enactment does not contain the provisions cited to be present in it, one cannot derive these provisions from a proviso.
[Para 8] C Document - When a later clause only qualifies an earlier clause and does not destroy it entirely, the two clauses should be interpreted together, and the parties' intent as a whole should be given effect.
However, if a later clause follows an earlier clause that destroys the obligation created by the earlier clause altogether, the later clause should be discarded as contradictory, and the earlier clause should prevail.
