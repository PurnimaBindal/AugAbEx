Constitution of India, Article 16 - Retiral benefits - Refusal of - Reinstated after acquittal - Deceased - The husband of the Petitioner was suspended but reinstated after being acquitted in criminal proceedings, However, no decision was made in the departmental proceedings initiated against him until the husband of the Petitioner died - The State Government passed a resolution stating that if an employee dies before the conclusion of departmental or criminal proceedings, his services are to be counted for all purposes and benefits, and the period of suspension is to be considered as being on duty - It was ruled that since no final order was made against the deceased, the Petitioner is entitled to all benefits, including pension - Therefore the petition was granted.
