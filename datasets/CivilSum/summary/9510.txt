Guru Nanak Dev University Calendar 1999, Vol. III, Ordinance 6 and Ordinances 1 to 5 of Chapter III(i) - Admissions - Professional Courses - Candidates for professional courses must adhere to the provisions of Ordinances 1 to 5 of Chapter III(i) and the stated cut-off dates for admission.
Those who do not produce their final exam results at the time of interview, as specified in the Prospectus, will be deemed ineligible for admission, regardless of any delays in result declarations.
Strict adherence to these rules is necessary to regulate admissions and avoid chaos.
Judgments have also established the importance of clear cut-off dates for admissions to professional courses, in the interest of students.
