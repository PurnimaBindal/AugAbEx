Mr. Kowturu Vinaya Kumar, Counsel represented the Petitioner, while Smt. YB Saraswathi, learned Additional Central Government Standing Counsel represented the Respondent.
According to the Constitution of India, under Article 300-A and the Swatantra Sainik Samman Pension Scheme of 1980, the Freedom Fighter's Pension was granted.
In the petitioner's application, he mistakenly stated that he was detained in Cam pat Hanam Konda, but the Report of the State Government and certificate of Camp incharge showed that the petitioner actually participated in the camp at Chanda during 1947-48.
Therefore, the petitioner is eligible to receive the granted pension.
