Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Criteria for qualification - Eligibility will be evaluated solely with regard to the last date of receipt of applications specified in the advertisement - Individuals who obtain qualifications after this date will not be considered at all.

Constitution of India, Article 141 - Precedents - Per incuriam ruling - A reference to an already-decided matter by the Supreme Court on that point would be redundant; a High Court decision made in ignorance of a Supreme Court ruling would be considered per incuriam.
