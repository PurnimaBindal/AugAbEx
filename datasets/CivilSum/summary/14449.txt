Constitution of India, Article 16 - Andhra Pradesh Education Act, 1982, Section 2 - Andhra Pradesh Educational Institutions (Establishment, Recognition, Administration and Control of Schools Under the Private Management) Rules, 1993, Rule 10(13) - Age - The government has not specified any age limit for teacher appointments in the AP Education Act rules.
Therefore, the selected petitioner cannot be rejected for exceeding the age of 36, as determined by the duly constituted selection committee.
