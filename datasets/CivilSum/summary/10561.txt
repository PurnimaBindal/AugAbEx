The Gujarat Lower Revenue Qualifying Examination Rules, 1978, Rule 5 Proviso is in accordance with the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309.
It requires clerks to pass the departmental examination for promotion after five years of continuous service.
The classification of employees is justifiable as it is based on the objective of efficiency in administration.
The rule is not discriminatory or arbitrary, and does not violate Articles 14 and 16.
[Para 14] B The Gujarat Lower Revenue Qualifying Examination Rules, 1978, Rule 5, requires employees to pass the relevant revenue qualifying examination before being considered for promotion.
However, the proviso treats unequals as equals and limits equally situated candidates to unequal chances, consequently, it violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution, and is declared null and void.
