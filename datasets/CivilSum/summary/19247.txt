Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - The Selection Board must assess all candidates in the lower rank to make promotion decisions - No one has a right to promotion and decisions must be based on objective assessment of an entire record - The petitioner was found unfit for promotion compared to other officers in his batch - Mala fides are not attributed to the Selection Board.
[Para 4] B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Promotion is based on merit, not seniority alone - The petitioner cannot complain against the decision of the Selection Board - The Selection Board found him fit for promotion in 1992 and therefore, no mala fide can be attributed to them - The petitioner's request to be promoted with his batch-mates of 1973 cannot be granted.
