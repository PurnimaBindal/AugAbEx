Constitution of India, Article 14 - Allotment of houses - Cut off date - Low Income Group Housing Scheme - Govt. employees must purchase houses on hire-purchase - Houses were converted from rental basis scheme - Resolution dated June 22, 1972 resolved to allot all houses to Govt. employees on hire-purchase - June 22, 1972 was used as the date for fixing entitlement for priorities mentioned in the resolutions and allotment of houses - Fixation of the date is necessary for allotment of houses - The date is rational and does not offend Article 14 of the Constitution.

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Allotment of houses - Discrimination - Hire-purchase scheme - Conversion of rental housing scheme into Hire Purchase Housing Scheme for low income Govt. employees - Category (iii) persons treated on par with category (vi) employees - Those who managed to remain in possession cannot claim right to allotment under hire-purchase scheme.
[Para 13] C. Constitution of India, Articles 19(e), 21, 38, 39 and 46 - Allotment of houses - Poor Govt. employees - The right to settlement and residence is a fundamental right under Article 19(1)(e) and a facet of the right to life under Article 21 - The State must provide permanent housing accommodation for the poor in the housing schemes it undertakes or through its instrumentalities within their economic means, so they can pay the price in easy instalments and have permanent settlement and residence under Articles 19(1)(e) and 21 of the Constitution.
