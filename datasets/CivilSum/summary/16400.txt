Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 7.18 - Termination - Notice - Temporary employee - Service can be terminated with one month notice - Appointment letter cannot give power to terminate service with one month notice - Residuary clause cannot be invoked to give such power - Termination is considered arbitrary in the absence of evidence that the employee was appointed on purely temporary basis and was unsatisfactory in work and conduct, thus having no right to the post.
[Paras 6, 7 and 8]
