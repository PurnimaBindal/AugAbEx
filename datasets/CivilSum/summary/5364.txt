Board's Miscellaneous Rules, 1947, Rule 157(3)(j) read with correction slips - Promotion - Efficiency bars - Employees working in rural offices - Whether they need to pass accounts exam for promotion to selection grade - Government had issued administrative instructions to fill the gap to the extent of laying down principles for promotion to higher grades or posts - Petitioners claimed passing of test was required prior to amalgamation of posts of LDC and UDCs and before creation of common cadre and not thereafter - However, court held that clerks are required to pass the accounts exam as a condition for promotion to selection grade since the existing provision was made applicable to selection grade by the State Government by amending Rule 157(3)(j).

Constitution of India, Article 309 read with Rule 157(3)(j) of the Boards Miscellaneous Rules, 1947 - Administrative instructions - Statutory Rules - Validity - In the absence of Statutory rules, the government can issue administrative instructions to fill the gap to the extent of laying down principles for promotion to higher grades or posts - However, it cannot amend, modify, or supersede the statutory rules - The only limitation is the administrative instructions should not be inconsistent with statutory rules - In this case, since the statutory rule has become redundant, there is no inconsistency.
