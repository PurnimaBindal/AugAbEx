The respondent was a probationary clerk for six months, with a possibility of extending for another six months.
In his extended period of probation, the respondent received a week's notice under standing orders, discharging him from service.
Although the tribunal and High Court disapproved the order of termination, they upheld the validity of the notice.
It was then discovered that the respondent had committed forgery in the receipt of the notice.
Eventually, a settlement between the parties was reached, and the respondent received a total of Rs. 45,000/- in full and final settlement.
