Army Act and Rules - Constitution of India, Article 311 - Retirement/Option/Rules - The Petitioner, a Naib Subedar, retired after 24 years of service when he had just crossed the age of 42 years.
According to the revised rules, he was entitled to serve for 26 years or up to the age of 50 years, whichever is earlier.
The Respondent pleaded that he never opted for revised rules.
The Petitioner asked for his option.
The Petitioner's services have to be governed under the revised rules.
The order of retirement was quashed, and the petition was allowed with costs.
