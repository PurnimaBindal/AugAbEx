Rajasthan (Recruitment of Dependents of Government Servants Dying while in Service) Rules 1975, Sections 5 and 10 - Compassionate ground Appointment for a Petitioner with specialized qualification of BE (computer) - A Petitioner applied for a post on compassionate grounds, but was offered a lower position - The Petitioner argues that they qualify for a post of AE as stated in a state circular - The court ruled in favor of the Petitioner, stating they must be considered for a post that suits their qualification, even if there is no available opening, and allowed the petition.
