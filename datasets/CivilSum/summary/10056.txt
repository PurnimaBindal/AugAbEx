Articles 14 and 311 of the Indian Constitution - Punjab Agricultural University Statute pertaining to Pension and Provident Fund Part A (Pension Scheme) and Pension Rules (Chapter II General Provision related to granting of pension), Rules 113 to 115, Clause 24 - Disciplinary proceedings - Ex parte disciplinary proceedings - Natural justice - Furnishing of inquiry report - The delinquent must be given a copy of the inquiry report if the punishing authority is not the inquiring authority, to enable the punishing authority to reject the findings of the inquiry officer - This is an aspect of natural justice.
