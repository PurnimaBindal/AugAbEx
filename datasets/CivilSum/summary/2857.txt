The Dental Council of India Regulations, under the Constitution of India's Articles 226 and 227, state that migration from one institute to another in the BDS course is a concession and not a legal right.
The petitioner, who is suffering from acute asthma and skin allergy and is in the third year of the course, does not meet the court's guidelines and regulations and therefore is not entitled to migration.
