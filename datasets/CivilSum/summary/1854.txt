Section 33-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Articles 12, 21 and 226 of the Constitution of India require adherence to the principles of natural justice.
Failure to comply with these principles means that the administrative authority cannot exercise powers under Section 33B without providing the opportunity for a hearing and recording of reasons.
No notice or reasons were given in this case.
