According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, promotion from the post of foreman is decided by a selection committee that considers the eligibility of candidates based on three criteria.
These criteria include qualification bearing 30 marks, length of service in the present post bearing 40 marks, and one mark awarded for every completed year of service for seniority purposes.
A Departmental Promotion Committee must be constituted, and the selection committee must follow established rules.
The private respondent in this case could not be granted 30 marks for length of service as they joined on 2.1.79.
The entire selection process in this case has been compromised.
[Paras 3, 6 and 14]

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 state that a Departmental Selection Committee must be constituted when considering promotion.
However, the constitution of the selection committee is illegal in this case as no notice was served to the power department to nominate someone to attend the meeting.
The notice is a pre-condition for holding a valid meeting, and thus the selection process in this case was compromised.
