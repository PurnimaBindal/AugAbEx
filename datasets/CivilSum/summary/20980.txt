The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 226, provide guidelines for transfer policies.
In this particular case, a police officer was harassed by superior officers through transfers and vacating his official residence.
The senior officers relied on false and frivolous reports and their conduct was condemned.
Despite this, the transfer order cannot be interfered with.
The state government was directed to provide a residential quarter for the appellant near his current place of residence.
