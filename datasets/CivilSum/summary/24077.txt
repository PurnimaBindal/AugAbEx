Constitution of India, Article 311 - West Bengal Service Rules, Part I, Rules 34(3), 174 and 175 - Termination - Absence - The respondent was terminated from service due to absence and the State Administrative Tribunal dismissed the application on the basis of delayed approach.
However, the High Court set aside the dismissal as it stated that the lapse of time does not take away the power of the court to interfere.
It was found that the proper procedure was not followed when terminating the services, so directions were given for reinstatement with seniority but no back wages, allowing authorities to take action in accordance with the law.
It was contended that the High Court erred in interfering with the termination order, but this was deemed untenable as the authorities have been given liberty to take appropriate action as per the law.
Therefore, the order of the High Court is unassailable, and the petition was dismissed accordingly.
