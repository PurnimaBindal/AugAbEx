Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Classification of employees/Pay/Pay scales - Equal pay for equal work - The Sub Divisional Engineers/Assistant Engineers working in the Haryana State Minor Irrigation Tubewells Corporation must receive equal pay scales as their peers in other Govt.
Departments and those working on deputation in the Corporation - Govt. can classify employees only if it is reasonable and related to the object sought to be achieved - There cannot be any difference in pay scale if the qualifications, duties, responsibilities etc. are the same and the posts are inter-transferable - Appointees from different sources become part of one cadre, and the source of appointment loses its significance - Discrimination based on the source of appointment is violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - The petitioners are entitled to the same pay as Government employees, and with arrears alongwith interest @ 12% from the date of accrual to the date of payment.
[Paras 10, 13 to 15]
