Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 323 - Selection - Recommendations - Approval of selection - The UPSC (Commission) is a recommendatory authority only - It is up to the Government to decide whether or not to accept the Commission's recommendations - The Government can reject the selection of an officer based on adverse remarks in their CR after selection - The Government is allowed to take into account developments that occur after the Commission makes its selection.
