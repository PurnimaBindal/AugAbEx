Army personnel who become disabled and invalidated from service may be entitled to disability pensions.
A petitioner who was invalidated out of service due to a disability and subsequently had his pension claim rejected on the grounds that his disability was not attributable to military service was found entitled to pension and arrears.
Delays by the government were considered irrelevant.
[Paras 6 to 10]

If a disease is not noted in an army personnel's enrolment records but leads to invalidation out of service, it is generally assumed to be aggravated by military service unless the Medical Board offers a sound explanation proving otherwise.
[Para 6]
