Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Eligibility - Departmental examination - If a candidate is promoted after passing departmental exams, and has been deemed qualified, fit, and actively working for at least 15 months, revoking their promotion due to an error cannot be upheld - The actions of the competent authority cannot be used to deny the employee the benefit of their exam success and force them to begin the process anew.
