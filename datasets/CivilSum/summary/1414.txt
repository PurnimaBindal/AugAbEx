Guru Nanak Dev University Calendar Volume II, 1986, Ordinance 10(a), (g), (k) & (z) and 11 - Unfair means/Misbehaviour/Punishment/Disqualifying for two years - A student charged with misbehavior with the Center Superintendent during an exam was disqualified for two years.
Despite her mother's interference and lack of proof that she actually used the hand-written paper, it was considered a blot on her career.
The authorities were urged to adopt a reformatory attitude and the disqualification was ultimately reduced to 1-1/2 years.
