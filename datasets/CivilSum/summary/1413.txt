According to Constitution of India, Article 14 and Evidence Act 1872, Section 115, a candidate who applies for selection or appointment does not acquire any right.
Even if a candidate passes the written test and interview, they cannot claim appointment as a matter of right.
Moreover, the principle of promissory estoppel does not apply against the State.
[Para 14] B Under Regional Rural Banks Act, 1976, Section 24, Recognised Banks/Appointments/Policy matters, Regional Rural Banks are controlled by the Government of India and its nationalised Banks.
As such, the Bank must abide by the directions issued by the Government with regard to policy matters, including how recruitments should be made and the life of panel.
[Para 21] C. Regional Rural Banks Act, 1976, Sections 17 and 24, Appointment/Panel/Term of panel/Extension of term of panel.
The term of panel can be extended in suitable cases by the Board of Directors, and extension is discretionary, as the rule is directory.
Those who were not adjusted claimed extension of the panel further, as the exercise of discretion had resulted in discrimination.
The reasons for non-extension of the panel were found to be wrong and posts were reserved by way of an interim order.
Therefore, the petitioners, who have knocked at the doors of the court, should be adjusted against those posts.
