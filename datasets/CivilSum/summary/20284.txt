Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19 - Selection - Service conditions - Amendment of Rules - Change in service conditions - Jurisdiction of Tribunal to interfere - The State is entitled to make changes to the rules governing service conditions and qualifications, eligibility criteria, avenues of promotion, and other conditions of service at its discretion.
The Statutory Tribunals do not have the power to direct the government to adopt a particular method of recruitment or eligibility criteria, or impose their views on the State.
The State may also restructure the pattern and cadres/categories of service by amalgamation, bifurcation, or reconstitution as required, and employees have no right to challenge the State's authority to amend the rules governing their service.
