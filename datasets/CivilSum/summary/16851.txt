Constitution of India, Article 16 - Admissions - Discrimination - LLB evening course - Admission requirement - The LL.B.
evening course is intended for employees who work within a 30 KM radius of the law faculty.
The objective of this requirement is to ensure that students can both serve and study by living within a reasonable distance.
It is not arbitrary or discriminatory for the university to impose this condition, as students who travel 120 KM and return home late at night may not be able to properly devote themselves to either their studies or their work.
