Constitution of India, Article 226 - National Airport Authority Act, 1985, Section 13 - Petitioner was an employee of respondent No. 2-Director General of Civil Aviation - On representation of petitioner, respondent No. 2 agreed to repatriation - Order of repatriation passed on 421987 intimating respondent No. 1 to relieve the petitioner - Instead of relieving the petitioner, he was transferred by respondent No. 1 - Petitioner is entitled to all consequential benefits and promotion in the office of respondent No. 2 from 421987 as no repatriation of petitioner after 421987 is illegal.
