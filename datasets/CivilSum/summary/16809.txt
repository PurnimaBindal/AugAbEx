According to the Nagaland Information and Publicity Service Rules, 1983, Rule 2, for the position of District Relation Officer/Information Officer, only the prescribed educational qualifications can be considered for eligibility, and a degree in veterinary science is not included in them.
Furthermore, under the provisions of Article 309 of the Constitution, the Service Rules take precedence over any other rules regarding selection and promotion.
Therefore, even if the petitioner had passed both the written and oral interview, they could not be qualified for the position because they did not meet the prescribed educational qualifications.
