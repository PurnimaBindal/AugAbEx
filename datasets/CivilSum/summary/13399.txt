Under the Disability Pension regulations of the Army in India, a claim for disability pension was rejected for a petitioner who sustained injuries after being thrown from a running train while returning from annual leave and was subsequently discharged from service.
However, the court ruled that it did not matter whether the injury occurred while on leave, as long as the official was on public duty when the injury was sustained.
Since the petitioner availed of his annual leave at the public's expense before his injury, he was found eligible for disability pension.
