The Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981, Rules 9(1) and 22-A along with the Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Aided Schools Retirement Benefits Scheme, 1992, Clauses 6, 7 & 8 state that an employee can only club service rendered in two recognised aided schools against an aided post if they obtained the necessary permission and relieving orders before joining the second institute.
Ineligible employees will not be entitled to continuity of service under Rule 9(1) & (2).
[Para 15] B.
The Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol. II, Rule 6.1 accompanied by the Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Aided Schools Retirement Benefits Scheme, 1992, Clause, 6, 7 & 8 specify that qualifying service for pension by granting benefit of fraction of last six-month to be rounded about for one year is unavailable under the Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Aided Schools Retirement Benefits Scheme, 1992, Clause 6.
The Scheme excludes applicability of any other Rules and has overriding effect.
[Paras 16 & 18] C. The Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Aided Schools Retirement Benefits Scheme, 1992, Clause 6(5) outlines that qualifying service for pension only counts the period in which an employee contributed to their Provident Fund account, provided there is no uncondoned break in service rendered in two institutions and the PF account is maintained.
Failure to deposit the PF contribution in a post office account during litigation does not satisfy the condition nor can the default be removed by depositing during litigation when it was the employee's responsibility to deposit.
