Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rules 4 and 9(3) - Petitioners who joined Army during the proclamation of Emergency and later joined service under the State in 1983-84 benefited from 1965 Rules, which granted seniority, pay and pension towards State Service for Army service.
However, these rules were repealed in 1982 and therefore, those who joined service under the State after the repeal of these Rules are not entitled to their benefits.
[Paras 9 to 12 and 19] B.
The legislative power to frame rules under Article 309 of the Constitution of India is valid, and the validity of Rules framed under Article 309 proviso is not questioned.
Therefore, the plea of estoppel cannot be sustained.
[Para 17] C. When posts are advertised and Service Rules are amended retrospectively during the selection process, the selection must be held in accordance with the amended Rules as per the judgment relied upon in JT 1991 SC 282.
