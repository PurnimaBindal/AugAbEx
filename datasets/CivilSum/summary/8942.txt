Education Act, 1959 (Kerala), Section 33 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 9 and Order 39 Rule 1 - Jurisdiction - Nullity - The Education Act specifically prohibits the Civil Court from granting any interim injunction related to proceedings under the Act - If the Civil Court still grants an injunction, the order is null and cannot be enforced in any court - Nullity can be pleaded at any stage of any proceeding or even in collateral proceedings in other courts - Obtaining interim orders by concealing material facts on the last minute, as practiced by some institutions, is condemned.
