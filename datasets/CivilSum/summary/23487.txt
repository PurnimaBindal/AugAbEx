Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F and 25-G - Closure of establishment - Retrenchment - Absorption - A construction company awarded construction projects cannot be demanded to absorb local persons hired for a specific job after closing their establishment particularly when distinct units existed in Hyderabad - Unity of ownership, management and control do not carry much significance - Industrial Tribunal can consider excessive man-power as a factor while determining relief.
