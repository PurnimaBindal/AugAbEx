According to Constitution of India, Article 16, voluntary retirement schemes offered by the employer allows for resignation, but if withdrawn before the cut off date, the employer cannot act upon the offer of resignation.
[Paras 12, 15, 16 and 17] B.
As per Constitution of India, Article 16, if a petitioner submits their resignation, but with the condition that their entire dues be paid, and those dues are not paid, the employer cannot act upon the resignation.
