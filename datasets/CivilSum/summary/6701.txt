Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 138(2) - Seniority - Fortuitous promotion - An individual promoted as exempted Head Constable in Sports Quota without going through the Lower School Course, in opposition to the rules, and who is reverted from their position without carrying out the duties of Head Constable, will be placed on List C-1 instead of List C-2 - Such individual is not entitled to claim the benefit of List C-2 and cannot count the period of fortuitous promotion as HC toward their seniority.
