The case pertains to the derecognition of a particular Education Board and its applicability to the petitioner.
The petitioner had completed her 10+2 from Bihar Intermediate Education Council and taken admission in a 5-year LL.B.
course.
However, her admission was cancelled by the University on account of the derecognition of the said board.
The court held that derecognition can only be given effect prospectively in relation to the eligibility clause.
The last date of eligibility for candidate must be determined on the last date of submission of application.
Since the Board was recognized at the time of petitioner's admission, cancellation of admission is unlawful.
Thus, the petition was allowed accordingly.
