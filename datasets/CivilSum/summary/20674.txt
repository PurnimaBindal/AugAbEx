Constitution of India, Article 14 - Fundamental Rules, FR 9(21) and 14 - House Rent Allowance- Employees of Central Plantation Crops Research Institute were denied the benefit of HRA as they refused to occupy official accommodation offered to them.
The denial of HRA was held justified, and the tribunal was incorrect in including HRA in the definition of wages.
The appellant-organization was directed to deduct HRA from the salary of respondent-employees only for the duration the quarters remained vacant.
