DRDS Rules of 1979 - Transfer - Challenge as to - Mala fide alleged - Appellant serving in Army Medical Corps being moved to non-medical branch to accommodate junior officer - Absorbed in DRDO, allowing for transfer - Respondents claim Appellant is suited for specialized training - Courts have limited interference in cases of armed forces - Authorities decide where and when a member should be posted - Transfer in public interest and not mala fide - Appeal dismissed.
