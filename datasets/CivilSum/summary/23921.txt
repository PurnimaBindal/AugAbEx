Indian Administrative Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1955, Regulations 5(1), 5(2) and 5(3) - Promotion from State Civil Service to IAS - The Selection Committee recommended the promotion of State Civil Service officers to IAS in December 1988.
However, the selection list was deemed invalid and the Union Public Service Commission ordered for a new one to be made.
The Selection Committee reconvened on 1631989, and the 12-month period under Regulation 5(1) began counting from that day until 15.3.1990.
Officers of State Civil Service who had turned 54 years old on 1st January, 1988, are the only ones ineligible for promotion.
