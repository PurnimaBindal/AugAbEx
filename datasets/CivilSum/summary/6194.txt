Punjab State Cooperative Services (Common Cadre) Rules, 1967, Rule 2.6 - Probation period/Reversion/Confirmation - The probation period under the rule is one year, extendable by the Administrative Committee by six months, and after that, employees must be confirmed - Reversion cannot be done without proper enquiry and principles of natural justice.
[Paras 5 and 6] B. Punjab State Cooperative Services (Common Cadre) Rules, 1967 - Reversion - The High Court invalidated a resolution that led to reversion - Any action based on such resolution is also nullified - The petitioner has the right to be reinstated.
