According to Rule 5(1) of the Central Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, and Article 311(2) of the Constitution of India, if a temporary ad hoc employee is not fit to be retained due to unsatisfactory performance or misconduct, the appointing authority can use temporary service Rules to terminate their employment with a simple order, without the provisions of Article 311(2) being applicable.
The appointing authority has the power to make such a decision.
