Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 138 and 139 - Promotion - Head Constables on list 'C' must undergo the Intermediate School Course in order of seniority, with confirmed Head Constables being sent to the course first followed by Head Constables on probation, and officiating Head Constables last.
Successful completion of the course entitles Head Constables to seniority and promotion benefits in accordance with Rules 138 and 139.
B Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 138 and 139 - Seniority - Head Constables' seniority must be determined based on their Range and not the entire state as per Rules 138 and 139.
