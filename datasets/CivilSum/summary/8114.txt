Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Disciplinary Action - Quantum of punishment - After being acquitted by a criminal court of charges of rash and negligent driving resulting in death, the Respondent, a driver, was dismissed from the service following an inquiry.
The Disciplinary Authority must be more cautious while awarding punishment in cases where extenuating factors are apparent and dismissal becomes unjustified.
The Respondent should be reinstated, but not as a bus driver.
[Para 5]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Back wages - The Respondent, a bus driver who was dismissed following the death caused due to his actions, is entitled to back wages, subject to the deduction of compensation already paid by the Corporation.
The court is required to tailor the relief based on the unique facts of the case, relying upon the concept of contributory negligence.
[Paras 6 and 8]
