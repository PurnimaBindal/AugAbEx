The appellant's reason for not promoting the respondent to the post of Lt. General was that no post was available, but the selection board had placed him at serial No. 1 for promotion.
However, according to the file containing the note put up to the Prime Minister for approval, the procedure of chain posting was worked out in order to ensure that respondent would be able to pick up rank of Lt. General before his retirement on 30.4.96.
Posting orders were issued, but were not given effect to.
It was held that the respondent, who was qualified for the promotion, could not be denied the rank of Lt. General.
