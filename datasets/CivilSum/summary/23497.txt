Bihar Pension Rules, Rules 43(b) and 139 - Pension - Withholding of - A retired government servant may face fresh departmental proceedings against them even after retirement for grave misconduct committed during their service career, but only for misconduct within four years of the proceedings initiation - Alleged grave misconduct committed prior to four years of the show-cause notice issuance does not warrant invoking Rule 139(a) and (b) against the respondent - The proceedings under Rule 139 are, therefore, invalid - Rule 43(b) proviso (a)(ii) bars any departmental proceedings after four years, rendering the allegations of grave misconduct incapable of establishment - Hence, there is no need for remanding proceedings under Rule 139(a) and (b).
