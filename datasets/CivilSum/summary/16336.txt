Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service of Employees) Act, 1974, Section 4 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appeal - Alternate Remedy - The petitioner, whose services were terminated as a probationer by the management of an aided school, is challenging the termination on the grounds that the probation period was successfully completed and that the penalty could not have been imposed without the Director's approval.
However, the Act provides for a remedy of appeal, which the petitioner did not use before seeking alternate remedies.
The petitioner has the option of approaching the DPI (Colleges) within a month, and a decision will be made on the merits after the hearing.
