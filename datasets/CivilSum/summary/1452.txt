Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 7-A - Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provisional) Act, 1986, Sections 22(3) and (4) - Provident Fund - Recovery proceedings - The proceedings for recovery of Provident Fund and Wages of workers are not similar to what is contemplated under Section 22(4) of The Sick Industries Act.
The fact that the industry was declared sick under the provisions under Section 22(4) of Sick Industries Act does not affect the mandate of Statutes under which the establishment has to comply with its statutory obligations towards its employees or Tax Revenue.
The Sick industry is protected only towards its properties from its creditors during proceedings for its rehabilitation before the BIFR, not for its other obligations.
