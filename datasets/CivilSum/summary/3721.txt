Regarding the Industrial Disputes Act 1947 and an ex parte award, the petitioner did not appear before the Labor Court despite receiving notice.
The petitioner's argument that the respondent had already been paid under a settlement was taken into account when considering the justification of the Labor Court's decision not to set aside the ex parte award.
The petitioner cannot address the court on the merits of the case that were not previously tested in the original court.
Since the petitioner did not respond to the notice, there is no fault or illegality found in the Labor Court's order.
