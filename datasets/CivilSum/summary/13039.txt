According to Oriental Bank of Commerce Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1982, Regulation 12(4), if an order of dismissal is set aside, the petitioner is entitled to receive subsistence allowance during the period of enquiry.
In a specific case where the petitioner was dismissed from service in 1995 and the order of dismissal was set aside in 2002, the petitioner sought payment of subsistence allowance, and it was held that the petitioner is entitled to receive subsistence allowance for that period as per the 1982 Regulations.
[Para 9]
Oriental Bank of Commerce Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1982, Regulation 12(4) states that subsistence allowance cannot be subjected to a lien as it is crucial for the survival of a person.
In a charge of misappropriation where respondents claimed a lien on subsistence allowance, it was held that they cannot do so as subsistence allowance cannot be subjected to a lien.
