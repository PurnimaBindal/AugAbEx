According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, a doctor was denied admission to a PG course due to colour blindness.
However, a specialist from AIIMS provided a comprehensive report stating that the petitioner could pursue courses in specialties such as psychiatry and community medicine.
It was held that the decision of the committee to deny admission was illogical, lacking objectivity, and pragmatism, and therefore invalid.
[Paras 22, 23 & 25]
