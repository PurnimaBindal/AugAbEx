Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 226 - Reservation - Appointments/Reservation for Backward Classes - Only Backward Classes candidates should fill seats reserved for them - If a candidate belonging to a Backward Class is appointed to a general category post, then a vacant seat reserved for Backward Classes must be filled by a Backward Class candidate - The right of a Backward Class candidate to be appointed on the reserved seat cannot be taken away by an earlier appointment made through general selection.
