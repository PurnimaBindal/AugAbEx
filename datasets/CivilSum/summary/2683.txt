Sections 10, 25F Schedule II Item 3 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Workman - Termination of service - The Labour Court made a ruling that termination was illegal, but its decision was influenced by extraneous considerations that were not present at the time of termination.
The Labour Court also ruled that the petitioner is entitled to the same relief as any employee who was retrenched after following Section 25-F when the sub-division was abolished.
The court's decision is contrary to the law and has resulted in a miscarriage of justice.
The petitioner is entitled to either reinstatement with full backwages or compensation.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to reinstatement with continuity in service, but without backwages.
