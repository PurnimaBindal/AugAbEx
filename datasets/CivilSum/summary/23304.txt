In accordance with the Constitution of India, Article 16 and the Karnataka Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules of 1957, seniority in promotion must be taken into account.
The appellant was appointed in 1970 after all eligible candidates were interviewed by the State Public Service Commission, and worked uninterruptedly in that position until being promoted to the next higher post alongside respondents Nos. 3 to 7.
It was noted that the appellant's experience as a Junior Engineer (Mechanical) from 1970 until his promotion to the next higher post could not be disregarded, especially since the Recruitment Rules had also gone into effect.
