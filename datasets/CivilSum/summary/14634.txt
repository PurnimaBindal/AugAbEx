Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Delhi School Education Rules, 1973, Rule 110 - Discrimination/Doctrine of legitimate - Expectation - Retirement/Extension at the stage of superannuation - An employee has no legal right to claim extension in service after retirement and the employer is not under any obligation to do so - Before an employee can claim a writ of mandamus, they must show their legal right - If another employee is re-employed on a lower post for a limited period of time due to exigencies of service until an alternative is made available, it does not constitute discrimination towards those not retained - The doctrine of legitimate expectation is not applicable in such situations.
