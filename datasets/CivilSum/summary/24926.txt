According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India and Punjab Educational Service, Class III, School Cadre Rules, 1955, Rule 10, there should be equality in service matters.
Furthermore, under the government circular decided on 23-7-1957, Paragraph 3, Category B, Group II, junior basic trained teachers will receive a higher pay scale upon acquiring a higher educational qualification such as BA, B.T./BA, B.Ed.
There is no requirement for professional experience in JST/JAV training.
It is not permissible to discriminate between graduate teachers with JST/JAV training and those without such training.
