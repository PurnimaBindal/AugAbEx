The Disciplinary proceedings under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule II Item 3 - Hindustan Machine Tools (Conduct, Discipline and Appeal) Rules - Karnataka Co-operative Societies Act, 1959, Sections 69 and 70 cannot be maintained.
The Act of misconduct done by employee in the capacity of Director of co-operative society cannot be controlled by the company.
As the Employees appointed to discharge functions of Society are not employees of the company and the Act of 1959 provides for remedies, the domestic enquiry against petitioner could not have been initiated by the disciplinary authority of the company.
