The orders of transfer under executive powers of Constitution of India Articles 14 and 16 did not comply with principles of natural justice and rules that were brought in at a later stage.
The 16 categories of posts in the cadre were not equal in scale of pay and the rules were not given retrospective effect, leading to civil consequences such as reduced pay for respondents.
[Para 15]
Transfer under executive powers should adhere to the existing rules and comply with principles of natural justice, as an order of transfer can result in civil or penal consequences.
Unauthorized exercise of power would amount to malice in law and transfer must be made to an equivalent post.
The order of transfer should not prejudicially affect the employee's status, or else it would be illegal.
