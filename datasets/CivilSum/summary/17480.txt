Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Proficiency step ups - Time bound increments - Length of service - Absorption in other Govt. Department - The petitioners were absorbed into the Punjab Govt. Education Department upon abolition of the Adult Education Scheme.
However, their seniority cannot be based on their service in the said scheme.
The benefit of service rendered in the Adult Education scheme must not be denied in computing length of service for granting proficiency step ups.
