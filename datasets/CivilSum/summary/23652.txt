The decision to reorganize the existing cadre into two cadres was a policy decision based on administrative exigencies, which is not subject to judicial review unless it is mala fide, arbitrary or bereft of any discernible principle.
The procedure followed is fair, reasonable and beneficial to all employees without impacting their scale of pay or their seniority from the date of initial appointment.
However, due to the amalgamation, the respondent/writ petitioner lost some seniority, which was a consequence of the amalgamation.
