Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 162 - Departmental enquiry - Dismissal - The petitioner was dismissed from service based on a departmental inquiry regarding his absence from duty for 33 days, but the inquiry officer did not consider all the relevant facts and the finding of guilt was perverse.
The order of punishment and the order of the appellate authority were initiated without proper application of Rule 162.
It is imperative for a competent authority to record a finding that an employee is guilty of gravest acts of misconduct or continued misconduct which proves his unfitness for Police Service before exercising power under Rule 162.
Failure to examine the case of the petitioner in a correct perspective of Rule 162 caused grave prejudice to him.
The petitioner/appellant was reinstated in service with all consequential benefits.
[Paras 16, 19, 20 and 21]
