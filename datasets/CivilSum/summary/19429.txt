Article 14 and 311 of the Constitution of India; Section 49 of the State Bank of India Act, 1955; and Rule 92 of the State Bank of India (Supervising Staff) Service Rules (1975) state that abandonment/absence from duty may result in forfeiture of appointment; however, absence cannot be considered abandonment unless the employee intends to not return to work in the future.
Deemed abandonment cannot be assumed without following principles of natural justice.
As such, termination has been quashed.
[Paras 10 and 11] B.
Termination of service due to deemed abandonment of service has been considered illegal and in violation of natural justice.
Conversely, an employee who has not acquired leave in advance but was absent without permission is not entitled to wages during that period.
