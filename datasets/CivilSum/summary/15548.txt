The ASI in HAP appeals against adverse remarks in his Annual Confidential Report, arguing that the remarks were recorded without proper consideration and were not relevant to HAP specifically.
The appellant claims the remarks should be expunged.
However, there is no evidence of malice or ill will in the reporting officer when recording the remarks.
The appeal is granted to some extent, as the reporting officer is ordered to re-record the ACR, taking into account the absence of remarks in specific columns and whether or not an overall assessment upgrade is justified.
