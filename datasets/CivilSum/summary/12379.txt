Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - "Teaching Experience" - The question is whether coaching in the sport of wrestling that the petitioner has provided to his students can qualify as 'teaching experience' for registering for the PhD Degree - Although there is no clear definition of 'teacher', the fact that the petitioner imparts instruction in physical activities does not detract from his identity as a teacher and his vocation as teaching experience - The University is ordered to allow the petitioner to pursue the PhD program in question.
