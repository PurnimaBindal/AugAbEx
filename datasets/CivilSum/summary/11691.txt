Article 311 of the Constitution of India deals with Dismissal due to Absence from Duty.
In this particular case, the petitioner left his place of posting on the pretext of taking leave for a few days, but he kept extending it on various pretexts.
Finally, he joined duty after a delay of nine months and 25 days.
Despite several opportunities given to him, the petitioner failed to take part in departmental proceedings.
As a result, the order of dismissal is infirmity-free.
[Paras 6, 9 and 11] B Article 226 of the Constitution of India concerns delay/laches in Dismissal cases.
In this instance, a Writ Petition was filed nine years after the conclusion of the appeal proceedings due to lack of funds, making it inadmissible.
