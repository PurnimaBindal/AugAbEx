According to Sections 5, 14(1A), 14(2A) and 17(4) of the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, an exempted establishment must contribute to Provident Fund as per its own rules to avoid punishment under Sections 14(1A) and 14(2A).
Even if the exemption is cancelled under Section 17(4), it would not be considered as a penalty.
[Paras 10, 11, 13 and 20] B.
The Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 14(1A) and 14(2A) hold that failure of the employer to contribute to Provident Fund as per the exemption rules and conditions granted is considered a contravention and punishable by law.
These provisions are applicable to exempted establishments as well.
