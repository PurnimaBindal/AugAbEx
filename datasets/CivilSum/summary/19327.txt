Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admissions/Education - Reservation in admissions - Domicile - A petitioner can claim admission against seats reserved for candidates belonging to Punjab settled in other states/abroad as well as those reserved for domiciles of Punjab, even if they provide different certificates that are contradictory.
However, rejection of the claim for admission by the college was upheld.

Constitution of India, Articles 14,16 and 226 - Admission - Certificate - Domicile certificate - Certificates required for admission were not attached with the application form nor produced at the time of interview.
As per Admission Brochure rules, production of these certificates after finalization of selection is not allowed.
As a result, no direction can be issued to admit the petitioner due to seats being already filled up and none being available.
