According to the Constitution of India 1950, specifically Articles 14, 16, and 226, regularisation of service can only take place if certain criteria are met.
These include requiring two high average reports out of the last three years for confirmation/regularisation eligibility.
However, fixing such criteria without communicating average reports or giving the petitioner an opportunity to represent themselves is arbitrary and contrary to the rules of natural justice.
In this case, similarly situated individuals were taken into service and had their services regularised, while the petitioner was disqualified due to their average ACR.
Thus, the petitioner must be regularised with continuity of service.
