Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948 - Section 15 and 78 - A - Bihar State Electricity Board Junior Electrical Engineer (General) Cadre Rules, 1982, Rule 7 - Appointments/Policy of the Government - The post of Junior Engineer is not included in the Government's policy, and thus, the appointment must follow the Rules.
Any Government directions in this matter will be considered as an encroachment on the Board's powers.
The Board is instructed to re-advertise and fill the posts in the ratio of 75:25 in accordance with the Rules.
