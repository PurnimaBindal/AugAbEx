Public Service Commission Rules of Procedure (Kerala), 1976, Rule 14 - Duty vacancies - Non-joining of - Public Service Commission - Obligation to advise - Rule 14 states that the Commission is required to advise candidates for all vacancies while rank lists are valid.
However, unless a vacancy is reported to the Commission, they are not obligated to advise candidates because the vacancy is not considered to be pending before the Commission.
The appeal was allowed accordingly.
