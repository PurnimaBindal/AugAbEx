Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Promotion - Seniority-cum- merit - Ranking in ACRs - Estoppel - Promotions based on seniority-cum-merit will prioritize seniority unless the senior is deemed unfit for the promotion - In the case of no set rule, promotions must follow the seniority-cum-merit criteria, not merit-cum-seniority - An authority cannot suddenly promote a junior over a senior based solely on a better ACR ranking if they had previously promoted seniors first - Employees must be promptly informed of any negative Confidential Report to allow for improvement.
[Paras 15, 16, 17]
