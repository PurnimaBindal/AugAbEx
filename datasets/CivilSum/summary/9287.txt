Article 20 of the Indian Constitution, along with Sections 19 and 121 of the Army Act, 1950, and Army Rule deal with double jeopardy.
If an officer is not punished due to some technicality, then the power under Section 19 can be invoked for dismissal from service.
However, if the Court Martial's proceedings have already been upheld by the Central Government, then action under Section 19 cannot be taken.
