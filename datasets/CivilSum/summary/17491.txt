Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 14 - Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Electrical) Recruitment Regulations, 1965, Regulation 9(1) - Assistant Engineer Cadre is a singular cadre - Junior Engineers in the Generation Cadre can transfer to the General Cadre, and vice versa.
Therefore, discrimination between those working in the General Cadre and those in the Generation Cadre cannot occur when promoting to Assistant Engineer.
The writ petitioners were higher in merit than all private respondents, and they were assigned to the Generation Cadre not by choice, but by the orders of the respondent-Board.
Impugned orders are quashed, and Respondent No. 1 must consider the promotion of petitioners to the post of Assistant Engineers.
