Constitution of India, Article 226 - Replication - Rejoinder - New plea - A new plea taken in the rejoinder which was not previously mentioned in the petition cannot be considered, especially if it was based on the record to which the petitioner had no access.
[Para 17] C. Words and Phrases - The term "ordinarily" used in para 524 of UP Police Regulations does not mean "invariably" or "always" - Instead, it allows competent authority to exercise discretion depending on circumstances and exigencies of the situation.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Transfer - Reasons - Discretion of competent authority - The competent authority is not required to record sufficient and good reasons in the transfer order while exercising its discretion in the consideration of the exigencies of administration.
