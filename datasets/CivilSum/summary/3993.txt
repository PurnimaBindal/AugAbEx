Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Petitioner and other employees selected for promotion - Except for the petitioner, the candidates listed along with persons junior to petitioner were promoted - Petitioner was denied promotion on the basis of a decision to initiate a departmental enquiry - The plea of decision to initiate a departmental inquiry is not tenable in the absence of a charge sheet - Even after promotion, the petitioner can proceed against in a departmental inquiry - Based on these circumstances, the denial of promotion is unjustified - The respondents are directed to consider the petitioner's promotion case in accordance with the law.
