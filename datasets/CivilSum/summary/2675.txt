Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 14 - Rajasthan Civil Service (Pension) Rules, 1996, Rule 53 - Order of compulsory retirement - Interference scope - Respondent was punished with withholding of one grade increment on 3121988 and censure through two different orders dated 31.10.1991 - Respondent was promoted within two years of the last penalty orders and promotion was confirmed on 1421995 - No adverse remark or penalty was imposed after the promotion - The High Court can interfere since the order is arbitrary, as no reasonable person of ordinary prudence, upon a given material, would have concluded that the concerned government servant has become a dead wood.
Service record, specifically the latest one, should form the foundation for the opinion and provide a basis to exercise power under rules to compulsory retire, citing the Apex Court judgment in the Baikuntha Nath Das case.
