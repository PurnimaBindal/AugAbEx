Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Qualifications - Trained Teacher - Temporary appointment as a teacher in a school for deaf and dumb students - The petitioner, who is not trained to teach deaf and dumb children, is appointed until a regularly selected trained teacher is available - As an untrained teacher, the petitioner cannot claim the post - Appointing untrained teachers to a post intended for trained teachers lowers the standard of education.
