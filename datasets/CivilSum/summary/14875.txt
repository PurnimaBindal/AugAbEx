Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1)(c) - Reference - The government denied making a reference due to delay, but the question of placing the workman in the post of Chargeman Miscellaneous instead of Chargeman Mechanical was not considered in their decision.
The Union of India was directed to reconsider the case and make a reference in accordance with the provision of Section 10(1)(c) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, following ILR 1999(1) Punjab & Haryana and RSJ 1996(4) 250.
