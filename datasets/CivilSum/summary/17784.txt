Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Seniority - Promotion - Reversion - Petitioners were promoted provisionally as officiating ASIs with a condition that they qualify in the subjects noted against each within three months from the promotion.
The promotion order mentioned this condition, indicating that it does not provide an indefeasible right to the petitioners.
If the petitioners did not meet the promotion condition strictly, they could be reverted.
The court dismissed the petition.
[Paras 4 and 8]
