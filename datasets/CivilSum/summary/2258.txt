Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Appointment - Post of primary teacher - Advertisement Condition - Challenge - Condition requires candidates to have completed 10th and 12th class examination from Delhi - Condition defended as policy decision - However, schools cannot be differentiated on basis of location alone - Institutional preference plea has no connection with recruitment objective, instead, it eliminates possibly qualified candidates - Condition violates Articles 14 and 16(2) of Constitution - So cannot be upheld.
