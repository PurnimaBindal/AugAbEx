According to Rule 43(b) of the Pension Rules 1950, recovery from gratuity and leave salary is only permissible if there is evidence of pecuniary loss to the government and departmental proceedings have found the pensioner guilty.
The Respondent in this case passed an order of recovery from the gratuity and leave salary of the Petitioner who had already superannuated.
However, there is no evidence of any such loss or finding against the Petitioner.
The proviso to the rule states that departmental proceedings cannot be initiated against a retired person without the sanction of the state government.
Since no such sanction had been issued, the officer had no authority to pass such an order.
The Petition was therefore allowed.
