Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 1(5) - Words And Phrases - According to AP Government Notification No. G.O.Ms.
297, Decided on 25-3-75, a "shop" refers to an establishment that has employed more than 20 individuals and engages in activities related to the sale of goods.
The Act shall be applicable, and delivery of products on the premises is not required.
Even a limited company that deals with importing fertilizers and employs more than 20 people in its central office is included in the definition of "shop."
