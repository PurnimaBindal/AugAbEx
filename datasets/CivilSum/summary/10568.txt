According to Section 11-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, if a workman is terminated illegally, the typical protocol is reinstatement with full back-wages.
However, if it is determined that reinstatement would not be beneficial for the institution, this rule may be disregarded.
In the case of an Overseer named Petitioner, he was granted compensation of Rs. 25000 instead of Rs. 2000 as he was not reinstated.
This decision was based on the precedents of 1951(2) LLJ 483 and 1970(1) LLJ(SC) 228.
