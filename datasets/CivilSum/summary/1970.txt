Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - Mala fides - A commandant in CRPF challenged their transfer on the grounds of mala fides by alleging it against respondent No. 3.
However, the court held that vague allegations of mala fides without any material to support them do not warrant interference.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - The petitioner was transferred before completing the minimum tenure period of three years as per the policy.
Although there is no statutory rule fixing tenure, the violation of the guidelines relied upon by the petitioner, which were only executive instructions, cannot be enforced.
