Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 22 and 11-B - Strike period wages - Jurisdiction of Labour Court to award - The Labour Court can't award wages in sympathy cases - Workmen can't receive wages for the strike period without proving it was justified, regardless of legality - Strike without waiting for the decision of the management isn't justified - No work, no pay for strike period.
