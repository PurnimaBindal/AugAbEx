Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation - Pay-scale - Petitioners working as canteen staff in acknowledged canteen of respondent-bank - Advocating for regularisation of service and pay scale equivalent to sub-staff of bank - Other canteen staff members of merged bank accorded privileges - No valid reason for favoring employees of one bank over another - Petitioners have right to be recognised as sub-staff in respondent bank.
[Paras 5 and 6]
