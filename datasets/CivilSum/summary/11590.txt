Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Sealed Cover - Petitioners appeared in departmental examination on 25-7-99 but their results were kept in sealed cover - The results of the Petitioners were announced on 23-3-2001 while the results of other candidates were announced on 1-11-99 - Respondents calculated the seniority of Petitioners from the date of the sealed cover opening - It was concluded that since Respondents did not open the sealed cover despite Petitioners having passed the examination, all such officers' seniority must be determined from 1-11-99.
