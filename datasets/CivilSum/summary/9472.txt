Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Selection - Change in Qualification - Post of professor - The employer has the right to prescribe necessary qualifications for the post, but any changes made to those qualifications afterwards should not disadvantage other similarly situated individuals without proper publicity.
[Para 13]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Selection - Change in qualification - Post of Professor - A circular changing the qualifications for the post of Professor, which was only circulated internally within the university, cannot be upheld.
The university must give proper notification and proceed with recruitment.
[Paras 13 and 14]
