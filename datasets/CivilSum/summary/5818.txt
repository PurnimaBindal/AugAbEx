Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 (1) and 11-A - Reference - Dismissal or removal from service - Sectory authority - Belief on a fair enquiry - Workers may ask for further investigation to the Labour Tribunal regarding the awarded punishment and the validity and fairness of the enquiry that lead to it.
If the Labour Tribunal recognizes any unscrupulous behavior of the employer, they can also intervene with the punishment exercised and obliterate the proceedings.
Consequently, the government has a scant possibility to refuse the consultation of disputes to the Labour Tribunal regarding dismissal and removal from service.

Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 (1) and 22 - Reference - Discretion of the appropriate government - After the 1971 amendment, the government's mandate has been restricted - With respect to public utility services, if notice is issued under Section 22, the State Government has no choice except to forward the dispute to the Labour Tribunal unless it is deemed flagitious or vexatious.
