Constitution of India, Article 226 - Government Residence (Chandigarh Administration Pool) Allotment Rules, 1972 - Allotment of Government Accommodation - When a Government servant is allotted government accommodation, he is not considered a lessee or a tenant but only a licensee.
He does not have the right to nominate his successor or pass any right, title or interest to his children or dependents.
Any rule or policy made by the Government that violates the principle of equality is unlawful.
[Para 5]
