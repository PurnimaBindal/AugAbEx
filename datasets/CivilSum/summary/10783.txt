Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 165 and 128 - Discharge during probation - An order of discharge on grounds of misconduct, absence from duty, or making false reports cannot be considered an order under Rule 128, but instead must be deemed a dismissal under Rule 1628, for which an inquiry must be held.
[Para 8]

Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1628 - Appeal - Enhancement of punishment by appellate authority - Discharge during probation - The appellate authority must provide proper and adequate opportunity to the concerned person before enhancing the punishment, as to do otherwise would violate their right under Article 14 of the Constitution.
[Para 7]
