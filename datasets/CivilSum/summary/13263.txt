Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Discrimination - Writ petition filed by two library attendants claiming entitlement to scale of Rs. 950-1400 - Petitioner also performs duties of officer-in-charge of library - Petitioner's claim for same scale rejected due to limitation to writ petitioners only - State expected to treat similarly placed individuals equally - Court held that petitioner is entitled to scale of Rs. 950-1400.
