The petitioner, who was appointed as Junior Field Investigator in the Statistical Department on a regular basis, can only claim benefits of military service towards increments on their first appointment and not subsequent appointments under Rule 4(i) of the Punjab National Emergency (Concessions) Rules, 1965.
Rule 4-A of the Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Haryana Amendment Rules, 1979, which limits service benefits to only the first appointment under the government, is violating Articles 14 and 16.
The court has directed the respondents to grant military service benefits to the petitioner as per the provisions of Rules, 1965.
