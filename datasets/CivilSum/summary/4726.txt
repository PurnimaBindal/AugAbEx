Haryana Agricultural University, Hissar Statutes, Chapter 3(III) - Selection and appointment to the post of Director Research - The selection committee must recommend a panel of two names and the Board must approve in consultation with the Deans.
A panel of two or more names can only be sent if there are multiple eligible and suitable candidates.
If the Selection Committee creates a panel of two names but deems the person at No. 2 as ineligible or unsuitable, the Appointing Authority must appoint the person at No. 1 in order of merit.
If only one candidate is eligible and suitable and recommended by the Selection Committee, the decision cannot be faulted.
