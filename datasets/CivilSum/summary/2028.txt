According to the policy of LIC, candidates with CBSE score of B2+ and above in Life Insurance Stream at +2 level can be absorbed in the cadre of Assistants/Typists/Stenographers.
However, absorption is not automatic and candidates cannot be absorbed in any other division other than the one in which they underwent the CBSE course.
Only 25% of the posts are reserved for such candidates with due SC/ST reservation.
Vacancies are counted at the end of each fiscal year and the reserved 25% vacancies are held until all candidates from that specific division are absorbed.
[Paras 12 and 13] B.
Even if authorities made wrong orders of absorption and recruitment, such orders are contrary to the policy decision and do not give other candidates the right to seek direction from the court for their absorption outside of the policy.
The court cannot issue directions contrary to the government policy unless the policy changes.
The principle of legitimate expectation is also not violated until the right to claim in accordance with the policy exists.
