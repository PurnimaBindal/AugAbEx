The petitioner was seeking an appointment as a Teaching Guide under the Rehbar-e-Taleem Scheme.
While the school was within a distance of 0 Km from the petitioner's house, it was located 2 kms away from respondent No.5's house, who lived in another village.
Despite respondent No.5 scoring higher in merit, the appointment was to be made from a panel prepared by the village-level committee where the school was located.
In case no candidate was available in the village where the vacancy existed, the zone of consideration was shifted to nearby villages.
However, this was not the case here.
Hence, the rejection of the petitioner's candidature due to being lower in merit did not follow the mandate of the Scheme because merit was to be determined amongst candidates of the same village.
Thus, the court ordered the issuance of an appointment order to the petitioner.
