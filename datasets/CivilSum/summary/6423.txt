Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 2(i)(g) - Partial disablement - Reduction in earning capacity - If unrebutted evidence on record shows that an employee's disablement led to decreased earning capacity in any employment they were capable of undertaking before the accident, they are entitled to compensation for their disablement in proportion to it.
This entitlement to compensation remains regardless of whether the employee retains the same position and salary.
[Paras 10 and 11] B. Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Loss of earning capacity - Commissioner Workmen's Compensation has the discretion to assess the loss of earning capacity with reference to medical evidence.
The court will not interfere with the commissioner's assessment unless it is grossly excessive.
