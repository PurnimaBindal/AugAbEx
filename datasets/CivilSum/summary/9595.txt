The petitioner was appointed as Assistant teacher of Bio Science against a deputation vacancy by the Headmaster, however, it was found that the Headmaster of a School is not an appointing authority of a teacher.
Therefore, the petitioner's appointment violated the provisions of Recruitment Rules and Article 16 of the Constitution.
As a result, the appointment was declared wholly illegal, void ab initio and non est in the eye of law, leaving the petitioner not entitled to absorption on permanent basis.
Additionally, it was clarified that regularisation does not mean absorption in the State Services and so, such absorption by way of regularisation is violative of Article 309 of the Constitution.
