Constitution of India, Article 136 - Administrative Tribunals Act, Sections 19 and 21 - Orissa General Financial Rules, Rule 65 - Changing date of birth in service record - Financial Rules state that if application for changing date of birth is made after five years, it will be rejected summarily - Petitioner joined service in 1962 and knew of incorrect birth date in 1970 - Application for correction filed in 1989 and rejected as time barred - Tribunal allowed the change, but Supreme Court determined that Tribunal was wrong to ignore Rules - Tribunal's decision set aside.
