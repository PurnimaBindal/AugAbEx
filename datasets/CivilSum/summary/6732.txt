When the order of termination is a simplicitor order and does not cast any stigma upon the employee, the services of a temporary employee can be terminated on the grounds of unsuitability as per the Constitution of India, Articles 16 & 31.
The employee's services were not proven to be regularised, and no allegation of any kind was levelled against him, so the termination order was not illegal.
[Paras 8, 10 & 11]
