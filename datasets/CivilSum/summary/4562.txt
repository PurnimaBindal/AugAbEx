Constitution of India, Article 14 and 309 - Appointment - Selection - Eligibility - The petitioners were found to not meet eligibility conditions on the dates of application, selection, and appointment.
An amendment in the Rules after their appointment could not reverse the illegality of appointing an unqualified person.
[Para 7]
Practice and procedure - Finality of order - The High Court ordered the appointing authority to investigate allegations of ineligibility, and those deemed ineligible had their appointments invalidated.
The decision of the High Court was not challenged and was enforced, hence the appointee cannot later argue that they were ineligible after their appointment.
[Paras 5, 6, 8 and 10]
