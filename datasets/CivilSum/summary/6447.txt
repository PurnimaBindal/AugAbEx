Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Promotion - Selection Board recommends merit-based seniority for promotions - Promotions follow seniority order of Selection Board recommendations - Promotion entitlement goes to person highest in seniority rankings of Selection Board - Petitioner's juniors promoted above them unjustly; petitioner retired while case was ongoing - Court rules petitioner entitled to notional promotion with all benefits retroactive to the date their junior was promoted.
