Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Education - Private educational institutions that are not controlled by the State or educational authority do not qualify as an `authority' under Article 226.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Expulsion of petitioner from school - The writ petition is not maintainable as recommendations made to the Board were not shared with the students and the petitioner had no opportunity to meet them; therefore, order of quashing expulsion cannot be given.
However, the American Embassy School is advised to review its action and allow the petitioner to complete his studies subject to conditions that the school may impose.
