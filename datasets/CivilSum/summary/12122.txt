Constitution of India, Article 16 - Dying in Harness Rules (1974), Rule 5 - Compassionate appointment - The Dying in Harness Rules do not define the specific cause of death required for heirs to be eligible for compassionate appointment - Even if a person has gone missing and is presumed dead, their heirs are entitled to be considered for employment in a compassionate appointment.
