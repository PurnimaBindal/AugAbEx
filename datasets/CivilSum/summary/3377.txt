The Petitioner-School appointed fourth respondent as School Assistant based on previous experience instead of the third respondent, leading to an appeal against denial of promotion.
However, denial of promotion cannot be considered a violation of service condition, and hence cannot be appealed against unless there is a variation in pay, allowances or other service conditions that disadvantages the employee.
[Para 8]
Non-consideration of third respondent for promotion in a minority institution where there is no rule enabling appointment only by promotion is not a violation of service condition.
The management can appoint a person of their own choice where two people of similar experience and qualifications are available.
Fourth respondent is duly qualified and experienced.
[Para 12]
Under Rule 6 of Govt. Aided Private School Teachers (Discipline, Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1959, the Appellate Tribunal must consist of the Director of Education (D.O.E.)
and two non-official members.
Therefore, appeals heard only by the D.O.E.
is without jurisdiction as the D.O.E.
alone cannot act as the Tribunal.
