Pension Rules for University Teachers, 1992, Rule 9(4) and 44 - Retiral Benefits - Withholding of - The University cannot decide on withholding retiral benefits without resorting to judicial process of law, even if there is an alleged violation of the bond executed by the petitioner while going on study leave - No department or judicial proceedings pending against the petitioner - Respondent-University directed to release retiral benefits with 12% compound interest - Costs quantified at Rs. 5000/-.
