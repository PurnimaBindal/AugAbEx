Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Reservation - Ex-servicemen quota - Backlog of vacancies - Advertisement for six posts of Forest Rangers - Four posts reserved for SC category and two for Ex-servicemen category - Petitioner recommended against Ex-servicemen quota, but not appointed due to backlog of vacancies - Petitioner eligible on the basis of recommendations of Subordinate Services Selection Board - Backlog of vacancies in Ex-servicemen quota not disputed - Respondents directed to consider petitioner for appointment to the post of Forest Ranger since she had been recommended by the SSS Board.
