Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Regularisation - Adhoc PCMS Doctor - According to the Sandhawalia Committee report, the Petitioner was eligible for regularisation, as he had also served for three years in a rural area.
The Court directed for his services to be regularised after considering his merits.
Despite directions from the Court, the Petitioner was being denied admission to post-graduate courses due to the delay in regularising his services, which is unfair.
Therefore, he will be considered eligible for admission to post-graduate courses in various disciplines.
The Court issued directions to consider his case of regularisation of services under relevant rules and on merit, with an order to be passed within one month.
