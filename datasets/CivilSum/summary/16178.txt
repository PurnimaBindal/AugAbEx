Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Termination of service/Absence from duty - Plaintiff lying in the Hospital for treatment sent applications for leave but received neither sanctions nor rejections - Services were terminated as absent from duty - The plaintiff was an ad hoc employee and juniors of the plaintiff were regularised in service on the basis of instructions of the Government - The case of the plaintiff was also covered with the instructions and she was rather entitled to be regularised into service - Order of termination of service was wholly illegal, arbitrary, unreasonable, capricious and hit by Article 14 of the Constitution of India, according to the AIR 1966 SC 492.
