Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-F - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Retrenched employees receive ex gratia payments equal to their position - Tribunal deemed retrenchment invalid due to failure to comply with Section 25-F - However, compensation was already paid under "ex gratia" description - Thus, termination is still valid even if treated as retrenchment and all mandatory conditions are met.
