Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Reference/Second Reference/Natural Justice/Opportunity of hearing - The State Government was directed to re-consider the matter of declining to make a reference to the Labor Court due to the employee not being covered under the definition of workman and the Punjab Government having no jurisdiction to make the reference.
The State Government made a reference to the Labor Court without giving notice to the employer, resulting in the order of reference being quashed.
It was decided that even administrative action that interferes with a person's civil rights must adhere to the rule of natural justice and both parties should be given the chance to be heard.
The government was instructed to re-consider the matter while providing that opportunity.
