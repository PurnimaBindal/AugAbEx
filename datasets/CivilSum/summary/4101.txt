The Employees' State Insurance Act and the Andhra Pradesh Shops and Establishments Act define an employee and shop, respectively, and determine which provisions of the Acts are applicable.
The word "shop" in a government notification should be given its ordinary meaning, which includes the head office of a firm if it is under the control of the principal employer.
This interpretation ensures that all employees, including those in administrative and branch offices, are covered under the Act.
