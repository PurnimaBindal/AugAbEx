Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab CSR Vol. II, Rule 5.32-A(C) - Punjab C.S.R.
Vol.
I, Part I, Rule 3.26(d) - Petitioner retired after receiving three months' notice at the age of 55 - Orders for compulsory retirement are not stigmatic or punitive - Entire period of service should be considered - Adverse remarks lose weight over time - Adverse remarks before earlier promotion lose strength but not when related to dishonesty or lack of integrity - Order of compulsory retirement can be passed without communicating adverse remarks or giving an opportunity to make a representation - Public interest and service record considered in passing the order of compulsory retirement - Petition dismissed.
