Constitution of India, Articles 136 and 311 - Resignation - Withdrawal of resignation - A case wherein an Assistant Sub Inspector in Police was accused of delay in despatch of leave application of the SP and of not keeping copy thereof for record - The duty of the Sub Inspector was not to do so - The threats advanced by SP created insecurity in the mind of the Sub Inspector leading to his resignation - Resignation was withdrawn before communication of its acceptance - Acceptance was made on the same date - The petitioner should be retained in service - Quashing of the acceptance of resignation is justified.
[Paras 1 and 2]
