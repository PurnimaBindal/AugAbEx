Constitution of India, Article 136 - Punjab Municipal Employees Pension and General Provident Fund Rules - Pension - The respondent started work as a Driver with a fixed salary of Rs 80/- per month on 17.7.54 and was given regular pay for the first time w.e.f.
1268 in line with the Third Pay Committee report.
The respondent began contributing to the Provident Fund on 1268 and retired on 30694.
The Pension Rules were introduced after his retirement, on 28794 and allowed the option to choose between Provident Fund or pension.
The respondent chose pension, but the appellant believes he is entitled to it only since 1268 when he began contributing to the Provident Fund.
The High Court allowed the writ petition and set aside the judgment under appeal.
However, the matter is sent back to High Court for reconsideration as the relevant Rules need to be taken into consideration to determine if and when the respondent was entitled to pension.
