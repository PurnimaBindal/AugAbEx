In an Education-related case, a petitioner who was disqualified from a Regional Engineering College examination for allegedly using unfair means was ordered to be allowed to appear in the next semester after a Single Judge found the disqualification order to have been based on no evidence.
The petitioner was accused of having incriminating material written on the question paper supplied to him in the examination hall, but there was no evidence or allegation that he received any outside help.
There was no reason to interfere with the Single Judge's decision and the order of disqualification was quashed.
