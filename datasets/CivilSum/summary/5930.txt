Constitution of India, Article 16 - Seniority - Provisional seniority list - Competent authority entitled to adjust the list as it does not confer an absolute right - Authorities invited representations from the affected individuals and reviewed the list considering the objections raised.
[Para 8]

Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion - Promotions based on provisional seniority list - Changes in promotions can be made with a retrospective effect after the finalisation of seniority list - The basis of promotion being provisional, any changes will require a review after the finalisation of the list - The appointing authority is empowered to rectify mistakes, undo wrongs, and make retroactive appointments.
Citing 1979 SLR 59.

Constitution of India, Article 16 - Seniority - Provisional seniority list - Inter-se seniority dispute - Delay in finalising the list - Delay in deciding an inter-se seniority dispute does not strip the competent authority of its jurisdiction to complete the list and rectify mistakes.
