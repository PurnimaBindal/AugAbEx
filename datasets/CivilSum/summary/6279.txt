Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work - If an employee is made to work on a higher post of group 'C' while being appointed on a lower post of group 'D,' he is entitled to pay of the higher post on the principle of equal pay for equal work.
The non-issuance of any specific order of appointment to such higher post and the non-submission of a joining report will not defeat the right to claim wages of such higher post.
This is because the worker might not be in a position of equal bargain with the strong employer.
