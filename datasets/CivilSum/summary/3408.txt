Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Jurisdiction - The Petitioner, posted in West Bengal and belonging to Rajasthan, filed a Writ Petition challenging the appointment of an enquiry officer made by DIG BSF, Gurdaspur, in the Jammu & Kashmir High Court.
However, as no part of the cause of action arose within its territorial jurisdiction, the Petition was deemed not maintainable.
