Punjab Courts Act, Section 41 - Limitation Act, Article 113 - Cause of action - Limitation - The respondent's claim for promotion from the date his juniors were promoted to Forester and Deputy Ranger is barred by limitation, as the suit for the declaration was filed 14 years after his juniors were promoted as forester and 4 years after they were further promoted as Deputy Ranger - Relief of declaration must be sought within 3 years from the date of the accrual of the right - The respondent is not entitled to any relief.
