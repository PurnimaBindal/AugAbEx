Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 2 (e) (amendment of 1984) - Gratuity - Managerial capacity - Employee - The husband of the petitioner worked as a Manager in Ware Housing Corporation with a salary of over Rs. 1000/- per mensum in a managerial capacity, not in a supervisory capacity.
Prior to the amendment of the Act, such employees were not considered as employees and were ineligible for the gratuity benefit.
As a result, the benefit under the amended Act cannot be provided as the petitioner's husband passed away prior to the amendment coming into effect.
