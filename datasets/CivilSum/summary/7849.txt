Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Sections 2(p) and 18(1) - Settlement - Binding Effect - If a settlement is reached outside of conciliation proceedings between a union and employer, it will be binding on all members of the union unless mala fides or fraud is alleged.
[Paras 7, 11 and 12]
Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Sections 25G and 25J - Dispute regarding reinstatement/re-employment - Disputes regarding reinstatement or re-employment that are not related to retrenchment or lay-off cannot be covered under Section 25G and 25J of the Act.
[Paras 13 and 15]
