Constitution of India, Article 311(2) Proviso (ii) - Enquiry Report - Failure to provide copy to delinquent vitiates disciplinary proceedings - Violation of natural justice.
Principle applies to all cases, including those pending in courts at the time of judgment in Mahmmed Ramzan Khan's case.
[Paras 4, 6 and 7]
Punjab Civil Service (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 13(2) - Enquiry Report - Non-supply of documents relied upon against delinquent vitiates disciplinary proceedings.
Authority must provide all documents to delinquent and copy of enquiry report, violation of natural justice.
