Constitution of India, Article 226 - Andhra Pradesh Private Institutions Employees (Discipline and Control) Rules, 1983, Rule 7(2) - Departmental proceedings - Enquiry - Petitioner alleges bias - Basis of allegation - Enquiry Officer is a part of Management of the college - Simply being part of the college management does not automatically imply bias - Therefore, the plea of bias cannot be accepted.
