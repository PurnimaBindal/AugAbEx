The appointment of respondent No. 7 for the post of Assistant Teacher is in violation of Rule 14 of the West Bengal Service Rules, as the procedure was not followed.
They were not issued a Disability Certificate by a competent authority and were not tested.
The reference itself is illegal and fraudulent, as respondent No. 7 was not verified by the Distt.
Inspector of School under the Special Category.
The appointment is cancelled, and the petitioner, who has sufficient medical proof of disability, is entitled to be recruited in place of respondent No. 7 as the second in the panel.
