Central Services (Medical Attendance) Rules, Rule 2(i) - Follow up treatment - Denial of medical reimbursement - Ground of not having approval - The Standing Committee approved the respondent's wife's case for treatment abroad, but bills for follow-up treatment were rejected on the grounds of no approval.
The respondent relied on a circular from the Govt. of India which allowed for follow-up treatment without prior approval.
The Tribunal rightly directed reimbursement with interest at 6% per annum based on the circular and the fact that necessary approval was granted at first instance.
