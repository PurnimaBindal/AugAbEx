Compassionate Ground - When considering appointment, authorities must examine the family's financial status before offering a position in Class III and IV, with the intention of relieving the family.
Offering compassionate employment as a routine matter, regardless of financial condition and even for posts above Class III and IV, is illegal.
[Paras 2 and 3]

Compassionate employment - It is necessary to have rules or executive instructions for compassionate employment.
Individual's cannot offer jobs in this capacity.
It was also determined that there is no vested right to consideration for such employment, which can be exercised at any time in the future.
[Paras 5 and 6]
