Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Order 6, Rule 17 of the Civil Procedure Code, 1908 deal with amendment of written statements.
In a situation where an enquiry report is presented during the pendency of a petition filed by a workman after the filing of the written statement, an application cannot be rejected solely on the grounds of delay in moving it.
If the amendment sought is essential for the adjudication of the case, the workman can be compensated with costs for the delay by the petitioner.
The amendment is allowed, subject to the payment of costs of Rs. 3,000/-.
