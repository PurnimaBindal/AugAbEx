AP State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1962, Rule 24(a)(ii) - Termination of Probation - Using the term "unsatisfactory work and conduct" in the order of termination does not necessarily carry a negative implication - An otherwise lawful order cannot be deemed invalid due to statements in supporting affidavits - The petitioner's pay terminated with one month and the termination of probation due to unsatisfactory work was not illegally executed.
