According to the Rajasthan Education Subordinate Services Rules of 1971, the appointment and termination of the Senior Teacher Grade II is governed by the University Grant Commission Act of 1956 and Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
The question arose as to whether a B.Ed.
(Child Development) degree followed by a Bridge course awarded by the Rajasthan Vidhya Peeth Udaipur, a deemed university, constitutes a degree in education or Montessori for the purpose of appointment as senior teacher.
The verdict was that the degree granted by Rajasthan Vidhya Peeth, being a deemed university, must be accepted, and the state cannot decide whether the degree is equal to the degree prescribed under the rules governing the field.
