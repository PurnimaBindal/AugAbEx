Fundamental Rules 22(1)(a)(2), 22(1)(a)(3) and 15 - Transfer on lower post - Protection of pay on lower post transfer where reversion is not involved but the transferred employee unilaterally accepts the terms and conditions of the transfer - In this case, the terms and conditions of the transfer govern and the employee's pay is protected under FR 22(1)(a)(2) for ordinary transfers that don't involve reversion, FR 22(1)(a)(3) for employee-requested transfers that don't involve reversion and require the employee to accept the maximum pay in the time scale of the transferred post if it is lower than their current pay in the old post, and technical resignation by the transferee is required to accept a lower-ranking post and relinquish the benefits associated with the previous post.
In such cases, the pay of the transferee is fixed based on the lower pay scale of the new post to which they were transferred.
