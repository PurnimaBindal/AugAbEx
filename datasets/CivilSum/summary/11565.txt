The Central Bank of India (Employees) Pension Regulations from 1995 outlines its pension scheme for employees and requires the option to be exercised within 120 days of notification.
The petitioner was given the option on December 13, 1995, but the case for pension processing does not seem to have occurred.
The technical objection of limitation is wholly unjustified, and the grant of pension is the fulfilment of a constitutional promise - the writ was therefore allowed with costs of Rs. 1000/-.
[Paras 11 to 13] B.
The Constitution of India, in articles 300A and 226/227, states that technical objections of limitation cannot disentitle an employee from receiving their pension.
The grant of pension to employees is the fulfilment of a constitutional promise, and any technical objections are wholly unjustified.
