Army Pension Regulations, Regulation 173 - Disability pension - The petitioner was found fit on his initial posting and was still fit upon re-employment in the Defence Security Corps, but later lost 3/4 of his eye vision and was discharged.
Despite the disease not being attributable or aggravated during his service, he is entitled to disability pension based on the strenuous training and duties performed by jawans.
The Pension Authority has been instructed to release the disability pension to the petitioner within 3 months, with arrears to be paid with 12% interest.
[Paras 2, 5 and 6]
