The Constitution of India has a provision for Inter-se Seniority in cases of Abolition of Post.
Four individuals were temporarily promoted to the position of computor, and although the position was later abolished, they cannot contest their subsequent reversion.
Meanwhile, other individuals who were also appointed to the same position have had their services regularized, and respondent nos. 1 to 4 were therefore reverted from the cadre.
As such, respondent nos. 5 to 37 are now considered the most senior members of the computor cadre.
