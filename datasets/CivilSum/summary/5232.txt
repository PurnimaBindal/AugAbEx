Prospectus for Punjab Medical Entrance Test-2001, Clause 8(a) for Baba Farid University of Health Sciences, Faridkot - Admissions - Appointment - Sports quota - 'Candidate' - Reservation for S/C and S/Cs - Qualifying standards for - The eligibility condition of obtaining minimum marks in the competitive examination does not apply to the candidates of the sports category, and they are not required to secure 50% marks in the competitive test.
Admission to the sports category is controlled by Clause 3(iv) as a reserved class apart from general category 'candidates.'
However, the sports category is a distinct class than reserved category for which the minimum qualifying standard has been relaxed.
Although the sports category has been given the benefit of reserved quota posts, they are still within the definition of 'candidates' entitled to be considered in the general category based on their merit.
The interpretation of the prospectus states that the rule of exclusion hardly operates in the field of interpretation by implication, and Exclusion or relaxation must be specifically provided in the provision itself.
Clause 3(vi) merely indicates the manner and methodology to be adopted for issuances of gradation certificate.
The rule is unambiguous in its language, and no wider explanatory connotations can be given to it so as to extend the benefit of relaxed qualifying standard for the sports category while treating it, equal to reserved categories.
