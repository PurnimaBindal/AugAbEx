Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Grave misconduct - Quantum of punishment - If a worker is discovered to have committed a serious offence or breach of discipline, the Labour Court is not allowed to exercise discretion under Section 11-A and replace the termination penalty under the principles of proportionality of punishment - Disruptive behavior and gross insubordination should not be encouraged in the workplace.
