Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab PWD (Electricity Branch) Provincial Service Class III (Subordinate Posts) Rules, 1952, Rule 9 - Promotion - When promoting from Assistant to Deputy Superintendent, the seniority of officiating members of the Service will be determined by the order of their appointment prior to confirmation and by the dates of their confirmation after confirmation - The exception to Rule 9 only applies to temporary promotions for purposes other than inefficiency of senior members.
