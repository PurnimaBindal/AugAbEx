Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Termination - Reinstatement - Compensation in lieu - Workman's services were terminated without any inquiry or charge-sheet.
The workman was in the habit of making false and defamatory complaints and misled the workmen.
His activities became unwanted, uncontrollable, and unmanageable.
It was difficult to hold an inquiry in this situation.
The Labor Court held that the order terminating the workman's services without any proceedings is vitiated and there is no ground to interfere.
However, since 20 years have elapsed, it would not be proper to direct reinstatement, and hence a compensation of Rs. 15,000/- has been granted.
[Paras 3, 6, 7, and 8]
