The rules for selection and promotion for Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) employees are outlined in the Constitution of India, the Life Insurance Corporation Act of 1956, the Life Insurance Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations of 1960, and the LIC Class III and Class IV Employees (Promotion) Rules of 1987.
When promoting Class-III employees to Higher Grade Assistants, the selection criterion can be based on seniority, service record, written test, and interview.
The allocation of marks under different heads does not make the rule-making authority biased or arbitrary.
For direct appointments of fresh entrants, interview marks should not be excessive, with about 12% of marks being recommended for interview.
When selecting for promotion from experienced and matured persons, reserving even up to 33% of marks for interview is not arbitrary.
Ultimately, the competent authority is responsible for deciding the best and most reasonable method for selection and promotion.
