Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1970, Rule 17(1) - Reimbursement of medical bills - By-pass surgery - Claim based on disputed fact - By-pass surgery treatment received from outside the State without certificate under Rule 17(1) - No evidence that petitioner could not obtain the certificate from SMS Hospital in Jaipur - No evidence that the treatment was not available in any State Hospital - Accepting claims without proof would make Sub-Rule (1) of Rule 17 redundant - Disputed fact claims cannot be allowed under Article 226 writ jurisdiction - Petition dismissed.
