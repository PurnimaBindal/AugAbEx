Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Claim from retrospective date - A General candidate promoted on a reserved category post as petitioner did not pass departmental examination - The condition in promotion order will be reverted on availability of an SC candidate - Petitioner eligible from 20.3.1990 - Petitioner promoted from 14.12.2001 - Respondent fully supported petitioner's claim in civil suit by General candidate - Given condition, petitioner had to be considered for promotion from 20.3.1990 - Respondent cannot take contrary stand after defending petitioner's right in civil suit.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Claim from retrospective date - Subsequent amendment in rules - Any subsequent amendment in Rules cannot take away accrued right of petitioner - Referring to Supreme Court judgment in case of YV Rangaiah - Plea of reduction in posts due to amendment in rules dismissed.
