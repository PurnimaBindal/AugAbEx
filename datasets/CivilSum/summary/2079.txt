Constitution of India, Articles 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 151 Order 39 Rule 1 - Interim stay/Vacation of stay/Interest of justice/Natural justice - The management filed a petition against orders of authorities under the Act for the service matters of an employee without impleading them as a party - The employee had attempted to remedy the issue through legal channels, but was unsuccessful due to technical grounds - The conduct of the management was not fair, as they accepted the employee's resignation via an incompetent authority - The management reinstated the employee as Principal of the college before obtaining a stay order in the petition - Given these circumstances, the court felt that an ex parte stay should be vacated.
[Paras 10, 11 and 12]
