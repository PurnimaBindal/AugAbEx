In accordance with Section 12 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, the award of compensation is based on the determination of the principal employer.
However, in a case where the petitioner argues that they were not the employer of the deceased and had only provided work to the respondent as the owner of the property, the court held that the work provided was not part of the Petitioner's trade and did not constitute a sub-contract, thus the petitioner could not be identified as the principal employer under Section 12 of the Act.
