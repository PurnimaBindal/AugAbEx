The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 pertain to Service Rules and the Promotion Scheme.
Rules and policies for recruitment and promotions must be framed.
Statutory rules framed by the Legislature or under Article 309 of the Constitution can be changed by amending the provisions of law retrospectively.
However, executive instructions are not sufficient.
If there are no statutory rules governing the service conditions of employees of a society and the competent body of the society frames rules laying down conditions of service of its employees, such rules can be amended by a resolution of the competent body of the society.
Article 309 of the Constitution is not involved.
If the amended scheme introduces a uniform criteria, it can constitute a better position for all scientists and cannot be termed to be in any way unreasonable, arbitrary, or irrational or violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
The New Career Assessment Scheme of U.G.C.
applicable w.e.f.
111986, which requires 12 years of service for consideration for promotion to higher grade of scientist (senior scale) and replaced the old scheme of five years of service, is not discriminatory, arbitrary, unreasonable or irrational.
