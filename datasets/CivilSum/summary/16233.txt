In a writ petition regarding pension, it was claimed that the period of work-charged service of the petitioner should be included for calculating pensionary benefits.
Referring to the Kesar Chand v. State of Punjab judgment, it was decided that work-charged service must be counted.
[Para 1]
The petitioner was initially appointed as a work-charged Beldar, but his services were regularised and he has since retired.
It was determined that the period of work-charged service should be considered when calculating pensionary benefits.
