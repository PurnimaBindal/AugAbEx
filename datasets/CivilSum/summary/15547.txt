Constitution of India, Article 226 - Salary - Promotion - The petitioner is a Traffic Manager who has been holding the current duty charge of the post of General Manager since 1993.
He filed a petition requesting promotion on a regular basis and for salary of the post of General Manager from the date he has been holding the rank.
One post of General Manager in the quota of Traffic Manager fell vacant on 112000.
Respondents claimed that the petitioner signed an affidavit stating that he would not claim the salary of General Manager.
The court found the affidavit to be meaningless as it is against public policy and not enforceable.
The petition was admitted for hearing to determine the entitlement of petitioner for the pay of General Manager from 1993.
Interim directions were issued to the respondents, stating that petitioner is entitled to the pay of General Manager from 112000.
