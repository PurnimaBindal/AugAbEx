Punjab Subordinate Agricultural Service Rules, 1933, Rules 8 and 12 - Departmental Examination - Agricultural Inspectors, Soil Conservation Inspectors and Sectional Officers are obligated to pass the departmental examination in Accounts.
Rule 12 solely applies to the direct recruits and not the promotees.
Rule 8 too does not mandate the departmental examination in accounts to be passed by all those who are promoted to the posts equivalent to Agricultural Inspector.
The rule does not require any such condition for promoted Sectional Officers.

Evidence Act, Section 115 - Estoppel - Acquiescence - No estoppel is being applied against statute and petitioners are not estopped from refuting the conditions as they have accepted to the condition by taking a departmental test, unless it is proven that the conditions have been enforced by virtue of any statutory Rule or instructions.
