Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2 Item 6 - Casual Labour - Advertisement for engagement - The direction given by the Tribunal to include names of applicants in the Live Casual Labour Register is misconceived since the advertisement was issued to engage casual labourers from outside rather than from the Live Casual Labour Register.
[Para 12]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25H - Casual labour - Advertisement for engagement - According to Section 25-H of 1947 Act, when Petitioners propose to engage persons as casual labourers, they are duty bound to give an opportunity to retrenched workmen to offer themselves for reemployment and those retrenched casual labourers who offer themselves for reemployment shall have preference over other persons.
[Para 14, 16]
