Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, under Section 33-C(2), only allows for limited scope of enquiry to determine dues and interpret settlements and awards for said purpose.
Proceeding under this section is only for execution, and therefore, the court cannot adjudicate a settlement's validity or check for fraudulent activity between workmen and management.
The Labour Court is to compute only on existing rights.
