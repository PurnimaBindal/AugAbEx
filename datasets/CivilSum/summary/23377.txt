Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2A and 33-C(2) - Industrial dispute - Claim of wages - Death of workman during the pendency of dispute - The legal heirs and legal representatives of the deceased workman retain their right to continue proceedings and claim benefits as successors - The application of the maxim 'actio personalis moritur cum persona', 'a personal action dies with the person', is limited and depends on the relief claimed and facts of each case.
[Paras 11, 12, 13 and 19]
