Delhi University Calender, Ordinances - Ordinance XVIII, Clause 7 - Promotion/Appointment - According to the language of Clause 7, the post of Principal can be held by someone who can teach in either the college or the University.
The qualification for teaching any particular subject in the college is not necessary for appointment to the post of Principal.
The petitioner was found fit for appointment by the Selection Committee and recommended by the Vice Chancellor.
The Vice Chancellor's denial of approval was illegal and the University has been directed to appoint the petitioner as principal due to her experience in teaching.
