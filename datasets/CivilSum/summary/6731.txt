The Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Sections 2(4), 21(4) - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 1(b)(b), 2(2) - Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Section 2(d), 2(e)(6) - Liability for payment of gratuity under contract labour - Although petitioner is not immediate employer, they are liable for payment as premises of contractor can be viewed as commercial establishment making them a principal employer - Payment will be made as per section 21(4) of Act of 1970 and recovered from contractor with whom initial responsibility to make payment lies.
