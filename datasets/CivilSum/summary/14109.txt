According to Sections 39 and 25L(b) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the question arises if the delegation of power under Section 39 would strip the delegator of its powers.
However, since the delegator under Section 25L(b) is the Central Government, and the delegate (i.e. the State Government) can never be the "appropriate Government", the delegation does not denude the delegator of its powers.
Additionally, the delegation also creates a legal fiction that validates actions of the State Government as if they were done by the Central Government, thus further supporting the notion that the delegation does not diminish the delegator's powers.
