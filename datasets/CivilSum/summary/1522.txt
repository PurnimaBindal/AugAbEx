Indian Police Services (Pay) Rules, 1954, Rule 3 - All India (Confidential Rolls) Rules, Rule 4 - Annual Confidential report - Downgrading in confidential report not considered as adverse remarks - Such downgrading need not be communicated to concerned officer under the Rules - Explanation required only when grading is downgraded severely from 'outstanding'.
