The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 regarding transfer invokes writ jurisdiction, but writ courts usually do not intervene in such matters.
However, to safeguard the well-being of lady officials with newborn infants, they must not be transferred far from their residence until the infant reaches 16 months of age.
Although protection for such officials is not currently a part of policy, the government is directed to make necessary provisions to address this issue.
