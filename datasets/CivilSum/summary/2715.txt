Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rules 208 and 244(1) - Rajasthan Civil Service (Pension) Rules, 1996, Rules 25 and 50(1) - Resignation and voluntary retirement from service - Recognition of resignation as voluntary retirement - Two classes of government servants - Regulations by separate rules - Distinct effects and consequences - Resignation possible anytime - Acceptance at discretion of competent authority - Eligibility for voluntary retirement based on qualifying period of service - Resignation entails forfeiture of past service - Non-punitive forfeiture in case of voluntary resignation.
