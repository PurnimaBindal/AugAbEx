Kerala Education Rules, 1959, Rules 1, 2, 5, and 6 of Chapter XXXII - Appointment as Headmaster/Principal - The petitioner cannot claim appointment under the rules and is not qualified due to criminal cases pending against him.
The denial of appointment is deemed legal and not arbitrary.
[Paras 25, 26 and 28]
Kerala Education Rules, 1959, Rules 1, 2, 5, and 6 of Chapter XIV(A) - Headmaster - Only one Academic and Administrative Head, i.e.
Principal, can be present in a school, and appointing Headmaster as Principal is a device to neglect eligibility, which is academically improper, administratively unsound, and legally untenable.
Hence, it cannot be sustained.
[Paras 29 and 30]
