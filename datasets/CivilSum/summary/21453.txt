Constitution of India, Article 16 - Voluntary Retirement - Corporation accepting resignation of respondent under Voluntary Retirement Scheme subject to clearance of dues by the Respondent - However, since the dues were not cleared, the Scheme of Compulsory retirement was withdrawn by Corporation - It is important to note that the jural relation of employer and employee does not come to end till employee is actually relieved which means that no right is created in favour of respondent to seek compulsory retirement.
