Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(c) and Section 126(2)(c) (J&K Constitution) - Dismissal - Removal - Reinstatement - Departmental Inquiry - The petitioner, who was appointed as a teacher, was initially suspended, reinstated and later dismissed from service.
The dismissal is under challenge in this petition and a distinction is drawn between the provisions of the amended Articles 311(2) and 126(2).
The latter provides an additional safeguard of granting a reasonable opportunity to the delinquent employee for making representations against the proposed penalty, which stands deleted in Article 311(2).
As the respondent was at default in resisting the petitioner's case and producing the relevant record to indicate the basis for the Governor's satisfaction in dispensing with the inquiry, it is not possible to uphold the action.
The only inference that can be drawn in the circumstances is that either there was no material placed before the Governor to derive the requisite satisfaction or such material, if any, was not relevant to "the interests of the security of the State".
This is corroborated by the attending circumstances of the case, which show that the petitioner was facing trial before the criminal court and was also served grounds of detention under the Public Safety Act.
If that be the material, it would hardly constitute a basis for dispensing with the departmental inquiry against the petitioner.
The petitioner's right of inquiry guaranteed to him by Sec. 126(1) and (2) and Article 311(1) and (2) was also violated.
The petition succeeds.
