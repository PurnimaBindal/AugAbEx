Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Discharge during probation - Motive and foundation of the order or discharge are the true tests to determine its nature - A constable may be discharged within three years of enrolment if found unlikely to prove an efficient police officer, despite any misconduct - Conclusions from a fact-finding enquiry regarding fitness for police service may be the motive, but not the foundation, for discharge - Such a discharge order is not punitive in nature and does not violate Articles 14 and 311 of the Constitution.
[Paras 4, 5, 6, 10 to 12 & 15]
