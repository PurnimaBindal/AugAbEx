Constitution of India, Article 16 - Karnataka General Service (Motor Vehicles Branch) (Recruitment) Rules, 1962 - Advertisement - Appointment - Selection - Amendment of rules - Effect - Candidate applying for a post in response to an advertisement - Satisfied the requisite qualifications - Process of selection stopped due to court orders - Rules changed later - Became ineligible - Effect - Whether such a candidate has the right to be considered under current rules - Ruled in favor of the candidate - The amending rule applies retrospectively.
