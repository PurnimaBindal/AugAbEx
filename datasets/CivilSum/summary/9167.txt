The Uttar Pradesh Sewakal Men Mritiya Sarkari Sewakonke Ashriton Ki Bharti Niyamavali of 1974, along with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, relate to compassionate appointment, termination of service, and selection.
The petitioner's father, who was an instructor, died while in service, and the petitioner was appointed as an instructor on the post of compassionate grounds.
Therefore, the petitioner's services cannot be terminated simply because he was not selected, especially since the sub-committee constituted by the executive council considered his appointment to be proper and entitled to be continued.
Additionally, his appointment cannot be questioned by any other petty official, and his services cannot be terminated without due course of the law.
[Paras 8, 9 and 10]
