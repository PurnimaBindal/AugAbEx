The Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Section 18 and the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 47 deal with medical unfitness and rehabilitation.
In this case, the petitioner was invalidated out of service due to medical unfitness that was acquired during service.
However, there were no specific claims that the petitioner's case was referred to the Rehabilitation Board, nor was it shown that the petitioner was unable to perform any other duties within the department.
Therefore, the respondents have been ordered to provide relief to the petitioner.
