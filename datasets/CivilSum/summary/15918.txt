Article 226 of the Constitution of India and the West Bengal Service Rules, Part I, rules 9(1) and (5) govern date of birth reviews for employees.
The Appointing Authority issues a provisional date of birth which is subject to review.
This review must be completed only on the basis of existing evidence or material available at the time of the employee's service start date.
