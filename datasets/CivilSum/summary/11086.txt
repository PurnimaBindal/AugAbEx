According to the State Bank of India (Supervising Staff) Service Rules, Rule 50 and the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 226, an eligible officer must be considered for promotion even if they are under suspension or facing disciplinary proceedings.
The lack of a charge-sheet does not qualify for sealed cover promotion, therefore, the judgment of the Single Judge stating the petitioner's entitlement to promotion from the due date is upheld.
The Bank's appeal is dismissed.
