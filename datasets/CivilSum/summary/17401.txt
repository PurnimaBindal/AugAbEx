According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, an employee of the State Govt. who is entitled to a specific higher pay scale according to the pay scale Rules prescribed by the Govt. and recommended by department authorities cannot have the pay scale withdrawn if deemed not entitled due to not being in the cadre.
The respondents have been instructed to release all arrears of pay and recoveries made from the petitioner with 12% interest.
