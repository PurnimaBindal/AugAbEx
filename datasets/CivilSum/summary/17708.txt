Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation - Daily Wage workers - Regularisation of services of daily wage workers based on the Haryana Government instructions dated 651993 - Service length requirements - Breaks in service - The continuous period of service cannot be deducted for holidays, leaves, forced idleness, absence due to illness, and other reasonable and sufficient causes - Petitioners have been directed to verify the records and explain reasons for absence - Appointing authorities have been directed to objectively and sympathetically consider their cases and condone the breaks/absences if explained with sufficient cause, and not to interrupt their services until a decision has been made.
[Paras 2 to 4]
