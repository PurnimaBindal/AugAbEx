Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Probation - The petitioner was terminated from his services during his probation period of two years on the grounds of not being able to complete it successfully.
The termination order was issued while the probation period had not yet completed and while an enquiry was still pending against the petitioner after he was charge-sheeted.
As a result, he could not be thrown out of service in the midst of the probation period or before a decision had been made on the enquiry.
Consequently, the order of termination was quashed and the petitioner was reinstated with all consequential benefits.
However, the competent authority would still be at liberty to proceed with the enquiry if deemed necessary.
[Paras 10 and 11]
