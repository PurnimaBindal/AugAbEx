Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A Quantum of punishment - Disproportionate punishment - Accused Conductor did not issue tickets to passengers leading to a Rs.35.10 loss to the Corporation - The Labour Court deemed the punishment of dismissal to be excessive compared to the offense and rightly nullified it.
