Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Act - Invoking writ jurisdiction - Appointment to non-existent post - Only a legally enforceable right can be specifically enforced under Article 226 of the Constitution.
A claim made de hors the Rule or the law in violation to Articles 14 and 16 cannot be enforced.
If no Service Rules are present, executive instructions/Policy or guidelines will operate.
Even without Service Rules or instructions or guidelines, a fair procedure for recruitment in accordance with Articles 14 and 16 should be used.
Appointments made in violation of the rules cannot be protected.
