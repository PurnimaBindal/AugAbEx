The transfer of a petitioner with a certain medical disability to a station 8 KM away was justified as it was an exigency of service.
There was no explanation given as to how the transfer caused harm or harassment to the petitioner and no malafide was found in the order of transfer.
One cannot insist on being posted against a particular post at a particular place.
Therefore, the transfer order cannot be interfered with.
[Para 8 & 12]  B As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16, it is for the employer to decide which person fits best at which place and not the court.
The court should not interfere in a transfer order unless it violates any conditions of service or statutory rules, in addition to being biased or malafide.
