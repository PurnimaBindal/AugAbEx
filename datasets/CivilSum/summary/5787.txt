The Constitution of India's Articles 226/227 and 311 concern pre-mature retirement, and in this case, a writ was presented against the notice to retire the petitioner at the age of 55.
The notice was considered invalid as the Competent Authority did not review the petitioner's record, instead following the DIG of Police's instruction.
This decision has been set aside, and the competent authority may review the record and determine whether the petitioner should have been kept in service beyond the age of 55.
[Para 13]
The Constitution of India's Articles 226/227 and 311 also concern pre-mature/compulsory retirement, and when the competent authority acts as a mere post office to issue the retirement notice based on higher authority's evaluation, retirement cannot be upheld on the grounds that the higher authority approved the competent authority's action.
In this case, the decision to retire the petitioner was not made by Respondent No. 3 Commandant, HAP but rather by the DIG of Police.
As such, the notice cannot be deemed lawful, and it has been invalidated.
