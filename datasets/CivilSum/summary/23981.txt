Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(q) - Strike - Closure - Reference - To determine whether a strike is justified, the Tribunal must examine if the dispute referred to it is an industrial dispute, which depends on whether the employer's action was a closure or lock-out.
If the industry has been closed and the closure is real and bonafide, there can be no industrial dispute.
Therefore, it is incorrect to assume that the Tribunal cannot consider the question of closure if it was not referred to it.
Even if closure was not specifically mentioned in the reference, if the reference is to adjudicate the validity and justification of retrenchment and the employer defends with a closure, the Tribunal must first check whether there was a closure and if it was bonafide.
A permanent discontinuance of process necessarily means closure.
