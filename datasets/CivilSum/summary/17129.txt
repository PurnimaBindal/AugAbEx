As per the Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 226, the post of Arts and Craft Teachers has seven reserved positions for ex-servicemen.
The petitioner, who is the spouse of an ex-serviceman and dependent on him, applied for one of these positions.
Even though the petitioner submitted a certificate of her dependent status on her ex-serviceman husband after submitting her application form, there was no requirement to attach any certificates, including the one related to her dependent status, with the application form.
The Commission was not justified in not recommending the petitioner's name when she was one of the seven most meritorious candidates among the dependents of ex-servicemen who applied for the post.
Therefore, the Commission must comply with the given directions to recommend the petitioner's name and offer her the appointment for the post.
