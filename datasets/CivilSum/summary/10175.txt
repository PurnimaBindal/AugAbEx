Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations 1971, Appendix II, Item No. 2 - Competent authority – A delinquent who was appointed by the Senior Regional Manager as Technical Assistant Grade III in Food Corporation of India and was later promoted to Technical Assistant Grade II by the Zonal Manager could not be disciplined by the Zonal Manager as he does not hold the authority to appoint under Appendix II, where Technical Assistant Grade II falls under category III posts.
[Paras 5 to 7]  B Constitution of India, Art. 311(1) - Applicability - The principles under Article 311(1) of the Constitution of India apply only to persons in civil employment as the employees of the Corporation are not civil posts.
