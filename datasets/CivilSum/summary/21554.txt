Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(1) - Disciplinary proceedings - Subsistence allowance - Workmen have the right to receive subsistence allowance during the period of suspension, even during the pendency of application U/s 33(1) of ID Act.
While an employee cannot sustain without any subsistence allowance, the management can wait for the disposal of such application.
The right of Management to suspend and the right of the employee to receive Subsistence Allowance are intertwined and both must survive together.
The rate of Subsistence allowance should be according to the Rules, and if there is none, the employee would be entitled to full salary during suspension.
The decisions in Hotel Imperial's case and Fakirbhai's case deal with different situations, and there is no conflict of opinion.
