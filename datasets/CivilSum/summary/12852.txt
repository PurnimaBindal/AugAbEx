Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 13.7(1) Extract of - Selection of constables for admission to Lower School Course - Two prescribed tests: parade and written test - Interview and examination of service record not considered tests - Passing score of 50% required for parade and written test, not for interview and examination - Petitioner ranked 50th and no lower-ranked candidate sent for training - Merit list of 54 candidates created, only 30 deputed for training - Impugned selection of Constables for admission of Lower School Course deemed legal.
