The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 16 and 226, prohibits sex discrimination in compassionate appointment.
In a specific case, a woman applied for compassionate appointment on the death of her husband.
However, the institute committee held the view that it would not be appropriate to appoint a woman in a boys' school without considering the matter of selection and appointment.
The court held that this decision was violative of Article 16 of the Constitution and directed the institute to initiate the recruitment process for appointing the petitioner.
