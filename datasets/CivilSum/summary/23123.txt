Constitution of India, Articles 15(4) and 16(4) - Reservation - Reservation in admissions - Scheduled Tribe - Social Status Certificate - False certificates - Admission to an engineering college on the basis of false scheduled tribe certificates constitutes fraud on the Constitution and is prosecutable.
Guidelines have been provided for the streamlined issuance and scrutiny of social status certificates.
Admission obtained through false certificates must be nullified, but in the case of a minor who has since completed the course, the only action that can be taken is to deny them the benefits of entry into service as a Scheduled Tribe.
The student must also pay Rs. 25,000/- to the college/State to defray the costs of the course.
The student's father/guardian, who obtained the false certificate, cannot be prosecuted as they are not a party before the court.
