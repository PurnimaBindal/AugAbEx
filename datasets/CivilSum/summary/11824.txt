The time limit for requesting employment on compassionate grounds may be extended to three years, but a request must be made to the Board within one year from the date of the employee's death.
In this case, the application was submitted on July 2, 1986, which was more than three years after the death of the husband, and the son was still a minor at the time.
Therefore, the application was rejected in accordance with the Board's instructions.
[Para 11] B.
Although there were no time limits prescribed by the Board for requesting employment on compassionate grounds when the employee died, such limits had been put in place by the time the widow submitted her application.
Furthermore, the widow had not requested special pensionary benefits and instead waited for five years to request employment for her son, which suggests that she was not in need of employment.
As a result, the Board's rejection of her application was justified.
