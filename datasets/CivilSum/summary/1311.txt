Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 17-B states that wages must be paid during the pendency of a writ petition against the award of reinstatement.
If a writ petition is allowed without deciding the application under Section 17-B, a fresh application can still be maintained to recover the arrears of wages accumulating under Section 17-B There is no bar against the maintainability of such an application after the decision of the writ petition unless it is shown that the application was dismissed earlier or wages were paid.
The only requirement is that there must be an award or reinstatement, and even if execution is stayed, that does not prevent a claim under Section 17-B [Paras 4 to 7]
