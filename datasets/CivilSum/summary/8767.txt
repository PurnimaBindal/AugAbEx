The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 state that a probationer's services can be terminated during probation, but only if a conscious decision is made by the competent authority.
However, the plaintiff in this case was relieved without any notice, without following the principle of last come first go, and without there being any decision of the authority to dispense with his services during the probation period.
This action is arbitrary and discriminatory.
[Paras 8 and 9]
While a contract of personal service cannot be enforced when it is a service of trust, confidence or strictly of personal skill, the court can declare invalid an act of a statutory body that terminates service of its employee in violation of the provisions of that statute itself.
In this case, the municipal committee terminated the employee's services during probation without following the terms of the appointment letter or the rules, but on a non-existing and invalid ground.
Thus, the termination is illegal and the municipal committee is directed to reinstate the appellant.
[Paras 10 and 11]
In cases of termination during probation, back wages are not granted if the employee worked only for a short period.
In this case, the employee's services were terminated after only seven months during probation, and the termination was found to be illegal.
However, he is not entitled to back wages.
