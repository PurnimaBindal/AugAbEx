Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Recruitment - Appointment - Medical fitness - Defence services - Recruitment for the post of Airman - Medical examination required after passing technical trade test - The opinion of the Medical Board constituted by the appointing authority, rather than other doctors or hospitals, is crucial - Even if declared fit by All India Institute of Medical Sciences, New Delhi, it holds no significance over the opinion of the Medical Board.
