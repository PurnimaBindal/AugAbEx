Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952 Rule 7 - Dismissal - Natural justice - Enquiry Report - Right of petitioner - The Enquiry Report needed to be provided to the petitioner before the Board approved the findings of the Enquiry Officer - The petitioner only received a copy of the enquiry report after the Board's decision to dismiss him had been made - The petitioner was unaware of the report's contents, despite it being used against him by the Board - The argument that the Board was not required to provide the petitioner with a copy before approval and that the petitioner was given an opportunity to make a representation prior to the dismissal order being passed is not acceptable - The termination order has been revoked.
