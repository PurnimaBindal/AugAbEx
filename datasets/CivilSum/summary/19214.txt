Jammu University Calendar, Chapter XXX, Statutes 2, 3, 11 and 12 - Examination - Shortage of lectures - Eligibility - Only eligible students receive Roll No. slips and can appear in examination, with verification of eligibility - Controller of Examination can block a student from examination if ineligible - No Controller order - Superintendent of examination has no power to prevent a student from taking one subject after approving other papers - Respondents are instructed to permit petitioner to take the paper and announce his result.
