The minimum qualifying marks for admission to post-graduate medical courses must be in accordance with the standards laid down by the Medical Council of India.
States may prescribe further qualifications, but not reduce the standards.
The respondent's prescription of minimum marks of 40% is in contravention of MCI regulations and must be ignored.
