Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 25-F - Rajasthan Industrial Dispute Rules, 1958, Rule 22-A - Ex-parte award - Application for setting aside - Rejection of - Non-appearance by management due to non-service of notice - The acknowledgement receipt from which service was deemed effected did not bear the seal of office - The application for setting aside the ex-parte award was made within 24 days of the award - The contention that the workman concealed the fact of the notice and compensation given - The Labour Court has taken a hyper-technical view - Not giving the source of information of the award would not render the application lacking basic detail, where they stated on oath that non-appearance was due to want of service - The management was required to give and prove only a prima facie reason for absence - The petition was allowed.
