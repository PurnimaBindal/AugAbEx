Circulars issued by the Director of Employment, Government of West Bengal in 1987 and 1993 established a 10% reservation for individuals with experience in adult and non-formal education for the appointment of primary teachers under the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 226.
In 1999, rules were formulated for the appointment of primary teachers, with provisions for reservations for SC and ST candidates, but no provision for recruitment of those with experience in adult and non-formal education.
Even under a scheme, appointment cannot be claimed as a matter of right, and writs cannot be issued in violation of statutory provisions.
