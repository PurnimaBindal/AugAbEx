Constitution of India, Article 226 - High Court Establishment (Appointment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1973, Rules 8(iv), 24 and 38 - Promotion - Educational Qualification - Relaxation of - Post of Assistant Registrar - The petitioners contend that non-graduates were promoted as Assistant Registrars through the power of relaxation, despite the presence of eligible candidates in the same cadre, and that no relaxation should be given in such circumstances - However, the power to relax educational qualifications for promotion rests with the Chief Justice, and there is no restriction on the exercise of this power even in the presence of eligible candidates.
