Food Corporation Act, 1964, Sections 6, 45 - Central government directives on recruitment process under scrutiny for sustainability - Complete ban on recruitment imposed by the central government - Contention that this is beyond the scope of Section 6(2) of the Act - Held that under Section 6(2), the Board of Directors shall be guided by the central government's "instructions on questions of policy" - Questions of policy apply not only to organization, management, and function of FCI but also to its employment policy, recruitment, and other details related to interests of consumers/producers - Therefore, directives were within the ambit of Section 6(2) of the Act.
[Paras 15 and 24] B Food Corporation Act, 1964, Sections 6, 45 - Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 7(2) - Recruitment process under scrutiny - Departmental candidates aged 52-53 proposed for appointment despite recruitment rules specifying a maximum age of 35-40 - Contention that power of relaxation is envisaged in rules - Held that power of relaxation is intended for marginal cases where exceptionally qualified candidates are available and cannot be used to completely distort regulations - Hence selection process was declared null and void.
[Paras 15 and 24] C. Food Corporation Act, 1964, Sections 6, 45 - Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 7(2) - Recruitment process - Plea of discrimination - Alleged ban on recruitment for Deputy Manager's post - Contention that discrimination exists as Joint Manager (Accounts) and Joint Manager (General Administration) posts were filled but Deputy Manager's post was not - Held that discrimination can only arise between similarly situated individuals and in this case, since there is nothing common between two posts, discrimination cannot be alleged.
[Para 19] D. Food Corporation Act, 1964, Sections 6, 45 - Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 7(2) - Recruitment process - Allegation of contravention of regulation - Alleged freeze on recruitment process for Deputy Manager's post in contravention of Regulation 7(2) of the 1971 Regulations - Held that Regulation 7(2) vests power to grant relaxation in Board - Since Section 45 of the Act puts a restriction on FCI regulations to be consistent with the Act and Rules made thereunder, any regulation inconsistent with Section 45 or overriding the central government's directive cannot be read.
[Para 24] E. Constitution of India, Article 14 - Recruitment process - Obligation to fill vacancies - State is not obligated to fill all vacancies unless there is provision to the contrary in applicable rules - Decision not to fill vacancies must be taken bona fide and must pass the test of reasonableness not to fail on touchstone of Article 14 of the Constitution - If vacancies are proposed to be filled, State is obliged to fill them as per merit from the list of selected candidates - Whether to fill or not fill the vacancies is a policy decision and there is no scope for interference in judicial review unless it is infected with the vice of arbitrariness.
[Para 17] F. Special Leave Petition - Permissibility of new arguments - Judgment in writ appeal only argued one question before Single Judge - Held that facts obtained from judgment of High Court must be accepted as correct, which cannot be controverted by claims made in special leave petition or statements made across the bar - Hence, any contention other than those raised before High Court cannot be accepted.
