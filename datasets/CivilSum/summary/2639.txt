Constitution of India, Article 311 - Departmental enquiry - Criminal proceedings - The charge for departmental enquiry may differ from the offence committed in criminal proceedings against the petitioner, but some facts may overlap - The scope of a departmental inquiry and criminal cases differs - It is not justifiable to halt a departmental enquiry during the pendency of criminal proceedings, as per 1996(4) SCT 708(SC).
