Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Retrenchment/Termination - Respondents were employed on work charge-basis on different posts at Anandpur Sahib Hydel Project - The services of the respondents were terminated on the completion of the project in conformily with the standing orders - They have no legal right to claim appointment to the post on another project which was to be run by different authorities - The appellants cannot be expected to keep creating jobs without any need merely for accommodating retrenched employees.
