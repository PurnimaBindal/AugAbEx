The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 21, state the principle of "no work, no pay".
However, the authorities of the government have deprived the petitioner of his right to serve the department for almost two years despite his repeated representations.
The government even found their action unjustified, which makes the failure to pay the petitioner's salary and allowances during the intervening period arbitrary and violates his right to equality and livelihood.
As a resolution, the authorities were directed to pay the petitioner's arrears of salary with 18% interest.
