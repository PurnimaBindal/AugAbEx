The DPI sent a show cause notice to the petitioner regarding proposed changes to inter-se seniority in the Superintendent of Officers rank, as dictated by the Constitution of India Article 16.
The Secretary to Govt. was the Appointing Authority for the petitioner and private respondents in the matter.
The Govt. decided to rearrange the inter-se seniority of the petitioner and other officers based on private respondent representations, with no regard for the petitioner’s representation.
The concerned officers were not given a personal hearing, and the impugned order failed to give any reason for why the representation of the petitioner was not considered.
The notice did not indicate the grounds for the change in seniority, and the petitioner was not provided copies of representations made by private respondents.
Since the impugned order is not a speaking order, it is quashed.
