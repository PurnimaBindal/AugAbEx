The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, deals with education and admissions criteria, especially regarding reservation in admissions.
Additional seats are created for the admission of NRI/NRI sponsored candidates to generate foreign exchange for the developmental activities of the University.
In such cases, no NRI candidate can be admitted against the originally advertised seats.
The petitioner, being a general non-NRI candidate, does not have the right to challenge the admission of NRI candidates, as it does not affect the rights of non-NRI candidates.
[Paras 1 and 2]
