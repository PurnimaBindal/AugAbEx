Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1978, Sections 2(24A), 5(2A), 12 (as amended by Act 14 of 2007) - Whether a Shikshan Sevak who has completed three years or 30 months of service and has been appointed as an Assistant Teacher in accordance with clause 8 of the Government Resolution dated 13th October, 2000 must be appointed as a confirmed teacher or a teacher on probation - Once he has successfully completed a probation period of three years, he will be considered to have been appointed and confirmed as a teacher.
