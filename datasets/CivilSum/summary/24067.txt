As per the Uttar Pradesh Regularisation of Ad-hoc Appointments (on posts within the purview of the Public Service Commission) Rules, 1979, Rule 7(2), seniority shall be determined based on the order of appointment and inter se.
The respondent was treated better in merit than the appellant and rightly placed senior.
The fixation of seniority cannot be faulted.
The appellant cannot demand the court to investigate the selection committee's evaluation of merit for the sole purpose of seniority fixation.
[Paras 9 and 11]
