[Para 5] B.
Articles 14, 16 and 309 of the Constitution of India - Regularization rules for daily wage workers - Sustainability - It has been established by the Constitution Bench judgment of this Court in Umadevi's case that appointments made in violation of the constitutional scheme of equality are illegal and thus void ab initio - Therefore, the State cannot make any regularization rules in violation of the statutory or constitutional scheme - In any event, it is impermissible in law to regularize employees based on any policy decision taken by the State.
