The Industrial Establishments Act of 1958 in Kerala, along with Sections 3 and 5, as well as Sections 23(b) and 24 of the Industrial Disputes Act, states that workers are entitled to wages for national and festival holidays, regardless of any individual disputes being handled in the Labour Court under the provisions of Section 2(a).
Workers are allowed to strike on matters that do not affect individual rights, and employers are obligated to pay national and festival holiday wages to workers on strike.
