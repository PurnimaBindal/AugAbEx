Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - Closure of some disciplines in College of Animal Science - An Office order was issued to adjust dislodged students in different M.Sc.
(Agriculture) disciplines, and the petitioners' claim to be accommodated in the same discipline as the dislodged students they believed were lower in merit than them was not justified.
The order only applied to those who were dislodged from their course, and did not seek to reallocate candidates based on their merit.
As none of the petitioners were dislodged from their course, their claim had no merit.
[Paras 4 and 5]
