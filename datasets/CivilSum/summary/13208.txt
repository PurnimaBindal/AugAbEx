Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary action - Departmental proceedings initiated against Petitioner on anonymous complaint charging him of irregularities - Respondents contend that Petitioner resisted throughout and refer to chronology of events resulting in delay - Held, although delay is problematic, it loses its impact if explained - Respondents given directions to proceed with enquiry and conclude it within six months - Defaulters to face quashing - Petitioner considered for promotion subject to outcome of enquiry - Petition disposed of.
