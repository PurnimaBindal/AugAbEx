Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Increment - Qualification - Benefit of two advance increments - Individuals who obtain B.E./AMIE degree after 1.1.92 are eligible for this benefit - Plaintiff cannot be deprived of this benefit simply because he obtained the necessary qualification before 1.1.92 when no such requirement was included in the notification.
