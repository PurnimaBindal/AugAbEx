Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 15 - Gujarat Universities Services Tribunal Act, 1988, Sections 7(1)(3) and 13 - Forum with jurisdiction over dispute between University and employee - Industrial Tribunal lacking jurisdiction in regard to such dispute - Jurisdiction of forum created under special statute - 1983 Gujarat University Act providing for special form to settle this kind of dispute - Any other form lacking jurisdiction - Industrial Tribunal's award deemed null and void - Award quashed and set aside as it went beyond Industrial Tribunal's jurisdiction.
