According to the Constitution of India, as stated under Articles 14 and 16, the posts of Deputy Engineer, Assistant Engineer, and Assistant Surveyors of Works (Civil) together make up a common cadre in Dadra and Nagar Haveli PWD Engineering Civil Works.
The officers working on these posts are interchangeable, and the pay scale is identical.
Although there may be some differences in duties and responsibilities, they are interchangeable.
The Sectional Officer is the feeder cadre for all, and all officers on these posts are included in the common gradation list prepared by the Collector.
Therefore, all officers in this common cadre are eligible for consideration for the post of Executive Engineer and should be considered accordingly in compliance with the law.
