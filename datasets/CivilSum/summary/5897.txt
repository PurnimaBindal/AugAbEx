As Amicus Curiae, TP Kelu Nambiar argued that while academic bodies are not bound by the rules of procedure that govern court or tribunal enquiries, they must still act fairly when dismissing a college student following an enquiry.
The court can only interfere if the procedure was grossly unfair.
Additionally, under Constitution of India Article 21 and Criminal Procedure Code sections 149-154, police have the power to investigate crimes committed on college campuses and can enter the premises without the Principal's permission.
The state should not interfere with the legitimate exercise of power for the prevention of crimes.
