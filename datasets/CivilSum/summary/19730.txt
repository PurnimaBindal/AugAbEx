Letters Patent Appeal, Clause 10 - Pension - Payment of pension to former employees of Zila Parishad who were later absorbed in Govt. service - Appellant entitled to pension from the date of absorption by Haryana Govt - Latest instructions state that employees like appellant can benefit from previous service with Zila Parishad when calculating pension - Entire service from date of appointment by Zila Parishad must be taken into account - Respondents directed to compute pensionary benefits according to latest instructions.
