Constitution of India, Article 14 and 311 - Termination of service - Abandonment Of job - Penality/Punishment/Absence from duty - If an employee doesn't respond to the employer's call to return to work and has abandoned their job, it may be assumed that they have abandoned their job and termination of their services is not considered as punishment and no further opportunity is required to be given to the defaulter.
