Bank of Baroda Employees' Pension Regulations, 1995, Regulations 2(k), 14, 22 and 28 - Pension - Forfeiture - Retirement - Retiral benefits - Petitioner's request for retiral benefits rejected on the premise that petitioner had relinquished his right to receive retiral benefits by resigning - The respondents state that the petitioner became superannuated on October 31st, 1996, but was declined permission to contest an MC election on November 27th, 1996, and subsequently applied for voluntary retirement and had his resignation accepted on December 2nd, 1996 - The respondents argued that the last day of the year was the retirement date but have not provided any evidence to support this claim - The petitioner is entitled to the pension.
