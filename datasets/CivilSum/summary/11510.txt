Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rules 7, 8 - Dismissal - Disciplinary proceeding - Legitimacy - No opportunity given to cross-examine witnesses, including the author of the preliminary report, leading to a breach of the principles of natural justice and causing great prejudice to the petitioner's right to defend - Enquiry Officer's lack of adherence to rules turns proceedings into a mere eyewash - Hence, entire enquiry proceedings are vitiated.

Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Non-payment of subsistence allowance - Enquiry proceedings - Sustainability - Subsistence allowance awarded after completion of the enquiry but following writ petition filed by the delinquent - Employer's conduct deemed totally unfair and illegal as it handicapped petitioner's defence - Entire proceedings are vitiated as a result.
