The court had ordered the refixing of seniority with consequential benefits, which included notional promotions but no monetary benefits.
Another officer from a different organization was granted consequential benefits.
However, it was held that since the court had previously ordered the grant of consequential benefits and the respondents had opted for a pick and choose policy, willful disobedience had to be proven and compliance must be given opportunity.
