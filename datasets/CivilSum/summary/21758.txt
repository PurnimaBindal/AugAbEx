Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 33 - OM No. F-19(4) - E.V./79 dated 25.5.1979 - Retiral benefits - Superannuation - Validity of Office Memorandum challenged as pay for retirement benefits is treated to a portion of the dearness allowance in respect of Govt. servants retiring on or after 30th September, 1977 but not for those who retired prior to the specified date - Held, setting a cut-off date of 30th September, 1977 was not arbitrary or irrational - The option given to employees who retired between the specified dates to get their retirement benefits calculated based on whether dearness pay is included or excluded from their pension and death-cum-retirement gratuity, had a nexus with the price index level at 272, which it reached on the specified date - The decision was just and valid.
