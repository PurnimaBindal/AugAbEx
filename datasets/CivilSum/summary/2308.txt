Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, Section 32 (5)(b) - Hospital - Determining if Applicable to an Educational Institution - Double number of beds required for a Medical College - 945% of income comes from patients - Selling of 144 medicinal items - 90% of treatment is done on a profit basis - Therefore, the Society in question is not considered an educational institution under section 32(5)(b) of the 1965 Act because it is primarily a hospital operating to make a profit - As a result, the 1965 Act is applicable.
