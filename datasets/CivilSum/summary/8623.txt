According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, the Labour Court had to consider a very comprehensive question in reference to the termination of a workman's services.
They had to determine whether the termination was justified, not just whether it violated certain provisions or if the workman had abandoned their job.
Once the management's defences were made known, the workman could not argue that the plea was beyond the scope of the reference.
In a specific case where a conductor did not deposit a cash bag containing Rs. 2,20280 and the embezzlement was admitted and based on evidence, the Labour Court's decision to terminate the workman was justified.
