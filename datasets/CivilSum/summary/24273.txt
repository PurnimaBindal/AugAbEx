Constitution of India, Article 226 - Scope of Disciplinary proceedings - Removal from service - Quantum punishment - Writ jurisdiction cannot question the disciplinary action conducted with natural justice compliance, unless the punishment is disproportional.
[Para 7]

Delhi Police (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1980, Rules 8(a) and 10 - Absence from duty on medical ground - Removal from service - Quantum of punishment - Rule only authorizes removal from service in case of gross misconduct or unfitness to serve - Absence from work due to medical reasons and later authorized leave cannot be considered misconduct - Removal order is disproportionate, nonsensical, and is liable to be quashed - Reinstatement granted without consequential benefits as a way to end the long legal proceedings.
[Paras 7 to 11]
