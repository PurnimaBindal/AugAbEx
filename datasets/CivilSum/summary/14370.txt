Constitution of India, Article 226 - Employees' Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 7A(2) and 7A(3A) - Officers conducting enquiry for determination of objection and/or liability have been granted power - It is not appropriate for court to make final determination, as it affects proposed determination required to be made by concerned authorities in accordance with law - For determination required by law, parties must co-operate with Provident Fund authorities.
