Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Compulsory Retirement - Superannuation - Retiral benefits - Petitioner was ordered to mandatory retirement - However, the petitioner continued to work with the aid of an interim civil court order and eventually retired - Post-retiral benefits were not fully paid but partial payment was made - The Respondents were ordered to make the balance payment within 90 days with an interest rate of 18% per annum - The Respondent Bank may recover any outstanding payment due from the petitioner in accordance with the law.

Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 10, Order 2 Rule 2 - Constructive res judicata - The petitioner filed a civil suit and withdrew the same on the respondent's request - No judgment was rendered by the civil court - The question of whether constructive res judicata applies does not arise.
