Bihar Development of Ayurvedic and Unani Systems of Medicine Act, 1951 - Punjab Ayurvedic and Unani Practitioners Act, 1963, Section 15 - Indian Medicine Central Council Act, 1970, Sections 17, 23 to 25 - The appellants are registered as medical practitioners with Bihar State Council of Ayurvedic and Unani Medicines, set up under the Bihar Act, 1951, and are currently practising as registered medical practitioners in the State of Haryana, despite having qualifications prescribed under the Punjab and Central Act, which provide a higher and elaborate system for registration - The registration qualifications under the Bihar Act are inferior, and the Bihar Council has much discretion in allowing or refusing registration - Therefore, the right to practise in Haryana is subject to necessary qualifications laid down under the Punjab and Central Acts, and the mere fact that the appellants are registered with Bihar Council under the Bihar Act would not entitle them to practice their profession in Haryana State.
[Para 5] B Bihar Development of Ayurvedic and Unani Systems of Medicine Act, 1951, Punjab Ayurvedic and Unani Practitioners Act, 1963, Section 15 - Indian Medicine Central Council Act, 1970, Sections 17, 23 to 25 - The interpretation of Section 17(3) of the Central Act and the scheme of the Punjab Act and Central Act - A person who has registered under the Central Act is allowed to practice throughout the country, whereas a person who has registered under a State Act, such as the Bihar Act or only under the Punjab Act, is entitled to practice only in that respective State - If the appellants were allowed to practice in Haryana with the qualifications under the Bihar Act, the very purpose of providing qualifications for registration under the Central Act given in Sections 23 and 25 thereof would be frustrated.
