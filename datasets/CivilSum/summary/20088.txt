Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 136 - Fitment in categories of posts - Petitioners who worked as fitters had requested equal pay to be on par with Jig Borers regarding the fact that they drew higher pay scales than fitters.
Instead of elevating their cadre and placing them in a higher pay scale, they were brought down in the category as a fitter after the removal of two nomenclatures.
The nomenclature and fitment are part of the executive policy of the Government.
It would be difficult for the courts to judge equation of posts or fitment into a particular scale of pay unless actions are arbitrary or there is invidious discrimination between similarly situated persons.
Such decisions are mandated to be made by Expert Committees and the Government.
The tribunal's refusal to engage in the matter is therefore justified.
As a result, the petition was dismissed.
