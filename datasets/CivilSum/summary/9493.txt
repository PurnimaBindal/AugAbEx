Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Recruitments - Reservation - For reservation to be implemented, the advertisement of reserved posts is necessary - Vacancies cannot be turned into reserved posts during selection and appointments if no reservation was advertised - This would impact the rights of both general and reserved category applicants - Applicants who did not apply due to the lack of reservation in the advertisement - Selected candidates must be appointed based on merit without reservation.
