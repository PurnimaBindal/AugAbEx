The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - Open invitation is the basis for appointments in public service.
Compassionate appointment is only an execution and not a rule.
The applicant was 25 years old at the time of his father's death, and his father received Gratuity, Group Insurance, Leave Encashment, as well as family pension.
The screening Committee recommended him for consideration, but due to a lack of vacancies under the 5% quota meant for compassionate appointments, he was unable to be appointed within the relevant period.
Subsequent policies or vacancies do not allow him to lay claim to the appointment.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - Appointment in public service cannot be claimed on the basis of discrimination due to illegal appointment; Article 14 does not promote illegalities.
