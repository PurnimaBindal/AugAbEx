The absence of an ability test means that anyone who is eligible can be nominated by a Competent Authority, including the Chief Secretary, for the selection process of PCS (Executive Branch) by PPSC.
While there may be some element of arbitrariness in the nomination process, this cannot be a ground for the nomination to be quashed.
The selection of the most suitable person starts only after the duly nominated candidates are forwarded to PPSC.

When the power to relax rules exists, it is assumed that it may only be exercised in extraordinary circumstances.
It cannot be inferred that where similar circumstances exist, rule 28 must be used for rule relaxation.
The court cannot replace the opinion of the State Government with its own.
The Chief Minister, as per the rules, has the power to make four nominations instead of two but it is not required that a nomination should be made from each department by either of the Competent Authorities described under Rule 10.
[Para 18]
The Competent Authority can recommend anybody they want to follow any criteria or without criteria.
It is not the right of anyone to claim a nomination.
The petitioners had no right to claim nomination as their respective ministers had already made nominations and the Rules did not allow for the addition or substitution of such nominations.
The petition is dismissed.
