Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25-F - Retrenchment - If an employer strikes off a workman's name from the rolls without complying with the provisions of Section 25-F and the workman has completed 240 days of service, it will be considered illegal retrenchment as defined under Section 2(oo).
The workman will be reinstated with continuity of service and 50% backwages.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-B - Retrenchment - To calculate if a workman has rendered service for at least 240 days in twelve calendar months preceding the termination date, the relevant date needs to be determined first.
If the answer is positive, then it will be assumed that the workman is in continuous service for a period of one year.
