Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Superannuation - Higher scale - Recovery - Improvement of qualification - A petitioner who improved his qualification by earning a master's degree was granted benefits according to the relevant Government orders.
The petitioner did not receive the next increment in the higher scale to which he entered in the ordinary course.
Furthermore, any mistake in the date of the increment requires a correction, and the petitioner may be required to pay back excess funds received.
