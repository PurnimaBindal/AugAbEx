Constitution of India, Article 16 - Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, Section 10 - Voluntary retirement scheme - After accepting voluntary retirement and receiving encashed retirement benefits, a workman cannot later argue that he was not in a sound mental state when the scheme was accepted.
It is too late to do so.
The encashment of retiral benefits caused a complete severing of employer-workman relationship, so no industrial dispute under Section 10 can be deemed present or impending.
