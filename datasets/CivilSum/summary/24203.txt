Constitution of India, Article 14 - Superannuation/age of superannuation - The petitioner worked as an employee for the State of Madhya Pradesh where the retirement age was 58, but was transferred to a corporation where retirement age was 60.
Although the petitioner retired at the age of 58, it was not deemed illegal.
The case was differentiated from SP Dubey's case where it was determined that employees transferring from a company to the Corporation could remain in service until the age of 60.
