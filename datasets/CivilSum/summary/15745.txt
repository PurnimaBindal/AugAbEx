examination - A petitioner, working as a Copy Holder in a press, requested to participate in the exam - One person holding a post of a compositor was permitted to participate in the examination - The court held that an illegal order issued in one case cannot serve as the basis for issuing a writ directing a public authority to pass an order in violation of the statutory rules.
