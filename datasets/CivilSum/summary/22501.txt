Article 311 of the Constitution of India addresses disciplinary proceedings.
The Senior Deputy Accountant General, instead of the Appellant's appointing authority, initiated proceedings for the Appellant's withdrawal of funds in an illegal manner regarding the Leave Travel Concession.
In the absence of any explicit rule, this action does not violate Article 311.
Any superior authority who can be classified as the controlling authority can initiate such proceeding.
[Para 4]
Article 311 of the Constitution of India involves reduction in rank and reversion in disciplinary proceedings.
An appointment via direct recruitment to the post of Upper Division Clerk cannot be reverted to the post of Lower Division Clerk, which is lower in rank than the post initially filled via direct recruitment.
A reversion is deemed invalid.
[Para 6] While imposing the punishment of reduction in rank, the order should have a link to the post held by the delinquent officer.
If the officer did not hold that post or was not a member of that cadre, then that officer could not be reverted to a lower cadre or a lower rank that they did not have at any stage.
