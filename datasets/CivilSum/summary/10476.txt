According to Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rules 4 and 5, military service is applicable to General Reserve Engineers Force since it is a part and parcel of the Armed Forces to which the Army Act is also applicable.
Therefore, the petitioner, who worked as Superintendent (Building and Roads) Grade-I in the service of General Reserve Engineering Force, was entitled to military service benefits.
[Paras 2-5]
