Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Selections - Filling up of vacancy - A Head master of a school petitioned that there were three vacancies in the school, but the District inspector of Schools (DIOS) did not give permission to fill up the vacancies following recruitment procedure - Completing the selection process is allowed following Recruitment Rules - The DIOS must grant prior permission to fill up the vacancies - The Managing Committee has been instructed to finish the recruitment process.
