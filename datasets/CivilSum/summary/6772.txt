Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Haryana Revenue (Group B) Services Rules, 1988 - Appointment - Post of B-Class Tehsildars - Financial Commissioner issued a memo to the irrigation department to sponsor eligible candidates, which was not circulated among the competent authorities until the day before the submission deadline for application forms.
Petitioners' ACRs were withheld during the forwarding of the record, and even after receipt of a second memo, authorities did not forward the names of other Ziledars.
Respondent No. 14 showed unusual haste in securing a copy of the order of appointment, getting himself relieved, and joining as Tehsildar on the same day.
The court held that the appointment of respondent No. 14 was arbitrary and quashed the selection and appointment.
