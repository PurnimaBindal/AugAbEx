Constitution of India, Article 16 - National Council for Teachers' Education Act (73 of 1993), Section 31 - National Council for Teachers' Education Rules, 1997, Rule 5 - Post of Head Master - A circular stating that B.Ed.
degree must be recognized by NCTE was issued.
However, the State Government had already recognized the B.Ed.
degree, and since the recognition had been granted prior to the functioning of NCTE, it could not be withdrawn retrospectively.
The circular requirement for recognition by the Council would not apply.
It is unjustifiable to deny the benefit of the degree for selection purposes.
[Paras 5 and 6]
