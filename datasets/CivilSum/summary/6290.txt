The HSEB introduced a pension scheme for transfered government servants from the Electricity Department and allowed employees to switch over to the new scheme by refunding their EPF and providing a written option.
Those retired before the scheme was introduced were not excluded and required to exercise options before retirement.
Failure to provide options cannot be used to deny the claim.
[Paras 10 and 11]
The petitioner, similarly placed to those already granted pension benefits under the scheme, cannot be discriminated against on the grounds that the scheme was not made retrospective.
[Para 11]
