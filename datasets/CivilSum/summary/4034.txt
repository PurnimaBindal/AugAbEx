Constitution of India, Article 226 - Voluntary Retirement - Withdrawal of - A petitioner chose to retire voluntarily under a scheme, but later tried to retract their decision through two letters sent under certificate of posting.
The respondent denied receiving these letters, as the address given was of Dhanbad while the stamp affixed was of Parsa Bazar Post Office.
The letters were not mentioned in the letters sent after acceptance of voluntary retirement and were seemingly created only to be filed along with the writ application.
The prayer for voluntary retirement was accepted before receiving any letter of withdrawal and there are no grounds for interference in the order accepting voluntary retirement.
[Paras 17 and 18]
