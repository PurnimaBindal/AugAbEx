Petitioners who are graduate engineers challenged the approval granted by UPSC to an amendment of recruitment rules for the post of Superintending Engineer, and the advice to review existing promotion regulations for the post of Execution Engineer (Civil) in Delhi's Municipal Corporation.
They argued that the amendment made diploma holders in Civil Engineering eligible, which is in violation of Central Government guidelines stipulating that a degree is essential for appointment to technical and scientific posts in the grade of Rs. 1100-1600.
The respondents argued that the amendment aimed to give relief to diploma holder executive engineers facing stagnation, and that diploma holders have successfully functioned as executive engineers and are eligible for promotion.
The court held that diploma holders can perform the duties of a Superintending Engineer and can be promoted to the position, and that permitting diploma holders to be eligible for the post will not decrease efficiency.
The petition was dismissed.
