Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4(1)(c)(ii), Part I and II, Schedule I - Employee injured in course of employment - The certificate of the doctor was not marked and the doctor was not examined, and consequently, there was no evidence to prove loss of earning capacity.
The Commissioner assessed loss of earing capacity at 70% and granted compensation.
Despite this, the order was held as perverse and contrary to Section 4(1)(c)(ii), and the matter was remanded to Commissioner.

Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 4(1)(c)(ii) and 30 - The appeal was deemed maintainable as a substantial question of law arises for consideration since the Commissioner had totally ignored the provisions of Section 4(1)(c)(ii) of the Act.
