The ITBT Act and Rule 27 of ITBP Rules came into force in 1992, Section 8 of the 1992 Act prohibits resignation by a member of the force, and Rule 27 refers to some special circumstances.
The petitioner cannot rely upon the said Act and Rules for claiming pension.
[Paras 4]
Rule 49(2)(b) of the 1972 ITBT Pension Rules is applicable only in the case of a government servant retiring in due course in accordance with the Rules, not resignation.
[Paras 5]
The Rule 26 of the 1972 ITBT Pension Rules is applicable in case of government servant resigning from service.
The Rule contemplates that resignation entails forfeiture of past service, and there is no question of pension.
[Paras 5]
The BSF Rules cannot be relied upon by the ITBP employees.
Rule 49(2)(b) of the 1972 ITBT Pension Rules is applicable, and Rule 19 of 1969 BSF Rules is not.
[Paras 6]
The petitioner resigned from service in 1976, filed a writ petition for pension in 2006.
It is held that the writ petition is liable to be dismissed on the ground of delay.
