Section 10 (1) (C) of the Industrial Disputes Act (XIV of 947) and Sections 102 and 103 of the Haryana Co- operative Societies Act, 1984 provide for a statutory appeal under Co-operative Societies Act.
The decision of the appeal would not operate as res judicata in proceedings under Section 10 of Industrial Disputes Act.
The ruling in (1993-1) 103 P.L.R.
424 (F.B.)
is being followed.
