Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference - The labour commissioner and Arbitrator made an award in favour of the opposition party regarding Equation Allowance, but the petitioners are challenging it.
According to a judgment by the apex Court reported in 1976 Lab IC 263, the eligibility for the equation allowance of coal trimmers referred to the Tribunal is under its jurisdiction, but making it retrospective is beyond the scope of reference.
