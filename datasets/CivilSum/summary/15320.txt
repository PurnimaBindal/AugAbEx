Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Promotion - Refusal of - Cadre has three posts that are filled using seniority as basis - The petitioner is from a reserved category - Four of the seven posts have been converted to a higher post that carries a special pay - The petitioner asserts that he should be promoted to the fourth converted position according to the reservation roster - The court rules that the petitioner cannot be promoted within his own cadre because there are no remaining posts to promote him to - The court has determined that he is ineligible for promotion to the converted post and, as a result, he cannot claim a reserved position in it based on statements made in writing.
