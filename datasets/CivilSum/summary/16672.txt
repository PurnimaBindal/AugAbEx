The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 16 and 14, outline pensionary benefits.
In this case, the petitioner was a Vice-Principal in a Government school and her pay scale was not changed during her service.
However, just before her retirement, an amendment was made to the pay scale which was deemed arbitrary, discriminatory, and in violation of Articles 14 and 16.
The authorities cannot take away the benefits accrued by the petitioner under existing rules through retrospective amendment.
