Constitution of India, Articles 15(4), 16(4) and 46(4) - Super Speciality Courses/Reservation in admissions/Education - Although the authority of the State can reserve certain fields for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, it does not have complete discretion in this matter - The highest qualification and super speciality courses do not have reservation and are based solely on merit - Selecting based on merit alone for these courses is in the national and public interest - There is no need for special provisions in the case of students pursuing the highest level of education, as they are already post-graduates in medicine - Therefore, the Medical Counsel's decision to prioritize knowledge and merit for super speciality courses is justifiable and not unconstitutional or arbitrary - The petitioner cannot demand reservation for super speciality courses.
