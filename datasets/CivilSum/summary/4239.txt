AP Agricultural University Admission (to PG Course at M.Sc.
Level) Regulations, 1989, Regulation 2 - AP University Act, 1963, Sections 21, 22 (2) and 40 - The University has the power to determine the total number of seats in any discipline or in a faculty - Academic Council of University has the necessary power and competence to determine the total number of seats in any discipline or in a faculty - The reduction of total number of seats from 80 to 72 was not illegal or ultra vires of the Statute - University acted reasonably and not arbitrarily - Judicial review of such large expert body's collective decision cannot be interfered with lightly.
AP Educational Institutions (Regulation of Admission) Order, 1974, Clause 6 - A.P.
Agricultural University Act, 1963, Section 23(2) - The University applied the reservation of 85% in favour of `Locals' subject-wise or discipline-wise and not faculty-wise - The reservation policy did not violate any legality or rule of law - The University's action to give 54% seats to the locals by adopting procedure of applying 85% of reservation discipline wise was not illegal.
