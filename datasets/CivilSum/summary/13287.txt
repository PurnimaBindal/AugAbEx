Constitution of India, Article 226 - The petitioners, who were appointed on work charge basis in the generation/transmission wing, are seeking direction to be considered for the Office of Assistant Collectors/Assistant Supervisors in the pay scale of Rs. 1640/- 3275/- - However, the court held that the petitioners have no case for grant of relief or parity in employment - The petitioners accepted the offers of employment and got themselves absorbed in various posts by appearing in various tests - They cannot now claim to have been employed against specific posts since no such post is claimed to have been lying vacant - Hence, the petition was dismissed.
