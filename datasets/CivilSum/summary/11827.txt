According to Punjab Agricultural Produce Markets Act, 1961, Section 38 and Punjab State Agricultural Marketing Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1988, Rule 4(1), the power of suspension of a Secretary of a Market Committee lies with the Board, which is represented by the Mandi Board and not the State Government.
The Government can only suspend the Board in exercise of its power under section 38 of the Act.
However, the power of suspension of an employee of the Board cannot be invoked under this provision.
Therefore, the order of suspension passed by the Government is held without jurisdiction and is liable to be quashed.
[Para 4] B.
As per the Punjab State Agricultural Markets Service (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1988, Rule 4(1), an order of suspension can only be passed during the pendency or contemplation of departmental proceedings.
In this case, there were no such proceedings pending or contemplated on the date of the suspension order.
Hence, the order of suspension is illegal.
