Constitution of India, Articles 226, 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 18(3) - A petitioner was dismissed from his service, due to a conviction order by court in an accident case, but the dismissal was not considered proper - The punishing authority failed to consider that the petitioner was already subjected to a departmental enquiry for the same incident, and two increments were already stopped - The order of dismissal was not sustainable solely because of his conviction on a criminal charge without reference to his conduct - Consequently, the order of dismissal was set aside.
[Paras 5 and 6]
