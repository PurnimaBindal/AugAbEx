Constitution of India, Article 226 - Misconduct - Criminal case - Disciplinary enquiry - Principles applied to distinguish between criminal trial and domestic enquiry are - (i) disciplinary proceedings are not a criminal trial; (ii) the aim of criminal proceedings is to protect the public while the aim of disciplinary proceedings is to maintain the purity and efficiency of public service; (iii) high standard of proof is required in criminal proceedings to convict an accused, but in disciplinary proceedings a preponderance of probability suffices to find a person guilty; (iv) unlike criminal proceedings, the strict rules of evidence and provisions of Evidence Act do not apply in disciplinary proceedings; and (v) initiating disciplinary proceedings against a civil servant acquitted in a criminal trial for the same charge is not against any law or principles of natural justice.
