Railway Establishment Code, Vol.1, Rule 158 - Constitution of India, Articles 136, 14 and 311 - Railway Cadres - A post in Production Control Organisation in Railways has been declared an ex cadre post - No negative impact on the rights of the employees has been demonstrated - If there was a good reason for differentiation in order to streamline the organisation, and it had a rational connection to the goal, an exception made cannot be considered a breach of the equality rule.
The decision to declare this post ex cadre was not unjustified.
[Paras 6, 7 and 10]
