According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16(1), appointments to Class IV posts must be made after advertising the position in a newspaper or notifying the Employment Exchange.
In the present case, appointment orders were given to the petitioners without issuing any advertisement, which is a clear violation of the Constitution's basic structure.
The State and its subordinate offices must follow the rules of advertising posts and consider all eligible applicants before making any appointment.
