Constitution of India, Article 311 - Suspension - Absence of Charge-Sheet – A Tehsildar who was promoted as District Revenue Officer has been suspended without a charge-sheet issued – The Motion Bench allowed the writ petition - The respondent counsel reported that the government has appointed an inquiry officer on 27.1.1992 - In the event that the petitioner cooperates, the inquiry proceedings would be conducted expeditiously which will be completed within four months – The court directed that the inquiry proceedings should be concluded before 26th June 1992 - Should the inquiry proceedings not be completed by the aforementioned date, and without any justifiable cause, the order of suspension shall automatically come to an end.
[Paras 1, 2 and 3]
