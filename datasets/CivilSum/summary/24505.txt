Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Reference - Delay - While there is no set formula for the limitation of making a reference under Section 10 of the ID Act, it is only acceptable within a reasonable time.
The appropriate Govt. will not entertain a belated claim and refer it.
A workman can lose their remedy under the Act if it has been a long time (around 4-5 years).
The Court must determine if the delay was justified.
It is the workman's responsibility to demonstrate that the dispute was raised within a reasonable time, and they were not responsible for the delay.
