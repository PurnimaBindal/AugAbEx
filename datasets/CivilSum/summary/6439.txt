According to the Constitution of India, Article 14, the Syndicate Bank Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976, regulation 4, and the Punjab Civil Services Rules, rule 412, minor penalties and major penalties, including stoppage of increment with cumulative effect, must be imposed with natural justice in mind.
If an employee's pay is reduced to a lower stage in the time scale of their post due to stoppage of increments, it would be considered a major penalty and cannot be imposed without proper inquiry.
It is not acceptable for an employee to be given a major penalty instead of a minor penalty under the guise of a minor penalty order.
