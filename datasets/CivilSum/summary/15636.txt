Civil Procedure Code, Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 - Interim injunction - Contract of service - The law states that a contract for service cannot be enforced specifically or prevented by injunction, with the sole remedy being compensation/damages - Plaintiff's claim for arrears of salary and continuation of monthly payments as per contract of service can only be granted if the plaintiff succeeds in the lawsuit - No prima facie case for interim injunction.
