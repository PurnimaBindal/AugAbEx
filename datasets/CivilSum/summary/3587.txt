Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 9.18(2) - Compulsory Retirement - The petitioner's entire service record was used to make the decision - Records after promotion show unsatisfactory work - Punishments of censure and stoppage of increment given on two occasions - No accusations of wrongdoing by petitioner - The impugned decision was made in the interest of the public.
[Paras 3 and 5]  B Judicial Review of Compulsory Retirement - Court interference only allowed when there is evidence of mala fides or when the decision is arbitrary.
[Para 4]  C Procedures for Compulsory Retirement - To remove an unproductive employee, the appointing authority can assess the entirety of their service record and decide whether they should be removed.
The case of 1992(2) SCT 92 (SC) is relevant in this regard.
