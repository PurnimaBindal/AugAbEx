Constitution of India, Article 311 - Constitution of Jammu & Kashmir, Section 126 - Jammu & Kashmir Police Rules, Rule 187 - Probationer - Discharge from service for the grounds of contracting a second marriage during the lifetime of her husband and leading an immoral life without reasonable opportunity to defend - As a stigmatic order, the probationer must be reinstated in service with all consequential benefits.
