Affiliation of educational institutions - Disaffiliation - An institution that is affiliated to an Education Board or University cannot be disaffiliated at the discretion of the authority concerned, unless a violation of specific conditions, Rule or order is pointed out to it and a proper opportunity is given.
The mere issuance of show-cause notice is not sufficient.
The explanation to the show-cause must be considered and dealt with, otherwise, the order of disaffiliation would not be sustainable.
[Paras 8, 9, 10 and 12]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 19 - Natural justice - Enquiry - Once an enquiry is initiated, a final order cannot be passed without waiting for the final report of the enquiry.
Orders passed without awaiting the enquiry report cannot be sustained.
[Para 11]  C Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 19 - Natural justice - Show- cause notice - A final order cannot be passed on any other ground except those taken in the show-cause notice.
