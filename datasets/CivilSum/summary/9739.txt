According to the Haryana State Policy for Regularisation dated March 7, 1996, daily wage and casual workers who meet certain conditions are entitled to regularization, including those who have completed three years of service.
Additionally, terminated workmen who have settled with their employer before the Labor Court and have been taken back with continuity of service are also entitled to regularization.
Although a plea from a casual worker was not taken before the Labor Court, the workman in question had already completed more than three years and is therefore entitled to regularization in accordance with the policy.
The award of the Labor Court is considered final as it has not been challenged.
