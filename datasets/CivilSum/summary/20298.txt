The appointment and disciplinary authority for Sub-inspectors, Assistant Sub-Inspectors, and Head Constables in Delhi Police is the Deputy Commissioner of Police, with the Additional Deputy Commissioner also being delegated the power of appointment.
The Deputy Commissioner, by force of Section 19 of the General Clauses Act, can dismiss an officer whom he is competent to appoint.

Under Article 311 of the Constitution of India, a disciplinary authority can partly agree or disagree with conclusions reached by an inquiry officer in an Enquiry Report, and its conclusions in a show-cause notice are not considered vague.
