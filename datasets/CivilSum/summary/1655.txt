Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Transfer - According to the employer, it is their decision when and where a public servant is transferred - Public institutions serve the public interest while the activities of Trade Unions perform the work of benefit and welfare of the laborers, not the public - Hence, transfer of a Union leader isn't necessarily considered an act of victimization - Courts typically don't intervene in transfer orders.
