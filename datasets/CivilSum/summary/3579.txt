The authorities have the power to examine the entire service record of an employee to determine whether their services should be discontinued.
In this case, despite no adverse entries since 2002, the decision to compulsorily retire the petitioner was valid.
[Paras 8, 9 & 12] B.
The principle behind compulsory retirement of a government servant is based on the evaluation of their entire service record in the public interest.
This decision falls within the subjective discretion of the authorities, as determined by relevant rules and legal principles.
[Para 12] C. When evaluating whether an employee should be continued in service, the authorities have the right to examine their entire service record, and this evaluation is subjective.
A court will not act as an appellate authority and substitute its view unless the decision is arbitrary, in violation of rules, or goes against established records.
