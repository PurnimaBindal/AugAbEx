The jurisdiction of the tribunal under Section 33(2)(b) of the Industrial Disputes Act is limited and cannot involve disputed questions of fact requiring parties to lead complex evidence.
[Para 5] B.
The tribunal does not have the function of an appellate court over the domestic enquiry conducted by the employer for approval.
[Para 5] C. For judicial approval to be granted, the management must show that the enquiry was conducted properly and fairly and that the employee had misconducted himself within service rules.
[Para 12] D. The limited scope of jurisdiction vested in the tribunal does not intend to oust the basic concept of judicial satisfaction and its ingredients.
The absence of appropriate pleadings and complete lack of evidence to support the pleadings can lead to the rejection of the application for its approval.
[Para 14] E. The corporation's conduct is sufficient for the tribunal to conclude that prima facie case did not exist to justify approval of the tribunal in exercise of its judicial discretion.
