Tamil Nadu Private Schools (Regulation) Act, 1974, Section 22(1) - Dismissal of teacher - The management cannot dismiss a teacher without obtaining prior approval of competent authority, even if the representation of management is pending with the competent authority.
Therefore, the proposal of dismissal is void and inoperative in the absence of approval.
[Para 8]
Tamil Nadu Private Schools (Regulation) Act, 1974, Sections 22(1), 44(4) - Appeal - To maintain an appeal before Tribunal by an aided or unaided school, the deposit of arrears of pay and allowances from the date of dismissal till the date of the order by the competent authority is a prerequisite.
[Paras 3 and 4]
