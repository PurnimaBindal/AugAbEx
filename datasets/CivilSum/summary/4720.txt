Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Disability pension - The respondent rejected the petitioner's claim on the grounds of a lapse of 20 years from the date of discharge and lack of jurisdiction of the court - The court held that disability pension is a civil right and successive cause of action for the petitioner - The petitioner had been making representations until 1994 without resolution - Cause of action arose in 1996 when the claim was finally declined - Inordinate delay cannot be considered in defeat of a citizen's rights, especially for military personnel - Objections of jurisdiction should not be raised by the Union of India or State - Respondent is directed to assess and pay disability pension to petitioner at 20% from date of discharge until payment.
