According to Section 30 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 and Order 41, Rule 19 of the Civil Procedure Code, 1908, the existence of sufficient cause is important to appeal an award of compensation.
However, in a case where notice was not filed for over 20 months, it is hard to argue that there was any cause, let alone sufficient cause, for moving application under Order 41, Rule 19, C.P.C. [Para]
