Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control & Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rules 14, 16 and 17 - Applies minor penalty of withholding of two annual increments without cumulative effect - Disciplinary Authority exempt from following rule 16 procedure - Disciplinary authority must follow every step prescribed under Rule 17 - The evidence presented follows all the safeguards provided under Rule 17 for imposing minor penalty - Appellate Authority has provided cogent and convincing reasons for dismissal after carefully considering material on the record and applying his mind - The petitioner's recommended disbursement of the loan in favor of agriculturist for installation of Diesel Pump-set was not justified; Explanation submitted by petitioner that he is not technically competent, has rightly been found to be unsatisfactory - Rule 16(9) can't be applied as no further investigation was conducted.
