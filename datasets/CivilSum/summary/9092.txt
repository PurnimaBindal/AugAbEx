The State Government has the power to appoint a Commissioner for the Corporation for a period of up to five years, and this is not a violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
A person with a lien on a Government post can be appointed as Commissioner of a Corporation, and working in both positions does not amount to holding two posts.
The Administrator of a Corporation has the power to make appointments, issue charge-sheets, and act as a disciplinary authority after the Standing Committee of a Municipal Corporation is dissolved.
Unless a suspension order is shown to be in breach of statutory powers or passed mala fide, the court should not interfere with such orders that are purely of administrative nature.
Allegations of malafide cannot be interfered with if they are vague.
