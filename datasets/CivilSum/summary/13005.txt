Constitution of India, Article 14 - UP Secondary Education Services Selection Board Act, 1982, Section 33-A(1-A) - UP Secondary Education Services Selection Board Rules, 1993, Rule 2 - Post of Principal - Senior most teacher of a college must be considered for the vacancy and cannot express a preference to be posted in any other college.
Additionally, that teacher cannot be absorbed in any vacancy in other colleges.
