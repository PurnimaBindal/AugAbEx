Constitution of India, 1950, Article 226 - Extension of service - Petitioner, a principal seeks extension of service - State award given to petitioner - Circular allows State Awardees to get 2 years extension and then on year to year basis - Extension subject to medical fitness and vigilance clearance - Petitioner retired in 1999 but reappointed in 2000 - Petitioner wants 2 years extension - Respondents say extension on year to year basis based on vigilance clearance - Petitioner's documents show financial irregularities - Court held that petitioner does not have a clean vigilance record on which extension depends - Therefore, petitioner to be superannuated immediately.
