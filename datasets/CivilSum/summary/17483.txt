Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 16 - Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rules 13 and 18 - Departmental enquiry - Revision petition - Petitioner who was a revenue Petwari, was accused of issuing a wrong domicile certificate and faced dismissal from service after undergoing a regular departmental enquiry.
Petitioner's appeal before the Commissioner Ambala Division was dismissed, and as a result, the petitioner filed a revision petition under rule 13 of Punishment and Appeal Rules.
The Commissioner withheld the revision petition, and it was not forwarded to the Financial Commissioner, Haryana.
The revision petition could only be withheld if one of the four grounds, as enumerated in rule 17, is fulfilled.
The respondents failed to refer to any provision that would allow the petitioner to be barred from approaching the State Government or the Financial Commissioner.
The impugned order was overturned, and the Financial Commissioner was directed to decide the petitioner's revision petition on its merits.
