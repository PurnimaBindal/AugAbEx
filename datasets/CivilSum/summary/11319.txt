Punjab Ayurvedic Department (Class III- Technical) Service Rules, 1963, Rule 8(1)(a) - Appointment - Regularisation - Adjustments - Adjustments made in Govt. Ayurvedic College, Patiala were not appointments as Assistant Professor but only deputation to teach the students of Under-Graduate courses - Consequently, the order to regularize the services of such Adjustees as Asst.
Professors was cancelled by the Govt. - The petitioner being an adjustee had no right to be regularised as Asst.
Professor, however, it is necessary for the Govt. to fill up posts according to the rules and consider the eligible candidates, since the temporary arrangement continued for more than 6 years and the petitioner was the only eligible person.
