Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Section 124 - J&K Civil Service Regulations (1956), Regulation 226, Proviso I - Retirement age - A government servant has the right to work until they turn 55, however, the continuation of their service can extend to 60 years if they fall within the ambit of the inferior service and were in service before 10-10-1966, according to proviso I The petitioner was an appointee of 1971 and was in service on 1-1-1987, thus, entitled to benefit from proviso II, which allows them to continue their service until they are 58 years old.
