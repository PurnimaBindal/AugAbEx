The Constitution of India, including Articles 14, 16, and 226, provides for compassionate appointment policies.
The Chandigarh Administration has instituted a policy for compassionate appointments that includes fixing a time limit for offering appointments and deleting names from the list after the expiration of that period.
Rejection of a request for compassionate appointment in accordance with these rules cannot be viewed as arbitrary or discriminatory.
The policy is designed to provide employment to dependents of a deceased employee in rare cases of great hardship due to financial crises.
The financial situation of the family can be assessed by the appointing authority, and the appointment cannot be offered after a long period of time or used to fill a post.
The deletion of an applicant's name from the list after 5 or 7 years following the death of the deceased cannot be considered unjustified.
