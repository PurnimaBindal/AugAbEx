Rajasthan Educational Subordinate Service Rules, 1971, Para V states that ad hoc appointees to a post by direct recruitment or promotion have no right to the post.
They must vacate the post for regular recruits or promotees.
The reversion or removal of ad hoc appointees in accordance with their terms of appointment to adjust the seniors or the regular appointees does not require compliance of natural justice.
[Paras 14 and 15]

Rajasthan Educational Subordinate Service Rules, 1971, Para V conforms to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
According to the seniority, promotions and no junior of the petitioner promoted is considered.
This does not discriminate with the petitioner.
[Para 17]

Rajasthan Educational Subordinate Service Rules, 1971, Para V must be followed according to Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
When vacancies are available, promotion should be made on the basis of eligibility of the candidates on the date of vacancy or consideration.
Those who attain qualifications after the date fixed cannot be considered.
The existing area of work should be maintained as far as possible if vacancies are available.
The promotion should be made as soon as possible, and ad hoc promotions should be avoided.
[Paras 19 and 21]
