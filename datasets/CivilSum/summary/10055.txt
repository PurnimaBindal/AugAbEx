[Para 10] B.
Disability pension is granted when it can be established that the disease arose during service and the conditions of duty in the armed forces determined and contributed to the onset of the disease.
If this is not the case, but the service influenced the subsequent course of the disease, it would be regarded as aggravated by the service.
Both ex-servicemen are entitled to a disability pension, but the level of severity differs.
