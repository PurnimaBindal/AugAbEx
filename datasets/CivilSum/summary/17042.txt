Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Panjab University Calendar, Vol. II, 1995 Edition, Regulation 26 - Admissions - Examination - Eligibility - The petitioner was granted admission in a three-year degree course and allowed to take the examination for the first year's course.
The university did not raise any objections at any stage.
The regulation clearly stated that candidates who passed the BA/B.Sc.
examination were eligible to appear in any additional subjects prescribed for the examination, except for the subject they already passed.
Even if the candidate did not strictly belong to the eligible category, they cannot be permitted to suffer due to the university's fault unless fraud or misrepresentation is established.
