Constitution of India, Article 226 - Public Interest Litigation - PIL - In 1985, the petitioner filed a PIL, alleging prejudicial actions by respondent No. 2, an Executive Engineer in the Irrigation Division, and seeking appropriate action be taken against him, including removal from his post.
The Court found the reliefs prayed for incapable of being granted and imposed an exemplary cost of Rs. 10,000/- on the petitioner for the misdirected litigation, which had over-stretched the Court process for 14 years.
