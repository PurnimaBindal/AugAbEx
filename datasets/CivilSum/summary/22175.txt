The court has declared that any order passed by an authority in violation of the law cannot confer rights upon any individual for similar treatment, and as such, the same mistake cannot be perpetuated on the ground of discrimination or hardship.
Additionally, the court held that an illegal or unwarranted order cannot be made the basis of issuing a writ that compels the authority to repeat the illegality or to pass another unwarranted order.
This principle is supported by 1999(2) RCR(Civil) 568 (SC), 1999(9) SCC 240 and 2007(3) SCT 703 (SC).
