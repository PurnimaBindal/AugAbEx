Constitution of India, Article 311 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(j) - Compulsory retirement - Public interest - Disciplinary actions can be taken against an officer discharging quasi-judicial functions who has exhibited doubtful integrity during the decision making process - The competent authority can decide to compel retirement or continue the officer in the public interest - The officer's retirement on reaching superannuation age will not end the disciplinary proceedings.
[Paras 6, 9 and 10]
