Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8(11) - Departmental proceedings - Order of termination - Non-compliance of provision - Effect of - The trial court rejected the order of termination because the proceedings were not adjourned for 30 days after recording ple of delinquent - However, since the delinquent was given ample opportunity to defend himself, no harm was caused - Further, the rule relied upon is not applicable, and the procedure followed by the enquiry officer was not invalid.
