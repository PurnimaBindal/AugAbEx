The Chief of Army Staff issued a show cause notice to the petitioner for termination of his service, which was confirmed by the confirming authority.
Although the finding of not guilty on the charge was declined to be confirmed, the confirm authority was empowered to confirm or refuse to confirm the finding of court martial under Rule 70.
Based on proceedings and findings, the opinion of the Chief of Army Staff was valid in issuing the show cause notice since trial by court martial is not barred by limitation, as outlined in Sections 63 and 122 of the Army Act and Army Rules, Rules 14 and 160.
The confirming authority also has the power to seek a revision of the order of sentence under Rule 160.
Therefore, the petition was dismissed.
