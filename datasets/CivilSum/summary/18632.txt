The Allahabad Bank (Employees') Pension Regulations, 1993 allow for employees to choose pension as a second Retiral Benefit if they have made contributions towards the provident fund.
One petitioner submitted their option form, but it was not accepted, unlike those of other employees.
The bank claimed that the form was not traceable and therefore not submitted, which the petitioner did not accept.
The bank had failed to provide a justifiable reason for rejecting the submitted option, and the court directed them to reconsider it.
If the option is still not traceable, the petitioner can submit another form.
