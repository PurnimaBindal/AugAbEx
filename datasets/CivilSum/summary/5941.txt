The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309, as well as Punjab Labour (Class-II) Service Rules, 1982 Rules 7 and 19, are upheld in this case.
The appointment of ex-cadre posts is ineligible under the Punjab Labour Service (Class-I and Class-II) Rules of 1955.
However, the amendment of rules with retrospective effect and conversion of these ex-cadre posts into cadre posts is not ultra vires the provisions of the Constitution of India.
Respondents No. 3 and 4 may be included in the cadre of Inspectors or Factories from when the posts were created.
Importantly, the inclusion of certain posts in the cadre of a particular service doesn't denote the initial appointment as irregular or illegal.
The administration retains the authority to change cadres, including the posts of one cadre into another in the interest of administration.
