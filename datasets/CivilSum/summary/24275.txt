Sections 2(k), 2(s), 10, 11, and 18 of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947 are discussed in this text, which pertains to the service conditions of non-workmen employees.
With regards to the jurisdiction of an industrial tribunal, it is limited to workmen only and cannot adjudicate disputes for non-workmen.
Additionally, the jurisdiction of the tribunal cannot act beyond its limits and the doctrine of estoppel will operate when it comes to non-workmen.
The service conditions of non-workmen should be estimated and considered financially, lest the award cause the company to incur financial losses.
Finally, it should be determined which class of employee a worker belongs to not on the basis of grades, but rather their duties, responsibilities, and powers as laid down in section 2(s) of the Act.
This text serves the purpose of evaluating the decisions of the industrial tribunal.
