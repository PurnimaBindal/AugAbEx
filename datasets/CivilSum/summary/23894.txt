As per the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, the Indian Police Services (Recruitment) Rules, 1954's Rules 28-A and 33 and Rajasthan Police Service Rules, 1954's Rule 9(1), promotions and seniority are subject to reservation.
Reserved candidates who are eligible for promotion to the selection scale are entitled to it by way of roster points, as per RK Sabharwal's case (1995(2) SCT 46), but general candidates who are senior in the feeder cadre but are promoted later based on roster points are to be considered senior to those reserved candidates who were junior to them.
However, those promoted before 141997 shall not be reverted but shall have their seniority in the promoted category governed by the principles enumerated under Points 1 to 3 in Ajit Singh No. 1 and Ajit Singh No. II.
For instance, a reserved candidate promoted before 141997 to Level 4 does not have to be reverted, and if the senior general candidate at Level 2 has already reached Level 3 by the reserved candidate's promotion before 141997 from Level 3, then the said general candidate must be considered senior at Level 3 to the reserved candidate.
Nevertheless, if the reserved candidate was already treated as senior at Level 3 and promoted to Level 4 before 141997, it must be rectified by following Ajit Singh No. 1 as explained in Ajit Singh No. II after 131996.
In other words, in this case, the promotion to Level 4 is reviewed, and seniority at Level 3 and Level 4 is refixed when the general candidate is promoted to Level 4.
