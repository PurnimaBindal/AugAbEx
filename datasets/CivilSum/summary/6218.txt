The Constitution of India under Articles 14 and 16 gives a constitutional right of equality before the law, which must be upheld - When a petitioner's constitutional right has been violated, relief must be granted - The pick and choose method of granting admission by way of migration does not meet the requirements of Article 14 - The petitioners, who were better suited to admission and had a pressing need to migrate, were ignored, and less qualified candidates were chosen instead - This was a clearly arbitrary action - Therefore, it is directed that admission be given to the petitioners, even if extra seats have to be created.

The Constitution of India, Article 145 clarifies that if seats are given to ineligible students, it cannot be argued that those seats were not available for eligible candidates who have a better claim on them.
