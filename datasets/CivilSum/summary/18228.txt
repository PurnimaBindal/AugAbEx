Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 532-A and Rule 326(d) & (e) of Volume I - Retirement - Compulsory pre-mature retirement - Different retirement age for Class-IV and other employees - Class-IV employees not subject to Rule 3.26(d) - Rule 5.32-A applies to all employees - Aim of the rule is to eliminate ineffective, dishonest and corrupt employees - Petitioner deemed as deadwood and incapable of work due to age and ill health - Receives salary without working - Compulsory retirement at age 55 considered legal.
