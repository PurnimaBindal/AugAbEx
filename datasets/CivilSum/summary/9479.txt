Constitution of India 1950, Article 226/227 - Admission - A petitioner appeared provisionally in the entrance test, qualified, but failed to submit their final year result within the prescribed date and even by the extended date - It was not the fault of the respondents - No relief can be granted in terms of admission to the MBA Course for the petitioner - Additionally, admission of candidates of lower merit who fulfilled the terms/conditions for admission cannot be revoked.
