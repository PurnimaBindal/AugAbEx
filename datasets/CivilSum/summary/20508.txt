Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Andhra Pradesh State and Subordinate Service Rules, Rule 33 - Inter Section seniority - Direct recruits - Promotees - The direct recruits to the post of Deputy Executive Engineers have contested the principles used to determine inter Section seniority between them and promotees.
They argue that their promotion, while exceeding the quota established in Statutory Recruitment Rules, is protected by the judgment of this Court, but that seniority is not.
However, based on an earlier decision, seniority of such promotees beyond the quota will not be disturbed in any way.
Therefore, it cannot be argued that the Court has only protected promotions and not seniority.
Special leave petitions have been dismissed accordingly.
