According to Mr. Rajesh Sharma, under the Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 36-A and 33-C(2), a fixed dearness allowance must be paid over and above the minimum wages fixed by government notification.
Failure to provide the fixed dearness allowance to workmen entitled to it can result in recovery proceedings through Section 33-C of the I.D.
Act.
Additionally, the Minimum Wages Act of 1948, Section 2(h) applies.
