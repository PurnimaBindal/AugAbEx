The appointment of a part-time Water Carrier in the Education Department was quashed due to violation of natural justice.
The petitioner was a person with 60% permanent disability and required crutches to move around.
The petitioner was convinced by some influential people in the area that the respondent-state would take care of his case.
However, the impugned order showed that the petitioner was not present before the Tribunal and was proceeded against ex-parte.
The order violated the golden rule of audi alteram partem, which incorporates the principles of natural justice.
No one should be condemned unheard, especially in a case where the penal consequences of quashing the appointment without giving the petitioner an opportunity to be heard resulted.
Therefore, the impugned order was quashed.
[Paras 2 and 3]

The order made in connection with the appointment of a part-time water carrier was not a speaking one, and it was issued without following the principles of natural justice.
The impugned order was passed in a mysterious way, without disclosing the thought process of the members who passed it.
It was a decision made without the application of the mind and was not a speaking order.
The order was quashed.
[Para 4]
