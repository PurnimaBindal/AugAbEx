According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the jurisdiction of the Tribunal is restricted to the specific points of dispute.
In a case regarding facilities and HRA, the Tribunal issued an injunction to prevent the management from interfering with the staff room/worker quarters and the workmen's peaceful enjoyment of them.
However, this order was overturned and the matter was sent back for further consideration.
