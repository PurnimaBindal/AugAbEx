Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labor Practices Act, 1971, Item 9 of Schedule IV - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(p) - Settlement between appellant-Management and respondent-Union - The Management argued that there was a mistake and ambiguity in the settlement regarding seven non-aided school employees, and they were not entitled to the settlement's benefits.
However, the settlement applied to all employees of the Management, regardless of whether they were in the aided or non-aided section.
There were no allegations of any mistakes in extending the settlement's benefits to non-aided section employees.
The Management tried to avoid the settlement by distinguishing aided and non-aided school employees, which was not mentioned in the settlement.
