Tamil Nadu State Housing Board Act, 1961 Sections 118, 119 - Charge-sheet - Challenge as to - Delay and laches - Charge memo issued after a decade of alleged irregularity - Explanation for delay in finalizing audit account cannot stand scrutiny in view of provisions as per Sections 118 and 119 of 1961 Act, accounts have to be submitted every year for annual audit - Appellant must not face departmental proceedings at this distance of time as it will be prejudicial - Protracted disciplinary enquiry against Govt. employee must be avoided in interest of employee and public - Charge-memo quashed.
