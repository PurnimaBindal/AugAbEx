Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Compassionate Appointment - Petitioner, who is the brother of a deceased employee, is seeking appointment on compassionate grounds - However, since the petitioner is already employed as a labourer on a daily wage basis and is around 35 years old with a regular income, it is apparent that he has been earning his livelihood for some time - It is also clear that his family is not currently facing any acute crisis, therefore the petitioner is not entitled to compassionate appointment.
