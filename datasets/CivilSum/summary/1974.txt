Constitution of India, Articles 14, 15(1) and 16 - Rajasthan State and Subordinate Services (Direct Recruitment by Combined Competition Examination) Rules, 1963, Rule 13 - Under the rule, Scheduled castes and Scheduled tribes are allowed cut off marks upto 5% less - This benefit does not extend to other backward classes due to a lack of provision - It is not possible to compare other backward classes with Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes - Thus, there is no violation of principles of equality.
