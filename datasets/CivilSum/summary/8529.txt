Under the provisions of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, termination of the petitioner's services while junior employees were retained constitutes retrenchment, falling outside the parameters discussed in Section 2(oo).
Per Section 25-G, the employer must give reasons for retrenchment when a junior workman is retained.
Furthermore, there can be no agreement to the contrary, and the impugned order passed 20 years ago does not necessitate the reinstatement of the petitioner.
As compensation, the petitioner is entitled to Rs. 10,000.
