Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - Petitioner transferred in the latter part of their career due to administrative exigencies - After a long legal battle with authorities, the petitioner was reinstated.
In light of the factual matrix, it is established that the transfer was based on extraneous considerations, institutional bias, and colourable exercise of power.
Therefore, malafide is satisfactorily established, and the petition is allowed.
