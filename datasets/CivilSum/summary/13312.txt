Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Reimbursement of medical bill - Petitioner's medical expenses for by-pass surgery at Apollo Hospital, Madras not reimbursed by respondent due to lack of recommendatory letter from Civil Hospital - However, Apollo Hospital was an approved hospital for the surgery and petitioner was entitled to Rs. 75,000/- as per Government resolution - Officers should take practical and justice-oriented approach in such matters - Respondents are ordered to pay petitioner Rs. 75,000/- with interest and consider sympathetically the balance amount of the bill.
