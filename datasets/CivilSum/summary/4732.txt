According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, regularisation of ad hoc and temporary service is possible by making an appointment for a fixed duration and limited work on a fixed salary with clear terms and conditions.
In such cases, services may be terminated at any time without any notice or in accordance with the conditions of appointment.
The court might provide instructions and directions to regularise their service and they could be considered along with candidates invited by open advertisement.
If an ad hoc appointee is not chosen on merit, termination of their service would not be a violation of any rule or law nor Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
An ad hoc appointee has no right to the post and must make way for regular appointees.

According to the Constitution of India, Article 14, an ad hoc or contractual appointment can be terminated in accordance with the terms and conditions of appointment.
Therefore, a petitioner can have no legitimate grievance.
[Para 10] C. According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, when challenging the termination of service, necessary parties (regularly selected persons) must be impleaded in the petition, or the petition will not be maintainable.
[Para 10] D. According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, availability of posts is not sufficient grounds for claiming an appointment in Government Departments or its statutory bodies.
The competent authority has the discretion to fill up or not fill up such posts, and appointing individuals to the service in cases of non-availability of work is against public interest.
