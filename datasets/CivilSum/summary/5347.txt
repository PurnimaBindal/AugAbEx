Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Retrenchment - The Petitioner, a Public Trust, terminated the employment of Respondent, who was responsible for construction and repair work, after he completed 240 days of continuous service.
Respondent claimed that the Petitioner did not follow Section 25-F of the Act, while the Petitioner argued that the Respondent had been absent from work.
However, the Labour Court ruled that the termination was illegal and awarded compensation instead of reinstatement, along with full back wages.
It is correct that the Labour Court reached a fair and lawful conclusion, and the petition is therefore dismissed.
