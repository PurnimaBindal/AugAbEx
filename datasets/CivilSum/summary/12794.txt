Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Selection - Merit list - The petitioner was appointed to a reserved category post as Naib-Tehsildar based on merit list selection, but candidate with higher merit was left out - Petitioner's service was intended to be terminated by show cause notice due to representation made by the higher merit candidate - However, the High Court stayed the termination and the petitioner continued in service from 1984 - During a final hearing in 1997, it was ruled that it would be unfair to terminate the petitioner's services after such a long time.
