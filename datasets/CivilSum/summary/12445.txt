In accordance with Article 21 and 226 of the Constitution of India, medical reimbursement requires prior approval and sanction.
However, if the patient's life is at risk, treatment expenses cannot be dismissed for lack of approval.
[Para 3] B.
When assessing expenses for medical reimbursement, bills of treatment expenses should not be ignored.
Additionally, opinions from the Medical Board should be based on material evidence and cannot be the sole basis for assessment of expenditures.
