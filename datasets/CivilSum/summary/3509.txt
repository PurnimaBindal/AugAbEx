Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995 (1 of 1996), Section 47 - Assistant Steward in Hotel - Applicability of Act - Medical disablement due to accident - The petitioner's claim cannot be entertained under the 1995 Act as the employer is not a government or government aided establishment but private.
The termination of service on medical ground is valid under Section 2(oo) of the 1947 Act and does not amount to retrenchment.
