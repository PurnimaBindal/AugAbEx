Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate appointment - The petitioner claims appointment as a Conductor, but was rejected due to not meeting the required height set by the rules of APSRTC.
The petitioner argues that the Indian standard height for females is significantly less than that of males.
An SLP has been filed against the HC's judgment, pending in the SC, with no interim orders granted.
The petitioner has been denied appointment on any other post due to a supposed ban on recruitment, though no official ban orders have been produced.
Regardless, a supernumerary post could have been created by the appointing authority.
The respondent-Corporation has been directed to consider the petitioner for appointment on a position such as a clerk, sweeper, attender, or any suitable post, taking into account their educational qualifications.
[Paras 3 and 4]
