Border Security Force (Subordinate Officers and Under Officers) Promotion and Seniority Rules, 1975, Rule - Border Security Force General Duty Cadre (Non-Gazetted) Recruitment Rules, 2002, Rule 2 - The 2002 Rules apply to all existing and subsequent vacancies, including promotion to the post of Sub-Inspector, with eligibility requiring 18 years of regular service and 5 years of regular service as Head Constable.
Once a candidate becomes a Head Constable, their lower post qualifications are no longer relevant, and all Head Constables are subject to the same eligibility conditions of the 2002 Rules.
If a petitioner does not meet these conditions, their promotion can be withheld in accordance with Rule 5.
