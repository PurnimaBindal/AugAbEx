MP Civil Services (CCA) Rules, 1966, Rules 2(d), 9 - Power of suspension - The appointing authority or any subordinate authority that has been conferred with powers of disciplinary authority can suspend an employee - Since the power of the Governor has been delegated under Rules to take appropriate action including the power of suspension of Naib Tehsildar, the suspension by the Commissioner was considered to be proper and valid.
