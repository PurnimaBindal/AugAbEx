The cancellation of the petitioner's registration as a research scholar for PhD in the History Department, Pbi, University based on allegations of involvement in a serious crime violates Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226.
While a Committee was constituted to hold enquiry against the petitioner and submitted a report with a recommendation for punishment, the copy of the report was not furnished to the petitioner before his registration was cancelled.
As a result, the impugned order is being set aside and respondents are directed to furnish the report of the Committee to the petitioner first before proceeding.
