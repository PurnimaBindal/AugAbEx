The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 226 and Rule 19(1) of the Border Security Force Rules, 1969 discuss pre-mature voluntary retirement and re-induction into service.
Those who do not return to service cannot seek equity, and they are responsible for their own situation, thus receiving no relief from Writ Court.

Rule 19(1) of the Border Security Force Rules, 1969 discusses pre-mature voluntary retirement and pension.
According to the BSF Rules, if there is no eligibility for pension under CCS Rules, Rule 19 does not provide a right to pension.
