Central Civil Services (CCA) Rules, Rules 11 and 27 - Dismissal from service - The Appellate Authority set aside the dismissal order and declined to refer the case back to the punishing authority for review - The punishing authority lacks the jurisdiction to reopen the matters brought up in the charge-sheet - Any subsequent dismissal order from the punishing authority would be invalid and cannot be justified.
