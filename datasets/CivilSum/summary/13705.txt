Constitution of India, Article 226 and 12 - Punjab Co-operatives Societies Act, Section 8 - "Other authority" envisaged by Article 12 - Writ petition against Co-operative Society for violation of any principle of natural justice etc. is not maintainable as Co-operative Society, having come into existence Co-operative Societies Act is not instrumentality of State - Tests to determine whether Co-operative Society is a State or other authority - Mere holding of some shares by State in ordinary business venture would not convert Co-operative Society running such venture into instrumentality of State or other authority within meaning of Article 12.
