Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Speaking order - Administrative authorities must issue well-reasoned judicial or quasi-judicial orders and ensure that the decision-making process is free from external influences.
[Para 6] B.
Constitution of India, Article 14 and 311 - Natural justice - Documents - Preliminary enquiry - In cases of theft charges, the disciplinary committee must disclose all the relevant material and provide the petitioner with access to documents and statements of witnesses recorded behind his back, along with names and addresses of witnesses.
Failure to do so may invalidate proceedings and violate the petitioner's rights under Art. 311 of the Constitution.
[Para 9] C Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Dismissal from service - Stigmatic order - When dismissing an employee for theft, the employer must issue a show cause notice and offer the employee an opportunity to be heard, failing which the order may carry the consequences of debarring the employee from future employment, and thus affect his or her civil rights.
