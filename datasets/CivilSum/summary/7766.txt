Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Gallantry/Police Medal - The Medal is awarded to individuals who have displayed extraordinary courage and expertise or demonstrated noteworthy dedication to duty - It is not mandatory that all members of the raiding team receive a Medal - There is no evidence on record to suggest that the petitioner exhibited the same bravery and skill as those who actually received Gallantry Medals - The decision taken by the High Powered Committee was lawful.
