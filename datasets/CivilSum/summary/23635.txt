Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Qualifications - If the same qualifications are prescribed for two posts, the person already employed must be deemed to have had the necessary qualifications for the other post too, and it must be assumed that the appointing authority was convinced that the individual already appointed possessed the required qualifications for the other role.
Qualifications for the posts of Associate Professor and Professor in Neurosurgery are identical.
Once the appointment as Associate Professor is considered valid and unchallenged, it cannot be argued that the person appointed as Associate Professor was not eligible for the position of Professor.

B Indian Medical Council Act, Section 33 - The Medical Council of India's recommendations made to the Government in 1982 and 1989 have yet to be approved by the Central Government.
The recommendations of the Medical Council, even though they become regulations established under Section 33 of the Indian Medical Council Act, are merely advisory in nature and do not have legal force.
It would be at the discretion of the appointing authority to either adhere to or ignore the suggestions that have not received approval.
It would not be improper for the appointing authority to also follow such suggestions, if they are found to be appropriate.
