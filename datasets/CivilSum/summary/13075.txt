According to Rule 31(1) of the Jammu and Kashmir Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, only the appointing authority or an authority empowered by the government has the power to suspend an employee.
A challenge was made on the grounds that the Divisional Manager lacked both competency and authorization when suspending the petitioner.
It was held that the suspension of the petitioners was ordered by an incompetent authority in contravention of rules, rendering it without jurisdiction.
Therefore, the suspension is not sustainable.
