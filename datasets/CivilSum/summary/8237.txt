Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dismissal - Unauthorised absence - The second respondent was absent without leave for around six months, and charges levelled against him in a domestic inquiry were found to be true.
The Labour Court found the domestic inquiry to be fair and proper, but it only ordered reinstatement without back-wages on sympathetic grounds.
It was held that the second respondent did not deserve lenient treatment while awarding punishment, and the Labour Court exercised its discretion in an improper and capricious manner.
The award of the Labour Court was quashed.
