The State Bank of Travancore (Officers') Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 17 specifies disciplinary action, suspension and punishment for misconduct.
A respondent was found guilty of misconduct following a departmental proceeding that resulted in his suspension.
The punishment of stoppage of three increments was imposed while directing his reinstatement, which has become final.
In accordance with the policy circular and Regulation 17, promotion shall be in accordance with the policy laid down.
Therefore, there was no illegality committed in not considering the respondent for promotion due to the policy, which states that a person who has been brought down to a lower stage in the time scale of a pay, which is a major punishment, is not eligible for promotion until he reaches back his initial stage.
Notably, the respondent was mistakenly called for promotion interview and, therefore, did not have the right to be considered for promotion.
Moreover, there is no requirement for notice to be issued before limiting payment due to the subsistence allowance during the suspension period.
