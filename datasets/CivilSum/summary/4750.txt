Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Punjab Transport Department (State Service Class-I) Rules, 1963, Rule 6 - Appointment by way of promotion - The petitioner was denied promotion to Joint Provincial Transport Controller on the ground of a pending vigilance enquiry against him.
However, the court held that this sole ground was contrary to law and directed the respondents to consider his case for promotion when the post became available.
The eligibility criteria must be the basis for consideration for promotion to the next higher post.
[Paras 5 to 8]
