Admissions - Education - Senior School Certificate Examination from the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) - Qualifications for admission, Clause A - To be admitted, a candidate must have passed 10+2 with 50% aggregate marks and at least five of the six compulsory subjects, as the optional subject will not be considered for results or admission purposes.
Institutions must not wrongly interpret the clause as requiring a pass in all subjects, and the Division Bench of Pb.
& Hy.
High Court ruling in Kusum Lata v. State of Hy.
2002(2) SCT 457 is overly technical and wrong.
