The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Contract Act, 1872, Section 23 - Termination of Contract - Employment Agreement between the Government and employee- The Government has the power to freely terminate an employee's service- The Agreement was signed between unequal parties; the powerful State and a poor man seeking a meager position - The Agreement violates Articles 14 and 21 of the Constitution of India and opposes public policy, therefore, rendering it void under Section 23 of the Contract Act.
