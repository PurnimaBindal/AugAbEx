Army Act, 1950, Section 191 - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 13(3) - Medical re-examination during training - Discharge from Army service due to being declared medically unfit - Challenge to this decision - Petitioner's flat foot was discovered on medical re-examination supported by X-Ray and specialist advice, resulting in permanent disability - Respondents carried out a second medical examination in the interest of the Armed Forces, and there is no indication of bias - The Court cannot replace the decision of the Medical Board as it is conclusive.
