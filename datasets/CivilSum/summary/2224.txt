Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Judicial Review - Post of Additional Deputy Inspector General - Promotion is based purely on merit - The court cannot exercise power of judicial review in promotion if there are specific rules in place and the competent authority follows them - The court cannot modify ACR appraisals either - Downgrading ACRs not required to be communicated to employee if remarks were not adverse - If employee was not selected based on comparative merit and overall profile of the post, then there will be no interference.
