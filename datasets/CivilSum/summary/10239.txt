Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 309 - Retiral Benefit - Recovery - The Respondents ordered that excess salary be recovered from the Petitioner due to late passing of departmental exam and wrong fixation of pay to avoid anomaly with junior whose pay was later reverted - Recovery for wrongful grant of increment to employee without misrepresentation from his side is not permissible - However, recovery is permissible when the basis of grant of higher pay scale has since vanished.
