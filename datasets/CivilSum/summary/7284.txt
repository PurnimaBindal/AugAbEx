According to Section 2(Z) of the U.P.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Labour Court does not have jurisdiction to entertain disputes related to the dismissal of an Assistant Foreman who is supervising work but is not considered a workman.
The Award of Labour Court was held bad in law.
[Para 6]

As per Item 6 of Schedule 2 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, disciplinary proceedings for misconduct resulting in dismissal and reinstatement can be initiated.
Even if criminal proceedings are initiated, that would not make the findings of the disciplinary authority unsustainable.
Thus, reinstatement with backwages directed by Labour Court was not proper in the case of an employee acquitted by appellate Court for theft, based on the benefit of the doubt given.
[Paras 7, 8 and 10]
