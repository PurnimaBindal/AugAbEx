Promotion is not a fundamental right under Service Law, but being considered for promotion is.
[Para 7]
According to Article 309 of the Constitution of India, the right to promotion can only be limited by valid rules, which cannot be interpreted to restrict the right more than what is allowed by the law.
[Para 8]
In a case involving a "Government Servant under cloud", a Departmental Promotion Committee recommended the respondent for promotion, with no pending vigilance enquiry or decision to initiate departmental proceedings against them.
Although a charge sheet was later issued on 24.9.1999, it was concluded that the respondent could have been promoted between 1411999 and 27.8.1999.
