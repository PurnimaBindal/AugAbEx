According to the Punjab Agricultural University Admission Brochure for 1999, Para 7-A, admission to the B.Ed.
Course involves an interview process and candidates are called three times the number of available seats.
However, this rule does not apply to B.Sc.
Agriculture.
In this case, 25 candidates were called for the 25 available seats and there were no irregularities.
The University was not obligated to call candidates three times.
The petitioner was granted provisional admission under court orders due to available seats from some non-joining candidates.
Since she completed the course, it is in the interest of justice to allow her admission to be regularized and result declared after completing all formalities.
[Paras 8 to 13]
