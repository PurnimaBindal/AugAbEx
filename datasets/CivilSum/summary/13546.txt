Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Probation - Confirmation - According to the Rules, promotion to a higher grade on probation may last for up to six months - The period of promotion begins on the day of assuming the responsibilities of the higher post in the higher pay scale.
An extension order of probation must be passed within one month of the expiration of the six-month period.
After this, there must be a specific order of confirmation and a plea of automatic confirmation will be rejected.
