According to Articles 14, 16 and 45 of the Constitution of India, Aided School Teachers have the right to receive equal pay for equal work and cannot be deprived of their salary in regular pay scales by withholding the grant in aid for a specific class.
Additionally, the government has a constitutional obligation to provide grants in aid to recognized affiliated schools and their teachers must receive the same salary as teachers in government schools.
As a result, the Chandigarh Administration has been instructed to release grants in accordance with the grant in aid policy.
