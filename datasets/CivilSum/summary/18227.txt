According to Section 39 of the Punjab Municipal Act, 1911 and Rule 3 of the Punjab Municipal (Executive Officers) Rules, 1976, the Executive Officer does not have the power to suspend non-provincialized employees, as this authority lies with the Municipal Council or Committee.
Rule 4 of the Punjab Civil Service (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, is also not applicable in this matter.
Therefore, Rules 3(1) of the 1976 Rules and Rule 4 of the 1970 Rules are irrelevant when it comes to the suspension of such employees.
