The petitioner was denied regularisation with effect from 3131993 as his service had been terminated at the relevant time.
However, it was decided that once an employee is reinstated in service with continuity, he is considered to be on duty for all intents and purposes, and benefits accruing on the basis of deemed reinstatement have to be granted as if he was actually on duty from the date when his services had been illegally terminated.
Hence, the petitioner is entitled to regularisation with effect from 3131993.
