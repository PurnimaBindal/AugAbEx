Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Promotion by selection - Determining availability of vacancies - Authorities are required to take existing and anticipated vacancies into account when determining availability.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Short-listing of eligible candidates - Violation of policy/rule - Including excess candidates in the selection process is a violation that renders the selection process invalid.
[Para 16]  C Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Reservation - Dereservation - Prior approval for dereservation - Prior approval from the appropriate Government is mandatory for dereservation, and failure to obtain approval violates the rights of reserved categories.
[Para 17]  D. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection - Alternate remedy - Option to take recourse to alternate remedy - When an alternate remedy is provided, the concerned person has the option to choose which recourse to take.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Selection - Territorial jurisdiction of High Court - Bank employees - Territorial jurisdiction of J&K High Court - Bank employees who participated in the selection process in J&K and were promoted there are subject to the territorial jurisdiction of the J&K High Court, and it is incorrect to suggest otherwise.
