According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, the terms of absorption offer and option must be followed for deputation/absorption.
The petitioner was entitled to be absorbed on the post of equivalent status in the absorbing department, where he was working on deputation, as per the terms of option for absorption.
Equivalence was to be given with the substantive rank held by him in his parent department, where he was a Havildar in Indo-Tibetan Border Police.
However, his officiating rank as JIO Grade-I cannot be considered for absorption on substantive post equivalent to the rank held by him in his parent department.
Despite this, his absorption in the rank of JIO Grade-II was upheld.
Also, for the purpose of seniority, the service rendered by him in his parent department on the equivalent post has to be counted.
