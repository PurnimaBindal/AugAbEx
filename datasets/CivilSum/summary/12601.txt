Haryana Municipal Act, 1973, Sections 38 and 257 - Haryana Municipal Services (Integration, Recruitment and Conditions of Services) Rules, 1982 - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 - Gratuity - Retirement of Municipal Employees - Upon retirement, Municipal Committee employees have the right to receive gratuity payment.

Haryana Municipal Services (Integration, Recruitment and Conditions of Services) Rules, 1982, Rules 8, 15, 18 and 19 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume 1, Part 1 - Pension - Eligibility of Municipal Employees - A municipal employee who is not paid through the Consolidated Fund of the state and has benefits of Contributory Provident Fund, is not eligible for pension.
