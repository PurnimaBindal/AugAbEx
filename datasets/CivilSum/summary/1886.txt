According to the Punjab Civil Services Rules (as applicable to Haryana) Vol. 1, Rule 44, one increment of higher responsibility is required for promoting JBT teachers as Head teachers.
The promotees must receive this increment.
[Para 2] B The Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Sections 2(00)(bb), 25F mention that termination of services of contractual employees without paying retrenchment compensation is an unfair labor practice.
The Petitioners appointed on fixed term and salary against vacant posts and whose services were terminated after more than 3 years of work without paying any compensation are allowed to seek regularization as per the policy.
[Paras 8 and 13] The Respondent University is not disputing the fact that the Petitioners worked for more than 240 days in a calendar year.
Hence, there is no alternative remedy required, and petitions are allowed.
