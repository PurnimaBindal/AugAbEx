Pension Rules, 1996, Rule 2 - Family pension - An Agricultural Marketing Board employee is entitled to family pension under the State Government's pension rules, which include parents in the definition of family.
The mother of a deceased employee, who was included in the definition of family before the employee's death, is entitled to family pension.
