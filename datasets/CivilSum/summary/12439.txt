Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - J&K Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 17(d) and the J&K Civil Services (Verification of Character and Antecedents) Instructions of 1969 - Appointment - Character verification - In order to be eligible for appointment, an individual's character must be verified and the appointing authority cannot exercise this power arbitrarily.
The authority must make a decision on the basis of available material and declare the individual unsuitable and disqualified for the post in service.
The authority is also obligated to provide an opportunity to the appointee and cannot sit silently on vague information.
However, an appointing authority was directed to appoint the petitioner retrospectively with all consequential benefits after the information collected by the authority was found incorrect and vague.
[Paras 10 and 11]
