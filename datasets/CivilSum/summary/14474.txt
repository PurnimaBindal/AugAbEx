Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226/227 - Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rule 4 - Benefit of military service for purpose of seniority, increments etc. - The benefit of military service cannot be denied to employees who have joined the armed forces prior to the proclamation of emergency - This benefit has to be given to employees in the State of Punjab irrespective of their date of joining - A petitioner who rendered military service during the period of emergency is entitled to the benefit of his service towards his seniority and increments.
