Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions Act, 1989, Section 2(p) & (v) - Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions (Recognition, Grant in aid and Service Conditions) Rules, 1993, Rule 1 - Applicability of estoppel on educational institutions deemed as university - A contention was raised regarding Institution receiving aid and stating that it's not a Non-Government Institution - It was held that since Institution is a university and not covered within the definition of Non-Government Institution, the doctrine of estoppel cannot be applied.
[Para 25] B Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions Act, 1989, Section 2(p) & (v) - Educational Institutions - Jurisdiction of Tribunal - Institutional jurisdiction not applicable for the Tribunal, since Institution is not considered as a Non-Government Educational Institution under the Act provided in 2(p) of the Act.
