Under Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 162, a Police Constable can be dismissed for repeated absence from duty, even if treated as leave - the punishing authority's order is only open to interference if it is proved that the charge was not proved or the Order was vitiated by malafides or arbitrariness.
Therefore, the order of dismissal cannot be substituted with a lesser punishment by the Civil Court.
