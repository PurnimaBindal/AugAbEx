The seniority of employees should be counted from the date of their regular appointment, and not from the date of any stop-gap appointment, according to the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226/227.
Ad hoc appointment/initial appointment was made only as a stop-gap arrangement, and regular appointment was to be made by Punjab Public Service Commission.
Officiation in such post cannot be taken into account for considering seniority, and therefore the petitioners are not entitled to count their ad hoc service for purposes of seniority [Paras 5 and 6].
B.
The duties of Drawing and Disbursing Officers were added to the petitioner's usual duties, as it was part of their duty at Headquarters to act as such officers.
Since the pay scales of Homoeopathic Physicians at Headquarters and other places were the same, it was not necessary for only the senior-most physicians to be posted at Headquarters.
The petitioners are not entitled to additional or higher pay scales [Para 9].
