Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Natural justice - Reversion - Conditional promotion in the absence of availability of senior subject to decision of his case pending in the Court - Review of promotion after decision of case of seniority in favour of the senior employee - There is no role for rules of natural justice in such cases - If only one conclusion is possible, it would be a waste of time for the authority to pass fresh orders after complying with rules of natural justice - Courts do not grant pointless writs.
