Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 103 - Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, Section 2 - Maternity leave - The denial of the Maternity benefit to employees on consolidated salary is against the equal rights for all employees and the betterment of status of women, even if they are on muster roll or daily wage basis.
In reference to Rule 103 of RSR, Article 42 of the Constitution of India, Section 2 of the 1961 Act and Govt. order dated 2521955 and the decision of the Apex Court in the case of Female Workers (Muster-Roll) and Anr. (2000 LIC 1033), entitled to maternity benefit.
Therefore, the order is quashed and the benefits must be given within 30 days.
