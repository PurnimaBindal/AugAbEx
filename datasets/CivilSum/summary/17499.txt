According to Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules 1975, Rule 3, Treasury Officers can be subjected to premature retirement, but only if their service record shows a lack of efficiency.
In this case, the petitioner received good and very good reports throughout their tenure, except for a few average ones.
The petitioner was also singled out for criticism with regards to family planning and saving schemes, which cannot be solely attributed to their work efficiency.
The petitioner earned various commendations and appreciations during their time as Treasury Officer, making the order of premature compulsory retirement with immediate effect in public interest seem arbitrary.
Therefore, the petition was allowed with costs.
