According to Articles 16 and 226 of the Indian Constitution, Haryana Transport Department (State Service Class III) Rules 1988 (Draft Rules) Rules 7 & 9 - Promotion - The eligibility of the post of Store Purchase Assistant requires careful consideration.
The appointment of Respondent No. 2 on the transfer from the post of Junior Auditor was not eligible to be the Store Purchase Assistant, resulting in a violation of the rights of petitioners to be considered for appointment by direct recruitment/promotion.
The order of appointing Respondent No. 2 as Store Purchase Assistant by transfer nullified, and Respondent No. 1 has been instructed to fill the post of Store Purchase Assistant in compliance with Draft Rules within two months.
If Draft Rules are finalised in the meantime, the position will be filled in conformity with notified rules.
[Paras 10, 11 & 13]
