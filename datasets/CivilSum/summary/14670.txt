Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) Rules, 1930, Rule 5 - Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Eligibility - Appointment - Age limit - The petitioner, a Tehsildar, challenged the Government's notification setting a maximum age limit of 45 years for candidates applying for appointment to H.C.S.
- The statutory rules state that candidates above the age of 45 years are normally ineligible for the position - The notification is in line with the statutory rules, and it cannot be said that the Government violated any legislative policy in making the decision - The prescribed age limit is not grossly unfair or arbitrarily discriminatory - The petition is dismissed.

Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) Rules, 1930, Rule 5 - Appointment - Age limit - Eligibility - The executive has the authority to decide on eligibility criteria - Courts should not interfere in this prerogative unless the decision is shown to be arbitrary or an abuse of power - The petition is dismissed.
