Sections 25-F, 25-G and 25-H of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, along with Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution, dictate that a workman can only be terminated within certain provisions.
In the event of contravention of these provisions, a reinstatement award can be issued by the Labour Court.
However, in a case where a workman has been appointed de hors rules of appointment, the award may not be sustainable, as per case law including Mahaboob Deepak's case and various decisions by High Courts.
