Constitution of India, Articles 14, 300-A and 226 - Pension Scheme - Cut-off date - Pension scheme introduced with a cut-off date extending benefits to those who retired on or after 181991 - Petitioners retired prior to 181991 - They received benefit under CPF Scheme - Pension and PF retirees are not identical - Court ruled 181991 as cut-off date is not discriminatory.
