Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Fixation of seniority in accordance with judgment of Supreme Court - Natural justice - A competent authority is not obligated to grant an opportunity for personal hearing when seniority is fixed or refixed based on a Supreme Court judgment, and the principles of natural justice cannot be applied if setting aside such an order would restore an illegal order - The Court has the power under Articles 32 and 226 to refuse to strike down such an order.
