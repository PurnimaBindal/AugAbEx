Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Criminal trial - A bank employee had charges of misappropriation leveled at them, leading to the initiation of both criminal and departmental proceedings.
The criminal case has been pending for four years, but this does not mean the departmental proceedings can be delayed indefinitely.
Both can be conducted at the same time.
[Para 6]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Departmental proceedings - Criminal case - When a departmental case and a criminal case are grounded in the same facts or evidence, departmental proceedings cannot be stalled unnecessarily.
Even if they are temporarily stayed due to pending criminal proceedings, the departmental proceedings can continue if the criminal trial is not concluded quickly.
[Para 5]
