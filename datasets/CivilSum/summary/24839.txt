The Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 7(2) and 8, do not provide for the payment of interest when it is quantified before the Collector is approached for payment.
However, Act 22 of 1987 added sub-section (3A) to Section 7 to address this gap in the law.
It is important to note that this provision only applies prospectively.
