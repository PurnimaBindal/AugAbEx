Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Reinstatement/Promotion - Despite being eligible for promotion, the petitioner was passed over in favor of colleagues who did not have departmental enquiries pending against them.
This resulted in the petitioner's termination.
The High Court overturned the termination order in a writ petition and directed that the petitioner's case for promotion be considered on the same date as his juniors.
If found fit, the petitioner is entitled to be promoted.
However, if a test is required, the employee must pass it before being considered for promotion.
[Paras 8 and 9]
