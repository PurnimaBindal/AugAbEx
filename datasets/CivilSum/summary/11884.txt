Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Natural justice/Termination of services/Selection - Employers can initiate proceedings to terminate the services of an appointee if a violation of the rules is discovered after their selection and regular appointment.
However, if there is a report against the selection of appointees containing adverse material affecting their interest, a copy of that report must be furnished to them.
In compliance with the rule of natural justice, they must also be provided with a hearing.
An order of termination made without giving an opportunity is illegal and liable to be quashed.
