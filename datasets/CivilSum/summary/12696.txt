Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Counting of past service - An employee who has rendered past service in an institution aided by the State Government is entitled to the benefit of past service.

Constitution of India, Article 227 - Administrative Tribunals 1985, Sections 19, 21 - Application beyond limitation - The Tribunal may condone delay if there is sufficient reason for not approaching within the stipulated period.
The decision of the Tribunal will not be interfered with if the finding of fact is that there is sufficient reason.
