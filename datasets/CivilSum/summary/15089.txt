The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, requires natural justice during an enquiry.
The petitioner was appointed as an external examiner of Semester examination but was given notice for the charge of dereliction of duty.
The petitioner gave a prompt explanation, and the lapses were mainly due to oversight.
However, the University permanently debarred her services without proper enquiry and without affording sufficient opportunity to the petitioner to have her say in the matter.
The order is therefore vitiated for violation of principles of natural justice.
[Paras 2, 6 and 7]
