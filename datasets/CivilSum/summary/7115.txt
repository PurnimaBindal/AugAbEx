Bombay Industrial Relations Act, 1946, Sections 79(3) and 42(4) - Bombay Industrial Relations Rules, 1947, Rule 53 - Reinstatement - Back wages - The petitioner was not allowed to join duty after a strike, but no order of dismissal was passed nor any show cause notice given.
The period of limitation for filing the dispute would be governed by Section 79(3)(b) of the Bombay Industrial Relations Act and not by Rule 53 of 1947 of the Bombay Industrial Relations Rules.
The dismissal of the application on the ground of limitation is not justified.
Due to the delay on the part of the petitioner in submitting an approach notice, the Labour Court held that it was justified in depriving him of part of the back wages.
