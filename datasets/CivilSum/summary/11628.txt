Rajasthan (Recruitment of Dependents of Government Servants Dying while in Service) Rules, 1975, Section 2(f) states that for compassionate Appointment, the dependent must be a member of the deceased government servant's family and not the counterpart's family.

Despite the Constitution of India, Article 226 that permits compassionate appointment, the court dismissed the petition because it was filed after a gross delay of 19 years since the employee's death in 1978.
The widow's application was only made in 1992 to appoint her son-in-law as a dependent of the deceased employee, and despite finally receiving a response in 1995 denying the appointment, the petition was filed in 1997.
