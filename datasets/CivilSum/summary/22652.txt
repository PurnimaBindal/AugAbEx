It is important to note that a witness giving false statements in proceedings before a Magistrate may be punished directly by the Magistrate under Section 344 of the Criminal Procedure Code.
The Magistrate has the power to take this course of action instead of sending a complaint against the offender under Section 340 of the same code.
In the event that this action is taken, a summary procedure of trial will be followed.
In a case where the prosecutrix alleged rape by two accused persons, the Magistrate found her guilty of giving false evidence and sentenced her to 3 months of simple imprisonment under Section 344 of the Criminal Procedure Code.
This section provides an alternative remedy available to Magistrates.
