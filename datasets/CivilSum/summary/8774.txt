Constitution of India Articles 14 162 and 309 - Appointments - Regularisation of ad hoc employees - Ad hoc appointments - The state has the authority to create and fill civil posts according to executive instructions within the constraints of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
However, appointments must follow statutory rules once they have been established.
There is no express power to relax recruitment rules, and no provision in the rules for ad hoc appointments.
The power to relax age requirements cannot be used to relax other eligibility conditions.
Ad hoc appointments made to meet temporary needs must be replaced by direct recruitment as soon as possible.
However, the power to relax conditions cannot be used to make ad hoc appointments and subsequently regularize them.
Such appointments made without following recruitment rules cannot be regularized or deemed to be regularized after working for a particular time period without any breaks.
At most, such appointees may be considered favorably during future recruitment processes in accordance with the rules.
[Paras 10 to 12]
