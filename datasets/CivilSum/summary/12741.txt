Civil Procedure Code, 1908 - Suit for Injunction - Petitioner was not admitted into MA Previous (Economics) due to being ineligible and not on the merit list in the qualifying exam - Despite less meritorious candidates being admitted, the petitioner was refused admission - The petitioner did not turn up on the day the three seats became vacant - Notice regarding vacant seats was put up on the notice board - It was up to the college authorities to inform the next candidate in order of merit if a seat had fallen vacant - The college did not need to put up a notice on the board asking for candidates to apply against the vacant seats - The candidate originally selected higher in the order of merit should have been given admission - The court directed the college to provisionally admit the petitioner without displacing the respondents until the suit is finally settled.
[Paras 11, 14 and 15]
