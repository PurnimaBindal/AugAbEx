Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 133 - Practice and Procedure - A writ petition challenging the appointment of Advocate General - The objections as to the maintainability of the writ were upheld - The observations relating to accusations made in the writ petition as to the character of the appellant were deemed unwarranted and unnecessary - These observations made in para 18 of the order made by Single Judge (AIR 1995 Madras 78) that were made without giving the appellant an opportunity to show cause against them should be ignored and treated as expunged.
