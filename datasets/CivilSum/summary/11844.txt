Limitation Act, Article 113 - Punishment order - Void order - A null and void order, even if illegal, remains in effect until a competent court declares it to be so - A declaration must be sought within the limitation period by filing a suit - A suit for challenging a punishment order is not covered by a specific Article under the Limitation Act, so Article 113 applies and the limitation period for such a suit is three years - Relief cannot be granted after the three-year limitation period.
