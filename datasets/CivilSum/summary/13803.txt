Central Board of School Education (Examination) Bye-laws, Bye-law 75(i)(b) - Non-releasing of class XII mark-sheets - Ground of illegal admission - According to Bye-law 75, admission to class XII can only be granted to students who completed a regular course of study for class XI and passed it from a Board-affiliated school.
The petitioners failed their Class XI examination but were still admitted to Class XII after passing their Class XI exam in a new school.
However, the internal exam conducted by the respondent school is seen as an attempt to bypass the Bye-law 75 rule as the petitioners were not students of the said school in Class XI.
They should have been aware of the Bye-laws which made them pay a handsome fee for admission.
Thus, the Board's action is not seen as arbitrary, unreasonable or discriminatory.
