Constitution of India, Articles 14, 124(3)(b), 226 - High Court and Supreme Court Judges (Salaries and Condition of Service) Amendment Act, 2005, Section 7 - Claim of parity in benefit - The addition of ten years to service of High Court Judges for pension, as done for Supreme Court Judges, does not fall under Article 14 as Supreme Court and High Court Judges are separate classes.
