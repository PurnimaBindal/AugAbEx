Andhra Pradesh Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1963 - Prevention of Corruption Act, Section 5 - Acquittal in criminal proceedings - Departmental proceedings - Even though the charge in Criminal proceedings was only possessing assets disproportionate to the known source of income and the delinquent was acquitted for want of sufficient evidence, departmental proceedings are not barred because the delinquent was charged with violating Andhra Pradesh Civil Service (Conduct) Rules, 1964 by acquiring and disposing of property without permission of the competent authority.
