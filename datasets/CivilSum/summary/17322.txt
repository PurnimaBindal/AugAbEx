Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rule 2 - Benefit of Military service - A petitioner discharged on compassionate grounds from military service prior to emergency and then joined civil service - No explicit prohibition in Rule 2 for including military personnel who joined before the emergency for benefit of service - Therefore, benefit of military service allowed for increments and pension - However, delay and inaction on pressing for seniority benefit.
