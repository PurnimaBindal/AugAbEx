The appointment of seasonal workers is governed by directions and recruitment rules that differ from those that apply to regular appointees.
Appointments are for a specific duration only and cannot be treated as permanent appointments.
Denying appointment to a worker who has been working for more than a decade on the grounds of being overaged is illegal and arbitrary.
The petitioner is to be considered for appointment as a seasonal worker.
