Constitution of India, Article 14 - Discrimination - Right of equality - Acquisition - Livelihood - Employment - The government's acquisition of lands for HSEB resulted in land-owners losing their only means of livelihood.
In response, HSEB made a policy decision to offer employment to one family member of each land-owner whose land was acquired.
The petitioner argues that other similarly situated persons have been given employment in accordance with this policy decision and there is no justification for denying employment to the petitioner.
The Board and its functionaries have been directed to appoint the petitioner to a post that is commensurate with their qualifications.
[Paras 5 and 8]

Acquisition - Employment - Policy decision - The land belonging to the petitioner's family was acquired for the construction of Panipat Thermal Power Plant.
The award passed by the State stipulated that the land was acquired for this purpose.
The Respondents cannot reasonably argue that the petitioner is not entitled to the benefit of the policy decision to give employment to one dependent of each landowner whose land is acquired.
[Para 6]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Mandamus - Acquisition - Employment - Policy decision - The respondents have not presented any justification for denying the petitioner's claim for employment.
The petitioner has applied for employment as a dependent of a landowner whose land was acquired as early as November 24th, 1989.
The petitioner is entitled to a writ of mandamus compelling the respondents to appoint them in accordance with the policy decision.
[Para 7]
