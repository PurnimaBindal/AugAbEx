Enquiry proceedings to remove a respondent from service have been deemed illegitimate due to violations of natural justice, including denial of opportunity for hearing and lack of access to documents.
Consequently, the termination order has been set aside, along with all consequential benefits of service.
[Para]
A suit filed by a respondent against a private institution for termination of service without due process is deemed maintainable since civil rights can be enforced through legal action.
Violations of principles of natural justice, such as denial of opportunity for hearing, are observed.
