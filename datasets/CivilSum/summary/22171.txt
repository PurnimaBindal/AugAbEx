Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-O(1) - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 11 - The principle of res judicata - Withdrawal of first petition under Section 25-O(1) was made in good faith for an amicable settlement - While the Code of Civil Procedure does not strictly apply to proceedings under Section 25- O(1) of Industrial Disputes Act, some of the general principles in CPC may be applicable.
[Para 21] B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Bench hunting - The Hon'ble Bench inclined to dismiss the petition - Counsel withdrew the petition so he could file a second writ petition before a more suitable or convenient bench - If he withdraws such a petition, he should not be allowed to file a second petition unless granted liberty to do so - Bench hunting should not be permitted.
[Para 13] C. Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 7, Rule 7 - Industrial Disputes Act- Relief - Courts must mold relief in a given situation - All provisions of the CPC do not strictly apply to proceedings under the Industrial Disputes Act.
[Para 23] D. Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25(O)(1) - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order XXIII, Rule 1(4) - Withdrawal of suit - Where the plaintiff withdraws a suit without permission of the Court, he is precluded from instituting any fresh suit - An application under Section 25(O)(1) is not a suit - Said provision will not apply to such an application.
[Para 24] E. Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 25-(O) (1) and 25-O(5) - Closure application - Company withdrew its closure application - No order was passed on merits either granting or refusing to grant permission for closure - Section 25-O(5) has no application.
