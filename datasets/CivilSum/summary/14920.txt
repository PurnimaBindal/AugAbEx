No. 2 - Mr. S K Bhanot, Advocate.
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Pay scales - Recommendations of Pay Commission - Employees of Corporations owned by the Central Government - The same pay scales must be applicable to both employees of the Central Government and those of the Corporation owned by the Central Government - Employees of the Corporation must receive pay scales and other benefits, including Gratuity and other retiring benefits, on the same pattern - No distinction should be made between Government and Corporation employees for this purpose.
