Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 162 and 1624 - Dismissal from service/gravest misconduct - Dismissal may be ordered on proof of either a grave act of misconduct or cumulative effect of continued misconduct, which renders the individual incorrigible and unfit for police service - The competent authority must provide categoric findings based on reasons for concluding that gravest misconduct was committed, as a single act of absence from duty may not warrant dismissal.
