Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Appointment - Payment of wages - To receive payment of salary, a valid appointment by a Competent Authority is mandatory and if lacking the court can give direction for the same - Salary cannot be held back alleging forged appointment letters - If petitioners cannot produce valid appointment, they are not eligible to receive any direction for salary payment.
[Para 4]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disputed facts - Appointment - Payment of wages - Writ Court has no jurisdiction to decide on disputed questions of fact in matters of appointment and salary payment.
[Para 5]
