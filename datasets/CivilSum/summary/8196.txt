Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10, 11-A, 25-F - Back wages - The burden of proof falls on the party opposing back wages to show why they should not be awarded - The Palwal Coop.
Sugar Mills case establishes this principle and does not conflict with the Hari Palace case - If the termination is deemed illegal, then full back wages must be awarded and reinstatement granted [Paras 5 and 7].
Illegal and arbitrary termination - Reinstatement with back wages - Recovery from the officer/officials who illegally and arbitrarily terminated services of a person - Recovery may be possible, but only after the individual has been given the opportunity to challenge the decision [Para 7].
