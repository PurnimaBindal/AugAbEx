For Respondent SK Bhanot, DAG, Punjab, under Constitution of India, Article 311 (2) (c), the dismissal of an Inspector of Police from service on the grounds of leaking information to terrorists, accepting illegal gratification and associating with anti-social and anti-national elements can be subject to judicial review.
In the case at hand, no material was presented to support the satisfaction reached for invoking Article 311(2) (c), and thus, the order of dismissal cannot be sustained without any such records.
The order is quashed with permission to proceed with departmental proceedings against the petitioner.
