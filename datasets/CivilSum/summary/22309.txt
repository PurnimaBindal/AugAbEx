Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Reference - Correction of reference - The name of the employee may have been inaccurately stated in the reference, but his identity is not in question - The Labor Court is authorized to request that the State Government correct the reference - If the employee has requested a correction from the government, the Labor Court must wait until the corrected reference is submitted - Once the corrected or amended reference is received, the Labor Court may arbitrate the dispute.
