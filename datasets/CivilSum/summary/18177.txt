Under the West Bengal College Service Commission Act, 1978, and the West Bengal College Service Commission (Manner of Selection of Persons for Appointment to the posts of Teachers including Principals) Regulations, 1980, the selection process follows a specific set of rules.
In accordance with Regulation 9, the empanelled candidates may only be recommended by the Commission upon receiving a request made by a College Principal.
On 15062006, the case of the petitioner was considered, but as no request from the Principal was given in her preferences as on that date, the Commission did not consider recommending her.
The Commission had no power to review their decision on the merits, and therefore, the petitioner was not entitled to any relief.
