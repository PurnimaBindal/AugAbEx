Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Section 7 - Pay-scale - After a writ was filed by employees of Privately Managed Government-aided Schools citing the Supreme Court's judgment in AIR 1990 SC 968, the Supreme Court directed the revision of pay scales for Government-aided schools to match those of Government schools in accordance with section 7 of the Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Act, 1979 - The judgment would be applied uniformly to all employees of Government aided schools covered by it, entitling petitioners to claim any due arrears.
