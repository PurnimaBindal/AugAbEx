The charge memo issued by the Director of Medical and Health Services is not invalid due to the fact that the power to initiate disciplinary action had been delegated to the Head of Department.
The Managing Director of the appellant was the appointing authority/disciplinary authority, and the Director of Medical and Health Services was the controlling authority.
The High Court did not accept the contention that the delegation of power was not proven.
However, the Supreme Court considered the authority who issued the charge sheet as the controlling authority and set aside the judgment of the High Court, allowing the appeal.
This aligns with the ruling in 1996(4) SCC 708.
