Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admissions/Fraud/MD University Ordinance (General Rules for Examination), Clause 32 - Cancellation of candidature/disqualification/Ineligibility - A student who obtained admission by forging eligibility certificates deserves no leniency in regularising their studies for the course, even with compassionate grounds or loss to the candidate - Vice-Chancellor has the power to cancel the candidature of such candidates who were ineligible to appear in the examination - Despite the petitioner being allowed to appear in exams from time to time, they were ultimately found guilty of fraud and ineligible for admission - Any benefit of interim orders to get the studies regularised and declaration of result cannot be taken - Proposition that "once the student is allowed to complete their studies, the same should be regularised and result declared" will not apply in the case of fraud - Petition dismissed.
