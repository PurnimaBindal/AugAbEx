According to Punjab Khadi and Village Industries Board Act, 1955, Sections 17, 31, 31-A, 26 as well as the Punjab Khadi and Village Industries Board Pension Regulations in both 1958 and 1994, the Board itself is the final authority in determining its pension scheme.
Once the Board decides to grant pensions to its employees, it only needs to inform the State Government; no approval from the State Government is necessary.
