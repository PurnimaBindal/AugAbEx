Constitution of India, Article 16 - Apprentices Act, 1961, Sections 7 and 22(1)- Apprenticeship Rules, 1991, Rules 6 (2) and 7 - Appointment - Improving employment prospects for graduate engineers and diploma holders through training - Although the letter dated 31.8.1978 of Government of India cannot be viewed as a commitment for employment, it does entitle those who underwent training to preference in employment over direct recruits.
