Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21, 226 - Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 39 - Denial of admission - Cancellation of admission form - Sustainability - If a handicapped student's admission form has been accepted, it would be arbitrary and a violation of Article 14 of the Constitution to cancel it without affording the student an opportunity to be heard or issuing a show-cause notice.
If the petitioner fulfills all conditions and his or her form is accepted, and a seat reserved for handicapped students is filled from the general category, then directions will be given to admit the petitioner in the next academic session since the current session has already begun.
