Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Railway Servants (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1968, Rules 1708 and 1715 read with Rule 25(1) - Natural Justice - Writ Jurisdiction - Remand - A writ of certiorari can only be granted in cases of excess of authority or flagrant and culpable violation of law resulting in a grave and obvious miscarriage of justice - If the accused had adequate opportunities for defence during the departmental inquiry - Prosecution and defence witnesses' statements were recorded on different dates - No violations of any Rules or procedures were identified - Punishment was given after serving a show-cause notice and completely considering the defence - Dismissal from service on proven corruption charges - The accused cannot claim miscarriage of justice for granting a writ of certiorari - Even if the request for remand to the punishing authority is accepted, no useful purpose would be served because it has already applied its mind as necessary by law - Petition refused.
[Paras 19 to 21 and 26 to 29]
