The Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Sections 1(6), 2(i) & 15 - Definition of "Employed Person" - Arrears of wages can be claimed - The act is applicable to employees receiving a salary less than Rs. 1600/- - The petitioner, employed during the relevant period and earning less than Rs. 1000/-, is entitled to claim under the Payment of Wages Act - A rejection of his claim on the basis that he held a managerial position is not justifiable.
