Constitution of India, Article 16 - Compassionate appointment - The petitioner is being denied appointment on compassionate grounds, as the authority has scrapped the policy for the same - Appointment on compassionate grounds cannot be demanded as a right - Such appointments are made to alleviate immediate hardship caused to the family of a deceased employee, and are not meant to be a replacement for the deceased employee - Reliance is placed on 1994(3) SCT 174 (SC).
