According to Section 2-A of the Industrial Dispute Act, 1947 and Sections 9 and 11 of the Civil Procedure Code, the jurisdiction of Civil Court is excluded in matters covered under the ID Act.
It was held that workmen have two independent remedies available to them, but they cannot use the second remedy if the first one was unsuccessful.
Workmen must choose one remedy and cannot use both.
