Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - Admission to two years Veterinary Pharmacist training course - Petitioners were selected based on academic performance in qualifying examination for course starting on 12.9.1995 - Misconduct against members of selection committee reported in newspaper - Respondents stopped two years training course on 2991995 - Petitioners had accrued certain rights on their admission that couldn't be taken away without notice - Order quashed.
