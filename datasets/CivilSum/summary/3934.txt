Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate Appointment - Petitioner requesting appointment on compassionate grounds due to the death of his father in 1977 - Petitioner was a minor at the time of his father's death - Appointments on compassionate grounds cannot be made after a long period of time - Petitioner did not have the right to appointment at the time - The right to appointment cannot be fulfilled when the petitioner reaches the age of majority - Petitioner not eligible for appointment on compassionate grounds.
