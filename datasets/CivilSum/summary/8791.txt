Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 300-A - Punjab Privately Managed and Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981 as amended by 1991 amendment, Rules 22 and 22-A - Scheme - New pension scheme - Cut off date - Determining a cut off date for the introduction of a new pension scheme taking into account financial implications and government capacity to meet liability is not discriminatory - Retirement prior to the cut off date does not confer a right to complain and introduction of the new scheme is a distinct matter from the amendment of an existing scheme - Therefore, the cut off date is not discriminatory in this instance.
