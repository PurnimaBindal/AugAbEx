Constitution of India, Article 14 - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, Rule 13, Appendix I - Discharge / Dismissal / Natural justice - While the petitioner was dismissed, this cannot be considered a dismissal - Even if an employee is discharged due to misconduct without any chance to respond, it is not a valid order - Any order that breaches principles of natural justice or rules is liable to be quashed.
