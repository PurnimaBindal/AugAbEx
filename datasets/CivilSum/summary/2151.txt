Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Education - Cancellation of MBBS degree - Petitioner falsely claimed to belong to Scheduled Caste while seeking admission for MBBS course - University failed to verify during admission process - Petitioner completed MBBS course and internship - Cancelling degree after 14 years deemed improper by Court - Petitioner to be granted MBBS degree on condition of not being treated as Scheduled Caste in future for obtaining employment or perks.
