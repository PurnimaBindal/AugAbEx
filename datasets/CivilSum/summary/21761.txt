Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 14-B - Delay - Penalty - The power under Section 14-B must be exercised within a reasonable time, but due to the large number of establishments in the State, many defaults are discovered late and the effect of delay is diminished.
However, delays still occur even with regard to employee contributions, which the respondent used for its own purposes without paying any interest, and interference on technical grounds will only encourage employers to undermine the purpose of the Act.
Therefore, the High Court's judgment is overturned and the appeal is granted.
