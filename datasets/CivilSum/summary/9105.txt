According to Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, an employee must have worked for 240 days during the preceding 12 calendar months before termination.
Labour Court found this requirement was not met, thus the termination was illegal.

Section 25-F and 17-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 were cited in the Constitutional case.
Workman admitted to working sporadically as a labourer for 15 to 20 days per month.
Compensation was reduced in proportion to the days worked, awarding the workman 50% of his wages.
