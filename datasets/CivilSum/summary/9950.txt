"Under the Rajasthan Recruitment of Dependents of Government Servants Dying While in Service Rules, 1975, compassionate appointment is available to the dependents of government employees who died while in service.
However, the rules do not provide the same entitlement to dependents of workcharge employees who died while in service.
This is because workcharge employees do not have a status as semi-permanent employees under the rules.
A petitioner cannot be granted relief based purely on pitiable situations and statutory provisions cannot be overlooked.
Therefore, the petition is dismissed.
[Para 11]"
