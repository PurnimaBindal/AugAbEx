The case involves the removal of an Inspector from the Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF) on grounds of misconduct.
The order was issued by the Deputy Inspector General (DIG) after a departmental enquiry and a statutory appeal was dismissed.
The contention was that the removal was in accordance with the Act and Rules.
However, the Appointing Authority of the Inspector was the Commandant as per Rule 7(B), and the requirement of approval from the Inspector General (IG) did not change this.
The original record was destroyed, but the court held that there was no illegality in the order since the removal was done by the DIG.
The single judge ordered reinstatement with consequential benefits, but on appeal, the court allowed the appeal and gave directions not to weed out records of pending cases.
