Under Rule 22 of the Rajasthan Higher Judicial Service Rules, a Judicial Officer Civil Judge-cum-Chief Judicial Magistrate's officiating appointment as Additional District Judge does not provide the right to hold the post.
This appointment is given to assess suitability and is distinct from an appointment on a substantive basis.
Reverting an officer on the grounds of unsuitability under Rule 14 of the Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958 is not a punishment.
Hence, a simple order to send back an unsuitable officer does not violate Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution.
