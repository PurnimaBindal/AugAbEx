Constitution of India, Article 136 - Seniority - Seniority list - While respondent No. 1 was senior to respondent No. 2 in the cadre of Chargeman-B, respondent No. 2 was promoted to Chargeman-A and Boiler Supervisor on 1189 before respondent No. 1.
Respondent No. 1 only reached the grade of Boiler Supervisor during the pendency of the matter before the Tribunal.
Since there was no specific rule stating that continuous length of service would be the basis of seniority in a particular grade, entry into the grade is the normal rule of promotion.
Unless it can be established that respondent No. 1 was promoted to the post of chargeman A earlier than respondent No. 2 or that respondent No. 2 was erroneously promoted, there is no question of respondent No. 1 gaining seniority over respondent No. 2.
Thus, the Tribunal was wholly unjustified in interfering with the gradation list of Boiler Supervisor.
