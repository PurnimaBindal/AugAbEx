The Constitution of India, through Articles 14, 226 and 311, states that if an employee who is entrusted with the handling of cash and property of an employer, commits fraudulent acts by tampering with the records and misappropriating funds, dismissal from service is a proportionate course of action.
The employer can justly plead loss of confidence as a result.
It is not the court's place to substitute its own punishment unless the order is illogical, unreasonable, or illegal.
There is no room for sentimentality in such cases.
In the context of criminal and departmental proceedings, evidence presented in both charges is distinct and serves different ends.
A governing body cannot rely solely on an acquittal in criminal charges as a justification for retaining an individual with a track record of misappropriation in their employ.
The purpose and standard of proof in both the criminal and departmental spheres are vastly different from one another.
