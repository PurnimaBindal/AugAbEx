Constitution of India, Articles 132 and 226 - Policy matters - The UGC and CSIR's Post-Doctoral Research Associateship scheme is not an employment arrangement, but rather an interim measure to help these research fellows get a job and continue to pursue further research - The scheme is mainly a social welfare measure to support research scholars during their five-year waiting period when they have no job, rather than solely aimed at promoting scientific research - UGC or CSIR cannot be directed to create a better scheme.
