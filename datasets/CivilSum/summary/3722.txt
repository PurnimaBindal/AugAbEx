Appointment - The respondent was never considered a regular employee by the board and was hired on a contract basis for several years.
The board was justified in not renewing his contract and the doctrine of promissory estoppel does not apply unless there is a significant change in position.
[Para 10] B Regularisation - Regularisation is not a method of recruitment and two conditions must be met for a regular appointment to be made: the position must be vacant and the employer must fill it in accordance with recruitment rules and regulations.
