Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Sch.
II, Item 6, Sch.
IV, Item 9 - Unfair Labour Practice Jurisdiction - The Labour Court cannot adjudicate on the issue of the existence of an employer-employee relationship with a union, as it can only be decided under Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act.
The respondents denying this relationship was deemed a proper reason for dismissing the complaint on grounds of absence of jurisdiction.
