Article 16(4) of the Constitution of India and the LIC of India (Class III and Class IV) Recruitment Instructions, 1979, Para 22 permit compassionate appointment under specific conditions - Decisions on such appointments are only made where none of the family members are already gainfully employed - This condition is reasonable given that the death of a bread-winner does not always leave a family in distress and in need of employment assistance.
[Para 7] B.
A compassionate appointment was denied based on the ground that the deceased's other daughter was already gainfully employed at the time of the death and continues to be so employed - The subsequent marriage of the other daughter does not provide a basis to challenge the decision to deny the appointment.
