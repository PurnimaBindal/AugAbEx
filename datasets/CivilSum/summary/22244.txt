Constitution of India, Articles 32 and 311 - Termination/Back wages - An employee who was terminated illegally was reinstated with back wages, despite the authorities' adamant behavior towards them.
The litigation pursued by the authorities resulted in a waste of public money and a loss of the employee's working life due to 20 years of avoidable litigation.
Arrears of back wages were ordered to be recovered from the salaries of the officers responsible for termination and for pursuing the litigation unnecessarily.
