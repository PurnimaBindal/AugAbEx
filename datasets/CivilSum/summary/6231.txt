The Punjab Civil Services (Premature Recruitment) Rules, 1975, Rules 3(1)(a) & 4, and Articles 14 and 311 of the Constitution of India - Premature retirement - Exercise of authority - The authority may compel the retirement of a government servant, but only in the public interest if he or she is of the opinion that keeping the person in service is not in the public's best interests - The opinion must be exercised with great caution, and any orders for compulsory retirement made without any justification are not sustainable - Adverse remarks made before a promotion cannot be used as a basis for invoking the power of compulsory retirement - Such opinion should be made with great circumspection.
[Paras 10 and 11] B The Punjab Civil Services (Premature Recruitment) Rules, 1975, Rules 3(1)(a) & 4, and Articles 14 and 311 of the Constitution of India - Premature retirement - Representation - A government servant's right to representation is important, and the authority must fully consider all aspects and pass a well-reasoned speaking order that reflects full application of mind - A sketchy rejection order violates natural justice and cannot be sustained.
