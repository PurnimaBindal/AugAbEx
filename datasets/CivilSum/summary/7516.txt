Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 - Departmental proceedings - Stay of - The trial court correctly stayed departmental proceedings as there was a prima facie case established by the respondent and the balance of convenience favored the respondent.
Notice of enquiry was issued to the respondent after her written complaint with the police, with grudge and ill feelings cited as the motive behind the inquiry.
The respondent received no opportunity to defend herself against allegations of gender harassment, which were labeled as baseless without any investigation.
