Constitution of India, Article 21 - Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of the Deceased Govt. Employees Rules, 2003, Rules 7(b),(h) and 8(a), (b) & (e) - Financial assistance for the indigent family - The family and eligible members of the deceased civil servant include a married son - If the married son is earning a good salary, the widow of the deceased cannot claim financial assistance according to the Rules - If the son is not supporting the widow, she may claim maintenance from the earning son as a remedy.
[Paras 5 and 6]
