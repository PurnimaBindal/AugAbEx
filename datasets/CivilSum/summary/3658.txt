The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 state that the abolition of a post and resulting termination, in accordance with the terms and conditions of an appointment offer, cannot be challenged on the grounds of arbitrariness or illegality.
The abolition of a post is an Executive policy decision.
[Paras 7, 9, and 11]
According to the Constitution of India, Article 311, the protection of employment cannot be guaranteed in the case of the abolition of posts.
When authorities have made a decision in good faith, Courts cannot interfere with the termination order and declare that the petitioners are entitled to protection of employment.
[Paras 9 and 11]
