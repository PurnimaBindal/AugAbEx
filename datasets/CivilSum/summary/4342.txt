Constitution of India, Article 226 - Bombay Police (Punishment and Appeal Rules, 1956, Rule 3(1A) - Bombay Civil Services Rules, Rule 156(c) - Criminal proceedings - Suspension - A constable in the Armed Police is governed by Special Rules of his service, namely the Bombay Police Rules - Such an officer cannot invoke Civil Services Rules - Suspension pending criminal proceedings upheld - Court not to substitute its own findings for that of the disciplinary authority - Disciplinary authority's decision not arbitrary, unreasonable or unjust.
