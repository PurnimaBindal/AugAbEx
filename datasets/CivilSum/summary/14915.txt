Constitution of India, Article 14 - Pension/Army Pension Rules/Disability pension - The petitioner was granted disability pension but it was later reduced and stopped due to the disability being below 20%.
However, there was no order from any competent authority holding the petitioner's disability below 20%.
The order to stop the pension was justified based on an opinion rendered by the Appellate Medical Authority after the initial order was passed, but the appellate authority did not call the petitioner for a medical examination.
Moreover, the initial order was not supported by any evidence.
The orders of both the competent authority and the Appellate Authority cannot be sustained.
The petitioner is entitled to arrears of pension until the next review and the petition is allowed with costs of Rs. 2,000/-.
