Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulations Act, 1978, Sections 2(20) and 9 - Appeal against punishment - Secondary or Higher Secondary Ashram Schools or Blind Schools are considered "private schools" per Section 2(20) of the Act - Employees of these schools have access to the remedy of appeal under Section 9.
[Paras 18 & 20(b)]
Assam School Code - Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulations Act, 1978, Sections 2(20) and 9 - Appeal against punishment - The statutory remedy for private school employees under Section 9 cannot be invalidated by the Assam School Code - Executive orders cannot override statutory Rules.
[Paras 17, 19, 20(c), and 21]
