Army Regulations, Regulation 185-A - Re-assessment of disability - Disability pension - The Medical Board is the competent authority to assess or re-assess the ratio of disability.
The accounts branch responsible for the pension cannot challenge the judgment of medical experts commenting on the extent of disability.
[Paras 5 and 6]

Disability suffered during Army service - Whether attributable to service - A rigorous medical test is taken at the time of entry into Army service.
If the disease was not noted at the time of entry, the disease leading to the person's invalidation from service is assumed to have arisen out of and is attributable to service.
[Para 5]
