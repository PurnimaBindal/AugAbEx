Constitution of India Articles 226 and 311 - Government Employees (Conduct) Rules 1966 Rule 3(1) - Misconduct - The Executive Engineer being charged for a loss of Rs. 28000/- based on an erroneous certificate executed by his subordinate causing deduction of payments to the contractor, was acquitted as he was not in charge of the area where work was executed and he only acted on the basis of existing records.
Therefore, charge-sheet was quashed.
