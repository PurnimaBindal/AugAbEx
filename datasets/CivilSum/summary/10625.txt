Constitution of India, Article 226 - Judicial restraints - Structures - Adverse remarks in judgments - Only in rare, compelling circumstances should expunction of such remarks be passed, as outlined in the guidelines of AIR 1997 SC 1157.
Judges should practice judicial restraint and keep their virtue refurbished while dealing with matters, whether in appellate, revisional, or supervisory jurisdiction.
Higher courts exist to set right errors in lower court rulings, not to belittle judges in lower positions.
It should be remembered that "a Judge who has not committed any error is yet to be born."
