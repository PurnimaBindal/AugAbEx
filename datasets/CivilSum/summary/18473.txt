Constitution of India, Article 226 - Directorate of Adult Education (Group A and Group B Posts) Recruitment Rules 1981 - Adhoc promotion in 1989 - Regular promotion w.e.f 1995 - Petitioner seeks promotion from 1989 based on argument that ad hoc promotion was against regular vacancy - Court holds that since regular vacancy arose in 1993, petitioner should be deemed regularly promoted from 1993 rather than 1995.
[Paras 14, 18 and 19]
