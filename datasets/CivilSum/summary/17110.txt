Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical reimbursement - Petitioner underwent treatment at AIIMS, and the bill of Rs. 13,000/- was not reimbursed because he was not wholly dependent on the income of his wife, who was a government servant under the State of Haryana - Despite receiving a family pension after his wife's death and his own pension @ Rs. 3000/- pm, it was held that since the wife's income benefited the entire family, the petitioner was not independent of her income and is thus entitled to reimbursement.
