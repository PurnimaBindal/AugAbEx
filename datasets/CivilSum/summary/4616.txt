Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Banaras Hindu University Act, 1915, Sections 2(i) and 16B - Equal pay for equal work - Qualifications - Research Assistants in the University who have been given teaching duties in lieu of vacant posts can qualify for appointment to those same posts and receive pay equivalent to that of lecturers, despite lacking the same qualifications as prescribed for lecturers.
The University cannot deny appointment or pay to these Research Assistants based on non-fulfillment of qualifications, when it allowed them to continue in those duties for an extended period of time against existing vacancies.
