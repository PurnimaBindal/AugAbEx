According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, compassionate appointment can be given to the family member of a deceased employee.
However, in a case where the son of a deceased bank employee was not eligible on the date of death and the wife declined to accept employment due to illness, compassionate appointment could not be considered after 5 years.
This was especially true in light of the fact that the family had already received service benefits of Rs. 2,72,61434 and was receiving a family pension of Rs. 3200/- as well.
