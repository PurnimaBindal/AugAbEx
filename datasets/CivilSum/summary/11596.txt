Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 2(b) - Employment injury - Respondent who was assaulted while returning home from night shift can claim benefit admissible for employment injury under ESI Act - EI court found respondent entitled to benefits - However, injury cannot be treated as employment injury as it does not arise out of employment - For an injury to be considered an employment injury, the employee must be doing something incidental to their employment - Appeal allowed accordingly.
