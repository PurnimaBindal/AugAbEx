Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Compassionate appointment - Only one person can receive compassionate appointment - Dispute arises between widow of deceased and her stepson - No clear directives regarding preference - In determining preference, authorities must consider which claimant is the primary financial provider for the family - Court cannot make this determination - Remand the case back to the authorities to determine effective bread winner through an inquiry.
[Paras 14 and 18]
