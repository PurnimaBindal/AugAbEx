Clause X of the Letters Patent Appeal involves the dismissal of a Junior Engineer for the unauthorized construction of a market under his jurisdiction.
The punishment of dismissal was originally inflicted but was later converted into a minor penalty by a learned Single Judge.
However, the respondent had a duty to check and take appropriate action against unauthorized construction, and therefore a lenient view is not called for.
The Impugned judgment is set aside.
[Paras 10, 11 and 15]
