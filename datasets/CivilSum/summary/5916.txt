Constitution of India, Article 311 - Back wages/Retirement before superannuation date Class IV employee entitled to remain in service up to the age of 60 years illegally retired at 58 year - If an employee's retirement is deemed illegal and void, their continued service mandates entitlement to the corresponding emoluments unless evidence of gainful employment post-retirement is presented - Even if the employee reached the age of superannuation during litigation, benefits cannot be withheld.
This interpretation aligns with A.I.R. 1977 SC 1233, 1983(1) SLR 151, and 1983 (2) LIC 1738.
