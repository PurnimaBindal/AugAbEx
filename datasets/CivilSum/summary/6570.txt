Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Disciplinary proceedings - Quashing due to delay in their finalization - Constitution of India, Article 16 - Withholding of pending disciplinary proceedings - Charge against appellant for issuing fake Jamabandi and taking bribe - Delay in final decision cannot be grounds to absolve appellant - Executive instructions for prompt disposal of departmental inquiries not sufficient to invalidate proceedings - Petitioner not illegally deprived of promotion as Kanungo due to departmental proceedings - Appeal dismissed.
