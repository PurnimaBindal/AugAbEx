According to Sections 25F and 25N of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the principle of 'last come first go' applies to continuous service.
However, when a casual employee works in different establishments under the same employer with varying administrative setups, requirements, and projects, the concept of continuous service is not applicable.
The appointment of the respondent to another establishment of the railway for a project constitutes a fresh appointment.
If the project ends, the requirement of Section 25N does not need to be followed.
