Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 13.7 - Lower School Course - Passing of B-I Test - Petitioners assigned to Commando Force - From 1986 to 1994, they did not take B-I test - They were informed about B-I test but did not appear - Not publishing the date of B-I test does not prejudice them because they were duly informed - They cannot get ahead of those who passed the B-I Test just because they were assigned to a course based on an interim order without taking the test.
[Paras 7 to 13]
