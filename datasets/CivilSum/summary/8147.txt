According to Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a workman is defined as an employee who is not in a supervisory or managerial role.
The petitioner, who works as a clerk in the Accounts Department and does not have any supervisory or managerial responsibilities, qualifies as a workman.
The term "Supervision" should be taken to mean only the supervision of subordinates and not of computers.
The checking of ledgers, statements, registers, records or computer printouts cannot be considered a supervisory or managerial function.
