Article 311 of the Constitution of India and Rule 50(3) of the State Bank of India (Supervisory Staff) Rules, 1975 deal with disciplinary proceedings and reasonable opportunities for the delinquent.
Delinquents under these rules are entitled to a copy of the enquiry report to have full opportunity to meet any adverse conclusions.
Failure to supply the report violates the principles of natural justice and denies the delinquent a reasonable opportunity to defend themselves.
[Para 20]
Article 16 of the Constitution of India deals with non-speaking orders and the need to communicate reasons for disagreement.
When a disciplinary authority fails to communicate reasons for disagreement to the report of an enquiry officer, it vitiates the proceedings and leads to a non-speaking order.
In this case, the appellate order was quashed due to the vital infirmity of being a non-speaking order.
[Para 22]
Rule 51(2) of the State Bank of India (Supervisory Staff) Rules, 1975 sets out the procedure for appeals.
The appellate authority must record reasons to show that the points raised in the appeal were duly considered and decided.
Failure to provide reasons in the appellate order renders it liable to be quashed.
