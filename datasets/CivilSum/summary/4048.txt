Air Force Rules 1969, Rule 18(2)(g)(2i) - Absent without leave - Charge-sheet - Court martial preceding - Staying of - A separate cause of action is the convening of DCM based on allegations in chargesheet and is not associated with the main petition - Petitioner's application for staying of DCM is early as they are entitled to challenge the final order after exhausting departmental remedies - An application for a stay on DCM is not viable.
