Haryana Technical Education Department (Group A) Service Rules, 1986, Rule 9(1)(c)(i), 9(1)(d)(i) - Promotion - Promotion from Head of Department to Principal of Polytechnic offering Engineering diplomas - Promotion of Respondent No. 3 as Principal challenged due to ineligibility as parent department is not a department of Engineering - Affidavit by Respondent No. 2 confirms that the Department of Applied Science is not a department of Engineering - The same is clear from the corresponding Rules - Decided that Department of Applied Science is neither a trade in Engineering or non-engineering - Cannot be considered a Department of Engineering - Appointment of Respondent No. 3 to the post of Principal is against the Rules, and thus quashed.
