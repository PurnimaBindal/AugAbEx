According to the Central Administrative Tribunals Act of 1985, Section 19 and Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, transfer and posting of civil servants are guidelines and not justifiable in court.
Transfer is considered an administrative order and an incident of service.
Unless a case of mala fide or arbitrariness is made, the Court or the Tribunals have no jurisdiction to interfere with transfer orders.
[Paras 7 and 12]
