Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 16.38(1)(2) (As applicable to Haryana) - Offences by Police officers - An Inspector was in charge of a police station where some persons were tortured and beaten - A departmental enquiry was conducted and the petitioner was ultimately dismissed from service - The respondent sought to have the dismissal order quashed - A single judge held that the concurrence of the District Magistrate was not valid due to lack of application of mind - An LPA was filed by the appellants - It was determined that the SP in Haryana is to make the decision but must have the concurrence of the District Magistrate who must apply his mind but does not need to record his reasons - The LPA was accepted.
