The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Administrative law - Natural justice - An inquiry report - Punishment - The report of the Enquiry Officer is a relevant material that the disciplinary authority uses to form its mind - Breaching the rules of natural justice is a clear breach when the disciplinary authority forms an opinion without making the enquiry report available to the concerned officer and without hearing from the officer - The proceedings are vitiated - The 42nd Amendment of the Constitution does not affect the legal position.
[Paras 7 and 11] B The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Administrative law - Writ jurisdiction - The court in its writ jurisdiction does not sit in appeal over the finding of the Enquiry Officer but conducts only a procedural check and a jurisdictional supervision - The finding of the Enquiry Officer that cash certificate was credited from the over draft account to conceal the writ petitioner's conduct is a finding of fact - No supervisory or appellate jurisdiction is permissible.
