According to Section 31(3) (b) of the UP State University Act, 1973, as well as Section 2 of the UP State University (Validation of Appointments) Act, 1984, an appointment made against a leave vacancy for a post that is later taken up by the regular incumbent for higher studies will be cancelled upon their return, even if the leave vacancy remains unfilled for over 10 months.
It's important to note that there is no provision for a regular vacancy in this case, and Section 2 of the Validation Act 1984 does not apply.
