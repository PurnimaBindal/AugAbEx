Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Disciplinary proceedings - Compulsory retirement - Employee must be removed from service due to charge of unauthorised absence being proved, but in the present case, the employee was not in sound mental health and left home in a state of unsound mind - F.I.R. was lodged by the employee's wife - CAT chooses to impose the punishment of compulsory retirement - After considering 18 years of service, it is found that the employee's family should not be deprived of retirement benefits - Hence, the decision taken by CAT is not liable to be interfered with.
