The remedy under Sections 29 and 33-C(2) of ID Act for implementation of the Award of the Labour Court is not an equally efficacious alternative remedy, which is a ground to deny relief under Article 226 of the Constitution.
The Court must entertain the lis of poor where the question of their livelihood is involved and protect the rights of the people.
The rule of not entertaining the writ petition under Article 226 in a case where an equally efficacious alternative remedy is available is a rule of self-imposed restraint, not a rule of law.
[Paras 2, 5 to 9, 10 and 11]

On reinstating a low paid daily wage employee by the court, posting him at a far away place is an act intended to frustrate the award of the court.
Such a placement makes it impossible for the employee to make his both ends meet, and he will be compelled to leave the job.
On reinstatement, such an employee must be reinstated at or near the same station from where his services were terminated.
