Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Wages - Holiday - Duties performed on public and general - Petitioners who work as sweepers and cleaners in the Police Department are claiming remuneration for their work on public and general holidays - They argue that despite having worked on Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays, they have not received any additional payment - According to Article 14, 16, and 226, the petitioners are entitled to remuneration for the holidays they worked.
