Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Bank of India Employees Pension Fund Rules, Rule 22 - Pensionary benefit - Denial - The petitioner worked with National Bank of Lahore before its amalgamation with State Bank of India in 1970.
He was re-employed by SBI as there was no corresponding scale available, however, those over 35 years of age in 1970 were not entitled to the pension scheme.
The petitioner was denied pension even though there was no provision in the amalgamation scheme that all employees of the former organisation would not be entitled to pension.
The decision of SBI to deny pension to the petitioner is unreasonable, and a legitimate right to claim pension should not be denied on the grounds of lack of accommodation in corresponding scales.
The petitioner is therefore entitled to a grant of pension.
[Paras 25, 26, 33, 34, 40 and 41]
