According to the Constitution of India's Articles 309, 311, and 73 and other relevant railway regulations like the Manual of Railway Pension Rules in 1950 and Rule 2046 of the Railway Establishment Code, it is valid for railway employees to face compulsory retirement.
Additionally, the provision regarding compulsory retirement is consistent and not invalid as per Para 620 of the Pension Rules Manual.
