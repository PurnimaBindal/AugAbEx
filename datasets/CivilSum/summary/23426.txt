Constitution of India, Article 16 - Reservation - Reservation in recruitment for Class III and Class IV posts - Family - The State Govt. defined family as including parents, brothers and unmarried daughters of candidates for the purpose of giving weightage of marks to those without a family member in employment - The High Court quashed the definition, but it was not justified for the State Govt. to provide weightage or incentive for a class of citizens based solely on non-employment of a family member not socially and educationally backward - The High Court was not justified in quashing the selection procedure and holding of interview - The judgment applies only to future selections, not retrospective dates - The selection already made is upheld.
[Paras 7 to 10]
