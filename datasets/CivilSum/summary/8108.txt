Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - Petitioner, the Head of Department of Pathology at Patna Medical College, has been transferred to another medical college despite the course of Pathology not being available there.
The normal procedure for proposing transfers was not followed, and it is unclear how the petitioner's proposal came before the Establishment Committee.
This transfer is not part of the regular chain transfer and is deemed to be arbitrary, unfair, and malafide in law.
If the petitioner is transferred, his seniority and experience will be wasted and it is also likely to harm the interest of students.
As a result, the transfer of the petitioner is quashed.
[Paras 16 to 19]
