Constitution of India 1950, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Subsistence allowance - The order of punishment was issued after an investigation, and two annual increments were withheld with cumulative effect, limiting the subsistence allowance received.
When the penalty was reduced to a warning, the claim for salary during this period was denied.
The authorities should have given the petitioner a fair chance to challenge the claim.
