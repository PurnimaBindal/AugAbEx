Provisions of Section 15(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 are not applicable to the State of Madhya Pradesh, therefore, the Labour Court committed a serious error in granting relief under this provision.
[Para 8]
The Labour Court must consider relevant facts and then determine the existence of a prima facie case, balance of convenience, and irreparable loss when granting interim relief under Section 15(2)(b) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
Interim relief cannot be granted solely based on the employee's completion of 240 days working or non-compliance with the Act of 1947.
