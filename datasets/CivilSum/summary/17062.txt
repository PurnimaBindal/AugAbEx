Constitution of India, Articles 30 and 226 - Education - Affiliation of educational institutions to the University - If an educational institution does not have valid affiliation for providing medical education and training, the University cannot be directed to hold examinations for the students enrolled in such courses - Collecting fees and donations from the students by misrepresenting the institution's affiliation is a criminal offense - The Medical Council and State are responsible for taking appropriate action against such institutions under criminal law - However, students admitted to such institutions will suffer - College of Nursing for Women, Nangal Kalan (Mansa), run by the Khalsa Rural Nursing Training Institute and Hospital Trust, Nangal Kalan has never been affiliated with any Universities and the Punjabi University had no authority to grant any decision for affiliation after 22.3.1999 - The Institute failed to comply with the conditions imposed by the inspection committee and never received an N.O.C from the Govt - Baba Farid University cannot be forced to accept the Institution that has accumulated funds from students fraudulently and committed offences.
