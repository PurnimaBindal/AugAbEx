Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 16 - Disciplinary proceedings - An employee can face disciplinary proceedings for actions taken while exercising judicial or quasi-judicial powers - Petitioner, a Tehsildar who verified a false certificate faced disciplinary action - A full-fledged enquiry was held and the Disciplinary Authority forfeited 1/5th pension for three years - The impugned order was held to be faultless.
