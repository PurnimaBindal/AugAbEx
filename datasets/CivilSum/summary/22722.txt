According to Section 2(8) of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, an employment injury is defined as any accident that happens to a worker while traveling to or from work or performing duties for their job.
However, it cannot be considered to have arisen out of employment unless the worker was engaged in some task associated with their employment at the time.
Additionally, the accident must occur within the time frame of their employment.
