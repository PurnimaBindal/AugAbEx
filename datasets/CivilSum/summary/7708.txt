Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Merit list - A candidate was selected for the post but placed in the waiting list at serial No. 206 - The waiting list was exhausted until serial No. 203 - Five candidates refused to join - The petitioner claimed to be considered for appointment - The mere inclusion of a candidate's name in the merit list does not guarantee appointment, as it is up to the discretion of the Government to fill any vacancies - The court may only interfere if the power is used in a malafide or arbitrary manner - No appointment was given to a junior candidate and no discrimination was observed - The court cannot interfere.
