The termination order of an employee was deemed invalid because the plaintiff was not given a copy of the enquiry report with the show cause notice, which violated the mandate of Punjab State Electricity Board Employees (Punishment and Appeals) Regulations, 1991, Regulation 8.
This deprived the employee of an opportunity to make an effective representation, as stated in Para 7.
Punjab State Electricity Board Employees (Punishment and Appeals) Regulations, 1991, Regulation 8 ensures that delinquent officers have a fair opportunity to make a representation against the conclusions arrived at by the Inquiry Officer or the disciplinary authority.
This provision safeguards the interests of the delinquent officer to avoid arbitrary actions as per Para 6.
