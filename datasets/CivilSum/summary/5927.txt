Punjab State Public Service Commission (Conditions of Service) Regulations 1958, Regulations 2(d) and 3 - Constitution of India, Article 316(1-A) - Public Service Commission - The Commission is a constitutional body with the power to regulate its own internal functions - Lack of regulations cannot cover all possible eventualities - A conscious decision to delegate any functions to the Chairman is not considered tainted - If the Commission selects a candidate based on a uniform criteria, committees formed by the Chairman do not impact the selection.
[Para ] B Punjab State Public Service Commission (Conditions of Service) Regulations 1958, Regulations 2(d) and 3 - Constitution of India, Article 316(1-A) - Public Service Commission - For conducting interviews, Committees consisting of member(s) and two experts were constituted - The vacancy in the Committee cannot prevent the Chairman and remaining members from functioning - Regulations provide for the constitution of a Commission - The Chairman and four members can be increased by the Governor up to five - Members of the Commission validly appointed can function until the vacancy is filled.
