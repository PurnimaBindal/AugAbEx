Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection - Eligibility - Experience - Selection for the India Trade Promotion Organisation at New York - The Selection Committee did not find the petitioner eligible due to lack of two years of experience as prescribed by the employer.
The High Court was not correct in faulting the selection process for not accommodating the petitioner by reducing the period of foreign service to a few months to fulfill the condition of eligibility without a writ of quo warranto before the court.
