Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - General Insurance (Conduct, Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1975, Rule 25(b) - Applicability of Rule 25(6) - Departmental enquiry - In India, there is no general right of representation by counsel or agent in departmental enquiries unless expressly granted through standing orders or law - The right to be represented is subject to status, rules, regulations or Standing Orders - In the present case, the petitioner is not entitled to representation by a legal practitioner under service regulations or otherwise, even if the Presenting Officer is a C.B.I.
Officer - It is held that the impugned order, which denies the petitioner the right to representation by counsel, is not invalid under Rule 25(6).
