Respondents represented by the Standing Counsel, are guided by the Constitution of India, Article 226 and the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-B regarding the awarding of back wages as a form of relief.
The court has the discretion to refuse back wages, especially if the workman's own negligence contributed to the situation.
The discretion, however, remains subject to the facts and circumstances of the particular case, and must be judiciously exercised by the Labour Court.
In cases where the punishment order was set aside on a technical ground, the delinquent may be reinstated without back wages.
If the proceedings leading to this situation were prolonged, the delinquent may still receive 50% back wages.
