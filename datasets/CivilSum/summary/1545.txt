Bar Council of India Rules, Chapter 3 Part iv, Rule 7-A as inserted by Resolution No. 20 of 1999 of Bar Council of India - Advocates Act, 1961, Rule 24-A(I) - Introduction of New Rule - The petitioner had applied for Law Practice which was rejected based on Rule 7-A introduced by Bar Council which was claimed to be not aligned with section 24-A of Advocate Act - Petitioner contended that Rule 7-A permanently bans the applicant like petitioner despite a lesser misconduct as compared to what is prescribed under section 24 of Advocates Act - However, it was held that no rule can be formulated by Bar Council that is in contrast with its parent Act - Therefore, Rule 7-A is unconstitutional and invalid - The direction was given to permit the petitioner to practice the law.
