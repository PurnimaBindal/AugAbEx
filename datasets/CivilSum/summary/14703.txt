Punjab Service of Engineers, Class-I, PWD (Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1964, Rules 12(6) - Seniority/Inter se seniority/Appointment on Ex-cadre post/Confirmation/Quota rule - Petitioners who were promoted at different dates, but confirmed as Executive Engineers in 1975-76, belonged to the service within their quota from the year of the junior-most Executive Engineer.
A member of service can still be a promotee in an ex-cadre post.
[Para 11]  B.
Delay and laches/Seniority - The belated argument that the petitioners were not members of service because there was no quota post available at the time is invalid since they became members within their quota upon confirmation in 1975-76.
