The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(k), 2-A and 10(1) state that termination, removal or dismissal of a worker can be an industrial dispute even if it is an individual cause and even if not supported by the workers union.
[Paras 7 and 8]
According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25(g) and 10(1), a dispute as to termination can be an industrial dispute or a claim for enforcement of a right under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
A writ petition can be maintained for the same.
[Para 8]
