Constitution of India, Article 16 - Transfer - Natural Justice - The petitioner was transferred for not being present in school on 422002 and even though he submitted his leave application on 522002, he was unable to inform the school authorities of his illness.
Additionally, the telephone of the school was not operational and no steps were taken to verify the validity of the petitioner's excuse.
It is important to note that the petitioner's absence was not intentional or wilful.
As a result, the order of transfer is punitive, and should be quashed.
