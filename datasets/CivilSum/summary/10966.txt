In a case challenging the selection of Assistant Directors, a petitioner belonging to the scheduled caste was recommended but not appointed for the post of Research Assistant due to a misleading statement.
The court found that the misleading statement was made by linking the appointment of Research Assistants to the case.
As a result, the petitioner was allowed to be appointed as a Research Assistant on a temporary basis and awarded costs amounting to Rs. 5,000.
