Under Section 17(1), 17(A), 17(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, an ex parte award can be set aside subject to the provisions of Section 17A After the award is published under Section 17(1), it attains finality and cannot be challenged in any court as stated in Section 17(2).
An award becomes enforceable in 30 days after its publication.
If an application for setting aside an ex parte award is made after 30 days from its publication, the Industrial Tribunal has no jurisdiction to entertain the application.
The Industrial/Labour Court loses jurisdiction after 30 days and cannot deal with an application filed beyond that time as per paras 4, 5, 6, and 7.
