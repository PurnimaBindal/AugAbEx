Demobilised Armed Forces Personnel (Reservation of Vacancies in the Punjab State Non-Technical Services) Rules, 1968 Rule 5 (as amended in 1976) - Conditions of service - Selection process - Candidates cannot claim right on the basis of mere advertisement or appearing in interview - The suitability of the candidates is assessed through the selection process.
[Paras 8 to10]
Demobilised Armed Forces Personnel (Reservation of Vacancies in the Punjab State Non-Technical Services) Rules, 1968 Rule 5 (as amended in 1976) - Retrospective amendment - If the amendment is retrospective, the amended provision is applicable from the given date.
[Para 9]
Demobilised Armed Force Personnel (Reservation of Vacancies in the Punjab State Non-Technical Services) (First Amendment) Rules, 1976 - Benefit entitlement - Soldiers and Commissioned Officers - Even a soldier who has been invalidated due to disability is entitled to the benefit under Rule 5 - The benefit cannot be denied based on rank.
[Paras 12 and13]
Demobilised Armed Forced Personnel (Reservation of Vacancies in the Punjab State Non-Technical Services) Rules, 1968, Rule 5 (as amended in 1976) - Seniority - Soldiers and Commissioned Officers - In case of a competition between Commissioned Officers and Soldiers for the same post, their seniority is determined by their dates of birth - The rank of a soldier cannot be ignored.
