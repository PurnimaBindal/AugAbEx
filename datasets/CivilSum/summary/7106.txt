Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Jurisdiction and powers - Military relief - Petitioner, a displaced employee due to the nationalization of bus service, was not employed while other employees were - Writ petition was filed and disposed of, eventually appointing petitioner as conductor - Petitioner filed for relief of seniority, but their application for recovery of arrears was rejected - The Labour Court was correct in rejecting the claim, as relief of seniority without any consequential direction does not result in any monetary benefit.
