Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Ad hoc appointment - Stop gap arrangement - The maximum probation period for a Clerk in employment of Pepsu Road Transport Corporation is three years.
The petitioner, who was appointed as a cleaner on ad hoc basis, has been discharging the duties of a Clerk for the last 6 years and has received appreciation and cash reward.
The competent authority of Corporation assigned him the duties of store issue ledger.
There is no justification for not appointing him as Clerk by the Respondent Corporation.
The Respondent is directed to consider the petitioner as having been appointed as Clerk on a regular basis after he has completed three years of service from the date he was assigned the duties of a Clerk.
[Paras 4 and 5]
