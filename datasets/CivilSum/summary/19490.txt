According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, the policy of the government is to grant a premature increment to those employees who did not participate in a strike.
However, the petitioner, who was under suspension on the date of the strike and could not come to work, cannot claim a premature increment because the suspension order itself prevented him from attending the office.
Although he was granted all benefits of service upon reinstatement, he cannot be considered to have been physically present on duty and entitled to a pre-mature increment.
As the authority wanted to keep him away from duty and the office, the judgment of the single judge has been set aside.
