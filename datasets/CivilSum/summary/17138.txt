The Chairman of the Board is authorized to pass suspension orders, but the suspension of the petitioner was passed by the Secretary of the Board without approval of the Chairman, which is not appropriate.
[Para 8] B.
The petitioner Field Publicity Officer in Jammu and Kashmir Khadi and Village Industries Board was suspended, but there is no provision of suspension in the regulations framed by the Board.
Since the government has exclusive control over the Board's officers and employees, the Jammu and Kashmir Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956 will apply to such employees.
[Paras 12 and 13] C. The suspension pending inquiry continued for more than two years, and no Inquiry Officer was appointed nor were any effective proceedings taken, thus, the impugned order of suspension is quashed.
