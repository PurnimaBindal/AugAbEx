Constitution of India, Article 226 - A resident doctor died while performing duties due to Hepatitis B - An Inquiry Committee report notes that the resident doctor would have avoided contracting the disease had preventive measures been adopted and she vaccinated earlier - The petitioner, father of the resident doctor, shall receive Rs. 5 lacs as interim compensation - The amount shall be adjusted against the final compensation when the petitioner files a civil suit - The State Government is directed to establish a High Power Committee to investigate the circumstances leading to the resident doctor's death and suggest preventive measures to avoid future incidents.
