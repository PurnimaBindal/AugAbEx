Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 and 311 - Pre-mature Retirement - Adverse Reports - The petitioner's integrity was doubted in ACRs of 1984-85 and 1988-89 - Consequently, the petitioner retired pre-maturely in 1994 - The trial Court was justified in declining the claim for promotion, as the report of doubtful integrity was found to be correct - The Second Appeal was dismissed.
