Constitution of India, Article 226 - University Non-teaching Employees (Terms and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1971, Clause 49(v) of Section II - Termination - The respondent was absent from work continuously for over 90 days without permission, so his services were terminated, after being treated as absconded from work on 11.12.1984.
The writ petition had a time limit of three years, but it was not filed until about eight years after the termination decision, and was dismissed due to delay and laches.
