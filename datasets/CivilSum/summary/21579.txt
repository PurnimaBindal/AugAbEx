As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226, the quantum of punishment for misappropriation by a bank manager depends on the seriousness of the charges proved and sentiments and compassion have no role to play, such as in cases where serious charges of misappropriation by a bank manager in the name of poor villagers are proved which would warrant removal from service.
[Para 5] B.
In disciplinary proceedings under the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226, only those documents relied upon to prove the charges are to be supplied, not those that are irrelevant or found to have only collateral purposes.
The non-supply of such irrelevant documents does not prejudice the defense in any manner, and the principles of natural justice can be invoked only where real prejudice is shown to have been caused.
