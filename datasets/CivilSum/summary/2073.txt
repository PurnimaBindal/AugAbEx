Kerala Government Law Officers (Appointment and Conditions of Service) and Conducted of Cases Rules, 1978, Rule 9 - Evidence Act, 1872, Sections 157 and 145 - Conduct Rules allow for termination of service - Merely being named in an FIR is not sufficient to terminate the service of a government pleader without evidence of guilt and unfitness for the position.
