The Swatantrata Sainik Samman Pension Scheme, 1980 entitles freedom fighters to receive a pension.
To be considered for the pension, the scheme requires evidence to be presented with the claim.
In a specific case, a petitioner filed some documents, but it was deemed best for the government, not the court, to examine and determine the sufficiency of the evidence.
Therefore, the court issued directions to reconsider the claim.
