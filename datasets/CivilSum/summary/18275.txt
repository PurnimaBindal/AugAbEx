Constitution of India, Article 226 - Railway Servants (Discipline & Appeal) Rules, 1968, Rules 2(1)( c), 8(1)(b) and (2) - Disciplinary proceedings - Change memo - The Senior Scale Officer initiating enquiry proceedings can impose some of the penalties under Rule 6 of the Railway Servants (Discipline & Appeal) Rules and is competent to institute disciplinary proceedings by virtue of Rule 8(1)(b) and (2).
The change memo issued by the third petitioner is legally valid and the order passed by the tribunal is set aside.
