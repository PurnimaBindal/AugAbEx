Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 13.28 - Provident Fund - Delay in payment of G.P.F.
- The Maintenance Authority is responsible to ask legal heirs of the deceased employee to fill out form PF 10, according to para 28(iii) of instructions dt. 2111989 - Any delay or mistake in submission of form PF 10 may result in interest being levied beyond permissible period of six months, but the beneficiary is not at fault.
