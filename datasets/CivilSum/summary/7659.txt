Constitution of India, Article 226 - Misconduct - Punishment of dismissal - Proportionality - Employee who used abusive language against a superior - Prolonged punishment is not appropriate due to the employee's good work history and the lack of previous similar incidents.
[Paras 8 and 10]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Past conduct - Assessment of - Validity - The Labour Court did not assess past conduct independently to determine if the punishment was reasonable - Therefore, the Labour Court's order had a major flaw in it.
[Para 9]  C Constitution of India, Article 226 - Reinstatement/compensation in lieu of - Suitability - For using abusive language against a superior, the employee was fired - The employee cannot be retained due to the possibility of causing embarrassment to the superior - The employer can choose either to reintegrate the workforce with 50 percent back wages or pay equivalent compensation to employees.
[Paras 10, 11, 14]  D. Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compensation in lieu of reinstatement - Assessment of - Employee salary of Rs. 6400 per month - The employee's salary should be discounted to Rs. 3000 per month for 12 years since they did not work.
