Constitution of India, Article 311 - Bihar Service Code, Rule 21 - Appointments - Selections were made on the basis of merit list and in accordance with the reservation policy of the State Government, including the reserved posts.
However, the State Government set aside the appointments due to the appointing authority not being the head of the department.
The High Court set aside the State Government's order, but the appeal against the dismissal was dismissed.
