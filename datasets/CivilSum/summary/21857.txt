Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 32 - Appointment - Eligibility Criteria - Judicial officers - Petitioners, who worked as legal assistants in the education department, were declared ineligible for appointment due to not having completed the minimum three years of active practice at the Bar - Representing their department before a Tribunal or court while in service was not considered as equivalent to practice at the Bar - After the State amended its rules, the petitioners became eligible to appear for the ensuing examinations - The court held that it would be improper to reopen the selection process after all other eligible candidates had been interviewed and the select list had been finalized.
