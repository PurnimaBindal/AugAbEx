According to Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India, eligibility for selection generally requires qualification and certification.
In this case, the petitioner applied for the post of Assistant Personnel Officer but did not meet the prerequisite qualification of possessing a PG Diploma in Personnel Management/Industrial Relations.
Although he had achieved a PG Diploma in Labour Laws with Administrative Laws, he was deemed ineligible by the respondents.
As a result, no Communication was issued to the petitioner to participate in the written test, which was a proper decision by the respondents.
[Paras 10 and 11]
