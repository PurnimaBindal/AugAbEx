Workmen's Compensation Act (8 of 1923), Section 30 - Motor Vehicles Act (59 of 1988), Sections 149, 167 - The purpose of the Workman Compensation Act and the Motor Vehicles Act is to provide compensation to victims of accidents.
The only difference between the two statutes is that the WC Act applies only to workers defined under that Act, while Chapters X to XII of the MV Act's relief is available to all victims of motor vehicle accidents.
Under the WC Act, there is no need to prove negligence, unlike the MV Act, under which negligence and quantum must be demonstrated.
