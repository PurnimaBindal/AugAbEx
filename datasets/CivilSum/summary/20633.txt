Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Departmental and criminal proceedings - Withdrawal of criminal proceedings - Termination of departmental proceedings on withdrawal of criminal charges - Unnecessary - The purpose of departmental enquiry and of prosecution is different - A crime is an act of violation of law or omission of public duty - Departmental enquiry is to maintain discipline and efficiency of public service - There is no hindrance for both proceedings to proceed simultaneously except if the criminal charge involves complex issues of fact and law and could lead to serious prejudice to the accused.
[Paras 11 to 16]
