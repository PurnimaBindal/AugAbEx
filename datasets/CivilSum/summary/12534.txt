The petitioner completed his training and was discharged from service due to doubtful antecedents and character, based on a special verification report which has no statutory sanction.
This decision violates the Constitutional guarantees of public employment and the principles of equality of opportunity for all citizens.
No distinction should be made between candidates based on their state of origin.

While the decision to retain a person in Army service is up to the concerned authority, it is subject to judicial scrutiny if exercised in an arbitrary or irrelevant manner or for a collateral purpose.
The Court has the right to examine the legality of such decisions.
