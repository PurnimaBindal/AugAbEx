Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Casual labours - Regularization - The petitioners have requested the release of wages and directions to allow them to work until they are permanently absorbed - The petitioners are seeking the benefit of SRO 64 of 1994 for regularization, however, SRO 64 only applies to daily rated workers and work charge employees, not casual labours - It was decided that casual labours cannot be treated as daily rated workers/work charge employees under the SRO 64 of 1994, which the petitioners have referenced for regularization - Therefore, the petitioners cannot claim under any statutory rule and the petition was dismissed in limine.
