According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, the pay scale of the Assistant Librarian in the Advocate General's office in Haryana is not the same as that of the Librarian in the Haryana Civil Secretariat due to the significantly different qualifications required for each position.
Consequently, the State Government's decision to approve different pay scales is not discriminatory or in violation of Articles 14 and 16.
