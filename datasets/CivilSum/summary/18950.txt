Constitution of India, Article 226 - Demonstration - Dharna - Demonstration in front of a Hotel - An appellant Union was prohibited from conducting demonstrations or dharnas within 80 meters of the respondent Hotel's border - An appeal was filed - Demonstrations and dharnas are allowed as long as they do not cause a disturbance or harm to the other party - By defining a distance, the right to hold a dharna is not unduly restricted - The appeal was denied.
