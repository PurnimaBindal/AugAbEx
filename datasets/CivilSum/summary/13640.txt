Constitution of India, Article 311(i)(b) - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.1 - Enquiry - Dis.
missal - Natural Justice - The charge against the petitioner of having links with extremists is too vague, considering his commendation certificates for courageous acts in curbing terrorist activities before his dismissal.
Furthermore, there is no evidence that an enquiry was not practicable.
Mere reproduction of the statute's words is not sufficient to justify a finding of fact.
Dispensing with an enquiry on pretexts violates the cardinal principle of natural justice.
As the order of dismissal was passed without application of independent mind or satisfaction of the appointing authority, it cannot be sustained.
