According to Rule 17(1)(9) of UP Police Officers of Subordinate Ranks Punishment and Appeal Rules, 1991, the petitioner was suspended on the basis of disciplinary proceedings being contemplated against him.
However, no departmental enquiry has yet been initiated or a charge sheet issued, and there is no evidence to show the grounds on which the proceeding was contemplated.
Based on this, an order of suspension passed without proper consideration cannot be maintained and is quashed.
[Paras 4, 6, and 11]
