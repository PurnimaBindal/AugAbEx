Under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, the Border Security Force Act, Section 117, the Border Security Force Rules, Rule 99, and the Army Rules, Rule 62, the defendant in a case is entitled to an appeal to the appellate authority, which must present clear and specific reasons in support of its orders on the appeal.
Failure to provide reasons violates the principles of natural justice, as the writ court cannot determine the basis for the order.
Therefore, the matter must be remitted to the appellate authority for a new decision with speaking orders.
[Paras 20 & 22]
