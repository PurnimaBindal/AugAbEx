Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 19 and 21 - Admissions - Law and order - Selection - The State is responsible for maintaining law and order and should not give in to the threats of bad elements, as this would derail the entire selection process.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 19 and 21 - Selection - Cancellation of - Natural justice - Simply being selected does not guarantee the right to be appointed.
If irregularities are discovered that affected the outcome of the selection process, it is appropriate to cancel the process.
Such action is not considered arbitrary or mala fide.
Even appointees can be dismissed if it is found that they were appointed on fraudulent grounds without being given an opportunity to show cause.
[Paras 12, 13 and 14]
