Constitution of India, Article 14 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25(F) - U.P.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 6N - Regularization of service - Labour Tribunal held that respondents, engaged on a daily wages basis, would be deemed to have been regularized after three years of service - However, respondents did not possess the necessary qualification for regularization - Thus, the regularization could not have been allowed in light of Uma Devi's case - On appeal, it was held that while the Industrial Adjudicator can alter the terms of the employment contract, it cannot do so in a way that violates Article 14.
