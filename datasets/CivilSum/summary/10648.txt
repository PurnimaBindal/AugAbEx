Fundamental Rules, Rule 10 - Date of birth - No School Certificate - In case an employee has recorded a different age and date of birth in various records, the opinion of the Medical Board should take precedence - The petitioner recorded his age and date of birth in various records before different authorities, resulting in different dates being recorded each time - Therefore, it is appropriate for the issue to be re-examined based on the advice of the Medical Board.
