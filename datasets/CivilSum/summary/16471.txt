According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume-I, Part-I, Rules 4.4(a)(ii) and (b), an employee who is transferred inter-state must fulfill the necessary requirements of such transfer in order to be absorbed and cannot be treated as a fresh appointment.
The petitioner, who was absorbed by way of transfer from Punjab to Haryana in accordance with the rules, is not entitled to the benefit of past service in terms of Rules 4.4(b).
