Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.28 - Punishment - Review - Scope of - Authorities higher than the punishing authority may alter or cancel punishment orders, but must provide valid reasons for doing so and review all evidence on the enquiry file.
They cannot collect additional material to support the desired outcome, however.
In this case, the I.G.P.
sought another report to suspend an official but could not obtain a favourable one, so he obtained a different report from another officer, which is considered mala fide and unsupported.
