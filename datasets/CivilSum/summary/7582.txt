Constitution of India, Article 226 - Reinstatement - After a workman sustained an injury in his left eye that made him visually unfit for his role as a driver, he was given the option to work as a peon.
The workman refused and was prematurely retired, but a Labour Court determined that he should have been absorbed into a post that paid emoluments equivalent to his previous position as a driver.
It was not appropriate to give all backwages to the workman who refused the lower post, but 25% backwages for the intervening period from the date of the award up until the date of reinstatement were payable to class IV employees granted with the order of reinstatement.
[Paras 2 and 7]
