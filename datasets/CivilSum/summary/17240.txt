Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - Prospects - Elementary Teacher Training Course - Petitioner contends that candidates with D.Ed and OT qualifications were admitted to the course despite clarifications in the prospectus.
Respondent states that the word "only" was introduced in the later prospectus and that no OT candidate was admitted in the year mentioned by the petitioner.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to admission.
