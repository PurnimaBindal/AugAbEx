Constitution of India, Article 226 - Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 33 - Public Interest Litigation - Recruitment - Competitive examination - Vacancies reserved for physically handicapped - The advertisement stated that the use of a scribe would not be allowed - Denial of an opportunity to a blind person to compete in the examination if they cannot use a scribe - Respondent authorities instructed to permit all eligible blind individuals to take different competitive exams conducted by PSC with the aid of a scribe.
