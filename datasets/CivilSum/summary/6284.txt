Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Civil Procedure Code Order 39 Rules 1 and 2, Section 80 - Punjab Municipal Act, 1911, Section 49 - In a Suit for declaration and permanent injunction, a Schedule Caste plaintiff who had been working as a peon in Municipal committee for the last 22 years requested a promotion to Octroi clerk.
Although his request was granted, the defendants who were junior to him were later promoted.
The defendants claimed that notice under Section 49 of Punjab Municipal Act and Section 80, Civil Procedure Code had not been served, rendering the suit not maintainable.
Despite the trial Court decreeing the plaintiff's suit, the defendants argued that the plaintiff was not matriculate and therefore not eligible for the promotion, which the Court held to be baseless based on records and circulars.
Additionally, the plaintiff was not given an opportunity to be heard before the impugned order was passed, leading to the success of the suit on such grounds.
The appeal was dismissed accordingly.
