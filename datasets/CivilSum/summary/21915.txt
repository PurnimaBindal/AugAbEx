Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 309 - Executive policy - Grant of pay scale - The government has a policy to grant a higher pay scale to a certain percentage of employees within the total strength of the cadre, including permanent and temporary posts and consequential benefits for a limited time period - The decision of when to pay arrears is up to the government to decide as part of their executive policy, and such a decision cannot be considered arbitrary.
