Goa University Act, 1984, Section 22 - Goa University Statutes, 19(xx) - Re-appointment of Superannuated teachers/principals - Court held that the State cannot interfere with re-appointment under the guise of cabinet policy and opposition based on providing aid to college - The Amendment to Goa University Act, 1984, reducing age of retirement is not applicable because it is a case of re-appointment and not retirement.
