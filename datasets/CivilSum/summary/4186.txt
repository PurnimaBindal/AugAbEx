Respondent VK Singh, Standing Counsel, claimed that employees of Regional Rural Bank's managerial positions should not receive equal pay with their counterparts in Commercial Banks.
The reason for this denial of equality is the sponsorship of the Rural Bank by the Co-operative Bank, while Commercial Banks are sponsored by Nationalised Bank.
However, this discrimination is unfounded and violates Article 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
Those performing similar jobs are entitled to equal pay scales.
