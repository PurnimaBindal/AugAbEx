Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Criminal proceedings - A tribunal found that staying departmental proceedings during criminal proceedings is not a matter of course but a decision that should be carefully considered.
The standard of proof, mode of enquiry, and rules governing the trial are different.
Furthermore, delaying departmental proceedings may not be tolerated.
The tribunal erred in staying the disciplinary proceedings.
[Paras 1 and 14 to 19]
