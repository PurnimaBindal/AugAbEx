Constitution of India, Article 300A - Kerala Service Rules, Rule 3, Note 3, Ruling 1 - Death-cum-Retirement Gratuity - Bank loans - A bank is considered an institution under Ruling No. 1 to Note 3 Rule 3, Part-III of Kerala Service Rules.
Therefore, the loan amount can be deducted from the Death-cum-Retirement Gratuity.
