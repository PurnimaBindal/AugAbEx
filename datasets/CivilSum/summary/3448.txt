Constitution of India, Article 311 - APJMS Rules, Rule 50 - Reversion - Stop Gap Arrangement - According to the rule, temporary promotions made by stop gap arrangement cannot be claimed as preferential treatment by the promotees.
Reversion and ouster from service of petitioners is deemed valid to accommodate candidates sponsored by the State Public Service Commission.
The petitioners are not entitled to hold the posts even after serving for several years.
If any person is reverted or ousted from service, they will have the right to claim appointment/promotion if any vacancies arise in future.
[Paras 6 and 7]
