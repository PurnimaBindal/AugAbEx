The Constitution of India, including Articles 14, 16, 162, 166, 309, and 311, is cited to explain the circumstances of the petitioner's ad hoc appointment and its termination.
The Government appointed the Managing Director under bye law 24(d) of the cooperative sugar factories' by-laws, which gives the government the authority to appoint the Managing Director.
The appointment was not made by virtue of the executive powers of the state under Articles 162 and 166 of the Constitution, and the fulfillment of Article 309 and 311 is not necessary.
The petitioner's appointment was made on an ad hoc basis and extended only from time to time, and there is no arbitrary action on the part of the respondents in not renewing his service.
Finally, it is stated that Article 14 of the Constitution has not been violated.
