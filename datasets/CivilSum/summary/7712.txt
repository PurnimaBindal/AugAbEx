Punjab Ayurvedic Department (Class III Technical) Services Rules, 1963, Rule 8 - Post of Vaidya - Promotion - Qualification for promotion - Degree & Diploma - Promotion to the post of Vaidya requires a Diploma or Degree in Ayurveda of any recognised University or Board.
The Petitioner holds a diploma from the All India Ayurveda Vidyapeeth, Delhi which is recognised by the State Govt.
Hence, promotion cannot be denied on the ground that he holds a diploma instead of a degree.
[Para 10] B Punjab Ayurvedic Department (Class III Technical) Services Rules, 1963, Rule 8 - Degree & Diploma - Res judicata - The controversy regarding the qualification for promotion to the post of Vaidya has already been decided in an earlier writ petition.
Therefore, the same controversy in another writ petition is subject to the doctrine of constructive res judicata.
