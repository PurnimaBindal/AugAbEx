Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Length of service - Original appointment in another department - Government instructions to fix seniority from the date of original appointment, even if in another department and after absorption into another - Appointment as Clerk in the Consolidation Department of State of Punjab - Later absorption in the Printing and Stationery Department - Seniority must be determined from the date of original appointment, which was in the Consolidation Department.
