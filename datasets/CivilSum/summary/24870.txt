Constitution of India, Articles 235/311 - Compulsory retirement - Judicial Service - The power of control is vested in the High Court under Article 235 of the Constitution - While the Governor may be the appointing authority for members of the subordinate judicial service, the passing or signing of orders by the Governor does not take away the High Court's control - Thus, compulsory retirement of subordinate judicial service members falls under the purview of the High Court's control.
