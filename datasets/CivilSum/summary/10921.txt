Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311(2) - U.P.
Temporary Government Servants (Termination of Service) Rules, 1975 - Termination - Validity of - The termination order of the respondent No. 1, who was holding a temporary position as a clerk, was based on his misconduct of making a complaint against his superiors without following the rules.
The termination order was considered punitive and was in violation of Article 311(2) of the Indian Constitution, as the respondent was not given a chance to be heard before the decision was made.
The decision of the learned Tribunal to set aside the order of termination was correct.
