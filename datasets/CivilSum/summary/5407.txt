Section 94-A and 85-B(1) of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948 concerns the levy and recovery of damages and delegation of power to subordinates by the competent authority.
The power to authorise other officers to exercise the power under Section 85-B(1) of the Act by the Director General of ESIC is deemed ultra vires Section 95-A Nevertheless, the resolution of ESIC that the power to levy and recovery of damages under Section 85-B(1) can be exercised by the Director General or Regional Director does not transgress Section 94-A A technically defective order which is justified in content should not be overturned for reasons that would disturb an already settled matter and undermine public interest.
