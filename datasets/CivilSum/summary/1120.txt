Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regional Rural Banks Act, 1976, Sections 17, 19, 29 - Selection - Seniority-cum-merit - Promotion - Criteria for selection to the post of Area/Senior Manager involves seniority-cum-merit - Greater emphasis must be placed on seniority for selection - Allocation of fewer marks for seniority and more marks for work assessment and interview is not allowed - Viva voice marks cannot override seniority as the decisive factor for selection - Making the minimum percentage of marks in viva voice a condition for selection is arbitrary and impermissible.
[Paras 21, 26, 28]
