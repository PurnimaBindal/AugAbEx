The Scheme of Medical Re-imbursement from Life Insurance Corporation does not include Hepatitis CV+ in its list of 26 major diseases/surgeries.
However, "Diseases of the liver leading to hepatic failure or transplantation" at Serial No. 15 covers Hepatitis, as Hepatic refers to the liver and Hepatitis is an inflammation of the liver caused by a virus or a toxin.
Rejecting a claim for medical expenses due to Hepatitis CV+ not being covered by the Scheme is deemed incorrect, and the petitioner is entitled to reimbursement.
