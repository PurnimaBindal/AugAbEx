The Termination of a Trainee Service Technician's employment must be without prior notice if their service is unsatisfactory.
If the employee is absent from work, the employer may withdraw the facility of training without any notification.
The Labour Court directed reinstatement of an employee whose industrial dispute arose from their service being wrongly terminated, and although the High Court upheld the decision contrary to the provisions of Section 25F, the employee's services were terminated before their probation expired according to the terms of their employment, thus exempting them from the protection of Section 25F.
The termination did not amount to retrenchment.
[Para 6] B.
The Constitutional argument that the plea of termination of services is not affected by Section 25F was not raised before Labour Court or High Court, so it cannot be raised in a special appeal.
Any argument emerging from documents relied upon by the Labour Court would not require any fresh investigation of facts, as this is a simple case of application of law.
The issue could be raised for the first time in a special appeal.
