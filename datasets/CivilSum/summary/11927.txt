Constitution of India, Article 16 - Tamil Nadu State and Subordinate Services Rules - General Rules, Rules 10(a)(1)(i) and 35 - Seniority - Temporary service - Geological Assistants and Geophysical Assistants temporarily appointed under rule 10(a)(i) of the Rules must have their temporary service period converted into regular service for seniority purposes - Appointees were fully qualified and eligible for induction into service - The Govt. directed a special test to be conducted by Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission, and all appointees were selected - Officiating temporary service should be considered when determining seniority after regularisation.
