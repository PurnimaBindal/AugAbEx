Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F - Fixed term appointment against leave vacancy - Termination from - Section 2(oo)(bb) of the Act applies to termination from fixed term appointments against leave vacancies, even if it occurs after the employee has completed 240 days of service, and not Section 25-F [Para 5]
