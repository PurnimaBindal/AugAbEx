Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Maintainability of writ petition by Army Personnel - Any member of the armed forces can file a writ petition challenging the validity of orders that violate their fundamental rights, including rights under Article 14 of the Constitution.
[Para 9] B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 20(2) - Double jeopardy - Penalties imposed on an employee can be considered for denying promotion - Denying promotion to an employee because of penalties is not a further punishment but is a necessary consequence of misconduct.

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Army Act, Section 3(v) - Army Regulations, Regulations 9 and 15 - Pre-mature retirement - The jurisdiction of Army authorities to record censure and the Army Chief to declare an officer unfit to be retained in service due to inefficiency cannot be disputed - The Army Chief has the power to provide criteria by way of Army Orders for guiding his discretion under Rule 15 - Administrative instructions can supplement statutory rules but not replace the rules.
[Paras 11 and 12] D. Army Act, Sections 63 and 19 - Army Rules, Rule 14 - Criminal and administrative proceedings - Exoneration or acquittal in criminal charges will not prevent the competent authority from taking administrative action on the same allegations.
[Para 15] E. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Army Act, Section 19 - Army Rules, Rule 14 - Promotion - Merely being placed on the select list for promotion does not give an officer the right to claim promotion, especially when the promotion was contingent on maintaining a satisfactory service record until actual promotion - If an officer is found guilty of misconduct, the promotion order may be withheld.
