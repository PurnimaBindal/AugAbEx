Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Promotion/Probation period/Reservation during probation - An air official who has been promoted and placed on probation can be reverted at any time during probation if their work and conduct is deemed unsatisfactory.
A.C.R.
does not need to be recorded in the case of a probationer as a routine.
In this case, the petitioner's work and conduct were found to be very bad and they were reverted during probation.
The reversion was entirely justified and is not a suitable case for interference in the writ jurisdiction.
