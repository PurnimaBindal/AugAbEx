Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Promotion - Departmental proceedings, criminal proceedings, and sealed packet promotion cannot be used unless proceedings have begun - Charge sheets do not count as pending proceedings unless they are taken up - Criminal cases are not considered pending without presenting a Challan to the court - Issuing charge sheets or registering criminal cases is not sufficient to withhold promotion by sealed packet promotion - Haryana Govt. instructions from 13.2.1962 and 18.11.71 are valid and do not contradict Supreme Court dictum in KV Jankiraman's case AIR 1991 SC 2010.
