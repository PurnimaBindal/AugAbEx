The Govt. may give promotions by relaxing rules regarding period of experience without notifying all affected persons, as per AP Revenue Subordinate Service Rules, Rules 6, 8(ii), 22(ii)(g) and 47 and Constitution of India, Article 16.
[Paras 9-12]
While basic qualifications cannot be relaxed, in individual cases, the Govt. can relax conditions of service, as per Constitution of India, Article 16(1).
One employee received promotion despite not fulfilling the required period of service, through such relaxation.
[Paras 9-12]
Rule 47 of AP State Subordinate Service Rules allows for retrospective relaxation of any rule, and this includes Rules 6 and 8(ii).
