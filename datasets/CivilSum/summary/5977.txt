According to Articles 15(1) and 15(4) of the Constitution of India, 53% of seats in Medical Colleges are reserved and cannot be challenged as exceeding the 50% limit.

The Constitution of India's Articles 15(1), 15(4), and 14 govern admission to Medical Colleges through competitive exams.
However, reducing the passing percentage for Scheduled Castes and Tribes from 45% to 40% and later to 35% to fill reserved seats is deemed invalid.
