Constitution of India, Article 226 - Reinstatement - Superannuation - The Labour Court had ordered the full reinstatement of a workman with back wages and continuity of service, but the establishment had already closed in 1995 - Therefore, the order of reinstatement could not be fulfilled - The workman will receive back wages from the date of reinstatement until the closure of the establishment - The award was upheld with the mentioned modifications.
