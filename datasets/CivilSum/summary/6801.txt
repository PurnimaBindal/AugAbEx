Constitution of India, Article 16 - Pay - Absorption - The petitioner joined the Health Department of MP Govt. as Assistant Surgeon through PSC and later joined ITBP as Junior Medical Officer Grade II.
Although, thirty Junior Medical Officers in ITBP were placed in the revised pay scale of Rs. 700-1300 w.e.f.
111973, the petitioner was not put in that grade.
The petitioner is entitled to his pay fixation in the revised grade from 111973.
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - CRPF (Medical Officers (Cadre) Rules, 1976, Rule 8(i)(b) - Seniority - The petitioner joined the Health Department of MP as Assistant Surgeon through PSC and later joined ITBP.
The petitioner exercised his option to join ITBP when option was invited.
The petitioner is entitled to seniority based on the length of service he has spent in the State of MP in his cadre.
