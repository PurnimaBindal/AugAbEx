Constitution of India, Article 16 - Rajasthan University Teachers and Officers (Selection of Appointment) Act, 1974, Sections 3(3), 4 and 5 - Ad hoc appointment - Stop gap arrangement - Appointment made on ad hoc basis for a short duration or until a regularly selected candidate joins as a stop-gap arrangement - No vested right to claim continuity in service unless services are regularised by passing a specific order - Regularisation cannot be done in the absence of a sanctioned post - Petitioners not entitled to claim any continuity in service - Appellants not entitled to claim any vested legal right of continuity in service since their appointments were made in absence of sanctioned posts - Petition dismissed.
[Paras 14 and 17]
