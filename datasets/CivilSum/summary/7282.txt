West Bengal Primary Education Act, 1973, Section 106 - Recruitment and Leave Rules, 1991, Rules 18 and 19 - Eligibility - Appointment of Head Teacher - To qualify for the post of Head Primary Teacher, a candidate must possess a Junior Basic Training Certificate or Primary Teacher Training - Petitioners with a B.Ed.
degree or BT degree argued that their degrees fulfilled the training qualification based on a previous judgement - However, Primary Teacher Training Course is specifically designed for primary level students, while B.Ed.
is for matured students - Therefore, candidates not meeting the requirements of Rule 18 cannot be selected for the post - The matter referred to a larger bench for a final decision.
[Paras 4 and 5]
Precedent - "Judgement per-incurim" - A decision is considered "per incurium" when it is given in ignorance of a statute or rule with the force of statute - The decision is not considered an authority for what it has not decided or for a point that has not been argued.
