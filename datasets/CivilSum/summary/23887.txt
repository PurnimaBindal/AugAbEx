Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 14 - Proof of wilful and deliberate contempt - Order appointing teachers against existing or future vacancies with back wages - Petitioners must show regular vacancies existed and respondents wilfully disobeyed court orders - Non-payment of back wages not wilful disobedience without court direction to pay - Contempt power should be used carefully.
