Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1063 read with Table B, Sr. No. 42 - Special pay - Clerical/Executive duty of police officers - The circular issued by the Finance Department of Pb.
Govt. on 18.10.1979 does not deprive police officers posted on clerical posts from Special Pay prescribed under Rule 1063 of PPR.
Police officers may choose to work without special pay if they so desire, but they cannot be deprived of their statutory right without being notified of their options.

Instructions - Scope of executive instructions in the field governed by statutory provisions/Rules - Executive instructions may supplement statutory Rules but cannot supplant them.
Executive orders or instructions contrary to the statute cannot overlap the field governed by statute.
