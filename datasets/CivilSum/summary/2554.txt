Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punishment/Natural justice - Hearing - Disciplinary Authority and the enquiry officer must consider matters not included in the charge sheet when determining the quantum of punishment - Previous conduct and punishments may influence the mindset of the punishing authority and affect punishment - It is therefore necessary for the punishing authority to give an opportunity for explanation before determining the quantum of punishment - Failure to do so results in violation of natural justice.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Punishment/Evidence/Disciplinary proceedings - The role of the high court in writ jurisdiction is not to review evidence like an appellate authority, but it may interfere when there is a lack of evidence for a charge deemed proved.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punishment/Appeal/Hearing - Natural justice - An employee now only has the chance to explain their viewpoint before the appellate authority after the amendment in Article 311 of the Constitution of India - The appellate authority must therefore hear the delinquent and pass a reasoned order.
