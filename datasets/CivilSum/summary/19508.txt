Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Termination of services/Natural justice/Enquiry/Appointment - Employer has the right to terminate an employee's service if they have concealed facts from their declaration at the time of entry - This clause is valid as long as the employer conducts a proper enquiry and gives the employee the opportunity to defend themselves according to the principles of natural justice.
