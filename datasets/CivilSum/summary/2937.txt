Jammu & Kashmir Cooperative Societies Act, 1960, Section 65, 66 and 104(2) - Winding up - The Government has the power to examine the record of any inquiry or proceedings of any officer under it, to ensure the legality or propriety of any order made by such officer.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Retrenchment - Employees with more than 240 days of completed service in a calendar year are entitled to reinstatement with backwages, if terminated without complying with Section 25-F of the ID Act.
[Paras 11 & 13]

Constitution of India, Art. 14, 16 and 226 - Discrimination - Equality - The court can issue a direction to extend equal treatment in cases where an equally placed person is extended a benefit, and another person is denied the same.
