Constitution of India, Article 226 - Domestic Enquiry - Dismissal - The competent authority did not consider the order of acquittal passed by the Criminal Court while passing the order of dismissal for the same charge - No show cause notice was given to the petitioner before imposing the major penalty - The impugned order is set aside and the matter is remanded to respondent No. 2 for reconsideration of imposing a penalty while giving due consideration to the order of acquittal in the criminal case.
