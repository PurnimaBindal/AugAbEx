Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Punjab - Recruitment of Ex-Servicemen Rules, 1982, Rule 4 (as amended in 1992) - Ex-Servicemen/Amended in service Rules - Appointment - Amendment in service Rules will be applied prospectively and cannot be applied to vacancies that arose before the amendment - Amendment came into effect in 1992, vacancies occurred in 1991 and posts were advertised in 1993 for Ex-Servicemen - Petitioner's status as an Ex-Serviceman covered under the definition before the amendment of Rules cannot be denied the benefit with respect to the posts vacant in 1991 as the unamended Rules will apply - The amendment will not apply retrospectively.
