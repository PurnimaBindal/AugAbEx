According to the Constitution of India, Articles 226/227, committing a serious crime or grave misconduct could result in conviction and sentence, as well as dismissal from service.
If a petitioner is convicted under Sections 302, 148, and 149 of IPC, they will face dismissal from service and may not be eligible for a compassionate allowance under Rule 41 of CCS Pension Rules, 1972.
While the petitioner may argue that the serious crime and grave misconduct explanation under Rule 8 of 1972 Rules is not applicable to them since their actions were not connected to their departmental duties, the court ruled that the crime committed by the petitioner falls within the definition of a serious crime.
