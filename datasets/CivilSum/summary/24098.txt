The Kurukshetra University Ordinance XVI stipulates that to be eligible to be selected for the position of Principal, a candidate must hold the B.Ed.
and M.Ed.
qualifications.
The academic qualifications in the advertisement must adhere to the applicable rule and ordinance, and the word 'and' in the eligibility criteria cannot be substituted with 'or' unless there are compelling reasons to do so.
Sub-clause (c) outlines the eligibility criteria for the post of Principal, where a candidate must have either a Masters in Education with B.Ed.
or a Masters degree in any subject with M.Ed.
A candidate must also fulfil the qualification in (b), which requires either an MPhil.
degree or a recognized degree beyond the Master's level or have published research-level work.
However, if a candidate with both these qualifications is unavailable or found unsuitable, a candidate fulfilling the qualifications laid down in (a) only may be selected.
Clause (c) does not give preference to a candidate with an MPhil.
or PhD but only provides an option to appoint a candidate without the qualifications specified in (b).
[Para 8]

or a Master's-level degree beyond that which may include a PhD [Paras 9 and 13]

Any proposal or resolution of the Executive Council to amend and curtail the scope of the Ordinance to limit relaxation to marks only cannot be considered an amendment of the Ordinance or a relaxation of qualifications, unless the decision is approved and implemented by the competent authority in the particular case of the individual concerned.
