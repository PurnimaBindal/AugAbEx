Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Dismissal - Reinstatement - The respondent, a conductor, failed to issue tickets to 32 passengers he collected money from.
However, the Labour Court found that the inquiry was conducted fairly and that the bus was overcrowded, causing him to be in the process of issuing tickets.
Due to these mitigating circumstances, the court ordered his reinstatement.
No further intervention is necessary.
