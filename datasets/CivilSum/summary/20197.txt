Constitution of India, Article 309 - Tamil Nadu Civil Service Rules, Rule 4 (as amended in 1972) - Promotion - Post of District Revenue Officer - The feeder post for this role is the Deputy Collector position, which can be filled through direct recruitment or promotion - Current Rules require six years of experience in the Revenue Department for direct recruits, causing an anomalous situation - To resolve this, the Court interpreted the Rule to mean that the six years of experience must be specifically as a Deputy Collector, regardless of whether the individual was promoted or directly recruited to that position.
