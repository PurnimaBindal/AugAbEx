The petitioner with a Diploma in Mechanical Engineering and ten years of experience was appointed as Assistant Engineer, but the genuineness of the diploma was disputed upon verification, which led to the cancellation of his appointment.
However, the petitioner was promoted to the substantive rank of Corporal after completing his Diploma, and a letter stated that 10 years of experience as a Sergeant with a Diploma in ME is equivalent to a degree in ME.
The High Court held that since the petitioner was entitled to a degree in ME after retiring from the Air Force, the court would not examine the details of his certificate/degree in writ proceedings, and thus allowed the petition.
