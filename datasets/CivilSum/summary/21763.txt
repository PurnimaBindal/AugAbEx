The Bombay Industrial Relations Act, 1946 and the Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Union and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1917 operate in tandem and complement each other in industries to which the former has been made applicable.
The provisions of section 21 do not allow any other union to represent employees in proceedings relating to unfair labour practices apart from those specified in items 2 and 6 of Schedule IV.
The second respondent, being the representative union, has the sole privilege of representing employees in the first respondent's industry.
