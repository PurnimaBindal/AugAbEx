When conducting a dismissal, the disciplinary authority is obligated to supply the petitioner with a copy of the report of inquiry and the reasons for disagreement on any charges.
Failure to do so is a violation of natural justice.
[Paras 4, 5 and 10]

The punishing authority must record its own findings and reasons for disagreement with the inquiry officer in a dismissal case.
Failure to provide the reasons to the affected person puts them at a disadvantage and violates the rules of natural justice.
[Para 7]
