Government instructions state that anonymous complaints against officials cannot be entertained unless the complainant is willing to provide an affidavit of the facts.
These instructions may not be mandatory, but a strong justification is necessary before deviating from them.
[Para 11]
While instructions state that preliminary enquiries should be completed within 2 months and regular enquiries within six months, this does not mean that authorities are not obligated to abide by them and can delay proceedings for years.
No explanation was given for the delay in completing the preliminary enquiry, and all the relevant documents were already in the possession of the department.
These actions were deemed prima facie mala fide, and the proceedings were quashed.
