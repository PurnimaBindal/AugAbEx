Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Disciplinary Action - Natural justice - Admittance of misconduct - Full enquiry - Punishment of removal from service granted relying on preliminary fact-finding enquiry report which included delinquent's admittance of his misconduct - No further regular enquiry is necessary when misconduct has been admitted - Departmental proceedings only need to comply with the principles of natural justice - No violation of natural justice if delinquent is given chargesheet and given opportunity to explain, lead evidence in defence, and show cause against proposed order of punishment - With serious charges admitted, removal as punishment is not excessive.
[Paras 11, 15 and 16]
