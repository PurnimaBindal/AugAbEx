The Departmental Promotion Committee is responsible for promoting individuals from the post of Assistant Commandant to the post of Second-in-Command.
In cases of promotion, the committee must objectively assess suitability and upgrade candidates from "good" to "very good".
One candidate who contended that his service record was similar to that of a promoted individual ('RSV') was directed by a Division Bench to receive promotion with retrospective effect if found suitable upon reconsideration by the DPC.
In the end, the review DPC found that the respondent did not deserve upgradation and gave detailed reasoning to support their decision.
The court ultimately upheld the decision of the review DPC, stating that interference is not warranted if the DPC has proceeded in a fair, impartial, and reasonable manner.
