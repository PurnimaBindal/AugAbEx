Article 311 of the Constitution of India states that the termination of an employee's service cannot be deemed as punitive or by way of punishment, even if allegations were made against the employee, as long as it does not attach any stigma and is only a termination simpliciter.
[Paras 5, 6 and 8]
Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act is not applicable if a workman has not completed 240 days of work before termination.
[Para 9]
A petitioner who raises a demand under Section 10(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act after 8 years has passed cannot be awarded continuity of service and back wages; thus, the award of the Labour Court is not unjustified.
[Paras 3 and 9]
