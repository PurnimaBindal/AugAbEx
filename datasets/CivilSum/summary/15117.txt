According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, in a case where the Petitioner and respondent No.5 are disputing over the post of Professor and Head of Microbiology Deptt., it is unacceptable for an officer, who has previously displayed bias and animosity towards the petitioner, to record the Annual Confidential Report (ACR).
The Petition is granted.
