Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - U.P.
Recruitment of Dependents of Govt.
Servants Dying in Harness Rules 1974, Rule 5 - Compassionate appointment - If a government servant dies in harness and their minor son applies for appointment after the expiration of limitation upon attaining majority, they are not eligible to receive appointment.
