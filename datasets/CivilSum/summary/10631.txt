Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Railway Circular No. RBE No. 30/90 dated 14290 - Increment on acquiring higher qualifications - The Railway Circular No. RBE No. 30/90 dated 14290 specifies that the increment benefit can only be availed if the higher qualifications are acquired during the service period and not on the basis of qualifications acquired prior to joining the service.
