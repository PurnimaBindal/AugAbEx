Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-B(1) & (2) - Re-employment - A workman who had an accident while on his way to work and who was hospitalized for six months.
The company refused to take him back due to no work, and the company is not obligated to provide any job even when there is no work.

Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 25-F and 2(s)(2) - Retrenchment - Compensation - Workman who did not complete 240 days before the date of termination is not entitled to the benefits of Section 25-B of the ID Act.
