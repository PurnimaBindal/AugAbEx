According to the Punjab Municipal Employees Pension and General Provident Fund Rules of 1994, an employee is entitled to pension benefits after completing ten years of regular service.
In this case, the petitioner completed ten years of service on 1.12.96 and retired on 30.6.98.
There is no evidence to suggest that the petitioner was given a choice between switching to the pension scheme or the contributory provident fund scheme.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to the benefit of a pension.
