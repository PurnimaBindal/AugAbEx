Admissions - Giving weightage to students of a particular college or making college-wise difference in weightage is discriminatory according to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
If weightage is given to all students in a state, it cannot be labeled as institutional weightage but it is regional preference, and the variance in weightage of marks within colleges in the same state is against the intended goal and reason for the policy.
Therefore, giving equal weightage to all colleges was mandated.
