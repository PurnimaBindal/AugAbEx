Petitioners who were appointed as attendants for electricians on 1141987 and 2341987 respectively had their services terminated on 1671987 on the grounds that the proposed plant was not commissioned.
The respondents did not take any action to defend the case, despite making an appearance.
There is no evidence on record that the respondents were prevented from producing the required documents before the court.
The learned Single Judge was justified in concluding that the respondents' reason for terminating the petitioners' services was incorrect, as the plant was not functional and the petitioners had not been trained as electricians.
The respondents' actions were deemed arbitrary and unreasonable.
[Paras 2 and 4]
