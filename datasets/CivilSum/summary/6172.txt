Reversion from a promotional post without any reason or opportunity after 14 years of work is not a sustainable order under Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
The doctrine of acquiescence is applied.

Reverting a direct recruit from a higher post to a lower one is deemed illegal under Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
The reversion to lower post was quashed.
