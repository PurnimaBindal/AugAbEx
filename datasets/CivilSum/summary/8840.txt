The case of the Appellant before a writ court was that he was eligible for the post of Asstt.
Engineer as he had obtained the BE Degree on the last date of receipt of applications.
Despite his eligibility, his result of selection was withheld by the Commission on untenable and unsustainable grounds, which violated his rights under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
It was held that the Appellant must be treated fairly and equally with those who were similarly situated with him in the select list, and his appeal was allowed.
It was also determined that if a candidate passes an examination, they acquire the academic qualification on the date the result is declared, notwithstanding that the result was withheld on account of some technical deficiency or mistake, and was not communicated to them before the deadline fixed.
As long as the proof of qualification is available before or on the date of the interview, the candidate cannot be deprived of the opportunity of employment, and must be deemed eligible for consideration and entitled to selection, subject to their overall performance and suitability.
The Public Service Commission was directed to declare the result of selection, to forward it to the respondent-state, and to appoint him to the post of Asstt.
Engineer on the basis of recommendation of the Commission, with all consequential service benefits.
