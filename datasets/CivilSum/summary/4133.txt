Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Qualifications - The authority responsible for appointment cannot require different qualifications from those laid out in the recruitment rules.
If other individuals with equivalent qualifications have been hired, the authority may not refuse to hire another individual with similar qualifications and cannot apply different standards to discriminate against individuals contrary to the principles of equality embodied in Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
