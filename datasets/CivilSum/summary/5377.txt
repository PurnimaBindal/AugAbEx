The petitioner opted for the Voluntary Retirement Scheme introduced by the respondent in 1995.
The effective date of retirement is when the employee is relieved, and before that, the application for voluntary retirement can be withdrawn.
The petitioner was made to work even after the cut-off date set by the Corporation, which dented the benefit available before the cut-off date.
It was held that because the Corporation failed to fulfill its commitment before the cut-off date, it would be unreasonable to deny service benefits to the employees.
