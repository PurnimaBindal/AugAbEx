Kerala Service Rules, Part III, Rule 3, Note 3 - Kerala Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1960, Rule 10 - Death-cum-retirement gratuity - Withholding - The liabilities of an employee or pensioner can be recovered from death-cum-retirement gratuity after quantification of the liability within three years of becoming a pensioner, along with a reasonable opportunity to explain - The date of becoming pensioner is a fixed factor, which is the date of attaining the age of superannuation.
