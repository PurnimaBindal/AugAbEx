Punjab State Education Class III (School Cadre) Service Rules, 1978, Rule 9, Appendix 'B' - Promotion - Seniority - Education - To promote employees from the JBT cadre to Masters, promotions cannot be made solely on the basis of joint seniority.
Available vacancies must be bifurcated subject-wise for promotion.
Authorities are reminded to adhere to the promotion quota of 15%, and evaluate the eligible candidates subject-wise as per the criteria set down in the Rules.
