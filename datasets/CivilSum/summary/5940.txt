The Constitution of India, Articles 20 and 311 - Departmental enquiry/Double jeopardy - A police officer who has been acquitted in a criminal case can still be subject to a departmental enquiry for the same charges - This is because the officer's conduct may have been unbecoming of a police officer.
[Para 3] B The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Departmental enquiry-Charge-sheet - If a charge-sheet contains allegations that cannot be considered as misconduct on the part of the accused, then the charge-sheet is illegal and invalid under relevant discipline and appeal rules.
