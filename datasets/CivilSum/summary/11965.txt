Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 25F, 33C(2) and 11-A - Misconduct/Transfer/Not Joining/Removal - Disobedience of lawful orders - Transfer not contested by petitioner - No victimisation - Disobedience of transfer orders constitutes serious misconduct - Removal from service commensurate with gravity of misconduct.
