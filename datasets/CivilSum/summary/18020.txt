The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, along with Punjab Educational Services (College Cadre) Class I Rules of 1976, were cited in a case involving a blind petitioner who had an outstanding academic record.
He had been appointed as a lecturer and was subsequently promoted to PSE Class II and then to PSE Class I.
Despite being issued a certificate praising his efficient administrative work, the position of Principal was left vacant by the DDO and the petitioner was not given a chance to try it.
Despite this, he was later reverted from PSE Class I to PSE Class II due to presumptions that his handicappedness would prevent him from performing administrative duties as a principal.
This reversion was ruled to be unfounded and based on unjustifiable assumptions, especially since the petitioner had never been given the opportunity to prove himself as a principal.
