Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25(O)(5) - Closure of establishment - Permission of State Government - Review of decision - Time limit - An order denying permission to close an establishment under Section 25(O)(5) is valid only for one year - If an application for review of this order is filed, it must be done within one year and the order must also be passed within that period - If not, the review application is rendered useless after one year of the original order - The State Government cannot review the order after one year - The employer can file a fresh application under Section 25(O)(5) after one year of the previous order.
[Paras 29, 31, 32, 34 and 35] B.
Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 25(O)(5) and Section 10(1) - Reference - Any application for permission to close an industrial unit has to be made under Section 25(O)(5), not Section 10(1) - Even if the reference is made under both sections, the application under Section 25(O)(5) must be decided without the assistance of Section 10(1) of the Act.
