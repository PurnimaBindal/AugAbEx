Constitution of India, Articles 311, 14 and 226 - Misconduct - Dismissal from service - The appellants, who were members of the police force, marched in a peaceful procession in defiance of a prohibitory order to bring attention to their genuine grievance regarding inadequate housing.
This behavior does not constitute misconduct deserving the severe penalty of dismissal from service.
Instead, a punishment of the stoppage of one increment without cumulative effect was imposed.
