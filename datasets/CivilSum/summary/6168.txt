An employee who has been promoted based on seniority and confirmed in the position cannot be reverted without proper opportunity for hearing, according to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 6]  B After a court quashed an order changing the seniority of a petitioner and allowed for fresh orders with afforded opportunity, no action was initiated for 15 years.
During that time, the petitioner was promoted and confirmed on higher posts.
The government cannot unfairly revert the petitioner by altering their seniority after such a significant delay.
