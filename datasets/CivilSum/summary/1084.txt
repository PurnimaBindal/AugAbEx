Constitution of India, Article 226 - Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1978, Sections 4(6), 16 - Maharashtra Employees of Private School (Conditions of Service) Regulation Rules, 1981, Rule 28(1) - Termination of service - Post of Assistant Teacher - Termination of service of candidates appointed on a purely temporary basis for one year, on roster points of posts reserved for backward classes, is not illegal as long as it is done after giving one month's notice in accordance with Rule 28(1).
[Para 6]

Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1978, Sections 4(6) and 16 - Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Rules, 1981, Rule 9(a) - Appointment - Reservation - Termination - Post of Assistant Teacher - If a candidate belonging to SC category is appointed in a vacancy reserved for OBC category as no OBC candidate is available, her appointment in the vacancy of OBC is deemed to be on a regular basis as per rule 9(a).
Thus, she cannot be appointed for one year and terminated thereafter on the basis that she was purely temporary.
The order of termination is quashed.
[Paras 3 and 5]

Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1978, Sections 4(6) and 16 - Termination service - Post of Assistant Teacher - The order of termination from service of a petitioner appointed as Secondary School Teacher, and not in Primary Section in 1992 in clear vacancy, is illegal and improper.
Her name and signature appear on progress books of Secondary School Student countersigned by Head Master of High School.
Therefore, she is entitled to be reinstated as a Secondary School Teacher with continuity of service and all consequential benefits.
