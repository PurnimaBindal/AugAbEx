Article 16 of the Indian Constitution and Section 47 of the Civil Procedure Code of 1908 address pay and qualification requirements.
In this case, plaintiffs filed a suit claiming entitlement to a pay scale revision after improving their qualifications as per government instructions.
The suit was decreed by the trial court and as the State did not file an appeal, the decree became final.
The State Government initially implemented the pay scale revisions, but later withdrew them and began recovery from the plaintiffs.
The trial court issued directions for all benefits to be given and the executing court upheld these directions in impugned orders, with the exception that the decree-holders would only be entitled to arrears for 38 months prior to filing their suit.
