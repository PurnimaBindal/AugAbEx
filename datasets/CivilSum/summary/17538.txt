As specified in the Punjab Recruitment of Ex-servicemen Rules, 1982, Rule 4 on Appointment and Selection, an employee working in the Armed Forces expected to discharge/retire within a year is to be regarded as an ex-serviceman.
However, the petitioner was not considered as an ex-serviceman or against the general category.
In any event, he should have been assessed for the general category post.
The respondents agreed to interview the petitioner and if found suitable, must be granted an appointment against the available reserved category post or, in the general category above the candidate below his merit, from the date of his discharge from Armed Forces with all benefits, salary arrears excluded.
