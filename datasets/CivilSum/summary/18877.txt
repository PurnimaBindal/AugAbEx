Constitution of India, Article 21 - Punjab National Bank's Scheme for compassionate appointment dated 2031997, Clause 10 - Compassionate Appointment - Family pension, gratuity, income of family from other sources, employment of family members, size of the family and liabilities etc. are taken into account to determine the family's condition - If the family has enough income to sustain, the Bank is not required to provide compassionate appointment.
