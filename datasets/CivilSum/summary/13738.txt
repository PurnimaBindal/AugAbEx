Under Rule 9 of J&K Civil Service Regulations Vol. II and Article 177 of J&K Civil Service Regulations Vol.
I, an employee who serves less than five years as quasi-permanent and is not confirmed is not eligible for pension.
However, if a government servant serves continuously for more than three years, he is declared quasi-permanent according to Rule 3(1) of J&K Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1961.
The petitioner in this case has served for 13 years and retired after being regularized as quasi-permanent on a substantive post.
As per Article 31(1) of the Constitution, an employee's right to pension is considered a right to property and cannot be denied easily or on technicalities.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to pension in accordance with Article 177 of J&K Civil Service Regulations Volume I [Paras 5, 6, 7, 9, 10]
