Constitution of India, Article 311 - Petitioner dismissed from service - Departmental enquiry - Allegation against petitioner of destroying a government-owned horse shed - Departmental enquiry conducted and petitioner dismissed from service - Dismissal from service was set aside by Tribunal and replaced with compulsory retirement - It is the responsibility of the Disciplinary Authority or Appellate Authority to reconsider the punishment imposed, except in exceptional cases where the punishment is shockingly disproportionate and the court may impose a proportionate punishment to expedite litigation, provided that cogent reasons are given - Tribunal's decision to reduce the punishment was valid and based on sound reasoning.
