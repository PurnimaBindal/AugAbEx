Rule 153(8) of the Railway Protection Force Rules, 1987, held that even if representation through an agent is not granted, there is no illegality or unconstitutionality.
Additionally, refusal to grant representation by a lawyer does not infringe on the principles of natural justice, and in a domestic/departmental enquiry, the accused must defend themselves.
[Paras 8, 10 and 11]

In case of misconduct, representation by a lawyer is not permitted in a departmental enquiry.
Such an inquiry is not a criminal trial or a suit where the party has the right to be represented by a lawyer.
[Para 8]
