According to Sections 2(12) and 2(14AA) of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948 and Section 2(k) of the Factories Act, the definition of "factory" includes the repairing of equipment if it is intended to be used in the hospital.
The Equipment Maintenance Department of the Medical College of the University repairs hospital equipment with 45 employees working there.
Therefore, the department is liable to pay the contribution premium under the ESI Act, and the test of dominant nature does not apply since the department does not claim application of the Act to the entire institution/Hospital.
