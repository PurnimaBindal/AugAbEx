Constitution of India, Article 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination of services - Petitioner's workman was terminated due to termination of contractor’s contract and unavailability of licence- Petitioner argued that his employees couldn't be considered as principal employer's employees even after the winding-up of the contractor company and the nonexistence of their licence - Employees cannot be automatically absorbed as employees of the principal employer - Daily rated or ad hoc or casual employee could not be regularised - the Court decided that workman could not be considered as Govt.'s employees and thus not entitled to protection of Section 25-F of the Act.
