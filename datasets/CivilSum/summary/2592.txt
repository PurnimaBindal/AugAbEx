Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Reservations in Appointments - Two contenders for the post of Naib Tehsildar reserved for Ex-Servicemen - According to Govt instructions, preference for the post of Naib Tehsildar goes to those Ex-servicemen disabled between 20-50% first, then up to two dependents of service personnel who are killed/disabled beyond 50%, and lastly to other Ex-servicemen - Even if a male issue has income from personal property, he will be considered dependent if he benefits from his father's income - Respondent No. 4 falls under Category II while the petitioner is in Category III - Petition has been dismissed.
