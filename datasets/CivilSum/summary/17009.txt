Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Sports events - Sports grading - The Director of Sports must take into account a candidate's participation in sports events during their internship training when issuing gradation certificates, as it is an integral part of the academic course for MBBS candidates.
Additionally, seats in postgraduate MD/MS courses may be allotted based on sports gradation certificates earned during the MBBS academic course, including the internship period.
