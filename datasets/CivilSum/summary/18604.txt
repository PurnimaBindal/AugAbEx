Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Claiming promotion to a vacant position - Mere existence of vacancy does not automatically entitle an employee to seek promotion to it - It is at the discretion of the employer to either fill the vacancy or keep it unfilled - The employer has the authority to determine its own requirements - Unless the rule explicitly mandates filling the vacancy, the employee has no right to demand promotion, unless it is a case of denied equality of opportunity or promotion of juniors.
