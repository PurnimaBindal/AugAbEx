Constitution of India, Article 309 - Rajasthan Education Subordinate Service Rules, 1971, Rule 1 - Appointment - Post of Lab Assistant - Select list publication - Failure to offer appointment - Justification of post being surplus - According to the 1971 Rules, all appointments for the post of Lab Assistant must be based purely on merit - It was decided that the appellant's selection by the selection committee headed by DEO could not be withheld for administrative purposes or deemed surplus without amending the Rules - The decision to not offer appointment to the appellant was malicious - The respondents were therefore instructed to appoint the appellant in accordance with the select list.
