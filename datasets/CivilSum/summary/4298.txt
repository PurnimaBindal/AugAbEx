Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2(1) - Misconduct/Absence from duty/Gravest misconduct - Police service - Discipline in service - Absence from duty - Petition - A constable in police did not show up for duty for a period of 5 months and 5 days - The police service, being a disciplined profession, must maintain discipline - A lack of discipline leads to the erosion of discipline itself - This can have serious consequences for maintaining law and order - A single grave instance of misconduct constitutes the gravest misconduct - The Supreme Court has not endorsed the view taken in Chanan Singh's case which is no longer valid - The petition has been dismissed.
