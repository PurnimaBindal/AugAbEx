Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dismissal from service - Departmental Proceedings - A petitioner who worked as a Scientific Officer in BARC was found to have unsatisfactory work and conduct and was transferred.
However, he did not comply with the order of transfer and unauthorisedly entered the laboratory.
Departmental proceedings were initiated against him and he was served with a charge-sheet and notices of hearing.
Despite this, he did not show up and the allegations made against him were proven.
There was no evidence to prove mala fides and thus, the order of dismissal from service was upheld.
[Paras 25, 27 and 28]
