Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delhi School Education Act - Delhi School Education Rules, 1973, obtaining Rule 117(b)(iv) - Dismissal - Enquiry - Supply of documents - The petitioner did not appear before the Enquiry Officer but still the enquiry was concluded against the petitioner ex-parte despite repeated opportunities given to him.
The petitioner only asked for inspection of documents when he was given a show cause notice.
Even at that stage petitioner was given opportunity to inspect the document but he failed to do so.
Thus, there is no illegality in the order of dismissal.
