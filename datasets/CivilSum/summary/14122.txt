Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 121 and 139 (as applicable to Haryana) - Compulsory retirement - Validity of order - A sub-inspector was compulsorily retired from service by Superintendent of Police - The petitioner contended that Rule 121 stipulated that only a Deputy Inspector General was authorized to pass an order of retirement - The court ruled that the order of compulsory retirement was invalid since according to the rules, the appointing authority was the Deputy Inspector General - As a result, the petition was granted.
