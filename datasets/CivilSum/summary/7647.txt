Punjab Homeopathic Practitioner Act, 1965 - Homeopathy Central Council Act, 1973, Sections 7(2), 13 - Increase of intake capacity of Medical institution - The authority to permit an increase or reduction in the sanctioned strength of students lies solely with the Central Council - In cases of conflict between the State and Central Legislation, the provisions of the Central Act will take precedence - The Central Council is responsible for setting the conditions for granting recognition to medical colleges, including determining their intake capacity - Once an approval is granted by the Central Council, the State Council cannot deny the same.
