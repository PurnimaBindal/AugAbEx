The Constitution of India, 1950, specifically Articles 14 and 16 establish the Principle of Merger.
According to Service Law, the appellants were granted service benefits from the presentation of the writ application, not from any earlier date, as directed by the Learned Single Judge of the High Court.
The appellants accepted this order, and as they did not appeal to any other higher authority, it now attains finality.
As a result, the appellants cannot claim any new relief solely on the observation made by the Division Bench at a later stage.
