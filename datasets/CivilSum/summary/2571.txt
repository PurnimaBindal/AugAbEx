Termination of service of a teacher in violation of provisions of the Andhra Pradesh Education Act, 1982 can be challenged under Article 226 of the Constitution, regardless of whether the institution's management is unaided or private.
Relevant articles and sections include Articles 30, 31, and 226, as well as Sections 24, 28, and 31 of the act.
[Paras 12, 14, and 16]

The management of a private educational institution must obtain prior approval from the competent/higher authority before terminating an employee, regardless of whether the termination is penal or non-penal.
This requirement is outlined in the Andhra Pradesh Education Act, 1982, Section 79, and is supported by the Constitution of India, Articles 30 and 226.
