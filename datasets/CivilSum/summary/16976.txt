Constitution of India, Article 226 - Armed Forces Personnel (Reservations of Vacancies in the Punjab State Non-Technical Services) Rules, 1968, Rule 5 - Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rule 4 - Seniority - Military Service - Benefit - The Petitioner claimed benefits of seniority and increment in accordance with the 1965 rules which consider military service towards increment, seniority and pension - The Petitioner served in Army from 1964 to 1979 before being selected as Inspector in Punjab Co-operative Bank - The Respondent contested that the Petitioner did not qualify for the Inspector position at the time of joining the Military - The Court held that the Petitioner acquired eligibility in 1979 and therefore not entitled for benefits of military service - Petition dismissed accordingly.
