Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16(4) - Reservation - Roster point - The petitioner was selected and appointed as a Lecturer against a reserved roster point in the roster of 63 posts.
Five roster points are reserved for backward classes candidates in 63 posts in the 11% reserved quota.
However, the appointment letter of the petitioner was withdrawn treating one point in Group A, reserved for Scheduled Castes candidates.
The action of the respondents was deemed wrong and unjustified and they were directed to offer the petitioner the first available vacancy for backward class.
[Paras 5 and 6]
