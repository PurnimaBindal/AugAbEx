In accordance with Constitution of India Article 226, Disability Pension, the petitioner underwent a medical test before entering into army service and no mention was made of any disability.
There is no indication that the petitioner's current disability could not be detected at the time of enrollment, therefore the petitioner is eligible for disability pension.
The respondent authorities are advised to grant the disability pension to the petitioner.
[Paras 5 and 6] B.
According to the Pension Regulations for Army 1961, Regulation 179, military personnel are eligible for disability pension even if they are discharged or retired upon completion of service.
