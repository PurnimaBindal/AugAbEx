Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation - Appointment after selection - Those selected in general category cannot challenge the appointments of those selected in reserved categories.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment after selection - Merely being selected does not guarantee appointment unless it is proven that a candidate of lesser merit was appointed.
[Paras 6 and 8]
Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14 and 16 - Necessary parties - Appointment after selection - Those claiming appointment against already filled posts must include the appointees as parties for the writ to be maintainable.
[Para 8]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Fraud in selection - If authorities discover fraudulent or dishonest actions by any of the selection's candidates or officials, necessary action must be taken without waiting for orders from the court.
