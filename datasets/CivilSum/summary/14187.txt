The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, specifically Sections 11 and 25-F, pertain to termination without notice.
In a case where a workman completed 240 days but was terminated without notice, management claimed the workman willingly stopped attending.
However, the Tribunal did not believe this claim and ordered reinstatement with full back wages.
The officer of the workmen confirmed the workman had worked for over 240 days, and management failed to provide any evidence.
As such, the Tribunal's decision was upheld.
