Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 3 - Compensation - Claimant asserts deceased was working with corporation during time of accident - Evidence from claimant and co-worker of deceased proves that deceased was on vehicle driven by corporation officer during work - Name of deceased not being found in NMR, document produced by corporation not detrimental to claim - Compensation properly awarded to claimant based on preponderance of probability.
