Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination of Service - Natural Justice - The petitioner, who is a teacher, faced charges of unauthorized absence, involvement in private tuition, and misbehavior with students - However, the petitioner was deemed guilty without proper inquiry in accordance with natural justice principles - Hence, the termination order is nullified ab initio - Petitioner reinstated with 50% back wages.
