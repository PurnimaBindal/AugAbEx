In a suit for recovery, the plaintiff had not been paid his wages for several months, including October 1990 and February 1991, and was not paid for three days in March 1990 and five days in June 1990.
The trial court decreed the suit and ordered 12% interest on unpaid salary, but the first appeal was dismissed.
The absence of the plaintiff during the unpaid periods is not confirmed, and no notice had been issued regarding his absence.
Only the evidence of DW-1 provides confirmation of the plaintiff's absence.
The attendance register was not produced, and his salary for October 1990 was paid after a year and a half.
The plaintiff was entitled to interest for the delayed payment.
Finally, it was the responsibility of the concerned authority to obtain a sanction before his term expired.
Consequently, no interference is needed, and the appeal is dismissed.
