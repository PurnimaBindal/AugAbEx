Section 69 of the Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961 states that the government and the Registrar have the authority to review any decision or order made by a co-operative society.
This includes the legality and propriety of an employee's removal, which can be appealed to the Appellate Authority.
In this case, the decision to remove the employee was overturned by the Appellate Authority, but the society filed a revision with the government.
[Para 9]  B According to Section 69 of the Punjab Cooperative Societies Act, 1961, a revision petition against the Appellate Authority's decision is maintainable.
