Petitioner Mr. KL Arora, Advocate and Respondent P.C.
Chaudhary Advocate are involved in a case pertaining to the regularisation of service under Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 16.
Petitioner Nos. 1, 3, 6 and 7 have worked for more than five years while others have worked for less than five years prior to filing of present writ petition.
The petitioners argue that they deserve to be regularised in service based on a judgment of SC in State of Haryana v. Piara Singh, 1992(3) SCT 201 and instructions issued by Govt.
The respondents are directed to consider the case of regularisation of other petitioners as per judgment of SC and instructions issued by Govt. and if found eligible.
