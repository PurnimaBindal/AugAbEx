According to Section 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a workman can only be terminated from their service if they completed less than 240 days of service in a 12 month period prior to their termination.
However, this does not mean that a workman is not entitled to benefits if they complete 240 days of service in less than 11 months.
The Labour Court made a mistake in their interpretation of counting 240 days prior to the date of termination and wrongly declined relief to the petitioner who had completed 261 days of service.
As a result, the petitioner has been directed to be re-employed with 50% backwages since no notice or charges were given and juniors were retained.
