Constitution of India, Article 136 - Pension - The Supreme Court invalidated the order of Tribunal that granted pension benefits to the applicant, given the principle of gratuity, due to AIR 1990 SC 1782.
The interlocutory application by the appellant for recalling the order was dismissed since the grounds for the recall were found to be false by record, and it was also hard to believe that he had no notice of exercising the option for pensionary benefits.
The order for recalling the order was also found to be legal.
[Para 2]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Interlocutory application - The interlocutory application by the appellant for recalling the order was dismissed as the petitioner was expected to make note of the subsequent dates and make himself available and appear when the case was called out, after an intimation for adjournment was given.
