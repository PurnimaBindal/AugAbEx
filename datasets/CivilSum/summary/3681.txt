According to Section 4 Ordinance XX-B of the Delhi University Act (8 of 1922), the Principal of a college can only be suspended by the competent authority and not by the governing body of Desh Bandhu College, even though the latter has the power to appoint the Principal.
It has been ruled that the suspension of the Principal by the governing body without prior approval of the Executive Council is improper.
