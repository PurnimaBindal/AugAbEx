Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226, 227 and 309 - Age of superannuation - Change in service conditions - The appellant worked for a company from 1947 where age of superannuation was 60 years.
The company was taken over by the State Govt. in 1955.
The State Government Employees are bound by age of superannuation 58 years, but the appellant has the right to continue in service until the age of 60 years since the State Service Rules will not be applicable to him.
