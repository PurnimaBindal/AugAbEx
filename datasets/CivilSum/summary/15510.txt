According to the Constitution of India, in Articles 12, 310, and 226, an employee who decides to resign from Government service for the purpose of contesting an election is not entitled to rejoin the service.
This decision is considered final, and the resignation cannot be withdrawn.
The desire to contest an Assembly election is not a good enough reason for an employee to resign from service.
Additionally, the instructions provided by the Haryana Government also do not dictate that an employee who leaves service to contest an election should be taken back in service.
[Paras 2-6]
