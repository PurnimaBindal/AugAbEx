Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(s) and 25F - Apprentices Act, 1961, Section 18 - Definition of `Workman' and `Apprentice' - Dispute regarding termination of services of Apprentice referred to Labour Court - Decision made on whether an `apprentice' is a `workman' under Section 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act - It was ruled that an `apprentice' is not considered a `workman' under the Industrial Disputes Act - The Labour Court was incorrect in reinstating the apprentice trainee on the basis that his services were terminated in non-compliance with Section 25F of the ID Act - The provisions of the ID Act do not extend to an `apprentice' - The award of the Labour Court is overturned.
