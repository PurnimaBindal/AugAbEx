Constitution of India, Article 14 - Karnataka Medical Colleges and Dental Colleges (Selection for Admission to Post - Graduate Courses) Rules, 1989, Rules 7 and 11 - Education/Admission - Selection for admission - Selection is determined based on merit in the Entrance Test.
If a selected candidate fails to join within the specified time and a subsequent vacancy occurs, the Committee has arbitrary power to consider any candidate, even those not selected for any course, regardless of their merit.
However, the latter part of Rule 11 that empowers the Committee to exclude candidates already selected on merit for any course violates Article 14 of the Constitution and is therefore declared ultra vires.
