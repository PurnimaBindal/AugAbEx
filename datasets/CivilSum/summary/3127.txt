The selection process for the position of Reader at Iqbal Institute of Kashmir University was conducted through an interview, which both the petitioner and respondent No. 6 participated in.
The petitioner filed a writ petition prematurely, as no appointment had been made yet, apprehending that respondent No. 6 had been recommended.
However, there was no evidence to support this claim.
The court found the petitioner's contentions of deliberate postponement of the interview and mala fides to be too vague.
As the determination of eligibility and merit during the final selection process falls under the domain of the university, subsequent challenges to the validity of the interview were not maintainable due to the Rule of Estoppel.
The petition was dismissed.
