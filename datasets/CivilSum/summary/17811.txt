Factories Act, 1948, Section 2(m) defines "factory" as any premises where a manufacturing process takes place within its precincts.
B The Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25L(a) requires proof that a corporation has premises and carries out a manufacturing process to be considered an "industrial establishment."
C. "Manufacturing process" is a wide-ranging term that must be proven by evidence led by parties to determine if a particular factory is carrying it out, and the opportunity to lead evidence to establish the fact is needed.
D. If an industrial establishment has more than one industrial unit that is severable, each unit is deemed a separate industrial establishment.
E. For retrenchment under Chapter V-B, Section 25-N of the Industrial Disputes Act, term "industrial establishment" means only a factory as defined under Section 2(m) of Factories Act.
F Agricultural activities, sale and purchase, and land reclamation by a corporation do not necessarily make it an "industrial establishment" carrying out a "manufacturing process" to bring it within the definition of "Factory" under Section 2(m) of Factories Act, and such questions of fact can only be proven by adducing evidence.
Parties must seek remedies under the law.
