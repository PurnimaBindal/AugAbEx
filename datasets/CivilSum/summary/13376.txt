Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 300-A - Disability pension - The petitioner, who was boarded out of military service due to eye disease with a 40% disability assessment, had his claim for pension rejected because CCDA(P) Allahabad determined his disability was less than 20%.
The findings of the Invaliding Medical Board must be accepted based on the physical examination of the patient.
The petitioner was directed to receive disability pension based on the 40% disability assessment by the Invaliding Medical Board.
