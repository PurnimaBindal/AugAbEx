Jawaharlal Nehru University Act, 1966, Section 13(2), Ordinance 14, Clause 12 - Special examination prior to due dates - The Chairman of the Centre had a bias towards one candidate, and the Academic Council of the University invalidated the test and withheld the results as per the rules- Candidates need to qualify in both sessional performance and examination results of each semester - The candidate completed the 10th semester in 1992 while the sessional performance was from 1991 - The Academic Council's decision was considered correct - The Academic Council contemplated the possibility of providing relaxation on compassionate grounds, but such a decision will not be a precedent.
