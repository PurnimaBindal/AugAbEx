Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(1) and 12(5) - Reference - Industrial dispute - A workman claimed that his services were unfairly terminated after 11 years without being given a chance - The government declined the reference and stated that the workman abandoned his job - However, the government's decision goes against established legal principles - The government cannot determine an industrial dispute based on merits - Therefore, they are directed to reconsider the case based on settled law.
