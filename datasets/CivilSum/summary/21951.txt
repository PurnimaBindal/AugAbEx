Constitution of India, Article 136 - Grant of OBC Certificate - Creamy layer - Appellant's father is class II officer - Father's salary is only source of income - Government instructions specify that salary income is not considered for OBC reservation - Therefore, father's gross salary is irrelevant for granting OBC benefit - Deputy Commissioner's denial of OBC certificate and High Court's judgment upholding denial set aside - Appellant entitled to OBC certificate.
