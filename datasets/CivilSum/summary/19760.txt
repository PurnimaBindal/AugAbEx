As per Section 12 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, in the case of a workman's death, the principal employer is responsible for compensation.
In this case, the corporation hired a workman through a contractor, and the workman sustained injuries while repairing their store, which is directly linked with the activities of the corporation.
Therefore, the corporation is eligible for indemnification by the contractor.
