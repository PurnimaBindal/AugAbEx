Constitution of India, Article 226 - Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Rules, 1981, Rules, 26(2)(iii) and 26(5) - Retrenchment - Absorption - Repatriation - The petitioners, who were working as teachers in a school run by Respondent No. 2 Society, were wrongly declared as surplus and absorbed in schools run by another society, but subsequently repatriated to their parent school.
This all resulted from incorrect factual reporting, as vacancies were already available in the schools run by respondent No. 2 Society itself.
The assertion on the part of the petitioner that there was a violation of their right under Rule 26(5) is unacceptable, as no hearing was liable to be given before taking action for sending them back to their original school.
The principle of review is not applicable in such cases, and the education officer was perfectly justified in cancelling his own order after having realized the error.
The principles of review are not applicable to an administrative action.
[Paras 10, 11, 14 and 14]
