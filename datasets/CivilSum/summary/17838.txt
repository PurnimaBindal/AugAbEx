Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Premature Retirement/Adverse reports/Natural justice/Uncommunicated adverse reports - The adverse reports that were taken into consideration for prematurely retiring the petitioner were conveyed after his retirement - It is not permissible to consider uncommunicated reports - Such action is violative of principles of natural justice - The order was set aside by the single judge and the appeal was dismissed - The decision was in line with AIR 1987 SC 948 precedent.
