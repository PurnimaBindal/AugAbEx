Constitution of India, Article 226 - Reinstatement - Increments - Upon reinstatement, all increments were granted to the petitioner as if she had continued in service from 141976.
The respondent's position that the petitioner is not entitled to count the interregnum period for the purpose of increments, since she had not physically worked during that time, was held invalid.
It was ruled that if the petitioner is notionally deemed to be in service from 141976, all consequences except arrears up to the date of reinstatement should follow.
The petitioner would, therefore, be entitled to all the increments, both notionally up to the date of reinstatement and actually after that date.
A direction was given to keep releasing grants in aid against the petitioner's post as per the calculated pay.
