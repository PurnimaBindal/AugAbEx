Kerala State and Subordinate Services Rules, Rules 14 and 17-A and Article 16(4) of the Indian Constitution govern reservation for Scheduled Castes for Class I, III, and IV positions in relation to recruitment by the university syndicate.
The advertisement for direct recruitment to the reserved category was authorized and made in accordance with all rules, and the selection was conducted by a selection committee.
The advertisement not stating that it was a special recruitment is immaterial, and the selection is lawful.
