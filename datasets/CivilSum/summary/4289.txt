The petitioner was dismissed from service but the District Judge set aside the order of dismissal and deemed the suspension order to have continued in force until further orders.
The petitioner was ordered to be removed from service and filed an appeal and writ.
The challenge to the validity of the suspension order was belated and the petitioner is estopped from raising it.
The fact that the suspension was ordered from a different date did not affect the disciplinary proceedings.
The petition was dismissed.
[Para 7]  B The validity of the Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1957 was upheld.
[Para 11]  C. The revival of the old order of suspension made no difference to the disciplinary proceedings conducted against the petitioner, who had shown complete lack of discipline and remained absent from duty.
There is no ground to interfere with the impugned orders.
The petition was dismissed.
