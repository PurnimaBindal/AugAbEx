Constitution of India, Article 309 - Constitution of India, Article 16 - Service Matter - Recruitment - "Recruitment action" - Recruitment could mean any method of induction in public service such as appointment, selection, promotion, or deputation.
The appropriate legislature can make rules to regulate recruitment to public services and post but these rules should comply with the other provisions of the Constitution.
