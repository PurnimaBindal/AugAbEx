The promotion and retirement of army officers must adhere to the procedures outlined in the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311, as well as the Army Act of 1950 and Army Rules, Rule 13-A.
To terminate an officer's service, a show cause notice followed by a second opportunity to submit a representation to the government is mandatory.
Termination without providing this opportunity is invalid and poses a risk to the army's adherence to the law.
[Paras 13 and 19]

Delegation of authority in matters of compulsory retirement and show cause notices is not permissible unless specifically outlined in the rules.
The Legislature's objective in conferring the authority to retire an officer lies in utilizing the experience and wisdom of the senior-most authority.
Laws cannot be violated by usurping this authority through delegation.
[Paras 14, 17, and 19]

When an officer opts for a specific line of service and is recommended for promotion or posting by his commanding officer, he should not be removed merely for failing to pass a test in another line of service.
Removing an eligible and competent officer without legal justification violates Rule 13-A and other relevant laws.
[Paras 18 and 19]
