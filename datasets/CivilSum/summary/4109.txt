Army Rules, 1956, Rules 34(1), 115(2) - Nature of Rules 34 and 115 - The statute indicates that compliance with Rule 34 is mandatory - A warrant must be scrutinized to determine if there is any prejudice - Rule 115 was violated when the accused was not given sufficient time to prepare his defense and his confession was recorded - Both Rules 34 and 115 are mandatory.

Army Rules, 1956 - Rules 34 and 115 - Constitution of India, 1950 - Articles 14 and 226 - Punishment - Quantum - Interference - This court lacks the authority to interfere with the imposed punishment since it is not an appeals court - Interference is only justified for correcting errors of law or procedure.
