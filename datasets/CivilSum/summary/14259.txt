The Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act of 1970, Sections 2(b), 10(1) - This Act prohibits the use of contract labour and a notification dt. 8994 forbids establishments from using contract labour for building surveillance.
The absence of security guards in the notification does not negate their rights under the Act.

The Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act of 1970, Section 2(b) - After a worker is initially hired through a contractor, if the employer starts directly hiring on a term basis due to the abolition of the contract labour system, the worker does not lose their right to claim benefits under the Act.

Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India - Contract of service - Unfair contract conditions contrary to rules - A worker accepting conditions at the time of joining to avoid unemployment does not have an equal bargaining position with the employer and cannot be denied statutory rights.
A contract signed by the worker waiving the conditions of the Corporation's Standing Orders is unfair, arbitrary, and unconstitutional.
[Para 11]
Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India - Contract of service - The contract of service containing fixed wages but not a pay scale - Age of superannuation - The contract applies only to fixed wages as all other service conditions are governed by Rules applicable to all Corporation employees, including the age of superannuation.
Petitioners are entitled to continue working until the age of 60 as prescribed by Rules.
