Constitution of India, Article 226 - Misappropriation of funds - A Railway TTI was found with Rs. 40 short in cash during the journey.
The total receipt was required to be deposited at the final destination of the train.
It cannot be said that there was temporary misappropriation of money.
One passenger claimed that he had paid Rs. 20 to the respondent and did not receive a receipt.
However, during cross-examination, the passenger stated that he did not attempt to give illegal gratification of Rs. 20 to the respondent.
No presumption can be made that the amount was given by way of illegal gratification.
[Paras 8 and 9]
