Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admissions - Qualifications - The cut off date for fulfilling necessary qualifications is the last date for submission of applications.
Subsequently acquired qualifications will not make the applicant eligible unless the competent authority relaxes the requirements.
However, the court cannot direct the authority to relax eligibility conditions based solely on past relaxation.
[Paras 17, 18, 19 and 21]
