The Bihar Assistants of the Secretariat and Attached Offices Joint Cadre Ordinance, 1989, Section 9, regarding promotion, is not a violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
The policy decision of the Cabinet decisions dated May 27, 1967, which relates to promotions of Assistants to higher posts and their confirmation in various Secretariat Departments and attached offices, was not translated into a rule.
The mandamus issued by the High Court compelled implementation, and once the decision of May 27, 1967, was effected by the Ordinance/Act, the question of implementing that decision did not survive.
To protect the promotions made between 1967 to 1988 and to overcome the difficulty arising from court orders, Section 9 was enacted.
The judgment of the High Court under challenge does not suffer from any infirmity requiring interference in appeal, and the appeal is dismissed.
