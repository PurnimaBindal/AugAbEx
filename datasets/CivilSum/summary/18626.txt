For promotion, educational qualifications and subjects of education are important.
However, once the prescribed qualifications are met, the subjects in which the candidate passed become immaterial unless it is specifically stated as a condition.
A petitioner who has passed matric but failed in English cannot be deemed non-matric simply because of his failure in English.
Even if he passes English as an additional subject, this objection cannot be raised to revoke his qualification status.
[Paras 4 and 5]
