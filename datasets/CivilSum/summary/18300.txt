Private respondents' Counsel, Mr. PM Kansal, referred to Railway Establishment Manual, Volume I, Rule 124 regarding the recruitment and selection process for the Post of Goods Guard.
The rule states that 15% of the posts are reserved for direct recruitment through open competition.
Since the chances of promotion are low, employees are given the opportunity to compete for direct recruitment.
The selection criteria is limited to existing graduate employees of railways.
The upper age limit for general category candidates was increased to 40 years and 45 years for SC/ST categories candidate.
Any employee can appear in the limited departmental examination based on the decision of the Railway Board.
This decision is not illegal.
The 15% quota applies only to candidates who are not eligible for promotion under the 85% quota.
Any order by the General Manager limiting the selection to only the Operating Branch, contrary to the Railway Board's order, should be ignored.
