According to Statute No. 32 of the Pondicherry University Statutes under the Pondicherry University Act, 1985, the Academic Council has the power to review affiliation applications and establish an inspection committee.
The inspection committee must submit a report to the Academic Council, which then verifies that all conditions for affiliation are met before submitting the application to the Executive Council.
The decision made by the Executive Council is final.
A committee consisting of the Chief Minister, Vice-Chancellor, and applicant is not authorized to review affiliation applications or create a committee to review them under the University's statutes.
The minutes of such a committee are not legally binding, and the University cannot be directed to enforce its decision.
