The respondent No. 2 was appointed as Lecturer of Music (Vocal) on a contract basis with a fixed salary on August 1, 2002, but was later relieved of duty on October 4, 2002.
She was allowed to rejoin on January 16, 2003, and continued performing her duties until her contract ended on July 28, 2003, because of surplus staff.
It was held that a contractual appointment for a short period does not give rise to any substantive rights in favor of an employee, and the respondent never worked on the TGT post for which some vacancies were available.
The tribunal's directions to consider her for appointment in the lower grade of TGT were set aside.
