Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Rule 105 - Promotion from the Rank of Subedar Major to Senior Subedar - Eligibility - Promotion to Senior Subedar is granted upon passing the Senior Subedar's Refresher Course.
There is no percentage of marks required, however, instructions issued recommend 45% in the written test and practical, as well as the aggregate.
There are no instructions issued regarding minimum qualifying marks in the interview.
If a candidate secures 45% in the written and practical, their results must be declared, regardless of their marks in the interview.
