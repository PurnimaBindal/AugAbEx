In Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, recovery applications are only maintainable for pre-existing benefits obtained from pre-existing rights and not from just and fair considerations.
Only the former falls under the jurisdiction of the Labour Court.
In this case, the relief granted was only for reinstatement without mention of back-wages, so the High Court should not have presumed that the award included back-wages.
The appeals were allowed and in the unique circumstances of the case, any payment already made in compliance with the Labor Court or High Court order will not be recovered.
