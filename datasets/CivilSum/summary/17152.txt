The appointment of lecturers on a contractual basis until the end of the academic session serves as a stop-gap arrangement in recruitment.
However, a mere two-year continuation of the contractual appointment does not lead to its conversion into substantive appointment.
The constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, J&K Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956 and Jammu and Kashmir Education (Gazetted) Service Recruitment Rules, 1977, govern appointments to gazetted posts, and even the government does not have the power to make such appointments beyond the rules.
Therefore, the petitioners cannot claim absorption and appointment to substantive posts and must give way to regularly selected and appointed persons in accordance with the rules.
