As per Constitution of India, Article 229(2) Proviso - High Court Establishment (Appointment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1973, approval from the government is needed only for rules relating to salaries, allowances, leave or pensions and not other rules pertaining to conditions of service - Chief Justice framed all the rules except Rules 26, 27 and Schedules 1, IA, 2 and 3 which alone were sent for approval - Rules 16(quota) and 30(seniority) came into effect from March 1, 1974 - Quota for direct recruits and promotees has to be calculated accordingly - High Court has been directed to calculate the vacancies and adjust the appointments based on that.
