Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 244(1)(a) - Voluntary retirement - Notice to retire voluntarily - Unless there was a pending case against the employee, a notice to retire voluntarily could not be refused - Retirement becomes effective upon expiration of notice.
[Para 8] B Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 244(1)(b) - Voluntary retirement/Refusal/Communication of refusal - The respondents claimed that the notice of voluntary retirement was rejected, but the rules require rejection to be communicated - No communication was made to the petitioner, so the rejection was illegal.
[Para 10] C Estoppel against law/Voluntary retirement/Rejection - Rejecting a voluntary retirement notice is not valid - The petitioner participated in proceedings after retirement became effective due to notice period expiration - Estoppel cannot be applied against the law.
