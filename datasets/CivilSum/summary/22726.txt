The officer in charge of writing a confidential report has a public responsibility and must do so objectively and impartially.
[Para 7]
The officer writing a confidential report cannot be accused of malice for making adverse remarks based on a vigilance enquiry.
[Para 8]
In writing a confidential report, the responsible officer should inform the concerned official about any issues and give them a chance to improve.
If the subordinate fails to correct their behaviour, the officer can make a note of it in the report and provide a copy to the affected officer.
