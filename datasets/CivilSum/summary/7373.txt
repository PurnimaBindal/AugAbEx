Border Security force Act, 1968, Sections 26, 40 - Constitution of India, Article 14 - Dismissal - Challenge as to - Charge of misbehavior of petitioner in a sensitive area along the international border, after getting intoxicated with civilians - Even though the petitioner was tried by SSFC, and the charge sheet was explained, witnesses were cross-examined and brought in defense, an officer was appointed as the friend of the accused.
The given assertion that the petitioner was not aware of the nature of proceedings and was denied reasonable opportunity of defend, is not tenable, and there is no violation of the principles of natural justice.

Border Security Force Act, 1968, Sections 26, 40 - Punishment of dismissal - Proportionality - charge of misbehavior with civilians after getting intoxicated in a sensitive area along international border - The petitioner was a habitual offender and was previously punished on various occasions.
Any disciplined force cannot endure acts of indiscipline, and enforcing with an iron hand becomes necessary in all eventualities.
Under the circumstances, any punishment short of dismissal can result in being counterproductive, and the order was upheld.
