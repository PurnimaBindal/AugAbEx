Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 16 - AP College Service Commission Act, 1985 - Promotion to the post of Lecturer in English in Private Aided Degree College from the post of Junior Lecturer - The petitioner's claim for promotion was rejected due to not meeting the requirement of 55% marks in the qualifying examination, as specified in GOs.
dated 1011992 and dated 761993 - Those GOs.
were issued after an amendment was made to the AP College Service Commission Act, 1985, deleting Private Aided Colleges from its provisions - However, it was not shown that the subsequent GOs.
were issued in supersession of earlier GOs.
governing service conditions of Lecturers working in Private Aided Educational Institutions - The intention of the Government in issuing those subsequent GOs.
is that post-graduation is the prescribed qualification for promotion to Lecturer from the post of Junior Lecturer.
Thus, the petitioner's case has to be considered under the earlier G.O.
dated 1911984 which allows the promotion of a Junior Lecturer in a Private Degree College even if he has not secured 50% marks in the qualifying examination, subject to the condition of improving the marks to 55% within three years.
The concerned authorities are mandated to consider the petitioner's case under the said earlier G.O.
