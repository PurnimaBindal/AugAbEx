The petitioner, who sought admission in LL.B.
in Punjabi University, was denied migration to Panjab University due to a shortfall of 028 marks compared to the last candidate admitted.
However, he would have been eligible for migration if he had scored the same or higher marks.
The Board of Control, Department of Laws, recommended his case by disregarding the minor deficiency in the marks.
Although there were vacant seats in the third semester, the university refused to allow migration.
The petitioner had performed well in his first-year LL.B.
course at Punjabi University, but fractions in the original evaluation were rounded off, causing a small fraction of marks to fall short.
The university cannot waste a year of the student's time, and the petitioner deserves to be allowed migration by ignoring the minor shortfall of marks.
[Paras 4 and 5]
