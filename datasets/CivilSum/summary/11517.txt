The Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16, the Rajasthan Municipal (Subordinate & Ministerial) Services Rules of 1963, and the Rajasthan Municipal Services Rules of 1963 all enforce that promotion cannot be wrongly made against the posts intended for 100% direct recruitment and from outside the cadre.
It cannot be allowed to continue as a wrong cannot be perpetuated on the grounds of discrimination.
Thus, no one can claim any right based on a decision that is outside of the statutory rules, nor can there be any estoppel against the wrong law passed by mistake.
An erroneous order was made based on a misinterpretation of facts.
