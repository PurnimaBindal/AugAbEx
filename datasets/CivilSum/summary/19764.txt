According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner was denied a promotional grade due to adverse entries in the last 10 years.
However, the petitioner's case was considered along with others who were promoted despite censure or warnings which are not major punishments and are just part of the total record.
In cases where the service is divided into two parts - one higher pay scale and other junior scale - the rule of seniority-cum-merit must be followed and a senior person with a good record must automatically get a higher pay scale.
Comparing the records of several persons as done in this case was not necessary and thus, the petitioner should be placed in the promotional higher scale effective from the date when his juniors were promoted.
