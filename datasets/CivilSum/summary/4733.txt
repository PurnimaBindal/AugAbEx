According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 and the Universities Act of 1969, if the Selection Committee has been improperly constituted, candidates still have the right to challenge its constitution, despite their participation in the selection process.
[Para 7] B The Universities Act of 1969 states that two individuals with specialized knowledge who are unaffiliated with the university will be nominated to the Selection Committee by the Vice Chancellor, however, if individuals who have been Head of Departments and benefited from university funds are involved, there is a reasonable likelihood of bias, which is enough to invalidate the selection process.
Therefore, the constitution of the Selection Committee is deemed invalid.
