Doctors of a government company prayed for the revision of their pay scale as per the 5th Pay Revision Commission under Articles 16 and 226 of the Indian Constitution.
However, the company refused due to serious financial crisis.
The court held that if the company continues to sustain losses and cannot afford to enhance the pay scale, then the employees have no legal right to ask the central government to meet additional expenses incurred in this regard.
