Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, 1957 - Contractual appointment - Age relaxation - The Act of 1957 does not define the term 'employee', so it includes all types of employees, including temporary, regular, short-term, contractual, or ad hoc - The writ petitioners who were appointed on a contract basis were entitled to the benefit of age relaxation for the number of years they had worked as Medical Officers (Ayurved) - The High Court's judgment extending the benefit of age relaxation was correct.
[Paras 7 and 13] B Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, 1957 - Constitution of India, 1950, Article 311 - All employees of MCD - The term 'government employee' does not include municipal employees governed by Article 309 to 311 - Though permanent employees of municipal corporation or other statutory bodies may be governed by statutory rules, they are not considered government servants - All persons employed by MCD, whether permanent or contractual, are considered 'employees of MCD'.
