The management was justified in terminating the services of a workman who remained absent without leave and did not join despite calls, as they formed an opinion that the workman was not working satisfactorily, as per Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(oo) and 25-F [Para 3]
Termination cannot be retrospective from the date of absence but from the date the termination order is passed, as per Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311.
However, the workman will not be entitled to backwage on the principle of no work no pay.
