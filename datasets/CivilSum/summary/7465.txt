Guru Nanak Dev University Calender, Ordinance 18 Chapter 17.
Vol III - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 - Lecturers in Private aided Colleges - Ordinance 18 of the University mandates payment of gratuity to lecturers of private colleges - All private colleges are bound to obey rules and regulations framed by the University - 1972 Act does not affect operation of Ordinance 18 - Directions given to pay gratuity with 12% interest.
[Paras 10, 13, 14, 17] B Guru Nanak Dev University Calender, Ordinance 18 Chapter 17, Vol. III - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 - Lecturers in Private aided Colleges - Liability on State Government - State Government giving 95% aid to college does not mean it has to bear 95% amount to be paid as gratuity to retired lecturers - Grants the State makes to private educational institutions and on what terms are matters for the State to decide - No directions can be issued to the State to meet liability of Petitioner towards gratuity payable.
