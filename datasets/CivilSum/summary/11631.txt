Army Regulations, 1961 (Part-I), Regulation 53 - Army Pension Regulations 27(C) & 173 - Disability pension - The disability suffered by the petitioner was confined to a moderate injury incurred while on duty.
However, the Adviser/Appellate Medical Authority held that the disablement was not attributable to or aggravated by military service.
The Release Medical Board mentioned that the disease suffered by the petitioner was due to stress and strain, but no entry of any decease was recorded at the time of his entry in service.
Therefore, it is assumed that the stress and strain of military service contributed to the subsequent development of the disease, which is attributable to or aggravated by military service.
[Paras 17 and 18]

Army Regulation, 1961 (Part-I), Regulation 53 - Army Pension, Regulations 27(C) & 173, - Disability pension - The authority failed to produce the complete proceedings of the Adviser/Appellate Medical Board, which has allegedly contradicted the Release Medical Board's findings.
In the absence of a complete record, an adverse inference has to be made against the authority.
