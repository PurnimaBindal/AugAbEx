Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-G - Termination - Retrenchment - Even if the workman had not completed 240 days of service, the principle of 'last come first go' will be applied on them if the juniors are retained - Completion of 240 days is not necessary to activate the provisions of Section 25-G of the Act - If the juniors are retained, termination of the senior employee would not be justifiable - 2001(1) SCT 103 was adhered to.
