Constitution of India, Article 311, - Appointment by fraud - Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1964, Rule 3 (1)(i)(iii) - Termination of Services - The departmental enquiry held against the respondent for obtaining employment wrongfully and in contravention of Rule 3(1)(i)(iii) of the Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1964 found the charge to be proved and an order of dismissal was passed against him - The tribunal erred in holding that the strict rules of procedure and proof do not apply to departmental enquiry - The respondent having obtained 486% marks and then not producing the certificate disclosing correct percentage of marks obtained by him then he would not have been selected at all - The order of termination cannot be said to be improper or bad and the Tribunal was in error in holding otherwise.
