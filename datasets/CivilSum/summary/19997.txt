The requirement of indicating reasons in a judgment has been recognized as imperative, as reason is the heartbeat of every conclusion and without it, the decision becomes lifeless.
The right to reason is an indispensable part of a sound judicial system and reflects the application of mind on the part of the court.
The affected party has the right to know why the decision has gone against them, and spelling out reasons for the order made is one of the salutary requirements of natural justice.
