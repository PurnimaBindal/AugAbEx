The Constitution of India, Articles 226/227, discusses pay scales for government employees.
The petitioner is a Stenographer Grade "C" at the Indian Institute of Kayachikitsa and has been given a pay scale of Rs. 4500-700.
However, it was discovered that those working in the Headquarters receive a pay scale of Rs. 5500-9000.
The court held that the post of Stenographer "C" is interchangeable between the Head Office and the field office.
Therefore, the petitioner and all others in similar positions are entitled to a pay scale of Rs. 5500-9000.
The petition was allowed.
