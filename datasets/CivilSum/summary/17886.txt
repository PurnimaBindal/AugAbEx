The petitioner, a JBT teacher, has filed a case against the respondents for not regularising her services as per instructions from March 9, 1987, and onwards from November 1, 1986.
The government has prescribed a condition for regularisation in a circular from March 9, 1987, and a notification from February 28, 1991, which states that the qualifications are identical.
The department has deemed the petitioner eligible and possessing the prescribed qualifications for the post when she was appointed on an ad hoc basis, which fulfills the precise condition laid down in the circular from March 9, 1987.
The petitioner has been considered eligible under the circular from April 10, 1991, and therefore, there is no administrative or legal reason for her not being regularised under the circular dated March 9, 1987.
The respondents have been directed to consider the petitioner's claim for regularisation from November 1, 1986, in accordance with the rules which were in force at that time on the hypothesis that she meets the qualifications.
