As per Clause 7 of ROPA Rules, 1981 and Articles 16 and 226 of the Indian Constitution, an Assistant Teacher who was originally approved as a clerk is entitled to pay protection when they are appointed to the role.
A Director's order depriving the petitioner of this pay protection has been deemed illegal and is set aside by the respondents, who are also instructed to protect the petitioner's pay.
