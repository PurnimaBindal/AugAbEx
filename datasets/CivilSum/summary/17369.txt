The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Resignation - Withdrawal - A Pharmacist appointed in 1956 was transferred from Civil Hospital to a Rural Dispensary, Makhu, but went on leave and remained absent for about one year.
On 29101977, the plaintiff submitted a resignation that would take effect from 31101977.
He had the right to withdraw the resignation before the expiry of the date, but once operative, had no right to withdraw it, even if acceptance was not communicated to him.
Silence is considered abandonment.
