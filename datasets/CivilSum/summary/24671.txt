Constitution of India, Articles 16, 32 and 309 - Promotion - Promotion is considered a normal aspect of service - The non-medical 'A' Group Scientists in the establishment of the Director General of Health Service should not be deprived of the benefit of promotion, considering that similarly placed officers in other Ministries receive the same advantage - It is the obligation of the Ministry of Health to provide an efficient public service and attend to the representations of the Council and its members, including providing promotional avenues for this category of officers - Appropriate rules should be framed within four months, based on the model of rules framed by the Ministry of Science and Technology, with necessary alterations, to provide a promotional avenue for the 'A' category scientists in the non-medical wing of the Directorate.
