Rule 13(1) of Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal, Rules) in 1958 states that suspension by another authority, not the appointing authority, is permissible.
It is clear according to the rule 13(1), and the contention that anyone to which it is subordinate can order suspension is not maintainable.
