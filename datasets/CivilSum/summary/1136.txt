Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Orissa Education (Establishment, Recognition and Management of Private High Schools) Rules, 1991 - Appointment - Concern about fairness - A member of the selection committee was a close relation to the selectee who participated in the selection process that led to their appointment - The key issue is whether there is a justifiable reason to believe that the member of the selection committee was likely to be biased towards the selectee - Given their relationship, there is a reasonable basis for concern - Therefore, the appointment is vulnerable and must be nullified.
