The Rajasthan Public Works Department (B&R) including Gardens, Irrigation, Water Works and Ayurvedic Departments Work - Charged Employees Rules, 1964, Rules 3(2) and 12 state that after 10 years of service, an employee who has satisfactory records shall be eligible for the permanent work-charged employee title, and given a regular pay-scale as per Rule 12.
But this doesn't include Selection Grade.
[Paras 3 and 4]

Proviso to Rule 3 of the Rajasthan Public Works Department (B&R) including Gardens, Irrigation, Water Works and Ayurvedic Departments Work-charged Employees Rules, 1964, states that a permanent or semi-permanent work-charged employee is not entitled to the status or benefits of permanency or semi-permanency a regular Govt. servant enjoys under the Rajasthan Service Rules.
Selection-grade is not automatically applicable to a permanent work-charged employee and will not be given to them.
[Para 6]

Selection-grade is a higher salary for the same position.
The aim of a selection-grade is to ensure that a capable Govt. employee, who cannot be promoted due to limited promotional opportunities, remains at the same level but with a higher salary, in the interest of greater efficiency.
[Para 5]
