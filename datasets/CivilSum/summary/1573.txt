Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Revision of pay scales - Ordered prospectively - Petitioners seek retroactive pay - IV Central Pay Commission recommended uniform pay scales for all personnel in Central Police Organizations (CPO) - However, while implementing the recommendations, higher pay scales were only granted to Subordinate Officers of Delhi Police, creating a disparity - Petitioners sought revision of pay scales from 111996, not the same as Delhi Police from 111986 - Order dated 10101997 rationalized the implementation of V Central Pay Commission, making 111996 the relevant date - Pre-revised scales cannot be granted to the petitioners from 111986 - Petition is granted, and petitioners are entitled to revised pay scales from 111996.
