According to Section 7-A of the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, if two restaurants owned by the same management operate from different locations and have a common nature of business, then they should have two separate kitchens.
However, an employee from one restaurant can be engaged in carrying out the business of the other unit as well.
The two units can be considered as one establishment due to the unity of supervision, control, finance, and functional integrality between them.
This holds true for all practical purposes.
