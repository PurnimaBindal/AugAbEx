According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India and Rule 13 of the Haryana Civil Services (Punishment & Appeal) Rules, 1987, an employee can be dismissed from service if they exhibit delay and laches.
In a recent case, the Punishing Authority granted the petitioner the opportunity of a personal hearing and supplied them with a copy of the enquiry report.
However, the petitioner filed an appeal after a delay of more than 8 years, and it cannot be accepted that they were not aware of the enquiry report or the order of dismissal passed in 1991.
The court found no plausible explanation for the inordinate delay, and therefore dismissed the petition on the grounds of delay and laches.
