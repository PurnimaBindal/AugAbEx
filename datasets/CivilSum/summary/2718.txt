According to the Air Force Act, 1950, Section 173, dismissal from service and detention are two possible forms of punishment.
However, it should be noted that Section 73 considers detention to be more severe than dismissal.
Additionally, imprisonment is always seen as more severe than civil consequences.
Therefore, an order of dismissal from service after someone has already served three months of detention cannot be considered a punishment of greater severity than detention.
