Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 309 - Disability pension -Eligibility - Respondent was discharged from military service due to mental illness and claimed disability pension which was rejected.
The respondent filed a writ petition and argued that the illness was attributable to military service which was accepted by the court.
The UOI appealed, claiming that there was no material to prove that the disability was related to military duties.
The opinion of the medical board was accepted as correct unless proved otherwise.
The respondent had not been posted to any border areas or areas with adverse climate during his service, and his job did not involve any such risks.
The learned Single Judge was not justified in attributing the illness to military service.
