The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 33(2)(b), 34, 31 and 2(g) provide for the prosecution of employers who have contravened the Act.
With regard to authority letters for prosecution, they can only be granted against the person who is classified as the employer in Section 2(g), and not against the management alone.
Representatives of the employer cannot be prosecuted, but if the union subsequently furnishes the name and address of the concerned person, then the grant of authority cannot be refused.
