Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission - The petitioner missed her admission interview due to a family death, while two other candidates were given admission even though they also did not attend on the scheduled date - It is necessary that if the university waived a condition for one candidate, it should do so for all of them - The Admission Committee is directed to grant the petitioner admission in any of the three departments of her application - If no seat is available, an additional seat should be created to accommodate the petitioner.
