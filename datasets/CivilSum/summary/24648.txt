Contract Act, 1872, Section 2(a) - Notice inviting tenders - In order to supply tender books, tenderers must fulfill pre-conditions of work experience and a minimum annual turnover - Failure to provide required documents can result in exclusion from consideration - Appeal dismissed.
[Paras 8 & 17]
Constitution of India, Article 136 - Appeal - Notification inviting tenders for construction work - Pre-qualifications of tenderer for issue of tender forms - Authority issuing tenders to decide on sufficiency of tenderer's work experience certificate, not Court - Appeal dismissed.
[Para 10]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Works contract - Tender - Relaxation of pre-qualification allowed by authority issuing tenders - However, such relaxation cannot be arbitrary - Rule of invalidation of contract grant for relaxation not applicable if applicant is attempting to gain immunity from competition.
