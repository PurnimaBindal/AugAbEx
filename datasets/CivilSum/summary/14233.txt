Constitution of India, Article 226 - Recruitment Rules - Promotion - Junior Scientific Assistant Grade-I with three years of service may be promoted to Senior Scientific Assistant - Five vacancies arose for Senior Scientific Assistant - Recruitment Rules were amended - Question of whether Amended Recruitment Rules are applicable to the vacancies that arose prior to its enactment - The employer can choose not to fill vacancies until amended rules are in force - Tribunal erred when concluding that unamended rules applied to pre-amended vacancies - Petition allowed.
