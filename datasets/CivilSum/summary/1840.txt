Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Indian Railways Establishment Manual, Para 228 - Notional promotion with retrospective effect - Arrears of pay of higher post - Not eligible for promotion - The "no work no pay" principle dictates that no employee can be paid wages for a post on which he did not actually work - Promotees will only receive seniority and pay fixation while computing their service for all other purposes except arrears of pay - Para 228 of IREM is considered valid.
