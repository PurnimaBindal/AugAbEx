"According to Articles 14, 16, and 226 of the Constitution of India, petitioners with equivalent qualifications must be admitted to the Shastri Part-I course.
The petitioners in this case had acquired Uttar Madhyama II of Maharshi Dayanand University, which was rated equivalent to eligibility for admission to Shastri Part-I They were given admission and have already undergone studies on that basis, including appearing in Shastri Part-I Therefore, their eligibility could not be questioned at this stage on the ground that Uttar Madhayma II is not equivalent to the 10+2 course.
As a result, the petitioners have been declared eligible to continue their studies in the Shastri Course.
[Paras 9 to 11]"
