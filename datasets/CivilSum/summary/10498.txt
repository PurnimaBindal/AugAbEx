The Industrial Employees (Standing Orders) Act of 1946, Sections 12-A and 13-B, the Electricity (Supply) Act of 1948, Section 79(c), the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Punjab (Haryana First Amendment) Rules of 1969, and Model Standing Orders dictate the transfer of workers of Industrial Establishment.
The Haryana State Electricity Board (HSEB) developed its own regulations to govern the service conditions of its employees, but these regulations were never published under Section 13-B of the 1946 Act.
In the absence of certified and notified Rules, Regulations, or Standing Orders, any draft Rules will have no effect on the applicability of 1946 Act which provides automatic general application of Model Standing Orders.
According to the 1946 Act, employees of an industrial establishment governed by the act cannot be transferred unless the Schedule allows or is amended to that effect.
As a result, Standing Orders may only contain provisions with regard to the matters included in the Schedule to the 1946 Act.
In the case of transfers made from Thermal Plant, Faridabad to other nearby projects for non-availability of work, Transfer orders were made without jurisdiction.
The orders were quashed, with liberty to retrench the workers in accordance with the law.
The petition was allowed.
