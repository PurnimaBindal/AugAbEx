Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Quota and Rota system - Carry-forward of vacancies - The quota of 'insiders' cannot be carried forward if it's not filled due to the non-availability of insider candidates.
The roster considers the requirement of increasing outsiders in the quota of Direct Recruits.
However, the policy cannot be implemented as per the quota following carry-forward of vacancies.
The tribunal is unable to direct the implementation of the policy as per the roster.
