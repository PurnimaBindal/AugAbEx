The second proviso introduced by the amendment to Rule 14(1) of the Punjab Superior Judicial Service Rules, 1963, as amended by the Haryana Second Amendment Rules, 1988, which denies special pay to judicial officers on being granted selection grade, is stated to be arbitrary, discriminatory and violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
Following this amendment, orders have been issued in various cases as a result of which the amendment has been struck down.
