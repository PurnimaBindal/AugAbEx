Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo)(bb) - Contract service - Non-renewal of contract - Employers must plead and prove that a workman's employment was for a specific term and purpose and ended automatically with the non-renewal of their contract of service, in order for such a plea to be allowed in court - It cannot be raised for the first time during SLP if there are no such pleadings before the labour Court.
[Para 6]

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Reinstatement on the other/higher post - While a workman can be reinstated on a higher post, in this case, the workman in question only worked as a Helper in the Computer branch and cannot be reinstated as a Computer Programmer - They must be reinstated as a Helper but not as a manual labourer.
[Paras 8 & 9]
