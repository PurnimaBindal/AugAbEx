Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Seniority - The period of apprenticeship cannot be counted towards service qualifying for determination of seniority as an apprentice is not a member of any service - Petitioner was given only a stipend of Rs. 45/- per month during his training and was not granted regular pay scale of the post of Sectional Officer - No rule or instructions have been issued by competent authority whereunder period of training may be counted towards period of service as a Sectional Officer - It was held that it cannot be said to be wrong or unfair that period of training shall not be counted towards seniority.
