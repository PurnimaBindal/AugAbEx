Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation/Admission - Education - Allahabad Agriculture Institute, a Christian minority institute, granted weightage to non-Christian students for admission to BTech (Agricultural Engineering) and B.Sc.
(Agriculture) courses - Weightage amounts to reservation for non-minority communities - Minority institutions can only reserve seats for the minority community that established the institution - Weightage for a particular class without defined criteria is arbitrary and violates Article 14 of the Constitution - Court quashes the rules providing such weightage and directed admission to all candidates exceeding marks obtained by any non-Christian candidate admitted.
