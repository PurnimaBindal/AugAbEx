The petitioner under the Border Security Force Act, 1968, Section 117(2) is only permitted to submit a petition, with no provisions for a hearing.
The principles of natural justice are not applicable after a trial observing due process.
Furthermore, the authority deciding the petition is not a Court and therefore every administrative order passed by such an authority is not subject to the strict requirements of principles of natural justice as stated in the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
