According to Sections 2(00) and 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the termination of a workman's services in accordance with the terms of their appointment letter after 240 days of service is considered retrenchment.
Removing a workman's name from the rolls without complying with Section 25F of the Act is not a lawful action, and the workman is entitled to reinstatement with full back wages.
