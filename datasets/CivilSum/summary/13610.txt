Kamal Sharma, Adl.AG, argues that Constitution of India Articles 14 and 16 entitle the petitioner to pension despite his qualifying service falling slightly short.
The petitioner worked with the Haryana Government for almost 20 years, with a gap for army service, but his juniors in the same batch were granted pensions.
Therefore, Sharma asserts that the authorities should have taken care not to affect the petitioner's right to pension when allowing him to retire voluntarily; they are now estopped from denying him the benefits he is entitled to.
