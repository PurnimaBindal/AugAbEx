The Post of Assistant Market Officer is governed by Jammu and Kashmir Subordinate Service Recruitment Rules, 1992 and recruitment is through SSB Board.
The Marketing Officer is not competent to appoint and any appointment made by the Marketing Officer with conditions not in line with rules cannot be sustained.
