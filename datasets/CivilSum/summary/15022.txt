Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - Eligibility - The petitioner was not selected for a post as he did not meet the required qualifications - The petitioner argued that he was eligible for a vacancy in a different category - However, the existence of a vacancy does not guarantee an eligible employee the right to demand that his employer fill the position - Ultimately, it is at the employer's discretion to decide whether or not to fill the vacancy.
