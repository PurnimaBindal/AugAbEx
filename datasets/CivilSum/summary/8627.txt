As the Industrial tribunal was considering a dispute, the company sought permission to terminate the workman's services for misconduct under Section 33(3) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
However, the application to dismiss the workman was made while the dispute was still pending.
The tribunal granted permission, but all disputes came to an end by the time the application was considered.
The workman was deemed to be an undesirable person whose continuation in service would be harmful to the management, and the management was directed to pay a lump sum amount of Rs. 1 Lac within three months.
If not, the workman would be entitled to reinstatement.
The petition was partly allowed.
