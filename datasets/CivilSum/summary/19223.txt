Post Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, Chandigarh Regulations, 1967, Regulation 37-A - Age of superannuation for teaching staff - The post of Tutor (non-medical) involves scientific research in addition to teaching, and the incumbents must be treated equally as other members of teaching faculty - Similarly, the post of Tutor (Experimental Medicine) should not be excluded from the teaching faculty solely due to the absence of a separate pay scale in the regulations - Therefore, the petitioner, as a member of teaching faculty, should retire at the age of 60 years and not 58 years.
