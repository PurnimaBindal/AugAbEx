Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - The persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities Protection of Rights and Participation) Act, 1995 - Admission - Reserve category - Government instructions - Petitioner desires admission to OT (Hindi) course under category of Ex Servicemen - Government instructions have withdrawn reservation for ex servicemen and have provided same for physically handicapped category - Respondents assert that such reservation was contingent on reservation policy of government - Prospectus indicates that reservation of seats is subject to and regulated by reservation policy of State Government -Court determined that instructions are given by government to meet statutory obligation - Therefore, the guidance cannot be deemed to breach protection of equality under Articles 14 and 16 of Constitution - Petition rejected as a result.
