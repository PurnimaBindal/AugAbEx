MP Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1976, Rule 42 - MP (Work Charged and Contingency Paid Employees) Pension Rules, 1979, Rule 6(2) - Pension - Qualifying service - Service rendered in work charged establishment must be counted as qualifying service upon absorption into a regular post.
The lack of clear criteria for discarding regular work charged establishment service is especially perplexing since this service is also eligible for pension.
