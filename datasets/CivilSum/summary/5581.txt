The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 10 and 33C(2), allows for the setting aside of an ex parte award by the Labour Court, but only within 30 days of its publication.
Once that period has passed, the Labour Court no longer has jurisdiction to do so.
In a specific case where an ex parte award was announced on Aug 23, 1991, and published on Oct 14, 1991, an application for setting aside was filed on Sept 22, 1992.
However, the Labour Court was found to have no power to go beyond the scope of the proceedings pending under section 33C(2) and set aside the original award.
Consequently, the order of the Labour Court was quashed.
