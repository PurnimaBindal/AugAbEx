Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate Appointment - Dying in harness - Ad hoc employee - The instruction of the State Govt. for the appointment of a government employee's surviving spouse on compassionate grounds applies equally to an ad hoc employee.
The respondent has been directed to appoint the deceased employee's wife from the date of the first representation to the State authorities.
The case of 1992(2) SLR 646 has been relied upon.
