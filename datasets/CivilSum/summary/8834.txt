Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Misconduct - Correction in date of birth - Change in service record - While working as Sr. Time Keeper in the establishment, a workman changed the dates of birth of two workers based on original documents and as per the prevalent practice in the establishment - No evidence of any procedure, instruction, or rule being violated - Even the Managing Director approved the employees' application for correction without raising any objections - Workman's action deemed bona fide and in accordance with the establishment's practice, thus not subject to any action - Labour Court's award upheld.
