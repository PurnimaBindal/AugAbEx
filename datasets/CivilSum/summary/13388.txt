Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Seniority List - Natural justice - Promotion from the post of Lecturer to the post of Principal - The petitioner contested the order that terminated his lien from the post of Lecturer and removed his name from the seniority list of Lecturers.
The petitioner's consent was not obtained before the order was issued.
The impugned order is not valid as the opportunity for a hearing was not provided.
The respondents are directed to consider the petitioner for promotion to the post of Principal.
[Paras 7 & 8]
