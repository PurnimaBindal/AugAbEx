Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural Justice/House Rent Allowance - Previously, Petitioners were receiving house rent allowance due to their location within 8 km of the Municipal Area.
However, the allowance was terminated due to an allegation that the area was no longer within the required limit.
Although record certificates from the Deputy Commissioner and Executive Engineer were present to confirm the area's proximity, disputed information on record required an opportunity for the Union to comply with the principles of natural justice.
The Quashed Order was distinguished from the Division Bench judgment in C.W.P.
No. 8534 of 1988 based on facts.
