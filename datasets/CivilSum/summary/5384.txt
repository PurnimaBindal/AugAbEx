Constitution of India, Article 226 - Notional increments-Reinstatement - When reinstated in service, the petitioner is entitled to computing notional increments he would have drawn had he been continuing in actual service.
This will result in proper fixation of salary on reinstatement.Hence, respondents must re-fix the pay and make payment of difference in wages on such refixation.
[Paras 3 and 7]
