Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(j) - Industry - Nehru Yuva Kendra Sangathan's status as an industry, under Section 2(j) of ID Act - The petitioner's function is not a sovereign function, meaning it is not exempt - Exemption only applies to functions that are distinctly sovereign, and not to welfare activities or economic adventure by the Government or statutory bodies - While the petitioner's activity may be a welfare activity, it is not a sovereign function in a strict sense - Therefore, the petitioner is considered an 'Industry' under Section 2(j) [Paras 9, 11 and 14]
