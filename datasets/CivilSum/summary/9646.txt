Constitution of India, Article 16 - Superannuation - Recovery of salary paid for work done beyond superannuation date - Corporation was unsure if employee was to retire at 58 or 60 years - Employee allowed to continue until clarification was received - No misrepresentation made by employee, not liable for continuance - Remuneration for actual work cannot be deducted.
Writ petition granted.
