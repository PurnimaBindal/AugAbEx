Articles 16 and 311 of the Constitution of India give the Disciplinary Authority the power to frame charges and initiate or dispense with the holding of an enquiry.
If necessary, an Enquiry Officer may be appointed by the Disciplinary Authority.
Regardless of whether it is the Disciplinary Authority or the Enquiry Officer making the conclusions and recommendations, the decision to accept those findings is ultimately in the discretion of the Disciplinary Authority.
[Para 9] B The Disciplinary Authority is responsible for providing a copy of the Enquiry Officer's report and allowing the employee to challenge the findings before independent conclusions are drawn.
Failure to comply with this obligation violates the principles of natural justice and invalidates any proposed action.
