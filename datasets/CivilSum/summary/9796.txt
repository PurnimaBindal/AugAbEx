According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Section 25 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, if a workman fails to prove he worked for 240 days in a year, the finding of fact cannot be interfered.
[Para 3]
An employee's medical leave cannot be included in the 240 days of work required for a daily wage worker's employment contract, as stated in Section 25 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
The petitioner cannot be reinstated in his job as a result of his medical leave for arthritis.
