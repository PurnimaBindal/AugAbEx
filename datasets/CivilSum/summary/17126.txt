Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - The petitioner joined the Army in 1976 but was later discharged on medical grounds in 1986 due to unfitness.
His disability was assessed at 20% and placed in the 'EEE' category.
The petitioner was medically examined and declared fit to join the army, and there was no previous history of idiopathic epilepsy.
The petitioner had served the army for about 10 years and never experienced an epilepsy fit during this period.
Thus, it could be reasonably inferred that his disease was attributable to his military service, making the respondents liable to pay the petitioner disability pension.
The petitioner is entitled to receive 38 months' worth of disability pension that was due to him before the writ petition was filed.
[Paras 5 to 7]
