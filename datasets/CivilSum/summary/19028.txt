The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Disciplinary Proceedings - Natural Justice - Principle of Nemo debet esse judex in propria causa - Complaint initiation by the disciplinary authority necessitates impartiality - Punishment orders cannot be passed by the disciplinary authority - In cases where the disciplinary authority cannot be a judge of his own cause, there is a likelihood of bias - Deciding authority must be impartial and unbiased.
[Para 5]
The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 - Disciplinary Proceedings - Enquiry Report - In cases where the disciplinary authority disagrees with the report of the Enquiry Officer, it is required to assign reasonable grounds for such disagreement - The disciplinary authority cannot rely on material outside the record or examine a witness not previously examined during the enquiry proceedings.
[Paras 7 to 11]
