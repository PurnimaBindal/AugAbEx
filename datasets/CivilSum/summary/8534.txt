Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Kerala Headload Workers Act, 1978 - In the event of law and order problems, irrespective of the dispute settlement machinery provided under the Act, employers may seek police protection.
[Para 22] B Kerala Headload Workers Act, 1978, Section 2(m) - Definition - The loading and unloading of goods for domestic purposes made by a worker are excluded from the definition of headload worker.
[Para 14] C Kerala Headload Workers Act, Section 2(m) - The definition of headload worker is not altered even if he is permanently attached to an establishment carrying on other work as well.
[Para 24] D Kerala Headload Workers Rules, Rule 26-A - Where the scheme is applicable, only registered headworkers are allowed to work in the area; however, a permanently employed headload worker is required to register.
