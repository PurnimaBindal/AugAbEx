The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 311, outline departmental proceedings, the importance of natural justice, and the need for a copy of the enquiry report to be supplied to the employee.
Failure to provide a copy of the enquiry report will result in a violation of the employee's constitutional protection.
Any resulting action cannot be sustained.
However, the employer must be given the liberty to proceed afresh in accordance with the law.
[Paras 7 & 8]
