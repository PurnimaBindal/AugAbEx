According to the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 11, a domestic enquiry regarding an industrial dispute cannot be considered invalid simply because it was conducted by someone from the management.
[Para 5]
In cases where bias is alleged in a domestic enquiry, it must be specifically raised and proved before the adjudicator.
