Punjab Civil Services Rules (Volume I) Rule 8.121(i) - Extra Ordinary leave - Voluntary Retirement - The Petitioner was granted earned leave when he applied for it from 7.7.1990 to 28.9.1990, but later left India on 1141997 without explicit permission or the grant of appropriate leave.
This resulted in departmental proceedings being initiated against the petitioner.
After not returning for three years, he requested voluntary retirement, which was declined due to proper reasons.
It is held that a Govt. employee must adhere to the Service Rules that govern the grant of leave or otherwise.
[Paras 5 & 6]
