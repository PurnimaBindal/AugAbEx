Constitution of India, Article 16 - Transfer - Transfer and absorption of employees of National Thermal Power Corporation in the National Power Transmission Corporation Ltd - Writ - Interim order - Court has ordered that NTPC cannot recruit personnel from any source apart from the National Thermal Power Corporation whilst the writ petition is still pending - An application for clarification of the term "recruitment" has been approved and will not be mistaken to mean and include the transfer of employees from various corporations to the NTPC.
