Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Uttar Pradesh Temporary Government Servants (Termination of Service) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 - During probation, unstigmatic termination order cannot be questioned as violative of Articles 14 or 311 of the Constitution - Complaints, even if the motive, cannot be the foundation of such an order if no punitive measures or enquiry is sought - Employers can choose any action to dispensing the unsatisfactory services of probationary employees, including termination - The background of such an order is beyond the Court's scope.
