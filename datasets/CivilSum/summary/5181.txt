As per Section 36(4) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, if there is a need for another advocate to be engaged after the granting of leave or consent, it is necessary to obtain fresh leave or consent.
However, in a case where implied consent was given earlier for the representation of the company by an advocate, objections raised by the workmen when another advocate is engaged cannot be entertained.
It is also held that the workmen cannot withdraw a consent already given and neither can the Court or Tribunal recall leave granted to a party.
