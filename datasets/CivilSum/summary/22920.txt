Constitution of India, Articles 14, 136 and 226 - Suspension - Suspension of employees pending criminal case under Prevention of Corruption Act - Writ Petition filed by employer regarding subsistence allowance only - Rule scope never expanded later - Suspension order revoked by High Court due to employer not initiating and completing disciplinary proceedings for 2 years - No appeal made by respondent or Rule scope expanded later by High Court - Since writ was limited to subsistence allowance, suspension validity not under purview of petition and should not have been revoked.
