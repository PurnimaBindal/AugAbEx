Legal Services Authority Act, 1987, Sections 21(1), 22 - Award by Lok Adalat - Unsustainability in absence of settlement or compromise - Writ disposed with directions to respondents by Lok Adalat in absence of settlement or compromise between parties - Decision outside of settlement or compromise is beyond Lok Adalat's jurisdiction - Award passed is without jurisdiction, according to "Sham Lal Sharma v/s State of Haryana" court judgment.
