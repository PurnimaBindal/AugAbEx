When a matter is referred by DB to FB for answering a question of law, the FB should only answer the question and send back the matter.
According to High Court Rules and Orders, the FB has no jurisdiction to decide the matter on merits.
In the case mentioned, the FB not only answered the question but also decided the revision petition on merits, violating their jurisdiction.
Therefore, the Order of FB on merits has been set aside, and the DB will decide the merits.
The legal sources used to support this include 1981 (Supp.)
SCC 38 and (2006)6 SCC 258.
[Paras 10 and 11]
