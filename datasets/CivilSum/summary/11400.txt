Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25(f) - Casual/temporary employment of a workman - The management/establishment must maintain a muster roll and attendance register with markings for worker presence, and present them in Court as evidence.
The muster roll helps in determining whether the worker has completed 240 days of work, if he left service on his own, and if the establishment forced him out using money or muscle power.
Additionally, the establishment must furnish the required details to the worker to support him in Court.
