Constitution of India, Article 226 - Recruitment - Postal Assistant - Age relaxation - The petitioner asked for age relaxation due to her widow status - An investigation uncovered the fact that she had remarried ten years prior to her selection as a Postal Assistant, but had concealed this information - The postal authorities removed the petitioner's name from the select list - The CAT upheld the decision of the postal authorities, and the writ jurisdiction does not require any intervention.
