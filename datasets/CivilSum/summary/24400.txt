Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14 and 311 - Writ jurisdiction - Alternative remedy - Exceptional circumstances must be established to entertain a writ petition under Article 226 when a statutory remedy is available under the Act.
These exceptions include ultra vires provisions of the remedy, breach of principles of natural justice or fundamental rights, or jurisdiction of the authority taking impugned action.

Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14 and 311 - Natural justice - Technical deficiency in compliance of procedure - Non-serving of proper notice - When pleading a breach of natural justice, the Court must first verify if the alleged breach caused prejudice to the delinquent.
Only then, it should remit the case relating to the parties to the stage of the alleged breach.
