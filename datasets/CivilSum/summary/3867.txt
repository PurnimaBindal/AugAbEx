Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Date of birth - Superannuation - Petitioner superannuated from service due to incorrect recorded date of birth - However, petitioner was later allowed to join but period of idleness was treated as dies-non - The contention raised is that even after obtaining a judgment and decree before superannuation, no steps were taken by respondent authorities to correct the date of birth in service records.
The court held that since the respondent authority subsequently rectified the date of birth and allowed the petitioner to join, there is no reason to treat the period of idleness as dies-non and directions were given to grant relief for the period of idleness that occurred due to respondent inaction.
As a result, the petition was granted.
[Paras 15 and 16]
