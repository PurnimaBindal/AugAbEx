According to the Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 7(6) and Article 311 of the Constitution of India, a tentative view on the punishment proposed in a show-cause notice must be expressed by the punishing authority to effectively allow the delinquent to show cause against the proposed punishment, and the formation of a tentative view does not prejudice the matter against the delinquent.
[Para 5]
The provision for a show-cause notice before passing an order of punishment in the Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules of 1987 is in compliance with the principles of natural justice, and is not inconsistent with Article 311(2) of the Constitution of India.
