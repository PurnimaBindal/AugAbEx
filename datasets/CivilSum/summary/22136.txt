Factories Act, 1948, Sections 2(1) & 46 - Regularisation - Status of employees working in canteen - The management has no authority to appoint or discipline erring workmen, nor is it required to reimburse wages of workmen to the contractor.
A settlement was reached between the contractor and workmen of canteen under the supervision of an Assistant Labour Commissioner regarding labor issues relating to workmen hired by the contractor, which the management was not party to.
Workmen cannot be considered workers of management for reasons other than Factories Act.
