Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Transfer - A Statistical Assistant (SA) was appointed on a temporary basis to the E & S Department.
The Transport Department requested applications from various departments to transfer individuals for SA positions.
The petitioner was selected and joined the transferee department but was later recalled to the E&S office.
The petitioner was instructed to be posted on the post that he had vacated in 1984.
The court found that the petitioner had severed all connections with his earlier department upon appointment by transfer in the Transport Department.
The only evidence presented was that the petitioner remained on the roll of the parent department and it was not suggested that petitioner had been sent on deputation to the new department.
