According to the Constitution of India, Articles 26 and 30, privately managed medical colleges, whether aided or unaided, have the right to conduct entrance tests separate from the University or the State.
This right has been recognized by the Supreme Court in Islamic Academy case, subject to the maintenance of transparency for standards of education.
