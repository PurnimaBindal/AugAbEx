Constitution of India, Article 311 - MP Public Health and Family Welfare Department Non-Ministerial (Related to the Directorate Health Services) Class III Services Recruitment Rules, 1989 - The appointment/termination/qualifications/natural justice - Principle of natural justice did not apply to selection for the appointment of Auxiliary Nurses-cum-Midwife, who were sent for training without fulfilling the minimum qualification requirement, then appointed, and subsequently terminated on the ground of ineligibility and illegal appointment - Termination was appropriate.
