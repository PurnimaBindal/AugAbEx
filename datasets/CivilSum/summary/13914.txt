Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Disability pension - The counting of the 'service element' of disability pension should be done from commission date till release date - Rejection of counting of service element on the basis of assessment of disability at 70% not justified - It is unjust to deny disability pension benefit solely based on assessment at 70% while PRS officers can count entire commission service period as qualifying service.
