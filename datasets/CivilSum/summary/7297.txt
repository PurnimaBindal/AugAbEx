The Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 226, the Electricity (Supply) Act of 1948's Section 79-C, the Bihar State Electricity Board Service Regulations of 1976's Regulation 1, and Bihar State Electricity Board Service Board Standing Orders dated February 18th, 1994's Clause (d) all mention the term "dependent" in relation to compassionate appointment.
It is clearly stated that an adopted son/daughter cannot be considered as dependent for the purposes of claiming appointment on compassionate grounds.
Appointment cannot be claimed as a matter of course and is subject to rules and regulations.
