Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 311 - Conditions of service - Option - Petitioner was an employee of The Barnala Co-operative Spinning Mills Ltd Society and served as the Chief Executive, later designated as Managing Director - He received a charge sheet and suspension order from the Managing Director of the Punjab Co-operative Spinning Mills Federation Ltd under the Punjab Co-operative Spinning Mills Federation Services (Common Cadre) Rules, 1989 - The petitioner did not opt for his services to be governed under the 1989 Rules of the Federation nor was he ever asked to be put at the disposal of the Federation for that purpose - An enquiry was held and a termination order was passed, which was later declared to be without jurisdiction - However, the Mill can still take legal action against the petitioner.
