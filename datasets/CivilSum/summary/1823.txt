Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India - Promotions - The authority cannot ignore a senior eligible employee from consideration for promotion while promoting his junior without violating legal as well as fundamental rights of equality before law - The petitioner should be promoted from the date his juniors were promoted.
[Para 6] B Articles 226, 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India - Alternative remedy - Promotions - Pursuing an alternative remedy is not necessary when the facts and relief sought in the writ petition are different from that in the civil suit - The existence of an alternative remedy does not bar the High Court from issuing prerogative writs under Article 226.
