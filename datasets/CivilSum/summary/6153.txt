Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Thirty Lab Assistant posts were advertised, with some reserved for girls, but no girl candidate of the general category was appointed to any position.
The petitioner ranked fifth on the merit list, and there were purportedly only six positions available.
Three positions were reserved for Schedule Caste candidates, and three were held vacant by court order and directorial directions.
The reasons given by the respondent were either non-existent or misconceived, and twenty-five new posts were advertised.
This arbitrary denial of appointment should entitle the petitioner to a position.
[Paras 9 and 10]
