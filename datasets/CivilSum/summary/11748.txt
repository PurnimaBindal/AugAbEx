Constitution of India, Article 226 - Cut off date - Government letters offered benefits to employees retiring on or before 31.3.1985 - Petitioners argued that they should also be entitled to the same benefits since they were part of the class of pensioners, despite retiring earlier - However, it was held that the Government can impose a cut off date when extending benefits to its employees.
