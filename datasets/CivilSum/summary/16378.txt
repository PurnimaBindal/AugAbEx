According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India and the Gujarat Education Institution Regulation Act of 1984, a person must pass a Diploma Course from a recognised institution in order to be appointed as a teacher.
However, in this case, the petitioner had passed the Diploma Course from the Gujarat State Examination Board, which was duly authorised to issue such a certificate.
Therefore, the termination of their service on the basis of not having passed the Diploma Course from a recognised institution is not sustainable.
[Paras 4 & 5]
