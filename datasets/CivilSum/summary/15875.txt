Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 14 - Recognition of institutions for the purposes of permitting students to appear in examinations - Petitioners passed the Parveshka Examination conducted by the Hindi Vidyapeeth, Devghar (Bihar) - Punjab School Education Board does not recognize it as equivalent to the matriculation examination of Punjab School Education Board - However, Parveshka examination of Hindi Vidyapeeth, Devghar (Bihar) is recognized - The court acknowledges the existence of dubious institutions and dismisses the writ petitions while directing petitioners to look for remedies in civil courts.
