The Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16 and 226, along with General Insurance (Rationalisation of Pay Scales and other Conditions of Service of Development Staff) Scheme of 1976's Rule 11, apply to Development Officers whose performance can be judicially reviewed.
In case of discharge for not meeting business requirements, administrative decisions leading to prejudice to citizens' rights can be considered by the Court.
B Development Officers appealing a discharge must be afforded a personal hearing and the appellate committee must pass a speaking order after considering all material on record and reasons given in the appeal or representation.
Orders passed without affording reasonable opportunity and recording reasons will be considered invalid.
AIR 1986 SC 1173 is a similar example.
