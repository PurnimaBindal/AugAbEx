Punjab Civil Service (Executive Branch) (Class I) Rules, 1976, Rule 8 - Punjab Tehsildars (Class II) Service Rules, 1984, Rule 13 - Completion of probation period - Confirmation - Rule 13 prescribes a maximum probation period of three years, following the completion of which an employee is considered confirmed for purposes of being brought on Register A-1.
[Para 8] B Punjab Civil Service (Executive Branch) (Class I) Rules, 1976, Rule 9(5)(a) - Eligibility for PCS appointment - Qualifying service - Rule 9(5)(a) requires 10 years of continuous service under the government, rather than 10 years of government service solely in the Revenue Department as a Tehsildar or Naib Tehsildar.
Service rendered in the Food and Supplies Department will be counted towards the minimum 10 years of government service required for consideration for appointment to PCS (Executive Branch).
[Para 9] C Punjab Civil Service (Executive Branch) (Class I) Rules, 1976, Rules 2(c), 8 and 12 - Promotion - Direct recruitment - Appointment from Register A-1 is not a direct recruitment, but rather a form of promotion.
This interpretation is consistent with the ruling in 1986(1) SLR 644.
