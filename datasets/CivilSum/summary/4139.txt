Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Probation - Confirmation - Extension of probation - Deemed confirmation - The completion of probation period initially fixed does not result in deemed confirmation - Only if the Rule specifies a maximum probation period and the employee is allowed to continue beyond that, will they be deemed to be confirmed - Without such a stipulation, no deemed confirmation can occur.
