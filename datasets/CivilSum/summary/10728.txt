Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate appointment - Petitioner, aged 39 - Deceased nominated two brothers of Petitioner to receive terminal benefits - Compassionate appointment granted to younger son cancelled due to misrepresentation - Claimed that shares, ancestral land and old house are inadequate for survival - Determination based on objective relative assessment of merits of claimants - Given to those who are financially hard pressed, considering objectives of schemes - Court cannot conclude that determination in such case is illegal or mala fide, except in exceptional circumstances.
