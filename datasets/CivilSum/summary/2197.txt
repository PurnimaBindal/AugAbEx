Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Reserve Police Act, 1949, Section 12 - Dismissal - Employee penalized for overstaying leave by 28 days - No prior record of misconduct - Being part of disciplinary force not sufficient reason for dismissal - Especially extreme considering the employee had already received 30 days of imprisonment for the same offense - Punishment deemed excessive - Employee to be reinstated.
