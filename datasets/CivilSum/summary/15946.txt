Army Pension Regulation, Para 173, Rule 2(c) to the Appendix II - Disability pension - Hypertension - The petitioner was discharged from military service due to essential hypertension, resulting in a 20% disability assessment by the medical board of the Army Hospital.
However, there was no mention of any disease in the record prepared at the time of the petitioner's enrolment in military service after medical examination, and no competent authority recorded any reasoned conclusion that the disability or disease suffered by the petitioner is such that it could not have been detected at the time of his entry into service.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to the benefit of presumption contained in first para of Rule 7(b), and the disability that led to the petitioner being boarded out from service is deemed to have arisen during the course of service and is attributable to military service.
As such, the petitioner is entitled to disability pension as per Rule.
