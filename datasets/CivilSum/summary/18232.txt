Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - ROPA Rules, 1998 - West Bengal Schools (Control of Expenditure) Act, 2005, Section 14 - Additional increment - A graduate category teacher commenced teaching at higher secondary level and sought an additional increment - The petitioner did not make any claim that possession of a master's degree entitled him to benefits - Therefore, the petitioner is not eligible to claim an additional increment.
