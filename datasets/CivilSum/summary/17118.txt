Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pension Regulations for Army, 1961 (Part-I), Rule 50 - Disability Pension - Despite completing only 20 years of service as per rule, the petitioner had completed the qualifying service limit for pension and had also served for 20 years, making him eligible for disability pension - The petitioner is entitled to receive disability pension regardless of having sought voluntary retirement - The respondent authorities are directed to release the disability pension to the petitioner within three months.
