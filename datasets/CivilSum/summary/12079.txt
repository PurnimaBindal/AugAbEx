The petitioner was denied appointment to the post of PGT (History) on the ground that his degree was not MA History but MA in History of Art.
However, it was held that History of Art is only a small part of History and cannot be treated as equivalent to MA in History unless equivalence is granted by the competent authority.
The petitioner failed to show such equivalence and the nomination was rightly cancelled by the board.
It should not be presumed that the candidate is eligible just because he was allowed to appear in the written examination/interview and the board reserves the right to cancel his candidature at any stage if the application is not as per the provisions of recruitment rules or advertisement.
[Paras 7 & 8]
