Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion/Policy decision - Promotion from U.D.C.
to Head Clerk - Department creates an intermediary post of Tax Assistants between UDC and Head Clerk - One seniority list maintained for UDCs and Tax Assistants for promotion to Head Clerk - Tribunal orders Department to make a policy to consider Tax Assistants separately for promotion - Direction from tribunal does not grant right to claim policy - Department responsible for creating promotion policy for employees, as long as it is not arbitrary or discriminatory, the courts/Tribunals cannot interfere.
[Paras 10 and 12]
