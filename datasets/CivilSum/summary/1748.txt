Constitution of India, Article 12 and 226 - State - Cooperative Mini Bank - A Co-operative Mini Bank's functions are not those of a State authority under Article 12 of the Constitution, which means that a writ petition under Article 226 cannot be filed against it.
[Para 3]  B. Haryana Cooperative Societies Act, 1984, Sections 114(e) and 27 - An order passed under the Act's Section 27 can be appealed under Section 114(e) of the Haryana Cooperative Societies Act.
Without using the alternate appeal process, a writ petition cannot be filed.
