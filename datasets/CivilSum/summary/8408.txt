Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Compassionate appointment sought by the respondents in Municipal Council - The respondents filed an application for interim relief, seeking a direction that respondent No. 1 (original complainant) be appointed on a Class-IV post by MC - The Industrial court granted the application - The appellant (MC) argued that the interim relief granted by the Industrial Court was essentially the final relief sought by the respondents - The Single Judge's failure to interfere with the erroneous order of the Industrial Court evidenced a patent error - It cannot be sustained - The appeal prevails.
