Constitution of India, Article 136 - Uttar Pradesh Universities Act, 1973, Section 35(3) - Resignation - Termination - If a teacher voluntarily resigns, the question of termination does not arise - The Managing Committee accepted the resignation and was lawfully in office - The relationship between master and servant ended - Voluntary resignation does not equate to termination - Approval of the Vice Chancellor was not needed for the decision of Management to terminate the services of the employee.
