The Application for condonation of delay based on a filed suit in 1988 has no grounds, as the cause of action arose in 1983, making the suit itself time-barred.
[Paras 1 and 2]
Under Section 19(3) of the Administrative Tribunals Act, the applicant's claim for arrears of salary as a workman falls under Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, which provides an alternate remedy.
Therefore, the applicant's application is barred.
