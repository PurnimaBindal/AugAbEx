Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300-A - Pension - Pensionary Benefits - After serving for over 22 years, the petitioner opted for voluntary retirement, however, the State Government did not approve his pension application - The Court has held that individuals who have rendered more than a decade of service have the right to receive pension, whether voluntarily retired or resigned - Pension is an entitlement that is earned due to one's services rendered - The right to pension is a continuous and recurring right.
