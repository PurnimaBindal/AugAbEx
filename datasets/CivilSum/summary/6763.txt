Administrative Order - Natural justice - The old distinction between a judicial act and administrative act has withered away.
Rules of natural justice must be consistent with administrative orders which involve civil consequences.
The term 'civil consequences' encompasses infringements of not just property or personal rights, but also civil liberties, material depravations, and non-pecuniary damages.
Everything that affects a citizen comes under the purpose of civil rights.
