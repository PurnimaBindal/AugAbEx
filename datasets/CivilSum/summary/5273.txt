According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, classification based on qualifications and duties is allowed when it comes to equal pay for equal work.
This was held to be permissible in the case of Law-Officers who were recruited in various years based on different essential qualifications and for different duties.
Therefore, the doctrine of parity or equal pay for equal work cannot be applied in such situations.
It is a fundamental right, but subject to other factors being equal.
The problem of equal pay cannot always be solved through a mathematical formula.
[Paras 9, 10, and 15]
