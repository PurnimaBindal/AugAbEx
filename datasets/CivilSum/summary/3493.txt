Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 11 - Superannuation - Age of superannuation - Principle of res-judicata - Civil Court passed a declaratory decree stating the petitioner's date of birth as 2.11.1948 - Despite this, the notice of superannuation was issued based on a different date of birth - Writ Court directing appellant to make new orders regarding superannuation of petitioner - Res-judicata principle applies and the Civil Court's declaratory decree on the petitioner's age cannot be modified by the writ Court.
