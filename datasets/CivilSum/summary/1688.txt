The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 17-B, states that if an appeal or other proceedings are pending in the High Court or Supreme Court regarding an award passed by the labour tribunal to reinstate a workman, the employer must pay the full wages last drawn upon proving the workman's unemployment for that period.
If it is established that the workman has been receiving sufficient remuneration during that period, the court can order that no wages be paid for that time.
