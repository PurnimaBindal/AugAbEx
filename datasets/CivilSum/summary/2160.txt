Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - Haryana Co-operative Societies Act, 1984 - Writ jurisdiction - A writ petition against Shahabad Cooperative Sugar Mills Limited is maintainable because the Authority under the Act controls, regulates, and supervises the Mill's activities.
The action or inaction of this authority is amenable to writ petition.
[Para 9]
Service Rules for employees of the Cooperative Sugar Mills in the State of Haryana, Rule 48 - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Relaxation of rules - Respondent No. 4 was not qualified under the Rules to fill the post of Chief Accounts Officer in the Cooperative Mill.
The Board of Directors committed a complete breach of the Rules by extending the benefit of illegalities one after another, even authorizing the absorption of respondent No. 4 into a post that was not vacant, in violation of Rules.
As a result, the order of absorption of respondent No. 4 was set aside.
