The Allahabad Bank (Officers) Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 19 - Compulsory retirement - Assessment of work and conduct - The remarks "want of application to Bank's work", "lack of potential" and "found not dependable" made only in relation to his work and not for any other purpose cannot be said to have cast a stigma as regards the conduct or character of the employee.
[Para 19]
Compulsory retirement - Stigma - Punitive order - Compulsory retirement does not cast a stigma on the Government servant but differs from dismissal and removal in its nature, incidence or effects.
Whether the order of compulsory retirement attaches a stigma would depend upon the facts and circumstances of each case.
[Paras 5 and 17]
Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Stigma - Misconduct - Enquiry - If a formal enquiry is made on an allegation of misconduct and a finding holding him guilty is recorded against a Government servant, the court would infer in such cases that the real intention was to punish the employee not to compulsorily retire him.
[Para 5]
Words and Phrases - Stigma - Misconduct - Stigma according to the dictionary meaning, is a mark or label indicating deviation from a norm, a statement in the order that indicates misconduct or lack of integrity of a Government Servant.
