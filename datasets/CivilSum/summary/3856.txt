Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Select list - Petitioners were placed in a select list for 35 posts of Teachers Grade-II but not appointed - responding with a second advertisement - Respondent contends no right to claim appointment on basis of select list - Govt. may decide on how many selected candidates are appointed to service based on service rules - Petition dismissed.
