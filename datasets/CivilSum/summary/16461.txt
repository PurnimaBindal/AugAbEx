The Punjab Services (Medical Attendant), Rules, 1940 indicate that claims for medical reimbursement cannot be rejected due to delayed submission.
Objections raised by the government in such cases only add to the claimant's difficulties and reflect poorly on the government.
In a specific case where a widow submitted a claim for her deceased husband's treatment after recovering from the shock of his death, there is no issue of limitation.
