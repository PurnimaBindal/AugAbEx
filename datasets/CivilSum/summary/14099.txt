Constitution of India, Article 226 - Revision - Natural Justice - The promotion of the petitioner was dependent on the outcome of court cases or Departmental enquiries against senior colleagues.
Departmental proceedings against seniors were dropped, and the petitioner was reverted to make room for one of them.
The principles of natural justice are not applicable in such a situation, as the petitioner was reverted according to the terms of his appointment.
[Paras 3 and 4] B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Reversion - Reversion orders cannot be invalidated just because there are vacancies available.
Authorities cannot be forced to fill posts if the workload does not justify such hirings.
