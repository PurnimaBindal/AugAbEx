Appointment/Procedure/De-reservation/Reservation - Appointment to a post reserved for a Backward Class candidate was filled from the general category without following the procedure of de-reservation.
According to the procedure, posts should have been advertised three times before de-reservation.
The appointment to the post is illegal.
[Para 17] B. Reservation/Reserved post/De-reservation/Approval - The post reserved for Backward Classes available as Lecturer in Sanskrit was shifted to the post of Lecturer in Sanhita without making any efforts to fill it from candidates of Backward Classes as required by Govt. resolution and University directions.
The sanction to the appointment from the general category was obtained on the basis of inadequate information to the Sanctioning Authority.
The sanction was, therefore, defective in law, and the appointment invalid.
[Para 18] C. Appointment/Selection Committee/Sanction - The nominee of the Vice-Chancellor, the Expert nominated by the University, or the nominee of the Director of Education were not present during selection.
The trust did not apprise the University about the absence of the Expert during selection.
Moreover, the University had not resolved the power to relax the rules and permit the selection without the presence of the Expert.
Nothing on record shows that the University relaxed the defect.
The approval given by the University in ignorance of the true facts is ineffective and cannot validate the appointment.
