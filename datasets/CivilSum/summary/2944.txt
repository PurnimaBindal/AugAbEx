AP University Act 1991, Sections 2(22), 2(23), 19 and 33 - Transfer - Transfer of appointed University service workers from one college to another within the same University premises - Petitioners who were appointed to University service are obligated to follow the terms of their agreement even when transferred - Mere amalgamation of colleges does not change the situation - Modaliites or guidelines set are only internal arrangements without any statutory force - Breaching any condition thereof does not give petitioner the right to invoke Article 226 - No changes in service conditions and no harm caused to petitioner by the transfer proceedings.
[Paras 14 and 16]
