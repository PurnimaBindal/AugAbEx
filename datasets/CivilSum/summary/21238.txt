Goa, Daman and Diu School Education Rules, 1986, Rules 31, 2(1)(i) and 2(1)(f) - Opening of new school - Upgrading of existing school to include new classes - Upgrading from VIIth to Secondary standard requires permission from authorities under Section 31 and compliance with necessary norms - The authority must ensure all required facilities are in place and consider other factors before granting permission for new school - If a request for opening a new school is declined, the authority must review all relevant considerations as per Rule 31 - Previous rejection overturned and authorities directed to make a fresh decision based on relevant factors.
