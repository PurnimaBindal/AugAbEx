The Constitution of India, along with the Data Processing Division and Survey Design and Research Division (Data Processing Assistant) Rules of 1977 and the Data Processing Division and Survey Design and Research Division (Data Processing Supervisor) Recruitment Rules of 1978, were reviewed by the Redesignation of Post.
Following the redesignation, directly-recruited Data Processing Assistants were changed to Data Entry Operators.
However, it was held that recruitment qualifications could not be retroactively altered to deny employees their rightful posts or affect their confirmation.
Therefore, the orders redesignating the appellant were considered arbitrary, illegal, ultra vires, and violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
[Paras 42 & 44]
