Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Revision of pay scales - PGI Chandigarh made a decision to address the grievance of Assistant Store Officers and Senior Storekeepers by revising pay scales on the Punjab pattern, effective from January 1, 1986.
However, the higher pay scales and special pay available in Punjab were not implemented and no reason was given for this.
Despite opportunities, no reply was submitted to the writ petition and authorities did not appear at the final arguments.
The action taken by the authorities was deemed improper and they were directed to release the revised pay scales and special pay on the Punjab pattern.
