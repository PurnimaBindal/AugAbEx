Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay scale - Reversal of order - Two SLPs (Nos. 19257 of 95 and 16646 of 95) were filed regarding the same order of the Central Administrative Tribunal.
SLP No. 16646 of 96 was upheld, but present SLP No. 19257 was dismissed.
An application to reverse the order was granted, and leave was granted.
The appellants are eligible for all the benefits granted to them in the civil appeal that arose from SLP No. 16646 of 1995.
