Constitution of India, Article 14 - Selection - Recommendations of SSS Board - The Board cannot recommend more candidates than the requisitioned amount, and the appointing authority has the right to refuse to appoint from recommendations for valid reasons - In this case, the Board recommended 28 candidates against the requisition of 4, and the appointing authority was justified in returning the recommendations and refusing to make appointments - Ad-hoc appointees received a stay against termination of their services.
