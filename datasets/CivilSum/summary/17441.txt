According to Section 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act, an order was issued in a writ petition instructing Police Authorities to issue appointment orders to SPOs/Auxiliary Constables through absorption in PAP with the benefit of seniority and pay fixation.
The petitioner had already been selected and appointed as Constable in PAP with others.
Further proceedings in the contempt matter were deemed unnecessary.
The petitioner was advised to approach the appropriate authority if he was dissatisfied with seniority in the cadre.
However, the authorities were directed to permit the petitioner to begin his duties.
