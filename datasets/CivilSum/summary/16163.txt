Punjab State Agricultural Marketing Board (Class II) Services Rules, 1988, Rule 8, Appendix 'B' - Seniority/Experience/Promotion - The requirement for promotion to the post of Sub-Divisional Officer (Civil) is an experience of 10 years of working as a Draftsman.
This experience can be acquired from any capacity and does not have to be specifically in a particular service, Board or department.
This is to ensure that the individual has the necessary skills for the job, and it would be unfair to exclude experience acquired outside the Mandi Board from consideration.
[Paras 7 to 10]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab State Agricultural Marketing Board (Class II) Service Rules, 1988, Rule 8 - Promotion/Seniority/Merit-Cum-Seniority - When considering promotion, a senior person has the right to be considered before his juniors.
However, he does not have the right to be promoted over his juniors solely on the basis of seniority.
Merit must also be taken into account when considering promotions.
[Paras 12 and 13]

Constitution of India, Article 226-Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 Res judicata/withdrawal of petition/second petition - An employee can challenge the promotions of his juniors through multiple petitions.
The withdrawal of a petition against one promotion does not prevent him from challenging future promotions.
[Para 14]
