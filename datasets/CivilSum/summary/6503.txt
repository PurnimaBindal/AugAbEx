Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Criminal case - Employee termination due to FIR against their private affairs - Acquittal - If the employee was entitled to continue in service on a regular basis and their services were terminated solely due to an FIR against them, upon acquittal they are eligible for reinstatement with continuity of service, full back wages, and to be considered for regularisation according to rules and instructions.
