Section 55(i) of the MP Cooperative Societies Act and Rule 49(i) of the Cooperative Central Bank Employees Rules, 1977, along with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India are valid.
Dismissal under Rule 49(i) is confined to cases where the employee has been convicted and sentenced for an offence by a sentence of imprisonment.
The presumption is that the employee has had a proper trial where they have had the full right to defend themselves in accordance with the law.
Thus, a separate departmental inquiry is considered unnecessary and there has been no violation of the principle of natural justice.
[Para 5]

The right to reinstatement does not carry with it the right to back wages.
When the appellant was convicted and subsequently terminated but ultimately acquitted in revision, he was reinstated without back wages.
The employer has the discretion to grant back wages after taking into account all relevant circumstances and such a fetter cannot be considered arbitrary.
