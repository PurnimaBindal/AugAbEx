Constitution of India, Article 226 - Interpretation of Statute - Selection - According to Rule 6(k) of Management of Recognised non-Government Institutions (Aided and Unaided), 1969 Rules, candidates up to Honours Graduate level must be sponsored by the Local Employment Exchange and for Post- Graduate candidates the authorities must approach the National Employment Exchange.
It is a well-settled principle of law that the Statute must be read as a whole, and interpreted keeping in view its object and purpose.
The statutory authority must act within the four corners of the Statute.
When a particular procedure is laid down by the Statutory Authority to do a thing in a particular manner, the same must be done in the manner prescribed.
The School authorities and the Authorities of Employment Exchange were found to follow the statutory provisions.
Once it is prescribed that Post- Graduate candidates should be sponsored by the National Employment Exchange, the Local Employment Exchange could not sponsor the petitioner.
The School Authorities cannot be directed to issue an interview letter to the petitioner.
