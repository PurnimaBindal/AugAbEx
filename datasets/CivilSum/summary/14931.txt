Article 16 of the Constitution of India, Rules 9(3) and 9(4) of the Punjab Service of Engineers Class II Public Works Department (Public Health) Rules - Selection and Promotion - The Committee should meet annually to consider eligible officials for promotion to Class II service - If the Committee fails to meet in any given year for some reason, the selections made in subsequent meetings will not be invalidated - Rules should not be interpreted to imply that selections made in subsequent meetings will stand nullified if the Committee has not met in the preceding year.

of Engineers Class II having been selected as a Sectional Officer - The Committee shall meet once a year to consider the cases of candidates eligible for promotion to Class II service - The committee could not hold its deliberations in 1976 and 1977 due to respondents' applications - The meeting was held in 1979, following which Petitioner was informed of his eligibility - Petitioner was not interviewed, and respondents were placed on the selection list - The rule was to be interpreted as an enabling provision and it was fair to assume that the committee would meet once a year, but if it could not meet for some reason, the selection made thereafter would not be invalidated.
