Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab State Electricity Board Employees Punishment and Appeal Regulations, 1971, Regulation 17 - Departmental Proceeding - In an enquiry report, if exoneration is found, an order of punishment cannot be given.
Members of Board in this case had cut 10% permanently on the pension of the Petitioner which is arbitrary, whimsical and violative of Article 14 of the Constitution.
Therefore the order is quashed.
[Para 3]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punishment - Non-speaking order - It is an established principle of law that authorities are required to pass detailed speaking orders when it comes to the civil rights of employees which may be adversely affected.
As a result, the order is liable to be quashed on this ground alone.
