Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - NBARD (Staff) Rules, 1982, Rule 3(b) - Promotion - Selection Committee - The writ petition of the appellant, who challenged his non-consideration for promotion due to the Selection Committee being improperly constituted and executive powers exercised arbitrarily, was wrongly dismissed on the basis of estoppel due to his participation and success in the 1987 interview - The Single Judge must consider and evaluate all grounds challenged by the appellant in the writ petition - The case is remanded and to be decided on fresh merits by the Single Judge.
