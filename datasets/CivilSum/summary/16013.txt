The Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Admission Rules, Para 2249 - Grading of petitioner in the category of sportsperson for admission in Punjab Engineering College and Chandigarh College of Architecture - Paragraphs 2.2.4.5 and 2.2.4.9 state that grades are awarded based on candidates' performance in various sports disciplines and tournaments, as well as on their performance in an interview and trial before the committee.
The purpose of this evaluation is to determine if a candidate is capable of delivering a good performance in a particular sport.
Since a candidate may be awarded a certificate of participation without physically participating, a fresh evaluation is required to check their actual performance.
It is unreasonable to ignore the grading assigned to the petitioner by the committee.
The petitioner cannot claim discrimination or arbitrary denial of admission to the Engineering College, and the petition is dismissed.
