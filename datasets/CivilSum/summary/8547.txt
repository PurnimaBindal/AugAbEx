Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 41 Rule 5 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 162 - Absence from duty - Dismissal - The respondent-plaintiff was dismissed from their service due to unauthorised absence, but raised a plea for non-application of mind and the absence of two required elements under Rule 162 in their civil suit.
Despite multiple opportunities, the appellant-defendants failed to file a written statement and their defense was struck off.
The trial court ultimately concluded that absence from duty was not a grave misconduct that warranted dismissal and the order of striking off defense was not challenged.
The respondent discharged their onus by presenting unrebutted evidence, while the appellant-department failed to meet their primary onus regarding the consideration of the two elements under Rule 162.
As a result, the order of dismissal was overturned for a different reason than stated by the trial court.
