Under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, transfer on administrative grounds is permitted by the State Government, but it must be done reasonably and in the public interest.
While the government has the power to transfer employees, there is a distinction between the existence of power and the exercise of it.
The government cannot use its power to treat employees as "ping-pong balls" to be moved from one station to another without any administrative reason and without discussing the matter on affidavit.
Such arbitrary exercise of power violates Article 14 of the Constitution.
