Constitution of India, Articles 226, 16 - Family pension and retiral benefits - Non-payment - Widow of a class-IV employee whose husband died in 1982 files plea - Claiming that she has been trying to get her rightful dues since then - Authorities claiming delay is due to non-supply of required documents - Court rules that this is not a valid excuse for the non-payment - Orders payment of pension with 12% interest from the due date to the date of payment - Rules that no penal rent be charged for staying in Government quarter for the period till 1985 after the death of the husband of the petitioner - Petition allowed.
