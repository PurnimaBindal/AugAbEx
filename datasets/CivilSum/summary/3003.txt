According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, and 226, as well as the West Bengal Primary Education Act of 1973 and the Rural (Bengal) Primary Education Act of 1930, a petitioner who worked as an unapproved Assistant Teacher on a temporary basis for about 13 years cannot claim regularisation, despite being recommended by the school authority for a permanent post.
The petitioner's entry into service was done illegally and in disregard of Recruitment Rules, making her ineligible for regularisation in violation of statutory and mandatory provisions.
The petition has been dismissed.
