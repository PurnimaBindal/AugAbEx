Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Appointment - It is not permissible to order vacancies that have been unfilled due to candidate nonattendance to be offered to further candidates based on their merit.
This is because there is no rule about preparing a waiting list for such applicants.
Simply being selected does not provide an inviolable entitlement to job appointment.
[Paras 1 & 6]
