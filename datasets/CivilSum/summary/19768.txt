According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, appointments for Veterinary Officers are subject to concessions for the children/grand-children of freedom fighters.
However, only those freedom fighters and their wards who were granted a freedom fighter's pension from Punjab Govt. or awarded Tamra Patras for political sufferings during pre-Independence freedom movements are eligible for these concessions.
Despite the petitioner's grandfather being well to do and never seeking to avail of the scheme for grant of pension for freedom fighters, the petitioner's grand-father had suffered imprisonment during freedom struggle, as confirmed by the Deputy Secretary to Govt. Punjab and observed by the District Magistrate.
The respondents' exclusion of the petitioner from the category of freedom fighters due to the lack of formal Tamra Patra or availing the scheme of freedom fighters pension is arbitrary and discriminatory.
Therefore, the respondents are directed to consider the petitioner's claim against one of the two posts reserved for the children/grand-children of freedom fighters.
