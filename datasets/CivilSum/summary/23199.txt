According to Rajasthan Govt. Circular dt. 2511992, Class-IV employees in Ministerial and Subordinate Services can receive selection grade for promotion if they have not been promoted until after completing nine years of service and if the first selection grade granted to them was lower than the pay scale of 2nd selection grade, and if they have not received three promotions earlier in their existing cadre with the selection grade granted being lower than the pay scale of 2200-400.
The third benefit will become available upon completion of 27 years of service.
It is not available to those who have already received three promotions.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7] B.
Promotions are governed by service rules and conditions, not general principles of justice and fair play.
Discrimination is only relevant among equals and not between different cadres.
