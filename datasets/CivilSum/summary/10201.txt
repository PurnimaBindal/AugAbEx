Chartered Accountants Act, 1949, Sections 16, 21, 22 Part I Clause 9 of First Schedule - Professional misconduct - Enquiry - Further enquiry ordered by Council - Maintainability - Council's decision made without observation or finding - Not tenable, matter remanded for new consideration.
