Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Instructions dated 6.4.1964 - Appointment - Military service benefit - For shortage of doctors and engineers in Armed Forces, a scheme was introduced to attract young people by conferring benefit of seniority, increment, and pension - The scheme also applies to medical students enrolled in medical institutions in the State - They will be given special concession upon re-employment under State Services on discharge from the Army - The words 'deemed to have been seconded' indicate that the appointment of petitioners was a deemed process, a service under Punjab Government - On their release, they were to be absorbed in their initial posts in Punjab State Services - Petitioners seeking a fresh appointment in 1974 does not prevent them from claiming military service benefit as per instructions - State directed to consider petitioners as State Govt. employees from 11.12.1968.
