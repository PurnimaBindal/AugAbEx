It was argued that the order relied upon by the Tribunal was not the appointment order but a selection order and that the order of removal was passed by an authority lower than the appointing authority, but this contention was not raised earlier.
Nevertheless, it is uncontested that only the appointing or a higher authority can pass a final order.
In light of this, the orders of the CAT and High Court are modified to the extent that the DRM can investigate all relevant aspects after giving the respondent a chance to participate and conclude the departmental inquiry as quickly as feasible.
