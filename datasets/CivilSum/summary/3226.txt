Sections 15 and 22 of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936 and Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act gives workmen two independent remedies - The remedy of one does not prohibit the other - Even if a claim under Section 15 of the Payment of Wages Act is dismissed as time-barred, it can still be pursued through an application under Section 33-C(2) of the ID Act, as it is not a civil suit which could be blocked by Section 22 of the Payment of Wages Act.
