The Constitution of India, Articles 16, 39(a) and 311 - Bombay University Act, 1974, Section 409, Item No. (XXIII) (A) - Petitioners, who had worked as part-time teachers for 12 to 34 years, were deemed to be permanent employees and thus could not be terminated under the law.
[Paras 9 and 12]
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Part-time teachers are entitled to equal pay for equal work compared to their similarly situated counterparts, and a violation of the doctrine of equal pay for equal work infringes both Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
