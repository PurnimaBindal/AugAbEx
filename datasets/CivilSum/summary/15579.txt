According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Family Pension Scheme, 1964 and Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Aided Schools Retirement Benefits Scheme, 1992, Clauses 6, 8, 12 and 13, the length of service of a deceased employee is irrelevant for deciding eligibility for family pension.
Once a regular employee joins the service, he is covered by the scheme and the dependents of the deceased employee are entitled to the benefits of the scheme, regardless of the length of service of the employee.
