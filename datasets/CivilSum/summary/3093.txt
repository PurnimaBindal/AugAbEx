Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25F(a), 25-F(b) - Termination/Retrenchment of services - Unfair labour practice - The petitioner was given an ad hoc clerk appointment for 89 days with no commitment for regularisation of services.
After the expiry of the 89 days period, the tenure of her services was extended with similar extensions granted each time.
The petitioner was not given any posting order after maternity leave and no notice or compensation was paid upon termination of her service, despite the availability of clerk positions.
It was held that the termination amounted to retrenchment and was declared null and void.
