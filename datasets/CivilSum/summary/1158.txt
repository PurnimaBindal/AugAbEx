Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2(1) - Dismissal from service - Gravest misconduct - Absence from duty - The punishing authority must be satisfied that the police official's conduct is incorrigible and that he is unfit for police service before imposing the penalty of dismissal - A single act of grave misconduct is sufficient to warrant dismissal, but the order must conform to the stipulations of Rule 16.2(1) - In this case, the petitioner was dismissed without proper consideration of the gravest act of misconduct and his incorrigibility - The order of dismissal is quashed.
[Paras 9,10 and 14]  B Constitution of India, Article 311 - Misconduct - Past act of misconduct - Absence from duty - Once a past act of misconduct, such as absence, has been condoned and the punishing authority is satisfied that it was bona fide, it cannot be considered for future punishment purposes.
