The Petitioner was appointed as Assistant against a reserved post for Scheduled Tribe and was seeking promotion to the post of Senior Assistant.
However, after the conversion of vacancies belonging to Scheduled Tribe category into Scheduled Castes, the petitioner's prospects were foreclosed.
Since the petitioner cannot be promoted before completing five years of service, he is not entitled to any relief in this writ petition.
[Para 6]

The Petitioner contended that the 4th point of reservation in a 40 point roster has to be in favor of Scheduled Tribes and that at least one vacancy ought to have been made in favor of Scheduled Tribes in the year in question.
However, reserving one more vacancy for Scheduled Tribe point will exceed the principle of reservation by 50%.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to any relief.
[Paras 19 and 20]
