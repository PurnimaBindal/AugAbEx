Punjab Civil Service (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8(17) - An individual facing charges is entitled to a fair opportunity to present their case - If this opportunity is denied, the trial is invalidated and must be dismissed - Government employees can serve as their own witness, either at the beginning or end of their examination - This allows for evidence to support the employee's plea, as well as their personal testimony - It is clear that the plaintiff was not given the chance to examine witnesses in support of their argument - The appeal was subsequently rejected.
