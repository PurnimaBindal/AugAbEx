Under the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 7A and 2A, if an owner of an export establishment sets up another export establishment and both are separately registered under the Central Sales Tax Act and the Employees State Insurance Act, they cannot be considered branches of one another unless there is clear evidence of supervisory financial or managerial control, as per the ruling in the 1998(2) SCC 446 case.
Total independence of the two units is also noted.
