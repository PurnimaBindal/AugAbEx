As per Constitution of India, Article 311 and Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 2125(2) and 138, deputationists who receive officiating and fortuitous promotions while on deputation under Rule 21.25(2) cannot claim to be treated as confirmed regular promotees after a probationary period has expired.
They cannot be considered as regularly absorbed in the borrowing department unless there is a specific order to that effect.
