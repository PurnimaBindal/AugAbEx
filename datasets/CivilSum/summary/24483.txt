Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission in Educational Institutions - Eligibility conditions - Unreasonable Conditions - A requirement for candidates seeking admission to an MBBS course at a Medical College in Delhi to have received education in a Delhi school for the last two years was deemed unreasonable for those whose parents had been posted outside of India - Petitioner was otherwise eligible for admission based on merit - Directives were given to admit her to the Course.
