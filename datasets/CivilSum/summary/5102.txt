Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Daily wagers - Regularisation - The Tribunal directed the creation of posts to regularize workmen who have been in service for 18 to 20 years, as the work being done is not of temporary nature and the petitioners are unfairly trying to elicit respect out of fear of insecurity.
This inference is drawn as the scheme of regularization was not implemented and the liberty granted by the Apex court was never utilized.
The Tribunal's findings and consequential directions are upheld.
