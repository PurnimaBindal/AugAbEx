The Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 and 309 and Bombay Civil Services Rules, 1959, Rule 161(aa)(ii) mention voluntary retirement.
In this case, the petitioner's request for voluntary retirement was refused but the refusal was communicated after the expiry of the notice.
However, it was found that material facts about departmental enquiry and criminal case pending against the petitioner were not disclosed.
The writ petition was dismissed due to the petitioner's concealing of these material facts.
Rule 161 empowers authorities to withhold permission for voluntary retirement and there was no prejudice caused to the petitioner by the delay in communication of refusal.
Moreover, the petitioner had also reached the age of superannuation.
This is stated in paragraphs 9 to 11.
