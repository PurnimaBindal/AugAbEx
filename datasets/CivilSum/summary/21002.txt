Constitution of India, Article 32 - Court proceedings - Conduct of Officer - Impropriety of Office - The court expressed disapproval of the conduct of the appellant, a senior IAS Officer, for writing letters to high-ups in a matter involving his close relations.
An application was made to expunge the remarks, but the court ultimately decided that the letters were not written with a malicious intent, but rather to expedite the investigation, and thus the remarks were expunged.
