According to Limitation Act, Article 113, a suit for declaration must be within a certain time frame.
When an order was passed on 131980 and the representation was dismissed on 1251981 with no provision of representation, a suit filed for declaration on 2131984 was not within the permitted time frame.
[Paras 9 and 10]

Changes in date of birth/retirement require proper evidence.
The evidence presented by the plaintiff, a school leaving certificate from Pakistan, was neither authenticated by the Indian Embassy, nor otherwise proved on record.
Furthermore, producing a certificate after retirement means that it cannot be relied upon.
[Paras 11 and 12]

According to Civil Procedure Code, Order 6, Rule 17 and Order 41, Rule 27, amendments to pleadings must provide proper explanation as to why the fact sought to be incorporated was not pleaded at the first instance.
Without proper explanation, amendments will not be allowed.
