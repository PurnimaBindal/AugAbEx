Section 12(3) of the Industrial Disputes Act 1947 states that even if minority unions did not take part in the settlement, it is still binding on them.
This was established in the case of Barauni Pragtisheel Shrmik Parishad v. Indian Oil Corporation Ltd AIR 1990 SC 1801.
[Para 4] B According to Sections 18(1)(3) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, settlements can be divided into two categories: those made outside conciliation proceedings and those made in the course of conciliation proceedings.
While a settlement of the first category only binds the parties to the agreement, a settlement of the second category is binding on all parties to the industrial dispute, those summoned to appear in conciliation proceedings, all persons employed in the establishment or part of the establishment relevant to the dispute, and all others who join the establishment afterwards, with the objective of upholding the sanctity of settlements.
