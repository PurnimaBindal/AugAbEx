Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 & 21 - Compassionate appointment - Claims submitted after significant delay are not to be considered - The objective of granting compassionate employment - Railway Board's Circular authorizing appointment even after 20 years of an employee's death is not in line with the general appointment provisions and is discriminatory as other eligible and more meritorious candidates could lose their right to fair consideration for such appointment.
