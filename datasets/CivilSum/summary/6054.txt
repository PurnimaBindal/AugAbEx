Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 139 - Deputation to Intermediate School Course - Head Constables must be deputed to the Intermediate School Course based on their seniority - This rule was established by a full bench in AIR 1970 Punjab and Haryana 481 and has been stated repeatedly in numerous subsequent cases - Despite this, authorities continued to violate the rule of seniority - They have been directed again to adhere to the rule, and any violations would be considered contempt of court - The responsible officer for such violation would face consequences.
[Paras 6 and 8]

Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 13.9 and 1922 - Seniority/Deputation to the Intermediate School Course - Head Constables should be deputed to the course according to seniority for training - However, Rule 1922 cases should be an exception - Furthermore, there are no rules allowing for individuals working at the Police Training School to be deputed for the training course out of turn - Therefore, all those who have been deputed in violation of the rule of seniority must be called back and disregarded.
[Para 10]
