Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay - Equal Pay for equal work - Qualification - Reasonable Classification - Petitioners, Physical Directors who work in private aided Junior College, assert that they should receive the same pay scale as Junior Lecturers in Government Junior Colleges - the qualification to obtain the benefit of the said scale of pay is prescribed by G.O.Ms.
No. 159 dated 2241991 applicable to Physical Directors Working in Govt.
Junior colleges as well as those working in private aided college - the requirement of higher qualification to receive higher pay is not unreasonable, arbitrary, or against Article 14 - Article 14 ensures equal treatment and prevents arbitrary state action while it permits reasonable classification for legislation or administrative mandates.
