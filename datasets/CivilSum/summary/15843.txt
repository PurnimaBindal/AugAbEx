Constitution of India, Article 16 - Resignation of post - Generally, a Government employee's resignation becomes valid and their service terminated upon acceptance.
In cases where an employee resigns and joins another service before their resignation is accepted, the acceptance becomes effective from the date they joined the new service.
Such an employee cannot claim to still be in the service of their former employer just because their resignation has not been formally accepted.
