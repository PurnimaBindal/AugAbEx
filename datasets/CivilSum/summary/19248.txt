The Rajasthan Employment of Physically Handicapped Rules, 1976, Section 2(v) and Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 were utilized in denying a polio-affected petitioner's appointment as Assistant under the reserved quota for physically handicapped individuals.
However, according to section 2(v) clause (c), orthopaedically handicapped individuals are defined as those with major physical defects or deformities that interfere with normal bone, muscle, and joint functioning.
As the petitioner falls under this definition, the Respondents were directed to consider their name for appointment immediately.
