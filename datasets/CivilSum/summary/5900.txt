Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 331 - Compulsory retirement/Natural Justice/Departmental Enquiry/Enquiry Report - Delinquents must receive a copy of their enquiry report, and have the opportunity to be heard on the quantum of punishment before an order of punishment is passed.
An order of pre-retirement as a measure of punishment cannot be sustained if natural justice principles have not been followed during departmental proceedings.
[Paras 10, 16 and 17]
Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 311 - Civil Servant - Bank employees - Bank employees are not considered civil servants, and therefore are not eligible for the protection of Articles 309 and 311 regarding second notice.
However, the principles of natural justice still require that delinquent officials are given a fair and reasonable opportunity to defend their case.
[Para 15]
