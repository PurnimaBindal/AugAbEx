The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Executive instructions - Ad hoc appointment - Regularisation of service - The petitioner in this case has served for a period of 10 years prior to filing this writ petition.
The court orders that the respondents should consider the petitioner's case for regularisation in accordance with government instructions and the Supreme Court's decision in State of Haryana v. Piara Singh, 1992(3) SCT 201.
Until the final orders are passed, the petitioner should be allowed to continue working in her current position.
