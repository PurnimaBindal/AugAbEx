Rajasthan Subordinate Offices Ministerial Staff Rules 1957, Rules 7(3), 12 - Promotion - Withdrawal of - Recovery - After the petitioners were promoted from class IV post to post of LDC, their promotions were re-given from the date when provision to make such promotion was inserted in the Rules.
An order was passed to recover the excess amount already paid.
However, it was held that the petitioners worked on a permanent post which was available and were entitled to a minimum of pay-scale with allowance of post.
Additionally, since Rule 26(3) permits appointing authority to make such temporary appointment, the order of recovery was deemed perverse and arbitrary.
[Paras 7 and 8]
