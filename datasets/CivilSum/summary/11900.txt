Constitution of India, Article 16 - UP Recruitment of Dependent of Government Servants (Dying-in-Harness) Rules, 1974 (As applicable to educational institutions vide GO dated 1391981), Rule 5 - Compassionate appointment - Supernumerary post - The petitioner's father was a CT Grade teacher, but the petitioner was not initially appointed because he did not possess a teaching qualification.
However, after obtaining a B.Ed.
degree following his father's death, the petitioner is entitled to be appointed as a CT Grade teacher.
If there is no vacancy available, a supernumerary post must be created in order to appoint the petitioner to the position of CT Grade teacher.
[Paras 5 and 7]
