According to Section 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the termination of a Haryana Roadways conductor's employment is in question.
While the conductor was accused of charging an excess fare, the claim against him was not one of embezzlement, but of defaming the Department.
As such, the petitioner should not have been terminated from employment.
The court thus directs that the petitioner is to be reinstated without backwages; and is to be given continuity of service.
