Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 226 - Husband and wife posted at same place - Husband transferred - No reasons stated - Article 21 guarantees the right to dignified life, which includes a livable life for both the husband and wife - Additionally, State Policy aims to keep couples together at the same location as much as possible - Therefore, the transfer cannot be justified in the absence of any valid grounds - The petitioner is directed to submit a representation to the State Government, and until then the transfer order is stayed.
