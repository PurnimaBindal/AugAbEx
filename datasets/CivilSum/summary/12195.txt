According to Sections 8F(3)(i) and 11(2) of the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, an employer is liable to make contributions.
However, enforcement against third parties can only be made when the Commission finds that the employer's money is or will be in the hands of such party.
If not, any demand notice made to a third party would be without authority of law and ultra vires of the Act as stated in Paras 11 and 12.
