Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Punishment - Adverse remarks - Promotion - Employee punished on the basis of adverse remarks conveyed through a departmental enquiry; however, the punishment was later overturned on appeal.
Although a representation against the adverse remarks was previously dismissed, the setting aside of the punishment order renders the very basis of the adverse remarks questionable.
As such, a second representation against the same remarks must be considered in light of changed circumstances, and the employee's case for promotion from the date his junior was promoted over and above him must be assessed accordingly.
