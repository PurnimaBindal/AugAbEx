Article 16 of the Constitution of India allows ad hoc service to count towards seniority and other service benefits as well as regular service.
In addition, according to existing judgments, the ad hoc period which is countable for purposing seniority and other service benefits can be counted towards regular service.
[Paras 43 to 48]  B.
Regular service encompasses continuous service, but the expression "continuous" can sometimes overlap with "regular."
Continuous service becomes retrospectively regular only if it is permanently made under the law or service rules applicable.
[Para 40]  C The courts are required to interpret laws, regulations and orders in such a way that they define the objective and purpose of the said laws, regulations and orders.
If a provision was made for the benefit of the civil servant after the removal of anomaly, the court should not adopt a conservative or technical approach which may frustrate the object for which the provision was made.
[Para 38]  D If the initial appointment was made by following the procedure laid down by the rules and the appointee continues in the post without interruption until the service is regularized, the officiation or ad hoc service shall be counted towards seniority.
However, if the initial appointment was only ad hoc and not according to rules and made as a stop-gap arrangement, the officiation in such a post cannot be taken into account for considering the seniority.
