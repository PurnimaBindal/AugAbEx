Rule 16.2 of Punjab Police Rules, Volume II, states that a delinquent official guilty of misconduct while on duty can be dismissed.
The petitioner was found under the influence of liquor on only one occasion and there is no evidence that he created a nuisance under the influence of liquor.
Also, the petitioner had completed 10 years of qualifying service for pension under Punjab Civil Service Rules and no regard was shown to the length of his service and pension claim.
Therefore, the order of dismissal from service is arbitrary and illegal.
[Paras 5 and 6]

As per Rule 16.2 of Punjab Police Rules, Volume II, the punishment of dismissal from service is not appropriate in case of a misconduct of ordinary nature, like a single stray case of taking liquor by the petitioner.
[Para 5]
