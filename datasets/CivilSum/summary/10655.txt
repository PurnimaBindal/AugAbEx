Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Post of Accounts Clerk - The appointment order must be issued after the selection process is completed, and Circular dated 1911993 cannot be used to deny the selected candidate the fruits of their appointment.
The circular cannot make the appointment order issued prior to its date defeasible, and the petitioner did not challenge the order of the Tribunal.
As a result, respondent No. 1 had to file a contempt petition, and he was not paid any salary for over eight months.
However, respondent Nos. 2 and 3 were under an obligation to pay salary to respondent No. 1 from the date he had joined.
There is no error or infirmity in the order passed by the Tribunal, and respondent No. 1 is entitled to salary from the date he had joined.
