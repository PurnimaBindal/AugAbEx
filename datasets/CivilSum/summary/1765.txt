The petitioner, who was a regular JBT Teacher in a private school, is entitled to pension/retiral benefits for the period he served the government from 25.10.1966 to 30.6.1982.
He is also eligible to receive interest at a rate of 12% pa. on the arrears of pension payable to him.
[Paras 9 and 15] B.
The claim for pension/retiral benefits cannot be rejected on the grounds of delay and laches, as it is a recurring right that accrues every month.
Additionally, the arrears payable to him are restricted for a period of 38 months prior to the date of filing of the writ petition.
[Paras 13 and 14] C. A civil suit filed for the grant of pension may have been dismissed on the grounds of limitation, but this should not be used as the sole reason for denying the claim for pension/retiral benefits.
