The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(1)(C) & 10(4), outlines termination of service for a Chowkidar in the Food and Supplies Department who was a daily wager working on fixed wages that were set by the Deputy Commissioner.
Unfortunately, services were dispensed with as deemed to be surplus, without any record regarding the non-applicability of the ID Act by the Labour Court.
Moreover, a claim was made nine years later, and the belated approach was not explained.
Thus, the order of the Labour Court was affirmed by the High Court and cannot be sustained, so the matter was remitted back to the Labour Court for adjudicating about the applicability of the ID Act.
