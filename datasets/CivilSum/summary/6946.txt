During the Regular Second Appeal, it was found that the termination order of the petitioner was based on their conduct leading to their conviction under section 354/452 of the IPC for outraging the modesty of a fellow employee’s wife.
The petitioner was released on probation of good conduct for one year, but the termination order was sustained due to their conduct rendering them unfit to be retained in service.
[Paras 3, 4 and 5] B.
In another Regular Second Appeal, it was held that termination of service can be based on conduct leading to conviction, especially when the disciplinary authority considers the employee's behavior as rendering their retention in public service undesirable.
Hence, there was no infirmity in the order of termination, and the appeal was dismissed.
