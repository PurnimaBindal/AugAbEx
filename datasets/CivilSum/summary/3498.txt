The Constitution of India's Article 311, Army Act 1950's sections 116, 120, 164, and Army Regulation's para 381 deal with Summary Court Martial.
The Summary Court Martial has the power to dismiss a person who is guilty of absence from duty without leave and has committed an offense of housebreaking at night with the intention of committing an offense punishable with imprisonment.
During the trial, the petitioner admitted guilt and was given a due opportunity of hearing.
The trial of an absentee is carried out in the same way as the trial of a deserter.
The summary court martial proceedings were deemed to be free from illegality or error in law, and there were no grounds for interference.
The petition was thus dismissed.
