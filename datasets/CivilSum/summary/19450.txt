Respondents, represented by DS Dhillon, AAG of Punjab, faced an issue regarding the denial of compassionate appointment of the petitioner.
The Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution were invoked to argue that it was discriminatory to deny the petitioner's appointment solely based on the fact that the deceased was a work charge employee.
Other work charge employees have been appointed on compassionate grounds, and thus, the petitioner should be afforded the same treatment.
The order of denial was quashed, and the petitioner's claim was directed to be considered by the respondents.
