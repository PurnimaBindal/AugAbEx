The Constitution of India's Article 226 states that in cases of embezzlement and recovery, the respondent is liable to repay the amount embezzled.
In this specific case, the Secretary, respondent No6, was found to be responsible.
The arbitrator noted that if the amount is not recovered from respondent No. 6, the petitioners (members of the Managing Committee) would be responsible.
However, no reasons were provided to justify this decision.
Therefore, the case has been sent back to the arbitrator to determine the liability of the petitioners.
