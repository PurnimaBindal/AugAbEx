Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 39 - Equal pay for equal work/Regularisation of services - Tikka Mazdoors who were petitioners were denied regularisation on the basis that their duties were unequal to those of regular workers - However, further investigation found that these petitioners were capable of performing the same duties if given the opportunity - Therefore, denying their regularisation on these grounds is not justified.
[Paras 6 and 8] B. Regularisation of service - The petitioners working as Tikka Mazdoors were denied regularisation due to their alleged wrongful appointment - But it was discovered that these petitioners were appointed by the appropriate appointing authority in accordance with recruitment procedures - Therefore, denying their regularisation on this basis is not justified.
