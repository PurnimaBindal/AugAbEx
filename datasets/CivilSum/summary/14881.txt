Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ of quo warranto - Qualifications - Cadre post - Appointment to the post of Director of Electronics without specified qualifications deemed illegal and arbitrary - Govt. to establish rules or executive order outlining proper qualifications for the position before selecting an appropriate candidate - Respondent No. 2 prohibited from acting as Director of Electronics until then - Court unconvinced that a high cadre position can be established without explicit qualifications.
