In accordance with Civil Procedure Code, Order 2, Rule 2 and Section 11, if a plaintiff abandons part of their claim in a previous suit, they cannot bring a subsequent suit for the same claim that was available at the time of filing the earlier suit.
This was demonstrated in a case where a plaintiff sought service benefits from her deceased husband's Provident Fund based on a Will, despite knowing the respondent was nominated to receive the funds.
In the first suit, the plaintiff did not request that the nominee hand over the funds if deemed entitled, so a second suit for that claim is prohibited by Order 2, Rule 2, CPC.
