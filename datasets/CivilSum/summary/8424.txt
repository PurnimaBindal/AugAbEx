Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25-F - Termination - Management terminated a workman who was medically unable to continue in service - The management argued that it was not retrenchment because it falls under section 2(oo)(c) of the Act - However, there is no evidence to support this claim - The labor court upheld the award granting reinstatement, back wages, etc. to the workman.
