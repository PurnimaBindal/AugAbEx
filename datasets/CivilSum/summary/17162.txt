According to Section 14-B of the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, in case of default in payment of contribution, the employer must pay a penalty as damages that do not exceed the amount of arrears.
Additionally, the RPF Commissioner can only claim damages equal to the interest rate of 12% on the loss incurred to the beneficiaries and delay.
Any justification for delay will not result in granting exemption to the defaulter from paying penalty or damages.
