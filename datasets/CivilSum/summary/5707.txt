Constitution of India, Article 311 - Haryana Police Rules, Rule 1624(1)(VII) - Misconduct - A Constable who was dismissed on account of previous bad conduct and misconduct must receive a detailed explanation for the six major and two minor punishments he had suffered.
The length of service must also be considered along with his claim for pension.
The order of dismissal is not sustainable as the mandatory provisions of Rule 1624 were infringed.
