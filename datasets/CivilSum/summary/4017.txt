Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Section 126(2) - Dismissal - Domestic Enquiry - Discretionary - The petitioner, who is a government employee, was arrested for activities that were considered a threat to the security of the State and in violation of Conduct Rules - There was no evidence of any malicious intent - The Governor deemed it not appropriate to conduct an inquiry in the interest of the State's security, and hence the order of dismissal was upheld.
