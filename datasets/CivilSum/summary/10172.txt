Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1)(c)(d), 10(3), 12(5), 22 to 24 - Prohibition of lock-out - Dispute regarding wages and general demands referred - Challenge raised - Claim that the real dispute was not referred - Ruling: conciliation proceedings about general demands were ongoing and non-compliance with Section 10(3) cannot be alleged - Petitioner can seek such reference - Lock-out during dispute adjudication is forbidden under Act - Order stood.
