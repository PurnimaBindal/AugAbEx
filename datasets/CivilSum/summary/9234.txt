Civil Procedure Code, Order 21 Rule 30, Section 34/Excution proceedings/ Interest/Jurisdiction of executing court - The executing court is limited to executing the decree as formulated by the court that passed it, and cannot go beyond it - If a relief is not granted by the court that decided the suit, it will be deemed to have been declined - Furthermore, the provisions of Section 34 of the CPC cannot be used by the executing court to assume jurisdiction to grant interest.
(1989 (2) RSJ 595 State of Punjab v. Radha Ram etc. [Para 16])
