Constitution of India, Articles 39(d) and 14 - Principles - Municipalities - Employers must remunerate equal pay for equal work - In Delhi Municipal Corporation, Vaccinators/Immunisors who have worked for more than eight years on daily wages are paid less than maximum wages - However, other employees regularly appointed doing the same type of work receive higher salaries and allowances - To resolve this issue, the Corporation has been directed to absorb the claimants as regular workers and develop a scheme for equal pay for equal work.
