Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 227 - Disciplinary proceeding - Punishment of removal for non-disclosure of matriculation - Enquiry Officer found no proof of petitioner's higher qualification - Photocopy of genuine document can be considered in departmental proceedings - Non-disclosure of qualification constitutes misconduct - However, penalty of removal is excessive and unjustified in light of petitioner's long-standing employment and satisfactory conduct.
