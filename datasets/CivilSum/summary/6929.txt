The Constitution of India and the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19, the Railway Services (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, Rule 14 (ii) are applicable in a case regarding disciplinary proceedings, misconduct, and removal from service.
An Assistant Engineer was assaulted by the petitioner and an associate while on duty, inciting other employees to stop work.
The charge was proved in an enquiry, but the Tribunal found that it was not possible to hold a normal enquiry.
The application filed under Section 19 was dismissed by the Tribunal, and the Writ petitioner did not appeal the order of removal.
Therefore, there is no reason to interfere with the well-reasoned order of the Tribunal.
