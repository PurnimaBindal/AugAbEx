Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Retrenchment compensation - Calculation method - To properly calculate retrenchment compensation, one must first determine one day's pay by dividing the average monthly pay by 26 (not 30), as employees are expected to work 26 days per month and earn 4 weekly rests as a right.
[Para 11]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Relief - Back wages - In a case where retrenchment was set aside for technical reasons related to the calculation of retrenchment compensation, and the worker had reached the age of superannuation in the meantime, reinstating them with full back wages would not be appropriate.
Instead, the petitioner had already paid Rs. 30,000/- in a lump sum and Rs. 2,000/- per month for over five years, without being on duty, so back wages were limited to the amounts already paid.
[Paras 17 and 18]
