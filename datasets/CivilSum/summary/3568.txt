Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F and 17-B - Termination of services - Daily wager - A petitioner was appointed against a permanent vacant post as a daily wager until a regular employee was appointed - The services of the daily wager were terminated months earlier than the regular appointment made on the post - The petitioner completed 240 days of continuous service in each year - The non-compliance of Section 25-F of the ID Act resulted in the termination of services being ruled as improper.
