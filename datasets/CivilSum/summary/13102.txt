Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Part I, Rules 5.3(1) to (3) and 6.17 - Family Pension Scheme, 1964, Note 2(i) - Receiving pension or family pension is dependent on qualifying service for pension, and employees can only receive pension for the period of service rendered in pensionable Govt service.
If an employee is permanently absorbed in a service of any Govt owned body in public interest, they will be deemed to have retired from Govt service and retiral benefits will be available as per the employee's option.
However, if no option is exercised, the retiree will be automatically governed by alternative Sub Rule (b) and will receive DRG and lump sum payment with no further liability for pension or family pension.
If Sub Rule (a) was applied and the retiree was receiving pension at the time of their death for the period of pensionable Govt Service, the liability of family pension cannot be avoided by relying on Sub Rule (b).
