Kerala State and Subordinate Services Rules, 1958, Rule 38 - Promotion - Relinquishment - Withdrawal of - Petitioner seeks promotion on the basis of withdrawal of relinquishment - The court determined that accepting relinquishment does not prevent a person from retreating their steps, as any other interpretation of the rule allowing relinquishment would violate fundamental employee rights under the Constitution.
