Punjab Police Service Rules, 1959, Rule 6-2, 1317 - Promotion order - Tribunal quashing - Challenge - DPC recommended petitioner's name for promotion as DSP on 'G' list after finding 50% of petitioner's ACR's were good for the years under consideration till 1992 - Promotion given to petitioner as soon as SC candidate vacancy in DSP cadre became available - DPC made mistake in not noticing gist of Respondent No. 4's ACR's correctly - Allegation that IG spoiled petitioner's ACR is apparent from record - Held, rule of promotion being seniority, petitioner rightly recommended for promotion to 'G' list - Tribunal examined ACR's as if promotion is based on merit when promotion is based on seniority cum merit - Order promoting petitioner restored.
