Sections 11 and 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act prescribe punishment for disobedience of court directions or non-implementation of court orders.
A Director of School Education, Jammu, was found guilty of contempt for not implementing the directions of the court and showed disregard by tendering an unconditional apology after conviction.
The courts use the weapon of contempt of court with restraint to maintain the public's confidence in the rule of law.
In general, apology after conviction cannot be accepted.
Unlike bureaucrats, the courts have an open heart, graciousness, and a clear vision without ill-will towards any individual.
However, the contemner was warned to be careful and realize the magnanimity of the law and learn to honor court orders, with no further punishment given in light of the young officer's career.
