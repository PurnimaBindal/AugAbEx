Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion - Seniority - Probation - When promoting Assistants Grade II to Grade I in Kerala Public Services on the basis of seniority-cum-fitness, seniority is not based on direct recruits' completion date of probation - Probation and date of joining depend on other circumstances - Seniority is not affected by these circumstances - Normally, the senior-most in the lower cadre will be promoted when promoting to the next higher post by seniority-cum-fitness - However, if the official was on probation until that date, his promotion will depend on satisfactorily completing his probation period - His service period in the higher cadre will be counted in the lower cadre for probation period - A senior officer cannot be passed over merely because his probation period had not yet been completed.
[Paras 4, 5 and 7]
