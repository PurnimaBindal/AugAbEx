Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection/Promotion - Selection criteria - The Selection Committee used guidelines that outlined certain standards for how marks were allocated under different categories - All candidates were evaluated under the same criteria - The petitioner received interview marks that were equal to or even greater than some of the other candidates - Selection cannot solely be based on one's performance in service - Making selections based on various considerations does not mean unfairness.
