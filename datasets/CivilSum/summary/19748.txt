Under the Constitution of India, as stated in Articles 14 and 16, discrimination is prohibited.
According to the petitioner's merit in the test, they should have been sent to a lower school course, but a lower-ranked female candidate was sent instead.
The petitioner had a preferential right to be sent, and they were denied an equal opportunity in the matter of condition of service because they were not allowed to undergo the lower school course to acquire departmental qualifications.
Thus, the petitioner is entitled to a writ of mandamus against the respondents, directing them to send the petitioner to the course, even if it is necessary to create an additional seat to accommodate them.
