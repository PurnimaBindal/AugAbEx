Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Petitioner seeks admission to "Diploma in Physical Education" (D.P.Ed.)
on the basis of superior merit compared to R-3, R-4, and R-5 who have been granted admission - However, clause 6 of the prospectus states that only certificates issued by the Sports Department will be recognized to give an advantage in admission - As the petitioner's non-graded certificate has not been approved by the admission committee, the petitioner is deemed ineligible - Petition dismissed.
