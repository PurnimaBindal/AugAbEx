Under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 1(3)(a)(b), 2(e) and 14, a Notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Labour (dated 831982) clarifies the applicability of the Act to Municipal Council under question.
It was argued that the Act isn't applicable to people who are not regularly employed and that the Municipal Council isn't an establishment under Section 1(3)(a)(b) of the Act.
However, it was held that the notification refers to Municipal Council as a local body with over 10 employees, including those working on daily or monthly wages.
