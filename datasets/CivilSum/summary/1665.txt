The issue of whether a departmental inquiry should be stayed due to the pendency of a criminal case is a factual question that must take into account the complexity of the case.
Holding a domestic inquiry is not grounds for staying departmental proceedings merely because a criminal case is pending, especially if the facts are not complicated.
[Para 13]
The determination of whether to exercise jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution of India in relation to the Bina Ticket Yatra Ki Rok Adhiniyam, 1974, Sections 7 and 9 charge of failing to issue tickets to passengers will depend on whether the facts of the case are complicated or not.
If not, exercising such jurisdiction is not proper, and the petition will be dismissed.
