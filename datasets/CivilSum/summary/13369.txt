Constitution of India, Article 300A - Disability pension - Fracture - The petitioner suffered a disability due to fracture, but it was only assessed between 6 to 10%, falling short of the required eligibility of 20%.
Additionally, another disability of bladder obstruction was deemed to be an organic and constitutional disease unrelated to Army Service, and therefore not considered.
As a result, the petitioner's claim for disability pension was appropriately denied.
