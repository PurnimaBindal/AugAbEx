Constitution of India, Article 226 - Correction of date of birth - If there is no provision in the statute for correcting the date of birth, an employee cannot compel the employer to make corrections in the service record - Determining the date of birth is a factual matter that can be resolved in a Civil Court based on evidence - The petitioner has a viable alternative to determine their date of birth - Filing a writ is not a valid option.
[Paras 6, 8 & 10]
