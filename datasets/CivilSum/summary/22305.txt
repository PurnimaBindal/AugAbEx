According to Section 2(22) of the Employees State Insurance Act 1948, Incentive bonus is considered a part of wages as it is an additional remuneration, but Sales Commission will only be included in the definition of "Wages" if it is payable within every two months period.
The determination of whether Sales Commission will actually form part of an employee's wages or not will depend on the facts of each individual case under the ESI Act.
