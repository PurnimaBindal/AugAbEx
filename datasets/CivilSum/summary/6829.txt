Back door entries into public employment cannot be safeguarded by invoking Article 14 of the Constitution of India or Section 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
Illegal appointments made contrary to statutory provisions for public employment cannot be protected and no relief can be granted against termination.
However, if a workman has served for a long period and is retrenched, despite a violation of statutory provisions of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the authority must pay compensation of Rs. 40,000.
