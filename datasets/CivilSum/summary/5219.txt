Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - U.G.C.
Guidelines for selection of principals - Dissent note - The dissenting note of the nominees of the Director will not annul the selection if it is in accordance with the prescribed procedure - Majority decision must override the dissenting note unless there is proof of bias or mala fide - A decision cannot be thwarted simply on the basis of a dissenting note.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - U.G.C.
Guidelines for selection of principals - Academic qualifications - The post of principal involves both academic and administrative responsibilities, and so, an individual's academic qualifications may not determine his ability to be a sound principal - The committee selection is arrived at through collective intelligence, and cannot be invalidated solely on the basis of academic qualifications.
[Paras 6 and 12] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - U.G.C.
Guidelines for selection for principals - Selection - The validity of a dissenting note in the selection process - Since the committee's decision is based on collective intelligence, the views and dissents of an individual member should not have an undue weight.
[Para 10] D. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - U.G.C.
Guidelines for selection of principals - The resolution or decision cannot apply retrospectively, it can only have a prospective effect in relation to selections that have already been carried out.
