Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination - Revision - The petitioner was initially appointed as a clerk on an ad hoc basis in Family Court and later reverted to the position of copyist.
The order of reversion was quashed, and the petitioner reported to join duties but was not allowed to do so.
A contempt petition was filed to put him back into service, but instead, the respondent terminated his services, which is not justified when other employees, especially those on an ad hoc basis, have been permitted to continue.
