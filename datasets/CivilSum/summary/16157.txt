The petitioner, a dependent child of an ex-Serviceman, claimed admission on the basis of reservation of seats in an Educational Institution.
However, the claim was opposed on the grounds that the instructions were only meant for reservation in appointments and not for admissions in Educational Institutions.
It was argued that once the father, an Ex-Serviceman, gets service on that basis, his children are not entitled to the benefit.
The court held that an Ex-Serviceman still remains an Ex-Serviceman even if he takes up a Government job after being discharged from the Military Service, and accordingly, a dependent child of an Ex-Serviceman is entitled to get admission against the reserved seat for the dependent of Ex-Serviceman.
