Under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, Section 66 and the Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Sections 457 and 451, if a motor vehicle is seized by the Taxation Authority due to non-payment of taxes levied under the Act and non-possession of the required permit, it cannot be released on Superdari under Cr.PC The Criminal Court does not have jurisdiction in this matter and the provisions of Sections 451 and 457 Cr.PC do not apply.
The verdict of Durgaprasad v. MN Gupta (FA No. 116/1971, decided on 2621975 (D.B.))
has been approved, and 1994(1) MPWN 126 has been overruled.
