The pay scales for skilled and semi-skilled employees, including pump operators, have been linked to educational qualifications since 1969, granting them a higher pay scale of Rs. 140-300.
Pump operators cannot be subjected to hostile discrimination, as such a segregation would not stand the test of Article 14 of the Constitution.
Those with qualifications of ITI of a two-year diploma course with matriculation and have been working as pump operators are entitled to the pay scale of Rs. 140-300 from the date of their employment, but arrears are only granted for up to 38 months before the date of the petition filing.
On the other hand, petitioners without the required qualifications will not receive this relief.
The constitution's Article 14 demands reasonable classification, founded on intelligible differentia that has a rational relation to the object sought by the impugned legislative or executive action.
