Article 226 of the Constitution of India concerns writ jurisdiction and states that disputes on facts cannot be addressed In proceedings under Article 226.
B.
Section 29 of the Punjab Water Supply and Sewerage Board Act, 1976 pertains to the regularisation of work-charged employees.
The Punjab Water Supply and Sewerage Board decided that all work charged employees who have completed five years of service be given full benefits allowable to regular work charged employees.
Petitioners who have had their services regularised claim that this order is discriminatory in nature as individuals who have served for much less time have been equated with those who have served for a much longer duration.
They also claim that this order violates the guarantee in Section 29 of the Act.
The Board only undertakes works allotted to it by the Local Bodies, and the work charged cadre has no regular posts, so the services of the petitioners cannot be regularised retrospectively.
As it is a dispute on facts, the petitioners have the permission to approach the State Government for reference of dispute to the Labour Court/Industrial Tribunal.
The petition has been dismissed.
