Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 12(3) and 18 - Settlement - Award of Labour Tribunal - The inquiry into the fairness of a settlement and an award by way of a settlement with intervention of the Conciliation Officer or the Tribunal is explicitly different from a private settlement between the employer and the employees, which is based on mutual bargain and may not be binding on everyone.
However, a settlement with the intervention of Conciliation Officer or the Tribunal is deemed to be fair as it is approved after judging it by the competent authority, making the nature of inquiry into the fairness of both entirely different.
