Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 21 - Compassionate appointment - Death of Bank Employee in harness during pendency of disciplinary proceedings - The dependents of the deceased should receive all of his terminal benefits, full back wages, and also the opportunity for compassionate appointment at the establishment.
The refusal of these benefits on the basis of tainted employees being excluded is deemed arbitrary, as this argument lacks evidence.
It is important for statutory establishments to not only act fairly, but also humanely in such cases.
