Constitution of India, Articles 215 and 226 - Contempt of Courts Act, 1971 - Regularisation - A previous writ petition sought regularisation and equal pay for equal work, but the order was not implemented.
A successive writ petition was filed to enforce the order, but it is held that a writ petition is not an appropriate and effective remedy to enforce a judgment.
The petitioner should have resorted to the provisions of the Contempt of Courts Act within a reasonable period under the provisions of Article 215.
Therefore, the writ petition is not maintainable.
[Paras 2 and 23]
