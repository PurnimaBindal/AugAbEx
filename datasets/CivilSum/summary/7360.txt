As per the Constitution of India, Article 16 and Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 14, if a candidate approves their appointment as Head Constable on compassionate grounds, they cannot suppress any material facts while filling the verification form.
In a similar case, the petitioner failed to mention their involvement in a criminal case in the verification form but later provided the information when the verification was under process.
The committee reconsidered the case and held that the petitioner was not fit to be appointed in the police force.
The omission of such material facts cannot be considered as a mere mistake as the attestation form had false information and suppression of material fact.
Hence, the view taken by the committee cannot be termed arbitrary, irrational, or unwarranted.
