Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Post of Director (Finance) - Challenge to constitution of interview committee for lack of quorum provision - Court upholds legality of interview committee comprising two remaining members due to absence of chairman and one full-time member - Court notes that as an appellate authority, it cannot interfere with assessments made by respondent-board - No interference called for.
