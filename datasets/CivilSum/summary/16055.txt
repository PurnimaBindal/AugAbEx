Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 2(1)(d) - Compensation - Legal heir - Re-marriage - According to the Act, a widow of a deceased is still regarded as a dependent even after remarriage - Therefore, compensation due to her as a legal heir under the Act cannot be taken away based on the fact she has remarried.
[Para 4] Imp Point.
B Re-marriage - Compensation - Widow status under the Workmen's Compensation Act as a dependent is not lost even after remarriage - Accordingly, compensation cannot be denied even if she has married subsequently.
