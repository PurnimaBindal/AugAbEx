The petition filed by a driver engaged on daily wages claimed regular pay scale based on Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
However, during the pendency of the writ petition, the driver remained absent without leave and was removed from the rolls.
Despite this, the driver moved an application for a direction to join duty and release his salary for the period of absence.
Nonetheless, once the driver's name was removed from the employer's rolls, the writ petition for grant of regular pay scale was no longer applicable and the application for direction to release salary for the absence period was not maintainable.
