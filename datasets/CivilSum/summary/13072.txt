Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 - Revaluation - Reduction in marks - Challenge to circular with such instruction - Petitioner experienced increase in marks for one paper and decrease in marks for another - Court held that there was no illegality, and if the petitioner benefits from increased marks, they must also bear the disadvantages of decreased marks - Therefore, the provision has been previously upheld by a Single Judge in an earlier petition and cannot be deemed arbitrary, unreasonable, illegal, or violative of Article 14 of the Constitution.
