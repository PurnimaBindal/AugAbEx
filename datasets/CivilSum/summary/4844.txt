The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2, Item 3 states that departmental proceedings may result in punishment of dismissal.
If criminal proceedings then result in acquittal, the order of acquittal subsequent in time to the departmental proceedings order must be considered.
In this case, the Labour court examined evidence and material on record before determining that the dismissal was legal and valid.
Even though the criminal court found the employer had lost confidence in the workman, the Labour court's determination cannot be considered erroneous on the basis of the preponderance of probabilities.
