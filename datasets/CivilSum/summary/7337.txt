Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Retrenchment - Abandonment of service - Evidence required - Workman terminated without notice or compensation after three years - Chief Engineer claimed abandonment of service by workman - Executive Engineer testified that services were terminated due to project closure - No proof that workman was aware of temporary nature of employment and project duration - Workman is eligible for restoration with corresponding perks.
