Under Army Pension Regulations, Regulation 173 Paragraph 432 (C) Army Act and Rules/Disability Pension - a petitioner who was invalidated from military service in 1967 due to illness from Cerebral - Thrombosis with Hemiplegia is entitled to disability pension - This is because there is no note of the disease at the time of entry into service and nothing shown that the disease couldn't be detected at that time - It is presumed that the disease was attributed and aggravated during service.
[Paras 4 and 5] B.
Even though a petitioner was declined disability pension in 1967 and communicated in May 1967, he is entitled to it according to the Army Rules and Regulations - The petitioner was finally deprived of what he was entitled to - Delay cannot be a reason to decline the claim for a pension - Directions were thus issued to pay arrears and further pension.
