All India Service Act, 1951, Section 2(a) - All India Services (Confidential Rolls) Rules, 1970, Rule 9 - Confidential Reports - Employees can only challenge confidential reports made against them when they are conveyed to them - The Rule does not give any opportunity to challenge expunction of remarks against an employee.
[Paras 20, 22 and 23]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - All India Services (Confidential Rolls) Rules, 1970, Rule 9 - Indian Forest Service (Regulation of Seniority) Rules, 1968 - Seniority/Promotion - If an employee's adverse remarks in their confidential roll are expunged, they have a right to be reconsidered for promotion from the same dates, but this does not guarantee automatic promotion as eligibility and actual promotion are separate.
