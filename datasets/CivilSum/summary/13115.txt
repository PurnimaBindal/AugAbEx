According to Sections 25-O and 25-R of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the Director cannot be prosecuted for a violation of the Act.
In this case, the petitioner resigned as Director before the resolution of voluntary winding up was passed.
Although the prosecution was ordered, since the petitioner was not involved with the day-to-day management of the company, they cannot be held liable for the alleged violations.
