Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 Admission/Reservation - Admissions in Government Medical Colleges - Providing Roster points for admission to the Post Graduate Medical Courses to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes candidates - 25% reservation in both inservice and open quota - Three seats, including points 1, 5, and 9, are reserved for SC and ST candidates - Admission granted to two candidates in the inservice quota and one seat from the open quota also goes to reserved categories - Denial of the third seat, i.e. 9th point of the roster for the joint quota of 60% and 40% to the reserved categories, is an arbitrary action.
The petition is allowed.
