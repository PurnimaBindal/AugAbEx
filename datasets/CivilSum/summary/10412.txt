A suit was brought by the respondent for a declaration that his date of birth was 1281942 as opposed to 28.4.1940 as recorded in his service record, after his commissioned service of 27 years.
The only evidence the respondent had presented was a certificate issued by Chief Registrar of Births and Deaths which the Plaintiff failed to connect with his birth.
The plaintiff's suit was incorrectly decreed by the District Judge.
