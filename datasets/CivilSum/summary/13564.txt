The Punjab State Employees (Security of Service) Act, 1979 and the Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981 outline conditions of service for employees of private schools taken over by the government.
Despite issuing notifications and circulars for counting past service for benefits, the government has been accused by petitioners of failing to do so.
The court has directed respondents to count service rendered by employees in privately managed government-aided schools before their takeover by the Punjab Government for the reckoning of 8 years and 18 years of experience.
This is for the awarding of proficiency step-up increments, senior scales/selection grades and all post-retirement benefits.
