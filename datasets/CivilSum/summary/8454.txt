Constitution of India, Article 226 - All India Council for Technical Education Act, 1987, Sections 10 and 23 - Education - Admission - Professional college - Reservation Policy - Admission against reserved categories - Respondent argues that petitioner institute must follow reservation policy of either State or Central Government.
In compliance with Supreme Court's directives in Unni Krishnan J.P. and others v. State of Andhra Pradesh, respondents are instructed that institute is not obligated to reserve seats for scheduled castes, scheduled tribes, and other weaker sections of society.
However, institute is barred from canceling previously granted admissions.
