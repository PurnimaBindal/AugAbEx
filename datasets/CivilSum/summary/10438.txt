Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 14 - Removal from service - Severity of misconduct - Proportionality of penalty - Allegation of drunkenly misbehaving with hotel manager and staff by petitioner, a former Driver of Distt.
Magistrate, at a Govt. officers' conference held in the hotel - Removal from service penalty considered excessive in light of the offense - While the competent authority has the power to determine the appropriate punishment, it must be commensurate with the offense and offender - It cannot be vindictive or excessively harsh - Petitioner's removal from service voided and reinstated with three increments stopped cumulatively.
[Paras 5, 7 and 8]
