Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I Part I, Rule 7.3B - Suspension - Acquittal from charges - The punishing authority must order payment of wages for the suspension period while reinstating the accused.
If the charges against the accused are proven false, they are entitled to both their balance wages for the suspension period and consideration for promotion from the date their juniors were promoted during their suspension.
