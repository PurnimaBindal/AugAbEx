According to Sections 25F and 25G of the Industrial Disputes Act, workers who have not been in service for 12 calendar months but have worked over 240 days are not entitled to benefits under Section 25-F However, they are entitled to the benefits of Section 25-G Employers must follow the "last come first go" rule, but giving special reasons to depart from this rule is allowed.
Termination in violation of Section 25-G can be quashed, with reference to AIR 1981 SC 1253, AIR 1960 SC 762, 1980 SC 1454 and 1981(ii) LLJ 174.
