In the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948 (as amended by Act No. 44 of 1966), Section 2(12), the use of the word 'includes' in the amendment expands the scope of the definition of 'seasonal factory', resulting in more establishments being exempt from the provisions of the Act.
A factory engaged in a manufacturing process for up to seven months in a year is also exempt.
[Para 5 to 7] B.
When defining words and phrases, the word 'includes' is used to expand the scope of the definition and not to limit it.
This means that the interpretation clause will include not only the things that are signified according to its natural meaning but also those declared to be included.
