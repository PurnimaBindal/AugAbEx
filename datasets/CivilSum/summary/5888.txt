The dismissal of a workman from service was invalidated by Labour Court due to improper domestic inquiry.
The management failed to examine the enquiry officer and no witness was examined by the management before the enquiry officer.
The enquiry conducted against the workman cannot be read into evidence.
Consequently, the award was upheld.
[Paras 1 and 3] B.
The award of Labour Court reinstated a workman in 1986, three years prior to his death.
The benefit of the award cannot be negated for the widow of the workman.
[Para 3] C The operation of the award was stayed by High Court, and the workman and his widow have not been paid back wages.
The back wages must be calculated and paid to the widow of the workman within a month.
