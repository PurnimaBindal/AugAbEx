Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Criminal proceedings - Departmental proceedings are not necessarily suspended due to pending criminal proceedings resulting from the same employee’s acts - The adequacy of evidence to serve a charge-sheet is primarily the disciplinary authority's responsibility - Court interference is only necessary in exceptional cases - Dual proceedings may continue unless serious harm would arise from complicating and similar scenarios - Employers have the right to investigate and terminate employees on valid grounds - Court interference is limited and subject to recognized principles - Rigid rules cannot be established - Some guidelines mentioned - Petition rejected.
[Paras 5 to 10]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and laches - Criminal proceedings - A petition to suspend departmental proceedings due to pending criminal proceedings on the same grounds as the employee is dismissed because it is filed long after the charge-sheet is served.
The departmental proceedings began after the charge-sheet was served, not when it was dispatched.
