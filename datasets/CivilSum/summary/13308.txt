The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Family Pension - The deceased individual worked continuously and uninterruptedly for 15 years, even though employed on a casual basis, and had acquired the status of a temporary servant - The widow of the deceased is entitled to family pension.
[Para 3]
The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate Appointment - Delay - Despite being a member of a scheduled caste, the petitioner, a widow of the deceased, was denied compassionate appointment due to failure to file the application within the stipulated time - Limitation is irrelevant in matters pertaining to welfare schemes such as compassionate appointments - It was concluded that either the petitioner or her son, as deemed appropriate, is entitled to compassionate appointment.
