Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 136 - Appointment - Variation in usual date of inviting applications - Unless it is shown to be capricious or whimsical or wide off the reasonable mark, the choice of date for inviting applications cannot be dubbed arbitrary even if no particular reason is forthcoming for the same.
The State Government had followed the past practice of fixing the last date one month to one and a half months after the date of actual publication of the advertisement, and had fixed the last date for receipt of applications as 31st January 1988.
The High Court wrongly believed that the past practice was to fix the last date of applications as 30th June of the relevant year.
The High Court did not assign any reasons for the particular date, and merely qualifying for appointment by a later date is not a reason for dubbing an earlier date as arbitrary or irrational.
Therefore, the High Court was clearly in error in striking down the Government's action of fixing the last date for receipt of applications as 31st January, 1988 as arbitrary.
