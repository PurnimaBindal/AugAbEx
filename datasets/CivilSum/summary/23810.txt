Food Corporation of India Act of 1945, Section 45 - FCI (Staff) Regulations - Amendments in 1976 (30th) and 1977 (43rd) relate to classification and promotion of graduates and non-graduates - Dispute arose regarding the constitutionality of the amendments, and it was found that no justification was provided for the introduction of the classification - The differentiation between graduates and non-graduates in promotion for AG-I and AG-II positions infringes the equality clause and is therefore unconstitutional - Appeal by the Corporation is dismissed.
