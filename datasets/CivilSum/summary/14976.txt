Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Craft Teacher - Petitioner given appointment as Craft Teacher and worked from 31101963 till 3181985 - Petitioner granted pay scale of class IV employee throughout service - Held, claim of petitioner is not unreasonable when similar employees were treated as Class III employees on basis of job experience - Respondents directed to issue certificate or otherwise treat petitioner as Class III employee and release retiral benefits.
