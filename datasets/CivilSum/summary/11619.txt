Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - By absorption - Petitioners are claiming to be absorbed in Andhra Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation - They have the required experience and qualification for absorption, arguing that the route and bus have been nationalized.
However, since the routes have been nationalized in 1987, they cannot be absorbed by the year 1997.
Thus, the petition is dismissed.
