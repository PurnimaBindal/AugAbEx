Education/Training/Life Insurance Course - The Corporation provided the training schools guidelines specifying that students who score grade B and above will be given priority for appointment, subject to interview and medical examination, while students who score C-1 and C-2 will be allowed to compete with general candidates in the entrance examination for appointment.
These guidelines are considered reasonable, and petitioners who failed to secure grade B2 and above or 50% marks will have to compete with general candidates in the pre-recruitment test.
