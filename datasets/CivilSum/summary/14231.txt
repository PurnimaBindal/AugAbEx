Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admissions - Education - Duration of diploma course - Though the petitioner was admitted to a 3-1/2 years Electronics and Communication Engineering Course, he was wrongly given only 3 years in the prospectus - The Director Education circulated information about the amended duration to all the relevant Institutions - The petitioner wants the authority to declare his course results despite having one more semester to clear -The competent authority can change the duration of a course at any time through amending Rules and there was no promise made by authorities at the time of admission that the duration or rules would not be amended - The petitioner's reliance on the error is not valid.
