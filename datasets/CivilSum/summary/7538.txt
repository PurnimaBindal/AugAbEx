The charge-sheet against the petitioner in Departmental Proceedings has been quashed because of an 11-year delay in initiating proceedings.
The charges relate to unauthorized absence and retaining of two G-8 books containing receipts without accounting for the money.
The department's explanation for the delay caused is not convincing since certified copies of the documents could have been obtained from the criminal court where the receipts were filed.
The petitioner was acquitted in the criminal trial and prejudice has been caused by the unexplained delay on the department's part.
The petitioner's charge-sheet has been quashed.
