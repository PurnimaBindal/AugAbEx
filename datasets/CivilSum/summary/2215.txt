Constitution of India, Article 226 - Claim of Pension - The petitioner, who was initially appointed in 1956, was ordered to be reinstated in 1985 with the benefit of continuity of service and seniority but without backwages.
He insisted on preconditions and joined duties in 1993.
A circular in November 1992 granted the benefit of pension to existing employees.
The court held that the previous period of service of the petitioner as well as the period from April 1993 to August 1996 must necessarily be taken into consideration for pension.
Additionally, since the petitioner approached the court in 2000, arrears of pension must be paid from April 2000 till the current date.
[Paras 17, 18, 20 to 22]
