Border Security Force Act, Section 117 - Border Security Force Rules, Rule 26 - Order of compulsory retirement - Petitioner claiming violation of principles of natural justice and lack of justification in service record for retirement - Respondents citing seven punishments and lack of improvement as reason for retirement - Court upholds examination of entire service record and defers to authority's decision unless arbitrary, contrary to record, or violative of rules - Petitioner's objections deemed after-thought and no special or equitable circumstances exist to reverse decision.
