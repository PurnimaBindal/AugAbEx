Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 311 - Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1958 Rule 27 - Termination of service - Petitioners who were appointed as Junior Public Health Nurses on a provisional basis due to a delay in appointing Public Service Commission recruited candidates have had their services terminated.
They claimed for regularisation, but their services were terminated to accommodate the Public Service Commission hands and candidates under bonded obligation.
It is deemed necessary to accommodate the Public Service Commission hands as it is an institutional mandate, and the right of Public Service Commission hands cannot be disputed.
There was no illegality in the government terminating the services of the petitioners.
However, if vacancies exist and there is a necessity, the petitioners could be accommodated entirely at the discretion of the appointing authority.
[Paras 10 and 11]
