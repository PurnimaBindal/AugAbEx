According to the Constitution of India, Article 300-A, Army Act 1950, Section 192, and Pension Regulations for the Army 1961, if a deceased member has served for more than 15 years but less than 20 years, they are entitled to proportionate pension and retiral benefits.
The widow of the deceased member cannot deny service pension on the grounds that he had not rendered 20 years of service.
