According to the Constitution of India, Article 14 and the Haryana State Government Policy dated 731996 and 1831996 regarding regularisation, benefits cannot be denied to employees due to a break in service caused by the employer.
The only case where benefits can be denied is when the break is attributed to the employee.
In this case, the break in service was due to a government ban on muster roll, and thus the order of denial of regularisation is not sustainable.
This decision was based on the precedent set by "Tek Chand & others v. State of Haryana & others, 2001(3) RSJ 605".
