The Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of Deceased Government Employees Rules, 2003, 2005 and 2006 have been amended and the question of which policy is applicable to pending cases has arisen.
The court held that the policy in force at the time of death of the deceased employee shall be applicable and ex-gratia claims should not be rejected based on technicalities.
[Paras 12, 15 and 17] B.
Rule 6 of the Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of Deceased Government Employees Rules, 2006 addresses the applicability of the rules to already pending cases and provides options for the applicants.
The applicants of the pending applications governed by the rules in existence prior to 2006 Rules are entitled to opt for the benefits under any of the rules.
