The petitioner's claim for leave encashment and Air tickets after voluntary retirement was dismissed due to delay and latches.
The court held that the claim of leave encashment for one month was without merit since the petitioner had already been paid at the time of retirement.
Additionally, those who opted for voluntary retirement were not entitled to the facility of Air Ticket.
As the petitioner did not provide any explanation for the delay, it was open to them to approach the Court if their representations were not being responded to.
