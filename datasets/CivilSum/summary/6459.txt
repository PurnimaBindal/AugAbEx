Under Section 4 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, the income of a deceased driver must be properly assessed before compensation can be given.
Employer denies claimant's plea that deceased driver earned Rs. 5000 per month, due to lack of evidence on record.
The compensation is correctly assessed by taking income of Rs. 65 per day, which is minimum wages.
[Para 8]
According to Section 4A(3) of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, the Commissioner is obligated to impose interest @ 12% on the employer and insurance company, as well as levy a penalty of up to 50% of the amount due on the employer.
