The appointment of Registration Clerks to work on a daily wage basis is made subject to the Governor's annual sanction, which limits appointments to no more than three months in any given financial year.
Claims that such appointments are made in accordance with the relevant rules are not valid.
[Para 14]
Regularisation of ad hoc appointments is governed by the Regularisation Rules, which prescribe a three-year period of continuous service.
Unless the petitioners meet this requirement, they cannot be regularised.
While the petitioners do not form a separate class, they may be given relaxation in the matter of age prescribed, and may be considered for appointment under the same conditions as other similarly placed persons.
[Paras 17, 19, 22-24]
If any petitioner worked as an unpaid Apprentice under paragraph 101, they will be paid emoluments on a daily wage basis for that period, and this period will be counted as part of their service for the purpose of computing the required three years of continuous service.
[Para 21]
Petitioners who are appointed as Registration Clerks on a daily wage basis are subject to the Governor's order, which limits appointments to no more than three months per financial year.
As such, these appointments are considered ad hoc.
