The Punjab Sub-Divisional Engineers Class-II Rules, 1967, as well as Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, dictate that there is a common seniority list of Junior Engineers in the PWD (P.H.)
Branch (Pb.)
and that joint seniority should be taken into account when promoting engineers.
Therefore, the petitioner, being senior in the joint seniority, should not have been overlooked when categorized by Electrical or Mechanical and Civil Action of the authority.
The court found this action in violation of Articles 14 and 16 and subsequently quashed the recommendations and promotion of juniors to the petitioner.
