According to the Constitution of India's Articles 226/227 regarding Termination of Service, ad hoc appointment of JBT Teachers was terminated in supposed compliance with Government directives.
However, the apex Court's decision regarding the termination of service for those exceeding advertised post limits back in 1992 was not applicable to the ad hoc JBT Teachers.
As a result, the petitioner and those in similar situations must be reinstated within 15 days, as directed.
