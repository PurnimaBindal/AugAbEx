Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25(fff) - Compensation - Closure of Project - The project was completely wound up in 1990, and workers would have been retrenched but for an interim court order.
Since then, the project authorities have been paying these workers salaries without assigning them any work.
Therefore, any instruction to compensate these workers according to the Scheme would not be in the interest of justice.
Nevertheless, the workers are entitled to their rightful dues due to the project's closure, as outlined in Section 25(fff) of the Act.
