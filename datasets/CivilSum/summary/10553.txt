Punjab Co-operative Financing Institution Service Rules, 1958, Rule 10 - Punjab State Cooperative Financing Institutions Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1970 - Applicability and scope of the Rules - Prior to the creation of the common cadre in 1970, each Co-operative Bank operated as a separate entity with its own Board of Directors as the appointing and punishing authority of its employees.
However, after the creation of the common cadre, the Board of Directors of the apex Bank became the appointing and punishing authority.
As such, any termination order passed by a co-operative bank under the 1958 Rules would be without jurisdiction.
