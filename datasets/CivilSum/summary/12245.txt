Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Eligibility - The petitioner qualified for promotion after five years of service on September 24th, 2000 - Notification was released on December 12th, 2000 establishing January 1st, 2000 as the cut-off date for promotion eligibility - The petitioner argued that this could be detrimental to officials who qualified for promotion in different years - The tribunal found the cut-off date reasonable since the purpose was to fill positions that arose in 1998/1999/2000 - It was determined that fixing the cut-off date is an executive function and cannot be considered arbitrary unless it is excessively unreasonable - The petition was thus dismissed.
