Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Appointment on probation - Termination - The petitioner was appointed on probation with a probation period of six months which could be extended for three months - The petitioner was discharged from service after five months and given payment in lieu of notice, which was less than their last drawn salary - The court held that the management cannot terminate an employee's services during the probation period before the six-month term is completed, except on grounds of misconduct - The petitioner was wrongfully terminated and is reinstated with the Respondent Bank directed to pay Rs. 50,000/- for back wages.
