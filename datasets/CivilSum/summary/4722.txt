Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Jammu & Kashmir Educational (Gazetted) Service Recruitment Rules, 1977, Article 87-B - Regularisation - Relaxation of Rules - A petitioner who was transferred from a senior master position to a lecturer position in a government college sought regularisation by relaxation of rules - However, the petitioner was not the senior-most master and her case was not approved by the Departmental Promotional Committee - The petitioner cannot bypass the seniors and seek regularisation through relaxation of rules - As a result, the petitioner is not entitled to regularisation - The petition has been dismissed.
[Paras 6, 7 and 11]
