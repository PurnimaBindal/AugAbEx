Mr. Ritu Bahri AAG, Har.
cites Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 in regards to suspension, natural justice, discharge, reinstatement, retirement, and age of superannuation.
The petitioner was suspended during a criminal case but was later discharged by the Chief Metropolitan Magistrate without any charges being framed.
This discharge order still stands and has not been overturned by any higher court, yet the petitioner has not been reinstated.
The petitioner is retiring from government service upon reaching the age of superannuation and a direction has been issued to treat the petitioner as being in service after the discharge order by the CMM.
Consequential benefits and pension must be provided to the petitioner.
