Contempt of Courts Act, Section 12 - A writ dismissed by the High Court against an order of transfer does not become a contempt of court when the Authority stays the order thereafter.

Constitution of India, Article 16 - The court can interfere with an order of transfer of a government employee if it is mala fide or violates any norms or principles, and if the employee's career prospects are unaffected and not harmed, according to (1994) 6 SCC 98.
