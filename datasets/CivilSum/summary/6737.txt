As per the Army Act 1950 Section 191, Army Rules 1954, Section 13 Item 3(iii) - Army Pension Regulations 1961, Regulation 173, if a person's disability is caused during military service then a disability pension should be granted.
The petitioner was discharged from service due to a ground of disablement caused during participation in a competition organized by HO.
The court held that disability arising from such injury will be regarded to have occurred while on duty, thus the petitioner is entitled to disability pension under Regulation 173.
