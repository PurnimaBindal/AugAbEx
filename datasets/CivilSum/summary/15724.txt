The Employment Exchange (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959, Sections 4 and 2(f) requires the notification of vacancies in public sector establishments to the Employment Exchange, but not in the private sector.
Primary Co-operative Societies are not covered under Section 2(f) or Section 4 of the Act as they are not an establishment in public sector.
Therefore, the Managing Committee of the Society can appoint the petitioner as Clerk-cum-Cashier without the necessity of rescinding their resolution due to lack of notification of vacancy to the Employment Exchange.
