According to Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1961, Rule 3(1)(A), constitutional Articles 16 and 19(i)(f), an employee's pay dues are considered their property.
This is a fundamental right in the State of J&K, and as such, denying such dues is bad in law.
A petitioner working as a teacher since Oct '89 without a break, who has acquired the status of a quasi-permanent employee, made a claim for protection of services and pay dues.
The petitioner has been performing her duties without any selection made by the Selection Board, and her salary has not been paid.
