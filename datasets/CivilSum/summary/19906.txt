Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion - Assessment of suitability for promotion - The DPI has the authority to promote teachers to the rank of lecturer based on suitability.
A petitioner was promoted, but the DEO, who lacks suitability assessment authority, refused to allow him to join.
The competent authority cannot assign juniors to judge the suitability of those appointed by him.
However, since the promotion order was conditional, it creates no right.
DPI has been directed, within 3 months of the date the petitioner was promoted, to reconsider his suitability.
[Paras 6 and 8]
