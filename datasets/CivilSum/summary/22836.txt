The Representation of the People Act, 1951, Section 83(1)(a) and Civil Procedure Code, Order 6 Rule 2(1) state that an Election Petition must disclose material facts constituting the cause of action.
In the case of a government servant resigning by waiver of notice period, the candidate must mention the particulars of resignation, including the dates of submission and waiver of notice - failure to do so makes the petition liable to be rejected.

There is a difference between "voluntary retirement" and "resignation" in service jurisprudence.
While both are voluntary acts of an employee, they bring distinct results - resignation may disentitle an employee from retiral benefits of service which may not be the case with voluntary retirement.
