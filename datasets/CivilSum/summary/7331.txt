In 1994-95, due to the alteration of Municipal Corporation boundaries by the inclusion of 27 villages, primary school teachers were absorbed by the Municipal Corporation and became part of an integrated cadre of primary teachers.
Subsequently, the State Government excluded the 27 villages, and the petitioners argue that the absorbed teachers should have been repatriated.
However, the plea for repatriation cannot be accepted as the teachers are now part of the Municipal Corporation's service.
