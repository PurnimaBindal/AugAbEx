According to Sections 17-B and 33(2)(b) of the Industrial Disputes Act, an employer's application for approval can be rejected, but if they choose to proceed against the resulting award directing reinstatement, they will be expected to pay the terminated employee's last wages during the proceedings.
It has been established that the rejection of an approval application under Section 33(2)(b) of the 1947 Act would be considered an award.
Therefore, if a workman makes an application under Section 17-B, it is maintainable, and if it is found that they have been out of employment since their termination, they will be entitled to both arrears of wages and monthly wages.
The petitioner made no denial of the workman's claim that they have been unemployed since their termination.
