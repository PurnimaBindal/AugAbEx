The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) (c) - Departmental enquiry - The absence of cross-examination of an ex-employee whose statement is used as evidence does not invalidate a departmental enquiry.
[Para 2]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-A - A plea that a writ petition filed after 30 days from the publication of an impugned award is not maintainable is not valid.
S.17-A does not limit the powers of the Court under Article 226 of the Constitution to entertain petitions after 30 days of the award's publication.
