According to Sections 11 and 18 of the Border Security Force Act, 1965, an employee can be removed on the grounds of absence from duty without leave.
However, just because the petitioner did not obtain a leave certificate does not mean they can be held as a "deserter."
In fact, the petitioner had applied for leave and it was sanctioned by competent authorities.
Therefore, the removal of the employee from service is illegal.
[Paras 12-14]
