West Bengal Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1971, Rule 10(5)(ii) - Removal - Departmental proceedings - Provisions for assistance of lawyers during disciplinary proceedings depend on the specific case - Government servants may provide assistance on behalf of delinquents, but lawyers are prohibited unless the presenting officer appointed by the disciplinary authority is a legal practitioner - The presenting officer appointed in this case was not a legally trained person - The petitioner was provided ample opportunity to present their case and was heard exhaustively - No breach of natural justice occurred due to not providing the petitioner with a lawyer.
[Paras 7, 8 and 9]

Departmental proceedings - Natural justice - The Enquiry Officer thoroughly dealt with the charges and found the petitioner guilty - No breach of natural justice occurred - Interference by the learned Single Judge regarding the quantum of punishment was not warranted.
