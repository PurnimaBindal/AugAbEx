Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 7A - Notice for non-payment of employees' Provident Fund contribution - The Act became effective in Khasi Hills District after the Council was established on June 27, 1952 - The law made by Parliament applies to any part of India, and is not considered invalid for having extra-territorial operation - Therefore, the Act came into effect for Khasi Hills district upon the Governor's loss of power under paragraph 19, and would continue until the President excludes its application or publishes modifications and exceptions.
