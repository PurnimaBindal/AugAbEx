Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 311 - Suspension - Departmental proceedings - The Courts have the power of judical review.
This means that discretion should be exercised judciously, based on good reasons and with judicial scrutiny.
The suspension must be passed after considering relevant material and explanation of the employee concerned, if necessary.
An employee cannot be suspended when there is no charge against them before the court nor any departmental proceedings.
A pending criminal case against another person is not sufficient to suspend an employee unless the allegation against the employee has been specifically stated.
While a suspension isn't considered a punishment or stigma, it does have an effect on an employee's career.
Hence, if the order is not fair and passed arbitrarily, the court can interfere to ensure justice.
[Paras 7, 7, 10 to 13, 26 and 27]
