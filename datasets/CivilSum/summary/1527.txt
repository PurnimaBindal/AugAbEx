Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 226, 227 and 20 - Departmental proceedings - Petitioner facing criminal trial on the same charges - Given a charge sheet and suspended - Enquiry Officer appointed - Whether departmental proceedings should be halted until the criminal court concludes - Decided the criminal charge concerns a particular incident while departmental proceedings are general - The facts and evidence in each case are distinct, and it won't cause prejudice to the petitioner.
