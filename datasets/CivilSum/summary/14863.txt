Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Higher Secondary Part II - Eligibility to appear in Part II exam - A petitioner was denied the opportunity to take the class XII exam after failing two subjects in class XI - Respondent No. 4 claimed an Achievement Card was mistakenly issued to the petitioner and the mistake was later identified by Respondent No. 5 - The court held that the petitioner did not meet the statutory criteria for passing class XI and therefore could not take the class XII exam - Petition was dismissed.
