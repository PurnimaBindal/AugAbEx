Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(b) and 18 - Industrial Dispute (Central) Rules, 1957, Rule 58(4), Form H - Settlement - The Rule 58(4) is mandatory to be followed strictly before a settlement can be considered valid - Non-compliance affects the settlement's validity - However, parties are estopped from challenging the existence of a settlement once both have acknowledged and acted upon it.
[Paras 4 and 6]
Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10(1)(D) and 12(5) - Reference - Adjudication of dispute - Jurisdiction of Govt. - The Govt. can only refer, not enter into adjudication of the merits of the case, and cannot make a reference on a limited point - The Tribunal only bound to answer the reference alone, and cannot go beyond the terms of the reference to adjudicate legality and justification of lock-out or strike - Case of the management is prejudiced by making a reference in limited terms, and the Govt. acceded its jurisdiction to adjudicate the issue.
[Paras 9, 10, 11 and 16]
Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Reference - Validity of reference - Validity of reference unchallenged does not estop parties from challenging it after the award if the Labour Court also failed to frame proper issues to adjudicate the dispute - Estoppel is not applicable to the law.
[Paras 18 and 19]
Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Burden of proof - Lock-out - Wages for the period of lock-out - No specific issue regarding the lockout - Workmen needed to prove their entitlement to wages in the absence of any specific issue on reference.
