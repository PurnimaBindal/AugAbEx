The petitioner, a Selection Grade Constable, was dismissed from service under the Constitution of India, Art. 311(2)(b), Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Section 126(2)(b), Terrorists and Disruptive Activities Act, 1987, Sections 3 and 4, and the Jammu and Kashmir Public Safety Act, 1978.
The disciplinary authority was satisfied of the petitioner's involvement in militant and subversive activities, and due to the ongoing militancy in the state and witnesses being unavailable, the departmental enquiry was dispensed with.
The Order of dismissal was found to be proper and not based on irrelevant grounds.
