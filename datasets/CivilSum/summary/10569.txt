According to Rule 1928 of the Punjab Police Rules, 1934, if a candidate is unable to meet the desired standard on the first attempt, they are given two more opportunities to qualify.
However, it is necessary to inform the candidate of their test results, and the failure to do so results in the candidate being deprived of their chances.
In this case, the plaintiff's appeal was dismissed due to the authority's failure to convey the results on time, resulting in a lapse and the plaintiff being deprived of their two additional chances as per Rule 1928.
