The appointment post for Art and Craft Teacher requires a qualification of a 2-year diploma in Art and Craft exam from Haryana ITI.
Petitioners who possess a 3-year diploma from Government Polytechnic for Women, Ambala were rejected, but it was held that their qualification should be recognized by the State of Haryana as per government instructions dated 21199, and cannot be withdrawn subsequently to advertisement.
Therefore, the petitioners are eligible.
[Paras 5, 6] B.
Out of 287 posts for the appointment of Art and Craft Teacher, 256 general candidates were appointed, and there were 27 unfilled vacancies belonging to the BCA category.
The eligibility of the petitioners was upheld by the High Court, and they were directed to be appointed against the 31 unfilled vacancies that belonged to the General Category, and one of the petitioners was directed to be appointed against the vacant BCA post.
