The Haryana Government Ex-gratia Rules, 2003, Rule 4(1) states that there is a limitation for claiming compassionate financial assistance in place of compassionate appointment.
However, an application made for compassionate appointment within the prescribed period can be converted into an application for financial assistance.
This cannot be rejected as time barred particularly when the respective authorities recommended for it.
Thus, the authorities are directed to release the prescribed grant as per Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21.
