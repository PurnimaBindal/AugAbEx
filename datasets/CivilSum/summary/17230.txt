As per Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume II, Rule 6.17(2)(i), the widow of an employee who died while in service is entitled to a family pension if the employee had served for a period of at least one year.
However, in the case of the petitioner, the Pension Scheme became applicable to the department where her late husband was working in 1982, denying her the pension for over 17 years without justification.
The petitioner is entitled to the pension and interest at the rate of 10% per annum.
