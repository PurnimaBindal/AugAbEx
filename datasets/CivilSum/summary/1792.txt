Ms. Beenashaw Soni, Advocate, has been identified as the Respondent.
According to Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a Daily Wager was terminated illegally resulting in the award of reinstatement with Back Wages.
The contention that the juniors were regularized and given the same post was made by the Petitioners.
However, it was held that Petitioners are not eligible to claim regularization or appointment to the post as claimed.
This is because the mere setting aside of the termination order being non-compliant with Section 25 does not lead to Petitioners' entitlement to these claims.
The claim involves disputed questions of facts concerning nature of work, eligibility, entitlement for promotion.
Furthermore, it was outlined that Labour court has not gone into the question of regularization or continuity.
