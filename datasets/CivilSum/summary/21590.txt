As stated in Punjab Civil Service Rules (according to Rule 8122 of Vol. 1), Article 16, when an employee is absent from their designated duties without proper authorization, increments may be withheld as punishment.
If this unauthorized absence exceeds five years and no satisfactory explanation has been provided, a punishment of withholding five increments with cumulative effect will be imposed.
Even if a subsequent order granting extraordinary leave is passed during the period of absence, this does not condone the unauthorized absence or negate the punishment already imposed.
Rather, the order is merely a consequence of the imposed punishment.
As such, the order of withholding five increments is upheld.
