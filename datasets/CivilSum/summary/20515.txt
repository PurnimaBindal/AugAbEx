In the case of carpet factories in UP employing children under 14 years and subjecting them to physical torture, the Constitution of India's Articles 14, 21, 23, 24, 27(1), 28, 31(1), 36, 39(e)(f) 45 and 46, 51A, and Preamble - along with the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, Article 26(1) - mandates the total prohibition of child labor.
An order was given to the Government of India to convene a meeting to eliminate child employment progressively and to provide reports of progress to the Court, according to JT 1996(11) SC 685.
