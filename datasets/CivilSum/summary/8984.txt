Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 – Labour Court's award challenged.
A workman's case was rejected for not completing the required 240 days of service before termination.
However, evidence suggests that the workman had actually completed 265 days.
This finding is an error on the court's part and the termination was held illegal.
The workman is entitled to full back wages and reinstatement in service as per S25-F of Industrial Disputes Act.
