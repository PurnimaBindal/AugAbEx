Civil Procedure Code Order 41 Rule 33 - Pleadings - Jurisdiction - Appeal - If a selection is challenged through a Civil Suit, any subsequent orders of promotions passed during the pendency of the appeal cannot be annulled in the appeal, unless they are also the subject-matter of the suit.
Any relief granted in the appeal can only be that which could have been granted in the suit by the trial Court.
