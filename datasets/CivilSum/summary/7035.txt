Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 47 Rule 1 - Review - Applicant cannot seek review of an order on the basis of a subsequent Supreme Court judgment as it does not constitute a mistake or error apparent on the face of the record and the applicant cannot claim to rehear the matter.
The Court held that the petitioner cannot seek regularization in view of the Constitution Bench judgment in Uma Devi's case.
