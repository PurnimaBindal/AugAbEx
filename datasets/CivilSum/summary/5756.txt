According to the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 162, executive instructions cannot be applied retrospectively unless they are issued for the benefit of employees.
Failure to adhere to a policy decision intended to benefit employees may violate Article 14, and a writ can be sought.
[Paras 7 and 20]

As per the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, when a dependent of a deceased employee is entitled to compassionate appointment on the death of the employee, they have a legal right to the appointment according to the existing policy at that time.
Even if the policy changes later to provide a financial benefit in lieu of employment, the dependant retains this right.
[Para 22]
