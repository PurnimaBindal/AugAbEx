Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1998 - Punjab State instruction dt. 1.12.1988 - Proficiency step-up - Qualifying service - Ad hoc service - Service in accordance with rules entails service provided by an employee following the appointment in accordance with the rules governing recruitment to the post - Computation of ad hoc service period cannot grant the benefits of higher scales of pay after eight and eighteen years of service under the proficiency step-up scheme.
