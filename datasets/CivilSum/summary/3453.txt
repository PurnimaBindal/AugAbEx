Workmen at Delhi receiving DA as per Vaidya Lingam Award entitled to claim same DA when relocated to Himachal Pradesh - Ruling based on Apex Court judgment in MC Mehta's case - Terms and conditions of employment to be protected - Wages and holidays to be carried over from Delhi to Baddi.
[Paras 19 to 21]
Unregistered association of workmen can take up cause on behalf of workmen - Ruling based on law laid down by Apex Court in case of Newspapers Ltd Allahabad.
[Para 23]
Individual workmen have locus standi to challenge validity of award initially espoused by Union - Petitioners have necessary locus standi to challenge award - SLP was withdrawn and no decision on merits - Petitions are maintainable.
