According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, the filling of the post of Deputy Operations Manager by promotion or selection is determined by Promotion and Recruitment Rules.
As long as these Rules are not in violation of Regulations or Acts and are not arbitrary, Courts have little authority to interfere.
In this case, the Rules regarding promotion or selection to the post of Dy.
Operations Manager are considered valid and free of any flaws.
Consequently, the promotions are upheld, and the High Court's order is overruled.
