Kerala Co-operative Societies Act, 1969, Section 80-A - Kerala Co-operative Societies Employees Self-financing Pension Scheme, 1994 - Fixation of cut off date for benefit of Pension Scheme - No suspicion of mala fide or bias - Government has the authority to determine cut off date for implementing pension scheme - Government lacked the power to introduce pension scheme before amended Section 80-A - Amended Section 80-A came into force on 3693 - Therefore, Government could not implement pension scheme before 3693 - Setting cut off date as 3693 is not unreasonable.
