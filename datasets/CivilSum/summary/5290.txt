The ownership of shares by the Central Government does not turn it into an appropriate government to govern the financial activities of a company registered under the Companies Act, especially when the company is located in a particular state like Madhya Pradesh.
In such a case, the government of that state would be considered as the appropriate government.

While considering the validity of retrenchment, the Labour Court has the jurisdiction to investigate into the basic central issue that hovers around retrenchment, which is the factum of closure resulting in retrenchment.
In this regard, the Labour Court should enter into the question of closure/shifting of the industry only if the question referred to it is about the validity of retrenchment.
