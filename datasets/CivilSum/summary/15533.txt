Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16(4) and 226 - Letters Patent clause X - Education Admission - Post graduate course in medical College - Reservation - Single Bench has ordered the petitioner to be admitted against one of the two available seats under the handicapped category.
The reservation for physically handicapped persons is set at 3%, sub-divided into three categories, subject to seat availability based on government instructions.
The State Government maintains a hundred point roster with a base year, and the reservation is applied to the overall roster rather than each year.
According to the roster, only one of the two seats was available for physically handicapped persons, which has been rightfully filled.
The appeal is allowed, and the judgment of the single Judge is set aside.
