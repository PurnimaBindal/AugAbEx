Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Quantum of punishment - The Labour Court does have the jurisdiction to replace punishment with another - In the case where there is no allegation of embezzlement, the Labour Court did not err in replacing termination with stoppage of four annual increments with cumulative effect.
[Paras 4 and 5]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Back wages - It cannot be assumed that a surviving workman was gainfully employed - There is no evidence to suggest that the workman was gainfully employed - The Labour Court awarding full back wages is to be upheld.
[Paras 6 and 7]
