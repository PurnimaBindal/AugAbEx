Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Agricultural University Statutes, Statute No. 4(3) - The Vice Chancellor holds the power to constitute the Selection Committee for selecting candidates for appointment - In this case, the Selection Committee recommended candidates for appointment and the Vice Chancellor accepted the recommendations - However, an ad hoc committee was illegally constituted by the Board of Management to interview the same selected candidates again - The final authority in the matter was the Vice Chancellor and not the Board of Administration - The action of the Board was without jurisdiction and illegal - Therefore, the candidates selected by a legally constituted Selection Committee must be appointed and the Government has no jurisdiction to refuse their appointment.
