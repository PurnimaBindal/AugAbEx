Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4A - Workmen's Compensation (Amendment) Act, 1995, Section 4(1)(b) - Accident - Compensation - An accident occurred on September 14, 1995, at 1:45 pm The bus driver was also injured seriously.
After calculating, the claimants were awarded compensation of Rs. 1,23,650/- as per the new Act since the Workmen's Compensation (Amendment) Act came into force on September 15, 1995.
The owner and insurance company appealed to reduce the compensation; however, it was held that the provisions of the old Act applied instead of the new Act due to its non-retrospective effect.
The commission was supposed to determine the compensation by multiplying it by 50% rather than 60%.
Consequently, the claimant was found entitled to receive Rs. 1,03,312/- instead of Rs. 1,23,650/-, with interest being awarded at 6% pa instead of 12% pa [Paras 5 and 6]
