The State's Notification on Revision of Dearness Allowance under Minimum Wages Act, 1948, Section 5(1)(a) was challenged by the Association as not complying with High Court orders.
However, the minor difference in the rate of minimum wages as stipulated by Central and State Government was due to a mathematical calculation error.
Unless there is a serious defect, the Notification cannot be interfered with, and thus the writ application has no merit.
