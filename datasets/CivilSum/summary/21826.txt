Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16(1), 16(4A) and 335 - Promotion - Roster points promotions - Reservation in promotions - Clarification of propositions laid down in Virpal and Ajit Singh's, RK Sabharwal and Jagdish Lal's cases :- The primary purpose of Articles 16(4) and 16(4A) is due representation of certain classes in certain posts but the same are to be construed with full care bearing in mind and not ignoring other important provisions of Articles 14, 16(1) and 335.
Right to be considered for promotion is a fundamental right - Non-consideration of an eligible and within the consideration zone candidate will violate his personal and fundamental right and right to equal opportunity.
Reserved candidate do not go up through the normal process of selection as applicable to general candidates.
Reservation in promotions requires balancing of fundamental rights under Article 16(1) and right to reservation under Articles 16(4) and 16(4A).
The court has to ensure that in matters relating to affirmative action by the State, the rights under Articles 14 and 16 to equality of opportunity are not affected - A reasonable balance has to be struck so that the affirmative action does not lead to reverse discrimination or `overkill.'
Roster point promotion will not confer a right of seniority - Seniority will be granted from the date of promotion in terms of the promotion rule of selection - Seniority on the basis of `continuous officiation' is available only in the cadre-class of service in which roster points are available not in class I and II services where there is no reservation prescribed.
