Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Rajasthan Agricultural Service Rules, 1960, Rule 21 - Eligibility - Advertisement - The date and terms included in the advertisement should normally be considered conclusive unless they are contrary to the service rules - It would be fair if all candidates knew the qualifications required by a specified cut-off date - Applicants who do not meet the educational qualifications on the specified date cannot be considered; candidates obtaining qualifications after the cut-off date cannot be considered, as it would be misleading - Therefore, the eligibility date should be specific, and it cannot be changed - Since the petitioners did not hold the degree on the specified date, they were not eligible to apply - The fact that they obtained the degree at a later stage was not taken into account.
