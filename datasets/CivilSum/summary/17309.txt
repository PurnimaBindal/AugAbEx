Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Termination of services - Reinstatement - The petitioner, who joined service as a Helper on daily wages in Haryana Roadways at Fatehabad in 1991 and was appointed on an ad hoc basis after three years, did not press for reinstatement in proceedings pending before Industrial Tribunal that were based on this appointment.
After serving for about a year and four months, the petitioner's services were terminated on the ground that he was overage at the time of his initial appointment when he had completed nearly 7 years of service.
However, the respondents did not allege or prove that the petitioner secured employment by misrepresentation or by concealing any fact.
It is unfair to hold the petitioner responsible for the mistake committed by respondents No. 3 and 4 in engaging him as a daily wager and then appointing him on a regular pay scale.
Therefore, the court has ordered directions for the petitioner's reinstatement.
