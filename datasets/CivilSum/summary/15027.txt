Constitution of India, Article 226 - Examination - Applicant submitted application for Registration for examination within time - School Authority failed to forward the application to council - The school admitted its fault in a representation made thereafter - Council authority found collusion, but without any convincing evidence - The authority is not competent to give a finding in absence of clinching evidence - Mistakes are natural - The petitioner cannot be punished for the mistakes of the school - The order is set aside - The school is directed to pay a cost of Rs 20000/-.
[Paras 3 to 5]
