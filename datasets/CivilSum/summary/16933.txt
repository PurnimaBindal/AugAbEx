According to the Constitution of India, Article 16 and the Andhra Pradesh Ministerial Service Rules, Rules 27 (i), (ii) and (iii), seniority must be fixed for the petitioner transferred to the Unit of District Panchayat Officer from the Gram Panchayat as a Junior Assistant.
The petitioner requested seniority from the date of initial appointment, however, there is no dispute that the cadres of Junior Assistant in the two units have not been merged and separate seniority lists are maintained with a separate quota for promotion.
Rule 27 (iii) states that the petitioner should be placed at the bottom of the seniority list at the transferred place.
The court held that provincialisation or salary payment from the consolidated fund cannot determine merger of the two cadres and thus the petitioner's seniority will be fixed from the date of subsequent and not initial appointment, and the petition is dismissed accordingly.
