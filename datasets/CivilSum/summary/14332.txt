Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Public appointments - Ad hoc appointment - Regularisation of ad hoc appointees - Appointing individuals through ad hoc appointments for a period of 89 days without advertising the posts to the general public and then regularizing them in government service is a violation of the right of consideration of eligible and meritorious candidates - This technique is a way of appointing individuals who were unable to compete with eligible candidates - No appointment to a public office can be made in a secretive manner - This action is deemed to be violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
