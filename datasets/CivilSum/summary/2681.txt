Constitution of India, Article 226 - Successive writs - Concealment of facts - A petitioner cannot file multiple writ petitions on the same cause of action - The petitioner had previously filed a writ petition on the same facts and allegations in her husband's name, which was dismissed - Her subsequent writ filed in her own name on the same facts is not maintainable because she deliberately concealed the fact of the earlier writ - She is guilty of misleading the court by concealing a material fact and costing the court time - Both of her writ petitions were dismissed and she must pay Rs. 25,000/- in costs for each.
[Paras 9 and 10]
