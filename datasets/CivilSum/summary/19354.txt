According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, regular service should be based on length of service and regularisation.
The petitioner was appointed as a Junior Engineer on an ad hoc basis in 1982, but was only regularised in 1990.
It was determined that his services should have been regularised from the date he became eligible/entitled to regularisation.
To be eligible for admission to a three-year Bachelor of Engineering Degree Course in Civil Engineering at the Regional Engineering College in Kurukshetra, the petitioner must have his entire period of service considered for the purpose of length of service as a JE [Para 5]
