Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 24 - Seniority - Ad hoc appointment cannot be counted for seniority if appointment to the post must be made only based on Public Service Commission's competitive examination - The respondent, who was appointed in relaxation of rules, cannot be made senior to those appointed as per rules.
[Paras 1 and 9]
