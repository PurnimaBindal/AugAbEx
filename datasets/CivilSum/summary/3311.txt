Constitution of India, Articles 12, 226 and 227 - Karnataka Private Educational Institutions (Discipline and Control) Act, 1975, Section 8 - Remedy - Appeal - Order - Termination - Jurisdiction - An employee was deprived of the opportunity to make an appeal when their service was terminated without a passing or communicated termination order.
However, an appeal was made to the appellate Tribunal, which was dismissed on the grounds that there was no order against which an appeal could be entertained.
After filing a Writ Petition for a direction to reinstate, the Writ was dismissed as not maintainable against a society which was not a State.
Later, an appeal was made against the order in the writ petition, which included an additional prayer for a direction to the society to pass or communicate the termination order.
The appeal was allowed, and it was observed that there was no bar to entertaining the appeal by the Tribunal without an order.
Consequently, the Tribunal was directed to decide the appeal on merits without insisting on the order to be appealed against.
[Paras 5, 6, 20 and 21]
