Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Appointments - Selection - Termination - Natural Justice - The appointment of 6000 candidates without any authority to appoint in excess of sanctioned posts and selection without adherence to recognized procedures is illegal - Any appointment made in such a manner will not be binding on the State Government - Termination of such individuals can be done after giving show cause notice - The appointments cannot be regularized when no posts are available - A special committee appointed by the competent authority must give input before any termination occurs - The fact that candidates completed training successfully is not sufficient ground for regularizing their appointments - No detailed inquiry can be made when the competent authority has already deemed the appointments invalid and illegal - The authority is instructed to repeat the selection process.
[Para 13-19]
