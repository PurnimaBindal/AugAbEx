Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 - Family pension - The petitioner, a widow, seeks family pension - The respondent argues that the deceased divorced the petitioner and she is therefore not entitled to the pension - However, the petitioner's entitlement to the pension is upheld due to the absence of proof that she was divorced by her late husband and the magistrate's order for the deceased to provide maintenance to his wife - It is also noted that even if the petitioner was divorced, the deceased was obligated to provide for her during the iddat period - Petition allowed.
