Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Natural justice/Second inquiry - The employee cannot be subjected to a second inquiry on the same charges if he was found innocent in the first inquiry and was not aggrieved of any violation of natural justice.
[Para 2]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Second enquiry/Enquiry after retirement - The petitioner was found innocent during the first inquiry held against him while on duty.
However, a second inquiry on the same charges was conducted after his retirement.
The whole enquiry proceedings are arbitrary and must be quashed.
[Para 3]
