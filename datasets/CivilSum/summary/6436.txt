The petitioner, who has served as a Junior Engineer in Haryana Public Health Department for 12 years on an ad hoc basis, is fully qualified for the post and was properly selected by the Selection Board.
The Selection Board recommended the petitioner for regular appointment at the department's behest.
Although some other ad hoc employees have obtained a stay against termination of their services, regularisation cannot be refused on this basis.
The respondents are instructed to regularise the petitioner's services.
