Andhra Bank (Employees) Pension Regulations, 1995, Regulations 2(k), 2(y), 22, 29 and 30(1)(b) - Andhra Bank Officer Service Regulations, Regulation 19 - Voluntary retirement - Pension - Resignation - Retirement due to voluntary retirement is considered as retirement for all legal purposes, and must not be viewed as a resignation, which can't be used as a means for the bank to deprive pension and other entitled benefits.
