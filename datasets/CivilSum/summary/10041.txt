Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Absence - Salary - The employee's long absence prevented him from performing his normal duties in the school under duress - The authorities treated the period of absence as period spent on leave - The School Management's contention that incremental benefits can be withdrawn without initiating disciplinary proceedings was not convincing to the court - The Managing Committee sought to punish the employee without following legal procedures - The court did not find any prejudice to the appellants if the employee was granted financial benefits - The judgment of the Single Judge was affirmed.
