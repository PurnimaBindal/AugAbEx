The retirees of the contributory fund, provident fund, and pension scheme introduced from a particular date are considered different classes due to various factors, according to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
The Supreme Court followed this in Nakara's case.
[Paras 6 & 16] B.
A cut-off date for the pension scheme introduced from a particular date is not a violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
The petitioner cannot claim a pension under the subsequent scheme as he had already withdrawn his PF amount before it came into force.
