Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Termination of service on ground of absence - Termination without an enquiry would be against natural justice - Reinstatement with back wages is the right of the employee - However, the employee suffered disablement during the litigation - This disablement was not connected to work duties - The matter has been remanded to the Labour Court to decide on compensation and whether the employee is entitled to back wages until the judicial order passed by the Labour Court.
The Labour Court will also determine whether the employee is disabled to an extent where they cannot perform any useful work.
