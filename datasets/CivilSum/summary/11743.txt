Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Writ of mandamus - Qualifications - Eligibility - Amendment in Rules - Draft rules - To obtain a writ of mandamus, the petitioners must be able to make an enforceable claim and the respondents must be under a legal obligation - The qualifications for the appointment of Lecturers were a three-year diploma, and those with such qualifications would not receive additional pay as Lecturers until they completed two short courses organized by the TTTI - The petitioners were not selected for the course on the basis of unjustifiable reasons as the Rules were still being amended and draft rules were only submitted for approval of PPSC - The petitioners' claim was upheld.
