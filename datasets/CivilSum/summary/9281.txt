Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Issues/Evidence - Strike/Illegal Strike - If the court did not question the legality of the strike, then no evidence would be considered - The finding of an illegal strike is invalid - Workmen cannot be denied back wages for this reason.
[Paras 7 and 9] B.
Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Termination/Reinstatement - Back wages - Illegal termination warrants full back wages unless the employee is responsible for something - If the strike is not declared illegal, reinstatement is not required - If there is no issue concerning the strike's illegality, then back wages should be granted - Employer making alternative arrangements is not a sufficient reason to refuse back wages.
