Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, 1948 Section 92(2) and Section 511 - Constitution of India, Articles 16(1)(4), 46, 309 and 335 - Reservation for members of Scheduled Castes - Section 92(2) of the Delhi Municipal Corporation Act is the same as Article 335 of the Constitution of India - The Corporation must consider the claims of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes when making appointments to its services - A resolution passed to give such consideration is not in conflict with the Act's provisions.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 Promotion - Conditions of service - Promotion is a condition of service while mere chances of promotion aren't - Rules only affecting promotions' chances aren't considered varying conditions of service - Employer still considers petitioner for promotion even if there are chances of promotion due to instructions - Instructions deemed valid and don't violate any right.
