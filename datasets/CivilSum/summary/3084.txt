Constitution of India, Article 311 - Management for Recognised Non-government Institutions (Aided and Unaided) Rules, 1969, Rule 28(9)(viia) - Enquiry Report - Copy, non-supply of - Termination - Natural justice - Termination of services of the employee before the operative date of the Supreme Court decision in the Mohd.
Ramzan Khan case - Termination order cannot be voided just by invoking the above case - The employee must prove that non-supply of the enquiry report caused prejudice to them - The termination order without such findings does not violate the principles of natural justice - The case is remanded back for fresh decision in accordance with Regulations.
