The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, allows for promotions and classification based on merit and experience.
As such, it is not considered discriminatory or unreasonable to classify individuals for the purpose of promotion to Super-Time Scale.
In fact, the promotion from the post of District Judge to the Super-Time Scale District Judge is based on merit and prescribed experience.
Thus, the classification has been deemed valid.
