Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - RPF Rules, 1987, Rules 90, 93 and 1534 - Transfer - If a complaint regarding corruption and misconduct is received then the employee should be given a chance to be heard before any transfer is made.
Since this did not happen in this case, the order of transfer cannot be upheld and is thus nullified.
