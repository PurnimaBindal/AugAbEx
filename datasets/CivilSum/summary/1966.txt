Constitution of India, Article 226 - Quo warranto - Writ of quo warranto - The writ of quo warranto is utilized to prevent the public from being usurped by individuals in important and substantial public offices or from the misuse of said office - Every citizen has an interest in ensuring that public duties are only exercised by competent individuals.

Societies Registration Act, 1860 - Service conditions of employees of societies - Adoption of service Rules - Publication - If the organization does not prescribe it, there is no necessity to publish in the official gazette for any service rules adopted by the society for them to be enforceable - Any officer or authority of the Society is obliged to follow such rules.
[Paras 12 to 14]

Haryana State Rural Development Agency Employees Service Rules, 1991, Rules 13, 33, 36, 37 - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Method of recruitment - Appointing Authority - Qualifications - Relaxation - The Financial Commissioner and Secretary, Govt. of the Department or its Commissioner and Secretary are the only individuals competent to make appointments for Class-I and II posts - For other posts, the Chairman of the appointing authority - There is no provision for delegation of powers of appointment or relaxation of rules -  Such power can be exercised only on the basis of recommendations of the State level sanctioning/coordination committee - Any appointment made by a delegated Chief Executive Officer to Class II posts is null - Any appointment can not be validated by mere approval of the governing body - Exercise of such power amounts to illegal usurpation of power.
[Paras 16 and 17]
