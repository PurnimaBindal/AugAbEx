Article 226 of the Constitution of India outlines that the power of judicial review was intended to guarantee fair treatment to individuals and not to interfere with the decisions made by authorities authorized by law, even if they are not correct in the eyes of the Court.
This implies that the Inquiring Authority is typically the sole judge in these matters, and if any legal evidence is available on which the findings cannot be sustained, the correctness of those findings cannot be challenged before the High Court under Article 226 of the Constitution.
