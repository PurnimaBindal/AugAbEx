All India Radio Civil Construction Wing (Class I and Class II Posts) Recruitment Rules, 1975 (as amended) - Chief Engineer (Civil) level I Rules, 1990 - Appointment - A post of Chief Engineer (Civil) level II was created on 28886, which was not included in the schedule appended to Recruitment Rules (as amended), and therefore, the rules were not applicable to the post.
The government of India adopted the composite method for making appointments by promotion and by transfer on deputation failing which.
Recruitment rules for this post were notified on 161190.
The appellant was selected on deputation from another department in absence of a suitable departmental candidate.
The tribunal erred in holding the appointment of the appellant illegal.
It was also noted that in the absence of any rule regulating appointment for the post of Chief Engineer (Civil) level II, the Recruitment Rules of 1975 would apply.
[Paras 7, 8 and 9].
