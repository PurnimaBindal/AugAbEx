Article 16 of the Constitution of India states that misconduct should be considered when promoting employees.
The petitioner was eligible for promotion and was selected by the Departmental Promotion Committee.
However, the promotion was withheld due to alleged misconduct before their last promotion.
The Authority had condoned any misconduct prior to the date of promotion.
Withholding the promotion was not justified and the respondents were directed to give effect to the recommendation of the Departmental Promotion Committee.
[Paras 28 and 44] B Article 311 of the Constitution of India covers disciplinary proceedings.
In this case, there was an unusual delay in issuing the charge sheet, and it was only issued after the petitioner moved a writ petition against withholding of promotion.
No satisfactory reason was given for the delay, and the charges were quashed.
