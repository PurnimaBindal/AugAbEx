Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25F, N and G, 10(c) - Delay and laches - Reference - Termination of service without payment of retrenchment compensation - Reinstatement - Respondent's service terminated without notice or order from 26.3.1989 - Respondent moved to Civil Court based on erroneous advice and had a dismissed suit and appeal - Workmen served demand notice on 3.12.1997 - Labour Court reinstated the respondent with continuity of service and full back wages due to peculiar facts - Appellants directed to pay Rs. 60,000 as full and final settlement - Direction for reinstatement vacated.
