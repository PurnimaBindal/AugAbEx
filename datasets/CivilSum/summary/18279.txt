According to Regulation 230 of the Pension Regulations for the Army, 1961, in the event of the widow of a deceased army personnel remarrying and becoming ineligible for family pension, the widow's mother would be eligible for the family pension.
This provision is based on the idea that the grant of family pension is a social legislation that aims to provide financial assistance to the dependent family members.
Therefore, a liberal interpretation of the regulation should be followed rather than a strict construction principle.
