Army Rules, Rule 13(3) - Extension of service - Refusal of extension - After being discharged from military service, the petitioner was re-enlisted in the Defence Security Corps for a period of 5 years - According to the Commanding Officer's note, the petitioner is in the Low Medical category, is lethargic, incompetent, lacks discipline, and has served physically for 126 days out of 309 days - It was determined that the reasons for not recommending extension were given after considering all relevant factors, including the petitioner's entire service profile - As a result, the motives cannot be regarded as arbitrary, whimsical, or unreasonable.
