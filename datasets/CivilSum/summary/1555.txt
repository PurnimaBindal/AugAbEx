According to UP State University Act, 1973, Section 68 and Statute 21.30 of Garhwal University, termination of an employee on grounds of moral turpitude is permitted.
However, a petitioner who was convicted for possessing illicit liquor and released under Section 4 of Probation of Offenders Act, had maintained good conduct thereafter.
Hence, the termination of his service by the respondents cannot be justified and is considered arbitrary and discriminatory.
