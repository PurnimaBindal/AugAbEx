Based on the Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 226, the petitioners filed for a writ of mandamus to direct authorities to continue engaging them on a contract basis, as they were employed as teachers and could work up to the age of 63.
Employers were allowed to retain the teacher in employment until the age of 65, but there was no evidence presented to the court that the employers did not adhere to the GO It is impossible for a court to force an employer to enter into a contract of service by issuing a mandamus writ.
