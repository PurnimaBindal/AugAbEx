Article 14 of the Constitution of India covers punishment for dereliction of duty.
Two employees were proceeded against with the same set of charges for improper supervision.
While one employee was reverted, the other was removed from service.
The court held that different treatment in punishment violated Article 14 and ordered the delinquent petitioner to be treated the same as the other employee, with the punishment of reversion only.
The court also considered the question of whether full back wages should be given and determined that payment of 50% of the wages on the reverted post was appropriate.
B Article 226 of the Constitution of India deals with the question of relief in writ jurisdiction or whether to leave the matter to the appellate authority or disciplinary authority to determine the quantum of punishment.
The court held that there was sufficient material available to grant relief in writ jurisdiction, citing the fact that a similarly situated employee had been visited with the penalty of reversion as one such factor.
