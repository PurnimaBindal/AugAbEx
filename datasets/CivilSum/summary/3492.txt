Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Re-verification of result - 436 candidates requested recounting - Following re-verification, 294 candidates were deemed to have passed - However, re-verification process was later cancelled based on allegations of irregularities - The re-verification was conducted under Vice Chancellor's orders - It was held that without a proper inquiry into the alleged irregularities, cancellation of the re-verification result is not justified - Therefore, the impugned order is unsustainable.
