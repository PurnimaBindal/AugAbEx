Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate appointment - Limitation - Application made within 5 years period prescribed under scheme from date court declared missing employee as dead - Government computing 5 years on expiry of 7 years time when employee went missing - Government cannot assume employee as dead after 7 years of missing - Declaration of person being dead after 7 years can only be granted by competent court - Reconsideration and decision of application afresh ordered.
[Paras 13 and 15]
