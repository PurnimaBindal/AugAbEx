Constitution of India, Article 226 - Reversion - The petitioner was promoted on an ad hoc basis to the position of clerk with the only condition being that their work was to be monitored for six months.
No negative review of the petitioner's work or conduct during the six month period was presented.
The petitioner had worked as a clerk for fifteen years before the reversion order was passed.
The petitioner cannot be demoted without proper enquiry.
As a result, the order of reversion was quashed and the respondent was instructed to adjust the petitioner as a clerk and provide clerk pay.
[Paras 3 and 4]
