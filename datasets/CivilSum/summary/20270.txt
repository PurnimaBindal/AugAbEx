The respondent was appointed as a Junior Assistant Surgeon in 1966, despite already having a D.M.S.M qualification.
Later, in 1974, he obtained an MBBS degree under the condensed course.
The respondent was declared as Assistant Surgeon with effect from 19.7.1974 in 1980.
It was found that the Tribunal was in error in directing that the seniority of the respondent in the gradation list of Assistant Surgeons should be refixed based on the date of appointment and not the date he obtained the condensed course in MBBS [Paras 7 and 8]
