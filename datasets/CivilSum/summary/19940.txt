Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointments - Eligibility for a Sociology Department Professor - Applicant needed 10 years experience in Post Graduate teaching and/or research at University - Appellant had 8 years 7 months 2 days of experience - Appointed as professor - University had disregarded their experience as a Research Assistant and placed them below already functioning lecturers - Even appointment letter had placed them below - Whether the selection was improper or the appellant was ineligible?
Held :- As there were no allegations of mala fides and the selection was made on comparative eligibility, the appointment of the appellant as professor could not be interfered with - High Court should not have interfered with the expert committee of University for the writ jurisdiction.
[Paras 19 to 28]
