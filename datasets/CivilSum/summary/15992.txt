Constitution of India, Article 311 - Voluntary retirement - Withdrawal of request of voluntary retirement before acceptance - The voluntary retirement request made on June 1, 1992 remained pending till August 1, 1992 - The competent authority cannot decline the request for voluntary retirement without any valid reasons - Although the voluntary retirement can only happen upon the acceptance of the request, withdrawing the request before acceptance renders the acceptance moot - Therefore, the employee will be deemed to continue in service for all purposes - The impugned order is quashed.
