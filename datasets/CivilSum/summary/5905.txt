Constitution of India, Article 311(i) - Absence from duty - Termination - Appointing Authority - Plaintiff was appointed by Chief Engineer, HSEB but termination order was passed by Superintending Engineer - Termination order passed by a subordinate authority to the appointing authority - This violates Article 311(i) of the Constitution - Subsequent authorization in favor of the authority passing the removal order cannot give him the power to remove - The reasoning provided by the lower Appellate Court is not correct - The termination is set aside.
Krishan Kumar v. Divisional Assistant Electrical Engineer, Central Railway, AIR 1979 SC 1912 is followed.
