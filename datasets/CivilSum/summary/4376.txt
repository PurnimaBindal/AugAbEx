The Constitution of India, Article 227 and the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 11-A, deal with Termination and Reinstatement of employees.
The Workman admitted to having taken up employment elsewhere, and the Labour Court awarded compensation in lieu of termination.
However, a Single Judge in the High Court ordered reinstatement, which was beyond their jurisdiction.
If the award was not tenable, the High Court could only have remanded the case.
