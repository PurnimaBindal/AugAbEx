In FMA 443 of 2006: Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Selection process - The Selection Committee made the selection based only on self-appraisal forms submitted by the candidates and failed to record the assessment of candidates by expert members, nor did they provide any reasons while reviewing the case of the petitioner along with other non-recommended cases - Despite not indicating any specific deficiency regarding academic qualifications/eligibility of writ petitioner as per declared norms, the Selection Committee repeatedly refused to recommend the case of the petitioner for promotion - The appellants were directed to comply with the directions of the Single Judge by granting promotion to the post of Professor to the petitioner.
[Paras 21, 22, 38 and 39]
