Article 311 of the Constitution of India and rules 16.2(1) and 16.2(4) of the Punjab Police Rules, 1934 state that a police constable's absence from duty for 48 days is the gravest misconduct and can result in dismissal from service.
In light of the petitioner's record of 17 previous punishments within 12 years of service, the authority deemed the petitioner incorrigible and unsuitable for retention in police services.
Dismissal from service was an appropriate punishment.
[Para 7] B Dismissal from service deprives one of the right to claim pension, and the authority must consider this in cases of dismissal for misconduct.
The petitioner's claim to pension must be considered in light of the decision in Major GS Sodhi v. UOI and the rules 16.2(1) and 16.2(4) of the Punjab Police Rules, 1934.
