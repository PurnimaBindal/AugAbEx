Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Sections 319, 204 - Cross-examination - Summoning of a person as additional accused to stand trial - Persons can be summoned to stand trial on the basis of statements made in examination-in-chief, even without cross-examination.
The term `Evidence' in S 319 Cr.PC does not require evidence to be completed by cross-examination.
[Paras 35, 41, 25, 30, 31 and 34]

Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Sections 319 and 193 - Summoning of a person as additional accused - The court's power to summon a person as accused under S 319 Cr.PC is different from the power under S. 193 Cr.PC Session Judge is empowered to summon a person as accused under S. 193 Cr.PC without recording evidence if the material on record annexed to the report under S. 173 Cr.PC revealed their involvement.
[Para 10]

Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 319 - Evidence Act, Section 3 - Summoning of a person as additional accused - The word `Evidence' in S. 319(1) Cr.PC only relates to oral evidence, not to statements under Section 161 Cr.PC, nor confession, dying declaration, etc., unless provided by oral evidence.
[Para 22]

Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Sections 193, 209 - Case committed to Sessions Court for trial - Sessions court has power to take cognizance of the offence as a court of original jurisdiction - Such power would include summoning of a person or persons whose complicity in the commission of the crime can prima facie be gathered from the material available on record.
[Para 11]

Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 245 - Discharge of accused - The Magistrate can discharge the accused if he finds that no case has been made out against the accused from the recorded evidence.
The Magistrate is required to frame charges if a prima facie case is made out against the accused and give them the opportunity to cross-examine witnesses and then proceed to decide the case.
[Para 29]

Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Sections 213, 234 - Cross-examination - An accused may be convicted even without the opportunity of cross-examination in cases where the witnesses are dead, incapable of giving evidence, or beyond the limits of India if the accused is absconding.
[Para 32]

Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 319 - Summoning of a person as additional accused - The court can only exercise its power under S. 319(1) Cr.PC in cases where evidence recorded during the inquiry or trial reveals involvement of persons other than those arraigned as accused, regardless of whether the Police filed a charge sheet against them.
[Para 9]
