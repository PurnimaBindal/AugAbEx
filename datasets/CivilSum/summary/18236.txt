Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Increment - The petitioner was appointed as Pump Khalasi on 1141975 and later promoted to the post of Pump Operator on 1.3.1981 - However, entries were not made in the service book for several years, resulting in the petitioner being unable to receive an increment in his salary - The court ordered the completion of the service book and the payment of arrears to the petitioner within 3 months.
