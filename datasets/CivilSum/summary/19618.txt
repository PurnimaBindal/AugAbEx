According to Punjab State Accounts Service (State Services Class-II) Rules, 1966, Rules 2(b) and 7(b) and Punjab State Food and Supplies (State Service Class-II) Rules, 1966, Rule 6(1)(c), only Accountants working In the Food and Supplies Department are governed under Punjab State Accounts Service (State Service Class-II) Rules, 1966 and are eligible for promotion to the Class-II service.
Those who are not members of SAS Class-III service cannot be promoted as Assistant Accounts Officers.
The Government is the only competent authority to make appointments under the Rules, not the Heads or the Departments.
Therefore, the promotion of departmental candidates against the Rules by the Food and Supplies Department is not sustainable and directions have been issued to fill up the posts in accordance with the Rules.
