According to Rajasthan Service of Engineers (B&R Branch) Rules, 1954, Rules 7-AA and 27, ad hoc appointees on the post of Assistant Engineer cannot claim treatment in a substantive capacity based on the ground of holding the post prior to upgradation of posts.
In the case of the petitioners who were promoted in 1987, their reliance on Rule 7-AA is deemed misconceived as the rule was introduced in December 1996 after the upgradation of posts and would obviously be prospective.
The court cannot issue a writ requiring the respondents to treat the urgent temporary and ad hoc promotion of the petitioners made by recourse to Rule 27 as substantive.
It is well established in law that ad hoc period of service cannot be counted for seniority.
