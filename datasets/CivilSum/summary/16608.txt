Para 3 of the text talks about the appointment of a Reader in Pharmacognosy and the qualifications required for eligibility.
The post requires a PhD degree with a first-class degree at the Bachelor's and Master's levels in Pharmacognosy with a basic degree in Pharmacy.
The petitioner, who works as a Drug Inspector in the Health Department, cannot claim to belong to either industry or profession just because they took leave from their employment to pursue a PhD qualification.
Additionally, the petitioner is not eligible for the post as they do not possess a first-class degree at the Bachelor's level.
