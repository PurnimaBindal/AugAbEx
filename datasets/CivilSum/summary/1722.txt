Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Bihar Pension Rules, 1950, Rule 43(b) - Retiral benefit - After retirement, recovery from retiral dues can only be made under the provisions of law, such as Rule 43(b) of the Bihar Pension Rules.
Recovery ordered on the ground of petitioner's wrongly received promotion has been set aside.
The order of promotion was not cancelled by competent authority after due enquiry and no opportunity of hearing was given to the petitioner.
The order of recovery cannot simply be based on audit objection and is liable to be set aside.
