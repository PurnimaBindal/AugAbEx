Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 310 - Discharge from service - The appellant was discharged from service due to irregularities in attending to his duties, specifically missing one day or night of work - However, this absence cannot be considered as a regular pattern and therefore cannot determine his efficiency as a Constable - The order of discharge is overturned, but the appellant is warned that another absence of two consecutive days within a year without permission will result in dismissal from service.
