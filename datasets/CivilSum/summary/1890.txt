The Post-Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, Chandigarh Act, 1966, Sub-section (3)(B) 3(3) and Sections 5, 7, 10(2), 12, 13(m), 31, 32, as well as the Post-Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, Chandigarh Rules, 1967, Rule 7(4), 7(5), and Post-Graduate Institute of Medical Education, Research Chandigarh Regulations, 1967, Regulation 34, established that the Institute is the competent authority to make appointments to the post of Dean of the Institute.
The decision-making process leading to the appointment must be exhibited on record and meet the accepted norms of administration.
The decision of the governing body with no reasons, views, or suggestions to support the appointment of Respondent No. 4 as Dean, infringes the rule of fair play and cannot be sustained.
The recommendation of the Director must always be specific in writing and not binding upon the appointing authority, but must have some criteria and basis of opinion to be supported by the record.
The appointment of Respondent No. 4 violated the prescribed rule for appointments, and the court could not overlook the fundamental features of the rule, even if it is an expert body or people.
Finally, the principle of equality demands that all eligible people must have a fair opportunity to be considered based on their seniority and merit, and the decision-making process must be supported by some record.
