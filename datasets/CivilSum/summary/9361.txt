Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 80 - Waiver of Notice - The court heard an injunction suit without granting exemption to dispense with notice, as no objection had been raised by the defendants in either the written statement or grounds of appeal regarding non-urgency or lack of notice.
This conduct implies that the authority for whose benefit the provision is made has waived the notice, therefore the court had jurisdiction to entertain and try the suit.
[Para 12]

Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 99, Order XLI Rule 2 - Remand of suit - Appellate Court remanded back the matter to the trial court to return the plaint to the Plaintiff for presentation after complying with provisions.
However, in view of the deemed waiver of the provision by the authorities, the order of remand cannot be sustained, and the matter is now remanded back to the appellate authority for the decision on merits.
[Paras 16 to 18 and 21]
