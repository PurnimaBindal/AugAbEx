Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Sections 16(3) and 28 - Delhi School Education Rules, 1973, Rules 138, 144 and 145 - Admission - CBSE Examination - The head of a private unaided school cannot deny admission to students of its own school to class XI who had passed Class X, CBSE with marks less than 50 per cent in aggregate and cannot regulate admission by prescribing the criteria of cut off level of marks under Rule 145 - Once admitted to an educational institution, a student continues to progress class after class until he leaves the school - Admission to the next higher class cannot be considered a fresh or readmission.
