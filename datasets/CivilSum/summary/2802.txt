The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 16 and 226, dictate that appointments must follow a proper advertisement process, and selecting a candidate for a lower, unadvertised post violates this protocol.
Furthermore, the advertised position's clause granting the selection committee the power to appoint recommended candidates against unadvertised posts does not authorize such an action.
Therefore, the appointment of the applicant has been nullified.
