Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation - Petitioner employed as Helper on a fixed salary for approximately 12 years - Opponent argues against regularisation due to pending disciplinary proceedings against petitioner for absenteeism - Employer's right to take disciplinary action is separate from worker's right to regularisation - Temporary employment of worker even after quarter of total service in department is unjustified - Court orders respondents to regularise petitioner's services within two months.
