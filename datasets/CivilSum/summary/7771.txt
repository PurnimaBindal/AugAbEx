According to Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, holiday wages and overtime must be established as existing rights to be considered for compensation.
However, there is no award, order, or notification granting such rights to the workmen in question.
Additionally, the resolution passed by the Committee to grant overtime has already been cancelled by the government.
Therefore, an application under Section 33-C(2) is not maintainable.
