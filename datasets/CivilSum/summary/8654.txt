Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Back wages - A Conductor with Punjab Roadways was dismissed from service and found guilty of misconduct by the Labour Court.
However, the punishment of dismissal was deemed too extreme and the Court directed reinstatement without back wages.
The Court's decision was challenged by the petitioner, but upon consideration of the evidence, no illegality or infirmity was found with the Labour Court's order.
The petition was dismissed.
