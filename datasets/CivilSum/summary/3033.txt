The Punjab Emergency Concession Rules, 1965 are ipso facto applicable to UT Chandigarh employees.

Under Rule 4 of the 1965 Punjab National Emergency Rules, length of service granted for seniority benefits cannot be counted towards experience.
The difference between experience and seniority should be noted as well, as deemed seniority cannot be mistaken for actual physical experience.
