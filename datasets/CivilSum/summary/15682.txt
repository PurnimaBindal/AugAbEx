According to Punjab National Bank (Employees) Pension Regulations, 1955, Regulation 33(1), the validity of compulsory retirement and pension entitlement is determined by the cut off date of 11193.
In this case, the petitioner was compulsorily retired by way of penalty on 181291 and is therefore disentitled to pension.
The rationale behind fixing the cut off date is the signing of settlement between various Employees Union and I.B.A.
The classification is deemed reasonable and permissible as the petitioner already collected his retiral benefits.
