Constitution of India, Article 311 and 226 - AP Education Act, Section 79(3) - Suspension - Re-instatement - Preliminary enquiry - Disciplinary proceedings - Mis-conduct - The Principal of Technical Institute faced serious allegations of financial and administrative irregularities and disobedience.
A preliminary enquiry found the allegations to be prima facie proved and a regular enquiry was ordered after a period of suspension.
The High Court may direct the authority to continue paying the delinquent's full salary and allowances until the completion of enquiry.
However, if the enquiry is not completed within the prescribed period, the High Court cannot direct reinstatement and restoration of position without considering the facts of the case.
An order of reinstatement during suspension was overturned and the respondents were instructed to continue paying the delinquent full salary and allowances until the enquiry's completion.
