Constitution of India, Article 14 - Equal Pay for equal work - Research Fellows cannot demand equality in emoluments because they work for different agencies - Funding agency can only determine the compensation of Research Fellows on their projects - Chandigarh Administration is not obligated by Central Government guidelines unless expressly adopted - The principle of `Equal pay for equal work' does not apply to employees of different agencies.
