Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Transfer order - Chief Accounts Officer of Development Authority filed writ petition - Development Authority is a separate, independent and statutory body - Its employees are governed by the regulations laid down by the Authority themselves - The petitioner's post is an ex cadre one and not part of the centralized service - As there is no provision for transferring such an employee, the Government cannot transfer him even if it is his appointing authority.
[Paras 9, 10 and 12]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ petition - Necessary party - Non-impeachment of - The Vice Chairman of Development Authority appointed an Accounts Officer to perform the duties of CAO until someone is appointed by the Government - Such a person performing duties of Chief Accounts Officer is not necessary party to writ petition.
