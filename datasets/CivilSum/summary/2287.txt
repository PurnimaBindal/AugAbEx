The Constitution of India specifies in Articles 14 and 16 that appointment to H.C.S.
(Executive Branch) must be based on merit.
Therefore, once candidates are selected, appointments must strictly follow the order of merit.
Appointing a candidate of lesser merit over higher merit candidates constitutes a breach of the fundamental right to equality under Articles 14 and 16.
[Para 11] B The Haryana Civil Service (Executive Branch) and Allied Services and other Services Common/Combined Examination Act, 2002, Section 4 is not retroactive to appointments made by the government from the selection list.
Hence, even with the ban on appointments, the government must follow the constitutional provisions of Articles 14 and 16.
