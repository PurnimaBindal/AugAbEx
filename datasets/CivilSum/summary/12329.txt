Constitution of India, Article 226 - Discharge from service - Natural Justice - The petitioner, a probationer, was discharged from service for providing forged academic qualification documents.
The document was thoroughly investigated and was officially declared forged.
The petitioner failed to present any evidence to rebut the decision.
Even if relief is granted based on technical violations of natural justice, the petitioner would be unable to defend the case resulting in the same outcome.
The court cannot grant relief solely based on formalities.
Terminating the petitioner's services was a just and reasonable decision by the appointing authority.
[Paras 6 and 7]
