When it comes to departmental enquiries, strict rules of the Evidence Act are not in place, but procedures are in place to ensure fairness.
Any officers who provide testimony must be cross-examined at the demand of the accused, or else the document cannot be used as evidence.
The accused also has the right to prove the document is false by presenting evidence.
Denying the accused the opportunity to cross-examine officers or disprove their testimony violates natural justice principles.
[Para 5] B The Enquiry Officer must not be biased, and must not adopt a predetermined procedure that aligns with government interests.
If an Enquiry Officer is found to have done so, he must be replaced to ensure fairness.
