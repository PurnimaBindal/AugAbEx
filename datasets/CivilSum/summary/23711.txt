PGI is obligated to follow the government's reservation policy for appointments to the posts of Asstt.
Professor and above for conducting research or for organizing, conducting and directing research and posts classified as Scientific or Technical.
Reservation in appointments to research posts is in conformity with Articles 16(4) and 15 of the Constitution, and failure to grant the benefit of reservation to Dalits in such appointments is a violation of Article 16(4).
Recruitment for backlog vacancies will occur in the next recruitment year, without violating Articles 14, 15, and 16 of the Constitution.
The government can make special drive recruitment of Dalits against long-standing backlog vacancies meant for them and fill all such vacancies at one go.
A court or any other authority cannot direct to de-reserve the vacancies meant for Dalits without first approval of SC Commission and the Government or to fill up the reserved vacancies by General candidates.
The application of roster to single post cadre and appointment by promotion to carry forward post is constitutional and not violative of Articles 14 and 16(1).
The reservation relates only to the post and not vacancy.
Relaxation in merit for admission to research courses of super-specialties does not affect the efficiency of the discipline.
