According to the service book, the petitioner's date of birth is 1771950, but according to a certificate from the Registrar of Births and Deaths, it is actually 17111952.
The petitioner's mother got married on 2041951, which conflicts with the recorded date.
The petitioner did not try to change the date of birth in accordance with the notification from the Punjab Government in 1994.
The lower courts were reasonable in deciding that the petitioner's correct date of birth is 19.11.1952 and that the relief claimed was justified.
The employee should have sought to alter the date of birth under civil law if he could not get it changed under the rules of service.
