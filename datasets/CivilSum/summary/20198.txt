The Railway Servants (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, Rules 2(a) and 9 under Article 311 of the Constitution of India discuss the authority to impose disciplinary action on railway servants.
In the case of Divisional Railway Manager, Railways, who was not the appointing authority, the power to impose punishment is not competent.
However, the delegation of appointment power to General Manager and disciplinary power to Divisional Manager was made.
Therefore, the doctrine that a delegator cannot further delegate his powers to delegatee has no application and the order of removal from service that was set aside by the Tribunal was held illegal.
