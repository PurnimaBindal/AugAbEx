Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 39 Rule 1 - Temporary Injunction/Transfer - Petitioner is to be transferred to the nearest station considering her difficulties - Temporary injunction was granted by both courts below based solely on the plaintiff's unmarried status and need for parental protection, which is not a valid reason to interfere with administrative orders - When the lower courts make rulings without following established law, the High Court can intervene under section 115 C.P.C. - No evidence of malicious intent on the part of transferring authorities - The temporary injunction orders of the lower court have been overturned.
