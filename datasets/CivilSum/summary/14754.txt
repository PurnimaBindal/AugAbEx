Constitution of India, Article 16 - Contingency paid staff - The petitioner was hired as a contingency paid staff member at P.A.P.
Jalandhar and the pay and benefits given to such staff are approved by the Deputy Commissioner of the District.
Upon moving to Patiala district, the petitioner is only eligible for the compensation rates for the contingency staff approved by the Deputy Commissioner of Patiala district and not those of Jalandhar, as he is not a temporary or regular employee of P.A.P.
