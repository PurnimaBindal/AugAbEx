As per Constitution of India, Article 311, departmental enquiry must follow principles of natural justice and the delinquent petitioner deserves no interference on said ground as he has not denied writing the incriminating letters or having confessed to the embezzlement charge.
This admission establishes at least temporary embezzlement or criminal breach of trust and justifies the punishment of dismissal from service.
[Paras 5, 7 and 8]  B.
While Constitution of India, Article 311 also mandates observing principles of natural justice in cases of removal from service, the court must determine if violating such principles has resulted in miscarriage of justice.
If not, re-exercising the case and reinstatement of the delinquent would serve no purpose as he does not deserve it.
