Under the Karnataka Secondary Education Examination Board Act, 1966, Sections 26 and 33, the State Government has the authority to create regulations and approve those framed by the Education Board.
The State Government can also issue any directive concerning anything performed or completed by the Board.
As a result, the State Government can instruct the Board not to factor in certain language subject marks when determining whether a candidate has passed the examination, and this direction does not conflict with the Act's provisions.
