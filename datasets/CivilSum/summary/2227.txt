According to the Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol. 1, Rule 44, ad hoc and temporary employees have no right to the post they are occupying.
Additionally, if the post was created temporarily, it does not give the employee any right to it.
Courts cannot issue directions for the regularization of such employees if there is no vacant post available.
[Paras 21, 22, 24]

As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, courts cannot direct the consideration for regularization of an employee if there is no statutory rule of regularization or vacant post available.
Mere possession of eligibility qualifications is not the basis for regularization of temporary employees.
The courts are supposed to determine if the actions of the legislature or executive are in accordance with the law.
[Paras 23, 26, 32, 33]

The Supreme Court's simple directions for regularization of ad hoc and temporary employees do not set a principle of law for others to claim the same relief.
It is only when the Supreme Court sets a principle of law that it is seen as a precedent.
[Para 27]

Ad hoc and temporary employees have no right to the post and unless there is specific evidence of equality and discrimination, the claim for equal pay for equal work cannot be upheld.
There may be a difference in the nature and culture of the employment which results in varying qualities and types of work.
[Paras 34 to 39]
