Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 39 - Education - Providing free education for children of deceased Government servants - Father of the petitioner retired from a non-pensionable post - Petitioner was dependent on his mother, who was a Government servant and died thereafter - Punjab Government policy of free education applied to the petitioner - Petitioner eligible for reimbursement of tuition fees due to the expensive cost of MBBS - Petitioner was dependent on his mother during the service period of his father - Respondents instructed to pay the petitioner's tuition fees from the date of his mother's death.
