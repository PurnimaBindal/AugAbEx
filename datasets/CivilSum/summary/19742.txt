Constitution of India, Article 309 - Kerala Service Rules, Rule 60(c) - Retirement/Date of Superannuation - Limitation/Correction of date of birth - Members of the Teaching Staff of educational institutions shall serve until March 31st of the academic year, except in exceptional cases.
The date of retirement is not the date when an officer completes 55 years, or even the last day of the month in which they complete 55 years.
The limitation for correcting date of birth will be calculated from March 31st of the academic year when the individual turns 55 years old.
