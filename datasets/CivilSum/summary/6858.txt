The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 state that a widow of a workman can claim pensionary benefits by treating her husband as a regular employee.
A case was presented where the initial appointment was made on December 1st, 1983 and termination on September 15th, 1984 with the award of reinstatement with continuity passed on August 3rd, 1991.
The single judge dismissed the appeal, and the workman qualified the screening test for the post of telephone mechanic on May 2nd, 1999.
The petitioner's husband was not considered for regularization of service due to the pendency of a special appeal.
The court held that it was unfounded and violative of Articles 14 and 21 to deny regularization of service to the petitioner's husband and directed the respondents to regularize the service from the date the junior was regularized.
They must also consider the case for grant of pensionary benefits under the circular of DOPT dated November 16th, 1992.
