Under the Aligarh Muslim University Act, 1920, Sections 24, 27, and 31, the Executive Council has the power to establish regulations for the appointment of Professors and Readers under the Merit Promotion Scheme.
Due to this power, the Executive Council has created rules that provide for inter se seniority between Professors appointed under the Merit Promotion Scheme and those appointed under the general scheme.
As such, Professors appointed under the Merit Promotion Scheme are entitled to their inter se seniority with those appointed under the general Promotion Scheme.
The mode of appointment of professors in Aligarh Muslim University differs from that in UP State University, so the Rashmi Srivastava case's ratio does not apply to the seniority inter se between the Professors appointed in general and Merit Promotion Scheme in Aligarh Muslim University.
Those promoted as Professors in Merit Promotion Scheme in Aligarh Muslim University are a part of the cadre of Professors and are entitled to seniority above those promoted/appointed later in time in the general scheme, according to Paras 8, 9, and 12.
