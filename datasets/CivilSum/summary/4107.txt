According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226 and Punjab Civil Service Rules, Rule 7.5, a petitioner who resigns from government service to contest elections cannot simply withdraw their resignation after the fact.
The resignation must have been made under compelling or overwhelming circumstances, which must be proven at the time the resignation is tendered.
In this specific case, the petitioner was unsuccessful in the election and wanted to withdraw their resignation, but the court determined that this was not a justified reason to do so.
Therefore, the petition was dismissed.
[Paras 14 to 16]
