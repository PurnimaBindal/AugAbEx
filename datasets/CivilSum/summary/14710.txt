Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay - A petitioner was posted to a specific station and reported for duty but was not allowed to join - The concerned officer was obligated to allow the petitioner to join once he had reported for duty - The petitioner's interests cannot be adversely affected by the failure to do so - The petitioner may use earned leave as they choose - The department cannot demand the petitioner apply for leave for the duration of time they were waiting for posting orders to be effected.
