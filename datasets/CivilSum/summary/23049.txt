According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 311, a cadet's training can be terminated under the Air Force Order (FAO) 79/33 if they are deemed unsuitable or found to be engaging in indiscipline or lacking in necessary officer qualities.
If the Training Review Board finds evidence of the alleged indiscipline and has granted the cadet adequate opportunity to defend themselves, then they cannot claim any prejudice or arbitrariness.
