The Central Public Works Department (Director General of Works) Recruitment Rules, 1986 (As amended by Amendment Rules 1992) - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Promotion - Qualifying service - Supersession - Individuals who have the required years of service will be considered for promotion, even if they are junior.
In cases of supersession, the actual service period prescribed is not required and a national promotion given to the person superseded will count towards actual service for eligibility to be considered for the higher post.
This is to correct the wrong done to the person and deny this would perpetuate the wrong.
[Paras 16, 17 and 18]
