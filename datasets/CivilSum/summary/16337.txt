The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Haryana Housing Board Act, 1971 (as extended to the Union Territory, Chandigarh), Section 7 - Appointment - Engineering Department - The chairman of the Housing Board, who was also the Petitioner Chief Engineer and Secretary in his Department - had his term fixed for 3 years - Recalling him before his term ended without a notice or an opportunity for hearing was illegal.
[Paras 6 and 8]
Haryana Housing Board Act, 1971 (as extended to the Union Territory, Chandigarh) - Deputation/Appointment on deputation in a statutory body - Departmental officials can be sent to work in a statutory body - Holding a post as a member even after superannuation is not prohibited - Appointment in a statutory body does not constitute as reappointment - There was no illegality or irregularity in the appointment.
