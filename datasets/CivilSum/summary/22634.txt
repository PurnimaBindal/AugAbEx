Rajasthan Education Subordinate Services Rules, 1971, Rule 9(3) - Recruitment of teachers - Determination of vacancies - Wait list - The intent of the legislature is clear in Rule 9(3) that vacancies are determined only once a year, and select lists prepared in the previous year could be used to fill vacancies of the subsequent year.
