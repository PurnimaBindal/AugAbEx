Admissions - Education - The petitioners in this case had the opportunity to repeat their examination under the unamended regulations.
However, with the introduction of amended regulations, the criteria for passing the semester were relaxed, but the right to repeat the examination was abolished.
Since they had already taken advantage of the repeat examination under the unamended rule, they are not entitled to the benefits of the relaxed criteria under the amended regulations.
[Paras 12, 13 and 14]  B Constitution of India, Article 224 - Education - It is the responsibility of specialized bodies to regulate academic standards.
The court does not have the expertise to prescribe its own standards.
