In accordance with the Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 15(1), disciplinary proceedings require mandatory compliance with Rule 16, such as providing the enquiry report and witness list to the petitioner and allowing cross-examination.
Failure to adhere to these requirements can result in a miscarriage of justice and violation of the principles of natural justice.
