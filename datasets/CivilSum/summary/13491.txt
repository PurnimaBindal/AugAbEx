The Guru Nanak Dev University, Amritsar Act of 1969 stipulates in Sections 14(5), 20 and 28 the University's authority to formulate a policy of reservation for admissions in line with constitutional provisions.
The Syndicate of the University made a conscious and deliberate decision to provide reservation in specific border areas, excluding districts Ferozepur and Gurdaspur, for candidates seeking admission to the LL.B.
program.
This decision was deemed to be in keeping with the objective of reservation and was upheld.
[Paras 8, 10 and 11] B.
In matters of educational and administrative policy, the court is unlikely to interfere with the decision of academic bodies like the Syndicate.
Respect and weightage must be afforded to their decisions unless they are found to be palpably wrong and unconstitutional.
[Para 9] C A candidate who is not found entitled to the benefit of reservation after appearing for an Entrance Test and having their result declared does not acquire a right to admission.
No plea of equitable or promissory estoppel may be made on their behalf.
