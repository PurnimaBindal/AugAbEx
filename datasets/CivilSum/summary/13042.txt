Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Revised pay scale - Consideration of previous service in other department for higher post of Professor - According to the resolution dated 3042004, passed by Syndicate of University Grants Commission, previous services rendered by the teachers in any other university/organisation would be considered, as long as there is no break in service.
Therefore, arguing that only service rendered after the date of the resolution is valid, is not valid.
[Paras 19 to 23]
