Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission to P.G.
Courses - Eligibility - Only in-service candidates who have completed two years of service in rural, desert, hilly, or tribal areas are eligible for admission - The cut-off date is fixed to count two years of actual experience - In-service candidates cannot claim parity with open category candidates - The classification is reasonable - The petitioners appeared in the written examination without any protest - In-service candidates are barred from challenging the fixing of the cut-off date to count experience.
[Paras 32 to 35]
