Constitution of India, Article 14 - Leave - Leave without pay - The mere mention of "LWP" alone does not grant leave - The competent authority must make a positive decision to grant leave - The employee still needs to provide a reasonable explanation for his absence from duty.

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Absence from duty - Enquiry - Habitual absentee - Past service record - Natural justice - If the authority finds the workman to be a habitual absentee while dealing with a charge of absence from duty, a conclusive finding as to the conduct of the workman can be reached without complaint of a violation of the principles of natural justice - Matters that cannot possibly be defended do not need to be put to a regular inquiry.
