Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1)(c) - Award/Delay and Laches - Termination of workman's services in 1974 - Notice given to management after 71/2 years - Labour Court ordered reinstatement of workman but back wages awarded from notice date - Management filed writ petition - Court ruled workman not entitled to relief due to delay without explanation - No limit prescribed, but disputes should be referred as soon as possible.
