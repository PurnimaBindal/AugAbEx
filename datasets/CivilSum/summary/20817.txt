Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300-A - Provident Fund Scheme for employees of market committee - Calculation of Qualifying service is to commence from the date when contribution towards Provident Fund started - Pension is not a gift of the state, it is earned post retirement - Rule interpretation - When encountering contradictory rules, the one later in time should be read down to conform to the earlier rule, making the rule valid and prevent it from being arbitrary and thereby violate Article 14 of the Constitution.
