The Payment of Gratuity Act of 1972, specifically Sections 2(e), 5 and 14, as well as the Post and Telegraphs Extra Departmental Agent (Conduct) Service Rules of 1964, apply to Extra Departmental Agents (EDAs) in the post office department with regards to gratuity.
There is no exemption for EDAs from the Gratuity Act, and the benefits available to them under the EDA Rules are not more favorable than those available under the Gratuity Act.
The argument that workman are not covered by the definition of "employee" in Section 2(e) of the Gratuity Act has been rejected as EDAs hold posts under the Central Government for the purposes of pension and gratuity.
