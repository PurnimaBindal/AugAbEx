Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Municipal Corporation Act, 1976, Section 74 - Alternative remedy - A writ petition challenging the election of Mayor of Municipal Corporation on the same grounds as an election petition provided for under the Act was filed.
However, the Act also provides for the remedy of appeal against the order of Election Tribunal.
The petition was dismissed on the grounds of an alternate remedy being available.
