Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 - Promotion - Cancellation of - Tribunal cancelled the promotions of two officers as they were found to be illegal - Neither of the officers challenged the order of the Tribunal - In a petition filed by the State, a Single Judge overturned the promotions of individuals who were not even the subject matter of the tribunal's challenge - It was held that without officers challenging the order, it was final against them - The order of the Single Judge was set aside as the State could not have passed such an order in a writ filed by them.
