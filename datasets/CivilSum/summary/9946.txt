Punjab Civil Service (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, (amended by Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) 1st Amendment Rules, 1979, Rule 4(2) - Voluntary retirement - Plaintiff sought to withdraw notice for voluntary retirement before expiry of notice period - Defendants failed to provide valid explanation as to why plaintiff, who had good service record and was recommended for promotion, was not allowed to withdraw - Lower courts erred in upholding order for plaintiff's retirement - Plaintiff entitled to declaration that he remains in service until superannuation with all corresponding benefits as per Rules.
