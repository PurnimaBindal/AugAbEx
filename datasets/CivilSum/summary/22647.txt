Penalty or damages cannot be levied by subordinate legislation against the principle legislative provisions - To impose damages, an individual must have had mens rea or actus reus to violate the law - Penalties should be strictly interpreted.
[Paras 12, 17, and 21] B.
When a disciplinary jurisdiction has been established to levy penal damages due to an enabling provision, it cannot be interpreted as mandatory - Section 85B of the Employees State Insurance Act gives an enabling provision, not a mandatory one - Therefore, the same does not necessitate the computation of the quantum of the penalty prescribed by the regulations.
