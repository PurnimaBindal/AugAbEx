Petitioners who worked as Masters on an ad hoc basis before February 1979, drew a salary in the same pay scale as those appointed on a regular basis.
However, they declined the benefit of advance increments due to their lack of eligibility for regular service.
The petitioners later improved their qualifications, but the condition of regular service imposed on them in order to receive the advance increments is unjustified and discriminatory.
There should be no different treatment given to members of the same cadre of service.
As a result, the respondents are directed to release the benefits of advance increments with arrears of salary to the petitioners.
