Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Reduction of pay - Petitioner appointed as conductor and found to have an excess of Rs. 162/- in his bag during his journey - Disciplinary authority passed final order reducing pay, without furnishing inquiry report to petitioner - Proceedings held to be bad in law due to violation of principles of natural justice, as petitioner was not supplied with copy of enquiry report - Respondents ordered to provide copy of inquiry report to petitioner and to initiate fresh proceedings in the matter.
[Paras 51, 52 and 53]
