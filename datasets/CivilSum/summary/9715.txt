The Payment of Gratuity Act (39 of 1972), Section 4, Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institution Act (19 of 1992), Section 43, and Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institution (Recognition, Grant-in-aid and Service, Conditions etc.) Rules 1993, Rule 18(2) state that employees of aided educational institutions shall be eligible for gratuity provided under the Act of 1972.
It is the primary duty of the employer to pay gratuity to the employees.
Retired teacher's non-payment of gratuity was an improper violation of Rule 18(2) of the Rules.
