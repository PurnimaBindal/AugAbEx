Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission - To Engineering Courses - Petitioners claim respondents did not extend benefit of reservation and violated policy by classifying students as 'free' and 'paid' for admissions - But, petitioners' merit position was well below last candidate admitted and no violation of admitting more candidates than available seats occurred - Ist and second merit list were issued strictly based on merit and only those higher in merit than petitioners were admitted - The criteria adopted by respondents for admission cannot be challenged.
