Admission/Misrepresentation/Merit - It is the right of a candidate to get admission based on their merit alone.
In the case where a petitioner was admitted using higher marks shown in the gazette by mistake, a show cause notice was issued to cancel her admission as she did not contest this.
It is clear that she knew she did not actually earn the displayed marks and so cannot benefit from the error.
The order to cancel her admission is therefore justified.
[Para 8 and 9] B. Admission/Equal Merit/Merit - When two candidates have the same merit, the candidate who is older must be granted admission.
