Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Staff Services Rules `Confed', 1979, Rule 13 Seniority - Fixation of seniority - The petitioner was unfairly denied seniority from the date of appointment without justification, while a junior colleague was granted such seniority - Since cadre and seniority are common, the petitioner must be considered for seniority from the date of appointment - The lack of substantial explanation for the discrepancy constitutes discrimination.
[Paras 5 and 6]
