Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Examination - Grace marks - A petitioner who obtained below 50% on Paper I and IV was asked to retake Paper I and failed again - The petitioner argued that she was entitled to seven grace marks but had already used four during the May, 1996 examination - Since only three grace marks remained, the petitioner argued she still would not have passed - The petition was ultimately dismissed.
