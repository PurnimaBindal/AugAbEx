Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Punjab Police (First Amendment) Rules, 1990, Rule 2 - Executive Clerical Cadre (Extra Hand) - Independent Cadre - The Executive Clerical Cadre is an independent cadre.
If employees have already opted for this cadre under Rule 2 and their choice has been accepted, a transfer order to a different cadre cannot be justified.
[Paras 2 & 4]
