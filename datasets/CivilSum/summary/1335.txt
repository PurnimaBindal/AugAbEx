Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 and 11-A - Punishment - Court's jurisdiction to interfere - The court can only interfere with the punishment given by the punishing authority if it is extremely disproportionate and shocks the conscience of the court.
Mere absence from work because of non-sanctioned leave for a few days cannot be considered such serious misconduct that warrants dismissal.
