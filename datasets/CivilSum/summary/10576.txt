Constitution of India, Article 226 - Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 24 - Bar Council of India Rules, Rule 36 - Appointment - Appointment as Public Prosecutor - District and Sessions Judges sending out notifications for applications on bio-data from advocates with certain standing not sanctioned by Section 24 - Under the rules made under Section 49(1)(c) of the Advocates Act, 1961, advocates cannot apply for or solicit work for the appointment of a professional person for rendering service to the state - Rule 36 of the Bar Council's rules clearly states that advocates cannot solicit or advertise work through various means.
