The cut off date for pension policy, which considers State and Railway Service, was implemented through a 1982 Govt. of India Circular, with the cut off date set as 3131982, in conjunction with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
However, the appellant was denied pension benefits since he retired on 121973.
The cut off date was deemed arbitrary and in violation of Article 14, and the appellant was entitled to pension benefits starting from the date the policy came into force.
