Railway Protection Force Rules, Rule 47(b) and Constitution of India, Article 311(2) were examined in this case.
The competent authority may dispense with a departmental inquiry if they are satisfied based on the circumstances of the case, rather than arbitrarily.
The defendant, who was tasked with guarding a train, did nothing to apprehend the culprits when the train was robbed.
It can be inferred from this that the delinquent was complicit with the criminals and committed severe misconduct.
The dismissal order was therefore validated.

In this case, a writ petition was found to be inappropriate because the alternative statutory appeal process was available.
Dismissal from government service has its own legal remedy.
