According to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo)(bb), if a person is initially appointed on a 89-day basis and such arrangement continues for over 240 days, the termination of that employee's services without giving retrenchment compensation would be deemed a fraud on the statute.
As a result, the employee would be entitled to reinstatement with back wages.
Furthermore, in cases where it is unclear whether the employee was gainfully employed or not, the burden of proof is on the employer.
Moreover, the provisions of section 2(oo)(bb) should be liberally interpreted.
Finally, if a workman is allowed to continue by making periodic appointments, then their case does not fall within the jurisdiction of Section 2(oo)(bb).
