Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2(1) - Misconduct - Gravest misconduct - Rule 16.2(1) applies to even a single act of the gravest misconduct - The word "acts" in Rule 16.2(1) encompasses both singular and plural forms.

Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2(1) - Dismissal/Misconduct/Absence from duty - Dismissal from service on the grounds of misconduct or absence from duty - Dismissal under Rule 16.2(1) of Punjab Police Rules requires proof that the act of misconduct renders the employee totally unfit for police service - Punishing Authority's orders lack a proper legal basis.
