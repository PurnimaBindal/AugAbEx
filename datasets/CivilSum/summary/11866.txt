Punjab Civil Secretariat (State Service Class-II) Rules, 1952, Rule 9 - Seniority - Temporary/ad hoc service - According to Rule 9, Seniority should be determined based on the date of substantive appointment to the post.
Members of service appointed on the temporary/ad hoc basis and their services regularized with the recommendation of the Board, can use such service towards seniority.
However, those appointed as a makeshift arrangement without the recommendations of the Board, cannot claim the benefit of ad hoc service after regularisation.
[Paras 6, 7 and 8]"
