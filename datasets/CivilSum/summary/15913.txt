Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Promotion - Retrospective promotion - Arrears of salary - Petitioner never declined higher post - If the petitioner was not promoted to the higher post due to pending litigation, he cannot be denied arrears of salary - The condition that he would not be paid difference in arrears of salary on the promoted post when retrospective promotion was given is unsustainable in law - The rule of "No work No pay" would not apply - Respondents must pay arrears of pay and allowances to petitioner on the post of Transport Sub Inspector w.e.f.
the date of his retrospective promotion and recalculate his pay after giving him benefit of increments due on promoted post - He shall also be entitled to any further consequential benefits admissible to him as a result of promotion.
