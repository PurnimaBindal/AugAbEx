Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 11 and 25-F - Retrenchment/Retrenchment Compensation/Compensation in lieu of back wages - Petitioner's services were terminated prematurely during the contract of service after working for only 9 months post-return from foreign assignment - Management was unable to provide him a similar position in India, with promised wages as per the contract - The only mistake committed by the management was not paying the petitioner retrenchment compensation, which would have been only Rs. 5000/- - The Labour Court's compensation award of Rs. 40,000/- in lieu of back wages is reasonable, even if termination was in violation of Section 25-F; the petitioner is not eligible for full back wages.
