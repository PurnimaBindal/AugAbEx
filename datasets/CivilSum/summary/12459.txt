The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226, along with Section 115 of the Evidence Act, establish the concept of estoppel.
In this instance, the Punjab Government issued a notification merging the cadres of Inspectors Grade-II and Inspectors Grade I in the Cooperation Department, and placed the Inspectors Grade-II below their counterparts.
The notification was endorsed to both wings of the Cooperation Department, but the merger was implemented only in the Registrar wing and not in the Audit Wing.
The notification was issued due to equal duties and salary, and the surplus posts were abolished, but the Government cannot later claim that the notification was only meant for the Registrar wing, and not the Audit Wing.
A clarification issued by the Superintendent on behalf of the Government cannot override the notification, as the Government is obliged to implement it for all purposes, equally in both wings.
[Paras 7 to 10, 13 and 15]
