Constitution of India, Article 14 - Haryana Municipal Employees Pension and General Provident Fund Rules, 1993, Rule 2 (1) (i) - Cut-off Scheme - The cut-off date for implementation of Pension Scheme was prescribed on April 16, 1992 in consonance with the amendment introduced in sections 38 and 57 of the Haryana Municipal Act, 1973.
This date was not arbitrary or imaginary and was open to the State to fix.
Before the cut-off date, employees who had retired got the benefit of the Contributory Fund Scheme while employees in service were given the option to continue with the Contributory Fund Scheme or be governed by the Pension Scheme.
These provisions do not violate Article 14 of the Constitution.
