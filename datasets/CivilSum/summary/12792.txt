The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 focus on natural justice in regards to promotion and reversion.
The principles of natural justice vary based on the specific circumstances of each case and its application.
The Courts have recognized the importance of allowing an individual to participate in the decision-making process that affects them as part of Article 14 of the Constitution.
In this case, the petitioner was appointed as Director, Distance Education after fulfilling all requirements under the rules and was entirely capable of holding the position with the revised qualifications.
Unfortunately, the petitioner's appointment was reviewed without giving him a chance to participate, and his overall qualifications were not taken into account.
This action by the respondents has been deemed a violation of natural justice and Article 14 of the Constitution.
