"Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Reversion - Petitioner has been employed by Respondent No. 1 since 7th June, 1989 - In 1992, there was a merger and the petitioner was appointed on 12th January, 1994 - A seniority list was prepared and published, taking into account his continuous service, and objections were invited from all officers - Based on merit, he was promoted as Section Officer in 1997 - Respondent No. 4 did not raise any objections - In law, Respondent No. 4 cannot claim to be senior to the petitioner in the post of Assistant - The petition is granted and the order of reversion is set aside.
