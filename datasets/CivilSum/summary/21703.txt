According to Section 17 of the Industrial Disputes Act, a Labour Court's order for reinstatement of a worker does not necessarily entitle the worker to fixation of pay after accounting for notional increments earned during their absence.
This is because there is a difference between an order for reinstatement with a simple direction for continuity of service and an order where reinstatement is accompanied by a specific direction for entitlement to all consequential benefits, including increments earned during the period of absence.
The case 2003(4) SCT 785 (SC) is referred to for support.
