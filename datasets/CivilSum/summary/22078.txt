Constitution of India, Articles 136 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 11-A - Powers of Labour Court - The Labour Court's power under section 11-A of I.D.
Act is extensive enough to change a punishment of dismissal into a lighter penalty - A workman who was dismissed from service for unauthorised absence was ordered to be reinstated by the Labour Court with continuity of service without backwages - The High Court can modify the award given by the Labour Court - In appropriate cases, the Supreme Court can also adjust the punishment imposed on the workman - The order of dismissal was annulled, and the workman was treated as a probationer and required to pay Rs. 25 lakhs.
