Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Seniority - Continuity of service - Reinstatement with continuity of service - A claim for promotion from peon to clerk was rejected on the basis that the petitioner was ineligible due to lack of five years of experience.
However, if reinstatement is done with continuity of service, the employer cannot disregard the period of forced absence when calculating experience for promotion consideration.
[Paras 5 to 8]
