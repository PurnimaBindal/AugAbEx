Constitution of India, Article 311 - West Bengal Co-operative Societies Act, 1983, Sections 129 and 147 - Dismissal from services - Misconduct - Enquiry conducted - Enquiry Officer instructed payment of subsistence allowance to delinquent while enquiry was ongoing - Noncompliance of directive by initiating fresh enquiry - Bank did not provide reason for starting new enquiry - Notification of second enquiry received by employee 2 weeks after the fixed date - Second enquiry concluded ex parte on scheduled date - Order of dismissal deemed unlawful.
