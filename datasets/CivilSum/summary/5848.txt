Section 33-C(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act states that for a workman to utilize its provisions, he must show that he has money due to him according to a settlement, an award, or Chapter 5A or 5B of the Act.
Section 33-C(2) can be applied when a workman has a right to and is capable of receiving money or benefits from his employer that can be computed by the court.
B Once an employer has admitted a workman's right to a benefit, Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act applies.
C. The petitioner in this case did not argue before the Labour Court that Section 33-C(2) was not relevant or that the claim of the second respondent should fall under Section 33-C(1) instead of Section 33-C(2).
Therefore, the petitioner cannot raise this issue for the first time in a writ petition, and a small amount of money that a workman is entitled to cannot be denied to him based on a technical argument that was not previously raised in court.
