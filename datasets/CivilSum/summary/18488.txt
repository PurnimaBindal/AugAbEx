Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Education - Affiliation of schools - Study centres - Additional facility of classes provided in the affiliated schools - Making a general allegation that schools will not provide adequate facilities during additional classes would be arbitrary - Competent authorities may inspect and identify deficient facilities in individual cases - Haryana Govt. orders to not allow National Open Schools to use school buildings with Govt. permission invalidated - Additional education facilities must be acknowledged and appreciated.
[Paras 1 & 7]
