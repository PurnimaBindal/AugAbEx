The appointment of a whole time Government servant on the post of Lambardar does not violate the provisions of Rule 310 in the Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I, Part I, Article 311 of the Constitution of India will be attracted if dismissed or removed - The post of a Lambardar not only attracts the provisions of Article 311 but also qualifies as a civil post - A Government servant's appointment as Lambardar will not interfere with his government duties and should not be ignored only because he is a Government servant.
[Paras 2 and 3] B.
Rule 14 of the Punjab Land Revenue Rules, 1909, states that the appointment of a whole time Government servant as Lambardar will not interfere with their duties as a Government servant - Being a Government Servant is not a disqualification for the appointment of Lambardar.
