Constitution of India, Articles 16, 14 and 141 - Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume I, Part I, Rule 8126 - Principles of promissory estoppel - Reservation for in-service candidates to pursue Diploma and/or Post-Graduate Course in Medicine and surgery - The petitioner was granted one year study leave in a Diploma course under rule 8126 of CSR Vol.
I, Part I, and later permitted to join a PG course at a State Medical College - The petitioner executed a bond to serve the Punjab Govt. for 5 years or pay a penalty - The State cannot retract its permission to the disadvantage of the applicant, as the bond even specified the period for which the petitioner was given permission to join the course at State expense - The petitioner is entitled to get pay and allowances/stipend for the full duration of the course.
