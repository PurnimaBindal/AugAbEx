GOM 304 dt. 20.11.1979 issued by the Govt. of Andhra Pradesh contemplates option only from Divisional Accountants in Accountant General's Office on the date of take over in the State Service not from the cadre of SAS - The State Govt. has the power to create a separate cadre in the State by absorbing the Divisional Accountants working on deputation in the State under the Control of the Accountant General - The Court cannot direct the State Govt. to have a different policy and absorb SAS Accountants in its newly constituted service - Any action cannot be declared invalid on the ground of discrimination - If the language of the policy is plain, no question of any strict or liberal construction would arise.
