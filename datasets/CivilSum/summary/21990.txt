Uttar Pradesh Industrial Peace (Timely Payment of Wages) Act, 1978, Section 3(1) - The Act applies in cases where there are no other Acts that provide regular remedies for the recovery of wages - The inquiry is summary in nature and is intended to provide immediate redress for undisputed due payments of wages to prevent industrial unrest, without affecting other remedies - The ALC is not authorized to decide questions that are in dispute regarding fact or law.
[Paras 7 and 9] B Uttar Pradesh Industrial Peace (Timely Payment of Wages) Act, 1978, Section 3(1) - The Act applies irrespective of the limit or amount of the wage bill that exceeds Rs. 50,000/- - The critical date for determining jurisdiction pertains to the date on which the claim is lodged - Any payment made thereafter that reduces the amount owed to less than Rs. 50,000/- will not impact the authority's jurisdiction.
