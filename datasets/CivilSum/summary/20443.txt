Constitution of India Articles 14, 16 and 47 - Reservation - Admissions - Admission to Medical College - Graduates of local colleges are given a weightage of 10% while local candidates with graduation from Colleges outside the state get 5% weightage - Extra marks intended for better medical facilities and tackling the shortage of doctors - The weightage given to graduates of local colleges and local candidates is not discriminatory or arbitrary.
