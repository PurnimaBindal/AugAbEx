Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Himachal Pradesh (Recruitment, Conditions of Service and Conduct) Rules 1992, Rules 10 and 4 - Promotion - Seniority - The petitioner is seeking seniority over the respondent, claiming that the respondent was not eligible for promotion.
The petitioner's name was the only one considered with the respondents and they were shown as senior.
The respondents argue that private respondents were senior to the petitioner in their cadre, but it was decided that since the petitioner was the only eligible person and was promoted according to the rules, the petitioner will be shown senior to the respondents.
Therefore, the petition is allowed accordingly.
