Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delhi University Statutes, Statute 28A(1)(h) - Pension - Qualifying service - The petitioner, who was appointed to a post with specific qualifications and age requirements, was granted the benefit of 5 years of added service when calculating pension.
The Executive Committee's resolution allowing for the choice of only one of two benefits is in violation of statutes.
The respondents have been instructed to calculate the petitioner's pension by adding 5 years of service to the service already rendered.
