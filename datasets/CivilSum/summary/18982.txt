According to the AP State Civil Supplies Corporation Ltd Employees Recruitment and Service Regulations, Regulation 34, the Managing Director (MD) is the disciplinary authority and has the power to punish employees for misconduct.
While no specific authority is prescribed for passing transfer orders, it is presumed that the MD has the power to transfer as it is incidental to their position of disciplinary authority.
Therefore, the transfer order challenged by the petitioner (a Senior Officer Grade II) on the grounds of jurisdiction should be deemed valid.
[Paras 1 and 2]
