Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Reimbursement of medical expenses - Employees cannot claim reimbursement for treatment from private hospitals not recognized by the government - There are sufficient listed/government hospitals available for Coronary Bypass Surgery which are not inferior to private ones - Rejection of claim for reimbursement of private treatment is not unjustified - Argument that rejection is a denial of fundamental right to life and health under Article 21 is untenable.
