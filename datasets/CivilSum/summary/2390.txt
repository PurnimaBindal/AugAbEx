Constitution of India, Article 226 - Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Section 17 - Alternative remedy of appeal - A writ petition is not maintainable if the statute provides a remedy of appeal against the order, as it is deemed to be an effective alternative remedy to the writ remedy provided under Art. 226 of the Constitution - A writ petition cannot adjudicate on issues of fact and evidence that require a detailed investigation which can be examined in an appeal - The petition was dismissed for not being maintainable.
