Delhi Right to Information Act, 2001, Section 7 - Recruitment - Age Relaxation - Petitioner cannot claim age relaxation benefit based on previous employment with IVRI as they are not considered government servants.
While IVRI falls under "State" under Article 12 of the Constitution and writ jurisdiction under Article 226 can be exercised, the petitioner was not selected or appointed by the Central Government.
[Paras 15 and 19]

Government Servant - When determining if a person works for or under the Union or State, the criteria to be applied is the existence of a jural relationship and the ability of the employer to exercise control over the employee's discipline and work.
