Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Interview - Appointment - Petitioner interviewed for the post of Riding Inspector and now seeking appointment for the same - It was found that none of the 21 candidates who appeared before the Selection Committee were deemed suitable for the position - The petition was filed in 1993, despite the interview taking place in 1988 - As a result, the petitioner was found guilty of delay and laches - In addition, the Departmental Selection Committee determined that the petitioner was not eligible for the position - Therefore, the petitioner cannot assert the right to appointment - The writ petition was dismissed as without merit.
