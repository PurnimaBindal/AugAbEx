Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1972, Section 28, Schedule IV, Item 6 - A temporary employee claims permanency based on a 1988 settlement and Model Standings Order - The petitioner's initial appointment was made in 1975 but he did not receive employment for 10 years from 1975 to 1985 - Service rendered in 1975 cannot be considered for implementation of settlement - The Industrial Court's decision that there was no breach of settlement is valid - The case has been sent back for further consideration of whether the petitioner is eligible for permanency under the Model Standing Order.
