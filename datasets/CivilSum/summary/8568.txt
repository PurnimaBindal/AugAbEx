Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Temporary appointment - Regularisation - Termination - The petitioner was appointed as Gram Sewak on a purely temporary basis for a span of 29 days or until regular candidates were available.
The petitioner continued working regularly on the post from 18.1.1985 to 2241988 purely on a temporary basis, with multiple extensions granted.
Despite working for almost a decade, the petitioner has no right to hold the post as a temporary employee.
However, the respondents may consider his candidature for the same or a similar post when he meets all other eligibility criteria and may even grant him a relaxation of age, if required.
The order of termination of the petitioner, deemed to be meritless, has led to the dismissal of the petition.
[Paras 1, 6 and 19]
