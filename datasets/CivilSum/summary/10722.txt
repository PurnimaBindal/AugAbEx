Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Bigamy - Compulsory retirement - The petitioner was accused of performing a second marriage without prior permission or intimation to concerned authorities - Although being a Muslim, he could contract more than one marriage - The punishment of compulsory retirement was deemed disproportionate and set aside - Stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect was deemed sufficient.
