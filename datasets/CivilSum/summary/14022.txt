Central Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, Rule 5 - Termination of Services - Unauthorised Absence - Although the petitioner was appointed as a temporary labor, his absence from duty for three continuous months during a period when the unit was operationally committed in counter insurgency affected the unit's functioning.
Therefore, the termination of the petitioner on the grounds of unauthorized absence could not be considered harsh or excessive.
[Para 5]

Termination of Services - Probationary Period - The order of appointment is silent about the maximum period beyond which the probationary period cannot be extended.
Therefore, even if the petitioner successfully completed the two-year probationary period, his probationary period has to be treated as extended, as permanent status can only be acquired through a specific order of confirmation.
[Para 4]
