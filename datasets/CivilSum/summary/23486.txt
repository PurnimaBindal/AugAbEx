Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab State Assistant Grade Examination Rules, 1984, Rules 4 and 12 - Promotion/Seniority/Departmental Examination - Rule 12 of 1984 Rules permits provisional promotion from clerk to Assistant without qualifying the Assistant Grade Examination - However, promotion is subject to passing the exam within three years - Failure to pass the exam within the specified period will result in consequences - Promotees who pass the exam within the period prescribed are treated at par with those who were promoted after passing the exam - Incorrectly fixed seniority and reversions made have to be corrected accordingly.
