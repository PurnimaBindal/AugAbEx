Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Petitioner was placed on a waiting list after a competitive examination - A significant number of selected candidates did not join and there were many vacancies during the validity of the panel - The Public Service Commission did not recommend any additional suitable candidates during the currency of the selection and waiting list - Authorities cannot deprive the petitioner of appointment due to their inaction or delay.
