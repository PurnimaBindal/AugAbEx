Punjab Civil Service (Pre-mature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3(i)(a) and 5 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 99 - Premature retirement - The respondent was unable to perform their duties due to medical disabilities.
However, the claim that the order was punitive due to their absence from duty was dismissed.
The respondent argued that they should have been retired due to medical disability under Rule 99 of the 1934 Rules.
However, it was found that the 1975 Rules have an overriding effect, and Rule 99 of the 1934 is not inconsistent with the Premature Retirement Rules 1975.
The order was deemed to be passed in the public interest and therefore valid.
