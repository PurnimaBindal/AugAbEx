Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion - The High Court could not hold that the respondent was promoted with consequential benefits despite the DPC finding them unfit for promotion.
The High Court's decision was illegal and should have remitted the matter to the Govt. for a fresh DPC in the future.
The previous order from the High Court was set aside, and the DPC is the only competent authority to decide on merits.
The Govt. was directed to constitute a DPC to consider the respondent's case according to the rules and then give appropriate direction to the Govt. accordingly.
