Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Promotion - Average ACRs - A benchmark and grading based on ACRs are required for promotion- failing to obtain the necessary number of good reports within a specific time disqualifies the employee from claiming promotion- Un-communicated average reports do not qualify as good reports unless specifically mandated by instructions or rules- If reports that negatively impact promotion must be communicated, failure to do so will not disadvantage the employee.
