The Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of Deceased Govt. Employees Rules, 2003, Rules 3(e)(ii) and 8(g) along with articles 14 and 21 of the Constitution of India dictate compassionate appointment age limit.
When these specific rules are applicable, the general age limit rule for entry into service does not apply, but the special rule for compassionate appointment does.

The Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of Deceased Govt. Employees Rules, 2003, Rules 3(e)(ii) and 8(g) along with articles 14 and 21 of the Constitution of India deem financial assistance as the objective of compassionate appointment.
The appointments are not intended to provide a post to the family member but to offer financial assistance in a crisis situation when the family is unable to provide for themselves.
If the family is already receiving family pension, then they cannot claim the appointment of a family member as their right under the rules.
