Instructions in LIC of India (Agents) Regulations, 1972, Regulation 4 that prevent the appointment of LIC agents if their spouses are employed in government or public sector undertakings are not a violation of the regulation.
Instead, they serve as an addition to the rule to ensure that the agencies are given to those who truly need them, and not to those who are simply greedy.
It is possible that someone in a government position may use their power to pressure others into purchasing insurance policies through a particular agent, making the Corporation's decision to implement such instructions neither arbitrary nor unfair.
