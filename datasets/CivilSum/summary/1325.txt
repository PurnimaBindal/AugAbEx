Constitution of India, Article 226 - Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Section 103 - Concealment of facts - Grant of relief in Writ jurisdiction is discretionary and equitable - A petitioner who conceals material facts or mis-states them in their petition is not entitled to relief - A petitioner who was never appointed to the post they seek to continue on, but was asked to work there, and who was found ineligible even on court orders, is not entitled to relief - Filing another writ petition without disclosing the true facts and earlier orders of the court declining the same relief is not permissible - The petition is liable to be dismissed with costs.
[Paras 10 and 11]
