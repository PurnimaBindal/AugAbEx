The University Grants Commission Regulations of 1982 and Constitution of India, Article 14 require that an applicant for the position of Professor in a research academy have "about ten years experience of teaching and/or research," with marks granted for experience.
The appellant in this case had a total of 9 years and 1 month of teaching and research experience, and should not be deemed ineligible for not having the required experience.
The Chancellor must follow the Rules and law and cannot act illegally, irrationally, or arbitrarily, but had committed a grave irregularity and illegality in ignoring the appellant's research experience and only granting zero marks for their experience.
