Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Ad hoc appointment - Doubtful integrity - Termination - Industrial dispute - Reference - At the time of termination, retrenchment compensation was offered - Labour Court held that Section 25-F of the Act was complied with - As per its award against the worker and in favour of management, the reference was decided by the Labour Court - Award challenged under Article 226 - Held, based on the evidence presented by the parties, Labour Court's findings are sound - Therefore, the termination order was legal and justified - Petition dismissed - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(1)(c) and 25-F [Paras 1 and 2]
