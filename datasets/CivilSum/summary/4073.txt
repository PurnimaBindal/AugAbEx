Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Police Departmental Enquiries Punishment and Appeal Rules, 1958 (Kerala), Rule 17(i)(b) and 17(ii) - Manual of Disciplinary Proceedings, Rule 64(2) - The conviction of the petitioner in a criminal case does not merit the extreme punishment of dismissal, removal, and compulsory retirement from service - Petitioner must receive less severe punishment due to lacks of nexus in charge and conduct with his official duties - The removal of service that was enforced on petitioner is unwarranted, stating the AIR 1985 SC 772 verdict.
