The instructions of the Government specify that reserved posts must be filled from the relevant category and ad hoc arrangements should continue until regular appointees are recommended.
The appellant was appointed on an ad hoc basis as an ex-serviceman on a post reserved for ex-servicemen.
However, the appellant's services were terminated to make way for the ward of a deceased Government servant, which violated the Government's policy and rendered the termination illegal.
[Para 6]
The petitioner's case is covered by the precedent established in Piara Singh's case 1988(4) SLR 739, which pertains to regularisation of ad hoc appointments after 3-1/4 years of service.
