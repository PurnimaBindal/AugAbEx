Regarding Admissions to Post-Graduate Medical Courses, those who migrated to other states and were unable to advance their careers in their home state due to either reservation or institutional preference will be admitted in the 15% All India Quota.
The Supreme Court's decision in Pradeep Jain's case did not introduce any new principle, but simply extracted principles from previous rulings and balanced the interests of students selected under this quota who migrated to other states.
The judgment made in Dr. Parag Gupta's case only applies to these students, not all students in the country.
