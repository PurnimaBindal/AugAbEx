Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Termination of Service - Payment of subsistence allowance - Petitioner contested the termination of his services and non-payment of subsistence allowance for the suspension period as per the Management's orders - Respondents clarified that the DPI didn't approve the termination proposal yet, and no further action was taken - Hence, the petitioner's request for quashing the termination of services order cannot be granted - Respondents directed to release the subsistence allowance to the petitioner as per the rules within a month.
