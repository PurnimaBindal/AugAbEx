Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Termination - A worker was accused of driving away a parked bus without consent, leading to some financial loss, and was subsequently terminated.
- An Industrial Dispute was raised and referred to the Labour Court, which ruled that the worker's termination was unjustified, directing their reinstatement with continuity of service but without back wages.
- In a writ petition challenging this decision, it was held that the Labour Court acted within its jurisdiction by denying back wages, as it granted all other reliefs to the worker under Section 11-A of the Act.
- The case was thus dismissed.
- Constitution of India, Articles 226/227.
