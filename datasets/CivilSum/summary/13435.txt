Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Service benefit - Ministerial staff of State Police personnel contended that police employees in Executive Cadre were receiving special pay, special work allowance, fixed house rent, motorcycle subsidy, etc. - They argued that this was discriminatory and they should be treated on par - A Mandamus was issued, commanding the respondent to treat the petitioners equally with their executive counterparts in the police force in context of service benefits.
