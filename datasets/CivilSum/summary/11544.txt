Constitution of India, Article 226 - Increment - Advance increment - Benefit recovery of excess advance annual increment given by mistake - It was a case of redesignation, not promotion - Order of promotion reversed after following principles of natural justice - But in light of the Supreme Court Judgment in Sahib Ram v. State of Haryana, 1995 (1) SCT 668 (SC), financial benefits awarded due to a departmental error cannot be recovered.
