The Jammu and Kashmir Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 24(1)(b) - Seniority - Dispute - Appellants and respondents were appointed as lecturers on a regular temporary basis, and the rule states that seniority is to be determined based on the merit position.
Appellants joined immediately while respondents joined after the prescribed period; therefore, Rule 24(1)(b) applies to respondents, and they cannot claim seniority as per merit.
Writ petitions by respondents were dismissed as withdrawn and rendered infructuous.
Appeals were allowed.
