Punjab Panchayat Secretaries (Recruitment and Conditions of Services) Rules, 1979, Rule 5 - Recruitment - Age - Cut off date - Post of Panchayat Secretaries - A government advertisement invited male candidates for the post of Panchayat Secretaries with an eligibility cut off date.
A year later, a similar advertisement was issued for female candidates with the same cut off date.
An Appellant challenged the rule regarding cut off date and age for appointment, claiming it lacked a rational basis.
The High Court dismissed the Petition, ruling that the rule only specified eligibility based on age for appointment and did not prohibit the setting of a cut off date.
Accordingly, the appeal was dismissed.
