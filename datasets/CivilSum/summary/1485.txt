The petitioner claimed that he would be regularized under the Pre-paid Taxi/TSR Scheme of the Delhi Traffic Police with all consequential service benefits w.e.f.
his date of initial appointment and that the doctrine of equal pay for equal work be implemented at par with regular employees of the department, though appointments under the Scheme were made without following the regular process of recruitment prescribed for recruitment.
The court determined that direct absorption and regularisation in service cannot be ordered in such cases.
It stated that the experience of such an employee must be taken into consideration when considered during regular recruitment.
Grant of relief of equal pay for equal work is also not always possible as it negates the right under Article 14 whereby the regular employees may in some cases be performing longer and more onerous duties with greater responsibilities as compared to casual workers.
The doctrine followed in Daily Rated Casual Labour case was not followed in subsequent decisions of the Supreme Court and that prayer could not be granted.
