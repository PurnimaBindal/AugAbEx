According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 40 of the Beedi and Cigar Workers (Conditions of Employment) Act, 1966, and Rule 26 of the West Bengal Beedi and Cigar Workers (Conditions of Employment) Rules, 1968, disputes between workers and contractors in a Beedi and Cigar establishment are to be resolved under the provisions of the 1966 Act, which has overriding effect.
Therefore, any disputes arising from the retrenchment of a contractor in such an establishment under the Industrial Disputes Act are not maintainable.
[Paras 45, 46, 49 and 50]
