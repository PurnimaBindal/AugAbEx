According to the Delhi School Education Act and Article 226 of the Constitution of India, regular salary and retiral benefits can be claimed by employees.
A petitioner who worked as a Class IV employee in a school had her service terminated in 1996.
While she was paid gratuity and compensation, the absence of an appointment letter and her name not appearing in returns or staff lists negates her plea.
Her photograph may have appeared in a school magazine, but this does not assert a claim for regular appointment with the school.
The petition is dismissed.
