Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Voluntary retirement - Petitioner an employee of a Government of India Undertaking - Requested voluntary retirement under Voluntary Retirement Scheme with a conditional offer for retirement on 30.6.1992 - Personal Officer informed that his request could not be accepted and he would be relieved from his duties on 3151992 instead - Petitioner withdrew his offer but was still relieved on 3151992 - Court held the company's action to be illegal and arbitrarily, as petitioner should have either been retired in accordance with his conditional offer or his application as a whole- Petitioner had the right to withdraw his retirement request before 30.6.1992.
