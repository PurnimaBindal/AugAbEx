Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rules 2 and 4 - Punjab Recruitment of Ex-Servicemen Rules, 1982, Rule 9(3) - Military service - Second emergency - The Government letter dated 2361998 declaring the military service rendered during the second emergency under Rule 2 of 1965 Rules is held not to be within its jurisdiction - Rule 9(3) of 1982 Rules only protects rights that have accrued prior to the repeal of 1965 Rules - Service rendered during the second emergency was not declared as "Military Service" under Rule 2 of 1965 Rules until the same were repealed in 1982 - Therefore, the service rendered during the 2nd Emergency is not deemed as "Military Service" for the purpose of Rule 4 of 1965 Rules.
