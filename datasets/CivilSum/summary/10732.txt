Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Promotion - Reservation in promotions - The entitlement in reservation quota grants only consideration and not automatic promotion or seniority.
If found ineligible or unfit, one cannot claim seniority from the date of becoming entitled to promotion.
[Paras 2 and 3]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Delay and laches - Promotion - Writ petitions challenging promotions with unexplained inordinate delay beyond three years may be subject to dismissal.
