Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - Arrears of salary - 'No work no pay' rule - In certain circumstances, if an incumbent wrongfully denied promotion and suffers due to administrative reasons not under their control, courts may order arrears of salary to be paid upon promotion.
