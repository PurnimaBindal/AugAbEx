Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sch I Item 6, Section 6E - Scheme for Amalgamation - Benefit of - Petitioners chosen before the amalgamation but not provided with appointment letters - Attempt to obtain benefit of award directing absorption of those who received appointment letters - Determined that the petitioners cannot be considered employees of the former Bank solely by their inclusion in the select panel - Consequently, they cannot request the benefit of the scheme or award.
