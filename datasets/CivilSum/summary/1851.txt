Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Volume-II, Rule 16.2 - Dismissal from service on charge of absence from police duty - Habitual absenteeism not desirable in police service - Punishment of dismissal from service is reasonable.
[Paras 17, 18 and 19]

Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Volume-II, Rule 16.2 - Dismissal from service - Consideration for length of service and benefits - If the punishing authority considers all aspects including the length of service and dismissing authority has passed speaking order, then delinquent cannot allege non-consideration of service length.
[Paras 17, 21]

Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Volume-II, Rule 16.2 - Dismissal from service - Conclusions drawn based on past record of habitual absentism - Delinquent cannot raise the issue of consideration of the past record for the first time in writ petition.
[Para 20]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Volume II, Rule 16.2 - Enquiry report - Challenge to the enquiry report not possible first time in writ petition if not made before the departmental authorities in appeal or revision.
