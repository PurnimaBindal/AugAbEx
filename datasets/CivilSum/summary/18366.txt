Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Rajasthan Compassionate Appointment of Dependants of Deceased Government Servants Rules (1996), Rule 5 - Compassionate appointment of elder son after death of father - In case of death of elder son within 6 months of appointment, the family reaches the same stage where her husband had died, and the younger son may be considered for compassionate appointment if eligible.
[Paras 3 and 4]
