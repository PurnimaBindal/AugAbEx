Response: The petitioner claimed to be an employee of a work charge establishment, but failed to prove that his engagement was made upon an actual execution of a specific work or a sub-work of a specific project.
Therefore, his claim as a work charge employee was rejected and the protection provided under the Rules was not available to him.
On the other hand, the court directed that all employees who have completed three years of services as daily wagers be regularised in the absence of policy or guidelines, but once a policy has been formulated and necessary government orders issued in that behalf, daily wagers engaged by different departments will have to be governed by that policy.
[Paras 3, 6, 7, 8]
