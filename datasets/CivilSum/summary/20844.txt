Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - If an appointee was selected in open selection, completed probation period successfully, promoted, and worked continuously and satisfactorily since years together, their appointment cannot be declared improper at the instance of competing interests at a belated stage - The appointment cannot be considered a back door entry - The employee cannot be denied seniority based on such grounds.
[Paras 8, 9 and 11]

Greater Cochin Development Authority (Establishment) Bylaws, 1977, Rule 27 - Seniority - Seniority of employees has to be determined by the date of the order of first appointment in service unless he has been reduced to lower rank as a punishment or a part of his service forfeited - The question of whether initial appointment was regular or not cannot be considered when fixing seniority under the Rule since the language of the Rule is clear and unambiguous.
[Para 11]
