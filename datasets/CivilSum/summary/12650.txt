The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226, and the Education Code, Article 81(d)(3), dictate the consequences for unauthorized absence.
In this case, the petitioner was absent for more than 4 years without justification or a medical certificate proving their incapacity to work.
As a result, the petitioner did not receive any salary, and no action was taken to remedy that fact.
Consequently, the rule of automatic loss of lien for unauthorized absence was upheld.
