J & K Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeals) Rules, 1956, Rule 22(1)(a) - Seniority - Probation Period - Departmental Examination - Petitioners have been appointed as Naib Tehsildars subject to their passing departmental examination within two years - Seniority to be based on continuous length of service and the date when the petitioners were appointed as they passed the departmental examination within the probation period - Seniority list challenged setting aside the fixation of seniority - Petition allowed.
[Paras 9, 19, 22, 27 and 28] B. J & K Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeals) Rules, 1956, Rules 13, 22(1)(a) - Appointment - Probation period - Departmental examination - Seniority List - If petitioners have appeared in the examination within the prescribed period of probation and passed the examination, even if the result was declared after the probation period, they are entitled to a higher position in the seniority list - Petition allowed.
[Paras 11 and 12] C. J & K Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeals) Rules, 1950, Rules 23, and 24(b) - Direct appointment - Departmental examination - Probation - Seniority - Petitioners having passed the departmental examination within the probation period are entitled to be confirmed - Seniority assigned in violation of the terms of the order of appointment - Petitioners to be assigned seniority as per their rating in the order of appointment and promoted retrospectively from the date any of their juniors was promoted on a notional basis till they are actually promoted on stop-gap basis to entitle them seniority in the cadre of Tehsildar and also retiral benefits - Petition allowed.
