The Navy Act, 1957, Sections 3, 9, 10, 14, 16 and 17 - Navy (Pension) Regulations, 1964, Regulation 79 - Pensionary benefits - The appellant qualifies for pensionary benefits for his service under the Navy because all service from the date of enrolment or advancement to the rank of ordinary seaman or equivalent to the date of discharge qualifies for pension or gratuity, and he has served for over 15 years, including his training period as an Artificer Apprentice.
