According to Articles 136 and 141 of the Constitution of India, dismissing a special leave petition does not qualify as a declaration of law under Article 141.
Additionally, a special leave petition under Article 136 is voluntary for the court.
The dismissal of an appeal filed under Article 136 does not merge the decision of the High Court with that of the Supreme Court.

It cannot be used to make ineligible candidates eligible for interviews, as it would result in unequal treatment.
Previous rulings in 1993(3) RSJ 1 and 1996(3) PLR 430 have been overturned.
