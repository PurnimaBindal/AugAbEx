According to Section 115(7) of the States Re-Organisation Act, 1956, retirement age is a condition of service that requires approval from the central government.
Those who were protected under this section and entitled to retire at age 60 will retire at age 60.
Additionally, service rules of successor states will not automatically change the service conditions of protected employees without approval from the central government under the Act.
The Amar Nath Bansal case (AIR 1977 SC 718) does not apply as there was no covenant or contract to protect the service conditions of Jind State employees.
