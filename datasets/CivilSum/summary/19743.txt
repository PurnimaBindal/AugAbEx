Punjab Civil Services (Punishment & Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 7 - Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules 1975, Rule 3 - Premature retirement/Compulsory retirement - No adverse remarks were given to the petitioner, and his performance was consistently assessed as good and very good.
Unless charges are proven, the issuance of a charge sheet is not sufficient grounds for the petitioner to be deemed as a dead wood requiring retirement.
The order for premature retirement cannot be granted if the government wishes to punish the official.
An inquiry must take place to establish charges.
