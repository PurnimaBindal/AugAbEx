Punjab Civil Secretariat (State Services Class III) Rules, 1952, Rule 5(a) allows for ad hoc appointments to be granted and provides for regularisation of services.
Any person appointed on an ad hoc basis, including the petitioners involved in this case, does not have any right to claim regularisation in service under any law.
They must wait to be selected through the Board for regular appointment.
The grant of ad hoc appointments was a measure of concession shown to such workers by the government.
The petitioners, in particular, gained the right to regularisation through a notification dating back to 311983.
However, any prior benefits they may claim would only start from that date and not from an anterior date.
Sohan Lal v. State of Haryana, 1992(4) SLR 701.
[Para 6]

Respondents in this case were appointed on a regular basis prior to the regularisation of services of the petitioners who were initially appointed on an ad hoc basis.
For this reason and given their earlier appointment date, respondents would rank senior to the petitioners.
Class IV employees, after promotion to a regular post, are entitled to regularisation from the date the regular post becomes vacant.
This, however, cannot be compared to the petitioners who are distinct from promotees from Class IV.
Ad hoc service cannot be counted as part of seniority.
Sohan Lal v. State of Haryana, 1992(4) SLR 701.
