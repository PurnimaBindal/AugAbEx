Constitution of India, Articles 12, 16 - A.P.
(Regulation of Appointments to Public Services and Rationalization of Staff Pattern and Pay Structure) Act, 1994 (Act No 2 of 1994) as amended by Act No 3 of 1998 - Regularisation - Petitioners petition for regularization benefit based on an order that directed the regularization of their juniors who worked in the corporation for a long time - Held, Appellant corporation is considered a 'State' within the meaning of Article 12 of the Constitution, which means any employment given by the 'State' would be a public employment - Regularization can only be directed if the Recruitment Rules of the corporation are complied with - No employee has an absolute right to regularization unless he fulfills the requirements for the post and a clear vacancy exists - Directions were given to consider the case of the Petitioners provided there is a clear-cut vacancy.
[Paras 10, 11, 14 and 15]
