Pay Scale - Group-C and Group-D posts carry different maximum pay scales - Classification of posts into groups does not change pay scales - Pay scales are usually determined by the government or a specialized body like a Pay Commission - Classifying posts and prescribing pay scales are two separate functions, and changing the former does not automatically change the latter - The tribunal's decision to fix the respondent's pay in category D rather than C was unlawful, as the reasons given for the decision were unclear.

Tribunal should not interfere with prescribed pay scales unless there is evidence of discrimination - The government usually determines pay scales based on the recommendations of an expert body - The tribunal's decision to change the respondent's pay scale without clear evidence of discrimination was inappropriate.
