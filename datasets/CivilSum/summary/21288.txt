Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Education/Admissions -Rules for the selection to MBBS course and BDS course in Maharashtra for the year of 1995-96 - Reservation for Scheduled Castes - If a student in the reserved category is eligible to be admitted on the basis of merit in the general category, they cannot be considered for a reserved seat - Only candidates in the reserved category who were unable to secure admission through general merit can be given reserved seats - All the reserved category seats should be given only to such candidates who could have been admitted based on general merit - The petitioner with higher merit than the last candidate admitted to general category seats was not given admission, thereby violating Article 16(4) of the Constitution - The authority has been instructed to provide admission to the petitioner against any vacant seat or to create one for him in any college.
