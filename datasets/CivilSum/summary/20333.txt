Railway Establishment Manual, Para 303-A and 303-B - Inter se seniority - Post of Senior Publicity Inspector - The appellant and respondent No. 4 were appointed with the condition that their continuance in the post is dependent on successfully passing the training as per principles.
Upon completing training, inter se seniority would be determined by Para 303-A rather than Para 303-B The Railway authorities did not specify whether inter se seniority given after the first part examination was altered after the second part examination.
The matter was referred back to the Railway Authorities to re-determine the inter se seniority between the appellant and respondent No. 4.
