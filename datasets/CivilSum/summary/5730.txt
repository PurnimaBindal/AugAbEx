Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Termination - Charge of theft - The Labour Court affirmed the termination of a workman after finding him guilty of theft - However, the date of dismissal cannot retroactively relate back to the date of the management's illegal order - The workman is entitled to his salary from 2731970 to the date of the impugned award - The Labour Court was unjustified in granting Rs. 60,000 on humanitarian grounds, as such grants are not permitted under law.
