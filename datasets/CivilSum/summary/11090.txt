Industrial Disputes Act, Section 33-C(2) - Pay scale - The pay scale of the Driver of Road Roller was given as the pay scale of a driver until the post of Road Roller driver was introduced.
Upon the introduction of the position, a lower pay scale was prescribed for it.
The incumbent was then asked to pay back the extra salary he received earlier in a higher pay scale, but the Labour Court found this illegal and granted the workman's claim.
The workman had an existing right to be granted under Section 33-C(2).
The order of the Labour Court is upheld.
