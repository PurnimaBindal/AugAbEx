According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India regarding reservation and regularization, the Respondent was initially appointed as a Section Writer on a daily wage basis in 1986.
He has since remained in service for over seven years and possesses the necessary qualifications for the position.
However, he has not been regularized nor paid a salary that is commensurate with that of his regular colleagues doing similar tasks.
According to the State of Haryana v. Piara Singh, 1992(3) SCT 201 (SC) : AIR 1992 SC 2130 decision of the Supreme Court, the absorption of a casual or temporary employee can be determined by their qualifications and eligibility under the rules, provided it does not clash with the reservation policy of the corporation.
On 2011993, a writ petition was filed, and the corporation was directed to regularize the services of the Respondent from 1.2.93 and pay him a salary equal to that of a regular Section Writer/Junior Assistant.
