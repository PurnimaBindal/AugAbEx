Merit should not be ignored for seniority when making promotions based on merit-cum-seniority.
The DPC must consider various attributes and parameters beyond just grading in the ACRs, or else the seniority becomes the only criterion.
Ignoring these considerations will result in a non-application of mind and the entire decision-making process being vitiated.
The selection is set aside and the matter remitted to UPSC.
