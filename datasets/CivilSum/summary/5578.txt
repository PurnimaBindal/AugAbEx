As per the Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226, Seniority holds importance.
In a case related to the final seniority list for Deputy Engineers (Mechanical), the recruitment rules stated that a diploma holder must have at least eight years of experience to get promoted to the post of Deputy Engineer.
However, on the date of promotion, the petitioner was ineligible.
Thus, seniority was determined from the date when the petitioner became eligible.
The petitioner filed a petition after ten years, which made him liable to be dismissed due to laches.
[Paras 11 and 12]
