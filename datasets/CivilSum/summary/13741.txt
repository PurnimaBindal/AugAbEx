A lecturer at a Deemed University was denied benefits under the Career Advancement Scheme by the Technical Education Department of Haryana because their previous service at another institution was not being counted.
However, during the pendency of a writ, the benefits were granted starting from December 4, 2007.
The court held that according to a letter by UGC dated November 27, 1990 and Clause 9 of the scheme, the petitioner's previous service as a lecturer should be counted towards grant of senior scale.
Therefore, the petitioner will be entitled to benefits from the date of eligibility when their past service is counted.
