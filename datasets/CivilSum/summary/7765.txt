Under Section 28 of the Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Union and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1972, a complaint can be maintained if the order passed was mala fide or in violation of transfer guidelines.
[Para 7] B.
In accordance with Section 30(1) of the same Act, if a transfer order is malicious or mala fide, the Industrial Court has the power to grant interim relief if unfair labour practices can be proven.
