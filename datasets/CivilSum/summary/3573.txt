Writ Petition No. 15529 of 1994 and Writ Petition No. 8537 of 1994 both refer to Constitution of India, Article 226 and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) regarding termination of services.
The Labour Court has found the termination to be illegal and ordered reinstatement, with full back wages being the norm and denial of back wages being the exception.
No reasons were given for the denial of back wages, and the award for denial of back wages was set aside.
