The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 state that in cases of dismissal, the enquiry report must be furnished to the delinquent, with the opportunity to be heard, and disagreement by the disciplinary authority over the punishment itself amounts to prejudice.
Hence, the order cannot be sustained without the opportunity of hearing being given.
[Paras 5 and 8]
According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226, dismissal cannot be sustained if the enquiry report is not furnished to the delinquent.
