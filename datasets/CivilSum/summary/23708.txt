The Punjab Govt. amended Rule 16 of the Punjab Superior Judicial Service Rules, 1963, concerning pension and retiral benefits.
The purpose of the amendment was to make the scheme more attractive to current and potential employees to prevent them from leaving or to attract new employees.
This amended rule applies only to those who retire after 22290.
The principle established in DS Nakara's case, which prohibits the computation of pension using different formulae that provide unequal treatment, does not apply in this situation.
Schemes for retirees cannot always be extended to everyone due to financial constraints, and cut-off dates in such schemes are not unconstitutional.
