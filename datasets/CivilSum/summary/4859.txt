RSRTC Karamchari Sewa Niyam, 1965, Rule 54(2) - Suspension period - Emoluments except subsistence allowance not claimable - Reasonable - Petitioner involved in criminal case filed under Prevention of Corruption Act - Acquitted on account of benefit of doubt - Charge sheet against him withdrawn - Suspension was due to his involvement in criminal case - Rule 54 permits discretion to authority for pay and allowance during suspension period - Refusal to grant benefit to petitioner in light of his involvement in trap case was valid and relevant - Appeal rejected.
