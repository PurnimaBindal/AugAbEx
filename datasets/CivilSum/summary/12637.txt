Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equivalence of posts and pay scales - Eligibility for - All India, Council of Technical Education Rules - Professors (MBA) must meet eligibility qualifications - Technical institution teacher hierarchy differs from Humanities, Social Sciences, Commerce, Education, Physical Education, Foreign Languages, and Law - Assistant Professor in technical institutions and other institutions have differing roles - Eligiibility for Assistant Professor differs between UGC norms and Technical Education Institutions - Appointee with UGC norms does not fulfill AICTE's Assistant Professor experience requirement - Equivalence should be assessed by appointment mode, eligibility, pay, duties etc - Assistant Professor in Technical Institutions is higher than Assistant Professor governed by UGC norms - AICTE norms are binding to Technical Institutions - A petitioner appointed with UGC norms cannot claim promotion to Professor Technical Education or its pay scale.
