Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, Sections 7 to 13 - Criminal and disciplinary proceedings - ASI of Police charged with gravest misconduct and corruption - Departmental proceedings not to be stayed despite criminal charge not yet being framed - Different standard and mode of proof required in criminal proceedings - No prejudice shown in allowing departmental proceedings to continue and be finalized - Delay in such serious allegations cannot be permitted.
