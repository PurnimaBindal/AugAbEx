The Appellant filed a suit for damages against the Respondents, who were police officers, after the acquittal that was more than a year ago without prior notice.
The suit will be governed by Section 140 of the Delhi Police Act instead of Article 74 of the Limitation Act.
Due to the reasonable nexus with the duties attached to the office, Section 140(1) of the Act applies and the suit filed over three months after the expiry was barred by limitation.
The dismissal of the appeal was based on Para 6, 8, and 19 of the Delhi Police Act, 1978.
