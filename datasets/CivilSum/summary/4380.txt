The Constitution of India, including Articles 14, 16, and 226, concerns appointment for a one-year term, and a regular appointment may be given to the same person.
In this case, the appointment of the individual was challenged because they were not called again for an interview by the Selection Committee for regular appointment.
However, the respondent claimed that the appointment was proper and that the appointee was properly invited and selected by the selection committee.
The facts alleged in the petition were denied and the petition was ultimately dismissed.
The appointment was deemed proper and it was emphasized that the High Court cannot act as a court of appeal over the decision of the selection committee in scrutinizing the merits of the candidates.
