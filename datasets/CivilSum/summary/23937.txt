According to Sections 2(i) & 32 of the Pharmacy Act, 1948, the Orissa Govt. advertised for unemployed registered pharmacists (Gents & Ladies) or persons having medical shops, who have engaged registered pharmacist/person undertaking to engage registered pharmacist to apply for running 24 hours medical stores.
However, the petitioner, who is a registered pharmacist in the State of Madhya Pradesh, should not be considered ineligible as even those not registered nor being pharmacist, but undertake to engage registered pharmacists were invited to apply.
[Paras 6 and 8]
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 15, does allow for reservation in self-employment and reservation for ladies.
Despite not advertising the ratio of reservation for ladies in the selection for allotting 24 hours medical stores in the district, the policy of the State Govt. providing 30% reservation for ladies and giving preference to ladies within the ratio of reservation provided in the State policy cannot be considered illegal and does not invalidate the selection process.
Therefore, the selection can still be made while giving effect to the State policy.
[Para 10]
