The United Commercial Bank can impose minor and major penalties on its officer employees according to the regulations of 1976.
There is no indication in the rules that disciplinary authorities cannot impose more than one penalty on an employee.
The punishing authority, after examining the findings of the Enquiry Officer, held that the petitioner's promotion to the next higher scale shall be withheld for three years and his five annual increments were stopped with cumulative effect.
As per the rules, this action is in conformity.
