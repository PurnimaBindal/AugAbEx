Civil Procedure Code of 1980, Order 21 Rule 11 - Execution of a Decree - In a suit, orders for punishment or recovery from pay and censure are considered illegal and null and void.
There is a provision to conduct a fresh inquiry.
In a recent case, the judgment debtor did not make the payment despite directions issued by the executing court and the warrant of attachment was issued.
The law presumes that the petitioner-Corporation should have complied with the decree, and as it has attained finality, the executing court is directed to realize the amount from the petitioner-Corporation at an interest rate of 12% per annum as the amount has been unlawfully withheld by the Corporation.
