Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pension - Amendment of pension policy - Punjab Govt. Pension policies as amended in 1988 w.e.f.
111986 - The revision of pay scales recommended by the 3rd pay Commission resulted in the earlier date of 111986, which is not intended to benefit all pensioners retrospectively.
No arbitrary date was fixed to give retrospective effect to the amended policy, so Nakara's case is not applicable.
