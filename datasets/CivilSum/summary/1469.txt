Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Part I, Rule 213 - Regulations for the Medical Service of the Armed Forces, Section 423 - Death due to AIDS - Special family pension - The availability of special family pension is contingent on causes that can be attributed to or made worse by service, and it is granted more freely in cases of death while on active service.
Decision-making authority is under the purview of the rules, and the opinion of the Medical Board in such cases cannot be disregarded.
Pension claims for special family pension were rightfully denied due to medical opinion that the infection that caused the death was sexually transmitted.
This determination is not subject to intervention by the court.
