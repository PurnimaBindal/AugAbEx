The petitioner, a Brigadier in the Army Aviation Corps, was denied consideration for selection to the post of Major General on the basis that there is no post allocated to the Aviation Corps and because the petitioner could revert back to his parent Artillery Corps and seek consideration with his batch mates.
However, the court held that once the post is for the Corps, the respondent must consider all eligible officers and that the petitioner is entitled to be considered on his own service profile.
The court also noted that it cannot sit over policy decisions of the government, but that chance of promotion cannot be deemed as a condition of service and an employee cannot be denied right of consideration for promotion without any basis.
[Para 4, 16-17]
