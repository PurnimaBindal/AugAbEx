Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 9 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 14 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2 Item 6 - Jurisdiction of Civil Court - Lawsuit involving the setting aside of verbal termination orders and the request for the continuation of service with antecedent benefits - The defendant argued that no such orders were made and that the plaintiff left work voluntarily - The court ruled that it lacked jurisdiction to hear the case - The proper course of action was to seek redress under the Industrial Disputes Act or State Administrative Tribunal.
