Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10(1) and 12 - Reference - Jurisdiction of the appropriate Government - The appropriate Govt. cannot decide the dispute on merits under the guise of its power to determine the prima facie of the existence of a dispute between the parties for the purpose of reference - The Government is incapable of assuming responsibility for adjudicating the dispute submitted to the Labour Court - The Government has a legal obligation to refer the dispute to the Labour Tribunal once it has determined that there is a prima facie case.
[Paras 4 to 12]
