Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4(b), Schedule I Part II and Schedule IV - Compensation - A workman suffered an injury to his left index finger and a fracture to another finger - The Commissioner granted compensation based on a disability certificate stating the claimant had a 45% disability - However, the doctor who issued the certificate did not treat the claimant and issued it more than one year after the accident - Moreover, the doctor did not confirm that the claimant has no earning capacity - The Act’s Part-II of Schedule I states compensation for loss of left index finger is 14% and for fracture of other finger is 12% - Thus, the total disability is 26%, and the Commissioner's award has been modified accordingly.
