Lime industry falls under the category of "Heavy and Fine Chemicals" and therefore, is covered under Schedule-I of the Employees Provident Fund & Miscellaneous Provisions Act (19 of 1952), and the provisions of the Act shall apply.
[Para 10]
The Act is applicable to any establishment which is a factory engaged in an industry specified in Schedule-I and where 20 or more persons are employed.
Therefore, for an establishment to be covered under Section 1(3)(a) of the Act, all the conditions mentioned in the Section need to be fulfilled.
[Para 8]
Clause (b) of Section 1(3) of the Employees Provident Fund & Miscellaneous Provisions Act (19 of 1952) is applicable to non-factory establishments.
It does not mention that the non-factory establishment has to be an industry.
