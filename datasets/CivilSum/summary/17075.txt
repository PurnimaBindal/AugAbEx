The petitioner, a conductor, was charged for negligently losing tickets and was penalized for the cost of the tickets, printing, and the tray in which the tickets were kept.
However, there was no mention of the charge sheet regarding the misuse of the lost tickets, and the Corporation did not suffer a loss of the amount that the tickets could have fetched.
Consequently, the petitioner cannot be punished for any other charges not specified in the charge-sheet.
The impugned order was quashed, and a directive was given to recover only the amount lost due to the petitioner's negligence.
