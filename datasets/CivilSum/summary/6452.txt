Rajasthan Educational Service Rules, 1970, Rule 19 - Appointment - Post of Teacher Grade I - The marks obtained in the screening test are not considered while assessing the merit of candidates.
Rule 19 provides for screening test to shortlist applications.
As there were 15608 applications received for only 309 posts, the marks obtained in the written test could not have been taken into consideration while assessing merit of candidates in contravention of Rule 19 of 1970 Rules.
[Paras 15 and 17]

Writ petition - Contention not raised in writ - Since the contention was not raised in the writ petition, the court is not inclined to consider it further.
