The Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Sections 39 and 33 require that no less than three percent of seats in all Government and aided educational institutions be reserved.
However, the government has discretionary power in this matter, similar to the power it holds in identifying service and posts.
The Act does not mandate that seats in all educational institutions across the state be pooled together, as reservation is intended to be institution-wise.
