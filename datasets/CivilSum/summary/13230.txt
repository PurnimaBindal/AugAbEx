The Petitioner sought to quash an order that denied him pension under the Pension Regulation for the Army, 1961, on the ground that he had not completed the requisite period of qualifying service.
The period of desertion was not considered part of the qualifying service, according to Clause (b) of Regulation 123.
However, Regulation 122 stated that all service from the date of appointment until discharge was eligible for pension, with the exception of unauthorized absence.
The Petitioner claimed that the term "prior service" included the period of desertion.
The court held that desertion was an absence without intimation, and as no service was rendered during that time, no right to pension accrued.
Therefore, the question of forfeiture of rights did not arise.
