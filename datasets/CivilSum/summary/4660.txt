Punjab Civil Services Rules, Rule 4.4(c) - A suit for declaration regarding pay fixation after promotion from the post of Lecturer to the post of Head Master of Government High School was held.
The impugned order was set aside and the appellant's claim for a fixed salary of Rs. 2450 (basic pay) was decreed, as Headmaster is considered a post of higher responsibility than that of Lecturer in School Cadre.
The appeal was allowed.
