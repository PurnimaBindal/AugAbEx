In accordance with the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, as well as government instructions regarding promotion quotas, the decision of authorities to provide an ad hoc promotion within the direct recruit quota instead of promoting the petitioner who was duly selected through the Commission to the post of Principal at a government senior secondary school in Haryana is seen as arbitrary and in violation of Article 14 of the Constitution.
The petitioner was available for the promotion and the decision to include the ad hoc promotion clause in the order and to revert individuals as soon as recommended by the Commission is deemed unacceptable.
