As per Sections 2(oo) and 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, retrenchment can only occur after 180 days of continuous work, and seasonal work is not included.
In a case involving the closure of a sugar mill and termination or retrenchment, the appellant was found to not be entitled to retrenchment under clause (bb) of Section 2(oo) of the Act, with reference to 1995(5) SCC 653.
