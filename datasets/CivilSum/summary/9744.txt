Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(j) - Sikh Gurdwaras - Industry - Gurdwara Committee - When there is cooperation between employer and employee and the commercial element is substantial, an industry is established - Gurdwara Committee does not deal with distribution of goods and services for human wants and is not a commercial organization - Hence it does not fall under the definition of `Industry'.
