Writ petition filed by a Deputy Mamlatdar, who received a reduction of INR 100 per month from his pension for committing misconduct by preparing a supplementary memorandum in the form of an award of compensation under Land Acquisition Act which amended the nature of the land and additional compensation for which he was not authorized.
It was held that the petitioner himself had prepared and signed the memorandum and his actions amounted to deliberate attempt to favour land owners.
The punishment given was not excessive or harsh and the writ petition was dismissed.
[Paras 5 and 6]

The petitioner acted unauthorisedly in issuing the supplementary memorandum, and while sitting under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the court has limited judicial power of review in the matter of penalty imposed by the Disciplinary Authority or an Appellate Authority.
The court can only interfere when the punishment is shocking to the conscience of the court.
The reduction of INR 100 per month from the petitioner's pension was not disproportionate to his misconduct.
[Paras 5 and 6]

Rule 189-A of the Gujarat Civil Services Rules contemplates that punishment can be given when misconduct has resulted in financial loss to the Government and provides for recovery of loss from the delinquent.
The petitioner's actions were a serious lapse and not a case of error or mistake, and he is liable to make good the loss caused to the Government.
