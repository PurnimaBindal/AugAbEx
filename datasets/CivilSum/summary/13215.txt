Constitution of India, Article 226 - Select list - Reservation Roster - Second Notification - Post of lecturer - A scheduled caste petitioner was denied appointment despite a recommendation from the Public Service Commission - The Commission issued a notification for an earlier vacancy which was challenged - It was acknowledged that a vacancy was notified for the quota - The appointing authority cannot neglect the select panel without any reason - There was no justification for the slow appointment process by the Central Government - The petition was granted.
