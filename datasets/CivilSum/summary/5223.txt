As per Article 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India and Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, workers are entitled to monetary benefits for working on Saturdays and Sundays.
This has already been declared by the Supreme Court in the case of "Municipal Employees Union (Regd.)
Sirhind & others.
vs. State of Punjab and others" in March 2000.
Therefore, the workers' claim cannot be denied and the competent authority should pass appropriate orders accordingly.
The workers can also invoke Section 33-C(2) to claim their monetary benefits.
