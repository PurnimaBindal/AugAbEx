Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 21 - Pension Scheme for employees of the Bank, Rule 22 - Pension - Qualifying service - Rules that implement the pension scheme mandate a minimum of 20 years qualifying service - Employees who do not qualify under the scheme cannot demand pension as a right, and the related rule is not detrimental to their interests – The mere fact that some employees are ineligible for pension due to lacking qualifying service does not make the rule invalid.
