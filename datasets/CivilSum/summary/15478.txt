Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Notional promotion - Arrears of pay - A circular was issued to remove injustice by providing notional promotion and no arrears of salary on such promotion - Any monetary benefits beyond the executive order could not be claimed - There is no entitlement to arrears of pay when it comes to notional promotion and seniority from the past.
