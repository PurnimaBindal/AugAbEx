The petitioner's illegal termination was affirmed in an appeal after a suit was filed.
After the application for arrears of back wages was rejected, the petitioner moved the High Court and received directions.
Back wages for a period of three years were then paid to the petitioner, but the respondent argued that the High Court was not the proper forum for determining back wages.
However, as the termination was declared null and void, it was unnecessary to seek further clarification regarding payment of full wages.
The petitioner was not required to approach the Industrial Tribunal and the respondent was directed to pay full back wage from the date of termination until reinstatment.
