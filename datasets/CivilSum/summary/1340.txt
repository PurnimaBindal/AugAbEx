Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Discrimination - Petitioner denied arrears of pay and allowances for period of notional promotion due to adverse remarks which were deemed illegal, null and void in Civil Suit and upheld by High Court.
There was clear-cut bias by officer regarding these adverse entries against petitioner, while an officer in a similar position did receive arrears of pay and allowance.
Respondent ordered to make payment of arrears of pay and allowance for period of notional promotion.
