Constitution of India, Article 311 - UP Intermediate Education Act, 1921, Regulation 25 - Probationer - Termination - The management can choose to terminate a probationer's services by giving one month's notice or one month's pay in lieu thereof, according to Regulation 25.
However, there is no specification in the rule regarding the timing or method of payment.
This means that a probationer is only entitled to pay for the period of notice.
As a result, it was decided that payment of one month's pay in lieu of notice is not a prerequisite for effective termination of service, and the High Court was correct in overturning the Deputy Director's decision.
