Punjab National Bank (Employees') Pension Regulations, 1995 - Option for pension - The 120-day period for exercising the option is calculated from the date of notification of the Regulations, not from the date of issuance of the circular.

Punjab National Bank (Employees') Pension Regulations, 1995 - Option for pension - If the employer accepts the option and transfers the provident fund amount to pension trust fund immediately, they cannot claim that the option was late and wrongly accepted to deny pension to the employee post-retirement - Employer cannot have inconsistent positions.
