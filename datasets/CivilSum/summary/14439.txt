According to Punjab Food Supplies Department (State Service Class-III) Rules, 1968, Rule 11, seniority in the cadre of Inspectors is based on continuous appointment and selection.
The Petitioner was not appointed despite being selected due to his significantly lower merit, but he was eventually promoted after four years.
His seniority is fixed from the date of his actual promotion as outlined in Rule 11.
The period from his selection cannot be counted.
[Para 5]
The seniority list was prepared in 1976, and the Petitioner made his first representation in 1981 and the second in 1988.
However, the writ petition was filed in 1995.
As per Constitution of India, Article 226, the highly belated petition is liable to be dismissed on the ground of delay and laches.
[Para 6]
