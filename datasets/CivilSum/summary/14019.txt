Constitution of India, Article 226 - Haryana State Electricity Board Employees (Punishment and Appeal) Regulations, 1990, Regulation 7 - Departmental Enquiry - Petitioner charged with major punishment, but respondent imposed minor penalty without conducting inquiry - Such short cut method not allowed even for minor punishments - Respondent can either hold inquiry on cases under Regulation 7 or pass fresh order on cases under Regulation 8.
[Paras 2 & 4]
Haryana State Electricity Board Employees (Punishment and Appeal) Regulations, 1990, Regulation 4 - Minor punishment cannot be imposed without good and sufficient reasons - Reasons necessary for quasi-judicial orders as they connect the guilt of the delinquent officer to the conclusion of the punishing authority.
