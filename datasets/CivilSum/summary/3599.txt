Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16(2)(1) - Absence from guard duty - Absence from guard duty is considered as "Gravest misconduct" under Rule 162.
Even a constable who is absent for a day on guard duty without permission and information can be classified as gravest misconduct if it leads to the personnel of the force being murdered due to lack of guard.
Though in some situations, long absence may not constitute gravest misconduct and even one day's absence may suffice.
Once labeled as gravest misconduct by the competent authority, the Court will not substitute its opinion to determine otherwise.
