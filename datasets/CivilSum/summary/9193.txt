According to Section 18(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, in cases of settlement and termination, the Labour Court may award a 'No Dispute'.
However, this does not bind the workman who is not a member of the union or does not have any authority to compromise his dispute on his behalf.
Thus, if an award of 'No Dispute' is given without any basis or evidence, the matter needs to be decided on merits by the Labour Court after giving full opportunity to all parties.
In this particular case, the Labour Court has remanded the matter to be decided in accordance with the above rules.
