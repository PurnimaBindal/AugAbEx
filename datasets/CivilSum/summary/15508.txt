The petitioners, who are Assistant Surgeons, are seeking directions to be allowed to complete one year of training in Blood Transfusion as specified in Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, Rule 122-G.
They are contending that the respondents had promised to extend the training period.
However, the respondents argue that selection for the diploma is based on an entrance examination.
The court held that the petitioners cannot bypass the selection process and their request for a back door entry can have serious repercussions for others.
The petition was consequently dismissed.
