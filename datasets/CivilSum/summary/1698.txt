The petitioner sought to benefit from Higher Judicial service left at the time of taking voluntary retirement.
However, his representation to add additional service left for the purpose of fixing his pension was rejected.
Rule 8 provides for separate pension payable to a member of the tribunal on the basis of service rendered in the tribunal, and the rules contemplate two separate pensions for services rendered under State/Central Government and for service rendered in CAT.
The Government servant has no indefeasible right to claim benefit of additional service.
It was held that since the Petitioner is already getting pension for the period in dispute from the Tribunal, there can be no overlapping of the periods of service rendered.
