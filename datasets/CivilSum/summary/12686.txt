Under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b), an order of removal must be approved by the tribunal.
Evidence presented on record indicated that the petitioner did not issue tickets after collecting fare, despite being charged with this offense.
The tribunal rejected the petitioner's explanation that he was experiencing stomach ache and couldn't issue tickets, as evidence provided by the checking staff and unpunched tickets contradicted the petitioner's version.
The tribunal held that being unwell cannot justify misconduct and dismissed the petition.
[Paras 9 and 10] B.
According to Section 33(2)(b) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the quantum of punishment for charges of not issuing tickets after collecting fare imposed on the petitioner is not unduly harsh and no reason has been offered to justify leniency.
Thus, the punishment is commensurate with the misconduct committed.
