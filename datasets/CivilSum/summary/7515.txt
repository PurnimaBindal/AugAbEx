According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India and Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I Part-I, Rules 422 and 413, a government servant who takes on the officiating duties of a higher post with greater responsibilities is entitled to the higher pay applicable to any one of the posts for as long as they serve in that role.
Denying such benefits solely due to a lack of regular promotion or finalized seniority contradicts the principle of "quantum meruit."
