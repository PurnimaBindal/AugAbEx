Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Admission - Ayurvedic Post Graduate classes - The petitioner argued that the institute was understaffed, lacked affiliation from Panjab University, and used a selection method that violated rules.
On the other hand, the respondent argued that the institute was granted provisional permission to offer the course by the Central Council of Indian Medicine and the Chandigarh Administration as Panjab University had no faculty for Ayurveda.
The petitioner's eligibility was also doubted as she did not prove that she passed the BAMS degree on her first try.
As a result, the petition was rejected.
