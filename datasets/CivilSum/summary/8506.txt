According to Section 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, an order of termination may be issued in the event of unauthorized absence from duty for 22 days.
However, in a case where a petitioner was admitted to hospital during the timeframe in question, a lenient view was required.
As a result, the order of termination was modified, and the petitioner is entitled to reinstatement as well as two annual increments stopped with cumulative effect.
