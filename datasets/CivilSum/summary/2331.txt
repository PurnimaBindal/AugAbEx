Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 - Pay Scale - Parity in - Physical Education Teachers seeking parity in pay scales with NDS instructors - The pay of NDS instructors is safeguarded upon absorption in the State Government.
The petitioners are already receiving revised scales on par with NDSIs Grade II, as per the recommendations of the Pay Commission.
It is determined that PETs cannot be regarded as equal to only a few senior NDSIs Grade I, who receive scales corresponding to PGTs, and cause an imbalance in the entire cadre of PETs, which could have a cascading impact.
[Paras 36, 38 and 46]
