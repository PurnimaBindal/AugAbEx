According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, suspension pending enquiry or contemplated enquiry does not deprive an employee of the right to receive an increment.
Stoppage of increments is considered a penalty, which must follow regular procedure and can only be imposed with proper authority.
The fact that an employee on earned leave or half-pay leave is not entitled to an increment is not a valid reason to deny it to someone under suspension.
Since an order to stop increments has not been issued, it is not justified or within jurisdiction.
A writ petition is maintainable since the issue at hand involves inaction and discrimination, rather than just a purely monetary claim.
