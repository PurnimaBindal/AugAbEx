Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Reversion - The petitioner had been reverted from the position of Clerk to Work Munshi - According to Annexure P-1, the petitioner was freshly appointed as Clerk with no indication that he was temporarily adjusted - The petitioner continued in the position for more than four years - He was deemed to have been regularly appointed - As a result, he cannot be transferred or posted as a Work Munshi.
