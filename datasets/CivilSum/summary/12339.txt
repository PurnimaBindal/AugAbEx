The Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Fellowship - Respondent No. 2 is inviting applications from eligible persons for an overseas fellowship in post-graduate studies.
Although the petitioner was awarded an overseas fellowship, respondent No. 2 discontinued the fellowship abruptly on the grounds that the governing body had decided to do so.
The petitioner challenges the injugned order after a delay of 15 months without any reasons given for the delay.
The court held that the decision taken by Respondent No. 2 is not illegal or irrational and is not unreasonable.
Thus, the petition was dismissed.
