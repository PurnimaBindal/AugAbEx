Civil Procedure Code, Section 47 and Order 21 Rule 32 - Declaratory decree - Execution of - Declaration of seniority and entitlement of arrears of salary after deemed promotion can be executed - The executing court cannot refuse execution of such decree merely on the technical ground that a declaratory decree is not executable - A decree of declaration cannot be treated as a bare decree - Technicalities should advance justice, not defeat it.
[Paras 3 & 4]
