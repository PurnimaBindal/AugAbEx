Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Post of Lower Primary/Upper Primary School Assistants - The Gazette notification included a clause stating applicants for one district cannot apply in another district, but this clause was not present in the short notification published in the newspaper.
The petitioner applied for posts in multiple districts and their application was rejected.
The rejection of the application is illegal and violates Article 16 of the Constitution as the short notification did not have a restriction clause.
It is not necessary to refer to the Gazette notification for further details if sufficient information is provided in the short notification.
