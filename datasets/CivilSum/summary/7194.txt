The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, including Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25F, relate to the appointment and termination of workmen for specific periods.
If a workman is terminated for non-compliance with Section 25F, they may be awarded reinstatement.
However, in a case where a workman's appointment is covered by Section 2(oo)(bb) and their termination follows the terms of their contract, it is not considered retrenchment under Section 2(oo) of the Act.
Consequently, Section 25F is not applicable.
