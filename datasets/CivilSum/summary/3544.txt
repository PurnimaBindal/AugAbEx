Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - DTC employees - Claim of pay parity with Government employees in corresponding categories - Tribunal awarded same pay-scales as MCD employees - Tribunal's approach was erroneous as they cannot go beyond the referred disputes - Award was quashed.
[Paras 20 and 21]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 39(d) - Principle of equal pay for equal work - Applicability - Comparing pay-scales in different departments is not applicable - Claimants need to establish their positions, responsibilities, efficiency, and confidentialities are identical to those being compared to claim parity.
