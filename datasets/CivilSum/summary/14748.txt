Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975 - Constitution of India, Article 311 Premature retirement - Adverse remarks Criminal proceedings - Efficiency - The petitioner, a PCMS = I at TB Hospital, Patiala, was given adverse remarks for 1980-81, based on items that were subjected to an enquiry and an FIR lodged on one item.
In the departmental enquiry, the petitioner was exonerated of two items and even allowed to cross efficiency bar.
Although criminal proceedings were pending at the time of passing the premature retirement order, they concluded in acquittal.
The order of premature retirement was passed on non-existent material, and the adverse remarks lost their significance after the exoneration of the petitioner and crossing the efficiency bar.
Therefore, the order was quashed.
