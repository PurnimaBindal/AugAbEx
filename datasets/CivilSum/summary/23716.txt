Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Writ petition - Judicial review - Disciplinary proceedings - High Court in the proceedings under Article 226 exercises within the limits of judicial review to correct errors of law or procedural errors leading to manifest injustice or violation of principles of natural justice - Procedural steps required by appellant have been followed, and dismissal orders have become final - High Court examined the evidence as if it is a Court of first appeal and reversed the finding of fact recorded by enquiry officer and accepted by disciplinary authority - Judicial review is not akin to adjudication of the case on merits as an appellate authority - Held, it is wholly illegal for High Court to examine the evidence, as was done by it, as a first appellate court, cannot be sustained, set aside - Order of dismissal stands upheld.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]
