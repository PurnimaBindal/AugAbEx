Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Reversions - Adda Incharge in Haryana Roadways - Temporary promotions were made without affecting the rights of seniors whose cases were still under review or whose service records were not available during the selection process that included juniors.
Once the seniors are found eligible for promotion, the juniors previously promoted will have to make way for their seniors, subject to their rights.
Thus, the reversions were deemed appropriate.
