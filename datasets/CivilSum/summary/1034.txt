Constitution of India, Article 311 - Pune University Act, 1974, Section 43-B and Statute 417 - Termination of services for appointment on purely temporary basis - The appellant was appointed on a temporary basis for one academic year only, so the question of deemed confirmation does not arise, and the provisions of Statute 417 of the Pune University Act are not applicable.
Therefore, the termination of services is deemed valid.
