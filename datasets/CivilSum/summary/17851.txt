According to Constitution of India, Article 14 and Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1988 Rule 8, government employees are classified into two categories for pay promotion to higher posts.
Those promoted before the implementation of the Rules are ineligible for the two available increments, while those promoted after implementation receive them, resulting in a situation where juniors earn higher pay than seniors.
This classification system is discriminatory and subject to legal challenge.
[Para 5]

The Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1988, Rule 8 create an anomaly in implementation.
Corrections to the rule should be made from the date they are enacted, rather than retroactively.
While the Rule was amended to fix the anomaly on a future date, individuals retired or still in service were left dissatisfied.
The Rules amendment should be retroactively applied from the date of enactment.
[Paras 6 and 7]
