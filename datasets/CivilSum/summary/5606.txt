The Central Industrial Tribunal found that the petitioner's retrenchment was illegal under Sections 15 and 17 of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936.
While the Single Bench modified the award and allowed for half of the back wages, the Division Bench later confirmed the award.
However, an application was moved before the Chief Judicial Magistrate for recovery of payment, and the Another Division Bench modified the award to state that the applicant workman was not entitled to any back wages.
Despite this, the Chief Judicial Magistrate stayed recovery of back wages.
There was an illegality committed by the Learned Magistrate by closing the proceedings, and the remedies available to the petitioner were either to file a review petition before the Division Bench or file a Special Leave Petition so that the matter may be finally decided by a Larger Bench.
Ultimately, the revision was dismissed.
