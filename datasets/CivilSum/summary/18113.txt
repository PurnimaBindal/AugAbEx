Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependants of the Deceased Government Employees Rules, 2003, Clause 18 - Compassionate appointment - The applicant's claim was alive under Clause 18, since the application was made within three years of attaining age of majority and the three-year period applies to orphans until one child attains majority - Therefore, the petitioner's claim is eligible for consideration under the 2003 Rules and not the 2006 Rules - The court directed that the petitioner be appointed on compassionate grounds.
