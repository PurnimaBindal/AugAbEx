Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion/Pay scale - Determining pay on the promotion post - The petitioners voluntarily accepted promotion - Their pay scale was reduced, but the total emoluments drawn from their promotion post increased - Promotion post comes with a higher status and rate of increment, as well as a higher basic pay scale - An effective and reasonable formula must be developed to address the matter - The current fixation is not unreasonable.
[Paras 13 to 16]
