The termination of the petitioner's employment was deemed illegal by the Labour Court on the grounds that the petitioner had completed more than 240 days of continuous service in one calendar year, granting him a statutory right under Section 6-N of the U.P.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
The respondent did not receive notice or retrenchment compensation, and the termination was found to be in violation of Section 6-N of the Act.
The petitioner-employer's challenge to the Labour Court's order of reinstatement with back wages was not accepted.
