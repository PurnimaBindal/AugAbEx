Constitution of India, Articles 300-A and 226 - Pension - Contributory Provident Fund Scheme (CPFS) - Petitioner working as a teacher in a High School - The Petitioner has requested to convert their option from CPFS to Pension Scheme, which includes GPF - The option form was submitted before the school authority, however, there was a delay on the school authority's part in filing the same before the District Inspector of Schools - The teacher cannot be held responsible for any fault or laches - The Petition is hereby allowed.
