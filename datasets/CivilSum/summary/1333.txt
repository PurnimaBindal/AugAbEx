Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - State or Authority - Writ jurisdiction - Previous examination of whether Maruti Udyog Limited is defined as State or an authority under Article 12 of Constitution by Delhi High Court in CWP No. 3102 of 1990 by order dated 11.9.91 - Determined that Central Govt. lacks the amount of control necessary over the company to classify it as an instrumentality of State - Therefore, Writ petition against respondent-company is void.
