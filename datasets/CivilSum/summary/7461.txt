Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised Pay Scales) Rules, 1983 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Withdrawal of pay scale - Principles of natural justice - The petitioner's pay scale was withdrawn after 17 years of service on the ground that she was mistakenly appointed as Librarian Grade II instead of Grade III.
However, no notice or opportunity for a hearing was given, rendering the order violative of the principles of natural justice and unconstitutional.
The petitioner shall continue to hold the post of Librarian Grade II.

Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised Pay Scales) Rules, 1983 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Withdrawal of pay scale - Recovery - Permissibility - The petitioner's pay scale was changed from time to time, even though no fault was attributed to her, on the ground of being wrongly appointed as Librarian Grade II instead of Grade III.
However, it would be just and proper to allow the petitioner to continue to enjoy such benefits.
