Banking Regulations Act, 1949, Section 36A(2) - Lien - The Petitioner was appointed as Chairman while holding the position of Deputy General Manager of the bank.
However, when the Petitioner was subsequently removed from the position of Chairman, he did not cease to be an employee, and retained his lien as Deputy General Manager while continuing as Chairman.
As a result, the Petitioner is entitled to hold the post of Deputy General Manager upon termination of appointment as Chairman.
