Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertaking) Act, 1970, Section 19 - Central Bank of India Service Regulations, Regulation 20 - Resignation - Acceptance/Withdrawal of - Petitioner resigned from service citing forceful circumstances - Petitioner withdrew resignation before being accepted - Respondent accepted resignation with the condition of 3 months' pay deposition - Non-compliance with condition - Held, failure to fulfill conditional order makes it impossible to claim that resignation has been accepted.
