In accordance to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, it is justified to classify the Cadre of Laboratory Attendants into Laboratory Attendants and Senior Laboratory Attendants and prescribe higher pay scale for the latter, as the qualification of Matric pass has been made essential for Senior Laboratory Attendants.
[Para 6]
In view of the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, providing the benefit of selection grade to non-Matric Laboratory Attendants who have no avenue of promotion is reasonable.
