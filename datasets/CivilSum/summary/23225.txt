Under the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 2(s), 10 and 25-F, it is established that the termination of services of employees who were appointed on the recommendation of an MLA and in violation of recruitment rules cannot be upheld.
The appointment of such individuals is deemed void, and monetary compensation may be awarded in the given circumstances.
Furthermore, no authority, no matter how high their position, can direct the recruitment of individuals of their choice.
