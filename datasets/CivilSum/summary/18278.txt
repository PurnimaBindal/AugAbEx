As per the Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Schedule 2 and Item 3, a petitioner employed as a booking clerk was punished with stoppage of two increments after being alleged of charging passengers with excess fare; however, since no passengers complained at the bus stand or were presented before the enquiry officer, and there was no evidence to suggest that cash was checked at the bus stand and found in excess with the petitioner, the charge cannot be considered valid; hence the punishment order was dismissed.
