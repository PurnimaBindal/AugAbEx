Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(m) Note 5 - General Financial Rules, 1979 - Date of birth - Correction of date of birth on the basis of entry in matriculation certificate - Note 5 to FR 56 (m) provides that request should be made within five years from date of entry into service for correction of date of birth - The intention of the rule making authority in providing a limitation period for seeking correction after entry is to discourage stale claims and belated applications - Courts must interpret the rule to promote its intention, which is to provide a reasonable time for correction and not to allow unlimited time - Those who joined service before 1979 for a period of more than five years and seek correction of DOB after 1979 must do so within five years after the amendment in the rule in 1979.
