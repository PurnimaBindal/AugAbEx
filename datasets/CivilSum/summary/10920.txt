According to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-G & H, last come first go is the basis for termination and seniority list is crucial to this process.
Thus, the workman cannot be terminated without following the procedure prescribed under Section 25-G and the seniority list must be produced as required under the Industrial Disputes (Punjab) Rules.
Failure to produce the seniority list suggests that the management is hiding something and the workman is entitled to reinstatement with full back wages.
