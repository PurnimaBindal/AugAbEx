Employees (Conduct, Discipline & Appeal) Rules, Rule 33 - Departmental Proceedings - Enquiry Officer recommending removal from service - Disciplinary Authority while agreeing with the recommendation passed the removal order - The Disciplinary Authority is not obligated to provide reasoning for agreeing with the Enquiry Officer's report and is not required to issue notice to the delinquent before passing the order of punishment.
[Para 10] However, the Appellate Authority is directed to reconsider the respondent's objection if it has not been appreciated correctly.
