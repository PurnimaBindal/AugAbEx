The Mizoram Civil Supply Department (Grade C Post) Recruitment Rules, 1987, Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 are relevant in this case regarding inter-se seniority.
The petitioner was appointed on direct recruitment against a regular post, whereas the private respondents were then working on a lower post.
The inter-se seniority list showed the petitioner's name below those of the private respondents.
The petitioner claims that the arbitrary fixation of seniority violated their rights under Articles 14 and 16 of the constitution.
The impugned notification dated 622004 denied equal treatment to the petitioner by treating two unequal parties as equals by taking the private respondent's service in the lower post for counting seniority in the higher grade.
The inter-se seniority Govt. order dated 622003 and the impugned notification were held to be illegal and arbitrary, and thus was quashed.
