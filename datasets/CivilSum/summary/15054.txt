The Rajasthan Technical Education Service Rules, 1975 govern the appointment of Junior Instructors for the DPCS/COPA post.
The petitioner's candidature was denied by the respondents due to their lack of COPA qualification.
However, the training manual of ITI shows that DPCS has been renamed as COPA and the circular by Director Technical Education, Ministry of Labour mentions the same.
Furthermore, the petitioner was appointed to take the examination for COPA, making the rejection of candidature erroneous and illegal.
The court gave a direction to consider the petitioner's candidature for the post since they had already passed the examination.
