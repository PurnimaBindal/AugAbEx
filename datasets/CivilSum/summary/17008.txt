Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 21 - Compassionate appointment - Financial crises - The applicant, a widow receiving family pension at Rs. 3940/- pm, received several other monetary benefits indicating that she is not in financial distress - Compassionate appointments are only given to families in dire financial need due to the death of an earning member - As a result, the petition was dismissed.
