Punjab Employment (Class I & II) Service Rules, 1963, Rules 9 & 13- Ad hoc service - Seniority - An individual who is appointed on an ad hoc basis as a stop-gap arrangement without following the competitive selection procedure prescribed in the Rules and without providing an opportunity to all eligible candidates on that particular date, cannot accrue seniority by adding such ad hoc service period to their length of service.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Ad hoc service period - If an ad hoc appointment was made without following the prescribed procedure in the Rules, such a period cannot be included in seniority - If such an illegal benefit has been given to someone, it will not entitle others to claim the same benefit in contravention of the Rules and the law.
