Constitution of India, Article 226 - Resignation - Withdrawal of - A Secretary in respondent-Bank resigned to contest an election but later sought withdrawal of his resignation.
The Bank allowed the withdrawal subject to approval, and the petitioner was appointed as Secretary, but only subject to the Board of Directors' approval.
The Registrar of the Bank subsequently directed the Bank to follow instructions, with no prohibition on the petitioner's re-entry into service.
The impugned order reliving the petitioner from service with immediate effect had no justification, as the Board of Directors had not confirmed the Managing Director's action.
The petitioner would be deemed to be in service of respondent-Bank but rejoining would be subject to instructions.
