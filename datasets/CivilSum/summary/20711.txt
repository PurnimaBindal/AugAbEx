The Certificates of Service issued under U/s 80 of Merchant Shipping Act, 1958 can be considered as a Certificate under the 1978 Convention, subject to specific conditions.
The 1978 Convention aims for a uniform standard for eligibility and competency certificates of Masters of Ships, sailing on international waters, which means that the validity of a Certificate of Service and Certificate of competence is important.
Unqualified Indian Navy Officers can obtain a Certificate of Service to be a Master of Ships based on experience, but only if they fulfill the requirements.
[Paras 3 to 10 & 15]
Rule 15 of Merchant Shipping Rules is not in violation of Articles 14 and 19 of the Constitution because the difference between SoS and CoC is based on distinguishable characteristics that are legally acceptable.
Article 14 does not condone discrimination but allows a reasonable classification based on distinguishable characteristics.
Classification based on qualifications is an established and acknowledged concept, so the Merchant Shipping Rules, 1998 are lawful under the Constitution and the Act.
