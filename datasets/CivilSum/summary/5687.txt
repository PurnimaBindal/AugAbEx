Constitution of India, Articles 20, 311 - Departmental proceedings - Criminal proceedings - After being acquitted in criminal proceedings for an offence u/s 409, IPC, the petitioner/accused faced departmental proceedings - The subject matter of charges was not identical in both proceedings - While the case of misappropriation of funds was not proven in criminal proceedings, charges of issuing receipts without receiving the amount were considered sufficient for a gross misconduct and serious negligence, which provided justification for the respondents to impose penalties through the departmental proceedings.
