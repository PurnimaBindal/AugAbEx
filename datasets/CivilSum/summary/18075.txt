Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administrative action Jurisdiction - Compassionate grounds Transfer - Transfer orders that were canceled on 25690, after being passed on 15/25.5.90 - The action was not fair.
Although the transfer of employees falls under administrative action, it is vital to keep in mind the fairness in action, convenience of the parties, and efficiency of the administration.
It is essential to allow orders once passed to remain in force for an extended period.
This approach not only enhances the authority's reputation but also restores employees' confidence, thereby avoiding the need for them to approach the courts, alleging unfairness in administrative action.
