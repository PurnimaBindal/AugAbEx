Constitution of India, Articles 14, 309 and 226 - Selection - Appointments - Recruitment for Class III and Class IV posts governed by Service Rules and Regulations - A Departmental Selection Committee should make the selection - District Magistrates and Deputy Commissioners can only prepare lists after selection, but cannot take the place of a recruitment board - The High Court was not justified in issuing directions to follow the list prepared by the DC - The Court's direction was set aside.
