Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 311 - Compulsory retirement - Adverse remarks - An order of compulsory retirement based solely on adverse remarks in an Annual Confidential Report (ACR) cannot be justified.
Adverse remarks were recorded in the ACR only in reference to an FIR filed against the petitioner, which was later canceled.
The petitioner has requested the expunction of the adverse remarks from the ACR, and an inquiry into the charges is still pending.
The order of compulsory retirement could prejudice the pending proceedings.
[Paras 9 & 10]
