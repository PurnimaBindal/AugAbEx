Under the Punjab State Policy dated 2981991 and the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, the petitioner, who worked as an ad-hoc employee from 26.11.1971 to 3181991, is entitled to regularisation and pensionary benefits.
The policy stated that ad-hoc or temporary employees holding Class III posts who completed 2 years of service on 31121990 should be regularised.
