Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 137, 138, 139 and 1319 - Promotion/Out of turn promotion/Sportsmen and Award winners - Rule 13.19 allows for out-of-turn promotion for constables who receive the President's award, but does not provide the same consideration for those who demonstrate exceptional courage or duty in challenging situations or excel in other activities like sports - The rules were not designed to address the unique situations faced by police in recent times - Therefore, changes are necessary to provide for out-of-turn promotion in extraordinary cases - The government has been instructed to consider amending the rules and to consider the petitioners for out-of-turn promotion until then - Petitioners are advised to make their case known to the relevant authorities.
