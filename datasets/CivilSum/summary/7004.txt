According to Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2 Item 3, a daily wager can be reinstated with continuity but no backwages.
The management claimed the workman was gainfully employed, and the petitioner did not question the management's witness on this point.
The workman did not state her unemployment in her affidavit and merely claimed it during cross-examination, which was not enough to shift the onus on the respondents.
Therefore, the labour court's decision cannot be challenged under these circumstances.
