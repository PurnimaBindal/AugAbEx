In regards to Relief against Law, respondent 2 did not contest the Tribunal's decision that respondent 1 was senior to them.
The Union of India implemented the Tribunal's decision by appointing respondent 1 as Secretary to Government.
Respondent 1 only seeks compensation based on the promotion being deemed retrospective from March 19, 1987, when respondent 2 was appointed as Secretary.
The government will consider this claim independently.
The Union Government and its Secretary should not be on litigating terms.
Regarding Promotion under Article 136 of the Constitution of India, the Tribunal's propositions were difficult to accept, therefore they were set aside, while other conclusions were sustained.
