The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300-A state that pension is not a bounty or an award, but a deferred wage, and that pension earned by an individual cannot be denied simply because they were absorbed in a public sector undertaking.
Once an individual is allowed to be absorbed in the public interest with the understanding that they will receive their full pension entitlement, the CDA is barred from denying them their disability pension.
[Paras 5 to 7]
Under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300-A, an individual is entitled to a disability pension from the date of their retirement.
In this case, the petitioner was entitled to a pension at the rate of Rs. 1,400/- per month, calculated based on the date of retirement and applying factor 1540.
Although there was a delay in the Medical Board process due to a mistake in sending the earlier Medical Board report to the wrong department, this delay was not the petitioner's fault.
As such, any deduction in pension on account of the weightage element due to this delay is deemed illegal.
The qualifying service prescribed for earning full pension is included in the provision of regulations.
[Paras 8, 10 and 11]
The Constitution of India, Articles 300-A and 309 provide that pension entitlement under the Pension Regulations for Army cannot be affected by any clarificatory letter that lacks statutory force.
There is no rule or clause in the Pension Regulations that permits deductions to be made on account of weightage element, nor does it exclude officers who have been absorbed in public undertakings from the provision of full pension.
[Para 8]
Based on the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, there can be no discrimination between pensioners based on whether or not they were absorbed in public sector undertakings.
There is no rationale or connection to the object sought to be achieved by this classification.
