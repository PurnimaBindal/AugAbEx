Constitution of India, Article 226 - Constitution (15th amendment) Act, 1963 - Constitution (42nd Amendment) Act, 1976 - Major General of Indian Army - Supersession - The single Judge erred in holding that cause of action did not arise in the territorial jurisdiction of Patna High Court solely based on the location of the selection and recording of ACRs - If the exercise of power falls within the court's jurisdiction, it cannot be precluded from exercising jurisdiction under Article 226 - The impugned order of supersession has been set aside and the matter will be remanded back to the single Judge for the hearing of the writ petition on merits.
