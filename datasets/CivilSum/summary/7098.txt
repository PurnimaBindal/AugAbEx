The Railway Protection Force Rules, 1987 and the Constitution of India's Articles 226 and 311(2) state that discharge from duty can be initiated when false information is provided.
However, in this case, the petitioner was not aware of any criminal case against him at the time of filing the attestation form and therefore could not have been accused of furnishing false information.
The Chief Security Commissioner, RPF terminated the petitioner's services unjustly, without evidence of any false or incorrect information.
As a result, the petitioner has been wronged by the authorities and the impugned order has been quashed.
