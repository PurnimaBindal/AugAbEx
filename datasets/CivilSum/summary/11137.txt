Constitution of India, Article 14 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 21, Rule 30 - Back wages/Reinstatement - In the event that a termination order of a Government employee is deemed null and void or is set aside, said employee is entitled to recoup any and all missed salary payments as well as interest dating back to the original due dates.
The limitation period does not apply, therefore arrears are not limited to a maximum of 38 months.
The following cases were cited as precedence: AIR 1980 SC 1773, 1983 PLR 21 FB, ILR (1974) 11 P&H 56 FB and 1990(a) RSJ 714.
