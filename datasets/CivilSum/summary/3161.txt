The Navy Pension Regulations, 1964, Regulation 15(2) allows the Competent Authority to forfeit pensionary benefits (part or whole) following due procedure under Regulations 4 and 5, in case of criminal offense by a Navy personnel and punishment of dismissal awarded by a Court Martial.
This provision is not ultra vires the provisions of Sections 81, 82, 47, and 27 of the Navy Act, 1957.
[Para 18]

The forfeiture of pensionary benefits merely on the ground of punishment awarded by Court Martial for criminal offense by a Navy personnel is not acceptable.
Navy Pension Regulations, 1964, Regulation 15(2) requires the Authority to consider satisfactory service of the officer and award pensionary benefits accordingly.
[Para 27]

When Court Martial is imposing punishment of dismissal or dismissal with dishonor for criminal offense by a Navy personnel, it is acting as a Court of Criminal jurisdiction.
In such cases, Court Martial cannot forfeit pensionary benefits, except in the case of dismissal with dishonor.
[Para 13]

The Competent Authority can forfeit pensionary benefits of a Navy personnel who committed a criminal offense and was awarded a punishment of dismissal by Court Martial, but this should be done within six months of cessation of service of the personnel.
If the order of forfeiture of pensionary benefits is passed after 10 years of cessation of service, it is not acceptable, and the Authority must pay the amount of pensionary benefits with 12% interest.
