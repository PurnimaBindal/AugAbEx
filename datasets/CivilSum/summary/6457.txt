Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, Section 33C - Award - Pre-existing rights - The Labour Court directed the reinstatement of the workman to an alternative job within a month, but that job was given after a year.
The workman claimed monetary benefits for being unjustly deprived of the alternative job.
The application was accepted.
The Labour Court's decision was based on the admitted facts in determining the pre-existing rights of the workman.
There is no need for interference under Article 226 of the Constitution.
[Paras 4 and 5].
