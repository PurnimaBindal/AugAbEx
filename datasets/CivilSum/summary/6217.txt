Article 14 of the Constitution of India concerns probationers and deemed confirmation.
There are no statutory rules governing service conditions, so the court must examine the appointment order to determine if an employee becomes permanent after completing one year of service using the principle of deemed confirmation.

In a case where a transfer order was challenged almost three years after it was issued, repeated representations were made against the order, with none providing a response to justify the delay in approaching the Court.
These repeated and delayed representations do not relieve the petitioner of the obstacles of laches, as the equitable rights of others arise.
