Based on the Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226, bonus marks can be given to sports persons for participation in sports.
However, the petitioner was denied bonus marks because certificates were not issued by the proper authorities as required by the rules.
Nonetheless, the petitioner is entitled to receive 15 bonus marks for participating in tournaments, and the Authorities have been directed to give the petitioner those marks, and to also consider her for appointment.
[Paras 9, 13, 15, 17 and 18]
