The Karnataka State Services (Direct Recruitment of Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and Backward Tribes to Class III Posts) (Special) Rules, 1977, Rules 3 and 4 - Reservation - Have been declared violative of Article 16(1) read with Article 335 of the Constitution, and cannot be protected under Article 16(4) of the Constitution.
The High Court's judgment has been upheld and the appeals have been dismissed.
