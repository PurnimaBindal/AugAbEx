Compassionate appointment - When making a compassionate appointment, the circumstances and hardship of the family must be taken into consideration.
Applicants cannot be denied benefits solely because their application was made later or after obtaining educational qualifications.
B Jammu & Kashmir Compassionate Appointment Rules, 1994, Rule 3 - Qualification Acquisition - The interpretation that a qualification should be acquired within six months of death does not mean that the application for compassionate appointment must be made within six months of the death.
"Acquire” refers to “acquiring by a conscious effort.” A qualification that becomes available due to death is not acquired by the person.
C Words and Phrases - Acquire - "Acquire" means to acquire something by conscious efforts.
