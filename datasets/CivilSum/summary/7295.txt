The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 along with the Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 12.21 and 162 govern the discharge of police officers.
In the case of a constable discharged under Rule 1221 of the Punjab Police Rules, no regular enquiry is required.
The order of discharge, dated 1721980 was proper, and a suit filed on 1381985 was barred by limitation, based on the concurrent findings of the courts.
