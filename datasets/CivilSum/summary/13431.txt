The petitioner in a case regarding pay parity with Personal Assistants of Principals of Government Engineering Colleges located in Regional Engineering College must demonstrate that the employer is the same and functions of both positions must be the same.
The college of the petitioner is created by society, indicating that there is no parity between the petitioner and the Personal Assistants of Principals.
The functions of both roles also differ, with different administrative hierarchies.
