Air India Regulations, Regulation 46 - Voluntary Retirement Scheme - Providing a Voluntary Retirement Scheme - If the Corporation issues another Scheme, any previous Scheme becomes insignificant and the latest Scheme will prevail.
[Para 13]
Air India, Regulations, Regulation 46 - Voluntary retirement - Notice - The Voluntary Retirement Scheme of the Corporation requires three months' notice by the employee and reciprocal obligation on the Corporation to convey its reasons within that very period if the request was not to be accepted.
If the Corporation does not convey any reasons within the stipulated period, the request for voluntary retirement shall be deemed to be accepted.
As per the Scheme, the employee is entitled to all retiral benefits.
[Paras 14, 17 and 18]
