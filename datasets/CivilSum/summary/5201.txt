The Constitution of India, Article 226, and the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 10 make it clear that once the appropriate government has made a reference, they are not competent to cancel or supersede it.
However, there is nothing in the Act to prohibit a Labour Court from interpreting the term of the settlement in their award and passing the award accordingly.
Once a settlement is reached between the management and the worker, its validity cannot be questioned on the grounds of arbitrariness or discrimination - as per (2002) 3 SCC 411.

As per the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 2(k), in case any disputes or differences between the parties have been amicably settled and have resulted in the filing of an application for withdrawing the demand notice, then no dispute is deemed to exist, and no reference is required to be made.
