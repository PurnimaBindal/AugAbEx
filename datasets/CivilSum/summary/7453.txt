Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2-A - Disciplinary proceedings - Order of punishment - Jurisdiction of civil Court - An employee of the State or its instrumentality has the choice to seek remedy either before a civil court or by way of writ petition or raising an industrial dispute, as held.
Therefore, the finding that the civil court had no jurisdiction is not sustainable.
[Para 4]
Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970 - Departmental enquiry - Putting of questions to witnesses by Enquiry Officer - No Presenting Officer was appointed, so the asking of questions to witnesses by the Enquiry Officer cannot be deemed illegal so as to vitiate enquiry proceedings, as held.
[Para 7]
Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970 - Departmental enquiry - Non-maintenance or order sheets - Non-maintenance of order sheets is a mere irregularity, but in the absence of any prejudice such an irregularity will not vitiate inquiry proceedings, as held.
[Para 7]
Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970 - Departmental enquiry - Non-speaking order by punishing authority - The plea is not tenable, as the petitioner had raised all such pleas earlier and they were considered by the Inquiry Officer while submitting report, procedure prescribed under rules was followed, and principles of natural justice were complied.
