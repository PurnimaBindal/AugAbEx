According to the Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(oo)(bb), abandonment requires that an employee be removed from their position without notice, hearing, or termination order issued in accordance with the law.
After being removed from the Municipal Committee, the petitioner made a request for reinstatement with back wages and continuity.
The Labour Court's decision that the workman abandoned his job cannot be sustained.
[Para 6]

The Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-F outlines the guidelines for retrenchment of employees.
In this case, the petitioner was a muster Roll worker in the Municipal Committee who was removed from service without notice, enquiry, or order of termination.
The petitioner's claim is that they worked continuously with the Committee for eight years.
While the Committee argues that the worker did not work for 240 days, they did not provide the muster Roll records to prove it.
As the employer maintains the service record, it is the Committee's responsibility to prove that the workman did not meet the 240-day requirement.
