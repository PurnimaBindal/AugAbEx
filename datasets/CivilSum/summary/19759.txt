Sections 22 and 23 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 state that an opportunity may be given to lead evidence and request for adjournments may be made in cases of lockout.
In a specific case where workmen pleaded that the lockout was unjustified, their request for adjournment to produce evidence in their favour was denied.
The denial was grounded on the fact that the witnesses could have been summoned earlier.
This order was not justified.
The union, however, is entitled to prove that the lockout was unjustified.
