According to the Constitution of India, Articles 226/227, Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol. II, Rule 2.2(b), and Punjab Civil Service (Punishment & Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8, a superannuated petitioner claimed deficient gratuity amount by way of direction.
Complaints were filed against the petitioner alleging irregularities in purchases made by him.
The petitioner was charge-sheeted, and an enquiry found him guilty.
The loss was assessed at Rs. 25,00090, and the loss amount was ordered to be recovered out of the payable gratuity.
The petitioner was given one opportunity, and the impugned order cannot be considered illegal or violative of principles of natural justice.
