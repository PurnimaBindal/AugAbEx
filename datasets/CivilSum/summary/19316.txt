When a person is promoted or transferred to a higher position, according to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part I, Rule 4.4, it is assumed that they are performing duties and responsibilities of greater importance, which entitles them to pay fixation.
A petitioner who worked as an Assistant Soil Conservation Officer and was later posted as a Divisional Soil Conservation Officer, which has duties of greater importance, is entitled to pay fixation under Rule 44 of Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol.
I, Part I.
