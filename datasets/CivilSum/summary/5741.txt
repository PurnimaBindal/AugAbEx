Civil Procedure Code, Section 60 - Attachment - Subsistence allowance - An employee of Central Co-operative Bank, Amritsar, in an execution of an award passed by a competent officer for embezzlement, is entitled to subsistence allowance as a general rule under the Service Rules, although the provisions of Section 60 of the Code are not applicable to him as he is neither a government servant nor an employee of a railway company or of a local body.
The bank's action of depositing the amount accumulated on account of the subsistence allowance in the employee's account without notice and then getting it attached ex parte in execution, without providing an opportunity to the employee to withdraw it, was mala fide.
Hence, the order of attachment as well as the application for revoking attachment have been set aside.
