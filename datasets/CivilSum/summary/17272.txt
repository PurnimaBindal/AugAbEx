Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Punjab Municipal Act, 1911, Section 232 - Appointment - Suspension of resolution of Committee - Qualifications - Selection - The petitioner was not approved for appointment by the Director twice and despite a fresh selection, the petitioner was still not found suitable.
Moreover, the petitioner did not even possess the eligible qualifications necessary for the post, hence he is not entitled to hold the post.
