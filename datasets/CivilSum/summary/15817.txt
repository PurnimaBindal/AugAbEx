Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Break in service - Condonation - Writ Petition filed to claim a break in service due to a junior employee being retained while the petitioner was relieved - Petitioner argues the principle of first come last go should have been followed, but the junior employee was not included in the writ petition - Petition denied due to the junior employee's longer tenure and earlier date of appointment, despite having spent time in a lower post.
Petitioner's argument is deemed without merit.
