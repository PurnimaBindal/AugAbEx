Constitution of India Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code 1908, Order 9 Rule 7 - Ex parte Proceedings - Setting aside of - Delay - The petitioner had filed an application to set aside an ex parte order three years after its passing, with the evidence of respondent No. 1-workman already concluded.
The proceedings were still pending when the application was moved.
While the petitioner had entrusted the case to a lawyer who made periodic inquiries and assured them that appropriate steps were being taken, the court held that after three years, the petitioner should have been given a chance to participate in the proceedings.
As a result, the impugned order of the labour court was set aside.
