The Karnataka Civil Services (Special Recruitment of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes candidates to certain Group `A' and Group `B' posts) Rules, 1985, Rules 3, 4 and 7, and Karnataka State Civil Services (Direct Recruitment by Selection) Rules, 1973, Rule 6(2), state that Rule 6(2) of the 1973 Rules is inapplicable to selection made under the Rules due to its inconsistency with Rule 4(5)(b) of the Rules.
