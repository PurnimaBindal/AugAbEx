Constitution of India 1950, Articles 226/227 - Annulment of documents issued by respondents regarding "written tests and personality test" - Following a consented order by the LPA Bench directing the corporation to consider the petitioners for absorption against the advertised posts, it must be interpreted and implemented as specified in the order.
The court cannot alter the meaning of the order.
The respondent authorities were obligated to absorb the petitioners as Counter Incharges if they were eligible for the said post according to the statutory qualifications laid down in the rules.
