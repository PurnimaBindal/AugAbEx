The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 state that there is no definite right to claim grant of selection grade in a particular service.
If the respective heads of Departments consider certain posts to be deserving of selection grades, they can grant it.
The competency to decide which posts are eligible for selection grades falls with the competent authority.
The Ex-cadre posts were granted a selection grade for two out of the number of posts.
Petitioner's claim cannot be sustained as these posts were separate and distinct from what he held and no rules or laws were violated by the action of respondents in not granting the selection grade for the post of Sports Organiser.
[Para 10]

The inclusion of a post in a particular service is solely the prerogative of the competent authority.
If a post is not included in a particular service, its action cannot be interfered with unless it is malafide or violative of a rule or law.
The post of Sports Organiser has been treated as an Ex-cadre post and cannot be shown that its duties are similar to those of Class-II Service.
The action of respondents is not arbitrary or unjust, and the petition is dismissed.
