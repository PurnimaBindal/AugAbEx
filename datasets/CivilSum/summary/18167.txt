Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Qualification improvement - A writ petitioner claimed that the District Inspector of Schools refused to grant permission for improved qualification through correspondence courses - The state feared that petitioner would seek financial benefits based on the improved qualification in the future - The order without granting permission was invalid - The court stated that permission could not be withheld in anticipation of future benefits - The District Inspector was ordered to grant the petitioner permission to take the correspondence course and examination.
[Paras 3 to 5]
