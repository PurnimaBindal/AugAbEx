Section 45-A and 45-B of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948 allows for recovery of insurance contributions due from employers without necessarily passing an order under Section 45-A in every case.
Determining the contribution is only necessary under certain circumstances and the ESI Corporation is responsible for this determination.
Employers may approach the ESI court for grievance resolution under Section 45-B and the corporation can recover underpaid contributions as arrears of land revenue.
In the case of an order being passed under Section 45-A, the person in question is entitled to a reasonable opportunity.
An employer's application was rejected by the ESI Court as there was no dispute to be determined.
The matter was remitted back to the ESI Court to pass an appropriate order after giving the employer a reasonable opportunity, considering the long periods of recovery involved.
