Constitution of India, Article 226 - Ordinance of University of Rajasthan, Ordinances 67, 67-A and 65 - Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions Act, 1989, Section 16 - Grant-in-Aid to Non-Government Educational and Cultural Institutions Rules, 1963, Rule 3(16) - Though Rule 16(3) of the Rules of 1963 stipulates the age of superannuation as 58 years, it is not a statutory rule with force of law - While Ordinances 67, 67-A and 68, which provide for the age of superannuation as 60 years, are statutory conditions for whole-time teachers in colleges affiliated with the University - The terms and conditions of service related to superannuation must be 60 years, instead of 58 years stipulated in the Grant-in-aid Rules of 1963, which hold no statutory force.
