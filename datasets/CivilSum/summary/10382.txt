Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Selection - Cut off date - Eligibility - Appointment on the post of Sanskrit teacher - Condition of eligibility requires result to be declared before date of interview for all provisional candidates - Petitioner applied for Sanskrit teacher post - Application deadline was 16121996 - Petitioner appeared in 1997 - Candidature rejected - Petitioner claims respondents No. 4 and 5 were interviewed and selected by the Commission - Court concludes that wrongful selection of respondents No. 4 and 5 was made - Court cannot direct respondents to commit another illegality by considering petitioner for selection and appointment to the post of Sanskrit teacher - Appointment of respondents No. 4 and 5 being contrary to law, set aside.
[Paras 13 and 14]
