Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Disability Pension - The petitioner was admitted to the Air Force on 151970 in Category `A' - Suffered from NIDDM to the extent of 30% and chronic cholcystitis - Claim for disability pension was rejected - However, it was found that the petitioner's composite disability was 20% which was admitted by the respondents - Therefore, the petitioner is eligible for disability pension.
