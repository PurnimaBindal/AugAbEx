Constitution of India, Article 311 - Staff Service Regulations, 1983 (Kakatiya Grameena Bank), Regulation 31(2) - Appeal - Speaking order - Departmental appeal - Dismissal from service - While the Appellate Authority does not need to give a detailed order when confirming the decision made by the Punishing Authority, it must provide reasoning to demonstrate its consideration of the appeal's arguments - Brief and unclear orders are invalid and subject to dismissal.
