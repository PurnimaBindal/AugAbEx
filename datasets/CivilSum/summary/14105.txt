According to the Postal & RMS Employees Co-Operative Bank Ltd Staff Service Rules, when an employee is dismissed from service, a show cause notice must be issued before imposing the punishment of dismissal.
However, in the case of dismissal in question, no such notice was given, resulting in a clear violation of the principles of natural justice.
Consequently, the Registrar, Co-operative Societies was right to set aside the orders of dismissal.

If the Board of Directors dismisses a Manager, then Section 74 of the Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 1984 will be applicable.
