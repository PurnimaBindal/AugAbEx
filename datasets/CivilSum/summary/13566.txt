Constitution of India, Article 309 - Instructions - While benefits conferred by a statutory rule cannot be taken away with an executive order, it is within the competent authority's power to issue executive orders in matters not governed by such rules, so long as it does not impinge on the rules' provisions.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Classification - The government can create classifications that have a rational relationship with the objective they seek to achieve.
Providing additional benefits as an incentive for a specific job performed by a group of employees is not discriminatory or unfair.
