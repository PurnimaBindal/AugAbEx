Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Regularisation of service - The petitioner was out of service from 131990 until their reinstatement, and regularisation was denied on this basis.
However, the Labour Court overturned the termination order and granted relief of reinstatement, backwages, and continuity of service.
It was determined that the petitioner is deemed to be in service and shall be considered to have completed five years of service, granting them entitlement to regularisation under the government policy.
