Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination of service - Unauthorised absence from duty without leave - Termination of service from the date of absence - The Tribunal determined the order of termination was bad in law and reinstated the employee in service without all consequential benefits.
The High Court, in the writ petition, held that the order of termination contained stigma, as no opportunity of hearing was given, hence the order was rightly set-aside.
The State appealed against the judgment of the High Court.
The State Government is directed to take necessary steps and pass a fresh order.
[Para 7]
Service Law: Termination of service - Stigma - It is held that mere description of a background fact cannot be called as stigma.
[Para 7]
Words and Phrases: Stigma - A blemish, imputation, a mark or label indicating a deviation from a norm.

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination of service with retrospective effect - Whether sustainable?
- No.
It's held that the order giving retrospective effect is not sustainable.
