Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Cadre provincialised on 1472000 - 9 Posts vacant at the time of provincialisation - Prior to 1472000, the corporation was responsible for completing the channel of promotion for the posts - The petitioners have the right to be considered for promotion against those posts, and the corporation should carry out this exercise at its own level.
