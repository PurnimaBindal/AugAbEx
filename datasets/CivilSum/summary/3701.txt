State Bank of India (Supervising Staff) Service Rules, Rule 50(2)(XXI)(a) - Enquiry report - Disciplinary authority disagreeing with finding - Sustainability of order of punishment - Hearing - The disciplinary authority is required to provide tentative reasons for disagreement and an opportunity to be heard before passing a final order if the finding differs from the enquiry officer - The matter has been referred back to the disciplinary authority for a fresh decision.
