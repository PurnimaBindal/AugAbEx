Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 16 - Termination of Services - Misconduct - Natural justice - The Petitioner was appointed as a part time class IV employee in a School, but her services were utilized by the Respondent throughout the entire school hours.
Despite working as a regular class IV employee, her services were terminated due to allegations of misconduct without any inquiry as required by the Rules.
This violates principles of natural justice and thus, the order of termination is quashed.
