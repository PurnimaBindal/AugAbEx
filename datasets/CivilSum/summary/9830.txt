The Judgment-debtor cannot raise objections related to orders passed prior to 1987, which they did not raise during the main suit, according to Civil Procedure Code, Order XXI.
Furthermore, since the judgment-debtor did not challenge the order dated 1011995 by way of appeal or revision, it is too late to bring it up now.
The principle that the executing court cannot go behind the decree is settled in law.
As a result, the petition is dismissed.
