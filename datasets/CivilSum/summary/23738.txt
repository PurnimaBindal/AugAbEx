UP Secondary Education Services Commission and Selection Board Act, 1982, Section 33 - Removal of Difficulties Order Dt. 31-7-1981, Para 2 (as substituted by Second Order, Dt. 11-9-1981) - The power to make ad hoc appointment of teachers is not limited to existing vacancies or vacancies arising within 2 months of the Order - Future vacancies are included under Para 2.
[Paras 11, 12] B.
Interpretation of Statutes - Removal of difficulties - The Order for removal of difficulties is a permanent provision with ongoing effect.
[Paras 11, 12] C. UP Secondary Education Services Commission and Selection Board Act, 1982, Section 33 - Removal of Difficulties Order Dt. 31-7-1981, Paras 4 and 2 - When making ad hoc appointments to substantive post of Principal through promotion, Para 2 (vacancy) and Para 4 (procedure) both apply - Para 4 cannot be independent of Para 2.
[Para 13] D UP Secondary Education Services Commission and Selection Board Act, 1982, Sections 33, 18, 33A - Removal of Difficulties Order Dt. 31-7-1981, Para 2 - The power to make ad hoc appointments under Section 18 and under Para 2 of the 1981 Order or Section 33A (which includes Para 2 of the Order) is not inconsistent.
