Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Dues/Wages - Workmen can claim amount of money and other benefits admissible to them under Section 33-C(2), regardless of whether they are wages or not - Computable benefits like provident fund and bonus fall under dues that can be claimed - Labour Court acted within provisions of the Act by awarding these benefits to the respondent-workman.
[Para 12]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Interest - Dues/Wages - Labour Court was justified in awarding interest to workman, since they were denied their rightful dues for almost 12 years - Payment of interest is only on the amount awarded by the Labour Court - Action cannot be considered unjust or illegal.
