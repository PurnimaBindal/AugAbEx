The government cannot appoint Notaries in a State exceeding the specified number according to Rule 4A as amended by the Second Amendment Rules 2001.
Although a proposal by the Commissioner and Inspector General of Registration to appoint the Petitioner as Notary was accepted by the government, the Petitioner was not given an appointment as the appointees had already exceeded the number specified in the Schedule.
The Government, therefore, could not issue an appointment to the Petitioner in accordance with Rule 4A.
[Paras 4, 9] (Parchuri Kishore v. Government of AP, 2002(2) ALT 751 referred)
