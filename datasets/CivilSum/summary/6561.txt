Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 9(2) - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Removal of members or Chairman of Central Administrative Tribunal - A petition cannot be filed by a petitioner who was convicted in contempt by a member of the CAT solely with the ground to remove the member - No direction can be made by the petitioner for the government to frame rules under Section 9(3) of the Act regardless of if rules are framed or not.
