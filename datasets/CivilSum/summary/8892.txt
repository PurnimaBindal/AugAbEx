Constitution of India, Articles 13, 32 and 226 - Selection - Declaration by the Court - Applicability of - Judgment in rem - The Court's decision declaring selection and appointment as violating Rules is only applicable prospectively and with regards to the selection being challenged - It will not affect recruitment processes and appointments that have already been made - Public appointments require reasonable certainty, which is essential - Interpretation of Rules - Termination orders that are retroactively applied based on the decision in Ghewar Ram's case are invalid - This decision only has retrospective application - Interpreting the Rules.
