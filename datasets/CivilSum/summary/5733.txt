Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination/Natural justice - Misconduct - A Lecturer appointed on a temporary basis was terminated due to allegations of derogatory behavior made by students and negative comments from the Director.
Despite this, the complainant was not given a copy of the complaint nor were they allowed an opportunity to explain against the allegations.
The Executive Council's decision was in violation of the principles of natural justice and should be set aside.
The termination was overturned and the case was remanded back to proceed in accordance with the law.
[Paras 21 & 22]
