Section 10(1)(c) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 discusses dismissal from service due to misconduct and proportionality of punishment.
In a case where a respondent was accused of assaulting a town warden during an execution of a decree of vacation of unauthorized quarters, the Labour Court conducted a fair and proper inquiry and established the allegations.
However, the Labour Court's decision to reinstate the respondent with 50% backwages, without specifying reasons for disproportionate punishment, was incorrect.
As a result, the High Court affirmed the dismissal from service order.
