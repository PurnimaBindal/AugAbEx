According to Army Act, Sections 52(d), 109 and Army Rules 1954, Rule 37(1) and (2), a Deputy General Officer Commanding is not authorized to order the convening of a General Court Martial without authorization from the Chief of Army Staff or any officer empowered by him.
[Paras 10, 11] B.
Army Rules 1954, Rule 37(1) and (2) mandate that an officer must satisfy himself that evidence justifies trial on charges before ordering convening of a General Court Martial.
Since no such satisfaction was recorded, the order of removal cannot be sustained.
