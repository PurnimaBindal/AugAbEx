Punjab State Electricity Board (Punishment & Appeal) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 9(4) - Show cause notice - Order passed before the amendment of Regulations without giving show cause notice - Dispensing with the show cause notice through the amendment of Regulations later on is not significant.
It makes the order illegal.
[Paras 4 and 5]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Speaking order - The employee had the right to file an appeal against the punishment-order.
As no speaking order was given to him, the order is defective.
The inclusion of reasons recorded on the file, but not communicated to the employee, does not cure the defect.
Thus, the order is set aside.
[Para 6]
