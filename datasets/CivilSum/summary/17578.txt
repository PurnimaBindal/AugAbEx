As per Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulations 7(b) and 173, a disease is considered to be attributable to army service unless it can be proven that it could not have been detected during medical examination prior to acceptance for service, subject to 20% or more disability.
The petitioner in the given case was discharged due to a diagnosis of Grandmal Seizures, but there is no evidence to suggest that this disease could not have been detected during the pre-service medical examination.
Hence, the petitioner should be eligible for Disability Pension subject to a disability of 20% or more, provided the condition persists.
