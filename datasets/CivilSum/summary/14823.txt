Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923, Section 4A - Penalty Allocation - Penalty cannot be imposed on an employer without proper notice or opportunity to show cause, in accordance with the proviso to subsection (3) of Section 4.
As a result, the order imposing penalty is annulled for not following the correct Procedure and the matter is referred back to Commissioner for fresh orders.
