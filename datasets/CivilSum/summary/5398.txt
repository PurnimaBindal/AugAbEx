Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination - Burden of proof - The employer must show evidence to support the termination of a workman.
In this case, the workman claimed to have completed 240 days of service prior to termination and the employer failed to produce records for the relevant period.
The employer was unable to prove seasonal work and was found guilty of withholding evidence.
As a result, the Labour Court drew an adverse inference against the petitioner.
