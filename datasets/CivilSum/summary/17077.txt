According to Section 22 of the Workman's Compensation Act of 1923, the assessment of physical disability is not the only criterion for determining the loss of earning capacity.
In cases where an individual is incapable of performing the duties they were performing at the time of the accident, the loss of earning capacity is considered to be 100%.
[Para 4]
When quantifying compensation under Section 22 of the Workman's Compensation Act of 1923, the entitled minimum wages must be used rather than the actual wages paid.
Moreover, the calculation done by the Commissioner is deemed appropriate.
