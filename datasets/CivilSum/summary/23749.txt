The Payment of Bonus Act applies to the Tamil Nadu Housing Board, and Section 36 of the Act gives powers to the State Government to exempt the Housing Board under Section 32(v)(c) of the Act.
However, the State Government's action of issuing a notification of exemption retrospectively to earlier accounting years without giving employees a hearing is bad.
The exemption orders issued during the relevant years are also null and void as no hearing was given to affected employees.
Employees must be given the chance to provide rebuttal evidence against the material furnished by the claimant establishment to allow the appropriate Government to make an objective assessment of the situation and make a rational, well-informed decision.
