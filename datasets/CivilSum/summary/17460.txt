Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16(4) and 335 - Reservation Appointments - Cadre posts - Reservations may only be claimed in public appointments against cadre posts - Angan Wari workers are neither Government employees nor have they been appointed to any cadre posts - It is a fixed honorarium engagement - Every widow, helpless, and handicapped woman is eligible for consideration for such engagement regardless of castes and tribes.
