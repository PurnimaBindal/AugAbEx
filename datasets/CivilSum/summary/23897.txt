The validity of Section 7-A of the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 was questioned for its lack of provision for an appeal.
As the debates were ongoing, an amendment was made to the Act introducing Section 7-D, which now provides for an appeal before the appellate tribunal.
Consequently, the challenge to Section 7-A's validity is no longer relevant, and appellants must instead pursue the statutory remedy of appeal before the appellate authority to determine their case's merits.
