Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 226 - Medical disability in employment - Alternative job - If Roadways drivers sustain injury in employment resulting in medical disability, as per the directions issued by the Supreme Court in Anand Bihari's case, they may be offered any alternative reliefs; however, providing posts of drivers to their dependents would be opposed to those directions.
Nevertheless, the authorities may consider providing other posts to their dependents on the condition that they meet the necessary qualifications.
