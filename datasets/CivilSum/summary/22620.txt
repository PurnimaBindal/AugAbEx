Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 11-A, 25-F and 25-H - Unfair labour practices - Termination after expiry of 88-day fixed term of appointment - No unfair treatment given to workman - No findings of any kind of unfair practice - Reinstating the workman with back wages would place him in a better position than what he could have had if he remained in service - Workman not entitled to continue in service - Discretion under Section 11-A cannot be exercised illegally or arbitrarily that would be unfair to the employer.
