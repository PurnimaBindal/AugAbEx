The delay in deciding the claim for compassionate appointment cannot act to the detriment of the claimant, as per Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14 and 21.
Despite 13 years passing due to the pendency of a writ petition, the claimant's rights must still be adjudicated upon as of the date the proceedings for redressal were intimated.
