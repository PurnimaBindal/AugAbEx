Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - Cancellation of - Post-Graduate MD/MS Courses - The admission process for this course was to be completed by 30999, with a later extension to 3152000 through a corrigendum dated 2252000.
Although the petitioner was selected against a seat vacated on 322000 and had submitted the necessary documents and fee by 2052000, her admission was cancelled on 2952000.
It was found that the university could not ignore the requirement of completing courses within the time schedule fixed by it and the Medical Council of India, and as the academic session was almost over, the action of the respondent was not approved.
Accordingly, respondent Nos. 3 and 4 were justified in cancelling the admission granted to the petitioner.
