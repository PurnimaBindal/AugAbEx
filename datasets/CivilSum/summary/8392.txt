All India Services Act, 1951, Section 3 - Indian Administrative Service (Recruitment, Rules, 1954, Rule 8(1) - Indian Administrative Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1955 - Appointment by promotion to I.A.S.
- It was decided that the select list must be prepared year-wise and that creating a common list after combining vacancies from several years was deemed unlawful.
Instead, the committee must form a separate select list for each year, taking into account the number of vacancies.
