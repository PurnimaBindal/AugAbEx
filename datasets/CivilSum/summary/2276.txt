Border Security Force Act, 1968, Section 11(2) - Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 22 - Dismissal from service - Without enquiry - The order of delinquent's dismissal from service can be sustained as delinquent was informed about long absence and proposed punishment and was asked to furnish defense, but delinquent neither replied nor denied allegations.
Therefore, it cannot be said that procedure prescribed under Rule 22 was not followed before passing order.
[Para 12]
Border Security Force Act, 1968, Section 141 - Central Civil Services Pension Rules, 1969, Rule 48-A - Notice of voluntary retirement - Whether deemed to be accepted - Employee dismissed on ground of unauthorized absence - Petitioner had requested for leave, regularization of overstay period and voluntary retirement which would be considered on reporting back to duty - However, the petitioner had not completed one year of service after resuming charge in India after foreign assignment - Hence, it cannot be said that request of petitioner for voluntary retirement is deemed to be accepted after 3 months notice.
[Para 15]
Border Security Force Act, 1968, Sections 11, 2 - Central Civil Services Pension Rules, 1969, Rule 48-A - Punishment of dismissal - Length of service - Non-consideration of petitioner's twenty two and half years of unblemished service - The punishment is vitiated due to non-application of mind as it has the effect of depriving Petitioner of pension and other retiral benefits, which is wholly arbitrary and shocks conscience of court - Therefore, the punishment has been substituted with compulsory retirement.
