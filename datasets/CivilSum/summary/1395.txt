The petitioners in this case failed to establish a strong foundation for challenging the Constitutional validity of Rule 8(2) of the Punjab State Agricultural Marketing Board (Class II) Service Rules, 1988, which prescribes academic qualifications for the post of Secretary of Market Committees.
As a result, the court could not strike down the provision on the grounds of discrimination or violation of Article 14 or 16.
It is the responsibility of the petitioner to establish prima facie evidence of arbitrariness or unconstitutionality, and the court is ill-equipped to determine the necessary qualifications for recruitment to a particular service or cadre.
Furthermore, the mere curtailment of avenues of promotion does not automatically imply arbitrariness or unconstitutionality, as chances of promotion cannot be equated with a right to be promoted or even a right to be considered for promotion.
