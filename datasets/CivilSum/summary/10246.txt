Selection - Prescribed qualification - Post of Physical Education Teachers - A higher qualification than the prescribed minimum qualification can be considered equivalent to the prescribed qualification for the post.
For example, qualifications such as BP Ed and M.P.Ed.
can be treated as the prescribed qualification of matric with a certificate course in physical education.
[Paras 8, 10 and 11]
Selection - Post of Physical Education Teachers - The petitioners for the post applied pursuant to the advertisement notice, participated in the selection process and appeared in the interview.
The fact that the petitioners were not selected does not give them the right to challenge the selection.
[Para 11]
