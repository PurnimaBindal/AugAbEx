Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Concealment of facts - In the case of appointment to the post of Fire Operators requiring a heavy vehicle driving licence, it is necessary to provide accurate information about age and date of birth - Petitioner concealed information and provided false statement to obtain driving license, which is a violation of provisions of Motor Vehicles Act - Petitioner refused appointment due to misrepresentation of facts and wrong information supplied to authorities - The decision of the authorities to not appoint the petitioner is justified and fair - Petition dismissed.
