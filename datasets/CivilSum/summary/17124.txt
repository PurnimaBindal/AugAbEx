Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - The petitioner was found fit during the medical test at the time of his entry into the army on 6.7.91 - However, he was later diagnosed with unspecified psychosis when he was discharged on 221094; the respondent declined his prayer for disability pension claiming that his suffering constituted a constitutional one- The respondent couldn't provide evidence of any deformity noticed at the time of petitioner's entry into service - The types of diseases petitioner suffered are common among Jawans as a result of the laborious nature of their work - The petitioner's disability was assessed to be 30%, he is therefore entitled to a disability pension.
