Constitution of India, Articles 16 & 38 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1321 - Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995 - Handicapped - Police officials with physical handicaps are granted relaxation from passing the Upper School Course - The claims of such officials must be evaluated fairly and objectively with relevant considerations in view - The authority must not act arbitrarily or whimsically - Exemption should not be denied to those with good and very good service records if others with inferior records have been granted exemption - Comments by the authority about "tolerating handicapped persons" are undesirable and unnecessary - The decision for exemption should be reconsidered.
[Paras 6 to 9]
