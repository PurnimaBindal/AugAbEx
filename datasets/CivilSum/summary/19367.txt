The denial of compassionate appointment due to a ban on fresh appointments is unacceptable as the ban does not affect the balance of appointments and compassionate appointment is meant to enable the deceased employee's family to meet the crisis.
Any ban extended to compassionate appointments will be in violation of Article 21 of the Indian Constitution.
[Para 2] B The denial of compassionate appointment for the post of conductor to the wife of a deceased employee on the grounds of gender discrimination is unjustified, and the corporation is ordered to appoint women as conductors and make a beginning by appointing the petitioner.
Her claim cannot be rejected based on gender once she is ready to work as a conductor.
