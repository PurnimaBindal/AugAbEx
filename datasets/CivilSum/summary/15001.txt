Delhi Police Act, 1978, Section 147 - Delhi Police (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1980, Rule 25 - Obtaining a refundable loan from GPF account - disciplinary action taken due to undisclosed fact of granting earlier loan - Appellate authority's decision of removal found to be disproportionate - Respondent's first loan was for Rs. 75,000 and second loan, taken after 15 months, was of Rs. 1,20,000 - Respondent contributed Rs. 1,100 per month - The facts regarding the loans and contributions did not need to be separately proven - The punishment of forfeiture of two years of approved service was deemed appropriate by the disciplinary authority instead of removal.
