Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, Sections 8 and 32 - Bonus - Ex-gratia - Govt. employees - Govt. authority - Section 32(iv) of the Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, excludes employees employed in an establishment engaged in any industry carried on by or under the authority of the State Government from the operation of the Act - Haryana Roadways falls under the State Government's authority - Hence workers under the Transport Department do not have the right to receive bonus or ex-gratia as per the Act.
[Para 5]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, Sections 32 and 8 - Classification - Policy of the Govt. - Employees of an establishment engaged in any industry carried on by or under the authority of the State Government do not have the right to claim bonus or ex-gratia benefits under the Act - However, if the Government introduces a policy extending these benefits for employees with a certain length of service, those who are not eligible under the policy cannot complain of discrimination.
