Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Delay - Management didn't mention delay before the Labour Court - There is no specific time limit for making demand notice - Labour Court will only consider plea if raised before them - Any delay not raised by management cannot be considered by Labour Court - Delay point cannot be raised for the first time in writ petition - Management's claim declined due to delay not being raised - Award invalidated.
