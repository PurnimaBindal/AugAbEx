According to Section 37 of the Punjab Cooperative Societies Act, 1961, lawsuits must be instituted by a duly authorised person.
If this requirement is not met, the suit is not maintainable.
A plea that there is no need to prove the original resolution authorizing the secretary to sue, or to produce any of the signatories who allegedly authorized him to institute the suit, is not valid.
[Paras 5 and 6]
