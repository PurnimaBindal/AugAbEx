The petitioner argued that he began serving as an Assistant Engineer on an ad hoc basis on January 4th, 1980, and was appointed on a regular basis beginning January 29th, 1982, after being selected by HPSC.
After completing 12 years of service, the petitioner applied for selection grade and requested that his continuous service from 1980 be counted towards the 12-year requirement.
The respondent argued that since the petitioner was regularized in 1982, the period should be counted from that date.
The court determined that the petitioner's 12-year period for selection grade eligibility could be counted from 1982 under Government circular.
