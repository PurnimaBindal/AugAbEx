Constitution of India, Article 226 - Trade Unions Act, 1926, Section 10(b) - Cancellation of registration of Trade Union - Show-cause notice - Industrial Tribunal's finding that petitioner-Union must have received show-cause notice is grossly erroneous - Neither copy of show-cause notice nor postal acknowledgment receipt carries Union's registered postal address or rubber stamp for receiving the notice - Order of cancellation for registration of trade Union set aside.
