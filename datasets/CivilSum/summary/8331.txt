Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 37 - Pension - Retiral benefits - The issue of whether an individual who voluntarily foregoes past service can claim the benefits of that service after being permanently absorbed in a new position was debated - The court determined that no, the individual could not claim those benefits - Since the individual resigned, their lien with their previous department was terminated and, because their appointment was not in the public interest, they could not receive the benefits of past service rendered in their earlier post.
