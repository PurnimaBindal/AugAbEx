Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 Selection/Merit list/Mistake in merit list/Appointment - Petitioner selected in PCS (Executive Branch) in the merit list one ineligible candidate placed above him mistakenly by the Commission - Petitioner would have been offered the appointment in PCS had that candidate not been placed above him - Petitioner should not face the consequences of the Commission's mistake - Government obliged to enroll him in PCS (Executive Branch).
