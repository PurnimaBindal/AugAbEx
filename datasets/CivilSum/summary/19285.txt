According to Rule 10 of the Punjab Education Service (Class II) Rules, 1934, seniority is determined by the date of confirmation.
As the petitioner's probation period expired six months before that of the respondents, he was deemed to be confirmed earlier than them.
Therefore, he cannot be made junior to them.
The seniority list cannot be upheld and must be recalculated in accordance with the Rule.
