Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226 and 227 - Reversion - Violation of Principles of Natural Justice - The competent authority promoted the petitioner after considering the cases of all eligible persons.
However, a subsequent order was passed reverting the petitioner without any notice or opportunity to be heard.
It was held that the impugned order violated the rules of natural justice as well as Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India and is set aside.
