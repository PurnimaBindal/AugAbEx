The claim for compassionate appointment was rejected due to an application being submitted more than six months after the individual attained majority and nine years after the death of their mother.
The deceased had a daughter, but she did not take advantage of the benefit and had attained majority in 1994.
The Punjab Government Circular from 8.8.1998 does not allow for relaxation of the six-month period for submission after attaining majority for the minor defendant.
The decision to reject the claim for compassionate appointment was upheld.
