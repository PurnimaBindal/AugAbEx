Constitution of India, Articles l4 and 311- Punjab State Electricity Board Regulations, Regulation No. 11 - Probationer /Termination of services/Opportunity of hearing - The authorities started disciplinary action against a probationer but terminated his services with a simple order.
However, if it can be proved that the order is based on misconduct, even a seemingly innocuous dismissal can be challenged.
[Paras 11 and 12]
Punjab State Electricity Board Regulations, Regulation 11 - If a probationer's services are to be terminated due to unsatisfactory performance or unfavorable reports, he must be informed of the grounds for such a proposal and given an opportunity to defend himself.
The use of the word "shall" denotes that the grant of opportunity is mandatory, and Regulation No. 11 does not require any other interpretation.
[Para 13]
