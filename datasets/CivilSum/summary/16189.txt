Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Classification of employees/Reasonable classification - Equal pay for equal work - Classifying employees in one cadre based on date of joining for different pay scales must be based on the same nature of duties and responsibilities and conditions of service - Different pay scales based on date of joining have no logical reasoning - Artificial and farcical distinction made - Petition allowed - Board instructed to give same pay scale to similarly situated employees with arrears, if any.
