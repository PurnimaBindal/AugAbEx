Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Termination - Absence from duty - Petitioner was denied further leave and informed via telegram, leading to termination of services after a show cause notice was given and no medical certificate was produced - Argument of non-compliance with natural justice principle rejected, as court will not intervene without evidence of injustice or prejudice to the employee - Respondent did not need to conduct full inquiry when employee's abandonment of service can be inferred - Petition dismissed.
[Paras 32, 34, 38, 55]
