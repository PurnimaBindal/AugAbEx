[Para 19] B.
The Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Appointment - The post of Director-in-Chief is a solitary and ex-cadre post with no established criteria for appointment by the state government.
As per eligibility criteria and convention, senior officials are considered for appointment.
The principle of reservation cannot be applied for a solitary and ex-cadre post.
The state government has allowed Respondent No. 7, who was not recommended by the Commission, to function as Director-in-Chief, which is improper.
The State Government is directed to submit the list of eligible candidates before the Commission for recommendation, and the appointment should be made according to the Commission's recommendation.
