Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference of dispute - A breakaway group from the parent All India Union submitted a dispute regarding service conditions and linkage of DA with CPI - During the dispute, many settlements were reached between the Company and All India Union, which were accepted by most employees - The Tribunal rejected the reference, and the award was challenged - The settlements were different from the award and could not be taken in parts - The gains of the settlements were accepted by the majority, and the reference by the petitioner was likely a prestige issue - The petitioner cannot persist with independent and separate adjudication - Therefore, the petition was rejected.
