Constitution of India, Articles 16, 441 - Compassionate Appointment - Medical invalidation - Allowance of - The government cannot take away the right to compassionate appointment by extending the benefit of the scheme to the family of a government servant retired on medical invalidation.
This benefit is an exception to the doctrine of equality and is intended to provide support to families in dire circumstances due to the loss of their earning member.
Thus, offering appointments on compassionate grounds exclusively to those retired on medical invalidation does not satisfy the requirements of Article 14 of the Constitution, and any such policy by the state is considered to be unconstitutional.
