The Punjab P.W.D.
(Irrigation Branch) Overseers' Engineering State Service, Class III, Rules, 1955, Rule 2(j) and Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India state that ad hoc appointments can be made.
Petitioners cannot object to being transferred to a different department, as their appointment did not create a right to the post.
The petitioners can seek reversion to their original post if they do not want the higher post offered to them in Rural Development and Panchayat Department, Punjab.
[Para 8] B.
The State Government offered the petitioners an appointment in Rural Development and Panchayat Department, Punjab, as Junior Engineers in surplus.
This appointment was not entitlement, but accommodation.
The petitioners have the option of seeking reversion to their original posts or facing termination.
The petitioners' rights have not been violated, and the petition is dismissed.
