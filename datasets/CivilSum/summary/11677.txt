Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology, Patiala, Academic Regulations of the Institute, Regulations 17(a)(i) and (ii) - Repeat - Students who fail to achieve the required minimum standard of 40% can register to repeat the first year course and improve their CGPA, as per Regulation 17(a)(i) - However, registration to repeat is not automatic, and the student must apply for it - If the candidate does not apply, they alone are responsible - The Regulation does not prevent registration to repeat.
[Paras 2, 7, and 10]   B Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology, Patiala, Academic Regulations of the Institute, Regulations 17(a)(ii), 7(ii), 9(a) and (b) - The interpretation given to Regulations 17 and 9, which require candidates to leave the Institute for failing to earn a CGPA greater than or equal to 450, despite earning 50% of the credits offered, is harsh and penal - A student who has paid fees and got admission is expected to complete the course - Requiring the student to leave the Institute at the end of the first year cannot be approved - The word `and' in the context of the regulation means both conditions referred to in clauses (i) and (ii) must be met - The word `and' must thus be read conjunctively - Unless a student is deficient in both, they cannot be asked to leave the Institute.
