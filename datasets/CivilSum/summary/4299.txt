Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Absence from duty - The termination of the workman cannot be sustained as his name was removed from the rolls without following the requirements of Section 25-F - Workman is entitled to be reinstated with continuity of service.
[Para 9] B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Back wages - The workman is awarded 50% of wages last drawn by him as back wages for the period he remained out of job till the date of reinstatement - No other pecuniary benefit for the period is allowed.
[Para 10] C. Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F and 2 (oo) - Retrenchment/Termination - The abandonment of job by a workman amounts to termination, which is retrenchment as per Section 2(oo) of the Act - Management failed to offer retrenchment compensation and paid in lieu of notice period as a condition precedent to retrenchment - Compliance with Section 25-F is required.
