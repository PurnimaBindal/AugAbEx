Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 and 311 - Abolition of post - Termination - Labor Court determines benefits amounting to Rs. 100 per month for an application under Section 33C(2) ID Act claiming an increase in salary from Rs. 35 to Rs. 250/- per month and payment of difference of pay for last seven years - Deduction of Rs. 35/- pm which workman was already drawing from among granted is permissible - However, the amount due must have been either voluntarily deposited by petitioner or recovered through execution proceedings taken out by workman - Since the respondent is no longer employed, it would be unjust to recover any amount after so many years - Thus, petition is dismissed - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C [Para 1]
