As per the Constitution of India, Arts.
14 & 21, the minimum qualification for the post of meter reader is matriculation.
However, the petitioner possesses a certificate of Pratham/Madhiam of Hindi Sahitya Sammelan, which is equivalent to matric in Hindi.
The post of meter reader does not require much skill and expertise, and the petitioner has sufficient experience having already served for 9 years.
The petitioner has not committed any fraud, misrepresentation or forgery, and it would not be in the interest of justice to terminate his service after 9 years.
This follows AIR 1990 SC 371, 1995(1) RSJ 563, 1996(3) RSJ 210 (SC).
[Paras 9 & 10]
