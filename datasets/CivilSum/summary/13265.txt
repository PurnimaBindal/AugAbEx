According to Kerala Education Act, 1959, Section 36 and Kerala Education Rules, 1959, Rule 28, members of staff of aided schools have the right to make claims for the correction of date of birth within a given time frame.
A recent amendment to Rule 28 was made with retrospective effect to eliminate discrimination between teachers of government schools and teachers of aided schools.
This amendment is not violative of Article 14 of the Constitution and is neither arbitrary nor unreasonable.
