The Constitution of India Articles 15, 16, and 226, and the Admission Process - The Government has set a 50% quota for service candidates and a 50% quota for non-service candidates to be admitted to super speciality courses.
The Government has the authority to determine which source the admission will come from.
A Single Judge held that in the remaining 50% quota, service candidates were eligible to be considered based on the previous year's prospectus.
This is not appropriate, and only the current year's policies should be taken into account, and previous year's guidelines should not be followed.
[Para 25, 33, 34 and 35]
The Constitution of India Articles 14, 16, and 226, and Evidence Act, 1872, Section 115 - Admission - Petitioners who participated in the written test knowing the terms and conditions of the prospectus are estopped from questioning the selection process.
[Para 46]
Constitution of India, Articles 226 - Selection process - Candidates who are eligible to apply in the service quota are allowed to challenge the selection process if their merit has not been considered, and another candidate of lesser merit has been chosen.
The Petitioners were not eligible to apply in the non-service quota and cannot question the selection made in that quota.
The challenge should also be limited to their respective quotas.
[Para 53]
