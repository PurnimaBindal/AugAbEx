"Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Fixed term appointment - Extension of term of contractual appointment - An ex-serviceman re-appointed to an ex-cadre post on contractual basis cannot claim to continue till he attains the age of superannuation if age bar is prescribed in service rules - Contractual appointment expires by efflux of time on expiry of its term and does not have protection of Articles 14 and 16 against such expiry.
