The Petitioner was accused of being absent from duty, which resulted in his removal from service.
He claimed to have left to seek medical help after handing over a leave application to a colleague since the dealing hand was unavailable.
The colleague supported the Petitioner's version of events.
However, given the Petitioner's past record of having 15 disciplinary actions against him, the disciplinary authority upheld the view that the application could be a fabrication.
This decision was considered reasonable based on the totality of circumstances and evidence, and the order was upheld.
