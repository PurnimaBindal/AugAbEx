National Insurance Company's Promotion Policy for Class-I Officers, Rules, Rules 7, 14 - Promotion as Deputy Manager - Criteria for promotion based on seniority, insurance qualifications, confidential report traits, performance, and growth potential - It has been decided that promotion of juniors will be based on their respective grading.
It is not feasible to argue that juniors have been promoted unlawfully and arbitrarily just because the petitioner was not able to be selected among the 31 chosen candidates.
The selection committee used its administrative discretion to grade officers for promotion.
Additionally, it should be noted that the promotion policy has been approved by the Hon'ble Apex Court, as evidenced by the case 2003(4) SCT 328 (SC).
