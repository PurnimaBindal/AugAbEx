The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 & 226 - Deputation - Absorption - The petitioner, who is an employee of BSF sent on deputation to Intelligence Bureau for five years, has not been repatriated to the parent organisation even after the expiry of five years.
The petitioner was refused to be granted a 'no objection certificate' for proposed absorption in Intelligence Bureau because he hasn't completed the mandatory 20 years of service - this decision was not improper.
It is within the parent department's discretion not to grant 'no objection certificate' in terms of their policy guidelines.
The fact that a wrong decision taken by BSF contrary to its own policy in a particular case cannot be a ground to direct the issuance of 'no objection certificate' in another case.
[Paras 9 & 15] B Deputation - Absorption in borrowing department - No one has a right to claim permanent absorption in another department.
Courts usually hesitate in issuing mandamus for the grant of a 'no objection certificate' unless it is shown that the parent department has acted capriciously or in an arbitrary manner amounting to violation of Articles 14, 15 & 16 of the Constitution.
The parent department has discretion not to grant a 'no objection certificate' in terms of their policy guidelines.
The issuance of a no objection certificate can also be denied where the parent organisation feels that it requires the service of an employee for administrative or operational requirement.
[Para 9] C The Constitution of India, Article 14 - Discrimination - Article 14 is a positive concept.
No direction can be issued on the plea of discrimination wherein the earlier decision itself was improper and wrong.
