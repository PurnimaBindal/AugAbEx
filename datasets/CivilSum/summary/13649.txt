Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 226 - Examination/Punishment/Natural Justice/Major Punishment - After the petitioner appeared in Combined Defence Service Examination held by the Union Public Service Commission, he was unjustly debarred from appearing in all the examinations to be held by Union Public Service Commission for five years - The petitioner was not given any notice and was not provided the opportunity of hearing- This amounts to a major punishment against the petitioner and completely ruined his career - The Government should have followed the principles of Natural Justice - The orders against the petitioner are arbitrary and violate Natural Justice principles - The court has quashed the orders and allowed the petition with costs of Rs. 2000/-.
