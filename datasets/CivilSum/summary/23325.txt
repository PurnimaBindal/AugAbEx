Constitution of India, Article 16 - Public Service Commission Business Rules (Jammu & Kashmir), Rule 37 - Qualification - Interview - Selection - Appointment - Seniority - Prospective Junior Engineers who were fully qualified at the time of interview, but whose results had not been declared at the time of submitting their applications, were entitled to be considered for appointment to the post of Junior Engineer.
The appellants became qualified on the dates of interview and obtained higher marks than the respondents, who were also appointed as Junior Engineers and subsequently promoted.
However, as the appellants were held to be eligible and on equitable considerations, it was unjust to place them junior to those who had scored lesser marks in the same examination.
[Paras 15, 18 and 23]
