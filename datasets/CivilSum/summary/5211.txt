The denial of compassionate appointment on the ground that the income of the family exceeds 60% of the gross salary drawn by the deceased is invalid.
The scheme's inclusion of 11% interest on terminal benefits in family income is unfair.
Lump sum benefits received by the family cannot be considered when determining their financial condition.
This scheme goes against the basic philosophy of socio-economic justice and the Apex Court's judgment in the Balbir Kaur and Umesh Kumar Nagpal case.
