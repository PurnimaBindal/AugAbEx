According to Sections 45(1)(b), 75, and 75(2)(b) of the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, an application under Section 75 pending cannot prevent E.S.I.C.
from realising the due amount, unless there is an injunction order stating otherwise.
Also, the amount already recovered by E.S.I.C.
cannot be ordered for refund by the ESI Court.
[Para 8]
Section 82 of the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, requires an appeal against an order of ESI Court without jurisdiction.
In case of such an order, even if there is an alternative remedy by way of an appeal, Article 227 of the Constitution of India can be invoked, if the impugned order lacks jurisdiction.
Also, no substantial question of law is involved in such a scenario that would make the order appealable.
