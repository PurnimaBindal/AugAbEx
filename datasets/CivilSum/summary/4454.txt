Constitution of India, Article 16 - Electricity Supply Act, 1948 - Regular post - Whether a vacancy can be filled by a deputationist or on contract - Incumbents deserving promotion are available - Appointment made on contract based on the Minister's letter - Board is not empowered by the Act and Regulation to make such appointment - The decision of the Board is deemed illegal, arbitrary, mala fide, and motivated by extraneous considerations, hence unsupportable.
