Constitution of India, Article 226 - Central Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, Rule 5 - Termination - Double Jeopardy - The petitioner was fined after being charged with lack of integrity and devotion to duty in an enquiry - The penalty was recovered from the petitioner's salary - However, the order of termination passed after the recovery of the penalty amounts to double jeopardy - The order has been quashed and the petitioner has been directed to be reinstated in their original post.
