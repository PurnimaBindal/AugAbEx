The Mizoram Judicial Service Rules, 1986, under Article 217(2)(a) of the Constitution of India, lay out the appointment process for Judges of the High Court.
Those eligible must have ten years of experience in Judicial Office, which refers to individuals who make judicial decisions in a professional capacity, without any executive control or discipline.
The Legal Remembrancer-cum-Secretary Law and Judicial is a Non-Judicial Office under the executive's control, making it unsuitable experience for appointment.
Any individual who has less than ten years of experience is ineligible for the position of High Court Judge, and appointing such a person would be illegal.
[Paras 22, 25, 26, 35, 36 and 37]
In exceptional cases where an individual is appointed despite not fulfilling the qualifications laid down in the Constitution, it is the duty of the Court to prevent the appointment of any ineligible or unqualified person as a Judge of the High Court by issuing a Writ for quashing the appointment.
[Para 28]
