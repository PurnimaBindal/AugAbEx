Haryana Service of Engineers, Class I, Public Works Department (Buildings and Road Branch), (Public Health Branch) and (Irrigation Branch) Act, 1955 (Act 20 of 1995), Sections 15, 5 and 12 - Cadre post - Held, the petitioner's seniority as Executive Engineer couldn't be altered under the new law.
Altering seniority is legally unsustainable and cannot be used to disturb someone's promotion.
