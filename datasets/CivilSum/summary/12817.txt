Civil Procedure Code, Section 60(1)(1) - L.I.C.
(Staff) Regulations, 1960, Regulation 37 - Suspension allowance - Government employees - Since Life Insurance Corporation is a Statutory Corporation, its employees are not entitled to the benefits of Section 60 of the Civil Procedure Code- The authority of the  "State" according to Article 12 of the Constitution is not all covered by Section 60(1)(1) CPC - Appellant can not argue that amounts liable to be deducted from his salary can not be deducted from his suspension allowance due to Regulation 37.
