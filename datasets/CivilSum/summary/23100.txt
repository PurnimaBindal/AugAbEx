In accordance with the Constitution of India, Articles 32 and 226, students of unrecognized and unauthorised educational institutions may appear in examinations conducted by the appellant and be accommodated in recognized institutions for further studies, if permitted by High Court on a writ petition.
However, it was held that doing so is equivalent to condoning the establishment of unauthorised institutions.
In addition, it was concluded that the respondents had no authority to seek a writ of mandamus and that the High Court had committed a clear legal error by ordering the appellant to permit the respondents' participation in the examination.
