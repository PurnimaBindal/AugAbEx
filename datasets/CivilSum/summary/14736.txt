Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 12 - Petitioner cited Piara Singh's case to argue for regularisation of his services - The court ordered respondents to address the petitioner's claim within six months and maintain status quo - However, the petitioner's services were terminated before the status quo was granted - As a result, the respondents did not commit contempt - The petition has been dismissed.
