Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Annual Confidential Reports - Adverse entries - Promotion - If promotion is denied due to adverse entries in the service record, it would be considered an administrative act of recording ACR - High Court cannot replace its opinion with that of the administrative body when the ACRs are based on relevant considerations and supported by the material on record - The court cannot reverse the administrative decision and is limited to examining the decision-making process.
