Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Section 43(d)(i) - Voluntary retirement - An employee who voluntarily retired and was relieved from service cannot assert a right to be reinstated.

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Section 43(d)(i) - A disciplinary inquiry has been carried out against an employee and a penalty of 10% deduction from pension for one year has been imposed - The scope of judicial review is very limited to the imposition of penalty as a result of disciplinary proceedings - The court can intervene in the punishment only if it deems the penalty to be shockingly disproportionate to the charges found to be proved - In such an instance, the court will refer the matter back to the disciplinary authority for consideration of the punishment - In an appropriate situation, the court can impose a lesser penalty itself to avoid delay.
