Intervenor Arjun Bhandari, Advocate cites Delhi University Act,1922 sections 30 and 31 along with Delhi University Ordinance XVIII(4)(4) to argue that the function of the Selection Committee is similar to that of the Public Service Commission.
Governing bodies are liable to accept recommended selections unless there are justifiable reasons not to, which is not the case if they merely disagree with the recommendations.
In another instance, an allegation without proof about a candidate's style of functioning, even with a 21 year clean career of service, lacks weight at the insistence of a teacher when the Principal of the College agrees with the selection.
Lastly, Bhandari notes that the absence of a member at a Selection Committee meeting does not invalidate the deliberations of the entire committee as long as all members are informed about the meeting.
This was established in AIR 1976 SC 1441 and AIR 1972 SC 1812.
