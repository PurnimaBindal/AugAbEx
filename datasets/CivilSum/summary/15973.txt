Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pension - Policy matter - Cut-off date - The grant of pension is a policy matter with financial implications - Universities must establish a cut-off date based on their available financial resources - The selection of a specific cut-off date cannot be deemed unlawful or arbitrary - Using the revised pay scales date for government employees as the cut-off date is a reasonable decision.
