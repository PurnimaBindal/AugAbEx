The recruitment of Assistant Engineer is being carried out through Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board under the Employment Exchange (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1956 as per Constitution of India, Article 226.
An advertisement was placed in two well recognised newspapers, although not having the highest circulation.
However, this is not enough to make the recruitment process invalid.
Furthermore, EE Act is not applicable to the recruitment of vacancies that are filled through Union or State Public Service Commission.
Thus, the petition was rejected.
