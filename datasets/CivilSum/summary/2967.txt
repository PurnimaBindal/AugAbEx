Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularization - Compassionate appointment - Petitioners, who work as cooks or helpers in a hostel on a daily wage basis, want regularization after five years of service.
They are dependent on the GOMs as they are the sons of deceased employees.
The Government has taken over the management of the hostel.
However, they are not working against any sanctioned post and do not satisfy the conditions of GOMs.
The Single Judge ordered regularization, sanction of post and compassionate appointment for Petitioners.
This order is not in conformity with basic principles of law that regularization is not a mode of recruitment.
Therefore, the mode of appointment, qualification, and other factors must be considered before appointing a person to public post.
No order can be passed unless the posts are held to be vacant, and Petitioners satisfy the conditions of GOMs.
The appeal is allowed accordingly.
[Paras 15 and 19]
