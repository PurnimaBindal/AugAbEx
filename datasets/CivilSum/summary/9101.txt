The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Termination - Date of termination - Management can establish misconduct of employees prior to Labour Court proceedings - Labour Court found the workman guilty of misconduct and upheld the termination - Termination is effective from the date of the award of the Labour Court - The workman is deemed to be in service until the date of the award and is entitled to full back wages until that date - AIR 1990 SC 2174: 1991(1) SCT 86 (SC) applied.
[Para 2] B Article 226 of the Constitution of India - If the findings of the Labour Tribunal are based on evidence, they cannot be challenged in writ jurisdiction.
