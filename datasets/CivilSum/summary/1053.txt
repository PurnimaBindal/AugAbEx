Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-B - Back Wages - An order was passed by the Single Judge awarding back wages to the Workman based on Section 17-B of the Act.
The Employer is challenging the award claiming that the Workman had been operating a business as a contractor.
However, it was determined that the earnings from self-employment are not relevant for the purpose of Section 17-B.
