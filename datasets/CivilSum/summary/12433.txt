The petitioners, who were initially recruited as Sanitary Inspectors with the additional powers of Food Inspectors, challenged the withdrawal of their Food Inspector powers as illegal and violative of Article 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution.
However, the Court held that the petitioners were not adversely affected in any manner, including their status, salary, seniority, and chances of promotion.
The conferring and taking away of duties from a particular person in a cadre cannot be considered discriminatory under Article 16, and it is the state's prerogative to decide what job is given to a particular person.
Hence, the writ petition was dismissed.
