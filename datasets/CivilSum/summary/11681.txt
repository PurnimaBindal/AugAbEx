Punjab Education Service, Class III, School Cadre Rules, 1955, Rule 14 - Petitioners sought regularisation in service based on a policy decision by the Punjab government - A Single Judge allowed the petition and ordered regularisation of their service - The Appellant/State challenged, stating that the petitioners lacked the necessary qualifications - The court upheld the previous ruling, while also acknowledging that certain conditions could be imposed during regularisation - The case was remitted to the concerned authority to reconsider - However, given the petitioners' 25 years of experience, it was determined that any imposed conditions could not exceed 27 years of experience - At this stage, conditions upon the petitioners were deemed unnecessary - Appeal dismissed.
