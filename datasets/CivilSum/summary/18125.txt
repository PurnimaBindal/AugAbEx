According to the Military Pension Regulations - Medical Guide 2002 for Military Pensions Regulations, Paras 9 and 10, special family pension is applicable in cases of diseases attributable to military service, such as Hodgkins disease.
In a case where the son of the petitioner died from Hodgkin disease and the petitioner claimed special family pension after 30 years, the petitioner is entitled to receive the special family pension.
