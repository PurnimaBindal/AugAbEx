The Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol.
I, Part-I, Rule 3.14 states that the method of appointment of an employee must be established by record, whether it was direct or by deputation.
If an employee applied through proper channels against a post advertised by another department and resigned subject to his appointment against the same, and the employer also accepted his resignation subject to his appointment and confirmation, kept his lien to ensure protection in case of non-confirmation, then no deputation allowance can be paid.
If an employee's service is terminated by the subsequent employer, the employee cannot be said to be on deputation.
Service of a deputationist cannot be terminated but has to be only repatriated, and action can only be taken by the parent department.

Equivalence of posts cannot be determined simply by the nomenclature of the posts.
The question must be decided by reference to various factors like pay scale and hierarchy.
On the recommendations of the Pay Commission, designations were changed, but pay scale and revision of pay cannot be denied equivalent to corresponding posts.
