National Council of Teacher Education Act, 1993 - For the selection and ad hoc appointment of lecturers, valid advertisement of the posts and their selection by the University through a constituted selection committee is required, along with the approval of the University.
Just approval of the Council is inadequate, rendering the appointments invalid.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regarding admissions, the creation of additional seats in Technical/Engineering institutions must not happen without the proper approval and sanction of the competent authority under the Rules.
Otherwise, the Court cannot give directions that defy the rules and validate such an illegality.
Educational institutions who breach the rules by admitting students into unapproved seats jeopardize their careers, and they must be accountable for the damages.
However, the NTCE granted permission for creating additional seats illegally, and the university accepted fees, among other things, for those seats.
The students expected that these seats had approval, so the University should announce their results as well.
