Constitution of India, Articles 141 and 142 - Judgments and precedents - Overruling of judgments - Doctrine of perspective overruling - Precedents cannot be retrospectively overruled, but can only be overruled prospectively - The doctrine of perspective overruling doesn't apply if the judgment was reviewed, as the review order will relate back - There can be no perspective overruling without it being mentioned in the particular decision itself - The doctrine of binding precedent plays a crucial role in ensuring consistency and certainty in judicial decisions.
