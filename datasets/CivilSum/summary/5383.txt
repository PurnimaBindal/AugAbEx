Constitution of India, Articles 16(4-A) and 226 - Reservation Policy of Andhra Bank Chapter 9, para 92 - Reservations - Promotion - The petitioner believed that reservation policy in relation to promotion avenues existed and is protected under Article 16(4-A) - The petitioner argued that reservations are provided by reason of Article 16(4-A) and a duty is cast upon the respondent to continue and extend the same - However, it was held that Article 16(4) does not confer any right on the petitioner and there is no constitutional duty imposed on the Government to make reservation - The article is enabling provision confers discretionary power on the state to make the reservation either at the stage of initial recruitment or at the stage of promotion - The court cannot issue a writ directing the respondents to provide for such a policy unless a reservation policy exists.
