Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Admission - Education - Dependent Certificate - Purpose of - The purpose of a certificate is to ensure that only a genuine person is considered for admission against the reserved seats - Certificates issued by the Sub Divisional Magistrate and Additional Magistrate - Additional District Magistrate exercises the same powers as District Magistrate - Parents of the petitioners killed in terrorist activities/or were riot victims - Petitioners could not be denied admission for want of proper certificate - Certificate held to be proper - Direction issued to consider the claims of the petitioners for admission.
