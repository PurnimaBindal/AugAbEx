Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Ad hoc appointment - The status of an ad hoc appointee is not higher than that of a temporary employee - Termination of services can be done in accordance with the terms of appointment - No vested right to continue until regular appointments are made and employer's power cannot be restricted - However, if exercise of power is arbitrary or malafide, courts can examine allegation.
[Paras 8 and 9] B.
Employment Exchanges (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959, Section 4 - Notification of vacancies - Ad hoc appointments - Failure to notify vacancies to employment exchanges and advertise them to the public would make ad hoc appointments invalid - Termination of service of such an ad hoc employee in accordance with appointment order does not warrant any fault.
