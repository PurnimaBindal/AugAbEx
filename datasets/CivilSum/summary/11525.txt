Constitution of India 1950, Articles 16 & 226 - Posting order - Petitioner who had taken the Punjabi examination before cutoff date and whose results were declared close to cutoff date could present original testimonials before joining as per the appointment letter - Respondent's claim that petitioner didn't have essential qualifications at the time of application not valid - Order overturned - The respondents had no reason to prevent the petitioner from assuming duties.
