For Respondent No. 1: Ashok Aggarwal, Sr. Advocate, Rakesh Garg, with him A Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10(1) (c) - Appropriate Government/Territorial jurisdiction - The reference to the Labour Court under Section 10 can only be made by the Government within whose territorial jurisdiction the workman was working before termination - Award made by a Government with no jurisdiction must be set aside.
[Para 5] B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Jurisdiction - The objection to jurisdiction can be made at any stage.
[Para 6] C. Termination - Re-instatement - The workman was re-instated in compliance with the award of the Labour Court but then terminated again - Subsequent termination is not disputed - The workman is no longer entitled to the relief of re-instatement - Only monetary benefits can be awarded prior to subsequent termination.
[Para 7] D. Industrial Disputes Act, Section 33-C (2) - Wages - Claim based on an award of re-instatement with all benefits - Award made without jurisdiction - No objection with regard to jurisdiction ever raised before the Labour Court - Claim under Section 33-C(2) is maintainable - Back wages can only be granted from the date of demand notice if delayed unreasonably - The workman was granted only compensation in lieu of all claims.
