Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 21, 39(d) and 226 - Regularisation - Balsevikas seek minimum regular scale paid to some regular Balsevikas - Petitioners received training from the institute and earn a consolidated salary of Rs. 400/- per month - Contention that individuals who completed training in earlier sessions are not being hired on a regular basis despite performing identical duties as regularly employed Balsevikas - No provision for petitioners to receive equal pay for equal work - Respondents argue regular appointments made after assessing suitability of individuals by competent authority - Petitioners haven't shown qualifications nor discharge duties of regular Balsevikas - Allegations of petitioner vague and uncertain - Method used by respondent No. 1 unwarranted and unknown to law - Directions for concerned authority to establish guidelines/policy/Rules and Regulations for employee appointments and regularisation.
