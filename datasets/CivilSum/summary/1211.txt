Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - A.P.
Ministerial Service Rules, Rule 6 - Promotion by exemption from passing departmental test - The Proviso to Ad hoc rule in G.O.Ms.
Nos. 165 dt. 22.4.1997 and 225 dt. 1851999 that restricts the benefit of exemption to only those who have not been promoted even once after their initial appointment in service applies to both selection and non-selection posts - The Division Bench's contrary view in Rednam Satyanarayana Murthy's case is incorrect and has been overruled - Interpreting the rule in that manner would defeat the very purpose it seeks to achieve.
