Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Enquiry - Natural justice - An enquiry was conducted into whether the petitioner filed a false affidavit for registration with the Employment Exchange - However, the termination order did not provide any reference to the allegations, the enquiry, findings, the petitioner's reply, or the reasons for termination - The court held that the impugned order violates natural justice principles, and therefore, it should be quashed.
