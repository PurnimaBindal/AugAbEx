Constitution of India, Article 14 - Probationer - Termination of service of a probationer - Natural justice - If a probationer's services are terminated due to misconduct, without holding an enquiry, the termination order cannot be classified as a simple punitive order.
Even probationers are entitled to protection against such imputations.
The authority must follow the principles of natural justice before taking any such action and must provide a well-reasoned explanation.
