Petitioners, who were working as Teachers/Headmasters in the schools run by local bodies/District Boards before being taken over by the Punjab Govt. are entitled to count their service rendered prior to taking over of their schools towards length of their service for the purpose of fixation of pension according to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
The High Court and LPA have already granted a similar claim and SLP of the state have also been dismissed.
The petitioners cannot be denied the same relief as per the decision of the court.
Petition allowed.
