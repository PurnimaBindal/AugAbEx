Gujarat Service of Engineers Class I Recruitment Rules, 1979, Rule 4 - Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 14 - Waiting list prepared - Selected candidates who do not join can be replaced by candidates from a waiting list - Waiting list must be under rules or for a reasonable period prescribed under rules or for a reasonable period where no specific period is prescribed in the rules - Notification is for a specific examination and not for future vacancies unless a policy decision is taken by the government.
