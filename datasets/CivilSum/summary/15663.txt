Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical Reimbursement - Unapproved hospital - If a person receives treatment from an unapproved hospital, they are not entitled to medical reimbursement, unless approved hospitals are unavailable, or the patient cannot receive immediate treatment or the approved hospital does not meet the patient's needs.
The petitioner was not entitled to reimbursement for treatment from an unapproved hospital.
