Constitution of India, Article 226 - Kurukshetra Urban Cooperative Staff Service Rules, 1990, Rule 29(a) - Enquiry Officer - The petitioner claimed that the competent authority did not charge-sheet him and the enquiry officer was not appointed by the competent authority.
Additionally, they alleged malafides against respondents No. 6 and 7.
The respondents argued that the Board of Directors had not made a final decision and that no malafides had been alleged against them.
They also claimed that the petitioner had the option to appeal to the Registrar if the Board of Directors punished him.
Respondent No. 7 was authorized to take disciplinary action against the petitioner by the Board of Directors.
The disciplinary enquiry against the petitioner was initiated by the competent authority.
