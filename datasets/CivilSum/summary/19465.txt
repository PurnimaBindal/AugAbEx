Constitution of India, Article 14 - Moter Vehicles Act, 1939, Section 7 (7) (1) - Driver - Appointment as driver - Qualifications - A driver who holds a licence to drive both heavy duty and light duty vehicles may be qualified to be appointed as a driver of light motor vehicle as well.
However, an individual who has only passed a test in driving a heavy goods vehicle cannot be appointed as a light motor vehicle driver.
