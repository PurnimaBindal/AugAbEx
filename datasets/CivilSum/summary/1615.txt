The termination of temporary meter readers, Respondents 1 and 2, was found by the Labour Court to not comply fully with Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
As a result, the Labour Court ordered their reinstatement with full back wages and continuity of service.
However, this award is now being challenged as regularly employed meter readers have taken their places.
Therefore, instead of reinstatement, a lump sum compensation of Rs. 60,000/- to each worker would be just and appropriate, from which nothing shall be deducted.
This is in accordance with sections 2, 7, and 8 of the Constitution of India and the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F
