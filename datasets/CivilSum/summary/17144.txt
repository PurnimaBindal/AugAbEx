Constitution of India, Article 21 - Medical re-imbursement - Entitlement - The petitioner worked for the Punjab Government before joining the Punjab Education Board.
Although he was allowed to join as a fresh employee, he cannot claim reimbursement from the Punjab Government anymore.
Even though he retired from the Board, he is not entitled to medical reimbursement from either the State Government or the Board as the Pension Scheme only became effective from 141991.
