According to Articles 226 and 300-A of the Constitution of India, Disability pension must be granted based on the opinion of a medical expert.
The Medical Board in this case found that the petitioner's disability was aggravated by military service.
Even though the disability was assessed at only 30 percent, an instruction dated 3112001 deems disabilities under 50% to be more than 50%.
Therefore, the petitioner must be considered for the pension.
PCDA (Pension) cannot override the medical expert's opinion, and the respondent is directed to release the disability pension by considering the disability as 50%.
