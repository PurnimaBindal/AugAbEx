Article 226 of the Indian Constitution and Rule 1444 of the Punjab Police Service Rules, 1934 state that during ongoing departmental proceedings, the Inquiry Officer and punishing authority must determine whether an employee's objectionable statements to the press, made in the capacity of a union leader, violate the prohibition under the rule - the writ court will not consider the merits of the charge during pendency of the proceedings.
[Paras 6, 7, 8]
Rule 14.44 of the Punjab Police Service Rules, 1934 and the Indian Constitution's Article 226 specify that disciplinary proceedings and criminal proceedings can occur at the same time, except when they stem from the same facts and evidence.
Disciplinary proceedings should not be stalled on claims that they will prejudice criminal proceedings.
[Paras 9 to 11]
