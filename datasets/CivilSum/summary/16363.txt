Constitution of India, Article 16 - Indian Railway Establishment Manual, Rules 127, 219(j) and (k) - Promotion - Cancellation of - The petitioner was selected, empanelled for promotion, sent for training and appointed to the promoted post.
Following revaluation, two of the petitioner's marks were reduced, making them ineligible for viva voce and causing their name to be deleted from the provisional panel.
Though the petitioner is not guilty of fraud, misrepresentation, or a wrongful act, and there are no Rules/Guidelines for the revaluation of answer scripts, the mere change in marks cannot be grounds for canceling a promotion in light of the doctrine of finality and estoppel.
