According to Army Pension Regulations, Regulations 173 and 423, a serviceman who suffered a loss of eyesight can apply for disability pension.
In this case, the petitioner was invalidated out of service after 20 years due to low medical category and 20% disability because of depreciation of his eyesight.
The petitioner was exposed to snow without snow goggles which aggravated the situation, and no specialist treatment was provided.
The Medical Board declared it as an "old case of eye problem" without considering the petitioner's duty circumstances.
The petitioner cannot be denied disability pension as his problem is attributed and aggravated by the nature of his duties during service.
