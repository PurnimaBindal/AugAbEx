In relation to Civil Appeal No. 5712 of 1995, which arose from SLP 1289 of 1995, the Constitution of India's Article 16 pertains to Appointment.
Compassionate appointment is allowed, but in the case of a husband's death in harness where only the wife was appointed on compassionate grounds, promotion was allowed to U.D.C.
The deceased employee's brother, who was not dependent on him financially, has no entitlement to employment due to the death of the employee in harness.
