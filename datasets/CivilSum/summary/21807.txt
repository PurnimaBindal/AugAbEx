Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(j) - Industry - Board - According to the interpretation of the term "Industry" in the Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board's case, an organization with both employers and employees that provides useful work for others must be considered an "industry" under the Industrial Disputes Act- whether this sweeping test was intended by the Industrial Disputes Act is questionable and should be revisited by a larger bench - The Coir Board, Ernakulam's purpose is to promote the coir industry, open markets for it, and provide facilities to make coir industry products more marketable, but it is not established to operate any industry - Due to its predominant purpose, it cannot be considered an "Industry," but in light of the Full Bench judgment in the Bangalore Water Supply Board's case, the matter should be referred to a larger bench for reconsideration.
