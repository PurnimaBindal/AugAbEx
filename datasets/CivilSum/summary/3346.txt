Constitution of India, Article 226 - Locus standi - Unions and employee associations have no right to challenge a promotion of employees from another group simply because those employees are not members of their union.
It is assumed that individuals affected by such promotions are not aggrieved if they do not personally challenge the promotions.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Policy for promotions of staff at General Insurance Corporation of India for the post of Assistant Administrative Officer - Ranking list - A single ranking list in descending order of total marks obtained by selected candidates should be prepared along with a contingency list equal to 20% of the declared vacancies.
The policy does not allow for separate select and waiting lists which could result in candidates from one particular state being deprived of promotions due to lack of open vacancies and unfilled slots being carried forward to the next year of selection.
