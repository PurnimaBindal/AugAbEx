VSNL (Conduct), Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1992, Rule 29 Clause (iv) and (vi)(a) - Disciplinary proceeding - Denial of Assistance by an advocate deemed unsustainable - The Enquiry Officer in question is a practicing advocate, has been the standing counsel for Union of India and has also worked as legal manager - In the event the charged allegations are proved, there could be a serious penalty - The Enquiry Officer can be fairly considered a legal person, hence it is just and fair to allow the petitioner to engage a legal practitioner for defense purposes without any adverse effect on the respondents.
