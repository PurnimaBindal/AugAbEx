Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Dismissal - Unauthorised absence from duty - Enquiry - The Disciplinary Authority dismissed the petitioner from service and categorized the period of absence as "leave without pay" - The petitioner challenged that the absence period cannot be considered a ground for dismissal as it was treated as "leave without pay" - However, the court ruled that the dismissal order was lawful - The regularization of leave without pay only served to maintain accurate records of service and did not alter or nullify the order of dismissal from service.
