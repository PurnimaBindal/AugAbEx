Army Pension Regulations, Regulations 173(A) and 179 - Army Service Pension Rules, Annexure II, Para 7 - Entitlement Rules for Casualty Pensionary Awards, 1982, Rule 27(c) - Disability Pension - The medical board determined the disability to be 40%, leading to the granting of a disability pension.
However, CCDA (Pensions) later reduced the disability and stopped the pension.
CCDA is not authorized to unilaterally reduce the disability without a re-examination by the medical board.
The pensioner is entitled to arrears of the disability pension from the date it was stopped.
