Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission to Postgraduates Courses in State Medical Colleges - Waiting list - When a candidate does not join the course, authorities should offer the seat to the next person on the waiting list - Petitioner was on waiting list but was not offered a seat - Petitioner allowed to join the course on provisional basis subject to result of writ petition - After completing the course and examination, petitioner's admission must be regularized and result of MD declared - Petition allowed.
