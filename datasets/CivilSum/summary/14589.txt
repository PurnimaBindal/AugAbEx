The Punjabi University, Patiala Ordinance 20 states that there must be a one-year gap between a candidate's Second Professional Examination and their Final Professional Examination of the MBBS Course at Medical College, Patiala.
This gap is counted from the date of the examination and not from the date of the result.
Students should not be penalized for any delay in result declaration, and once a student has passed an examination, their result should relate back to the date of examination.
[Para 10]
The Punjabi University, Patiala Ordinance 20 (ii) relates to the requirement for a 4-1/2 academic year period of certified study before a student can appear in the Final Professional Examination of the MBBS Course at Medical College.
This requirement must be read alongside Clause (2) of Sub Clause (iv) which requires a 75% attendance of lectures delivered.
If a student attends 75% of the lectures delivered, they should be eligible to appear in the examination.
[Para 11]
The Punjabi University, Patiala Ordinance 20 (ii) and 20 (iv) (2) cannot be applied discriminately.
Even if a student joined the MBBS Course three months later than others and has completed only 75% of the lectures, they cannot be held ineligible when other students who joined at the same time were allowed to appear in the Final Professional Examination of the MBBS Course without having completed the 4-1/2 years of study.
