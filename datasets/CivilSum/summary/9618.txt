Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Qualifications/Experience/Regularisation - Although the petitioner did not meet the necessary qualifications during her initial appointment, she had been teaching the relevant subject for over 15 years - As time passed and she gained more experience, she acquired the necessary expertise in the subject - Denying regularisation based on this ground after such a long time would not be justified.
This follows the decision in AIR 1990 SC 371.
