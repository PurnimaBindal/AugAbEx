Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Schedule 2, Item 3 - Syndicate Bank Employees Pension Regulations (1995) Regulation 48, Proviso 3 - Departmental proceedings - Validity of initiating proceedings against a pensioner who caused pecuniary loss to the bank - The employee retired after committing the irregularity and receiving pensionary benefits - Charge-sheeted within 4 years of the misconduct - Argument that departmental proceedings were initiated after the period of limitation - Petitioner challenging the penal part of the Pension Regulation as a pensioner - The court held that since the employee retired on attaining superannuation, it cannot be considered as delayed initiation of proceedings - The irregularity committed by the petitioner is classified as grave misconduct - The petitioner cannot benefit from the Regulations and also challenge the same - The petition was dismissed.
[Paras 14-18]
