Article 14 of the Indian Constitution outlines the selection and appointment process for assistant-teachers in primary schools.
Candidates residing in a particular district are preferred to cater to the educational needs of that district.
The panel of candidates is district-wise and only meant to provide an eligibility list of qualified and trained teachers arranged in chronological order.
Such a panel does not create any vested or indefeasible rights for appointment.
[Para 10] B.
The cancellation of a panel prepared after selection for posts of teachers, with some appointments already made, is considered fair and reasonable to save the appointments of teachers who were already appointed.
This decision does not offend Article 14 and is not arbitrary.
