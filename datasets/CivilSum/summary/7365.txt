Haryana Irrigation Department Signallers (Group `C') Rules, 1985, Rule 9(c) - Recruitment - Promotion - The 1985 Rules have no provision for appointment by promotion, and ad hoc promotions to such posts violate Rule 9(c).
In any democratic society, the rule of law is an important aspect that requires courts' rulings to be respected by everyone.
The authorities have been ordered to replace the ad hoc promotees with direct recruits and complete the recruitment process within the prescribed time limit.
