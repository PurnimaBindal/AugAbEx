Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service of Employees) Act, 1974, Sections 3 and 4 - Termination of temporary/ad hoc employee - Filing an appeal with the Director or the Tribunal is not allowed - In the absence of an alternate remedy of appeal, a writ petition can be filed against wrongful arbitrary termination.
[Paras 11 & 12] B Punjabi University Calender, 1994, Chapter 20, Regulation 4 - Temporary appointment - Appointment as a Teacher as time gap arrangement - Approval of the University is not necessary - Termination of the appointment cannot be supported using reasons other than those in the order - The termination on different reasons would mean that it was made arbitrarily, which is unsustainable.
