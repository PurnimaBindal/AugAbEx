Constitution of India, Article 14 - Waiver - Estoppel - Arrears of pay - Promotion - Affidavit - The plaintiff was required to provide a attested affidavit stating that he would not demand arrears of pay should he be promoted.
- The plaintiff did so of his own will and without any coercion or undue influence.
- As a result, the plaintiff is unable to request the arrears of pay after submitting the affidavit.
[Paras 9 & 11]
