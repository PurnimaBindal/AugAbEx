According to the J&K Govt Medical Education (Gazetted) Service Recruitment Rules (SRO- 225/88), a candidate applying for the post of Lecturer must have a minimum of two years' experience as Registrar, Tutor, Demonstrator, or Senior Resident in a medical college or recognized teaching medical institution recognized by the M.C.I.
or the University of Jammu/Kashmir.
However, the petitioner did not have any such experience and was only a Resident Teaching Fellow at PGI with some teaching experience.
Hence, he cannot claim to fulfill the prescribed eligibility clause.
[Paras 8 and 9]

The J&K Medical Education (Gazetted) Service Recruitment Rules (SRO - 225- 88) and the Commission's (Business and Procedure) Rules, 1980, Rule 36 state that candidates can be provisionally called for an interview in relaxation of their teaching experience, if found suitable.
However, the PSC had to retract its decision to call petitioner and other candidates for interview provisionally and to consider their case for selection in relaxation of their teaching experience because it had received a representation from a candidate which persuaded PSC to re-advertise the posts.
The decision of the PSC to re-advertise the posts cannot be faulted as it would be discriminatory to other candidates who had not applied for these posts merely because they fell short of experience at the relevant time.
Hence, the petitioner cannot claim selection merely because he was interviewed.
[Paras 10, 11 and 12]
