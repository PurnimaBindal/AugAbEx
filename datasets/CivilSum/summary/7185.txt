Constitution of India, Article 226 - Suspension following criminal case - Claim for backwages and service benefits after acquittal on benefit of doubt - Employer should consider the nature of the charge, ignoring acquittal, when determining the issue - Denial of backwages due to pending corruption charges is not illegal - An order depriving continuity of service benefits for more than 11 years without reason is arbitrary and oppressive - Consequential orders to be passed.
[Paras 9, 10, 11]
