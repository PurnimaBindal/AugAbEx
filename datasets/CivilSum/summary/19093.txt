The Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 226 and the Army Regulations of 1962 include provisions for age benefits and promotions that can increase an individual's tenure.
Government circulars provide details about age benefits that Army personnel can avail themselves of by exercising the options mentioned therein.
The petitioners have indeed exercised such options and received age benefits as a result.
Although the circulars represent an amendment to the rules regarding an increase in the age of tenure, nothing indicates that promotions would be denied.
The court held that if an individual is promoted in the normal way by exercising an option, it cannot deprive them of the benefits of promotion and an increase in service tenure or an extension in the age of retirement.
