Constitution, of India, Article 226 - Date of birth - Correction of date of birth - The service book of the petitioner incorrectly records a wrong date of birth - Despite the fact that there is no declaration made by the petitioner, it is reasonable to change the date of birth as supported by documents provided - The authorities must change the date of birth of the petitioner based on the school leaving certificate and adjust the age of superannuation accordingly.
[Paras 2 to 5]
