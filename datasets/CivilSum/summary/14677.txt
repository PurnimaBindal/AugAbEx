Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Employment Exchanges (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959 - Registration with employment exchange - Eligible individuals are not required to register with the Employment Exchange - The right to compete for a public service cannot be restricted if an eligible person's name is not sponsored by the Employment Exchange authorities - This would violate Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
