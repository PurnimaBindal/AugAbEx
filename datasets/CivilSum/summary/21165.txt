Advocates Act, 1961, Section 38 - Appeal - Lesser punishment - Right and locus-standi of Bar Council to appeal - The Bar Council fulfills a dual role as both prosecutor through its Executive Committee and quasi-judicial body through its Disciplinary Committee, thus making the State Bar Council an "aggrieved person" eligible to appeal under Section 38 of the Act.

Advocates Act, 1961, Section 38 - Professional Misconduct - Gravity of misconduct - Misappropriation of client's money by an advocate - Lesser punishment - The misappropriation of client's funds by an advocate, even in compelling circumstances, is a severe form of professional misconduct.
Excuses such as being in dire need at the time of the misappropriation are not accepted.
The failure to address misconduct that is not bona fide and is dishonest could lead to a loss of confidence in the legal system by the general public.
