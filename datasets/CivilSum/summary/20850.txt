Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 4 - Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions Act, 1989, Sections 2, 7, 16, 29 - Rajasthan Non- Government Educational Institutions (Recognition, Grant in Aid and Service Conditions Etc) Rules, 1993, Rules 9 to 22, 82 - Aided Educational Institution - Payment of Gratuity - Whether to be included in grant-in-aid calculation - Rule 82 states that employees of aided educational institutions are entitled to gratuity - Rule 14 specifies that gratuity is not part of 'actual salary' - Under Rule 9, the State Government may authorize grants under four categories that do not include gratuity - Therefore, in light of the aforementioned, gratuity as defined by the Act and Rules cannot be considered in the computation of payable grants.
