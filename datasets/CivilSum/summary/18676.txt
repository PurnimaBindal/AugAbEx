The petitioner challenged a notice issued by the State Contract Labour Advisory Committee for a proposed visit to their factory, citing the term "Appropriate Government" under Section 2(a) of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970 as a basis for their authority.
The petitioner is a Government of India undertaking with 100% shares held by the Central Government, making it under the jurisdiction of the Central Board as outlined in Section 3 and 4 of the Act.
Therefore, the State Advisory Committee had no jurisdiction, and the writ petition was allowed.
