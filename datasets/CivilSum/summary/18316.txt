Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dismissal - Enquiry - Principles of natural justice - A Sramik of West Bengal State Electricity Board admitted to printing and using false money receipts, collecting money from consumers, and causing financial loss and loss of reputation to the electricity board.
The disciplinary authority decided to impose a penalty of dismissal from service and after examining the evidence, agreed with the findings of the enquiry officer.
Despite the charge of breach of principles of natural justice, the enquiry was deemed valid and the decision of the Disciplinary Authority was upheld.
The petition challenging the decision was dismissed.
[Paras 18 to 20]
