Probation of Offenders Act, 1958, Section 12 - Dismissal - Disciplinary proceeding - Dismissal of employee after disciplinary proceeding - Although a criminal case was initiated on the same charges, the employee was convicted and released after due admonition using Section 3 of the Act - Employee argues that he is entitled to reinstatement under Section 12 as he had not suffered disqualification - Court holds that Section 12 only applies if an employee is dismissed solely on the basis of a conviction by a criminal court.
[Para 17]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dismissal - An order of dismissal based on disciplinary proceedings cannot be overturned by an acquittal or release on admonition in a criminal trial on same charges.
