University Grants Commission Act, 1956, Sections 2 and 26(1) - University Grants Commission (Qualifications required of a person to be appointed to the teaching staff of the University and institutions affiliated to it) Regulations, 1991, Regulation 2 – Qualifications of teachers - Relaxation of qualifications - Eligibility test - Notification of decision of UGC - U/s 26(1) of the Act, only an amendment of Regulations is required to be notified, not a decision of UGC taken under the Regulations - Universities are obligated to implement a decision taken by the UGC to relax qualifications for teachers who were working prior to the enforcement of the 1991 Rules and after it was circulated - Candidacy rejection of a lecturer for consideration for a permanent post on the grounds of not passing eligibility test is illegal if the UGC decision for relaxation of the said test's passing condition already applies to the petitioner - Petitioner meets all the requirements for the post of lecturer.
