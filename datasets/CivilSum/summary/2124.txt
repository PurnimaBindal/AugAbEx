Constitution of India, Article 226 - Seniority list - Challenge as to - The principle of erosion of rights by the passage of time has been well established - Seniority that has been settled long ago cannot be unsettled after a number of years - Petitioner lost the right to challenge irregular seniority assignment to juniors due to complacency over time - Seniority recognized cannot be brushed aside at the whim of others - Authorities directed to keep the list up to date, renew it every year and make a supplementary list for new appointees approved as per rules.
[Paras 4, 18]
