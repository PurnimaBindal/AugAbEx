Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(1) and 2(j) - Industry - Workman - The activities of Circut House and Punjab Bhawan in the National Capital cannot be determined whether they fall within the definition of 'Industry' U/s 2(j).
The Appropriate Govt. at the reference stage cannot make such a decision, and any attempt to decline the reference on these grounds is wholly without jurisdiction.
The labor court should assess this issue based on the evidence presented by the parties after the reference, and rejecting a reference on these grounds is not sustainable.
