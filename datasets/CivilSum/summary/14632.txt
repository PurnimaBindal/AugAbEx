As per the Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226/227, a job advertisement specified a retirement age of 60 years for the Registrar position.
However, the order of appointment stated "re-employment for two years which can be extended further."
Before the initial term of two years ended, the petitioner filed a writ with the Court, arguing that since they had not turned 60, the respondent should not have the right to retire them.
The Court allowed the petitioner to continue in service while issuing a notice of motion.
Eventually, the petition was disposed of with a directive that the respondent was authorized to conduct a new selection for the position, but the petitioner was entitled to remain in service until such time.
Furthermore, the petitioner should be permitted to compete for the position if and when it is freshly advertised.
