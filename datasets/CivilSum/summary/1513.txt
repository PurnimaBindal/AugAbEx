Sections 10 & 29 of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970 pertain to regularisation.
Workmen who are engaged as contract labour for perennial jobs may be regularized.
It was found by the Tribunal that such workmen had been engaged in a camouflaged manner and that the contractor was only a veil between management and workmen.
The regularisation award was thus upheld.

Articles 226 & 227 of the Constitution of India regulate judicial review.
High courts may intervene if there is mala fide or perversity, but would not interfere with factual findings.
