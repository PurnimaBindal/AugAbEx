The appointment and selection process for the post of Higher Grade Assistant, as per the Life Insurance Corporation of India Class III and Class IV Employees (Promotion) Rules, 1987, must follow the instructions outlined in No. 6(2) and 10.
However, the respondents in this case called 95 candidates for 12 vacancies in a ratio of 1:8, which goes against the 1:5 ratio stated in instruction No. 6(2).
The panel of eligible candidates must be prepared based on seniority, qualifications, and work record, and if multiple candidates secure equal marks, seniority should be determined by their ages.
Therefore, the actions of the respondents cannot be supported under the law.
