Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - Repeated transfers - As long as a transfer is not arbitrary, discriminatory, or jurisdictionally flawed, the fact that one or two orders have been issued in succession to indicate different postings or that postings have lasted for different periods is not sufficient grounds to interfere in transfer matters meant to meet service exigencies.
- An employee holding a transferable post has no right to demand posting to a particular place.
