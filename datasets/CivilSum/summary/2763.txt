Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2-A(2) and 11-A - Resignation - Acceptance and revocation - If an employee's resignation has not been accepted yet or has not come into effect, they are entitled to withdraw it - However, if the resignation has been accepted, revoking it will not have any effect.
[Para 8] B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2-A(2) and 11-A - Charges of theft - Loss of Confidence - Termination of services - Evidence of misconduct presented before Tribunal - Charge of possessing stolen property is proven - Employee's defense of resignation not being termination of service is invalid - Once the charge of theft is established, the employer has the right to terminate the employee's services.
