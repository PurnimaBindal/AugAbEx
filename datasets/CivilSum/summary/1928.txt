Section 17 of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936 pertains to appeal and specifies the period of limitation within the Act itself.
Section 5 of the Limitation Act has no relevance to this.
[Para 12]
According to Section 17(1-A) of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936, an appeal is not maintainable in the absence of a certificate of authority regarding the deposit of the entire allowed amount along with the appeal.
[Para 13]
If an appeal is filed after the expiry of the prescribed period of limitation specified under Section 17 of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936, and if the certificate of authority for the payment of the claimed amount is not filed along with the appeal, then the appeal is not maintainable.
