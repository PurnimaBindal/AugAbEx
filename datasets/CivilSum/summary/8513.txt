Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Compulsory retirement - The petitioner was alleged of pilferage and mis-appropriated due to gross negligence and was penalized with compulsory retirement.
However, the original handing and taking over report was not produced before the enquiry officer and the petitioner's signature only appeared on the second page.
Despite the petitioner's protest, the enquiry officer considered the exhibit.
The first page of the report was material in determining the controversy.
The case resulted in miscarriage of justice as the appointing and appellate authorities did not take into account the discrepancies in witness testimony.
The report of the Enquiry Officer was based on surmises and conjectures.
Reinstatement with all consequential benefits ordered.
[Paras 7 and 10]
