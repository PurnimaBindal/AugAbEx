Punjab School Education Board (Penalties for Misconduct and Use of Unfair Means in the Matriculation and Higher Secondary Examinations - Regulations 1979, Regulations 9(a), 10 and 11 - Incriminating material Examination/Copying - The Board investigated a complaint that alleged mass copying in the center, where photo-copies of solutions to the questions were circulated.
The Board asked a student to try solving some questions again after showing him the photo-copies of solutions.
The student was unable to solve the questions again.
However, as the student was not found copying nor any incriminating material found on him in the examination hall, the case was not covered under Regulations 9(a), 10, or 11.
Moreover, the show cause notice and punishment order did not rely on Regulation 19.
Thus, the impugned order is quashed.
[Para 8]
Punjab School Education Board (Penalties for Misconduct and Use of Unfare Means in the Matriculation and Higher Secondary Examinations) Regulation, 1979, Regulation 19 - Unfair means -To apply Regulation 19, the Unfair Means Committee must give a finding that the candidate has either received or attempted to receive help from any source.
Merely establishing that the candidate's answer was similar to any solution paper or book will not attract Regulation 19 per se.
As the case lacked concrete evidence to show guilt under Regulation 19, the punishment order cannot be sustained.
