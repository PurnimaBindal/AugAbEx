According to Section 10(1) (c) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, disputes regarding termination of a workman's service may be referred to the Labour Court for appropriate relief.
The Labour Court has inherent powers to grant such relief as it deems fit in cases where the termination of a workman's service is found to be illegal or unjustified.
[Para 5] B.
In this case, the Labour Court declined to grant back wages to the workman based on alleged misconduct.
However, there is no legal justification for denying back wages between the date of termination and the date of the award.
Moreover, there is no evidence to suggest that the workman found gainful employment elsewhere.
Therefore, the case has been remanded to the Labour Court for further consideration of the issue of entitlement to back wages.
