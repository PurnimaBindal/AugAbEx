According to Rule 15 of the Rajasthan State & Subordinate Service (Direct Recruitment by Combined Competitive Examinations) Rules, 1999, and in accordance with Articles 16(4) and 335 of the Constitution of India, the term "General Category" refers to candidates who obtain their position based purely on merit, regardless of their belonging to any category.
It's important to note that such candidates cannot be excluded from the "General Category" merely on the grounds that they are eligible for reservation benefits.
If reserved categories are excluded from the "General Category," then the latter would end up receiving reservation benefits, which is not desirable.
