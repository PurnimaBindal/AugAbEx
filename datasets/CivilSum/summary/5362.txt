Industrial Disputes Act - Constitution of India, Article 226, 227 - Termination - Reinstatement with continuity - Full Back wages - The services of the respondent were terminated, and an ex-parte award was passed in his favour.
However, the same was later set aside, and the case remitted for rehearing.
The petitioner argues that the respondent wrongly mentioned its name when the case was earlier proceeded ex-parte, and thus should not be taxed with back wages from the initial stages.
Even though there has been a consensus between parties regarding service, back wages cannot be awarded prior to remanding the case.
Since the witness of the petitioner did not support its contention that the respondent was gainfully employed, back wages can only be allowed from the date of the case's remand onward.
[Paras 2 and 4 to 9]
