Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 deals with Award/Evidence.
Tribunals cannot rule in general terms that charges are not based on evidence without discussing the evidence considered by the enquiry officer.
The findings of the tribunal are therefore considered to be arbitrary and capricious.
[Para 8]  B The establishment used to give goods to its employees in connection with the social functions of the employees which were to be returned.
Non-return of such goods dishonestly and dishonourably amounts to misconduct of the employee.
It cannot be said that the charges have no relation to the duties of the workman.
[Para 9]  C According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, once the Tribunal concludes that a valid and proper domestic enquiry was held, it is beyond its jurisdiction to give adverse findings to those given by the punishing authority.
This was established in AIR 1973 SC 1227.
