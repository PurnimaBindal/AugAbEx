Constitution of India, Articles 32 and 142 - Compassionate appointment - Compensation - Widow and children of an electricity supply company employee who died tragically while on duty left in destitute - In the peculiar circumstances of the case, the Electric Company ordered to pay compensation of Rs.1 lakh and LIC to provide appropriate job to the son of the deceased according to his qualification - Directions not to be considered as precedent due to special facts and circumstances of the case.
