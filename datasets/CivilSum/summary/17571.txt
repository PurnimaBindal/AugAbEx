Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservation - Selection - Admissions - Separate merit lists for general and reserved categories may result in admission of a general category candidate with lower marks than a reserved category candidate, but the former cannot be considered in the general category.
Candidates claiming reservation must first be considered in the general category, and only after the quota for the general category is filled, should remaining candidates of reserved categories be considered against reserved seats.
Ignoring a candidate of reserved category and appointing a less meritorious candidate of open category, only because he had applied for the benefit of reservation, violates Article 16(4) and puts reserved category candidates at a disadvantage.
Accordingly, respondents must give admission to the petitioner as per merit.
