The claim for Family Pension was denied on the ground that petitioner's deceased husband did not render one year regular service.
However, it is admitted that the deceased did render regular service from 7 June, 1979 to 28 July, 1979.
The judgement of the Division Bench of High Court in Savitri Devi's case has clarified that one year regular service is not required for an employee to be eligible for family pension.
Thus, the court has directed to calculate and release family pension with arrears within two months.
In case of failure, petitioner will be entitled to 6% interest on the same.
