Punjab Municipal Corporation Employees Pension and General Provident Fund Rules, 1994 - Pension - It is the contract of employment or the governing Rules which determine the conditions of service of an employee - The date of April 1, 1990 was made applicable for the Pension Rules - Only the employees who retired after this date are entitled to receive the pension - The date chosen cannot be termed arbitrary as it has a historical relevance.

1.4.90 - It is not reasonable for those who retired much earlier to make claims for pensionary benefits.

Pension/Provident Fund - The grant of pensionary benefits is subject to the refund of the amount of provident fund that has already been withdrawn, along with a 10% simple interest - This is considered just and fair as the employees have utilised the amount for that specific period.
