Under Rule 5(1) of Central Civil Services Rules (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, a temporary employee cannot fault termination made without wages in lieu of notice period being paid simultaneously with termination order.
[Paras 151] B Rule 5(1) of Central Civil Services Rules (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, makes it clear that no reason needs to be assigned for order of termination of a temporary employee.
[Paras 152] C. If a respondent wants to invoke power of Rule 5(1) to terminate a temporary employee, they must comply with requirements as contemplated under the proviso to the rule.
Failure to do so will result in the order of termination being set aside.
