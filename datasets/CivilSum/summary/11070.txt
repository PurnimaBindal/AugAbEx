Respondent SK Bhanot, DAG Punjab has presented arguments regarding Article 311(2) (b) of the Constitution of India and Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 161 & 1631.
In regards to dismissal, the order states that the petitioner has developed links with extremists and leaks secrets of the department but does not reference any record on which the conclusion is based.
Additionally, the disciplinary authority did not provide any evidence or reasoning as to why an enquiry was not reasonably practicable.
The order of dismissal was quashed, and the petitioner was directed to be reinstated in service with consequential benefits.
Furthermore, the appellate order was not a speaking order as it should have been, and the impugned order was quashed as it showed hardly any application of mind.
The Appellate Authority ignored points raised by the petitioner for consideration and disposed of the appeal with a brief order.
