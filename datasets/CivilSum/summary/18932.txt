Constitution of India's Article 226 emphasizes the importance of natural justice concerning the examination procedure.
If accused of using unfair means, the committee must ensure that accused parties receive a hearing before taking any action.
In one particular case, the petitioner was accused falsely of using unfair means, and no flying squad was present.
Additionally, they were not allowed to cross-examine a witness.
Because of this, the Unfair Means Committee was directed to provide a fresh hearing to the petitioner.
