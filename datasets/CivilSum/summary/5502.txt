Regulation for Medical Services of Armed Forces, 1983, Para 423(d) - Disability Pension - The plaintiff, who joined service in 1957 and had no record of ailment in his service book, suffered a head injury in 1973 which downgraded his medical record.
However, for 16 years prior to the injury, he had not faced any problems.
In light of these circumstances, it is reasonable to infer that the disease was attributable to the armed forces.
The plaintiff challenged the Medical Board's opinion in the Civil Court and proved that the injury was indeed attributable to the armed forces.
As a result, the suit for disability pension was rightfully decreed by the first Appellate Court.
