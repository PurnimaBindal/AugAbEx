According to the Workmen's Compensation Act (8 of 1923), Schedule I, Item Nos. 25, 26, 26A and the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act (1 of 1996), Section 2(i), a "person with disability" refers to someone who is suffering from a minimum of 40% of any disability.
In this case, the worker is blind in their left eye but has normal vision in their right eye, resulting in a 10% disability.
As a result, the son of the workman is not eligible for appointment.
