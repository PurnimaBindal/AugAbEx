Army Act, 1950, Sections 39, 71, 72 - Absence of 5-1/2 hours - Court Martial Proceedings - Punishment of dismissal and rigorous imprisonment for one year - Judicial review - Petitioner jailed for six months before being granted bail - Petitioner intended to go on leave on day after absence - Judicial review covers even those aspects within exclusive purview of court martial - Sentence considered irrational or perverse can be corrected - Disproportionate sentence set aside and matter remanded to summary court-martial - Detention does not prohibit reinstatement of service.
