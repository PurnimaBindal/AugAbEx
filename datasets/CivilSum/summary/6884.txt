Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 47, Rules 1 and 2 - Review - An accidental slip and error - The material facts and documents on record, including earlier court orders, and admissions and undertakings of the respondents were not brought to the court's attention by the petitioner's counsel, resulting in an adverse ruling - The court held that this was an error evident from the record and provided sufficient grounds for reviewing the order - The review application was granted, and an appropriate order was issued to provide necessary relief to the petitioner in light of the case's circumstances.
