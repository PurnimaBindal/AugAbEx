Article 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India - Qualifications - Appointment - Lecturer in Dance - No evidence provided for Punjab Govt.'s derecognition of degrees from Prayag Sangeet Samiti, Allahabad - Petitioner possessed a Master's degree in Dance from Prayag Sangeet Samiti, Allahabad, when she applied for the position in 1982 - Her appointment must not be revoked.
[Paras 10 and 12]
Article 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India - The petitioner held a Master's degree in Dance from Prayag Sangeet Samiti, Allahabad, as well as Master's degrees in both Music and Dance from Panjab University - She was appointed as a Dance Lecturer and served with only notional interruptions - In regular fashion through PPSC, she was appointed as a Music Lecturer - Applied for and appointed as a Dance Lecturer in response to an announcement - The State Govt. rescinded her appointment on the grounds that PPSC had removed their recommendation that she did not have the necessary qualifications until the deadline for submitting applications - Punjab Govt.'s derecognition of MA degree in Dance from Prayag Sangeet Samiti, Allahabad for the position of Lecturer in Dance was not established by any bulletin - When she applied for the job in 1982, the petitioner was completely qualified, and her appointment cannot be revoked.
[Paras 5, 10, 11, and 12]
Article 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India - Qualifications, Appointment - The petitioner had a Master's degree in Dance from Panjab University when she was selected on 20.7.1984, but she did not possess the qualification by the last date for submitting applications, i.e. 2291982, as the result was announced on 14.10.1982 - She was selected once more in 1988 - She has been in service since 1972 - After her appointment was revoked, she remained in service by order of the High Court, but she has been continuing in her position since February 1988 - The petitioner is entitled to the remedy she demands.
