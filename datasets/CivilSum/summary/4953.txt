Backwages - Workmen who were wrongfully terminated are entitled to full backwages.
It was noted that in similar cases, workmen of Kapurthala and Ferozepur Bank were granted full backwages.
No clear reason was provided for why the same relief was not granted to the present workmen when the decision in those cases had become final.
Therefore, it was held that the workmen must receive their full benefits regarding their wages.
