The Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 10 and Constitution of India, Article 14 were invoked to interpret a notification that prohibited Iron and Steel Company from employing contract labour.
The notification granted a benefit to workmen in the Bricks Department, who were doing cleaning and stacking jobs, but excluded workmen who were doing loading and unloading jobs.
However, based on the judgement, the stacking, loading and unloading jobs were all incidental or allied jobs.
Thus, the exclusion of loaders and unloaders from the benefit was deemed discriminatory, and the petition was allowed.
