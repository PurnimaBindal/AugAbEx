According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, badli workers can have their services regularized.
However, the Labor Tribunal must take into consideration whether or not the workmen have been subjected to unfair labor practices.
The management must be notified by the Tribunal so that relevant records may be brought to their attention.
If unfair labor practices are found without there being any such issue, the case will be remanded.
