Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2-A - Termination due to delay in issuing demand notice - Statutory appeal and demand notice filed after termination - Cannot be rejected as belated - Labour Court considered delay and granted backwages from date of filing of demand notice, based on "Ajaib Singh v. The Sirhind Cooperative Marketing-cum-Processing Service Society Ltd. and other, AIR 1999 SC 1351" - Order of Labour Court deemed reasonable.
