Ministry of Works and Housing Deputy Advisory (Training) Recruitment Rules, 1985 - Eligibility - Contention of respondent regarding promotion to the post of Deputy Advisor (Training) in Central Public Health and Environmental Organization of Urban Development Ministry -  Departmental Scientific Officer must fulfill the essential educational qualification of Degree in Civil Engineering or equivalent prescribed in Columns 7, 8 and 10.
Educational qualifications prescribed for direct recruits will apply in the case of promotees while age qualification will not.
