Constitution of India, Article 226 - Resignation - Acceptance of - The petitioner submitted his resignation, stating that it could be treated as 24 hours notice, which was accepted by the authority.
However, after that, the petitioner and his wife made a request for him to continue in service, which was declined.
The acceptance of the resignation was challenged, on the ground that one month's notice was not given by the petitioner.
However, the failure to give notice would have only entitled the institute to deduct one month's wage of petitioner, and the petitioner is not entitled to any relief.
