Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - J&K Civil Service (Temporary Service) Rules, 1961, Rule 5 - Age relaxation - Eligibility - Regularisation - The petitioner, who served in a make-shift arrangement and not in a substantive capacity, requested relaxation of age bar at the time of entry of temporary service.
However, his services were terminated in accordance with the law, and the discretion exercised held not to be discriminatory.
