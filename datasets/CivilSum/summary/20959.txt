Jammu and Kashmir Civil Service Rules, Rule 11 of Schedule 15, Vol. II, Para 240B(II) of Vol.
I - Family pension - Family - Grandson - If an employed son of a deceased Govt. employee passes away, they are not eligible for family pension and a grandson is not considered to be a member of the family, so they are not entitled to family pension, even if it is stated in the Will.
