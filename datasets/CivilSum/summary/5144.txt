Kerala Headload Workers Act, 1980, Section 13 - Schemes made applicable - When not applicable, employer may freely engage workers of his own choice - Such workers are merely casual and do not form an employer-employee relationship.

Kerala Headload Workers Act, 1980, Section 21(4) - Dispute settlement - Procedure - Failure to comply - Duty of conciliation officer to send report to Appellate Authority - Mandate must be followed within two weeks - Non-compliance results in an illegal order.

Binding precedents - Doctrine of 'Stare Decisis' - Universal application - Personal preference cannot replace binding precedent - 'Keep to what has been decided previously' - Must be followed.
