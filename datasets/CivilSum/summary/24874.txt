It is undisputed that the Government resolution dated May 23, 1974 did not apply to the Corporation's services on the day it was passed by the government.
It would only come into effect after the Corporation passed its resolution on September 12, 1975.
Any promotion made in the Corporation's services after that date would be subject to the reservation policy that the Corporation adopted.
[Para]
Article 16(4) of the Indian Constitution applies to promotions that occur after a corporation adopts the state government's resolution regarding the reservation of Backward Classes.
Subsequent promotions would be subject to the resolution.
