According to Rule 4 of the Rajasthan Municipalities (Contributory Provident Fund and Gratuity) Rules, 1969, and Rule 3 of the Rajasthan Municipal Service (Pension) Rules, 1989, benefit holders of the contributory provident fund cannot seek pension.
The petitioner in this case retired prior to the introduction of pension rules and is therefore unable to claim the same benefits as pension retirees, as retirees in this position are considered to be a different legal class.
