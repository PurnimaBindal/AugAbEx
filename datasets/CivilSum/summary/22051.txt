The seniority of Supervisors who later became Junior Engineers versus graduate Junior Engineers initially appointed on a temporary basis and later regularized under GOMs No. 647 dated Sept 14, 1979 was discussed in Andhra Pradesh State and Subordinate Service Rules, Rules 10(a)(i) (1) read with GOMs No. 647 dated 1491979.
The Tribunal held that the employees did not fall within the purview of the expression "last regular appointment" in GOMs No. 647 date 1491979 [Para 15].
B.
The interpretation of Andhra Pradesh State and Subordinate Service Rules, Rule 22(e) was that limited recruitment of SC/ST candidates can be seen as a normal recruitment and be equated to the "regular appointment" referred to under GOMs dated 1491979.
Two candidates belonging to the SC/ST group were appointed through PSC under Rule 22(e), which was deemed perfectly in order and normal and would attract the "regular appointment" expression employed in GOMs No. 647 [Para 13].
