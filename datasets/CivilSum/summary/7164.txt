Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Ex parte - Remand for trial on merits - It is not always justified - If a litigant intentionally delays the proceedings, they cannot claim that the proceedings were conducted in violation of natural justice - When the Court's process is misused for an improper motive, the Court will not grant leniency to such a party.
