Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 2(22) - Incentive - Overtime wages - Good work wages - The employer is required to contribute towards both good work wages and overtime wages - Any payments that a worker receives under their employment contract, regardless of whether they are express or implied, are considered wages according to the definition provided in Section 2(22) of the Act.
