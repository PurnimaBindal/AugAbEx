According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, promotion and seniority are based on regular appointments.
Ad hoc appointments cannot be counted towards seniority and promotion unless the appointment followed the proper rules and procedures and the appointee was qualified and eligible for the position.
Any initial appointment made as a stop-gap arrangement cannot be taken into account for seniority or promotions, regardless of if the appointee held the position without interruption until regularisation.
[Paras 9 to 13]
The Constitution of India and Limitation Act Article 113 states that the petitioner cannot receive the benefit of ad hoc service from 1962 to 1964 unless their appointment was in accordance with the proper rules and procedures.
If the petitioner's representation for the benefit was rejected on July 8th, 1982, then a suit could only be filed up to July 8th, 1985.
Since the petitioner filed suit on September 28th, 1990, the suit was held to be time-barred and liable to be dismissed on the ground of limitation.
