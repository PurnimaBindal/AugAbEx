Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Promotion/Selection/Adverse entries - The service record may contain remarks that are sometimes not fully justified but this is an accepted way of assessing an official's merit - If a candidate's service record is found lacking at one stage and promotions are made based on seniority-cum-merit, the court cannot reassess the previously assessed merit - If the official received adverse remarks which were noted, they cannot claim mala fide in promoting juniors - Non-communication of adverse remarks cannot be used as evidence of prejudice - These entries can be considered when evaluating merit for promotion.
