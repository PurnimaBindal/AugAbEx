Punjab Civil Services, Vol.
I, Part I, Rule 3.26(d) & Vol. II Rule 46 - Compulsory retirement - Pension - Qualifying service - Notice - If an employee is forced to retire at his retirement age without being given sufficient notice or three months' salary, as the Rule mandates, he is considered to be in service until the end of that three-month period.
If he accumulates the necessary qualifying service during that period, he will be eligible for a pension.
