Constitution of India, Article 226 - Initiation of disciplinary proceedings - Acquittal at trial stage - Delay in initiation of departmental proceedings - Petitioner employed as cash peon in respondent Bank - Unauthorised absence from duty - Bank robbery on the day of his absence - Cash recovered from his residence - His wife convicted under Section 395 IPC, but he was acquitted - Sufficient grounds to commence proceedings against petitioner - Delay in the initiation of proceedings - Petitioner's failure to provide an explanation for the delay - Decision to proceed departmentally not invalidated by the delay.
