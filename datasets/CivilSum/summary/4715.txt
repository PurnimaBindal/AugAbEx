Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Correction of Date of Birth - While High Courts generally do not involve themselves in disputes over a person's date of birth, the court may intervene if clear, unchallenged evidence exists that establishes a specific date of birth.
In such cases, High Court may exercise its jurisdiction under Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India.
