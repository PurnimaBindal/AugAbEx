Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 15 - Equal Remuneration Act, 1976, Sections 2(h) and 4 - Pay - Discrimination on the basis of gender - Male and female stenographers receive different pay scales while performing the same duties - This difference is the outcome of a settlement between the Company and the Union - This discrepancy is in violation of Section 4 of the Remuneration Act.
