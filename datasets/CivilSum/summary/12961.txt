According to the Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 226, as well as the Kerala Co-operative Societies Act from 1969, Section 109 and the Kerala Co-operative Societies Rules from 1969, Rules 186 and 187, eligibility for appointment and reservation for posts must meet certain criteria.
The first half of the notification requires that 50% of appointments come from the open market, while the second half mandates that the remaining 50% come from individuals who already work in various affiliated Primary Co-operative Societies.
In this case, an appellant who had previously worked in Primary Co-operative Societies but was not an employee at the time of their application was deemed ineligible for the appointment.
