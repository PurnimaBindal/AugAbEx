The Classification, Control & Appeal Regulations, Clause 13(3) of Constitution of India, Article 311 state that assaulting a superior officer because of being denied favored work arrangements can still be a serious form of misconduct - even if done outside the working hours or outside the workplace - because it undermines discipline and hampers the performance of duty.

Constitution of India, Article 14 allows disciplinary proceedings to proceed without necessarily waiting for the outcome of any criminal trial that may be ongoing regarding the same charges - unless the criminal charge is so serious and complex as to make proceeding with the disciplinary proceedings inappropriate.
