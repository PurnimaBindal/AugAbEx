Rule 5 of the Central Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1949 (as amended in 1965) requires one month's notice or notice pay to be given upon termination, which can be paid subsequently within a reasonable time after the amendment.
Failure to pay notice pay will not affect the validity of termination.
The decision in the case of Senior Superintendent RMS v. KV Gopinath 1973(3) SCC 867 is no longer valid as per the Raj Kumar v. Union of India 1975(4) SCC 13.

According to Section 25-M and Section 2(oo) and (bb) of the Industrial Disputes Act, termination of temporary government servants without notice, notice pay or retrenchment compensation is considered retrenchment and is invalid if not made in accordance with the rules.
Any non-compliance will lead to the workman being reinstated with full back wages and all other benefits.
