Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Termination - Punishment - Enquiry officer's rejection of medical documents as bogus for an employee suffering from serious ailments being Tuberculosis and Cancer who ultimately died of it, was extra technical without proper application of mind - Evaluation required an elementary degree of human consideration to the special facts of the case - Proceedings being quasi-judicial in nature, a fair decision was required - Punishment of removal from service excessive and not sustainable - Only emoluments for the period of absence beyond approved leave can be forfeited - Order of removal from service set aside - Widow entitled to all other terminal benefits of the deceased.
