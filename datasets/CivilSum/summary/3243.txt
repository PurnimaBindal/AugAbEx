Constitution of India, Articles 16, 39 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - Maintenance - The petitioner, a widow of a deceased employee, is entitled to maintenance @ Rs. 500/- under the State policy - Despite this policy, the maintenance has not been paid - Delaying appointment on compassionate grounds due to non-availability of vacancy is not acceptable - Ordered to establish a supernumerary post and pay maintenance under the policy while waiting for the vacancy.
[Paras 5 to 8]
