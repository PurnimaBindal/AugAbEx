Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - OT Course - Legally divorced women category - Age eligibility - The petitioner argued that the prescribed maximum age limit of 41 years for legally divorced women was arbitrary, but no rule or law was presented to challenge the respondent's ability to set the limit.
This petitioner's category has already been granted substantial age relaxation for admission eligibility to the course.
It is the state government's discretion to determine the age limit, considering the productive life span of the teacher.
The petitioner is not entitled to any relief.
