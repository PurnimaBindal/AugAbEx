The Appellant-Management claims that the Respondent abandoned his services and overstayed leave without permission, but later took an alternative plea of misconduct.
These alternative pleas are contradictory, and therefore, the amendment of the written statement cannot be allowed.
The Appellant is attempting to both approbate and reprobate.
It is considered a case of retrenchment, not dismissal.
[Paras 31 and 32] B.
The High Court ruled that the dismissal of the workman was improper and that it was a case of retrenchment.
The matter of what relief the workman is entitled to has been remanded back to the tribunal.
