Bombay Public Trust Act, Section 47 - Scheme of Karveer Peeth/Trust Deed, Clause 5 - Appointment of Sole Trustee of Trust - Challenge - In accordance with Clause 5 of the scheme, the presiding Swami at Karveer Peeth is appointed as the sole trustee of the trust.
However, Clause 5 was not considered by the Division Bench of the High Court in the decision of the issue.
Thus, it was held that the matter must be reconsidered afresh by the Division Bench of the High Court as Clause 5 of the Scheme was relevant for the purpose of determining the question of appointment of the sole trustee.
[Paras 22 to 25]
