Under Section 6N(b) of the UP Industrial Disputes Act (28 of 1947), a petitioner appointed as an apprentice and who completes 240 days of continuous service is entitled to retrenchment compensation.
The termination of the petitioner's services without proper communication of the same is deemed inappropriate.
The employer argues that the petitioner could not be reinstated in service as the unit in which the petitioner was working was declared as a sick industry and likely to be closed.
The matter is remitted back to the Labour Court for appropriate relief.
