Constitution of India, Articles 14, 15, 45 and 226 - Education - Teachers - Teachers training institutions - Recognition of - Court cannot direct issuance of diploma/certificates of training to teachers trained from un-recognised or de-recognised training institutions - Petitioners entitled only for declaration of result - Passing public examination alone not enough for teacher training course - Discrimination in allowing certificates to be issued with the note of unrecognized institutions cannot be perpetuated - Teachers must receive proper training in recognised institutes before teaching children.
