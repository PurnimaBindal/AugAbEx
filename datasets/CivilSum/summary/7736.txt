Rule 244(2) of Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951 and Rule 53(1) of Rajasthan Civil Service (Pension) Rules, 1996 provide for compulsory retirement.
Even if a wrong statutory provision is mentioned, the order will not be invalidated if the authority has the power to pass it.
The State Government has the powers to pass orders of compulsory retirement.
