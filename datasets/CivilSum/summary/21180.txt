Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 12 - Contempt of the Court - Non-compliance of the order - The High Court did not take action against alleged contemners because the order and judgment had been fulfilled, albeit late.
The contempt jurisdiction cannot be used to seek personal revenge.
Unless the order is entirely perverse, the appellate court will not intervene in the contempt proceedings.
[Paras 1 to 3]
