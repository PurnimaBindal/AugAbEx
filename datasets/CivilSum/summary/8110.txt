Bombay Civil Services Rules, Rule 198-B - Pensionary benefits - The petitioner worked as a daily wager starting in 1970 and became a work charged employee in 1987.
After retiring on 30.11.996, the petitioner claimed pension under the provision of a circular dated 17101988.
It was determined that if found eligible, the respondent must consider granting pension and pay with interest at a rate of 12% per annum.
