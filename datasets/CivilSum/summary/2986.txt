The Industrial Disputes Act and the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act provide for suspension allowance for workmen.
However, there is a dispute over whether the Standing Orders or the Act govern the benefit.
The Labour Court has the jurisdiction to decide and grant relief in such disputes.
The Standing Orders made in conformity with the Model Standing Orders also need to be considered.
The workmen cannot be compelled to mark their presence as required under Standing Order 30(d).
The case has been remanded to the Labour Court for adjudicating upon the applicability of the Standing Orders.
