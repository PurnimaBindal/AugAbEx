Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - Criminal Trial - Acquittal - Adverse remarks - The petitioner who was honorably acquitted from a criminal charge seeks promotion from the date his juniors were promoted.
No departmental enquiry was conducted for charges levelled against the petitioner in the criminal complaint.
Furthermore, the respondant's averment of being junior was not controverted.
No adverse report was ever communicated to the petitioner.
It was held that when average reports are considered against an employee for promotion, then they are adverse and have to be communicated.
Hence, when such remarks are not communicated, promotion cannot be denied.
As a result, the petition was allowed and the respondents were directed to consider the case of the petitioner for promotion from the date his junior was promoted.
[Paras 3 to 6]
