In accordance with the Punjab Civil Services Rules, Article 226, the petitioner was informed that he was not eligible for admission to the BDS first year course as he had not taken chemistry in grades 11 and 12.
His completion of chemistry only in grade 10 was not equivalent to 10+2 education level in India, and as per the system of education in America, it is an option of the student to complete the study of a specific subject in any class.
B.
The petitioner was given provisional admission to the BDS first year course after failing to produce the required eligibility certificate.
He was allowed to continue studying for one year without raising any objection.
It will be too late for the University to raise any objection on his eligibility at the eve of annual examinations, especially since he had acted in good faith.
Therefore, the court ordered the result of the examination be declared.
