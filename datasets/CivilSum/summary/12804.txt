Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch Class I) Rules, 1976, Rule 24 - Selection Grade - Eligibility - Promotion from Tehsildar to PCS (Executive Branch) - The government cannot use different criteria for grading eligibility, as many other persons, including even juniors who did not complete 12 years PCS (EB) actual service, have already been given selection grade by computing their 15 years by giving them allotment year from retrospective date while working in the next below grade - Selection grade is not allowed based on the criteria of 15 years service as per circular dated 331980 of Punjab Government - Government directed to grant benefit of selection grade to eligible candidates, including those considered from their eligibility date.
