Constitution of India, Article 14 - Probation - Extension of probation period - The maximum period prescribed by the Rules cannot be exceeded - If there is no such Rule and confirmation is based only on an order passed by the competent authority, probation may and will be deemed to continue until the order of confirmation is issued - If the employee's work or conduct is found unsatisfactory during the deemed extended period, they may be dismissed from service in accordance with the appointment order.
