The State Bank of India Officers Service Rules, Rules 50(4) and 68(3), and the Specific Relief Act, Section 14, cannot enforce a contract of personal service.
In public employment, governed by statutory rules, an action for declaration of deemed continuation of service is maintainable.
B Pleadings must be specific in stating all material facts, and evidence cannot be looked into in their absence.
In writ petitions, the High Court has discretion to call for and examine the record.
C. The courts should scrutinize the quantum of penalty of removal from service if no other valid ground of challenge is present.
D. An authority having exercised quasi-judicial power cannot review its decision once it has become functus officio, which happens only when the order is pronounced or communicated to the party concerned.
E Tentative approval by disciplinary authority for reduction of employee pay does not amount to passing a final order and the authority becoming functus officio.
F. The advice or opinion of a vigilance officer is not binding on competent authority, and passing punishment order by independent application of mind, without mechanical acceptance of any suggestion or advice, cannot be said to be a case of consideration of extraneous material.
G Pleadings as to violation of principles of natural justice not shown despite permission to amend the plaint, and a plea cannot be agitated for the first time in appeal before the Supreme Court.
H. A bank employee must show absolute integrity and honesty in handling funds, and misconduct, even a temporary misappropriation of bank funds, is serious and can invite severe punishment.
