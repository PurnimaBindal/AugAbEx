Delhi Transport Corporation Authority Regulations, 1952, Clause 14(10)(c) states that termination requires a show cause notice sent to the petitioner's address.
Despite her address being available, the petitioner did not receive the notice until five days after it was sent and had no opportunity to explain their case before being fired.
The termination order was subsequently overturned for being unjust.
[Paras 10 and 12]
Clause 14(10)(b) of the Delhi Transport Corporation Authority Regulations, 1952, allows for a leave of up to 18 months for extraordinary circumstances.
The petitioner was absent from work to care for their ailing mother and ultimately received a diagnosis of schizophrenia.
They were not given the opportunity to explain their illness-related absence and were terminated without due process.
The order of termination was quashed.
