Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependants of Deceased Government Employees Rules, 2003, Rule 3(d)(iii) - Compassionate appointment - The denial of claim based on the father's age of 65 at the time of death is not sustainable in view of Rule 3(d)(iii), however, in subsequent instructions adopted by respondents, the bar of 55 years was omitted - The claim of petitioner was made in 2002 and rejected in 2003, but under the circumstances, there is no justification to deny the appointment to the petitioner - Directions have been given to consider the claim of the petitioner.
