Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Right of Equality before law - Discrimination - Two petitioners, who were appointed as Clerks in their respective departments, have questioned the different treatment meted out to them and similarly situated persons appointed as Inspectors, Food & Supplies and Taxation Inspectors respectively - It was observed that the cases of petitioners were not distinct or different from the cases of those who have been appointed as Inspectors and the respondent failed to justify the different treatment - The plea that Chief Secretary had the discretion to give approval for appointment of different persons on different posts and that no person can claim appointment on compassionate grounds as a matter of course - Cannot be accepted - Respondents have violated the petitioners' fundamental right of equality before law as guaranteed to them under Articles 14 and 16 - Respondents were directed to examine cases of petitioners for appointment as Inspector, Food & Supplies and Taxation Inspector respectively.
