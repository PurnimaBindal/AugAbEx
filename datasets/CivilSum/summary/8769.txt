Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2 - Dismissal from service - Overstayal of leave due to employee's own illness or illness of the employee's wife - No evidence recorded by the punishing authority to classify the overstayal as gravest misconduct - Failure by the authority to take into account the employee's right to pension based on his length of service - Dismissal order declared invalid - Appeal of the State rejected.
