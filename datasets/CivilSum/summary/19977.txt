The Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14 and 16, guarantee parity in pay scale.
The respondents employed as SDO, SDE and AE with the HSMITC have had their pay scales treated and equated on par with those in Government whenever pay scales were revised.
Thus, the corporation can't only cite financial loss as a ground for denying pay scale revision to a limited category of employees.
The High Court was right in ruling that the respondents must receive a revision of pay scales at par with their counterparts in the State of Haryana.
When the pay scale anomaly for Government engineers was corrected, the same correction should be applied to Corporation engineers to maintain parity.
