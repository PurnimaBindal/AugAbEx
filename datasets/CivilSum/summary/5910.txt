Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo), 25-F, 25-G, 25-H and 25/FFF - Closure/Retrenchment/Termination - If a workman's services are terminated with the closure of an establishment, Section 25-F, 25-G, and 25-H of the Act would not apply, but the case would fall under Section 25-FFF.
The services of an ad hoc employee terminated for non-renewal of the contract of service after the expiry of the term fixed, with no pleading or proof before the Labour Court for the closure of the establishment brought on the file.
The juniors to the petitioner were retained in service, and fresh appointments were also made.
The termination would not be covered under Section 2(oo) nor under Section 25-FFF.
The management could not be allowed to make a new case of closure the first time in writ jurisdiction, especially when provisions of Section 25-FFF of the Act have not been followed.

Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(oo) - Pleadings and Evidence/Closure - No pleading and document brought on the record to prove closure of the establishment resorting to termination of services of workman consequent thereof - An admission or statement of fact made in the statement beyond the pleadings have to be ignored.
