Guidelines issued by the Government for transfer of Government employees must be followed, and failure to comply with these guidelines does not provide legal grounds to challenge a transfer.
Unless a transfer order is affected by mala fides or violates statutory provisions, the Court cannot interfere.
[Para 7]  B The jurisdiction of the Central Administrative Tribunal in service matters is comparable to that of the High Court under Article 226 of the Constitution.
The Tribunal must abide by the same constraints and norms as the High Court.
The Tribunal cannot act as an appellate authority and cannot substitute its own judgment.
