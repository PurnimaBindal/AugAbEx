Constitution of India, Article 226 - Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 47 - Disability during service - The termination of services on the grounds of "mental sickness" is a violation of Section 47 - Petitioner was certified as unfit for driving but suitable for lighter work - The order of termination was quashed - Reinstatement of the petitioner with full back wages is ordered.
