Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Examination/Grace marks/Compartment - Standards of education - The appropriate authority or examining body of a university has the right to determine the standards for passing examinations and awarding diplomas and degrees, with educationists being the best judges for that purpose.
The court should not interfere unless the rule is discriminatory or arbitrary.
Granting grace marks is a concession available only to those who meet the fixed target for availing that grace, to help them save a year and possibly be declared pass.
The rule is not discriminatory or arbitrary but rather a reasonable classification with a clear objective.
Diluting academic standards is discouraged, and the court should not interpret rules beyond the intention expressed in their language.
Naresh Joshi and Vishal Kumar's cases are overruled.
Raj Kumar's case 1990(2) PLR 179 (FB) is followed.
