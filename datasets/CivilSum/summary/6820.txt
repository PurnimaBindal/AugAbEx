Guru Nanak Dev University, Calendar Vol II, 1991, Ordinance 10(h) (j) r/w Ordinances 11 and 13 Unfair means - Disqualification - Petitioners were found to have no material in their possession during the examination that would warrant their disqualification - Therefore, they could not be held guilty under Ordinance 10(h)(j).
Disqualification was quashed.

Guru Nanak Dev University Calendar Vol. II, 1991, Ordinance 13 - Provisions - Unfair means - Disqualification - No allegations were found against the petitioners that they had given any help or attempt to give any help to any other candidate in any manner - Unless there is a categorical finding recorded, the petitioners could not be disqualified under Ordinance 13.
[Para 5]
Guru Nanak Dev University, Calendar Vol. II, 1991, Ordinances 10(h)(j), 11 and 13 - Unfair means - Disqualification - The committee had given the benefit of doubt to five out of eight candidates but disqualified the petitioners - The only mistake found was that petitioners had written (n-1) instead of (m1), and there was not enough evidence to conclude that the petitioners had resorted to copying or had received any help.
Therefore, disqualification was quashed.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Jurisdiction - High Court's jurisdiction is limited to cases where either principles of natural justice have been violated or it is a case of no evidence - Courts are generally reluctant to interfere with the functioning of universities, especially in setting aside orders of disqualification - Interference is permissible only where there is no evidence.
