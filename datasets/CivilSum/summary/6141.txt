Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Standing Order 16-A of corporation - Age of superannuation - According to standing order, the age of superannuation for all workmen is 58 years, while workmen in the status of IV class employee retire at 60 years.
It was held that the corporation can choose to give this benefit only to class IV employees and not to class III employees, without any fault.
