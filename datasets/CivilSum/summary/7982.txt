Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Education - Admissions in educational institutions - Writ court cannot determine the number of students to be accommodated in a particular stream of study by an institution - Generally, writ court should not interfere with a school's internal administration - However, if there is a violation of statutory provisions or hostile discrimination, and the institution authorities have made a completely arbitrary decision that negatively affects the students and their education, then interference may be necessary.
