As per Section 2(kkk) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Management is prohibited from using lay off as a solution when the concern is unable to continue its operation due to electricity supply cut off resulting from non-payment of electricity dues.
Furthermore, the Industrial court has determined that the lay off was bad.
B.
According to Section 2(kkk) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a claim for wages during the lay off period is applicable to temporarily laid-off workers, as they are considered unemployed.
The Labour Court has the jurisdiction to settle the matter.
