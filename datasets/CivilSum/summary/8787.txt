As per the Army Act, 1950, Sections 71 and 109 - Army Rules, Rule 37, a Court Martial can award punishment based on its findings.
However, the punishment cannot be upheld if the appropriate authority has not convened it correctly.
In this case, the punishment orders have been quashed, but the fact that the charges were serious cannot be ignored.
As a result, the authorities have been directed to proceed afresh in accordance with the law.
