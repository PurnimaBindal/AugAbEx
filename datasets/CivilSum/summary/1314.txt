Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Revision of Pay Rules, 1990 - Implementation of Pay Commission's revised pay scale recommendation to Lecturers/Professors in Arts, Science, and Commerce faculties in Govt. College, University faculties, and aided institutions - Teachers in College of Education have same duties and responsibilities as those in Arts, Commerce, and Science faculties in Govt.
Colleges and aided institutions - State cannot unfairly withhold benefit of pay revision from College of Education teachers.
