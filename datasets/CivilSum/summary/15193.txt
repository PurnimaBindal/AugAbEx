According to Constitution of India, Article 16, Railway Establishment Manual, Volume I, Para 159(1)(i), and Apprentices Act, 1961, Section 21, an apprentice must possess a certificate of proficiency to be eligible for employment under para 159(1)(i) of Railway Establishment Manual.
Respondents who have not obtained such certificate of proficiency are ineligible for employment and skilled artisans as envisaged in 159(1)(i).
Additionally, they are not serving staff and are therefore ineligible under para 159(1)(ii).
