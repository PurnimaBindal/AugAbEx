Under Article 16 of the Constitution of India, a temporary employee is not entitled to absorption in service if they have not worked for 240 days continuously and the petitioner was appointed temporarily from time to time as Hind Pandit and did not work in the academic year from 1985-86 to 1989-90.
[Para 4]
For appointment as Hindi Pandit, the Employment Exchange was given a requisition to sponsor suitable candidates below 34 years of age under Sections 4 and 10 of the Employment Exchanges (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959.
As the petitioner had crossed the age of 34 years, they were not sponsored by the Employment Exchange, which was an appropriate action.
