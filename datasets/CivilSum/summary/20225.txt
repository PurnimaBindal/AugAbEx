UP Secondary Education Services Commission and Selection Board Act, 1982, Section 18(3) - Ad hoc appointees - When Education Commission-selected candidates join service and certain posts remain unfilled, ad hoc appointees appointed by college will have their appointments cease from the earliest of (a) recommended candidates joining post, (b) one-month period after Section 11(4) expires, or (c) June 30th following ad hoc appointment.
