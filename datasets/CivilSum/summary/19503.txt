Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Life Insurance Corporation Instructions of 1979 and 1993 and circular dt. 2011987 Para No. 21 (ii) and (iii) clause (4) - Purpose of - Compassionate appointment - Instructions provide for the appointment of one family member of the deceased employee on compassionate grounds if no family member is employed - If one family member is employed, then another member of the same family cannot seek compassionate appointment - No grounds are available if the employed family member is not fulfilling their moral obligation to the bereaved family.
