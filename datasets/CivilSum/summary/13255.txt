Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1978, Sections 9 and 11(2) - Appeal - The Tribunal cannot broaden the scope of inquiry by re-examining witness testimony on record before the inquiry committee and substituting it's judgment with that of the committee or management, unless it is evidenced that there was no evidence - The Tribunal should not evaluate evidence beyond a reasonable doubt in a manner similar to trying a criminal case - this is not appropriate.
[Paras 6, 8 and 9]  B. Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1978, Sections 9 and 11(2) - Imposition of punishment - Appeal - Jurisdiction of Tribunal - The Tribunal must not take a more lenient approach by overturning a termination order and granting only two annual increments and punishment of stoppage for insubordination and habitual absence without permission, particularly since the Tribunal had already found the employee guilty of repeated disobedience and unauthorized absence for years - As a result, the order passed by the Tribunal has been dismissed.
