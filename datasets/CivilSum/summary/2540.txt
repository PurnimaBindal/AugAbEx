According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, students pursuing post graduate studies in a medical college are entitled to a monthly stipend.
However, a petitioner who was granted admission after a seat had become vacant was denied this stipend due to being placed in the quota for Assistant Surgeons.
This classification of students is not valid, as the object and purpose of the course is the same for all.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to receive all the benefits that are being released to other students.
[Paras 4 to 9]
