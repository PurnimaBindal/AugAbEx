Bank Peon - Termination of employment - Award - Rejection of claim - Challenge on the basis of Tribunal's failure to discuss evidence, witness examinations, and cross-examinations, and instead focusing on only domestic proceedings - Four witnesses testified against the petitioner, including admission of guilt - It is well-established in legal principle that if an inquiry has been conducted and the Tribunal determines that it was fair and followed natural justice principles, it cannot replace the findings of the inquiry officer.
