Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(s) - Workman - Legal Assistant - To be considered a workman under Section 2(s), an employee must fulfill any one of the conditions and be employed in an establishment to perform manual, unskilled, skilled, technical, operational, clerical, or supervisory work - The dominant nature of duties must be determined to assess whether an employee falls under the category of workman - A Legal Asstt.
performing multifarious jobs involving creativity, legal opinions, and drafting pleadings on various subjects cannot be considered a workman within the meaning of Section 2(s).
[Paras 14 to 20]

Constitution of India, Articles 141 and 215 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 - Res judicata - Principle of res judicata applies on procedure not on the points of jurisdiction of a court - An order passed without jurisdiction cannot be supported or opposed on the principles of res judicata.
[Paras 24 and 25]

Constitution of India, Articles 141 and 215 - Judgments and precedents - A judgment once held to be per incurium will no longer be a good law as a precedent.
