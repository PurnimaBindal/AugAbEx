The transfer of the petitioner from the National Museum to the Dr. Zakir Hussain Memorial Museum is permissible since both units are under the same administrative control, and the latter is part and parcel of the former despite being located in a separate location.
The transfer will not result in any civil consequences as there is no reduction in pay or change in cadre and responsibilities.
Moreover, there is no evidence of mala fides on the part of the petitioner, and as such, there should be no interference in the order of transfer.
[Paras 7 & 8]
