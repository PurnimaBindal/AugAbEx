Minimum Wages - Interest - Retroactive revision of minimum wages - Arrears of wages of contract labor - Contractor liability compared to principal employer - If it is demonstrated that the contractor paid the arrears of revised minimum wages in accordance with government notification, the principal employer is responsible for indemnifying the contractor - The Tribunal awarded 18% interest, which was upheld by the High Court and reduced to 9% by the Supreme Court in the current case.
