According to Section 36(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a legal practitioner can represent an individual or group in a dispute.
In this case, the petitioner argued that he was competent to represent the Industrial and Commercial Employer Association of India because he held positions of President, Treasurer, and officer on special duty.
However, the tribunal wrongly disallowed him from representing the Management under Section 36(4) for being a practicing advocate and for not obtaining consent from the workman and leave of the court.
