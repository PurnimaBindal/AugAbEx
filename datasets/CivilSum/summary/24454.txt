Constitution of India, Article 32 - Right to legal remedy and justice - The recruitment process for judicial officers must be completed in a timely manner to prevent delays and ensure a smooth system - States have been directed to comply.
[Paras 14 & 15] B UP Judicial Service Rules, 2001, Rule 10, Second Proviso - Age bar - Eligibility with reference to year of recruitment - Candidates eligible for a recruitment year in which a test was not conducted will not be rendered ineligible - They will be entitled to benefit of age and considered in the selection process the following year.
[Para 17] C UP Judicial Service Rules, 2001, Rules 4(m) and 10, Second Proviso - Age bar - Eligibility with reference to year of recruitment - Those becoming eligible and attaining maximum age in the last recruitment year cannot be deprived of eligibility - Similarly, those who were eligible in earlier recruitment years but did not take exams will be eligible by virtue of Rule 10's second proviso - This is a harmonious construction of the Rules.
