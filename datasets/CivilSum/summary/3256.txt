Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ jurisdiction/Question of fact - The High Court is not prohibited from entertaining writ petitions even if they involve questions of fact.
Not every question of fact is considered as a disputed question of fact and a writ court may determine a question of fact to provide proper relief to the affected party.
[Para 6]

Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Writ jurisdiction/Date of birth/Retirement/Natural justice - Despite the information in the petitioner's service record, he retired due to the opinion of the Medical Board.
The court found no evidence of fabrication pertaining to the petitioner's date of birth.
Moreover, the authorities did not provide any show-cause notice nor a copy of the Medical Board's report, nor did they produce the relevant service record.
Thus, the court ruled that the petitioner's retirement was against the principles of natural justice and should be cancelled.
[Paras 6 and 9]
