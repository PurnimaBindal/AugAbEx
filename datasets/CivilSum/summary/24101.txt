Andhra Pradesh State Higher Judicial Service, Rules, Rules 2 and 6 - A.P.
State and Subordinate Service Rules, Rule 22-A - Andhra Pradesh State Government Office Memorandum No. 172 allowing for 10 additional posts of District and Sessions Judge, Grade II for appointment to Family Courts and Mahila Courts - Quota and rule of reservation - Criteria for selection to Higher Judiciary - The selection of female candidates for appointment to Family and Mahila Courts who do not meet the criteria, procedure, and provisions for appointment to Higher Judicial Services, Grade II is not allowed - There are no cadre posts in the service and no provision for creating ex-cadre posts - Appointing such selectees in violation of the quota rule and reservation policy or considering the posts as ex cadre posts can't be directed - 100% reservation through the selection of all female candidates is not allowed.
