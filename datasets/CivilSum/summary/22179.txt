Voluntary Retirement Scheme - Respondent worked as an Assistant Spinning master and opted for a Voluntary Retirement Scheme offered by the appellant corporation.
At the time of opting, he was over the age of 57 years.
The competent Authority did not communicate its decision in respect of the respondent's offer.
He continued in his service after the said date and reached the age of superannuation.
The respondent's right to obtain the benefits of the voluntary retirement scheme must be waived as he did not receive any communication regarding the offer.
The appeal is allowed [Para 28].
Voluntary Retirement Scheme - Offer for Voluntary Retirement must be made and accepted, but the competent authority did not communicate its decision.
The mere offer itself does not confer any legal right on the employee to obtain the benefits of the Voluntary Retirement Scheme.
Respondent did not derive any legal right.
[Paras 25, 26 and 29].
