Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Medical Attendance Rules, 1940, Rule 2, Clause K - Reimbursement - Medical treatment - The petitioner underwent a heart surgery at the Escort Heart Institute in New Delhi, which is a recognized institute by the Punjab government for the treatment of its employees and pensioners.
However, the reimbursement was denied as prior approval was not obtained.
The government has been directed to reimburse the amount with 12% interest, following the 1994 (1) SCT 552 case.
