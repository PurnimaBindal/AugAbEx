Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Enquiry - Principles of natural justice - The petitioner was not given the chance to defend himself as no charge sheet was provided and he was not given the opportunity to explain the charges against him.
Despite this, the respondents held him guilty of causing loss to Respondent No I - State Federation and ordered recovery of the loss from his salary with interest and issued a strict warning to him.
Only after holding an enquiry could the respondents have taken action against the petitioner.
Therefore, the order is liable to be quashed.
