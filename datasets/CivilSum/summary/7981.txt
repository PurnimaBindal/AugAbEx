The appointment of a Government employee to the post of Chief Architect Punjab after superannuation must be based on exceptional circumstances, public grounds, and compliance with State instructions.
The Government must balance the need for re-employment in the public interest with their obligation to consider other eligible candidates.
Failure to consider other eligible officers for promotional posts is arbitrary and violates the principles of equality enshrined in Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, thus vitiating any State action.
[Paras 35, 21]
Denial of consideration to other eligible officers for promotional posts would vitiate the State action by undermining the principle of equality enshrined in Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
The record and attendant circumstances determine arbitrariness in State action.
[Para 35]
The State is strictly bound to adhere to its instructions once issued.
[Para 28]
Any administrative order, like the appointment of a Chief Architect Punjab, must serve a greater public interest passed on public grounds, even though it may offend rules and instructions.
[Para 27]
Re-employment of a Government employee after attaining the age of 58 years must be in exceptional circumstances and in public interest, as per the Punjab Civil Services (General and Common Conditions of Service) Rules 1994, Rule 3.26 and Rule 7.17.
The competent authority must consider exceptional circumstances to re-employ a Government employee.
