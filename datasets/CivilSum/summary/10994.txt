Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination of service - Petitioner, a project employee on a contract basis, has completed almost 11 years of service with the respondent.
The respondent must provide a justification for terminating the petitioner's services without assigning any reason since he continued to work on one project or another without a break.
The respondent has shown uncertainty about which project the petitioner was appointed for.
As per the letter dated 11th January 1990, the respondent cannot terminate the petitioner's services without following the proper procedure and providing a reason.
The petitioner's initial appointment in 1986 as a contract employee on a consolidated salary is not a sufficient reason for terminating his services.
