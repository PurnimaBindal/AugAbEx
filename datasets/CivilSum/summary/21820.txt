Compassionate grounds appointment is not limited to just sons, daughters, or widows, but may also extend to other close relatives.
The death of a breadwinner in a family creates an urgent need for relief, and thus, compassionate grounds appointment must be considered.
[Para]
Appointments on compassionate grounds based on descent are a violation of Article 16(2) of the Constitution However, if the need for immediate appointment is to alleviate economic distress from the loss of income due to the breadwinner's death and there are no other earning family members to provide support, appointing the deceased government employee's son, daughter, or widow becomes necessary and acceptable.
