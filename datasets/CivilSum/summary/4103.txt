Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment to public service in the police department - 100 marks for physical fitness, 100 marks for written test, and 25 marks for viva voce are prescribed - All candidates must possess the 100 marks for physical fitness - The argument that 25% of the marks should be considered in light of the 200 marks was not tenable - The prescription of 25% marks did not require interference - The Supreme Court's interpretation in Ashok Kumar Yadav's case was binding, and the later view expressed in Anzar Ahmeh's case was not considered - The selection was not vitiated, and the State's appeal was allowed.
