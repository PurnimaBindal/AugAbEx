The charge levied against the petitioner in the second charge sheet dated 931990 is not considered a new charge but a part of the old charge.
The authority to conduct a new inquiry under Section 63 is illegal.
The petitioner's alleged actions are not covered by Section 63, but by Section 55(a).
If the charge sheet itself contains errors, the proceedings taken in pursuance of it would be invalid.
Therefore, this action by the respondents is deemed invalid.
