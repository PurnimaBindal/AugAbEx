Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.28 - Police Act, 1861, Section 7 - If an authority is empowered to dismiss or remove an employee or reduce them in rank and decides it is not possible to hold an enquiry for reasons that must be recorded in writing, Clause (b) of the second provision of Article 311 provides for dispensation of enquiry - However, in this particular case, since the authority did not record in the order that the conditions precedent for invoking said provision exist and the appellant was already exonerated of charges in a previous disciplinary proceeding, the imposition of the punishment of dismissal straightaway could not be sustained - The appeal was therefore allowed.
