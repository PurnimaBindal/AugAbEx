Constitution of India, Article 226 - Deputation - Shortening of - The petitioner was loaned out for one year, but they held a lien in the original department.
Afterward, they were returned to their original department.
If the borrowing institution determines that it is no longer necessary for them to remain on loan, they may be returned to their parent department where they hold a post.
Even if the period is cut short, the individual cannot assert any deputation post rights.
