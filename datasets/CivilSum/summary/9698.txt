Jadavpur University First Regulations, Part-II, Clause 7(II) - Admission - Interview - Doctrine of estoppel - Petitioner complains of receiving less marks in oral interview than in Gate Examination - However, marks chart reveals only 7 marks allotted to petitioner in interview while petitioner scored 85% in Gate Examination - Interview marks are based on answers given by candidates rather than academic achievement - Once a candidate has participated in selection process, they cannot challenge results based on lack of success.
[Para 7]
Judicial review - Interview - Examining body allots subjective marks in interviews - Written test marks can be verified by courts, but interview marks depend heavily on oral responses and subjective satisfaction of the examining board - Court sees no case for judicial review - Petition is rejected.
[Para 7]
