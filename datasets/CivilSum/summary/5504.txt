The Petitioners in this case suffered a disability during their service and as a result, the respondent issued premature retirement orders leaving them unemployed.
It was held that Section 47 of The Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995 prohibits establishments from dispending with or reducing in rank an employee who acquires a disability during service.
In such cases, the employee should be shifted to another post with the same pay scale and service benefits if they cannot be found suitable for their current post.
Additionally, there is provision to create supernumerary post until a suitable post is available and until the concerned person attains the age of superannuation.
