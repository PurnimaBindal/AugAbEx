In Writ Petitions No. 21106 of 2000, based on Constitution of India Articles 14, 16 and 226, Technical Assistants in the Optics Department, who receive common in-service training and perform identical functions, must be provided with equal opportunities for career advancement, regardless of whether they hold a B.Sc.
or Diploma.
The court held that classification between the two groups was contrived, especially since no diploma course in Optical Engineering was available.
[Paras 36 and 40]
