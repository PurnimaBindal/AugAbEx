Under Section 2(b) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, there is a fitment formula for promotion from clerical to officer cadre.
Both the officers' union and the award staff union are entitled to negotiate and come to an agreement with management.
While the management should try to negotiate with both unions, there is no legal barrier to negotiating and reaching a settlement with only one of them.
A settlement by management with one union does not stop the other from raising the matter, but Courts/Tribunals cannot re-open any settlement already reached with the other union except in cases of fraud, collusion, malafides, or patent harm to the concerned employees.
