Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Seniority - Relaxation of qualifications - Public Health Nurses (Teaching) - District Public Health Nurses - The Government is authorized to prescribe and relax qualifications for promotion and appointment - Petitioners trained and acquired eligibility for promotion as District Public Health Nurses but senior Public Health Nurses with less qualifications were promoted due to the policy of relaxation of qualifications - The petitioner's higher qualification cannot be the sole basis for their promotion.
