Constitution of India, Article 311 - Appointment of petitioner on a one-year contractual basis - The petitioner's services were terminated before completion of the tenure by offering one month's salary as per the contract's terms - The services of the petitioner were not bound by statutory rules - The termination order cannot be faulted - The concept of lifting of the veil and stigma does not apply.
