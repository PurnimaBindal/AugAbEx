Under Articles 41 and 309 of the Constitution of India, the regularisation of service for contingent employees is discussed.
The petitioners have been working as Group-D workers since 1978-79 and were provided with a pay scale under the West Bengal Services (Regulation of Pay and Allowances) Rules, 1990, on a regular basis, effective from 141990.
Their services cannot be terminated due to lack of funds, as they are part of the regular government establishment and have been working as whole time contingent staff for over 13 years.
Therefore, they are entitled to be absorbed as permanent staff.
