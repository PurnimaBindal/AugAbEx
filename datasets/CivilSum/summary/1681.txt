Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Rajasthan High Court Rules, 1952, Rule 55(XI) - General Clauses Act, 1897, Section 3(51) - Rajasthan General Clauses Act, 1955, Section 32(68) - The phrase 'rules' used in rule 55(XI) of the Rajasthan High Court Rules should be interpreted more broadly - It should include all other statutory provisions, such as Ordinances, Regulations, Orders, Notification or Bye-laws that were framed under a particular Act and have the force of law and Act - The Division Bench should handle the validity of these matters.
