Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Punjab Employment Department (State Service Class-III) Rules, 1964, Rule 8 - Pay scale - Qualifications – The post of statistical assistant is superior to other ministerial posts, including the posts of Head Clerk and Assistant - The petitioner on promotion has been receiving a lower pay scale than the one he was receiving on the lower post - The action of respondents in placing the petitioner's higher post in a lower pay scale is wholly arbitrary and violative of Article 14 - The court has directed the respondents to immediately fix the pay of the petitioners in a higher pay scale than the one from which they were promoted.
