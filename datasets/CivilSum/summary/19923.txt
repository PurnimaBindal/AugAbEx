According to Section 32-A of Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985 and Section 3(1)(d) of Haryana Good Conduct Prisoners (Temporary Release) Act, the accused who was convicted under NDPS Act and sentenced to 10 years RI cannot be granted parole for repair of house as the government will not exercise power of remission and commutation during the pendency of appeal.
