According to Section 18 of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, a settlement achieved through conciliation proceedings is binding upon the employees who have accepted its benefits.
They cannot later claim that the terms of the settlement do not apply to them.
[Para 7] B.
If the demands of employees are not settled during conciliation proceedings, the government can refer them for adjudication to the Central Industrial Tribunal as per Section 18 of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947.
The reference made in this case was valid.
