The denial of subsistence allowance to an employee under suspension while waiting for a departmental enquiry deprives them of the means to defend themselves, which offends Article 21 of the Constitution of India.
If an employee abstains from appearing in the enquiry, it is bad practice to set them aside at the domestic inquiry itself.
[Para 6]

After being found guilty, an employee has a right to lead evidence at a domestic enquiry under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311.
If a tribunal deems the enquiry to be fair and proper, it is up to the employee to prove otherwise.
The question of whether an employee can prove the contrary has yet to be answered by the HC.
[Para 8]
