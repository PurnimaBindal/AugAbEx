Constitution of India, Article 136 - The respondent was initially allowed to attend an Orientation Course, but then had permission withdrawn.
An employee filed a Writ Petition and the Single Judge directed the appellants to admit him to the course.
However, the Division Bench dismissed the appeal due to a lack of merit.
The court held that as the Single Judge did not address the right of the employee to attend the course, the Division Bench should have done so and therefore the matter was remanded to the Division Bench for further consideration.
