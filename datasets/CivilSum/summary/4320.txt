Haryana Vidhan Sabha Secretariat Rules, 1981, Rule 9, Appendix B - Selection/Appointment/Rules - Eligibility - Eligibility to be assessed at time of selection - The selection process begins with advertisement - Selection carried out following Rules and orders in force at time of advertisement - However, competent authority can apply rules with retrospective effect or make amendments - The legislature's motives in passing the statute are not subject to court scrutiny - AIR 1985 SC 724 followed.

Writ of quo warranto - Eligibility - If a candidate's appointment is challenged for lack of eligibility and they later become eligible during the petition process, issuing a writ of quo warranto would be pointless - AIR 1975 Delhi 66 followed.
