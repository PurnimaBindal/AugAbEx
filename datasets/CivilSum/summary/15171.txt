Constitution of India, Article 226 - Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 47(1) - Retirement - Supernumerary post - A driver who was declared unfit to drive heavy vehicle and retired with no suitable post to accommodate him is entitled to a supernumerary post until a suitable post was available or he attained superannuation.
The petitioner attained the age of superannuation in April, 2008, and the court directed that he remain on supernumerary post until a suitable post is available or till he attains the age of superannuation.
