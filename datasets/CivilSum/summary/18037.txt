Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Providing equal pay for equal work/equated posts - Prescribed equal qualifications and duties for Building Inspectors and Sectional Officers of Municipal Corporation, Amritsar - Designated Building Inspectors as Sectional Officers (Building) to equate the posts with the Sectional Officers - Once posts are equated, different pay scales cannot be prescribed.
