Constitution of India, Art. 16 - Suspension order - Stay of - Inability of the Standing Counsel to obtain instructions cannot be the sole ground for passing a stay order, particularly when no case is made out.
[Para 4]
Constitution of India, Article 16 - Suspension - The mere fact that an order was passed after 8 years cannot be the sole ground for quashing it, especially when misappropriation of funds is the reason for the suspension.
[Para 5]
Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 - Suspension order - A suspension order was passed by the Governor on an appellant who is alleged to have prepared fictitious records and misappropriated a huge amount.
The competent authority appointed an officer to hold an enquiry, and directions have already been issued to complete the enquiry within six months.
No interference is called for while exercising Article 226 of the Constitution.
