Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo), 2(A) & 25-F - Retrenchment - Termination Of Service - The employee's absence beyond the period of leave and failure to provide a proper explanation for it led to the retrenchment being void and ineffective in contravention of mandatory provisions.
Clause 24(e) of Standing orders favoured the employee as he returned within 10 days of the expiry of his leave.
Compensation of Rs. 50,000/- was granted without reinstatement.
