Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 and Order 2 Rule 2 - Res Judicata - Subsequent cause of action - Retrospective notional promotion on reinstatement - An employee who is reinstated with all consequential benefits is entitled to promotion from the date his juniors were promoted.
Ignoring an employee only because of his involvement in a case later found to be not proved is not permissible.
A fresh suit for the declaration of the rejection of a claim of retrospective notional promotion from that date cannot be considered to be barred by res judicata or Order 2 Rule 2 CPC.
