Haryana Government Instructions authorize the regularisation of Class IV and Class III ad hoc employees based on their work and good conduct under Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
However, in Pavan Kumar v. State of Haryana etc. followed, it was determined that if employees have a history of poor work and conduct, they are not entitled to be regularized.
Petitioners, who received various adverse remarks and were only assessed as average overall, failed to meet the requirement of good quality work and conduct, and thus their services were properly terminated.
