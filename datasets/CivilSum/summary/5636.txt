Pepsu Road Transport Corporation (Conditions of Appointment and Service Regulation), 1981, Regulation 20 and proviso of Regulation 22 - Disciplinary proceedings - Employee found guilty - Punishment of stoppage of four annual increments and not entitled to recover excess salary during suspension period - Plaintiff argues double jeopardy - Regulation 20 permits two penalties - Disciplinary authority acted appropriately - Civil court lacked jurisdiction to replace the disciplinary authority's punishment - Order overruled and trial court's verdict is reinstated.
