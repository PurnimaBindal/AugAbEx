Pensions Regulations, 1961 (For the Army), Section 16(a) - Pension or Gratuity - An Army Officer, who was charged with misappropriation and false complaints against superiors during his last leg of career, received punishment via general Court Martial under Army Act, 1950, including cashiering under Section 71(d).
However, no further penalty was imposed under Section 71(h).
After applying for pension and gratuity with no response, the Officer filed a writ petition, which showed no evidence of an order passed under Regulation 1961 depriving him of his pension or gratuity, nor any return from the Union of India stating such.
Therefore, the Officer is entitled to receive his entire pension and gratuity under the Rules.
