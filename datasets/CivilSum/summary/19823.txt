Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission - Education - In 1988, the petitioner was admitted into BA Part I based on her qualifications of 10+2 passed from Pradesh Shiksha Parishad, Patna.
She was able to continue for three years up to BA III until the University decided not to recognize the Parishad certificate in 1989.
The University's decision to cancel the petitioner's candidature had a negative impact on her career, and an administrative order prejudicially affecting an individual or a class can only be prospective.
Therefore, the University's action is not sustainable in law.
