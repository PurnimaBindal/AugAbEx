Dentists Act, 1948, Sections 10-A and 31 - The degree of Bachelor of Dental Surgery awarded by Punjabi University is a recognized dental qualification, and a student holding this degree is entitled to inclusion in Part-A of the State Dental Council Register - Section 10-A pertains to prohibiting the establishment of institutions without permission, but this pertains to the person or authority and not the students.
[Paras 21 to 23] B. Dentists Act, 1948, Sections 10-A, 10-B, 10-C, and 31 - Requirements for establishing and granting permission to a dental college - Mere delay in the grant of final permission by the Central Government does not render qualifications acquired by students ineligible or unrecognized, provided the college fulfills all requisite conditions and formalities - A deemed permission will apply after one year of unjustified delay - Degrees awarded by the college are therefore recognized qualifications.
