Section 7-A of the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 outlines the issue of arrest warrant for employers who fail to provide information on amounts payable towards contribution to provident fund.
The Provident Fund Commissioner's issuance of an arrest warrant for employers in such cases is considered a gross misuse of power, as he could have drawn an adverse inference and passed an ex parte order to hold the employer liable instead.
