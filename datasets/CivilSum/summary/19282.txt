Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Reduction in pay scale - The petitioner’s pay scale was reduced because they lacked Punjabi qualification up to matric at the time of appointment although they were graduates with Punjabi as an additional subject - The petitioners argue that the impugned orders were issued without hearing or any show cause notice - The court held that the petitioners were indeed graduates and possessed the necessary qualifications at the time of appointment - Thus, the impugned orders cannot be upheld.
