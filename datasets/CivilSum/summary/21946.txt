The Employees State Insurance Act, 1949, Sections 9, 40, 85-B and 94A allow for delegation of powers, particularly in the imposition of damages by the Regional Director.
The Corporation may delegate powers to the Director General who can further delegate powers to the Regional Director.
However, the permissibility of such delegation is in question regarding the general importance of the matter.
As a result, the matter has been referred to a Bench of three Judges.
