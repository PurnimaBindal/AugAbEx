Article 16 of the Constitution of India on disciplinary proceedings against an employee without genuine cause states that just like a criminal case, the law brings heavy machinery against the accused party.
This includes disciplinary proceedings faced by an individual accused of a service offence by the state or an authority.
As per the judgment of paras 14 and 35, such proceedings were quashed, and the employee filed a civil suit to claim damages for harassment and loss of reputation.
A valid decree of Rs.1,00,000/- was subsequently passed.
Article 16 of the Constitution of India allows for damages against an employee in the case where a departmental enquiry is conducted without genuine cause.
