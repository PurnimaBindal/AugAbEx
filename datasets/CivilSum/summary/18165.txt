The selection process for the post of Assistant Headmaster is governed by the Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 226.
The petitioners argue that the managing committee violated recruitment rules when selecting candidates for the position.
However, the petitioners left their previous jobs to become Headmasters elsewhere and ceased to be employees of their former school.
Even though they were granted the benefit of lien and could return to the school after resigning, they cannot challenge the managing committee's decisions regarding the recruitment of the Assistant Headmaster.
