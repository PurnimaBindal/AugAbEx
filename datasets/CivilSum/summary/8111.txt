Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dismissal - Writ petition - Maintainability of - Bank of Cochin's orders of dismissal towards the petitioner in course of disciplinary proceedings - Writ petition against orders of dismissal was filed post amalgamation of Bank of Cochin with State Bank of India - The order was given by Bank of Cochin which is not a statutory Body and the dispute does not relate to public duty - Therefore, writ petition under Article 226 is not maintainable.
