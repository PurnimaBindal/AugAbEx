When an employee files an appeal regarding their punishment of removal, they are entitled to a personal hearing which must be afforded to them before the appeal can be decided.
The appellate authority has been directed to reconsider the matter due to this requirement.
[Paras 6 to 9] B.
The appellate authority must always pass an order affording personal hearing, even in disciplinary matters.
If this is not done, the appellate authority has been directed to redecide the matter after affording the employee due opportunity.
