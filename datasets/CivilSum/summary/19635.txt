Punjab Municipal Corporation Act, 1976, Section 248 - Punjab Municipal Service (Recruitment and Conditions of Services) Rules, 1975 - Promotion/Reservation/Reversion/Natural justice - Municipal employees holding a Sanitary Inspector's Certificate are eligible for promotion to Sanitary Inspector posts - If no qualified Scheduled Caste individuals are available, the position can be offered to non-reserved category candidates - Reversion of promotees is not possible without a hearing unless a suitable candidate from the Scheduled Caste category is available - However, candidate reservations must be honored when suitable candidates become available.
