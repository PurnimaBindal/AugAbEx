Bihar Police Mannual, Rules 853 and 853-A - Suo moto powers of Revision - Inspector General has the power to pass an order after calling for proceedings in any disciplinary case, even when there is no appeal or memorial of appeal filed.
The purpose of this power is to set aside or modify any order or proceedings to either punish or exonerate an employee due to a wrong order from a lower authority.
If Inspector General decides to review and enhance the punishment, a show cause notice must be issued if necessary.
He can examine the file at any stage and pass an appropriate order.
Filing an appeal is not a requirement to exercise this power.
[Paras 12 to 16]
