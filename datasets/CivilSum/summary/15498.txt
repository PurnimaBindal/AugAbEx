Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 1638(1) and 16.2(1) - Gravest act of misconduct - Absence from duty - Rule 16.2(1) of Pb.
Police Rules states that dismissal can only be given for the most grievous misconduct or the cumulative effect of repeated misconduct which render the offender incorrigible and totally unsuitable for police duty - The length of service and pension entitlement of the offender shall be considered when making such a judgement - A constable who was assigned to guard a politician left his loaded rifle with 5 cartridges at the politician's shop while he went elsewhere and returned highly intoxicated with 20 additional cartridges - Despite not breaching any laws while on official duty, his dismissal from service was deemed disproportionate - Retiring him early while still preserving his right to pension and other retirement benefits is a more just punishment.
