Article 16(4) of the Constitution of India permits reservation for backward classes only, not on a communal basis.
[Paras 29, 32 & 33]
Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1958, Rules 15 to 17 state that the roster points reservation in any recruitment year should not exceed 50%, even by following the carry forward system in case of non-availability of the reserved category of candidates; Rule 15 refers to an integrated cycle of rotation and provides protection to open categories as well; court should not direct the authority to resort to reservation in a manner that violates Rule 15.
[Paras 17, 26, 30, 31, 33, 43 to 45]
Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1958, Rules 4, 15 & 17 provide for the preparation of a waiting list for both categories, not just the reserved category, and the list is maintained for one recruitment year only; the non-preparation of the waiting list for the open category is a violation of these rules.
