According to Section 80 of the Karnataka Village Local Boards Act, 1959 and Section 15(1)(b) of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Secretaries of Panchayats are considered government servants and subject to rules made under proviso to Article 309 of the Constitution regarding their service conditions.
They are also considered a part of the civil service of the state or civil post under the state, within the meaning of clause (b) of Sub-section (1) of Section 15 of the Tribunals Act, allowing them to invoke the jurisdiction of the Tribunal for redressal of grievances regarding any service matter concerning them.
Therefore, the Tribunal made an error in dismissing the review application of the appellant over the termination of his services as Panchayat Secretary.
The order of the Tribunal under appeal has been set aside.
