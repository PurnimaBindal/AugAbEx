Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 2(e) - Applicability on employees of Delhi Water Board - The mere fact that the Delhi Water Board Act stipulates that the terms and conditions of service for board employees will be governed by rules and regulations applicable to government employees and central government orders and directions does not automatically mean that employees of the board become state government employees and are therefore disentitled from gratuity benefits under the 1972 Act.
The board itself applied to the appropriate government for an exemption from the 1972 Act.
[Paras 17 and 25]

Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 5 - Delhi Water Board - The Delhi Jal Board, which among other things deals with the supply of water, cannot seek an exclusion from the applicability of the Gratuity Act.
[Para 28]

Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 7 - Claim of gratuity - Despite delay and latches, the claim of gratuity is not invalid if the applicant provides sufficient cause, as Rule 7(5) specifies.
Gratuity and pension are rights that accrue to employees upon their retirement.
As gratuity is earned by the employee through long and meritorious service, it is appropriate to condone delay in filing the application while also shaping relief accordingly.
[Para 29]

Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 7 - Claim of Gratuity - Employees retiring before and after 24 September 1997 - Employees who were paid more than 1 lakh as gratuity under the CCS (Pension) Rules before the amendment of the 1972 Act is not entitled to seek additional gratuity payment.
For petitioners who retired after the amendment, the differential amount with interest must be paid to them.
[Paras 33, 34, and 35]
