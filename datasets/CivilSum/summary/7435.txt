Industrial Disputes Act (XIV of 1947), Section 33(2)(b) - Application under Section 33(2)(b) proviso, for approval of dismissal by employer - The Authority responsible for approving the employer's actions must assess the sincerity of the dismissal or discharge, whether it was due to victimization or unfair labour practices, and if all the conditions in the proviso were met.
