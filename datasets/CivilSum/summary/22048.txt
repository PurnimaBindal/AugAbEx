The term "Pension" mentioned in Rule 9(1) of the Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972 should also include gratuity, meaning that gratuity cannot be withheld as a punishment for government servant's misconduct before retirement.
The Tribunal was wrong to think otherwise.
[Paras 8 and 9]

The court will only question the jurisdiction of the administrative authority to adjudicate the proportionality/quantum of punishment awarded under Article 136, 226 and 311 of the Constitution if it violates fundamental rights under Articles 19 and 21 and not under Article 14.
Courts should not question the proportionality of punishment in their plenary jurisdiction, unless the punishment is illogical, shocking or a result of a non-reasonable decision.
The Tribunal was wrong to substitute the punishment without recording its conclusions, and its order has been quashed.
[Paras 25, 26 and 27]
