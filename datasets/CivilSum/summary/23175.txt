Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 4-K and 6-E(2)(b) - Industrial Dispute - Dismissal/removal during pendency of dispute - The Industrial Tribunal must approve it - The proceedings on dispute and approval can continue simultaneously - The scope of proceedings under the two provisions is substantially different - A separate set of rights, remedies and protections have been provided under Section 6-E(2)(b) - The proceedings under section 4-K would not come to an end on grant of approval to the employer under section 6-E(2)(b) - There is no prohibition on continuing two proceedings at the same time.
