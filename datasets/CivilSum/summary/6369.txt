Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 6 - Pension Scheme of 1992, Clause 6(6) - Qualifying service for pension - Calculation of service rendered prior to the Enforcement of the Pension scheme - Employees should be able to count their service from the date of their constant appointment or date of contribution to the Provident Fund - Pension scheme qualification service must not be restricted to the date of taking charge of the first appointed post or date of contributing to the Provident Fund - If an employee has not contributed to the Provident Fund, it can be adjusted and deducted from the arrears of the Pension.
