Constitution of India, 1950, Article 16(4) - Haryana Medical Education Service Rules, 1965, Rule 13, 2nd Proviso - Inter se seniority - The general candidate who is shown as more meritorious by the PS Commission than the reserved candidate shall be considered senior to the reserved candidate if appointed through the application of roster.
If the reserved candidate steals a march over any general candidate, they will acquire seniority, but this is not arbitrary and is in fact constitutional.
