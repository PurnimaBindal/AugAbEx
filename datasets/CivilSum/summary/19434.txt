West Bengal Service Rules, 1984, Rule 76 (b) - Revision of Pay and Allowances Rules, 1981 along with revised clarification in Memorandum dated 2041983 - Age of retirement for Group D employees becoming Group C employees through revised pay scales - Even if they are not new entrants as Group C employees, these employees are entitled to retirement benefit at the age of 60 years.
