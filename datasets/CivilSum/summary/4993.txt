According to Section 9-A and Schedule 2, Item 3 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, changes to pay and allowances, including back wages, must be made at the same rate as government employees, not according to society bye-laws.
Despite the management arguing that they could alter basic pay and allowances annually, this is not a viable argument without proper notice under Section 9-A [Paras 15 to 17]
