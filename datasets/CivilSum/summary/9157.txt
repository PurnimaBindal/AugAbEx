Referring to Article 311 of the Constitution of India and Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a domestic enquiry was conducted to address the grievance of the petitioner, who was promoted by the company as an employer and had not received proper due process in the decision-making.
The Labour Court failed to decide all issues of law and factoring, which lead to certain vital questions regarding the applicability of provisions of the MP Industrial Relations Act and Standing Orders that required investigation into relevant facts.
The adverse findings by the Labour Court and the Industrial Court regarding the applicability of such provisions and standing orders were consequently quashed.
[Para 13]

The State Industrial Court upheld the orders of the Labour Court, which included the valid holding of a domestic enquiry and the setting aside of a punishment with the relief of reinstatement and back wages.
Such orders were not to be interfered with in writ proceedings under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
