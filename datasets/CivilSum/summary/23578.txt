Assam Frontier (Administration of Justice) Regulation, 1945, Clause 5(1) - Appointment - Post of Head Gaonburah - Requirements for an appointment to be effective - The State or its functionaries must communicate an order to the person who would be affected by the order for it to be effective - If the order is not communicated, it remains provisional in character and can be reconsidered or rescinded by the authority concerned.
[Paras 8 & 9]
