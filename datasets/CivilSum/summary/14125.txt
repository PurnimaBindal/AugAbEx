Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulations 173, 178, 179 and 48 read with Appendix II, Entry 7(b) - Disability pension - Retirement pension - Disability pension for officers who are invalided out of service on account of disability that is 20% or more attributable to or aggravated by military service consists of both service and disability elements - Regulation 173 may not mention both elements, but entitlement to both is clear from Regns.
178 & 179 - Superannuation with disability is also deemed as an "invalided out of service."
