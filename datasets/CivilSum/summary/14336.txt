Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Physically handicapped quota - A petitioner made a representation for appointment on a post reserved for a physically handicapped person.
However, one physically handicapped candidate has already been appointed under the quota.
Respondent No. 2 did not respond to the petitioner's representation, leading to unnecessary litigation.
To prevent such situations in the future, all departmental authorities must promptly attend and reply to representations or notices of demand for justice sent by or on behalf of physically handicapped persons.
[Paras 5 and 6]
