According to AP Educational Institutions (Establishment, Recognition, Administration and Control of Schools under Private Management) Rules, 1993, Rules 12 and 13, in the selection process for unaided Secondary Grade Teachers, the selected list of candidates must be approved by the management.
However, in this case, the management failed to submit the list due to internal differences, and their claim of not being able to hold the Selection Committee meeting for want of quorum was rejected.
As a result, the respondents were ordered to submit the list for approval and pass orders on it.
