Central Warehousing Corporation Staff Regulations, Regulation 17 - Constitution of India, Article 311 - Misconduct - Removal from service - Withholding of increments and promotion - A Warehouse Manager that was removed from service after an enquiry must have the penalty imposed commensurate with the gravity of the misconduct.
It is obligatory for the disciplinary authority and appellate authority to come to a conclusion whether the delinquent officer should be punished with a major penalty or minor penalty.
However, in this case, neither disciplinary authority nor appellate authority applied their mind to the nature of the misconduct and as to whether a minor penalty or major penalty should be imposed.
As a result, the vastly disproportionate penalty imposed against the petitioner has been quashed and a minor penalty of withholding of increment and promotion has been imposed instead.
