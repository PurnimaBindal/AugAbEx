Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Qualifications - Relaxation - Although the minimum qualification set by the UGC is 55 per cent, there is provision for a relaxation to 50 per cent.
In a DO letter, the Commission informed the petitioner that they had decided to relax the marks from 55 per cent to 50 per cent for candidates who had cleared the JRF test conducted by UGC/CSIR for the appointment of lecturers.
However, it was up to the university to decide whether to relax the condition or not.
In this case, the university had decided not to relax the minimum qualification condition, and therefore, the petitioner had no legal right to seek appointment by requesting the condition be relaxed.
