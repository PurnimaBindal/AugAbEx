Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Appointment - Air Force Order amended periodically - Petitioner was initially appointed for 15 years with option to serve up to 20 years - Service can be extended for additional periods under Air Force Order 12/5/48 - Petitioner served for 20 years and was granted one extension of 6 years - Request for further extension was denied due to failing to meet requirements of Regulation 4(f) - Court upholds decision to deny extension and dismisses petition.
