Constitution of India, Article 226 - Full Pension - The petitioner retired from CRPF after 31 years and 9 months' service at the age of 55.
In light of the decision in Civil Writ No. 2421/97 entitled as SS Ranade and others v. Union of India and others, the respondents are instructed to recalculate the petitioner's pension entitlement based on a qualifying service of 30 years or more for full pension.
