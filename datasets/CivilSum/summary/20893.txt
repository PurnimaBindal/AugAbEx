Section 8 of the Air Corporations (Transfer of Undertakings and Repeal) Act, 1994 does not save the Air India Employees Service Regulations, 1963 under the Air Corporation Act, 1953, Section 45.
For subordinate legislation to be saved under a parent statute, it must be specific and clearly mentioned in the repealing statute.
The limited saving in Section 8 of the 1994 Act does not extend to the 1963 regulations.
Therefore, Air India cannot evade new certification regulations under the amended act even if the 1963 regulations were not repealed along with the 1953 Act.
