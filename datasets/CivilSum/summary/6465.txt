According to Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol-II, and Vol-I, Part-I, Rule 3.26(d) along with Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3, if an employee's service record includes an entry of 'integrity doubtful', it is deemed to be in larger public interest to retire said employee according to Rules 5.32-A(c) and 3.26(d).
