According to Section 2(oo) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, termination of services of a bus driver due to medical unfitness because of weak eyesight falls under sub-clause (c), and is not considered retrenchment under the Act.
However, the employer should try to adjust the employee in an alternative post based on their age.
[Para 2]
When an employee becomes medically unfit for a specific post, the Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16, and 226 state that the department should have a policy in place to compensate or adjust the employee in an alternative post.
