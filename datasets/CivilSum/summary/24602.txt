According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, Railway Establishment Manual, Paragraphs 2829 and 2832, and Factories Act, 1948, Section 48, Railway Servants working in Civil Posts are not obligated to manage or provide any facilities including furniture, utensils, electricity, and water to non-statutory non-recognized canteens that are started in the premises belonging to the Railways.
These canteens are run on an ad hoc basis by private contractors who keep changing, and the workers engaged in these canteens are not entitled to claim the status of railway servants.
Furthermore, there are no rules whatsoever applicable to their recruitment and service conditions.
