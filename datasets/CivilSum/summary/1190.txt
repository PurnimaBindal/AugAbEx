Andhra Pradesh Electricity Reform Act, 1998, Sections 23 and 55 - Andhra Pradesh Electricity Reform (Transfer Scheme) Rules, 1999, Rule 7 - Transfer - Personnel of Electricity Board reorganized into six separate companies - Allotment of employees to new companies determined by High Level Committee based on seniority and work experience - Challenge to allotment rejected by court as High Level Committee considered revised options based on length of service, experience, availability of vacancies, and first option preference - Option criteria and process are final and binding.
[Para 33] B Andhra Pradesh Electricity Reform Act, 1998, Sections 23 and 55 - Andhra Pradesh Electricity Reform (Transfer Scheme) Rules, 1999, Rule 7 - Transfer - Promotion - Employees allotted to one company not eligible for promotion on par with colleagues in other companies under tripartite agreement -Seniority list no longer applicable as employees considered of allotted companies only - No basis for promotion from combined seniority list.
