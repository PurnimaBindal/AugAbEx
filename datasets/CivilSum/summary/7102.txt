The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 226 and 300-A, govern the payment of pension and other retiral benefits.
A previous case involving the same issue was decided by the court and affirmed by the Supreme Court, yet retiral benefits have still not been received by the employees of the Ravindra Manch Society.
The court has issued directions for the petitioner to receive their retiral benefits along with costs of Rs. 20,000/-.
