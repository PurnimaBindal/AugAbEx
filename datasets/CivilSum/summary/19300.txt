According to Punjab Municipal Act, 1911, Section 38, the employer has the right to abolish a post, and the employee has no right to be heard.
Despite not being a member of the provisional service, the petitioner was allowed to continue working for the Municipal Committee until reaching the age of superannuation, and his rights against the original employer were protected.
Therefore, the petitioner cannot seek remedy due to lack of cause of action, and the petition is dismissed.
