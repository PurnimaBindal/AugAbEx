Punjab State Agricultural Marketing Board (Class III) Service Rules, 1989, Rule 2(i) and Rule 12 - Punjab Agricultural Markets Act, 1961, Section 20 - Transfer - When transferring employees of one Market Committee to another Market Committee, the Market Committee is the only competent authority to transfer employees directly appointed by the Committee itself, and the Marketing Board, its Chairman, or the Secretary have no power or jurisdiction to transfer employees of the Committee which is an independent body created under the Statute having its own employee.
Under the guise of general superintendence of the Board over the Market Committee, the Board cannot interfere in the day-to-day working of the Committee.
[Paras 10 to 12 and 14]
Punjab Agricultural Markets Act, 1961, Section 3(11) - Punjab Market Committees By-laws, Bye-law 24(1) & (2) - Transfer - The Chairman of the Board has the power to transfer an employee who deals with accounts of one Market Committee to another.
An employee who handles accounts of the Committee does not mean any or every person assisting or doing something in the process of maintaining the accounts.
A Mandi Supervisor-cum-Fee Collector cannot be said to be a person dealing with the accounts of the Market Committee, and only the Head Clerk-cum-Accountant deals with the accounts of the Committee.
The Mandi Supervisor-cum-Fee-Collector cannot be transferred by the Board, and Section 3(11) or Section 20(4) of the Act does not apply in his case.
The decision in 1992 (5) S.L.R.
746 is based on an admission and is, therefore, not binding as it is based on concession.
[Paras 13 and 14]
