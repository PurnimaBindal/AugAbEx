As per Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, regularisation of casual/temporary appointees against posts with prescribed eligibility qualifications and conditions and recruitment by selection in a prescribed manner cannot be done without undergoing the selection process.
[Para 6]  B.
As per Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, daily wage employees cannot claim equal pay in regular pay scales as available to regular counterparts as there may be differences in the duties and responsibilities.
These differences may be quantitative or qualitative, and can be considered a valid basis for payment of different wages.
