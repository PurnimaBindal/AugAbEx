Constitution of India, Article 226 - Back wages - Delay - The workman's services were terminated in 1991 and a demand notice was served in August 1993.
Despite a delay from the date of termination until the date of the demand notice, the delay between 1993 and 1999 is not the fault of the petitioner.
As a result, the order for back wages has been modified, with wages to be paid from the date of the demand notice until the date of reinstatement.
