Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Sub-Inspector of Excise Recruitment Rules, Rules 6 and 8 - Training - Petitioners who were promoted from Assistant Sub Inspector to Sub-Inspector and had already undergone training, were directed to undergo training again at the end of their service.
They contended that they should be exempted from training under Rule 6.
The petitioners were advised to request the Board of Revenue to grant such exemption under Rule 8.
