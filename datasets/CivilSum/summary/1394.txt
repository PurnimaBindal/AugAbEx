According to the State Road Transport Corporation Act of 1950, Sections 5, 15, and 45, a Resolution passed by the Board that grants the Managing Director the power to institute and verify legal proceedings would allow the Managing Director to initiate legal action.
However, if a subordinate authority makes a decision to the contrary, the Managing Director must not adhere to it.
