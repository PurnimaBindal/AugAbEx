Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Act, Section 122 - Defence Service Regulations, Regulations 67 and 68 - Army Rules, Rule 16-A - Promotion to Rank of Brigadier from the rank of Colonel - Acting rank promotion followed by substantive rank promotion - Mere selection for acting rank does not lead to substantive rank promotion - Officer's performance in acting rank is considered for substantive promotion - Regulation 67 outlines conditions for granting substantive promotion by selection - Regulation 68 states that promotion to substantive rank will only be granted when an officer is deemed fit in all respects - If an officer is not qualified for substantive promotion, the Court cannot order the authority to promote him to substantive rank and allow him to continue in service up to the age of 56 years as Brigadier.
