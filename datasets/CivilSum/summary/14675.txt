Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 Appointment - Cancellation of - Discrimination - The petitioner was appointed but later had her appointment cancelled on the ground that her passed subject of Arithmetic was not considered equivalent to Matric standard in Haryana.
The petitioner had passed the Higher Secondary Examination (Three-year Course) from CBSE, New Delhi, which the State of Haryana treated as equivalent for admission to higher classes.
Direction was issued to the state to reinstate the petitioner from the date of her appointment to the post of JBT Teacher on an ad hoc basis with continuity of service, seniority, and all other consequential benefits.
