According to Sections 21(2), 27 and 26 of the Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions Act, 1989 and Section 9 of the Civil Procedure Code, civil suits regarding the service conditions of employees of Non-Government Educational Institutions covered under the 1989 Act cannot be tried by the Civil Court.
In such cases, the court is required to transfer the pending suits to the Tribunal.
The Civil Court was mistaken in rejecting the petitioner's request to transfer the suit to the Tribunal and their request for permission to withdraw the suit with the option to approach the Tribunal for the same relief.
The revision petition has been granted with the direction for the trial Court to transfer the suit to the Tribunal.
