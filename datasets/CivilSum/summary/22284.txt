Sections 35, 36, 37 and 38 of the Advocates Act, 1961 - Misconduct of an Advocate/Evidence - Request for expert opinion on signatures before relying on the documents not granted - Disciplinary committee made an error by relying on documents to find advocate guilty of professional misconduct even though no plea was made in the complaint - Committee also erred in relying on photo copy of affidavit to reject version of the advocate - Writing a letter to an advocate of the High Court for obtaining stay for his client is not professional misconduct unless payment is made to advocate and kept with him - Suspension of license is a harsh punishment for not paying over an amount of Rs. 1,500/- to DH - Reprimand is sufficient for justice.
