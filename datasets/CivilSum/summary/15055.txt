Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Constitution of Jammu & Kashmir, Section 103 - Compassionate appointment - Affidavits of no objections and claim for compassionate appointment and undertaking not to remarry - SRO-43 of J&K Govt. does not require such affidavits - Applicant does not need to submit fresh affidavits or undertake not to remarry if they have already filed affidavits from other beneficiaries.
