Constitution of India, Article 12 - State - Delhi Public School management is not considered a "State" under Article 12 of the Constitution as it does not receive any financial aid or grant-in-aid from any Government agency and there is no Government control over management.
[Paras 8 and 76] B The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), Ajmer, is considered a State under Article 12 of the Constitution as it has been established and controlled by Government and government servants or representatives and entrusted with national and international level educational standards that relate to public policy, having taken over the functions of the Board of Higher Secondary Education of Union Territory Delhi.
[Para 20] C. The Bye-laws framed by State authorities and those adopted with a private agreement between the parties in regards to service conditions do not get a statutory force to be enforced by invoking writ jurisdiction under Article 226.
[Paras 31, 34, 76] D. Termination of services due to deficiency in performance and acting contrary to the interest of the institution would be on account of misconduct and requiring conduct of an inquiry.
If termination is contrary to the bye-laws adopted by the school as service rules to govern service conditions of its employees, the affiliation of the school may be canceled.
