Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Merit list - Subordinate Selection Board selects and prepares merit list for appointment to posts of Assistant Foods and Supplies Officers - Some appointed below petitioner without offer to petitioner - Court held petitioner's rights under Constitution violated - Authority directed to consider petitioner's case from date person below him was appointed - No entitlement to arrears of salary as petitioner did not physically work on the post.
