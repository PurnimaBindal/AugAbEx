Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Termination - Award of reinstatement - Denial of backwages - It was held that the claimant should have made an assertion as to unemployment, and it is the first duty of the workman to do so, as the state of employment or unemployment is within his special knowledge.
The contention that the appellants are entitled to backwages was denied as no averment of remaining unemployed was made in the statement of claim.
The order was upheld.
