The Construction Circle of Chandigarh Administration has a scheme for the regularisation of services for casual daily wage workers, which has been approved by the Central Government.
All daily wage employees must be regularised in accordance with the rules governing recruitment and reservations, as well as the existence of vacancies.
Although there are currently no rules governing this field, all daily wage employees have been continuing on a daily basis.
The scheme requires enforcement, and the appellants have been directed to regularise employees who have completed the prescribed period of 240 days against existing vacancies, applying the rules of reservation in the order of seniority in respective categories mentioned in the scheme.
Such regularised employees would be entitled to all regular payments at the scale of pay prescribed for Central Government employees.
Those who cannot be regularised will be directed to do so in the order of seniority as and when vacancies arise.
