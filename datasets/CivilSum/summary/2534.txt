Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Education - Reservation of seats for admission in favour of employees' wards is not Constitutionally permissible - Such preferential treatment requires justification which is not present - Even private institutions are not exempt from this rule.
[Para 19]
Constitution of India, Articles 141 and 142 - Judgments and precedents - Supreme Court judgments are binding on lower courts, and even in cases where the Court exercises discretion, its observations must still be followed by all lower courts as the highest judicial authority in the country.
[Paras 13 and 16]
Constitution of India, Article 19(1)(g) - Education - Profession - Education is not a trade or business and cannot be left to the private sector without government regulation - State-imposed guidelines are permissible to ensure equal education standards - It is the executive's duty to take action to ensure this equality.
[Para 20]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admissions - Criteria for admission based on reservations for employees' wards is invalid - Previously admitted students will not be affected, but additional seats must be created to accommodate petitioners.
