Punjab Civil Services Rules (Vol. II), Rule 4.2 - Pension - Qualifying service - Additional benefit of special/essential qualifications towards qualifying service - Graduation in Law/LLB - To qualify for the benefit of Rule 4.2(a), the appointee must possess specialist qualifications and experience in scientific, technology, or professional fields after graduation.
Those with special qualifications and experience before appointment, like judicial officers, may receive additional years towards qualifying service.
However, LLB is not a necessary qualification for PCS (Judicial Branch) appointment, so they cannot claim additional service benefits for being LLB holders.
