Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 7(1) - Advertisement of aided posts - Method and conditions of advertisement - Rule requires that posts must be advertised in two newspapers - Additional administrative instructions specify the ad must be split into two parts - While non-compliance may be an irregularity, it is not severe enough to void the selection process - A previous legal challenge to the selection process was upheld by the High Court - Merely not splitting the ad into two parts is not a sufficient basis for refusing approval and grant-in-aid for the post.
