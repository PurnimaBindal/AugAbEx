In accordance with Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 179, a petitioner who was invalidated from service due to injuries sustained from an IED blast by terrorists is entitled to disability pension at 50%, along with arrears.
Regulation 179 leaves no room for doubt that the petitioner is also entitled to Disability Benefit cover under Army Group Insurance Fund for his disability.
