The dispute regarding the management or business of a cooperative society is a comprehensive term under the M.P. Co-operative Societies Act, 1960, Sections 55(2) and 64.
As a result of the special provision provided by this Act, Section 10(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act is excluded.
Therefore, the decision of the Registrar under Sections 55 and 64 will serve as res judicata in the Labour Court proceedings.
Consequently, the reference under Section 10(1) of the ID Act was unlawful, and the Labour Court lacks jurisdiction to rule on the dispute.
