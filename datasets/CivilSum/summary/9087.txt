Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjabi University Patiala Ordinance 15 - Education - Admission - The period of study for the academic course of Ist Professional MBBS Examination, fixed by the University ordinance, is considered statutory and cannot be questioned unless there are specific provisions stating otherwise.
The court cannot offer relief in situations where only one interpretation of the statutory provisions is possible.
[Para 4]

Punjabi University Patiala Ordinance 15 - Education - Admission - Despite the petitioner being allowed to sit in an examination and granted admission, their result was withheld without fault on their part.
The University is therefore directed to regularize the admission and declare the result.
