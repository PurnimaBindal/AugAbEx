Haryana Municipal Service Rules, 1982 - Jurisdiction/Termination - The termination of an employee of a Municipal Committee by the Administration of another Committee is not within jurisdiction - In this case, the order of termination by the Administrator of the Municipal Committee, Yamuna Nagar, where the petitioner was transferred from, was without jurisdiction - The order of termination is quashed and the petition is allowed with a cost of INR 1000/-.
B Termination/Ground for termination - Estoppel - Grounds that were not raised during the termination order cannot be used to justify the order.
