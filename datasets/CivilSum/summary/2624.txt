Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Sch.
2 Item 6 - Compassionate employment - Aim is to provide assistance to distressed family members - The dependent must have the right to seek employment at the time of the employee's death - The petitioner cannot be appointed if more than 22 years have passed since the death of their father - The petitioner's family cannot be considered distressed anymore.
