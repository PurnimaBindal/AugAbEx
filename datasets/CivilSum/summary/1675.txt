Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1958, Rule 9(a)(i) - Provisional appointment/Termination - Ad hoc or provisional appointment under Rule 9 terminates on the date of expiry of periods of appointment, and such appointees have no legal right to continue in service beyond their appointment period as they can be replaced by regular appointees through PSC - Termination on the date of expiry of periods of appointment is not arbitrary.
