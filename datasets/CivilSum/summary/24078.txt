Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 2(c) - Contempt - Directions therein - Validity - Contempt proceedings initiated against management - High Court's order regarding payment of Basic pay as per revision by 5th Pay Commission not complied - Division Bench of HC relied on order by single judge directing the Respondents to give a certain pay scale - Challenge to directions - High Court's directions are revoked as they could not have been passed in the contempt proceedings.
