Karnataka Co-operative Societies Act, 1959, Section 30A - State - Co-operative Society is not a State and therefore not subject to writ jurisdiction - Special Officer appointed by the State Government - Special Officer is appointed by the State Government, is subject to the control of the State Government and the Registrar, performs duties under the Act and functions as an instrumentality of the State - The action of the Special Officer appointed under Section 30A is amenable to writ jurisdiction.
[Para 8] B Pension - Disciplinary action after retirement - In the absence of rules, no disciplinary action can be taken against a retired employee who has attained superannuation - Employer loses power of disciplinary control upon cessation of master-servant relationship - No justification for withholding pensionary and other benefits of retired employee.
[Para 9] C. Writ Jurisdiction/Alternative remedy - Existence of alternative remedy does not bar the exercise of Writ Jurisdiction under Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India - Court justified in exercising Writ Jurisdiction in cases of wholly illegal and jurisdiction-less actions that do not involve disputed questions of fact.
