Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Government Residences (Chandigarh Administration Pool) Allotment Rules, 1972, Rule 26 - Administrator of UT Chandigarh has the power to relax the conditions for allotment of houses in some special cases - Those claiming allotment in the general category cannot claim equal treatment with special allottees who receive relaxation of conditions - Claims of discrimination under Articles 14 and 16 of the constitution are not applicable when such circumstances arise, as Article 14 operates only in cases of discrimination between equally placed persons.
[Para 4] C. Constitution of India, Article 14 - Scope of - Illegal favour - Unlawful benefits granted to individuals cannot be claimed by others invoking Article 14 of the constitution - Article 14 does not operate in these instances.
