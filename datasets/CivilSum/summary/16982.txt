Constitution of India, Article 226 - Adverse Remarks - Suit challenging remarks filed - Decreed holding that there was no ground for recording of adverse remarks - State filed Appeal - Reporting officer stated in confidential report that respondent lacked honesty, without any evidence provided - No complaint showing some instance of lack of integrity - Judgments and decrees of courts below not objected to - A reporting officer should have a judicious approach while recording remarks on integrity, efficiency and honesty - As no material available to sustain such remarks, appeal dismissed.
[Paras 3, 6 and 9]
