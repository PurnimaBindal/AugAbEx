According to Article 309 of the Constitution of India and Rule 1 of the Rajasthan Subordinate Courts (Ministerial Staff) Rules, 1986, recruitment for advertised posts must be made specifically for those posts, including anticipated vacancies, but not vacancies resulting from transfer or deputation.
A petitioner who seeks appointment on vacancies due to deputation, since they were not anticipated at the time of advertisement, is not eligible for appointment.
