The Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 along with the Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16 and 226, make it clear that equal pay must be given for equal work, whether the employee is part time or regular.
The National Tribunal's award is binding on all branches and head offices of the Bank across India.
Even employees who were not part of the reference cannot be denied relief once a decision has been made based on an agreement between the parties.
The Bank cannot reconsider and give different wages to some of the part time employees, as they have already admitted before the National Tribunal that all part time workers perform the same duties as regular employees.
