Articles 14, 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India promote "equal pay for equal work."
However, work charged employees and regular employees cannot be considered equal, as they are employed for specific times and purposes, paid under different heads, and have different employment statuses.
It is not possible to equate them even for the purposes of pay scales.
[Para 8]
The Bhakhra Beas Management Board (BBMB) policy states that work charged employees would be entitled to the revised scales of pay as applicable to the regular employees where corresponding posts/scales of pay on regular establishment exist.
The regular employees were given a higher pay scale than work charged employees, and respondents cannot claim discrimination during the period of 111986 to 31121988 as they were not on the regular establishment of BBMB.
