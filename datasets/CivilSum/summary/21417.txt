Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 45-B - Recovery of arrears - Provisions of Section 45-B of the ESI Act are considered procedural and thus can be applied retrospectively.
The right to apply provisions with retrospective effect applies only to those provisions that relate to an existing right.
The application of S.45-B of the ESI Act permits the collection of any outstanding arrears of contribution as land revenue, that were not paid until the provision came into effect.
