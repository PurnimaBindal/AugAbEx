Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Mala fide - Transfer - If unwarranted interference is proved to be with mala fide intention, it can be quashed regardless of the person's rank - However, baseless allegations of mala fide cannot be used to justify the allegation - A petition is dismissed based on false statements of the Minister's influence on a transfer.
