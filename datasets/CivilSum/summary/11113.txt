In 1972, the Government declared Sub-Divisional Magistrates to be the Authority under the Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Section 15.
However, the Senior Sub-Judges were only given the same authority in 1988.
Any orders passed by Senior Sub Judges on an application U/s 15 of the Act before 1988 will be considered without jurisdiction, and executing courts can refuse to execute such orders.
Similarly, an order passed in an appeal U/s 17 cannot be considered to be the original authority's order to decide the application U/s 15 of the Payment of Wages Act, even if the appeal was heard by the Distt.
Judge as they are a competent appellate authority.
