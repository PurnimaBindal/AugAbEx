Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Educational Service Class III School Cadre Rules, 1955, Rule 7 - Promotion - Qualification - Posts of Lecturer - In order to be considered for promotional post of Lecturer in Sanskrit (School Cadre), Sanskrit teachers must have an MA in Sanskrit with a minimum of 50% or more marks.
[Para 5]
Qualification - If a particular educational qualification is deemed equivalent to the qualification required for promotion to a higher post, it cannot be ignored.
[Para 5]
Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Educational Service Class III School Cadre Rules, 1955, Rule 7 - Promotion - Qualification - Claim for promotion to the posts of Lecturer, Head Master, and Block Education Officer - Petitioners who are teaching Sanskrit or Hindi can only be promoted to the post of Lecturer, not Head Master or BEO [Para 6]
Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Educational Service Class III School Cadre Rules, 1955, Rule 7 - Promotion - Qualification - Posts of Lecturer - Petitioners with qualifications equivalent to an MA in Sanskrit should be considered for promotion to the post of Lecturer sanskrit and be promoted against vacant posts without disturbing those who have already been promoted.
Petitioners who have not worked on promotional posts are not entitled to arrears of salary.
[Para 7]
