According to the Haryana Excise and Taxation Commissioner's Office Group (C) Services Rules, 1980, Rule 9(h), government servants must meet certain conditions to be eligible for promotion.
If a government servant is transferred and later reverted for failing to fulfill the promotion condition, but had fulfilled the condition in their previous department, their experience in the previous department cannot be ignored in the transferred department.
This is because the transfer was made with the intention of establishing parity between both departments.
Therefore, the reversion of the government servant has been quashed.
