Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Qualifications - Punjab Home Guards Class III (Field) Services Rules, 1983 - Bandman - Qualifications - The petitioner was recommended for ad-hoc appointment as band-man, but did not fulfill the qualifications as per rules - The Government declined the recommendation for relaxation of qualifications - The authorities are willing to absorb him in the Class IV post - However, since the bandman position is a Class III post and the petitioner does not fulfill the qualifications, he cannot be appointed as a bandman - The court directed him to be absorbed on a Class IV post.
