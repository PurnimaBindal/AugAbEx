Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C - Termination - An arbitrator determined the termination was illegal and ordered reinstatement with back wages, but respondents 2 to 38 were not allowed to return to work or paid their back wages.
Those respondents filed an application under section 33-C(2) for back wages and other monetary benefits.
The petitioner argued that only workers who requested to resume their duties were eligible to file an application under section 33-C(2).
However, it was held that just because the management refused to implement the award, it does not mean that other interested parties who didn't approach the management cannot seek to implement the award.
Therefore, Respondent No. 2 to 38 is entitled to claim monetary benefits from the management based on the award.
