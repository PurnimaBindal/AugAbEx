Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(i) - Reference - Industrial Dispute - Termination of services of the workman - The appropriate government declined to refer the dispute to the Labour Court, stating that the workman did not adhere to the Management's offer to rejoin his duties.
Since the Management is not sticking to its termination order, there is no dispute to be referred - The Court holds that if the workman only desires to pursue his case and receives back wages, without any intention to honour the offer of the Management to rejoin his duties, the Court will not provide discretionary jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution to direct the Government to refer his dispute.
