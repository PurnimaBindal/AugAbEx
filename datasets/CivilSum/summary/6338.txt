The Constitution of India, Article 12 - `Authority' - To determine whether a body is a government instrumentality or agency, the Court must evaluate whether the authority falls within the meaning of Article 12, which must comply with the Constitution's provisions - Whether the `Authority' is created by a statute or has some obligation under the statute will be irrelevant.
[Para 10]  B The Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226/227 - Authority/Disputed questions of facts - Respondent-Sangrur Central Co-op.
Bank is not an instrumentality or agency of the State - It cannot be considered an authority under Article 12 - It is not subject to writ jurisdiction and, therefore, the writ petition is not maintainable and liable to be dismissed.
