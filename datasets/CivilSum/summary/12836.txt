According to Punjab Wakf Rules, 1966, Regulation 24, and Constitution of India, Article 226, only the Chairman of the Board is competent authority to deal with the transfers of Wakf Board's Class I employees, not the Administrator.
A transfer order passed by the Administrator is against Regulation 24 and liable to be quashed.
As the transfer order can be challenged on grounds of mala fide and rule violation, the High Court can interfere under Article 226 of the Constitution.
