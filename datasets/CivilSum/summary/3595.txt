Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-FF and 25-FFF - Closure of establishment - The government can make necessary pragmatic adjustments and policy decisions in unique cases, after full consultations with all stakeholders - Workers of Vayudoot should be absorbed in Indian Airlines or other public undertakings, based on a humanitarian approach - Administrative decisions, if found to be fair and just, will not be intervened by the writ Court.
