According to the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, alongside sections 3 and 38, as well as the Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules of 1957, specifically Rules 41, 42, and 43, there are regulations for the formation of a works committee in an industrial dispute.
However, Rule 41 to 43 establish that when there is a registered trade union in an establishment that represents over 50% of the workers, the distribution of constituencies is not necessary and that Rule 43 is not required.
