The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 35-F and 25-H as well as the Employment Exchange (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959 are concerned with contract labour and the payment of retrenchment compensation.
The question of whether the legality of the workman's appointment can be investigated in court has been answered in the negative.
In situations where a workman's entry into public service is fraudulent or unfair, the court may refuse relief of reinstatement.
Even if the termination of a workman's service is without compliance of mandatory provisions contained in Sections 25-F and 25-G, if a workman is a beneficiary of a fraudulent system of employment or a backdoor appointee, the prayer for reinstatement will be refused in order to uphold established legal principles.
The petition has been dismissed.
