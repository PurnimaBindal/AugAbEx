Sections 7-A & 7-(O) of the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 1952 involves pre-deposit amount as a penalty.
The establishment in question had 38 employees, although the appellant claimed that only 20 were actually employed, and the others were friends.
The appellant's failure to produce records because they were lost in a fire was perceived as lacking in good faith.
The order declining waiver of the pre-deposit amount was deemed legal.
