Maharashtra Employees of Private School (Conditions of Service) Rules, 1981, Rules 37(4), 37(5) and 37(6) - Departmental proceedings - Three Member Committee - If one Member fails to submit a report and finding within the time prescribed in Rule 37(6), the report and finding may be submitted by the other Members - In this case, although one Member submitted the report in time, the other two submitted theirs later - The award of punishment on the basis of the first report is not sustainable, as two of the three Members exonerated respondent No. 2 - Rule 37(6) is mandatory, and all three Members must submit a combined report, whether consenting or otherwise - As the report is not in accordance with mandatory provisions, the order by management is set aside, the orders by Tribunal and High Court are quashed, and the management is directed to go into the matter afresh by constituting committee as per rules.
