Punjab Police Rules, Rule 162 - Dismissal with retrospective effect is prohibited - It has been established that an order of dismissal only takes effect from the date of its communication to the delinquent official and not from the date when it is passed by the disciplinary authority, let alone from a retrospective effect.
[Para 7]  B Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Retrospectively - Taking away service benefits with retrospective effect is not allowed - It has been held that to deprive someone of such a right with a retrospective effect is not permitted by law.
[Para 9]  C Punjab Police Rules, Rule 162 - Dismissal - Rule allows for dismissal of convicted and sentenced enrolled police officials - It has been upheld that the competent authority rightly used the power given by Rule 162 of the Rules, which allows for dismissal of enrolled police officials who were found guilty and sentenced to imprisonment on criminal charges.
