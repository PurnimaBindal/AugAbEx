Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission/Examination/Education - Writ jurisdiction - Academic matters - The Court should not easily interfere in academic matters - The jurisdiction of academicians for examining the appropriateness of syllabus or examination conduct cannot be taken over by courts - The criteria in the Regulations specifying a one-year gap between exams is sensible and fair, not random or unfair.
[Paras 3 and 4]
