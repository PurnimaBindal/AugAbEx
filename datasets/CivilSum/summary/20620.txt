Tamil Nadu Water Supply and Drainage Board Services Regulations, 1972, Regulation 19(2)(h) - Promotion - Promotees can have their qualifications relaxed if they show exceptional merit in their work.
Diploma holders are able to receive promotions equal to degree holders with a favourable proviso - conditions are held to be reasonable.
[Para 20]

Tamil Nadu Agricultural Engineering Service Special Rules, Rule 2(h) - Classification - Promotion - When classifying based on qualifications, ratios of 3:2 are provided for direct recruitment and promotion.
The ratio is acceptable and does not violate Article 16 of the Constitution - the classification is upheld as the educational qualifications for the held promotional post have relevance to the position.
[Paras 22 to 24]

Tamil Nadu Electricity Board Service Regulation - Promotion to the post of Assistant Engineer - Classification - The Tamil Nadu Electricity Board amended the Service Regulations of the Board such that the classification, which is based on educational qualifications and higher qualifications for higher promotional posts, is valid - the classification is upheld.
[Paras 26 and 27]

Promotion - Classification - Qualification - Promotion quotas for higher posts are established based on the qualifications of degree for direct recruitment and diploma for promotion.
The court must consider whether the higher qualifications, as prescribed, are necessary to discharge the duties associated with the said post.
If the qualifications are directly linked to the objective that the rule aims to achieve, then the differentiation that the rule seeks to make must be upheld.
[Paras 1, 13, 14, 16, 22 to 24]
