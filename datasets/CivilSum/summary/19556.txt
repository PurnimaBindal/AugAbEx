Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 300 - Disability pension - If a petitioner suffers a disability while in service, it must be treated as a disability attributed to Military service as long as it persists - Even if the petitioner had been discharged and continued to have the same disability, it cannot be assumed that it was due to natural illness and not due to military service.

Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 300 - Disability pension - The cause and extent of a disability suffered during service, whether it was attributed or aggravated by Army service and even if below 20%, is immaterial if it was not caused by natural reasons.
