Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol. 1 Part I, Rule 44(a)(i) - Additional increment on promotion - Fixation of on higher post one step above than lower post - The application of this Rule is limited to cases where the employee was previously in the pay scale of the lower post and received pay below the minimum of the pay scale of the higher post before being regularly promoted.
If the employee was already receiving a higher pay scale equal to or greater than the pay scale of the higher post, no additional benefit shall be granted under Rule 44(a)(i).
