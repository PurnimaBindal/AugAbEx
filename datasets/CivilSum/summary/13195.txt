Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Regularation of Services - Petitioner worked as a Research Assistant in the Department of Mathematics since 2311985 - Department of Statistics advertised three posts of lecturers, including one leave vacancy - Petitioner's name was on the waiting list - She was appointed in the leave vacancy from 23.1.1992 until 28.2.1996, and then directed to join her substantive post in the Mathematics Department - A person chosen against a leave vacancy for a specific period does not have the right to demand regularization against the same or any other vacancy subsequently if and when the post falls vacant permanently - The resolution through which the petitioner was adjusted has not been endorsed by the State Government, which is the financing and funding authority of the University - If the Executive Council takes any action illegally, it does not bind the University itself - Petition dismissed.
