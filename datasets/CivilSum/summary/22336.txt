Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 10 - Contract Labour - Absorption and regularisation in the service of principal employer - The burden of proving employment with the contractor lies with the workman, but the Assistant Labour Commissioner can request the management and contractor to produce records for inquiry.
The authority also has the power to decide on the right of the workman from the ex-contractor after the abolition of the contract labour system and may direct the absorption and regularisation of their services, provided they meet the necessary conditions and requirements of service.
