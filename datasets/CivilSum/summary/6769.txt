The petitioners, who work as Water Pump Operator Grade II and have completed over 5 years of service, are entitled to claim regularisation of their services.
They are now performing the same duties as Water Pump Operators, for which they possess the required qualifications.
This court and SC decisions make it clear that the State Government must consider their qualifications and length of service and give a decision on the regularisation of their services as Water Pump Operators, and must do so through a speaking order.
[Paras 5, 6 & 7]
