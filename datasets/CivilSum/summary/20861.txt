The Limitation Act (36 of 1963), Articles 65 and 54 - Adverse possession - To determine adverse possession, it is important to establish when it began and when the plaintiffs asserted their title based on their father's estate succession.
The onus of proving the date of adverse possession lies with the appellant who filed the suit within ten years.
As the appellant hadn't perfected their title by possession, the court correctly applied Article 65 based on the facts presented.
