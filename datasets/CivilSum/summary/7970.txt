Civil Procedure Code, Sections 115 and 151 - Limitation Act, 1963, Article 119 - Revision - Lapse of time - Amendment of objections - When new pleas or objections are taken during pendency of appeal to set aside the award after a long period of time, they cannot be allowed.
[Para 3 and 8]

Civil Procedure Code, Sections 115 and 151 - Fraud - Mis- representation - Since fraud vitiates everything and a judgment obtained through fraud is null and void, it cannot be allowed to thrive on fraud.
[Para 8]

Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Sections 115 and 151 - Fraud - Mis- representation - While material was being supplied and received between the parties for more than two years regularly, there is nothing on record to indicate that the petitioner committed any fraud or misrepresentation that caused them to receive the award, therefore it cannot be said that they played any fraud.
[Para 8]
