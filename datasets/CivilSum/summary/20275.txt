Railway Establishment Manual, Rule 180 - Seniority - Promotion - Group D employees in Railways are entitled to temporary status and ad hoc seniority as per their length of service, but once selected for promotion, the promoted individuals have seniority in the higher grade/cadre - Those not promoted cannot claim seniority with those promoted, even if the promoted individuals were previously junior to them in the lower group - Selected individuals cannot be reverted to make room for fresh direct recruits.
[Paras 5, 12, 13 and 14] B Central Administrative Tribunal Act, 1985, Section 19 - Delay and laches - Promotions - A petition challenging a respondent's 1983 promotion on the grounds of seniority in the lowest cadre after 4 years is liable to be dismissed for delay and laches, especially since the respondent was subsequently promoted twice more.
