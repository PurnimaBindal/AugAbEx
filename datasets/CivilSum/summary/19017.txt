Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Ex-servicemen quota - Backlog of vacancies - Petitioner, who applied for the post of Forest Ranger under the Ex-servicemen quota, was not appointed - However, there are vacancies in the Ex-servicemen category and the petitioner's name was recommended by the SSB - In light of this, the respondents have been directed to consider the petitioner's candidature for the post.
