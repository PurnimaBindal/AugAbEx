Constitution of India, Articles 14, 234, 235 and 226 - Appointment of Judicial Officers - Selection/merit list - State Government changed the merit recommendations of the Selection Commission without informing the successful candidates - High Court made its own list of candidates, which was inconsistent with the Commission's and the State's merit list, and directed the State to appoint based on High Court list without notifying successful candidates/appointees - This direction is not valid - The issue is sent back to the High Court to make a fresh decision based on merits and giving all important parties an opportunity.
