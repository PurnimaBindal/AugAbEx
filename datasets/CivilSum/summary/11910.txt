Constitution of India, Article 311 - Karnataka Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1957, Rule 11 - Enquiry - Charge-sheet - Disciplinary proceedings - The Disciplinary Authority appointed an Enquiry Officer before serving a charge-sheet on the delinquent, which violated Rule 11 of the Rules.
However, it was deemed a mere technicality and did not result in any harm to the employee, and therefore the proceedings were considered valid.
