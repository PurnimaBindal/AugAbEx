Sections 2 and 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971 deal with Civil Contempt.
An ad interim mandamus was given to pay salary and arrears to the petitioner, which was not complied with.
Despite the confirmation of ad interim order, the opposite party was given time to comply but still failed to do so.
The opposite party did not even apologize for the deliberate and intentional contempt of the Court's confirmed interim mandamus.
The High Court found the opposite party guilty of civil contempt for intentionally flouting the order without justification.
As a result, the opposite party was sentenced to one month of simple imprisonment in addition to a fine of rupees of two thousand.
