Constitution of India, Articles 14, 15 and 226 - Re-employment in Defence Services - Fixation of pay - The question of whether an ex-Armyman re-employed in Defence Services can compare himself with a re-employed ex-Armyman working in a civilian post in Govt. or Public Sector Undertaking is raised - It is also raised if ignoring part of the pension while fixing pay in the latter case and deducting the entire pension in the former case is discriminatory - It is held that two officers belonging to different categories, one re-employed in the Armed Forces and the other as a civilian in the Public sector - The officer re-employed in the civil side severs his links with Armed Forces and is in a new set up with a new employer - The benefit of ignoring part of pension is extended in case of a re-employed person in a civilian post but in case of a person re-employed in the Defence Services his last drawn salary has to be protected - Therefore, the circular letter dated 3191 that does not extend benefit of ignorable pension to retired Defence Officer re-employed in Defence Services, is deemed valid and not violative of Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
[Paras 13 and 14]
