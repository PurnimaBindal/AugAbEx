Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 16.2(1) and 163(1) as applicable to Haryana - Absence from duty - The most serious misconduct - A Police Constable must be dismissed from service for unauthorized absence from duty - Rule 16.2(1) can be applied for even a single act of serious misconduct because the gravest act does not have a clear definition - High discipline standards are expected from disciplined personnel - The delinquent official was absent from duty for one and a half months without permission, and was deemed by the punishing authority to have committed the gravest act of misconduct making him unfit for police service - The order of dismissal from service is not excessive or harsh - The amendment of the rule after the commission of the act of misconduct does not help the delinquent.
