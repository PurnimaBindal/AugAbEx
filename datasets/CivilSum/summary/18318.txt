Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Punjab Transport Department (State Service Class III) Rules, 1963, Rule 7 - Promotion - Anomaly in pay scale - Motor Vehicle Inspector (Diploma-holders) promotional post - Petitioner with diploma in Automobile Engineering selected for post of Motor Vehicle Inspector - Service Station Incharge is a feeder cadre post for promotion to Motor Vehicle Inspector - Diploma-holder Service Station Incharges receive higher pay scale than Motor Vehicle Inspectors - It is held that a promotional post cannot be assigned a lower pay scale than the feeder post - There appears to be an anomaly in pay scales for both cadres - Respondents directed to reconsider pay scales for posts of Motor Vehicle Inspectors and Service Station Incharge.
