Constitution of India, Article 36 - Regularisation - Ad hoc employees and daily wagers - The government has decided that these employees hired during a vacancy period should be absorbed in a seniority-wise manner against available vacancies - The municipality stated in a counter affidavit that no vacancies existed - However, when vacancies do become available, employees will be absorbed according to their seniority - In this scenario, the High Court cannot issue a writ of mandamus - The direction of the High Court was quashed and the appeal was allowed.
