Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(1) and 10(3) - Lock out - Validity - Workmen's activities causing employer to suspend factory operations and declare lock out - Employer not liable to pay compensation to workmen during said period - Govt. order mandating restart of factory operations futile due to lack of assurance from workmen regarding discipline and production.
