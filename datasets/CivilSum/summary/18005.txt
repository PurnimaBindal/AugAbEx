The petitioner took part in the B.A.
Part I examination but the results were held back because the certificate presented by the Bombay Hindi University was not recognized by the Panjab University.
The petitioner had no evidence that the Bombay Hindi University was legally constituted or its certificates had been recognized by the Panjab University.
Even with these findings, the petitioner was given some reprieve by the University that will enable him to receive his result and take the compartment exam if necessary due to the hardship experienced.
