The Sainik School Kapurthala is established and controlled by the Sainik Schools Society, but the State is the appropriate Government with regards to the employees.
Merely having certain authorities/officers of Central Govt. within the society does not mean the school is being run by or under the Central Government.

Ten references were decided by one award, with five petitioners challenging the award promptly and five others challenging it after more than four years.
It is not reasonable to decline the claim on the grounds of delay, and once the award is set aside, the Labor court will need to answer the ten references made to it by the appropriate Govt. [Paras 9 and 10]
Objections as to appropriate government in a reference under section 10 should not be decided only as preliminary objections, as giving a piecemeal award results in avoidable delay.
