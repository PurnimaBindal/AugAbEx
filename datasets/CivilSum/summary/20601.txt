The Indian Customs and Central Excise Service Group A (Amendment) Rules, 1998, Rule 18 - Seniority - Promotion - Quota rule - The specific ratio of promotions between Superintendents Excise, Superintendents (P) Customs, and Appraisers Customs does not have to be maintained in every 9 posts in Group A after officers from the three feeder categories are promoted to Group A and identity of their feeder channel ceases to exist - Vacancies must still be filled in the fixed quota, regardless of the identity of retiring persons - If the Govt. decides to modify the quota to off-set any advantage that one group may have received from previous promotions, it is not unfair - The Rule is considered valid and fair.
[Paras 7, 8, 9, 11, and 13 to 16]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Criteria for recruitment and promotions - Source of - The quota and ratio of posts allocated to each feeder cadre can be modified by the Govt. by fresh determination.
