Uttar Pradesh State Electricity Board Service of Engineers, Regulations, 1970, Rule 8(1) of Appendix `D' - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Promotion to the post of Superintending Engineer - The promotion scheme is based on merit and reasonable classification.
Once an executive engineer is considered eligible and fit for promotion and placed in category I or category II, he retains his interse seniority in the category.
A category II executive engineer cannot claim promotion over a category I executive engineer under the scheme of regulations.
Article 14 of the Constitution prohibits discrimination but allows classifications that are based on intelligible differentia with a rational relation to the object sought to be achieved by the Statute or legislation.
