Regulation for the Degree of Masters of Engineering Examination, Regulations 6(a), (b), (c), (d) and (e) - Education - Interpretation of statutes - A candidate who fails in a subject of the Masters of Engineering Examination will have two more chances to appear in subsequent exams under Regulation 6(e) - Meanwhile, according to Regulation 6(a), students should clear the backlog within six semesters - The court found that the Respondent was absent in one paper, leading to disqualification, despite the fact that he was given another chance to appear in subsequent exams - The Single Judge permitted the writ on the grounds that the absence did not amount to failure, and the court concluded that Regulation 6(e) should be liberally interpreted - The court's decision was upheld.
