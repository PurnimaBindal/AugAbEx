Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Sections 2(oo)(c) and 25-F - An employee suffered up to 30% disability due to an accident, and the employer terminated his employment on the grounds of continuous ill-health - The order is not legitimate - It is incorrect to stretch the employee's accident and disability to the extent of "continuous ill-health" as stated in Section 2(oo)(c).
