Constitution of India, Article 19(1)(b) (c) and (g) - Trade Union Act, 1926, Section 18 - Strikes and demonstrations - The right to freedom of speech and expression does not extend to holding meetings and shouting slogans at premises legally occupied by another.
The freedom is limited by responsibility.
Nevertheless, the legitimate right of the Unions can be protected by allowing them to hold demonstrations at a distance of 50 or 100 meters from the legally occupied premises.
