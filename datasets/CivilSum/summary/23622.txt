Constitution of India, Article 311 - Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rule 7(5) - Natural justice/Legal Assistance/Departmental Enquiry - If the charges against an employee are serious enough to result in dismissal and the prosecution is represented by a legally trained individual, the accused should not be denied the request for legal assistance.
Denying such assistance violates rules of natural justice.
[Paras 8 and 9]
