The Punjab National Bank Officer Employees' (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1977 state that in case of suspension, the subsistence allowance paid is based on the employee's basic pay on the date of suspension.
Even if there is a subsequent revision of pay scale, the employee is entitled only to the specified portion of their basic pay as was prior to the date of suspension under Regulation 14.
[Para 14]   B.
When it comes to subsistence allowance, the payable amount is strictly governed by the specific rule in place and there is no scope for claiming a higher rate of allowance.
Both parties are bound by the terms and conditions of the rule, and any variation can disadvantage either of them.
Even during the suspension period, the master-servant relationship does not end, but the servant is not entitled to normal pay and allowances.
