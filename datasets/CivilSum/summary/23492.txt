Appointment of Commercial Clerks, Ticket Collectors, and Train Clerks in the Indian Railways was advertised eight years ago, but selected candidates were not appointed.
The result was declared in 1988, and the authorities have not fulfilled their commitment to appoint candidates by March 1995.
The explanation in the affidavit for not implementing the Court's order is not satisfactory.
The Railway appellants are directed to appoint all remaining candidates within twelve weeks.
