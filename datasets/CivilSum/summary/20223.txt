Constitution of India, Article 14 - Indian Administrative Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1955 - Mysore Govt.
Servants (Probation) Rules, 1957, Rules 5 and 9 - Probation - Seniority - Confirmation - Promotion - Probation should be determined by both seniority and merit.
Delayed confirmation can adversely affect the right of consideration for promotion due to non-placement in seniority at the proper place.
The rule that a probationer cannot be confirmed until an order of confirmation is passed cannot be used to unreasonably delay confirmation, and incumbent cannot be made to suffer.
Confirmation cannot be unreasonably delayed when the prescribed period is satisfactorily completed.
Directions were issued to give the petitioner an allotment year from the date confirmation was due and consider him for promotion as of that date.
