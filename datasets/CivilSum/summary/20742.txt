Constitution of India, Article 136 - Retiral Benefits - Deduction - The deduction of the appellant's retiral benefits was based on the belief that his pay fixation was incorrect and that he had not passed the Hindi drafting and noting examination.
As a result, increments were given incorrectly.
The board passed a resolution stating that employees who had completed 30 years of service did not need to pass the Hindi drafting and noting examination to earn increments.
As a result, the board was directed to refund the deductions made from the appellant's retiral benefits.
