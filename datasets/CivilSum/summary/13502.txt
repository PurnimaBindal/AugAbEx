The Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rule 4 denied the benefit of military service to petitioners who had not joined the armed forces during the period of emergency, even though they had served during that period.
The Rules introduced a fiction that a person receiving the benefit of military service during an emergency is assigned a deemed date of appointment.
Failure to do so would make the entire 1965 Rules meaningless.
Therefore, the Respondent-State was wrong in rejecting the claim of the petitioners for benefits related to their military service rendered during the period of emergency, such as pay, seniority, and pension.
The petition was allowed.
[Paras 4 and 5]

A petitioner who had been discharged from the Army on compassionate grounds was denied the benefit of military service by the Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rule 4 proviso.
The proviso was held to be ultra vires of Articles 14 and 16.
The Respondent-State was not justified in rejecting the petitioner's claim for the benefits of military service rendered during the period of emergency on the grounds mentioned above.
