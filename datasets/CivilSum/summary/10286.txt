As per the Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-H and Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227, a workman who has been terminated should be reinstated with continuity of service.
In a case where a workman was denied 50% back wages due to the contention of incomplete 240 working days, the workman was able to present a muster roll showing his thumb impression in another's name on a revenue stamp paper during the month when the 240 days were to complete.
The management was unable to provide a specific explanation for this.
Therefore, the labour court did not err in granting reinstatement with continuity of service and restricting back wages from the date of the demand notice, although the termination was prior to that.
The petition was dismissed accordingly.
