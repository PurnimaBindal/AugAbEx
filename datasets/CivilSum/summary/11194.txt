The petitioners have requested for promotion from Havaldar to Naib Subedar in Junior Commission Cadre as per Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226.
The Concerned Officer must have two 'Above Average' gradings.
The respondents have argued that there was a difference of opinion about the grading given by the Initiating Officer and the Reviewing Officer.
This claim is not valid, and they should follow the grading given by the Reviewing Officer.
The petitioner meets the requirement of securing two 'Above Average' gradings and should be sent for training.
