Constitution of India, Article 226 - Quantum of punishment - In all cases involving penalties, judicial and quasi-judicial authorities must exercise judicial discretion - The High Court has unrestricted power under Article 226 of the Constitution - The court may impose a heavy penalty for misconduct in certain locations, such as universities and hospitals, but must do so with self-restraint to maintain the necessary balance - Special attention must be paid to the type of activity being conducted in the location where the misbehaviour took place.
[Paras 5 and 6]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Backwages - Reinstatement orders do not always require the payment of back wages - Due to the petitioner's misconduct, they are not entitled to back wages - The only benefit given is the continuity of the termination period.
