Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 311 - Assault - Misconduct - Suspension - Termination - The petitioner has been uncooperative and failed to attend the enquiry, despite multiple notices, and as a result, he has been dealt with severely for his assault on his officer.
This case is highly exceptional and requires a no-nonsense approach, even if there were some technical errors in the conduct of the enquiry.
The petitioner has been removed from service based on proven charges of misconduct.
[Para 14]  B.
Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 311 Misconduct Termination - The petitioner was given ample warning and opportunity to attend the enquiry proceedings, but he failed to do so.
As a result, the authorities had no choice but to proceed ex parte and terminate his services based on the report submitted by the Enquiry Officer.
There was no breach of natural justice in conducting the enquiry, and no impropriety was committed by the authorities.
The petition has been dismissed.
