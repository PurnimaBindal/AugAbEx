Karnataka Civil Services (Probation) Rules, 1977, Rule 6 - Discharge - During the probation period, appellants were discharged from service due to being deemed unsuitable to hold the post of Munsiff - The impugned order was passed in strict compliance with Rule 6 requirements, and it does not carry any stigma or punitive action on the appellants - Therefore, there was no need to comply with natural justice provisions or any formal enquiry proceedings before making the order - The impugned discharge order is not illegal.
