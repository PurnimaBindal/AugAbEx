Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(ra), Schedule Fifth - Unfair labour practices - Fact of whether there was any undue haste by an employer while dismissing an employee must be based on evidence - A complainant must provide the facts to establish undue haste - The conclusion of undue haste depends on the facts of each case - Workman failed to plead undue haste in the complaint except in an affidavit before the Labour Court - High Court could not hold that management showed undue haste in dismissing the workman without proper pleading and evidence - Workmen were found guilty of theft and unauthorised absence after such arrest, violating the standing orders of the Company - Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Union and Prevention of Unfair - Labour Practices Act, 1971, Sections 26, 28 and Schedule IV, Item 1(f).
