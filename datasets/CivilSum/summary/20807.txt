Constitution of India, Articles 12, 14, 16 and 226 - Daily wage workers - Regularisation - Termination - The services of daily wage workers cannot be terminated as long as there is work available for them.
When the workload decreases, the principle of last come first go must be followed, and seniors must be reappointed first as and when necessary.
The State authority must apply principles of natural justice, equity, justice and good conscience while dealing with daily wage workers.
[Paras 6, 7 and 8]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Equal pay for equal work - Daily wage employees - The question of making pay on par with regular employees does not arise as long as no regular job posts are available for appointment.
However, the daily wager must be paid the minimum prescribed wages or the prevailing wages in the locality.
[Para 8]
