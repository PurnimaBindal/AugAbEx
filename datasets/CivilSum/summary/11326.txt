The Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961, Sections 27(1) and 27(2) govern the removal of a committee, and no order of suspension may be passed under Section 27(2) if no removal proceedings have been initiated under Section 27(1).
If the entire committee is to be removed, all members should have the opportunity to show cause.
Thus, the suspension order passed in this case was not in accordance with the provisions of the Act, rendering the appointment of the administrator and suspension of the committee unsustainable.

According to the Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961, Sections 27(1) and 27(2), the Registrar may order the suspension of a committee or a member only if proceedings have been initiated under Section 27(1).
As no such proceedings were initiated in this case, the show cause notice given to the Society's President only concerned suspension.
Therefore, the suspension order could not have been passed, and the notice itself was illegal.
Any orders passed in response to the show cause notice cannot be sustained.
