According to Sections 41, 40, and 45-A of the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, impleadment of the contractor as a party before the ESI Court is not necessary in proceedings under Section 45-A The immediate employer does not need to be a party in proceedings before passing the order under Section 45-A, which means the contractor is not a necessary party to the proceedings.
The relief is already provided to the principal employer under the Act itself, so there is no need to involve the contractor.
