Constitution of India, Article 16(2) - Recruitment - Reservation - Post of police constable - The validity of a policy providing for a 10% special quota to children/wards of serving/retired/deceased personnel of police and like forces cannot be challenged before a Tribunal or High Court.
It is improper for a High Court to dismiss an application on the grounds of unconstitutionality when the applicant is seeking relief under the policy decision.
The portion of the High Court's order that invalidated the policy decision is set aside.
[Paras 7 & 13]
