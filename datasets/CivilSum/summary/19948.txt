The University has accepted a reservation policy for women candidates with a 20% reservation for filling up Associate/Assistant Professor posts.
The post of Assistant Professor is the feeder post for promotion to the higher post of Associate Professor.
The advertisement has referred to this policy and it cannot be considered invalid or exceeding the permissible quota under the Roorkee University Act, 1947 and the Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14 and 16.
