According to the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948 Sections 2(22) and 88, the amount spent on meals and tiffin expenses cannot be considered wages under Section 2(22), while the amount paid at the end of the year as overtime payment doesn't fall under the definition of wages and doesn't require payment of contribution.
Sales representatives are also exempt from payment of contribution under section 88, but for amounts paid to security guards, the respondent is liable to pay contribution.
