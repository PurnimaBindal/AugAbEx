Constitution of India, Article 311 - Pepsu Road Transport Corporation (Conditions of Appointment and Service Regulations), 1981, Regulation 22(j) and (k), and Regulation 27 - Punishment - The Punishing Authority is not bound to follow the recommendations of the Enquiry Officer and must assess the evidence and facts on file and in the enquiry report before awarding a punishment.
Additionally, the Punishing Authority's order must provide reasons and will not be considered invalid.
[Para 4]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Reversion - Promotion - An employee who has been reverted as punishment remains eligible for promotion when the next round of promotions is available if there is no specific period given to prevent promotion.
[Para 4]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punishment/Suspension/Arrears of salary - Forfeiture of arrears of salary over and above the suspension allowance - An employee must be given the opportunity to show cause and be heard before the Punishing Authority can forfeit their arrears of salary.
An order of forfeiture passed without providing an opportunity to be heard cannot be sustained.
