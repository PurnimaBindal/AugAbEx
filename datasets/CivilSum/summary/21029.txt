Constitution of India, Article 14 - Adverse remarks - The purpose of writing confidential reports or character rolls of government servants and communicating them is to give the opportunity to improve their efficiency and integrity in public service - Entries should be based on an objective assessment of the work and behaviour of the Govt. Servant - The Superintendent of Police evaluated the reputation of the officer in terms of his honesty, reliability and other similar aspects and found certain shortcomings - The remarks were made based on an objective assessment and therefore cannot be considered unfair - It is the responsibility of the government servant to improve their conduct, honesty, and integrity going forward to gain appreciation from the authority - The appellate authority reviewed and rejected the petitioner's representation - Repeated representations will not be effective.
