According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, a dispute arose as to whether the petitioner was a workman or not.
The petitioner fell under the supervisory capacity category, thus he wasn't considered as a workman.
Consequently, the reference was declined since the government couldn't delve into the merits of the dispute, but the claim of the petitioner couldn't be held as absurd, and a reference to the appropriate forum must be made.
