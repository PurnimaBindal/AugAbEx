Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 21 and 39 - Compassionate appointment - Purpose - Delay - Minor dependents - The purpose of compassionate appointment of a dependent of a deceased employee who dies in the line of duty is to alleviate the family's immediate financial difficulties resulting from the sudden death of the sole breadwinner.
This is an exemption to the typical procedure of entering public service through open invitation and contest.
Legal heirs of deceased employees cannot claim compassionate appointment as if it were a right of inheritance in the line of succession.
If the widow of the deceased does not apply on behalf of their minor children and the minor is not eligible within the period set by the employer/state, the appointment cannot be claimed after that period, even if the minor becomes an adult at any point thereafter.
The petitioner was four years old when his father passed away and asked for compassionate appointment after reaching maturity.
The High Court granted the request, but the Supreme Court reversed the order, stating that the High Court was incorrect in instructing the Board to consider the claim far beyond the period fixed by the Board.
