According to Sections 32 and 106 of the West Bengal Primary Education Act, 1937, as well as Rules 6 and 8 of the West Bengal Primary Education Rules and the West Bengal Commission for Backward Classes Act, 1993, the selection process for employment requires candidates to be within a certain age limit.
Although the appellant sponsored the names of the writ petitioners, they were not issued a call letter on the basis of being over-aged.
However, a government circular showed relaxation of 3 years for backward class candidates.
Rule 6(a)(ii) mandates that candidates should not be above 40 years of age.
These rules have statutory force and cannot be amended by departmental instructions.
The writ petitioners failed to demonstrate a legal right in themselves and a corresponding legal duty in the respondent.
As a result, there is no reason for interference with the appellant's decision.
