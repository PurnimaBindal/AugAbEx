Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-B and 25-F - Retrenchment - Claiming entire retrenchment compensation - If a workman is in continuous service, they can claim the entire amount of retrenchment compensation even if they had not worked for 240 days in some years during their long employment.
[Para 10]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-B - `Continuous service' - Definition of - A workman must be considered to be in continuous service of their employer if they remained in service continuously but their service was interrupted due to the cessation of work which was not their fault.
[Para 6]
