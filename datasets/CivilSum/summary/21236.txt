According to Section 18(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and Rule 58 of the Industrial Disputes (Central Rules), as well as Paragraph 521(10)(b) of the Sastry Award, employees can be suspended before a charge-sheet is issued.
This is due to Clause 12(iii) of Paragraph 521(10)(b) of the Sastry Award, which was modified in a settlement with the State Bank of India Employees Union.
The suspension before the charge-sheet has been upheld.
