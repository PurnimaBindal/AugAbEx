Constitution of India, Article 226 - Jharkhand State Universities (Amendment) Act, 2000, Section 67(a) - Age of superannuation - Teaching/Non-teaching employees of Universities - It has been determined that the age of retirement for these employees is sixty years - The recruitment of employees to Universities does not have a minimum age requirement - Furthermore, even if a minor was appointed in violation of Majority Act, it is not permissible to reduce their age of superannuation as punishment.
[Paras 23 and 24]
