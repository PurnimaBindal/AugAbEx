Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 227 - Meaning of Instrumentality of State - Writ Jurisdiction - Indian Council for Research on International Economic Relations - Despite receiving financial support from government and private organizations, records of the case do not indicate government control over the society - Therefore, the respondent cannot be considered a state or agency under Article 12 and is not subject to the writ jurisdiction - The writ is dismissed.
