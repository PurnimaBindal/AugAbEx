The Constitution of India, Article 16 - An applicant chosen by the Union Public Service Commission for the post of Public Prosecutor was refused an appointment by the government due to reports on his character and antecedents which were based on a single incident in 1983.
The supreme court held that the government reached an unwarranted conclusion as no reasonable person, based on the record mentioned, could conclude that the applicant was unfit for the post.
It was a lack of application of mind.

The Constitution of India, Article 16 - An appellant selected by the Union Public Service Commission for the post of Public Prosecutor was wrongly denied the appointment.
The supreme court held that the denial was unjustified.
However, due to a 9-year delay, the court did not issue directions to the government to make the appointment as the appellant had turned 50 years of age and had served as an advocate for 23 years.
