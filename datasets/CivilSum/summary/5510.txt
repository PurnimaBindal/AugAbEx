MP Vishwa Vidyalaya Adhiniyam Section 49-A was added to create a single cadre of Professors and Readers from the merit promotion scheme and direct recruitment, and to prepare a combined seniority list - Amendment was made to fill the gap in the Act of 1973 - Constitution Articles 14 and 16 are not violated - The combined seniority list cannot be nullified.
[Para 9]
Section 49-A of M.P. Vishwa Vidyalaya Adhiniyam was introduced retrospectively - Article 309 of the Constitution allows the legislature to introduce Section 49 retrospectively.
