Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14 and 226 - Disciplinary enquiry - Retirement - Retrial Benefits - During the pendency of disciplinary proceedings, the petitioner was allowed to retire on 1994 by the respondent.
Petitioner holds a post that does not offer pension benefits on retirement.
Therefore, the respondents cannot pursue the disciplinary proceedings against the petitioner and withhold the rightful terminal benefits.
