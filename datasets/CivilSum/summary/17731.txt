Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion/Reversion - Misconduct - A petitioner alleges that he was ignored while his juniors were promoted, despite the lack of any pending proceedings or notice of hearing against him.
While an order withholding promotion was issued based on shortages involving the petitioner, it was found to violate principles of natural justice and subsequently quashed.
As a result, the petitioner is entitled to all consequential benefits, including arrears of salary, though the department may still proceed against him in accordance with the law.
