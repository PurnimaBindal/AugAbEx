Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab Labour (Class-II) Service Rules, 1982, Rule 7 - Appointment - Promotion - Roster - Instructions - Eligibility for promotion to the post of Labour-cum-Conciliation Officer extends to Inspectors/Field Investigators - 30% of vacancies must be filled through direct recruitment, while 70% must be filled through promotion - Of the promotion quota, 10% is reserved for persons working as Scrutiny Inspectors or Field Investigators - The rule making authority has mandated strict adherence to a roster - Govt. instructions and promotions made during petition pendency violate statutory provisions contained in the Rules and have been quashed - Petitioner's claim for appointment against the relevant serial number must be considered.
