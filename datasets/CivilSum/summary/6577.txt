Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Conviction of petitioner in criminal cases - Petitioner dismissed from service - Appellate Court found him not guilty and acquitted him - Conviction was nullified - Article 311(2) of Constitution of India, does not apply - The Petitioner must be reinstated and acquittal with the benefit of doubt cannot be used as a ground to refuse reinstatement - Regardless of the reason for acquittal, the conviction is nullified.
