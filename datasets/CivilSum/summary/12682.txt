Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Grant of selection grade - While selection grade was granted to compensate for career stagnation, it was not prescribed as a condition of service for SO/JE's, and cannot be claimed as a right except if promotional avenue is provided - Eligibility conditions for grant of selection grade do not adversely alter service conditions of petitioners.
[Para 8] B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Grant of selection grade - Petitioners were granted higher time scale and selection grade than recommended by Pay Commission - Hence, contention that they were denied fair treatment by State in matter of grant of selection grade is belied.
[Para 9] C. Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Grant of selection grade - The condition of having 15 years of service is applicable in almost all cadres of the state service - Uniform application of the condition without indulging in pick and choose is held as sustainable - Claim that prior to 111978 there was no condition is not an exceptional circumstance for the petitioners - Furthermore, prior to 111978, selection grade was admissible only to 10% of posts and not 20% as now - Thus, no grounds for discriminatory or arbitrary treatment of petitioners are made out.
