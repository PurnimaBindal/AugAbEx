Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 2(e) [as amended by Act No. 34 of 1994] - Gratuity - Payment of - The amended Act no longer has a limitation regarding salary - Municipal Committee employees are eligible for gratuity under the 1972 Act - The respondent, who was relieved from service on 30495, is entitled to the gratuity that he was supposed to receive on 1595.
