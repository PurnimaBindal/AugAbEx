Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Appointment - Selection - Advertisement of vacancies - Individuals must meet statutory eligibility criteria in order to be recruited for Govt. jobs, and all eligible individuals must have the opportunity to contest advertised vacancies.
Relaxation of conditions is only possible in special cases according to Government policy or Rules.
Appointing an unqualified individual who is related to a Cabinet Minister without advertising the vacancy is a violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
