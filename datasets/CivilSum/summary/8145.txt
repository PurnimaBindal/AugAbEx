According to Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Section 2, Item 3, if a casual laborer is kept out of duty pending a disciplinary enquiry, he cannot be denied wages for the period he was kept out of duty until the order of disengagement is passed.
[Para 7] B.
As per Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Section 2(s), casual laborers are also included in the definition of a workman.
Thus, they can approach the Tribunal under the Act for redressal of their grievances.
