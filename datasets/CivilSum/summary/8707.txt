Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Back wages - Termination - If a workman's termination is deemed unjustified by the Labour Court, they are entitled to reinstatement with full back wages - Back wages cannot be denied without substantial reasoning - The award in this case did not provide sufficient reasons for the denial of the workman's backwages - The workman is deemed entitled to backwages from the date of the demand notice.
