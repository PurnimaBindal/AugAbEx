A. Punjab State Education Class-III (School Cadre) Rules, 1978, Rules 2 and 9 - Promotion - Post of Block Primary Education Officer - Head Teacher - The promotion to the post of BPEO must be through seniority-cum-merit basis from the post of JBT Teachers as there is no post for Head Teacher in the cadre of Teachers according to the rules.
The post of Head Teacher is an Ex-cadre post and appointment as Head Teacher will not entitle a person to claim the post of BPEO to the exclusion of JBT Teachers.
In order to be appointed as BPEO, the rules have to be amended.
Any ministerial instructions contrary to the rules must be ignored.
[Paras 9,10,11,13,14 and 15].
B.
Constitution of India, Articles 166 and 309 - Rules - Instructions - The executive is not authorized to amend the statutory rules.
The Minister incharge cannot amend the rules and instructions issued by them cannot modify or vary the statutory rules.
The executive instructions can only be used to fill the lacuna of the rules.
[Paras 12 to 16] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 16 (4) and 226 - Ex-cadre post - Reservation - Promotion - Appointment on an Ex-cadre post does not give any exclusive right of promotion to a higher post.
Promoting from ex-cadre post under the pretext of reservation will not affect the right of persons entitled to be promoted from the cadre according to the rules.
It is not required for them to challenge the promotion to ex-cadre post which does not act as estoppel against them.
[Para 14]
