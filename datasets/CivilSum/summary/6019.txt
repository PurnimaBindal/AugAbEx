The amendment order issued by the President in 2000, designating Teacher post as district-wise, is not unconstitutional or void.
This is in accordance with Article 367(1) making General Clauses Act applicable for interpretation of the Constitution.
The amended Presidential order dated 422000 is not contradicting the Presidential order issued in 1975.
It is held that the amended Presidential order dated 422000 is valid and intra vires the Constitution.

The requirement of annexing a certificate evidencing educational qualification with the application form cannot be termed as arbitrary.
There is a nexus for the object to be achieved for making the scrutiny of eligible candidates.
Candidates are not entitled to produce a certificate of qualification at the time of interview.
