Constitution of India, Article 226 - Seniority list - Delay and laches - The petitioners did not contest the seniority list during their time of service and cannot claim relief now simply because some other employees were successful in challenging the seniority list.
Additionally, the petitioners did not make themselves a party to the civil suit contesting the seniority list they accepted while in service and therefore are not entitled to any relief based solely on the grounds of delay and laches.
