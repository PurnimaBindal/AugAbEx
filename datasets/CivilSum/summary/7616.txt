Gujrat Civil Services (Discipline & Appeal) Rules, 1971, Rule 5 - Suspension - Power of suspension must not be exercised routinely or automatically against an employee facing charges.
The authority must use sound reasoning and apply their judgment.
[Para 7]
Bombay Civil Services Rules, 1959, Rule 152 - Suspension period - Natural justice - The employee must have an opportunity to represent themselves when deciding if the suspension period will be designated as on duty or not.
The authority must provide reasoning for not accepting the employee's plea, and justification for the suspension.
An order lacking justification cannot be sustained.
[Paras 9, 10, 12 and 13]
