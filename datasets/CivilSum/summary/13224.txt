According to the Army Act, Section 23 and Army Rules, Rule 13(3)(v), and Artillery Record Instruction 1990, Para 590, an individual can be discharged if they are deemed undesirable for retention in service.
In a specific case, the appellant received four red ink entries and was subsequently discharged by a competent authority who communicated the order and furnished a discharge certificate.
There were no legal issues with the order of discharge.
