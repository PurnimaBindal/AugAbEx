According to Sections 7 and 10 of the Haryana Development and Regulation of Urban Areas Act, 1975, when joint owners of agricultural land located within urban limits sell their land holding, the area sold must not exceed 100 sq meters to be considered a Colony.
In the case of joint holdings by joint owners, it cannot be considered a Colony as individuals cannot sell more than the area that falls within their share of the joint holding.
This conclusion was supported by the case 1984 CCC 499.
