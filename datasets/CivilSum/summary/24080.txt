Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Selection process - Cancellation on ground of malpractices - The departmental authorities discovered significant irregularities and illegalities in the selection process which cannot be upheld by law.
An individual applicant for a particular post has no right to be enforced by Mandamus unless selected in the process and given a letter of appointment.
The learned single judge was not justified in interfering with the order of cancellation passed by the competent authority and directing the completion of the selection process.
