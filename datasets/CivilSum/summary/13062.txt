"Appointment to regular posts in the Punjab Social Welfare Department Class-II Service must be advertised and follow recruitment rules according to the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, as well as Punjab Financial Rules, Vol.
I Rule 19.6.
The appointment of a General Supervisor on a consolidated salary without advertisement or application violates these laws and cannot be covered by regularisation policies.
[Paras 12 and 14]"
