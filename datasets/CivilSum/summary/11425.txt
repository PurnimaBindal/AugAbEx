In accordance with Section 33(2)(b) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, disciplinary proceedings may result in dismissal from service with the approval of the order.
Misconduct, such as theft, fraud, or dishonesty in connection with employee business, can lead to this outcome.
Despite the lack of direct evidence, the tribunal found sufficient prima facie evidence to support the charges against the workman, and as such, the order of the tribunal rejecting the approval application was set aside.
