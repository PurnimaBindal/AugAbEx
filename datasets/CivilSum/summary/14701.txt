According to Constitution of India, Article 311, compulsory retirement cannot be based on record prior to crossing of the efficiency bar and old and stale entries cannot be taken into consideration.
Additionally, the belated communication of adverse remarks for the majority of adverse reports used for compulsory retirement is unacceptable, as it inhibits the person's ability to represent and improve.
These factors were upheld in court cases such as Dr. Om Parkash Gupta v. State of Haryana (1988(6) S.L.R.
370), Baldev Raj Chadha v. Union of India (1980(3) S.L.R.
1), and Brij Mohan Singh Chopra, A.I.R. 1987 SC 948.
In one case, an order for compulsory retirement was quashed as the authorities' lapse in communication failed to enable the person to represent or improve (Constitution of India, Article 311, Para 7).
