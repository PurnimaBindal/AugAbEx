Article 311 of the Constitution of India and Rule 918(2) of the Punjab Police Rules, 1934 stipulate that an Assistant Sub-Inspector of Police can be compulsorily retired after 25 years of service with the Inspector-General of Police exercising this power subject to the prior approval of the State Government in public interest.
The employee must be given an opportunity to make a representation against the proposed action which must be considered before passing the final order of compulsory retirement.
Furthermore, the competent authority cannot consider adverse entries that were not mentioned in the show cause notice.
An order that violates these rules is illegal and must be quashed.
