The appointment process for the posts of clerks was flawed, as the selection list was prepared in excess of the requisitioned number of posts, and the candidates recommended by the Service Selection Board were not in order of merit.
The resulting exclusion of highly qualified candidates prompted the High Court to issue directions for all future appointments to be made from the same list.
However, these directions conflicted with instructions that allowed the select list to be valid for only one year.
While the directions were not challenged, various petitioners filed cases based on this judgment, ultimately leading to the Full Bench overturning the earlier ruling.
The Order of the Full Bench specified that appointments already made from the select list were not to be annulled, as the appointees had already served for nine years.
Instead, the case of any persons occupying a higher position than the last appointed person should be considered for appointment, provided vacancies existed prior to advertisement.
