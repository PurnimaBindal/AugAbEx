Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 2(b) and 6 - Dearness allowance - Provident Fund - The employer has the obligation to pay provident fund contributions not just on basic wages, but also on Dearness Allowance.
The appellant, the employer, is responsible for paying the provident fund for the Dearness Allowance contribution made by the State Government to the teaching and non-teaching staff of the school.
