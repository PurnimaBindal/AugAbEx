Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Post of teacher - Village level Committee and another Committee recommended case of the respondent - The petitioner had more marks in BSc, B.Ed.
while the respondent had an additional qualification of C.P.Ed.
- The court held that the petitioner cannot be considered superior in merit simply because she has more marks in BSc, B.Ed., when the respondent has an additional qualification - Furthermore, the court in writ jurisdiction cannot overturn the recommendation of the prescribed authority/committee who considered comparative qualifications and merit.
