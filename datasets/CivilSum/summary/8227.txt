Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1972, Section 28(1), Schedule IV, Items 9, 10 - Unfair Labour Practice - Company dismissed five union office-bearers - Workmen went on strike and did not work - Company refused to pay wages for that period - According to Paras 6, 8 and 11, company's decision to deny wages during that period does not constitute an unfair labour practice.
