Constitution of India, Article 14 - Dismissal from service - Natural justice - The petitioner's service as Distillery Manager of a Sugar Mill terminated, accusing him of causing huge losses to the Mill in the 1994-95 - However, he was only appointed to that post in November 1994 - The punishment order was given without giving him a chance to explain himself - The petitioner was not responsible for losses caused before his appointment - The Managing Committee in charge of the Mill did not explain why he was held responsible for the losses prior to his joining, whilst no other person had been questioned - As such, the order of dismissal was deemed totally unjust and unfair, and contravened the law - The order was thus annulled, with all the petitioner's due benefits.
