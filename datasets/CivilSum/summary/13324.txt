J & K Subordinate Services Recruitment Rules, 1992, Rule 13(ii) governs Recruitment for the position of Drug Inspector.
The Petitioner was not short-listed because he lacked the required 65% marks.
However, Petitioner argued that no criteria related to marks was stated in the Advertisement Notice.
However, the court held that Rule 13(ii) is fair and reasonable in restricting the number of candidates eligible for selection, and it does not exhibit discrimination or arbitrariness.
Since there is no challenge to the validity of Rule 13(ii), the petition cannot be entertained and is, therefore, dismissed [Paras 4, 5 and 6].
