Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Qualifications - Classification based on qualifications - Promotion - The employer has the authority to classify based on educational qualifications as long as they have a reasonable connection with the job requirements - For instance, a Commerce graduate would be more familiar with financial matters than a Science graduate - Therefore, if the competent authority decides to prescribe a Commerce degree as an essential qualification for the promotion to the post of Assistant Manager (Finance) based on relevant facts, it would not be considered discriminatory or a violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution as it has a reasonable nexus with the job requirements.
