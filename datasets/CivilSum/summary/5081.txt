Kashmir and Jammu Universities Act, 1969, Section 40 - Amendment in Society Bye-laws of Regional Engineering Collage Education Society - Rule 19-A concerning retirement of College/Society employees must obtain Central Government approval - Introduction of Rule 19-A without Central Government approval is a breach of Society Memorandum of Association under Rule 3(h)(ii) and Rule 24(ii), and is therefore invalidated.
[Para 16]
Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Article 124 - Premature retirement - Competent authority must consider employee performance and service records - Extraneous material cannot be considered without affording an opportunity to employee - Political or other extraneous considerations cannot be used for the basis of retirement - Authority had no power to retire petitioner and patently illegal and mala fide order cannot be sustained.
