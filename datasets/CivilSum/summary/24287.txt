The Jammu & Kashmir Public Service Commission (Commission) is competent to frame rules to conduct its own business, but these rules must be strictly in consonance with the service Rules framed by the legislature.
Rule 51 of the Commission's Rules prescribing additional qualifications and considerations that have not been in Rule 8 of 1979 Service Rules and prescribing 100 percent marks for viva-voce is violative of Section 124 of J & K Constitution and Rule 8 of 1979 service rules and hence without jurisdiction resulting in violation to Article 14 of the Constitution Therefore, Rule 51 of 1980 Rules of the Commission has been declared ultra-vires, entitling candidates ignored for the considerations prescribed in Rule 51 to be placed in the select list above those selected by virtue of Rule 51 or 1980 Rules.
Allocation of viva-voce marks without a hard and fast rule is considered liable to be struck down as ultra vires Article 14 of the Constitution Lastly, higher qualifications than the basic (minimum) prescribed for the post would mean the department of superspeciality for which the appointment was made and not any other superspeciality.
