According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, candidates must fulfill eligibility requirements on or before the last date.
Additionally, certificates of reserved category are not accepted in any form.
However, if a candidate can prove they do belong to a reserved category, they should be allowed to participate.
This is not a case where eligibility was acquired retroactively or subsequent to the date fixed.
Since the law laid down by the Supreme Court in St of Hy.
vs. Anurag Srivastava (1998) 8 SCC 399 is not applicable, exclusion of the candidate would be a drastic and unfair result.
[Paras 9 and 10]

As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, benefits stated in an advertisement can only be claimed if they are available on or before the last date.
Any right or eligibility acquired after the last date will not entitle a person to the benefit.
[Paras 12 and 13]
