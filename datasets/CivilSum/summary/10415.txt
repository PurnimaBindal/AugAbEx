According to articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution, teachers can receive a higher pay scale based on their acquired qualifications.
The Punjab Government also issued a circular on July 23, 1957 which entitles a teacher to a higher pay scale upon acquiring a higher qualification in their assigned subject.
However, a Domestic Science Teacher cannot claim the same pay scale as Classical and Vernacular teachers upon acquiring qualifications in Giani.
