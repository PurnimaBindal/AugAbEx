Despite working for 12 years and having been granted dues by the Labour Court under Section 33-C(2), the appellant did not pay the respondent his due as per the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2).
The plea that the services of the workman who had completed 240 days of continuous service were not regularized and that direction to pay a regular scale could not be issued was rejected when the state considered the workman a regular employee of the industry.
The authorities have been directed to pay the amount due to the workman.
