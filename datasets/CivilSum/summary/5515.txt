Constitution of India, Article 226 - Charge Sheet - Enquiry - Penalty - Plaintiff who was working on deputation with appellant-defendant-bank was suspended and subsequently charged before getting penalized with stoppage of five annual increments - Plaintiff contested that punishment and filed a lawsuit for declaration - Trial Court dismissed the lawsuit, treating only issues No. 2 and 3 as preliminary - Plaintiff's appeal accepted by the first appellate court, leading to the present appeal by the bank - Orders of both courts set aside, and trial Court directed to re-admit the suit to its original number and consider all issues without treating issues No. 2 and 3 as preliminary.
