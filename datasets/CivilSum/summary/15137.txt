Punjab Government National Emergency (Concessions) Rules, 1965, Rule 4(3) - Military service - Break in service - When interpreting sub-rule (3), a gap between military service and joining civil services has no effect upon the computation of service rendered in the military during an emergency.
Sub-rule (3) only provides for computation and non-computation of periods of gap if it is longer than one year.
Thus, the benefit of military service under Rule 4 cannot be denied solely because there is a gap in joining civil service after discharge from military service.
