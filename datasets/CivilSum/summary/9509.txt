According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, disability pension was granted to the petitioner, who was initially found medically fit upon entering into military service and placed in category 'A', with no adverse remarks regarding his health.
After serving in the army for 24 years and 10 months, a Medical Board determined the petitioner to be a case of hypertension with a disability assessed at 30% for two years, which can be attributed to his service in the army.
As a result, the petitioner is eligible for the benefits of a disability pension.
