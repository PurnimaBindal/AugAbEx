Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Regularisation - Petitioners were employed on contract basis - They claim entitlement to regularisation and equal pay with regular employees - The court disagrees, noting that the petitioners were hired specifically for a project on a consolidated salary - The fact that they hold same qualifications as regular employees does not entitle them to the same salary - The job responsibilities of regular employees are qualitatively different and greater - This contractual appointment does not give the petitioners the right to continue to work after the project finishes - The writ has been dismissed.
[Paras 12 and 14]
