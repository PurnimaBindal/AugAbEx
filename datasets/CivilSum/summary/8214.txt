According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, an application was made by the management to seek a Handwriting Expert report to prove that the appointment letter and joining report were forged by the workman.
However, the application was rejected and the Labour Court's award was deemed unsustainable.
The case has been remanded in order to allow the employer to examine the Handwriting Expert.
