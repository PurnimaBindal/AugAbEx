Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - Schedule Tribe - Recruitment based on false certificate of ST - A commitment must be made to not take advantage of a false certificate of ST for promotions, other family members, or legal representatives - Such commitment will not affect or influence the court's decision - In the public interest, a person who secures a job under fraudulent circumstances cannot remain in service - However, in the case of Milind, a doctor was retained in service in the public interest because the state had invested a significant amount of public funds in him.
