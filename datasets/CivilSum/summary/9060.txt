Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Delay/Appointment/Selection - The petitioner, along with other candidates, was selected and issued appointment letters.
However, the appointment letters were cancelled before they could join.
The court then directed the appointment of a candidate below the petitioner in merit, which would be a violation of Articles 14 and 16 if it denies the rightful claim of a more meritorious candidate.
The petitioner had cause of action from the appointment of the person who ranked below them in the merit list.
It would be unfair to deny the petitioner's legitimate right, especially since the delay was only technical.
The LPA was dismissed.
