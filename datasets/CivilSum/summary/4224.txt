The Industrial Disputes Act's Section 33-C(1) and (2) scope covers lay-offs and closures.
Computation of benefits for claims related to money and determination of their existence, as well as any incidental enquiry, falls under Section 33-C(2).
Even if it was a case of lay-off or closure, the management's dispute of a claim does not bar jurisdiction.
To grant a claim under Section 33-C(2) of the Act, the Labour Court has the authority to hold an enquiry if the employee has been deprived of work illegally and in violation of the law or any ID Act provision.
The case law is discussed in Paras 5 and 6.
