Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 2(1)(d) - Compensation - Dependency - Deceased was an unmarried son of claimants - According to Section 2(1)(d), parents are dependants of deceased unmarried son - Commissioner's decision to not disburse share of father's compensation amount on the ground of not being a dependant of his son was improper.
[Paras 7 and 9] B. Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 8(6) and (7) - Compensation - Disbursal of compensation amount to female-claimant - It is not obligatory to invest compensation amount in fixed deposit payable to female-claimant in every case.
