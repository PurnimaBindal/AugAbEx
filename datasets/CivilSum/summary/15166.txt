Punjab Tubewell Corporation Service Bye-Laws, 1977, Annexure `B', Clause 28 - Promotion from Tracer to Draftsman - Requirements for promotion - Promotions are based on diploma and experience - Tracers who hold an ITI diploma in Draftsmanship and have two years of experience as Tracers do not need to pass the Departmental Examination.
