The Uttar Pradesh Krishi Utpadan Mandi Adhiniyam, 1964, has provisions about the recruitment of employees of the Board and Market Committees on ad hoc and daily wage basis.
Appointments made without following the procedure prescribed in the regulations are not legal and authorised, and no appointment can be made in violation of the provisions of the Act and the Rules.
Those hired as daily wage, work charged and part-time employees do not fall under the definition of "employee" and such appointments have no legal relationship with the employer and have no right to continue in service.
The Board cannot make decisions beyond the scope of the Act and the Rules.
In terms of policy decisions of the State for its statutory bodies, the State government can issue directions only on questions of policy but cannot interfere in the day-to-day functioning of its statutory bodies.
Additionally, the powers of the Board are to be exercised by the Board, not by the State Govt.
The principles of natural justice are not attracted in the case of retrenchment in terms of the statutory provisions of the UP Industrial Disputes Act 1947, and appointments made de hors the statutory provisions cannot be regularised.
Directions to reinstate should not be issued by the Court unless there exists a post, and even if the post exists, the employer may not be interested to fill up the same.
