Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Retrospective promotion - Arrears of pay in promotional pay scale - The employee cannot be held responsible for delay in promotion if it is due to the non-initiation of his promotion case by the competent authority - The competent authority is obligated to initiate promotion cases as required - If an employee is retroactively promoted, they must be granted monetary benefits from the date of promotion.
