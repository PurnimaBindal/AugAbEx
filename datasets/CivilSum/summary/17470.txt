Based on the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, the classification and promotion for Sub-Station Attendants was made according to their experience in working as Auxiliary Plant Attendants.
It was deemed reasonable to only appoint those dealing with power generation as Auxiliary Plant Attendants, while Sub-Station Attendants were promoted to Sub Station Operators.
This classification was based on the differences in nature of duties and experience between the two categories of employees, and all eligible persons were considered, without violating any rules or instructions.
The Board's actions cannot be considered unfair, illegal or arbitrary in any manner.
