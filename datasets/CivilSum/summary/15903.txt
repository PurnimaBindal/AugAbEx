Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering and Technology Rules, Rules 512 and 9 - Admissions - Education - Promotion to Degree Programme - Students who pass the Diploma Programme can apply for admission to the higher module against 50% of the seats and promotions are based strictly on merit.
Petitioner, who was admitted in the 1993 batch but couldn't attend classes due to illness, was allowed to join the 1994 batch.
He will be considered as completing all credit requirements with the 1994 batch and cannot claim promotion with the 1993 batch.
However, he is not ineligible under Rule 9 and is entitled to promotion to the Degree programme on the basis of merit with the 1994-96 batch even though he couldn't complete the Diploma Programme within the prescribed duration from 1993.
His duration would be computed as if he joined the 1994 batch originally, as he had severed his connection with the 1993 batch.
