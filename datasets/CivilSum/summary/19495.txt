Education - According to Punjabi University's ordinance, if a candidate fails the supplementary exam, their provisional admission to the next class shall be cancelled.
However, if a candidate only fails one subject, they may continue in the next class and their results would only be declared after they pass the re-appear exam.
The petitioner failed in two subjects and the University was right to deny permission to appear for the second year exam.
Until Panjab University creates its own regulations, Punjabi University's ordinances will apply to the courses.
