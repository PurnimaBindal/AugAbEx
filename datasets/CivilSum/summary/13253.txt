Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume 1 Part I, Rule 44(a)(i) - Pay fixation - Petitioners appointed teachers granted higher pay scale on acquiring higher qualification - After allowing one more increment under Rule 44(a)(i), petitioners were subsequently given promotion as masters - However, one increment was withdrawn as petitioners were already drawing pay in the same scale and not discharging duties of higher responsibility - The court held that the petitioners were promoted to a post of higher responsibility and duty, and were therefore entitled to the increment - The order issued by the respondent is not sustainable and is liable to be quashed.
