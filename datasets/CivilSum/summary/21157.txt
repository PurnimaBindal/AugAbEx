As per Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 and Police Office Manual, Para 191, an individual appointed against a supernumerary post on court orders should still be considered for promotion even if their appeal is pending.
The petitioner in this case, who retired before the appeal decision was made, was entitled to be considered for promotion and the authorities have been directed to take this into account according to the court's directions in the appeal.
