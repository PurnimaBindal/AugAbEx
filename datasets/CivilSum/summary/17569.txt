Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Qualification/Pay scale - JBT teachers with higher/improved qualification are entitled to higher pay scale as per Punjab Govt. instructions dated 23.7.1957 adopted by the Haryana Govt. Those who upgraded their qualifications by passing intermediate or BA (I) examination before cut-off date i.e. 931990, are eligible for higher pay scale as per Wazir Singh's case.
