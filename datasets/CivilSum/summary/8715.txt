Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F - Retrenchment - Termination of services of a typist - The Labour Court deemed the termination of the workman to be illegal and unjustified and ordered for reinstatement with full back wages and continuity of service as the workman had worked for over 240 days and Section 25-F of the Act was not complied with - The department did not produce any evidence before the Labour Court and, after reviewing the record and award of the Labour Court, no error or illegality was found in the award - Thus, the award of the Labour Court was upheld and the writ petition was dismissed.
