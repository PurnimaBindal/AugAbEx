Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work - Qualifications - While Article 14 prohibits discrimination, it still allows for reasonable classification based on intelligible differentia - And the classification based on qualifications is a well-established and accepted concept - Therefore, despite equal work, different qualifications can lead to different pay scales.
