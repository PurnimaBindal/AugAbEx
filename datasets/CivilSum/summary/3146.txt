The Trial Court ordered defendant College to admit plaintiff No. 1 and 2 and cancel admission of defendants Nos. 2 and 6 in the year 1997-98 for BE (Mech.
Engg.).
The Appellate Court initially stayed the execution of the trial Court's order, but later vacated it.
The petitioner college argued that the stay could not have been vacated due to the pendency of the appeal.
It is deemed inappropriate to create an additional seat in a professional course, especially when the first semester of the course has already been completed.
The impugned order was overturned, although the Lower Appellate Court was directed to dispose of the appeal quickly.
