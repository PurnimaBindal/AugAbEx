According to Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, a workman appointed on 18121989 and whose services were terminated on 3890, is not entitled to the protection of the Act - as the workman did not render service for the statutory minimum required 240 days during 12 calendar months.
[Paras 14, 17 and 18]
In the case where a workman continued to be on probation at the time of termination of service, the clause (bb) of Section 2(oo) of the Industrial Disputes Act would be applicable - therefore, Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act would not be attracted.
[Para 43]
