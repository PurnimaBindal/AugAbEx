According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, students who challenge the discontinuance of an MD course with a college cannot expect an admission assurance from Panjab University.
The Central Council of Indian Medicine and the Chandigarh Administration granted provisional permission, and while the college claimed to have obtained prior approval from the University, the Inspection Committee found that the infrastructure facilities were inadequate.
In the end, petitioners who were admitted without authority or proper infrastructure assumed the risk and responsibility of their own actions, and are not eligible to pursue a writ against the Council and the University.
