Constitution of India, Articles 309, 310 and 311 - University Grants Commission Notification dated 24121998 prescribing the retirement age for University and Colleges teachers - UGC recommendations for retirement age do not have to be followed by the 'State' - However, these recommendations can be binding for Universities and private colleges - State Govts.
have the right to set different service conditions for their teachers/employees - The UGC's suggestion to increase the retirement age for College Teachers to 60 from 58 was held to not be binding on the States - The Central Govt.'s actions are not necessarily binding on the State Govt. unless the State Govt. decides to accept these actions consciously.
