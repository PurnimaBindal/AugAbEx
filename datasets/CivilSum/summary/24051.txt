The termination of a temporary employee during probation through Regulation 19(3)(b) before enquiry report reception with an inconspicuous order is illegal as it is found to be punitive by the court.
[Paras 4 and 5] B.
The termination of a temporary employee who retained his juniors is in violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
[Para 6] C. An employee who was unlawfully terminated is entitled to full back wages, even if they were practising as an advocate during that period.
However, an amount equal to only subsistence allowance after deducting the admitted income must be paid for the period that they were practising.
