Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Forest Subordinate Services (Ministerial Section) Rules, 1943 - Absorption - Seniority - Absorption in the Department is not a guaranteed right - The employment of the private respondents was associated with the schemes for implementation, which meant that they could be terminated on completion of the schemes or if found surplus in the Schemes - Therefore, they were not entitled to demand absorption in the Forest Department - Their absorption was a result of the government's decision to create some posts instead of laying them off - Even the Chief Principal Conservator of Forests had specified that, on absorption, they would be ranked last in terms of seniority among the existing employees of the Department - The terms and conditions agreed upon for absorption must be followed.
[Para 9]   B Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Forest Subordinate Services (Ministerial Section) Rules, 1943, Rule 9 - Seniority - The seniority of employees must be determined starting from the date of their appointment to a permanent vacancy in each class - The clauses (a) and (b) of Rule 9 are applicable in cases where two or more employees are appointed on the same date - Rule 9(b) is not applicable in the case of the private respondents, who were not transferred from another department but were absorbed as surplus staff of the DRDA/DPAP Schemes - Their seniority in the Forest Department will only be calculated from the date of their absorption, and not from their appointment in the DRDA/DPAP Schemes.
