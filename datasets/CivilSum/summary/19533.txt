Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Qualification - Eligibility - Laboratory Attendants must possess a minimum qualification of Matric with Physics and Chemistry and have passed Matric with Punjabi - Although many petitioners met these requirements, they were not considered for appointment and no valid reasons were provided - The official respondents did not have the right to discriminate between the petitioners and respondents 3 to 5 who possessed the same qualifications and were similarly situated - The seniority of the petitioners gave them the right to be considered for appointment to posts of Clerks/Laboratory Attendants in Class-III - Since there is no record of private respondents undergoing the required type test in Punjabi, the official respondents cannot ignore the case of the petitioners - The petitioners are entitled to the appropriate relief.
