Transfer/Seniority - Govt. servants have the option to transfer to Electricity Board with pre-transfer seniority being disputed.
After settling the dispute, the seniority was revised by the government and the revised seniority was sent to the Board to correct the seniority list.
The government has the power to revise pre-transfer seniority.
[Paras 12 and 15]

Govt. Servant/Transfer - A Govt. servant who is transferred to Electricity Board is no longer a Govt. servant from the date of transfer to the Board.
[Para 15]
