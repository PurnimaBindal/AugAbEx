The Court ordered the authority to verify the authenticity of the workmen's experience certificate himself by involving them in the investigation, as per Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 on natural justice and hearing.
Simply having a hearing for the sake of having one would not be sufficient to comply with the principles of natural justice.
The Labour Court rightly overturned the order and allowed for a proper investigation to be conducted.
