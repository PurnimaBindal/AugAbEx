L.I.C.
(Agents) Regulations, 1972, Regulation 16 - Termination of Agency - Agent - Appeal - The petitioner received show cause notice for possible termination of his agency under Regln.
16 of LIC Agents Regulations due to his lapses.
The petitioner accepted the allegations and appealed for a sympathetic view.
Despite the petitioner's plea, his agency was terminated in the best interest of the Corporation and policy-holders.
The appeal was also denied, with the appellate authority thoroughly reviewing the record and considering all claims.
Due to the petitioner's conduct, the authorities lost confidence in him, making it an unfit case for court interference.
However, the authorities may still reconsider his case for a fresh appointment.
