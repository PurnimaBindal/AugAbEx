According to the Constitution of India, as per Articles 14 and 16, in a case where an inquiry conducted by the management is found invalid, and the Tribunal orders reinstatement of a worker without back wages, the employer has the right to sustain its action of dismissal before the Tribunal by adducing evidence before it in case of 'no enquiry' or 'defective inquiry'.
A lenient view was taken, and the workman was reinstated without back wages.
Due to the invalidity of the inquiry, the workman was not entitled to back wages, as rightly held.
[Paras 7 & 8]
