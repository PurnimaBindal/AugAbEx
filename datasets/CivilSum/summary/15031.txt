Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Delay in payment of retirement benefits - Interest to be granted - The appellants could have only withheld a reasonable amount of gratuity in the presence of an enquiry against the respondent at the time of retirement - There is no valid reason to withhold the payment of GPF and leave encashment - The trial and first appellate court were correct in awarding interest of 18% on delayed payment - The order was appropriate.
