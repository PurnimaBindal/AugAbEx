According to Secs.
25(F) and 2(oo)(bb) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, retrenchment allowance is required.
However, in the case of a daily rated worker who has worked for one year and is terminated due to a decrease in work, the termination is not illegal for non-payment of retrenchment allowance because the worker is covered by Sec. 2(oo)(bb).
