Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 -`Own Rank and Pay' - Departmental enquiry pending against the petitioner, who is a Head Constable holding the post of ASI in his own rank and pay - The withdrawal of own rank and pay is permissible -It is held that an individual holding a particular rank on own rank and pay basis does not have the right to continue and enjoy that rank - The rank can be taken away regardless of whether the individual is under suspicion or no inquiry has been held - The petitioner has no right to continue on own rank and pay as ASI.
