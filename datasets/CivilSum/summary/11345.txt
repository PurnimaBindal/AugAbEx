Punjab Electrical Inspectorate (State Service Class II) Rules, 1979, Rule 4-B - Scope - The scope of the rules does not extend to Assistant Electrical Inspectors.
[Paras 7 and 8]  B. Punjab Electrical Inspectorate (State Service Class II) Rules, 1956, Rule 4-B - Scope - Qualifications are prescribed under the 1956 Rules only when the State Government appoints Electrical Inspectors or Assistant Electrical Inspectors.
