Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Officer - Promotion/Consideration - If an Army officer converts their Furlough into annual leave, their report for that year must be considered for their suitability for promotion for the full year.
However, this does not automatically result in promotion, only the right for consideration accrues.
The writ court will not interfere unless there is a violation of any Rule or procedure for promotion.
It is not the function of the writ court to function as a court of appeal in the matter of consideration of claims for promotions.
The appropriate Selection Boards must perform this function.
The court is more reluctant in cases of defence, as the needs and standards are fairly different from those applicable to civil posts.
[Paras 11 and 12]
