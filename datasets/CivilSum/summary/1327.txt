The appointment of an Additional Advocate General for the state is made in accordance with a government notification under Articles 165 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
The duties and functions of the Additional Advocate General must not conflict with those of the Advocate General, and the appointment itself does not violate Article 165.
Furthermore, a public interest petition was filed more than three years after the appointment, which demonstrates an unreasonable delay and unsuitability for involvement.
