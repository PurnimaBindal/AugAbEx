Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Withholding of Service Benefits and documents - A petitioner alleged of defalcation, joined another school as Headmaster and sought transfer of benefits.
However, the said allegation was not substantiated before any court of law and there was no legal sanction for demanding the petitioner to pay Rs 64448/-.
As a result, the non-deposit of the amount cannot be considered as the basis for withholding benefits.
The school can initiate appropriate legal proceedings as per law.
