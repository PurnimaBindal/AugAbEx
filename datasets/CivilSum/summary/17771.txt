According to Jammu and Kashmir Subordinate Service Recruitment Rules, 1992, Rule 17(2), the competent authority responsible for ordering the transfer of a member of Divisional cadre is the State service.
The Head of the Department must approve any transfer to another Divisional cadre or to another District cadre.
The order of transfer from Doda to Udhampur passed by the Divisional Commissioner, Jammu, who is not the Head of Agrarian Reforms Department, is against the rules, and the order has been quashed.
If necessary in the public interest, the competent authority may pass fresh orders.
