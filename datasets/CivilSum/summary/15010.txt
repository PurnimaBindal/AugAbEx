In the case of compassionate appointment under Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India, if the family of the petitioner has access to two pensions, they cannot be considered financially distressed and unable to provide for themselves.
Though the petitioner may feel entitled to a compassionate appointment and equal treatment with others, the Director is within their rights to reject the application, particularly if similar proposals have also been rejected.
This is the decision handed down in accordance with the Constitution and the law.
