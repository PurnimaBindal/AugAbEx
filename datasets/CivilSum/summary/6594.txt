A retired civil servant cannot be denied retiral benefits during the pendency of a contemplated departmental enquiry as per Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 2.2.
(b) and Constitution of India, Article 300-A In this case, the appellant retired on attaining the age of superannuation when no departmental enquiry or action was pending against him.
Despite a charge-sheet being served upon him, no action was taken by the respondents for about two years and the charge-sheet was eventually withdrawn.
After a long period, a fresh show cause notice was served upon the appellant with the same charges, but the government once again dropped the disciplinary proceedings.
Therefore, the appellant was denied his valuable right without due course of law and the respondents have failed to show any bona fides.
The appellant is liable to be compensated by paying interest on delayed payments as per the Govt. instructions issued in letter No. FD-Haryana No. 1/2/74-88-3-FR-II dated 2351990.
