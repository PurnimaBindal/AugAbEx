Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Absorption of project employees in regular service of other Govt. departments - Employees in Census Department are typically discharged after completing 10 to 15 months on projects - This results in them losing their jobs as well as seniority in employment exchanges and eventually becoming too old - Therefore, it is recommended that State Governments create reasonable programs to absorb such workers in other departments - The Tamil Nadu Government has approved a scheme with certain modifications that cannot be reasonably met.
