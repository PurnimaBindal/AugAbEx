Sections 11 and 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act deal with regularisation of services for work-charge employees and casual labor.
The non-compliance of court directions led to the contempt petition against the State of Haryana in Piara Singh's case.
However, the Supreme Court has emphasized that regularisation can only be considered after two-thirds of service is completed.
As a result, non-regularisation of such employees based on High Court directions does not attract contempt of Courts Act provisions.
The contempt petition against the State of Haryana was therefore dismissed.
State of Haryana v. Piara Singh, 1992(3) SCT 201 was referenced in this matter.
