Section 3 of the Haryana Private Colleges (Taking Over of Management) Act, 1978 states that termination of services of a teacher during probation and not appointing a senior person as acting principal are not enough to warrant taking over of the Management.
The Act also specifies that the period for which the Management had been taken over must be published and that the Management of a college cannot be taken over for an indefinite period without such specification.
Constitution of India, Article 30(1) applies to this situation.
