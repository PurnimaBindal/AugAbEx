The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, set out a Cut-off date for Revision of Pay.
According to the Tikoo Committee Report, teaching specialists would receive a salary of Rs. 4,5000 - 5,700 four years after receiving the scale of Rs. 3,700 - 5,000.
The functional grade and non-functional grade distinction was eliminated, and promotion to the Rs. 4,500-5,700 Grade was offered on a time-bound basis upon completion of eight years of service, beginning on 1121991, rather than 31.10.1990.
The Cut-off date was fixed due to an Office Memorandum containing the Government's decision to implement the report, and it is not arbitrary or whimsical, rather it is fair.
