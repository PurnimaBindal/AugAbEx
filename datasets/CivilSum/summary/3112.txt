The Constitution of India's Articles 14, 300-A and 226, Army Pension Regulations and the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 state that the deceased husband of the petitioner, who was a Civil Servant before reaching superannuation age after leaving Army, must not be denied the benefit of family pension in the absence of any other objection.
Additionally, the petitioner's entitlement to family pension shall not affect her availability to receive any other benefit under the family pension scheme introduced under the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act.
