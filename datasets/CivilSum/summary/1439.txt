Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Recruitment - Post of Teacher Grade II - Reserved woman of other States - Whether they are eligible for reservation benefits in State of Rajasthan after marriage - Even after marriage their caste is considered reserved in State of Rajasthan if they belong to a reserved category - Issuance of caste certificates by Rajasthan authorities - It was held that RPSC cannot deny the validity of caste certificates issued by State authorities for public employment - Women belonging to a reserved category in their parental State before marriage and whose caste after marriage is also in a reserved category in State of Rajasthan are entitled to reservation benefits - However, women who were not eligible for reservation in their parent State before marriage cannot get the benefits solely based on marriage to a husband belonging to the reserved category in State of Rajasthan.
