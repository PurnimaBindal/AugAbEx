Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - Admission - Course of Diplomate of National Board in Radio Therapy - Guideline challenged - The guideline of scoring 50% marks in the interview for selection is unreasonable, as it would give respondents the power to selectively choose a candidate, regardless of the written test.
The guideline has been deemed arbitrary and struck down.
[Para 10]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - Admission to Medical course - Only one candidate selected for two available seats in Radio Therapy - The petitioner, having cleared the primary examination, is entitled to admission as a matter of right.
The holding of any further tests by the respondent would be arbitrary.
[Para 3 and 11]
