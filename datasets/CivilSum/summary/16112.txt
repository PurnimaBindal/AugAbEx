Central Industrial Security Force Act, 1968, Section 9 - Central Industrial Security Force Rules, 1969, Rules 45 and 42-A - Punishment - Appeal - Delay - Jurisdiction - The order appellant appealed against can be withheld by the authority who made it only when no cause is shown for the delay - However, in this case, the appellant showed some cause for not filing the appeal in time - Therefore, it is the appellate authority's decision whether or not the reasons given are sufficient - The punishing authority's withholding of appeal was wrong and beyond jurisdiction.
