Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 21 - Haryana State Central Cooperative Banks Staff Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1975, Rule 16-A - Widow of a deceased ad hoc employee seeking compassionate appointment - Relief denied due to only regular employee dependents being eligible - Petitioner argues that deceased worked for over two years and was entitled to regularisation per Government instructions - Directive given to consider petitioner's case - If deceased was eligible for regularisation, then petitioner may be eligible for compassionate appointment.
