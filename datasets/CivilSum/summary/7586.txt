According to Sections 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, an illegally terminated workman would typically be entitled to backwages, unless they were gainfully employed during that period.
In this particular case, the award did not provide a reason for denial of backwages, and there was no evidence or plea suggesting that the Petitioner was gainfully employed.
Therefore, the Petitioner should receive full backwages.
