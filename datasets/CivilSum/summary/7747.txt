Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribes Orders (Amendment) Act, 1996 - Admission to Professional Degree Course - If a person is classified as 'Paraya' in Tamilnadu, they cannot claim the same benefits if a caste with a similar name is considered as a Scheduled Caste in Kerala State.
The requirements for inclusion of a specific caste or community as a Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe in one State may be entirely different from those in another State where the same caste or community may have migrated.
