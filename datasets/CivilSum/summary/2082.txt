The petitioner was prevented from attending school and no necessary protection or facilities were offered by authorities, revealing state lawlessness.
The State, specifically the police administration, had a duty to protect his safety and security.
The decision to terminate his service was in gross violation of rule 28(8) and principles of natural justice were offended.
Scant regard was shown for his grievances and the show cause notice was rendered a nullity, revealing a capsuled mind of authority.
This imperiled his right to livelihood and work, which the court cannot ignore.
[Paras 17, 19-31, 35-36, 38-39]
