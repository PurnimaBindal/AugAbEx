Constitution of India, Article 226 - Andhra Pradesh Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1991, Rule 20 - Disciplinary Proceedings - The Petitioner contests the disciplinary action he received, citing that he was examined before the witnesses produced by the disciplinary authority, and that the proceedings are based on the 1963 Rules as opposed to the 1991 Rules - However, the court holds that the Petitioner did not suffer any procedural infringements as both sets of rules have fair procedures and the Petitioner has not shown any prejudice - Since the Enquiry officer's findings are supported by legally permissible evidence, the court cannot act as an appellate authority.
