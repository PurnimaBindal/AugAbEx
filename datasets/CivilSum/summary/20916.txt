Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4(1)(b) - Compensation - The appellant lost both hands while operating a machine belonging to the respondent - Evidence shows that the respondent took the appellant to the hospital after the injury - The appellant had no reason to falsely accuse respondent as his employer - Since the work in the farm was seasonal in nature, there was no appointment letter - The injury occurred during the course of employment with the respondent, entitling the appellant to compensation.
