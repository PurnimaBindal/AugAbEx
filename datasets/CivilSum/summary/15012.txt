Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Arrears of salary - Delay - Petitioner is seeking arrears of salary of her deceased husband that were payable for the period from January 1, 1987 to February 29, 1992 - No approval letter was produced before the Court - It is not evident if appointment of the deceased teacher was approved or if any salary arrears were demanded from 1992 to 1995 - There is an undue and unexplained delay - The petitioner cannot receive any relief.
