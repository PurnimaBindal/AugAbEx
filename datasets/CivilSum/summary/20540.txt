According to Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the appellants have been consistently paying the daily-rated employees in the regular pay scale.
Therefore, they cannot claim that the respondent's right to receive salary in the regular scale of pay should be decided by the labour court before being paid.
The labour court has rightfully granted the application under Section 33-C(2).
