According to Section 24 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the Strike Tribunal found the strike to be illegal, making the lock-out declared also not illegal.
However, the Tribunal directed for 50% payment of wages to the workmen, including staff members, for the lock-out period due to its unjustified and unreasonable duration.
Nevertheless, it cannot be concluded that the lock-out lasted a long time since the petitioner had attempted to resume work and lifted the lock-out, yet the workmen continued with their illegal strike.
Thus, the workmen are not entitled to wages for the strike period, and the Tribunal's award is void.
