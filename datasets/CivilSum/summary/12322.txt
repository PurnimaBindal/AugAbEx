According to Punjab State (Class IV) Service Rules, 1963, Rule 5(d), only knowledge of the regional language and English, as prescribed from time to time, are required qualifications.
Being matriculate is not necessary to fulfill the requisite qualifications.
[Para 7]

As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, candidates must be selected based on their suitability.
While sponsors from the Employment Exchange are selected, ignoring candidates from the open market cannot be done.
[Para 8]

According to the Constitution of India, Article 226, it is up to the appointing authority to determine the relevancy of higher qualifications when considering who is suitable for the job.
The court cannot interfere unless mala fides is proven to have influenced the selection process.
The candidate has the fundamental right to be considered.
[Paras 9 and 10]
