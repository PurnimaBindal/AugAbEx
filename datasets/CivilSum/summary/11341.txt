In accordance with Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, an ad hoc appointment can result in termination and criminal proceedings.
A termination order may be issued without any charge or allegations and it will not carry any negative connotation.
This order would also be compliant with the terms and conditions of the appointment.
[Para 6]

When a termination order is passed in accordance with the terms and conditions of appointment, there is no discrimination to be proven unless it can be shown that the terms and conditions of the junior employee who was retained when a senior ad hoc employee was terminated were the same.
[Para 7]

If services were terminated in 1987 and a writ was not filed until 1989, the petition is subject to delay and laches.
Therefore, it will be dismissed.
[Para 8]
