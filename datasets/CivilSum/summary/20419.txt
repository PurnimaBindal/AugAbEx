According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, seniority and absorption are important considerations in relation to officiating service on deputation.
The Seniority Rule states that Junior Investigating Officers on deputation in the Investigation Bureau of Govt. of India, who opt for absorption by transfer, will rank below all direct recruits or promotes as of the date of their absorption.
The Tribunal's decision to count services rendered in the officiating cadre by deputationists prior to absorption towards seniority was incorrect.
Moreover, after opting for absorption under the Rules to avoid repatriation, a person cannot claim seniority that goes against the Rules.
