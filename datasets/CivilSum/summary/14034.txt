According to Central Civil Service Pension Rules in 1972, Rule 48-A(1), the rejection of pension due to not completing 20 years of service is not arbitrary or unlawful.
If a person requests to be discharged from their service, a claim for reinstatement may be denied.
[Para 2]
The Constitution of India's Article 16 allows for voluntary retirement.
In a case where a petitioner retires voluntarily, they cannot make a claim for reinstatement.
