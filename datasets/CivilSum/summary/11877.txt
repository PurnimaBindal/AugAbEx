Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay and allowances - Government pay scale revision - Government equates pay scales for Class IV and Class III employees - Class III employees carry out technical duties and possess higher qualifications - Inappropriate to put junior and higher posts in the same pay scale - Combining promotional and inferior posts in the same pay scale restricts aspirations of employees to move up in their careers - Government directed to provide fair compensation for duties performed by fixing petitioners' salaries at an appropriate pay scale.
1987(5) SRR 545, 1990(5) SLR 108 cited.
