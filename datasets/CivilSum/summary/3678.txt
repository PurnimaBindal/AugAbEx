The Police Act, 1972 operates by virtue of Articles 313 and 372 of the Constitution, and appointment of Constables and Sub Inspectors of Police will be governed by Rules and Orders issued under the 1861 Act.
Age limit for the police service is prescribed under Section 2 of the Act.
[Paras 16, 17, 33 and 64] B.
Different age limits and physical standards can be prescribed for the police service as compared to other public services, and there is no violation of Article 16 by prescribing a lower age limit for recruitment in the police service.
[Paras 33, 72, 85, 86, 88, 93, 94 and 101] C. Publication of Government orders relating to Police Service in the Official Gazette is not necessary, as publication in the Police Gazette is sufficient.
[Paras 61 and 63] D Legislature has wide power of classification, and there is always a presumption of constitutionality in favour of a legislation proving classification.
A person challenging it has to show clear transgression of the constitutional principles.
