Punjab Public Works Department (Buildings and Roads Branch), Chief Engineer's Office (Class III- Ministerial) Service Rules, 1967, Rule 7 and 9 - Temporary appointment/Regularisation /Termination/Natural justice - The petitioner was hired as a clerk on a temporary basis and was later promoted to a permanent position, during which time he completed his probationary period.
He became a permanent employee for all intents and purposes.
Without complying with the provisions of punishment and appeal rules, his services were terminated with a simple order and without even issuing a show cause notice, rendering it an illegal order.
[Para 5, 7, 8]

Civil Procedure Code, Order 17, Rule 3 - Pleadings/Admission and denial - The rule does not require the dismissal of every suit for non-compliance with directions given during the proceedings/dates.
If material facts are admitted or not specifically denied, the court must make decisions based on admissions and denials in the pleadings, and dismissing a case without considering the parties' positions in their pleadings is illegal.
[Para 3 and 4]
