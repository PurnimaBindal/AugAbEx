Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination - Part time Instructors under Non-formal Education Scheme - Abolition of scheme - The petitioners are arguing that they are eligible to receive payment for the duration of their actual service.
They were not working for the four-hour daily requirement stated in AIR 1988 SC 1504.
There is no difference noted between the petitioners' case and the one decided by the SC and in C.W.P.
No. 125755 of 1989 decided by DB on 2311991.
As a result, the petition is granted, and the respondents are directed to payout arrears of the salary to the petitioners.
