Constitution of India, Article 30(1) - Minority Educational Institutions - The appointment of the Headmaster of a Minority Educational Institution can only be done by the Management of the Institution - Interference by the Government, except in setting eligibility qualifications, would violate Article 30.
[Para 5]
Kerala Education Act, 1958, Section 36 - Kerala Education Rules 1959, Rule 44A - The eligibility qualifications for the Headmaster's appointment must be acquired before the date of vacancy - Favoritism cannot be shown by the management delaying the appointment until the qualifications are acquired - Only qualified individuals on the date of vacancy can be appointed.
[Paras 7 and 9]
Kerala Education Rules, 1959, Rules 43 and 44 - Promotion to higher cadre is based on qualifications and seniority at the time of occurrence of vacancy.
