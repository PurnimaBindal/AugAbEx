Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Interview - Regulations mandate educational/professional qualifications and assign marks for different components - 20 marks for performance/CR, 30 marks for written test, and 30 marks for oral interview - Challenge made to oral interview marks as excessive, contending it should not exceed 125% or 15% - Court held that in promotions with service, provisions for 20% oral test is not excessive, and the regulation is not illegal or arbitrary - Decision follows D.V.
Bakshi v. Union of India, JT 1993(4) SC 180 - Petition dismissed.
