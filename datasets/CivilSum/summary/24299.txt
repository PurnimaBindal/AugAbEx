Sections 191(1)(A), 100(1)(A) and 100(1)(A)(iv) of the Representation of People Act, 1951 stipulate the disqualification of government employees to hold office of profit under the government; however, the petitioner must establish a master-servant relationship between the candidate and the government before disqualification can be initiated.
A casual engagement as a teacher at a Madrasa created by a private managing committee recognized by, but not maintained by, the School Education Board on a contractual basis for no remuneration does not fall within the scope of holding an office of profit on a provincialized post under government employment.
No presumptions of employment or termination can be made in the absence of an offer and its acceptance.
