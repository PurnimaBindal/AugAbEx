Constitution of India, Article 30 - Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Section 8 - Societies Registration Act, Section 21A - Minority institution/Private Schools - The society, a registered minority institution running a school, terminated its teacher's services but did so under the amended Rules and Regulations which were not required to be registered under the Societies Registration Act - Therefore, the teacher's termination order was invalid under Section 8 of Delhi School Education Act and Rules, 1973 - High Court's order set aside - Appeal allowed.
[Para 11]
Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Section 8 - Suspension - A teacher at the society's school was suspended prior to the Education Act, 1973 - The order of suspension cannot be judged under the provisions of the Act and Rules - High Court order quashing the suspension was set aside.
