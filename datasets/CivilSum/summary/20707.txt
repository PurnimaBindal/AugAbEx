Central Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1986, Rules 5, 6(3) and 7(A)(i)(ii), 7(A)(a) - Pay - The government clarified the Rules in an Office Memo dated May 4, 1987 to remove any anomalies - The appellant received an increment in the pre-revised scale and was then given the revised pay scale, with basic pay fixed as of January 1, 1986 - However, the government's action based on the clarification dated May 4, 1987 had a negative impact on the appellant - Since the minimum of the revised pay scale was greater than the calculated amount under Rule 7(A)(i)(ii), the appellant was entitled to be fixed at the minimum of the revised scale under Rule 7(A)(a) - Therefore, the appellant should have been entitled to the revised scale of pay at Rs. 1700/- as opposed to Rs. 1640.
