The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 discuss the case of a contractual employee who has been working for 9 years, and a fresh advertisement is put out to fill the post.
The petitioner claimed that they were entitled to regularization, however, it was held that simply being appointed via an advertisement did not give them any special right to continue in service or receive regularization.
Additionally, the petitioner did not meet the necessary ten years of service and was not appointed to a duly sanctioned post.
It was also noted that age alone cannot be the grounds for claiming relief.
[Paras 11, 12, and 18]
