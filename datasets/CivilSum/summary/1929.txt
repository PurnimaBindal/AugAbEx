Section 8-B of the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 and Section 482 of the Criminal Procedure Code, 1973 - Recovery of amount due from employer in relation to Employees Provident Fund - Recovery Officer issued demand notice to petitioner and other directors of company to pay the amount due - Petitioner failed to pay the amount of recovery - Recovery Officer issued warrant of arrest and arrested the petitioner and detained him to civil prison for a period of 45 days - Petitioner's application for bail under Section 482 not maintainable as impugned orders have no connection with criminal or penal law of the land.
