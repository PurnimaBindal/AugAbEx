Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Rajasthan Service of Engineering (Building and Road branch) Rules, 1954, Rules 4(c), 4(i) - Appointment - Counting of experience - In accordance with Rule 4(i), experience gained by officiating, temporary or ad hoc appointment counts only if the appointment is in the regular line of promotion and if it is not invalid under any law - Appellant was given an appointment on workcharge basis and not in the regular line of promotion at the initial stage, so experience from such appointment cannot be counted.
