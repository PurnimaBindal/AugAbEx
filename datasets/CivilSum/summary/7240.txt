Constitution (Scheduled Caste) Order, 1950 - Migration of a scheduled caste from one State to another - The degree of disadvantages faced by the caste in each State should be considered, even if the caste is listed in both states with the same name.
It cannot be assumed that a person from one State's scheduled caste will have the same rights and benefits as a member of the same caste in another State.
