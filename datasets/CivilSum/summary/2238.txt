Constitution of India, Articles 21 & 14 - Central Civil Services (Medical Attendance Rules) - Central Government Health Scheme, Rule 9 - Medical reimbursement - Employees of the Scheme and former employees are entitled to medical treatment - Reimbursement of medical expenses incurred for two by-pass surgeries must be claimed - Retirees who received their CGHS Card after 1.4.1984 and when the Scheme was in effect should not be denied reimbursement solely because of the late acquisition of the Card.
