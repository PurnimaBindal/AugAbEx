Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Reference - Limitation - The reference of an Industrial Dispute cannot be refused on grounds of limitation if raised within time.
In the case of a workman dismissed on 8.7.1988 and a dispute raised on 741990, the order of refusal of reference was quashed.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Reference - Res judicata - The decision of the executive officer in an appeal before the Registrar Cooperative Societies will not operate as res judicata in court proceedings or for making reference under Section 10 of the ID Act.
