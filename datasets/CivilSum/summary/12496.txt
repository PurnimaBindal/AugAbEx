Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 12 - Standing Orders, 1984, Order No. 16(iii) - Departmental enquiry - Misconduct - A disciplinary inquiry against a hotel's workman, as per the law-enforced Standing Order, must be fulfilled by an officer/employee of the Hotel/Corporation.
Also, the Enquiry Officer to conduct the enquiry should not be of a lower rank than the accused workman.
However, in this case, the Corporation hired an Enquiry Officer on a retainer basis who was not an officer/employee of the Corporation.
This move made the conducted inquiry illegal and without jurisdiction.
