The validity of Section 63 of the Army Act, which embodies a residing provision, is not offensive towards any law, including Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
It is meant to be used in cases where a particular Act is not covered under the offences specifically defined under the Act.
[Para 11]
The charge-sheet against the Deputy Commandant and Security Officer charged with failure to ensure adequate security measures in the Centre has no evidence to suggest that the petitioner had failed to do something which was expected of him.
The punishment order is set aside as the charge is being vague and liable to be quashed.
