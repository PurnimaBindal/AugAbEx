Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Appointment/Qualifications - Vacancy - Withdrawal of appointment - The Vice Chancellor appointed a Vegetable Botanist in the Agriculture University by relaxing the qualifications.
However, the post required a Ph.D in Vegetable Crops with specialization in Vegetable Breeding, which the petitioner did not possess.
The appointment was not approved by the Board and was withdrawn with retrospective effect, making the petitioner liable to deposit back the salary received.
The Court upheld the order of withdrawal.
