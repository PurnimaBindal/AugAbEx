Punjab Civil Medical Service (Class-I) Rules, 1972 (as amended in 1979) Rule 9 - Promotion/Deputation/Ex-Cadre post - When an employee is sent on deputation to another cadre and then returns, their entire service in the other cadre will be taken into account in the parent cadre for all purposes, as though they were working in the parent department.
This applies to the appellant, who was sent on deputation as Director in Chandigarh and will be deemed to be holding the post of Joint Director in Punjab.
[Para 16] B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Lien - If an employee is promoted on an ex-cadre post on a permanent basis, it cannot be said that they are still holding a lien on their previous post.
This applies to the promotion of an ex-cadre post of Director, Health Services (Social Insurance) from PCMS (Class-I) on a permanent basis.
[Para 19] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion/Consideration - Each contender for a promotion must be considered on their merits, as no employee can claim transportation to a specific post.
However, if the exercise of power by the appropriate authority is found to be unfair or tainted, the court may intervene.
Ultimately, it is up to the government as to who should be appointed to a particular post, and the court may only set aside this discretion if it is clearly perverse, illegal or discriminatory.
[Para 21] D. Punjab Civil Medical Service (Class-I) Rules, 1972 (as amended in 1979), Rule 9 - Ex-Cadre Post/Equation of posts - Two posts are deemed to be equivalent only in decisive circumstances, and not just based on incidental circumstances.
Therefore, the ex-cadre post of Director, Health Services and Family Welfare cannot be considered higher in status than the post of Director, Health Services (S.I.).
