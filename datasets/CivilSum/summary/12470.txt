Bhanot, DAG, Punjab, Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965 - Military service benefit - The argument of whether the individual was already in military service during the imposition of the emergency does not arise if the claimant holds a position in the Punjab Civil Services.
The classification was made by Haryana, not by Punjab.
Rule 4 of the Rules entitles all those who served during the emergency to the benefit in Punjab.
