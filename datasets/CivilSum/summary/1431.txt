Rajasthan High Court Ordinance, 1949, Section 18 - Rajasthan Sub-ordinate Courts Ministerial Establishment Rules, 1986, Rule 14(iv) - Promotion - Post of Stenographer Grade I - The speed test was not conducted according to the rules and the required speed is 100 words per minute to be promoted from Stenographer Grade II to Stenographer Grade I - The appellant-petitioner did not qualify the speed test as their shorthand speed was below 100 words per minute - Therefore, the appellant-petitioner has no legal right to be declared successful and is not entitled to any relief.
[Paras 7 and 8]
