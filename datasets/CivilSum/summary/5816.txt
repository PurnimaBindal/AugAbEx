Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 & 226 - Delay and laches - Reversion - Petitioner was reverted for being found unfit during probation - The reversion order was passed in 1986 and challenged in 1993 - The petition suffers from delay and laches - Junior colleagues obtaining relief on time is not a valid reason for the petitioner's delay in filing the petition nor does it entitle him to any relief simply because his juniors were promoted again based on court orders.
[Paras 3, 4 and 5]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Necessary parties - Promotion of juniors questioned without impleading them as a party to the petition - the petitioner cannot be granted relief in their absence against them.
