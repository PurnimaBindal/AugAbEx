Indian Railways Establishment Manual, Rule 302 r/w Rule 306 - Seniority - In determining inter se seniority between regularly promoted in-service candidates and those selected by direct recruitment, the material factor is the date on which they join the working post after completion of the process.
For former, seniority starts from the joining date after completion, while the latter's seniority starts from the date of their entry into the grade.
The process of selection is irrelevant.
The process for direct recruits started in 1989, before the petitioner and others joined the posts in August 1990.
The criterion is inapplicable in determining inter se seniority between promotees and direct recruits, and the tribunal was correct in rejecting the seniority claim.
The petition has been dismissed.
