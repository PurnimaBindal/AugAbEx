Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission - Disability - A petitioner with 30% blindness had their case referred to the Disablement Committee, which concluded that the petitioner was not eligible for admission.
The State Government argued that according to admission Rule 54, only individuals with blindness up to 75% are qualified for admission.
However, it was held that the petitioner's disability of 30% falls within the defined limit of 75%, as the rule does not stipulate a minimum threshold of 75%.
