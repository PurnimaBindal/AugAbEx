Gujarat Secondary Education Act of 1972, Sections 36 and 38 - Punishment - Proportionality - Interference by Tribunal - A delinquent was dismissed on the basis of an admitted charge of misappropriation, which was also proven during the enquiry.
It is determined that, unless the punishment imposed by the Tribunal is conscionable, it is not permissible for it to interfere with the findings of the enquiry officer.
In view of the charge, the penalty imposed cannot be considered arbitrary, unreasonable, excessive, or disproportionate.
