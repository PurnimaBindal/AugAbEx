Petitioners argued that they were entitled to equal treatment in pay scale since they were teachers in two cadres performing the same duties, citing Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
Respondents argued that each cadre had separate rules governing their conditions of service and that different scales of pay did not violate Articles 14 and 16, as the Provincial Cadre and State Cadre were independent services.
The court found that the petitioners began and continued their services differently, and that differences in their treatment would not amount to a denial of equal opportunity.
The petitions were therefore dismissed.
