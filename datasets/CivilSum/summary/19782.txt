The petitioner was granted admission and allowed to finish one year of studies, as well as being permitted to take the examination after receiving a Roll Number.
The petitioner was open and honest throughout the process and did not withhold any information.
Withholding the result on the basis that he was ineligible for admission was deemed illegal, and directions were given to release the petitioner's result.
The petition was granted with costs.
