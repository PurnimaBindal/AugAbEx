Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - Petitioner enrolled in Army on 2572 after being found medically fit - While in service, he became ill and stayed at the hospital for treatment - The disease was diagnosed as Neurosis - Discharged on 26478 after serving for 5 years and 360 days in army - Disability not granted because it was not attributable or aggravated by military service - Ruling in 681046 B.
Sergeant Satnam Singh Bajwa v. UOI and others fully applies to this case - Petitioner to receive relief and disability pension to be released as per order.
