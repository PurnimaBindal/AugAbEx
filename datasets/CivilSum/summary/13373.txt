Constitution of India, Article 226 - Annual Confidential Reports - Average remarks - The petitioner received delayed remarks relating to the periods from April 1987 to August 1987 and for the year 1990-91 in September and December 1997.
However, the ACRs themselves were not recorded until 1997, with no explanation for the significant delay.
As a result, the court ruled that the criticized remarks were arbitrary and should be disregarded.
[Paras 9 and 10]
