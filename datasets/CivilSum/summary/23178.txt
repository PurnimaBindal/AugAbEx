The All India Council for Technical Education Act of 1987, Section 10(k) states that obtaining the approval of the State Government for starting a college is not a statutory requirement.
As per University Statute 9(7), the views of the State Government must be obtained before granting affiliation, but this does not constitute "approval".
[Para 23]
The University should have granted further orders for affiliation based on the permission granted by the AICTE and other relevant factors in the University Act or Statutes that are not in conflict with the AICTE Act or its Regulations.
There was no need for any approval from the State Government.
