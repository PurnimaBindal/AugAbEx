Police Act, 1861, Section 7 - Rajasthan Police Subordinate Service Rules, 1989, Rule 28-A - Promotion under discretion - Discrimination - Petitioner received a cash award/commendation role for exceptional work in an FIR case, while another Constable was promoted under similar circumstances - Rule 28-A allows the DGP to choose to promote or not - It was found that the non-promotion of the petitioner was discriminatory and violated Article 14 of the Constitution, as there was no evidence to distinguish his case from that of the other Constable.
