According to Army Act, 1950, Section 129 and Army Rules, 1954, Rules 39, 40 and 102, in Court Martial Proceedings, a Judge-Advocate who holds lower rank than the accused officer cannot take part in General Court Martial Proceedings.
[Para 46]  B.
Army Rules, 1954, Rule 40 states that when Court Martial is made for performing judicial functions, it must be composed properly with the help of a qualified Judge-Advocate, so that the judicial discipline in Military should not be compromised, although discipline is a crucial criterion in Military law.
