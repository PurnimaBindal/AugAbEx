The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 - Regularisation - Reinstatement - The petitioners requested regularisation claiming they had worked for a few years - The petitioners were unable to prove that they were appointed and paid by the respondent - Evidence showed that the petitioners were hired to work on a specific project for a limited time - Therefore, the petitioners have no right to be reinstated or regularised in the current scenario, and the petition is dismissed.
