The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 25-F and 2(oo)(bb), pertains to fixed term appointments.
In such appointments, employees are hired until the completion of a specific task.
However, if the task for which an employee has been hired is not mentioned clearly, it cannot be considered as a valid reason for removal.
The Labour Court has deemed the removal of an employee to be a violation of Section 25-F, and its findings cannot be contested without evidence to prove its prima facie wrong.
Thus, there should be no interference.
