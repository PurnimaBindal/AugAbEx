Rules for Admission to Post Graduate Courses in Medical Colleges in State of Andhra Pradesh Rules, Rules 3(2) and 19(1) - Extraordinary leave - Selection for Post Graduation Course - The Tribunal discovered that all inservice candidates are entitled to receive their salaries regardless of being selected against inservice quota or outside such quota - However, the Tribunal's view on the meaning of `inservice candidates' is flawed as it reads the rules in compartments rather than together - Those who are selected against the quota reserved for inservice candidates have the right to extraordinary leave - Thus, the Order of Tribunal is overturned.
