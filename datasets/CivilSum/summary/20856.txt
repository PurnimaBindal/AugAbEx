According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, engineers in any branch are eligible for promotion to the post of Superintending Engineer, subject to the conditions under the Punjab Service of Engineers, Class I, P.W.D.
(Buildings and Roads Branch) Rules, 1960.
Rule 9(3)(c) states that eligibility for being considered for appointment to the post of Chief Engineers requires three years of experience as a Superintending Engineer, but there is no indication in the rule that it is limited to the Civil branch.
Therefore, Superintending Engineers in Electrical or Mechanical branches may also be considered.
[Paras 1, 2, 5, 6 and 10]

The Punjab Service of Engineers, Class I, P.W.D.
(Buildings and Roads Branch) Rules, 1960 Rules 2(14) and Rule 9(3)(c) say that until an eligible person is available within the cadre, the promotional post of Executive Engineer cannot be filled by deputation or transfer.
The appointing authority cannot appoint someone outside of the cadre except in exceptional cases where public interest is at stake and no officer from the state is available.
However, the scarcity in practice cannot exclude eligible persons from within the cadre itself.
[Paras 8 and 9]
