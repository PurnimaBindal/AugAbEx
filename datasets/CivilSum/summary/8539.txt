Jadavpur University Act of 1981, Sections 4(13) and 47(j) - Appointment - Cancellation of Appointment - The petitioner was denied appointment as an Assistant Head Master due to having completed two study courses in the same session, however, only the University has the authority to withdraw or cancel a degree under the prescribed rules - Since the University did not cancel petitioner's degrees, the school authority cannot deny him the benefit of them.
[Paras 13 and 14]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Interim Order - Appointment of Assistant Head Master - As per an interim order, the petitioner was permitted to be appointed on an ad hoc basis subject to the result of the writ application - Another teacher's appointment does not negate the need for the writ application, therefore the order of the learned trial judge is set aside.
[Para 18]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Specific Relief Act of 1963, Section 34 - Void and Voidable Orders - Unless such orders are declared void or voidable, they remain valid.
