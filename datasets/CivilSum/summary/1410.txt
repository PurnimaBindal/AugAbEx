Constitution of India, Articles 26 and 229(2) - Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules 1988, Rule 1.2 - Power of Chief Justice to recall the earlier order of the Chief Justice - The former Chief Justice allowed the petitioner to opt for the revised pay scale from 1st October, 1988 and directed the petitioner to make a fresh option, which was considered valid by the High Court - Any subsequent intervention by the new Chief Justice to revoke the previous decision would be a violation of established legal principles and natural justice - The writ of certiorari to quash such a recall is therefore appropriate.
