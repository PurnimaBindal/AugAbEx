Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal Rules) 1987, Rule 7 states that in cases of punishment, natural justice must be observed.
Stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect constitutes a major penalty.
In major penalty cases, the holding of an inquiry is mandatory and punishment without holding an inquiry is not sustainable.
[Paras 4 and 5] B.
Only the High Court has the jurisdiction to punish for contempt of court, and the Commissioner & Secretary to Govt. has no such jurisdiction.
