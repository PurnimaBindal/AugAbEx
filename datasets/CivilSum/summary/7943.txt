The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 2 Clause (oo), 11A and 25F are mentioned in regards to an award concerning retrenchment.
The respondent management issued a memo to the petitioner after he was unauthorisedly absent from duty, and on the failure to show cause, the respondent issued an order declaring that the petitioner had abandoned his job on his own accord.
The labour court directed the reinstatement of the petitioner, holding that the termination was contrary to the provisions of Section 25F of the Act.
However, it was argued that the termination would not fall under the meaning of 'retrenchment' under Clause (oo) of Section 2 of the Act because the petitioner's services were terminated by management invoking Certified Standing Orders.
The court held that since the petitioner failed to report to duty despite the issuance of memos and remained unauthorisedly absent, the termination was fair and in compliance with principles of natural justice.
It was also noted that the labour court was fair while passing an order under Section 11A in denying backwages and continuity of service while reinstating the petitioner.
Therefore, the petition was dismissed accordingly.
