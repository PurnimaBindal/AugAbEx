The Railway Establishment Code, Rule 1802, Proviso, along with Constitution of India Articles 14 and 311, deal with Promotion/Probation.
The appellant received promotion to Group 'B' service of the Mechanical Department of Railway, and was placed in the senior scale for 10 years until his compulsory retirement.
It is evident that the appellant continued as a Group 'B' officer in a substantive capacity and could not simultaneously continue as a Group 'C' officer in a substantive capacity.
Therefore, the proviso does not apply to this case.
The order of retirement is not flawed by any legal errors.
