Pension Regulations for the Army, 1961, Part II, Regulations 167 and 186 - Pay and Allowance Regulation Rules, 1955, Rules 51 and 123 - Army Act, Section 38 - Natural justice - An employee must be consulted before an order of forfeiture of pay and allowance, service and pension due to absence is passed if it likely to affect their interests negatively - Once pension on service element is granted, it cannot be cancelled without notice and hearing, even in cases of forfeiture - Illness and admission to a military hospital are valid reasons for absence from duty, and cannot be considered an act of error.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Pension Regulations for the Army, 1961, Part II, Regulations 167 and 186 - Pay and Allowance Regulation Rules, 1955, Rules 51 and 123 - Army Act, Section 38 - Delay and laches - Although a ground of delay and laches is not a rule of law, it is a rule of prudence which authorities cannot use to defeat the payment of pension, particularly in the case of army personnel where natural justice has been breached for forfeiture of pay and allowance, service and pension due to absence.
