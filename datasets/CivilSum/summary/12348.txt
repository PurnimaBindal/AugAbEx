According to Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Intermediate School Course, confirmed Head Constables have to be sent for the course in order of their working seniority as Head Constable.
The grievance of the petitioners, who believe they had prior right to be deputed for the course, is not valid.
[Para 3]
In accordance with Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Intermediate School Course, it is permissible to send officiating Head Constables for the course, and the contention of the petitioner, who claimed that such officiating Head Constables had been deputed in supersession of confirmed Head Constables, cannot be sustained.
The private respondents who have been deputed for the course before the petitioners are from the reserved category, and a general category candidate cannot stake his claim against a reserved seat.
Therefore, it is justified to send the private respondents for the course prior to the petitioners.
