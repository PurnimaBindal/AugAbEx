Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 311 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F and 2(oo) - Punjab Nurses Registration Council Act, 1932, Section 17(3) - Termination - Employer cannot argue ineligibility of workman after keeping them employed for 12 and 2 years respectively - Especially when they have completed Nurses Training Course by the employer - Termination equates to retrenchment as specified in Section 2(oo) of Act and due to non-compliance of mandatory provisions of Section 25-F of the Act - Action of employer rightfully declared void - Respondents entitled to receive back wages - Interest of 15% on arrears of back wages must be paid.
