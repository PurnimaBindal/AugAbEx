Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Electrical) (Chandigarh Amendment) Regulations, 1985, Regulation 9(8)(a) - Promotion - Quotas for direct recruitment and promotions among decree holders and diploma holders - Once quotas are set by the rules, authorities must adhere to them - The Rule's mandate cannot be ignored based solely on seniority - Rule 9's provisions do not suggest that a promotee Assistant Engineer who received accelerated promotion through Clause (8)(i) is ineligible for further promotion based on AMIE qualifications - Promoted Assistant Engineers from the AMIE holder quota are considered equal to degree holders - Within the promotion quota, further promotion of a quota promotee is not restricted and cannot be deemed contrary to the law set out in SG Jaisinghani's case by the Supreme Court.
