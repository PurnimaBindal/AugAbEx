According to the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, as well as the Karnataka State Universities Act, 1976, Section 11, the question arose on whether the appellant was entitled to a hearing prior to the cancellation order being made.
The appellant, a Physics professor, had been appointed as Vice-Chancellor of Mysore University, but an order rescinding the earlier order was passed immediately as he was facing a criminal case.
This order was passed much before the appointment could take effect and thus there was no need to give him an opportunity for a hearing.
Additionally, there was no stigma cast on the appellant.
