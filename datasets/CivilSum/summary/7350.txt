The Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14 and 16 specify that transfers are purely administrative orders and courts will not interfere unless it is found to be mala fide or violates some rule or policy without sufficient reasons or public interest.
[Paras 16, 18, 19]
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 describe the Government's policy for posting and transfer, including protection for those retiring within two years.
The policy is mandatory, and any deviations require sufficient reasons or paramount public interest.
Without valid and acceptable reasons, transfer orders cannot be sustained.
[Paras 16, 17, 20, 21]
