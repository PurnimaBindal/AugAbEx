Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Selection - Right to appointment - The petitioner was selected through a selection examination conducted by the Banking Service Recruitment Board for tentative vacancies in different Rural Banks.
They received a letter of selection, which qualified them for the written test and interview and recommended them for appointment subject to need and after verification of testimonials.
However, the letter explicitly stated that it should not be considered as a letter of appointment.
The court held that selection does not confer the right to appointment, and therefore the writ petition has no merit.
Additionally, the court cannot give a direction for the petitioner to be absorbed.
[Paras 7 and 8]
