As per Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner is entitled to family pension based on the husband's government service.
Even though he was transferred to Minor Irrigation Tubewell Corporation without his consent, and then absorbed as a patwari in the State Revenue Deptt.
before passing away, his entire service should be considered as government service as it was not on his option or with his consent and it exceeds the one-year qualifying service required for family pension.
