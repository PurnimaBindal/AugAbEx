Code of Border Road Regulation - Para 501 - Casual workers/Regular employees - The service conditions, requirements of eligibility, and duties and responsibilities of casual workers and regular employees of the GREF department differ significantly.
Casual workers cannot be regarded as Group C regular employees, and no equality can be claimed.
