Employees State Insurance (General) Regulations, 1950 - Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 2(19-A) - Regulations 29, 31 and 33 - Definition of a seasonal factory - A seasonal factory is one which is exclusively engaged in one or more manufacturing processes such as cotton ginning, cotton or jute pressing, decortication of groundnuts, manufacturing of coffee, indigo lac, rubber sugar (including gur) or tea, or any manufacturing process that is incidental to or connected with any of these processes - The petitioners' factories, which are engaged in rice shelling, do not fall within this definition as they do not participate in any of the manufacturing processes listed in Section 2(19-A) of the Act - The State Government issued a notification extending the provisions of the Act to factories working with power and having more than ten employees on their establishments - The Petitioners cannot claim that the Act does not apply to them - After the Act was made applicable, Rice Shellers/Rice Millers were exempt from the purview of the Act - When the period of exemption expired, the Act automatically became applicable to Rice Shellers/Rice Millers - They are therefore responsible for complying with the Act's provisions and making contributions.
