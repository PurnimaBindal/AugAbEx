Constitution of India, Article 14 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(S) - Workman - A labour Law Advisor engaged by a company as a practising lawyer on a retainership basis is not considered a workman under Section 2(S) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and there is no employer-employee relationship between the parties - Therefore, no relief can be given.
