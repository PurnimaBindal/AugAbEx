Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311(2) Proviso - National Fertilizers Limited Employees (Conduct, Discipline and Appeal) Rules, Rule 39 - Dismissal from service - Upholding natural justice - Provision of inquiry report - Necessity of valid (sufficient) grounds and reasons for imposing disciplinary action - Delinquent has the right to receive a copy of the inquiry report and to offer his or her own representation opposing the findings of the Inquiry Officer - The authority must assess and deal with any contentions the delinquent makes in his or her representation - It is not satisfactory to simply say that 'the reply has been assessed and is found to be invalid' without providing any reasons for dismissing it - This course of action will undermine natural justice and make providing the inquiry report practically meaningless; it goes against what the 42nd amendment of the Indian Constitution was meant to do - The use of 'for the sufficient cause and reasons' in Rule 39 of NFL Rules also requires the punishing authority to record its own reasoned opinion after giving due consideration to all of the employee's defences.
