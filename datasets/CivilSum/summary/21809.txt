The Constitution of India Proviso (c) to Article 311(2) deals with dismissal from service.
The Government of India established an Office Memorandum dated 2671980 outlining various subversive activities for which a case should be referred to a Committee of Advisors, along with supporting documentation, to justify action under Proviso (c) to Article 311(2) of the Constitution instead of the normal disciplinary rules.
If the Committee of Advisors recommends taking such action, the recommendation is required to be submitted to the Minister in the Ministry of Home Affairs for his approval.
An order of dismissal under Proviso (c) to Article 311(2) can be issued thereafter.
The Court is bound by the order issued so long as there is material before the President that is relevant for arriving at his satisfaction as to the aforementioned action.
The Court has defined the scope of judicial review of the President's satisfaction for passing an order under Clause (c) of the second proviso to Article 311(2).
