Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference of dispute - Adjudication of dispute - Appropriate government has discretion to decline reference if the claim is belated and frivolous - Government must have opinion that an industrial dispute exists or is apprehended, but lacks jurisdiction to investigate the facts - In a case where the government determined that the workman abandoned the job, it exceeded jurisdiction - Order quashed and government directed to reconsider in accordance with the law.
