The closure of a department in a factory with less than 100 employees was upheld as valid by the Financial Commissioner and Secretary, even though no reasons were presented in the order and the workmen were not heard.
However, this approach violates the spirit of Section 25-O(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and is therefore void and illegal.
Furthermore, there is no evidence to suggest that the closed department was separate from the factory, and the motive for the closure appears to be solely to terminate the services of employees.
The argument that no government sanction was required under Section 25-O is unsubstantiated and seems to be a ploy to take advantage of the 1000 employees working in the factory as per Section 2(cc) of the Act.
Therefore, the closure is illegal and goes against the spirit of the Act, as per Paragraphs 19, 20, and 22 to 24.
