Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Regularisation - Work-charge Service - Pension - Estoppel - Consent against one's own interest - An employee cannot be held to a consent obtained through taking advantage of their innocence or illiteracy, as there is no estoppel against the law.
This applies even in cases where the employee has given their consent for non-regularisation of their services, as no insane person can give consent for such matters.
In a case where an employee has provided continuous service on a work-charged basis for 23 years, they have the right to be regularised and granted pension as per the rules, irrespective of any previous consent given.
The authority has thus been directed to regularise the petitioner's services and grant them pension.
