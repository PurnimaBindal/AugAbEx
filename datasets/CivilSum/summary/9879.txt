Constitution of India, Articles 12, 14, 16 - Deputation - Corporation - It is necessary for a State Corporation to treat its employees fairly like a welfare state and not hinder their chances for promotion by hiring deputation employees without considering their own workers for promotion until their own employees are available for promotion.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay - Promotion - Change of policy - A petitioner has the right to challenge a decision regarding promotion and change of policy of filling posts by deputation that was conveyed after much delay and after keeping the representation pending for a long time, and no delay or laches can be attributed to the petitioner.
[Para 7]
Instructions - Instructions that are circulated later on will not have a retrospective effect, and those who were eligible for promotion before the circulation of instructions cannot be denied the benefit of the earlier policy of promotion.
