According to the Constitution of India, in Articles 16 and 226 concerning Selection and Select list, a Petitioner's name was previously removed from the list due to a filed criminal case against him and overwriting in one section of his application form.
The Petitioner filed a petition, claiming that the information regarding the case was present in the application and that there was no omission on his part.
However, it was revealed that the Petitioner falsely stated that he was acquitted when, in reality, the offence was compounded on the basis of a compromise.
The appointing authority was found to be justified in not appointing someone with such a record as a Constable in a disciplined force.
Thus, the Petitioner's appeal was dismissed based on the focus on his conduct and character at the time of his removal from service.
