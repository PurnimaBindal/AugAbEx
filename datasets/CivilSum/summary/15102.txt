Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, Sections 7, 13 and 49 - Employees Conduct Rules, 1987, Rule 3 - Disciplinary proceedings - Premature retirement - The Enquiry Officer found the employee guilty under Sections 7, 13 and 49 of the Prevention of Corruption Act and Rule 3 of the Employees Conduct Rules for accepting illegal gratification - The departmental enquiry was put on hold until the decision of criminal case - Upon acquittal in criminal case, the petitioner requested reinstatement in service - However, the petitioner was forcibly retired - The court held that the acquittal in criminal proceedings did not necessarily lead to exoneration in departmental proceedings - The court did not interfere and upheld the order of pre-mature retirement.
