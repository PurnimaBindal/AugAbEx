Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - University Circular No. UG/155 of 1998, Clause 7 - Examination - Grace marks - The petitioner's mark in one subject increased from 58 to 69 and they were awarded 10 grace marks as per the University Ordinance - However, grace marks cannot be included for determining the rank in merit list - Consequently, the claim for awarding first rank in merit was rightfully rejected.
