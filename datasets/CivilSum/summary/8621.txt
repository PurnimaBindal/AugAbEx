Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Workmen appointed on 22.10.86 - Services terminated on 27.10.87 - No Industrial dispute raised - Workman re-appointed on 21788 wherein he worked for 178 days - The non-renewal of the previous contract was not challenged by the workman therefore, there is no link between the previous and subsequent appointment - As per the provisions of Section 25-F, 240 days have to be counted in the 12 calendar months before the date of calculation, which is the date of termination, workman did not complete 240 days - Hence, the provisions of Section 25-F are not required - Impugned award set aside.
