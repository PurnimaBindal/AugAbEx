Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Government accommodation/Penal rent - Unauthorised occupation - If an employee seeks voluntary retirement, he or she must vacate the Govt. accommodation based on the Rules - Even if the employee's son or dependent applies for re-allotment to keep the accommodation, it does not guarantee allotment - The application is considered during the consideration period, and if an order of extension is later withdrawn, a request for re-allotment can be declined, and the petitioner must deposit penal rent - According to the rules, the petitioner is obligated to pay penal rent.
