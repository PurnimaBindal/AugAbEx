Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(p) and 18(1) and Industrial Dispute (Central) Rules, 1957, Rule 58 - Absorption - Employee-workmen appointed on a casual basis and rendered long service but not absorbed - As per last settlement, some vacancies filled, still, a large number of workmen-employees including petitioners remained unabsorbed - Dispute an industrial one - Writ Court cannot inquire into facts regarding implementation or settlements - Dispute falls outside the sphere of judicial review - Writ petition not maintainable - Matter to be settled under ID Act - The order of a single Judge to initiate proceedings against Bank officials, unwarranted - Order of single Judge set aside.
