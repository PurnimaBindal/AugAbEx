Under the Haryana Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Section 6, employees of affiliated colleges are granted pay scales as established by the government.
For Librarians, the government implemented pay scales starting at Rs. 700 and increasing to Rs. 1600 from 161980.
As per the verdict in CWP No. 832 of 1987, the petitioner is deservedly eligible for the pay scale of Rs. 700-1600.
