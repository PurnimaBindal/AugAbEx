The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 cover the post of JBT Teacher.
An Ex-serviceman applied for the post but was denied appointment on the grounds that the Teachers Training Course from Bihar Vidayala Pariksha Samiti, Patna was not recognized by the Haryana Government Education Department.
This denial of appointment was deemed illegal as the petitioner's Teachers Training Course was recognized even by the University Grants Commission.
Furthermore, the petitioner was found to be more meritorious than the person who had been appointed, and as a result, the respondent was directed to consider the petitioner's case.
