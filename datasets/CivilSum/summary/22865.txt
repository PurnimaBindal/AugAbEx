Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Jammu and Kashmir (Compassionate Appointment) Rules, 1994 - Compassionate appointment - Objective - Prolonged delay - Utilization of compassionate appointment policy against the Constitution Article 14 and 16 as an alternative recruitment channel is invalid - Such appointments are only an exception to the standard rule, which must not be overlooked - They are only for unexpected crises - If the family has survived for a long period, there is no financial emergency to qualify for compassionate appointment - After 15 years of an employee's death, if the court is reviewing a writ petition, the plea cannot be granted since the family is no longer in a sudden financial crisis.
