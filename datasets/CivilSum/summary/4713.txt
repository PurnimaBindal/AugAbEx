Army Pension Regulations, Regulations 173(A) and 179 - Army Service Pension Rules, Annexure II, Para 7 - If a person is invalidated from service and placed in medical category "C" by the Medical Board due to "Seizure and Neurosis" that is attributable or aggravated by army service, the onus is upon the Army Authority to prove otherwise.
The Army Authority must prove that the disease suffered by the person was not caused by army service.
There is no note of any disease made at the time of entry in Army service, and the duties of Army personnel are indeed very arduous.
As such, this type of disease is suffered by many Jawans of the Army.
Therefore, it cannot be said that "Seizure and Neurosis" is not attributable or aggravated by military service.
The petitioner is directed to receive disability pension from 38 months prior to filing the petition.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Pension Regulations, Regulation 173(A) - A retiree can file a petition for his pension in the High Court of the territory where he is residing.
Such a claim cannot be defeated on the ground of territorial jurisdiction.
No prejudice is likely to be caused to the Union of India if the rights of a retiree are adjudicated by any High Court.
