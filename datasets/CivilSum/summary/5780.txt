Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Winding up - Policy decision - Judicial review - Govt.'s decision to close down the National Council for safety in Mines and consequent retrenchment of employees - The decision was based on the report of Expert group and considered certain aspects of the matter - The policy decision by the Government falls within its administrative domain and cannot be interfered by the Court - Even if the decision is controversial, it cannot be termed as arbitrary simply because the Court may consider it less prudent - The decision is not subject to judicial review.
