Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Central Civil Services Rules, Rules 13 and 14(3) - Pension - Qualifying service - Government Servants transferred to service to which Central Civil Services Rules apply are eligible for full pension if they complete more than 33 years total/combined qualifying service in all, including service under the State Government in an officiating or temporary capacity - The last employer must take the responsibility of effectively seeking contribution from earlier employers for their portion of the pension - The PGI cannot deny full pension to a retiree for lack of qualifying service or non-contribution of earlier employers - The petitioner in this case is entitled to 12% interest on arrears of pension and costs of Rs. 10,000/- to be paid within three months by the PGI, who can recover contribution from other States.
