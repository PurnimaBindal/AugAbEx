According to the Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Section 28 and item 9 of Schedule IV, workers who had availed the Voluntary Retirement Scheme are entitled to recover their dues under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act.
Moreover, if an employer tries to use Section 22 of the Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provisions) Act, 1985, for staying recovery proceedings, it cannot bar the proceedings for the recovery of wages and terminal benefits of workers.
