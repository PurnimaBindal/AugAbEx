Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pre-mature retirement - Resignation - Withdrawal of - The request for pre-mature retirement will not be complete until approved by a competent/appropriate authority - The acceptance will be effective from the date the employee intended to retire/resign or from the date of acceptance if no future date is specified by the employee - The right of a government servant to pre-mature retirement cannot be taken away by executive instructions or rules that are not legally binding unless there is a legal provision to restrict the right of withdrawal.
