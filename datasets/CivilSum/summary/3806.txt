Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 94(c) and (e), Order 39 Rule 7 - Attachment before judgment - Scope of - It is held that the court can only issue orders for detention, preservation, or inspection of property that is the subject matter of the suit or related questions that may arise during the course of the suit.

Provident Fund Act, 1925, Sections 3 and 4 - Pensions Act, 1871, Sections 11, 60(1)(g) and (k) - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 94(c) and (e), Order 39 Rule 7 - Pensionary benefits - Attachment of - It is permissible to attach such benefits until they reach the employee's hand as they are protected under Sections 3 and 4 of Act of 1925, Section 11 of Act of 1871 and Section 60(1)(g) and (k) of Code.
