Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Ad hoc service - Ad hoc service of selectees through PSC and non-selectees - Non-selectees should receive seniority in the cadre upon regularisation as per the provided Rule, but not above the selectees - Quota rule and roster system to be strictly adhered to - If a Roster Point was available for direct appointment, non-selectee ad hoc appointees would receive seniority from the date of availability of roster point - Directions given to determine seniority between selectees and non-selectees.
