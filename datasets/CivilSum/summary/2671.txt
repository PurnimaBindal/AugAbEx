Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - The Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependants of deceased Government Employees Rules, 2003 as amended in year 2005 and replaced in 2006 - Claim of compassionate appointment and financial assistance - The scheme applicable at the time of death of the deceased will be considered while evaluating the claim.
Compassionate appointment is only available in cases of extreme financial distress, and it has not been pleaded that the family's income was below the criteria laid in the scheme.
Financial assistance is only available to dependents whose breadwinner died after 4303 under the Schemes of 2003, 2005, and 2006, whichever is applicable, since there is no provision for financial assistance in any policy before 4303.
As a result, the request for a compassionate appointment was denied.
