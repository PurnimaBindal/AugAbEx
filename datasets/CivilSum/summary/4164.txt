Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Promotion - Post of Addl.
General Manager - Petitioner was not denied promotion, but rather it was withheld due to pending investigation in a vigilance case, which could not be processed properly.
Meanwhile, the petitioner's promotion was validly considered on the relevant date, but the orders were kept in abeyance and he was being discriminated against in comparison to another employee, respondent No. 5, who was exonerated of charges against him and given clean chit.
On the other hand, respondent No. 5 was also ineligible for consideration due to pending charges against him.
The denial of the petitioner's promotion based on extraneous and mala fide factors is liable to be struck down, and subsequent orders of withholding ad hoc promotion must be quashed.
[Paras 8 to 13]
