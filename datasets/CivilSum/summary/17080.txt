Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - Prospectus - Where two conflicting prospectuses exist - one issued by the university for the Punjab Medical Entrance Test and one issued by the college where the student was admitted - it was held that the college (respondent No. 3) is obligated to allocate seats in accordance with the terms and conditions of the university prospectus - any deviation made by the college in its own prospectus is unlawful and does not bind students.
