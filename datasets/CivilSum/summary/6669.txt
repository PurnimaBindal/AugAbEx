Constitution of India, Article 226 - Air Force Rules, 1969 - Pension Regulations for Air Force, 1961 - Disability pension - Grant of - The decision of the respondents to not grant disability pension to the petitioner is unsustainable and arbitrary as the disease leading to the petitioner's discharge is deemed to have arisen during service.
The respondents must sanction disability pension at 60% for one year, as assessed by the Invalidating Medical Board.
