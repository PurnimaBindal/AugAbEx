The petitioner's writ petition can be admitted even if there is an alternative remedy available, as long as it involves a constitutional right and alleges discrimination.
The court cannot throw out the petition on the basis of a preliminary objection when the alternative remedy is time-barred.
If the Division Bench admitted the petition without reserving the right of the respondents for preliminary objections, it must be presumed that there was no force in the objections.
In such circumstances, the court is duty-bound to decide the petition on merits.
[Paras 6 to 9]

The Syndicate is empowered to fix the pay scales and allowances of university employees.
Seeking approval from the government for the release of arrears on account of a revision of pay scales is unjustified.
Once the Syndicate has made a decision, the government has no jurisdiction to disapprove it.
[Paras 12, 13, 18 and 19]

All employees must receive arrears equally when there is a revision of pay scales.
One group cannot be denied arrears without valid reason when others are paid from the same date.
This constitutes discrimination.
