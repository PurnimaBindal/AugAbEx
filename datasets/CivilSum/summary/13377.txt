Constitution of India, Article 226 - Complaints by local people - Transfer - Transfer allegedly made on the basis of complaints of people - A College Lecturer transferred about six times alleging transfers made on the behest of State Minister against whom his brother fought the election - Respondents instructed to produce records containing any complaints considered for transferring the petitioner - No complaint/record produced - Allegations of the petitioner must be accepted as there is no other option - Transfer quashed.
