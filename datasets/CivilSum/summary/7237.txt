The petitioner applied for voluntary retirement and requested to be retired with effect from a certain date.
Although the petitioner was required to serve a three-month notice as per rules, the authority accepted his request and passed an order retiring him from the requested date.
The authority waived the notice implicitly.
The petitioner later attempted to withdraw his application within a month after his retirement, arguing that he was allowed to withdraw the application within three months.
However, this plea was not acceptable.
[Paras 15, 16 and 17] B.
An employee requested voluntary retirement, and the employer accepted the request and relieved the employee from service.
In such a case, the employer cannot withdraw the application if it was accepted and the employee has already been relieved from service.
