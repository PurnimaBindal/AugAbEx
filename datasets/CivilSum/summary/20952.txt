Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - All India Service (Death-Cum-Retirement Benefits) Rules, 1958, Rule 16(3) - Compulsory retirement - A Court cannot quash an order of compulsory retirement based on uncommunicated adverse remarks being taken into consideration.
- Uncommunicated adverse remarks can be considered when exercising power of compulsory retirement.
