Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - The respondent was terminated from her position as Lady Attendant, despite being senior to those who were retained and new employees who were hired.
The petitioners argued that her position was purely ad hoc, only lasting 89 days, and that the termination was lawful.
The Labour Court disagreed, finding that the respondent's continued employment, despite the initial order of 89 days, constituted unfair labor practices.
The High Court agreed with the Labour Court's decision, stating that the employer could not take advantage of the workman's acceptance of the 89-day condition.
As a result, the petition was dismissed.
