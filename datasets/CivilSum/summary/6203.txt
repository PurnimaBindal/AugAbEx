Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 110(b) - Deputation - Repatriation - The appellants agreed to the terms of deputation with a clear understanding - They cannot now contest their repatriation after the deputation term has expired, claiming a right to the post - Administrative order/instructions do not prohibit deputation for a fixed period - Deputation is not contrary to Fundamental Rules pertaining to transfer or deputation - The repatriation order is deemed valid.
