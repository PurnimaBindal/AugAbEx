Constitution of India, Article 16 - Seniority - Ad hoc service - Regularisation of ad hoc service - Appointments made on ad hoc basis without conducting any competitive examination by calling candidates from local employment exchange - There were no rules in force at the time of appointments - The appointments on ad hoc basis continued until the Rules came into force - When the Rules were established, all the appointments were made permanent - The determination of seniority is only valid from the date the Rules came into force.
[Paras 5 and 7]
