Admission criteria for MDS course was to be based on marks obtained in BDS However, the selection method was arbitrarily changed after the applicant had submitted their application.
This mid-stream change is deemed unreasonable and petitioner is allowed admission to the MDS course.
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 26/227.
[Para 3] B The Guru Nanak Dev University Act, 1969, Section 21, Guru Nanak Dev University Calendar, Vol. II 1986, Ordinance in Chapter (iii) of Part B (misc. Ordinances) lays out the admission criterion for the MDS course.
Enforcing a change in the Ordinance can only be done by the Senate, which in this case was not followed.
The original criterion was not changed in accordance with the law and therefore admissions must be regulated based on the criterion laid out in the Ordinance, specifically marks obtained in BDS Petition allowed.
Constitution of India, Articles 226/227.
[Para 4] C Respondent was provisionally allowed admission to the MDS course because they had received higher marks in BDS than the petitioner.
It was ordered that respondent's admission be regularised.
