Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay scales - Equating posts and pay scales - The pay scale of Well Supervisors and that of Sectional Officers in the Agriculture department of Punjab were equated for the purpose of granting the same pay scales because they carry the same qualifications - Sectional Officers' pay scale was revised on the recommendation of the anomalies committee, and Well Supervisors are entitled to the same pay scale - If a post is equated with another subsequently, different pay scales cannot be prescribed for such posts if no cogent reasons are there - If the qualifications, work, and responsibilities of the two remain the same in the future, there is no reason why one of the posts should be prescribed a lower pay scale when the other is prescribed a higher one.
