Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical reimbursement - Refusal of - Petitioner's son underwent kidney transplantation from a specialized hospital not recognized by the government after being moved around in other hospitals for two years - The court held that since it cannot be disputed that the petitioner rushed to the specialist hospital to save the life of his son, the State is bound to reimburse the amount spent by him under the view taken by this court in Nand Kishore's Case - Reimbursement to be made as per rates applicable in AIIMS or PGIMS - Costs of Rs. 10,000/- were awarded as the respondent failed to follow the judgment laid in Nand Kishore's case.
