Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(1) and 12(5) - Reference - Refusal of - The question arises whether refusal to refer a dispute for adjudication by Industrial Tribunal is proper or not?
- While acting under Section 10(1), the Government could take broad features into consideration - However, it could not delve into the merits of the dispute and only performed administrative acts while making or refusing to make a reference - The concerned Government had examined the merits of disputed factual aspects, which is improper - Therefore, the matter has been directed to be decided afresh.
