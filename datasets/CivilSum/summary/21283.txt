Income Tax Appellate Tribunal (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1963, Rule 4 - Selection - Recommendations of Selection Board - The appointing authority must receive the recommendations of the Selection Board headed by a sitting judge of the Supreme Court within two months to consider for appointment.
The life of a select-penal cannot be deemed to have expired if recommendations are not considered.
[Paras 6 & 13]  B Income Tax Appellate Tribunal (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1963, Rule 4 - Selection/Appointment - While selection does not guarantee an appointment, it does provide a right of consideration.
The appointing authority cannot disregard the select-panel or reject appointment arbitrarily.
