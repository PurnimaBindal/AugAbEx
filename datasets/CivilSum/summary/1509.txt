Central Civil Service (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 47-A - Retiral benefits - The petitioner gave notice for voluntary retirement from 181985 but received no response from the respondents.
After three months from the notice, it was deemed that the respondents accepted the voluntary retirement.
Despite the lapse of 17 years, the pension and retiral benefits were not paid.
Therefore, the respondents were directed to finalize and pay the benefits within three months.
