Administrative Tribunals (Procedure) Rules, 1987, Rule 28(3) - Fundamental Rules, Rule 49 - Delegation of powers - Pay and allowances of higher post - The incumbent will not be entitled to the pay and allowances of the post of Registrar unless the appointment or delegation is made by the appointing authority - Chairman has no power and jurisdiction to make appointments to Class A posts in the Tribunal - Authority is with the President of India - In case the authority of appointments to Class A posts is delegated to the Chairman, the incumbent discharging the functions of Registrar would be entitled to claim the pay and allowance of the post of Registrar.
[Para 9 to 15, 17, 19 and 21]

Constitution of India, Article 309 - Contract Act, Section 70 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 49 - Pay and allowances - Delegation of powers and functions of higher post - Section 70 of the Contract Act cannot be invoked to claim pay and allowances governed by FR 49.
