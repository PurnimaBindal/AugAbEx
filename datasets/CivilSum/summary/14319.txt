According to Sections 11 and 36 of the Kerala Education Act, 1959 and Rules 51-B and 9-A of the Kerala Education Rules, 1959, the question of whether a married daughter of a person who died while in service is eligible for compassionate employment arises.
The 1992 government order had initially included married/unmarried sons and daughters for granting benefits.
However, the 1995 government order specifically excluded married sons and daughters from the list of dependents.
As a result, the petitioners are not entitled to compassionate employment.
