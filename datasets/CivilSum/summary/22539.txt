According to the Constitution of India, particularly Articles 14, 16 and 226, eligibility for admission to MBBS/BDS/BAMS courses at Himachal Pradesh University is specified in a combined prospectus which states that only serving Central Government employees are eligible.
An appellant whose mother is a State Government employee, but who went on deputation to work as a Central Government employee on her own request for health reasons, is questioning whether such an interpretation of the eligibility clause in the prospectus is fair.
The appellant argues that her mother's situation was not due to compulsion or exigencies of service, therefore her mother should not be considered a Central Government employee.
