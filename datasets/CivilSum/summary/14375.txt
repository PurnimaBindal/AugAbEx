Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 17 - Provident Fund - Rate of interest - Any establishment exempted under Section 17 must pay interest at the statutory rate, equivalent to the scheme.
The Central Government's determined rate of interest must be applied uniformly to all establishments, and any attempts to evade statutory benefits must be avoided.
Additionally, the exempted establishment must not provide less favorable benefits to its employees compared to those in other establishments under the Act and scheme.
