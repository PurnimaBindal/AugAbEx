Constitution of India, Article 226 - Judicial Review - The decision to make a special concession for Gopal Sahayaks in the matter of additional training as Veterinary Pharmacists was considered a policy decision, which is generally not interfered with by Courts unless it is "demonstrably capricious or arbitrary and not informed by any reason whatsoever or it suffers from the vice of discrimination or infringes any statute or provisions of the Constitution."
[Para 14]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Classification - The classification founded on an intelligent differentia distinguishing two groups having a rational relation to the object sought to be achieved by the policy decision must be upheld.
[Para 16]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Classification - Estoppel - A person who benefits from one of the schemes on classification is prevented from challenging it.
