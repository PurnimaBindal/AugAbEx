Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Award of Labour Court - Adjudication - Reference against new management dismissed on maintainability grounds without merit - High Court cannot reinstate workman without proper adjudication and fixing parties' rights and liabilities according to the law - In such cases, High Court should remand matter to Labour Court for appropriate adjudication.
