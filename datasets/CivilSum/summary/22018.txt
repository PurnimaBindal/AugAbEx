Rule 3(3)(c) - Indian Administrative Service (Regulation of Seniority) Rules, 1987, Rule 3(3)(iii) - Seniority - Inter se seniority of a State Civil Service Officer and non-State Civil Service Officer recruited to the service by selection - The validity of the upheld Rule 3(3)(c) by the Supreme Court.
The Court has upheld the validity of Rule 3(3)(c) in its entirety (including the proviso), and the order passed by the Tribunal that the Proviso to Rule 3(3)(c) is invalid cannot be upheld and must be ignored.
