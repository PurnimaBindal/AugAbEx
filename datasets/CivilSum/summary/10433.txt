Constitution of India, Article 226 - CBI Enquiry - Vigilance Enquiry - Departmental proceedings - Petition filed for a direction to the State Govt. to order CBI Enquiry against Managing Director and another officer of the Sugar Mill for allegations of irregularities resulting in loss to public exchequer - However, a Vigilance enquiry and departmental proceedings are already underway for the same matter and delinquents are disputing the allegations - Court concludes that once proceedings are in accordance with the law, law will take its own course and if allegations are proven, they will be punished - Court cannot take on the role of Criminal Court or punishing authority - Petition dismissed as infructuous with direction to complete proceedings within six months.
