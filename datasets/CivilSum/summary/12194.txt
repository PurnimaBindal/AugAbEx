Article 16 of the Constitution of India, 1950 deals with appointment and no candidate can be denied an appointment solely on the grounds that they acquired ETT qualifications from Chandigarh.
The State's action in not permitting the appointed petitioners to join their duties is not sustainable and not in consonance with the advertisement.
The directive of the letter must be followed prospectively and not retrospectively as this practice of the state in the past had given the impression that candidates who have done their ETT Course from Chandigarh were eligible and would be appointed to the post.
