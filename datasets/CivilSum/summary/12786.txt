Navy Pension Regulations, Regulation 101 - Regulations for Medical Services of Armed Forces, Appendix-V and Para 423 - Entitlement Rules - Disability Pension - A signal man in the navy, who was permanently placed in category 'Bee' and invalidated out of service after surgery, had his disability pension claim rejected on the basis that his disability was not attributed nor aggravated by military service.
However, a conjoint reading of the rules reveals that invalidment from service is a necessary condition for the grant of disability pension and that if no note to the contrary is made at the time of an individual's acceptance for naval service, the disease that led to his discharge or death will be deemed to have arisen in service.
The presumption is rebuttable, however, provided reasoned medical opinion is given showing that the disease could have been detected on medical examination prior to acceptance for service.
Therefore, the appellant is entitled to disability pension.
[Paras 7 and 8]
