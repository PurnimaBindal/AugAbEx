UP State University Act, 1973, Sections 31(5) and 66(a) - Clauses 168, 169 and 171 of the Statute of the University - Selection - Selection Committee - Experts - The post of Reader in Linguistics in Hindi Department was selected, but the experts for linguistic and Hindi literature were separate - The Selection Committee consisted of only the experts from the Hindi subject panel, even though there was no mention of the post in linguistics being common with any other post - Thus, the appointment of experts from the Hindi language panel for the selection of Reader in linguistics was completely illegal.
