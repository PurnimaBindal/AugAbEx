Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Act, Section 113 - Army Rules 22, 24, 38 and 41 - Constitution and jurisdiction of Court Martial Committee - Court Martial - Joint Court Martial - For an offence committed collectively by several Army Officials, a Joint Court Martial is permitted, though an individual can also be charged with a separate offense for which others are not answerable - If the Court Martial is legally and properly constituted and the procedure followed, the writ court will not review the Court Martial's conclusions in the exercise of its Article 226 authority.
