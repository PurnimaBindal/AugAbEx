Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order VIII Rule 1 - Special Review (Instructions laid in Army HQ letter dated June 10, 1985) - Promotion - Single judge has directed to reconsider the petitioner's case for promotion according to special review instructions - Petitioner claims that in the absence of written statements, allegations were considered to be admitted - It is said in a writ petition under Article 226 that even in the absence of a written statement, allegations made in the petition would not be considered as deemed admission - In a writ petition, the original record or written statement that reflects the accurate facts on record must be presented and taken into account to make a proper decision about the matter - As a result, the finding that each successive consideration was made on the same material is incorrect and is overturned - As a result, the appeal is approved.
