Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation of services - Ad hoc employees seeking regularisation of services from the date of initial appointment - The services of employees must be regularised based on their initial appointment and completion of 240 working days -Principles for regularisation of services laid down in State of Haryana v. Piara Singh - High Court cannot issue a blanket direction to regularise the services of the employees on completion of a particular period - Order of learned single Judge modified to allow workmen to receive regularisation of their services from the date of their initial appointment to such posts upon completion of 240 working days - Claim for arrears or back wages is not accepted.
