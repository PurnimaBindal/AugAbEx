Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Regularisation - A person appointed on a stop-gap arrangement, or till regular appointments are made, does not have the right of regularisation on that post simply on the basis of working for 8/9 months.
- For the issuance of a writ of mandamus, the petitioner must establish a legal right and corresponding legal obligation in the respondents to provide the relief claimed.
