Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission - Reservation in admissions - Waiting list - Degree course in Engineering/Technology/Architecture - 85% seats reserved for Punjab Quota and 15% for All India Quota - A candidate of State quota has the ability to beat a candidate of All India quota based on merit, but a candidate of All India quota cannot demand a seat in State quota solely based on merit in All India Quota - Petitioner, an All India Quota candidate at Sr. No. 13 in the waiting list, cannot claim a seat in State quota against a candidate who was at Sr. No. 8 and got admission in All India Quota - Even if some seats are still available, petitioner is not entitled to relief above other waiting list candidates.
