As per Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, promotions, reversions, and qualifications should be fair and non-discriminatory.
The petitioner in this case was originally recruited as a Sub Inspector in the Food and Supplies Department in Punjab in 1970.
Over time, the petitioner was promoted to Junior Analyst and then to Inspector.
On July 2, 1986, the petitioner was promoted again to Head Analyst.
However, the petitioner was later reverted on appeal of another employee who claimed that an Inspector cannot be promoted as Head Analyst.
The petitioner argued that since he was already an Analyst, his appointment as an Inspector (whether confirmed or not) should not affect his right to be promoted as Head Analyst.
Previous case law on similar matters was discussed, and ultimately the judge quashed the reversion order.
