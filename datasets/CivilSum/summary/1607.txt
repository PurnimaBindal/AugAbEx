Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39(d) - Pay parity - Shepherds of Animal Husbandary Department seeking equal pay with guards of Forest Department - Both services have similar modes of recruitment, qualifications and eligibility - Shepherds have to take care of sheep and goats in high altitudes round the year, which is a more dangerous duty than that of the forest guards, who also have to take care of trees in higher altitude - Shepherds do not receive the special allowance and uniform given to guards - The duties and responsibilities of both categories are remarkably similar, as are their working conditions - Shepherds have been discriminated against after the pay revision of 1982, despite starting as common category posts with identical grade and pay - The doctrine of 'equal pay for equal work' must be applied to ensure pay parity between shepherds and forest guards.
