Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Termination of services - Natural justice - Selection - Recruitment - An employee selected for a job cannot be dismissed solely on the basis of complaints that the selection procedure was illegal or unjust without giving the employee a chance to defend themselves - Employees are entitled to know the allegations against them and the material used to terminate their services - Once appointed, employees have a right to hold their post - Newspaper reports and complaints do not provide conclusive evidence for termination - The petitioner's termination on the basis of such reports and complaints have been quashed.
[Paras 11, 13, 14, 16 and 25]
