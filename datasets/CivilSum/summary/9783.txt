Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(l) (c) - Termination - Departmental enquiry - A workman had their services terminated without any enquiry, notice, or retrenchment compensation.
The labour court has directed their reinstatement with continuity of service and back wages, however, no action has been taken against other similarly situated individuals.
It was held that the workman was entitled to be informed of the charges levied against them when it was the management's case that the termination was due to misconduct.
The management failed to observe the bare minimum principles of the Rules of natural justice and subjected the workman to hostile discrimination.
Therefore, the award does not suffer from an error that is apparent on the face of it, and the petition is dismissed accordingly.
