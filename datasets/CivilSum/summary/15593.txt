Constitution of India, Article 311 - Removal - Retirement - The appellant-employee retired from service while facing disciplinary proceedings against him - No explicit or implicit directive to keep him in service during disciplinary proceedings - No rule to impose punishment of removal from service on ex-employee - Thus, the penalty of removing service of delinquent cannot be upheld - Respondents must process appellant's retiral benefits.
