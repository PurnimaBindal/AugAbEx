Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 16 - Adverse Confidential Report - Premature retirement - The petitioner received a report claiming that he was `below average' and his integrity was `doubtful' resulting in his premature retirement - However, all other reports before and after this report made no such negative comments regarding the petitioner's work or conduct - As such, the adverse remarks were unjustified and in contradiction to established norms of law, leading to the quashing of both the adverse remarks and the petitioner's premature retirement.
