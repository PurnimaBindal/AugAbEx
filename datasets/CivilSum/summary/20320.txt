Regarding promotions in the Department of Posts, Postal Superintendents, and Postmaster Group B Recruitment Rules, 1987 (as amended in 1994), Article 16 of the Constitution of India states that departmental examinations must be taken into consideration.
An amendment to the rules meant that clerks in the RMS could no longer be considered for the 6% quota reserved for them.
The respondent was eligible but did not apply against the 19% quota.
As a result, the tribunal's decision to set aside the examination already conducted was incorrect, and conducting the examination afresh would be illegal.
