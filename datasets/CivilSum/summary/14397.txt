The Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Appointment on compassionate ground- The purpose of compassionate appointment is to provide quick relief to a grieving family after the passing of an earning member - It does not grant a guaranteed right that can be claimed at any time in the future - Even if one applies late, such appointments cannot be given or demanded after the crisis has ended - In the case of a petitioner who applied for a compassionate appointment six years after his father's death, his request was turned down as it was barred by time - The petition was dismissed.
