Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 - Education - An interim injunction in mandatory form for issuance of BA degree was granted - The respondent obtained a BA degree by fraudulent means - The petitioner-University argued that the respondent cannot practice law since their LL.B degree rests on the non-existent BA degree - The court held that while it may be a case of hardship, there is no equity in favour of the respondent - The mandatory injunction granted by lower courts was vacated.
