Sections 25-G and 25-H of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, pertain to termination and reinstatement of workers.
The case at hand involved a Security Guard employed on a contract basis, for a duration dependent on work availability.
The Guard had worked for 243 days before being terminated.
However, the nature of the appointment precluded the possibility of reinstatement or regularization of employment.
Additionally, the worker had already received dues under Section 17-B of the Act, and no compensation was awarded accordingly.
This judgement is informed by 2006(4) SCT 772 (SC).
