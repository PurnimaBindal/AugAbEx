According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part I, Rule 8.138(A) - Evidence Act, Section 115 - Estoppel - Service conditions - Terms and conditions of appointment, employees who have accepted the terms and conditions of their appointment cannot challenge such conditions unless they were oppressive or offensive, after taking full benefit of its beneficial clauses.
Those who have been adjusted from surplus employees of a temporary position in another department cannot claim benefit of their earlier service, and permanent protection under the rule is just for employees coming from one permanent department of the State to another.
