Under the Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Sections 15(2) and 15(3), providing uniforms and shoes to the Class-IV employees is governed by the Rules of service.
If the uniforms and shoes are provided to employees who are not entitled to receive them, this is considered as an amenity.
Employers may discontinue or even not provide uniforms and shoes at all.
Employees who are not entitled to uniforms cannot demand them from the employer as a matter of right.
The petitioners are Class III employees and there is no provision to supply uniforms or shoes to them.
The fact that uniforms were supplied to them for a few years and then stopped does not give them the right to demand them.
Therefore, this claim does not fall within the definition of the Act, and the application is time-barred.
