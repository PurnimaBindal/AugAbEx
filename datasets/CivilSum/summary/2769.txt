Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(ra) and 25-T - Challenge to order of transfer as unfair labor practice - Writ petition - Maintainability - Remedy for prevention or grant of interim stay against Unfair Labour Practice not available under ID Act - One should not be debarred from filing a writ petition due to disputed questions of fact - Affidavits may be required as evidence in the writ petition - Dismissal of writ petition on the ground of the existence of an efficacious alternate remedy violates the judgment of the Supreme Court in Hindustan Lever Limited v. Ashok Vishnu Kate (1995 Lab.
IC 2714) - Review petition granted - Writ petition to be disposed of on merits.
