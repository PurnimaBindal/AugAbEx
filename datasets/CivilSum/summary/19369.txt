UP Higher Education Service Commission Act, 1980, Section 31(C)(3) - (as amended in 1992) - Ad hoc appointment against leave vacancy - Lecturer - Selection - The petitioner, who was working as a lecturer on a leave vacancy on an ad hoc basis, was selected by the Commission for further appointment on leave vacancy.
However, the petitioner did not take up the new post and demanded regular appointment instead.
The State Government found the petitioner's claim to be justified and recommended accordingly.
However, the petitioner's name was deleted from the select list without affording any opportunity of hearing.
This action is illegal, and the petitioner is entitled to benefit under Section 31(C)(3).
