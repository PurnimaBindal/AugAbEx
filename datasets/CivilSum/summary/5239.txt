Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, Section 45 - Premature retirement - Pendency of vigilance investigation - Doubtful integrity - Public interest - No adverse entry in service record of employee - The premature retirement order is not sustainable in the absence of evidence supporting the suspicion of doubtful integrity, especially since the report of the vigilance investigation was in favour of the employee, and there are no adverse entries in the employee's service record.
