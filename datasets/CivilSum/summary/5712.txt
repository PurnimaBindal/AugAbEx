Industrial Employment Standing Orders Act, 1946, Section 6, Clause 16 - Maintenance of essential services - Clause 16 grants Management the authority to request workmen to come to work on national or festival holidays, when essential services are needed, or in the interest of maintaining continuous manufacturing processes and essential supplies for the organization - Appellate Authority affirms the clause's legality, and it is comprehensive in specifying the eventualities and details under which workers are deployed - Moreover, the workers so deployed are entitled to additional compensation for work on holidays - The Appellate Authority found that Management's freedom is essential to ensure safety and continuous operation of the undertaking.
