Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Probationer - Reversion - Natural Justice - If a probationary period is not completed satisfactorily, a simple order should be passed upon the expiry of the extended period.
A reversion order based on complaints is considered punitive and a reversion order without issuing a proper charge sheet and without holding a regular enquiry violates principles of natural justice.
[Paras 4 and 5] B Appeal - Speaking order - When appealing against a punitive order, it should be decided with a speaking order after proper consideration.
