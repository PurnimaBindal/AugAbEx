Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Kerala Public Services Act, 1968, Section 2 - Indian Systems of Medicine (Kerala) Service Special Rules, 2005, Rule 3 - Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1958, Rule 4 - Promotion - select list - The position of Chief Medical Officer (CMO) - The vacancies had to be filled by appointing members belonging to SMO (Ayurveda) and SMO (Specialist), being the feeder categories, by transfer - Members of qualifying service belonging to the category of SMO (Specialist) with a higher time scale had to be considered first for filling vacancies - The select list failed to include the names of SMO (Specialist) - The selection was vitiated - The select list was set aside.
