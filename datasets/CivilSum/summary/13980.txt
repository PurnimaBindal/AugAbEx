According to Section 128 of the Rajasthan Cooperative Societies Act, 1965, both the State Government and Registrar have revisional jurisdiction.
The power vested in both authorities are concurrent, but the Additional Registrar must decide revision as a delegate of the State Government and not as a delegate of the Registrar.
In case of a second revision, the State Government is entitled to invoke its own revisional jurisdiction under section 128.
