Punjab Tehsildars (Residuary Service Matters) Regulations, 1984, Regulation 4 - Seniority - Petitioners were ignored for promotion due to wrong junior status on the tentative seniority list - Directed respondents to consider their case for promotion to the post of Tehsildar if found entitled for promotion.
