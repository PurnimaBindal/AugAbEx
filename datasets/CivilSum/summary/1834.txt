Army Regulations, 1961, Regulation 173, Appendix II, Rules 4 and 7 - Entitlement Rules for Casualty Pensionary Awards, 1982, Rule 9 - Disability Pension - The authority must prove that the constitutional disease was not attributable or aggravated by military service.
If there is no note of the medical board or history of the disease in the official's family, the disease cannot be considered constitutional and the official is entitled to the benefit of doubt for disability pension.
The official can only be denied the benefit if the medical board categorically finds that the disease was undetectable at the time of enrollment and actually existed then.
Only a casual connection between disablement or death and military service is required for attributability or aggravation.
