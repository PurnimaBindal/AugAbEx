Rule 8 of the Punjab Service of Engineers Class - 1, P.W.D.
(B & R) Rules, 1960 states that promotion can be granted even during a pending departmental enquiry.
In this case, an officer faced a misconduct allegation in 1973 and an enquiry was conducted from 1975 to 1989.
The officer was refused promotion due to the pending enquiry, but the government's order was not valid as any punishment awarded would relate back to 1973.
Only the record of the 5 years preceding the promotion was to be considered, and the officer was found suitable for promotion.
[Para 9]

According to Articles 16 and 14 of the Constitution of India, suitability for promotion is determined by the government based on the officer's record.
The court cannot question the suitability of an officer, but can examine if the consideration was done on relevant record and material.
