Constitution of India Articles 14, 16 and 39(d) - Equal pay for equal work - A service juriesprudence necessitates equal pay for those in similar positions unless there is a statutory provision to the contrary.
Petitioners, Laboratory Instructors, who claim to be on par with teaching staff and physical instructor, should be given similar scale of pay.
If individuals with lesser qualifications are given the same pay as those who have higher qualifications and responsibilities, it will constitute reverse discrimination.
As per GO dated 26.12.94, the Petitioner can continue to be a teaching staff but would only be entitled to the pay scale payable to non-teaching staff.
