Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Punjab Police Rules, Volume 2, Chapter XVI, Rule 161 & 164 - Reversion from the post of Sub Inspector to ASI - Transfer from one Range to another Range - The reversion order passed by DIG Rohtak Range is not sustainable as the transfer order of the petitioner had already been passed from Rohtak Range to the office of DIG/HAP Madhuban, and DIG Rohtak had no control or jurisdiction over the petitioner.
It does not matter that the order of transfer was received in the Rohtak office later than the reversion order.
