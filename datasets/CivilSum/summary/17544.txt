Constitution of India Article 311(1) - Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations 1971 Regulations 50 54 55 56 58 - Competent Authority for punishment - Initiation of disciplinary proceedings - Only the appointing authority of an employee can initiate disciplinary proceedings against him - In this case, the appointing authority was the Zonal Manager - The Senior Regional Manager, who is of lower rank, had no jurisdiction to issue a charge sheet - Therefore, the disciplinary proceedings cannot be continued and the charge sheet has been quashed.
