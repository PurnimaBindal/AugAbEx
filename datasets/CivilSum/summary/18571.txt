Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Trust Services (Recruitment & Conducts of Service) Rules, 1978, Rule 9(1) - Compassionate appointment - Reversion of - The reversion of an employee can take place if their work and conduct are deemed unsatisfactory, despite being given a compassionate appointment.
In this case, the petitioner was reverted due to not passing matriculation in the second division, although it was not a condition stated in the appointment letter.
The reversion order was found to be unsustainable due to no fraud or misrepresentation on the employee's part.
