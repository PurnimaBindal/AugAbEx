The Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 14 pertains to Termination of service.
The petitioner who worked as a class IV servant in Air Force had their services terminated due to being considered a habitual offender.
However, it was held that petitioner cannot be classified as a habitual offender solely based on having received three or more punishments.
The Disciplinary Authority didn't consider petitioner's reply to show cause notice before passing termination order, so the principles of natural justice were violated.
Order termination was set aside.
[Paras 9, 12 and 13]

The Constitution of India Article 226 provides for Exemplary cost.
The petitioner, a Class IV employee in Air Force, had their services terminated in violation of natural justice principles.
Since petitioner already completed 20 years of service, there is no question of re-instatement.
Full back wages were granted along with consequential benefits as if the petitioner was never terminated.
Respondent Central Government was further directed to pay exemplary cost of Rs. 10,000/- to petitioner since they suffered financially and mentally because of the respondent's rigid attitude.
[Paras 13 and 14]
