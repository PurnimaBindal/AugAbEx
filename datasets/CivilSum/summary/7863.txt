Constitution of India, Article 309 - Rajasthan Civil Service (Pension) Rules 1996, Rule 53(1) - Compulsory Retirement - Tribunal overturned the decision of compulsory retirement being passed under repealed provision - Stated that the order cannot be invalidated solely due to mistakenly stating wrong statutory provision, as long as the authority had the ability to make the decision.
