The appointment of ex-servicemen as Hindi Teachers is governed by the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 16 and 226.
In cases where an ex-serviceman possesses a higher qualification than what is prescribed, appointment is not offered.
However, in a situation where the dependent of an ex-serviceman possesses the essential qualifications, appointment may be given to them instead.
The respondents have been directed to offer the petitioner the appointment without affecting the current employment status of the dependent who has been serving for two years.
[Paras 3 and 4]
