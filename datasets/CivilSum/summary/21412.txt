Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 - Regularisation - Daily Wage workers - Appointment on daily wages does not equate to an appointment to the post - Recruitment based on rules is a prerequisite for post appointments - Once the project that daily wage workers were engaged in is completed, the court cannot direct their re-engagement in different work or for vacancies - Allowing this would turn court processes into a recruitment method not based on rules - Appointment through daily wages would be a back door entry.
[Paras 2 and 4] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 - Work-charged employees - Regularisation - Temporary appointment - Work-charged and temporary appointments are made due to the temporary and urgent nature of work, not to appoint someone to a post - Temporary employees cannot simply be replaced by another temporary employee.
