Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Examination - Revision of result - Petitioner's result declared on 5.8.2003 and declared to have passed - Petitioner was employed - After 14 months, respondents revised result without hearing petitioner - No evidence of fraud or misrepresentation by petitioner - Court deemed the revision of result totally illegal and arbitrary - Orders quashed.
