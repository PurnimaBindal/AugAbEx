Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Punjab Police Rules, Rule 1411, Vol. II - Resignation - Duress and coercion - The appellant claimed that their resignation was not voluntary and was due to pressure and coercion - Trial Court and appeal dismissed the claim - A second appeal was filed - It was determined that there was no evidence to support the claim and that the resignation was accepted at the appellant's own request - The appellant had not provided any specific details to support their claim of undue influence or coercion - Both Courts had reached the same verdict - No further action was necessary - Appeal dismissed.
