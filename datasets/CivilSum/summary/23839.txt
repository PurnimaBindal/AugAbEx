The Constitution of India, Article 16 - High Court Judges (Conditions of Service) Act, 1954, Schedule I, Rules 2 and 7 - Pension - Retired puisne judges who served as acting Chief Justice are not eligible for higher pension under Article 223.
Only those who retire as a Chief Justice are eligible.
In this case, the retired judge worked as Acting Chief Justice for 28 months and 23 days and is not entitled to higher pension.

High Court Judges (Conditions of Service) Act, 1954, Schedule I, Rule 2 proviso, Rule 7 - When the language of a provision is clear and unambiguous, it is not permissible to interpret it differently.
This rule applies to beneficial legislation, such as this case.
