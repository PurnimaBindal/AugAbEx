According to Rules such as Punjab Municipal Election Rules, 1994, Rule 87, Department of Posts, Gramin Dak Sewak (Conduct and Employment) Rules, 2001, Rule 22(4), and Punjab State Election Commission Act, 1994, Section 11(g), a Gramin Dak Sewak is considered a government servant and is therefore disqualified from becoming a member of Nagar Panchayat under Section 11(g).
Government employees are banned from participating in Panchayat or Nagar Panchayat elections, as the legislative objective is to ensure that a candidate's status does not prejudice another candidate who does not enjoy it.
Moreover, an office of profit is any office that can yield a profit or pecuniary gain, and holding an office under the State or Central Government, where pay, salary, emolument, remuneration, or non-compensatory allowance is attached, is considered an office of profit.
The holder of an office of profit under the Government may not necessarily be in the service of the Government or have a master-servant relationship with them, as the Constitution draws a distinction between them and officeholders under local authorities or authorities subject to Government oversight.
