Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 21 - Jurisdiction - Objection - It has been held that the annulment of an entire trial on the basis of a territorial jurisdiction objection should not occur unless there is a proven failure of justice.
Both parties litigated, produced evidence, and the trial was completed.
The matter remained pending before the trial court from 1982 to 1988.
No failure of justice occurred and the respondents did not suffer any prejudice by the trial of the suit.
As no finding by the Single Judge was made that prejudice was caused, the judgement and decree passed by the Single Judge were set aside and the appeal was allowed.
