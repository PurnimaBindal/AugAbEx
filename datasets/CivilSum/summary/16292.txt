According to Rule 1221 of the Punjab Police Rules, 1939, the petitioner was discharged from his duties due to the belief that he would not be an efficient police officer.
The order was passed by an officer who had never seen the petitioner's performance, and it came after three years and four months.
However, upon representation, the release order was set aside, and the petitioner was reinstated.
It is important to note that there is no provision for passing a discharge order by reviewing a reinstatement order.
