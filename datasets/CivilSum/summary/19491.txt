According to the Punjab Civil Services Rules (as applicable to Haryana) Vol. 1, Rule 44, Banks have their own policies for ex gratia payment to the family of deceased employees killed in terrorist activities.
The policy of the State Government is not applicable to the Banks and the family of the deceased is entitled to ex gratia payment under the Policy of the Bank only.
[Para 5]
As per the Constitution of India, Article 14, the family of employees killed in terrorist activities are entitled to ex gratia payment.
However, if the employee was under suspension on account of a criminal case or was not killed in terrorist activities or during the course of employment, the family of the deceased is not entitled to ex gratia payment under the policy of the Bank.
