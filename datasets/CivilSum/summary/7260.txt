According to MP Public Works Department Work Charged and Contingency Paid Employees Recruitment and Conditions of Service Rules, 1976, Rules 2(b), 8 and the MP Work Charged and Contingency Paid Employees Revision of Pay Rules, 1977, Schedule, the age of superannuation for gangmen is 62 years.
Furthermore, even though gangmen are not included in the schedule of 1976 Rules and the age of superannuation is 62 years as other Class IV employees of the State Government, their services are governed by the Rules applicable to work charge and contingency paid employees because they are in a comparable category.
