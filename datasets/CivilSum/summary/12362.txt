Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33 - Writ petition - The petitioner-Union has invoked the adjudicatory machinery provided under the Industrial Disputes Act and the matter is referred to an Industrial Tribunal for adjudication.
As a result, the writ petition is not maintainable due to the availability of an alternative remedy.
The petitioner may raise all its points before the Industrial Tribunal instead.
[Para 4]
Alternative Remedy - `Doctrine of Election' - The `Doctrine of Election' stipulates that once a litigant has selected one Forum, as the petitioner-Union did by invoking the adjudicatory machinery provided under the Industrial Disputes Act, they cannot subsequently look to another Forum provided for the same purpose.
