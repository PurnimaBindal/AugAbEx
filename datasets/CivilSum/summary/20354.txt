Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Indian Forests Service (Recruitment) Rules 1966 - Seniority - Quota Rule - Appointments made in violation to quota are fortuitous - Service Rules require 1:1 quota - Quota rule remains unless special reasons are given for not following it - Promotees cannot benefit from seniority based on fortuitous appointments - Tribunal direction to determine seniority by following quota rule upheld - Appeals dismissed.
