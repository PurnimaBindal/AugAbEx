Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Badli workmen - Permanent status - Two Abad Dairy workmen assert that they are entitled to be made permanent because they worked for 900 days in 5 years.
They resist the demand in line with the award rendered in Ref.
No. 179 of 1975 of Industrial Tribunal, Ahmedabad.
The said award was rendered in connection with the workmen employed in the Engineering Department of Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation.
Abad Dairy was initially an establishment of Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation.
Now it has an independent autonomous existence under the control of Gujarat Dairy Development Corporation.
The employer pleads that the said award is not applicable to present workmen.
And Badli workmen are not entitled to be made permanent.
The ruling is that the employer is allowed to extend certain benefits to its workmen based on general principles, without any binding award or adjudication from any quarter.
There is no prohibition against the employer for acting reasonably in consonance with fairness, equity, and justice.
[Paras 1 and 7]  B Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provision) Act, 1985, Section 22 - The provision of section 22 does not prevent the competent Tribunal from raising the industrial dispute for granting permanent status to an employee.
The proceedings are not in the nature of execution or the like against the properties of the Industrial company, nor is it a suit for the recovery of money or the enforcement of any security against the Industrial company.
