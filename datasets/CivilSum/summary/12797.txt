According to Section 16 of UP Higher Education Services Commission Act (16 of 1980), petitioner who has been appointed as an officiating principal cannot be removed from service without proper following of fair procedure and principles of natural justice, as the petitioner has been performing the duties of the principal for more than 4 years.
[Paras 9, 13, and 14]

In accordance with Article 16 of the Constitution of India, a person who is officiating as a principal and performing all the duties of the principal cannot be denied the salary of the said post.
[Para 14]
