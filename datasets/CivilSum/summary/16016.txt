Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Reimbursement of medical expenses - The Medical Board cannot refuse ex-post facto permission for medical treatment taken at a private hospital, especially in the case of emergency open heart surgery.
The petitioner should not be held responsible for a lack of prior approval in a life-threatening situation.
The Respondents are directed to reimburse the petitioner's medical expenses, with interest at a rate of 18% per annum from the date of treatment until the amount is paid.
[Paras 9, 10 and 11]
Estoppel - Medical reimbursement - The fact that the petitioner had previously waived his right to claim medical reimbursement does not prevent him from doing so if the rules permit it.
The petitioner is entitled to reimbursement of his medical expenses, as established by DB in the case of Dr. Prem Nath Garg v. State of Punjab.
[Para 9]
