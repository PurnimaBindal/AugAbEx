Constitution of India, Article 16 - An employee sent on deputation - The parent department retains the right to recall its employees and no consent is necessary if there is no contract guaranteeing a specific tenure for the deputation post.
[Para 5]
Constitution of India, Article 16 - Deputation - The parent department can always recall an employee on deputation unless they have been absorbed by the borrower department.
The mere proposal for absorption does not prevent the parent department from recalling its employees.
