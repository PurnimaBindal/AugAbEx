Under the U.P.
Industrial Disputes Act 28 of 1947, Sections 2(z) and 2(a), ``Workman'' only includes apprentice as defined under Section 2(a).
Therefore, someone appointed as ``trainee'' for ``training'' who is not an apprentice is not considered a workman.
[Para 8] B The UP Industrial Disputes Act 28 of 1947, Sections 2(a) and 2(z), define an ``apprentice'' as someone who is paid for ``hire'' or ``reward'' which is proportionate to their service rendered.
As the trainee appointed was only paid Rs. 5/- or Rs. 8/- per day, which was much less than the Rs. 21/- per day paid to other workmen, and they did not produce any work, they cannot be considered a workman.
