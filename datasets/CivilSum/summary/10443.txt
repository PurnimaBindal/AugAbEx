According to Sections 207 and 192 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, if a motor vehicle plies without a permit, it can be impounded.
However, if a Magistrate determines that Supardari is appropriate in the case, they have the power to release the vehicle.
It was argued that releasing the vehicle on Supardari would interfere with the power of the State Transport Authority, but this argument was rejected.
Section 207 of the Motor Vehicles Act does not take away the power of the court to release the vehicle on Supardari.
