Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Back-wages - Ex parte award against State - Although the State was given numerous adjournments it failed to file a reply - Even if the court was inclined to pass an ex-parte order, it should have directed the petitioner to present relevant evidence - There was no evidence to suggest that the workman was not gainfully employed over the last decade - The Labour Court granted 50% back wages which this award sets aside.
