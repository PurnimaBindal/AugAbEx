According to Rule 252 of Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume I, Part I, special pay is an additional payment to the emoluments of a post or a Government employee, granted in consideration of the arduous nature of the duties or a specific addition to the work or responsibility.
It has been held that drivers working in the Ministers' Car Section perform more arduous duties than drivers in Haryana Roadways, and therefore, a higher rate of special pay has been sanctioned for them, which is not arbitrary or violative of the principle of equal pay for equal work.
Hence, the Court has dismissed the petition challenging the action of the respondents in granting special pay of Rs. 150/- to drivers attached to Ministers and Rs. 25/- only to drivers in the Haryana Roadways, as it does not interfere with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
