Posts for Ayurvedic Medical Officers require re-examination due to it being conducted in Roman Script (English language only) by Haryana Staff Selection Commission.
Petitioners argued that they could not understand and answer the questions as all Ayurvedic Medical Colleges had Hindi and Sanskrit as their medium of instruction.
However, Constitutional Authorities have no prescription on the language of exams as stated in the Constitution of India, Articles 343, 345, 347, and 351.
There are no Statutory Rules stating that the test can only be conducted in Hindi and not in English.
[Para 10] B Petitioners declared fail after attempting papers for examination.
Despite this, they cannot challenge the usage of Roman Script in the written examination as stated under the Constitution of India, Article 16.
Petitions have been dismissed.
