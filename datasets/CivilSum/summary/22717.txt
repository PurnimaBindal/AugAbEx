Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admissions - Education - Reservation in admissions - While regional considerations for admissions in Medical Colleges (MD and M Ch.)
do not meet the standards of Article 14 of the Constitution, a rule stating that preference is to be given to students of the university in question for admission to super speciality courses out of the prepared merit list cannot be considered arbitrary, unreasonable or in violation of Article 14 - The intention is to avoid the students who were enrolled in a specific university from suddenly finding themselves without any options for admission into super speciality courses.
