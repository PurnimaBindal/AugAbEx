Constitution of India, Article 14 and 226 - Karnataka Conduct of Entrance Test for Admission to Post-Graduate Degree and Diploma (Medical and Dental Courses) Rules, 1987, Rule 2(h) - Post Graduate Courses - Reservation - Admission limited to doctors in government service, ESI and autonomous institutions - Industrial employees do not qualify for admission - Purpose of classification is to provide further education to those working in institutions who serve the general public - State owned industrial employees rightly excluded from in-service candidates - Therefore, classification under Rule 2(h) is just, rational, and constitutional.
