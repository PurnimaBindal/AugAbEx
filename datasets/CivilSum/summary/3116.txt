Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Central Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, Rule 5(i) - Termination - Reinstatement - The fact that an employee has been acquitted of criminal charges does not automatically entitle them to reinstatement in service - Accusations in a criminal case may be grounds for termination, but not automatic reinstatement - In this case, the termination was valid and not preceded by a stigmatizing inquiry.
