Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation/Salary - The petitioner was appointed as a peon in a joint department, which was later bifurcated, and allocated to one of the departments.
Despite working for 12 years, the petitioner was not paid salary and refused work.
The government authorities could not simply end the petitioner's uninterrupted 12 years of service.
The authorities are expected to act fairly and responsibly as government officers, which they failed to do.
The court directed the authorities to reinstate the petitioner and pay their salary while regularising them from the date of reinstatement.
