Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Interest - The Labour Court can grant interest to the parties concerned in case of special circumstances.
Furthermore, if the employer is found guilty of delaying the payment and/or implementation of the order of the Court, the Labour Court can grant interest on delayed payment.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Interest of justice - The discretionary remedy provided under Art. 226 is not appellate or revisionary jurisdiction but is supplemental to law based on principles of justice rather than pure application of law.
The discretionary remedy should be used to achieve the ends of justice and should not be used to set at naught the decision arrived at by the Subordinate Court.
