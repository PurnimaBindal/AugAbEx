"At the time of admission, students were required to have passed B.Sc.
Agriculture from Guru Nanak Dev University, Amritsar with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks.
However, this condition was later abrogated without the knowledge of the students, only to be reinstated after one session.
It was determined that the conditions made at the time of admission would govern the result of the course and could not be aggravated mid-session.
The university was instructed to declare the results of the petitioners without applying the subsequent change in the conditions.
[Paras 2 to 5]"
