Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation - Petitioners were appointed as job trainees for various posts such as waiters, kitchen trainees and counter incharge.
The training period was two years with a stipulation in their appointment letter that the corporation would not be obligated to offer them employment upon completion, but they would be considered for regular appointment if the post was available.
During the pendency of the petition, 139 waiter posts were advertised.
The petitioners not only completed their training period successfully but also worked for more than three years.
The court directed the corporation to absorb the petitioners against the advertised posts provided they are found eligible.
