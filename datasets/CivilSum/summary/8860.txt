Article 226/227 of the Constitution of India - Admission - Mechanical Engineering (PE & IM) Course - A candidate was eligible for admission only if they were a resident of Punjab State and had Punjab Domicile - Candidates claiming the benefit of domicile were required to furnish domicile certificate and an affidavit that they or their children/wards have not obtained the benefit of domicile of any state other than Punjab - Petitioner was not eligible for admission as he did not file the required affidavit before the last date - He had stated his father was serving in HP and did not attach the certificate of Punjab domicile required according to instructions - Thus, he cannot be considered for admission against the seat reserved for wards of ex-servicemen.
Article 226/227 of the Constitution of India - Admission - Result - Mechanical Engg.
(PE&IM) Course - A petitioner was not eligible for admission to the course against a seat reserved for wards of ex-servicemen, but was admitted based on a writ petition - During the petition, he completed seven out of eight semesters, appeared in the examination of the first seven semesters, even qualified in the examination held for three semesters - Court ruled that it was not just and proper to cancel his admission at this stage and directed respondents to allow petitioner to pursue his study in the course, declare his results for fourth to 7th semester, also allow him to appear in the examination of the 8th Semester - However, this case should not be considered a precedent for future cases.
