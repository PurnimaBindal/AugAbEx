According to Section 14(1)(a) of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Dhobis employed to wash cadets' clothes at NDA and paid from Regimental Fund cannot be considered as Civil Servants occupying any civil post.
Therefore, Administrative Tribunal cannot entertain an application under Section 19 of the Act on behalf of Dhobis due to the nature of their appointment and service conditions, even if authorities have some control over them.
