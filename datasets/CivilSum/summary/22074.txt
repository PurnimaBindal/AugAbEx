Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Natural justice - Dismissal from service - Enquiry - Failure to examine complainant and eyewitness of the occurrence that led to the termination of the employee's services causes immense prejudice to the employee - If crucial witnesses are not cross-examined and have not been examined even to support the allegations made by them, punishment based on those allegations constitutes a violation of natural justice principles and renders the proceedings defective.
