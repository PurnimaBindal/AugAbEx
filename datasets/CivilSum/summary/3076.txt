According to Section 19 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the petitioner is an employee of the contractor who is engaged by the company.
The contractor hires workmen on a daily basis as and when required.
The writ petition is dismissed because the High Court has no reason to hear it directly for determining questions of fact related to an industrial dispute, as the workman has other remedies available to him under Industrial Laws.
