Jammu & Kashmir Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 31(1)(a) - Misconduct - Suspension - Chargesheet - According to the rules, employees charged with unlawful sale of Evacuee Property may be suspended or transferred pending or during an enquiry against them - Government instructions cannot supersede the rules - The decision to suspend or transfer an employee lies solely with the authority - The court should not interfere in this decision or substitute its own judgment - An independent enquiry must be conducted.
[Paras 9, 10, 11 and 13]
