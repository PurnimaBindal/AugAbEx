Constitution of India, Article 226 - Kerala Service Rules, Rule 60 of Part 1 - Compassionate Appointment - Despite being denied appointment under the dying in harness scheme, the Petitioner is eligible for consideration due to Rule 60's provision that a last grade employee's retirement age can be 55 or 60.
While the employee in question had passed away after reaching the age of superannuation, it is held that the Petitioner should be considered for appointment on the basis of merit alone.
Even following the regulations, the Petitioner is deemed eligible.
