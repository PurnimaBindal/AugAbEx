According to the J & K Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 31(1), a person may be suspended for up to three months while an investigation is conducted.
In the present case, the petitioner is seeking to have their suspension order quashed, arguing that the investigation prior to the registration of an FIR was conducted by vigilance, and that a recommendation was made to close the enquiry as not proved.
However, the court held that the suspension order was not passed malafide and that the purpose of suspension is to facilitate the enquiry.
Additionally, none of the petitioners have been exonerated in the departmental proceedings for which challans have been filed.
Thus, the petition was dismissed.
