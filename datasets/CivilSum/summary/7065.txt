Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 2(j) - Delay and laches - Existence of industrial dispute - Industrial dispute raised after 10 years of termination - To be deemed to be existing or subsisting, satisfactory explanation as to delay and subsistence of the dispute is necessary from the workman - The Govt. must take into consideration of this aspect before making a reference, even if there is no limitation prescribed for.
