Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Pension and Gratuity - The petitioner's retiral benefits were delayed, so he approached the court for justice.
The court agreed to list the petition within six months, but the counsel for the petitioner did not remind the court.
Without any sufficient reason, the case remained pending for a decade, causing the petitioner to suffer.
As a result, he should be compensated with heavy costs and 15% interest on the delayed amount for the period of delay.
