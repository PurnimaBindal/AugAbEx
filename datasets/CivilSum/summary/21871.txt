According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, double jeopardy is not applicable if an earlier proposal of minor punishment was not approved, and a subsequent order of punishment passed in accordance with the law.
[Para 10]
Article 14 of the Constitution of India mandates the proper punishment of termination of services for a charge of embezzlement proved in a disciplinary inquiry.
[Para 10]
If the order of termination is not challenged in the writ petition and only the punishment is alleged to be unreasonable, termination cannot be quashed under Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 10]
Acquittal in criminal proceedings cannot be a ground for setting aside the termination as a matter of course under Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
A conclusion different from that arrived at by a criminal court may be arrived at in a disciplinary inquiry, as the burden of proof required to establish guilt in a criminal court is not required in disciplinary proceedings.
