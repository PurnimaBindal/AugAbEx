Constitution of India, Article 39 (d) - Posts equality- Pay scales - It is the job of specialised agencies and Executive Authorities to determine wages and parity of pay scales for the posts.
Once posts are equal and the equality exists, they must continue to be on the same pay scale.

Constitution of India, Article 39 (d) - Posts equality - Pay scales - If there has been a change in the roles or responsibilities of equated posts, the equality of posts may end.
If the cessation of equality is necessary, it should be backed by sound reason.

Letters Patent Appeal, Clause 10 - Appointments - Challenge to members of Engineering Service's appointment in specialised posts in the Directorate- The challenge was dismissed since the persons who had come from Engineering Service had not been impleaded in the written petition as respondents.
Appointments of respondents made in 1981 were not challenged by the year 1990.
It would be inappropriate to disrupt these appointments.
