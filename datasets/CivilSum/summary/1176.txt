Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10, 11-A & 15 - Enquiry/Opportunity to lead evidence - Departmental enquiry - Dismissal of workman from service - Management has the right to request permission to lead evidence to support charges in addition to evidence already brought on the enquiry file - Opportunity cannot be denied even if charges were vague and there is ample evidence of unfair labour practice and victimization - Permission to lead evidence to establish charges cannot be declined taking the request as an admission of unfair and improper enquiry by the management - If the enquiry is not found proper, an opportunity should be given to the management to lead additional evidence.
[Paras 6, 7, 11 and 12]

Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 11-A - Powers and jurisdiction of the Labour Tribunals - Quantum of punishment - Proportionate punishment - Labour Court has jurisdiction to evaluate the severity of misconduct and proportion and quantum of punishment - High Court cannot interfere with findings regarding the severity of misconduct and punishment under Article 227.
[Paras 15 and 16]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10, 11-A and Schedule II, Item 6 - Probationer - Termination of services during probation - No requirement to record reasons for termination of services during probation even if the probation period was not completed satisfactorily - Termination is not invalid for want of recorded reasons.
