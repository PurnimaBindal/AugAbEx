Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 39 and 162 - Regularisation of casual, daily rated and ad hoc employees - Constitutional validity of regularisation instructions - Instructions issued by the Haryana government for regularisation must comply with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - Regularisation is necessary to address exigencies and to avoid hardship faced by employees who may become overage for regular public services - However, policy and guidelines for regularisation must be strictly adhered to and should not be unfair, discriminatory or arbitrary - Condition that an employee who remains absent from work for more than 30 days at a time is ineligible for regularisation held to be valid, but not when the break is attributable to the authority itself or to any fault of the employee - High Court powers under Article 226 can ensure that the instructions conform to the requirements of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
