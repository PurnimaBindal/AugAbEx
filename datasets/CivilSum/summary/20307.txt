Orissa Service Code, Rule 71(a) - Compulsory retirement - Adverse confidential reports - Promotion - The Government can use the entire service record of an employee to make a decision on compulsory retirement after they reach the required years of service for pension.
Adverse remarks made before promotion won't impact further promotions but can be considered for compulsory retirement.
[Para 7] B.
According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India, compulsory retirement is not a punishment, and the employee is still entitled to receive all pensionary benefits.
