Constitution of India, Article 227 - Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Sections 15 and 17 - Delay - If an order is issued directing a time-barred application under Section 15(2) of the Act, such a directive can be contested in an appeal under Section 17 - However, the goodness of an order condoning delay by utilizing the power under the proviso of Section 15(2) cannot be challenged in an appeal against an order under Section 15(3) of the Act.
