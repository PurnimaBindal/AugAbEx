According to the Constitution of India, Article 16 and Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Section 5, a challenge to the transfer of a teacher may not be applicable if there is no provision in the appointment letter specifying transfer.
In this particular case, both schools are run by the same society and there is another school nearby.
As there is no allegation of mala fide, it cannot be claimed that the transfer would cause great inconvenience or ordeal to the petitioner.
Therefore, the transfer is considered an incident of service with no indication of malice or arbitrary power.
There was no legal restriction for transferring the petitioner, and thus the petition is dismissed.
