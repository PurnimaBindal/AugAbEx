Jammu and Kashmir Road Transport Service Conditions Rules and Regulations, 1979, Rules 148 and 152 - Discrimination - Rule 148 outlines a disciplinary process for department employees, while Rule 152 allows for summary removal of drivers and other positions.
The summary removal provision in Rule 152 is discriminatory and should be removed from the rules.
[Para 13] B Jammu and Kashmir Road Transport Service Conditions Rules and Regulations, 1979, Rule 152 - Satisfaction/Removal/Salary procedure - Rule 152 allows the Chairman of the Transport to remove drivers and other positions using a summary procedure, but only if they are satisfied.
"Satisfaction" can only be met after a charge-sheet is issued, an explanation is received, and the charges are supported by evidence.
Any other process would not satisfy the rules.
[Para 9] C Constitution of India, Article 311(i) - Competent authority - The Chairman is the competent authority outlined in the rules for removal from service.
Orders passed by an authority lower in rank than the Chairman who was not delegated the powers are not sustainable.
