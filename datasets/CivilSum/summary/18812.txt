Instructions dated 671971 in regards to Medical reimbursement by the Punjab Government are considered irrational, illogical and oppressive in Article 21 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
Any claim submitted after six months following treatment will not be accepted.
Such policies are unacceptable and require striking down.
[Para 5] B Delay in reimbursing medical bills should not be viewed as an act of charity by the State, but as an entitlement of employees under the rules.
The State should view such requests sympathetically.
