Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 15(4) - Reservation - Reservation for Scheduled Castes and Tribes - Roster points - After the required percentage of reserved category in a cadre has been filled, those who receive accelerated promotion on the basis of reservation will only be considered for future promotion alongside general category candidates based on their seniority in the feeder cadre.
Promotions are based on posts, not vacancies.
Promotions made in accordance with the earlier law, such as in RK Sabharwal's case, will not be affected, but future promotions will be governed by the principles laid out in that case and in Ajit Singh's case.
