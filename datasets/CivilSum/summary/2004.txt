Articles 226 and 300-A of the Constitution of India state that pension and other retiral benefits are statutory rights of employees and must be paid on time.
If there is no justifiable reason for delay, the retiree is entitled to interest on the delayed amount.
This means that a writ petition for pension and retiral benefits, including interest, is maintainable.
[Paras 8, 9, 16 and 17] B.
Officials who are responsible for the delay in payment of retiral benefits must be held personally responsible for compensating the loss to the State exchequer caused by their inefficiency or apathy.
