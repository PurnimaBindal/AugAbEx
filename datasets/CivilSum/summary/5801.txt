Karnataka Electricity Board Recruitment and Promotion Regulations, Chapter V, Clauses 7(d) and 10(f) - Under the up-gradation, the posts of Assistant Engineers were upgraded for graduate Engineers to provide relief under Clause 10(e).
Thus, the upgraded Available Assistant Engineer posts can only be held by graduates.
Non-graduates cannot claim any of the Assistant Engineer posts.
[Para 13]

Interpretation of Statutes - No provision in a statute is a surplusage.
The addition of Clause 7(d) serves a purpose and cannot be an otiose clause.
[Para 14]

Karnataka Electricity Board Recruitment and Promotion Regulations, Clauses 7(d) and 10(f) - Appointment - Method of appointment against any upgraded post available needs to be regulated by a specific provision made by Rules.
The appointments would be made according to the qualifications prescribed by regulations.
[Para 19]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Classification - Higher Educational qualifications - Promotion - Discrimination - Classification based on higher educational qualifications to a higher post is not unfair or discriminatory.
Both Article 14 and 16 cannot interfere with the provisions of Clause 7(d).
