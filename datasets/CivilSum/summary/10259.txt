As per Section 33 of the Haryana Co-operative Societies Act, 1984, an Administrator has the power to take any action necessary for the betterment of the society, including retrenchment of employees.
In a case where a petitioner working as a Daftari in a society was retrenched by an Administrator, it was found that the requisite amount of compensation had been paid at the time of retrenchment.
It was also noted that there was no evidence to suggest that the society had the income to bear the burden of employee salary.
Thus, the contention that the Administrator had no jurisdiction to pass the retrenchment order was dismissed, and the petition was accordingly rejected.
