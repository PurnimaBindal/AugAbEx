Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16(3) and 226 - Education/Admissions/Reservation in admissions/Domicile certificate - Reservation in admissions based on residence, district or state-wide, is permissible under Article 16(3) of the Constitution.
Administrative instructions can supplement rules if they are silent.
The petitioner failed to produce a bona fide resident certificate, which was a condition advertised for the course.
Therefore, they cannot be allowed to continue and take the examination to complete the course.
The petitioner does not deserve any relief under Article 226.
