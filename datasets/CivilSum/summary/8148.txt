Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 154 - UP Public Examinations (Prevention of unfair means) Act, 1998, Section 3 - Usage of Unfair Means - While there is no charge of committing a criminal act (such as intimidation, manhandling, violence, disturbing examination, threat, etc.), lodging of FIR isn't a requirement for an examinee who is allegedly caught and charged with using unfair means.
It can be submitted after an inquiry is conducted by the concerned authority (as may be stipulated under the applicable Act).
If deemed necessary, criminal charges may be filed after the determination of the unfair means case.
