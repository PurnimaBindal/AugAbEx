The Punjab Medical Education Service (Class-I) Rules, 1978, Rules 9(1)(d) and 9(3) mandate that only individuals with the requisite qualifications or experience for a given vacancy on the relevant date can be considered for promotion or reversion.
The petitioner, who had already acquired the necessary teaching experience after obtaining a Magister Chirugise (M.Ch.
), was appropriately appointed to the position of Professor.
In contrast, the respondent lacked the five years of teaching experience required for the promotion.
The respondent's claim that his teaching experience prior to the amendment of the rules should be taken into account is unreasonable, given that only the rules that exist when the position becomes available should be considered.
The government has been ordered to let the petitioner continue to serve as Professor, as per the August 14, 1991 appointment letter.
