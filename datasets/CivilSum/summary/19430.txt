Students seeking admission to Professional Colleges must have the required qualifications at the time of application, and eligibility is determined by the beginning of the academic year.
Applications submitted after the deadline are not acceptable, even if the applicant gains eligibility during the selection process.
[Para 4]  B If a student does not have the required qualifications at the beginning of the academic year, they are not eligible to apply for admission, regardless of any delays in the allocation of seats or start of the course.
This rule applies to all courses, not just professional ones.
