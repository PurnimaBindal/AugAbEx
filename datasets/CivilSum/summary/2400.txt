According to the Punjabi University Calendar, Volume-IV, 1995 Edition, candidates who wish to take the BDS training course must undergo the complete three years of training to be eligible to take the exam and qualify for the degree, as required by the University Calendar and Medical Council of India.
The conditions cannot be diluted by the University, institutions, or Courts when no option has been left to forgo any part of the training period by the experts.
Therefore, any candidate must meet the requirement of three years of the course, and admission granted late for any reason is no excuse to condone the shortage of training period required.
The legal position cannot be ignored by the Court, and it cannot issue a direction to the University to disobey the Medical Council's directions on compassion and sympathy.
