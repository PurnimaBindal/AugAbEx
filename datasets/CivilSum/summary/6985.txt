Under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F, 25-N and 25-K, a workman may be terminated with payment of one month's notice salary.
It was contended that three months notice was required under Section 25-N, but this plea was not raised before the labour Court and there was nothing to substantiate it.
Therefore, it was held that the plea of violation of Section 25-N could not be entertained.
The retrenchment of the workman after compliance of Section 25-F was in accordance with the law.
