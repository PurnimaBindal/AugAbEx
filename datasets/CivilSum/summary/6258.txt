Constitution of India, Article 226 - Central Government Health Scheme - Petitioner denied full medical reimbursement - Even though hospital recognized under CGHS, petitioner underwent By-pass Grafting - Court held that petitioner entitled to full reimbursement and not just rates specified in circular - Respondent responsible for settling matter if hospital charged higher rate - Respondent fined 20,000 for not following earlier court decisions.
