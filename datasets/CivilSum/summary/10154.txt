The Scheme for Appointment of Dependants of Deceased Employees on Compassionate Grounds in Regional Rural Bank, Clause X, denies a compassionate appointment on the grounds that the mother of the petitioner is in Government service, which stands as a barrier.
However, in the given circumstances, it cannot be concluded that the discretion was exercised arbitrarily and capriciously or that the petitioner's right has been violated.
[Paras 13,14,15]
