Kerala Agricultural University Act, 1971, Section 21 - Notification No. GA/18128/AS/82 dtd.
6.7.1984, Para 5, Clause (6) - Promotion - Promotion to the rank of Professor - Criteria for eligibility - An Associate Professor must complete 22 years of active service to be eligible for promotion - Maximum leave period of 3 years for obtaining PhD is included in qualifying service calculations - Petitioner taking 5-year leave for PhD is not entitled to include the excess 2 years for qualifying service - The denial of promotion to the petitioner is just.
