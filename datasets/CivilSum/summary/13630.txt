According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, equal pay should be provided for equal work.
However, daily wage workers are not in a similar position as regular employees.
If an employee performs the same duties as their regular counterparts, they can claim parity in pay.
However, there is no proof on record to establish that the petitioner was performing the same duties as regular employees.
Therefore, they cannot be entitled to the same salary as regular employees.
This is referenced in 1986(1) SCC 637 and 1986(1) SCC 639.
