Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Criminal proceedings - Acquittal in criminal charges - Being acquitted of a criminal charge does not necessarily absolve a delinquent employee of the same charge in departmental proceedings, due to differences in the standards of proof required for each proceeding - Criminal charges require proof beyond reasonable doubt while departmental proceedings require proof by preponderance of probabilities.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Natural justice - Waiver of opportunity - Employers must provide a reasonable opportunity for a delinquent to participate in proceedings - If the delinquent chooses not to take advantage of this opportunity for a known reason, they will be considered to have waived their right and cannot subsequently claim violations of natural justice.
