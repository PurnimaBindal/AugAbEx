Constitution of India, Article 16 – Food Corporation of India Act, 1964, Section 46 – Food Corporation of India Regulations, Regulation 15-10 – Inter-se-seniority/ Seniority/Instructions – The Government issued a circular of instructions detailing the principles for promotion and seniority fixation for both direct recruits and Food Transferees, including those promoted on an ad hoc basis to determine their seniority on the promotion post.
The circular acknowledges the need to review and determine the seniority of `Food Transferees' and direct recruits according to the principles laid out.
The whole seniority does not need to be quashed and redetermined.
