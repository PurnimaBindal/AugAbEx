Constitution of India, Article 311 - State Bank of India Act, 1955, Section 49 - State Bank of India Officers Rules, Rules 19 and 20 - Voluntary retirement/Disciplinary proceedings - The authority in charge is able to reject a voluntary retirement application from a delinquent officer seeking voluntary retirement if there are pending disciplinary proceedings regarding irregularities involving crores of rupees against them.
[Paras 5 and 6]
Constitution of India, Article 311 - State Bank of India Act, 1955, Section 49 - State Bank of India Officers Service Rules, Rule 20 - Show cause notice - Disciplinary proceedings - When disciplinary proceedings are initiated, the request for voluntary retirement can be declined even if it has not yet been completed.
The main point is the tenor of the notice and not the form.
[Paras 19 to 21]
