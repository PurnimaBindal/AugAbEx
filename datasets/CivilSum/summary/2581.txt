"The Land Acquisition Act of 1894, as referenced in Sections 18 and 26, outlines that any award granted by a court should be considered a decree and grounds for the award declared a judgment as defined in Clause (2) and (9) of the Civil Procedure Code.
The award given by the Ld.
DJ cannot be considered an award, therefore it does not qualify for the benefits under this Act.
The appellant's appeal is dismissed.
