According to Pension Regulations for the Air Force, 1961, (Part-I), Regulation 153 and Regulations 71. and 76, personnel who are invalided out of service and not discharged at their own request due to a disability of 20% or more attributable to or aggravated by Air Force Service, are eligible for the grant of a disability element.
The disability element is constituted of two things, i.e. service element and disability element.
However, if a person seeks discharge on his own request, he will not be entitled to the benefit of service element and disability element which are two parts of disability pension.
In the case presented, the petitioner was discharged on compassionate ground after 30 years of service following his request to do so.
As a result, he was not declared entitled to any pension.
However, according to Regulation 76, the disability element is independent from Regulation 71, so the petitioner is entitled to the disability element alone.
No cogent evidence on record suggests that the disease suffered by the petitioner is not attributable to Air Force Services.
Therefore, the authority is directed to release the arrears of disability element pension with 12% interest.
