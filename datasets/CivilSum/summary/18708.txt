According to Rule 7(2) of the Punjab Prisons Service Rules (Class-II) of 1963 and Para 251 of the Punjab Jail Manual, promotion to the post of Senior Assistant Superintendent is based on seniority-cum-merit.
However, it is stated that seniority alone is not the only criterion for promotion.
The appointment to the post, despite being referred to as promotion, is in fact made through selection, which is the reason why the word "selection" is used.
