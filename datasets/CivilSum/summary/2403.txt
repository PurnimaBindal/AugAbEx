The Bar Council of India Rules were amended in 2000 to improve legal education, including the addition of "Even Classes."
However, an amendment to Rule 2(1) of Section B in Part-IV of the Rules was made without any consultation with the Universities and State Bar Councils.
It is required that these parties be consulted for any amendment promoting legal education and laying down standards.
This lack of consultation renders the amendment violative of Section 7(1)(h) of the Advocate Act, 1961.
