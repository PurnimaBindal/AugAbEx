Vijay Galotra, Advocate argues that Article 311(2) of the Constitution of India must be applied if the termination order during probation is based on the guilt of the probationer and a departmental inquiry must be held before holding him guilty of misconduct or loss.
However, if the probationer is found unsuitable to be retained in service based on an overall assessment of work and conduct during probation, he can be discharged with a simple order.
The order of termination during probation is still subject to judicial review and the court can examine if the probationer was found guilty.
The court rejected the argument that the court cannot review an innocuous order and found that the finding of guilt is indeed punitive in nature.
This decision is in line with previous rulings by seven constitution benches and several three judges benches.
Therefore, the appeal is dismissed.
