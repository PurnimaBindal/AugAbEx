Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Departmental proceedings - Bias - If the delinquent initially objected to an outsider as enquiry officer but was left with no choice but to act himself as the inquiry officer to punish, the delinquent cannot claim bias and act as the prosecutor and judge by the punishing authority.
[Paras 12, 14 and 15]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural justice - The principle of natural justice is based on two pillars i) no one shall be judged unfairly and ii) no one shall be judge in his own cause but the principles of natural justice can be excluded by a statute.
[Para 12]  C Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Bias - The order of punishment passed by Lok Ayukta, a former Judge of the Supreme Court, cannot be attributed with bias or to be passed based on no material.
[Para 13]  D Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Judicial review - Quantum of punishment - Once the employee is found rightly guilty of serious misconduct, the writ court cannot go into the quantum of punishment chosen by the punishing authority unless it is found queasy to one's conscience.
