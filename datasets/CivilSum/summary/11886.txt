The Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 and the Bonus Act, 1965, Section 6(c) were relevant to the situation in which the company's balance sheet allegedly showed less profit to avoid providing workers with bonuses.
The Industrial Tribunal ordered the payment of a bonus, including an additional amount claimed as statutory taxes of 368 lacs.
However, no record or account books were produced to the Tribunal to support this claim.
Despite this, the Tribunal's findings were accepted by a single judge based on both oral and documentary evidence.
The L.P.A. bench decided not to take a different view, and consequently dismissed the appeal.
