UP Jal Nigam Engineers (Public Health Branch) Services Regulations, 1978, Rules 5, 10 and 18 - Selection for promotion - Promotions - Merit and integrity - Consideration for promotion - It is well-established that merit and integrity are the only factors taken into account when selecting individuals for promotion.
The idea is to ensure that people who lack these qualities are not given the advantage.
