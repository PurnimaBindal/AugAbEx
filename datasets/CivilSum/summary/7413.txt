The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(s), 25F highlight that contract workers, including piece-rate workers, are considered workmen for all practical purposes and must be treated as employees of the Board.
The Meter Reader at the Electricity Board was appointed and works under the control of the Board's officers as per its rules and instructions and falls under the category of workmen.
Termination of such employment in contrary to Section 25-F is not acceptable, and reinstatement with 25% back wages is justifiable based on the nature of engagement and wages.
