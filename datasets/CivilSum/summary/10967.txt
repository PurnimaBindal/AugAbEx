Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Privately Managed Aided Schools (Security of Services) Rules 1981, Rule 8(1)(d) - Termination during probation - Probationer must be given a fresh order without any stigmatic words after withdrawing the earlier stigmatic order - Reasons for termination recorded in the record must not be incorporated in the termination order if they may affect the probationer's future employment prospects - Mere possibility of the competent authority seeing the earlier stigmatic order is not sufficient grounds for quashing an order of termination during probation.
