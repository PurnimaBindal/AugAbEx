Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 22 - Evidence Act, Section 40 - Contested issues alleged in the charge sheet and refuted by the petitioner must be resolved in disciplinary proceedings under C.C.A.
Rule 1958, with reasonable opportunity given to the petitioner - This Court cannot address such issues under Art. 226 of the Constitution, when the impugned suspension order has already been served on petitioner, unless it does not cause bias to the parties in disciplinary proceedings.
