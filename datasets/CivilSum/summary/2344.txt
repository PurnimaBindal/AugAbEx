Constitution of India, Articles 21, 47 and 226 - Public interest litigation - Prohibition of tobacco products - The court cannot force the Legislature or Executive to initiate legislation, even for matters of public interest, as that is not its role.
The Union of India alone has the power to make policy decisions in this matter.
[Paras 27 and 35]
Constitution of India, Articles 21, 47 and 226 - Jurisdiction of Court - Matter seized by Parliament - If a bill is pending before Parliament on a subject matter, it is not within the court's mandate to deal with the matter under the guise of redressing public grievances.
Parliament, as the voice of democracy, should be allowed to make decisions on behalf of the public in accordance with established Parliamentary procedure.
[Para 31]
Constitution of India, Articles 21, 47 and 226 - Jurisdiction of Court - The court can only interfere with a policy decision made by the State government if the decision is patently arbitrary, discriminatory or malafide.
The court cannot interfere on the basis of preferring a different policy decision.
[Paras 33 and 35]
