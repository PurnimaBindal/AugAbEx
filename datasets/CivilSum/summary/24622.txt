Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administration Action - An administrative order that results in Civil consequences must adhere to the principle stated in the Latin maxim Audi Alteram Partem - The decision maker must provide a chance.
[Paras 11 and 22] B Quasi judicial functions - Administrative functions are considered quasi-judicial when there is a duty to use a judicial approach and follow the essential principles of justice - Without such an obligation, the decision is called 'purely administrative, ' and there is no third category.
