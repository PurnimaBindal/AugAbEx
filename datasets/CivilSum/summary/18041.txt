Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 227 - Appointment - Selection - Professional qualifications - The petitioner, who holds a diploma in Medical Laboratory Technology from Travancore Institute of Medical Technology, was initially denied consideration due to the institute's derecognition by the Director Health Services, but subsequent recognition was granted and the recognition letter was produced - The petitioner's diploma from an unrecognized institution was the sole reason for non-consideration - The court has directed the consideration of his claim against a current vacancy.
