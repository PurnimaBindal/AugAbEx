Punjab Economic and Statistical Organisation (State Service Class-I) Rules, 1962, Rule 7(2) mandates promotion on basis of merit cum seniority for Departmental promotion to the post of Joint Director in Economic and Statistical Organisation, Punjab.
The D.P.C.
considered all eligible candidates' cases on the basis of merit, where none of the juniors had exceptional merit, and seniority should prevail when all the candidates are equal in merit, with the junior only if extra meritorious.
Promotion was upheld.

In the absence of any rule to the contrary, every D.P.C.
has the freedom to adopt its own criteria for making selection and is not bound to follow the same criteria as used by earlier D.P.Cs.
