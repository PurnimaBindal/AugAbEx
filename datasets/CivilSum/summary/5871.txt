According to Sections 2 and 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, if a workman's services are terminated in accordance with the terms of the fixed term appointment or specific work order, Section 25-F would not be applicable.
The management must prove that the appointment was for a specific period and specific work, and if there was no written order, it may be presumed that there was no contract for a specified period and that services have not been terminated at its expiration.
