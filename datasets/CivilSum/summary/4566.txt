Selection of candidates under the Constitution of India is governed by Articles 14 and 226.
The selection committee responsible for assessing the petitioner's merit included respected individuals like a retired Chief Justice of the High Court, Vice Chancellor of a University, and persons in reputable positions.
Their selection was made based on merit after interviewing both the petitioner and private respondents, and thus stands without fault.
Direct proof of mala fide is required to support allegations to the contrary.
[Para 6] B.
Even if a retired head of the department of a university serves on the selection committee, selection is not necessarily compromised.
It cannot be assumed that one member would hold undue influence over the entire committee.
As such, the petition is dismissed.
