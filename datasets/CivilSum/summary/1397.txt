Kerala State and Supporting Services Rules, 1959, Rules 14 and 17 - Backward classes - Reservation - Changing from forward class to backward class - A person cannot marry or adopt to change their class from forward to backward class to claim reservation benefits.
A Syrian Catholic girl who marries a Latin Catholic boy does not become a Latin Catholic and therefore cannot claim reservation benefits.
The cases of 1980 KLT 18 and 1981 KLT 24 were overruled, while 1992(1) KLT 436 was approved.
