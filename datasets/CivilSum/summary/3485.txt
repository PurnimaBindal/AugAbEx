Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Schedule 2, Item 3 - Departmental enquiry - Scope of opportunity for employer to provide additional evidence - If a finding about the fairness of the departmental enquiry favors the employer, employee does not require an opportunity to present additional evidence.
