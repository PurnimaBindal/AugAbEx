Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule II, Item 6 - Termination - Fresh appointment - Benefit of regular service - The petitioner was terminated after completing a project despite all other employees being absorbed and regularized - The High Court determined that the petitioner had a legitimate expectation that the project had become a permanent unit of respondent and ordered absorption in a similar department - The petitioner is entitled to receive retiral benefits after attaining superannuation.
