Andhra Pradesh Gram Panchayats Act, Section 17 - Employee/Part-time employee - Petitioner, a seasonal employee of a Cooperative Society, won the election of Panchayat despite the Act barring employees of Cooperative Societies from contesting elections - At issue is whether temporary or part-time or seasonal employees of Cooperative Societies are generally barred from contesting elections - The correct decision was to set aside the petitioner's election.
