The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, along with the Army Pension Regulations 1971, Part-I, Regulations 173 and 423, state that the attributability of Schizophrenic Reaction (300) to service for disability pension depends on the opinion of a Medical Board.
If the Medical Board declares that Paranoid Schizophrenic is a constitutional disease not attributable to or aggravated due to military service, that opinion is final.
Therefore, if the Medical Board opines that the disease or disability is not related to the military service, then the court cannot direct the authorities to release disability pension.
