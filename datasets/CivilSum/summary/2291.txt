The Court held that even though the petitioner was subjected to court martial proceedings under the Army Act, since they were a juvenile and there is no exception in the Juvenile Justice (Care & Protection of Children) Act of 2000 for its non-applicability to the Army Act, the proceedings of the General Court Martial (GCM) were quashed.
While the liberty was given to proceed with charges that were not related to the petitioner being a juvenile.
