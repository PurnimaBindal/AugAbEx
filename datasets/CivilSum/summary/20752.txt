Indian Administrative Service (Appointment by Selection), Second Amendment Regulations 1989, Regulation 2 - Promotion to IAS - Previously non-state Civil Service Class-I Officers with 8 years of service were eligible for IAS selection, but an amendment after 33 years increased the required period to 12 years without a clear reason - This change was struck down as unconstitutional under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India - The original rules were reinstated, but any appointments made under the amended regulation will still be recognized.
