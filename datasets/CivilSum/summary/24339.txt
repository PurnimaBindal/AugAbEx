As per Section 2(oo) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, if termination is found to be illegal retrenchment, implementation of the award can be stayed during the pendency of a writ petition and appeal, on the condition that Rs. 200/- per month is paid.
The remaining entitlement can be referred to the Labour Court for determination, after deducting any amount earned.
