Constitution of India, Article 16 - Orissa Govt. Press Industrial Employees, Classification, Recruitment, Promotion, Condition of Services and Appeal Rules, 1978, Rules 9, 9(A), 10 and 11 - Selection - Appointment to the post of Copy Holders in Orissa Government Printing Press - The waiting list of selected candidates was valid for one year w.e.f 13.7.1993 and the Central Administrative Tribunal directed the Government to fill up the vacant posts by appointing candidates from the selection list prepared on 13.7.1993.
Supreme Court held that the mere empanelment of one's name in the selection list does not give him a right to be appointed, and whether to fill up a post or not is a policy decision that is not open to the Tribunal to interfere with unless it is shown to be arbitrary.
The selection list expired on 12.7.1994, and the Government was justified in not making any further appointment from the said list after this date.
[Paras 4, 7, 9, and 11]
