Rajasthan Civil Services (Service Matters Appellate Tribunals) Act (34 of 1976), Sections 2(f) - Service matter - Civil Court's jurisdiction barred - Termination of services on 2561976, before commencement of the 1976 Act on 1.7.76 - Plaintiff filed suit after the 1976 Act had come into force - Remedy available to the incumbent must be considered on the date when the action was initiated - Bar under Section 10 applies - Termination order prior to commencement of the 1976 Act was not legally sustainable.

Constitution of India, Articles, 311, 309 and 14 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Service Matters Appellate Tribunals) Act, (34 of 1976), Section 2(f) - Rajasthan Civil Services Rules, 1974, Rule 36 - Termination of Services - Services of a Police Constable were terminated for unsatisfactory performance/work during period of probation - Impugned order of termination imputes upon his character, and conduct, which undisputedly is stigmatic and punitive in nature, had been passed without holding disciplinary inquiry under service rules - Violation of principles of Natural justice - Delinquent, a Police Constable who had already crossed 50 years of age, was entitled to be reinstated - The arrears of salary paid as a consequence of impugned decree shall be considered total sum of full and final compensation in lieu of reinstatement.
