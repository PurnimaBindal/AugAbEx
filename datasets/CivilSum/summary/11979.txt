Petitioners who were initially appointed on a temporary basis on a project claimed seniority from their initial date of appointment.
They were absorbed after the completion of the project by the state government as per an order dated 1641985.
The court held that since they were absorbed as per the state government's order, they would receive benefits in accordance with that order.
Additionally, based on the judgment of the Apex court in Direct Recruits and Keshav Chandra's case, if an initial appointment is ad hoc or as a stop-gap arrangement, officiating on a post cannot be taken into account for determining seniority, which must instead be counted from the date of substantive appointment.
