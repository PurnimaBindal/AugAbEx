Arbitration Act, 1940, Section 30 - Misconduct of Arbitrator - As misconduct has not been defined in the Act, whether or not an arbitrator has engaged in misconduct is a question of fact - The Civil Court cannot serve as an appeals court over the arbitrator's decision - However, if the arbitrator's misconduct has seriously harmed one party's cause, the arbitrator has engaged in misconduct - If the arbitrator takes a document behind a party's back and relies on it to make an award, it is a betrayal of confidence and harms that party - In this case, the arbitrator relied on a report from the Electricity Department behind the consumer's back, and as a result, the consumer was prejudiced - The award cannot stand.
