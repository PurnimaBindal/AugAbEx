Advocates Act, 1961, Section 35(3)(c) - Bar Council of India Rules, 1961, Rule 47 - Advocate's professional misconduct - Dealership in petroleum products - Allotment of petrol pump - Working as sleeping partner - Cancellation of licence on account of getting dealership - Not enough to establish "doing a business inconsistent with the dignity of the profession" - If the advocate is a business partner who proves to the Bar Council that they were only a sleeping partner, no misconduct is committed.
