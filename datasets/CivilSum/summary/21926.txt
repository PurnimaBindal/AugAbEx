Uttar Pradesh Absorptions of Retrenched Employees of Government/Corporations in Government Service Rules, 1991, Rules 2(b) and 2(c) - Whether or not the Uttar Pradesh Absorptions of Retrenched Employees of Government/Corporations in Government Service Rules, 1991 is applicable to the petitioners-appellants - ruled not applicable due to the public corporation not being established or constituted by or under any Uttar Pradesh Act; also, employees cannot question the closure even after receiving compensation.

Constitution of India, Article 12 - Societies Registration Act, 1860 - IERT not an instrumentality of the State - ruled that institutes registered under Societies Act may not be considered an instrumentality of the State if the State Government did not have deep and pervasive control over affairs; fundamental requirement is constitution by or under any Uttar Pradesh Act, which is not the case for Societies Act.
