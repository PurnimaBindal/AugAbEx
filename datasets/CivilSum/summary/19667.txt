Constitution of India, Article 226 - Haryana Housing Board Act, 1971, Section 72-A - Alternative remedy - Natural Justice - Cancellation of Registration - An Army Officer applied for a house in a reserved category - His application was registered and deposit was made - However, the registration was cancelled without any reason or giving any opportunity of being heard - The order violates principles of natural justice - The cancellation cannot be dismissed on the ground of an alternative remedy - Petitioner had the right to be considered for allotment in the order of merit - Cancellation order is arbitrary and contrary to the principles of natural justice - The Board has been directed to allot a house to the petitioner within three months at the same price as that of other applicants.
AIR 1985 SC 86 followed.
