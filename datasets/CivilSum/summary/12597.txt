The Punjab Tehsildars (Class II) Rules, 1984, Rule 13, stipulate that the maximum probation period is three years.
Upon completion of the maximum probation period, the petitioner is deemed to be confirmed.
[Para 8] B According to the Punjab Tehsildars' (Class II) Service Rules, 1984, Rule 9(5)(a) (unamended), nominees for appointment to PCS (Executive Branch) require ten years of continuous service under the Govt.
The Rules do not specify that this service has to be in the Revenue Department as Tehsildar or Naib Tehsildar.
Thus, the services of the petitioner in the Food & Supplies Dept.
should be considered to make the petitioner eligible for appointment to PCS (Executive Branch).
[Para 9] C The appointment of Register A-I to PCS (Executive Branch) is not a direct recruitment; it is a sort of promotion by nomination.
