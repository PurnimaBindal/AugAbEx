Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Rule 27(cc); Constitution of India Articles, 226, 16 - Removal from service - The recruitment Board committed large scale irregularities and the competent authority has power to impose penalty under Rule 27(cc) without conducting an enquiry - It was not reasonably practicable to hold enquiry due to posting of 107 candidates across different parts of the country - Entire selection is in violation of Article 16 - Removal order does not carry stigma and principles of natural justice are not applicable - No interference warranted - Writ petitions dismissed.
