Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act 1946, Section 10A(1)(b) - Subsistence Allowance - Appellants received a reduced subsistence allowance due to inquiry delay caused by them - They had obtained a stay of proceedings in domestic enquiry during a criminal trial - Despite seeking 100% allowance under Model Standing Order, it was held that delay cannot be solely attributable to a workman who is protecting himself from potential prejudice - The Appellants are entitled to 75% subsistence allowance as Model Standing Orders do not fall within the definition of `other law' as stated in section 10A(3).
