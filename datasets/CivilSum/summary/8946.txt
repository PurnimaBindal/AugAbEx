State Bank of India (Supervising Staff) Service Rules, Rule 50 - Natural justice - Report of Vigilance - Despite being purely advisory, the approval of the Vigilance Commission did not violate principles of natural justice when it was never considered before passing the punishment order and no prejudice was caused to the delinquent official.
A collective body of the Bank's Board cannot be influenced by one line of approval from the Vigilance Commission.
[Paras 8 to 10] B.
State Bank of India (Supervising Staff) Service Rules, Rule 50 - Speaking order - Termination - The Disciplinary Authority provided elaborate reasoning in its Show cause notice, and the Board applied its mind twice when passing the termination order.
A non-speaking order expressing satisfaction on the reasons already recorded is not grounds for setting aside the final order of termination.
[Paras 12 to 14] C. Punishment/Disciplinary proceedings - Delinquent given a full opportunity for a personal hearing - The disciplinary authority acted with fairness and prudence in the application of mind.
If the punishment order is supported on any findings of substantial misconduct, it is not for the Court to consider which ground should have weighed with the authority.
