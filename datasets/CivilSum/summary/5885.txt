In F.M.A.T No. 3541/90, the West Bengal Service Rules (Part I) 1971, Rule 9(5) regarding correction of date of birth after entry in service is discussed.
The correction can be made on the basis of a Matriculation Certificate that was not produced at the relevant time and the employee's oral declaration at the time of entry in service.
The employee is entitled to seek correction based on material that was not brought to the notice of the appointing authority due to a genuine mistake.
Additionally, the right to work and the right to be in service are considered part of the right to livelihood, and fall under the scope of Article 21 of the Constitution of India.
