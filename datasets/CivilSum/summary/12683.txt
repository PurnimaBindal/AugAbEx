The claimant working with Delhi Development Authority who underwent a laproscopic surgery using staplers for right umbilical hernia in a hospital duly empanelled by DDA is entitled to be reimbursed for the cost of consumables, including the cost of staplers as the decision to use staplers in laproscopic surgery cannot be evaluated by DDA.
[Para 8] B The Medical Scheme that permits treatment at any empanelled hospital and CGHS Govt. approved Hospital does not justify confining the reimbursement rates to any one particular hospital under Constitution of India, Article 16 - Medical reimbursement.
