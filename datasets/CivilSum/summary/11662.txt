Under the Uttar Pradesh Public Services (Reservation for Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and other Backward Classes) Act, 1994, Rule 3(4) for Recruitment Eligibility, the State Engineering Services (Special Recruitment) Examination is conducted for the recruitment of SC and ST candidates.
In this process, it is reasonable to fix a minimum aggregate of 40% marks as the standard for evaluating candidate suitability.
It was established that the petitioner did not obtain the required minimum marks and was subsequently not called for an interview, therefore, there is no justification for intervention.
