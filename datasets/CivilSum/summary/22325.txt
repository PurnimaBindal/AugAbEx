Section 13 of the Medical Council Act, 1956, amended by Act No. 34 of 2001, discusses the registration of foreign medical degree holders and recognition of degrees acquired from non-recognized or resolved institutions.
The revised provision includes a screening test for the situation and the issuance of an eligibility certificate by the Council.
The guidelines are approved by the MCI, Govt., and the Supreme Court subject to the same being a one-time measure, with future cases governed by the revised Regulations of the Medical Council approved by the Govt. [Paras 4 to 7]
