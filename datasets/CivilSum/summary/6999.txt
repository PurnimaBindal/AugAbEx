The term "good and sufficient reasons", used in Rule 5 of Punjab State Electricity Board Employees (Punishment and Appeal) Regulations, 1971, is of broad extent and requires reasons and application of mind to be disclosed explicitly in the order by using the word "considered" - Recovery of money and withholding of increment for minor punishments need "good and sufficient reasons" as per Regulation 5(ii)&(iii) - Natural justice must be followed.
[Paras 6 & 7] B.
In case of contested misconduct charges, an inquiry must be conducted by adhering to the procedures set forth in Regulations 8(3) to 8(24), even if the charge sheet is under the rule specifying only minor penalties - The delinquent must be given a proper charge-sheet to allow him to show cause and contest if necessary - The response must be objectively examined.
