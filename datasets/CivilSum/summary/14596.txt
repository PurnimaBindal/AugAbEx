According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, promotion and supersession eligibility must be followed.
The promotion from a Class IV to Class III post depends on the Rules provided.
The Rules specify eligibility for the Class III post, and the petitioner is not eligible for the post.
Thus, the promotion of a person eligible as per rules cannot be challenged based on seniority.
