Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Education - Result - Petitioner denied admission in reserved category for Diploma in Education course - Court provided provisional admission during pending petition - Course completed and exam held - Result of other candidates declared, but petitioner's result held due to pending petition - Regularizing petitioner's admission will not cost anyone and failing to do so wastes state funds and petitioner's time - Respondents ordered to declare result and issue certificate if petitioner passed.
