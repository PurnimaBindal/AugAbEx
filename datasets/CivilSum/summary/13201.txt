Constitution of India, 1950 Article 226 - Validity of dismissal order - Petitioner received charge sheet for misconduct, explanation found unsatisfactory, and was held guilty and subsequently dismissed from service - Petitioner seeks quashing of orders citing false departmental enquiry and violation of principles of natural justice as documents were not furnished - Respondents argue charges proven and documents provided - Enquiry officer and disciplinary authority passed speaking orders based on evidence and facts - Court will not reappreciate evidence and petition is dismissed.
