Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Section 28, Schedule IV, Items 5 and 9 - Alternative relief instead of reinstatement - The Industrial court, after examining the evidence presented by both parties, found that it was not fair and just to reinstate the appellants.
The court directed the respondent, the Mill, to calculate and pay 40 days' salary for each completed year towards the voluntary retirement scheme, compensation and gratuity.
The payment included 25% backwages applicable as per law.
The orders of the Industrial Court, affirmed by the High Court, were not a fit case for interference.
