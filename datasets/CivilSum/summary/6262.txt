Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Civil Service (Punishment & Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rules 8 & 9 - Departmental proceedings - Evidence - Procedure of - Bus conductor who collected fare without issuing tickets - Procedure of checking of cash and examination of passengers - Report of checking staff - Evidence Act not strictly applicable to departmental proceedings - Enquiry officer can use report of checking staff without questioning passengers - All logically probative materials can be considered, including hearsay evidence with reasonable nexus and credibility - Sufficiency of evidence is not within scope - Court can only consider absence of evidence.
