According to Section 21 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, the place of proceeding should be determined in case of a claim petition filed by the mother of a deceased worker.
In this case, the claimant is an illiterate poor widow who chose to file the claim at the place of her residence.
Because of this, the Commissioner had jurisdiction to hear the claim petition, and compensation has been ordered to be released.
