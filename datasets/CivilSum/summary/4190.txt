Panjab University Calendar Volume-I, Chapter V(A), Regulations 1.1., 4, 5 and 6 - U.G.C.
Merit Promotion Scheme - Promotions under the UGC Merit Promotion Scheme to the post of Professor are not considered appointments to the Professors' cadre.
Posts in the cadre of Professors must be advertised, and direct appointments made through selection from the open market.
Recipients of promotions under the Scheme form a distinct class, and their appointments are intended to promote academic excellence and faculty discipline.
Promotions under the Scheme are personal and do not confer any right to seniority or promotion to higher posts.
Promotions of promotees as Chairman/Head by rotation are against the Rules, and only direct recruits to the cadre of Professors are eligible for such promotions.
Panjab University Calendar Vol-III, 1990, Chapter LIV, Rule 2.1 [Paras 10 to 14]

Panjab University Calendar Vol.-III, 1990, Chapter LIV, Rule 2.1 - Seniority - Promotion - Seniority of Professors directly recruited under the Rules and those promoted under UGC Merit Promotion Scheme - Seniority of teachers in a Department is to be determined only amongst the members of the cadre - Merit promotees under the UGC Merit Promotion Scheme are not members of service in the cadre of Professors and are not entitled to seniority in the cadre or for promotion to the post of Chairman/Head of the Department.
