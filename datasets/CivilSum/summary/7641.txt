Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Appointment - Concealment of material facts - Termination - Order of termination justifiably passed due to petitioner concealing material information regarding involvement in a criminal case - Acquittal from the criminal case does not excuse the delinquent's lack of honesty and integrity in not disclosing the information - Employer's decision to terminate upheld.
