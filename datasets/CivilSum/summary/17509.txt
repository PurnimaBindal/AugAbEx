Constitution of India, Article 311 - Natural justice - Misconduct - Punishment - A conductor was punished with a stoppage of increment on the charge of not issuing tickets to some passengers before the bus moved, but it was only a technical defect.
Witnesses, including a Municipal Commissioner and a Lecturer, complained against the behaviour of the checking staff and stated that the conductor was issuing tickets before being charged.
Despite this evidence, the punishing authority only relied on a statistical return and ignored the attending circumstances.
The punishment order was quashed since the findings of guilt were clearly irrational and there was no evidence to support them.
