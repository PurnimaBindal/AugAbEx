Constitution of India, Article 16 - Ad hoc appointment - Regularisation - The petitioners were appointed as ad hoc employees from a panel sponsored by the Employment Exchange, and although they rendered regular work, they were given intermittently breaks in service to retain their temporary status - They are entitled to substantive appointment and seniority from the date of their initial appointment - The court determined that the petitioners were essentially appointed for continuous work and the breaks in service were only introduced to distinguish them from those given substantive appointments later on - Therefore, they should be regularised through substantive appointment and treated on par with those appointed on a regular basis.
