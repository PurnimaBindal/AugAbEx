The required qualifications for direct recruitment were a driving licence and knowledge of Punjabi language up to the middle standard.
The appellants were initially employed as cleaners and promoted to drivers in the Poultry Department of Punjab.
However, they were eventually retrenched due to being deemed surplus.
The appellants applied for direct recruitment but were not selected because they lacked the necessary educational qualifications.
The rule regarding educational qualifications for direct recruitment cannot be adjusted, and therefore, the appellants cannot be considered for recruitment due to their failure to meet the criteria.
The appeal is dismissed.
