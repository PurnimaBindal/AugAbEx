Constitution of India, Article 226 - Government Residences (Chandigarh Administration General Pool) Allotment Rules, 1996, Rule 2(h) - Allotment of houses - Declaring the Chairman and members of Commissions as ineligible employees from category of "Eligible Employees" through an amendment issued vide notification dated 2871998 is ultra vires the Constitution and has been invalidated.
