Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Agricultural Services (Class II) Rules, 1974, Rule 8(b) & Rule 9(ii) - Post of Assistant Geologist - The promotion to a higher category requires a minimum of five years of experience in that category, as per Rule 8(b).
Furthermore, promotions should be made within the respective categories of service as per Rule 9(ii).
Although the petitioners were promoted in the Geological wing due to a dire need, their juniors were promoted in the Engineering wing without consideration of their claim.
Therefore, they cannot be reverted back on the grounds of improper procedure in their appointment and must receive the benefit of seniority for their service in the Engineering wing.
