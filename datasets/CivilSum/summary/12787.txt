Constitution of India, Article 14 - Selection grade - Seniority - Transfer to other district - If an employee transfers to another district on their own request, they must be placed junior-most in the seniority list of that district - In cases where the employee was receiving the selection grade prior to the transfer, they cannot retain that grade in the transferee district as only 15% of employees can be granted selection grade based on seniority - Allowing the transfer to retain their selection grade would exceed the 15% limit already in place, unless the transfer was made on administrative grounds - This information is detailed in paras 3 to 6.
