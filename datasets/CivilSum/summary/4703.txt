The Andhra Pradesh State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1996, Rule 6(b) and 23, as well as the Andhra Pradesh State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1962, Rules 4(a)(i)(ii) were applied in a panel promotion case.
Petitioners were initially reverted due to the inclusion of unofficial respondents, but they claimed that the respondents were responsible for regularly preparing a promotion panel.
Although their names were omitted from the 1995-96 panel, they did not address the issue at the time.
The court ruled that the petitioners did not have a right to appointment as the unofficial respondents were ranked higher in the panel and had more seniority.
The petitioners were only entitled to a hearing, which was ultimately provided, and no injustice was done.
