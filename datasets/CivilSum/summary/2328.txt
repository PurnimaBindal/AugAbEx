Under the Working Journalists and other Newspaper Employees (Condition of Service) and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, Section 5(1)(a)(i) and Payment of Gratuity Act, Section 4, a Journalist who has been terminated has the right to receive gratuity.
This entitlement applies if the termination is not a form of punishment imposed through disciplinary action.
However, it is worth noting that "disciplinary action" in sub-section only applies to employees dismissed for an offence that involves either moral turpitude or riotous or disorderly behavior.
