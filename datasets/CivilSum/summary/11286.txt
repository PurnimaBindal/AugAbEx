Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Selection - Eligibility - Experience - Appointment on the post of Estate Officer in PAU, Ludhiana - In order to be eligible for the post, candidates were required to have twelve years of experience in construction and maintenance of buildings, utility service, etc. in a University/Government/Semi-Government organization - The issue at hand is whether the 3rd respondent fulfills the prescribed qualifications - However, as the 3rd respondent had only been holding a teaching assignment at the University, they do not meet the prescribed qualifications - Consequently, their selection has been set aside.
