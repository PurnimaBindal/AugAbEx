According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India and the MP Civil Services Rules of 1965, withholding two increments with cumulative effect is considered a major penalty.
However, this penalty cannot be imposed without conducting a regular departmental enquiry, as per the judgment of Kulwant Singh Gill v. State of Punjab in 1991(2) SCT 30 (SC).
[Paras 5 and 7]
Stoppage of increments by withholding two increments with cumulative effect is a major penalty.
It cannot be enforced unless a proper enquiry is conducted.
Furthermore, if the order of such a penalty is passed without due enquiry, it is considered unlawful and must be quashed.
This is in accordance with the Constitution of India's Article 311 and the MP Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeals) Rules of 1965.
