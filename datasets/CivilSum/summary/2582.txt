Section 15 of the Representation of the People Act, 1951 allows the Election Commission to announce an election within six months prior to the expiry of term of Assembly, without violating any statutory provision.
It cannot be termed as mala fide.
[Paras 7 and 8]
Part XV of the Constitution of India defines the word 'election' to include the entire process of election, from the date of notification to return a candidate to the legislature.
The Election Commission has the power to issue directions to the Government and political parties and enforce the Model Code of Conduct from the date of Notification itself until the termination of election process.
This convention is accepted by all political parties for conducting fair and pure elections.
Even if there is no provision of Code of Conduct in the Act or the Constitution, it does not violate any statutory provision.
The restriction on the Govt. from announcing any welfare policies that may influence the voters is in the interest of fairness and purity of election.
[Paras 10 to 20]
