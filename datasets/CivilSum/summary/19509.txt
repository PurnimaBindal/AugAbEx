Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Drawing Teacher - Senior grade teacher - Although there was no vacant post of Senior Drawing Teacher available for promotion, 20 posts of senior teachers were created without any reference to subjects taught by the teachers - The petitioner's juniors were granted senior grade despite petitioner's higher seniority - Since the posts in senior grade were created irrespective of subjects taught by the teachers, petitioner cannot be denied senior grade based on non-availability of the post of Senior Drawing Teacher - Senior grade has to be granted solely based on seniority - The respondents are directed to consider the petitioner for promotion to the post of Senior Teacher in the senior grade from the date his juniors were promoted - The appeal is allowed with costs of Rs. 2000/-.
