Indian Penal Code, Section 409 - Accused serving as Conductor in Road - The failure to return bus tickets does not fall under Section 409 IPC as an offence - To show dishonest appropriation or usage is necessary for a prosecution to be made.
AIR 1977 SC 1766 relied.
[Para 4]
Indian Penal Code, Section 409 - Accused a conductor of Roadways - Though accused was a conductor, he had been accused of absconding with cash - Case had been registered under Section 409 IPC - Accused had deposited more than the due amount - Complainant Roadways had no grounds to claim that there was misappropriation after accepting the amount - Accused was acquitted.
