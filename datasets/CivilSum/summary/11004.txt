The petitioner was regularised to a lower post in 1974, and regularisation was not objected until 1984.
However, a writ petition challenging the regularisation of respondents was filed in 1984, after a delay of 9 to 10 years.
The long and unexplained delay caused the petitioner's claim to be dismissed.
As a result, a petition filed after the period of limitation prescribed for filing a civil suit should typically be treated as belated, and relief refused unless a cogent and satisfactory explanation is given.
[Paras 11, 12 and 13] B.
The policy of regularisation allowed for work-charged employees who had completed five years to be regularised.
However, the petitioners did not meet the eligibility requirements.
Moreover, the employee was not entitled to be regularised in the post where he was promoted a few days earlier.
Only one post of Assistant Foreman/Electrical was available, therefore the single judge order was set aside, and the petitioners were not entitled to regularisation.
