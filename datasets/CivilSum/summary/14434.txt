Constitution of India, Articles 14, 300-A and 310 - Retiral benefits - Pension - Pension is a valuable right and a property in the hands of the employee and not a bounty.
In case of delayed payment, retiree is entitled to interest at a rate of 18% unless the employee is at fault for the delay.

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Continuity of service - A Master serving in the Punjab Govt. Education Department who also worked in District Board Primary School is eligible to include his District Board service while computing pensionary benefits.
