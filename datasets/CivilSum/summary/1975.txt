Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Termination - Overstayal of leave - Absence from duty - Termination of services of a permanent employee without departmental proceedings is illegal and arbitrary - Employee's absence from duty due to non-extension of sanctioned leave for two years doesn't permit termination.
