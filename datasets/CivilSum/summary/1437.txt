Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 72 - Disability pension - Establishing a casual connection between the cause of disability and military service is necessary to consider the disability as attributable to service - Injuries sustained during leave outside the place of posting/duty and not connected to military service cannot be considered as service attributable - However, claiming disability pension for each and every injury sustained during leave is not entitled - Disability resulting from an activity not connected with military service is not attributable to service - Despite sustaining injuries during leave, a road accident not related to military duty does not qualify for disability pension - Petitioner's prayer for disability pension was rejected.
