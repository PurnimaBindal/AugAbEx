Cause of action for dismissal from service shall be taken to arise not from the date of the original adverse order but on the date when the order of the higher authority where a statutory remedy is provided is made, or from six months after the appeal or representation is made if no order is issued.
This principle may not be applicable when the remedy availed of has not been provided by law.
Repeated unsuccessful representations not provided by law are not governed by this principle.
[Para]

The Administrative Tribunals Act limits the period for making an application to challenge dismissal from service to one year, with the possibility of condonation of delay for a total of six months.
The act also takes away the jurisdiction of civil courts for government servants, so Article 58 may not be applicable for them.
Suits outside the purview of the Administrative Tribunals Act shall continue to be governed by Article 58.
[Para]

According to Limitation Act, 1963, Article 58, cause of action for dismissal from service shall first arise only when the remedies available to the public servant under the relevant Service Rules are disposed of.
