The provision of an option for promotion only available to promotees and not direct recruits is not considered discriminatory under Constitution of India, Article 14, Marine Products Export Development Authority Act, 1972, Section 7(4), and Marine Produits Export Development Authority Standing Instruction dt. 15.12.1979, Clauses 9.
The right of the option is only applicable at the promotion from Group C to Group B post, but employees referred to in Sub-Clause (3) of Clause 10 can exercise the option only in accordance with the requirements of that Sub-Clause and cannot be treated differently from rest of the employees.
Petitioners who were directly appointed on the post of Quality Supervisor in the particular cadre cannot opt for promotion in other cadre, as the appointment was made on the post of Quality Supervisor which falls in Channel III under Schedule II referred to in Sub-Clause (1) of Clause 10 of the Standing Instructions.
