Industrial Disputes Act (XIV of 1947), Section 10 - Whether private companies can be subject to writ jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Indian Constitution and what are the limits - Determined that only violations of the Industrial Disputes Act that entail public duties can be subject to writ jurisdiction under Article 226; employee service condition matters, such as dismissals and transfers, must be adjudicated solely by forums created under the Industrial Disputes Act, not otherwise; disputes relating to matters not governed by the Industrial Disputes Act must be resolved only by common law courts; the transfers in question do not involve public duties and concern disputed questions of fact that should be resolved before the forums under the Industrial Disputes Act; the appellants/petitioners-employees have the right to make an application under Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act for a referral within two weeks of receiving a copy of this decision.
