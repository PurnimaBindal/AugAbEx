Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Sections 125 and 300 - Indian Penal Code, Section 494 - Double jeopardy - Maintenance to wife - Payment of maintenance to wife is required even if a person is acquitted of bigamy charges under Section 494 of the Indian Penal Code as it does not fall under the purview of the double jeopardy doctrine - Maintenance to wife under Section 125 Cr.PC is not an offence according to Section 40 I.PC [Paras 19 and 20] B.
Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 300 - Doctrine of jeopardy - The doctrine of jeopardy could be applied only when a person is convicted or acquitted of a charge for a crime - The term "offence" in Section 300 of the Code refers to something that is punishable under the Indian Penal Code - As per Section 300 of the Code, once a person is convicted or acquitted, they cannot be tried for the same offence again.
