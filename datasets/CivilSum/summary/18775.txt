The National Cadet Corps Rules, 1947, Rule 26(3) and Indian Reserve Force Rules, Rule 3(c) state that seniority is determined by the officer's service in the Regular Reserve of Officers Service.
In this case, the respondent was appointed as a 2nd Lieutenant in the Regular Reserve of Officers Service while serving as a customs officer, but his lien was never transferred.
The learned single judge was incorrect in directing the appellant to count the period of service of respondent in the Regular Reserve of Officers Service for the purpose of his seniority in NCC.
