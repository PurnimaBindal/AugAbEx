According to Section 12 of the Maternity Benefits Act, 1961, if a woman is appointed against a temporary post and is on maternity leave, her order of termination must not be indefinite and must provide a reason.
In a case where no reasons were disclosed in the termination order, it was held that the manner in which the termination was carried out may indicate that it was due to the employee's pregnancy.
Since many years have passed since the termination in 1980, and the petitioner has not been employed since then, the court ruled that the employee is entitled to 50% back wages.
