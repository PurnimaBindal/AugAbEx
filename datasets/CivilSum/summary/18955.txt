Constitution of India, Article 16 - Retiral Benefits - Late payment - Interest - The petitioner retired voluntarily on 31.1.1997 and experienced a delay in receiving their retiral benefits - The Court ruled that all retiral benefits based on the unrevised pay scale should have been released within 6 months of retirement - As a result, the petitioner is entitled to interest of 12% per annum starting from 181997 until the actual payment of retiral benefits based on the unrevised pay - Payment of retiral benefits on the revised pay scale, GIS, and arrears due on account of sanction of proficiency increment should be made within 3 months of the court's order, and if not, the amount owed will earn interest of 18% starting from 142000.
