Constitution of India, Article 309 - Army Act, 1950, Section 192 and Sections 71 to 89 - Instructions/Punishment/Censure/Severe Displeasure - Army Officers must not be punished through summary procedures, and censure or severe displeasure cannot be recorded in an officer's service record.
Punishments must align with the Act, Rules or Service Regulations, and severe displeasure cannot affect an officer's promotion opportunities.
Only the Central Government is authorized to issue instructions under Section 192 of the Act.
The Acting Brigadier in this case was denied promotion due to severe displeasure recorded in his service record, but once the instructions under which Severe Displeasure is awarded, are held bad, the award of Severe Displeasure cannot subsist.
The authorities have been directed to consider the petitioner's case for the grant of Substantive Rank of Brigadier, ignoring the entry of severe displeasure.
