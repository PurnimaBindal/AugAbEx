Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Section 11A - Termination - Back wages - The Labour Court cannot award full back wages to a workman who has been terminated for misconduct without setting aside the disciplinary proceedings and punishment, but can only grant reinstatement without back wages under section 11A - Refusal to grant back wages by the Labour Court is deemed legal and reasonable.
[Paras 13 to 15]
