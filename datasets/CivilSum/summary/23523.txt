Control over subordinate courts in judicial service of a State is vested in the High Court as per Constitution of India, Article 235 - Specifically, control over promotion, posting, and granting of leave to persons holding any post inferior to that of a District Judge - Despite this, an inquiry was initiated and officer chosen and appointed by the State Govt., with a tentative decision to impose penalty of dismissal even before a 2nd show cause notice was issued to the delinquent - The final decision to impose penalty of dismissal by the Govt. was in violation of the jurisdiction of the High Court under Article 235.
[Para 4]  B Gujarat Civil Services Rules, 1975, Rule 5(4) does not apply to situations where an inquiry and consequential order passed pursuant thereto was by an authority precluded from doing so under Article 235 - The order of dismissal passed by such authority has no efficacy in law and is to be ignored - Rule 5(4) is applicable only to orders passed by a competent authority but declared void by a court on grounds of violation of principles of natural justice - Order of suspension passed on 26.6.1977 cannot be treated as period of "deemed suspension" - Directed lump sum payment of Rs. 50,000/- in full and final settlement of the claim of the appellant instead of leaving it to the Govt. to calculate the amount after deducting what he had earned as a practitioner.
