Kerala Education Rules 1459, Rule 9A, Chapter XXIV A - Compassionate Appointment - Claim - Married son/daughter - It is held that the appointment claim of a married son/daughter in the dying in harness category can be considered, provided it can be proven that they were dependent on the deceased and could not overcome sudden crises.
The claim cannot be lost simply because of marriage as this would defeat the provision's purpose and the government's liberalized scheme.
