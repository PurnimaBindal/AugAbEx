Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - University Regulations, Regulation XV - Education - Qualifying marks - Degree in Physiotherapy - The respondents-University had detained the petitioners in the III semester because they didn't obtain 50% of aggregate marks in the examination.
But according to Regulation XV, a candidate can be declared passed if they get a minimum of 40% in theory and 50% aggregate of combined theory and oral marks.
Since no oral examination was taken, the matter of getting marks in oral exams doesn't arise.
The respondents have been directed to consider petitioners' cases, who have secured 40% marks in the theory, where no oral exam is present, as passed in the examination.
