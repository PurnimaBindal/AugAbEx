Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Disciplinary Proceeding - Modification of Punishment - Employee charged with unauthorised absence, ordered to be removed from service - Employee claims not to be in sound mental health - Prayer for reduction/modification of punishment - After considering 18 years of service and family of employee, CAT directs punishment of compulsory retirement instead of removal from service - CAT's decision not based on sympathy alone - Decision of CAT should not be interfered with.
