Constitution of India, Article 226 - Claim for disability pension - Disability attributed to Army service - The court found that the petitioner was allowed to undertake his duty in the Army when he was declared fully fit by medical authorities, and was initially placed in category A.
Therefore, the court had no difficulty in holding that the petitioner's disease was attributable to his service in the Army.
As a result, the writ petition was allowed.
