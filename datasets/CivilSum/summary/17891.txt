Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Removal from service/Termination - Charges - Enquiry - Service Record - The punishing authority can only consider the charges levelled and enquired into during a departmental enquiry against an employee.
The authority cannot punish an employee based on their past service record, which was not part of the enquiry.
Termination orders based on past service record are illegal, vitiated, and violate natural justice principles.
[Paras 4 and 5]
