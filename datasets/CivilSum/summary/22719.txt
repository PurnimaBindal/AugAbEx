Constitution of India, Article 226 - Review - Recalling of earlier orders in other case - A court order can only be reviewed or recalled after following the legal procedure - The judge recalled a previous order of reinstatement in favor of the appellant, passed in 1991, in a 1993 writ petition of another co-employee, without issuing a specific notice for recalling her reinstatement - The High Court's order is not valid and the appeal is allowed with costs - Civil Procedure Code, Order 47 and Sections 152 and 153.
