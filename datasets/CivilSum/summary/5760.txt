The Public Service Commission may use a process of selection that it deems most suitable for the post.
However, the process must not be arbitrary, discriminatory, or mala fide.
Marks for interviews and academic qualifications are the basis for selection, with a 40% allocation for interviews and 60% for qualifications.
Even if 40% marks are used ipso facto in selections for Head Masters, the selection will not be vitiated.
[Paras 6, 7, 9, and 11] B Screening tests can be used by the Public Service Commission for shortlisting candidates.
However, it is not necessary to include the marks of screening tests in the final marks of selection.
Instead, selection must be based only on interviews and academic qualifications.
[Para 11] C. Amendments to Rule 10 of the Procedure Rules have jurisdiction over vacancies within the Punjab State Public Service Commission and Commission regulations.
