According to the Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 226 and the Punjab Police Rules, 1934, under Rule 13.8(1), there is a quota for promotion with seniority reckoning during the probationary period.
The petitioner was promoted as Head Constable under the 10% quota outlined in Rule 13.8(2) of the Punjab Police Rules, 1934, and was not reverted thereafter.
As such, he is eligible for seniority dating back to when he was promoted.
