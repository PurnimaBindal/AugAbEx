According to the Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, Sections 22 and 23, workmen will only be entitled to wages for strike periods if the strike was both legal and justified.
If the strike was not justified but legal or not legal but justified, workers will not receive wages for the strike period.
Determining the legality and justification of a strike is the responsibility of the Industrial Tribunal based on evidence.
The High Court cannot make such findings.
The public interest must also be considered when examining the justification of the strike, both in the public and private sectors, to ensure neither management nor workers exploit public funds.
