Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights And Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 47 - Alternative employment - A disabled APSRTC employee requested alternative employment - It was determined that the legislation was enacted to benefit disabled individuals and their rights should be protected in order to lead a normal life - The appellant was instructed to file a representation to seek the relief sought in the appeal and the appropriate authority should consider the case and issue the necessary order.
