Himachal Pradesh Excise and Taxation Department (Inspectorate Staff- Class III) Service Rules, Rule 11(iv) - Seniority - Probation - Those appointed on probation are required to pass a departmental exam within two years as per the rules, and the exam takes place twice a year - Passing the exam would result in having seniority date back to the date of appointment - However, since the respondents claimed their seniority from the date of passing the exam, their seniority should accordingly be fixed.
