Constitution of India, Article 14 - State Bank of India (Supervising Staff) Service Rules, Rule 3(c) - Misconduct - Quantum of punishment - Dismissal - The Appellate Authority's decision stated that there was no loss caused to the bank.
The employee's unblemished service of 32 years prior to the charge sheet was not taken into consideration, and the penalty of dismissal was disproportionate to the charges.
The order for dismissal is not sustainable, and the petitioner should be treated as compulsory retired while also being entitled to terminal benefits.
