According to the Constitution of India, as stated in Articles 226 and 227, the petitioner was originally designated a seat in Mechanical Engineering, but was not permitted to attend Electrical Engineering due to a mistake in registration.
Despite following all required procedures, the petitioner was denied his preferred course.
The petitioner had a higher merit than respondent No. 4, and the courses for all engineering diplomas in 1st year are identical.
Additionally, there was no harm that would come to anyone if the petitioner was granted admission in Electrical Engineering.
Thus, the respondent has been told to allow the petitioner to switch to Electrical Engineering from Mechanical Engineering in the 2nd year of his course and interchange positions with respondent No. 4, given that a vacant seat is available.
However, respondent No. 4 must not be permitted to take precedence over the petitioner.
