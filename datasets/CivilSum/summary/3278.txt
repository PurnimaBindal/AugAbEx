The petitioner was temporarily appointed as a daily rated LDC under the NREP Scheme, which was later converted to the Jawahar Rojgar Yojana Scheme.
No excess work in the NREP Scheme meant his services were no longer required.
As per Rule 23 (1) & (2) of the Rajasthan Panchayat Samitis and Zila Parishads Service Rules, 1959, the petitioner was irregularly appointed temporarily and his services could not be extended.
The Panchayat Samiti tendered one month's notice pay and retrenchment compensation to the petitioner, terminating his services, and duly complied with the mandatory provisions of Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
Therefore, the retrenchment is not void ab-in-itio, illegal or non est.
[Paras 9 and 10]

Under the NREP or Jawahar Rojgar Yojana Scheme, the petitioner was neither entitled to the prescribed pay scale of LDC.
nor to be regularised on the post of LDC.
Therefore, the doctrine of equal pay for equal work cannot be made applicable to daily wages employees under temporary or time-bound schemes.
[Paras 10, 13 and 16]
