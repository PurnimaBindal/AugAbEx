Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Punjab National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rules 2 and 4 - Military service - Benefit of - The benefit of military service would only be granted to those who joined the armed forces during the emergency period and not to those who joined prior to the emergency - The petitioners, having joined the Army before the emergency, and having joined the Zila Sainik Board, are not entitled to the military service benefit they are claiming.
