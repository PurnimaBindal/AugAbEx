Constitution of India, Articles 77 and 311(2) - Railway Protection Rules, 1959, Rules 44, 45 and 47 - Removal - Departmental Enquiry - The right to a departmental enquiry should not be easily dispensed with unless it is really impracticable and reasons are recorded in writing - The circumstances did not warrant dispensing with the enquiry and the order of removal was not compliant with executive instructions - The order of removal is therefore bad in law.
