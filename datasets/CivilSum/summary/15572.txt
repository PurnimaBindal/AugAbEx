Andhra Pradesh Universities Act, 1991, Section 6-A - AP University Employees (Option and Other Conditions for Teaching and Non-Teaching Staff) Rules, 1993, Rule 3 - Transfer from one University to other - Respondents 5 to 8 exercising option to remain with parent university - Rules state that they must wait for a clear vacancy in their department to arise - The maximum limit is five years, after which they will be absorbed in the other university - Rules are compliant with Section 6-A of the Act - In this case, respondents 5 to 8 were posted within the jurisdiction of the first respondent-university, but there was no clear vacancy in the Telugu department, in which they wished to remain - During the pendency of the petition, respondents 5 to 8 were transferred to other departments in the parent university, but this would not affect the seniority of the petitioners in the Telugu department - Respondents 5 to 8 cannot be absorbed in the Telugu department unless clear vacancies arise under the rules.
