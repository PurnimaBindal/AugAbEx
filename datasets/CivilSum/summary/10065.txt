Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Army Pension Regulations, Part-I, 1961, Paragraph 173 - Entitlement Rules for Causal Pensionary Awards, 1982, Rule 14 - Disability pension - Arrears of pension - A discharge of service due to military-related schizophrenia cannot deny disability pension - Pension is a statutory right, not a charity, and arrears of pension cannot be restricted - Courts exercising Article 226 of the Constitution are not limited in the relief they can provide - Once pension entitlement is established, the complete right to a pension must be granted.
