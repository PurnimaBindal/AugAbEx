In departmental proceedings, the workman cannot be provided assistance of a lawyer unless the rule allows it and without legal assistance, the workman will face difficulties as they cannot interpret the documents that have been presented as evidence.
As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, the National Seeds Corporation (Conduct, Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1992, Rule 31(7), Central Civil Services (CCA) Rules, 1965, Rule 14(8), Railway Establishment Code, Rule 1712 and Maharahstra Recognition of Trade Unions and Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Section 22(ii), it is considered that the workman or his colleague is capable of providing better assistance to the inquiring authority and hence, a different view is not taken based on the physical handicap of the workman.
