According to Section 12(3) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, workers are entitled to raise additional demands such as concessional rates for rain coats, firewood, and way expenses to their village.
In a recent case, management refused to grant these additional demands, citing the absence of provisions for such demands in an earlier settlement.
However, it was decided that the earlier settlement cannot restrict unions from raising new demands that are not covered by the settlement.
