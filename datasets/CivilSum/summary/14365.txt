The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Natural justice - Seniority - Correction of seniority - Change of seniority - The law has established that a citizen cannot be subjected to an unfavorable order without being given a fair opportunity to be heard - However, this requirement only applies when there is a conscious decision to issue an unfavorable order, even if it might be wrong - When it comes to correcting records that incidentally affect a citizen, forcing a hearing would only lead to further injustice - The principle of natural justice does not come into play in correction matters.
