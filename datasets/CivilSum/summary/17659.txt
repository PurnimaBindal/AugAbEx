UP Intermediate Education Act, 1921, Section 16-E - UP Secondary Education Service Commission and Selection Board Act, 1982, Section 16 - Appointment of Lecturer - Candidate for reserved post of Scheduled Caste/Scheduled Tribes not available - Backward class candidate was sent to Selection Committee for appointment, but was found not suitable for the post and also ineligible due to being over-age - Managing Committee of the College accepted recommendation of Selection Committee, therefore, no other candidate was selected by Selection Committee, but he was appointed by Dy.
Director of Education without recommendation by Commission - Ad hoc lecturer working on the post was removed without giving him opportunity of hearing - Person aggrieved has locus standi to file writ petition, was entitled to continue till candidate selected by the Commission joins the post - Order of Dy.
Director appointing backward class candidate, is contrary to provisions of Sections 16 and 16-E, is invalidated.
