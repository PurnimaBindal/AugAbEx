The Punjab Town and Planning (State Services) Class-I Rules, 1972 and Punjab Town and Country Planners (States Service Class II - Technical) Rules, 1988 are discussed in regards to promotion and the quota allotted for direct recruits and promotees.
The post of Assistant Town Planner was removed from the 1972 rules by the superseding Rules of 1988.
The new draft rules only provide for a 50% quota for direct recruits and one of the petitioners has already benefited from these rules.
It is stated that the rules cannot be challenged solely on the basis of providing less quota for direct recruits.
As a result, the petition is dismissed.
