Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 2(e) - `Employer' - Refers to those who had temporary control over the workman lent or hired by the person who has a contract with the workman.
[Para 12] B Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Liability of compensation - The owner of the jeep is required to comply with the requisition order of the vehicle for election duty and the driver was under the control of the requisitioning authority while performing his duties leading to his accidental death; the Commissioner erred in directing the appellant bank to deposit the amount of compensation.
Therefore, the requisitioning authority is liable to pay the compensation to the driver's family and the appellant shall be reimbursed the amount already deposited.
