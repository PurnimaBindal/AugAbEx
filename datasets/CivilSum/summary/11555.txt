Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation - Petitioner's representation for regularisation not decided in compliance with the law - Petitioner had been employed with respondent since 1987 and continued his service after being awarded continuity of service by the labour court - Hence, his service can be deemed continuous - Petitioner had the right to be considered for regularisation in accordance with government policy - In light of the ruling in 2001(3) SCT 307, respondent was instructed to review petitioner's case for regularisation.
