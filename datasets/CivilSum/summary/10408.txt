Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Education - Admissions - Admission to Post-Graduate courses - The University cannot restrict the number of seats available to autonomous colleges.
College-wise preference is not allowed, therefore the Admission Committee cannot limit seats for graduates of the autonomous college, even if they are from the same University.
This would result in lower scoring students being offered seats over higher scoring ones.
