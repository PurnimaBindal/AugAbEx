Constitution of India Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Punjab Education Class III School Cadre Rules, 1955, Rule 7 - Higher Scale - Post of Sanskrit Teacher - If only teachers who possessed OT qualification at entry level were granted higher grade benefits, it would make a distinction between teachers who gain higher qualification during their service, which is unreasonable - The teacher who gains higher qualifications would also be eligible for higher grade from the date of obtaining the degree.
