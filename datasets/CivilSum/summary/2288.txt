Bihar Pension Rules, 1950, Rule 43(b) Proviso and Rule 139 - Pension - Pension and retiral benefits are governed by service Rules - They are not a charity or dependent upon the employer's sweet-will - They are a deferred portion of past service and become a property U/Art.
31(1) of the Constitution - they cannot be taken away by executive orders.
[Paras 12 and 29]

Bihar Pension Rules, 1950, Rule 43(b) Proviso and Rule 139 - Pension and gratuity are to be paid to an employee in terms of statutory Regulation framed under the Indian Electricity (Supply) Act - The Board cannot withhold any of these benefits by framing Rule or issuing executive orders - Rule 43(b) cannot be invoked by the authority in the absence of any such punishment to the employee.
[Paras 16, 17, 29, 31, 33, 34, 35]

Bihar Pension Rules, 1950, Rule 43(b) Proviso - Departmental proceedings initiated prior to retirement, automatically lapse on retirement unless there is a specific rule under which such proceedings are deemed to continue - The Bihar Rules do not provide for any such provision and there is no question of automatic continuation of such proceedings after retirement.
[Paras 21 and 22]
