Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Defence Production - (Directorate of Production and Inspection, Naval) Group A and Group B Technical Post Recruitment Rules, 1976, Schedule Columns 10 and 11 - Quota rule - Strict adherence to the rules that fill vacancies for direct recruits as soon as recruitment is made, which denies promotees the benefits of their service is viewed unfavorably - Authorities should make direct recruitment promptly to avoid adverse consequences and ensure fair treatment of promotees.
