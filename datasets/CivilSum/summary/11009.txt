The petitioner was discharged from the police force under Indian Penal Code Sections 366, 376 and Punjab Police Rules 1934, Rule 1221, with authorities citing that he would not make a good police officer.
However, the respondents' reply stated that his discharge was based on an overall assessment rather than any specific misconduct.
This was found to be erroneous as the only material against him was his involvement in a rape case.
Although he was acquitted, he was not reinstated in the force and instead given a lumpsum payment of Rs.1 lakh, which was deemed sufficient to address his claim in the petition.
This resulted in the dismissal of the petition.
