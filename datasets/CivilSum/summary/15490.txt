Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Civil Rules Volume II, Rule 3.17(a)(f)(i) - Pensionary and Retiral Benefits - Petitioner worked as a member of Bhajan Party from 1978 to 1987 and from 1988 to 1996 - Petitioner requests Pensionary and Retiral Benefits - The service period of the Petitioner has been treated as contingent service but there has been no break in service due to the Petitioner's fault - As the break in service is attributable to the employer, the Petitioner is entitled to receive the benefit of full service for pensionary benefits - Hence, the petition has been allowed.
