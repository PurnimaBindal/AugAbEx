Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 21, 226 - School Teachers - Adhoc/Contract appointees - Regularisation - The petitioners have worked for approximately five years - Qualified, eligible applicants are appointed after selection - The court has held that if an adhoc/temporary employee is continued for a reasonably long period, the authorities must consider their case for regularisation if they have a satisfactory service record - A scheme for regularisation should be created for such employees - The court has directed the framing of a rational policy within six months.
