Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Haryana Aided Schools (Security of Services) Act, Sec. 3 - 1971 - Haryana Aided Schools (Security of Services), Rules, 1974, Rules 24 and 28 - Termination - Enquiry - Govt. aided school employee - Termination of services - The employee was terminated without any enquiry and was not granted a reasonable opportunity in respect of charges.
The procedure was not followed in accordance with the law.
The government authority had set aside the termination according to rule and dismissed the appeal of the petitioner.
The petitioner was required to hold a departmental enquiry as per the law and there was no infirmity in passing the orders of setting aside the order of termination.
