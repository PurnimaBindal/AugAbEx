Ad hoc promotion for nine months or earlier is recommended by the SSS Board.
After nine months, the petitioner may revert to a lower position if the Board's recommendee has not joined.
The reversion is not arbitrary because the appointment order did not grant the petitioner the right to remain in the higher post.
The reversion is a natural consequence of the terms of appointment, and it is distinguished from 1990(6) SLR 1.
[Para 7] B.
An ad hoc promotion for a nine-month period is valid, and the petitioner may be reverted once it expires.
The contention that some employees promoted for a nine-month period are still employed is not valid.
It cannot be claimed if any person appointed for a term of appointment mentioned in the appointment order has been retained after the term's expiration.
