Constitution of India Article 311 states that the petitioner's termination from service is not appropriate at this stage.
The petitioner submitted a false certificate with a wrong date of birth and was appointed at the age of 31, while the maximum age for appointment was 25 years.
However, no charge was specifically framed against him on this ground during the disciplinary proceedings initiated against him in 1982.
With his clean service record of 22 years at the age of 54, if the petitioner is sent out of service, it will be unreasonable and cause him hardship.
Therefore, only withholding of future increments till retirement will be a sufficient punishment.
Additionally, fraud in getting appointed by showing the wrong date of birth after being overage cannot be tolerated, but it should be detected within a reasonable time with due diligence.
A period of 13 years is not reasonable to take an action.
[Paras 7 and 8] According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311, fraud in employment cannot be overlooked, but the services cannot be terminated at the verge of retirement.
