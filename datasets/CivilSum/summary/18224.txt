Under Section 4 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, workers employed by a contractor of a principal employer who sustained injuries in a road accident would be covered by the policy if they were found at the location.
The insurance company would not be liable for workers who were not injured on the site but elsewhere.
As the workers were carrying out work for the principal employer, he would be responsible for compensation.
