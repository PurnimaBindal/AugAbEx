The Agricultural Marketing Board advertised posts for peons and prepared a merit list, but the appointment of the petitioner was not made on the grounds that the list was only valid for six months.
This contention was found to be untenable.
[Para 7] B.
After a selection list was prepared by the Board for a post advertised, the authority does not have an absolute right to deny appointment.
A candidate who has competed for the post and found suitable is entitled to a reasonable hope that they will be appointed.
No valid reason was shown in the instant case, and the authority was directed to consider the petitioner's claim for appointment.
