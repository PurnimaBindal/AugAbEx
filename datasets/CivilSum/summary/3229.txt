Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Appointment - Respondent's appointment as dealer and distributor of LPG set aside due to concealment of gainful employment from Indian Oil Corporation - Corporation directed to conduct fresh selection.
[Paras 21, 23 and 26] B.
Gainful Employment/Self employment - The term 'employed' refers to a person in employment of someone else - 'Gainful employment' implies that the employed person should be able to maintain themselves and their family - 'Self-employed' refers to a person running a business with adequate turnover.
[Paras 15 and 16] C. Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - State - Writ jurisdiction - Indian Oil Corporation can be subject to a writ petition.
