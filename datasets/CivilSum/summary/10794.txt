Based on the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226, the appointment of sports category candidates can not be discriminatory.
In this case, the petitioners and respondents 5 to 7 were recommended simultaneously by common recommendation and their credentials were verified and cleared by common order.
Their fitness was determined through a medical report from a Board appointed by different Govt. orders.
They were all certified to be sports persons possessing proficiency in sports as defined in SRO, and were equals in all respects.
The respondents did not demonstrate any distinguishing feature in respect of qualification, eligibility, merit or any other factor to prove that private respondents 5 to 7 were having superior merit compared to the petitioner for their appointment against higher post or any disqualification suffered by petitioner for his appointment against lower post.
Therefore, the respondents have been directed to appoint the petitioner against the higher post of Inspector in the Police Deptt.
on the analogy of the respondents No. 5 to 7 or revert them to the lower post of sub-inspector.
[Paras 6 & 7]
