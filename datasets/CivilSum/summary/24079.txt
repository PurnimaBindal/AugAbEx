Administrations Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 17 - Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 12 - The power to punish for contempt should not be lightly exercised - Courts should not take offense when they are informed that their orders have not been followed immediately - Courts must be gracious and magnanimous when dealing with cases of contempt - The court should be lenient when considering the punishment of contemners who apologize satisfactorily.
