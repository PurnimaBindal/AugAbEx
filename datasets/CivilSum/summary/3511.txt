Punjab State Electricity Board Employees (Punishment and Appeal) Regulations 1971, Regulation 14(i) - Constitution of India, Article 311 - Dismissal of an employee from service on ground of his conviction in a criminal case - The dismissal of an employee cannot be based solely on their conviction, as the competent authority must also consider the employee's conduct, the gravity of the misconduct, its potential impact on the organization, and any extenuating circumstances.
A 1997 case from Punjab and Haryana High Court (1997(1) SCT 282) was used as reference.
