The Rajasthan Civil Services (Pension) Rules (1996), Rule 50 - Voluntary retirement - was recently discussed in the context of Articles 311 and 309 of the Constitution of India.
An application seeking voluntary retirement on the ground of family circumstances and ill health was rejected because of pending disciplinary proceedings.
The guidelines provided under the (1996) Rules allowed for such a rejection.
Additionally, even if retirement is sought on the ground of contesting an election, it can also be rejected as per the guidelines if disciplinary proceedings are pending.
