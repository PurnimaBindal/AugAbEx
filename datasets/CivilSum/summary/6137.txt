Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Settlement between employer and Workmen Union - The issue of whether Machine Operator employees can refuse to clear the surrounding area of machines is being questioned - The Machine Operators refused to clean the surrounding area of machines, claiming that it is not their duty - The appellant-Company deemed the alleged refusal as a strike - The Company insists that the employees undertake any work allotted to them - The Industrial Court can resolve this small issue - Both parties have staked their so-called prestige for a small issue - The High Court has directed the Industrial Court to promptly decide the complaints based on evidence.
