According to Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rules 72 and 73 - Suspension allowance, during suspension period, the amount of wages earned by the employee shall be deducted from the suspension allowance.
The employee must furnish a certificate of non-employment to that effect.
Subsistence allowance directed by the court can only be paid after complying with the requirements of the rules, and the employee is not entitled to the full amount of suspension allowance ipso facto.

Civil Procedure Code, Section 47(1) requires the executing court to decide all the questions between the parties or their representatives related to execution, discharge, or satisfaction of the decree in accordance with the rules.
