Indian Police Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1955, Regulation 5 - Non-meeting of Selection Committee - Seniority and consideration for promotion - Petitioner's case not considered in 1988 and 1989 due to the Selection Committee meeting only in 1990 - Respondents instructed to determine Petitioner's seniority from 1987 and consider him for promotion against the 1988 vacancy for which no meeting was held and no explanation given.

Limitation Act, 1963, Section 5 - Deprivation of seniority and promotion - CAT dismissed OA on the grounds of delay - Communication regarding date of allotment of Petitioner received after a considerable delay and which he then represented - CAT erred in not condoning the delay when it was not a case of inaction, negligence or mala fides on the part of the Petitioner - Judgment in 'N Balakrishnan v. M Krishnamurthy' followed to condone delay in a justice-oriented way, not hyper-technically.
