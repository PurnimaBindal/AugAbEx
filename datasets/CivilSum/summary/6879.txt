The Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1996, Section 73 - Rajasthan Employment of the Persons with Disabilities Rules, 2000, Rule 7-A - Appointment - Post of Teacher Grade III - Rule 7A(iii) does not allow for any concession in basic qualification for disabled persons, and instead applies to temporary appointments with completion of training essential to post in two years - the requirement of courses cannot be taken as training and not `essential qualification' - therefore, no such concession under the rule can be claimed by a disabled person under Rule 7A(iii).
