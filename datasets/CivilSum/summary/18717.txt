Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Haryana Civil Services (Executive Branch) Examination - Disclosure of candidate identity - Petitioner received a remark "D" in Hindi exam and seeks quashing of Instructions Clause 3(a) and Note 1 at Page 12 regarding disclosure of identity as arbitrary and illegal - Petitioner also seeks declaration of result in other papers - Petitioner had already disclosed identity in Hindi exam - Court holds that decision of respondent is valid as petitioner had already disclosed identity and dismissing petition stating declaration of result in other papers would be pointless as petitioner did not pass the compulsory exam.
