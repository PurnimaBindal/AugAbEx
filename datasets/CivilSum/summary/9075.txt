According to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(j), the Irrigation Department is classified as an industry and an employee working in Canal Lining Division still falls under the category of an employee of Irrigation Department, as established in the Des Raj v. State of Punjab, AIR 1988 SC 1182 case.
[Para 5]

The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 (1)(c) deems ex parte awards to be valid and reinstates workmen with back wages.
The Labour Court denied the application for reviewing the order of the already published award being functus-officio.
Although a written statement was filed by the management, no representation was made in court.
The Labour Court could only proceed ex-parte against the management.
A representative of both management and the workman appeared on the adjourned hearing date.
The plea raised in the review application and writ petition that no further dates were given after filing the written statement is false.
The case cannot be remanded back to the Labour Court, and the petition is dismissed under the Constitution of India, Articles 226/227.
