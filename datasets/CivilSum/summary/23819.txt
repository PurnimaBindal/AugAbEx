Karnataka Civil Services (General Recruitment) Rules, 1977, Rules 1(3), 3(2) and 4 - Karnataka General Srvice (Motor Vehicles Branch) (Recruitment) Rules, 1976 - Promotion to the post of Deputy Transport Commissioner - Promotion must be made through selection and not on the basis of seniority-cum-merit - Karnataka Rules 1976 are applicable for this purpose, and not Karnataka Civil Services Rules 1977, which are general rules, while the former are special rules - Amendment in Rule 3(2) of 1977 - Rules will not supersede the 1976 Rules, which are special rules while former are general rules.
[Paras 1, 17, 14 and 38] B Karnataka Civil Services (General Recruitment) Rules, 1977, Rule 3(2) - Non obstinate clause - Two sets of rules are in place for recruitment - General rules and Special rules - Amendment made in General rules will not supersede Special Rules despite the insertion of non obstinate clause in General Rules - There should be a clear indication in the amended rules to supersede the Special Rules.
(Majority view of Kuldip Singh and K Jayachandra Reddy, JJ Yogeshwar Dayal, J dissenting) [Paras 10,11 and 9] C Karnataka Civil Services (General Recruitment) Rules, 1977, Rule 3(2) - Amendment in General rules for recruitment - Insertion of non obstinate clause - It will not abrogate the earlier special rules unless there is clear indication to that effect.
