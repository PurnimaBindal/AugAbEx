Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Seniority - Promotions - Inter se seniority of promotees and direct recruits - New posts of Clerks sanctioned in addition to the posts already existing - Direct recruits fill up posts already existing - Promotions made against existing vacancies with retrospective effect disturb seniority of direct recruits - Retrospective promotions set aside by High Court - Promotees retire during appeal in the Supreme Court - Authorities directed to give them notional promotion for all monetary and pensionary benefits.
