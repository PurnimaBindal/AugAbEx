Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Sections 49 and 115 - Jurisdiction - The defendant had a preliminary issue of jurisdiction decided against them by the Civil Court.
Furthermore, a revision against this decision was also dismissed by the District Judge.
Despite this, they did not challenge the order in revision or the decree.
As a result, any objections regarding jurisdiction were deemed not maintainable in execution.
[Paras 10 and 11]

Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Sections 3(q)(i) 28 - Service matters - Pension - Family pension - The widow of an ex-employee's claim for family pension under the Family Pension Scheme is not considered a service matter under Section 3(q)(i) of the Administrative Tribunals Act.
Despite this, the jurisdiction of the Civil Court to entertain suits for Family Pension is not prevented U/s 28 of the Act.
