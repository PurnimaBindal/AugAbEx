Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Departmental proceedings - Delay - Charge sheet - Charge sheet cannot be quashed solely due to delay unless it negatively impacts the accused - Whether or not the delay is prejudicial is determined by the facts of each case - No set rule exists to determine prejudice - However, if the delay is lengthy and unexplained, the court may infer prejudice if it is likely to hinder the accused's ability to defend themselves during the inquiry.
