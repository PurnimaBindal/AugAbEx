Constitution of India, Articles 16, 309 - U.P.
Dependents of Govt.
Servants (Dying in Harness) Rules (1974), Rule 5 - Compassionate appointment - An applicant who was not the legally wedded wife of the deceased employee cannot claim the benefit of compassionate appointment under the Rules.
She does not fall under the definition of "family."
The son of the legally wedded wife of the deceased is entitled to claim the appointment instead.
