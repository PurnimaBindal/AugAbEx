According to Punjab Civil Services Rules (Vol.
I) Part I, Rules 813 and 821, the petitioner had requested to convert his earned leave to commuted leave, but his claim was rejected without any explanation.
The petitioner argues that the authority was obligated to provide a speaking order and that the claim is based on established laws and rules.
The fact that the petitioner questioned the order after three years should not lead to the dismissal of the writ, as no decision was made due to the lack of a speaking order.
It is also stated that informing an employee cannot be considered as a decision.
