Constitution of India, Article 234 - Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (Regulation of Procedure and Conduct of Business) Act, 1974 (as amended in 1976), Section 51 - UP Nyayik Sewa Niyamavali, 1951, Rule 19 - Judicial Officers - Selection of - The objective of the scaling system was to eliminate potential injustice resulting from inconsistent marking practices among examiners - Section 51 of the 1974 Act and Rule 19 of 1951 Rules of U.P.
Nyayik Sewa complement each other without conflicting (19) - The Commission has the power to draw on any proven method of selection to carry out a just and fair selection - Expert opinion on selection procedures, such as the scaling system used by the U.P.P.S.C., should not be interfered by the court - The scaling system employed by U.P.P.S.C.
was deemed valid.
