Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - Grant of - The petitioner, who joined the military as a sepoy in 1965, was denied disability pension after he met with an accident while on leave and was found to have a disability of over 25% by a Medical Board.
Despite the fact that he was on leave when he suffered the injury, as per the cited judgment in 1999(3) SCT 55, the respondents have been directed to provide the petitioner with disability pension.
