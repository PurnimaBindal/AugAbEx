Constitution of India, Article 235 - Control of Subordinate Judges by High Court - Committee of five judges constituted by Full Court recommends dismissal of Subordinate Judge for misconduct - Committee's recommendations treated as those of High Court - Governor's dismissal order not violative of Article 235.
[Paras 4, 7 and 8]
Constitution of India, Article 235 - Majority decision of committee of judges - Four judges present recommended punishment against Subordinate Judge - Absent member's views not considered - Committee decision accepted by Governor is legal.
[Para 8]
Constitution of India, Articles 136 and 141 - Dismissal of Judicial Officer - High Court's recommendations to Governor based on material presented - Court will not reevaluate evidence to replace committee's decision on misconduct.
[Para 16]
Constitution of India, Article 217 - Judges and their status - Judges are not government servants but holders of public office - Their role is to exercise sovereign judicial power.
[Para 10]
Judges/Judicial Officers - Corruption in Judiciary - Every judicial officer's conduct must be above reproach and not influenced by personal interests - Oversight by High Courts is necessary to limit corruption and inefficiency in subordinate judiciary - Nepotism and apathy often enable corrupt officials.
[Paras 10 to 15]
Constitution of India, Articles 235, 311 and 14 - Enquiry Officer and Bias - No allegation of bias made at start of enquiry against Enquiry Officer - Allegation only made in response to final show cause notice after enquiry completed - Attempt to undermine Enquiry Officer's report.
[Para 16]
Corruption in Judiciary - Judges should refrain from commenting on irrelevant matters for decision-making even if they have strong opinions - Corruption hinders efficiency.
