Punjab Wakf Board Act, 1954, Section 61 - Punjab Wakf Board Regulations, Regulation 24 - Transfers of Wakf employees/Jurisdiction - If an Imam's appointment letter doesn't mention transfer liability, the transfer order cannot rely on the appointment order.
[Para 8]

Punjab Wakf Board Act, 1954, Section 61 - Punjab Wakf Board Regulations, Regulation 24 - Transfers of Wakf employees- Jurisdiction - The Secretary of the Board cannot make appointments and transfers of Class II employees without the approval of the Chairman or the Administrator.
[Paras 9 and 11]

Punjab Wakf Board Act, 1954, Section 56 - Notice - Notice is mandatory under Section 56 of the Act only when the challenge is to an act done by the competent authority under the Act or Regulations.
If a transfer order has been passed by an incompetent authority, notice U/s 56 is not necessary.
[Paras 10 and 11]
