Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 13.2A - Withdrawal of officiating rank - The petitioners were fulfilling duties one rank higher than their substantively appointed rank under the "Own rank and Pay" status of ASI/SI - The rank granted to the petitioners was withdrawn due to a major punishment that they did not actually receive - The impugned order contained incorrect factual information - The petitioners were not subjected to any punishment (major or minor) after being granted the higher rank - The order violates Articles 14 and 16 and is set aside.
