Constitution of India, Article 226 - Examination/Education - Revaluation - Criteria for revaluation - Court's authority to interfere with the criteria laid down by academicians - University policy states that benefit of revaluation will only be awarded if the difference between original and revalued marks is above 10% of maximum marks - Petitioner's argument is that since the difference is only 9 marks, it should still be considered a significant difference - However, the authorities in an academic matter have to establish a limit somewhere - The 10% line established is considered reasonable.
[Paras 2, 5, 8 and 9]
