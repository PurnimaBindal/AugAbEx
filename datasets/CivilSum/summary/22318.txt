According to the Constitution of India, the Supreme Court has the power to pass orders in the interest of justice or to correct inconsistencies, even in cases that are pending before other courts.
However, this power cannot override the substantive law or the rights of the litigants.
Orders passed under Article 142 without notice to some affected persons are not necessarily invalid, although the affected person can make their case to correct any prejudice.
The petitioner in this particular case failed to show any prejudice.
This ruling confirms the observations made in a previous case regarding conflicting judgments and unfair advantages in the workplace.
The petition is therefore dismissed.
[Paras 2, 12, 13 and 18 to 23]
