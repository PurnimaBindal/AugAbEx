Section 10 of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 46 of the Factories Act, 1948, Rules 93 to 99A of the Karnataka Factories Rules, 1959, and Rules 3 and 25(v)(a) of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Karnataka Rules, 1974, allow for a notification prohibiting the employment of contract labour in the canteen (Industrial) in factories employing 250 workers and above.
A general notification under section 10 of the Act abolishing contract labour from a class of industries can be issued.
However, the exercise of powers under Section 10 would fall under "Conditional Legislation" and not "delegated Legislation," thus restricting the scope of judicial review.
The impugned notification is based on intelligible and discernible criteria forming a reasonable classification, and it is not over-inclusive, as establishments with 250 or more employees are a separate category, and they are statutorily required to run canteens.
