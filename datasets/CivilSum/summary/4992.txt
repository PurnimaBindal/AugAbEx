The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226, the Electricity (Supply) Act of 1948, Section 19, and Regulation 8 framed by the Karnataka Electricity Board govern the appointment, probation, and selection process of employees.
Physical endurance testing is also prescribed, and the Board must set minimum qualifying marks for the test.
The selection process is not deemed arbitrary or malicious, and the petitioner was not considered because they failed to achieve the minimum marks required in the endurance test.
As a result, the Court sees no illegality in the non-consideration of the petitioner and will not interfere.
[Paras 7 and 8]
