The petitioner's claim for pension was rejected due to incomplete service and short war engagement, but the certificate of his service revealed that he had served for more than 15 years and was transferred to the pension establishment upon discharge.
The rejection of his pension claim was unfounded and set aside.
[Para 5] B.
Despite approaching the court after 36 years and records being destroyed, the petitioner's discharge certificate shows that he had served for more than 15 years and had been transferred to the pension establishment.
The respondents have not suffered any prejudice due to the delay and cannot reject the claim for pension based on laches.
