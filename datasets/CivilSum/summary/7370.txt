Indian Police Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1955, Regulations 5(4) and 5(5) - Promotion - Disciplinary or criminal proceedings - Proceedings are only considered pending once a charge-sheet has been issued or charges have been framed in a criminal proceeding, not before - The mere contemplation of proceedings cannot hinder the promotion of a government employee.
[Paras 8 & 9] B.
Appointment - Selection - Expiry of period of select list - If a selectee is entitled to appointment, the appointment cannot be denied even if the period of the select list has expired - The authority cannot benefit from its own mistake by claiming that the select list has expired.
