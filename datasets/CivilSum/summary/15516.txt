Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Punjab Municipal Act, 1911, Section 38(6-A) - Demotion - Natural Justice - The petitioner was promoted to the post of Chief Sanitary Inspector after approval from Deputy Commissioner on 7775, but was later offered the lower post of Sanitary Inspector without his consent after the services were provincialised by Punjab Government on 9.11.1979 - The respondent did not comply with rules of Natural Justice, and the petitioner suffered a loss of Rs. 50/- pm - This action violates Section 38(6-A) and is discriminatory as a person junior to him was appointed as Chief Sanitary Inspector - The respondent must treat the petitioner as Chief Sanitary Inspector from the date of provincialisation of his services.
