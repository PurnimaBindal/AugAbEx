Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Withholding of Result - The petitioner's result was wrongfully withheld by the University even though they had issued a Roll Number to him and allowed him to take the examination.
The University must declare the petitioner's result as it was arbitrary to withhold it.
[Para 10] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Withholding of result - The University decided to withhold the petitioner's result after the examination and claimed that he was not eligible for admission to BA Part III course.
However, the petitioner had already passed BA Part II in 1988 and the University's position in the WS was entirely different.
The University was not clear on whether the petitioner had to appear in BA Part II or BA Part I and neither the order of the University nor the communication of the decision by the College can be sustained in law.
It was held that the University could not withhold the petitioner's result as they had permitted him to take the examination.
