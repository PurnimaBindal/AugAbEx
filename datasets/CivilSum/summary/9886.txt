Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Employment Exchanges (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959 - Adhoc appointment - Termination - The petitioner was appointed as peon for a period of 89 days on purely adhoc basis - Following the court's directive, the corporation requested the Employment Exchange to refer names of eligible persons for consideration for appointment to the posts of peons - However, the provisions of the aforementioned Act did not apply to the posts of peon, therefore, the reference was not required by law - The employer can make selections from any eligible candidates from any source, including those sponsored by Employment Exchanges - The petitioner was interviewed along with the candidates who were already working on an adhoc basis with the corporation and selected candidates were appointed - The petitioner's service was terminated as he was not found suitable for regular appointment - The respondent's action was legal.
