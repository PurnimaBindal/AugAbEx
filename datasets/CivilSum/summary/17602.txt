Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 14 - Medical reimbursement for treatment of heart problem - Respondents refused to reimburse room rent charged by AIIMS for staying and post-operative care - The policy decision is being challenged as invalid, as there are no reasonable grounds for declining reimbursement of the room rent charged by AIIMS - The interpretation is completely negative and does not consider the ground realities or the objective of medical reimbursement - The clause declining reimbursement for diet, stay of attendant, and stay of patient in hospital or any other place is arbitrary and violative of Article 14 - The directive is to strike down Clause (vii) as ultra vires and pay room rent to the petitioner.
