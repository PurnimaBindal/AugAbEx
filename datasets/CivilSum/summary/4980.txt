Border Security Force Act, 1968, Sections 20 and 21 - Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 177 - Removal from service for absence from duty - Natural justice - Following the procedure prescribed in Rules 20 and 21 of the BSF Rules is mandatory if the competent authority concludes a personnel is unfit - The delinquent must be afforded full opportunity after a show-cause notice, even if they choose not to reply - The issuance of a show-cause notice is essential - If no record shows that the notice was sent and served upon the delinquent and the further procedure was not followed, the punishment order cannot be upheld.
