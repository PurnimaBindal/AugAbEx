Constitution of India, Article 311 - Border Security Force Act, 1968, Sections 22(e) and 40 - Dismissal from service - A writ petitioner who was attacked by militants while on duty had disciplinary proceedings initiated against him for violation of provisions of the Border Security Force Act, 1968.
The petitioner was dismissed from his service, but the order of dismissal passed under Section 22(e) and 40 of the B.S.F.
Act 1968 was deemed unsustainable since the charge levelled against petitioner that he did not retaliate cannot be treated as misconduct.
The findings of the disciplinary authority based on no evidence, and the order of the Single Judge quashing the order of dismissal was upheld.
[Paras 10 to 15]
