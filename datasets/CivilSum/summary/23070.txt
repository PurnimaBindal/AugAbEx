According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, adverse remarks in ACRs should be graded based on work performance and temper/behavior.
Any additional remarks that are contrary to an officer's consistent track record should be ignored, especially if they are for a small service period.
If an officer uses hard language in their representation against such inconsistent remarks, the Tribunal and the Court should not be prejudiced.
However, government servants should still restrain themselves from using intemperate and abusive language.
[Paras 20-24]
