Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Seniority on transfer on condition that employee shall lose seniority - It is held that eligibility for promotion cannot be confused with seniority, and an employee at a permanent post acquiring permanent status cannot be excluded from consideration for promotion despite being at the bottom of the seniority list at the transferred place.
The transferred employee does not lose their chance of promotion and shall be considered but cannot claim seniority subsequently.
