Regarding a change in the order of absorption, a petitioner had previously been absorbed as an LDC and was later ordered to be absorbed as a Sahayak Parcha Vitarak.
The petitioner possessed the necessary qualifications for the LDC position and the Municipal Board passed a resolution to absorb them as such.
The government granted approval on reference.
However, the respondents argue that surplus employees were required to be absorbed on equivalent positions per policy decision, despite a clear vacant post of LDC being available.
The respondents' argument is questionable due to the large number of employees who have been absorbed on higher positions and the availability of the LDC position.
The order of withdrawal passed without notice or hearing violated principles of natural justice, displayed evidence of hostile discrimination (particularly in comparison to how Sub-Nakedars were absorbed as Gram Sewak), and is illegal.
[Paras 10, 13 to 15]
