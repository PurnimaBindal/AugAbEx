Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Essential qualification - The Haryana Government denied appointment to the petitioner because they did not recognize their Art and Craft diploma from Punjab as equivalent to one from Haryana.
The advertisement did not mention this stipulation, and similarly situated candidates were appointed.
The petitioner's representation was also rejected.
It was held that a candidate's eligibility must be determined as of the date of form submission.
[Para]
Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Recognition of essential qualification - It was held that a candidate's eligibility must be determined as of the last date of application submission.
[Para]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Recognition of diploma/degree - The decision to recognize a qualification falls under the domain of expert bodies, and the administrative or executive authority's decision is subject to certain conditions.
It was held that the decision should be made by competent authority with proper application of rules and that it should normally only be effective prospectively.
Additionally, individuals who already possess the recognized qualification from another university or state should not normally be denied further education or employment opportunities.
[Para]
