Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Post of Principal - GOMs specifies that the post of principal should be filled up by the recruitment of suitable lecturers with 10 years of service.
In the event of insufficient lecturers, direct recruitment can be conducted.
On the proposal of Teachers' Association, Government has issued clarification to limit the zone of consideration to three senior-most lecturers, in order to curtail arbitrariness in appointments of Principals.
The clarification issued by GOM does not have any illegality.
