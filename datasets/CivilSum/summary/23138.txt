Constitution of India, Article 311(2) Proviso (b) - Evidence Act, Sections 25 and 26 - Confession - Departing from the process of enquiry - Dismissal from service based solely on confession without departmental enquiry - Confession obtained in departmental proceedings can be relied upon by competent authorities to make decisions on whether or not an employee is suitable for continued employment - The strict rules of evidence and proof do not apply to departmental proceedings - An employee's confession of involvement in terrorist activities before the police must be scrutinized by the competent authority to determine its voluntariness and truthfulness - If due to police involvement in terrorist activities, it is infeasible and unreasonable to carry out departmental proceedings, the decision to conclude and take action against the confession is valid - As long as the decision includes sufficient material to support the conclusions made, the resulting punishment cannot be considered unreasonable or excessive - Dismissal from service is upheld.
[Paras 8,10 and 11]
