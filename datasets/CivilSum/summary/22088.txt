Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 3, Item 1 - Industrial Dispute - Sastry Award - Paras 602, 603 and 604 - Canteen facilities to employees of Bank - The Sastry award does not mandate State Bank of India to provide canteen facilities by running a canteen.
[Para 26] B.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 3, Item 1, Section 2(s) - Industrial Dispute - Employees of canteen run by Local Implementation Committee cannot claim to be absorbed as employees of Bank - The canteens run by Local Implementation Committees are non-statutory and non-recognised canteens.
The appointments of the canteen employees are not governed by any rules framed by the SBI and they are not under the control of State Bank of India.
The appeal has been granted.
