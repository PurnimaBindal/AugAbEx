According to the Punjab Cooperative Societies Act of 1961, an order of termination cannot be appealed if there is no statutory provision allowing it.
Any order given by a jurisdiction outside of their power is invalid, and merely participating in proceedings does not constitute consent to jurisdiction.
The Assistant Registrar is only allowed to refer disputes to the Labour Court for adjudication and is not authorized to hear appeals by way of Bye Laws.
An order made by the Assistant Registrar is therefore without jurisdiction and will be set aside.
