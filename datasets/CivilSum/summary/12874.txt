Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and latches - Pay scale - A petitioner promoted in 1988 as a Superintendent who reached superannuation in 2003 filed a petition in 2004 claiming higher pay scale than the one given to him - The petition was dismissed on the ground of delay and latches.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equation of pay - Personal pay - Promotion - If another employee in the same cadre or post has been promoted from a higher post and carried with him his personal pay obtained as ACP, resulting in fixation in lower pay, the latter cannot claim parity with the former's pay merely because both were holding the same posts.
