Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Service of Engineers Public Works Department (B&R) Class II Rules, 1965 (as applicable to Haryana) Rule 6 Promotion/Qualification of AMIE - In 1981, the Petitioner was appointed for a short term vacancy for six months and later acquired the required AMIE qualification for promotion to the post of Sub-Divisional Officer within the allotted quota - Despite becoming entitled to be considered as the top candidate and a permanent vacancy being available, the Petitioner was reverted in 1995 while other candidates were promoted - The promotion of other candidates and the consequent revertion of the Petitioner was bad - The Court issued a direction to treat the Petitioner as S.D.E.
with all benefits of salary and seniority - The Petitioner was also awarded costs of Rs. 1000/-.
