Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compulsory retirement - Proportionate assets to known sources of income - The Appellant served as a peon in the office of a Sub Divisional Controller, Food and Supplies, charged with discrepancies in assets - Upon an enquiry, the employee was found guilty and compulsory retirement was awarded as punishment.
The appeal was rejected.
The appellant contended that the disciplinary authority was biased and charges were vague.
The court held that there was no evidence to suggest that the disciplinary authority conducted the enquiry with a closed mind or showed bias.
The Enquiry Officer duly considered the evidence from both parties, analyzed information, and applied correct legal principles.
Petition dismissed.
