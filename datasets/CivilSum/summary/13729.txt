Based on the Constitution of India, Articles 21, 14 and 16, in cases of compassionate appointment, a priority list is established based on the date of receipt of complete applications.
Applicants who submit incomplete applications and are required to complete them will only be given a priority date based on the resubmission date of the complete application, not the initial submission date, unless there are allegations of mala fide return.
[Para 12]
Following the Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 26, if no vacancies are available in the quota for compassionate appointment, no appointments can be made and names may be removed from the priority/waiting list after three years.
In the absence of any allegations that sufficient vacancies were available, the authority is not obligated to provide details of such records.
