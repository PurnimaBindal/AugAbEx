Constitution of India, Article 19 - Right to freedom of speech - Demonstrations - Question - What distance should demonstrations be held by unions from premises - Union's right to hold demonstrations can only be protected by allowing them to hold demonstrations 100 metres away from premises - This right is not absolute or in the nature of free for all.
