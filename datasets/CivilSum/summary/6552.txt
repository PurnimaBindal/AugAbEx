The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Resignation - Election - An employee of the Government, who resigns to contest an election, cannot demand to be reinstated in the same post he resigned from - In the case of the petitioner, who contested for both Sarpanch and M.L.A.
and never withdrew his resignation, he is not entitled to be reinstated in the same post - While he may be entitled to participate in open selection, it cannot be assumed that his resignation was not accepted.
