Constitution of India, Article 226 - Central Industrial Service Force Rules, Rule 54 - Central Industrial Service Force Act, Section 9 (as amended in 1999) - Disciplinary proceedings - Superior Officer in exercise of suo motu power of revision set aside the penalty, ordering a de novo enquiry for the petitioner - The subsequent de novo enquiry was held without remixing the matter or passing a reasoned order, and the punishment of pay reduction was passed without giving the delinquent an opportunity to be heard - The order cannot be sustained.
[Paras 13, 14 and 15]
