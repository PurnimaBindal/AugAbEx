Under Punjab Civil Services Rules, military service can be counted towards civil pension benefits.
In the case of the petitioner, who was appointed to PCMS in 1949 and received the benefit of military service rendered during war time, a break in service of more than one year was condoned.
The petitioner cannot be asked to get the break condoned again for the purpose of receiving pension benefits, nor can he be required to refund any gratuity paid to him.
It was not justified to ask the petitioner to refund the amount of Military Service Gratuity and to get the break in service condoned for granting military service benefits, as this was already recognised by the appointing authority under statutory rules.
This claim cannot be rejected as time-barred.
[Paras 19 to 25]
