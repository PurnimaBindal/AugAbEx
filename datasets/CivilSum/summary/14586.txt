Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10(1) (d) - Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 - Res judicata - Injunction - A lawsuit seeking an injunction to prevent the management from terminating the services of a worker who has been dismissed as infructuous is not barred by a reference under Section 10(1)(d) challenging the termination under the Industrial Disputes Act, even if the management has already produced termination orders before the injunction was issued.
