Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules 1930, Rule 49 - Distinction between Dismissal and Removal - Removal is exit from service that does not disqualify from future employment - Dismissal is exit from service that generally disqualifies from future employment.
[Para 3]  B Constitution of India, Article 14 & 16 - Natural justice - Petitioner previously dismissed from previous employment - Information not revealed to subsequent employer - No right to opportunity of hearing - Inherent disqualification cannot be defended even with opportunity provided - Natural justice is not necessary if it serves no useful purpose.
