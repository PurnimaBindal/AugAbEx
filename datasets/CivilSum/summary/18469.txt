According to Section 30 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, when a workman is injured during the course of employment, attendance at work for a few days after the accident cannot be used to deny the claim for compensation.
[Para ]  B.
The convenience of the injured workman is given precedence over that of the employer when filing a claim petition under Section 30 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, allowing the injured party to file the petition in the jurisdiction in which they temporarily reside.
