Railway Servants (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, Rules 9(21) - Railway Vigilance Manual (Manual), Paragraph 704 and 705 - Departmental trap - Tribunals must not interfere with the order of punishment - The appellant was found guilty of overcharging a passenger and was penalized with a reduction in pay and rank - However, the trap was not conducted according to the Manual and the Enquiry Officer acted as a prosecutor, not as an independent quasi-judicial authority - This rendered the departmental proceeding invalid - The appeal was allowed and the judgment of the Tribunal was restored.
[Para 27] B.
In the decision-making process of departmental proceedings, the Tribunal is entitled to evaluate the sufficiency of the evidence presented by the department to support a guilty verdict of the accused officer.
