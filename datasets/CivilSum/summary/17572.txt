Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 14 and 16 - Pay scale - A petitioner had taken technical courses in missile stream that exceeded six months while serving in the Air Force Aeronautical Engineering Branch on Short Service Commission Technical, which earned him technical pay - According to the Govt. of India, Ministry of Defence letter dated 1441982, the petitioner is not eligible for technical pay - The decision was made to withdraw the pay scale dated 30111992 on abolition of policy - Even though there may be no further situations where the pay scale would be granted, it is not right to treat the petitioner differently and deprive him of the technical pay - The court orders that individuals who have already acquired the qualification and drawing technical pay should not be deprived of it - Any deducted amount should be refunded to the petitioner.
