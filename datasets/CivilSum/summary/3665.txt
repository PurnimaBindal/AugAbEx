Haryana Compassionate Assistance to Dependants of Deceased Government Employees Rules, 2003 - Compassionate appointment - Petitioner's claim rejected - Rules do not allow appointment of dependant of medically unfit retired employee - Ruling of Supreme Court in Umesh Kumar Nagpal applies - Compassionate appointment is not a guaranteed right but meant to assist family in case of sudden crisis due to death of sole breadwinner - As employee received retiral benefits, petitioner not eligible for compassionate appointment under 2003 Rules.
