Constitution of India, Article 19(1)(g) - Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Sites and Remains Act, 1958, Sections 18 and 38 - Govt. of India Ministry of Tourism and Civil Aviation, Department of Tourism Instructions, 1979, Clause 17 - The government can regulate the profession of guides who assist tourists in visiting monuments under Section 38, but cannot impose any age limit or place unreasonable restrictions on the profession.
Every citizen has the right to access monuments under Section 18 of the Act.
Clause 17 of the 1979 Instructions providing an age limit of 65 and refusal to renew the identity cards of guides is unconstitutional and ultra vires, violative of Article 19(1)(g) of the Constitution.
The judgment in JK Aggarwal as followed in Virender Kumar Chadha held "does not appeal to reason" and overruled.
Clause 17 is quashed.
