The Rajasthan Pensioners Medical Concession Scheme dictated that a patient's medical expenses would not be reimbursed if they did not seek the opinion of a Medical Board before going to AIIMS, New Delhi.
However, the court held that medical assistance is a fundamental right under Article 21, and it is the State's duty to provide it to its citizens.
In an emergency, procedures and laws become irrelevant.
The patient is entitled to reimbursement of medical expenses, regardless of where they received treatment.
As a result, the writ petition was granted, and the patient would receive Rs. 20,000/- in costs.
