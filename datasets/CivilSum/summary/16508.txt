Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Unequal treatment - Bias - Enrollment - Admission test - A petitioner requested to be excused from the entrance exam for PhD study and to be treated the same as groups who are exempted from the entrance exam - Certain groups are exempted based on a reasonable distinction that has a reasonable connection to the intended outcome - Lack of uniformity alone does not necessarily denote disparity - Justified categorization on a reasonable basis is acceptable.
[Paras 5 and 6]
