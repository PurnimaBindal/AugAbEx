Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - Examination - Petitioners, who were admitted to the B-Tech course on a first come first serve basis prior to the implementation of new criteria, were allowed to take all the examinations under the Court's orders and have already completed their studies.
Regularizing their admission would not have any negative impact on candidates or the institute, and there are still vacant seats available.
The Court directed the admission of the petitioners be regularized and their results declared.
