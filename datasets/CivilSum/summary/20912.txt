Constitution of India, Articles 141 and 136 - Seniority - Government's takeover of District Boards and Zilla Parishad-run schools - Previous service rendered by respondents from erstwhile teachers in District Boards and Zilla Parishad not counted for seniority or any other benefits - As per terms of takeover, they are to be treated as fresh entrants into Govt. Service - Thus, their previous service stands wiped out and they are not eligible for any benefits, including pension.
