Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Pension Regulations for the Army (Part-I), 1961, Regulation 173 - Appendix 2 (Entitlement Rules) Rule 7(b) - Disability pension - Entitlement to - The petitioner was enrolled in the Army as a sepoy/driver and declared unfit under medical category 'E' (EEE) due to Psychoneurosis, resulting in the decline of his disability pension on the basis that his disability was not attributed to nor aggravated by his military service.
However, according to the regulations, disabilities that result in discharge from service are presumed to have arisen during the service if no note of the disease was made at the time of entry in Armed Forces or at any subsequent stage.
Since there was no note about the petitioner's disability at the time of his entry in armed forces, it is deemed to have arisen during his service, and as such, the disease is attributable to his military service.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to his disability pension.
