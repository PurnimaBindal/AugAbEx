The government's cancellation of appointments of petitioners who had been guided by the `Spoils Systems' without following due procedure for recruitment was challenged.
The court held that appointments should be consistent with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution and that procedural safeguards could not be asked for.
Simply joining the post did not confer any right that did not exist before the order was passed.
Thus, the petition was dismissed.
