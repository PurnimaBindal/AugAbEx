Constitution of India, Article 226 - Ad hoc teacher - Regularisation OF service - Petitioner-respondent filed a supplementary affidavit requesting appointment under Dying in Harness Rules due to her husband's passing, but no prayer was made in the writ petition for a compassionate appointment.
The writ petition was not amended to incorporate such grounds and relief, so the court could not issue a direction for her appointment as a teacher on compassionate grounds.
The appropriate course of action would have been to direct the appropriate authority to consider her case for compassionate appointment, as the court cannot usurp the powers and functions of such authority under Article 226.
