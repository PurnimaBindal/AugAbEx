Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(b) - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.1 - Discharge from service - Dispensing with the enquiry - The punishing authority must show that there was material to justify dispensing with the departmental enquiry and satisfaction was based on objective material - An order to dispense with the departmental enquiry on the ground of impracticality must be supported by the authority - Despite being tried for the same charges and acquitted, services of the petitioner were dispensed with without a departmental enquiry due to alleged links with extremists and involvement in murders - Since the same charges could be tried in open court, a departmental enquiry between the delinquent and the enquiry officer was not impracticable - The order was quashed.
