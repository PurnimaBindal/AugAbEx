Punjab PWD (B&R Branch) (Selection Officers) Engineering Service Class III Rules, 1964, Rule 11 - Conditions of service - Seniority must be determined according to rule 11 - Petitioners are not in the position to argue their seniority has not been established - The department treated the petitioners as junior to all persons whose names had been recommended before the approval of the Board to change the allocation - Petitioners cannot argue that a common merit list had not been drawn up - Engineer-in-Chief rejected the claim submitted by certain persons in the representation - The department's action in treating the respondents as senior to the petitioners is just and fair and calls for no interference.

Delay in writ petition - Petitioners did not challenge the list in the earlier writ petition - The impugned list was issued in 1988, and the petitioners did not take any steps to challenge even this list for almost five years when they filed the present writ petition in 1993 - A suit against this list would have been barred by limitation on the day the present petition was filed - Interference under Article 226 is not possible - Writ petition dismissed on account of delay as well as on merits.
