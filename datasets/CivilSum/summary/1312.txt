Constitution of India Articles 12 and 226 - Central Administrative Tribunal Act, Sections 14(2) and 19 - `other Authority' - Writ petition - Council of scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) - The society must be incorporated as per the provisions of the Societies Registration Act and must be controlled, managed and administered by a council appointed and funded by the Govt. of India to be deemed an `other authority' under Article 12 of the Constitution.
CSIR is included in the Govt. of India's Notification issued under section 14(2) of Central Administrative Tribunal Act.
However, the petitioner cannot seek jurisdiction of the court under Article 226 of the Constitution without first exhausting alternative remedy available under section 19 of Central Administrative Tribunal Act.
[Paras 12, 15, 16 and 17]
