Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination - A non-petitioner had been employed by the petitioner since 1989 and had their services regularised by order dated 25.2.97.
Following the direction of Hon'ble Supreme Court in Dharmpal's case, they were given regular pay scale by order dated 15.8.96.
However, their service was terminated by order dated 3132000 as they were not eligible for regularisation.
Despite working with the petitioner for a long period from 1989 to 1999, it cannot be stated that they did not complete 240 days in a calendar year.
The order of Labour Court awarding compensation has no illegality.
