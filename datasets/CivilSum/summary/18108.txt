Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Release of Retiral benefits - If transferred and absorbed from one committee to another, can retiral benefits be received from the initial committee or the current committee?
In this case, the petitioner was transferred to Market Committee Kalanaur after initially joining at Jalandhar and is now requesting to collect retiral benefits from MC Jalandhar instead of MC Kalanaur.
The court dismissed the writ petition as without merit, but directed MC Kalanaur to release the retiral benefits within two months.
The petitioner was also directed to complete necessary formalities within 15 days.
[Paras 5 and 6]
