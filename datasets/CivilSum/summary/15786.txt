Constitution of India, Article 226 - Group Insurance - The petitioner's husband passed away while in service on December 1st, 1985.
He was absent from duty due to his illness starting from May 6th, 1985 until his death.
The 1985 Scheme went into effect on July 1st, 1985.
During the period the petitioner's husband was absent from duty, he was not entitled to benefits under the 1985 scheme.
Only after his death was he granted leave.
While making payment of his salary, an 80/- per month deduction was taken as provided under the 1985 scheme.
Since the petitioner did not request to be governed by the 1982 scheme, she is entitled to a lump sum of Rs. 96,000/- as provided under the 1985 scheme.
[Paras 6, 7, 8 and 11]
