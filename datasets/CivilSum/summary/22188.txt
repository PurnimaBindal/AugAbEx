Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Transfer - Public servants - The employer has complete authority to determine when, where, and at what point in time a public servant is to be transferred - Interference by the court should be avoided - The transfer order is based solely on administrative grounds, and is intended to prevent the transferee from interfering with an ongoing investigation - The order does not cause any harm, and is not subject to being overturned through writ jurisdiction.
