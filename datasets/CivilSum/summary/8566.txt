Constitution of India, Article 311 and Section 126(2)(c) (Constitution of J&K) - Dismissal - Inquiry - Judicial Review - Reinstatement - Petitioner was dismissed from service without inquiry, as the Governor deemed it not expedient for the security of the state - However, the impugned order can be subject to judicial review, with the court examining the material on which the Governor relied upon.
The sufficiency and relevancy of the material can be considered, as well as if the material would be prejudicial for the security of the state if put to an inquiry.
The court can determine whether the material against the petitioner was sufficient or irrelevant to terminate their services.
The satisfaction recorded by the Head of the State was wayward and mala fide.
Thus, the order is vitiated by law and not maintainable.
The petition has been allowed, with respondents directed to reinstate the petitioner into service with all consequential benefits.
[Paras 3, 17, 20 and 21]
