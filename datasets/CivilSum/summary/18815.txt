Constitution of India, Article 226 - Family Pension - The petitioner claimed that his son died on duty due to stomach pain at home while serving in the army.
However, the claim was declined as the death was due to a disease that was not linked to military service.
The respondent argued that a new policy permits the father of a soldier to receive ordinary pension regardless of when the soldier died.
The petition was resolved, and the petitioner was directed to receive the benefit of ordinary pension from 1.1.98 onwards.
