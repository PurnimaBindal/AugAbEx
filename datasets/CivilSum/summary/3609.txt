Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311(2) - Railway Servants (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1968, Rules 6 and 10(5)(i) - Inquiry report - Natural justice - The punishing authority must furnish the delinquent employee, who is to be penalized with Clauses (v) to (ix) of Rules 6, with a copy of the inquiry report and a show cause notice as this requirement is statutory, not just in compliance of principles of natural justice unless specifically waived by the delinquent - Orders for punishment, as well as appellate orders and orders of the CAT, were canceled with permission to proceed afresh if the authority decides - Petition was allowed with a cost of Rs. 10,000/-.
