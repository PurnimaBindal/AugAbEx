Under Section 6N of the U.P.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a daily wager appointed for a fixed period of three months is not entitled to any compensation upon termination of their service.
[Para 17]
In a case where an industrial dispute was raised after the termination of a daily wager, the High Court directed the employer to pay compensation of Rs. 50,000/- in lieu of reinstatement, as it was found that Section 6N of the Act had not been complied with.
