Punjab Public Relations Department (Class III) Non-Gazetted Service Rules, 1958, Rule 9 - Pay/Higher pay for higher post/Promotional post - The post of Assistant and Draftsmen serves as the feeder post for the post of Exhibition Assistant; therefore, the pay scale for the higher post, Exhibition Assistant, must be higher than that for Assistant and Draftsmen.
[Paras 7 and 10]  B Articles 14, 16 and 309 of the Constitution of India - Relationship between promotion post and feeder post - The promotional post must always be higher than the feeder post and cannot be equated to it; equating two unequal posts even for the purpose of pay scales would be unconstitutional.
