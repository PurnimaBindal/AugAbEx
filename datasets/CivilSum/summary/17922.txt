The promotion of a petitioner as Head Constable stands in violation of the principles of natural justice.
The petitioner must have earned increments during his 9 years and 4 months of work as a Head Constable.
The impugned order for reversion was against equity and must be quashed.
The order detrimented the interest of the Government employee, who has a right to know the circumstances that led to its passing.
No opportunity of being heard was afforded to the petitioner, and no reason for the reversion was recorded.
[Para 11] B.
The respondents' stand shifts on promotion challenged in earlier writ petition.
They had earlier taken a categorical stand based on provisions of Rule 13.8(2), which strictly governed promotions to the rank of Head Constables.
Since the promotion of the petitioner was found to be in order, it is beyond respondents' authority to state that the promotion did not follow the rules.
