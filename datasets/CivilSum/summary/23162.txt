Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, Sections 2(ee) and 5 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control & Appeals) Rules, 1965 - Model Standing Orders - Aim - Natural justice - Departmental proceedings - Co-worker representation in departmental proceedings - The objective is to provide uniformity in terms and conditions of same category workers - Service rules must specifically provide for representation by another person or a lawyer in departmental proceedings - The right for representation is limited to co-workers in the same establishment and is not contrary to the Model Standing Orders or unfair to vitiate the provision or proceedings - After amendment in 1956 Act, Standing Orders must be just and fair, and only members of the same union can represent workmen in departmental proceedings - Standing Order is not unfair.
