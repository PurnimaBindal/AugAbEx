Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(oo) and 25-F - Retrenchment - Abolition of post - Qualifications - If an unqualified workman's service is terminated due to the abolition of the post without complying with the Section 25-F procedure, it is considered retrenchment void ab initio - In case of abolition of post, the workman has the right to receive full back wages until the award date - Awarding compensation equivalent to only six months' salary is not reasonable.
