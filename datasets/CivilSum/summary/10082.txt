The Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16 and 226 dictate direct recruitment guidelines for the position of Sr.
Laboratory Attendant, which the petitioner was qualified for but denied appointment.
The appointment was denied on the grounds that this was a promotional post and not open to direct recruitment, but the relevant rule was not presented.
The judgement declared the order passed by DPI (Colleges) to be erroneous and arbitrary, thus quashing the order.
