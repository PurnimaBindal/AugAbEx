Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 9 - Specific Relief Act, 1963, Section 14 - Suspension - Domestic enquiry - Mandatory injunction - Jurisdiction of Civil Court - A lawsuit seeking an ad-interim injunction to restrain the employer from carrying out a domestic enquiry and to issue a mandatory injunction to revoke the order of suspension, pay salary and allowances for the period of suspension, and permit the employee to resume duty is not maintainable - The employee does not have the right to request re-instatement or any other relief while disciplinary proceedings are ongoing - The domestic enquiry process cannot be delayed.
