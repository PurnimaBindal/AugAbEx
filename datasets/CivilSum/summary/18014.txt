Article 226 of the Constitution of India deals with back wages/reinstatement in the case of a terminated Secretary of a Co-operative Society.
The Registrar Co-operative Societies set aside the termination order, but denied back wages.
The employee filed a writ petition claiming back wages, and the only question is whether they are entitled to it.
The validity of the order of reinstatement cannot be challenged as the society has not done so.
[Para 6] B.
In cases where the Registrar had set aside the termination of the Secretary of a Co-operative Society, back wages cannot be denied once the termination is held illegal.
