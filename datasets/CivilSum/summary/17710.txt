As per the Constitution of India, Article 311, if an employee has already retired, disciplinary proceedings cannot be initiated against them and they are entitled to all terminal benefits due to them.
[Paras 4 and 5] B Similarly, if an employee has retired, the relationship between the employer and employee ends and the employer cannot proceed with any enquiry against the retired individual.
[Para 4] It is important to note that once employment comes to an end, the authorities have no power to proceed with any departmental enquiry.
This was established in the case of State of Assam v. Padmarain, AIR 1965 SC 473.
