Indian Railway Establishment Code, Vol. II, Rule 1316 - Promotion - Stepping up - If a difference arises between the pay of the promoted junior and senior, and the senior's salary is fixed to a lower amount, stepping up may occur under certain conditions.
To apply, both officers must belong to the same cadre and be promoted to the same and identical post and grade.
The principle of stepping up cannot apply to Mr. Sood, who was senior and in a different cadre of running staff, as he was fixed in a higher salary due to a higher salary in the feeder cadre.
