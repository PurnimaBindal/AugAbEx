Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Air Force Regulations 48/75 - Conditions of service - There are disparities in the service conditions between persons promoted from Airman up to Warrant Officer and granted Branch Commission with two years ante-date for their salary, and those who were similarly placed and granted Permanent Commission.
The Petitioner chose Permanent Commission, aware of its advantages, and had served the Air Force for almost eighteen and a half years before being considered.
The mode of promotion was introduced in 1975 to address stagnation in the service.
Permanent Commission is more prestigious and can lead to promotion until the rank of Air Marshal, while Branch Commissioned Officers can't go beyond the Squadron Leader rank.
The Authorities are directed to review and remove grievances by comparing the service conditions applicable to the two types of commissioned officers, Branch Commissioned and Permanent Commissioned officers.
