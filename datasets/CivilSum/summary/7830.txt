Army Rules, 1954, Rules 177, 179, 180 and 181 - Preliminary enquiry - Fact finding enquiry - Second fact finding enquiry - There is no restriction on conducting a second fact finding enquiry to gather facts and evidence - The official in question is not required to provide any explanation while conducting such an enquiry - Hence, there is no possibility of double jeopardy or prejudice to the official.
[Para 6]

Army Rules, 1954, Rules 177, 179, 180 and 181 - Preliminary enquiry - Fact finding enquiry - Transfer during enquiry - Attachment to another Brigade during enquiry is not the same as transfer - Such administrative orders do not justify interference by the court.
[Para 7]

Constitution of India, Articles 226 - Administrative orders - Departmental proceedings - Challenging enquiry proceedings on various grounds of defects - Such challenges cannot be entertained until the conclusion of the proceedings - However, it is possible for the delinquent to challenge the orders of the punishing authority on these same grounds.
[Paras 7 & 8]
