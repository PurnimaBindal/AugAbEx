Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ of Mandamus - Appointment on Compassionate ground - Late claim - Around 29 years after the event - Frivolous petition - Appointment permitted to save family from financial crisis when the sole bread earner dies - Claim for compassionate appointment loses its importance if raised late - Writ petition misconceived and utterly frivolous - Petition dismissed.
