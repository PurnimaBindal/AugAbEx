Haryana Co-operative Group C Service Rules, 1980, Rule 11 - Seniority - Officials appointed prior to the enforcement of amended/new rules would have their seniority governed by the provisions of the Rule applicable prior to such Rules, as the rules are not retrospective - In this case, seniority of officials appointed prior to 1980 would be governed by Rule 6 of Punjab Cooperative Subordinate Services Rules, 1936.
[Para 4]
Punjab Cooperative Subordinate Services Rules, 1936, Rule 6 - Seniority - While the rule provides seniority be determined by age if all things are equal, it is not violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution as merit prepared by selecting agency loses its significance after appointment, and passing of departmental examination and successful completion of training is required for appointment - Therefore, Rule 6 of 1936 Rules is not in violation of Articles 14 and 16.
