Constitution of India, Article 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(p) and 18 - Settlement - The Industrial Tribunal modified the terms of settlement, directing that 3% of the amount shall be paid into the pension fund of each workman according to their claim instead of being deducted and paid to the Trust.
This decision was not in line with the best interests of the labor class as a whole.
The High Court has the ability to intervene under Article 227 in such cases, with the guiding principle being to favor workmen against a workman rather than a workman against workmen.
Thus, the Industrial Tribunal's decision was quashed and set aside.
