The appointment process considers various factors, but there are no specific marks allocated for each head.
The Interview Board assesses administrative ability and knowledge based on the candidate's service record.
However, the service record is not presented before the Board, resulting in potential lost marks for this category.
The High Court found defects in the selection process, including the lack of a service record for a direct candidate and a failure to follow service rules and regulations.
The Commission did not challenge the High Court's findings on these issues, and as a result, the selection cannot be upheld.
