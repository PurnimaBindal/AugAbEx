Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Bank service - Selection - The number of vacancies advertised for filling in clerical cadre posts in 28 Public Sector Banks was reduced by the government of India.
Accordingly, the Recruitment Board made selection as per the revised indent sent by banks in line with the guidelines issued by the government of India.
The banks are not compelled to fill up all the posts advertised, and their actions in not making selections according to advertisement is neither arbitrary nor violative of Article 14 of the Constitution.
The applicants participating in the selection process do not have any legally enforceable right vested in them, and the inclusion of their names in the selection list does not provide a right for appointment irrespective of the existence of vacancies.
The action of reducing the number of vacancies without notifying the petitioners is not violative of the principles of natural justice.
The writ petition is not maintainable on the ground of non-joinder of necessary parties, with 28 public sector banks and the government of India being necessary parties that were not impleaded.
