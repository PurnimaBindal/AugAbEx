Delhi Education Act, Section 8 - Delhi University Ordinances, XVIII, Clause 9 - Suspension of Principal - The Managing Committee cannot suspend the Principal without the prior approval of the Vice-Chancellor as it would be without jurisdiction - There is no provision for "administrative leave" in any rule, and being forced to take such leave unwillingly counts as suspension.
[Paras 13 to 15] Jurisdiction - Suspension - The Rules state that the Principal can only be suspended with prior approval from the VC - The Managing Committee cannot order the Principal to take "administrative leave" without prior approval for suspension - If a rule specifies a certain manner of action, that manner must be followed - Activities that cannot be done directly cannot be accomplished indirectly.
