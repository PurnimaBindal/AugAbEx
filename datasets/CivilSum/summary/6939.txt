Constitution of India, Article 226 - Recruitment and Leave Rules, Rule 14(a) - Compassionate appointment - Time Limit - Rule 14(a) states that application for compassionate appointment must be submitted within two years of employee's death.
Appellant's application was made after the prescribed period, although appellant obtained required qualification.
It is not reasonable to grant compassionate appointment after a lapse of time.
Therefore, claim for compassionate appointment was rightfully rejected and there is no illegality.
