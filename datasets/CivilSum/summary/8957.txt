Industrial Disputes Act, Section 33-C(2) -Scope-Stoppage of increments - According to settled law, Section 33-C(2) proceedings before a Labour Court are execution proceedings and the workman must have a pre-existing right for enforcement in order to make a claim - If there is a question of entitlement, it must be adjudicated in the competent court of jurisdiction - The employer's order of stoppage of increments was ignored by the Labour Court and arrears of salary were paid, which was without jurisdiction.
