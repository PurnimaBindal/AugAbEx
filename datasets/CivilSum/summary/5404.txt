Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4 - Compensation - A cleaner of a lorry sustains injuries during employment - Medical evidence shows the applicant is physically disabled by 20 to 25% - The evidence also shows the applicant is unable to perform his previous duties - Despite the physical disability being less than 100%, the applicant has lost earning capacity by 100% - The Tribunal's decision to assess the loss of earning capacity at 70% is incorrect - The compensation awarded must consider the loss of earning capacity to be 100%.
