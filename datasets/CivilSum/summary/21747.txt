Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rules 37 and 48 - Pro-rata pension for services rendered in Government - Eligibility for absorption in a Corporation, company or body - Rules 37 and 48 are to be read with terms of absorption - The pro-rata pension date is based on when the employee would have been eligible for voluntary retirement in Government service or the date of permanent absorption in the corporate sector, whichever is later.
The employee is not eligible for pension prior to that date.
