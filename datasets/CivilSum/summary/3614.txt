Constitution of India, Article 16 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(a) - Compassionate appointment - In the event of a government servant dying on their last day of retirement, it will be considered that they died in service and their dependents may be eligible for compassionate appointment.
However, such appointment cannot be claimed by the deceased employee's son and is not a right to succeed to property on account of death.
In this case, compassionate appointment was not given as the family was not put in sudden crises.

Constitution of India, Article 16 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(a) - Retirement of government employees - As per the rules, a government servant may retire on the afternoon of the last day of the month in which they attain 60 years of age.
The word 'afternoon' is to be interpreted as the time from 12 o'clock till the end of the day, i.e., 12 pm Therefore, in the case of a government employee who passed away at 710 pm on the last day of their retirement, they are considered to have died while in service.
