Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Dismissal from service/Non-speaking order - The petitioner's services were terminated with a non-speaking order and no evidence produced by him was considered.
The order of dismissal was not supplied to him and no counter affidavit filed to the allegations nor the alleged order placed on record by the respondents.
The petitioner is deemed to be continuing in service with full back wages, as the facts stated by him are presumed to be true.
[Para 9 and 17]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 311 and 226 - Absence from duty - Quantum of punishment - Dismissal from service on the charge of wilful absence - Punishment is disproportionate - The charge of wilful absence from duty does not attract the extreme penalty of dismissal, and when punishment is grossly excessive not commensurate with the charge proved, the High Court must interfere in the interest of justice using its powers under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
[Paras 11 and 16]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Natural justice/Copy of inquiry report - The non-supply of a copy of the inquiry report amounts to a violation of principles of natural justice and vitiates the punishment order.
[Para 10]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Dismissal from service/Non-speaking order - The punishment order of dismissal from service must be a speaking order based on reasons.
