Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Absence - Punishment - The appellant with 18 years of service was terminated due to one month of unauthorised absence from duty - Despite the Appellate Authority's lenient view of reinstating the appellant as a fresh appointment, it was not considered whether this punishment differed greatly from determining if the punishment was grossly disproportionate to the misconduct - The case was remitted to the Appellate Authority for further review.
