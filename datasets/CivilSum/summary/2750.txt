As per the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2, Item 6, a probationer whose work is unsatisfactory can be terminated if they have completed one year of probation but have not been confirmed.
In this case, the appointment order stated that the probationer's services would automatically be terminated if not confirmed after the probation period.
The termination based on unsatisfactory work was deemed valid.
