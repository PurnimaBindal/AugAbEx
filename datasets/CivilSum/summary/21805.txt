Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pension - The appellant was temporarily appointed on 20.11.1968 and their services were terminated on 15111974 with no challenge made to the order.
They were then appointed on an ad hoc basis which was also terminated, with no challenge made to the order.
Following a gap of three years, the appellant was appointed on daily wages and later transferred as T-Mate.
On 811991, the appellant challenged the termination order of 1974 and was granted relaxation in age by the board.
The first termination order was withdrawn for that limited purpose.
A petition was filed claiming seniority, but no relief was given.
However, the pre-regularisation period of the third appointment was directed to be treated as a regular one for the purpose of pension, etc. [Para 7]
