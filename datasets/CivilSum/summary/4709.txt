Madurai University Act (33 of 1965), Section 8 - Duties - Scope - Proceedings initiated by Registrar of University - It is determined that the Registrar can initiate action against a university employee when directed to by the Syndicate.
[Para 10]
Madurai University Act (33 of 1965), Section 8 - Enquiry proceedings - Sub-committee formed - A delinquent submitted his explanation to a sub-committee - no objection was initially raised against it - the report was unfavourable - It is inappropriate for the petitioner to question the constitution of the Committee at this stage since the plea was not raised earlier in proceedings- waiver thus applied.
[Para 13]
Madurai University Act (33 of 1965), Section 8 - Misconduct - Act of plagiarism - Order of demotion passed by Syndicate - An act of plagiarism can be seen as misconduct and dereliction of duty, leading to the lowering of the University's image - the demotion and reduction in basic pay is, thus, justified - Petition dismissed accordingly.
