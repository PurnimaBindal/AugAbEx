The petitioner was working in Bidar in Karnataka when the alleged misconduct occurred, but the impugned order was issued in Mumbai.
However, since the disciplinary enquiry was held in Secunderabad, which falls within the territorial jurisdiction of this Court, this Court has the authority to adjudicate the matter since disciplinary proceedings are a substantial component of the cause of action.
[Para 2]
The petitioner was charged with alleged misconduct for refusing to part with documents in possession without acknowledgement.
The charge is considered irrational and misconceived, and no penalty can be enforced from such allegation.
The impugned order is clearly erroneous and therefore must be invalidated.
[Para 4]
