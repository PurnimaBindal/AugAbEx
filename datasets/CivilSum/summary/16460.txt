Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order, 1950 - For OSP, meaning Outstanding Sportsperson, who reside in Haryana, a certificate of reservation from the competent authority of the State of Haryana is required - Since the petitioner does not possess a certificate indicating that they belong to the Scheduled Castes notified for the State of Haryana by the President, they are not eligible for the post of Lineman reserved for Outstanding Sportspersons belonging to Scheduled Castes.
