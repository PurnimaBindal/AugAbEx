Punjab Civil Medical (State Service Class I) Rules, 1972, Rule 6(i) - Age relaxation - Direct recruitment to Punjab Civil Medical Service Class I - The government has the authority to relax the age limit for direct recruitment to PCMS Class I To prevent individuals from becoming ineligible due to government delays in filling a position on a regular basis, the government's instructions dated July 28, 1972 state that the benefits of the period of ad hoc service should be given to ad hoc position holders.
In addition, this ensures that the government is not denied the knowledge and experience gained by incumbents who have previously held such positions.
