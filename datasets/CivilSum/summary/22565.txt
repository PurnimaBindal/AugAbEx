Constitution of India, Articles 136 - Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations 1971, Regulations 54, 58 and 60 - Minor penalty - Dispensing of enquiry - Minor penalty of monthly instalments recovery from employees who have caused loss due to negligence or breach of order - Relevant regulations state that conducting a regular departmental enquiry is at the discretion of the disciplinary authority, to be decided on a case-by-case basis, and its misuse or arbitrary use is subject to judicial review - High Court was incorrect in interpreting the regulation as mandating an enquiry in cases where the employee denies the charges.
