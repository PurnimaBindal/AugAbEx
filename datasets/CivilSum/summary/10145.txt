Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 & 226 - Pay fixation - Pay parity - According to petitioner, persons who are claiming to be junior have been treated as seniors to him because of their service duration - Such juniors have been given pay and other benefits by Govt.
Instructions - Petitioner isn't entitled to any pay fixation relief, but respondents can’t take back extra payments made to the petitioner.
