Smt. Laxmibai & another v. Manager M/s Vijayanand Roadlines Limited - Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 (Central Act No. 8 of 1923) - Sections 4 and 4-A (As amended by Amendment Act No. 30 of 1995 with effect from 15-9-1995).
Death of workman prior to amendment - The compensation payable is determined by the amount on the date of the accident and not the amendment made.
Therefore, amendment of Sections 4 & 4A enhancing the compensation cannot be operative retrospectively.
