Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Disciplinary proceedings - Termination of services - The petitioner was charge-sheeted and an Enquiry Officer was appointed by the management.
The officer found the allegation of temporary embezzlement proved and the management recommended to terminate the petitioner's services.
However, the Director Higher Education Haryana disagreed with the proposal, but the management filed a revision and the Education Secretary approved it.
The impugned order was upheld as proper procedure was followed by the Inquiry Officer and it was not a case of "No evidence".
[Paras 2, 8 and 12]
