Sections 4, 4-A, 22, and 30 of the Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923 - Accident involving a vehicle - Claim made by labourer, cleaner, driver - Loss of earning capacity - The High Court concluded that the loss of earning capacity was 100% without providing any explanation - The injuries fall under Section 4(1)(c)(ii) - Loss of earning capacity cannot replace the percentage of physical disablement, but it is one of several factors to consider - The claimant's doctor noted the factors relevant to loss of earning capacity - Since the High Court did not give a basis for the decision, the order is not valid.
[Paras 7 and 8] B.
Rate of interest payable on award under Sections 4, 4-A, 22, and 30 of the Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923 - Date from which interest is payable - Interest is payable at a rate of 12% if the compensation is not paid within one month of when it is due, in accordance with Amending Act 14 of 1995 - The date of the accident cannot be the due date - The date of claim adjudication shall be regarded as the date on which the amount falls due.
