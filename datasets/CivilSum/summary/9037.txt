Constitution of India, Article 14 - Efficiency bar - An employee must have a sufficient justification to be stopped from crossing his efficiency bar - If no reason is given, the action is arbitrary - Petitioner to be allowed to cross his efficiency bar with all consequential benefits.
[Para 5]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Pension - Service book - The departmental authorities are responsible for maintaining the service book and other service records of an employee - No service rule or statute imposes such a duty on the Government servant - Pension cannot be delayed due to incomplete pension papers.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21(1)(f) - Pension - Right to property - After the 42nd amendment, right to property is a constitutional right under Article 300(A) - Denial of pension and retiral benefits without justification is a denial of their benefits without any authority of law.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21(1)(f) - Pension - Pension is a valuable right - Government is liable to pay interest at 18% if pension is delayed beyond two months from the date of retirement of the employee.
[Para 7]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Pension - Right to employment - Right to employment is part of the right to life - Denying payment of pension and other retiral benefits violates the employee's fundamental and constitutional rights - Employee entitled to 18% interest on arrears.
