The petitioner's father was earning Rs. 1400-2600 before his untimely death.
However, when the petitioner was granted a compassionate appointment, it was given in the pay scale of Rs. 750-940 instead of Rs. 950-1500.
The reason for this discrepancy was that the deceased was receiving a higher standard scale after completing 10 years of service.
However, the court determined that government instructions required compassionate appointments to be made at least one step lower than the deceased's pay scale.
Therefore, the court directed the consideration of the petitioner for an appointment carrying a pay scale of Rs. 950-1500.
