Administrative Tribunals Act, 1984, Section 19 - Selection - Merits - The selection committee must award marks for the selection process.
The Tribunal may only nullify the selection if it observed an apparent discrepancy in marks awarded to candidates.
In this case, the Tribunal erred in nullifying the selection only because the respondent was awarded 21 marks in viva voce instead of the 16 awarded to the applicant.
Deciding on the merit of candidates and marking is the responsibility of the selection committee and not the Tribunal.
There is no evidence to support the claim that the appellant deliberately manipulated the selection process.
The Tribunal's order is invalid, and the case should be reassessed by the Tribunal in accordance with the law.
