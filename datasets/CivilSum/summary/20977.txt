Constitution of India, Articles 164(4), 164(1), 177 and 175 - Appointment as of Chief Minister and Ministers - Rajya Sabha legislators as respondents - Disqualification?
No - Hold: (i) The appointment of Chief Minister and Minister, who are not members of the State legislature, is not illegal.
(ii) The appointment of a person as Chief Minister cannot be challenged on the ground of not being a member of the State Legislature at the time of appointment.
(iii) Article 164(4) is a restriction or disqualification for a non-member appointed as Chief Minister or Minister to remain in office without getting elected within six consecutive months.
(iv) A member of the Legislative Assembly can be appointed as the Prime Minister.
[Paras 5, 13 and 14]
