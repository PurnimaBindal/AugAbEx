Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation - Deemed to be service - An award was issued reinstating workmen with continuity of service and was not challenged - Therefore, it is presumed that they were in continuous service from the day they were retrenched - Since the workmen completed 3 years of service on 3111996 in compliance with the policy for regularisation, the respondent is directed to consider their regularisation as per the principles outlined in Avtar Singh's case, 2001(3) SCT 307 (P&H)(DB).
