Constitution of India, Article 16 - Extension of service period - Government order granting a two-year extension of service to teachers who received the State Teacher's Award - Petitioner received award after reaching age of superannuation - Petitioner not eligible for extension as it applies only when the incumbent is still in service and not after they cease to hold the post.
The term extension means lengthen or prolong, which is not applicable to retired individuals.
