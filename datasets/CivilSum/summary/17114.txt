The Constitution of India, Articles 226, 16 - Appointment - Cancellation - Validity - The appointments of Lower Division Clerks/Accountants have been cancelled due to irregularities in the selection process, with a cursory look at records indicating widespread malpractice and manipulation.
The petitioners were found to be involved in the illegal process of selection and cannot benefit from such activity.
Therefore, the order of cancellation and termination of services is proper.
The petition has been dismissed.
[Paras 6,8 and 10]
