The Inspectors of Cooperative Societies have petitioned the State Government for equal pay as Inspectors in the Department of Agriculture, alleging discrimination and violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
However, different job qualifications and functions separate these two categories of employees who fall under different sets of rules and are granted different scales of pay.
Therefore, there is no violation of the Constitution's provisions and the discrimination charge cannot hold.
[Paras 10, 13, 14] B. Equivalence of pay scales is up to the authorities to decide, and the court can only step in if there is dissimilar treatment between two individuals holding similar posts with comparable duties.
Inspectors of Cooperative Societies have no evidence that their functions are the same as those of Inspectors in the Agriculture Department.
Indeed, the disparity between the two categories of employees has existed for a long time, and the refusal to grant higher pay scales to petitioners cannot be seen as arbitrary.
Thus, the claim for parity with Inspectors in the Agriculture Department's pay scales cannot be sustained.
