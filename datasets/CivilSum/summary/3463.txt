Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection for promotion - Written test - Interview marks - Compulsory fixation of interview marks to obtain 45% of the total marks is deemed arbitrary - When selecting candidates for promotion or appointment from within a pool of already employed personnel, the requirement of obtaining a minimum of 45% interview qualifying marks can be seen as arbitrary - It provides the selection committee with the ability to exclude a candidate from selection by degrading their interview performance, without any means of recording the same - This policy is capable of being misused for wrongful exclusion - The imposition of a minimum 45% qualifying marks for interviews has been declared unjustified and arbitrary - The petitioner, having scored more than 50% aggregate marks with 38% in interview, has been appointed with all consequential benefits from the same date as the other candidates.
[Paras 10, 11]
