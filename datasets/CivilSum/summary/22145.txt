Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Limitation of interference in punishment of reversion - Inquiry officer's bias:
(a) The Labour Court lacks jurisdiction to apply Section 11-A if it finds that the inquiry was conducted fairly and adhered to the principles of natural justice, unless the punishment is grossly inadequate or involves unfair labour practices.
[Paras 9, 10 & 12]
(b) The Labour Court is unable to assess victimization, bias or unfair labour practices unless such allegations have been made - It is not authorized to substitute its own judgment for the management's.
[Paras 11 & 12]
