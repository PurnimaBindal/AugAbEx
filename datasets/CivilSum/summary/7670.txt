Backwages - Settlement - A settlement was reached between the management and workman before the Industrial Court, agreeing to receive 51% back wages as full payment.
There is no reason to question the settlement made with management at a rate of 51%.
The employee received the agreed amount and no objections were raised before the Industrial Court regarding the settlement being unfair.
Thus, there should be no interference.
