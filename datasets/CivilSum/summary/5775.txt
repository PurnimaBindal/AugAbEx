The appellant was appointed to the post of Superintendent on probation in accordance with the Central Warehousing Corporation (Staff) Regulations, Regulations 7 and 10.
However, an extended period of probation was imposed due to a lack of satisfaction with his work.
While the appellant was ultimately found not to be involved in a police case relating to theft, an overall assessment by the respondent revealed that his work was not found satisfactory.
The police case could not be considered as the sole basis for the order of termination.
The Court cannot re-assess the respondent's assessment of the appellant's work and performance, and the order of termination was not stigmatic in nature.
Therefore, there is no need for interference.
[Paras 10, 14 and 18]
