Industrial Disputes (Punjab) Rules, 1958, Rules 18 and 22 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 9, Rule 13 - An ex-parte award was issued by the Labour Court and the question is whether they had the power to reject the application for setting it aside - Despite attempts to serve the president of the petitioner, he was not available and the summons was affixed instead - The rules state that personal or registered post service is necessary for the first attempt - The Labour Court did not find it impractical to attempt personal or registered post service - Therefore, the Labour Court was not justified in proceeding ex-parte.
