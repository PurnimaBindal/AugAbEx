According to the Constitution of India, in Articles 14 and 16, lowering an employee's performance rating from "very good" to "good" cannot be classified as downgrading - the employee must receive only an adverse entry and no further action is necessary.
If the Countersigning Authority graded the employee's work as "good", then there has been no downgrading.
