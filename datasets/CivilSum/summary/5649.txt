Constitution of India, Article 226 - Locus Standi - Union - Association - Registration - When a senior bank officer allegedly obtained a civil court decree by fraud in order to change his date of birth, the Andhra Bank Officers Union and the Andhra Bank Depositors Welfare Association challenged the correction.
However, the petitioners were not registered under any Act or recognized by any competent authority as a legal entity in the capacity of an Association of individuals, and therefore did not have the norm of a juristic person.
As such, they lack locus standi and the order of the single judge holding the appellant a fraud was set aside.
[Paras 3 and 6]
