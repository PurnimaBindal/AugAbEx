According to the Punjab State Electricity Board Employees Punishment and Appeal Regulations of 1971 and the Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules of 1970, Rule 5(iv), stopping one annual increment with cumulative effect can be considered a major penalty.
However, if the penalty is imposed without conducting an enquiry, it is inherently unlawful and cannot be sustained.
This interpretation is supported by the case of 1990 (6) SLR 73.
