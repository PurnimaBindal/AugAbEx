Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14, 16 - Absence from duty - Joining report - The Executive Engineer issued notice to the petitioner to rejoin work within 7 days as the petitioner overstayed his leave - The petitioner joined within the period but the joining report was not accepted - As per the directions of the executive engineer, respondents were directed to allow the petitioner to work on resumption of duty - Disciplinary Authority would decide on the absence of petitioner according to the rules - Competent authority could take action for misconduct of absence in accordance with the law - The petition was allowed.
