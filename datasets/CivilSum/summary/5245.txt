The Payment of Bonus Act 1965, Section 2(13) and Constitution of India, Article 226 are involved in an argument about the ceiling on wages for entitlement to bonus.
The government frequently raises these limits.
The argument is that one may claim gratuity or compensation without a ceiling under relevant Acts.
The court holds that other statutes offer only external aid to interpretation.
For two Acts to be compared, they must be similar.
The aim is to prevent contradiction between two statutes dealing with the same subject.
The section 2(13) of the Payment of Bonus Act is Constitutional and legal because the ceiling limit keeps increasing over time.
