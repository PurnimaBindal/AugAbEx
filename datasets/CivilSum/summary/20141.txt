Railway Establishment Manual - Railway Board communication No. P(S) 443(I) Misc./MP/MAS/Vo.X - Recruitment - Grant of temporary status - Absorption in regular cadres - A qualified Technical Mate with diploma in technical subjects who attained temporary status in 1981 is entitled to be absorbed as a skilled Artisan in Grade III in the scale of Rs. 950-1500 against a post in the quota for direct recruitment, according to the Petitioner.
The rejection of this claim by the Chairman of the Board on the basis that all casual workers are in the same status, regardless of the posts held by them, is not justified and cannot be sustained.
The Chairman has been directed to fit the appellant in appropriate scale with all increments due.
