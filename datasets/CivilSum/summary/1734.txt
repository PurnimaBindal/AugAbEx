Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Damodar Valley Corporation Service Regulations, Regulation 12 - Termination simpliciter - An employee on probation can be terminated before their full term is served by way of termination simpliciter- Any such order of termination will not be seen as punitive or stigmatizing in nature - The order of termination was neither illegal nor irregular - Therefore, the High Court cannot interfere with it.
[Paras 14, 16 and 17]
