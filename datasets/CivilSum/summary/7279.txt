Constitution of India, Articles 309, 162 and 73 - Draft Rules - Service conditions - Executive instructions - In order for draft service rules to have legal power they must be notified or followed without exception by issuing office orders or instructions from a competent authority.
If there are no statutory rules or provisions to govern a specific area, the competent authority may issue executive instructions which will then have power over said area.
