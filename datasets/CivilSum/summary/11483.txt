Central Reserve Police Force Act, Sections 9, 10, 11 - Termination - Provision Applicability - A petitioner was charged with submitting a fabricated ST certificate to be enrolled - It was argued that Section 11 did not include termination as a punishment - However, the court held that the impugned order could have been passed under Section 11 even if the petitioner was not prosecuted under Sections 9 and 10 of the Act in view of Ram Bihari's case.

Central Reserve Police Force Act, Section 11 - Termination - Violation of natural justice principles - No assistance provided - It was held that since the petitioner did not specifically allege a violation of natural justice principles or the provision of assistance in the appeal or petition filed, such arguments could not be entertained - Furthermore, the CRPF Act did not require assistance to be provided to the accused in the investigation.
