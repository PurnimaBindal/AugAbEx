Constitution of India, Article 226 - Adverse remarks - Writ petition challenging adverse remarks of four years and rejection orders against them cannot be made in one petition; separate writ petitions against each cause of action must be filed - Every year of adverse remarks gives a separate cause of action - Long delay in filing petition against adverse remarks of a particular year renders the petition not maintainable.
