The appointment of teachers in India is governed by the Constitution of India, specifically in Articles 14 and 16.
There was an amendment made to the statute which prescribed minimum qualifications for teachers, and some candidates were terminated on the grounds that they did not possess these qualifications.
This termination was deemed arbitrary, and the amendment cannot have a retrospective effect.
Therefore, those terminated were reinstated with all consequential benefits.
[Paras 7 and 8]
