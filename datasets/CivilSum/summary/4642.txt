According to Rule 10(1) of the Central Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, a petitioner who has rendered more than 10 years of contiguous service is entitled to terminal gratuity, even if they are not entitled to other pensionary benefits.
However, the petitioner in this case, who was released from the Army in 1968 and joined the CRPF in the same year, but retired before the amendment to the rules on 20985, is not entitled to the benefit of their CRPF service towards their pensionary benefits as there was no provision for the absorption of retired Army personnel and they remained in temporary service.
