Punjab Civil Services (Judicial Branch) Rules, Rules 7 and 8 - Appointment - Petitioners, law graduates belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, are seeking enforcement of their right to equality of opportunity in the matter of appointment to posts in the subordinate judiciary in the State of Haryana.
The minimum marks prescribed for all categories of candidates, including scheduled castes, scheduled tribes, and general candidates, are at 55%.
Efficiency in administration is mandated constitutionally and the minimum percentage of marks must be determined on an objective and rational basis.
The government has been directed to determine a minimum percentage of marks consistent with efficiency and the need for ensuring equality of opportunity to scheduled castes and scheduled tribes and age relaxation is also to be considered.
The Constitution of India's Articles 14, 15, 16, 38, 234, and 309 are applicable.
