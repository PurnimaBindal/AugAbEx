According to the Karnataka Subordinate Courts (Ministerial and Other Posts) Recruitments Rules, 1977, as well as the Karnataka State Civil Services [Recruitment to Ministerial Posts] Rules, 1966, appointments made by the Chief Justice are illegal for posts of Second Division Clerks on the establishment of Subordinate Courts within the jurisdiction of the State Public Service Commission.
The High Court upheld the order quashing such appointments; however, on humanitarian grounds, the appointees are allowed to continue.
The appellants have been in service for the past 10 years, and due to their qualifications and promotions, they are over-aged for entry into any other service.
It would be an act of cruelty to ask them to appear for a written test and viva voce to be conducted by the Public Service Commission for fresh selection.
