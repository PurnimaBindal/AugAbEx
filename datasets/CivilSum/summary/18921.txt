Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Transfer - Repeated transfers - Transfer policy - The policy of transfer after two years of an employee's stay is an administrative matter - Instructions regarding transfer do not have statutory force - They are only executive instructions - Employees on transferable posts do not have the right to stay in a specific location - A transfer order cannot be invalidated on vague allegations unless there is compelling evidence of misconduct or extraneous influence.
