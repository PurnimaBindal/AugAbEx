Article 16 of the Constitution of India was applied to the Rajasthan State Agricultural Marketing Service Rules, 1986, which contained Rules 4, 6, 11(iv) and 17, specifically pertaining to Recruitment and Appointment.
On November 5th, 1993, the Public Service Commission advertised for the recruitment of Assistant Directors (Service) and Assistant Director (Junior).
However, after the State Government requested the PSC, the Rules were amended on April 19th, 1995, abolishing these posts and introducing a new hierarchy.
The newly-created posts were advertised and the previous vacancies no longer existed.
Despite these changes, the recruitment process did not involve any illegal practices.
