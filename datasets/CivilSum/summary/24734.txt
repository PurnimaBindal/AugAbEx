Constitution of India, Article 311 - Adverse Entries - Compulsory retirement - Validity must be assessed based on the record, particularly for the period leading up to the order - An employee who remained under suspension and had adverse entries from 1980 to 1982, as well as an order withholding three increments and strictures passed by the Court against him cannot rely on a solitary good entry from 1985 as a decisive factor - the decision was "No." [Paras 7 and 8]
