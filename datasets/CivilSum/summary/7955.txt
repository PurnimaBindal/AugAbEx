The petitioner was dismissed from service after being convicted by a Judicial Magistrate for unauthorized absence in violation of sections 9(f), 10(p), and 12(1) of the Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, and Article 226 of the Indian Constitution.
The court determined that no enquiry or opportunity of hearing was required for a person dismissed on grounds of conduct that led to their conviction under Section 12 of the CRPF Act and Article 311 (2) (a).
Therefore, the petitioner's appeal and revision were dismissed.
The petitioner was convicted of desertion from duty and sentenced to simple imprisonment till rising of the court.
Consequently, the petitioner was dismissed from the service under sections 9(f), 10(p), and 12(1) of the Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, and Article 226 of the Indian Constitution.
Even though the petitioner argued that the punishment imposed was excessive, the court held that it would not interfere since the petitioner was part of a disciplined force and was declared a deserter.
