Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 38 - Constitution of India, Article 21 - Invalid Pension - Temporary incapacitation - A constable who received treatment for hypertension was referred to medical boards and experts several times, and the medical experts declared the constable fit for active duty.
However, the authorities passed an order under Rule 38 that held the constable permanently incapacitated for holding duties.
The Learned Single Judge allowed the claim of the constable, holding that permanent incapacitation is mandatory for invoking Rule 38.
The action of the appellant was found to be suffering from grave legal infirmity in light of Article 21, which provides the right to continue in service.
The appellant had no jurisdiction to take a contrary decision against the advice of the medical experts.
The appeal was dismissed accordingly.
