According to Section 3(1) of the Workman's Compensation Act, 1923, compensation must be provided in case of an accident resulting in the natural death of a workman while on duty.
In this case, the workman fell down while taking a meter reading, and the doctor's evidence suggests that the deceased died due to cerebrovascular haemorrhage, a condition that can be caused by stress and strain at work.
Therefore, the appellants are entitled to compensation.
