Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pay scale - Petitioner appointed as Plumber in pay scale of 950-1500 - Petitioner argues that pay scale of plumber in advertisement notice was displayed with a grade of 1200-2040 - Respondents contend that the higher grade was a result of inadvertence and clerical error - The presence of an inadvertent error in displaying the wrong grade does not entitle the incumbent to claim higher grades that do not actually exist.
