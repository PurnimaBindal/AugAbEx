Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Released Emergency Commissioned Officers (Reservation of Vacancies) Rules, 1971 - Reservation/Seniority/Military service benefit - The above rules were framed to compensate emergency commissioned officers for opportunities they might have missed out on by choosing to serve in public services during times of national crisis - In accordance with these rules, a certain percentage of reservations were made for them in Central Civil Services and their relative seniority was determined by their services in the military - This benefit was available only to those officers who were appointed against the reserved vacancies, and not to those who joined through the general category - The court cannot create a right to this benefit for those in the latter category as they belong to a different category - The function of providing benefits to specific categories is that of the executive, and the court's role is to examine the rationality of such a policy, not create one.
