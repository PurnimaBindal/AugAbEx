According to Sections 50 and 15 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, recovery of poppy husk was involved and the accused gave his consent to be searched before a Gazetted Officer.
However, a search before a Magistrate was deemed illegal.
The Investigating Officer was required to conduct the search before a Gazetted Officer, but the provisions of Section 50 were not met.
Therefore, the conviction has been overturned.
