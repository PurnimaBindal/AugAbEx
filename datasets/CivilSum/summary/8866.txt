Appeal/Revision/Resolution - The filing of a revision against an interlocutory order is not the same as an appeal against the final order, and it is not necessary to pass a fresh resolution for the former as is required for the latter - A revision petition filed by the Registrar on behalf of the University against an interlocutory order in a suit without a fresh resolution is allowed.
[Paras 8 and 9] B.
Civil Procedure Code, Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 - At the interlocutory stage, a petitioner cannot be granted a relief that is available once the matter is disposed of - If a relief requested is in violation of the statute of the University, no court order should be passed to violate it - The trial court erred by showing unwarranted sympathy to the plaintiff by directing a provisional result to be given to him - The order is set aside and proceedings in the suit are expedited.
