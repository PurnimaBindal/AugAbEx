Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation of services - Break in service - Illness-related absence of a daily wage worker does not count as a break in service for continuous service computation for regularisation under Haryana Government Policy - Absence beyond the worker's control cannot be considered a breach - A break would refer to the employer releasing the worker or the worker deserting the employment - Illness-based break cannot be used to deny a daily wage worker's legitimate claim to regularisation - In accordance with instructions, Respondents are ordered to regularise the petitioner's services.
