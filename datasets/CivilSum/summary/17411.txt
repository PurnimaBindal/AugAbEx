As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 30, in Aided Colleges, the Director of Higher Education is responsible for granting sanction for creation of new posts, not the State Government.
If the University allows the college to introduce new courses and the Director's nominee participates in the selection of lecturers, resulting in the Director's recommendation for approval and sanction to the Government, it is deemed and implied sanction.
No sanction from the Government is required.
The Director is instructed to release the grant-in-aid for the posts with arrears.
