Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Section 7 - Recovery - House building loan - The petitioner did not use their house building loan for its intended purpose and the amount became recoverable with interest - The authority's decision to withhold the entire salary and only pay five rupees a month is deemed arbitrary - Instead, the authority is directed to make a deduction of 50% of the monthly salary drawn.
