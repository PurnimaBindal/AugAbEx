Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Exams - Unfair means - Natural justice - Personal hearing - A B.Sc.
student was found using unfair means during an exam and was apprehended by a flying squad official - The student claims to have found a slip and was handing it over to the class supervisor, but was caught - The student was debarred for three semesters and the appeal was dismissed - The unfair means committee asked the student to cross-examine the flying squad official but the student refused - It was held that a full opportunity was afforded to the student and no prejudice was caused.
[Paras 6 to 8]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural justice - Opportunity to be heard - Personal hearing - It is not always necessary for a personal hearing to be granted when principles of natural justice require an opportunity to be heard before an adverse order is passed on any appeal or application.
