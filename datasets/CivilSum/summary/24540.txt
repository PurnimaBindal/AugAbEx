Constitution of India, Article 14 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(j) - Date of Birth - Correction of date of birth at retirement - Employees cannot contest or ask for corrections to date of birth at the time of retirement to claim further retention in service, after receiving the benefits of the original entry or even later change - The workman accepted the initial entry based on medical assessment and the revised assessment which provided him with an extension of two more years - In the absence of authentic proof of birth date, he cannot challenge the date of birth, and he is estopped from doing so.
[Paras 25, 26, & 29] B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Scope of interference in Labour Tribunal's award - The writ court has the power and jurisdiction to re-evaluate and re-appreciate the evidence and reach its conclusions if the award suffers from an apparent perversity on the face of the record, ignoring material evidence or based on mere conjectures and surmises.
