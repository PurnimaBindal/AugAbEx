Constitution of India, Articles 12, 226 - State - Automotive Research Association of India - According to Article 12, the Association does not fall under the category of authority, agency or instrumentality of the State - Govt. doesn't hold entire share capital or pervasive control in administration or finance - Association doesn't have a monopoly status - Its functions are not closely related to Govt. functions - Just because the Association was established by Govt. of India and receiving some financial assistance from Govt. but not solely dependent on it does not fulfill the requirements of Article 12.
