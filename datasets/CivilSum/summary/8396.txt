Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Regularisation - Petitioners appointed on daily wages on muster rolls without any appointment order during the years 1988 to 1994 - Services not regularised - Govt.
Instructions state that daily wagers who complete 240 days of work in a year without any break in service for more than 30 days at a time shall be considered for regularisation - Petitioners fail to fulfil this condition - Hence, they are not entitled to have their services regularised.
