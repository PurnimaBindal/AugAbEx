Constitution of India, Article 226 - Retiral benefits - Interest - Principles of Res judicata - A petitioner who had previously sought directions for reconsideration of regularisation of service and payment of retiral benefits, including interest, was not granted relief with regard to the claimed interest rate of 24% pa - Due to the principle of res judicata, the petitioner's claim for interest relief is barred - Furthermore, since the cause of action for claiming interest had already arisen when the earlier writ petition was filed, the relief for interest cannot be granted.
[Paras 5 and 6]
