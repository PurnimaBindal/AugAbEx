The Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 311 - Army Act, 1950, Section 120 - Army Rules, 1954, Rules 33(7), 34(1) and 149 - Court-martial - Summary Court-martial - Compliance with the rules - Delinquents need to be given at least 96 hours after the service of a charge-sheet - Any tentative charge-sheet issued before the final one cannot be considered - Breaches of these rules will render proceedings as vitiated - Such breaches cannot be considered mere irregularities which may be protected under rule 149.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 311 - Army Act, 1950, Section 120 - Army Rules, 1954, Rules 33(7), 34(1) and 149 - Court-martial - Summary Court-martial - Compliance with natural justice - Delinquents have the statutory right of defence assistance of their choice - They must be promptly apprised of it - However, the authority nominated defence assistance of its own choice, without the accused's consent and in breach of Rule 129 - These proceedings were held to be vitiated as a result.
