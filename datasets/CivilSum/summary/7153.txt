Constitution of India, Article 226 - Cochin University of Science and Technology Act, 1986, Sections 3(22) and 22 - A Head of Department of Physical Education appointed to supervise all sports activities in the university is considered a "teacher".
The duties entrusted to the petitioner are more in line with that of a "teacher", despite not being a Professor, Reader or Lecturer.
Therefore, he is entitled to continue in service until the age of 60 years.
