Constitution of India, Article 311 - Appointment and Punishing Authority/Competent authority - Enquiry/Show cause notice - Only the appointing and punishing authority prescribed in the service rules can initiate and appoint an enquiry officer to conduct a departmental enquiry against an employee - No other subordinate authority can exercise this power unless specifically conferred - Any proceedings initiated by an incompetent authority are invalid and non-existent - The show cause notice issued by the competent authority based on the enquiry report from an authority with no power is also flawed.
