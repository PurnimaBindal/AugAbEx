Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Haryana Legislative Assembly (Allowances & Pensions of Members), Act, 1975, Section 7-A - Pension - The petitioner, who served as a member of the Punjab Legislative Council from 1926 to 1930, is entitled to the benefit of pension under the 1975 Act.
The provisions restricting benefits to those who served after the Constitution's coming into force are not rational or reasonable in light of the goal to be accomplished.
