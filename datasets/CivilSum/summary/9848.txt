Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II Rules 4.19(a), 317-A and 4.19(b) - Pension - Qualifying service - Resignation without permission - An employee of Central Government who resigns without prior permission and subsequently takes on an assignment with the State Government after an eight-year break in service for professional reasons is not entitled to count service with the Central Government towards qualifying service when retiring from the Haryana Government.
Rule 419(a), not Rule 317-A, applies in this instance.
Furthermore, it was held that Praduman Kumar Jain's case was distinguishable based on the facts.
