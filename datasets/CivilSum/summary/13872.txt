Section 30 of the Workmen's Compensation Act (8 of 1923) establishes that findings of fact regarding the death of an employee during the course of employment must be based on proper appreciation of evidence and cannot be disturbed by appeals under this section.
Such findings in this case are not shown to be perverse in any manner.
[Para 11] B Claimants are entitled to receive interest at a rate of 12% per annum on the compensation amount if it is not deposited within one month of the employee's death while discharging official functions.
This is outlined in Section 4-A of the Workmen's Compensation Act (8 of 1923).
