Constitution of India, Article 12 - Society - Writ petition against - Maintainability - Society not constituted under an Act - State has no direct or indirect control over affairs of Society for deep and pervasive control - State is not majority share holder - State has power to nominate only one director - According to Pradeep Kumar Biswas case, since respondent does not meet any of the tests laid out, the High Court was correct in concluding that Society is not a State within the meaning of Article 12 of the Constitution - Additionally, no violation of Act or rules has been shown in terminating services of appellant - Appeal is dismissed.
