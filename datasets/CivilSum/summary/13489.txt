Constitution of India, Article 226 - West Bengal (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, Rule 7(1)(a) - Suspension - The trial court's decision to set aside the order of suspension was deemed illegal on the grounds that the charge-sheet was not issued immediately after the order of suspension.
The appellant was not given an opportunity to issue the charge-sheet within a certain time frame.
No limitation on issuing a charge-sheet within a specific time frame is prescribed under this rule.
The appellant was directed to issue a charge-sheet within three weeks; otherwise, the order of suspension will be revoked.
Therefore, the order of the trial court was set aside, and the appeal was allowed.
