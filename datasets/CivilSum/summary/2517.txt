Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Haryana Service of Engineers Class-II Rules, 1970, Rule 9 - Promotion - Ranking list - Natural justice - Petitioner contests his exclusion from the promoted officials' list due to the violation of Articles 14 and 16 and the Rules of 1970 - Impugned order is claimed to be contravening natural justice principles since petitioner was not issued a show-cause notice before excluding his name from the order - Petitioner and similarly situated persons have been promoted to the posts of SDOs since 1974 but do not feature in ranking list of 1992, indicating that they are to be considered promotees of years after 1992 - The government was under obligation to issue notice and consider their representations before passing the final order regarding changing the year of their promotion - The ranking list excluding the petitioners' names/changing their promotion year is a nullity as far as petitioners are concerned.
