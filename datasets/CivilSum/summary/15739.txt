Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Seniority - Calculation of - A petitioner appointed as a Chemistry lecturer for a permanent position - The petitioner was confirmed as an employee after completing her probationary period, with an order extending her probation on November 12, 1986 - In contrast, the fourth respondent only completed his probationary period on November 26, 1986 - The fourth respondent cannot be considered senior to the petitioner - The Director of Higher Education's decision cannot be upheld - It is invalidated - The petitioner is declared senior to the fourth respondent.
