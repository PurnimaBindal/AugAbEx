Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 86(3) - Removal of police constable from service for willful absence from duty - The police constable was accused of being absent for 37 days from 2003 to 2005, but only 24 days of absence were proven in the enquiry - As per Rule 86(3) of service rules, the allegation of continuous absence from duty beyond 30 days was not established - Therefore, the punishment of removal was not appropriate - Instead, a penalty of withholding 3 annual grade increments with cumulative effect was imposed.
