Under Section 2(oo) of the Industrial Disputes Act, the removal of a workman's name from the rolls due to absence from duty is classified as retrenchment - if this occurs, Section 25-F must be followed.
B Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act specifies that the removal of a workman's name from the rolls due to absence from duty is retrenchment and must comply with this section.
C. In accordance with Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, an offer of retrenchment compensation must be made to an employee - instructing an employee to clear their accounts does not qualify as such an offer.
