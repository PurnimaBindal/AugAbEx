Constitution of India, Article 226 - Granting service element of disability pension - Petitioner enlisted in the Army on October 27th, 1948 - Discharged from military service on November 2nd, 1958 due to disease of "Contracture Right Elbow (Hysterical)" - Petitioner's disease was deemed attributable to military service - Disability assessed at more than 20% at time of discharge - Disability pension composed of service element and disability element granted for 20% disability from November 3rd, 1958 to September 14th, 1960 - Disability pension was not extended thereafter as C.C.D.A.
(P) reduced disability percentage to less than 20% - Service element of disability pension terminated as of November 7th, 1949 - The granting of the service element of pension was denied on the grounds that the petitioner had not completed 10 years of minimum service - It was ruled that the petitioner was entitled to the service element of pension for life at the rate determined from the date it was terminated - Dalip Singh v. UOI, CWP No. 2611 of 2003 was referenced [Paras 2, 9, 10 and 11].
