Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical bills - Reimbursement of - Indoor/outdoor patient - Petitioner's son was diagnosed with Leukemia and received treatment at Sir Ganga Ram Hospital - The petitioner submitted three bills for reimbursement but only one bill was considered as the other two bills included expenses incurred while the patient was an outdoor patient - The outdoor treatment was closely connected to the indoor treatment and could not be separated - Therefore, the outdoor treatment was considered a follow-up and integral part of the indoor treatment and must also be reimbursed.
[Paras 3 & 4]
