Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Promotions/Supersession - Petitioner ignored for promotion while his junior was promoted under old rules - Authorities applied new rules, which were not applicable during the junior's promotion, to reject petitioner's case - Denying petitioner promotion by applying new rules is arbitrary - Petitioner must be considered for promotion as his junior was considered on the same date.
