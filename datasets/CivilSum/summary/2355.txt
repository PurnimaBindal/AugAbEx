Constitution of India, Article 226 - Amalgamation of banks - Scheme framed - Interference in - The judicial review of schemes framed under Section 45 for amalgamation is limited - Employees of transferor banks are not entitled to a hearing as there is no change in their conditions of service - The Reserve Bank's decision on the equivalence of experience is final - Interference by the High Court in writ jurisdiction is only allowed in extreme cases of arbitrariness or unreasonableness.
