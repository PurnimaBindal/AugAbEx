Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Pension Regulations 173, 181 and 183 - Disability pension - A discharged petitioner known to have suffered from epilepsy disease was assessed with a 60% disability - Petitioner's fall from an Army tank which resulted to head injury led to the declaration of being unfit to serve the Indian Army - The disease is said to be attributable to Army Service - Court orders the release of petitioner's disability pension, with the arrears benefit limited to 38 months only.
