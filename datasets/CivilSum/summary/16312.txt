Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 2.2(b)(2) - Charge-sheet/Delay/Laches - Allegations of irregularities dating back to 1974 - Charge-sheet issued in 1987 - Petitioner retired in 1988 - Charge-sheet not served during employment - Published in The Tribune dated 3.10.88 after superannuation - The promotions earned by petitioner went up to Superintending Engineer - Rule restricts the initiation of proceedings after the lapse of 4 years of the incident date - The charge sheet has been dismissed - Ordered to receive retirement benefits with 12% interest within two months - Incurs costs of Rs. 1,000/-.
