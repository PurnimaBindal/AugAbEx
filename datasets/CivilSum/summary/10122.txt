The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vo.
I, Part-I, Rule 410 - Advance increments - The authority is required to examine the facts of each case before granting advance increments - In cases where the authority's inaction or lapse resulted in loss to the employee, the authority must exercise its discretion under Rule 410 and grant the benefit from the date the disparity began, including seniority and additional increments.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Recruitment - Reservation - A person who is eligible to apply under the reserved category must also be allowed to apply under the general category simultaneously, unless specific prohibition is prescribed - The person's application shall be considered under both categories.
