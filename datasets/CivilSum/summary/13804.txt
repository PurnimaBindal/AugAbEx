Constitution of India, Article 21 - Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 47 - Termination of services on account of disability - The 1995 Act prohibits termination of services on account of disability under section 47.
It is mandatory to comply with this section unless a specific government notification exempts a particular department.
If a disabled person is terminated in violation of Section 47, it cannot be sustained.
The government notification issued after the termination of a disabled person does not have a retrospective effect.
