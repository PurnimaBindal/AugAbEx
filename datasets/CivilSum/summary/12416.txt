Constitution of India, Article 311 - Kerala Police Departmental Inquiries, Punishment and Appeal Rules, 1958, Rules 15 and 17(b) - Minor punishment - Enquiry - A full-fledged enquiry is not necessary when only a minor punishment is considered.
However, the charge memo must contain specific allegations and the accused should be given a reasonable opportunity to explain their position.
