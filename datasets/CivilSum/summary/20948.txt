The Tribunal, when exercising judicial review jurisdiction under Section 19 of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, does not investigate the truth or correctness of claims made against an employee, unless those claims are perverse and unsupported by evidence.
The Tribunal's jurisdiction is similar to that of the High Court under Article 226 of the Constitution.

According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India, the initiation of a misconduct inquiry can be done by an officer who is subordinate to the appointing authority.
However, only the appointing authority has the power to issue dismissal or removal orders.
[Para 8]

The charges of misappropriation and falsification of accounts made against the employee were general and did not specify what role the employee played or what account he falsified or how much money was misappropriated.
The Tribunal normally calls for the authority to reinitiate the process with clear charges in such cases - Nonetheless, since the respondent was about to retire, the Tribunal upheld the punishment order quashing and the matter was put to rest.
