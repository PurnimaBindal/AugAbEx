Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226, 300-A - Discharge from service - Principles of Natural Justice - Pension - Respondent was discharged from service as a class IV employee in the Air Force.
His response to the Show Cause Notice was not considered.
Though he had served for 16 years at the time of the discharge order, he could have completed 20 years of service by 28.2.1993, to be eligible for earning pension.
As his case is pending in court for the past 9 years, he only wants to claim pension.
The discharge order was set aside, and the respondent was directed to be treated as retired from service from 28.2.1993 and accordingly paid his pension.

Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Discharge from service - Vexatious litigation - Exemplary cost - The single Judge awarded exemplary costs to the respondent against the appellants of Rs10,000/- for vexatious litigation.
However, the order was not sustainable due to lack of records supporting the single judge's view that the appellants were engaged in vexatious litigation.
