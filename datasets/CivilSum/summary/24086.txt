The University has the discretion to determine the eligibility criteria for promotion to the post of Professor of Marine Science, including the experience required in guiding research at doctoral level.
The approach taken by the University should be based on wisdom and intention rather than a technical or legalistic approach.
Even if the University requires pre-doctoral research experience as a condition of eligibility, this will not invalidate the selection process.
[Paras 26 and 27]
Candidates who participated in the selection process using amended qualification criteria cannot later challenge their selection on the basis of eligibility criteria.
[Para 21]
The Executive Council is authorized to empanel members for the post of Professor of Marine Science based on their preference.
If those preferred members are unavailable, the Academic Council may recommend alternative members without violating Statute 15.
[Para 29]
Bias may be defined as partiality or preference in a judicial or quasi-judicial decision-making process.
Not all forms of bias are unjustified or impact a fair assessment of merit.
Bias must be an unjustified state of mind or an influence that affects a fair assessment of merit in order to invalidate an action.
Proving a reasonable possibility of bias or circumstances of influence is necessary to challenge a decision.
