Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Andhra Pradesh Educational Institutions (Regulation of Admission and Prohibition of Capitation Fee Act, 1983 - Education Act, 1982, Sections 2(16), (18) and 21 - Educational Institutions - Public Interest Litigation - Reservation in admissions for weaker Sections of Society - A registered company formed Institute to teach Information technology (IT) under Andhra Pradesh (TA) Public Societies Registration Act without profit aims which does not receive Government aid - Institute not imparting education of the nature as defined in expression "educational institutions" under clause (c) of Section 2 of the Prohibition of Capitation Fee Act, 1983 - Institute is not covered by the definition of "educational institution" and so not bound to comply with the rules of reservation.
