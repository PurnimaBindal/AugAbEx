Constitution of India, Articles 300-A - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 2.2(b) - Pension - Prompt payment of retiral benefits - Government employees have the right to their retiral benefits in a reasonable timeframe unless there are pending departmental proceedings based on a charge-sheet.
An explanation memo doesn't qualify as a substitute for a formal charge-sheet.
The respondents must pay 18% interest on delayed payments for the period between 9 to 12 months.
