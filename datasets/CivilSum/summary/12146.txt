Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16(1) - Daily wage worker - Not regularised - Petitioner was denied work on the day when he would have reached 5 years of service and become eligible for regularisation, as per policy instructions - Court held that it was unfair to terminate the Petitioner's service and would violate Articles 14 and 16(1) of the Constitution - Following the principle established in Tek Chand's case, the Petitioner is entitled to be regularised with all consequential benefits.
