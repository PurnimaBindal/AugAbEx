Respondent RC Setia, Addl.
AG Haryana argues that under Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226, arrears of salary for claims regarding leave concession, medical reimbursement of bills, medical allowance cannot be denied due to delay, if some of the claims are available at the time of retirement.
However, arrears of such benefits can only be claimed for up to 38 months from the date of filing the petition, and claims beyond this time period become time-barred.
