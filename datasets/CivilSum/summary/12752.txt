Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Disability pension - Petitioner, who joined Army Service on 23.9.1976, developed a heart problem in May 1994 and was diagnosed as IHD with effort Angina, Class II.
He was placed in Medical Category "CEE" temporary and subsequently placed in low medical category "CEE" permanent, and advised to be released.
The respondent/UOI did not admit in their written statement that the discharge of the petitioner from military service was due to disability attributed to army service, but rather stated that the petitioner was discharged upon completion of their engagement period.
