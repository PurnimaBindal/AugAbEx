According to the Staff Service Rules of the Haryana State Federation of Consumers Cooperative Wholesale Stores Ltd, Rules 9.4(a), 950, 97(c)(i), 132 and the Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227, a revised seniority list for the reversion of General Managers was prepared despite clear directions by the High Court to consider the quota of promotees and direct recruits based on their dates of appointment.
As a result, the circulated seniority list is in clear disregard to the High Court's direction and is quashed.
The court has directed to prepare a new seniority list within two months.
