Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rule 4(iii) - Military service - Calculation of pension contributions - A period of break up to one year is automatically counted towards pension accumulation when a member transfers from Military to Civil service - When the break is between one and three years, the authority may choose whether or not to count it - A break exceeding three years is completely disregarded - Contrary to Rule 4(iii), courts are unable to order the inclusion of this period of time - The start of the selection process is irrelevant.
