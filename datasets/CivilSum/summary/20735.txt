According to Tamil Nadu State and Sub-ordinate Service Rules, 1961, Rules 49 and 49-A regarding Date of birth/Superannuation, the respondent could have applied for correction to their date of birth within 5 years from the enforcement of Rules.
However, they only applied for correction right before retirement which resulted in a rejected request by the authorities.
The Administrative Tribunal allowed the correction, but went beyond its jurisdiction by allowing the respondent to remain in service for two more years after superannuation.
The appeal was allowed and the order of the Tribunal was set aside.
