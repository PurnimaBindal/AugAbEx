Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 - Appointment - Ex-employees children's quota - Petition by respondent seeking appointment on the basis of his father's retirement allowed by High Court - Brother of respondent has already been appointed under the circular providing appointment benefits to the ward of a retired employee - High Court's direction to give appointment to the respondent is incorrect as the purpose of the circular is to provide employment to one child of such an employee for economic assistance and stability to the family - Absence of provision restricting other children from seeking employment will not make a difference.
