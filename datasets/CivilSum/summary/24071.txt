Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dismissal - Appeal accepted - An employee was dismissed from services for embezzlement charge, but after due inquiry, the Appellate authority found no embezzlement or established charges on examine the record and set aside the dismissal orders - Even though the order was assailed, the High Court refused to interfere and it was held that the HC rightly refused to interfere in the matter under Article 226 - High Court's interference is justified only if the order passed is contrary to law, based on inadmissible evidence, or if the conclusion is such that no reasonable person would arrive at it - Appeal dismissed accordingly.
