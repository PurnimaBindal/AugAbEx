The validity of restrictions imposed on employers under Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-N and Section 25-O (as it stood prior to amendment Act, 49 of 1984) is not violative of Article 19(1)(g) of Constitution of India, as they are reasonable and in the interest of workers as well as establishments.
Furthermore, the management can approach Article 226 of the Constitution for remedy against any arbitrary decisions made by the government.
The power of the appropriate government to grant or refuse permission for retrenchment under Section 25N(2) is quasi-judicial and requires a speaking order based on objective considerations and reasons being given.
It is not necessary for the government to grant or refuse permission in respect of all workers.
Lastly, no appeal lies to the Supreme Court against an order passed under Section 25-N(2).
