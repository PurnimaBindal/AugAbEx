Constitution of India, Article 14 - Admission - Residency scheme for admission - For a post-graduate course, there are eight seats and six of these seats are reserved for institutional candidates while the other two are for external candidates.
External candidates are selected through an All India entrance test but due to unavailability, all eight seats were filled only by institutional candidates who were on house jobs on 22.8.1989.
Thus, applications of external candidates were ignored as their cases were not taken into consideration.
