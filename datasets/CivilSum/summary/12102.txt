Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Non-Scheduled injury resulting in loss of earning capacity - A vehicle driver with 60% injuries and limping suffered a shortening of the left lower limb - Despite the lack of a schedule listing the injury, the court concluded that the disability was 100% and the ruling stands as it is absurd to assert that absence of an injury from a schedule corresponds with no loss of earning capacity.
