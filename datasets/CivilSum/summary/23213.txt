Canara Bank Officer Employees (Discipline & Appeal) Regulations, 1976, Regulation 18 - Review - Revisional Authority must operate within bounds of time limit - Power to relax time-limit must be used reasonably - Review cannot be done without invoking power of relaxation under Regulation 21 - Order of review made after 6 years without invoking Regulation 21 would be invalid and outside jurisdiction.
[Paras 11 & 12]
