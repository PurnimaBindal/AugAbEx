Constitution of India, Article 226 - Shifting of Machinery from Kurla to Butibori - Respondent Co requires machinery in Butibori - Machinery lying unused at Kurla - Industrial Court allows for machinery to be moved with one higher court confirming but with some conditions - The appellant union, representing 78 workers, challenges the order - The judge dismisses the appeal, stating 612 out of 690 workers chose to retire voluntarily, and the company offered alternate employment to the rest - The judge concludes there is no grounds for intervention in the appeal.
