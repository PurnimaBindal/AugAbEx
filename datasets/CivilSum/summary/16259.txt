Admission/Cancellation of Admission/Recognition of qualification - Unless a candidate has misrepresented or concealed facts, or committed fraud, admission cannot be cancelled even if granted on the basis of qualifications not recognized by the university - Cancelling admission later would be unfair to the candidate, as they would lose a year through no fault of their own - Qualifications must be assessed at the time of admission, not later - Petition granted with costs.
