Constitution of India, Article 309 Proviso - Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised Pay Scale Rules) (1998), Rule 1 - It is not permissible to take away benefits already available to employees under existing rules by an amendment with retrospective effect - The State's issuance of notifications retrospectively to retract a benefit already available to the petitioners under the existing rule is not fair, prejudiced and infringes Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
