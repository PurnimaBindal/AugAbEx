Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Irregularities and fraud in process of selection - Various illegalities such as incomplete applications, tampering with the entries in the receipt register, appointment of ineligible/unqualified/over/lower age persons, appointment of large number of persons in excess of advertised vacancies, and relaxation of qualifications/conditions after appointment constitute fraud and violation of Articles 14 and 16.
[Paras 23, 29, 32, 33 and 35]
Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 33 - Reservation for handicapped/disabled persons - 3% vacancies should be reserved for divided amongst three different categories to the extent of 1% each - Selection made without dividing the vacancies amongst 3 categories - Majority of candidates selected from one category - Selection invalidated.
[Para 25]
Punjab Panchayat Samitis and Zila Parishads Services Rules, 1965, Rule 6 - Selection and appointments - Age - Advertisement prescribing age different to that prescribed in the Rules - Over age candidates selected - Entire process of selection vitiated.
[Para 24]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Panchayat Samitis and Zila Parishads Services Rules, 1965, Rule 4 - Selection - Termination - Appointments made by an incompetent officer - Terminations made by the same officer cannot be said to be by an incompetent authority.
[Para 26]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Panchayat Samitis and Zila Parishads Services Rules, 1965, Rule 4 - Selection - Termination - Applications of selected candidates bearing dates after the last date of receipt of applications - Interpolation and tampering with the record invalidates the entire process of selection.
[Para 27]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Panchayat Samitis and Zila Parishads Services Rules, 1965, Rule 4 - Selection - Termination - Natural justice - Notice of termination should contain the material particulars regarding illegalities and irregularities committed in the process of selection as the main reason for termination - The entire selection has to be invalidated if the mischief played in selection is pervasive enough to affect the result.
The principle of natural justice would not be attracted in such cases where the appointees work just for a short period and know the reasons of termination.
