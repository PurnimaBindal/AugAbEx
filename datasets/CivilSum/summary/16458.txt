Constitution of India, Article 311 - Army Rules, Rule 13 Item III(V) of table - Order of discharge from Army - Challenges to discharge order include stigmatic grounds, retrospective orders, non-issuance of show cause notice, and allegations of the CEO being inimical - The CEO was not made a party and no specific allegation was raised against him - The petitioner refused to accept the show cause notice - The petitioner awarded four punishments - Rule 13(III)(V) allows authorities to discharge undesirable individuals from service - Such discharge is permissible where retention of the individual is not desirable, and this doesn't lead to misconduct or stigma - Condition barring petitioner from future employment is unsustainable as he has not been removed due to misconduct or conviction - Retrospective discharge condition is also not allowed under rules - Mala fide allegations deserve no cognizance in the absence of impleadment.
