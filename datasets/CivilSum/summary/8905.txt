Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - UP Public Services (Reservation for Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and other Backward Classes) Act, 1994, Section 3 - Reservation - Promotion - As per Article 16(4) of the Constitution, reservation can only be provided in initial appointments and cannot be extended to promotions - Any provision in Rules or instructions for reservation in promotion becomes invalid after the expiration of the 5-year period granted by the Supreme Court in Indira Sawhney's case, as it goes against the Constitution.
