Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Quantum of punishment - Lack of interference - Among allegations of fare collection without ticket issuance, conductor's punishment of withholding three increments with future effect from disciplinary authority remains unchallenged - Amount involved considered non-relevant and disciplinary authority found dismissal unsuitable - With full consideration of the circumstances, it cannot be said that punishment is disproportionate - Therefore, Industrial Tribunal could not rightly invoke Section 11-A of the Act and modify labor court's punishment of withholding annual increments with no permanent effect - [Para 5]
