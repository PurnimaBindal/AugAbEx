Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 7A(3)(a) (as amended by Ker.
Act 28 of 1961) - Constitution of India, Articles, 16, 39(d), 233, 234 - Industrial Tribunal Presiding Officers have a claim of pay parity with District Judges - Parity has been recognized by state government for the past 30 years and Industrial Tribunal Presiding Officers perform the same functions and duties as District Judges - The benefit cannot be denied to them on the ground of different appointments under Article 233 or 234 of the constitution.
