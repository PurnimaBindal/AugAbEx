Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Dismissal - Bus Conductor dismissed from service on the ground of misappropriation - The Labour Court ordered reinstatement on the post of helper or peon without back wages.
However, the Single Judge overruled the award of the Labour Court on the ground that the delinquent had committed several irregularities and illegalities earlier and had already been dismissed once before.
The Judge held that the Labour Court exceeded its jurisdiction and did not exercise the power under Section 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act with caution in the light of past and present misconduct.
The order passed by the Single Judge was deemed appropriate.
