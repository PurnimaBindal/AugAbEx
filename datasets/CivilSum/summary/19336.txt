The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation - A reserved category candidate who is higher in merit than general category candidates selected can compete for non-reserved post - If appointed to said post, their number cannot be added for working out percentage of reservation - The petitioner's merit for appointment to the general category should not be declined just because they also claimed entitlement to the post in the reserved category - The action of the respondents violated Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
