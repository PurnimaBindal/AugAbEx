According to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(S), 11-A, 17-B and Sales Promotion Employees (Conditions of Service) Act, 1976, Section 2(d), Medical Representatives are not considered workmen.
Even if an employee mainly performs duties of a Medical Representative, simply performing some duties of receipt and dispatch of consignment would not be enough to classify them as workmen.
This relies on AIR 1967 SC 678.
