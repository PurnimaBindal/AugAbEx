Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 1959, Sections 63 and 64 - Suspension of Chairman of Municipal Board - An order passed in furtherance of an enquiry report does not disclose any reasons to support the conclusion - It is a well-established legal principle that orders passed by quasi-judicial authorities must be justified - Since the impugned order is arbitrary, it is set aside.
