Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 2(9) - Employee - The appellants engaged hamalis for loading and unloading goods for carriage as carriers.
The appellants have control over the loading and unloading of goods, and the hamalis work under their supervision.
Although the appellants collect charges from customers and pay hamalis at a piece rate, the hamalis are not appointed or controlled by any other agency and work in connection with the establishment.
The issue of payment of salary or wages is irrelevant.
The Insurance Court held that hamalis are employees under Section 2(9) of the Act, and the appellants must contribute to the insurance benefits of the workmen under the Act.
The appeal was dismissed.
