Constitution of India, Article 16 - Compassionate appointment - Delay - Petitioner, who claimed appointment on compassionate grounds as a dependent, is approved for appointment by the State Government - However, more than 10 years have passed since the death of the employee, and even after 3 years of sanction, the petitioner has not been offered appointment - The objective of granting such appointments is to alleviate the immediate hardships faced by the bereaved family due to the loss of breadwinner - If such appointments are delayed for an infinite period due to administrative exigencies, the very scheme would lose its purpose - Therefore, the respondents are directed to appoint the petitioner to the post for which they were found eligible.
