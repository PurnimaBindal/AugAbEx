Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Reservation - Merit - Candidates belonging to the reserved category who have scored higher marks than candidates belonging to the General category cannot be considered as selected candidates of reserved category by availing reservation benefits - Reserved category candidates who have scored lower marks than General category selected candidates are entitled to reservation benefits.
[Para 7] B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Selection - Merit - The petitioner has challenged the selection of another candidate on the basis of scoring higher marks than the selected candidate - As the selected candidate was not made a party, no finding can be recorded against them - Petition dismissed.
