As per the Constitution of India, specifically Article 16, a temporary lecturer's service can be terminated.
If a temporary lecturer claims regularization but has not cleared NET, they cannot be entitled to it.
Their appointment letter clearly states their purely temporary and terminable position.
The college has made it a policy not to appoint anyone who has not cleared NET.
The petitioner cannot go beyond their appointment and acceptance letter and therefore cannot be regularized without clearing NET or having PhD Mere fact that other colleges applied for relaxation does not ipso facto entitled petitioner to get the same relief, and there are no legal impediments in the matter of policy.
