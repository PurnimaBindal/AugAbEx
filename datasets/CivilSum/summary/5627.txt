The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pay fixation - Claims for pay fixation according to rules or instructions is a recurring cause of action for an employee, and cannot be dismissed on the grounds of delay and laches in a writ petition - Pay fixation does not affect the rights of a third party - Court can restrict arrears up to 38 months from filing of the petition and disallow arrears for which a suit has become time-barred.
[Paras 9 to 11]
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Ad hoc employees - Regular employees - Ad hoc employees cannot be treated identically to regular employees who are protected by Article 311 - Ad hoc employees are not entitled to claim the same benefits as regular employees - Benefit of advance increment is only available to regular employees - Ad hoc employees are not entitled to complain about discrimination.
[Paras 12 to 17]
Seniority - The ad hoc service followed by regular service is countable for seniority.
