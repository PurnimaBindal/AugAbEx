As per the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, dependents of Freedom Fighters are entitled to reservation.
If the posts reserved for Ex-servicemen and Backward classes are not filled, then 2% of the total posts will be filled from the category of dependents of Freedom Fighters.
Currently, 14 posts of the Ex-servicemen category are vacant, and only one post has been filled from the dependent's category.
The petitioner is the only claimant for the available vacant post of Ex-servicemen quota and must be appointed.
