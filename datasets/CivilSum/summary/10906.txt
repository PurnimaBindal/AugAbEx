Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - Degree Course in Engineering under entry scheme - Despite being in the merit list after the second counselling for a free seat, the petitioner was not considered due to the non-production of a no-objection certificate or original certificate from the institute where he was previously admitted against a paid seat.
Meanwhile, respondents No. 5 and 6, who were lower in merit than the petitioner, were given admission.
The action of the respondents in not considering the petitioner's claim is not in accordance with the law.
Therefore, the respondents have been directed to adjust the petitioner against a free seat in preference to respondents No. 5 and 6.
