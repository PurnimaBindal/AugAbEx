The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16 and 226, is concerned with pay scales and discrimination.
The clerks of Kurukshetra University argued that they should be afforded the same pay scales as those enjoyed by Haryana State Government clerks, citing their prior higher scale and the fact that Class-D employees were also granted equal pay.
However, it was held that since the Class D posts had been upgraded and given a uniform pay scale with clerks or other Class III posts, the clerks currently employed in that role could not claim any adverse effect or cause for action.
The court ultimately determined that it was fair and just to equate the pay scales of University clerks with those in the State Government service.
It was acknowledged that the courts are not capable of conducting a detailed mathematical analysis of pay scale equivalence.
