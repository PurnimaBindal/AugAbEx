Punjab Courts Act, 1918, Section 35 - Subordinate Services Rules, Rule VI - Appointments - Promotion appointment - The Judge is responsible for promoting individuals to the posts in the ministerial cadre, except for the process serving and menial establishments in the Small Cause Courts, regardless of the rule of rotation prescribed in the first provision of Rule VI(1), which cannot be applied when there is only one U.D.C.
- Seniority cannot be used as a basis for a claim.
[Paras 8 and 9]
