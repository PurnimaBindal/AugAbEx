The Constitution of India, Article 16(4) allows for reservation in promotions and seniority must be followed according to roster points.
Reservation is a policy of the State and it is up to the State to determine the ranking of candidates promoted against reserved points of the roster.
Such promotions are not unconstitutional according to the Railway Establishment Manual Vol. 1, Chapter III, Para 306.
[Paras 24, 26, 27 and 33]  B.
Reservation for SC/STs in promotions is not necessary according to the Constitution of India, Article 16(4).
The purpose of reservation is to ensure adequate representation for backward classes, not to provide accelerated promotion.
Granting double benefits to members of a reserved category violates the rule of equality in Article 16(1).
[Para 21]  C. The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 make no mention of a "panel" for promotions.
"Panel" refers to a merit list or select list and is only used in the case of selection posts.
There is no requirement for such a list in the case of non-selection posts.
