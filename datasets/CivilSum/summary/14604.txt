The State Bank of India, Act, does not provide for the grant of military service benefits to Class II officers, as stated in State Bank of India Officers Service Rules.
The petitioner cannot claim the same benefit as probationary officers Class-I appointed against the reserved vacancies for ex-servicemen unless he himself was appointed as a Class I Officer against a reserved vacancy.
Even though the bank is autonomous, unless the employer has made a provision for the grant of such benefits to its employees in their service rules, the bank cannot be forced to grant the same.
[Para 6]

The petitioner claiming military service benefits towards seniority and promotions will adversely affect persons ranking senior to him.
Proper parties need to be made aware of the issue at hand, and unless they are made party before the court, a lis cannot be properly adjudicated, and no adverse order can be passed against them in their absence.
This is in accordance with the Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16, and 226.
