Army Pension Regulations, Regulations 173(A) and 179 - Disability Pension - The term "Medical category EEC Permanent" is unclear - The petitioner was discharged from military service without an extension due to ineligibility based on this term only in his discharge-certificate's Part-VI - It cannot be assumed that the petitioner was invalidated out from service on medical grounds solely from this term - The authorities must form a Re-survey Medical Board for examination of the petitioner within three months, and if his disability is declared 20% or above, he may claim the disability benefit.
