Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical reimbursement - Heart surgery - Employees receiving treatment at hospitals and medical institutes recognized by the Government of Punjab cannot be denied total medical reimbursement for expenses incurred, regardless of whether the rates at those institutes exceed those of AIIMS - Restricting reimbursement to AIIMS rates is unjustifiable, and conditions that do so have been declared illegal.
[Paras 2, 8 & 9]
