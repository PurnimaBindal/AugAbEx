Admission/Education - As per the interim orders of the Court, the petitioners abandoned their admission in other institutions where they had already enrolled after four months of study.
Despite being placed at No. 9 and 10 on the merit list for only 8 available seats, it is just and equitable to create two special seats and adjust the petitioners as a special case to prevent a delay in their career for two years.
Institutions are therefore directed to assess their cases sympathetically.
