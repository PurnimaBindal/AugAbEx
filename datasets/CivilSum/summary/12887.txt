Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 21 - Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of the Deceased Govt. employees Rules, 2003 - Haryana Compassionate Employees Rules, 2006 - Compassionate appointment - Financial assistance - In the event that an application for compassionate appointment is made under the 2003 Rules wherein no compassionate appointment is offered but financial assistance is provided, the authorities should request an option from the applicant.
If the applicant chooses to accept financial assistance, he must be paid the prescribed amount.
The claim for compassionate appointment was rejected, but the applicant was given the liberty to claim financial assistance.
