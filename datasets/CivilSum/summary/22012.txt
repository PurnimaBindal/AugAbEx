According to Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33 (C) (2) and Constitution of India, Article 16, when a State Government employee is sent on deputation to a Corporation, the Corporation cannot terminate the employee's services.
In the case where the services of the appellant were wrongly terminated by the Corporation, the appellant should be treated as an employee of the State Government.
It was ruled that the appellant must be reinstated after 15 years and be paid a consolidated sum of Rs. 10,000 in lieu of full wages.
