The Executive Order limiting eligibility for the Civil Services Examination (CSE) under Proviso 2 of Rule 4 of the Civil Services Examination Rules, 1983 is within the jurisdiction of the executive authority.
[Para 43]
Proviso 2 of Rule 4 of the Civil Services Examination Rules, 1983, which provides that candidates already in service must resign from their first allocation in order to be eligible to appear in the CSE again, is not ultra vires the Indian Administrative Services (Appointment by Competitive Examination) Regulations, 1955.
[Paras 50, 78, 80, 81 and 100]
The condition of eligibility introduced by Proviso 2 of Rule 4 of the Civil Services Examination Rules, 1983, that candidates who have already accepted service must resign first to avail a second chance, applies to those who abstained from training with permission to reappear and accepted service otherwise on the basis of their selection in CSE in IAS and IFS.
[N/A]
The condition of eligibility introduced by Proviso 2 of Rule 4 of the Civil Services Examination Rules, 1983, that Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe candidates who have already accepted service on the basis of the selection in CSE and allocated to Group A services must resign from service to avail second chance, is not discriminatory.
[Paras 85, 86, 92 and 93]
The condition of eligibility introduced by Proviso 2 of Rule 4 of the Civil Services Examination Rules, 1983, which is applied only to those appointed to I.P.S.
or group 'A' services, is based on the difference in two categories of services and is not discriminatory.
