According to Section 4 of the Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923, compensation must be given to the deceased.
However, in this case, the deceased was in a Gypsy driven by another person when an accident occurred.
Although the witness from the respondent-Company claimed that the service record of the deceased was destroyed in floods, they did not state whether the deceased was not on duty at the time of the accident.
Therefore, the finding of the Labour Commissioner that the deceased did not die during the course of employment cannot be accepted.
The case has been remanded to the learned Commissioner for the computation of the compensation amount.
