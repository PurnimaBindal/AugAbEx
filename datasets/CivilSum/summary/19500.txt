According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution, as well as Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 2125 (2), a constable in the police department who was sent on deputation to CID and received ad hoc promotions up to the rank of SI - due to the fact that CID did not have its own cadres - has sought to be reverted back to his parent department as a constable.
The state is justifying this reversion by claiming that promotions from constable to SI required completion of various tests and courses, of which the petitioner had not participated in or passed.
However, the petitioner claims that he was never informed of these tests while on deputation.
The petitioner's service record is unblemished and there are no allegations of misconduct or incompetency against him.
Moreover, it is reasonable to assume that the petitioner would have risen to the positions that his juniors have reached if he had not been sent on deputation.
The rules concerning promotion requirements can be relaxed, and given that police personnel on deputation in CID have been allowed to retire in the same ranks as they held in CID, it is unfair to make the petitioner suffer.
