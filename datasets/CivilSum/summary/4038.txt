Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Examination Rules, Rule 8(i) - Matriculation examination - Eligibility - The requirement that a private candidate who passed their middle class examination from a Board other than the respondent-Board cannot take the matriculation exam is invalid - The petitioner's application form for the matriculation exam was rejected because they did not pass the middle exam from the Board of School Education, Haryana - The condition that the candidate must have passed the eighth class exam from the respondent Board is arbitrary and has been quashed.
[Paras 10 and 11]
