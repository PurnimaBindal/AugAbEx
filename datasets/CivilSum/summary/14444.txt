Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Applicability of - The petitioner's employment with the DDA was purely temporary and specific to a project, which lasted for 197 days, and after it was completed, his services were terminated.
As a result, the petitioner is not protected by Section 25-F of the Act and has no legitimate grounds to demand reinstatement.
Consequently, the petition is dismissed.
