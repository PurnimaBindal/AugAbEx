Constitution of India, Articles 15 and 16 - J&K Reservation Rules, 1964 Rule 10 - Appointment - Reservation category - Resident of Backward Areas - The SRO 126 of 1994 cannot be given retrospective operation to bring pre-promulgation vacancies and posts within its realm - R10's "available vacancies" only applies to those vacancies available after the rule's implementation - Selection processes that had already begun before its implementation are not covered - These rules do not provide any right to open category candidates who offered to undergo selection - The SRO-255 of 1994 merely clarifies the application of Reservation Rules and does not show any discriminatory behavior - The petition has been dismissed.
