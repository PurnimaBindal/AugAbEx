Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection - Appointment - Selection held for the post of Head Constables - Petitioners deemed fit for the post of Constable only - Petitioners are estopped from claiming appointment as Head Constables after accepting the offer for Constable - Qualifications and appearing in the selection process for a post does not guarantee such post - If the selection was made for the post of Constable, the petitioners have no right to claim appointment as Head Constables.
[Paras 6 to 9]
