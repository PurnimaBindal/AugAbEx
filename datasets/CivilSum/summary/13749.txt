Central Civil Services (Extraordinary Pension) Rules, 1939, Rules 3 & 52 - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955 - Opinion of Court of Inquiry that the deceased was on bona fide government duty/service and recommended for Extraordinary Departmental Family Pension - Competent authority returned the proposal on the ground that father of the deceased is an ex-army pensioner and a landlord - Rules cover the entitlement of mother, father, and siblings in different ratios in different eventualities - The rejection of the family pension claim for both the mother and father is unjustified - The authority is directed to calculate and release the family pension.
