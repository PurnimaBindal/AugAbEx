Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 13(1) - Limitation Act, 1963, Article 113 - Constitution of India, Article 311 - Order of dismissal - The respondent was dismissed from duty due to imprisonment for murder and subsequently filed a suit against the order after nine years - The trial court decreed the suit, stating that the Deputy District Education Officer was not competent to pass the order - However, it was held that the suit was filed beyond the limitation period as the respondent had not claimed salary for nine years, indicating his knowledge of the order of dismissal - Therefore, the appeal was allowed.
