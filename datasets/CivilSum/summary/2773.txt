Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Workman - Termination of service - Challenge as to - Burden of proof - The workman cannot rely solely on the management's plea and shift the onus of proof - He must conclusively establish his case with reliable oral and documentary evidence - An affidavit in evidence alone is insufficient to discharge the burden of proof - The refusal to consider relevant documents was unjustified - Award based on no evidence is unsustainable.
