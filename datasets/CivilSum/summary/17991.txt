The posts of Legal Assistants in the Transport Department were equated with their counterparts in the Department of Law and Legislation in 1969, which entitled them to the same pay scale until 1983.
However, in 1983, Legal Assistants in the Department of Law and Legislative were given a higher grade.
Despite this, as the State government had previously equated the posts and the nature of work in both departments is almost identical, there should be no discrimination in the grant of higher revised pay scales at any later stages.
Therefore, the petitioners working in the Transport Department are entitled to the same pay scale from the same date as their counterparts in the Law and Legislation Department.
