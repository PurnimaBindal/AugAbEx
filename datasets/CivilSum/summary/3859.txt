Under Section 130 of the U.P.
Co-operative Societies Act 1966 and Regulation 104 of the U.P.
Co-operative Societies Employees Services Regulations 1975, a compassionate appointment can only be granted within a reasonable period of time after the death of the sole breadwinner.
In this case, the petitioner, who became a major in 1990, sought a compassionate appointment in place of his father who was kidnapped in 1980.
However, due to the unreasonable delay in claiming the appointment, the petition was dismissed.
The purpose of a compassionate appointment is to help families in sudden crises caused by the death of their sole breadwinner.
The courts have also held that the cause of action for a compassionate appointment arises on the death of the person who died in service.
