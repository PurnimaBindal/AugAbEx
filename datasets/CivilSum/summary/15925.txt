Under the Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Section 7 and Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, petitioners who are employees of schools receiving 95% aid from the State Govt. are entitled to rural area allowance.
It is the policy of the government to give this allowance to eligible employees and the government must pay a proportionate amount as per aid given to the institutions, while the management must pay the rest of the amount.
