According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, in cases where employees are recruited for a specific job/project that has been allocated to a specific establishment due to bifurcation, such an action cannot be classified as transfer and the principle of last come first go does not apply.
The employees continue to work in the same job and place, and allocating these staff members along with the part of the establishment is not illegal.
[Paras 10, 12 & 13]
