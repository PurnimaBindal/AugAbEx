Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Police Rules, Rule 162 - Dismissal - Length of Service - Consideration of - The punishing authority did not take into account the petitioner's 20 years of service in the Police Department and his claim for pension while passing the dismissal order - Impugned order set aside - The punishing authority may issue a new order for the petitioner after following the legal procedure.
