Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equating degree and diplomas - Expert Committee's opinion is not absolute, court can take different view if the opinion is arbitrary - MBE Post Graduate Degree from Kurukshetra and Guru Jambeshwar Universities is equivalent to Management course and makes candidates eligible to appear in the NET test by UGC for the subject of Management - Difference between MBE and Economics courses, as there is no subject combination - UGC directed to provide eligibility certificate for NET in Management subject.
