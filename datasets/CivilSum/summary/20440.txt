According to Section 12(3) and (4) of the Burmah Oil Company (Acquisition of Shares of Oil India Ltd and the undertakings in India of Assam Oil Company Ltd and the Burmah Oil Co (India Trading Ltd) Act, 1981, and the 1983 Indian Oil Corporation Ltd (Assam Oil Division) Staff Pension Fund Trust Deed Scheme, 1983, retirees prior to the enforcement of the 1981 Act and 1983 Pension Fund Scheme are entitled to pension from the successor company under the 1983 Scheme.
The Central Government and successor company assumed all the assets and liabilities of Assam Oil Company Ltd under this Act.
Petitioners who were previous beneficiaries of the 1973 pension scheme and members of the 1983 scheme are entitled to the benefit of the formula for revised pension made effective from 1995.
The respondents must make available the pensionary benefits to the petitioners in accordance with the formula of 1995 for revised pension, and clear any arrears found due within a period of four months.
The entitlement of the petitioners to pensionary benefits according to the revised formula is in line with the provisions of the Act and Pension Scheme of 1983.
