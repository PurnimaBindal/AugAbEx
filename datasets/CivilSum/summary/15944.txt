According to Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, termination of a contract of service during probation is subject to certain conditions.
Specifically, the probation period cannot be extended beyond one year, and initial appointments are typically for three years, with the possibility of extension year to year based on performance.
However, termination before the end of the three-year term without evidence of inefficiency or unsuitability can be legally challenged and may result in the petitioner being deemed to have continued in service until the end date of their contract.
Additionally, it is within the authority of the employer to consider the petitioner's case for regularisation of their services based on their service record.
