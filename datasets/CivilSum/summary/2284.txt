Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 33A, 33(2)(b) - Termination - Pending Industrial Dispute - The workman was correct in filing an application under Section 33A alleging violation of Section 33(2)(b) of Act since no approval was taken from the Tribunal.
Furthermore, in the absence of any approval from the Tribunal, the employer must consider the employee as continuing their service and grant them all the compensatory benefits.
As a result, the termination is deemed null and void.
[Paras 6.3.3, 634, 6.3.5, 6.4.2]
