Constitution of India, Articles 309, 16 and 162 - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 56 - Age of superannuation for Agricultural Market Board employees - Bye-laws state that conditions of service other than recruitment shall be regulated by government - Service Bye-laws 1977 specifically provide for revision of pay scales according to government orders - Age of superannuation is a service condition and if amended to 60 years by the government under RSR 1951, it also applies to Board employees - Market Committee Service Rules, 1975 do not apply to Board employees.
