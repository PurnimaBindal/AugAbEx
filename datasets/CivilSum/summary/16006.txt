Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Equal pay for equal work - Daily wage employees - Mali-cum-chowkidar in Public Health Department, Haryana - Those employed on daily wages, such as Mali-cum-chowkidar in the Public Health Department of Haryana, are entitled to the same salary and allowances as regular employees holding similar positions in the Department.
This is in accordance with AIR 1986 SC 584.
