Constitution of India, Article 311 - Navy Act, 1957, Section 115 - Dismissal from service - The punishment of rigorous imprisonment and releasing taking into account detention period, coupled with the dismissal from service, is of greater gravity for the offence of using criminal force with intent to outrage modesty of a young lady as per orders of punishment passed under the Navy Act.
Writ jurisdiction cannot interfere in the quantum of punishment.
