Study Leave Rules, 1963, Rule 3(5) - Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume I Part II Appendix 20 - Admission to MD/MS/PG Diploma course - Revised policy allows in-service employees with three years of service rather than five to be eligible - Argument was made that employees with three years cannot even avail study leave under the rules - It was held that since there was no proof indicating that the state government was not authorized to revise the policy, matters such as this were best left to the discretion of policymakers - Furthermore, even though study leave is not typically granted to government employees with less than five years of service, the word "ordinarily" used in Rule 3(5) indicates that the state government may authorize study leave for such employees - Therefore, no amendment to the rules was actually necessary in order to grant study leave to employees with less than five years of service.
