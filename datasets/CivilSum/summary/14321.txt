Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - Admission to Diploma in Physical Education Course - The petitioner received his confidential result in a sealed cover because it was not yet publicly declared.
However, there was a mistake in the tabulation of his marks, and the petitioner presented an amended result before the deadline for admission.
As a result, the respondent-college had a duty to grant admission to the petitioner, who had superior merit when compared to respondent No. 5.
In light of the fact that the petitioner has not yet undergone the course of study, he should be granted admission to the course in the next academic year.
[Paras 3 and 4]
