Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I, Part I, Rule 3.26(2) - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 5.32-A(C) - Compulsory Retirement - In the interest of the public, an employee whose integrity is questionable may be forced into retirement to eliminate dead weight, as per 1992(2) SCC 299.
[Para 17]  B.
Compulsory retirement - According to Baikuntha Nath Das v. Chief District Medical Officer, Boripada, 1992(2) SCC 299, an order of compulsory retirement is not a punishment and can be carried out by the government if it is deemed to be in the public interest.
Although the decision is subjective, it cannot be made arbitrarily or maliciously and must be based on the employee's complete performance record, with greater emphasis on recent years.
