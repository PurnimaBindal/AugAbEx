Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Madhya Pradesh Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1976, Rules 9(2), 9(1) and 2(a) - Pension - Withholding of Departmental proceedings initiated for recovery of loss to Government on retirement of Government servant - If departmental proceedings are not completed within 2 years from the date of institution, Rule 9(4) mandates that the entire amount of pension withheld shall be restored.
