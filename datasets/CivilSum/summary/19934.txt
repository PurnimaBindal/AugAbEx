The High Court ordered a re-assessment of the answer scripts for the Lower School Training Course BI test examination as respondent No. R did not make the grade and allegations of burgling were made.
During the re-assessment, respondent Nos. 6 to 10 received lesser marks than before and had already been sent for training and completed 2-1/2 years of it.
The court deemed it unfair to deprive them of the benefits they had already been given, despite any deficiency in the marking process.
As a result, the respondent's appeal was denied.
