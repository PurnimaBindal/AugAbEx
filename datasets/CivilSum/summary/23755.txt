Income Tax Act, Section 117(1) and (2) (prior to amendment of 1987) - Classification of Income Tax Officers as Class I and Class II officers - Government allowed to classify based on different skill, equipment, and talent required - Power for classification not invalid.
[Para 47]
Interpretation of Statutes - Income Tax Act, Sections 117(1) and (2), 116, 118 and 120 - Classified posts mentioned in one provision and not in another - Part of legislation cannot be rendered redundant unless a specific case is made out.
[Para 47]
Malafide legislation/Amendment - Amendment providing classification of Income-Tax Officers in class I and class II - Mala fide challenge unsuccessful - Law cannot be challenged on the mala fide ground.
[Para 48]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal duties/Classification - Classification of Income Tax Officers as class I and class II - Challenge on the ground of equal duties and responsibility, qualification, jurisdiction, responsibilities powers, and position - Classification valid - Mere entrustment of duties of higher post-entitles employee to claim emoluments for that post but cannot obliterate the difference between the two posts.
