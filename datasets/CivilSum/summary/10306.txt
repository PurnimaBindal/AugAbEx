In accordance with the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, and 226, an Incentive Scheme was established to provide certain benefits to Grade 'C' staff members upon acquiring a higher technical qualification.
However, Grade 'B' employees were not given the same benefits and were denied the opportunity to improve their performance through increased knowledge.
The petitioner, who holds a Grade 'B' post, is entitled to similar benefits with retrospective effect.
This is supported by various cases including Virendra Kumar & Ors. v. Union of India & Ors., Gopal Krishna Sharma v. State of Rajasthan, Lt.
Governor of Delhi v. Dharampal & Ors., KC Sharma & Ors. Union of India & Ors., and Parsion Devi & Ors. v. Sumitri Devi & Ors. [Paras 26, 30, and 31]
