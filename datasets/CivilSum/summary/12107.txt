Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 1986, Section 38 - Bureau of Indian Standards (Recruitment to Scientific Cadre) Regulations, 1988, Regulation 9 - Promotion - Flexible Complementing Scheme (FCS) - Post of Group-D Scientist - In 1999, the petitioners became eligible for promotion, with no rules existing to the contrary at the time.
The prevailing FCS should therefore have been implemented in 1999 itself, and the petitioners are hereby directed to be promoted as soon as they have completed five years of service to the post of Scientist-C, in accordance with the FCS.
