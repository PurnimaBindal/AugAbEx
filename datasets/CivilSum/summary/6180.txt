Termination - Principles of natural justice - Even if an order passed by a departmental authority violates the Rules or fails to follow principles of natural justice, the authority is not prevented from proceeding again on the same charges, nor does a declaration of it being illegal entitle an employee to reinstatement - In a case where an employee dies before completion of the enquiry, their right to the post becomes non-existent, and no right to the post is transferred to a dependent - The employee's dependent is, however, entitled to pensionary benefits in accordance with the Rules, as upheld by the courts below [Para 7]  B Enquiry proceedings - Termination - Wages - If a departmental enquiry is found to have been vitiated by a legal defect, it will not erase the charges against the employee, and the State can commence a fresh enquiry - An illegal order of termination will not automatically lead to reinstatement and entitlement to wages - In the event of the death of the employee, their dependant has no cause of action to claim relief - As held in previous cases, the plaintiff is entitled only to pension and other allowances, not pay and allowances to which their husband may have been entitled but for the termination order [Para 6].
The rulings of AIR 1966 Madras 260 and AIR 1962 SC 1044 were cited.
