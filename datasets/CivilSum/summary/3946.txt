Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Ad hoc service - The period of ad hoc service can be considered towards seniority and for all other purposes only if the ad hoc appointment was made through the proper channel with opportunities given to all eligible persons available, but without following the Rule of law, period of such service cannot be counted towards seniority.
[Paras 16 and 18]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Delay and laches - The seniority list was circulated in 1981 after providing full opportunity and various persons were promoted before a Civil Suit was filed challenging the seniority declaration in 1987 - the permission to grant the relief could unsettle the fixed rights resulting in the dismissal of the petition on the basis of delay and laches.
[Para 22]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Non-joinder of necessary/affected parties - The petitioner filed a case seeking direction to count the ad hoc service period towards seniority and challenged the clause of ad hoc appointment in the appointment letter - Granting the relief will affect the rights of those shown senior to the petitioner even though they were appointed regularly after the ad hoc appointment - No order that affects the rights of others adversely can be passed without their knowledge, causing the petition to be dismissed for non-joinder of necessary parties.
