Constitution of India, Article 311 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 16 - Termination of services of a temporary employee - The termination of services of a temporary employee without conducting an enquiry is against Article 311 of the Constitution.
Even a temporary employee is entitled to protection.
The order of termination without following principles of natural justice is unacceptable, the order must be set aside.
[Paras 8 and 9]
