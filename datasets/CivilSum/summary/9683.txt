UCO Bank (Employees) Pension Regulations, 1995, Regulation 33 - Any action negatively impacting an employee's pension rights must follow principles of natural justice - Compulsory retirement is not equal to termination or dismissal - Process for imposing a cut on pension must involve notice and opportunity for the retired employee - Operation of Regulation 33 by competent authority must comply with principles of natural justice.
[Paras 11, 15, 16 and 19] B. UCO Bank (Employees) Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 46(1)(e) - Compulsory retirement as punishment does not result in automatic forfeiture of gratuity - Process for withholding gratuity must involve notice and opportunity.
[Paras 17 and 20] C. UCO Bank (Employees) Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 38 - Compulsory retirement does not automatically result in forfeiture of leave encashment - Leave encashment benefit cannot be denied without complying with principles of natural justice.
