The rules of natural justice aim to prevent any miscarriage of justice and guarantee fairness in action of administrative authorities exercising quasi-judicial or judicial functions.
If requirements about such authorities recording reasons for decisions are dispensed with, then they have no obligation to do so.
B.
Section 164 of the Army Act and Rule 66 of the Army Rules have no requirement for recording reasons in proceedings for confirmation.
Neither the confirming authority nor the Central Government are obligated to record reasons for the orders they pass.
C. Under the Army Act and related rules, there is no right to obtain copies of proceedings of a court-martial before the finding and sentence have been confirmed.
Thus, an appellant cannot object to not receiving copies and losing the right to representation until the confirmation has taken place.
