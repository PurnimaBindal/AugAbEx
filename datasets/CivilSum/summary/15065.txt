While governing payment of allowances to private teachers, terms and conditions of their service contract must be considered - it is not permissible to equate with those having different terms and conditions, as per Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Section 10(1) and Constitution of India, Article 309.
[Para 7] B.
Teachers of private schools are not entitled to avail benefits under the Office Memorandum of Govt. of India, Ministry of Personnel Public Grievances and Pensions, Department of Pension and Pensioners Welfare, dated 151987, which is only applicable for Central Govt. employees, as per the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952.
