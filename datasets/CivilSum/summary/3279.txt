Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 39, Rules 1 & 2 - Provisional Admission/Admissions/Irreparable loss - A clause of the Eligibility policy for LL.B admission prescribing minimum marks - Difference of opinion amongst judges - One judge holds clause to be violative of the Constitution and another judge upholds it's validity -Validity of the clause yet to be decided - Petitioner has a strong prima facie case - Denial of provisional admission would result in irreparable loss - No harm to University if petitioner granted provisional admission - University directed to grant provisional admission and conduct first semester exam.
