The State Bank of India Officers Order of 1979 requires eligible candidates for promotion/consideration to have two years of experience, which has been raised to four years.
However, vacancies are limited, so all eligible candidates are allowed to compete without limiting the zone of consideration.
Calling a large number of candidates for a limited number of vacancies does not invalidate the selection, and the change in policy is not arbitrary.
