Constitution of India, Article 21 - Right to life - Compensation for victims of riots - It is the responsibility of the State to protect its citizens from miscreants, anti-social and anti-national elements and to maintain public order for all to enjoy democratic benefits regardless of religion, caste or creed - The Constitution sets goals for the state to achieve, and fundamental rights are the means by which those goals are realized - The state must enforce civil standards of behavior to protect individuals from harm, and if it cannot fulfill these duties, it is liable to compensate the family of a person killed during riots – Rs. 20,000 is an inadequate compensation, and Rs. 2 lakh with interest awarded as compensation - officials of the State must be held responsible for failing to fulfill their duties - Those responsible must be identified and their properties confiscated to pay compensation - [Paras 8, 10, 11, 16, 21 and 22]  B.
Constitution of India, Articles 48A and 49 - Culture and heritage - Media - To prevent pollution, the State should check the telecast of movies that glorify violence and obscenity, which are a foreign culture brought to India by powerful media, and instead promote Indian culture - [Para 11]
