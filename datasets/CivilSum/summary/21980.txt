Karnataka Private Educational Institutions (Discipline and Control) Rules, 1978, Rules 6 and 16 - Karnataka Private Educational Institutions (Discipline and Control) Act, 1975, Sections 3 and 6 - Appointment of regular teacher in a government aided institution - To fill the gaps in the rules by issuing administrative instructions to promote the appointment of regular teachers, the government must have the power to do so if the statute or Rule allows it.
In the absence of such provision, the government cannot supplement the rules by executive orders repugnant to Sections 3 and 15.
