Under Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 916, gratuity may be withheld.
For instance, if an employee retires but has an outstanding miscellaneous advance of Rs. 4,98,79750 or unlawfully occupies a house allotted to them during their service for 3 years, the respondents are justified to adjust the amounts against the gratuity owed.
