Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, 1957, Section 98 - Delhi Electric Supply Undertaking (DMC) Seniority Regulations, 1968, Regulation 7 - Recruitment Regulations for the post of Assistant Engineer (Electrical & Mechanical) 1980, Regulations 8 and 11 Seniority/Rota & quota rule/Direct recruits v. Promotees - Direct recruits and promotees are subject to the quota-rota system - If direct recruits are unavailable in a specific year and appointments are still made without simultaneous appointments from both quotas, quota-rota provisions will not apply, and seniority of promotees must be calculated by clubbing all posts - Direct recruitments made long after promotee appointments cannot encroach upon the seniority of promotees if rotational appointments were violated - The seniority list must be re-framed, placing junior/direct appointees below promotees holding higher ranks in the cadre - [Paras 19, 24, 25, 28, 29 and 31] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Delay and laches - Repeated provisional seniority lists mean seniority is not finalized, so the cause of action arises only when seniority is finalized with the objections of the aggrieved - Final seniority list circulated in 1993 - The challenge to the final seniority list does not suffer from delay or laches - [Paras 13 and 23]
