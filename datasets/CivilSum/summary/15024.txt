According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India, disciplinary proceedings can lead to the termination of services for unauthorized absence or other misconduct.
In this particular case, the petitioner had been charge-sheeted for unauthorized absence in 2001 and again in 2002, with an enquiry and punishment imposed.
In 2003, the petitioner was again charge-sheeted for the same offense and punishment was once again imposed.
The disciplinary authority was authorized to consider the petitioner's past conduct when determining the appropriate punishment, ultimately leading to the termination of the petitioner's services.
The punishment was found to be proportionate to the gravity of the proven misconduct.
