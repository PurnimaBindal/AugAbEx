Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Misconduct - Punishment - An employee was caught smoking in a non-smoking zone on the factory premises, and when he was stopped, he physically manhandled the incharge of the plating shop and used abusive language towards him.
Due to the nature of the misconduct, the punishment of dismissal could not be reduced by the Labour court, and the order of reinstatement was quashed.
The punishment of stoppage of three annual increments without backwages was substituted instead of dismissing the employee from service.
