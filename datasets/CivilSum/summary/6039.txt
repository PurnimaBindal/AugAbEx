Constitution of India, Article 16 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 44 - Evidence Act (1 of 1872), Section 115 - Dearness Allowance - A commission had recommended that State Govt. employees receive dearness allowance at Central rates, and the government's acceptance of said recommendation led employees to believe they would receive the allowance - The government is estopped from denying the legal right of its employees to the allowance.
