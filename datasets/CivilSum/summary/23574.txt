Constitution of India, Article 226 - Strictures in the judgment - Strictures against Director of GB Pant Hospital - Power to expunge remarks made by the court in its judgment - The court must exercise this extraordinary power only when there is a clear case to do so - A judge should take great care when commenting on a person's conduct, especially when the person is not a party to the case - Strictures in the High Court's judgment found to be justified after a thorough examination of the record - Remarks upheld - The fact that the person concerned was not a party to the case and was not given an opportunity to respond is not sufficient grounds to expunge the remarks.
[Paras 8 and 13]

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Enquiry - Administrative law - Enquiry into the conduct of Head of an Organisation - No fair and proper enquiry can be made into the conduct of a person who is Head of an organisation until he no longer holds that position.
