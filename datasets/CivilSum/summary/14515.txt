The Constitution of India, in Articles 14, 21, and 226, allows for compassionate appointment.
In this case, Respondent No. 3's father, an employee of the College, died in 1982, when Respondent No. 3 was a minor.
Respondent No. 3 was appointed under the Dying in Harness Rule in 1993.
Compassionate appointments are meant to help families during sudden financial crises, but there was no justification for such a long delay in this case.
Therefore, the appointment was quashed.
