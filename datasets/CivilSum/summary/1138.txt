As per Section 2 of Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, it is mandatory for the thermal power station Board to provide and maintain a canteen for employees, which becomes part of the establishment.
Workers employed in such canteen will be considered as employees of the Management, irrespective of being hired by a contractor or by a Co-operative Society.
[Paras 24, 25, 39 and 32] B.
If employees working in the canteen under the statutory obligation seek relief of declaration as Board's employees under Article 226 of the Constitution, they cannot be directed to go before the Labour Court or Tribunal for remedy, especially considering the pronouncements made by the Supreme Court and various High Courts.
