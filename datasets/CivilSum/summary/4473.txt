Section 19(1) and (3) of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 provides that an order of dismissal in limine can be considered vitiated if there is error on the face of the record, such as non-consideration of material evidence.
The power to dismiss an application is vested with the Tribunals, but it should be exercised sparingly and judiciously as they act as substitutes for all courts except High Courts and Supreme Court.
The Tribunals have a corresponding duty to apply themselves carefully to the disputes before them and pass orders after due consideration of the entire material presented before them.
The present case is remanded to be decided after notice to the respondents.
