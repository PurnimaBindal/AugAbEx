Constitution of India, Article 226 - Maharashtra Housing and Area Development Authority Employees' Compulsory Retirement Scheme, 1978, Clause (3) - Compulsory retirement - The Retirement Scheme does not apply to individuals who are "on probation" - The petitioner, who was promoted to Senior Cashier during her two-year probationary period, was not returned to her original position and was subsequently compulsorily retired from her new position - This Retirement Scheme does not apply to her retirement from the new position - Therefore, the retirement order cannot be upheld and is set aside.
