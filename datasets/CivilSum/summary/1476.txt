Constitution of India, Articles 30(1), 304 and 226 - Delhi School Education Act, Section 5 - Delhi School Education Rules, Rules 43, 98(2) and 96(3A) - Aided and Un-aided Private schools - Management of - The managements of privately managed aided and unaided educational institutions have the right to select and appoint employees, including the Head/Principal of the School, as per their choice under Article 30(1) of the Constitution - Govt. nominees in the selection committees only have an advisory role and their disagreement is not binding upon the management - The selection of the Committee is only recommendation and person not selected cannot claim a writ for appointment.
[Paras 24 and 28]
