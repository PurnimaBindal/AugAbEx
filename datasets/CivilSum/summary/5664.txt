Haryana Civil Services Pay Rules, 1987, Rule 3(2) - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - No discrimination or arbitrariness can be attributed to the classification of employees based on their length of service when it comes to equal pay for equal work and promotion to higher grades - Those appointed much later in time cannot claim parity with those appointed earlier if their length of service has earned them a higher scale of pay - Clerks recruited prior to 1980 who were in the personal scale of Rs. 420-700 were given a higher revised scale of 1,200-2,040 as a measure of protection of their existing pay scale, while those recruited after 1980 who were in the pay scale of 400-660 were placed in the revised scale of 950-1,500 to be at par with the State Govt. pay scales - This is not an omission or commission, but rather a necessary protection of the existing pay scale of the seniors.
