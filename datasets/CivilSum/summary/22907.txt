According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 as well as Uttar Pradesh Cooperative Societies Act, 1965, Section 122-A and Uttar Pradesh Cooperative Dairy Federation and Milk Union Centralised Service Rules, 1984, Rules 2(k), 17(1) and 17(3), a post which has not been in existence for five years cannot be considered as a regular post.
As a result, a temporary employee's work and conduct can be reviewed and his services can be terminated with one month's notice if they are found unsatisfactory.
