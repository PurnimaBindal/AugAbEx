Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Sections 19(1) 2(p) - Ex-gratia payment - The payment of ex-gratia must be restricted to the relevant year mentioned in the settlement between the employer and the union.
The ex-gratia amount is paid in order to achieve assured cooperation, discipline, and harmony by the workers.
In the year of the dispute, the employer refused to pay ex-gratia since the union had resorted to strike which led to lockout.
In absence of any settlement, the ex-gratia for the disputed year could not be paid.

Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Schedule 3 item 5 - Ex-gratia Bonus - The ex-gratia bonus is paid to employees for all previous years, whose pay exceeds bonus ceiling.
The tribunal refused to pay the ex-gratia bonus as it was treated on the same footing as the ex-gratia paid on account of settlement.
The matter was remanded back to consider whether the union has made out a case for payment of ex-gratia bonus since the payment of bonus is a service condition and constitutes a customary bonus.
[Paras 13, 18, and 20]  C. Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act 1972, Section 20, Schedule 2 Item 5 - Recognized Union - It is an unfair labor practice if the employer refuses to bargain collectively in good faith with a recognized union.
Such unions are entitled as a matter of right to meet, discuss and reach a settlement.
