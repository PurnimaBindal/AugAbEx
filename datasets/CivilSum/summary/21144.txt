Indian Medical Council Act of 1956, Section 15, as well as Indian Medicine Central Council Act of 1970, Section 17, and United Provinces Medical Act of 1917, Section 30 state that the State government, District Magistrates, and Chief Medical Officers are responsible for stopping unauthorized medical practitioners whose practices are hazardous to human life.
The UP State Government is required to take steps to stop unqualified/unregistered practitioners from carrying out medical practice in the state by following the guidelines pointed out by the Court.
