Departmental proceedings - Natural justice - Dismissal from service - When the punishing authority disagrees with the findings of the enquiring authority, they must communicate the reasons in detail to the delinquent who must be given the opportunity to offer comments.
The delinquent must also be given the chance for a personal hearing before the final punishment order is passed - but can decline the opportunity.
The punishing authority must consider all the material on record as well as any representation by the delinquent, and this entire process is not in violation of natural justice principles.
[Paras 10 and 11]  B. Quantum of punishment - Article 226 of the Constitution of India - Regarding court interference in the proportionality of punishment, the scope is limited and the court will not interfere with the decision unless it's illogical or suffers from procedural impropriety or is shocking to the court's conscience.
