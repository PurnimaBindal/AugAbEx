Rule 23 of the Haryana State Supply and Marketing Cooperative Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1969, is connected to Article 16 of the Indian Constitution and discusses Promotion, Seniority, and has been challenged over the promotion of a Field Inspector to the rank of Field Officer.
The Administrative Committee appointed the petitioner on an ad hoc basis for the ex cadre post of Senior Sales Officer until a regular officer is appointed.
The post of Senior Sales Officer was previously the same pay scale as that of Manager A Grade and was redesignated as such resulting in the change affected the Field Officer, one of the sources for promotion to the post of District Manager.
The writ was granted ordering the transfer of the petitioner from the cadre of Senior Sales Officer to that of Manager A grade to be not in order and cannot be sustained as the seniority and opportunity of hearing for private respondents was not considered.
B Rule 23 of the Haryana State Supply and Marketing Cooperative Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1969, connected to Article 16 of the Indian Constitution, specifies that the promotion or appointment to the post of Manager A Grade can only be via direct recruitment, transfer on deputation from any department of the Government or any institution, or by promotion from the rank of Field Officer.
As the petitioner was holding an ex cadre post of Senior Sales Officer, they are not entitled to seek promotion as Manager A Grade.
