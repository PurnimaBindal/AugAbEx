Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Gujarat Secondary Education Act, Sections 35(4) and (5), Regulation 20(1) - Promotion - Eligibility - Post of Head Master - Selection criteria is based on Merit-cum- Seniority - Petitioner argues he is Senior and eligible while Private Respondent does not fulfill requirements - Tribunal dismisses Petitioner's claim - Respondents argue Private Respondent is meritorious and eligible - Seniority is considered only when candidates have equal merits - Private Respondent is more suitable and meritorious - Hence, no illegality in the selection process - Petition dismissed.
