Road Transport Corporation Act (64 of 1950), Section 45 - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Conductor - Bribery charge - Removal from service - Quantum of punishment - Interference - Tribunal deemed enquiry report fair and justifiable, leading to the conclusion that removal from service was warranted - Past conduct has no bearing on the decision of quantum of punishment or taking a lenient view.
