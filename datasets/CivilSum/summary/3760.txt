Constitution of India, Article 226 - Alternative remedy - Availing of - Petitioner requests the quashing of the seniority list - However, due to a previous challenge of the seniority list before the Tribunal that is still pending, the petitioner cannot maintain a petition under Article 226 - As the petitioner has already availed the remedy by appeal under ordinary law, two alternative remedies cannot be pursued simultaneously.
