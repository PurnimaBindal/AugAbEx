Central Civil Services Pension Rules, 1972, Rule 49 - Pension and Gratuity - Retirement/Qualifying service/Date of superannuation - The petitioner, who joined GREF as an ex-serviceman in 1962 and retired in 1978, had a dispute over his date of birth which led to his pension not being fixed.
However, based on the department's admitted facts, the petitioner had rendered 12 years of service till 1974 and was entitled to pension and gratuity based on his qualifying service of 10 years.
The court directed the release of pension and gratuity, excluding the period alleged to be beyond the date of superannuation.
