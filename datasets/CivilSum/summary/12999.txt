Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary proceedings - Imposing a minor penalty on the petitioner in disciplinary proceedings - No regular inquiry was conducted, nor any evidence was recorded.
The decision was based on an undisclosed report by the Commandant and the petitioner's reply.
The petitioner's account was supported by the only corroborating evidence, and hence, the decision of the Disciplinary Authority was deemed perverse.
Resulting proceedings and penalty order were quashed.
[Paras 15, 16 and 17]
