Under Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rules 6.17(3) and 835, Family Pension, Gratuity, Provident Fund, and the Employees Insurance Scheme, the widow and children of a deceased employee are eligible to receive family pension under a special scheme.
The mother of the deceased employee is not eligible for family pension under the scheme or by way of Will of the deceased.
Family pension does not form part of the estate of the deceased or provide any right to bequeath it or to make a nomination.
An employee's widow is entitled to the family pension when there is one.
Furthermore, the mother is not eligible for any share from the GPF, gratuity, leave encashment, etc., as these properties do not form part of the estate of the deceased.
The mother, however, is eligible to share the insurance policy's proceeds with her daughter-in-law in equal proportions.
