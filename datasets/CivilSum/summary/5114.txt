Under the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 2(F), personal drivers of Managers are considered employees.
Drivers must be paid by the establishment according to their length of service and also for any overtime used for company duty beyond normal working hours.
Even without evidence of exclusive supervision and control by Managers, such drivers are still considered employees under the Act of 1952.
