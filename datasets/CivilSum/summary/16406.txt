Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Admission - Reservation - Sportsmen quota - Admission to Bachelor of Engineering in the 1st year course - Reservation of 2% seats for sports persons - Candidates must have passed 'one lower qualifying examination' from schools/colleges recognised by Chandigarh Administration - Petitioner denied admission due to not passing 10+1 examination from said institutions - Liberal interpretation of 'lower qualifying examination' necessary to avoid overlooking exceptional candidates due to technicalities - Respondents ordered to grant petitioner admission.
