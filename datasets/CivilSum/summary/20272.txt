Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Transfer - Conditions of transfer - Reduction to time scale - If a transfer is made on request and it is accepted that the applicant would be treated as a fresh appointee in the original time scale at the transferred destination, the transfer must occur - Rectification of wrong fixation of pay with order of recovery of excess amount paid - Although the order of the authority was appropriate, the excess amount paid should be recovered in easy instalments.
