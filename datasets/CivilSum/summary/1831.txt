Constitution of India, Article 311 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 300 - Double Jeopardy - Criminal offence - Departmental inquiry - The petitioner, who was involved in a criminal case, had an enquiry held against him and his services were terminated during the pendency of the case.
The discharge of the petitioner means they have no right to claim reinstatement, and the action of the department can be considered as double jeopardy.
[Para 13]  B Civil Procedure Code, Order 23 Rule 1 - Constitution of India, Article 226 - A writ petition was withdrawn without liberty to file a fresh petition as it was not pressed.
Therefore, a second petition on the same cause is not maintainable.
