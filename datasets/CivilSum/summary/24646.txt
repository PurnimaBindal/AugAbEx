Constitution of India, Article 309 - PWD (Public Health Branch) Rules, 1961, Rules 9(3) and 11 - Promotion to post of Executive Engineer - Five years of service as Assistant Executive Engineer is required for promotion - However, relaxation is granted to direct recruits by Government under Second Proviso to Rule 9(3) - The promotion obtained by the promotee is legal - The promotee need not pass departmental examination in Public Health Branch again since they have already passed it while working in the Building and Road Branch - Additionally, the non-completion of the probationary period of two years on the post of Assistant Executive Engineer will have no effect on the validity of the promotion - The appeal has been dismissed.
