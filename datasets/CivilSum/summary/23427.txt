Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 320 - Writ jurisdiction - The High Court has the discretion to grant writs under Article 226, however, this cannot be used by a member of the Public Service Commission to challenge the functions performed by the Commission according to established procedures.
[Para 28]
Constitution of India, Article 318 - Conditions of service - Amenities - Chairman and member of the Public Service Commission - The facilities and amenities received by the Chairman cannot be expected to be the same for members of the Commission.
Withdrawal of facilities for members does not violate Article 318.
[Para 32]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Interim orders - The High Court's power to issue interim orders under Article 226 should be exercised in accordance with established judicial norms.
The Court cannot grant interim orders that do not aid or contribute to the main relief sought in the writ petition.
