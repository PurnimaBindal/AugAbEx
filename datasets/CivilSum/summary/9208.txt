"The Haryana Ware-housing Corporation, which is constituted and financed by the State Govt. and thus falls under the definition of 'State' in the Industrial Disputes Act, cannot terminate an employee without proper notice and opportunity in accordance with Section 25F Even if a workman has alternative remedies under both ordinary law and the Industrial Disputes Act, they may choose either one.
The respondent had previously had their services terminated for a conviction that they did not disclose, but the corporation did not have a rule or advertisement stating that such individuals were ineligible for appointment.
Even on the grounds of concealment, termination without notice is not permissible.
Furthermore, the Haryana Government has already established policies for the rehabilitation of convicts.
As such, the termination order was arbitrary, illegitimate, and in violation of natural justice principles.
The Corporation's appeal was dismissed."
[Paras 5 and 6]
