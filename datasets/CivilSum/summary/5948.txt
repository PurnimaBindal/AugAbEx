Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 14 - Regularisation of service - If an individual is appointed as a trainee and has no adverse remarks during their training period, an extension of the training period is not permissible.
Additionally, appointment to probation and subsequent appointment for tenure of two years upon expiration of probation is arbitrary.
Any appointee in this situation is entitled to absorption into regular service.
