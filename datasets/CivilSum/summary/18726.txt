Constitution of India, Article 226 - Nature of appointment - Transfer to other jobs - Petitioner was appointed and confirmed as Senior Technical Assistant in an ex cadre post, but later transferred out of it and assigned other duties without his consent.
The respondent argued that the petitioner's assigned apparatus had become disfunctional.
However, the petitioner had passed up on promotional opportunities and benefits available to him in his cadre.
The order transferring the petitioner out of his ex cadre post and any disciplinary action taken against him for not joining the new post are quashed, and the petitioner is entitled to all consequential and ancillary reliefs.
[Paras 9 and 10]
