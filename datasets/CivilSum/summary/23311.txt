According to the rulings of the Court, it is evident that the power of the Supreme Court and High Court as Courts of Record cannot be limited or restricted by ordinary legislation, including the Contempt of Courts Act.
This inherent power is vast and boundless, as stated in Articles 129 and 215, and is not constrained by any restrictions.
It is relevant to look to English authorities that deal with the power of superior Courts as Courts of Record.
