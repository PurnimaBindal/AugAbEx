Bye-laws of Central Board of Secondary Examination - Bye-laws 73, 102, 16, 21 and 22 - Admission to Class-X - Student must have cleared her IX examination to be eligible for Class-X admission.
Overlooking legal requirements in consideration of sympathy is not a solution and can lead to more violations.
