Constitution of India, Article 14 - Appointment - A widow, the petitioner, seeks appointment on compassionate grounds - Her husband, a watchman, passed away while working for Velacherry Town Panchayat after undergoing a procedure under the Family Planning Scheme - After his death, Velacherry Town Panchayat merged with Madras Corporation - The petitioner is deemed eligible for appointment - Madras Corporation cannot argue that her deceased husband was not its employee.
