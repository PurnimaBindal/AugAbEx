The petitioner for admission to the MDS course was denied an NOC due to having less than 5 years of service on the last date of making the application, even though a new policy was introduced during the selection process that reduced the required experience to 3 years.
The court dismissed the petition, reasoning that even though the new policy was prospective, accepting the petitioner's plea would be discriminatory towards candidates who did not apply under the new policy since the petitioner was ineligible on the last date of making the application.
