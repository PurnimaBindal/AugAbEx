Disciplinary Proceedings - An employee who is promoted from Clerk-cum-Cashier to an officer's rank and was charged with misappropriation of funds can face disciplinary action under the rules governing officers.
This action is neither illegal nor outside the jurisdiction.
As an employee progresses in their career, they must be responsible for all their actions, both beneficial and detrimental.

Misconduct - If an officer commits misconduct while working as a workman, they must be charged under the disciplinary rules for workmen.
