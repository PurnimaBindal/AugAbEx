Constitution of India, 1950, Article 226 - Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Sections 2 and 10 - Writ for restructuring of pensionary benefits - Petitioner was employed as a teacher and had retired three months before the Act came into force - `Existing employee' defined in the Act refers to employees who worked 12 months before the Act came into force - The petitioner falls under the category of existing employees and is eligible for the benefits.
