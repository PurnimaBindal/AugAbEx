Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Indian Penal Code, 1860, Section 379 - Dismissal - Acquittal in Criminal case - Departmental proceedings - The departmental action taken against the petitioner was not precluded by the order of acquittal by the court as it was not an honorable acquittal by way of complete exoneration but by giving the benefit of doubt.
Hence, the power of department to proceed against the accused was not taken away even after the delinquent was acquitted.
The impugned order passed by Tribunal was upheld.
[Paras 9, 10, 11]
Service Matter - Dismissal - Acquittal in Criminal Case - Even though there was a delay of about six years in initiating departmental proceedings after the petitioner was acquitted in 1983, the department had the right to do so.
The impugned order of the Tribunal affirming the dismissal order was held to be legal.
The Department was not at fault in initiating departmental proceedings against the petitioner after getting a copy of the judgment rendered by the Trial Magistrate.
Hence, the petition was dismissed.
[Paras 12 and 13]
