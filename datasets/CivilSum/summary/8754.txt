As per Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo) and 25F regarding daily wage and retrenchment, calculation for 240 days of service includes Sundays and paid holidays.
Any worker who has completed 240 days of service in the establishment cannot be retrenched without complying with the provisions of section 25F.
[Para 3] B.
In the case of termination of a lady employed on daily wages under Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11, back wages were awarded in violation of section 25F.
The petitioner was unmarried at the time of the termination and took up a job in a private concern for survival.
Later, after marriage, she was awarded 60% of backwages from the date of the demand notice.
