"Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Section 10-A of the Medical Council Act address admissions to Medical Colleges including the establishment of new institutions and increase in intake capacity.
In cases of pending matters regarding permission and approval of the Medical Council, the court must refrain from issuing any interim directions to admit students, as it may compromise the standards of education.
The court should only consider issuing such directions in exceptional circumstances and take all precautions before doing so.
[Paras 4, 12, 13 & 15]"
