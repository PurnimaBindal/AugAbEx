Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 11 - Delay of proceedings before the labour Court - A deceased workman's widow's claim was rejected ex parte without considering her plight - Despite various adjournments granted to management and several adjournments due to the presiding officer being on leave, only one adjournment was sought by the widow - The tribunal held this rejection to be unjustified and has remitted the case to the Labour Court to be reviewed on merit.
