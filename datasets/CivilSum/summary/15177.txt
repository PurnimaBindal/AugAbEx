Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Ad hoc appointment- Regularisation of service - The petitioners, who were appointed on an ad hoc basis, have gained enough experience through the extension of their appointments from time to time.
They are qualified Engineers and are eligible for the posts of Assistant Engineers.
Although there are posts for Assistant Engineers, the ad-hoc nature of their appointments should not be exploited.
Therefore, the petitioners are entitled to the pay scale admissible to the post of Assistant Engineers.
[Paras 14 and 16]
