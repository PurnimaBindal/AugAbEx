Constitution of India, Articles 14, 25, 26, 29, 30, 226 - Minority Educational Institution - Fundamental right of minority educational institutions guaranteed - Directions given to such institutions to admit 50% of students on centralized admission list - Respondents argue measures not discriminatory to general category students - Articles 29 and 30 grant minority fundamental, cultural, and educational rights - Regulations imposed can be applicable to minority institution - Regulations are reasonable if they preserve the minority's right to administer institution - Respondents have power to direct minority educational institutions to fill 50% of seats from centralized admission list.
