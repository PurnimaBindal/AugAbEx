The Delhi State Civil Supplies Corporation Ltd (Recruitment and Promotion) Policy Rules, Rule 9, which outlines the validity and guidelines of promotion, was challenged by the Petitioners.
However, Rule 9 was found valid and not discriminatory, as it differentiated between administrative staff and Sales Representatives based on the terms of appointment and pay scales.
The Petitioners were initially recruited for a limited period, and their services were regularized in 1985 after screening.
It was contended that Rule 9 had deprived the Petitioners of the benefit of continuous service from 1981.
Nonetheless, it was found that the exclusion of previous service at the time of screening was not arbitrary or unreasonable.
Ultimately, the petition was dismissed.
