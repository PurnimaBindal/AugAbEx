Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Guru Nanak Dev University Calendar Volume III, 1999, Chapter XI, Regulation 8(i) proviso (ii) and Volume II, 1999, Chapter IX, Regulation 12 - Requirement of getting Gold Medal - Re- evaluation - Candidates will not be awarded a medal based on the results of re-evaluation, as stated in Regulation 8(i).
However, Regulation 12 stipulates that the marks obtained in the re-evaluation will supersede the original marks.
The Proviso to Regulation 8(i) is in violation of Article 14 of the Constitution of India as it does not conform to Regulation 12 of the University Calendar.
As a result, the outcome of the re-evaluation will be the only final result.
One exam cannot have two different results for different purposes.
[Paras 12 to 14]
