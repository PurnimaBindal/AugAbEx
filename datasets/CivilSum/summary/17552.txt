For Respondent :- Atul Lakhanpal, Advocate.
A.
Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Service Bye-Laws of the Corporation, Clause 3.1(b) - Promotion - Reversion - Qualification - The Board of Directors of the Corporation did not ratify the promotion of the Petitioner to AGM (Export) due to him lacking the required qualifications, even though no qualifications were mentioned for promotion.
[Para 4] B Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Service By-Laws of the Corporation Clause 3.4(a) - Promotion - Seniority - The seniority of persons appointed to posts carrying the same scale of pay shall be determined by the dates of their substantive appointment.
The senior persons were not considered while considering the case of Petitioner, in accordance with the By-Laws 34(a).
[Para 4] C. Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226/227 - Promotion - Seniority - The order of promotion was without prejudice to the right of seniors, and it was found to be violative of Article 16 of the Constitution by not considering senior persons.
[Para 4] D. Constitution of India, Article 226/227 - Promotion - Reversion - Respondent was justified in passing the impugned order of reversion due to the promotion order being conditional and the cases of senior persons not being considered.
[Para 4] E Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Promotion - Reversion - Writ - The Respondent-Corporation was directed to consider the Petitioner qualified for promotion to the post of Assistant General Manager (Export) or the equivalent post, and to consider his case along with his seniors.
