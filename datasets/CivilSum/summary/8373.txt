Kerala State Handloom Development Corporation Ltd Service Rules, Rule 107 - Manual for Disciplinary Proceedings, Clause 64(2) - Dismissal - Re-instatement - Suspension of execution of sentence pending disposal of appeal - An employee who has been dismissed from the corporation on the grounds of a criminal conviction by the CBI cannot be reinstated even if execution of the sentence is later suspended pending the outcome of an appeal - Rule 107 of the Service Rules does not allow for the dismissal order to be revoked upon suspension of sentence by the Appellate Court - Therefore, the dismissed employee cannot be treated as being under suspension until the appeal is disposed of and cannot be reinstated due to the suspension of sentence.
