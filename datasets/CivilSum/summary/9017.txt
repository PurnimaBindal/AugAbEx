The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 25-F and 25-H - Back wages - Retrenchment - The workman was not terminated due to any deliberate intent by the petitioner-management.
The petitioner-society closed down 14 centers, causing some employees to be laid off due to significant recurring losses.
The workman's single statement alone could not refute the evidence provided by management, as he had not worked for several years and was only seeking back wages.
[Para 5] B The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 25-F and 25-H - Retrenchment - Back wages - The workman was laid off while persons other than workman were either re-employed or recruited as per advertisement.
After his retrenchment, the workman had found employment elsewhere and was only pursuing the case before the Labor Court to receive back wages, which could amount to several thousands of rupees.
It wasn't clear why the workman issued a demand notice to the management more than two years after others were re-employed.
As a result, he was not entitled to receive back wages.
