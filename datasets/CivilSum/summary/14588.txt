Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion - Punishment - Stoppage of increments - Petitioner was punished with the stoppage of one increment and the punishment had cumulative effects.
The University's plan indicated that the penalty would remain in effect for ten years.
The promotion merely upgraded the teacher's pay scale while in the same post with the same duties.
After ten years from the date of the punishment, the petitioner should have been considered for promotion under the plan.
