Articles 14 and 32 of the Constitution of India, Sections 2(d) and 3(2)(d) of the Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993 - Selection and Appointment - Inclusion of Police Officers in the National Human Rights Commission is not excluded by the Protection of Human Rights Act or the Paris Principles or UN Resolution - Two people with knowledge or practical experience in human rights issues are eligible to become members of the Commission - Public perception about the Police will not influence the decision to appoint eligible candidates - Appointing a former Police Officer to the Commission does not conflict with the provisions of the Protection of Human Rights Act.

Section 4 of the Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993 - Appointment - Selection - The Act outlines a self-contained procedure for the appointment of members to the NHRC, and no other mandatory procedures may be added - [Para 12] C. Sections 2(d), 3(2)(d), and 4 of the Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993 - Appointment and Selection - The Act does not prescribe a quorum for the selection process - No specific procedure is outlined - Consultation by circulation is not allowed under the Act - If one member of the six-member committee does not provide input, the other five members' opinions will not be invalidated - The mere existence of a vacancy in the committee will not invalidate the appointment of a Chairman or member.
