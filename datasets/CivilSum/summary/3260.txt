Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Government Residences (Chandigarh Administration Pool) Allotment Rules, 1972, Rule 26 - Allotment of Government houses - Rule 26 allows for broad power, only to be exercised by the Administrator, in relation to out of turn allotment - Comparison between allottees to determine arbitrariness is not necessary - When demand for houses exceeds supply, equal distribution is not possible.
[Paras 11 & 12]
Government Residences (Chandigarh Administration Pool) Allotment Rules, 1972, Rule 26 - Haryana Government employee has no right to question the allotment of UT or PGI employees within their quotas.
[Para 15]
Public Interest Litigation - Allotment of Government houses - Writ petition seeks relief for specific house, not for other Government Servants - Petition cannot be converted into a public interest litigation - Judicial process must not be misused for individual whims under the guise of public interest litigation.
