Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(j) - Orissa Service Code, Rule 71(a) - Order of compulsory retirement is not a punishment and is based on subjective satisfaction of the Government, so principles of natural justice do not need to be followed - High Court can only interfere if order is mala fide, based on no evidence or arbitrary.

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Compulsory retirement/Adverse confidential reports - Entire service record, including confidential records, to be considered before taking decision - Adverse remarks lose their significance if promoted based on merit and not seniority.

Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(j) - Orissa Service Code, Rule 71(a) First proviso - Order of compulsory retirement based on subjective satisfaction of Government - Cannot challenge decision based on public interest, only if it is arbitrary or based on collateral grounds.
[Paras 31 and 32]

Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Compulsory retirement/Adverse remarks - Only adverse remarks need to be communicated, not every observation.
