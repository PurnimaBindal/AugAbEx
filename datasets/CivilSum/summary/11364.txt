Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion from retrospective date - Arrears of pay of promotion post - The doctrine of no work no pay should not be applied if the competent authority did not promote the employee at his due date without sufficient reasons or fault of the employee - It is unfair to deny the employee his rightful claim for promotion and the benefits of the post, even after giving him promotion from the date his juniors were promoted illegally - The petitioner is entitled to arrears of pay from the date of his retrospective promotion.
