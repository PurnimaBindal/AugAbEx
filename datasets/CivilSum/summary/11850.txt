Selection had to be based on a composite process that incorporated written tests, type tests, trade tests, and viva voce, according to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India - Specific marks for each test were not prescribed - 50% marks were reserved for viva voce - The selection was arbitrary and subject to being quashed.
[Paras 9, 12 and 13]

The Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16, and 226 stipulate that when selection is made through a composite process, including written, trade, etc. tests, giving more than 15% of marks for viva voce alone is arbitrary - The selection was tainted.
[Para 12]
