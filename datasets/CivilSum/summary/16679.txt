Punjab Civil Services (Revised Scales of Pay) Rules, 1979, Rule 71(i) - Annual increment date disputes - Petitioners drawing annual increment dated from their pay fixation in the revised scale - Personnel with salaries below the minimum revised scale received annual increments after a year from the revision, whereas, persons with salaries exceeding that amount had an unchanged increment date - The petitioners will be allowed annual increments in revised pay scale from their entitled old scale date - Arrears to be paid accordingly.
