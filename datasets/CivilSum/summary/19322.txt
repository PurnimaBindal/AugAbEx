According to the Haryana and Punjab Agricultural Universities Act, 1970, Section 14 and the Statue of University, Chapter IV clause 4(2) (vi), the Board of Management has the power to appoint the Head of the Department of Horticulture in the Agriculture University.
The statutes do not allow for the Vice-Chancellor to create another selection committee if there is a difference of opinion amongst the members of the original committee.
Additionally, the Vice-Chancellor cannot withhold the recommendations of the Selection Committee simply because it was not unanimous.
Therefore, the Vice-Chancellor's actions of not putting up the recommendations of the Selection Committee before the Board of Management and forming an ad hoc selection committee were ultra vires of the statutes.
The appointment of Respondent 3 as Head of Department of Horticulture has been set aside, and the Vice-Chancellor has been directed to present the recommendations of the Selection Committee with his comments before the Board of Management for a final decision.
