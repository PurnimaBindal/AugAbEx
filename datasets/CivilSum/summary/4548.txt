Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 244(2) - Compulsory Retirement - The petitioner should not be retired prematurely based on insufficient evidence proving that it would be in the public interest to do so.
There is no report or adverse material against the petitioner that would support his premature retirement and the reviewing authority did not subjectively evaluate his record.
The impugned orders are arbitrary and must be set aside.
The respondents should treat the petitioner in regular service until his superannuation, and provide him with pensionary benefits.
