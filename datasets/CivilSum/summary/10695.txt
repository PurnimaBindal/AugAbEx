Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb), 10, 25-F - Daily wage worker - Termination of employment - Breach of 1947 Act's provisions - Government determined that termination was covered by Section 2(oo)(bb) - Assessment of order - Authority acting in an administrative, rather than quasi-judicial, capacity to make determination on whether dispute exists and requires adjudication - However, authority required to ensure that dispute exists and requires adjudication, and cannot act illegally or on illegal considerations - Petitioner was a daily wage worker employed under public employment and, therefore, governed by regulations, and completion of 240 days of work alone was insufficient to invoke Section 24-F of the Act.
