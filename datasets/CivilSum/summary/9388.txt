Bureau of Indian Standards (Recruitment to Administration, Finance and other Posts) Regulations, 1988 - Bureau of Indian Standards (Recruitment to Administration, Finance and other Posts) Regulations, 2007 - Promotion to the post of Section Officer - Stenographers cannot be promoted to the post of Section Officer because they are not in the prescribed feeder cadre.
The issuance of circulars contrary to the rules cannot add stenographers to the eligible feeder cadres.
