The petitioner claims that they should be admitted to LL.B.
Ist Semester because candidates from the scheduled castes category could not have been admitted against reserved category seats if they had higher merit than general category candidates.
The respondent-University's action of granting admission to reserve category candidates only against reserved seats is held to be discriminatory and arbitrary, violating university policy for giving admission to LL.B.
Course of 1994-95.
[Para 10]

The Court cannot ignore the candidates' right to consideration for admission according to their merit solely because they may not have filed a writ petition due to poverty or lack of resources.
The Courts must respect the pervasive and wide right of equality while passing orders under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
