Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Departmental proceedings - Natural justice - Enquiry report - To ensure the delinquent has the opportunity to defend themselves, a show cause notice with reasons for the disagreement of the punishing authority with the findings of the enquiry officer is necessary - If such reasons are not given prior to or after the passing of the punishment order, it would be against natural justice and render the order invalid.
[Paras 7 and 8]
