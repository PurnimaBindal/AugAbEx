Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Sections 2(vi), 15(2) - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25F, 25F(b), 33-C(2) - Claim of backwages - The definition of `Wages' under the 1936 Act covers such claim, including any remuneration payable under any award or settlement and any sum payable by reason of termination of employment, as stated in Section 2(vi)(d).

Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Sections 2(vi), 15(2) - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Award - Even if the contention is that the claim should be made under Section 33-C(2) of the 1947 Act, the authority under the 1936 Act has the jurisdiction to decide the application filed under Section 15, despite the jurisdiction also being held by the Labour Court.
Therefore, there is no illegality or irregularity in granting the amount as wages by the authority.
