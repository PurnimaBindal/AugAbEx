According to Sections 3, 4-A(3)(a) and (b) of the Workman's Compensation Act, 1923, in the event of a dispute over the penalty, an award of indemnification must be provided.
An insurance company is liable to indemnify the insured for compensation, interest, and penalty as long as the liability under the Act is covered by the policy.
This is supported by the fact that the company accepted an additional premium to cover all liabilities.
