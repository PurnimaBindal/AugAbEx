According to Articles 311 and 226 of the Constitution of India, when a constable fails to attend election duty and does not provide a reason for their absence within the designated period of time, a compulsory retirement order may be issued.
However, if the order of dismissal is considered excessively severe when compared to the charge of unauthorised absence from duty, an appeals tribunal may vacate the order.
Under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, there is no need for interference with this decision.
