Constitution of India, Articles 31, 19(1)(f) and 21 - Employment against acquisition of land - State is obligated by the principle of promissory estoppel - The State's policy providing a promise of employment at the time of land acquisition must be enforced, even if later rescinded - The right to compensation does not negate the right to employment guaranteed by the State's existing policy at the time - The State is instructed to pay lease money for delayed compensation and provide employment in accordance with the existing policy.
