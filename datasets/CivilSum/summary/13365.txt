The request for migration from Medical College Bangalore to Medical College Delhi has been rejected based on Clause 3 of Delhi University Ordinance upheld by a Division Bench, citing Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, Medical Council Act, 1956, Section 33, and Graduate Medical Education Regulations, Regulation 6.
The University cannot be compelled to forward the application for migration received by the University to the Council.
