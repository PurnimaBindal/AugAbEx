Constitution of India, Article 226 - Employees Provident Fund & Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 2(a), 5A, 6, 6A - Employees Pension Scheme 1995 - The rejection of the scheme by an Assistant Provident Fund Commissioner is not sustainable as the right to recommend lies with the Central Provident Fund Commissioner.
The Regional Provident Fund Commissioner cannot use guidelines that are only a result of the scheme and the Act.
Therefore, the order of rejection is set aside.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Employees Pension Scheme 1995 - The matter of the interlocutory application regarding the scheme is different from the prayer in the petition.
The court is bound to accept such a cause if it is an obvious outcome of the original judicial proceeding, as the governance of the court is prevailing.
