Constitution of India, Article 16 - Ad hoc appointment - Vacancies on post of Lecturers - A G.O.
allows for ad hoc appointment until a regularly selected candidate is available - A petitioner selected for one such post challenges the stipulation of time limiting the appointment - Based on the Manju Vati case, if a regularly selected candidate is not available, the petitioner is eligible to continue - However, once such a candidate is available, the petitioner cannot claim any right to continue on the post.

Civil Journal 1009.
