According to Section 10A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, an award from an Industrial Tribunal or Labour Court cannot be enforced if it is inconsistent with an employee's statutory service conditions.
There are rules in place that dictate a retirement age of 55 years.
Therefore, an award that allows a workman to retire at age 58 cannot be put into effect.
