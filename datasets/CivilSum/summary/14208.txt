Article 226 of the Indian Constitution and Section of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Departmental enquiry, Reduction in Rank - Reduction in rank to the post of messenger constituted illegal termination for an employee, who was initially employed as a salesman with a cooperative society.
The Industrial Tribunal passed award in favour of the employee, holding that his reduction in rank amounted to termination.
However, the petition was dismissed as the employee was not being reverted to a Class IV post from the higher position of clerk or salesman, but to a Class III post, thus the reversion order amounted to illegal termination of services.
[Para 4] B Article 226 of the Indian Constitution - Departmental enquiry, Reduction in Rank - A departmental enquiry conducted without joining the delinquent cannot be considered a valid enquiry and cannot be used to justify termination of services or reversion as a punishment.
