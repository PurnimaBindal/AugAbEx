The petitioner challenged an order of dismissal from service for remaining absent without leave, arguing that the enquiry was conducted in his absence and no show cause notice was served before imposing punishment.
However, it was held that Article 311 only requires sufficient opportunity to explain the conduct and since the petitioner did not avail of the opportunity afforded to him, the mandate of Article 311 was complied with.
The enquiry was conducted in accordance with rules and there was no fault found with it.
[Paras 5 and 6]

The petitioner was dismissed from service for absence without leave, but the punishment was reduced to compulsory retirement against his representation.
The appellate authority considered the charge of absence and decided to modify it to compulsory retirement based on the length of the petitioner's service and his medical conditions.
The punishment was awarded on the basis of enquiry and the order was deemed to be fair and just, requiring no interference.
[Para 7]
