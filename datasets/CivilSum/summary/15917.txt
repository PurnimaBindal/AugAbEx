Constitution of India, Article 226- Enquiry- Quashing of- The nature of charges against a delinquent employee affects the enquiry- There is no rule indicating that delay should result in the dropping of enquiry proceedings- The Government does not have unrestricted powers to conduct an enquiry indefinitely- Enquiry must conclude within six months.
[Para 3 and 4]
Constitution of India, Article 226- Pension- A 10% cut to petitioner's pension was imposed due to pending enquiry- Petitioner was charge-sheeted just before retiring from the post of S.E.
and no Enquiry Officer was appointed for the case- State cannot avoid paying pension based on the anticipation of disciplinary action- Impugned order quashed.
