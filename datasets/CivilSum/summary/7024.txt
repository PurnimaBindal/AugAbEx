Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F - Termination of service - Ex parte award directing reinstatement and backwages - The denial of signatures on summons does not rebut the presumption of proper service; management's contention that it was not properly served is not tenable.
[Para 9]
Backwages - The tribunal must consider the contention of the workman not being gainfully employed and pass an order about back wages.
There is no presumption that the workman remained unemployed all along.
[Para 12]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F - Backwages - The decision regarding the award of back wages should weigh all factors, including the length of service, nature of appointment, and special qualifications required for the job.
[Para 11]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F - Ex parte award - The application for setting aside the ex parte award can only be considered by the tribunal if it is filed before the expiry of 30 days of its publication.
