Constitution of India, Article 311 - Army Act, 1950, Section 18 - Termination of services in exercise of pleasure of President - Denial of Terminal benefits - If services are terminated solely on the President's pleasure, the denial of Terminal benefits is not permissible.
[Paras 6 and 8]

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Army Act, 1950, Section 18 - Pensionary benefits - Denial of on the ground of delay in making claim - Pensionary benefits accrue from month to month and is analogous to salary - Delay in making claim cannot be a ground to refuse pension - However, benefits can be restricted to three years prior to approaching the court - Court can refuse interest on arrears due to delay.
[Paras 8 and 9]
