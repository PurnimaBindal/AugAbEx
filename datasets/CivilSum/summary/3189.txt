Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Delhi Municipal Corporation Service (Control and Appeal) Regulation, 1959 - Unauthorised absence from duty - Insubordination - Removal from service - In the department proceedings, both charges against the petitioner for unauthorised absence and refusal to hand over the charge were proven.
Allegations of irregularity, unpunctuality, and refusal to obey orders were raised against the petitioner.
Since the petitioner refused to obey the transfer order and refused to hand over charge, he exhibited absolute insubordination.
Additionally, his unauthorised absence from duty was noted.
Thus, the penalty of removing him from service without disqualification for future employment cannot be said to be disproportionate to the charges proved.
