Petitioners, Inspectors of Central Excise at Calcutta, argue under Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 and Fundamental Rules, Rules 15(a), 22(a)(ii), 23 and 26(b)(i), that their pay scales of Rs. 425-800, revised to Rs. 500-900 w.e.f.
1.1.80, entitle them to receive their first increment in the new pay scale on the due date as in the old pay scale without waiting for 12 months.
