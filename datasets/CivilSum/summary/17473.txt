The Life Insurance Corporation of India (Employees) Pension Rules, Rule 3(1)(4) and 23, explain the option to resign from service.
One employee, who resigned in 1992 and opted for Provident Fund membership, later withdrew their fund and did not deposit it into the pension fund.
According to Rule 23, an employee's resignation from the Corporation's service leads to forfeiture of their entire past service, rendering them ineligible for pensionary benefits.
Ultimately, the petitioner's claims for pension were considered frivolous and appropriately rejected by the authorities.
