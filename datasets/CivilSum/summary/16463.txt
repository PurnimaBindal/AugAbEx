Railway Establishment Manual Vol.
I Rules 310 and 215(a) - Seniority - Promotion - Viva voce test - Estoppel - Passing of viva voce test for selection to OS-II post is mandatory - Candidates may be exempted from the requirement if they have been officiating in a higher post for more than 3 months - However, if a candidate accepts a lower post on mutual transfer and forgoes promotion, they cannot later claim the benefits of the higher post and are bound by the principles of estoppel - Once a candidate takes advantage of a mutual transfer, they cannot then claim what they forwent in that transfer - They cannot claim that their prior promotion should exempt them from the test.
[Paras 6 and 7]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Railway Establishment Manual Vol.
I Rules 310 and 215(a) - Estoppel - Promotion - Viva voce test - Passing the viva voce test for selection to OS-II post is mandatory - Once a candidate participates in the selection process with a full understanding that they had foregone their earlier promotion, they cannot turn around and claim exemption from the test and straightaway promotion - The doctrine of estoppel will apply in such cases.
[Para 7]
