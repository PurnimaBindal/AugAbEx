Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Discharge from service - A discharged Constable's order is not stigmatic since he was deemed unable to become an efficient police officer - Any argument that the order was actuated by misconduct is not sustainable - The power of discharge under Rule 1221 of the Punjab Police Rules cannot be challenged unless exercised with mala fide intention.
