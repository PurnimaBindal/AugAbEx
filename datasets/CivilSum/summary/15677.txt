Constitution of India, Article 226 - South Bengal State Road Transport Corporation Employees Service Regulations, Regulations 36 and 38 - Show Cause Notice - Admission - Disciplinary proceedings - Natural Justice - The admission must be considered in its entirety and cannot be culled out from a portion of the show cause.
The disciplinary authority is obligated to follow the procedure laid down in the Regulations, even if the writ petitioner has filed a show cause.
Non-supply of the list of documents and witnesses can cause prejudice to the delinquent.
The matter is remitted back to the disciplinary authority for fresh disposal.
[Paras 8, 9, 14, and 16]
