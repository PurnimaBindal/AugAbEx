Petitioner's service was regularised by the Vice Chancellor upon the recommendations of the Head of Department and Dean of College, which granted him a right to continue on the post.
However, after an order was passed by the Vice Chancellor, which relieved the petitioner on joining the regular incumbent of the post, the legality of the termination of service of the petitioner was questioned.
The action of the respondents was considered as vitiated as principles of natural justice were not followed, and the petitioner was denied an opportunity.
[Para 5]  B The petitioner's services were regularised, and it was considered unjust to remove him without granting him any opportunity.
[Para 1]  C. The petitioner cannot be denied his salary for the period he had worked, nor can his services be terminated retrospectively.
Failure to grant an opportunity was considered a material miscarriage of justice, and the termination vitiated.
