Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Affected party - Post of Additional Director, Public Relations, against the post of Press Secretary - Petitioner was not among the top three senior-most Joint Directors - One of the three senior-most Joint Directors was usually selected and appointed based on merit and seniority - The petitioner argues that the appointment of respondent No. 2 was not made by the Selection Committee in accordance with the Rules, and there is some merit to this claim - However, since no affected parties have raised objections and the petitioner was not in the zone of consideration, there is no good reason to cancel the selection - Selection should not be quashed.
