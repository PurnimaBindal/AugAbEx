Constitution of India, Article 226 - Government accommodation - Vacation - A Government employee is permitted to keep the allotted accommodation during his employment but must vacate after retirement.
[Para 18]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Government quarter - Sale of - Although the petitioner desired to buy his allotted quarter post-retirement, the Government did not intend to sell it - The petitioner cannot force the Government to sell and if they decline to sell, the court cannot compel the Government either - The petitioner directed to vacate the quarter.
