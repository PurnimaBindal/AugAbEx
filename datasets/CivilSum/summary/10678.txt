According to Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the question is whether a night supervisor is considered a workman.
The petitioner in this case has admitted to being appointed as a representative of management in several inquiries and has been responsible for the working of multiple watchmen under his supervision, as well as recommending and making disciplinary decisions.
Based on the evidence, it is apparent that the appellant was employed in a role with managerial and supervisory responsibilities and is not considered a workman.
Therefore, the dispute cannot be referred and the order of the single judge setting aside the award is upheld.
