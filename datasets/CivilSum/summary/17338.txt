Under the Workman's Compensation Act, 1923, a worker who lost both their eyes and has become totally handicapped was paid only Rs. 7,477 as compensation, which is hardly enough.
However, upon appeal, they were awarded Rs. 57,57675 as compensation and Rs. 20,727.63 as interest.
The order of compensation was deemed just and fair, and no interference was called for.
