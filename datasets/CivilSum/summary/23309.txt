Constitution of India, Article 226 - Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition Act, 1970, Sections 7, 12, 23 and 25 - Employer - If the principal employer does not register themselves under Section 7 of the Act, the person appointed cannot be considered a direct employee of the principal employer.
The Act does not provide for total abolition of contract labour, but it allows for abolition by the appropriate Government in specific cases under Section 10 of the Act.
Furthermore, the Court cannot issue any mandamus for considering contract labour as having become employees of the principal employer solely because the contractor or employer violated provisions of the Act or rules.
