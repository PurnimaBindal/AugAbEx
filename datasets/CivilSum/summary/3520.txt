Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Pension - Part-time teacher - The right to pension is subject to rules and cannot be considered inherent in employment - Part-time teachers are not covered by the pension scheme and are therefore not entitled to pension.
[Para 20]  B Bombay University Act, 1974, (Since Repealed), Sections 2(3), 42 - Pension-cum-Gratuity Scheme -Retiral benefits - Part-time teachers are not intended to be covered by the Scheme and are not entitled to said benefits - The definition of teacher in Section 2(30) cannot be imported into the said resolution - Classification between part-time lecturer and full-time teacher cannot be deemed unconstitutional - Part-time lecturers form a class by themselves.
[Paras 14, 18 and 21]  C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Classification between part-time lecturers and full-time teachers - Classifications for the purpose of granting retiral benefits cannot be considered unconstitutional or bad in law - Part-time lecturers form a class by themselves.
[Para 21]  D. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pension - Pension is not a matter of grace but is subject to rules and regulations.
