Constitution of India 1950, Articles 226/227 and 311 - Reduction of pay - The principle of natural justice was violated in the passing of the order - It is up to the respondents to decide if the petitioners are entitled to the previously granted pay scale - The order has been set aside and the respondents are free to pass a new order after a proper hearing.
