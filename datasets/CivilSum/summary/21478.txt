According to Section 33(2)(b) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, an approval of punishment by a Tribunal must be granted and an admission of guilt is the best piece of evidence unless it is shown to be made wrongly and why it should not be acted upon.
However, in a specific case, refusal of approval of punishment on the ground that the statements of passengers who paid excess money to the checking officer is in the nature of hearsay evidence, and admission of guilt on the part of the workman in writing not considered in proper perspective, indicates that the refusal to grant approval may not be proper.
