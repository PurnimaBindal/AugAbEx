As per Article 16 of the Constitution of India, the petitioners who were transferred from T/L (C) Division, Narwana to Operation Circle, Jind and had requested for the transfer were notified that they would lose their seniority of old circle.
While being promoted from the post of work-mates to the post of Assistant Linesmen in the new circle on 661997, a mistake based on their seniority in the earlier circle had occurred and their promotions were subsequently reverted on 2681997.
The promotion order was passed under the pretext of a mistaken impression and the impugned action is being initiated to rectify the error in conformity with rules, and therefore, the petition is dismissed.
