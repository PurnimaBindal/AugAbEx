Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Post of Constable in Haryana Armed Police - The petitioner's grievance centers around the fact that persons below him in order of merit have been given appointment.
The petitioner had a right to be considered for appointment before any one below him in order of merit could be appointed - and has been found to be medically fit with good character and antecedents.
The only objection to his appointment is his age, which is deemed wholly irrelevant as he was within the prescribed age limit both at the time of his submission of application and at the time of consideration of his claim by the Selecting Authority.
As a remedy, the respondents are directed to appoint petitioner as constable, with his seniority vis-à-vis other persons appointed in pursuance of the same process determined by the order of merit [Para 7].
