Constitution of India, Article 226 - MP Civil Services (Commutation of Pension) Rules, 1996, Rules 7,11(1) and 44 - Commuted value of pension - The petitioner has the right to receive the commuted value of pension that was submitted to the competent authority, even if the employee died before obtaining it.
This benefit cannot be denied to the nominee, who in this case is the petitioner, as he is the son and member of the family.
