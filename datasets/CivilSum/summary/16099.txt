Respondents represented by HN Mehtani, Advocate cited Telephone Allotment Rules, 1980, Rule 2.3 (e) and Constitution of India, Article 14 in a case regarding granting telephone connection under Non-OYT SS Category to employees retiring from P and T Department with basic pay of Rs. 1100 or more.
Employees retiring from other departments of Centre and State were also eligible, but only if their basic pay was Rs. 1600/- PM The court found the rule discriminatory and ultra vires to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, and held that employees of Central and State Governments who retired or left service drawing basic pay of Rs. 1100 (now 3000) have a right to grant of telephone connection in the Special Category.
The court also upheld the legitimacy of granting telephone under special category to employees of Centre and State Governments.
