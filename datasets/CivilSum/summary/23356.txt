UP Intermediate Education Act, 1921, Regulations 5(2) and 6(1) - Qualification - Promotion from CT grade teacher to LT grade teacher - The minimum qualification cannot be equated to the notification.
The notification serves as an executive order and the qualifications stated in it cannot supersede the statutory qualifications mentioned in the regulations.
The appellant was found possessing the minimum qualification on the relevant date and was thus granted a deemed promotion to the LT grade post.
