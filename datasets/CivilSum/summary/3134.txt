Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation of services - Daily wagers - Equal pay for equal work - While daily wagers are appointed to address unexpected or temporary increases in workload, some have been serving for more than twenty years, indicating permanency in their role - Government forest departments may undertake various schemes, transferring daily wagers to new projects- However, the government cannot exploit prevailing unemployment by employing daily wagers when permanent or semi-permanent employees are required - The prolonged employment of daily wagers at low wages without regularisation is a violation of Articles 14 and 16.
