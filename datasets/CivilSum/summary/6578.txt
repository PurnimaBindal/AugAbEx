As per the Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 311 and Andhra Pradesh State Electricity Board Service Regulations' Regulation 16(a) and (b), a petitioner was directed to present himself before the Medical Board to determine his correct age and date of birth.
The Medical Board provided a certificate indicating that the petitioner was 55 years old on December 2, 1985, and his date of birth should be considered July 1, 1930.
Although the respondents failed to enter this date in the Service Register, it remains valid and binding as per the provision.
As per Regulation 16, the petitioner retired when he turned 58 unless the respondents took some positive action to retain him in service.
Since this did not occur, the petitioner retired on June 30, 1988, and was no longer an employee of the Board.
As a result, no disciplinary proceedings could have been initiated against him after he left employment.
