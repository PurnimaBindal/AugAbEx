Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Eligibility - Appointment on the post of Professor in Govt. Unani Colleges in UP - One of the necessary qualifications for the position included "ten years teaching experience of the subject (eight years only in case of post-graduate) including three years on the post of reader in a recognised institution."
- The respondent had worked as a demonstrator for eight years and as a lecturer and reader for three years, but did not have a post-graduate qualification required for considering this experience.
Hence, the respondent cannot be considered for appointment.
[Paras 5 and 6]
