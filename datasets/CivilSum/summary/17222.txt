Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Contractual appointment for a fixed term - Challenge to termination of employment - The petitioner was hired by BBMB as an Air Conditioning Mechanic for the summer months until September 30, 1998 - The petitioner's services were not extended beyond that period - Additionally, it was reported that the petitioner did not appear interested in his job responsibilities nor possess suitable knowledge - The petitioner acknowledged that his appointment would conclude on September 30, 1998 - It would now be unjust to challenge the condition stated in the appointment, which the petitioner had previously consented to.
