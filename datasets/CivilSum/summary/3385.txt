Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - Purpose - Compassionate appointment is intended to assist the family during a sudden financial crisis - The family cannot claim appointment as a matter of right solely due to the death of the employee - It is necessary for the relevant Government/Authorities to review the financial status of the deceased's family and set a reasonable time frame for providing employment - Employment can only be granted if the authority determines that it is necessary to alleviate the crisis arising from the employee's death.
