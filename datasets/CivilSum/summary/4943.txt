Rajasthan Panchayat Samities and Zila Parishads Services Rules, 1959, Rule 24 - Seniority - Ad hoc service - Only the substantive service is calculable for seniority - The service ordered to be counted must be in accordance with rules - In the absence of any rule, ad hoc service cannot be counted.
