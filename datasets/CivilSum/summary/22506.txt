The Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Section 7 grants private teachers parity of pay with government teachers, but the other conditions of service have not been extended to the former.
The incentive of advance increments for improving educational qualifications was applicable only to government teachers and cannot be automatically extended to private teachers who were not in service at the time the instructions were issued.
The High Court judgment was set aside.
