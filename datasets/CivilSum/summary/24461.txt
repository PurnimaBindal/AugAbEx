Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25F - All India Industrial Tribunal (Bank Disputes) Award, Paragraphs 521(5)(e) and 521(10)(c) and 522(1) - Retrenchment - The termination of services that result from disciplinary proceedings is not considered a retrenchment under Section 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act.
It is important to recognize the discharge under paragraphs 521(5)(e) and 521(10)(c) as being related to disciplinary proceedings as this allows the employee the right to challenge the legality and propriety of the disciplinary action taken against them.
This could include refuting the claim of misconduct or arguing that the punishment was not warranted.
