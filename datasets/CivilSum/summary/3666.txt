According to Section 3(1) of the Workmen's Compensation Act (8 of 1923), an accident that occurs during the course of employment is covered.
In this instance, a bus conductor suffered chest pain on the job and experienced a heart attack, which can be fatal if prompt medical attention is not provided.
The workman in question was admitted to a moffisil hospital and, according to the employer, did not receive the best medical treatment available for his condition.
This ultimately led to the workman's death, which was linked to his employment as a conductor.
