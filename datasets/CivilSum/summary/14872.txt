Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion - Departmental Enquiry - Pendency of - Indefinitely - If an enquiry is kept pending without any progress from the respondents, the consideration for promotion cannot be withheld.
In this case, the respondents took more than one and a half years to decide on whether to appoint an enquiry officer, and no final decision was taken despite the enquiry report being submitted in December 1997.
The petitioner's right to consideration for promotion under Article 16 of the Constitution of India was denied.
As a result, directions were issued to consider the petitioner's case for promotion.
