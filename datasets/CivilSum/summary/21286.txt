Constitution of India, Articles 166(2) and 163(1) - order of Governor - Administrative functions can be allocated by Governor under Rules of Business - Orders issued with Governor's name under Articles 166(2) or 163(1) are immune from challenge on the basis of non-issuance by the Governor himself - Governor's sanction for prosecution is an executive act and is not subject to the Governor's personal discretion as per the Constitution - Council of Ministers took decision in accordance with the Rule of Business - Sanction is valid - Even in cases falling under Article 163(1) of the Constitution where the Governor needs to use personal discretion, the Governor acts on the advice of the Council of Ministers.
