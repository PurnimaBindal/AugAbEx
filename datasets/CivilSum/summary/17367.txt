Panjab University Calender, Vol. II, 1995, Regulation 7 (amended by the Syndicate of the University on 12.12.1987) - Examination - Eligibility - For the course of B.Sc.
Nursing (Post Basic), passing at least one semester before the November/December first-semester examination is required - As the petitioner had passed the Higher Secondary or Pre-University Examination before 1986-87, the time gap regarding the qualifying examination and the entrance test did not apply in his case, as the entrance test he wished to take was in 1997 - If Regulation 7 is ambiguous, the student cannot be punished; instead, they are entitled to the benefit of the doubt - University directed to allow the petitioner to take the test for the next B.Sc.
(Post Basic) course.
