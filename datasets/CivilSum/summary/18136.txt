As per Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, appointment and reservation are subject to the use of genuine documents.
In this case, the petitioner secured appointment on the basis of a forged certificate of Scheduled Tribe, which allowed him to benefit from reservation and age relaxation.
The petitioner held the post for over 25 years by dragging the matter in technicalities, despite not being entitled to it.
Therefore, the petitioner is ineligible for any relief.
