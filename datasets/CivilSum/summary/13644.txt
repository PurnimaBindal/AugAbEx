Based on Constitution of India, Article 16, waiver of qualificators was allowed for the appointment of Stenography Instructors in Punjabi, enabling the petitioners to accept their appointments without reservation, but preventing them from claiming a revised pay scale from their initial appointment.
Respondents' objection that the petitioners are estopped from claiming the revised scale is valid.
[Para 7]

However, based on the same Constitution of India, Article 16, the petitioners are entitled to a grant of the revised pay scale from the date they acquired the required experience of 5 years, in accordance with the decision of the government.
Therefore, the petitioners will be placed in the scale of Rs. 225-500 from the date they completed 5 years of service, whether ad hoc or not, in the department.
