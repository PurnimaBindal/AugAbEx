Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Management Rules, Rules 25 and 27 - Writ Court - Jurisdiction - Conflict between the Secretary and Joint Secretary of a school's Managing Committee regarding custody of school records - Rule 25 defines the specific duties of each office bearer, with the Secretary tasked with maintaining important documents such as investments and legal records while the Joint Secretary's duties do not extend to this area - The Joint Secretary cannot refuse to hand over school records - The Secretary is pursuing legal action for enforcement of their rights under the law - The court's jurisdiction to hear the case is not blocked.
