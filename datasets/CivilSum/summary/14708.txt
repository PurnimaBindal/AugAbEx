In accordance with the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, a termination of employment based on a reduction in staff cannot be deemed arbitrary or a violation of appointment letter conditions, provided that the termination is executed in accordance with the terms of appointment.
This action may be taken as a measure of economy without discrimination or unfairness towards the petitioner.
In addition, if certain individuals continue to work for separate subsidiaries of the Corporation, which operate with independent cadres, it cannot be said that they are junior to the petitioner, or that Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution have been breached in dismissing the petitioner.
