According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Rules 46 and 47 of the Delhi Education Rule, if a society abandons an aided school or its teachers, they cannot close the school without receiving approval under Rule 46.
In a case where a society opens an unaided school after securing a regular plot allotment, it cannot be considered as a closure of the previous school.
Rather, the character of the school is being changed and the employees must be absorbed in the new institution while their status is preserved and protected by management, as per Paras 19 to 24.
