Air Force Regulations, Paras 52, 54 and 1725 - Air Force Order dated 11.2.1995, Para 29 (a & c) - Occupation of station married quarter - Imposition of market rate damage rent - Petitioner was allotted quarter on 161999 but received compensation in lieu of quarter since 15.5.1996 - On posting, petitioner was asked to vacate quarter - Employee entitled to compensation or stay in quarter for 5 years as per para 29 (a & c).
The period starts from the date the Airman is brought within the married establishment and given compensation in lieu of quarter.
Hence, the respondents can charge damage rent from the expiry of 5 years i.e. 1452001.
