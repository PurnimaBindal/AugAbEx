Constitution of India, Article 226 - Voluntary Retirement Scheme (VRS) - The petitioner's name appeared on a list of surplus employees of the Federation and was given the option to opt for the VRS or a Golden Hand Shake Scheme to avoid retrenchment/premature retirement - The petitioner had the choice to take or decline the option - Court held that the petitioner was not being forced to seek VRS and dismissed the petition.
