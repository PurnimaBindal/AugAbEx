Constitution of India, Article 226 - Equal pay for equal work - Bill distributors of water and electricity consumption - Introduction of combined water and electricity bills in April 1991 - Employees in the Electricity Operation Circle are granted the same scales as PSEB employees, whereas employees of the Public Health Circle are paid on the Punjab pattern - Chandigarh administration now filling up Bill Distributor posts according to the Punjab pattern - No distinction between the two categories of Bill Distributors - It is held that the respondents' decision to reject parity in pay scales is not sustainable, especially since both categories of Bill Distributors work under the Department of Engineering, U.T Chandigarh.
