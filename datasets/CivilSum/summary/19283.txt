Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay scale - Equalisation of pay scales - Non-teaching staff members in aided colleges are now entitled to revised pay scales equivalent to those in government colleges, effective from 1st September 1978 according to the decision of the Punjab Government.
In spite of this, those working in government colleges received arrears from the said date but those employed in aided colleges were denied such compensation without adequate justification or reasonable rationale for their discrimination.
The government cannot renounce its own resolution of discriminating them only due to heavy financial implications.
