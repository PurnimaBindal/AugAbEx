Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Regularisation - Rejected due to a break in service of over one month at a time during the year - However, it was held that if the break in service is due to medical illness beyond the employee's control, the employee cannot be unfairly treated - Guidelines were issued to regularize the petitioner's service according to policy and provide all constitutional benefits within three months - 2002(4) SCT 992 was cited as a reference.
