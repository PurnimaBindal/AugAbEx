Constitution of India, Article 226 - AP (Telangana Area) Public Societies Registration Act (1 of 1350) Fasli, Section 11 - Dissolution of Public Society - A writ against the dissolution of a Technology Society formed with the government as member can only be raised by a member of society and not by any employee of society.
A petition for compelling the society to continue operation for the sake of employees is not maintainable.
