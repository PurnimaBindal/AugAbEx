Rule 82 of the Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions (Recognition, Grant-in-Aid and Service Conditions) Rules, 1993 states that aided educational institutions are entitled to proportionate grant-in-aid based on the amount paid to their employees as gratuity.
The court held that private educational institutions are performing state functions and denying grant-in-aid on obligatory expenses is not in line with the 1993 Rules.
As a result, the state government is liable to contribute towards gratuity.
