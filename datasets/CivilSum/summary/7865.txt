Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56 - M.P. Civil Services (Pension) Rule, 1976, Rule 42 - Compulsory retirement - The satisfaction of the authority must meet the standard of a prudent and fair individual - It should not be based on perversity - Even though an order is based on subjective satisfaction, it is still subject to judicial scrutiny - Compulsory retirement orders should not be passed as a shortcut to avoid departmental inquiry - The order must be based on the officer's complete service record, and should not be based on no material or prima facie mala fide.
