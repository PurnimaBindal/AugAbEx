Constitution of India, Articles 12, 226, 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33C(2) - Payment of wages - Promotion - Back dated - Granted - Employee not ordered to be promoted with the benefit of back wages - Labour Court directed payment of difference of wages to employee - Court held that when a willing employee is unjustly kept away from work, they cannot be deprived of the benefit of higher wages when the injustice is removed with retrospective effect - Therefore, if the employee is deemed to be promoted from a back date, they are entitled to the higher wages from the date they should have been promoted - Petition dismissed accordingly.
