Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol. II, Rule 5.3 - Pension - Family Pension - No Bar for Permanent Employees Absorbed in Non-Government Companies - Rule 5.3 (2) Excluded - According to Rule 5.3 of Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, permanent employees of the Government will not lose their right to receive monthly pensions if they join co-operative societies or private companies.
Moreover, their family members can claim family pensions too.
However, if absorbed in a corporation or company wholly or substantially owned or controlled by Government, or in or under a body controlled or financed by Government, or Municipality, Panchayat Samiti or Zila Parishad, the bar in sub-rule (2) of Rule 5.3 applies.
