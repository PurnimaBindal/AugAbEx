Constitution of India, Article 311 - Resignation - Acceptance of resignation before effective date - Withdrawal of resignation before effective date - Resignation cannot be accepted before effective date and must be made effective with petitioner being paid salary for the remaining period of notice.
However, petitioner retains the right to withdraw resignation before the effective date.
Relevant cases such as 1983(3) SLR 21 and AIR 1987 SC 2354 are cited.
