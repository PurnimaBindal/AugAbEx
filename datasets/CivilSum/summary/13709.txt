Indian Penal Code, Sections 376 and 363 - Attempt to rape - Rape on a minor girl of 12/13 years - The Chemical Examiner discovered human semen in the vaginal swab and smear but the hymen was not ruptured - It was concluded that it was a rape rather than an attempt to rape - Since there was slight degree of penetration, physical force was not used - Even a slight degree of penetration is sufficient to constitute the offence according to Section 376 IPC.
