The issue of question paper leaks during various exams has caused a loss of confidence in students and parents on the system, leading to a Public Interest Litigation.
The Union of India filed a report on the security measures taken to secure question papers from the time they are set till the results are declared.
The court held that adequate and satisfactory steps have been taken to ensure the sanctity of the admission process and expressed the hope that these measures continue to be implemented strictly according to the rules.
