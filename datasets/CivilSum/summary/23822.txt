The Public Service Commission Rules, 1979, Rules 4, 5, 6 and 8 - Adverse entry - Integrity - The appellant was directed by the HC to reconsider the case of the respondent for regularisation, after considering that the adverse entry was expunged and integrity was certified.
However, the HC did not take into account the fact that the respondent had been debarred by the Commission for a period of five years due to interpolation in his original High School certificate.
Therefore, it was held that the HC could not give any direction for the reconsideration of the respondent.
