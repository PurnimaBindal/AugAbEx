Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Absorption - Readjustment in parent department - Reversion - Protection of pay - If a Government employee opts to be absorbed in Municipal service and requests readjustment in the parent department upon abolition of municipal service, he cannot demand the same post and pay scale he held in the municipal service.
However, his readjustment according to his previous seniority cannot be challenged and he is not entitled to any post or pay higher than his seniors in the parent department.
