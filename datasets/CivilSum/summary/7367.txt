Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Sections 28, 29 - Bar to jurisdiction of Civil Court - Interim orders passed by Civil Court staying termination of services - Dismissal of civil suit - Filing an application for the same relief in the Tribunal is not allowed once the civil suit is dismissed - The applicant's status is evaluated with reference to the date of application and not the fact that they continued employment on the basis of interim orders.
- Simply continuing to work on the basis of interim orders does not entitle the employee to benefits.
