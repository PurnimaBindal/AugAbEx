Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 13(i) - Conviction on Criminal Charge - Dismissal - Validity of - Petitioner, a teacher, was dismissed after being convicted under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act and awarded sentence till rising of Court along with fine, despite having started a medical practice as a hobby with permission of Block Education Officer- Held, conviction on a criminal charge does not automatically entail the penalty of removal - The competent authority is duty-bound to consider whether the conduct of the employee which led to his conviction was such as to warrant the imposition of penalty before imposing any penalty - Therefore, an order passed without considering the nature of offense, past record and length of service is ultra vires to the provision of Rule 13(1) and Article 311 of Constitution - Petitioner's appeal was allowed accordingly.
