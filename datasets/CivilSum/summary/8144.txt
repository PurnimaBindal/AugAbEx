Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2, Item 6 - Temporary workers employed for over two decades - Company failed to provide evidence regarding their work during said period - Denied benefits given to permanent workers - Temporary workers performed same work as permanent workers - Tribunal awarded regularisation and backwages - But, due to the company being declared as sick, relief must be structured accordingly.
