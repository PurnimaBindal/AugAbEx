The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(s), 25B&F and the Working Journalists and Newspaper Employees (Conditions of Service and Miscellaneous) Act, 1955 are applicable in this case discussing the termination of a UNI Stringer who worked as a part-time journalist.
The respondent contends that he was not a workman or a working journalist, and had not completed 240 days of service, but had retired in February 1991 upon reaching the age of superannuation.
The relief of reinstatement has become infructuous since the petitioner has retired on superannuation.
However, it has been rightly held that even part time working journalists fall within the definition of workman and, since the respondent joined the petitioner in 1972 and worked continuously, the fresh appointment letter given in 1978 does not help the petitioner.
Withholding full backwages for the respondent's work as a part-time journalist would not be justifiable.
