The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, specifically Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F, provides for the topic of Retrenchment, including Compensation in lieu of reinstatement.
In this case, the catering counters of the establishment were closed and handed over to the Transport Department, with staff engaged on a daily wage basis without any criteria for eligibility or selection.
This matter was brought before the Labour Court, where no specific plea was taken, resulting in an award of reinstatement with full back wages upheld by the High Court.
However, considering the facts of the case, it was determined that compensation in lieu of reinstatement would be the appropriate relief.
Each workman was therefore directed to be paid Rs. 70,000/-.
