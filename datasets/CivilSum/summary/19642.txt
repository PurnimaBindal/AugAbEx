Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Life Insurance Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1960, LIC of India (Class III and Class IV) Recruitment Instructions, 1979 - Para 22 - Compassionate appointment - The fact that other sons of a deceased employee are employed is not a valid reason to deny employment on compassionate grounds.
The objective of these instructions is to provide employment to one son of a deceased employee in the Corporation on a suitable post based on his qualifications.
