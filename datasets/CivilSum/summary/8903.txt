The petitioners' advocates are M Ramkrishna Rao, Mr. MR Harsha, and Mr. K Srinivasa Murthy.
The Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Order (Amendment) Act, 1976, outlines that only Parliament can amend the Schedule to add or remove any caste or tribe.
Moreover, the President of India is the only one who can notify a Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe, making it irrelevant if the State Government notifies a caste or tribe as Scheduled.
The petitioner's entitlement to be included in the list of Scheduled Tribes 'Sugali' while belonging to 'Wanjari' caste is not within the Court's domain to hold an inquiry.
The appointment of the petitioner was conditional and subject to verification under the Evidence Act, 1872, Section 115, making it possible to terminate her appointment if she is not detected as belonging to the Scheduled Tribe.
Even equitable estoppel cannot prevent the authorities from terminating her employment, as there is no justification to let the petitioner continue to hold the post only because she has been able to do so for 13 years due to interim orders obtained from the Court.
