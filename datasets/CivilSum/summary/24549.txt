Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 - Interim stay - When interim orders are granted pending disposal of writ petitions or suits, they come to an end with the disposal of the substantive proceedings.
It is the responsibility of the court to ensure that the parties are returned to the position they would have been in if no interim order had been granted.
Ultimately, only the final order will prevail.
However, it should be noted that mere reinstatement in compliance with interim direction will not render the proceedings redundant.
[Paras 5 and 9]
