"Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Petitioner challenging action of respondent for not appointing him to the post of H.C.S.
(Executive Branch) - Respondent claims writ petition is belated and appointments were made according to the directions of Supreme Court - Court held that benefits given to similarly situated persons below petitioner in merit should have been given to all, regardless of whether they were within advertised posts or not - Hence, the right of petitioner cannot be defeated and respondents must consider his case for appointment to the post of H.C.S.
(Executive Branch) according to his seniority in merit list declared on 19.6.92.
[Paras 12, 14 and 18]"
