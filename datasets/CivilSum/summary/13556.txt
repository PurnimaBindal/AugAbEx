The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226, Government of India, Act, 1935, Section 241(2)(6), and Fundamental Rules, Rules 15(a) and 6, deal with the transfer of Education Service employees between Directorates.
The two Directorates in Education Department are headed by two Directors and have a common gradation list of employees.
However, according to FR-15, the power to transfer a Govt. servant is vested in the State Govt, not the Directors.
There has been no express delegation of power to Respondent No. 2 to make inter-directorate transfers in accordance with FR-6.
Therefore, the impugned inter-directorate transfer order passed by Respondent No. 2 without jurisdiction is deemed quashed.
