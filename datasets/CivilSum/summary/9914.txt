Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Rajasthan Land Revenue (Land Records) Rules, 1957, Paragraph 17 - Rajasthan Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 13 - Suspension - Writ jurisdiction - Rules allow for an employee to be suspended if criminal proceedings are either under investigation or under trial or during pendency of disciplinary proceedings - The court cannot make any distinctions between the two rules, whether criminal proceedings are minor or major - Writ petition can only be entertained against suspension in rare cases with valid grounds.
