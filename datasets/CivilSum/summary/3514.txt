Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Paras 173 and 173-A, Appendix II - Entitlement Rules, Rule 7(b) - Regulations for Medical Service of Armed Forces, 1983 - Disability pension - If the Medical Board did not find the "Neurosis" disease to be too complex to determine at initial tests or not attributable to military service, then it is assumed that the personnel contracted the disease while in service and is therefore deemed to be attributable to service.
