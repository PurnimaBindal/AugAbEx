Staff Service Rules of Haryana State Federation of Consumers Co-operative Wholesale Store Limited, Rule 13 - Common Cadre - The amendment in Rule 13 excluded the posts of Peons, Helpers and Store Keepers from the common cadre and allocated them to the stores where they were working, resulting in automatic termination of the petitioner's relationship with the Government.
The validity of the amended Rules was not contested and therefore the petitioner cannot assert to be a common cadre employee.
