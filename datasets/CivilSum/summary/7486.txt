Constitution of India under Article 226 states that a petitioner who is declared surplus and belongs to the reserve category can be transferred, but the transfer should consider the convenience of the petitioner and they should be posted near their home town.
This direction is based on a previous Supreme Court decision in the case of Laxmi Narain Mehar v. Union of India.
With this in mind, the respondents have been directed to retain the petitioner or adjust her in any adjoining district.
