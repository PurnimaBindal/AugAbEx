According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India relating to promotion, a sealed cover promotion can only be adopted if departmental proceedings are initiated, pending, or final orders are awaited to be passed by the punishing authority.
The promotion committee must bring the name of a person on the select list if, on the date of consideration for promotion, the person is found suitable and is neither under suspension nor is any departmental action initiated against him.
[Paras 3 and 4]
