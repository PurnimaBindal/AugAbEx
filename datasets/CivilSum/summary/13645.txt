Constitution of India, Article 14 - Govt. Employees Conduct Rules, 1966, Rule 4 - Haryana Cooperative Societies Rules, 1989 - Appointment/Selection/Qualifications/Experience - The petitioner was appointed as Manager, Electronics Data Processing Cell in the Haryana State Cooperative Apex Bank Limited, despite not having the requisite experience or qualifications.
This appointment is illegal and against the rules.
The appointing authority has the power to rectify such mistakes.
[Paras 16 to 23 and 26]

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Experience/Qualifications - Previous experience is irrelevant if it was gained prior to acquiring the requisite qualifications.
[Paras 17 and 18]

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Evidence Act, Section 115 - Estoppel - The appointing authority can review and correct a mistake at any time.
The appointment being illegal, it must be reviewed following the principles of natural justice.
There is no estoppel against the law, and the action is not barred by the principle of equitable estoppel.
[Paras 24, 25 and 26]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment/Eligibility - Appointing a person against a post for which he is not entitled is arbitrary.
There is nothing arbitrary and improper if a wrong is undone.
[Para 27]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Malafides/bias - Specific averments must be made in the petition to prove allegations of bias.
Otherwise, they will be ignored.
[Para 10]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Malafide/bias - Adjournment or preponement of a board meeting due to some difficulty expressed by the Managing Director is not proof of arbitrariness or bias, especially when there are other superior and senior members of the Board of Directors and the Managing Director is not the final authority.
[Para 14]
