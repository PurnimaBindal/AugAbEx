Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Recording of reasons - Disciplinary authority must provide reasons for disagreement when holding charges as proved - Failure to give reasons in show cause notice and informing delinquent that a lack of response would be taken as acceptance - Punishment of stoppage of two grade increments subsequently awarded - Provision of reasons necessary to enable officer to defend against punishment - Order of punishment set aside.
