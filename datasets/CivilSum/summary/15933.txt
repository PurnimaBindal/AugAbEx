In accordance with the Bombay Doc Workers (Regulation of Employment) Scheme, 1956, Clause 44(5), if an employee has already been exonerated during a disciplinary enquiry, the Disciplinary Authority cannot hold a fresh enquiry in regards to the same incident.
The Disciplinary Authority must either accept the findings or, if they disagree, provide the delinquent with an opportunity to show cause.
This ruling was based on AIR 1975 SC 2277.
