I Part I, Rule 3.26(d) and Vol. II Rule 5.32(c) (as applicable to Haryana) - Judicial Review - Scope of - Administrative actions - Pre-mature compulsory retirement - Compulsory retirement is not a punishment but simply a request to retire a government employee in the public interest.

[Paras 7 and 8]  B. Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I Part I, Rule 3.26(d) and Vol. II Rule 5.32(c) (as applicable to Haryana) - Pre-mature compulsory retirement - Service record - Uncommunicated remarks - The whole service record should be considered when deciding on compulsory retirement, including any uncommunicated negative remarks.
If a government official has been promoted despite adverse entries, such remarks lose their significance, particularly if promotions are earned on merit rather than seniority.
Additionally, an average annual confidential report that contains no negative commentary on an employee's integrity or effectiveness is not enough to justify compulsory retirement.
If the government's instructions required an average or above-average review to be communicated, unannounced assessments could not be used to justify compulsory retirement of an employee.
