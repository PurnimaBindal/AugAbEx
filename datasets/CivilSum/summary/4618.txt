Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3(1) - Premature retirement - Retirees cannot be judged on basis of adverse remarks recorded 29, 28 and 16 years prior to year of retirement - It is legally untenable to make a decision on a single advisory entry regardless of the 5 good and 4 satisfactory reports in the Annual Confidential Reports of last 10 years - The petitioner's promotion to higher post and benefit of proficiency step up cannot be denied despite adverse annual confidential reports - The theory of wash out/dilution of adverse entries of those years must be applied - The impugned order of retirement is flawed and requires interference by Court under Art. 226 of Constitution of India.
