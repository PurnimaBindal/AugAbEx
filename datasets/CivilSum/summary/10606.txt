According to Punjab Co-operative Societies Act (XXXV of 1961), Section 26 (1-B), the committee will be considered to have taken office on the day of election and its three-year tenure will begin from that same date.
Delay in the election of office-bearers or co-option of members will not invalidate this assumption.
This has been made clear in the case of 1980 PLJ 44.
