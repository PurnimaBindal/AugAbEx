Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Punjab Superior Judicial Service Rules, 1963, Rules 2(2), 8 and 12- Seniority quota - Seniority in the Cadre - Inter se Seniority between promotees and direct recruits - Amendment in Rules - Appointment on temporary posts - The appellants were promoted to temporary posts before the amendment, which did not form a post in the Cadre, and were not appointed to any post in their cadre.
Therefore, they cannot claim any benefit under the amended rules.
Direct recruits were appointed to permanent posts in their cadre, and seniority was fixed according to when the posts were available in their respective quotas after confirmation in the cadre.
The seniority fixed by the High Court is correct according to the rules.
