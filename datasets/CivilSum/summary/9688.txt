Constitution of India, Article 226 - Tender - Award of contract - Judicial Review - Contract of handling transport of stock of FCI - Despite failure to adhere to tender condition, the decision of FCI to not disqualify and award contract to respondent No. 2 is not arbitrary or capricious - No interference necessary in judicial review.
[Para ] B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Award of contract by State agency - Power of judicial review of administrative decision - Courts must exercise discretion with caution and only in furtherance of public interest - Administrative decisions must be reasonable, free from arbitrariness, bias and mala fides - Fair play necessary in administrative sphere - State must have freedom of contract.
