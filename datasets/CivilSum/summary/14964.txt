According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 3.17, an educator who applied for a Headmistress position in the Education Department of Haryana must obtain permission from the competent authority in order to claim pensionary benefits from the State of Punjab.
Despite not receiving permission from the District Education Officer, the petitioner was selected for the role and subsequently relieved to take up the position.
As no action was taken by the Punjab Govt. regarding her absence from duty, the petitioner should not be denied the right of pension on the basis of technicalities.
Therefore, the Punjab Govt. is liable to contribute its share towards pension.
