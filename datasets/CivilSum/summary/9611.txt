Constitution of India, Article 226 - Minimum Wages Act, 1948, Section 5(1) (b) - Notification to fix Minimum Wages - After hearing objections, the Government fixed the minimum wages and invited suggestions.
Petitioner/Employer failed to submit any suggestion/objections against the proposal published in the notification.
High Court cannot interfere in its writ jurisdiction with the minimum wages fixed by the Government that used the Consumers Price Index Number, taking the average of all the centres compiled by the Economic Adviser to the Government - AIR 1985 SC 1391 followed.
