As per AP State & Subordinate Service Rules, Rule 34(b) (ii), seniority-cum-fitness is the criteria for promotion.
Due to penalties imposed on him, the appellant was not eligible for promotion as he was under disability undergoing punishment and did not regain fitness for consideration.
Fitness is one of the considerations for promotion based on merit and ability under Special Rules and since he was undergoing punishment during the relevant period, he cannot be considered for promotion.
Despite his juniors stealing a march on him as Senior Assistants, he is entitled to be considered for promotion according to Rules after March 1, 1994.
