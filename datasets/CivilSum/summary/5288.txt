Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 14 - Jurisdiction - A petition filed by an employee of the Board was dismissed ex-parte on the grounds that the matter was within the domain of the CAT.
However, the central government had not issued any notification regarding the amenability of local or other authorities owned or controlled by it to the jurisdiction of CAT.
As a result, the order cannot be sustained as it was passed ex-parte, which is a violation of the principles of natural justice.
It has been held that the appellant, as an employee of the Board, is subject to the jurisdiction of the State Administrative Tribunal or High Court.
[Paras 9, 18, 19 and 22]
