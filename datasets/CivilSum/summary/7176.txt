Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Promotions - Sealed cover promotion - Sealed cover used on account of pending investigation in an FIR case - When a report of investigation points out that no offence has been made out, the envelope must be opened immediately, and recommendations must be implemented retrospectively - The employee cannot be penalized for the delay on the part of investigation and the authority.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Promotions - Departmental proceedings - Consideration for promotion - Sealed cover promotion - Sealed cover used on account of pending investigation in an FIR case - When a report of investigation points out that no offence has been made out, the envelope must be opened immediately, and recommendations must be implemented retrospectively - The employee's consideration for further promotion cannot be delayed because of late implementation of recommendations - The employee's right to consideration for the period when there was no case pending cannot be denied - Even if some penalty is imposed, the effect of the penalty has to be considered by the DPC.
