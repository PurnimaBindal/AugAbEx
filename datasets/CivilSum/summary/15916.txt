Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Section 11-A, Schedule 2, Item 3 - An employee who allegedly overstayed his leave by 20 days was granted leave, but his subsequent leave application was rejected.
The employee was not aware of the rejection and cannot be said to have intentionally violated any orders.
As a result, the punishment of removal from service was considered too severe, and the employee was to be reinstated as per directions.
