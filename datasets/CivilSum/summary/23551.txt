Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation of services - Medical Officer - According to the prescribed procedure, the regularisation process of a Medical Officer requires a regular appointment - The selection of candidates suitable for the position must be made by the appointed Selection Committee - A petitioner who has been working since 1976 was found unsuitable for regular appointment by the Selection Committee in 1981 - High Court's direction for regularisation deemed improper and subsequently set aside - However, the petitioner could possibly be eligible for equal pay for equal work.
