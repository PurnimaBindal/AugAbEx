The appellants, who were initially appointed on a temporary basis on July 31, 1976, were later regularized from October 1, 1977.
The appellants are claiming the benefit of regularisation from the initial date of their appointment in 1976.
However, they made their representations after 19 years had elapsed and approached the High Court after 20 years had elapsed.
The learned single judge dismissed the writ petition on the grounds of inordinate delay and laches, which cannot be faulted.
