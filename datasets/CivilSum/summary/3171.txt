Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Police Rules, Rule 13 - Punjab Recruitment of Sportsmen Rules, 1988, Rules 2 and 3 - Recruitment - The Rules mandate that all eligible persons be allowed to apply for and compete for advertised posts - This also applies to recruitment for reserved posts - Recruitment of candidates without advertising the posts is a violation of the principles of equality of opportunity enshrined in Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - No direction can be given to appoint a candidate who was recruited in contravention to these constitutional provisions.
