Industrial Disputes At, 1947, Sections 25-F and 25-G - Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules, 1957, Rule 77 - Termination - The petitioner was appointed as a casual helper for 397 days due to an interim order of the High Court.
According to Rule 77 of the Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules, 1957, the petitioner cannot claim benefits equivalent to those of regularly appointed casual workers.
Furthermore, the termination of the petitioner's service cannot be regarded as retrenchment under Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes At, 1947, as it was carried out according to the High Court's orders.
