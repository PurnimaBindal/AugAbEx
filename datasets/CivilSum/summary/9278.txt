The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section- 11-A states that in cases of serious allegations of fraud against a petitioner, the labour court has discretion to award compensation in lieu of reinstatement.
Though the court was not inclined to interfere, it revised the compensation from Rs. 2,000/- to Rs. 10,000/- based on the salary drawn and period of services rendered by the workman.
