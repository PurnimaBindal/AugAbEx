The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226, deal with project employees regarding their regularisation of services, equal pay for equal work, contractual wages, and fixed terms and conditions.
According to the terms and conditions of their engagement, fixed-wage employees cannot receive higher wages under the principle of equal pay for equal work nor can they be regularised into service, as doing so will disrupt the scheme.
As daily wagers, they are not protected by any constitutional provisions or service rules.
