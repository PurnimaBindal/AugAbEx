Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2(1) - Misconduct - Dismissal - The petitioner was accused of disobeying orders of the SP, raising slogans in favour of an unrecognised Police Sangathan, and instigating members of the police force to join the Sangathan - Allegations relate to the petitioner's superiors and not the public - These acts are considered grave misconduct falling under clauses (iv) and (vii) of Explanation to Sub-Rule (1) of Rule 162 of Police Rules - No criminal offence committed in connection with official relations with the public - There is no flaw in the dismissal orders being challenged - The petition has been dismissed.
