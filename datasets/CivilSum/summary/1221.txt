Constitution of India, Articles 215 and 226 - Finality of orders passed in writ jurisdiction - Industrial Dispute - Back wages - A Labour Court awarded 30% back wages and reinstatement of a worker, which was modified to full back wages.
The orders became final after the management's writ petition was dismissed.
The authority responsible could not overrule the High Court's judgment by adjudicating over the back wages claim.
Such actions would be contemptuous.
The authority was required to implement the order by providing full back wages with interest.
Rs. 15,000/- costs were imposed on the authority and to be recovered from his salary.
[Paras 14 and 15]
