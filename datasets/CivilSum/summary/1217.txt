Constitution of India, Articles 14,16 and 226 - FCI (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 59(4) Reinstatement - Waiver of right - Constructive res judicata - Back wages - The respondent, who was dismissed from service, had his order of dismissal set aside.
After disciplinary proceedings, he was removed from service again.
This order was also set aside, and the respondent was allowed to complete his service tenure and receive service/retiral benefits.
Although he did not claim any back wages, his right to do so is barred by constructive res judicata, and he is debarred from making such a claim in the fourth writ petition.
