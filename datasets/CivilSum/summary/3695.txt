The final order between parties operates as res judicata in all subsequent proceedings.
[Para 16]
De novo inquiry means conducting the inquiry from the initial stage and observing principles of natural justice.
Failure to follow the entire procedure will violate principles of natural justice and vitiate the proceedings.
[Paras 17, 18, 20, and 21]
Once the procedure for imposing minor penalty is followed, the delinquent cannot complain of any prejudice.
Even violation of procedural provisions does not vitiate the inquiry or order if no prejudice is caused.
