Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference - A reference regarding employees of ONGC who had completed 240 days or more must not limit the scope of reference to only those names included in the schedule by the Union, as excluding similarly situated persons is illegal.
All entitled to the same relief must be included.
[Para 8]  B Constitution of India, Article 16 - ONGC (Recruitment and Promotion) Regulations, 1980 - Qualifications - An employee who has been working for a number of years must be considered at par with other regular employees for the purpose of all other benefits, even though regularisation as a permanent workman may not be possible due to unavailability of a permanent post.
This is not a case of giving regular appointment, but of converting to a regular employee.
