Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(1) - Proceedings under - Notice - The employer acted unlawfully by not obtaining permission from the State Govt. for `lay off' or `closure' - A Union applied under Section 33-C to recover wages owed to certain workers - An order was issued for recovery of a certain amount as arrears of land revenue without a hearing being granted - The absence of notice did not cause any prejudice to the petitioner, so the order cannot be invalidated on a mere technicality when the workers were suffering.
