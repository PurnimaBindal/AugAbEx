According to Punjab Electricity Board Service Rules, Rule 8.22(aa) on Pensionary benefits and Leave encashment, an employee's leave encashment cannot be withheld solely based on their conviction in a criminal case.
However, if there is a possibility of recovering some amount from the employee due to pending departmental or criminal proceedings, payment towards leave encashment may be stopped.
Furthermore, the rule does not specify that a retired employee's leave encashment can be recovered solely based on their conviction in a criminal case.
