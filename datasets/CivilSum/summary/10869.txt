Constitution of India, Article 226 - Seniority - Inter-se seniority - The determination of inter se seniority must be based on the rules that were in place at the relevant time.
It is not permissible to review inter se seniority after a long period of time has elapsed, particularly when candidates have already been promoted to multiple positions, due to subsequent changes in rules or the creation of new rights and obligations.
