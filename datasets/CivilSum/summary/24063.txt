Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 12 - Settlement - Winding up - Absorption of industrial workers in Government service - Executive decision - Government decision to absorb workers in Government service must take into consideration all financial and other implications and be made by due deliberations, taking into account the decision of bureaucrats.
The decision cannot be superseded merely on the advice of bureaucracy and ignoring ground realities.
The Government has to balance several interests and devise methods to suit the needs of the situation.
Any subsequent decision guided by bureaucracy that it will be financially unsuitable cannot be sustained.
[Para 19]
