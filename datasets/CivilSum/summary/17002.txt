According to Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, when an employee's services are terminated, a demand notice must be given.
In this particular case, the employee's services were terminated on 31.10.1995 and he served the demand notice on 4111995.
As the notice was served within a short span of time, the management cannot use the plea of abandonment.
