Rajasthan Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 16 - Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Constable - Disciplinary proceedings - Dismissal order challenged - Inquiry officer failed to record defence witness evidence, and no documents were supplied before serving show cause notice - Enquiry conducted in violation of procedure contained in Rule 16, in disregard of principles of natural justice, in a perfunctory and lackadaisical manner - Orders quashed with liberty to hold enquiry.
