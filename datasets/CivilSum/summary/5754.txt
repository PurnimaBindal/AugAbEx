According to Section 12 of the Industrial Disputes Act, Conciliation Officers do not have the power to reject a demand made by an employee or to decide if an industrial dispute exists or not.
They are required to send a report of the conciliation proceedings to the Government, regardless of whether or not the matter is resolved.
[Paras 5 and 6]  B Under Section 10(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, the government is obligated to make a reference of a dispute unless it is determined that no dispute exists or the matter is frivolous.
