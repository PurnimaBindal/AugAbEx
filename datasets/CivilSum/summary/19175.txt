Constitution of India - Articles 14, 16, 226 - Selection - The petitioner, along with other candidates, appeared in a type test for the post of Clerk-cum-Typist.
Out of the applicants, only 183 candidates qualified, with 30 W.P.M.
prescribed as the minimum speed in typing.
The petitioner gave 58 W.P.M.
and stood among the successful candidates who qualified for the type test, but was later asked to undergo a re-test alone on the grounds that another person had appeared on their behalf in the earlier test.
In the re-test, the petitioner showed poor performance and respondents 4 and 5 were appointed as they attained more than the minimum speed in the type test.
The court held that since the petitioners were not able to make the required grade, there could be no grievance made of their non-selection.
Additionally, there was nothing on the record to suggest that the petitioners were more meritorious than the candidates selected, and thus the petition was dismissed.
