Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Education - Admission in MBBS/BDS courses - The admission secured through deliberate misstatement of facts cannot be regularized only on the basis of compassion and sympathy, even if the student has already completed most of the course and his/her career is at stake - Father of the appellant secured domicile certificate as bona fide resident of Haryana by misrepresentation and fraud - However, the college may regularize the admission with the approval of Medical Council and in accordance with the rules.
