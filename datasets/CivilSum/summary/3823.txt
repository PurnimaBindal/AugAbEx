Constitution of India, Article 309 - UP Transport (Subordinate) Technical Service Rules 1980, Rule 15(3) - Interview - Qualifying marks - Post of Regional Inspector - The petitioner was denied candidacy for failing to secure 40% marks in both written and practical tests - The practical test must be aggregated with the written examination to determine whether the candidate has obtained 40% qualifying marks for the interview because it is an integral part of the written examination.
