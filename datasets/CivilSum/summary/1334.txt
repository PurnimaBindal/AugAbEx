Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes, Act, 1947, Section 17(1) and 17(2) - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 23 Rule 1 - The maintainability of a writ petition claiming the regularization of the service of BHEL workers based on a Supreme Court judgment is in question - An earlier petition on the same matter had been dismissed as an alternative remedy was available to the workers and a reference was pending before the Industrial Tribunal - The petitioner did not approach the Industrial Tribunal or join the pending reference or pursue the High Court's order - The award dismissing relief for workers has been made public and is final - This writ petition is not maintainable and should be dismissed.
[Paras 16 and 17]
