Punjab State Co-operative Service Class II Rules, 1958, Rules 8, 9 and 17 - Punjab Civil Services (General and Common Conditions of Service) Rules, 1994, Rules 10(3) and 7(2) - Promotion - Departmental Examination - Reversion - Natural justice - Petitioner, who was appointed as Inspector, Co-operative Societies, failed to pass departmental examination in Accounts, and as a result was reverted to the post of Inspector.
The decision was taken in accordance with the relevant rules governing the service conditions of the petitioner and, since it was not a punitive measure, the opportunity of being heard was not required.
