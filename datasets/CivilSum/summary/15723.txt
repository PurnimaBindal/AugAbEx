Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Writ petition - Delay/Laches - A writ petition was filed claiming appointment for the post of Assistant Teacher after an interview held in 1991 - The petition was disposed of by order dated 18.8.1994, directing appointment to be given based on marks obtained in the test held in June, 1991 - The order was implemented and four posts were filled - The petitioner's cause of action was not revived by the order and the post cannot be left vacant indefinitely - The writ petition's delay results in laches.
