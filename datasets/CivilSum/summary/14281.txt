Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Change in date of birth - Petitioner, a Safai Karamchari, seeks to change date of birth given at the time of appointment - Claims it was given by mistake and is actually the date of birth of his elder brother - Court rejects plea, stating it is a tactic to postpone superannuation - Birth certificate does not contain name, making it impossible to determine if he or his brother was born on that date - Writ jurisdiction cannot investigate disputed question of fact - Date of birth cannot be re-opened after 25 years - Petition dismissed.
[Paras 2 and 3]
