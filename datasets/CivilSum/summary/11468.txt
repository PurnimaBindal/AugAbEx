Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Section 11(1) - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Rules 10 & 27 - Removal from Service - Natural justice - Backward Caste and domicile certificate - Fake certificates - If an employee provides evidence in support of the genuineness of documents, he must be given an adequate opportunity to prove his case - Inquiry held in Anantnag, J. & K., which was a disturbed area and no one agreed to help the petitioner - Punishment order quashed for violating principles of natural justice - The petitioner's request for an inquiry at Unit Headquarters Delhi with legal assistance was improperly denied.
[Paras 12 to 16]  B. Domicile - The petitioner produced a Ration Card belonging to his uncle that was issued by an authority in Delhi, which contained his name - He had lived in Delhi with his uncle for an extended period in search of employment, while his father resided in the village - The Ration Card should not be disregarded solely because it was in the name of his Uncle - Domicile can be established by presenting a Ration Card, ID Card, Voters Card or Domicile Certificate, among other things.
