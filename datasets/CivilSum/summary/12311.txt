Kerala Education Rules, 1959, Chapter XIV-A Rule 1(1) Note (1) and Rule 51-A - Appointment - If a non-teaching staff member is appointed in a short term vacancy of a teacher and meets the qualifications, they become eligible under Rule 51-A for further appointment in the next available teacher vacancy.
When such an appointment is accepted, the staff member's claim is that of a teacher and not a non-teaching staff member.
