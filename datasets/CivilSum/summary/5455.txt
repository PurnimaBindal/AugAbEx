Section 14-B of the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 was violated when Respondent No. 1 delayed payment of Provident Fund contribution, family pension contribution, insurance fund contribution and more.
The Petitioner had passed an order for the levy of damages, but the Tribunal overturned it on the grounds that Respondent No. 1 did not intentionally default in payment of dues.
However, this decision was deemed to be an error of law and was overturned.
