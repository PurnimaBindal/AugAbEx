The Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(1)(i) and 25-F - Definitions of workman and retrenchment - Section 25-F is applicable only if the worker has been retrenched after completing 240 days of service in one unit - Days of employment under different units cannot be combined to meet the statutory period of 240 days.
[Paras 7 & 8]

The Constitution of India, Articles 227 & 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-F - The labor court rejected the relief sought under Section 25-F, citing the legal principle established by the division benches of the high court - In supervisory jurisdiction under Articles 227 of the Constitution, the high court may not interfere - If the subject matter is covered by Article 227, labeling a writ as a Writ under Article 226 will not suffice.
[Paras 8 & 9]

The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Parties - The award of the Labor Court is being challenged - If the relief requested is granted, it will impact the State's public exchequer - The State has not been made a party to the petition - No direction may be given to state officials in such instances unless the state is made a party to the petition.
