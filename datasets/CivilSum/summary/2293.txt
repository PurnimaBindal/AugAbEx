The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 10(1)(c) and Section 10(1)(d) - Challenge regarding Reference - Tribunal has the right to decide on retrenchment and reinstatement issues of workman - Under Section 10(1)(c) and Section 10(1)(d) of the Act, a reference falls within the purview of the Tribunal, especially if the union's demands concern retrenchment, which falls under item number 10 of the Third Schedule of the Act.

[Para 9]  C Rule 16 of the Gujarat Industrial Dispute Rules of 1966 - Failure to comply with provisions - Petitioner claims that notice was not served according to the prescribed forms - The dispute about retrenchment is to be treated as an 'individual dispute,' not as a general reference regarding a group of employees mentioned along with a schedule - Regardless, even if the rule was not followed, no prejudice can be attributed to the Petitioner, who has already filed its written statement - Therefore, the Petitioner cannot complain that he was prejudiced in this regard.
