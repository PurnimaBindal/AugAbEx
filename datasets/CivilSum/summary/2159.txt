The Swatantrata Sainik Sanman Pension Scheme of 1972 and Pensions Act of 1871, Section 4 specify the criteria for granting pensions to freedom fighters.
The petitioner's husband worked as a bodyguard of Netaji Subhash Chander Bose and was awarded the Tomar Pattar by the Prime Minister.
Despite an ex parte decree in favor of the petitioner's husband, execution proceedings were hampered by the respondents invoking Section 4 of the Pension Act, 1871.
The respondents neglected the issue, and therefore, the petitioner is entitled to special costs of Rs. one lac.
The respondents are directed to reinstate the pension from the date of discontinuation with interest at a rate of 10% per annum.
