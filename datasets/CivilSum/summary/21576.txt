Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 -Disciplinary proceedings - Misconduct - Dismissal from service - A workman, who was dismissed from service on charges of repeated misconduct with co-workers and superiors, was reinstated with 25% back wages by the Labour Court.
However, the Court stated that the workman must be given an opportunity to improve.
The Court held that a habitual offender cannot be given multiple chances to improve and cannot be retained in service.
The award passed by the Labour Court was quashed as a result.
