The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16 and 309, are relevant when it comes to regularisation and eligibility qualifications in regards to ad hoc appointments.
Petitioners appointed as Law officers on an ad hoc basis were assessed and selected based on their qualifications and have been continuing in their roles for several years, with the assumption that the positions will be required on a regular basis.
Although there are slight differences in qualifications specified in later rules, these rules will only apply to future recruitment.
The regularisation of these petitioner's services cannot be denied on the grounds of the new rules alone, as regularisation is an exceptional procedure.
The court can direct regularisation on a one-time basis but this procedure will not apply to future recruitment where the rules are applicable.
[Paras 8, 10 to 12]
