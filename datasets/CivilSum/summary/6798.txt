Constitution of India, Article 226 - Adverse report - Delay and laches - The petitioner received the adverse report in 1969, which was superseded in 1970.
The petitioner filed an appeal in 1976, which was decided in 1985.
However, the subsequent writ petition filed in 1988 after an inordinate delay should be dismissed as barred by delay and laches.
This would affect seniority and promotions of others up to twenty years back.
Interference under Article 226 of the Constitution is not necessary if the authority could take a particular view on the materials before it.
