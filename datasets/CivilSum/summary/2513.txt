Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Law Officers (Recruitment, Conditions of Service and Remuneration) Rules, 1967, Rules 30(1), 30(5) and 30(7) and Rule 36 - Legal Fees - The appellant claimed his fees for 101 Land Acquisition cases from the Government, but the Rule only provides for payment of fee as fixed by the Court or any other fee fixed by the Govt. at its discretion.
No fee is to be paid in the Misc. applications without an award of costs.
The appellant is not entitled to fees for those applications, but the statutory provision for payment as per fee fixed by the court has to be followed in the main cases.
The fee was not paid for 8 years, causing the appellant to be deprived of its use, and an excessive claim 8 years back has to be allowed in view of delay in payment.
The appeal is allowed.
