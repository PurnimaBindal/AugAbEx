Under the UCO Bank Officer Employees Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 20(1)(a)(iii), punishing authority cannot use charges that were not proven by the inquiry officer as a basis for punishment.
Additionally, the authority cannot impose punishment on allegations that were not in the initial charge-sheet, and doing so is a violation of natural justice and illegal.
[Paras 17 & 18]

Under the same regulations, a court may interfere to ensure justice is served in appropriate cases.
In this case, the punishing authority imposed punishment on charges that were not proven, and the punishment of removal from service was excessive for the simple irregularities that occurred at retirement.
As such, the petitioner's order of removal was substituted with an order of compulsory retirement with all benefits.
[Paras 19 & 20]
