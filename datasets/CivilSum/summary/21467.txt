In Section 7-A of the Employees' Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, it is mentioned that the appellant is obligated to contribute towards a specific class of people who are covered by an agreement for sugarcane transportation.
However, the Regional Provident Fund Commissioner, Appellate Tribunal and High Court did not analyze the evidence to determine whether transporters or carriers are also covered under the Act as persons towards whom the appellant must make contributions.
Therefore, the matter has been referred back to the Regional Provident Fund Commissioner for a fresh adjudication.
