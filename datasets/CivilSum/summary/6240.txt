The petitioner's claim for a compassionate appointment was rejected based on the 2003 Rules, which stated that the waiting list must remain operative for three years.
However, at the time of the petitioner's father's death, the ex-gratia appointment policy dated 851995 was applicable.
The petitioner's case was kept pending for four years and ultimately denied based on an instruction that did not exist at the time of the petitioner's father's death.
Despite an earlier specific direction by the High Court to consider the case in light of Jai Ram's case, the respondent again considered the case under the 2003 Rules instead of understanding the ratio of judgment in Jai Ram's case, which stated that the case of the petitioner should be governed by the instructions of 1995.
The impugned order has been quashed and the respondents have been directed to reconsider the petitioner's case in light of the instructions of 1995 as held in Jai Ram's case.
