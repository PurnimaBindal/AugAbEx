The Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 5 states that one year of wage earning service was necessary to be entitled to family pension under the scheme, before the 1988 amendment.
Thus, the husband of the petitioner, who fell ill before earning wages for one year, and subsequently died, was not entitled to family pension.
The petitioner cannot claim family pension because the cause of action arose to her when the law did not provide for it.
B.
According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 309, a change in service conditions will only apply prospectively from the date of the commencement of the policy.
Thus, those who are not covered under the new policy because of superannuation before its commencement cannot complain about a violation of Articles 14 and 16.
New laws cannot be made retrospective and must be considered prospective.
