Under the Indian Medical Council Act, 1956, Section 13(3), a person possessing medical qualifications granted by recognized institutions outside of India, which are included in part II of the Third Schedule, shall be recognized medical qualifications.
The petitioner-respondent acquired medical qualifications and training from U.S.S.R, which is included in part II of the Third Schedule.
Furthermore, the petitioner has undergone practical training in the U.S.S.R and is currently undertaking the required internship according to the Medical Council.
As a result, the petitioner-respondent is entitled to enrolment in the Medical Register.
The Council's review petition has been dismissed.
