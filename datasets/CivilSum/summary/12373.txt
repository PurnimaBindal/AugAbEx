Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Punjab Police Rules, 1934 - Chapter XVI, Rule 162 - Dismissal - Misconduct - Punishment - Length of service - In this case, a police Constable was charge-sheeted and dismissed from service for performing relief duty as Sentry in plain clothes and in a sitting position.
However, Rule 162 indicates that dismissal should only be awarded for the gravest acts of misconduct and length of service must also be taken into consideration.
The allegations against the respondent were trivial in nature and a dismissal from service seems too harsh for such a minor lapse, particularly for an employee with 22 years of service.
