The Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16, and 226 state that appointments should be made on a regular basis with regular pay scales.
The petitioners are regular Multipurpose Health Workers in the Department of Health and Family Welfare, but they were offered Block Extension Educator positions on a contract basis with a lump-sum salary, which they declined.
The State Government denied their request for regular appointments and pay scales since the positions were created under a Centrally Sponsored Scheme that required fixed-term and contractual appointments as per Finance Department instructions.
However, it is unconstitutional to deny the petitioners regular appointments and pay scales based solely on the origin of the positions.
Appointing the petitioners directly violates Articles 14 and 16 since there are numerous Block Education Educators and Multipurpose Health Workers appointed regularly under these very Centrally Sponsored Schemes with regular pay scales.
