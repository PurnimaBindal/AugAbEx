Constitution of India, 1950, Article 21 - Karnataka Government Servants (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1963, Rule 31 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Medical Attandence) Rules, 1970 - Medical Reimbursement - Employees may only claim medical reimbursement according to the Rules and not outside of them - Reimbursement for treatment from a hospital of the employee's choice may be limited and subject to certain conditions - In a specific case, the Supreme Court ordered the States of Karnataka and Rajasthan to pay the balance amounts to the employees for medical treatment received at private hospitals, but this order cannot be considered a precedent.
