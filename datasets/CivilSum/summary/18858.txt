The petitioner failed to prove that he was invalidated from service due to medical reasons under Army Rules, Rules 4 and 14(c) despite being given an extension of two years.
The petitioner retired from service after availing a full period.
Additionally, the onus is on the petitioner to demonstrate that their disease was caused by conditions of military service.
Therefore, no case has been made out by the petitioner for disability pension under Constitution of India, Article 226.
