Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-B - Termination - Legality of - Section 25-B of the Act states that a period of 240 days in twelve calendar months preceding the date with reference to which calculation is to be made must be considered for calculating termination - Petitioner appointed as Mali-cum-Chowkidar on daily wages on 1111990 but was terminated on 13.9.1991 - Despite petitioner having worked more than 240 days as per muster rolls, termination was done without following provisions of the Act - Termination was illegal.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(j) and 25-B - Industry - Public Health Department's function of providing water to both rural and urban areas would bring it under the definition of industry - There were no charges for water distribution services - Since petitioner had completed more than 240 days in service, removal without following procedures of the Act was not permissible - Petitioner is entitled to reinstatement with back wages.
