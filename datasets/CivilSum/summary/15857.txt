Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2 - Dismissal from service - Dismissal from service for absence from duty must be a result of grave misconduct.
Petitioner, a constable from a poor, scheduled caste family, was dismissed for being absent from duty for a short period of time.
He had gone to give his pay to his ill, widowed mother and was not allowed to rejoin the force for four days.
Although the petitioner was not likely to be a good police official, there was no allegation of continued misconduct or incorrigibility on his part.
The order of dismissal was quashed and he was directed to be reinstated without back wages.
[Paras 4 to 6]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and laches - Challenging a punishment order passed on 1-12-94 after it was conveyed to the petitioner and his appeal was dismissed by the appellate authority on 13-7-95 is not considered inordinate delay.
There is no triable issue of limitation involved and the objection of delay was rejected.
