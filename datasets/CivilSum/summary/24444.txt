The case involves the dismissal of a worker by a department and subsequent efforts to challenge the order of reinstatement passed by the Industrial Tribunal in 1992 with full back wages.
The worker's appeal before the CAT was dismissed in 1997 as not maintainable, and in February 1998, the department filed a writ challenging the award made by the Industrial Tribunal.
The High Court dismissed the writ, holding it to be delayed.
It is held that the High Court was in error in dismissing the writ without considering the relevant factors and intervening circumstances.
The matter is remitted to the High Court for decision on merits.
