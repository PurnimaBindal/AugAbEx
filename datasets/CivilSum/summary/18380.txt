Constitution of India, Articles 16 - All India Services Act (61 of 1951), Section 4 - Indian Police Service (Pay) Rules (1954), Rule 4(1)(a) and (b) - Annual increments - The petitioner, who joined the IPS Cadre, received pay protection in their previous position via a higher scale than the minimum in Rule 4(1)(a) of the Rules - Therefore, it is not appropriate to deny them annual increments until they reach a higher pay scale in their present position.
