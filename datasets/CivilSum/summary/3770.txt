The pay scales of two posts were made equivalent on the basis of recommendations by Pay Commission and Equivalence Committee.
However, the petitioners claimed that there was a lack of parity in pay scales.
They argued that both posts fall under the same department, have similar duties, and belong to the same cadre.
Thus, the respondents' action has been deemed arbitrary and discriminatory, violating Article 14 of the Constitution Therefore, the decision is unsustainable.
