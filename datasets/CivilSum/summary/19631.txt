Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Retirement benefits - Despite audit objections pending for 12 years on payments made by the petitioner to department employees, only a small amount is involved and there are no allegations against him.
Therefore, withholding the petitioner's retiring benefits due to the authorities' failure in deciding on the objections is not justified.
The authorities are ordered to release the petitioner's retiring benefits along with interest.
