Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Orissa Civil Service (Rehabilitation Assistance) Rules, 1990, Rule 1 - Compassionate appointment - Despite the State's refusal to give appointment to the petitioner on the grounds that her husband was not serving under the State Government, the Court advised authorities to treat the petitioner's case as a special one and grant her compassionate appointment following the death of her husband while performing duties in the Indo-Bangladesh Border under the Border Security Force.
