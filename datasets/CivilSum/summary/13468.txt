Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Army Officer - Seniority - Consideration for promotion - The petitioner, originally from the 1964 batch but who did not get selected in the first attempt, was moved to the 1965 batch as per instructions - Petitioner's original seniority will be valid from the date of appointment in the batch of 1964, but only when it comes to purposes other than consideration for higher rank promotions - If he has attempted promotion and failed, his seniority can legally be lowered to be considered with the next batch of officers.
