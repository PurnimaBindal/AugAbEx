Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Reversion - Petitioner's reversion from Class III to original post of Class IV service deemed illegal - Matriculation Certificate produced by petitioner was from Adult Education and Training Board, New Delhi, which was not a fake institution - State government had not specified that certificates of academic qualifications from unrecognized institutions were not acceptable at the time of petitioner's promotion - Petitioner's certificate had been accepted during promotion - No evidence that certificate was fake or institution did not exist - [Para 4]
