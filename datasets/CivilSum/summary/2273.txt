Army Rules, 1964, Rule 15A - Release from Service on medical ground - Review petition - The petition cannot be considered a challenge to the findings of the Medical Board regarding the petitioner's alcoholism as the petition itself admits to it.
The contention that the petition should be forwarded to the Central Government before the petitioner can be released on medical grounds is not accepted.
If the findings of the Board are not challenged within 15 days, they become final and the petitioner can be released.
Once the petitioner retires, any representation is deemed rejected by implication.
[Para 6]
Army Rules, 1964, Rule 15A - Release from Service on medical ground - Pension - Disability pension - If a person is released from service before completing any qualifying service due to becoming unfit for army service because of alcoholism, and there is no disability attributable to or aggravated by Army service, that person is not entitled to any type of pension.
