Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary Proceedings - Punishment - Compulsory retirement - Order on charge of unauthorised absenteeism - Employees who fail to inform their concerned authority of their absence will receive punishment in the form of compulsory retirement.
The employee cannot claim absence was out of their control if they only explain that they were unable to attend the office.
The order of compulsory retirement does not count as punishment.
[Paras 10 and 17]
