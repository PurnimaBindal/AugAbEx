Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 41 Rule 25 - Additional issue - The first appellate court can request a report on any additional issues on which parties provided evidence in support of their claims.
The trial court should make a decision about any additional issues and send both the findings and the evidence to the first appellate court.
The first appellate court can review the entire record and decide whether or not the judgment and decree of the trial court are sustainable.
It was not necessary for the first appellate court to set aside the entire judgment and decree of the trial court.
Instead, the Additional District Judge was directed to re-admit the appeal.
The trial court should record evidence related to any additional issues framed by the first appellate court and send the record and findings to the first appellate court.
