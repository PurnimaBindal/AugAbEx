Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Jails Department State Service (Class III Executive) Rules, 1963, Rule 11 - Seniority Ad hoc service - According to Rule 11, direct recruits' seniority shall be determined by their order of merit.
However, petitioners and respondents did not compete for seniority during their ad hoc appointments.
Relating their regular appointment to their ad hoc appointment date is unfair to respondents who were not given the opportunity to compete with the petitioners.
Therefore, the ad hoc service period of petitioners should not be considered for determining their seniority.
