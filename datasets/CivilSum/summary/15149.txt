Disciplinary proceedings - Enquiry report - Disagreement note - Nature of - The Tribunal permitted application stating that the disagreement note should be considered final and not tentative.
However, the note prepared and finalized by the council was found to be tentative since the language used in it suggests that there is a proposal to differ from the findings of the enquiry officer.
The note also mentions that the disciplinary authority would take a suitable decision after considering the report and submissions of the charged officer.
Therefore, the recorded contents were tentative and not a final decision.
Consequently, the order was cancelled.
