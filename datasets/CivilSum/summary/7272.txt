Industrial Disputes Act (XIV of 1947), Sections 2-A and 10 - Promotion - Misconduct - An office boy was promoted to a security guard but declined the promotion - The management prevented him from resuming his old post, resulting in dismissal - The employee has the right to raise an industrial dispute and refer it to the Labour Court - No one can be forced to accept promotion - Declining promotion is not misconduct or abandonment of service - Disciplinary action cannot be taken against employees for declining a promotion.
