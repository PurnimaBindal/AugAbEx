The Transfer policy formulated under Constitution of India, Articles 16, 149 and 309, provides for Transfer and Deputation; however, the Service Rules in place do not contemplate a transfer outside of the territorial jurisdiction of the cadre.
Therefore, the order to transfer employees of the office of Accountant General in the State of UP to the State of Uttaranchal was held illegal as the concerned employees did not give consent.
Since deputation carries with it the consent of the person, the transfer cannot be termed as transfer on deputation.
