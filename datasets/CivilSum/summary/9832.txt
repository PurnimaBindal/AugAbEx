Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Reversion - Seniority - Reversion on account of revision of seniority - Principles of Natural Justice - Concealment of facts - A similar writ petition was already allowed with the holding that reversion without giving an opportunity to the petitioner was improper and illegal - Although the petitioners did not disclose that they had already filed a Civil Suit for the same relief, the court still allowed the petition to meet the ends of justice and imposed a fine of Rs. 2,500/- each for concealing facts.
