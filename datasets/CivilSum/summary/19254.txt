Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Natural justice - Enquiry report - After dropping all charges except one of receiving higher rate of house rent allowance while residing in a rest house at a lower rate, the petitioner was punished with the stoppage of two increments permanently and recovery of the house rent amount without being given the opportunity of defense and hearing - Principles of natural justice were violated - Therefore, the order of punishment cannot be sustained - Nevertheless, since the petitioner did not pay the rent, he is not entitled to a refund of the house rent amount.
