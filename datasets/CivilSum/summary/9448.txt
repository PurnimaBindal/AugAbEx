Punjab National Bank (Employees) Pension Regulations, 1995, Regulations 7(3) and 56 - Family pension - When options are delayed, the competent authorities must consider the reasons and explanation for it - Rejection of option or application for family pension cannot solely be based on delay - Grounds for delay must be taken into account.
[Paras 9 to 11, 13, 14]  B. Punjab National Bank (Employees) Pension Regulations, 1995, Regulations 7(3) and 56 - Family pension for minors - The Regulations grant family pension to minors dependent on bank employees - The delay in making options by major members should not affect minors - The authorities must condone the delay - The scheme is designed to safeguard their welfare.
