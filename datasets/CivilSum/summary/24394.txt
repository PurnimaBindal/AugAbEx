Termination - Challenging the invalidation of a caste certificate due to delayed reference - The respondent was employed as a Scheduled Tribe candidate in 1976 and was promoted as such.
In December 1998, the Scrutiny Committee invalidated the certificate, and the High Court reinstated the respondent but held that they were not entitled to promotion as an ST.
The contention that the period to initiate proceedings was unreasonable is dismissed, as the foundation for the respondent's appointment has collapsed, and the appointment to a post meant for a reserved category was not an appointment in the eyes of the law.
The High Court's conclusions are contradictory, and the delay loses significance when the foundation for the delay is alleged fraud.
The appeal is allowed.
