J&K Civil Services (Judicial) Recruitment Rules, 1967, Rule 10 - If a candidate appears at an interview, they cannot subsequently challenge the validity of the interview process or results based on the fact that they were not selected.
[Para 9 and 10]

Writ court cannot re-assess the relative merits of interviewed candidates or challenge the assessment made by the interviewing committee.
The committee's judgement should be based on relevant rules governing such interviews.
[Para 10]

Members of the interviewing committee are not required to make a separate assessment of candidates based on individual faculties, as Rule 10(1)(b) of the J&K Civil Service (Judicial) Recruitment Rules, 1967 does not provide for this.
[Para 14]

Rule 10(1)(b) of J&K Civil Service (Judicial) Recruitment Rules, 1968 does not require tape-recordings of interviews to be made, therefore, the absence of a recording does not invalidate the selection process.
[Para 10]

The validity of viva voce tests cannot be challenged solely based on the results obtained unless there is evidence of mala fides or bias.
[Para 18]

The "actual practice" requirement for being appointed as a Munsif can be fulfilled by attending law courts regularly, in addition to being enrolled as an advocate for 2 years and having a certificate from the District Judge.
[Para 20]

The merit list and waiting list of selected candidates prepared by the Commission as per Rule 41 of J&K Service (Judicial) Recruitment Rules, 1967 will be valid for one year from the date of publication in the Government Gazette or until all vacancies are filled, whichever is earlier.
