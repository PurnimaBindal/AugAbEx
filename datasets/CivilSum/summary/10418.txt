Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Selection - Cancellation - Appointment as Urdu Translator/Junior Clerks - The order of cancelling appointment without opportunity of hearing is against the principles of natural justice, even if the report of the District Magistrate did not disclose any malpractice in the selection process - Complaints of corruption and favouritism in the selection process - State Government's order quashed.
