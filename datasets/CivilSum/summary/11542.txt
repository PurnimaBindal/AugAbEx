Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Promotion - Restrospective promotion - Arrears of pay - If a civil servant is not provided the work he is legally entitled to, he cannot be denied the wages for the post he is subsequently entitled to - The principle of ‘No work, no pay’ does not apply in this scenario - Allowing this practice would result in a double penalty, refusing the civil servant his right to promotion and depriving him of the emoluments he would have received on promotion.
