Constitution of India, Articles 311, 16 and 226 - Karnataka Civil Services (General Recruitment) Rules, 1977 - Termination - Contractual appointment - Petitioner appointed as Principal on contract basis - First respondent terminating services of petitioner without any notice - The petitioner was appointed on a contract basis with a narration until further orders, and the authority has the power to revise, amend or withdraw orders before the expiry of the period of appointment - There's no ground to interfere with the impugned notification terminating the petitioner.
