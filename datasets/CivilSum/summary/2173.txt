Regular Second Appeal - Promotion - Seniority - Right of Promotion - According to the Constitutional provision of Article 16, the senior employee has the prior right to promotion and no junior employee can be promoted without considering the case of their senior colleague.
Any deviation from this principle will lead to demoralization of the employees.
The respondent no. 1 being senior to other respondents must be considered and promoted prior to his junior.
The findings of fact of the lower courts cannot be interfered with.
The appeal has been dismissed.
