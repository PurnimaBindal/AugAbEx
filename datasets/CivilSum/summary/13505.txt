Mr. S Venkata Reddy, Advocate represented Respondent No. 2 and 3 and invoked Constitution of India Articles 14, 16 and 226 as well as Andhra Pradesh Co-operative Societies Rules, 1964, Rule 28(1) in discussion of delayed promotion and claims of Petitioners.
The respondents were previously promoted without requisite qualifications, and the illegal promotion was subsequently challenged by the Petitioners who were then appointed via direct recruitment with required qualifications in 1979.
Respondents were then further promoted to Asstt.
General Manager in 1987 without considering the claims of the Petitioners, however, the Petitioners' belief that no further illegal promotions would be allowed entitled them to challenge the initial illegality in 1987 without facing any undue delay or laches.
Therefore, the Petitioners are entitled to their consideration for promotion.
