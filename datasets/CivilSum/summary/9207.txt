The Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10, and Civil Procedure Code - Sections 2 and 11 expound upon Res judicata, Jurisdiction, Industrial Dispute, and Dismissal from Service.
Upon an order of dismissal of service, a workman has two remedies: he can challenge the order in the Civil Court or in the Labour Court.
Once the workman chooses one remedy, he cannot resort to the other.
The order of the Civil Court will act as res judicata in the proceedings before the labour court, which can hold the dispute barred by res judicata.
[Para 4]
The Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 deals with delays in demands for referral of disputes.
In the case of a demand notice by a workman after dismissal from service after a delay of six years, the appropriate Government ought to have denied a reference of such a belated dispute.
Although the labour court was bound to address the reference, it could have denied the back wage on this ground.
