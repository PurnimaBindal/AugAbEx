Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 7(7) - Appeal - Limitation - Delay - Condonation of - A petitioner filed an appeal after being aggrieved by the refusal of the payment of the balance amount of gratuity.
However, it was rejected as it was filed beyond the permissible period of limitation of 120 days, which is 60 days normally but can be extended to a further period of 60 days.
The order was received on 1361988, and the appeal was filed on 12101988.
Unfortunately, the application for the condonation of the delay was abruptly rejected without giving the petitioner an opportunity.
As a result, the impugned order was set aside and the matter has been remitted back to the appellate authority.
[Paras 2, 4 and 5]
