Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 311 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rules 19(2), 10(4) - Fundamental Rules, Rule 54 - Suspension from date of original order of dismissal - Tribunal quashed dismissal order with liberty to conduct enquiry - Petitioners passed order of retrospective suspension - If original order was made by way of penalty and was set aside by court, power to place delinquent under suspension from date of original dismissal order will be available - Decision to hold fresh enquiry does not attract Rule 10(4) as there was no enquiry leading to removal - Therefore, retrospective suspension is unjustified and without authority of law.
