According to the Punjab Government Policy dated 10121997 on admission to Engineering Technology/Architecture under Sports category, softball game has been deemed included in the games recognised under the policy, but it does not satisfy the criteria prescribed under Clause 8 of the Policy as it is an individual game and requires competition with 7 or more competitors.
The expression "individual event" cannot be read in complete dis-junction from the other clauses of the Policy, and where there is a specific provision, there cannot be any deeming fiction that is not mentioned in the provision itself.

The jurisdiction of the court to interfere in policy matters regarding admissions to sports category is limited, as various considerations of the expertised nature involved in determining eligibility for affiliation/recognition by the Olympic Association of the State cannot be determined by the courts.
The expert body considers only specified games to be fulfilling the criteria of the policy, and there can be no deemed inclusion where an expert body considers only specified games to be fulfilling the criteria of the policy.
