The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admissions/Education - Admission to MD/MS Post Graduate courses - Entrance test - The petitioner failed to obtain the minimum eligible marks in the entrance test during her only available chance, according to the terms and conditions - As a result, she cannot claim admission or a second chance in the entrance test.
Furthermore, the fact that a candidate who was mistakenly ignored from consideration in the SC category and given a second chance as an SC candidate will not entitle the petitioner to a second chance.
