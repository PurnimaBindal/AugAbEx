The Constitution of India, Articles 310(1), 309, and 311 - Suspension - Pleasure of Government - Government Servants are employed under the Government, and their services can be terminated at any point of time due to the pleasure of the Government.
Rules framed under Article 309 do not affect the pleasure of the Government to terminate, suspend, or appoint any Government employee or delegate such authority to any other authority under the prescribed rules.
[Paras 6 and 7]  B The Pleasure doctrine - Appointment and termination of Government servant - Being the ultimate employer of government servants, the Government has the power to direct any authority to take action against any government employee.
Powers granted to the appointing authority are subject to Article 310(1) of the Constitution.
