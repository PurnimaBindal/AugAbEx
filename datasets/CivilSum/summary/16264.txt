According to Article 30(1) of the Constitution of India and Sections 3(2), 4(6), 5 and 9 of the Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1977, a senior teacher who is promoted and appointed as the Head of the institution cannot be demoted to the post of senior teacher again, as per the Rules.
Once the vacancy is filled and the incumbent completes two years, they become a confirmed employee and are protected under Section 4(6).
A reversion under these circumstances is deemed illegal and has been quashed.
The Act extends protection to the Head of the institution in line with its objective.
