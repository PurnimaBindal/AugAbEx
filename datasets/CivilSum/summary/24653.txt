Rajasthan Civil Courts Ordinance, 1950, Section 21(1)(a) - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 21, Rule 63 - Execution - To promote a suit to set aside auction sale, the valuation should correspond with the amount of the decree and not with the value of the property.
If the suit is decreed for an amount less than Rs. 10,000/-, it falls within the pecuniary jurisdiction of the District Court under Section 21(1)(a) of the 1950 Ordinance.
Consequently, the decree passed by the District Court is not nullity.
[Para 5]

Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 47, Order 21, Rule 63 - Executability of the decree - After a property is sold in execution, if a plaintiff chooses to file a suit to set aside the sale, the decree passed is declared not binding on the plaintiff.
Directions for the restitution of suit property, which are contained in the decree, make it an executable decree.
Considering the long pendency of litigation, the Supreme Court directed the defendant to pay the market value of the suit property instead of restitution.
[Paras 6 and 7]
