High Court must determine whether laches on the part of the applicant is enough to disqualify them from relief, given that there is no limitation period specified for filing an application under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.

[Para 14] B The Tribunal directed that employment be given to all empanelled candidates beyond sanctioned strength.
However, the number of posts was not sufficient, and all applicants ranked below 50 in the panel at the time of advertisement.
The approach taken by the Tribunal was entirely incorrect.
The application for reassessment was granted, and the order issued by the Tribunal was reversed.
