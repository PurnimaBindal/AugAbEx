Constitution of India, Article 16 - Service Matter - Appointment - Dismissal - Compulsory Retirement - A Gangman in Railway Department was appointed and claimed to be a member of Malayalee community scheduled tribe.
However, an order of Removal from service was passed due to a bogus Community Certificate filed by the Respondent.
After a revisional authority modified the order of Removal from service to one of compulsory retirement, a writ petition was filed by the Appellants UOI and State Govt. to oppose the decision.
The High Court set aside the orders passed by the Departmental Authorities and held that the Respondent belonged to ST, although the State Govt. later filed an order of Social Welfare Department showing that Lambadi is not a ST and the HC judgment was set aside.
