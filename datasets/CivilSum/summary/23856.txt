The complaint of professional misconduct under Section 35 of Advocates Act is applicable only if the advocate committed the misconduct while on the roll of the State Bar Council, and punishments under Section 35(3) must be given to advocates found guilty of misconduct while practicing law.
If a person is dismissed from judicial service and then resumes practice as an advocate, they cannot be punished under Section 35 of the Act for misconduct committed during their service as a judicial officer.
[Paras 5, 6 and 11 to 13]
Under Section 35(3) of the Advocates Act, an advocate can be punished for any misconduct, not just professional misconduct.
Advocates on the State Bar Council who committed misconduct, such as rash and negligent driving or consuming illicit liquor, can be dealt with under Section 35, even if their misconduct was not professional.
[Paras 5, 7 & 8]
A judicial officer whose name has already been removed from the State Bar Council roll as per Section 26-A cannot be punished under Section 35(1) for any misconduct that occurred during that period.
Section 35 only deals with misconduct committed while practicing law, and non-practicing advocates cannot be punished under Section 35(1).
[Paras 12 & 13]
Misconduct of corruption by a judicial officer does not result in disqualification under Section 24-A of the Advocates Act.
