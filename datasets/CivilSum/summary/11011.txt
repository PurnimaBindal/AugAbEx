Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Schedule 2, Item 3 - Misconduct - Punishment - Scope of writ jurisdiction - Writ court cannot substitute Enquiry Officer or punishing authority's findings of fact by reappreciation of evidence, even if material suggests inconsistency - Findings of fact returned by the enquiry officer will not be interfered with by the court, where sufficient material is available to link the delinquent with the charge.
[Paras 7 & 9]
