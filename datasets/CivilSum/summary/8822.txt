According to Rule 8 of West Bengal Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1971, and Article 311(2) of the Constitution of India, the Court/Tribunal should not set aside the punishment order solely on the grounds of non-supply of the enquiry report if it would not have impacted the finding or the punishment given.
[Para 12]  B.
As per Article 311(2) of the Constitution of India, notice of proposed punishment is essential for national justice and compliance with the principles of natural justice.
Punishment cannot be awarded without providing a copy of the enquiry report and notice.
The Court has directed that the delinquent must be informed of the punishment proposed against him, and his representation is to be considered before final order is passed in accordance with the rules and directions of the court.
