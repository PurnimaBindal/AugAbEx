According to Section 2(9) of the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, a Managing Director of a Company can be considered an employee under certain circumstances.
However, simply having the legal ability to act as an employee does not always mean that a Director or Managing Director must be treated as an employee.
Further examination is necessary to determine if there was a separate agreement or provision in the Articles of Association establishing a contract of employment between the Company and the Managing Director or Director.
