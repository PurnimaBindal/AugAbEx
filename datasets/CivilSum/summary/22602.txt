According to Section 2(22) of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948 and Section 10-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, "wages" includes any amount paid to workmen during the pendency of proceedings before the special Tribunal on the reference qua revision of pay scales, regardless of the nomenclature used.
As a result, such payments can legally be claimed as contributions by the ESI Corporation.
