Constitution of India, Article 16 - Reversion from the specialized post of Dog-handler - Show cause notice - Rules of natural justice - The petitioner was being reverted on the ground that the out-of-town promotion was not allowed under the Punjab Police Rules/Government instructions, which is against the judicial pronouncement - The petitioner's explanation was not considered, and certain information/documents were not supplied to him - The impugned order did not give any reason for reversion - No reasonable opportunity was given to the petitioner to show cause - The order of reversion is set aside.
