According to the Industrial Disputes Act, workman who has been terminated due to the closure of an establishment is entitled to compensation as per Section 25F.
In this particular case, the services of a staff nurse were terminated due to the closure of the Maternity Hospital, and she was not paid retrenchment compensation.
The order granting reinstatement with backwages is unsustainable.
