Section 22 of SICA, 1985, does not prohibit the recovery of Gratuity under Bengal Public Demands Recovery Act, and cannot resist any proceeding for realizing gratuity amount that is a fundamental right of a retired employee.
The respondents are ordered to recover the amount with interest.
