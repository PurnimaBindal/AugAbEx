Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Temporary appointment/probation period/termination without notice or notice pay - During a probationary period, a temporary employee may have their services terminated without notice or notice pay if their work and conduct are deemed unsatisfactory based on the conditions of their appointment - As the services of the employee in question were terminated during their probationary period due to unsatisfactory work and conduct, the order cannot be considered illegal for failure to provide one month notice or payment of salary in lieu thereof.
