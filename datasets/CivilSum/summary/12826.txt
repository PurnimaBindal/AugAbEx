Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226/227 - Policy decision - Higher qualification - Advanced increments - The petitioner, a Social Studies Master, claims that he is entitled to two advance increments for having acquired the degrees of LL.B.
and MA during service as per the policy decision of the Govt. in the Circular dated 26.9.1972 - The Circular, of an administrative nature, contains binding instructions for the Govt. - Any deviation or violation of the policy decision, without a valid reason, would contravene the principle of equality enshrined in the Constitution - The petitioner has a right to receive two advance increments for higher qualifications - Directed respondents to grant petitioner the benefit of two advance increments as per the policy decision.
