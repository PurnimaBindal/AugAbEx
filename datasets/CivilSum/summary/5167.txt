The Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1996, Preamble, Section 47 - History - Scope - Applicability - The Act is in line with the Proclamation on Full Participation and Equality of People with Disabilities in the Asian and Pacific Region.
Thus, it must be read in the context of the said proclamation.
The Act aims to provide social security for disabled individuals, and if statutory liability is imposed upon the employer for the same purpose, it cannot be considered arbitrary.
The contention that the Act only protects those who suffer injury during the course of their employment is not viable.

The Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights, and Full Participation) Act, 1996, Preamble, Section 47 - Retrospective effect of Act - If an accident occurred before the enforcement of the Act and services were terminated prior to the coming into force of the Act but then the termination was effected after the enforcement of the Act, it would be considered unlawful.
