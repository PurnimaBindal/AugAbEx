Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Andhra Pradesh State Co-operative Societies Act, 1964, Sections 116-A and 116-B - Reservation/Promotions - Reservation for Scheduled Castes/Scheduled Tribes - During the first appointment only, the reservation is applicable - Promotions do not have any reservation - It is not a violation of Article 16(4) of the Constitution if reservations are not made in Class II cadre posts promotions - It is a policy matter and no specific direction can be given for policy enlargement or restriction.
[Paras 4, 5 and 8]  B. Andhra Pradesh State Cooperative Societies Act, 1964, Section 116-A - Power of the Registrar to frame Common Cadre Regulations means as approved by the Registrar - Regulations must be framed in consultation with the 'Cadre Committee' closely associated with the selection process - The Cadre Committee can be consulted and cannot be considered an outside body - Consultation with the Committee does not vitiate Regulations - The Registrar can still add, delete, substitute or modify them.
[Para 6]  C Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Experience - Marks for the experience - Reservation of 22 marks for the service provided in the feeder cadre is not excessive - The service should be in accordance with the Rules after the appointment and may be either temporary or regular, but it should not be against the Rules.
