Rajasthan Municipal (Subordinate and Ministerial Service) Rules, 1963, Rules 25, 26 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Service Matters Appellate Tribunal) Act, 1976, Section 2 - Writ - Maintainability of - Petitioner holds a civil post and is considered a government servant, hence alternative remedy is available before Tribunal.
Therefore, the petition is not maintainable.
[Para 7]  B Interim order - Grant of - Though writ petition is not maintainable due to alternative remedy available, stay is extended for a week to allow the Petitioner to file an appeal before the Tribunal.
