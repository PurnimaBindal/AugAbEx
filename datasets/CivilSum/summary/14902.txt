Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission - Education - Fraud - Petitioners' admission was cancelled at the end of their two-year course without any opportunity, claiming that they fraudulently obtained admission because the last candidate admitted on merit obtained more marks - No evidence of fraud was found - Petitioners did not conceal anything and did not resort to any deceitful means for obtaining admission - Cancellation of their admission at the end of the session after they had lost two years of their career was unwarranted- Authorities failed in their duty to establish the allegations and act promptly - Order of cancellation was arbitrary and illegal and thus quashed - The authorities were directed to issue diploma to the petitioners.
[Paras 7 to 17]
