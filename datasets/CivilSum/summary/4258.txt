Indian Airlines is considered a 'State' under Article 12 of the Constitution and can be subjected to a writ petition under Article 226.
[Para 9]
A trade union of a particular category of workers cannot be denied limited purpose recognition if it has a reasonable basis and the state cannot act in an unfair or discriminatory manner.
[Paras 9 and 11]
The doctrine of legitimate expectation and its scope must be considered in State actions to ensure non-arbitrariness and fairness.
Reasonable expectations beyond enforceable legal rights are also taken into account.
[Para 9]
Refusal or granting recognition to a trade union dealing with private management may be a policy matter but state undertakings cannot avoid their responsibilities.
Court intervention is not limited to quasi-judicial functions and can issue fair directives to state undertakings.
