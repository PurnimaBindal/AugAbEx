Constitution of India, Article 226 - Conduct - Discipline and Appeal Rules, 1985, Rule 33 - Termination of Service - Enquiry - Dispensing of - According to Rule 33, termination of service must follow an enquiry, unless there are exceptional circumstances that make it impracticable or contrary to the security of the Corporation or State to do so.
The reason for the penalty of dismissal against the petitioner is his alleged unauthorized and forceful entry, in a drunken state, into the chamber of the Chairperson of the Corporation, where he allegedly threatened her, necessitating his forcible eviction.
Additionally, he allegedly attempted to re-enter the room.
As no satisfaction was recorded that it was not expedient or impracticable to hold an enquiry, the order of termination was passed contrary to the rules and is therefore liable to be set aside.
