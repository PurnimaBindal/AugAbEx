Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Examination - Bye Laws 41.1 and 42 - Admission - Question of eligibility for petitioners, who are students of class 11th, to appear in final exams - Petitioners had compartment in matriculation exam, but examination forms not accepted according to Bye-laws - However, Brochure allows for such admission and cumulative effect of Bye-laws is that compartmental candidates can continue with next higher academic course - Petition accepted, petitioners entitled to roll number and right to appear in 11th class exam conducted by respective schools.
