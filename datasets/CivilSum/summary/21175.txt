Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Central Engineering Service Group A Recruitment Rules, 1954, Rule 21(3) Proviso (as inserted on 31.10.1972) - Promotion - Proviso to Rule 21(3) does not suffer from an arbitrariness vice in service jurisprudence, as it uses the concept of `outstanding merit' - Promotion to a selection post on the basis of merit is a well recognized notion - Therefore, it is not violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - Promotion of non-graduate Assistant Engineers to Executive Engineers must include those with `'outstanding ability and record'` - Before the 1996 Rules came into force, promotions from the cadre of Assistant Engineers (graduates as well as diploma holders) to the cadre of Executive Engineers were governed by Rule 21(3) of the 1954 Rules, as amended in 1972 to include the proviso - Regularisation of diploma-holder Assistant Engineers who are working as ENs on ad hoc basis against vacancies that occurred after the 1996 Rules promulgation - Must be carried out in accordance with the 1996 Rules.
