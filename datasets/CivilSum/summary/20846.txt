Kurukshetra University Information Brochure for MBBS/BDS Entrance Examination, Clause 18 - Change of course - Reasoning behind bar - Ensure medical course seats are not wasted - However, the bar is only in effect during the course term, regardless of whether the candidate undertakes the course to which they were admitted - Once the course term ends, the bar is lifted and the candidate can seek admission to any desired course.
