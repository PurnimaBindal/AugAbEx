According to the UP Development Authorities Centralised Services Rules, 1985, Rules 3, 7, and 28, along with the UP Government Servants Seniority Rules, 1991, Rule 6, the seniority among the Govt. servants of UP must be determined in accordance with these Rules.
Rule 3 of 1991 Rules takes precedence over all other rules made earlier in other services in the UP State, whereas Rule 7 of the Centralised Services Rules has overriding effect against Rule 28 of those Rules only.
It is important to note that Rule 6 of 1991 Rules cannot be ignored as it has overriding effect on Rule 7 of Centralised Services Rules.
