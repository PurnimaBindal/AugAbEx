Constitution of India, Article 16 - Seniority - Dispute regarding seniority and promotions - A deputationist, who was absorbed as a Junior Intelligence Officer, Grade 1, filed an appeal after the Tribunal deemed that services rendered by the petitioner as a deputation promotee in one officiating cadre of ACIO-II must be taken into consideration for seniority and promotions - However, since the petitioner's earlier ad hoc promotion as ACIO-II was against the deputation quota, that service cannot be claimed by a deputationist once they opt for permanent absorption in the department - As a result, the petitioner cannot claim benefits from both absorption and the service put in the deputation quota at ACIO-II - The appeal has been allowed.
