Army Act, 1950, Section 191 - Army Rules, 1954, Rules 33 & 34 - Court-Martial - Summary Court-Martial - Natural Justice - Charge sheet - Opportunity to reply and prepare defence - The accused must be given at least 96 hours notice, as well as 24 hours when on active duty, under Rule 34.
Starting and finishing proceedings on the same date without any emergency constitutes a violation of natural justice.
[Paras 8, 9, and 13] B.
Army Act, 1950, Section 191 - Army Rules, 1954, Rules 33 & 34 - Court-Martial - Summary Court-Martial - Natural Justice - Charge sheet - The principles of natural justice are violated by taking action without any emergency.
[Para 13] C. Army Act, 1950, Section 191 - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 147 - Court-Martial - Summary Court-Martial - Supply of enquiry proceedings - The accused must be supplied with a copy of inquiry proceedings within a reasonable amount of time under Rule 147.
Providing them with the order passed in a petition under Section 165 does not replace this requirement.
[Para 10] D. Army Act, 1950, Section 191 - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 129 - Court- Martial - Summary Court-Martial - Natural Justice - Opportunity to defence with the assistance of friend or legal practitioner - The accused must be informed of their right to assistance under Rule 129.
Failure to do so violates this rule and cannot be fixed by only changing the punishment.
