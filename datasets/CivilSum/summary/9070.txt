Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion/Consideration for promotion - Bank employee - Reversion - The civil court set aside the reversion order and the employee was reinstated with all consequential benefits.
His service record was compared to his juniors who had been promoted and he was granted promotions where found eligible and better than those promoted earlier.
The orders ignoring him at stages where his service record was bad or he was ineligible did not constitute illegality.
An employee is entitled only to consideration for promotion and may be promoted only if found fit.
[Paras 4, 5 and 7]
