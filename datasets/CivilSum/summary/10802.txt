In accordance with the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provision Act, 1952, Sections 17 and 27, the Commissioner has the authority to impose conditions for the exemption of individual employees, as stated in paragraph 27-AA.
Compliance with these conditions may be required for the grant or continuance of an exemption under paragraph 27, and their applicability will be consistent across all establishments, individuals, or categories of employees.
