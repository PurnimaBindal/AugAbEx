Constitution of India, Article 14 - Selection/Appointments - The Public Service Commission recommended only 62 out of 98 candidates requested by the government due to concerns about the suitability of a candidate who scored less than a certain level for the Assistant Sub-inspector position.
The Commission had the option to recommend their favourites but no malice or bias can be attributed to them.
It is up to the Commission to determine the specific number of marks required for a candidate to be considered suitable, and if no criteria are established during an interview, the Board can reject a particular candidate.
The Commission's decision in this regard is final and cannot be challenged.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection/ Recommendations - It is not mandatory for the Selection Committee to recommend the same number of candidates as there are vacancies.
The selecting body may consider the number of suitable candidates available and recommend a smaller number of candidates than the number of vacancies if there are not enough qualified candidates to fill all vacancies.
