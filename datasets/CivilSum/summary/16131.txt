Petitioners, who have higher merit, claim they were entitled to admission but the date of the interview was changed without notice, rendering the interview a mere formality.
No marks were reserved for interview.
It is argued that the petitioner, with higher merit, should be admitted for all available seats.
The authorities must serve personal notice of any date changes to the meritorious candidates, as it is unreasonable to expect candidates to continually check newspapers for updates.
