Rajasthan Medical (Collegiate Branch) Rules 1962, Rule 24-A(11-B) and Article 309 Proviso of the Constitution of India allow for promotions and the formation of DPCs.
In the case of an error being made in the decision-making process, a review DPC can be formed, which is valid.

In the context of a promotion to the post of Reader under the Rajasthan Medical (Collegiate Branch) Rules 1962, Rule 24-A(11-B) and Article 309 Proviso of the Constitution of India, the disqualification of a candidate's foreign service record and qualification during the DPC meeting validates the formation of a review DPC meeting.
[Para 16]

The Constitution of India, Article 16 mandates the consideration of candidates' qualifications and experience in promotions.
