Punjab Police Rules, Rule 16.2 - Dismissed from service - A constable was dismissed from service for being absent from duty, but it was argued that the petition was filed after too much delay and that the impugned order is in line with the rules.
The court found no extreme delay and could not observe that his absence resulted in serious misconduct.
In fact, as a member of the Schedule Castes, he had gone to his village to give his pay to his ailing mother and was unable to return to work as a result.
Given his circumstances, the petitioner did not deserve the extreme penalty of dismissal.
The impugned orders were set aside, and the petitioner was reinstated without any arrears of salary.
[Paras 3, 5, 6 and 7]
