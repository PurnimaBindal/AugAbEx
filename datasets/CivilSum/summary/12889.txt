The Family Pension Scheme, 1964 - Claim for family pension filed by a widow - The deceased served as a driver for 11 months on a work charge basis - The deceased underwent a medical examination at the time of joining and his presence was marked in the register of work charge employees - However, it cannot be considered that the deceased was in service on a regular basis solely because of the medical examination, which may have been done to assess his ability to drive - As per the judgement of the Apex court in Surji Devi's case, which states that the scheme does not provide family pension to dependents of employees working on a work charge basis, the petitioner cannot receive any benefits.
