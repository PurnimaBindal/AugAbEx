According to Section 14 of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 and Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules (1972), R. 1, employees of the MCD are eligible for payment of gratuity even if the MCD has already adopted the provisions of the Pension Rules which also include the provision for gratuity.
This entitlement remains despite the fact that provisions of Pension Rules which provide for gratuity are made applicable to them.
