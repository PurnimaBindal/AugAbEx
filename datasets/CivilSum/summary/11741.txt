Punjab Police Rules, Rule 162 - Absence from duty - Misconduct - Dismissal from service - The plaintiff, a Constable, was dismissed from service for treating his absence from duty as without permission.
Trial court decreed the suit of the plaintiff but the State appealed.
Respondents had regularised the absence of 44 days prior to the conclusion of the departmental proceedings.
Hence, the unauthorised absence was regularised.
The impugned order of dismissal was found not being in conformity with Rule 162 of the Punjab Police Rules and not applying mind to the matter.
The lower courts affirmed the findings and the State's appeal was dismissed.
