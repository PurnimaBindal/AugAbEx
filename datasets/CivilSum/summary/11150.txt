Service matter - Delay and laches - Limitation - The plaintiff filed a suit claiming for last pay which was not paid after four years.
However, the claim for pay was declined due to being barred by time.
[Para 6]
Service Matter - Regularisation of Service - The plaintiff was appointed as an apprentice Clerk but was relieved of the said post as he failed to pass the Apprenticeship Examination.
The plaintiff worked as a Ticket Collector and then as a Helper, paid for the same job.
However, the plaintiff is not entitled to be regularised in service as a Clerk.
[Paras 6 and 7]
