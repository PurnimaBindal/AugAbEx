Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Promotion - Petitioner was absorbed in the Revenue Department from the Consolidation Department and later transferred back to Consolidation Dept.
where he was promoted as Kanungo and then Assistant Consolidation Officer - Benefit of service rendered in Consolidation Dept.
before his absorption in Revenue Department was not taken into account while fixing seniority - Petitioner denied right to be considered for promotion when his juniors were promoted in parent department - Petitioner is entitled to benefit of service rendered in Consolidation Dept.
as no justification was given for not considering this service while fixing seniority and promotion - Respondents directed to refix seniority from petitioner's date of appointment in Consolidation Dept.
Kartar Singh v. State of Punjab, 1989(4) S.L.R.
