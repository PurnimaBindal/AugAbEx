Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 141 - Interpretation of statutory provisions in judicial review - It is not permissible for the Court to add to statutory provisions beyond what is plainly stated - Interpretation cannot be given which defeats the objective of the statute or leads to illogical results - The Court can only supply principles of doctrine of 'casus omissus' if there is clear necessity after examining the entire statute - Alternative interpretations cannot be applied to statutory provisions just because they are possible - Explains principles of interpreting statutes.
[Paras 7 to 15] B Kerala Education Rules, Chapter XIV(A), Rules 62 and 60(c) - Revising pay scales after the retirement date - The prohibition on increment and promotion does not apply to pay scale revisions - Lawmakers deliberately left out certain things - Teachers can get retiral benefits and pensions calculated based on their pay scale revisions before their actual retirement date irrespective of their post-retirement superannuation on completion of the retirement age.
