Constitution of India, Article 16 - Madhya Pradesh Services (Gazetted) Recruitment Rules, 1966 - Recruitment to Assistant Director, Veterinary - Two sources available - Direct recruitment and promotion in proportion of 1:1 - Appellants challenged validity of Notification and selection - Government decided to provide recruitment by promotion with 100% from the feeder cadre - However, rules were not amended - Recruitment and selection done according to unamended rules - Thus, valid in Law.
