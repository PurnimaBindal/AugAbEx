Constitution of India, Article 226 - Remarks in ACR - Expunction of - The Respondent requested that remarks in ACR for the year 1985-86 be expunged alleging bias, prejudice, and subjectivity and the Tribunal granted the request.
The Petitioner challenged the decision arguing that the reporting and accepting officers of the Respondent acted fairly and objectively.
The Petitioner asserted that the ACR was duly recorded on proper evaluation and careful assessment.
However, the Respondent was contesting litigation on one hand and holding departmental enquiry and writing ACRs on the other, which raised apprehension of bias in recording the ACR.
Consequently, the controversial ACR is expunged from the record, upholding the order of the Tribunal.
The Petition is dismissed accordingly.
