Constitution of India, Article 226 - Advocates Act, 1961, Section 10-B - Bar Council of Delhi disqualified a member for not attending three consecutive meetings without notice or hearing - The Council was only required to declare the happening of a contingency and the principles of natural justice do not apply in such cases.
[Paras 18, 21, 22, 27, 29 and 31]  B.
Principles of natural justice - Flexibility of application - The principles of natural justice cannot be rigidly applied as they depend on the facts and circumstances of each case and may be excluded in certain situations.
