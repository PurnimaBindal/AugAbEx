Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Discharge/Dismissal from service/Departmental Enquiry - Dismissal from service without resorting to provisions of Article 311(2) of Constitution of India - Only allowed within three years of recruitment - A decision conveyed after 3 years of recruitment was deemed as illegal and violating the rules - As a result, the petitioner was reinstated with all consequential benefits.
