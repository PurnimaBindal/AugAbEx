J&K Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 27 - Transfer - Deputation - A Head Draftsman was transferred from R&B(J) and posted in the Jammu Development Authority Department in the public interest and for the sake of administration.
The petitioner challenges this move as an unauthorized transfer out of the cadres without consent and deputation, citing malafide near retirement.
The interference in transfer matters is only justified if a prima facie case of bias/malafide is established.
Mere apprehension is insufficient to prove that the order is arbitrary, unjust, or malafide.
[Para 11]
