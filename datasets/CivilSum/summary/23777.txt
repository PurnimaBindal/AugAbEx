Legal strikes may still result in deducted wages, but may protect workers from disciplinary action, according to Punjab Civil Services Rules (as applicable to Haryana) Vol. 1, Rule 44.
[Para 26]
Conduct during a mass strike cannot be equated to individual conduct for disciplinary action, according to Punjab Civil Services Rules (as applicable to Haryana) Vol. 1, Rule 44.
[Paras 28 and 29]
When employees strike for any length of time, the employer has the right to deduct wages in accordance to the Employment Act, even if employees worked during a portion of the day.
[Para 5]
The Payment of Wages Act allows employers to deduct strike wages if contract standing orders or service rules and regulations do not specify otherwise.
[Paras 22, 23 and 24]
Employers have the right to deduct wages when employees do not fulfill their allotted work according to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(rr) and 2(q).
[Paras 22, 25 and 27]
Employers may recover wages for go-slows as misconduct, but only after holding an enquiry.
