Indian Majority Act, 1875, Section 4 - Age - Superannuation - To determine retirement age, the day of birth is counted as a full day.
Respondent No. 1 reached the age of 60 on 30.6.1993 and was scheduled to retire on that day.
As he retired before the start of the next academic session on 171993, he was not entitled to continue working until the completion of the 1994 academic session.
