Punjab Privately Managed Recognized Schools Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 6 - Pension Scheme of 1992, Clause 6(6) - Qualifying service for pension - Calculation of service rendered prior to implementation of Pension Scheme for private aided school employees - Qualifying service will begin either from the date of taking charge of the post in which the employee was initially appointed or from the date they started contributing to the Provident Fund, whichever came first - Provision of Clause 6(6) of the 1992 Scheme has to be interpreted as qualifying service beginning from the date of continuous appointment or when the employee started contributing to PF - Employees will be eligible to count their service from the date of their appointment - If the employee's contribution to PF was not made, it may be adjusted or deducted from their pension arrears.
