Army Act, 1950, Section 192 - Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 173 - Disability pension - Petitioner was boarded out of military service due to a disease called neurosis, which he did not have at the time of recruitment.
He was posted on high altitudes in Jammu and Kashmir where he suffered from neurosis, possibly caused by the stressful nature of his service.
His condition worsened, and he was discharged from service, but disability pension was denied to him without proper examination.
This action was deemed irrational and arbitrary, and the petitioner's case should be reconsidered in accordance with the rules.
