Contempt of Courts Act, 1971 - Purpose - The introduction of the Contempt Act is to instill confidence in the general public and ensure proper administration of justice in the country.
The jurisdiction exercised should be done with caution and for the larger interest.
[Para 1]
Contempt of Courts Act, 1971 - Proof of - Nature of proceedings - The burden and standard of proof is on the accuser, and the proceedings are quasi-criminal.
The breach must be established beyond reasonable doubt.
[Paras 2 and 7]
Contempt of Courts Act, 1971 - Promotion - The petitioner was not mandated to be promoted, but only to have seniority recasted based on eligibility.
The petitioner was considered but not promoted, making it clear that there was no contempt committed.
[Para 7]
