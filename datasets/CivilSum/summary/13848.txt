Constitution of India, Article 226 - Penal rent - For retaining residential accommodation - Petitioner's husband went missing from 582000 - DDR was lodged - Police could not trace him and submitted an untraced report - Via order dated 182003, petitioner instructed to vacate residential house on 14.9.2003 - Retrospectively forfeited the allotment from 8.6.2001 following order dated 13.8.2003 - Petitioner ordered to pay licence fee at penal rent of Rs. 5000/- per month from 8.6.2001 until she vacates the house - Court ruled it is unfair to impose penal rent retrospectively - Ordered the respondents to charge licence fee based on order dated 182003 and make a suitable decision about licence fee from 1592003 to 15102004.
