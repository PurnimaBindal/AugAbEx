According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 and Govt. of India Act, 1935, Section 241(2)(b), along with Punjab Services (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1940, Govt. employees are entitled to full reimbursement of medical expenditures.
However, the circular dt. 29.11.1989, issued by Deptt.
of Health and Family Welfare, Govt. of Punjab, limiting the cost of Intra-Occular lens to a maximum of Rs. 800/- once in life time for each eye for a Govt. employee, is considered violative of Art. 14 & 21 of the Constitution of India.
The decision in Renu Saigal v. State of Haryana, 1998(4) SCT 565 has been followed.
[Paras 14, 15 & 16]
