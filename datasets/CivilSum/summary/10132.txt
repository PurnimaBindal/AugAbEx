Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Arrears of Pay - Interest - Corporation found liable to pay interest at the rate of 7% pa on arrears of pay due to the deceased employee, as there was sufficient time to release the arrears of pay either in favor of the employee himself or in favor of his legal representatives.
Furthermore, the claim of the deceased employee regarding the release of arrears of pay is deemed to have attained finality and can be deposited in a bank to earn interest.
