Pharmacy Act, 1948, Section 34 - State Pharmacy Council Rules, 1951, Rule 74 - Selection - Pharmacist - The petitioner was not called for an interview on the basis of being deemed ineligible due to not being a registered pharmacist, despite having paid for renewal before the registration expiry date.
The commission did not provide notice of doubt to the petitioner's eligibility nor an opportunity to present supporting documentation.
As a result, the commission's decision was deemed arbitrary and the petitioner is to be interviewed.
