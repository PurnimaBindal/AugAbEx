Constitution of India, Articles 21, 14 and 16 - Punjab Govt. instructions dt. 2571991 and 2981995 - Excluding Central Government employees and Army personnel, all employees including bank employees and their dependents are eligible to receive ex-gratia relief for terrorist affected persons under the instructions - Dependents of an employee should not be denied compensation in comparison to the dependents of an unemployed person - An employee or their dependents may receive little benefit depending upon the service conditions and length of service but that cannot be a substitute for the ex-gratia compensation payable by the Govt. - The instructions do not exclude any other persons/employees - Court cannot infer a bar regarding payment to the family of a person who is in service.

Constitution of India, Articles 226, 21, 14 and 16 - Punjab Govt. instructions dt. 2571991 and 29.8.1995 - Undue delay cannot bar the claim of ex-gratia compensation - The only effect of delay is that the Govt. utilised the amount for such a period depriving off the person entitled to it.
