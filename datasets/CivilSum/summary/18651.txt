Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25FFF(1) - Retrenchment - Principle of last come first go - Winding up of corporation - Seniority or juniority is not relevant when retaining a few employees to carry out the winding up process - Corporation is allowed to make use of employees with different work knowledge regardless of their original cadre - Last come first go principle is not applicable.
[Paras 10 to 12]
