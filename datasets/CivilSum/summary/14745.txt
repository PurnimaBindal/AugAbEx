Punjab Cooperative Societies Act, 1961, Sections 55 and 56 - Reference/Jurisdiction - Additional Registrars of Cooperative Societies can decide references made to the Registrar under Section 55 and 56, as they have been given all the powers of the Registrar.
[Para 3]
Punjab Cooperative Societies Act, 1961, Sections 55 and 56 - Maintainability of proceedings - If a party disputes the maintainability of proceedings and does not challenge it as a preliminary objection, they cannot challenge the final order on the basis of maintainability.
The principle of estoppel applies.
[Para 5]
Punjab Cooperative Societies Act, 1961, Sections 68 and 69 - Delay in filing Revision and Appeal - If an appeal or revision is filed after a long unexplained delay and there is no sufficient cause for the delay, it can be rejected on the ground of limitation.
[Para 7]
Punjab Cooperative Societies Act, 1961, Sections 55 and 56 - Amendment of claim/Estoppel - If an amendment or addition to a claim is allowed and not opposed or challenged, the final order cannot be challenged on the basis that the amendment or addition should not have been allowed.
[Para 6]
