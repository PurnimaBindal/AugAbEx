The Constitution of India, Article 16 stipulates that absorption of casual workers into regular Class IV service is allowed.
However, the University cannot terminate the services of petitioners who have completed more than 240 days of continuous service without conducting an enquiry.
The University can still choose not to absorb petitioners who are found unsuitable on merit.
After absorption, the University can terminate the services of any employee, but only after proper enquiry.
