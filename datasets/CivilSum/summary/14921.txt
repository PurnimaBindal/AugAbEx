Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education/Unfair Means - Unfair means in exams is a social evil that should be stopped.
Academic bodies need to adhere to Regulations.
[Para 4] B Panjab University Calendar Vol. II, 1988, Regulations 4 & 5 - Unfair Means/Examination/Education - To impose a penalty, the paper found must be of help to the student.
[Para 5] C. Constitution of India, Article 226 - Panjab University Calendar Vol. II, 1988, Regulations 4 & 5 - Unfair Means/Examination/Education - A paper found in possession of a student was not found to be of any help and the student performed well even after being given a new answer book.
In the interest of justice, the student should not be punished.
