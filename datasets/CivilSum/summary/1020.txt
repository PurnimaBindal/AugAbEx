Constitution of India, Article 226 - MDU Ordinance Concerning Revaluation of Answer Books, Clause 42 - Validity - The clause deprives the benefit of re-valuation for determining merit, distinction, and awarding of Gold Medal.
The Court has held that the clause nullifies the benefit of re-valuation and has no connection with the object sought to be achieved.
It is therefore considered arbitrary, unreasonable, oppressive, and violative of the equality clause of the Constitution.
