Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 310 - Resignation - Withdrawal of resignation - Acceptance - Communication of acceptance - Communication of acceptance to higher authorities - The resignation is considered effective from the date of acceptance indicated, not from the date of communication to the employer or approval from higher authority, for resignations that are prospective in nature - The order of acceptance need not be communicated to the resignor by the competent authority - It is prohibitied to withdraw a resignation after the date of acceptance indicated.
[Paras 15 & 19]
