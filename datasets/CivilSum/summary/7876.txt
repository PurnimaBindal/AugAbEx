Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 6-A - Contributory Superannuation Benefit Fund Scheme - The scheme is managed by the Employer Corporation and the employer is responsible for contributions.
According to the Memorandum of Understanding, only the employer can appoint trustees and the Chairman has power only by agreement.
The employer contributed Rs. 100/- per year to get the scheme approved under Income-tax Act, but it cannot be stated that the scheme was floated solely by the employer or jointly with employees.
