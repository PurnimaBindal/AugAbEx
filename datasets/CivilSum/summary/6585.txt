Punjab State Education, Class-III (School Cadre) Services Rules, 1988, Rules 3 and 20(2) as amended in 1988 and 1987 - Appointment as Master/Mistresses - Promotion as Masters from the clerical posts and class IV posts - The petitioners had asked to perform the duties of Masters while they held the posts of Senior Laboratory Attendants and gained long experience in such duties.
However, under the Rules, they were not eligible to be considered for appointment as Masters.
The court cannot issue a writ to enforce a right that goes against the Rules and any orders or instructions contrary to Rules shall be ignored.
[Paras 9 and 10]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Appointment - Eligibility - The petitioners, who worked as Senior Laboratory Attendants, had performed the duties of teachers for a long time, and some persons were regularised as teachers based on their experience.
However, such illegality cannot be allowed to continue by issuing a direction under Article 226 of the Constitution contrary to the Rules.
The petitioners were not eligible to be considered for appointment as teachers, and eligibility must be determined by the appropriate authority under the Rules or the Rule-making authority.
The court cannot issue such directions in the writ jurisdiction.
The petitions were dismissed.
