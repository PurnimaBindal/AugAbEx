Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Admission - Reservation - Petitioner opted for Computer/Electronics Engineering but was given admission to Chemical Engineering based on merit in CET - Completed First Year of her course - Claims belonging to Backward Area, and was deprived of admission to discipline of her choice due to respondent No. 3 not providing reservation for candidates belonging to Backward Area - Held that admission by respondent No. 3 was governed by its own brochure and not the one issued by PTU, and thus Petitioner cannot claim any reservation in Computer Engineering at respondent No. 3.
