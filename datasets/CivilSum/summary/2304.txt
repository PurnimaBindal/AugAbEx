The provisions of section 34(4) of the University Act require that selections and appointments be approved by competent authorities and audited.
An objection raised during a subsequent audit is not valid if the selection and appointment were previously cleared by the first audit.
The Director Local Funds Account does not have the authority to declare properly appointed candidates as unqualified.
[Paras 12, 14, 19, 23] B.
If a candidate is appointed without the necessary qualifications and there was no misrepresentation on their part, their services cannot be terminated for that reason alone.
[Paras 14, 15, 19] C. When terminating the services of a properly appointed employee, natural justice principles dictate that reasons must be recorded.
This ensures clarity and prevents arbitrary decisions.
An order to terminate an employee's services without recorded reasons violates the principles of natural justice.
[Para 12] D. If an employee was properly appointed and has gained sufficient experience, lack of formal qualifications may be less relevant in their termination.
