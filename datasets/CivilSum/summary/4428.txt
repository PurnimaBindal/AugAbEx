In regards to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, a question of whether experience should only be counted after acquiring necessary educational qualification for the post or if experience prior to it should also be considered is dependent on the advertisement of conditions and criteria of selection as well as those prescribed in the Rules.
If there is no exclusion of experience prior to higher educational qualification and the selected candidates have obtained such experience after acquiring that qualification, dismissing a candidate based solely on that ground or using it as a reasoning for the first time in court to revoke the appointment of esteemed candidates otherwise having high merit on all other counts is invalid, arbitrary and prejudiced, and ultimately violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
As a solution, the authority must evaluate the petitioners for appointment on the basis of their merit.
