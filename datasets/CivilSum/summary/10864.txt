Border Security Force Act 1968, Section 19(b) - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Notional Promotions - Petitioner seeks direction for notional promotions at various ranks with consequential benefits - Petitioner failed to pass Pre-Promotion Cadre test, but claims that he was prevented from taking the test due to exigencies of service - Petitioner had challenged an earlier order treating his absence period as 'Dies Non', but that petition was dismissed - This petition is also dismissed on similar grounds, as relief sought could have been sought in the earlier petition.
