The Bank of Thanjavur was amalgamated with Indian Bank and the petitioners' employees who died in service had claimed appointment in Indian Bank on compassionate grounds.
The Amalgamated Bank is bound by Section 18(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act to honour the Memorandum of Settlement between the management and the employees that was in force at the time of amalgamation.
This is also enforceable under Section 19(d) of the Specific Relief Act as a contractual obligation of the amalgamated Bank, contrary to the plea that the transferee Bank was not bound by the contract between the transferor Bank and its employees.
Thus, the petitioners are entitled to compassionate appointment, subject to all other conditions.
Exceptions to the general rule of unenforceability of the contract of service has been discussed.
