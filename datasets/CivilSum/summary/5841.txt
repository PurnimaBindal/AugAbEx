Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10, 12(3), and 18(3) - Industrial Dispute - Termination - Reference - The question at hand is whether an industrial dispute can be raised when there is a subsisting settlement - The workman made an illegal and without jurisdiction reference to the labour court, as the dispute was squarely covered by the binding settlement - [Paras 3, 6, 7 and 10] B.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 12(3), and 18(3) - Effect of a settlement on the workman - If the workman was in the employment of the executing authority when the settlement was made, it is binding - The workman's absence from the union or failure to sign the charter of demands that led to the establishment of the settlement is insignificant.
