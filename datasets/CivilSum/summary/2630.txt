Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Andhra Pradesh Public Employment (Recording & Alteration of Date of Birth) Rules, 1984, Rules 2(1)(5) - Alteration in date of birth - The date of birth is established based on school records or any evidence provided at the time of employment - No alteration of the date of birth on school records is allowed for any reason - The petition is dismissed.
