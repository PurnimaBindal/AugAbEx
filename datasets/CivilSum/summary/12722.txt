Constitution of India, Article 309 - UP Government Servants (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1999, Rule 4(1) - Service of charge sheet and order of suspension on Assistant Engineer of a Development Authority - Failure to arrest unauthorised construction and carelessness in discharge of duties do not count as misconduct warranting disciplinary action - Petitioner charged with encouraging unauthorised constructions - Charges against petitioner, although vague, do not have any infirmities - Transfer of petitioner out and suspension appear unjustified - Self Compounding Schemes have been introduced to regularise unauthorised constructions, and the petitioner may file an explanation - Enquiry must be completed within two months; if not, the petitioner stands reinstated.
