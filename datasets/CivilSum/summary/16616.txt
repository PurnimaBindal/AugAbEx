Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pensionary Benefits - Fixation of Pension - The petitioner claimed inclusion of his service at Allahabad University between 1958 and 1971 for pension purposes after retiring from JNU in 1992 - A subsequent employer can demand that pro-rata benefits from a previous employment be deposited before granting combined pension benefits - Pension calculations are dependent on the corpus created during employment - Therefore, it is not unreasonable for a subsequent employer to require pro-rata amounts to be deposited by a previous employer.
