Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission to Medical College - Claim to seat reserved for scheduled tribes candidates - A student who claimed to belong to the Scheduled Tribe of `Mahadeo Koli' was rejected as her father had recorded his caste as `Son Koli' in his school - The Scrutiny Committee relied on this entry and rejected the student's claim - Both "Mahadeo Koli" and "son koli" fall under "other Backward classes" - Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to a reserved seat.
