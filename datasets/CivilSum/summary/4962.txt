Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rules 3.17, 317A(g)(i), (ii) and (iii), (as in Haryana) - Option for pension from EPF - Notice of instructions as to option to pension must be given to the employee - Employee must exercise option in time - Claim for pension cannot be declined in absence of proof of notice - However, employee must deposit employer's contribution towards EPF with interest.
[Paras 6 & 8]
Work-charge service - Additional increments and higher standard pay scale granted after 8 and 18 years service respectively - Qualifying service - Period of work-charge service to be counted towards qualifying service for the purpose of grant of benefits - Subsequent revision of pay scales after qualifying for the benefit, is inconsequential.
[Paras 7 & 8]
Work-charge service - ACP scale - Regular service - Qualifying service - Period of work-charge service cannot be counted towards qualifying service for the purpose of grant of ACP scale - Policy requires computation of only regular service - If employee already reached above or equal to ACP scale on qualifying date, he is not entitled to any benefit of ACP scale.
