The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 226 and 227, address issues related to education, including the correction of mark-sheets.
To correct a mark-sheet, the petitioner had to resort to the extraordinary jurisdiction of the High Court, and during the pendency of the writ petition, the mark sheet was corrected to reflect a higher grade than previously shown.
Due to the careless attitude of the authorities, the respondents were required to pay an exemplary cost of Rs. 10,000/- to the petitioner as court costs, and disciplinary action was also to be taken against the responsible person.
