Special Services Bureau (Junior Executive) Service Rules, 1976, Rules 6, 7 and 10 - Seniority while on deputation - The service that an employee renders in their parent department prior to absorption on deputation cannot be disregarded when fixing their seniority in the deputation post.
Any rules, regulations or instructions that deprive employees of the benefit of service rendered in their parent department prior to being sent on deputation will be in violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
