Delhi Municipal Corporation Act (66 of 1957), Sections 98(c), 480(1) - Allotment of Government Residence (General Pool in Delhi) Rules, 1963, Rule 4 - Allotment of Municipal accommodation - The MCD made an illegal out of turn allotment of a Type V quarter to a respondent who was only eligible for a Type III.
The MCD's reasoning for the allotment was flawed and showed a disregard for the law.
The Rules provide for out of turn allotment to quarter one type below then entitled.
The original letter of allotment to the petitioner has been restored and the allotment of the respondent has been quashed.
