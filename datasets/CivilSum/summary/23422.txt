Civil Procedure Code, Section 9 - Res judicate/Limitation/Remand order - The Supreme Court remanded the appeal to be decided on merits after dismissing it on grounds of limitation, which was later allowed by the High Court.
However, the point of limitation is no longer in question for the appellant, as the earlier order on the point of limitation will be treated as res judicata.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 311 - Termination of service/Temporary employee/Terms of appointment - In case of temporary employees, their service can be terminated based on the order of appointment or relevant rules.
Such termination is not considered a punishment or visited with penal consequences.
Additionally, Article 311(2) of the Constitution does not apply to the University and therefore, the Court's findings that the University's order is violative of Article 311(2) are illegal.
The court may investigate the cause of termination and if it is punishment, the charges of misconduct must be enquired into.
