The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, and 141, uphold the binding effect of Supreme Court judgments on qualifications and employee pay scales.
If relief has already been granted to other employees in a similar circumstance decided by the Supreme Court, there is no reason to not grant the same relief to others.
Any High Court order that fails to acknowledge or reference the Supreme Court's prior rulings on the same question is invalid and must be disregarded.
