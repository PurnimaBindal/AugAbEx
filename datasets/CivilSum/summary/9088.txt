Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation/Temporary appointment/Adhoc appointment - Right against the post - Appointments made without the approval of the Chief Minister cannot be regularised and legalised - A person who seeks to obtain a writ of mandamus must prove a statutory or public duty owed to him by the respondents and a corresponding right in himself - Those who have not been appointed by a Competent Authority or who have not followed the rules cannot receive a writ of mandamus allowing them to continue in their position until regular appointments are made or to regularise their service in that position - The rule required adhoc/temporary appointments to be made with the Chief Minister's prior approval in coordination.
