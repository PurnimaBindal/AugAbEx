The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, in Section 25(F), specifies that retirement can occur after completion of one year of service, meaning 240 days within a 12-month calendar year.
Upon calculation, it was found that the petitioner worked for 240 days up to the date of termination within the previous 12 months.
Despite the Labour Court's erroneous approach, the petitioner can still invoke Section 25(F) and receive all associated benefits, including reinstatement with full back wages.
