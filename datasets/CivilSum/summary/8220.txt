According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, and 226, as well as the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(p), any settlement made between an employer and employees that goes against statutory rules is invalid.
This means that a workman cannot demand promotion based on the fact that junior employees were promoted due to an illegal settlement.
Furthermore, if the statutory rules outline a specific method of promotion, it must be followed.
Thus, any award made by a Labour Court that goes against these rules must be overturned.
