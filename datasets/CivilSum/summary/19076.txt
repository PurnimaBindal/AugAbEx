Constitution of India, Article 14 - UP State Universities Act, 1973, Section 31 - Appointment - Cancellation of approval - Petitioner lacked necessary qualifications for post of Lecturer in Civics - Approval for appointment obtained through suppression of facts - Cancellation of approval was appropriate - Non-compliance of natural justice cannot negate cancellation, as it would be meaningless if opportunity was granted.
