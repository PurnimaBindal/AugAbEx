The Rajasthan (Regulation of Employment of Public Services and Rationalization of Staff) Act, 1999, Sections 9 and 11(c) - Regularization - The appellant, a daily wage Junior Engineer appointed by the Panchayat Samiti in 1986, was recommended for regularization but his services were terminated in 1989.
On the basis of an interim order obtained by the High Court, the appellant continued in service for 9 years until the dismissal of his petition, at which point the Rajasthan Legislature enacted an Act barring regularization and abating all pending proceedings.
The court recommended that the State Government consider the appellant's case in light of his past records.
