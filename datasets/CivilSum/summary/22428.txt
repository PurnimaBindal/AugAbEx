The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16 and 226, relate to Writ Jurisdiction.
A government that allowed a withdrawal of resignation after accepting it through the employee's application was left with the High Court's decision, which directed the withdrawal of the resignation and the employee's reinstatement.
However, such actions do not enable the right to claim parity or enforce a wrong order.
Thus, in these circumstances, the High Court was incorrect in ordering the respondent's reinstatement with all consequential benefits through mandamus.
Article 14 cannot be evoked in these circumstances.
