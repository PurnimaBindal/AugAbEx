Pay scales for Statistical Assistants working in different departments of Punjab Govt. were equated and maintained in subsequent revisions, until the Finance Department did not agree to equal pay scales for those in the Agriculture Department.
Once the Govt. decides to equate pay scale of certain posts with other posts, it cannot discriminate between the two at the time of subsequent revisions.
[Para 3]

Statistical Assistants allocated to different departments cannot be discriminated in the matter of pay scales only because they have different merits and are working in different departments of the Govt. [Para 4]
