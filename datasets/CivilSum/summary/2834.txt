Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rules 16, 170 - Retired employee - Enquiry against - Stoppage of 50% pension as punishment - Suatainability - Charge-sheet served after 3 years of retirement - Charge of deciding cases even after receiving transfer order - No pecuniary loss caused to State - Under Rule 170, punishment for grave misconduct - No standing order or circular prohibiting deciding cases after transfer - Misconduct at most minor - Full pension with arrears to be released.
[Paras 15, 18, 36, 38]  B Grave Misconduct - Definition of - Misconduct in office is defined as 'any unlawful behaviour by public officer in relation to duties of his office, willful in character' - Misconduct is a violation of definite law, carelessness or abuse of discretion and forbidden act - Misconduct is decided with reference to facts, situation in which act alleged was committed, and circumstances leading thereto.
[Paras 22, 23, 25] C. Grave Misconduct - Definition of - Dishonesty, corrupt practice, prejudicial to the interest of the State, involving serious loss, and knowingly making false statement in document pertaining to employment in the State is considered as grave misconduct.
[Paras 27, 28, 29] D Quasi-judicial officer - Delivering wrong judgments -Not considered as misbehaviour or misconduct - Delivering wrong judgments while discharging quasi-judicial functions is not considered misbehaviour or misconduct.
[Para 37] E Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary proceedings - Scope of interference - The High Court under Article 226 does not act as an appellate authority over actions of subordinate authorities or tribunals, unless the order imposing punishment suffers from manifest basic illegality and infirmity.
