As per Article 16 of the Constitution of India, if a government quarter is allotted but not utilized, penal rent can be imposed.
In the case at hand, the petitioner retired on 3111993 and his son, who was also in government service, applied for regularization of the allotment in his name or an alternative quarter.
Although ad hoc sanction of an alternative quarter was given in November 1994, neither the petitioner nor his son followed up and took possession, leading to the house being vacated on 16.12.1995.
As a result, the petitioner or his son are held liable to pay penal rent of Rs. 3060 after intimation of the ad hoc sanction, as well as license fee of Rs. 210 up to November 1994.
