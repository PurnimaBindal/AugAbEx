Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Denial of permission to take university examination due to shortage of attendance and delayed application for condonation of attendance - Petitioner suffered from mental seizure disorder and fell short of attendance by 3 percent - Despite suffering from serious ailment, she was not allowed to take the exam and was denied permission to apply for shortage of attendance - Court can interpret statute and gather legislative intention - Delay of two weeks in filing the application should have been excused - Petitioner permitted to appear in 3rd year BA Exam and directed to be given admission forthwith.
[Paras 29-31]
