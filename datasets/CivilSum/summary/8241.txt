Service law - Disciplinary proceedings - Natural justice - A charge memo was issued in 1997 for charges that occurred during the period of 1982 based on a vigilance report from 1993.
However, the delay in explaining this situation was not provided.
This unexplained delay is unacceptable and violates the principle of natural justice, which denies an individual the reasonable opportunity to defend themselves.
Consequently, the charge memo has been quashed.
