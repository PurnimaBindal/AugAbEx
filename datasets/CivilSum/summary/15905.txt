Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Qualifications - Eligibility - Promotion - An appointment through recommendation without considering the eligibility and qualifications prescribed in the Rules cannot be upheld when there are specifications in the eligibility conditions for both promotion and direct recruitment - Qualifications are not only meant for direct recruitment but also for promotions - Even if the person challenging the appointment is not eligible, the respondent cannot defend their illegal appointment and can still challenge the appointment of the petitioner independently.
