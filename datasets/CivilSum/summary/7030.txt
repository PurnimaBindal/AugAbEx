Industrial Disputes (Punjab) Rules, 1958, Rule 18 - Service of summons - According to Rule 18, service should be done personally or through registered post.
However, if neither is possible, the Labour Court may direct that a copy of the notice be affixed on a conspicuous part of the house or business.
The Labour Court cannot make presumption under Order XV Rule 5-A of the Civil Procedure Code, 1908, if it does not follow this mode of service.
