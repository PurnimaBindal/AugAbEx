The Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 refer to the situation where petitioners are seeking direction against respondents for declaration of their Matriculation Examination results.
The writ is justified since the respondents have failed to comply with principles of natural justice before withholding the results of petitioners.
The writ petition is allowed and the respondent is directed to declare the results of petitioners within 15 days.
[Paras 3 and 4]
