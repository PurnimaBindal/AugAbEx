Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compensation for mistake in result of HSSC examination - Petitioner declared failed in one subject due to mistake of evaluator, despite earning distinction in all other subjects - Board's insensitive attitude caused delay in scrutiny and subsequent denial of admission - Valuer expressing remorse - Petitioner experienced distress and mental shock, losing one academic year - Board held liable for languorous behavior and must pay compensation of Rs. 10,000/- - Valuer, giving due weightage to their feelings of regret, shall pay an additional compensation of Rs. 15,000/-.
[Paras 11, 12]
