According to Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India, alteration of date of birth is exceptional and can only be corrected with clinching and irrefutable evidence.
In the case of the petitioner, the department altered their date of birth based on a school certificate and the letter of the Head master.
Despite having three opportunities to rebut the material, the petitioner remained silent.
Normally, the date of birth as entered in a service book at the time of entry in service is not disturbed.
