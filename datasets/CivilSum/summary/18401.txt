Constitution of India, Article 14 - Claim for revision of pay scale - Surplus teachers absorbed in Government service after division into five categories - Pay revision granted to persons in other categories - The categorization has a direct nexus with cause as it is based on qualification, length of service, need of employment, and necessity of absorption - Therefore, the denial of pay scale to one category cannot be considered arbitrary or in violation of Article 14 of the Constitution.
