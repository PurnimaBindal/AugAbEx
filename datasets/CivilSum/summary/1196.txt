Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Absorption - Taking over of aided schools by the State Govt along with their staff - Other employees similarly situated working against unaided posts absorbed in Govt service - Petitioner alleged to have secured employment in the aided school fraudulently for back-door entry in Govt Service, but this allegation is unfounded since other teachers were also appointed who were absorbed in Govt service - Report of competent authority showed workload and post available, and was never rejected - Petitioner held entitled to be absorbed in Govt service and receive all consequential benefits.
[Paras 11 to 14]
