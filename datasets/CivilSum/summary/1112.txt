Constitution of India - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(j)(ii) - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965 - Compulsory Retirement - Public Interest - The Union of India retired the respondent from service through rule 56(j)(ii) - However, the Union failed to provide evidence for the Court to decide if the respondent's retirement was in public interest - Therefore, the impugned order of compulsory retirement was rightfully revoked by the lower Courts.
