Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - Revaluation of answer script - The petitioner was originally declared a fail because she did not meet the minimum mark requirement in one subject - However, after the court directed a revaluation, her marks were improved to 26 in that subject - Consequently, the Respondent-University was directed to declare her results based on the revalued marks and allow her to undergo compulsory rotatory internship - Additionally, the Respondent was ordered to refund the petitioner's examination fee and the Rs. 5000/- she paid for the answer script revaluation during the pendency of the writ petition.
