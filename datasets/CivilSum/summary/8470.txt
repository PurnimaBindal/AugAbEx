Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 4 - Payment of Gratuity (Central) Rules, 1972, Rule 7 - Gratuity - Interest cannot be claimed on delayed payment of gratuity if there is an alternative remedy available, such as approaching the Controlling Authority under the Act.
It is not within the Court's power to entertain a writ petition seeking interest unless it is clearly shown why the remedy under the Act cannot be availed of.
[Para 10]
Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 7(3-A) - Interest on delayed payment of Gratuity - Simple interest is mandatory on delayed payment of gratuity, however, the percentage of interest is not prescribed under the Act or Rules.
It is within the discretion of the Controlling Authority to award interest, subject to a maximum limit prescribed.
The Court cannot direct a specific rate of interest to be paid, as this power rests with the Controlling Authority.
The Court can only review the decision of the Authority in the matter of awarding interest.
[Para 11]
