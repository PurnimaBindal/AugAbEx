Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Reversion - The petitioner was promoted as Headmistress of a middle school despite being junior to the respondent, who was then posted as Headmistress of the same middle school and the petitioner was transferred to the position of Headmistress of a primary school.
The petitioner was granted the pay scale applicable to Head Mistress of a middle school.
As such, the petitioner cannot complain about being reverted to a lower post.
[Paras 7 to 9]
