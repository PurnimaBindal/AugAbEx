The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection/Advertisement - Vacancies - Recruitment in excess of the advertised posts is deemed illegal - The primary purpose for job postings is to ensure equal opportunity for all eligible applicants to compete - Appointing candidates beyond the notified vacancies denies and deprives constitutional rights under Articles 14 and 16 - Over-hiring appointments are quashed.
[Paras 5 to 9] B.
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection - Even candidates who participated but were not selected can challenge appointments made in excess of advertised positions - Such individuals have the right to compete for additional positions that were not advertised.
