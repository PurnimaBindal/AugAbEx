Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226, 311(2) - Departmental enquiry - Charges admitted - Departmental enquiry dropped - Enquiry report - Quantum of punishment - High Court interference - Evidence Act applicable to departmental enquiries - Petitioner charge sheeted - Evidence Act not strictly applicable to departmental proceedings - Admission of charges not considered a confession under criminal law - Application bearing the petitioner's signature is valid - Enquiry dropped based on the petitioner's admission - High Court cannot interfere with the punishment based on admission.
