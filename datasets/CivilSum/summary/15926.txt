Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Government Instructions dated July 23, 1957 - Pay scale - Qualifications - Petitioners working in privately managed Govt. aided Schools claim parity with teachers in Govt. Schools.
However, they are entitled to higher pay scale on acquiring higher qualifications in accordance with Govt. instructions issued by erstwhile State of Punjab which stated teacher on acquiring higher qualifications is entitled to higher pay scale - Respondents directed to release higher pay scale to petitioners w.e.f.
date of acquisition of higher qualifications in accordance with JT 1996(6) SC 710.
