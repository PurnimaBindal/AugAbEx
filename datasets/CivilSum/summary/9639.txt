Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 12 - An Order of stay was received in the office but was not brought to the attention of the contemner.
Once the contemner becomes aware of the order, the petitioner will be reinstated from the back date.
Contempt proceedings will be dropped and Rs. 300/- of cost will be paid to the petitioner by the respondents for unnecessary harassment caused to him.
