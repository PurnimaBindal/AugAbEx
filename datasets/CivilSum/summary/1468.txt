Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, Rule 173 - Entitlement Rules, 1982, Rules 17 and 27 - Disability pension - Claim of - Scope of power exercised by PCDA (P) - The PCDA (P) is limited in its power and would normally be bound by the findings of the medical board.
If the medical board has determined that a person should be invalided out of army service due to a disability attributable or aggravated by army service, the opinion will be forwarded to the commanding officer to pass appropriate orders.
If the commanding officer disagrees, they may refer the matter to an appellate board.
In the petitioner's case, the medical board approved their discharge due to noise trauma suffered during service, and the PCDA (P) had no jurisdiction to decline relief or sit over the opinion of the medical board.
The petitioner's claim for disability pension is allowed.
