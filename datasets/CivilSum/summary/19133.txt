Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Cooperative Sugar Mills Service Rules, 1991, Rules 33, 34 and 310 - Model Bye Laws of the Cooperative Sugar Mills Ltd, Batala, Bye Law 20(XXII) - Appointment - Regularisation - Competent authority - The committee empowered under the rules did not grant orders for regularisation or promotion as permanent Token Boys for the petitioners.
Additionally, the promotion order was illegal because eligible persons were not considered, and thus, the order of reversion was justified after proper notice.
The employer can revise illegal promotion orders by following the correct procedure.
Thus, the Order of Reversion was upheld.
