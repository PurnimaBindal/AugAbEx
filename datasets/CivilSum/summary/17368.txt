Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Part I, Rules 8.26(7)(i) & (8) and 833 (as applicable in Haryana) - Additional pension - Family pension - The definition of the "Special risk" must be read along with clause (8), which defines `violence' to get its actual import - Special risk means an assault or injury made or given to a Govt. employee while resisting or presenting him in the discharge of his duties to such risk - When the deceased husband of the petitioner went to arrest the accused in an FIR, the accused abused and assaulted him and snatched his service revolver and used it against him to murder him - He was killed in the course of discharge of his duties, which incurred special risk, leading to his death - Under Rule 833, his widow is entitled to a pension.
[Para 5] JUDGMENT H.S.
Bedi, J. (Oral) - Late A.S.I.
Mahender Singh, the petitioner's husband, was posted at the Police Station Gannaur, district Sonepat.
On 27th March, 1994, he was called to visit village Umedgarh in connection with a complaint.
As he tried to arrest the miscreants, he was abused by one of them and encircled by others.
One of the miscreants took out his service revolver and fired four shots at him, killing him on the spot.
The widow seeks a special risk pension.
According to Rule 826(7)(i), (8) read along with Rule 833 of Chapter VIII, Part-I, (Volume II) of Punjab Civil Services Rules, as applicable to Haryana, she is entitled to an additional pension.
The case of Head Constable Mohindner Singh is also cited to support her claim.
