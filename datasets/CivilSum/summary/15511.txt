AP State Electricity Board Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, Regulation 11 - Suspension - If an employee is facing criminal charges, they may be suspended during the proceedings.
The order of suspension is not flawed, but employees should not remain suspended for an unreasonable amount of time.
The appellant must regularly review suspensions as per regulations, and authorities must complete investigations as soon as possible.
[Paras 5 and 6]
