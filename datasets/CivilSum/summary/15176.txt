Constitution of India, Article 309 - UP Co-operative Societies Act, 1966, Section 122 - UP Co-operative Societies Employees Service Regulations, 1975, Regulations 85 and 95 - Retrial benefits - Petitioner did not receive balance amount of gratuity and ex gratia due to audit objections - No inquiry was conducted on the petitioner - Bank improperly withheld the retrial dues relying on circular and Office Memorandum issued by the Bank - It was determined that the Bank did not have the authority to issue or enforce such circulars or Office Memorandums - The Bank was ordered to pay all retrial dues and ex-gratia amounts with interest rate set at 6% pa [Paras 7 and 11]
