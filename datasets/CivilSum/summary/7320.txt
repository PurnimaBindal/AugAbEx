Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Remuneration - Whole time Director of the Company - The Trial Court declared that the plaintiff cannot receive a money decree as he cannot prove that he did his service for the company throughout August 1998 to July 1999.
However, evidence shows that the plaintiff functioned as Director, fitted to the company's Board of Directors, and was not disputed by defendants with regards to the remuneration or the amount received.
The plaintiff shall receive remuneration for the stated period with interest.
