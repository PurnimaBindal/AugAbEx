As per Regulations 173 and 14(b) of the Pension Regulations for the Army, 1961, constitutional disorder of the nervous system such as "neurosis" can be considered attributable or aggravated by military service, unless medical opinion suggests otherwise.
In this case, the petitioner was found medically fit at the time of enrollment, but later developed neurosis while serving in a forward area of Jammu and Kashmir.
Since neurosis is a species of psychoneurosis, which is a general term for nervous system disorders that can be caused by stress and strain, it is reasonable to attribute it to military service.
As a result, the petitioner is entitled to disability pension.
