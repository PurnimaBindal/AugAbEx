Constitution of India, Article 16 - University Regulations, Rule 8 Explanation - Seniority Expression 'foreign service' means service in the University by an employee who was not initially hired by the University.
The petitioner would not have been on 'foreign service' had he left the Government of India before joining the University.
The Regulations permit the permanent absorption of persons initially taken on foreign service, and such foreign service shall count for seniority.
The petitioner had already been given pro-rata retirement benefits by the Government of India for the period when he was on foreign service with the University.
Therefore, it is mandatory that the service rendered by the petitioner on foreign service must count towards his seniority.
The University is ordered to grant him all consequent benefits as a result of his being senior to the respondent.
