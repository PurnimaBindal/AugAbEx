Constitution of India, Article 226 - Public Interest Litigation - Revaluation of answer sheets - Allegations against the Vice Chancellor - A doctor and Research Scholar, the petitioner was aware of the regulations stating that answer books are destroyed within six months of the examination - A petitioner in public litigation must have clean hands, heart, mind, and objective - Filing a writ petition at a belated stage, after the distribution of answer sheets, is not permissible - The petitioner had no locus standi to file the petition at such a delayed stage.
