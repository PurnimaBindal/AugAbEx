Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Recognition as Headmaster - Petitioner was promoted as Headmaster before the school was recognized by the Education Board.
However, two years after recognition, another person was appointed as Headmaster.
The Petitioner's application was initially rejected by the High Court, and the Petitioner accepted the order.
Later, the same person was granted relief, and the Petitioner re-agitated the matter.
However, it is wholly inappropriate for this Court to re-open the matter merely on the ground that in some other matter filed at the behest of similarly situated persons, the Court granted relief.
[Paras 9 and 10]
