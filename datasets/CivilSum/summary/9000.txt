Jammu and Kashmir State Constitution, Section 45 - Jammu and Kashmir Government Business Rules, Rules 12 and 14 - Orders of the Governor/Government - The authentication of government orders by the empowered officer is distinct and separate from passing government orders - The empowered authority for authentication cannot take the place of the Government in making or executing orders - The existence of an order or instrument cannot be saved under the State Constitution's Section 45 if it's under question - Orders made by the Chief Secretary in violation of the set procedures cannot be considered as a government order and cannot substitute for one - Constitution of India, Article 166.
