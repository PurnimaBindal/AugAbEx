Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Discrimination - Absorption - The State absorbed all employees of Guru Nanak College with full benefits, except for the petitioner who was appointed as Principal and later treated as a senior lecturer - This was discriminatory and violated Articles 14 and 16 - The single judge ordered the petitioner to be retrospectively absorbed as Principal with all consequential benefits and entitled to interest on arrears of pay and allowances - The order was upheld without any illegality or error of jurisdiction.
