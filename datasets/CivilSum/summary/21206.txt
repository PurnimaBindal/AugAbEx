According to the Electricity Act, 1910 and the Indian Electricity (Uttar Pradesh Second Amendment) Ordinance, Sections 6-A(2) and 6-A(3), the license of the company was revoked under a memorandum of understanding and agreement with the board, which prohibited the hiring of employees with pending disciplinary proceedings.
The license was revoked prior to the ordinance taking effect, rendering Section 6-A(2) inapplicable.
As a result, sub-section 5 is not extended, meaning that the respondent employee was not considered a deemed employee by law.
