According to Sections 2(oo) and 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, in case of retrenchment, if the employer disputes the completion of 240 days by the workman, the burden of proof lies with the workman to prove that they have worked continuously for 240 days or more through positive oral and documentary evidence, rather than merely filing an affidavit.
