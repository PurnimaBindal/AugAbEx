Constitution of India, Article 311 - Enquiry Report - Punishment - The punishing authority has the discretion to award the punishment, irrespective of how the enquiry report was prepared - The recommendations made by the enquiry officer are not binding.
[Para 7]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Departmental enquiry - Evidence - Jurisdiction - The court cannot question the orders of the punishing authority if the enquiry was carried out fairly and the delinquent was given a full opportunity - The court cannot decide the punishment for serious charges involving acts of moral turpitude or unlawful acts that cause embarrassment to the employer - Petition dismissed.
