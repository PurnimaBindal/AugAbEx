The respondent, appointed on an ad hoc basis as a stop-gap arrangement, worked for eight years continuously without break and requested regularisation and a higher pay-scale under the UGC scheme for merit promotion of teachers working under post-partum programming, relying on a Government letter.
One condition of the letter was that the incumbent should have completed 8 years of continuous regular service.
However, the respondent was not regularly appointed, and the tribunal made a grave error by directing the authorities to provide the respondent with the benefits of the UGC merit promotion scheme, as well as to consider their case for regularisation.
