Constitution of India, Articles 136 and 309 - Punjab Excise and Taxation Department Subordinate Officers (Ministerial) Class III Service Rules, Rules 2(h), 3 and 10 - Seniority - Appellant joined office of Deputy Commissioner of Excise and Taxation on 4177 - Rule 10 determining seniority states that seniority shall be determined by date of continuous appointment in service - Past services rendered in earlier department cannot be counted for purpose of seniority - Therefore, any service rendered prior to 4177 in any other department cannot qualify for counting appellant's seniority in Office of Deputy Excise and Taxation Commission.
