Army Officer - Grading - Grading after completing course - Generally, grading of a candidate should be based on the recommendations of the officers under whom the candidate works during the course of training, with the exception that other specialists may provide their opinion.
Assigning a candidate for observation by an advisor or consultant who is not under whom the candidate studies is not a regular practice or rule.
[Paras 11 and 12]  B Army Act, Section 27 - Delay in considering representations - It is required that authorities dispose of employee representations promptly.
Delay in addressing the representation can create suspicion in the employees' minds about the authorities' fairness in handling this matter.
