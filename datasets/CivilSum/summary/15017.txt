Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 deals with public employment termination and reinstatement.
The workman was categorized as a daily wager, not being appointed following any rules and regulations.
Therefore, the burden is upon the workman to prove that he was not employed after termination.
The payment of back wages is not justified and the direction for the payment of 50% back wages is set aside.
