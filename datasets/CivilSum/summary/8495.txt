Constitution of India, Article 226 - Absorption - Petitioners formerly worked as Lecturers in a Government Engineering College that was later handed over to a society.
The apex Court directed that the petitioners could be offered positions in a Government Engineering College or its equivalent, but such equivalents must not be in polytechnical institutions.
However, the respondents disregarded the directive and absorbed the petitioners as Lecturers in Mechanical Engineering in Government polytechnic.
Petitioners applied for four posts of Temporary Engineer in irrigation and power department, revealing an advertisement published on behalf of PPSC.
Respondents were thus directed to consider the case of absorption of petitioner against the posts of Temporary Engineer in irrigation and power department.
As a result, the order passed by respondent absorbing petitioner in polytechnic college was quashed.
