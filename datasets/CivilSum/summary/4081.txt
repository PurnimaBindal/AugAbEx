Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, Section 36 - Exemption - Denial of exemption under Section 36 of the Payment of Bonus Act - The power under Section 36 of the Act is not a quasi-judicial power - The case of Amal Kumar Ghatak which states otherwise is no longer valid law - AIR 1997 SCW 1325 lays down the guidelines to be followed when considering an application for exemption under Section 36 - Rejecting an application without following proper procedure is not acceptable and the case is remanded.
