<P Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, Section 45 - Appointment on compassionate grounds - Right of applicant governed by scheme - Corporation providing appointment - Respondent applying for post of Attender on compassionate grounds - Corporation had issued a circular stating that there were no vacancies available - Although the Corporation had offered additional monetary benefits to the respondent, they were denied - The High Court directed the Corporation to create a post and appoint the respondent as an Attender within three months - However, such an order was held to be contrary to the existing scheme of the Corporation - The order was therefore quashed - Appeals were allowed.
