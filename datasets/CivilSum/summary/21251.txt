Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule II, Item 3 - Services of Bank employees on deputation - Termination of services after deputation - Upon absorption by absorbee Bank, managers of Primary Agricultural Co-operative Society no longer had substantive lien on their posts - The absorbee bank later terminated their services by cancelling orders of absorption - However, the Registrar Co-operative Societies gave directions for the absorption of the erstwhile managers into the Bank's service, subject to certain educational and other qualifications - While the employees have yet to be absorbed into the service of the Bank, it has been directed that they should be absorbed due to the long period over which they have functioned in the posts.
