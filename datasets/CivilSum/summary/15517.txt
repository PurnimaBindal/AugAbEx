Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Minimum Wages Act, 1948, Section 20(3) - Claim for payment of wages as per notification - Though petitioner-management cannot be blamed for delay in receiving notification - However, once the petitioner-management became aware that workmen were entitled to increased wages, they had a responsibility to pay the increased wages - The award directing the petitioner-management to make payment has no visible flaws - Therefore, there is no reason to interfere.
