Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Rajasthan Civil Services (CCA) Rules, 1958, Rule 16 - Fair and proper inquiry - Natural Justice - The order of punishment cannot be sustained if the inquiry is held in violation of mandatory Rule 16, non-supplying of vital documents and inquiry report as it amounts to the violation of principles of natural justice and miscarriage of justice.
