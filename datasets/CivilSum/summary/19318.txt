Northern India Canal and Drainage Act, 1874, Section 30-FF(2) - Change of water-course - Enquiry at the spot - Spot enquiry to be conducted by subordinate official/other functionaries - After a spot enquiry, Divisional Canal Officer concluded that change of water course was necessary for proper irrigation of a particular area - Parties concerned were given proper notice and hearing before the order was passed - The order was upheld by the appellate authority after a full opportunity was provided - There is no justification or scope for interference in writ jurisdiction.
