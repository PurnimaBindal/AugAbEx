The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Departmental enquiry/Fresh enquiry/Double jeopardy - After the enquiry officer found the petitioner not guilty of grave misconduct in charges of overstayal of leave and recommended only stoppage of one increment, the competent authority ordered the petitioner's reinstatement without making any final decision on the enquiry report.
However, when fresh material emerged that the petitioner misrepresented himself as a businessman instead of an employee to obtain a passport, a fresh enquiry could be conducted based on the new evidence.
[Para 10]
The Constitution of India, Article 311 - AP Revised Pension Rules, 1980, Rule 9(2) - Departmental proceedings/Retirement - If an employer orders the retirement of an employee while departmental proceedings are ongoing without prejudice to the employer's right to finalize the proceedings, it is not up to the employee to argue that no further action can be taken due to his retirement.
Rule 9(2) of the AP Revised Pension Rules will apply if punishment is to be imposed after retirement.
