Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - Extraneous considerations - Anonymous complaints - The petitioner's transfer order was based solely on anonymous complaints claiming the petitioner was misusing LTC and medical claim funds - however, these complaints were found to be baseless and motivated by individuals with a vested interest in the petitioner's removal from a sensitive position without valid cause - the order was therefore deemed to have been passed on extraneous considerations and stayed.
