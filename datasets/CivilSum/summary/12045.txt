Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Madhya Pradesh Fundamental Rules, Rule 26 - Madhya Pradesh Regularisation of Ad-hoc Appointment Rules, 1986 - Ad-hoc service - Benefit of increment - A lecturer appointed on ad-hoc basis on fixed pay, who happens to receive an amount equivalent to the initial pay in a time scale, is not entitled to claim seniority nor to receive benefits of pay-scale attached to the post while working in an ad-hoc capacity.
