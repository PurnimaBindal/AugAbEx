As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, it is the responsibility of the employer to determine whether an employee is eligible for promotion based on the Rules.
The Court can only direct the employer to consider the employee's case for promotion, leaving the employee to challenge the decision in an appropriate forum.
However, in this case, the Executing Court ordered the promotion of the plaintiff-decree holder instead of giving a direction to consider the case for promotion.
This order has been set aside, and the judgment debtor has been directed to consider the case for promotion from the due date based on the service rules.
If found eligible, promotion orders should be passed accordingly.
