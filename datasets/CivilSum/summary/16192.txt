Pension Regulations, Regulations 132 and 126(1) Qualifying service - If a person has served for over 15 years, was originally employed or re-appointed, and completed the qualifying service, then they are entitled to a pension - Even if their service period after re-appointment is declared as irregular, it cannot be excluded unless the rules and regulations specify otherwise - Any period in which they served and drew salary must be counted towards their qualifying service.
