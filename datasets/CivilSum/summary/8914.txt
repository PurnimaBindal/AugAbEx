Article 14 of the Constitution of India - Reservation of seats in Professional/Medical or Para Medical Course for "deserted woman" - A woman who obtained a divorce from her husband cannot claim to be a deserted woman and is not entitled to any marital obligations from her erstwhile husband after the divorce.

Article 14 of the Constitution of India - Sections 10 and 13 of the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 - Reservation of seats in Professional Educational College - A widow cannot claim to be a deserted woman, and a divorced woman cannot claim the status of a deserted woman after her marriage has been dissolved by a competent Court of law - There must be a subsistence of marital relationship for the plea of desertion to sustain.
