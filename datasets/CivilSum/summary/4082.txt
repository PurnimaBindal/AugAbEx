The GNDU prospectus for admission to Post-graduate Medical Courses allocated 60% of seats to eligible in-service candidates based on an entrance test, while the remaining 40% were open to both fresh graduates and in-service candidates.
Though the criteria initially required a minimum 50% marks to be called for interviews, this was waived later on.
The petitioners, who secured 50% marks, disputed these changes in criteria, asserting that they cannot be altered to the disadvantage of any candidate.
Court judgments have established that the prospectus carries the force of law and candidates are justified in seeking admission on its basis alone.
Any changes to selection criteria cannot withstand legal scrutiny.
Therefore, the court allowed the petition and instructed the respondent to admit students as per the original notification.
