The petitioner seeking appointment as a Social Studies Mistress against a reserved post for physically handicapped persons was denied the appointment on the ground that her medical certificate was not on the prescribed proforma.
However, the court held that the form of certificate could not be a ground for non-consideration of petitioner for appointment.
[Para 5]

The petitioner seeking appointment as a teacher, retrospectively against the post reserved for physically handicapped persons, was denied the appointment on the ground that her medical certificate was not in accordance with the instructions issued by the State of Punjab.
However, the Supreme Court set aside the decision to deny appointment to the petitioner, and she was appointed on a regular basis.
The court ordered that she shall be entitled to the benefit of seniority from the date when a person who applied later to her under the category of physically handicapped persons but was given appointment under this category before the petitioner came to be appointed.
However, she shall not be entitled to back wages as she gave a statement before the court that she would not claim back wages.
