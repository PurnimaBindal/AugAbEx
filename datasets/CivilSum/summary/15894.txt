The petitioner was promoted to the position of Head Master and performed his duties until retirement.
Without explanation, the promotion was revoked after retirement and the petitioner was not given a chance to defend himself.
This reversion is arbitrary, unfair, and cannot be supported.
[Para 5]

According to Punjab Civil Services Rule, Volume I, Part I, Rule 44, if an employee is appointed or given extra duties of higher responsibilities, they will receive an initial pay equal to the next higher stage of their previous post.
However, this rule does not apply to the petitioner who was not eligible for promotion to Head Master and held a higher pay scale because of their position as a lecturer.
The Court cannot assume that the duties of Head Master carry more significant responsibilities without sufficient evidence.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to a pay increase under Rule 44.
[Paras 6-8, 10]
