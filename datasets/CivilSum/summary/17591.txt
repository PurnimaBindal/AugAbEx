The stoppage of an employee at the Efficiency Bar on ground of unfitness does not amount to withholding increment of pay by way of a penalty according to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Article 226/227 of Constitution of India.
[Para 10]
Rule 4(v) of Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987 prescribed withholding of increment of pay as a penalty for stopping employees at the efficiency bar, but an explanation added to it provided that such stoppage of an employee on the ground of unfitness does not amount to punishment within the meaning of this explanation.
Therefore, stoppage at the Efficiency Bar does not constitute a penalty.
[Para 9]
Punjab Civil Services Rules, Article 226/227 of Constitution of India stated that employees who earn consistently good reports should be classified as 'good' and be permitted to cross efficiency Bar.
Those who secure at least 50% good reports should be classified as 'fair,' and unless the Head of Department is satisfied that they merit promotion and can handle heavier charges in the grade, they should not be permitted to cross the Bar.
Employees classified as 'poor' should not cross the bar.
Petitioner, who was stopped at the Efficiency Bar, admitted not meeting the criteria prescribed in these instructions, so the action taken was in strict conformity with the provision of Rule 4.8 and instructions issued by the Govt. [Para 7]
The decision to stop an employee at the Efficiency Bar due to their performance and record of service does not appear to be penal according to Article 226/227 of Constitution of India.
