In accordance with the Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act from 1971, Section 30(2), interim orders must be granted in order to support final relief - a court must come to a clear decision about unfair labour practices before it can use its power to provide interim relief - the decision to grant or not grant interim relief rests on the findings of the Court's inquiry - if the Court determines that unfair labor practices did not occur, it is no longer able to provide interim relief - the contested order has been voided.
