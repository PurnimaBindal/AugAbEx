The application of Rule 5(v) of the Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970 mandates a certain procedure be followed before a major punishment, such as stoppage of increments with cumulative effect, can be imposed.
Without following procedures under Rule 8, orders passed without notice, charge sheet, and hearing are not sustainable and violate natural justice.
[Paras 12 & 13] B.
The Limitation Act's Articles 5B and 113, in conjunction with Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rules 5(v) and 8, dictate that the time limit for challenging punishment orders communicated after due notice to the employee is three years.
After the limitation expiry, suits filed against such orders are not maintainable.
