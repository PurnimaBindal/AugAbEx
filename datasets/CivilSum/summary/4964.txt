Delhi Police Rules, 1980, Rules 15 & 16 - Departmental proceedings - Absence from duty - If the delinquent appears in departmental proceedings but does not provide any explanation for their absence from duty or any medical certificates, and no witnesses are cross-examined, it is not a violation of natural justice to take ex-parte action under Rule 16(3) - Natural justice may be excluded in special circumstances - Bias cannot be claimed against such action.
[Paras 24 to 32] B Delhi Police Rules, 1980, Rule 167 - Validity of Rule 16 - Ex-parte proceedings - Appeals can be made against orders passed under Rule 16 - If orders are based on no evidence or are perverse, they can be challenged and set aside - As long as there are safeguards like this, the Rule is not unconstitutional.
