Army Act, 1950, Section 191 - Army Rules, 1950, Rule 13(3), Item 1 (II), 1 (ixb) - Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, Part 1 - Grant of disability pension - When personnel in low medical category (permanent) are not given disability pension but are retained in public service, difficulties may arise in performing duties - If request for medical discharge is made, it is not considered voluntary discharge but discharge on medical grounds - Therefore, individual is entitled to disability pension in addition to full service pension - Denial of disability pension due to voluntary discharge before completion of service following retention considered improper.
