Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Essential qualification - Eligibility - Post of clerk in added Secondary School - The Government order states that knowledge of type writing is not mandatory for the post of clerk, but only a desirable qualification - The petitioner cannot be denied appointment on the basis of not possessing the desirable qualification - It cannot be argued that only those with both academic and type writing qualifications would be eligible for the post of clerk.
