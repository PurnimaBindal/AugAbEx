Constitution of India, Article 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Misconduct - Domestic enquiry - Dismissal - Reference - Evidence - After finding the domestic enquiry to be unfair and improper, the Labour Court scheduled arguments for the issue.
The appellant, being the employer, attempted to file an application to introduce additional objections and evidence concerning the misconduct of the respondent.
However, the Labour Court denied the application, stating that the plea to lead evidence in regards to the misconduct had not been made earlier but only after the order regarding domestic enquiry had been passed.
The order of the Labour Court was deemed valid.
