Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Discharge - Wilful absence from training - Stigma - The impugned order discharged the plaintiff for not being likely to become a good police officer, but this is not considered a stigma.
Rule 1221 aims to remove inefficient, indisciplined, and incompetent police personnel, and plaintiff's unauthorized absence during training means that the discharge order is legal and in accordance with the rule.
Court found no evidence that the discharge was a mere ruse.
