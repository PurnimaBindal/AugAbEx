Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 320 - Public Service Commission - Appointment - Selection for appointment to civil services in the State of Bihar - Bihar Public Service Commission conducts Combined Competitive (Main) Examination and follows Rules of procedure for centralised evaluation adopted by Commission in 1993 - A committee consisting of the Chairman and nominated Members possess power to appoint examiners - No evidence suggests that the Chairman acted in bad faith by reconsidering evaluation matter without convening meeting of Full Commission - Appointment of examiners is legally sound.
[Paras 17 and 21]
