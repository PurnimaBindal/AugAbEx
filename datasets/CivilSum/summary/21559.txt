General Insurance Business (Nationalisation) Act, 1972, Sections 16 and 17A - Framing of scheme for service conditions of the employees and subsequent amendments - Scheme of re-organisation of companies and rationalisation of service conditions of employees of all the nationalised companies was ongoing - Provident Fund contribution increased from 8% to 10%, with a ceiling on salaries, change in leave policy and retirement age reduced for new employees - Within the jurisdiction of Central Government - Notification of Scheme not ultra vires Section 16(1) of the Act or Articles 14, 16 and 19(1)(c) of the Constitution, unions still have the right to form.
[Paras 21, 22, 24 to 29]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 19 - Legislative delegation of powers - Excessive delegation rules - A liberal construction is given to a statute, its legislative policy and guidelines for its execution - statutes upheld to be valid but not carried to an extent of discovering a dormant or latent legislative policy to sustain an arbitrary power conferred on the executive - Legislature cannot delegate essential legislative functions - Legislature cannot delegate uncanalised and uncontrolled power, it must set limits and guidelines.
[Para 19]

General Insurance Business (Nationalisation) Act, 1972, Sections 16 and 17-A (as amended in 1973) - Retrospectivity of amendment - Vide proviso to Section 17-A(7)(2) amendments are made to the scheme framed in 1976 and 1977 - Amendment in the Act came into force w.e.f.
211973 - The Scheme for reorganisation of general insurance business was to commence from that date - Plea as to retrospective application of the amendment does not stand to reason.
