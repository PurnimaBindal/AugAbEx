Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Section 15 - Motor Transport Workers Act, 1961, Sections 25 and 37 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33- C(2) - Suspension - Wages payable - Computation of - Jurisdiction of Labour Court - According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, if there is any inconsistency between the provisions of the Motor Transport Workers Act incorporating the Payment of Wages Act and any other law, the provisions of the Motor Transport Workers Act would prevail.
The Claim under section 15 could be preferred by the person affected or any legal practitioner or any official of a registered trade union authorised in writing to act on behalf of the person affected or any Inspector appointed under Wages Act, or any person acting with the permission of the authorities appointed under Section 15.
On the other hand, an application under section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act can be moved only by the person affected himself.
The Labour Court did not have jurisdiction to entertain the application moved by the Respondent No. 2 in this case.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Section 15 - Jurisdiction - Labour Court or the Authority under the Payment of Wages Act is competent to return a finding as to what is the rate of suspension wage if such a rate is disputed.
But if there is a challenge to the existing right of the employer to suspend, the same shall have to be appropriately adjudicated upon only in a reference.
