Under the Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights & Full Participation) Act of 1995, Sections 2(i), 2(u) and 47, if an employee acquires low vision as a result of a work-related injury, this condition is considered a disability.
In the case where a conductor lost one eye during the course of his or her employment, the premature termination of his or her services is not allowed as it is covered by Section 47 and the employee's disability is covered by Section 2(i) of the Act.
