Constitution of India, Article 226 - CCS Rules 3(1) and 14 - Disciplinary proceedings - Habitual Failure - Punishment challenged for Disciplinary Authority's failure to recognize that a single incident could not warrant a charge - Ruling states that a Government servant lacks dedication to duty when they engage in one or a series of actions that harm the interest of the Government - Negligence, even if it is a one-time occurrence, can still be considered a charge.
