Section 10(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 grants powers to the appropriate Government to refer disputes, but must provide reasons for declining the request.
The appropriate Government cannot decide the disputed question of fact, only whether the dispute merits adjudication.
[Para 1]

When a worker is absent without permission for more than 8 days, Rule 9(b) of the Standing Orders deems them to have voluntarily left service.
Unless an appropriate court determines otherwise, the appropriate Government should refer the matter to an Industrial Tribunal for adjudication under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
