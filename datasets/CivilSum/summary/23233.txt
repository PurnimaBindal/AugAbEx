Rules 7, 9 and 18 of the Haryana Revenue (Group B) Service Rules, 1988, and Rule 6 of the Punjab Tehsildari Rules, 1932 - Challenge to the recruitment procedure by transfer of Ziledars - State government established a committee of three members to determine the merit and set the procedure for recruiting Ziledars, based on the determined ratio for sharing vacancies - Financial Commissioner accepted merit list and appointed candidates - Contention that seniority-cum-merit criteria should have been used, based on Rule 9(2), and not merit-based criteria - However, Rule 9(2) only refers to criteria for promotion, and the method of recruitment for transfer from a different cadre is not seniority-cum-merit - The procedure followed by the committee, assessing inter se merit based on ACR's and an interview, was reasonable and rightly upheld by the High Court.
