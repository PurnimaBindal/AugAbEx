Constitution of India, Article 14 - Bihar Universities Act, Sections 4(14) and 35(3) - Affiliated college - Termination - Opportunity of hearing - The termination of appellant's services as a lecturer in an affiliated college by syndicate cannot be sustained as an enquiry was required to be held before arriving at the conclusion that the appointment was contrary to the provisions of the Act and no notice was given.
The finding has to be recorded before termination and the appellant must be granted an opportunity of hearing.
