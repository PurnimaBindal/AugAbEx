Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Military service benefits - The petitioners joined the Indian Army in 1968 after passing their MBBS under a scheme sponsored by the Central Government and promoted by the Punjab Government to attract young men to the Army due to a shortage of doctors and engineers in the Armed Forces.
Under this scheme, the petitioners were promised civil adjustment after discharge from the Army, and to be treated in service with all the benefits of seniority.
The petitioners cannot be deprived of the promised benefits of seniority and other benefits arising from it.
