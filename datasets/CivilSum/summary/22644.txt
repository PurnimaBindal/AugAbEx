Article 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India pertain to service law and public appointments.
In a case involving the appointment of respondents claiming to be members of the Scheduled Tribe 'Konda Reddy', the District Level Committee later found that the respondents did not actually belong to the community and terminated their services.
However, the formation of the District Level Committee was deemed illegal by the Supreme Court and the High Court ordered the respondents to be reinstated, with the direction for the Government to establish a new committee to determine the matter.
This ruling was based on the precedent set by (1997)5 SCC 437.
