Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo)(bb) - Award - Termination - The petitioner served for 240 days in a calendar year - The management admitted regularization of juniors and non-payment of compensation - Petitioner previously reinstated - According to Section 2(oo)(bb), the management did not follow the provisions - Therefore, petitioner is entitled to be reinstated with 50% back wages.
