The petitioner seeking promotion to the post of lecturer in Art Education, who has the necessary qualifications, is protected under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
However, since no individual lower in rank than the petitioner has yet been promoted, the petitioner's rights have not been violated.
While the writ of Mandamus cannot be used to compel the respondents to fill the vacancy, the state government is ordered to do so as soon as possible.
