Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(00)(bb) and 25-F - Termination of services/Retrenchment/Punishment - The termination of services for absence from duty must comply with the applicable standing order 26(8).
Termination cannot be considered automatic under Section 2(00) and Section 25-F must be complied with.
Since it was a punitive action, proper procedure and natural justice must have been followed before passing the order.
