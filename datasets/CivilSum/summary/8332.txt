Recruitment Rules must not be kept in abeyance for the purpose of any ad hoc/temporary recruitment, as per the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16.
[Para 20]
Even if a temporary employee, one cannot be replaced by another temporary employee as per the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16.
The service of a temporary employee cannot be terminated without giving a reasonable opportunity of being heard.
