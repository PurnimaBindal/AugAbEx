According to Sections 10(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a reference must be made within a reasonable time and delayed claims will not be accepted by the appropriate government.
Although there is no set formula for determining this time limit, if a lapse of 4-5 years occurs, the workman will lose their remedy under the Act.
It is up to the Court to decide whether the delay is justified.
