Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary authority - Delegation of power - Disciplinary action - Specific delegation is required for the power of taking disciplinary action and acting as a punishing authority - Delegation cannot be inferred or presumed - Manager authorized only as Drawing and Disbursing Officer - Manager cannot assume power to take disciplinary action as punishing authority - Order passed by the Manager as punishing authority is null and void as there was no jurisdiction - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, Rules 7 and 8.
