Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 8 - Punishment - Minor penalty - The petitioner, who was working as Superintendent, failed to file a reply within the prescribed time in a petition filed against the State.
The reply was eventually filed after 15 months and as a result, a cost of Rs. 5,500/- was imposed by the Court on the respondent - State, which was to be recovered from the erring officer.
The punishment received by the petitioner is deemed reasonable and no interference is called for.
[Para 5]
