Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Part-I, Chapter II, Rules 48(a) and Rule 173 - Entitlement Rules for Casualty Pensionary Awards, 1982, Appendix II, Rules 4 to 8 - Disability pension - Cervical Spondylosis aggravated by military services - If a disease due to disability causes an officer to be invalided out of service, it is deemed attributable to service, unless there is some note to that effect in the findings of Medical Board recorded at the time of his recruitment - If the condition was found to be aggravated due to cold weather, and the medical advice of not posting the officer in cold high altitudes was ignored, it cannot be said that the condition was not attributable to service to disentitle the officer from disability pension - To attribute aggravation of disease to service, only a casual connection between the service conditions and the disease is sufficient.
