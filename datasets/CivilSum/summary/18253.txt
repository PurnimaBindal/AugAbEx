Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - SRO 161 of 1995, Rule 8 - Constitution of J and K, Section 124 - Interview - Recruitment - A petitioner who did not pass the General English section of the Civil Service main examination was initially not called for an interview, but a note in the Appendix notes that General English marks do not count towards ranking and only serve as a qualifying criterion, therefore the petitioner was deemed eligible for interview.
