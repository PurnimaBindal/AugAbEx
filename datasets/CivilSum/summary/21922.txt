The Railway Servants (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1968, Rule 10(3) - Disciplinary Proceedings - When an inquiry officer submits their findings and report to the competent authority, a direction to clear the findings on each charge separately cannot be considered a directive under Rule 10(3).
Without recording reasons for disagreement if the authority disagrees with the findings and a chance for the delinquent to be heard, the inquiry officer cannot hold a fresh inquiry and file different findings.
Nonetheless, punishment for misconduct such as termination that depends on new findings and a new Inquiry Report cannot be supported.

Judicial Review - Natural Justice - If an act of misconduct leads to an employee losing their means of livelihood, the Rules laid down must be followed carefully - If a statutory authority uses its authority in manners outside of the statute or makes an order without the application of reason, judicial review would be warranted - A mistake of fact may prompt the principles of judicial review depending on sufficient reasons.
