Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate appointment - The respondents refused to grant the petitioner a compassionate appointment on the grounds that they did not have an existing policy for such appointments.
However, the petitioner is the dependent of a deceased Armed Forces personnel, and according to Rules 2 and 5(3) of SRO 43 dated 2241994, CA should be given not only to the dependent(s) of a deceased employee who was in State service, but also to a dependent of a family member of a deceased member of Armed Force or paramilitary force.
As a result, the petitioner has been directed to be given a Compassionate Appointment.
