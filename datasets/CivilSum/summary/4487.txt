According to Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rules 244(1) and (5) and 179, an employee appointed on daily wages towards anticipated work is eligible to treat the services rendered by him as daily wager for computing qualifying service for pension.
The authority cannot accept a voluntary retirement application if the employee has not completed twenty years of qualifying service as per Rule 244(1).
However, if an employee has been permitted to retire under such provisions, estoppel operates against the respondents to take the plea that they have not completed the qualifying service.
Although an estoppel does not operate against law, if the authority mistakenly accepted the application, the petitioner cannot use it as a plea.
The petition is successful, according to Paras 8, 10 and 11 of the text.
