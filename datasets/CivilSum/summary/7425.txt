Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 21 - Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of Deceased Govt. Employees Rules, 2003 - Purpose of Rules - Delay in hiring - Financial condition of family - Request for compassionate appointment cannot persist indefinitely - The aim is to assist the sorrowful family in overcoming the financial difficulties caused by the sudden death of its breadwinner - However, if the authority determines that the family's financial situation is such that it can manage without compassionate appointment or financial assistance provided by the Rules, it would be unconstitutional to grant public appointment as a matter of course and an alternative method of recruitment would be in violation of public policy.
