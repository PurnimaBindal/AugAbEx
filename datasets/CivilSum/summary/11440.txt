As per Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 19 and instructions dated 2761979, a person who retires voluntarily or resigns is entitled to pension.
However, Supreme Court judgment in Raj Kumar's case has clarified that those who retired after 1996 and have not received pension, will have to refund the benefits and report for service in accordance with circular dt. 17101998, failing which they will forfeit their pension.
Those who cannot meet this condition cannot claim the pension or allege arbitrariness.
[Para 4]
It is worth noting that there can be no estoppel against a statute or statutory instructions.
