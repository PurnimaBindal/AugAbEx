In accordance with Army Pension Regulations Regulation 173, those with disabilities attributable and aggravated in service are eligible for a disability pension.
A petitioner who was released from naval services on medical grounds due to epilepsy was denied a disability pension, claiming that the disease was not attributable or aggravated by military service.
However, a Division Bench has previously held that epilepsy can indeed be contracted during military service and is therefore an attributable condition.
The petitioner is thus entitled to be considered for a disability pension as a result.
[Paras 7 and 8]  B Under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the right to a pension is a recurring cause of action, so the petitioner may be entitled to a disability pension for up to 38 months before filing the writ petition.
Due to this, the petition cannot be dismissed on the grounds of delay.
