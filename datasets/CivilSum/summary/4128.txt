As per Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act, a teacher in a school is not classified as a workman based on the authority of the Apex Court in the case of Miss A. Sunderambal.
The status of an individual as a workman is decided in view of statutory provisions.
[Paras 7 and 8]

Whether the respondent school teacher is a workman or not based on section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act is a legal question and can be taken even for the first time in the writ petition if it involves no determination of disputed facts.
[Para 8]

Although there is no prescribed limitation for filing a writ petition, it should be filed within a reasonable time.
[Para 9]
