Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab Municipal General Rules 1979, Rules 7 and 8 - Bias - Allegations of bias in selection for post of Clerks, Peons etc in Municipal Council due to relationship between selected candidates and members of Selection Committee  - Rule 7 is based on rationale that in small places like the one in question, most people are related to one another - Challenge against appointments made not by those who were not selected, but those involved in approving the selection - Unless it is found that the candidates and members of Selection Committee were closely related as per Rule 7 and Rule 8 was not followed, allegations of bias alone are not sufficient to quash the selection.
