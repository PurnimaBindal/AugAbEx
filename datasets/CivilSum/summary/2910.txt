Industrial Disputes Act from 1947 under Section 10-B states that disputes and demands can be referred by the government in terms of essential services and supplies, and an interim order can be issued by the appropriate government.
Only the government has the power to refer the matter of interim relief and demands to the Tribunal while issuing an interim order is not mandatory.

As per Article 166 of the Indian Constitution and the Karnataka Government (Transaction of Business) Rules from 1977, the Chief Minister has administrative power including the ability to review any departmental files and come to a decision.
The Chief Minister can call for the files of any department.
