Constitution of India, Articles 311, 19(1)(a) and (c), 19(2) and 19(6) - Kerala State Small Industries Development Corporation Employees Service Rules, Rules 19 and 22 - Freedom of speech - Participation in Politics - Public interest - Article 19 imposes reasonable restrictions on the right to freedom of speech and expression - A rule not primarily designed to restrict freedom of speech, expression, or association, cannot be challenged as violative of article 19(1)(a) or 19(1)(c) - Rule 19 of Service Rules of Corporation prohibits acts detrimental to the interests or prestige of the employer - Such restrictions fall under the 'Public Order' clause of article 19(2) - The employer can establish a reasonable code that promotes discipline and efficiency - The appellant violated this code by writing a letter to the Governor, making baseless allegations against various officers of the corporation - His behavior was detrimental to the organization's proper functioning, internal discipline, and lowered its prestige - An order of dismissal under Rule 22 was thus justified.
