Admission into an eight-semester educational institution requires passing the first two academic years.
Clearing these years in the middle of the academic year still qualifies candidates for admission, fulfilling the purpose of the semester system.
The moment a candidate gains the qualification, they become eligible for admission.
[Paras 10 to 12]
In interpreting statutes, all words must be given their meanings, including the clause "eligible to resume his studies in the subsequent academic year in the semester in which withdrawal was effected."
Ignoring this clause would be incorrect.
[Para 12]
According to Jawaharlal Nehru Krishi Vishwa Vidayalya Regulations, candidates who acquire the necessary qualification during the middle of the academic year are entitled to admission, given the eligibility clause "to resume his studies in the subsequent academic year in the semester in which withdrawal was effected."
