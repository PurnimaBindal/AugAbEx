Limitation Act, Article 113 - Declaration against an order - If an order under Rule 1221 of Punjab Police Rules dismisses or discharges Plaintiff from service, the time limit for challenging the order is 3 years - Challenging an order beyond the time limit is not valid - Even a null and void order must be challenged within the limit - Filing a mercy appeal does not create fresh cause of action where no appeal or representation is provided in the Rules.
[Paras 4 and 5] B Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Appeal - No appeal or representation is provided against the order passed under Rule 1221 - Filing a non-statutory appeal or representation will not extend the time limit for challenging the order passed under Rule 1221 - Limitation Act, Article 113.
