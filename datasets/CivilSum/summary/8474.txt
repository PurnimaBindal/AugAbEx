Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(s) - Workman - Definition of - The 2nd respondent, employed as a Milk Procurement Assistant, cannot be categorized as working in a supervisory capacity as they merely supervised milk collection - thus, the Labour Court's decision to recognize the respondent as a workman was correct.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Removal - The petitioner's removal from service due to misconduct was deemed unjustified by the Labour Court, who reinstated the 2nd respondent-workman with continuity of services based on evidence presented to them - the 2nd respondent has since been reinstated for 14 years, making further enquiry unnecessary.
