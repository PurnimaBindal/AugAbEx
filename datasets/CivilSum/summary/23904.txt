Employees' State insurance Act, 1948, Sections 1(5) and 15 - Scope and applicability of the Act - An establishment's branches situated in different states will be covered under the Act if the state where the headquarters or main activity is situated has issued a notification covering it under the Act.
The Bombay Branch of Transport Corporation of India will be covered under the Act as it is an integral part of its activities, and the main establishment in Andhra Pradesh is covered by the Act.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 1(5) - Applicability and Scope of the Act - The Act is a beneficial piece of legislation intended to spread the network of the Act wide enough to cover all employees working for the main establishment even though stationed at different branches outside the State.
