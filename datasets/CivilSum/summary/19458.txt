Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Qualifications - Employers hold the right to define qualifications for a post and candidates who meet these requirements should be appointed - The selection committee chose unqualified candidates which invalidates the selection process - However, to serve the interests of justice, the competent authority should review the qualifications of all selected candidates and any doubts should be addressed - Anyone lacking the necessary qualifications must be removed.
