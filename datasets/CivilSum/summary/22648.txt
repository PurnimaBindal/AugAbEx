Punjab Civil Service, Rules, Rule 121 - With holding of annual increments - Department of Enquiry - An order of punishment was passed against the respondent by the Senior Superintendent of Police, Ferozepur which was legal and enforceable, despite the contention that the SSP had no authority to do so because the respondent's promotion was made by order of DIG.
The suit was dismissed.
[Paras 8 and 10]
Departmental Proceedings - Prejudice - The respondent was unable to show the relevance of certain log books/documents to the enquiry into the charges against him, and therefore did not suffer any prejudice due to their non-production.
The departmental proceedings were not vitiated by this.
