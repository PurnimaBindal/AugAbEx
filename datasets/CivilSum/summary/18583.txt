Territorial Army Act, 1948, Section 7(3) - Army Regulations, Regulation 290 - Pension - Petitioner seeks to be granted pension by including a period when they weren't fulfilling the conditions of Section 7(3) - It was decided that qualifying service is necessary for the grant of pensionary benefits, which means the period for which the petitioner was not working cannot be considered for their eligibility for the pension.
