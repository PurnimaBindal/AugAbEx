Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pension - Service rendered under other employer - A petitioner employed by a Panchayat Samiti but posted in a Gram Panchayat must not be denied the benefit of service rendered under the Gram Panchayat, which was granted to others who were physically working in the Panchayat Samitis.
[Paras 7, 8 and 10]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Panchayat Secretaries (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1979, Rules 14 and 15 - Government servant/Panchayat Samiti employees - Secretaries appointed in the Panchayat Samitis are Government servants - Their services rendered under any office have to be counted towards qualifying service for pension, even if they are liable to be transferred to another Panchayat Samiti, Panchayat or office of the DDPO [Paras 9 to 11]
