UP Recruitment of Service (determination of Date of Birth) Rules, 1974, Rule 2 - UP Recruitment of Service (determination of date of birth) (first Amendment) Rules, 1980 - Correction of date of birth - The Rules clearly state where corrections to the date of birth are allowed.
The date of birth recorded in the service book was mistakenly entered as 31.7.29 instead of 31739 by an employee of the department, and the respondent signed page at serial No. 8 whereas the entry relating to date of birth is at serial No. 5.
The current stand taken by the respondent is different from what was stated before the trial court and the first appellate court, and thus the date of birth of the respondent has to be taken for all purpose as 31.7.29 and not 31739.
[Paras 14, 15, 16 and 17]

Correction of Date of Birth - The correction of date of birth should not be dealt with by Courts, Tribunal or the High Court considering only the public servant concerned.
Any direction for correction has a chain relation and, unless a clear case is made out within a reasonable time as provided in Rules, the courts or Tribunal should not issue directions for correction of date of birth.
[Para 11]
