The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 deals with misconduct and termination of service.
The respondents are arguing that the action taken goes against the Standing Orders created by the Corporation under Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act.
The case is governed by the decision in Jitendra Nath Biswas v. Empire of India & Ceylon Tea Co & Anr. (1989(3) SCR 64), which held that the jurisdiction of the Civil Court is excluded.
Due to the restraint imposed by the Court order on 18101989, the matter has been referred to a panel of three Judges.
