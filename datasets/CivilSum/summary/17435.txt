Army Rules 1954, Rule 16-A - Age of Retirement - Acting Colonel - The petitioner Acting Colonel's claim to continue in service till the age of 57 is not justified under Rule 16-A - The Rule does not differentiate between Acting and substantive colonels - Age limits are not only applicable to substantive colonels - The Rule does not set different limits of age for those holding the rank of Colonel on an acting basis.
