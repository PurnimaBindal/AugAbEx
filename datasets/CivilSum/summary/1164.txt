Rajasthan Civil Services (Absorption of Surplus Personnel) Rules, 1969, Rule 15 - Seniority - Absorption - The seniority of petitioners, who were absorbed in the Transport Department as Sub-Inspectors from the Ground Water Board Department, must be determined in accordance with Rule 15 of 1969 Rules, by taking into account their service rendered in substantive capacity in their parent department.
[Paras 8 and 10]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Necessary parties - Non-joinder of necessary parties - When the State is the only necessary party for a petitioner who challenges the fixation of seniority as violative of Seniority Rule, then the State is the only party that must be joined.
If the interest of other parties who haven't been joined is being effectively represented, such non-joinder will not be fatal.
[Para 9] C Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pleadings - Facts and evidence - A petitioner alleging certain facts must plead and prove such facts in the writ petition, otherwise, the court will not entertain the point.
[Para 9] D. Constitution of India, Article 226 - High Court of Judicature for Rajasthan, Rules, 1952, Rule 376 - Counter claim - Respondents may make a counter claim in the writ petition, which will be in the nature of a writ petition under Article 226, but it will only be admitted and entertained if not rejected at the preliminary hearing.
A counter claim based on a cause of action from 10 years ago may be dismissed due to delay and laches.
