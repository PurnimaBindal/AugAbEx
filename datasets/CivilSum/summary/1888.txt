Constitution of India, Articles 45, 46 and 226 - Meghalaya School Education Act, 1981, Sections 4 and 31 - Meghalaya Interpretation and General Clauses Act, 1972, Section 36 - Tuition fees - Public schools - The right to education has limitations based on the economic capacity of the State and its development - When fixing the fees of public schools, the government must consider Constitutional obligations and ensure that fees are affordable for weaker sections of society, including the schedule caste and schedule tribes - The government shall take into account the objections or suggestions given by parents or guardians of students enrolled in public schools before the fixation of fees.
