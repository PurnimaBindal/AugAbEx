Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(a)(i), 2(a)(ii), 25(f) - Labour Court has upheld the reinstatement of a workman with continuity of service and full backwages, finding his termination to be illegal and unjustified.
However, the award was challenged, and it was established on record that there was non-compliance with Section 25(f).
Evidence showed that while the workman reported on duty, the management refused to accept him.
The court found the findings recorded ex facie to be based on record and held that there was no evidence to show that the workman was gainfully employed elsewhere after termination.
The award cannot be interfered with in terms of the order of reinstatement with continuity, but management was deprived of the services of the workman, so 50% backwages would suffice.
Accordingly, the petition is dismissed with the modification of the award.
