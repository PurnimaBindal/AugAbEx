Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work - After the merger of State Forest Development Corporation with Orissa Forest Development Corporation, petitioners were assigned the same post of Accounts Assistant, but were given a lower pay scale than their counterparts - Petitioners were found to be performing the same duties as other Accounts Assistants of OFDC - Court held that OFDC acted discriminatorily and unreasonably by treating petitioners Junior Clerks and allocating a lower pay scale than that of the Accounts Assistants (now redesignated as Junior Accountants) - The redesignation and transfer orders issued were solely for the purpose of creating an artificial distinction - The court deemed the impugned orders and actions as violative of the conditions of merger, arbitrary, discriminatory, and unsustainable in law.
