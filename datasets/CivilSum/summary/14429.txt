According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16(4), the petitioner, an SC candidate who was appointed as a Sweeper/Garderner, had their services regularised and was paid arrears of pay in the regular pay scale.
Although the petitioner's services were terminated due to the ban on fresh appointments, the ban was lifted for completing the backlog of SC candidates and the DIG Police, head of the Dept, recommended that the State Govt. regularise the services of employees belonging to the SC/ST category.
There was a policy in place to fill back-log vacancies, so the services of the petitioners could not be terminated and the termination was set aside.
