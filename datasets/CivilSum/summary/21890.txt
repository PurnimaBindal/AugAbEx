Madhya Pradesh Industrial Relations Act, 1960 - Constitution of India, Article 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Daily wage worker - Termination of service - Compliance of provisions of 1947 Act - Whether applicable - Respondent had claimed to be classified in the permanent category prior to termination - It was held that the respondent was not appointed against a clear vacancy in a permanent post, had not been given a ticket of a permanent employee, and working on daily wages alone would not entitle him to the status of a permanent employee - The policy of state regarding regularization does not apply to the respondent and is even unconstitutional being hit by Article 16 of the Constitution - In the interest of justice, the benefit already given to the respondent shall not be recovered and he will be paid Rs. 1,50,000 as compensation and costs for condoning massive delay in filing SLP.

Constitution of India, Article 12 - Illegal and irregular appointment - Distinction between - It was held that an appointment made in total disregard of the constitutional scheme and recruitment Rules framed by the employer, which is a State within the meaning of Article 12 of the Constitution, would be an illegal one.
However, where substantial compliance of the constitutional scheme and Rules have been made, the appointment may be irregular in the sense that some provisions of Rules might not have been strictly adhered to.
