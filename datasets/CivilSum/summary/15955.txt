Punjab State Federation of Co-operative Sugar Mills Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1981, Rule 1.9(b) Proviso - The proviso to Rule 1.9(b) states that those working in a higher pay scale will be deemed senior to those in a lower pay scale - Seniority within a particular pay scale will be determined based on qualifying service - This provision recognizes the difference in categories that existed prior to the creation of the common cadre and is not unjust or arbitrary - It is also not a violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution as it acknowledges the merit and qualifications of those in higher pay scales.
