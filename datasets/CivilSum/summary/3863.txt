According to Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol.
I (Part 1) Rule 4.13 (ii) and Rule 4.4.
(a)(i), JBT Teachers appointed as Head teachers on promotion and granted benefit of a promotional increment are entitled to it.
The revision of pay scales on January 1, 1996 does not justify the withdrawal of this benefit since Head teacher posts carry higher responsibilities.
The increment is linked to the performance of the function of the higher post with higher responsibilities, and cannot be withdrawn.
