Sections 10 and 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act were applied in a case regarding the admission policy for MBBS/BDS courses from Sports category.
While the matter had already been referred to Full Bench, the petitioner was allowed provisional admission.
Furthermore, due to the reference order, the petitioner was entitled to provisional admission.
The respondents could not benefit from their non-compliance with the court's direction and deny admission to the petitioner.
Therefore, the respondents agreed to give admission to the petitioner, resulting in the dismissal of the contempt and discharge of the rule.
