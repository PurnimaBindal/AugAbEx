Uttar Pradesh Regularisation of Ad hoc Promotions (on posts within the purview of the Public Service Commission) Rules, 1988 - Promotion of Junior Engineers to the post of Assistant Engineers - Ad hoc appointments made after lawful selection upheld - Non-appointed candidates to be deemed as appointed from the entitled date - Non-covered candidates to be considered for regularisation based on executive instructions - Selections to be appointed on a regular basis from their ad hoc appointment date - Service conditions to be newly set by the State Government for the Public Works Department's three branches.
