The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, specifically Section 25(F), states that the termination of daily waged employees must follow the same procedure and comply with the same conditions as those employed on regular wages.
The petitioner had worked for 240 days and was terminated under the provision of retrenchment.
However, this termination was not compliant with Section 25(F) of the Act.
When the matter was brought before the Lok Adalat, the Management offered to reinstate the petitioner, putting him on regular employment but without back wages.
This condition was not acceptable to the petitioner.
As a result, it has been determined that the petitioner was wrongly terminated from his employment and is therefore entitled to reinstatement with all attendant benefits.
