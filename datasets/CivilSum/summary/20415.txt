Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 309 - Karnataka Civil Services (General Recruitment) Rules, 1977, Rule 8 - Reservation for scheduled castes/Scheduled Tribes - The government, under proviso to Article 309, introduced provisos first and second for filling up reserved posts in the promotional quota effective from April 1978 retrospectively - The rules made under the proviso to Article 309 are legislative in character and cannot be sustained if made with retrospective effect is ex-faice illegal - The Tribunal's striking down of the rules on the ground that they are non-est until guidelines have been provided is incorrect as operation of rules does not depend on guidelines to be laid - Until guidelines under Article 16(4) of the Constitution have been issued by the State Government, the Second proviso will remain unworkable, and previous promotions will remain valid subject to the laying down of guidelines and working out of the backlog vacancies based on guidelines provided thereunder - The State Government must complete the process of issuing guidelines within three months.
