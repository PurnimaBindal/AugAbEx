Constitution of India, Article 226 - Voluntary retirement - Revision of pay scale - Petitioner, an employee of the respondent-Company, had opted for voluntary retirement under the scheme framed by the latter effective from 31.7.1995, but then a revision of pay scale occurred w.e.f.
1.1.1992 - The petitioner is entitled to the salary difference due to the revision of grade as well as terminal benefits accordingly - The respondent-Company's refusal to pay the arrears is inappropriate - It was ordered to pay the arrears within 2 months' time.
