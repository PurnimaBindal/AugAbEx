Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Financial Rules, volume-I, Part-I, Rule 10.15 - Penal interest on loan advance - Rule 10.15 does not specify a rate of interest - Even if the penal rate of interest was increased, if it was not included in the agreement between the parties and the prescribed rate was 4%, only 4% additional interest can be charged.
