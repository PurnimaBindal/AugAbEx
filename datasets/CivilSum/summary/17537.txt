Punjab Civil Services Volume II, Rules 511 and 616 - Invalid pension - Rule 511 is a unique Rule that supersedes the general Rule for pension on qualifying service of 10 years - If a specific matter is addressed in a separate Rule, it will take precedence over the application of a general Rule - Invalid pension is provided for those who become physically or mentally unfit to serve prior to the age of superannuation or qualifying service - If an employee retires after completing 10 years of qualifying service, they are entitled to pension under the general Rule and special Rule provisions do not apply - The petitioner retired before completing 10 years of qualifying service and is entitled to invalid pension under Rule 511.
