Constitution of India, Articles 14, 311 and 226 - Resignation - Disciplinary proceedings - The petitioner, a Lower Division Clerk in the HSEB, requested that the authority accept his resignation so he could file nomination papers for the Vidhan Sabha election.
However, due to serious embezzlement charges amounting to Rs. 1178 lacs and ongoing disciplinary proceedings, it is not in the public interest to accept his resignation.
As a result, the petition was dismissed.
[Paras 1 and 4]

Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Resignation - Acceptance of resignation - The petitioner submitted his resignation on the 26th and filed a writ petition on the 29th.
Despite holidays on the 27th and 28th, there was not enough time for the authorities to dispose of the request within a day as required by the petitioner.
The petitioner had known about the prospect of elections and had enough time to submit his resignation.
As the petitioner had been under suspension for 1-1/2 years, no direction can be given to the authorities on such short notice.
[Paras 5 and 6]
