Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 26 and 33-C (2) - Implementation of orders of the Labour Court by the Labour Commissioner - Despite filing an application with the Labour Commissioner for the payment of Rs. 1,43,000/- from Parbhat Finance and Investment Ltd to the workman, no action was taken - Subsequently, the High Court directed the Manager of the Bank, holding the company's bank account, to make the payment to the office of ALC who would then release the amount to the workman.
