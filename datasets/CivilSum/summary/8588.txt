Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Enquiry - Dismissal from Service - Violation of Natural justice - Service terminated without appointment of enquiry officer nor examination of alleged enquiry officer - Inspection report of Inspector and alleged admission recorded by workman cannot be considered evidence - Order of termination found to be contrary to principles of natural justice - The Labour Court's reinstatement order for the workman was confirmed.
