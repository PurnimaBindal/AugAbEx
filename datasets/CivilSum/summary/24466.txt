Sections 19, 20(1) and 21 of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 state that one cannot file an application under Section 19 without first exhausting remedies under the Rules and waiting for the prescribed period.
Section 21 of the Act provides the commencement and conclusion of the period for which an application under Section 19 can be filed.
The view of the Chandigarh Bench in Sital Singh's case was overruled and it was further observed, in line with the view taken by the Guwahati Bench, that the word "ordinary" denotes discretionary power that should be exercised in exceptional circumstances and not casually.
