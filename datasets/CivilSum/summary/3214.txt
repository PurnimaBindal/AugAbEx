Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 15 - Reservation for Scheduled Caste/ST candidates in MBBS/Post Graduate Courses in Medical Colleges - The Constitution permits reservation for members of Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe.
[Para 8] B Constitution of India, Articles 14, 15 and 16 - Reservation - In-service doctors in PCMS eligible for reservation - Government responsible for laying down eligibility criteria and sources of admission - Classification reasonable as long as sources are properly classified on a reasonable basis - Admission to post-graduate courses from different sources is reasonable - Fresh MBBS candidates are not discriminated against as they have not been deprived of becoming that class.
