According to Section 7-A of the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, the Provident Fund Authority can proceed on information given by the husband or partner of a firm.
Any action taken by the Provident Fund Authority cannot be challenged even if the person furnishing the information had no authority to do so.
There is no provision in the Employees' Provident Fund Act that requires the RPFC to take into consideration only such information as was furnished by the authorised representative of the employer.

B.
As per Section 6 of the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 and Paras 30 and 36-B of the Employees' Provident Funds Scheme, 1952, casual workers and employees engaged through a contractor must be included in the number of employees.
The Provident Fund Act does not make any distinction between casual and regular employees and instead includes employees engaged through contractors in connection with the work of the petitioner's establishment.
