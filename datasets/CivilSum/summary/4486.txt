Rule 49-A(1), (2) and (5) of the U.P.
(Civil Services Classification Control and Appeal) Rules, 1930 states that an employee who is detained in jail for more than 48 hours on a criminal charge is deemed suspended from that point of time.
No specific order needs to be passed to effectuate this.
However, an order of the competent authority placing the employee under suspension from that date cannot be considered as suspension from retrospective date.
Such an order only communicates the deemed suspension under Rule 49-A(2).
The competent authority can pass, modify and revoke such orders at any time.
[Para 3]

The Constitution of India, Articles 311, Proviso (a) and Article 51-A, and Rule 49-A of the U.P.
(Civil Services Classification Control and Appeal) Rules, 1930 govern the suspension of an employee on a criminal charge.
In a case where an employee is arrested and detained in jail on criminal offences like dowry death which amounts to moral turpitude, the competent authority rightfully refused to revoke the suspension order.
