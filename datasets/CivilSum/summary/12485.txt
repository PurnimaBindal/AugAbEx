The Constitution of India, Article 311 - The principle of double jeopardy does not apply to departmental proceedings and criminal cases involving the same charges - A constable is facing criminal charges under section 377 of the IPC, and departmental proceedings have also been initiated for the same misconduct - As no charge has been framed by the criminal court, it cannot be claimed that departmental proceedings cannot continue.
