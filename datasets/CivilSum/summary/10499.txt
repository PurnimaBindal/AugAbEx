Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Promotion - Conviction - If an employee who has been convicted by a Criminal Court and dismissed by the competent authority challenges the order of his dismissal from service and is successful, the competent authority shall consider him for promotion from the date his juniors were promoted - The court should not determine whether he is entitled to promotion or not - The competent authority has been directed to consider the appellant for promotion from the dates his juniors were promoted - The appeal has been allowed.
