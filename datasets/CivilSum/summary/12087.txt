Indian Bank (Employees) Pension Regulations, 1995, Regulations 17 and 18 - Indian Bank (Officers) Service Regulations, 1979, Chapter VII, Rule 37 - Central Civil Services Pension Rules, Rule 21 - Pension - Qualifying service - Period under suspension - The period under suspension pending enquiry into conduct will be counted as qualifying service where on conclusion of the enquiry, he is exonerated or the suspension is considered wholly unjustified.
