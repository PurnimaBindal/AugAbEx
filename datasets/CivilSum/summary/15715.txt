AP Executive Officers Disciplinary (Control and Appeal) Rules, 1977, Rule 5(1) - A.P.
Charitable and Hindu Religious Institutions and Endowments Act, 1966, Section 107 - Suspension - Executive Officers appointed before 1977 are subject to the suspension rule in Rule 5(1) pending investigation or enquiry on serious charges.
The Commissioner has the power to suspend an Executive Officer under Rule 6(1).
[Paras 4, 6, 7 and 9] B.
Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19(4) - Suspension - The Tribunal suspended the suspension order of an Executive Officer made by the Commissioner, but the Govt. again suspended the officer while the matter was still being investigated by the tribunal.
This action violated Sec. 19(4) and the tribunal's decision to uphold the Govt.'s order was deemed to be legally erroneous and set aside.
