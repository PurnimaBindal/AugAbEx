Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 2(9), 2(15) & 2(17) Health Insurance - Regional offices - Establishment - The principal and regional offices will be included as an Establishment if it's controlled by the principal employer for the Act's applicability and benefits - The control over employees by principal employer is the true test, and the test of predominant business activity or too remote connection are not relevant - The Act becomes applicable when there is a connection between the factory and the finished products that are sold or distributed in the regional offices or establishment and the principal employer has control over employees.
