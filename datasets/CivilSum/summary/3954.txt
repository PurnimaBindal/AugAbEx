The Employees' State Insurance Act of 1948, Sections 45-A, 45-B, 75, and 77, cover the recovery of contributions, including the limitation period for this action.
However, the act doesn't specify a period of limitation for recovering the contribution amount from an employer in default.
Also, it's important to note that the limitation period stipulated in Section 77 applies exclusively to filing an application before the ESI Court under Section 75.
