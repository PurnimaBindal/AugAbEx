Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Voluntary Retirement - An applicant for voluntary retirement under VRS of a bank can have their application rejected - If a serious complaint of financial irregularities committed by the petitioner is received during the pendency of such application, subsequent circumstances can be taken into consideration, even if eligibility conditions were fulfilled on the date of application - It is at the discretion of the bank to accept or reject an application - A mere application is not sufficient, its acceptance in writing and communication must be received - The order of rejection was held to be legal and within jurisdiction.
