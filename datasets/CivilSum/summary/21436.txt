Constitution of India, Article 14 - Death of Class I or Class II Officer - Member of deceased officer cannot receive equivalent post - Appointment may be made to Class II or Class IV in cases of economic crisis.
1989(4) SLR 327.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment in public service - Only open invitation of applications and merit-based appointments allowed - No other mode or consideration allowed.
[Para 2]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Death of employee in service - Family without means of livelihood - Mere death does not guarantee employment for family - Employment may be given in Class II or Class IV service but not higher.
[Paras 2-4]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Death of employee - Compassionate employment - Rules granting employment to family members in Class III and IV service are not discriminatory - This treatment is justified in preventing financial destitution.
