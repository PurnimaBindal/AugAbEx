Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation - The question of whether migrants from a particular state belonging to a Scheduled Caste would be eligible for benefits of reservation upon migrating to another state or Union Territory was not addressed in Chandigarh Administration's case 2004 (1) SCT 305 (SC).
The argument that allowing reservation benefits of the original Scheduled Castes of the Union Territory/State would harm the interests of those residing in that State/UT was not brought up.
The matter has been recommended to be presented before a three-judge bench.
