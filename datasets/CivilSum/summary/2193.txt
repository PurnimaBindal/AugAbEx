Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-J and 25-F - Retrenchment - Any government instructions that contradict the substantive provision of Section 25F of the Act, which states that the services of an employee who has completed 240 days of continuous service cannot be terminated without complying with said provision, are invalid.
Chapter V-A of the Act prevails over any other law, even government instructions.
