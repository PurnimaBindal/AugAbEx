Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25-F - Retrenchment - Any termination by an employer shall be considered retrenchment, regardless of the nature of appointment, unless it falls under Section 2(oo) Clause (a)(b)(bb), or (c) - If a temporary employee has worked for over 240 days, the termination of their service constitutes retrenchment, and Section 25-F must be complied with.
