According to Punjab Police Rules - Rule 12.3-A and Article 16 of the Constitution of India, if ASI Language Stenographers are appointed from the departmental candidates, then approval of SSS Board is not needed as per SSS Board letter No. 1/12/2/R-91/2918 dated 1191991.
[Para 3]
The petitioner was appointed as ASI Language Stenographer from the Departmental candidates under Punjab Police Rules - Rules 123-A and Article 16 of the Constitution of India.
Although the qualification for said post was Intermediate and 27 years age, the petitioner did not fulfil the said qualifications, but worked as ASI Language Stenographer for a long time.
The petitioner was not regularised as ASI and not promoted, even though junior to him was regularised and promoted.
The court directed that petitioner should also be regularised and promoted from the date his junior was regularised and promoted.
The court also issued directions that in case relaxation was required, the same should also be given.
[Paras 3 and 4]
According to Punjab Police Rule - Rules 12.3-A, the petitioner who has worked for a long time on the post of ASI Language Stenographer is entitled to be regularised as ASI [Para 3]
