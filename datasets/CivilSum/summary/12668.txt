Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 311 - MP Civil Service (Conduct) Rules, 1965, Rule 3 - Misconduct - Removal from service - Proportionality of punishment - The petitioner obtained four conductor drums from the area store without authorization, and out of those, he misappropriated two drums which resulted in financial loss to the Board.
The charges against the petitioner were proven in the enquiry.
The punishment of removal, in this case, was not considered disproportionate to the charges.
Therefore, no interference is called for in the punishment order.
