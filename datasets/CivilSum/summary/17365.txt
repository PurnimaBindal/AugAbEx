Constitution of India, Article 226 - Enhancement of injury pension - State Government clarifies that a minimum pension of Rs. 375/- per mensem must be given as of 1.1.86 - Petitioner and another person in similar situation receive same pension - Petitioner's pension reduced when claim is made - Court holds petitioner entitled to injury pension at rate of Rs. 375/- per mensem.
