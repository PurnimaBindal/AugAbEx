According to Article 226/227 of the Constitution of India, a petitioner was provisionally admitted to a BAMS course through an NRI seat, courtesy of an extension of time granted by the State Government, under the condition of University approval.
However, the University denied approval as the admissions process for the academic year had ended, and issued a letter stating that candidates admitted after the last date would not be regularized.
Nonetheless, following the payment of fees by the petitioner and an approval granted by the Principal, the petitioner attended classes with a roll number.
Finally, the University was directed to regularize the petitioner's admission and the petition was allowed.
