Bye-laws of Haryana Small Industries Export Corporation Service - Promotion - Merit-cum-seniority criterion for promotion to Deputy Superintendent from Assistant/Assistant Accounts in the Haryana State Small Industries & Export Corporation - Promotion must be given based on comparative merit of the candidates - If two persons are equally meritorious, the senior one must be promoted - Petitioners were seniors to those promoted and had a right to be considered - Merit can only be determined after considering all candidates - Promotion of a junior without considering seniors cannot stand.
