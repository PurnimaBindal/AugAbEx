A stenographer who fulfills the qualifications prescribed in Rule 3(1)(i) and (ii) will be considered for promotion to a higher post on clerical side under the A Stenographers and Assistants - Subordinate Service Rules.
The inter se seniority of assistants and stenographers with successful completion of the period prescribed in Rule 3(1) will be determined by the date of their continuous appointment as against post of stenographer or assistant.
If two officiating employees had been appointed on the same day, seniority shall be determined by higher pay, and if the rates of pay are equal, then the older shall be considered senior to younger.
The continuous officiation for the post of assistant will be the determining factor to fix inter se seniority.
If two employees holding the same scale of pay had been appointed on the same day, the older employee will be considered senior to the younger.
The appellant cannot scale over the respondents in seniority and the appeal is dismissed under the A Stenographers and Assistants - Subordinate Service Rules, Rules 3(1) and 3(2).
