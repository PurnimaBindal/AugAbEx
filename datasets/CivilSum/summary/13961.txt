Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Regularisation from back date - The petitioner worked on an adhoc basis from September 1976 onwards, and was appointed again on a temporary basis from March 1979.
The service of the petitioner was regularized from September 1982.
The petitioner seeks regularization from December 1979, but the respondents maintain that there was a gap of more than three months which could not be condoned.
The court held that the petitioner must file a detailed representation on the basis of the law laid down by the Full bench in Tek Chand's case, which covers the issue.
