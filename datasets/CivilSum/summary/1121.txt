Constitution of India's Article 226, Administrative Tribunals Act of 1985's Section 20, and Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules of 1985's Rule 23 state that a statutory remedy should be available for the relevant service rules.
Under Rule 23 of the CCS (CCA) Rules, any order passed under Rule 11 is subject to appeal, as Rules are statutory in nature.
In cases where a statutory remedy exists, CAT would negate an application and would instead ask the aggrieved party to take up the matter with the Appellate Authority.
