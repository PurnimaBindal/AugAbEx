According to Contempt of Court Act, Section 12 and Army Pension Regulations, Regulation 25, the court directed the government to pay pensionary benefits to the petitioner in accordance with the rules.
However, the payment was not made as the petitioner had less than 20 years of service which made him ineligible for pensionary benefits.
Though Contempt of Court was not made out, the government was directed to pay the pensionary benefits as the matter was belated and no inquiry could be conducted to determine whether the petitioner had completed 20 years of service.
The court clarified that these directions were only limited to the present case and should not be treated as a precedent.
