The Punjab Government Finance Department's instructions dated 25111988 state that proficiency step-ups exist.
Chapter 9 of the 3rd Punjab Pay Commission Report discusses the granting of increments at the stage of 8/18 years of service due to non-promotional opportunities.
The introduction of the first proficiency step-up intended to provide an increment to employees who have satisfactorily worked.
The second proficiency step-up aimed to grant one additional increment to employees who would have been promoted but could not be due to the lack of a vacancy, thus compensating them for their stagnation.
However, an individual cannot claim three increments at the stage of the second proficiency step-up.
In lieu of stagnation, they are only entitled to one extra increment.
