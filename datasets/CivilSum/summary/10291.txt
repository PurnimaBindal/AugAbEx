UP Intermediate Education Act (2 of 1921), Section 16-G(7) - Order of suspension - Disapproval - Non-speaking order - District Inspector of Schools must approve or disapprove suspension order in writing to prevent high-handed suspension of teachers or heads of institution - Any order stating suspension is contrary to Act must be reasoned and include explanation for decision - Failure to do so renders the order illegal and quashed - District Inspector of Schools directed to pass a new order in accordance with law within two months.
