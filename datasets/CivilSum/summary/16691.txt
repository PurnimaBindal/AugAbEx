According to Section 100(2)(v) of the Hindu Religious and Charitable Endowment Act, 1951, and Section 10(1)(d) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the government does not have the authority to refer a dispute between an employee and a temple governed by the provisions of the Hindu Religious and Charitable Endowment Act, 1951 to the Industrial Tribunal under Section 10(1)(d) of the Industrial Dispute Act.
