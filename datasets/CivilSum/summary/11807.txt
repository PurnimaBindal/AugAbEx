Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Conviction - Probation - A criminal case was registered against petitioner under Section 380/457 IPC while he was working at Food Storage Depot - Petitioner benefited from probation and was later convicted - An order dismissing him from service was passed while the conviction remained - However, the stigma of conviction was not obliterated - Departmental proceedings could result in the dismissal, removal, or reduction in rank of the delinquent due to the conduct that led to their criminal conviction - The Punishing Authority considered the petitioner's conduct leading to his conviction - His behavior was completely reprehensible, as he, a Watchman assigned to protect property, had committed theft himself - Deterrent punishment was necessary, and dismissing him from service was the only option - The petition was dismissed.
