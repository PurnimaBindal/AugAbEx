Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - Sports quota - MBBS Course - The petitioner was admitted to the course after verifying his documents and certificate produced under the sports quota, but a fifth respondent challenged the admission as having been made without enquiring into the genuineness of certificates.
The court directed a fresh enquiry, and the petitioner's priority changed from 12 to 35 on the basis of the proceedings.
A further enquiry was conducted on the representation of the fifth respondent, and a show cause notice for the cancellation of the admission was given but later set aside.
The Sports Authority is now free to conduct a fresh enquiry after notifying the petitioner and all sports bodies and authorities of the genuineness of the certificates produced by the petitioner.
[Paras 18, 21, 22, 23 and 24]
