Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo), 25F - Retrenchment - Employers are required to fulfill certain conditions to carry out retrenchment of a workman, such as providing written notice and compensating the workman appropriately.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Illegal termination - Employees who are subject to illegal termination are entitled to reinstatement as relief.
[Para 17]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Reinstatement - The grant of full back wages upon reinstatement is not automatic and several factors must be considered.
[Para 17]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Back wages - The question and quantum of back wages are influenced mainly by the illegality of the termination order, but other factors must also be considered.
[Para 19]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Reinstatement with 25% back wages - In some cases, a compensation of a certain amount may be granted in place of direction for reinstatement and back wages to avoid long-drawn litigation.
