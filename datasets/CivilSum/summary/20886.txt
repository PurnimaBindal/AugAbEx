According to Section 5(6) of the MP Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, the Single Member Bench has the power to decide cases related to the constitution of cadre and designation.
The Member is authorized by the Chairman to do so.
However, the Chairman should consider the nature of litigation when allotting work to a single member, whether judicial or administrative.
If the case involves a question of law and its interpretation, it should be decided by a Division Bench consisting of one Judicial Member.
