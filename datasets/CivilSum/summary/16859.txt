The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, along with the Jammu and Kashmir Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, Sections 34 and 35, prescribe that Service Rules must be approved by the State Government and published.
If previous sanction of the State Government is required, the Rule cannot be implemented unless approved and published.
Even if there is a defect in publication, it can be ignored.
[Paras 5 and 6]
