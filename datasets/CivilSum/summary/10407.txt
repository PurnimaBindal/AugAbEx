The Plaintiff was only seen failing in two papers on her June 1995 matriculation exam.
In September 1995, she retook these two papers, but was later informed that she had failed another subject.
Based on the certificate she received as a result of the exam, she was able to get admitted into 10+1 class, with the condition that she passes the compartment exam.
The First Appellate Court properly instructed the Board to consider her eligible for the exam and declare her result.
