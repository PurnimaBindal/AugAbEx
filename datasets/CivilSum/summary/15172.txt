The petitioner's claim for regularisation of services was rejected due to being a part-time employee and not recruited through an employment exchange, but the need for a full-time sweeper had been recommended in his case.
The respondents' argument that there was no post available for him was disputed since there was no specific qualification required for a sweeper except for satisfactory job performance.
Furthermore, being not appointed through employment exchange was not enough reason to deny the petitioner's right to regularisation of his service.
The court gave directions for the regularisation of the petitioner's services.
