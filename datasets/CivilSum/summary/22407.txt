Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo) (bb) - Termination of Service - Compensation in lieu of reinstatement - The order of termination must comply with the terms and conditions of service - The clause XIV of the 1st respondent's offer of appointment was considered as camouflage by both the Labour Court and the High Court - There are no grounds to interfere with the finding of fact - In the interest of justice, the appellants must pay compensation of Rs. 56000/- to the 1st respondent instead of reinstating them.
