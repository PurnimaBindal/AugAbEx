Regarding service conditions of employees, Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 defines the jurisdiction of Labour Court including ‘equal pay for equal work’.
A question can be referred to Labour Court relating to whether an Assistant Mechanic is entitled to the wages of a Mechanic if the Assistant Mechanic has been performing the duties of Mechanic.
It is the duty of Labour Court to adjudicate such question and pass an appropriate order if it is found that the Assistant Mechanics are performing the duties of Mechanics.
[Paras 1 to 3]
