Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume 2 (as applicable to the State of Haryana), Rule 3.12 - Family Pension Scheme, Appendix I Para 4 - Family Pension - Work charge employees do not constitute a category of permanent or temporary employees, thereby de barring the deceased employee's family members from receiving family pension entitlements.
