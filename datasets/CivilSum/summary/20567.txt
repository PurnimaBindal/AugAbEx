Constitution of India, Article 136 - Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 33-C(2) - Workman - Identification of workman - Labourers who complained about denial of work in FCI centres were engaged in loading and unloading - Despite three rounds of litigation, the matter was not resolved - The Court previously ordered FCI to identify and reinstate the workmen without providing any relief for back wages - FCI and the Tribunal had raised technical issues regarding the Evidence Act and evaluated the material provided by the workman with strict provisions of Evidence Act as direct proof - The Tribunal is not considered a Court, and thus, Evidence Act is not applicable to proceedings before the Tribunal - Only sufficient material is required to resolve the dispute instead of strict proof - FCI is ordered to reinstate the workmen previously identified with the help of responsible representatives of the Union of workmen and provide them back wages starting from the date of the earlier direction to identify and reinstate - The appeal of the workmen was accepted, and costs of Rs. 25,000/- were levied - The order should be complied with within three months.
