Contempt proceedings - Reinstatement - Consequential benefits - Reinstatement order does not mention consequential benefits - Consequential benefits cannot be claimed or granted in contempt proceedings.
[Paras 6 and 7]  B Contempt of Courts Act, Section 12 - Contempt proceedings - Contempt of Court proceedings are like criminal proceedings - Double jeopardy rule fully applies - Alleged contemner cannot be punished twice - Second contempt petition cannot be maintained if full compliance was admitted in earlier proceedings.
