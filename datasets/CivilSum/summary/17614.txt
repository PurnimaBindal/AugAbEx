Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 13.8 - Reversion - Natural justice - Promotions granted to individuals with distinction in sports - Petitioners' promotions were granted for their sports achievements and had served in their positions for a significant period - Reversion could not be done without a fair hearing - It was held that petitioners need to be given a hearing before being reverted.
