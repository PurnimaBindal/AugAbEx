Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Departmental enquiry/Dismissal - Denial of reasonable opportunity/Natural justice/Award/Enquiry Report - Failure to provide the charged official with the enquiry report results in the infringement of natural justice and reasonable opportunity to conclusive defend his case - Dismissal order cannot stand - Petitioner eligible to continuous employment with all related benefits - Labour Court verdict is reversed.
