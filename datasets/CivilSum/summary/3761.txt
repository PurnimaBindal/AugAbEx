The Karnataka Educational Institutions (Prohibition of Capitation Fee) Act, 1984, Section 14, and Karnataka Selection of Candidates for Admission to Teacher Certificate Higher Course and Bachelor of Education Course Rules 1999, Rules 2(m) and (o), 7(8), state that private educational institutions are not allowed to fill seats under government quota without explicit orders from the State Government.
