According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, there is a reservation for ex-servicemen and their children in admissions for education based on merit.
However, the petitioner was not considered for admission due to non-availability of ex-servicemen.
Although the petitioner is not the candidate highest in merit, he may still be entitled to be considered if no other candidate questions the institution's action.
Only the petitioner challenged the institution's actions, and thus he should be considered for the only available seat.
This is in accordance with AIR 1987 SC 169 and AIR 1993 Punjab and Haryana 17.
