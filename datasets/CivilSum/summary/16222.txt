Constitution of India, Article 16 - The petitioner enrolled in the JBT course of Punjab State in 1980 when the JBT Diploma of Punjab was acknowledged by Haryana State - After completing one year, the Haryana Government revoked the recognition of the Diploma from Punjab State.
The Haryana Government cannot decline to consider the petitioner's appointment as a JBT teacher because the petitioner passed the Punjab State-issued diploma, according to AIR 1987 SC 2027.
