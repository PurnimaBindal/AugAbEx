Sections 47 and 48 of the Madhya Pradesh Nagar Tatha Gram Niwesh Adhiniyam, 1973 state that promotions at the relevant time are to be made by the Town and Country Development Authority with some posts being filled by promotion.
However, the State Government has exercised power in making direct appointments which has been held to be invalid.
It is directed that the matter be reconsidered and the quota of promotion posts be filled as per the law prevailing at the time when the State Government exercises power.
