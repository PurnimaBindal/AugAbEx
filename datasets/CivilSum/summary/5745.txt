The doctrine of last come first go under Section 25-G of the Industrial Disputes Act has its roots in Article 14 of the Indian Constitution.
Senior employees cannot be retrenched before their junior counterparts.
When there are multiple interpretations of a statutory provision, the Court should choose the one that makes the provision constitutionally valid.
Termination of a regular employee with a notice of a specified period or pay in lieu thereof is not constitutional.
This is supported by Punjab Municipal Act, 1911, Section 45(1) and the Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-G. [Paras 3 to 8]
