Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 11-A - Disciplinary proceedings - The Labour Court confirmed imposed penalty of removal from service on a conductor of bus in APSRTC - The charges framed against the appellant were that he failed to issue correct luggage tickets and also failed to return change due to the passengers.
The Labour Court held that the workman misappropriated fares collected with fraudulent intention to defraud the Corporation of its revenues.
However, the court misunderstood the charges as there was nothing in the language of charge indicating any intention on part of the workman to misappropriate the amounts so collected nor was there any allegation that the under-issued tickets were found with the intention to defraud the Corporation of its revenues.
The findings of the Labour Court are erroneous and cannot be sustained in the eyes of law.
Allegations of irregular exercise of official functions do not constitute adequate foundation for a rational exercise of disciplinary power to visit the workman with the penalty of removal.
In the interest of justice, the workman is to be appointed afresh as a conductor but without backwages.
[Paras 4 to 8]
