Delhi School Education Act, Section 16 - Pre-Primary Education - Admissions in Pre-Primary education - Cut-off date and age of children - Administrative instructions and Statutory provisions - The admission process cut off date should be the day the admission process starts, not the end date - Delays in the admission process beyond the prescribed cut off date (1st of April) would not justify extending the cut off date - Any administrative instructions or orders allowing admission to children who were under age on the cut off date would violate statutory provisions - If there are any proposed amendments to the prescribed age of children, the matter should be referred to the appropriate statutory body, such as the Ganguly Committee, which is already considering similar matters.
[Paras 4, 9, 10, 13 and 26]
