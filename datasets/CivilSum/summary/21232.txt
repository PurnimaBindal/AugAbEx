Constitution of India, Article 14 - Civil Services Examination Rules, 1998, Rule 3 - Interview - Selection - The advertisement for number of vacancies is subject to change and may only give an approximate number.
The number of candidates called for interview after short listing will be in proportion to the changed number of vacancies.
Calling candidates beyond the fixed proportionate numbers will harm the cause of candidates within the already fixed proportionate numbers.
The authority must call proportionate candidates according to the ratio fixed based on the changed number of vacancies and exam merit.
[Paras 6 to 8]
