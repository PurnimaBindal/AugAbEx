Constitution of India, Article 226 - Quantum of punishment - Disciplinary proceedings - Misconduct - Fraud - Enquiry officer finds that the delinquent obtained forged medical bills for both himself and fellow employees, while the others only obtained forged bills for themselves and admitted their guilt - The punishment of dismissal from service given to the delinquent is not discriminatory, and the court cannot question the punishment given the findings of misconduct.
