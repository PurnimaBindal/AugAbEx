Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Qualification - Appointment for the post of teacher - The petitioner met the qualification requirements outlined in the rules and advertisement and was selected as number seven on the merit list - It was later discovered that the qualification requirements had been changed - The court established that candidates must be evaluated based on the current qualifications outlined in the rules or advertisement - Any changes to the qualifications during the selection process cannot unfairly impact the rights of candidates - Such changes must be implemented prospectively.
