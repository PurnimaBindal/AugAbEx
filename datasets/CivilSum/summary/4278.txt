New Bank of India (Officers) Service Regulations, 1982, Regulation 8(4) - Fitment - Constitution of India, Article 14 - Seniority - Interview - Discrimination - Managers in the Bank file a petition seeking directions from the respondents to call them for interview for filling posts of Middle Management Grade Scale III as they are senior to the persons who have been called for interview.
The policy and guidelines of fitment after nationalisation of the Bank have to be followed by the respondents without any random selection.
If persons junior to petitioners have been placed in MM-II earlier to the fitment of petitioners, action of respondents has to be dubbed as arbitrary, thus, violation of Article 14 of the Constitution Petitioners are directed to consider the case of petitioners for their fitment in MM- II w.e.f.
date the persons immediate junior to them were placed in said grade.
