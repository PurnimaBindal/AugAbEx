Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab State Agricultural Marketing Board (Class I) Service Rules, 1988, Rule 8 - Appointment - Post of Manager Marketing - The appointing authority is not allowed to fill the post of Manager Marketing through direct appointment, promotion, or transfer.
It is necessary for the authority to first consider the claims of departmental candidates for promotion, and to resort to other means of recruitment only when such candidates are not available.
