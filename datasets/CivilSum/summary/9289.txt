The term "dependent" in Bombay Civil Services Rules (1959), Rule 10(c) Commuted leave includes socially dependent as well.
Government Resolution No. LVE-1080/2251/P dated 25780 states that the definition of dependent includes economically, financially, socially or otherwise dependents.
Even if someone is not a family member but economically dependent on the employee, they may receive the benefit of commuted leave.
