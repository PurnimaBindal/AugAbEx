Constitution of India, Article 226 - Maharshi Dayanand University Calendar Vol. III Chapter VII Clause 7 - Promotion - The evaluation of merits made by experts during the promotion process cannot be re-evaluated by the writ court under Article 226 of the Constitution, and the decision making process and competence of the authority is the primary concern, not the merits of the ultimate decision.
Judicial review of administrative decision is only permissible in case of illegality, irregularity, and procedural impropriety.
[Paras 27 to 29]

Maharshi Dayanand University Calendar Vol. III Chapter VII Clause 7 - Assessment of research work - The designation of "eminent subject expert" only requires expertise in the subject concerned, rather than extensive eminence in the subject area/field.
[Paras 28 & 29]

Constitution of India, Article 14 & 16 - Maharshi Dayanand University Calendar Vol. III Chapter VII Clause 7 - Promotion - Bias - The test for bias is whether there is a genuine danger of bias, not just a mere apprehension of bias.
The decision cannot be declared biased and unsustainable based on fanciful allegations.
[Para 31]
