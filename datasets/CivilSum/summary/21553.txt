Constitution of India, Article 136 and 226 - Delay/laches - Respondents who were dismissed from their service in March 1967 - Filed Writ petitions in 1978, relying upon the same judgment that reinstated their similarly placed counterparts with full back wages - Writ petitions were filed in 1983 and 1985 - Tribunal granted the same relief - No valid explanation for delay and laches - No grounds to condone a delay and laches of 16/18 years - Reinstatment without back wages would have been proper - Respondents are not required to reimburse any part of the arrears of back wages they had earlier received.
