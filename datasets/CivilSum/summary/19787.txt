Punjab State Electricity Board Employees (Punishment and Appeal) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 9(4) Compulsory retirement/Show cause/Departmental proceedings/Amendment of Rules - The petitioner was subject to proceedings in 1962 which led to compulsory retirement on 1771985.
However, as per amended rules, a show cause notice was necessary, which was not given.
Although the amendment came after the penalty was imposed, it was still necessary to follow the new rules.
The order of compulsory retirement was quashed as the appeal was rejected without any explanation.
[Paras 3 to 5]
