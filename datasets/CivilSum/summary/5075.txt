Army Act, 1950, Sections 91(a) and (b), 93 - Period of police and judicial custody - Pay and allowances for - The respondents deducted the pay and allowances of the petitioner based on Section 91(a) and (b) of the Army Act for every day of his custody, after his conviction and suspension of sentence.
The court held that the respondents were authorized by the Act to do so and there was no issue with their actions.
Therefore, the petition was dismissed.
