Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Selection - Cancellation of - Natural justice - Post of Constable in CRPF - The selection process adopted by the Recruitment Board was flawed due to two major issues - candidates who had failed were declared passed, and the merit list was not prepared according to reservation rules.
This resulted in a number of SC/OBC candidates with higher marks being left out of the general quota.
The petitioners were selected provisionally but did not contest the respondent's finding that the selection process was flawed.
The petitioners had a fair opportunity to contest the respondent's findings.
The contention of the petitioners that the cancellation of their selection was against the principle of natural justice was not valid.
[Paras 4 and 7]
