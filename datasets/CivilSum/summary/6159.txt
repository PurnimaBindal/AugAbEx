Based on Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21, the petitioner was entitled to compassionate appointment and a supernumerary post with salary.
Despite directions from the Director, the petitioner was not paid salary on the grounds that supernumerary posts cannot be created in aided schools.
The petitioner was also not adjusted against any post after a new one for that purpose was sanctioned.
Due to the management's illegal and arbitrary actions, the petitioner was deprived of salary and appointment for 5 and 7 years respectively.
The court criticized the bureaucracy's lack of duty towards the Nation and citizens and directed the management to pay the petitioner on time-scale of pay from the date of appointment to the date of Director's order, and for the Director to release grant-in-aid to the post from that date.
