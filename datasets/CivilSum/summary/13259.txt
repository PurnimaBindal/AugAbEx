Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ jurisdiction - The appellant has been accused of grave misconduct for producing a bogus matriculation certificate during the time of appointment.
The appellant is questioning the correctness of the order of termination passed by the disciplinary authority.
However, the remedy for the appellant is to resort to the provisions of the Industrial Dispute Act.
Hence, a writ petition is not maintainable.
[Paras 2 and 4]
