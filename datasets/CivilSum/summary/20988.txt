Fundamental Rules, Rule 48-A - Non-payment of death-cum-retirement gratuity - Government employee did not receive gratuity due to remaining in unauthorised occupation of Government accommodation - Gratuity withheld until Estate Officer issues No Demand Certificate, pending claim for damages for unauthorised occupation - Court ruled that right to gratuity is not dependent upon officer vacating official accommodation - Government ordered to pay gratuity and recover damages under Rule 48-A as per 1985(1) SCC 429.
