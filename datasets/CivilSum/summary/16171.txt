Provisions of Chapter V-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 have been made applicable to industrial establishments that are not seasonal and have at least 100 workmen employed on average per day for the preceding 12 months.
A factory is defined as occupying a fixed site or premises, and if the manufacturing process takes place at different premises, they may still be considered a factory if other conditions are met.
However, retail marketing outlets cannot be clubbed with the manufacturing unit to bring them under the applicability of Chapter V-B For employees working in manufacturing establishments with 16 or fewer workers, or in retail outlets, retrenchment management is not required to comply with the provisions of Section 25-N, and they remain governed by the provisions of Chapter V-A of the Act.
