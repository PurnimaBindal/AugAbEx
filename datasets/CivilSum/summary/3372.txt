Constitution of J and K, Section 126 - Disability Pension - Grant of - A petitioner, who was discharged after seven years of service in the army due to being diagnosed with `Schizophrenic Psychosis Catatonic', has been denied pension despite a 30% disability assessment.
However, no mention of the disease was made at the time of the petitioner's entry into service, leading to the conclusion that it occurred due to stress and strain of army service.
As a result, the petitioner is entitled to disability pension.
