Civil Procedure Code, Order 39, Rules 1 and 2 - Dentists Act (as amended by 1993 amendment Act), Sections 10-A and 10-B - Interim injunction - Prima facie case - Balance of convenience - Irreparable injury - The court must consider the balance of convenience and irreparable injury when deciding whether to grant an interim injunction.
In this case, the Petitioners are students who were admitted into the BDS course by duly recognised and affiliated colleges approved by the Dental Council of India.
Although the New Baba Farid University has not recognized these colleges, the government and university are working to accommodate the students in other recognised institutions.
Prima facie case and balance of convenience favour the students, who are entitled to interim injunction allowing them to appear in the examination and have their results declared, subject to the decision of the suit.
