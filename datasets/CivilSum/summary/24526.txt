The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 136 and 226 - Employees' State Insurance Act 1947, Sections 40 and 41 - Contribution to ESI - During pendency of a writ petition, an interim order of stay of recovery of employer and employee contributions can be issued if the employer provides adequate medical allowances and facilities.
Although an interim order cannot delay implementation of the Act, the ESI Corporation cannot recover contributions during a period when medical assistance was not provided, and the employer was providing it in good faith - The employer cannot be burdened twice for acting under a court order - The Courts are authorized to issue orders for complete justice.
