Constitution of India, Article 226 - Evidence Act, Section 115 - Estoppel - The mere compliance of an order does not waive the right to appeal, as the court retains jurisdiction to evaluate and modify the order; hence, an appeal can still be submitted after the order has been executed.

Constitution of India, Articles 20(3) and 22(1) - Investigation of offence - Natural justice - The assistance of a lawyer during investigation or limitations on the investigative authority are not warranted by law because the accused is not entitled to such protection in proceedings related to the recovery of money.
