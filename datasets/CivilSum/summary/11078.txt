Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Sections 465, 467, and 477 of the Penal Code, 1860 were violated by the respondent who later admitted his guilt in a successful criminal prosecution.
Despite being given the benefit of Probation of Offenders Act, the respondent's suspension was revoked and a departmental inquiry was initiated, resulting in an order of dismissal.
No principles of natural justice were violated and the respondent's suppression of a material fact was just cause for dismissal.
