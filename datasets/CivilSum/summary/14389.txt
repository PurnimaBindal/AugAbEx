Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Regularisation - Petitioner's case is covered by the ruling in Giri Raj v State of Haryana as he has completed five years of continuous service and worked for more than 240 days each year - Hence, Respondents are ordered to consider his case for regularisation in accordance with the law laid down in Giri Raj's case.
