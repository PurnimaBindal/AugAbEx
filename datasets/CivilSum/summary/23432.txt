Admission - Admission of girls in Diploma in Education (D.Bd.)
Course in a Christian Minority Institution - The High Court granted admission to all students by way of interim order, which went against the law - Granting such orders encourages indiscipline and allows educational institutions to flout orders and exploit students and parents - Final directions were issued for admission of girls in various institutions - Interim orders are necessary to protect the interests of the petitioner until the rights can be finalized - The appellant concealed important information and put the career of students at risk, but no exemplary costs were imposed on the State.
