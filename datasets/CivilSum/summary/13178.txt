Constitution of India, Article 226 - Misconduct - Public servants are guilty of serious misconduct if they falsify official records to favor specific institutions or individuals.
[Paras 7 and 8]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Departmental proceedings - In a case where the petitioner confesses to charges of forging and editing official records, she cannot claim she was not given a proper hearing during departmental proceedings.
The department's case is supported by documentary evidence, and the inquiry officer provides detailed findings for each accusation.
It cannot be said that the Inquiry Officer's discussion lacks evidence.
The petitioner is not entitled to any remedy.
[Paras 9 and 14]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Departmental Inquiry - Judicial Review - Although judicial review is not completely prohibited in departmental inquiries, guilty judgments in such cases are seldom challenged.
Judicial review may only occur if the verdict is baseless, or a reasonable, prudent person couldn't reach it, or if it is unjust or due to orders from a superior authority.
