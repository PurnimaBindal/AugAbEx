Employees' State Insurance Act (34 of 1948), Sections 40, 93-A (as inserted by Amendment Act 38 of 1975) - Arrears of ESI dues - An establishment registered under the ESI Act - The establishment is required to pay ESI arrears - After purchasing the establishment in an auction sale, the petitioner is not responsible for paying the ESI dues.
