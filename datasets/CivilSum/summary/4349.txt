Constitution of India, Article 309 - Orissa Judicial Services Rules, 1964, Rule 16 (as amended by notification dated 26.12.1986) - Recruitment - Viva voce test - Candidates who applied for the judicial service recruitment before the amendment of the Rules, which stipulated that all candidates who obtained 30% marks in the written test would be called for a Viva voce test, were affected when the rules were changed to require a minimum of 40% marks - The action of the Public Service Commission in not calling candidates who scored below 40% but above 30% for interview is challenged - However, the proviso to the rule allowed the government to stipulate higher qualifying marks, and therefore the action is unchallengeable.
