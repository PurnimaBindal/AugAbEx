Constitution of India, Articles 32, 141, 142, 226 - Industrial Disputes (Amendment) Act, 1982, Clause (c) and Section 1(2) - Amendment of Act - Enforcement of - Appointed date - Court's jurisdiction to direct the Govt. to enforce - The court has the power to direct the consideration of an appropriate appointed date for enforcement of a law, but cannot compel the government to enforce it.
