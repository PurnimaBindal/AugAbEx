As for the second respondent, Mr. L Ravichander, his counsel argued that the selection process was based on extra special qualifications and that the court does not have jurisdiction to examine the basis of consideration of merits, as that is the function of the Selection Committee made up of experts.
The court held the selection to be valid, citing Constitutional Articles 14, 16, and 226.
