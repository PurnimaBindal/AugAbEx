Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Indian Railway Employees Manual, Rule 188 - Reversion - Deputation post - Regularisation - Petitioner returned to parent division from deputation post and claimed regularisation on deputation post - The post that the petitioner claims as regularisation can only be filled through promotion of group-D category as no regular avenue promotion exists for that post - Petitioner has a regular channel in his own cadre - Regularisation in group-C is only for casual labourers - The petitioner is only to be considered for promotion in his own cadre - Being a casual labourer, he is not entitled to regularisation - On the deputation post, he has no right to be considered for promotion or absorption - The petitioner is only entitled to regularisation on a post in his parent division according to the rules.
[Paras 15 and 16]
