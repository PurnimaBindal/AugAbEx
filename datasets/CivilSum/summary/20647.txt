Constitution of India, Article 309 - Maharashtra Civil Services (General Conditions of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 36 - Date of Birth - Correction of - Petitioner submitted School Leaving Certificate as evidence of birth date at the time of entry into service and requested correction based on birth register entry inconsistent with the certificate - However, birth register entry was deemed dubious and no other proof was presented to dispute the date on the certificate - Consequently, directing correction of the date of birth in the service book on that basis was not considered appropriate.
