Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb), 2(k) and 25F - Industrial Dispute - Daily wage workers - Retrenchment - Workman - Employers relying on Section 2(oo)(bb) must provide evidence to support their claim and cannot argue otherwise without such evidence - Daily wagers not covered by Section 2(oo)(bb) unless it is proven that they had a fixed-term contract - Nonetheless, daily wagers cannot be reinstated or receive back wages but may be entitled to compensation for wrongful termination.
