According to Constitution of India, Article 226, once an appointment is made on contractual basis for a fixed period, no interim directions can extend the tenure beyond the period prescribed.
This is supported by the 2006(2) SCT 462 (SC) case.
[Para 4] B.
When the Post of demonstrator was advertised for only three years and an appropriate condition was incorporated in appointment letter, the petitioners cannot claim that restricting their tenure to three years is violative of Articles 14 and 16.
They were fully aware they were appointed for a period of three years and cannot demand an extension until a regular appointment is made, as per the rulings in the cases 1998(4) R.S.J.
152 and 2000(3) R.S.J.
499.
The 2006(2) SCT 462 (SC) case is also relevant.
