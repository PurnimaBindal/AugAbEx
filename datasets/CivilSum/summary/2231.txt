Assistant Recruitment Rules, 2003, Rule 5 - Amended new rule - Alleged reduction in chances of promotion challenged - Employee absorbed in BSNL and earlier promotion based on seniority-cum-merit - Rule 5 requires limited Internal Examination for promotion - Court holds that reduction in promotion chances does not warrant judicial review - Assurance of existing terms and conditions and career progression channels does not limit BSNL's power to formulate new policy - Methodology applies to other cadres and aims at ensuring efficiency and talent acquisition.
