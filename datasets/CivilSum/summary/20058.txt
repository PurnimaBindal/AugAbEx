Constitution of India, Article 309 - Chandigarh Education Service (School Cadre) Group C Recruitment Rules, 1991, Rule 4 - Promotion - The promotion of respondents to the posts of Masters/Mistress (TGTs) on different dates in 1993 is not against recruitment rules since Classical and Vernacular teachers cannot be excluded from consideration for promotion, being feeder categories for promotion from PST teachers.
