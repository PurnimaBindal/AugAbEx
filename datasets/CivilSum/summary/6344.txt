Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - U.P.
Direct Recruitment through Public Service Commission Examination Rules, 1986, Rule 3(3) - Selection - The Court has no jurisdiction to interfere with the standard set by the Commission for selection and should not attempt to dilute it.
A policy decision made by the Commission or any other authority regarding candidate selection cannot be challenged unless it is unconstitutional, illegal, or violates a statutory rule.
The Commission is an expert body and may set its own policy and standards for candidate testing.
[Paras 10 and 11]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - U.P.
Direct Recruitment through Public Service Commission Examination Rules, 1986, Rule 2(viii) - Selection - The Commission may determine the percentage of marks required for the preliminary examination and take into account other factors when doing so.
The requirement of 75% marks for suitability for the final examination is not arbitrary or contrary to the rule.
[Para 12]
