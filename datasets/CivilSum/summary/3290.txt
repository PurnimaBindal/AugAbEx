Code of Civil Procedure, Sections 115, 151 Order XVI, Rules 1(2) and 6 - Summoning of witness - Bank's application for recalling summons rejected - General Manager (P&D) had no relevant authority in the personnel department or promotion of private respondents, making the prayer for summoning not bona fide - Trial Court's order set aside since it had the inherent power to refuse to summon a witness.

Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 16 Rules 1(2) and 6 - Any person summoned to produce a document is deemed to have complied - Court may refuse to summon a witness if it would be an abuse of the process of the Court - No justification for summoning General Manager to produce certain documents as he has no authority in the relevant department.
