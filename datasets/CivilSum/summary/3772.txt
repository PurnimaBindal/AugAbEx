According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Section 128 of the Haryana Cooperative Societies Act, 1984, provision of a departmental appeal cannot bar raising an industrial dispute.
The Full Bench of the Punjab and Haryana High Court held that the provisions of Section 128 of the Haryana Cooperative Societies Act barring the jurisdiction of Labour Court were ultra vires in The Ambala Central Co-operative Bank Limited v. The State of Haryana 1993(2) SCT 310 (P&H).

In the case of back wages, Section 11-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 states that it is the employer's responsibility to prove gainful employment of the workman during termination and pendency of proceedings.
However, if the workman fails to plead that he was out of a job during the period and does not refute the management's assertion that he was gainfully employed, the employer does not need to provide evidence of anything else.
