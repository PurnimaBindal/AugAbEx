Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - A Stenographer seeks promotion to Senior Scale Stenographer - The petitioner was initially ineligible due to lack of 3 years of service and was subsequently bypassed - The petitioner's case must be considered for promotions made since they became eligible - To remedy the situation, the petitioner should be considered for promotion to Senior Scale Stenographer with a retrospective date, without affecting previous promotions made over the past ten years - A supernumerary post must be created - If found competent, the petitioner will receive all relevant benefits and back pay.
