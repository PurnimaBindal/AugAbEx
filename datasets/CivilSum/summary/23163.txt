Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21, 38 and 39-A - Policy for medical reimbursement of imported medicines for life saving drugs - The Govt. of India has directed that if drugs manufactured in India are ineffective, life saving imported drugs can be reimbursed on certification issued by the CMO - The Board will only reimburse imported drugs that are life saving, not those for diseases that are not life threatening, such as pituitary gland disease (a disease which affects growth in height) and the Norditropin injection - The High Court erred in directing reimbursement based on a general assumption that the State reimburses foreign treatments.
