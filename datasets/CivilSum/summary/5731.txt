Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - West Bengal Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1971, Rules 10 and 12(a)(b) - West Bengal Government Servants Conduct Rules, 1959, Rule 15(2) - Writ jurisdiction - Punishment of Dismissal - Natural justice - Misconduct - Power of Court to interfere in the quantum of punishment - Property acquired by petitioner was disproportionate to the known sources of his income - Conducting departmental proceedings resulted in petitioner being found guilty of all charges - A show cause notice to dismiss the petitioner from service was issued - The legality of this order was challenged - The imposition of higher punishment must be based on adequate reasons - In this particular case, the penalty imposed was unjustified and disproportionate to the known sources of income, and therefore, the Court can interfere in its quantum - The Court has directed the respondent authorities to re-examine the penalty in accordance with the law, rules and regulations after giving the petitioner an opportunity to be heard - The penalty must be proportionate to the misconduct.
