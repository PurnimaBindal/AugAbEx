Constitution of India, Article 226 - Retirement benefits - Voluntary Retirement scheme - According to the circular dated 3393, completing 10 years of service or reaching the age of 40 makes an employee eligible for voluntary retirement.
The petitioner was eligible and retired on 30.4.93.
However, the respondent issued another circular dated 2496 that denied the petitioner's pension due to less than 20 years of qualifying service as per CCS Pension Rules, which was improper.
Therefore, the respondents have been instructed to pay all retirement benefits, including pension, to which the petitioner is entitled as per the circular dated 3393.
