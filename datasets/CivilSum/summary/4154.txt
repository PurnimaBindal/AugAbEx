Section 10 of Industrial Disputes Act states that once an award is passed and gazetted, the Labour Court cannot review it after 30 days.
However, clerical mistakes can be rectified at any time to advance the cause of justice.
[Para 9]
Labour Court has the power to lift the veil and determine the relationship between employees and employers.
In this case, the labourers who were initially engaged through a labour contractor for 13-14 years were entitled to regularisation of their service and to be treated as employees of the management.
[Paras 10 and 12]
If casual workers were eligible for regularisation, they cannot be discriminated against regarding the regular pay scale.
This would violate Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution.
[Para 11]
