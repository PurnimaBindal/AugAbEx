Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Medical reimbursement - Claim - Rejected - Petitioner's claim for medical reimbursement, pertaining to her husband's heart surgery, was denied by respondents - Respondents argued that the petitioner's husband, a retired central government employee, was receiving a pension exceeding Rs. 500/-, therefore, not meeting the requirements of dependent status as defined by government instructions - Court stated that the petitioner's husband falls within the definition of "family" as per relevant rules, due to his meagre government pension - Additionally, the court deemed the husband wholly dependent in accordance with Supreme Court precedent - Petition allowed - Respondents to process petitioner's reimbursement claim following the applicable rules.
