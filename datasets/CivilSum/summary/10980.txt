The Constitution of India, Article 16 and IPS (Appointment by Promotion) Regulation, 1955, Regulation 5(2) - Promotion - Regularisation - The petitioner was entitled to be promoted as Assistant Commandant effective from 8383, but was actually promoted on 25186.
Because of this delay, the petitioner was not considered for promotion to the IPS.
However, it was held that the petitioner cannot be held responsible for the delay in regularisation and confirmation.
The rejection of the petitioner's claim by the Authorities was viewed as arbitrary and discriminatory.
The petitioner is entitled to consider the date of regular promotion as the date for counting the qualifying service for appointment to the Indian Police Service.
