Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume-I, Rule 8.116 and 821 - Earned leave entitled to a Govt. employee in permanent employment - Leave credited to the leave account of a Govt. employee will lapse when he retires - Liberalised leave provisions have been granted by the Govt. to retiring employees - Earned leave can be converted into money, but only up to a maximum of 240 days - Amendment to Rule 821 seeks to offer an incentive for employees to dedicate more time to the Govt. and to pay compensation to those employees who do not take a certain amount of leave during their service - This benefit is not a legal or constitutional right, but rather a concession - Thus, limiting the benefit to a maximum of 240 days is not arbitrary or irrational.
[Para 6] B. Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume-I, Rules 8.116 and 821 - Benefit of Rule 821 has been extended to all employees without any discrimination - It cannot be claimed that the Govt. has violated the equality clause as it is a concession given to retired employees - The petitioners cannot demand that the Govt. extend this concession to all unutilised leave at the time of superannuation - Petition rejected.
