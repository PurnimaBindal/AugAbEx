Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service of Employees) Act, 1974 Section 3 - Appeal - Alternate Remedy - Dispute of facts - Resignation - Acceptance of resignation - Whether an employee's resignation was voluntarily submitted or was prepared with misuse of signatures and whether the Director has the authority to question the order of acceptance of resignation are factual disputes - The petitioner may appeal to the Colleges Tribunal - A writ petition is not the proper solution - The petitioner is referred to the remedy of appeal before the Tribunal.
[Paras 4, 5 and 8]
