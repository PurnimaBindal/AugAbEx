The correction of the date of birth in the service register for an industrial dispute is addressed in Item 2 and Item 6 of Schedule 2 of the Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947).
The petitioner must file an application within six months of the standing orders' coming into force.
However, the petitioner failed to do so, as evidenced by the fact that he was aware of the erroneous entry, which was periodically disclosed to him in pay slips and seniority lists.
The petitioner's request for correction was four years after his retirement and five years after he was informed of the date on which he would retire, demonstrating a delay that is likely to and therefore cause his plea to be dismissed.
