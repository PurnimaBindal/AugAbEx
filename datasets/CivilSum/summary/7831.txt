Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol II, Part II, Rules 4.19(a) & 6.16(2) - Proportionate pension - Voluntary resignation - Entitlement - Rule 4.19(a) will only be enforced if the Government official resigns to avoid an order of dismissal or removal due to anti- national activities such as Sabotage, Espionage etc - If a Government official retires under Rule 4.19(a), the past service will be forfeited and no pension will be provided - Rule 4.19(a) cannot be applied to voluntary resignation.
