Constitution of India, Article 309 - Indian Meteorological Service (Group A Posts) Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 1983 - Indian Meteorological Service (Group A Posts) Recruitment Rules, 1978 - Promotion - Eligibility - The contention that "unequals have been made equals" holds no merit - A person who has held the post of MG-II for five years cannot claim that a person who has held the post of MG-II and AM for a combined service of 8 years is not equal to them, nor can a person who has held the post of AM for ten years be considered unequal to either of them - The matter of post equation falls within the purview of the rulemaking authority, and the court will not interfere unless the rule is entirely unreasonable and irrational - There is no basis for the respondents' concern that juniors who had served as AM in the lower cadre would go above them and be considered for promotion as MG-I over them - There is also no truth to the contention that a person holding the post of AM, promoted from the post of professional Assistant with lesser qualifications, would be equated with those having the essential qualifications requisite for the post of MG-I - There is no illegality or unconstitutionality in the 1978 Rules or the 1983 Rules - The appeal is allowed.
