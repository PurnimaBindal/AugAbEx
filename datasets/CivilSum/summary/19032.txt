Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - Qualifications - Appointment as Primary Teacher in Municipal Corporation School - According to the Association of Indian Universities, a passed Pre-University (1995) Course from MD University of Rohtak is equivalent to an 11-year Higher Secondary Programme - The Corporation cannot claim otherwise - The petitioner, with a score of 72 marks, is entitled to the appointment.
