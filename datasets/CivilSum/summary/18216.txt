In accordance with the Constitution of India, Article 16, the order of compulsory retirement can be challenged, however, if there is a delay or laches in filing the application, it may be dismissed.
In a case where an OA was filed two years after the cause of action, the Tribunal dismissed the application due to the lack of explanation for delay.
Further, a fact-finding enquiry revealed that the incident of misconduct was confirmed by multiple witnesses, with the petitioner admitting to the same stating he lost his equilibrium.
Despite the petitioner not leading any defence evidence, the inquiry was conducted in accordance with the principles of natural justice, leading to the order of the Tribunal dismissing the application as time-barred being upheld.
