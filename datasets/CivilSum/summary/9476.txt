Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 15(4) - Admission - Reserve Seats - The majority of reserved seats were designated for residents of Punjab, while 10% were allocated based on merit to candidates from other states.
This renders candidates from Punjab ineligible, unless the seats remain unfilled by candidates from other states and are subsequently surrendered to them.
