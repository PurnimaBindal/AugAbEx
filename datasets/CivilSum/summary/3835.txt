According to Constitution of India, Article 226 - Central Civil Service (Conduct) Rules 1964, Rule 15 - ESI Corporation (Staff and Condition of Service) Regulations 1959, Regulation 23 - Punishment, if an employee is reverted from one post to another due to misconduct, the decision is subject to judicial review.
In a case where the employee rendered part-time consultancy services to a private firm without seeking permission from a competent authority, it was held that the conduct of the employee is indeed misconduct.
