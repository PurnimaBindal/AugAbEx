According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, the appointment of petitioner as class IV employee against substantive post must follow proper selection process.
In this case, the petitioner was appointed without undergoing any selection process and the post should have been referred to the District Recruitment Board.
It is the responsibility of the appointing authority to fill the post on the recommendations of the Board.
The appointment order issued by DC, in this case, was a gross misuse of his authority as the Chairman of the District Board and is neither enforceable nor liable to be implemented.
As a result, the respondents were justified in ignoring the order altogether due to its lack of legal force.
Thus, the petition was dismissed.
