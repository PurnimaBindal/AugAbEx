Constitution of India, Article 226 - Candidature - Rejection of - Post of Motor Vehicle Sub Inspector - Petitioner applied for post in response to advertisement - Petitioner passed examination and was called for interview - Candidature of Petitioner was rejected - Respondents contend that qualification should have been possessed till last date of receipt of application - It was held that commission should reject application form prior to allowing candidate to appear in examination - Petitioner's form could have been rejected before issuance of roll number - Therefore, respondent was directed to pay cost of Rs. 1000/- and petition was dismissed accordingly.
