Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana Aided Schools (Security of Service) Rules, 1974, Rule 45 - Qualifications - Relaxation in qualifications - Revised pay scale cannot be withheld from an employee appointed by relaxation of qualifications in accordance with Rules due to their higher qualifications - Equivalence of qualifications made the basis of relaxation applies to the entire service, including promotion and revised pay scale - Hindi Teachers appointed based on post-graduation in Hindi under Rule 45 must not be denied a higher revised pay scale due to the lack of diploma in Prabhakar and O.T./STC.
[Paras 8 to 13]
