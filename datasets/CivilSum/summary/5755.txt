Constitution of India, Article 311 - Banking Companies Regulations Act, 1949, Section 10(1) - Moral turpitude - Conviction in moral turpitude - Dismissal from service - A bank employee convicted of cheating in the course of employment is guilty of moral turpitude, and therefore, not entitled to be heard by virtue of the proviso (b) (1) to Section 10 of Banking Companies Regulations Act, which was introduced keeping in view the public policy - Principles of natural justice would not come into play at the level of punishing authority - A Govt. servant convicted of an offence of moral turpitude is not entitled to any opportunity of being heard before passing the punishment order [Para 6]  B Constitution of India, Article 311 - Probation of Offenders' Act, Section 12 - Conviction - Release on probation - Release on probation does not erase the conviction - An employee is not automatically entitled to reinstatement in service by getting the benefit of probation - Section 12 of the Act does not exonerate a Govt. Servant of his liability to departmental punishment for misconduct and does not afford immunity against the disciplinary proceedings for the misconduct - The offence need not involve moral turpitude.
