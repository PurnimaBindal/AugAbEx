Motor Vehicles Act, 1939, Section 110-A - Compensation - Excluding benefits like income tax, insurance, GPF from compensation is not allowed.
[Para 6]
Motor Vehicles Act, 1939, Section 110-A - Monthly income - When calculating monthly income of the deceased, the gross or carry home salary should be used as the basis.
Financial benefits like insurance, provident fund, family pension or gratuity are treated as the deferred earnings of the victim or as a result of their savings.
Dependents of a deceased person are always entitled to retiral benefits, even if no compensation is claimed.
Claimants should not be denied the benefit of deductions, like provident fund, family pension or gratuity when calculating the monthly income of the deceased in a claim application.

Motor Vehicles Act, 1939, Section 110-A - Compensation - The deceased earned a total salary of Rs. 3139/- per month at the time of the accident.
He held the position of DSP/Assistant Commandant and it is expected that he spent at least Rs. 1000/- or more to maintain his status.
Rs. 2000/- is deemed to be monthly dependency.
The deceased, being 40 years old, would have held his position until the age of 58 or another 18 years if he had not died.
A multiplier of 15 is applied, and claimants are entitled to a total amount of compensation of Rs. 3,60,000/-.
