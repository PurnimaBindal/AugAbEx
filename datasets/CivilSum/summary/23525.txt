The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 19, 21, 38, and 39 - Universal Declaration of Human Rights, Article 25(2) - International Covenant of Economic, Social and Cultural Rights, Article 7 - Social legislation - Insurance policy - Insurance policy for employees is a social security measure contemplated under the Constitution - The insurer has the right to request reasonable conditions, including proper health and capacity verifications, before entering into a life insurance contract.
However, these conditions must be based on reasonable classifications consistent with the provisions of Articles 14 and 21 of the Constitution - Restricting social policies only to the employees of government, quasi-government, or reputable commercial firms is not justifiable or reasonable - The scheme must be open to all salaried class, including those in rural areas.
[Paras 19, 30, 53 & 54]  B The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21, 38, and 39 - Contracts - Government contracts - Public authorities must follow constitutional principles and must meet the standards of Article 14 even in contractual relationships with the public - If the public authority engages in one-sided and unfair clause insertion in the conditions of such contracts, judicial review may challenge such action.
