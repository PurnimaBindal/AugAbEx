According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner was appointed as an Assistant Teacher on probation for two years against a permanent post, and was allowed to work from 1988 to 1991.
However, he was unceremoniously stopped from working in the school and was appointed even before the school was recognized.
Based on this, the petitioner is entitled to regularization and the District Inspector of Schools is also directed to approve his service against any vacant post.
[Paras 10-12]
