The suit filed under Section 115 and Order 6 Rule 17 of the Civil Procedure Code, 1908, for declaration that defendant was not entitled to be selected/promoted under the scheme, was amended by the plaintiff during the pendency of the suit, whereas the defendant moved an application for dismissal of the suit as infructuous.
However, the application by the defendant to dismiss the suit as infructuous was dismissed, and both orders were challenged in different Revision Petitions.
Nevertheless, there were no illegality or infirmity in the orders, and the Revision Petitions were dismissed.
