Under Rule 22 of the Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol. II, a court cannot entertain a challan against a government servant for misappropriation of Government money if it is presented four years after the commission of the offence, even if the offence falls under Sections 409/420/467/468/471/120 of the Indian Penal Code and Section 5(2)(47) of the Prevention of Corruption Act.
Additionally, Section 468 of the Criminal Procedure Code is not applicable and does not repeal Rule 22, as per the decision in 1986(1) Recent Criminal Reports 66.
