The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, state that pay scale and qualifications must be considered.
In this case, teachers with a similar Post Graduate Diploma qualification are already receiving a higher pay scale, but the petitioner was denied this on the basis that it was meant for degree holders.
This is a clear case of discrimination, and the petitioner should be entitled to the same higher pay scale granted to others with a diploma qualification.
[Paras 5 and 7]
