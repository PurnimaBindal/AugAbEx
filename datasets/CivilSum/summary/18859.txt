The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 covers promotion, particularly in the case of the post of Computors.
In this circumstance, the Petitioner was initially working as a compositor and had passed the necessary test to be promoted as Computor.
However, the requirement to pass the test was later withdrawn.
The court held that since the petitioner had previously passed the test and the condition was removed, the respondent is required to consider the petitioner for promotion.
[Paras 4 and 5]
