Road Transport Corporation Act, Section 45 - Voluntary retirement - Petitioner requested voluntary retirement from the Corporation with effect from July 31, 1996 - The Corporation granted the request on June 12, 1996 - However, when the petitioner attempted to withdraw their request on June 19, it was rejected - After sending a notice, the petitioner filed a Special Civil Application - According to the right to withdrawal, if the petitioner withdraws their application before the effective date, the Corporation must accept it - The Corporation cannot rely on the June 12, 1996 order.
