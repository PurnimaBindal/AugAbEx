Constitution of India, Articles 14, 311 - Termination - A daily wage worker on a temporary post was terminated without any departmental proceedings or inquiry conducted.
The Trial Court did not find a departmental inquiry necessary in the case of the daily wager, but the first appellate court reversed the conclusion of the Trial Court.
The High Court failed to take note of the unreasoned and palpably wrong conclusions arrived at by the first appellate Court and recorded findings contrary to the materials on record.
The case was remitted to the High Court for a decision in accordance with the law after giving due opportunities to the parties.
[Paras 6 to 9]
