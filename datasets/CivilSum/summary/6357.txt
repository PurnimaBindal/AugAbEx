Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Section 4(2) - Suspension - Petitioner convicted of an offence under Section 5 (1) (e) r/w Section 5 (2) of Prevention of Corruption Act and sentenced to imprisonment exceeding forty-eight hours - Rule 4 (2) (a) mandates suspension on detention in custody exceeding forty-eight hours, while Rule 4 (2) (b) mandates suspension on conviction - Suspension order is automatic even if the operation of the sentence has been stayed in appeal by High Court after the sentence was in operation for more than forty-eight hours - Impugned order of suspension is referable to Rule 4 (2) (b) of the Rules and is perfectly valid and legal.
