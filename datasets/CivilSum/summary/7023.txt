Rajasthan Co-operative Societies Act, 1965, Section 148 - Qualifications for appointment to the post of Assistant Executive Officer - Clause (a) of the Schedule pertains to promotion, not to direct recruitment.
An applicant must be a graduate with at least three years of experience in a clerical position.
[Para 18]
Rajasthan Co-operative Societies Act, 1965, Section 148 - Resolution of the Board of directors - Power of Registrar to rescind - The Board cannot function as the supreme authority when the Act empowers the Registrar to rescind its resolution.
Claims that the Board holds absolute power in matters of appointments/promotions are not valid.
[Para 18]
Rajasthan Co-operative Societies Act, 1965, Section 148 - Absorption - Qualifications - Employees cannot claim absorption into a higher post for which they do not possess the necessary qualifications.
