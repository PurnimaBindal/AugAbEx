Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, Regn.
173 - Disability pension - Claim for - Petitioner's claim for disability pension rejected as disease (Myocarditis-acute) not attributable to military service - Nevertheless, disease covered under Rules under "B-diseases affected by stress and strain" category - Even if not attributable to service, disability may be aggravated due to stress and strain of Army duties - Respondents ordered to consider petitioner's case again for disability pension in accordance with law.
[Paras 8 and 11]
