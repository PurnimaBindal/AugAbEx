The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 are violated by the classification/weightage to language given by granting 5% weightage marks to Telugu medium candidates in graduation.
It is unconstitutional and not valid and reasonable.
[Paras 16 and 17]

Granting weightage of 5% marks of total aggregate by adding 40 marks on the basis of Telugu language medium in graduation frustrates the concept of recruitment to public posts on merits.
The weightage given has no constitutional sanction.
[Para 17(2)]

The grant of special weightage for Telugu language medium candidates does not strictly conform to the provisions of the Official Language Act, 1966.
[Para 17(3)]

Selection based on giving much weightage for Telugu language is violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
However, the appointments already made will not be disturbed.
Those selected on the basis of language will be given one more chance by offering age relaxation, if their appointment could not be made due to the stay orders of the court in this matter.
[Para 17(3)]
