Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3(1)(a) - Premature Retirement - Punishment of censure and warning imposed prior to promotion of Head Constable to ASI - Insignificant after promotion - Cannot be used as grounds for Premature Retirement - Premature Retirement order nullified.
