According to Section 23 of the Army Act and Rules 12 and 13 of the Army Rules, a petitioner who was initially discharged due to low medical category can be re-enrolled after being found fit in a medical examination, but can still be discharged based on their service record.
In this case, the court held that the petitioner should be re-examined by a Medical Board.
If found fit, they should be allowed to continue, but if found to be in a low medical category again, they can be discharged.
