The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16 and 226, along with the Punjab Police Rules from 1934, highlighting Rule 137(1) and 13.7(2) state that seniority for constables must be maintained District-wise, not Range wise.
The petitioner in this case is the senior-most constable in the District, and thus their name must be included in the `B-1' list.
