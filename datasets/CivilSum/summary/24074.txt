Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Education - Promotion - Qualifications - Consideration of ACRs and Efficiency Bars - Research work - Promotion of Lecturers to Professors - Research work is an important factor in promoting lecturers to professors in the medical field.
Recording ACRs, allowing Efficiency Bars, and assessing promotability, research by a teacher in a medical college is desirable and relevant for maintaining a high degree of proficiency in teaching.
It is incorrect to believe that research work is not relevant to teachers in medical colleges for the crossing of efficiency bars or promotion.
