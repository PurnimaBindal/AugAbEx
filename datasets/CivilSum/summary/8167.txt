The provisions of Rule 1624 and Article 311 of the Constitution will only apply if the authority decides to punish a delinquent under Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 12.21 and 1624 - Discharge from service - Misconduct - Enquiry - Appointment and discharge of SPOs.
SPOs serving on daily wages under Standing Orders have no claim to the post and they are not covered under the Punjab Police Rules.
Even in the case of a dissatisfied SP who discharged them due to reasons mentioned in the discharge order, the order does not become punitive to require a formal enquiry into misconduct.
The DB judgment in Rakesh Kumar's case, 1999(4) RSJ 194, has been overruled by the Supreme Court in Jagdish Mitter's and Kaushal Kishore Shukla's cases, AIR 1964 SC 449 and 1991(1) SCC 691 and Full Bench in Sher Singh's case, 1994 SLR 100.
