When promotion rules are based on merit-cum-seniority, the senior candidate is only preferred if the merit of the two candidates is equal.
[Para 8]
Under the bye-laws of Punjab Small Industries and Export Corporation, promotion from Sub Divisional Engineer to Executive Engineer must be made based on merit-cum-seniority and only amongst eligible employees of the Corporation.
The lack of a test in the bye-laws or the absence of a resolution from the Board of Directors to adopt Punjab Government PWD Rules cannot be used to deny promotion.
[Paras 9 and 10]
Only after promotion to Class-I service do the Punjab PWD (B&R) Class-I Rules' test requirements apply.
Passing the test cannot be a condition for promotion.
[Para 10]
When filling promotional posts by deputation at a State Corporation, the Corporation must prioritize its own eligible employees first.
If none are available, the Corporation may request someone from the State Government.
Promoting a deputationist over eligible employees is against Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
[Paras 12 and 14]
Denying the right of consideration for promotion by filling promotional posts with a deputationist instead of considering eligible employees is illegal, arbitrary, unjust and unfair.
[Para 14]
