Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - Inter-District Transfer - Imposing a condition that employee shall not be transferred outside the District for first ten years of his service - Although not improper, the payment of Rs. 20,000/- as an application fee for transfer outside the District is not appropriate - The petitioner is not entitled to transfer outside the District by paying Rs. 20,000/- merely because similarly situated persons were transferred.
[Paras 4 and 5]
