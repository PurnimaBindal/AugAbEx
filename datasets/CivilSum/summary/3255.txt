Constitution of India, Article 311 - Superannuation - Confidential reports - Adverse remarks - Premature retirement - The petitioner has not received any adverse remarks in his confidential reports for the past ten years and has not been punished for any misconduct during his service - The petitioner's ACRs indicate that he has been described as an average, good, very good, and outstanding employee over the past ten years - An average report does not equate to inefficiency, and the petitioner cannot be considered unfit for public service or have his efficiency impaired to warrant premature retirement - The competent authority failed to objectively consider the record of the petitioner while recommending his premature retirement, which is enough to vitiate action by the respondents in retiring him before attaining the age of superannuation.

Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 311 - Superannuation age - Pre-mature retirement - Judicial review - While the government has the power to retire an employee before he reaches the age of superannuation, this power is usually guided by the rules set forth in the proviso to Article 309 of the Constitution or administrative instructions - The enforcement of this power of premature retirement is administrative and is subject to judicial review, like any other administrative power.
