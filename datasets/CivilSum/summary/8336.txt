According to Rule 56(1) of Kerala Service Rules, the government has the authority to determine how the period of suspension should be treated in cases of compulsory retirement and appeals against orders cancelling such retirement.
[Paras 8, 10 and 11]
In cases of appeals against orders cancelling compulsory retirement under Rule 56(A)(3) of Kerala Service Rules, the government holds the power to decide how the period of suspension is to be reckoned.
Rule 56A(3) only applies if the proceedings have been overturned by the Court, otherwise, Rule 56(1) applies.
[Paras 8, 9 and 11]
