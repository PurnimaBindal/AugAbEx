According to Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, workers cannot be allowed to complete 240 days and termination with an intent to prevent their entitlement to rights under Chapter V-A is considered an unfair labor practice.
The worker is deemed to have worked for more than 240 days even if there is no proof of their gainful employment, and they are entitled to full back wages.
