Petitioners sought direction to the authorities to not consider the respondent eligible or qualified for the post of Associate Director, stating that the respondent lacked a PhD in any discipline of Agricultural Science.
The respondent disagreed, claiming to have a PhD in Botany, which is one of the sciences in Agriculture.
The court ruled that since the petitioners had already appeared before the selection committee and failed, they cannot challenge the committee's constitution.
Additionally, the petitioners failed to make committee members parties to the case to investigate allegations of malice.
The court held that the respondent is qualified for the position and dismissed the petition.
