Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Promotions - Classification on the basis of qualifications - The Damodar Valley Corporation (DVC) classified Civil Engineers into two groups based on their qualifications as either Diploma holder engineers or Degree holder engineers - Diploma holders were directly recruited as Assistant Collectors or equivalent level, while existing diploma holders were upgraded to the level of Assistant Collectors equivalent to degree holders and a separate cadre was created for civil engineering by Diploma holders.
This classification between degree holders and diploma holders was considered permissible under the law and did not constitute discrimination against degree holders.
