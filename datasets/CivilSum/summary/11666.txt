Constitution of India, Article 226 - Resignation to contest election - Acceptance of - The petitioner's application for resignation was not an outright tender of resignation but was instead qualified by the petitioner's desire to retire voluntarily and, if not entitled to do so, by the acceptance of his resignation - Payment in lieu of notice was not tendered along with the application, and disciplinary proceedings were pending against the petitioner - The acceptance of resignation was not automatic, and the decision to withhold acceptance was correctly made as the petitioner was under suspension - The government had decided, based on an enquiry report, to inflict a major penalty on the petitioner and to issue a show-cause notice - The petitioner is still a government servant, and there has been neither inaction nor undue delay in dealing with the disciplinary proceedings against him or in considering his application for resignation - Interim directions permitting the petitioner to file his nomination papers have been vacated.
[Paras 10 and 11]
