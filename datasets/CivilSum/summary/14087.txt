Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - Malafides - The petitioner was transferred from his own posting to hand over charge to a Revenue Circle Officer who has no connection with the functions of a BDO - The impugned order is not only malafide but also against public interest as the Sr. BDO's functions are handed over to someone who is not familiar with their workings - The impugned order is quashed.
