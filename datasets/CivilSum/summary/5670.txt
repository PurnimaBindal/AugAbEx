The Railway Property (Unlawful Possession) Act (29 of 1966), Section 3(a) and Railway Protection Force Rules, 1959, Rule 47(a) provide for dismissal in cases of petrifying.
The petitioner served as a Rakshak in the Railway Protection Force and several items of things pilfered from Railway Wagons were recovered from his possession.
The delinquent was not given an opportunity to be heard or submit a representation, which is a requirement implicit under Rule 47.
However, the infraction of procedural requirement did not result in any prejudice to the petitioner.
Thus, the petitioner is not eligible to be reinstated.
