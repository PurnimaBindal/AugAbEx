The power given to a corporation under regulation clause 20(2)(a)(b), to terminate even a confirmed employee's services without assigning reason is in violation of Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 5]
The termination of an employee's services after acquiring a disability during service is in direct conflict with Section 47 of the 1995 Act, which prohibits dispensing with or reducing the rank of such an employee.
Therefore, the impugned order has been quashed.
