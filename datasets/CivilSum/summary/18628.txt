As per the Constitution of India, Article 226 and the Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Section 11, with reference to the Central Reserve Police Force Rules, Rules 27 and 28, any use of the term 'Sub-Inspector' in Rule 27 must be taken to encompass all types of Sub-Inspectors, including Sub-Inspector (M).
Therefore, a punishment order that has been passed under this Rule, confers the right of appeal under Rule 28 to the petitioner.
Therefore, the Writ Petition cannot be maintained when an alternative remedy of an appeal is available to the petitioner, and when such an appeal has already been filed by the petitioner.
