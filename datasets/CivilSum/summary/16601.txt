The appellant was denied disability pension under Article 16 of the Constitution of India on the grounds that their disability fell between 15% to 19%, which is below the minimum requirement of 20%.
However, it is not reasonable to believe that disability can be precisely calculated to such a degree, and it is more likely that the disability could fall within 15% to 20% range, making the appellant eligible for the pension.
