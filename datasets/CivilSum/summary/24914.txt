Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(p) and 18 - Industrial Dispute - Trade Union - Members' rights to Union funds - Where a Union receives funds from management on behalf of workers for benefits, such as gratuity and accident cover, and no agreement exists between workers and the Union whereby the funds received become part of the Union's funds - A worker's resignation entitles them to a refund of any amounts received on their behalf.
