Constitution of India, Article 32 - Disabilities (Equal opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1955, Section 2(i)(v) - Concession in travelling by air for orthopaedically impared - Indian Airlines was accused of not providing adequate facilities like aisle chair and ambulift - Similar facilities already provided to those with visual disability - Court ruled that those with 80% locomotor disability should receive the same level of concession as those with blindness.
