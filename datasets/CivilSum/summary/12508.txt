Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Eligibility - Result - Petitioners were admitted to a Diploma course in Education - While awaiting the result, the State Government initiated an enquiry into the admission process, causing a delay in the result declaration - During this time, the Board advertised available positions - The petitioners applied for the positions, but were denied consideration due to their diploma certificates bearing a later date than the last date fixed - Had the result been declared on time, the petitioners would have been eligible - The charges against the admission were found to be baseless - The Board cannot unfairly penalize the petitioners - The Board is directed to interview the petitioners.
[Paras 4, 5 and 6]
