Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 states that in order to claim protection, a workman must prove that he has worked for a Continuous period of 240 days in the preceding year.
In this case, the workman failed to provide any evidence to prove that he had worked continuously for 240 days, making him ineligible for protection under Section 25F [Paras 15, 16 and 17] B.
The management argued that the workman was appointed for a specific period and work, therefore, the exception to retrenchment provided under Section 2(oo)(bb) of the Act applies.
The court held that since the management failed to show that the case fell under the exception provided, it cannot be held that the exception is attracted.
