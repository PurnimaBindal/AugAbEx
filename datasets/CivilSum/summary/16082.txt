According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India and Sections 25-F and 25-FFF of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, employees appointed on a temporary basis for a project, even if confirmed, will remain temporary and can only continue until the project exists and a post is available.
Termination of such an employee at the completion of the project or work is not considered a punishment, and principles of natural justice will not apply.
However, the employee is entitled to compensation for retrenchment and one month's salary in lieu of notice pay.
Section 25-FFF, not Section 25-F, of the Industrial Dispute Act will apply.
