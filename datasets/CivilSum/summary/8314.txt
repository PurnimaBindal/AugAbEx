Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Promotion - Gradings - Initiating Officer graded petitioner as " High Average" - Commandant downgraded the petitioner to "Average" - Downgrading not communicated to petitioner - Junior of petitioner promoted - Non-communication of the down-grading to petitioner deemed illegal - Petitioner has a good service record - No disciplinary action taken against him - Has received Medals - Maintain the "High Average" remark made by Initiating Officer - Quash the "Average" remark by Commandant - Petitioner to be promoted as Naib Subedar - Maintain seniority in Naib Subedar post - Petition allowed.
