Constitution of India, Article 311 - AP State Electricity Board Employees' Discipline and Appeal Regulations, Regulation 10(2)(a) - Departmental Enquiry - In cases where a fatal procedural defect prejudicial to the official occurred in the first enquiry, the Disciplinary Authority can order a fresh enquiry without waiting for the delinquent official to object.
The Disciplinary Authority's ability to order a de novo enquiry for officials exonerated in a non-fatal first enquiry is uncertain.
However, the Disciplinary Authority cannot order such an enquiry solely on the basis of an unexamined witness or unconsidered document, unless there was no serious procedural defect.
The Disciplinary Authority or the Higher Authority can reach a different conclusion based on the evidence recorded by the Enquiring Authority.
