Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Eligibility - Employee gains the right for consideration of promotion when juniors are promoted over seniors - It is the employer's decision to determine the eligibility of candidates - The Court cannot decide the eligibility of the candidate before the employer does - The Labour Court directed that the employer consider the employee's case and eligibility from the date his juniors were promoted - The Court could not have issued an order for straightaway promotion of the employee.
