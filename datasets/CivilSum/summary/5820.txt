Central Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 37 (b) and Sections 14 and 29 - Central Administrative Tribunals (Procedure) Rules, 1985, Rule 7 - Execution of decree - Service matters involving arrears of pay and allowances are exclusively decided by the Tribunal, after the coming into force of the Act - Civil Court cannot execute the decree - Filing an application before the Tribunal requires a fee of Rs. 50/- and no additional court fee can be demanded or required to be paid.
