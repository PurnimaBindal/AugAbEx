Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, Part I, Rule 6(b)(iv) to Appendix II - Disability pension/Casual leave - Treatment of Army personnel on casual leave - Regardless of whether travelling on personal or public expense, an Army personnel on casual leave is considered on duty - Furthermore, if a permanent disability occurs during leave, it is attributed to Army Service and the personnel is entitled to disability pension in accordance with the Rule.
