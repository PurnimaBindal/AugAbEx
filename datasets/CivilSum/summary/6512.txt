Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Discrimination - Malafide Intention - Necessary parties - Any person against whom allegations of malafide are made must be named as a party.
[Para 11]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Affidavit - Affidavit requirements - Affidavit must comply with writ rules or Order 19 of the CPC, otherwise allegations made cannot be considered.
[Para 12]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Appointment - Reservation - Reserved categories - If a person belonging to a reserved category is selected based on their own merit in open competition, they will not be counted against reserved quota and will be treated as an open category candidate.
This follows the decision in Indra Sawhney etc. v. Union of India, AIR 1993 SC 477.
[Paras 13 & 14]
