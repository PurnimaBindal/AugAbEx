Under Section 79 of the Electricity Supply Act, 1948 and Regulation 4 of the Rajasthan State Electricity Board Employees Conduct Regulation, 1976, an employee may be reinstated after probation if their misconduct did not relate to their duty, discipline, or a moral turpitude offense.
The petitioner is entitled to reinstatement with consequential benefits, including pay and pension, but without backwages.
