Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 9(1) - Speaking order - Punishing Authority must provide reasons for its disagreement with the inquiry report of the inquiry officer, rather than simply stating disagreement with findings - Recording of reasons allows the employee to identify irrelevant or extraneous reasons and challenge them in court, providing an additional safeguard.
