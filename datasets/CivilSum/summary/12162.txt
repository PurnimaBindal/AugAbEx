Haryana Municipal (Integration, Recruitment and Condition of Service) Rules, 1982, Rule 11(2) - Haryana Civil Service (Revised Pay) Rules, 1998 - Pay Revision - Arrears of pay - Municipal Committee employees and State Govt. employees treated equally in pay scales since 1969 - Revision of municipal employees' pay-scale to be same as State Govt. employees' pay-scale from 1196 - However, arrears of payment only given from 1499 to Municipal employees - This creates unfair distinction - Petitioners have the right to claim arrears of revised pay scale from 1196.
