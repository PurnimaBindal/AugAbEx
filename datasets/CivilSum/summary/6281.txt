Pursuant to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 22, Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, and various sections of the Penal Code, 1860, the accused-petitioners were accused of committing a crime in July 1991 with an FIR dated 131295.
Even though the petitioner retired before the FIR was lodged, it has been determined that the FIR cannot be quashed solely based on the time elapsed since the cause of action.
Based on evidence collected by the prosecution, the learned Magistrate was entirely justified in ordering the framing of charges against the accused petitioner.
