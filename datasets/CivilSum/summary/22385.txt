Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 25F - Regularization of Service - Temporary workman - Worked more than 240 days in each of the six years - Labour Court directed salary and benefits as regular employee - Factual details on sanctioned posts not discussed - Matter remitted back to Tribunal - Held: (i) 240 days' work doesn't give right of regularization under the Act.
(ii) No regularization against rules.
(iii) Employer obliged to termination obligations.
(iv) Working for more than 240 days doesn't give legal right to be regularized.
(v) No automatic regularization.
[Paras 8 to 10]
