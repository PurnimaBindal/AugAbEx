Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission - N.D.A.
Course - 141 candidates from Batch 101 denied admission due to reduction of seats for leftover candidate from Batch 100 - While respondents may need to accommodate successful students from one batch into other, it must not be at the expense of successful students of next batch - Court declared denial of admission to Batch 101 candidates unjust and a violation of Article 14 - Respondent ordered to grant admission to left over students of Batch 101 without delay.
