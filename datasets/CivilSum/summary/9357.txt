Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 1 Rule 10 and Order 30, Rule 1 - Reference made/Necessary parties to the reference - The management company, which is a partnership firm, did not disclose the names of its partners, but one partner represented it in an effective reply on merits.
Presumption of permission of other partners exists, as the act of one partner binds others as well.
The reply was filed through an authorized agent and the management was served with a notice in its registered name.
The labor court denied relief on hyper-technical grounds, which goes against the intent of the legislation.
The case remains to be decided on merits.
