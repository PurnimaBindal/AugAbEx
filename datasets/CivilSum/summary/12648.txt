Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Writ of Mandamus - West Bengal Board of Secondary Education Act, 1963 - Appointment - Regularization - Approval of service - Appointment as organizing non-teaching staff - The appointment was made against the recruitment rules - Permanent appointment cannot be claimed in an educational institution on the ground of working there as an organizing staff member - The concept of an organizing staff member goes against the Act - The appointment was illegal - It is held that there is no entitlement to claim approval of service - The petition is dismissed.
