Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Advance increments - Typing test - Eligibility - LDCs are eligible for an advance increment upon passing a Hindi typewriting test if the eligibility conditions at the time of their appointment did not prescribe such a condition - However, those appointed after the condition was prescribed are not entitled to an advance increment - The cut off date for granting advance increment based on the eligibility condition change is not considered unjust or arbitrary.
