Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(ra) and 25T read with Item No. 9 to Fifth Schedule - Unfair Labour Practice - Bias - Bias is partiality or preference – it is subjective and cannot be defined – to prove bias, one must demonstrate reasonable possibility of bias or prove circumstances from which influence can be inferred – a rational preference, unaccompanied by personal interest is not enough to vitiate an action – two voluntary retirement schemes, offering different financial benefits and conditions, make distinct classes – no one can complain about bias or unfair labour practice when both schemes were open to all.
