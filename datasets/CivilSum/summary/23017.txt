Paragraph 4 of the University Grants Commission Scheme of 1986 talks about the retirement age, according to which, the service rules made by the State Government apply to teachers in private or affiliated colleges.
The UGC Scheme does not apply to them unless the State Government adopts it.
The Service Rules set the retirement age at 55 years, which means that teachers in private and affiliated colleges cannot demand the benefit of the UGC Scheme's retirement age of 60 years.
