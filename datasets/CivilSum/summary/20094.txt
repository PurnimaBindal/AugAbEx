Central Civil Service (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rules 13 and 28 - Qualifying Service - Pension - Interruption in service between temporary and substantive appointment to Government Service - Prior to the amendment dated 1951980, automatic condonation of interruption was not available - Unamended rule did not allow interruption to be counted towards qualifying service for pension - Rule 13 and Rule 28 are not inconsistent, but work in harmony - If both provisions apply to a particular situation, both should be allowed to operate, unless it leads to a patent inconsistency or absurdity - As there was an interruption in service, the period of temporary service cannot be considered as qualifying service for pension benefits - Appeal allowed.
