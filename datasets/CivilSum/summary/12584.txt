The seniority of Professors belonging to different departments is based on continuous lengths of service as Professors.
The 1985 amendment to the Punjab Medical Education Service (Class 1) Rules was not retroactive, and thus, it did not affect the seniority of members of service determined under the 1978 Rules.
The deletion of "Additional Professor" did not take away the rights of those who had already become senior under the 1978 Rules.
[Para 12]

An Additional Professor cannot claim seniority to those already promoted as Professors.
[Para 13]

An administrative order passed in an individual case does not become general policy and cannot bind and affect the rights of parties for determining their seniority.
[Paras 13 and 18]

The tentative seniority list cannot determine rights, and no claims can be made based on it.
The mere publication of a tentative seniority list does not affect rights under the Rules.
Such seniority is always liable to alteration.
[Paras 14 and 17]

The seniority already determined under the prevailing Rules can be altered by subsequent changes in service rules except for protecting the promotions already made.
[Paras 15 and 17]

The mere fixation of a tentative seniority or gradation list does not affect the employee's right to be placed at their proper places in the final seniority.
A challenge to the final seniority list can still be made even if the tentative seniority list was not challenged earlier.
[Para 17]

Administrative orders cannot go against statutory rules under Article 309.
