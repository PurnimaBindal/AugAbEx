Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(C)(2) - Claim for back wages - Jurisdiction of Labour Court - The petitioner was being asked to pay an amount by the respondent who claimed to have been employed and worked for a certain period - However, the Labour Court did not have authority to pass the impugned award as it exceeded its power under Section 33-C(2) - The Award directing the employer to pay the balance amount with interest and assessed costs is without jurisdiction and has been quashed.
