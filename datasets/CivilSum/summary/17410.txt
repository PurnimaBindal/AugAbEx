Workmen's Compensation Act Section 30 - Appeal - The Commissioner made a finding of fact based on the evidence and admission of the owner that the deceased were the truck driver and helper.
This finding is not subject to appeal under Section 30.
[Para 5]
Workmen's Compensation Act Section 3 - Compensation - The truck driver and helper died in the course of their employment and thus, compensation was sought.
The finding of the Commissioner, which was based on surmises and conjectures, is vulnerable.
The compensation was calculated by the rates of wages admitted by the owner, and an earlier calculation was based on wages of the helper at 1100/- per month.
Thus, the compensation amount was reduced.
