Constitution of India, Article 226 - APSE Board Employees (Revised) Conduct Regulations, Regulation 5, 7(1), 10(2)(a) and Schedule I - Failure to adhere to rules - Departmental investigation - Unauthorized absence - Basis of Penalty - Non-disclosure of property statement and property details - However, the accusation of not submitting an immovable property statement was never included in the charge memo nor in the departmental inquiry - The imposition of a major penalty was done without conducting a departmental inquiry - Failure to follow the protocol established under Regulation 10(2)(a) before implementing a major penalty - Punishment nullified.
