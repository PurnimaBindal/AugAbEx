According to Rule 5(2) of Bombay Judicial Service Recruitment Rules, seniority of a promotee District Judge would start from the date of his promotion.
On the other hand, in case of direct recruit, the seniority would start from the date of his appointment to work as District Judge after completion of probation of two years or further, and not from the initial appointment to work as an Additional District Judge.
The administrative decision of the Bombay High Court dated 6th of May, 2000, is erroneous and should be struck down.
The confusion between clauses 2(c) and 2(iii)(a) is because of addition of clause (c) in draft rule and alteration thereof as (a) in the final rule.
The State Government should make appropriate amendment of Rule 5(2) to remove the confusion between clauses 2(c) and 2(iii)(a).
