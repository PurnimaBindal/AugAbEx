The appellant, who was initially appointed as Registration Clerk on daily wages, is seeking regularisation of service.
The appellant's main argument is that although a vacancy was available in the District, the appellant was not appointed while other candidates who appeared below the appellant's name were regularised.
The High Court directed that the appellant be paid a regular salary and the writ petition was heard along with other appeals and petitions for Registration Clerks appointed on daily wages.
However, the High Court dismissed the writ petition of the appellant without considering the relevant facts.
The court has since directed the High Court to consider the writ petition on its merits.
