According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, surplus staff who are offered appointments must receive posting orders.
In this case, the petitioner was declared surplus after ten years service and offered an appointment in BBMB, to which he submitted a joining report.
Despite this, no posting orders were issued for him.
When he was asked to submit a joining report in the military, the petitioner did not respond because he had already reported in BBMB.
The petitioner cannot be held responsible for the government's failure to issue posting orders.
[Paras 1 and 3]
