Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 2(8) - `Employment injury' - Refers to injuries that arise out of and occur during the course of employment - In this case, the deceased employee died due to electrocution near a borewell that was situated near his residential quarter - There was no connection between the cause of death and his employment as a Mechanic in the factory - Although the employer provided accommodation for the deceased, it cannot be said that the death of the employee arose out of and during the course of his employment - The doctrine of the notional extension of employment is not applicable.
