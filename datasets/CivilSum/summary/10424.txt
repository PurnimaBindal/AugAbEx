According to Chapter 31 Regulations 13, 14, 26-A and 27 of Allahabad University Calendar, rechecking of marks does not equate to revaluation.
The omission of provision of revaluation in Regulation 27 was made with the provisions of rechecking in Regulations 13 and 14 and reappear in Chapter 26-A in mind.
Therefore, candidates do not have the right to apply for revaluation; reappearing for improvement is the only option.
Regulation 14 does not include the scope of revaluation.
