The Constitution of India, Article 226 states that disputed questions of facts cannot be examined by the court.
[Para 4]
The Constitution of India, Article 226 outlines the basis for admission, which is the merit of a Joint Entrance Test.
Seats are reserved for candidates from rural areas, who are given a weightage of 5% of marks if they passed their Matriculation Examination while studying in a rural school.
The evidence presented by Respondent No. 5, a certificate that showed him to be a resident of a rural area, was verified by prescribed authorities, accepted by admission authorities, and he had completed one year of studies.
Therefore, the petitioner's case for interference under Article 226 was dismissed.
