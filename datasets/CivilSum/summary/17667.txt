Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 - Pension - Initially, employees were governed by Employees Provident Fund/Contributory Provident Fund Scheme but later converted to GPF Scheme.
The petitioner chose to surrender the employer's share of Provident Fund and exercise his option for payment of pension.
An undertaking was given by counsel of parties and the petitioner was directed to be paid pension in accordance with the undertaking given.
However, the petitioner was not entitled to payment of gratuity in accordance with orders by controlling and appellate authorities.
The amount already paid to him shall be adjusted towards the amount due on account of pension.
