Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Regularisation - Part-time Mali - Petitioner was appointed as Mali in 1984 and his service terminated in 1992.
However, he was reinstated in 1997 on the basis of a compromise award stating that he would not claim back wages.
The interim period was treated as an absence and the benefit of the circular, which provides for regularization after rendering 5 years of continuous service, was denied.
The court held that the waiver of monetary benefits by Petitioner cannot be deemed as a waiver of continuity of service to show it as a case of reappointment.
The action of respondents is wholly irrational, illegal and misconceived.
Therefore, Petitioner must be deemed to have completed the requisite service to be considered for regularisation.
[Paras 12, 13, 16, 17, 21] B.
Claim of regularisation - Non-denial of specific averment.
The specific assertion of Petitioner stating that one post is lying vacant in the office was not specifically answered in reply.
The court held that under the circumstances when the averment of Petitioner is not controverted, this Court proceeds on the basis that averment has been admitted.
