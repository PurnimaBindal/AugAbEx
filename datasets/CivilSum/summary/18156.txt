Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation for SC/BCs - Domiciles of State concerned - Origin of candidate from Delhi - Candidate applying for recruitment in Haryana State employment - There was no specific condition in the advertisement that only domiciles of Haryana will be considered for reserved vacancies.
Therefore, the candidature of an SC candidate cannot be ignored simply because he originates from Delhi.
