Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab State Class IV Service Rules, 1963, Rule 5(d) - 3rd Punjab Pay Commission Recommendation dated 26.10.1988 - Post of helper - The petitioner, an under matric, has worked on the post of helper for 14 years and is duly qualified as recommended by the 3rd Punjab Pay Commission.
The acceptance of the Pay Commission's recommendations by the State of Punjab binds them, and administrative instructions cannot override statutory rules.
