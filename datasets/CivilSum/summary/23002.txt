Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Standing orders 9312 - Absence from duty - Automatic termination - Natural justice - Even if a rule provides automatic termination due to 10 days consecutive absence without leave, the Doctrine of natural justice must be read inherent in every rule - The employer must comply with the provisions of natural justice, as the employee may be in a position to explain a sufficient cause for their absence without leave - Termination was considered illegal retrenchment and not sustained by the Labour Court.
