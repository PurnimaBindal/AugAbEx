Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment/Reservation - If a candidate is the dependent of a deceased employee, they are in a different situation than general candidates and therefore cannot claim parity of treatment with them.
This does not violate Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
