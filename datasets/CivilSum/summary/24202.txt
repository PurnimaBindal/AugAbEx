Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39(d) - Pay Parity - Discrimination - Between part-time and permanent BWHGs (Boarder Wings Home Guard) - The work of both permanent and part-time BWHGs is the same and performed under the same circumstances.
Part-time BWHGs perform their work regularly, and the scheme did not discontinue them after three months.
They have been continuously deployed for long numbers of years, which negates the voluntary character of the scheme.
The scheme was not implemented as framed.
Part-time BWHGs are guarding the border of the country, checking insulation from across the border, and under the control of the authority of BSF.
Hence, they cannot be treated differently from permanent staff of BWHGs.
[Paras 17, 18 & 19]
