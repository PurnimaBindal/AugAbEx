Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16(4-B) - Reservation - Carry- forward of unfilled reserved vacancies - Excessive reservation - Conversion of reserved vacancies to general - The principle stated that a maximum of 50% reservation would not apply to carried forward vacancies, but would apply only once to vacancies of the particular year of recruitment.
All vacancies reserved by applying the 50% principle would be filled in their respective categories.
As a result, it is not possible to convert such vacancies to general.
