Constitution of India, Article 16 - Education - Research work - Appointment - Termination of services - The petitioner, who was appointed as a Lecturer in Pharmacy, was sponsored by the university to undergo Post Doctoral Research work in Germany for a minimum period of 16 months, which could be extended for one more year.
The petitioner applied for an extension of one year, which was rejected by the university, leading to the termination of his services.
The court held that the university's decision was illegal.
