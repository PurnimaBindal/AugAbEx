Pension Regulations for Army - Army Act, 1950, Section 193A - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, Rule 55A - Constitution of India, Article 309 - Dearness Relief - Pension Rules are statutory in nature - Pension Regulations based on administrative instructions can be modified by administrative instructions - Dearness Relief can be terminated on re-employment.
[Paras 7 and 11]  B. Dearness allowance/Dearness Relief - Dearness Relief and Dearness Allowance are the same, but Dearness Relief is not a part of pension - Denial of Dearness Relief to re-employed ex-servicemen in government service or its bodies does not deprive them of the right of possession, as per Para 16, 17, and 18.
C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Ex-servicemen/Classification of - Ex-servicemen who have been re-employed and are receiving a pension and Dearness Relief are a separate class - They are not eligible for Dearness Relief on Pension - The difference is intelligent, logical, reasonable, and impartial.
[Para 18] D. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Ex-servicemen - Government re-employment is not equal to private employment or business - Because the government does not have jurisdiction over private business or employment.
