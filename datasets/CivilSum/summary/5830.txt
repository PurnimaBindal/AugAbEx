According to the Constitution of India, specific criteria apply for the appointment and termination of an Advocate as a Government Pleader and Public Prosecutor.
In the State of Madhya Pradesh, this is governed by the Law Manual, and appointment can be terminated by notice as required under the Rules.
The termination order should have some basis on government file, although reasons need not be communicated to the appointee.
The tenure of a government pleader may not exceed three years and can be brought to an end by giving one month's notice.
If challenged, a court may scrutinize the government file to ensure that the order is not mala fide or based on extraneous consideration.
In a specific case, the State Government terminated the services of the Government Pleader based on concerns raised by the High Court, and this order was upheld.
