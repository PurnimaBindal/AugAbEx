Punjab State Assistant Grade Examination Rules, 1984, Rules 10 and 4 - Promotion - Seniority and qualifying test - Both eligible candidates who passed the examination and those with 18 years of service should be considered for promotion to the post of Assistant on the basis of seniority-cum-merit.
Exemptees, who are exempt from the qualifying examination after 18 years of service as a clerk, cannot obliterate the chance for qualifiers of promotion, and if possible, qualifiers should be given some share in vacancies over exemptees.
The court has given specific directions on filling vacancies based on the qualifications of the applicants.
