Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - Grant of - Petitioner examined medically at the time of enrolment in Air Force and was categorized as `A' - Invalidated out of service due to medical unfitness and placed in category "EEE" - Petitioner suffers from `Paraoxymal Arterial Techycardia', a heart problem common among serving Jawans in high altitude - The disease is reasonably inferred to be attributable to army service - Petitioner entitled to disability pension - Respondent-authorities directed to release disability pension benefit to petitioner for 38 months prior to filing of writ petition.
