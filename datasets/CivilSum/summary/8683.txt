In accordance with the Constitution of India, in regards to disciplinary proceedings and suspension, a Headmistress from a private school sought an interim relief order to prevent possible mischievous action from the management.
The court issued a mandamus ordering that the respondents are restrained from suspending the petitioner, yet the management proceeded to issue a charge sheet without following proper procedure and without affording the petitioner any opportunity.
The proceedings were declared illegal and quashed due to violating the mandamus and rules, but the management complied with an interim order and paid subsistence allowance to the petitioner, allowing her to rejoin her duties with the liberty to proceed in accordance with the law.
