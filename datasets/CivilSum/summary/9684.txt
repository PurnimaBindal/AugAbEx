The permissibility of simultaneous departmental and criminal proceedings under Constitution of India, Article 311 is determined by various essential conditions including whether the case is of grave nature with complicated questions of facts and law.
It has been held that in the absence of a charge-sheet in criminal proceedings, it cannot be determined whether these conditions are met.
The intention of the petitioner to cheat and misappropriate will be scrutinized during trial, while the charge-sheet in disciplinary proceedings mentions the petitioner's failure to maintain proper register and entries according to bank rules.
Therefore, it cannot be assumed that evidence in both proceedings will be identical and based on the same set of facts.
Consequently, criminal and departmental proceedings can proceed simultaneously.
