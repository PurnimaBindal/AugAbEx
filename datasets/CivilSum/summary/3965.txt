Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Promotion - Enquiry - Promotion during pendency of enquiry - Successive enquiries - Departmental proceedings - Sealed cover promotion - An employee's record only needs to be considered up to the date of consideration for promotion, and if there is a pending enquiry at that time, the sealed cover method may be used or the promotion may be kept in abeyance until the enquiry is completed - If the employee is exonerated, the promotion cannot be withheld due to ongoing enquiries - If this procedure is not followed, an officer may never receive a promotion - If a subsequent proceeding finds an employee guilty, they may be punished in their promoted rank, but their promotion cannot be withheld based on the earlier exoneration - The petitioner is directed to be promoted from the date of the DPC's recommendations.
[Paras 8 and 9]
