UP Forest Service Rules, 1952, Rules 5(b), 6 and 24 - Seniority/Temporary post - Asst.
Conservator, Forest - The seniority of direct recruits and promotees in their respective quotas must be determined, taking into account the fixation of inter se seniority.
Moreover, the temporary posts likely to continue for an extended period should be treated as permanent while counting seniority.
The State Government must follow a specific procedure to determine inter-se seniority.
