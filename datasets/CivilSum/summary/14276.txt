Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Quashing - Alleged misappropriation charge against petitioner - Petitioner is a member of Indian Police Service - The offences occurred within the State of Jammu & Kashmir - Petitioner claimed that the charge framed under IPC and the prosecution ordered under Section 197 of Central Code of Cr.PC is unlawful as both codes are inapplicable to J&K - Determined that only the Ranbir Penal Code governs charges initiated within the State of J&K - Prosecution must be sanctioned under Section 197 of State Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898, not Central Code of Cr.PC - Impugned order deemed illegal and quashed - However, the UOI retains the liberty to issue a fresh order sanctioning prosecution of the petitioner.
