Constitution of India, Article 226 - Contract of service - Termination - Mala fides - The petitioner desires to enforce a pure contract of service which is not governed by statutory provisions - There are no grounds compelling the respondents to maintain the petitioner in service for the entirety of the contract period - Nevertheless, the petitioner is entitled to hold respondent No. 2 accountable for damages should he be able to demonstrate that he has been mistreated and refused his right to fulfill the entire term of the contract.
