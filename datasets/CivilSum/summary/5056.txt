Under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(j), and 25-F, the responsibility of ascertaining whether or not the party proceeded against in an industrial dispute is an industry within the meaning of Section 2(j) falls on the shoulders of the workman.
The Labour Court, in this case, placed onus on management that it was not industry within the meaning of Section 2(g) and recorded a false finding.
The court did not follow the procedure laid down by the Supreme Court in Pratamsingh Narsinh Parmar's case, 2001(2) SCT 1081 (SC).
Consequently, the matter has been remanded back to the Labour Court to re-examine the dispute.
