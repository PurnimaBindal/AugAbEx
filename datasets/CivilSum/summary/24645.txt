The Geological Survey of India (Group 'A' and Group 'B' Posts) Recruitment Rules, 1967, Rule 1 has been amended to make a degree in Engineering a requisite qualification for promotion to the post of Director (ME).
An employee who was eligible for promotion under the original rules but did not possess the requisite degree cannot challenge the amendment on the grounds of retrospective effect.
The appeal for this matter has been dismissed.

The rules for promotion to the post of Director (ME) under the Geological Survey of India (Group 'A' and Group 'B' posts) Recruitment Rules, 1967, have been amended to require a degree in Engineering.
The requirement for a graduate degree for the post of Deputy Director General (Engineering Service) does not make the degree requirement for Director (Mechanical) unreasonable or bad in law.
The government has the authority to decide the qualifications necessary for the position of Director (ME), which cannot be considered bad in law unless completely irrelevant or unreasonable.
The appeal for this matter has been dismissed.
