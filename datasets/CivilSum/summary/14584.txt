Pension Regulations for the Air Force, 1961 - Disability pension - The Controller of Defence Accounts (Pension) and the Medical Board have jurisdiction in assessing and holding degree of disability, with the Controller not always being bound to accept the opinion of the Board, but if this is overruled, cogent reasons must be given following the prescribed procedures.
The denial of disability pension with no reason for differing with the opinion of the Board when the disability was above 20% was illegal and arbitrary, and the petitioner was directed to be paid disability pension with arrears.
