Provisions in Para 5(2) of the Presidential order can be utilized by State Government in favor of a specific individual for the benefit of administration, even if a Rule framed under the proviso infringes the Presidential order.
The tribunal challenged the rules established under the Proviso to Article 209 of the Constitution, arguing that they violated the Presidential order issued under Article 371-D of the Constitution The Supreme Court has given overriding power to the State in the public interest and administrative urgencies.
However, it was held that the rule contravening the Presidential order is susceptible to being stricken down.
A hearing was ordered to be placed before a Three Judge Bench of the Supreme Court since the sitting judges do not agree with the decisions already delivered by Two Judge Bench.
