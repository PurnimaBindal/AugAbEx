As stated in Article 226 of the Constitution of India, disability pension is granted when a petitioner's disease is found to be attributable and aggravated to Army Service, as determined by a medical board evaluation.
The petitioner, who was initially placed in category "AYE" during a medical examination upon joining Army Service, was later downgraded to category CEE(T) at time of discharge, and there is no indication of any intervening events that could have been responsible for his condition.
Consequently, the petitioner is eligible for disability pension.
