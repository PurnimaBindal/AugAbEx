Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 10 - Contempt of Subordinate Courts by Govt. Officers - Judicial orders must be obeyed by Govt. officers - Deliberate disobedience of judicial orders is a reprehensible act and interferes with the administration of justice - Govt. officers must not give the impression that orders of subordinate courts can be ignored - Though contemnors can be punished, if they apologize unconditionally, the rule is discharged and the contempt matter is settled.
[Paras 2 & 3]
