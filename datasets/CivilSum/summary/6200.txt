Admission - PMT a joint entrance test - Admission to MBBS and BDS Courses - The petitioner sought admission to MBBS only and was not considered for admission to BDS.
The admission form requires the candidate to give their option for both courses or for one, and the petitioner clearly mentioned 'MBBS only' in column No. 1 of the admission form.
Therefore, the petitioner cannot be granted admission to the B.D.S.
Course.
[Paras 11 and 17]

Admission - Admission form - Practice - Students seeking admission to professional colleges are mature enough to understand the full implications of filling admission forms.
The forms are invariably signed by their parents or guardians, and the candidate is bound by the information supplied in the admission form.
The student cannot take a stand that may suit them at a given time.
[Para 14]

Admission - Prospectus - MBBS and BDS Courses - The candidate did not fill the required column for opting names of colleges in order of preference if selected in the B.D.S.
Course.
They wrote "MBBS only" against column No. 1, leaving the column blank.
Therefore, the college that granted admission had no option but to grant admission as per the options exercised in the admission form and as per the prospectus.
The candidate is bound by what is written and cannot easily wriggle out of it.
Hence, the petitioner cannot be granted admission to the B.D.S.
Course.
[Paras 11, 15, 17, and 18]

Admission - Prospectus - Eligibility for admission to a course has to be seen according to the prospectus issued before the entrance examination.
Admission has to be made based on the instructions given in the prospectus.
[Para 15]
