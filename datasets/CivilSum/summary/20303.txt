According to the Constitution of India, under Articles 215 and 32, a decision made on the basis of concessions made by direct recruits cannot be held as a decision on merit, and Union of India being an affected party is not obliged to follow such decision.
[Para 12]
As per the Constitution of India under Articles 14 and 32, seniority and promotions of direct recruits and promotees must follow the Quota Rule, which has not been broken in this case.
There is no need to go into further legal aspects consequent to this fact, unless it is disputed.
