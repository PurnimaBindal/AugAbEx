According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, candidates on a waiting list can be appointed to a post if it remains vacant due to a non-joining candidate.
The expiry of the waiting list during court proceedings does not affect the right of the petitioner to appointment.
The validity period of a select list is a matter of fact, and cannot be raised for the first time during an appeal.
[Para 9]
Non-joinder of necessary parties cannot be raised for the first time in a Supreme Court appeal according to Articles 226 and 136 of the Constitution of India.
