Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 7.2 - Appointment - Misrepresentation for getting appointment - Dishonest misrepresentations that led to a government employee's appointment can result in termination without the need for a regular inquiry, as specified in the conditions of their appointment.
