The Uttar Pradesh Recruitment of Dependents of Government Servants Dying in Harness Rules, 1974, Rules 4, 5 and 8, provide for compassionate appointment by relaxing certain conditions, but cannot override all other provisions related to recruitment for posts outside the scope of the UP Public Service Commission and the Rules and Acts established under Art. 309 of the Constitution.
In particular, Rules 5 and 8 specify which requirements are to be relaxed, and to what extent.
While the procedural requirements for selection can be waived, the appointing authority may still conduct an interview to ensure that the candidate can meet the expected minimum standards of work and efficiency for the position.
