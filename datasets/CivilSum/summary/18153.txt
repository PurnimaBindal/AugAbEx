Military Engineer Services (Industrial Group `C') Recruitment Rules, 1990 - Appointment - Promotion - Appointments and promotions for group 'C' posts, specifically for electrician and painter, must follow the Rules that have been established.
The respondents cannot claim to be placed in the skilled grade simply after completing a two year probation period, as promotions are subject to passing a prescribed trade test and the availability of vacancies at the time.
These rules are clearly outlined in the Instructions dated 821988 and must be followed accordingly.
