Constitution of India, Article 311 - Jammu and Kashmir Administrative Officers (Gazetted Service) Recruitment Rules of 1996 - Despite respondent No. 5 being asked to assume charge from the retiring incumbent, he was not to receive any additional benefits.
The mere assignment of current duties charge, however, does not equate to conferral of actual status.
Petitioner became eligible for retrospective benefit under a later order.
Both petitioner and respondent No. 5's claims must be re-examined in accordance with the 1996 Rules.
Respondents are directed to consider the petitioner's case, and it must be noted that the mere holding of current duties by respondent No. 5 does not warrant automatic induction into the Service under the Rules of 1996.
