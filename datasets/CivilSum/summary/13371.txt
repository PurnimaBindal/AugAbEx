As per the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 300-A, ad hoc employees are entitled to be regularised in service after five years of continuous service as per the state government's instructions.
Regularisation following ad hoc service would be considered towards qualifying service for pension calculations.
In a writ petition, the authorities recognised a petition's entitlement to regularisation after five years of ad hoc service.
Delayed orders of regularisation cannot disqualify an employee from receiving pension by stating that they did not complete their qualifying regular service.
