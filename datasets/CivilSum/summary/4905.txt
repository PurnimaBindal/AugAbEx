The petitioner, who served the State of Punjab, was wrongly denied the benefit of military service towards increments and seniority under the Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rules 3 and 4, on the basis of a proviso which did not apply to the rules of the State of Punjab.
Therefore, the action of the respondents was illegal and the petitioner is entitled to receive the benefit of military service towards increment and seniority, according to Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
