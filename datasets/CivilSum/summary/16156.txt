Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 227 - Punjabi University Ordinances, Ordinance 7(ii) - M.B.B.S/B.D.S Course - Admission - Examination - All India Pre-Medical Examination - Petitioners notified via a letter dated 1171991 that they were selected for admission with a deadline for admission set for 31.10.1991 - After admission, they attended over 75% of total lectures - However, they were not permitted to sit for the first professional examination scheduled for 15.1.1993, due to Director General of Health Services' delayed response - Other students admitted prior to 31.10.1991 who also had not completed 1-1/2 years of study allowed to take the examination - Court held that the delay was not the fault of the petitioners and they cannot be held ineligible - Respondents' action was ruled illegal and arbitrary - Petition allowed.
