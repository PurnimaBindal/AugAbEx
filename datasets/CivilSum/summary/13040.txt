Constitution of India, Article 21 - Medical Reimbursement for post-operative follow-up treatment as indoor patient - Kidney Transplantation - While the Corporation policy may reimburse in-door treatment expenses and compensate out-door treatment through a fixed monthly medical allowance, it is also necessary to reimburse the post-operative follow-up treatment that is a continuation of in-door treatment, as this is not represented by the monthly allowance.
[Paras 7-10]
