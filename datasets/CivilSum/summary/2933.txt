Article 226 of the Indian Constitution dictates that if an employee is terminated without a proper enquiry, they are entitled to re-instatement to their post, but are not eligible for back wages during the period of absence.

Article 226 of the Indian Constitution addresses the issue of migrant salary, and it was held that a petitioner registered as a migrant with the Government Medical College, Jammu Migrant Cell but residing in Delhi is only eligible for migrant salary if they reside within the local limits of the State's jurisdiction, and not while they are residing in Delhi.
