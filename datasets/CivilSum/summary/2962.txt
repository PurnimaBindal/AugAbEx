Constitution of India, Article 226 - Necessary party - Impleadment of - A petition was filed against the award of Labour Court by an Executive Engineer in an individual capacity.
The petition was dismissed on grounds that it was not maintainable in the absence of the State being a petitioner.
The contention was that the concerned Executive Engineer was the only one who could file the petition since the State was not the employer.
Furthermore, it was argued that the reference was made by the State Government under Section 10 of the Act, so the petition could not be maintained on behalf of the State.
It was held that the State Government is a necessary party in a writ petition challenging the award where the workman is an employee of the State.
It is up to the court's discretion to add or implead proper parties for completely deciding all questions.
Hence, leave to add the State as a co-petitioner was granted on oral request.
The matter was referred to a single judge for a decision on merit.
Appeal allowed accordingly.
[Paras 7 and 8]
