According to Army Pension Regulations 1961, Regulation 173 and Rules 7(c) & (b) of Appendix -II, a petitioner suffering from 'schizo affective disorder' within three months of enlisting as a soldier in the Army is not entitled to disability pension.
The Invalidating Medical Board opined that the disability was unrelated to military service or service conditions, and therefore, there was no recommendation for the grant of a disability pension.
If there is no evidence showing that the disease was related to or worsened by military service, the rejection of the claim cannot be deemed illegal or arbitrary.
