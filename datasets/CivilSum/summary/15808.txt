Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Removal - Enquiry Report - Opportunity of hearing - Natural justice - The respondent was removed from service without being provided with a copy of the enquiry report.
The respondent was not given the opportunity to demonstrate that the findings of the enquiry officer were not in accordance with the evidence on record.
Additionally, no opportunity was given to represent against the proposed penalty.
The respondent has stated that he will not claim any arrears of salary if allowed to continue working on the post.
The order of punishment is illegal.
[Paras 3 and 4]
