Regarding the starting of a post graduate course in a Homoeopathic College, it is held that a college with an existing undergraduate teaching facility is not required to obtain a no objection certificate from the State Government, according to the Homoeopathy (Post Graduate Degree Course) Regulations, 1989, Regulations 2 and 9.
This is held under the Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227, the Homeopathy Central Council Act, 1973, Sections 20(2), and 33.
