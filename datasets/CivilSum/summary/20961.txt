Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 136 - Recruitment - Appointment - List of recruits for the posts of Sepoys without vacancies, quashed - The preparation of such a list is not in the public interest and must not be done as an allurement to poor unemployed youngsters - Authorities directed to make selection afresh following the required Rules and procedure - In cases where the applicants were affected by the impugned selection, the condition of the age bar should be relaxed - Errant officers recommended to face disciplinary action.
