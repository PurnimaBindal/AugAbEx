Article 309 of the Constitution of India and Section 124 of the Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir both concern recruitment and conditions of service, and provide for plenary legislation rather than delegated legislation.
Until the State Legislature enacts a law to regulate a condition of service, the Governor or delegate may regulate it through rules.
[Para 14] B.
Raising the retirement age of a government servant is a condition of service that cannot be achieved through an executive order alone, but must be accomplished through legislative enactment under Section 124.
[Para 14] C. The government order dealt only with the grant of implementation of UGC scales to teachers of Government Degree Colleges from 111988, and did not address raising the retirement age of these teachers.
Unless the government specifically mentions the raising of retirement age in the order, it cannot be assumed to be part of the order.
