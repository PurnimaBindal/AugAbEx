According to the Constitution of India, Article 30(1), the Punjab Affiliated College Act, 1974, Section 4, and the Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service of Employees) Rules, 1978, Rule 3, the rules govern the service conditions of teachers.
Natural Justice dictates that terminating an employee's services goes against the rules which provide security of service.
This action gives cause for a competent writ petition against it.
Rule 3 outlines only the minimum requirements of principles of natural justice before taking action against employees.
Institutions receiving grant-in-aid from the government must follow the regulatory provisions of the rules, and such a rule cannot be said to be violative of Article 30(1) of the Constitution.
