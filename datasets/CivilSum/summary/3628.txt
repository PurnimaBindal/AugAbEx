Revised Pension Rules, 1950, Rule 8 - Pension - Govt. employee - Resignation from service - The rules state that the payment of pension is only envisaged for employees who retire from service.
Thus, any employee who resigns from service is not entitled to pension benefits.
However, the petitioner retired in 1976 and did not claim pension until 2000, therefore their claim is barred due to date, latches, and acquiescence.
