In disciplinary proceedings, a joint enquiry and joint charge-sheet can be conducted.
Each officer charged together can cross-examine each other.
The concept of co-accused does not apply.

When it is determined that one officer received an unjustly lenient punishment, it would be wrong to repeat the mistake and impose a lesser punishment on another officer.
Doing so would be discriminatory.
