Rajasthan Medical Service (Collegiate Branch) Rules, 1962, Rule 11 - Age relaxation - Temporary appointment - Appointment to post of Civil Assistant Surgeon - The upper age limit for direct recruitment to the post of Lecturer in Skin/V.D.
is 40 years for individuals who have entered into service as Temporary employees before crossing the age of 35 years.
Promotees must receive one increment of higher responsibility.
[Paras 5 & 6] B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Eligibility - Temporary appointment - The petitioner's services were terminated for regular appointment, which held him ineligible for the post of Lecturer due to exceeding the upper age limit.
The petitioner has valid grounds to rebut the objection of age limit and deserves an opportunity to explain the termination.
The order must be quashed.
