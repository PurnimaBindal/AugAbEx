Constitution of India, Article 136 - Orissa University Act, 1989, Section 5(10) - Appointment - Termination of - Qualification stipulated - Appellant, who applied for a post of Lecturer in Political Science, was appointed on recommendation by the selection committee.
However, the Chancellor issued a show cause notice to cancel the appointment, citing lack of minimum academic qualification.
The appointment was annulled, and the High Court refused to set aside the order.
The Department for which appointment is to be made is that of Political Science and Public Administration, while the appointment disputed is that of Political Science.
The UGC rejected the case for the relaxation of the minimum prescribed qualification.
It was held that the UGC is to have the last word on the claim of appellant and that merely because a post is available in a department, it cannot dispense with the essential academic qualification in the relevant subject.
As a result, the appeal was dismissed.
