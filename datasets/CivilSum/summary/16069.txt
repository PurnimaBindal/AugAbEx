Rajasthan Transport Subordinate Service Rules, 1963, Entry No. 2 - Rajasthan (Revised) Pay Scale Rules, 1983 - Pay scales - Motor Vehicles Inspectors - The petitioner did not meet the qualifications needed for pay scale 640-1180 due to not being a matriculate or having passed the Departmental Examination after completing Course in Automobile Engineering.
