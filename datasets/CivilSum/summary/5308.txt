Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - The petitioner, an untrained teacher, received selection grade after 15 years of service in accordance with prevailing rules - In 1995, directions were revised and made applicable only after untrained teachers completed 10 years of service, with overpayments to be recovered - Petitioner became entitled to selection grade only after 1992, but revision of directions and recovery of payments were challenged as violative of natural justice - It was held that such violations could not be sustained and proceedings were quashed, while recovery could not be initiated and refunds were to be made if any.
