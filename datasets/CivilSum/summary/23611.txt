Constitution of India, Articles 319 and 226 - Selection - Challenging irregularities in selection - Validity of selection - Appointments for Assistant Jailers - Despite recognizing irregularities in recommendations made by SSS Board, the High Court directed appointments to those recommended - SSS Board had already been dissolved and had no authority to release a revised list - Revised list provided by the Board considered as being without authority - Nevertheless, appointments that had been made would not be interfered with.
