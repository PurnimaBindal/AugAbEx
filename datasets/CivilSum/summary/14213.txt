Rule 3.17 and 17-A of Punjab Civil Services Rules, as well as the Punjab Govt. notification from May 25, 1982, state that ad hoc service must be included in pension calculations.
The notification covers employees who served on a temporary basis in the Central Govt./State Govt. prior to joining State Govt. service through proper channel.
Rule 3.17 only applies to individuals who voluntarily transfer from Central to State Govt. service.
However, those who were selected in State Service while serving on ad hoc or temporary basis in Central or State service through the proper channel must follow the instructions dated 2551982.
Regardless of whether the employee resigned from the ad hoc/temporary post held by him in the Central/State services, they are entitled to the benefit of such service towards pension according to the instructions of the Punjab Govt. from March 25, 1982.
