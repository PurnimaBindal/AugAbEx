Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 21 - Compassionate appointment - The concept of Compassionate appointment is an exception to the general rule and cannot be substituted for the general rule to exclude the rights of all other eligible individuals to compete for the role.
Compassionate appointment may only be offered in unique circumstances in accordance with the Rules or policy established for that purpose.
Without any Rules or State policy, the compassion cannot be considered a right and may violate Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Industrial Disputes - Contract of work - Employment - If there is a proper contract of work and the contractor has been given all the benefits to engage the labour for the performance of the contract, the Labour Court or the High Court cannot order reinstatement, particularly if no appointment with the employer was needed, without any material before it.
