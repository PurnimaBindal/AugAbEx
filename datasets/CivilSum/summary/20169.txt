Constitution of India, Articles 136 and 300-A - Option for pension - Delay/laches - The petitioner, who was appointed as a clerk on 1141950 and resigned from service on 31.7.1963 opting to take Provident Fund, is not entitled for a change in his option for pension in the year 1992, nearly after 28 years - as accepting the amount of Provident Fund offered to him, the petitioner is guilty of laches.
