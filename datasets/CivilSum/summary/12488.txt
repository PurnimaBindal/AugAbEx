Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Part II, Rule 2.2(c) - Gratuity/Enquiry - Contemplated inquiry - An inquiry is considered initiated only when a charge-sheet is framed and issued by the competent authority, not when a notice is issued by an inferior officer - Withholding gratuity due to a notice issued by an inferior officer is illegal - The respondents are directed to pay the petitioner's gratuity with 12% interest.
