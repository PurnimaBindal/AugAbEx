Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dismissal from service - Enquiry Officer did not provide reasons for finding the petitioner guilty of charges - Management and Enquiry Officer failed to consider the impact of dismissal on the petitioner's livelihood and reputation - Both parties were required to analyze evidence and provide reasons for their decision - Dismissal order overturned - Petitioner to be reinstated.
