According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, the Authority selected the Respondent for the position of Driver.
However, no appointment order was issued to him because he failed to respond to a paper notification given by the Corporation to attend duty during the workers' strike period.
The Respondent did not receive an individual notice or call letter instructing him to join duty.
The Corporation's failure to issue an appointment order after completing the selection process is unacceptable.
The Authority is directed to reconsider the appointment of the Respondent, despite his failure to attend duty during the strike period.
