According to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) (c) and UP Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 4-K, if a strike is determined to be illegal and results in a lock-out, the lock-out is considered legal and justified.
The principle of "no work no pay" applies to public utility services, and wages during a strike are only payable if it is both legal and justified.
Forms of strikes include go-slow, work to rule, refusal to work overtime, irritation strikes, etc.
Therefore, workmen are not entitled to payment of wages for the period of a lock-out resulting from an illegal strike organized by workmen.
