According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, a person who is selected, promoted, or appointed to a higher post cannot be denied the pay of the higher post if they are required to discharge duties and responsibilities of a higher degree.
The petitioner in this case was appointed as a Driving Instructor in a higher pay scale while working as a Mechanic.
However, the appointment letter entitled him to work in his previous pay scale instead.
Such a unilateral condition, which is arbitrary and illegal, cannot be sustained.
