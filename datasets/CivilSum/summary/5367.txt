According to Sections 2(9), 49, and 51 of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, an employee who contributed during a certain period and then ceased to be defined as an employee due to a change in wage level is still entitled to permanent disablement benefits if an accident occurs.
This ruling was made in the case of an employee who contributed from 1486 to 30.9.86, saw an increase in wages on 1.10.86, and experienced an accident on 15687.
