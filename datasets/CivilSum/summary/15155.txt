Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 148 - Appointment - Selection - Post of Auditor - The recruiting agency can only appoint to advertised posts, unless the statute allows for exceeding advertised posts or deviating from the number of vacancies notified - Four posts of auditors and one post of clerks were advertised - However, the two posts of auditors were converted into two additional posts of clerks - This is not appropriate.
[Paras 9 and 10]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 148 - Appointment - Selection - Eligibility - The notification called for the best sportsmen from both hockey and table tennis disciplines to fill the advertised post - It is not valid to argue that the four posts of auditors were to be filled only by hockey players and that the post of clerk should be filled by table tennis players.
