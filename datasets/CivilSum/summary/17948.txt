Kerala State and Subordinate Services Rules, 1958, Rule 28(bbb) - Qualifying Test/Date of passing test/Seniority/Promotion - Promotions from L.D.C.
to UDC are dependent upon the passing of a test - Some junior L.D.C.s passed the test before certain seniors - Promotions were made on 1841970, retroactively from 27.11.1969, even though the seniors were not test-qualified at that time - Candidates cannot be considered test-qualified before the results are declared, even if the test was already taken - November 27, 1969 is the seniority cutoff date - L.D.C.s who passed the test on 21.11.69 will have seniority over seniors who did not pass the test on that date.
