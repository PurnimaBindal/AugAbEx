As per Section 42 of the Warehousing Corporation Act, 1962 and Regulation 37 of the Rajasthan State Warehousing Corporation (Staff) Regulations 1974, seniority cannot be challenged due to delay or estoppel.
In this case, petitioners took advantage of a promotion and only challenged the decision to create a new post of ASPO six years later.
Due to their conduct and delay, the settled position of seniority should not be disturbed.
