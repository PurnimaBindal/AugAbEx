Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Reinstatement - Backwages - The order of dismissal of workman was set aside and reinstatement was ordered, but backwages were denied.
However, it is held that payment of backwages on reinstatement is a normal consequence to follow, and the workman is entitled to all benefits of service as if the contract of service continues.
[Paras 13 & 14]
