As per the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F, termination of services of a workman in a master-servant relationship must be established.
In this case, the driver of the bank's vehicle transporting currency to different branches was considered a workman and reinstatement was ordered due to the establishment of the master and servant relationship.
The bank's claim of the driver being a personal driver of its Sr.
Manager was not supported by sufficient evidence.
[Paras 11 and 12] B.
With regards to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F, the award of reinstatement with full back wages was considered excessive due to the short period of service and lack of evidence of the workman not being gainfully employed during the termination period.
The award was modified to only include reinstatement and 25% back wages in accordance with the doctrine of no work no pay and the facts of the case.
