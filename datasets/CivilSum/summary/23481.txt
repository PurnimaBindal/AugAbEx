According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, admission to medical and dental colleges in the State of Haryana is subject to eligibility requirements, including having completed 10th, 10 + 1 and 10 + 2 from a recognized institution in the Haryana State.
In terms of admissions, it has been established that preference based on residence and residential preference is acceptable, provided that reservation on those grounds does not lead to full reservation.
The eligibility condition applies only to 85% of seats, with the 15% allotted for the All India quota being exempt from the reservation.
The State Government's decision to implement these conditions aligns with the Supreme Court's ruling, and it does not violate Article 14 or 16.
