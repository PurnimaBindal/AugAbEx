Constitution of India, Article 311 - Limitation Act, 1963, Article 113 - Dismissal from service - If an employee wishes to contest an illegal order of dismissal from service, they have three years from the date of order or from the date of order of appellate authority if an appeal is filed to do so.
If the employee does not file a suit within this three-year period, their right to challenge the dismissal order is lost.
It is important to note that an illegal order cannot be ignored without first obtaining a court order to have it set aside.
