Employees State Insurance Act, 1948 (Central Act No. 34 of 1948), Sections 51(a) and 75(1)(g) - Death of workman - Compensation - In the case of death on the way to employment or during employment, employer liability determined - ESI court denied compensation on the basis that the death was not in course and out of employment - It is generally agreed that compensation requires an accident within or during the period of employment - Presumption under Section 51-A as to accident had arisen "in course of employment" would only arise if it was established that an accident occurred while the deceased was on duty - The appeal was dismissed due to the fact that the deceased had not yet begun his employment responsibilities.
