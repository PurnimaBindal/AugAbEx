Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 -Selection Grade - Promotion - Adverse remarks - Uncommunicated adverse remarks Selection grade and promotion were withheld due to adverse remarks in AC - Adverse remarks were not communicated in a timely manner - It has been held that confidential reports need to be recorded without delay - When three consecutive ACRs.
giving adverse reports were delayed in their communication, it gave a valuable right to the respondent - Thus, belated adverse reports should not be considered as stale entries - Moreover, there were no complaints of dishonesty or corruption against the respondent - Therefore, such ACRs.
could not be a hindrance in granting selection grade and considering the case for promotion.
[Paras 3, 4, 9 and 10]
