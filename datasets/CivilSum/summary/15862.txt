Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Punjab Public Works Department (Irrigation Branch) Patwaris State Service Class III Rules, 1955, Rule 10(iii) - Appointment by transfer - Advertisement - Patwaris of Irrigation Branch of PWD (B & R) department are employees of State Government - They have the opportunity to be appointed as Revenue Patwaris by transfer under Rule 10(iii) - Appointments were challenged on the grounds of lack of opportunity for similarly situated employees from other departments, but the challenge did not extend to a violation of Article 14 - No eligible candidates filed a writ of quo-warranto - The appointment of the petitioners on the posts of Revenue Patwaris, given their promotion, was not flawed - The appeal was allowed.
