Civil Procedure Code, Order 41 Rule 22 - Cross objections - Respondents are not obligated to file cross objections and are entitled to defend their claim if they are not seeking any further benefit, which has already been granted in the judgment they are challenging.
[Para 5] B Punjab Public Works Department (Buildings and Roads Branch) Chief Engineer's Office (Class III-Ministerial) Service Rules, 1967, Rule 2 Sub-clauses (c), (d), (j) and (m) and Rule 10 - Seniority/appointment to service - An appointment to the ex-cadre post implies an appointment to service and the order of merit as determined by the Board is to be upheld, with the temporary appointees' seniority determined below that of personnel appointed by the Board on that date.
[Para 8] C Punjab Public Works Department (Buildings and Roads Branch) Chief Engineer's Office (Class III-Ministerial) Service Rules, 1967, Rule 2 sub-clauses (c), (d), (j) and (m) and Rule 10 - Seniority/temporary service/ad hoc service - The rules require that the period spent on a temporary post/ex-cadre post be counted for seniority, whereas the period of service spent on an ad hoc basis should not be counted towards seniority.
Any orders that contradict the rules and count ad hoc service towards seniority are invalid.
[Para 8] D. Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Public Works Department (Buildings and Roads Branch) Chief Engineer's Office (Class III-Ministerial) Service Rules, 1967, Rule 2, Sub-clauses (c), (d), (j) and (m) and Rule 10 - Seniority - Appointment in service - The appointment in service refers to the initial appointment, either temporary or against a temporary post, and the member remains in continuous service until their services are regularised.
The entire service period counts towards seniority in such situations.
