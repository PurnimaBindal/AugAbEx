Contempt of Courts Act, Sections 11 and 12 - Wilful disobedience - Legal advice - The interpretation of the judgment based on legal advice of a senior advocate is considered bona fide.
Advocates who admit to giving advice that is not inconsistent with the letter and spirit of the judgment will not be guilty of wilful or deliberate disobedience in implementing court orders.
Rule discharged - The court has issued directions for the implementation of its orders within three months.
