Civil Procedure Code, Order 13 Rule 5 - Pension/Freedom-fighter's Pension/Admission/Specific denial - The fact that the petitioner served as a freedom-fighter in INA and remained in Jail for 7 months is taken as admitted because there was no specific denial to the averments of fact.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to pension with arrears and interest @12% and costs of the petition, which amounts to Rs. 3000/-.
