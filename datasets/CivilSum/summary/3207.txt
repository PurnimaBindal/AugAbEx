Constitution of India, Articles 12, 14, 16 and 226 - Work charge employees/Termination - The instrumentalities of State must act fairly and without undue discrimination towards employees who were recruited for a project and were to be given a fair deal and absorbed in other projects, according to the Rules, Memorandum of Understanding, and correspondence.
Their acts should not be arbitrary and must adhere to the standards they profess to follow.
Petitioners who continued to work in a project sponsored by ICAR for more than six years and up to 16 years in one case as Researchists should not be turned out of employment based solely on the closure of a particular project.
[Para 10]  B Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 311 - Termination - In the case of termination, the Appointing Authority/Competent Authority is the Registrar.
The termination order issued by the Deputy Registrar is not valid because he is an inferior person.
[Para 12]  C Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and laches - There is a writ petition challenging the termination filed by the scientists 11 months after approaching the authorities unsuccessfully.
Though there is a delay, the writ cannot be dismissed on the grounds of delay alone, and the delay has been explained.
