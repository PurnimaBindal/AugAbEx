Constitution of India, Article 226 - Ayurvedic Department (Class III Ministerial Service) Rules, 1963 - Transfer - A petitioner was transferred from their original department of Homeopathy to the Ayurvedic department - The ministerial staff of both departments can be freely interchanged - The transferring authority is not accused of any wrongdoing - The government was within its rights under the Rules to transfer the petitioner - There is no reason to contest the order of transfer.
