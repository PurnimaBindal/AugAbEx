Railway Establishment (Volume I - Revised Edition, 1989), Para 302 - Inter se seniority - The filling of Senior Clerk vacancies requires selecting from three streams, including Grade I Clerks, direct recruits from the open market, limited recruit graduate Grade II Clerks and graduate promotee-Grade II Clerks.
Para 302 will determine seniority between direct recruits and limited recruitment graduate Grade II Clerks and promotees.
The seniority of ad hoc appointees will not be granted from their initial appointment, but from their actual selection and appointment in accordance with the Rules.
Their appointment and seniority will be effective from the date of selection after due completion of the process, making them junior to in-service and direct recruit-candidates, accordingly.
Inter se seniority will be reckoned in accordance.
[Paras 11, 14 and 15]
