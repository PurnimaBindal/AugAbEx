The Appellant has challenged a demand for payment of contribution for the year 1982-83 under Section 75 of the Employee's State Insurance Act, 1948, Sections 77(1)(A) and 75.
According to ESI Court, the challenge under Section 75 of the Act can only be made within three years as per Section 77(1)(A) of the Act.
The Application has been dismissed.
While considering the merits of the case, the ESI Act specifically excludes mining operations.
Thus, the Corporation has no authority to levy the contribution.
This issue does not require any further consideration since the challenge itself is time-barred.
