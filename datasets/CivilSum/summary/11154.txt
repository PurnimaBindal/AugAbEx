According to Section 7-A of the Employees' Provident Fund and Misc.
Provisions Act, 1952, the Commissioner has the authority to conduct an inquiry to determine the applicability of the Act to an establishment and the amount due from an employer.
As per Clause 2, the Commissioner has the same powers as a Court under CPC, including the ability to enforce attendance of any person, examine them under oath, require document production, receive evidence on affidavit, and issue commissions for witness examination.
However, in this case, the Commissioner failed to conduct an inquiry to establish whether or not the petitioner had made a deposit for employees working in the institution.
Without verifying the facts, the Commissioner determined the liability of the petitioner, which is unfair, and therefore, the impugned order determining the petitioner's liability is quashed.
The petitioner is directed to pay the actual amount after holding a proper inquiry.
