Dismissal from service - Misconduct - Negligence of duty - The inquiry officer found the petitioner constable negligent, which resulted in the escape of the accused.
This finding was affirmed by the appellate and revisional authority.
The High Court also held that the petitioner's misconduct warranted his dismissal from service.
Even the SLP was dismissed.
The fact that the petitioner was acquitted by the Magistrate is not sufficient grounds for overturning the order of dismissal upheld by the competent authority and the Supreme Court.
[Paras 3 & 6] B Departmental Proceedings - Standard of Proof - Merely being acquitted of a criminal charge is not enough to exonerate an employee in a departmental inquiry.
This is in accordance with the case of 1993(1) SCT 133 (SC).
