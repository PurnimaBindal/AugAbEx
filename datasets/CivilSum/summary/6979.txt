Constitution of India, Article 311 - Dispersion of service of probationers - A probationer can be discharged from service due to unsatisfactory work and conduct, or if their services are no longer required.
Such a discharge is considered a non-stigmatic order.
If a probationer is let go due to unsatisfactory performance, behavior, or conduct, the order must include remarks explaining why.
Without such remarks, the order could be challenged as unreasonable.
[Paras 6 and 7]
