The Bihar Superior Judicial Service Rules, 1946, Rule 6 dictate that for determining the quota of direct recruits, both temporary and permanent posts have to be counted, and the quota cannot be confined to permanent posts alone.
[Para 14]
Appointments or promotions made in violation of the quota rule will not confer any right of seniority over the persons appointed within their quota, and appointments in violation of the quota rule are not valid.
[Para 19]
In appointments to judicial posts, the proviso to Rule 6 contemplates a conscious decision taken by the State Govt. after consultation with the High Court and does not give the State Govt. untrammeled power to deviate from the substantive provisions of the Rule.
Without any material to show that the State Govt. had taken such a decision, it cannot be assumed that the promotions made beyond the two-third quota were legal or valid.
[Para 20]
Non-observance of the quota rule for a long time will not, in itself, give a right to dispense with the compliance of the rule unless the rule is struck down or amended.
As and when the appointments are made, the quota rule must be strictly observed.
