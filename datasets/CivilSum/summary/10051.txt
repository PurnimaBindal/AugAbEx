Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 7(3-A) states that in case of delay in gratuity payment, the retiree is eligible for simple interest at a rate not exceeding the long-term deposit rate notified by the Central Government.
However, the specific rate of interest notified by the government is not mentioned.
In such cases, the authorities have been instructed to pay a simple interest of 12% on the delayed payment of gratuity.
