Rajasthan Administrative Service Rules, 1954, Rule 28-B(6) - Promotion by selection - Promotion from junior scale to higher scale categories of posts - Merit and seniority-cum-merit are equal criteria for the two categories - Excessive promotions in merit category are not allowed against declared vacancies, so as not to take away the right of seniors of seniority-cum-merit category - Equal proportion must be maintained even when Court directions are followed.
[Paras 16, 17 and 19]  B. Rajasthan Administrative Service Rules, 1954, Rule 28-B(6) - Promotion by selection - Merit based promotion ensures equal opportunity for capable individuals - All officers in consideration for promotion on merit are efficient and meritorious, but comparative merit must be assessed.
[Para 17]  C. Rajasthan Administrative Service Rules, 1954, Rule 28-B(6) - Promotion by selection - Delay and latches - Seniority cannot be reopened after reasonable lapse of time, but challenges to promotion of juniors pending consideration are not affected by delay and latches.
