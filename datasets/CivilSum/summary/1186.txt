Haryana State Education (School and Institution Cadre) Group-B Service Rules, 1988, Rule 9(d) - Promotion of teachers - Petitioners who possess the qualification of Shiksha Shastri (OT in Sanskrit) and not the qualification of BT/BEd, are eligible for promotion as Headmaster - The qualification of B.A with Sanskrit as one of the subjects is not necessary for their promotion to the next higher post as per the rules - Petitioners, who are Sanskrit Teachers with years of experience, are eligible for promotion to the posts of Headmaster of High School.
