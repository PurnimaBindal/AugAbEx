Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Civil Services (General and Common Conditions of Service) Rules, 1994, Rules 5 and 15 - Transfer of private school staff to Punjab Govt. - Eligibility conditions - Increments granted after meeting eligibility criteria - Those already in regular service and receiving regular pay with annual increments while meeting the necessary eligibility conditions for their position cannot be denied their current pay scale and periodic increments upon being taken over by the government - They are not obligated to pass additional eligibility requirements to receive further annual increments.
