Constitution of India, Article 14 - Admission discrimination between NRI and non-NRI students to MBBS courses - After admission, students become a uniform class and discrimination cannot exist - A detained student admitted under State PMT quota is not required to pay an extra fee to clear MBBS course studies in the next examination - A petitioner NRI student's demand for the same purpose results in hostile discrimination - Demand for an additional fee is dismissed.
[Paras 9 to 11]
