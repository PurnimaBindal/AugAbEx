The Haryana Municipal Employees (Pension and General Provident Fund) Rules, 1993, Rules 1(2) and 2(1) - Employee retirement - The term 'working immediately before the day' implies no interval of time.
If an employee retired before the cut off date of the pension Rules, they cannot be covered within the term 'immediately before that day.'
Even with a liberal interpretation, they cannot be helped by the rules.
The employee's unilateral act of opting for pension will not attract the Rules since the payment of pension involves financial implications.
Therefore, there must be a cut off date as per the financial sources of the State.
[Paras 10 and 13] B Pensioners/classification - Retired persons form one class but not a homogeneous class for all purposes.
