Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - Admission - Reservation for candidates of backward area - Admission to MBBS and BDS courses - The petitioner did not reside in a notified backward area when she passed her essential qualifying examination - Although she passed her middle and matriculation exams in a backward area school, this did not qualify her for admission against seats reserved for backward area candidates - Holding that the petitioner is not entitled to be considered for admission under reservation for candidates of backward area.
