Fundamental Rule 54 - Back wages - Termination on account of conviction for absconding from duty - It is up to the competent authority to determine how to deal with the period in which an employee was not working during the proceedings against them - The employee is not entitled to back wages for that time - If back wages are granted during that time, the court must provide justification - If an employee was terminated without due process due to a conviction for absconding from duty, the authority itself is at fault and the employee is entitled to back wages.
[Paras 5 & 6]
