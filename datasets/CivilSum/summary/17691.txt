According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, the selection, appointment, and cancellation of selection must incorporate the principles of natural justice.
In the case of a petitioner selected for the position of a petrol dealer by Hindustan Petroleum, complaints arose regarding the petitioner making false statements.
However, the petitioner was given the opportunity to explain and provide an account of his actions.
Ultimately, the selection was cancelled and the corporation was well within its rights to verify the background of the candidates before making an appointment.
Therefore, the petitioner cannot claim any violation of principles of natural justice, and the petition was dismissed.
