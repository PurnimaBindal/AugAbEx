The Army Personnel's death due to Coronary Occlusion, which was not caused or aggravated by the condition of service, qualifies for Special Family Pension.
The refusal to grant the pension is based on the deceased's lack of history regarding cardiac problems or family history.
Moreover, the Camp lacked adequate medical help and took time to transport the deceased to the hospital before collapsing.
However, a court of enquiry found that the death was attributable to Military Service.
As a result, the deceased's widow is entitled to not only ordinary pension but also Special Family pension and an order to pay the arrears of difference is passed.
