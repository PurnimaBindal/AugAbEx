Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - State - Red Cross Society - Although not considered a State under Art 12, it is still required to adhere to the Statute and Rules established.
A writ petition can be filed against the Society if it violates the Act or Rules and principles of natural justice, but not against the violation of fundamental rights and action being without jurisdiction.
[Paras 10 to 14]

Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - Transfer - Local cadre post with no liability to transfer - The post cannot be transferred, as there is no regulation or rule of the Society to indicate that it is transferable from the Distt.
to another.
Despite the claim of the employee that his post is in local cadre, the order of transfer from one district to another cannot be upheld simply due to the employee working at one place for a long period.
[Paras 14 & 15]
