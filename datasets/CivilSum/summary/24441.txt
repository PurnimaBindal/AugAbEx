Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1964, Rules 3(1)(ii) & (iii), 11(iii)(a) and 11(v) - Major and minor penalty - Simply mentioning a wrong provision or rule does not affect a charge that falls under the provisions of a major penalty - If the charge is serious enough to be covered under Clause (v) of Rule 11 and the prescribed procedure for a major penalty is followed, imposing a major penalty is valid even if the provision for a minor penalty is mentioned - This suggests that the authority never intended to impose a minor penalty - However, both major and minor penalties cannot be imposed simultaneously - Minor penalties are cancelled while major penalties remain.
[Paras 8 to 11]
