According to Section 10-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the government holds sovereign powers to take immediate and necessary actions in the event of an emergency.
The rule of audi alteram partem does not apply to such situations.
An immediate decision must be taken without delay to effectively handle an emergency.
If a notice or hearing is required before a decision is made, it may not be efficient enough to address the situation.
