Constitution of India, Article 226 - Kurukshetra University Act, Section 12-A - Promotion - Pay of higher post - Retiral benefits - Petitioner held the post of Junior Engineer which was upgraded to that of SDO by Executive Council as a personal measure - However, the approval for upgradation of post was rejected by State Government - Petitioner claimed pay scale of the post of SDO and retiral benefits on that basis - The court held that petitioner cannot be given retiral benefits as if he retired from the post of SDO as he was never promoted as such on that post - Yet, respondents cannot deny him pay of that post for the period he remained on that post as he was conceded to have worked and discharged duties of the post of SDO [Paras 5 and 6]
