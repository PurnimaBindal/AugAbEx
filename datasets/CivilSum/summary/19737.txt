Constitution of India - Articles 162, 226/227 - Executive order - Retrospective effect - In August 1981, the decision to reduce House Rent Allowance became available in the Agency and a direction was issued for the deduction of excess amount if any paid.
An executive order that is to the prejudice of an employee must be prospective in operation and cannot be applied retrospectively.
No recovery can be made prior to August 1981, and the order will be operative only with effect from the date of its acceptance by the Agency.
As a result, the petitioner is not liable to refund any amount that may have been disbursed to them.
