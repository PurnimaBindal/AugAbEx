According to the Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 16(4), promotion to the post of Superintendent Engineer must adhere to reservation policies.
However, regular promotions were not being ordered due to a stay on promotions as ordered by the Apex Court in Ajit Singh Janjua's case and Jagdish Lal's case.
Nevertheless, this stay order would not hinder the State Govt. from ordering regular promotions on the basis of DPC recommendations.
The petitioners do not belong to any Scheduled Castes or Backward Class category and even if the ratio of Janjua's case or Jagdish's case is applied, it would not negatively impact their seniority.
Ultimately, regular promotions will proceed on the basis of DPC recommendations.
