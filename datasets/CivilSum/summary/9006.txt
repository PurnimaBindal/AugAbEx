The petitioner requested admission as a Backward Class candidate based on improved marks in the Higher Secondary Examination, after the admission process had concluded.
However, his admission was rejected because he did not take the exam at the first opportunity on his own.
The Supreme Court's decision in Abhijit v. Dean, AIR 1987 SC 1362, does not apply to this case and thus no relief can be given.
As a result, the petition was dismissed.
