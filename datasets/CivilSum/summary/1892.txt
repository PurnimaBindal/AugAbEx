In accordance with the Constitution of India, Article 311 and IIT Statute, Para 13(9), an order of Suspension passed during pendency of disciplinary or criminal proceedings does not stigmatize the person suspended.
It is the unqualified right of the employer to place an employee under suspension, and unless it is passed as a measure of punishment, no hearing is required.
Suspension during proceedings prevents the influence over material witnesses.
Suspension made after the preliminary enquiry report and consultation of Professorial Committee cannot be deemed to be unjustified or malicious.
[Paras 8, 9, 11, 12, and 13]

According to the Constitution of India, Article 311 and IIT Statute, Para 13(9), instructions stating that a person could be placed under suspension even before issuance of the charge sheet should not be misconstrued to mean that at no subsequent stage one can be placed under suspension.
The rule does not prevent the authority from placing an employee under suspension at any later stage of issuance of the charge sheet.
It is important to give instructions a harmonious interpretation with the Rules.
