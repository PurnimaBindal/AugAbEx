Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer Policies - Employees pursuing higher education may be transferred without pay during study leave - Management has the right to control posting and transfers - Courts will not intervene in discriminatory transfers - In this case, the petitioner was pursuing an LLB course without permission and refused study leave - The petitioner was transferred only 60 kms away from Delhi with no allegations of wrongdoing, and therefore the court refused to intervene.
