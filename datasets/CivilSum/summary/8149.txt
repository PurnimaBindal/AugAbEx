Constitution of India, Article 226 - Railway Pension Rules, Clause 74 and Rule 70(5) - Retiral benefits - Nomination - The Tribunal made an error by awarding retiral dues in favour of the petitioners without verifying the fact that the original applicant was legally married to the deceased - An application was filed by the petitioners claiming to be heirs and legal representatives of the deceased who was a lorry driver Grade-I of Eastern Railway - The application was filed by one who claimed to be the legally married wife and in whose favour nomination was made in respect of PF, GIS etc. - The application filed by the petitioners for intervention was allowed - A civil suit regarding the status of the petitioners and the original applicant is pending before a competent court of law.
