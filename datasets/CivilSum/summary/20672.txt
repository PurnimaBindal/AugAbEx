Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Pay scales - Promotion - Seniority - Temporary posts of Assistants were created and some Senior Accountants were appointed to those posts, which consequently placed them in a higher cadre and pay scale eligible for promotion as Gazetted Class II posts.
Following statutory Rules under Article 309 of the Constitution, All Head Clerks, Assistants, Stenograplhers etc. were placed in a higher cadre and pay scale from which they were eligible and entitled for promotion as Gazetted Class II posts.
The earlier executive instructions with a footnote that Accountants en bloc be kept senior to all others in the combined seniority list were superseded and had to yield place to the statutory Rules.
As a result, Accountants were no longer part of the same class as the Head Clerks and were not in the feeder cadre for promotion as such in the statutory Rules.
In directing the Govt. to consider the Accountants senior to the appellants and to give them promotion over and above the appellants, the Tribunal was in clear error.
