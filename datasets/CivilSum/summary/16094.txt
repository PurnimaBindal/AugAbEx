The Disciplinary Authority under Regulation 11 of Central Bank of India, Officer Employees (Conduct) Regulations has unguided powers to impose any penalties from those specified in Regulation 4, despite not being obligated to adhere to the major penalties procedure.
The authority must still give the delinquent officer an opportunity of hearing against any proposed punishment.
An order of dismissal without affording such opportunity violates the first proviso of Regulation 11 and principles of natural justice.
Therefore, the order cannot stand.
The petition is allowed.
[Para 4]

Regulation 11 of Central Bank Employees Conduct Regulations stipulates that an officer convicted on criminal charges, on which they could be dismissed from service in departmental proceedings, can be dismissed from service without following the procedure for imposition of a major penalty, after affording the officer an opportunity of hearing.
An order of dismissal passed without providing any opportunity cannot stand.
Filing an appeal against the dismissal order and providing an opportunity of hearing in appeal is no substitute for an opportunity of hearing on the proposed punishment before imposing it.
The order is therefore quashed.
