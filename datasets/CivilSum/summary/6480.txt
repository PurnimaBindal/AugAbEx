Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - The petitioner must be granted consequential benefit for the period spent on extraordinary leave - The Executive Council condoned the break in service and fixed seniority - Previous writ filed by petitioner resulted in finality of judgment - The petitioner is entitled to entire consequential benefits - It is held that the petitioner should receive the benefit of leave period counted towards 8 years of continuous service in promotion cadre and respondents must grant full consequential relief.
