Constitution of India, Article 311(1) - Competent Authority - Appointing Authority - Authority competent to initiate and take disciplinary action - Deputationists - Article 311 secures protection to civil service members of Union or State from dismissal or removal by an authority lower in rank than the one that appointed them - It does not prohibit dismissal or removal by an authority other than the appointing authority - There is no need for the authority taking action to be competent to appoint the affected person to that post - Any other non-subordinate authority is free to take action - The action taken by a deputationist officer against a deputationist is still valid even if the officer is not the appointing authority of the deputationist.
