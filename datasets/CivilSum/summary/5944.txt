Constitution of India, Article 14 - Admission to Post Graduate Degree and Diploma (Medical and Dental) Courses Rules, 1987, Rule 4(1)(b) - Reservation/Admission - Reservation based on residence - All seats reserved for candidates of Karnataka origin - As per the Supreme Court decision, 50 percent of seats for admission to Post Graduate Courses must be made available for general merit and open to all India competition - The eligibility condition of Karnataka origin for all seats specified in the rule is contrary to the ratio of the Supreme Court decision and is a violation of Article 14.
AIR 1985 SC 1420 and AIR 1986 SC 1877 cited.
