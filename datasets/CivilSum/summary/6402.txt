Articles 14, 16 and 226 of the Indian Constitution - Reserved Category - Experience relaxation - Reserved category members being promoted against certain earmarked posts does not affect chances of promotion for general category if selected on the basis of experience relaxation.
[Paras 20 and 25] B Life Insurance Corporation of India, Class III and Class IV Employees (Promotion) Rules, 1987, Rule 8 - Rule Validity - Efficiency of service - Experience Relaxation - Criteria laid down being the same for both categories, reserved category awarded less marks for experience and required to secure more marks on other merits; efficiency not compromised by experience relaxation.
[Paras 21 to 25] C. Life Insurance Corporation of India, Class III and Class IV Employees (Promotion) Rules, 1987, Rule 8 - Rule validity - Efficiency of service - Relaxation in departmental test marks - providing less qualifying marks of evaluation in favour of reserved category candidates compromises administration efficiency; Appendix-II to LIC Rules which provide less pass marks for SC & ST candidates for promotion violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution and must be quashed.
[Paras 26, 27, 28, 30 and 32(i)] D Life Insurance Corporation of India, Class III and Class IV Employees (Promotion) Rules, 1987, Rule 8 - Rule validity - Efficiency of service - Relaxation in experience - Relaxation to give social justice to depressed class permissible under Article 16 of Constitution; Guidelines for relaxation made evident and power exercised in public interest; Rule 8 of LIC Staff Rules cannot be struck down as being unguided for relaxation exercise.
