Bihar Police Manual, 1978, Rule 660-C - Out of turn promotion to the post of Dy.
S.P.
- Rule 660-C allows for out of turn promotion up to the rank of Inspector, not Dy.
S.P.
- This provision does not apply to promotion to the rank of Dy.
