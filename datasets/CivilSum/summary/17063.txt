Constitution of India, Article 220 - Medical Reimbursement - Despite being an ex-member of Parliament, a petitioner who was also a member of Haryana Legislative Assembly and a sitting M.L.A.
is entitled to medical reimbursement from the State of Haryana - He has the option to claim reimbursement from either the Government of India or Haryana - The State of Haryana is directed to release his medical claim.
