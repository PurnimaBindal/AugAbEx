Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion/Qualifications/Service Rules/Past practice - Promotion is challenged on the basis of qualifications - The respondent (promotee) had higher qualifications than the petitioner - There are no rules or instructions governing service conditions that require the alleged essential qualifications - If there are no rules, the authorities are justified in selecting a person with higher qualifications - No data on past promotions were presented - The claim is unfounded.
[Paras 5, 6] B Departmental promotion/Administrative function/Quasi-judicial functions - The authorities responsible for departmental promotions do not perform quasi-judicial functions - No reasons need to be recorded.
