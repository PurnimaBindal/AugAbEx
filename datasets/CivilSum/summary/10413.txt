The Workman's Compensation Act of 1923 and the Employee's State Insurance Act of 1948 deal with compensation for work-related injuries.
In this case, the respondent, a machineman, suffered a severe injury to his right leg while on duty that resulted in a 100% loss of earning capacity.
The management failed to prove that the respondent was insured under the ESI Act.
The respondent produced a letter from the ESI Corporation stating that they wouldn't pay any compensation.
Consequently, the appellant has been ordered by the court to pay the compensation awarded to the workman and the costs of Rs. 20,000.
