Madhya Pradesh Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1976, Rule 47(7)(a)(i) and (8)(i) - Family Pension - Widows - Second marriage - The only widow who was legally married is entitled to family pension in case the second marriage is prohibited.
However, in the case of Muslims where more than one wives are legally permissible, more than one widow can share the Family pension.
[Paras 2 and 3]
Madhya Pradesh Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1976, Rule 47(7)(a)(i) and (8)(i) - Family Pension - Widows - Succession - In case the entitlement to family pension is subject to the conditions prescribed in the Pension Rules itself, the law of succession would not be applicable.
