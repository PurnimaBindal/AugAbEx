Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4(1) - Assessment of compensation for injury - Workman sustained a severe facial disfigurement - Injury classified under Schedule I of Serial No. 5 of Part I, which qualifies for compensation of 100% earning capacity as permanent total disablement - Medical Board assessed the disability at 5% and awarded compensation accordingly by the Commissioner - However, according to the Act, the appellant is eligible for compensation covering the entire loss of 100% - The case is remanded for the Commissioner to continue with fresh disposal.
