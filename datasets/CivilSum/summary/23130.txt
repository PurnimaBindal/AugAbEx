According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India and Punjab Civil Services Rules Rule 48, an efficiency bar may be prescribed in a time scale, and the next increment above the bar must be granted with the approval of the authority who may withhold the increment.
No hearing is required for the employee concerned to make a decision about clearing their efficiency bar, but a subjective opinion must be recorded in a speaking order to allow for representation against the order.
Additionally, the order must be based on material on record.
