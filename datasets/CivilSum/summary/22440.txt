Constitution of India, Article 136 - Promotion - The petitioner was promoted to Group-I in 1979 but claimed promotion from 1972.
The Civil Judge concluded that the appellant was entitled to promotion from 1975, while the Additional District Judge granted promotion from 1972.
The High Court set aside the entire order stating that it had no jurisdiction to grant the relief sought.
It was held that the High Court was justified in its order, granting relief from 1972, but the relief granted by the Civil Judge could not be challenged by the State before the Additional District Judge.
The impugned order was modified, and the appellant was directed to be promoted to Group-I from 1975, as decided by the Civil Judge in the executing decree.
