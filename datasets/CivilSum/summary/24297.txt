Constitution of India, Articles 136 and 226 - Education policy - UGC's policy of introducing "Jyotir Vigyan" (Science of astrology) as a course of study - Court holds that this would not saffronise education and is a matter of policy framed by experts - Court lacks expertise in education policy matters - Judicial review cannot interfere unless statutory provisions are violated - Astrology is a pure science requiring study of celestial bodies - Students are free to choose any field of study - Origin from a cult does not dictate propagation of a particular religion.
