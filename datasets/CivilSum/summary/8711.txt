According to Section 2(8) of the UP Industrial Disputes Act (28 of 1947), the definition of "retrenchment" does not have any exceptions, including Clause (bb) of Section 2(oo) of the Central Act.
Therefore, termination resulting from non-renewal of contract is considered retrenchment.

B.
Under Section 122(2) of the U.P.
Co-operative Societies Act (2 of 1966), employees of co-operative societies are governed by regulations framed under the Act.
It is established that the UP Act is a special law that is included in the meaning of other labor laws for the time being in force.
Furthermore, the provisions of the Central Act and the UP Act will be deemed inoperative if any inconsistency arises under Regulation 103 of the U.P.
Co-operative Societies Employees Service Regulations, 1975.
[Paras 9 and 13].
