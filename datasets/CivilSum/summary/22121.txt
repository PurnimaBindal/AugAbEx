Constitution of India, Article 32 - Swatantrata Sainik Samman Pension Scheme, 1980 - Freedom fighters pension - To receive pension for claimed jail/underground suffering, claimants must provide necessary documentary evidence as required by the Scheme.
[Para 2]  B Constitution of India, Article 32 - Swatantrata Sainik Samman Pension Scheme, 1980 - Freedom fighters pension - Court has no power to interfere with finding of State Advisory Committee/State Government if none of the claimants have produced necessary documents as required by the Scheme and the Committee has concluded that none of the claimants are eligible for pension.
