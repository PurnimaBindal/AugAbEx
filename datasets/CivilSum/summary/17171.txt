Sections 2(1)(1) and 3-A of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 deal with total disablement.
In the case where a driver lost his right hand after the shoulder joint was amputated due to an accident, the learned Commissioner determined compensation based on the loss of earning capacity, which was found to be 80%.
As the appellant would be unable to perform his duties as a driver with the amputation of his hand, the court deemed him totally disabled and awarded enhanced compensation.
