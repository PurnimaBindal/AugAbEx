Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 226 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 13.1 - Pre-mature retirement after promotion - The competent authority has discretion over retaining a person in public service after they reach 55 years of age, which must be exercised subjectively through consideration of their entire service record - Adverse entries made in the service record prior to promotions may only be disregarded for the purpose of promotions, not for determining retention in service after 55 years - Temporary and fortuitous officiating promotions that do not confer seniority or provide notice of reversion will not affect adverse entries - If the employee does not meet the criteria for extension in service after reaching 55 years of age, premature retirement is possible even after promotions with adverse entries.
