Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 and 309 - Appointment - Promotion - Qualifications - The basic qualifications and conditions prescribed in the statutory rules framed by the government cannot be relaxed to help a person if qualified and eligible candidates are available, even if the Rule allows the power to relax such conditions in exceptional circumstances - Choosing ineligible candidates over eligible ones violates Articles 14 and 16 of Constitution.
