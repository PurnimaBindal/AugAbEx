Constitution of India, Article 226 - Retention of Govt accommodation after retirement - Permissibility - Ground of eligibility of ward/spouse for regularisation/allotment - According to the OM of 1990, retention from general pool and vice versa was allowed.
However, OM of 1999 replaced the previous one and does not permit regularisation in cases where the allottee is in possession under general pool but the ward is not eligible for allotment under the same.
Because of the shortage of Government accommodation, giving an advantage to retired officials who are not eligible for the same can be detrimental to other Government servants who are patiently waiting in line.
Thus, retaining Government accommodation after retirement should only be allowed in the most stringent circumstances, and the latest OM of 1999 has statutory content.
