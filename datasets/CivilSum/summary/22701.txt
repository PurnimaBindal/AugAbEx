Constitution of India, Article 309 - Kerala Judicial Service Rules, 1991, Rules 5(3) and 10(1) - Appointment - Qualifications - Experience - Appointment on judicial posts - The court held that having a minimum of three years' legal practice is a requirement for appointment in the lower rank in judicial posts.
Even if the State Government prescribes a longer standing in the Bar, it does not go against the court's decision.
