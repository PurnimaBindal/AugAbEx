Constitution of India, Article 16 - West Bengal Panchayat (Recruitment and Conditions of Service of Gram Panchayat Karmee) Rules, 1995, Rule 8 - Absorption - Promotion - Despite a circular by the Government regularising services of Dafadars and Chowkidars, promotion cannot be directed to appellants appointed as Tax Collector on contract basis since they are not Dafadars and Chowkidars - There are no statutory rules for their absorption.
