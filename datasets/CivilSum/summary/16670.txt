Section 478 of the Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, 1957, prescribes a six month limitation for challenging an order of punishment.
In this case, the suit was filed after the six month period had expired and was therefore barred by time.
As such, it was rightly rejected.
[Paras 4, 10, and 16]

According to Regulation 7 of the Delhi Municipal Corporation Service (Control and Appeal) Regulations, 1959, the Deputy Commissioner has the power to impose punishments, including withholding increments.
As the appellant held a post with a minimum monthly salary, the Deputy Commissioner did not act without jurisdiction in passing the order of punishment.
[Para 14]
