Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - The Petitioner, who is promoted to Executive Grade of E-1 and holds a transferable post, cannot be transferred to any other project in the public interest or administrative exigencies.
The order of transfer does not violate any statutory regulations.
Additionally, malice in fact cannot be attributed to a legal person like a company, but only to a natural person who holds a position in the administration of the company.
