Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Disability pension - Petitioner, who was medically boarded out of service in Medical category EEE for Epilepsy Grand Mal, was denied a disability pension by the Controller of Defence Accounts (Pensions) on the grounds that his disability was not attributable to military service.
However, the court held that since the petitioner was physically fit at the time of his enrolment and no medical board had found any issue at that time, his disability must have been caused by his Army Service and thus the respondents were directed to release the disability pension to the petitioner.
[Paras 5 and 8]
