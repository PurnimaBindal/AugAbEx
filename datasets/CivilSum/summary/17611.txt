"According to the Constitution of India, specifically articles 14 and 16, a bank implemented a policy requiring a test for promotions.
However, one employee was under suspension during the implementation of this policy and was not given any notice regarding their participation in the examination process.
The actual examination was conducted three years later.
The bank claimed that the employee was asked to partake in the written test but did not do so, which was found to be untrue.
