Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rules 14 and 27 along with Constitution of India Articles 14 and 311 discuss the wilful absence of delinquent employees - Such absence is only deemed wilful if there is no reasonable justification - A disciplinary authority cannot find an employee wilfully absent if they were suffering from an illness like tuberculosis during the period of absence - The High Court found interference in the case was justified as the disciplinary authority's finding was arbitrary and capricious.
