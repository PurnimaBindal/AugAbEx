Respondents must disclose material in support of subjective satisfaction recorded in dismissal order when Article 311(2)(b) is invoked, according to Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(b) - Departmental enquiry.
Merely stating in a dismissal order of initiating proceedings against delinquent may cause harm to the Enquiry Officer or witnesses is insufficient.
As per the Punjab Civil Services Rules, the order of dismissal was quashed.
