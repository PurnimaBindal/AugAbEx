Punjab Development and Panchayat (Class-II) Service Rules, 1974, Rules 12 and 13 - Seniority - Probation - Extension of probation period - Seniority should be determined after the extended probation period is excluded - If the order of extension of probation period is not contested, then seniority and promotion cannot be claimed by challenging the tentative seniority determined by the exclusion of the extended probation period.
