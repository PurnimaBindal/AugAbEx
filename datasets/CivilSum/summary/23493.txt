Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection Grade - Classification - The principle of 'equal pay for equal work' cannot be disregarded with categorisation into Grade I and Grade II among Inspectors - Sorting within the Cadre based on merit and seniority or seniority-cum-merit for awarding selection grade or super time grade post a specific period of service within such Cadre is acceptable - The Pay Commission's findings were inaccurately challenged by the High Court.
