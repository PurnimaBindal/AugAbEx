Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 162 - Dismissal - The appellant, an ASI, was punished with dismissal from service for being absent while posted in Police Lines and for being found drunk in a public place.
Rule 162 allows for punishments lesser than dismissal from service to be awarded, specifically allowing consideration of length of service and credit towards pension.
However, both the Punishing Authority and the Appellate Authority neglected to take these aspects into consideration, indicating that they were not fully aware of the mandatory nature and intent of Rule 162.
The impugned order violates Rule 162 and is therefore quashed, according to Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 100.
