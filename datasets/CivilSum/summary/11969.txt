Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - Selection Committee choosing petitioner (at Sr. No. 1) and respondent (at Sr. No. 2) - One post of Assistant Headmaster - Respondent had been serving as graduate teacher since 1975, as opposed to petitioner, who had been there since 1979 - Mere tenure of service did not make respondent a more suitable candidate - It was held that the tribunal had erred in interfering with the decision of the Selection Committee without providing specific reasons - Selection of petitioner required no intervention.
