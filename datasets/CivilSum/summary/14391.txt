Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - A writ petitioner was offered a post of Lecturer in Commerce on an ad hoc basis up to 31.3.1996.
He filed a writ petition, under Article 226 of the Constitution, requesting that his appointment be recognized as regular, and that the stipulation of his appointment up to 31396 or his services termination within 24 hours' notice be dismissed.
The Learned Single Judge approved the request but the present letters patent appeal has been filed by the College and its Principal.
No record suggests that the petitioner was ever appointed on a regular basis.
Moreover, the Selection Committee found him ineligible now to work on a regular basis, and allegations of mala fide against the Principal were unfounded.
The judgment of the learned single Judge was set aside and the appeal was granted.
Finally, the writ petition was dismissed.
[Paras 6, 8, and 15]
