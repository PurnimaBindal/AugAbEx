According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, and UP GO No. 822 EBR/XXIII- PWD date 19.10.1968, a transferred employee is entitled to seniority and promotion benefits based on their previous service in the parent department.
In this case, the petitioner had been working as an Asstt.
Engineer on ad-hoc basis in the Irrigation Department until being selected through competitive examination for employment on a substantial basis in the PWD Deptt.
The petitioner should have been deemed to have been transferred from one department to another and afforded the same seniority and promotion benefits they would have been given had they remained in their previous department.
However, the petitioner was illegally excluded from promotion to S.E.
by their department, which erroneously brought down their seniority by excluding their previous service.
The court deemed this action as erroneous and directed the department to consider the petitioner for promotion from the date their junior was considered, granting all consequential benefits if found entitled to promotion.
[Paras 7 to 14]
