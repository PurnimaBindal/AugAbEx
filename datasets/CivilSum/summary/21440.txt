Punjab Public Service Commission (State Service, Clause - III) Regulation 1967, regulation 7 - Constitution of India, Article 16 - Qualifications - Estoppel - The appellant, who was appointed as a clerk, did not possess the necessary requirement of being Matriculate 1st class, although he was Matriculate IIIrd class - The appellant was subsequently removed from the job on the basis of not fulfilling the compulsory criteria, and the removal was considered valid - Estoppel does not apply in this case, and the plea put forward by the appellant is not credible - However, the appellant later acquired the necessary qualifications, and the Government has the authority to serve since that subsequent date.
[Paras 13 and 14] B.
Constitution of India, Article 14, and 16 - The principle of estoppel is not relevant in the scenario when a person is appointed to a post of clerk without possessing the required qualifications, and the subsequent removal of the employee from service is justified - If the appellant acquires the requisite qualification after being removed from service, the government can regularise their appointment from that specific date.
