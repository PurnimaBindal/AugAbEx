Appointment procedures must be followed as outlined in the statutory rules, and any appointment made in violation of those rules, particularly regarding minimum educational qualifications and other essential qualifications, will be considered wholly illegal.
[Para 9]  B Jammu and Kashmir Education (Gazetted) College Service Recruitment Rules, 2004 (SRO 115) Schedule II, Clause 7 - Rules 5 and 9 - Appointment qualifications - The Chief Minister only has the power to relax rules with the concurrence of the Public Service Commission.
Eligibility for appointment to the posts of Lecturer/Librarian/PTI's is limited to those candidates who have qualified National Eligibility Test (NET) or State Level Eligibility Test (SLET).
The appointment of private respondent No. 4, who does not meet the eligibility criteria, is challenged.
The Rules allow for relaxation for a period of five years starting in July 1998; thereafter, any relaxation can only be granted with the permission of the competent authority if suitable candidates are not available who meet the prescribed qualifications.
The petitioner, who has a Master's degree in Business Administration and passed the SET, is eligible under the prescribed qualifications.
Therefore, the Respondent Commission's stance that the relaxation was necessary as no candidate was selected for five years from 1998 onwards cannot be accepted.
The selection of private respondent No. 4 in violation of the mandatory Rules is held to be invalid.
The requirement of NET/SELT in the Notification dated 1722004 is held to be in violation of the Rules, and the appointment of Respondent No. 4 is quashed.
