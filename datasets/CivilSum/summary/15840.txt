According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner was selected for the post of Trainee Apprenticeship Grade-II after a test and interview and was ranked at Sr. No. 33 on the merit list of selected candidates.
However, he was relieved of his post after completing 2 years of apprenticeship without being appointed to a TA Grade-II position even though vacancies were available and there were still 22 spots to be filled from the existing penal.
The authorities neglectfully allowed the list to expire despite the availability of existing vacancies.
The circular dated 9121968 was used as a reference, specifically clause (v) of Item No. 1, but it was noted by the Single Judge that this circular is not actually part of any statute.
The revocation of the list after only one year was deemed arbitrary since there were no statutory rules and the posts were still vacant, yet no new panel was created during that period.
[Paras 7, 8 and 10]
