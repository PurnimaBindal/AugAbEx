Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reinstatement and grant of back wages - Despite the belated filing of the writ petition, the Supreme Court directed the respondent to be paid Rs. 50,000/- in full and final settlement of his claim due to the project already being closed and the writ petition being filed years after the service of workman was alleged to be terminated.
The original direction for reinstatement and/or back wages was set aside.
