Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination of services - Departmental enquiry - Natural Justice - Enquiry Officer who was not authorized by Managing Director lacks competence to frame charges and has not examined any departmental witness.
The Enquiry Officer referred to various documents and statements of other persons without allowing cross-examination by the petitioner, violating the service Rules and the principles of natural justice.
Dismissal order is set aside.
[Paras 11 and 13]
