Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - An employer is not required to give an inquiry or an opportunity of hearing to an employee prior to his transfer on the basis of a complaint - The employer is within his rights to effect a transfer on the basis of a complaint or report after verifying the contents through an independent source - Exceptionally, the employer may transfer an employee without inquiry if he believes it to be in the interest of the public.
[Para 12] B Transfer is sometimes considered a condition of service, and other times an incident of service.
[Para 9] C Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - Employers have the power to transfer their employees and this power is not generally interfered by the Courts - The employer has the prerogative to transfer an employee from one place or post to another.
