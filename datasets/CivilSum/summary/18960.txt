Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 - Appointment - Petitioner provisionally selected for appointment as Driver - Directed to attend training which he did not undertake - Later treated as fresh case on recommendation of minister and directed to attend training - After training, did not report for duty - Reported nine months later, citing illness and not notifying employer earlier - Not allowed to join duty due to temporal nature of appointment and no future appointment rights - Refusal to allow him to join duty deemed proper by authorities and does not warrant interference.
