The option to seek voluntary retirement was given to save the Petitioner from removal, so it cannot be considered 'dictated voluntary retirement'.
[Para 8] B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Premature retirement - Petitioner challenged it on grounds that it was not necessary to cross the efficiency bar on the date of the notice.
However, since a Government letter existed on the date of assessment and the date of the show cause notice was irrelevant, the Petitioner was rightfully retired.
[Paras 12 and 20] C. Constitution of India, Article 226 - Air Force Rules, Rule 17 - Premature retirement - Petitioner challenged the decision on grounds that the Chief of Air Staff could not have changed the criteria of assessment laid in a Government letter.
However, under Rule 17 it is within the Chief's prerogative to set standards of efficiency, which this Court is reluctant to interfere with unless such action is irrational, illegal, incapable of performance, or in violation of rules.
