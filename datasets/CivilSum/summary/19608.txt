Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 300-A - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II Rule 25 - Compassionate pension/allowance - While it is true that a dismissed employee is not entitled to any pension benefits, under Rule 25 of the Rules, it is left to the discretion of the government to grant compassionate allowance to deserving persons on a case-by-case basis.
The person in whose favor Rule 25 has been applied is entitled to the amount due to him, which should not exceed two-thirds of the amount of pension he would have received upon retirement.
The government cannot choose a date for payment that goes against the provisions contained in Article 14 of the Constitution.
Therefore, the petitioner should be paid a pension/allowance at the rate that has been fixed.
[Paras 4 and 5]
