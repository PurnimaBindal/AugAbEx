The previous dismissal of a petition and SLP for delay and laches will not operate as res judicata in a subsequent writ petition against the award of the labour court according to Constitution of India, Articles 131 and 226 and Civil Procedure Code, Section 11.
[Paras 5 and 6]
The dismissal of an earlier Writ Petition and SLP on grounds of delay and laches cannot refuse the claim of the petitioner in the writ petition according to Constitution of India, Article 226 and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10.
[Para 6]
The High Court can scrutinize and reduce the punishment if it is found excessive under Section 11-A of Industrial Disputes Act and Constitution of India, Article 226.
[Paras 8 and 10]
A workman cannot be allowed to raise the issue of prejudice due to non-supply of enquiry report first time in the writ petition according to Constitution of India, Articles 311(2) and 226.
