Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Reinstatement - Backwages from the date of final publication of award to the date of reinstatement denied - Corporation argued that appointment could not happen unless the workman approached and expressed willingness - However, as per the ruling, the Corporation suffering an adverse award must send posting to the workman immediately after publication of the award - Therefore, the petitioner is justified in claiming salary and lapses in reinstatement with back wages cannot be denied.
