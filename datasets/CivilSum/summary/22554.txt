Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Pensionary Benefits - The State Government took over private schools where the respondents worked and became liable to pay their salaries.
When the respondents retired, the appellants refused to give them pensionary benefits as they served for less than 10 years.
The High Court ordered the appellants to pay pension to the respondents without giving them a chance to file a written statement, based on a decision in a cited case.
The Supreme Court reversed the decision and set aside the judgment of the High Court under appeal.
