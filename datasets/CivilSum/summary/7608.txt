Road Transport Corporations Act, 1950, Section 45 - Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation Servants (Conduct and Discipline) Regulations (1971), Regulation 23 - Bus conductor - Despite a satisfactory explanation of possession of excessive cash, the conductor committed a technical breach of not recording excess cash in waybill - Therefore, a punishment of dismissal from service is disproportionately harsh for such misconduct - A more appropriate penalty of wage reduction in two stages was imposed.
