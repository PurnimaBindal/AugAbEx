Demobilised Armed Personnel (Reservation of Vacancies in the Punjab State Non-Technical Services) Rules, 1968, Rule 2(d) [As amended in 1977] - Released Indian Armed Forces Personnel - Defined as a person who joined military service between November 1, 1962 and January 10, 1968 and was released on demobilisation thereafter.
[Para 3]
Demobilised Armed Personnel (Reservation of Vacancies in the Punjab State Non-Technical Services) Rules, 1968, Rule 2(d) [Prior amendment in 1977] - Released Indian Armed Forces Personnel defined as a person who joined military service on or after November 1, 1962 and was released at any time thereafter.
[Para 3]
Demobilised Armed Personnel (Reservation of Vacancies in the Punjab State Non-Technical Services) Rules, 1968, Rules 3 (1) and 5(1) - Benefit of military service - The petitioner who joined the military on May 20, 1969 and was released on his own request on April 16, 1975, was rejected seniority and pay benefits on the ground that the Rules were amended on April 20, 1977 - The Court held that as the petitioner was appointed prior to the amendment of Rules, he is to be governed by the Rules as they stood prior to their amendment - Accordingly, the petitioner has a vested right to claim seniority and pay fixation under Rule 5(1) of the Rules which cannot be taken away by the amendment in the Rules with retrospective effect.
