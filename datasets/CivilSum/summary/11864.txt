Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation - Equal Pay for Equal Work - The petitioner, employed as a helper in the work charged establishment, was granted the opportunity to serve as a helper-cum-trailor driver for a limited period.
Although employed as a Driver for a brief time, the plaintiff did not have the appropriate qualifications and was unsuccessful in his application before the Selection Board as a departmental candidate.
However, the principle of 'equal pay for equal work' is not applicable in this case, since the position is not permanent and the petitioner served as a Driver for only a brief period.
Moreover, he is not eligible for regularization in such a role unless he had worked for more than a year.
