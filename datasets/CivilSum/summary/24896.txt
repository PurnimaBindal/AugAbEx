The Indian Police Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1955, Regulations 3 and 5, the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Sections 19 and 29, and Article 16 of the Constitution of India deal with the setting aside of adverse remarks.
The Selection Committee cannot consider such remarks, and after expunging them, they must be treated as non-existent in the eye of the law.
[Para]
Article 16 of the Constitution of India states that expunged remarks cannot be considered during promotions.
