Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 Central Civil Services Rules, 1972, Rule 48 - Pension Benefit Scheme - The benefit must be extended to all employees who have rendered qualifying service - There should be no distinction between those who retired and those who resigned, as both have completed the requisite service - Term "retired" found in clause 3 should be interpreted liberally to include those who resigned after meeting the 30-year service requirement stated in Rule 48 - [Para 10]
