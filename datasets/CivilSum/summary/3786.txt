Constitution of India, Article 21 - J & K Govt. SRO 43 of 1994, Rule 2 - Compassionate appointment - Claims for compassionate appointment filed beyond six months of death in harness of deceased employee are not maintainable and must be rejected as time barred - Object of compassionate appointment is to alleviate crises for bereaved family.

Constitution of India, Article 21 - J & K Govt. SRO 43 of 1994, Rule 2 - Compassionate appointment - In general, claims of parity in appointment to Gazette or Class II posts are not available - Exceptions may be made if the rule permits such relief - Appointment to a higher post does not constitute discrimination.
