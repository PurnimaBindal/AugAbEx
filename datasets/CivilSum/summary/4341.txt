The Food Corporation of India's Staff Regulations, Regulation 63 provides that where an employee has been convicted of a criminal charge, no regular enquiry is required before imposing a penalty.
The Regulations contain the principles natural justice in Regulations 58 to 62.
The Court cannot use the principle of natural justice to override the Regulations.
[Paras 7 and 8] B.
In the case of Food Corporation of India, Staff Regulations, Regulation 63, the respondent was not dismissed for the conduct that led to their conviction, but for the conviction itself.
The termination has therefore been set aside.
[Para 9] C In accordance with the Food Corporation of India's Staff Regulations, Regulation 63, disciplinary action can be taken for conduct that has led to a criminal conviction.
The respondent in this case was dismissed from their job based solely on their conviction, without consideration given to the conduct that led to it.
As a result, the challenged order of dismissal cannot be upheld.
(Rajinder Singh v. Punjab State, 1969 Curl.LJ 821 foll).
