Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited Conduct, Discipline and Appeal Rules, 1975 - Disciplinary proceedings - Punishment of reduction in rank - The management investigated 14 witnesses, and one of the witnesses whose affidavit was filed by the appellant in support of his defence testified against him when produced by the management.
The appellant subsequently withdrew from proceedings and another witness was examined without his knowledge.
It was held that strict rules of evidence do not apply to such proceedings; the only requirement is that the allegation must be established by evidence that would allow a reasonable person acting reasonably and objectively to arrive at a finding upholding the gravamen of the charge.
The court will only interfere in cases of mala fides or perversity.
In the present case, the charges were established by overwhelming evidence, and the Enquiry Officer acted reasonably and objectively by holding the charges proved.
Therefore, the petition was dismissed.
