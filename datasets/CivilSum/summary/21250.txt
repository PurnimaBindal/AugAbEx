Under Section 22 of the Trade Union's Act, 1926 and Section 10-B of the Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, an interim restraint order was issued to appellants No. 1-Union and its members prohibiting them from assembling within 100 meters of the boundary of the Company, obstructing ingress and egress of vehicles, loyal workers, foreign customers, and visitors into the company premises until the suit was disposed of.
However, the interim injunctions would not affect the appellant's right to strike or exercise any other rights according to the Industrial Disputes Act or the Trade Union Act.
Furthermore, the government directed Company's management to provide work to all strikers who returned to work, except those who were accused of committing crimes against the management.
Ultimately, nothing remained in the appeal, and it was disposed of.
