Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administrative Tribunal Act, 1985, Sections 15 and 28 - Jurisdiction - A casual laborer/worker like a Nominal Muster Roll employee has no civil post nor appointment to any civil service of the State - A Writ in High Court is maintainable.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Administrative Tribunal Act, 1985, Sections 15 and 28 - Discrimination - Govt. cannot force workers to work on starving wages - Casual laborer to agree to Nominal Muster Roll entitled to remuneration as regular establishment counterparts.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Administrative Tribunal Act, 1985, Sections 15 and 28 - Jurisdiction of High Court - Casual laborer/worker like Nominal Muster Roll employed by the State Govt. can invoke for enforcement of his fundamental right under Article 14 and payment of equal pay for equal work to end his exploitation.
