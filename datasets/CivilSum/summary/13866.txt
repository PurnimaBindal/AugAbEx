Fundamental Rules, Rule 22 - Pay Fixation - A petitioner promoted to the grade of stenographer from the post of LDC has requested "reversion" back to the post of LDC because of their medical condition.
The intention behind the petition was to receive relief due to their medical condition.
The respondent has granted the request as the petitioner was unable to continue their duties as stenographer.
The expression "reversion," in this sense, is a loose one, and the question of semantics should not cloud the intention of the parties.
There has been no impediment pointed out that prevented the petitioner from being treated as having been posted or transferred back to the post of LDC.
As such, the petitioner is entitled to the benefit of Rule 22 of FR and consequent pay fixation at the maximum basic pay of the grade of LDC.
[Paras 11, 12 & 14]
