The petitioners are seeking to quash bye-laws requiring owners of nursing homes and clinics to obtain licenses and pay fees, arguing that as registered medical practitioners they have a right to practice without impediment.
However, the court ruled that municipal boards are empowered to make bye-laws and levy license fees, and that this does not impede the right to practice.
The fees are regulatory license fees, not taxes, and must be observed.
As such, the petition was dismissed.
