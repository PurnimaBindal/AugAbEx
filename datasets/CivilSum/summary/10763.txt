According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II Part I, Rule 7.17 and Red Cross Society Act, 1920, Section 5(b), an employee cannot work beyond the age of retirement and if re-employed, their term cannot exceed one year at a time and beyond 60 years in any case.
The Society's resolution regarding the maximum age for retirement of its employees is not without jurisdiction.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 state that appointments must follow the prescribed procedure.
Any appointments made outside the statutory service rules are void ab initio and do not create any legal rights.
