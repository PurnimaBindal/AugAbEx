Constitution of India, Article 309 - Rajasthan Educational Service Rules, 1971, Rule 4 - Laboratory Assistant - Appointment to post of Head Master - The legislature intended to treat Laboratory Assistants and Teachers at par, as evidenced by their common feeding channel for promotion to Senior Teacher.
Despite differences in their work style, experience acquired by Laboratory Assistants is still considered teaching experience.
Thus, the petitioner should be considered for appointment to the post of Head Master.
