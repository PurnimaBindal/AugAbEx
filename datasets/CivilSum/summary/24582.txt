The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 state that experience is a qualification for promotion if it has been gained for a sufficient period.
[Para ]
The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 require the state to consider the case of individuals who do not possess any qualifications for promotion.
[Paras 6, 7 & 8]
Experience can be considered as an alternate qualification, as experience gained for an adequate amount of time can be considered a qualification in itself.
[Para ]
