Punjab Civil Services (General and Common Conditions of service) Rules, 1994, Rule 8 - Seniority - Ad-hoc Service - Only regular service can be taken into account for calculating seniority, meaning that the period of ad-hoc service rendered by employees cannot be counted.
However, if an employee has already received payment based on a court's decision to include this period, the state government cannot retrieve the funds.
This decision is based on the case of 2000(4) SCT 664 SC.
