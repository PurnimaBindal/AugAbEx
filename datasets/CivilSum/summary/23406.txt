Haryana Senior Secondary Certificate Examination Regulations, 1990, Regulation 26(b) - Grace marks/Compartment/Education - A rule that did not allow for grace marks to obtain a compartment was previously rejected by a Full Bench of the High Court.
The Board's decision to deny grace marks to earn a compartment was inexplicably reversed by the High Court, and the benefit of grace marks for the Senior Secondary Certificate Examination in March 1993 was not granted.
The High Court's ruling was set aside, and the appeal was granted.
