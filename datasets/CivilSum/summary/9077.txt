Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-B, 25-F and 10(1)(c) - Unfair practice - Back wages - Termination - Reference - A workman who has worked for more than 240 days and has been found to have satisfactory work by authorities must not be unfairly terminated by the employer.
The Labour Court has ruled that attempts to deprive a workman of continuity of service for a specified period amount to an unfair labour practice unless the conduct of the workman was unsatisfactory.
The Labour Court ordered the workman to be reinstated with continuity of service and awarded 50% of back wages, which is a just and legal decision that does not require interference in writ jurisdiction under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India.
