As per Constitution of India, Article 226 regarding admission on Education subject, admission can only be granted subject to verification of the certificates from the respective University.
Upon investigation, it was found that certificates issued by Varanaseya Sanskrit Vishwa Vidyalaya, which was not established by any Government, were found to be fake.
As a result, all such admissions were cancelled and the principle of estoppel cannot be applied.

As per Constitution of India, Article 226 regarding interim orders, admission granted on the basis of interim direction does not confirm any right.
If the writ petition is dismissed and no such relief is granted on final decision, the interim relief becomes non-existent.
