Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Qualifications - Approval to appointment - Rejection for want of qualifications - The Respondents advertised the post of P.T.I.
with matric and CPEd., stating that preference will be given to persons with higher qualifications.
The Petitioner was selected and appointed despite possessing higher qualifications than those prescribed.
However, the DPI wrongly refused to accord approval, disregarding the Petitioner's qualifications.
The court directed DPI to accord approval forthwith and the Petitioner's salary to be paid from the date of their appointment.
The Petition was allowed with costs of Rs. 5,000/-.
