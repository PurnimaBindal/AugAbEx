Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Family pension - Requirements for qualify as regular service for family pension - Medical examination for regularization in service - A person who has completed all formalities for regularization in service is assumed to have been regularised - Every government action is understood to be in accordance with the law unless proven otherwise - The authorities cannot benefit from their own mistakes and argue that the deceased was regularised in service without medical examination, especially without producing his service record - It is accepted that the deceased was in service for 25 years and was regularized in service much before his death - His family cannot be allowed to suffer - The authorities should take a positive stance in such a situation and improve their policies accordingly - The petitioner is entitled to family pension with arrears and interest.
