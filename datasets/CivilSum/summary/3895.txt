The petitioner, a Director at the Centre for Adult Continuing Education and Extension at Panjab University, filed nomination papers to run for the Panjab University Senate in the Registered Graduates Constituency.
Despite objections from Respondent No. 4, the Returning Officer deemed the nomination papers valid.
However, Respondents Nos. 5 to 7 submitted further objections to the Vice Chancellor, arguing that the petitioner was not eligible to run because he was a whole-time paid servant of the university not on the teaching side.
The Vice Chancellor ultimately rejected the nomination papers.
The Court held that Regulations made under Section 31(2)(a) of the Act can provide for procedural as well as substantive matters.
While the Vice Chancellor has the authority to adjudicate disputes raised under Section 13(6) of the Act, he cannot decide on the eligibility of a candidate to run in the election.
The Returning Officer has the power to decide on a candidate's eligibility, and any appeals against that decision can only be made after the election.
Regulation 17(viii) of the Regulations, in so far as it provides for appeal to the Vice-Chancellor, is considered ultra vires Section 31(1) of the Act and in conflict with Section 38 of the Act.
