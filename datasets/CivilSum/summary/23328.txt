Constitution of India, Article 226 - Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Section 22 (Clause ii) - Certified Standing Orders framed under the Act - Right to legal assistance - Delinquent workman entitled to be represented by an office-bearer of another Trade Union not affiliated with any recognised or non-recognised union within the concerned undertaking - Statutes, Rules, Regulations or Standing Orders may regulate or restrict the right to be represented by counsel or agent - The principles of natural justice do not extend to the right of representation through Counsel or Agent - These provisions aim to ensure speedy and efficient completion of domestic enquiries, and representation by someone from the department or establishment would lead to better service.
Moreover, this would result in swift and economical proceedings.
Hence, it cannot be argued that Section 22(ii) or Standing Orders conflict with natural justice.
