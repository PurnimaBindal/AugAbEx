Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, Section 21 - Termination - Backwages - A conductor was terminated on charges of tampering with way bills - After a fair and proper enquiry was held, the termination was found justified by the Labour Court - The petitioner filed a writ petition but was killed by terrorists within 10 days - The issue of payment of backwages arose - It was directed that 50% of the backwages of the deceased be paid to his widow - This direction was deemed to be a decree under section 21 of the Legal Services Authorities Act.
