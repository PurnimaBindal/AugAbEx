Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 39(d) - Administrative Tribunals Act, Section 14 - Regularisation - A primary school designed by the government for the children of employees of Ordnance Factory - A secondary school set up by the employees of Ordnance Factory - Central Government cannot be held responsible for this arrangement.
Teachers of the secondary school cannot claim the pay scale available to Govt School Teachers or expect the Central Govt to regularize their service.
The Tribunal's directions to the government to authorize the school and create positions and contemplate such teachers for the position are unsustainable.
