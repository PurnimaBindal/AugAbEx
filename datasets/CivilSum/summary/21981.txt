Constitution of India, Article 226 - Select panel - Subsequent appointment - Permissibility - Post of Assistant Advocate General - A committee made a select panel of 3 candidates - The first appointee resigned and the second refused the appointment - The petitioner, ranked third in the panel, seeks appointment to the post - The court rejected petitioner's contention that mere empanelment confers a right to the appointment - The panel ceased to exist after the appointment of the first candidate, and no one else in the panel can legitimately contend for the post in case of a vacancy.
