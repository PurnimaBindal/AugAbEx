The petitioner is entitled to count his entire period of service until his retirement for the purpose of computing pension because his service, whether under one department or the other, was liable to be taken into account for determining pension under Punjab Civil Services Rules - Volume 1, Part 1, Rule 10.9.
This includes his service as Prosecuting Sub-Inspector in Punjab and his later appointment as Executive Officer under the Department of Local Govt., Punjab, where he was given benefits of promotion and regularisation in the Directorate of prosecution and litigation.
[Para 3]
Non-compliance with Section 44 of the Punjab Municipal Act, 1911, which requires an employee partly funded by the government and partly by a committee to pay contributions towards their own pension and leave allowance, means the liability is transferred to the committee.
The Department of Local Govt. is liable to contribute towards the petitioner's pension and leave salary contribution, and despite being advised of this, the petitioner has not been granted the requisite relief.
A direction has been issued for payment of pension with interest at a rate of 12% per month.
