Section 2A of the Uttar Pradesh Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 deals with granting backwages to employees.
In a case where a workman raised a dispute against their termination seven years after it occurred, the Labour Court ordered their reinstatement with full backwages.
However, the High Court later reduced the backwages to 50%.
The court held that the question of payment of backwages is independent of the question of reinstatement, and all relevant circumstances must be considered when determining backwages.
The workman is not entitled to backwages from the date of termination till the date of the award, but is entitled to 50% backwages from the date of the award till the reinstatement.
The award was modified accordingly.
