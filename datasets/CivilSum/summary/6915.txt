Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Merit list - Expiry of merit list - Any merit list of a selection will lapse after six months, as per Government instructions.
The court cannot direct appointments from lapsed lists unless there was arbitrariness by the appointing authority in violation of the merit.
The appointing authority has the jurisdiction to declare a select list as lapsed in accordance with Government instructions.
[Paras 13 to 15]
