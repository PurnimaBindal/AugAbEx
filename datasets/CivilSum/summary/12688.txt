Letters Patent Appeal, Clause X - Departmentalising of workmen - The learned Single Judge did not consider the written statement filed by the respondents before making a crucial decision about the dispute.
The written statement contains important documents and information that would help in reaching a correct conclusion on the crucial question involved in the case.
Therefore, considering the written statement is essential, and the case has been remitted back to the learned Single Judge for a fresh decision.
[Paras 4 and 5]
