Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 226 - Delhi Judicial Service Rules, 1970, Rule 33 - Judicial Service Examination - Revaluation - The Delhi Judicial Service Rules do not have a provision for rechecking or revaluation, unlike the UPSC Rules - Rule 33 of the Delhi Judicial Service Rules only pertains to "conditions of service" in the absence of such provision in the rules - If there is no rule or provision for revaluation, no direction can be given for revaluation.
