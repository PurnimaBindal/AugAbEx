The appointment of petitioner as the post of Member Company Law Board is challenged by respondent No. 3, claiming that it was done in violation of rules.
Respondent No. 3's name was rejected by Appointment Committee of Cabinet (ACC) due to an allegation against him.
ACC directed the Ministry to submit a fresh proposal but the Ministry forwarded the petitioner's name.
This gave respondent No. 3 cause to challenge petitioner's appointment, as if he succeeds in proving his point, there will be a fresh Selection.
However, if petitioner's appointment is upheld, respondent No. 3 would lose the chance of reconsideration by the Selection Committee.
It is held that respondent No. 3 has locus standi to challenge petitioner's appointment.
[Para 9] B The status of a member of CLB is treated at par with corresponding status employees of Central Government.
Even if a member of CLB wants to relinquish his officer, he must first obtain permission from the Central Government.
Therefore, a member of CLB is deemed to hold a 'civil post' under the Government of India, and his service conditions fall under the jurisdiction of the Central Administrative Tribunal.
