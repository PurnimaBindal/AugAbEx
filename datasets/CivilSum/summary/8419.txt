Canara Bank Service Code, Chapter XI, Regulations 4(d) and 12(3) - Suspension period - Service benefit - The respondent was suspended pending an enquiry and later dismissed.
The suspension order merged with the dismissal order, but the appellate authority modified the penalty to four increments with cumulative effect.
When the dismissal order is set aside, the suspension order does not revive.
Therefore, the period of suspension cannot be taken into account for the purpose of computing service benefits.
