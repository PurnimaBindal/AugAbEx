As per Constitutional guidelines, a volunteer should be given preference over a non-volunteer for transfer on compassionate grounds, as outlined in Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
Despite being the senior-most petitioner who volunteered for transfer, with vacant posts at Calicut Air Port and the requisition of Calicut authorities, the petitioner's request for transfer was not properly considered.
However, Article 16 of the Constitution guarantees equality in all matters related to public employment, including transfer requests, and the court has the jurisdiction to interfere when power is exercised arbitrarily and violates Articles 14 and 16.
Ultimately, the respondents are directed to post the petitioner at Calicut within two weeks.
