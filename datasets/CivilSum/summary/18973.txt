Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Full leave encashment - Whether an Air Force personnel taking voluntary retirement before completion of the qualifying service in Air Force would be entitled to encashment of entire leave balance?
The petitioner sought voluntary discharge from service after 15 years and 8 months of service, but was given only 90 days leave encashment instead of 196 days.
The challenge to the denial of leave encashment of 196 days asserts discrimination as respondents were giving full leave encashment to personnel discharged on medical ground as services of whom are no longer required.
Respondents were liable to follow a uniform policy.
Air Force personnel who have been medically boarded out or of whom services are no longer required have to be treated differently from those who seek voluntary retirement.
Service being below 17 years quantum of leave encashment admissible to the petitioner would be 90 days and not 196 days.
[Paras 8, 9 & 10]
