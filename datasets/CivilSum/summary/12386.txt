Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab State Electricity Board Ministerial Services Class-III Regulation, 1985, Rule 8 - Seniority - Initially employed as a peon, workman's services were regularised and promoted to LD Clerk via 15% promotion quota on ad hoc basis - Industrial tribunal ruled in favour of workman, awarding them seniority and proficiency benefits - Petitioners seeking to reject this award - Ruling established that seniority cannot be affected by a promotion's ad hoc designation once the promotion is made according to the rules - Any procedural errors can be fixed after subsequent regularisation.
