Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 300A - Entitlement Rules 1982, Rule 17(b) Disability Pension- Petitioner retired with disability pension of 20% due to disability occurred during service - Received pension payments for 24 years continuously - At 73 years old, denied pension due to reduction in disability - Medical Board found petitioner's disability of 20% remained - Medical Authority disagreed without giving any reason or basis - Responsibility lies on respondent to provide explanation - Impugned order is arbitrary - Order cannot be upheld and is quashed.
