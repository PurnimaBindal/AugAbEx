As per the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, the petitioner who is employed as an Executive Engineer must pass a test.
Although the petitioner appeared in two papers, unfortunately they were unable to obtain the minimum 45% overall aggregate even after being granted grace marks, missing the required grade by just 050 marks.
The rules regarding this matter are clearly stated and there is no room for interpretation.
However, if the state deems it permissible, they may relax the rule.
Therefore, this petition is considered without merit.
