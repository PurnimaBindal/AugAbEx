The Calcutta Dock Workers (Regulation of Employment) Scheme of 1970, Section 2, only applies to registered dock workers and their registered employers, and those who are not registered workers are not entitled to any benefits.
The petitioners who have been doing loading and unloading work at the Calcutta port for over 30 years, even if working under registered employers, are not entitled to the relief sought because they are not registered workers and no employer-employee relationship subsists.
This is in accordance with Section 3 of the Dock Workers (Regulation of Employment) Act, 1948.
