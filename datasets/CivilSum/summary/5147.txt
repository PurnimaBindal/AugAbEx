Constitution of India, Art. 226 - Writ - Service - Petitioner seeking full pension and other retiral benefits - Petitioner retired as Commandant in BSF - Petitioner retired at age 55, three years earlier than higher-ranking officers and other central government employees - Petitioner granted pension on pro-rata basis - Ruling requires Respondents to re-fix petitioner's pension to full amount - Petitioner is entitled to arrears of unpaid pension within 3 months with 9% pa. interest if not paid.
