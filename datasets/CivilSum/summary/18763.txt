Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 309 - U.P.
Government Servants (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1999, Rule 4(1) - Suspension - The order of suspension that does not pertain to any inquiry against an employee or the fact of any pending inquiry is invalid under Rule 4 of the UP Government Servants (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1999 and cannot be upheld.
