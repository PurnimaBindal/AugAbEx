According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, a recovery of payments already made due to wrong fixation of pay cannot be permissible if the employee did not contribute to the mistake and there was no fraud or misrepresentation.
Unauthorisedly made recoveries should be refunded to the employee with 6% interest.
[Paras 6 & 7] B Article 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India allows for correction of mistakes in pay at any stage, however, only recovery may not be possible.
Article 14 cannot be used to claim similar benefit if some similarly situated persons had received the same under mistake.
