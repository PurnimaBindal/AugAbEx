The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 14, Punjab Civil Services (Revised Scales of Pay) Rules, 1979, Rule 7(1) Proviso (ii), and Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service Rules), 1979, Rule 7 all deem that the pay and allowances received by employees working in Privately Managed Recognised Schools should not be less than those of employees of the State Government holding similar posts.
When the scales of pay are lower, the managing committee should be directed to bring them up to par with those of State Government employees.
Additionally, the method of pay fixation should not differ, and if a junior receives higher pay, the senior person should be entitled to an extra increment in the same way as in a Government School.
This petition has been allowed.
