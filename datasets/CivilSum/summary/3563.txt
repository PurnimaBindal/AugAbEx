Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, Section 45 - Powers of Corporation to make regulations - The term "Previous Sanction" is not defined - A mere sanction by the government does not ensure the regulation's implementation - The draft of the regulation for merging two posts in different departments was not approved after its government sanction - The respondent's seniority computation under said regulation is improper - The petitioners have the right to be included in the seniority list of junior assistants (purchase) and to be considered for promotion to higher posts in the purchase department.
