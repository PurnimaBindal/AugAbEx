The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 and Pension Regulations of Army, 1961, Part I, Regulation 173 states that disability pensions can only be granted if the disability is attributable to military service and has been assessed by a Medical Board.
Thus, the rejection of the petitioner's disability pension due to lack of evidence cannot be considered unjustified.
