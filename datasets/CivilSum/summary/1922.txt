Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(1) and 12(5) - Reference - The appropriate Govt. can only examine the existence or apprehension of an industrial dispute between parties without delving into the merits of the dispute - The determination of whether workers were Helpers or Badli workers is a question of fact to be determined on evidence, and the Govt. cannot decline a reference on this ground - The Govt.'s decision in this case cannot be upheld.
