Letters Patent Appeal, Clause X - Parity of pay - The appellant contested the decision granting daily wagers equal pay with regular employees under Clause X of Letters Patent Appeal - The verdict of the Apex Court in the Ghaziabad Development Authority case was noted, which indicated that regular employment was required for a regular pay scale - The daily wagers' service terms should not be compared to those of regular employees - Daily wagers are not subject to the disciplinary authority of their employer - The judgement of the learned single Judge was reversed - The appeal was granted.
