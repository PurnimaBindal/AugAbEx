Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work - Discrimination - While the post of librarian cannot be deemed equivalent to that of Research Assistant in regional languages due to differences in qualifications and other factors, the Fifth Pay Commission recommended that the respondent be granted a replacement Scale of Rs. 6500-10,500/- and the learned tribunal was directed to consider this.
However, there was no factual foundation for this decision and no precise mathematical formula can be used in such a situation.
The High Court and Tribunal did not agree with this finding.
