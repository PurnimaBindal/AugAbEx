In accordance with the Kerala Co-operative Societies Act of 1969, Section 80, and Rules 187 and 189(3) of the Kerala Co-operative Societies Rules, Dearness allowance can be claimed at the rate applicable to Government employee.
Rule 189(3) applies to all employees of the Co-operative Societies, classified or unclassified.
Thus, the respondent is entitled to dearness allowance at the Government rate.
