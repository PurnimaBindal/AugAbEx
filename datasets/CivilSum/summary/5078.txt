Involuntary termination results in the general rule of reinstatement for the petitioner, with compensation being awarded only in exceptional cases when illegality is proved.
Section 9, 11 and 19 of the 1999 Act relied upon by the Labour Court were deemed unconstitutional, therefore the petitioner is entitled to reinstatement.
Back wages, however, will not be granted due to a lack of exceptional circumstances.
