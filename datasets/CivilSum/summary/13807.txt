According to Sections 2(d) and 3(b) of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, the term "dependant" includes the mother of the deceased, even if she is living with the father and not entirely dependant on her son.
Additionally, a parent other than a widowed mother who is partially dependent on the earnings of the workman also falls within the definition of "dependant."
Thus, the order refusing the claim of the mother is inappropriate.
