Motor Vehicle Act, 1988, Sections 166 and 168 - Motor accident - When a driver stops the vehicle for unloading of goods, compensation can still be claimed for accidents that occur not only when the vehicle is moving but also when it is stationary.
[Paras 6 and 8] B Motor Vehicle Act, 1988, Section 167 - In a case where a claimant was employed as a driver and is bitten while unloading his vehicle, the insurance company with whom the vehicle was insured is liable to indemnify the owner for compensation awarded by the Workmen's Compensation Commissioner as the injury occurred during the course of employment and when the motor vehicle was under use.
