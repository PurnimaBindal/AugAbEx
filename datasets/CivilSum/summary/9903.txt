The petitioner, who is a trained teacher Grade III, filed a writ petition contending that he was entitled to regular pay scale since he had attained the requisite qualification of B.Ed.
as per rules, and the appointment was regular.
However, the respondent denied regular pay scale on the ground that the petitioner's B.Ed.
(correspondence) was not recognized by the State.
The court held that B.Ed.
(correspondence) from Maharishi Dayanand University is equivalent to the said qualification obtained by any other similarly placed candidate from the University of Rajasthan.
Consequently, it is not permissible for the respondent to discriminate against the petitioner, which would be grossly violative of Articles 14 and 16.
The petitioner is entitled to the benefit of revised pay scale with effect from November 23, 1991.
