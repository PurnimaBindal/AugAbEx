The petitioner was selected for deputation from Air Force to Public Sector Undertaking, and opted for premature retirement from the Air Force upon permanent absorption into the public sector undertaking.
However, he was denied weightage on account of absorption into the public sector undertaking, despite other officers in the same stream being allowed weightage for taking premature retirement for personal reasons.
This denial is deemed arbitrary and violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution, and the respondents have been directed to include 7 years weightage in computing pension and 5 years for calculating gratuity.
