The lecturers working in Vaish Arya Kanya Mahavidyalaya, MD University, are entitled to selection grade and senior scale as per University Rules and Regulations, according to Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226.
The court has directed the respondents to consider the case of the petitioners for grant of selection grade and senior scale from the due date and recommend their cases to the Dean, College Development Council, MD University.
[Paras 2 and 31]
