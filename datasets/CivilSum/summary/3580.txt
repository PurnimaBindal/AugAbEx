Constitution of India, Articles 14, 311 and 226 - Absence from duty - Dismissal from service - Quantum of punishment - Natural justice - The punishment authority must not take into consideration things extraneous to the charge-sheet as it results in violating the principles of natural justice.
The delinquent in this case proved his illness and submitting a leave application through his son for the period alleged in the charge-sheet, which the authority wrongly took as an admission of guilt.
In such a scenario, the Court can apply the "Wednesbury Principles" to determine the appropriate punishment, where wholly irrelevant material and considerations have been taken into account, or relevant material and considerations have been ignored.
The Court can substitute the order of dismissal with an order of compulsory retirement with all consequential benefits.
