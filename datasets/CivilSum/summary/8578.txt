According to Section 2 Item 6 of the Industrial Disputes Act, the petitioner claimed promotion as Junior Assistant after acquiring a BA degree and serving for 7 years in the bank.
Despite fulfilling both conditions, she was not given the promotion because she opted for the position of typist.
However, denying her promotion is not justifiable.
Moreover, the notional promotion awarded to her in 1977 cannot be considered unjust or unreasonable.
[Paras 3 and 4]
