The appellant challenged an order removing him from the service of the Board, arguing that it was passed by an incompetent authority and demanded full wages and benefits.
The court found that if an order of termination is null and void, the employee is entitled to full wages and to be treated as continuing in service.
The Board has been instructed to reinstate the appellant and pay full back wages.
[Paras 7, 11 and 12] B.
The power of removing an employee of the Board lies with the Board, and the Superintending Engineer's order removing the appellant is null and void.
