Constitution of India, Article 311 - Warehousing Corporation Act, 1962, Section 42 - AP State Warehousing Corporation Employees Regulations, 1965, Regulation 4 - Reversion - The A.P.
State Warehousing Corporation made appointments of Senior Regional Managers after upgrading their posts, but the State Government objected and downgraded the posts.
The petitioners argued that they were entitled to continue in the upgraded posts, irrespective of any Rule or Regulation of the Corporation. The State Government had no power to raise the objection.
