According to the Haryana Employment Department (Group `B') Service Rules, 1991, Rule 9, ad hoc promotions cannot be given outside of the rules for recruitment, which stipulate that 25% of posts are to be filled by promotion and the remaining 75% by direct recruitment, as stated in the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226.
The petitioners were illegally given ad hoc promotions for six months against direct quota vacancies, but the policy of the government is not to allow such promotions and instead recruit regular direct quota appointments.
The reversion of the petitioners to their original post held is considered legal, and they have no legal right to hold the posts of direct quota.
