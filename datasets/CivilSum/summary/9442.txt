Correction of Date of Birth is being sought by the Appellant, but there are contradictory dates in his horoscope and municipal record.
The date entered in his Matriculation Certificate cannot be relied upon, as it was taken after eight years of joining service.
The Appellant failed to produce the best evidence, i.e. School Leaving Certificate or extracts of admission register at the time of entry to school.
Thus, the Appellant could not prove his case for correction of the date of birth in service record.
