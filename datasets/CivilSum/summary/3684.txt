The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947's Section 2(ra), and the Employment Exchange (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act of 1959 all apply to the case of a daily wage worker receiving equal pay for an equivalent workload.
The principle may not be invoked if the daily wager is hired to fill a vacant position, provided that the hiring violates the 1959 Act and the equality doctrine enshrined in Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution; furthermore, there is no clear evidence on record to show that the nature of duties for the daily-wage worker is comparable to that of regular workers.
