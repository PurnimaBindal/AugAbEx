The successful completion of the selection process for a sub-inspector of Police does not guarantee appointment if the petitioner is involved in a criminal case.
Although the petitioner was eventually acquitted, the subsequent acquittal does not grant the petitioner the right to seek employment in respect of a selection completed several years ago.
Thus, the writ appeal is dismissed.
[Paras 5 and 6]
The petitioner's failure to challenge the endorsement to representation in 1990 deprives them of the foundational right to seek employment after a lapse of several years.
Therefore, there is no vested right to seek employment after years of delay and laches, leading the court to dismiss the petition.
