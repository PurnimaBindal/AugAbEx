The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 state that admissions in Army Institute of Law, privately established without any aid from the Government, reserve spots for Army personnel, serving or retired.
However, Navy and Air Force personnel are not eligible for the reservation.
The court cannot direct to make reservation for any additional categories, as it is up to the authorities of the Institute to decide which categories receive preference through reservation.
Additionally, if some reservation is made for the general category, the Air Force personnel cannot claim discrimination.

Admissions in Army Institute of Law follow the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226’s guidelines for reservation of Army personnel serving or retired.
Only eligible Army personnel may apply for these reserved spots, and they are classified into specific categories.
Candidates must submit the appropriate eligibility certificate for the category they are applying to and cannot switch to a category requiring a different type of certificate.
