Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rajasthan Subordinate Service (Recruitment and other Service Conditions) Rules, 1960, Rule 17 - Seniority - Posts of Excise Inspectors Gr.II - Respondents No. 3 to 7 were appointed according to the procedure prescribed under Rule 17, and their appointment should be considered as regular substantive appointments for all purposes.
The appointment orders of the respondents using the word "temporary" would not make any difference, and the seniority lists were rightly issued.
Hence, respondents No. 3 to 7 were made senior to the petitioners as they were appointed in 1970 while petitioners in 1973.
[Paras 17, 18 and 19] B.
Appointment - Regular and substantive capacity - The court has to look into the mode of selection, manner of selection, and nature of vacancy in determining whether a particular appointment was in a substantive capacity.
An appointment made after calling for applications through an advertisement and preparing a select list/merit list on the recommendation of a duly constituted selection committee, indicates that it is a regular and substantive appointment and not just 'ad hoc'.
