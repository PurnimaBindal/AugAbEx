Constitution of India, Article 16 - Mizoram Information and Public Relations and Tourism Department (Class III posts) Recruitment Rules, 1973 - Mizoram Information Public Relations and Tourism Department (Group B) Posts Recruitment Rules, 1976 - Seniority - Petitioners were listed behind RG-II Officers who were actually junior to them - According to the recruitment rules and principles of law, the appointment of petitioners was correct - As a result, seniority must be calculated from the date of appointment - The assertion of the respondents that RG-II officers were selected in a higher scale and thus, are senior to petitioners was dismissed - Educational qualifications, duties, and powers were comparable for both - It is unjustifiable to rank the writ petitioners below the RG-II officers and allocation of inter-se seniority is completely arbitrary - reversed - Petitioners are eligible to be positioned higher than RG-II officers.
