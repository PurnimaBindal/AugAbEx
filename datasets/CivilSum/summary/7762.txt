Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1972, Section 44 - Termination of Service - The Labour Court has determined that the petitioner had completed 240 days of continuous employment and that the respondent violated section 25-F of the I.D.
Act.
The Court has directed the employer to reinstate the petitioner with full back wages and continuity of service.
The Industrial Court is authorized to interfere with the order of the Labour Court only when there is an apparent error of law on the record.
Therefore, the Industrial Court’s reversal of the Labour Court's determination is not appropriate.
