The Constitution of India states that only those who were in the service of Development Authorities can be absorbed under Section 5-A(2) of the Uttar Pradesh Planning and Development Act, 1973, and employees of Jal Nigam are not entitled to the benefit of Rule 7.
Section 7(1) of the Act and Rule 37(2) of the Uttar Pradesh Development Authorities Rules, 1985 will apply to Jal Nigam employees, and they will be placed at the bottom of the seniority of those absorbed under Section 5-A(2) and Rule 7(1), without being able to claim the benefit of their past service rendered in Jal Nigam.
Seniority is a civil right, and not a fundamental right.
While making an offer of adjustment and absorption of employees of different establishments, the Uttar Pradesh Development Authorities Centralised Services Rules, 1986, as amended in 2002, imposes conditions.
Once a deputationist accepts the conditions of absorption as to his seniority to be placed below the officers already appointed in the cadre, he cannot complain of violations of Articles 14 and 16 in fixing his seniority.
No fundamental right exists for counting service rendered in another body unless Rules permit the same.
When Rules are framed in exercise of powers conferred by an Act, the Rules so framed would be deemed to be a part of the Act.
In the absence of rules, no employer can force an employee to join the service of another employer on deputation.
Benefit of past service can be given only when there exists a rule directing consideration of seniority on that basis.
However, where recruitments are made from various sources, it would be reasonable to frame a rule considering past services of the employees.
When the question of seniority inter se different groups of employees is involved, no writ can be issued in the absence of those whose rights are likely to be adversely affected.
