Constitution of India, Articles 226, 341 and 342 - Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes - Declaration of - The court is not authorized to declare a specific caste or tribe as Scheduled under Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Order 1950 - Simply because Lohars is synonymous with Loharas, the court cannot declare both to be equal and eligible to receive the same status.
(Paras 20 to 23)
