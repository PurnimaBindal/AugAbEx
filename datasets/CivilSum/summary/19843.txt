According to the Constitution of India, which includes articles 14, 16, and 226, pay scale, Assistant Grade Examination, and retrospective amendment, the petitioner was promoted without the condition of passing a qualifying test.
However, later on, a condition was added by way of amendment to the rules.
The petitioner cannot be forced to qualify the test as equality of grades has no relation to the Assistant examination.
