The Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II Rule 2.7, pertains to Ex-gratia Grant on account of disability.
The Punjab Govt. Policy Instructions based on 4th Punjab Pay Commission recommendations, emphasize that claims of disabled persons cannot be decided based on the Rule and policy instructions that relate to persons, matters, and topics other than ex-gratia payment awarded to an employee who is medically incapacitated during his service.
In addition to other terminal benefits, disabled persons are entitled to ex-gratia payment according to the policy.
