Constitution of India, Article 14 - MD University Calender Vol. II Clause 4(I) - Unfair means - Petitioner's behavior of not tying up answer sheets 15 minutes before the examination does not constitute disobedience or misbehavior and cannot be considered as use of unfair means - The punishment imposed on the petitioner is held as without jurisdiction.
