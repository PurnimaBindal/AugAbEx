Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Appendix II, Rule 2 - Entitlement Rules to Casualty Pensionary Awards to the Armed Forces Personnel, 1982, Para 13 - Disability pension - To claim disability pension, the injury suffered during leave period must have some direct or casual connection with the functions of the military service - If the injury occurs while going home on leave or coming from home to join, it may be attributed to service - However, injuries received while doing personal work at home during leave period have no connection to service - In this case, the petitioner got injured while driving his tractor for agricultural purposes during leave - Therefore, this injury cannot be attributed to service - Although, once he is discharged on account of disability at the completion of his engagement term, he will be entitled to the service element of the disability pension.
