The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F state that a meter reader appointed on contract may be terminated in the event of non-compliance with provisions.
In this case, the Labour Court directed the reinstatement of a worker who had completed 240 continuous days of work in the last 12 months.
However, the High Court saw the contract as a facade, the material on record showing that the contract was for a specific and conditional period that was dispensed with once regular meter readers were appointed.
The contract clearly governed the terms of employment.
Furthermore, the provisions of Section 2(oo)(bb) of the Act specifically applied to the situation.
The orders were ultimately set aside.
