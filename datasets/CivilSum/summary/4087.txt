The Employees Provident Funds & Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 7-A only applies to daily wage employees not covered by a Provident Fund scheme.
The petitioner has no entitlement to the benefits of a contributory provident fund or old age pension for daily wagers employed and therefore cannot claim benefits under S. 16(1)(b) and (c) of the Act.
[Para 13] B.
If the petitioner wishes to claim the benefit of S. 16(2), it must apply to the Central Government to establish why it should be exempted from the provisions of the Act.
If the Central Government is satisfied, it may issue a notification granting the exemption.
The petitioner cannot escape the provisions of the Act without such exemption and may seek exemption if advised to do so.
