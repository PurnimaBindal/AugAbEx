Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation), Act, 1996, Section 47 - Alternative employment - A petitioner who was unfit for the position of conductor was not offered any alternative jobs.
The argument that the petitioner could not claim benefits under the Act after benefiting from voluntary retirement is not valid.
There is no legal doctrine that can thwart the statute.
Moreover, there is no evidence that the petitioner's early retirement was due to an option he exercised.
The respondent has been instructed to assign the petitioner to either the position of conductor or a suitable position with the same salary scale and employment benefits or to keep him on a supernumerary post until the appropriate job becomes available.
