According to Article 21 of the Constitution of India, compassionate appointment cannot be refused simply due to the fact that the family of the deceased is already receiving benefits from various welfare schemes.
[Para 14]
As per Article 300-A of the Constitution of India, pension is not a form of concession or a favor to either the employee or their family, but is earned by the employee for their years of service.
It is considered a property under the Constitution.
