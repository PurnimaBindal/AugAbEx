Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Army Pension Regulation, Regulation 173 - Disability pension - Petitioner's claim for disability pension rejected - Petitioner was downgraded to Medical category EEE and invalided from army on the same date with 60% disability - However, the medical report did not state that the disability was due to a constitutional disease or an undetectable disease - As the injury occurred after six years of unblemished service and military service can cause strain and stress, it cannot be said that the disability was not attributable nor aggravated by military service - Direction given to respondents to consider granting disability pension to petitioner.
