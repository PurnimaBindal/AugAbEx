Based on Punjab Police Service Rules, 1959, Rules 6(1)(i)(a) and 14 with reference to List `G', promotion of DSP is dependent on several factors like seniority, length of service, and experience.
Relaxation of eligibility criteria and regularization of temporary promotions with retrospective dates must also be considered.
Inspectors become eligible and entitled to be placed in List `G' only after completing six years of service.
The promotion to DSP would require a minimum of four years' service as an inspector and being listed in List `G'.
However, it is important to note that procedural lapses will not lead to employee suffering.
The Rule has the power of relaxation, and promotions made without fulfilling the criteria have been legalized and regularized through its exercise.
If so, the period of temporary promotion will count towards the length of service and seniority, even if initially the promotions were inconsistent with the Rule.
