The criteria for adhoc promotions is seniority-cum-merit as per Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
The promotion of a junior over the petitioner, who had no adverse report, is deemed unacceptable according to Para 5.
If promotions are based on seniority-cum-merit, the Constitution of India (Articles 14 and 16) requires the respondents to give due weightage to seniority.
Unless a senior candidate is unfit or unsuitable, they cannot be overlooked for a promotion as stated in Para 6.
