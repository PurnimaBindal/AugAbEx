Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Termination of service for unauthorized absence - Departmental Enquiry conducted - Employee found guilty of unauthorized absence - Enquiry deemed fair and proper under natural justice principles - Dismissal from service imposed - Workman had a record of 15 absences - Gross violation of discipline - Labour Court and High Court ordered reinstatement without continuity of service and backwages - However, habitual absentism constitutes gross violation of discipline - Labour Court and High Court not justified in directing reinstatement - Orders overturned.
