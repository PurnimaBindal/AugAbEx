Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - M.P Development Authority Services (Officers and Servants) Recruitment Rules, 1987, Rule 4 - Regularisation - Petitioner is claiming regularisation against a vacancy reserved for the post of Sub-Engineer by claiming the benefits of reservation extended to the other backward classes.
However, no such reservation was provided in the recruitment rules and there was no existing vacancy in the post of Sub-Engineer.
It is not within the authority of any administrative order to modify or supplant statutory rules.
[Paras 16, 17 and 18]
