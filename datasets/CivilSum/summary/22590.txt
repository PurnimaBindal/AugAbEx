The State of Bihar Water Resources Department cannot legally issue an order regarding the seniority of Junior Engineers due to lack of power delegation from Personnel Department and the absence of any statutory provisions or rules.
Even the Resolution dated 1711979, an executive order passed by Bihar, did not lay down criteria for fixation of inter se seniority.
Order dated 22.12.1992 by Water Resources Department was therefore unauthorized and violates Article 162.
[Paras 15, 16, 30] Moreover, Experience of five years upon acquisition of a decree in the engineering is not considered a qualification laid down in Resolution dt. 1711979.
[Para 19]
A public notification has a binding effect on the authority that issued it and must be followed.
[Para 24]
There is no acquiescence on the part of affected persons or delay if the seniority list has been under challenge throughout.
[Para 26]
The principle of literal meaning and continuous officiation in the lower post should be applied in making promotions when there are no statutory rules.
[Para 28]
Sub-delegation is not permissible, and the Water Resources Department overstepped its authority by issuing an order regarding seniority.
