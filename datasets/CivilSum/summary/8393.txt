As per Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, an employee claimed a specific benefit for his services while he was at Ratnagiri.
However, he was subsequently transferred to Nasik where he retired.
An application was filed at the Labour Court, Nasik, but it was held that the claim for a one-time allowance did not pertain to the period of service that the employee rendered within the jurisdiction of the Nasik office.
As such, the Labour Court, Nasik did not have the jurisdiction to entertain the application.
