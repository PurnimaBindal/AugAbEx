Under Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the Labour Court has the authority to add any party deemed necessary for a fair and equitable decision - This ruling has been supported previously in cases such as Sub-Divisional Inspector of Post v. Theyyam Joseph, 1996(3) S.C.T.
786 and The Pepsu Road Transport Corporation v. Presiding Officer, 1991(3) S.C.T.
123.
