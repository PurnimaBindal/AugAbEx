According to Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India and Bye Laws 37-B of Haryana Co-operative Societies Act (22 of 1984), the Registrar alone has the power to declare a person as having ceased to be a member of the Managing Committee after proper notice is given to them.
Additionally, there was no evidence that the petitioner was served prior to a relevant enquiry, and no enquiry was conducted to determine if they had missed three consecutive meetings of the Managing Committee.
Therefore, the order of Deputy Registrar to remove the petitioner from the membership of the Managing Committee is invalidated since that power lies with the Registrar only.
