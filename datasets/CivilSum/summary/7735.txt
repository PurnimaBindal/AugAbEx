Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rajasthan Service Rules, Rule 26-A - Excess payment - Recovery of - It is appropriate to let a person keep any benefit received without fault on their part - Petitioner given benefit of Rule 26-A of Rajasthan Service Rules - Petitioner did not make any mistake receiving benefit - Mistake made by respondent - It is fair to let petitioner keep benefit received in these circumstances - Asking a retired person to repay an amount already received would result in legal injury.
