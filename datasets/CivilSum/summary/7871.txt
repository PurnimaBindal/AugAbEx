Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Termination - Validity of - Petitioner, appointed against a substantive post through selection, had service terminated on ground of no longer being required - Respondent claimed petitioner had no right to hold post as a temporary employee - Court held that the concept that temporary employees have no rights is not absolute but subject to Article 14 of the Constitution - Termination based on no longer being required after years of service was deemed unreasonable and arbitrary - Petition allowed.
