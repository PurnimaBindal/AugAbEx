The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - U.P.
State Universities Act, 1973, Section 50, 1st statute, Paras 16, 24(3) - Superannuation - Re- employment - Officiating post - The petitioner was re-employed as a teacher after superannuation, during which he worked as an officiating Principal but was paid as a teacher.
He is eligible for the difference in salary between teacher and Principal for the period of his officiation as Principal, but cannot officiate in that capacity during re-employment.
