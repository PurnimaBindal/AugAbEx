In the BHMS course, a petitioner claimed a seat under a quota reserved for children of ex-servicemen, but no free seats were available during counseling, and a seat later became available.
The seat was awarded to the respondent despite having a lower rank because of their choice of option.
The petitioner contends that clause 1110 of the prospectus required the candidate's choice of option to prevail.
However, a single judge rejected the respondent's position and found clause 1110 to be discriminatory and non-operative.
The commissioner of Entrance Examinations must comply with the quota, rank, and option rules to offer a seat that becomes vacant subsequently to the candidate of the particular quota in accordance with their rank.
The petitioner is directed to be admitted from academic year 2001-2002 and the respondent to continue their studies as one year has already passed.
