Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo)(c) - Retrenchment - A worker from Electricity Board was ruled medically unfit to work due to continued ill-health by Medical Board.
it was decided that the period he worked without difficulty was not a decisive factor.
Therefore, the orders of Labour and Industrial Courts have been quashed as it was held to be an improper case of retrenchment.
