According to the Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 25-B and 25-F and 2(oo), retrenchment is defined by service of 240 days in a calendar year, regardless of actual working days, ignoring all breaks.
Continuous service for an entire year is not required, and repeated appointments on 89-day bases between notional breaks count as retrenchment if termination follows after 240 days of work.
Any removal without notice and compensation under section 25-F contravenes the law.
[Paras 7, 8, and 9]

When invoking the Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 25, a workman employed on 89-day bases for three terms is not eligible for back wages if removal from service contravenes section 25-F.
Furthermore, the claimant must provide evidence of unemployability after discharge from 1977 to 1996.
[Para 10]
