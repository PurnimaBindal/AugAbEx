As per the Constitution of India, Article 226, absence from duty can lead to dismissal.
In this case, the petitioner, a constable, left without proper sanction of leave and failed to report back to duty after requesting four days leave.
Additionally, there is no evidence to suggest that the petitioner attempted to reschedule their meeting with their commanding officer.
Given the serious nature of misconduct for absence in a disciplined force, dismissal from service is not an illegal action.
