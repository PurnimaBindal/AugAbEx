Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25F - Termination - A workman, who was appointed as Secretary in a bank and whose appointment was periodically extended, was relieved of his services after completing 240 days of service.
The post of Secretary in the bank was deemed a regular post.
The provision of Section 25-F cannot be extended by simply claiming that the appointment was made for "89 days on daily wages".
Consequently, the Labour Court rightly awarded reinstatement with full back wages.
