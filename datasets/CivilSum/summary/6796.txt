The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1)(2) specifies that a compromise related to an industrial dispute reference cannot debar a workman from raising a dispute against his dismissal.
Accepting cheques sent after a workman's dismissal also does not count as a compromise.
Therefore, the government cannot decline a reference only because the workman accepted such cheques.
Refusal to make a reference due to non-application of mind is incorrect, and the government must reconsider making the reference.
