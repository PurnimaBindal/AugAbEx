Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F and 10 - Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Termination - Reference - A workman who was appointed on a daily wage basis had his services automatically terminated at the end of his appointment period - If the workman's appointment was of a contingent nature and for a specific period, it cannot be argued that his services were illegally terminated - The Labour Court's award passed in reference cannot be upheld and must be invalidated.
