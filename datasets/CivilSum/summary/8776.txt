Industrial Disputes Act, Section 33-C(2) - Scope - Holding back employer's provident fund contribution due to illegal retention of staff quarters after retirement - Withheld amount validly exercised under rules and instructions made under Life Insurance Corporation Act, 1956 Section 49 - Competent instructions deemed valid - Withholding of PF deemed justifiable and valid - Order to refund amount withheld found to be outside jurisdiction of Labour Court Section 33-C(2) - Ruling invalidated.
