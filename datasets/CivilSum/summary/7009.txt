As per Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, an award for reinstatement with 20% back wages must be given upon removal unless certain conditions are fulfilled.
In this case of alleged retrenchment in January 1985, the appropriate government was not notified until May 1997 and the management argues that compensation is more appropriate than reinstatement due to abandonment of the job.
The writ petition was considered 29 years later and the workman had only worked for eight months.
Therefore, it was deemed unjust to direct reinstatement and instead a compensation of Rs. 50,000/- should be paid.
