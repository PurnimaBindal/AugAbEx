Constitution of India, Article 311 - Departmental enquiry - The punishment given for misconduct should be proportionate - Proceedings were initiated against a police constable for alleged misconduct after he had an illicit relationship with another lady constable while still being married - The petitioner's earlier marriage ended in divorce - Dismissal from service for the alleged misconduct was disproportionate and has been overturned.
