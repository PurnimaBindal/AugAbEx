Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226 - Appointment - Denial of - Appellant's OBC certificate being deemed as not complying with rules - Appellant's request for an opportunity to correct the mistake, which was given to others in similar situations, was refused - The court found that given that no errors were made on the appellant's part, this denial is a clear example of favoritism and nepotism - Court ordered the consideration of the appellant's appointment.
