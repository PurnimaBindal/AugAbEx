Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Claim of pay scale - Denial on basis of source of recruitment - Petitioner retired as Assistant Executive Engineer from PSEB - Requests pay scale in which pay of juniors is fixed - Respondents argue that existing anomaly is incurable under current instructions and pay of juniors is fixed in scale being direct recruits whereas Petitioner is promotee - Court must intervene if there is no reasonable nexus between difference and/or recruitment - Petitioner cannot be denied benefit merely on basis of source of recruitment, since it is not a case where junior has higher qualification or performs special nature of duties - Ruling of `BBMB v/s Rajinder Singh Patpatia' is relied upon.
[Paras 8, 10 to 12]
