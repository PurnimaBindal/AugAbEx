Constitution of India, Article 26 - Correction of date of birth in service record in public services - Strict adherence to rule prescribing time limit - Record entry of date of birth based on employee's matriculation certificate and particulars submitted at appointment - Delayed requests for date of birth correction must be rejected in the absence of explanation and irrefutable evidence - Only reasonable requests made within an unspecified time limit can be considered - Contradictory public records should be avoided - No interim relief granted when compensation is adequate - Adverse affect on others with interim orders must not be ignored.
[Paras 9 & 12]
