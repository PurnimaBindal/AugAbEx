Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - West Bengal Primary School Teachers Recruitment Rules 2001, Rule 8 - Appointment as Primary School Teacher - The statutory rules state that 10 eligible candidates must be sponsored for every notified vacancy.
However, an employment officer sponsored 35 eligible candidates for a single vacancy, which was deemed unauthorized interference with the recruitment process and the order was therefore set aside.
