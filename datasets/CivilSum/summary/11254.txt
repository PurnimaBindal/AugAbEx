Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Estoppel - Criteria of selection - The selection cannot be challenged on the basis of unfair criteria if the authorities have laid down the criteria before the selection took place.
[Paras 10 and 26]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Estoppel - Interview marks - The mere difference in marks of interview alone is not a valid ground to interfere with the selection process on mere suspicion against fairness if there is no allegation of bias or interest of any member of the selection committee in any individual candidate.
The person alleging bias must substantiate the same with concrete evidence.
[Paras 16 to 18]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Result - Publication of result in press - The non-publication of the result in the press will not jeopardize the selection where every candidate who passes the exam is individually informed and called upon to participate in the interview, and cases of Pinki and Kuldeep Singh Rawat cannot be read to mean that every authority has to notify the result of selection in press.
[Paras 22 to 24]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Interview - Award of 15% marks for personal interview to determine the candidate's capacity to work in the rural area, their aptitude to deal with rural masses who are the main livestock owner, and their alertness and intelligence for the post of Veterinary Pharmacists is viewed as a good rationale.
