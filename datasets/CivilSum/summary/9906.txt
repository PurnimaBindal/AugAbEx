Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rules 7 and 8 - Department Proceedings - Natural Justice - Proceedings initiated under Rule 7 of the Rules - Converted without issuing show-cause notice to impose penalty under Rule 8 of the Rules - Imposing penalty under Rule 8 requires following the proper procedure - Order of punishment under Rule 8 declared improper and set aside - Department allowed to take further action according to the law.
