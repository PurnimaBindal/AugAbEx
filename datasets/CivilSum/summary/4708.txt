Constitution of India, Article 311 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(1)(c) - Compulsory retirement - Validity - The order of compulsory retirement must be based on a general evaluation of ACRs as well as financial irregularities.
If the screening committee did not consider the aspect of financial irregularity and if the petitioner was not heard, the order is considered to be a disguised dismissal which is punitive, stigmatic, and must be quashed.
The petitioner was entitled to be heard at least on misconduct involving financial irregularity.
[Para 9]

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Fundamental Rule 56(c) says that the appointing authority is not obliged to record reasons for decision but if the grounds stated in the order shows an erroneous legal approach, the decision will be quashed.
If there are allegations of ill will and caste bias and the officer who passed the order on the basis of the adverse entry in a confidential report himself had given such entry and was presiding the screening committee, it would be considered contrary to the principle of fairness and the decision would be illegal.
