According to Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, if an employee is absent from work and then reports for duty after a few days, the management's claim that he has sacrificed his service due to standing orders is not valid.
The principle of retrenchment still applies, and terminating the employee without paying retrenchment compensation would be illegal.
[Paras 4 to 8]
Even if an employee is absent from duty, the automatic termination concept is not applicable.
Termination without holding an inquiry would be illegitimate, and even in the case of absence from duty, an inquiry is required.
Standing orders that state removal would be automatic due to absence are not acceptable.
[Paras 6 to 8]
