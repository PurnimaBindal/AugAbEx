Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 229 - Ad hoc appointment - Regularisation of services - The petitioner, who was appointed as Assistant Medical Officer after 1101984, cannot receive the benefit of Dr. A K Jain's case as it only applies to those who were in service on 1101984 - The petitioner's case was sent to the Union Public Service Commission for regularisation, but they did not recommend his regularisation, leading to the termination of his services - Ad hoc appointees must meet the standards and be selected by the Public Service Commission - If one is found not fit, no grievance can be raised against their termination.
