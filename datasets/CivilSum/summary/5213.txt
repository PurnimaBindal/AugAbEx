Constitution of India, Article 226 - Corporation - Maintainability of writ - National Hydro Electric Power Corporation - Corporation engaged in producing electric energy - Since the task involves the public, the Corporation falls under the definition of "authority" as stated in Article 12 of the Constitution - Consequently, the Corporation is amenable to writ jurisdiction.
