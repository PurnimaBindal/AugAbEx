Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission - Reservation - Candidates admitted on merit, even if belonging to a reserved category, cannot be counted against the quota reserved for that category.
Such candidates must be treated as open competition candidates.
[Paras 11 and 17] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Education - Admission - Reservation - Quota for posts - Once the State prescribes a quota for reserved categories, only the remaining posts can be filled by general category candidates.
Candidates of reserved categories who are selected in open competition will not be counted against the reserved quota, but as open competition candidates.
[Para 23] C. Constitution of India, Article 226 - West Bengal Primary Education Act, 1973, Section 107 - Interpretation of Rules - Recruitment rules must be in harmony with Government orders regarding reserved category candidates.
Under Section 107, the Government may issue notifications or orders in place of making rules if necessary.
