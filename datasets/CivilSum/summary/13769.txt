The Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 226 govern the selection process for police services.
In this case, the petitioner applied for the role of Constable against current vacancies and underwent numerous recruitment tests.
However, he neglected to disclose in the Verification Roll that he had been arrested, detained, or convicted by a court for an offense, which constitutes a serious misconduct.
As a result, the Authority's decision not to consider the petitioner's application cannot be deemed unreasonable.
