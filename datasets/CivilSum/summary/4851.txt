Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination of services - If a workman invokes the remedy under the ID Act, they cannot withdraw from the proceedings and switch to the writ jurisdiction of the High Court without disclosing the same, or they may not be entitled to any relief under Article 226.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination of services - If a writ petition challenging earlier termination becomes infructuous due to termination of services in terms of the contract of employment by expiry of the term of employment during the pendency of the writ petition.
