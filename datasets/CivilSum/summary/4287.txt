Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 9.18(2) - Adverse reports - Compulsory retirement - Despite adverse remarks, a government employee may be promoted to a higher post and the adverse remarks will lose their significance - In considering compulsory retirement, the entire service record must be taken into account, with more weight given to the opinions of the reporting officer during later years - The High Court must only interfere on limited grounds if the order is found to be mala fide or arbitrary.
