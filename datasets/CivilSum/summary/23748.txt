According to Section 16-F of the Intermediate Education Act 1921, the appointment of Principal by direct recruitment in an upgraded school must be approved by the competent authority.
When a school was upgraded to a Higher Secondary School and the position of Headmistress was changed to Principal, the appellant was selected as Principal even though her qualifications fell below the necessary standard.
Despite her appointment not being approved, she was appointed anyway and her services were later terminated.
Although she argued that the termination was not valid since it lacked approval from the competent authority, this argument was not valid because her appointment was not approved in the first place.
Another plea made by the appellant regarding promotion from the serving Head teacher under Regulation 16 was also not sustainable since her claim was not based on the provisions of Regulation 16.
