Constitution of India, Article 129 - Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Sections 12, 2(a) and 2(b) - Contempt of Court - If the Court directs an authority to consider a matter in accordance with the law, they must best understand before taking action.
Therefore, a mere error in judgment regarding legal position does not constitute contempt of court.
Willful disobedience can only be claimed if the accused didn't make the best of their efforts to comply with the order.

Constitution of India, Article 129 - Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Sections 2(a) and 2(b) - Contempt of court - The meaning of civil contempt means "willful disobedience of any judgment, decree, direction, order, writ or other process of court of willful breach of undertaking given to a court.
If someone violates an order of the Supreme Court, the Court can investigate the allegation under the Act, punish the offenders for having committed "civil contempt," and pass consequential orders or execution of their order.
