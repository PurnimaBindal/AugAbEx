The Constitution of India's Article 16 does not allow relaxation of experience or age for candidates.
Over-aged or below-experienced candidates are disqualified from selection, and there is no provision for condonation.
Any relaxation would be fraudulent and arbitrary.
Therefore, all candidates who were not informed of any relaxation are ineligible and disqualified.
