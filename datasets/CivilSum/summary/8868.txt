Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - Minors - The limitation period for compassionate appointment of minors starts from the date they attain majority, not from the death of the deceased - The father of the petitioner died while they were 6/7 years old - The petitioner is entitled to apply for consideration within the prescribed time from the date of attaining majority - The claim of a minor cannot be rejected on the grounds of limitation - The Board is instructed to consider the claim of the petitioner's son without objection to limitation.
