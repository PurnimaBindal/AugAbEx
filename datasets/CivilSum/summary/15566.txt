According to the Constitution of India, Article 16 and the Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, Section 45, House Rent Allowance should be given to employees.
However, in this case, the corporation deducted economic rent and electric charges from the petitioners' salary despite refusing the offer of allotment.
This action is illegal and contrary to the terms and conditions of the allotment order.
Thus, the impugned order has been quashed and the corporation has been directed to pay the deducted amount of economic rent and electricity charge to each of the petitioners.
