The petitioner's husband, a Lancenayak in the Indian Armed Forces, sacrificed his life in Kargil in 1999.
An ex-gratia payment of Rs.10 lacs was promised by the government through Memo No13/8/92-4D-III dated 25th July 1999, but has not been paid yet.
A direction has been given to the Government of Haryana to release the payment within one month, and costs amounting to Rs. 10,000/- have also been awarded.
