Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection - Competitive test - Re-evaluation of answer books of competitive tests for selection - The Court cannot order the re-evaluation of answer books if it is not permissible under any Rule/provision of law, as it will result in delaying the selection process and appointments.
Such re-evaluation is not reasonably acceptable or permissible.
[Paras 7 & 8]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection - Competitive test - Re-evaluation of answer books of competitive tests for selection - Re-evaluation in selections will result in the adoption of different standards and parameters, hence it is not reasonably acceptable and permissible to get the same re-evaluated from some independent evaluator without supplying them model answers, especially where the examiner is required to evaluate answers in the light of model answers given to them.
