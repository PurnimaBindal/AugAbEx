Punjab National Bank (Employees) Pension Regulations, 1995, Clauses 14, 28, 29 - Banking companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertaking) Act, 1970 - Claim of pension - Petitioner voluntarily retired under Scheme - Qualifying service in terms of regulations is 10 years service in the Bank on the date of retirement - Pension denied based on circular which provides for 15 years of service - The decision in the circular is pending amendment in Regulation 28 for which the Bank has taken steps under the 1970 Act - Clause 35 of the Scheme extends the benefit of pension under pension regulations as per Regulation 14, 10 years is the period of qualifying service - Therefore, on conjoint reading of eligibility condition and benefits payable under the scheme read with Regulation 14, petitioner is entitled to pension - The decision to enhance qualifying service came after option and is in the nature of executive instructions and hence cannot override the scheme.
[Paras 14 and 15]
