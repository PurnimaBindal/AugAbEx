Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 21 - Public Interest Litigation (PIL) - A petition filed by the petitioner, who is a correspondent accredited to the Punjab Government - The petitioner alleges that highly placed government officials have misused their positions for personal gain - The petitioner requests that the CBI investigate these claims - The respondent argues that the petitioner's motives are suspect and no cause of action has been disclosed - The court cannot give directions for an investigation unless a prima facie case of an offense has been established - Directing an investigation without evidence of an offense would violate the principle of Life and Liberty guaranteed by Article 21 of the Constitution.
