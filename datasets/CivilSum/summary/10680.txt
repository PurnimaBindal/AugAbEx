Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 14 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 54(B) - Involvement in criminal case - Withdrawal of charge sheet - Treating of suspension period as non-duty for all purposes except pensionary benefits - Challenge as to - Rule 54(B) mandates that if competent authority deems suspension as wholly unjustified, petitioner will receive full pay and benefits - However, in this case the criminal case remained pending for 11 years, which was outside the control of the department - Authorities acted in accordance with Fundamental Rule 54-B and the suspension of the petitioner cannot be deemed wholly unjustified - Therefore, petitioner not entitled to wages for entire period.
