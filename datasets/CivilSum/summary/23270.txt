DSIDC (Staff Service Rules), 1978 Rule 3(iv) - Regular Employee - Termination of Service - The Corporation cannot terminate the services of an employee who has completed more than 5 years of continuous service by using the Temporary Service Rules.
[Para 16]
Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14, 16 - Re-employment - The Corporation's decision to not redeploy the appellant, who was similarly placed as other retrenched employees, was held to be unjust and arbitrary.
[Paras 11 to 16]
Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14 and 16 - Central Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965 Rule 5(1) - Staff Service Rules of DSIDC, 1978, Rule 7(ii) - Termination of Service - Discrimination - The termination of the appellant's services, when only his services were terminated out of a list of 275 retrenched employees, was arbitrary and illegal.
The Corporation's explanation of the appellant being junior cannot be accepted.
The appellant was reinstated.
