Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 30(2) - Pensionary benefits - Even though the Petitioner retired voluntarily, she was denied her pension benefits because her qualifying service was less than 20 years on the date of her retirement.
However, the fact that her request to retire early was accepted suggests that the department was satisfied that she had completed 20 years of qualifying service.
Therefore, it is now too late for the respondents to deny her pension.
They are hereby directed to release the Petitioner's retiral benefits and pay her interest at 12% for the delay.
[Paras 7 and 10]
