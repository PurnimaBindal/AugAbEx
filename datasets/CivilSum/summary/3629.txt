According to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2, Item 3, gross misconduct can be a reason for dismissal from service.
In a case where a bank employee committed fraudulent acts by crediting proceeds of clearing instruments in his own SB accounts, including "Account Payee" and "not negotiable" dividend warrants and refund orders, he was dismissed from service after being given a reasonable opportunity of hearing.
The destruction of bank records to suppress fraud also occurred, making any interference unnecessary.
