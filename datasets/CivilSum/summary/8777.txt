The case involving the Reference of service dispute of Sales Promotion Employee to the Labour Court is maintainable as ruled by the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) read with Section 24 of Amendment Act No. 46 of 1982 - Sales Promotion Employees (conduct of Service) Act, 1976, Section 6(2).
Contrary to the Labour Court's understanding, it was not found in the AIR 1994 SC 2608 case that Industrial Disputes Act is not applicable where Sales Promotion Employees (Conduct of Service) Act, 1976 is applicable.
Therefore, the case must be decided afresh on merits and remanded to the Labour Court as per Paras 9 and 10.
