Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection/Interview/Viva Voc Test - Interview plays a crucial role in assessing the abilities of a candidate and cannot be excluded from the selection process as a well-recognised test - The selection committee has the authority to determine the selection criteria and reserve marks for interviews - There are no hard and fast rules regarding the reservation of marks for interviews.
[Paras 7 and 8]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection Misrepresentation - The court cannot investigate allegations of misrepresentation made by candidates during the selection process - This is within the executive power of the state to investigate before making appointments.
[Paras 9 and 10]
