According to Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226, Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 9, and Specific Relief Act, 1963, Sections 34 and 37, a dismissed employee is not entitled to specific performance of a service contract.
Considering the relevant authorities and facts, it is enough for the respondent to receive three years of salary as damages.
In addition, reinstatement is not applicable in this case since the respondent has not been teaching for many years and was not in touch with it, based on Constitution of India, Articles 136 and 226.
