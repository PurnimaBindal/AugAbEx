Constitution of India, Article 226 - General Court Marital proceedings - Maintainability - The Petitioner was accused of obtaining admission for his daughter on the basis of a false disability certificate, however, it was found that there was no concealment or misinterpretation of any material fact and the certificate issued was genuine and valid.
The DRDO enquiry report was ignored and the Petitioner was fully exonerated.
[Paras 12, 13]

Disability certificate - Challenge as to - The Petitioner argued that there is a difference between injury and disability and that the rule does not state that disability has to be permanent.
The court held that if a person suffers an injury that leads to a disablement, the disability may be temporary or permanent.
The submission that the Petitioner was in Shape I at the time of issuance of the certificate is irrelevant.
[Para 14]
