Article 14 of the Constitution of India - Promotion/Seniority-cum-merit/consideration for promotion - The most senior and fully qualified person must be promoted when promotion is on the principle of 'seniority-cum-merit,' and cannot be followed by others even if eligible.
However, when a promotion is made by way of selection, cases of all eligible persons should be considered.
Deva Ram Bajaj v. State of Punjab 1992(1) RSJ 572 is distinguishable on facts and not directly in issue.
[Para 3]

Article 14 of the Constitution of India - Promotion/Consideration for promotion - No one can claim promotion as a right.
Hence, promotions cannot be deemed.
Even if a promotional post becomes vacant, the state has the prerogative to fill it or not.
Eligible persons can only claim consideration when the state decides to fill the post.
[Para 3]

Article 14 of the Constitution of India - Promotion/Limitation/Delay and laches - In the challenge to promotions made from 1987 to 1991 in 3/1992, the petition suffers from delay and laches.
