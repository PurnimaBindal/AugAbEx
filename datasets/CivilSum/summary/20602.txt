Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Sections 12 and 13 - Disobedience of direction of court - Court directions issued under a mistake of fact regarding M/s Birla Textiles (Prop Texmaco Ltd) functioning, which led to the management being required to pay back wages and shifting allowance to all workmen desiring to rejoin at Baddi.
The orders were later modified, and the management was directed to pay additional expenses and travelling charges to the workmen - Order dt. 18.12.1998 modified.
