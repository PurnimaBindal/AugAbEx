Punjab State Civil Supplies Corporation (Revised Service) Regulation, 1985, Regulations 10 and 11 - Post of Deputy District Manager - Probation period - Illegally extended - Reversion - The petitioner, a regular Senior Auditor, was directed to look after the work of Deputy District Manager in his pay scale after completing all the necessary qualifications and experience.
Although juniors were already promoted to the post, nothing was placed on record to show that the petitioner's services were unsatisfactory.
The reversion order was illegal and unsustainable as it was only passed due to a change of cadre position.
[Para 8]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Delay and laches - Pay scale - Arrears of pay - The petitioner performed the duties of Deputy District Manager from 1988 but only filed a petition claiming pay scale after more than 10 years in 1999.
This delay in seeking relief renders the claim highly belated.
[Paras 9 to 11]
