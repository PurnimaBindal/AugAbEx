Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - Petitioners who were admitted into the Veterinary Compounder and Stock Assistant courses in the years 1974-75 and 1978-79 respectively, obtained admission based on their educational qualification and interview conducted by the selection committee of All India Vidwat sammelan (AIVS).
After completing the course, they were appointed to the posts of Veterinary Compounder and Stock Assistant in the Department of Animal Husbandry.
However, after 9 and 5 years of employment respectively, the university served them a show cause notice that matriculation examination of AIVS had not been recognized by any Board/University, and decided to cancel the diploma certificates issued to them.
Despite the university scrutinizing their cases at the time of admission, the petitioners who had completed 23 and 19 years of service on account of an interim order in their favor, claimed that the university's decision to cancel their admission after a gap of many years was unjustified.
