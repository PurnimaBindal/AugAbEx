Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Dismissal - Reinstatement - Negligence - Back wages - Bus Driver dismissed on account of negligence - Criminal court acquitting workman - Motor Accident Claims Tribunal absolving workman and ascribing negligence to driver of other bus - Labour court reinstating workman without back wages - Back wages granted but with Rs.150/- per month subject to deduction (amount earned by workman driver) - Workman not at fault as held, therefore, entitled only to back wages subject to above deduction.
