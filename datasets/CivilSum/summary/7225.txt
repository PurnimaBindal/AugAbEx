Article 16 of the Constitution of India covers the tripartite settlement of deputation, stating that the approval of the parent department, the acceptance of the borrowing department, and the consent of the concerned employee are necessary for deputation.
Deputed employees cannot claim an enforceable right for absorption in the borrowing department, but if the borrowing department deems the employee's services necessary, they may absorb them with the consent of the concerned employee and parent department.
