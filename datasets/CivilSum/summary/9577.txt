Constitution of India, Articles 16, 21 and 39 - Rajasthan Recruitment of Dependents of Government Servants Dying - While in Service Rules, 1975, Rule 2(6) Proviso 5 - Compassionate appointment - Wife of the deceased who applied for compassionate appointment was refused due to physical handicap and inability to perform duties - She sought employment for her brother - The Rule's proviso allows for ineligible dependents to name another person to receive the benefit, and the Head of Department is obliged to take immediate steps to consider the same - Even when there is no vacancy in the Department where the deceased served, the Authority should still find a post according to the qualifications and eligibility of the applicant.
