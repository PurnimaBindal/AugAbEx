Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Award - The labour Court stated that workmen refused to report for duty intentionally and willfully - Although the workmen filed writ petitions, representation, demand notice etc., their peaceful conduct shows that they were trying to resolve their grievances and agitate for the implementation of the settlement - It is concluded that the award should be quashed and the matter remitted to the labour Court for fresh decision.
