According to Delhi Police (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1980, Rule 15(2), if a preliminary enquiry reveals the commission of a cognizable offense, a departmental enquiry must be initiated after obtaining prior approval from the relevant Additional Commissioner of Police.
In the case of a constable accused of overwriting in their daily diary, a departmental enquiry was conducted after a preliminary enquiry but without the necessary prior approval.
This constitutes a violation of the law, and the order of dismissal has been overturned.
However, in light of the facts and nature of the misconduct involved, the officer is not entitled to backwages until reinstatement under the principles of 'no work no pay.'
The Disciplinary Authority is also free to initiate a fresh proceeding from the stage of obtaining prior approval from the Additional Commissioner of Police, using the principles established in 1994(1) SCT 319 (SC).
