If the punishment of dismissal from service of the workman was deemed unwarranted by the Labour Court despite a fair and proper enquiry, Section 11-A may be invoked, as per Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(1) and 11-A [Para 9]
In cases where the workman has valid reasons for disobeying an employer's command, even if the enquiry was conducted properly, the Labour Court cannot deprive them of full back wages.
By invoking Section 11-A, the Labour Court has directed 40% of back wages to be paid and for the workman to be reinstated as per Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) and 11-A [Paras 15]
If natural justice principles are fully complied with, and no prejudice is caused to the workman despite being deprived of legal assistance during a departmental enquiry, the management's action cannot be deemed vitiated solely for that reason as per Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1).
