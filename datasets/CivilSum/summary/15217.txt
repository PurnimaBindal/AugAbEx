The UP Recruitment of Dependents of Govt.
Servants Dying in Harness Rules (1974), Rules 2(c)(iii), 5 state that an illegitimate daughter of a deceased employee is eligible for compassionate appointment under Article 16 of the Indian Constitution.

As per the UP Recruitment of Dependents of Govt.
Servants Dying in Harness Rules (1974), Rules 2(c)(iii), 5 and Article 309, the daughter of a second wife who was married to the deceased Government Servant while the first wife was still alive is entitled to be considered for compassionate appointment.
