Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Withholding of - To withhold a promotion, there must be an existing promotion order - Cancelling and withholding are two distinct actions - Withholding is merely a deferment, while cancelling would strip an individual of a vested right - Thus, cancellation cannot occur without adhering to principles of natural justice - If a benefit is simply withheld, it will be automatically restored once the term of withholding is over.
