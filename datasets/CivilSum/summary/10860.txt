Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order XXI Rule 10, Section 151 - Execution application - The court clarified that arrears of pay must be limited to 38 months prior to filing of writ petition, as per consistent views of the Apex Court and this court.
Respondents argued that state is only liable to pay arrears of pay for 38 months prior to filing of writ petition.
Application was dismissed as satisfied.
