The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo), (bb) and 25-F discuss termination and retrenchment of workmen.
A clerk who is a daily rated employee is not subject to the provisions of the UP ID Act.
In a case where the respondent's contract was not renewed by the employer due to unavailability of work, and the petitioner's daily payment contract came to an end as per the agreed terms, it does not amount to retrenchment.
Therefore, the provision of Section 25(F) of the Act is not applicable.
