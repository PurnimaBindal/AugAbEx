Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 12 - Petitioner's services were put on status quo by High Court on 16.5.1990 - Petitioner's services terminated on 9990 and reinstated on 2811991 - Respondent justifies action of termination citing lack of work, however, action deemed as violation of status quo orders and found guilty of contempt of court - Respondent apologizes with justification, but past legal notice and lack of bona fide apology result in punishment of imprisonment and Rs. 1000/- fine.
