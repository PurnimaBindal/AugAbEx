As per Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, during probation, an employer can terminate an employee's appointment due to unsatisfactory work without an inquiry.
However, in this case, the Labour Court held that a domestic inquiry was necessary before terminating the employee's services.
The High Court dismissed the writ petition and ruled that evidence must be presented to show unsatisfactory work.
The Labour Court's award revealed evidence of unsatisfactory work and found that the employer was not mala fide to the employee.
The High Court erred in interfering with the employer's satisfaction and in holding that evidence was necessary to show that the employee did not meet expected performance standards.
The termination order was validly passed.
