Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pay fixation and seniority - Petitioners claimed a right to pay fixation and seniority - After being terminated, petitioners were reappointed with notional consequential benefits upon verification of degrees - The High Court ordered for their fresh appointments - The term "re-appointment" was read in a broad sense to mean that the petitioners would be entitled to seniority and pay fixation from the date of their initial appointment, but payment would only be given from the date of their reinstatement.
