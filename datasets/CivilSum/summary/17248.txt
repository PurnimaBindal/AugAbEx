Central Civil Service (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 9 - Major Port Trust Act, 1963, Section 24(1)(a) - Visakhapatnam Port Trust Employees (Classification, Control and Appeal) Regulations, 1968, Regulation 11(3) - Disciplinary proceedings - Validity of disciplinary action against a retired Port Trust officer - The petitioner was allowed to retire while disciplinary action was still pending against him - As the petitioner was not the Head of the Department, consultation with the Central Government was not necessary for imposing penalties under Section 24 - Therefore, the disciplinary proceedings against the petitioner were not illegal.
