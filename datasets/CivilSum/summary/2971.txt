Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rajasthan Subordinate Services Ministerial Staff Rules, 1957, Rules 7 and 27 - Deputation - Lien - Repatriation - Legality of - Petitioner transferred departments with consent of both departments - Petitioner repatriated to parent department after 11 years of satisfactory service - Petitioner argues that Respondents cannot repatriate him and his lien is not continuing in Agricultural department - Court rules that deputation does not create appointment right for Petitioner in Transport department and 11 years of service does not entitle Petitioner to have lien transferred - Petition dismissed.
