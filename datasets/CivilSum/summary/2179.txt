According to Section 2-A of the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, the Bombay Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Amendment Rules, 1959, Clause 4-C grants permanent status to workers who have completed 240 days of work.
However, Clause 4-C cannot be incorporated into previously certified orders.
The Model Standing Orders would apply without amendment to certified standing orders if they were established after January 15, 1959.
