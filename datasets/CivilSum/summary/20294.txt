Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservation of Vacancies in the Post or Service (for SC and ST) Act, 1975, Section 7 - Reservation of Vacancies for Scheduled Castes/Tribes - Notification of Reserved Vacancies for Recruitment of Post of Assistant Law Officer - Each recruitment year must attempt to notify reserved vacancies for recruitment, and in cases where suitable candidates are not found, the question of carrying forward shall arise.
