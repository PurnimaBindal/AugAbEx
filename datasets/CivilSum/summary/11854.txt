Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Medical reimbursement - Prior approval/sanction - Petitioner underwent open heart by-pass surgery but did not seek prior permission from Director Health Services, resulting in denial of his claim for medical reimbursement - Denial solely based on lack of prior approval is deemed harsh and inhumane since individuals facing death should not have to wait for procedural formalities - Courts order authorities to apply compassionate consideration and guidelines when dealing with medical claims, rather than denying them solely on the grounds of lack of prior approval and sanction.
