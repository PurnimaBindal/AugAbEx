Punjab Police Rules, Rule 12.21 - Discharge - Absence from duty - The order of discharge for the respondent-plaintiff was not stigmatic or punitive in nature as a reasonable opportunity to explain his absence was given and the power under Rule 1221 of Punjab Police Rules was rightfully used.
[Paras 10 and 14]
Punjab Police Rules, Rule 12.21 - Discharge - The order of discharge can be considered stigmatic in nature if it is based on past conduct of the employee being integral to the order.
However, if the order of discharge only has a motive, it is difficult to determine whether it is stigmatic or punitive.
[Para 8]
Punjab Police Rules, Rule 12.21 - Discharge - Absence from duty - The mere mentioning of absence from duty does not automatically classify an order of discharge as stigmatic.
