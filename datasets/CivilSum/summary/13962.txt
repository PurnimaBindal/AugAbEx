Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Regularisation and regular pay scale - Petitioners who work as clerks, drivers, electricians and peons in Nagar Panchayat and perform the same duties as regular employees on corresponding jobs are entitled to a minimum of pay scale and dearness allowance.
State Government is directed to implement its policy of regularization and consider cases of all Petitioners.
[Paras 8,9]
Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Regularisation and regular pay scale - Deputy Commissioner cannot suspend the resolution according to which Petitioners were appointed on daily wages.
