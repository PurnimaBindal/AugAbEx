Section 14 of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 states that its provisions shall override any legislation or contract that contradicts it.
In Rajasthan Municipalities (Contributory Provident Fund and Gratuity) Rules, 1969, Rule 1, it is clarified that employees of municipality are governed by the Act of 1972, which has an overriding effect on State Rules of Gratuity.
As the Act of 1972 is a central legislation, its provisions have superiority, as explicitly stated in its preamble.
