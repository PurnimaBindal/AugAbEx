Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Civil Services, (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, Rules 8 and 9 - Termination - Charge-sheet - Show cause notice- Opportunity of cross-examining the witnesses - Since the employee did not respond to the show cause notice and repeatedly shifted residences without updating his address, the procedure laid down under the Rules can only be followed if the person concerned is available to avail the opportunity envisaged under the rules and to cross-examine the witnesses.
Therefore, the management/employer cannot be held responsible for not serving the notice of proceedings upon the absent employee at his new address.
