According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution, ad hoc appointments and promotions are acceptable only for a limited period, as prolonged ad hocism violates these articles.
Provisional appointments may be used as an administrative solution when rules cannot be followed, but cannot be extended indefinitely at the expense of the rights of eligible rule-governed employees.
Promoting employees on an ad hoc basis without following the proper procedures violates constitutional protections, but the competent authority is required to form the departmental promotion committee and phase out ad hoc arrangements as soon as feasible.
