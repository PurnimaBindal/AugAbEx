The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Tribunal's Jurisdiction - Approval application - In an approval application under Section 33(2)(b), the Tribunal has limited jurisdiction to evaluate the validity of the domestic enquiry.
A detailed review of facts and circumstances is not justified.

The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(rr) - Definition of Wages - The term wages applies to all monetary remuneration that a worker would receive for their employment or work completed within the scope of their employment, subject to the terms of employment, express or implied.
The earning of increments during a suspension period is not automatic or an entitlement.
