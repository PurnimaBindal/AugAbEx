Section 7-A of the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, establishes the unity of management between the petitioner and respondent No. 4 institute.
In addition, because of the geographical proximity and financial integrity of both establishments, as well as the inter-linking and inter-dependence of the two units, the Commissioner's order treating the petitioner and respondent 4 as one establishment for the purposes of the PF Act is not inappropriate.
