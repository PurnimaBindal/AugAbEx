Claim for payment of ex-gratia amount in lieu of compassionate appointment has been rejected due to family income exceeding 60% of last drawn salary of deceased employee, as per Punjab Civil Services Rules (as applicable to Haryana) Vol. 1, Rule 4.4.
The petitioner widow is receiving Rs. 392437 as family pension.
The claim has been rejected as per the ruling in 1994(3) SCT 174 (SC).
[Paras 3, 4 and 5]
Compassionate appointment is not a right and cannot be granted to replace deceased employee with one of the dependants.
The aim and object of granting appointment on compassionate grounds is to help the family who may have been reduced to abject poverty by sudden demise of sole bread winner of the family, as per Constitution of India, Article 16.
[Para 6]
