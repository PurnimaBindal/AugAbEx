The Commission rejected applications for the post of Veterinary Assistant Surgeon (Direct Recruitment) due to non-signing of the OMR application, declaration column, and photo identity.
This rendered the application particulars unauthenticated and invalid, justifying the rejection.
[Paras 10, 12 & 13]  B The Commission's rejection of applications for the post of Veterinary Assistant Surgeon (Direct Recruitment) on the ground of non-production of a Veterinary Council Registration Certificate was not proper.
The notification/advertisement did not require the production of such a certificate, and candidates had participated in the examination as per a court order.
The Commission is directed to immediately publish results and complete the selection process.
