Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Premature retirement - Pension and retiral benefits - An employee is allowed premature retirement once during pendency of departmental proceedings, and if the proceedings are dropped, then he must receive pension and other retiral benefit available to a pre-mature retiree.
Also, he cannot be deemed to be dismissed from service.
Furthermore, a retired old man who was harassed by the State authorities arbitrarily has been awarded costs of Rs. 10,000/- .
