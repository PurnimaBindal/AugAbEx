Constitution of India, Article 14 and 16 - Special increment/Suspension period/Incentive - It is not possible for a suspended employee to earn an incentive increment during the suspension period and this also applies to those who did not participate in a strike - As a suspended employee cannot work or participate in a strike, they cannot receive the benefit of an incentive increment.
B Constitution of India, Article 14 and 16 - Incentive increment - Non-grant of increment/Penalty - Not allowing an incentive increment is not necessarily a penalty or arbitrary action unless it can be proven that it is discriminatory.
