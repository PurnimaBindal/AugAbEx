The Labour Court lacks jurisdiction to decide whether the petitioner is entitled to a higher salary, like those of Punjab Government or PUNTEX holding company employees, under Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
[Para 3] B.
The company is considered a separate, distinct entity by law and had their pay scales revised.
Thus, the respondent cannot be governed based on the pay scales of the Holding Company under Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
