The Constitution of India, Article 16 - Advertisement - Appointment - The petitioner claims to be entitled for appointment as Home Science Mistress having been selected and recommended by HSSS Board - The Board must not make recommendations beyond the number of vacancies advertised - A waiting list must be prepared and candidates can be offered appointment in case a selected candidate does not join - Recommendations beyond advertised posts are not lawful - Mere selection and recommendations by Board does not grant any right of appointment to petitioner.
[Paras 1, 4 and 5]

The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ petition - Delay - The selection was made in 1989 and this writ petition was filed in 1992 - Hence, it cannot be entertained.
[Para 6]
