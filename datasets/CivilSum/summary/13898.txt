Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1997, Sections 4 and 16 - Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Services) Rules, 1981, Rule 9(3) - Compassionate appointment - The petitioner, who is the Chairman of a Society running a secondary school, had appointed his younger son as a peon after an employee who served as a clerk had died.
When the respondent refused the offered position, petitioner No. 2 was appointed to the vacant clerk post in 1997 and petitioner No. 3 to the position of a peon in 2001.
The respondent filed a complaint in 2004, and the Zilla parishad ordered the appointment of the respondent as clerk in place of petitioner No.2.
The order was deemed improper as there was no basis for the complaint.
The cancellation order for the appointment of petitioners and the order appointing the respondent were declared improper.
