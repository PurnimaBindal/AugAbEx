Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(s), 2(oo) and 2-A) - Retrenchment - The appellant was working on the Tung Machine in `C' grade job against a clear vacancy and ceased to be an apprentice - The Labour Court found him to be a workman and awarded him accordingly - The High Court erred in setting aside the Labour Court's award.
