Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 115 - Appointment - Post of Social Studies Mistress - The Petitioner received a temporary ad hoc appointment based on a court decision.
She filed an application before the executing court to not terminate her service until her execution application was resolved.
The executing court relied on a Supreme Court judgment in AIR 1998 SC 18 and dismissed all three applications.
It was held that the judgment of the Hon'ble D.B confirmed by the Supreme Court could only have a prospective, not retrospective effect.
The Impugned order was overturned.
