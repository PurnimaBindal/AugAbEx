Constitution of India, Article 311 - Madhya Pradesh Public Works Department Manual Vol.
I, Para 107 - Termination - Natural justice - Civil post - Work charged employee - Consolidated pay - Services of work charged employee must be terminated according to principles of natural justice.
If the right of "hire and fire" is applied to terminate the services of work charged and contingency paid employees, and the termination is for misconduct, it would be invalid and void.
Article 311 is applicable.
Even a daily wage employee is considered to hold a civil post under Article 311(1) of the Constitution.
