Indian Police (Pay) Rules, 1954, Rule 8 - Government of India, Guidelines No. 16011 dated 491983 is only applicable to appointments and promotion in the State Cadres and not to those on deputation under the Central Govt. [Para 8] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 309 and 312(1) - All-India Services - Criteria for appointment and promotions by selection - No one can claim prior right to appointment on the basis of their position on the panel - Length of experience and seniority is not the sole criteria for appointment to top level posts, and no one can claim appointment as a matter of right on that basis - The procedure and criteria can be evolved by the Central government if it does not breach any statutory rule or procedure.
[Paras 9, 11 to 13] D. Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 2 - Jurisdiction - Administrative Tribunal does not have the jurisdiction to consider the issue of non-appointment/non-consideration of the petitioner for the post of Director General, RPF - Writ petition is maintainable under Art. 226 of the Constitution.
[Para 15] E. Constitution of India, Article 226(2) - Territorial jurisdiction - Cause of action - The Calcutta court does not have the jurisdiction since the consideration and appointment took place in Delhi, and the petitioner's cause of action is not direct but only a psychological or mental feeling.
