Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 13.16 - Promotion - The Departmental Promotion Committee must evaluate an official's entire service record to assess their suitability for promotion.
If the Committee finds an official unsuitable, their decision cannot be challenged in court and the court cannot reassess the suitability of candidates, particularly when promotions in the police force are based on seniority, efficiency, and honesty.
[Paras 3 and 5] B.
Adverse remarks - If an annual confidential report (ACR) is positive regarding honesty and moral character but includes a warning to be careful in the future, it is not considered adverse, and no need to convey it to the official in question, but the official can still be ignored for promotion.
[Para 5] C Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 13.17 - A.C.Rs/Promotion - There are three types of ACRs: A, B, and C. A recommends promotion regardless of seniority, B recommends promotion based on seniority, and C recommends the official be passed over for promotion.
