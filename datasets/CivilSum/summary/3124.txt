Selection of B.Ed Lecturers is governed by a complex set of regulations and policies, including the Andhra Pradesh College Service Commission (Manner of Selection of Persons for the Appointment to the Posts of Teachers) Regulations, as well as several sections of the University Grants Commission Act.
The commission invited applications and prescribed qualifications via advertisements, with weightage given for academic record.
However, only candidates who have passed the 'NET' or 'SLET' are eligible for 10 out of the 50 earmarked for academic record.
Candidates who possess PhD are exempted from these eligibility tests, but those who acquire PhD or MPhil after January 1, 1994 must pass these tests to be eligible for the posts of B.Ed lecturers.
These tests have been prescribed by the U.G.C.
and commission, both expert bodies in the field of education.
The Tribunal is not justified in interfering with the selections, and the petition is therefore allowed.
