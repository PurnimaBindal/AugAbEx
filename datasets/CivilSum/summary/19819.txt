Haryana Municipal Act, 1973, Sections 38, 39, 41 and 257 - Haryana Municipal Services (Integration Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1982, Rules 2, 3 and 14 and Appendix 'A' - Seniority/Lien - Members of the 'District Level Service' must maintain their seniority on district level only - Lien can only be terminated on permanent absorption into another post - Petitioner transferred from District Rohtak to District Kurukshtra in public interest - His lien couldn't be terminated from Rohtak and he remains eligible for promotion there - Order quashing termination of lien.
