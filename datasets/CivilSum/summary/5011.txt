Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Pension - Revision of - Cut-off date - The criteria for calculating revised pension of pre-1996 pensioners is different from that of Govt. employees who retired on or after 111996 based on the cut-off date.
The imposition of the cut-off date for the revised pension scheme by denying it to the pensioners who retired before the cut-off date is not valid.
