"As per the arguments made by respondent RC Setia, Addl.
AG Har., the discharge of a police constable on the grounds of being unlikely to prove their efficiency within probation is a stigmatic order and therefore is to be set aside.
The court directed the State Government to conduct a fair enquiry ensuring natural justice and hearing, and make a decision based on the outcome.
This falls within the purview of the Constitution of India, Article 226/227. "
