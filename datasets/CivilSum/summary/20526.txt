Andhra Pradesh State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1965, Rule 10(1)(a)(i) - Seniority - Inter se seniority - The State Government banned direct recruitment of Junior Engineers by GOMS No. 682 dated 1881970.
As a result, temporary and ad hoc appointments of Juniors Engineers were made on an emergency basis, without selection by the State Public Service Commission.
The Seniority in the post of Assistant Executive Engineer, previously known as Junior Engineer, in the Public Health and Municipal Engineering Department, and the inter-se seniority between direct recruits and temporary ad hoc appointees were reviewed.
The Tribunal directed the State Government to ensure that the seniority list was strictly in accordance with the directives given in 1993 Supp (3) S.C.C.
425 and to submit the revised seniority list to the Tribunal.
