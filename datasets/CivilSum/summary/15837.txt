Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Ad hoc appointment - Replacement of ad hoc appointees - Petition filed for direction to regularize services of Part-time Lecturers appointed in Government College on payment for per period and maximum to Rs. 2,000/- - Single judge issued directions not to replace them till regular persons are appointed and pay them at the minimum of Rs. 2,000/- p.m. - No claim that petitioners were appointed without considering competing claims nor their performance questioned - Recruitment on Part Time basis not governed by any Rules - Article 16 applies - State cannot contend that part-time appointment being contrary to rules, the court could not direct not to terminate the same - Single judge's direction being just and fair, deserves no interference.
