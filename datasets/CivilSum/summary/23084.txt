Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14 and 16 - Service Law - Promotion - In cases where an employee falls under a category excluded from promotion, denial of promotion opportunity for an extended period (such as 30 years) is not justified and can be remedied by a Superior Court order.
[Para 12]  B Constitution of India, Article 16 - Service law - Promotional avenues - Selection grade and Accelerated Career Progression are two distinct and separate schemes; the grant of selection grade does not automatically mean a promotion under the promotional scheme.
[Para 23]  C Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Service law - Promotion - While an employee of a State does not have a fundamental right to promotion, they do have a right to be considered for it.
Ensuring opportunities for advancement is necessary as promotion is a reasonable expectation in service.
