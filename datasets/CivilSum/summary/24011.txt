Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Appointment - Necessary parties - A candidate on a panel has no guarantee of appointment in an existing position, as selection only indicates provisional selection - The State can choose not to fill any vacancies - Still, if the selection is challenged, they remain essential parties in protection of the list.
[Paras 9, 12 and 18]  B.
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Advertisement of vacancies - Increase of vacancies without notifying - Vacancy notifications lessen number of qualified candidates that could apply - If vacancies have already been widely advertised and selected candidates have already been chosen, additional vacancies without re-advertising do not infringe on Article 14.
