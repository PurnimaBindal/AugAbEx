As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, the petitioner was not appointed to the post of Tehsil Welfare Officer in the Department of Social Welfare due to the unavailability of a post in either the Scheduled Castes or general category.
The court ordered that when a vacancy in the general category becomes available, those selected should be considered in order of merit, and if the petitioner's turn arises, he should be appointed.
This decision is supported by C.W.P.
No. 9006 of 1990, which was decided on 151190 (DB).
