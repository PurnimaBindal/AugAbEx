The Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 1317 and 131 - Adverse Reports - Doubtful integrity - Promotion - In the event of doubtful integrity being reported, it will have serious consequences for the concerned person's career.
Remarks made about doubtful integrity should be based on definite material and should follow the instructions of October 4th, 1956.
Vague remarks should be avoided and no external considerations should influence such remarks.
If these remarks are challenged, the reporting officer must be able to explain their basis to the court.
[Paras 6 and 10]

The Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 131, 1314, 1317, 1218 - Confirmation - Adverse remarks - Promotion - If an officer holding the post on probation after promotion is confirmed, any adverse remarks against them from the probationary period cannot be taken into consideration at any later stage.
One petitioner's case was kept out of consideration due to such remarks, but they were directed to be reconsidered from the date his juniors were considered for promotion.
[Paras 7, 8 and 10]
