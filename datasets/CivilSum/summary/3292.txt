Constitution of India, Article 14 - Admission in post graduate course in Medicine Petitioner sought direction from the Court to grant admission after being denied admission Other candidates with higher merits not admitted - Petitioner has no right over candidates higher in merit - Admission rejected.

Admission to Post Graduate Course in Medicine - Cut off date - Admission cannot go on indefinitely even during the middle or end of the academic session - Practical training and lectures must be completed before qualifying for the course Admission cannot be granted towards the end of the course University can determine a cut off date Courts should not interfere with it - Determining a cut off date is reasonable.
