Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Haryana Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Section 7 - Termination - Enquiry - The petitioner was not given any explanation or charges, nor was there an enquiry held - Additionally, the order of termination was never approved by the Director as required by Section 7(2) of the Act - As a result, the order of termination has been nullified and the petitioner has been ordered to be reinstated to their position with full back wages.
