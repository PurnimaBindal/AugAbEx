Under the Punjab Service of Engineers (Irrigation Branch) Class II, Rules 1941, Rules 3, 4, 5, and 19 - P.S.E.B., Petitioner is entitled to time-bound promotional/revised promotional scales after completing 16 years of service.
The Petitioner worked as a draftsman for the Irrigation department before being promoted to the post of SDO on an officiating basis in 1971 and on a regular basis in 1982.
Even though Petitioner's representation was initially rejected since a higher pay scale was admissible only to those who were directly recruited or promoted within AMIE quota, the Regulation 7-A(ii) deems AE appointed by promotion equivalent to AE appointed directly.
Since Regulation 102 requires the possession of a diploma and not just the qualification of AMIE, the Petitioner is eligible for the benefit since they completed 16 years of service in 1987.
As a result, the appeal was dismissed.
