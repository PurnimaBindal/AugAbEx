Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 25-F - Burden of proof - The onus is on the workman to prove that he was employed by the management, the management was an industry, he was a workman, he had completed 240 days within the preceding twelve months before termination, and the management did not comply with the provisions of Section 25-F - It is not appropriate to place the burden of proof on the management for these issues - The workman should first lead evidence to prove all such issues - The management is only required to refute these claims later - An order by the Labour Court directing the management to lead evidence first and placing the burden of proof on the management cannot stand.
