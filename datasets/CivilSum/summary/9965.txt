Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25F(b) - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965 - Rule 10 - Punishment - Absence from duty - Over-stayed leave - Enquiry - Dismissal from service - Termination of services of petitioner who had applied for extension of leave without holding any enquiry and affording opportunity to the delinquent is violative of principles of natural justice - No punitive order can be passed without offering the affected person a fair hearing - The award of the Labour Court and the termination order are both set aside, and the petitioner is entitled to be reinstated with all consequential benefits.
[Paras 9 and 10]
