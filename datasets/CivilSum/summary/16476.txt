Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Army Instruction No. 75 of 1978 (as amended on 552006) - Short Service Commission - Extension in service beyond contractual term of Short Service Commission - Extension is not allowed - Discrimination cannot be justified by repeating past wrongs - The rule prohibits extension beyond 10 years - Those with contracts lasting longer than 10 years cannot continue after their contract expires.
