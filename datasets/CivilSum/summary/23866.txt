The Orissa Municipal Act, Section 81 and the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 explain that those in a common cadre with interchangeable duties cannot claim to be a different cadre.
Those working in the Octroi wing and General section in this Municipality form one common cadre under Section 81.
Therefore, since no cadres are being clubbed, none can claim a different status.
