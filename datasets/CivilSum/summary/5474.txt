Constitution of India, Article 226 - Selection - Panel - The court does not interfere with the selection process of the Selection Committee.
However, the Director of Public Instructions can approve or disapprove the process based on wrong observations that the Selection Committee has made a unanimous decision contrary to the record itself.
The impugned order is set aside due to the substantiated illegality or material irregularity.
The concerned authority is directed to reconsider the matter, taking into account the objections of the majority of members of the Selection Committee, and giving a full opportunity of hearing to all the concerned parties, including those giving objection or no objection, while recording their statements in unequivocal terms.
The order should be reasoned and an ultimate conclusion reached, including subsequent positive steps, within four weeks.
[Paras 13 and 14]
