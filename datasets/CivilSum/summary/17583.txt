Constitution of India, Articles 19(g) and 14 - Natural justice - Travelling agency - The petitioner operated a Travelling Agency and had a valid license to deal with the Passport Office - The Agency was canceled based on certain allegations without any chance for the petitioner to defend themselves - It is a well-established principle that even in administrative matters, the principles of natural justice should be applied - If the order is based on allegations, it is necessary to grant an opportunity of hearing - Orders that violate the principles of natural justice are subject to being overturned.
