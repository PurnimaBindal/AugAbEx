Although the Civil Procedure Code's provisions apply to the Industrial Disputes Act's proceedings, the Industrial Tribunal's authority is limited in that regard.
If an award is published, it becomes final after one month in accordance with Section 17-A.
The tribunal becomes functus-officio of the matter and cannot consider an application under Order 9 Rule 13 CPC due to a delay in filing for setting aside an ex parte award.
