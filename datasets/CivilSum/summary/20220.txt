According to section 2(J) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the Postal Department is not considered an industry due to the State's duty to provide telecommunication service as part of its sovereign functions.
This makes the post office exempt from being classified as an industry.
[Para 6]
Extra departmental agents of the Postal Department are not considered workmen due to being civil servants and therefore exempt from the definition of workmen under section 2(B) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
