The Labour Court determined that the termination of employment was illegal under Sections 25-F and 25-B of the Industrial Disputes Act.
Workmen seeking relief under Section 25-F must have been in continuous employment with the same employer for at least one year, which includes the days they were employed and for which they were paid wages.
The finding that the workman had completed more than 240 days in the last 12 calendar months was legally sound and free from error.
