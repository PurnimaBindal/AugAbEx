Respondents represented by Anusuya Salwan, Advocate argue that Class IV employees appointed against Class III posts by an incompetent official are not entitled to equal pay for equal work or the higher pay scale of the Class III post.
The competent authority is not bound by the unauthorised act of an incompetent person and any claim for equal pay must be made against the person who made the employees work on higher posts.
The employees are not entitled to regularisation or the pay scale of the higher post they worked on.
[Paras 10, 14, 15 & 16]
