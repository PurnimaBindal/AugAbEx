Section 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 deals with discharge from service.
In a particular case, a workman was accused of instigating co-workers to go on strike, and was issued a charge-sheet on 6-7-1989, then discharged on 9-7-1989 without holding a domestic enquiry.
The Labour Court emphasized that although the penalty of discharge was warranted, it was not listed in the Standing Orders.
Thus, the court awarded Rs 120 lakh as compensation since the employer had failed to hold a permissible enquiry and did not order reinstatement.
However, the High Court enhanced the compensation to Rs. 175 lakh.
