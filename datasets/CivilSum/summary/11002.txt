Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 1, 2, 4, 14A and 7A - Applicability of Act to industry manufacturing Shotguns - Act applicable to Guns made by mechanical process - Specific provision under section 1(5) ensures the Act's operation regardless of the number of employees - Petitions filed to challenge employment of 28 persons in 1996 are dismissed as the Act remained applicable even when employee count falls below 20, and the company complied with its provisions.
