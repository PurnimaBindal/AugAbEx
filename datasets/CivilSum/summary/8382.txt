In accordance with Section 10(4-A) of the Industrial Disputes Act, an industrial dispute was raised challenging the dismissal of a KSRTC driver found guilty of negligent driving resulting in the death of a cyclist.
The Labour Court, who found no evidence to support the charges, set aside the order of dismissal and reinstated the worker with 75% backwages.
However, it is held that this decision was not reached through an adjudication of the dispute.
As such, both parties are responsible for providing evidence, particularly the worker to prove he was not negligent when driving.
Any failure on the part of the worker would render him liable for reimbursement of compensation by the employer to the victim of the accident.
Therefore only the balance should be awarded to the worker as backwages, with the rest being used to offset any compensation paid by the employer to the victim.
