Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment on the basis of Select Lists to the post of Hindi Stenographers - Select List operated till 429th candidate out of 578 candidates declared successful - Some candidates did not join, and more time was given to them - Persons below 429 in merit were not offered appointments - Some appellants did not raise grievances during the life of list, and some were found below in merit than the last person to whom appointment was offered - Mere inclusion of name in select list does not give the right of appointment to persons below in the list - The claim cannot be entertained at a belated stage being raised for the first time - Therefore, no relief can be granted to the appellant - Appeal dismissed.
[Paras 10, 12, 14 and 16]
