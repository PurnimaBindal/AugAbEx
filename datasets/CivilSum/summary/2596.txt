According to the Punjab Police (Haryana Amendment) Rules, 1998, Rule 12.16(16) - Recruitment - Viva-voce - Post of constable, 15 out of 35 marks are allocated for interview.
Article 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India allow the State to establish appropriate selection criteria for recruitment.
The allocation of 15 marks for personality assessment is reasonable and not arbitrary, and therefore the rule is not unconstitutional.
