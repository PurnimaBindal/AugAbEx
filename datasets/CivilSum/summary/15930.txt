Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Education - Study leave with full pay for studying in a Post Graduate medical course - Eligible candidates must complete three years of regular and rural service - Petitioner granted study leave before meeting eligibility conditions on the condition of forgoing salary during study period - However, instructions changed and other candidates with similar or even lesser qualifications granted full salary - Petitioner entitled to full salary during study period like others - Denial of benefit unjust - Ordered to pay full salary to the petitioner.
