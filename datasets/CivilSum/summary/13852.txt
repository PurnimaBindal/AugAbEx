Rajasthan Municipal (Subordinate & Ministerial Service) Rules, 1963, Rule 23 - Promotion - Cancellation of - Post of Electrical Inspector - Wiremen are the feeding channel for promotion to the post of Electrical Inspector, while the petitioner, LDC, does not fall under the said channel - Promoting the petitioner to the post of Electrical Inspector is against the rules and cannot be regularised - The order cancelling the petitioner's promotion should not be interfered with.
[Paras 4, 5 and 7]
