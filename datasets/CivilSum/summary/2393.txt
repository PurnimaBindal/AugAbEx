Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compulsory Retirement - The petitioner's earlier order of compulsory retirement was quashed for being ultra vires, null, and without jurisdiction.
The respondent then passed a fresh order of compulsory retirement of petitioner with a retrospective date.
The second order must be passed without influence or consideration of previous actions - The second order compulsorily retiring petitioner is unsustainable.
