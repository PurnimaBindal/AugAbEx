Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25FF - Plea of transfer of undertaking maintainability - No evidence of transfer of ownership and management - Therefore, Section 25FF of the Act cannot be attracted.
[Paras 6, 9] B.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25FF - Whether two units of an industrial establishment under the same management constitute separate entities - Held, both units cannot be regarded as separate for purposes of claiming benefit of Section 25FF of Act - Factors considered include common muster roll and seniority list of employees, joint maintenance of accounts, and uniform wages paid.
