Constitution of India, Article 14 and 16 - Eligibility requirements for appointments - Advertisement of qualifications - Once eligibility qualifications are advertised, they cannot be relaxed without notice to the general public.
Appointments made by relaxing qualifications without notice to the general public are illegal and liable to be quashed.
The 1992(1) SCT 700 (P&H) (DB) and 1990(4) SLR (SC) 237 decisions were followed.
