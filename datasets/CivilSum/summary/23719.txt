Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1948, Sections 2(s) and 10 - Workman - Industrial disputes - Service Rules - The High Court's jurisdiction in judicial review is limited and cannot return its own findings by re-assessing evidence nor can it delve into the sufficiency or insufficiency of evidence as found by the tribunal.
However, if the findings are based on no evidence and there is no other evidence supporting the decision, the Court may interfere.
The respondent worked temporarily as a dental surgeon, but there was no evidence that he was a workman.
The post was solely governed by the Rules and advertised accordingly.
The petitioner applied, but was not selected.
The tribunal and the High Court erred in determining that the respondent was a workman entitled to the benefits of employment with the Corporation.
