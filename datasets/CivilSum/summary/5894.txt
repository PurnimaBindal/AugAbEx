[Paras 8 and 19] B.
The Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923 and its corresponding rules comprise a complete system of legal procedure.
The provisions of the Civil Procedure Code do not apply except those mentioned in Rule 41.
Decisions made by a court without proper territorial jurisdiction are not binding.
[Paras 9 to 12 and 14] C. A corporation classified as an authority under Article 12 of the Constitution of India should not be able to use technical arguments, such as limitation, to escape responsibility to their workers.
The court should be lenient in condoning delays in order to ensure fairness.
