Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Punjab Privately-Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981 - Appointment - Grant-in-aid - Qualification of Art and Craft Teacher - Petitioner worked as Art and Craft Teacher and received salary on receipt of grant-in-aid until approval was withdrawn by DEO due to de-recognition of certificate by Director Education Department (Schools) Punjab - It was held that de-recognition would not apply retrospectively and grant-in-aid to the extent of 95% should be released to post which petitioner is holding.
