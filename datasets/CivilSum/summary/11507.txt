Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Claim of promotion - Petitioner allowed to file fresh petition if not considered for promotion with juniors - Promotion of juniors were ante-dated - It was held that the Petitioner's case was apparently not considered when the promotion orders of juniors were passed; therefore, directions were given to consider the Petitioner's claims from the date his juniors were promoted.
