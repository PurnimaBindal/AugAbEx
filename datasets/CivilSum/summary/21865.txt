Section 20 of the Specific Relief Act, 1963 states that specific performance of a contract of personal service is not allowed by law, particularly in cases of private employment that are usually regulated by the terms and conditions in the contract - if there are no written terms, the court is unable to grant specific performance.
Refusing to comply with a transfer order by a private employee is seen as insubordination and disobedience to the employer's orders, and may result in dismissal from service.
