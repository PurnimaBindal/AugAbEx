Section 4 of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 and Regulation 131 (i) of Chapter VIII of the Punjab University Calendar Vol.
I both discuss the payment of gratuity and leave encashment to employees of affiliated colleges receiving grant in aid.
The liability to make these payments is solely that of the employer, not the government or any other authority.
This obligation was confirmed in the Hindu College case on July 30, 1992, and any wrongful withholding of such payment would result in the liability to make the payment with 12% interest.
