Central Civil Services Rules 1965, specifically Rules 2(a)(iii), 12(4) and 11(v) to (ix), dictate that only the appointing authority can impose penalties outlined in Rule 11(v) to (ix), and not any lower-level authority.
However, if a presidential notification designates a disciplinary authority with the power to impose specific penalties on a certain category of employees, then the punishment order will still be valid despite not coming directly from the appointing authority.
