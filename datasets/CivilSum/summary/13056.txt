Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Eligibility - Post of Additional Director General - As per existing recruitment rules, eligibility for promotion to the post of Additional Director General required two years of service in the rank of Deputy Director General.
The petitioner fell short of this requirement with one year and nine months of service.
Despite efforts to waive the eligibility norms, before the appointment committee of the cabinet could take recommendations from the select committee for the petitioner's appointment, new recruitment rules came into force.
Under these new rules, the qualifying service for promotion became five years, rendering the petitioner ineligible for promotion when the vacancy occurred.
Therefore, the petitioner did not have the right to be considered for promotion as a matter of right.
[Paras 19 and 20]
