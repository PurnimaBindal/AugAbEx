Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Termination - Regular employee - Policy decision - The petitioner's entitlement to regularization in terms of a policy decision was evaluated.
The condition placed in the policy decision that only those class-III employees who were working against posts within the Board's purview would not be entitled to regularization was considered arbitrary.
Instructions stating that they were not applicable to employees working against Board-purviewed posts were held to be bad.
These instructions were to be applied to all employees who had worked for two years as ad hoc employees as of 1111986.
The petitioner had more than two years of service as of 1111986 and was entitled to regularization and deemed to have been regularized as of that date.
The order terminating his services was deemed to be illegal as the services of regular employees could not be terminated on the grounds that their services were no longer required by the department.
The service of a regular employee can only be terminated in accordance with rules framed by the State Government.
The impugned order was set aside, and the petitioner is ordered to be reinstated.
