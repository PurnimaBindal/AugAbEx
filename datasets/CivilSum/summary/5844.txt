Constitution of India, Article 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) and Section 10 - Res judicata - Jurisdiction of Labour Court Inquiries under Section 33(2)(b) and under Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act are separate in nature - The Labour Court may still examine the validity of a domestic enquiry in a reference made to it under Section 10 of the Act even if the dismissal of an employee has been approved by the Labour Court in an inquiry under Section 33(2)(b).
