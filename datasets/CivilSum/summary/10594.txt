According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, a change in policy must be fair to avoid suspicions of capricious or mala fide behavior and to meet the tests of Article 14.
This applies even when the policy was formerly in place secretly.
[Paras 12 and 15]

The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 state that the right to be considered for promotion is a condition of service, but employees who were considered and not selected cannot make complaints.
Although reducing the chances of promotion does not count as a shift in service conditions, it is still a consideration when thinking about promotion opportunities.
