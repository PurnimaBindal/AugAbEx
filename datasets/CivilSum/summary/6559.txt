Vijaya Bank Employees Pension Regulations, Regulation 29 - Two employees sought Voluntary Retirement, but later withdrew their request - Challenge to the validity of refusal to withdraw notice of retirement - The employer's reason for refusal is what matters, not any change in circumstances - The respondents failed to provide any reason for their refusal - Petition granted, with directions for the reinstatement of petitioners.
