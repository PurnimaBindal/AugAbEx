Railway Establishment Manual Vol-I Chapter III, Para 302 - Seniority - Benefits of ACP Scheme - The decision on whether the training period should be counted for the purpose of seniority and regarded as a period in service depends on the rules or the authorities' decision in the absence of rules.
According to the agreement, respondents were required to undergo training for 24 months as apprentices after selection.
The training period of 24 months should not be reckoned and counted for the purpose of seniority and ACP Scheme.
Seniority will be counted from the date when they successfully completed their training, as per note to para 302 of IREM.
