According to Jammu and Kashmir Compassionate Appointment Rules 1994, Rule 3, an eligible family member of a deceased person can be appointed on compassionate grounds against a vacancy in a lower rank of non-gazetted service or a Class IV post if they have a qualification of matriculation.
The rule does not specify that a candidate with a qualification above matriculation must be appointed to a post higher than a Class IV post.
Once an appointment is made on compassionate grounds, it cannot be reopened, and the appointee cannot claim a higher post subsequently.
In a relevant case 1994(3) SCT 590 (SC), an appellant who was appointed as an Attendant on compassionate grounds under SRO-43 of 1994 had worked on that post for a year and then requested authorities to appoint him to a post above Class-IV based on his qualifications.
The appeal was dismissed.
