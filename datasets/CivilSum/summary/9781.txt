Constitution of India, Articles 226, 311(2)(b) - Punjab Police Rules, Rule 16.1 - Dismissal - Retiral Benefits - The petitioners who were involved with terrorists were dismissed from service and their impugned order challenged upheld by civil courts.
Their mercy petition was also rejected and there is no provision for revision after dismissal of all appeals.
Despite being entrusted with the duty of protecting the law, they were violating it with impunity, indulging in anti-national activities.
Consequently, the petitioner is not eligible for any post-retiral benefits and the petition is dismissed with costs.
