Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, 1957, Section 98 and Central Civil Service (Temporary) Service Rules, 1949, Rule 5 - Appointment of Temporary Section Officer - Duration Limited - Mandatory notice of termination required - Salary payment requirement along with notice - Held, due to non-compliance with the payment requirement, the notice of termination is not valid in law, and the termination of service is ineffective.
