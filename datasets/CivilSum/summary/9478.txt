Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-A, 25-F - Seniority - Retrenchment - The petitioner, although having joined service earlier than Bhawani Shankar and Darshan Kumar, was correctly placed junior to them due to the benefit of past service awarded to the latter two by the Labour Court.
As a result, the petitioner was retrenched while his juniors were retained in service.
The petition was dismissed.
