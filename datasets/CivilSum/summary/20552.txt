Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 19(1)(a)(g) - Working Journalists and other Newspaper Employees (Conditions of Service) and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1955 (as amended by Act 31 of 1989), Section 10(4) - Wage fixation - Classification of newspaper establishments on an all India basis for the purpose of wage fixation is not considered bad in law, nor is it violative of rights under Articles 19(1)(a) and 19(1)(g) - It requires the financial capacity of all units taken together to be considered, and it cannot be assumed that all India Newspaper Establishments are viable regardless of their individual unit's financial incapacity - Board's finding that units falling in any of the Classes VI to IX were not to be stepped up by more than two classes, while the units falling in Classes IA to V were to be classified as belonging to a class, based on the gross revenue of all the units, results in discrimination for wage scale fixing - Therefore, directed parity by stepping up all such units by two classes.
