Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Dismissal from service - Re- instatement after acquittal in criminal case - The Board is the competent authority to re- instate a board employee who was dismissed from service after departmental proceedings, if he seeks reinstatement after being acquitted by a criminal court; not the Chairman of the Board - The Chairman's re-instatement, even if treated as a fresh appointment, is not binding on the Board while the findings of misconduct and dismissal order remain - The employee will not be entitled to the benefit of past service without any rules specifying such - No promotion or seniority can be claimed based on past service - Such an employee cannot claim parity with those ordered by the court to be reinstated after their dismissal.
[Paras 5 and 9]
