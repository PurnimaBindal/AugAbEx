Constitution of India, Articles 311, 14 and 16 - Grant of higher standard pay scales - Ad hoc service not countable for length of service for the purpose of higher pay scales - Haryana Govt. abolished scheme of additional increments to Group `C' & `D' employees on regular satisfactory service in 1994 - Introduced scheme of higher standard pay scales in 1994 on completion of 10 and 20 years of service without promotion or selection grade - Finance Department clarified in 1995 that period of ad hoc service not to be counted for grant of higher pay scales - Petitioners denied benefit of ad hoc services in calculating length of service - Held that ad hoc service countable for regular satisfactory service in context of circular dated 8.2.1994 - Benefit of higher standard pay scale to a senior not admissible if junior's pay is higher in terms of scheme.
[Paras 16 and 17]
