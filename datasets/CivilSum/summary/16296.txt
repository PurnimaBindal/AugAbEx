The petitioner, who was initially placed at seniority number 31 in the Sub-Inspector list in 1970 and was subsequently promoted to Inspector, was eventually reverted back to Sub-Inspector in 1981 for administrative reasons.
However, his seniority was altered without any justification and he was placed at seniority number 200.
The changed seniority list was deemed invalid by the court and was ordered to be re-fixed on the basis of seniority as ASIs as it existed on 111970.
