Haryana Compassionate Assistance to Dependants of Deceased Government Employees (Amendment) Rules, 2004, Rules 3(d)(iii), 4(c) - Constitution of India, Article 14 - Compassionate appointment - Petitioner's claim for compassionate appointment rejected - Reason being that deceased employee was 55 years old and family entitled to retirement benefits - Rule 4(c) allows for Rs. 25 lacs ex gratia compassionate financial assistance if employee dies at age 55 - Court holds that exception made for compassionate appointment is to aid family in crisis and not provide employment for employment's sake, citing case of Umesh Kumar Nagpal.
