Constitution of India, Articles 16 & 311(2) - Dismissal of workman - The employer challenged the award but the workman was granted reinstatement with continuity in service and back wages of 60% of the amount payable to him in accordance with the rules.
During the legal proceedings, the workman passed away.
However, his legal representatives filed a written statement and a consented order was passed in the petition stating that 60% of the arrears of wages should be paid within a year.
The mother should receive 50% of the payment to use for herself and for the benefits of the minors, while the remaining 50% should be kept in a Fixed Deposit Receipt created by the management in the name of the minors and encashable upon their attaining the majority or by orders of the High Court in case of an exigency or emergency relating to the welfare of the minors.
