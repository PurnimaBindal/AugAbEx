Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 9 and Order 2 Rule 2 - Subsequent writ petition - The provisions of CPC are not applicable to writ proceedings under Article 226 of the Constitution - It is incorrect to bar the writ petition by applying Order 2 Rule 2 CPC - However, the erroneous reasoning adopted by the Judge will not affect the ultimate conclusion which can be supported by the correct view - If the relief of reinstatement claimed in the subsequent writ petition was available to be claimed in the earlier civil suit for recovery of wages due to wrongful termination but was abandoned, it cannot be claimed by filing a subsequent writ petition, especially if it was no longer enforceable even if granted.
