Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1983, Rule 14(2) - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 31 - Rectification of inconsistencies - Next increment date - Protection of pay of senior above or equal to his junior whose due date of increment falls prior to his senior after revision of pay scales - Rule 14's intention is to have the junior's pay be lower but a strict literal interpretation would undermine this objective - If a literal interpretation may undermine any rule's purpose, it is acceptable to revise the provision in order to attain its aim - The authority's only choice is to move the senior's due date of increment and increase his pay.
