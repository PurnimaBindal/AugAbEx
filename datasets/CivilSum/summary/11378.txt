Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I, Part I, Rule 44 - Pay scale - Higher Qualification - Re-fixation to Higher pay scale - Revision of pay scale - Promotion - Promotional increment - Withdrawal of promotional increment - The petitioner received an increment on promotion under rule 44, but since she was already enjoying the higher pay scale of 1400-2600, no relief could be granted.
The order of recovery was set aside, but the order of withdrawal of the benefit of the promotional increment was upheld, and refund was ordered.
