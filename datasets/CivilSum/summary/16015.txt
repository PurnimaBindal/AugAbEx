Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Punjab Service of Engineers Class II P.W.D.
(Public Health Branch), Rules, Rules 11 and 12 - Probation - If an employee's work and conduct are unsatisfactory during the probation period, the government can revert them or extend their probation - The probation period with extension must not exceed three years - The employee has the right not to have their probation extended after three years - The order extending the petitioner's probation after nine years is unjustified, as the petitioner has already crossed his efficiency bar and been appointed to P.S.E.
Class-II - The order extending the petitioner's probation is quashed, and the date of the petitioner's appointment remains fixed by Punjab Govt. [Paras 4, 5 and 6]
