Haryana Medical Education Service Rules, 1988 - Rules 7 and 9(1)(g) - Medical College Rohtak (Conditions of Service or Teachers) Act, 1986, Section 3 - Qualifications/Promotion/Experience - The petitioner passed the MD examination while working as Registrar in Medical College, Rohtak and then worked as a Specialist Anesthetist in Al-Fateh University, Libya in General Hospital, Misurata, a teaching institution, from 5/1979 to 1985.
She was appointed as a Lecturer at Medical College, Rohtak after having her experience recognized.
Petitioner refused to be re-designated as Reader/Associate Professor under Rule 9(1)(g) since she lacked experience as a Lecturer in Medical College, Rohtak.
The respondents are estopped from raising such objection.
Also, it is not a requirement of the rule that one must have experience as a Lecturer in Medical College Rohtak only.
Therefore, the petitioner deserves to be re-designated as Associate Professor.
[Paras 11, 12 and 14]

Haryana Medical Education Service Rules, 1988, Rule 9(1)(g) - Efficiency Bar - Redesignation as Associate Professor/Reader - The petitioner was working as a Lecturer at the time of promulgation of 1988 Rules.
Her suitability for re-designation as Associate Professor/Reader needs to be determined on the basis of record irrespective of the fact whether or not she had crossed the first or second efficiency bar.
[Para 15]
