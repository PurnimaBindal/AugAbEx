Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - High Court Establishment (Appointment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1973, Rule 8(ii)(b) - Promotion/Considration for promotion - The Registrar must present all eligible candidates for consideration along with his own views, and cannot omit the name of a senior candidate for consideration based on personal doubts of eligibility - The petitioner, having previously been promoted on merit and being eligible in all aspects, had a reasonable expectation of consideration for promotion to Deputy Registrar - The Registrar's conduct was criticized and a direction was given to place the petitioner's case for consideration from the date of his equals and juniors' promotions.
