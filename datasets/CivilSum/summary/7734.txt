Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pay scale - Pay Parity -

The Labour Court does not have the power to require that respondent No1 workman be placed in the same pay scale as the conductor which was given to employees of Rajasthan State Road Transport Corporation.
The court is only authorized to give direction that the pay scale of conductor available in the Department of petitioner be made available to respondent No1 workman.
[Para 6]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) -

Failure to pay wages in accordance with the judgment and award amounts to alterations in service conditions.
Application under section 33-C of I.D.
Act is maintainable.
However, the Labour Court does not have the power to order to fix respondent No1 workman in the same pay scale as the conductor given to employees of State Road Transport Corporation under section 33-C(2) of the ID Act.
[Para 8]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pay scale -

The judgment and award give clear direction to reinstate respondent No1 worker as a conductor with all associated benefits.
The court has no grounds to award Jamadar pay scale in place of conductor pay scale, as it is in conflict with the directions outlined in judgment and award.
Respondent No. 1 workman is to be given the pay scale of conductor available in the Department of petition in place of pay scale of Jamadar.
[Para 9]
