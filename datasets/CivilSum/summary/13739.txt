Under the Punjab Nurses Registration Council (Employees Conditions of Service) Regulation, 1977, Regulation 11 and the Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal Rules, 1970, Rule 8, the President of the Council is authorized to initiate disciplinary proceedings against the Registrar of Council.
According to Clause (ii) of Regulation 11, the President must report all matters relating to disciplinary action against the Registrar to the Council for a decision.
Even if the President makes a decision to take disciplinary action, issue charges, and get decisions ratified by Council, action without jurisdiction cannot be ratified.
Therefore, since the foundation of the termination of service of the respondent was by the President who had no jurisdiction to do so pursuant to Regulation 11(ii), the ratio of the judgment in the case of Bijender Kumar Chopra applies.
The LPA was dismissed.
