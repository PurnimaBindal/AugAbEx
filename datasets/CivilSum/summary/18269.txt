Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - Inter-district transfer of teachers - Govt.
Resolution (GR) stipulates that the benefit of inter-district transfer can only be availed once - Petitioners, who had already availed the benefit before the issued date of Govt.
Resolution 462004, were denied such benefit - However, the policy only applies prospectively - Therefore, petitioners who had opted for inter-district transfer prior to 462004 cannot be excluded based on the policy according to instructions dated 462004.
