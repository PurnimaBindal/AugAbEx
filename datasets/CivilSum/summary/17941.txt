Petitioner was promoted on an ad hoc basis as a Horticulture Supervisor and found to have satisfactory work and conduct.
No one with a better right to the post was available, and it was not suggested that the post was unnecessary or that authorities did not want to fill it up.
However, an order of reversion was passed, a decision that is arbitrary and unfair and should be quashed.
[Para 7]
Employers hold an almost absolute right to revert an individual promoted on an ad hoc basis if their work and conduct are not up to the mark or if their term of appointment expires.
