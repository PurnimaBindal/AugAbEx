Respondent No. 1, K Ramakumar (Advocate) and No. 2 and 3, PK Ravikrishnan (Govt. Pleader), are concerned with the Kerala Co-operative Societies Rules, specifically Rule 197 on the correction of date of birth in service records.
The employees of Co-operative Societies fall under the provision outlined in Rule 197, which requires a correction of any errors in the recorded date of birth.
In this case, the date of birth of the first respondent has already been corrected in his SSLC book, and therefore cannot be questioned.
The correction must be entered into his service records as well.
