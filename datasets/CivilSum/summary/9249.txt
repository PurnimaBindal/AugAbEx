The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33C(2), provides the Labour Court with jurisdiction to determine the arrears of pay or wages of terminated employees.
The Labour Court is also authorized to decide whether an employee is a "workman" under the Act in proceedings under section 33C(2) of the Act.
[Para 3] B.
In proceedings under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1)(c), the finding of the Labour Court that the petitioner is not a 'workman' as defined in the Act was based on the different managerial and supervisory functions of the petitioner.
The fact that one of the functions of the petitioner was to get service and repair of tractors did not automatically make him a "workman".
[Para 4] C. The impugned order based on the finding of the Labour Court not challenged by the petitioner in any proceedings is valid under the principle of constructive res judicata.
