The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(a) and Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Schedule IV, Item 9 discuss the jurisdiction of Industrial Tribunal over unfair labour practices and the role of the appropriate government.
The Industrial Tribunal entertained a complaint of a workman challenging a punishment order based on alleged unfair labour practices and set aside the punishment order of reduction in rank, stating that the National Building Corporation was not notified as an industry by the Central Government.
However, as the Corporation is fully controlled in finances, control, and appointments on its Board of Directors by the Central Government, no separate notification is necessary to declare it as a Central Government industry.
In addition, the Corporation is deemed a State within the meaning of Article 12 of the Constitution Therefore, the Industrial Tribunal had no jurisdiction to entertain the complaint and set aside the punishment order.
Hence, the order of the Tribunal was quashed, as it was passed without the application of mind.
