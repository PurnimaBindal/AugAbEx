Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, Sections 168 and 173 - Compensation - Enhancement of - The deceased was an Assistant in the forest department and was earning a salary of Rs. 3,685/- per month at the time of his accident.
The revision of the pay scale was in progress at the time of his accident and his pay scale was later revised to about Rs. 5000/- per month.
Age of deceased was 47 years.
After deducting Rs. 40,000/- per annum towards dependence and multiplying by 12, it would yield a sum of Rs. 4,40,000/-.
In addition, the claimants are entitled to Rs. 30,000/- towards loss of consortium and Rs. 15,000/- for loss of estate.
The total amount comes to Rs. 4,70,000/-.
The Tribunal had only granted a sum of Rs. 3,53,400/- which was less than the amount to which the claimants are entitled i.e. Rs. 4,70,000/-.
It was held that the claimants are entitled to Rs. 4,70,000/- as compensation along with an interest of 12% per annum from the date of enhancement.
