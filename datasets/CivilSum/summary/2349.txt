Constitution of India, Article 16 and 226 - Regularisation - Appointment of respondent in a temporary post on adhoc basis - Contention of petitioner that he was discriminated as he was not appointed on regular basis despite having required qualifications and availability of post - Court held that employee should be appointed according to provisions of Recruitment Rules - Employee's status cannot be changed without statutory provision - Matter may be reconsidered by UPSC sympathetically in light of provisions of relaxation in Recruitment Rules and peculiar facts and circumstances.
