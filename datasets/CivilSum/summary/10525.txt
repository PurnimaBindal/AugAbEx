The termination of services during probation on abolition of post amounts to retrenchment and is considered void without payment of compensation after the workman has worked continuously for 240 days within a year.
[Para 4] B.
A workman who completes 240 days within nine months is entitled to compensation under Section 25F failing which termination is made void.
[Para 5] C. Management's weak financial condition is not a valid ground for non-payment of retrenchment compensation and back wages if retrenchment is declared void.
[Para 7] D. Abolition of post is not an excuse to avoid the reinstatement order, it would amount to giving licence to the employer to illegally terminate the services and render the Court helpless in granting reinstatement.
