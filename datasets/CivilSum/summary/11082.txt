The selection process for the position of Agriculture Mechanic Instructor advertised by the Subordinate Services Selection Board, Haryana was challenged on the basis of excess interview marks and impossibility of interviewing all candidates on a single day.
As selected candidates were not parties to the writ petition, the court could not pass any orders that would adversely affect them without hearing them.
Therefore, the petition was dismissed.
[Para 4] B.
Although the challenge to the entire selection made by SSS Board, Haryana was dismissed, the selection of Respondent No.5 was declared illegal because he did not possess the minimum qualifications required for the position.
Accordingly, his appointment was also deemed illegal, and Respondents Nos. 1 and 2 were directed to appoint the candidate next in order of merit prepared by Respondent No. 3 - SSS Board, i.e., the candidate who would fill the vacancy caused due to setting aside of the selection of the Respondent No.5.
If the petitioner is the next meritorious person, he too shall be appointed.
