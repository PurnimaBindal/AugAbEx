Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Railway Protection Force Rules, 1987, Rule 57 - Misconduct - Absence from duty - Enquiry - Probationer - Termination of Probationer Constable on his alleged unauthorised absence on four occasions without intimation, refusal to accept official documents proving his indifferent attitude, amounting to serious misconduct, unbecoming a Member of the R.P.F.
- The impugned notice of termination punitive in nature amounts to violation of Article 311(2) without proper enquiry, but does not result in termination simplicter since punishment was found on the said misconduct - However, the order shall not prevent respondents from taking any appropriate action in future against the petitioner for such alleged misconduct only after proper enquiry and observance of the rules of natural justice.
