Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Medical reimbursement - Only recognised institutions can provide treatment to patients seeking medical reimbursement, otherwise patients may seek treatment from private institutions which may not be acceptable to the State Govt.
However, there is an exception for urgent situations.
[Para 5]

Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Medical Reimbursement - Patients who have not had the opportunity to seek treatment from a recognised institution due to exigency should not have their claims rejected.
They should receive medical reimbursement at the rates applicable to a recognised institution like PGI or AIIMS.
The amount spent on a Pace Maker, like the Rs. 19000/- spent by the petitioner, should be reimbursed in full.
[Paras 5 and 6]
