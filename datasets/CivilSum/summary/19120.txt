Constitution of India, Article 16 - Indian Administrative Services (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1955 - Consideration for promotion - The Departmental Promotion Committee must meet once every year to consider promotion to IAS - The claims of all eligible candidates must be taken into consideration from the date of their eligibility according to the Rules - If the committee does not meet for a certain number of years, eligible candidates cannot be made to suffer - Even if a candidate retires during this period, the DPC must consider their case and if found suitable, they will be deemed to be promoted and entitled to all benefits from the date they were eligible to be considered.
