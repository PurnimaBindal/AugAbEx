Life Insurance Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1960, Regulations 25(4) and Proviso (iii) - Permission to contest election - The Proviso (iii) to sub-clause (4) of Regulation 25 of LIC Regulations doesn't require prior permission.
Even if an employee had applied for permission beforehand but didn't receive it before nomination papers were filed, they can't be accused of violating the regulation.
[Paras 9 and 10] B Life Insurance Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1960, Regulations 39(2) - Departmental proceedings - Removal from service - Natural justice - If an employee is charge-sheeted and given a chance to present a defense with evidence, the order of removal cannot be sustained if the employee wasn't given the opportunity to do so and is a violation of LIC Regulation 39(2) and principles of natural justice.
