Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dismissal - Alternative Remedy - Order of dismissal without providing inquiry report or second show cause notice not acceptable- Writ court has power to set aside dismissal order, even if there is an alternative remedy of industrial dispute.
[Para 1]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Delay - The limitation act cannot be the only measure for determining writ jurisdiction - Delays can be caused by laches, inaction, negligence, or external circumstances - The actual delay should be determined rationally.
[Para 6]
