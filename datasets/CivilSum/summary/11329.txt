The recognition of Prathama Examination by the Hindu Sahitya Sammelan, Allahabad as equivalent to Higher Secondary Examination of Rajasthan Board was withdrawn on 23985 under Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 pertaining to Education and Recognition of degrees and diplomas.
However, certificates obtained from the Hindi Sahitya Sammelan, Prayag before the withdrawal date, could still be treated as equivalent to Rajasthan Board's Higher Secondary Examination for appointments and promotions.
