Compulsory Retirement - Grounds for ordering compulsory retirement cannot merely be based on the pendency of a criminal case against an employee.
This is particularly true when the case concludes in the employee's favor with an acquittal.
Furthermore, the entire service record must be taken into consideration, including there being no entry in a confidential report indicating doubtful integrity.
Additionally, the fact that the employee was given promotion with retrospective effect for a ten-year period should also be taken into account.
Lastly, failure to fill out a confidential report cannot be deemed a sufficient ground for ordering compulsory retirement.
