Constitution of India, Article 235 - Departmental Enquiry against a Judicial Officer on charge of passing bail orders recklessly in four cases - The record lacks evidence indicating male fide or extraneous reasons in passing said orders - No explicit confirmation of the Officer's guilt in accepting a bribe - Mere chance of a differing interpretation is not a sufficient basis to indict a Judicial Officer for their opinion - The order of removal from service is liable to be nullified.
