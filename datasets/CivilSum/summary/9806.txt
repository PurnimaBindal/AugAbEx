Advocates Act, 1961, Sections 35(1) and 36(B) - Disciplinary proceedings - Need for a preliminary inquiry - Petitioner challenges notice to appear for a hearing on a complaint - Held that the State Bar Council must form a reasonable belief about the existence of a prima facie case of misconduct before referring to the Disciplinary Committee - Referring without forming an opinion makes the reference incompetent - Section 35(1) implies that the State Bar Council must form a prima facie opinion before referring the case.
[Para 3]
Advocates Act, 1961, Section 35 - Withdrawal of a complaint - Petitioner makes baseless allegations against a member of the disciplinary committee - Respondent punishes the petitioner - The Bar Council of India modifies the punishment - Petitioner allowed to withdraw the petition - The petitioner's act is deprecated and condemned - Rs. 1000/- imposed as costs on petitioner.
