Orissa Financial State Corporation Staff Regulations, 1975, Regulation 44 - Orissa State Financial Corporation Employees Provident Fund Regulations, 1959, Regulation 17 - Disciplinary proceedings - Superannuation - Disciplinary proceedings cannot continue after superannuation without provision in the Rules and Regulations - Provident Fund Regulations do not have provisions for continuation of disciplinary proceedings after retirement for punishment or reduction from retiral benefits - Automatically lapsing of ongoing proceedings upon retirement - Any proceedings conducted after retirement would be null and void - Order of punishment deemed invalid - Petitioner can claim arrears of salary above and beyond substenance allowance till date of retirement.
