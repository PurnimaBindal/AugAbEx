According to Section 9(1) of the Prevention of Food Adulteration Act, 1954 and Rule 8 of the Prevention of Food Adulteration Rules, 1955, Tehsil Sanitary Inspectors should not be given powers of a Food Inspector if they have been subject to strictures by the Court.
This is a policy for the Prevention of Food Adulteration Act, 1954.
A notification can be issued to rescind the powers given, and a hearing is not necessary for the rescission of such powers.
The powers should be withdrawn in the same way that they were conferred.
The petitioner received strictures from the Chief Judicial Magistrate, and since they were not challenged, they are deemed to be accepted.
Hence, the petitioner cannot argue that the strictures were baseless and uncalled for.
