Constitution of India, Article 14 - Natural justice - Adverse remarks - Government pleaders - Pleadings - If the State fails to file a written statement despite two or three opportunities, the government pleader appearing on its behalf cannot be blamed or accused of negligence unless there is enough evidence before the Court to support such claims.
- The Court expunged remarks alleging negligence and mala fide made against the Government Pleader.
