Constitution of India, Article 14 - Termination/Re-instatement/Back wages - If an employee has been kept out of job due to an illegal action of the employer, and the action is set aside, the employee is entitled to receive all the emoluments of the post for the period of absence - In this case, the Trial Court decreed only re-instatement and denied back wages - Both parties appealed - The employee's appeal for back wages should be allowed as the appeal against re-instatement was dismissed.
