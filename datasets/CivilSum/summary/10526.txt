Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Domestic enquiry/Punishment - Reversion Jurisdiction of the court - The punishing authority must pass a speaking order reflecting application of mind, and must not award punishment based on conclusions drawn by the Enquiry Officer that are perverse or unsupported by evidence.
If the punishing authority fails to pass a speaking order disclosing reasons, the punishment order may be invalid and liable to be quashed.
The order of a single judge has been upheld, and the appeal has been dismissed.
