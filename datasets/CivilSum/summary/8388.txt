Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Rajasthan Universities Teachers and Officers (Selection for Appointment) Act, 1974, Section 11 - Career Advanced Scheme, Rule 13-A - Seniority - The petitioner was promoted to Associate Professor from Assistant Professor after 13 years of service, following the recommendations of the Screening Committee and a direction from the Court, and thus his seniority should be reckoned accordingly.
He joined the university before a candidate who is currently his junior, and so the respondent-University is directed to publish the final seniority list and determine the petitioner's seniority and pay fixation in relation to candidates placed immediately junior to him.
