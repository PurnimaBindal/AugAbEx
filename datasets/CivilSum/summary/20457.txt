Article 311 of the Indian Constitution states that delinquent employees must be given a copy of the enquiry report during departmental enquiries.
Failure to provide the report violates natural justice and renders the final order invalid.
This statement of law is effective prospectively, following the Supreme Court's ruling in Mohd.
Ramzan's case (1991(1) SCC 588).
[Para 6]
If an administrative tribunal observes a departmental enquiry held by an authority in accordance with set rules and subsequently imposes a punishment of dismissal, said tribunal does not possess the power to act as a court of appeal and supplant its own decision.
[Para 8]
During departmental enquiries, adjournments at the will of the delinquent official's recourse may only be granted 18 times.
If the official appeared only five times, and an order of dismissal was issued based on available evidence, the rules of natural justice have not been violated.
