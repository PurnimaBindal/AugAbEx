Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 - Education - Admission - Admission to professional courses - Merit - Pre-entrance test - Bachelor of Dental Surgery course - Admission to professional courses is on the basis of competitive examination and merit alone - Admissions made by violating rules and regulations and the law set down by the Supreme Court must be set aside and cannot be permitted to continue, even if the court's sympathy is invoked - Allowing such admissions to persist will mean that many deserving candidates will be left out, while those who are not entitled to admission will be admitted by unfair means - A petitioner was admitted as the daughter of an SGPC employee against reserved seats, although she did not apply as such or was eligible in that category - The absence of the certificate required with the application rendered the consideration of her candidature against the reserved seat null and void - Even being the adopted daughter of an employee did not qualify her as the child-heir of an employee, and thus, she was not eligible to claim admission in the reserved category.
