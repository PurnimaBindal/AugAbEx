Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Transfer - Writ Jurisdiction - Petitioner, a member of Schedule Caste and Bank employee, transferred to non-family station in contravention of policy decision - Policy decision is not mandatory but directory - High Court cannot interfere with transfer order via writ jurisdiction unless it was passed mala fide or in violation of mandatory statutory rules.
