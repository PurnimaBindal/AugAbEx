Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 311 - Compulsory retirement - In cases where an employee has been exonerated of baseless charges in departmental proceedings, compulsory retirement in public interest is not permissible as it goes against the enquiry report - Even pending departmental proceedings on a second charge-sheet cannot justify compulsory retirement in public interest - Moreover, if no adverse remarks are found in an employee's service record within 10 years prior to compulsory retirement, arbitrary punishment for minor issues or pending proceedings cannot be a valid reason for ordering compulsory retirement.
[Paras 8 & 9]
