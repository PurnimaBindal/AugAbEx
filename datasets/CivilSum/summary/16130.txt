Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Service of Engineers, Class III, PWD (Building and Roads Branch) Rules, 1965, Rule 8(1)(c) - Promotion/Age relaxation/Amendment of Rules - The government has the authority to advertise the positions for recruitment and provide relaxation in rules by amendment.
The court cannot add to the rule anything that is not mentioned in it.
Someone who becomes overage due to late advertisement cannot claim age relaxation or consideration for the post's creation date.
