Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability pension - The petitioner, who had been found medically fit by the Medical Board when he was enrolled in the army at the age of 20, served in the army until he was invalidated on medical grounds.
Though he had received the benefit of service elements, his claim for disability pension was rejected by the CCDA on the basis that his condition was not attributable to or aggravated by his military service.
However, the court ruled that, as the Medical Board had assessed the Jawan's disability pension, the respondent had no jurisdiction to reject the claim of disability pension from their office.
As a result, the respondent was instructed to release the benefits of disability pension to the petitioner for a period of 38 months, calculated immediately prior to the date of filing of the writ petition.
