Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(j) as amended by Act 46 of 1982 - Industry - The amendment to the definition of "Industry" by Act 46 of 1982 was never enforced as it was not notified by the Central Govt. - Petitions requesting the Govt. to notify the amendment were dismissed through writ petitions and SLP.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F - Retrenchment - Services of an appointed worker cannot be terminated if the appointment was made by a non-competent authority.
Even if the appointment was made incorrectly, the provisions of Section 25-F must be followed before terminating services as no exception has been made to Section 2(oo) for defective appointments.
