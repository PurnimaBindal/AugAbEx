Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 33-C(2) and 25-FFF - Scope of - Closure compensation - The closure of an establishment and non-payment of compensation in terms of Section 25-FFF is not being disputed.
However, other entitlements which were upheld under the Payment of Wages Act, but not paid, can be claimed through an application U/s 33-C(2).
