The absorption scheme drawn up by the State outlines that all casual/daily rated employees who were employed on or before 2nd July 1974 shall be treated as monthly rated establishment employees on a fixed pay of Rs. 780/- per month without any allowance from 1st January 1990.
They qualify for an annual increment at the rate of Rs. 10/- until their services are regularised.
When their services are regularised they shall be adjusted at the basic pay scale applicable to the lowest Group D cadre but will be entitled to all other benefits available to regular employees of their class.
Of the casual and daily rated employees who have completed ten years of service as of 31st December 1989, 18,600 will be immediately regularised based on seniority and suitability effective 1st January 1990.
The remaining casual or daily rated workers who completed the ten years service by 31st December 1989 but were not included in the limit of 18,600 will also be regularised in a phased manner on the basis of seniority-cum-suitability by 31st December 1990.
The remaining casual or daily rated employees will be absorbed and/or regularised in a phased manner on seniority-cum-suitability basis by 31st December 1997.
