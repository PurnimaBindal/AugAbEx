Response: According to Constitution of India, Articles 14, 311 and 226, if a temporary employee or probationer is terminated during probation based on an assessment of performance and service record, they should receive one month salary in lieu of one month's notice with an order that does not disclose any stigma or penal consequences.
However, if the order is passed during the probation period, it is up to the employee to prove that the order was not given as a punishment.
In addition, a court cannot normally question the employer's satisfaction with the employee's conduct or work.
