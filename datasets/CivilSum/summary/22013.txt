The Indian Ordinance Factories Recruitment and Conditions of Service (Class III) Personnel Rules, 1956, governed the service conditions of diploma holders and science graduates appointed in various ordnance factories.
A circular was issued on 6111962 stating that diploma holders appointed as Supervisors 'B' would be entitled to be promoted as Supervisor 'A' on completion of one year service in the grade.
However, automatic promotion was not guaranteed upon completion of minimum service and promotion was made in accordance with recruitment rules.
The Order of the Tribunal cannot be supported and is set aside.
[Paras 1, 2, 4, 9, 16 and 17]
