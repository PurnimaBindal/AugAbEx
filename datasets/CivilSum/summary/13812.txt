According to the Constitution of India and the Border Security Force Act, accused individuals who are acquitted by the Summary Security Force Court cannot have their services terminated again by invoking Rule 22.
If Rule 22 was meant to be invoked, it should have been done so during the initial stages without holding a trial.
This is stated in Sections 115 and 74(5)(a) of the Border Security Force Act, 1968, as well as in Rules 22 and 160 of the Border Security Force Rules, 1969.
