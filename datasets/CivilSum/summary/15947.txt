According to Article 226 of the Indian Constitution, the court cannot substitute its own opinion for the selection committee's assessment of the relative merit of candidates in terms of selection based on merit.
It is not possible to contend that one's own performance is better but less marks were given by the selection committee as the candidate is only permitted to participate and be considered for the selection.
The process cannot be challenged as unfair after participation in the process.
[Paras 4 and 5] B.
The selection for the position of Lecturer cannot be alleged to be unfair if the candidate selected meets all eligibility qualifications as per the amended Ordinance of the University Calendar, according to Ordinance XXI as amended vide Resolution No. 17 of 971993, Volume I of the Kurukshetra University Calendar.
