As per Regulation No. 226 of the Jammu and Kashmir Civil Services Regulations, a mistry, who is a member of inferior service, is entitled to work until they reach the age of 60.
If a petitioner, who is working as a mistry, files a writ petition after 10 years, they may be entitled to have their pension counted by treating their retirement age as 60 years.
However, they may not receive the actual benefit.
