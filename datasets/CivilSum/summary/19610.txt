The Board's differential treatment accorded regarding grant of special pay to employees who had undergone sterilisation operation at different dates is discriminatory and violates Article 14 of the Constitution, as per the Constitution of India, Article 14.
[Para 9]
The Board's restriction of benefits already granted and the order of recovery, without affording any opportunity to the petitioners, resulted in the material miscarriage of justice.
The Constitutional Articles of India, 14 and 226, protect against discrimination and natural justice, respectively, as per the circulars initially issued.
The respondent is directed to hear the petitioners and decide the matter afresh.
[Para 10]
An administrative order with civil consequences must comply with the principles of natural justice, as per the Constitution of India, Article 226.
