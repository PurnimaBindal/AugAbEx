Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1959, Rule 27(c) and 17-A - Seniority - Effective date of Service Commission recommendations - Delay in notifying vacancies - The petitioner was included in the merit list of BDPOs in 1983, however, appointed only in 1993 due to the intervention of High Court - Inaction of authorities in notifying vacancies despite appointment of petitioners cannot negatively affect their right - The special recruitment outlined under Rule 17A can only apply to future vacancies and cannot impact candidates already advised for appointment - The High Court can issue instructions to establish seniority providing a deemed date of appointment in line with the ranking list to prevent injustice in such cases.
