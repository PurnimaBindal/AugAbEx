Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2 (oo), 25-F - DRT Act Clause 14(10)(c) - Deemed resignation - Retrenchment - Back wages - Employee who invoked clause 14(10)(c) of DRT Act, was treated as resigned from service - Employee's Industrial Dispute raised - Employer not appeared and proceeded ex parte - Matter referred to the Labour Court which ordered reinstatement with full back wages and continuity of service by holding a case of retrenchment - The order of Labour Court was proper in absence of any material produced by the employer - However considering elapsed time since termination of services of the employee, the relief of full back wages was reduced to 50% back wages - Civil writ petition was dismissed.
