Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Pension/ Gratuity/ Contemplated Enquiry - Payment of pension and gratuity to the petitioner have been halted only on the basis of an inquiry being anticipated against the petitioner.
These actions were taken despite the fact that the petitioner retired three years ago and no charge-sheet or proceeding towards the inquiry has been shown.
The petitioner suffers from daily losses and thus, directions have been given to issue the payment while also quashing the order of payment stoppage.
Interest @ 12% and costs Rs. 1000/- have also been awarded.
[Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol. II, Rule 22(C)]
