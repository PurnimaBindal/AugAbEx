Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 - Doubtful integrity - Pre-mature retirement - If a charge sheet issued to an employee includes allegations that cast doubts on their integrity, they can be considered for premature retirement.
This is not to be seen as a punishment, and even an employee with an overall good record can be removed if grave misconduct is proven even once.
