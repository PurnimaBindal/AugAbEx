According to the Orissa Service Code, Rule 7(9), workman - artisan draughtsman in P.W.D.
in the staff of the Architectural Branch are not classified as artisans and therefore, they cannot be retained in service until the age of 60 years.
Their age of superannuation is fixed at 58 years under Article 310-A of the Constitution of India.
