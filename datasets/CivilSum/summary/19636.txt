According to University of Rajasthan's Ordinance 329-M-14, a petitioner can appear in the next class examination without objection even if they did not sit for the supplementary examination.
The ordinance mandates that the petitioner clear the subject in which they had failed unless they failed in another optional subject.
Withholding the result of a higher class is illegal, and the University is directed to declare the result.
