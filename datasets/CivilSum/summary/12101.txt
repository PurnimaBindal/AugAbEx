Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Reference - Questions of fact as to dispute - Jurisdiction of the writ court - The State Government can initiate a reference based on the failure report of the Conciliation Officer, provided they believe that an industrial dispute exists.
Determination of facts in this regard lies within the purview of the State Government and the conciliation officer, and not the writ court.
Therefore, the matter has been referred back to the conciliation officer.
