The appointment of a Principal by the President of a college must be recommended by the College Service Commission as stated in the West Bengal College Service Commissions Act, 1978, Section 9, and is subject to approval by the governing body.
In a case where the appointment of the appellant was declared invalid, the governing body had approved it which meant there was no legal infirmity in the appointment.
Thus, the impugned order declaring the appointment as invalid was set aside as stated in the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 in Para 17.
