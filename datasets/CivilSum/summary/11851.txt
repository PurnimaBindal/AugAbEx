Constitution of India, Article 14 - Natural justice - Dismissal - The Petitioner was appointed as Mali-cum-Chowkidar in Panchayat Samiti in 1969 but was dismissed in 1987 without holding any departmental enquiry.
As no proper procedure was followed, the dismissal which cast a stigma on the Petitioner cannot be sustained, being opposed to rules of natural justice and wholly arbitrary.
The order is, therefore, quashed.
[Paras 4 and 5]
