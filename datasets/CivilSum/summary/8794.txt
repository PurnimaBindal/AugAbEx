The Labour Court's award to reinstate the workman without back wages was challenged and ultimately set aside because during the departmental inquiry, it was found that the workman deliberately did not issue tickets to passengers.
The explanation given by the workman to the Labour Court was not supported by evidence and was not a plausible explanation.
While the Labour Court observed that it was not a mere case of negligence, there was no evidence to show that the workman was unable to issue tickets.
Therefore, the plea of the workman should not have been accepted.
This was not a case of simple negligence, but a case of ticket non-issuance with dishonest intent.
[Paras 3 and 6]

In a separate case, the Industrial Disputes Act, Section 11-A did not apply because the workman was charged with embezzlement for failing to issue tickets despite collecting bus fare.
The charge was proved, and no evidence was provided by the workman to refute it.
Thus, the Labour Court's decision to reinstate the workman was also set aside.
[Para 4]
