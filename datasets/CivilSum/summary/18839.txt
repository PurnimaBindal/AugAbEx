The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 firm against discrimination, while the Homoeopathy Central Council Act, 1973's Section 13 addresses recognition of medical qualifications.
Medical qualifications from various institutions in India included in the Second Schedule should be treated as recognised for the Act.
The Schedule shall only be amended when a new qualification is added, a power which rests with the Central Government.
The qualification of DHMS from the Council of Homoeopathic System of Medicine, Punjab is included in the Second Schedule, meaning anyone possessing it should be treated as having a recognised medical qualification.
Merely changing the course content cannot require fresh recognition.
The Medical Council's refusal of recognition has been deemed illegal, leading to the petitioner being awarded costs of Rs. 10,000/-.
