Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Promotion/Arrears of salary/ Pension & gratuity/No work no pay - Failure to promote an employee to their rightful position and date is equivalent to denying them the opportunity to work in a higher post - The principle of 'no work no pay' will not apply - In cases of entitled retrospective promotion, the employee must receive the refixation of their pay, grant of arrears of salary, and refixation of their pension & gratuity if any - If a person retires during pendency of litigation and later on his claim for promotion from retrospective date is allowed and it is held that he will not be entitled to any benefits from the retrospective date, it will render all their efforts and wait meaningless.
[Paras 6 to 9]
