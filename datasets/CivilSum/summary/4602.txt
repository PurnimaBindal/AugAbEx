Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Advertisement - Qualification - Eligibility - Refusal to appoint an individual with a special BA Degree in Arabic to the Language Group despite having equal qualification - Advertisement only specifies a Bachelor Degree from any recognised University without strict specification of a special BA Degree - Petitioner allowed as it is clear from the existing advertisement that the special BA Degree in Arabic cannot be eliminated and should be construed as an additional degree.
