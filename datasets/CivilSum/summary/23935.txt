Article 317 of the Constitution of India concerns the Public Service Commission and states that the appointment of the Chairman and Members of the Commission should be based on their merit, ability, and suitability.
They are expected to serve as good examples in their performance and behaviors because of their unique position.
The Chairman is a constitutional trustee whose morals are held to a higher standard than those of the general public.
Vague and unfounded allegations are insufficient grounds for removal, and even if the Chairman's behavior falls short of expectations, minor lapses alone do not constitute misbehavior according to Article 317.
