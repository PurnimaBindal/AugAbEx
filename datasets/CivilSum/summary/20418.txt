Constitution of India, Article 14 - Indian Medical Council Act - Medical Institution - Recognition - Education Trust received provisional affiliation of a college from the university - College cannot be granted recognition until all defects pointed out by Medical Council are resolved - College authorities can address defects listed by Medical Council - Medical Council will assess whether all facilities for proper medical education are present in the college - No orders can be given to Medical Council and Union of India to approve the college - College cannot admit students until proper recognition is obtained - Petition partially granted.
