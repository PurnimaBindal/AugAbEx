The Payment of Gratuity Act of 1972, including Sections 2A and 4, and the Evidence Act of 1872, including Sections 58 and 115, are relevant to the case.
The respondent filed an application seeking payment of gratuity for her late husband from the date it was due.
The application was allowed by the controlling authority, and the petitioner's appeal was dismissed by the appellate authority.
The resignation letter shows the assertion that the age of superannuation was January 1992.
The respondent contends that her husband's services should be considered as continued until he died, as there was no practice of retiring an executive officer at a certain age.
However, the relevant records and evidence show the respondent's contention to be misconceived, and even if the resignation was withdrawn, the fact remains that the husband was to retire in January 1992.
Therefore, the provisions of the Act will not apply as it came into effect from May 1994, and the order passed by the authorities cannot be sustained.
The petition succeeds accordingly.
