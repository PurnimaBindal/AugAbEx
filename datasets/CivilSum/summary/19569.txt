According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India and Section 64(1) of the Wakf Act, 1954, as well as Regulation 2 of the Punjab Wakf Regulations, 1966, the Estate Officer of the Punjab Wakf Board can be transferred within his jurisdiction.
In this case, the Administrator of the Board transferred the estate officer from Amritsar to Batala.
A plea was made that only the Chairman of the Board could order the transfer of Class I Officers, but this was rejected.
