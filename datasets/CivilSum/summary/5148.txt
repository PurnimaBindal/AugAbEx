Constitution of India, Art. 226 - Writ - Services - Pension and other retiral benefits - A petitioner, who retired from service as Deputy Commandant in the BSF, claimed that he was granted pension on a pro-rata basis due to age of superannuation upto the rank of Commandant being 55 years at that time, which is three years less than officers of the higher rank in the same force and other central government employees - The decision ruled that the Respondents are directed to re-fix the petitioner's pension as he was retired at the age of 55 years and completed the qualifying service of 30 years, entitling him to full pension - The petitioner shall be paid arrears of unpaid pension within three months or he would be entitled to interest @ 9% pa [Paras 2 and 6]
