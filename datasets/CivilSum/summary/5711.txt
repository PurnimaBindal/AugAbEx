Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 162 and 311 - Maharashtra Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1982, Rule 10(4)(b) - Compulsory retirement - The petitioner's order of compulsory retirement did not specify reasons as to why he was no longer useful to the service or why he would be a burden if he continued to work.
Any orders issued by the Statutory Authority must have valid reasons mentioned within and cannot be supplemented by new reasons in the form of an affidavit.
