According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part II, Rule 616(2), an individual who voluntarily resigns after completing 10 years of service is entitled to a proportionate pension.
[Para 4]
Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part II, Rule 4.19(a) applies only when a government servant submits a resignation to avoid a dismissal or removal from service.
This rule does not apply to voluntary resignation or retirement.
