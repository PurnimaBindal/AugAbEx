Constitution of India, Article 14 - UP Basic Education Act, 1972, Section 9(1) - Appointment - Absorption in the service of Basic Shiksha Parishad - The employee is entitled to all benefits and is deemed to be in service in the absence of a termination order - No limitation exists for this recurring cause of action - The petitioner presented evidence of transfer orders, joining report, and payment of arrears of salary, thus the findings that she was not in Basic Education service cannot stand - When Basic Education was taken over by Basic Shiksha Parishad, the employee is deemed transferred to the same - If the employee's name was missing from the list of employees taken over, the authorities must provide an explanation.
