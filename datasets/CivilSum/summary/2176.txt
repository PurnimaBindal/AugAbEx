Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1932, Rule 16.28 - Punishment - Natural justice - Court can quash orders based on breach of natural justice and has jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution.
However, if exercising such jurisdiction would result in the restoration of an earlier order that was also passed in violation of natural justice or was otherwise illegal, the court is not obligated to do so.
