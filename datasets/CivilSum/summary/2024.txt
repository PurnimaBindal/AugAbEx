Punjab Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1958, Sections 22 and 33 - Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 11-A - Termination - Retrenchment compensation - Workman is entitled to the more beneficial provisions of any one of the Acts - Industrial Disputes Act provides for security of service, protection from unfair labour practices and machinery for adjudication of disputes between the employees and employer and inter-se the both and therefore contains more beneficial provisions for appropriate relief - By virtue of Section 33 of Punjab Act, the workman is entitled to take benefit of provisions of ID Act - The two Acts operate in two different fields - Provisions of Section 22 of Punjab Act 1958 cannot be said to have the effect of excluding or barring the remedy available to the workman under the 1947 Central Act against wrongful termination.
1980(3) SLR 358 Overruled.
