Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission/Experience - To gain admission to Post-Graduate Courses in Medical, three years of experience in rural areas was mandatory - The petitioner provided service in rural areas of Himachal Pradesh, which was not included while evaluating his eligibility - The court ruled that the services rendered in rural areas of Himachal Pradesh should be considered as rural area experience.
