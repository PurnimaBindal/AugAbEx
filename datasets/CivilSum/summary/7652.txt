It has been held that in a departmental enquiry, a minor girl may be called as a witness to prove illicit relations of her mother with the delinquent.
This is provided that the minor is able to give a statement and face cross-examination.
The Enquiry Officer should assess her credibility, and necessary directions must be issued to provide safeguards.
This is according to Constitution of India, Article 51A(e), Evidence Act, 1872, Section 118, and Delhi Police Act, 1978, Section 147.
