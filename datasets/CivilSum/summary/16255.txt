Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 - Promotion/Seniority - A Head Constable in the police forced to resign from service, and his representation against forced resignation was enquired into by the Vigilance Department.
The petitioner was taken back in service, but pay, seniority, and promotion benefits were not given.
The court ordered the release of pay, restoration of seniority, and promotion from the date when the petitioner's junior was promoted, even if he did not have the opportunity to pass the higher examination for promotion to the post of Assistant Sub Inspector.
