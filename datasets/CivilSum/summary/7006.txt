According to Section 581 of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, when an establishment is transferred to another, without the payment of contribution dues, the new employer is responsible for the contributions owed by the previous employer.
This liability is both joint and several.
[Para 9]  B As per Sections 45A, 45B, and 76 of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, the amount of contribution of Provident Fund is determined under Section 45A If this amount is not paid, it can be recovered under Section 45B as arrears of land revenue.
The ESI Act does not prescribe any period of limitation for the recovery of the due amount from the defaulting employer.
Section 77 is applicable only to cases where a case is filed before the ESI Court under Section 76.
