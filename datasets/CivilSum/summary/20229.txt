Constitution of India Article 311(2) Proviso - Enquiry report - Natural justice - The punishment order was cancelled by the Tribunal because the delinquent was prejudiced due to the non-provision of the copy of the enquiry report before the punishment was awarded.
The punishment order was issued prior to the decision taken in Ramzan Khan's case.
The question of competency of the punishing authority was also raised.
The case was decided by a single member of the Administrative Tribunal and was sent back to the Tribunal to be reviewed by a bench with at least one judicial member.
