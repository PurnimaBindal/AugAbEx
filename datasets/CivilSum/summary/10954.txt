APSRTC Employees Service Regulations, 1964, Regulation 7-A(e) - APSRTC (Pay and Allowances) Regulations, 1964, Regulation 2(x) - Petitioners, who worked as conductors and were granted three additional increments due to their graduation qualification, were later appointed as Junior Assistants by direct recruitment.
Management must consider the personal pay granted to the petitioners when they worked as conductors as additional increments while fixing their pay in the time scale of Junior Assistants.
The court has issued a direction to fix the pay of the petitioners in the time scale of Junior Assistant, including personal pay.
[Paras 5, 6 and 8] B APSRTC Employees Service Regulations, 1964, Regulation 7-A(e) - APSRTC (Pay and Allowances) Regulations, 1964, Regulation 2(x) - Definition of pay - The definition of pay in Regulation 2(x) includes personal pay.
