Constitution of India, Article 16 - AP Ministerial Service Rules, 1966, Rule 27(1)(iii) - Seniority - Fixation of - The third respondent voluntarily moved from a subordinate office to Head office and agreed to be placed below probationers and approved probationers in seniority - Having accepted this placement, the respondent did not object to the provisional seniority list - Thus, his seniority has attained finality and cannot be claimed from the date of his initial appointment in subordinate office - Tribunal's order for fixation of seniority from the respondent's initial appointment date is set aside.
