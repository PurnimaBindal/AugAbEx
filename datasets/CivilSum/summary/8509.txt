Army Act, 1950, Sections 20 and 37 - Court martial - Disciplinary proceedings - A military personnel failed to share information regarding mutiny with their superior officer - In response, disciplinary action was taken and the personnel was dismissed - This action is considered valid - Additionally, since the charge was triable in a court martial, it was not required for the disciplinary authority to wait for conviction in court martial.
