Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Delhi School Education Rules, 1973, Rules 97 and 103 - Promotion - Provisional promotion - Cancellation of promotion - Rectification of mistake or illegalities - Principles of natural justice - An order of promotion granted by an incompetent officer granting relaxation of requisite experience ignoring seniors and eligible candidates showing illegality in the selection process on the face of the record, with relaxation of experience in violation to rules, is void ab initio - It does not attract principles of natural justice to cancel a mere provisional promotion - Cancellation of such an order is not illegal, unjust or violative of any right.
