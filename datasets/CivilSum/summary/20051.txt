For Lecturers and Senior Lecturers to be eligible for senior scale and selection grade, they must have eight years of experience as a Lecturer and Senior Lecturer.
This requirement means that service rendered in Class-III cannot be counted as service in Class-II.
The pay scales for Assistant and Associate Lecturers are different from those of Lecturers and Senior Scale Lecturers, and they cannot count their service as Lecturers or Senior Lecturers for the purpose of eligibility, even if they hold post-graduate or doctorate degrees.
