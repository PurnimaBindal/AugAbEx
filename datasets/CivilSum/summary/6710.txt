Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Uttar Banga Krishi Viswavidyalaya Act, 2000, Section 57 - Petitioner seeking absorption against an existing vacancy of Senior Superintendent, same category of post as petitioner holds - Respondents arguing petitioner cannot claim absorption since post is of unified cadre - Not a promotion case, but rather a claim of reasonable expectation - Petitioner deserves absorption in regular post in which they were acting - Gross violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - Petitioner should be promoted when a vacancy in the post becomes available.
