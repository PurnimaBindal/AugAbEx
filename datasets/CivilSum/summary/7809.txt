Constitution of India, Article 14 - Annual Confidential Report - Adverse remarks - Natural Justice - The Commandant observed the work and conduct of the ASI for a year and included those observations in the ACR - According to the rule, one year is enough to assess the work and conduct of an employee and record adverse remarks in ACR - Only if the adverse remarks are a result of malice, ill will or spite towards the officer commented upon, can the Civil Court overturn them.
[Paras 13 & 14]
