Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admissions/Entrance Test - An entrance test for admissions to Medical Colleges is mandatory to assess the real intelligence of candidates due to differing standards of academic qualifications among institutions and universities - Allotting weightage to qualifying examinations, in addition to marks obtained in the entrance test, is invalid and goes against the purpose of conducting an entrance test - As a result, regulations that grant 20% marks for good performance in qualifying examinations have been quashed - However, elimination of qualifying examination marks does not diminish the academic charm - Although the Government has the power to set admission policies for Medical Colleges, this does not deter the petitioner from challenging the criteria - Unfortunately, since the admissions were made 1-1/2 years ago, the petitioner cannot receive any meaningful relief at this stage - Nevertheless, the Government has been instructed not to consider qualifying examination weightage in the future aside from the merit obtained in the entrance test.
[Paras 12 and 14]
