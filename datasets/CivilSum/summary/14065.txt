According to the Pension Regulations for the Air Force 1961, Regulations 153 & 160-A, disability pension may only be granted to an individual who is invalidated from service due to disability that is attributable to or aggravated by Air Force service, and assessed at 20% or over.
However, the petitioner retired from service and is not invalidated.
Therefore, the provisions of Regulation 153 are not applicable to retirees.
Granting disability pension to a retiree under Regulation 160-A, who was discharged from Air Force service on completion of engagement, is at the discretion of the President.
Since the petitioner's disability is assessed at less than 10%, it is not covered under Rule 160-A Moreover, the authority cannot be directed to reconsider the case.
