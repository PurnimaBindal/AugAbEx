Madhya Pradesh Public Health Engineering (Gazetted) Service Rules, 1980, Schedule IV-Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16-Seniority - Quota System in appointments-Posts reserved for different sources-The Rules state that diploma-holders obtaining an engineering degree while working as Sub-Engineers shall be eligible for promotion as Assistant Engineers after 8 years of experience with 10% of posts reserved for this category.
If the 8 years of experience are counted from the date of acquiring the necessary qualifications, the incentive for promotion is defeated.
Furthermore, the seniority of individuals holding similar posts in the same cadre without a specific rule is determined based on length of service rather than fortuitous circumstances.
[Paras 12, 13, 14, 16]
