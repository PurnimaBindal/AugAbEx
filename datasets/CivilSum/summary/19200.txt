Article 16(4) of the Constitution of India allows for reserved category candidates to be promoted, as long as they are shown as Scheduled caste in the seniority list, roster points are maintained, and it is indicated in their service book.
The fact of their appointment as a reserved category candidate does not need to be specifically mentioned in their appointment letter.

Even if Appendix 'A' of the Punjab State Agricultural Marketing Board (Class-1) Service Rules, 1988 has not yet been corrected and procedural formalities are still in progress, promotions can be made against newly created posts as long as they have been created by a resolution of the Board in its budget meeting and have been sanctioned by the government.
The decision of the Board does not depend on the completion of such formalities.
