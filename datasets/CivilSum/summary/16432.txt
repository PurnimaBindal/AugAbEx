Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Advertisement of vacancies - Eligibility qualifications - Cut off date - If the last date for application submission is extended with no other changes, the cut off date for eligibility qualifications is automatically extended - Those who acquire eligibility qualifications between the original and extended last dates cannot be rejected for not meeting requirements.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation based on economic criteria - Exclusion of "creamy layer" due to higher income - Increase in income limit for "creamy layer" - Those who were already eligible for a reserved category due to their lower income are not affected - An increase in income limit expands the reservation umbrella to cover more people.
