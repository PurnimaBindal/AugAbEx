Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate Appointment - Application for - Reasonable period - Although the petitioner made the application for compassionate appointment three years after the employee's death, trial judge directed authorities to decide on the application's merits.
While the contention was that it was filed after the prescribed time limit, there was no indication of any such fixed period for making such application.
The period prescribed in Residuary Article 137 of Limitation Act should be taken as a rough measure for judging delay.
A reasonable time to file an application for appointment on compassionate ground can be considered as three years.
As a result, the current application is within the time limit, and the appeal is dismissed.
[Paras 10 to 13]
