An officer's lapse causing loss to a bank can result in the loss of the employer's confidence in said officer, leading to termination without an inquiry.
Such a termination casts a stigma and violates natural justice.
A reinstatement with back wages was ordered for one such officer as the termination without an inquiry violated the Oriental Bank of Commerce (Officers') Regulations, 1982, Regulation 20.
The Bank's suggestion to pay compensation instead of reinstatement was rejected.
[Paras 17 and 22]
