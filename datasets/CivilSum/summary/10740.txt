Text: "Appointment of Chief Parliamentary Secretaries and Parliamentary Secretaries in Himachal Pradesh - No authority with Chief Minister to make appointments or administer oaths - No statutory or Constitutional provision for such appointments - Orders not issued in terms of Article 166 of the Constitution - Appointments are non est - Executive power of the State vests in the Governor.
[Paras 16 to 24]"

Response: "The orders for the appointment of Chief Parliamentary Secretaries and Parliamentary Secretaries in Himachal Pradesh were not issued in accordance with the Constitution.
The Chief Minister has no authority to make such appointments or administer oaths, and there are no statutory or Constitutional provisions for such appointments.
As a result, the appointments are non est.
The Executive power of the State vests in the Governor.
[Paras 16 to 24]"
