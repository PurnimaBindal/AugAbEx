Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 139 - Promotion/Promotion Course - Those entitled to promotion prior to the promotion Rules did not need to take the promotion course.
The plaintiff's promotion as ASI under the old rules was valid.
[Para 5]  B Limitation/Cause of action/Promotion/Superession/Adverse order - The plaintiff's juniors were promoted repeatedly without considering his case.
No order was made regarding the plaintiff, let alone an adverse one.
The plaintiff was not required to file a suit.
The cause of action only arose when the plaintiff was promoted to Inspector while his juniors were promoted to D.S.Ps, making the suit timely.
