Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Removal from service - Writ filed while awaiting Order of appellate authority - During writ proceedings, order of disciplinary authority removing petitioner changed by appellate authority - Removal order now merged with appellate order - Petitioner cannot challenge the appellate order in current writ - While appeal is pending, petitioner cannot file a writ petition contesting removal - But petitioner may file a new writ if they wish.
[Paras 3 and 4]
