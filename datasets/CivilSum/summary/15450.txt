According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, in cases where a departmental inquiry has neither been completed against a deceased employee nor initiated during their lifetime, the inquiry cannot proceed any further.
In a situation where a charge-sheet was issued to the petitioner after the notice of motion was received, the legal heirs of the deceased are entitled to retiral benefits with interest at a rate of 9% per annum.
