Army Act, 1950, Section 191 - Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 173 - Disability pension - Medical Board initially assessed disability at 40% - The disease was attributable to military service - Disability was reduced to 20% without a fresh medical evaluation - The reduction of the percentage of disability was deemed arbitrary - Consequently, family entitled to Special Family Pension.
[Paras 9, 10 and 14]

Army Act, 1950, Section 191 - Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 173 - Disability pension - Delay - Compensation - Single Judge awarded compensation of 3 lacs - The decision by the authorities was neither deliberate nor mala fide - Since no fundamental right was violated, the award of 18% interest on the amount due is sufficient to recompense for the financial harassment caused.
