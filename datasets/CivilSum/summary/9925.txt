The appointment of the workmen for a fixed period and their subsequent termination was the subject of a reference case under several sections of the Industrial Disputes Act.
The letter of appointment indicated that the workmen were appointed by the Executive Engineer of Thermal Project, which is a unit of HSEB.
On this basis, it was deemed that they could not be contract labourers.
The termination of the workmen's services on August 31, 1982, was found to be illegal by the Labour Court as it was in violation of Section 25-F of the Act, despite being a result of the lapse of time.
Although the amendment to the definition of retrenchment under Clause (bb) of Section 2(oo) came into effect on August 18, 1984, it was determined that its provisions did not apply in this case.
Thus, the Labour Court's decision was upheld and the petition was dismissed.
