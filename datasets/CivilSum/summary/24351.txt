Karnataka State Road Corporation (Cadre and Recruitment) Regulations, 1982, Regulations 4, 9, 10(5) - Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, Section 45 - Badli workers - Termination of service - A worker who remains badli does not receive the same protection as one who has completed probation, and his service may be terminated upon compliance with contractual or statutory requirements.
