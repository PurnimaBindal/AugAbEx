The petitioner, a physically handicapped employee, has claimed an enhanced retirement age from 58 to 60 years based on instructions dated 3112006.
The petitioner's medical certificate reflects a physical disability of 70%.
Instructions have stipulated that the normal retirement age of Group A to D employees with 70% disability be raised from 58 to 60 years.
As a result, the court has directed the respondents to consider the case of the petitioner for continued service until the age of 60 years.
