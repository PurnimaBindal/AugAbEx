Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical Reimbursement - Petitioner's claim denied due to being an outdoor patient - Petitioner had undergone kidney transplantation and had been taking drugs prescribed by doctors who conducted operation - Although petitioner was an out-door patient, treatment received was inherent and inseparable from the treatment received when admitted in hospital - Hence, Government is obligated to reimburse petitioner's medical expenses.
