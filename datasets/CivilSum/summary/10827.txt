Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Criminal proceedings - Acquittal in criminal proceedings on the same charges - If the charges presented in a criminal trial and the departmental charge sheet are based on the same facts and require same witnesses for proof, then acquittal in the criminal proceeding will result in the nullification of the departmental proceeding - This will effectively abate the departmental enquiry.
[Paras 5 and 6]
