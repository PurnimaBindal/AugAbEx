Appointments made without advertising the posts and in violation of eligibility criteria under the rules for the Secretariat of Assembly can be challenged by non-applicants, as they have locus standi.
[Para 24]
The appointments made by the Speaker to the staff of Assembly secretariat found invalid for violation of the recruitment Rules and Article 14 of the Constitution cannot be sustained even though the appointees have been continuing in service for the last five years.
[Paras 75 and 76]
The orders of the Speaker of State Assembly passed in the capacity of appointing, punishing, and appellate authority of the staff can be judicially scrutinized like other authorities under the similar rules.
The Speaker does not enjoy any immunity from judicial scrutiny even while acting in exercise of his power under Section 6 of the Tenth Schedule to the Constitution.
[Paras 57, 58, and 66]
Appointments made by the Speaker of State Assembly without advertising the vacancies and in violation of the procedure and eligibility criteria prescribed under the recruitment rules cannot be sustained, even if it was made in emergent requirement, as the Speaker had no power of relaxation or emergency appointments under the Rules.
