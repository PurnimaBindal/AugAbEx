The Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 29, 30, 14, and 15(4) imply that reservation for the sons or daughters of teachers from the respondent institute in minority institutions is not permitted.
The law does not allow preferential reservation within the minority community students, and thus it is set aside.
[Para 17]
Minority institutions, according to Article 14, 29, and 30, cannot prescribe impermissible reservation and create a microcosm class of preferential reservation within the minority reservation itself.
[Para 15]
Preferential reservation within the reservation is impermissible in law, as stated in the Constitution of India Article 14.
