State Bank of India Subsidary Banking Act, 1965, Section 34 - Jurisdiction - State Bank of Hyderabad invited tenders for construction of a branch building - A circular from the Government of India (Banking Division) stated that tenders without earnest money should also be considered.
However, it was ruled that the Central Government does not have the authority to issue such directions to State Bank of Hyderabad.
According to Section 34 of the Act, State Bank of India has the power to issue directions to State Bank of Hyderabad, not the Central Government.
