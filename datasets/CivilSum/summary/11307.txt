Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation - The State Government acted illegally by appointing a candidate belonging to Backward Classes Block-B against the post reserved for appointment as A Class Naib Tehsildar from Backward Classes (Block-A) on compassionate grounds, instead of appointing the petitioner whose name was recommended by the SSS Board at No. 2 in the merit list - According to the instructions, candidates of Block-B category cannot be appointed against the posts reserved for Block-A category - The court directed the State to give the petitioner the appointment and seniority in the cadre as per his merit determined by the Board, and the compassionate appointee can be adjusted against a different post if available.
