Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Punjab National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965 (as applicable to Haryana) Rule 4 (as amended) War Service Benefits - The petitioner, who was released from the Army on compassionate grounds and later selected as a clerk by the SSS Board, was denied military service benefits due to being released on compassionate grounds.
However, the amendment that added a proviso to Rule 4, creating a bar on such benefits to those released on compassionate grounds, was held as ultra vires.
Directions were given to decide the matter afresh, treating the writ petition as a representation in light of the law set out in KC Arora's case.
