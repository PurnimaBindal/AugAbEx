The UP Basic Education Act, 1972, Section 19, and Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India govern the appointment of teachers, with eligibility requiring a Basic Teacher's Certificate (BTC).
However, a candidate who possesses a BTC from the State of MP may not have their certificate recognised as equivalent to either of the prescribed qualifications.
In a case where a candidate was found ineligible, and a Single Judge refused to intervene, their order was not overturned.
Furthermore, the petition was filed after the select list had been exhausted and all vacancies had been filled.
As a result, the appeal was dismissed.
