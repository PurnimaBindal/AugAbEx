Letters Patent Appeal, Clause X - Appointment - Promotion - Post of Tourist Officer - The respondent-writ petitioner challenged the appointment of the appellant-respondent because he did not possess the prescribed qualification for the post.
The respondent-writ petitioner also claimed regular appointment by promotion.
The prescribed qualification for the post was a BA with History, but the appellant-respondent had an M.A.
with Geography.
The learned single judge correctly invalidated the appointment of the appellant-respondent because he was ineligible for the post.
However, the learned single judge should not have directed the consideration of the case of the writ petitioner for promotion, and it should have been left to the government's discretion to devise an appropriate mode of appointment.
[Paras 8 and 9]
