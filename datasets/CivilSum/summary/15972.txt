Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Natural justice - Bank employee - Disciplinary proceedings - The employee had requested for some documents to file his explanation, but the authority only allowed him to peruse the documents.
Additionally, the employee asked for permission to engage an advocate to defend him and requested for financial help for that purpose, but both requests were declined.
As a result, an ex-parte order of dismissal was passed, and the bank's actions were found to have denied the employee a reasonable opportunity to defend himself.
The order of dismissal was quashed with the liberty to proceed afresh in accordance with the law.
