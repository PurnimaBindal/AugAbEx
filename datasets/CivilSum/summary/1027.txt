Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-B - Interpretation of term 'Gainful employment' - Workman earning of 5000 from shop of sweets and tea stall - The term gainful employment as used in the context of 'had not been employed in any establishment' should consider if the employee receives adequate remuneration - The legislature's intention is not for employees to live a destitute life and then be entitled to benefits under Section 17-B of Act - Self small business continued by an employee to make both ends meet cannot be equated with being employed in an establishment and receiving adequate remuneration - Therefore, the workman is entitled to last drawn wages from the date of award.
