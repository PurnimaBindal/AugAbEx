As per Sections 51, 53 and 258 of the J & K Municipal Act, cancellation of grant for advance increments is possible.
However, according to section 258, the state government needs to provide an opportunity of hearing to the Council and affected individuals before suspending any order or resolution of the Municipal Council.
In this case, no such opportunity was provided to either party.
Thus, the order of withdrawal of increments is considered null and void under the given circumstances.
