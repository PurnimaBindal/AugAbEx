In accordance with the Apex Court's ruling in RK Sabharwal's case, reservation is an additional category and therefore individuals selected based on merit in the general category cannot be included in the reserved category.
The court has directed for the merit list to be reviewed to ensure that no person of lower merit than the petitioner has been appointed in the reserved category.
[Paras 8, 9 and 11]
The petitioner's claim can only be considered in the event that a person of lower merit is selected and appointed after the merit list has been revised.
As no person has been selected, the petitioner's argument holds no weight.
[Para 8]
The validity period of the panel is not relevant to the petitioner's situation since a faulty list was used to fill the position.
This ruling was established in Pursushottam's case and therefore the petitioner should not be made to suffer.
