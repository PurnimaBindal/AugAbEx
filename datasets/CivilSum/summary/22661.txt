Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Govt. of Orissa Resolutions dated 23.7.1983, 2561988, 1311987, 1321991, 1331992, 17.6.1993 - Seniority/Promotions - Inter-se seniority between matric and non-matric teachers - The two categories of teachers cannot maintain two gradation lists - The promotion of teachers will be based on the date of their promotion as of August 25, 1988, regardless of whether they are matric or non-matric teachers - A combined seniority list of matric and non-matric teachers must be prepared, district-wise as on 2561988 - Promotion to Head Pandit position will depend on the application of the principle of training, seniority, and suitability, taking into account whether the schools have three or more teachers or less than three - The Full Bench of the Tribunal found no conflict between resolutions dated 1321991 and 1331992 and held that the earlier Division Bench was wrong in determining that the Resolution dated 1331992 conflicted with the Resolution dated 1321991.
