Life Insurance Corporation of India Act, 1956, Section 49 - Life Insurance Corporation of India (Agents) Regulations, 1972 (made statutory Rules in 1982 with publication in Gazette on 2951982), Regulations 5, 17 and 25 - Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1955, Rule 15 - Circulars issued by Chairman of LIC of India debarring spouses of employees of Central/State Government or Public Sector Undertakings from being appointed as Agents of the Corporation - The circulars are unconstitutional as they violate Articles 19(1)(g) and 21 of the Constitution of India - Executive instructions cannot be taken as policy decision - The directions in the impugned circulars cannot be considered as policy decisions - There is no prohibition under C.C.S.
(Conduct) Rules against the appointment of spouses of Government employees as Agents.
AIR 1984 SC 2148 distinguished.
