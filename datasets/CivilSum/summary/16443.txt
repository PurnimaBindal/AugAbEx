According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, a transfer cannot be challenged unless there is an allegation of mala fide.
In this case, the petitioner was accused of not fulfilling their duties, and the transfer was made based on working conditions and administrative needs.
The court held that administrative authorities have the power to deploy employees as necessary, and employees who fail to perform their duties cannot question such deployment.
Therefore, the petition was dismissed.
