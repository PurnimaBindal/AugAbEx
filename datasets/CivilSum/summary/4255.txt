Rule 10-A of the Punjab Service of Engineers Class II P.W.D.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Conditions of service - Seniority is a condition of service of a civil servant that cannot be altered to his disadvantage without following due process and giving notice to affected parties.
[Para 15]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Conditions of service - Conditions of service refer to all the conditions governing a person's tenure from appointment to retirement, as well as pension matters.
[Para 15]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Conditions of service - Conditions of service cannot be modified or altered to the detriment of the employee by subsequent administrative instructions with retrospective effect.
[Paras 17 and 18]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Conditions of service - Assurance - To obtain court direction for compliance with a promise made by the State, the individual must prove that an assurance was given, and in reliance on that assurance, they altered their position - In the absence of such proof, no direction can be issued.
[Para 20]

Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 309 - Statutory Interpretation - Provisions of the statute should be interpreted in light of the act's intent and purpose and cannot be interpreted in the absence of specific language that would disadvantage a particular group.
