Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Union Bank of India (Officers) Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 19(1) - Age of Superannuation - Although the petitioner argued that the change to Regulation 19(1) of 1979 did not apply to him due to the lack of a board resolution accepting 58 years as the age of retirement, after considering all the facts, Regulation 19(1) and its provisos were deemed a valid piece of legislation.
Additionally, the Central Government had issued guidelines that determined the age of retirement for cases like the petitioner's to be 58 years in respect of Regulation 19(1) which is applicable.
Therefore, legally, the petitioner was retired from service when he reached the age of 58 years and there is no basis for filing a grievance against such action.
