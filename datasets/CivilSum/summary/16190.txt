Admission/Education/Examination/Punjab Engineering College Students Discipline and Conduct Rules, Rule 1(9) and 12 - Punjab University Calendar, Volume II, 1988, Regulation 7 - Examination form - If a student has fallen short of requisite lectures due to illness and has not submitted the examination form, they may be asked to repeat the course of instructions in those subjects - Students who miss the first semester may not enter the second semester or any later semesters under Regulation 7 of Punjab University Calendar, Volume II - Illness-related absences and non-submissions cannot result in removal from college rolls - The student can only repeat the first semester courses.
