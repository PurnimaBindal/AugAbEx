The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226, and Rajasthan Administrative Service Rules, 1954, Rule 41, relate to out of turn promotion.
In this case, the petitioner recovered a significant amount of arms and ammunition from a terrorist hideout and demonstrated exemplary duty.
The authorities praised the petitioner's work and instructed them to examine the case for out of turn promotion by relaxing Rule 41 of the Rules, 1954.
