Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Recovery from pay - Natural Justice - Penal Rent - Principles of Natural Justice must be followed when recovering Penal Rent from an employee's salary.
An employee must be given a chance to show that they were not at fault or that the authority cannot impose penal rent.
The order of recovery of penal rent without affording the employee an opportunity is against natural justice, and the order has been quashed.
