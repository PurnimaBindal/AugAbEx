The Punjab Govt.'s instructions dated 10121997 provide guidelines for admissions via sports quota, including the issuing of sports gradation certificates and the reservation of seats for sportsmen/women.
These instructions have been revised to state that certificates shall not be graded after five years of issuance and the team must have placed 1st, 2nd, or 3rd.
Therefore, certificates issued over five years after participation or without indicating team position cannot be considered valid for admission under these instructions.
The admission granted to Respondent 3 under the sportswomen quota with such a certificate is invalid.
