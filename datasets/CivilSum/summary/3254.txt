Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(rr) - Employees State Insurance Act, Section 2(22) - Wages/Workman - Ex gratia payment - Whether an employee receiving monthly emoluments more than Rs. 1600/- is a workman - Ex gratia payment is not considered wages - For computing the wages of a workman under Section 2(rr) of the ID Act and Section 2(22) of the ESI Act, ex gratia must be excluded - In the event that the wages of a workman, after excluding the ex gratia payment, fall below Rs. 1600, the workman may initiate an industrial dispute.
[Paras 17 and 18]
