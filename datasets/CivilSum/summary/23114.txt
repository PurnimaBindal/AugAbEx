Constitution of India, Article 14 - High Court of Himachal Pradesh Recruitment (Conditions of Service) Rules, 1992, Rule 10 - Promotion - Post of Superintendent - The rule is deemed to be unjust and iniquitous as it treats unequal sets of posts on the same level.
Additionally, it provides unwarranted benefits to those holding lower posts than those holding the higher posts.
Therefore, the rule violates Article 14 of the Constitution and has been struck down.
