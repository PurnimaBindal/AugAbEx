Constitution of India, Articles 16, 14 and 309 - Grant of special pay - Retrospective application - Udyog Sahayak employees were initially granted special pay at 20% of basic pay per month up to Rs. 300/-, but subsequently on May 31, 1982, it was clarified that a special pay of only 10% of basic pay up to a maximum of Rs. 200/- per mensem would be allowed, and the change would be effective from the date of the earlier notification - It was held that rules made under Article 309 can be enforced retrospectively, but an executive order that is to the disadvantage of an employee cannot be enforced retrospectively - State's decision to reduce the special pay rate retrospectively cannot be sustained - Employees do not have an absolute right to demand special pay at a specific rate - The writ petition is partially granted.
