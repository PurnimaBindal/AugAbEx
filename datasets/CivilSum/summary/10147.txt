Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Petitioners were deemed ineligible due to completing a two-year Art and Craft diploma from the Punjab State Board of Technical Education and Industrial Training, which is not considered equivalent to the two-year diploma offered by the Industrial Training and Vocational Education Department of Haryana.
The 1984 instructions recognizing the diploma of the Petitioners have been overruled by the 2.11.1999 instructions.
As a result, all Petitioners who qualified or were admitted prior to the implementation of the new policy must undergo an interview and their appointment eligibility should be considered.
