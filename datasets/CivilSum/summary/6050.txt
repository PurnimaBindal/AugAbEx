Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Question of fact/Admission - The High Court should not usually intervene in questions of fact in its writ jurisdiction regarding education - However, there are some exceptions to this rule - The High Court can take action if there is a miscarriage of justice due to the authority's arbitrary or non-application of mind - In a case where a student with higher marks and merit is denied admission in favor of a lower-ranked candidate, the court directed the respondents to admit the petitioner within two weeks.
