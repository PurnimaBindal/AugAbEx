The Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 3(3) states that the most qualified member of teaching staff should be appointed as the head of the school.
Despite this rule, the petitioner's claim for promotion was superseded.
The appointment made contrary to Rule 3(3) of MEPS Rules, 1981, is warranted to be set aside.
[Para 6]

The Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1977, Section 9 establishes an appeal process.
Although no period is prescribed for making representation against supersession, the petitioner made an adequate representation within a reasonable time.
The delay and latches which superseded petitioner's claim for promotion did not make the petitioner guilty of delay and laches.
[Para 7]
