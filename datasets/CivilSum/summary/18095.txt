The ad hoc appointment order stipulating that the petitioner has no right to claim seniority does not take away the petitioner's right to appear in the trade test as eligible Group-D serving employees were invited to apply.
The petitioner was already working as a Driver and had better merit than the cleaners, Trolleyman, and khalasies.
Depriving employees of different divisions of their legitimate right of promotion on the grounds that this benefit should be given by the parent division is not acceptable.
The petitioner has been performing duties as a Driver for the last two decades, and if anyone junior to the petitioner was placed in the grade of Rs. 3050-4590, the petitioner would be deemed substantively promoted from that date with all consequential benefits.
