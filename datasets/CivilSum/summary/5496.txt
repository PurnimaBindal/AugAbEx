Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo)(bb), 25-N - Termination - The workman had been employed by the petitioners from February 21st, 1990 until February 29th, 1992, and had worked for more than 240 days in the 12 months preceding his termination.
The petitioners could not provide evidence that the workman's services were terminated due to non-renewal of his contract, and there was no indication that the nature of the job was for a specified period and specific purpose.
As such, the Labour Court was right to find that the petitioners could not make use of section 2(oo)(bb) of the ID Act, and there was no issue with the court's award to reinstate the workman with continuity of service.
