Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work - Ticket verifiers in Punjab Roadways who were engaged on daily wage basis against vacant posts and later regularised and appointed on regular basis in regular pay scales, are entitled to the same pay scales as their counterparts in Pepsu Road Transport Corporation and Haryana Roadways.
Denying such pay scales is unconstitutional and violates Articles 14, 16 and 47 of the Constitution.
[Paras 16 and 17]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation of services - Daily wage workers - State has to consider their cases for regularisation within reasonable time and cannot keep them on daily wage basis for an indefinite period.
The non-existence of regular posts is not the essence of regularisation, and the state can create posts as per the requirement of the establishment.
Keeping the employees on daily wage basis under the garb of non-existence of regular posts is unconstitutional and violates Articles 14, 21, 41 and 47 of the Constitution.
[Paras 17 to 21]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Equal pay for equal work - Arrears of pay - Employees can claim the benefit of equal pay for equal work at any time, and the court should allow arrears only from the date of filing the petition and not from retrospective dates.
There is no estoppel against constitutional rights.
[Para 20]
