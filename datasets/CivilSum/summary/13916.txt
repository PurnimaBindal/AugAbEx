Employees State Insurance Act (34 of 1948), Sections 85(g) and 44 - Coverage of establishment under E.S.I.
- Maintaining relevant records and presenting them to authorities is compulsory for the establishment - Unfortunately, the establishment in question did not maintain those records - It refused to produce records when asked by ESI Inspector - As a result, the employer-accused is held guilty of the offence punishable under Section 85(g).
