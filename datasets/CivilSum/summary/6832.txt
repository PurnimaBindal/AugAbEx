In accordance with the Constitution of India, Article 226, the scope of judicial review of departmental proceedings is limited to violations of natural justice, disproportionate punishment, malice, infringement of statutory rules, and other illegitimate reasons - a writ court will not grant judicial review without valid grounds or evidence.
[Para 17] B.
The Constitution of India, Article 226 states that successive petitions for judicial review of disciplinary action taken against a Government employee are not maintainable - past events cannot be reconsidered unless it is relevant to the present situation.
[Para 18] C. When government officials order an employee's compulsory retirement and leave disciplinary proceedings open for an indefinite period of time, they are acting unfairly - disciplinary proceedings must be concluded and the employee must be paid their retirement benefits within a stipulated time period.
