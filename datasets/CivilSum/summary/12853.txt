Army Act and Rules/Army official/Promotion/Consideration/ Performance/Average report/Confidential report - The performance report on the petitioner's working should have been initiated only by the incharge of MI Room who had seen his performance during the relevant period - Those who did not work under him during that period were not competent to initiate the report - If no report was given by the immediate incharge of MI Room, the competent authority should be directed to initiate one - Only reports initiated by the competent authority or immediate incharge should be taken into consideration - The petitioner's performance report was initiated by the immediate incharge of the MI Room and assessed as Above Average, making him eligible for promotion to Naib Subedar - Therefore, the authorities have been directed to reconsider the petitioner's case for promotion accordingly.
