Referring to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, as well as Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, and Punjab Family Pension Rules, Rule 617(3), Note 2, the subject of Family Pension arises.
In this case, the petitioner retired on 131986 as a Head Constable, and his first wife passed away on 2111980.
The petitioner remarried in January 1994 and requested to update his family pension papers with the name of his second wife.
However, his request was denied based on note 2 of Rule 6.27(3).
Following the judgement of the Apex Court in Laxmi Kunwar v. State of Rajasthan, 1993(8) SLR 427, the respondents have been directed to nominate the petitioner's second wife as the nominee in his pension papers.
