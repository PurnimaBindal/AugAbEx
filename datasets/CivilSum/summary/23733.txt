Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work - The respondent argues that since he is a Private Secretary attached to the Vice Chairman of the Tribunal, he should receive the same scale of pay as Private Secretaries to Ministers and Judges of the High Court.
However, the applicant did not provide evidence of recruitment mode, qualifications, responsibilities, or promotional opportunities to support their claim of equal pay for equal work.
The responsibility of substantiating the point is with the applicant, not the respondent.
The case is remanded for redetermination after granting the parties an opportunity.
