Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Industry - The appellant, an Agricultural Produce Market Committee, established under the Karnataka Agricultural Produce Marketing (Regulation) Act, 1966 (State Act), falls under the definition of `industry' under Section 2(j) of the Central Act.
Merely being a statutory corporation or having government servants as employees does not exempt it from being considered an enterprise under the Central Act.
The appellant is an undertaking that performs its duties systematically and in an organized manner, rendering services to the community by regulating the marketing and trading of agricultural produce.
The committee's activities are not of a sovereign nature.
