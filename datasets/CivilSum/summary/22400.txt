Article 311 of the Constitution of India permits the removal of an employee for habitual absenteeism so long as all the appropriate procedures have been followed - if the misconduct has been proven within the legal framework, then judicial intervention is unlikely to be invoked and the punishment imposed cannot ordinarily be interfered with.
