The Dairy Helper-cum- cleaner accused of misconduct was dismissed by the disciplinary authority following a domestic enquiry.
The Labour Court found the domestic enquiry defective and allowed the management to adduce evidence afresh.
[Para]

The Industrial Disputes Act, Section 11 states that the punishment imposed should not be disproportionate to the gravity of the misconduct committed.
In this case, the Labour Court found the dismissal by the management was valid after rectifying the defective domestic enquiry.
Therefore, the enforcement of the order would relate back to the date of the original dismissal and not from the date of judgement of the Labour Court.
[Para]
