Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Punjab Police Rule 918(2) state that at the time of compulsory retirement, the entire record of a public servant must be taken into consideration, including uncommunicated entries in the confidential record and single adverse entries given due weighage.
The petitioner's entire record was considered, and they are deemed fit for compulsory retirement.
However, the petitioner is entitled to six months leave encashment with interest of 9% pa from the due date until payment.
[Paras 11, 12 & 14]

The State of Gujrat v. Umedbhai lists crystallized principles for compulsory retirement, such as the officer being no longer useful for the general administration and taking into account the entire service record, with adverse entries given due weightage.
Compulsory retirement should not be used as a short cut to avoid departmental inquiry, and it should not be imposed as a punitive measure.
[Para 10]
