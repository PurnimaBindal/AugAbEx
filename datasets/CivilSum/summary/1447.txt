Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Section 17-B - Termination - Dismissal of application seeking approval of order - According to law, the termination of an employee's service is deemed void and the employee is considered to be in service for all practical purposes if an application for approval of an order is dismissed - The application of the employee under Section 17-B was allowed by the Single Judge, who directed payment of last drawn wages.

Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Section 17-B - Wages during pendency of appeal - Claim rejected due to lack of evidence - A workman's plea of being gainfully employed during the intervening period is rejected - The Single Judge found that there was no evidence to disprove the workman's statement - It's not acceptable to reject the contention in the absence of any rebutting evidence to disprove and dislodge the findings of fact - The business of the respondent's wife, under no circumstances, could be considered to be the business of the respondent.
