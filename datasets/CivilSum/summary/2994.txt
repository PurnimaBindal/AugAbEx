Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Schedule II, Item 6 - West Bengal Municipal Act (22 of 1993), Sections 53(3) and 54 - Regularization - The petitioners were appointed by Municipality on a contract basis for a certain purpose on non-sanctioned posts.
The appointment was not made according to statute and rules.
Moreover, the appointment of petitioners in violation of provisions of the Constitution and Statutes is a nullity.
The petitioners have worked for 240 days.
However, no regularization is permissible on completion of the project.
Just because a person worked for 240 days does not entitle them for regularization.
[Paras 11, 12, 15 and 18]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ Petition - Alternative remedy - The writ filed by petitioners on apprehension that their services will be terminated without complying with the provisions of Section 25-F on the Industrial Act is a disputed question.
It cannot be taken into a writ proceeding nor can a writ court be converted into an Industrial Court.
The proper remedy for concerned workmen would be to raise an Industrial Dispute.
[Paras 24 and 25]
