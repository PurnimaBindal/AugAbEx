Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rules 4 and 5 - Military service - The adoption of Punjab Emergency Concession Rules, 1965, with amendments by the Punjab State Electricity Board and State of Haryana, does not require incumbents to have joined the Military service during Emergency to receive benefits.
However, State of Haryana and PSEB have limited the benefit to those who joined Military during Emergency from October 26, 1962 to January 10, 1968.
The Supreme Court struck down the retrospective applicability of the Haryana amendment.
Those who joined civil services before the amendment are entitled to the benefit, regardless of when they joined Military service.
The Board amended its instructions based on the Haryana amendment, effective from 2141987.
Petitioners who joined the Board before the amendment are entitled to Military service benefits, even if they joined before the imposition of Emergency.
