Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Termination - Natural Justice - The termination process must follow the principles of natural justice under Article 14 of the Constitution.
According to Article 311, the termination should be made only after holding an enquiry into the allegations of misconduct and affording the employee an opportunity to defend themselves.
If the termination takes place with a simpliciter order without casting any stigma of misconduct, it cannot be held to be punitive and must follow the terms of appointment.
However, where definite charges have been leveled, the employee has been held guilty in the enquiry report, and the services are terminated without supplying a copy of the report against them and without show cause notice, such an order must be held stigmatic and punitive in nature in violation of the principles of natural justice.
