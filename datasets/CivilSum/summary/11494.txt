Punjab Chowkidara Rules, 1965 and Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 & 21 - Village Chowkidars are not Government servants.
They are full-time workers who must receive fair wages.
Despite not being equivalent to any civil post, the nature of the duties and responsibilities makes it a full-time job and not just a part-time option.
It is thus unconstitutional to pay them inadequate remuneration of Rs. 400-600 per month.
The Government should grant them adequate wages and security of payment.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - The Court does not have jurisdiction to prescribe pay or pay scales for particular posts.
