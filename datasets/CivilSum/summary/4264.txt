Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 311 (2) - Bank employees - While the Haryana State Co-operative Bank is considered a State under Article 12, its employees are not considered Government servants and therefore not protected under Article 311(2).
However, employees of these types of State agencies or instrumentalities must be afforded the minimum requirements of natural justice similar to the principles of Article 311(2) as condemning someone unheard is unacceptable.

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Termination simpliciter - Employers cannot terminate an employee under the guise of termination simpliciter based on allegations of unauthorised absence, tampering with records, or misbehaviour towards superiors without proper investigation and compliance with natural justice.
[Paras 8, 10 and 11] C. Constitution of India, Article 14 - Probationer/Enquiry/Disciplinary proceedings - Employers must conduct an investigation and afford the employee an opportunity to defend themselves against any allegations of misconduct.
Even if a termination is labelled as simpliciter, the court must examine the circumstances and background surrounding the order.
