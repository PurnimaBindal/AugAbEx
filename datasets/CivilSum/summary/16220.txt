Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Ad hoc appointment/Regularisation - The prescribed Rules govern every post under the State and every eligible person has an equal right to compete for recruitment.
Ad hoc appointments are temporary and cannot be allowed for years.
Regularizing ad hoc/temporary employees violates the equality of opportunity principle, and even getting names from Employment Exchange cannot cure this defect.
Every stipulation in the order, especially qualifications, must be enforced strictly, and mere basis for a long time does not entitle regularization.
[Para 8]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment/Regularisation/Qualifications - The petitioner has worked as an SV teacher for fifteen years, but doesn't fulfill the post's qualifications.
Other teachers with the same qualifications have been regularized, but a writ of mandamus cannot be issued to repeat the mistake.
Regularizing unqualified persons is improper and cannot be ordered on the ground of discrimination.
[Paras 9 and 11]
