Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Dismissal of daily wage constables due to allegations of indiscipline - No charge-sheet given nor inquiry held - A simple removal order cannot be considered based on misconduct - A temporary or probationary worker can be discharged with a simple and unstigmatic order, even if the reason for discharge is alleged indiscipline - Distinguishing between motive and foundation.
[Paras 7, 9 to 11]
