Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 16 - University Grants Commission Guidelines - Selection Grade - Petitioners requested that their service in different colleges be included in the calculation of 16 years for the grant of selection grade - The issue is covered by a previous court decision in Dr. Romila Jain v. The State of Haryana and others, 1995(3) SCT 53 - The petition was allowed, and the respondents' decision to withhold senior scale from the petitioners was deemed unlawful - The respondents were instructed to evaluate the petitioners' case in line with the University Grants Commission guidelines.
