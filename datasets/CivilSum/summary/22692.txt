Constitution of India, Article 326 and 311 - Regularisation Of Service - Marketing Inspectors and clerks working in Food and Supplies Deptt.
of State Government - A Division Bench of the High Court ruled that they were not entitled to an extension and were appointed to work until the end of August 1984, until procurement ceased - this order became final.
Later, a single judge ordered their absorption into service as seasonal posts discontinuing from August 1984 due to a lack of sanction.
This single judge is bound to follow the decision of the Division Bench, and his order is liable to be set aside since it is opposed to the Division Bench's order.
