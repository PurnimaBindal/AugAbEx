Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical Reimbursement - A government employee suffering from chronic renal failure received medical reimbursement for actual expenses related to kidney transplant surgery but spends approximately Rs. 12000/- per month for regular follow-up and checkups for the same disease.
The court finds that a uniform policy of only Rs. 6000/- per year for outdoor patient treatment cannot be applied to this case.
The authorities are therefore directed to reimburse the petitioner for all amounts incurred for follow-up treatment.
[Paras 6 and 7]
