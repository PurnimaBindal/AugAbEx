Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Selection - The government is not allowed to select more candidates than the advertised posts for future appointments from the waiting list due to the guarantee of equality of opportunity.
This applies even to ad hoc promotions from the waiting list.
If ad hoc promotions/appointments are given through "back door" entry, they are allowed to continue until regularised.
If vacancies were available but not advertised, such posts cannot be filled with candidates on the waiting list.
Waiting list cannot be used for appointment to a vacancy unless it was advertised and a selection held.
It does not provide a source of recruitment, it only acts as a contingency for filling unoccupied positions.
Appointments in excess of advertised vacancies are invalid.
[Paras 8, 9, 11, 14, 16, 19 and 20]
