Constitution of India, Article 226 - Departmental Proceedings - Non- supply of copy of documents - Delinquent employees are not entitled to each and every document they request.
It is the duty of the Enquiry Officer/disciplinary authority to only supply relevant documents.
The employee must demonstrate that the non-supply of a specific document had a detrimental impact on their case.
[Para 11]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Departmental Proceedings - Judicial Review - The correctness of charges cannot be reviewed judicially.
The court has no authority to assess the sufficiency of evidence in the context of departmental proceedings when exercising its jurisdiction under Article 226.
[Para 15]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary Proceedings - It is not a criminal trial - The standard of proof required is a preponderance of probability, not proof beyond a reasonable doubt as required in a criminal trial.
