The fixation of seniority between direct recruits and promotees, interim relief, and the post of Assistant Engineers are addressed in this text.
Respondents were appointed according to the directions of the High Court, while appellant promotees were regularized from 11285, and respondents direct recruits were regularized from 11287.
The senior position assigned to respondents in the seniority list was challenged by appellants.
The stay on promotion of respondents to a higher post was vacated by the learned Single Judge, subject to the outcome of the writ petition, with the interest of appellants fully protected.
The order of the learned Single Judge was found to be legal, and there was no illegality evident.
