Andhra Pradesh Education Act, 1982, Sections 20, 21, 99 - Andhra Pradesh Unaided Private Medical and Dental Colleges (Establishment, Management and Admission) Rules, 1992, Rule 4 - Establishment of Colleges - The competent authority is essential for the proper execution of the Act and rules relating to the establishment of educational institutions.
No institution can be established without the formation of this authority, which is responsible for granting recognition to institutions after ensuring adequate financial provisions for their continued maintenance and efficient operation.
The government cannot use its rule-making power to replace or undermine the authority's powers and functions, as this would be a violation of the Act and the rules.
The 1992 rules contain several irregularities and illegalities, including the lack of guidelines for selecting and eliminating applications and the Expert Committee's autonomous establishment of criteria for this purpose.
The Committee's proceedings are not on file.
Until the competent authority is established under Section 2(12) of the Act, no private educational institution may be established.
The appeal is dismissed.
