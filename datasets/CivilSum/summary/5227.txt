In "Municipal Employees Union (Regd.)
Sirhind & others v. State of Punjab and others" Civil Appeal No. 8434 of 1997 decided on 1532002 Supreme Court ruled that employees or workmen who work on Saturdays & Sundays have the entitlement of monetary benefits for the services rendered by them on those days, thus their claim cannot be denied.
As the matter has already been resolved, it would be inappropriate to force the petitioners to go through the procedural Law, and the competent authority should pass an appropriate order in accordance with the judgment.
The workers can use the provisions of section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act since the right to monetary claim has already been established.
