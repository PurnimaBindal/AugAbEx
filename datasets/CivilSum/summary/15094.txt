Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - JBT Teacher Post - An ex-serviceman petitioner was denied appointment due to the Teachers Training Course from Bihar Vidayala Pariksha Samiti, Patna not being recognized by the Haryana Government Education Department - The course has since been recognized even by UGC and the matter is no longer res integra - Respondent ordered to issue the petitioner an appointment letter within two months.
