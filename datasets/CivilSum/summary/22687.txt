Maharashtra Workmen's Minimum House Rent Allowance Act, 1983, Sections 2(i), 4 and 13 - Power to exempt in special cases - The High Court reached the conclusion that the Act requires payment of house-rent allowance to all workmen, without any differentiation between classes or categories of workmen - The statutory minimum rate of house rent cannot be reduced for any class of workmen - A notification reducing the rates of house-rent allowance below the statutory minimum by putting a ceiling depending upon geographical areas in which factories or establishments are situated is beyond the power provided in Section 13 - The Supreme Court agreed with the High Court that the Notification dated 9.10.1992 is ultra vires of the Act, void and unenforceable, and it was properly quashed.
