Hyderabad City Police Act, Section 7(2) empowers dismissal of Hyderabad City police constable, and this provision holds the field according to the provisions of Andhra Pradesh Police Subordinate Service Rules, Rule 1 and Andhra Pradesh Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules 1963, Rule 1, as well as the Constitution of India, Article 311.
State Acts do not supersede the City Act.
