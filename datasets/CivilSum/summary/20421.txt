As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, and Memorandums dated 14121983 and 2041987, civilian employees of the Central Government with 'All India Transfer Liability' will be eligible for 'Special duty allowance' upon posting to the North Eastern Region.
However, this allowance is not solely based on the appointment order's clause relating to 'Ail India Transfer Liability', thus the principle of equal pay for equal work does not apply.
