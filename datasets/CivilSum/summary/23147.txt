Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311(2) - Food Corporation of India Staff Regulations, Regulation 58(8) - Departmental proceedings - Natural Justice - Assistance of legal practitioner - The Regulations prohibit assistance of legal practitioner to the delinquent - Only Govt. employee allowed to assist the delinquent - High Court directed the authority to allow assistance of a retired employee, who is not a legal practitioner and prohibited from appearing to assist the delinquent - Allowing the retired employee to assist results in regular practice, which is impermissible - High Court erred in directing the same.
