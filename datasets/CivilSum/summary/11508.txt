Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 21 - Compassionate appointment - Waiting list of applicants - Criteria and goals of policy for compassionate appointments - The policy aims to assist the dependents of a deceased member of service in overcoming the immediate economic hardship they may face - It cannot be claimed as a matter of right - The policy should consider time constraints, economic circumstances of the family, and the earning members to determine eligibility - If the policy does not account for these factors and objectives, creating a waiting list cannot be justified.
