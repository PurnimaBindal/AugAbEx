Constitution of India, Article 226 - Travel Regulations, Rules 263 and 268 - Cash Allowance - Petitioner, a Commanding Officer in the Air Force, was posted on operational duties at Palaly Air Field near Jaffna in Sri Lanka and was not provided with any accommodation or facilities.
Despite being in a war-like zone, the petitioner was not granted a cash allowance, unlike other Air Force officers who went to Colombo for administrative reasons.
The non-payment of a similar allowance is considered arbitrary and discriminatory.
The respondents are directed to make payments to the petitioner in accordance with Annexure 'H'.
