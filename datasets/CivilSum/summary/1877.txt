As per Section 192 of the Army Act, 1950 and Regulation 213 of the Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, a special family pension is provided in the case of a decease army personnel who died in an accident while on authorized leave.
Rule 6(d) also considers accidents that occur when a person is not strictly on duty as attributable to their service.
While the petitioner is not entitled to an ordinary pension since it is not admissible to parents and the deceased had not completed the minimum qualifying service of 15 years, the death of their son cannot be considered as unrelated or unconnected to military service.
Therefore, as per Regulation 213 of the Pension Regulations, the petitioner is entitled to a special family pension.
