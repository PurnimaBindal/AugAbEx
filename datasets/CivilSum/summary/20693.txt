Constitution of India, Article 16 - Calculation - Admission - Qualification - The appellant passed the 'Maha Vidushi' examination conducted by Mahila Gram Vidaypith in 1985 - Petitioner was admitted to the first year of a three-year degree course and subsequently failed - She discontinued her studies - In 1993, she applied to the Board of Secondary Education for permission to appear in the twelfth standard examination - The examination form was rejected - It was held that the Maha Vidushi qualification was derecognised by Rajasthan University on 21.7.86 - Therefore, in 1993, the appellant was not eligible for admission to the twelfth standard examination.
