The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 and West Bengal Secondary Education Act, 1950's Section 37 require prior sanction of posts.
However, the authorities have allowed the petitioner to work without remuneration for years without creating the additional post of a teacher in the junior school.
The refusal to sanction additional posts of teachers because new sections were introduced without prior permission is invalid per the Order of Refusal.
Therefore, the authorities must sanction additional posts and permit the petitioner to be considered even if they have become too old.
