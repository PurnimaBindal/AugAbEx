The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 - Recognition of Diploma Certificate/Appointment on compassionate grounds - The petitioner received a Diploma Certificate in Fine Arts and Crafts Teachers Training Examination from Haryana State, which was recognized in Punjab at that time, and claimed for compassionate appointment.
The State of Punjab decided to de-recognize the Diploma certificate obtained from the State of Haryana, but such de-recognition cannot apply with retrospective effect.
The de-recognition will apply prospectively, and the appointment on compassionate grounds cannot be denied for that reason.
The decision in CWP 14364/1990 Harpal Singh v. State of Punjab, DB, is deemed incorrect, and 1987(4) SLR 119(SC) is followed.
