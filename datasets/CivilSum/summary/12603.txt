Article 311 of the Constitution of India pertains to confidential reports and premature retirement.
The petitioner earned four very good reports, one good report, and five 'average' reports in the last ten years and cannot be considered 'dead-wood.'
Furthermore, his service record did not justify retirement as per the law stated in 1990(1) SLR 1.
Consequently, the impugned order is nullified.

Articles 162 and 311 of the Constitution of India relate to premature retirement.
The government instructed the premature retirement order but failed to provide adequate guidance resulting in multiple litigations.
Moreover, there is no reasonable explanation for the government's actions.
It is therefore suggested to the state government to issue appropriate instructions in such instances.
