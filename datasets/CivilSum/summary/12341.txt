According to Article 227 of the Constitution of India and Section 14 of the Administrative Tribunal Act of 1985, a Mazdoor who holds a civil post in connection with defence services but is not a member of the Armed Forces and is dealing with a service matter can only bring their case before the Central Administrative Tribunal.
The Trial Court ruled that the plaintiff's case should be returned to them to present before the Central Administrative Tribunal with territorial jurisdiction to hear the case.
