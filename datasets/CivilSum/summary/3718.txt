According to AICTE Regulations, 2003, private un-aided colleges have the right to make admissions to Diploma Course in Pharmacy against management seats.
However, even against the management seats, admissions cannot be made ignoring the merit and in violation of admission policy.
Such actions are not legal and valid, and colleges are liable to penalty and pay damage to the students.
Admissions made prior to the Supreme Court judgment also cannot be regularised on that basis.
[Paras 17, 19, 21, 22]
