Constitution of India, Article 311 - Karnataka Private Educational Institutions (Discipline and Control) Rules, Rule 12 - Disciplinary proceedings - Suspension - In case of a defect in disciplinary proceedings leading to a void punishment order and subsequent fresh inquiry ordered by the Authority or Court, the proceedings will start again from the stage where the defect occurred.
Moreover, the delinquent will be considered under suspension from the date the punishment order was given.
[Para 5]
