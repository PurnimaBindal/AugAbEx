Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rules of Management of Recognized Non-Government Institution (Aided and Unaided) Rules, 1969, Rule 28(8) - Initiation of disciplinary proceedings - Managing Committee's proposal for the first stage of departmental proceedings does not require personal hearing of the delinquent employee and/or Managing Committee - Approval at the first stage is based solely on papers submitted by the school - There is no opportunity for the committee to hear from the delinquent employee, Headmistress, Managing Committee, or Administrator of the school during proposal consideration.
