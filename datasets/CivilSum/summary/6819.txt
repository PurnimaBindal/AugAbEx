Punjab Civil Service (Judicial Branch) Rules, 1951 Rule 7 - Selection - Criteria of selection - Public Service Commission must follow the same procedure and criteria of selection for all types of posts, and must round off the fraction of marks - Commission used the rounding off formula for friction of marks, and treated 495% marks as 50% and 325% as 33% - No special classification made for selection in Judicial Branch - Not adopting the same criteria in the selection for Judicial Branch is discriminatory - Commission instructed to treat the friction of marks 325% as 33% and declared the petitioner qualified in paper IV (English) and to call her for an interview if found to have obtained over 50% marks in aggregate of five written papers.
[Paras 5 and 6]  B.
Only the petitioner questioned the selection criteria adopted by the Public Service Commission, and those who did not challenge it cannot be granted the same benefit extended to the petitioner by the Court, even if entitled to it - AIR 1987 SC 169 followed.
