Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 320 - West Bengal Service Commission Act, 1978, Section 9 - Empanelment - Candidates selected and placed on the panel for appointment to the post of college teacher have an inchoate right that becomes a vested right when their turn for recommendation becomes matured within the validity period of the panel.
The Commission has a fundamental duty to strictly adhere to the provisions of the Act and regulations, with a reasonable amount of transparency in its actions.
The empanelled candidates must be kept informed about their status and the position of the panel when required.
