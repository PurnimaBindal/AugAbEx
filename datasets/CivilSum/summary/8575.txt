Indian Army Rules, 1954 - Summary Court Martial - Havildar was appellant - Evidence showed tampering with answer sheets - Summary of evidence conducted - Punishment of six months imprisonment, reduction in rank, and dismissal from service was awarded - No evidence provided to challenge the correctness of this decision - Section 116 of the Act made it clear that the Commanding Officer alone constitutes the Court, which was followed in this case.
