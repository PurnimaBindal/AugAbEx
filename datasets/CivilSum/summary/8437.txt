Indian Medical Central Council Act, 1970, Section 14 and Schedule 2 - Medical Courses - Admissions - The Medical Council is responsible for setting standards of admission and necessary qualifications for the courses - The Council has complete control over the maintenance of standards for the Indian system of medicines - Degrees of different Universities in Medical Courses recognized in Schedule 2 are considered equivalent - Admission cannot be denied to students possessing such degrees due to lack of recognition by a specific University - The denial of admission to the petitioners was deemed unjustified and contrary to the law - Respondents are directed to provide admission to the petitioners by creating additional seats.
