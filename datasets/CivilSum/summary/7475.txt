Limitation Act, 1963, Article 58 - Dismissal from service - The limitation for challenging an order of dismissal is three years under Article 58.
However, the cause of action will arise when the statutory appeal is decided and a notice period of an additional 60 days under Section 80 CPC must also be allowed.
If the appellate authority fails to decide the statutory appeal, the cause of action will arise after six months from the date of preferring an appeal or representation.
Nevertheless, repeated unsuccessful representations will not provide a fresh lease of limitation or cause of action.

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion - Premature retirement - Even if there is a pending appeal or representation against adverse remarks, the authority may still take them into account for withholding promotion or issuing a pre-mature retirement order.
Additionally, un-communicated adverse remarks can also be the basis of decision.
However, employees have the right of consideration, not the right to promotion.
