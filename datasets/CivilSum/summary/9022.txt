Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 39 - Equal pay for equal work - Daily wage workers - FCI's discriminatory action of giving different wages to its workers, appointed through a labour contractor and those appointed directly, is condemned - All employees working in the godowns of the Corporation performing same duties - The Corporation must pay the contract labour equal wages as its permanent staff while performing the same duties adhering to the principle of equal pay for equal work.
