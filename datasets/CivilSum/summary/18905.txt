According to Rule 9 of the Punjab State Assistant Grade Examination Rules, 1984, if reversion is necessary, the principle of "last come first go" will apply, regardless of seniority in the rank of clerk.
This means that those who were promoted later, even if they were senior as clerks but qualified later, would have to be reverted.
In this case, the petitioner was promoted on 3011986 as an Assistant along with seven other clerks, but others who were senior as clerks but qualified later were promoted on 551986.
Due to reduction in posts, the petitioner was reverted.
