Allotment of Government Residence (General Pool in Delhi) Rules, 1963, Rule 317-B - Regularisation - Transfer of premises in the name of son which was earlier allotted to his father - Petitioner No. 1 was allotted Govt. accommodation Type-C - Despite being entitled to Type-C accommodation, petitioner No. 2 is only entitled to out of turn allotment of Type-B accommodation - Petitioner No. 2 has refused Type-B accommodation and remains in the premises allotted to his father - As a result, he is liable for damages for his overstay - The government does not possess the authority to waive charges for unauthorized occupation.
[Paras 18 and 19]
