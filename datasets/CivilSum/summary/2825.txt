Section 25-T and 2(ra) of the Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947) - Mala fide transfer is deemed an unfair labor practice that can be contested through an industrial dispute - The Industrial Court has the authority to enforce the provisions of Section 25-T of Act of 1947, which includes the power to grant stays.
[Para 9]

Allegations of mala fide transfer do not warrant a writ petition under Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India - The petitioner can seek recourse through the Industrial Disputes Act, which provides a specific mechanism for such disputes - The petitioner must provide evidence to substantiate their claims against the employer - The writ petition is not maintainable.
[Paras 12 and 14]
