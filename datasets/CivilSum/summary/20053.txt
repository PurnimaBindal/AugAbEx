The Municipalities Act, 1965, Section 74 states that any selections made before the implementation of GO No. 413 on March 10, 1992 would be subject to scrutiny by the Scrutiny Committee.
Despite the Commissioner pointing out the rule and issuing directions not to proceed with the finalisation of selection, the Committee headed by the Chairman went ahead with it.
The Tribunal's direction to implement the selection was not justified and the appeal was dismissed.
The Municipality may conduct a fresh selection based on qualifications and merit and make appointments as per the rules.
