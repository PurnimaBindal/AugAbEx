According to Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, Rule 49(2)(b) and Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 19, an individual with a ten-year service will only be qualified for pension if the rules of retirement are followed and not if they resign from service.
[Para 4] B Rule 19 of Border Security Force Rules does not automatically qualify a person for pension.
The eligibility for a pension will be measured according to Central Civil Service (Pension) Rules.
