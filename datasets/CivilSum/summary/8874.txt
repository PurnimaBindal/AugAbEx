According to the Bihar Non-Government Elementary Schools (Taking over Control and Management) Act, 1976, Section 7 and notification dated 1781988, appointments of Assistant Teachers in Govt.
Basic Schools must be approved by the Director Primary Education.
Without approval, appointments made in anticipation cannot be sustained, and employees are not entitled to payment of any salary for the period worked.
This was upheld in a dismissed petition based on Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 309.
