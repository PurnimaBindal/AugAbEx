Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 309 - Bihar Pension Rules, 1950, Rule 43(b) - Recovery of excess payment made to employee during service from retiral dues - No hearing or proceedings conducted while petitioner was in service - Rule 43(b) allows for recovery from pension and gratuity, but not resorted to by State Government or competent authority - Relying on Sahib Ram's case, excess amount cannot be recovered unless it was due to employee misrepresentation - Also, no order causing prejudice to employee can be passed without giving a hearing and Rule 43(b) proceedings cannot be initiated beyond the prescribed time limit.
