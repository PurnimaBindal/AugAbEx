As per Constitution of India, Article 226, a person who is unhappy with the promotion of a junior over them must approach the court within a year.
Delay in filing the petition will result in dismissal.
Moreover, filing a fresh representation after the rejection of earlier will not be considered as a fresh cause of action.
Interference at a belated stage will violate Articles 14 and 16 and unsettle settled matters.
[Paras 8 to 10].
B.
Under the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226, incorrect orders passed in one case will not result in another claim on the grounds of equality.
A writ cannot be issued under Article 226 to direct the authority to promote in the same wrong manner in another case.
[Paras 11 and 12].
