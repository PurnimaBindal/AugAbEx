The UP Public Services (Reservation for Physically Handicapped, Dependents of Freedom Fighters and Ex-Servicemen) Act, (4 of 1993) Section 3, pertains to appointment in Public Service and posts in connection with affairs of the State.
It does not apply to grant reservation in respect of admission in Management courses, therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to a 2% reservation quota under the Act to seek admission to MBA.
The petition is dismissed.
