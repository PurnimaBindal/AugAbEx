Retiral benefits - Petitioner seeks interest on delayed payment - Argues that benefit was delayed due to failure to produce No Due Certificate - Court holds that pension and gratuity are no longer a privilege, but a valuable property of the government - Any delay in settlement of such benefits must be penalized with interest at the market rate - Petitioner awarded an interest of 12% on their pension and commuted pension.
