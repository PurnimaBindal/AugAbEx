Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission - Examination Petitioner, employed as Lab.
Attendant in the Department of Electrical Engineering and a matriculate, had their application to appear in the ITI examination for the trade of Wireman/Electrician rejected because they did not possess the necessary qualifications and experience.
However, the petitioner argued that a private candidate seeking admission did not need qualifications specific to the trade and could have experience in an allied trade, and since the petitioner was employed in an allied trade of wiring work for computer/power electronics and control labs, they should be considered eligible.
The court allowed the petition.
