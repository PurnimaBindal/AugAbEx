Under Section 10(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1948, the Appropriate Government cannot refuse a reference on the basis that the domestic enquiry was conducted properly.
The Labour Court must determine and decide this matter.
The government has been instructed to refer the matter within a month, in line with the ruling in Veerarajan's case (AIR 1987 SC 695).
