The defendants, who are restaurant workers, were threatening to hold demonstrations and other illegal activities within 500 meters of the restaurant's boundary walls.
While the right to freedom of speech and expression is guaranteed under Art. 19 of the Constitution, it is subject to reasonable restrictions.
As such, the defendants cannot engage in activities that disrupt the restaurant's operations or block ingress or egress.
Allowing such behavior would violate the plaintiff's rights to conduct their business smoothly.
Accordingly, the defendants have been restrained from holding dharnas within 200 meters of the restaurant's boundaries and entrances, as well as the managing partner's residence.
