The Constitution of India in Articles 16 and 226 allow for Judicial Review of the Selection process for candidates during Interviews, but a Panel that has been finalized is not open to comparative assessment of merits for the purpose of Judicial Review.
The Court cannot substitute its own findings in place of the assessment made by the selection committee, and there is no obligation to select a petitioner even if they are an outstanding candidate.
The Court held that there was no merit to the petitioner's claim and dismissed the petition.
