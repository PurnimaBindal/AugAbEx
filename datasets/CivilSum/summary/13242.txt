Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Promotion in reservation - Roster point promotion - The Supreme Court has determined that roster-point promotees of reserved categories cannot count their seniority in the promoted category from the date of officiation in the promoted post vis-a-vis general candidates who were senior in the lower cadre but promoted later.
Rosters for reservation under Article 16(4) or 16(4-A) are only intended to provide adequate representation at the promotion level and do not determine seniority.
Articles 16(4) and 16(4-A) do not confer any fundamental right and are enabling provisions, and rights of reserved candidates must be balanced with the rights of equal opportunity under Article 16(1) and 14.
The decision operates retrospectively as the rule in promotional controversies, and further imbalances can be alleviated by applying the ad-hoc principle.
[Para 4] B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Promotion in reservation - Roster point promotion - The "catch up" rule would enable a general candidate to regain his seniority in the higher cadre to which he is promoted later than a roster point reserved category promotee against reservation.
If an SC/ST candidate gets promotion in general merit or seniority, his senior in the lower level will not regain the same seniority by "Catch up" rule over him getting promotion later in general.
Such an SC/BC candidate will remain senior in the higher level.
Thereafter, merits and excellence in performance would prevail and be encouraged.
