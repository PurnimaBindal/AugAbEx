Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(s) - Workman - Supervisory duties - To determine whether an industry employee is a workman or not, both job nature and job performed are relevant, not just designation - Supervision involves direction and control - The main duties performed must be considered - The presence of employees under his supervision must be proved - Being in charge of a small quality control section is not sufficient.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-B - Quantum of punishment - The Labour Court has limited jurisdiction over the punishment's quantum - It can only interfere if there are sufficient reasons.
