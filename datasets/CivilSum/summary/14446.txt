Constitution of India, Article 311 - Departmental enquiry/Preliminary enquiry - The appointing authority is not the only entity permitted to initiate disciplinary proceedings - If no rule mandates it, a preliminary enquiry before a regular departmental enquiry is not required.
[Para 4]
Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Speaking order - The appointing or appellate authority must provide a reasoned and speaking order when issuing punishment - Failing to do so makes the order illegal.
