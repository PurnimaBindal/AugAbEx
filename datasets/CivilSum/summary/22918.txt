Under Section 40 of the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, employers are responsible for paying contributions even after closing a business establishment, if they failed to deposit contributions for a period of about three years prior to closure.
The argument that notice of demand was only sent after closure of the establishment is not a valid defense.
