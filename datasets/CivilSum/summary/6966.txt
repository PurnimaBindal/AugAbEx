The CAT ruled that the Industrial Disputes Act had not been fully utilized and dismissed the petitioner's challenge against a termination order.
The Supreme Court also dismissed their appeal.
Eventually, the petitioner raised the Industrial Dispute with the Industrial Tribunal, but it was also dismissed due to latches and delay.
The Labour Court was wrong in dismissing the dispute raised by the petitioner, and the matter is sent back to the court for a decision on merits.
The parties have been asked to appear before the Labour Court.
Consequently, the petition has been granted.
