Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Sections 8(3), 12, 15 Chapters IV and V - Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, Sections 2(4), 5, 8(1) - Montfort School Staff Rules, Rules 24, 31 - Assistant teacher - Order of dismissal - Appeal before Delhi School Tribunal - Sustainbility - Argument that if there are multiple remedies, a party cannot be compelled to go to a particular forum - Held, this argument is baseless as principle of `Dominus litus' will apply, meaning one remedy does not override the other - Additionally, requiring the tribunal to refer the matter to an arbitrator could render provisions of Section 8(3) of 1973 Act useless.
