According to the Constitution of India, Articles 226/227, an employee is entitled to their monthly pay and has to be given a chance to present their case if that entitlement is to be denied.
The petitioner in this case wasn't given the opportunity to be heard, so the order was quashed.
[Para 7]

The Department had earlier promised to pay the petitioner their arrears of salary, so it isn't right for them to deny it now and without any notice or hearing.
The Department has to pay the petitioner's monthly emoluments for the relevant period, with interest at the rate of 12% per annum.
[Paras 8 and 9]
