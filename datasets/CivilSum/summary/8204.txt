The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, 226, and 320, addresses regularisation of ad hoc appointees to the post of Lecturer.
Holding an ad hoc appointment for an extended period does not automatically convert it to a regular appointment, especially where the post is under the purview of the Public Service Commission.
Therefore, there is no requirement for the regularisation of those who have held ad hoc positions for many years.
However, the State must obtain prior permission from the court before making any appointment, whether on an ad hoc, temporary, or fixed-term basis.
