The Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 21 and UP Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Rules, 1975, Rule 25(2)(v)(a) discuss payment of wages and the term of Licence granted to contractors.
Violating any terms of the contract would result in consequences against the contractor alone, and the principal employer cannot be held liable.
The obligation of the principal employer is limited to wages mentioned in Section 21 and cannot entertain disputed claims between the contractor and its workmen.
