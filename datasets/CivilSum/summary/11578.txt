Constitution of India, Article 14 and 16 - Test for Regularisation - Appellant was appointed as a Stenographer for a limited time, and failed the test for regularisation - She continued working on an adhoc basis for 11 years due to interim orders - It would be unjust to terminate her at this point, as she has no other income and is not eligible for other posts - The respondent is directed to conduct the regularisation test again, and if she passes she will be regularised, otherwise the respondent can pass any order.
