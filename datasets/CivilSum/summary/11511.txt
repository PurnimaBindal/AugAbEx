According to Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 173 and Entitlement Rules, 1982, Rules 14, 17, 27, disability pension cannot be denied without proper reasons.
A medical board cannot hold that the decease was caused due to the stress of military service without proper justification.
The competent authority can only modify the recommendations of the Medical Board for reasons that must be recorded in writing.
If no reasons are given, the order cannot sustain.
Therefore, it is directed to determine disability pension.
