Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference - Demand notice - Delay and Laches - Time limitation not specified under Act - If plea of delay not raised, delay not considered - If raised, court must consider - However, stale disputes not revived after long time lapse if plea raised - Relief not denied solely due to delay - Back-wages may be restricted only from date of demand notice and reduced from full back wages, as per court discretion.
