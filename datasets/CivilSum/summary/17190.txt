According to the Constitution of India, Articles 226/227, the post of PGT (History) was up for promotion, and the petitioner was the only teacher available for consideration.
Despite this, the DPC did not consider the case of the petitioner and postponed the filling of the vacancy for the respondent No4 to acquire qualification in History.
When the DPC was held again, the respondent No4 was promoted instead of the petitioner due to seniority.
The court found that the DPC did not provide an explanation as to why the petitioner's case was not considered initially and allowed the writ, declaring the petitioner as appointed as PGT (History), and the appointment of the 4th respondent could not be sustained.
