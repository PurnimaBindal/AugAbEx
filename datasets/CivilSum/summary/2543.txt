Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 1 Rules 3 & 5 - Necessary parties - Notification - Notification amending Rajasthan Education Subordinate Service Rules, 1971 - Writ petition challenges notification on the grounds that all existing posts of Vice Principals or its equivalent will be made available to Head Masters of Secondary Schools if it stands - Association of Head Masters of Secondary Schools not initially impleaded as party, but applies to be impleaded as all its members will be adversely affected if the notification is quashed - Association allowed to be impleaded as party to the petition - Relevant case law discussed.
