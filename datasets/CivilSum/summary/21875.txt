In accordance with Section 47 of the Civil Procedure Code and Order 21, Rule 17, an employee's termination of services was deemed void ab-initio by the Civil Court.
As a result, the court passed a decree with continuity of service.
However, in the execution of the decree, the Executing Court was not authorized to grant wages as there was no decree for monetary benefits.
The case of 1996(8) SCC 37 was referred to.
