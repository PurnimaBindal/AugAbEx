The termination of certain workmen was based on their unauthorized use of a company conference room after office hours, without permission from the management, resulting in a serious view from the employer.
The workmen refused to answer an Enquiry Officer's questions regarding the incident, causing the management to take adverse action and lose confidence in these employees.
A labor court rejected the workmen's grievances so there will be no interference with the dismissed petition.
This case involves the Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226, as well as the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1)(C).
