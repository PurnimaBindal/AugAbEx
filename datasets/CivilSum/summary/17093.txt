The petitioner requested an extension of service based on Articles 226 and 16 of the Indian Constitution and Section 22 of the Air Force Act.
However, extension of service in the Air Force is not guaranteed and is at the discretion of the respondent.
Only those who pass all parts of their promotion exam are eligible for an extension.
Since the petitioner did not pass the exam, the refusal to extend their service did not have to comply with natural justice principles.
The policy letter applied to the petitioner, and the refusal to grant an extension was legal and justified.
