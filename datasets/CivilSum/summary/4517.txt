According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, a university has the right to set eligibility criteria for admission/entrance tests.
As long as these conditions are not arbitrary or unreasonable, they cannot be challenged.
In fact, setting eligibility criteria is an additional way to ensure the best candidates are chosen for the program.
Therefore, classifying successful candidates based on these criteria is rational and reasonable and helps the university to select the most qualified individuals.
As a result, courts should not easily interfere with the standards set by universities.
