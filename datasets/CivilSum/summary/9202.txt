Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25F - Retrenchment - Notification of employee's cessation of service by management due to unauthorised absence beyond seven days - Such action constitutes retrenchment - Failure to comply with procedures laid out in Section 25F of the Act renders the action null.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Labour Court cannot use Section 11-A of the Act to award relief while overturning disciplinary authority's punishment order - Section 11-A applies only in cases where the guilt and findings are upheld and the appropriate punishment is to be determined.
