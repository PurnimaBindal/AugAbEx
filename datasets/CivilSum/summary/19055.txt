Constitution of India, Article 16 - Haryana Education Directorate (State Service Class III) Rules, 1974, Rules 6(e), 7 and 9(d) - Promotion of Clerks - Rules amended to impose condition of passing type test - However, petitioners were promoted as Clerks prior to amendment - Therefore, amended Rules cannot be applied retroactively to them - Imposing type test condition would be contrary to Rules - Petitioners cannot be demoted for failing to pass type test.
