Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Compensation - Job alternative - Disablement - Drivers in the Haryana Roadways with defect in their eye sight who were declared medically unfit for driving heavy vehicles retired from service - Claim for dependent to be provided with suitable employment in the department - Earlier judgments stating that drivers should be given alternative job or compensation according to their length of service - High Court not justified in directing employment of dependent commensurate with qualifications - Appeal allowed - Authorities directed to give alternative jobs or compensation to respondents in the terms of Anand Bihari v. Rajasthan State Road Transport Corporation, 1991(2) SCT 44 (SC).
