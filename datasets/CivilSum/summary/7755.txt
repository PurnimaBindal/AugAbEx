The Border Security Force Act Sections 20(a), 26, 117, Border Security Force Rules Rules 43, 45, 47, and Constitution of India Article 226 address Dismissal for Assault on superior officer.
The Trial is held by Summary Security Force Court.
It is contended that a person charged with an offense under Section 20(a) of the Act cannot be dealt summarily.
However, the rule imposes no prohibition on trial and dismissal on conviction by Summary Security Force Court.
Therefore, the procedure followed is not flawed.
