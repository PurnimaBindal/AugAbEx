Constitution of India, Article 16 - Canteen employees - Regularisation of services - Employees of canteen constituted by State Govt. for its Secretariat Department from Govt. funds would be considered as State Govt. employees and eligible for regularisation - The Managing Committee of canteen consisting of Govt. officials is an arm of the State, and the canteen cannot be considered an independent unit simply because it is run by a committee appointed by the Govt. [Paras 11 to 18]
