Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 16(1)(b) - Infancy Protection - Whether an establishment qualifies for infancy Protection is determined by whether it is a new business or a continuation of an old one, whether there is any evasion of liability, or if the Act's provisions are being circumvented to claim such protection.
If a new business is established, protection cannot be denied.
