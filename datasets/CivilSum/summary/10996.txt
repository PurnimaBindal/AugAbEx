Guru Nanak Dev University recognizes the one sitting BA Degree but did not recognize the regular BA Degree of Panjab University granted under Regulation 22 of the Panjab University Calendar, which resulted in refusal of admission to Bachelor of Journalism and Mass Communication Course.
It has been held that the Court cannot go into the question of equivalence of degree or its recognition by the Universities as it is within the exclusive domain of the University.
However, the petitioner was not at fault at the time of seeking admission, and the Supreme Court directed the University to consider all possible ways to adjust him and not to cancel his admission.
In that perspective, the University has been directed to reconsider his case again.
