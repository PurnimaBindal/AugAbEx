Rajasthan Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1958, Section 28-A - Maintainability of complaint - Eligibility for - The employer cannot raise a new plea of break in service at the appellate stage, as the finding of fact recorded by the Prescribed Authority on the pleadings and evidence of the parties, as well as upheld by the Single Judge, that the employees were in continuous service for more than six months, cannot be interfered with at this point.
