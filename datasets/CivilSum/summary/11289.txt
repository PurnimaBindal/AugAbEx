The petitioner, who has been working as a Chowkidar in Vigilance organisation for the past 15 years, has claimed promotion as Constable Vigilance.
Despite earning commendation and cash rewards, there are mixed opinions about the promotion within the officers.
However, the respondents have been instructed to consider and make a final decision regarding the conversion of the post of Chowkidar to Constable.
