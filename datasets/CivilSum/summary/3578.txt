Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F - Termination of services - The petitioner was appointed as a waiter only for a specific period when a team of specialists from USSR were staying in the mess.
The appointment was purely temporary and continued for some time after the period indicated in the offer of appointment due to the prolonged stay of the USSR team.
However, this did not change the nature of the appointment, which was still for a specific project.
The petitioner's appointment came to an end after the USSR team left, and the termination of the petitioner from service is covered under Section 2(oo)(bb).
Therefore, the petitioner does not have the right to be regularized.
