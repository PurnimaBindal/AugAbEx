Constitution of India, Articles 224-A and 226 - Judiciary - A former High Court Judge appointed as President of the Industrial Tribunal - The State Government did not offer re-employment in accordance with Notification - The retired Judge filed a writ petition which was granted by the High Court - Upon reconsideration, the Judge withdrew the petition in accordance with the dignity of office - The judiciary must guarantee that future insensitivity by any government in offering retired superior judges employment does not cause further embarrassment to others.
