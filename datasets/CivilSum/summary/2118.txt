Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Education/Examination - Cancellation of examination - Natural justice - When taking action against malpractices during an examination, courts are hesitant to intervene unless there is a complete absense of principles of natural justice.
Disciplinary proceedings and cancellation of examinations for malpractice are considered quasi-judicial proceedings and hence, principles of natural justice must be followed.
If an examination is cancelled without warning, it is illegal and violates the principles of natural justice, in such a situation, the court can intervene.
[Paras 5 to 8]
