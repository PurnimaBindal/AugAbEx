Constitution of India, Article 226 - Previous service - Benefit of - Petitioner, who joined the Indian Army as Emergency Commissioned Officer (ECO) in 1964 and was released in 1968, was granted Special Short Commission in 1971 with the benefit of previous four years of service.
However, while joining the CRPF in 1973, he was wrongly extended the benefit of his entire army service, leading to a proposal to not count his first spell of service from 1964 to 1968.
He was found entitled to only his second spell of service from 1971 to 1973 as an SSCO, making the order ignoring his first spell of service in accordance with the law.
