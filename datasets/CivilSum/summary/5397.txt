Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 11-A - Back wages - The Labour Court must provide cogent reasons for denial of back wages if termination is deemed illegal and reinstatement is ordered.
The normal rule is for full back wages to be granted and the objecting party must justify the circumstances requiring departure from this rule.
Since no reason was recorded justifying denial of back wages in this case, the order of the Labour Court is non-sustainable and the workman is entitled to back wages.
