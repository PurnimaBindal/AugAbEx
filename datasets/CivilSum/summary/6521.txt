Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 16.38 and 16.2(1) - Termination of Services - Appellant involved in a quarrel with a co-constable while under the influence of alcohol - Medical examination not conducted to test for intoxication - Doctor gave no opinion on intoxication - Both constables had injuries - Cause of quarrel unknown - Appellant argued for fair treatment and consideration of all evidence before making a decision - Punishment deemed unfair and was overturned.
