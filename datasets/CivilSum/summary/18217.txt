As per the Constitution of India and Railway Act, and as stated in the Railway Servants (Pass) Rules of 1986, a Financial Advisor or Chief Accounts Officer who is promoted to the grade of Financial Commissioner is entitled to a platinum pass.
However, according to a ruling in Sections 12 and 13, a petitioner who was upgraded to the grade without the conferred rank and status of a Financial Commissioner is not entitled to the same privileges and perks available to Members of Railway Board and Financial Commissioner, such as a gold pass which is considered a duty pass.
