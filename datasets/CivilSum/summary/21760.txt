Andhra Pradesh State and Subordinate Service Rules, Rule 10(a)(i)(1) - Rule 23(a), 33, Rule 3 - Andhra Pradesh Panchayat Raj Engineering Service - Seniority - Temporary service cannot be counted for determining seniority under Rule 33(a) - Temporary appointees cannot be ranked higher than those who passed the merit test and secured ranking - Appointments of temporary service appellants were made due to administrative exigencies - They cannot be compared to those appointed during the period of recruitment ban - The appeal was dismissed.
