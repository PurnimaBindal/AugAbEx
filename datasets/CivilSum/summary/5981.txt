The instructions in Financial Commissioner's Standing Order No. 28 regarding reference applications under Section 18 of the Land Acquisition Act are binding and mandatory, and strict compliance is necessary.
[Para]
A written application is necessary for a reference for enhancement under Section 18 of the Land Acquisition Act, and statutory procedural requirements must be fulfilled.
Without fulfilling these requirements, no right can be derived.
[Para]
Procedural wrangles cannot erode or set aside the valuable substantive right to enhancement of compensation under Section 18 of the Land Acquisition Act.
[Para]
To have the right for enhancement of compensation, it is necessary to hold that a written application for reference was duly filed within the period of limitation under Section 18 of the Land Acquisition Act.
Without fulfilling the statutory procedural requirements, no right for enhancement can arise.
