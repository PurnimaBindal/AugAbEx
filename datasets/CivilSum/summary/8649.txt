The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 states that overstayal of leave is considered a misconduct subject to a regular enquiry.
However, each case must be examined on its own facts.
If a workman does not acknowledge communication from management regarding an enquiry at the given address, an enquiry in his absence is impossible.
A Labour Court found evidence that a workman was deliberately absenting himself unlawfully, and his defense was proven false.
The findings are not vitiated, and the termination was justified.

The Petitioner was charged with wilful absence and persisted in telling a lie in his defence.
Others admitted the truth, leading to the termination of petitioner's services and stoppage of increments to others.
This cannot be considered discriminatory as there is a difference between a person who admits the truth and one that persists in lying.
