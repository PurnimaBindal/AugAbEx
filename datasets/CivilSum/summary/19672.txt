According to the Haryana Government circular dated July 20,1981 Sub paras (i) to (v), all individuals who have undergone or whose spouses have undergone sterilisation operations contribute to the objective of the state and must be treated as one class.
As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, there is no rational basis for classification.
Therefore, all those who meet the requirements outlined in sub-paras (i) to (v) of the circular are eligible and entitled to receive special Increment Incentive.
