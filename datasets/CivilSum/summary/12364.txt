Constitution of India, Articles 14, 311 and 226 - Termination of services - Natural Justice - The petitioner was appointed as Swasthya Sahayak for spraying DDT under the Rural Malaria Scheme - However, his services were terminated based on a representation made by another Swasthya Sahayak who claimed to be senior to the petitioner under Rural Malaria Scheme - But the petitioner was not informed about such representation - Being given no reasonable opportunity, the impugned order is clearly violative of principles of natural justice and Article 14 of the Constitution - Consequently, the order is quashed.
[Paras 9, 10 & 11]
