Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Civil) Regulations, 1965, Regulations 7-A and 9 - Eligibility for higher pay scale - Petitioner was appointed as Sub Divisional Officer through promotion - Promoted employees were considered as directly recruited officers - Petitioner previously received higher pay scale after 9 years of service, but was later demoted to lower pay scale after completing 16 years of service - Punjab State Electricity Board issued a circular stating that employees promoted against promotional quota are deemed to have been appointed as Assistant Engineers like direct recruits for higher pay scale - Petitioner deemed eligible for next higher scale.
