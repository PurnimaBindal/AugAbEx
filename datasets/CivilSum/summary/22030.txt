Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(s) - Ad hoc appointment - Termination - Inducting Respondent No. 2 as a Doctor in the General Duty Medical Officer Grade-II temporarily for a period of six months - During his shift, respondent No. 2 was the sole person in charge of the first aid post while having subordinates taking orders from him - Since he could not be considered a workman under Section 2(s) of the Act due to his additional supervisory duties, the termination cannot be deemed illegal for lack of one month's notice under Section 25-F of the Act - The judgement of the Labour Court is set aside.
