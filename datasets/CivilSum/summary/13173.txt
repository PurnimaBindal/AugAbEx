According to Kerala Service Rules, Part II Rule 5(a), individuals who have been dismissed or removed from service due to misconduct are still eligible to receive compassionate allowance.
However, if an employee has obtained wealth through bribes, their claim for compassionate allowance may be rejected on that basis alone.
It is possible for an employee to have been dismissed for a single act of misconduct, and it should be noted that punishment to an employee also affects their family members.
