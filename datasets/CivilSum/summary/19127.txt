Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 33 and 33-A - Protected workman - Suspension - The employer cannot prevent a protected workman from attending the office of the Union within the company premises - If such action is taken, it will be prejudicial to the conditions of the protected workman - The orders passed by the Tribunal restraining the petitioner from obstructing respondent's access to the office premises of the factory are not against the provisions of Sections 33 and 33-A.
