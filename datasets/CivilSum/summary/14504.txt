For Respondent Nos. 4, 7, 8, 10, 11, 14 to 16 and 19 :- Mr. Surya Kant, Advocate.
A.
In regards to the Selection criteria of Assistant Linemen and Shift Attendants throughout the State of Haryana, the different selection committees adopted non-uniform standards and criteria.
This resulted in a lack of uniformity throughout the state, as some candidates did not receive marks for extra-curricular activities while others did, and different committees had differing standards for the qualifying examination.
These actions violated the clause of equality under Article 14 of the Constitution, making the selection vitiated.
[Paras 15, 16, 17, 19 and 20] B.
The Selection committee for the Assistant Linemen and Shift Attendants exercise discretion for allotment of 60% marks on unguided criteria, which is unfair on the selection process as a whole.
[Para 18] C. The Selection committee interviewed candidates giving only one or two minutes each.
As the criterion for interview was totally vague and unclear, this factor played a significant role in making the selection process unfair.
[Para 18] D. Selecting candidates in excess of the advertised posts is illegal, and therefore invalid.
[Para 22]  E. Apprentices cannot be outrightly ignored while filling up the posts, as they have to be given some credit for their training and gained knowledge from their apprenticeship.
[Para 25] F. In regards to unsuccessful candidates, they are not totally estopped from challenging the legality of the selection.
If the selection process was based on an unguided and undisclosed criterion, the unsuccessful candidate still has the right to challenge the selection.
