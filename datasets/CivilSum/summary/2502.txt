Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F, 25-G - Back wages - The Labour Court ordered reinstatement of the petitioner with continuity of service but only 50% back wages, due to the petitioner's litigious perseverance and delay in raising the demand for back wages.
Despite the termination of his service being treated as illegal, as the petitioner had worked for less than 12 months, the respondents cannot be burdened with the liability to pay his salary for the period during which he had prosecuted an untenable remedy.
