Haryana Electrical Inspectorate (Group-A) Service Rules, 1997, Appendix B Column 3 - Degrees and Diplomas - Recognition of - Power of State Govt. to recognise - Amendment of Rules - Recognition of degrees and diplomas by State Govt. equals that of recognized universities - Executive instructions may supplement, yet not alter the Rule itself - Diploma in electrical engineering awarded by the State Board is equivalent to those recognized by universities and upheld by Haryana Govt. and promotions of senior Asstt.
Engineers Executive Engineers made on the basis of diploma awarded by the State Board also upheld - High Court judgments that held promotions of three Executive Engineers to be contrary to the rules set aside and that no vested rights were taken away.
[Paras 8 to 15]  B.
Rules - Instructions - No vested rights can be taken away by retrospective amendment of rules - However, clarifications can be made that do not affect vested rights and establish equivalence between diplomas awarded by the State Technical Education Department and those from universities - Both diploma holders and degree holders can be considered for promotion, especially when no university in the State grants the said diploma - This does not violate the Rule or the rights of any party.
