Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 1(2)(a) - Scope and applicability of the Act - The Act applies to all categories of Central Government servants and others, including those posted in the State of Jammu and Kashmir.
[Para 8]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 14 - Constitution of J&K, Section 103 - Jurisdiction of CAT - Autonomous bodies - Kendriya Vidyalaya - Registered Societies under the control of the Central Government - The Act covers employees of Kendriya Vidyalaya, an autonomous body registered under the Societies Registration Act and under the control of UOI- Their posting in the State of J&K is irrelevant - The High Court cannot decide service disputes of such employees - The writ petition must be transferred to the Tribunal.
