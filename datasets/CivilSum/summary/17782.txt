Under the relevant Rules of the Board, qualifying service of ten years is necessary for the grant of pension under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India.
Period spent on work-charged basis as a Driver must be counted towards qualifying service for the grant of retiral benefits.
If this period is counted, the petitioner would have more than 10 years of qualifying service, making him eligible for retiral benefits.
It was held that the petitioner is entitled to get his work-charged period counted for the grant of retiral benefits.
The court followed the ruling in Kesar Chand v. State of Punjab, 1988 P&H 265 (F.B.).
It is important to note that the work-charged period of an employee must be counted towards qualifying service for grant of retiral benefits.
