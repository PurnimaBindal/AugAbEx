Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - Frequent transfers of an employee result in financial hardship and inconvenience to both the employee and their family.
This also creates issues with the education of the employee's children.
However, if a person is employed in a business undertaking with varying tasks and periods of work, then they may need to be shifted accordingly.
As a result, the petition could be dismissed.
