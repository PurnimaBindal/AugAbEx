The Professional misconduct of the Advocates Act, 1961 is discussed wherein the appellant is accused of demanding a bribe of Rs. 2,000 from the complainant to obtain a favorable result from a particular Magistrate in regards to an application under Section 125 of Cr.PC However, the particular Magistrate was not the Presiding Officer of that Court, and therefore, obtaining an order from him was not possible, and there was no justification for the Disciplinary Committee to conclude that the appellant was guilty of misconduct.
As a result, the order made by the Disciplinary Committee was set aside.
