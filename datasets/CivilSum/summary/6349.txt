Constitution of India, Article 226 - Alternative remedy/Revision - The remedy of a revision petition is not considered an alternative remedy and does not bar the jurisdiction of the High Court to hear a writ petition.
The court has discretion whether or not to entertain a writ petition with an alternative remedy available and there is no constitutional restriction against entertaining a writ petition even if the petitioner did not avail of an alternative remedy.
[Para 3]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion/Supports incentive scheme - The petitioner is to receive promotion out of turn, from Head Constable to the post of Sub-Inspector and Inspector, and to be awarded incentive for their two gold medals at national level games.
Though the All India St John's Ambulance Competition may not be considered a national level game, the respondent's decision to not grant the petitioner promotion to the post of Sub-Inspector based on the same achievements as other beneficiaries of the incentive scheme is arbitrary and unreasonable.
The respondent is directed to grant the petitioner promotion with retrospective effect.
