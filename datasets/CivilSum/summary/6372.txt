Under Punjab Civil Services Punishment and Appeal Rules, 1970, Rules 8 & 10, stoppage of increments with cumulative effect is seen as a major penalty and can't be imposed until a regular inquiry is held and principles of natural justice are followed.
Therefore, where an employee is charge-sheeted for major penalty, and notice is issued for dismissal after regular inquiry, it's not considered to violate any Rule or natural justice to impose a penalty of stoppage of four increments with cumulative effect.
[Para 3] B.
According to Punjab Civil Services Punishment and Appeal Rules, 1970, Rules 15 and 10, if a plaintiff wants to challenge the order of punishing authority, they should file an appeal under Rule 15.
If the order of dismissal or appeal is placed on the record of the appeal but never challenged, the order of punishing authority becomes final due to the doctrine of merger.
Any challenge to the original order then remains of no consequence.
