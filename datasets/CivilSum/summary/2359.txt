The dismissal of a bus conductor on charges of not issuing tickets after collecting fares was deemed invalid by the Labour Court due to discrepancies in the reporting officer's testimony.
As a result, the employee was rightfully reinstated with continuity of service.
[Paras 8 and 9]
The Labour Court denied the claim for back wages based on the petitioner's counsel statement that it was not being pressed for.
However, the employee is still entitled to back wages and retiral benefits for the period between the date of the award and reinstatement.
[Paras 7, 8, 11 and 12]
