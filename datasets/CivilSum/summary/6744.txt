Constitution of India, Articles 12, 226, 217 and 311 - Indian Contract Act, 1872, Section 32 - Termination - Validity of - Service of Respondent terminated on ground that it was not required - Company argues that clause of appointment letter is not ultra vires of Constitution - Appellant argues that it does not fall under definition of authority under Article 12 - Clause 3 of appointment letter allows company to terminate the service of temporary employee by giving three months notice - However, such a clause is invalid under the Contract Act and opposed to public policy - The condition is not binding as the contract is between two unequal parties - Therefore, the company cannot act upon it.
