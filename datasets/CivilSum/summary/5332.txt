The Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14 and 16 - Promotion - Limitation on consideration and reconsideration for promotion - The Ministry of Defence's instruction to limit consideration to three times is permissible - If an officer has been assessed multiple times by the competent Selection Board according to the necessary procedures but has failed to impress based on his service records, a court cannot compel the competent authority to reconsider - Such a directive is not justifiable.
