Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 216 - Interviews - Selection process - The petitioner had registered their name with the employment exchange with improved qualifications before the interview, but the employment exchange failed to sponsor their name - The board did not show proper consideration in making their decision - Due to this, the petitioner was denied additional marks for their higher qualifications and their name appeared lower on the panel of selected candidates - This indicates an unfair and illegal selection process - The court has given directions to re-cast the panel after taking the petitioner's improved qualifications into account.
