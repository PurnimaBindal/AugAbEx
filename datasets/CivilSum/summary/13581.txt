Constitution of India, Article 311 - Civil Services Regulations, Regulation 351(a) - Retirement - Disciplinary proceedings after retirement - A show-cause notice issued in 1992 regarding events from 1979-80 is invalid as per Regulation 351(a), which states that departmental proceedings cannot be initiated for events that occurred more than four years prior to institution of proceedings.

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Civil Services Regulations, Regulation 470(b) - Petition - Reduction in pension - Notice to petitioner for reduction in pension on ground of unsatisfactory service - No evidence or report against employee to support conclusion of unsatisfactory service, rendering the notice issued under Regulation 470(b) of the Civil Service Regulations invalid.
The competent authority must balance executive efficacy with protection of citizen's rights when exercising discretion to reduce pension, as per the statute.
