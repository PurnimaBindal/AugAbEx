The amended provisions of the Rules of Business of the Government of Kerala and Kerala Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1960, Rule 10 allow the Secretary to Govt. Vigilance (D) Department to suspend a Government servant, without the need for a separate notification under Rule 10(1) of Civil Services (CCA) Rules.
This amendment is not in conflict with any other statute or rules, and the power to suspend a Govt. servant is also available to the Commissioner and Secretary to Vigilance (D) Department.
