The Constitution of India under Article 14, Statute of the Overseas Functionaries under Articles 26A and 63, the Goa, Daman & Diu (Repeal of Posts and Telegraphs Laws) Regulation of 1962 and the Goa, Daman & Diu (Absorbed Employees) Act of 1965, protect only those who are defined as "absorbed employees".
These are individuals who held the post prior to 20121991 and continued to serve under the administration of UT of Goa, Daman & Diu or any Deptt.
of Central Govt. on an "absorbed post" which existed under the former Portuguese Administration in Goa, Daman & Diu before 20.12.1961, and who are not ex-Goa Staff.
Hence, a person appointed on a temporary basis, on a fixed pay scale in June 1962, in pursuance of the order of Military Govt. of Goa, Daman & Diu, is not entitled to protection under these laws.
