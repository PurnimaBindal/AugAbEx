The petitioner, a sepoy in the Army, claimed disability pension after being discharged from service due to a mental illness known as 'Schizophrenia'.
However, the claim was rejected as the disease was not attributable to military service.
According to Regulation 173 of the Pension Regulations, 1961, disability pension may be granted if the disability was aggravated by military service to the extent of 20% or more and if the individual was invalidated from service.
Despite the patient's aggressiveness during treatment, there was no evidence of a nexus between the disability and the service conditions, so the claim for disability pension was correctly rejected.
