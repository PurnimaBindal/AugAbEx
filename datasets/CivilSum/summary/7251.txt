Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Central Reserve Force Act, 1949, Section 11(3) - Central Reserve Force Rules, 1955, Rule 27 - Dismissal from service - The delinquent, who faced serious charges of murder while confined in jail, must be provided with a fair and proper opportunity to defend himself - Procedure prescribed under Rule 27 mandates that the charge be read to the delinquent, asking him to plead guilty or not, asking him to accept the charges, holding an enquiry even ex-parte, examining prosecution witnesses and offering them for cross-examination - The petitioner, who was not in a position to seek proper legal advice while lodged in jail, did not receive a fair and proper opportunity to defend himself - Therefore, the order of dismissal from service cannot be sustained in law.
