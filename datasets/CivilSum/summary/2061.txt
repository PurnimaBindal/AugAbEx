Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Rajasthan Education Service Rules, 1986 - Regularisation/State service - No provision in rules to regularize the services of temporary/ad hoc lecturers - Posts under jurisdiction of Public Service Commission are of much importance - Hence, no mandamus can be issued for regularisation of services of ad hoc/temporary lecturers appointed as lecturers on ad hoc and temporary basis in Government Colleges.
