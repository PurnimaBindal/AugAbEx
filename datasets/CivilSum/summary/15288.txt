Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol II, Rules 3.17, 3.17A and 423 - Pensionary Benefits - Counting of ad hoc service for qualifying service - Ad hoc temporary service prior to regularisation of service must be counted for gratuity and pension purposes while allowing for interruption in service due to summer vacations beyond petitioner's control.
