Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - Adverse remarks - The appellant was denied promotion in the 1995 selection due to adverse remarks received in 1985-86 and 1986-87.
However, since the appellant was allowed to cross the efficiency bar on 2051992, the adverse entries could not have affected further promotion.
Moreover, the adverse remarks of 1993-94 were deleted before the 1995 selection, making the appellant eligible for fresh consideration.
Respondent is directed to reconsider appellant's case for promotion.
