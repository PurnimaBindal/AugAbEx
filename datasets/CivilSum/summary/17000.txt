Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Medical claim - Reimbursement of medical expenses - Fixed medical allowance - The fixed medical allowance of Rs. 250/- received by employees only covers minor diseases - Major medical expenditure and operations are not covered under this allowance - The intent behind this allowance is to reimburse employees for petty expenses incurred from their own pockets - Due to lack of sufficient funds, major medical claims cannot be reimbursed from this allowance - The release of reimbursement amount has been ordered.
