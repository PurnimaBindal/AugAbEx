Constitution of India, Article 226 - Public Interest Litigation - Maintainability of - Allegation regarding less payment of minimum wages to kotwars - It is acknowledged that the "kotwars" were employed and receiving wages - However, they had not raised any complaints about their employment conditions - Additionally, the petitioner was not authorized to represent them and there was no public interest involved - Consequently, the petition is not maintainable.
