The Writ petitioners had filed an application challenging the selection process under Section 19 of the Administrative Tribunals Act, which was yet to be disposed of.
They then requested permission to withdraw the application, which the Tribunal rejected on the grounds that order was reserved and main application was concluded.
However, it was held that the Tribunal had no other option but to allow the withdrawal of the application.
As a result, the withdrawal of the application was permitted.
[Paras 11 and 12]
