Constitution of India, Articles 14,16 and 311 - Regularisation of Services - Canteen workers - Workers in the canteens established in the establishments of Reserve Bank of India - Workers of non-statutory recognised canteens, non-statutory unrecognised canteens and the canteens run by the contractors under the supervision of the managing committee of the bank employees and officers - Their demand for equal status to bank employees is unsustainable, as the canteens cannot be termed as a statutory canteen in its premises, due to no obligation on the bank to run and maintain any canteen in its premises under any law - Consequently, Bank has no right to direct the manner in which the work shall be done by various workers or to take any disciplinary action - In the absence of any effective or direct control in the Bank to supervise and control the work done by workers of the canteens, it cannot be said that the relationship of master and servant existed - Appeal accepted - Award of the Tribunal quashed.
