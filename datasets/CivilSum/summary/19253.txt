Punjab District Service (Class-III) Rules, 1979, Rule 9 - Punjab District Subordinate Service Rules, 1942, Rule 5 - Promotion from Superintendent Grade-III and Grade-II to Superintendent Grade-I - Assistant Superintendents holding positions in Deputy Commissioner's offices as Superintendent Grade-IV and those in Commissioner's office as Superintendent Grade-III are eligible for promotion to higher grades - It is important to note that mere redesignation does not preclude someone from being considered for promotion - All Superintendents working in either the Office of Commissioner or Deputy Commissioner must be treated as the same class for promotion purposes - Those working as Superintendent in the Office of Commissioner can be promoted as Superintendent Grade-II in the Office of Deputy Commissioner, and for Superintendent Grade-I in the office of Commissioner, all Superintendent Grade-II can be considered - However, since both the petitioner and private respondent have already retired, the petition has become infructuous.
