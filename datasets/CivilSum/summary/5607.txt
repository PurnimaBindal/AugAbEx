Limitation Act, Section 5 - Condonation of delay - Application for condonation of delay filed on account of negligence of counsel - Counsel filed affidavit denying insinuations made against him - Appellant not illiterate man - Plea of the appellant having been duped by his earlier counsel wholly misconceived - Court concludes that appellant abused the process of the court and played fraud on the court by submitting a plea of negligence to obtain orders condoning delay in connivance with his counsel and clerk - Court orders that the appeal be dismissed with costs and a copy of the order be sent to the Bar Council for necessary action against the counsel.
[Paras 27, 28 and 29]
