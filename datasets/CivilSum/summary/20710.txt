Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion - The upgradation of unskilled workers to skilled grade is a one-time measure, as per the Central Government letter dated 15101984, after completing two years of service.
It is not a promotion scheme for the future.
Those who were not eligible on the date of the implementation of the letter dated 15101984 cannot claim induction to future vacancies in skilled grade and will be governed by the existing rules or statute applicable.
