According to Section 5 of the Limitation Act, 1963, delays in filing appeals can be excused if there is evidence of significant effort made to file on time, and if the delay was not caused deliberately or with malicious intent.
The doctrine must be interpreted reasonably to prioritize substantial justice over technical considerations.
[Paras 6 and 8]

Non-impleadment of a necessary party is reason enough to condone delays in appeals under Section 5 of the Limitation Act, 1963.
[Para 8]
