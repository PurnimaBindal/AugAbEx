Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 11 - A finding of misconduct should be based on the record of enquiry file - The Enquiry Officer held misconduct allegations as unproven - The punishing authority upheld the charge of guilt without affording any opportunity to the employee - Management never requested any further evidence to prove the allegations, but agreed to argue on the evidence already on the file - The remand order passed by the Single Judge of the High Court, which set aside the award of the Labour Court and directed a fresh trial after giving the opportunity to lead evidence to the parties, is unsound in law, and was rightly set aside by the Division Bench.
