According to Bihar Private Educational Institutions (Taking Over) Act, 1987, Sections 6 and 11 and Lalit Narayan Mishra Economic Development and Social Change Institute Rules, 1990, Rule 11, the petitioner, who worked in a private institute that was taken over by the government, cannot claim pension benefits as it was not part of his initial appointment.
However, the petitioner is entitled to gratuity for the entirety of his service, even though he had not completed 10 years of service after the government takeover.
