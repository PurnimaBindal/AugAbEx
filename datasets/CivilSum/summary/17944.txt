Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 Work-charged Service - Pension/Qualifying service - Regularisation of services only from prospective date for petitioner - Deprives petitioner of pensionary benefits - Order arbitrary - Provident Fund membership of petitioner not sufficient ground to deny pensionary benefits -  Directive issued to regularise petitioner's services from date of joining and grant consequential pension benefits instead Provident Fund.
