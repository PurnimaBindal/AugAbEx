The employees of erstwhile State of Pepsu who had been recruited as clerks claimed for an increase in retirement age under the Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16, and 226.
However, the promotion of petitioners from clerks to the post of Accountant showed that the post of clerks is not class-IV or that of the inferior service.
Therefore, the retirement age of 60 years is not applicable.
Additionally, the writ has been barred by limitation, leading to the dismissal of the petition.
