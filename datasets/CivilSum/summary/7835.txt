The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311, the Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Section 126, the Border Security Force Act, Section 19, and the Border Security Force Rules, Rules 22 and 177 all pertain to absence from duty.
Dispensing with the holding of an enquiry without stating how the departmental enquiry was not practicable is considered illegal and goes against the principle of natural justice.
There must be enough evidence available to determine that holding an enquiry was not reasonably practical.
