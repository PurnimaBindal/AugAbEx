Promotion to the post of Signal Inspector Grade-III under the Railway Establishment Manual, Volume I, Rules 215 and 219(j) is based on merit alone.
Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India are also to be considered.
Seniority is not a factor in promotion from different feeder cadres, as there is no common seniority.
Therefore, Clause (j) of Rule 219 is illegal and has been struck down.
Promotions should be made according to merit of selection.
