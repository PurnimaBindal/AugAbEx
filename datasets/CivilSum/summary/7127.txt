Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C (2) - Voluntary retirement scheme - Payment claim - Labour court can entertain a claim under VRS with no limitation period - Even belated claims can be entertained.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C (2) - Voluntary retirement scheme - Payment under VRS - Payment determination - Labour Court has no authority to examine the legality or correctness of the VRS terms - 240-day statutory period under VRS must include weekly offs - Public holidays cannot be included as a claim under Section 33-C(2) of the Act.
