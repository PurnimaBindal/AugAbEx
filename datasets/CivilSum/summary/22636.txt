The employer in a case involving the misappropriation of funds by a dismissed employee took the position in the High Court that they were not required to seek permission under Section 33(2)(b) based on the precedent set by a previous case.
However, the High Court allowed the employee's writ and ordered their reinstatement, restricting back wages to Rs. 2 lacs.
The employer was still granted the option to take action under Section 33(2)(b).
Overall, the High Court's decision was deemed sound.
