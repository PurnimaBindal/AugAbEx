Under Article 16 of the Constitution of India, clerks can be promoted to the position of Assistant as long as they have at least five years of clerk experience, even if it was on a temporary or ad hoc basis.
Holding a substantiated clerk position is not necessary for qualifying for promotion.
[Para 4]
According to Rule 9 of the Haryana State Prosecution Group 'C' (Clerical Headquarters) Services Rules, appointment of Assistants can only be made by transfer or deputation from other department if the employees had previously worked as Assistants in the same.
Clerks working in other departments are not eligible for appointment as Assistants.
