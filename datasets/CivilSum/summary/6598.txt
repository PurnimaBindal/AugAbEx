Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Promotion - "No work no pay" - The principles of "No work no pay" do not apply when promotion is denied to a senior employee while his juniors are promoted - An employee cannot be penalized for the inaction of the employer - If an employee is wrongly denied a promotion, he is entitled to arrears of salary of the promotional grade from the date his juniors received the benefit.
