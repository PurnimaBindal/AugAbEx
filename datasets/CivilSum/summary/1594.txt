Section 3 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 states that compensation must be provided to the deceased worker.
In this specific case, the driver died while sleeping in the cabin of the truck and it was proven that the accident was causally connected to his employment.
Therefore, the employer is liable to provide compensation under Section 3 of the Act.
[Para 7]

Although the accident took place on 11593, the award was made on 11397 after the 1995 amendment to Sections 4 and 4-A of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 came into force.
The benefit of these amended provisions is applicable to the date when the case is finally adjudicated.
The learned Commissioner made an error in not giving effect to the amended provisions of the Act.
As a result, the award has been modified accordingly.
