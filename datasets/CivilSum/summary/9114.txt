Admission selection criteria must be based on a minimum percentage decided by experts, with precedence given to sports persons based on their grading in sports according to University norms.
The practice of calling five times the number of students as seats available for interview and group discussion is arbitrary and ignores realities.
B.
When considering admission to a reserved category of sports, a candidate must meet the minimum selection standard before their gradation or achievements in sports are considered.
The petitioner in this case satisfied the selection criteria for the written test and should be considered for admission based on his sports gradation.
