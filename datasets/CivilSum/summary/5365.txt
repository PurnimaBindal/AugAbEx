Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Termination - Reinstatement - A workman employed by DDA since 1979 and regularized in 1983 was acquitted of a charge of murder in 1985 after being arrested in 1983.
Upon release, the workman reported back to work but was refused by the petitioner without any opportunity for being heard or a charge sheet, enquiry, notice, retrenchment compensation, or suspension order.
The Labour Court deemed the termination of the workman to be illegal and ordered for reinstatement with full back wages and continuity of service.
The appeal against the Labour Court decision found no justification for the petitioner's refusal to reinstate the workman, and their failure to do so compounded the suffering that the workman had already experienced for no fault of their own.
[Paras 5 to 8]
