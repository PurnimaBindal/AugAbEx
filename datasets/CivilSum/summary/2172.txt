Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Disciplinary proceedings - Domestic Enquiry - Demotion - The punishment of demotion to Grade II rank, along with recovery, was imposed for a charge of misconduct and misappropriation of premium amount - Due opportunity of hearing was given - The doctrine of proportionality was found not applicable and there was no arbitrariness - Admission of guilt was made - Held, there was no basis to interfere with findings and observations, decision and order - Petition was partly allowed - Recovery order was quashed and order of demotion was sustained.
