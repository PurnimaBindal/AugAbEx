Punjab Public Health Department Subordinate Posts Rules, 1940 - Rule 5 - Ex-cadre post - Promotion - Petitioner was promoted to Senior Laboratory Technician post, but refused to accept it as the additional power of Food Inspector would be divested - Court ruled that the petitioner cannot be forced to accept a promotion post outside his cadre - Order of promotion set aside for petitioner, but authorities can pass such orders regarding his appointment as Food Inspector as they deem fit.
[Paras 2, 3, 4 and 6]
Punjab Public Health Department Subordinate Posts Rules, 1940, Rule 5 - Rules repealed by Rules 1984 - Repealing clause did not specifically mention the post of Senior Sanitary Inspectors held by the petitioner prior to his promotion as Chief Sanitary Inspector - Thus, 1940 rules still applicable to petitioner.
