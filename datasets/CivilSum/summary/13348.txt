The petitioner challenged the selection of respondent No. 6 as a lecturer in Electrical Engineering under Articles 14, 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India via Public Interest Litigation.
The post was supposed to be filled by direct recruitment, but none of the candidates who appeared for the interview were suitable, so the departmental candidate, respondent No. 6, was considered and promoted to the position.
However, eligibility for the post required a 2nd Class Bachelor's Degree in Engineering or Section A or B of AMIE, which respondent No. 6 did not possess, making them ineligible.
Hence, the selection and appointment of respondent No. 6 was quashed.
