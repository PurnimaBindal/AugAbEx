Petitioners are claiming regularisation from the date of completion of 7 years of service, but there is no provision for such a claim in SRO 64/94.
According to Rule 4(f) of SRO, a work charged employee can be considered for regularisation after completing 7 years of service.
However, while considering a case for regularisation, the availability of a post is essential and regularisation can only be given from the date when the post was created or becomes available.
As in this case, the posts were not available when the petitioners completed 7 years of service, no direction can be given, relying on Maharaj Krishan Bhat's case.
