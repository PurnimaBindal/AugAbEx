The provisions of the Punjab Warehousing Act and Rules specify norms for moisture loss and gain in stored foodgrains, including regulations for recovery of losses due to employee negligence.
However, the Managing Director of the Corporation issued contrary instructions, and the administrative policy decision to hold employees responsible for failure to show storage gain less than 1 kg per quintal of wheat is inconsistent with statutory rules.
The action taken by the Corporation against employees is therefore unsupportable in law, as demonstrated by the judgment in Inspectors Staff Union v. State of Punjab.
The petition is granted, with proceedings and impugned orders quashed and liberty to proceed in accordance with the law.
