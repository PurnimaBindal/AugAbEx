Under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 and Rajasthan Municipalities Act (38 of 1959), Sections 98 and 101, junior engineers who were appointed on a temporarily basis and screened after completing two years of service were deemed unsuitable for regularisation because their names were not sponsored by the Man Power Department.
However, the State is not arguing that the posts of Jr.
Engineers were temporarily created to fulfill the needs of JRY and the petitioners could not be continued due to a lack of funds.
The regularisation of services for the other three similarly placed junior engineers without sponsored names was held to be discriminatory.
The petitioners, who have rendered 10 years of service and have become overage, are entitled to regularisation.
