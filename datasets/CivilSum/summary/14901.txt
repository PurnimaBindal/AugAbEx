Punjab Privately Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Section 4 - Termination - Probationer - The termination of a teacher during probation was challenged for not being in accordance with their terms of appointment.
The Director rejected the appeal, claiming he had no jurisdiction to hear an appeal against a termination order.
However, it was deemed necessary for the Tribunal to determine whether the termination was in compliance with the terms of the appointment and if other provisions of the Act were followed.
As a result, the order of the Director was set aside, and the case was remanded to him for a decision based on merit.
