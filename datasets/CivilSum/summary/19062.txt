Punjab Civil Services (Promotion of Stenographers and Steno-typists) Rules, 1961, Rule 3(1) after amendment by Haryana First Amendment Rules 1978 - Promotion - Conditions for promotion - The rule states that Stenographers need to work as Assistants for a minimum of two years on an existing vacancy or by sharing work to be eligible for promotion - Without completing this two-year training, Stenos are not eligible for promotion - Additionally, promotions with the condition of receiving annual increments after two years of experience violate the rules and cannot be upheld - The promotion of the respondent has been nullified.
