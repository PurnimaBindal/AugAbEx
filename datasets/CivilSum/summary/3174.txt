Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 110 - Deputation/Repatriation - It is customary for the master to have a choice over his own employee and his decision should not be restrained unless there is a statutory or contractual control - Deputation is an agreement made by two authorities - The conditions of deputation may be defined by statutory rules or agreement - If there is no specified period of time, the parent department authority can recall the deputationist, and similarly the borrowing authority can also repatriate him - However, in both cases, the action-taking authority needs to inform the other in advance to prevent administrative complications - If the period is defined, at the end of the deputation period, the departmental authority should allow the employee to return to their parent department unless the deputing authority agrees to an extension.
