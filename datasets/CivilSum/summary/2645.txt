Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 21 and 41 - Absorption - Right to work - The petitioner, a teacher for a number of years, moved a writ petition prompting the Director of School Education to consider approving his services - Despite this, the Director rejected the petitioner's appointment - Petitioner raised a writ petition contesting this decision - It was determined that an employee working for several years deserves absorption and specifically, the petitioner has a right to be appointed to the position of Assistant Teacher - The Director of School Education must approve the petitioner's appointment as their order contained an inherent gap.
