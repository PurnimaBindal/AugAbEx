Constitution of India, Article 226 - Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Section 11 - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1956, Rules 11(h), 28, and 29 - Dismissal - Petitioner who admitted to furnishing a false educational certificate pertaining to his age had served respondents for over seven years - While Section 11 does not mandate that a dismissal order must be passed in all cases, the petitioner could have been punished with a reduction in rank or a fine not exceeding one month's pay - The exercise of discretion was not correctly exercised in holding that the intentions of the delinquent were purposeful - The impugned order was set aside - The petitioner was reinstated but not entitled to any salary from the date when petitioner was dismissed till the passing of this order.
