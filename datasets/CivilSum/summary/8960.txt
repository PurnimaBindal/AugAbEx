Civil Procedure Code, 1908 - Dismissal in default of appearance - The Court has discretion to decide what orders to pass when a party is absent during a properly listed case, based on the merits and situation.
Petitioner fails to show any reason for restoration of the petition, and it may be better for interests of justice to hear the case on merits.
Restoration is subject to payment of Rs. 5,000/- costs within four weeks.
[Paras 4 and 5] B Procedures of restoration in dismissal in default of appearance of parties are established in civil proceedings, acknowledged in the Code of Civil Procedure.
Although not all provisions are applicable to Article 226 petition proceedings, CPC principles are often applied unless they are inconsistent with the Article's purpose.
