Orissa Electricity Reform Scheme Rules, 1996, Rule 9(7) - Option - Absorption in Corporation Service - Lien - Deputationists have the option to be absorbed in the service of the Corporation or revert back to State Service, and the State-Govt. cannot refuse their return to State Service where their lien is maintained.
The State-Govt. is directed to consider the options of all deputationists afresh in accordance to Rule 9(7).
