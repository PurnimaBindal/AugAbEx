Constitution of India, Article 16 - Industrial Tribunal Act, Section 10 - Contract labour - Absorption with principal organisation - Members of Delhi Multi-storeyed Building Employees Congress petitioners, they have been employed with O.N.G.C.
for several years - ONGC notification dated 891994 states that all employees who have been working with them have to be absorbed automatically - However, making the contract labour employees of the respondent organisation without any consideration of the conditions and stipulations of recruitment rules would result in chaos and immense problems - The court directed the petitioners to be absorbed by their primary employer subject to the age and medical fitness conditions.
