Punjab Privately Managed Recognized Aided Schools Recruitment Benefits Scheme, 1992 - Taking over of aided schools by the Govt. subsequent to the enforcement of 1992 Scheme - Pension - It is necessary to calculate the service rendered in aided schools before taking over - Qualifying service for pension - The service in aided schools must not be ignored after taking over, as it had already become pensionable under the 1992 Scheme.
