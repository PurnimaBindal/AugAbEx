Constitution of India, Articles 300A and 14 - Family pension - Denial of dearness allowance on family pension for the reason that a person receiving such pension is already receiving dearness allowance on his or her own pay is deemed illegal unless there is an instruction or rule prohibiting the payment on both sides.
Dearness allowance is considered based on the surge in price index and decline of the rupee which is an independent right for an employee to receive in his/her salary.
