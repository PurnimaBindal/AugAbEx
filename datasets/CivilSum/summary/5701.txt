For determining an employee's seniority in a particular cadre/grade, adhoc or temporary service should not be counted.
The key factor is whether their initial appointment was according to rules, fair competition, and amongst others who have rendered service in a regular grade while availing the benefits available to regular employees.
This principle prevents back door entries and consequent prejudice to candidates appointed via proper channels and in accordance with rules.
The underlying aim is to prevent the frustration of appointments on merit, and adversely affecting the seniority of lawfully selected candidates by individuals picked up without following rules and primarily with a sense of favoritism.
[Para 3]

Under the Haryana Public Relations Department (Technical Group-C) Services Rules, 1985, the seniority of all members of service must be determined by the length of continuous service on any post in the service.
The appellant had worked continuously with an uninterrupted regular pay scale for the entire period.
As such, the Respondents must determine the appellant's seniority by giving him the benefit of service rendered from his initial appointment, in accordance with the principles laid down by the FB of High Court in the case of Chambel Singh v. State of Haryana, 1995(1) RSJ 382.
