Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Petitioner hired against leave vacancy while respondents were appointed on an ad hoc basis - Petitioner's services were terminated upon return of the incumbent from leave - Respondent's action challenged on grounds of violation of Articles 14 and 16 of Constitution of India - Petitioner argued that Rule of `last come first go' should have been followed - Court held that the order of appointment is not determinative of the nature of appointment - Petitioner should have been appointed as an ad hoc Mistress if not against the leave vacancy, and was therefore senior to the respondent - Ruling relied on the case The Manager, Govt.
Branch Press v. DB Belliappa - Court concluded that the termination of petitioner's services was contrary to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India - Petition allowed.
Additionally, under Constitution of India, Article 226/227 - The question for adjudication is whether respondents were bound to prepare seniority of ad hoc Mistresses and follow the Rule of last come first go during the termination of petitioner's services - Respondents were directed to determine the seniority of ad hoc Mistresses in accordance with the equality clause embodied in Articles 14 and 16.
