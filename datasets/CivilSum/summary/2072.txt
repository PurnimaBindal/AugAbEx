Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Enquiry/Fresh enquiry/De novo enquiry - If, after reviewing the documents and other material presented during an enquiry, the Enquiry Officer determines that the accused is innocent, the Disciplinary Authority has no right to overturn the Officer's decision and call for a fresh enquiry with new evidence - If there are shortcomings in the initial enquiry, the Disciplinary Authority may only instruct the Enquiry Officer to do further inquiry with respect to those issues - A fresh enquiry is not possible.
[Paras 11, 12, 16 and 25]
