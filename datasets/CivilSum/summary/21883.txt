According to Sections 79 and 55 of the Punjab Cooperative Societies Act, 1961, a dispute regarding the business of a society does not pertain to the service conditions of its employees.
Hence, a lawsuit asking for dearness allowance on fixed wages as per the Wage Board Report cannot be deemed invalid by the provisions of Sections 55 and 79 of the Act.
