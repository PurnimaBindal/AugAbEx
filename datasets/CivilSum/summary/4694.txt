As per Constitution of India, Article 311(2) and Jammu and Kashmir State Constitution, Section 126(2), along with Jammu and Kashmir Police Rules, 1960, Rules 334 and 359, constables cannot be discharged again by the government once a disciplinary case is closed and the official has been reinstated unless specific power to do so is vested by rules.
[Para 6] B.
Violation of principles of natural justice occurred in an enquiry concerning an order of discharge of constables as neither minutes nor memo of the enquiry were maintained and the record was not produced to show conformity with statutory rules and mandatory constitutional provisions.
[Para 9] C Petitioners who were discharged on the ground of being under height were found conforming to the physical standards at the time of entry in service and many constables on roll were also under height.
The assertion of fraud while entering the service was not supported by any material and consequently, the petitioners cannot be singled out for being under height.
