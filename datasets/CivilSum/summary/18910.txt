Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 13.7 - Promotion - Head Constable - DGP Haryana introduced a Standing Order which granted promotion to Head Constable to those Constables who had completed 16 years of regular qualifying service, even if they had not passed the lower school course - Constables who were already on list C-I and had qualified the Lower School Course challenged the Standing Order as it affected their chances of regular promotion - However, the promotion of Constables to the rank of Head Constables under the Standing Orders is personal to them and does not affect the sanctioned strength of Head Constables - The Constables on list C-I will continue to be promoted against the sanctioned strength as per Rule 13.7 and their rights are preserved.
