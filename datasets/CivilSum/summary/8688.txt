Industrial Disputes Act, Section 33-C (2) - Adjudication of claim - Length of service - Gratuity - The management must follow the Award U/s 10(1)(c) when computing gratuity, as the question of the workman's length of service has already been determined in the reference U/s 10(1) of the Act.
Therefore, the management cannot ask the workman to show his length of service again for the purpose of gratuity.
