Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Pension Regulations for the Army in India 1940 (Part II), Regulations 227 - Pension/Qualifying service - In order to be eligible for pension, Regulation mandates a qualifying service of 15 years.
The petitioner, who only served for ten years, three months and twenty-one days, cannot claim pension.
