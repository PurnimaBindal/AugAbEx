Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised Pay Scale) Rules, 1998, Rule 8 as amended by Third Amendment Rules, 2001 - Retroactive revision of pay scales and recovery on that basis - Retrospective applicability of amendment is unconstitutional - Vested rights under existing Rules cannot be taken away by an amendment with retrospective effect - No statutory rule or administrative order can destroy crystallized rights - Rules framed under Proviso to Article 309 of the Constitution cannot take away vested rights.
