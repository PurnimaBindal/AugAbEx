Andhra Pradesh Civil Services (Disciplinary Proceedings Tribunal) Act, 1960, Sections 4 and 4A - Reference - Departmental proceedings - The Government was given the power to withdraw the reference at any stage from the Tribunal only after the insertion of Section 4A in the Principal Act by the Andhra Pradesh Act 6 of 1993.
In Section 4, the word `shall' was replaced with the word `may', granting the Government discretionary power to refer the case to the Tribunal or not.
Prior to this amendment, the Government had no jurisdiction to hold departmental proceedings for employee misconduct and the only option was to refer the case to the Tribunal.
