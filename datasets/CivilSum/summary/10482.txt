The pension fund rules state that recovery of wrong payment to employees cannot be made from the Provident Fund, and the bank has no right to recover any amount from the fund except the employee's own liability.
In a case where a petitioner realized pension after removal from service but was later found not eligible for pension due to lack of qualifying service, the recovery made from his Provident Fund deposited in the bank without notice is held to be illegal, and the bank is directed to refund the amount with interest, with liberty to initiate civil proceedings for recovery.

According to the Provident Fund Rules of State Bank of India, the bank has no right to recover any amount from the Provident Fund except the employee's own liability, and recovery of wrong payment to employees cannot be made from the Provident Fund.

Entitlement to pension is based on fulfilment of conditions of eligibility only and is a condition of service under the Constitution of India, Article 300-A [Para 9]
The Pension Fund Rules of State Bank of India state that medical disability or incapacity cannot be equated to removal from service and that the term 'incapacitated for further active service' must be considered with reference to the principle of ejusdem genesis and co-related to the medical fitness of the employee.
The case of an employee removed from service is not covered under Clause (c) of Rule 22(1), and his case could be covered under Clause (b) of the Rule only if he would have completed 25 years of pensionable service.
