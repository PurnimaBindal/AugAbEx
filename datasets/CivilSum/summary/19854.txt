Seniority - In case of a shared cadre and power to transfer to different functional areas, a common seniority list on the grade basis is necessary.
Failure to maintain such a list can result in an individual losing their seniority when transferred to another functional area with juniors.
This can adversely affect their right to promotion.
However, in a water-tight department where transfers are not allowed, seniority will be determined within the department.

Promotion - The petitioner was entitled to promotion but was neglected.
A Grievance Committee determined that the petitioner was indeed entitled to promotion, but was ignored.
It must be concluded that the petitioner is entitled to promotion from the moment she became eligible for consideration.
This includes all subsequent benefits available to her, as well as the opportunity to be considered for promotion to higher posts.
