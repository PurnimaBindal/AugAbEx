Rule 3 of the Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975 requires that the service record of an employee be good, very good or outstanding before issuing premature retirement orders for misconduct or criminal proceedings.
The petitioner had no adverse reports at the time of the order and the criminal case against him was acquitted.
The department should not have removed him from service without first clearing the cloud over his career.
The order is therefore arbitrary and liable to be quashed.

Rule 3 of the Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975 states that pending police enquiry or investigation, the departmental authority must have satisfactory evidence of the allegations.
However, the appellant had already been suspended without such evidence and the premature retirement order was found to be punitive in nature.
