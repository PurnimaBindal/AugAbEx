Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Retrenchment - Compensation - Labour Court directed reinstatement with last drawn wages for respondent whose 11-month probationary services were terminated without complying with Section 25-F - Appellant's contention that dues were collected before leaving was dismissed as the letter did not specify if Section 25-F compensation was included - As respondent was not employed for over 25 years, a lump sum compensation of Rs. 50,000 was awarded.
