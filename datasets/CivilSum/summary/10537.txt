Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Degree and diplomas - Education - Recognition of degree and diplomas - A certificate issued by the Department of Industrial Training, Punjab for a two-year course of Arts and Crafts Teachers Training was deemed equivalent to the certificate issued in Punjab - Thus, the petitioner was eligible for the post according to the advertisement - There were no objections regarding the qualification during the interview - Furthermore, no specific order of derecognition of the certificate was passed by the Government - The petitioner was not appointed solely because the certificate issued by the Department of Technical & Industrial Training, Punjab is not recognized by the State of Haryana due to a lack of reciprocal recognition - According to the authorities, the certificate obtained from Punjab cannot be accepted in Haryana - This action is deemed arbitrary and should be quashed.
