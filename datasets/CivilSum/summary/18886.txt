According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the maintainability of a writ petition for termination is in question when the petitioner, a driver of a bus belonging to an Educational Society, cannot provide documents to support their claim as an employee of the respondent school.
The determination of whether the petitioner was an employee of the school or the society is a disputed factual issue that requires evidence to be taken.
As a result, the writ petition is not maintainable, but the petitioner can pursue a remedy before the appropriate forum under the law.
