Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion/No work no pay - The basic principle that an employee should be paid for the work they have done does not apply if the employee is unable to work in a higher-paying position due to the employer's inaction or illegal actions - The petitioner was retroactively promoted to correct an error made by the authorities, and is entitled to payment and benefits from the date of their promotion - The petition is granted.
