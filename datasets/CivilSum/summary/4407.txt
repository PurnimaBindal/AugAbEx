Constitution of India, Article 226 - Air Force Act, 1950, Sections 16(1) and 162 - Promotion - Examination - Extension of service - Stay of order of discharge - Passing promotion examination is a requirement for promotion to a higher rank - The petitioner did not pass the necessary promotion examination - They are not entitled to an extension of service due to their inability to clear the examination, and they had a chance to do so after being reinstated pursuant to a Chief of Air Staff order - An extension in service is not a guaranteed right - The petitioner cannot claim relief in regard to the stay of their discharge order, which depends on an extension in service.
