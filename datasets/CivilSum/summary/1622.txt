Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - West Bengal Secondary Education Act, 1963, Rule 5(a)(v) - Appointment - Selection Committee member appointment - When the managing committee of an affiliated school allows the headmaster to contact prospective experts for selection committee member appointment, then compliance with Rule 5(a)(v) is achieved if a list of three names is proposed and the headmaster contacts them one by one in the order they were listed.
