Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary proceeding - Removal from service - Sustainability - Disciplinary authority disagreed with inquiry officer's finding of not guilty and ordered removal without communicating tentative reasons for disagreement - Held, disciplinary authority had to record tentative reasons and communicate them to the delinquent before proceeding - Judgment of Supreme Court in Kunj Behari's case cited - Order set aside.
