Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Termination - The petitioner was originally selected by the SSS Board and after due procedure and relaxation of permission, he was absorbed and confirmed in the Municipal Committee - The Govt. terminated his employment after more than five years, claiming that the appointment should have been made through the Board - No show cause notice or hearing was given - The termination was unfair, arbitrary, and violated natural justice principles - Consequently, the termination was overturned - Once the Govt. granted approval, it is prevented from withdrawing it.
