Indian Police Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1955, Regulation 5(4) & 5(5) - Selection - Lack of merit grading - Principles of natural justice do not require recording of selection or non-selection reasons - Selection Committee is not obligated to give reasons for grading.

Indian Police Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1955, Regulation 5(4) & 5(5) - Selection - Grading in ACRs - Selection Committee has freedom to create its grading categories - Grading given by authorities is non-binding.

Indian Police Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1955, Regulation 5(4) & 5(5) - Selection - Grading in ACRs - Court cannot interfere with selection agency evaluation done with relevant material but can review illegal or arbitrary agency guidelines.
