Bihar Forest Service Rules, Rule 35 - Promotion - Seniority - Members of the service are appointed against a vacancy and hold a substantive post in a cadre - Promotions made against a non-existing post do not give the right to claim seniority or promotion - Such appointments are null and void - Legal appointees take precedence over tortuitous appointees.
[Paras 11, 12, 15] B Affidavit on behalf of State - Officer holding a key post files an affidavit  based on official record, which the State is bound by - Such pleadings cannot be ignored.
