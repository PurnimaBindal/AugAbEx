According to the Constitution of India's Articles 226 and 16, the suitability of candidates for selection must be judged on their academic qualifications, co-curricular activities record, and evidence from the interview.
The Selecting Authority is ultimately responsible for making the decision, and the High Court cannot substitute its opinion for that of the commission.
The candidate's academic record up until the last submission date must be considered, and the commission's selection cannot be faulted.
The petition is dismissed.
[Para 4] B.
The selection process for 17 advertised posts resulted in 21 recommended candidates.
However, the petitioner cannot make any grievances about this since the increase of recommendees was in their favor.
Even with 21 candidates recommended, the petitioner did not appear in the merit list.
Furthermore, since the petitioner does not belong to a Scheduled Caste or Backward Class, they cannot complain about the increase in these categories.
The petition is dismissed.
