Constitution of India, Article 32 - Court Rooms and Judges Chamber - Concerns over insufficient number of court rooms - Close to 18 Judges in Delhi Judicial Service lack a courtroom and Judge's chamber, causing them to be unable to fulfill their duties as Judges - The Chief Justice of Delhi High Court should address this matter with relevant Government officials - Through mutual discussion, the matter of providing proper hall and chamber accommodations for judicial officers can be resolved.
