Constitution of India, Article 226 - AP Fundamental Rules, Rule 45-A-VIII - Penal Rent - Imposition - The petitioner occupied a government quarter and paid Rs. 280/- per month initially.
The respondent imposed a penal rent of Rs. 1590/- per month on the petitioner for retaining the quarter even after being transferred.
The respondent relied on Rule 45-A-VIII to charge the penal rent, but there was no provision produced to show authorisation to do so.
The court held that while the conduct of the petitioner was deplorable, the respondent could not have imposed penal rent under Rule 45-A-VIII as it is inapplicable.
This rule refers to rent payable by a pensioner who is unauthorisedly occupying the quarter and not the rent payable on transfer.
A statutory authority must act within the four corners of the statutes.
The impugned order was set aside.
[Paras 4, 5 and 7]
