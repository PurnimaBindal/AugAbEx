Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Rajasthan Civil Service Rules, 1991, Rules 224 - Voluntary retirement - In order to retire voluntarily, employees must provide notice - Competent authority must communicate its acceptance or withholding of the request within the notice period - While the authority can withhold permission, it cannot withhold the decision - If the authority does not communicate a decision within the statutory notice period, the employee will be deemed to have retired voluntarily.
