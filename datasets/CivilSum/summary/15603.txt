According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Rule 4.4(a), an increment for higher responsibilities is provided when an employee is promoted to a post of higher responsibility.
In the case of promoting a JBT teacher to the post of Social Study Master, this is considered a promotion to a post of higher responsibility and duty, resulting in a higher pay scale and entitlement to an increment under Rule 4.4(a).
