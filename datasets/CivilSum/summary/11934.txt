West Bengal Jail Code, Rule 25 - Constitution of India, Article 14 - Transfer - Seniority - Warders and other jail employees have a separate "Circle list" for inter-se seniority within their circle - The Inspector General can transfer a Warder to any location in West Bengal without affecting their seniority or promotional prospects - The state is obligated to be a model employer and not cause adverse civil consequences without proper reason - If there is no statutory provision regarding seniority upon transfer to another circle, the transfer order may be quashed due to adverse civil consequences.
