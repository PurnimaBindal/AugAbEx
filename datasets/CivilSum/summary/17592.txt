Punjab Government National Emergency Concession Rules, 1964, Rule 4(i) - Interpretation of - Words and Phrases - Military service benefit - Eligibility for appointment - The petitioner's age during his period of service in the Navy made him ineligible for appointment to his current post below the age of 21 - As such, he is not entitled to military service benefits under the Punjab Emergency Concession Rules 1964 - The legislative language clearly requires that individuals attain a minimum age for appointment before receiving benefits under Rule 4(i).
