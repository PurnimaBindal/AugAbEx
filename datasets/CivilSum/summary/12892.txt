The petitioner, who was initially posted as a Constable but later assigned to work as a Draftsman, filed a claim for revised pay scale for the post of Draftsman.
Despite the fact that the pay scales for the two positions were initially the same, the Haryana state government later revised the pay scales for technical posts, but not for police department employees.
The court ruled that the petitioner's claim was supported by the judgment in Mukesh Kumar's case.
The petitioner performed the duties of a Draftsman from July 1994 to December 2006 and is entitled to the pay scale for that position, but the financial benefit is limited to 38 months prior to being relieved from the post.
