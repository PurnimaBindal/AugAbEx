A casual worker who suffers injury while working in a factory is not entitled to compensation under Sections 2(n) and 30 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 - However, the Labour Court and the High Court wrongly granted compensation to the worker - If the amount has already been paid, it need not be recovered - If not, it shall be paid to the claimant.
