Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compensation - Denial - Deceased murdered while performing his duty as a Forest Guard - Although there are no rules or provisions regarding compensation for daily wage workers, the court asserts its jurisdiction to provide compensation due to the compelling facts and circumstances of this case - Hence, petitioners are entitled to compensation.
[Para 9]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Motor Vehicles Act (59 of 1988), Section 168 - Compensation - Principle of assessment - When assessing compensation for a person killed while under employment of State, the principles guiding motor accident claims should be used - Therefore, a multiplier of 16 and interest at the rate of 12% from the date of claim representation would be fair for the petitioners.
[Paras 10, 11 and 13]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Additional compensation - Under head gallantry or valour - Whether can be awarded - Deceased, a daily wager, killed while performing his duty under the State and protecting its property - It is possible to award additional compensation under the head gallantry and valour in cases where the employee is a daily wager, as seen in this particular case.
