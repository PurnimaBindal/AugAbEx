Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Education - Admission - Admission to the Post Graduate Degree/Diploma Course - Candidates who have served 12 months continuously at a peripheral hospital, in the army, or in a rural area are entitled to 5 extra marks - The respondent, who had only served from 171995 to 31121995 and 1.7.1997 to 31121997, was granted 5 marks which were not valid because the two terms were not continuous - After subtracting the 5 marks, the respondent only had a total of 116 marks, while the petitioner had 118 marks - Although the admission was granted based on a misinterpretation of the Rule, the respondent who had already undergone one year of instruction cannot be made to suffer and is directed to give admission to the petitioner in the ensuing academic year against an additional seat that will have to be created by the college.
[Paras 7, 10 and 11]
