Removal from Service - Case of evidence - Non-examination of key witnesses and complainant - Allegation of misappropriation by petitioner of funds collected for connection - The findings of the enquiry officer are perverse and there is no evidence against the petitioner.
The fact of the petitioner being in charge of the counter is not enough to substantiate the charge.
The non-examination of key witnesses and complainant is fatal to the enquiry proceedings and the enquiry report.
As a result, the orders are quashed.
[Paras 23, 24 and 26] B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary proceedings - Interference by High Court - The factual findings in departmental enquiry would typically not be subject to judicial review unless they are based on no evidence or are totally perverse or legally untenable or vitiated due to the non-observance of the principles of natural justice.
