Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Date of birth - Delay - Late requests to change an employee's date of birth recorded in their service record will not be entertained.
[Paras 12 and 13] B. Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part I, as amended by Notification dated 2161994 - Date of Birth - Change in date of birth - The notification dated 21.6.1994 does not give employees an unconditional right to request a change in their recorded date of birth.
Valid reasons and material must be presented to the government for such a change to be made.
