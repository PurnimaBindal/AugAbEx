According to Section 192 of the Army Act, 1950, personnel who did not meet the prescribed mark for disability benefits at the time of pension or release are not eligible for disability pension under the Army Group Insurance Scheme.
[Paras 10 and 11]
Conferment of honorary rank by the President of India does not result in an extension of service or additional benefits under Section 192 of the Army Act, 1950.
