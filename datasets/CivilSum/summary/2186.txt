Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rules 29 and 54 - Departmental proceedings - Suspension - The suspended employee is not eligible for annual increments but will receive pay with all increments upon reinstatement, regardless of exoneration - Unless the competent authority imposes a punishment of withholding increments, the employee cannot be denied them.

Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 54 - Suspension - Upon reinstatement with a punishment of censure in disciplinary proceedings, the delinquent may be denied pay and allowances except for subsistence allowance paid during suspension - The benefit of pay and allowances is not a guaranteed right.
