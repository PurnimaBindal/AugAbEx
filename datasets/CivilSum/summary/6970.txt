Under Section 33-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and Rule 10-B(7) of the Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules, 1957, a workman filed an application to recall witnesses who had given affidavits under duress and coercion during a tape-recorded conversation.
However, the workman failed to demonstrate whether the conversation was before or after cross-examination, or how the witnesses were coerced.
The labour court asserted that its inquiry was only to determine whether there had been a change in conditions of service, and under the circumstances, rightly refused the documents and affidavits.
Moreover, the workman denied giving false statements or deposing wrong facts under management pressure during cross-examination.
