UP Secondary Services, Commission and Selection Board Act, 1982, Section 18 - Uttar Pradesh Secondary Education Services Commission and Selection Board Removal of Difficulties Order, 1981, Para 5 - Ad hoc appointment of teachers - The initial order from 1981 is permanent and supplements the authority to choose and employ teachers on a temporary basis in accordance with the procedure stated in Section 18 of the Act.
In the event that the notification of the selection of candidates is not provided within two months, the management has been authorized to make temporary appointments to overcome the hindrances in providing education to the students.
