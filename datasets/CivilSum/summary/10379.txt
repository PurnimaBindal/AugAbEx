Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission in BAMS Degree Course - Reservation - One seat in the course is reserved for the ward of a freedom fighter, and if unfilled, goes to the ward of an ex-serviceman - Petitioner was given provisional admission by mistake but was not eligible for grant of admission - Upon detecting the mistake, the admission was cancelled - The reserved candidate who was a ward of an ex-serviceman and had better merit with requisite eligibility, was granted admission - It was held that neither the principle of promissory nor equitable estoppel, nor the theory of legitimate expectation, could assist the petitioner.
[Paras 2 and 10]
