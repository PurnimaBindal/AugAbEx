The termination of employees can be challenged through a writ under Article 226 of the Constitution of India and if the writ is allowed, the employee might be reinstated; however, the reinstatement would mean that the employee's service would start again from scratch on the date of reinstatement and an undertaking regarding this needs to be given.
Moreover, any subsequent writ petition seeking continuity of service or challenging the order of termination would not be maintainable.
