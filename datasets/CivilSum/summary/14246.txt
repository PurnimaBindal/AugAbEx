Constitution of India, Article 226 - Seniority List - Delay/laches - A seniority list of casual workers for regularisation was created in 1988, per the Single Bench's direction.
The petitioner's name appeared at S.No.
1969 with a year of joining of 1985.
In March 1998, the petitioner filed a petition claiming his joining date as 18.4.84 and his name should have been at S.No.
1677.
No objections were raised at the time the seniority list was published.
Filing the petition almost 10 years later is considered barred by delay and laches.
