Constitution of India, Article 226 - Discharge - During probation, a petitioner was released from service due to misconduct and a regular charge-sheet was provided.
Despite an inquiry officer conducting a disciplinary inquiry, the impugned discharge order was passed before its conclusion.
It is held that the seemingly innocuous discharge order was a cover for an unjust punishment that cannot be upheld.
Therefore, the impugned order must be quashed.
[Paras 13 and 19] B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Discharge - Back wages - After quashing the discharge order, the petitioner is entitled to full back wages until the date of their enrolment as an advocate.
They are also entitled to back wages equaling half of the subsistence allowance per month from the date of enrolment until the date of reinstatement.
However, a sum of Rs. 50,000/- deemed income earned by petitioner as a practicing lawyer must be deducted after computing the back wages, and the remaining balance amount must be paid to the petitioner.
