According to Chapter V, Regulation 24 of the Guru Nanak Dev University Edition 1986, as per Article 226 of the Constitution of India, a candidate can earn grace marks up to one percent of total marks to achieve a higher class.
However, these grace marks cannot have already been given for passing the examination.
The petitioner obtained 5981% in Electronic Engineering and is therefore eligible to receive grace marks up to one percent of total marks to obtain a higher class.
