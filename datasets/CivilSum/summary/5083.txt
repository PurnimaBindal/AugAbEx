According to Punjab Civil Service Rules Volume II, Rule 312, Chapter XV (as applicable to Haryana) - Family Pension Scheme, 1964, Paras 4 and 11 - Even daily waged employees are entitled to service benefits on their death, given that the family pension scheme is applicable to government employees who have rendered one year of continuous permanent/temporary service.
The petitioner, who had rendered five years of continuous service in a pensionable establishment, is eligible for family pension.
