The Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 7(1), 7-A, 7-O and 14-B - Appeal - Employers are required to deposit 75% of the demanded amount as a pre-condition for filing an appeal.
However, this pre-condition does not apply to appeals made against orders under Section 14-B [Paras 6 and 7]
