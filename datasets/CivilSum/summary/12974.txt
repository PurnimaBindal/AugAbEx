Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II - Family Pension Scheme, 1964, Clause 4 - Pension - Family Pension - Pensionary benefits - The claim of petitioner for family pension was declined because her husband served the department for less than one year after regularisation.
However, the scheme does not exclude work charge period for pension.
The husband of petitioner was appointed on 19.12.77 on work charge basis, and his services were regularised on 311091.
He completed 15 years before regularisation, and mere absence of a medical certificate does not prove that he did not undergo a medical test before joining.
It has been held that the petitioner is entitled to family pension and other pensionary benefits, and directions have been issued to release them with interest @ 9% P.A.
from the date of death till realisation.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]
