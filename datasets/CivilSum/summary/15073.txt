Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311(2) - Departmental proceedings - When a regular inquiry into an accusation is not practicable, sufficient and valid reasons based on material must be recorded by the authority concluding this - The dismissal order of the delinquent fails to demonstrate any specific acts he committed or justification for why an inquiry could not be held - Consequently, the dismissal order cannot be upheld.
