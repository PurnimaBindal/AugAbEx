Payscale - The plaintiff agreed to be posted as a Health Educator in his own payscale to get the benefit of being posted in Chandigarh.
He waived his right to claim a higher payscale when he accepted the posting.
Therefore, he cannot question the lawfulness of being posted on his own payscales as a Health Educator.
[Para 6]  B.
Limitation - For the period from 1984 to 1989, the plaintiff made a claim for a difference in payscale.
The claimed amount was calculable, and the right to file a lawsuit arose first in 1984 and lasted until 1989, when the suit was brought in 1997.
The plaintiff filed a suit for a declaration after the deadline for filing a suit to recover the ascertainable amount of back pay had passed.
The plaintiff cannot be given a judgement for the recovery of the difference in payscale in a lawsuit for a declarative decision without claiming back pay arrears.
