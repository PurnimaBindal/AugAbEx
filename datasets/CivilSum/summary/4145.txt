Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Evidence Act, Section 101 - Termination - Enquiry - If the labour court grants the management permission to present evidence justifying the termination of a worker's services, it is the responsibility of the management to provide evidence - The order of punishment cannot be upheld unless the person making the allegation and claim has provided proof - The management admits that no inquiry was conducted into the misconduct that led to the termination of the worker's services - The onus of proof was correctly placed upon the management - The general rule stated in Section 101 of the Evidence Act is applicable to proceedings before the Labour Court.
