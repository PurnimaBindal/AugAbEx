Regarding the admission of NRI Category B in MBBS course, a challenge was made based on the grounds that the petitioner earned more marks on 10+2 than the selected candidate and had no knowledge of the third counseling.
The respondents put notice of counseling on the website as well as notice board of college, and when the petitioner voluntarily appeared for the 2nd counseling, it is not understandable as to how she could claim ignorance of the third counseling.
The prospectus clearly stated that no separate information would be given to failed, absent, or ineligible candidates, and the petitioner had already taken admission to another institute.
Thus, it can be inferred that she knew about the third counseling but chose not to appear for reasons best known to her or lost interest.
Therefore, admission was granted as per the admission procedure and law.
[Paras 15, 21 and 22]
