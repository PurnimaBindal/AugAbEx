Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Qualifications - Termination - Having a degree in Shiksha Shastri of Rashtriya Sanskrit Sansthan is equivalent to having OT (Sanskrit) - Having Prabhakar with OT in Hindi is sufficient for appointment as a Hindi teacher - The petitioner's services were terminated because he did not have the OT qualification in Hindi, but the order has now been quashed.
