According to the Constitution of India, under Articles 12, 14, 266, and 311, termination of services from military canteens, which are operated by individuals controlled by Commanding Officers, is not protected under Article 311.
Although military canteens are not included in the definition of 'State,' if an arbitrary termination has been made without the application of natural justice principles, the court can issue a writ against an individual or private entity to quash such orders of termination.
