Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Uttar Pradesh Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(e) - Voluntary retirement - Petitioner sought voluntary retirement after completing over thirty-one years of service - An amendment was made adding a proviso to rule 56(e) of Uttar Pradesh Fundamental Rules, which granted an additional five years of service for purposes of pension and gratuity - Petitioner filed a writ claiming the benefit of the amended rule - Supreme Court ruled that there was no cut off date for the petitioner to claim the benefit and that the petitioner was entitled to the relevant provisions of the Fundamental Rules at the time of his retirement - The amendment was not made retroactive - Impugned order of High Court set aside.
