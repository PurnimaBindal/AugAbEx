Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Education - Admission - Petitioner who qualified CEET and applied under Backward class (A) category had their application rejected due to missing caste certificate - Candidates required to submit application within specific period along with certified copy of relevant document - Court found no wrongdoing on part of Admission Committee when certificate was not appended - Petitioner cannot insist on admission - Petition dismissed.
