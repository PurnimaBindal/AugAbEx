Constitution of India, Article 226 - Allotment of Plot - The highest bidder in a tender invitation by Development Authority does not have a right to allotment if there are pending interim orders restraining dispossession of occupants of a disputed plot.
Petitioners in such a situation cannot claim relief, as their bid is not accepted and the tender amount is returned to them.

Advocates Act, 1961, Section 35 - Allahabad High Court Rules, 1952, Chapter VI, Rule 12 - Contempt of Courts, Act, 1971 - Professional Misconduct - Denying assistance and argument to a court is a professional misconduct and contempt of courts, as decided by a court when rejecting an advocate's request for adjournment in a case.
