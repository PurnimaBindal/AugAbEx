Constitution of India, Articles 300A and 21 - Family Pension - The husband of the petitioner passed away in 1991 while holding the post of Executive Engineer, but family pension not paid until 1999 citing incomplete service book and failing to provide any justification.
The authorities dealt with the case in a very careless manner, depriving the widow of her livelihood, failing to act as a welfare state.
The court allowed the petition with a cost of Rs. 5000/- and ordered 18% interest on the arrears of family pension and post-retiral benefits.
