Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection/Interview marks - Promotion by selection - Promotion from Purchase Assistant-B to Assistant Purchase Officer - Written test, interview, and confidential reports carry respective weightage of 50%, 30%, and 20% along with a requirement of minimum 50% in each and 60% aggregate - DPC consisted of five department heads, Addl.
Chief Engineer, Joint Secretary to GOI, and Scientific Secretary ISRO - Allegations of arbitrariness or favoritism unfounded - Claims of mala fides vague and unproven - Interview weightage of 30% not violative of Articles 14 and 16 due to its importance for the duties and responsibilities attached to the post - Tribunal's reliance on Ashok Kumar Yadav's case in quashing selection due to excessive interview marks misconceived as Yadav's case did not concern promotion - Order of Tribunal overturned.
