Madhya Pradesh Civil Services (General Conditions of Service) Rules, 1963, Rules 8 and 12 - Madhya Pradesh Civil Services (Executive) Classification, Recruitment and Conditions of Services Rules, 1975, Rule 13 - Seniority claim - Individuals who passed departmental exam during extended probation period secured higher seniority - Petitioner passed after completion of extended probation period - Court held that individuals must complete probation and be confirmed before becoming a member of the service - Passing departmental exam is a prerequisite for completing probation and establishing seniority - Authority is authorized to assign lower seniority than the one assigned by Public Service Commission.
