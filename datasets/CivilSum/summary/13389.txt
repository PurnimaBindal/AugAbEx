Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - For the MA English (Final) Regular Course - The original detailed mark card given to the petitioner was incorrect, but the mistake made by the university was later rectified with a new detailed marks card issued on August 25th, 1998.
The petitioner quickly represented herself to the Vice Chancellor for admission soon after, within the allotted two days, but her belated candidature was unfortunately rejected.
Although the petitioner was not at fault, the respondent No. 1, the university, cannot claim delay, especially since the classes for the M.A.
(Final) Regular Course had only started a short while earlier.
The respondent has been directed to regularize the admission already granted to the petitioner under the interim court order.
[Paras 5 & 6]
