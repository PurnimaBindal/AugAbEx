The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, deals with the absorption of employees.
Qualifications are necessary for the absorption process and employees are given the option to remain with the project or join the Board.
Failure to provide an option leads to termination.
The petitioner, who was an Overseer with appropriate qualifications, opted to join the Board without any objection.
However, the petitioner cannot seek parity with those who were absorbed as Assistant Engineers as they had the qualifications of the post and were already holding the position in the project before joining the Board.
The High Court's decision that qualifications were only necessary for appointment and not absorption is based on incorrect premises.
[Paras 9 to 14]
