The Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 6C, 14B & 17(3-A)(a) mandates the Employees Deposit Linked Insurance Scheme, 1976 and the Provident Fund.
The appellant sought exemption from the scheme for three years from the Central Govt and while awaiting their decision, he sought interim exemption from the commissioner and paid the entire inspection charges.
However, the inspection charges for the above period were sought to be recovered again and damages were also imposed.
The court held that there was no delay in the payment of inspection charges and hence, no damages could be levied.
The Commissioner's orders were quashed, and refunds were allowed.
