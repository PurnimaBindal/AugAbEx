The Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16, and 226, as well as the Railway Protection Force Act of 1957's Section 2 and the Railways Act of 1989's Section 2, outline that members of the Railway Protection Force cannot serve as one of the Railway Board's departments.
As a consequence, the actions of the respondents in denying the petitioners the opportunity to switch cadres and become clerks were not illegal.
