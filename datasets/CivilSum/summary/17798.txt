As per Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, the Government sanctioned the merger of Junior Industrial Promotion Officers with Senior Industrial Promotion Officers, resulting in one cadre.
The pay scale of Senior Industrial Promotion Officers was revised from Rs. 1500-2640 to Rs. 1640-2925, considering their equal qualifications and duties with the Inspectors of other Departments of the State.
This action was taken on the recommendations of the Department of Industries to ensure equal pay for equal work.
The petitioners, who had filed a writ petition, were granted the revised pay scale during the pendency of the case, and are entitled to the same pay scale from the date the Inspectors of other departments were given.
Furthermore, they are also eligible for the grant of selection grade under the same government instructions.
