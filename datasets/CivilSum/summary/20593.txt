Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 16 and 335 - Appointment - Appointment in reserved class - Eligibility - Criteria of family income for candidates of backward class - A candidate born into a backward class who was adopted into another family of the same class about 10 years ago will be eligible for appointment based on the income criteria of the adoptive family, even if the income criteria of the candidate's natural family would make them ineligible.
[Paras 2, 5]
