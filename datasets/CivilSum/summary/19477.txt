As per Article 12 of the Constitution of India, Jamia Milia Islamia University is classified as a 'State' but not as a 'government'.
[Para 5]
Rule 71 of Central Civil Service (Pension & Gratuity) Rules deals with the collection and adjustment of "government dues".
While Jamia Milia Islamia University falls under the category of a 'State' under Article 12 of the Constitution, it is not considered a 'government', thus, no deductions can be made from the gratuity of its retiring workers.
[Paras 2, 4, 5 and 6]
