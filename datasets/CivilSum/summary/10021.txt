Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary proceedings - Stricter rules of evidence do not apply - Charges do not have to be proven beyond doubt - High Court exercising its extraordinary jurisdiction cannot overturn disciplinary authority findings.
[Para 9]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Misconduct - Consumption of liquor - Drinking liquor after office hours at home is not misconduct on its own.
ON FACTS, petitioner was not on duty and was at home when called on holiday to discharge duties - Whether such an act by itself constitutes misconduct is debatable and cannot be ignored - Evidence does not support the guilt of the petitioner - The finding of the guilt recorded against the petitioner is not supported by evidence - Finding is ex facie perverse punishment set aside.
[Paras - 10 to 11]

Medical Opinion - Consumption of liquor - Departmental proceedings - Odour of liquor emiltting from the mouth of petitioner cannot be made basis for holding him guilty.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary proceeding - Disciplinary authority ignoring the statements of witnesses attributing towards the innocence of petitioner - Finding cannot be sustained.
