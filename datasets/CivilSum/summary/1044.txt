Constitution of India, Article 226, - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(1) & 11-B - A workman is punished with removal from service due to an attempt to cheat the employer through presenting and claiming LTC with forged tickets - Enquiry Officer, Punishing Authority and Labour Court all found the workman guilty of misconduct - The punishment was fully justified and cannot be considered unjustified due to loss of confidence in the workman.
