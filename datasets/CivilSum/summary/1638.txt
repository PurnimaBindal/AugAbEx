Punjab Agricultural Produce (Markets) Act, 1961, Section 20(5) Suspension - Appeal - Dismissal from Service - If suspension order is not challenged independently within a reasonable time and dismissal order is passed, suspension merges into dismissal order, and cannot be challenged for being passed without jurisdiction anymore.
[Paras 6 and 7]
Constitution of India, Article 311(1) - Punjab Agricultural Produce (Markets) Act, 1961, Section 20(5) Proviso - Dismissal from service - The power of punishment of dismissal was delegated to the Chairman of the Board, who is an officer nominated by the Govt. and not subordinate in rank to the Board, hence not violative of Section 20(5) of the Act.
[Paras 13 and 14]
Constitution of India, Article 311(1) - Punjab Agricultural Produce (Markets) Act, 1961, Section 3(17) (ii) and proviso to Section 20(5) - Delegation of power - Delegation of power of punishment of employees to the Chairman is not excessive delegation, as the Board has been constituted specifically to regulate the marketing of agricultural produce.
[Para 15]
Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Natural justice - Dismissal from service - If the dismissal order is merged in appeal and the appellate authority provides a hearing, the punishment order cannot be challenged again on the grounds of lack of hearing.
[Para 16]
Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Serious charges involving public funds - Technicalities cannot be invoked to provide sympathy for the delinquent.
