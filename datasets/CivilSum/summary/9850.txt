Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, Section 19 - State Bank of Hyderabad (Officers) Service Regulations, 1979, - Regulation 16(3)(a) - The appellant worked on probation and was terminated for unsatisfactory performance.
The order of termination was challenged as being biased and arbitrary.
However, the order was passed in accordance with established parameters of judicial review and applicable law and is neither punitive nor stigmatizing.
Despite being in service by interim court order for over a decade, the appellant is not entitled to continue in service.
