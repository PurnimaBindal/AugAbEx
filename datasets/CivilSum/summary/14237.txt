The Tribunal determined that petitioners working at the Civil Hospital under a contract with a labor contractor were not government servants under the Administrative Tribunals Act, as they had no appointment order from the Department and no record was found with the Hospital or Engineering department.
Both the contractor and the Hospital were licensed to engage contract labor under the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970.
Therefore, the Tribunal ruled that petitioners were not eligible for regularization under Section 3(q) the Act.
[Paras 8 to 12] B Petitioners employed as contract labor in the Government Hospital through a contractor filed a petition for regularization in the Engineering Department without demonstrating any relationship with the Government Department or any policy on the government's part to regularize them.
The Tribunal ruled that the request for regularization was liable to be dismissed, as the petition was filed even before completing one year of service and did not meet the requirements under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India and Sections 19 and 3(q) of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985.
