Army Rules, Rules 38 and 44 - Court Martial - Constitution of Court Martial and retirement of member or Presiding Officer - If a member of a Court Martial retires, the Presiding Officer has the power to appoint a replacement from available officers on the waiting list - Even if there are only two officers on the waiting list, appointment of one of them is acceptable.
[Para 5] B.
Evidence Act, Section 114(e) - Army Rules, Rules 44 and 41 - Court Martial - Constitution of Court Martial - Appointment by Competent Officer is not challenged - It is the responsibility of the accused to raise any objection to the concerned Authority if they believe that the appointment was not made by the Competent Authority - If the Court Martial finds that the requirements of Rule 41 have not been fulfilled, it will report its opinion to the convening authority - Once the Court Martial has started the inquiry after reviewing the evidence, it is presumed that the Court Martial was satisfied with procedural compliance and Section 114(e) of Evidence Act will apply - In this circumstance, all official acts are presumed to be completed properly.
