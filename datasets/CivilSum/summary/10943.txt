Admissions - Revaluation of marks - Result of entrance test - Admission to LLB by competitive entrance test - The revaluation of marks of the qualifying exam cannot be taken into consideration for admission after the completion of admissions - The admissions process has to come to an end at some point to avoid uncertainty for other potential students - The prospectus already prescribes a last date for admission - If the court was not informed of the last date of admissions intentionally in the writ petition, then the petitioner cannot use Amit Sharma's case to their benefit - Therefore, the petition is dismissed.
