The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admissions - Reservation in admissions to Post-graduate Medical Science Courses - It is impermissible to exclude seats of quota for the All India Entrance Examination when computing reserved seats - Such computation after excluding the 15% seats of the All India Examination will contradict the provisions of Delhi University and the law established by the Supreme Court in UOI v. R Rajeshwaran.
