The Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion - Amendment of service rules - Any amendment to the rules of instructions will not have retrospective applicability - The existing vacancies prior to the rule amendment will be governed by unamended rules - Candidates who already had the right to be considered for promotion to such posts cannot be denied promotion due to a subsequent change in the eligibility criteria.
[Paras 9 and 10]
The Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion - Seniority-cum-merit - In cases where promotion criteria are based on seniority-cum-merit, a junior employee cannot be promoted ahead of their senior, except when the senior is found to be lacking essential qualifications or is unfit for the position.
[Para 10]
The Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion - Adverse remarks - Candidates cannot be excluded from consideration for promotion based on uncommunicated remarks or remarks that are pending consideration of the employee's representation.
