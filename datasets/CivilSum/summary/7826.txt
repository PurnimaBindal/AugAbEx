The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 25F specifies that a Labour Court may direct the payment of wages and continuity of service if the removal of the workman is deemed illegal.
However, if the workman has worked for less than 240 days in a calendar year, the award may be set aside, as indicated for the first time in a writ petition.
In this case, the award is set aside and the labor court is directed to reconsider the case and take into account the factual averments regarding the working days of the workman.
