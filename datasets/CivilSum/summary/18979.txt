Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate Appointment - Petitioner's claim for compassionate appointment was rejected because of ineligibility, while the written statement cited failure of other eligible family members to apply - hence, petitioner was not eligible at the time and claim was rejected - However, the stand taken by the respondents was deemed unreasonable and unjustified - The court has now directed the respondents to consider the petitioner's case for compassionate appointment.
