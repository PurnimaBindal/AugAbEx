Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Removal from service - Legality of departmental proceedings - Petitioner found guilty of misconduct - Specifically, driving a Govt. bus under intoxication and allowing an unauthorized person to drive it, resulting in an accident causing injuries and damage to the vehicle - Admitted to the charges leveled against him - Previous instance of drunken driving - Conducted departmental enquiry observed the principles of natural justice - Removal from service punishment was not disproportionate to his misconduct - High Court should not interfere.
[Paras 11 to 14]
