The Haryana Government had instructions about appointments on compassionate grounds, limiting the concession to class IV and III positions.
However, some individuals were appointed to class II gazetted posts in that category without such instructions, including the petitioner, whose father died while working as an Assistant Excise and Taxation Officer.
The Single Judge allowed the petitioner's claim directing the government to appoint him as ETO, but such a directive could not be made as the petitioner had no right to make such a claim.
Instead, the petitioner could join as Excise and Taxation Inspector in the post which was offered to him.
The government was ordered to frame a policy for appointments on class I and class II posts also on compassionate grounds and reconsider the petitioner's case.
The Order of the Single Judge was set aside.
[Paras 7 and 8]

The petitioner had been offered the post of Excise and Taxation Inspector in 1984 but refused it, insisting instead on being appointed ETO and making representations to that effect.
Having filed a writ in 1992, the petition suffered from laches and delay, and the request was dismissed.
