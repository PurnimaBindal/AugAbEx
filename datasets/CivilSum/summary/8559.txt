The Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, Section 31-A and Companies Act, 1956, Section 211(2) cover the issue of Bonus in case of Inter unit Transfer, including Bad/Doubtful debts/Advances.
A controversy arose when the amount of inter unit transfer disclosed in the balance sheet could not be treated as expense.
To market its products, the petitioner-company took services of Marketing Division, Bangalore and paid commission for this but the Commission is expense of the unit at Pinjore.
Debiting of the account for this service is correct and nothing is illegal in the profit and loss account.
However, bad/doubtful debts have to be included in the gross profit.
