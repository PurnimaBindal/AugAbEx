Recovery of Debts Due to Banks and Financial Institutions Act (51 of 1993), Sections 17 and 18 - Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act (19 of 1952), Section 11 - Provident Fund dues - The Provident Fund Commissioner issued an order to attach and sell the immovable property of the defaulting establishment - The establishment had mortgaged the property to the bank - The order to attach and sell the property by the Provident Fund Commissioner cannot be overturned - Provident Fund dues take priority over all other assets of the establishment.
