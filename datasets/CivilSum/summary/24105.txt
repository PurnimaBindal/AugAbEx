The Constitution of India, Articles 234 and 235 - The Assam Public Service (Ad-hoc) Appointment Rules, 1986, Rules 3(1) and 7 - The Doctrine of Separation of Powers - Appointments and service conditions of judicial officers - Deputation in State Legal Services - Absorption in State Legal Services - Consultation of High Court - The need for an independent judiciary as a bulwark of constitutional democracy has been advocated by the Constitution's founding fathers - Article 235 places control over subordinate courts and district courts in the High Court's hands - The High Court has control over all matters relating to incumbents' service careers in subordinate judiciary, including posting and promotion - Entrants into judicial service cannot depart without leave of the High Court - The State cannot absorb a judicial officer in State service without prior consultation - "Consultation" with the High Court is mandatory, as it is required for any rules affecting the service structure of judicial services - Para 15.
