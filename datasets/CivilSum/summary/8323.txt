Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(1) and 10(4A) - Determine whether the Government's refusal to refer a dispute to the Labour Court earlier amounts to res judicata for a petition under Section 10(4A) of the Act - Ruled that rejection of dispute referral under section 10(1) by the Government does not prohibit the filing of an application under section 10(4A).
