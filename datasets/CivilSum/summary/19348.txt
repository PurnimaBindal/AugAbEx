According to Section 2(d) and (e) of the Consumer Protection Act, 1986, a dispute related to payment of arrears on account of a revised pay scale filed in the District Consumer Forum does not fall within the jurisdiction of the forum as it is only empowered to decide disputes relating to goods.
The petitioner is not considered a consumer under the Act and therefore, the Consumer Forum is not the appropriate forum for an employee's grievance redressal as there is no element of sale or service involved.
