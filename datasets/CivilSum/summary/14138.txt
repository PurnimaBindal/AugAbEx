Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol.
I, Part I, Rr.
7.2(1) and 7.2(2) - Subsistence Allowance - Under suspension from service, an employee has the right to subsistence allowance - This allowance is payable on furnishing certificate of not being gainfully employed - An employee who is arrested in a Criminal Case is incapacitated from producing the requisite certificate - If this certificate is not furnished, the allowance is not paid - In this case, the claim of arrears is allowed - The employee has claimed interest on arrears and a higher subsistence allowance - The writ petition for these claims is allowed by the Ld.
Single Judge- Certificate need not be produced in the given circumstances - Employer should arrange appropriate mechanism for payment - L.P.A. is unsuccessful.
[Para 7]  B Punjab Civil Service Rules Vol.
I, Part I, Rr 72(1) and 7.2(2) - Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 and 21 - Writ petition claim for enhanced subsistence allowance and interest on arrears - Non-payment can be considered as depriving the employee of the source of sustenance - This violates the right to life guaranteed under Article 21 of the Constitution - The writ petition for the aforementioned claims is maintainable.
