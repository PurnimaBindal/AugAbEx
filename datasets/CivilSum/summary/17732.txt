Constitution of India, Article 14 - Service conditions for employees of Public sector undertakings or statutory bodies do not necessarily have to be uniform.
Comparing employees of distinct organisations, even if they are owned and controlled by the Central Government, does not legitimately arise.
Article 14 cannot be invoked to compare the service conditions of separate organisations.
[Para 2]
Constitution of India, Article 16 - Pay - The petitioner, who was employed as a Combatant Clerk in the Indian Air Force, was appointed as an Assistant in the Oriental Insurance Company Limited.
The instructions issued by the Ministry of Finance Department for the fixation of pay for re-employed Ex-servicemen, including the benefit of service rendered as a Combatant Clerk, were not being followed by nationalised Insurance Companies.
The denial of the benefit was held to be valid.
[Paras 1 and 2]
