Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961, Sections 68 and 69 - Speaking order - The Appellate and Revisional Authorities have overturned the decision of the Society to terminate the services of a Peon-cum-Chowkidar after holding an enquiry which found him guilty of misconduct and misbehavior.
The delinquent did not attend the enquiry but appeared later before a punishing committee where he was drunk and abusive.
The Appellate and Revisional Authorities have not given reasons for their decision to overturn the finding of guilt and misbehavior, so the termination of the delinquent has been upheld.
[Paras 9 and 10]
