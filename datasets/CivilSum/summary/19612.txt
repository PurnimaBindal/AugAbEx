The Rajasthan Engineering Subordinate Service (Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1967, as per Indian Constitution Article 16, allows for promotion based on seniority.
A petitioner was temporarily promoted to the post of Ziledar but was reverted back due to withdrawal of the seniority list by the government.
The court has directed the respondents to finalize the seniority list of Patwaries and promote eligible persons accordingly under the rule.
