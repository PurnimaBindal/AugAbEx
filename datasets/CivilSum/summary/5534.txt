Daily Wage Worker - Services Discontinued - Petitioner did not hold a sanctioned post - Employed on a daily wage basis - No entitlement to claim permanent employment - Posts can be created on a permanent or temporary basis - A post refers to an office with a specific rate of pay - If a post is sanctioned without any time limit, it is permanent - If it is sanctioned for a specific time, it is temporary - Daily wage workers can have their services discontinued due to lack of work - High Court jurisdiction under Article 226 cannot be invoked - No termination of services occurred - Section 25(F) of Industrial Disputes Act does not apply - The petitioner has no legitimate grievance in this regard - Petition dismissed (Constitution of India, Article 226, Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25F.
