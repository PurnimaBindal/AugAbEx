Rajasthan State Agricultural Marketing Board Work Charge Service Rules 1964 - Appointment by promotion to the post of Munshi - Two categories of work charged Munshi in the rules - Stores Munshi can be filled by direct recruitment with Matriculation qualification and the second post can be filled by promotion from Mate with Middle qualification - If a Mate with Matriculation qualification is selected for the vacancy of Store Munshi, they cannot be denied the pay scale of Store Munshi just because they were earlier eligible to be promoted to Munshi in a different pay scale - Denial of pay scale to petitioner on that ground was deemed illegal - Officials directed to release pay scale of Store Munshi to petitioner from their appointment date.
[Paras 6, 8]
