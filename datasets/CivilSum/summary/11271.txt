Constitution of India, Article 16 - Bombay Civil Service Rules, Rules 246A and 247 - Revision of pension - Petitioner made a claim that his military service should be accounted for his Civil pension.
He returned the bonus/gratuity received by him for military services.
Department informed him that his pension case was being processed and orders will be issued soon.
Later, the petitioner's representation was rejected stating that military service cannot be counted towards civil pension.
The impugned order was inappropriate and needing to be overturned.
Respondents are instructed to consider petitioner's case for pension revision.
