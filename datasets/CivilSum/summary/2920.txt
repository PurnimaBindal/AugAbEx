The Workmen Compensation Act of 1923, Section 30 outlines that an appeal for award money is not maintainable if the compensation has not been deposited by the insurance company at the time of the appeal.
The insurance company is responsible for compensating the workmen, and hence the appeal is not valid without the deposit of compensation.
