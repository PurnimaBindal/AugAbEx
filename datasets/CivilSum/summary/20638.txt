Indian Railway Establishment Code, Articles 1802(b) and 1801(b) – Suspension of Govt. servant pending enquiry – Notice of premature retirement served by Govt. – No order passed by authority with regards to withholding of permission to retire or retention in service – Govt. servant will stand retired without any order from Govt. after the expiry of the notice period – Order of removal passed by authority after notice period is not valid.
[Paras 4 and 5] B Indian Railway Establishment Code, Artiles 1802(b) and 1801(b) – Fundamental rules, rule 56(c) – Govt. servant notice on authority – Upon the completion of the notice period, retirement comes into effect and does not require any acceptance.
