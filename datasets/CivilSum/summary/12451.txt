When a writ petition is filed five years following an order regarding provincialisation and seniority, it may be dismissed due to delay and laches according to the Constitution of India, Article 226.

Even if seniority is typically fixed from the date of confirmation, determining inter se seniority on the basis of continuous appointment is considered just and fair for officials in the Punjab Medical Department Subordinate Posts (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1945, Rule 7.
[Para 6]
The promotion of respondents to Superintendent Grade 11 without considering the seniority of petitioners, who were previously promoted 11 months prior, was regarded as arbitrary and illegal through the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, and the Punjab Medical Department Subordinate Posts (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1945, Rule 7.
As a result, the authority was ordered to consider the petitioners' cases from the point their juniors were considered.
