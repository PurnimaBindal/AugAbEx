The casual driver in A.P.S.R.T.C.
was put on put off duty after causing an accident while driving a vehicle.
He was later removed from service following a regular inquiry, but the driver claimed daily wages for the period he was on put off duty under Section 33-C(2) and his application was allowed.
The respondent driver was deemed a daily wager and thus a workman under Section 2(s) of the ID Act, and the claim was rightfully allowed.
The APSRTC's appeal was dismissed.
