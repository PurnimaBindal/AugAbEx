The petitioner, who was appointed as a constable, was discharged due to doubtful credentials after being involved and arrested in an offense with his brother, who was involved in militant activities.
Even though the petitioner was later acquitted in the case, the respondents denied reinstatement and cited a CID report that stated that the petitioner's character antecedent was not good.
The court held that the petitioner was discharged during probation not for misconduct but due to his involvement in the FIR and the CID report, which could not be brushed aside.
While the acquittal in the criminal charge was one thing, accepting credentials was another.
Despite the discharge order speaking of doubtful antecedents, it did not constitute a stigma and did not require any further inquiry.
The court thus directed the petitioner's case to be examined on acquittal.
