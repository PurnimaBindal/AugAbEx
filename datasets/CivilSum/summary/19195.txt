The Indian Constitution, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Post Existence - Sanctioned post - Post transfer - Post sanctioned by a specific Govt. institution for existing work - After appointing a person to such a post, it cannot be transferred to another institution without proper reasons - The existence of the post cannot be compromised by such a transfer - Appointments made after the post cannot be deemed invalid for lack of a post - It is illegal to terminate appointments on that ground.
[Para 7]

The Indian Constitution, Articles 14 and 16 - Termination - Due process - After appointment, services cannot be terminated without giving an opportunity for a hearing if the person appointed was not against an existing post - [Para 8]
