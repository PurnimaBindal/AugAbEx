Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-B - Transfer of reference from one Tribunal to another Tribunal - Two references pending before two Tribunals against same employer involving different workmen - Evidence and basic question involved in both references same - Employer would not be prejudiced if both references are sent to the same Tribunal for adjudication - Power exercised under Section 33-(B) administrative in nature - The employer is not entitled to any opportunity of being heard, and there should be no interference.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]
