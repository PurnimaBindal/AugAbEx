Section 36 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, contains a bar under sub-section (4) that prohibits advocates from claiming immunity under clauses (a), (b) and (c) of sub-section (2) on the basis of shareholding in a company.
This means that advocates cannot represent the company before a Labour Court/Tribunal without the consent of the other party and leave from the Court.
The decision of the Labour Court to disallow an advocate/shareholder from appearing in court was upheld.
