The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, along with Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I Rule 3.26(d) and Vol. II Rule 3.26(d), state that premature retirement can only be based on reports of 70% good or above.
Average or uncommunicated reports cannot be used as reliable basis for such decisions and any orders for premature retirement based on these reports are not sustainable and must be quashed.
