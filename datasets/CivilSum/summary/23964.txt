Jammu and Kashmir Civil Service (Judicial) Recruitment Rules, 1967, Rule 9 - Certificate of practice - Actual practice - According to Rule 9, the certificate of actual practice at the Bar for appointment to the judiciary can be issued by a competent authority.
The actual practice means the time period when the advocate was enrolled as an advocate by the Bar Council and was appearing in the courts.
For the period of appearance in the High Court, the Registrar of the High Court is the appropriate person to verify and provide the certificate.
The district judge can issue a certificate only for the appearance in the district courts and not for the High Court.
Also, the certificate issued by the Registrar of the High Court is acceptable to satisfy the requirement of Rule 9 and the PSC cannot reject it.

Appointment - Delay in appointment after selection - Seniority - In case a candidate was wrongly denied appointment while the candidates below in merit were appointed instead, such a candidate cannot be denied the right to seniority for the period of the delay.
