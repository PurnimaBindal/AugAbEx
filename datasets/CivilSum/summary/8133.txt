Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1958, Rule 27(c) - Kerala Service Rules, Part I, Rule 10-B - Leave without allowance - Permission granted before completing period of probation to take employment abroad - Rejoining duty after a long period - Individuals are not eligible for benefits outlined in Rule 8 and cannot claim seniority over others whose probation has already been completed.
Officers taking leave are required to abide by conditions outlined in orders granting leave.
