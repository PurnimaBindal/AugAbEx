Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 19 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-A - Vires of Section 25-M - Lay off - The provision of Section 25-M cannot be considered arbitrary, unreasonable, or far exceeding the need for which the restriction was imposed.
There must be a direct connection between the restriction imposed and the objective sought to be accomplished.
The court should be aware of the felt needs of society and the complex challenges faced, which the legislature intends to address using effective laws.
The judicial approach should be dynamic, pragmatic, and flexible.
The reasonableness has got to be tested both from the procedural and substantive aspects.
It should not be bound by processual perniciousness or jurisprudence of remedies.
A restriction imposed that has the effect of promoting or effectuating a directive principle can be considered a reasonable restriction in public interest.
[Paras 15, 16, 19, 21 and 22]
