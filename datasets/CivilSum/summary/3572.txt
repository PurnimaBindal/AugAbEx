Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Sections 33 and 38 - Promotion - Physical Test - Qualifying standard - In the case of the post of Examiner/Inspector in Customs, candidates must meet certain physical standards - The duties of the position are challenging and require a level of physical fitness that a person with disabilities may not be able to meet - The tribunal did not adequately consider the requirement to pass the physical test as a condition for promotion - Because the respondent failed to meet the physical standards, her candidacy could not be considered for promotion - The tribunal's decision is overturned.
