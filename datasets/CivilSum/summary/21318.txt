Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(j) - `Industry' - Definition of `Industry' - The expanded definition of `Industry' under Section 2(j) of ID Act given by the seven Judges Bench in Bangalore Water Supply & Sewerage Board v. A Rajappa is doubted.
A Five Judges Bench differs with Seven Judges Bench.
The matter was sent to a larger Bench of five judges which subscribed to the doubts raised by two judges Bench and agreeing with the unanimous decision of six judges in Safdarjang Hospital and Harinagar Cane Farm cases, recommended for constituting a suitable larger Bench for reconsideration of the judgment in Bangalore Water case.
It was observed that: (i) `Sovereign functions' of the State excluded from definition of `Industry' should not be given such a narrow meaning; (ii) Small and individual business activity should be protected from the rigors of the Act.
(iii) Social and public welfare and utility activities of the State should be protected.
(iv) Definition of `Industry' in Bangalore Water case was tentative only till an exhaustive definition is prescribed by the Legislature and was not on unanimity of opinion and even on mutual cross consideration of opinion of majority and dissenting opinions by the member judges of the Bench.
(v) As per observations of the Bench, the Legislature passed an amendment Bill prescribing amended definition to `Industry' but for 23 years it remained unenforceable on account of definition given in the judgment in Bangalore Water case.
(vi) Interests of both, employers and employees must be protected.
B.
Constitution of India, Articles 141 and 142 - Reference to larger Bench - On a reference made by two judges, it is open to the Chief Justice of the Supreme Court to constitute a Bench of more than seven judges for reconsideration of the decision of larger Bench.
