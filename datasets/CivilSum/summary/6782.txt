Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 163 - Essential Services Maintenance Act, 1981 - The petitioner was charged with remaining absent from duty and prosecuted under section 7 of the Act.
However, the prosecution failed to prove his guilt beyond reasonable doubt and he was acquitted.
Later on, the competent authority found him liable for punishment for misconduct after examining the evidence produced during the enquiry.
The petitioner raised an objection, alleging action contrary to Rule 163.
However, the court held that the charge of misconduct could have been levelled and proved against the petitioner, even if he was exonerated of the charge of absence under Section 7 of the Act.
The objection was raised after the charge was proved and hence, the court dismissed the petition.
[Paras 3, 5, 8 and 10]
