According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the seniority list of 67 casual laborers was decided by the University authorities, and the petitioner-union chose to support the cause of a single casual employee who was aggrieved by the seniority list.
However, it has been held that a trade union cannot take sides with a particular individual or group of individuals in a matter, and must act impartially while espousing the cause of its members.
It is always open to the aggrieved individual employee to challenge the seniority list separately.
