Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Transport Department (State Service Class II) Rules, 1963, Rule 5 - Amendment in service Rules/Promotion - Promotion to the post of Service, Manager - A Diploma in Automobile Engineering is required for promotion according to the amendment in the Rule on 28.6.1974 - Those who are to be promoted before this date do not need to meet this requirement - The petitioner, who benefited from Military Service, became eligible for promotion on 21.10.1969 - The amended Rule does not apply - Thus, the petitioner was eligible for promotion on that date.
