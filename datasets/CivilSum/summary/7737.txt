In the Jammu & Kashmir Army Rules, Rule 23(3) and Constitution of India articles 14 & 16, the principle of natural justice must be followed in summary court-martial proceedings.
When an admission of guilt is made, the delinquent must be given the opportunity to cross-examine.
Additionally, the punishment order cannot be based solely on an admission of guilt recorded on a separate paper without specifying the date it was recorded and which proceedings or offense it relates to, as this would render the punishment order unsustainable.
