Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Sections 25F, 2(oo) - Retrenchment of workman - Workman takes plea of abandonment of job - Workman later re-employed as daily wager and then retrenched again - Erroneous finding that workman was unwilling to work is based on misappreciation of facts, as workman was not cross-examined about stopping work.
[Para 18]

Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Section 25F, Schedule 2 Item 3 - Backwages/compensation - Quantum - Workman employed for 2-1/2 years - Constitutional scheme for employment not followed, juniors retained - Workman out of job for 22 years - Compensation increased from 25000 to 40000 as provisions of Sections 25G and H in addition to Section 25F are vitiated.
It is difficult to believe that the workman was idle all this time due to the nature of his job.
[Paras 19, 20 and 22]
