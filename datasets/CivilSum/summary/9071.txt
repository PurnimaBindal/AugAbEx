"According to Sections 1(3)(b) and 5 of the Payment of Gratuity Act (1972) and Sections 2(4) and 54 of the Bombay Shops and Establishments Act (1948), an educational institution is considered a "commercial establishment" under state law.
Therefore, Section 1(3)(b) of the Gratuity Act applies to them.
