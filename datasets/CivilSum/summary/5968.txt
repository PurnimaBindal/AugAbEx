Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 Qualifications/ Discrimination/Concession - Petitioners were reappointed as Sanskrit BEd.
Teachers, granting them the benefit of non-participation in the mass strike.
Subsequently, their services were regularized under a new policy.
However, their appointments were challenged by other unemployed teachers, and the petitioners' services were deregularized illegally.
The petitioners' lack of qualifications was later cited as the reason for termination, despite the fact that similarly situated employees were being retained and regularized without any relaxation of qualifications.
The petitioners had worked for around 14 years in their positions, and any slight deficiency in their qualifications would be immaterial after such a long time.
Additionally, since other employees in similar situations were being retained and regularized, the petitioners' qualifications must also be deemed to have been relaxed.
As a result, the petitioners have been ordered to be reinstated and treated as having continued in service with all benefits.
