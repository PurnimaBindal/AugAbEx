Constitution of India, Article 226 - Kurukshetra University Act, 1986, Section 11(5) - PhD degree - Transfer from one department to another - If the Vice-Chancellor had decided to move the petitioner from one department to another and the decision had not been revoked, the Pro Vice-Chancellor could not have postponed his transfer - The petitioner's transfer matter had been held in abeyance for nearly three years - In fact, the petitioner had completed the entire process at the time of the viva-voce on 4.10.94 - The transfer of the petitioner from one department to another is a ministerial task that could not have delayed the date on which he completed his PhD - The decision made by the Vice-chancellor on 22.3.96 would relate back to 4.10.94 when the petitioner appeared for viva-voce - The petitioner is considered to have completed his PhD from 4.10.94 onwards.
