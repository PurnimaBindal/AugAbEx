Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 14 - Disabled employees during employment - Delhi Transport Department has a policy for re-designating disabled employees - The department cannot claim it is not obligated to provide alternative employment to an employee unable to perform duties of a specific post (in this case, conductor) and prematurely retire them - As such, the authority must provide a suitable alternate job to the petitioner.
