Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Railway Property (Unlawful Possession) Act, Section 3(a) - Departmental Enquiry - The petitioner was charged with delivering excess scrap during a departmental inquiry.
However, a criminal court found him not guilty of the charge.
The same witnesses were examined in both cases.
The respondents' action in pursuing the departmental inquiry is null and void since it amounts to assuming the petitioner's guilt.
Another employee who was acquitted of the same charges in a criminal case was reinstated, so the denial of the same benefit to the petitioner would be discriminatory and a violation of Article 14 of the Constitution.
The impugned order was quashed, and the petitioner was ordered to be reinstated in service.
