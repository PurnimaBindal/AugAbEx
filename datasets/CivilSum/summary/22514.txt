Army Act, 1950, Section 109 - General Court Martial - Disciplinary proceedings - The appellant requested multiple adjournments and was dissatisfied with the defense officers provided to him - Although the appellant argued his own case, he had ample opportunity to cross-examine witnesses and was given sufficient indulgence by the court - The Supreme Court cannot fault the Court Martial on grounds of non-compliance with principles of natural justice - The Court Martial's finding was not examined on its merits and the appeal was dismissed.
