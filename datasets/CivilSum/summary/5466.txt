The Rajasthan Educational Services Rules, 1970, Rules 4, 9 and 22 along with the Constitution of India's Articles 14, 226 are applicable in regards to the reversion from the post of School Lecturer (Commerce) to the post of School teacher due to the subsequent abolition of the post.
Rule 4 of the 1970 Rules grants the State government the power to create, abolish, or leave unfilled any temporary or permanent post.
The respondents have an excess of Commerce teachers and there are no vacancies available, so the Single Judge's decision to not issue a direction to the State Government to determine year-wise vacancies was correct.
