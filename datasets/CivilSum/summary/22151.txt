The driver of the State Road Transport Corporation who was found guilty of rash and negligent driving resulting in deaths and injuries to passengers cannot be exonerated from his misconduct based on the absence of an eye-witness.
In cases of negligence, where the act is apparent, the burden of proof shifts on the delinquent.
In the domestic enquiry, only a preponderance of probability is required, and the function of the principle of "res ipsa loquitur" is to prevent injustice particularly when the driver has knowledge of the cause of the accident.
The accident itself constitutes evidence of negligence.
