For charges of misappropriation under Indian Penal Code Sections 409, 406, and 420, as well as Criminal Procedure Code Section 197, a Government servant had their proceedings quashed for lack of proper sanction.
However, a fresh sanction was obtained and the proceedings were prolonged for 13 years, ultimately causing the accused to suffer strain, harassment and large legal expenses.
The charge of misappropriation was only for Rs. 8422/-, but the accused had to endure these consequences.
A case ruling from 1991(4) JT 151 was cited in support.
