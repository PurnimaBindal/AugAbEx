The Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, Sections 16(1)(d) and 2-A - Infancy Protection - According to Section 2-A, all departments or branches of an establishment should be considered as part of the same establishment - This means that if a person gets a benefit under Section 16(1)(d) and opens different departments or branches, they will not be entitled to infancy protection - Section 16(1)(d) states that the Act will not apply to any newly set up establishment until three years have passed from its setup.
