The Respondents reduced the pay of the petitioner and similarly situated untrained masters, who were supposed to receive the regular running grade of Masters, as they did not have a B.Ed qualification and were untrained Science/Master.
The Respondents made payments to the petitioners inadvertently, without considering the decision rendered by the High Court which was approved by the Apex Court.
Therefore, recoveries from the salary of petitioners on account of excess payment made to them - are allowed.
