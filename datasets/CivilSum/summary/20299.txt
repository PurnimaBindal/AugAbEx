Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 10(4) - Inquiry Report - Doctrine of Pleasure - Defence personnel - The Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules do not apply to Defence personnel; they are not entitled to receive the Inquiry Report prior to the imposition of the major penalty - Rules established under Article 309 are under the control of the President under Article 310 and subject to Article 311 of the Indian Constitution.
The Tribunal's order to provide a copy of the Inquiry Report and reinstate the employee until the inquiry is over has been set aside in this case - the appeal has been approved.
