According to Sections 2(s) and 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the person holding a supervisory post and discharging functions of managerial nature cannot be treated as a workman.
The petitioner, who held the post of Advertisement Manager, pleaded that his status was reduced to that of a workman.
However, the pleadings showed that his status had been determined as that of Departmental Manager.
The Labour Court found that the petitioner was not a workman, and these findings were not considered perverse.
