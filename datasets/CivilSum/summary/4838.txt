Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 and 311 - Dismissal from service - Writ petition - Alternative remedy - Despite earlier acquittal in criminal court for embezzlement charges, petitioner was dismissed from service following departmental enquiry - As a work man seeking benefits of Shahi Award and Bipartite settlement under Industrial Disputes Act, petitioner has been advised to resort to remedies under the mentioned Act - Therefore, writ petition is deemed not maintainable.
