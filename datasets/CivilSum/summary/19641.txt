Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Regularisation/Seniority/and Experience - Workcharged staff - Employees working on a regular basis claim a right to regularisation superior to that of work charge staff - The employer regularised those working on workcharge basis on the same posts in that particular project based on their experience in a particular project as per specific job requirements - The employer can even disregard regular employees working on other projects based on this criterion - The employer is the best judge when it comes to selecting individuals with the necessary expertise for the job.
