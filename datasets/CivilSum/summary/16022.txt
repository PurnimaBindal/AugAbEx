Punjab District Attorneys Service Rules, 1960, Rule 15 - Punjab Superior Judicial Service Rules, 1963 - Pension - Qualifying service - Amendment of Rules - An amendment to the Rules states that the actual period of practice at the Bar, not exceeding the minimum period of practice prescribed, shall be added to a person's service qualifying for superannuation pension and other retiral benefits - All pensioners constitute a class and this benefit cannot be limited to those retiring after the amendment - Those eligible for this benefit must receive it from the date of the amendment - The Court has directed that the petitioner's pension be recalculated and arrears with 15% interest be paid within four months.
