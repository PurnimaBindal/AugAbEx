Constitution of India, Article 226 - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Rule 15 - Dismissal - Absence from duty - Second marriage - Departmental enquiry - The petitioner was dismissed from service after being found absent from duty without permission and entering into a second marriage, which violated statutory provisions.
Despite the second marriage being permissible under personal law, prior government permission was required.
The petitioner now seeks a minor penalty, but no interference in the dismissal is warranted.
