Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Appointment - Waiting list - The respondents originally advertised 2127 posts of SS Teachers, which was later reduced to 1270, and the petitioner was placed on the waiting list.
Respondents only issued appointment letters for 1236 posts and later appointed 66 candidates from the waiting list as 136 candidates did not join.
The court held that there was nothing on record to show that the petitioner's name was higher in order of merit than those who were offered appointment.
The selection process was completed on December 5, 2004, and no direction can be issued.
