Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary Proceedings - During the pendency of criminal proceedings under Section 409 IPC, disciplinary proceedings must be stayed - The allegations in both proceedings are identical and the evidence is likely to be similar, although the standard of proof may differ - The matter was reported to the police by a senior bank officer - While a FIR was lodged on 311095, the charge-sheet in disciplinary proceedings was served on the petitioner on 18.12.97 - It would be unjust to subject the petitioner to two identical proceedings involving the same facts and allegations - The question to be decided in both proceedings is almost the same - It would be fair and just to stay disciplinary proceedings until the conclusion of the criminal trial.
