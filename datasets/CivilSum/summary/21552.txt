Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Pension - The employer is legally obligated to provide pension - Voluntary increase in pension is not a legal obligation and can only be given from a prospective date - The decision to grant an increase should consider financial resources and burden - Pensioners covered by the decision would form one group and those not covered would form another - This classification is permissible and does not violate Article 14 of the Constitution - The High Court made an error in issuing a writ to enforce retrospective implementation of the voluntary increase.
