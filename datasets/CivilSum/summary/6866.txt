According to Constitution of India, Army Act of 1950, Army Rules of 1954 and Army Regulations of 1962, Article 311, Sections 191 and 192, Rule 16 and Para 105, employees have the right to withdraw their voluntary retirement if there is a change in policy that was unforeseen at the time of submission of the application.
This was the case for the Petitioner, who submitted an application for withdrawal before the effective date.
The respondents' refusal to cancel the retirement was found to be illegal and arbitrary, and the writ was allowed.
