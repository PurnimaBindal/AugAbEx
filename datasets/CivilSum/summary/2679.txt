Indian Administrative Service (Appointment by Promotion) - Regulations, 1955, Regulations 2(i), 3(3)(ii), 5 - Year of allotment - Dispute as to - The petitioner has claimed to have been allotted in 1989 as year of allotment instead of 1990 pursuant to their appointment as IAS on 1211994 on vacation of stay by High Court and Tribunal.
The fact of grant of year of allotment must be assigned from the date of actual appointment.
Though vacancies were available on 31121993, mere fact that there was an interim order is inconsequential as the petitioners have been appointed on 1211994.
However, court has jurisdiction to undo wrong in case of delay in appointment due to some interim order.
