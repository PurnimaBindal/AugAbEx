Admission to Post-Graduate Medical courses with a reservation of 33 - 1/3% for women is not in violation of the ceiling on reservations.
This was established in 1992 (Suppl)3 SCC 217 and is in accordance with the Andhra Pradesh Medical Colleges (Admission into Post-Graduate Medical Courses) Rules, 1997 (as amended by 2005 Rules), Rule 3.
[Paras 32, 33, and 35]
The 3% reservation for physically handicapped individuals in admissions to Post-Graduate Medical courses is valid and horizontal in character, according to the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights, and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 39.
[Paras 36 to 38]
The excess reservation in favor of local candidates, as well as the clubbing together of all several Post-Graduate Medical courses of study and of the Degree and Diploma courses, does not comply with the provisions of the Presidential Order.
The excess number of seats for local candidates must adhere to the equality injunctions of Articles 14 and 15, and the grouping of several distinct PG Medical courses of study is incompatible with the provisions of the Presidential Order.
The Seat Matrix followed by the State is also invalid for violating the provisions of the Presidential Order.
[Paras 71, 72, 73, and 107]
The State cannot raise the plea of estoppel against candidates who appeared in the counselling because the procedure adopted was contrary to Fundamental Rights and in violation of the Presidential Order.
Candidates are not estopped from challenging the procedure so adopted.
[Paras 121 and 122]
