Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 and 309 - Madhya Pradesh School Education Gazetted Service Recruitment and Promotion Rules, 1982, Schedule 3 - Post of Deputy Director (Education) - Candidates must have ten years of teaching experience up to Higher Secondary level and three years of administrative experience in education to be eligible - The failure to include "in education" with reference to administrative experience in the advertisement has consequential effects.
