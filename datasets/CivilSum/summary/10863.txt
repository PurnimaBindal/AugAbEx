The constitution of India's Articles 226 and 227, along with the Criminal Procedure Code's Sections 397(3) and 482, and the Indian Penal Code's Section 420, all pertain to the caste certificate's validity.
A charge-sheet was filed against the Petitioner, alleging that even though he was hired for a scheduled caste position in 1979 on the basis of a scheduled caste certificate, he submitted a schedule tribe certificate in 1989 requesting a change of faction.
The charge under Section 240 of the IPC was ordered to be framed against him, and a criminal revision was filed against the order but dismissed.
The petition, being comparable to a second revision, is barred under sub-section (3) of Section 397 of the CrPC, and the petition is consequently dismissed.
