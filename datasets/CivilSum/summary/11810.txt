Under Articles 226 and 311 of the Constitution of India and Section 35(4) of the State Universities Act, 1973, the Vice-Chancellor has the power to stay, revoke, or modify the order of suspension pending an inquiry.
This stay order is passed as an interim measure during the pendency of proceedings before the court or authority, and the authority can pass an ex-parte interim order; however, such an order cannot be made final until the other party has been heard.
The petitioners in this case have already made a representation to the Vice-Chancellor for recalling the impugned order, and the Vice-Chancellor must deal with and decide it in accordance with the law.
Therefore, this is not a fit case to entertain a writ petition at this stage.
Additionally, the question of the propriety of the stay order is a question of fact that can be decided on the basis of the material on record and is not for the court to go into, as a representation has already been filed.
