The Minimum Wages Act, 1948, Sections 2(e), 2(g), 2(i), 20(2) and 27, along with the Delhi Shops and Establishments Act, 1954, Section 2(5), stipulate that all employers, including voluntary organizations and charitable trusts that are not engaged in any commercial activities for profit, are liable to pay minimum wages to their employees.
Even if the employer is receiving government grants or not carrying any commercial activity for profit, they cannot avoid statutory liability under the Act as long as the employer, employee, and employment are covered within the Act's definitions in Section 2.
