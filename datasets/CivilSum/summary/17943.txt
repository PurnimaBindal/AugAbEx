According to Punjab Civil Service Rules (Vol. II), Rules 619-C Notes 3 and 624, if a government department employee is sent on deputation to a higher post and earns higher emoluments but retires while on deputation without actually being promoted in the government department, their pensionary benefits will be based on the emoluments they would have received if they had not been sent on deputation.
