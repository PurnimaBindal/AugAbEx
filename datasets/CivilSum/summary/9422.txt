Constitution of India Article 226 - Constable of Border Security Force - Concealment of facts in attestation form - Discharge from service - Petitioner argues that his discharge was not due to concealment of material fact but because he was unlikely to be an efficient soldier - However, the withholding of material information from authorities reflects negatively on the character, discipline, and personality of the Petitioner - It was concluded that the Petitioner was not likely to be an efficient soldier and subsequent acquittal cannot undo this conclusion - Petitioner was not discharged on the ground of conviction.
