According to Section 2(oo)(bb), Section 25-F and 25-T of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, giving a fixed term appointment to a workman periodically is not considered an unfair labour practice if the work is not of a permanent nature and there is a likelihood that the project will finish after a certain period.
Moreover, the provisions of retrenchment do not apply to casual employees.
