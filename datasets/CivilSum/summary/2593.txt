Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation - Petitioners working on consolidated wages for a fixed term - not in possession of any civil post - They cannot claim regularisation or further continuation of their services due to their contractual appointment.
[Paras 10 and 11]
Contract Labour Act, Section 10 - Applicability - Petitioner working as a security guard hired directly by the Principal of medical college - Not a case of industry or trade, business or occupation conducted in hospital - Therefore the Provision of Contract Labour Act does not apply.
