Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Misconduct - Stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect - The petitioner, a conductor, was found guilty of misappropriation and embezzlement of fare collected from passengers without issuing them tickets - According to an inspector who checked the bus himself, eight passengers were found without tickets, and fare had already been charged from them - The Labour Court ordered the stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect and reinstated the petitioner without backwages - No interference was found.
