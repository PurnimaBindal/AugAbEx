Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Section 15(2) - Authority under the Act cannot set aside punishment orders - The Authority's jurisdiction does not extend to granting difference of wages and setting aside orders of punishment of stoppage of increments found to be illegal and unsustainable.
[Para 5]  B Constitution of India, Article 227 - High Court can set aside orders of punishment of stoppage of increments - Orders of punishment of stoppage of increment must be issued after regular inquiry and compliance of principles of natural justice - Order of the Authority and orders of punishment of stoppage of increment quashed as they were illegal and not sustainable.
