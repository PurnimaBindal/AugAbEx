The Workmen's Compensation Act (8 of 1923), Section 2(1)(n) and Schedule II, Item (xiii) identifies a workman engaged in motor repairing work as falling within the Act's purview.
[Para 9]
As per Section 10 of the Workmen's Compensation Act (8 of 1923), the appellants, being joint owners of the premises where the deceased workman was engaged in motor repairing work, are all liable to pay the compensation as awarded by the Commissioner.
