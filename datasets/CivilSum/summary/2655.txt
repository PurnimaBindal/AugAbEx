UP Constables, Driver and Taxi Supervisors Services Rules, 1983, Rule 13 - Recruitment - Minimum qualifications for the post of Excise Constable must be met - Respondents lacked minimum physical standards and their services were terminated - Learned Single Judge quashed the termination order - If a candidate doesn't meet the prescribed minimum standard, they are unqualified and illegally appointed.
[Paras 11, 12 and 17] B. Allahabad High Court Rules, Chapter VIII, Rule 5 - Special appeal - State filed special appeal against interim order granting stay of termination order - Special appeal is maintainable if the order grants principle relief claimed in Writ Petition.
