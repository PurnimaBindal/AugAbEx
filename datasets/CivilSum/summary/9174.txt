Appointment of an ad hoc employee to a regular post through the selection committee against the available vacancy is equivalent to a regular appointment and cannot be terminated without following the principles of natural justice and Article 14.

The Doctrine of Lassaize Faire is not recognized in India, and employers cannot establish unfair, arbitrary, or public policy-opposing terms and conditions, which cannot be enforced against the employees and can be declared invalid.
[Para 15]
