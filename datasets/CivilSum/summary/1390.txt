Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pharmacy Act, 1948, Section 10 - Indian Council for Technical Education Act, 1987 - Education - Admission - Admissions made to professional colleges that defy Council approval despite specific directions to stop such admissions and classes - Admissions clearly contrary to the provisions of the Pharmacy Act, Indian Council for Technical Education Act and admission brochure - College not fulfilling the conditions of required infrastructure - Admissions clearly a fraud played by the College against the State as well as the students - Admission brochure have the force of law and conditions therein are binding on each concerned - Inaction of the Council in such a case cannot be ignored since the Council will fail in discharging its statutory duty if such admissions are regularised - Admission made in contravention of the Acts, Rules and procedure laid down for that purpose cannot be sustained.
[Paras 7 to 9]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admissions/Education - Admissions made in complete disregard and violation of the provisions of the Act, Rules and admission brochure without fulfilling the requisite conditions by the college for diploma in Pharmacy - Action of the College found to the fraudulent - Such admissions cannot be sustained - Mere ground of compassion cannot be a reason enough to permit violation of Rules - The Council will fail in discharging its statutory duty if such admissions are regularised and inaction of the Council in such a case cannot be ignored - The Court would not endorse illegal action of the Authorities on compassionate grounds alone - Anything done or permitted to be done in violation of provisions of the Act, Rule and admission brochure will not only be prejudicial to the interest of the students but would adversely affect the requisite standard of high education and training - Completion of required course without studying for the complete period of study and complete lack of required infrastructure is bound to prove fatal to the academic career of the students and public interest - College liable to compensate innocent students awarded admission fraudulently.
[Paras 9 to 11]  C. Education/Technical education/Admissions - Council being highly placed technical body must ensure timely compliance of its orders and directions in exercise of its statutory powers - When violation of the Acts and Rules comes to the notice of the Council, it is duty bound to take immediate action and stop the same - Slackness on the part of the Council has to be viewed seriously.
