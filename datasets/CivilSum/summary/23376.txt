Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection/Arbitrariness/Interview - Selection of Unani Medical Officer (Gazetted Class II) by Public Service Commission - Written test not conducted, only interview held as per Government instructions - Selection criteria based on 50% academic qualifications and 50% interview - Criteria deemed not arbitrary and may reduce subjectivity - Appeal allowed against High Court order.
