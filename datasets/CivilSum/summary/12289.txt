The Disciplinary Authority's order for a fresh enquiry against the delinquent, despite the enquiry officer's report exonerating them, is not within their jurisdiction, as per Section 49 of the Life Insurance Corporation Act, 1956 and Regulation 39 of the Life Insurance Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1960.
However, the corporation may still proceed with further enquiry if they wish to do so.
