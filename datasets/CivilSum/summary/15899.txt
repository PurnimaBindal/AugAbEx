According to the Army Pension Regulations of 1961, Regulation 132, a circular letter from the Government of India dated October 30, 1987 provides instructions for special pension and gratuity for Combatants who were discharged due to a reduction in the strength of Armed Forces or for reorganization.
However, a petitioner who requested discharge to attend to his agricultural land is not entitled to special benefits as he does not meet the conditions of the aforementioned instructions.
Although pension is not considered a bounty, but rather, a right, it can only be granted if it falls within the regulation conferring the right.
Therefore, the petition is dismissed.
