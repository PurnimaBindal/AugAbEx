Advocates Act, 1961 - Professional misconduct - The appellant was accused of misconduct for persuading the complainant to loan him Rs. 3,000 and not repaying the amount.
The State Bar Council found him guilty and suspended his practice for two years, an appeal before the Bar Council of India also failed.
However, the court held that the appellant cannot be guilty of professional misconduct as he was not acting in his professional capacity when taking the loan and there was no obligation towards the complainant as he was a third party creditor.
Therefore, the order passed by the Bar Council was set aside.
