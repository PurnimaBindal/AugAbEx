Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Admission to Medical Course - Cancellation of admission - Petitioners were granted admission to BDS course, but their admissions were subsequently cancelled on the grounds that they had secured less than 50% marks in PMET.
The University argued that the admissions were fraudulent, lacked merit and transparency since a large number of meritorious candidates who scored higher marks were available.
However, the petitioners had already undergone studies for one year, and the University failed to take any action against the college for granting non-transparent admissions.
Besides, opportunity of hearing was not given to the petitioners before cancelling their admission.
Consequently, the Order of cancellation of admission was quashed, and the respondent was directed to take fresh appropriate action against the concerned college.
