Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Promotion - Respondent No. 2 received continuity of service by the previous award of the Labour Court - Certain juniors were promoted during the period respondent No. 2 remained out of job - Respondent No. 2 claimed promotion from the date his juniors were promoted - The Labour Court ordered the promotion of respondent No. 2 with effect from the date his juniors were promoted - It was not shown that after consideration the petitioner could not have been promoted - There were no reasons to interfere with the award of the Labour Court.
[Paras 2 and 6]
