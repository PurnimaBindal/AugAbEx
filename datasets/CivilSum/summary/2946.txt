Constitution of India, Article 226 - Quo warranto writ - Qualifications - To maintain a writ of quo warranto, it must be alleged that the appointee did not possess the qualifications required for the post they hold.
[Paras 14 and 15]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Quo warranto writ - Public interest litigation - Only a person who was a competing candidate for the post and had a locus standi for some cause of action can maintain a writ of quo warranto in the nature of public interest.
Misusing the jurisdiction for ulterior motives by a stranger is not possible.
[Paras 15, 24 and 25]  C. Constitution of India, Article 226 - Quo warranto writ - If the selection of the appointee has been made properly by the competent authority using proper procedures, the court cannot evaluate the merit of the selection committee.
