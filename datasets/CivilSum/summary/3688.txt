Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Removal - Charge of issuing ticket of less amount - The domestic enquiry can use all logically probative materials and hearsay evidence can be used if it has reasonable credibility and nexus - Evidence of checking staff produced in enquiry - Written statements given by passengers signed by respondent placed before enquiry officer - Petitioner's contention that best witnesses were withheld like driver and passengers does not hold ground because legal evidence having nexus with events being enquired into has been established.
[Paras 7 and 9]  B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Domestic enquiry - Charge of issuing ticket of less amount - The non-production of passenger witnesses is not fatal to domestic enquiry and it is highly impractical to expect all passenger witnesses to be traced, chased and brought before enquiry officer or Labour Court - This has been held to be not at all necessary by various High Courts.
