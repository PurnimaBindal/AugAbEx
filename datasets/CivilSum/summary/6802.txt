Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 15 - Reservation - Validity - Elementary School Teachers - Reservation for women- Reservation is valid - Reservation based on psychology of children- Article 15(1) provisions not violated- The government order is to appoint only women teachers in primary schools so that children can develop more interest in studies which is based on psychology and not only on gender, thus the reservation is valid and Article 15(1) provisions are not violated.
