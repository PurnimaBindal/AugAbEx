Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 39 Rules 1 & 2 - Injunction - Defendants must be restrained from participating in demonstrations and dharnas in a disruptive or threatening manner - This order is in place to ensure the smooth functioning of the plaintiff organization and protect the general public - Demonstrations and dharnas are permitted as long as they are peaceful and do not impede traffic flow - Lunch hour demonstrations are not to be held inside the premises of the plaintiff.
