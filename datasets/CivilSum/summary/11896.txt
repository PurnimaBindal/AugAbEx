The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 226, address promotion and the court's jurisdiction in such matters is limited under Article 226.
Once a selection committee has deemed a candidate unsuitable for promotion based on their service record, there is no further scrutiny that can be performed by the court.
The petitioner's performance reports were only satisfactory and a minor penalty had been imposed on him.
As a result, the respondent's decision to promote other candidates found more suitable is not arbitrary or unfair, and the petitioner's grievance is misguided.
