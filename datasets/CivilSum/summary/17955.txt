Constitution of India, Article 14 - Army pension Regulations, 1961 (Part 1), Rule 122 - Army Service - Pension - Qualifying Service-service as boy - Although there is a difference between service after enrollment and 'Boy service', the latter is specifically excluded from being counted towards qualifying service under the Rules - This is because a person below the age of 17 years is presumed to be not capable of performing all the duties required of an enrolled person after attaining that age - This Rule is not arbitrary and does not violate Articles 14-16 of the Constitution.
