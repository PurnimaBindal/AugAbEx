The authorities committed an error converting these cases into disciplinary cases.

[Para 7]
The Enquiry Officer is authorised to recall witnesses to clarify his doubts.
This is in accordance with Rule 154.4 and 15333 of the Railway Protection Rules, 1987.
[Para 9]
The Delinquents failed to examine all persons coming out of the factory, which resulted in the theft of railway material.
The confession was in the handwriting of the delinquent.
It cannot be said that there is any perversity in the finding of the Disciplinary Authority.
The petition was dismissed.
