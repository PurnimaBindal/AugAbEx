Constitution of India, Article 226 - The petitioner, who was selected as an Arts and Crafts teacher by a departmental selection committee, had three increments and higher pay scale stopped due to a disagreement with the Headmaster.
The petitioner was erroneously listed as a tailoring mistress in the order book and later transferred, leading to a legal challenge.
The court found that the transfer order was invalid and ordered full pay during the transfer period.
The respondents intentionally withheld pay, and the District Education Officer had no authority to change the petitioner's designation.
The petitioner was not afforded an opportunity to be heard prior to the three advance increments being withdrawn, rendering the impugned order a violation of natural justice principles.
The writ petition was reportedly granted.
