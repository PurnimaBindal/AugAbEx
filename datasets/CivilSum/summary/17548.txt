Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation - admissions/Education-Reservation for wards of Freedom Fighters in admissions to Medical Colleges - The certificate required for admission should be issued by the General Administration Department, not the Deputy Commissioner; thus, it was deemed illegal to deny admission due to lack of a DC's certificate - Authorities instructed to consider petitioner's merit based on the certificate from General Administration Department.
