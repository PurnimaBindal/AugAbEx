Constitution of J & K, Section 124 - J & K Public Service Commission (Conduct of Examination) Rules (1973), Rules 2(a), 8, 10 - Direct recruitment - Gazetted cadre service posts - The allocation of candidates to departments will be based on merit, suitability, and preference of the post.
It is not permissible for the candidates to change their department as there is no such provision under the Rules.
However, respondents who have served in the Police department in their past are allowed to change the department to the Police department, not based on merit-cum-preference, but only on the basis of their past service in the Police department.
The petitioner's request for a change of department is declined and held that the criteria adopted for allowing a change is irrelevant and not sustainable in law.
The order is arbitrary, discriminatory, and liable to be quashed.
