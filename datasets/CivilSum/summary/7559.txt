A) Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Natural justice - Termination - Failure to comply with Section 25-F renders termination void ad-initio - Management witness affidavit not admissible as evidence - Workman entitled to reinstatement with full back wages.
[Paras 10 and 11] B) Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Reinstatement - Back wages - Interest on arrears - Workman entitled to back wages with interest from date of termination to actual payment - Employer failed to prove workman's gainful employment during termination period.
