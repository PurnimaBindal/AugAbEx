Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana State Agricultural Marketing Board Service Rules, 1974, Rules 10 and 12 - Ad hoc service/Seniority/Experience - Appointment/Promotion - Ad hoc and fortuitous appointment on temporary or stop-gap arrangement cannot be taken into consideration for the purpose of seniority - Promoted purely on ad hoc basis giving benefit of ad hoc service for the purpose of experience only - Such service rendered purely on ad hoc basis cannot be counted for the purpose of seniority or experience for promotion - Temporary tenure cannot be counted for seniority in any system of service jurisprudence.
