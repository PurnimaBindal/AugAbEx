Indian Penal Code, Section 304-B - In a case where a husband demands dowry "soon before death" and his wife commits suicide within two months, any harassment during that period would be considered to be "soon before death."
A husband who demanded gas connection and cash from his wife, who then committed suicide within two months, was convicted under Section 304-B [Para 19]

Indian Penal Code, Section 304-B - When a bride dies within 9 months of her marriage and there are allegations of harassment and dowry demands from her husband, Jeth, and Jethani, the absence of mention of harassment in letters written by the bride in the first 4-5 months of marriage is not sufficient to dismiss the prosecution's case.
It is common for newly-wed women to avoid complaining about ill-treatment in their initial correspondence.
Hence, the conviction was upheld.
[Para 22]

Indian Penal Code, Section 304-B - In a dowry death case where the bride was living separately and a complaint was made against her husband, his brother and wife, evidence showed that the brothers had strained relations.
Therefore, the brother and his wife were rightfully acquitted.
[Para 21]
