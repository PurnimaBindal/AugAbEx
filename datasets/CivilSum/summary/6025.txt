Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 and 311 - Rules for Management of Recognised Non-Government Institutions (Aided and Unaided) 1969, Rule 28(4) - Compassionate appointment - Dying-in-harness - Fraud - The order obtained by fraud after the death of the serving High School husband of the petitioner's brother without impleading her for compassionate appointment cannot be sustained and she should be considered for compassionate appointment according to the Recruitment Rules.
