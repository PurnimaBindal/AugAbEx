Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 136 - Education - Admission - Precedent - Admission to MBBS Courses - Appellants-students seeking admissions to 1st Year MBBS Courses colluded with members of the Selection Scrutiny Committee (SSC) to obtain higher rankings in their admission forms in order to gain admission to various medical colleges in the State - Fraudulent behavior detected and admissions cancelled - Meanwhile, members of the then SSC (who colluded with appellants) were promoted to higher posts - State cannot demand punishment of appellants alone while promoting members of the then SSC - Both promotion and punishment must be treated alike and rise and fall together - Students have studied for about two years and their actions did not deprive any other deserving student of their legitimate seat - Cancellation order set aside - However, this decision should not be considered a precedent.
[Paras 6 and 7]
