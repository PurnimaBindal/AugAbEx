West Bengal Service Regulations (Part I), Rule 34 (b) - Instructions contained in Memo No. 4145(2) dated November 22, 1985 of the Assistant Inspector General of Police, West Bengal - Discharge - Armed Reserved Constables - The respondents were appointed as Constables in the State Armed Police from a list furnished by Employment Exchange.
However, they were discharged from service due to being found on a fake list provided by the Employment Exchange.
The principles of natural justice require that they should be given a reasonable opportunity for representation in the enquiry to be conducted, and appropriate orders with reasons need to be passed.
As the discharge would cause a stigma on the respondents for future recruitment, the appellants must issue a notice to all respondents stating grounds of discharge and then pass appropriate orders with reasons after considering their objections.
