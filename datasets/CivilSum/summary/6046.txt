The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation/Promotion - When promoting on the basis of roster points reserved for Scheduled Castes, individuals in the general category cannot be skipped over by selecting a Scheduled Caste candidate for a post in between - This approach is not allowed by any instructions or legal precedent, nor is it just or fair - Individuals in the Scheduled Caste category can be considered for promotion only to the next higher post, skipping their senior colleagues in the same cadre, but not for a post two levels above - Punjab Civil Secretariat (State Service Class II) Rules, 1963, Rule 8.
[Paras 9, 11, 13, and 14] B. Punjab Civil Secretariat (State Service Class II), Rules, 1963, Rule 8 - Promotion - Only people holding the post of Superintendent Gr.
II are eligible for promotion to the post of Superintendent Gr.
I in the office of Advocate General Punjab, whereas Assistants are not - The Draft Rules of AG Punjab are similar in substance to the Punjab Civil Secretariat Class II Rules, 1963.
