Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(p) - Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules, 1957, Rule 58 - Settlement - Oral settlement - The Act and Rules require a written settlement, not an oral one.
A written settlement cannot be altered or modified by an oral settlement or undertaking.
[Paras 11 to 14]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(1) - Recovery Certificate - For pre-determined and ascertained amounts due to a workman, resorting to summary proceedings under Section 33-C(1) of the Act is both appropriate and desirable.
A recovery certificate issued by the Labour Court for recovery of determined amount claimed by the workman suffers from no infirmity.
[Paras 15 to 22]
Evidence Act, 1872, Sections 91 and 92 - Settlement - Agreement - Evidence of any oral agreement or settlement is not admissible if it is proved that the terms of the settlement, agreement or contract between the parties were reduced into a written document.
