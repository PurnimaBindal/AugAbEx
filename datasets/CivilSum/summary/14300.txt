Constitution of India, Article 226 - University Calendar, Volume II, 1986, Clause 3.5(b) - Readmission - Entrance examination - M.B.B.S course - A petitioner who had previously joined an MBBS course after taking the entrance test but had failed to pass the Ist professional examination, and whose name had been struck off from the institute's rolls, was denied the opportunity to take the entrance test for fresh admission.
However, it was held that the petitioner was not debarred from taking the fresh entrance test because it constituted a fresh admission, and he had a fresh right to be admitted into the MBBS course.
