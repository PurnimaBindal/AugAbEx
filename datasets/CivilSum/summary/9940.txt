Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punishment/Transfer - It is important to distinguish between imposing punishment during disciplinary proceedings and overstepping one's authority by extending the punishment through administrative actions.
The power to transfer is generally not disputable, but the manner in which it is exercised must be closely examined.
Sometimes when a poor employee is transferred maliciously, it can have disastrous consequences on the employee's career and dependents.
However, if the employee himself has created trouble and the transfer is made to shift him to a different location in the interest of the organization, the transfer is not punitive but rather a correction of administration and cannot be interfered with.
[Paras 2 to 7]
