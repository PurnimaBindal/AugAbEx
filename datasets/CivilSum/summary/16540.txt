Army Rules, 1954, Rule 13(3) - Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, Regulation 173 - Disability Pension - Disability Pension is awarded to individuals when disability is caused or worsened by military service in non-battle casualty and is assessed at 20 percent or more - The petitioner's disability is assessed at 11 to 14 percent - Therefore, he is not entitled to disability pension.
