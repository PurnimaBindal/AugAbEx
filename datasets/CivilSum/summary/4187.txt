According to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 Section 2(k) and the Constitution of India, Article 226, when a workman is reverted by a company, the workman can file a writ petition if he can prove that a remedy before Labor Court is not adequate and efficacious.
[Para 10]

Under the Constitution of India, Article 226, when a private company reverts an employee, the company is not amenable to writ jurisdiction if it is neither statutory nor a public body nor discharging any public duty.
This is a matter that is governed by private law.
[Para 9]
