Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rule 4 - Demobalised Armed Forces Personnel (Reservation of Vacancies in Punjab State non Technical Services) Rules, 1968, Rule 5 - Military service - An employee is only eligible for the benefit for the duration of service performed during the emergency, and only to those who were called to join the military during the emergency for the purpose of national service, and not to those who joined during peacetime for a career, as the two are separate categories.
