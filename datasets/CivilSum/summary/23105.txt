Indian Medical Council Act, 1956 - Admission/Migration - When it comes to admission by way of migration, it is not considered unreasonable or arbitrary for a recognized college to refuse migration of students from a non-recognized medical college.
The eligibility conditions for migration, prescribed by the Medical Council of India, are also deemed not to be unreasonable or arbitrary, as these conditions have a rational purpose.
The main objective behind these conditions is to maintain the standard and prevent back door entry.
