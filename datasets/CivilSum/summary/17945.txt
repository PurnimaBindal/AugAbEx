Punjab Dairy (State Service Class-I) Rules, 1981 - Reservation/Single Cadre Post - Reservation cannot be applied to a single 'cadre post' according to Articles 14 & 16 of the Constitution of India.
1988(2) SCR 114 has supported this rule.
