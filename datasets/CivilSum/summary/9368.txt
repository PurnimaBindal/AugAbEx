Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 173(8) - Indian Penal Code, Sections 498A and 406 - Police submitted challan against accused after investigating FIR under Sections 498A and 406 IPC - Later, police conducted further investigation and submitted fresh challan exonerating accused - As permission of court for further investigation was not obtained, Magistrate cannot take into account supplementary challan.
[Para 14] B Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 173(8) - Police submitted challan after investigation - Police is not authorized to carry out further investigation without court's permission - Police does not have the right of fresh investigation or reinvestigation.
