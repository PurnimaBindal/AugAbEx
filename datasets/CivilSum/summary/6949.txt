West Bengal Primary School Teacher Recruitment Rules, 2001, Rule 8 - West Bengal Primary Education Act, 1973 - Recruitment - According to Article 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India, the source of recruitment of Primary school teachers is clarified in Rule 8.
It is mandatory for the employer to obtain the names of eligible candidates from the Employment Exchange.
The obligation to obtain these names is held not to violate any provision of the Constitution, therefore, the petition is dismissed.
