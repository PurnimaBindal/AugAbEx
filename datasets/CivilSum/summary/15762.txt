After conducting a Departmental Enquiry and issuing a Charge sheet, the delinquent was punished with the stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect.
There is no specific rule or authority that requires the punishing authority to serve the charge-sheet directly to the delinquent.
Moreover, the issuance of the charge-sheet by the Enquiry Officer does not have any negative effect on the inquiry proceedings.
Therefore, there are no grounds for interference.
