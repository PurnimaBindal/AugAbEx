Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 300-A - Provident Fund - Re-employment - The petitioner, who retired from Military service and received retiral benefits, was re-employed in the Army.
After some amount of provident fund was deducted from his pay, he resigned from re-employment.
When a contingent Bill was required from his previous employer, he submitted it before the Civil Court in a civil suit but refused to submit it before the PF Commissioner.
As a result, the PF Commissioner directed that the dues would only be released once the petitioner submits a copy of the contingent bill within one month.
[Paras 4 and 5]
