Panjab University Calender Vol.
I Para 14 Rule 14 of Chapter VIII (E) - Punjab Educational Services (College Cadre) (Class II) Rules 1976 - Retirement/Pension & Gratuity/Age of Superannuation - The petitioner had served continuous service in a private college for 15 years which was later taken over by the government.
If he had stayed in private service, he would have been retired at the age of 60.
However, as per the gift deed of taking over, the Government only accepted the liability after the takeover date.
So, the petitioner is now governed by Punjab Educational Rules and would retire at the age of 58.
As per rule 326 of Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol.
I Part I, the petitioner is entitled to service benefits only for the period he remained in Government service.
