Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion - Eligibility for promotion - Consideration for promotion - Scope of writ jurisdiction to order promotion - Placement in the panel of eligible persons - While promotion is an advancement in rank, grade and status, its effect will depend on the nature of the right created by the governing rule - Being empanelled does not guarantee automatic promotion - The competent authority must also consider entitlement, eligibility and merit as per the rule - If eligible persons are due, the court can direct the authority to consider them but cannot grant retrospective promotion.
[Paras 6 to 10]
