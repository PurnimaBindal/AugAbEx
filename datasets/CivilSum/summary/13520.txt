The service of the petitioner was terminated from the post of Gunman, alleging that there was no post available, despite the record showing that the post was still vacant at the same place.
The termination was deemed illegal, and the authority was directed to reinstate the petitioner.
[Para 2]

The petitioner's service was terminated from the post of Gunman, and though he was entitled to reinstatement, it took more than 10 years for the matter to be resolved.
It was also unclear whether the petitioner was gainfully employed during the termination period.
As a result, it was deemed fair to grant 50% back wages.
[Para 3]
