Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-G and 25-F - Workman - Termination of Service - The burden falls on the workman to prove 240 days service in the preceding year, and self-serving statements or an affidavit will not suffice.
Without relevant muster rolls or cogent evidence, no adverse inference can be made against the management, and the termination is upheld.
