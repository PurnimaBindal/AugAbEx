Andra Pradesh State Transport Corporation Employees (Recruitment) Regulations, 1996, Regulation 30 - Promotion - Reversion - The petitioners were reverted to their substantive posts of stenographer, challenging their reversion to the post of head clerk.
The orders of promotion stated that their promotions were on an emergency basis, and they were liable for reversion to their former post without notice or reason, if their work was found unsatisfactory.
The petitioners were subsequently promoted as Head Clerk, but the court affirmed that the grievance of petitioners, that they should be deemed to have been promoted from the date of their initial temporary promotion, was barred by res judicata as well as constructive res judicata.
The temporary nature of petitioners' appointments had been recorded by the court in their writ appeal, and having submitted themselves to the process of interview for substantive promotion, the petitioners could not claim their earlier temporary promotion as substantive.
