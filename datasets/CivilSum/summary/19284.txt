Constitution of India, Article 226 - Model Staff Services Regulations, 1983, Regulation 31 - Removal from service - The petitioner filed a writ without taking advantage of the available right to appeal under Regulation 31 - The petitioner did not file an appeal as the punishing authority was a member of the Board of Directors and the Appellate Authority - It was undisputed that the punishing authority was no longer a member of the Board that hears the appeal - The issues raised in the petition involve contested factual questions that cannot be addressed in a writ proceeding - Thus, the petitioner must seek remedy through appealing under the Regulations - The respondents are instructed not to contest the timeliness of the filing of the appeal.
[Paras 1, 3 and 4]
