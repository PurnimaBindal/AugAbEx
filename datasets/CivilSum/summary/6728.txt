Article 226 of the Constitution of India on Termination and its grounds - Permanent employee with requisite qualifications for the job was terminated via an order due to project completion - Employee rendered satisfactory work for a total of 12 years and was not given an opportunity to be heard - Impugned order was quashed and employee given the right to continue his service.
[Paras 18, 20, 21, 24 & 25]

Article 226 of the Constitution of India on Regularisation - Courts are allowed to interfere if adhoc/causal/temporary appointments are continuously followed over a long period of time without any attempt to fill those posts on a regular basis.
[Para 17]
