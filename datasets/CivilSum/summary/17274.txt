Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(k) - Industrial dispute - Dispute regarding compassionate ground appointment - Dispute over non-employment of son of deceased workman - Competent union allowed to raise such disputes - Dispute referred to Industrial Tribunal considered industrial dispute based on Section 2(k) of the Industrial Disputes Act.
[Paras 7 and 8] B.
Section 2(k) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Industrial dispute - Definition - A dispute between an employer and their workmen concerning the non-employment of any person can be considered an industrial dispute - The beneficiary of the claim does not have to be an employer's workman at the time when the dispute is raised.
