Constitution of India, Article 311 - Police Act, 1861, Sections 7 and 12 - Misconduct - The punishing authority has the discretion to give the appropriate punishment for the proven misconduct.
However, the employee's misconduct should be grave and the punishment should be necessary to maintain discipline.
One employee, charged with 132 days of absence from duty without explanation, claimed to have been ill.
The punishment of removal from service was deemed to be too harsh and disproportionate to the charge.
The order of punishment was quashed and the authority was directed to reconsider and award a suitable punishment in proportion to the charge.
