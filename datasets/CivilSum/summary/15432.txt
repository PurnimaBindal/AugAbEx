Punjab Services Medical Attendance Rules, 1940, Rules, 3 and 4 - Reimbursement of outdoor treatment - Haryana Government employees can avail of free indoor treatment from PGI, but they cannot be denied reimbursement for an outdoor treatment.
The petitioner, suffering from Diabetic Nephropathy and IInd stage Renal Disease, a chronic condition, was unable to receive dialysis at PGI and opted for treatment at Chandigarh Medical Centre on their own expenses.
As a result, the petitioner is eligible for reimbursement of both indoor and outdoor medical treatments.
