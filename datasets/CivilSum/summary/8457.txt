Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Absorption - Disengagement - Pendency of suit before trial Court - Writ petitioner, who had been absorbed in Education Department as a teacher, was disengaged in another order as a FAT teacher.
In the Writ petition, the petitioner challenged this decision.
However, the court held that approaching the trial court directly would have been more appropriate instead of coming to the writ court.
Since the petitioner had an effective remedy available to pursue the matter before the trial court, the court decided not to intervene and dismissed the petition.
