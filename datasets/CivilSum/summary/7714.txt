The petitioner was terminated for being appointed after the cut-off date for regularisation of adhoc employees, but this was not improper since the termination was done in accordance with the Adhoc Appointment Regularisation Rules and the petitioner was given one month's salary.
[Paras 10, 11 and 14]  B.
The cut-off date for regularisation of adhoc employees in the UP Regulation of Ad hoc Appointments Rules was fixed to accommodate employees in accordance with need and vacancies and cannot be considered unreasonable.
