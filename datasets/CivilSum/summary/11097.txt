Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 41 - Temporary Government Servants Rules, 1975 - Absorption - Bonded Labour Rehabilitation Scheme - Petitioner, who was appointed as a project worker under the Bonded Labour Rehabilitation Scheme on a temporary basis, was later promoted to the post of Assistant Development Officer and continued serving for 13 years.
The sudden termination of the petitioner's service violated Article 14 of the Constitution Even though the Scheme was still ongoing in other districts, the petitioner's termination was quashed.
After some employees were absorbed, the petitioner was also entitled to be absorbed in any department of the government.
