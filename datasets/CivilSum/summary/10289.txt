As per Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol. II, Rule 3.17-A, Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226, a petitioner who has worked on an ad hoc basis in various government colleges before regular appointment is entitled to the benefit of ad hoc service towards qualifying service for pension.
Even though the authorities denied the fact, they failed to produce requested records.
Therefore, the petitioner's plea cannot be disbelieved and they are entitled to pension computed with the benefit of ad hoc service.
