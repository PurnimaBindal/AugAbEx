"Workmens Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30(1)(a) - Award - The appellant deposited the awarded amount with a Demand Draft and covering letter, which was received and receipted by the Commissioner's office.
Despite this, the High Court refused to accept it as a valid Certificate under the Act and dismissed the appeal.
However, as no specified form of Certificate is required, proof of deposit of compensation constitutes substantial compliance with Section 30 of the Act.
[Para 4]"
