Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Pension - Revision of - Petitioner claiming revision of pension due to special increments and higher standard scale upon completing certain years of service - Respondent stating that special increment and higher standard scale have already been granted for 8 and 10 years of service - Petitioner claiming to have completed 20 years of service but denied benefit of 18 and 20 years service - Court ordered further examination of completion of 20 years of service and allowed Petitioner to make a representation within 15 days for a proper decision to be made by the department within three months.
