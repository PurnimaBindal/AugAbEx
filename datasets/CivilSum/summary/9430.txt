Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Education - Attendance - Condonation of shortage of attendance - Shortage of attendance beyond a certain point cannot be excused - Promotions to the next semester are not dependent on passing a required number of subjects - Held, if a candidate has a shortage of attendance due to reasons beyond their control, the university can examine the feasibility of promoting them to the next semester.
