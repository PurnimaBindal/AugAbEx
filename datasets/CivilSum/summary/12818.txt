Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Selection - Opportunity of hearing - Petitioners alleged that the Government of Punjab's Education Department cancelled both departmental selection committees for teaching and non-teaching personnel without giving petitioners a chance to be heard.
The writ petitions were disposed of with a directive to the respondents to consider the representation that the petitioners would make within two weeks concerning the grievance that similarly situated petitioners were chosen in 1992, 1993, and 1994 by the Departmental Selection Committee without any distinguishing factors between selected individuals and the petitioners.
If the Respondents agree with the counsel's argument that there is no distinguishing feature, they will reconsider the order of 18.3.1992 that was challenged in writ petitions.
[Paras 4, 5 and 6]
