Constitution of India, Article 16 - Secretariat Subordinate Service Rules, Rules 10 and 12 - Department test - Seniority - Promotion - Petitioners must have passed Account Test (Lower) for promotion.
A probation period of three years has been fixed with no rule stating that a person who completed necessary test qualifications earlier must wait for promotion until the expiry of probation for a higher post.
Juniors promoted before seniors who cleared departmental tests later within the probationary period cannot claim promotion over their juniors.
The plea of the petitioners that their juniors should not have been promoted on a regular basis during the probationary period of the appointment is not tenable.
[Paras 8 and 10]
