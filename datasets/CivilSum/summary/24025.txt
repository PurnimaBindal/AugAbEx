Punjab Police Rules, 1934 (Haryana) - Selection to the post of Constable - High Court quashed the selection of 1600 posts of Constables due to lack of publicity and notification of vacancies - Appellants argue that the procedure has been used since 1934 and that there was sufficient publicity despite the lack of advertisement or notification to Employment Exchange - Some selected candidates had completed five years of service - High Court's judgment upheld due to correct approach to the problem - Directions given to complete the selection process within six months, allow current selectees to continue until fresh selection is made, and give preference to experienced selectees in the new selection process.
