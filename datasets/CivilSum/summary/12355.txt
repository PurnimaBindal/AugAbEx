Constitution of India, Article 309 - MP Fundamental Rules, Rule 56 - Compulsory retirement - Validity of - Screening Committee - The impugned order of compulsory retirement was based on the recommendations of a screening committee that had only two members instead of three, and one of them was not authorized to participate in the proceedings as a valid member of the committee - Therefore, no order of compulsory retirement could have been made on the basis of such a Screening Committee - Moreover, the entire screening process, which disposed of the cases of 559 officers, was conducted and completed in a single day, resembling a farce - Hence, it is invalid.
