The Tamil Nadu Administrative Tribunal increased the quota for deputations of Superintendents to become Tehsildars in the Tamil Nadu Revenue Subordinate Service, but was directed to review and create a different scheme.
The goal is to provide Secretariat staff a broader range of executive work opportunities as revenue officers are grouped together.
[Para 1]

Recruitment for the Tamil Nadu Revenue Subordinate Service as Tehsildars can happen via promotion from Deputy Tehsildars or deputation from Section Offices in the Secretariat and Superintendents in the office of the Board of Revenue (Land Revenue), according to the Tamil Nadu Revenue Subordinate Service Rules.
[Para 2]

The Government has the right to establish a policy to ensure efficiency and proper administration and to provide suitable channels of promotion, the Tribunal can't substitute its views for the views of the Government or direct a new policy, all of which is pursuant to Jurisdiction of Administrative Tribunal to issue directions.
Unless a provision of the Constitution clearly violated, the Tribunal is not authorized to set new policies or quotas.
The impugned order was set aside, and the Tribunal's applications were dismissed.
