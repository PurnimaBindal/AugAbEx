The employee failed to give notice in writing to her employer during pregnancy as required by Sections 6 and 12 of the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961.
The Labour Court denied relief on the basis that the employee was absent from work and that there was no termination.
The provision in question does require employees to give notice in writing during pregnancy or immediately after delivery to ensure that they receive maternity benefits without causing inconvenience to the establishment, but the petitioner did not take any steps in this regard.
The award further notes that this is not a case of termination.
