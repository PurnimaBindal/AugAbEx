All India Institute of Medical Sciences Recruitment Rules, 1981, Rule 17(B) - AIIMS Regulations, 1981, Item No 21(ii) - Appointment - Waiting list - Regular Appointment - The respondent, who was on the waiting list for recruitment to the temporary post of Assistant Professor, was appointed on a regular basis.
The petitioner claimed that the fact that the respondent was on the waiting list was not brought to the notice of the governing body or competent authority for making regular appointments.
The petitioner also claimed that the waiting list was cancelled.
The court found that the waiting list was never cancelled, or at least such cancellation was neither known nor made known to the selection committee.
Until the meeting of the governing body, the waiting list was not cancelled and there was no occasion to invite cancellation of the waiting list.
It was not within the province of the selection committee to cancel the selection list; its job was merely to select and make recommendations but not to cancel recommendations already made.
A resolution was not put before the governing body or before the President and remained a waste paper.
The appointment of the respondent is temporary and in a temporary post.
The petition was dismissed.
[Paras 21, 22, 30, 32 & 34]
