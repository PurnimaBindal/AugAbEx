The Prevention of Corruption Act, Section 5(2) and the Indian Penal Code, Section 161, along with Articles 14, 16 and 311(2) of the Constitution of India and Rule 13 of the Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, were taken into account in a case where an individual was convicted of corruption and sentenced to one year rigorous imprisonment.
The mandate of Article 311(2) was found not to apply in such circumstances, and due to the serious nature of the offence, the only valid punishment was dismissal.
Any sympathy shown towards the individual in such cases was deemed to be opposed to public interest.
These findings were made in Paragraphs 6, 7, 10 and 11 of the text.
