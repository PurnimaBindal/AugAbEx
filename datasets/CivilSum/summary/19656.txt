Constitution of India, Article 16 - Transfer - The Petitioner, who joined Osmania University as a Lecturer in the Department of Chemistry, was promoted as Reader and transferred in the exigencies of service as Reader incharge of the PG Centre at Mirzapur - The transfer is valid - Plea that the Petitioner shall be posted only in the earmarked post in the University College of Science and that neither the post nor incumbent shall be transferred to any of the constituent colleges except in accordance with the choice of the incumbent is not tenable.
