Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 25F and 25H - Retrenchment - Regularisation - If an employee has been reinstated on the basis of an award by the Labour Court, and his/her position is then retrenched, the post cannot be filled by direct open recruitment.
This is because the award of the Labour Court cannot be avoided in the name of regular selection.
The State's policy of regularisation for ad hoc/casual employees must be considered so that there are no allegations of unfair labour practices.
Therefore, the authorities cannot proceed to fill the post through regular selection unless they have determined that the employee is not entitled to be considered for regularisation under the State's policies.
The retrenchment order has been set aside.
[Paras 9 to 14]
