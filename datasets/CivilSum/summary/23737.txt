The Central Government is the appropriate government for Air India Statutory Corporation under the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act of 1970, and the government's notification prohibiting contract labour for certain services was within its powers.
Additionally, the Central Government cannot reverse the abolition of Contract Labour once it has been abolished for specific services.
