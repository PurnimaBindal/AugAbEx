Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - State Bank of India (Subsidiary Banks) Act, 1959, Section 50(2) - Equal pay for equal work - Employees of Subsidiary Banks of State Bank of India are requesting equal benefits of service, terminal, and pension benefits as are available to the employees of the State Bank of India, on the basis that all its subsidiaries are under the direct control and management of State Bank of India - However, the employees of the subsidiary banks cannot be considered employees of the State Bank of India for all purposes, as each subsidiary bank has its own operations, staff and terms of service.
The employees of the subsidiary banks are not entitled to claim the same benefits as the employees of State Bank of India.
Additionally, there is a difference in work, operations and responsibilities between the two and the subsidiary banks are already providing similar benefits.
Consequently, there is no discrimination, and employees of one establishment cannot claim parity with others - The petition is dismissed.
