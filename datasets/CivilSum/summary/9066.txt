Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(oo), 2(oo)(bb), 25-F - Retrenchment/Resignation/Termination - Under the Service Rules, employees must give three months' notice or deposit an amount equal to three months' salary to resign from service.
The petitioner wrote to higher authorities claiming that his resignation was obtained forcibly.
The committee did not condone payment of three months' salary or take notice of this fact at the time of passing the resolution accepting the resignation.
These circumstances demonstrate that the resignation was not voluntary but was obtained by force, tantamounting to termination of service without following the mandatory provisions of the Industrial Disputes Act.
The petitioner is, therefore, entitled to be reinstated with full back wages and all consequential reliefs.
The petition is allowed with costs of Rs. 5,000/-.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 11 - Findings of fact/Writ jurisdiction - The writ court is not usually inclined to interfere with the findings of fact reached by Industrial Tribunals.
However, where the findings are perverse or the result of misreading and misquoting oral and documentary evidence, the writ court has a duty to interfere to further the cause of justice.
