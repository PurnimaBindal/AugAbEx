Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 and 227 - Medical Reimbursement - Kidney transplant surgery from private hospital not recognised by Haryana Government - Petitioner allowed 75% reimbursement in relaxation of instructions - Court rules that the order is not illegal or arbitrary - However, denial of reimbursement as outdoor patient for follow-up treatment after kidney transplant is not justified - Respondents instructed to release full amount of medical expenses for outdoor follow-up treatment.
