Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39-A - Freedom Fighters Pension Scheme - Samman Pension - Despite being in an earning capacity and only applying for the pension in 1981, the petitioner was recommended by the State Government and Deputy Commissioner of the Distt.
for grant of the Samman Pension under the Freedom Fighters Pension Scheme, 1972.
However, the Government rejected his case without proper consideration.
As it was evident from the documents that the petitioner was entitled to the Samman Pension, the Government was directed to grant him pension with effect from the date of his application.
