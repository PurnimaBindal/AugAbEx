According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India and Regulations 67(c) and 68(2)(i) of the State Bank of Hyderabad (Officers) Service Regulations, 1979, it is important to supply enquiry reports to the individual being subject to disciplinary proceedings, as this is necessary for natural justice.
This rule applies to any type of establishment, whether government or non-government, public or private.
Even if the Rules governing the situation expressly prohibit the furnishing of a copy of the report or remain silent about the matter, the non-supplying of the report is unlawful and violates natural justice principles.
B When it comes to dismissal and removal from service, courts and tribunals will direct the supply of the enquiry report to the individual and give them the chance to explain how non-supplying it was unfair.
Only then will proceedings be discontinued and reinstatement be ordered subject to satisfaction.
In other instances, where dismissal or removal from service is not involved, courts will set aside the penalty and enquire into the question of unfairness associated with non-supplying the report before inflicting the penalty.
