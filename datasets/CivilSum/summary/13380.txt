Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Reimbursement - Outdoor treatment - Despite being unable to secure a PGI admission for her operation due to non-availability of beds, the petitioner was required to undergo a further operation and certain tests, including CT scanning, ECGs, and purchasing equipment and medicines essential for surgery as prescribed by PGI.
Although the reimbursement was denied for tests, medicine, and equipment bought as an outdoor patient, the court held that these expenses were entailed for the continuation of treatment, and the petitioner was eligible for compensation following the procedure.
