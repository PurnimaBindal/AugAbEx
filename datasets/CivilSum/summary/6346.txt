In the case of termination challenges as being illegal, under the Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10, the mere acceptance of dues sent to the workmen in due course is not reason enough to reject the referral of the dispute to the labour court.
This does not count as a compromise between the workman and the management, according to PS Saini, Advocate.
Furthermore, with the introduction of Section 11-A in the I.D.
Act, the government may no longer refuse a reference to the labour court for cases of dismissal, termination and removal solely on the grounds that the employer has conducted an inquiry and the workman was found guilty of misconduct.
Instead, it is the role of the labour court to determine whether the inquiry was fair and whether the punishment was proportionate to the established misconduct, as per PS Saini, Advocate.
