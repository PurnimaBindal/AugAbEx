Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 138 and 139 - Seniority - The Deputy Inspector General of Police is responsible for determining seniority of Head Constables based on Range and not state-wise or by the Inspector General of Police.
[Para 3] B. Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 138 and 139 - Training - Eligible constables must be deputed to Intermediate School Course by the Deputy Inspector General of Police of each Range based on seniority.
