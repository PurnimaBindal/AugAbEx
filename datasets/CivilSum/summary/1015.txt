Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 3(1)(v) - Jurisdiction/Allotment of Government accommodation/Service matter - The transfer of cases related to the allotment of Government accommodation to a Central Government employee is a service matter, and thus, it falls under the jurisdiction of the Administrative Tribunal.
Therefore, a writ cannot be maintained, and if filed after the Tribunal has been constituted, it cannot be transferred to the Tribunal and must be dismissed.
