Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 14 - Appointment of dependents of deceased employees under ex-gratia scheme - The petitioner's application for appointment was turned down due to not being made within the stipulated 3-year period after the death of his father while in service on August 13, 1982, when he was still a minor - However, the court held that the instructions cannot be so mandatory as to deprive the dependents of their right to get compassionate employment - The period prescribed in the instructions shall not affect the dependent's right to get employment after they have attained majority and acquired some educational qualifications - Petition allowed.
