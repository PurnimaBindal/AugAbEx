Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1970, Section 19 - State Bank of Bikaner and Jaipur (Employees) Pension Regulations, 1995, Regulation 29(1) - Pension scheme on voluntary retirement - Introduction of pension on voluntary retirement prospectively cannot be considered as unreasonable or irrational - Petitioners who applied for voluntary retirement prior to cut off date cannot be treated equally - They are not entitled to pension.
