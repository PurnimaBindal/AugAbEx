Workman's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 4, 5, 17 - Compensation - Claim of - Agreement - Commissioner dismissed claim as settled on basis of agreement of parties placed before him - The appellant sought recalling of the order on the ground of fraud - The Commissioner set aside the previous order upon challenges - The remedy to move for recalling of order on basis of newly discovered facts amounting to fraud cannot be foreclosed as no court or tribunal is powerless to recall its own order if the same was wangled through fraud or misrepresentation which affect the very basis of the claim.

Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Supervisory Jurisdiction - Interference - The power conferred on the High Court under Art 226 and 227 of the Constitution is to advance justice and not to thwart it - The High Court is not to look out merely to pick an error of law through the academic angle, but to see whether injustice has resulted on account of any erroneous interpretation of law.
