Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Delay in filling a vacancy cannot be a reason for quashing an appointment.
The High Court's statement that the University did not explain the delay in filling the vacancy is not a valid ground for setting aside the appointment.
The University has provided a satisfactory explanation in a detailed affidavit.
Furthermore, if delay is considered a ground for quashing appointments, no appointment can be made after the delay period.
[Para 6]

Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - High Court cannot sit in appeal over Selection Committee's decision.
It is not the court's function to hear appeals over Selection Committee decisions or scrutinize candidates' relative merits.
Selection Committee decisions can only be interfered with based on limited grounds, such as illegality or procedure irregularities vitiating the selection.
The University constituted the Committee in compliance with relevant statutes, and the Committee consisted of experts who selected the candidates after reviewing all relevant material.
The High Court exceeded its jurisdiction by sitting in appeal over the Selection Committee's decision and setting it aside based on its own assessment of candidate merits.
