Constitution of India, Article 12, 16 and 226 - Equal pay for equal work - Daily wage workers are entitled to receive wages and salary the same as regular workers who perform similar duties - Even if a daily wage worker is not punctual, they cannot be punished due to their given status - Authorities are ordered to set the salary of the petitioners and other daily wage workers on regular pay scales with effect from the date of filing writ petitions and to pay arrears of salary due as a difference since that date.
