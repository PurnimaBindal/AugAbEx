All India Services (Death-cum-Retirement Benefits) Rules, 1958, Rule 16 - General Clauses Act, Section 21 - Natural justice - Extension of service granted to a member of IPS by the governor of Uttar Pradesh was cancelled on the grounds of violations of the model code of conduct issued by the Election Commission.
The incumbent's right under the extension order had not vested, so there was no necessity for a hearing before cancellation.
Since the order of extension did not create any right, the cancellation order could not have withdrawn any such right.
As a result, there was no violation of the rules of natural justice, and the principle of estoppel was not applicable.
