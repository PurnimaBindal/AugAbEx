Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Standing orders 27th September, 1988 - Seasonal workman - Work performed by some respondents during the crushing reason - Labour Court considered them permanent workmen and declared them permanent - High Court dismissed the writ petition against the award - However, the case is not about fitment depending on the work but about promoting to fill vacancies - This function is a managerial function and cannot be executed by the Labour Court - Therefore, the judgement of the Labour Court and High Court are invalidated.
