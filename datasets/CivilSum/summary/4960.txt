Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 8(3)(b) - Termination - During period of probation - If an employee is terminated on account of being absent without leave or engaging in immoral activities that are harmful to the mental health of young students, and if the order of termination casts a stigma on the employee, it is the responsibility of the employer to conduct an investigation and provide the employee with an opportunity to defend themselves.
