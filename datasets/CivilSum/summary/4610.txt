Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 300A - Rajasthan Civil Services Rules, Rule 268 - HH(2) - Grant of ex-gratia - A constable who suffered a cardiac arrest and died due to service strain from devotion to his duty while on duty is entitled to one increment of higher responsibility.
The immediate act that led to the accident should not be considered as foreign to the sphere of his duties.
His widow is eligible for an ex-gratia payment of Rs. 50,000/- and two group insurance policy coverage transactions worth Rs. 1,00,000/-.
[Paras 11, 13 and 14]
