Whether or not the disciplinary authority has the power to withhold payment of arrears of salary during a period of suspension as punishment- one of the modes of punishment as indicated by the rules- if the charges have been proven?
The disciplinary authority has the right to impose appropriate punishment if the charges have been proven, and withholding of payment of arrears of salary is one of them.
The disciplinary authority in this case had rightly exercised its power, and the Civil Court did not have the jurisdiction to substitute the punishment imposed by them as it is not a Court of appeal in a Civil suit.
