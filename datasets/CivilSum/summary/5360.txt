Medical Reimbursement - In certain situations, an employee may have to seek medical treatment from a nearby hospital in case of an emergency - Denying a claim for reimbursement solely based on the hospital not being recognized by the state government would be unfair and unjustified.

Medical Reimbursement - If it is established that the plaintiff received medical treatment after an accident, the claim cannot be rejected on the grounds of hospital non-recognition.
