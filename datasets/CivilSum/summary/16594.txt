Constitution of India, Article 21 - Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of Deceased Government Employees Rules, 2003, Rule 3(d)(ii) - Compassionate appointment - Dependants of deceased government employees must meet eligibility requirements to claim compassionate appointment.
The qualifying service of the deceased must include ad hoc service followed by regular service.
The Rule is intended as social welfare legislation.
Therefore, it requires liberal interpretation.
A hyper-technical interpretation would defeat the Rule's benevolent object.
As such, Rule 3(d)(ii) must include the phrase "regular service" to be read down.
The plain meaning of the Rule is that an employee should have served the Govt. for at least for three years.
