When it comes to pension and pensionary benefits, government employees must count their entire service in accordance with the rules for the purpose of receiving these benefits.
This ruling was upheld in a suit for declaration, as decreed by the courts below.
The Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 100 was followed in making this decision.
[Para 6]
Pension and pensionary benefits for government servants are considered property and are not at the discretion of the Department to grant or deny as a matter of bounty.
[Para 6]
Pension and pensionary benefits are recurring and therefore not subject to the three-year limitation period.
[Para 6]
The question of limitation doesn't come into play for pension and pensionary benefits because they are considered a recurring cause of action.
