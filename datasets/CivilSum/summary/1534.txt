Constitution of India, Articles 8, 14, 19(1)(G), 15(3) and 31(C) - Factories Act, 1948, Section 66(1)(b) - Constitutionality of Provision - The Madras High Court has determined that Section 66(1)(b) of the Act, which prohibits the employment of women in factories during the night shift, is unconstitutional, and therefore, it has been struck down.
