The terms and conditions of a brochure for admission in education cannot be merely declaratory if they use peremptory language.
Instead, they must be treated as mandatory, and following them is essential to maintain fairness in a competitive entrance examination.
Giving unrestricted discretion to an individual to waive or dilute the stipulated conditions of the brochure will introduce discrimination, arbitrariness, and unfairness.
Submission of complete applications is a prerequisite for valid acceptance and consideration of an application for seat allotment, and the brochure itself has the force of law.
