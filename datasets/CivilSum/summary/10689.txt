Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 300A - CCS (Pension) Rules 1972, Rule 54(6), Rule 54(12)(c) - Family Pension - Permanent Disablement- Even though the Govt. did not deny the petitioner's disablement, the Govt. servant did not declare the said disability during service thus her claim was rejected.
However, the pension rules provide the right to lifetime payment of family pension to disabled child - Hence, the disabled child is entitled to family pension even though disability was not declared during service.
