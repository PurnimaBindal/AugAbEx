The Prevention of Corruption Act, Sections 7, 13(2) and 13(1)(d) and Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16 and 226 lead to the suspension of Respondent DSP of Police, who was charged with accepting a bribe, and a chargesheet was filed against them.
However, the suspension was set aside by the tribunal citing that the chargesheet had been filed, so the grounds for continuing with the suspension were no longer present.
The decision was overruled, and it was determined that the respondent was still under suspension until further notice, as the cause had not ended even after the filing of the chargesheet.
The appeal was allowed.
