Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education/Admission - Equivalence of diploma and certificates - Petitioners were truthful in their admission and did not hide any facts - The objection from the authority that the Certificate from the Board of Adult Education and Training, New Delhi, is not equivalent to Matriculation came after one year of admission - Equivalence of qualifications must be decided before admission, not after - Revoking recognition of a diploma or certificate cannot make past admissions invalid.
