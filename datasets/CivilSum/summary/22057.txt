Constitution of India, Article 32 - Education - Admission - A successful candidate for medical college admission was unable to report in time to obtain admission - An admission letter from 1971990 required the petitioner to receive admission by 25.7.1990, but was alleged to have been received on 481990 - An envelope was not filed in the received state, and no certificate was obtained from the Post Office regarding the actual date of delivery - Delay in delivery of the admission letter was caused by the candidate altering their address - No illegal act took place in refusing admission.
