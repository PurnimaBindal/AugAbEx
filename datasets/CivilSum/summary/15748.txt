Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Services Medical Attendance Rules, 1940 - Medical reimbursement - The petitioner was denied reimbursement of medical expenses incurred due to kidney transplant treatment as an outdoor patient, despite being granted fixed medical allowance for outdoor treatment according to government instructions.
In response, the respondents were directed to reimburse the expenses incurred by the petitioner as an outpatient, citing the 1998(3) R.S.J.
705 case.
