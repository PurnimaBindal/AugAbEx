Section 33-A of the Industrial Dispute Act, 1947 deals with the termination of services.
If there is an industrial dispute and a settlement has been reached between the parties but is later violated by the management, a complaint can be filed under this section.
In a specific case where a petitioner's services were terminated during the pendency of an industrial dispute, but prior to the settlement, the Labour Court dismissed the complaint stating that the termination was not maintainable.
Further, it was found that there was no change in the service conditions during the pendency of the reference to attract the provisions of section 33-A The petitioner may still approach the Labour Court to conclude the reference.
