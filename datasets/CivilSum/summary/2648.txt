Punjab Town Improvement Trust Surcharge Rules, 1961, Rule 4(1) - and Amended Rules of Haryana Government, 1978, Rule 4(3) - Concerning Retirement - Regarding Disciplinary proceedings - The proceedings can be launched within four years of the employee's loss or misappropriation, or up to one year after the employee ceases to work for the Trust.
The Petitioner being one whom retired on 31101995 and the loss caused to Trust by petitioner in the year 1976 - Disciplinary proceedings against petitioner for loss caused to Trust by petitioner in 1976 were initiated through the impugned order on 6.3.2006, which was about 20 years after the alleged loss and about 9-1/2 years after the petitioner's retirement - Departmental proceedings were quashed since they were initiated after a long delay - The Order of Financial Commissioner was also quashed, as it violated all the cannons of Limitation Law.
SS Seera v. State of Punjab in CWP No. 3404 of 1992; NS Brar v. State of Punjab in CWP No. 17429 of 1991 and AIR 1990 SC 1308 were cited.
