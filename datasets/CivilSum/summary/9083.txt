Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act states that Labour Court must record its findings after evaluating oral and documentary evidence.
Moreover, if the evidence has not been discussed, the award is open to being set aside with the case being remanded.
[Para 1]
The Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 11 mention that the worker would be entitled to full back wages until the date of the Award of the Labour Court, if the misconduct which led to their termination is established in the Labour Court for the first time.
This case for full back wages is based on the ruling followed in AIR 1980 SC 1896.
