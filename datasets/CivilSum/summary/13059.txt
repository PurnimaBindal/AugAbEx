Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Regulations 54, 58, 59 and 60 - Misconduct - Departmental Enquiry - Imposition of penalty - A charge-sheet can only be issued under regulation 58 for imposing major penalty.
Disciplinary authority cannot impose minor penalty without holding an enquiry as defined and examining the reply to charge-sheet.
