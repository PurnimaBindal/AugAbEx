Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Regularisation of services - The Regularisation policy of 731996, as modified via the letter dated 1831996 of the Haryana Government, stipulates the qualifications for eligibility.
One such requirement is that a daily wager should complete three years of service to obtain regularisation.
Subsequent or earlier breaks that may exist become irrelevant under this policy.
Even the breaks during the relevant three years of service can be ignored if it was attributable to reasons beyond the control of the employee or the employer.
However, an unexplained break exceeding thirty days will disqualify the employee from being regularised.
[Paras 7 & 8]
