Punjab Police Rules 1934, Rule 13.10, List E - Promotion to the rank of Sub-Inspector - Confirmation as ASI - Deemed confirmation - If the competent authority fails to pass an order after considering the entire service record, there is no deemed confirmation as per the rule.
However, the consideration is mandatory and ASI's must be sent for Uppor School Course in their respective seniority, regardless of confirmation status.
The judgement in ASI Gurmit Singh & others v. The State of Punjab & others C.W.P.
No. 3548 of 2003, decided on 2242003, is followed.
