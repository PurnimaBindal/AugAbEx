The Constitution of India, particularly Articles 14, 21, and 12, states that equal pay for equal work and minimum wages must be enforced.
A woman, who has been working as a helper in the Health Department of the State since 1985, has been receiving only Rs. 50/- per month over the last 17 years.
As per the law, the State is obligated to pay a citizen wages for their services and not less than the prescribed minimum wages.
Depriving an individual of minimum wages for work done amounts to depriving them of the right to livelihood under Article 21, and the State cannot deny someone their means of earning a living for a meaningful life.
[Paras 3 & 4]
