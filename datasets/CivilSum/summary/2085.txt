Teachers in government schools in Haryana and Punjab are entitled to higher pay scales upon obtaining higher qualifications such as a Master's degree, regardless of their position.
This is supported by cases such as Chaman Lal and others v. State of Haryana, AIR 1987 SC 1621 and Bhagwan Dutt Sharma and others v. State of Haryana etc., ILR 1988(2) Pb.
