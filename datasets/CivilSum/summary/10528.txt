Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Ex-servicemen - Ad hoc appointment - Examination - Regularisation - Ad hoc appointment of petitioner - Ex-servicemen as clerks - Petitioners, who were unable to pass the test for recruitment of clerks on a regular basis held by SSS Board, cannot raise any grievance regarding holding of the test.
This is consistent with the judgment of Punjab and Haryana High Court in Naib Subedar Babu Lal Nagina v. State of Haryana 1992(3) SCT 565 (P&H) (DB) : 1992 (4) SLR 470 (P&H) and Moji Ram v. State of Haryana, Civil Writ Petition No. 2622 of 1992 decided on 24492.
The writ petition has been dismissed due to lack of merit.
However, the respondent-authorities are not barred from examining the eligibility of petitioners for regularisation in conformity with the Rules and guidelines issued in relation to the same.
