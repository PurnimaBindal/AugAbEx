Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pay and allowances - Posting on officiating basis - Despite being entitled to receive higher pay and allowances for his officiating position, the petitioner was not allowed to draw them - The petitioner was appointed on a regular basis as a carpenter for an existing vacancy - Under policy, a person can officiate in a higher post for more than 21 days, with approval from the Head of the Department - The petitioner received a letter of appointment with the competent authority's approval - Since the petitioner had fulfilled the duties of the higher post and was appointed to it, he is entitled to its pay scale - The respondents are ordered to pay the petitioner the salary and allowances he is entitled to as carpenter.
