Constitution of India, Articles 16, 309 - Rajasthan Police Service Rules, 1954, Rule 14 - Appointment - Physical fitness - The appellant was appointed based on the opinion of the second Medical Board.
A Single Judge directed re-examination by an independent Medical Board.
However, the second Board, consisting of seven experts, agreed with the first Board's view.
It was held that the courts should not substitute the Board's opinion, even if there are two possible interpretations.
Therefore, the directions given by the Single Judge were not sustainable.
