Punjab Agricultural Produce Markets Act, 1961, Sections 8 and 11 - Punjab Market Committees (Class III) Service Rules, 1989, Rule 8 Appendix `B' - Promotion - Promotion to the post of Accountant - Clerks are not eligible for promotion to the post of Accountant under the Rules - Prior to the Rules, clerks were eligible for promotion to the post of Accountant only if they were found suitable - If the petitioner was not suitable and eligible under the Rules, a suitable and eligible person from another Committee should be appointed - Petitioner cannot claim the post of Accountant while working as a clerk.
