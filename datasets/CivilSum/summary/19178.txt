The Constitution of India addresses termination of service due to a forged marks-sheet and certificate of having passed the Praveshika examination in the first division.
However, if the petitioner has submitted an explanation with a certificate from the Principal and has also passed a qualifying test for promotion, the termination of service for seeking promotion is considered arbitrary and will be quashed.
In such cases, reinstatement will be directed.
[Paras 11-16]
