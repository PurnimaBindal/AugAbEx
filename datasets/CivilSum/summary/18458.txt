Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule II Item 6 - Absence from duty - Termination - After 13 years of serving of charge sheet, an enquiry was conducted - The period of absence had already been regularized - The petitioner had been exonerated in another enquiry for absence - It was held that since the entire period of absence had already been regularized, there was no justification for proceeding with the enquiry based on the charge sheet issued 13 years back, especially in light of the exoneration in the earlier enquiry.
