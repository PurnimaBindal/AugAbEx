Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - The DPC that assessed the petitioner's eligibility for promotion was conducted in June 1990 - However, due to an ongoing departmental inquiry regarding chargesheets from March 10, 1986, and February 20, 1990, the decision was kept in a sealed cover - The petitioner contends that they were cleared of any wrongdoing in both inquiries and that any subsequent chargesheet should not affect the DPC's recommendation - The respondents are directed to open the sealed cover, and if the petitioner is recommended for promotion, they should be promoted accordingly with all consequential benefits.
