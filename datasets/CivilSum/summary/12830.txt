Constitution of India, Article 14 - Selection/Select List - Creating a select list aims to avoid repeating the selection process soon after - If a vacancy opens before the next selection, an appointment needs to be made from the select list - Dismissing the idea that the select list is invalid, if a candidate from the select list declines the offer, the next in line should get the appointment but a person who was not even a candidate for selection cannot be appointed - The petitioner who was on the select list for Assistant Advocate General deserved to be hired.
