Regularization of Casual Labour was denied on the grounds of not being engaged prior to the cut-off date mentioned in the circular.
The case was examined on the direction of Ld Single Bench in an earlier petition.
However, since the consideration was done in a one-sided way without considering the merits of the case, the rejection order has been nullified.
The case has been sent back for wholesome and due consideration.
