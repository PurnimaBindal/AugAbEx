Constitution of India, Articles 14, 15, 16, 38, 234 and 309 - Punjab Civil Services (Judicial Branch) Rules, Rules 7 and 8 - Reservation - In order to ensure equality and enable the less privileged groups to compete on an equal footing with others, certain relaxations and other measures are necessary - Groups that cannot compete with the members of other communities or groups in terms of absolute equality because of historical or other reasons must be given an opportunity to compete on equal terms - However, it should be noted that the aim is to achieve equality of opportunity for public services or employment.
