The appointment of one dependent family member of a deceased employee through compassionate appointment is governed by the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16.
The policy of granting such appointments is limited, and the concession can only be granted within three years of the employee's death.
However, this policy does not apply when the deceased has left behind infants who have to achieve educational qualifications to become eligible for appointment.
It is also not a valid reason to deny the concession merely because the original applicant got employment at their own efforts while the application was pending.
Under such circumstances, the original applicant can be replaced by other dependent issues.
