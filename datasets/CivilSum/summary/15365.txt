The Constitution of India, Article 16 - Individuals who filed the petition were not promoted, while their juniors were promoted - The state has been instructed to review the case of the individuals who filed the petition - If it is discovered that individuals who are junior to those who filed the petition have been promoted, those who filed the petition must also be promoted from the date that the individuals who are junior to them were promoted, on the condition that they meet the requisite qualifications and are deemed fit for promotion.
