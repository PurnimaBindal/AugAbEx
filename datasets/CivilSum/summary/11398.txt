Section 11 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Section 22 of the Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provisions) Act, 1985 were invoked in a dispute between a company and its workers.
The government referred the dispute to the Labour Court and the management claimed that it was a sick company registered with the Board for Industrial and Financial Reconstruction.
The Labour Court stayed the proceedings under Section 22 and this order was upheld.
The ruling stated that Section 22 would apply to proceedings under the Industrial Disputes Act as well, as they are initiated to enforce the rights of workers.
Additionally, Section 22 prohibits the recovery of any dues from a company declared as sick, including through a lawsuit.
