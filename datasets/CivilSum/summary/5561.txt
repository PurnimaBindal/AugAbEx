Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Tamil Nadu Electricity Board Employees Discipline and Appeal Regulations, Regulation 8(b) - Compulsory retirement - Impugned order quashed - Delinquent officer was denied his right to submit a further written statement of defence despite being entitled to one after the enquiry - Such action flagged a violation of Regulation 8(b), which mandates a written statement post-enquiry - Principles of natural justice disregarded - Petitioner to be reinstated in service.
