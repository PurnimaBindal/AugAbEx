Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Co-operative Department (State Service) Class II, Rules, 1978, Rule 3 - Government merged Inspectors Grade I and Grade II cadres by abolishing 200 Grade II posts - Seniority of merged Grade II Inspectors to be fixed below existing Grade I Inspectors - Pay not re-fixed for this reason - Grade I Inspectors who were already in the cadre have no right affected - Power to merge and abolish posts within a cadre falls under the government's capability to increase or decrease cadre strength - Such actions can occur in the interest of service and public interest, even if it affects one's promotion opportunities - Notification upheld.
