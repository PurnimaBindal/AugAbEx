Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Delay and laches - Second representation - Respondent made a second representation for change of seniority after 16 years following the rejection of his first representation - Petitioner was considered senior to him throughout - No appeal was viable under the Rules - The delayed representation should have been dismissed on the basis of delay and laches - Upholding the acceptance of such a delayed representation is not reasonable.
