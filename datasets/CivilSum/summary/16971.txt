Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Bihar Agricultural University Act, 1988, Section 25 - Promotion - Fixation of pay-scale - Petitioner applied for the recruitment of the Post of Deputy Director, Administration based on the UGC pay scale as per the advertisement - Administrative Staff must be given the UGC pay scale as per the directions of the Central Govt. - It was held that the petitioner is entitled to the UGC payscale.
