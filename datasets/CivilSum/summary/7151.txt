Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Rayalaseema Grameena Bank (Staff) Service Regulations 1980, Regulation 13(3) - Probation - Fixation of seniority - According to Regulation 13(3) of the Rayalaseema Grameena Bank (Staff) Service Regulation, when an employee's probation is extended, their seniority must be calculated as the last person in the same batch.
Seniority cannot be determined from the date of regularisation.
