Based on Articles 14/16 of the Constitution of India, JBT teachers who acquire higher qualifications must receive correspondingly higher pay.
A petitioner who has acquired a BA, BEd.
qualification while in service is entitled to receive the pay scale of a BEd.
teacher from the date they acquired the qualification, regardless of when they were adjusted against the post of Master.
This assertion draws upon references to ILR 1988(2) Punjab and Haryana 246 and AIR 1987 SC 1621.
