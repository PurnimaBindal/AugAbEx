Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 12 - Contempt petition - The Respondent deliberately disobeyed a Court directive that required them to grant LTC to the petitioner and their wife within 15 days of issuing the order.
The Director assured the Court that the petitioner's wife, who was also employed in the same Department, would receive LTC This benefit would include the petitioner's status while in office, and the Court accepted this.
In addition, both an unconditional and unqualified apology were tendered and accepted, and the notice of contempt against the Respondent was dropped.
The Respondent was discharged.
[Paras 3 and 4] B Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 12 - Costs - Contempt petition - The petitioner had to file multiple petitions to get the order passed by DB to be complied with, causing them undue hardship.
As a result, the respondents were ordered to pay costs.
