The Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16, and 226, the Dock Workers (Regulation of Employment) Act of 1948's Section 2(6), 2(c), and 3, and the Contract Labour (Abolition and Regulation) Act of 1970's Section 10 deal with regularisation of services for contract labourers.
A group of petitioner-workers, who have worked as Cargo Pool workers in the Haldia Dock Complex area for 11 years, have claimed regularisation, but there is no notification prohibiting the employment of contract labourers in the complex.
However, even if the petitioners have worked for over 240 days, they are not entitled to regularisation.
