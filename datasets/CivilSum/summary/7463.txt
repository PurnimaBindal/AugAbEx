Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Dispute - Refusal of reference by appropriate government - Validity - Based on the fact that the government did not initially consider the dispute suitable for adjudication - Ruled that the appropriate government is not authorized to impartially resolve the dispute as per the Section 10(1) of the Act, as its role is purely administrative - Ordered to make a reference.
