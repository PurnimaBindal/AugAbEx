Constitution of India, Article 16 - Haryana Vidhan Sabha Secretariat Service Rules, 1981, Rule 8 - Appointment - To become Deputy Secretary of Haryana Vidhan Sabha, rule requires applicant to be a Law Graduate with at least three years of experience as an Under Secretary.
Constitution of India, Article 16 - Haryana Vidhan Sabha Secretariat Service Rules, 1981, Rule 3 - Appointment - Eligibility for appointment to the post of Deputy Secretary is being a Law graduate having 3 years of experience as Under Secretary.
The appointment can also be made by Secretary to Speaker by relaxing the rules, who holds the rank higher than Under Secretary.
Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab Reorganisation Act, 1966, Section 82(6) - Creation of post - New posts in Haryana Vidhan Sabha Secretariat can be created by State, post reorganisation, if they are in line with the rules.
