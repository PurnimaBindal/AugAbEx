Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Sections 15(2) and 15(3) - Jurisdiction - Writ petitions filed by petitioner-employees in Municipal Corporations, against orders transferring them from their current workplace to another Municipal Corporation in the State - Since no notification has been given under Section 15(2) and (3) of the Act, the State Tribunal does not have jurisdiction over these service matters and the High Court's jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution is not excluded.
