Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Selection - Post of Headmaster - Eligibility - Correspondence course - Notification listing recognized Universities for appointment - Petitioner received a master's degree through a correspondence course from Himachal Pradesh University which was not recognized - Petitioner ineligible for the post without a recognized degree - No relief for petitioner without challenging the notification through an independent writ.
