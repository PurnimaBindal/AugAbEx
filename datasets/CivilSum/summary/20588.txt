The Indian Administrative Service (Regulation of Seniority) Rules, 1954, Rule 3(3)(C) as well as the Proviso to Rule 3(3)(c), the Indian Administrative Services (Recruitment) Rules, 1965, Rule 4 and Rule 8, all deal with seniority between state and non-state civil service officers.
Non-state officers are said to have less administrative experience than their state counterparts and thus their length of service cannot be equated with state officers for the purposes of seniority.
The determination of the respondent's year of allotment as 1977 was made according to applicable rules and the Tribunal was wrong to quash the order.
