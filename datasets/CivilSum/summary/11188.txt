According to Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, a petitioner who was appointed as a Constable and later promoted to Head Constable in the Executive Clerical Cadre decided to transfer back to District Police.
By doing so, he gave up his lien for all future times from Head Constable to Constable.
In 1981, the petitioner made the request to be transferred to District Police when his seniority was not high enough, and therefore was not granted the transfer.
Once his turn came, he was transferred to District Police.
Despite his argument, the writ petition was dismissed and he could not claim a transfer to the General Police Cadre in 1982 due to his low seniority.
