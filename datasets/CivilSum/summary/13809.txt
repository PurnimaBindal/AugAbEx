Haryana Municipal Services (Integration, Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1982, Rules 8 & 12 - Integration of existing regular employees - Seniority - For integration of existing Municipal Councils employees, only continuous and regular service on the post will be considered, ad hoc or temporary service before regular appointment will not be counted for seniority.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and laches - Seniority - Objections - Any tentative seniority issued subsequently to settle the seniority of new entrants will not provide a fresh cause of action to existing employees once seniority in the cadre has been finalized.
Therefore, any Writ petitions would be dismissed for delay and laches as the writ court will not exercise its jurisdiction to disrupt previously settled rights.
