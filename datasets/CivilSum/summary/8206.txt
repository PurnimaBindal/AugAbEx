In accordance with Section 2(oo)(bb) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, non-renewal of a contract of service cannot be deemed illegal retrenchment or unfair labor practice even if work is available after the non-renewal.
The employer may have valid reasons for not renewing the contract further.
The non-renewal may only be considered illegal retrenchment if mala fide actions of the employer are proven, although it still does not fit within the meaning of retrenchment under Section 2(oo).
The decision and action of the employer may be marked as unfair labor practice, leading to the reinstatement of the workman.
A 1994 case was overturned.
