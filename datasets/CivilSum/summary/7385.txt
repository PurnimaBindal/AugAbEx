Article 21 of the Indian Constitution, Haryana Family Pension Scheme 1964 Rule 4(1), Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol.
I Part I Rules 12.2(b) and 31, and Rule 616-A(2)(b) of Vol. II, Part I highlight regulations regarding family pension granted to the widows of temporary employees.
Denial of the same violates the fundamental right under Article 21 of the Constitution Courts must give a broader interpretation to beneficial provisions, to avoid negating their purpose.
The rules apply even if the government employee had just one year or six months of ad hoc or temporary service before death.
Widows are entitled to receive family pension.
