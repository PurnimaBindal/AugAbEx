Constitution of India, Article 227 - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 7 Rule 11, Order 14 Rule 2 - Issue of jurisdiction - Application to treat as preliminary - Dismissal of - Suit for absorption and claim of equal pay for equal work at Panipat - According to Order 14 Rule 2, the preliminary issue of the question of jurisdiction of Court or relating to the bar to suit created by any law must be addressed before moving forward with the suit.
The court held that the question of jurisdiction was likely to dispose of the suit, and therefore it was not open to postpone the decision of the preliminary issue as done in this case.
