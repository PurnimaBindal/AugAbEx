According to the Delhi University Act, 1922, Sections 8, 17, 21, and 30, along with Ordinance XVIII Clause 7(2)(b) and (c), the appointment of a Principal requires the recommendation of the Governing Body.
In this case, the Vice-Chancellor did not approve the Governing body's recommendation for the petitioner, leading to the post being re-advertised.
The Vice-Chancellor overstepped his bounds by bypassing the Selection Committee, which had the power to decide.
As there was no emergent situation, the entire selection process could not be voided.
The court directed the recommendations of the Governing Body to be placed before the Selection Committee for final decision.
