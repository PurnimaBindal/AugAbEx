Constitution of India, Article 226 - Indian Air Force Act, 1950 - Reserve and Auxiliary Air Forces Act, 1952 - Military Service - Benefit of - Petitioners are challenging the decision to grant the benefit of military service to the 2nd respondent by the Punjab Government as he is not a member of the Armed Forces.
However, the 2nd respondent served the Auxiliary Air Force during emergency, and as those enrolled under Reserve and Auxiliary Air Force Act, 1952 are members of Indian Air Force, they must be treated as members of the military service.
Therefore, the Govt. of Punjab rightly granted the benefit of military service to the 2nd respondent, placing him senior to the petitioners.
