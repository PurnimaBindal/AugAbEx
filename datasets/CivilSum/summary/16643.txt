Constitution of India, Article 226 - Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities/Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 47(1) - Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume II, Rule 5.8 - Retirement from service on medical grounds - Petitioner, due to eye ailment, was declared 100% physically handicapped and retired from service after 14 years- According to Section 47(1) of Disabilities Act, the petitioner could be given another post that has the same pay scale and service benefits if they are not suitable for their current post - If not possible, they may be kept on a supernumerary post until a suitable one becomes available or until their superannuation - Since the petitioner was a teacher, they could be allowed to continue in service after acquiring the disability.
