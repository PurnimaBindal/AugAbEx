As per the instructions given by the Government through SRO 310 of 1986, the dependents of a deceased employee are entitled to a family pension that is equal to his last drawn pay for the first seven years, and thereafter, either half of it or the pension applicable under Rule 20(ii)(aaa) of the Pension-cum-Gratuity Rules, whichever is less, for the next seven years, and then at an ordinary rate as per Rule 20(ii)(aaa).
The petitioner in this case has not had their family pension fixed as per SRO 310 of 1986.
As per the law, they have the right to claim pension, and a writ petition is maintainable.
Pension cannot be considered a mere concession.
Once the petitioner has been deemed eligible for a family pension, there should not be any discrimination between persons within the same class.
SRO-310/86 cannot just be applied prospectively.
Anyone within the same class should be entitled to the benefits of the liberalized pension policy from the date of its applicability, and no cut-off date can be applied unless there is a demonstration that the classification is based on a reasonable differentia and rational consideration having a nexus to the purpose and object thereof [Paras 4, 5, and 6].
