Punjab Municipal Executive Officer Rules, 1976, Rule 3 - Amendment - The Executive Officer of the Municipal Committee serves as the Principle Executive Officer of the Committee under Rule 3 and is the appointing authority for workmen.
A written statement cannot be amended to challenge the proper impleading of the committee.
[Para 2] B Gainful employment/Amendment - An application for amendment in the written statement may be filed after the completion of evidence.
The Labour Court must grant the workman one month's time and the opportunity to rebut the evidence.
