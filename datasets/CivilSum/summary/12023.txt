Constitution of India, Article 16 - Pay and allowances - Pay of petitioner was inaccurately fixed by the Department, but recovery of Rs. 32893/- from terminal benefits cannot be made because the petitioner did not make any misrepresentation resulting in the erroneous pay fixation.
The order of recovery has been set aside.
Raghbir Singh v. Pepsu Road Transport Corporation, Patiala, and Kishori Lal Soni v. State of Punjab and others are relied upon.
