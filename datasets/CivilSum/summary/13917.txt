Delhi School Education Act (18 of 1973), Section 28 - Delhi School Education Rules (1973), Rule 115(4) - Applicability - A School Tribunal set aside an order of termination of service, stating that no enquiry was conducted and that the provisions of the Act and Rules were violated - A writ petition was filed against the School Tribunal's order, which was withdrawn and became final - The school is no longer allowed to argue that an enquiry was conducted - The school cannot use Rule 115(4) to pass a suspension order retroactively from the date of the original termination from service.
