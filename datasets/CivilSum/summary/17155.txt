Civil Procedure Code, Section 115 - Dispute related to gratuity, pension, GPF and arrears of salary of deceased Government employee - According to the Government Rules, the widow of the deceased is entitled to receive pension and gratuity, while the GPF and arrears of salary are to be shared among all legal heirs of the deceased - The law of succession should not be overridden by any Rules and Regulations of Government.
