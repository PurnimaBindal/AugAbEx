The Constitution of India, Article 226 outlines rules regarding retrenchment and unfair labor practices in public sector undertakings.
The services of NMRs were terminated even though they had worked for 15 years, claiming there was no work.
However, the same work was given to a labor contractor and the company refused to re-employ any of the retrenched NMRs as security staff, instead hiring new staff on a contract basis - a clear violation of Unfair Labor Practice.
The petition has been allowed.
