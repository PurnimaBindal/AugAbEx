Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Police (Haryana Amendment) Rules, 1998, Rule 12.16(16) and (19) - Selection - Alternate remedy - Candidates upset with recommendations of Selection Board for Constable selection have recourse under Rule 12.16(19) of the Rules.
Rather than getting a writ petition against the Board's recommendations, they can appeal to the concerned Deputy Inspector General of the Police Range, who shall give a speaking order after providing an opportunity to such people and notify them - Petitioners sent to appeal remedy.
