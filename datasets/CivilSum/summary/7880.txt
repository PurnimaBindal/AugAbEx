The Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 226 and 227 - Judicial review - Failure to produce relevant documents before the Labour Court - Court unable to consider evidence not produced or tendered before the Labour Court - Court cannot sit in appeal and there is no evidence contradicting the appraisal reports.
[Paras 7 and 5] B The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F, 25-G, 25-H - Termination of services during probation prior to the completion of 240 days - Section 25-F is not automatically applicable - Employers have the right to terminate employment with the organization.
[Para 6] C. The distinction between an ad-hoc employee and an employee serving on probation - Appointment on probation implies suitability and caution for the duration of the probation period, whereas ad hoc service is devoid of such conditions.
