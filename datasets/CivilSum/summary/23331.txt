Constitution of India, Article 14 - Transfer/ Mala fides - Transfer of Chief Secretary - The transfer of Chief Secretary cannot be assumed to have been made to defame him due to differences with the Chief Minister and cannot be considered mala fide unless there are concrete evidence to support it.
[Para 12]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Indian Administrative Service (pay) Rules, 1954, Rule 9(1) - Transfer - Even if the Chief Secretary is transferred to a new position of Secretary, the transfer will still be valid as long as it is done in accordance with the Cabinet's decision to declare the two positions to be equivalent before the transfer.
