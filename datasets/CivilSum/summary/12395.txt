Navy Regulations Part III, Chapter XII, Regulation 268 - Petitioner employed as an artificer in 1981 - Completed 15 years of service in 1996 - Initially agreed to serve for four more years to protect pensionary benefits but changed his mind due to a Supreme Court judgment - The court held that the granted re-engagement order cannot be cancelled.
[Paras 5 and 6] Anuj Kumar Dey v. Union of India, 1997(1) S.C.T.
