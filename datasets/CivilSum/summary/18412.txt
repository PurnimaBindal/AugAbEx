MP Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1976, Rule 9(2)(b), Section 608 - Retiral benefits - The petitioner retired from service, and after 7 years, the government ordered him to pay Rs. 79,229 due to his negligence.
They also withheld his pension without holding a departmental enquiry.
However, the order was quashed, and the government was directed to pay arrears of pension with interest at the rate of 9% per annum within one month from the date arrears became due until the date of payment.
