Constitution of India, Article 226 - West Bengal Payment of Subsistence Allowance Act, 1969, Section 4 - Bengal Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Rules, 1946, Section 10-A - Subsistence allowance - Suspension - Petitioner suspended for misconduct - Subsistence allowance initially granted at 50% and later reduced to 25% - Claim that Assistant Labour Commissioner did not have the jurisdiction to decide that standing order overrides the Act - Rejected, as an authority granted relief by a statute can decide any jurisdictional facts necessary to exercise that power - The Act, being more beneficial, overrides Section 10-A and applies to the petitioner - Petition dismissed.
