The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F regulates termination.
If a workman is not found medically fit for permanent absorption, it does not necessarily mean that their services as a casual workman can be terminated on the grounds of continued ill health.
The employer bears the burden of proving that the workman's termination was due to continued ill-health.
The order of termination is illegal for non-compliance with the provisions of Section 25-F The order of the Tribunal that the respondent-workman is entitled to full back wages for the period during which he was kept out of employment is not flawed.
