The Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 9(a)) states that a Nomadic Tribe candidate must be appointed to a vacancy earmarked for them.
However, if no candidate belonging to the Nomadic Tribe is available, the rule mandates year-to-year appointment only if an available candidate does not belong to the Backward Classes.
Whether the appellant belongs to a Backward Class is in question, and the case is remanded to the High Court.
