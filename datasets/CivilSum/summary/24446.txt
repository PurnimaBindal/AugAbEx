As per Section 2(j) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the term "industry" encompasses a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 1913, that owns around 300 houses rented out to different tenants.
The company employs a staff of over 50 persons for managing, maintaining and repairing the flats, as well as providing other amenities to the tenants.
The appellant fulfills all the requirements to be classified as an industry within the aforementioned definition.
