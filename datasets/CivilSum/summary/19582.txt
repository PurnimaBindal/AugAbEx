Punjab Co-operative Department (State Service) Class-III, Rules, 1978, Rule 9(1) - Promotion/Option/Reversion - Promotion from the post of Inspector to that of Lecturer - Pay scale of Lecturer after revision - Appointments to the post of lecturer from Inspectors were based on options when pay scales were equal - Revised pay scales made the post a promotional one and entitled senior Inspectors who did not earlier opt for appointment to be considered for promotion as Lecturers - However, those who were already appointed as Lecturers on their options and had worked on regular basis for more than three years could not be reverted without an opportunity of hearing.
