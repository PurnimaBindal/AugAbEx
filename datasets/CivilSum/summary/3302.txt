Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Suspension/Suspension allowance - In 1985, the petitioners were suspended, but neither the departmental authority nor the criminal court passed an order of punishment - Authorities must either prosecute or re-instate the officials and pay suspension allowance based on revised pay scales - Prolonged suspension equals punishment - Although not considered a punishment, the suspended official is entitled to the benefit of pay scale revisions, but increments during suspension cannot be claimed as a right.
