Head constables seeking promotion under Punjab Police Rules are entitled to be considered for such promotion based on length of service and confirmation upon completing two years probation, as per rules 124, 136, 138, and 139, as ruled in Punjab Civil Services Rules, Articles 226, and 227.
The petitioners, who have already undergone Intermediate School Course earlier than their juniors, have automatically fulfilled the requirements for confirmation and are entitled to be considered for promotion.
[Paras 9, 10, 12]

As per the Constitution of India, Article 226, head constables must be confirmed upon completion of two years of probation.
If no order extending probation has been passed and there is nothing against their integrity and working, head constables can be deemed confirmed after the expiry of their probation period.
The petitioners, therefore, stand automatically confirmed and only require a formal order to be passed.
[Paras 9, 11]
