Based on Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, an industrial tribunal cannot disrupt an employer's disciplinary actions against an employee who misappropriated funds as it would cause interference in the tribunal's findings.
Moreover, if a bank employee is found guilty of fraudulent activity such as embezzlement, then the employer is justified in taking strict action against them since bank employees are held to a high standard of trust.
In this case, the removal from service was deemed necessary.
