Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Initiation of - A disciplinary authority cannot investigate the genuineness of a caste certificate - The initiation of disciplinary proceedings without the cancellation of a caste certificate by competent authority is illegal.
[Para 5]
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Dismissal from service - In order to dismiss an employee based on a caste certificate, it must be proven that the certificate has been cancelled or withdrawn by competent authority - Without complying with these requirements, a dismissal order cannot stand.
[Paras 6 and 7]
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Dismissal from service - Reinstatement - Upon the quashing of a dismissal order, an employee is entitled to reinstatement with all consequential benefits unless the management can prove gainful employment - As there is no proof of gainful employment by the management, the employee is entitled to reinstatement and all related benefits.
