MP Civil Services (Medical Attendances) Rules, 1958, Rule 2(d)(ii) - Fundamental Rules, Rule 9 - Medical reimbursement - Dependents - A father of a Govt. Servant receiving Rs. 414/- pm pension from the Govt. who resides with his son at the age of 70 years after retiring from Govt. service, cannot be said that he was not "wholly dependent" on his son and that he has his independent income.
The son is expected to look after and maintain his old father, and in this case, the petitioner took his father to Bombay for treatment for his serious ailment after getting due permission from the competent authority.
As Father is wholly dependent upon the petitioner, he is entitled to reimbursement of Medical expenses for the treatment of his father.
