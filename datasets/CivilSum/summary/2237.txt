Constitution of India, Article 311 - Departmental Authority stopped petitioner's two increments due to absence from duty - Appellate Authority confirmed punishment - High Court cannot re-examine evidence as a Court of appeal - High Court can only ensure there was no non-application of mind by appellate authority - If appellate authority reached a different conclusion from earlier authority, it does not make order perverse - Appellate authority justified in making its own conclusion without influence from earlier order that was set aside by the Court.
