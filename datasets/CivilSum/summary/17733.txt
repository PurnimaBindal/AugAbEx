Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Absence - Striking off name for unauthorised absence - Even if an employee is absent without authorization, the management is legally required to hold a domestic enquiry and provide an opportunity for the employee to be heard before their name can be struck off - Striking off a delinquent employee's name without a fair hearing violates principles of natural justice - The order to strike off the name of the delinquent employee is revoked, but this does not prevent the management from conducting a domestic enquiry.
[Paras 4 and 5]
