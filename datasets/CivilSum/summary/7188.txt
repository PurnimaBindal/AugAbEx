Constitution of India, Article 16 - An employee who secured employment by submitting a forged medical certificate under the physically handicapped quota had his services terminated following a departmental enquiry.
Despite being acquitted by a criminal court, the termination cannot be set aside based on the benefit of doubt given during the forgery charge.
As per departmental proceedings, charges need to be proved by preponderance of probabilities while in a criminal case, the standard of proof beyond reasonable doubt is applicable.
[Paras 6 and 7]
