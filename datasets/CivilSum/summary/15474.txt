Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Rajasthan Grant of House Building Advances to Government Servants Rules, Rule 12 - House building advance/loans - Goverment servants availing loans for purposes other than paying for cost of land or construction are not eligible for the facility of advance loans - Liability to repay the amount obtained as house building advance used for re-payment of loan secured privately cannot be avoided through writ petition - The purpose of writ petition is for enforcement of law and not for avoiding implication of Rule or law - Additionally, direction cannot be issued to convert the same loan into an advance for construction without proper application and consideration by the authority.
[Paras 16 to 18]
