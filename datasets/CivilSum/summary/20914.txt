According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I, Part I, Rule 4(4), an advance increment must be given to a J.B.T.
Teacher who is temporarily appointed as a Head Teacher.
This is because the post of a Head Teacher carries more responsibility and importance.
The increment will be given on the teacher's temporary appointment or promotion as a Head Teacher, and it will be in addition to their old post in the time scale.
