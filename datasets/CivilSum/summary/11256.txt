Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 173 - Entitlement Rules, 1982, Rule 14 - Disability pension - Diabetes Mellitus - If a disease has been caused by the conditions of employment during the period of Military Service, it is deemed attributable to Military Service.
However, under Rule 14(b), if the deceased had diabetes mellitus at the time of enrollment, but it was not detected due to certain reasons, it cannot be deemed as arisen from or attributable to Military Service.
