Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Civil) Regulations, 1965, Rules 11 and 15 - Cadre/Promotion - Promotions of Assistant Engineers of Hydel Design Directorate and Civil Engineers - The Board only had one cadre of Civil Engineers and never made a decision to create a separate Hydel Cadre - Therefore, both groups were jointly considered as Civil Engineers and their promotions were valid.
