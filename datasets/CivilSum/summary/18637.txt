Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion - To the position of Assistant Provident Fund Commissioner - The petitioner had been duly considered for vacancies in 1984, 1986, and 1989 but was not recommended by DPC for promotion.
When more meritorious persons from the feeder category were available, there was no illegality or perversity or arbitrariness in the order of the Tribunal as the petitioner had been considered for the post but not recommended.
