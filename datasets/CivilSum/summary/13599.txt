Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Termination - Reinstatement - Selection - Promotion by selection - Even if promotion is by selection from eligible candidates, seniority is not crucial for selection - Appellant's idle period after reinstatement treated as dies-non - Civil court quashes order treating said period dies-non, leading to refixation of his seniority above person already promoted by selection - Appellant cannot automatically claim promotion based solely on seniority over person selected and promoted as Deputy Advocate General from Assistant Advocate General post - Appeal rejected.
