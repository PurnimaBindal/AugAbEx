Constitution of India, Article 311 - Departmental proceedings - Departmental proceedings initiated against the Petitioner were quashed on the basis of perfunctory, incomplete and defective audit reports - A fresh special audit was ordered by the government for the relevant period - The contention was that the previous audit reports could not form the basis of initiation of departmental proceedings as they were defective and incomplete - The special audit report revealed nothing against the Petitioner - The Tribunal dismissed the OA and declined stay - The finding of the Tribunal that the Petitioner will get a full opportunity to explain the allegations against him before the enquiry officer was considered to be perverse in the absence of any definite base to constitute misconduct - Accordingly, the petition was allowed.
