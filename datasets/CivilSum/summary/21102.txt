Sections 33(2)(b), 33-C(2), 33, 33-A, and 33(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, deal with dismissal or discharge during the pendency of proceedings and the approval of authority before proceedings are pending.
The order of dismissal or discharge becomes non-existent and inoperative if approval is not granted, or if an application for approval is withdrawn or refused.
Workmen are not required to seek any reference and can straightway proceed with their remedy under U/s 33-C(2).
Even complaints U/s 33(1) or 33-A obligate the employer to reinstate the workman or pay all their remunerations.
These provisions are made to protect workmen from unfair labor practices and are not an alternate remedy for them.
The view taken in Straw-board Manufacturing Co v. Gobind has been endorsed, and the one expressed in Punjab Beverages 1978(3) SCR 370 overruled.
