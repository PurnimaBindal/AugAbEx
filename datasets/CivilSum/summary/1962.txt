According to the Minimum Wages Act of 1948, Section 5(1)(b), if the Government of India has not fixed wages, Rule 25(2)(v)(b) of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Central Rules, 1971 will be implemented.
However, a notification issued under Section 5(1)(b) by the Government has been stayed by the High Court, so the Chief Labour Commissioner (Central) invoked Rule 25(2)(v)(b).
If the writ petitions were dismissed, the orders of the Chief Labour Commissioner under Rule 25 would become irrelevant and wages fixed under Section 5(1)(b) of Minimum Wages Act would be established.
