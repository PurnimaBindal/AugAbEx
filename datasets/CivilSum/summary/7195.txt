The Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923, Sections 4 and 10(1), discusses the limitation to prefer claims for compensation in the event of a workman's death.
Section 4 outlines the rates of compensation for both personal injury and death cases, while Section 10(1) lays out the time constraint for filing a compensation claim.
If the victim dies due to the accident, the period of limitation is extended by two years, as stated in Section 10(1).
