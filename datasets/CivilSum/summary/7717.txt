Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Punishment - Charge sheet - The Inquiry Officer submitted a report in favour of an accused employee - However, the Disciplinary Authority did not accept the report and issued an order against the employee - Moreover, the authority did not provide any reasons to differ with the inquiry officer's findings - Besides, the employee's appeal was rejected through a non-speaking order without proper application of mind - Thus, the order is arbitrary and contrary to the established legal principles.
