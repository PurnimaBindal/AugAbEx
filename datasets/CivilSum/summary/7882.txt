Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14 and 16 - Apprenticeship Act, 1961 - Appointment - The petitioner completed a two-year apprenticeship and passed a selection test, but was not appointed due to being much lower in merit - The challenge to the selection was unsuccessful as the petitioner does not have an indefeasible right to the appointment as Lineman unless he meets the merit and selection criteria.
