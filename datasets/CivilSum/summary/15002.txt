According to Section 192 of the Army Act, the order of extension of service may be recalled if the petitioner is ineligible due to a conviction under Section 41(1) of the same Act and subsequent detention for ten days.
Despite being ineligible, the petitioner was granted extension due to an oversight by the screening board.
However, discipline is a significant consideration in granting extensions and the petitioner's discharge would not infringe any fundamental or statutory rights.
Thus, the respondents are instructed not to recover the period of 14 months that the petitioner availed of during his two-year extension.
