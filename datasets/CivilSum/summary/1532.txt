"Interpreting provisions of Section 25B and F of the Industrial Disputes Act 1947 - It is not necessary to complete 240 days service in the preceding year for taking advantage of Section 25F of the Act - The period under Section 25B read with Section 25F of the Act cannot be limited to the immediately preceding year - This means that an employee who has completed 240 days in any calendar year is entitled to benefits.
