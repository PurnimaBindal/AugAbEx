The Tribunal decided to set aside an order of suspension of an employee who had been released on bail.
The employee had not been charged with any offense committed against the school or during their official duties.
The suspension was not in line with Rule 7(3) of WBSF (C, C & A) Rules, 1971.
As such, there was no basis for the suspension, and the order was stayed.
[Paras 17 and 18]
On acquittal in a criminal proceeding, the government must accept the order of acquittal and withdraw the suspension order of the employee.
