In Civil Appeal No. 9549 of 2003 (Arising out of SLP(C) No. 12160/2002), Reserve Bank of India Pension Regulations, 1990, Regl.
2(12), and Reserve Bank of India Staff Regulations, 1948, Regln.
26(3) were discussed in relation to the amendment of rules.
Any amendment to rules, which were framed with the sanction of the government, necessitates prior approval from the government.
Staff regulations that have been framed by the Central Board of the Bank as an administrative decision do not require approval from the government for amendment.
However, Pension Regulations require approval from the government for any amendment to be enforced.
In addition, Resignation and Voluntary retirement were discussed in terms of service jurisprudence and their consequences.
Voluntary retirement is a condition of service that can only be availed of in accordance with the service rules, while resignation is a purely voluntary action of the employee.
The approval of the employer is not necessary for resignation.
The consequences of both are different; in the case of voluntary retirement, the retiral benefits of the employee remain intact to that extent, while that may not necessarily be the case in resignation.
