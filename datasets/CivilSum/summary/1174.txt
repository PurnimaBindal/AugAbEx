Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311(2) - Enquiry Report/Natural justice - Despite the second opportunity being deleted from the scheme of Article 311(2) of the Constitution, a delinquent is still entitled to make a representation against the findings of the enquiry officer before the punishment order is passed - the amendment has no bearing on the supply of a copy of the inquiry report - it is necessary to supply a copy of the inquiry report before inflicting punishment and provide an opportunity of representation against the finding.
[Para 2]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Natural justice/Disciplinary proceedings - To avoid using any material against a delinquent without his knowledge is necessary.
[Para 23]

Constitution of India, Articles 226, 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - The High Court has no jurisdiction as a court of appeal over the findings of disciplinary authority, but where the findings are utterly perverse to the material on record, the High Court can always interfere with such findings.
[Para 26]

Constitution of India, Article 226 and 311 - Punishment - Quantum of punishment - Delay in joining of 72 hours - Not a misconduct for which one can be removed from service - The punishment must be proportionate to the misconduct - If the punishment is vindictive, Malafide, and so disproportionate to the allegations that would shake the conscience, it will be conclusive evidence of bias and warrants interference - Order of removal from service set aside.
