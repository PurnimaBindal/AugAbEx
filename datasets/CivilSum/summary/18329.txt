Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation of services - Discrimination in daily wage payment - The petitioner worked as a Muster Roll LDC typist for 15 years and received a daily wage of Rs. 84/- instead of Rs. 117/- meant for Muster Roll Group D employee - Out of five LDC vacancies, one post was available for regularisation of Muster Roll employees - Since the petitioner is the senior-most employee, the respondents are directed to regularise her services by appointing her as LDC against one vacancy - The underpayment is considered discriminatory, and the petitioner should receive Rs. 117/- per day prospectively.
