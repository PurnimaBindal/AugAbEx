Navy Act of 1957, Section 160 - Recommendations and opinion of Judge Advocate General of Navy - The recommendations and opinion of the JAG hold utmost importance in the process of judicial review and cannot be overlooked - When exercising his powers of review, the Chief of Naval Staff should give appropriate weight to the JAG's recommendations and opinion.
If it is clear that the Chief has not given proper attention to the JAG report, the decision of the Chief of Naval Staff cannot be supported.
