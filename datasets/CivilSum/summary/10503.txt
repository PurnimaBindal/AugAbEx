Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection for Post- Graduate Courses in Medical Colleges of Madhya Pradesh Rules, 1984, Rule 3210 (iii) - Admission - Judicial review - No rules exist regarding the transfer of Post-Graduate students, but transfer cannot be prohibited - State Government must create appropriate guidelines to avoid discrimination and arbitrariness in transfers - Arbitrary and discriminatory denial of transfer violates Article 14 - Discretion must be based on law and guided by rules - Fairness and reasonableness are essential components of Article 14.
