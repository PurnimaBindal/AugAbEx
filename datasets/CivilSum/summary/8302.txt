Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Dispute - Reference - Labour Court raised two issues, one of which concerned the maintainability of the reference.
However, no conclusion was reached by the Presiding Officer on this issue.
As a result, the Labour Court failed to respond to the reference in a legally sound and appropriate manner.
The award is therefore subject to being annulled, and the Labour Court is instructed to restore the reference to its original number.
