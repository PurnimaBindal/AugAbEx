Compassionate Appointment - The appellant Company follows certain Rules for appointment on compassionate grounds, and availability of vacancies in the respective staff cadre/authorisation is a determining factor.
No vacancies are currently available due to surplus labour, and the company has implemented a policy to gradually reduce its workforce, in addition to offering incentives for voluntary retirement.
Making appointments on compassionate grounds is not an obligation for the appellant-Company, but if it does make fresh appointments for Class III or Class IV positions, the respondent's application for appointment will be given due consideration in accordance with her ranking on the waiting list.
The High Court's impugned judgment has been set aside.
