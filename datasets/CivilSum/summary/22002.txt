Section 11 of the Civil Procedure Code and Article 32 of the Constitution of India are applicable to the Indian Railway Service Engineers (Class I).
The principle of res judicata and constructive res judicata will bar any dispute raised under Article 32 of the Constitution if it has already been decided by a competent court and the judgment has become final.
The dispute between Officers and Engineers in the Katyani Dayal's case was still a dispute before the Supreme Court.
Therefore, any dispute now raised under Article 32 of the Constitution is also barred by res judicata and constructive res judicata.
