The appellant had been temporarily appointed, and passing the Tamil language test was a pre-condition for regularisation of her service.
While temporarily employed, she passed the examination, but after her regular appointment, she was asked to pass the same test again.
Despite having previously passed, her paper was cancelled when she was accused of malpractice.
Her services were terminated for not passing the test, but it was held that the respondent cannot terminate her services for this reason because she had already passed the examination when she was temporarily in service, and the order allowing this had become final.
The respondent is directed to regularise her service and provide her with past benefits of temporary service.
