Constitution of India, Articles 16(4), 15(4), 46, 38 and 335 - Reservation/Public Policy/Roster points/Promotions - Officials are constitutionally obligated to uphold reservations in appointments, which contribute to the economic and social well-being of reserved communities - If a reserved category candidate is unavailable for a promotion, the authorities must exchange the reserved roster point - A breach of trust occurs when constitutional policies designed to support scheduled and backward communities are not implemented - In the absence of a suitable ST population, the SCs should be given alternative roster points, in accordance with directives dated 1261986 from the Government of India to U.T.
Chandigarh - When vacancy No. 1 in the roster is open to the STs, SC candidates should be considered - The violation of policy warrants action against responsible officials and the UT Chandigarh Administrator was advised to ensure compliance and submit a report on compliance to the Supreme Court Registry.
