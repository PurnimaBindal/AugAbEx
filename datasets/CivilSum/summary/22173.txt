Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14 and 16 - Contract Labour - Payment of Equal pay and similar wages at par with regular workers - Courts should not interfere and take a decision as if there is no competent authority for the same - The concerned authority must decide the issue.
[Para 5]

Despite the High Court ordering the appellant to pay equal and similar wages and other benefits to the Contract labourers, the order was not justified - The Supreme Court directed the Labour Commissioner to decide the issue after affording an opportunity to both the parties.
[Para 6]
