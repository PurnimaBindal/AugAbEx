The function of the appropriate Government under Section 10(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, is merely administrative and not judicial or quasi-judicial, and it is not authorized to adjudicate the dispute where there is prima facie evidence of an employer-employee relationship.
[Para 3]
Despite persistent refusal, the Court has directed the State Government to make a reference under Section 10(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, to the appropriate Court for adjudicating the petitioner's dispute, as provided under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
