Kerala Service Rules, Rule 90(6A)(1)(ii) Part III - Family Pension - Petitioner, as the mother of a deceased employee, seeks family pension after her husband, who was previously receiving it, passed away - The State argues that the pension scheme is only applicable to either the father or mother, as specified in Rule 90(6A)(1)(ii) Part III - The petition was granted by the learned Single Judge, who determined that Rule 90(6) includes both fathers and mothers in reference to family - Rule 90(6A) establishes that contributory family pension is available to parents of the deceased, but only if they can prove they were completely dependent on the deceased for financial support and do not receive income from any other sources - It was decided that if a father or mother of a deceased government employee meets the conditions spelled out in Rule 90(6A), they should not be refused a contributory family pension - In the event of the death of the father, the mother may receive the same pension if she meets the conditions outlined in Rule 90 - The appeal was concluded accordingly.
