The definition of "shop" in the Kerala Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1960 cannot be used to interpret the meaning of the term "shop" as used in the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, as per Section 1(5).
However, a review of Sections 2(4) and (15) of the Kerala Act demonstrates that water theme parks are included in the definition of "shop", as they are a form of commercial or economic activity for a price.
As a result, they are considered shops under the Act.
