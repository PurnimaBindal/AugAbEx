No. 14711(W) of 1991 Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39(d) - Amins and Surveyors have the same responsibilities and perform identical work - Both should receive equal pay - Even though they work in different departments - It is unfair to treat them differently based on the department they work in - Petitioners are entitled to the same pay as Surveyors - Respondent must provide petitioners with the same scale of pay - Amins and Surveyors must be on equal footing.
