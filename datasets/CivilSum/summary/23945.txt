Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Bihar Industries Service Cadre Rules, 1987, Rules 11 and 12 - Grouping of similar posts in the same cadre for promotion - Grouping together of employees in Group B and Group C with Group A officers for promotion as Manager in a higher scale is not a violation of Articles 14 and 16 - The chance of promotion is not a right or condition of service - The rule-making authority had valid reasons for this grouping - The classification made in the Rules is upheld.
