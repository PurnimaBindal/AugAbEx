Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab State Electricity Board Employees Punishment and Appeal Regulations, 1971, Regulation 9(2) - Enquiry - Termination - Natural justice - Before punishing the delinquent official, it is necessary for the disciplinary authority to furnish grounds of disagreement from report of the Inquiry Officer - Failure to do so renders orders of termination from service invalid - Board has violated principles of audi alteram partem.

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab State Board Employees Punishment and Appeal Regulations, 1971, Regulation 9(2) - Enquiry - Termination - Natural justice - Regulation 9(2) does not mandate furnishing grounds of disagreement on each item of charge from report of Inquiry Officer to the delinquent official - Requirement of natural justice is implied in the clause - There has been no violation of natural justice principles in respect of the order passed in this case.

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab State Board Employees Punishment and Appeal Regulations, 1971, Regulation 9(2) - Natural justice - The nature of charges and investigation determines whether a specific principle of natural justice has been violated or not - The disciplinary authority must record reasons for disagreement with the report of the Inquiry Officer - These reasons can only be used to inflict punishment if they were conveyed to the delinquent official and if he has been given a chance to explain - Failure to do so results in action being void for non-compliance with natural justice principles.
