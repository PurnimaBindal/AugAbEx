Response: According to the Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and Army Act, 1950, Sections 3(1) and 70, a military personnel accused of rape may be tried by either a civil court or a court martial, depending on whether they were on active service at the time of the offence.
In the case at hand, the Petitioner-Sepoy was on casual leave and not on active service, so his case was exclusively triable by a civil court with criminal jurisdiction under Section 70 of the Act.
Furthermore, as the Petitioner was not on active military service, he could only be tried by a civil court with criminal jurisdiction, and the military authorities correctly sent his case to such a court for trial.
