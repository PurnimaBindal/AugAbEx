According to Section 30 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 and Section 147(1) of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, if an employee dies while driving a vehicle in discharge of duty, they will fall under the first proviso of Section 147(1) and the insurance company will be liable to pay compensation.
The contention that the employee should be employed as a permanent driver by the owner does not affect this ruling.
