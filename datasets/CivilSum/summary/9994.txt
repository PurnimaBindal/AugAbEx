According to Section 24 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, termination of services due to participation in an illegal strike is not justified unless there is proof of vandalism, violence, or sabotage.
The services of the petitioners were terminated in breach of natural justice, and management does not have the legal competency to do so in a situation where the strike was illegal but there was some settlement in force.
