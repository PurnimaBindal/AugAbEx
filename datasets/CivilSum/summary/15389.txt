In accordance with section 26 of the Bombay Police Act, if a recourse is taken to Article 311(2), a departmental enquiry may be dispensed with, but the accused must be given an opportunity to show cause against the proposed punishment.
It was held that since no opportunity was given in this case, the order of dismissal cannot be sustained under Article 311(2).
The petitioner has argued that the reasons for the dismissal were grossly inadequate and incorrect under section 26 of the Bombay Police Act, and that he had a right to be heard in the matter and could have established his innocence.
It is argued that the petitioner is entitled to this right, despite the provision in Article 311(2)(b) permitting the Disciplinary Authority to dispense with the departmental enquiry.
