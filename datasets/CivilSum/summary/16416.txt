Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 335 - Reservation - Criteria for appointment - Possession of minimum qualifications does not automatically grant a right to appointment for candidates belonging to Scheduled Caste category - Employers have the right to evaluate whether a Scheduled Caste candidate is suitable for the post or not - Efficiency of administration must be maintained during appointments - Scheduled Caste candidates are not entitled to appointments - Non-appointment of petitioner deemed proper if they possess minimum qualifications but are not found suitable for the post.
