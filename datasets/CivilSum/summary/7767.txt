State Bank of India (Supervising Staff) Service Rules, Rule 20B - Scope of - It was determined that the scope of rule 20B is restricted to the continuation and conclusion of disciplinary proceedings, and during this time the employee will be considered to be in service.
[Para 8] B Appeal - Scope of - It was determined that when an appeal or revision is provided before a superior forum against a decision by a Court, Tribunal, or any authority, and such forum modifies, reverses, or affirms the decision - then the decision of the subordinate forum merges with such decision, and the later decision will continue to be in force, and is enforceable in the eyes of the law.
