Section 17-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 provides that if a workman files an affidavit stating that he has not been employed in any other establishment and cannot earn a livelihood from his own business, and the employer does not provide specific denial to refute the claims in the affidavit, then the conditions of Section 17-B are met and the workman is entitled to the benefits of this section.
This interpretation follows the ruling in AIR 1986 SC 842.
