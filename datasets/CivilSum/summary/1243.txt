Indian Medicines Central Council Act, 1970, Sections 17, 29 - East Punjab Ayurvedic and Union Practitioners Act, 1949 - Petitioners, who hold diplomas in Unani Medicine, allege to be governed by 1949 Act - However, their diploma-granting board is not recognized under the 1970 Act, which the Supreme Court has ruled to prevail over State Acts in regulating education and medical practice in Indian systems of medicine - Petitioners cannot practice Indian medicine as their case does not fall under the exception enumerated in Section 17(c) of the 1970 Act.
