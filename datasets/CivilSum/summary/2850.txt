Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - No finding given by Labour Court regarding violation of principle of natural justice - Tribunal not obliged to decide whether enquiry was conducted fairly and impartially in absence of pleading by petitioner - Petitioner cannot bring up such issue for first time in writ petition as it was not framed by Tribunal.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Dispute raised after 7 years - Labour Court dismissed claim as it was filed to take a chance - Tribunal has discretion to deny relief for delay, but can also mold it accordingly - As the tribunal exercised its discretion judiciously, the High Court has no reason to interfere with the decision.
