The Disability Pension of a Paranoid Schizophrenic cannot be granted once the Medical Board has determined that the condition is not related to or worsened due to military service, according to Army Pension Regulations, 1971, Part-I, Regulations 173 and 423 and the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226.
In a case where the petitioner was discharged after 24 years of service with 60% disability and the Medical Board held that Paranoid Schizophrenic was not attributed to constitutional disease or aggravated due to conditions of military service, the court cannot intervene and order the authorities to release the disability pension.
