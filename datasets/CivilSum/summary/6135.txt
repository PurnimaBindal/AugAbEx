The demand of money preceding its receipt by the employee is not an integral part of misconduct, and even if the money is accepted by some other in connivance with him, the ingredient of misconduct is complete.

The Kerala Civil Services (Vigilance Tribunal) Rules, Rule 8(15) under the Constitution of India, Article 311 indicate that a punishing authority is not required to be bound by the recommendations of the enquiry/Vigilance Tribunal.
If the facts and evidence warrant harsher punishment, but the recommendations are more lenient, it is open to the punishing authority to award an appropriate penalty.
The employee has the opportunity of representation against the proposed penalty.
