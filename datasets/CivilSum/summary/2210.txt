The UP Public Services (Reservation for Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and Other Backward Classes) Act, 1994, Sections 3 & 15, Schedule I - Direct Recruitment Selections - The reservation benefit provided by the UP Public Service (Reservation for Scheduled Caste/Scheduled Tribes and Other Backward Class) Act, 1994, for "Other Backward Class" candidates who are selected through direct recruitment applies exclusively to those categories or castes listed as Other Backward Classes in Schedule I of the Act until the closing date for application submission, unless there is a conflicting provision in the Service Rules, recruitment terms and conditions, or the advertisement.
