According to Punjab Civil Service Rules, Rule 3.26(d), the petitioner was compelled to retire.
However, the petitioner's claim for retention in the service beyond the age of 50 years was being considered, taking into account the relevant rules.
Specifically, Rules 3.26(d) of CSR Vol.
I Part-I, read with Rule 532A(c) CSR Vol-II of Punjab Civil Service Rules.
It should be noted that there were disciplinary cases pending against the petitioner.
In summary, the ex facie order of compulsory retirement was quashed and considered punitive and stigmatic.
