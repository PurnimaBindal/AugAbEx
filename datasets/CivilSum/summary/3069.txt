Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-B - Backwages - Maintenance allowance - Workmen can claim maintenance allowance during the pendency of a writ petition - Employer must prove that workmen were employed and receiving adequate remuneration - In this case, employer has not proven this to the Court's satisfaction - Therefore, the Court orders the employer to pay workmen benefits under Section 17-B of the ID Act.
