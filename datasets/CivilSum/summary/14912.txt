Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, Rule 5 - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Rule 16(1) - Termination - Natural justice - The petitioner was dismissed from service due to false claims of being a domicile of Gujarat State.
Although an enquiry report was conducted, the petitioner was not given a chance to defend himself.
This action not only violated the principles of natural justice, but also the Constitution's Articles 14 and 16.
As a result, the termination has been overturned.
