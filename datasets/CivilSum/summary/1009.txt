Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Probationer/Penalty/Reversion/Suitability of probationer/Work and conduct/Innocuous order - Petitioners who were on ad hoc basis appointment were appointed on probation in the higher posts found unsuitable and reverted - For persons appointed on temporary or ad hoc basis or on probation, the appointing authority is empowered either to impose penalty for misconduct or make an innocuous order of discharge or reversion in the interest of administration - Punishment can only be inflicted if an inquiry is held as per Article 311(2) of the Constitution or relevant service rules - It is up to the authority to decide which course of action to take - An order of discharge simpliciter may be passed if the record of service, including any attitude or tendency of a civil servant disclosed during probationary or ad hoc period is such as would justify and is sufficient - Declaring an order as punitive and violative of Article 311(2) of the Constitution for a probationer/ad hoc/temporary employee is not within the court's purview.
