Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Act, 1952, Section 11(2) - The contribution due to the EPF is the primary debt of the establishment's assets, and regardless of other laws, it must be paid in priority over other debts.
The dues under the EPF Act will always remain the first charge, and they will be paid first out of the establishment's assets, regardless of when the debt was incurred.
