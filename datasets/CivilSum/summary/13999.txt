Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Selection - Extension of last date for applications - The selection will not be vitiated by mere extension of the last date for applications.
In this case, the extension was made to attract more eligible candidates due to a low number of applications received, and no mala fide can be attributed to the authority.
[Paras 10, 11]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Selection - Interview marks - If interview marks account for only 40% of the total marks, the selection cannot be considered unfair due to an allegation that the technical member did not ask any questions or that the committee did not collectively assess the performance.
Without cogent material to support such claims, they cannot be considered.
[Para 12]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Selection - Mistakes in the declaration of result - If printing mistakes occur and are subsequently corrected by the editor of the newspaper, they cannot be considered as evidence of bungling or hanky panky in the selection.
[Para 13]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 & 226 - Selection - The court cannot interfere with the mental process of the selection committee if there is no material establishing an unfair or unreasonable selection.
It is not proper to consider such allegations solely based on the number of attempts it took for candidates to pass the qualifying examination.
[Para 14]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 226 - Selection - Necessary parties - Selected candidates who have been working for about 20 years at the time of the final hearing of the writ petition were not all impleaded as parties.
As most of the petitioners subsequently got employment and adopted a career, it is not appropriate to interfere with the selection.
[Para 15]
