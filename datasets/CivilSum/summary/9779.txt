Border Security Force Rules, Rule 19 - Central Civil Service (Pension) Rules, Rule 26 - Pensionary Benefits - The petitioner resigned on compassionate ground which was accepted, then requested re-enlistment in B.S.F.
after 4 years.
The re-enlistment was ordered without the benefit of past service, which the petitioner accepted without protest.
Therefore, Rule 26(4) does not apply in this case, and the petitioner is not entitled to the benefit of past service for any purpose, including pensionary benefits.
