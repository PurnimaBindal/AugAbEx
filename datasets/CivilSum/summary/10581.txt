The High Court directed the Registrar of Co-operative Societies to sanction posts of Chowkidars within a month, as proposed by the Co-operative Bank.
However, the sanction was not given in the stipulated time and contempt proceedings were initiated against the Registrar.
Nevertheless, more time was granted to the Registrar to accord the sanction.

According to the Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, the Administrator appointed by the Registrar Cooperative Societies has the right to exercise all powers and functions of the Board of Directors of the Cooperative Bank when they are not elected and in the absence of any provision to the contrary.
