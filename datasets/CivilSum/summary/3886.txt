Constitution of India, Article 226/227 - Revision of pay scales of petitioners effective January 1, 1978, and arrears paid from July 1, 1981, with additional DA merged in pay at 320 points of price index excluded - Petitioner accepted revised pay scale as per settlement between management and recognised Union/Association of DESU, examined by different authorities/committees including representatives of employees and management who found the decision valid.
Court interference only necessary in case of grave error in pay scale determination.
The principle of waiver estoppel applies as petitioner accepted Order conditions.
Negotiations between employer and recognised Trade Union encouraged, and Pay Commission Report recommendations not automatically applicable to DESU employees.
Court dismissed petitioners' claims deemed unjustified.
