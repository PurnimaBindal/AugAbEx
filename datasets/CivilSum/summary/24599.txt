Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Compensatory Allowance - Beas Project - Employees who joined Project after 1491972 had no choice regarding pay-scale - Order granting compensatory allowance was applicable on 14.9.1972 - No mention of employees who joined Project after 1491972 being given option for compensatory allowance or terminal cash benefit - If employees who joined Project before 1491972 had the option for compensatory allowance, then there is no rational basis for denying it to employees who joined Project after 1491972 - Even if they choose compensatory allowance, they cannot claim cash benefits that will only be credited to the State Government and will not cause extra burden for the State Government - Order denying option is arbitrary and discriminatory - Payment of compensatory allowance is not tied to unified pay-scale - Clear in the orders reducing rate of compensatory allowance that employees are entitled to receive compensatory allowance at a reduced rate regardless of their option to receive their pay in unified pay-scale.
