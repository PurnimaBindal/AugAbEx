Sihota, AAG, Punjab argues that as per Constitution of India, Article 310, pension is a right of an employee and must be paid as per statutory rules.
Despite demands, the petitioner was not paid pension for 16 years after retirement, which caused him pain and suffering.
The petitioner's legal heirs are entitled to 18% interest on delayed payments and Rs. 10,000/- as litigation costs.
