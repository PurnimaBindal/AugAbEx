Education Rules, 1959 (Kerala) Ch.
XIVA, Rules 1(3) and 51A - Preference - Appointment on permanent vacancies - The petitioner was appointed on a permanent vacancy temporarily, but Rule 51A only provides preference for future appointments to teachers who have been relieved under rule 49 or 52, or due to termination of vacancies and not appointed permanently.
As such, the petitioner cannot seek benefits under Rule 51A The court held that the petitioner is not entitled to preference for future appointments to permanent posts.
