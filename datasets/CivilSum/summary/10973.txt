Appellant, a constable appointed by Inspector General of Police, was dismissed by Superintendent of Police without jurisdiction, and the order was converted into an order of compulsory retirement by DIG on appeal.
The orders violated Article 311(1) of the Constitution of India, and the respondents were directed to compensate the appellant with salary and benefits from the date of superannuation.
[Paras 8 and 9] B.
Although the Superintendent of Police had become the appointing authority by virtue of Rule 2-A of the Rajasthan Police Subordinate Service Rules, the plaintiff's constitutional guarantee under Article 311 could not be taken away by framing the rules.
The plaintiff, who was appointed by IG Police, could not be dismissed by SP, and subsequent authorization did not undermine the protection provided by Article 311.
