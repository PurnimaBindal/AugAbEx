According to Articles 226 and 311 of the Constitution of India, if criminal proceedings and departmental proceedings are initiated against an employee on the same set of facts and the charges are severe, it is advisable for the employer to suspend the departmental proceedings.
This is to ensure that the employee doesn't have to reveal their defense which could adversely impact their criminal trial.
However, if the charges are not serious, the employer need not suspend the departmental proceedings until the criminal proceedings are over.
