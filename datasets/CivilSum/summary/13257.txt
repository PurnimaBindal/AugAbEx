According to Section 4-A(3) of the Workman Compensation Act, as well as Sections 95 and 96 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, an insurance company is required to cover not only the principal amount of compensation awarded, but also any interest and associated costs imposed by either the Commissioner under the WCA or the Tribunal under the MVA.
However, any penalty amount must be borne by the truck owner.
