Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punishment - Allegation that petitioner negligently cleared two forged drafts - Bank loss of Rs. 28,500/- - Appellate authority mitigated stoppage of three annual increments with cumulative effect to lesser punishment of stoppage of only one annual increment without cumulative effect - Petitioner permitted to pay Rs. 28,500 in installments - Punishment imposed does not warrant interference.
[Paras 2 and 8]
