According to Section 10(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, during conciliation proceedings for a reference, the government cannot refuse to make a reference based on the ground that the workman was offered retrenchment compensation.
Furthermore, if the employer offering the compensation denies the relationship of being the employer of the workman and does not reference back wages, the workman cannot decline the offer to make it a reason to not refer the dispute to the Labour Court.
