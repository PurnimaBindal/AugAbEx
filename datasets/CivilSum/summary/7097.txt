Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Punjab Civil Services Rules Volume II, Rule 15.32-B(1) - Resignation - Limitation - In the event that the resignation was not accepted within one month of 2261992, the limitation for such a suit is three years - Petitioner submitted resignation on 2261992 and withdrew it on 31121999, which is after a period of 7-1/2 years - The Order of Lower Appellate Court dismissing the suit on the ground of limitation was confirmed.
