According to Sections 2(1), 2(g) and 4 of Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 (Central Act.
No. 8 of 1923), a vehicle driver who is injured in an accident and deemed unfit to drive cannot be considered to have lost his complete earning capacity.
Even if the worker is incapable of performing their previous duties due to partial permanent disablement, this does not constitute total disablement unless the injury causes a 100% or more than 100% loss of earning capacity, as stated in Part II of Schedule I of the Workmens' Compensation Act.
