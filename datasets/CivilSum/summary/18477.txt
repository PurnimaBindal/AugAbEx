Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Central Secretariat Service (Preparations of Common Seniority List) Regulations, 1970, Regulation 3 - Central Secretariats Stenographers Service Rules, 1969, Rule 2(C) - Promotion - Ad hoc promotions were used as a stop-gap measure and not considered regular promotions - The cause of action relates to the threatened reversion of petitioners from promoted posts to which they were promoted on an ad hoc basis when their seniors became eligible for promotion and when they were given promotion on an ad hoc basis - The petitioners are not entitled to relief, but they may challenge regular promotions if necessary.
