State Bank of India (Supervisory Staff) (Services) Rules, rule 50-A(1)(a) - House rent-Suspension - A bank employee who is suspended from service and was provided lease-based accommodation, which was paid for by the bank at a 12 1/2% deduction from the employee's salary, must have their rent paid by the bank to the landlord until a final decision is made on the suspension case.
