According to the Administrative Tribunal Act, 1985, Sections 14 and 19, the jurisdiction of the Tribunal extends to the resolution of disputes and complaints regarding the recruitment and conditions of service of individuals appointed to public services and positions connected to the affairs of the Union or State within the territory of India and controlled by the Government of India or any other authority it owns or controls.
[Para 7]
Writ jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution of India is not applicable to disputes regarding the recruitment of Group-D posts under the Central Government.
In such cases, the Central Administrative Tribunal is the appropriate forum for grievances to be heard.
