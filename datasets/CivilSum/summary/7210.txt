The Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 47 strictly prohibits discharge or removal by way of pre-mature retirement from service, or reduction in rank of pay on account of disability acquired during service - Persons disabled must be adjusted in any suitable job in the same pay and emoluments, even if a supernumerary post is to be created - Any policy made prior to the Act or after the discharge of the person on that ground will be of no consequence as to exclusion of the post from the Act.

I, Part-II (as in Haryana), Rule 5.18 only allows for pre-mature retirement on specified grounds - It does not allow for pre-mature retirement on medical grounds unless the employee requests it.
