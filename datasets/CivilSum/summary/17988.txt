Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 173, Para 7(b) of Appendix II - Disability pension - The petitioner was initially denied disability pension due to the belief that the disability was not caused by or exacerbated by their military service, as they had not served in a field area or undergone strenuous duties - However, according to the regulations, if a disease leads to a discharge, it is considered to have arisen within the scope of military service - The petitioner was examined upon joining the military and was not found to be suffering from any such ailment - Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to disability pension, and their petition was granted with the payment of arrears with an interest rate of 12%.
