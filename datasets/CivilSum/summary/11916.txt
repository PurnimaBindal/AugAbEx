Punjab Service of Engineers, Class I PWD (Public Health Branch) Rules, 1961, Rule 8, clauses (10), (11) and (13) - Promotion - Appointment to the higher posts is based on seniority-cum-merit - The appointment of service must be made from the list approved by the Commission under Clause (10) in the order of the names placed by the Commission, and the officer cannot be denied the right to appointment to Class I Service at their turn, except in situations of deterioration of work according to Clause (13).
[Para 8]

Punjab Service of Engineers, Class I PWD (Public Health Branch) Rules, 1961, Rule 8 - Promotion/Supersession/Discrimination/Conditional promotion/Reversion - The Government can correct earlier orders and restore seniority of a senior officer if they were ignored, and the junior promoted subject to the senior's pending claim, nor is a principle of natural justice involved in deciding representation of one officer.
[Paras 9, 11, 13, 15 and 16]

Punjab Service of Engineers, Class I PWD (Public Health Branch) Rules, 1961, Rule 11 - Probation - Seniority - Natural justice - Period of probation can only be extended if the officer's performance is found unsatisfactory, and if the result of such an order has the effect of restoring the seniority of a concerned employee, others cannot object.
