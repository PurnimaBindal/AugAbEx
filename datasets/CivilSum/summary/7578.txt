Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Instrumentality of State - Sports Association - Chandigarh Rowing Association receives public funds and is affiliated to the Rowing Federation of India and the concerned ministry of Union of India - Although it is not a private business, it was created to develop sports and uses public waters for sports events - Thus, it is an instrumentality of State under Article 12 and can be subject to writ jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection for sports events - Arbitrariness in selection - All actions of State instrumentalities must be transparent and fair - Rowing Sport event selection process previously held was arbitrary and ignored top performers - Petitioner called for a transparent procedure for selection - While previous events cannot be undone, no subjective relief can be granted - Petition dismissed.
