Constitution of India, Articles 310 and 311 - Compulsory retirement - Adverse remarks in ACRs - Promotion after adverse remarks - Promotion does not remove adverse entries from the service record - The competent authority may consider the entire service record to determine if the employee's retention in public service after the age of 55 years is in the public interest or not - Public interest is the paramount consideration - Courts cannot dictate how the competent authority must form their opinion - Criteria for promotion are separate from those for compulsory retirement - Mere promotion after adverse remarks will not prevent compulsory retirement.
