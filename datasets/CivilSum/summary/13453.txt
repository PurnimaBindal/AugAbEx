Industrial Disputes Act, Section 33-C(2) - Back Wages - After the revisional authority accepted the revision petition against the workman's termination order and ordered his reinstatement, the workman filled application U/s 33-C(2) three years later - The Tribunal was wrong in awarding interest @ 18% from the date of reinstatement order, as it should only be payable from the date of filing the application U/s 33-C(2) - Recovery of interest from officials responsible not allowing the workman to join - Rate of interest reduced to 6%.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]
Back Wages - Reinstatement - If the termination order is set aside, the reinstatement order relates back to the date of termination - Payment of back wages is necessary - If the employer cannot prove the workman's gainful employment during the intervening period, the Tribunal's direction to pay back wages cannot be challenged.
