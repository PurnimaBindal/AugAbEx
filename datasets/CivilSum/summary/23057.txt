Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation - Permanent appointment - Regularisation of a casual or irregularly appointed employee will not result in a permanent status in government employment.
[Paras 15, 16, 19] B Andhra Pradesh State & Subordinate Service Rules, 1962, Rule 29 - Confirmation - Probation - Lien - Termination of lien - Unless an individual was permanently appointed to a post, they do not have a lien on the post, and their lien cannot be terminated if they move to another department before confirmation.
