Punjab Govt. National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rule 4 - Military Service Benefit - Those who have served during the Chinese aggression and Indo-Pak War are entitled to increments, seniority and pension in civil services of the state - This benefit cannot be refused, even if the person was discharged on compassionate grounds from the military at their own request.
