Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Grant of Selection Grade - Payment of retiral benefits - Principles of Natural Justice - Assistant Librarian was granted Selection Grade of Rs. 3700-5700 w.e.f.
12.1.1989 and her retiral benefits were calculated on pay scale of Rs. 3700-5700.
However, after she retired from service, her Selection Grade was withdrawn without any notice to her, which was deemed unjustified.
The petitioner made requests to the university for a second refresher course, but timely action was not taken, resulting in her retirement without fault.
As a result, the petition was allowed and the respondents were directed to release the pension to the petitioner by holding that she deserves to get the Selection Grade w.e.f.
12.1.1989.
