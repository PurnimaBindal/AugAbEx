UCO Bank's Manual on Disciplinary Action and Related Matters, Clause 22(8) - United Commercial Bank (Conduct and Discipline and Appeal) Regulation, 1976, Regulations 15(2), 12(1)(b) and 21(9) - Suspension of Bank employee - According to Regulation 12, suspension can be imposed in two cases; when a disciplinary proceeding against the employee is being contemplated or is already pending, and when a case against him for any criminal offence is being investigated, inquired or on trial.

Article 311 of the Constitution of India - Criminal proceedings and departmental proceedings - A bank employee is facing criminal charges in a corruption case - Departmental proceedings have also been initiated against him - Criminal and departmental proceedings operate in different domains - Even if the person is acquitted in the criminal trial, he can still be subjected to departmental proceedings.
