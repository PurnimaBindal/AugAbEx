Constitution of India, Article 16 - Departmental enquiry - Pre-mature retirement - A Patwari was subject to disciplinary action and served with a show cause notice proposing compulsory retirement.
The petitioner's service record was reviewed and it was deemed not in the public interest to continue their service after 25 years.
No malicious intent was found.
The order for retirement was based on service records and not the enquiry report, therefore it was deemed to be a valid basis for the order.
