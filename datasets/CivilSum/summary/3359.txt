Sections 8-B, 8-C and 2(e) of Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 hold the employer personally liable for arrears of the Provident Fund.
According to Rule 8-B(1), the Recovery Officer can use any one or more modes of recovery, whichever he deems suitable.
However, the Recovery Officer must first proceed regarding the properties of the establishment.
It is possible for the Recovery Officer to adopt the mode specified in Clause (b) of Sec. 8-B(1) before the exhaustion of other modes of recovery since there is nothing in the relevant sections of the Act which suggests otherwise.
The provisions of recovery in ESI Act, 1948 and Factories Act, 1948 differ significantly from those of the PF Act, 1952 and therefore authorities qua personal liability of employer under those Acts cannot be applied to the provisions of PF Act.
