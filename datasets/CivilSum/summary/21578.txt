Punjab and Sind Bank Employees' Voluntary Retirement Scheme, 2000 - Voluntary retirement - The applicant has the right to withdraw their application for voluntary retirement before it is accepted, despite the Bank depositing some retiral benefits in their account.
Such a deposit cannot be used to apply principles of estoppel.
[Paras 4, 8 to 13, 17, 18]  B Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - Punjab and Sind Bank Employees' Voluntary Retirement Scheme, 2000 - Voluntary retirement - The Punjab and Sind Bank is considered a 'State' within the definition of Article 12 of the Constitution and is therefore expected to treat its employees fairly.
