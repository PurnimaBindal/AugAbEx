Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dismissal - Natural justice - Show cause notice - A Division Bench set aside the dismissal order of a Bank employee because a second show cause notice was not issued before imposing the penalty of dismissal.
The Bench allowed the Bank to proceed again only after issuing the second show cause notice against the proposed punishment.
The delinquent officer did not take advantage of this opportunity, and the Bank Management again dismissed the employee.
The employee was given a copy of the inquiry report and asked to submit an explanation in the light of the earlier Division Bench Judgment, but he raised contentions not relevant to the issue.
It was held that the bank employee was given a reasonable opportunity before imposing the penalty of dismissal.
The previous judgment and order were set aside, and the dismissal order passed by the management was held proper.
[Paras 11 and 12]
