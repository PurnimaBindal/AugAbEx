In accordance with Article 226 of the Indian Constitution, Regulation 12 of the Punjab State Electricity Board Main Service Regulations 1972, and Section 13-B of the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, employees of the Board and Hydel Industrial Establishment are subject to certified standing orders.
Clause 25 of these standing orders stipulates premature retirement, which is applicable despite the 1972 regulations not having been officially gazetted by the State Government.
As such, the petitioner's retirement at the age of 58 years is in violation of clause 25, rendering the impugned order liable to be nullified.
