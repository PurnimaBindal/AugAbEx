The punishment authority cannot take into consideration things that were never communicated to the delinquent.
Both the punishing authority and the appellate authority should not consider ex-parte information, comments, or past service records to impose penalties on employees during an investigation.
The punishment of removal from service cannot be based on extraneous information; hence the order of the punishing authority and the appellate authority is a violation of natural justice principles.
[Paras 8 to 10]
A minor gap of absences from duty cannot be made a basis for the punishment of removal from service.
The punishment of removal from service is shockingly disproportionate, especially when the medical certificates for leave did not cover the entire absence period.
The punishment of removal cannot be sustained given this situation.
