Jammu and Kashmir Police Manual, 1960, Vol. II, Rules 3341 and 359 - Constitution of J & K, Article 126 - Absence from duty - Removal from service - The order of removal was contrary to Article 126 of the Constitution of J & K as no formal enquiry was conducted, no charge-sheet was published in local papers, and no chance was given to the appellant to reply.
The impugned order was set aside, relying on several cases.
[Paras 1, 12, 17, 19, and 20] B Jammu and Kashmir Police Manual, 1960, Vol. II, Rules 334 and 359 - J&K Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956 - Enquiry Procedure - The mode of enquiry envisaged under Police Rules is not different in any manner from J&K Civil Services (CC&A) Rules, 1956, which mandates the procedure to be followed by the Competent Authority while imposing punishment on any Govt. servant for any alleged misconduct.
