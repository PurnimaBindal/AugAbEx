The Labour Court found that termination was carried out without following the procedure mentioned in Article 311(2) of the Constitution and ordered for reinstatement with full back wages.
During the writ petition, the respondent reinstated the petitioner in services, leaving only the issue of back wages.
The petitioner agreed to relinquish 50% of the back wages, while the respondents were required to pay the remaining 50% as per the direction from the court, which was considered a decree under Section 21 of the Legal Services Authorities Act.
