Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(a), 2(k) 10(1) (c) and 36 - Industrial Disputes (Punjab Rules), Rule 36 - Industrial Dispute - Espousal by the union or a considerable number of workers - In the case where a union has taken up the cause of individual disputes, such disputes will be treated as industrial disputes provided it is supported by a substantial number of workers.
- In a situation where there is no union of workers in a particular establishment, the espousal by the union of workers belonging to other establishments whose membership has been obtained by the employees is also considered valid.
The ultimate test for determining whether a dispute referred for adjudication is industrial is whether it affects the interests of a substantial number of workers.
