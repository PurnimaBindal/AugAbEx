Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Grant of Service Pension - The petitioner demanded service pension upon discharge from the Territorial Army after serving for over 15 years and being declined.
The petitioner relied on Regulation 132 of the Pension Regulations for the Army, 1961, Part-I and argued that the regulations were amended in 1985.
The court held that there is no evidence to suggest that the Rules of 1985 were effective for those Army personnel who retired from the Territorial Army prior to 1985 and therefore the petitioner is not eligible for pensionary benefits.
