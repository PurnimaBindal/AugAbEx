Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Promotion - Seniority - The court has directed that the case of the petitioner to the post of Clerk against the promotion quota must be considered from the date the post in the quota fell due, based on eligibility and seniority, in accordance with service rules and provisions of law.
The direction implies that the petitioner's case will be considered along with the seniors' case and not to ignore their claim.
