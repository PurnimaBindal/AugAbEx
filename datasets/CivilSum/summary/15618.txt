Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Absorption - Regularisation - The petitioners were initially recruited on an ad-hoc basis as TC Volunteers.
After serving for ten years, they sought regularisation, but their services were terminated instead.
The CAT provided temporary status to the employees without absorbing them.
After a writ petition, employees were ordered to appear for a screening test, and some were then absorbed.
The present petitioners allege discrimination.
The respondents' actions were deemed improper, and the respondents were directed to promptly absorb the petitioners.
[Paras 6 and 7]
