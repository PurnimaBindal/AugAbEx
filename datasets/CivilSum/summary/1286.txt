The Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 2(e), 12, 3 - Liability to pay for death of workman - In a case involving construction work, the Principal employer who engages a Contractor, who in turn hires a sub-contractor, will be liable to pay compensation and has a statutory right of indemnification.
