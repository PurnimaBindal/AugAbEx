CCS Temporary Services Rules, Rule 5 of Article 226 of the Indian Constitution states that a contractual agreement can terminate the service of an employee with either one month's notice or pay.
Payment of one month's salary is not a precondition for termination and failure to make this payment will not make the termination illegal.
However, the petitioner at the most can claim one month notice pay.
[Para 8]

The petitioner was appointed on a contract basis at a consolidated pay of Rs. 2,000/- per month and was not appointed on a regular basis.
Equal pay for equal work cannot be awarded in the petitioner's case as she was not employed as a regular employee and did not go through the regular appointment process.
Thus, the petitioner cannot be equated with those who are employed as salespersons on a regular basis, as a matter of Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 39(d), and 226.
