According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21, pensioners of the Central Government are entitled to reimbursement for emergency medical treatment given in private or un-recognized hospitals.
Denial of such reimbursement is not allowed.
[Paras 8 to 10]
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21, states that the fixed medical allowance of Rs. 100/- per month covers only day-to-day medical expenses which do not require hospitalization.
For expenses such as treatment for heart ailments requiring hospitalization and by-pass surgeries, the reimbursement will be provided irrespective of fixed medical allowance.
[Para 11]
