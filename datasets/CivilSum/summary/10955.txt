According to Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol. II, Rule 720, in a suit for mandatory injunction, pay should be determined in accordance with Rule 720.
The plaintiffs argued that they were entitled to receive the pay they were receiving in the Army at the time of their retirement under Rule 720, and the court decreed in their favor.
The defendants appealed, arguing that the respondents were not entitled to benefit from the revised pay scales for Central and State Govt. employees and that the rule did not cover retired Army personnel whose pay had already been revised.
However, the court found no dispute regarding the rule and no provision for excluding military retirees whose pay had already been revised, and thus upheld the original decision.
