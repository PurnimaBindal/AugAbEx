Constitution of India, Article 311 - Misconduct - Compulsory retirement - The respondent, a Police Sub-Inspector, was found absent on duty and in an inebriated condition and was subsequently issued an order of compulsory retirement.
The nature of the proved misconduct was serious enough to warrant such an order.
[Paras 8 and 10]

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Criminal case - The respondent, serving as a Police Sub-Inspector, was found absent on duty and in an inebriated condition.
While he was later acquitted in the criminal prosecution on technical grounds, it did not bar the Disciplinary Authority from initiating disciplinary proceedings.
Unlike in a criminal trial, a departmental inquiry did not require the same strict standard of proof.
As a result, the Disciplinary Authority's action in holding the proceedings and imposing punishment was upheld.
[Paras 6 to 9]
