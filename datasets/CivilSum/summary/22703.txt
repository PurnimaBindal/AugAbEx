Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Equal pay for equal work - The Labour Court's authority under Section 33-C(2) of the Act is limited solely to awarding claims based on existing or accrued rights, not to determining or establishing the right itself - Since there has been no determination by the appropriate court of jurisdiction, nor acknowledgment by the employer, regarding the entitlement of daily wage employees to the same pay as regular employees, the Labour court lacks the authority to make a determination on the issue of equal pay for equal work under Section 33-C(2) of the ID Act.
