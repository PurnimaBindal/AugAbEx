Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Persons with Disability (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 33 - Reservation - It has been ruled that individuals entitled to be hired based on their own merit and applying for the 3% quota reservation under the Act will be treated as general category candidates and not be included in the 3% quota, allowing for it to be preserved for those who are eligible to claim it.
