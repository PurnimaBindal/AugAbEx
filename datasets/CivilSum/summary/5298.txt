According to the Army Act, 1950, Section 20(3) and Army Rules, 1954, Rule 17, a dismissal on the grounds of desertion should include a copy of the dismissal order forwarded to the delinquent.
However, in this case, the mandatory provision of Rule 17 was not complied with as the petitioner did not receive a copy of the order.
As a result, the termination is not considered valid.
Although the petitioner did not join duty and therefore cannot receive financial benefits, the respondents are free to proceed against him afresh as per the Army Act and Rules on account of absence without leave.
