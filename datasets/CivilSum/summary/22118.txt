Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Promotion - Length of service - In cases where no seniority list is prepared according to a rule of service, the normal rule of service jurisprudence of length of service will apply.
For promotion to Superintendent Grade I, seniority of Secretarial Assistants and Superintendents Grade II must be taken into account.
Seniority of Superintendents Grade II should be counted based on their date of appointment in the grade rather than from the date of appointment as Assistant.
