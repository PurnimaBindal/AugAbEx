Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appropriate Government - Dispute as to - A dispute arose as to whether the Delhi Administration is an appropriate government according to the Labour court - The workman was employed in Delhi and respondent's office is also in Delhi, but the administrative decision was taken by the Head Office in Bangalore - The Labour court's reasoning was deemed fallacious as it was based on an erroneous view of the law - Delhi Administration, being the appropriate government, had the jurisdiction to make the reference - It was also emphasized that the branches located in different places lack functional integrality and are considered as separate industrial establishments.
