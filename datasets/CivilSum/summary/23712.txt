Constitution of India, Article 229 - Allahabad High Court (Conditions of Service of Staff) Rules, 1976, Rules 3, 27 and 40 - Creation of posts - Premature increments - Chief Justice of the High Court has the power to create posts and grant premature increments - Once an order is passed by the Chief Justice, it cannot be ignored unless it is challenged and set aside - Premature increments granted by the Chief Justice are relevant for pension purposes.
[Paras 13, 14 and 16]
Constitution of India, Articles 141 and 215 - Judicial discipline - Division Bench should refer issues to Full Bench - Judicial discipline requires that Division Bench should not examine de novo an issue concluded by the Full Bench decision.
