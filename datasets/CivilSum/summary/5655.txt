Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 39 - Equal pay for equal work - Daily wagers - Employer has no disciplinary control over daily wage workers - Daily wagers cannot be equated with regular employees for the purpose of pay scale on the principle of equal pay for equal work - Judgments in CWP 17741/91 Neelam Rani v. State of Haryana and CWP 9192/95 Rajkumar etc. v. State of Haryana are overruled by Ghaziabad Development Authority v. Vikram Chaudhary.
