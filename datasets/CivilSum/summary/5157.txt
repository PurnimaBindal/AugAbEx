Section 6(6) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 states that review of an award is not permissible in absence of any statutory provision.
An application for review under the guise of classification/modification/correction should not be entertained.
[Paras 17, 23, 37]  B.
According to Sch.
2 Item 3 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the punishment for misconduct should not be interfered with by the Labour Court unless it is shockingly disproportionate to the delinquency.
The Labour Court cannot interfere with the quantity of punishment if the enquiry was fair.
