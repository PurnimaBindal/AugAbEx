The Administrative Tribunal Act of 1985, specifically Sections 14, 15, 19, and 20, contains provisions for determining the jurisdiction of public interest litigation cases.
The scope and extent of the Tribunal's jurisdiction must be interpreted according to the provisions of the Act, which means that an Administrative Tribunal cannot entertain a public interest litigation at the instance of a total stranger.
Only "persons aggrieved" have the right to approach the Tribunal.
