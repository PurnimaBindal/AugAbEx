If an employee retires but is found entitled to promotion from the date their junior was promoted illegally, they are entitled to notional promotion and arrears of difference of pay on that basis, according to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16(4) and Punjab Market Committee Class III Service Rules, 1989, Rule 8.
The relief cannot be denied on the grounds that two persons cannot hold one post and both draw salary against the same post.
