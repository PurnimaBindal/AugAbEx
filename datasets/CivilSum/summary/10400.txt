Constitution of India, Article 226 - Home Guards - Regularisation of Home Guards - The Home Guards is a volunteer organization that aims to have its members serve for a tenure of three years, and even though they are volunteers, they receive emoluments/honoraria.
There are no eligibility or disqualifications prescribed, and even Government employees can volunteer their services.
Regularizing any individual carrying on voluntary activity will destroy the very ethos and character of the Home Guards as it is not an employment or a source of employment.
[Paras 10 to 13]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delhi Home Guards Rules, 1959, Rule 8 - Termination of Home Guards - Home Guards - Termination by efflux of time on expiry of three years is automatic, and no notice is required.
Rule 8 is not applicable.
[Paras 12 and 17]
