The UP Recruitment of Dependants of Governments Servants Dying in Harness Rules, 1974, Rule 7 states that compassionate appointment does not entitle members of deceased family to claim more than one appointment.
[Para 4] B.
The cancellation of a petitioner's appointment and subsequent dismissal from service was not improper, as the petitioner's elder brother had already been given a compassionate appointment in place of the deceased family member.
If the impugned order is quashed, it would result in restoring the petitioner's appointment which is not in accordance with law.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to any relief.
