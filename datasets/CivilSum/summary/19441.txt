Rajasthan Subordinate Officers Ministerial Staff, Rules, 1957, Rule 25(5) - Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeals) Rules, 1958, Rule 16 - Departamental examination result cannot be considered as misconduct - Petitioner was charged under Rule 16 of 1958 Rules for not clearing departmental examination, which was illegal since he was only entitled to a simple discharge under Rule 25(5) - Stigma of punishment quashed and order converted to a simple discharge - Back wages and reinstatement not awarded, but petitioner given compensation of Rs. 2,500/- as costs of litigation.
