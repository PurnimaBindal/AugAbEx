West Bengal Primary Education Act, 1973, Sections 60(1)(k) and 106(1) - West Bengal Primary Education Rules, Rule 9 - Appointment - Selection - Post of teachers - Despite the absence of provisions for a written test or oral interview carrying 10 marks in Rule 9(c)(ii), the Staff Selection Committee can hold a written test instead of an oral interview under rule 9(c)(ii) - The term "written test/oral interview" should be interpreted disjunctively - The learned single Judge rightly concluded that there was no illegality in conducting only a written test.
