Evidence Act, 1872, Section 114 - Adverse inference - It is not mandatory to make an adverse inference for non-production of evidence - There may be justifiable reasons for intentional non-production - The background of facts involved in the dispute must be taken into consideration before drawing an adverse inference.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Judicial review - High Court cannot interfere with the discretion of a Tribunal unless it is found to be illegal or irrational.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F and 25-B, Evidence Act, 1872, Section 114(f) - Termination of services - The burden of proof is on the workman to prove that he has worked for 240 days in a year - A Tribunal may draw an adverse inference if the relevant documents are not produced, but the High Court cannot interfere with the discretion of the Tribunal unless it is illegal or irrational.
