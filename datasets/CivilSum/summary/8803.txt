According to Regulations 26(4) and 3(3) of the Reserve Bank of India Pension Regulations 1990, employees who were on leave preparatory to retirement on 111986 are excluded from the application of the Pension Regulations.
This exclusion is neither arbitrary nor discriminatory, and satisfies the requirements of the Constitution of India Article 14.
Employees who were in service as on 111986 and later retired before 1.1.1990 are treated as one group and given the benefit of option for newly introduced pension scheme, and the service during the period of leave preparatory to retirement availed under Regulation 26(4) is a reasonable and justifiable differentiator.
The increase in increments during the period of leave preparatory to retirement was only allowed for the limited purpose of calculating gratuity, and there was no intention to confer further benefits or extend the period of employment beyond the date of superannuation.
When the petitioner opted in 1985 to avail of the unavailed earned leave as per Regulation 26(4)(a), there was no pension scheme in existence, and no injustice was done by excluding him from the application of the pension scheme introduced in 1990.
The provision in Regulation 3(3) is not arbitrary, unreasonable, unjust, or violative of the Constitution of India Article 14.
