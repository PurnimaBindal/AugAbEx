Orissa Revised Scales of Pay Rules, 1985, Rule 8(1)(a) and (b) - Fixation of pay - A liberal interpretation of beneficial Rule cannot be applied when the plain language of the Rule is unambiguous - If upon revision of pay scales an employee would receive a minimum of the revised scale that is higher than their previous pay, they will not receive any further increments in the guise of a liberal interpretation - Sub Rule (b) is only applicable when the employee was previously earning higher than the minimum of the revised pay scale - Clauses (a) & (b) of the Rule must be read separately - There is no ambiguity in Rule 8(1)(a).
