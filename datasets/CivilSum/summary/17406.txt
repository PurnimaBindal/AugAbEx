Andhra Pradesh Wakf Regulations, Regulation 25 - Walf Act, Section 68 - Promotion - It is not enough for an employee to rely on seniority alone to claim a promotion - The petitioner declined promotion due to his mother's illness and was subsequently not considered for promotion after being transferred to Hyderabad - The select list cannot remain valid indefinitely - The petitioner cannot demand that his name be included in future lists based on past selections - His case will only be considered along with others.
