Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Reservation - Change in criteria - A general category candidate for Elementary Teachers Training Course was left out because of a reduction in the number of seats.
The petitioner claimed that the ratio between girls and boys was disturbed due to a change in criteria and that 2% seats reserved for the wards of teachers was not justified.
However, since 25 seats had already been offered to girl candidates and no evidence of prejudice was found, the reservation for wards of teachers was quashed.
Remaining 2% seats would be filled if available.
[Paras 6-8]
