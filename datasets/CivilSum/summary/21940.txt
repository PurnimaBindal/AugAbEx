Constitution of India, Article 16 - Haryana State Agricultural Marketing Board Service Rules, 1974 - Qualification and experience - An Assistant Secretary was appointed to the Haryana Agricultural Marketing Board based on qualification and experience, but it was later discovered that his experience in non semi-governmental organization could not be considered.
Consequently, the High Court voided the appointment on the basis that the petitioner did not meet the requisite qualifications on the relevant date.
However, since the petitioner has since turned 36 years old and has acquired sufficient experience, and his work as Assistant Secretary has not been disputed, it was held that his appointment as Assistant Secretary should be re-considered.
[Paras 8 and 9]
