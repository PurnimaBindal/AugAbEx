Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.24(1)(ix) - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Show Cause Notice/Natural justice/Personal hearing - Issuance of a written show-cause notice to the offender and consideration of his reply satisfies the requirement of hearing, failing which vitiates the punishment order if request for personal hearing is declined.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2 - Gravest misconduct/Findings - Dismissal from service - The Punishing Authority must record a specific finding on whether the delinquent official's conduct was the gravest, as the rules distinguish between misconduct and gravest misconduct.
In the absence of specific findings, punishment cannot be justified.
