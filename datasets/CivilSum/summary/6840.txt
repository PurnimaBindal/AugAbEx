Constitution of India, Article 309 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2 - Punishment - Misconduct - If delinquent escorts an accused person from Court to District Jail while drunk, then they will be penalized with forfeiture of one year's service and stoppage of one increment which corresponds to the misconduct.
[Paras 5, 8] It is improper for the Director General of Police to change the punishment to dismissal from service during revision.
