The medical fitness certificate is used to determine the superannuation age of employees.
A relevant circular states that a medical assessment about age should be made by a Medical Board consisting of the Chief Medical Officer and two other Medical Officers.
However, the petitioner was never examined by any Medical Board, so any medical assessment made by the Medical Officer-in-Charge cannot be considered a proper determination of age.
[Paras 4 and 8]

The petitioner provided a school leaving certificate and baptism certificate to support their claim about their date of birth.
The authorities ignored the school leaving certificate and accepted a different date of birth based on an alleged medical assessment, which was not conducted by the appropriate Medical Board.
It was determined that the petitioner's date of birth should be accepted as recorded in their school leaving/transfer certificate, and recorded in their service book and considered for determining their superannuation age.
