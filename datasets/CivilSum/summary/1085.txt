According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, candidates who have already taken admission in any subject do not have the right to seek change or consideration in future counselling, even if they are higher in merit.
This applies even when candidates challenge the non-inclusion of lapsed seats of All India quota and seek consideration for future counselling.
The question of comparative merit only arises among candidates who attend counselling at the same time.
[Paras 24, 25]
