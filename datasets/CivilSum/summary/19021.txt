Constitution of India, Article 226 - Confirmation - The seniority for the purpose of confirmation is based on the district and not the range - The petitioner claims that respondent No. 4 was confirmed despite being junior to the petitioner as Head Constable, which ignores the petitioner's right to be confirmed earlier - However, respondent No. 4 works in a different district than the petitioner, and therefore, the petitioner cannot compare her case for the purpose of confirmation with that of respondent No. 4.
