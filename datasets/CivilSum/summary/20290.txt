DANI (Delhi and Andaman and Nicobar Islands) Police Service Rules, 1971, Rules 29 and 25 - Seniority - Inter se seniority between promotees and direct recruits - To ensure the efficient functioning of the service, despite their having been first appointment on officiating basis, respondents must be treated as having permanent appointments - Rule 29 requires dates of officiating appointments to be considered as the dates of regular appointments and respondents placed in seniority list accordingly - Denial of the respondents' long period of service for seniority is unjustified and arbitrary, not befitting a model employer.
