The Government has the power to classify ministerial service, including process servers, into lower and higher grade posts based on qualifications and duties, and to determine their placement in ministerial service or exclusion from it.
As long as the policy is based on rational criteria, the court cannot intervene.
However, the qualifications and conditions of service for process servers are different from those of Class III clerical posts in the Judicial Establishment.
They have a retirement age of 60, not 58, and cannot claim to be equated to Class III clerks for pay scale and status.
In terms of Fundamental Rule 56 and Articles 481 to 485 of the Civil Services Regulations, process servers continue to be regarded as non-ministerial and inferior Government servants.
