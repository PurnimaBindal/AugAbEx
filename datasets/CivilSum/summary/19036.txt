According to Karnataka Civil Service Rules, Rule 294(V), which relates to family pension, a retired government servant's spouse cannot claim for family pension if they were not married while the government servant was still in service.
The petitioner in this case got married to the government servant after he had already retired from service.
As per the aforementioned rule, the petitioner cannot be regarded as a member of the family and therefore does not qualify for the family pension.
Consequently, the claim for the family pension was denied.
