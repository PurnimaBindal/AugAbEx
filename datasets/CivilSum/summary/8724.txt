Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Punishment - Quantum of punishment - The Labour Court determined that the punishment of dismissal from service imposed on a workman charged with embezzlement of a small amount was disproportionate.
As a result, the workman was ordered to be reinstated with continuity of service without any error of jurisdiction.
[Paras 7 & 8]

Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 11-A - Back wages - Reinstatement - In a case where a departmental enquiry had been held vitiated on a technical ground, a workman made an admission of guilt at the time of enquiry.
As a result, the general principle of awarding back wages would not be applied in such situation.
[Para 9]
