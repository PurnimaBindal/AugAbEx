Constitution of India, Article 226 - Death-cum-retirement benefits - Delay - Interest - Cost - Petitioner experienced a 19-year delay in receiving death-cum-retirement benefits due to the laches and negligence of the respondent.
All retiral dues were eventually paid after a court order.
The petitioner is entitled to interest at the rate of 12% per annum on all retiral dues and Rs. 25000 to cover mental pain, agony, harassment, and torture suffered for 20 years.
The government may hold the responsible officer accountable for negligence and recover funds from them.
[Paras 6 and 7]
