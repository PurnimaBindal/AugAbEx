West Bengal Primary Education Act of 1973, Section 105, and Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India govern the selection process for primary school Assistant Teachers in 25 circles which were notified by the board.
Panels were prepared for each circle and provisional approval was granted by the Director of School Education (DSE) based on these panels.
However, the procedure for preparing the circle-wise panel was found to be improper, resulting in a recasting of the panel ordered by the Division Bench.
Although the DSE re-arranged the panel, rather than recasting it, which resulted in those with lesser marks being inducted and affected the petitioner's merit position, leading to a writ petition challenging their exclusion from the panel.
The trial judge issued directions based on this, but they are also under challenge.
The entire panel may be cancelled in the event of gross irregularities during its preparation, and those who have been appointed should be represented.
The matter should be considered again, with the DSE required to produce the panel.
The impugned judgment and order are set aside, and the matter is referred back to the appropriate bench to pass an order in accordance with the law.
