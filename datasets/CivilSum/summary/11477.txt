According to Section 21 of the Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions Act, 1992, teachers of private educational institutions can claim gratuity.
The petition is dismissed as the tribunal, which is a forum provided by the State Government under the 1992 Act, made an order for the payment of gratuity while keeping in view the Act and rules that provide for the benefit of gratuity.
