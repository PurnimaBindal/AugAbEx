Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Officiating service as current duty charge - Promotion - In the absence of a DPC for an extended period of time, employees who are given current duty charge of the promotion post will have that experience counted towards their seniority and experience on that post for future promotion - Any ad hoc, stop-gap, or officiating service, among others, will be accounted for in determining seniority.
