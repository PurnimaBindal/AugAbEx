Constitution of India, Article 226 - Police Act, 1861, Section 7 - Bengal Police Regulations, 1943, Rule 861 - Disciplinary proceedings - Principles of natural justice - Failure to provide reasonable opportunity of hearing - Non-supply of necessary documents - Petitioner cleared of charges in initial report - Despite petitioner's repeated requests, inquiry officer did not provide the initial report to the petitioner - Judged that the report was necessary for the petitioner to prove innocence - Failure to supply report means denial of reasonable opportunity of hearing - Cancellation of departmental inquiry and punishment order.
