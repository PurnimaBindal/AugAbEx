Army Act, Section 122, Rule 14 - Army Rules, Rule 53 - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Proceedings of General Court Martial - Time limitations - In 1995, an enquiry was conducted after complaints of irregularities were made against the Petitioner and the records of the enquiry were placed before the competent authority in 1996.
Additionally, an order for convening General Court Martial was passed in 1999.
The defence argues that the trial is barred due to the time limitations.
According to Rule 53, the accused has the right to raise such a plea in the proceedings of the General Court Martial.
The writ petition cannot be entertained at this stage since it involves disputed questions of fact which can be raised and decided in the General Court Martial proceedings, and thus the petition is dismissed.
