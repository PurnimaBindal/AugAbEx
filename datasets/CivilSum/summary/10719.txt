Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Suspension - Quashing of charge-sheet - A petitioner, who is a member of a disciplined force, is facing charges for outraging the modesty of his own colleague in the police department.
Additionally, the petitioner is facing charges of attempting to rape a lady Constable by showing her revolver before a criminal court.
Amidst criminal proceedings, the petitioner prayed for the stay of department proceedings.
However, the department had already examined three witnesses who have been cross-examined by the petitioner.
The court held that it is too late for the petitioner to argue that their defence will be prejudiced.
As a result, the petition was dismissed.
[Paras 4 and 5]
