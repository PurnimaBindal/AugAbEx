Constitution of India, Articles 16, 136 and 226 - Promotion - Petitioners- employees challenged promotion policy of Steel Authority of India Limited before High Court - High Court gave directions on policy, but did not challenge promotions given to private opposite parties individually - Supreme Court held, promotion policy was challenged on the basis that it was illegal, arbitrary and liable to be struck down - Since the basis does not survive in view of High Court directions, there is no serious challenge to individual promotions.
