Constitution of India, Article 30(1) and (2) - Bihar State Universities Act, 1976, Sections 35(a), (b) and 35(2) - Grant in aid - Appointment and post creation - In the case of minority institutions and those established or maintained by State Govt., prior approval of the State Govt. is only needed for creation of post not for appointment to the already created and sanctioned posts - The institution is eligible to claim grant-in-aid against such post and make appointment without prior approval of the Govt. [Paras 4 and 5]
