Employees are entitled to gratuity, which is not a bounty but is payable on successful tenure of service.
Regulation 77 provides the calculation of gratuity, and Regulation 51 provides the rule of measurement.
Retiring employees are entitled to the benefits of a revised scale of pay, granting them entitlement to gratuity.
