Constitution of India, Articles 32 and 12 "State" - Pension - Burmah Shell clerical employees who retired over 10 years ago and are currently receiving pension are seeking restoration of the full pension - They had opted for a lumpsum payment by way of commutation of a part of the pension at the time of retirement - Petitioners argue that the equitable principle underlying the rule for restoration of commuted portion after 15 years from the date of retirement, which applies to Central Government employees, should be extended to the Corporation's employees - Corporation is considered "State" within the scope of Article 12.
