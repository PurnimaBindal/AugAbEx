The petitioner's name was removed from List-D and reverted to Head Constable from ASI without proper enquiry and charge sheet.
Orders of reversion were violative of natural justice principles.
No useful purpose would be served by directing fresh action since an enquiry officer already exonerated him after witnesses were heard later.
[Paras 4 and 6]
Punishing authority's findings differed from the enquiry officer's and punished the officer despite the latter's exoneration.
Witnesses presumed to be won over but the delinquent's charges did not differ, show findings not called for and liable to be quashed.
[Para 5]
Appellate authority dismissed the appeal through a non-speaking and cryptic order without referring to the various grounds it took in the appeal.
This is not approved.
