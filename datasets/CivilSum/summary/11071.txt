Under the Haryana Excise and Taxation Inspectorate Rules, ad hoc employees have no right to be promoted and may be reverted by the employer.
There is no final seniority list for ministerial staff and the petitioners have already held the position of Taxation Inspectors for a significant amount of time.
The court believes that the ends of justice are better served by providing an opportunity for the petitioners to be heard.
The reversion order is quashed, with directions to determine inter se seniority among ministerial employees and distribute vacancies accordingly.
The respondents must then determine whether any excess persons are holding promotional quota posts and whether it is necessary to revert the petitioners or anyone else.
Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India are also relevant.
