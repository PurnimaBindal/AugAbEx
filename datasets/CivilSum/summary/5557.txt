Constitution of India, Article 12 - `State' - The Council being questioned whether it falls under the definition of 'State' in Article 12 of the Constitution of India Analysis of its Rules, Regulations and Bye-laws suggests a significant amount of control is exercised by the Central Government.
As the majority of the Council's objectives are public in nature and intended for the benefit of the public at large, it is deemed to come under the definition of 'State' as per Article 12 of the Constitution of India [Para 15]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 311 and 226 - Termination - The legality of termination of service during a probationary period without an enquiry being held is questioned, as well as whether termination on grounds of misconduct amounts to punishment.
In this case, the termination was not due to the employee's incompetence but rather a form of punishment over her refusal to perform specific lab tests, thus rendering the termination as not a simpliciter version.
As fairness principles dictate, the employee should have been allowed to voice her case before termination, which casts a shadow of guilt.
As a result of flawed termination order and lack of adherence to natural justice, reinstatement has been granted.
