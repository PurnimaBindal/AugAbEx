Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14 and 16 - Delhi Higher Judicial Services - Service Law - Recruitment to Delhi Higher Judicial Services - The selection process included a written exam and interview/viva-voce, with minimum qualifying marks specified for the written exam but not for the interview - Introducing a minimum mark requirement for the interview after the selection process was complete would be considered changing the rules after the game was played - As a result, prescribing a minimum mark for the interview after the written test was conducted was not allowed.
