As per the Constitution of India, Article 16, a seniority list must be followed for promotions.
In this case, the petitioner was eligible for promotion but was superseded due to an adverse report.
However, the relevant remarks were expunged and the petitioner's case for promotion to the post of Assistant Malaria Officer must be considered.
The official respondents are directed to consider the petitioner's case for promotion retrospectively according to the law.
