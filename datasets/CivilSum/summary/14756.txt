Andhra Bank (Officers) Service Regulations, 1982, Regulation 8 - Ex gratia payment - It is clarified that arrears of salary paid due to retrospective revision of pay scales are not ex gratia payments, but a right owed to the employee.
[Paras 10 and 15] B. Notional pay - Gratuity and Provident Fund - Wages - Wages include notional pay - If an employee retires before the refixation of pay on account of retrospective revision of pay scales, notional pay must be included in the calculation of gratuity and provident fund.
[Para 24] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equality before law - Classification - The decision to deprive retired persons of arrears of notional pay has been deemed to violate Articles 14 and is therefore struck down - All those who retired after 1779 are owed arrears of notional pay, as no reasonable classification can be made between those retired before or after the Regulations came into force.
