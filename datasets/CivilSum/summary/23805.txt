According to Section 31(3)(b) of the Uttar Pradesh State Universities Act, 1973, the respondent was appointed in a leave vacancy and continued to work in the college for over 2 years - during this time, she was selected for appointment in the same manner that regular appointments are made and was qualified for such an appointment.
Due to the circumstances surrounding her appointment and the fact that the college was given permission to start post-graduate classes, it was determined that her appointment did not need to be disturbed.
