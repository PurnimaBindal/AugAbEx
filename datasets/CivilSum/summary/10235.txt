Civil Procedure Code, Order 39, Rules 1 & 2 - Interim injunction - Although mutation entries made in revenue records based on valid transfer documents are considered true, it cannot be concluded that they confirm an absence of title.

Civil Procedure Code, Section 115 - The discretion exercised by the trial court with regards to valid documentary evidence cannot be considered as erroneous, without jurisdiction, or suffering from irregularity, to warrant interference through revisional jurisdiction.
