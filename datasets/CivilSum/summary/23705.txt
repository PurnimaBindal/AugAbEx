According to Section 2(m) of the Factories Act, 1948, bidi rollers are entitled to the benefit of social legislations.
This applies even if the rolling is done in their residences.
Despite 95% of bidis being manufactured outside factories, those involved in the manufacture of bidis can still avail the benefits of the Act, including those who manufacture bidis in their own residences.
Union and State Government are required to submit written schemes to address the issues and suggest remedial measures.
