Education/Migration/Examination - Re-taking of Exams - When a student migrates from one institution to another, they must be considered a student of the new institution - If the student was unable to pass one or two semesters at the previous institution and had the right to re-take those semesters, they should be allowed to re-take those papers at the new institution - Denying permission to re-take exams after migration is unwarranted.
