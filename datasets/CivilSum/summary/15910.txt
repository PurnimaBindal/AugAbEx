J&K Civil Services Regulations, Article 87 - Charge allowance - Additional charge - The petitioner, who was posted as Joint Director School Education Kashmir in her own pay grade of Chief Education Officer as per Government Order, was not assigned any additional charge.
As per the essential condition for grant of Charge allowance, the petitioner is held not entitled to charge allowance.
[Paras 14 and 15] B Jammu and Kashmir Government Business Rules, Rule 12 - Government order - The order, signed and issued by the Additional Joint Secretary, downgrading the post cannot be said to be not a Government order.
[Paras 10 and 11] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural justice - Posting and adjustment of employees against the different posts - Government order adjusting the officers against different posts must not require the employees to be heard before such an order is passed.
