Karnataka Government Servants (Seniority) Rules, 1957, Rule 6 - Seniority - The seniority of respondents appointed in the Family Planning Section of the Bangalore City Corporation on a temporary basis comes next to employees already in service, as they were subsequently absorbed in another department on their request.
When an employee is transferred from one department to another in the public interest, the entire service rendered in the department from which he is transferred is counted towards seniority.
However, where the employee is transferred on his request, the seniority is counted from the date on which he enters the new post.
The respondent cannot claim seniority over petitioners already in service, and the order of the State Government is wholly bad, invalid and illegal.

Karnataka Government Servants (Seniority) Rules, 1957, Rule 6 - Karnataka Municipal Corporation Act, 1976, Section 98 - Natural Justice/Seniority - Violating the principles of natural justice, the order passed without hearing those whose seniority would be affected amounts to a violation of statutory provisions.
According to the rules, the show-cause notice by the Government before cancelling or modifying the resolution is mandatory.
Any violation of statutory provisions is not only illegal but also null and void.
