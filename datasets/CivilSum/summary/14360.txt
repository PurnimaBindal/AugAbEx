The Punjab Excise and Taxation Department State Service (Class II) Rules 1956, Rules 5 & 6, and the Punjab Excise and Taxation Department (State Service Class III-A) Rules, 1956, Rules 5 & 6, have been amended in the State of Punjab and are also applicable to the posts of Taxation Inspectors and other ministerial staff.
The amendment to any Service Rules applicable to the Ministerial Staff in Punjab State would be applicable to the posts in UT Chandigarh including the posts of E.T.Os.
5% of the posts must be filled up by promotion from the cadre of Taxation Inspectors as per Class II Rules.
Class III-A Rules have become redundant after coming into force the Class-II Rules.
