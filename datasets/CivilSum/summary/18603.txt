Constitution of India, Article 19 - Freedom to form Associations and Unions - Defendants demanded revision of incentive scheme and resorted to agitational tactics, including demonstrations, dharnas, inflammatory speeches, and prevented the ingress and egress of company Directors, officers, and employees - Plaintiff company sought permanent injunction restraining defendants from holding demonstrations and raising slogans within 500 meters of their office - Defendants proceeded ex-parte - Court ruled that freedom to form Associations or Unions is not unfettered - Injunction granted for defendants not to hold dharnas or raise slogans within an area of 100 meters.
