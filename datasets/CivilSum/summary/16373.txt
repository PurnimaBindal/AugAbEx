According to Punjab Recruitment of Ex-servicemen Rules 1982, Clause (II), Proviso (II), Rules 2(AA) & 4(1), the post of Principal has a reservation for ex-servicemen, their wives, or fully dependent children.
However, if the petitioner is not dependent on her ex-servicemen husband, she cannot claim the reservation.
