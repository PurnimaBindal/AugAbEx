Constitution of India, Article 226 - Petition - Amendment of - The petitioner's desire to raise disputes to be resolved by statutory appellate authority cannot result in a change in the nature of the petition, therefore amendment cannot be allowed.
[Para 19]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Statutory remedy - In cases where the statutory remedy available under rules has not been exhausted before filing the petition, no interference is permitted, as the examination of points raised may affect the merits of the case of either party.
[Para 9]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Petition - Relief - Entitlement to - A party's contradictory arguments and conduct are enough to prove abuse of process of court, and it will be disentitled from seeking any relief.
[Paras 17, 20]
