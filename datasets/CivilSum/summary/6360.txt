According to the University's Ordinances, a candidate must have passed the Plus 2 examination to be eligible for admission to a higher class.
However, a decision by the V.C./Syndicate granted concession to candidates with re-appear/compartment in 10+2, allowing them provisional admission to B.A./B.Sc./B.Com Part I, but their admission was subject to cancellation if they failed to clear the reappear/compartment paper by September, 1992.
The High Court cannot extend this concession beyond the University's instructions, and it must be strictly enforced.
Therefore, students cannot complain about the cancellation of their admission due to failure to clear re-appear/compartment paper.
