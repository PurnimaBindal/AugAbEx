Constitution of India, Articles 219 and 226 - Judicial discipline - Conduct of Judges - Judicial system is equipped with an inbuilt mechanism of high standards which is known as self-discipline - This is the responsibility of the Judges themselves - The power and position of judges is such that no one can impose discipline on them - The character of a judge is assessed by the power he holds - People's trust in the judiciary is due to the integrity of judges - The judiciary should be careful to ensure that the public faith in justice does not diminish - Judges must not have ambitions that conflict with their divine duty - When a judge betrays their trust for personal gain, it is a misuse of the power vested in them - The public interest should prevail over personal interest - Judges who misuse their position to receive discretionary allotments are beyond forgiveness - The allotment should be cancelled, and the asset sold by public auction.
