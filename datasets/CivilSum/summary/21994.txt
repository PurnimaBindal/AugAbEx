The Constitution of India, Articles 12, 14, and 16 relate to appointments in the public sector.
The constitutional scheme must be followed.
The state is required to comply with any statutory provisions.
Neither the appellant nor the state can grant any exemptions from compliance with statutory requirements.
If appointments are made in violation of the Constitution or the statute, they will be unlawful.
[Paras 13 and 14]  B.
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Termination of illegal appointment - The employee failed to appear for the written test and typewriting examination.
She was also absent from work without authorization for a long time.
A complaint was filed against her, but the period of absence was unsatisfactorily explained.
In an attempt to avoid termination, she requested either to be appointed as a typist or to be granted medical leave.
This request appears to be mala fide.
She has no right to continue in the position.
Directions to consider her case given by the High Court are unsustainable.
