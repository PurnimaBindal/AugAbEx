Constitution of India, Article 14 and Punjab Civil Services Rules, Rule 16.17 on family pension.
Petitioner, a mother, requested family pension on the grounds that the widow of the deceased was remarried.
The request was denied because the mother was not included in the definition of family.
However, the purpose of rules regarding family pension is to provide a means of sustenance for members of the deceased employee's family.
As every executive action should meet the reasonableness test under Article 14 of the Constitution, Rules 6 and 17 of Punjab Civil Services Rules Volume 2 cannot be sustained to the extent that they exclude parents of the deceased from the concept of family.
As such, the petition is granted.
