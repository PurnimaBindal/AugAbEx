Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 25-M and 25-Q - Lay-Off/Closing down of undertaking/Prosecution/Sanction of Govt.- Lay-off in the company due to shortage of electricity supply and deteriorated financial condition - The previous Supreme Court decision upholding the vires of Section 25-M means the provisions cannot be challenged as ultra vires - Prosecution for violating Section 25-M may be possible, although special circumstances may change the outcome - If the company is already too financially weak to operate, prosecution would not be justified - Complaints dismissed.
