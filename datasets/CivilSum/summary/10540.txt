Punjab Gram Panchayat Act, 1952, Section 102 - Issuing a show cause notice only provides an opportunity to the petitioner for a hearing to explain why action under Section 102 should not be taken - The petitioner filed a reply to the show cause notice, explaining that some meeting notices were not served to him and that the reports stating his refusal are incorrect - The Deputy Commissioner will independently decide on this matter based on the evidence presented before him - A suspension order can only be issued if an enquiry is ordered or pending, which is not the case yet - The writ petition was dismissed as premature.
