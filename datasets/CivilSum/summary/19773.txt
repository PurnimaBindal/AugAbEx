Regulations 4 and 16 of the Life Insurance Corporation of India (Agents) Regulations, 1972 state that no person whose spouse is employed in government or public sector can be granted LIC agency.
Despite this policy decision, the petitioner was granted the agency.
As a result, the Corporation has ordered that no new business be conducted with the petitioner until an inquiry is completed regarding the violation of the policy.
The matter falls under writ jurisdiction and cannot be interfered with.
