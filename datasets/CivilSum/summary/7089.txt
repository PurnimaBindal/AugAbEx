Constitution of India, Article 311 - West Bengal Services (Death-cum-Retirement Benefit) Rules, 1971, Rule 10 - Departmental proceedings - Retirement - The withholding or withdrawal of pension or any part thereof is permitted under Rule 10 if a pensioner is found guilty of grave misconduct while in service or after retirement - The validity of Rule 10 is upheld and it is not unconstitutional.
