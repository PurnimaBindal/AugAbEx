Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay - Equal pay for equal work - When the duties and functions attached to the posts of Excise Inspectors of two different departments are dissimilar, equal pay for equal work cannot be claimed.
[Para 3]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Recommendations of Pay Commission - Pay scales - Employees in a certain category entitled to a specific pay scale under the recommendations of the Pay Commission cannot be given a lower pay scale without reason - They have the right to claim the recommended pay scale.
[Para 4]  C Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pay Commission Report - Recommendations of Pay Commission - If the Pay Commission report is accepted by the Govt., the court can direct its implementation - However, it cannot direct the grant of any allowances which the Commission did not recommend.
