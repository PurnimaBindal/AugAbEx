In accordance with Mohammed Ramzan Khan's case, an enquiry report and personal hearing must be given to delinquents before punishment can be imposed.
Even if the report was not supplied with the show cause notice, it does not vitiate the previous proceedings.
The contention that a reasonable opportunity was denied is not valid as the delinquents were given a hearing by the punishing authority.
The order is not in violation of natural justice principles.
