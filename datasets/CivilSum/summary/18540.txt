The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226, along with the J & K Civil Service Regulations, Regulation 110A, deal with suspension and revocation.
Suspension orders of similarly situated individuals are revoked upon review of cases, following rules.
Therefore, the respondents are directed to consider the petitioner's case for revocation of suspension in accordance with the regulations and policies laid down.
