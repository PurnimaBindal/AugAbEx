The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Discrimination - The court cannot issue a direction to the authority to grant the same benefit to others when a wrong order in favor of an employee is passed contrary to the Rules.
[Para 8] B The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Recovery - Misrepresentation - Pay scales - Recovery cannot be allowed when a competent authority has granted a higher pay scale by mistake and there was no misrepresentation on the part of the employee.
