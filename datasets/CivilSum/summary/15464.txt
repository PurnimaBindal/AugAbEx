Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Police Rules, Volume II, Rule 1221 - Discharge from service - A petitioner who was recruited as a Constable in 3rd Battalion by Haryana Armed Police Madhuban on 22.7.1991 and was later placed under suspension and discharged from service has pleaded that the impugned order is stigmatic - However, the petitioner was under the disciplinary control of Commandant, 2nd Battalion when he was placed under suspension and remained absent from duty on a number of occasions - Therefore, the impugned order is not stigmatic and was rightly passed by Commandant, 3rd Battalion - Petition dismissed.
