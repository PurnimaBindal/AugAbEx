Haryana Affiliated (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Section 11 - Haryana Affiliated (Security of Service) Rules, 1980, Rule 8 - Termination - Power of review - The Financial Commissioner-cum-Secretary holds quasi-judicial powers when deciding a revision petition under Section 11 of the Act.
The revisional authority does not have the power to review its own order under either the Act or the Rules.
The change in Financial Commissioners does not give the new one the right to review an earlier order.
Furthermore, the first order was passed by the Financial Commissioner after hearing both parties and reviewing the record.
