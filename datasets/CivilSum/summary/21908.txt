Employee's State Insurance Act, 1948, Sections 2(22), 5(a), 39 and 45-A - Employee's State Insurance (General) Regulations, 1950, Regulations 31, 31-A - Delayed payment - It is impermissible to waive the liability for payment of interest on delayed payment, as it is a statutory requirement - Any question pertaining to settlement or compromise is not relevant to the matter of interest payment.
