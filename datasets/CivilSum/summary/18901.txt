Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - A petitioner has requested the quashing of an order from respondent No. 1, which denied their admission to the second year of a Bachelor of Physical Education program - According to the rules, admission for a higher class can be taken within ten days of the result of re-evaluation being declared - The petitioners sought admission to the second year within ten days of the result being declared, so the action of respondent No. 1 cannot be upheld - The denial of admission is quashed and respondent No. 1 must recognize the petitioners' admission to the second year.
[Paras 12 and 13]
