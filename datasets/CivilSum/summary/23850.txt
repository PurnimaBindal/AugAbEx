The Punjab Medical College Education Service (Class-I) Rules, 1978, Rule 9(i)(d) governs the reservation for promotees and direct recruits, which must be judged based on Article 16(1) and the statutory rules governing recruitment to the post of Professor in the Punjab Medical Education Service (Class-I) rather than Article 16(4).
Therefore, the reservation quota between direct recruits and promotees should not be interpreted as a ratio of 75% and 25% between the two groups, as in reservations under Article 16(4).
Instead, the roster would take its full circle, and appointments would be made based on points falling due, with the first post going to a promotee and the next two vacancies also going to promotees, but the fourth and subsequent vacancies going to direct recruits.
[Paras 9 to 12]

In the cadre consisting of five posts, Rule 9(i)(d) provides for a ratio of reservation for promotees and direct recruits of 75% and 25%.
However, if a promotee promoted earlier obtains a point reserved for direct recruits due to delay in the direct appointment process, the sequence of rotation under the quota and rotation system of the Rule would be disrupted.
Nonetheless, the next vacancies would still fall due at the same points reserved in the roster.
[Para 13]

The proviso to Rule 3 of the Punjab Medical College Education Service (Class-I) Rules, 1978 applies only to persons holding posts as Professors immediately before the commencement of the Rules, and their incumbency will be protected.
For those regularised thereafter, the proviso creates a deeming fiction to regulate their incumbency in protecting their rank, grade, and scale of pay.
Therefore, vacancies for those previously holding posts must be advertised as per earlier instructions, rather than being treated as part of the cycle of rotation under Rule 9.
[Para 16]
