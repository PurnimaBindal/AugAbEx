Constitution of India, Article 166(3) - Disciplinary proceedings - Governor's satisfaction - Rules of business of Government - The Governor exercises executive power with the help of the Council of Ministers, in accordance with the Rules of Business made by him under Article 166(3) - Article 162 regulates the Government's executive power under its Business Rules - The Governor is not responsible for all administrative decisions, unless required by the Constitution to exercise personal judgment - The Authority can proceed with the inquiry in accordance with the Business Rules.
