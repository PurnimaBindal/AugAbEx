Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Re-verification of result - 992 students out of 4076 declared as failed - Committee formed to examine and verify answer scripts - 294 candidates declared passed on reverification - Alleged irregularities in the process of re-verification resulted in the cancellation of the process - It was held that the result of re-verification cannot be cancelled without conducting an enquiry into the alleged irregularities - Therefore, the impugned order was deemed unsustainable and set aside.
