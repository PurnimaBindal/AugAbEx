Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 and 309 - Certified Standing Orders of Haryana State Minor Irrigation Tubewell Corporation Ltd., Chandigarh, issued in 1986, Clauses 3 and 16A - Age of superannuation - Service conditions of the employees are governed by the certified standing orders of the corporation - Age of superannuation for regular workman and Regular work charged workman is 60 years - No justification to retire at the age of 58 when similarly situated persons of the corporation retire at the age of 60 - Employees of a corporation are not Government Servants - The legal precedent is well established that age of superannuation for workmen is 60 years and not 58.
