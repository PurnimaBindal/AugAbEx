According to Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, termination of employment after completion of 240 days is allowed.
However, if an employee is repeatedly appointed for 89 days with 2-3 day breaks, it can be considered an unfair labour practice.
Based on these findings, there is no reason to interfere with the award.
