As per Section 10(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a reference was made to the CGIT regarding the maintainability of canteen employees of MTNL.
The contention was that the notification dated 11-12-1979 states that all posts in canteens and tiffin rooms run departmentally in Central Govt. offices are civil posts, thus, persons holding civil posts can seek relief under the Industrial Disputes Act, even if they were workman.
In response, the matter was referred to a three-Judge Bench to consider the legality of the notification and office memorandum, and whether or not a person holding a civil post can seek relief under the Industrial Disputes Act as a workman.
