Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Absorption of employees of a dissolved Society - Pay protection - Settlement on conditions - If employees acted upon a settlement for benefit, they cannot dispute its fairness.
[Paras 42, 47, 48 and 52]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Project employees - Absorption of employees of a dissolved project - Conditions of service - Employer cannot be mandated to continue paying if project is shut down due to lack of funds.
[Para 43]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Abolition of civil posts - Absorption of employees of a dissolved project - Any abolition does not entitle the person to further employment or right to hold post.
[Paras 44, 46 and 52]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Rajasthan Civil Services Rules (Absorption of Surplus Personnel) Rules, 1969 - Absorption of employees from dissolved Society - Employees of a dissolved project cannot be treated as government servants and be entitled to the benefit of Absorption Rules or Government Orders or conditions of service.
[Paras 49 and 59]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Daily wage workers - Regularisation of - Absorption of employees of a dissolved Society - No right on daily wage employees to be reemployed or treated like regular employees.
[Para 51]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Pensionable service - Absorption of employees of a dissolved Society - Pension fund contribution - Employees of a society that has dissolved are not pensionable and services rendered by them cannot be counted towards pension and retiral benefits.
[Para 54]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Absorption - Absorption of employees of a dissolved society - Pay protection - State has no obligation to offer alternative employment, but if a scheme to adjust employees is framed it cannot be termed unfair or arbitrary.
