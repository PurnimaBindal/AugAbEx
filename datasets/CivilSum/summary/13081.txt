Based on the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, the pay scale must be revised when necessary.
The petitioner was promoted from LDC to UDC after passing the Departmental Accounts Examination, and should therefore also be entitled to the revised pay scale or a higher one.
It is not acceptable for seniors to receive a lower pay scale than their juniors.
The question of waiver or estoppel does not apply in this situation as the petitioner was appointed as UDC after passing the examination, and the pay scale was revised subsequently to their promotion.
