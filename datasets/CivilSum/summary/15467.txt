According to Constitution of India 1950, Articles 14, 16 and 226, an entrance test for B.Ed.
Course was conducted.
The petitioner chose the medium as 'Hindi', and challenged the entrance test on grounds that the question paper was partly in English and the age shown was incorrect.
However, the brochure stated that both English and Hindi question papers will be available except for the Language Proficiency Test.
The petitioner attempted the question paper, and thus no prejudice was caused.
It was held that taking the medium implies writing the answer in a preferred language, and after participation, the petitioner cannot challenge it if the result is unfavorable.
[Para 8]

The admission process for B.Ed.
Course requires submission by the petitioner who later participated, but cannot dispute the same if the result is unfavorable.
