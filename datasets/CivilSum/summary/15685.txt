Constitution of India, Article 311 - Dismissal - Misconduct - A workman was arrested and an F.I.R. was registered against him - An inference of theft from the arrest and FIR was drawn - However, an F.I.R. by itself cannot be used as evidence to find a person guilty - The charge of unauthorized absence from duty was also not proven - The dismissal was found to be disproportionate to the charge that was proved - As a result, the workman was reinstated with 75% of his back wages and other retirement benefits.
