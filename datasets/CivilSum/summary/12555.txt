Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Punjab Electrical Inspectorate (State Service Class II) Rules, 1979, Rule 2 - Minimum qualifications - Appointment - The authority responsible for appointments has the power to define the minimum qualifications required for a position in the service - Also, they have the discretion to state that candidates with higher qualifications will be given preference - Such a condition is legal.
