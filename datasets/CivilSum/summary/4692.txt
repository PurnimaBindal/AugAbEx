Rajasthan Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 17 - Constitution of India, Article 16 - An employee can be considered for promotion, but they do not have a right to promotion - Promotion can be withheld as a punishment, even for minor penalties like censure - The order withholding promotion from the petitioner was not illegal and did not violate any fundamental rights.
