Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - Grant of - A petitioner was invalidated out of service due to acute intestinal obstruction and later assessed with less than 20% disability by a medical board without any basis.
The court held that petitioner suffered disability while serving in the Army and is entitled to disability pension for a limited period of 38 months prior to the filing of writ petition.
This is because the petitioner was boarded out from the Army in the year 1979 and gave the first notice after a lapse of 10 years and the second notice after a lapse of 8 years thereafter.
