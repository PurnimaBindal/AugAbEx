Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952 - Pension - Cut of pension - Departmental enquiry - The Punishing Authority imposed a 5% cut in pension and ordered the recovery of Rs. 44,640/- - The petitioner argued that the impugned order lacked any reasons which are necessary for any judicial or quasi-judicial order - The impugned order was held to be without reasons and lacking the proper and valid requisites of an order - The order was set aside and the Government could reconsider and issue a fresh order in accordance with the law - The petitioner shall be paid his full pension until a new order is passed.
