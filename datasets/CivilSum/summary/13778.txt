Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Recovery of excess payment - Payment made without misrepresentation - Petitioner's pay scale incorrectly fixed at Rs. 5300 instead of Rs. 5000 - Petitioner asked to repay excess amount - Court ruled that the pay scale was upgraded due to the authority's wrong interpretation, and employee did not misrepresent facts - Recovery of excess payment cannot be enforced.
