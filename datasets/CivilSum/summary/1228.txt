According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, management has the right to select the location of its business.
In this case, the management of the Jute Mill chose to shift from one place to another, but workers resisted.
The employer, who invested a lot of money in the business, has the freedom to choose where it operates.
The State Government's reference to an Industrial Tribunal is not valid, and any order suspending or locking out the business on these grounds is unjustified.
