Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension- At the time of enrollment, the husband of the petitioner was deemed medically fit and placed in medical category `A'.
However, he was discharged on the grounds of disability after being diagnosed with a case of `Seizure'.
Despite serving at various stations, including high altitude areas, the husband of the petitioner suffered an attack while in service.
As a result, it has been concluded that the disease suffered by the petitioner is attributable to Army service and, therefore, he is entitled to the benefit of disability pension, as well as family pension.
