As per Chapter 12 of the Punjab Pay Commission 1977-79 and Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution, the cadre of the petitioners, Asst-cum-Accountant/Junior Auditors, is interchangeable with the same pay scale.
However, as of January 1st, 1986, higher pay scale was given to Asst-cum-Accountants than to the petitioners, Junior Auditors.
There is no differentiation between the two posts and other employees in the same cadre have been given higher pay scale.
Thus, the state's decision to not give the petitioners higher pay scale from January 1st, 1986 is considered arbitrary and is in violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution.
