According to Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 136 - Recruitment and Promotion Rules for Class II Posts (Gazetted) in the School and Inspection Cadre 1980 - Implementation of Sunita Sharma v. State of Himachal Pradesh's Supreme Court directions - School Lecturers were included in the feeder category along with non-gazetted Headmasters/Headmistress - The Supreme Court's order in Sunita's case does not override actions that occurred prior to the Rules coming into force - Rules were applied after examining which posts were available for promotion to the feeder cadres - If Headmasters/Headmistresses of High Schools in Gazetted Class II category were already occupied, no promotion was possible until they become vacant - Rules of 1980 do not provide for reversion and hence there was no violation of Supreme Court's directions - These posts cannot be promoted from the feeder category until they become vacant.
