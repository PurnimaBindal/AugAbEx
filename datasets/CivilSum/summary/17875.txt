The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226/227, Selection, Promotion, Admission to Lower School Course at the Police Training College, Phillaur - The petitioners, who are members of the Scheduled Caste, were excluded from the Course, while those who were lower in merit were promoted.
Interim order has allowed the petitioners to complete the Course.
Respondent will ensure that the Scheduled Caste received their quota for the relevant year.
Those who were promoted on their own merit will be excluded.
If the Scheduled Caste received their quota as per the Government's Instructions, then the petitioners will not receive any relief.
If their quota was deficient, then petitioners can be promoted in order of merit.
