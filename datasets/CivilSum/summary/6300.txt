Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 226 - Recruitment of Primary Teachers Rules, 1991, Rule 9 - Appointment - Reservation - Post of Assistant Teacher - Petitioner requested screening result revision and inclusion of their name as an eligible candidate from OBC category - Absence of special notice regarding OBC category minimum marks - Interpretation of words 'other eligible candidates' - Court held that 'other eligible candidates' are not necessarily general candidates - Petitioner entitled to interview/written test as a separate category of backward class.
