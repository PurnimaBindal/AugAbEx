Constitution of India, Article 14 - Special Army Instructions 2/S/1998, Clause 12(C) - Revision of pay scale w.e.f.
111996 - Exclusion of officers of equivalent rank in Defence Forces who retired prior to introduction of revised pay scales i.e. on 111996 - Held that it would be arbitrary to allow officers who retired prior to 111996 as Major General to receive the same pension as Brigadier and those officers who retired as Major General after 111996 will get higher pension amount due to the benefit of the revision of pay scales; Such a situation is not sustainable and offensive to Article 14 of Constitution - Since the date of retirement cannot be a valid criterion for classification, the differential and discriminatory treatment in matter of pension is not based on any rational principle - Appeal dismissed.
