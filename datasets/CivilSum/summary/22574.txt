The Electricity Board has adopted Meghalaya Fundamental Rules, Rule 57(b) by passing a resolution which enables the Board to compulsorily retire an employee if it is not in public interest to keep them in public service after attaining 50 years of age or completion of 25 years of service.
This action cannot be deemed without jurisdiction or incompetent as the Board can frame any policy and adopt any procedure in the absence of specific Rules.
[Paras 8-11, 13-14]
