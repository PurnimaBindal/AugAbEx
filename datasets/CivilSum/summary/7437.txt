The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F deals with termination due to unauthorised absence.
In this case, it is undisputed that a registered letter was sent to the wrong person and never delivered, and despite notices published in newspapers, the petitioners failed to report for duty.
However, it was held that before treating the absence as misconduct, the management should have sought an explanation from the workman, in compliance with natural justice and principles of equity.
The procedure followed should be just, fair and reasonable, and as such, the order was set aside, and benefits were granted until the date of the workman's death or superannuation, whichever is earlier.
