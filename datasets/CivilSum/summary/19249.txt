Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination/Misconduct - An Accountant of Distt.
Co-operative Marketing Society cannot be terminated solely on the basis of allegations that they sold fertilizers to private parties, provided that there is no provision in the Society's bye-laws prohibiting such sales and deeming it as misconduct - In such cases, the termination cannot be considered justified.
[Paras 7 to 9]
