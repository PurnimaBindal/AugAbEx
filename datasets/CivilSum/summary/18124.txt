Punjab Police Act, 1861, Section 17 - Punjab Police Rules - Special Police Officer hired as daily wages in Government Railway Police - Discharged from service - Reason for termination was violating discipline and deemed unfit to be a good police officer - No chance for a hearing or inquiry was given - Recruitment order stated discharge was possible at any time without notice - The petitioner cannot claim the status of a Constable since he was never absorbed, therefore, Punjab Police Rules do not apply in this case - Being a daily wage employee under Section 17 of 1861 Act, no hearing is required, especially when the appointing authority passed the order based on a report from the DSP.
