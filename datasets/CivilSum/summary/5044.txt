Army Act (46 of 1950), Sections 39, 125, 191 - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 13(3)(v) - Discharge from service - The petitioner, who had been punished four times for being absent from duty without leave, was discharged from service due to another instance of absence without leave, in accordance with the policy established by the Army authorities, which is not unlawful.
