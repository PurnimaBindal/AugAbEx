The Maruti Limited (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1980, Sections 4, 5, 13, and 25 state that the new employer is not obliged to offer jobs to employees retrenched by the original employer in terms of Sections 25FF & 25FFF.
In the case of conflict between statutes containing non-obstante clauses, the latter will prevail.
Section 25J of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 will have no application where a different provision is envisaged under the Statutory Scheme.
The right to compensation for retrenchment on closure under Section 25FFF lies with the previous company, and not the new one.
When interpreting a statute, sympathy should not play a role.
