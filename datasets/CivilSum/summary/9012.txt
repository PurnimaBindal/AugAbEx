Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 21, 311 and 226 - Appointment - Verification of antecedents/character - Termination - Natural justice - Petitioner had no blemish against him at the time of selection - Registration of a case by police does not automatically reflect doubtful or unsatisfactory character of a person - Conduct of Board authorities in mechanically acting on misleading report of Police regarding petitioner's character, without hearing petitioner, is in violation of rules of natural justice - Impugned order of termination liable to be quashed.
