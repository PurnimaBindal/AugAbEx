Haryana State Agricultural Marketing Board Service Rules, 1974, Rules 10 and 16 - Suit for declaration and mandatory injunction - Claim of promotion - Junior promoted - SDC working in the same cadre as Auditors/Account Clerks - Appellant did not dispute seniority, but asserted that for promotion as Assistant, the seniority of Clerk, not SDC, is taken into account - Rules stipulate 5 years of service as a Clerk for promotion - The post on which this experience is to be gained has not been specified, and the post of SDC is apparently a clerical post - Consequently, according to the rules, respondent No. 1 is qualified for promotion since he worked as an SDC for more than five years - Appeal is rejected.
