Constitution of India, Articles 21, 226 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Timely completion - Departmental proceedings against employee should be completed expeditiously, but guidelines issued by State Govt. do not grant employees the right to approach Court for enforcement after the fixed period expires - An employee can only approach Court to quash proceedings if the delay in the enquiry is unreasonable due to employer's actions - Quashed departmental proceedings against petitioner due to inordinate delay of 20 years and 10 years to serve show cause notice.
[Paras 3, 4 and 5] Imp.
Point: Employee may approach Court for quashing of proceedings, if pendency of enquiry has otherwise been protracted and delayed to an unreasonable extent by employer himself.
