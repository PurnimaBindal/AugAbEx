Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Reserved category - Eligibility - For post of Assistant Teacher in MCD/NDMC - To judge eligibility with respect to educational qualifications, professional experience and date of birth, a cut off date of 30698 has been fixed - No cut off date mentioned for submission of SC certificate in advertisement - Petitioners submitted SC certificate after 30698 - Respondents must consider petitioners for Assistant teacher post and appoint them if they are found suitable.
