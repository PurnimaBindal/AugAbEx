The Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16 and 226 deal with appointment against reservation.
The petitioner was denied appointment based on the grounds that he was not recorded as ST in the State of West Bengal, where he was seeking benefits, although his community was recorded as ST in Bihar.
It was held that the benefit of reservation is applicable to SC/ST individuals from their State of origin, not the State where they have migrated to.
Consequently, the claim was rightly rejected.
