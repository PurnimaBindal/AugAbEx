Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Petitioner's promotion was overlooked due to an ongoing investigation against him, and a junior person was promoted instead - Despite the completion of the enquiry, the petitioner's promotion case was not reassessed - The respondents have been instructed to reassess the petitioner's promotion case from the time his junior was promoted, taking into account his entire record, including the penalty imposed on him.
