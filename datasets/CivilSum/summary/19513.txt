Under Article 226/227 of the Constitution of India and Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 66(i)(a), the Senior Regional Manager serves as the Appointing and Disciplinary Authority for all category III employees, including the petitioner No.1, who was promoted to the position of Assistant Grade-I by the Zonal Manager, hence the disciplinary authority.
The Petitioner was suspended by the Senior Regional Manager, who is also the Competent Authority for appointments, due to the authority which falls under the superior's jurisdiction.
The petitioner was selected for promotion by the Committee, including the Chairman and the Dy-Zonal Manager, and appointed to the post of Deputy Zonal Manager, effectively constituting no illegality in the suspension order issued.
