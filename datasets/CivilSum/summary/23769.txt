The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 233, 234 and 235 pertains to the promotion of District Judges to the post of Additional District Judge.
The Full Court of the Bombay High Court selected the petitioner on merit and placed them in the select list but excluded them from the final list without providing any reasons for doing so.
The Division Bench further failed to justify the High Court's decision and violated Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution by not disclosing reasons for not promoting the selected officer.
