Constitution of India, Article 14 - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 54 - Criminal proceedings on corruption charges - Suspension - Pay and other allowances granted on reinstatement on acquittal of delinquent - If the delinquent is acquitted on benefit of doubt and not fully cleared of taking illegal gratification, pay and allowance benefits except for subsistence allowance during suspension may be withheld.
[Paras 13-15]
