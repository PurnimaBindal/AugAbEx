Employee State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 53 - Employment injury - Compensation - The appellant sustained an injury to his face while repairing a television set and lost vision in his left eye.
As an insured employee under the Act, he is not entitled to compensation under the Workmen's Compensation Act 1923, and Section 53 of the ESI Act bars him from doing so.
The High Court was correct in its ruling that the appellant's application for compensation under the Compensation Act was not maintainable due to the bar created by Section 53.
