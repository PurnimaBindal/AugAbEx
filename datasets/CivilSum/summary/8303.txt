Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Scope of - Proceedings under section 33-C(2) are similar to execution proceedings - A Labour Court can provide relief through implementation of adjudication but cannot adjudicate a dispute - The petitioner's claim for over-time wages is a statutory right - Formal adjudication of the right to receive claimed benefits is not necessary - The Labour Court was correct to grant relief to the extent permitted.
