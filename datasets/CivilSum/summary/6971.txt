Constitution of India, 1950, Article 226 - Demand for pension fixation and arrears from 111986 - Relief granted by the government effective from 1.1.1996 - Original Application (OA) not amended to challenge the cut-off date in OM - Tribunal denied claim due to lack of OA amendment - It is held that if petitioners are entitled to relief by law, they should not be denied benefits simply because they did not amend the OA - Courts are not precluded from taking cognizance of subsequent changes in facts or law when determining entitlement to relief - As the differentiation made between pre-1986 and post-1985 retirees with regards to pension fixation is arbitrary and irrational, a directive is given to grant benefits from 111989 to 111996.
