The disciplinary proceeding resulting in dismissal was marred by a lack of natural justice as the enquiry officer's letter stated that document inspection would occur at the time of the final hearing.
This is an incorrect procedure and the respondent should have been given an opportunity for inspection prior to conducting an enquiry and hearing the delinquent at the end of the proceedings.
The High Court agreed with this and set aside the dismissal order.
Despite the respondent's superannuation, a fresh enquiry must be conducted to determine any back wages or pensionary claims.
