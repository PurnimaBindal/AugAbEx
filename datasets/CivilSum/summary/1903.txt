Central Civil Service (Conduct) Rules of 1964, Rules 3(1), 37 - Disciplinary action - Inquiry proceeding - Removal from service - An officer charged with failure to maintain utmost integrity and exercise proper command and control was ordered to be removed from service after disciplinary action was initiated against him - The conclusion of the writ court that it was a case of no evidence was preposterous and perverse - Rule 37 mandates specific standards of integrity and devotion which an officer must maintain, and failure to do so constitutes misconduct - The findings of the disciplinary authority were not in violation of natural justice.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Removal from service - Interference - Writ Jurisdiction - In exercising its power of judicial review, the court does not act as an appellate authority - Interference is only warranted if the proceedings were inconsistent with the natural justice or statutory rules.
