Under the BSF Service Rules, 1969, after the contract of service has expired, the employer has the right to discharge the employee without notice.
Even if the employee has tendered resignation, the employer's discretion to discharge, terminate or retire the employee remains total.
In this context, the fact that the authorities refused to accept the petitioner's resignation and keep him in service by force is arbitrary.
Such actions are not warranted, and the impugned order has been quashed.
The authority has been directed to accept the resignation of the petitioner and relieve him within a month.
