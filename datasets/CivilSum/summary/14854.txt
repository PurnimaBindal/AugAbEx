As per Article 226 of the Constitution of India, disability pension should be granted to the petitioner.
The petitioner was thoroughly examined and deemed fit before joining the Army.
The medical board never found any constitutional disease in him.
However, he was discharged from the Army due to 'Schizophrenia' and placed in medical category 'EEE'.
Given the nature of their duties, it can be reasoned that the disease is a result of his Army service.
Following the decision in 1998(4) SCT 88, the respondents have been directed to release disability pension to the petitioner.
