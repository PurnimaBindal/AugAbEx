The revised pension for retired individuals from aided institutions under the Punjab Privately Managed Recognized Aided Schools Retirement Benefit Scheme, 1992 cannot be denied solely due to financial constraints.
The pension is a postponed earned payment for services rendered and not a mere bounty at the discretion of the employer.
The respondents' action is deemed arbitrary and unlawful, and the court has directed the release of all arrears of pension.
