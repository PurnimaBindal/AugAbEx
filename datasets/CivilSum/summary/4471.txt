The Tribunal denied retrospective promotion to a petitioner who sought to count his military service as per 1965 rules, holding that his application was time-barred under Section 21 of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985.
The petitioner cannot take advantage of a longer period of limitation as he has not shown that his representation was made under relevant service rules.
[Para 10 to 12] B.
A writ of Certiorari can only be issued for correcting an error of jurisdiction or error of law apparent on the face of the order passed by the Tribunal, court or quasi-judicial authority.
The mere possibility of a superior court forming a different opinion cannot be regarded as an error of law for the purpose of entertaining a writ of Certiorari.
