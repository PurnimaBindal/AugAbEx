Constitution of India, Article 226 - UP Public Services (Tribunals) Act, 1976 - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 11 - Res judicata - A second writ petition on the same matter before the same High Court cannot be entertained by a Single Bench and render the order of Division Bench as meaningless even though it does not amount to reviewing this order in exercise of the recognised power.
The writ petition was dismissed in limine by Division Bench on the ground of non-availing of alternative remedy and not on merits and its validity was not questioned in appeal or any legal proceedings.
