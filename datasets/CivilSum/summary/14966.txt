Rajasthan Compassionate Appointment to Dependent of the Government Servant Dying while in Service Rules, 1996, Rule 5 - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Compassionate appointment - After the petitioner attained the age of 18, 18 years had passed since the date of the govt. employee's death - It is unreasonable to expect the department to wait 14 years while reserving a post for the petitioner's appointment upon reaching adulthood - Petitioner's appointment cannot be a source of recruitment, especially given that the petitioner's elder brother is already employed by the department - Any policy by the department to wait for such an extended period of time would breach Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - Consequently, the petitioner's claim cannot be considered due to the inordinate delay in submission.
