Constitution of India, Article 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Taking over of Management - Private hospital acquired by the Government - Employees whose services were terminated by the previous management before acquisition cannot make any claims against the new management, unless stated in the instrument of acquisition - New management cannot be directed to rehire employees whose services were illegally terminated by the previous management - Any right the employee may have is to pursue damages against the previous management for wrongful termination.
