Haryana Education Service Class III Rules, 1955 - Haryana Aided Schools (Security of Service) Rules, 1974, Rule 6 - The petitioner was appointed as a Hindi teacher in a Govt. aided school despite not possessing the qualifications prescribed in the Haryana Aided Schools (Security of Service) Rules, 1974.
After almost two years, the Education Department disapproved the appointment and terminated their services without giving any opportunity to the petitioner.
The action of the respondents was found to be illegal and was quashed.
[Paras 6, 7 and 10]

Haryana Aided Schools (Security of Service) Rules, 1974, Rule 6 - Grant for the post of Hindi teacher - Despite not fulfilling the necessary qualifications, the Department continued to release the aid for the post held by the petitioner for almost 22 months.
However, without providing any explanation or reason, the aid was abruptly stopped.
This was deemed as arbitrary by the authorities.
[Paras 1 and 8]

Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 14 - Appointment - The Education Department disapproved the appointment of the petitioner without giving them any opportunity to rectify or explain the situation.
This action was considered arbitrary and against the Constitution.
[Para 8]
