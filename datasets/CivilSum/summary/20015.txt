Section 22 of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 and Rules 11, 12 and 13 of the Central Administrative Tribunal (Procedure) Rules, 1987 state that appellants must be given the opportunity to be heard before a procedure/counter affidavit is made.
The Tribunal's perception that expediency overrides the importance of a counter affidavit and hearing the other side is wrong.
The Tribunal failed to understand the procedural requirements of this case, and thus the orders under appeal are set aside.
The matter is remitted to the Tribunal for a fresh disposal in accordance with the law, including an opportunity for the appellant to be heard.
