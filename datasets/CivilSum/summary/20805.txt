According to the MP Forest Service (Recruitment) Rules, 1977, promotion to the role of Assistant Forest Conservator was based on merits and seniority.
A High Level Committee was formed to evaluate all potential candidates for promotion, but the appellant was deemed unfit based on their service record.
As the court cannot judge the relative merits of each candidate, there is no reason to interfere in this case.
[Paras 2 and 4-6]
