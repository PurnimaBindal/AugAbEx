Constitution of India, Article 226 - Haryana Affiliated College (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Sections 3, 4, 5, 6 and 12 - Appointment - Selection - Post of Lecturers - The selection process does not require a vacancy to be present before selecting candidates for the post - The petitioner was refused approval for appointment on the grounds that there was no clear cut vacancy at the time of selection - This was deemed improper by the court - A direction was issued to grant approval of appointment.
