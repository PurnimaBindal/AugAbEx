The change in priorities and inclusion and exclusion of certain games and sports by a new government order regarding admission under Sports quota for engineering courses does not alter the essential qualifications and eligibility criteria for admission.
Therefore, it does not create any confusion or uncertainty.
Even the earlier Government order in operation does not grant any vested right to candidates claiming a seat under sports and games category.
[Para 22]
There is no vested right to seek admission in sports quota.
The state can widen its scope by increasing the number of priorities and excluding an existing category from sports quota without invoking arbiter nature.
[Para 16]
Obtaining a rank in the entrance test itself does not guarantee admission unless the candidate satisfies other requirements of the rule in force as on the date of admission.
[Para 16]
Refixing the prioritization pattern for sports quota admissions, including and excluding certain games, is an expert body's jurisprudence, not for the court to question.
As such, it doesn't fall under the Article 226 of the Constitution of India's functioning of the High Court.
