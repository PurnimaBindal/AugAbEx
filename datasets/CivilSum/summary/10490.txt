Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admissions/Education - The Haryana Agricultural University PhD Students Hand Book Part-III for the year of 1990 contained a rule stating that a candidate who joins PhD should not be permitted to discontinue their studies for the purpose of joining service unless they have completed their preliminary examination.
Rule 20.2 did not specify that the application could not be addressed to the Vice Chancellor, nor did it provide that permission could not be granted for any other reasons.
If permission is granted on other grounds, it is not the student's fault, and they cannot be penalised for it.
If other students are permitted to rejoin the course, the petitioner should not be deprived of the same benefit.
The petitioner was allowed to discontinue due to financial difficulties, and the authorities must allow him to rejoin the course.
