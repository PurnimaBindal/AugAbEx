Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Reservation - Entrance exam for admission to MBBS/BDS Courses - Provision for reservation of seats for SC/ST and BC classes in prospectus - Petitioners from SC and BC classes who took the test were not admitted to the BDS course - Respondents filled up all seats from general category and did not admit any candidate from reserved category - Despite 25% seats reserved for SC/ST and 5% reserved for BC classes - Prospective conditions are binding and enforceable, as stated in the prospectus - Ordered to admit students from reserved categories on basis of merit.
