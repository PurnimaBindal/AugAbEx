Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-A - Misconduct - Disciplinary enquiry - Dismissal - Industrial dispute - The Corporation has created several independent zones for efficient coordination of transport services, and not all zones are part of a single unit - The High Court's decision that all zones should be considered as an integral part of the Corporation is incorrect - The pendency of an industrial dispute in one zone does not bar the management from taking disciplinary action against an employee in another zone - However, due to an undertaking between the parties, the respondent will continue to be in service without backwages.
