According to Section 7(1) of the Homeopathy Central Control Act, 1973 and Regulation 37 of the Homeopathy Central Control Regulations, an elected member and nominated member of the Council cannot hold both positions simultaneously.
An elected member must follow Regulation 37 meticulously and resign in order to become a nominated member, without any deemed resignation.
B Under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, when a statutory functionary makes an order based on certain grounds, its validity must be judged by the reasons mentioned and cannot be supplemented by fresh reasons in the form of an affidavit or otherwise.
C Rule 4 of the Homeopathy Central Control (Election) Rules, 1975 and Regulation 37 of the Homeopathy Central Control Regulations state that an elected member cannot be forced to accept a nomination to become a member and if they refuse, their name cannot be removed from the Voters list without voluntary resignation.
