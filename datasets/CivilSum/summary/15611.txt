Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - The Petitioner was deemed medically fit before joining the Army service and was discharged due to disability on 30.4.96 with an assessment of 20% disability.
The Respondent was unable to demonstrate that the Petitioner had a pre-existing disability prior to his enlistment.
The Court directed the Respondents to provide disability pension to the Petitioner from 1.5.96 onwards.
