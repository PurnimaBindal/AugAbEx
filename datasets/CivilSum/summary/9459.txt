Constitution of India, Articles 16 & 311 - State of Haryana Regularisation Policy dt. 18.3.1996 - Regularisation - The workman is not required to have worked the whole year for counting days - If the workman has completed 240 days in a calender year without a break of 30 days, that will be considered as working for a year - Petitioner worked for 240 days every year - Denial of regularisation only on the ground that the petitioner had not worked throughout the years is discriminatory and irrational - Judgement in Ram Dhari v. The Chief Wild Life Warden Haryana, 1996(1) SCT 615 has been followed.
