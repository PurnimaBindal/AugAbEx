As per Section 2(j) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Irrigation Department is not considered an industry.
[Para 1] B In regards to pay-scale equality under Articles 226 and 16 of the Constitution of India, it is not necessary that an increase in pay for one position must result in a similar hike for another position.
While two positions may have had the same pay scale at one point due to similar duties and responsibilities, the employer has the ability to alter duties and work nature as necessary.
Therefore, the Labour Court was incorrect in providing a commensurate pay increase for respondents based solely on the fact that their position had the same pay scale at one point.
