According to Punjab Municipal Staff Regulations, 1960, Regulation 39 (2), termination without providing an opportunity is not allowed.
Although municipal employees are not considered government employees, the municipal corporation falls under the definition of Article 12 of the Constitution as an instrumentality of the state.
Therefore, these employees are protected under Article 14 of the Constitution, and if their termination is found to be illegal, they are entitled to reinstatement due to their statutory status.
This is covered by Articles 12 and 14 of the Constitution of India.
