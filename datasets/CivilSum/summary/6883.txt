Constitution of India, Article 311 - Departmental proceeding and criminal proceeding - In cases where a disciplinary proceeding results in punishment prior to an acquittal by a criminal court, punishment of dismissal is deemed unlawful - In a case where a charge of fraud and theft is brought up against the accused, and the contention is made that disciplinary proceeding should be stayed until the conclusion of trial because a charge sheet was filed for the same charge - Ruling states that it is clear that on the same set of evidence, a charge sheet was filed in court and after a due trial, the petitioner was acquitted - Therefore, during the pendency of criminal trial, disciplinary proceedings against the petitioner were deemed to have been stayed - Dismissal of petitioner in these circumstances was ruled as an act of department in haste and oppression, and as a result, the order of dismissal and all consequential orders were quashed and set aside.
