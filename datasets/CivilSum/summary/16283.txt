According to Section 39 of the Punjab Municipal Act, 1911, the Municipal Committee is responsible for making appointments and selections in accordance with the rules and guidelines of the Director.
Cancelling appointments based on vague remarks from the Director against certain candidates is not enough; if the selections were illegal, the entire process should be cancelled, rather than just cherry-picking which appointments to approve.
Without specific reasons for approving some and discarding others, the selection process is subject to the vice of arbitrariness.
In this case, the Municipal Committee's selections and appointments were upheld.
