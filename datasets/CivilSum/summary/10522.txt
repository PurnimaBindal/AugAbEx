Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Alternative remedy - Despite the existence of an alternative remedy, the court has discretion in deciding whether or not to entertain a writ petition - Neither the Apex Court nor the Constitution considers the remedy of revision as an alternative remedy - Objections to the dismissal of a writ petition on the grounds of alternative remedy are summarily rejected.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Discrimination - A petitioner employee of Central Reserve Police Force argues that he has been unjustly denied promotion on the post of Sub-Inspector despite earning two gold medals - The introduction of an incentive scheme for outstanding sportsmen has resulted in non-medal winners receiving out of turn promotions - It is argued that the competent authority is bound by the equality clauses in Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution to apply the same standard to the petitioner as it has to other employees.
