Transfer of cases from Industrial Tribunal to Administrative Tribunal is not automatic even after a notification under Section 14(2) of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, includes the establishment in the Schedule to bring it under the purview of the CAT.
The jurisdiction of the Labour Court to adjudicate pending cases will not be affected by the said notification.
Provisions of Section 14 of the 1985 Act does not nullify the provisions of Section 28.
