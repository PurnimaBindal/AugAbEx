The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947 encompasses Sections 25-F, 25-C and 25-H In the case of termination of a workman's service, the labour court granted one month's notice salary but declined reinstatement, alleging a violation of provision.
It was held that since the service was terminated after 210 working days, the workman is not entitled to the protection of Section 25-F of the Act.
The finding recorded to grant one month's notice salary is not perverse as the department's action violated the principles of natural justice.
