Constitution of India, Articles 14, 23 and 226 - Appointments - Termination - Natural justice - Ad hoc appointments against non-existent posts without following the prescribed procedure - Such actions by authorities are arbitrary and defraud the public - Such appointments do not create any right to continue service - Cancelling these appointments does not require a hearing as it is not a breach of natural justice - Non-payment of wages for such fraudulent appointments does not violate Article 23 of the Constitution.
