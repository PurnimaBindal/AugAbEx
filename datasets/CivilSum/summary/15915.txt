Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation - Equal pay for Equal work - Petitioners employed as casual labor who do not hold regular posts.
The authorities state that they will be regularized based on their seniority on the list as soon as vacancies become available on a regular basis.
They are not entitled to regular pay until their services are regularized against regular posts or they are posted against a regular vacancy.
