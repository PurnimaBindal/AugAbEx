Trade Union Act (16 of 1926), Section 29 - UP Trade Union Regulations 1927, Regulation 17 - Election dispute - Disputability of - Jurisdiction of sub-registrar - Petitioner has challenged the legality of the registrar's order on the grounds that the registrar lacked jurisdiction to determine an election dispute between the parties after the expiration of their one-year term of office - According to bye-law 37, the term of office for the elected member is one year - The registrar has no jurisdiction to decide the dispute when it is known that a fresh election for the year 2001 has already taken place.
- As a result, the petition is granted.
