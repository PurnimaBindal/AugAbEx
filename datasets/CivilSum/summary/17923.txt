The Haryana State Supply and Marketing Co-operative Service (Common Cadre) Rules of 1988, Rule 10 - Probation - Confirmation - The period of probation is one year, which cannot be extended by more than one year.
Even if no order of extension of probation is passed at the expiry of one year, the person continues to be on probation.
Therefore, they cannot be deemed to have been automatically confirmed.

The Haryana State Supply and Marketing Co-operative Service (Common Cadre) Rules of 1988, Rule 10 - Probation - Confirmation - The period of probation is one year, which can be extended by one year.
However, extension of probation period not passed by the Competent Authority is of no consequence.
Even if no order of extension was passed, the petitioner would have continued to remain on probation for a period of at least two years.
Therefore, they could not be deemed confirmed before the expiry of the period of two years.
[Para 5]
The Haryana State Supply and Marketing Co-operative Service (Common Cadre) Rules of 1988, Rule 10 - Adverse reports - Reversion - An employee can remain on probation for a maximum period of two years.
They have the right to file a representation within three months from the date of communication of adverse remarks before reversion.
However, reversion before submission of representation is considered legal.
An appointment on probation is in the nature of 'trial', and the opinion is formed if the employee has not come up to the requisite standard.
Therefore, reversion order passed is legal.
