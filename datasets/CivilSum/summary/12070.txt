Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation of service - The court is unable to order the regularisation of service since it pertains to an executive function that can only be directed by the relevant authorities according to applicable rules.
[Para 19]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pay scale - Payment for higher emoluments - With regard to the petitioner's assignment in Algeria, the authority concerned must decide what pay scale to award him; furthermore, the court cannot interfere in this matter because the petitioner accepted the assignment of his own volition.
