Civil Procedure Code, Order 1 Rule 10 - Necessary parties - Pleadings and claim - Court must determine if an ex-employer who transferred an employee to another department is a necessary party for the purpose of adding parties to the suit - Only those persons who are necessary or proper parties for adjudication can be impleaded - If there is no pleading or claim against a person, then it is not necessary or proper for them to be a party.
