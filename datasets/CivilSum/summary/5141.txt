Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2) - Award - Claim of pay - Despite being prevented from joining their place of posting during the disputed period, a Labour Court made a clear finding that the respondent was employed as a clerk by the petitioner and should not be blamed for not joining - the respondent was subsequently instructed by the authorities to return to work - The Labour Court allowed the claim, ruling that the respondent is entitled to receive their salary for the disputed period.
