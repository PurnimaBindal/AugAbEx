Canara Bank has a Special Voluntary Retirement Scheme, 2000, and employees can apply.
Acceptance and communication of the application are required, and if an employee dies before the date of relief, benefits cannot be denied.
The date of relief is at the discretion of the bank and can be preponed or postponed.
The only thing the bank cannot do is withdraw acceptance.
Therefore, once acceptance is communicated, benefits cannot be denied due to death.
