Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - RPF Rules, 1987, Rule 90 - Standing Order, Clause 3 - Premature - Transfer - Tenure - Competent authority - Petitioner serving on Intra-Divisional Postings - Each posting constitutes a fresh transfer - Chief Security Commissioner passed a premature transfer order - Transfer order should have been passed by Additional/Deputy Chief Security Commissioner, who is the competent authority - Therefore, the transfer order is not legally valid - Consequently, the orders of transfer are set aside.
[Paras 10 and 11]
