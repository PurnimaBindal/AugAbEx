Indian Railway Establishment Code, Rule 1316 - Step up pay - In order to be entitled to step up pay under Rule 1316, it is necessary to satisfy certain conditions, including being in a similar situation to those claiming equivalence - However, in the case of Loco Supervisors, they are not in such a situation as compared to the Drivers in different grades - The Supreme Court has already considered the issue in OP Saxena's case and no further reconsideration is required.
