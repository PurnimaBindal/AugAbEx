Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 341 - Scheduled Castes - A scheduled caste candidate of one state can claim reservation benefits in another state, if employed by the government of that state - Instructions of the Government of Haryana state that a candidate from another state, when employed in Haryana, is considered a bona fide resident of Haryana and eligible for reservation benefits - Employment in Haryana does not cease one's residency in their home state - General Administration or Chief Secretary's clarifications do not override Government instructions.
