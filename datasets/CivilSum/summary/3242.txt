Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 - Haryana Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Section 7 - Res judicata - The principle of constructive res judicata, in accordance with Section 11 of CPC, applies to a subsequent writ petition challenging termination of services of a lecturer of an affiliated college on jurisdictional grounds, as the previous order of termination was upheld by the High Court in LPA.
However, the claim for suspension allowance, which was not involved in the prior case, is not barred.
[Paras 10 and 11] B Haryana Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Section 7A - Suspension allowance - According to Section 7-A, if suspension continues beyond six months without a report being submitted to the Director, the employee is entitled to full wages for the period beyond six months.
