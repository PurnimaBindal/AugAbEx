The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo), (j) and 25-F - Constitution of India, Article 311 - AP Shops and Establishments Act, 1966, Section 40(1) - Termination/Retrenchment/Retrenchment Compensation/Notice pay - According to Section 40 of AP Shops and Establishments Act, 1966, it is not necessary for one month's pay to be paid simultaneously as it is in Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, even though the terms of both provisions differ.
