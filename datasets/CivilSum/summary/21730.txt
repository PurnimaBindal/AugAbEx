Constitution of India, Article 16 - Voluntary retirement - Legal connection between employer and employee - The contractual relationship between an employer and employee does not terminate until the employee is relieved of their duties after accepting voluntary retirement or resignation - In this case, since the acceptance of voluntary retirement was conditional, the conditions had to be met before the order became effective - However, before the conditions could be met, the appellant withdrew the scheme - Therefore, the respondent did not acquire any vested right and the High Court was incorrect in claiming otherwise.
