The All India Institute of Medical Sciences Act, 1956, Section 11 states that terminated project employees should be taken in at Safdarjung Hospital as fresh employees, with pay protection and their earlier service counted for the purpose of leave, pension, and retiral benefits.
However, it was established that these directions were made on humanitarian grounds and not based on legal principles.
Moreover, these orders fall under Article 142 of the Constitution and are not binding on High Courts.
[Para 6]

In the case of violated Government directions for the termination of project employees, it was found that no writ of mandamus is ordinarily granted to enforce a non-statutory Government execution order.
