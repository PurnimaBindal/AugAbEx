The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 govern the removal of employees.
In this case, the petitioner was accused of misappropriating funds through manipulating records.
While departmental proceedings concluded with a punishment of removal before the petitioner was acquitted in a criminal trial on the basis of benefit of doubt, the disciplinary authority did not rely on the evidence produced in that criminal trial and did not examine the same witnesses.
Additionally, the order of the disciplinary authority was based on a concession made by the petitioner during a personal hearing.
As the standard of proof in both proceedings is different and termination of an employee is not based on a conviction in a criminal case, an acquittal of an employee in a criminal case cannot be the basis for taking away the effect of the departmental proceedings.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to reinstatement.
[Paras 7 to 11]
