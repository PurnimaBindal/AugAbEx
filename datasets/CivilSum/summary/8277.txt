Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Power of Labour Court - Scope - A former workman's claim of 188 days of leave encashment is considered a pre-existing right, and he is entitled to it as part of his service conditions - Contention is not regarding eligibility for leave encashment, but rather the number of days to which petitioner is entitled to - Labour Courts may address such issues in proceedings under Section 33-C(2) - The application made by the petitioner under Section 33-C(2) is admissible - The matter is referred back to the Tribunal to consider the petitioner's application.
