The petitioner in a case seeking compassionate appointment after the death of her mother was unable to show that the family was still in financial crisis and in need of the appointment.
Although the petitioner applied for the appointment once she was of age, there was no evidence that the family was still struggling without the bread earner.
The burden of proof is on the claimant seeking compassionate appointment, and in this case, the tribunal was justified in rejecting the application due to the lack of grounds for the appointment.
