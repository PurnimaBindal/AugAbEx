Part time teachers - Workload - University Grants Commission and Government Resolution recommended the workload for part time teachers to be from 8 to 11 periods - State Government directed to allot only 8 periods - Colleges given discretion to allocate within the specified range, not subject to oral or written directions - Colleges may allot minimum and maximum periods as per their requirement, without any additional conditions.
