Article 226 of the Constitution of India deals with promotion, specifically in relation to the position of Medical Social Worker Grade I The selection criteria for promotion is based on merit-cum-seniority; however, the petitioner was not considered for promotion due to alleged adverse entries in their Annual Confidential Report (ACR).
The case involves the encroachment of the judiciary into the executive domain, as it was the duty of the Departmental Promotion Committee (D.P.C.)
to evaluate the petitioner's suitability for promotion.
The court, acting under Article 226, cannot review the decision of the D.P.C.
[Para 19]

The promotion criteria for merit-cum-seniority take into account adverse entries, even if they haven't been communicated to the concerned party.
This decision is based on the case of (1992)2 SCC 299.
[Paras 17 to 18]
