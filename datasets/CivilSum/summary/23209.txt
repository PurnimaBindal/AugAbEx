The appointing authority and punishing authority for the Technical Assistant Grade-I post in the Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 56 is the Senior Regional Manager or Regional Manager or Zonal Manager, and the decision of imposing a major penalty of compulsory retirement can be made by anyone among them.
[Paras 16-19] B.
The imposition of a major penalty of compulsory retirement without providing a copy of the enquiry report to the concerned official is against natural justice and cannot be upheld under Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 56 and Constitution of India, Article 14.
The case must be sent back to the punishing authority for issuing a fresh order after sharing the report.
