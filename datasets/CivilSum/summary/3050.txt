Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 2(c) and 2-A - Continuous service - Holidays, weekly rests and cessation of work not due to any fault of employee - Cannot be excluded - "Continuous service" includes service interrupted by cessation of work not due to any fault of the concerned employee - Weekly and national holidays are not at fault of the employee - Holidays are to be considered when calculating an employee's service for gratuity.
