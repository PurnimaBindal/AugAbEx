Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Extension - Appointment for specified period, subject to future extension - No enforceable right accrued to petitioner - Term extended six times for three months each time - Authority refused further extension due to dissatisfaction with petitioner's work and conduct - It was ruled that if an employer has lost faith in an employee, they cannot be compelled to extend their tenure, which is not an enforceable right.
