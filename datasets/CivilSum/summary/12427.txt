The Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 226/227 allow for retrospective promotion.
However, a petitioner's explanation for a delay of over six years in challenging orders of promotion of 16 persons to the post of Chief Pharmacist is unsatisfactory.
Even a writ petition, filed against an order dated 311994, is now deemed belated.
As held by the Hon'ble Supreme Court, a petition challenging promotion/seniority must be filed within a period of six months of accrual of cause of action or, at the most, within one year.
Therefore, the failure of the petitioner to challenge the promotion within this period is sufficient to decline relief.
Consequently, the petition is dismissed.
[Paras 6, 10, and 11]

The Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 226/227 allow for reservation benefits during promotions.
As a member of a backward class, the petitioner is eligible for promotion to the post of Chief Pharmacist against roster points reserved for members of the backward class.
The record of the case also shows that the petitioner is entitled to the benefit of reservation made for backward classes.
As a result, the Respondents are directed to duly consider the case of petitioner for promotion to the post of Chief Pharmacist against existing vacancies and future vacancies, provided the petitioner is otherwise found eligible.
Therefore, the petition is partly allowed.
