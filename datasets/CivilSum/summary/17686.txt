Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1958 - Recovery of over-time allowance - Principle of natural justice - When the Petitioners were paid over-time allowance, no recovery can be made without allowing them to submit their case and to show cause - The benefit of the allowance cannot be withdrawn without following principles of natural justice - Therefore, the action of respondents, that is seeking to recover over-time allowance already paid to the Petitioners, cannot be sustained - The order is quashed.
