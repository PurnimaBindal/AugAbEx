Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311(2) - Certified Standing Orders, Clause 9(iii) - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo)(a) - Automatic termination - Abandonment - Absence without leave - An employee's service cannot automatically terminate on the expiry of their leave without adequate notice and opportunity being given to them.
Any adverse action taken against the employee must uphold their rights.
[Para 4] B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Delay - Back wags - A petitioner failed to make a timely demand for notice and suffered significant delay in challenging the Labour Court's award after the termination of their services.
The Court granted only 30% back wages.
[Para 5] C. Constitution of India, Article 226 - Finding of fact - The High Court cannot interfere with the finding of fact made by the Labour Court, which found that the workman did not report for duty on expiry of leave.
