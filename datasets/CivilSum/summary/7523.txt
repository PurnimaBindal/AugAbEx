Section 33-C(2) of Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 refers to execution proceedings of the Act.
The Executing Court is not allowed to make a finding of fact in execution proceedings, especially in disputes that are fundamental to the case.
If the Labour Court finds that juniors to the workman are still serving but decides to grant payment of wages as prayed by workman, the order is without jurisdiction and hence, not sustainable.
