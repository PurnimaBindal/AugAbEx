Constitution of India, Articles 16, 162, 226 - UP Agricultural Produce Market Committee (Centralised) Services Regulations, 1984 - Compassionate appointment - Claim based on the acquisition of land - Reliance on Government order - No provision in regulations for such appointment - Ruling states that the Government order cannot override statutory appointment rules, especially if no promises were made at the time of acquisition.
