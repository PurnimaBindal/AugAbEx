Article 16 of the Indian Constitution states that pay scales can be revised with retrospective effect.
The appellant was denied a revision in pay scale, while juniors were granted higher pay.
The appellant had the right to be placed in that scale before any junior to him could be granted benefit of a higher pay.
A senior person who has proved his merit vis-a-vis others in the same cadre should not be placed in the lower scale of pay while juniors who were either not qualified or had been found unsuitable for promotion are placed in higher scale of pay.
Such course of action would be arbitrary, unfair, and would amount to denial of equality of opportunity in promotion.
[Para 4]

Article 16 of the Indian Constitution also states that revision of pay scale is allowed.
The appellant was entitled to be placed in a higher scale but was illegally denied.
The normal rule of payment of interest on wages illegally denied to him deserves to be upheld, and the appellant shall be paid interest at the rate of 12% per annum from the date the wages accrued till the date of actual payment.
[Para 5]
