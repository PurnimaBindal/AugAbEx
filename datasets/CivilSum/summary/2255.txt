Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2-A and 33(2)(b) - Domestic enquiry - National Tribunal approved Management's application under Section 33(2)(b) but no finding given on validity of domestic enquiry - Industrial Tribunal order does not prevent workman from invoking provisions under Section 2-A(2) or 10 of Act - Validity of removal order can be decided in reference under Section 2-A or Section 10 of the Act at the instance of workman - Order passed under Section 33(2)(b) cannot be used as res-judicata to decide petition under Section 2-A(2) or Section 10 of the Act including the issue as to validity of the domestic enquiry.
