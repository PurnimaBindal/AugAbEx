The petitioner, who worked as a daily rated worker from July 1988 until August 1994 when he was detained by security forces and eventually released in 1995, is seeking regularisation of service and back pay for the period of detention.
The court ruled that the period of detention should be considered as part of the seven years required for regularisation under SRO 64 of 1994, but the petitioner cannot be given back pay for that period since he did not perform any work.
