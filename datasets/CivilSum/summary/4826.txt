Constitution of India, Article 226 - Change in status/nomenclature - Appeal maintainability regarding Disinvestment and Voluntary Retirement Scheme - Benefits computation - Maintainability of Appeal - Point in time when writ petition can be filed - Petition was maintainable when it was filed - Cause of action for filing writ petition crystallised at a point of time when authority was subjected to writ jurisdiction - Status of respondent Company was a Public Sector Enterprise on the date of filing of appeal - Subsequent decision of respondent on account of privatization of company by transferring its shares in favor of private individuals - Respondent by its subsequent action cannot render a pending appeal infructuous.
