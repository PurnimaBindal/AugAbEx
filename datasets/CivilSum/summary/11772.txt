The service conditions of employees of respondent-Bank are regulated by Rules framed under SRO 233, as governed by the Jammu and Kashmir Co-operative Societies Act, Section 124(2) and Jammu and Kashmir Co-operative Societies Rules, Rules 2 and 24.
The forum for appeal or review is provided through these rules, and civil court holds no jurisdiction in this matter.
It is found that the learned single judge had taken a sound view, and therefore, the appeal is dismissed.
