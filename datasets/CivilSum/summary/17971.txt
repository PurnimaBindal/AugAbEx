Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion/Adverse remarks/Efficiency Bar/Reversion - The petitioner was granted Promotion as Private Secretary during 1980 and was permitted to cross the efficiency bar in the same year.
However, he was later reverted to the post of Personal Assistant in 1983 based on annual confidential remarks from 1975-76 and adverse remarks from prior to crossing the efficiency bar.
It is deemed illegal to revert based on adverse remarks from before crossing the efficiency bar.
[Paras 2 and 4]
