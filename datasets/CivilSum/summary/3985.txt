Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 11-A - Coverage - Respondent-conductor charged with misconduct of transporting passengers without tickets, and received ticket fare - The respondent-conductor's explanation for not being able to provide tickets due to bus overcrowding was not accepted - Sufficient evidence before the Labor Court indicates that the respondent-conductor's past record of service was unsatisfactory, with frequent punishment for similar acts of misconduct - Therefore, the Labor Court's decision to set aside the dismissal order and reinstate the respondent-conductor with full back wages is not sustainable.
