Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 226/227 - Refixation of seniority - The petitioners in a writ petition had prayed for their seniority to be refixed based on Supreme Court decisions in Union of India v. Virpal Singh and Ajit Singh Janjua v. State of Punjab - Respondents confirmed that they were taking steps to implement these judgments and had issued instructions accordingly - Respondents were directed to refix seniority between petitioners and private respondents in accordance with the judgments within two months - As a result, the petition became void.
