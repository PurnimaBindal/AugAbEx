Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Regularisation - The petitioner, who works as a Chief Engineer, has been seeking regular appointment since 1982, as an Executive Engineer and since 1987 as a Chief Engineer.
The case of the petitioner has been reviewed at all stages and was placed on the seniority list according to the length of service and was promoted according to legal requirements.
The petitioner is not eligible for any relief.
