Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Industrial Security Force Act, 1968, Section 18 - Absence from duty - Misconduct - While absence from duty may not always be considered misconduct under inservice jurisprudence, the court must assess whether any act of lapse was wilful or intentional misconduct driven by ill motive.
Acts of negligence, errors of judgment, or innocent mistakes alone do not constitute misconduct.
Absence from duty being misconduct depends on the specific facts and circumstances of each case, so the authority must consider the background.
[Paras 4 & 5]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 311 - Punishment - Absence from duty - Removal from service due to absence from duty - The punishment should be proportionate to the gravity of the charge and the proved misconduct.
Any penalty that is disproportionate to the misconduct and beyond the required protective measures becomes violative of Article 14 of the Constitution.
The court may quash a punishment that is disproportionate to the misconduct and beyond the necessary protective measures.
