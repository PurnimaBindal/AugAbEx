In 1984, a dispute arose regarding bonus payment under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 18 and 19, and the Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, Sections 2(13) and 12.
Management and the Union reached a settlement under section 18(1) of the ID Act, which was acted upon.
However, an amendment was made to the Bonus Act, which raised the ceiling limit of wages, and was deemed to have been in force on the date of settlement.
Despite the settlement being acted upon, the 2nd respondent-employees' Union is entitled to the benefits of the amendment due to the deeming provision in the amendment.
