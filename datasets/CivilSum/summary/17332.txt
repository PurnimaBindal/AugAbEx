Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Increments - Resignation - Withdrawal of resignation - Re-employment with continuity of service - The employee will not receive back wages and will retain only the service benefits accrued prior to resignation - The employer will not treat the employee as a fresh entrant - The employee is not entitled to increments for the period of non-service - Increment is based on the satisfaction of the employer with the employee's service - If an employee was not in service, the employer cannot assess their satisfaction - Therefore, no increment will be granted for that period.
