Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 - Pre-mature/compulsory retirement - Haryana govt. instructions dated 1381983 - The instructions requiring 70% good reports during the last 10 years of service are simply guidelines for the competent authority to form an opinion, and are considered valid.
The decision in KK Vaid's case to declare the same instructions ultra vires is not a good law as held in Daya Nand's case by the Full Bench 1995(1) SCT 423 (P&H).
[Para 8]

Overstayal in service - Recovery of salary paid during overstayal - Pension - Qualifying service of pre-mature retiree - If the order of premature retirement was stayed by the court and the employee continued and overstayed after passing of retirement orders, the salary paid to him for that period cannot be recovered.
However, that period will not count towards the qualifying service for the purposes of retiral/pensionary benefits.
