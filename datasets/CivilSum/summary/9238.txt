According to Punjab State Electricity Board Employees Punishment and Appeal Regulations, 1971, Regulation 14, if an employee is convicted on corruption charges under the Prevention of Corruption Act and the Indian Penal Code, no hearing is required before passing dismissal order.
B.
Though there is no decision on merits as to whether the dismissal order is good or bad, principle of res judicata would come in if writ petition is dismissed on the statement that the conviction had been upheld up to the Supreme Court.
C. Under Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10, the State Government must check if the claim put forth by the worker is belated or delayed.
No reference should be made for the belated claims without explaining the delay.
D According to Constitution of India, Article 14, the dismissal order cannot be passed with retrospective effect.
It takes effect only on the date it is passed.
E. If operation of the award is stayed and ultimately the writ petition is allowed against the worker in Industrial Disputes Act, Section 17-B, for the interregnum period the worker is not entitled to wages.
