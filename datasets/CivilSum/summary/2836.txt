Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 11-B - Back Wages - Typically, when a workman's termination is found to be illegal, they should be reinstated and receive full back wages, with any deviation from this rule being an exception.
However, if the employer can provide evidence of the workman being gainfully employed during their idleness period, this exception may apply.
If the employer does not provide such evidence in front of the Labour Court, they cannot raise the question in a writ petition.
