The Indian Pharmacy Act, 1948, Section 10, and Regulation 16 of the Pharmacy Regulations, 1991 establish that candidates who have passed the Diploma in Pharmacy Part-I Examination are eligible for promotion to the Diploma in Pharmacy Part-II Class.
If a student fails in more than two subjects, they are not entitled to be promoted to the Diploma in Pharmacy Part-II Class.
In this case, the petitioners failed in more than two subjects of the Ist year course, and therefore, they are not entitled to be promoted to the IInd year course.
They are rightly denied promotion to the IInd year course.
[Paras 8 and 9]

If a student fails in more than two subjects, they are not entitled to be promoted to the Diploma in Pharmacy Part-II Class, according to Section 10 of the Indian Pharmacy Act, 1948, and Regulation 16 of the Pharmacy Regulations, 1991.
While the college authorities may admit students to the IInd year class and collect fees, it does not prevent them from debarring students who failed in more than two subjects from attending classes for the IInd year.
Thus, the petitioners cannot exploit the action of the college authorities in admitting them into the IInd year class by accepting fees for the IInd year, and there is no ground for quashing the impugned order directing them not to attend classes for Diploma in Pharmacy IInd year.
In the supplementary examination held in January/Feb 1996, the petitioners cleared the papers in which they had previously failed, and they are eligible to be admitted to the IInd-year course in the next session.
