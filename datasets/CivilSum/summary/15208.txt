The petitioner in this case relied on a notification issued by the DOPT, according to which order of suspension shall not be valid after 90 days unless it is extended after review.
The contention that the Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965 are not applicable since the authority has its own rules which do not specify time to review suspension was dismissed because of the provision in the ITPO Rules that rules, instructions, orders and clarification on any corresponding rule issued by CVC or Govt will apply to employees of authority.
The respondents themselves also accepted the application of rules by constituting review committee.
As a result, the suspension was revoked.
