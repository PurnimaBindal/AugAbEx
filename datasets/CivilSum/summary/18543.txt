Constitution of India, Article 309 - Annual Confidence Report - Adverse remarks - Expungtion of - Retrospective Promotion - A petitioner who received adverse remarks in ACRs from 1970-71, 1971-72 and 1972-73 cannot be granted retrospective promotion - Representations for expunging adverse remarks made years later, when the Administrative Member was favorable, should not be allowed as it may encourage post facto attempts to clean up confidential report files.
