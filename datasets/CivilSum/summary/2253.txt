Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2, Item 3 - Dismissal - A Bank employee (Daftri) was dismissed for misappropriating funds received from Bank customers by accepting the amount and giving passbooks with falsified entries.
Additionally, the employee was discovered to have given drafts that had been stolen from the bank to customers.
The findings of the Inquiry Officer were based on relevant material on the record and the Disciplinary Authority gave the delinquent an opportunity to reply to these findings.
Following the receipt of a reply, the order of dismissal was passed.
The punishment of dismissal was considered not disproportionate.
[Paras 10-13]
