Constitution of India, Article 16 - Uttar Pradesh Geology and Mining Service Rules, 1983, Rules 5(3), 16 and 23 - Promotion - Appeal for preparation of combined seniority list of officers on the posts of Geologist and Senior Geo-Chemist and then to promotion to the post of Deputy Director - High Court dismissed the appeal on the question of eligibility - Inter-se seniority of Deputy Directors not the subject matter of appeal - Inter-se seniority to be determined in accordance with Rule 23 - Judgment of High Court upheld as legal.
