UP Recruitment of Dependents of Government Servants Dying in Harness Rules, 1974, Rule 5 - Compassionate Appointment - The application for appointment on compassionate grounds made several years after the death of father will be ineligible to be accepted within Rule 5's purview.
The proviso doesn't grant the State Government an unrestricted right to forgo the requirements indicated in Rule 5(1)(iii).
