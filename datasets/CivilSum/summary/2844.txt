Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226 - Retrospective promotion - Arrears of pay - Tribunal ordered retroactive promotion from when juniors were promoted - Argued that respondent is not entitled to arrears of pay since he did not work on the post - Determined that since respondent was denied promotion due to petitioner's mistake and was willing to work on the post but not given the opportunity, the principle of no work no pay cannot be applied to deny him arrears of pay.
