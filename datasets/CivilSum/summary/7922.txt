The Petitioner seeks the quashing of the convening order of a general court martial, arguing that it violates Rule 43 of Air Force Rules and the principles of natural justice as different charge-sheets are set to be issued for similar accusations.
However, the competent officer has ordered the convening of the court martial in accordance with Rule 43 and the charges are not the same.
Since the finding and sentence of the court martial is yet to be confirmed and the Petitioner has yet to take recourse to Section 161(2) of the Act, there is no reason to consider the issues at this stage.
The petition is disposed of accordingly.
