Tamil Nadu Recognised Private Schools (Regulation) Rules (1979), Rule 15(9)(ii) - Reservation - Appointment as Teacher in private school - If a vacancy reserved for SC/ST candidates is not filled due to non-availability of candidate belonging to that category, the said vacancy can be carried forward to the next recruitment - It is illegal to appoint a candidate from the open category in such a vacancy - The decision to not approve such appointment is justifiable, but the State Government cannot withhold the salary of the appointee on the grounds of illegal appointment.
