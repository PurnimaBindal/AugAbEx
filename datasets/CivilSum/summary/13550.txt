Constitution of India, Article 311(2) (a) - Probation of Offenders Act, 1958, Sections 3 and 4 - Conviction on criminal charge - Probation - Being released on probation does not mean being acquitted from the charge - The individual's conviction is still valid, and they will carry the stigma of a conviction - The delinquent can still be removed, dismissed, or discharged from service based on the conduct that caused their conviction.
