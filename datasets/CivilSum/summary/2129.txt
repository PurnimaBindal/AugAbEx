Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical Council Act, Section 33 - Recognition of a degree - Registration denied - Petitioners completed MD in General Medicine from Medical Institute Tashkant, Uzbekistan in a reduced period of 5-1/2 years - Respondents imposed a restriction on registration for those who completed their medical degree in less than 6 years from erstwhile USSR - Respondents made the regulation without prior sanction of Central Government - The restriction imposed cannot be sustained - Respondent/Council ordered to register petitioners.
