Constitution of India, Articles 16, 311(2) - Compulsory retirement - The order of compulsory retirement must be made in the bona fide exercise of power in the public interest - The confidential record of the respondent reveals poor relations with the public and allegations of corruption against him - In the public interest, it was deemed appropriate to compulsorily retire him - The order of compulsory retirement was not meant to be a form of punishment - The impugned order of the High Court was overturned, and the respondent's suit was dismissed.
