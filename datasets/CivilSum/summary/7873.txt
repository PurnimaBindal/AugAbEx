Constitution of India, Articles 16, 14 - Appointment - Vacancies - Notification of vacancies - Vacancies must be advertised - Appointments cannot exceed the number of notified vacancies - Appointments made without careful evaluation of vacancies or based on arbitrary decisions cannot be upheld - However, appointments made in excess of advertised vacancies are not affected if appointees were not parties to the petition - Future selection processes must be transparent and follow strict norms set forth in the advertisement - The advertisement must evaluate vacancies carefully and adhere to the schedule set by public authorities - Extensions, if necessary, must be properly advertised and communicated.
[Paras 23 and 24]
