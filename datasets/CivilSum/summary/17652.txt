Article 309 of the Constitution of India and Rule 17 of the Punjab Public Service Commission's Rules of Procedure state that while rules and procedures are important for proper functioning, a minor deviation or departure from such rules cannot vitiate the selection/recommendations.
[Para 6(ii)]

Rule 17 of the Punjab Public Service Commission's Rules of Procedure notes that while it may be necessary to include technical experts in the selection process for certain posts, such as those in the civil services, selection for administrative posts on the basis of only service record does not require expert input.
Therefore, the absence of an expert does not vitiate the selection.
[Paras 6(iv), (v), 7(ii)]
