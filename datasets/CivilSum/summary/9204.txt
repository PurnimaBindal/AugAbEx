Sections 10 and 17 of the Industrial Disputes Act state that if an ex parte award is issued against the management, the workman is entitled to the benefit of current wages.
A labour court proceeded ex parte against the management in this case because they refused to receive notice, but the management moved an application for setting the ex parte aside on the same day.
Despite this, an award was passed ex parte without considering the application.
The Labour Court was responsible for deciding the application either way, and as such, the ex parte award was quashed and the workman was granted the benefit of current wages from the date of the application.
