Orissa Police Order 226 of 1981, Exhibit A – Promotion of Constables to Lance Naik – Seniority – Tests and securing pass marks in each test and aggregate – Candidates aspiring to be enlisted for training for promotion as Lance Naiks must meet the requirement of attaining pass marks in each subject of outdoor and indoor tests, as well as in miscellaneous tests, without depending solely on seniority or making aggregate.
Merely obtaining a high score aggregate is insufficient.
The authorities cannot select candidates who fail in important tests for promotion and training.
[Paras 8 to 10]
