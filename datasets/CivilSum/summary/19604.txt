Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Higher post - Jointing time - Post of HCM (Group A) Service - The petitioner had been selected for a higher post than the one he was on deputation with UT for, and it cannot be assumed that he would be content with only extending his joining time by letter and then ignoring the matter for over three years.
He had a desire to stay in Chandigarh, but since the post has already been filled, he has no right to it at this time, as he was selected for it over five years prior.
The petition has been dismissed.
