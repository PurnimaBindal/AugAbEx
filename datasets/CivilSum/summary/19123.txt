Constitution of India, Articles 12, 30 and 226 - Bombay Primary Education Rules, 1949, Rule 109(2) - Recognition - Withdrawal of recognition of a school - Societies running recognised educational institutes by the State are fulfilling the function of a State in imparting the education and fulfil the object of providing education as guaranteed under the Constitution - The withdrawal of recognition of such institutions impacts not only the Management but also the future of students - The authorities vested with the power to withdraw recognition must act in accordance with the provisions of the Act and Rules - The Education Committee or the School Board is the only competent authority under the Rules - An order signed by an Education Officer in furtherance of a letter issued by the Director of Education lacks jurisdiction.
