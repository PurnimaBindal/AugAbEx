The Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 4(6)(a) and the Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 56 provide for the recovery of loss to the establishment from gratuity.
This recovery is held to be permissible and does not require a separate order of recovery to be passed.
Additionally, when there is a punishment order holding the delinquent liable for the loss, the attributed amount cannot be set off against the gratuity amount.
