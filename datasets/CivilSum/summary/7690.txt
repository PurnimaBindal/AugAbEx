Haryana State Central Cooperative Bank Staff Common Cadre Rules, 1975, Rule 9.4 - Termination - Sustainability - Appointments made against Rules and Instruction - It is held that services have been rightly dispensed with under the circumstances.
[Paras 10, 11]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-G - Termination - Provision - Violation alleged - Juniors retained - Juniors were reappointed after fresh selection - It is held that under the circumstances provisions of section 25-G of Act cannot be said to have been violated.
[Para 8]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-H - Termination - Provision - Violation alleged - No opportunity of participation for selection given - Advertisement made but Petitioners never applied for posts advertised - It is held that the Petitioner ought to have applied and participated in the selection process - Thus, there is not infraction of the provision of the Act.
