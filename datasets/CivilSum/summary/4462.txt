Constitution of India, Article 226 - National Council for Cooperative Training (NCCT) of National Cooperative Union of India (NCUI) Employees Service Rules, 1994, Rule 11 - Retirement Age - The rules state that any amendment to the retirement age of employees from 58 to 60 years needs the government's approval.
No such amendment has been made, and the recommendation of the 5th Pay Commission doesn't apply.
Hence, the petitioners cannot claim an extension of their retirement age.
