Punjab University Calendar, Vol. II, 1995, Regulation 14(ii) - Unfair means - Examination - Punishment of four-year exam barring - Guilt need not be proved directly - Circumstances may indicate guilt - Evidence of unfair means on answer sheets - If guilt is proven, punishing authority can impose any deterrent punishment - Four-year barring not considered disproportionate.
