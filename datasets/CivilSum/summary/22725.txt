The Meerut University statutes and the Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 136 were considered in a case regarding seniority and promotion for acting principal in charge of a college.
The appellant and respondent had equal seniority as senior lecturers and the same pay scale, but the appellant was facing a charge of double murder.
The court ultimately denied the appellant relief.

The Constitution of India's Article 16 was invoked in a case regarding seniority and entry into service.
The court determined that someone who entered into service without the necessary qualifications, but was later regularized, would only be considered regularly appointed from the date of such regularization.
This person would be treated as having entered service from that date.
[Para 11] The case in question was Shitla Prasad Shukla v. State of UP and others, 1986 (Supp.)
