Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 141 - Judgments precidents of the Courts - Reserved jobs - Courts give only prospective effect to the judgments in the public interest so that settled matters are not unsettled again and again - In RK Sabharwal and others v. State of Punjab, 1995(2) SCT 646 retrospective effect was given only because matter remained under consideration of the Court from the date of selection - However, where the selections and promotions/appointments were made prior to the judgment in Sabharwal's case, the rule of reservation on the basis of total strength in the cadre and not only in the vacancies arising in future would operate prospectively.
