Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Air Force Act, 1950, Section 73 - Judicial Review - Punishment - Punishments must be in accordance with the punishment scale provided under the Act and Rules - The Court cannot interfere with or substitute the scales determined by the Legislature - Imprisonment under the Air Force Act is a higher punishment than dismissal from service and cannot be considered lesser - The Court's power to interpret the law is limited.

Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Punishment - Court Martial - Ordinarily, the High Court does not interfere with the sentence awarded by the Court Martial and approved by the competent authority - However, if the sentence is found to have been awarded against the provisions of law in force in the country, it may be corrected by the Court - In case of disproportion between the gravity of the offence and the punishment, the Court may interfere - Dismissal from service as the sentence awarded against the charge of absence from duty was converted into a more lenient sentence - The punishment awarded by the confirming authority was excessive and disproportionate to the offence.
