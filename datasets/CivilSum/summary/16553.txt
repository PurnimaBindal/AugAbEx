Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Madhya Pradesh Municipalities Act, 1961, Section 355 - Madhya Pradesh Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1966, Rule 2 - Suspension - District Collector suspending Chief Municipal Officer - As the Chief Municipal Officer held a Class-II post, the District Collector had no authority to suspend them, nor were they the appointing authority - The order of suspension was deemed invalid and overruled.
