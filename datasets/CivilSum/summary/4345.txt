Section 14-B of the Employees Provident Fund and Misc.
Provisions Act, 1952 - The Commissioner's discretion under Section 14-B cannot be controlled by administrative direction, but only by the Act and the scheme framed thereunder.
The guidelines for levy of damages are provided in a table, but actual decision-making should consider the facts of each case, and the Commissioner should exercise discretion within the framework of the Act and scheme.

Section 14-B of the Employees Provident Fund and Misc.
Provisions Act, 1952 - The word 'damages' in the context of the Act involves guidelines for their levy.

Section 14-B of the Employees Provident Fund and Misc.
Provisions Act, 1952 - When assessing damages, the Commissioner's order must show that it followed the correct procedure, applied rules of natural justice, and considered every relevant factor.
The order must also show a judicial and objective reasoning process when arriving at its findings.
Authority under Section 14-B must also demonstrate why it arrived at the decision it made, and provide reasons.

Section 14-B of the Employees Provident Fund and Misc.
Provisions Act, 1952 - Timely deposit of provident fund contributions is a statutory obligation, which cannot be diluted by any extraneous factors that may be present.

Provisions Act, 1952 - There is no requirement for immediate action to be taken upon default, nor is there a prescribed limitation period for recovery of damages.
