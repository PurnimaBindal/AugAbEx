According to Section 7(3A) of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, it is mandatory to pay interest on delayed gratuity payments exceeding 30 days.
The payment of interest is a statutory requirement and cannot be left to the discretion of the Court or authority, except in cases where the employee is responsible for the delay.
This act is intended to benefit employees and must not be interpreted in a way that disadvantages them.
