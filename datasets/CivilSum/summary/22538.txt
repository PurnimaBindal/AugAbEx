Article 311 of the Indian Constitution discusses the termination of probationary employees.
If someone is on probation, their performance is watched closely.
If it is deemed unsatisfactory, even after being given an opportunity to improve, the management has the right to discharge them simpliciter.
This is legal as it falls within the framework of the law and rules.
Additionally, it is important to note that probation is meant to assess the person's job suitability, thus, an order of discharge is not punitive and does not require a hearing before termination.
