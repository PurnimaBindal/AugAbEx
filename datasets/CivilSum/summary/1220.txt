Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, Sections 2(s) and 11A - Dismissal - Misconduct - Departmental enquiry - Charge of collecting illegal gratification from consumer - Order of dismissal passed - The Labour Court found the respondent guilty of the charge but ordered reinstatement with continuity of service but without back wages - The impugned award lacked reasoning to support why the order of dismissal was disproportionate to the proved misconduct - The court set aside the award.
