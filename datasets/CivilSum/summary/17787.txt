The Municipal Corporation employee was entitled to receive revised pay scale as per the Constitution of India Articles 14 and 226.
The State Government withheld the sanction without any explanation, which caused the petitioner's plight.
The Government was directed to approve the sanction within two months, failing which the Corporation was directed to release the revised grade and pay arrears of salary to the petitioner with 15% interest.
