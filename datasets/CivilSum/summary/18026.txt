The petitioner has been repeatedly superseded in promotions despite being considered based on seniority and term by competent authority, as per the Constitution of India Articles 14, 16 and 226.
The petitioner's representations were rejected, and even after crossing the efficiency bar and expunging adverse remarks, they were not found suitable for promotion.
The petitioner was promoted only when deemed suitable, and retrospective promotion cannot be granted.
The petition has been dismissed.
