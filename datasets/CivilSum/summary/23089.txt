The High Court ruled that the summary procedure for termination under Rule 40 of the Army Rules cannot be used to terminate the service of an army personnel when trial by Court-Martial has become "impracticable" under Section 122 of the Army Act, 1950.
The order was quashed and the matter was remitted back for fresh consideration in compliance with the law.
This ruling was based on the 2001(2) SCT 1018 (SC) case.
