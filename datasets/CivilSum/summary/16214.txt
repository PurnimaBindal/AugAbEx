Admission - Admission in General Nursing and Mid Wifery Course in Rajindra Hospital, Patiala - The top scorer of the pre-entrance test who made it to the merit list should not be required to go through any additional interview by a Selection Committee.
Moreover, the student should not have to constantly inquire about whether such a committee has even been established.
Disregarding the claim of a student who scored first in the test is considered a completely arbitrary action by the concerned authorities.
As a result, the petitioner was granted relief along with a compensation of Rs. 5000/-.
