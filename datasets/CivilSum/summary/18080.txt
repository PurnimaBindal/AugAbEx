UP Secondary Education Service Commission and Selection Board Act 1982, Section 21 - If a teacher or head mistress is being dismissed, the Commissioner must approve of such a proposal under Section 21.
Head mistresses are considered to be teachers.
[Para 6]

UP Secondary Education Service Commission and Selection Board Act, 1982 Section 21 - The Commission does not have jurisdiction to determine the validity of the Committee of Management that passed a proposed resolution.
The Commission must consider whether a proposal is just and proper, whether the prescribed procedure has been followed, whether the teacher was given an adequate opportunity to be heard, and whether the charges have been established on record.
The Commission is not required to consider all circumstances or to decide the validity of the Committee of Management.
[Para 7]

UP Secondary Education Service Commission and Selection Board Act, 1982, UP Intermediate Education Act, 1921, Section 16 - G(8) - The suspension/ delay of a proceeding is not caused by officials who are under suspension, but rather by the attitude of the Commission.
Revocation of a suspension order is justified.
[Para 9]
