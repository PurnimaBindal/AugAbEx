According to Section 22 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Section 115 of the Evidence Act, 1872, double salary paid to panchayat employees can be recovered as additional remuneration.
A subsequent resolution sought to withdraw the double salary, and it was found that the relaxation was misunderstood to mean that employees were entitled to double payment even without a call for strike.
Therefore, the withdrawal of the benefit was not illegal or unfair, as per the circular and resolution considered.
