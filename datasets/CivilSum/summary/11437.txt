Border Security Force Act, Section 62 - Border Security Rules, Rule 22 - Absence from duty - Deserter - Disciplinary action - Section 62 has no bearing on disciplinary action against a person for remaining absent from duty - Proceedings can still be taken under B.S.F.
Rules for unauthorised absence from duty - Termination by way of disciplinary action for such misconduct under the Rules is not invalid.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Border Security Force Act, Sections 11 and 17 - Border Security Rules, Rule 22 - Natural justice - Absence from duty - Disciplinary action - A termination effected under the Rules for misconduct according to prescribed procedure is not invalid for lack of compliance with principles of natural justice - Non-observance of general principles of natural justice does not invalidate a fair enquiry unless the rule is held arbitrary or unreasonable.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Border Security Force Act, Sections 11 and 17 - Border Security Rules, Rule 22 - Absence from duty - Deserter - Remaining absent from duty is a serious misconduct - Termination by way of disciplinary action for such misconduct under the Rules is not excessively harsh.
