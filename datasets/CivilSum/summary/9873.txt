Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Reversion - Petitioner initially held the position of T/Mate but was promoted to Works Inspector - Later, the petitioner was reverted back to their original position of T/Mate - Despite this, the Labour Court granted wages as if the petitioner had still been working as Works Inspector - However, there is evidence on record that the petitioner only worked as T/Mate after May 76 - Therefore, the Labour Court's decision was unfounded and should be overturned.
