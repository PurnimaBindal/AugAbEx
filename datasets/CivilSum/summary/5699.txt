Constitution of India, Article 226 - Jammu and Kashmir Subordinate Services Recruitment Rules, 1990, Rule 8 - Selection - Waiting list - In order to fill left out vacancies, the Selection Board must prepare a waiting list and offer the vacancies to candidates in merit order - Failure to do so may lead the court to favor those who approached the court in time.
