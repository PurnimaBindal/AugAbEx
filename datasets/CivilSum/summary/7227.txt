Education - BAMS course - Amendment of University ordinance - Applicability - The amendment reduces the duration of the third professional course from 24 months to 18 months, preponing the date of the exam.
To appear in the third professional course exam, a candidate must have cleared all papers of the first and second exam.
The petitioner had a backlog of one paper at the relevant time and argued that the preponement of the exam from Nov 2005 to May 2005 does not take away his vested right.
The petitioner provisionally appeared in the third exam under interim orders.
However, since the petitioner did not have the right to sit in the third exam being ineligible as per the orders of the Supreme Court in the Subhash Chander case, the examination undertaken is held to be void.
