Constitution of India, Article 16 - Qualification - Teachers obtaining higher qualifications during their service - Will not automatically receive higher pay scales - A Government circular dated March 9, 1990 is in effect and will only apply to individuals who obtained higher qualifications on or after the stated date.
Such candidates are not entitled to receive a higher scale of pay.
