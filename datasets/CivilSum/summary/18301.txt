The University stated that the petitioner was guilty of an unfair means case in their Pharmacy course, under Regulation 10(h)(iii), which deals with circumstances where a candidate assists in copying from objectionable material in his possession.
The petitioner allegedly threw a paper slip, but it was not in his handwriting, he was not caught copying from it, and the contents did not match with what was written in his answer sheet.
Furthermore, no candidate was named whom the petitioner allegedly helped by supplying incriminating material.
Therefore, the petitioner's claim that someone sitting behind him threw the paper slip in order to save himself is accurate.
As a result, the order debarring the petitioner from appearing in any University examination for two years is overturned, and the respondents are instructed to declare the petitioner's result without delay.
