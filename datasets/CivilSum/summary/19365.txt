Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 provide for equal pay for equal work.
The petitioners, Library Assistants and Junior Library Assistants of Punjab Agricultural University, Ludhiana claimed a pay scale of Rs. 700-1600 w.e.f.
14 1980 with subsequent revision of 2200-4000 w.e.f.
111986 at par with the Librarians appointed in Punjab Govt. Institutions.
However, the University has its own statutes and has a right to prescribe different service conditions for its employees and is not bound to adopt the pay scale prescribed by the Govt. for its employees.
The pay scale of 2200-4000 has been sanctioned for the post of Asstt.
Librarians and qualifications were recommended by the University Grants Commission and accepted by the University.
The post of Assistant Librarian requires graduate level qualifications in Library Science whereas the post of Junior Library Assistant only requires Matric with Certificate in Lib.
Science.
As such, there is no parity between the two posts and the charge of discrimination is misconceived.
