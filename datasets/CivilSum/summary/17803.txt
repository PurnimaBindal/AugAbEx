Rule 9.4 of the Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970 states that if the punishing authority does not agree with the findings of the Enquiry Officer and wishes to impose a different penalty, they must first provide the delinquent with an opportunity to be heard.
[Para 5]
If the punishing authority disagrees with the enquiry report which exonerated the delinquent and considers extraneous material that was not included in the report, this is a violation of natural justice.
A fresh charge must be framed and proper procedures followed before any action is taken.
[Para 5]
