According to Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311, a medical officer in a hospital was accused of demanding Rs 50/- for issuing a medical certificate, leading to termination of services.
The allegation was considered serious and contrary to professional ethics.
The case was deemed not fit for interference and the petition dismissed.
[Paras 12 and 13]   B.
As per the Constitution of India, Article 226, 311, an unconfirmed employee whose probation period has expired but has not received confirmation order can be terminated without any detailed procedure.
Such a termination is not punitive in nature.
[Para 8]  C Under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(s), 2(j), 25B, G, F, N, a workman in an industry brought a writ petition.
The petitioner himself admitted to being a workman with no dispute raised before a Labour Court or tribunal.
As disputed questions of facts were involved, there was no justification for involving writ jurisdiction and the petition was dismissed.
