According to Articles 14 and 21 of the Constitution of India, Petitioners who were safai Kamdars and resigned before reaching age of superannuation after rendering 30 years of service due to physical incapability were denied retirement benefits.
Although the Govt. resolution provides that employee wherein service condition of employee's age limit of retirement is not fixed and such employee become disabled and resigned due to age factor are eligible for the same.
It was held that officer of welfare state has acted unfairly in extending retiral benefits to petitioners.
Respondents have been directed to provide pensionary benefits to petitioners.
