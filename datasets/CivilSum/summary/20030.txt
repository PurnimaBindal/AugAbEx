Constitution of India, Article 311 - Reduction in rank - Petitioner appointed as Stenographer Grade II and subsequently promoted as Stenographer Grade I - After absence and being found guilty in an enquiry, petitioner was reverted to post of clerk/typist.
It was determined that petitioner could not be reverted to post of clerk.
Reductions should be made to the next lower rank in hierarchy and not a post outside the hierarchy.
As a result, the order of reversion to post of clerk was substituted to post of Stenographer Grade II; petitioner will be entitled to all consequential benefits flowing from the order.
