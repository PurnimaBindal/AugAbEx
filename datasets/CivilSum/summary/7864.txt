The non-deposit of compensation under the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 (as amended by Act 15 of 1933), Sections 20, 30(l) - Appeal - triggers the third proviso to section 30(1) which restricts the right of appeal on the condition of depositing the determined compensation amount.
For the hearing of an Appeal on its merits, the amount must be deposited within one month, as per the given directions.
