Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1964, Rule 21 - Disciplinary Proceedings - Natural Justice - The disciplinary authority must record reasons for disagreement with the enquiry officer's findings and inform the delinquent official to give an opportunity to respond.
[Para 5]
Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1964, Rule 21 - Disciplinary Proceedings - Natural Justice - The disciplinary authority gave their finding without providing a show cause notice against the enquiry officer's findings, which violates principles of natural justice.
As a result, the tribunal rightly set aside the order of punishment awarded to the delinquent official.
