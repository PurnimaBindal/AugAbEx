Constitution of India, Article 226 - Absorption - Post of Sub-Inspector - Head Constables of police who were promoted to post of Sub-Inspectors but later moved back to post of Head Constables - The High Court and Supreme Court have reviewed their case and directed that individuals who meet three criteria - have completed 40 years of age on 30784, maintained three years of continuous service as Sub-Inspector, and have demonstrated satisfactory work and conduct - may be absorbed as a Sub-Inspector of Police - As the petitioner has not completed 40 years of age on 30784 and all three conditions must be met to claim absorption, they are not entitled to be absorbed on the post of Sub-Inspector.
