Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311(2) - Punishment - Quantum of Punishment - Admission of guilt - Recommendation of Enquiry Officer for minor punishment - Imposition of punishment of removal from service by punishing authority - Such a punishment for the relevant misconduct is shocking and disproportionate - Employee challenging admission on grounds of advice from Enquiry Officer - Enquiry Officer also impleaded but failing to provide reply to contradict alleged facts - Removal from service cannot be justified - Punishing authority instructed to impose alternative suitable and proportionate minor punishment according to the case's facts and circumstances.
[Paras 12 and 13]
