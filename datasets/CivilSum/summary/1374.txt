According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I, Chapter III, Rule 326, Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 7, and High Court Rules and Orders, Vol.
I, Chapter 18, compulsory retirement is a form of punishment.
The respondent's objection that the wrong rules were cited in the disciplinary proceedings under Rule 7 of the Haryana Civil Services Rules is not valid, as the provisions of Punjab Civil Services Rules will apply in the absence of specific provisions made by the Chief Justice.
The petitioner was not prejudiced by the reference to the wrong provision, as the authority was competent to take action under other provisions.
