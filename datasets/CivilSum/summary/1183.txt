According to Section 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, a District Judge gave instructions for a Civil Suit, and the High Court directed the District Education Officer to pay the salaries of School Teachers until the court's decision.
The management of the school is protected from interference by Respondents.
No contempt was detected, and the petition was dismissed because the High Court's orders were not disobeyed.
