Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Compassionate Appointment - The Petitioner was allowed to work in place of his late father to claim continuous service for the purpose of regularisation.
However, his claim was rejected by the Tribunal.
It was held that claiming a job on the basis of the rule of harness or compassionate appointment was never the case of the Petitioner, and hence the Tribunal's decision was correct.
