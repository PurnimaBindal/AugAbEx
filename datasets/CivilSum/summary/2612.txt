Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 16 - Punishment - Employees cannot be punished or demoted below their original rank.
Typically, the court would remit the case and replace the punishment with a more fitting one.
However, if the employee has been enduring mental stress for an extended period of time, it would be unjust to reopen the case for past mistakes and errors.
[Paras 11 to 16]
