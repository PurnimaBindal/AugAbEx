Constitution of India, Article 311 - Domestic enquiry - Testimony given under Section 161 Criminal Procedure Code - Admissible in disciplinary proceedings - Person providing the testimony must be examined by the inquiry officer - Delinquent must be given statements made under Section 161 before witnesses are examined and offered opportunity for cross-examination - No legal issues arise from recording testimony under Section 161 Cr.PC - Considering such testimony does not invalidate disciplinary proceedings - High Court's conclusion found to be incorrect.
