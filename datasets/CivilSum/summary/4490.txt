Rule 1318 of Punjab Police Rules (as applicable in Haryana) states that ad hoc promotions made without giving right to the promotees to hold the post or claim seniority and overlooking the seniority of a large number of persons are liable to be nullified for being contrary to the equality clause of the Constitution.
Such promotions cannot be treated as under Rule 138(2) or as automatically confirmed under Rule 1318.
[Para 13]
Ad hoc promotions made under Rule 1318 of Punjab Police Rules cannot be considered as made under Rule 138.
They do not confer any right to the post and seniority.
The rules state that promotions of Constables can be made based on seniority and merit after passing the School Course.
Regular promotees have priority over ad hoc and temporary promotees according to the instructions dated 9993 of D.G.P.
Haryana.
