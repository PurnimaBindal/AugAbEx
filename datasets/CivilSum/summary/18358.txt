Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2 - Absence from duty - Dismissal from service on the ground of absence from duty and incorrigibility - The omission of the phrase "gravest act of misconduct" does not invalidate the order if the charges indicate that it is such a misconduct - Unauthorised absence of 121 days is considered a gravest misconduct for policemen.
