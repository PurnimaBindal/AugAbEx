Constitution of India, Article 226 - Advance increment - Instructions issued for 2 and 3 advance increments to Masters with IIIrd Division and Ist or IInd Division, respectively - The petitioner had the benefit of three advance increments withdrawn, as he passed his first MA examination with IIIrd Division and completed his second MA with IInd Division subsequently - However, the petitioner has the option to claim the benefit of one MA degree to his best advantage at any stage - It is unjustified to withdraw three advance increments.
