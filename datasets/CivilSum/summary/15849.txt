Constitution of India, 1950 - Articles 226 and 227 - A petitioner employed as an accounts clerk for the Border Area Project was appointed as Accountant Senior Assistant (Accounts), but was later reverted back to the position of accounts clerk by the Punjab State Social Welfare Advisory Board due to the desire of the Punjab CM to reinstate a dismissed employee.
The OSD passed the order of reinstatement despite lacking the power of the Appellate Authority for such decisions, indicating bias.
The decision to reinstate a dismissed employee required consultation with the Central Social Welfare Board, which was not done.
The petition was granted.
[Paras 7, 8 and 9]
