Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 9-A and 23-A - Conditious of service - Change in conditions of service - Requiring workers to sign an undertaking stating their willingness to work during a strike, maintain discipline, and not abandon work midway in their shift, along with a declaration of non-participation in the strike before entering factory premises, does not constitute a change in the conditions of service in the current circumstances.
