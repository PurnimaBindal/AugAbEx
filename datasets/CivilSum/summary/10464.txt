The petitioner, who was appointed as a Clerk in the former state of Jind in 1946, cannot claim the benefit of the Jind State Civil Service Regulations regarding superannuation age.
The Jind State Civil Service Regulations stipulated superannuation at the age of 62, but the petitioner cannot remain in service until that age after the merger of Jind State in Punjab and subsequent reorganisation of Punjab and Haryana States.
