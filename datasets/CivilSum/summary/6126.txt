Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - Reservation - Eligibility - Prospectus - MCA Course - A prospectus issued for a course has the force of law with provisions that bind candidates as well as the University under whose authority it was issued - Consequently, when interpreting a prospectus, a court must ensure that the eligibility criteria and conditions stipulated therein are scrupulously followed to avoid thwarting the rights of eligible candidates.
