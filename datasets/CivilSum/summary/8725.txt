Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(1) and 33-C(2) - Both sections are distinct - Section 33-C(2) has a broader scope than Section 33-C(1) - Once existence of a right is established, the Labour Court has jurisdiction to determine the amount due - A denial of a right by an employer does not take away the jurisdiction of the Labour Court to hear a petition under Section 33-C(2) - Section 33-C(2) covers cases where workers claim benefits, even if their employers dispute their right to them - The Labour Court is authorized to interpret the settlement, award, or statute that governs the claim just as an execution court is authorized to interpret the decree.
