According to the policy instruction under Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 12, disciplinary proceedings, resignation cannot be accepted during the pendency of proceedings.
Respondent acted within its jurisdiction and in compliance with the law therefore.
However, as a public sector undertaking, respondent does not qualify as 'Government' under Article 311.
Thus, petitioner will continue to be in service of respondent for the purpose of disciplinary proceedings completion.
