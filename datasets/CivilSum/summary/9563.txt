Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Suspension - The decision to suspend an employee should not be made routinely, but should depend on the severity of alleged misconduct or the nature of allegations against the employee.
In the case of the 'Yeleru Scam', in which certain individuals were accused of paying exorbitant compensation for land acquired for the Yeleru Reservoir Project, a C.I.D.
investigation revealed that the delinquent officer of the I.A.S.
Cadre had played a significant role in releasing large amounts of funds to the main accused in the case.
Thus, Suspension cannot be deemed as mala fide and without proper consideration.
Even if the officer's name was not in the FIR, it does not mean that he did not play a role in the commission of the offence.
The order of suspension is, therefore, justified, and appropriate actions should be taken against all officials found guilty after a thorough investigation.
