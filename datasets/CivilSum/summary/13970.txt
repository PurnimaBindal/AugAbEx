Army Act (46 of 1950), Section 191 - Disability pension - Reduction of disability percentage - Petitioner invalidated out of service with 20% disability and granted pension accordingly - Later re-evaluated by Medical Board which again confirmed the disability at 20% - It is impermissible for PCDA to reduce the disability percentage to 11-14% without a fresh Review Medical Board for the petitioner.
