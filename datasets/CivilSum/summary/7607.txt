According to Section 11 of the Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), if a dispute arises over dismissal, enquiry proceedings are not considered valid.
However, the management can still produce evidence to support their allegations against the worker.
The matter has been referred to the Labour Court for further proceedings and litigation expenses have been awarded, given that the workman is a senior citizen and the litigation has prolonged.
