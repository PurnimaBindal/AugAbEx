Constitution of India, Articles 226, 309 - Armed Forces Headquarters Clerical Services Rules 1968 - Seniority - Computation of - Petitioners, who were initially employed as LDCs in the Defence Ministry and later transferred to AFHQ, contested their seniority through a Tribunal.
The Tribunal relied on a prior case where the issue of giving the benefit of past service was not raised.
However, since the issue of entitlement to the benefit of past service was not addressed in the prior case, it was held that the decision would not apply.
Therefore, the matter was sent back to the Tribunal for reconsideration.
