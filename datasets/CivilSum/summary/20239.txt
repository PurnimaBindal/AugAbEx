Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19 - Quantum of Punishment - Corruption - The disciplinary authority has the right to impose a penalty consistent with the magnitude of the misconduct - If an officer, such as a Police Inspector, is found to have demanded and accepted a bribe of Rs. 3000/- for not prosecuting an offender, the authority can impose a penalty without interference from the Tribunal - The Tribunal's observations about the punishment being unconscionable cannot be sustained.
