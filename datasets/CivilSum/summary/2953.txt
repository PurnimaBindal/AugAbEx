(Judicial) - A complaint was received (against the petitioner) accusing him of being involved in running businesses while he was enrolled as an Advocate.
His name was subsequently removed from the roll of Advocate and he lodged an appeal; however, it was dismissed.

[Para 15] B.
Constitution of India 1950, Article 226/227 - PCS (Judicial) examination - Rejection of candidature - The petitioner's name was removed from the roll of State Bar Council, which is one of the essential qualifications for taking the exam.
The Court held that being enrolled as an advocate is not the end of the eligibility process, but rather the beginning, and that continuation of one's enrolment in the State Bar Council is an essential qualification for eligible candidates taking the exam or being appointed to the services.
[Paras 14 to 16] C. Constitution of India, Article 226 - Interpretation of Statute - Essential qualifications - The expression "essential" clearly indicates the intention of authorities to make the qualifications mandatory, and they cannot be rendered ineffective by incorrect reasoning.
Moreover, these qualifications cannot be interpreted in a manner that is contrary to legislative intent.
