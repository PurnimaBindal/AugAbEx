Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4A(3) - Compensation - Interest - Liability of Insurance Company - Insurance policy exempts liability to pay interest on compensation amount - Policy clearly states that Insurance Company's liability is limited to liability under the Act - It does not include liability to pay interest on compensation amount - Insurance Company only liable for the extent of liability undertaken - As the liability to pay interest on compensation amount was not undertaken, Insurance Company not responsible for it.
