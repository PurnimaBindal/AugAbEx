Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 252 - Pay scales - Adoption of pay scales - Each State has its own unique way of governance under the Constitution - One State is not obligated to follow the rules and regulations applied to employees of other States unless specified in the order of adoption - Himachal Pradesh High Court, even if it adopted the Rules and Regulations from Punjab & Haryana High Court, is not required to follow every change brought in by the other court - However, recommendations made by the Chief Justice to the Governor must be given due consideration by the State Government.
