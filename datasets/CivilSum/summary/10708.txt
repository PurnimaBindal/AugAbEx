Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Refusal of NOC by District Magistrate to set up a retail outlet - All departments provide no objection certificates except DM, who refused on the grounds that there was another petrol pump within 300 meters of the proposed outlet - Plea not denied that Clause 41 of IRC dated December 1983 is only a recommendation and not applicable to the State of Haryana - The recommendation is merely in the nature of a guideline and cannot provide the jurisdiction for the DM to reject the claim - Adherence to the recommendation would lead to a monopoly and loss to the consumer, which is not allowed under the current governance system - Directed the issuance of the required NOC.
