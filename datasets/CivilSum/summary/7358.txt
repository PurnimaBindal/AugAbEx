Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Pension - Voluntary Retirement - Employees who did not exercise any option for pension scheme within the prescribed time are eligible for the deemed option, applicable to those in service on the cut-off date - A clause in a circular cannot take away the right to pension available to every retiring employee - The petitioner cannot be denied pension benefit just because he opted for voluntary retirement.

Interpretation of Statute - If one construction would lead to a provision or condition falling foul of another provision, the preference should be given to an interpretation that would avoid that result.

Laches - Pension - Grievances of payments dues like salary, subsistence allowance etc. would be recurring and cannot be dismissed on grounds of unfairness or illegality - The proper approach would be to evaluate the case on its merits and determine relief based on the specific circumstances of the case.
