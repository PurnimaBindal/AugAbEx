Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Maharashtra State Road Transport Corporation Standing Orders, Order 32(a) - Promotion - Time gap arrangement by promotion - Reversion on arrival of regular appointees or promotees - Order 32(a) does not apply to cases where a time gap promotee is not found fit for promotion despite repeated considerations and his promotion was conditional and temporary.
It only applies to situations where a vacancy reserved for direct recruitment arises and a suitable candidate is not available despite repeated advertisement.
[Paras 7 to 11]
