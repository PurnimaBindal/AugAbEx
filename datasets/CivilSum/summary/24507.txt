According to Tamil Nadu State and Subordinate Services Rules, 1955, Rule 10(a)(i)(1) with Rule 2(1), when it comes to seniority, temporary service and promotion cannot be used in computing seniority in the regular cadre.
A person who is temporarily appointed to perform duties for a specific position without following the recruitment guidelines is not considered to be in service until their appointment is made permanent.
[Paras 22 & 23]
