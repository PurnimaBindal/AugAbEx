Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Termination - Opportunity of hearing - Petitioners who were appointed either on regular pay scales or as daily wage basis and later regularized or promoted to higher posts were terminated without giving them a hearing as they were appointed against rules and Government instructions.
It was held that the petitioners have the right to be heard before termination and the question of whether their appointments were according to government rules should be left to the authority that hears the petitioners.
The order of termination was set aside and the petitioners were directed to be reinstated.
