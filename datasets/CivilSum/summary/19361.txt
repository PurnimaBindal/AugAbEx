Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II Rule 532-A(c) and Vol.
I, Part I, Rule 3.26(d) - Compulsory retirement - The petitioner's service record showed reports that were mostly good or satisfactory, and it was unjust and unfair to consider him as dead-weight that deserves to be terminated.
The implementation of the impugned order was suspended during the motion stage.
As the petitioner is nearing retirement, there is no point in investigating the conflict's merits, and the compulsory retirement directive has been overturned.
