Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 33 and 33-A - Dismissal - Respondent worker dismissed without a departmental inquiry - Industrial Tribunal allowed subsistence allowance during proceedings after holding an inquiry - The order of the Industrial Tribunal is modified to protect the interests of the employer, requiring the workman to provide security to repay the subsistence allowance if he fails to do so.
