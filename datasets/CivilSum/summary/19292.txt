Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 226 and 227 - Punjab Police Rules, Rule 9.18 - Petitioner ordered to be compulsorily retired - Petitioner claimed that he had been ordered to be promoted in 1979, but instead of implementation of the order, he was ordered to be compulsorily retired.
He asserted that such order violated rules, and hence cannot be sustained.
The respondents argued that the petitioner had earned adverse reports, and therefore their action in ordering retirement was legal and valid.
However, the court held that the respondents have failed to comply with the provisions of Rule 9.18(2), and hence violated the rule.
Moreover, after 1979, there was no adverse report against the petitioner, and if he was suitable for promotion in 1979, it cannot be said that it would be in public interest to compulsorily retire him in 1983, and thus it was not just and proper to retire petitioner under Rule 9.18(2).
The impugned order was therefore quashed, and the petition was allowed.
