Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F, 25G and 25N - Work-charge employee - Termination of service - Non-compliance of mandatory provisions - Respondent contends that the post was reserved for a handicap with a 40% disability while they have a 10% disability - Allegations of forgery and concealment of material facts - However, the degree of disability was not specified in the appointment order and petitioners themselves only sought a disability certificate - Therefore, there was no concealment or misrepresentation - Termination is also invalid due to non-compliance with mandatory provisions.
