According to Article 141 of the Constitution of India and Section 11 of the Contempt of Courts Act, the department may be charged with contempt if they misunderstand a Supreme Court judgment.
In this case, there was a genuine doubt about interpreting the judgment and therefore no contempt was committed.
However, despite only restoring one third of the pension, the department was directed to provide the attendant benefits.
