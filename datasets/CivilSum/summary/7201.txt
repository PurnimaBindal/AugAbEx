As per Article 113 of the Limitation Act, a suit for declaration must be filed within 3 years.
In the case of 12 orders passed with or without cumulative effect, the first order passed on 5376 and the 12th order passed on 23784.
However, a suit filed on 12490 seeking declaration that the said orders are void is barred by limitation.
