The Constitution of India, Article 311 - Appointment/Transfer/Posting - In the case of the petitioner, who was appointed as Asstt.
Professor in Medicine and subsequently posted in the Department of Cardiology, it is unreasonable to expect that he was appointed as Asstt.
Professor of Cardiology.
Appointment and posting should not be confused.
Also, the petitioner's transfer in Medicines is valid and not irregular.

The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Transfer/Personal Inconvenience/Challenging a transfer order on the ground of personal inconvenience by writ petition to the High Court is not valid.
