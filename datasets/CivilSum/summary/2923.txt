Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(4A) and 2-A — Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, Section 45 — Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation Servants (Conduct and Discipline) Regulations 1971, Regulations 30 and 35 — Industrial dispute — Reference of — Alternate remedy — Not prohibited — Claimants are not required to wait before raising and referring an industrial dispute regarding dismissal, discharge, or termination until alternatives remedies such as appeal, etc. available under the Standing Orders are exhausted.
