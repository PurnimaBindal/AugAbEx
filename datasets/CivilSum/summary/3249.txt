Constitution of India, Article 311 - APSRTC Employees (Conduct) Regulations, 1963, Regulation 28(xxii) - Misconduct - Departmental enquiry - Dismissal - A driver for AP State Road Transport Corporation was dismissed from service due to his involvement in a fatal accident caused by rash and negligent driving which resulted in the death of a motorcyclist.
Although he was acquitted of criminal charges, allegations of misconduct led to departmental disciplinary proceedings and his eventual dismissal.
It was determined that the disciplinary authority could independently evaluate the evidence presented during the enquiry and come to a separate decision from the criminal court's verdict.
