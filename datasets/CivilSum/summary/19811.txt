According to Section 11 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the Tribunal has discretion in determining the appropriate punishment for misconduct.
In a specific case where a workman was removed from service for misconduct, the Tribunal found the punishment to be disproportionate and ordered reinstatement without back wages.
As the Tribunal acted within its discretionary powers, its award cannot be interfered with unless it is deemed illegal or corrupt.
