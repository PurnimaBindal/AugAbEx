According to the Punjab State Education Class-III (School Cadre) Rules, 1979 and a circular from the Punjab Government's Education Department dated 1161992, 25% of Head Teachers, Central Head Teachers, and Block Primary Education Officers are to be directly recruited.
Although not explicitly covered by the rules, the instructions from 1161992 govern the posts of Head Teachers and Central Head Teachers.
Selections for direct recruitment have been upheld, and appointments are to be made accordingly.
