As per Section 25 of the Administrative Tribunal Act, 1985, and Rule 6(1) of the Administrative Tribunal (Procedure) Rules, 1987, cases cannot be easily transferred from one Bench to another.
However, the respondent Deputy Superintendent of Police in Gujarat, who is under suspension, has requested to retain his application before the Principal Bench in Delhi.
The respondent has claimed to be undergoing medical treatment in New Delhi and also fears for his safety in Gujarat.
Although the medical certificates filed by the respondent do not suggest him to be critically ill, it is not appropriate to allow the respondent's prayer for an ex-parte order.
