In accordance with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, the pay scales of all Technical Supervisors were revised based on the recommendations of the IInd Punjab Pay Commission.
Despite similar duties being performed by all Technical Supervisors, three out of eight posts were kept in a lower pay scale.
The reason for treating the post of Technical Supervisor (Soap) as a lower post for the purpose of pay scale was not provided.
The petitioner was directed to receive the same pay scale for their post of Technical Supervisor (Soap) as the other Technical Supervisors as revised over time.
