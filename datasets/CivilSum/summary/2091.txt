The jurisdiction of the High Court under Article 226 cannot extend to disputed questions of facts involved in a Court Martial, as stated in the Army Regulations 1962, Para 518.
[Para 4]
Without specific allegations of prejudice due to an infraction of a rule by the General Court Martial, the proceedings of a Court Martial cannot be questioned.
Thus, the Court cannot interfere and the petition is dismissed.
