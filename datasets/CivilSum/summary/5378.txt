The power to retire a government servant is not absolute and is always subject to judicial review under Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 - Pre-mature retirement.
Courts can quash the order of compulsory retirement if it is found passed arbitrarily or mala fide or was actuated by consideration other than public interest, as indicated by Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226.
