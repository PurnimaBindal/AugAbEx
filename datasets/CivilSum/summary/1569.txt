Syndicate Bank Officer Employees (Conduct) Regulations, 1976, Regulation 20 - Disciplinary enquiry - Enquiry Officer - An enquiry officer is eligible to examine witnesses, and if a witness is replaced by one who is junior to the delinquent, the succeeding enquiry officer has to rely upon evidence recorded by any earlier one available on record.
[Para 17]
Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Syndicate Bank Officer Employees (Conduct) Regulations, 1976, Regulation 6 - Enquiry report - Orders passed prior to 20111990, when there was no provision for supply of a copy of the enquiry report before passing an order of punishment, are not invalid.
[Para 17]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary proceedings - Judicial review - The court can only ensure compliance with statutory rules, procedure, and principles of natural justice.
The court cannot reassess evidence and material on record and conclusions arrived at by the disciplinary authority, except in cases of no evidence, mala fide, or unreasonable conclusions.
[Para 18]
