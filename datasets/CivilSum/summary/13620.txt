According to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, labourers who are essentially involved in agricultural operations, which are seasonal in nature, are not necessarily to be kept employed for 240 days and can be discharged after the job is over.
As per Para 13, Circular and office order do not amount to unfair labour practice or victimisation.
In case of labourers who are engaged only for agricultural operations and those engaged in the office, if there is a vacancy and they are not engaged as daily rates employee for doing agricultural operations, they can raise an industrial dispute before competent Labour Court for redressal of their grievance.
This is stated under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 in Para 15.
