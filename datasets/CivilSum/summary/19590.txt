Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission/Forged Certificate/ Cancellation of admission - Delay - Petitioner allegedly used a forged certificate to gain admission to MDS course in 1977 - A complaint was filed in 1980, after the petitioner had already passed the MDS examination - Enquiry concluded in 1985 and punishment was given in 1992 - After a 15-year period, it is not appropriate to punish a person for alleged misconduct, especially since the person has already been promoted on the basis of having passed the examination, and there is no evidence to support the claim that a forged certificate was submitted.
[Paras 10 and 11]
