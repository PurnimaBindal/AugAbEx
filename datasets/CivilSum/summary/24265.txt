Constitution of India - Article 142 - An order passed against workers by the Labor Court under the Minimum Wages Act becomes final if not challenged - The Supreme Court granted relief to workers of another state and extended benefits to workers who did not challenge Labor Court orders - Invoking the provisions of Article 142 to meet the ends of justice.
