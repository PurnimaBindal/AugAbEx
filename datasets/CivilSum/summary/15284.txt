Under the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4, an employer is liable to pay compensation for injury suffered by a workman immediately on the date of the accident.
In the case of injury suffered by the respondent workman on 2451999, the provisions as amended by Act 46 of 2000 do not apply as they came into effect on 8122000.
Therefore, the case should be assessed using Section 4 as amended by Act 30 of 1995, and the quantum of compensation should not be determined using Section 4 as amended by Act 46 of 2000.
