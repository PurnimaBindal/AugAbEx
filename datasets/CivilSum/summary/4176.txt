Constitution of India, Article 14 - Education - Admissions - Supernumerary seats - Students who have been wrongly deprived of admission approach the Court, however, there are several requirements such as attendance and practical training that cannot be by-passed even if the Court orders are passed - Admissions are given in the following year and directions of Court are carried out - Students must be accommodated in the following academic year treating those cases as supernumerary seats to avoid reducing the number of available seats for the subsequent batch - Failure to follow this procedure will infringe the rights of students who would otherwise be eligible for admission.
