The Constitution of India, in Articles 14, 16 and 226 has provisions for the revision of pay scales to reduce disparity.
Experts are responsible for considering and determining the appropriate pay scale for a given post.
There are no set rules in this regard.
The Board has formed various committees at different times to recommend pay scales and address anomalies.
The problem of disparity has persisted for over 20 years, but the Board's actions cannot be deemed arbitrary or discriminatory.
