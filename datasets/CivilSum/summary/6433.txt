According to Section 3(31) of the General Clauses Act, 1898, Section 3(3) of the Haryana Housing Board Act, 1971, and Section 32(iv) of the Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, employees of the Housing Board are entitled to receive bonus.
A single judge determined that the Housing Board cannot be considered a local authority under S. 3(31) of the General Clauses Act, but employees are still entitled to bonus.
The Industrial Tribunal has set the bonus rate at 20%.
While the Board has filed a writ petition challenging this rate, the bonus must continue to be paid at the tribunal-ordered rate of 833% until the final decision is made.
The appeal was dismissed.
