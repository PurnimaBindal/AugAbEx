Rajasthan Civil Services Rules 1951, Rule 56(3) - Absence from duty - Application for leave - The rules do not allow an employee to be granted leave for several years - An application for leave does not entitle an employee to leave unless it is authorized - If an employee is absent for more than 30 days, disciplinary action may be taken against him according to the CCA Rules - A 9-year long absence during which an employee lived in foreign countries is unacceptable - Such a lengthy absence is considered serious misconduct - The employee cannot claim a violation of natural justice - Dismissal from service is an acceptable punishment.
[Paras 20, 22, 23]
Quantum of punishment - Long absence for duty - Serious misconduct - The powers of the High Court under Article 226 to review an administrative punishment in a disciplinary matter are limited - Emotional factors cannot set disciplinary action aside - The proportionality doctrine cannot be applied if all procedural requirements are seriously violated.
