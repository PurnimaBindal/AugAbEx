The appellant in the case of Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Letters Patent Appeal, Clause X - Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961, Section 84-A - Punjab State Cooperative Land Mortgage Banks Service (Common Cadre) Rule, 1978 - Amendment in Common Cadre Rules, contended that the rules were not amended in accordance with section 84-A of the Act.
However, an employees union sought amendment in rules, the Administrator of Bank proposed the amendment, it was forwarded to the Registrar for approval, and the amendment was ultimately approved by the Registrar.
Additionally, the facts which were pleaded by respondent were not contested by appellants, and the finding of the learned Single Judge was upheld.
