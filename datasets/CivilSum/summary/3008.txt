Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo)(bb) - Termination of service on non-renewal of contract of employment between employee and management under stipulation contained therein - The definition of retrenchment does not include this type of termination.
The power under clause (bb) of Section 2(oo) was not misused or vitiated by malafide exercise, as no opportunity was given to pass examination for regular appointment.

However, this provision applies only to the post corresponding to class I and II under the Central Government.
It was not shown that the post of LDC on which the petitioners were working was a post corresponding to class I or II posts.
