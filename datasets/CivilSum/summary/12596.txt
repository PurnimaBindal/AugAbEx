In Section 10 of the Workmen Compensation Act and Section 5 of the Limitation Act (36 of 1963), delay in filing claim application can be condoned.
While a litigant may not benefit from filing a time-barred matter, a petitioner who is 85 years old and whose only son has died can have the delay in the institution of claim application condoned without adhering to the strictness of the law.
