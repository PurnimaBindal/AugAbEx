Constitution of India, Article 14 - Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, Sections 7 & 13(2) - Criminal proceedings - Disciplinary proceedings - Both proceedings based on identical charges - Departmental proceedings need not be stayed until the criminal proceedings are finalized- There is no set method for deciding whether both proceedings should go simultaneously- If identical charges and intricate evidence requires technical or legal appreciation for final determination and are so intermingled simultaneous progress may cause serious prejudice to the right of defense, then it is only in such situations that both the proceedings should not go simultaneously- Merely being identical charges is not sufficient for discontinuing both proceedings together.
