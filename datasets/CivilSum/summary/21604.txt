Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Retirement - Show- cause notice - Disciplinary proceedings - A show-cause notice can be issued for disciplinary proceedings even near superannuation, as ruled by the High Court.
However, the court's quashing of the notice is not justified in light of the need to prevent the action from escalating.
In this case, though, as the employee has already retired, setting aside the High Court's order would not serve any useful purpose.
