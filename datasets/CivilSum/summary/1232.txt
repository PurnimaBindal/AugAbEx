According to the Madhya Pradesh Public Health Engineering (Gazetted) Service Rules, 1980, Schedule 4, Rule 13, promotion to the post of Engineer-in-Chief is only available to Chief Engineer (Civil) due to the nature of work in Public Health Engineering Department being centered around water supply and sanitation projects which require mostly Civil Engineers.
This classification is based on intelligible differentia as per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309.
