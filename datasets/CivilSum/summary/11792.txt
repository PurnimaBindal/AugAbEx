Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised New Pay Scale) Rules, 1976 - Pay - Revision of pay scales - State Government transferred all Tourist Bungalows, Youth Hostels, etc. to State Tourism Development Corporation - Petitioner's services deemed surplus and was offered employment by the Corporation under certain conditions - Petitioner accepted the offer - Claiming the terms were unreasonable is not accepted as no evidence was presented to prove that the Corporation's service conditions were unfair compared to those of the State Government's employees - Petitioner was not in an unequal bargaining position - Therefore, the petitioner cannot claim revision of scales applicable to State Government employees - AIR 1986 SC 1571 distinguished.
