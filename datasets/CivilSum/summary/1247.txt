Section 47 of the Persons with Disability (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995 applies to pending proceedings on the date of commencement of the Act even though it is not retrospective.
[Para 17]
Section 47 of the same Act is applicable to all establishments, including those under the control of Government, and requires compliance regardless of the location of the employee's disability.
It also states that an employee cannot be removed due to disability, and benefits must be given even if compensation for early retirement is paid.
[Paras 13 and 14]
Disabled employees must be retained by providing alternate duties or employment.
Denying job, promotions, and increments based on disability is not allowed, and successful past job performances cannot be discounted.
