According to Regulations 4(g) and 7 of the Central Bank of India Officer Employees' (Conduct) Regulations, 1976, a disciplinary authority must abide by the principles of natural justice before awarding punishment for discharge.
The inquiry officer's findings were not taken into consideration by the disciplinary authority, who then imposed discharge as punishment without providing reasons for doing so.
As a result, the court found that the disciplinary authority committed an error by not giving the petitioner an opportunity to be heard and quashed the order.
