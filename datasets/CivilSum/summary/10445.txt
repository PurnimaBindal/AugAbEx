Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Employment - Candidates who are not sponsored by Employment Exchange must also be given consideration for selection - Insistence on the validity of an Employment Registration card becomes irrelevant when there is no requirement for sponsorship by Employment Exchange - The selection of a candidate who was not sponsored by Employment Exchange is considered valid - The submission of a fraudulent Employment Registration card would normally disqualify the 3rd respondent from appointment, however, since the 3rd respondent has already been selected on merit and appointed, the Court has decided not to consider this aspect of the case.
