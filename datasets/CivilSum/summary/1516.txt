According to the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 2(12) and Factories Act, 1948, Section 2(k) and (m), Sub Stations of Electricity Board are not covered under the 1948 Act since no manufacturing process takes place in sub stations or zonal stations.
Therefore, they do not fall within the term `factory', and the ESI Act will not apply justifying demand for contribution.
