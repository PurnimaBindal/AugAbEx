According to CCS Pension Rules, Rule 69, if there is a departmental or criminal enquiry pending, the provisional pension should be determined based on the qualifying service until the retirement of the Government Servant, or until the date immediately preceding the date of suspension in case of suspension.
It is the duty of the respondents to follow Rule 69 and fix the provisional pension of the petitioner based on the salary he drew on the date of his suspension.
