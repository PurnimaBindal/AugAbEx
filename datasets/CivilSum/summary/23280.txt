According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the primary functions of executive include writ jurisdiction, job evaluation for fixation of pay, and determining pay-scales, which is not the role of the judiciary.
Normally, the job evaluation should be done by expert bodies and not courts.
[Para 12]

High Court's directive to place the Sub-Registrars of the West Bengal Registration Service in the higher pay scale of Rs. 660-1800 was not justified solely based on their inclusion in State Service and being conferred gazetted status.
The comparison of Sub-Registrars with Judicial Magistrates is arbitrary as the latter holds higher responsibilities.
The Sub-Registrars should not be compared with Judicial Magistrates as it is an unequal comparison.
[Para 13]
