Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Sections 19 and 21 - Written test - Results review - Limitation - Results of written test declared on 18111997 - No candidate qualified the test - A candidate requested a review of result after approximately 5 years - The remedy was barred by limitation - An application filed before Tribunal in 2003 for setting aside results was dismissed because petitioner did not acquire any enforceable or legal right - The application was also barred by limitation - The Tribunal erred in not dismissing the application.
