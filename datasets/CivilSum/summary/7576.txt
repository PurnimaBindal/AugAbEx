Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Violation of women's right to protection from sexual harassment - Disciplinary proceedings - Natural justice - Exoneration of delinquent by Enquiry Officer - Punishing authority has the right to differ and record own opinion after hearing the delinquent - If done, delinquent can't complain of prejudice - A teacher committing a criminal offense of sexual assault upon his student in the premises of educational institute doesn't deserve leniency - Minor girl's lack of resistance doesn't prove charge incorrect - Supreme Court's observations in Vishaka and other cases on employer's duty to prevent sexual harassment of women at workplace apply to such cases - Violators of women's fundamental rights to security entitled to no protection from technicalities.
