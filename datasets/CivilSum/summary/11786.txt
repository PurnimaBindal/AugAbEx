Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 39 - Compassionate appointment - Delay - The entitlement to claim compassionate appointment is not absolute and can only be availed of at a reasonable time - Petitioner's father passed away while they were still a minor, but the claim for appointment on compassionate grounds after 20 years is not valid - The purpose of compassionate appointment is to provide financial relief to the family of the deceased bread earner - Reservations cannot be made in favor of the family members for compassionate appointment.
