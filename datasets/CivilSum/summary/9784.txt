After the formation of a fact-finding committee to investigate and report on financial irregularities allegations, the petitioner was transferred based on administrative action, according to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
However, without any evidence of malice against the authority who formed the fact-finding committee, the decision cannot be considered biased.
Furthermore, in the public interest, no action can be taken to obstruct the course of justice regarding the serious complaints of grave financial offenses.
No interference with the transfer decision is therefore warranted, and no regular departmental hearing has yet taken place.
