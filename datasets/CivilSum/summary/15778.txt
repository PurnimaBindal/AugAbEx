According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, and Punjab Government's Instructions dated 331980, those who have been stagnant in a particular post for a prolonged period of time are entitled to the benefit of the policy to grant selection grade.
However, if a person has already received promotions during their 15-16 years of stagnation, they are no longer eligible for this benefit.
The objective is to eliminate frustration among those belonging to a cadre with limited avenues for promotion.
