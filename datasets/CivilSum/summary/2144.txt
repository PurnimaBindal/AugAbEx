According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, an employee's pension is payable at their place of residency after retirement.
In this case, the petitioner is over the age of 70, and it is not reasonable to expect them to file a petition at the respondent's head office or the state where they worked.
As such, the petitioner is entitled to receive their pension in their home state of Kerala where they currently reside.
