Constitution of India, Articles 226, 300A - Pension - GO No. 496-Edn (B)/I.M.-39/91 dated 16/12/1991 - Assistant Teachers who chose to avail of pensionary benefits under the GO are entitled to receive the employer's share of contribution with interest and additional interest refunded forthwith to the Government.
After the petitioners opted for pension-cum-gratuity within the given time, the concerned District Inspector of Schools wrote to the petitioners to refund the Government share of PF with interest and transfer their own share promptly.
The State authority cannot withdraw the letter giving a right to the petitioner for the scheme that was their mistake.
The Court orders the refund of all amounts under the scheme to the appropriate authority of the State.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]
