The Commercial Tax Inspectors Grade II are experiencing a grievance due to their inability to be promoted to Gr.
I Inspectors, despite having the prescribed qualifications.
The posts have been vacant for many years, and the Grade I Cadre has been agitating for its abolition.
Since 1971, the post of Grade I Tax Inspector has not been filled up, and it can be considered as abandoned by the State.
This situation does not adversely affect the Grade II Inspectors since promotions to Assistant Commercial Tax Officers come from Commercial Tax Inspectors, and Commercial Taxes Inspector posts are not sub-divided.
The eligibility requirement for promotion is five years of experience and passing of a qualifying examination, but petitions of the Grade II Inspectors have failed and have been dismissed.
