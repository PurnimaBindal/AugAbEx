Army Pension Regulations 1961, Part-I, Regulation 173 - Disability Pension - A soldier who was discharged from the army during training enrollment medical due to disability assessed by the Medical Board, which was placed in the category of 'SHAPE5' Colour Perception (CP IV) 367 under Rule 13(3)(III)(IV) of Army Rules within six days from his enrollment by 1st and 2nd Medical Board and recommended invalidation being medically unfit.
However, the disability of colour blindness was opined not to be attributable to or aggravated by military service.
Thus, for the grant of disability pension, the prerequisite condition is that such disability must either be attributable to or aggravated by Military service, which must be clearly opined by the Medical Board.
Unfortunately, the Medical Board opined the disease to be constitutional and not attributable to or aggravated by Military service.
As a result, Regulations 173 held not attracted, and the petitioner isn't entitled to disability pension.
[Paras 9 to 12]
