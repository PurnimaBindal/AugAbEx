Coal India, (Executive, Conduct, Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1978, Rules 29 and 30 - Departmental enquiry - Fresh enquiry - Changing of enquiry officer - If the punishment authority is unhappy with the enquiry, a new enquiry officer can be appointed for valid and appropriate reasons - However, it is obligatory for the punishment authority to provide written reasons before doing so - If the punishment authority fails to do so, their action is improper - It is not enough to simply say "for sufficient and valid reasons."

Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - Government-owned company - A company that is owned and regulated by the government falls within the definition of "State authority" under Article 12, making it susceptible to writ jurisdiction under Article 226.
