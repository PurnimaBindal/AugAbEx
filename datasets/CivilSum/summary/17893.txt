[Para 4]  B According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, the purpose of providing a written test for making selections for the post is to select the most suitable candidates.
If the number of candidates is more than the number of available posts, then conducting a written test will be the best option.
The posts will be filled from different categories, such as Ex-Servicemen and dependents of Ex-Servicemen, as provided in the advertisement.
If suitable candidates from the Ex-Servicemen category are available and all posts are filled, then dependents of Ex-Servicemen cannot be accommodated.
However, if suitable Ex-Servicemen candidates are not found on the test, then dependents of Ex-Servicemen who passed the test could be given an opportunity to fill the post.
As the petitioners did not receive the minimum marks prescribed, they cannot be appointed to the posts and cannot question the selection of others.
[Para 6]  C. According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, if the number of candidates is more than the number of posts to fill, a written test is required to select suitable candidates from eligible candidates.
The petitioners, who failed to obtain the minimum prescribed marks for passing test, cannot be granted any relief.
