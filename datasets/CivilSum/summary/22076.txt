Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Delhi Police (Appointment and Recruitment) Rules, 1980, Rule 5(h) - Deputation - Any Rule, Regulation or Executive Instruction that affects the service rendered by a deputationist in an equivalent cadre in the parent department while counting his seniority in the deputation post would be violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution and liable to be struck down.
Seniority on absorption in a deputation post is important.

Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 - Judgments and Precedents - An earlier judgment of another Coordinate Bench of the same Tribunal is binding on the subsequent Bench - However, if the subsequent Coordinate Bench is of the opinion that the earlier view taken by a similar bench is not correct, it should refer the matter to a larger bench.
It will not overrule a Co-ordinate Bench's judgment as it will be against judicial discipline.
[Para 12]
Construction of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Government litigation - When there is a dispute of rights between two employees, the Govt. must strictly implement their rights according to the Rules.
If the matter goes to court, the Govt. must act only as an amicus curiae, assisting the judicial forum to arrive at a correct decision and must not seem to be supporting a particular group of employees.
Once there is a judicial decision on the matter, the Govt. must give effect to it.
