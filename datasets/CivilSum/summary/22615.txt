Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Punishment - Amount of punishment - Courts should not interfere with the choice of proportionality and quantity of punishment administered by the punishing authority unless it is shockingly disproportionate - Even if the punishment is found to be shockingly disproportionate, it is appropriate to instruct the punishing authority to reconsider the punishment imposed - To do so, the Court must provide enough reasons as to why it considers the punishment to be excessive.
[Paras 11 to 13]
