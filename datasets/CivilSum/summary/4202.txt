Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Review - Second review - The Labour Court granted the claim of the workman under section 33-C(2) after due notice to the employer, and the employer's review application was also rejected after full contest.
However, the second application for review of both the earlier review order and the main order passed under section 33-C(2) was deemed not maintainable by the Labour Court.
Therefore, a writ petition is necessary to quash the order passed by the Labour Court without jurisdiction.
