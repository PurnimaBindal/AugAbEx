Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Voluntary resignation - Ex parte inquiry - If an employee receives a charge-sheet with frivolous allegations after resigning and is not interested in contesting it, they must provide a written statement detailing their lack of interest - In this case, the competent authority must proceed ex parte and complete the inquiry and issue an order on the voluntary resignation without unnecessary delay - Both proceedings should be finalized within 3 months as directed.
[Paras 8 to 11]
