Constitution of India, Articles 342(1) and 366(25) - Scheduled Tribe - Status of Lohar - According to Articles 366(25), Lohars were not considered as scheduled tribes - The court also ruled that the case of Nityanand Sharma v. State of Bihar, JT 1996(2) SC 117 did not mandate a recommendation - Hence, the Lohar community is not eligible for the Scheduled Tribe status.
