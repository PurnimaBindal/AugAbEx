Under the Andhra Pradesh Subordinate Service Rules, Rule 4 has been amended to specify considerations for promotion, eligibility, and vacancies.
Prior to this, it was required that vacancies exist and the government decides to fill them in order to prepare a panel.
The government did not make a decision before the amendment and therefore, employees did not have a vested right to be considered and promoted against any vacant positions.
A tribunal was mistaken in directing the government to operate the panel for promoting employees according to repealed rules.
