Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10(i) - Reference - Limitation - Dismissal of workman's lawsuit on the basis of civil Court lacking jurisdiction - In certain situations such as seeking an alternative remedy, the period spent on pursuing such remedy is to be excluded from the five years limitation period fixed by the Full Bench in Ram Chander Morya's case, 1999(1) SCT 141 which was later overturned by the Supreme Court in Ajaib Singh v. Sirhind Coop., 1999(2) SCT.
