As per Section 10(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, the jurisdiction of the appropriate government to entertain and decide disputes depends on the cause of action.
The petitioner, who worked as a Line Man in HSEB at Sadhaur in Distt.
Ambala, had his service record and conditions regulated from Panchkula, where the Board's head office is situated.
As the workman had no concern with the office of the Chief Engineer of the Board located at Chandigarh, where no termination order was served, the appropriate government and the Labour Court at Chandigarh did not have jurisdiction to decide the dispute.
