The validity of the appellant's termination is supported by Section 2(oo)(bb) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, which applies to the situation of a badli worker who was appointed as a daily wage worker in place of another workman.
When the original workman returns, the appointment of the appellant automatically comes to an end.
Therefore, the termination of the appellant does not constitute retrenchment and is considered valid according to Paras 5 & 7 of the text.
