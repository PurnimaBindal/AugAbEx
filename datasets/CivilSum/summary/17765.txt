"The petitioner, who was originally appointed as JE by the government of Punjab and subsequently allocated to the State of Haryana, was promoted multiple times in Bhakra Dam Project, Nangal Township.
However, despite not being treated as on deputation and not receiving any allowance, his pension was fixed under Rule 619-C(iv) of Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol. II, which the petitioner argues is inapplicable to him.
Instead, his pension and other retiral benefits should be fixed treating him as an employee of the Haryana Govt., in accordance with Rule 6.19-C(i) of Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II and Rule 2.44(a) of Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.I Part I (as applicable to the State of Haryana).
