Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16(4) - Reservation/Appointments - The institute has the right to select the best talent in every field, and this should not be limited by considerations of caste or creed or reservation of posts in favour of any class of citizens for populist reasons.
Any instructions issued by the government that contradict this may be in violation of the Act and the institution's objectives.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16(4) - Reservation/Single cadre post - Posts of Assistant Professors may not be interchangeable between departments, and reserving a single post is only allowed if it is demonstrated that a particular specialty has more than one post.
If a backward class is not adequately represented in the service, the state may make a reservation.
[Paras 9 and 11]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16(4) - Reservation in admissions - The goal of rules regulating medical college admissions should be to secure the best and most meritorious students, ensuring the most talented teachers are available to train students.
