Misconduct - Termination of services - Requests by management for permission to lead evidence of misconduct before Labour Court - Rule that application must be filed without unreasonable delay - Reference from 1982 - Written statements filed in January 1984 - Management requests for a formal inquiry in May 1989 - Court correctly declines permission to lead evidence as request was made after the earliest stage.

Reinstatement - Termination of Service - Full backwages to be awarded as a result of reinstatement is not always correct - Courts must examine the evidence of parties to determine the extent of backwages to be awarded - Case sent back to Labour Court for this purpose.
