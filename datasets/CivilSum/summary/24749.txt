WB Rural Employment and Production Act, 1976, Sections 4(1) and 4(2)(aa) as amended in 1981 and 1982 - CESS - Rural Employment Cess - The amendment imposing the levy in respect of despatches of tea, and not in respect of tea estates, was not introduced or moved in the State legislature with prior consent of the President, violating the provisions of Article 304(b).
As a result, the amendment is violative of Article 301 of the Constitution and void for legislative competence, as it pertains to a covered field.
