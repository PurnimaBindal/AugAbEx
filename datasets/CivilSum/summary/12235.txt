Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Municipal Employees Pension and General Provident Fund Rules, 1994, Rule 3 - Family Pension - The Government reserves the right to verify the validity of an alleged pension option and ensure it was granted in adherence to the law - The Government asserts that the alleged option was a forgery and thus cannot be granted - As a disputed question of fact, it may be resolved in a court of competent jurisdiction - The petitioner is not entitled to relief and may bring the matter to a civil court to dispute the respondents' actions.
