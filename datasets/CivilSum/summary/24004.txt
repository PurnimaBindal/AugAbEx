The Protection of the Human Rights Commission Act, 1993, Sections 3(2), 2(1), 8, 40 and 41, National Human Rights Commission Chairperson and Members (Salaries, Allowances and other Conditions of Service) Rules, 1993, Rules 3(b), 4(2), All India Service Death-cum-Retirement Benefit Rules, 1958, Rule 10, Central Civil Services (Fixation of Pay of Re-employed Pensioners) Orders, 1986, Rule 14, Fixation of pay of re-employed retired Judges, Gratuity, Payment of gratuity to re-employed officers, Eligibility for.
The re-employed Members in respect of any previous service under the Government of Union or Govt. of a State must have a deduction of pension from their salary.
Service of Supreme Court Judges and High Court Judges is also in respect of service of Union of India if independent in all respects and being a constitutional post.
Their appointment as Chairman or Member of a Commission after retirement is a re-employment within the meaning Rule 14 of 1986 Order.
Deduction of pension received by them in fixing their salary as Chairman or Member of the Commission under the Rules is not unconstitutional nor the Rules are unconstitutional.
It is within the power and jurisdiction of the Govt. to frame such Rules and regulations.
They are also not entitled to Gratuity and leave encashment after availing this benefit at the time of their retirement from the post of Judges.
