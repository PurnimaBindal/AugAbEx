Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Seniority - Counting of ad hoc appointment period towards fixation of seniority - Petitioner appointed on temporary basis and later regularised - Petitioner seeking seniority in rank of clerks - Petitioner's appointment was a stop-gap arrangement and not according to rules - Petitioner cannot count the period of temporary work for the purpose of seniority.
