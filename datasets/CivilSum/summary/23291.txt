According to Section 20 of the Evidence Act, an agreed appointment of a person with special knowledge to take an oath in a religious place is considered an admission of fact if the appointed person takes the oath as agreed.
Even though Sections 9 and 12 are no longer in effect, such a statement is still admissible.
In a case where the plaintiff's resignation was allegedly obtained under duress, fear, inducement, fraud, or coercion, they agreed to withdraw their claim if two named persons took a special oath in a Gurdwara and Mandir, respectively, confirming that the resignation was voluntary.
When the two named persons took the oath as agreed, the parties were bound by it and the claim was rightly dismissed by the court.
The trial court's order was upheld as the oath taken amounted to an admission of the respondent's claim, as per Section 20 of the Evidence Act.
