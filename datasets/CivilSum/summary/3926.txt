Kerala Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1960, Rule 11 - Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, Section 7, 13(1)(d) and 13(2) - If an employee is found guilty of committing an offence involving corruption, they can be dismissed without notice by the authority and are not entitled to receive a prior opportunity before the imposition of punishment.
The disciplinary authority is given discretionary powers in choosing an appropriate punishment, which may take into account an employee's past conduct and the remaining period of service.
