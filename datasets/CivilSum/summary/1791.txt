Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(1)(c), 33(2)(b) - Award - Previous findings on essential ingredients by Apex court under Section 33(2)(b) may bar subsequent adjudication - Apex court found unauthorized absence, misconduct and proper enquiry in application for dismissal approval - If findings on essential ingredients upheld by highest court, challenge to labour court's award by workman may not be appropriate for High Court to overturn.
