Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Salary - Voluntary Retirement - Petitioner, after taking voluntary retirement from Corporation, was appointed as Headmaster in Secondary School.
The petitioner did not have any obligation to refund any compensation to the Central/State Govt. On accepting employment, the petitioner did not enter into any public sector undertaking; furthermore, the State Govt. did not have control over the employer institution.
The terms and conditions brought into existence in the scheme through modifications do not oblige the petitioner to refund the compensation he received after voluntary retirement.
Therefore, District Inspector of Schools was unjustified in withholding the payment of salaries and allowances to the petitioner, simply because salaries and allowances are paid out of funds provided by the State Govt. [Paras 10 and 11]
