The termination order of a probationer and whether it is stigmatic by way of punishment was examined in this case.
The appellant's service was deemed unsatisfactory and detailed reference to all other correspondence did not reflect any malice or bias.
The High Court correctly found that the letter was not punitive in nature.
It was noted that no enquiry was held or procedure laid in the bye-laws adopted on allegations of serious misconduct.
The termination order, which explicitly states what is implicit, does not ipso facto become stigmatic.
[Paras 13 & 17]
