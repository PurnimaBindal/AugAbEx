Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 14-B - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 19 - Quasi-judicial Orders/Speaking Orders/Penalty - While dealing with penal consequences, a quasi-judicial authority must pass an order supported by reasons fulfilling the tests of natural justice and provisions of relevant statute and guidelines provided thereunder to expose itself to the writ jurisdiction of the court and correct any infirmities - Regional Provident Fund Commissioner's power under Section 14-B is quasi-judicial, and the order must be detailed on arriving at a particular percentage of penalty - Non-speaking orders like the impugned ones have been correctly quashed - Judgment confirmed.
