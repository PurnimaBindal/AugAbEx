Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion - Appointment - Qualification - A petitioner seeks consideration for the position of Teacher Grade-III despite not possessing the required Senior Secondary qualification, but rather having only passed Secondary School Examination.
They argue that previous appointments were governed by different rules that only required Secondary + Teachers training.
However, it is determined that calling for candidates every year to fill vacancies is not mandatory and the petitioner does not have a guaranteed right to appointment without the necessary qualifications.
[Paras 5 and 6]
