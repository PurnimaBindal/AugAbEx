Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 4 - Gratuity - Transfer of gratuity - According to the Act, an employee is entitled to receive gratuity on termination of employment after five years of continuous service - The employee in this scenario had not completed five years of service until he took up a new job in 1976 with the consent of his previous employer - The provision for transfer of gratuity from the first employer to the subsequent employer was made in 1979 and 1988, but it cannot be applied retrospectively - Therefore, the employee is not eligible to transfer gratuity from his first employer.
