Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Andaman and Nicobar Police Manual, 1963, Rule 5.4(c) - Promotion - Seniority - The petitioner, who was promoted to the rank of ASI on an ad hoc basis subject to his qualifying and being confirmed as Head Constable, has had his promotion challenged by respondents 6-10 on the grounds that their cases were not considered while giving promotion and that the petitioner was junior to them.
It was found that the petitioner was not a confirmed police officer, i.e. Head Constable, at the time of promotion and nothing was produced to show that the procedure was followed while giving promotion.
As a result, the tribunal below was justified in interfering with the order of promotion.
