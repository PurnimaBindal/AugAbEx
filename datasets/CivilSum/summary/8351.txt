Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - Back date - The plaintiff-respondent requested promotion from the back date of Dec 1990, but was instead promoted to the position of Supdt.
on 26.4.1994.
The trial court granted the plaintiff's claim, but the state appealed and the judgment was modified to consider the plaintiff for promotion from back date if deemed eligible.
The trial court then issued a declaration in favor of the plaintiff, which the state appealed.
According to basic law, no person has the right to be promoted.
Even if the plaintiff had the right to be considered for promotion from the back date, they could not have requested promotion outright, as the decision to promote an employee is ultimately up to the appointing authority.
The first appellate court relied on this law and the judgment is not illegal.
The second appeal was dismissed.
