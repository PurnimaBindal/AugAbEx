According to Section 18 of the Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, any settlement made between workmen and management under Section 12(3) of the Act will be binding on all workers, regardless of whether they were union members or not.
Even workers who previously agreed not to claim arrears of salary upon promotion and have since benefited from the settlement are bound by its terms and cannot later claim arrears that go against the settlement.
