Constitution of India 1950, Article 226 - Removal - Backwages - Commissioner's decision to set aside the removal of conductor was due to a violation of Rules of Natural Justice - No relief for back wages can be granted since the petitioner did not plead for it in the writ petition and did not mention that he was not employed during the intervening period.

Constitution of India 1950, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Sections 2(s) and 3(c)(2) - Back wages - Atternative Remedy - If a conductor's removal from service was set aside due to a violation of principles of natural justice, the workman should take recourse under Section 33(c)(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act for back wages instead of filing a writ petition in the High Court.
