The Government Notification dated July 23, 1957 under Constitution of India, Article 16 allowed JBT Teachers who acquired higher qualifications of BA, B.Ed.
to be granted Masters grade.
However, in March, 1990, the Government amended the instructions to only offer higher scales to JBT Teachers appointed for Masters posts, and these instructions will only apply from that point.
Teachers who obtained higher qualifications prior to March, 1990 are still entitled to the higher scale of pay for Masters, as per AIR 1987 SC 1621.
