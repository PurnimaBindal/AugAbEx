Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 3.17(iii) - Pension - Counting of ad hoc service - Once services have been regularised, the work-charge period and ad hoc period must be counted as qualifying service for pension.
Rule 3.17(iii) of Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, was struck down by Full Bench in Kesar Chand v. State of Punjab, 1988(5) SLR 27, as it excluded the counting of work-charge service for employees who were later regularised.
