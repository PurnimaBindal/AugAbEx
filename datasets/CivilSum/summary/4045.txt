The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(3), 23 and 24 - Lock out - Prohibition of - The petitioner company was unable to prove the functional integrality of their Bombay and Delhi units.
Additionally, section 23 prohibits the declaration of a lock out during ongoing proceedings before a Labour Court.
As two Industrial Disputes were already pending adjudication before the Industrial Tribunal, the lock out declared by the petitioner in the Delhi unit was held to be illegal.
