Constitution of India, Articles 136, 226 - Pay scale - Equal pay - Lecturers of Northern Regional Institute of Printing Technology at Allahabad claim parity in pay with lecturers in Govt. Polytechnics - Claim resisted on basis of difference in educational qualifications - Pay Commission had already taken this difference into consideration when recommending the current grade of 2200-4000 for the petitioners - Denial of the pay scale of Rs. 3000-4500 to petitioners by the government based on qualifications difference was unjustified - High Court correctly quashed the order withdrawing the benefit of the scale of Rs. 3000-4500 for the writ petitioners.
[Paras 5, 7 and 10]
