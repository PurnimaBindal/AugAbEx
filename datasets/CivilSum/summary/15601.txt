Constitution of India, Article 226 - Air India Employees Service Regulations, Regulation 46(ii) - Eligible employees can opt for voluntary retirement after completing 20 years or more of continuous service - The petitioners applied for voluntary retirement but did not receive a response from the respondent - The petitioner seeks a direction to the respondent to grant voluntary retirement - The request is subject to the approval of the Managing Director and is not automatically effective after the expiry of three months - The direction to grant voluntary retirement cannot be given immediately - The Managing Director of the respondent No. 1 must consider the petitioner's application for voluntary retirement as per Regulation 46(ii) within two months.
