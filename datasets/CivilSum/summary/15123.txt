Constitution of India, Article 311 - Delhi State Industrial Development Corporation Limited Staff Service Rules, 1978, Rules 1(iii), 2(h), 3, 45, 46 - Contractual appointee - Termination of service - Petitioner was employed for one year beyond contract expiration - Argument that termination order accused petitioner without an opportunity to be heard - Argument that termination is a penalty under Rule 45 but procedure under Rule 46 was not followed - Held, Rule 3(i) states that the petitioner's case will be governed by the terms of their appointment letter - If the petitioner continued working, it implies an agreement that came to an end upon termination - Use of stigmatic language is irrelevant to the facts of the case.
