According to Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a workman is defined as an employee who does not fall under the category of supervisory work.
This includes an Accounts Officer whose duty involves a supervisory character.
If an employee holds a supervisory position and receives a wage exceeding Rs. 1600/- pm, they are no longer considered a workman.
The court has carefully examined the Account Officer's duty and determined that they do not qualify as a workman.
The Bench agrees with this conclusion.
