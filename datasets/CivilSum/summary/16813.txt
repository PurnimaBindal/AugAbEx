The Rajasthan Animal Husbandry Subordinate Services Rules, 1977 and the Rajasthan Class IV Service (Recruitment and other Service Conditions) Rules 1963, along with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, deal with promotions.
Petitioners who work on Class IV posts seek promotion to higher posts, but challenge the condition that makes it mandatory to have worked on another Class IV post for at least 5 years.
The Rules do not provide any other avenue of promotion.
It is held that the condition cannot be deemed as irrational or unconstitutional and is for the Rule making authority to decide.
Directions are given to offer petitioners promotion to suitable posts and amend Rules if necessary.
