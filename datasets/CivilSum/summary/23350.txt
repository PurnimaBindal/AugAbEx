Constitution of India, Article 311 - Factories Act, 1948, Section 49 - Termination/Service Rules - Application of service Rules - Without an appointment order, a person appointed to a lower position to perform duties and work of a higher position cannot claim the application of Service Rules of the higher position - An Assistant Personnel Officer who handles labor issues does not become a Labor Officer and cannot claim protection under the Rules applicable to that position.
