Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pension Regulations for Army, Paragraph 173 - Disability pension - The petitioner was released from service due to an invalidating disease, despite being capable of serving beyond the contractual term of 20 years.
The court held that the petitioner was boarded out on medical grounds and not on completion of term.
[Para 5]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability pension - The petitioner was invalidated out on account of the disease `Para Sagital Meningioma (OPTD)', which is common among Jawans undertaking difficult duties.
The court held that the disease was attributable to army service and ordered the respondent-authority to release disability pension benefits to the petitioner.
