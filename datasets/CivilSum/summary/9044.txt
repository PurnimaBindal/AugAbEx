The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 2(s), and the Termination rules stipulated in it, was found to be applicable by a court in a case where a staff reporter who had completed an apprenticeship was terminated.
B The Working Journalists (Conditions of Service) and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1955, Sections 2(g) and 3, requires that the period of apprenticeship be added to the total length of the employee's service.
A petitioner who worked for over 240 days, with no break in service, had his services terminated in contravention of Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act and Section 3(3)(b) of the Journalists Act.
