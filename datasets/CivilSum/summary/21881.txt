According to the Workmen's Compensation Act, Insurance Companies are not required to pay interest on compensation unless stated in a contractual agreement with the employer, but under the Motor Vehicle Act, they do have a statutory obligation to pay interest.
In a scenario where a workman dies due to a work-related accident and a compensation award is made, the Insurance Company is liable to pay only the compensation and not the interest, unless the contract specifies otherwise.
To recover the interest, the Workmen must pursue the employer, and the Insurance Company can only be held liable if there is a contractual agreement between the Insurer and the employer.
This is in accordance with the 2004 ACJ 452 case law.
