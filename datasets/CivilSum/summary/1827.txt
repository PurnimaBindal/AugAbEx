Jammu and Kashmir Subordinate Services Rules, 1992, Rule 13(ii) requires promotion selection based on experience and qualifications.
While short-listing is prescribed, high percentage of marks is not a reasonable rationale when experience is the principal consideration, and such criteria violates Articles 14 and 16.
[Paras 4 & 5] B. District-wise promotion selection based on experience and qualifications is allowed under Jammu and Kashmir Subordinate Services Rules, 1992, Rule 13(ii).
Adopting different selection criteria in one district does not violate Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution if the posts are of district cadre.
