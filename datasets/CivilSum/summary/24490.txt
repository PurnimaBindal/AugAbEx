Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 19 - Letters Patent, Clause 15 - In contempt proceedings, appealable orders are limited to those punishing for contempt.
An appeal U/Art.
136 of the Constitution may be maintained in special circumstances where an appeal U/s 19 is not maintainable.
[Paras 10 and 11(I) and (II)]
Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 19 - Letters Patent, Clause 15 - In contempt proceedings, the Contempt Court cannot decide on any issue relating to the merits of the dispute between the parties.
Any such decision or direction made by the Contempt Court is not appealable U/s 19 except for incidental or inextricably connected directions.
[Paras 11(III] and (IV) and 18]
Letters Patent, Clause 15 - Interlocutory orders - Appeal against - A Letters Patent appeal is maintainable only from a `judgment' of a single judge in exercise of original jurisdiction to a Division Bench - In a writ proceedings directing payment U/s 17B of Industrial Disputes Act is final determination affecting the vital and valuable rights and obligations of parties, therefore, against such an order Letters Patent Appeal would lie.
[Paras 14 to 16]
Civil Procedure Code, Section 79, Order 29 Rule 1, Order 3 Rule 6, Order 27 Rules 1 and 2 - Appeal by public body - Chairman and Secretary-in-Charge are considered as representing the Bank and are competent to file an appeal against directions issued to them in the Bank's proceedings.
[Para 17]
Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 19 - Scope of contempt proceedings - Contempt Court cannot adjudicate or decide any issue relating to the merits of the dispute between the parties nor issue directions which could or could not be issued by the Court adjudicating the main case on its merits.
Issuance of such instructions in contempt proceedings would be wholly without jurisdiction.
