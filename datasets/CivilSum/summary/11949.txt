Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 4 states that stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect is a major penalty and cannot be imposed without a regular enquiry, as per Article 311 of the Constitution of India Penalty imposed without following proper procedure would violate Article 311.
[Para 7]
The imposition of a major penalty without holding a proper enquiry or giving a fresh notice is a violation of the principles of natural justice and Article 311(2) of the Constitution of India.
In this case, a show cause notice for minor penalty was proposed but major penalty was imposed without any enquiry.
