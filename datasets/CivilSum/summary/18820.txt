The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 require merit-based admission to the Elementary Teachers Training Diploma Course, with a 2:2 seat bifurcation between male and female candidates and the fifth seat going to a meritorious candidate from the waiting list.
In this case, the petitioner secured 104 marks and was placed on the waiting list for males, while the last candidate in the female category waiting list had scored 10350 marks.
Since the petitioner had the prior right against the fifth seat, the court directed the offering of a seat to him within 15 days.
