Respondent No. 1, represented by Shri Anita Agarwal and Ms. Priyana C Mehta, Advocates, relied on Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1977, Section 9 and Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 28.
The case involved the termination of an Assistant Teacher whose performance was found unsatisfactory by the school management.
The management issued two notices about the unsatisfactory performance and gave a two-month notice before terminating her services.
The order for her termination was deemed appropriate.
