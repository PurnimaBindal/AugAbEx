The Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Sections 21 and 19 state that claims of promotion and seniority should not be delayed or lapsed.
In 1972, the competent authority and appellate authority determined the applicant's claim of promotion and seniority.
The petitioner was eventually promoted in 1983, and accepted it without challenge.
The tribunal cannot justify entertaining the applicant's claim of being promoted retrospectively and consequent to his seniority as it is too belated.
This kind of claim cannot be heard by the Tribunals.
