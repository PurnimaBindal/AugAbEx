Constitution of India, Article 16 - Training of lady health worker course - For applicants from Haryana applying to the course in Rajasthan, proof of a bona fide resident certificate of Rajasthan is required.
Refusal to interview an applicant on the grounds of lacking such domicile is improper if the applicant is married to a bona fide resident of Rajasthan, as a married woman acquires the domicile of her husband upon marriage.
The decision in AIR 1959 Raj.
153 is a relevant authority.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ petition - If a petitioner in a writ petition seeking admission to the Training of Lady Health Worker Course has not claimed any relief against the selected candidate, nor sought relief against the advertisement for the course, the writ petition is maintainable.
