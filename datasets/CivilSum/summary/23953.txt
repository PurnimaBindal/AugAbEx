Constitution of India, Article 136 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Sections 14 and 29(2) - The question of whether pending appeals before High Court were transferable to Central Administrative Tribunal after the issue of notification under Section 14(2) - The courts held that as per the proviso to sub-section (2) of Section 29 of the Act, the appeal is supposed to be decided on its merits by the High Court - The case was sent back to High Court to be decided accordingly.
