The Disabilities Act of 1995, Sections 33 and 47, state that the benefits cannot be denied to individuals with disabilities.
In this case, the appellant suffered from an ailment while serving as a constable in the CISF and was later declared permanently incapacitated for further employment in 2004.
Despite this, the appellant can still perform non-combatant duties and cannot be denied the benefits granted by Section 47 of the Act.
