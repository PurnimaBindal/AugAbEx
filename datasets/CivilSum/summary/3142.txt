Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Interview - Appointment - 60 marks for written test, 35 for interview and 5 for extra-curricular activities - Reserving 40% marks at the discretion of selection committee for interview and other activities resulted in arbitrariness - Applying method of conversion of marks on the basis of percentage of marks, petitioner outranks selected candidates appointed on the strength of interview marks - Appellant-company ordered to appoint the petitioner due to arbitrariness.
