Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Punishment and Appeal Rules, Rules 7 and 8 - Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 22, Clauses (a) and (b) - Pension/Gratuity - Withholding of - If a pensioner is convicted of a serious crime or guilty of grave misconduct, the State Government has the authority to withhold pension or a portion of it - In this case, the government retired a petitioner from government service on his attainment of superannuation, under the condition that his pension/gratuity would be withheld until criminal cases were finalized - The court held that while the gratuity and pension had been rightfully withheld, it was unjustified to deny the petitioner his entire pension and ordered the respondent to grant provisional pension to him to prevent him from suffering from starvation.
