Sections 17 and 17-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 discusses the ex parte award.
An award published will become enforceable, and when it does, the Labour Court becomes functus officio after a year.
In a case where an application is filed by an employer challenging the award and for condonation of delay, the Labour Court had no jurisdiction to entertain the application for condonation and for setting aside the ex parte award on the date on which they were presented.
