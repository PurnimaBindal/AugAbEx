Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1)(c) - Reference - Proceedings under the Act on a reference - An aggrieved party can move an application for setting aside an ex parte award during the interregnum that does not conclude till after 30 days from the date of publication of the award, and Labour Court does not become functus officio.
[Para 4] B Practice - A party cannot raise a new plea before Labour Court for the first time in a writ petition unless it affects the jurisdiction of the Court.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Back wages - Workman shall be deemed to have been in continuous service if retrenchment has violated provisions of Section 25-F - Payment of back wages should automatically accrue to him - Petitioner has already reinstated the workman, hence, the workman is entitled to payment of back wages.
