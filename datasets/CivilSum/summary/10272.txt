Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 29 and 33C(2) - Award - Reinstatement - Closure of Industry - The award for workman reinstatement with continuation of service and back wages cannot be insisted upon until a closure is deemed invalid - Criminal proceedings are not applicable until this issue is decided.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 29 and 33C(2) - Award - Closure of Industry - Back Wages - Failure to comply with an award allowing back wages is a breach of Section 29 - The employer can still tender the back wages for the period - Criminal proceedings will not be held if the amount is deposited with interest @ 9% per annum within one month.
