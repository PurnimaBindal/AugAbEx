Seniority - While there are different specialities in the medical profession, not all are deemed equally important - Promotions to higher positions are determined by available vacancies in each respective speciality - It's possible for a junior employee with a less significant speciality to be promoted more quickly than a senior with a different speciality - However, juniors who receive expedited promotions due to their speciality and available vacancies should not be given priority over their seniors for administrative posts - Any benefits gained by juniors due to luck or circumstances should not undermine the promotion rights of their seniors for roles that don't require speciality or extensive teaching experience.
