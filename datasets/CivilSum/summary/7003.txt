Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - A challenge to the legality of an award made in favour of a workman was made by the State after a three-year lapse without explanation.
The court held that such petitions with such inordinate delays are filed only to gain the seal of court and abuse the powers of courts.
The State Government should examine it with a pragmatic approach and take appropriate disciplinary measures against defaulting officers.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - The termination of service was in violation of the Total days of service rendered by the workman in the twelve months preceding the date of termination, which was more than 240 days.
As a result, there was non-compliance with Section 25-F of ID Act, and the Labour Court correctly set aside the termination of the workman.
