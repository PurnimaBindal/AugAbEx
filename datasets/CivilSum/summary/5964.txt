According to Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Section 3, a single entry of doubtful integrity is enough to warrant the premature retirement of an employee, and the requirement of 70% good reports only applies when simple adverse entries are used.
[Para 10]
Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3, states that there is no fixed 10-year service record requirement, especially in cases with multiple spread-over adverse entries of doubtful integrity.
[Para 18]
Even if adverse entries made earlier to crossing efficiency bar are taken into consideration for premature retirement, the efficiency bar can still be crossed subject to conveying adverse remarks and no representation made against adverse remarks.
[Para 15]
The fact that certain adverse entries were made against which representations were pending, but were rejected later on, does not necessarily mean that the other entries are insufficient for warranting premature retirement; even if certain entries are ignored, other entries may still be enough to support the premature retirement order.
