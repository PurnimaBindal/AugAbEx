The petitioner had previously submitted an admission form as a regular student but was unable to appear as a result of an extended illness.
The roll number that had been issued earlier by the Board was irrelevant, and the school authorities had no objections as long as he was allowed to take the exam as a private candidate.
The Board's only objection was that he had failed to disclose that he had submitted his form for regular admission.
However, this error was deemed irrelevant, and the Board could not press Regulation 7, since the student's name was removed from the school's rolls.
The order of the single judge was upheld.
