The Pallavan Transport Corporation Longevity Pay Scheme and Conditions of Service Rules, 1975, mandated the choice of a cut off date for pension, which had to be rational and justified.
The choice of 151975 as the cut off date was not considered arbitrary, but it was subsequently found to have lost its significance since the corporation kept changing the last dates for employee options, and GO No. 378 was kept in abeyance.
The Supreme Court ruled that the process of absorption was not complete, and that the cut off date should be 141982, when the Commissioner and Secretary to Govt. Transport Department requested managing directors to obtain fresh options by 2821982.
The corporation was instructed to adjust monetary benefits in favor of employees accordingly.
