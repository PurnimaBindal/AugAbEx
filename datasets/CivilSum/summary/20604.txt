The Defence Research and Development Organisation Technical Cadre Recruitment Rules, 1995, Rules 3(a) and 3(b), and 6(3)(a) and 6(3)(b) were created to restructure the scientific and technical cadres, creating a new organisation service called DRTC.
The purpose was to rationalize the cadre structure and provide reasonable promotional opportunities to group B and C scientific and technical staff in the DRDO Organisation.
[Para 20]
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 state that classification can be based on qualities or characteristics of persons grouped together, with reasonable relation to the object being sought out.
Merit or experience can serve as a proper basis for classification to promote efficiency in administration.
Discrimination is not permissible.
[Paras 17 and 18]
Defence Research and Development Organisation Technical Cadre Recruitment Rules, 1995, Rule 6(3)(a) & 6(3)(b) - The action of putting the Assistant Foremen en bloc junior to the Senior Scientific Assistants was arbitrary and discriminatory and violates fundamental rights guaranteeing equality in service matters.
The rule is liable to be struck down because the action of depriving benefits to one section of civil service to satisfy another is unconstitutional.
