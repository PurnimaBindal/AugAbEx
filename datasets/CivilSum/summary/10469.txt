Constitution of India, Article 311 - Bombay Civil Services Rules, 1959, Rule 161, Clause 2(aa) - Compulsory retirement - The order of premature retirement was given based on the doubtful integrity of the petitioner, despite of lacking material on record or any service record to support the claim.
Besides, the petitioner was even promoted and allowed to cross the Efficiency Bar as his services were found to be meritorious, and he received a cash reward for the same.
Thus, the order of premature retirement has been set aside, and the petitioner is entitled to all consequential benefits.
