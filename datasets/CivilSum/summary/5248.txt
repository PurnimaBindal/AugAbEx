According to legal provisions, the Institute must fill additional vacancies from the panel of suitable candidates chosen by the selection committee as per Rule 10 of its Rules and Regulations.
In this case, one post fell vacant before the panel was exhausted for the Assistant Professor of Chemistry position advertised on 15121998.
The appointments could not be made due to a stay order.
As a result, it is crucial for the respondents to fulfill Rule 10 of the Institute's Rules and Regulations.
Therefore, the petitioner's writ was allowed.
