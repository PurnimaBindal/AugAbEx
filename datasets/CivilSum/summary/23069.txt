Section 27 of the Army Act allows for punishment of reprimand for Indian Army officers, and principles of natural justice must be followed.
The appellant in this case was served with a charge-sheet, allowed to inspect documents before Technical Board Officers, and made a statement.
The authorities considered all aspects before concluding that the appellant's workmanship was poor and supervision lacking, and sufficient opportunity was given.
Therefore, there is no perversity in the findings arrived at by the authorities.
[Para 14]

According to Section 27 of the Army Act, a non-selected officer can petition for promotion.
In this case, the Selection Board found the appellant suitable for promotion, but the punishment order was not presented before the board.
The Special Review Board was called on intimation about the punishment, and it was found that the appellant was not suitable for promotion due to a drop in performance.
This cannot be considered unjustified or bad as the appellant's case was reviewed based on performance.
