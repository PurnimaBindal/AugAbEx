According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, a temporary employee who has completed a Diploma course in Civil Engineering while working as a temporary labourer and has been selected for the post of Junior Engineer but not promoted due to being a temporary employee, cannot be kept in suspended animation indefinitely if they have 9 years of continuous service.
The conduct of the respondent is deemed unfair, unjust, unreasonable, and a violation of Article 14.
Therefore, the petitioner must be promoted as per the given directions.
