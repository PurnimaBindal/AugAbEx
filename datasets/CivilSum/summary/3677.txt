According to Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221, if a temporary appointed Constable is discharged on grounds of being unlikely to become an efficient police officer, the order of discharge should not be considered stigmatic or arbitrary.
In a case where the respondent claimed they were a minor during the time of conviction under Punjab Excise Act 1914, the appellate court considered the age claimed in the application for appointment as incorrect.
As such, the appeal was allowed.
