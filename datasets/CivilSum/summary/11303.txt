Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admissions - Delay - Petitioner's admission to Computer Engineering Course in the Sant Longowal Institute - Petitioner was in the merit list of general and reserved category - High Court issued direction to grant 20% weightage to local candidates in a writ petition filed by them - Petitioner being a local candidate entitled to benefit from the judgment- Despite his representations, the petitioner was not given that benefit and matter delayed - Institute cannot take advantage of its own mistake and argue that the petitioner's plea is delayed and lacks credibility.
