Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Reservation - Upgradation of posts - A tribunal determined that promotions of SC/ST officers based on their own merit should be counted against general posts and ordered officials to reconsider the matter.
Respondents No. 5 and 6 argued that if there had been no promotion, then no question of reservation would arise.
The petitioner failed to demonstrate the reservation policy applicable in case of upgradation, so the tribunal's decision was not unreasonable.
