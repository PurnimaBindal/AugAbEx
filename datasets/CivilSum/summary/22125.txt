According to Section 11A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, if a roadways conductor is dismissed from service for holding cash in excess than permissible, the issue cannot be deemed as misconduct unless passengers are examined.
The Rules themselves label it as misconduct, and no further proof is necessary in the absence of any explanation for having excess amount.
The evidence of the Inspector is sufficient, and the court cannot replace their subjective opinion with that of the domestic tribunal.
This law was correctly followed in 1977(2) SCC 491 as well as 2001(1) SCT 787 (SC) and 2002(4) SCT 609 (SC).
[Paras 7 to 9]

The question of the quantum of punishment under Section 11A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, arises when a roadways conductor is dismissed from service for holding cash in excess than permissible.
The court cannot consider such punishment as disproportionate since it's a matter of not just misconduct but loss of confidence.
A person who misappropriates the funds of the employer is unfit to stay in service.
[Paras 11 and 12]
