Imperial Bank of India Pension and Guarantee Funds Rules, Rules 11 and Rule 10 - Pension - Forfeiture of pension - Disciplinary proceedings - Rule 11 only applies in case of premature retirement or when an employee leaves the Bank's service before superannuating - Rule 11 does not apply when the employee automatically superannuates at the age of superannuation - In such cases, no approval is required for granting pension - In the present case, the Bank cannot hold departmental proceedings against the petitioner as he has already retired - The petitioner is entitled to full pension with interest.
[Paras 15 and 16]

Imperial Bank of India Employees Provident Fund Rules, Rule 20 - Rule 20 applies only if an employee retiring from the Bank is under a liability incurred by him to the Bank - The Bank must establish the employee's liability.
[Para 17]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Imperial Bank of India Pension and Guarantee Fund Rules, Rules 10 and 11 - Pension - Interest on delayed pension - Damages - The retiree is entitled to interest on arrears of pension, but not to damages if the withholding of the pension is justifiable - In this case, the action of withholding the pension was not totally unjustified as it was based on different judgments on entitlement - Therefore, the retiree is not entitled to damages.
