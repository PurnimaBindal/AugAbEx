Constitution of India, Article 311 - Andhra Pradesh Civil Services Code (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1991, Rule 20(7) - Dismissal from service - Allegations of misappropriation of Temple money - Termination of petitioner's services without any enquiry - Rule 20(7) mandates conducting an enquiry - Absence of enquiry completely invalidates disciplinary action and punishment - Orders imposing penalty and impugned order are nullified.
