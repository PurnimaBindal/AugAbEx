Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 and 311 - Ad hoc appointment - Termination - Executive instruction - Regularisation of Services - Services are terminated upon the appointment recommendation of a candidate by SSSB - Petitioner given a fresh appointment as Salesman on the same terms and conditions as his original appointment or until a suitable candidate recommended by SSS Board is found - SSSB did not grant approval to ad hoc appointment, so his services were terminated - Instructions of Govt. only apply to ad hoc employees working against posts not under SSSB - Petitioner's post is not one such post - Petitioner is not entitled to have his services regularised in terms of instructions - Petition dismissed.
