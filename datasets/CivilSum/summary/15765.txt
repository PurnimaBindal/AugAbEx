Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Conditions of service - Age of superannuation - Amalgamation of Oil Corporation with Indian Railways - A resolution made during the amalgamation process decreed a change in the age of superannuation to 58 years - Employee cannot claim ignorance of such a resolution, which governs their service conditions - Filing a writ petition 30 years after the amalgamation to allow continued service up to 60 years of age cannot be entertained.
