Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admissions/Education - Merit - Admission to MBBS Degree Courses - Waiting list - Candidates have the option to give their preference for admission to various Post Graduate Courses if they are successful in the entrance test.
They will only be considered in the specialities of their preference.
Even for admission against left out seats from waiting list, they can be considered for admission according to their preference.
However, they cannot claim admission in a better speciality after being admitted to another of their preference, only on the ground that all candidates considered for admission to that speciality in the entrance test are equal.
Moreover, candidates cannot be considered in the waiting list of other specialities to which they had not opted.
The provision made in the prospectus will be binding as it decides the rights of the candidates.
This provision is not unreasonable considering the fact that the admissions are to be given finality within a time-bound process that has to be honoured.
[Paras 7 to 9]
