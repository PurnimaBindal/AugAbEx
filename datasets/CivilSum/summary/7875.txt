Constitution of India, Article 226 - Part time workers - Regularisation - Petitioners who have been working as part-timers since 1987 are seeking regularization.
According to the government's policy to regularize the services of Class IV employees, petitioners are relying on.
It has been held that Petitioners have continuously worked on their post for the last 15 years, indicating the availability of work.
Therefore, the government is not justified in denying them regularization.
