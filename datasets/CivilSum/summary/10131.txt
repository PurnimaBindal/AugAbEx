Constitution of India, Article 16 - Part time workers - Regularisation of service - A petitioner who had worked as a part time sweeper for 8 years is seeking regularisation of her services which were not yet confirmed.
Although the wages paid to the petitioner were less than the minimum wages set by the State Government, the authorities have been directed to create a policy for the regularisation of part-time employees and assess the petitioner's case.
Until then, the petitioner may receive at least the minimum pay scale of Class IV as defined by the State Government.
