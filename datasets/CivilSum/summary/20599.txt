Indian Police Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1955, Regulations 5(1), 5(3) - Promotion - Select list - Being included in a select list for one year does not automatically guarantee inclusion in the next list - The only exception is for those in the previous select list who are 54 years of age or older - A Full Bench Tribunal's direction to consider applicants from the 1985 select list for the 1993 select list cannot be sustained.
[Paras 7, 8 and 9]  B Indian Police Service (Regulation of Seniority) Rules, 1954, Rule 3(3) - Indian Police Service (Cadre) Rules, 1954, Rule 9 - Seniority - Continuous officiation in a senior IPS post is recognized for seniority, provided that the officer's name is on the select list during their officiation.
