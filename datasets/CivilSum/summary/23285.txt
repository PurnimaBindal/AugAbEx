Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 136 - Interim direction - Transferred employees raised dispute relating to their service conditions by way of writ petition before High Court regarding transfer of transmission lines and sub-stations of the various generating organizations to a Corporation - Interim direction given to grant equal pay for equal work to the transferred employees - However, the interim direction was not justified as the subject matter itself was still pending for final adjudication.
