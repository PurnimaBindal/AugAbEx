Constitution of India, Article 136 - Tamil Nadu Civil Services (Disciplinary Proceedings Tribunal) Rules, 1955, Rules 9 and 10 - Punishment - Compulsory retirement - Punishment of compulsory retirement has been passed against respondent-delinquent, a Block Development Officer, after corruption charges had been proved against them - Administrative Tribunal interfered with order of punishment, solely on the basis that the BDO only passed bills and corrected arithmetical errors, without considering the enquiry report - Administrative Tribunal's conclusion and final order, which set aside the punishment order, is wholly erroneous and cannot be sustained - As a result, the order of compulsory retirement against the respondent stands affirmed.
