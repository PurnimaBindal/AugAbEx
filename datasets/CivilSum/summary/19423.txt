The petitioner, who was discharged from military service on medical grounds and granted disability pension, was denied the benefit of a policy decision to extend disability pension to Army personnel below officer rank whose disability fell below 20 percent at a later stage.
The decision was made applicable only to those on the effective strength of the Army on 111973, and the petitioner was receiving disability pension on that date.
The court found that the fixation of the date had no bearing on the object of granting disability pension and ordered that the petitioner receive the same benefit as those who were discharged from service after 111973.
