As per Constitution of India, Article 226, when termination is found to be illegal and unjustified, the petitioner is entitled to reinstatement with full back wages.
In this case, the Labour Court exercised discretion to grant 75% of back wages instead of full back wages, which is proper based on the facts stated in the award.
Therefore, there is no need for interference with the discretion exercised by the Labour Court.
[Para 4]

In accordance with Constitution of India, Article 226, the petitioner, whose services were terminated illegally and unjustifiably, is entitled to be reinstated with all benefits.
The effect of the award is to consider him as in uninterrupted service.
Therefore, the petitioner should get consideration for promotion to the next higher post w.e.f.
the date his juniors were promoted according to the rules existing at that time.
