Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Reservation for Ex-Servicemen - 22 out of 98 ASI posts are reserved for ex-servicemen, but only 7 have been filled by them.
The petitioner's name was recommended by the Subordinate Service Selection Board for appointment under the ex-servicemen quota.
The Director General of Police does not have the authority to refuse appointment.
The court directed the appointment of the petitioner from the date any of his juniors below him in the merit list have been appointed.
