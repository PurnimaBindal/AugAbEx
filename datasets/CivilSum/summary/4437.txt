Police Act, 1861, Section 7 - Madhya Pradesh Govt. Servant (Temporary and Quasi Permanent Services) Rules, 1960, Rules 3 and 12 - Termination of services - A temporary Stenographer in Police Department can have their services terminated at any point by giving them a one-month notice period, as per the rules.
In this case, the petitioner's service was terminated after being given one month's notice from the date of issuance, which was held as valid.
