Constitution of India, Article 16 - Letters Patent Appeal, Clause X - Parity of pay - The respondents' legal advisers in Punjab Roadways were granted the relief of parity of pay with Legal Assistants in the Department of Law and Legislation on January 1, 1978, as per the Judgment in this appeal - The Single Judge concluded that the nature of work performed by Legal Assistants in both departments is nearly identical.
The Second Pay Commission recommended a uniform scale of pay for all Legal Assistants throughout the State, acknowledging the different types of tasks performed by Legal Assistants in both the Transport Dept.
and Department of Law and Legislation, as well as other industries where these posts exist and found them to be almost the same - It was upheld - The appeal was dismissed.

Point Parity in pay - The parity in pay scales of two associated employees cannot be revoked once granted, and their parity must not be disturbed.
Once it is established that there was equal pay scale parity among employees spanning several categories, that parity cannot be disturbed.
