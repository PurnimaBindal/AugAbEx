Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Misconduct - Wilful absence from duty - Petitioner, while serving in the Armed Police, failed to report to his new station continuously for 34 days without informing the authorities - The wilful absence from duty for 34 days was the gravest act of misconduct and justified the petitioner's dismissal from the disciplined force like the Haryana Armed Police.
