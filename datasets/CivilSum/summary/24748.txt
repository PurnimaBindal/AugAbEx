Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1970, Section 9 - Nationalised Banks (Management and Miscellaneous Provisions) Scheme, 1980, Clause 3(c) - Constitution of Board of Directors for Nationalised Banks - Change in the mode of appointment for non-workman Director from appointment out of panel submitted by Association of non-workman employees to appointment of any employee regardless of union affiliation through circular Dt. 23-8-82 - Held to be beyond the scope of the Act and Scheme.
