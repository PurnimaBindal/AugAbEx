However, the period of disqualification expired in December 1991 and despite the scheduled examination being held in January 1992, the petitioner was not allowed to participate.
The university cannot deny the petitioner's right to appear in any examination in January 1992, even if the original semester examination was scheduled for November/December 1991.
As such, there is no justifiable ground for continuing the disqualification even for an examination after the period of disqualification has expired.
Therefore, since the petitioner has the right to sit in the examination and has not been disqualified from appearing in any specified number/semesters of examination, the petition is allowed.
