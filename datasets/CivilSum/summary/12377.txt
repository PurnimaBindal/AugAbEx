Constitution of India, Article 14 - Date of birth - Correction of date of birth in the service record follows the rules and instructions that are in effect on the date of the application - If the application is made in accordance with the instructions that are currently in place, and a subsequent change in the instructions requires the applicant to submit a corrected school record along with the application, the application cannot be rejected - The pending applications cannot be subjected to the subsequent instructions.
