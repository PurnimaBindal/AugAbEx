Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 10 - Suspension - The petitioner, who held a civil post connected with defense, was suspended pending an inquiry into grave charges for which a criminal prosecution was launched.
The petitioner challenged the validity of the suspension order on the grounds that the 1965 Rules do not apply to civilian employees in defense services.
The petitioner cited the decision in Union of India v. KS Subramanian JT 1988(4) SC 681 to support the argument that the suspension was without jurisdiction.
A bench of three judges needs to consider the matter as it requires an authoritative pronouncement in view of the decision in KS Subramanian being made by the same.
