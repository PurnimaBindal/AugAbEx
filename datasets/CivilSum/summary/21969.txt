Constitution of India, Article 14 - Council of Higher Secondary Education Orissa, Regulation 117 - Hard Case Rules, Clause 8 - Education - The clause (8) does not impose any penalty but simply states that candidates whose papers have been cancelled for violating Examination Rules will not be eligible for the benefit of hard case.
It is not a case of double punishment, and it is related to the purpose of Regulation 117.
Clause (8) is reasonable and not ultra vires the Regulation.
