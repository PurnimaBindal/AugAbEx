The entitlement of family pension under the Railway Pension Rules, as per Constitution of India, Article 300-A, was the subject of a question of law referred by a Division Bench.
The status of the petitioner as an employee of Railways was still in question and needed to be determined.
The Tribunal had refused to consider the application for family pension, but this decision was set aside and the matter was remitted back for fresh disposal since factual aspects needed to be determined by the Tribunal.
This resulted in the successful outcome of the petition.
