Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - Appellants, members of police force - The appellants challenge their transfer orders, alleging them to be mala fide and a form of punishment.
However, the respondent argues that the transfers were made in public interest due to the appellants' distribution of pamphlets that ridiculed and criticized senior police officers.
The pamphlets were believed to create ill-feelings and lower morale among the police force, hence justifying the transfers.
Additionally, the formation of any association was prohibited among members of the Kerala Police.
The transfers were held to be justified and in the public interest.
