Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 11B - Back wages - Labour Tribunal's discretion - High Court's interference - Normally full back wages are granted for violation of provisions of Section 25-F, but Labour Court can use discretion to calculate appropriate back wages in each case - Labour Court is final decision maker - High Court cannot interfere unless Labour Court commits illegality or irregularity in reducing back wages - Mere claims of perversity without pointing out the irregularity are insufficient - Writ of certiorari cannot be issued on grounds of insufficient or inadequate evidence - Perversity of order warrants High Court intervention - Reasoning must be recorded for High Court to interfere with an award.
