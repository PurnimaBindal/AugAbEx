Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Section 15 - A workman was dismissed from service due to absenteeism, but was later reinstated by the Appellate Authority, with the period of absence being treated as leave of kind due - The workman filed an application under Section 15 of the Payment of Wages Act, claiming wages - The Payment of Wages Act does not allow the authority to question the legality of disciplinary orders unless they have been challenged in an appropriate forum.
