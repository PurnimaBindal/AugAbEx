The appointment from the waiting list must adhere to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 while subsequent vacancies must be selected within six months.
The validity of the merit list is to be six months according to bye-laws, and if any post comes into existence within this period, it must be filled out of the already prepared merit list without advertisement.
Thus, termination of services of the petitioner was held illegal and invalid for being validly appointed against such post within six months.
