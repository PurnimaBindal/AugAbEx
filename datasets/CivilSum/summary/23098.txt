Army Act, 1950, Section 192 - Regulations for the Army (1987), Para 108 - Promotion to the post of Lt. General - Although the petitioner was recommended for promotion twice by Special Selection Boards, promotion was withheld because the petitioner had the weakest profile based on comparative assessment - Central Government performed the evaluation in an impartial manner - The appointing authority is not obligated to follow the Selection Board's recommendation - There is no basis for interference.
