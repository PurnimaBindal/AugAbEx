Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934 as applicable to Haryana, Chapter XIII, Rules 15, 131, 134, 138, 145, 1415, 1417, 1447 - Central Police Act 1888, Sections 3, 22 - Transfer - Separate Cadre - Petitioners transferred from District Police to Haryana Armed Police which is separate cadre upto rank of Sub-Inspector - The transfer violates Rules/Policy which state that transfer to a separate cadre is not permissible - Therefore, the transfer order has been nullified.
