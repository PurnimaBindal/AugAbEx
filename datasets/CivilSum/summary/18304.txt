Under Punjab Civil Services Rules, Rule 5.35 (applicable in Haryana), District & Sessions Judges, Additional District and Senior Sub-Judges and CJMs posted in the State of Haryana are eligible for rent-free accommodation or additional 10% House Rent on their pay.
This benefit is also available under Haryana Govt. Orders dated 3181979.
As for the Joint Legal Remembrancer post, while posted at Chandigarh, they must receive the same benefits as other Superior Judicial Officers of the State even if they are not posted in the Courts in the field or paid a Special Pay of Rs. 500/-.
Making unreasonable classification within the same homogenous class of employees for the same benefits is in violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
