Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Certificate - Not equivalent to Matriculation - Charge of misconduct - Although the petitioner did not misrepresent, they only provided a Prathma Certificate - The petitioner may be removed for not fulfilling eligibility criteria, but not for misconduct - The reason for misconduct is perverse and untenable - Therefore, the termination cannot be justified.
[Paras 5, 6]
