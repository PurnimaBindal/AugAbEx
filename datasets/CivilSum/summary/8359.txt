School Service Commission Act, 1997, Section 9 - Rules for Management of Recognised Non-Government Institutions, 1969, Rule 28 - Appointment - The question of whether Section 9 of the School Service Commission Act applies arose when a vacancy for an English teacher became available.
The District Inspector of Schools directed that the position be filled based on the School Service Commission's recommendation on 27111997, but the Act only became effective on 17101997 and the selection procedure was not completed until 811998.
Therefore, the approval of the District Inspector of Schools was irrelevant as the Act had not yet come into effect on the date of the order.
