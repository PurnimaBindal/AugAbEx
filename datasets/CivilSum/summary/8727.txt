According to Articles 12 and 226 of the Constitution of India, the National Council of State Agricultural Marketing Board (COSAMB) is a non-governmental Society registered under the Societies Registration Act.
It operates voluntarily without any grant or aid from the Central or State Government, and there is no controlling power of the Government over its working.
The nominees of the Government do not take part in the election of the council.
The council is not deemed as a State or even an authority under the meaning of Article 12 and Article 226 of the Constitution, so it is not appropriate to file a writ petition against it, and even the winding-up of the Society does not require the Government's approval.
