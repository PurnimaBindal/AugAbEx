The Warehousing Corporation Act, 1962, Sections 18, 23 and 42, and the Punjab State Warehousing Corporation (Staff) Regulations, 1960, Regulation 14, along with the Agricultural Produce (Development and Warehouse) Corporation Act, 1956, Section 34, govern the grant of conveyance allowance and petrol expenses.
However, these benefits were withdrawn as they were deemed inadmissible under any rule or regulation.
The Corporation must adhere to the regulations and cannot issue directions to follow a regulation that was formulated in contravention of the regulations.
The benefits were withdrawn by the order of the Government, and the Corporation's withdrawal order is subject to regulation 14.
