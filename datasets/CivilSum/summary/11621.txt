Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Petitioner, who was appointed on a purely adhoc basis for a period of 89 days, continued to work for one and half years beyond her initial period.
After proceeding on leave for health check up and tests, the petitioner failed to indicate that she intended to avail of maternity leave.
Consequently, the respondents filled the vacancy created by her sudden failure to report for duty, and the petitioner approached the court too late, as a third party interest had already been created.
Thus, the court cannot go into the question of whether her ad hoc appointment should be granted permanency, and it would not be proper to oust another person who has been validly and properly employed.
Lastly, the petition is barred by laches, and the respondents have not acted unfairly or in a biased manner, or contrary to law or rules.
