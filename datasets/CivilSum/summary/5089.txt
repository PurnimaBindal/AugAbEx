Constitution of India, Article 16 - Resignation - For a resignation to be valid, it must be unconditional - A resignation letter that is not unconditional cannot be treated as a resignation - The appellant's resignation letter was not unconditional and was therefore not intended to be treated as a resignation - The petitioner has been ordered to be reinstated with 30% back wages.
