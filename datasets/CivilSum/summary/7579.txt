Jammu and Kashmir State Constitution, Section 5 and Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 162 - Appointment challenge - Respondents were appointed without following due process, which renders the appointment illegal and invalid.
No individual has the right to take public office through illegal appointments, unless selected in accordance with the law.
Hence, the Respondent's appointment is liable to be quashed.
[Paras 27, 28, 33]

Jammu and Kashmir State Constitution, Section 5 and Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 162 - Illegal appointees seeking regularization - Respondents' backdoor entry was illegal, hence they cannot seek regularization by relying on the Government Order.
The service of all such appointees appointed in violation of the Constitution shall be dispensed with.
[Paras 31, 33]
