The jurisdiction of the labour court to try references is not barred by Section 128 of the Haryana Cooperative Societies Act, 1984 when it comes to termination during probation that leads to the raising of an industrial dispute.
This is because the provisions of Chapter V-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 will apply regardless of any inconsistent provision contained in any other law currently in effect.
[Para 9]
Termination during probation of a workman who has worked continuously for 12 months and has not been provided with the necessary statutory notice or pay in lieu of it will render their orders of termination null and void if the provisions of section 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 have not been complied with.
