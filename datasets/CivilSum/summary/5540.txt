The termination of respondent No1 workman during probation was a result of unsatisfactory work and not misconduct, and it was found that the management followed the provisions of Section 25-F of the ID Act by offering retrenchment compensation.
Despite receiving a letter to collect full and final payment, the respondent No1 workman refused to do so.
As the respondent No1 workman had completed 240 days or more preceding the date of termination, the impugned award of the labour court declaring the termination illegal and void was set aside.
