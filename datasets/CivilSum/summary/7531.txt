Constitution of India, Articles 19(i)(g) and 226 - Dentist Act, 1948, Sections 3, 4, 7(a) to (g), 10A & 20 - Dental College Regulations, 1993, Regulations 5, 6 & 7 - When issuing essentiality certificate for new Dental College, jurisdiction of State Government is limited to the location's desirability and feasibility for establishing a college, and not beyond - Decision on staff and facility sufficiency is for Dental Council and Central Government to make - Refusal based on grounds such as already sufficient dentist population ratio or non-desirability of one more dental college in district is invalid - Order to issue Essentiality Certificate within 30 days given.
