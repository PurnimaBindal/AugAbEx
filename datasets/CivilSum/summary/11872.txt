Engineering course admission in Polytechnic Technical Institutions has a reservation of 2% of seats for children of serving staff of Technical Education Department according to a government letter from August 28, 1987.
Even though the appellant would not have been admitted based on merit, they were given admission based on the government letter.
The appellant has completed six semesters and has passed under an interim court order, but the result for the 7th semester examination is withheld.
Reversing the entire admission procedure would be unfair to the appellant.
The reservation made by the state may not comply with Article 14 of the Constitution regarding equality.
Therefore, the appellant is allowed to continue the course and the respondents are directed to declare their result for the 7th semester and permit them to take the 8th semester in accordance with the law.
This decision is in compliance with Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
