Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(b) - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.1 - Discharge from service - Dispensing with the departmental enquiry - The Superintendent of Police dismissed the petitioner from service without a departmental enquiry based on the subjective satisfaction that he had links with extremists and therefore, was unsuitable for service in the interest of discipline and security without an enquiry.
The order was challenged and the authority failed to provide sufficient material to justify the action and objective satisfaction was not recorded.
The order was quashed.
