Constitution of India, Article 226 - Interpretation of order - Division Bench of court directs to count service rendered before 1/3/1991 only for the purpose of seniority and pension - Petitioners inquire about counting their service anterior to 1/3/1991 for fixation of their wages - Ruling clarifies that the directive intends to disregard service rendered before 1/3/1991 completely for any purpose other than seniority and pension.
