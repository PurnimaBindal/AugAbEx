Constitution of India, Article 311 - Natural justice - Admission of guilt - Misappropriation charges - Enquiry - Delinquent faced charges of misappropriation amounting to Rs. 21,000/- for a period of two months - Confessed to the charges in writing - The enquiry officer rightly closed the evidence and rejected his request to contest as an afterthought - Dismissal from service was a reasonable punishment for the offense committed - The order of the High Court quashing the dismissal cannot be sustained.
