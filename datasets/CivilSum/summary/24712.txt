Appellant who held a fresh appointment as an Excise Sub-Inspector was compulsorily retired from service by the Excise Commissioner in 1975 under Rule 3(1)(a) and (b) of P.C.S.
(Premature Retirement) Rules.
The appellant challenged the validity of the order before the High Court, stating that the Excise Commissioner was not competent to make the order since he belonged to the Police Department.
However, the High Court dismissed the writ petition and held that the appellant had not gone to the Excise Department on deputation from the Police Department.
[Para 1]
The rule regarding deputation and lien is for the benefit of a Government servant who intends to return back to his parent department.
[Para 5]
Lien refers to the right of a civil servant to hold the post substantively to which he is appointed according to Articles 16 and 311 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 6]
As an Excise Inspector in the Excise Department, the Excise Commissioner was competent enough to pass an order of premature retirement under Rule 314 of Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume 1, Part 1.
