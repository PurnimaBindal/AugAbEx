Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Charge-sheet - Quashing of charge-sheet - It is not feasible to quash a charge-sheet at the judicial review stage on the basis of correctness or vagueness, which would require evidence - The writ court cannot replace the departmental inquiry mechanism - The employee will have ample opportunity to challenge the accuracy of the charge during the inquiry.
[Paras 7 and 8]
