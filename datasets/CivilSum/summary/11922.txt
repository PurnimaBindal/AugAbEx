Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection/Appointment/Review of Selections/Natural Justice - The selection of a candidate leads to a civil right to be appointed - Failure to offer appointment makes the constitutional guarantee under Article 16 meaningless - Reviews of candidate selection must not be conducted without giving the selected candidate an opportunity to respond - If a candidate awaiting appointment is cancelled and becomes overage, it disqualifies them from the job.
