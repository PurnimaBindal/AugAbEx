Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 137 and 131.
(Proviso) - Reservation for Sportsmen - List-B - 5% of seats are reserved for sportsmen achieving outstanding distinction in the field of sports after joining Police services.
To be considered, sportsmen must achieve outstanding distinction at the All India or International level and fulfill other conditions such as efficiency, seniority, honesty, and merit in B-1 test.
The petitioner was not found suitable or eligible as they had no achievement at the National or International level.
Therefore, their petition was dismissed, and there is no ground to interfere.
