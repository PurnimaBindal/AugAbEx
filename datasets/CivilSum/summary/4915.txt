Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions & Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Section 28, Sch.
IV, Items 5, 6 and 9 - Unfair Labour Practice - The company and the union agreed to absorb 28 temporary workmen into permanent positions in phases - Item 6 of Schedule IV cannot be applied unless there is clear evidence of unfairness - The fact that the workmen were in temporary service for a long time is not enough to prove that the company engaged in unfair labour practices.
