Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Haryana Industries (Group B) Service Rules, 1993, Rule 9 - Pay scale - Promotion - Fixation of pay in the higher/promotion cadre - The rule that provides a time scale of Industrial Promotion Officer Rs. 1640-2900 while the feeder post of Block Level Extension Officer/Inspector of Industries is Rs. 1640-2900 after 10 years service and Rs. 2000-3200 after 20 years service creates an anomaly in the pay scales.
The pay scale of the promotion post cannot be lower than that of the feeder post in any case.
Although the court cannot mandate the formulation of rules in a specific manner, it may strike down State action that violates the law.
The rule creating an anomalous situation violates the equality clause by discriminating against those holding the higher post.
The state is directed to refix the pay scale of the post of Industrial Promotion Officer, which must not be lower than the pay scale of the feeder post in any event.
