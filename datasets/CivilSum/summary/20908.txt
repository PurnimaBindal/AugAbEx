Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Absorption/Deputation - Sub-Inspectors of Food Department of the Govt. of West Bengal employed on deputation in Food Corporation of India where they served as Assistant Grade II for over 18 years - Absorption in the Corporation - They must be absorbed as Assistant Grade II and it is not permissible to deny them the pay scale of that position.
[Paras 3 and 4]
