Response : "Mr. Gopal Mahajan, Advocate, appointed as Lecturer in Leather Chemistry and subsequently as Lecturer in Practical Training challenged on the grounds of lack of requisite qualifications.
The petition was dismissed as it was too late to question the granting of his diploma 12 years after the fact and to deny him the benefit of it.
Though he had not completed apprenticeship, he was granted the certificate for having done the diploma in Leather Technology while already in service, and there is no requirement for apprenticeship for those already experienced in the job.
