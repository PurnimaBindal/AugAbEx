According to the Constitution of India, including Articles 14, 16, and 39(d), all individuals are entitled to "equal pay for equal work."
Despite being appointed as a Tube Well operator, the petitioner was not given the appropriate pay scale and was instead given only the pay scale of an Assistant Pump Operator.
A legal suit and appeal were dismissed, but this should have no effect on the petitioner's claim to their constitutional right.
Additionally, since the Municipal Committee has already written to the Government to release the appropriate pay scale, the petitioner should be granted the pay scale of pump operator.
