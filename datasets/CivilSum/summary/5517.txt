Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Termination of services - Natural Justice - Petitioner, who was appointed as field officer by Board of Directors of the Federation, had his services terminated due to some irregularities committed by him.
A reference was made to the Labour Court, which held the termination legal and valid, despite no enquiry officer being appointed nor any enquiry being conducted under Industrial Disputes Act.
This constituted a violation of principles of natural justice, as both parties should be given a proper opportunity to lead evidence.
The matter was remanded back to Labour Court for further proceedings.
