Constitution of India, Article 226 - Gujarat Municipalities Act, 1963, Section 47 - Appointment - Post of Chief Officer - The petitioner held the post of Chief Officer, and despite the elected body's tenure still being in effect, a resolution extended the petitioner's services for a specific period.
However, the Municipal Administrator and Collector issued show-cause notices as to why the extension order should not be withdrawn or revoked.
Meanwhile, the government appoints Respondent No. 3 to also take on the post of Chief Officer in addition to his existing duties as Mamlatdar.
There is no illegality and no grounds to interfere with the said order.
