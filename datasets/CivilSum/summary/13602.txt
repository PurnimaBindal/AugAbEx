Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, Rule 7 - Punishment/Natural Justice - Orders of punishment that take into account materials prejudicially affecting the employee without affording any opportunity to explain his position are invalid and violative of natural justice principles.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, Rule 19 - Representation - Material considered when deciding representation against adverse remarks - Material not before the initiating or reviewing authority while writing the ACR cannot be considered in deciding representation against adverse remarks.
[Para 8] C Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Section 3 - Constitution of India, Article 14 - Premature retirement - Material considered - Competent authorities must take into consideration the entire record of service before ordering premature retirement, and cannot base their decision on one adverse entry alone.
If an employee has earned 70% good reports during the last 10 years, any notice of premature retirement will be bad and liable to be quashed.
