Section 70 of the Karnataka Cooperative Societies Act, 1959 (as amended in 1997) - Amendment in Act - Competence of State legislature - Jurisdiction of Labour Court or Industrial Tribunal - State legislature is competent to make amendment in view of relevant entry in Constitution.

Section 70 of the Karnataka Cooperative Societies Act, 1959 (as amended in 1997) - Amendment repugnant to some provisions of Central Act - Assent given by President - Amendment saved by the assent of President.
[Para 17]
Section 70 of the Karnataka Cooperative Societies Act, 1959 (as amended in 1997) - Amendment ousting jurisdiction of Labour Court or Industrial Tribunal - Validity of amendment - Covers only disputes raised and resolved by authority appointed under Act - Industrial Disputes Act applicable for all other matters, thus amendment is valid.
