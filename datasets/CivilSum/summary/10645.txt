Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Rules for the Management of (Aided and Unaided) Schools, 1969, Rule 28 - West Bengal Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Reservation of Vacancies in Services and Posts) Act, 1976, Section 4 - West Bengal Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Reservation of Vacancies and Posts) Amendment Act, 1994, Section 6 - Recruitment - De-reservation of Vacancies - The post of Commerce Teacher in Urdu - The Management sought approval from the District Inspector of Schools for a deserving ST candidate to fill the vacancy, as no suitable candidate was available - An advertisement for the post was issued in 1997 from the general category - The court held that since no order for de-reservation was made by the State Government, recruitment could not be made from the general category - De-reservation of the vacancy can only be done by the State Government.
