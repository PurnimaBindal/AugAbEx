Constitution of India, Article 311 - Police officers of subordinate Ranks (Punishment and Appeal) Rule, 1991, Rule 17(1)(a) - Suspension pending enquiry - The word "enquiry" in Rule 17(1)(a) cannot be interpreted to mean only a full-fledged departmental enquiry; even a preliminary enquiry falls under its scope.
The phrase "an enquiry is contemplated" refers to an earlier stage when a preliminary enquiry is being considered.
The Division Bench judgment in Kripa Shanker Prasad's case does not correctly establish the law.
[Paras 16, 21 and 22]
