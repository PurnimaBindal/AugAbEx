Constitution of India, Article 311 - MP Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1966, Rule 9(2b) - Suspension - Charge-sheet - Delay in completion of departmental proceedings - Employer must issue charge-sheet within 45 days and approval of Government to be within 90 days - Forfeiture of full pay for unjustified suspension period cannot be justified - Employer cannot blame the employee for prolonged enquiry without justification.
[Paras 6 & 7]
