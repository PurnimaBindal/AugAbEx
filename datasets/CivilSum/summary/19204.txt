Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Regularisation of service - Despite being appointed as a driver against a vacant post and fulfilling necessary criteria, petitioner's regularisation was denied due to the lack of minimum qualifications - After originally being regularised as a Cleaner, was later promoted as a Driver - Petition allowed and Respondents ordered to regularise petitioner's service as a Driver from 1.4.93 with all accompanying benefits.
