According to Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act, the title of "Workman" is based on the nature of an employee's work and duties, rather than any incidental tasks.
If an employee's main duties are manual, clerical, or technical in nature, then they fall under the definition of a "Workman" under Section 2(s) of the Act.
In the case of Co-operative Agricultural Service Societies, salesmen are not only responsible for manual work but also for clerical work, making them Workman as defined in section 2(s) of the Act.
