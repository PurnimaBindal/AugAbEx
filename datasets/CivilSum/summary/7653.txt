Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural justice - Departmental inquiry - Documents - The order of punishment cannot be questioned for non-supply of documents if full opportunity was provided to the delinquent to cross examine the witnesses and he did not raise any issue about not receiving any documents in his reply to show cause.
