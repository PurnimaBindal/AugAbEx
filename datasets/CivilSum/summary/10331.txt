Army Act, 1950, Section 191 - Discharge from service - Validity of Order - The petitioner was recruited under the incorrect assumption that he was a matriculate and was given a post in a lower category.
When this fact was discovered and he was offered the appropriate position, he refused.
After issuing a Show Cause Notice, the discharge order was passed and it was deemed proper.
