In accordance with the Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961, Sections 54 and 55, a dispute has arisen regarding the recovery made during an audit inspection.
It is noted that Section 54 is applicable in this case, rather than Section 55, as Section 54 pertains to specific and particular cases while Section 55 is more general in nature.
The rule of construction dictates that the exception must supersede the general rule, otherwise the exception would become obsolete.
