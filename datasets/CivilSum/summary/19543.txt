Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work - Nursery Teachers in Chandigarh Administration were treated equally to JBT Teachers, and the Second Punjab Pay Commission recommended equal pay scales for them, which Chandigarh Administration adopted.
There was no justification to withhold the same pay scale from Nursery Teachers.
The Order of the Single Judge was overturned, and the appeal was allowed.
