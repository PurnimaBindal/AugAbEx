Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rules 27 and 511 - Discharge from service on the ground of permanent disability - Employees who are permanently disabled while in service and rendered unfit for further service are eligible for ex-gratia payment of Rs. 50,000/- - If an employee is discharged without being considered for an alternative post and granted disability pension, it will be considered that he is disabled for any other post as well - The employee must be given the benefit of ex-gratia grant under the policy which is binding on the state authorities - Grant of invalid pension is an acceptance that the employee was rendered unfit for any public service in that branch - Both, ex-gratia grant and invalid pension can be granted at the same time.
