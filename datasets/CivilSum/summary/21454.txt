Article 21 of the Constitution of India regulates Compassionate Appointment of family members of deceased government employees.
In cases where a husband was entitled to appointment due to the acquisition of his land but has passed, his widow may claim the appointment.
It cannot be argued that she is not a family member.
However, once the appointment is given to the widow, no other appointment on compassionate grounds can be claimed.
[Paras 4 and 5]
