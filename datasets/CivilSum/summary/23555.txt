Army Act, 1950, Section 123 - An Army Officer retired from service and kept in custody for enquiry and trial into the charges against him - Section 123(2) prescribes six month period for commencement of trial - The accused cannot plead limitation, even if he escaped from military custody - Trial not vitiated.
[Paras 28 and 29]

Trial - Meaning of - Trial means act of proving or judicial examination or determination of issues including its own jurisdiction in accordance with the law or adjudging guilt or innocence of the accused including all steps necessary thereto - Trial commences with the performance of the first act or step necessary or essential to proceed with trial.
[Para 19]

Army Act, 1950, Section 123 - Trial by General Court Martial - When does the trial commence within the meaning of Section 123(2) of the Army Act - The trial commences as soon as the General Court Martial assembles to consider the charge and examines whether they would proceed with the trial - The preceding preliminary investigation only serves to find out whether the charge could be framed and placed before the competent authority to constitute General Court Martial.
