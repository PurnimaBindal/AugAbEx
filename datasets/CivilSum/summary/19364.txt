Himachal Pradesh University Act, 1970 - Degrees and diplomas - Recognition of degrees - Himachal Pradesh University is established under the provision of the University Grants Commission.
It is a statutory university that includes the Directorate of its correspondence courses.
The university recognizes all examinations, including those for correspondence courses.
Therefore, there is no reason why the M.A.
Degree of its correspondence courses should not be recognized for all purposes.
The MA Degree from Himachal Pradesh University is just as valid and potent as any other recognized university in India.
