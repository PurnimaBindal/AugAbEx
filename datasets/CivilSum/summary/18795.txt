Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I, Part I, Rule 2.52 - Third Punjab Pay Commission recommendations on `special pay' - Special pay - Entitlement of pre-retirees - The special pay being doubled from 111986 will also increase the pension of retirees who were drawing the same special pay at the time of retirement - The recommendations must be interpreted to benefit pre-retirees before 111986, and the only condition is that the benefit will come into effect from 111986 - Pre-retirees as of 111986 are also eligible for the benefit.
