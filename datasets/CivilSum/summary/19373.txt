Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation of services - Discretion of competent authority to condone break in service limited to one month - Break of more than one month cannot be condoned - However, authority must exercise discretion in favor of employee - Refusal to condone break must be in rare cases - Arbitrary exercise of discretion to regularize or not to regularize services by condoning one month's break deemed unconstitutional - Petitioner to be considered for regularisation by condoning break of one month, but not entitled due to break of three months.
