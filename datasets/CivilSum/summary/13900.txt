As per the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16 and 226, promotion on a temporary basis required certain qualifications, including knowledge of Hindi typing at a rate of 25 w.p.m.
and at least five years of service.
If an employee did not fulfill these qualifications, their promotion is void and they can be reverted back without the need for a hearing.
In this case, an employee admitted to taking the typing test in English instead of Hindi.
