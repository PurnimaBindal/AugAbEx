The Punjab Agricultural University Act, 1961, Section 2, 29 and 33 holds that the Vice Chancellor is the competent and appointing authority for teachers under Statute 3 made under the 1961 Act.
The petitioner's appointment was made by the Vice Chancellor and the removal order was also passed by the Vice Chancellor, showing there was no lapse in jurisdiction of the competent authority.
[Para 17]
The Vice Chancellor participated in the proceedings of the Board, which passed the removal order of the appellant, violating the bias principle.
The case was remanded to the Board for a fresh decision on the merits, without the involvement of the Vice Chancellor in the proceedings.
