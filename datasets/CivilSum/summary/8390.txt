Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised Pay Scales) Rules, 1987, Rule 14(1)(i) - Advance Increment - Validity of Cut Off Date - Petitioners were considered to have completed ten years of service by reckoning the period of training in service and were given benefit of advance increment.
Later the Board issued a direction refusing to count training period during computing the period of ten years of service for grant of advance increment.
This resulted in pay scale reduction of some petitioners.
Petitioners are now challenging the cut-off date.
However, the advance increment creates a significant financial burden not only on the Board but also on the State.
Thus, fixing of cut-off-date by the State and adopted by the Board is not violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution, and its validity cannot be challenged after more than 12 years.
