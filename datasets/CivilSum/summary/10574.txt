Private Schools/Taking over private schools - Employees of private schools/Mode of appointment - When the management of a privately managed school represented the State government for taking over the school, the government has the only authority to look into the qualification and suitability of the staff members on the date of taking over.
However, the government cannot investigate whether the employees were regularly appointed or if their mode of appointments was wrong.
Employees terminated from service by the management only have the option to file a civil suit and cannot claim reinstatement by the Government through a writ petition.
