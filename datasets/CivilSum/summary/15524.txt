Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - Seniority - Promotion - The services rendered by Assistant teachers in Zilla Parishad School before their absorption in municipal school cannot be counted for the purpose of fixing seniority among teachers.
According to the Government resolution, previous services of those employees in Zilla Parishad who have joined services of Municipal Council willingly and voluntarily with prior consent cannot be counted.
However, the petitioner was transferred to Municipal Council on request made by President of Municipal Council, which was not voluntary, thus not covered by the Govt. resolution.
The service of petitioner in Zilla Parishad should be counted for the purpose of fixing his seniority.
Therefore, respondents have been directed to consider his case for promotion.
