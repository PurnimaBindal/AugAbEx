The Respondent, Jaswant Singh, Advocate, argued that there was discrimination in pay between the petitioners, Store keepers in P.W.D.
(B&R) Branch of State of Haryana, and store-keepers working in other departments of the government, violating Article 14 of the Constitution.
The government's decision to place store-keepers in a lower scale of pay, without proper analysis, suffered from the vice of discrimination.
The claim for parity of treatment in scales of pay was only made after the issue of the April 29, 1987 notification, which revised pay scales w.e.f.
January 1, 1986.
The Court directed that petitioners be placed in the pay scale granted to Store-keepers in the Department of Industrial Training and Vocational Education w.e.f.
111986.
