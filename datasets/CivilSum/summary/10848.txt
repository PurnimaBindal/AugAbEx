Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - J & K Daily Rated Workers/Work Charge Employees (Regularisation) Rules, 1994, Rules 4, 5, 7, 9 and 10 - Regularisation - Those appointed by incompetent subordinate officers are not eligible for regularisation in Govt. service according to the Rules - This is to prevent illegal entry into govt. jobs - However, those in service prior to the 1994 Rules and meet eligibility requirements are entitled to continue and be regularised.
