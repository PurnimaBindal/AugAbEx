The Selection of Coaches for different sports must not be based solely on interviews, but also on their performance and Personal Achievements.
This is in accordance with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 16]
When selecting a coach for a single vacancy in each category, it would amount to 100% reservation if reserved against a single post in each category.
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 call for a rotational method of reservation in such cases.
