Examination Regulations, Regulations 8(i) and 52(ii) - Admission - Incorrect admission in higher class - Petitioner received admission in Matric without adhering to Regulations specifying exercise of studies of two years gap between middle examination and matriculation examination, and was subsequently declared pass and given admission in +1 - However, her certificate was withheld - It was found that even though the admission had been given erroneously, the authority could not cancel the admission after such a long time frame due to the fault of administration in accepting her admission forms without following the Rules - The Respondents were ordered to provide her with certificate.
