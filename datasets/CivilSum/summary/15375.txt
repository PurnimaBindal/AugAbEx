As per Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India and Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Para I, Rule 4.22, Clause (i), the petitioner who performed the duties of Assistant Examiner, Local Fund Accounts Haryana, is entitled to receive higher pay of Examiner, Local Fund Accounts, Haryana for the period from 241992 to 1721993.
This decision has been followed in "Balbir Singh & others v. State of Haryana & others, C.W.P.
No. 10152 of 2000 decided on 952002".
