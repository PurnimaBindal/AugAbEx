The petitioner, an office bearer of an employees association, was transferred from place B and then transferred again to place N within the span of two months which violates policy guidelines stating that office bearers can only be transferred within the same city.
The respondents were found to have acted against policy and the transfer order was deemed not bona fide and therefore cannot be sustained.
This is based on the Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226, Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1970, Section 19, Central Bank of India (Officers) Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 4, and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(P) and 18(1).
