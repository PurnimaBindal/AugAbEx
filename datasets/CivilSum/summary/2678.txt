The Rajasthan State Electricity Board's 1988 Employees Service Regulations, 1964, Regulation 18(3) address the application for voluntary retirement.
The acceptance of such an application can be revoked if the employee did not complete the requisite service of 15 years.
However, in this case, the order of revocation was not served upon the petitioner, and according to the judgment of the Apex Court in Amar Singh Harika's case, an unserved order cannot be taken into effect.
Additionally, the petitioner was not asked to join their duties.
As a result, the respondents must take direction to treat the effective date of voluntary retirement as the date the petitioner completed 15 years of qualifying service, and treat the employee as notionally in service from the date they were voluntarily retired.
