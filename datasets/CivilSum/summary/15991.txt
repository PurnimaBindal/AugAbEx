Under UCO Bank Officers (Employees Discipline and Regulation) Appeal Rules, 1976, officiating persons acting as competent authority have all the powers of regular appointees - in this case, the Assistant General Manager who issued the charge sheet is considered the competent authority.
The charge sheet is valid even though it was issued by an officiating Assistant General Manager.
The higher authority would only step in if there is no officiating arrangement.
[Paras 4, 5, and 8]   B.
In cases of delayed disciplinary proceedings, the charges must be so old that the delinquent and the witnesses cannot recapitulate the instance for it to be considered fatal.
In this instance, charges against a Bank Manager for giving unsecured advances in 1989-90 and a charge sheet issued in 1993 cannot be deemed belated.
