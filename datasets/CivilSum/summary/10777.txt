Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Deputation - The stipulated period of deputation for a deputationist does not grant them the enforceable right to continue in the borrowing department - All parties must agree for the tripartite contract to continue, and any of them can end the contract at any time, even after proposals and options for absorption have been made.
[Paras 12 to 14]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Deputation - Writ court will only issue a writ if there is a violation of a statutory or fundamental right - Deputation of Govt. institution employees onto a private institution without any aid from the Govt. does not serve any public interest - A writ court will not provide aid in such situations, especially when there is no public interest involved.
