Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume- 1, Part-1, Rules 416 and 4.22(1) - If a government employee is appointed to hold higher duties in the same office and cadre, in addition to their regular duties, they will receive pay admissible to officiate in the higher post, unless their officiating pay is reduced under Rule 416.
Moreover, if a person temporarily works in an officiating capacity for the higher post, they are entitled to the salary for that period.
