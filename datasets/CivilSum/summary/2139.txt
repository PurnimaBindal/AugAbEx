The appellant was appointed as Tamil Pandit Grade I under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
However, she was later reverted to Tamil Pandit Grade II because of lacking qualifications, despite having never suppressed any relevant information.
During this period, the appellant worked in Grade I The respondent cannot effect any recovery after taking duties on Grade I post from the appellant, and the reversion order passed coupled with order of recovery of excess payment is not valid.
