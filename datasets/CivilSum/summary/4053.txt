Kerala Service Rules, Rules 88, 102 of Part I - Maternity Leave - Extension of - Respondent seeks extension of maternity leave to care for disabled infant with medical certificate - Government conditions leave under Rule 102 that it will not count towards service benefits including pension - Respondent originally granted leave without allowance in continuation of maternity leave by appellant under Rule 102 - Appellant cannot later deny Rule 102 is applicable and impose Rule 88 - Denial of service benefits including pension is deemed illegal.
