Constitution of India, Article 14 - Equal pay for equal work - Protection of pay - Revision of pay scales - To prevent employee disadvantage, the doctrine of protection of pay must be applied to avoid pay reduction or protect service conditions of existing employees in changing circumstances - When lower pay scales are revised, higher pay and benefits can be granted without discriminating against earlier or subsequent appointees - Better benefits may always be granted.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 - Age of retirement - Protection of conditions of service - If the age of retirement is changed, existing employees entitled to retire at a later age may not be deprived of full benefits within the former retirement window - However, if an employee is retained past the superannuation age, a distinction may be made regarding regular pay scale and benefits.
