Constitution of India, Article 39(d) - Petitioners invoked the principle of equal pay for equal work, which has been found not to be abstract - While the State may set different pay scales based on varying duties, responsibilities, and qualifications, if all aspects are equal, the doctrine of equal pay for equal work applies - If the Court finds that the different pay scales are not reasonably related to the job requirements, it may issue appropriate orders.

Constitution of India, Article 39(d) - Though not explicitly declared a fundamental right in the Constitution, equal pay for equal work is a constitutional goal.

Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Ad hoc or daily wage employees are entitled to equal pay for equal work, irrespective of the lack of service rules or disciplinary control - Petitioners who have worked for periods ranging from 10 to two years should be compensated on par with regular employees holding similar positions, effective from the date of their placement on said positions - Respondents are directed to pay petitioners the same salary and allowances as regular employees.
