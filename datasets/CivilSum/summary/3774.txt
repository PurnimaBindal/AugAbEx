Rajasthan Subordinate Offices Ministerial Staff Rules, 1957, Rule 10 (as amended vide notification dated 12.10.92) - Regularisation - Post of L.D.C.
- Writ petitioners appointed on daily wage basis after being sponsored by Employment Officer and holding proper interview are entitled to the same wages and benefits as regularly appointed employees.
Rule 10 as amended provides that LDCs appointed on ad hoc or daily wages basis during the period from 1185 to 31390, who are still working as such on the date of amendment, shall be appointed on regular basis subject to passing a performance test.
The writ petitioners were appointed during this period and were continuously working until the date of notification, and there are 15 vacancies currently available, therefore they are entitled to be granted the status of a regular employee from the time the vacancies became available.
[Paras 21, 22, 23 & 25]
