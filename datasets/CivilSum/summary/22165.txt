Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Reinstatement - The workman admitted to being appointed on 111996 and receiving wages from 141996 to 30.4.1997 after signing muster-rolls.
However, in the claim petition, he stated that he worked from 3131993 to 30.4.1997.
Muster-rolls proved that he did not work during the period he claimed to have worked.
The orders of the Labour Court and the High Court have been overturned.
