Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3(2) - Voluntary retirement - Rule 3(2) does not require the employee to deposit their pay and allowance for 3 months in exchange for notice for their voluntary retirement.
Instead, a written notice of three months is necessary for employees who want to retire from service after fulfilling their 25 years of qualifying service or reaching the age of 50.
