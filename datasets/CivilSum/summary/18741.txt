Constitution of India, Article 226 - Central Secretariat Clerical Service Rules, Rule 12(1)(b) - Regularization - Petitioners working on LDC/Stenographer post for 10 years without substantive appointment - Respondents failed to fill posts substantively - Petitioners not given opportunity to take SCC exam - Respondents' actions foreclosed all avenues of employment for petitioners - It would be unjust to oust petitioners summarily for not taking SSC exam - Anomalous position could be remedied by either considering petitioners for regularization of service as a one-time exception or by relaxing age bar for taking SSC exam.
