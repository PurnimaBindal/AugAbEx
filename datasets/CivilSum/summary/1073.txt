The liberalised pension scheme of 1986 cannot be applied retrospectively due to financial constraints and a cut-off date was established by the Commission.
Pension benefits are only available to those who retired prior to the cut-off date.
[Paras 16 & 17]
A petitioner who retired in 1978 cannot benefit from the liberalised pension scheme of 1986 and challenge the cut-off date in 2003.
The petitioner cannot claim ignorance while simultaneously receiving other benefits under the same report.
[Para 18]
