According to Regulation 16(b) of the Pension Regulations for the Army, an Army Officer who voluntarily resigns from service is entitled to pensionary benefits.
Even officers who are cashiered, dismissed or removed from service are not subjected to automatic forfeiture of pension or gratuity under the regulation.
However, officers with qualifying service for pension are not eligible for these benefits unless a specific order is passed by the competent authority to forfeit the same.
Additionally, officers who resign cannot be automatically deprived of their terminal benefits.
The 1981 letter has no effect on this ruling.
The appeal has been dismissed.
