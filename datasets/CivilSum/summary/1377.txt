When it comes to admission and education, the High Court cannot use their jurisdiction to relax or rewrite rules.
Courts cannot replace the admission scheme established by statutory authorities or direct institutions to admit a particular candidate contrary to established rules.
If there are no violations of jurisdiction or statutes, regulations, and rules, the court will not interfere unless there is evidence of a failure to perform a statutory duty or an arbitrary or mala fide act.
The petitioner did not establish that the respondents' act of not admitting them to MS (Ortho.)
discipline was beyond jurisdiction or contrary to statutes, regulations, or unreasonable and therefore the petition was dismissed.
