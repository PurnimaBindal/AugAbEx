According to Section 73 of the Air Force Act, dismissal from service may be a lesser punishment compared to imprisonment for 14 years or detention for 2 years, but one loses their job.
However, in the case of reduction in rank and detention for 3 months, it is not a lesser punishment.
[Para 7]
Under Section 157 of the Air Force Act, the authority cannot award a higher punishment than that awarded by the General Court Martial Dismissal order quashed.
As a result, the petitioner is entitled to all benefits and deemed to be in service.
Moreover, the authority may re-examine the case.
[Paras 8 and 9]
