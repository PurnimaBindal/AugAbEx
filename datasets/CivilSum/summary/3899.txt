According to Section 17-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, if a workman is unemployed due to illegal termination, it is not mandatory for him to seek employment outside his village in an urban area.
This contention is not acceptable.
If the workman did not get any employment in a metropolitan area, he had to return to his village.
The workman is entitled to full wages that he was receiving along with any maintenance allowance that he was eligible for under any rule.
The wages last drawn should not be less than the minimum wages that are payable.
[Paras 5 and 6]
