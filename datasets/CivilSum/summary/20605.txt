Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 - Regularisation - Re-judicata - The Central Administrative Tribunal ruled that 172 workers of Railway Employees Co-operative Stores should be considered regular railway employees - The Supreme Court dismissed the civil appeal and review petition against the judgement of CAT - The judgement of CAT must be implemented by conferring status of regular employees with all monetary benefits - Parties cannot escape liability by taking a plea that in similar matter same forum has taken a contrary view.
