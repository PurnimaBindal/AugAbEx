The petitioner, a daily wager who was appointed to a permanent position that later became part-time, has worked for over a decade and seeks regularisation and absorption as a peon with the corresponding pay.
A civil suit for declaration of regular status has already been filed by the petitioner.
Since the reliefs sought are substantially the same, the filing of a writ petition is not allowed.
[Para 12] B.
The petitioner was hired as a special favour on humanitarian grounds as a daily wager and continues to be employed as such despite a rejected prayer for compassionate appointment and an unsuccessful civil suit.
An interim relief against termination of employment was granted in the writ petition but later vacated.
The back door entry is in violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
Thus, the petition is dismissed.
