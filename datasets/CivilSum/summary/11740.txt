Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Appointment - Direct appointment ban - Petitioner selected and placed in the select list for an anticipated vacancy within a year of selection - Ban on direct appointment imposed afterwards will not affect the select list or prevent petitioner's appointment.
[Para 8] B Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Estoppel - Reservation - Petitioner chosen for expected general vacancy - Authority cannot use reservation argument to deny petitioner appointment - Authority is bound by estoppel.
