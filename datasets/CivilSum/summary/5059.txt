The Examination Rules of B.Tech.
as amended on May 30th, 2000, in conjunction with the Punjab Technical University Act of 1996, specifically Sections 14(8), 18(3) and 19, are applicable to students of the AIET from the 1999-2000 academic year.
However, any decision made by the Academic Council without Board of Governors approval or notification under the aforementioned Act or any decision made by the Vice Chancellor, unless deemed an emergency that warranted immediate implementation, is not enforceable.
Furthermore, on February 14th, 2004, the Board of Governors approved the amendment without retroactive effect.
The Academic Council's decision to mandate that students earn a minimum of 24 credits from the first-year scheme of courses cannot be applied to the 1999-2000 batch.
