Constitution of India, Articles 311(2)(a) and 226 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rules 5 and 13 - Central Civil Services (CCA) Rules, 1965, Rule 19 - Double jeopardy - Conviction - Dismissal from service - Two increments and a recovery of misappropriated funds imposed as punishment - The punishments are based on different charges - Dismissal from service following a criminal conviction cannot be considered double jeopardy - The Court's finding of guilt and conviction operate in different domains and are independent of each other.
