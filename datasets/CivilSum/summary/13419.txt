The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, and 226, concern education and admission, migration, and examination.
In this case, the petitioners cleared their first MBBS professional in a joint 18-month course on their very first attempt but sought migration and also sat for a joint test.
The Medical Council of India (MCI) approved the migration, but later the petitioners were not allowed to take the examination for their second professional course.
The MCI cannot make a volte-face and prevent them from taking the exam for the 2nd professional course after having allowed migration.
The petitioners have already completed 18 months of study in their previous college and fulfilled other conditions of eligibility, such as attending the required lectures.
The MCI's actions were arbitrary, and the petitioners' petition is allowed.
