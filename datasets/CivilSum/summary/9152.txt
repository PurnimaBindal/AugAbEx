Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - PSEB Employees Punishment and Appeal Regulations, 1971, Rule 5 - Penalty - Discrimination - The plaintiff was the only one penalized with recovery of amount, while others in the same position were let off with just a censure of their conduct; there is no reasonable explanation for this discrepancy.
Moreover, other executive engineers who received similar notices were only given minor penalties of censure.
Due to the discriminatory nature of the Board's actions, both courts found the impugned order to be illegal and it was rightfully set aside.
