Constitution of India, Article 226 - Letters Patent Appeal - Maintainability - Civil Miscellaneous - Letters patent appeal against a Civil Miscellaneous order is not maintainable.
[Para 3]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Letters Patent Appeal - Necessary parties - Appeal isn't maintainable if the person affected is not made a party - For instance, if the sole person against whom relief has been claimed is not impleaded.
[Paras 2 and 3]
