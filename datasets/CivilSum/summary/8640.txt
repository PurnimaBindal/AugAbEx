Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Rajya Sabha Secretariat (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1957, Rules 16 and 17 - Providing copy of enquiry report and show cause notice - When an enquiry is held against an employee, the disciplinary authority must provide the charged employee with a copy of the enquiry report and show cause notice with proposed punishment and record the reasons for disagreeing with the findings returned in favour of the employee - Failing to comply with this violates natural justice principles and renders the punishment order void.
[Paras 6 and 7]  B Rajya Sabha Secretariat (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1957, Rule 17 - Minor penalty and natural justice - If an enquiry is conducted for imposing a minor penalty, the charged official must be provided with a copy of the enquiry report and given the opportunity to submit a representation - The penalty of withholding promotion for an extended period and treating absence as dies-a-non is of severe consequence for service career as it is a major penalty that cannot be imposed without following the proper procedure and complying with natural justice principles.
