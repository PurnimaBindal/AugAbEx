Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Degree and diplomas - Equivalence of - Eligibility for appointment of Assistant Teacher in MC Delhi - The required qualification for the post of Assistant Teacher in MC Delhi is a TTE diploma, as B.Ed.
diploma/degree holders are not eligible due to the nature of the job teaching small children.
The training for TTE and B.Ed.
diplomas are distinctly different, and obtaining a higher qualification does not guarantee eligibility for the position.
Furthermore, Assistant Teachers with TTE diplomas may also be promoted as B.Ed.
Teachers.
