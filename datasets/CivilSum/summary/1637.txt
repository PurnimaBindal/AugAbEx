Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8(5)(c) - Words and Phrases - In Rule 8(5)(c) of the Punjab Civil Services (P&A) Rules, 1970, `May' is interpreted as `Must' in the requirement for the appointment of a presenting officer in a departmental inquiry.
It cannot be seen as just an enabling provision but a requirement by law.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8(5)(c) - Departmental inquiry - Presenting Officer - Natural justice - The absence of a presenting officer in an inquiry must be considered.
The inquiry officer is left alone to determine what evidence should be used against the delinquent party to prove their guilt.
In light of this, the Labour Court justified its decision to nullify the inquiry.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 11 - Back wages - The Labour Court ordered the re-instatement of a worker but without providing back wages.
The worker remained unemployed throughout.
The management did not object to the award, so the worker cannot seek relief for back wages in the writ petition.
