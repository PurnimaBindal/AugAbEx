Rajasthan Civil Services Conduct Rules, 1971 - Removal - Four Months prior to superannuation - Concealment of fact at time of appointment as a ground - According to the ruling, it is highly unjustified for the department to take harsh penal action against the Petitioner on the verge of retirement after being silent for more than 36 years - Hence the action is not sustainable in law.
