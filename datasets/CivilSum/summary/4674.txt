Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 226 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 15 - National Institute of Public Cooperation and Child Development Bye-laws, Rules 32-B and 32-C - Validity of rules - Judicial review - Change of conditions of service - The Service Rules apply to all, and no public servant can claim an absolute right that conditions of service cannot be altered at any time.
The competent authority has the power to change the procedure if modification is required.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Service conditions - Amendment of procedure prescribed in Rule 15 of CCS Rules for communication of decision of appellate authority under its own signature - Changing the methodology of communication of the decision cannot have prejudice unless it materially affects the decision on the merits.
