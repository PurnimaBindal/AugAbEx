Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana State Order dt. 1981987, Clause 12 - Voluntary retirement - Pension - Fixation of pension and gratuity per revised pay scale during voluntary retirement while on extra-ordinary leave - Entitlement to increments during leave period - Extra- ordinary leave that has been sanctioned by competent authorities must count towards increments - Dismissing the petitioner's claim for pension, gratuity, and annual increments solely due to being on extra-ordinary leave on medical grounds sanctioned by competent authorities is not justifiable.
[Paras 3 & 5]
