Panjab University Calendar, 1988, Vol. II, Regulations 4 and 5 - Unfair means/Examination - The petitioner was found to have used unfair means during university exams and was caught with relevant notes on his hand.
Despite claiming innocence, the circumstances pointed to mala fide intention.
The punishment of disqualification for two years was deemed appropriate and upheld, as Regulation 4 does not apply in cases of proved mala fide intention.
No reduction of punishment was necessary.
[Paras 8, 9 and 11]
