Article 215 of the Indian Constitution grants the High Court the power to commit for contempt of court, but in this case, there was no question of contempt arising as the single Judge's refusal to pass an interim order of suspension of the transfer order was being appealed.
The Division Bench of the High Court had no reason to exercise its powers under Article 215 as there was no wilful violation of any direction or order of the Court.
