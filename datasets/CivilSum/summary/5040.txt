Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-B - Payment of wages - Workman who was not employed by management despite award - The court ordered the management to pay the arrears of wages last drawn by the workman or minimum wages, whichever was higher - However, the management misunderstood the directions and deposited only the arrears of wages last drawn to suit its own convenience.
Therefore, the court directed the management to deposit the difference between the last pay drawn and the minimum wages payable from time to time.
