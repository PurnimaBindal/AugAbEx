As per Articles 14 and 311 of the Constitution of India, a public authority must adhere to the principles of natural justice while terminating the service of an individual in public employment, even if there are no service rules specifying it.
[Paras 6 and 7]
The principles of natural justice must be followed while terminating the service of a government employee, in accordance with Articles 14 and 311 of the Constitution of India.
A show-cause notice is necessary, even if a regular enquiry is not conducted, to give the employee an opportunity to justify his conduct and defend himself against charges of misconduct if he has not benefited or affected the department.
