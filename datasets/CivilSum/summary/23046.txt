Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(ra), 25-B, 25-F and 25-H - Item 10 of Part I of Fifth Schedule - Unfair labour practice - Termination of temporary employee - The absence of a 14-day notice given to a temporary employee does not mean they continue beyond the original appointment period, and does not constitute unfair labour practice.
Only repeated employment on fixed terms with artificial breaks to avoid permanent status would be considered unfair labour practice.
[Paras 10 & 11]
