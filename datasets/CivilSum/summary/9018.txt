The Constitution of India's Articles 311 and 226 are relevant to the petitioner's case as they hold an ad hoc appointment for the position of Psychologist.
The petitioner was placed on the waiting list after being selected by the Commission, and although one of the posts became vacant, the government decided not to fill it as the incumbent had kept a lien on the position.
The petitioner has no right to claim an appointment on the basis of being on the waiting list, and the authority's actions cannot be deemed as arbitrary or illegal.
