Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Back wages - Punishment - Substitution of punishment - When exercising discretion under Section 11-A, the Labour Tribunal must evaluate the conditions under which relief should be granted, considering the wide-ranging terms of the legislation which gives the Tribunal substantial discretion - However, the Tribunal must also assess whether back wages should be granted and, if so, to what extent - Back wages cannot be presumed to be an automatic outcome of reinstatement pursuant to Section 11-A absent cogent reasons - In this case, the Supreme Court granted 50% back wages, given the lack of evidence presented by the employer regarding the workman's employment during the period prior to reinstatement.
