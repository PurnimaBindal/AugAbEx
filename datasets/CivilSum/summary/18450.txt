The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, and 226, along with the Punjab State Technical (Medical and Engineering) Service Rules from 1978, including Rules 1, 3(c), 3(e), 4(1), 5, and 6, establish that demobilised Indian Armed Forces personnel should have reserved vacancies.
The petitioner was selected through an advertisement published before the rules expired, so the court held that they should be seen as being appointed under the 1978 Rules.
Denying the petitioner benefits based on the date of their appointment is not fair and violates their fundamental right to equality, as guaranteed under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
