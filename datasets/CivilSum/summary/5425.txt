The termination of service of respondents, who had completed probation of more than three years, on grounds of misconduct and without any departmental inquiry results in stigma and their order of reinstatement with the continuity of service does not warrant any interference.
[Paras 4 and 5]
The respondents who were on probation and not permanent employee were terminated after completing more than three years of service.
The Labour court, after a reference period of 12 years, held that the respondents are entitled to 60% back wages from the date of reference until the date of award and full wages from the date of award until date of actual reinstatement, modifying the award.
