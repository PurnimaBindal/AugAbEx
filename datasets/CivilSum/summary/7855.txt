Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Border Security Force Act, Section 40 - Act prejudicial to good order and discipline of the force - Reduction in rank - In a case where an allegation of sleeping on duty was not supported by any evidence on record, Summary Security Force Court found the accused guilty - However, it was held that the mere fact that a person was found lying cannot, in itself, indicate that he was sleeping - The accused's guilty plea could only be read to the extent that he was lying, not sleeping - As a result, the accused was entitled to the benefits of the post he held before his compulsory retirement.
