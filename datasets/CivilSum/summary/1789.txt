Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Misconduct - Unauthorised Absence - An employee was found not guilty of misconduct by a tribunal after proper evaluation of evidence.
The leave taken by the employee was authorized by the sanctioning authority and not just recorded without pay.
The application for removal of the worker was rejected by the tribunal and no interference was made in that order.
