Experience - Qualifications - Post of District Public Relation Officers - The condition for appointment to the post is having two years of experience with necessary qualifications - However, the experience must be after acquiring the required qualification - Prior experience will not be taken into consideration.
[Para 6] B. Equivalence of qualifications - Employers are responsible for determining the essential qualifications for a particular position - Specific qualifications are necessary for the post and the benefit of equivalent qualification cannot be given.
