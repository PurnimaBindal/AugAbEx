According to Sections 30 and 4-A(3) of the Workmen's Compensation Act, an employee who was injured on the job must be compensated for the injury sustained.
In this case, the employee suffered a back bone injury and broken legs after falling from a pole while working with the Electricity Board.
After compensation was awarded, an appeal was filed, but it was determined that there was no substantial question of law involved in the appeal, thus rendering it not maintainable.
However, the employee was awarded interest at a rate of 6% due to the delayed payment.
