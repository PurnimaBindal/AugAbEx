Constitution of India, Article 14 - Date of Birth/Natural Justice - Changing the date of birth during the reconstruction of the service file - Correction upon receipt of original record - Complaint lodged with the Lokayukata against recorrection - Lokayukta discovered that the respondent had fraudulently fabricated the records and recommended that the original date of birth be restored - No further opportunity needed to be given to the respondent when ordering the correction based on the Lokayukta's report - Opportunities to adduce all evidence were available to the respondent before the Lokayukta - The principles of natural justice do not require an opportunity at every possible stage.
[Paras 9 and 10]
