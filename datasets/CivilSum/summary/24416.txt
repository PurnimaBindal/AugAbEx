Constitution of India, Article 310 - Retirement - Age of superannuation - Pre-mature retirement - Judicial Officers can be granted extension in service from 58 to 60 years only if the States have framed Rules providing for 60 years age for retirement.
In cases where no such rule exists, extension beyond 58 years has to be considered on individual merits.
A petitioner who was assessed as an average officer and had vigilance proceedings initiated against him on several allegations was denied extension beyond 58 years, which cannot be considered unjustified.
[Paras 9 to 13]
