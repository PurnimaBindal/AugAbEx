According to Section 11 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a workman who has proceeded on leave and fallen ill in his home town, but has not applied for an extension of leave, can produce a medical certificate from a government hospital at the time of joining after three months.
Additionally, the workman in this case had nearly 25 years of service, making the order of dismissal improper.
