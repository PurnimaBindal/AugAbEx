Income Tax Rules, 1962, Rules 82 to 97 - Air India Employees Self- Contributory Superannuation Pension Scheme Rules, 1994 (as amended in 1997) - The objective of the Scheme is to assist employees in receiving monetary benefits upon becoming incapacitated, as well as payment to beneficiaries in the event of employee death, through the creation and investment of a contributory superannuation fund in compliance with Rules 82 to 97 of Income Tax Rules, 1962 under a scheme of insurance or purchase of annuities at the time of retirement or death.
[Paras 16 to 21]

Income Tax Rules, 1962, Rules 82 to 97 - Air India Employees Self- Contributory Superannuation Pension Scheme Rules, 1994 (as amended in 1997) - While an amendment of the Pension Scheme would be made if its fund position became outright bad, it cannot be applied retrospectively - The trust established under the Scheme is unchangeable - The original Scheme is a "Contributory Defined Scheme" with a "Defined Benefit Plan," and retirees whose rights have already been determined under the current scheme on their retirement cannot be required to refund the payment received by them or make more contributions to make up for the gap between the amount of annuity and the contributions they have made, nor can their pension be reduced - The amounts they contributed and were paid were considered when calculating the annuity amount at the time of their annuity purchase - Therefore, amending the scheme cannot be made retroactively to increase or decrease retiree benefits - Once the annuity is purchased, there remains no link to the quantum of the fund or financial situation of the fund for which the annuity was purchased.
[Paras 40, 41, 46 to 50]

Income Tax Rules, 1962, Rules 82 to 97 - Air India Employees Self- Contributory Superannuation Pension Scheme Rules, 1994 (as amended in 1997) - 'Annuity' - 'Annuity' refers to an obligation to pay a set amount, usually on a monthly or yearly basis, to a designated recipient - Although paid out of personal assets, they may be transferred as real estate and even inheritable.
[Paras 42 and 43]

Insurance Act, 1938, Sections 2(11) and 45 - Income Tax Rules, 1962, Rules 82 to 97 - Air India Employees Self-Contributory Superannuation Pension Scheme Rules, 1994 (as amended in 1997) - An annuity bought from LIC creates an obligation on LIC - The privity of contract between LIC and retired employees who are entitled to receive annuities cannot be denied, solely because the payment was made by the Trust under the Scheme - There is always a deemed privity between the beneficiary and the insurer - The insurer is responsible for fulfilling its commitment.
