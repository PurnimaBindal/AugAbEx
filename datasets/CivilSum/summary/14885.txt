As per Sections 2(9)(b) and 95(1) of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1950, the Central Government has been empowered to prescribe the wage limit for the application of the Scheme.
The wage limit may be fixed from time to time by framing Rules as provided in S. 95(1) of the Act.
The legislature has provided safeguards by prescribing the manner and conditions subject to which the Rules have to be framed and they are subject to Parliament's approval.
Rule-making power is given to the highest executive authority, which is presumed to act in the interest of the public.
Excessive delegation cannot be found if the legislature provides the subject in which the delegated authority can frame rules.
