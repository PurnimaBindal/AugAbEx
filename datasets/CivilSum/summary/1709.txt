Under Article 226 of the Indian Constitution, equivalent qualifications in recruitment are not always recognized.
A petitioner claimed equivalence between his B.Ed.
degree and a post-graduate qualification, but this was dismissed in the case of Mrs. Dilsher Kaur v. State of Punjab and others.
