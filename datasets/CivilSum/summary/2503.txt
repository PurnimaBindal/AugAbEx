According to Punjab Civil Services Rules as applicable to Haryana, promotees for the Head teacher position must receive one increment of higher responsibility.
Failure to provide this increment due to the appointment of a candidate much lower than the petitioner in the merit list of S.Cs.
is illegal.
The Board cannot disregard claims of high-ranking candidates in favor of lower-ranking candidates based on representations alone.
Every citizen has a right to compete for public employment, and unscrupulous methods should not negate their claims.
[Paras 4 to 7]  B The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 discuss appointment, seniority, merit lists, and reserved vacancies.
In this case, Respondents violated the principles of equality and natural justice by appointing only two of five SC candidates and failing to appoint the petitioner, who stood at No3 in the merit list.
No reasons were given for not appointing the petitioner, who should have been appointed to one of the reserved vacancies.
As a result, the Court directed the respondents to appoint the petitioner as a Junior Engineer, even if a supernumerary post needed to be created for this purpose.
[Para 10] C. While the result of the selection for Junior Engineers was not conveyed to the candidates, the petitioner learned of the selection and approached the Court for relief.
The petitioner's rights were crystallized upon initiation of the proceedings, rendering the delay in the disposal of the case immaterial.
