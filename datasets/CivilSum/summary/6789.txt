Constitution of India, Article 12 - State - To qualify as a `State' under Article 12, a Limited Company must have a majority of shares held by Government, state-owned corporations, and Nationalised Banks, with only a small number of shares held by the public.
Additionally, the Government must have deep and pervasive control over the company.
When these conditions are met, the company is considered a `State' within the meaning of Article 12.
Based on these criteria, Cochin Refineries Ltd is deemed a `State' under Article 12 of the Constitution.
