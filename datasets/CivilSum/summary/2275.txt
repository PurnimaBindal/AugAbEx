Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948, Section 10(1)(e) - Suspension - Removal from service from a tenure post of Member of a Board - During suspension, an employee will be entitled to receive subsistence allowance in accordance with rules, or full pay and allowance in the absence of any rule.
If an employee is removed during this time and the order is later nullified by the Court, they may be reinstated with full pay and allowances for the intervening period, unless the Court orders payment subject to the outcome of fresh/further inquiry.
[Paras 11 to 21 & 27]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Departmental proceedings - Natural justice - Termination of service - Infractions of rules of procedure or natural justice will not automatically result in the setting aside of termination and reinstatement.
The Court must determine if real prejudice was caused and what the possible result would have been had the alleged defect or irregularity not occurred.
If no prejudice was caused, then no interference is necessary, but if it is required, parties may be relegated to the stage at which defect occurred and the employer may proceed afresh if they so choose.
[Paras 21 to 27]
