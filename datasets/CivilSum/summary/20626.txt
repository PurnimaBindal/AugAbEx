According to the Andhra Pradesh Public Employment (Organization of Local Cadres and Regulation of Direct Recruitment) Order, 1975, promotion eligibility of employees of factories and boilers units is precluded specifically to avoid promoting them to higher positions within Labour unit.
Para 5(2) of Presidential Order refers only to transfers and doesn't cover promotions, thereby excluding the promotion of employees of factories and boilers units to higher positions in Labour unit.
[Paras 12 and 14]

Transfer refers to the change of a place of employment within an organization and cannot be used to move a government servant to a higher post than the one they currently hold.
