The petitioner, who had appeared in an examination four times, was not given marks for weightage.
However, since none of the occasions resulted in selection, the court cannot intervene.
The percentage to be awarded in each category cannot be regulated by the court, and there seems to be no mala fide or bias against any particular individual officer.
The High Court was justified in not entertaining the writ petition, and as a result, the SLP was dismissed.
