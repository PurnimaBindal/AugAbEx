Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion - Qualifications - Reversion of promotees due to lack of qualification after a certain number of years - Adequate opportunity must be given to promotees as per principles of natural justice - Practical experience is a valuable aid in assessing the suitability of a person for a post - Initial minimum educational qualifications are only required for initial entry into service - Once a promotee has worked satisfactorily for a long time, the requirement of initial qualification no longer holds much significance, especially if there were no rules prescribing requisite qualifications at the time of entry/promotion - A promotee cannot be reverted for want of qualifications and it cannot be allowed to be corrected as an inadvertence of the authority.
[Paras 13, 14 and 15]
