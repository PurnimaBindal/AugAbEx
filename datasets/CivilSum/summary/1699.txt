Appointment of Director, Medical College, Rohtak was challenged on grounds of eligibility and qualifications under A Medical College, Rohtak (Conditions of Service of Teachers) Act, 1986, Section 3 and Haryana Medical Education Service Rules, 1988, Rule 5 and 9/Constitution of India, Article 226/Quo warranto.
However, in the reply, the appointee was found eligible and selected based on merit.
The petitioners were unable to prove that the respondent was not eligible for the post or holding it without legal authority, thus a writ of quo warranto was not issued.
Additionally, under B Pleadings/Admissions and denial/Replication, any new averments made in the Written Statement were taken to be admitted if not controverted by way of rejoinder.
