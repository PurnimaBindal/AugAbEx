Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Eligibility - Decree of declaration - If a person is granted a decree of declaration stating their entitlement to be considered for promotion, the authority cannot ignore them based on non-fulfillment of eligibility criteria once the decree becomes final.

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Natural Justice - Adverse report - An uncommunicated adverse report cannot be used against an employee to deny them a promotion.
