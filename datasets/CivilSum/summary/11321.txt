Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 311(2) - Probation - The petitioner was on probation, and his performance was being monitored.
If the employer found his performance unsatisfactory during the probation period, they could have terminated his services.
[Para 7]

Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 311(2) - Temporary appointment as Driver - The petitioner was on probation for the position of Driver, and his suitability was being assessed.
However, within a short period of time, the petitioner caused two accidents.
Based on this, the employer rightfully concluded that the petitioner was not suitable for the position and terminated his services.
The termination order was in line with the terms of the appointment, and no aspersion or stigma was placed on the petitioner.
There are no grounds for interference with this decision.
