Constitution of India, Article 16 - Assam Engineering (P.W.D.)
Services Rules, 1978, Rule 22 - Seniority - Inter se seniority - Dispute regarding inter se seniority for post of Assistant Engineer - Petitioners who were direct recruits and respondents who were promotees have conflicting seniority claims - Respondents who were regularised in 1987 were treated as Assistant Engineers of batch 1987, while petitioners who were regularised in 1988 were treated as appointees of 1988 batch - Petitioners contend that as they were selected in 1987, they should be treated as 1987 batch and given seniority over promotee respondents - Rule 22(3) states that promotee shall be ranked senior to direct recruit - Tribunal rightly gave respondents seniority over petitioners based on recruits of 1987 batch - Regularisation of respondents was not challenged for over 11 years, so petitioners cannot claim seniority at this stage - Impugned order of Tribunal has no infirmity.
[Paras 10 and 11]
