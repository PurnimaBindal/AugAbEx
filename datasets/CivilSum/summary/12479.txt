Constitution of India, Articles 12, 14, 16, 39(a) and 41 - Contract of Service - Termination - Inserting a clause in the conditions of appointment or service rule that gives the employer arbitrary and unfettered power to terminate services of a regular employee with one month notice or salary violates Articles 14, 16, 39 (a) and 41 of the Constitution as well as Section 23 of the Indian Contract Act due to being opposed to public policy.
