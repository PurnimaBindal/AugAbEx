Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16(4B) and 226 - Selection process - Reservation - Filling up of back log - A petitioner challenged an employment notice on the grounds that filling all 196 Constitution vacancies by reserved category candidates violated Art. 16(4B) of the Constitution of India - The company was authorized to fill all reserved category vacancies not filled between 1989 and 1998, since no provision forbids carrying forward backlogs - The petitioner was not entitled to claim appointment when the petition was filed - The company did not violate Article 16(4B) of the Constitution by initiating the recruitment process.
