Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Medical Reimbursement - Out-door treatment - Expenses - Claim for - Petitioner, an employee of PGI, was referred for an MRI test to AIIMS by attending doctors - Although she underwent the test, reimbursement for the expenses incurred was denied - It was held that the MRI test was an essential part of the treatment the petitioner received as an indoor patient at PGI - Respondents were directed to make payment to the petitioner assuming that the test was reimbursable as indoor treatment.
