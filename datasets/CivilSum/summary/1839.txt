According to Articles 19, 21, and 226 of the Constitution of India, there is no fundamental or statutory right for hawkers to conduct their business on public roads, streets, or pavements.
These public areas are intended for the use of the general public, and not for commercial activities.
With the absence of safe pedestrian paths, schoolchildren are at risk of accidents and fatalities on a daily basis in the cities of the State.
