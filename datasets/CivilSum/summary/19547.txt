Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 16 - Posts of Supervisors (Female) - Advertisement - Qualification - Selection - The Selection Board clarified that promotees must receive one increment of higher responsibility.
They also explained that before completion of their selection process, they received a requisition from the Department for additional vacancies, which was displayed on the Notice Board.
The Selection Board proceeded to select candidates for both the advertised posts and the subsequent additional posts, which was a practice that had been adopted in the past.
The petitioners were candidates for the advertised posts and were examined and interviewed but not selected.
The third petitioner did not meet the requisite qualifications on the last date of submission of their application.
Therefore, there cannot be any complaint made, and there is no issue with the Selection Board procedures.
The selection is valid.
