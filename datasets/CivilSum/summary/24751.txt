Junagadh State Pension and Parwashi Allowances Rules, 1932, Rule 241-A - Retirement - Employee retired at age of superannuation only in accordance with decision of Supreme Court - It is not appropriate to deduct an employee's pension and gratuity due to subsequent findings that his services were not satisfactory.
[Para 26]
Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 311 - Bombay Civil Services Rules, 1959, Rules 188, 189 and 189-A - Retirement - Pension - If an employee fails to prove that he rendered satisfactory service throughout and an order reducing his pension and gratuity on the basis of allegations of unsatisfactory service is issued, it is not illegal.
The employee is not entitled to the benefit of Clause (b)(ii) of proviso to Rule 189 if the proceedings were instituted long before his retirement.
[Paras 23,26]
Junagadh State Pension and Parwashi Allowances Rules, 1932, Rule 241A - Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary Proceedings - Retirement - even if enquiries become infructuous, considering charges which were subject-matter of the enquiry only for determining quantum of his pension and gratuity is not illegal.
