Constitution of India, Article 16 - West Bengal Service Rules, Part-I, Rule 55(4) - Pay scale - The petitioner was appointed as a Jute Field Assistant in 1963 and was promoted to Grade-A in 1981, with his pay refixed from 1987.
The petitioner contends that a joint gradation list was to be published in accordance with the order of the Governor dated October 1980, and that his pay was to be refixed but the private respondents are receiving a higher scale of pay despite the petitioner's seniority.
The respondent illegally directed the payment of a higher grade to others by an order passed in 1982 with retrospective effect from 1978, even though Rule 55(4) is not applicable as redesignation is permissible by law.
It was also found that the concerned employees were receiving a higher scale of pay than the petitioner, even in 1978.
Even if higher pay was wrongly granted, the petitioner cannot benefit from illegality, and no relief was granted as the concerned employee was not impleaded in the case.
