Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Rajasthan Service of Engineers (Building and Roads Branch) Rules, 1954, Part-V - Promotion - Promotable candidates must not be overlooked in favor of deputationists simply due to the absence of available vacancies - Such action is tantamount to violating the legitimate rights of those who are eligible for promotion - If necessary, the court may issue orders to the relevant authority to compel them to act in accordance with the law and fulfill their duties - The authority has been instructed to convene the DPC and complete the entire promotion process within six months' time.
