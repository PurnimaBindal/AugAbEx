According to Sections 14-A and 16(1)(b) of the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, as well as Clause 32-A of the Employees' Provident Fund Scheme, 1952, it is the employer's statutory obligation to deduct a specific percentage of contribution from the employees' salary for the Provident Fund.
Even if there is a delay in allotting a Code number, this does not excuse the employer from remitting the contribution.
The appellant had the option to use alternative modes of payment or remit the Provident Fund contribution under a separate account.
Damages for belated remittance are therefore justifiable.
