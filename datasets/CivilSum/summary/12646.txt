Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - CRPF Act, Section 10(m) - Group Centre and Battalion Officers Manual, Clauses 7.2 and 6.23 - Dismissal from service for absence from camp without permission, consuming liquor and molesting a lady outside - Dismissal from service is a major punishment and should not have been given without proper consideration of the circumstances - The discipline of a camp differs greatly from the posting of an individual outside the camp - The order of dismissal has been quashed and the matter has been sent back to the disciplinary authority to determine the appropriate punishment.
