Constitution of India, Article 226, 311 - RPF Rules, 1987, Rule 153, 1538 - RPF Rules 1959, Rule 44 - Departmental Proceedings - Charge sheet - Inquiry - Removal - Charge sheet issued without preliminary enquiry - Inquiry marked in predetermined manner, with the name of inquiry officer, venue, date, and time of inquiry fixed at the time of issuing the charge sheet itself - The act of the authority biased and closed-minded - The decision of quasi-judicial authority must be backed by reasons - Reliance on documents and statements of persons without making them witnesses to the proceedings held against the law - Hence, final order passed without reasons - The entire proceedings and final order are set aside.
[Paras 24 to 27]
