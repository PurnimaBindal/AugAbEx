Employments Exchanges (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959, Sections 4 and 8 - Appointment - Announcement of vacancies - Appointment granted on daily wages - Appointment made deliberately violating the provisions of Section 4 of Employment Exchanges Act, without making the vacancies known to the general public or notifying the Employment Exchange - The appointing authority is responsible for U/s 8 of the Act - In such a serious matter, an act of the holder of public office amounts to the seizing of public property to which all citizens have a right, and the court will not take a compassionate view - Prosecution of the Appointing Authority ordered.
