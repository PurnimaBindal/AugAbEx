Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - A & N Islands (Panchayat) Regulations, 1994, Regulation 157 - Posting and Transfer - Appellant employed under A & N administration - Position created in Engineering wing of Zila Parishad, Port Blair identical to appellant's - Appellant appointed to the position - After 11 months, transferred back to administration for further posting - Administration has the authority to provide any assignment to appellant - Appellant has no legal right to a particular posting.
