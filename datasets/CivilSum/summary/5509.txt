Constitution of India, Articles 20 and 226 - Army Act, 1950, Section 73 - Double jeopardy - Petitioner, who worked as a barber in the Indian Army, challenges his punishment of dismissal from service on the grounds of double jeopardy, as he had already been imprisoned under the Army Act for desertion.
However, the court rejects his contention, stating that the order of dismissal was passed in accordance with the provisions of Section 73 of the Army Act, and thus does not amount to double jeopardy.
