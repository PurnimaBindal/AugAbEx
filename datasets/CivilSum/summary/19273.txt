The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300-A and University of Delhi, Ordinance XII, Rule 3(A)(2) pertain to the Superannuation/Re-employment of academic staff at the University.
The University's rule, executive council resolution and service rules allow for such re-employment after the retirement age.
As per all the rules, the Board of Governors must consider academic staff for re-employment at the age of superannuation, but in the case of the petitioner, a Director of the Institution, his request for re-employment was not accepted.
This action of the Board of Governors is considered as abdication of powers and a disregard for the rules and regulations, and therefore the petitioner is entitled to be considered for re-employment.
