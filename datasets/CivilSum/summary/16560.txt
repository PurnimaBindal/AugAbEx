Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Illegal appointments - Removal - Individuals selected by Zila Parishad as Ayurvedic Doctors and working as Medical Officers in dispensaries - State Government takes over dispensaries and services of Medical Officers under Zila Parishad - Order passed in 1997 to return services of individuals to non-existent dispensaries - Application of de-facto doctrine - Appellants cannot be removed from Government Service - Order quashed.
