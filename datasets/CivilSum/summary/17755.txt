Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226/227 - Medical Council of India Recommendation on graduate Medical Education Regulation, Paragraph (V) sub-clause (a) - Migration/Transfer - MBBS Course - Clause (a) allows for students to transfer to another recognised medical college under another/same University, based on laid down conditions - Respondents were instructed to consider petitioner's case for migration to a medical College, despite not being sponsored by MCI - Indo-Soviet Medical Education, Care and Research, Foundation and Vinnitsa Medical College were both recognised by MCI, making it arbitrary for them to reject a student from the latter Institution.
