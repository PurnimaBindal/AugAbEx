Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Delayed payment of retiral benefits - Arrears becoming due only on account of retrospective amendment - Authority must not cause unjustified delay in payment - Pension made available with condition of depositing back amount of PF received with interest, but interest waived until pension made available - Some "ad hoc payments" made to serving employees cannot be grounds for discrimination - Justified classification between serving and non-serving persons made based on chances of excess payments due to anticipated final decision - Claim of interest rightly rejected by authority.
