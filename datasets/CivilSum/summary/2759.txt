Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Condition of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 9(10) - Promotion - Reversion - The appellant who belonged to the ST category was promoted to the position of Assistant Headmaster in 1984 and to the position of Headmaster in 1992.
No one challenged his promotions.
However, in 2003, he was reverted back to the position of Assistant Teacher in order to fill up the single post of Headmaster according to service seniority.
The reversion was deemed improper due to delayed action by the management.
