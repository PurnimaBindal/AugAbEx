Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical Services of the Armed Forces, 1983, Regulation 123 - Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Rule 173 and Para 7(b) of Appendix II - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 13(3)(iii)(b) - Disability pension - If the medical opinion stating that the disability is not attributed or aggravated by military service is supported by material and reasons, and not challenged, the court cannot interfere and substitute its opinion - The medical board, appellate authority, and medical officer considered all the stages, and the Court cannot intervene with the diseases that resulted in petitioner's disability and discharge.
