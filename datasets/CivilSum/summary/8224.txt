As per the Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 11-B, denying backwages to a workman, solely based on the assumption that he can earn a livelihood by doing labor jobs on a daily basis, is erroneous.
Mere survival by doing labor work cannot be equated to gainful employment.
As observed in AIR 1984 S.C. 1805, the workman in question is entitled to full back wages from the date of the demand notice.
