Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Departmental enquiry - Delay - Mala fide - When the post of Secretary to Govt. is to be filled in, the withdrawal of both earlier charge-sheets, along with their revival after 11 years and at the time when the petitioner's claim for promotion became due, gives the impression that the prospect of petitioner was more important than the desire to investigate into the irregularities - Petitioner's allegations of mala fides seem to be valid - Hence, the decision is questionable and liable to be quashed.
