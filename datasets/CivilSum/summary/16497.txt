Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Examination - Unfair means - Petitioner allegedly used unfair means in an exam and was disqualified from taking any university exam for two semesters - Petitioner was not allowed to complete the remaining part of the exam on a separate answer book - Unfair means was reported much later - Punishment was ultimately reduced.
