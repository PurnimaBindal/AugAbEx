The Administrative Tribunals Act of 1985, Sections 3(q), 19, and 28, along with the Rajasthan Civil Services Appellate Tribunal Act of 1976, Section 2(f), and the Civil Procedure Code, Order 39 Rules 1 and 2, make it clear that transfer disputes regarding a government servant fall under the category of "service matter."
As a result, the Rajasthan Services Appellate Tribunal has the jurisdiction to hear appeals on these matters, whereas the Civil Court has no power to grant injunctions in such cases.
