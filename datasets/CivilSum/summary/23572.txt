Constitution of India, Article 16 - State Bank of India Circulars dated 341978 and 28.7.1983 - Promotion - Reserved vacancies for the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in the matter of internal promotions - The scheme of reservation in regard to promotions made from the Award Cadre to the Supervising cadres has provisions for carrying forward of reserved vacancies for a period of three years, at the end of which they lapse.
No more than 50% of the available vacancies should be reserved.
This has been upheld.
The provision for subsequent relaxation, which was in excess of the relaxation originally granted, does not allow for retrospectively filling the lapsed vacancies.
Any relaxation granted in favour of SC and ST employees in respect of service norms is a matter of policy.

As per circular of 341978, reserved vacancies in the promotional posts, if not filed, lapse after three years and cannot be revived.

Constitution or India, Article 16(4) - Reservations for SC and ST - Article 16(4) is an enabling provision and confers a discretionary power on the State to make reservations either at the stage of initial recruitment or at the stage of promotion in favour of a backward class of citizens which is not adequately represented in the service of the State.
However, it does not confer any right on the petitioner and there is no constitutional duty imposed on the Govt. to make such a reservation.
