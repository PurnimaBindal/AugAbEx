Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F and 25-G - Unlawful termination - The re-employment of an individual during termination or legal proceedings is not considered a valid reason for upholding wrongful termination, as defined by Sections 25-F and 25-G of the Act, as the individual should not be left to starve until the final adjudication of their claim.
Additionally, the court cannot quash the award of reinstatement simply because the individual was able to find re-employment during the proceedings unless there is an obvious jurisdictional or legal error on the face of the record.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F and 25-G - Back wages - Re-employment during termination period or during pendency of proceedings - However, mere re-employment for a short period does not count as gainful employment sufficient to deny all back wages to the individual, and the normal standard is full back wages.
