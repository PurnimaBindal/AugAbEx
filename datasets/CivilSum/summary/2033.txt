Petitioners Sunil Kumar, Subodh Kumar, Rajeev Rakesh, and Priya Ranjana, represented by their advocates, brought forward a case against respondent Tarkeshwar Dayal, supported by the Constitution of India Articles 14 and 226, regarding promotion, reversion, and cancellation of promotion orders.
The show-cause notice issued by authorities for cancellation with complete prejudice predetermines the entire issue and offers no opportunities for show-cause justice, making it a mere ritual and ceremony.
The principles of natural justice require not only that justice be done, but also that it appears to be so, and thus the impugned show-cause notice and the final cancellation order based on it are unjustifiable and are quashed.
[Paras 25 and 26]
