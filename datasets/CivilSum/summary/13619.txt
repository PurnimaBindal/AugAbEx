Rajasthan Services Rules, 1951, Rule 268-D Note No. 2 - The inclusion of a second wife who contracted marriage after a government employee's retirement in the definition of 'family' was challenged in Note No. 2 - The court held that the second wife is indeed a member of the family and is entitled to receive family pension after the pensioner's death - Note 2 was struck down as discriminatory and violative of Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
[Paras 17 and 19] B. Rajasthan Services Rules, 1961, Rule 268-D Note No. 2 - The purpose of the pension scheme is to compensate the family of the employee in lieu of the services rendered by him after his death and to provide sustenance in old age - The distinction between the effects of marriage during service and after retirement is unjustified and violative of Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 17] C. Pension - The allowance paid to a retired Government employee is earned through long and efficient service and can be considered a deferred portion of compensation for services rendered.
[Para 10] Important Point - The second wife of a Government employee, who married after retirement, is considered a member of the family and is entitled to family pension.
