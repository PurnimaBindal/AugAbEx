Section 39 of the Specific Relief Act of 1963 pertains to the declaration and transfer of official accommodation.
A Railway employee who was in possession of government quarter transferred to another station but failed to vacate the quarter at the old station of posting.
The appellant filed a suit for declaration that he was not obligated to pay rent for the quarter.
However, it was deemed not maintainable.
