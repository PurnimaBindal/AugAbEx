Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 100 - Panjab University Act, 1947, Section 21, and Regulation 102 - Regular Second appeal - The dismissal of the appeal by the first appellate Court on the ground of competency of the Registrar to file the same is not sustainable - The matter has been remitted back to the first Appellate Court for its decision on merit because it is always in the interest of justice that the matter should be decided on merits.
[Para 8]

Panjab University Act, 1947, Section 21 and Regulation 102 - Competency of the Registrar to prefer any appeal without resolution - The Registrar of the University is not authorized to initiate any suit, appeal, or other legal proceedings on his own, as it must be taken by the competent authority - If the Registrar's action of filing an appeal is ratified, that ratification has the effect of relating back to the time when the action was taken without authority.
[Paras 7 and 8]
