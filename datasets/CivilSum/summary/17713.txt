According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 regarding pay and allowances, the government allows for a higher grade of pay to employees who have been stagnant in the same position for 13 years without any promotion.
An example is of an employee who started as a Village Officer, but was later promoted to Revenue Inspector and held that position for seven years before being reverted back to the role of Village Officer.
After completing 13 years of service, excluding the time spent in the higher post, the employee is entitled to a higher grade of pay.
