The petitioner, a minor, applied for a compassionate appointment to a private educational institution, which was denied.
Despite rejection of his first application, the petitioner filed another after attaining majority and it is held that the second application is a continuation of the first.
Since the petitioner is qualified, he is entitled to compassionate appointment in accordance with the rules of the institution.
[Paras 2, 3 and 4] B.
The respondents contended that there were no vacant positions for compassionate appointment, but vacancies were shown in documents that were not approved by the department.
The contention of the respondent that there are no vacant positions is not acceptable because non-approval of the department does not mean those positions are non-existent.
