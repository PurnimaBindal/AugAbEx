Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 26 - Concealment of facts - Correction of Date of Birth - If an interpolation is found in a service book, it can be corrected in the service record with employee's consent and after giving an opportunity to be heard.
Challenging the change after a long time while concealing the facts of opportunity and consent provided will not entitle the employee to any relief from the court.
