Haryana State Seed Certification Agency Service Rules, 1979, Rules 6, 7 and 9 - Appointment/Deputation/Qualifications - Director - The qualifications required for both the Chief Seed Certification Officer and the Director are the same - Once the petitioner was validly appointed as the Chief Seed Certification Officer, he is qualified for the Director position.
[Para 3]
Haryana State Seed Certification Agency Service Rules, 1979, Rule 6 - Appointment of Director - Jurisdiction of the Government - The Governing Board is the appointing authority for the Director position under Rule 6 - however, the State Government made the appointment without their jurisdiction - the appointment is declared illegal and quashed.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 & 226 - Public employment - Appointing Authority - Public employment appointments must follow the doctrine of 'equality' outlined in Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - any interference in public appointments by the government, politicians, or high-ups in the executive must be taken seriously and checked.
