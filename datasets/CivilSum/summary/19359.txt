Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Migration - Specific condition - Acceptance of migration with a condition of 18 months study in transferee College, cannot be reverted back and challenged by the petitioner - Total migration subject to all conditions including 18 months study - Promotees must receive one increment of higher responsibility.
[Para 2]
Migration - not a right but a privilege - Conditions apply to the benefit - Constitution of India, Articles 226/227.
