The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227, along with the Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 2 Rule 2, deal with termination of a contract period.
In this particular case, the petitioner was not qualified to hold the post, and the petition was filed after significant delay.
Additionally, the initial appointment was not made through selection, and the petitioner's name was not sponsored by the Employment Exchange.
Therefore, it was held that the petitioner did not have any legitimate cause for grievance which could be remedied through the present petition.
