Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Natural justice - Enquiry - A fair and proper enquiry must be conducted - Bias - Enquiry officer - In case of expressed opinion or lack of neutrality, the person appointed as Enquiry Officer should be changed - If an Enquiry Officer has already expressed their opinion on the matter, the enquiry cannot be said to be fair and there may be potential bias - The principle of justice being done and appearing to be done applies not only to judicial proceedings but also to administrative actions.
