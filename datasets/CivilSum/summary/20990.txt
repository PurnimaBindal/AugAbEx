The Haryana government has prescribed upgraded pay scale for librarians possessing specific qualifications.
The preferential qualification of M.Lib.
was originally required to be first or second class, but this requirement has been relaxed.
However, this relaxation does not apply to any other basic prescribed qualifications.
It is important to note that if an employee has been given the upgraded pay scale due to a misinterpretation of the relaxation, they will not be entitled to continue in the new pay scale without the requisite qualifications.
Additionally, the principle of equal pay for equal work is not applicable in this situation.
[Paras 4 and 5]
If an upgraded pay scale is released to an employee due to a mistake or misinterpretation of instructions, and there is no misrepresentation or fraud on the part of the employee, any amount paid may not be recovered from the employee.
However, the employee will no longer be entitled to the upgraded pay scale.
