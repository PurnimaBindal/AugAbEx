Direction was given by the Supreme Court in response to a petition filed by the All India Judges' Association seeking uniform conditions of service for its members in the country.
The directions include setting up an All India Judicial Service, bringing about uniformity in the designation of officers, raising the retirement age of judicial officers to 60 years, considering appropriate pay scales of judicial officers in Pay Commissions/Committees, providing a working library and sumptuary allowance, providing residential accommodation, providing State vehicles and pool vehicles for District Judges and Chief Judicial Magistrates, and, based on the nature of work, fixing the age of retirement.
[Paras 11-40, 65, and 19]
