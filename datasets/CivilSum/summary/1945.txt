Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Education - Polytechnic Course - Admission - Petitioners who failed to earn 26 credit points in 1st and 2nd Semesters were not allowed to sit for the 3rd Semester examination - By interim order, they were allowed to take examinations - Their exam results were withheld due to pendency of writ - Petitioners claimed ignorance of 26 credit point scheme but this was not believed - Their contention that they had taken exam up to the Sixth Semester was also rejected and the petition was dismissed.
[Paras 15, 16 and 17]
