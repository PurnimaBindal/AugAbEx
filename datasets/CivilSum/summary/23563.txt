Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertaking) Act, 1980, Section 9 - New Bank of India (Amalgamation and Transfer of Undertaking) Scheme, 1993 - New Bank of India (Determination of Placement of Employees (Officers and Workmen) of the New Bank of India in Punjab National Bank) Scheme, 1993, Clauses 4(a)(iii) and 4(b)(ii) - Promotion - Amalgamation of NBI with PNB - Promotion of NBI employees - Placement Scheme - The Promotion Scheme requires that years of service in the transferor bank be computed in the ratio of 2:1 with one year of service in the transferee bank.
Seniority of employees is determined without any hearing.

The Court cannot interfere with the Promotion Scheme.
