Constitution of India, Article 14 - Indian Penal Code, Sections 279 and 336 - Termination/Moral Turpitude - Commission of an offence under Sections 279/336 warrants termination of services for 'Moral Turpitude' - Alleged offences by petitioner not amounting to 'Moral Turpitude' - Termination of services is not justified - Order quashed - 1992(1) RSJ 175 upheld.
