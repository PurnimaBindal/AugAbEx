The Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 47 allows for the reemployment of disabled employees, even in cases where there are no available posts.
[Paras 17 & 18]
Section 47 of the Disability Act pertains to employees who acquire a disability during their service and prohibits their removal from service on the grounds of disability, regardless of the degree of disability.
This section is distinct from other chapters of the Act that aim to facilitate the employment of persons with disabilities who have yet to find a job.
[Paras 19 & 20]
Service conditions and other laws cannot preclude adherence to the provisions set forth in the Disability Act, particularly when it comes to the reemployment of disabled employees under Section 47.
[Paras 21 to 23]
The Authority established under the Disability Act functions as a quasi-judicial Tribunal with the power to issue binding decisions on establishments and government bodies regarding the reemployment of disabled individuals under Section 47.
