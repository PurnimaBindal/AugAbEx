Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 11 - Termination/Res-Judicata/Estoppel - The court found the closure and termination of the services of the petitioners to be legally valid and justified - Therefore, the second writ petition filed by the petitioners to challenge the order of termination is not maintainable - Even if a new ground is now raised, it will be deemed to have been raised and rejected.
