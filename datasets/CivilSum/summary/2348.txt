As per the Kerala Education Act of 1958, Sections 7 and 51A, the Appointing Authority cannot be validly appointed by himself.
However, the appellant argues that since the Manager who appointed the petitioner was not legally appointed, the appointments made by him are illegal and irregular.
Rule 9 specifies that appointment orders shall not be affected by a change in Educational Agency or Manager.
Accordingly, it is held that even if there is illegality or irregularity in the appointment of Manager, any actions taken by him during his tenure would not be considered illegal or irregular.
All rights and liabilities stemming from the actions would be saved by the Defacto doctrine, and therefore the appeal is dismissed.
