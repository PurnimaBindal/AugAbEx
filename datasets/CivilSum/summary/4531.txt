The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Writ jurisdiction - Alternative remedy - Retrenchment - According to the ruling, the workman must first take advantage of a statutory remedy that is efficacious and adequate before seeking the discretionary remedy under Article 226 of the Constitution - It is not the intention of Article 226 of the Constitution to evade statutory procedures - Therefore, the writ petition is held to be not maintainable.
