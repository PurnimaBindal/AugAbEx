Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Ad hoc appointment - Seniority - Petitioners initially appointed as General Deputy Medical Officer Grade-II on an ad hoc basis, with the understanding that the appointments would last for six months or until regular appointments were made through UPSC - The appointments were subject to termination at any time without notice by the competent authority - The petitioners were advised to pass the U.P.S.C examination for regular appointment - Though their services were extended from time to time, they were not considered regular until passing the U.P.S.C examination - The officiation in such posts cannot be taken into account for fixation of seniority - The petitioners' seniority should be calculated from the date they passed the U.P.S.C examination, not from the date of their initial appointment.
