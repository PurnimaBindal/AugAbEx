According to Punjab Financial Rules, specifically Rule 7.15, appointment and fixation of pay must be in accordance with the pay scale.
Those who have accepted a position in a lower grade cannot later claim a higher grade and are estopped from challenging the terms and conditions outlined in their order of appointment.
This is stated in paragraphs 9 and 10.

Rule 7.15 of the Punjab Financial Rules discusses laches and representation.
Constantly representing a claim cannot be considered laches and be rejected, as stated in paragraph 9.
