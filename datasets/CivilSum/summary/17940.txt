Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India - Dependents and Pension - Para 34 of instructions issued by Government of India, Department of Pension - Parents or parent of a government servant who dies as a bachelor or widower are eligible for Pensioners' Welfare.
[Paras 5 and 7] B.
Pension Regulations for Army, 1961 - Dependents' Pension - In case of death of a government servant as a bachelor or widower, his parents, brother, and sisters are entitled to the dependents' pension.
[Paras 5 and 7] C. Pension - Dependents' pension - Government servant dying as a bachelor - Parents, brothers, and sisters are qualified for the pension under liberalized pension policy of the Government.
[Para 7] D. Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, Para 7(b) - Disease during service - If there is no note of any disease recorded at the time of entry into service, and the death occurred due to some disease, then the disease is considered as attributable to and aggravated by military service.
