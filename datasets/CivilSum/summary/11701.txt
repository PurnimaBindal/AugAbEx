The respondent in this case was seeking compensation and regularisation after being permanently disabled in an accident while bringing a consignment for the State of Punjab.
They submitted a copy of a Policy Decision issued by the Punjab Government where many work charged, ad hoc, and daily wage workers were ordered to be regularised, subject to fulfilling specific conditions.
As per the Code of Public Works Department, Rule 1127, Chapter-1, the appellants were directed to examine the case of the respondent in light of the said Policy Decision, taking into account Article 16 of the Constitution of India.
