Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 22-A - Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Retirement Benefits Scheme, 1992, Clause 6(6) - Qualifying service - The employee's period of service that qualifies for benefits begins on the date of contribution to the CPF as per Clause 6(6).
The petitioner had CPF contributions beginning on July 29, 1991, and it cannot be attributed to the petitioner's disadvantage that CPF deductions were not made from the date of appointment of October 16, 1989.
The court interpreted Clause 6(6) in accordance with the precedent set in Vasant Gangaramsa Chandan's case, stating that the period of qualifying service begins with either continuous employment or the earlier date of contributions made by the employer towards the CPF.
Consequently, the denial of pension benefits is illegal.
