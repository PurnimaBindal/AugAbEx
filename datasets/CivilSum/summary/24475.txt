Constitution of India, Articles 141, 215, 212, 226 and 368 - Amendment of law - Judicial precedents - Legislatures can always change the law retrospectively as long as the premise on which an earlier judgement was declared invalid is removed - Such a change will be a fundamental alteration in the circumstances and cannot be challenged on the basis of judicial powers.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 162 - Revision pay of scales - Cut off date - Pension - The Government may fix a cut-off date while granting benefits as long as the reasons behind it are rational - Financial constraints can be a valid reason for introducing a cut-off date while implementing a revised pension scheme - The Government can refuse to pay arrears from a back date on the grounds of financial constraints.
