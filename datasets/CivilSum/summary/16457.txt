Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1973, Rule 50 - Retiral benefits - If no other legal heirs are available, the legitimate sister of the deceased is entitled to receive retiral benefits - However, the petitioner is not entitled to retiral benefits without obtaining a succession certificate - The question of the petitioner being a legitimate heir can only be decided in succession proceedings - Therefore, a writ is not maintainable.
