Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8(8) - Departmental enquiry/Natural justice - Assistance of retired employee during departmental proceedings - The enquiry officer cannot refuse permission if the competent authority allows a delinquent to receive assistance from a retired employee - The conduct of enquiry officer violates Rule 8(8) of the Rules and principles of natural justice, which invalidates the proceedings.

Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rules 5 and 8 - Departmental proceedings/Delay/Show cause notice - Once a delinquent official submits his explanation to the show-cause notice, the disciplinary authority must take action within a reasonable time - If there is no action taken, it will be presumed that the proceedings have been dropped - Final show-cause notice to a delinquent official must be issued within a reasonable time after the inquiry report receipt - The continuous hanging of democles sword affects the person's career progress.
