The Petitioner Association is seeking the regularisation of part-time employees under the A.P.
(Regulation of Appointments to Public Services and Rationalization of Staff Pattern and Pay Structure) Act of 1994.
While GOMs No. 112 set out a requirement of 10 years of continuous working, GOMs 212 applies to full-time employees.
A learned Single Judge erred in his decision by applying GOMs 212 to regularize part-time employees, without considering whether Section 7(1) of 1994 Act was unconstitutional and violated Articles 14, 21, 39(d) and 43 of the Constitution.
