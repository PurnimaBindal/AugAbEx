Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Termination - The petitioner obtained an ex parte stay order through untrue statements, and was reinstated based on the court's orders - However, this does not grant him eligibility for regularisation.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation - The petitioner worked as a beldar on a daily wage basis for varying periods of time, but was not employed at the time of filing the writ petition - Additionally, false statements were made claiming he was still employed - Therefore, the petitioner cannot use equitable jurisdiction under Article 226.
