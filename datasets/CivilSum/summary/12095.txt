The Railway Protection Force Act, 1957, Sections 9 and 21 - Railway Protection Force Rules, 1959, Rules 23, 25 and 41 - Probation - Termination of service - The appellant was appointed on probation with the understanding that his services could be terminated in accordance with Rules 23 and 25 of the Railway Protection Force Rules - The appointing authority passed the termination order in accordance with Rule 25 - This termination cannot be seen as a penalty - As a result, the second appeal has been rejected.
