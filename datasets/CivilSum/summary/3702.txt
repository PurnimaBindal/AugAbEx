[Paras 14 & 15]  B Rule - Experts - The interpretation of a rule by experts should not be alleged to have misled them, especially when there is no conflict between the rule and the advertisement and there is no dispute about the eminence and impartiality of the experts.
[Paras 15 & 17] C. Pleadings and evidence - It is not sustainable in the eye of the law if the findings are de-hors the pleadings and there is no evidence to sustain them.
[Paras 17 & 28] D. Selection - The assessment of merit by experts cannot be interfered unless it is shown to be erroneous or vitiated by any infirmity.
[Para 18] E. Equivalence of degrees - In the matter of selection, the opinion of experts assumes great importance, where there is a question of equivalence of qualifications involved.
The court will not interfere with the findings of experts in the absence of any established allegation of partisan or bias.
[Paras 19 and 23] F. Rule - In the matter of construction of rules, the court should not take an unduly technical view without considering the intention and object thereof.
[Para 24] G. Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 14 - Writ petition - A writ petition by an aggrieved party is maintainable against the judgement of a Tribunal, and the State and Selection Commission are performa parties.
