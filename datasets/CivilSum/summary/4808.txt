As per Section 11(a) of the Industrial Disputes Act, if a Labour Court finds that there is enough evidence in the enquiry file to support the charges against the workman, a writ court cannot interfere with this finding under Article 226, unless it is shown to be baseless or unreasonable.
This is applicable even if the enquiry report was not provided with the second show cause notice.
[Paras 11 to 13]
