Various legal principles and cases are discussed, such as the orders of the Governor during the President's Rule in a state being binding, departmental proceedings for employees, fairness in consideration for promotions, and the validity of adverse remarks.
The importance of fairness and non-biasness in the government's decisions is emphasized, and specific situations where bias or unfairness occur are discussed.
The court's role in ensuring justice and fairness in promotions is also discussed, as well as the need for the doctrine of necessity in cases where biasness is present.
