A person who is hired on a contractual basis cannot demand regularisation in service in accordance with Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226.
[Paras 1, 16, & 18]
If a person is recruited in violation of constitutional requirements in terms of Articles 14 and 16 and statutory recruitment rules, such appointments are null and void as backdoor entry.
Thus, the person cannot claim regularisation in service under Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226.
[Paras 1 & 17]
To determine whether an unskilled office worker appointed on a post was out of the 1959 Act mandate, one needs to plead and prove the fact.
[Para 12]
If an appointment was made without following the Rules, which is a nullity, regularisation is not a mode of appointment, and the confirmation of an employee after the expiry of the prescribed probation period will not arise under Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226.
[Para 16]
An appointment made without following any procedure prescribed, without advertising the posts, and without there being any existing post, even if it is on the basis of the person's own application, is illegal and irregular.
It cannot be regularised under Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226.
[Paras 16 & 18]
