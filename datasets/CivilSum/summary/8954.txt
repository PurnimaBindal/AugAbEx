Industrial Disputes Act, Section 33-C(2) and Sections 10 and 11-A - Back wages - Re-instatement - The Labour Court has deemed the termination of a workman as illegal - Workman who has joined another employer during the pendency of the reference is entitled to compensation for the period she remained unemployed - She will not have to join with the management.
