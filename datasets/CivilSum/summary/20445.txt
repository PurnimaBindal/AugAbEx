Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion/Deputation - Ad hoc promotion - An employee on deputation has the right to be evaluated for promotion in their parent department - Their failure to be promoted while on deputation does not impact their eligibility for promotion in their parent department - They are even entitled to ad hoc promotions from the date their juniors were promoted in their parent department.
