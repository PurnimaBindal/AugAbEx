Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(4) - Jurisdiction - The Labour Court can examine matters incidental to an industrial dispute if they are essential for the final resolution of the dispute under Section 10(4).
This includes determining whether an employee's service was terminated illegally or unjustifiably.
If there is a dispute surrounding the existence of an employer-employee relationship, then it would also be a matter related to the dispute under consideration.
