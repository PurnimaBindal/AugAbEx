Article 226 of the Indian Constitution, Sections 17 and 29 of the Regional Rural Bank Act of 1976, and Rule 4 of the Regional Rural Bank (Appointment and promotion of Officers and other Employees) Rules of 1988 dictate that promotion to the position of Area/Senior Manager may only occur after an officer has served for eight years and is selected based on seniority-cum-merit via interview and an appraisal of their performance from the previous three years.
However, a 1996 circular which only allowed for 30 marks out of a total of 100 marks to be assigned based on seniority and only enabled promotion for those who achieved the highest marks was determined to be contrary to the concept of seniority-cum-merit.
The circular was deemed invalid and the selection committee was ordered to reconvene and conduct a new selection process that adheres to the principles of seniority as outlined in BV Sivaliah's case.
