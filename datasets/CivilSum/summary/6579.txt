Constitution of India, Article 226/227 - Resolutions No. 666 dated 6111978 and No. 802 dated 311984 - Retirement - Superannuation age - Despite Resolution dated 6111978 entitling the petitioner to continue in MCD until the age of 60, he was retired after completing 58 years in contravention of the resolution - MCD acted unlawfully by creating discrimination amongst equally placed persons in light of Resolution dated 311984 stating the benefit of age increase for teachers appointed after 291983 - While reinstatement is not possible as the petitioner has exceeded 60 years, he does have the right to financial and other related benefits.
[Paras 3 to 7]
