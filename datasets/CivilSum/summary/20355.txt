According to Section 15(1)(b) of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Panchayat Secretaries appointed under the Panchayat Act and being governed by the Civil Services Rules of the State are regarded as State Government Servants.
As such, they fall within the meaning of individuals appointed in civil services of the State on civil posts under the State.
Thus, the Tribunal has the authority to address concerns regarding the termination of a Panchayat Secretary's services.
