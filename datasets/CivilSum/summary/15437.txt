Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Sections 25 F, 25G, 25H - Termination - Petitioner, who was appointed on a permanent post, has been terminated from service - Petitioner contends that this violates provisions of Act - However, the court ruled that since the petitioner has not completed 240 days of service, there cannot be said to be any violation of sections 25F and G of Act.
