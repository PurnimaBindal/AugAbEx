Under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 demand equal pay for equal work, pay scales, and the equation of pay scales.
This State Government decided to equate the pay scales of English teachers working in the ITIs with the Masters/Mistresses of Education Department, leaving their pay scales unchanged since 111978.
Thus, the English teachers working in ITIs must not be denied the benefit of pay scale revision.
If the pay scales are equated for the teachers of two departments of the State Govt., they are entitled to the same pay scale revisals in the future.
Depriving them of this benefit would breach Article 16 and equality of opportunity in employment.
The court directed the Government to release the petitioners' salary from 111978 in the revised pay scale.
