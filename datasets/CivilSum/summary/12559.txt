Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Termination of services of a regular employee - Even if the termination is due to the abolition of the post, the employer must issue a show cause notice before terminating the services of a regular employee.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - State - Co-operative Society - A wholly owned, financed and controlled Co-operative Society by the Government falls under the definition of "State" in Article 12 of the Constitution of India and is subject to writ jurisdiction.
