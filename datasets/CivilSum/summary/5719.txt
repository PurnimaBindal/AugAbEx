Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and laches - Termination of services - Representation against termination sent to the Govt. with recommendations by the Corporation Authorities - Long delay in rejecting representations and non-effectiveness of resolution - Writ application filed within two months of rejection - Principle of acquiescence not applicable - Principle of fairness must be applied - Third party's interest accrued can stretch delay and laches - Only inordinate delay can be fatal in case of terminations for equitable remedy under Article 226 since it affects public exchequer if reinstatement happens - Denial of relief on delay and acquiescence basis not justified because the Corporation is a public authority.
