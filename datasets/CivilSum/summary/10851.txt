Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Revised pay scale - The Railway Board issued a circular on 13583 to revise the pay scale of non-statutory canteen employees in response to Supreme Court's directions.
The revised pay scale was made equivalent to that of statutory Canteen Manager Grade-I The respondent will receive the revised pay scale from 161982, but arrears will only be paid from three years prior to the filing of the original application.
A representation was made in 1988, which was disposed of on 10791, and the original application was filed in 1995.
