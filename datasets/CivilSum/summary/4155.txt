Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Section 12(5) - Industrial dispute - Reference to Tribunal - The State Government's refusal to refer the dispute must be based on germane reasons and indicate application of mind, as the action is purely administrative in nature.
The High Court cannot sit in appeal and adjudge the sufficiency and adequacy of the reasons but can scan and dissect within the permissible parameters of law the mode and manner of exercise of authority by the Government.
In this case, the reasons given for the refusal to refer the dispute are not germane to the issue, dearth of application of mind, and in certain aspects, touch the realm merits of the case beyond prima facie merit of the dispute.
Certain reasons are vague, while others indicate an unwillingness to solve disputes by way of reference.
Consequently, the order is unsustainable and quashed in exercise of writ jurisdiction.
[Paras 11, 12, 15, and 16]
