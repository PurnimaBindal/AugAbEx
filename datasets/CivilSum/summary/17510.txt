Gujarat Non-Secretariat Clerks and Clerical Typists (Training and Examination) Rules, 1970 - Appointment/Termination - Petitioners appointed as Junior Clerks on compassionate ground filed writ petition to challenge their termination order.
Respondent argued that petitioners failed to pass required examination in specified chances as per Rule 6.
Held, according to Rule 8(i), if a candidate fails to pass the Post Training Examination as required by the Rules, he becomes ineligible for appointment to regular staff.
The order of Government to terminate their services is not illegal.
[Para 5]

Gujarat Non-Secretariat Clerks and Clerical typists (Training and Examination) Rules, 1970, Rules 3, 5, 6 and 8 - Compensate appointment - Termination.
Petitioners filed a writ petition on the condition that their services can't be terminated till all the additional chances exhausted by them.
The scheme for giving compassionate appointment states no concession in service matters to be granted to the compassionate appointees.
Petitioners cannot claim to continue in service after failing to clear the examination.
Further held, they cannot claim any exemption from the provisions of the rules enabling the government to terminate their services on failure to clear the examination as required by the rules in specified chances.
[Para 6]
