State Rural Development Agency Employees Service Regulations, 1983 - Abolition of Post - The employer cannot be directed to continue employing employees whose posts were created for a limited duration project that has ended due to fulfilment or lack of funds.
Such a direction would constitute the creation of posts not required by the employer and funded by the employer without available funds.
The decision to abolish posts by the respondent-State was made in good faith.
