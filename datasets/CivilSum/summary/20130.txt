Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Deputationist/Absorption - The appellant worked without interruption in the Food Department on deputation and received promotions from 1967 to 1986.
However, when the department attempted to repatriate them, an administrative tribunal ordered the appellant's absorption into the same department on January 28, 1988.
It would be highly unfair to send the appellant back, and the tribunal was incorrect in interfering with the department's order of absorption, which was issued on the tribunal's own instructions.
