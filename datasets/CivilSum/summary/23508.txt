Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay - Recognition of equal pay for equal work - The duty of the government to provide teachers with equal pay scales whether they are employed by government schools or recognized private schools.
The aided schools perform similar functions to those of government schools and are approved by the state.
The teachers employed by these schools teach the same syllabus, prescribe the same course material and participate in examinations similar to those taken by their government school counterparts.
Disciplinary actions against teachers in both government and aided schools are administered according to the rules and regulations framed by the State Government.
The court held that the teachers employed by aided schools should be entitled to the same salaries, benefits, and so on as those employed by government schools.
[Paras 8,9,and 10]


Constitution of India, Article 21 - Right to education - Grant-in-aid - The Government is obliged to provide free education to children until they reach the age of 14 and cannot evade this Constitutional duty on the grounds of lack of economic capacity.
The Government must grant aid to schools as laid out in the rules.
The High Court directed the State of HP to pay 95% of the authorized grant-in-aid from February 1988, and this judgment was modified by the Supreme Court to apply to aided schools from April 1, 1993.
[Paras 15 to 18]
