The Constitution of India, Article 19(1)(a) and 19(2) and Right to Information Act, 2005 Section 6 provide that candidates have a fundamental right to seek information regarding the criterion and procedure of selection adopted by the selection committee.
[Para 4]
It is well-established that the selection criterion must be laid down before the commencement of the selection process, and no changes can be made thereafter.
In case the criterion for allocation of marks is not made known, candidates and the selection process are likely to be adversely affected.
[Para 5]
Not only the candidate, but any taxpayer/citizen is entitled to know how public servants are selected, as transparency in the recruitment process prevents corruption and promotes impartiality.
[Para 5]
Precedents prior to the Right to Information Act, 2005, will not be applicable to cases arising after that.
[Para 6]
Under Sections 8 and 11 of the Right to Information Act, 2005, the authority can decline to supply privileged information, but it is bound to supply all other information.
[Paras 6 and 7]
An interim order staying the operation of an order passed in a similar case cannot be used as a precedent in any subsequent similar case without recording reasons.
[Para 8]
