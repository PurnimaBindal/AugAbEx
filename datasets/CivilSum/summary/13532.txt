Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Rajasthan Class IV Service (Recruitment and other Service Conditions) Rules, 1963, Rules 14, 15 and 16 - Selection - Deficiencies in selection - Appointments - Termination based on deficiencies in the process of selection - Deficiencies in not calling the names from Employment Exchange, giving appointments during the period of ban imposed by the Government, not publishing advertisement in newspapers, not putting notice on the notice board and deficiencies in conducting interview - None of the fault attributed to the selected persons - Deficiencies a matter between the Government and the authorities - Government can refuse to accept the selection and decline appointments based on deficiencies in the selection, however, appointments made on the basis of such selection cannot be terminated.
