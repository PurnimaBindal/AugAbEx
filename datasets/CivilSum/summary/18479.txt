Constitution of India, Articles 16 & 226 - Village Panchayat Secretary (Class III) (Panchayat Service) Recruitment Rules, 1988, Rule 2 - Promotion - Selection - Being included in the select list does not guarantee an indefeasible right for the candidate.
The framing of rules changing the mode of selection to direct selection means all posts to be filled up after the rules came into force will be filled by direct selection.
