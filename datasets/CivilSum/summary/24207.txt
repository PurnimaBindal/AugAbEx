Constitution of India, Articles 2, 153, 309, 315, 320 and 323 - UP Reorganisation Act, 2000, Sections 2(e), 3, 78 and 86 - Appointment - State Public Service Commission - Impact of reorganisation of State into two States on recommendation made by Commission - Selection made by UPPSC before formation of State of Uttranchal is not obligatory on State of Uttranchal - State of Uttranchal did not agree to appointment of joint Public Service Commission nor requested UPSC to serve its need under Article 315(A) - Simply because recommendations received by erstwhile State of Uttar Pradesh had been sent to State of Uttranchal and they were not reversed by Governor for being placed with reasons before Assembly of State of Uttar Pradesh under Section 78 of Reorganisation Act, it cannot be declared that recommendations made by UPPSC were binding on Govt. of Uttranchal.
[Paras 26, 28 & 29]
