Constitution of India, 1950, Article 311 - Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, Section 47 - Premature retirement on medical grounds - The Medical Board's decision for premature retirement of a driver, who had an accident not during his service, is unsustainable regardless whether the accident happened during or outside of employment.
Relying on 2000(2) SCT 318 (Delhi) [Paras 12 and 13].
