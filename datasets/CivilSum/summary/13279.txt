Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - The petitioner, who was discharged from service in a lower medical category 'C' with 30% disability at the age of 45 on 31.10.1985, must receive a service element.
Although his claim for disability pension was denied on 2911987, a presumption in favor of the petitioner must be made that his disease was contracted while serving in the army.
The petitioner is entitled to disability element only for the period of 38 months before the writ petition was filed.
