Indian Administrative Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1955, Regulations 5(2) & 5(3) - Consideration for promotion - The list for consideration must include the required number of officers based on the number of anticipated vacancies plus 20% multiplied by three, with a provision for candidates above the age of 54 who were not eligible otherwise but whose names appeared in the previous select list or were deprived of consideration due to non-holding of meetings.
The persons specified under Regulation 5(3) must be considered in addition to those under Regulation 5(2).
