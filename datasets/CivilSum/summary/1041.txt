Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2 and 25-F - Illegal retrenchment - Compensation in lieu of reinstatement - Workman is deemed to be in continuous service and entitled to full back wages if retrenchment is held null and void - Compensation cannot be less than full back wages - Absence of evidence of gainful employment during termination means nominal compensation without basis cannot be awarded - Workman entitled to full back wages.
