Response: K. Laxminarayan Rao, Advocate argues that when it comes to disciplinary proceedings, criminal proceedings, and civil proceedings that are initiated on the same set of facts, the disciplinary proceedings must be stayed to prevent affecting the defense of the delinquent in other ongoing proceedings.
Furthermore, the fact that trial in the criminal case has not yet commenced is not a valid ground to refuse to stay the disciplinary proceedings, and disciplinary and criminal proceedings initiated on the same set of facts cannot be allowed to go on simultaneously.
The court should stay the disciplinary proceedings regardless of whether or not they have already been initiated.
[Para 31, 13]
