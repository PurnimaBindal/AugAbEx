Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol I Part I (as applicable to Haryana), Rule 2.60 and Constitution of India, Article 16 and 226 - Revised pay scale - Unrevised scale of 2500-2750 merged into revised pay scale of Rs. 5900-6700 - Equal duties and responsibilities - No disparity in pay scales - Post of Additional Chief Electoral Officer and Engineer-in-chief of equal nature - No difference made between the two posts in the pay scales - Non-grant of same revised pay scale of Rs. 7300-7600 an act of invidious discrimination and violates Article 16.

Constitution of India, Article 14, 16 and 226 - Judicial review - Jurisdiction - The decision on revising the pay scale of one post and refusal of the State Government to revise the pay scale of another post is always subject to judicial review of the High Court to be tested on the ground of discrimination and arbitrariness.
[Para 7] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Identical time scales - Equal duties and responsibilities - If two posts in different departments carry the same pay scale, their duties and responsibility have to be treated at par and equal for all intents and purposes.
