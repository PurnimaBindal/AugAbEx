Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Rajasthan Subordinate Offices, Ministerial Staff Rules, 1957, Rule 27 (xi-a) (as added vide notification) - Seniority - Determination - Appellants who were transferred on request after 2 years and prior to notification were denied the benefit of service rendered by them prior to the transfer.
The notification stated that the inter-se-seniority of a person transferred on request shall be determined from the date of joining the new department.
However, since the applicability of the notification was prospective, the respondents' action was discriminatory, unreasonable, and violative of Article 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
The appellant is entitled to have his seniority counted from the date of his initial appointment and also to get seniority in the new unit from the date he submitted his transfer application.
Therefore, the court has directed to amend the seniority list given.
