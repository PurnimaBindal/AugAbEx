Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Employment Exchange (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1954, Section 4(1) - Selection - Appointment - No selection panel was formed, and only the list of candidates who passed the written and type test was displayed.
A note attached to the list was immediately withdrawn when it was discovered that the Employment Exchange had to be notified of vacancies.
As a result, the petitioners have no right to seek appointment.
The Employment Exchange would have sent the names of 2,800 candidates had 140 vacancies been notified, but only 2000 names were sent as just 99 vacancies were notified.
Granting relief to the petitioners would deprive 800 candidates of the chance to be considered.
The petition was therefore dismissed.
