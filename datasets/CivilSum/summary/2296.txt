Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation - Policy - Length of ad hoc service - Consideration may be given to long years of ad hoc/temporary service for regularising an employee, provided that their initial appointment was not against the rules, they fulfill the prescribed qualifications, and the Rules permit regularisation.
Additionally, the employee must be found fit on consideration as per criteria of regularisation.
If the authority was directed to frame a policy and consider the ad hoc/temporary employees for regularisation and failed to do so, discontinuing the service of a long-term (18 yrs.)
ad hoc employee without considering him for regularisation in compliance with court orders, would be arbitrary and unfair, and would result in a person losing their livelihood due to age restrictions.
The authority has been directed to consider the case of the petitioner and regularize their entire period of service if they are found fit.
[Paras 24 to 28]
