Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(C)(2) - Jurisdiction of Labour Court - Employer-employee relations - An application filed under Section 33(C)(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act - The Labour Court is not authorized to address the matter regarding the applicant's workman status - The issue of jurisdictional fact is relevant - This matter is not a minor aspect of the proceedings.
