Service jurisprudence - Disciplinary proceedings - Preliminary enquiry - Bias - According to service jurisprudence, a preliminary inquiry must be conducted and explanation asked from the employee who commits an illegality or irregularity during his tenure - Deviation from this principle brings bias or over-jealousness.
