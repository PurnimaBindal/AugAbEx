The candidate's appointment must be evaluated based on the rules in place during their appointment.
The petitioner applied for the post of Lecturer in English despite not meeting the qualifications required.
However, they were appointed on an ad hoc basis with the opportunity to acquire the necessary qualifications.
After one extension, their ad hoc appointment was not continued, and they had no right to demand continued service.
Even after obtaining a PhD, they did not respond to subsequent advertisements for the post.
As their ad hoc appointment period had ended, there was no reason to consider them a Lecturer.
Thus, their petition challenging the termination of their services and the advertisement was not valid.
