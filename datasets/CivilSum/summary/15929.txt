Constitution of India Article 16 - Punjab Government National Emergency Concession Rules Rule 4 - Benefit of Military Service - A petitioner who received military service benefits as a clerk in the sub-office of the Horticulture Department was later appointed as a clerk in the Head office of Horticulture Department, but did not meet the necessary qualifications - Despite being treated as on deputation with the Head office and receiving promotions, no relaxation was given in the appointment process - As a result, the petitioner is not entitled to receive benefits of military service for the Head Office position and lacks the basic minimum qualifications required for the post.
