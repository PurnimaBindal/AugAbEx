The order of the authority cancelling the appointment of a teacher based on police verification pertaining to criminal cases instituted against him is just and proper as per Constitution of India, Article 311.
Relevant cases 2003(3) SCT 762 (SC), 1997(1) SCT 474 (SC) and 2008(1) SCT 20 : 2007(6) RAJ 402 (SC) have been referred to in Para 11.
