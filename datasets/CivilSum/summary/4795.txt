Punjab Civil Service (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, Rule 14(11) - Order of punishment - Failure to follow Rule 14(11) - The decision of the appellate Court, that the investigation was compromised due to non-compliance of Rule 14(11), is a factual finding and cannot be challenged in a Regular Second Appeal - Additionally, upsetting the settled position after such a long period (since the punishment was given in 1980 and the appeal was accepted in 1986) would be unjust.
