Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay Scale - Reduction of Pay Scale - Natural justice - Opportunity of hearing - Orders detrimental to the service conditions of the petitioners, their pension, and retirement benefits - The respondent was obligated to follow the principles of natural justice before making the orders - These orders resulted in a reduction of the petitioners' pay scale and consequential reduction in their emoluments and retirement benefits - The orders had serious civil consequences - The court ruled in favor of the Special Civil Application, allowing for the order dated August 19, 1997, Annexure 'H', August 19, 1997, Annexure 'I,' and October 3, 1997, Annexure 'J' to be quashed and set aside - The ruling does not prevent the respondents from passing appropriate orders in accordance with the law after having given notice and an opportunity to the petitioners.
