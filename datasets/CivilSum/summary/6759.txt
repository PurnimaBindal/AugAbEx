Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Departmental promotion proceedings - If an employee is superseded by his juniors due to suspension and departmental proceedings, he is entitled to be considered for promotion once he is exonerated and his salary must be fixed on the promoted post from the date he is found fit for promotion.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Arrears of pay - Disciplinary proceedings that withhold an employee's promotion do not limit his right to claim it after exoneration.
However, claims for arrears of salary beyond 38 months prior to the date of filing suit will be time-barred.
