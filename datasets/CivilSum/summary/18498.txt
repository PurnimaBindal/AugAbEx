Constitution of India, Article 226 - Enrolment in Army - National Sports Talents Contestants Scheme - A scheme designed to recruit exceptional sportspeople into the Army, subject to meeting required physical standards - The petitioner's application was denied due to not meeting these standards - It is not possible to relax physical requirements for recruitment into the defence sector - Successful completion of education and sports training under the scheme does not automatically grant enrolment in the Army, nor appointment to a clerk position without advertisement.
