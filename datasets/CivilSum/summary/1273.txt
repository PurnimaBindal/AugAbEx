The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and Customs Act, 1962, Section 108, deal with reinstatement and granting backwages.
In a case where a workman was involved in smuggling a consignment and had sought reference on discharge, but the government declined to refer the dispute, the High Court quashed criminal proceedings against the worker.
The workman again sought reference, which was decided in his favor.
However, because the workman sought reference eleven years after the order declining reference and was embroiled in serious criminal allegations, despite their quashing, he is not entitled to backwages.
