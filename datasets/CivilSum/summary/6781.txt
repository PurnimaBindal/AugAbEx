Civil Procedure Code, Order 1 Rule 10 - Necessary parties - Proper party - A petition was filed by the petitioner to be impleaded as a co-respondent in an appeal, with the right to argue the appeal but not lead any evidence.
The petitioner's services were terminated by the management alongside two others who were already respondents in the appeal.
The Director granted relief to all three by setting aside the termination order in an appeal filed by the other two.
The suit against the Director's order was dismissed, but prima facie, the petitioner also had a right vested in him by the Director's order.
While no relief was claimed against him, if the appeal of the management was allowed to set aside the Director's order, the petitioner would be directly affected.
Although not necessary, he is definitely a proper party.
The order of the lower appellate court that disallowed the petitioner's application under Order 1 Rule 10 was set aside, and the petitioner was ordered to be impleaded as a co-respondent.
