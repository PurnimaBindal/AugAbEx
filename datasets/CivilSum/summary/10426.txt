Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ jurisdiction proper forum - Writ Court - Claim for thermal and conveyance allowance - Labour Court award not to be implemented through Writ Court - Whether workmen not party to the award entitled to benefit is a disputed question of fact, not to be decided by Writ Court - If quantum is only questioned, relief must be sought under Section 33(c)(2) of ID Act - All questions are vague legal and factual questions - Petitioner must pursue remedies before proper forum.
