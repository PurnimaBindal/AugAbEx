Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Without notice - Appellant's service was terminated on the ground of defalcation of money belonging to Respondent, causing loss, and Appellant's failure to repay.
The Appellant did not challenge these assertions.
The court concluded that serving notice or showing cause would not have improved Appellant's case, as the allegation of defalcation had been admitted.
Furthermore, the court held that Appellant cannot be imposed on society, which works as a trustee of public money, as the Appellant approached the court after four years to disturb the settled position.
