Rajasthan Cooperative Societies Act, 1965, Section 128 - Delegation of power - The delegator and delegate may concurrently exercise power - Delegation confers authority upon someone else without denuding the delegator of authority - Power exercised by delegate is deemed to be exercised by the delegator - Delegator cannot exercise power of review/revision or appeal over the same.
[Paras 7-11] B Rajasthan Cooperative Societies Act, 1965, Sections 125 & 128 - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 244(2) - Appointing authority has absolute power to retire any employee in public interest after 25 years or 50 years of age - Previous notice is required.
[Para 13] C Rajasthan Cooperative Societies Act, 1965, Sections 124(2), 125 & 128 - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 244(2) - Powers of revision not interchangeable or equal between Registrar and State Government - Chapter XIII distinguishes between State Government and Registrar - Section 128 excludes matters covered by Section 125.
