Constitution of India, Articles 14, 15(4) - Admission - Reservation for Rural Area candidate - A Clause in Rural Area Certificate that mandates parents to file an affidavit of their earnings from the village is invalid - It is not reasonable to claim that all citizens residing in rural areas are socially and educationally backward - Therefore, such reservation is not sustainable - Reservation for rural area candidates is declared ultra vires the Constitution.
[Paras 15, 16 and 18]
