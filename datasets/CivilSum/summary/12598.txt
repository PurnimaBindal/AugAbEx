The petitioner filed a writ petition to challenge an order of reversion after a period of three years and two months in which he did not make any challenge to the order.
The suit was barred by limitation and it is not appropriate to exercise jurisdiction under Article 226.
[Para 8]
The petitioner's ad hoc promotion was periodically extended with commission approval while the government finalized its policy for filling regular positions.
When the commission refused to grant any further extension, the petitioner was reverted.
There is no infirmity in the reversion order.
[Para 11]
There is no inter se seniority between lecturers of two departments when appointing the head of a department.
The appointment of another lecturer to the post regularized his appointment as head of department.
[Para 12]
Mere fact that Govt. allowed the petitioner to continue his ad hoc appointment for many years cannot be used to confer any right on him to be treated as a regular employee.
The ad hoc appointment continued as the rules were not finalized.
Commission having refused to grant further permission for extension, Govt. had to order reversion of petitioner without violating principles of natural justice.
[Para 13]
The period of service on ad hoc basis counts for the grant of increments when fixing pay for an ad hoc promotion.
The petitioner's pay should be refixed in the revised pay scale of the Head of Department post.
The period of service rendered by petitioner as head of department must be taken into consideration while fixing his pay.
