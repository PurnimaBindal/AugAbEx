Constitution of India, Article 14 - Contract Act, Sec. 23 - Probation - Termination during probation - Employers have the right to judge the suitability of their employees by placing them on trial for a specified period.
In this regard, termination of service during the probation period cannot be considered as arbitrary or unfair.
The decision to retain or dismiss the employee is based on their performance and does not violate any public policy or rule.

Civil Procedure Code, Order 47 Rule 1 - Review - Scope of - It is not permissible to introduce new grounds to request a review of an order that was made based on the merits of the case.
The mere change of counsel does not entitle the petitioner to raise new grounds.
