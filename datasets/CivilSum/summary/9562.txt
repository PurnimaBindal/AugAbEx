Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer -  Adjustment of couple cases - Although petitioner and her family members are active in politics and there is contention that petitioner was not taking her duties seriously, she pleads that she and her husband separated on account of transfers.
However, allegations based on certificates annexures P-6 and P-7 cannot be accepted on face value due to complaints produced of doubtful origin, with no diary numbers recording and the lack of full addresses of complainants.
Consequently, the petition is allowed.
