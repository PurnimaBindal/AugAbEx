Fundamental Rules and Supplementary Rules, Rules 28, 29(3) - Disciplinary proceedings - Reduction to lower post - Fixation of seniority - The appellate authority changed the punishment of dismissal to reinstatement on a lower post while protecting the pay of the petitioner.
However, the period of reversion was not specified and the interim period was treated as a break in service.
Therefore, the petitioner should be placed as the senior-most Executive Assistant (lower post to which he was reverted) and should be considered for promotion based on seniority.
The only effect of the "break in service" would be that this period will not be counted for increments.
