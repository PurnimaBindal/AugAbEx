"Pension Regulations of Army 1940, Part II - Pension - The respondent is seeking pensionary benefits for the total service he rendered in the Army, which included breaks.
However, he was only given minimum pensionary benefits for his first 10 years and 192 days of service, and no pension for his subsequent 23 years of service.
It was decided that the respondent is entitled to pension for his entire period of service in the Army as the breaks in service were not his fault.
[Para 12]"
