The Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Equal pay for equal work - Classification - As long as it is based on qualifications and experience, a reasonable classification within a class of employees is permissible.
The principle of equal pay for equal work does not apply to unequal employees.
The laying down of selective tests and qualifications for a post in question is not precluded by Articles 14 & 16.
