Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 173 - Pension - The denial of grant/sanction of pension is a cause of action that persists over time - The claim cannot be barred by limitation.
[Para 7]  B Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 173 - Disability pension - When a member of the armed forces is on casual leave for a specific purpose, they are considered to be on duty - If they are injured while on casual leave and are medically invalidated out of military service, their injuries would be considered attributable to army service.
