Constitution of India, Article 226 - General Insurance (CDA) Rules, 1975, Rule 25 - Disciplinary proceedings - Enquiry officer found charges not proven - Punishment imposed - Legitimacy - Petitioner not given show cause notice upon disagreement - Ruling held that according to the decision made in Kunj Bihari's case by the Supreme Court, if disciplinary authority wishes to contradict the findings determined by the Enquiry Officer, it must provide a fresh opportunity to the employee after issuing a show cause notice detailing the terms of disagreement - Order invalidated.
