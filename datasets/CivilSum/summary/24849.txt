Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal Pay for Equal Work - Any classification of posts with different pay scales must equally apply to the Government, Government Servants, management and its employees - The Court generally must accept the management's classification unless it is patently erroneous either in law or on fact.
[Para 4] Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay - It is the responsibility of the management to assess the nature of work of technicians.
