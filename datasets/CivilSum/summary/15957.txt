Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Discrimination - Compassionate appointments - The discretion of the Government is limited by constitutional and public constraints - The Government’s actions must conform to a principle that may be tested for reason and relevance - Equals must not be discriminated against through the exercise of discretion - Unfettered discretion in the State could give preference to those with influence in governance over ordinary citizens - Such a course infringes on the equality rule in Article 14 of the Constitution.
