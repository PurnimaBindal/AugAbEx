Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion/Eligibility - Promotion from Agriculture Sub Inspector to Agriculture Inspector - Promotions were previously based solely on length of service, but Diploma qualifications from a recognised institution were later introduced for the position of Agriculture Inspector which the petitioners lacked.
The petitioners are requesting promotion without meeting the outlined qualifications and have no legitimate claim or fundamental right violated.
The court is unable to issue any direction for the grant of selection grade, as it is the sole responsibility of the government.
