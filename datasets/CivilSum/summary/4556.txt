Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Selection - Eligibility - The eligibility conditions for obtaining a petroleum outlet for retail dealership involve having an income of less than Rs. 50,000/- pa and not already having a business with the Corporation.
The petitioner is ineligible due to already being a partner in a firm holding a dealership with the Corporation and having an income exceeding Rs. 50,000/- pa The eligibility conditions are meant to aid those in need of employment and were deemed reasonable and not contradictory to any laws.
