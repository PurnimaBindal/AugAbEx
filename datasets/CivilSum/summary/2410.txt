Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 244(2) - Compulsory retirement - An order compelling a petitioner's retirement was reversed by the Tribunal, pointing out that an entry that had not been conveyed could not be `taken into account' - The Tribunal failed to take note that the petitioner had previously been penalized with the loss of two annual increments with cumulative effect - It is necessary to consider adverse entries made prior to promotion or being made to clear an efficiency bar since they are not wiped out - If such entries are not taken into consideration, examining the entire record will be insufficient - In light of this, nothing goes in the petitioner's favour - Order of compulsory retirement upheld.
