Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation of services - Workers who have been working under the Contract Labour Act - After its abolition, they must be regularized in their jobs - Even after agitations and claims from their side, their demands were never considered by the Government - However, the Government must consider their claim now - The workers are entitled to be reinstated and regularized - High Court’s dismissal of their claim due to delay was incorrect.
