The competent authority has the authority to use Rule 1411-A to deal with an absconding police officer who has not joined voluntarily according to Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 14.11(A) - Police Act, 1861, Section 29 - Criminal Procedure Code Section 87/Departmental enquiry.
Despite the fact that the power to proceed under Rule 1411-A does not limit the competent authority's power to conduct departmental enquiry against absconding police officers.

Rule 16.2(1) of the Punjab Police Rules, 1934, states that expulsion from service is the most serious act of misconduct, and that the word "misconduct" is broadly defined and includes conduct that makes an individual unsuitable for their role or is likely to affect or embarrass the department.
Rule 16.2(1) states that if a police officer is willfully AWOL from work for thirty seven days, it constitutes misconduct, and a dismissal order will not be deemed invalid just because it is not a serious misconduct defined in the rules.
It is up to the competent authority to determine the course of action.
