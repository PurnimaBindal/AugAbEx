Border Security Force Act, Section 117 - Dismissal - The statutory appeal filed by the petitioners was disposed of in a cursory and casual manner, without any reference to the specific grievance raised in the appeal.
The impugned order dismissing the appeal was passed without the application of mind.
As a result, the respondent is directed to pass a reasoned order dealing with the grievance raised in the appeal.
