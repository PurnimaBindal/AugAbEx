State was unjustified in avoiding giving petitioners two advance increments like those given to Vaidyas with a degree of B.A.M.S./GAMS - Notification that identifies certain degrees from various institutions as qualifications for induction into service - Should be equal for the purpose of granting advance increments - Therefore, petitioners are entitled to two such increments.
