Constitution of India, Article 311 - State Bank of India (Supervising Staff) Service Rules, Rule 50(3)(i) - Enquiry report - In cases where the disciplinary authority disagrees with the findings exonerating the delinquent in the enquiry report, the delinquent must be notified in order to allow them to address the reasons for disagreement before any punishment is passed.
Failure to do so will result in punishment orders that violate natural justice principles.
[Para 4]  B Constitution of India, Article 311 - State Bank of India (Supervising Staff) Service Rules, Rule 50(3)(i) - Punishment - When considering imposing the major punishment of removal from service, the Punishing Authority must review and evaluate the evidence in the enquiry file and make an independent determination without being influenced by the enquiry officer or recommending authority findings.
The Punishing Authority must record its own findings for punishment.
