Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 2(f) - Conract employment - Principal employer - According to the definition of employee U/s 2(f) of PF Act, employees who do the work of the principal employer employed by contractor or by the establishment that solely does the work of the principal employer/establishment fall within the ambit of Section 2(f).
It doesn't matter if the contractor establishment has separate identity, license, and tax identity because this doesn't absolve the principal employer from liability.
