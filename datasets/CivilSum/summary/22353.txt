Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - West Bengal Subordinate Services and Subordinate Other Services (Recruitment of Employees of the West Bengal Civil Emergency Force) Rules, 1975 - Equal Remuneration Act, 1956, Section 2(h)- Equal pay for equal work - Agragamies in West Bengal Civil Emergency Force - In terms of pay, Agragamies cannot be compared with Firemen as their nature of duties, responsibilities, and functions differ significantly.
Although academic qualifications and physical requirements of both roles are similar, Agragamies cannot be placed at par with Firemen as they perform different duties and functions.
