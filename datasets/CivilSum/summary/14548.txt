Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Charge sheet - Show cause notice - Before charging an employee, the authority must have a tentative finding that there is prima facie material to proceed against the employee.
This does not mean that the matter has been pre-judged.
[Para 5]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311(2) - Competent Authority - Prejudice - As long as the punishing authority is competent to inquire into the matter, there should be no concern about arbitrary action.
If the punishing authority follows the proper procedure of issuing a charge-sheet and providing the delinquent with a full opportunity to respond, the action cannot be considered arbitrary.
[Para 6]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ jurisdiction - If an employee is accused of embezzlement amounting to thousands of rupees, an interim stay of proceedings should not be granted.
[Para 8]
