Constitution of India Articles 14 and 226 - Ad hoc relief/ Jurisdiction / Speaking order - In relation to cessation of ad hoc relief and repaying the excess payment received, a thorough order based on grounds presented must be issued - There is no need to intervene in the exercise of Extraordinary jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Referral to the larger Bench - Calling for reference to the larger bench would be fruitless if a comparable case is already awaiting consideration before the Supreme Court.
