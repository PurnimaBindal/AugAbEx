Rules and Orders of the High Court, Volume I, Rule 1 and 1-A of Chapter 16-B - Advocate Fee - A counsel can only be denied fee if they are found to have defaulted or been guilty of misconduct - In cases where petitioner was withdrawn on administrative grounds and no misconduct or negligence was found on their part, the petitioner is entitled to fee for cases where evidence had been filed but not yet recorded or concluded due to withdrawal.
