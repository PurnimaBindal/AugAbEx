Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Equal pay for equal work - Personal pay - Stepping up - In relief of stepping up, the High Court cited the existence of identical qualifications and service conditions between instructors, resulting in junior personnel receiving higher pay if not given the same sum - Juniors had already received higher pay through personal pay, thus earning more in the revision of pay scales - The High Court granted relief without considering factual evidence - Court ruling overturned.
