Accused of forging entries in Muster Roll of labourers and embezzling government money, the Prevention of Corruption Act's Sections 5(1)(c),(d) and 5(2)-Indian Penal Code's Sections 409, 420, 467, 468, 471 and 120-B and Article 21 of the Constitution of India were invoked.
The inquiry had remained pending with Vigilance, and the case was only registered after 11 years.
Nonetheless, proceedings were quashed due to the lengthy delay since the right of a speedy public trial is an inalienable fundamental right under Article 21 of the Constitution, 1986 Crl.
LJ 1771 & 1989(2) Recent Criminal Reports 193 were cited.
