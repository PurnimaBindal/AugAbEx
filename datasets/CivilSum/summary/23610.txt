The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Service conditions - Different service rules for work-charged employees and regular Government Servants and providing different service conditions for two different classes of employees is not discriminatory or violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - Workcharged employees cannot be treated as regular Government Servants and entitled to benefits equal to them - Rajasthan Service (concessions on project) Rules, 1962 (Project Rules), Rules 2(h) and (a) are upheld.
