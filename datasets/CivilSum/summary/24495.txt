The High Court has the power to prescribe bench marks for written and oral tests, as necessary to select the best available talent for judicial officer positions.
While the written exam tests academic knowledge, the oral exam assesses overall intellectual and personal qualities.
Standards for selection can be supplemented by executive instructions, and in the absence of eligible reserved candidates, vacancies may be filled from the general category.
Candidates who participate in the selection process knowing the criteria are estopped from challenging it later, and all necessary parties must be impleaded in any challenge to selection criteria.
[Paras 35-55]
