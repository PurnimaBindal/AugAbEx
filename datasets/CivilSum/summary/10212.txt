Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 7-A and 8-B - Criminal Procedure Code, 1974, Section 482 - If an employer fails to pay contributions, an order of detention can be challenged by filing an appeal and review provided under the Act itself - However, a petition filed under Section 482 of Cr.
PC for quashing order of detention without availing such remedy is not maintainable as an effective alternative remedy is already available.
