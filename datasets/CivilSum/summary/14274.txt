Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Reduction in pay - Petitioners were employed as Masters in School - Without being granted any opportunity, their pay was cut from Rs. 110-250/- to a basic pay of Rs. 80/- - The pay scale reduction of the petitioners from 110-250/- to 80/- will be reversed - After being given appropriate opportunities, their pay shall be refixed - Until their respective retirements, the re-fixing of their pay/salary in the revised scale shall be adjusted - Petition has been granted.
[Paras 3 and 5]
