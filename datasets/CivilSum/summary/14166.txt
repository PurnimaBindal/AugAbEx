Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8 - Stoppage of increments with cumulative effect - An order of punishment without conducting a departmental enquiry is unlawful - The petitioners have been denied the financial benefits of increased emoluments for two decades - It would not be appropriate to allow the department to proceed with the enquiry now in the interest of justice - The contested order is overturned.
