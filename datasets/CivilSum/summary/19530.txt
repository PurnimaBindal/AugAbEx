Payment of gratuity - An employee who has retired from service and is entitled to gratuity as per the rules framed by State Govt, not under the Payment of Gratuity Act - Rules such as the Haryana Municipal Employees (Integration, Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1982 and Civil Services Rules Volume II (as applicable to employees of Haryana State) must be followed - Any mistake committed by the Controlling Authority under the Gratuity Act needs to be corrected - The order passed under Gratuity Act cannot be valid as it's passed in ignorance of statutory provisions - Employees of Municipal Committee shall follow the rules framed by State regarding payment of gratuity and not the Payment of Gratuity Act.
