Kerala Secretariat Subordinate Service Special Rules, Rule 7 - Appointment - Post of Legal Assistant - The recruitment rules should not be breached and the Public Service Commission cannot overlook them - The existing list was closed and recruitment was made through the Public Service Commission, so the petitioner has no claim over the post - The fact that candidates were appointed from the panel confirms its publication - The interim appointment is subject to the final decision's outcome - If the petitioner is unsuccessful in the final decision, the interim order will stand void - Thus, the petitioner's appointment by an interim order does not create any rights or lead to regularisation - The appeal is rejected.

Constitution of India, Article 309 - Recruitment Rules made under Article 309 of the Constitution must be strictly followed and not ignored - Allowing the disregard of rules and the bypassing of the Public Service Commissions can lead to an unlimited number of illegal recruitments through the backdoor.
