The Act of 1989 and the Rules, 1993 govern the service conditions and age of superannuation for employees of Non-Government recognized affiliated aided institutions according to Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institution Act, 1989, Rajasthan Grant-in-Aid to Education and Cultural Institutions Rules, 1963, Rajasthan University Ordinances, Ordinance 67-A, Rajasthan University Act, 1946, and Rajasthan Non-Government Education Institution Rules, 1993.
