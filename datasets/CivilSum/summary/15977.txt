Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Admission - MBBS/BDS Courses - Dependents of persons boarded out of military service due to disability - The prospectus does not make a clear distinction between regular or irregular service in the military for the purpose of providing the benefit of reservation to the wards of ex-servicemen - The term "ex-servicemen" has not been defined in the prospectus - The definition of "ex-serviceman" given in Rule 2(c) of 1979 Rules is only applicable to the cases where people claim recruitment as an 'ex-serviceman' and cannot be applied for the purpose of admission to courses based on the prospectus issued by the respondent-University - The petitioner should not be denied the benefit of reservation just because his father served as a military personnel during emergency as a recruit and not as a regular Army man - The University wrongly defined the term 'ex-serviceman,' which deprived the petitioner of his right to be considered for admission to MBBS/BDS course as per his merit - The respondents must admit the petitioner to MBBS/BDS course as per his merit among candidates in the DDL category.
[Paras 5 to 9]
