The Life Insurance Corporation of India Class III and Class IV Employees Promotional Rules, 1987, Rule 7(2) requires six years of service as Section head or five years of service as Higher Grade Assistant (H.G.A.)
to be eligible for promotion.
The petitioners' contention that they were not considered for the posts of A.A.O.
because they had not become eligible for promotion was dismissed as they had not completed the necessary service requirement at the time the selection list was prepared.
The petitioners were not eligible for promotion under the Rules of 1987 and their petitions were dismissed.
