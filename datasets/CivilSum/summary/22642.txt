Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Misappropriation of funds - Departmental enquiry - Bus conductor recovered fare from passengers but did not issue tickets - Dismissal from service is the correct punishment for such offenses - Judicial forums should not show misplaced sympathy for a workman - Rulings state that (i) Removal/dismissal is the appropriate punishment for an employee found guilty of misappropriation of funds; (ii) Employers can lose faith in such employees and give dismissal as punishment.
(iii) Courts must not interfere with the quantum of punishment.
[Para 10]
