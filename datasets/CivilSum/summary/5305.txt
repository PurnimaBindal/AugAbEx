Civil Procedure Code, Section 100 - Second Appeal - Substantial question of law - Seniority - In order to file a second appeal, there must be a substantial question of law involved - This may include the non-consideration of important evidence that would have had a direct impact on the decision - According to the Rule, seniority is determined by the date of birth, with the older individual being considered senior - In this case, the lower courts erroneously held the junior individual to be older without properly examining the service record - This failure to consider important evidence constitutes a substantial question of law and allows for the second appeal to be maintained.
