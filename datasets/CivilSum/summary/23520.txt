The Constitution of India's Preamble and Articles 38 and 21 establish social justice as a means of ensuring life is livable with dignity, and the state must provide facilities for workmen to attain a minimum standard of health, economic security, and civilized living.
Articles 21, 41, and 42 provide that hygienic conditions in the workplace and leisure are fundamental rights of the workmen, and the right to life also includes traditional and cultural heritage.
Articles 21, 39(e), 41, 43, and 48A ensure workmen's right to health and medical care during and after service, and a remedy of claim for compensation is available under Articles 226 and 32.
Employers are liable for paying damages for occupational diseases, and factories are bound by rules regarding safety in the use of asbestos.
Asbestos industries are directed to use the Membrane Filter Test and review their standards of permissible exposure limit value of fiber/cc.
All factories must insure health coverage to every worker and consider inclusion of small scale factories to protect workers' health.
