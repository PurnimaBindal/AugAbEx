Central Civil Services (Redeployment of Surplus Staff) Rules, 1990, Rule 9 - Redeployment - Benefit of counting of past services - Surplus employees redeployed from parent department cannot claim the benefit of counting past services for the purpose of seniority or experience in the new organization.
The service conditions for redeployed employees are governed by provisions in Rules and instructions issued by the Government of India.
As per the instructions, past services of redeployed staff will not be counted for seniority in the new organization as the past experience will not be considered as service in the grade.
The appeals have been allowed.
