Disciplinary proceedings against an employee accused of misappropriation cannot be stayed simply because a police investigation is pending into charges similar to the one levelled against them, as the possibility of prejudice to that employee is not enough to justify stoppage of the enquiry.
Rather, the question of whether or not the proceedings should remain stayed pending the disposal of a criminal case would depend on the facts of each individual case, taking into account the likelihood of prejudice due to complex questions of law and fact.
[Paras 6 and 7] B.
The mere pendency of a criminal case against an employee does not bar the initiation of disciplinary proceedings against them, as the decision to stay such proceedings depends on whether the case involves questions of law and fact that would justify awaiting the outcome of the criminal case.
