Termination from service - Absence from duty - Enquiry - The workman did not respond to an enquiry regarding his long-term absence despite being aware of its pendency.
He submitted only an explanation of his ailment, but failed to provide proof of his submitted medical certificate.
Additionally, doubts remain regarding the authority of the alleged medical certificate bearing a future date.
The workman has a history of frequent absences.
Hence, his termination from service was justified.
[Paras 5 & 6]  B Termination from service - Absence from duty - Enquiry - A limited enquiry regarding an employee's reason for not reporting to work after leave has expired or failing to comply upon being asked is sufficient compliance with the principles of natural justice.
