Constitution of India, Article 226 - De novo Vigilance Enquiry - Allegations of disproportionate assets beyond known source of income - Whether Government can close enquiry without waiting for report of Enquiry Officer - Government closed the enquiry without giving time demanded by Chief Director, Vigilance Bureau for completing enquiry and without providing reasons - Held that matter could not be closed unless report of enquiry officer is submitted and matter is put up before competent authority - No grounds to quash letter requiring petitioner to appear before enquiry officer for regular enquiry.
[Paras 9 and 10]
