Medical Council of India Regulations on Graduate Medical Education, 1997, Regulation 10(A) - Admissions - Changing courses mid-session is not permitted.
For the Post Graduate course of Medicine (MD) (Medicine), the prospectus outlines conditions which prohibit any change of subject after one month of admission/counselling.
Seats that become available during second counselling for a subject course cannot be offered based on merit to those who already accepted seats in the first counselling for another subject course, as this may increase the seats beyond permitted capacity.
This is prohibited under Regulation 10(A), and if a candidate admitted during second counselling is made to vacate their seat after a long time due to a court order, they may lose the academic session and face undue hardship.
[Paras 7 and 10]
