The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay scales - Discrimination in prescribing minimum pay scale for a different cadre - Seniors Scale Stenographers have different pay scales in the Punjab Civil Secretariat and High Court - Only expert bodies have the power to prescribe pay scales - Unless it is hostility or arbitrariness, pay scales determined by expert bodies like Pay commissions cannot be interfered with - No discrimination can be complained about by persons in different cadres or departments with different pay scales.
