Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 13.7 - Selection of constables for B-I List - Jurisdiction - For lower school courses, the merit of constables that are to be chosen must be assessed by the Departmental Promotion Committee.
The DIG is responsible for naming the members of the committee, but it does not act as the DIG's delegate.
The committee evaluates the answer papers, not the DIG, and the revised selection made by the DIG after re-evaluation must be ignored.
If selected by the Committee but ignored by the DIG, petitioners will be entitled to seniority after being sent for the course they were meant to attend based on the Committee's selection.
