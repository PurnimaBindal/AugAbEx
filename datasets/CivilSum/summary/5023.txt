Constitution of India, Article 226 - AP (Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes & Backward Classes) Regulation of Issue of Community Certificates Act, 1993 and Rules framed thereunder - Cancellation of caste certificate - The petitioner, who had been appointed on compassionate grounds after the death of her husband and later charged with misconduct for providing a false caste certificate, had her certificate cancelled after an enquiry without being given an opportunity to be heard, which violated principles of natural justice - The impugned order was thus quashed.
