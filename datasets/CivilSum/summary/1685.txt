Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Nagpur University Act, 1974, Section 11(6)(b) - Reservation- No reservation for a solitary post in a particular discipline at the initial stage or for filling up future vacancy with respect to that post is permitted - Reservation can only be made in cadres available in a particular discipline, subject to the availability of more than one post - Grouping is allowed only for appointments if there are more than one in a particular discipline - Grouping of posts and appointments of a single post with similar single posts in different disciplines is not permitted.

Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 226 - Nagpur University Act, 1974, Section 11(6)(b) - Reservation - Reservation is an exception or proviso to the general rule postulated in clauses (1) and (2) of Article 16, and its interpretation or exercise cannot be so wide as to destroy the fundamental rights guaranteed under clauses (1) and (2) of Article 16.
[Para 33] C Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Nagpur University Act, 1974, Section 11 (6)(b) - Reservation Cadre - The rule of reservation should be applied on a discipline or subject wise basis - Cadre refers not only to the entire strength of service, but also to a part of service sanctioned as a separate unit - The word `cadre' is not synonymous with `service' - The cadre or unit which should be subject to the application of the rule of reservations should be the department, subject or discipline.
