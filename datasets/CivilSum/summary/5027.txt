According to the Fundamental Rules, Rule 49(iii) and the Appellate Tribunal for Foreign Exchange (Recruitment, Salary and Allowances and other Conditions of Service of Chairperson & Members) Rules of 2000, Rule 5, the pay and allowances of a member acting as Chairperson of the Tribunal are dependent on whether there is a specific appointment to a post with higher responsibilities.
If a member is asked to preside over the Tribunal by virtue of Section 26 of the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999, in the absence of the duly appointed Chairman, he will not be entitled to the pay and allowance of the Chairman under FR 49 if there is no specific appointment to a post with higher responsibilities.
This is clarified in paragraphs 8 to 11 of the text.
