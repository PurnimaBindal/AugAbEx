The exclusion of retirees who retired prior to 111986 in Rule 18 as amended by a notification dated 22.5.1987 in the All India Services (Death-cum-Retirement Benefits) Rules, 1958 means that a petitioner who retired in 1981 is not entitled to the benefits of the amendment.
[Para 8] B.
The amendment in pension Rules made with effect from 111986 in The Indian Administrative Service (Pay) Rules, 1954 applies only to those retiring on or after that date based on the recommendation of the Fourth Central Pay Commission, and the Government must prescribe a cut-off date for implementing this policy.
The court cannot direct the Government to grant the same benefits to all retirees regardless of their date of retirement since this goes beyond its jurisdiction.
