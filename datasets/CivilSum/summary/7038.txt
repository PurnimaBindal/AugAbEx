According to Rajasthan Industrial Disputes Rules, Rule 36 and Railway Pension Rules, 1950, Paras 309 and 310, a claim for compassionate allowance cannot be awarded to a dismissed Railway servant on a charge of moral turpitude who has also lost in his challenge to the dismissal order in the Supreme Court.
The petitioner in this case did not give the required option as per the circular and since they were not a pension optee, the Rules of 1950 cannot be applied to their case.
Furthermore, according to para 309 of the Rules, there can be no good cause for awarding compassionate allowance to a Railway servant whose service was dishonest.
Additionally, since the petitioner did not pray for this relief in earlier litigation, they are stopped by the principles of constructive res judicata.
Lastly, it is too late for the petitioner to seek such relief as their application was made 20 years after their dismissal.
