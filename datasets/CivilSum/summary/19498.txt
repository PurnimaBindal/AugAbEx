Employees of the abolished Department of Urban Estates Haryana were given options to join HUDA.
Those who did not opt were eligible for absorption in any other department.
A petitioner who was originally posted in the Industries Department later found his pay scale to be less than that offered by HUDA and opted for absorption there.
However, since he opted after the cut-off date given in the Government order, he cannot claim seniority or promotion over employees who joined before that date.
[Paras 2 and 3]
Employees are entitled to be considered for promotion, but cannot claim promotion as a right.
[Para 4]
