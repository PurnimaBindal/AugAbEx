Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Madhya Pradesh Civil Services (General Conditions of Services) Rules, 1961, Rules 8 and 12(a)(ii) - Seniority - Seniority assigned from passing of test - Probation condition precedent for confirmation - Promotion through RSC - The appellant was a direct recruit on probation for one year who had to pass a test for confirmation - The appointing authority had the power to grant seniority from the date of passing the test even if it was after the probation period - The decision to assign seniority from the date of passing the test was not illegal, even if it was on a lower level than that assigned by PSC.
