Indian Medicine Central Council Act, 1970, Section 17(3)(a)(b) and (c) - Recognition of qualifications - The degrees of Ayurved Ratna and Vaid Visharada issued by Hindi Sahitya Sammalan Paryag have been de-recognized - Section 17(3) only recognizes degrees and certificates obtained prior to 1967 - The petitioners did not acquire any such qualifications before the enforcement of Section 17(2) and were not registered under the Act - Therefore, they cannot claim protection under Section 17(3) - The Act justifies leaving the determination of proper education and requisite expertise to the proper authority with relevant knowledge in the subject.
