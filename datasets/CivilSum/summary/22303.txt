When departmental proceedings allow for co-worker assistance, employees are not entitled to representation by an advocate.
Even if given the option to be assisted by a co-worker, any resulting proceedings are not rendered invalid.
[Para 11] B.
In determining competent authority for termination, a Labour Court must refer to service rules, rather than merely assessing the superior status of the termination order issuer.
A case in which proper reference was not made to the service rules must be remanded to the Labour Court.
