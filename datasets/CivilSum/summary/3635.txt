Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Age of superannuation - A petitioner who was appointed as a Clerk but later promoted to the executive cadre - The age of superannuation for clerical cadre was 60 years and for executive cadre 58 years - Having received higher salary and emoluments due to the promotion, the petitioner cannot argue that their retirement age should be 60 years as applicable to clerical staff.
[Paras 15, 25, 27, and 28]
