Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Uttar Pradesh State Universities Act, 1973, Section 36 - Termination - Arbitration - Bias - Service of petitioner terminated after enquiry, and Vice-Chancellor approved termination - Petitioner challenged termination in writ petition - Court directed petitioner to raise dispute under Section 36 before the Tribunal of Arbitration - The Tribunal of Arbitration was formed with three members, including one nominee of the petitioner, another nominee of the Committee of Management and the third nominee of the Vice-Chancellor - Petitioner protested the appointment of the nominee of the Committee of Management due to his marriage to a person working in petitioner's place - Despite the protest, the Tribunal passed the impugned order which led Petitioner to infer the likelihood of bias - Order passed by the Tribunal quashed and Vice Chancellor was directed to reconstitute the Tribunal.
