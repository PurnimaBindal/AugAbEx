Constitution of India, Article 226 - Motor Vehicles Act, Section 112 - Termination of services - Misconduct - The plaintiff-appellant was employed as a Conductor in the Transport Department.
While on duty, he embezzled Rs. 1960 by not issuing tickets to 19 passengers after collecting their money and refused to hand over the unpunched tickets to the checking staff.
Additionally, he was found under the influence of liquor and misbehaved with the checking staff before running away from the bus.
As a result, the plaintiff engaged in misconduct which led to a regular inquiry.
The charges against the plaintiff were proven, and he was given a full opportunity to cross-examine the witnesses.
After due application of mind, the termination order was passed, and the petition was dismissed.
