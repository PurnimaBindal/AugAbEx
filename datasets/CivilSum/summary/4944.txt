Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and laches - The pendency of a petition does not constitute delay and laches.
Only delay that occurred before the filing of the petition is relevant.
[Paras 9 and 11]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Rajasthan Municipalities (Subordinate and Ministerial Service) Rules, 1963, Rule 28 and Provisos to Rule 8A - Seniority - If confirmation of temporary appointment in service has been recommended in terms of the proviso to Rule 8-A, the date of subsequent substantive appointment to the post will be deemed to be the date of temporary appointment.
If termination of temporary service was declared non est and reinstatement with all consequential benefits was granted by the Court, the subsequent substantive appointment will be deemed to be in continuation of the temporary appointment and the proviso to Rule 8-A will still apply.
Seniority will be computed from the date of initial temporary appointment, and Rule 28 will have to be read with the proviso to Rule 8A.
