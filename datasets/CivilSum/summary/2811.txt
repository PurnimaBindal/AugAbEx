The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 and the Sports Council (Chandigarh) Rules of 1989, Rule 9, 11 state that the administration cannot refuse grant of assistance after accepting recommendations of the Council and allowing the athlete to participate in competition abroad.
It must not discriminate between athletes or impose unfair conditions that violate Article 14 and 16 principles.
It must also formulate and follow a reasonable and uniform criteria and reimburse the petitioner's expenditure incurred as per rules.
Directions issued accordingly.
