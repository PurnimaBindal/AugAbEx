Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 11(2) - Voluntary Premature retirement - Discharge - The petitioner admitted that his request for discharge from the Army was approved by the Records Office and that his Unit was informed of his discharge from the Army.
The Records Office instructed the Unit Office to send his pension papers.
Once the petitioner's request for voluntary retirement was accepted, even if it was to take effect at a later date, he had no right to retract it.
The Authority competent to issue the order passed it in accordance with the Rules and it cannot be voided simply because the petitioner requested it.
The petition was dismissed.
