Karnataka Prohibition of Admission of Students to the Unrecognized and Unaffiliated Educational Institutional Act, 1992, Sections 3 and 4 - Grant-in-aid Code for Primary Teachers Training Institutions in Mysore State, Rule 7- Education/admission - Recognition - Section 4 of the Statute prohibits admission of students to an unrecognized institution or admission in excess of the intake capacity of recognised institution - No challenge to the provisions of the statute - Admissions granted in violation of statutory provisions specifically prohibiting such admissions, cannot be approved in exercise of writ jurisdiction under Art. 226 of the Constitution.
