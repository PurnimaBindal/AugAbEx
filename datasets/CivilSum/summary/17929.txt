Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. 1, Part I (Applicable to Haryana State), Rules 244 (a)(i) and 252 - Special pay - The action of the government was challenged for not considering special pay while determining the amount of DA admissible to officers/officials serving in different departments.
Before the revision of scales, special pay was treated as part of pay while paying DA After the revision of pay scales, DA is being paid only on the basic pay, and special pay is not being taken into consideration.
There was a specific entry made where special pay was granted in lieu of a higher time scale.
However, no mention was made that special pay had been raised in lieu of a higher time scale as to the post of A.DA Special pay granted to the petitioner did not fall within the definition of special pay under Rule 252 of the Rules.
Special pay is specifically excluded from the definition of 'Pay' and is not to be treated as part of pay.
There was no violation of law, and the claim made by the petitioner cannot be sustained.
There was no arbitrariness in the action of the government.
[Paras 6 and 11]
