Constitution of India, Article 16 - West Bengal Services (Determination of Seniority) Rules, 1981, Rules 5 and 6 - West Bengal Public Service Commission (Exemption from Consultation by Governor) Regulations, 1955, Rule 3-A - The employees promoted on ad hoc basis can have their temporary or ad hoc appointment period counted for the purpose of seniority upon satisfaction of certain requisite conditions, such as being promoted to the post and being justly borne in such cadre.
In the present case, such promotes can be considered as within the cadre from the dates of their respective ad hoc promotions, as all other requirements are fulfilled and consultation with the Public Service Commission is not necessary.
The finding of the trial judge that the seniority of the respondents should be counted from the dates of their initial ad hoc appointment is upheld.
