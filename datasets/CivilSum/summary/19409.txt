The Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226, 311 deals with retirement of a Lecturer in a Civil Engineering Institute, who has been served with a notice of retirement effective 191994, but according to relevant laws and rules, the petitioner had the right to continue until the end of the academic session, which was on 3061995.
Despite the petitioner's representations, no reply was received, and the respondents ordered the petitioner to retire on 3061995 at the end of the academic session, instead of the actual date of superannuation.
