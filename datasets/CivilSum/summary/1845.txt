Army Rules, Rule 37 - Misconduct - Dismissal - General Court Martial - The petitioner was awarded a sentence by a court martial for alleged non-compliance of Rule 37 of Army Rules.
However, it was found that the Brigadier who passed the convening order did not apply his own mind and there was no indication of this at any stage.
As a result, the finding of the court martial and the sentence awarded to the petitioner were quashed, and the respondent was directed to pay Rs. 5 lacs as compensation for the harassment and indignity suffered by the petitioner for almost 17 years, as well as other consequential benefits.
