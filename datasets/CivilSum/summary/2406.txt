Evidence Act, 1872, Sections 123 and 124 - Privileged communication - In a suit questioning validity of domestic enquiry proceedings, the defendant produced sealed files regarding the plaintiff and claimed privilege - The plaintiff requested to inspect the file - The confidential file contained various communications between superior officers and remarks known as Confidential Reports that are not to be published - The lower court was reasonable in only allowing the defendants to communicate adverse remarks to the petitioner and denying access to the entire file.
[Paras 10 & 12]
