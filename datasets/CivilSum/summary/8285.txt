Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 2.2(b)(2) - Charge-sheet - The charge-sheet which was issued against the petitioner after his retirement and on the basis of charges pertaining to December, 1998, has been deemed unacceptable in law due to being barred by provisions of Rules 2.2(b)(2) of Punjab Civil Services Rules - As a result, the impugned charge-sheet has been quashed.
