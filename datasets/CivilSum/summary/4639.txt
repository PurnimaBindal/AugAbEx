Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226/227 - Appointment on compassionate grounds - The petitioner's deceased spouse worked as Head Clerk/Assistant in Punjab Transport Department - The petitioner, who has passed the required Punjabi typewriting test, claims appointment under Government instructions - Even though none of the dependents has obtained appointment under these instructions, the petitioner has a right to have her case considered - She has submitted all necessary documents and waited for employment for over three years - The denial of appointment can be seen as arbitrary - The respondents have been instructed to give the petitioner appointment to the post she is qualified for within three months.
[Paras 2, 3 and 4]
Constitution of India, Article 16 and 226/227 - Compassionate appointment should not be delayed - Such an appointment is intended to alleviate the difficulties of families who have lost their breadwinner - These cases should be addressed quickly to assist families in distress - It is not correct to let these cases stagnate for years - If no suitable post is available, a supernumerary post should be considered to accommodate the applicant (AIR 1989 SC 1976).
