Disciplinary Proceedings - When disciplinary authorities do not agree with the finding of no guilt by enquiry officers, delinquent officers are not given notice before the authority reaches its final conclusion.
If a show cause notice is given only for recommending removal from service, it is not sustainable.
The proceedings must be recommenced from the stage of a new notice issued by the disciplinary authority, indicating their tentative disagreement with the enquiry officer.
[Paras 13 and 14] B. Disciplinary Proceedings - If disciplinary authorities do not agree with the views of enquiry officers, they must give notice to employees, setting out their tentative conclusions.
After hearing the employee, disciplinary authorities will arrive at a final finding of guilt.
Then, employees must be served with another notice relating to the proposed punishment.
