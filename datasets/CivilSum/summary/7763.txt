Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(c)(2) - Retiral dues - Recovery of - A workman who retired at the age of 58 years sought benefits under the contention that the retirement age should be 60 years - The Labour Court, as an executing court under Section 33(c)(2), cannot determine the legality of an order that amounts to jurisdictional error or procedural irregularity - It can only assess whether the workman has a pre-existing legal right or is owed an amount.
