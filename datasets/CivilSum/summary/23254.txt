The appellate authority in a disciplinary proceeding must consider the grounds raised by the respondents before rejecting an appeal.
In this case, the appellate authority enhanced the punishment without proper consideration, which violates the law.
[Para 11]
In disciplinary proceedings where the appellate authority differs with the order of the lower forum and enhances the punishment, they have a duty to provide at least some reasons for their decision.
The delinquent officer is entitled to understand the mind of the appellate or revisional authority in dismissing their appeal and/or revision.
[Para 19]
In a departmental proceeding, the appellate authority imposed a more severe punishment of dismissal from service without providing any reasons for doing so, after the disciplinary authority had already imposed the punishment of stoppage of (5) Annual Grade Increments with Cumulative effect.
The order of the appellate authority is set aside since it is not supported by any valid reasons.
