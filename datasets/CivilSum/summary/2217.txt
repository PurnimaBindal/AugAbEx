Scope of Section 47 of the Persons with Disabilities Act, 1995 and Punjab Roadways (Ministerial) State Service Class-III Rules, 1977, Rule 8 - Even if an employee with disability covered under Section 2(u) is to be adjusted to an alternative or new post, they cannot be removed from service or have their pay and allowances reduced based on their disability.
Section 47 surpasses any barriers created by State or Central Government rules.
