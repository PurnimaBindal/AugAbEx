According to Clause X of Letters Patent, the appellants who were appointed as Social Studies Instructors in an ad hoc manner were not eligible for regularisation as they lacked the required qualification.
The respondents had regularised those who had the necessary qualifications as per the vacancies available for filling.
Even as per the Government Instructions, the appellants were not entitled to regularisation.
Therefore, the appellants are not eligible for any relief.
