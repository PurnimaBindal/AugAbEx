Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Punishment - Departmental Enquiry - The workman cannot be reinstated with full backwages and continuity of service if the departmental enquiry is found fair and proper - Even if Section 11-A of I.D.
Act is invoked by the Labour Court, some form of punishment must be awarded - Abusing a superior in the office is a sufficient reason for removal from service if the workman is found guilty - The order of reinstatement may be maintained, but without back wages, taking a lenient view on the matter.
