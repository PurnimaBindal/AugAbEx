Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Ad hoc employee/Regular service - Admission in Post Graduate Courses - Differentiation between ad hoc and Regular service for the purpose of admission - Ad hoc or temporary employee's status is fragile and no benefit accrues from such service - Government made a distinct separation between the two types of service - State's policy of limiting the benefits of admission to in-service applicants of regular basis is rational and not unjust - Regularization of service of ad hoc doctors with retroactive effect does not entitle them to admission if they were not in regular service at the time of application submission - Nonetheless, once ad hoc doctors have obtained a favourable order from the court and completed two years of the course prior to the adverse decision, it would be unfair to revoke their admission at this juncture.
