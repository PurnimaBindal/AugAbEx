Under the Constitution of India, Article 16, an amendment to the Departmental Schedule II regarding the Industrial Tribunal determines that those appointed to the post of District and Sessions Judge Grade II or other Judicial Officers of equivalent rank, draw a pay scale equivalent to the post of District and Sessions Judges, Grade II, or other Judicial Officer of the same level.
However, a petitioner who worked as a District Sessions Judge Grade-II but was posted as Presiding Officer of Labour Court is only entitled to receive the pay of the District and Sessions Judge, Grade II, and not that of District and Sessions Judge, Grade I [Paras 2 and 3]
