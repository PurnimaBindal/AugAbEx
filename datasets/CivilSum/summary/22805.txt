In a case involving a departmental enquiry, a tribunal set aside an order of removal by relying on a Supreme Court judgment and giving it retrospective effect.
However, the tribunal failed to notice that the judgment is only operative prospectively, leading to the case being remanded.
Article 311(2) of the Constitution of India, regarding inquiry reports, was mentioned in the context of the Mohd.
Ramzan Khan case, stating that any punishment imposed prior to the judgment cannot be challenged on the basis of non-supply of the report.
