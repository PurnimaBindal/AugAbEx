The petitioner claiming pension after 30 years of discharge from the Army, on the basis of Regulation 126, is facing issues due to delay and laches.
Although old records have been destroyed, he had already received gratuity which was required to be returned to fulfill the conditions for pension.
There is no evidence to prove that the petitioner was eligible for pension, as he failed to provide any material despite being given full opportunity.
The court cannot grant relief in the absence of records showing eligibility for pension, and delay becomes fatal to the claim when the relevant records have been destroyed over time.
[Paras 2, 6 to 9]
