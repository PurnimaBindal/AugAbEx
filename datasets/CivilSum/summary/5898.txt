Regarding Respondent No. 1, Mr. Prabhakaran, the Constitution of India Articles 12 and 226 explain the writ jurisdiction stating that an "Authority" under Article 12 is not required to be an authority under Article 226 and in some cases, "any other authority" for the purpose of Article 12 may be an instrumentality or agency of the Government of the State.
What matters is not the appearance of the body, but its true character to determine whether it is an "authority" under either Articles.
An "authority" under Article 12 is unequivocally an authority for the purpose of Article 226.
In terms of the Constitution of India Articles 14, 16 and 311 regarding termination, the Court overruled the arbitrary and malicious order of termination of services for disobeying transfer order, as no inquiry was held.
Regarding delay in the Tamil Nadu Shops and Establishments Act, the appeal was rejected for being not maintainable, but the court ultimately found that the delay in filing the writ petition was excusable.
