UP Secondary Education Services Commission and Selection Boards Act, 1982, Sections 18(1) and 35 - UP Secondary Education Services Commission Rules, 1982, Rule 4 Appendix `A' - The Commission must be notified of a vacancy and subject selection for the selection of a teacher - Any selection made without following these guidelines is deemed invalid - Notifying the District Inspector of Schools of a vacancy is not enough.

Secondary Education Services (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 1981, Para 5(2) - Ad hoc appointment - District Inspector of Schools must make the selection for the ad hoc appointment of teachers - Only candidates selected by the Inspector are eligible for appointment - Appointments made by the Committee of Management without candidates being selected by the Inspector are invalid, and such appointees are not entitled to receive payment.
