Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(1)(c), 25-F and 2(oo) - Termination - Burden of proof - Workman worked for over 240 days- Services terminated in violation of Section 25-F - The workman must prove the illegality of the termination order, and the initial burden is discharged - Management claiming abandonment - The burden of proof is on the management to show that the workman had no intention to return - No evidence was provided by the management except a mere statement claiming abandonment - The workman was not offered employment even with a demand notice - Based on the Labour Court's findings, the workman's alleged abandonment of the job is unfounded and not valid - An award was made to reinstate the workman with Rs. 10,000 as the back wages lump-sum amount, which the Court set aside.
