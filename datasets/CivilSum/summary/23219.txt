Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25-F - Retrenchment - Burden of proof - If employer disputes completion of 240 days, workman must provide positive oral and documentary evidence to prove continuous work - Filing affidavit is not sufficient - Employer is not required to prove non-completion of 240 days.
