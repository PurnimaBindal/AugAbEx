Under Article 317(2) of the Constitution of India, a member of the Punjab Public Service Commission was suspended by the Governor without the required approval of the Council of Ministers as mandated by the Rules of Business of the Government of Punjab 1980.
The Council of Ministers' ex-post-facto approval was obtained.
However, since the petitioner held a constitutional post, the Council of Ministers must have approved the suspension before the Governor placed him under suspension.
The suspension by Governor was held to be void ab initio.
[Para 10]  B According to Regulation 15 of the Punjab State Public Service Commission (Conditions of Service) Regulation, 1958, there is no specific provision against the payment of subsistence allowances.
Thus, the petitioner was entitled to the same.
Moreover, the non-payment of subsistence allowance vitiated the whole proceedings for being violative of the principle of natural justice.
[Paras 12 and 19]  C. According to Articles 317(1) and 317(2) of the Constitution of India, if no adverse report has come out against the petitioner, he/she is entitled to pension for life.
Since no adverse report has come out against the petitioner, the petitioner was entitled to full pay with allowances until superannuation.
