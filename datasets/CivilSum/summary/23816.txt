Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pay Scale - Grant of higher pay-scale - The Appellant-University has separate cadres for Research Assistant and Senior Research Assistant, each with different qualifications and pay scales.
Research Assistants who possess an M.Sc.
degree were given the pay scale of the Senior Research Assistant after being declared a teacher, effective from 12121989.
The Respondent claims such pay scale from 1.4.1978 based on other universities' treatment of Research Assistants.
However, the Appellant-University is not comparable with other universities that have only one Research Assistant cadre.
The Respondent is entitled to that pay scale retrospectively.
[Paras 14 and 15]
