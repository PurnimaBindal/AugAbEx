According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India and the J&K Medical Service Recruitment Rules, 1979, Schedule II, the qualifications for the post of demonstrator in Physiology and Bio-Chemistry are indicated as MBBS with a field service certificate.
However, in the past, individuals possessing MSc.
qualifications were given appointments.
The petitioner, who has an MSc.
Bio-chemistry qualification, has been directed to apply for the position and allowed to compete for it.
