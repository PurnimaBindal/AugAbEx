In light of the Supreme Court's decision in the case of Mahaboob Deepak and numerous High Court rulings, it has been established that the reinstatement of a workman who was appointed outside the rules of appointment is not viable under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F and Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
Therefore, the award for reinstatement contravenes the provisions of the 1947 Act.
