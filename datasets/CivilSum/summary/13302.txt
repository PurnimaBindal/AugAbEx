Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Disability pension - Petitioner who joined Army in 1989 and was discharged from service in 1995 - CCDA rejected his case for disability pension - Court held that the disability must relate to the army service and not preexist before his admission - Thus, petitioner is entitled to disability pension w.e.f.
