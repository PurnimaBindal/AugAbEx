Petitioners, who were sanitary supervisors working in Jammu Municipality, claimed pay parity with their counterparts in Srinagar Municipality, citing Articles 14, 16, 39(d), and 226 of the Constitution of India.
However, Jammu Municipality has two grades to provide promotional avenues, while Srinagar Municipality has only one running grade.
Additionally, there are a lesser number of posts in Jammu Municipality, indicating that the work entrusted to sanitary supervisors of Jammu Municipality is lesser than that of Srinagar Municipality, which has a higher population and is topographically dirtier than Jammu.
Furthermore, the final grade to which a sanitary supervisor of Jammu Municipality is entitled is higher than that of Srinagar Municipality, leading to the conclusion that the petitioners are not entitled to pay parity with sanitary supervisors of Srinagar Municipality.
