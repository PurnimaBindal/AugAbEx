Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation - Merit - Reserved category candidates will not be affected in terms of merit by reservation - If a reserved category candidate achieves a rank in the general category through their own merit, they will be selected and given an adjustment against the general category vacancy, without any disadvantage to less meritorious candidates in the reserved category - When providing adjustments to better and less beneficial vacancies, a specific merit list inter se among the reserved category candidates must be prepared, including those who achieved adjustment in the general category through their own merit, and options given according to their inter se merit.
