J&K Civil Service Regulations, Article 128 - Absence from duty - Overstayal of leave - Dismissal from service - Natural justice - Overstaying leave without permission is considered misconduct but not necessarily abandonment - A show cause notice must be given to the employee and an inquiry conducted before dismissal is considered, so the employee has the opportunity to explain their constraints and absence - If the absence was not willful, dismissal may not be appropriate - Order of dismissal quashed with the option to make a new decision.
