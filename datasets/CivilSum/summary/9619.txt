In Letters Patent Appeal, Clause X, an appeal was made challenging an order to vacate an ex parte stay of termination.
The appellant was granted three appointments of 89 days each.
Upon admission of the appeal, the impugned order of the single Judge vacating the ex parte stay was again stayed by an ex parte order that was later confirmed after notice to the respondents.
As a result, the appellant continued in service.
However, the appellant cannot claim regularisation of her service solely based on those appointments.
[Paras 2 to 5]
As per the Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226, the temporary appointment of the appellant for 89 days cannot be used to add on the period of her continuance in service.
This is because the interim order passed by the High Court cannot fulfill the requisite length of service needed to claim regularisation of her services.
[Para 5]
