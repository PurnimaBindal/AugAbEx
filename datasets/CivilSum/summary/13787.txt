Constitution of India, Article 16 - Medical reimbursement - An employee who undergoes treatment for a chronic disease is entitled to reimbursement of medical bills incurred during the treatment.
Therefore, the petitioner who underwent bye-pass heart surgery in Escort Hospital and spent Rs. 2,40,000/- and another sum of Rs. 47,644/- on post indoor treatment is entitled to full medical reimbursement.
This decision is in line with the ruling in 2005(2) SCT 136 (SC).
