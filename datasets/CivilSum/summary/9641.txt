Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F - Retrenchment/Contract of service/Retrospectivity of enactment - Clause (bb) was added to Section 2(oo) of the Act by Amendment Act No. 49 of 1984 - Petitioner's services were terminated prior to this amendment - Section 25F applies - Clause (bb) cannot be applied retrospectively - Labour Court made an erroneous decision in rejecting the claim - The award was cancelled - The case is sent back to determine the claim's merit.
