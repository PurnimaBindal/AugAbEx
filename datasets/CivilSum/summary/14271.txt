State Bank of India Act, 1955, Section 49 - State Bank of India (Supervising Staff) Service Rules, 1975, Rule 51(2) - Enhancement of penalty - Appellate Authority has the power to enhance punishment of an employee who disagrees with the disciplinary authority - However, the Appellate Authority is legally obliged to provide reasons supporting their decision after considering the representation - In this case, the Appellate Authority failed to record reasons for their decision to enhance the punishment - Therefore, the order that increased the punishment should be cancelled.
