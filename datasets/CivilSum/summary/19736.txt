Food Corporations Act (37 of 1964), Section 45 - Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations (1977), Regulation 74 - Review - Departmental Proceedings on specific charges - The disciplinary authority found the delinquent exonerated based on the Enquiry Officer's report that included oral and documentary evidence admitted during the enquiry.
The disciplinary authority was satisfied with the report.
Therefore, the reviewing authority's decision to overturn the exoneration order after 4 years without notice and hearing to the exonerated employee on the basis of the wrong admission of documentary evidence by the Enquiry Officer was unreasonable and illegal.
The affected party must be given an opportunity to be heard.
