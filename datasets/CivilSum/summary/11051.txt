Constitution of India, Article 14 and 16 - Pension Regulation, Part I Rule 122(a) (II) - Qualifying service - Services rendered as minor before turning 17 years old - The provision stating that service rendered as a minor will not be counted towards qualifying service for pension and gratuity is unconstitutional and arbitrary.
There is no logical reason for this classification and it does not relate to the objectives of pension provision.
