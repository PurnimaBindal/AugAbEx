According to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F and 25-H, if an employee's appointment is for a fixed term and the services terminate automatically upon the expiry of said term, it does not constitute retrenchment.
In such cases, the provisions of Section 25-H need not be invoked.
Also, when making fresh appointments on the same posts, it is not a violation of Section 25-H to have ad hoc employees compete with other applicants each time such appointments are made.
