In regards to the employees of NDMC, those who have been granted the benefit of pay scales recommended by the report of Shiv Shankar Committee include daftry, peon, chowkidar and sweeper.
However, there is no reasonable justification for discriminating against the employees of the Auto Workshop that forms a part and parcel of the electricity wing of the NDMC.
Additionally, employees of Junior Navyug run by NDMC and Telephone Operators are not entitled to benefit from the pay scales recommended by the SSC.
[Paras 9 and 10]

The petitioner Class IV employees of NDMC holding posts of Duplicating Machine Operators and Gunmen/Dog Shooters, Juniors Technical Assistants (Hindi) and Pump drivers have been granted the benefit of pay scales recommended by the SSC.
Furthermore, ministerial staff working in all wings are entitled to the same benefit of pay scales recommended by the SSC as has been awarded to ministerial staff in the electricity wing of the NDMC as per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
