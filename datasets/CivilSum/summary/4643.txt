Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1972, Rule 7 - Departmental Enquiry - Avoiding enquiry for ulterior motives and arbitrary reasons - Enquiry should only be dispensed with if it is not reasonably practicable to hold one - No justification for dispensing with enquiry solely to avoid a weak case that is bound to fail.
[Paras 2, 5 and 7]
