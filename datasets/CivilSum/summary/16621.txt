Central Civil Service (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, Rule 5(1) - Termination of service for a temporary appointee - The respondent was appointed on an ad hoc basis, without undergoing any selection procedure, and without having the qualifications necessary for the post - An order was passed upon conducting an enquiry against the respondent employee for a complaint of sexual harassment - It is a well-established fact that a temporary appointee does not have the right to the post - Furthermore, the termination order will not be deemed as punitive just because an enquiry into the complaint by the respondent, which was proven to be false, was conducted.
