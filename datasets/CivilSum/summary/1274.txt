Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Section 9 - Central Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, Rule 5(1) - Termination of unconfirmed employee - Petitioner concealed fact of pendency of criminal case during verification process - Petitioner was later acquitted - Appointing authority had the right to terminate the petitioner's service for suppression of material information - Appointment obtained through incorrect information is not valid - Petitioner cannot claim the right to the post or protection of Act of 1949 - Show cause notice was sufficient, and no formal enquiry was needed in the absence of denial of the allegation.
