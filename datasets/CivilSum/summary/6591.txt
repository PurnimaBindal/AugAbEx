Constitution of India, Article 309-Gujarat Civil Services (Reservation of Vacancies for Ex-Servicemen in Class III and Class IV Posts and Services) Rules, 1975, Rule 6A-Reservation-Relaxation-The provisions of Rule 6A will be applied when there is a deficiency in the reserved quota, and it is necessary to fill the vacancy.
However, the Appointing Authority or Commission can determine the extent of relaxation that can be provided.
There is no reason for the Court to replace the decision made by the Appointing Authority unless it is shown to be arbitrary or mala fide.
