Constitution of India, Article 14 - Administrative Action - The Doctrine of equality and fair play - In Departmental proceedings - Misconduct cases - Respondents cannot discriminate between the appellant and 'V' when it comes to criteria of punishment - 'V' was cleared of charges due to acquittal in a criminal case - Therefore, the appellant deserves the same treatment in the matter of departmental punishment - Selective treatment cannot be allowed - The appeal has been dismissed.
