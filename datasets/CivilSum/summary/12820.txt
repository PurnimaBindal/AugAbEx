According to Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rules 244 (1), 244(2) and 91(B)(1) and Article 14 of the Constitution of India, cash payment of unutilised leave is mandatory to provide Govt. servants who retire from service with some money to handle their affairs.
There is no valid reason to differentiate between two classes of employees, and those who are compulsorily retired under Rule 244(2) are entitled to benefits under Rule 91(b) just as employees seeking voluntary retirement do under the same rule.
