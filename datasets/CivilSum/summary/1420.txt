Constitution of India, Article 311 (2) - Bombay Civil Services Rules, Rule 161 - Pre-mature retirement/Punishment/Disciplinary proceedings - The petitioner was retired prematurely under Service Rules based on a report of Vigilance in corruption charges.
The Review Committee made the decision to retire the petitioner prematurely because his integrity was reported to be doubtful.
However, pre-mature retirement cannot be used as a shortcut for disciplinary proceedings.
The order of pre-mature retirement has been quashed.
[Paras 20 and 21]
