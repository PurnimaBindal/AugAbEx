Constitution of India, Article 226 - Judicial review - Promotion - Scope of - Once the competent authority has taken into account relevant considerations and disregarded irrelevant factors, there is little room for judicial review as long as administrative decision has a nexus to the facts on record.
The court should only consider whether the correct process was followed, not the decision itself.
[Para 29]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Eligibility - Experience - A candidate cannot claim consideration for a selection post solely on the basis of seniority if they do not meet the eligibility criteria.
