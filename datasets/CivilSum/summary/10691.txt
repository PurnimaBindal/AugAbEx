Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - M.P. Civil Services (General Conditions of Service) Rules, 1961, Rule 12 - MP Class III Non- Ministerial Forest Services Recruitment Rules, 1967, Rule 13 - Determination of seniority - Seniority for the post of Forest Ranger is based on successful completion of training, not commencement of training.
The Notification dated 10.12.72 is irrelevant to determining seniority.
