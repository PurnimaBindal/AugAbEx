Under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sch2, Item 3, if a criminal proceeding and departmental enquiry are ongoing simultaneously, the departmental proceeding should not necessarily be stayed.
When criminal prosecution involves offences punishable under Section 304A and Section 338, IPC, and the departmental enquiry charges an employee with negligence for failing to anticipate and prevent the accident, the enquiry should not be stayed merely because of the criminal charges.
The charges in the enquiry are separate from the culpability of the criminal offences, and the enquiry should only be stayed if it is likely to cause serious prejudice to the defence in criminal trial.
