According to the Income Tax Act, 1961, Sections 2(6) and 36(1) and Income Tax Rules, 1962, Rules 89 and 91, an approved superannuation fund is set up from the employer's contributions who are given tax benefits.
In 1985, certain improvements were made to the executive staff pension fund of a company, but those benefits should not be available to existing pensioners.
This denial of benefits to existing pensioners is not arbitrary or violative of Article 14 of the Constitution.
The power to frame rules is not unguided, and Rules 89 and 91 are not unreasonable or arbitrary.
There is no reason to strike down any parts of the Act or Rules surrounding the approved superannuation fund scheme.
