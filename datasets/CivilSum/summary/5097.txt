Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Section 33-C(2) - Constitution of India - Recovery of dues from employer - According to orders passed under Section 33-C(2), the decree should be fully implemented - A suit was decreed in favor of the employee in 1981 stating that the dismissal was illegal - The employer has persistently and obstinately resisted the orders passed by various courts, including the Supreme Court.
- Finally, after nine years, the decree was complied with by giving only a fresh appointment.
- Since the petitioner's conduct was inequitable, he is not entitled to approach the High Court under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
