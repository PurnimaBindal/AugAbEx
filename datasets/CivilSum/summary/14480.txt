The petitioner was granted admission in Master of Engineering (Electronics) degree course based on his IETE degree.
The admission regulation states that for admission, the IETE degree should be attained after passing the Diploma examination.
While the IETE degree is higher than the Diploma, no objection was raised by the University at the time of admission, and even when an objection was later raised, the petitioner was allowed to continue and ultimately obtained a first division.
There was no suppression of fact or misrepresentation by the petitioner in securing admission, and therefore, the court directed the University to award a degree to the petitioner based on the results.
