Article 14 of the Indian Constitution pertains to Selection/Appointments/Recommendations.
The SSS Boards Haryana recommended the names of 157 candidates for Agriculture Development Officers (ADOs) against 79 available posts.
However, only 84 candidates were appointed while many posts remained vacant.
Several people were appointed on a temporary and adhoc basis based on Piara Singh's case and obtained stay orders.
Instructions have been given that if any vacancy arises due to the implementation of any order related to Piara Singh's case within 6 months up to 31.3.93, the department will fill it up strictly based on merit from the selected candidates recommended by the Board.
