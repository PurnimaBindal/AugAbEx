Andhra Pradesh Intermediate Education Act, 1971, Sections 7(3)(a) and 17 - Appointments - Promotion - The Board can appoint Officers and other Staff through direct recruitment, promotion from lower cadres or deputation.
There are no restrictions on the Board regarding the proportion of direct recruitment and promotions.
It is up to the Board to fix the proportion but the Secretary of the Board does not possess the power to fix any proportion.
[Paras 26, 33, 36 and 37]
