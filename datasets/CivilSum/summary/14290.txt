Constitution of India, Article 226 - Temporary status - Regularisation - Post of Peon - The petitioner worked as a daily wager from 7793 and only claimed temporary status as a peon on 10.10.95.
An office order dated 20.12.95 declared the appointment of the petitioner as co-terminus with the office of the Chairman.
The respondent was instructed to grant the petitioner temporary status as a peon after completing 206 days of continuous service.
Additionally, the respondent was directed to regularize the petitioner's service as a peon on a regular basis as and when a vacancy of a regular peon becomes available.
[Paras 10, 11, 12 & 15]
