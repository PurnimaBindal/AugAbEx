The government's instructions state that an employee must not be suspended for more than three months without permission of higher authorities due to sufficient reasons for suspension.
However, in this case, the petitioner was kept under suspension for over two years without any initiation of an enquiry.
There is no court case pending and no justification for the prolonged suspension.
The continued suspension of the petitioner is a violation of governmental instructions and the principle of natural justice.
As a result, the suspension order has been set aside.
