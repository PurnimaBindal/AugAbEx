Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Qualification/Appointment - The appellant was appointed as Principal, but later his appointment was cancelled due to lack of essential qualifications - A learned single judge dismissed the appellant's application for grant of stay and allowed the application seeking vacation of the ex parte order of stay - The appellant should be allowed to continue functioning as Principal until the writ petition is disposed of - The appellants filed an appeal - An interim order of injunction allowing the petitioner to continue service is not the norm, but rather an exception - An interim order allowing the appellant to continue in service may be justified if there is a clear case of illegality and arbitrariness where no other interim relief may be possible.
