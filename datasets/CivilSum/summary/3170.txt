Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Labour Court's decision to reinstate workman and award him half wages from demand date challenged - Evidence presented by both workman and management representative - Labour Court correctly concluded master-servant relationship existed and acted on workman's testimony that he was employed by petitioner firm for more than 240 days and terminated without inquiry or compensation - Management's evidence found to be unconvincing - Petition to interfere with Labour Court's award denied.
