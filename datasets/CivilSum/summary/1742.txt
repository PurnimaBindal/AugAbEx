Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39 - Equal pay for equal work - The doctrine of "equal pay for equal work" must take into account factors such as responsibilities, reliability, experience, confidentiality, function, and qualifications, instead of just designation or nature of work.
An expert body like the Pay Commission or the Government must determine parity of pay scales.
Court interference is only allowed when the decision of the Government is found to be unjust, prejudicial, or irrational.
Employees should present their grounds to the employer first.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39 - Equal pay for equal work - A demand notice is necessary for a writ of mandamus to be maintainable for the grant of equal pay for equal work.
Without a notice, a writ of mandamus cannot be sought.
