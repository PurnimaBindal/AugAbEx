The Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 govern misconduct/absence without leave.
“Misconduct” in the service regulations includes remaining absent without leave.
An employee can be punished for unauthorized leave, but if they establish a satisfactory reason for their absence and apply for extraordinary leave without pay, management must grant the leave.
If an application for extraordinary leave is rejected without proper consideration of medical certificates, the resulting absence cannot be considered misconduct.
Punishment for such an absence is unjustifiable.
[Paras 4 and 5] B.
The Constitution of India, Article 226, governs termination/reinstatement/back wages.
Services cannot be terminated without holding an enquiry on the ground of unauthorised absence.
If an absence is justified, it cannot be treated as misconduct.
The High Court ordered reinstatement with liberty to hold an enquiry.
When services were terminated after the enquiry, the petitioner was paid 50% of the back wages, but the remaining wages were denied since they didn't actually work during that period.
The order of termination was quashed.
