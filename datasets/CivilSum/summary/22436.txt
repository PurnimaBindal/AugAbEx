According to Article 309 of the Constitution of India, the Punjab Government's letters dated 651997 and 9.10.1989 provide instructions on extension in service.
The instructions of 651997 do not apply to State Award-winning teachers since they do not reference the instructions of 9.10.1989.
Thus, the High Court made an error in concluding that the letters dated 91089 were also implicitly revoked by the decision/letter dated 651997.
