According to the Constitution of India's Article 16 and 4, and the Intermediate Education Act, 1921's Section 16-E, a Junior High School Headmaster working in an upgraded High School must have four years of experience as a Junior High School Headmaster to be considered for the High School Headmaster position.
The appellant did not possess the necessary experience, and the selection committee's decision to appoint Respondent No. 5 as Headmaster is legal.
