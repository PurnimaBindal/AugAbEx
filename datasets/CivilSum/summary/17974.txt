The waiting list in the selection process under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India ensures that if a vacancy arises during the specified period, the selection made earlier is still valid and the whole process need not be repeated.
In the case of the Assistant Advocate General panel, where the first candidate resigned and the second refused to join, the third candidate on the panel was entitled to be appointed as Assistant Advocate General when the vacancy occurred.
