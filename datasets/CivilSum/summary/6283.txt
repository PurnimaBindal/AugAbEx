Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 317(a)(v) - Pensionary benefits - Qualifying service - The plaintiff, despite serving as NDS, had their service benefit taken over by the state of Punjab, even though control remained with the Union of India.
Following an amendment to the scheme, the plaintiff applied to transfer and serve as PTI.
If selected by the State Government, the plaintiff would have to resign.
The court held that this was essentially a transfer case and the plaintiff would be entitled to the benefit of their past service prior to submitting resignation.
This past service would now be considered as qualifying service, making the plaintiff eligible for pension benefits.
