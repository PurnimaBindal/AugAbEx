The Constitution of India, Articles 226, 15, 16 - Selection criteria - Shortlisted candidates summoned for selection by the commission - An argument was put forward that the Commission did not follow the selection criteria framed by the Board by eliminating experience in areas such as sports - It was held that there was no significant difference between the criteria followed by the Commission and that of the Board - The petitioner's certificate did not support his claim for sport achievements - The criteria applied by the Commission for shortlisting candidates for selection was not deemed invalid - The petition was dismissed.
