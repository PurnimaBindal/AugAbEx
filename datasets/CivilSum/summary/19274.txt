Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Merit - Fixation of seniority in order of merit - Authority's actions in accordance with non-contrary rules are not arbitrary.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Labour Service (Class I & II) Rules, 1956, Rule 9 - Seniority - Confirmation - Probation - Dates of confirmation and completion of probationary period are distinct - The competent authority decides when an official completes the probationary period and is confirmed - There is no automatic confirmation.

Punjab Labour Service (Class I & II) Rules, 1956, Rules 4 and 9 - Qualifications/Eligibility/Seniority - Aeronautical Engineering qualification for Inspector of Factories Class-II added only through 1979 amendment - Appointment and confirmation before the amendment does not grant seniority based on the qualification - Those who held the pre-existing qualification are senior to those appointed after the amendment.
