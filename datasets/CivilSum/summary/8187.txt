Punjab Subordinate Education Service Rules, 1937 - Amendment of service rules - Pre-existing vacancies - Posts against which the petitioners were appointed were in existence and vacant prior to enforcement of amended rules - The vacancies which were in existence prior to amendment of Rules for direct recruits had to be filled by following the unamended rules - Date of occurrence of vacancy would be the sine qua non.
[Paras 19, 20 and 23]

Punjab Subordinate Education Service Rules, 1937 - Regularisation of service - Selection - Appointments made in accordance with the existing Rules through a duly constituted Selection Committee against existing vacancies and allowed to continue on ad hoc basis for about 7 to 9 years may be considered as regular for all purposes.
[Paras 20 and 23]

Punjab Subordinate Education Service Rules, 1937 (as amended by 1990 Rules) - Recruitment Rules - Qualifications - Without qualifications having been laid down by the Rules or by executive instructions, the 1990 Rules are not workable.
[Para 24]
