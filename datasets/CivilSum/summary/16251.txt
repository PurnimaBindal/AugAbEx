Promotion to the position of Sub Divisional Officers under the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 can include Junior Engineers and Head Draftsmen employed by the Corporation.
However, the State Government has imposed a ban on regular promotions, resulting in appointments being made under deputation as a "redeployment."
This action is a fraudulent exercise of power and an abuse of jurisdiction violating Articles 14 and 16.
