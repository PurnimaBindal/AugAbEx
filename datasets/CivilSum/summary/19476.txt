Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Anomaly in special pay - Petitioner notes that district attorneys working in district offices receive higher special pay than those in other offices, and while the anomaly was removed, the order was passed with prospective effect after pay scale revisions, providing no meaningful relief to the petitioner - According to the petitioner, anomalies should be removed from the date they existed, not from the date they are corrected.
