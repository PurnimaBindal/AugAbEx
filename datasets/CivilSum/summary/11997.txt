West Bengal Schools (Recruitment of Non-Teaching Staff) Rules, 2005, and West Bengal Schools (Control of Expenditure) Act, 2005, Section 22 - Validity of Rules - The contention that the state government had no power to make Rules is unfounded as the provisions of Section 22 of West Bengal Schools (Control of Expenditure) Act empowered the state government to frame and notify the Rules the moment provisions came into force, which occurred on December 27th, 2005 - The provisions of West Bengal Schools (Recruitment of Non-Teaching Staff) Rules do not violate provisions in Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
