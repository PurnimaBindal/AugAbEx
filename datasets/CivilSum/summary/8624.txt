The appellant did not contest the proposition of law that the date of birth cannot be corrected at the end of one's service career, although it is no longer in question.
Despite the fact that the appellant allowed the decree to be implemented and full benefit to be received by the respondent, who retired on the correct date of birth, without raising this point before the trial court or lower appellate court, the concurrent findings of fact based on proper appreciation of evidence are typically not disturbed in second appeal.
In this case, it would not be fair or just to order the individual to repay the salary, even if later relief is denied, thus the appeal is dismissed.
