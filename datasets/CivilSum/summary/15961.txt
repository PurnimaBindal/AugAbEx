Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Departmental Enquiry - Natural justice - Show cause notice after ex parte enquiry - The petitioner has challenged the enquiry proceedings on the grounds of non-compliance of natural justice principles and non-supply of necessary documents.
However, the authority has alleged that the petitioner was intentionally avoiding the proceedings.
In the interest of justice and compliance with natural justice principles, authorities have been directed to hold fresh proceedings, furnish full opportunities to the petitioner, and supply the necessary documents to him.
