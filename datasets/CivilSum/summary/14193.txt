The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, and military pension instructions dated 30101997 for retired Army jawans are outdated and cannot be applied retrospectively.
The Supreme Court has determined that instructions are prospective, and the cut-off date must have a nexus and cannot be arbitrary.
[Paras 6 and 7]

Army Pension Regulations, Regulation 168, is only applicable to those discharged on becoming invalid, and the petitioner is not entitled to the benefit of Regulation 168.
[Para 9]

The classification for grant of pension to Army jawans who have been discharged from the Army on becoming invalid and those who retired in good health is reasonable and cannot be struck down as discriminatory as per Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, and Army Pension Regulations, Regulation 168.
[Paras 10 and 11]
