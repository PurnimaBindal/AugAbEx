Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Section 11-A - Compulsory retirement for misconduct - Petitioner, a clerk who had exchanged hot words and scuffled with his senior colleague (an Accountant), cannot be punished with compulsory retirement for this kind of conduct as it does not constitute gross misconduct - The charge of such an offense cannot result in extreme penalty of compulsory retirement from service - Therefore, the order of punishment is quashed.
