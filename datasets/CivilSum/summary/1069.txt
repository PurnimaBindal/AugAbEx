Constitution of India, Articles 12, 226 - Service dispute - Employee of Society - Writ jurisdiction - Society aims to eliminate untouchability - Society receives grant in aid from Government, but is not controlled by it - Ruling, respondent is not a 'State' according to Article 12 of Constitution - Private organization will be subject to writ jurisdiction only if a statute or regulations regulate their service conditions - Doing work for society's benefit does not mean it is performing a public duty required by law - Therefore, the petition is not maintainable.
