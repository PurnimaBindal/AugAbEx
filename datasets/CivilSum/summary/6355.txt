Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Jammu and Kashmir Private Educational Institutions Grant-in-Aid Rules, 1975, Rules 9(A) and Rule 13 - Grant-in-aid - Private Educational Institutions - Private Educational Institutions have no guaranteed right to grant-in-aid, only the right to apply for it - This right may lead to consideration by the authorities and the obligation to examine and process the cases - The court can only step in if the action of the authority is determined to be discriminatory - If the petitioners are found ineligible or the government cannot provide the grant due to lack of funds, the court cannot order the release of grant-in-aid.
