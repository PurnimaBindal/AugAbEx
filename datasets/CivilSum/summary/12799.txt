Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Evidence Act, Section 115 - Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation Servants (Conduct and Discipline) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 23 - Departmental Enquiry/Estoppel - Enquiry Officer - An outsider can be appointed as Enquiry Officer in a Departmental Enquiry to investigate allegations of misconduct against an employee, as Service Regulations do not forbid it - Authorities are not obligated to appoint only an employee of the Corporation as the Enquiry Officer - Delinquent who accepts the appointment of an outsider as Enquiry Officer cannot question their appointment after the proceedings have concluded without a favourable verdict.
[Paras 2 to 6]
