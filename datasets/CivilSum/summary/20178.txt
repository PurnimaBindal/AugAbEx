The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 136 and 311, outline the punishment of dismissal for government employees.
In the case of a constable being dismissed for charges of intoxication, smoking, and misconduct with a superior, no irregularity was found in the disciplinary proceedings or the finding of guilt.
Despite the claim that the employee was under suspension at the time of intoxication, the employee still has no right to freely conduct themselves within the workplace while on suspension.
The tribunal erred in law by interfering with the dismissal order passed by the disciplinary authority.
Therefore, the appeal was allowed by setting aside the impugned order.
