The Petitioner, represented by Mr. GC Gupta, Advocate, challenged the Order passed by the Director of the Archaeology and Museum Department, Punjab, which reverted him from the post of Gallery Assistant to a Clerk.
The Petitioner claimed that the reversion Order was illegal, ultra vires, without jurisdiction, opposed to law and rules of natural justice.
The Petitioner was appointed as a Gallery Assistant on an ad hoc basis and was regularised to the post of a Gallery Assistant.
The Order of confirmation as a Gallery Assistant cannot be termed as illegal.
The Petitioner cannot claim to be promoted as of right in clerical cadre.
The Department may consider his claim on compassionate ground, if a case is made out.
The appeal was dismissed.
