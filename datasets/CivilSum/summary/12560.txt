Constitution of India, Article 226 - Technicalities/Bona fide mistake/Admission/Education - Admission in Engineering Courses - If a student makes a bona fide mistake in filling up option forms, the institution should rectify the mistakes rather than penalize the student - Changing a student's branch of course without their clear option and against their wishes is unjust punishment and technicality that cannot be allowed by the Court.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admissions/Education - Number of seats - Admission and change of branches of courses in engineering courses - The Court has the power to direct the institution to create an additional seat in Engineering courses if it is flexible enough to increase and decrease the number of seats, in order to ensure justice.
