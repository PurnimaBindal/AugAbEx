Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Dismissal - Inquiry proceedings - Suspension - In accordance with rule 303 of the rules, an inquiry proceeding and action must be completed within the first year of suspension.
If not completed within the time limit, the case must be referred to the Board of Directors - The petitioner was suspended and the inquiry proceedings were not completed within a year from the date of suspension, but without causing prejudice to the petitioner, the Board took the decision to dismiss the petitioner from the bank's service - Rule 303 is only a directory rule and did not impact the case - The petition was dismissed.
