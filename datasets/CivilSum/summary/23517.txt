The petitioner, a government employee on deputation to a corporation as Chief Engineer, accused the government of trying to circumvent the court's order and deny the petitioner's benefits.
Once the claim inter se had been adjudicated and the claim of petitioner was declared final, the government could no longer go behind the orders and truncate the effect of the court's decisions.
The Respondent who committed contempt was found guilty and sentenced to undergo simple imprisonment for one month.
