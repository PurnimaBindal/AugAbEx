Jammu and Kashmir Classification, Control and Appeal (Rules) 1956, Rule 33(4) - Enquiry - Natural justice - The proceedings of an enquiry are to be vitiated and quashed if they are conducted without issuing a charge sheet and without giving any opportunities to the delinquent.
This includes the formation, report and appointment of the enquiry officer.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]
Jammu and Kashmir Classification, Control and Appeal (Rules) 1956, Rules 31 and 33(1) - Suspension - Enquiry - Natural justice - The imposition of a suspension following a final enquiry is considered a punishment.
The suspension order will be quashed if the enquiry is conducted in violation of Rule 33(1) and (4).
[Paras 9 and 10]
