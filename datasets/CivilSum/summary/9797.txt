The Labour Court denied the petitioner's request for reinstatement, citing the Industrial Disputes Act and Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India.
However, the court failed to consider the petitioner's argument that his juniors were still employed and did not offer any explanation for this issue.
In accordance with a Supreme Court decision, the employer is required to maintain a register of all seasonal workers and make a public announcement when a new season begins.
If the petitioner reports for work, the employer must engage him based on his seniority and the exigency of the work.
