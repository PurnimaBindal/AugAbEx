According to Constitution of India, Articles 14, 162 and 226, individuals must have Degrees and Diplomas.
The Academic Council of the University of Kanpur made a decision regarding a course of study for the GHMS degree that lasted for 5 years, but excluded a 6 month internship.
The UP Service Commission cannot disagree with the decision made by the University and Academic Council, as they were authorized to prescribe the specific course of study unless it was changed by the University.
