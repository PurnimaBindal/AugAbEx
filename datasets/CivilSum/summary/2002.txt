Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation - Writ of Mandamus - High Court has the power to issue a mandamus directing Authorities to consider the claim of Respondents for regularisation in service according to relevant Rules or to direct them to regularise the services - However, the Court cannot independently decide whether or not the employee is entitled to regularisation, as this involves various questions of facts.
The eligibility, conduct, and work of the employee, as well as any specific conditions for regularisation, must be examined by the authorities concerned before passing an order for regularisation.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation - Rules and Regulations - Petitioner-respondents worked in Forest Department for over three years - The learned Single Judge directed the appellants to regularise their services - However, the appellants must consider the case of petitioner-Respondents for regulation of their services in accordance with applicable Rules and Regulations for their appointments - The order of the learned Single Judge is set aside, and the appeal is dismissed.
