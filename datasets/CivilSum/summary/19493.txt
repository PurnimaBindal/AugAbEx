Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Medical and Health Department Nursing Superintendents, Matrons, Sister Tutors/Health Nurses (Teaching), Nursing Sisters, Public Health Nurses - Staff Nurses and Male Nurses (State Service Class III) Rules, 1964, Rule 11 - Seniority - For selection to the post of Public Health Nurses, interview is conducted by the Haryana Subordinate Services Selection Board.
Direct appointment is made to the post.
The period for which the Nurses had been working as Public Health Nurses before selection is not valid for determining seniority.
Seniority must be established in the order of merit as determined by the Board.
