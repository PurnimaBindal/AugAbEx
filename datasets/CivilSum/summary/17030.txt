Constitution of India, Article 30 - Prospectus and information brochure - Minority institutions - Admissions to minority community candidates - The prospectus provides reservation only for reserved category of residents of the State, but in view of Article 30 of the Constitution, it must be read to mean that if candidates from within the State belonging to that community were not available to fill all the reserved seats, the minority institutions established by the minorities must be allowed to admit the requisite number of students of their community from even out of the State - Cancellation of admission given in reservation to the candidates from out of the State held illegal - Cancellation of such admissions by the University held illegal and quashed.
