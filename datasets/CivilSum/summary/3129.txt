Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Transfer - Petitioner, who was employed as a Mile Mazdoor, was accused of negligence of work, insolence, and insubordination without proper enquiry into the allegations made against him or the opportunity to defend himself when transfer, which contained stigmatic remarks and was punitive in nature, was ordered - The powerful weapon of transfer had been used maliciously and vindictively - As a result, the order of transfer was clearly arbitrary and capricious and needed to be overturned.
