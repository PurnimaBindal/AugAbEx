Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 Section 25-F - Daily Wager - The respondent, who worked as a temporary sweeper for only one hour per day, was terminated from service.
The court held that there is a fundamental difference between a person who works on a part-time basis and one who is a daily wage worker on a regular basis.
Therefore, the order of reinstatement with 50% back wages for the period worked (240 days) was quashed.
