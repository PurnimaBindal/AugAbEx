The petitioner sought pre-mature retirement while in receipt of NPA in Haryana Law Department, but was denied counting of NPA towards grant of pension.
The petitioner contended that NPA is a part of special pay, which is a part of the basic pay, and therefore, with the enforcement of the new grades the pension should also be counted by taking into consideration the NPA.
However, it was held that rule 252 is not applicable in the present case as NPA has never been treated as a part of pay or special pay, and it is not an integral part of the special pay.
The government has already given benefit to pre-111996 retirees by allowing pension at the rate of 50% minimum of the pay scales which were introduced with effect from 111996.
The petition was ultimately dismissed.
