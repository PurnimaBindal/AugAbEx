The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 19, 21 and 226, along with other related laws, such as the Bombay Primary Education Act, 1947 and the Central Board of Secondary Education Bye-laws, Bye-Law 11, outline the parameters around fee hikes in private unaided schools affiliated to CBSE.
The CBSE has been directed to appoint a High Level Committee to examine complaints about fee hikes by a school, and the Committee must submit its report within two months.
Meanwhile, students must pay fees at last year's rates.
After receiving the report, the CBSE will make a decision on the fee structure and fee-hike.
