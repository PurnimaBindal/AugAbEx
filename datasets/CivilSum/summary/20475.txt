Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Recruitment Examination - Selection process - Recruitment to Non-Technical Class III posts in the department of Government of India and in subordinate offices - The purpose of recruitment examination is to choose the best candidates for the job.
In order to achieve this objective, a common select list of successful candidates belonging to all the zones must be made.
This is because zone-wise selection would result in injustice and discrimination against meritorious candidates who secured more marks than those who were selected from other zones.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection Process - Recruitment to non-Technical Class III posts in the department of Govt of India and in subordinate offices - The process of zonewise selection on the basis of separate merit lists drawn for each zone is not fair as it would result in the selection of a candidate with lesser marks over a more meritorious one, thereby violating the rule of equal chance for equal marks.
This practice is a violation of Articles 14 and 16.

Appointment - Rule of - In order to provide equal opportunity for every individual in the country, the merit list must be the best when selecting a candidate for recruitment for advertised posts.
