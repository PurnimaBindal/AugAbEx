According to Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, a petitioner who was transferred from Karnal to Kurukshetra claimed to have been transferred six times within a 5-1/2 month period.
However, the court ruled that it would not interfere with the order of transfer unless it was passed with malice or in violation of service rules and transfer guidelines without proper justification.
Since there was no evidence of malice or mala fide on the part of the respondents, any previous transfers of the petitioner would not help prove arbitrary or discriminatory actions on their part.
[Paras 6 and 9]
