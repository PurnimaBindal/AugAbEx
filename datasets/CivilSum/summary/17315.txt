Pepsu Road Transport Corporation (Conditions of Appointment and Service) Regulations, 1981, Regulation 26 - Punishment - Reversion to lower post - The appellant argued that the reversion was invalid because the duration of the order was not specified.
However, it was concluded that since no period was specified, the appellant could be considered for promotion at the earliest available opportunity and that Regulation 26 had not been violated.
