PCMS Class I (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1940, Rules 3 and 4 - The appointment of Respondent 1 to PCMS Class I - Challenged, to be in violation of Rules 3 and 4 of Rules - Respondent-Appellant admitted the averment of the writ petitioner (in CWP No. 3977/70) to the extent that Respondent No. 4 did not possess any degree like MBBS or an equivalent degree but argued that he was recruited against post of Bacteriologist and writ of quo-warranto not a proper remedy - The Punjab State after serving show cause notice to respondent 4 passed an order, thus taking the post of Bacteriologist out from PCMS-I and declared it as an ex-cadre post - Concluded, Respondent No. 1 cannot claim selection grade or promotion to the higher posts in the hierarchy of PCMS posts - Appeal filed by the State of Punjab granted and the one filed by the petitioner rejected.
