Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Regularisation - Appointment as a clerk - The petitioner's claim for regularisation was denied due to lack of appointment against any sanctioned post and failure of the school authority to notify the concerned authority of said appointment - The Minority Institution holds independent status with regard to the appointment of teaching and non-teaching staff as per their requirements - Petitioner's appointment cannot be considered as a back door appointment - Initial appointment was not against a sanctioned post, but later shifted to a vacancy of sanctioned post on 23391 - At the time of initial appointment petitioner's institution was a minority institution - It was held that petitioner's service shall be regularised as National service from the date of initial appointment but financial benefit will be provided only from the date of approval by competent authority.
