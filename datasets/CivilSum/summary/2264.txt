Section 30(1) Third Proviso of the Workmen's Compensation Act (8 of 1923) requires employers to deposit the entire amount of compensation as a condition for filing appeal, which is not deemed discriminatory or arbitrary.
The Workmen's Compensation Act, Payment of Wages Act, and Payment of Gratuity Act were created to protect the interests of employees, and thus, the legislature has imposed an absolute condition, which cannot be considered discriminatory or arbitrary.
