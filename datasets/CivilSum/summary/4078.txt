Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination - The petitioner's service was ended due to absence from duty.
However, the petitioner argued that they joined at a transferred location and later took medical leave, completing over 240 days of service.
The respondents did not follow the requirements of Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, leading to an invalid termination.
