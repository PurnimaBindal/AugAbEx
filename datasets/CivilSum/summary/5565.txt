Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability pension - The petitioner was denied disability pension, despite receiving a recommendation from the Medical Board at the time of invaliding out of service - Court must ensure that the ailment was caused due to military service - The Court considers an alternative remedy only when exercising its discretion - The fact that an appeal is pending before the Ministry of Defence cannot be used against the petitioner - The Ministry cannot take its own time in such matters - Therefore, the respondents are directed to pay the petitioner disability pension.
[Paras 9 to 12]
