Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Regularisation Policy - Illegal and irregular appointments - Any appointments made to public service without advertising vacancies to the general public or calling candidates through an employment exchange are illegal - Neither the state nor executive instructions under Article 162 or 309 can regularize such illegal appointments - This would violate Article 309 of the Constitution and the equality clauses in Articles 14 and 16 - Even short-term contractual appointments cannot be regularized unless advertised for regular positions - Exceptions can be made for fully eligible individuals who have been working in the position for over 10 years and are needed as a regular employee, but this is a one-time measure to avoid hardship.
