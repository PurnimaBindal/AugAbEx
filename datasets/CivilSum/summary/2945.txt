Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dismissal from service - Negligence - Respondent Company lost goods worth Rs. 15 lakhs - Criminal and Departmental proceedings based on identical set of facts - The Departmental Authority found the Petitioner guilty while he was acquitted in the criminal case - Considering that the Departmental findings should not stand once acquitted in criminal proceedings, it is held that it would be unjust, unfair, oppressive - There can be no charge against the Petitioner once the original charge evaporates, which could be merged with appellate or review decision in confirming the order of dismissal - The Petitioner, who has retired, is directed to be treated in service from the date of suspension till retirement - Wages and retiral benefits should be paid accordingly.
[Paras 10, 11, 13 and 14]
