Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Disciplinary proceedings - Removal from Service - Dismissed case of No evidence - The petitioner was removed from services on the basis of an enquiry report, which held them guilty of issuing a cover note for a bus by making the coverage of risk from the back date.
However, it is not a case of no evidence as findings of enquiry officer were not based on surmises or presumptions.
Besides, due procedure was complied with.
Thus, the petition is dismissed.
[Paras 6, 7 and 8]
