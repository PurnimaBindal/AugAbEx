The Medical Council of India granted migration to the appellant for the MBBS course after considering her rank as second in merit and the University adjusted her for the 1994 quota.
MD University permitted the appellant to join and complete the MBBS course, which is now in its final year.
The High Court wrongly cancelled the appellant's admission without considering her unique circumstances, as no grounds were given for such an action.
Thus, the appellant is allowed to continue pursuing her studies in the MBBS course, according to Article 136 of the Constitution of India.
