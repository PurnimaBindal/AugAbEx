Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Retrenchment - Workman who voluntarily abstained from work for long and did not report for duty despite a letter sent by employer cannot be considered for retrenchment - The case of such workman who voluntarily abandoned job cannot fall under Section 25-F and violation of the same cannot be claimed.
[Paras 20, 24, 25 and 27]
