Constitution of India, Article 226 - Service Rules for Postal ED Staff, Section IV, Clause (3) - Appointment - Qualification - Rule for appointment of Extra Departmental Branch Post Master [`ED BPM'] - Candidates must prove their solvency by providing evidence of property ownership and income, but certificate from Mandal Revenue Officer is not the only option, other forms of proof can be accepted - However, if there is a document of title like conveyance or gift-deed, the certificate from MRO cannot be used instead.
