Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - The petitioner was selected as ASI Police through SSS Board, Haryana and was undergoing training as ASI when he was also selected as Inspector of Police.
The training for both positions was the same and completed by the petitioner by virtue of court orders.
Although he was discharged from the post of probationer Inspector of Police due to a court order, he neither resigned from nor was discharged from his post as ASI.
The petitioner cannot be deprived of his ASI position for which he was regularly selected, and the court directed the respondents to continue him in that position with all benefits.
[Paras 8 and 9]
