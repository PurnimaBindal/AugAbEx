Life Insurance Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1960, Regulation 12 - The respondent was dismissed from service due to unauthorised absence from duty and was later declined re-employment.
However, the Division Bench allowed the appeal, ruling that the respondent should be deemed to have continued in service until superannuation and be entitled to terminal benefits and compensation.
The Single Judge's dismissal of the writ petition was deemed right, but the Division Bench's argument was not justified.
Despite the respondent's delay in filing the petition, the impugned order cannot be sustained.
The respondent is entitled to costs quantified at Rs. 25,000/- in consideration of his early dismissal and deprivation of other benefits.
