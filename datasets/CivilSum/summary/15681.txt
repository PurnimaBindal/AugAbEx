Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 37 - Retiral benefits - Question whether those who previously worked for the Central Government for 15-20 years are eligible for pro rata pension, gratuity, leave encashment etc on absorption in public sector undertaking - Held, No - Quasi-permanent employees at the time of absorption are excluded under Rule 37 of Pension Rules and deemed to have retired - IVth Pay Commission distinction between temporary and quasi-permanent employee took effect on 111986 and cannot be retrospective - Petitioners retired before 111986, therefore cannot claim benefits - Petition dismissed.
