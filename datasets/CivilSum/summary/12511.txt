Kurukshetra University Calendar, Volume I, Chapter II, Rules 20 and 20(v) - Termination of service/Retrenchment-Retrenchment of Lecturer due to reduction of work - The college failed to report the reduction of work, and the work remaining did not entitle the petitioner to be retrenched.
The petitioner's service was wrongfully terminated.
However, since there is no work left for the petitioner, the college cannot be forced to reinstate him.
The college is required to pay compensation of Rs. 5000/- to the petitioner.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Termination - Reinstatement - Compensation - The termination of the lecturer was deemed wrongful, but reinstatement was not possible because there is no work left in that subject at the college.
The college is required to pay compensation.
