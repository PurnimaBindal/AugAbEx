Jammu and Kashmir Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rules 5 and 14 - J & K Education (Subordinate Service) Recruitment Rules, Rule 13 - Appointment - The private respondents were appointed as Junior Assistants without following the rules or allowing open competition.
The Rehbar-e-Taleem scheme does not cover Junior Assistant positions and no advertisements were issued to call for applications, so the appointments cannot legally stand.
[Paras 15, 16] B.
Appointment - Petitioners challenging appointment - Locus standi - The Petitioners could not have applied for the position because it was not advertised, thus their objection is valid.
