Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226/227 - Army Act, Amended sub-section (2) of section 123 - A Retired Military person is liable to be tried by GCM for offences committed during service - The same punishment can be awarded as per the Army Act - If the trial initiates within 3 years after superannuation - The punishment given is valid.
