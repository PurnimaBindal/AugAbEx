According to Sections 19 and 21 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, the Commissioner has the jurisdiction to award compensation for the death of a workman.
The respondent's claim for compensation was allowed by the Commissioner as the owner of the vehicle admitted to employment, wages and death in the course of employment.
The appellant contended that the Commissioner lacked jurisdiction and the application should have been filed at the place of the accident.
However, the notification which stated the place of the claim fell under the Commissioner's jurisdiction and the employer's admittance of the accident taking place during the course of employment was rightfully relied upon.
Thus, the dismissal of the appeal was justified.
