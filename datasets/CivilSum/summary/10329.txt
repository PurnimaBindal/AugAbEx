Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Termination - Reinstatement - Back wages - Respondent No. 2, a terminated workman, was not appointed through proper procedure and was also the real brother of the Manager - The reason stated for not following proper procedure was the respondent's relation to the Manager - As the appointment was unlawful, the respondent was not entitled to notice - Additionally, the appointment was deemed mischievous, so unfair labour practice principles cannot apply - The Labour Court's award to reinstate respondent No. 2 with full back wages is overturned.
