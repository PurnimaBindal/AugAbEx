Constitution of India, Article 14 - Rules for Management of Recognized Non-Government Institutions (Aided and Unaided), 1969, Rule 28 - The trial court directed the District Inspector of Schools to form a selection committee - Recruitment Rules provide for sufficient safeguards to ensure a fair and unbiased selection process where no member of the selection committee can be a candidate's relative - It was held that the District Inspector of Schools should not have been directed to constitute the Selection Committee unless gross illegalities are committed while constituting the panel, as it falls under the power of the Managing Committee.
