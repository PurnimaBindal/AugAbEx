Constitution of India, Article 16 specifies the conditions of promotion.
In this case, the petitioner was considered fully qualified for promotion to the post of Subedar but was denied the promotion as the confidential report was not received from the Regiment.
The petitioner cannot be held responsible for the fault of the authorities, and the respondents cannot use their own error to deny the petitioner's legitimate right.
Such an action is violative of Article 16.
