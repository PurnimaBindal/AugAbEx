As per Article 14 of the Constitution of India, the qualifications required for a teacher in Haryana are Matriculation with JBT, however, petitioner BA, B.Ed.
was not considered despite having higher qualifications.
The question of whether someone with higher qualifications than those prescribed for the post can be considered was previously addressed by the Full Bench in Som Datt v. State of Haryana, 1983 (3) SLR 141, and is now being referred to the Full Bench for reconsideration.
