The Punjab National Bank (Officers) Service Regulations state that completion of training is necessary for confirmation in service, and passing the bank's confirmation test is included in this requirement.
Even if the probation period ends, failure to pass the confirmation test means a bank employee is not entitled to confirmation in service.
In the case at hand, the respondent was given three opportunities to pass the test, but was unable to qualify.
Therefore, he is not entitled to confirmation in service.
