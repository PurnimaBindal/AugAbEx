Appointments of ad hoc Lecturers in 1989 were made under the 1937 Rules for a Class III post, which is excluded from UPSC Regulations - Appointments were based on merit via departmental Selection Committee after advertisement and requisition to Employment exchange - Petitioners fulfilled eligibility and continuously worked satisfactorily - Previous appointments were held to be regular in nature - New Rules classifying post as Category 'A' cannot be applied retrospectively - Petitioners' appointments directed to be regularized from initial dates with all consequential benefits.
