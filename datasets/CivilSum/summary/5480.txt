Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Age of Superannuation - As per clause 22A of certified standing orders, the age of retirement on superannuation of a workman is 58 years, with a provision for extension for a further period of two years if the workman is medically fit.
In this case, the workman failed to produce any medical fitness certificate to the employer, and hence, the Labour Court held that the workman superannuated on attaining 58 years of age.
[Para 8]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Back wages - The workman was terminated from services in May 1979 and raised an industrial dispute with the appropriate Government for the first time on 16.7.1994 (i.e., after 15 years).
He claimed that he was corresponding with the employer during this period and could not seek legal remedy earlier due to paucity of funds and proper legal advice.
However, the Labour Court found this explanation unsatisfactory.
Furthermore, the workman continued to occupy the company quarter for the last 15 years.
Therefore, he is not entitled to any back wages from the date of termination until he raised the industrial dispute.
[Paras 10 and 11]
