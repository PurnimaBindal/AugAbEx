Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Selection process - Challenge as to - Appointment to the post of Assistant Professor in General Surgery - Merit list finalised by Selection Committee after following procedure laid down for selection - An unsuccessful candidate challenges as to the selection of two other candidates who scored 85% and 79% respectively - The petitioner secured only 73% - One of the candidates possessing 79% is also challenging the appointment of the candidate who scored 85% - The merit of the appellant and selected candidate was assessed based on marks obtained under different heads such as basics, research work, and interview in accordance with policy and rules - The Selection Committee comprises experts of acclaimed repute in their fields - There is nothing on record to show that the appellant was not treated fairly - The appeal is dismissed.
[Paras 11 to 16]
