Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Seniority-cum- merit - The senior employee is to be selected subject to suitability if the rule or policy of promotion considers seniority-cum-merit as criteria for consideration and selection - The senior cannot be bypassed or superseded by the employer by considering the comparative merit of the junior, unless the senior is found unfit for the promotion to the higher post - The employer's discretion to promote any person or to adopt any other method to fill up the post can be exercised only for justified administrative reasons - The petitioner was unfairly superseded despite being senior to the person who was promoted, as the decision was based purely on reports and service record - The respondents are directed to promote the petitioner from the date on which his junior was promoted and release him arrears of salary of the higher post from that date, since the petitioner has already retired during the pendency of the petition.
