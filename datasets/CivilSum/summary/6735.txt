Central Reserve Police Force Act 1949, Sections 9, 10(n) & 12 - Double jeopardy - Imprisonment - Dismissal from service - The conviction under Section 10(n) and subsequent imprisonment and dismissal from service under Section 12 do not constitute double jeopardy as the punishment and powers under both sections are independent.
The Commandant is authorized to take action under Section 12.
If the conviction under Section 10(n) becomes final, the order passed under Section 12 cannot be challenged.
