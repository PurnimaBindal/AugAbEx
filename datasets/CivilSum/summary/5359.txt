Constitution of India's Article 215 and 226 discusses Contempt, particularly Non-compliance of Award of Labour Court.
The case involved a Petitioner arguing that the respondent committed contempt by not fully complying with the award of Labour Court.
Respondents argued that Labour Courts are not considered as a court, and the court cannot initiate contempt proceedings to implement the award.
Despite the respondents' claims, the court concluded that non-compliance with the award of Labour Court is contemptuous and ordered respondents to fully comply with the award.
The case's petition was granted accordingly.
