The Constitution of India, Articles 15(1) & 16(2) - Admissions - Domicile - Admission to Post Graduate Courses in Govt. Medical Colleges favoring domicile is valid constitutionally.
The expression "place of birth" appears in clause (1) of Article 15, but "domicile" and "residence" are not synonymous to "place of birth".
