Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Railway Protection Force Rules, 1959, Rules 44, 45 and 47 - Departmental enquiry - Eliminating - There must be independent material to justify eliminating enquiry envisaged by Article 311(2) - Proviso can be invoked only when authority is convinced that it is not reasonably feasible to hold departmental enquiry - Decision to do so cannot be based solely on the assertion of concerned authority.
