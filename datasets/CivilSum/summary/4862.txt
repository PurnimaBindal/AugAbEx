Constitution of India, Articles 12, 14 and 16 - Termination - The termination clause in the appointment letter of the Assistant General Manager in Delhi Stock Exchange that enables DSE to terminate services without cause by mere issuance of notice, has been held to be arbitrary and violative of Article 14 of the Constitution by the Supreme Court.
This decision was made in reference to the applicability of standards of Article 14 to DSE, and following the formula in KC Sharma's case, the respondent must pay compensation of Rs. 15 lakhs to the petitioner.

Constitution of India, Article 12 - Delhi Stock Exchange - Whether a 'State' - The Delhi Stock Exchange has been held to satisfy the conditions laid down in Dayaram Shetty's case and is deemed a 'State' within meaning of Article 12, thus making it amenable to the writ jurisdiction.
