The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 gives employees the right to equal pay for equal work.
Employees working in the Health Deptt.
and Local Self Govt. who have the same qualifications, job requirements, and nature of duties should be placed in an identical scale of pay.
The Govt. granting different pay scales in such a situation is arbitrary, and employees are entitled to parity of treatment.
[Para 6 and 7]  B Under Articles 12 and 14 of the Constitution of India, municipal committees in the state of Punjab fall within the definition of State.
Thus, their employees, even if they are not of provincialised cadre, are entitled to parity of treatment with other Govt. employees.
