Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25 F - Termination - A Respondent who worked in two different departments of Punjab University is being questioned if he has worked for 240 days in a year.
The Respondent signs an Affidavit stating that he has not completed 240 days, has not served in any department earlier, and will not claim benefit if re-engaged.
However, the Affidavit is dated after the Respondent was re-engaged, which indicates that the petitioner had no doubt about the earlier employment of the Respondent when he joined.
It is held that the use of the Affidavit as a tactic to deny continuity, wipe out the earlier service of Respondent, and deny benefits cannot be considered legal.
Therefore, an Award of Labour Court directing re-instatement with continuity of service and all other consequential benefits without back wages is upheld.
