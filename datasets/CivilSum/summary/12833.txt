According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution and Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) (Class I) Rules, 1976, Rule 10, the government can fill up extra vacancies not informed to the Commission for a six month period using candidates recommended by the Commission.
However, the government can only consider making appointments for future vacancies if the Commission has recommended more candidates than the number of vacancies informed to them.
The government is obligated to request additional recommendations from the Commission for appointments on extra vacancies, and the Commission must assess the merits of each candidate and recommend those found to be suitable for appointment.
The rule doesn't restrict the Commission from recommending candidates exceeding the number of vacancies informed to them.
