Constitution of India, Article 226 - Parity in pay scale - Sub Divisional Clerk petitioners claimed they should have pay scale parity with Wild Life Inspectors - Before pay scale revision on 1186, petitioners and Wild Life Inspectors had the same pay scale - However, the petitioner cannot claim to be equal with other department employees who have different responsibilities, even if they perform similar functions.
