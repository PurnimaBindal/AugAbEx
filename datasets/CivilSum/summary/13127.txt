According to the State Bank of India (Determination of Terms and Conditions of Service) Order, 1979 - SBI (Supervising Staff) Service Rules, Rule 49(h), an employee facing dismissal may still be eligible for promotion if disciplinary action against them is confirmed.
However, it must be noted that if an employee receives excess salary for a higher post during their suspension, it does not necessarily mean that their promotion has become effective.
This was exemplified in a case where a petitioner lost their promotion due to its conditional and tentative nature, and had to return the excess salary paid to them without objection.
