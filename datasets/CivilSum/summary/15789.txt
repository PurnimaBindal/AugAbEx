According to Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 295(4)(a), if a pension has been wrongly fixed and an excess amount has been paid, the amount can be deducted from the pension and gratuity.
However, the department must hear the petitioner before recovering the amount from the pension.
Additionally, if the excess amount has been paid by mistake, it cannot be recovered from the gratuity.
