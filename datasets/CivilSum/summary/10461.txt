According to Articles 14 and 21 of the Constitution of India, an employee who has been promoted and rendered 20 years of service must be provided with either an alternative job in the same pay scale or the option to revert back to their previous position if they are unable to perform their current job due to a disability such as colour blindness.
In this case, the petitioner was terminated without being given the opportunity to choose an alternative job or to be reverted to their previous position.
Therefore, they are entitled to be reinstated as a peon and considered for any alternative job with back wages and continuity of service equal to that of a driver.
[Paras 5, 6 & 7]
