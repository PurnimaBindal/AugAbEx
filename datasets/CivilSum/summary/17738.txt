The petitioner, a retired District & Sessions Judge, claims for special addition to his service for qualifying superannuation pension under Rule 16 of Superior Judicial Service Rules, 1963, but is not entitled to it as he was a direct recruit to PCS (Executive Branch) and not to PCS (Judicial Branch) as required by the rule.
[Para 4] B.
To add a period to qualifying service for pension under Rule 4-2(1) of CSR Vol. II, it is essential that the service or post to which the employee is appointed requires a post-graduate qualification.
As the petitioner was directly recruited as PCS (Executive Branch) for which only graduation is required, he cannot claim the benefit under this rule, and his petition is dismissed with no order as to costs.
