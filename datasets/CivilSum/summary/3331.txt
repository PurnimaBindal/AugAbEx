Pension - Govt. of India's letter dated 2521994, para 28 - Ex-serviceman - The reduction of 10% pension for each year of re-employment up to 10 years is not arbitrary.
The letter provides a reasonable classification because the ex-serviceman benefits from emoluments and other benefits when re-employed in civil service.
The validity of the letter dt. 2521994 was upheld in Bhim Singh v. UOI.
