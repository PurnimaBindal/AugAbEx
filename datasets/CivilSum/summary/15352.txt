Constitution of India, Articles 15(4) & 16(4) - Constitution of Jammu & Kashmir, Section 124 - Jammu & Kashmir Reservation Rules, 1994, Rule 11 - Concession - Reservation - The distinction between concession and reservation is important - Rule 11 stipulates only preferential treatment of 2% for handicap candidates and 5% for children of defence personnel - No provision exists for separate reservation - Preference implies giving precedence when other factors are equal - Concession cannot be interpreted as reservation or right.
