Industrial Disputes Act, Section 6-N - Contract Labour - Workmen will be considered in the direct employment of the principal employer if it is determined that the appointment of the contractor was figurative and not genuine, and was instead used to conceal the status of workmen - In such cases, the termination of workmen after completing 240 days service without following the provisions for retrenchment would be illegal and void.
