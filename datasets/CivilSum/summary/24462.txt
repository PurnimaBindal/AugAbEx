Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 162 - Seniority - Powers of State Government - Junior Engineers who opted for service with State Electricity Board with effect from October 1st, 1973 - Employer and employee relationship between the Government and the transferred employee ceased to exist from the date of the transfer of service - Correcting or amending the seniority of these employees was within the Government's right until October 1st, 1973 due to reasons accrued before the said date.
