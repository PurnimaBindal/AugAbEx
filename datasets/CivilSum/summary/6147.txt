Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Employment (Class I and II) Service Rules, 1963, Rule 11 - Probation - Termination - Registration of criminal case - Natural justice - The petitioner was appointed as an Employment Officer on probation for two years, but after about five months of joining service, a criminal case was registered against the petitioner under the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988.
Even though the petitioner's probation period was extended up to three years, at the time of expiry of the extended period of probation, his service was terminated on the grounds of unsatisfactory work and conduct.
The order of termination was upheld and does not cast a stigma that would render the order punitive in nature.
