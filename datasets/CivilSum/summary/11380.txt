Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - West Bengal Board of Secondary Education Act, 1963 - Service matter - Transfer - Aided Institution - Transfer from one aided institution to another aided institution - State does not have the authority to make such transfers - Such authority cannot be assumed on humanitarian grounds - The appointment authority is specific to a school - The rejection order was appropriate, and writ power cannot be employed out of compassion - Petition denied.
