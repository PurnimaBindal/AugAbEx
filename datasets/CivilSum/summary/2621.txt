Pension and Gratuity Regulations, Regulation 219 - Family pension - Widow - Re-marriage of widow - Eligibility - If a widow of a deceased employee remarries her deceased husband's brother and contributes to the support of other living eligible heirs, she is still eligible and entitled to a family pension.
It is illegal to stop her pension.
[Para 4]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Jurisdiction - A petitioner's husband died in a Military Hospital in Jaipur.
She was granted a family pension while residing within the territorial jurisdiction of Punjab & Haryana High Court.
When her pension was stopped while still within the jurisdiction of Punjab & Haryana High Court, she filed a petition under Article 226 of the Constitution.
The Punjab & Haryana High Court has the jurisdiction to entertain the petition, and the objection that only Rajasthan High Court had jurisdiction was rejected.
