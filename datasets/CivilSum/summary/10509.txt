Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Transfer - Transfer is a part of government service - The employer has the discretion to shift an employee to another location due to administrative needs, subject to guidelines - Even if such guidelines are violated in public interest, the court will not intervene - The employee's remedy is with the employer, not the court - If a representation is made against the transfer order, it must be resolved promptly - The petition is dismissed, with the stipulation that any representation by the petitioner be addressed with a clear resolution within six weeks.
