Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Retrenchment - Compensation - The workman was not paid retrenchment compensation at the time of retrenchment.
The employer informed the workman on 3041982 that his services would not be required after 30.7.1982 and to collect his dues on or before 30.7.1982 from the Accounts Officer who had been directed to make arrangement for the payment of the retrenchment compensation.
The employer failed to send the compensation to the petitioner, even by money order.
The employer's claim that the compensation had been worked out and offered before 30.7.1982 was found to be incorrect.
Retrenchment compensation was required to be paid at the time of effecting retrenchment.
The petitioner was entitled to reinstatement with consequential benefits.
