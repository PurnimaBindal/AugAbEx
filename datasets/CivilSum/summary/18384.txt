Rajasthan Rural Development and Panchayati Raj State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1998, Rule 6, Schedules I & II - Regularisation of service - Ad hoc, casual, temporary or stop-gap employees do not have the right to hold any post or an unconditional right to be absorbed or regularized in service- Sympathetic consideration can only be given when an employee has been continuously working for a long period - However, one cannot claim regularisation on a particular post while working on another post, ignoring the rules and norms of appointment.
[Paras 7 to 10, 15 and 16] B Rajasthan Rural Development and Panchayati Raj State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1998, Rule 6, Schedules I & II - Appointment by promotion and direct recruitment - The posts of Junior Engineer (Civil) fall under Group B of Schedule II and are to be filled in only by direct recruitment, not by promotion.
