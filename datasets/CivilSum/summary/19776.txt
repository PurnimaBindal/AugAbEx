The criteria of determining inter se seniority of Head Clerks in Panchayat Samitis on the basis of date of birth instead of length of service under the Punjab Panchayat Samitis and Zila Parishads Services Rules, 1965 as mandated by Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 is discriminatory and unreasonable.
The rule that a person who is older in age will be ranked senior to a person who joined the service much earlier in point of time is arbitrary.
