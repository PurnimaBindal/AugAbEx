Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pension Regulations for Army 1961, Part-I, Regulation 50 - Voluntary retirement - Disability pension - The petitioner requested premature retirement due to personal reasons and being superseded for further promotion, and also mentioned his poor health - The request was accepted, but according to Regulation 50, voluntary retirement does not entitle an officer to receive disability pension - Therefore, the petitioner's claim for disability pension was correctly declined.
