According to Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules of 1958, Rule 17, punishments such as censure can only be sustained if issued lawfully.
This was supported by the case where a joint representation was made to the Governor and DIG on being declared surplus and relieved from service.
It was found that issuance of a charge-sheet by someone who was not the employer is illegal, hence punishment in pursuance of the charge-sheet cannot be sustained.
