Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 330-A - Pension - Officers of State forces of Princely States were absorbed in Indian Army or Territorial Army depending upon orders issued by appropriate Authorities - Grievance of petitioner that service rendered in State Forces counted for purposes of pension regarding those incumbents absorbed in regular army and then retired the same service not reckoned for purposes of pension in the case of those incumbents who happened to be absorbed in Territorial Army and then had retired from service - Held, if an incumbent had no choice, whether to be absorbed in I-A.
or TA, he had to obey orders of appropriate Authority regarding absorption from State Force Service - No justification not to count state force for purposes of pension, when such service counted for purpose of seniority - No reasonable nexus with any object which is sought to be achieved about this classification that for one Force State Force Service will be counted and not for the other - Action of respondents not counting State Force Service of petitioner for purposes of pension - Is arbitrary and discriminatory - Violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
