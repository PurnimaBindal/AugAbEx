Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1960 (Kerala), Rule 16 - Minor/Major penalty - Enquiry - Conducting of - The petitioner received a penalty of a three-year stoppage of annual increment.
The argument was that while the penalty inflicted was minor, it was necessary to prove charges via a formal inquiry.
While Rule 16 does not require a formal enquiry for the imposition of a minor penalty, this does not mean that such a penalty can be imposed arbitrarily without finding the employee guilty or conducting any investigation into allegations.
The principle of natural justice and doctrine of reasonable opportunity applies on these occasions, and what counts as reasonable opportunity is based on the facts and circumstances of the case rather than the quantum of the penalty.
Therefore, the petition is allowed accordingly.
[Paras 11, 14 and 15]
