The requirement for receiving a revised scale of pay similar to that of an Assistant Lecturer was changed to a shorter period of service, but previous service as a tutor cannot be counted towards service as an Assistant Lecturer.
As a result, the appellant is not entitled to have their pay fixed prior to 1121978.
While another petitioner benefited from a misinterpretation of the rules, this does not mean the same should be extended to the appellant.
Their claim of discrimination that violates Article 14 is unfounded, and previous illegal benefits given to other employees cannot be extended to them.
