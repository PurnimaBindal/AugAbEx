Constitution of India, Article 311(2) (b) - Satisfaction of competent authority - Discharge from service without holding enquiry - Competent authority's conclusion that holding of enquiry is not reasonably practicable must be based on some material - Mere two lower officers' notes that the petitioner (a Police Constable driver) has links with militants and helped transport arms and ammunition to them - Order solely based on two notes - Competent authority made no attempt to verify the correctness of notes - No material available to support conclusion that enquiry was not reasonably practicable - Order of discharge illegal - Order quashed - Petition allowed.
