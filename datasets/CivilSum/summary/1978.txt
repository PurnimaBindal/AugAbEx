The Kerala Government Law Officers (Appointment and Conditions of Service) and Conduct of Cases Rules, 1978, Rule 17 allows the state to terminate the services of law officers by giving one month's notice or salary in lieu of it without providing reasons.
This is permissible under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, and the Kerala Public Services Act.
The reason given for termination, loss of trust and confidence, is considered a fair and reasonable justification for action, and the impugned rule does not confer arbitrary or discriminatory power on the Government.
