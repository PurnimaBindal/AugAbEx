The Minimum Wages Act, 1948, Sections 3, 5, 9, and 27 - Minimum wages - The Act is intended to establish a statutory minimum wage in order to avoid labor exploitation as evidenced by the statement of objectives and reasons.
The Advisory Committee ensures the fair and impartial treatment of both employers and employees with the help of independent members, serving as an adequate safeguard against hasty or arbitrary decisions by the appropriate government.
[Paras 5 and 6] B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Minimum Wages Act, 1948, Sections 3, 5, 9, and 27 - It is unjust to interfere with the notification of minimum wages as it is incompatible with the aims and objectives of the Act.
Interference, if any, would be without substantial grounds and against the principles of Article 226's extraordinary jurisdiction.
