Limitation Act, 1963, Section 3 and Article 136 - Swatantra Sainik Smman Pension Scheme, 1980 - Swatantra Sainik Smman Pension - Denial of - Cause of action started from the date of Swatantra Sainik Smman Pension being denied - Filing of a lawsuit after 16 years from the cause of action is prohibited by limitation and a verdict given in violation of the mandate of Section 3 of Limitation Act can't be upheld if challenged on that basis.
