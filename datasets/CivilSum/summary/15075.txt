The Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, Sections 8(1)(a), 8(4) state that the appointment as Deputy Governor can be challenged if there is an issue with the executive director being junior to the petitioner in the appointment process.
However, the post of Deputy Governor is not considered a promotional post, and the Governor, in consultation with the Ministry of Finance, can nominate a wide range of candidates with expertise in economics, banking and administration.
The petitioner failed to demonstrate any need for laying eligibility conditions, and since there is no promotion involved, the petitioner has no right to be considered for the post of Deputy Governor.
The appointment process is appropriate and nothing is wrong with the current practices.
The legislature has preferred to keep a wide range of choices for the selection of the post in question.
