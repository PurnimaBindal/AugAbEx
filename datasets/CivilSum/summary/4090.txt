West Bengal Co-operative Societies Act, 1983 - West Bengal Co-operative Societies Rules, Rule 65(12)(vii) - Vires of the rule challenged - Appointment of manager for Primary Agricultural Credit Co-operative Societies - The rule requires a compulsory contribution of Re.
1 per centum to the fund of a common cadre authority, which is disproportionate to the service rendered by managers.
The rule-making authority cannot impose a levy if there is no express provision in the Act.
Rules de hors of the statute are ultra vires, and the levy made is wholly unauthorized by law.
