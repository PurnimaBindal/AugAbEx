The purpose of Articles 16(4) and 16(4A) is to ensure the due representation of certain classes in certain posts; however, they should not be construed in a manner that ignores other important provisions of Articles 14, 16(1) and 335.
Candidates who are eligible and within the consideration zone have the right to be considered for promotion and the non-consideration of these candidates will violate their personal and fundamental right and right to equal opportunity.
The right to be considered for promotion is not a mere statutory right but a fundamental right under Article 6(1).
The recruits under Articles 16(4) and 16(4A) do not automatically receive a fundamental right of reservation in promotions.
The court must balance the fundamental rights granted under Article 16(1) and those provided by reservation under Articles 16(4) and 16(4A).
Roster point promotions do not confer the right of seniority.
The rule of continuous officiation is only applicable to the cadre-class of service where roster points are available.
A balancing of rights must be considered for seniority inter se between general and reserved candidates at Level 3 & Level 4.
The roster in cases of reservation under Art. 16(4) or 16(4A) is not intended to determine seniority but to provide "adequate representation" at the promotional level.
The catch-up rule is applied to promotions of senior general candidates in turn.
The general candidates from the lower level will be allowed to catch-up those promoted earlier against reserved points.
The roster promotees will not have to wait for promotion of their seniors from the lower level if some post has become available for promotion to a higher level prior to this.
The purpose of Articles 16(4) and 16(4A) is to ensure the due representation of certain classes in certain posts and promotees given accelerated promotions will not be entitled to claim seniority on that basis in the higher level.
Seniority cannot be delinked from the recruitment rule.
