Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pensionary Benefits - Delay - Interest - No satisfactory reasoning for postponing post-retiral benefits on salary re-fixation - Errors committed by State were subsequently corrected - Petitioner is not accountable for these errors - Surviving spouse of petitioner is directed to receive interest at a rate of 6% per annum, three months after the revised pensionary benefits were due, until the payment is provided.
