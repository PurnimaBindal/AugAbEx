Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 and 300-A - Pension scheme - Revised option - An employee had to provide the revised option within ninety days of receiving the government order, and must refund the employer's share of contributions to PF with interest - If the option was not refunded, the employee could not compel the government to continue depositing contributions to his provident fund - In addition, the revised option would not be considered valid even if it was filed within the given time.
