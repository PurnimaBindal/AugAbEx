The Rules for Post Graduation (MD/MS Course) in Medical Colleges in Madhya Pradesh are in accordance with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
Rule 4(a) mandates admission based on the prescribed teacher student ratio of 1:1 as recommended by the Medical Council of India.
However, this rule is prohibitive in nature.
[Para 12]
The Rules of Post Graduation (M.D./M.S) Course in Clinical, Para-Clinical, and Non-Clinical in Medical Colleges in Madhya Pradesh state that vacant seats of the All India Quota can be diverted to the institutional candidates when the seats remain empty because of the unavailability of All India quota candidates.
