Punjab Municipal Act, 1911, Sections 16(1)(e) and 22 - Removal from Presidentship and Membership of Municipal Council - The competent authority must apply natural justice and give proper notice when removing a member from their position.
The petitioner was given proper show cause notice and the order of removal was given after recordings evidence and offering a full opportunity.
The order gave a full detail of the charges and evidence, and after considering the evidence, only four charges were found to be proved.
The Competent Authority considered all contentions of the petitioner and no violation of natural justice or non-application of mind by the Competent Authority occurred.

Mala Fide - Allegations of mala fide levelled against a person should include a disclosure of any direct conflict of interest between the petitioner and the alleged person.
The mere fact that a person previously supported one party and subsequently supported another is not sufficient to uphold the ground of mala fide.
