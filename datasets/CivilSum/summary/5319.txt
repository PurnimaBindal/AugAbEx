Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 7 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 100 - Factories Act, Section 46 - Regularisation - Petitioners, who were employees of a canteen run by a contractor were seeking regularization of their services by claiming that they are employees of the HMT - however, it was held that they do not meet the condition laid down by the Supreme Court in JT 2001(1) SC 36, VST Industries Ltd v. VST Industries Workers Union and another and therefore are not HMT employees - The petition was dismissed.
