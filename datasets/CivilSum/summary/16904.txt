According to Rule 29(A) of the Rajasthan Service of Engineers (B&R Branch) Rules, 1954, the petitioner was promoted based on seniority cum merit and reservation, but was later reverted due to unavailability of the post of Executive Engineer (Electrical) and being junior-most.
The petitioner argues that as per the roster point, they were entitled to hold the post.
Three vacancies were to be filled, two from general and one from SC, with the petitioner being the only eligible SC candidate.
The court held that since the petitioner was eligible on roster point, they could not be reverted even if vacancies were reduced.
As such, the petition was allowed.
