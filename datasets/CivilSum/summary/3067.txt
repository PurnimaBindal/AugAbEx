Constitution of India, Article 14 - Admission - Education - Agreement - There is no estoppel against law - If merit is the sole criteria for admission and seat allotment, entitled candidates cannot be deprived of the same or the stipend attached to it - An affidavit obtained from the candidate to not claim the stipend, will not prevent them from subsequently claiming it - Such an affidavit is of no significance.
[Paras 13 and 15] B Post-graduate studies in medicine - stipend - A stipend must be provided for pursuing the course, however, this includes working in the institute - These courses are not solely academic but rather patient-oriented, requiring doctors to also render services to patients in addition to their daily studies.
