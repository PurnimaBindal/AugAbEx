Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rules 8, 9, 10 - Minor Punishment - The punishing authority cannot impose minor penalty without following the proper procedure, after issuing charge-sheet for major penalty.
Thus, the competent authority is required to wait till the enquiry proceedings are finalized before imposing any punishment based on proven material.
As a result, the punishment order is nullified.
[Paras 4, 6 and 8]
