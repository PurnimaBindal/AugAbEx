Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 12(5) - Industrial Dispute - Reference - When the existence of the employer-employee relationship is in dispute, all parties interested in the subject matter of reference must be present for determination.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 12(5) - Daily Wager - Termination of Service - Reinstatement - A direction for reinstatement cannot be given when there is no post and the relationship between employer and employee is disputed.

Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 12(5) - Contractual Workman - When a workman is appointed on a contractual basis, the agreement between the parties must be given due weight.
