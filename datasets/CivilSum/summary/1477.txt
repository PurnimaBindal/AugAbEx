Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(q), 10(1) & (3), 12(3) and 24(3) - Settlement Reference - Lock-out declared by employer - One-sided reference - No strike or lock-out notice served by the management - Lock-out cannot be legal without evidence - Employer can lead defense evidence once reference is made - Demands of one party do not exclude the other from leading defense evidence.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(q), 10(1) & (3) - Reference - Maintainable even without a demand provided there is a dispute or difference between the parties related to employment or non-employment or terms of employment - A written cause is not necessary under Section 10(3).
[Para 19] C. Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(q), 10(1) & (3) - Reference - Delay tactics cannot be used as a ground to hold the reference bad due to delay.
