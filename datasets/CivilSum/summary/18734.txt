Jammu and Kashmir Industrial Training (Subordinate Service Recruitment Rules, 1979), Schedule 1 - SRO 75 of 1992 - Jammu and Kashmir Civil Services (Revises Pay) Rules, 1992, Rule 9(f) - Petitioners appointed as Junior Engineers seek to be deemed to have been appointed at pay scale Rs. 2000-3400 and receive applicable arrears - Courts hold previous appointments have been made at that pay scale and that there was a wrong application of SRO 75 of 1992 - Petitioners are to be treated accordingly and no arrears are to be granted - Petition is allowed.
