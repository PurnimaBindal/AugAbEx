Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation of services of ad hoc/contractual employees - Policy of regularisation - Initially appointed for six months only - Contract extended time to time and allowed to continue for more than six years - Policy framed by the State Government to regularize all those working with the Govt. for the last many years to take advantage of their experience - Once a conscious decision is taken by the Govt., the employees cannot be ignored as being appointed only on contract - The policy includes all types of casual, temporary, ad hoc, and contractual employees entitled to be considered for regularisation - Authorities directed to consider and place them in the initial regular pay scale.
