Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - Minority Institutions - Admission - The Convenor directed a certain minority candidates to be admitted into an MCA course in place of non-minority candidates already admitted by the Institution.
Admissions were made contrary to instructions and the petitioner contended that such orders could not be passed without notice to affected parties.
High Court held that question of issuing notice to non-minority students does not arise as they could not have secured admission in minority Institutions, and lack of notice has not resulted in any prejudice.
High Court cannot compel respondents to act contrary to law, and it is open to All India Council for Technical Education and the University to consider the request of the Petitioners and decide whether the number of seats can be increased or supernumerary seats can be created.
[Paras 12, 15, 18, 19 and 21]
