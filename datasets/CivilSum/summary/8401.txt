Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 9 - Retiral benefits - Disciplinary proceedings - The petitioner was not paid retiral benefits upon retirement, and the respondent claimed they could take action under Rule 9 of the Rules against the petitioner.
According to Rule 9, no order to withhold pensionary benefits can be passed until misconduct is established.
Disciplinary proceedings can be initiated even after retirement, and the petitioner cannot argue that no proceedings can be taken against them after retirement.
A departmental enquiry was held, and the second respondent was directed to give the petitioner a reasonable opportunity to explain what was stated against them in the enquiry report and pass final orders.
