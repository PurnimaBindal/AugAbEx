The State Government is authorized to take posts of JBT teachers away from the State Subordinate Service Selection Board for justified reasons, as per the Punjab Education Class-III (Primary School Cadre) Service Rules, 1997.
The urgency of fulfilling the over 7000 vacant teacher positions in the public interest is a justifiable reason.
The Cabinet's decision cannot be considered as malafide.
The Court does not have jurisdiction to review the State's appointment decisions.
Fairness, equality, and the public interest should guide the State's actions, and teacher appointments should be completed expeditiously to ensure adequate teacher availability for the upcoming academic session.
