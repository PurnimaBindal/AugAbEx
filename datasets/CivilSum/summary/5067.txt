According to the Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Section 11-A, Schedule II, Item 3, dismissal from service is justified if an employee is habitually absent from work, and the punishment of dismissal is not considered disproportionate.
The order of the Labour Court to forfeit 50% of back wages and granting reinstatement to the employee in question is not deemed appropriate based on Paras 11, 13, 16, and 17.
