Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 16 - Opportunity of hearing - The petitioners were appointed as Field Assistants in the Fisheries Department and were subsequently promoted as Assistant Fisheries Officer on an ad hoc basis.
Their promotion as Fisheries Officers was made regular with retrospective effect.
At one point, the Director of the Fisheries Department informed the petitioners that their services were being considered for regularisation and they needed to appear in his office.
Later, they received an order from the Department that the earlier order regarding their regularisation was cancelled without giving them any opportunity of a hearing.
The impugned order cannot be sustained.
[Paras 1 and 2]
