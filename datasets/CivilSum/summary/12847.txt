According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, ad hoc appointees on higher ex cadre posts have no right to the position and can be reverted at any time if deemed unsuitable.
Any lapses in performance do not necessarily constitute punishment, but rather show the employee's unsuitability for the position.
As the post was created on an experimental basis, there is no inherent right to it, and reversion may be deemed arbitrary only if good reasons are not given.
The cases of 75 SLJ 634 and 1978(2) SLR 379 are not applicable in this situation.
