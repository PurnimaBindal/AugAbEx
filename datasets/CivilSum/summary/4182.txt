Under the Workmen's Compensation Act, ex parte orders can be set aside by following the provisions of Order 9 Rule 13 CPC.
If the respondent can convince the Compensation Commissioner that they were not duly served or had sufficient reason for not attending the hearing, then the Commissioner has the power to set aside the ex parte order against them.
[Paras 5 to 10] B.
If a party does not attend a hearing along with their advocate and does not settle terms with them, the lawyer can withdraw from the proceedings without notice.
The party is responsible for attending the hearing, and they cannot simply rely on the engagement of an advocate.
If a counsel withdraws for lack of instructions, the court is under no obligation to issue a fresh notice at public expense.
