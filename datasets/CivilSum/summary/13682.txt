Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Electrical) Regulations, 1965, Regulation 9(4) - Electricity Supply Act, 1948, Section 79 - Provisional Promotion/Adjustment/Experience/Eligibility - The petitioners were provisionally promoted/adjusted as Assistant Engineers from the post of Circle Head Draftsmen in their own pay scale until they acquire the required 5 years of experience.
It was an order of promotion and the term 'adjustment' was incorrect.
The petitioners were entitled to the pay scale of Assistant Engineers upon completion of 5 years of experience.
[Para 6]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Executive order - Retrospective effect of executive orders - Reduction in quota - The quota for promotion from Circle Draftsmen to Assistant Engineers had been reduced by 3%.
The quota reduction cannot be implemented retrospectively via an executive order.
The petitioners who have already been promoted cannot be reverted.
[Para 7]
