Constitution of India, Article 226 - Alternate remedy - The petitioner, a process server in the court of Sub Judge, had his service terminated on the grounds of unsatisfactory work and conduct.
The court maintains that an effective alternative remedy of appeal before the District and Sessions Judge is available, and thus, the writ petition has not been entertained.
The petitioner has been advised to utilize the remedy of appeal.
[Paras 3, 4 and 5]
