The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300A, together with the Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, stipulate that Pension entitlement should be computed based on reckonable emoluments.
Claims for an identical amount of pension to every retiree from the same rank cannot be made under Nakara's case, since the computation of their pensions involves different reckonable emoluments that differ depending on the retirement date.
[Para 17]
The Death-cum-Retirement gratuity paid is computed on the basis of salary drawn on the day of retirement and cannot be re-evaluated to be increased based on subsequent increases in pensions.
It should be noted that the principles of pension and gratuity are separate.
