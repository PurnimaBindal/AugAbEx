Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pension and gratuity - Limitation - Although the payment of pension is a monthly occurrence, the right to pension cannot be taken away due to the bar of limitation, particularly when the employer is guilty of delaying the release of pension and gratuity of a retired employee - Writ petition filed in 2003 and contested only on the ground of delay and laches - Employee retired in 1991 - The employer is rather responsible for paying interest as exemplary costs - The petitioner was awarded 10% interest and Rs. 25,000/- costs.
