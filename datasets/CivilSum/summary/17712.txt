The Punjab Panchayati Raj Act, 1994 does not provide for reservation of Ex-servicemen in Panchayat elections.
Although Ex-servicemen have been given reservation benefits in some areas, they cannot claim benefits comparable with Scheduled Castes communities listed in the Constitution, and hence no discriminatory practice can be alleged.
[Para 2] B As Ex-servicemen do not fall under any caste category mentioned in the Constitution, they cannot claim reservation benefits in each field, except where they have been granted this benefit.
They cannot claim reservation benefits as Scheduled Castes in the absence of any rule, regulation or Government policy.
Consequently, the Court cannot grant relief of reservation.
