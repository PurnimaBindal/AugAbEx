Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation of service - The petitioner's prior appointment as a weaving helper was made without the stipulation that his appointment would continue until the grant-in-aid by the Government, unlike his appointment as a Master on the consolidated wages.
During the pendency of the writ, his services were terminated despite similarly situated individuals receiving benefits of regularisation.
This termination was in violation of the principles of natural justice and proper legal procedure.
Consequently, the petitioner's services were regularised on the post commensurate with his qualifications and entailing benefits of salary fixation.
[Paras 19 to 24]
