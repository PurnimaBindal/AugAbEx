Constitution of India, Article 226 - Standing Orders, Rule 25 - Dismissal - Without enquiry - Sustained - Petitioner who committed act of child molestation given notice to show cause but failed to avail himself of being heard - Spot investigation report prepared - Enquiry committee recorded statements of eye witnesses and submitted report - Notice given before passing order - Determined, no infirmity in the order.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Standing Orders, Rule 25 - Dismissal - Without enquiry - Court's requirement - Explained, where disciplinary enquiry dispensed with on plea of not being reasonably practicable, employer must justify to court that it was not a colourable exercise or mala fide action - It must also be shown that there were good and objective reasons along with proof of misconduct and reasons justifying the dispensation of enquiry.
