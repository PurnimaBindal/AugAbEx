Constitution of India, Article 226 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 15 - Post & Telegraphs Manual, Vol IV, Rule 37 - Transfer of employee guilty of misbehavior - The competent authority can find an employee guilty of misbehavior without regular enquiry.
The transfer of employees is solely the prerogative of the competent authority to maintain discipline, decency and decorum of public services in public interest.
Courts or tribunals cannot interfere unless there is a clear case of malafide and arbitrariness.
The rules do not bar such transfers.
