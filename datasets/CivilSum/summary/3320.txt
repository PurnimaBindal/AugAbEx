The Conservation of Foreign Exchange and Prevention of Smuggling Activities Act, 1974, Section 3(1) provides for an order of detention to be passed.
In this case, the petitioner was on bail in an offence under Customs Act and surrendered in the said case.
However, the order of detention was served when the petitioner was already in judicial custody.
The order of detention was quashed and it was necessary for the detaining authority to reconsider whether the detenu's detention under COFEPOSA was still necessary given that they were already in detention in a criminal case.
Failure to do so would render the detention order unenforceable against the detenu.
In support of this argument, 1995(2) Recent Criminal Reports 309 (DB) and 1995(2) Recent Criminal Reports 305 (DB Delhi) were cited.
