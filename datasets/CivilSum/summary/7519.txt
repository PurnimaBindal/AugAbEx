Rajasthan Model Standing Orders, 1963, Order 20 - Certified Standing Orders, Order 14(d) - Age of retirement - Order 14(d) of Certified Standing Orders conflicts with Order 20 of Rajasthan Model Standing Order by restricting the age of retirement - Order 20 does not have any provision for compulsory retirement, however Order 14(d) retires the employee upon reaching the age of superannuation i.e. attaining 55 years or completing 25 years of service whichever is earlier - The validity of a rule or statute should only be examined when necessary.
[Paras 13 & 14] B Rajasthan Model Standing Orders, 1963, Order 20 - Certified Standing Orders, Order 14(d) & (e) - Compulsory retirement - Certified Standing Order 14(d) & (e) mandates retirement upon reaching the age of superannuation i.e. 55 years or completion of 25 years of service, if physically or mentally unfit - No requirement for authority to assess employee efficiency or performance - Order of compulsory retirement cannot be passed after completing more than 26 years of service prior to completion of 55 years of age without a certificate from CMHO or any other medical officer - Order of compulsory retirement cannot be justified.
