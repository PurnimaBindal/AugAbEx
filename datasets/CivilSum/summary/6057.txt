Indian Penal Code, Sections 409/34 - Constitution of India, Article 21 - Speedy trial - Embezzlement of Panchayat funds - Case of ordinary nature - Accused had no contribution in causing delay - Challan presented 10 years after registration - Accused experienced prolonged investigation - FIR quashed - Article 21 of Constitution of India ensures the right of speedy trial with a fair, just and reasonable procedure - When there is inordinate unexplained delay, the accused must be released unconditionally.
