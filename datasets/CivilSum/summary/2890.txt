Madhya Pradesh Medical and Dental Post Graduate Entrance Examination Rules, 2001, Rules 94, 96, 97 - Admission - Vacant seats - Course in progress and admissions over - As per the ruling of the Supreme Court in the cases of Shafali Nandwani and Madhu Singh, admission to a medical course in the middle of a session is not allowed - Such admission would be against the principles of medical education.
