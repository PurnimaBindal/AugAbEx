Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservation for scheduled castes - Promotion - Reservation in promotions - Petitioner, a scheduled caste candidate, was not promoted to the post of Accountants and Assistants from clerk in the 20% quota reserved because the strength of scheduled caste candidates in that category was already more than 20% - Petitioner's promotion was ignored in violation of the reservation policy - However, no actual relief can be granted to the petitioner as seniors to him in his category are still awaiting their promotion in their quota.
