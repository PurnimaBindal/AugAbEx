Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Reinstatement - The Tribunal rejected the employee's application for reinstatement under Section 33(2)(b).
Though the order was not challenged, an industrial dispute regarding subsequent dismissal was pending before the Tribunal.
The Tribunal held that since the application under Section 33(2)(b) was dismissed, the subsequently raised dispute had become infructuous.
The Petitioner was directed to be reinstated with full backwages and continuity.
