Constitution of India, Article 14 - Goa, Daman and Diu (Absorbed Employees) Act, 1965, Sections 2(a), 2(c), 2(e), 3, 4, 7 and 15 - Goa Daman and Diu Administration Act, Section 5 - Absorbed Employees - Only employees who were in employment on the existing post on or before 20.12.1961 and were continuing as on the appointed day under the Rules are considered as absorbed employees and eligible to the benefit of Rule 7.
Respondents appointed after the appointed day and issuance of circular for the fixation of the scale of pay are not eligible for the pay of the post prescribed in the decree granted by the Portuguese Administration.
