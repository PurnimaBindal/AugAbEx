Promotions based on seniority-cum-fitness from Assistant Engineer to Executive Engineer depend on eligibility on the date of consideration.
If a person became eligible earlier but could not be promoted due to pending departmental proceedings, and if a senior became eligible later but before the next consideration date, then the seniority will prevail.
In light of RB Desai etc. v. SK Khanolker etc. and the three-judge bench judgment of the Supreme Court, the Full Bench of the Punjab and Haryana High Court's decision in Punjab State Electricity Board v. Ashok Kumar Sehgal, AIR 1990 P&H 117, which took a contrary view, is no longer valid.
