The Persons with Disabilities Act of 1995 and the Constitution of India's various articles inform and protect the appointment of a visually handicapped applicant for the post of Probationary Officer in Bank.
An expert committee declared that certain Group A, B, C, and D positions were suitable for the visually impaired.
In light of this, the petitioner should be allowed to sit and write any examination as a general/reserve candidate.
Her age restriction will be lifted, as the petition has been pending for four years.
[Paras 26, 30 to 22]

The petitioner was denied the right to sit and write the examination for selection as a Probationary Officer in Bank because she was visually handicapped.
This denial infringed on her right under Article 14, which provides fundamental rights to every citizen to be treated equally.
The rejection was arbitrary and discriminatory, which cannot be allowed, given that the petitioner was in the same position as other candidates and did not seek any preferential treatment.
[Para 13]

Denying the visually impaired petitioner the opportunity to compete was a violation of Article 16(1) of the Constitution, which mandates equal opportunity for all citizens in issues relating to employment or appointment under the state.
As the petitioner was similarly placed with the general candidates and did not wish for any bias for her blindness, the Board's decision to deny her the right to compete is nonsensical, arbitrary, and against the Constitution.
[Paras 13 and 14]
