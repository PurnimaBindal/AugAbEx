Constitution of India, Article 226 - Gratuity - withholding of - Petitioners are challenging the withholding of part payment of gratuity due to alleged excess payment of Border House Rent Allowance - There is no evidence showing any misrepresentation or fault on the part of the petitioners for the alleged excess payment - The respondents have been ordered to pay the remaining amount of gratuity to each petitioner within eight weeks.
