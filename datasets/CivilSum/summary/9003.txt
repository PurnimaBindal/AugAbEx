Industrial Disputes Act from 1947, Section 25-F - Employee applies for leave and falls ill - Services terminated on the grounds of overstayed leave without holding an enquiry - The concept of automatic termination is not sustainable and violates the decisions given by the Supreme Court of India on the matter - Such termination falls within the definition of 'retrenchment' and requires the employee to be entitled to reinstatement and other benefits.
[Paras 6 to 8]

Absence from duty - Automatic termination - Is not lawful - The termination without applying the principles of retrenchment compensation cannot be sustained.
[Para 8]

Note.
- The decisions given in Jai Shankar v. State of Rajasthan, AIR 1966 Rajasthan 492 and Nagar Palika Parishad Lahar v. Ram Naresh Rajoria, 1994(4) Service Cases Today 261 have been followed.
