According to Constitution of India, Article 311, if a lecturer is appointed on temporary basis to a leave vacancy in an affiliated private college, and the vacancy falls vacant due to the resignation of the incumbent on leave, the appointee is automatically permanently absorbed if appointed by the Departmental Selection Committee.
Contrary to prevailing instructions, the petitioner's services were terminated.
Subsequent instructions which were contrary to the interests of the employee and the rights accrued under earlier instructions will not apply.
The petitioner is entitled to reinstatement.
Additionally, under Constitution of India, Article 311, the principal who signed the appointment letter on behalf of the Managing Committee, which was the appointing authority, was not competent to terminate the employee's services.
