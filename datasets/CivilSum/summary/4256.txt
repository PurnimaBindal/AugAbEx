Jurisdiction of Civil Court regarding a plaintiff holding a civil post who filed a claim for medical reimbursement after the enforcement of the Administrative Tribunals Act was transferred to the Tribunal as per Section 29 of the Act.
As per Section 28 of the Act, medical reimbursement claim fell within the expression "service matters", and jurisdiction of Civil Court to pass decree was excluded.
Thus, any decree passed by the Civil Court lacked inherent jurisdiction and is considered null and void.
[Paras 7 and 8] B.
A decree passed by a court without jurisdiction is considered null and void, according to Section 9 of the Civil Procedure Code.
[Paras 8, 9, and 10] C. The expression "Service Matters" as per Section 3(q) of the Administrative Tribunals Act includes a claim for medical reimbursement.
