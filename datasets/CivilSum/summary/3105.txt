The West Bengal Board of Secondary Education Act states that no institution has an inherent right to recognition, but it is the responsibility of the concerned authorities to either grant or refuse recognition based on whether the school fulfills all prerequisites for recognition or not.
However, the State cannot delay the decision without proper reason.
The State Government is directed to make a decision on the petitioner's application for school recognition as soon as possible.
