Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Employment Exchanges (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959, Section 4(4) - West Bengal Primary School Teachers' Recruitment Rules, 2001, Rule 8 - Selection - Post of Primary School Teacher - The petitioner was not considered for appointment because he was not sponsored by the employment exchange, as per Rule 8.
It is required by statutory obligation for District Primary School Councils to only requisition names of qualified candidates for the position of Primary School Teachers from employment exchanges.
There is no reason to argue that Rule 8 violates Section 4(4) of the Employment Exchanges (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959.
