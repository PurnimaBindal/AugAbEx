Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection/Appointment - For the selection of one post of lecturer, the Selection Committee of the College prepared a panel of 3 candidates on merit.
The petitioner was at No. 2, while the candidate at No. 1 joined very late and left after 2 days.
But the post was not offered to the petitioner at No. 2.
Instead, the candidate at No. 3 was appointed on an ad hoc basis.
This action was arbitrary, unfair and illegal.
The petitioner is entitled to be treated as regularly appointed on the basis of the earlier selection.
Participation in the second selection does not take away the right of the petitioner.
If the Management decides to fill the post, it is bound to appoint the petitioner, otherwise not.
The petition has been allowed with costs of Rs. 1000/-.
