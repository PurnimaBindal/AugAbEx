Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 9 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(a), 25F and 25G - Civil Court Jurisdiction - Termination of service - Respondents terminated - Appellants argue Respondents not eligible for regularization and gave notice - Trial court dismissed suit despite finding termination violative of Sections 25F, 25G and Article 16 - Appeal allowed - Dispute may be treated as Industrial Dispute, but jurisdiction of Civil Court is alternative, and suit is maintainable in violation of Article 14 and 16 by retaining juniors - Appeal dismissed accordingly.
