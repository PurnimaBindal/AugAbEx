Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admissions in Postgraduate Degrees/Diplomas in various medical specialities - Sponsored candidates form a separate class - Selection process not unfair on the ground of classification between general and sponsored candidates - Sponsored candidates given equal opportunity to compete on merit against the reserved seats for in-service candidates.
[Paras 10, 11, 14 and 15]  B Admissions/Prospectus/Eligibility conditions/Instructions - Government can prescribe conditions to sponsor in-service candidates against the reserved quota seats - Policy decision of Haryana Government, dated 1131994 for admissions in Postgraduate Degrees/Diplomas in medical specialities upheld - Such conditions cannot form part of prospectus for general application to all categories.
[Paras 11 to 14]  C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Interview marks - 25% marks provided for interview - 75% for other relevant factors - Marks for interview held excessive - Amendment in future process recommended.
