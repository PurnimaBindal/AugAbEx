Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Entrance Test - Waiting List - Late Admission - The petitioner sought admission into B.Sc.
Nursing (4 years) course, secured a place as serial No. 11 in the waiting list, and was subsequently promoted to position No. 2 in the waiting list - Four seats were available after the last date for admission, following which provisional admission was granted by Court order.
However, petitioner had not been able to complete the final year and join internship as result for the 3rd year was not declared due to a pending writ petition - Considering eight years had elapsed since this decision, it would be unfair to decline the prayer of the petitioner at this stage - Court ordered directions to declare the result of the petitioner.
