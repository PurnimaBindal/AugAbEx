Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Pension - An employee of JNV University who joined the judicial service after serving for 10 years must be given the benefit of pension for service rendered with JNV University.
Although the employee is prepared to refund the amount of PF with interest, the benefit was initially denied.
However, directions have been given to count the petitioner's service as Laboratory Assistant from 1971963 to 3151973 for pension purposes.
