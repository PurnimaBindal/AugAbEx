According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, Rule 27 of the Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955 and Sections 10, 11 and 12 of the Central Reserve Police Force Act, a member of a disciplinary force who repeatedly remains absent from guard duty and disobeys lawful orders, even if on the pretext of visiting relatives and conveying messages while also refusing to accept a suspension order, cannot be considered to have committed a lesser offense than those punishable under Sections 10 to 12.
In such cases, it is not appropriate for the High Court to review the punishment orders.
