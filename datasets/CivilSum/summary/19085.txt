Punjab National Bank (Officers) Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 17 - Promotion - Reservation policy - Promotion from JMG I to MMG II - The policy allocates 10 marks for seniority and 100 marks for performance, interview, educational qualification, and branch experience.
The reservation policy is not solely based on seniority but also on fitness.
The judgment under appeal is set aside.
