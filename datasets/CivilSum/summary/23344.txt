The Constitution of India, Articles 77, 162, and 309, Representation of the People Act, 1951, Section 191(1)(a), and Himachal Pradesh Khadi and Village Industries Board Act, 1966 Section 7, explain the difference between resigning from a contractual service and government service governed by service rules, where specific provisions are provided for such situations.
Resignation means relinquishing the right to hold an office with the intention of doing so, and it may take different forms, unilateral or bilateral.
A unilateral act takes effect immediately upon communication, but a bilateral act requires further action and comes into effect afterward.
[Paras 16, 18, 22, and 24]

The resignation of a member of the Himachal Pradesh Khadi and Village Industries Board is required to be accepted and notified in the official gazette, and it is a bilateral act.
However, the resignation of the Chairman of the Board comes into effect from the date of its communication to the Head Office of the Board, and it is a unilateral act.
A member of the Board continues to be a member until their resignation is accepted.
[Para 24]
