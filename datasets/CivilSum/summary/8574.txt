Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33 - Termination - Distinction between defective and no enquiry - The management has the right to justify their action if there was a defective enquiry held before termination - If management's action is upheld, the order of dismissal will apply from the date it was passed - However, if no enquiry was held and management justifies their action before the Labour Court with evidence, the order of termination will be effective from the date of the Labour Court's order - Workmen will be entitled to full back pay from the date of termination until the Labour Court's award.
