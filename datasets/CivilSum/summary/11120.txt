After the amendment of the Punjab Civil Service (Judicial Branch) Rules, candidates must obtain a minimum of 50% marks in the written test to be eligible for an interview, and as the petitioners only obtained 45% marks, they were not entitled to be called for an interview.
Even if they had scored 100 marks in the interview, they still could not have met the eligibility criteria.

When a rule is amended to remove a lacuna, it is of a clarificatory nature, and it shall be assumed that the correct position has existed since the original rule came into being.
This applies to the amendment of Rules 7 and 8 of the Punjab Civil Service (Judicial Branch) Rules, 1951, as made in 1991.
