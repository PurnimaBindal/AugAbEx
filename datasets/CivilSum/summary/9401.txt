In relation to medical reimbursement for kidney transplant, if treatment was received from an unrecognized hospital, the respondent is required to pay the approved rates of PGI/AIIMS/government hospital at the relevant time.
[Paras 5 and 6] B.
In a case where kidney transplant treatment was received from an unrecognized hospital, the petitioner's disease was declared chronic on March 20, 2003, but bills for the period from August 24, 2002, to January 16, 2003, were only submitted on June 18, 2004.
The respondent was justified in rejecting the claim for these bills as the disease was not declared chronic during that specific period.
