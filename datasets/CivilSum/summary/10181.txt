The action of the authorities disallowing the petitioner to sit in the examination is considered not sustainable due to a change in rules.
The eligibility of the petitioner to the status of ex-servicemen will be determined by the definition in effect at the time of his discharge and not affected by changes in definition thereafter.
This is specified in the Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16, Punjab Recruitment of Ex-servicemen Rules, 1982, and PCS (Executive Branch and Allied Services) Examination, 1998.
Furthermore, due to irregularities in the result, a new examination was ordered to be taken, which is being considered merely a substitution.
