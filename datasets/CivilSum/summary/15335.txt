Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2A, 10(1) - Termination - Industrial dispute - Reference - Denial of - State Government denied reference on the basis that the petitioners were not employed by the management - It has been held that while exercising powers under Section 10(1) of the Act, the State Government exceeded its jurisdiction by adjudicating the dispute between the parties - Consequently, the matter has been remanded for reconsideration.
