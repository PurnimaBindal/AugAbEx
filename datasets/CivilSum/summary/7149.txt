Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 12 and 14(3) - Enforceable Rights - Preferential appointments - Appointment to the post of constables - The petitioners sought a writ of mandamus to direct the respondents to appoint them as Constables and allot them constabulary numbers.
However, since no advertisement was made for the vacancies, no circular was issued, and no applications were invited from all the eligible candidates, it was held that the petitioners had no enforceable right that could compel the respondent to enforce it by the issuance of a writ of mandamus.
