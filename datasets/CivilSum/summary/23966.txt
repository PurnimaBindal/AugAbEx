Constitution of India, Article 226 - Jammu and Kashmir State Govt. SRO No. 158 dated 1271995 to regulate admission to Post Graduate Degree and Diploma Courses in various Govt. Medical Colleges in the State by holding entrance examination every year and providing a waiting list to be alive for one month - Writ petitions challenging admissions and claims for admissions are pending in two different wings of the J&K High Court, and interim orders stalling the holding of the annual entrance examination have been passed, giving new life to a five-year-old waiting list to the detriment of graduates entitled to compete every year for admissions - Courts should not issue directions obstructing the admissions process as per rules - It affects students' careers - Chief Justice requested to consolidate all petitions before an appropriate Branch for speedy disposal - Authorities directed to conduct entrance test in accordance with the SRO and allow all eligible candidates to appear/reappear, including following the rule relating to reservation at the time of admissions.
