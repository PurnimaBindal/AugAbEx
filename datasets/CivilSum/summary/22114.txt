The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, protects the rights of canteen employees who are working as contract labor and advocates for their regularisation.
The principle of equal pay for equal work is also emphasized, as well as the importance of a good relationship between employer and employee.
Furthermore, in accordance with the Factories Act and Rules, canteen workers are considered to be in the employment of the main employer and are entitled to the protection under the Industrial Disputes Act.
