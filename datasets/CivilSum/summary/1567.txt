Rajasthan Service Rules, Rules 54(2) & 54(3) - Suspension allowance - Recovery of subsistence allowance paid during suspension period - Not permissible in any case, even if charges are proved and punishment awarded or not - The delinquent is entitled to full remunerations for the period.
