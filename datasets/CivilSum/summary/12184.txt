Termination - The petitioner was appointed to a Class IV post due to a resultant vacancy caused by promotion, but the promotions were cancelled for violating the law, causing beneficiaries of such promotions to revert back.
The petitioner, therefore, cannot continue in the position and no interference is necessary.
[Paras 2 and 3]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Second writ - The second writ on the same subject is not maintainable as the earlier writ petition was dismissed for non-prosecution and no steps were taken to restore or recall the dismissal order of the writ petition.
