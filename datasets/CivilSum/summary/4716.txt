The Canara Bank Officer Employees' (Conduct) Regulations, 1967, Regulations 3(1) and 24 and Canara Bank Officer Employees' (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976 were applied in a case of dismissal after an enquiry into an allegation of misappropriation of funds.
The appellant challenged the order of dismissal on the ground that he was not given an opportunity to respond to the charge and, thus, the enquiry was in violation of Article 311 of the Constitution.
The court found no substance in this argument because the appellant had subsequently appeared in the enquiry and defended himself.
The findings of the inquiring authority were found to be well-founded and based on evidence, without any infirmity or illegality.
Consequently, the punishment imposed on the appellant was appropriate and well-deserved, and no interference was necessary.
