Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo) and 25F - Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, Clauses 2, 2(a) and 37 - Absence from duty - Overstayal of leave - Abandonment of job - If a workman with 10 continuous days of absence has their name struck off of the roll, it amounts to retrenchment.
Termination on the basis of misconduct, due to abandonment of job, requires following the principles of natural justice and holding a departmental enquiry.
[Paras 10, 11] B.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25F - Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, Clauses 2, 2(a) and 37 - Quantum of punishment - Overstayal of sick leave - Non-sanction of leave - The management should take into account the workman's misconduct and previous record while awarding punishment.
Even a small overstayal of sick leave due to non-sanction of leave application, if considered as a misconduct, should not result in termination of services, which would be disproportionate and unjustified.
