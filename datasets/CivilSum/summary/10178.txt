Rajasthan Civil Service (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1970, Rules 6 and 7 - Medical reimbursement - A petitioner suffering from cardiac trouble was referred to AIIMS, New Delhi and treated at Escort Heart Institute, which was recognised for specialised treatment.
Reimbursement cannot be denied, and expenses incurred for tests performed at various Labs and Hospitals cannot be denied.
[Paras 7 to 11]
Rajasthan Civil Service (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1970, Rules 6 and 7 - Constitution of India, Article 21 - The right to preserve health and obtain medical aid in furtherance of self-preservation is a part of the right to life under Article 21 of the Constitution.
1996(2) SCT 234(SC) is relied upon.
