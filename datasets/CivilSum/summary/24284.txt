Reserve Bank of India (Staff) Regulations, 1948, Regulation 32 - Refusal to do additional duty - Misconduct - Disciplinary proceedings - The fact that an officer is unfamiliar with a specific type of work, such as the opening and closing of a vault, which he has never done in the claims department, cannot be considered misconduct.
This cannot be used to start disciplinary proceedings or exclude him from promotion consideration.
