Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Appointment - Termination of services as appointment itself being illegal and malafide in breach of equality enshrined in Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - The principles of natural justice must be followed during any action taken in this regard - Government should also initiate enquiry against the officer responsible for selecting and appointing the person in question - An enquiry against the officer must be held before any action is taken against the appointee.
