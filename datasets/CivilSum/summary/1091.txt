The Punjab Department of Public Works Junior Engineers (Public Health Branch) Class-III Service Rules, 1991, Rule 11 and the Punjab Service of Engineers, Class-II, P.W.D.
(Public Health) Rules, 1967, Rules 6 and 9, cannot link seniority with qualifications.
Furthermore, only those eligible on the first day of the year may be placed on the list of eligible candidates for consideration during that year.
Those who acquire eligibility qualifications before their seniors cannot claim seniority and preference based on that over and above seniority in service.

(Public Health) Rules, 1967, Rules 6 and 9, recommendations for promotion against year-wise anticipated vacancies must be made by the Section Committee, and promotions should be made strictly according to seniority subject to suitability, even for ad hoc or current duty charge promotions.
