Section 33A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970 are applicable to the termination of a workman engaged through a contractor.
An application made under Section 33A may be dismissed if the reference in relation to contract labour is prohibited by the provisions of the 1970 Act.
Furthermore, an application under Section 33 is only maintainable if there has been a violation of Section 33 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
If the proceedings themselves are not valid, there can be no pendency of proceedings in the eyes of the law.
