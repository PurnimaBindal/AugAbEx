Selection Process - Select List - Appointment - Posts of Teachers in District Cadre Rajouri - The Service Selection Board determined that the last selected candidate in the open merit category had a merit of 4706 points, while petitioners scored lower - All writ petitioners received lower merit scores than the last selected candidate in the open merit category, so the court held that they had no right to be chosen over the selectees.
Petition dismissed.

Selection process - Select List - Interview - Post of Teachers - The court rejected the petitioners' claim that they performed well in the interview but received lower marks - The Selection Committee judged the candidates’ merit and performance, and as there was no evidence of bias or favoritism, the selectees were given higher marks as judged by the Selection Committee.
