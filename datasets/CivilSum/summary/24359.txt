According to UP Recruitment Service (Determination of Date of Birth) Rules, 1974, Rule 2 and UP Board of Education GR, Rule 7, any application for correction of date of birth must be made within 2 years of issuing the certificate.
In this case, the respondent made the application after 15 years and filed a suit after 39 years.
The court held that since the respondent declared his date of birth in High School, completed his education up to MA, and had four decades of service, his date of birth could not be changed under the law, education rules, and service rules at the end of his service.
The respondent's suit is time-barred, and as per Rule 2, the existing date of birth shall be deemed correct, and no application for correction shall be entertained under any circumstances.
