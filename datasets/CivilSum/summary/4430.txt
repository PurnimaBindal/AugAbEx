Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 423 and Rules 7 & 8 of Entitlement Rules, Annexure-III to Appendix-II - Disability pension - Regarding disability due to or aggravated by Military service - The final opinion of the Medical Board - The Petitioner was retired having 20% disability rating from the service due to the HORSE SHOE KIDNEY WITH SOLITARY RENAL CALCILUS(LT) disease - The Medical Board ruled that the disease was constitutional in nature and not due to the military service - Held that :-  i) To get disability pension, it is essential to establish that the disease which led to discharge from service arose during the service due to the service conditions or influenced its further course leading to disability.
[Para 8]  ii) If the disease is constitutional and was always existing before the service and there was no prior note regarding it, the medical opinion would be final except under limited circumstances - Medical opinion is necessary to determine the relationship of a disease with military service - Rule 423 (d) - A disease which led to discharge would normally be deemed to have arisen during service if no note of it was made at the time of induction of the individual into service - Even if medical opinion holds that the disease could not have been detected prior to acceptance for service, it would not be deemed to have arisen during service.
[Para 10 and 11]  iii) The Pension Sanctioning Authority can conclude that the disease had a casual connection with service conditions while deciding the matter of entitlement to pension, despite the medical opinion that it was not attributable to or aggravated by military service.
[Para 11]  iv) The Annexure-III to appendix-II of Pension Regulations has to be taken into consideration to assess the aggravation of a disease.
[Para 12]  v) The presumption of disease having arisen or aggravated during service is available only for the diseases specified in Appendix-II, and there is no such material available that no stress or strain involved in military duty.
[Para 12]  Held that the constitutionally caused disease of the Petitioner does not fall under any of the categories of diseases mentioned in Annexure-III to Appendix-II and, therefore, held that the Petitioner is not entitled to disability pension.
