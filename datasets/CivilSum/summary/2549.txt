The Assistant Grade Examination Rules, 1984, Rule 12 - Instructions issued under the Rules - Validity - In 1984, rules were framed for the examination for promotion to the post of Assistant.
The government was given the power under Rule 12 to grant exemptions, for which reasons had to be recorded in writing.
In 1991, the government issued a notification granting exemption to employees who had completed 18 years of service.
The notification was challenged on the grounds that it violated Rule 12 as it did not contain reasons for granting exemption based on 18 years of experience.
However, it was held that the notification was valid as 18 years of experience is a sufficient reason for granting exemption.
Civil Writ Petition No 133102 of 1991 (DB) was overruled.
[Paras 71 and 72]

The Assistant Grade Examination Rules, 1984, Rule 12 - Retrospective applications of instructions issued under the Rules - In 1984, rules were framed prescribing examination for promotion to the post of Senior Assistants.
In 1991, the government granted exemption from passing the examination to employees who had completed 18 years of service.
This exemption will have prospective application.
Any orders issued under Rule 21 must have prospective application.
Posts of Assistants that were vacant prior to 2111991 must be filled under existing rules.
The exemption will apply to posts falling vacant on or after 2111991.
AIR 1983 SC 852 was relied upon.
[Paras 73, 74 and 75]

The Assistant Grade Examination Rules, 1984, Rule 12 - Rule amended in the year 1991 - Vacancies which fell vacant prior to amendment will be filled up according to existing rules and not according to amended rules.
