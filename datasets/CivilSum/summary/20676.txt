Constitution of India, Article 311 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19 - Termination - Remand - The respondent filed a petition with the Central Administrative Tribunal after being aggrieved by the termination of his services.
Several medical officers working on an ad hoc basis, who were junior to him, had their services regularized, but he was unfairly excluded from this.
The Tribunal held that the respondent was entitled to have his services considered for regularization.
However, the Supreme Court held that the Tribunal did not take into account the respondent's claim that the termination of his services was invalid.
Therefore, the order extending the benefit of regularization to the respondent cannot be upheld, and the matter has been remitted to the Tribunal for reconsideration in light of the observations of the Apex Court.
