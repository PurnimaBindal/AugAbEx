Constitution of India, Articles 29 and 30 - Delhi School Education Rules, 1973, Rules 47 and 96(3)(a) - Appointments in Aided Minority Institutions- Surplus Employees Absorption - Education Director’s Jurisdiction and Authority to enforce Appointments - Mere acceptance of aid by the minority institution from the government does not give the Education Director the power to dictate the appointment of surplus staff or of their choice - The Director's nominee in the Selection Committee serves only as an adviser without any decision-making power in the selection process - The Director is not empowered to enforce the appointment of any teacher, including that of surplus teachers.
