Suspension - Misconduct - First Bipartite Settlement, 1966, Clause 19(3)(a) - Moral turpitude - The investigation by CBI that resulted in a charge-sheet alleging misconduct charge offenses was sufficient cause for the respondent to be suspended, as it would be inconceivable for the bank to allow an employee facing serious charges of corruption and misappropriation of money to remain at work.
The appellant was bound to take action under Clause 19.3 of Settlement, 1966, even if nearly a decade has passed since the charges were laid.
The respondent may not return to duty, pending resolution of the charges against him.
[Paras 13 and 14]

Moral Turpitude - A banking company employee who engages in forgery and wrongful withdrawal of funds, not entitled to these funds, commits a moral turpitude offense given the position they hold.
