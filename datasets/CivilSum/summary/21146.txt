According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, seniority of an employee is to be reckoned from a particular date.
In cases where the transfer of an employee is in the interest of justice, the seniority of the employee should be counted from the date of their initial appointment.
For instance, an employee appointed as a driver in the Army must be entitled to count his seniority as a clerk from the same date.
