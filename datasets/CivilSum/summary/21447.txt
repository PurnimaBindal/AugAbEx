Constitution of India, Article 311(1) - Tamil Nadu Police Rules, Rule 3(b) - Competent authority - Departmental proceedings/Charge-sheet - The issuance of a charge-sheet and initiation of disciplinary proceedings against an employee is distinct from conducting an enquiry and imposing punishment - In the absence of a specific Rule, it is not unlawful for an authority subordinate to the punishing authority to issue a charge-sheet and initiate disciplinary proceedings - The conducting of the enquiry need not be concluded by the punishing authority itself - Instead, only the competent authority must impose the penalty.
