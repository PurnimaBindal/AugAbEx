Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Education Department (Subordinate Offices) Clerical Services Rules, 1941 - Promotion/Regularisation of services/Qualifications - Matriculate Class IV employees promoted as clerks on temporary basis - Non-regularised for around 15 years - Personnel with Ist or IInd class in matric appointed directly by department despite others having 3rd division being regularised - Qualifications must be considered during initial stage and cannot hinder future promotion or regularisation of service - Long experience on the job is the highest qualification - Petitioners have the right to regularisation of their service without requiring 1st and 2nd division in matric - Seniority must be given from the date their juniors have been given - Judgement of AIR 1990 SC 371 followed.
