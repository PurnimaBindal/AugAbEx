Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Equivalence of qualification - Respondents did not count marks obtained by Petitioner in Senior Secondary (Vocational) Examination, claiming that the qualification is not equivalent to Senior Secondary (Academic).
However, Supreme Court's decision in Sunita Sharma's case establishes the equivalence of the two qualifications - Therefore, respondents' action is not sustainable.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Grant of bonus marks - Petitioner was denied bonus marks for Inter University Certificate on the grounds that it is not of National level.
However, the decision of this Court in Madhu Sudan Joshi's case establishes that Inter University tournaments of Universities of other States are equivalent to National level Tournaments - Therefore, the Petitioner is entitled to 15 bonus marks for the certificate.
