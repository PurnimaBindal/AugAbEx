The Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 4, 2A - Payment of gratuity - An employee who has served for the last 20 years is entitled to gratuity.
In a case where the employer produced records showing that the employee had worked 240 days intermittently during a certain period, but could not prove the details of any artificial breaks or leave taken by the employee, authorities concluded that the employee was eligible for a gratuity grant.
As the decision is not considered unreasonable, interference is not necessary.
