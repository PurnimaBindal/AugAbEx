The appellant in the case of Technologist Grade I promotion under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India had his prayer for promotion rejected due to non-availability of vacancy.
Respondents did not produce any rules, and the appellant's seniority had to be followed.
However, the lack of provisions regarding promotion and non-consideration of the appellant's candidacy has led to a violation of his Fundamental right to equality.
As a result, the respondents are directed to consider the appellant's claim for promotion from the date that other junior individuals were promoted.
[Paras 5, 7, 8 and 10]
