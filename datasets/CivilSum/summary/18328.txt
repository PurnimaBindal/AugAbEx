Indian Railways Establishment Manual, Vol.
I - Selection - Promotion - Petitioners who were claiming conduct of selection against 25% rankers/promotion of Inspector of Works (IOW) Gr.
III from 1988 were denied their claim due to their failure to qualify in the selection held during the pendency of the OA.
The claim for seniority was rejected as no rotation has been provided in the Rules and the relief prayed for cannot be granted to the petitioners as they did not implead any of the direct recruiters in the OA or in the writ petition.
