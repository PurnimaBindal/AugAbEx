Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol. II, Rule 22(b) - Indian Penal Code, Section 409 - Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 482 - Misappropriation by Government Servant in the year 1989 - Criminal proceedings - Retirement of Government servent from service in 1990 - FIR lodged against him in 1995 - According to Rule 22(b) of Service Rules, criminal action can be taken against a government servant even four years after their retirement - However, the government cannot withhold their pensionary benefits due to pending criminal proceedings.
Previous cases, 1988(2) Recent Criminal Reports 564 and 1992(2) Recent Criminal Reports 417 are not valid anymore.
[Paras 10 and 11] B Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 482 - Indian Penal Code, Section 409 - Misappropriation by Government servant in the year 1988 - Govt. servant retired in 1990 - Case registered in the year 1995 - Challan not put up for another one year - The government has been ordered to put up the challan within two months, with a further direction that the FIR would be quashed if the challan is not put up within the said period.
[Paras 10 and 11] C. Punjab Civil Services Rules, Rule 22 - Indian Penal Code, Section 409 - Misappropriation by Govt. Servant - Retirement of Govt. servant - Criminal proceedings started four years after retirement - Rule 22 of service rules does not prohibit criminal proceedings, but the government cannot withhold the pensionary benefits of the employee in question.
Cases from 1988(1) RCR 564 and 1992(2) RCR 417 are no longer applicable.
