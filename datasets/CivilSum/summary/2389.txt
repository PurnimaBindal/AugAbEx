According to Section 15(2) and (3) of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936, if an employee is awarded punishment of stoppage of increment in a departmental enquiry, the authority under Payment of Wages Act has no right to intervene with disciplinary orders.
Competent authority has no role in deciding whether the employee should receive a refund for delayed or deducted wages.
The revision petition was allowed.
