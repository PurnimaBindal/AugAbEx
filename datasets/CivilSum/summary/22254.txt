Central Civil Service (Conduct) Rules, 1964, Rule 3 - Disciplinary proceedings - Granting stay in disciplinary proceedings at the interlocutory stage - Interim order - The disciplinary authority must hold an inquiry, not the Tribunal - Tribunal has no right to interfere at the interim stage, and yet it chose to interfere based on material that was yet to be produced in the inquiry - the Tribunal cannot take over the functions of the disciplinary authority and has no jurisdiction over the truth or correctness of the charges.
[Paras 4, 5 and 6]  B.
Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1964, Rule 3 - Disciplinary proceedings - Stay at interlocutory stage - Judicial review - Interference by Tribunal at the stage of framing of charges - Not sustainable - Judicial review is not an appeal from a decision but a review of the manner in which the decision is made - Held, if a Court cannot interfere with the truth or correctness of the charges even in a proceeding against the final order, it is un-understandable how that can be done by the Tribunal at the stage of framing of charges.
