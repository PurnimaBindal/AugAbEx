Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Selection - Post of Extra Departmental Branch Post Master - The required qualification for the post was VIII standard with matriculation shown as a preferential qualification.
A petitioner working as a substitute in the same post with a graduate degree submitted their BA degree certificate and mark sheet.
There was no rule for selecting candidates based on the percentage of marks secured in the High School examination.
Hence, rejecting the petitioner's candidature on the grounds of not submitting the High School examination mark sheet was not proper.
