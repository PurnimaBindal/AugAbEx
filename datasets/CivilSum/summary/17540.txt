The petitioner, a Municipal Committee worker sent to Public Health on deputation, sought regularisation in line with a Haryana Government policy decision.
However, his services were terminated instead.
The petitioner's case was supported by a Division Bench decision in CWP 9535 of 1994 Braham Chand v. State, leading to the quashing of his relieving order and direction for the municipality to regularise his services.
