Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1964 Rule 3 - Dismissal is a serious penalty as it deprives a person of their livelihood.
The authorities concerned should not take it lightly.
The Inquiry Officer failed to consider vital evidence that showed his lack of application of mind.
While the original charge of maintaining absolute integrity was not proven, the charge of negligence and dereliction of duty was.
There were no allegations that he received consideration, etc.
Dismissal is not appropriate in this case.
Instead, the penalty was converted into two increments being stopped with cumulative effect and the petitioner was reinstated without back wages.
