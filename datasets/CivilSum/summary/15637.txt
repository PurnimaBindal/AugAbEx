Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Compensation - A workman sustained injuries in an accident arising out of and in the course of employment - The doctor reported that the workman had a disability percentage of 90%, but did not mention anything about loss of earning capacity - The commissioner assessed the loss of earning capacity at 100%, but this was not justified - The matter will be remitted back for fresh disposal - However, since the claimant received nothing and the accident happened 7-8 years ago, the appeal is disposed of by reducing compensation.
