In regards to a labor matter, the Constitution of India's Articles 226/227 address the issue of a reference.
In the case at hand, the Labour Commissioner declined the reference on the basis that the workman's case could not be proven.
However, it was determined that the Labour Commissioner did not have the jurisdiction to make such a decision since they are not an adjudicatory authority.
As a result, the respondent was instructed to refer the matter to the appropriate authority, such as the Labour Court or Industrial Tribunal for adjudication.
