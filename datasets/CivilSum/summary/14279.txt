The J&K Subordinate Services Recruitment Board Act, 1990, Section 2(c) allows for the process of selection to be undertaken by the District Recruitment Board instead of the J&K Subordinate Service Selection Recruitment Board.
It was determined that the selection process was undertaken by the competent authority.
[Para 4]
The minimum qualification for selection is matric with science, which was not available with respondents No. 5, 6, and 10.
It was ruled that the respondent/authority must investigate whether these respondents possessed the minimum qualification and pass a detailed order.
[Para 8]
The petitioner claimed that respondent No. 9 was not entitled to the appointment on the basis of being a resident of Kathua since she had started staying in Jammu after marriage.
However, it was determined that there was no allegation that respondent No. 9 had abandoned her intention to stay in the District of Kathua.
