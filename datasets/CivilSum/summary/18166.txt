Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 300A - Age of retirement - Reversal - Voluntary retirement - Retiral benefits - Petitioners applied for voluntary retirement during the period 15122003 to 12.1.2004 - On 12122003 authority reversing age of retirement of employee to fifty eight years - Petitioners claiming benefits for left out service upto the age of sixty years - Benefits given to petitioners considering their age of retirement as fifty eight - It was held that just because petitioners applied for voluntary retirement before 3132004, their age of retirement could not be considered as sixty - Furthermore, the petitioners would not have been entitled to remain in service till the age of sixty even if they had chosen to continue - Therefore, the benefits given by considering their retirement age as fifty eight were deemed reasonable.
