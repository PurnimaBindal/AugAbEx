The regular second appeal deals with promotion, the deemed date of promotion, and the claim for promotion with effect from the date.
The lower courts allowed the claim for promotion for those who were junior to the respondent's deceased father and were promoted.
The claim for consequential benefits was made ten months after the order of deemed date of promotion.
The court held that it cannot be considered as not expeditions.
The rate of interest for arrears was appropriate at 8%, although it was later reduced.
