Sections 25-F and 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 state that delay in compensation for reinstatement is given when the demand notice is served after 9 years and if the proceedings in the Labour Court take more than 5 years to complete.
In a case where a workman worked for only one year and 10 months and was retrenched due to the specific work coming to an end, a reinstatement with back wages was awarded after 13 years.
However, it would not be justified to enforce reinstatement in such a situation.
Compensation for wrongful termination, in the form of Rs. 50,000/-, was awarded in lieu of reinstatement and other benefits.
