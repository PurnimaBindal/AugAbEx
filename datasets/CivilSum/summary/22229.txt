According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, and 309, and the Railway Servants (Pass) Rules and Railway Services (Revised Pay) Rules of 1986, Railway servants were classified into Groups A, B, C, and D based on their pay scale.
Assistant Audit Officers in the Railway Audit Department on the pay scale of Rs. 2000-3200 were treated the same as Railway Servants (Officers) in the pay scale of Rs. 2000-3200 when it came to facilities and privileges.
However, additional privileges such as passes and P.T.Os were given to A and B Group Gazetted Officers.
Assistant Audit Officers in the Railway Audit Department in the Pay Scale of Rs. 2000-3200 cannot claim the same facilities or privileges as B Group Gazetted Railway Servants by ignoring the classification based on their pay scale.
