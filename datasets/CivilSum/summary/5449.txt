Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 9 - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Industrial Dispute - Alternative remedy - While the ID Act and the Civil Court and writ jurisdiction of the High Court are not exclusive to each other, rather co-existing, a Govt. employee is entitled to avail any of the remedies available.
Additionally, the remedy of judicial review under Article 226 of the Constitution is provided as a fundamental right.
Therefore, two streams, namely remedy under the ID Act, Writ Petition, or a petition before the Administrative Tribunal are co-existing for an aggrieved person.
