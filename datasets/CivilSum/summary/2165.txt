The reservation of 50% seats for Sikh students in educational institutions operated by SGPC in Punjab has been quashed, as it was found to be violative of Article 14 of the Constitution Although Sikhs may be a minority in the country as a whole, within Punjab they are treated as a majority community, and therefore cannot be given special treatment.
Notifications from the Punjab government that declared Sikh educational institutions in the state to be "minority" and permitted them to reserve 50% seats for Sikh community members have been invalidated.
Additionally, it was found that there was no evidence to suggest that Sikhs were a non-dominant group in Punjab who were likely to be deprived of their rights by other communities, and that the notifications conferred additional protection on one group of citizens to the exclusion of others, which violated Article 14 of the Constitution Finally, it was determined that if a public authority exercises its authority based on irrelevant considerations or by ignoring relevant ones, the decision will be considered ultra vires and invalid.
