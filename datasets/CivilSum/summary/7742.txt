Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10, 33-C(2) - Determination of wages - Application under Section 33-C(2) - Permissibility - In case of a dispute between a workman and Petitioner, the Labour Court cannot use its jurisdiction under Section 10 of the Act while considering an application under Section 33-C(2) - Each provision of the Act is marked for a distinct remedy and cannot be mixed with each other.
