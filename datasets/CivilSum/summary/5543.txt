Under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, ex-casual laborers acquired the right to be considered for absorption of their services under Memo No. 1138/85-1 dated 2681985 rather than B.P.Ms.
No. 36 dated 1851997.
In response to an earlier filed court petition, the High Court directed the respondents to consider the case of petitioners for regular appointment in accordance with Memo No. 1138/85-1 dated 2681985.
The respondents were obligated to comply with this direction, and the claim of the petitioner for absorption should only be considered in terms of Memo No. 1138/85-1 dated 2681985, excluding restrictions imposed by B.P.Ms.
No. 36 dated 1851997 regarding the quota of 50% fixed.
