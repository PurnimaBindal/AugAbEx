Constitution of India, Article 311 - No work - No pay Rule - Promotion - When Employees approach High Court with regard to their seniority and promotions to grades in class III service, there are no directions for promotions to class II service by High Court.
However, the Railways have worked out promotions to Class II service on the basis of the new seniority list of class III service.
This has resulted in the employees gaining substantially.
However, emoluments of higher posts with retrospective effect are categorically denied.
There is neither equity nor justice in favour of employees to award them emoluments of higher posts with retrospective effect.
This is because the employees did not work in said posts, and the principle of "no work no pay" is attracted.
