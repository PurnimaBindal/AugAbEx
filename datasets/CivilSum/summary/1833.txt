According to Section 39 of the State Financial Corporation Act, 1951 and Regulations 66 and 92 of the Punjab Financial Corporation (Staff) Regulations, 1961, policy matters of the Corporation, such as granting allowances and concessions like House Rent allowance and Medical Re-embursement to its employees, are not within the jurisdiction of the State Government to issue directions on.
The State Government may only advise or guide the Corporation in such matters, and any directions issued must be treated as advice or guidance.
Ultimately, the Corporation must make its own independent decision.
[Paras 13, 15, 20, 21, 22, 23] B.
The State Government cannot issue directions to the Corporation without obtaining the advice of the Small Industries Bank, as this would be a violation of the statutory provisions of Section 39 of the State Financial Corporation Act, 1951.
[Para 22] C. In matters of judicial review of administrative orders, Article 226 of the Constitution of India mandates that the court examines the decision-making process and determines whether the decision taken was reasonable, taking into consideration all the relevant factors necessary for the decision-making process.
The decision must not be irrational or perverse.
