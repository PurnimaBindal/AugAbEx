In accordance with the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 2(9), the National Textile Corporation (APKK and M) Ltd.'s employees in its Administrative Office are subject to making Employees State Insurance Contribution as their work is connected with the administration of its Mills or Factories.
This liability cannot be avoided in areas where the ESI Act is enforced.
The term 'employee' covers anyone employed for wages on any work related to the factory or establishment.
[Paras 6 and 7] B.
Under Sections 88, 89, 90 and 91-A of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, only the State Government has the authority to grant an exemption for ESI contribution payments.
Insurance Court does not have the power to do so.
