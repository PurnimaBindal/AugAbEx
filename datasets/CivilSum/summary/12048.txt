Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Regularisation of services - Concealment of material facts - The petitioner was appointed as an honorary Circle Organiser on the condition that he would not claim permanent appointment.
The services were dispensed after five months.
While claiming a fresh appointment through the selection process, the petitioner withheld information about the dispensation of his services.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to any relief.
[Paras 13, 15, 19, 20 and 21]
