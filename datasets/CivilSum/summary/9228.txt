The appointment posts advertised under Constitution of India, Article 16 pertained to 120 AE (Electrical) and 30 AE (Electronics/Telecommunications).
The posts of AE (Mech.)
were not advertised though the advertisement stated that candidates with a Mechanical Engineering degree were also eligible for appointment.
Written test was the only criterion for determining suitability for selection.
The petitioner appeared in the first and second lists of successful candidates, but certain other candidates with lower merit were given appointments.
A re-advertisement for unfilled posts was conducted, and the Board appointed 77 Electrical Engineers.
The petitioner was at Serial No. 102, but the Board stated that his case could not be jeopardised by filling vacancies from the AE (Mech.)
quota which had not been advertised.
Thus, the Board was directed by the SC to give appointment to the petitioner, and he would be deemed to have been appointed from the date the last person from the list was appointed.
[Paras 4 and 6]
