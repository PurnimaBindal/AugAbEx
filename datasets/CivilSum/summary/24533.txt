According to Constitution of India, Article 226 - State Bank of India - Officers Staff Service Rules, Rule 32(4), writ jurisdiction cannot be used for cases of misconduct and High Courts cannot re-appreciate evidence already considered by the Inquiry Officer and Disciplinary Authority.
They cannot sit over the findings of the disciplinary authority as an appellate court/authority.
[Paras 6, 7 and 8]
In cases of disciplinary proceedings, the standard of proof required is that of preponderance of probability and not proof beyond reasonable doubt.
Jurisdiction of Tribunal or courts to interfere with disciplinary matters or punishment cannot be equated with an appellate jurisdiction.
Dismissal as punishment for fraudulent misappropriation of money of depositors is not disproportionate for an officer of the Bank expected to maintain a high degree of responsibility and integrity to protect the interest of the Bank and its customers.
[Paras 13, 14 and 15]
