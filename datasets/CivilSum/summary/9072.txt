The Punjab Re-organisation Act of 1966, Sections 79(1) and 80, addressing retrenchment indicates that Petitioners who are work-charged employees of the Beas Construction Board, may be retrenched upon the completion of the Beas Project, despite their entitlement to retrenchment benefits under industrial legislation.
Jaswant Singh v. Union of India, AIR 1980 SC 115 was relied upon.
[Para 6]
Petitioners who were work-charged employees of the Beas Construction Board were discharged from service once the Beas Project was completed.
They were later employed in the Power Wing of the Board, and as such, it was not open to them to claim that the period of service rendered under the Beas Construction Board should be tagged with the period of service under the Board for the purpose of regularisation of their services and consequential benefits.
Jaswant Singh v. Union of India, AIR 1980 SC 115 was relied upon.
[Para 6]
The Punjab Re-organisation Act of 1966, Sections 79(1) and 80, addressing the transfer of services indicates that the Board is an independent statutory body that has the power to employ such staff as it may deem necessary for its efficient discharge of its funds.
Additionally, they may frame service conditions of their employees.
The work-charged employees were discharged from the service of the Board upon the completion of the Beas Project, and so, their claim for transfer along with its components was negatived in view of the previously cited SC Judgment in Jaswant Singh v. Union of India, AIR 1980 SC 115, where it was held that the employment of persons employed in Beas project would cease upon its completion or the fulfilment of other works for which they were employed.
The instructions for regularisation of services of those Work-Charged employees who had completed five years' service issued by the Punjab Govt. were inapplicable to Board workers.
