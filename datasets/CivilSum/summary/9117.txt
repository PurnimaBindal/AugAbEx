Constitution of India, Articles 30(1) and 311 - Suspension - Subsistence allowance - Deprivation of - Civil Suit - Maintainability of - A civil suit can be maintained against the management of a school governed by a charity missionary society for depriving an employee of subsistence allowance during suspension.
