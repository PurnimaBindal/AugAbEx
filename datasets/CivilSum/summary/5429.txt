Constitution of India, Article 311 - Promotion - Denial - Promotion was denied due to the pendency of a preliminary enquiry - However, the charge related to a circular and did not amount to misconduct - No preliminary enquiry was necessary - The enquiry went on for years without the fault of the employee - Denying the promotion based on the pending enquiry was not appropriate - The State authority's action in this regard was criticized.
