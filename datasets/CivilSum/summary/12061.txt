In regards to Constitution of India, Article 226 and Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F, retrenchment due to the regularisation of another worker's services is not an exception under Section 2(oo)(bb).
This termination falls under the term "for any reason whatsoever" and is therefore illegal.
The labour Court's findings based on fact and law should not be interfered with.
