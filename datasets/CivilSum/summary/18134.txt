Pension Regulations for the Army, 1961, Regulation 173-A - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 13(3)(III)(IV) - Disability pension - Discharge from service on compassionate ground - If an Army personnel suffers injury causing 20% or more disability attributable to service, even if released on compassionate ground due to such disability, he is considered discharged and entitled to disability pension along with service element pension - Failure to perform job duties due to disability does not prevent the receipt of Regulation 173 benefits.
