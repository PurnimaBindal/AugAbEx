"The respondent resigned voluntarily in 6/1970 after 25 years of service, which did not meet the Pension Rules requirement of 30 years qualifying service at the time.
Despite an amendment in the rules in 1972, granting the respondent pension under the amended Rules of 1972 was deemed illegal by the Tribunal due to lack of retrospective effect.
However, the State Government can still consider eligibility and grant relaxation.
An order is expected to be passed within four months.
