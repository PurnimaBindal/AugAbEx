Sections 30, 20(1) of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 were utilized to argue against the non-service of notice to the State under Section 21, which was only raised during the appeal.
Nevertheless, it was deemed that the non-issuance of a notice to the State caused no prejudice to the insurance company.
However, the contention was raised the first time the issue was brought up on appeal, so it cannot be accepted.
Nonetheless, it is worth mentioning that the company was not effectively pursuing its case since the officer who oversaw the matter had suffered a severe heart attack.
In view of that, the case was remanded for redetermination, following the payment of Rs. 50,000/- to the claimant as interim compensation.
Furthermore, other parties could start afresh to adduce their evidence.
As a result, the appeal was allowed.
