A.
Per Section 79 of the Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948, and PSEB Service of Engineers (Electricity) Recruitment Regulations, 1965, Regulation 15(l), the Board determined that its decision, which failed to allow the settlement of seniority, was invalid as per Regulation 15 and did not violate any provision of law.
The Board subsequently revoked its decision as well as any rights vested by it, taking into account the eligibility of all engineers for promotion in conformity with qualifications obtained and the seniority acquisition.
B.
As per Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution, a promoter who acquires the required qualifications earlier must be considered for promotion before someone who acquires them later.
C. According to the Constitution's Articles 14 and 16, a person who becomes eligible first is entitled to be considered for promotion before someone senior to him, who has not yet met the necessary criteria, or has met them later than the junior.
D The Board's decision of 31121976, which established seniority promotion and disregarded a passed-over employee's seniority upon the acquisition of new qualifications, was repugnant to Regulation 15(l) of the 1965 Regulations and, as a result, void; the Board appropriately annulled this order in 1989.
E. The executive order passed by the Board on December 31, 1976, was void since it was not a regulation under Section 79 of the Act and did not qualify as an amendment to Regulation 15.
Since the Board did not finalize the seniority, no vested rights existed, and provisional seniority and promotions have continued ever since.
The impugned order could not be said to have taken away any vested rights.
