The petitioner is requesting a correction to his date of birth, changing it from 27.12.38 to 271240.
However, the petitioner has previously accepted the incorrect date of birth and confirmed it in his representation.
The petitioner argues that the mistake is due to an error on his matriculation certificate.
Nevertheless, the court concludes that this case does not qualify as a "clerical error," and therefore, there is no irregularity in the challenged order.
