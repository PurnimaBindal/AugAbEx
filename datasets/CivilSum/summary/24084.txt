Constitution of India, Articles 229(2) and 226 - Service conditions of High Court Staff - Equal pay for equal work - The Chief Justice of the respective High Court is the competent authority - The Chief Justice has evaluated and assessed work before framing Rules and prescribing pay scales for the various positions - Stenographers cannot demand higher pay scales based on revisions to those available to Chief Secretaries of the State - High Court, on the judicial side, cannot direct the Chief Justice to set pay scales equal to those of corresponding posts in the State Ministry for High Court Employees.
