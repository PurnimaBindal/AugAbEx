Constitution of India, 1950, Article 226/227 - Admission to MBBS/BDS Course - Despite already having taken admission in the previous year, the petitioner left the Course before it started, and was subsequently denied admission in the next year due to a condition in the brochure - No illegality was detected, and it was held that the terms and conditions of the brochure are binding and must be adhered to - Any misplaced sympathy in education matters should be avoided to prevent admission contrary to prospectus and calendar of the University.
