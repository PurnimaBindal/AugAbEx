Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Limitation Act, Articles 58 and 113 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rule 4(III) - Promotion - Supersession due to punishment of forfeiture of four years service which was finalized - Forfeiture has the consequence of reduction to a lower stage in time scale and reduction in seniority equal to the years of service forfeited - Punishment order unchallenged within limitation - Lawsuit challenging the supersession on that basis is rightly considered time-barred.
[Paras 15 and 16]
