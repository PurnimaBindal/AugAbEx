Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226, 227 - Compassionate appointment - Death of employee - Son of deceased denied compassionate appointment - Writ filed after 8 years of denial - Writ dismissed - Purpose of compassionate appointment is to assist family in sudden crisis and relieve financial distress due to loss of sole bread-earner - Family has survived for over 12 years since death - No evidence of ongoing financial distress - Therefore, matter may be rejected due to laches.
