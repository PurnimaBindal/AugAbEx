According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 and UP Basic Education Act, 1972, Section 19, regularisation of an ad hoc teacher appointment is subject to the terms of their contract of service.
[Paras 11, 20 to 22]
In regards to selection for teaching positions, Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 allow for written examinations and changes in the interview date do not provide adequate grounds for contesting a selection process.
[Paras 16 to 18]
UP Secondary Education Commission and Selection Board Act, 1982, Section 16 relates to the appointment of High School and intermediate college teachers and not to junior Basic School teachers.
The Board's recommendation is vital in such appointments.
[Para 19]
Teachers appointed to fixed-term positions with fixed salaries are not entitled to the same salary and benefits as those appointed under the Act and regularisation of such positions cannot be claimed.
This is according to UP Secondary Education Service Commission and Selection Board Act, 1982, Section 33-A [Paras 21 and 22]
