VL Mathur, Advocate was the representative for Respondent No. 2.
In this case, Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Sections 15(2) and 17 are relevant.
The management declared a lockout and the authority issued notice for attending the proceedings.
However, there was no response and as a result, an ex parte order was passed.
An application for setting aside the ex parte order was rejected on the grounds of insufficient cause.
The petitioner filed a writ but had already foregone the remedy of appeal under section 17.
It was concluded that the petitioner had deliberately chosen to circumvent the provision of the law.
Therefore, the writ was not maintainable.
The management was directed to disburse the arrears of salary and the writ was dismissed.
