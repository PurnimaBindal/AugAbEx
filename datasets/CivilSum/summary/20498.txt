The appealant argued that the appointment of the respondents to posts in the Group I services in the cadre of Deputy Collector for recruitment year 1980-81 violated the provisions of the Rules of Reservations.
Despite challenging the appointment of the respondents before the Tribunal in 1987, the appointments made in 1981 were not interfered with and the order became final as it was not challenged in any higher court.
A second petition was filed before the Tribunal in 1990, but was rejected due to latches after the lapse of nine years.
An application for review was dismissed on the grounds that there was no error of law apparent on the face of the order which could be reviewed by the Tribunal.
Additionally, the affected persons were not made parties to the proceedings.
The court held that no Court or Tribunal can pass any order against a person without impleading them as a party whose rights would be affected.
The impugned order of the Tribunal had no justification for interference under Article 136 of the Constitution, resulting in the appeal being dismissed.
