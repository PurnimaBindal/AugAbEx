According to Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rules 3(3) & 3(2), notice of retirement on attaining qualifying service must be given to employees not under suspension.
Premature retirement is automatic at the end of the notice period, with no formal approval necessary, except in cases where employee is under suspension.
If there's a delay in approval, employees cannot claim wages for the period after the notice period.
