The court granted the petitioner provisional admission to the second professional course of MBBS, but they did not complete 1-1/2 academic years and were not allowed to sit in the examination.
However, they were permitted to appear in the papers with the court's orders.
Although the petitioner attended 82% of the lectures, which was above the prescribed 75% requirement, the university withheld permission for them to join or appear in the second professional examination.
This was viewed as an unjustified and arbitrary action, and the court set aside the university's decision.
As a result, the petitioner's result will be deemed to have been declared.
