Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admissions/Education - Admission in the General Nursing and Midwifery Training Course on the basis of Intermediate Certificates issued by the Central Board of Higher Education, New Delhi, which were not recognized by the Jodhpur University nor granted equivalence to 10+2 or First Year T.D.C.
- Despite this, the petitioners were granted admission and allowed to take various examinations by the Rajasthan University - While the certificates may not be valid for want of recognition, they cannot be considered forged or bogus - Cancelling the admission at this stage would be highly unfair to the students and serve no useful purpose - Respondents have been directed to declare the result of the petitioners.
