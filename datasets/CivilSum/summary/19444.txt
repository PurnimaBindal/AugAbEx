Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Pay - Pension - Retiral benefits - Qualifying service - The petitioner, having received the benefit of previous service in the Animal Husbandry Department for his pay, cannot be denied the same benefit for pension and retiral benefits, particularly as both departments were under the same government of Haryana.
[Para 7] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Qualifying service/Non- qualifying service - Pay - Pension - Retiral benefits - In view of the continuity of service provided to the petitioner for pay and increments, the period of service he rendered in the Animal Husbandry Department should be counted towards his pensionary benefits, which would total more than 20 years of service, even after subtracting non-qualifying service.
Mandamus has been ordered by the respondents to grant the petitioner pension and other retiral benefits.
