Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical reimbursement - Interest on delayed payment - Petition claiming interest for delayed payment of dues not tenable in writ jurisdiction - Pure claim for payment of money must be made before Civil Court - Writ petition not competent - Petitioner can seek remedy in appropriate forum.
[Paras 2, 3 and 5]
Precedent - Show cause notice for writ petition - Not binding on other benches of co-ordinate jurisdiction - Uniformity in orders essential for judicial discipline.
[Para 4]
Medical reimbursement - Interest - Claim for interest on principal already paid not justified.
