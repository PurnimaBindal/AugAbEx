Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Compassionate Appointment - The petitioner lost both legs due to an accident while working as a driver and was subsequently retired from service on medical invalidation.
His son's application for compassionate appointment was denied due to not having 5 years of service left from the date of retirement, but the court ruled that the 5 years should be counted from the date of amputation of the petitioner's legs, as he still had 4 years and 8 months of service remaining.
The grant of ex gratia and pension to the petitioner was deemed an invalid reason to deny compassionate appointment to his son, and the respondents were directed to consider and appoint him on compassionate grounds based on his qualifications.
