IAS (Appointment by Competitive Examination) Regulations, 1955, Regulation 4(B)(ii) - Civil Services Examination Rules, 1992, Rule 6(a) - Constitution of India, Article 14 - The cut off date for IAS examination is fixed as 1st August, which is non-arbitrary as it remains unchanged even after the introduction of Preliminary Examination.
However, if the cut off date was randomly selected, it could be challenged as arbitrary in court.
[Paras 4 and 9]
