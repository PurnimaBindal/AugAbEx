According to Section 12(3)(e) of the Institutes of Technology Act, 1961, Pilot Instructor (Glider) is classified as a technical post with a retirement age of 60 years.
The post of Instructor outlined in Statute 11 is comparable to Assistant Lecturer, while the post of Senior Pilot Instructor is comparable to Senior Physical Instructor as mentioned in Statute 11(b).
The Senate of IIT holds exclusive domain over curricula and syllabi for courses, and the post of Senior Pilot Instructor was rightfully deemed a technical post with a retirement age of 60 based on the nature of the work assigned.
