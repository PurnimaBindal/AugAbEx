Government order was issued for the correction of date of birth in service record.
The order stated that those who crossed five years of service should apply within one year from the date of the order, but not beyond two years preceding retirement.
The order also clearly mentioned that belated applications would be summarily rejected.
The petitioner applied for the correction of date of birth a few days before retirement, but the application was rejected.
The court held that the application was hopelessly time-barred and that the employee must comply with the terms and conditions of the order strictly if they seek its benefits.
The Government was not found to have acted illegally in rejecting the petitioner's application.
