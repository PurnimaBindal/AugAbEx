Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana Govt. (Removal of Stagnation and Grant of Assured Career Promotion (ACP) Rules, 1996, Rules 5 and 9 and explanation to Rule 5 - Promotion from Class IV to Class III post - Prohibition on granting ACP after promotion - Anomaly and discrimination - When a junior is given a higher pay scale than his senior in the cadre, such an anomaly must be rectified by giving step up to the senior to bring them at par with their junior, a common practice applicable to all Govt. employees - Despite a specific bar in the Rules for grant of 2nd ACP scale if an employee is promoted from Class IV to Class III and has already received the 1st ACP scale, seniors are entitled to step-up in their pay scales as a general rule whenever a junior is fixed in a higher pay scale due to the grant of ACP scale, as there is no such prohibition in Haryana Govt. Rules.
