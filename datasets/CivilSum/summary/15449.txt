Punjab Civil Services Rules, Rule 423(2) - The condoning of interruptions and deficiencies under this rule only applies to officers who have retired on or after 511961 - It is not applicable to those who are still in service - The plaintiff was appointed as a work charge patwari on a temporary basis - Even though he was later rehired as a work charge inspector, he was informed that his previous service would not be counted - Therefore, the plaintiff cannot benefit from Rule 423 since his service prior to resignation cannot be counted.
