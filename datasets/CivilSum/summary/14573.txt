The revised scales of pay for Junior Engineers of Corporation granted w.e.f.
January 1993 is considered discriminatory and arbitrary, violating Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
Other Junior Engineers working in various departments of State Govt were granted the same scales of pay w.e.f.
January 1, 1992.
[Paras 10 and 12]
The Employer does not have the right to fix different dates regarding the grant of revised scales of pay.
Discrimination is not allowed unless good reasons are given.
All employees who are similarly situated have a right to be treated alike, as per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
[Para 8]
Imp.
Point Discrimination - Revised scales of pay - All employees who are similarly situated, are entitled to be treated alike - If the pay scales of one set of employees are revised w.e.f.
a particular date while others who are similarly situated, are given the same benefit w.e.f.
a later date - The action of concerned authority liable to be questioned - Unless good grounds are shown, it is liable to be dubbed and discreminatory - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
The State has a duty to direct its policy towards securing equal pay for equal work to provide adequate livelihood, in accordance with the Constitution of India, Articles 12, 14 and 16.
