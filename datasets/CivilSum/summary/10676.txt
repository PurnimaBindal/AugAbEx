Punjab Civil Service (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3(1)(a) - A constable can be retired at the age of 50 years - In this case, the petitioner was allowed to cross the efficiency bar on 18.2.1984 - Although the report from 141980 to 3091980 mentions that the petitioner's reputation for honesty was not satisfactory, there was no complaint about honesty thereafter - The contention that anything against the petitioner prior to crossing the efficiency bar stands wiped out is valid - There was no material available with the respondents to justify premature retirement, and one entry in the column of integrity is not enough to declare an employee unsuitable - The petitioner should be treated in service until the age of 58 years with financial benefits.
