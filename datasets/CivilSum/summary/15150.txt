Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission - Eligibility - LLB 3 year course - The petitioner, who had qualified in the Bachelor in Technology in Electrical Engineering Course, appeared in CET for admission to 3 years LLB Course.
He was placed at serial 47 in the merit list, and produced a certificate showing his eligibility and admit card during counseling.
However, his admission was denied on the objection that the certificate showing his eligibility was not issued by the university but by the institute from where he graduated, which was deemed improper.
The eligibility of the petitioner was not doubted at any point of time and therefore, it is not permissible to deny admission on such technical grounds.
The court ordered the provisional admission to be regularized.
[Paras 3, 5 and 6]
