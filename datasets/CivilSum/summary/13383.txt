Minimum Wages Act, 1948, Section 3 - Minimum wages - Applicability of the Act - A society registered under the Co-operative Societies Act is considered a society within entry No. 21 of the Schedule to the Minimum Wages Act - The nature of a Co-operative Society dealing with milk production remains unchanged according to the Co-operative Societies Act - Therefore, the petitioner-Society falls under the scope of the Minimum Wages Act.
