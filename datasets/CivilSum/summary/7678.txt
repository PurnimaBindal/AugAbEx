Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25(g) - Natural justice - Adhoc appointment - Termination - Juniors retained - The employer must follow the rule of last-cum-first go, enshrined in Section 25(G) of the Act, even if the workman has not worked for at least 240 days in a calendar year.
This principle was upheld in 1996(4) SCT 143 (SC) & 1999(2) SCT 284 (SC).
