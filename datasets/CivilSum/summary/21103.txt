Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 311 - Quantum of punishment - Intervention in writ jurisdiction - The court generally refrains from interfering in the punishment given in disciplinary proceedings in order to impose its own decision regarding penalty, except if it appears that the punishment imposed is shockingly disproportionate to the misconduct demonstrated by the offender - When the delinquent was charged solely with negligence in the misplacing of an office file which cannot be attributed to any deliberate or mala fide conduct, nor any ulterior motive, the punishment of dismissal from duty will undoubtedly be found to be shockingly disproportionate to the established guilt - Service rules offer minor penalties for such offences - Dismissal from service cannot be justified - Stoppage of one increment and one stage EB with the forfeiture of back pay in addition to suspension allowance for the duration of suspension was substituted as punishment to meet the ends of justice.
[Paras 5 and 6]
