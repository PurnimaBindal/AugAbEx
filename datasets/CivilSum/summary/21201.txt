In accordance with the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Sections 2(9), 2(14) and 46(c), there is a cut off wage applicable to insured persons.
The claimant was an insured person who experienced an accident in the course of employment, however, examination of the Act's provisions reveals that the claimant was not considered an employee after 30.9.1989 due to salary surpassing the cut off wage.
Therefore, any injury sustained after this date does not entitle the claimant to any benefit of disablement.
[Para 7]  B In order to obtain benefits under the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 46(c) mandates that for claimants to qualify, they must fulfill two cumulative conditions : (i) to be an insured person and (ii) the injury must have occurred whilst the claimant was an employee.
