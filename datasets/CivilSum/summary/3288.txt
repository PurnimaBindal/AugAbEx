Kerala Government Presses Subordinate Service Rules, 1976, Branch No. 10, Notes 1 and 9 - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotions - Non-discrimination - Kerala Government Presses Subordinate Service Rules, 1976 were retrospectively amended with effect from 3111978 to provide a 1:1 ratio for promotion between diploma holders and certificate holders.
Given the technical nature of the positions in government presses, the ratio between diploma holders and certificate holders is not unreasonable, and does not violate Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
