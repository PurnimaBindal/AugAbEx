Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 21 - Compassionate appointments - Guidelines - Compassionate appointments must comply with policy instructions - Failure to adhere to the specified time frame for requesting appointments is not permissible, even if a limitation has been prescribed - Failure to apply within the stipulated period cannot be justified on the grounds of disability or the applicant being a minor at the time of their father's death - Requests made outside the prescribed time frame will not be considered.
[Paras 5-7]
