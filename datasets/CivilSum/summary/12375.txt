Constitution of India, Article 16 - Higher Pay scale - Ad hoc service - Counting of - Formulation of Policy by Govt. for higher pay scale - The benefit of higher pay scale is to be released for those who completed 10 or 20 years of satisfactory service.
However, the petitioner was denied this benefit on the grounds that ad hoc service cannot be counted for granting of higher pay scale and that such benefit was only for regular employees.
Nevertheless, in accordance with the policy decision of the Govt., ad hoc service is held countable for the benefit of higher pay scale as evidenced in decisions 1995(4) RSJ 586 and 1997(4) RSJ 409.
