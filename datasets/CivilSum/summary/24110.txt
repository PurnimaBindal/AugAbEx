Constitution of India, Articles 132 and 226 - Selection - Parties affected by selection - A challenge to a selection cannot be maintained if the selected candidates are not present and do not have the right to hold office.
[Para 18]
Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Reservation - Past appointments without reservation - Court cannot issue directions for future selection based on past lapses - Future selections will need to comply with appropriate laws and reservation policy.
No particular principle will be set for future judgments.
