Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25F talk about the termination of service and compensation in lieu of reinstatement and back wages.
The Respondent was initially employed on an adhoc basis for 89 days, and her services were used to be terminated upon completion of each term.
The Respondent completed 240 days of work during the 12-month period prior to termination of services, and Section 25-F's necessary conditions were not met before those services were terminated.
Consequently, Section 2(oo)(bb) of the ID Act does not apply.
Although qualified, the Respondent did not apply when the advertisement was issued for filling the said post, and her services were terminated for back in 1992.
Given the peculiar facts and circumstances present, instead of reinstatement with back wages, the Respondent is directed to be paid a lump sum amount of Rs. 25,000/-.
