Jammu and Kashmir Family Pension-cum-Gratuity Rules, 1964, Section 11(b) deals with Family pensions.
In this case, the petitioner was denied her rightful claim of pension on the grounds of delay in filing her claim.
However, the rules state that family pension cases of widows of government servants who died in harness before 1.4.1965 shall continue to be processed, and there is no cut-off date for claiming pension benefits under SRO.
Therefore, recurring causes like delay cannot be considered grounds for declining claims, and the petitioner is entitled to claim pension benefits for 36 months prior to the date of filing the writ petition.
