Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Retrenchment - A workman was under a service contract for three years, which included a clause for early termination after serving a notice of three months - In this case, termination occurred before the completion of three years after serving notice - Despite the Industrial Tribunal reinstating the workman with full benefits, a challenge was made by the workman after three years - The workman, having accepted the Tribunal's award and the amount awarded, cannot later challenge it after a gap of three years.
