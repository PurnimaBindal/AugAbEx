Termination of service after 16 years of service is allowed under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311, giving one month salary in lieu of notice.
Termination order without any stigmas and passed according to the rules is not illegal.
[Para 9]
Termination after 16 years of service entitles an employee to compensation, like an ex-gratia of Rs. 75,000, even with poor service records and adverse entries.
The age and future job prospects of the employee must also be considered.
