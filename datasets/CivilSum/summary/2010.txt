Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Eligibility - Date on which eligibility conditions must be fulfilled - Posts under recruitment based on rules framed under Article 309 must be filled in accordance with specified eligibility conditions - Delay in selection process will not affect candidates' right to be considered against advertised vacancies with eligibility conditions - Eligibility must be determined as per date in advertisement - Candidates becoming over age after that date due to selection delay still eligible for selection against relevant vacancies at time.
