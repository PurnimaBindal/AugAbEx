According to Sections 38 and 39 of Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, along with Sections 2(17) and 2(13), "Principal Employer" and "Immediate Employer" are defined.
Although a watchman is employed through a contractor, they do not work for or in connection with the establishment's work.
The appellant cannot be considered the employer and is therefore not obligated to insure or make contributions for the watchmen who are not employed by them.
