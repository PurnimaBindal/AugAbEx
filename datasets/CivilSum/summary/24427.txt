Sections 20(1) and 22(3) of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Rule 17(iii) of the Administrative Procedure Rules, 1987, and Order 47, Rule 1 of the Civil Procedure Code, 1908, outline the guidelines for review and rehearing based on an error apparent on the face of judgment.
An applicant seeking new material must provide proper explanation for how they obtained knowledge of its existence.
Without such information, no new material will be considered and the applicant will not qualify for a fresh hearing.
