Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Pay scale/Revised pay scale - The petitioners were promoted from Keyman to Pump Operator in the pay scale of 400-600 which was later revised to 950-1400.
However, the pay scale of 950-1400 was withdrawn without any notice or opportunity being given to the petitioners.
The lack of justification as to why the petitioners were not entitled to the revised pay scale is unacceptable, and any action adversely affecting the rights of any person cannot be taken without compliance with the principles of natural justice.
Therefore, the order of withdrawal of the revised pay scale is now set aside with liberty to take appropriate action in accordance to the law and principles of natural justice.
[Paras 10 and 11]
