Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Confirmation in service - Uncommunicated adverse remarks - Natural justice - For confirmation of promotion, uncommunicated remarks that the employee was not given the opportunity to address cannot be considered against him.
[Paras 10 & 13] B.
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Statement of official witnesses - Administrative orders and instructions - The statements of official witnesses, who are not the competent authority, cannot be used as the basis of claims as an administrative order or policy.
Such statements hold no value in this regard.
