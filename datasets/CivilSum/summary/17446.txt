Army Rules, 1954, Rule 16-A(5)(g) - Age of retirement - Extension in service - There is no difference in age limits for retirement between a Substantive Colonel and Acting Colonel.
Both ranks have the right to continue their service until age 57.
The appellant's claim for service extension was wrongfully rejected due to not holding a substantive Colonel rank.
This action was illegal, and the rejection order has been quashed.
The authorities must re-consider and grant all consequential reliefs.
