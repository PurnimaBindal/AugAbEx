The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 300-A, along with the Pension Regulations of Army 1961, Rule 173, stipulate the qualifications for Family Pension.
In this case, the husband of the petitioner did not complete the required 15 years of qualifying service and was discharged due to an invalidity caused by neurotic depressive reaction.
As the initial illness was not attributable to military service, the petitioner is not entitled to family pension.
