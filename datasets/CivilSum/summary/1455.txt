Employees Provident and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 7-A - Clubbing of two establishments - Firms running business of kerosene and LPG under same management and control - Common phone and accountant, and combined employee strength of 24 - Tribunal held them as separate units dealing in different commodities - However, to interpret provisions of Act in view of employee welfare, two firms were considered as one establishment, as evidence showed that respondent had fragmented business to deprive employees of benefits of labour laws - Thus, employees were rightly clubbed together for applying provisions of Act.
