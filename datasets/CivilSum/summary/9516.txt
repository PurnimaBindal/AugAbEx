The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, outlines the process of promotion and consideration for promotion.
An Army Officer, who was eligible for promotion and had received approval from the Selection Board, was deprived of his right to consideration for promotion and was not promoted until retirement.
The Army Headquarters' letter dated 24.3.87, which allowed for relaxation of any deficiencies, was not followed, and the petitioner was not given an opportunity.
The Court ruled that the authority's actions were in violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution, and they were directed to grant the petitioner the acting rank of Colonel with all consequential benefits.
