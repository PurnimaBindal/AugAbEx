Gujarat State Civil Services (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1971, Rules 5(2)(a), 5(5)(a) and 5(5)(c) - Deemed suspension - Continuation of - Petitioner suspended for over 48 hours due to custody in relation to a case regarding personal conduct - Any suspension order must be considered for revocation or modification at earliest opportunity - Continuing suspension requires consideration of nexus between alleged offense and employment misconduct - Ruling: petitioner's suspension is illegal, and respondents must issue an appropriate order for reinstatement.
