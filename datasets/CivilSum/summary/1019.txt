Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226/227 - Regional Rural Banks Act - National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development Rules - Guidelines - Posts of Area/Senior Managers can only be filled via 100% promotion from one source, and non-selection rule of seniority-cum-merit must be applied - Guidelines do not involve assessment of comparative merit - Seniority-cum-merit takes precedence over merit in the selection of promotional post unless there are adequate grounds to deny promotion - Petitioners should be eligible for consideration for the promotional post as of the date their juniors were promoted.
