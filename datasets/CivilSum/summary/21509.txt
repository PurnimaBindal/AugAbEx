Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion opportunities - The Pay Commission reports highlight the need for a scheme of Assured Career Promotion for employees.
States should include provisions for this in the conditions of service and modify rules if necessary.
[Para ]
Constitution of India, Articles 226 & 14 - Writ jurisdiction - Pay scale - Authorities cannot be compelled to provide judicial officer pay scale to law officers who are not in a cadre post.
