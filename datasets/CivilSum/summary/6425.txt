Constitution of India, Article 311 - UCO Bank (Officers) Service Regulations, Regulations 19 and 20 - Voluntary Retirement - Waiver of notice - Disciplinary proceedings - An appellant, a General manager of the UCO Bank, requested voluntary retirement from his service with a request to waive the three months' notice period.
However, the prayer was not allowed, and the petitioner moved the High Court by writ application.
During the pendency of the writ proceeding, the appellant was served with a charge-sheet alleging dereliction of duty and abuse of power.
The regulations permit the employee to retire or leave the service or resign from the Bank on giving three months' notice, unless such period of notice is either waived fully or partially by the competent authority.
The right to voluntarily retire or leave the services of the bank or resign cannot be said to have accrued before 17th March 1992, since there was no waiver or reduction of the period of notice.
It was held further that since the articles of charge had been served before the expiry of the notice period of three months, a disciplinary proceeding could be said to be pending on the date the voluntary retirement was prayed to be effective.
Therefore, the appellant became liable to the disentitlement as envisaged in sub-regulation (2) of Regulation 20 and could not lawfully claim relief in terms of Regulation 19.
