Customs Act, 1962, Sections 2(22), 2(23) & 156 - Customs Valuation (Determination of price of Imported Goods) Rules, 1988, Rules 3, 4 and 9 - Payment of royalty and know-how fees - Import of capital goods - Determination of the method of valuation - It is held that: (i) The transactional value must be linked to the import of goods for which the amounts must be payable as a condition of import.
(ii) There is a clear distinction between an amount payable as a condition of import and an amount payable regarding manufacturing activities, which may not be connected with the import of the capital goods.
(iii) The decision in Essar Gujarat Limited (supra) cannot be used to support the proposition that regardless of the contract, licence fee, and charges paid for technical know-how, which may not have anything to do with the pre-importation stage, must be considered when computing the transaction value in terms of Rule 9(1)(c) of the Rules.
