Under Section 9 of Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1980, a scheme must be formulated by the Central Government in consultation with Reserve Bank of India to determine the placement of employees of the Transferor Bank in the Transferee Bank - this is known as the New Bank of India (Amalgamation and Transfer of Undertakings) Scheme, 1993.
After the scheme comes into effect, the undertakings of New Bank of India are transferred to Punjab National Bank.
The Central Government also formulated a scheme for the placement of employees of New Bank of India in Punjab National Bank, and Punjab National Bank developed its own guidelines for transfer of these employees, which must be followed in conformity with the scheme framed by the Central Government.
