According to Section 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act, the willful disobedience of court orders is forbidden.
The authority in question was directed to investigate if a permanent vacancy was available for the petitioner and to pay him full wages if one was found.
However, despite allowing the petitioner to withdraw charge allowance for the period during which he held the post and attempted to justify their actions under Rule 87-B of the J and K CSR, they denied the petitioner's claim.
This disregard for the court's directions qualifies as wilful disobedience and subjects the respondent to potential punishment under the Contempt of Courts Act.
The respondent is given an additional chance to conform to the court's instructions.
