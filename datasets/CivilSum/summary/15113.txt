The Constitution of India, Articles 16, 300-A and 226, along with the Person with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act of 1995, Section 47, pertain to the case of a petitioner driver from Haryana Roadway who was unable to continue driving duties due to a disability resulting from a 1999 on-duty accident.
The petitioner requested to be placed in another position with the same pay scale under Section 47 of the 1995 Act, however, a notification from June 27, 2005 exempting drivers and conductors from the operations of Section 47 was issued.
The petitioner relied upon the notification for retirement from service but was ordered to be reinstated on any position with a time scale due to the notification not being able to have retrospective effect.
