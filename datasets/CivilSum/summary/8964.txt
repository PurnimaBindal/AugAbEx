Constitution of India, Article 226 - Concealment of facts - Petitioner cannot receive relief under Art. 226 if guilty of concealing material facts - Clean hands are necessary when approaching the court.
[Paras 7, 8, 9, 10] B.
Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 7-A - Petitioner given clear notice to appear on a specified date - Despite repeated notices, failed to comply with the direction of the competent authority - Authority under the Act passed an order under section 7-A(1) on the basis of reports submitted by the inspectors - Compliance occurred with the provisions of the Act and principles of natural justice - No opportunity was given before the impugned order.
[Paras 13-16] C. Constitution of India, Article 226 - Concealment of facts - Although the petitioner concealed material facts, justice must not only be done but must appear to be done - The court must proceed to decide the list on merits.
[Para 11] D. Constitution of India, Article 226 - Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 7-A - Established that employees of the three establishments are the employees of petitioner establishment because they are established in the same premises and engaged in the same production - Finding of fact cannot be interfered in writ jurisdiction.
[Paras 21, 22] E. Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 7-A - Principle establishment is divided into three units - Finding that all the three are one unit for the purpose of application of provisions of the Act - New unit not entitled to fresh infancy benefit as establishment started deductions and payment of Provident Fund after three years.
