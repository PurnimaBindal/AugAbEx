The University's appointment of part-time teachers via a period basis, due to a shortage of regular teachers, is a novel method to get cheaper rates of service.
However, this method exploits the part-time teachers who perform similarly to regularly appointed teachers.
The job of a teacher carries status in society and cannot be equated with manual labor.
Article 39(d) has been raised to fundamental right status as violating the directive principles of State Policy violates Articles 14 and 16.
Therefore, these part-time teachers are entitled to a salary equivalent to the minimum of the pay scale of regularly appointed teachers of the University.
