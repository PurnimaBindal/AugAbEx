Coal Mine Nationalisation Act, 1973, Section 14 - Resumption of duty - The Central Government took over non-cooking coal mines on 31.1.73 and nationalised it on 1.5.73 - The learned Tribunal found that the employees in question continued to be employees, but were not allowed to work - The finding based on evidence cannot be disputed - Therefore, the amended provision of the Act that came into force on 15.12.86 will not apply to the right enforced by the employees - The Tribunal was right in allowing the workers to resume their duty from the date of takeover.
