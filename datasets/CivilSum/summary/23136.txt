According to Article 309 of the Constitution of India and Rule 244(2) of the Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, the mandatory retirement age can be amended by repealing and substituting provisions of the Rules by way of amendment.
The repeal of a rule takes effect immediately, but does not affect the operation of the law prior to the date of repeal.
The amendment of Rule 344(2) of the Rajasthan Service Rules is valid and does not apply retrospectively, but could have been made with retrospective effect under Art. 309 of the Constitution.
The repeal of the previous provisions of the statute and substitution with amended provisions are separate stages, and notification repealing the previous provisions cannot be deemed invalid.
