Constitution of India, Article 311 (After Forty-second Amendment) - Dismissal - The Tribunal set aside the order of dismissal as the respondent wasn't provided a copy of the Enquiry Officer's report.
The Tribunal relied on a decision of the Apex Court in Union of India and others v. Mohd Ramzan Khan, 1991(1) SCT 111.
It is uncertain whether the ratio of Mohd.
Ramzan Khan's case shall apply prospectively or not.
It shall also not apply in cases where the order of dismissal was passed before the decision referred to Constitution Bench be read along with CA No. 3056 of 1991 arising out of SLP No. 12103 of 1991 entitled Managing Director, Electronic Corporation of India v. B Karunakar in which a reference has already been made to Constitution Bench.
Since the gravity of the charge against the respondent was not set aside on merits, the Court did not deem it appropriate in the interest of justice to give any direction for the reinstatement of respondent in service or award of back wages as done in B Karunakar's case.
Accordingly, the respondent would be governed by an appropriate direction to be given at the time of the final decision of the case.
The operation of the impugned judgment of the Tribunal shall remain stayed concerning the respondent.
