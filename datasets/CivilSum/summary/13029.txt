Constitution of India, Article 14 - Natural justice - Reply to show- cause notice - Passing an adverse order without considering the reply given in response to show-cause notice violates principles of natural justice and cannot be upheld.
[Paras 10 & 11]
Constitution of India, Articles 226 & 14 - Res judicata - Withdrawing an earlier writ petition against one order - Permission granted to challenge subsequent final order also - Withdrawing the earlier petition will not prevent the petitioner from challenging either of the orders in a subsequent writ petition.
[Para 8]
