Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(b) - Enquiry - Dispensing with enquiry - Enquiry not reasonably practicable - A.S.I.
Police - To invoke the provisions of Article 311(2)(b) of the Constitution to dispense with the enquiry, the authority ordering dismissal must possess evidence existing at the time of the dismissal and must express satisfaction - Since the Respondents failed to produce any such evidence in court, the order of dismissal was invalidated with the option to proceed according to the law - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 162.
