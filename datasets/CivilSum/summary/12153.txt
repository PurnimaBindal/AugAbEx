Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - In case of pending criminal proceedings, employees are not allowed to take up a promoted post immediately after relieving.
However, if a criminal case is registered after the promotion order is passed, promotion cannot be withheld on the basis of the pending proceedings.
Keeping the promotion order in abeyance on account of criminal proceedings is considered as punishment and is not allowed under the Army Act.
The power of the executive cannot be used arbitrarily and must be backed by some rule of law.
The order of promotion not only grants a higher rank but also entitled the petitioner to higher salary and emoluments and they cannot be deprived of these benefits without following the proper procedure established by law.
