Clause X from the Letters Patent Appeal, Para 122 - Promotion Guidelines - In order to be eligible for promotion within Group A (Class I), an officer must have served for at least five years in Grade I/II and be permitted to participate in the Selection Board interview.
The zone of consideration for filling vacancies by promotion would be three times the number of available vacancies.
However, putting in five years of service only allows an officer to be eligible for consideration for the next grade, and not within the zone of consideration in the seniority list.
The appeal was dismissed.
