Constitution of India - Articles 14, 16, 21 and 311 - Double jeopardy - Departmental enquiry - De novo enquiry - Delinquent officer engaged in disciplinary proceedings initiated by the State Electricity Board - Findings of Enquiry Officer favoured the delinquent officer and were against the Board - No flaws detected in the enquiry - The management may disagree with the Enquiry Officer's findings, but it cannot continue conducting multiple enquiries till the employee is proven guilty - Such action amounts to harassment even if the principle of double jeopardy is not applicable.
