Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1964, Rule 3(1)(i) & (ii) - Kisan Vikas Patra Rules, 1988, Rules 7 & 8 - Disciplinary Proceedings - Delinquent officer failed to provide KVP after accepting Rs. 5,000 - Resulting in compulsory retirement.
[Para 24]
Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Imposition of punishment - Even the appellate authority can impose a punishment subject to a given condition that the officer is not deprived of the right to appeal.
An authority higher than the appointing authority may also act as disciplinary authority.
[Para 15]
Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Procedural requirements - The concerned officer must be given three opportunities: to deny guilt and establish innocence, to cross-examine witnesses produced against them and examine themselves or other witnesses on their behalf, and to show cause why the proposed punishment would not be proper.
[Para 22]
Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Natural Justice - Principles of natural justice are not embodied.
Non-observation of any said principles resulting in denial of justice may lead the courts to interfere.
Substantial compliance of procedure may lead the court not to interfere.
