Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Appointment to a post by test - In a case where both the petitioner and respondent secured equal marks (92) in the written test, but the petitioner was MA and senior in age while the respondent was BA and junior in age, the ranking list was prepared on the basis of roll numbers, resulting in the petitioner being placed below the respondent and ultimately not being appointed.
However, the petitioner was senior in age and possessed higher qualifications.
The court held that the method of appointment was arbitrary and violated Article 14, and directed the authorities to appoint the petitioner against a vacant post.
