Raman, Advocate.
A.
According to Section 5 of the Kerala Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1960, State Govt. can exempt any establishment from the Act if it is in the public interest; this power is a form of conditional legislation and not subject to rules of natural justice.
[Para 3] B.
Under Article 14 of the Constitution of India, the State Govt. has taken into account the rights of employees in all Scheduled Commercial Banks while issuing the notification, since such employees can be treated as a separate class and are exempted from the Act accordingly; this action was not influenced by any malicious intent and was taken in the public interest.
