Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Disciplinary proceedings - Charge-sheet - Delay and laches - Effect of - After alleged lapses, charge-sheet issued after 10 years - Respondents allege prejudice due to non-availability of record and loss of memory - However, report of preliminary enquiry submitted after 9 years of incident - Petitioner called upon to inspect all relevant records - Delay alone cannot justify non-holding of departmental enquiry unless it is found prejudicial to interest of delinquent, depriving him of fair trial - Petition dismissed.
