Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, Section 45 - Industrial Tribunal's authority - A bus conductor who misappropriated funds cannot be treated leniently - Imposing a penalty of stopping three increments is not disproportionate to the misconduct - The punishment should deter the employee and others in similar situations - The Industrial Tribunal should not interfere with the punishment - Reducing the penalty is not reasonable.
