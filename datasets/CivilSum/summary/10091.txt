Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I Part I, Rule 2.5 - Date of Birth - Correction of date of birth in service record - Time limitation of two years for application from the date of entry into service - Exception made if corrected certificate received much later - Eligible applicants cannot be refused correction if there was no fraud or misrepresentation and correction was requested after two years - Civil Court jurisdiction cannot be barred by administrative remedy.
