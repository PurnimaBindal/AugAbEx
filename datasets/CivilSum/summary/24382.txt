The Constitution of India (Articles 14 and 16) and the Indian Medical Council Act (Sections 25 and 33) specify the cut off date for admission, and the session during which the internship must be completed.
The court has issued directives regarding the admission process for MBBS doctors seeking Post Graduate Studies, including the waiving of interviews for candidates with strong merits.
The Court views the UP Government's disobedience towards its directives as serious.
Medical colleges throughout India have been directed to strictly adhere to the provisions of the Act with regard to admissions.
