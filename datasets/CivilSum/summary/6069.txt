According to the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 14(2), Central Administrative Tribunal (CAT) does not have jurisdiction over Bharat Sanchar Nigam Ltd (BSNL) employees unless there is a proper notification issued by the Central Govt.
Even if the applicants had taken the departmental examination prior to the formation of BSNL, CAT cannot entertain an application or issue directions to BSNL since it was not a party to the case.
