The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25F, deals with the termination of service.
In a case where a labour court believed oral testimony that a workman had worked for more than one year, despite a document produced by the management indicating otherwise, it was held that the workman was not in service for more than 24 years.
Therefore, instead of granting reinstatement, in such circumstances, compensations can be provided to balance equities.
