The main objective of the society is to distribute and supply electrical energy to individuals in its area of operation.
The Andhra Pradesh State Electricity Board controls the Society's day-to-day functioning, making it an agency or instrumentality of the State, and it is under the Administrative and supervisory control of the Government and the State Electricity Board.
As a result, the Respondent is subject to the jurisdiction of the High Court under Article 226.
