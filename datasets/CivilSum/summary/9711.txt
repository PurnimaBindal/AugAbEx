As per the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb), 25(F), the workman's termination was not considered to be retrenchment in accordance with their terms and conditions of appointment.
Although the contention was made that the workman was entitled to reinstatement with back wages, Section 25F only applies in cases of retrenchment.
Therefore, the workman's claim was deemed invalid.
Additionally, given that compensation had already been paid and not challenged by management, it is just and proper not to recover any funds.
