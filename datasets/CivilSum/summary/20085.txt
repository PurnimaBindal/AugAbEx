As per Articles 16 and 136 of the Constitution of India, defence employees working in Border areas were entitled to both Special Duty Allowance and Special Compensatory (Remote Locality) Allowance until 17111995.
After 1741985 they are entitled to only Special Duty Allowance.
However, defence civilians deployed at the border for operational requirements have to be doubly compensated due to the likelihood of imminent hostilities in hilly areas.
However, persons in barracks are not entitled to double payment.
