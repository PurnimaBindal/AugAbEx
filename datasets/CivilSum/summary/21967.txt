Constitution of India, Article 16 - Causal labourer - Scheme for temporary status/regularization - Benefit of - The scheme, which dates back to 1993, dictates that casual labourers who were in employment on the date of its commencement will be granted temporary status as per clause 4.
Therefore, to reap the benefits of the scheme, the causal labourer must have been employed as of the scheme's inception and served the required duration.
