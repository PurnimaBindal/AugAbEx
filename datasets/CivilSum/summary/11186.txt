Food Corporation of India Act, 1964, Section 45 - Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 60 - Disciplinary proceedings - Punishment - Sustainability - The punishment cannot be sustained since the explanation sought within three days was not received or considered while giving punishment.
The employee was not given a reasonable opportunity to represent against the proposed punishment as per Regulation 60(1).
