Army Act, 1950, Section 41(1) - Misconduct - Reduction in Rank - Petitioner, a Havaldar, allegedly disobeyed a superior officer's order to not proceed on any operation - The Havaldar intended to go to an area infested with terrorists, and his reply to the superior officer should not have constituted disobedience of orders for a disciplined soldier - A reduction in rank as a penalty was disproportionate to the alleged misconduct committed - Penalty must be quashed.
[Paras 8, 14] B Petitioner responded to a superior officer's order to not proceed on any operation by expressing his wish to go to the area infested with terrorists - He did not intend to sit idle and was over-enthusiastic about his duties - Despite his explanation, a reduction in rank was imposed upon him as a penalty, which was disproportionate to his alleged misconduct - Chief of Army Staff's failure to consider all grounds stated in appeal was deemed unjustified - Appellate order held liable to be quashed and set aside.
[Paras 7,8,14] C. If the punishment imposed on an employee is excessively harsh, it can be challenged in court - Penalties are subject to judicial scrutiny in such cases.
