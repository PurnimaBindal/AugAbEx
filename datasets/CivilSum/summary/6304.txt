The plaintiff is claiming promotion with effect from 28.8.92, and arguing that he did not know about the orders on 31590 and 28.8.92 when his juniors were promoted.
Furthermore, his representation was rejected on 20593/9693 and a legal notice was served on the State on 16.12.93.
The court held that the suit is not time-barred.
