The Constitution of India, through Articles 16 and 226, regulates seniority and promotions.
Petitioners, serving as Social Education and Panchayat Officers on an ad hoc basis, are affected by the recruitment quota set by the Government, which requires a 50% direct recruitment and 50% promotion from the position of Gram Sewaks (now Gram Sachivs).
With the fear of reversion pending, the petitioners have secured a stay of the reversion.
However, since the promotees including petitioners have already been listed as more senior than the direct recruits, the writ petitions have no further cause of action and are considered rendered infructuous.
