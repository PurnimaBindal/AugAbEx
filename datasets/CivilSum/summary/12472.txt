Border Security Force Act, 1968, Sections 11 and 62(2) - Border Security Force Rules, Rule 177 - Deserter - Absence from duty - Rule 177 permits termination, dismissal or removal of an individual who is declared a deserter after being absent from duty without proper authorization for 30 or more days; respondent was declared a deserter and received a termination order as a result - Any issues with the conduct of inquiry do not affect the conclusion that the respondent was absent without leave for two years - Explanation for the lengthy absence was deemed unbelievable and the termination order was considered fair and valid.
