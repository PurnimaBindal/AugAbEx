Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 309 - Tripura Civil Services Conduct Rules, 1988, Rule 3 - Punishment - Stoppage of increment - The employee was punished with the stoppage of three increments with cumulative effect due to absence from duty and misbehavior with superiors - Findings of guilt were based on evidence presented by prosecution and defense witnesses as well as available documents on record - The disciplinary action taken against the employee was deemed justified and the punishment does not require interference from the High Court - Therefore, the petition was dismissed.
