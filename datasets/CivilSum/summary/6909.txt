According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, Chauffeurs are classified as Group 'C', but their claim for the same benefits as Group C employees/LDC's is not justified.
LDC's and Chauffeurs belong to the same grade, but the nature of duties, qualifications, responsibility, and effectiveness with which duties can be discharged differ.
The classification is based on a rationale basis with a nexus to the objective sought to be achieved, and therefore, the demand for the same benefits on the grounds of equality is not valid.
This ruling is stated in paras 18 and 19.
