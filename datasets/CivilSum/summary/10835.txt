All India Council for Technical Education Act, 1987, Section 10(1) - Contractual employees - Termination of services - Guidelines laid down by the council are not mandatory - They are only in the nature of guidelines to be followed by various technical institutions.
- Norms laid down by the council are not legally enforceable, especially at the behest of the contractual employees.
- If the services of contractual employees are terminated in accordance with the terms of their contract or due to the arrival of regular appointees, they cannot raise any grievance.
