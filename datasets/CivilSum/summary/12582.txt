Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment on Ad-hoc basis - An employee does not have a guaranteed right to be considered for appointment or given the appointment in the future should a similar vacancy arise.
This is supported by AIR 1979 SC 149.
Jagdish Singh v. State of Haryana (DB), decided on 541990, was overruled and 1992(3)SCT 201 (SC) is relied upon.
