According to Punjab State Electricity Board Employees (Punishment and Appeal) Regulation, 1971, Regulation 14(ii), if an employee leaves the office without leave, and it is not reasonably practicable to conduct an inquiry, the authority can choose to dispense with the holding of the inquiry and terminate the employee's services.
In this case, the employee did not respond to the notice calling them back to work and notices were published in local newspapers.
As a result, the plaintiff's services were terminated invoking Regulation 14(ii), and the action of the punishing authority was held to be justified since the plaintiff did not return to duty until the time of the suit.
