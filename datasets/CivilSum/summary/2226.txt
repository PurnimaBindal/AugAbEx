According to Section 6-N of the U.P.
Industrial Disputes Act (28 of 1947), retrenchment is not legal after a company has been taken over by the State Electricity Board.
In this case, the employees are entitled to be reinstated and the Board must consider their absorption without any wage liability.
[Paras 12 to 14] The Bihar Colliery Kamgar Union and Bharat Coking Coal Ltd, AIR 1978 SC 979 : (1978) 37 FLR 144, represented the concerned workmen.
