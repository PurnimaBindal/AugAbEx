The competent authority may terminate the services of an ad hoc/temporary employee and replace them with another person at any time, as there is no bar to doing so in terms of the employee's appointment order.
The Full Bench in SK Verma's case overruled the Division Bench in Krishna Devi's case in relation to ad hoc appointment and termination.
[Para 8] The Supreme Court in State of Punjab v. Surinder Kumar etc. found no reason why the specific terms on which appointments were made could not be enforced.
[Para 10] In State of Haryana v. Piara Singh, it was observed that an ad hoc/temporary employee should not be replaced and instead should wait for a regularly selected employee, but this was a case-specific observation.
[Para 13] In Kaushal Kishore Shukla's case, the apex Court held that service of a temporary employee can be dispensed with in terms of appointment.
[Para 14]

If an ad hoc/temporary employee is terminated due to the budget not being sanctioned, later sanctioning of the budget for a particular date does not mean that the employee should be recalled.
