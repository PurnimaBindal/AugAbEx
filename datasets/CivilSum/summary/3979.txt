Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(j), 2(s) and 10 - Industry - Workman - The objective of Jawahar Rozgar Yojna was to provide employment to those living below poverty line rather than giving regular employment, thus persons employed in the scheme cannot be termed as 'workmen' under Industrial Disputes Act - The reference made by Appropriate Government is inconsequential, and any award made by Labour Court should be set aside.
[Paras 14 and 17]  B Constitution of India, Articles 141 and 226 - Under Article 141 of the Constitution, law laid down by Supreme Court is binding on the High Court - It is permissible to agitate a legal question for the first time in writ jurisdiction, although it may not have been taken before the Labour Court.
