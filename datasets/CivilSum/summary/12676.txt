Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - State Bank of Patiala (Employees) Pension Regulations, 1995, Regulations 3(b), 34(1) & (2) and 40(1)(c) - Family pension - Depriving illiterate widow and minor children of a deceased employee their right to family pension due to failure to submit option in time is unacceptable - The competent authority is required to inform them of their right for family pension and grant it.
