Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rules 3 and 4 - Premature retirement - Stigmatic order - Complaints - Service Record - The petitioner had no adverse entries in their 30 years of service record.
However, they were retired prematurely based on a complaint while working as ASI, which alleged misconduct.
No investigation was held to determine the truth of these allegations and the petitioner was not given an opportunity to defend themselves.
The impugned order did not consider the petitioner's service record but instead relied on claims of misconduct.
This amounts to casting a stigma and violates the principles of natural justice as well as Article 311 of the Constitution.
