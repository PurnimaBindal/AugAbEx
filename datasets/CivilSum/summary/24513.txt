Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Termination -Natural justice - Even if the employer did not conduct an inquiry or if the inquiry was deemed to be flawed, the employer must be given an opportunity to present evidence to the satisfaction of the Tribunal to demonstrate the legality and validity of their actions - It is permissible for the employer to introduce evidence to justify their actions for the first time.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Dismissal from service - If serious charges of misconduct are proven against the employee to the satisfaction of the Tribunal, the High Court is not authorized to reverse the finding of fact in writ jurisdiction and mandate reinstatement with back wages - In such circumstances, the writ court cannot evaluate the severity of the penalty.
