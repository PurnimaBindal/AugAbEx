Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Senior scale - Discrimination - Petitioners argue that after 8 years of service, they deserve the same senior scale as two other lecturers in similar situations - State claims that no final decision has been made on the matter, but without explanation as to why petitioners aren't being given the same benefit - Denial of senior scale to the petitioners is groundless and unacceptable discrimination - The respondents are ordered to grant the petitioners senior scale from the date at which they became eligible, counting service before the respondent-college received aid.
