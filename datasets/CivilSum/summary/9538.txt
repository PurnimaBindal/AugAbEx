According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I Part I, Rule 3.26 and Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 121, 139 and 1310, a petitioner who was confirmed after being promoted as Sub-Inspector in Punjab Police cannot be given a notice of compulsory retirement as per the Rules that empower Deputy Inspector General of Police to make promotions and order such retirements.
The order of compulsory retirement made by SP has been quashed as it cannot be sustained under the law.
The contention that petitioner was promoted by SP and therefore rightly retired has been rejected as Police Rules must be given pre-eminence due to their statutory force.
