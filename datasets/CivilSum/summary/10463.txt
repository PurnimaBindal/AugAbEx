Constitution of India, Article 16 - Discrimination - Promotion - Post of Assistant Engineer - Selection based on merits - In 1980, the petitioner was found unsuitable for promotion by the Screening Committee - That year, a junior officer to the petitioner was promoted as their performance was better - The petitioner was subsequently promoted to the post in 1986 - The petitioner cannot assert that the promotion should be from 1980 solely because they were senior - There is no equality between the petitioner and the respondent - There is no discrimination - When a selection for promotion is based on merits and seniority is taken into consideration, the promotion cannot be claimed as a matter of right.
