Guru Nanak Dev University Calendar, Vol. II, Part-A, 1991, Page 40, Ordinance 7(d) as amended on 30.6.1995 - Examination - Mid-session change in examination style - To assess acquired knowledge, a change in examination style during the semester, and in the absence of a model test paper, is not necessarily detrimental to students, even if it occurs in a short interval prior to the exam.
[Paras 7, 8, and 11] B. Examination/Result - Marks of questions - Failure to provide a detailed breakdown of marks in a question paper or to give a breakup of marks to answered questions, when students are given discretion to choose which questions to answer, may be disadvantageous to students, but the discrepancy is not extensive enough to disrupt ruled matters.
[Para 12] C. Examination/Question papers - Mistakes in the question papers - A large number of errors in question papers can mislead and unfairly disadvantage students, causing a shift in the meaning and sense of the questions being asked by the students - The University is expected to address these errors in a decent and thorough manner, rather than seeing them as minor errors - The effect of such errors can be unjust, so instead of providing grace marks, responsible action must be taken without tolerance - Those who are responsible must be held accountable.
