The petitioner's claim for regularisation under Section 31(c) was rejected due to lacking the minimum prescribed qualifications, but he had been appointed and paid without any complaints regarding his work and conduct until his termination.
There is a provision for relaxation of qualifications, which the Regularisation Committee seemed to be unaware of, rendering their decision illegal.
Therefore, the termination order has been quashed, and the petitioner can continue his job as a regularised lecturer and receive all associated benefits from the date of his appointment.
