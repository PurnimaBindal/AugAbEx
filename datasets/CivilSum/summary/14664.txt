Homoeopathy Central Council Act, 1973, Section 33 - Homoeopathy (Diploma Course) Regulations, 1983, Regulation 11(vii) - Rules for Compartment/Special chance - The fifth special chance is only available to those who have taken the Third DHMS Examination and have one subject left to pass, and have used their four permissible chances - Those who have failed to pass in four attempts in the Second DHMS Examination and have compartment cannot avail of a special chance - The petitioner's situation is not covered by the Regulation 11(vii) proviso, so the writ petition must fail - However, the petitioner has already passed the required compartment and was allowed to take the exam under court direction, and in the interest of justice and fairness, the authorities are directed to release his second DHMS Examination result, without it being considered a precedent.
