According to the Punjab Agricultural Produce (Development and Warehousing Corporation) Act, 1956 - Punjab Warehouse Rules, 1958, Rule 28 - Punjab State Warehousing Corporation Staff Regulations, 1960, Regulation 17, the Corporation must allow for a storage gain of 1% in weight or bulk due to moisture absorption during the July to October monsoon months.
The Corporation can only discipline its employees if there is a violation of this legal obligation, and not based on its own inconsistent administrative instructions or policy decisions.
Pursuing disciplinary action against employees for failing to provide the storage gain of 1 Kg.
per quintal of wheat stored is legally invalid.
