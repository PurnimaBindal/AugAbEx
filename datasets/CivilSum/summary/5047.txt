Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F - Termination - Notice - Termination without notice to the workman is illegal.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F - Retrenchment - Delay and laches - If the Labour Court concludes that the relief cannot be denied due to delay, the writ Court will not interfere.
[Paras 15 & 17]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F - Back wages - Discretion of the Labour Court to grant back wages - Extraordinary writ jurisdiction will only be exercised in cases of grave injustice, not the discretion of the Labour Court.
