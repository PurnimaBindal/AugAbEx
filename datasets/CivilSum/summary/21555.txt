Maharashtra Raw Cotton (Procurement, Processing & Marketing) Act, 1971, Section 2(j) - Cotton Season - Definition of "Cotton Season" - Employment Nature - The reference made in Section 2(j) to "Cotton Season" does not imply a reference to employment nature, but narrowly refers to the time period of the season.
The definition of "Cotton Season" was taken into account by the Supreme Court in a case regarding Maharashtra State Co-operative Cotton Growers Marketing Federation v. Employees Union etc. and the review application in the same case.
