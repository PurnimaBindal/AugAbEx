Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-E - Back wages - Interest - The Labour Court awarded full back wages to a respondent and found that the company had indirectly benefited from withholding the amount payable to the employee and his legal heirs after his death.
This deprived the petitioners of their property right without any authority of law, resulting in the company being bound to pay interest on back wages at a rate of 15% per annum from the date of the award to the date of payment.
B.
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226/227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Provident Fund - The Labour Court found the termination of an employee to be unlawful, which prevented the employee from contributing to the provident fund.
Once treated as an employee, the employer was required to pay its own contribution towards the provident fund.
The respondent-company was directed to deduct the amount from the arrears of salary payable to the employee and then to pay the amount to the petitioners by adding its contribution.
The company was not absolved of its responsibility to make its own contribution towards the provident fund even if it failed to deduct the share of the provident fund from the salary payable to the deceased employee.
