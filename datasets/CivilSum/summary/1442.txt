Regional Rural Banks Act, 1976, Section 30 - Departmental proceedings - Compulsory retirement order - Allegations of abusing and misusing position in dealing with public money, taking undue advantages, acting against bank's interests, and granting loans irregularly - Petitioner provided full opportunity to defend himself in proceedings leading to final order - Verdict: Petitioner's actions were reckless and constituted misconduct for breaching rules and regulations imposed on him as a custodian trustee of public money - No justification for interference due to absence of irregularity, illegality or perversity in the impugned order.
