Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Workman employed as compositor - Workman absented - Management terminated his services - Industrial dispute raised - Labour Court concluded that termination was invalid but awarded the workman compensation of Rs. 1500/- - The petitioner/workman argued that reinstatement with back wages should have followed the finding of invalid termination - The petitioner had been employed elsewhere for 15 years and it would not be fair to order reinstatement with back wages - The compensation was increased from Rs. 1500/- to Rs. 5000/- and the petition was dismissed.
