Industrial Dispute Act, Section 10 - Back wages - Reinstatement - The Labour Court limited back wages to 10% when ordering reinstatement without stating any reasons - If the court orders reinstatement due to unjust termination, the entitled back wages should be awarded in full, unless a valid reason is given - The award was changed to grant full back wages.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pleadings - Procedure and pleadings - The Labour Court ordered reinstatement with only 10% back wages, but the worker appealed for full back wages - The management did not challenge the award separately, and its pleadings about the order of reinstatement are irrelevant - There was no response to the claim for full back wages - The worker is entitled to full back wages.
