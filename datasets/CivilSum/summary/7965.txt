Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation of services - Temporary, contractual and project services - Regularisation is only possible when there are permanent and regular posts available - There must be evidence that the work is of permanent and regular nature - Appointments to temporary, contractual, or project posts have a set expiration date - Regularisation cannot occur unless there is a permanent, cadre post available for appointment.
[Paras 10 and 15]
