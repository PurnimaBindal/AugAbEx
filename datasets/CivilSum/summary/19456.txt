Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 3(1) - Works Committee - Works Committee may have equal or more representatives of workmen than employer - Representatives of workmen must not be less than representatives of employer.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules, 1957, Rules 38 to 43 - Works Committee - Nomination of representatives of workmen is not allowed - Rule 40 only allows nomination of representatives of employer - Representatives of workmen must always be elected.
