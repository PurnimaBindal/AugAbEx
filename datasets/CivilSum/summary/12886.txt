Haryana Compassionate Appointment Rules, as amended on 1042004, were made effective retrospectively from 432003; compassionate appointment is unavailable to dependents of those who die after the age of 55 during service as they are only entitled to financial assistance; the authority is directed to pay the prescribed amount under the amended rules.
[Paras 6 to 8] B. Compassionate appointment cannot be claimed as a matter of right, as the consideration for such appointment is not a vested right; its object is only to offer financial assistance to families in crisis.
