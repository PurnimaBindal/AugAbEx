Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) (bb) and 25-F - Retrenchment Provisions of Section 2(oo) (bb) must be read in conjunction with Section 25-F - After the workman has served for 240 days in 12 months, the employer can  terminate his services with Section 25F(ii) applying- Workman will be entitled to retrenchment compensation.
[Para 7]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F and 2 (bb)(oo) - Unfair labour practices/Retrenchment - The employer must not act in a way that causes complete injustice to the workman or deviates from the retrenchment provisions- Workman must receive notice or compensation in accordance with the law if continued after 240 days.
[Para 8]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Clauses 5(b) of Fifth Schedule - Unfair labour practices - Taking repeated breaks from work and re-employing thereafter amounts to unfair labour practices in clause 5(b) and (c) of the Fifth Schedule.
[Para 10]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Retrenchment - The employer can retrench workman - However, Section 25F must be followed- Discharging or dismissing workman with a view to do away with the law's mandate would be in bad faith - Replacing and rotating workmen when work is available is unfair.
[Para 101]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F - Back wages - When the retrenchment/termination is on account of unfair labour practices, the normal rule is to allow back wages.
