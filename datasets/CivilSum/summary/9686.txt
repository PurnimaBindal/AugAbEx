Constitution of India, Article 226 - Challenge to issuance of charge-sheet - Delay and laches - Financial irregularities charged - Delay explained in additional affidavit - Generally, charge-sheets should not be quashed due to delay when charges concern financial irregularities or serious misconduct since obtaining evidence and discovering misconduct often takes many years - As the accusations against the Petitioner are severe, there is no reason to dismiss the charge-sheet.
