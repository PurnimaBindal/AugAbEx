According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, the withdrawal of regularisation is not justified.
Petitioners who have been working on daily wages against Class III posts for more than 5 years are entitled to be regularised against Class III posts that are available.
The withdrawal of regularisation without any allegation that the posts were not available in Class III and without proper justification is illegal.
[Paras 5 & 6]
