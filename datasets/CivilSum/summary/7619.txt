According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India and the General Clauses Act, 1897, suspension of a public servant can be done by the appointing authority during an ongoing departmental enquiry or criminal case.
It is within the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent authority to suspend an employee, and even a lengthy criminal trial does not warrant interference from the courts.
The decision of the competent authority should be supported by evidence gathered during the investigation/inquiry, and where serious charges require prosecution sanction, interference with the suspension order should generally be avoided.
