In interpreting Rule 4-A of the Maharashtra Sales Tax Officers Class-I (Recruitment) (Amendment) Rules, 1987, the rule must be applied retrospectively and the word "may" must be understood to mean "shall," allowing the government to relax quota rules in favor of promotees if exigencies of service require it.
However, the period during which private respondents held the posts of Sales Tax Officers Class-I on a fortuitous basis cannot be included when determining their seniority in the cadre.
As a result, all seniority lists are unsustainable and must be quashed, with fresh seniority lists of promotees and nominees prepared on the basis of date of appointment, excluding the period of fortuitous service.
