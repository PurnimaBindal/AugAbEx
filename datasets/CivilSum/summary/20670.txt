Termination - Hindi Teacher - Appellant challenged termination order after being told that her Teacher's training from UP Secondary Education Board was not recognized by Haryana - Appointed in 1980 and confirmed in 1984, with services terminated in 1990 - Govt. letter clarified that ban on recruitment from non-recognized institutions did not apply to those working as teachers on stop-gap/ad hoc/six months' basis before summer vacation of 1981 - Appellant had earned regularity in her service and the Education Department and School were wrong to have deprived her of her job - Impugned order quashed.
