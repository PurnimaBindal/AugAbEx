The 42nd Amendment of the Constitution of India, Article 311(2), has not changed the supply of a copy of the Enquiry Report and recommendations to the delinquent as violating the rules of natural justice, which allow the delinquent to represent against the report's conclusions.
Any non-supply of the report would make the order libel to challenge later.
[Paras 15 and 18]

The requirement to supply the inquiry report arises only when there is an Inquiry Officer and a report.
There is no report if the disciplinary authority holds the inquiry.
Therefore, Article 14 of the Constitution of India does not apply, and there is a reasonable classification in this case.
[Para 16]
