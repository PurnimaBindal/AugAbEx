Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226/227 - Deputation - Unsuitable for job - Repatriation - The failure of the petitioner to provide evidence of the order of repatriation was done with a malicious intent.
The petitioner could not demand as a matter of right to remain on the post with BBMB when BBMB and State of Haryana had decided that petitioner should be repatriated and was thus ordered.
The said order cannot be deemed illegal.
[Para 22]  B.
Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226/227 - Deputation - Allotment of house - Misconduct - Repatriation - The house was assigned to the employees who worked with BBMB on deputation, after allotment of land.
The allotment of houses in such circumstances cannot be deemed a permanent right, in light of the petitioner's repatriation to Haryana state, and thus, no directions can be given to allow petitioner to remain in the house until retirement.
