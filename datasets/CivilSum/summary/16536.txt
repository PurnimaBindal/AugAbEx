Constitution of India, Article 16 - Himachal Pradesh State Commission Act, 1997, Sections 10, 11, 12 - Complaint about mother not being taken care of - Order to deduct from salary - Validity - It has been determined that the 1996 Act doesn't provide the Commission with the jurisdiction to issue orders or directions that affect civil rights, as this is the exclusive domain of the courts - The Commission exceeded its authority by issuing such an order and it has been deemed unlawful - The impugned actions have been invalidated.
