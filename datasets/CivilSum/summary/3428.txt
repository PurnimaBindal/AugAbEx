As per Section 2(q) of the Administrative Tribunals Act, the definition of "Service matter" encompasses all incidental and ancillary matters related to selection/shortlisting and interviews.
[Para 5]
Section 19 of the Administrative Tribunals Act states that the term "Order" is not limited to orders made in writing but also includes the actions and inactions of the authorities.
The act of shortlisting at the time of the interview can be challenged in a petition for quashing, even if there is no specific written order or prayer to that effect.
[Para 6]
