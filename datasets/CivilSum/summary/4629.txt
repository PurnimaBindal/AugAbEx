Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Punjab Public Relations Department (Class-III Non-gazetted) Service Rules, 1958, Rule 11 - Seniority - Inter se seniority - Rule 11 specifies that inter se seniority is based on date of appointment/substantive appointment - The petitioner has a right to be assigned seniority over respondents 4 to 7 as per their date of appointment/substantive appointment - The government overlooked the petitioner's appointment as Superintendent prior to the respondents and Rule 11 - The government was mistaken in considering the position before the petitioner's promotion and could not adjudicate on the regularisation of promotion or promotion date which were not under challenge - The court quashed the impugned order.
