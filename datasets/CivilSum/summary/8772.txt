Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Transfer of reference - The Labour Court cannot transfer the reference to another Labour Court on its own, based on an application by one of the parties involved.
[Para 5]
Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Working Journalists (Conditions of Service) Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1955 - Reference - The advancement of justice is the primary concern of the court, and penalties should not be imposed on the worker due to the negligence of their authorized representative.
Technical rules of procedure are not applicable in proceedings before the labour court, which can follow its own procedures.
The labour court is directed to restore the dispute and adjudicate based on merit.
[Paras 6, 7, and 11]
Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Res judicata - The earlier application under section 17 of the Working Journalists Act 1955 was dismissed in default, and the subsequent application under section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act was dismissed as not maintainable.
The earlier section 17 application can be restored and proceeded with, since no cause was decided on merit and there is no res judicata.
