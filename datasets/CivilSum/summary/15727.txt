According to Punjab State Board for the Prevention and Control of Water Pollution Employees Service Regulations, 1980, Regulation 9, the State Government has authorized the Board to create additional posts and set out the method of appointment and qualifications for the same, in accordance with Regulation 8.
The plea that the Board can only create posts but cannot prescribe qualifications for them has been dismissed.
[Paras 9 and 11]
Constitution of India, Article 14, along with Punjab State Board for the Prevention and Control of Water Pollution Employees Service Regulations, 1980, Regulation 9(2) recognizes the employer's right to impose qualifications for any position, provided that such qualifications are reasonable and applicable to the job requirement.
The requirements for eligibility, as set out in the Punjab Water Pollution Board Employees Service Rules, for promotion from Environmental Engineer to Senior Environmental Engineer are equitable and justifiable since they have a connection with the job.
[Para 13]
According to Constitution of India, Article 16, if Scheduled Castes candidates are unavailable or ineligible under the rules, posts can be de-reserved.
