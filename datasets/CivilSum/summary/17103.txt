Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - Elementary Teachers Training course - The prospectus reserved 2% seats for rural candidates, with one seat allotted to a female and one to a male candidate.
The petitioner was on waiting list number 2, and the seat was initially offered to a female candidate who did not join the course.
The seat was then offered to the candidate at waiting list number 1, who also declined.
The respondent-authorities were instructed to first offer the seat to the candidate at waiting list number 1, and only if she declines, to offer it to the petitioner.
[Paras 5 and 6]
