The Constitution of India, Articles 20 and 226 state the principle of double jeopardy that dictates that a delinquent cannot be punished twice for the same offense.
However, the Industrial Court disagreed with the Labour Court's conclusion that the delinquent had already been punished and ordered reinstatement with 50% backwages.
The punishment handed out by a criminal court does not prevent the disciplinary authority from taking action against a delinquent.
B.
The relief of reinstatement with 50% backwages granted by the Industrial Court cannot be interfered with under Article 226 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10.
