Constitution of India, Article 226 - Bihar PWD Code, Vol. II, Appendix IV - Increment - Departmental Examination - Exemption - The power to grant exemption from passing the Hindi Noting and Drafting examination lies with the competent authority and is not a matter of right.
However, it is discretionary and open for petitioners who have crossed the prescribed age to apply.
If the claim for exemption is allowed, no recovery of the increment amount shall be made.
[Paras 11 and 12]
