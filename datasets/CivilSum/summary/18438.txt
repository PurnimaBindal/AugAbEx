Under the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 29, 32, and 33(C)(1), a recovery certificate was being sought by the company from the Petitioner as Director even though the Petitioner had already resigned from their Directorship role.
However, the Respondent clarified that the order for recovery should not be enforced against the Petitioner personally.
As a result, the Petitioner's grievance was no longer an issue.
The authority can now proceed promptly for recovery in line with the law by considering the impugned order as a show-cause notice that the Petitioner should reply to, and the petition was disposed of accordingly.
