Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment/Regularise - Appointment against leave vacancy - The terms of the contract of appointment specify that services will be regularised once the leave vacancy becomes available.
- The post was advertised for regular appointment by direct selection after falling vacant - The petitioner appeared before the Selection Committee and was selected at Sr. No. 3, while the person at Sr. No. 4 of the select list was still working - The plea that the post had to be advertised for open selection is no longer valid - Respondents were bound by the terms of the contract of service - The petitioner was entitled to be regularised against the post as per the terms of the service contract - The advertisement was quashed, and the respondents were directed to regularise the petitioner's services as per the appointment letter.
