The Workmen's Compensation Act 1923, Sections 2(4), 3 and 30 deal with compensation for work-related injuries and the death of workmen.
The Respondent in this case was awarded compensation due to the death of a workman during employment, but the Appellant argued that the Act did not apply as the deceased was not employed.
However, the Appellant did not plead this argument in their written statement or provide evidence for it.
In light of this, the learned Commissioner's decision to award compensation cannot be criticized.
