Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Condition of Service) Regulation, Act, 1977, Section 5(2) - Deemed confirmation - The petitioner appointed to fill a leave vacancy cannot use their two years of service on probation to consider their confirmation.
[Paras 6 and 7]
Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Condition of Service) Regulation Act, 1977, Section 4 - Termination - The petitioner appointed against a leave vacancy was terminated due to a lack of sufficient workload and being the junior-most.
Their service continuation was gratuitous and under the expectancy of permission to run an additional division.
The order of termination was proper.
[Paras 14, 17 and 18]
Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Condition of Service) Regulation Act, 1977, Section 16 - Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Condition of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 26(3) - Termination - Notice - The petitioner appointed against a leave vacancy cannot claim three months' salary by using Rule 26 to take advantage of benefits available to a permanent employee.
