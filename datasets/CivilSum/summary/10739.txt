According to Section 2(oo)(bb) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (XIV of 1947), it is considered an unfair labor practice when a workman is repeatedly appointed for 89 days with notional breaks.
If a workman is terminated after 89 days and then reappointed again after a short period, it is a violation of the law.
Even if the workman's services are terminated on the expiry of the 89-day period, and a reappointment is given after a few days, it still counts as a violation.
The workman's retrenchment has been set aside because he actually rendered more than 240 days of service in the 12 months preceding his retrenchment.
