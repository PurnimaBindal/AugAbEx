According to the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, Sections 2(9) and 2(13), the terms 'employee' and 'immediate employer' are defined as individuals who are employed by or through an immediate employer and are under the supervision of the principal employer while working on tasks that are a part of or preliminary to the work carried out in the factory or establishment.
These individuals are considered to be the 'employer' under the Act.
