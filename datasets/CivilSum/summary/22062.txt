Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab National Bank Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1977, Regulations 6 and 7 - Disciplinary proceedings - Natural justice - Opportunity of hearing - In case of disagreement between the disciplinary and inquiry authorities on a charge, the disciplinary authority must record tentative reasons for such disagreement before recording its own findings - Furthermore, the officer must be given an opportunity to represent himself before the final findings are recorded - These principles uphold natural justice and fairness for the officer charged of misconduct.
