Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Judicial review - Promotion - Petitioner challenges denial of promotion - Judicial review only examines the decision-making process, not the decision itself - High Court cannot question the suitability of an employee as an Appellate Authority while examining the validity of a promotion denial.
[Para 16]  B Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - Interview - Although the petitioner believes his performance was excellent, the assessment was based on the interview conducted by the General Manager and two other senior officers - Their assessment cannot be questioned simply because the petitioner believes it to be unfair.
