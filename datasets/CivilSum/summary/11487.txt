Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 320 - Appointment by Public Service Commission - If a candidate listed for a vacancy refuses to join during the listed period, the vacancy must instead be filled by the next qualified candidate on the waiting list - For instance, the petitioner, who is at the top of the waiting list in the Reserved Category of OBC, should have been considered for the appointment if he was otherwise qualifying.
