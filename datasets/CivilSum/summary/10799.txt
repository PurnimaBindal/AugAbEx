The petitioner, retired as Executive Engineer, experienced wrong fixation of pay, but it was not due to any action on their part.
The controlling officer's error was made in good faith, and petitioner should not be penalized for receiving too much.
However, this does not prevent authorities from correcting the petitioner's retiral dues based on their lawful salary.
The impugned order, insofar as it relates to recovery of excess pay, is quashed.
Authorities can determine retiral and other dues based on the petitioner's lawful salary.
