Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 - Admission - Eligibility Conditions - Changed - Candidates were subject to a common admission test - Eligibility conditions varied after the result was declared - A distinction was created between candidates who studied in recognized and unrecognized schools - The court held that since the created distinction has no relation with selecting the best candidates for admission, the action of ignoring inter se merit of the candidates is illegal, unfair and unconstitutional.
