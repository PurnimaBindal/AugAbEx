Under Article 16 of the Constitution of India, an employee's Annual Confidential Report and adverse remarks cannot be considered while passing an order of premature retirement.
A petitioner who made a representation against adverse remarks, but no decision was taken by the government, had their order of premature retirement set aside.
[Para 9]
The Constitution of India's Article 17 states that a premature retirement may not be based on an employee's Annual Confidential Report with an average rating, which cannot be considered an adverse entry.
A petitioner with two "very good," seven "good," and one average report cannot be retired prematurely due to the average report not being sufficient grounds for premature retirement.
The order of premature retirement was therefore quashed.
