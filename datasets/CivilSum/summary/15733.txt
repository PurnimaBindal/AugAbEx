Constitution of India, Articles 311, 14 and 16 - DDA (Salaries, Allowances and Conditions of Services) Regulations, 1961, Regulation 19 - Removal - No opportunity of hearing - Petitioner, who was removed from service while officiating as Naib Tehsildar, did not receive any notice or opportunity to be heard - According to Regulation 19, the petitioner was entitled to receive notice - The principles of natural justice were not followed - The order of removal is set aside because there is no material on record to support the findings against the petitioner.
[Paras 6, 7 and 8]
