The appellant's appointment to the Class A Municipal Committee's post of Accounts Officer was valid, and he could only be removed or demoted by following the legal procedure under the Madhya Pradesh Municipalities Act, 1961, Sections 94 and 95, and the Madhya Pradesh Municipal Service (Scales of Pay and Allowances) Rules, 1967, Rules 7, 8 and 10.
Despite this, the respondents reverted the appellant to the post of Accountant by equating it to the higher post of Accounts Officer, which was completely unjustified.
