Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Education Service (School and Inspection) (Class 11) Rules, 1976, Rules 9 and 10 - Promotion to PES II Services - Scheduled Caste candidates are entitled to reservation - Even if no vacancies are available and the quota is exhausted, candidates belonging to the Scheduled Caste category cannot be refused promotion against the general category.
This ruling was followed by the Full Bench in 1989(2) RSJ 425.
