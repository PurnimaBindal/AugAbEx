Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Disciplinary proceedings - Punishment disproportionate to guilt - Reversion - The petitioner faced disciplinary action due to obtaining multiple identity cards - The disciplinary authority decided to take disciplinary proceedings after the petitioner's admission - The minimum punishment provided by Clause 12(a) of Standing Orders was imposed as it did not provide for a more lenient punishment - Therefore, it cannot be argued that the punishment was disproportionate to the misconduct.
