Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Workman - Labour Court has to determine all incidental questions to the dispute, including the existence of an employer-employee relationship, which is an essential precondition for being considered a workman.
An issue must be framed and adjudicated on this question.
[Paras 5 to 12]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Scope - Parties can raise questions before the Labour Court that were not raised before the conciliation officer.
[Para 10]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Scope - If the question as to whether the workman abandoned the job or was terminated is raised, it must be determined by the Labour Court.
