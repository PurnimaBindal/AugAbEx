Article 226 of the Constitution of India states that once a degree is granted by a university recognized by the Medical Council of India and included in Schedule I of the Medical Council Act, it is valid throughout the country regardless of the institution.
[Para 16]
Section 10 of the Medical Council Act stipulates that if Punjab University is recognized for awarding degrees, the Medical Council of India cannot question the qualifications required for the entrance test.
[Para 18]
Section 11 of the Medical Council Act provides that any medical qualification granted by a university or medical institution in the first schedule shall be recognized by the Medical Council of India.
[Para 14]
The petitioner received an MS Surgery degree from an institution affiliated with Punjab University, recognized by the Medical Council of India.
The court held that this degree was sufficient for the petitioner to be eligible for the M.Ch.
entrance test.
