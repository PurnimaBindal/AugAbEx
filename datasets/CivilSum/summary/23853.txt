Punjab Service of Engineers Class-I P.W.D.
(Roads and Buildings) Rules, 1960, Rules 2, 5, 8 to 12 - inter se Seniority - Year of allotment/Seniority - Direct recruits are allocated seniority from the year of allotment, which cannot be changed - Promotees receive seniority upon availability of a position in the 50% quota, with variable year of allotment - Seniority from Class II onwards is based on the availability of cadre posts.
[Paras 21, 25 and 27] B Punjab Service of Engineers Class-I P.W.D.
(Roads and Buildings) Rules, 1960, Rules 2, 5, 8 to 12 - inter se seniority/Direct recruits and promotees - Year of allotment/Seniority - The promotee cannot receive year of allotment before membership in the service - Seniority cannot be determined from initial officiating appointment on ex-cadre post - Officiating appointment by promotion to a cadre post outside the quota, continuous officiation, and declaration of probation period does not grant promotees seniority over direct recruits.
[Paras 21 and 27] C. Punjab Service of Engineers Class-I P.W.D.
(Roads and Buildings) Rules, 1960 - Proviso - Purpose of proviso - A proviso's role is to address cases that would otherwise be covered by general language of main legislation - Proviso may not be disconnected from main legislation, nor can it be used to nullify the main legislation's true intent.
