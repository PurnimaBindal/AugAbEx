The petitioner, who was not promoted earlier due to pending disciplinary proceedings, is entitled to claim promotion from the date when his juniors were promoted.
Even though the petitioner was promoted at a later point in time, this doesn't mean that he does not have the right to claim promotion from an earlier date.
As per the Punjab Civil Services Rules (as applicable to Haryana), the respondents are directed to consider the case of the petitioner for promotion with reference to the date of promotion granted to his immediate juniors.
If found suitable, he shall be promoted from said date with consequential benefits with interest at a rate of 6% per annum.
[Paras 5, 6 and 9]
