Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab State Co-operative Audit Service (Class I) Rules, 1977, Rule 7 - Promotion - The petitioner who is currently working as Joint Chief Auditor is eligible for promotion to Additional Chief Auditor.
As the petitioner is also performing some of the duties of Additional Chief Auditor along with their current duties, they are entitled to be considered for regular promotion to the post of Additional Chief Auditor - especially when the said post was vacant.
