Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Sanskrit Teacher Position - Candidacy Rejection - Notice of Ineligibility - The ground of disqualification for candidates who acquire the Praveshika qualification subsequent to higher education isn't eligible according to note (b) in advertisement - Note (b) precludes recognition of improved marks after obtaining a higher qualification - The classification made through note (b) has no application since it does not state that a lower-level examination undertaken by a candidate who has attained a higher qualification will not be considered - The order by a single judge is upheld.
