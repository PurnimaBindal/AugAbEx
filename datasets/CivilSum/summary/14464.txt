Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Pension - Departmental proceedings - During a departmental inquiry, the government cannot withhold the pension or commutation of pension - Retirees must receive 100% pension during the inquiry - However, the employer can withhold the payment of gratuity until the inquiry is finalized - Petitioner's pension and arrears must be released by the respondents with 12% interest.
