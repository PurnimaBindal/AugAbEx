Constitution of India, Article 16 - Orissa Service of Engineers Rules, 1941, Rule 26 - Appointment - Seniority of Direct Recruits vis-a-vis promotees - Direct recruits appointed in 1980 claimed seniority from the year 1978 when vacancies falling to their quota occurred over the Assistant Engineers appointed by promotion in the years 1979 and 1980 - The term "Recruited" applies to both direct recruits and promotees - Final selection for both groups is made by the State Government under Rules 15 and 18, respectively - The appointment order date is crucial for determining seniority under this rule.
