Constitution of India, Article 16 - Short term vacancy - Ad hoc appointment - An ad hoc appointment of a teacher in a short term vacancy does not give them the right to claim the post permanently, even if the vacancy is converted to a permanent one later.
They do, however, have the right to be considered for ad hoc appointment in the substantive vacancy with other eligible candidates if they have the necessary qualifications.
[Paras 16 and 24]
