According to Indian Constitution's Articles 14, 16, and 39, equal pay for equal work should be observed.
However, even after working on a higher post for ten years, a petitioner was only given a promotion and one higher pay increment.
Others in that position were given the regular pay scale for their position.
The principle of equal pay for equal work states that promotees should be paid the pay scale of the given position.
It is an essential part of the equality doctrine and thus should be upheld.
The petitioner should be given the pay scale of the post immediately.
The justice system shall not provide arrears of salary since the petitioner did not make any presentation within a reasonable time upon promotion to demand the higher pay scale.
Instead, the justice system would provide the petitioner with fixation of pay from the date of her joining until the new scales come into effect.
