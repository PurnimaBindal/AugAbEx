The petitioner applied for re-evaluation and re-checking of two of his papers within the time frame, but the result of re-evaluation was delayed for approximately one year.
He also applied for admission within the specified time, but the admission was refused due to delay in declaration of result of re-evaluation.
However, it was held that if the University failed to declare the result of re-evaluation within the stipulated time, the student cannot suffer loss of one academic year.
Refusal of admission was declared arbitrary, and direction was given to regularise the admission of the petitioner, which was granted provisionally through an interim order of the Court.
