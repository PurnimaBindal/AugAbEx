Constitution of India, Articles 162 and 309 - Draft Rules - Although Draft Rules may lack statutory force before coming into force, any action taken by the appointing authority to regulate service conditions under such rules will be considered fair and lawful, provided there are no rules or instructions that contradict it.
[Para 5]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Cadre - In order to determine inter se seniority, two individuals must be working in the same cadre, not different ones.
Working in the same cadre is a pre-requisite for determining inter se seniority.
[Para 7]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Right of consideration for promotion - In accordance with the principle of equality of opportunity in employment, an eligible candidate has the right to be considered for promotion.
However, promotion is only given if the candidate is found to be suitable.
If the candidate is deemed unsuitable for promotion, he or she cannot claim that the principle of equality of opportunity was violated, simply because juniors who were found suitable were promoted.
