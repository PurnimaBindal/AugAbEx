Constitution of India, Article 226, 227 - Compassionate Appointment - Petitioner requests appointment as a class IV employee on a compassionate basis - Claims that similar appointments have been made by respondent authorities - Respondents argue that Petitioner has not demonstrated dependency on deceased and that two family members are already earning - As Petitioner has not provided a dependency certificate and both wife and mother are employed, a case for compassionate appointment cannot be made - Petition dismissed accordingly.
