Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Disability pension - A sepoy in the Army who developed a backache and had their disability assessed at 20% was declined disability pension by CCOA(P) on the grounds that their disease was not attributable to military service.
An appeal was rejected on the basis that the disability assessed was less than 20%, but it was held that the CCDA(P) and Appellate Authority couldn't record a contrary opinion without examining the petitioner.
The petition was allowed, and the respondents were to pay disability pension at the rate of 20%.
