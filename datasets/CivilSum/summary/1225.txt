Provisions relating to the classification of manner of trial based on rank instead of offense in Summary Court Martial are alleged arbitrary and unconstitutional.
However, the provisions of Army Act and Rules provide sufficient safeguards and ensure a fair degree of transparency, and are not discriminatory.
[Paras 22 and 32]
A grievance as to provisions relating to Summary Court Martial being discriminatory and violative of Article 14 cannot be entertained, according to the constitutional mandate of Article 33.
[Paras 16 and 17]
In a judicial review of Summary Court Martial proceedings, the High Court can go into the correctness of the decision making process but not the correctness of the decision itself.
The High Court would not consider adequacy if there is some material to sustain findings.
