The Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 226 deal with parity of pay scales and revision of pay scales.
When revising pay scales, it was decided that promotions from the feeder cadre of clerk would be to the positions of Accounts Assistants, Store Keeper, Time Keeper, or Godown Keeper.
The decision to award assistants a pay scale of Rs. 570- 1080/- while others received a pay scale of Rs. 525-825/- was not justified.
The Division Bench's decision applies to this case, and the petitioners are entitled to their legitimate claims.
They were given a pay scale of 570-1080/- w.e.f 1.1.1978 and Rs. 1500-2640/- w.e.f.
111986.
