Punjab Police Rules 1934, Rule 128 - Probation - Extension of probation - Confirmation - Deemed confirmation - Discharge from service under Punjab Police Rules, Rule 12.8 during probation - The rule does not provide a maximum period of probation, so the mere expiry of probation period does not equate to deemed confirmation.
Only if the rule specifies a maximum probation period does deemed confirmation apply.
The petitioner, an ASI, earned various types of adverse remarks about his performance, and his representations against those remarks were rejected both by the appellate authority and the State Govt.
The conclusion that the petitioner is not suitable for continuing as a member of disciplined force is in accordance with the rules.
Therefore, the petition is dismissed.
