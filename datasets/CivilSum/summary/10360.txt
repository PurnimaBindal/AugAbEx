Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical Reimbursement - Limitation - The petitioner's father was instructed to undergo Angiography and open heart surgery for Coronary Artery disease.
The petitioner submitted a bill of Rs. 1,78,81855, but the respondents only paid Rs. 1,36,238/- because the claim for Angiography exceeded the time limit of six months set by government instructions.
Despite being done from 20.12.96 to 231297, the petitioner submitted the bill on 8.9.97, which cannot be justified as the stipulated period elapsed.
As a result, there is no fault on the part of the respondents for rejecting the claim.
