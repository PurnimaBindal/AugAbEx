Constitution of India, Article 14 - Back wages - Gainful employment - Court's directions to grant back wages with an enquiry to find gainful employment during termination period - Disciplinary authority considered irrelevant factors like employee's son's business and visits abroad, ignoring employee's explanation that the son-in-law bore expenses - Authority only assessed petitioner's family financial position, denying gainful employment - Authority's approach deemed arbitrary, fallacious, and irrelevant - Petitioner entitled to back wages - Court directed payment with Rs. 5,000/- as cost.
