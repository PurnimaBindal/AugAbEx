Constitution of India, Article 14 - Suspension - Alleged discrimination - Petitioners suspended during criminal proceedings while other officials in the department facing charges were not - Respondents did not provide any reason for unequal treatment - Court ruled that the petitioners were treated unfairly and against the spirit of Article 14 - State cannot use different standards for similarly situated persons - Court directed respondents to either suspend all co-accused or revoke suspension of petitioners.
