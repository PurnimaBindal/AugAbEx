As per the Constitution of India, and Punjab Civil Services Rules Volume I, Rule 2.44, along with Civil Procedure Code, Section 60, Explanation II, employees of the Board are entitled to various allowances and facilities as specified in their appointment letter.
This includes Dearness Allowance, which is normally awarded in accordance with the inflation rate indicated in the price index.
The petitioner in this case has claimed entitlement to DA/ADA and is entitled to it, as per the provided information in Paras 8, 9, and 11.
