According to Article 309 of the Constitution of India, Sections 11(3) and 31 of the UP Secondary Education Services Commission and Selection Boards Act, and Rules 2(hh) and 7 of the UP Secondary Education Services Commission and Selection Boards Rules, the panel of candidates for appointment does not expire the moment one candidate is hired.
Rule 7 of the UP Secondary Education Services Commission and Selection Boards Rules states that the panel's term is one year, and if a post becomes vacant within that year, the next candidate on the panel will be offered the position.
[Paras 17 and 13]

Under Article 16 of the Constitution of India, as well as the UP Intermediate Education Act, 1921 , and UP Intermediate Regulations, Chapter III, Regulation 59-A(c), a respondent may be appointed as a Principal while working as a lecturer in another college.
The total length of service is taken into account when appointing someone, and the service in the institution from which the respondent was transferred should also be included.
