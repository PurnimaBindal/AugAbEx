The petitioners’ seniority as Assistant Engineers is to be counted from the date of their initial promotion according to the regulations.
Their ad hoc appointment had the characteristics of regular promotions and followed the procedure laid down in the 1980 Regulations.
The period of their ad hoc service must be counted towards their seniority, as the vacancies they were appointed to were substantive and eligible for promotion.
The promotions were made within the quota and after considering all eligible candidates, and the Departmental Promotion Committee was duly constituted.
Petitioners are entitled to the benefit of their uninterrupted ad hoc service as there was no violation of the Regulations.
