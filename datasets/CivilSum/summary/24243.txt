Adverse entries made in Character Roll can be challenged by a Government servant through representation, but if representation is rejected, reasons for it should exist on record, though it may not be necessary to communicate the reasons in absence of rules/instructions.
However, if Authority's order is challenged, the reasons must already exist on record and it is not permissible for the Authority to support the order by reasons not contained in the record.

Principles of natural justice are applicable in administrative inquiries that affect a right and give rise to civil consequences, as the distinction between judicial act and administrative act has withered away, as per 1987(2) SCR 272 and 1970(1) SCR 457 rulings.
[Para 7] C. An adverse report cannot be considered against a Government servant if it has not been communicated and the opportunity of making a representation has not been given, as per 1979(3) SCR 518 ruling.
