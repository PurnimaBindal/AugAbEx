Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226, 309 - Army Instructions No. 4/S/75 issued by notification dated 19-12-1975 - Family pension - The Husband of the Petitioner was discharged from Army service in 1945, and the Army accepted his disease as attributable to military service.
However, his pension was denied on the grounds that Army Instructions only applied to those who were boarded out of Army service after 1-1-1973.
The High Court declared the date of applicability of the instructions to be arbitrary and unreasonable, creating classifications among the same class of individuals and violating Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
Therefore, the instructions should be applicable to all individuals regardless of retirement date.
As a result, the petition was granted.
