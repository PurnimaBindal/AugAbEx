Recruitment for State employment must be conducted throughout the State according to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
District-wise, zone-wise, or province-wise selection can lead to the exclusion of more meritorious candidates from one area in favor of less meritorious candidates from another area, which violates the equity clause.
[Paras 7-13]  B.
Article 226 of the Constitution mandates that the court cannot compel the State to appoint more people than the available vacancies if it determines that some candidates were discriminated against.
If the selection is flawed, it must be invalidated, with the liberty to conduct a fresh selection.
