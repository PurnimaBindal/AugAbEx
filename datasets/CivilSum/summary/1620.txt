Constitution of India, Article 226 - Territorial jurisdiction - The petitioners argue that because the order rejecting their pension claims was communicated to them in Rajasthan, they are entitled to receive their pension there and this court has jurisdiction to hear their writ petitions - However, the order was passed beyond the court's territorial jurisdiction - Simply communicating the order at the petitioner's residence in Rajasthan does not confer jurisdiction on this court.
