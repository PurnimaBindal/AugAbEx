Respondent No. 2, R.M.V.
Ratap Reddy, Advocate, invoked the Industrial Dispute Act of 1947, specifically Section 2(s), to challenge the writ petition on the grounds of factual ambiguity over the status of the employee in question and sought a preliminary issue on the matter.
The Labour Court denied the objection, reasoning that such contentious issues can only be resolved through evidence, and thus directed the framing of an independent issue to determine whether or not the respondent qualifies as a "workman" within the definition of Section 2(s).
