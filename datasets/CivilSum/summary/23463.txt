Constitution of India, Article 16(4) and 32 - Promotion/Seniority/Disputed questions of facts - Petitioners who are attempting to secure promotion through seniority and regularization of their services from a specific date may adversely impact many other individuals if their claim is granted - Before considering the claim, various rules and instructions must be considered - Petitioners have been advised to first approach the government for the consideration of their claims, as it is not feasible to go into such detailed records in a petition under Article 32 of the Constitution of India.
