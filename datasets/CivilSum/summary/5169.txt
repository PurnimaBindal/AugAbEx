In accordance to the Constitution of India, Article 226, voluntary resignation can be withdrawn at any time before it is effective, unless there is a legal, contractual or constitutional bar.
[Paras 7, 8] B.
According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, when resignation is declared withdrawn by court, it is held that the principle of no work no pay is not applicable, and hence, the employee is entitled to all monetary benefits including arrears of salary, other emoluments including increments etc. by treating him in service.
