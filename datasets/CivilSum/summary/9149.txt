Mr. KG Pandit, Advocate represented the petitioner, while LR Poojari served as Govt. Pleader.
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 set the premise for the allowance of a revised pay scale for Staff Nurses, which some employees received through a writ petition.
Other left-out persons also filed a writ petition, and their claim was approved.
The petitioner did not receive the revised pay scale because she did not file a writ petition.
The government must set an example and grant its employees the same revised pay scale without the intervention of the court.
The respondents are directed to provide the petitioner with the benefit of the new pay scale.
