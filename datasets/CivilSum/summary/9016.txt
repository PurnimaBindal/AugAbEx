Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227, 14 and 16 - Ad hoc appointment - Regularisation of services - The Labour Court had directed the regularisation of services of private respondents.
Against this, writ petitions were filed.
Instructions issued by the Haryana Govt. for regularisation of services of Class III ad hoc employees are to be adopted by Punjab Govt. mutatis mutandis.
Security of tenure is necessary to enable an employee to give his best to the job.
The Supreme Court has already decided the case State of Haryana v. Piara Singh, 1992(3) SCT 201 (SC) : A.I.R. 1992 SC 2130, therefore, there should be no interference with Award of the Labour Court.
The petitions were dismissed.
