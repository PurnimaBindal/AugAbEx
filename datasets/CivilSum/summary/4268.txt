The Declaratory suit under Article 113 of the Limitation Act, 1963 is limited by law to a period of three years from the date of order of dismissal.
In the case at hand, the cause of action to claim wages arose when the order of termination was set aside by DC on 31.7.1969.
However, since the suit was filed beyond the prescribed limitation on 1331981, it was held to be patently beyond limitation.
This suit falls under the purview of the residuary provisions of Article 113 that pertain to the declaration that the order of dismissal is wrongful or ultra vires the Constitution.
Section 80 of the Civil Procedure Code, 1908 applies to suits against the Government or a public officer, whether the action is related to past or future acts and is not limited to any particular relief that may be claimed in the plaint.
In this case, although the suit was filed for recovery of wages against the Panchayat Samiti or Zila Parishad, it was found that the employees of Panchayat samities or Zila Parishads are public officers.
Therefore, the provisions of Section 80 of the Code have no applicability to suits filed against these entities.
Consequently, the suit filed for recovery of wages was dismissed.
