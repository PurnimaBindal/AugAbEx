Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Opportunity of hearing - Death of petitioner's husband during suspension period - Widow and children of the employee are entitled to payment of arrears of salary due to the employee in the event of his death - If the widow's right was to be affected in any way, the respondents should have granted her an opportunity to explain the situation, where she may have shown that her husband was treated unfairly during suspension - The widow could have also argued that the period should not be considered leave and should be taken into account for the pension - Resolution of the controversy must be prompt.

Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Suspension - Opportunity of hearing - Enquiry/Disciplinary proceedings - Employers are allowed to suspend employees under certain circumstances - Suspension is ordered when disciplinary proceedings are contemplated or ongoing - Suspensions cannot linger on for an extended period and subsistence allowances must be paid - In the event of reinstatement, the Authority must decide how to treat the suspension period - If suspenison was wholly unjustified, the employee becomes entitled to full salary for the period - Employee must still be given an opportunity to explain their situation and show the suspension was unjustified.
