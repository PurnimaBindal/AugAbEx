The Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Services of Teachers) Act, 1974, Sections 3, 4, 5 - Probation/Confirmation/Deemed confirmation - The probation period cannot be prolonged beyond two years according to Ordinance 3, Chapter XIII of the Guru Nanak Dev University Calendar, Vol II - 1977.
The petitioner's work and conduct were marked as excellent throughout.
The order extending the probation period was baseless and null, and the employee is considered confirmed after the statutory period and entitled to the protection of Sections 3, 4 and 5 of the Act.

The Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Services of Teachers) Act, 1974, Section 5 - Review void order/Nullity - An order passed by an inactive authority or an ex-person who is not authorized to do so has no legal status.
There is no need to use the inherent power of review to make a void order null and void.
