The petitioner was appointed on a consolidated salary against a notice for a limited period, with no appointment for any prescribed post and no rules or regulations regarding said appointment.
Though the petitioner was allowed to continue, neither appointment letters nor advertisement mentioned that they were being appointed on a permanent or regular basis.
The petitioner cannot claim to be at par with regularly selected employees and the promotion of respondents is just, fair and valid.
[Paras 20, 22, 25 and 26] B.
Combining two categories of employees holding identical pay scales and promoting them to a higher post on the basis of the Delhi State Civil Supplies Corpn Ltd (Recruitment and Promotion) Rules, 1985, Rule 9 is not illegal.
