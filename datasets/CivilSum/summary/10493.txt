The promotion of a Senior Technical Officer, Class II to STO, Class I was made on a provisional basis for six months or until the approval of PPSC, according to Constitution of India, Article 311.
The ad hoc promotion was extended multiple times before the final extension on July 11, 1993.
However, there was no order promoting the petitioner to the post of STO, Class I from or after July 12, 1993, and no order of reversion when the Government refused to extend the period of the promotion.
Therefore, it cannot be said that the petitioner had been retrospectively reverted, and Article 311 does not apply.

Under Constitution of India, Article 311, an employer reserves the right to suspend an employee before taking any action without providing the employee with a hearing.
The Rules permit this practice.
