Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rules 5(v), 5(iv) and 8 and 9 - Stoppage of increments - Imposition of two increments stoppage with cumulative effect equates to reduction in the employee's position in the time scale two steps below and is considered a major penalty according to Rule 5(v), whereas stoppage of one increment without any linking to the employee's career is a minor penalty - Following Rules 8 and 9, there should be an enquiry conducted before imposing punishment of stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect.
