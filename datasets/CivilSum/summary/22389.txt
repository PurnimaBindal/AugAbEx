Section 30 of the Punjab Excise Act prohibits the employment of women in liquor bars or establishments serving intoxicating drugs and restricts the employment of men below the age of 25.
However, the court ruled that Section 30 is an infringement of Articles 19(1)(g), 14, and 15 of the Constitution.
The court also stated that legislation should not perpetuate the oppression of women and that it is the state's duty to ensure safety.
Ultimately, the impugned legislation is based on rigid morality and relevance to sexual role.
