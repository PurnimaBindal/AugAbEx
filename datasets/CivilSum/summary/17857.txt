for Nos. 1 and 2, and Mr. RS Rai for No. 3 are subject to the Constitution of India, Article 226 and 311 and Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8, regarding Charge-sheet Enquiry and Termination.
The petitioner was not afforded any opportunity to prove his innocence with no regular enquiry held after responding to the charge-sheet.
The revisional officer could not simply hold the charges as proven against the petitioner as he had not proved that he did not fabricate Receipt Book and bogus receipts.
The impugned orders of termination are in violation of the Rules and natural justice, therefore, set aside.
Rule 8 requires a detailed procedure, allowing for both the prosecution and the delinquent the opportunity to lead evidence prior to the enquiry report being generated, which is then relied on by the disciplinary authority.
In this case, there was no proper enquiry, no enquiry officer was appointed following the procedure outlined in Rule 8, and no regular enquiry was held, which all amounts to violation of the Rules and Natural Justice.
The termination without following these procedures, therefore, is set aside.
