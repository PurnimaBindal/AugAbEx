Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1930, Rule 49-A - Suspension/Enquiry - Departmental Enquiry finality - Suspension can be imposed anytime during an enquiry until a final order accepting or rejecting the enquiry report is issued - The suspension order cannot be invalidated on the basis that it was issued by the competent authority while the enquiry was still pending.
