Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - University Grants Commission Act, 1956, Section 3 - Admissions to a "Deemed University" - Reservation of seats - Mere notification without actual functioning of a deemed University will not affect the right of local candidates - Reduction of State Quota in the Punjab Engineering College is illegal in absence of an approved MoA - Rules of Punjab Engineering College are not deemed to be changed without approval of the MoA - Without approval of MoA, it cannot be said that a functional deemed University has come into existence - Allocation of 50% to All India candidates from local quota of Punjab Engineering College is illegal and arbitrary in the absence of an approved MoA.
