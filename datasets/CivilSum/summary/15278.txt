According to Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume II, Rule 4.23, interruptions between two spells of service will be automatically condoned for the purpose of counting pensionary benefits, except in cases where the interruption was due to resignation, dismissal, or removal from service.
As per Rule 4.23, adhoc/temporary service can also be counted towards qualifying service for pensionary benefits.
A petitioner was given directions to count their ad hoc service prior to regularization towards their qualifying service for pensionary benefits.
