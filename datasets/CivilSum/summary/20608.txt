Indian Administrative Services (Appointment by Promotion) Rules, 1955, Rule 5(1) - Amendments made via a Notification dated 31121997 are not unconstitutional as per Article 14 of the Constitution of India - The unamended rule made it difficult to determine the number of expected vacancies.
[Paras 29 to 31] B. Indian Administrative Services (Fixation of Cadre Strength) Regulations, 1955, Regulations 3 and 4 - A large number of ex-cadre/temporary posts outside the IAS cadre in the States are being held by IAS Officers for a long duration - issuing writ of mandamus to encadre such posts is not possible - Encadrement or creation of new posts is controlled by statute, however, directions have been given to Union of India to consider encadrement in consultation with State Governments.
[Paras 16, 17 and 18] C. Indian Administrative Services (Fixation of Cadre Strength) Regulations, 1955, Regulation 4(2) Proviso - Creation of the cadre and determining its strength is governed by statute - The responsibility of fixing the cadre strength and reviewing it lies with the Central Government - In case of an urgent need, the State Government can add one or more posts to its cadre as provided in the proviso.
