If a Civil Court has the power to handle a dispute between an employee and an employer involving an industrial dispute, its decision will be considered as res judicata in a subsequent reference of the same dispute before the Labour Court, as per the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) and the Civil Procedure Code, Section 11.
Even though both remedies are accessible to the worker, he is required to make a decision on one.
After losing before one forum of the Court, he cannot switch over to another remedy later, as explained in Paragraphs 4 to 8.
