Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 1(3-A)(b), 2(e), 2(o) and 4 - Bihar Shops and Establishments Act, 1953, Section 2(6) - Mineral Area Development Authority held to be an `establishment' within the meaning of Section 1(3)(b) of the Gratuity Act, and is liable to pay gratuity to its employees on termination of their employment after five years' service - No need to examine provisions of 1953 Act.
[Para 4]
Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 2(e) and 4 - If an employee was drawing salary less than Rs. 1000/- per month continuously for more than five years on the date of commencement of the Act, but subsequently drew more than the statutory limit on the date of their retirement, they cannot be deprived of gratuity - They are deemed to be an employee under the Act.
