Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Compulsory Retirement - Natural Justice - Stigma - Order of compulsory retirement on the charge of a second marriage without hearing is violative of natural justice- Stigma on face of order- Compulsory retirement order and benefits liable to be quashed- Natural justice requires a hearing before passing an order for compulsory retirement with a stigma- Such stigma takes it to the category of orders that inflict punishment.
[Paras 7, 9 and 10]
