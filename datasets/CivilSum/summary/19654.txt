Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Punjab Education Department (Subordinate Offices) Clerical Service Rules, 1941.
Rule 3.
Governer's Order dated 28th October, 1980 Para 2 - Appointment - Regularisation - Qualification - The Petitioner was eligible to be appointed as a Junior Clerk and had the required qualification to be appointed as a Librarian, having passed the Certificate Course in Library Science conducted by State Central Library, Ambala Cantt.
As per the statutory rules, regularisation may be granted if minimum qualification of three years on 3091980 is fulfilled and the petitioner had completed three years of continuous service on 30th September, 1980.
Therefore, the direction was given to the respondents to regularise the service of the petitioner in accordance with the Governor's order dated 28th October, 1980.
