Casual Labourers appointed in 1990 claiming their temporary status of casual Labourers under Scheme dated 7th November, 1989 were ineligible as the Scheme only applied to those appointed prior to 30th March 1985 and rendering continuous service from that period - Long service provided no legal right for absorption/regularisation/confirmation of status - Trial Judge's decision that the Scheme had no applicability was proper.
[Para 3]  B In public employment, adherence to the Rule of Equity is a basic feature of the Constitution and appointment in violation of constitutional scheme is nullity as it is contrary to Article 14's basic concept of equality - Breach of Articles 14, 16 and 19 in service jurisprudence is a breach of the basic structure of the Constitution.
