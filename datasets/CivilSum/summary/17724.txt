Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Pension - Gratuity - Family Pension - Pension is considered the property of retired employees - Delay in payment of family pension to a deceased employee is a violation of their right to property - Interest at a rate of 12% is entitled to the employee if payment is delayed - Ordered to release family pension within three months and to pay Rs. 3000/- within two weeks as interim relief, which will be adjusted later in the pension.
