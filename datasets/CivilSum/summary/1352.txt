Constitution of India, Article 226 - Date of birth - Correction in - The School authorities recorded the petitioner's birthdate as 1341967 in error, which was subsequently corrected to 18.3.1967.
However, the petitioner argues that the correct date is 1381967, as supported by the Hospital and Municipal Corp records.
The original record has also been presented in court, and the Examination Bye-Laws allow for the correction of typographical errors.
The respondents are directed to issue a revised certificate indicating the petitioner's correct date of birth as 1381967.
