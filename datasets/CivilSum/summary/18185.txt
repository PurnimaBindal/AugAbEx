Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Rajasthan Gram Dan Act 1971 - Rajasthan Khadi and Village Industries Board Act, 1955 - Pay scale - Petitioners seeking Equal pay for Equal work - Petitioners claiming that they should receive the same pay as the employees of board - The Acts governing both sets of employers differ in nature - Additionally, the salary is fixed in each respective Act - Furthermore, the work performed in both organizations is different in nature - Therefore, the petitioners are not entitled to pay parity.
