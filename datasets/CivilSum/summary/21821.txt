According to Section 11A of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, if a Labour Court determines that a workman's charges have not been established, then the workman should be reinstated with full back wages.
However, in this case, the High Court refused to grant back wages based on the belief that the charges could have been proved with better evidence.
This decision was deemed to be without jurisdiction, as the High Court should not have entered into the question of whether or not charges could have been established through better evidence.
This is not the function of the Court or any quasi-judicial authority.
