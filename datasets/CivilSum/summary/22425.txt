The respondent was appointed as an untrained teacher and offered opportunities to undergo training, but did not take advantage of them.
Consequently, he was not permitted to continue in service.
The High Court's order for reinstatement with consequential benefits was overturned on appeal, with the finding that the letter discontinuing his services was justified as he had failed to undergo the required training.
The High Court's conclusion that prescribed procedures were not followed was found to be incorrect.
