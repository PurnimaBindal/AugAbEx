Annual Confidential Report - All entries, whether good or adverse, in the report must be conveyed to the public servant.
The communication should not only consist of adverse entries.
As per the Constitution of India, Article 16, both good and adverse entries must be communicated to the public servant.
Fairness and transparency in the public administration mandate that entries of all grades in the Annual Confidential Report, be it good, poor, fair, average or very good, must be communicated to the public servant serving in civil, judicial, police or another state service.
This must occur within a reasonable period so that the public servant can represent for its upgradation.
This rule does not apply to employees holding positions in statutory authorities, public sector corporations and other instrumentalities of the state.
[Paras 9, 10, 39, 41, 24 and 12]
