Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Disability pension - Service element - The petitioner's claim for disability pension was rejected by the respondent due to disability below 20%, and service element was denied as well - However, as the petitioner was medically discharged due to Neurosis disease, which is attributable to army service, Regulation 186(1) states that the petitioner is entitled to service element - The respondent has been directed to release service element of petitioner for 38 months starting immediately before filing writ petition - The petitioner shall undergo re-examination by a Re-survey Medical Board, and if the disability assessed comes out to be more than 20%, he will be paid disability pension also.
