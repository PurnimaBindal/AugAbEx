Andhra Pradesh Education Act, 1982, Section 79(1) Proviso - Andhra Pradesh Private Institutions Employees (Disciplinary Control) Rules, 1983, Rule 7(2) - Dismissal from service - Reversion - Approval - Prescribed authority - No approval is currently required until a Prescribed Authority is appointed under the proviso to Section 79(1) of the Act to grant approval to proposed punishment orders - The authorities performing other functions under the Act cannot be required to perform the functions of Prescribed Authority - Order of dismissal not vitiated for want of approval in the absence of any such authority.
[Para 11]

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Andhra Pradesh Private Institutions Employees (Disciplinary Control) Rules, 1983, Rule 7(2) - Natural justice - Second show-cause notice - Reversion - Reversing an employee from the post of Principal to Junior Lecturer in a private institution recognized and aided by the State Government after holding an inquiry without giving a second show-cause notice violates the Rules as well as principles of natural justice - Order of punishment is not upheld, and the case is remanded to proceed afresh after issuing a second show-cause notice - It is also directed to maintain the status quo until the passing of fresh orders.
