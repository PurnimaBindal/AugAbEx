The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 16 and 226, outline the selection and interview process for promotion.
However, in a recent case, the Bank fixed a benchmark after the completion of interviews, causing the petitioners to miss out on a promotion.
This decision had a strong possibility of favouritism and nepotism, leading the Bank to act illegally.
In response, the selection process was quashed and a fresh one directed.
[Paras 10 and 11]
