Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Discharge from service during probation - Natural justice - Discharge for unauthorised absences does not require an inquiry, except in cases where allegations are serious such as stealing property or misbehaviour with a senior officer.
Exceptions only apply if prejudice has been caused to the employee.
[Paras 7 and 8]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Discharge from service during probation - Natural justice - Discharge due to not meeting required physical standards, determined on re-measurement of height and chest, does not require an inquiry.
[Para 10]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Discharge from service during probation - Character verification - Natural justice - Discharge due to concealment of material facts regarding antecedents - No inquiry is needed once disclosed facts are found incorrect during character verification and admitted.
