According to the Excise Subordinate Service Rules of 1943, Punjab Civil Services Rules (Volume I Part I) Rule 314 (b), Punjab Excise and Taxation Department (State Service Class III-A) Rules of 1956, and Punjab Excise and Taxation Department (State Service Class II) Rules, rule 5, an employee looking to be promoted from ministerial cadre to Excise and Taxation Officers must have five years of experience working in the ministry.
This experience cannot be claimed while working in the ex-cadre or while on deputation/transfer or holding a post in another cadre.
[Paras 18 to 21]
