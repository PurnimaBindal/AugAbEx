According to Sections 2(Z) and 6-N of the U.P.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, termination of services of apprentice clerks in a Sugar Mill after 15 months is not illegal.
The petitioners were not appointed as apprentice trainees under any State Government-approved scheme, and thus, they are not defined as workmen by the UP Act.
As a result, the provisions of Section 6-N of the UP Act would not be applicable.
