Constitution of India, Article 16 - Contract Act, 1872, Section 27 - Voluntary retirement - Benefit of - An employee cannot be denied the benefits of voluntary retirement solely because they plan to take up employment elsewhere.
Denial of benefits on such grounds, relying on guidelines or circulars not included in the voluntary retirement scheme, is invalid.
Such guidelines or circulars issued without lawful authority are against the basic principles of voluntary retirement and do not align with the spirit of Section 27 of the Contract Act.
Thus, the employee is entitled to the benefits of voluntary retirement.
