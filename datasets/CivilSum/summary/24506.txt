Assam Service (Discipline & Appeal) Rules, 1984, Rule 27 - Review - The authority to exercise the power of review must be within the limits of the provision or the order directing the review by the Court - Definitive decisions on closed matters cannot be reopened under the guise of the authority or direction to review - TN Godavarman Thirumalpad v. Union of India orders dt. 1511998 & 1252001 by the Supreme Court did not intend to authorize the reopening of any earlier decided matters.
