The Industrial Disputes Act defines retrenchment and lays out conditions for removing workers before completing 240 days of employment.
Unfair labor practices include termination in bad faith or for false reasons and without regard for natural justice.
Fixed-term conditions should not be imposed on work of a regular nature.
Ad hoc workers cannot be replaced with fresh ad hoc workers.
It is the duty of the court to determine the nature of employment, and only a bona fide exercise of an employer's right to terminate or not renew a contract is covered by the Act.
[Para 4] B Courts must follow the law as established by higher courts according to the Constitution of India.
