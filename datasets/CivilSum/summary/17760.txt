Study Leave Rules, 1979, Rules 61 and 73 - Grant of Study Leave - Eligibility - Class IV employee - Class IV employees are eligible for Study Leave and can attend BPEd.
Course as in-service candidates, without a strict requirement of five years service.
[Paras 3 and 4]
Study Leave Rules, 1979, Rules 61 and 63 - Competent Authority - Cancellation of study leave - Once Study Leave is granted, it cannot be cancelled according to the rules.
Directors, who are subordinate to the Government, cannot cancel Study Leave granted by the Government.
[Paras 4 and 6]
Study Leave Rules, 1979, Rules 61 and 73 - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural justice - Once Study Leave is granted to attend BPEd.
course, it becomes an employee's right to attend the course and cannot be taken away without adhering to the principles of natural justice.
Even administrative orders, if they cause prejudice to employees, must adhere to natural justice principles.
[Paras 6 and 7]
