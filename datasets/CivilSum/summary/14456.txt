Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Inquiry report - Natural justice - In order to ensure major punishment is not inflicted without cause, the principles of natural justice dictate that a delinquent must have access to the report against them before punishment is levied.
Punishment orders that fail to follow this rule will be considered vitiated and thus quashed.
The delinquent must then be allowed to submit a new explanation.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Suspension allowance - Forfeiture of - Show cause notice - It is unlawful and violates principles of natural justice to forfeit a portion of subsistence allowance without first issuing a show cause notice to the delinquent.
Such orders have been held quashed and the cases remanded.
