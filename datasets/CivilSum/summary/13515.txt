Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Ad hoc appointment - Automatic termination - Fixed term appointment - The termination of ad hoc employees on the expiration of their term of appointment, as per the conditions mentioned in their appointment letter, is illegal when there are vacant posts and employees are required - The clause in the appointment letter which sets employment termination on a particular day for the employee is unconstitutional, arbitrary, oppressive, and unconscionable - The petitioner was reinstated and allowed to continue until regular appointments were made.
