Constitution of India, Articles 12, 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 19(2) and 33(1) - Alternative remedy - The employer cannot raise the plea of alternative remedy when it altered the condition of service during conciliation proceedings - Alternative remedy is just a rule of policy and not a bar to the jurisdiction of Article 226 of Constitution.

Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - Mandamus - Maintainability - Despite the planters association not being a State or its instrumentality, a writ of mandamus is maintainable if public interest and peace are involved - Issuing such writ against private individuals or companies that violate statutory or public duties or indulge in monstrous activities is not prohibited.
