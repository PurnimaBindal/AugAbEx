Constitution of India, Article 14 - Calicut University Act, 1975, Section 57(6) - Abolition of posts - Reappointment - Removal from service on abolition of posts and courses - Preference to be given in reappointment to discharged employees - Section 57(6) of the Act only grants preference in reappointment to discharged permanent teachers or those on regular basis - Temporary appointments or appointments on leave vacancies for a limited period do not qualify as discharged employees and thus are not entitled to preference in reappointments.
