Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2-A, 10 and 2-A(2) - Conductor with irregularities - Removed from service after departmental enquiry - Appellate authority reinstated the conductor with modified punishment of deferment of annual increment for a period of two years with cumulative effect - Workman challenged the order, but no longer entitled to invoke provisions under Section 2-A(2) of the Act as the original order of punishment no longer exists.
