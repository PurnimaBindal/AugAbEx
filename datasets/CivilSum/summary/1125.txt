Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 - Res judicata - In earlier petitions, the withdrawal of higher scales of pay granted to teachers with post-graduate qualifications in non-Government Secondary Schools was challenged, and the petitions were dismissed.
The present petitions seek relief for the same grant of higher pay scale to these teachers.
The subject matter and cause of action in both petitions are the same, and thus, under res-judicata, the present petitions are barred.
