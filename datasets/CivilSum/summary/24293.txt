Army Act, 1950, Sections 121, 125 and 127 - Criminal Courts and Court Martial (Adjustment of Jurisdiction) Rules, 1978 - Criminal Procedure in criminal charges - Court Martial trial opted but case sent to the Criminal Court - Section 127 allows for trial by the Criminal Court after conviction or acquittal by the Court Martial - Sanction required for trial under civil authorities with no conviction or acquittal by the Court Martial.
[Para 16]
Army Act, 1950, Sections 121, 125 and 127 - Criminal Court and Court Martial (Adjustment of Jurisdiction) Rules, Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 475 Attachment - Petitioner attached to another unit during disciplinary proceedings - Sent to a regular Criminal Court for trial - Attachment no longer valid.
[Para 17]
Army Act, 1950, Sections 121, 125 and 127 - Criminal Court and Court Martial (Adjustment of Jurisdiction) Rules, Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 475 - Relinquishment of acting rank - Lt.
Colonel on trial by Court Martial relinquished acting rank, sent to the regular Criminal Court - Pending trial with no order of conviction or acquittal - Actin rank cannot be resumed.
