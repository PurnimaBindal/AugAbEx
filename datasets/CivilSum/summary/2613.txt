Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission - Bridge Course - Eligibility - Bridge Course allows for upgrading of `Post Diploma Course' certificate to Degree in engineering - Petitioners were denied admission on basis of not having 10+2 Senior Secondary Examination Certificate or equivalent with `Post Diploma Course/Advance Diploma Course' - However, qualification prior to `Post Diploma Course' is irrelevant for admission to Bridge Course - Having acquired `Post Diploma Course' qualification prior is insignificant - 10+2 Senior Secondary Examination qualification has no nexus to objective of upgradation of `Post Diploma Course' certificate to a Degree in Engineering - Petitioners are eligible for admission to Bridge Course.
