Based on the historical background and the long-standing terms and conditions of service and pay-scales, Workshop Superintendents in various Engineering Colleges or Government Polytechnics under the State Government were entitled to receive the revised pay-scales awarded to Assistant Professors (Senior Scale) at 3000-100-3500-125-5000 under the doctrine of equal pay for equal work, as per Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
