Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 77 - Transfer/Merger of cadre/Avenues of promotion - The time-keeper cadre has been merged with the clerical cadre in order to provide more opportunities for promotion.
A Presidential order issued on 51266 prohibits transfer without consent, but those who joined before the order cannot be transferred.
Others cannot contest on the basis of monetary loss, and the order regarding those who joined earlier to the Presidential order has to be disregarded.
