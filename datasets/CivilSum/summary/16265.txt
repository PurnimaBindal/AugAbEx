According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India and Article 113 of the Limitation Act, 1963, if a plaintiff fails to join their duties on the expiry of sanctioned leave and sends another application for further leave which is rejected, and if a registered notice sent to them is returned with the remark 'Not found in the village' and notice is published in the newspaper but the plaintiff still does not turn up, it can be assumed that the plaintiff has abandoned their post.
