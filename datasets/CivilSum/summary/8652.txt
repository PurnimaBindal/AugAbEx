Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2-A, 2(qq) - Industrial Dispute - Trade Union, which includes its Federations is authorized to espouse the cause of individual workman - The espousal made by the federation of workers is valid and competent - Workers need not join the dispute if the federation has been espousing different affiliated units - The reference of dispute on the cause espoused by the Federation is considered valid.
[Paras 6 and 11]
