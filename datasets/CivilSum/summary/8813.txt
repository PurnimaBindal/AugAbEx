In case WP No. 1167 of 1995, the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11A is discussed.
The section empowers the Labour Court to set aside the order of dismissal or discharge passed against the workman and order reinstatement.
The Labour Court can reduce the punishment if it finds that it was shockingly disproportionate to the misconduct attributed to the workman.
The workman, who was a national player in football and habitually late for work, was dismissed from service after an inquiry report was submitted to the employer.
The Labour Court found that the punishment of dismissal was shockingly disproportionate to the charge levied against workman and ordered reinstatement with continuity in service.
However, the Labour Court disallowed 50% back wages by way of punishment.
The decision of the Labour Court was held legal and valid.
