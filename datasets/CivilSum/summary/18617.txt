Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, Section 5 - Limitation - The Respondents argued that the claim was not filed within the 30-day period given by the petitioners.
However, Rule 7(5) of the Act permits applications to be considered even after the prescribed period has passed.
The claim cannot be invalidated solely due to the delay.
[Para 4] B Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, Section 5 - Applicability - The Act is applicable to Educational Institutions that employ more than ten people or had more than ten employees at any time during the preceding 12 months, according to a government notification.
