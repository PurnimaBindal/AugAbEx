The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - Government policy to provide a job for one family member whose total land was acquired - The policy governing each person's rights would be the one issued after the acquisition of their land - A revised policy for the future would not take away the vested rights of those affected by the change - The petitioner met all required conditions and was recommended for appointment - His right would not be affected by a subsequent change.
The government was directed to appoint the petitioner.
