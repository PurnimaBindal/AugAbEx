As per the Kerala Education Rules, 1959, Chapter 16-A, Rule 10, educational agencies under the Corporate Management must adhere to the principles outlined in clauses (1) to (8) of the amended rule, which have been crystallized into government order dated 1091987 and amended in 1989.
The principles and norms for transfer of teachers in these agencies are the same as those applicable in government schools, and seniority is taken into account when transferring teachers.
It should be noted that clauses (3) to (8) of Rule 10 are general in nature and are applicable to both teachers and headmasters.
