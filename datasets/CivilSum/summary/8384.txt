Under the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4 and Civil Procedure Code, Order 41, Rule 27, compensation for damages caused by an employer cannot be deferred due to the negligence of the employer in not filing an insurance policy and involving the insurer earlier in the process.
This is supported by medical evidence of the employee's injuries due to a gas leak and the delay in involving the insurance company as a necessary party would further delay the appeals process and granting of relief to the employee.
