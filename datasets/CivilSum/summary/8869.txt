Constitution of India, Article 320 - Haryana Service of Engineers Class II PWD (Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1970, Rules 8(1)(c) - Punjab Service of Engineers Class I P.W.D.
(Irrigation Branch) Rule, 1964, Rules 22(1) - The Public Service Commission's advisory function is only of directory nature - If the Government decides to relax age or any other eligibility condition without consulting or following the recommendations of the PSC, it will not invalidate the decision of the Govt. - The Commission has to select candidates based on their suitability according to the Govt.'s eligibility conditions - The Govt. may amend or relax rules before the selection process begins, and such a corrigendum becomes part of the selection process - Commission is required to issue a corrigendum and conduct selection as per the amended/relaxed conditions.
