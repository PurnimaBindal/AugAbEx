Education - Deceit - Deceit is when an individual or authority is led to take a particular decision or position as a result of written or spoken language, whilst negligence may not amount to deceit, it can serve as evidence.
[Paras 12 & 13] B.
Education - Deceit - A student who presents a forged marksheet, having previously received an unfavourable one, is guilty of obtaining the succeeding favourable one - Such a student does not benefit from equity and the court may not be lenient.
