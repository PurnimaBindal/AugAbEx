Constitution of India, 1950, Article 16 - Pay Fixation - Fixation of pay for those who retired from Army and were reemployed in civil service - Circular No. OM 8(34) from Ministry of Finance - Single Judge ruled that this circular didn't apply to the petitioner because they were reemployed before 1.7.86 - Impuged judgment overruled - Ordered to refer dispute to Ministry of Defence and Ministry of Agriculture for opinions - Any pay fixation anomalies for petitioners must be resolved according to law and no attempt should be made to recover alleged excess payment until direction is followed.
