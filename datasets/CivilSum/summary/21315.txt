The National Curriculum Framework for School Education (NCFSE) does not require prior approval or consultation with the Central Advisory Board Education before implementation.

Studying religious philosophy and culture, particularly for the purpose of developing value-based social life, does not violate Article 28 of the Constitution of India.
[Paras 43]
The word "religion" has different meanings, including duty towards society and the soul, or Dharma.
[Paras 34, 39]
Imposition of Sanskrit language, Vedic astrology, and Vedic mathematics, as well as treating Hindu festivals as national festivals, cannot be made mandatory under the National Curriculum Framework for School Education.
[Paras 45, 46]
Categorizing students into "gifted" and "non-gifted" categories based on their spiritual and intelligence quotients cannot be decided through a writ petition, and the criteria for evaluation should be established by experts.
[Para 81]
The word "secularism" can have a positive meaning, fostering understanding and respect for different religions.
