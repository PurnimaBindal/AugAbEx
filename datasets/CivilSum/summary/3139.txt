Article 235 and 14 of the Constitution of India prescribe the premature retirement of a District Judge based on performance and confidential reports.
In this particular case, the petitioner had received a grading of performance that was on a depreciating trend from Good to Average to `C' and satisfactory.
The Full Bench concluded that the recommendation for the petitioner's premature retirement was in the public interest and not baseless or arbitrary.
[Paras 6 to 7] B.
According to Article 14 of the Indian Constitution, the purpose of a three-month notice for premature retirement is to allow the employee to organize their affairs.
Although the petitioner was given a shorter notice, they were provided with pay and allowances in lieu of the period of notice falling short of three months.
As a result, the notice was not invalid, and no justifiable grievance could be raised on that account.
