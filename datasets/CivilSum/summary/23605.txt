Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14 and 16 - Education - Admissions - A resolution for reservation and nomination of seats and adjustment of left out and future vacancies was published by the Personnel & Administrative Department of State of Bihar - Anomaly in the resolution challenged - High Court's policy would not be in line with the principles of equality - Held that the resolution can be given effect only if the reserved category candidates qualifying on merit with general candidates consent to being considered as general candidates on merit-cum-choice basis for allotment of college and subject - Formula adopted by the High Court struck down - Resolution of the State Govt. ordered to be given effect to subject to modification and from the next session.
