Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 3 - Out of employment - Accident - Heart attack - Workman with chest pain before coming on duty at home - Death by heart attack while on duty - The workman's death was caused by a strain due to work, which accelerated his pre-existing heart disease and was not solely due to the disease - Therefore, the death occurred as a result of employment.
