The retirement age of teaching employees of universities and colleges governed by the Patna University Act, 1976 and Bihar State University Act, 1973 is fixed at 60 years.
The 1998 notification of the University Grants Commission suggesting that the retiring age of teachers should be 62 years is only recommendatory.
The State is not bound to act in accordance with the provisions of the scheme regarding the determination of the age of superannuation since the said provision in the scheme of University Grants Commission is beyond the legislative competence.
The University Grants Commission does not have the power to determine age of superannuation of teaching and non-teaching employees of the University.
However, it is for the University to comply with the same, and in case of its failure to comply, the consequences provided under Section 14 of the Act shall follow.
The Parliament has the power to legislate with regard to matters included in List I, including standards for higher education.
The State Legislature has no power to make law with regard to the listed matters, and any law made by the State Legislature would be invalid to the extent it is inconsistent with law made by Parliament under Entry 63 to 66 of List I.
Since education is in the Concurrent List, both the Union and the State have power to legislate on education including medical education subject inter alia to Entry 66 of List I, and if the Union has made a law, the State has no power to legislate in said field except as provided under Article 254 of the Constitution.
