Constitution of India, Article 16 - Claim for Seniority - Pay Fixation - The Engineer in PSE Class II (IB), who was previously appointed on an ad hoc basis in a centrally sponsored scheme, claimed the benefit of ad hoc service not for seniority but for pay fixation within the cadre.
The High Court dismissed the petition without considering this aspect.
The matter was remitted back to the HC.
