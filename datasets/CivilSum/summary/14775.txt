In Section 5 of the Limitation Act, 1963, the State must act through various individuals and must obtain permission from LR regarding delay in filing appeal.
In this case, permission was received late but the appeal was filed the following day.
The Petitioner cannot be blamed for negligence and has shown sufficient cause for the delay.
The delay should have been excused, and failure to do so resulted in a miscarriage of justice since the dispute will not be decided on merits.
