According to Section 2(j) of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, the Forest Department in the State cannot be seen as a sovereign function of the state.
Thus, the Department of Forest in the State falls under the definition of an `Industry'.
[Para 11]
According to Section 2(j) of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, the Forest Department can be classified as an 'Industry', but it is up to the workmen to prove this fact.
However, if no objection is raised in this regard by the Forest Department before the Labour Court, then it is not necessary for the workman to prove this fact by evidence.
[Para 10]
As per Schedule 2 Items 3 and 6 of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, if a workman has worked for at least 240 days, then he is entitled to be reinstated with full backwages.
In this particular case, the Labour Court found that the workman had indeed worked for 240 days, and no evidence was produced by the employer to show that he was appointed against some scheme.
Thus, the Labour Court concluded that the workman was illegally terminated and was entitled to be reinstated with 30% of the backwages.
No interference is called for.
