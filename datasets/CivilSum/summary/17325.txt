The petitioner was promoted in accordance with the Assistant Grade Examination Rules of 1984, which came into effect after his promotion.
However, he was required to pass a test under these rules at the time of his promotion, and it is now known that he did not pass the test.
As a result, the petitioner was demoted, and he is now ineligible to file a complaint about his demotion.
The petition has been dismissed.
