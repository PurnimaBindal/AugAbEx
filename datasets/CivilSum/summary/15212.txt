Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 1315, 1316 and 131 - Promotions from Sub Inspector to Inspector - Adverse remarks - Police services' promotion requires a selection based on seniority and efficiency and honesty are deemed essential for selection - Those with repeated negative remarks regarding poor performance and efficiency are not eligible and entitled to promotion to a higher rank.
