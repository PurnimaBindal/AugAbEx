According to the Haryana Veterinary (Group A) Service Rules 1995, Appendix A, the post of Deputy Director (Feed and Fodder) has not been included in the Class-I cadre posts, and even in the non-veterinary cadre, the post has been indicated in a lower pay scale than the Class I post.
The incumbent would not be entitled to the pay scale of Class I Veterinary Service, and those appointed to Class I cadre posts in a higher pay scale will be treated senior, regardless of their appointment later in time.
Furthermore, after the bifurcation of veterinary and non-veterinary services under the new rules, the issue of inter se seniority between them will not arise.
