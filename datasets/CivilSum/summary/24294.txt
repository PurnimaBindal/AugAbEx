Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rules 8 and 9 - Natural Justice - Enquiry - Imposing penalty of stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect falls under Rule 5(v) - a regular enquiry is necessary for major penalties - Non-issuance of notice and non-consideration of the explanation is against the rules - rendering the order no enquiry in the eye of law.
