Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Employment on compassionate ground - The respondent-Bank denied relief to petitioner No. 2 (her other son) based on the ground that he was not providing financial help, even though the petitioner (widow of deceased) had informed the bank that her other son was living separately with his family and was not obliged to help.
While deciding a case for employment on compassionate grounds, respondent-Bank does not exercise quasi-judicial function, but is required to provide cogent reasons for declining the claim.
In the present case, the communication from the Respondent-Bank did not provide any reason for the denial, suggesting arbitrariness.
The Court directed the Respondent-Bank to reconsider the case of petitioner No. 2 for employment on compassionate grounds within three months.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]
