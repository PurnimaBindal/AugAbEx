The Rajasthan Panchayat Samitis and Zila Parishads Service Rules, 1959, Rule 29(4), Amendment in Rules/Seniority - Fixation of seniority on transfer - According to the rules, seniority on transfer is based on length of service - However, the rules were amended in 1980 stating that transferred employees who requested the transfer have their seniority fixed from the date of transfer - These rules are not retrospective - The petitioner was transferred on his own request before the amendment and as such, his seniority must be determined from the date he originally joined the service in 1952.
