Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - J&K Compassionate Appointment Rules, 1994, Rule 4 - Compassionate Appointment - In cases of deceased employees, the first preference must always be given to the widow and children and not to the mother or sister.
The fact that the widow was already working on an ad hoc basis is not a valid reason to deny her the right to a substantial appointment under the rules.
The mother of the deceased, who is already receiving a family pension and living with her elder son who is a government servant, is not entitled to claim appointment for her daughter at the expense of the widow and children of the deceased.
[Para 11] B J&K Compassionate Appointment Rules, 1994, Rule 4 - The objective of Compassionate Appointment is to assist families overcome the enormous hardship caused by the death of a bread-winner.
It is not a legally-enforceable right but solely a concession.
