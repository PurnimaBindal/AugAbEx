A policy decision was made by the Haryana Government to regularise the services of ad hoc appointees who had held their post for at least two years as of 31.12.90.
The Petitioner in this case fulfilled all requirements for regularisation of his services and there was no issue with his work and conduct.
Respondent directed to regularise his services as a result.
The benefit of military service available at the time of appointment to a civil post has to be counted for pay, increments and seniority.
On regularisation of services, the petitioner is entitled to all benefits.
Posts held by ad hoc appointees for two years on 31.12.90 were taken out of SSS Board's purview, giving other departmental agencies the opportunity to make appointments on such posts.
Ad hoc arrangements can be made by departments on posts not within the purview of the SSS board until regular incumbents arrive.
There is a reasonable classification with no violation of Articles 14 or 16 of the Indian Constitution.
