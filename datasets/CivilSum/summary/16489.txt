Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Management of Recognised Non-government Institutions (Aided and Unaided) Rules, 1969, Rule 28 - Disciplinary proceedings - Suspension - If disciplinary proceedings are not initiated within 90 days from the date of suspension, the suspension order is deemed automatically withdrawn under Rule 28 of Management of Recognised Government Institutions (Aided and Unaided) Rules, 1969.
The authority must have given the petitioner an opportunity of showing cause before issuing the suspension order, and the question of giving a reasonable opportunity of hearing arises at the time of the enquiry, even if proceedings were initiated before the order of suspension was issued.
[Paras 6 and 7]
