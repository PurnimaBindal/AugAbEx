Constitution of India, Article 16 - No work no pay - Benefit of notional fixation of pay in promotional post available if acquitted in criminal case giving benefit of doubt- After acquittal, respondent was entitled to all consequential benefits of promotion with retrospective effect- The rule of no work no pay is not applicable if an employee is being kept away from work by authorities without fault of their own.
[Paras 6, 9 and 11]
