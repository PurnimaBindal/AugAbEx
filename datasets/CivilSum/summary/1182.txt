In WP No. 972 of 1988.
A.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11 - Termination - Reinstatement - Officers were detained by the workmen of the Company and held in captivity.
The management lost faith in the workmen due to their grave misconduct, so it would not be just to enforce such a workman on the management.
Compensation in lieu of reinstatement has been awarded to meet the ends of justice.
[Paras 23 and 24] B.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11(a) - Transfer - Punishment - The Industrial Tribunal can reassess the evidence presented during a domestic enquiry and modify the punishment correspondingly.
The High Court should not intervene under Article 226 of the Constitution if the verdict is otherwise fair and reasonable.
