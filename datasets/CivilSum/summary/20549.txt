Constitution of India, Article 310 and 311(2) - Natural justice - An employee who draws his salary from defence estimates and works for the Defence Services is not covered by the protection of Article 311(2) of the Constitution.
Under Article 310, his services can be terminated as per the procedure mentioned in the CCS (CCA) Rules, 1965.
The petitioner failed to show any prejudice caused to him, therefore, the Tribunal's order was set aside.
