Constitution of India, Article 226 - Government Accommodation - Possession - Regularisation of - According to the law, allotment of government accommodation does not grant any right or title to the allottee, who is merely a licensee and not a lessee - The petitioner, who is seeking regularisation of possession of the government accommodation allotted to his father, cannot be accommodated out of turn, as this would violate the rule of equality - The petitioner must follow proper procedure and stand in queue in his department for his turn - The petitioner has no legal right to retain possession of the government accommodation after his father's retirement.
