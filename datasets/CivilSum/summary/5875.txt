Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - P.W.D.
(Irrigation Branch) Revenue Clerks State Service Class-III Rules, 1956, Rule 10(1) - Anomalous pay scales of Patwaris and Assistant Revenue Clerks - The post of Assistant Revenue Clerk is a promotional post with higher responsibilities than that of Patwaris, who are in a junior post - The petitioners have been denied a higher pay scale since the First Pay Commission report - Placing a junior post and higher post in the same scale of pay is irrational - The court holds it to be illegal, against principles of natural justice, and violative of Article 14 of the Constitution to deny a person holding a promotional post a higher pay scale, particularly when that category was previously receiving a higher pay scale.
