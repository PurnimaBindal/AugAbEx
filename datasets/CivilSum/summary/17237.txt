Constitution of India, Article 226 - Retiral benefits - Delayed interest - The petitioner's husband voluntarily retired on 31.8.1994 and passed away on 1091994.
There was a delay in giving the petitioner's retiral benefits, and the state failed to provide an explanation for the delay.
The respondent state has been directed to pay an interest rate of 12% per annum on the amount released from 1031995 to 931999, when the payment was actually made.
Additionally, the petitioner is entitled to a revision of family pension starting from 111996 which must be paid within three months.
