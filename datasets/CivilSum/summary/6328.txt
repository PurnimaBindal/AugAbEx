Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Service Matters Appellate Tribunal) Act, 1976, Section 2(1) - Writ jurisdiction - Alternative remedy - The petitioner who was aggrieved of his reversion by changing his seniority without affording any opportunity, has an alternative and equally efficacious remedy of appeal before the Tribunal available.
The writ petition cannot be entertained on the misconception of the petitioner that only disputed questions can be agitated before the Tribunal and the complaint against violation of constitutional rights can only be entertained by the High Court.
There is nothing in the Act that the Tribunal cannot adjudicate upon complaints against the violation of constitutional rights of the Govt. servants in their service matters.
The petition is dismissed with liberty to the petitioner to approach the Tribunal.
