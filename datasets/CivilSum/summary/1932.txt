Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rajasthan Roadways Corporation Standing Orders, Section 34 - Misconduct - Dismissal - The appellant was accused of not issuing tickets to passengers and was given a full opportunity to defend himself during the disciplinary inquiry.
He had been previously dismissed for the same charge but was reinstated with the expectation of better conduct.
The termination order was not excessively severe based on the gravity of the charge, so there is no possibility of reinstatement or back wages for the appellant.
