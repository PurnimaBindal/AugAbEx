Sections 23 and 24 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 deal with the legality of strikes, trade union activities, and the consequences of participating in illegal strikes.
Workmen have the right to engage in trade union activities, and strike action is a negotiating tool that can be used to bring the management to the bargaining table.
However, if there is no proof of involvement in violence or coercion, dismissal orders cannot be issued against workmen who participate in an illegal strike.
In cases where workmen were found guilty of instigating loyal workers to participate in an illegal strike, they will be entitled to reinstatement with 50% back wages.
Those against whom no allegations or proof were established will be entitled to full back wages.
