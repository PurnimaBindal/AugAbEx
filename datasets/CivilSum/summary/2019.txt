Under Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, it was found that the petitioner had engaged in unfair means during an examination, resulting in the petitioner being punished under Ordinance 16(c) of Punjabi University.
A committee headed by a retired Judge of the High Court conducted an inquiry and found no evidence of hostile bias or animus, but did find that the petitioner was responsible for additions and interpolations in the original answer sheets.
As a result, the committee disqualified the petitioner for one year, although they took a lenient view.
The university is an autonomous body governed by its own rules and regulations, therefore, courts generally do not interfere with their actions unless they are found to be illegal or unjust.
The appeal was dismissed.
