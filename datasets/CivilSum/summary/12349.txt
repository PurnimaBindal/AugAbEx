In accordance with Article 226 of the Constitution of India, even a single act of misconduct can lead to dismissal from service.
There does not need to be a series of offenses.
[Para 8]
According to Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Volume II, Rule 16.2., a constable who leaves their assigned post of duty, drinks alcohol, and starts stopping and checking trucks without authorization, like in the case of Police Station Ambala's sentry, may be dismissed from service.
In this case, dismissal was the appropriate action to take.
