According to Section 4(1) of the UP Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, employees within the unified cadre of Meter Readers, Substation-Officers and Switch Board Officers can be transferred without any specific order of interchangeability required.
[Para 11] B.
In accordance with the UP State Electricity Board Operational Employees Category Service Regulations, 1995, Regulation 4, the amalgamation of the posts of meter readers, substation officers and switch board officers into one code does not violate Article 14 of the Constitution and is free of any infirmities.
