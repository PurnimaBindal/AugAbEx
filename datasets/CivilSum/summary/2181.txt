Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Punjab Civil Services (General and Common Conditions of Service) Rules, 1994, Rule 8 - Joining report - Medical certificate - Rejoining the same post on the orders of the court - The original appointment and joining of a candidate will be deemed to be continuing after the selection and appointment of the candidate is finally affirmed by the court, despite interim termination on the basis of wrong orders - Subsequent orders of reappointment will be deemed to be in continuation of the original appointment - The medical fitness certificate already submitted with the original joining will continue to be effective and a fresh medical certificate cannot be demanded nor should the joining of the candidate be rejected on that basis.
[Paras 7, 8 and 11]

Punjab Civil Services (General and Common Conditions of Service) Rules, 1994, Rule 8 - Joining report - Extension of Joining time - Only inter se seniority in a particular cadre is related to rule 8 of 1994 Rules - It does not prevent employees from joining after a period of four months from the order of appointment.
[Para 10]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Old selections and appointments are unaffected by a subsequent ban on appointments.
