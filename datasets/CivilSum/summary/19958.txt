Customs Appraisers Service Class II Recruitment Rules, 1961, Rules 4(c), 3, 15 - Department of Revenue (Customs Appraisers) Recruitment Rules, 1988, Rules 2, 3 - 'Quota' - 'Rota' - Interse seniority - Promoters and direct recruits - The 1961 and 1988 Rules had differences - 1961 Rules refer to 'Cadre' while 1988 Rules refer to vacancies - No separate quotas set for different categories of employees under Rules of 1961 - If no quota is present, the principles of 'rota' won't apply - Filling up a post to depend on vacancy's existence - Direct recruitment to have at least 50% representation - Seniority of direct recruits to be counted from the date of appointment as all appointments in the direct recruitment quota are regular in nature - Promotees appointed on adhoc basis can't be senior to direct recruits as per Rules - Statutory rules to be followed for determination of seniority.
