The Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulations 173 and 179 state that injury suffered by an Army official while on annual leave at home is considered the same as if it was suffered during the course of their employment in military service.
Even when a soldier is on annual leave, they are deemed to be on duty and subject to the Army Act.
There needs to be a clear connection between the normal living of the person under military law and the injuries they have suffered while on leave.
If a soldier is discharged or invalidated on account of a disability resulting from an injury suffered during their leave period, it will be deemed to be attributable to and aggravated by military service, entitling them to disability pension according to Paras 7 and 8 of the Army Pension Regulations.
