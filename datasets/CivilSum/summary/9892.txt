According to Article 16(4) of the Constitution of India, there is a reservation for Scheduled Castes in promotion.
When the 2nd respondent was promoted, there were seven available posts of Joint Director, with 14% of the vacancies reserved for Scheduled Castes.
However, only one vacancy was available for Scheduled Castes candidates.
The person who got promoted was junior to the 2nd respondent and also belonged to Scheduled Caste but wasn't party in the petition.
Hence, the promotion of the 2nd respondent exceeded the quota that was reserved for Scheduled Castes.
The State of Punjab has been instructed to review the case of the petitioners in view of the Supreme Court Judgment in Ajit Singh Januja's case, AIR 1996 SC 1189.
