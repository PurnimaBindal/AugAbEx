The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion - Discrimination - It is not acceptable to promote a much junior employee compared to the petitioner.
Differing seniority for work charged and regular caders does not provide a justification for promoting a junior work charged employee over a regular senior employee only because the vacancy arose in the work charged cadre.
The petitioner will be promoted from the date his junior was promoted with all subsequent benefits.
[Paras 6 and 8]
