Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(k), 10 & 25FF - Employer - An agreement to sell the company – Once the agreement to sell the company is cancelled and no actual transfer of the company's properties took place, the claim for reinstatement in the industrial dispute remains maintainable against the original employer, despite the claimants becoming the company's ex-employees - However, section 25FF only applies where the transfer of ownership is complete in accordance with law.

[Para 14] C. Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 & 11A - Delay - Back wages - Even if there was a 6-year delay in raising the industrial dispute, the workmen are entitled to an effective relief of reinstatement and the awarded reduced from 25% to 10% of back wages.
