Army Act, 1950, Sections 80 to 85 - Army Rules, 1954, Rules 22(1), 25, 53(1)(a) and 177(1) - For officers, Rule 25 applies to investigations and punishments, while for non-officers, Rule 22 applies - Proceeding under Rule 25 is not a criminal trial or court martial nor is it a summary trial; therefore, Rule 53(1)(a) does not apply.
[Para 12] B Army Rules, 1954, Rule 177(1) - An investigation under Rule 177(1) of the Army Rules, 1954 does not equate to a regular trial, and it does not prevent a regular trial.
