Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education/Academic decision - Academic institutions are responsible for maintaining academic standards and selecting eligible candidates.
Courts should avoid interfering with the decisions made by academic bodies unless the decisions violate statutory provisions or are arbitrary.
However, if the law includes specific requirements for admission or passing an exam, those requirements must be interpreted plainly without any added words or conditions.
The University's ordinance states that BAI admission is granted to candidates who earn at least 50% marks in aggregate in the qualifying exam.
The condition of passing every or any subject cannot be replaced to deny admission to those who pass with a compartment.
This would be unfair.
[Paras 6, 8 and 9]
