Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 10 - Regularisation of contract labour - Those engaged as watch and ward staff on the buildings of the Central Government and FCI cannot be automatically considered as regular employees on abolition of contractual labour and prohibition from engaging labour on contract.
However, FCI Class IV employees who are covered by para 121(6) of judgment in Steel Authority of India's case and clause (2)(b) of para 121, are entitled to relief limited to the extent that the Corporation would give preference to them.
