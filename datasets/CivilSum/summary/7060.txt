Jammu and Kashmir Medical Education (Gazetted) Service Recruitment Rules, 1979, Rule 11(2), (3) - Jammu and Kashmir Public Service Commission Business and Procedure Rules, 1980, Rules 56, 57 - Appointment - Post of lecturer - The Public Service Commission rejected a request to extend the life of the panel for a general category candidate who claimed against a reserved post.
The post was later released by the Commission with the stand that under Rule 13, it was to be carried forward.
The eligible, non-reserved candidates had a right to consideration and selection against the post once the reservation lapse.
Rule 11(2) did not apply as it was not a case of a reserved post remaining unfilled due to non-availability of candidates.
[Paras 20 and 26] B Jammu and Kashmir Medical Education (Gazetted) Service Recruitment Rules, 1979, Rule 11(2), (3) - Jammu and Kashmir Public Service Commission Business and Procedure Rules, 1980, Rules 56, 57 - Appointment - Post of lecturer - A candidate with no locus standi claimed an available post after the expiry of the waitlist.
Since the candidate was not even on the waitlist, he was left with no right to the post.
[Paras 21 and 22] B Jammu and Kashmir Medical Education (Gazetted) Service Recruitment Rules, 1979, Rule 11(2), (3) - Jammu and Kashmir Public Service Commission Business and Procedure Rules, 1980, Rules 56, 57 - Appointment - Post of lecturer - Select/wait list for a post remains valid only for its validity period.
Once it expires, it cannot be acted upon.
