Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(s), 2(oo) and 25-F - Termination/Retrenchment/Contract of service - Petitioner appointed as time-keeper and ticket-canvasser after retirement from police services - No retirement order passed - No valid Rule prescribing the age of superannuation - Labour Court found on the basis of evidence on record that the petitioner was a workman within the definition of Section 2(s) - No contract of service produced - Protection of Section 2(oo) cannot be claimed - Decision of a tribunal on the question of fact which it has jurisdiction to determine is not liable to be questioned in proceedings under Article 226 of the Constitution unless it is fully unsupported by evidence - Comprehensive definition has been effectuated to protect the weak against the strong - The employer's device of verbal termination without following the provisions of Section 25-F cannot be sustained - Award of the Labour Court upheld - The petition of the management is dismissed.
