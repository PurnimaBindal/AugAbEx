Under the Equal Remuneration Act of 1976, claiming the same pay rate as male workers is permissible for female workers under Sections 4 and 7(1)(b) and Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
According to a ruling, there was no evidence provided by the respondent to indicate that men and women were performing different tasks, despite having different job titles.
Furthermore, a female worker who was mistakenly listed as a male worker received a higher wage.
As a result, it was concluded that a worker's pay scale should be based on the type of employment rather than the job title, and any action that violated the Act would be considered unacceptable.
