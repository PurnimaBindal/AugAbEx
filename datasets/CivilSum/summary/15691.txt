Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation - Pay scale - Claim for - The Petitioners, who were working as Lab.
Attendants in a College, had their services regularised.
However, when the College was taken over by the State Govt, their designation was changed to Class IV employees.
They claimed a pay scale of Rs. 950-1500 against the post of Lab.
Attendant.
The respondents argued that they had been appointed on the sanctioned pay scale of 750-940 and that there is only one sanctioned post as Lab.
Attendant in the pay scale of Rs. 950-1500.
The contention was rejected, since a change in designation does affect the nature of duties.
The Respondents were directed to grant the Petitioners the pay scale that matriculate Lab.
Attendants are entitled to.
[Paras 7 and 8]
