Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Advertisement - The advertised criteria for eligibility must be adhered to, and subsequent changes cannot be made without a relaxation clause.
Without such clause, no condition can be relaxed, and excluding a candidate from the zone of consideration is a violation of Articles 14 and 16.
In fact, such fraudulent practices should not be perpetuated.
[Para 18]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Post-graduate degree - A post-graduate degree refers to a Master's degree and does not include a Bachelor's degree obtained after another Bachelor's degree.
Therefore, LL.B and BA, BT or B.Ed are not considered post-graduate degrees.
