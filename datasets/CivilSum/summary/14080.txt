Constitution of India, Article 226 - Extension of service - Unwillingness - Change of option - Petitioner discharged prior to completing 20 years of service due to unwillingness to extend - Application for extension rejected as unsatisfactory - Policy decision allows willingness to be changed only in exceptional circumstances - It is decided that the Petitioner had no legal right to extension and the discretion exercised was appropriate - Therefore, no grounds for judicial review are present.
