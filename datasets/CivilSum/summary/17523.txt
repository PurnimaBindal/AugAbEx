Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Absorption of SPOs/Auxiliary constable in Punjab Police as constable - The rejection of the petitioners from consideration for absorption, despite their juniors being considered and absorbed, was deemed arbitrary and discriminatory by the court.
The respondents were directed to consider and absorb them under the same policy as their colleagues, but without the payment of backwages.
