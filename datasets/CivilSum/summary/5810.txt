Constitution of India, Articles 15(4), 46 and 162 - State Policy - Instructions - Admissions - The State Government has the authority to pass executive orders regarding admission to Medical and Dental Colleges in the absence of any legislation - They may formulate policies to promote the welfare of any section or community.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Necessary parties - A writ petition challenging the policy of admissions in Medical and Dental courses is liable to be dismissed for not impleading necessary parties such as the Medical Council and affected candidates.
[Para 12] C. Constitution of India, Articles 15(4) and 16(4) - Reservation - If a Scheduled Caste candidate is selected on merit in open competition, they cannot be considered under the quota reserved for that category.
