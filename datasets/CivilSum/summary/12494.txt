Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Haryana Revenue Group 'B' Service Rules, 1988, Rule 9 - Selection - 'B' Class Tehsildars - Rule 9 does not mention seniority-cum-merit basis for selection of Tehsildars by transfer from various categories mentioned - 20% quota of Tehsildars selected by transfer was based on merit of candidates, not seniority-cum-merit - Govt.'s selection criterion is fair, just and equitable, rather than arbitrary.
