According to Section 189 of the Air Force Act, 1950 and Para 13 of Chapter III of the Air Force Rules, 1969, those who suffer a disability while serving in the Air Force are eligible for a disability pension.
Even if the disability is later removed after retirement, the eligibility still stands.
In the case of the petitioner, who suffered 20% disability while in service, the rejection of their claim for disability pension because it was found to be nil after one year of discharge is improper and unjustified.
