Constitution of India, Article 311 - Haryana Civil Services (Punishment & Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 7(6) - Natural justice - Enquiry report - The principles of natural justice require the supply of the enquiry report as an integral part of the proceedings.
If the enquiry officer and the punishing authority are different persons, the enquiry report must be supplied to the delinquent before the punishing authority forms any opinion on the correctness of the findings of the enquiry officer.
This enables the delinquent to represent against the findings of the enquiry officer, if necessary.
Rule 7(6), which only allows for the supply of the enquiry report with the final show cause notice, violates the principles of natural justice and should be amended as per the suggestions of Supreme Court in Mohd.
Ramzan Khan's case.
