Rule 8 of the Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970 - Dismissal - Violation of principles of natural justice due to non-supply of enquiry report - Copy of enquiry report must be provided to delinquent prior to passing of final order to enable representation and prevent prejudice - Delinquent entitled to defend himself even if charges are proved - Using material not disclosed to delinquent is unlawful - Order quashed.
