UP State Mineral Development Corporation Limited Employees Service Rules, 1978, Rule 27(iv) - Compulsory Retirement - Pension - Compulsory retirement can be given after 50 years of age.
A provision for compulsory retirement need not always provide for a minimum period of service or pension eligible employee on the date of retirement.
Rules cannot be declared ultra vires or arbitrary or violative of the Constitution only for the reason that it does not prescribe a particular length of service or qualifying service for pension as a condition for compulsory retirement.
[Paras 10, 12 and 14]
UP State Mineral Development Corporation Limited Employees Service Rules, 1978, Rule 27 - Amendment in Rules - Retrospectivity and prospectivity - Rule will apply prospectively from the date it becomes operative.
The rule will operate in respect of all the employees of the Corporation who were in employment of the Corporation on the date the Rule came in operation.
[Para 13]
