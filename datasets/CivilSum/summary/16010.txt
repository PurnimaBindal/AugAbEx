The posts were reserved for SC candidates, but no response was received to the advertised posts.
The posts were advertised again with a clear notice stating that the posts would be filled by candidates belonging to other categories if the SC candidates were not available.
However, no response was received from the SC/BC candidates even to the third time the advertisements were made.
Two BC candidates later applied, but they were not found suitable.
The General Category candidate was selected and appointed, and no grievance can be made by the candidates of the BC category.
[Para 5] B.
Candidates far in excess of the posts available on the date of advertisement cannot be selected.
Only a small percentage of candidates can be kept on the waiting list.
[Para 6] C. Only two posts were advertised, and the selected candidates were only at Sr. Nos. 3 and 4 of the merit list.
They could not legally be selected, and the selection cannot sustain.
[Para 6] D. The unsuccessful candidates have locus standi to challenge the selection.
If one fails in the first attempt, they can do better on the second occasion.
[Para 6] E. The selected candidates were working as lecturers for more than seven years, and their work and conduct were reported to be good.
However, the essential qualifications prescribed for the post changed in the meantime.
They are not in a position to secure reappointment as lecturers.
The petitioners were admitted later and have been working as lecturers on an ad hoc basis since then.
They will gain nothing if the selection under challenge is quashed, and the court is not inclined to quash the selection.
