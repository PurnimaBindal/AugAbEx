Constitution of India's Article 16 and Chaitanya Grameena Bank Staff Service Regulations, 1983, Rule 13 states that seniority of direct recruits should be prepared based on their ranking in the selection list.
Any other basis for preparation of seniority is against Rule 13.
Even if an employee reports for duty earlier, it does not change their ranking as per the selection committee and the roster arrangement.
