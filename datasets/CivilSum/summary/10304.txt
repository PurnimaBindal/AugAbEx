Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 11-B - Back Wages - Forced idleness - Gainful employment - If the workman was helping his parents in Agriculture during the forced idelness period or was found surviving along with his children, it cannot be said that he was gainfully employed - The normal principle is reinstatement with full back wages, except when the workman is proven to have been gainfully employed - Therefore, there is no reason to deny full back wages.
