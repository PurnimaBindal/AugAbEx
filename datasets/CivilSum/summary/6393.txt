Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Pay Scale - Fixation of - The Respondent argues that his second time-bound promotion, which would have allowed him a pay scale of 1400-2600, was canceled, and he is being denied such a scale even on promotion.
According to a government order, the Respondent is not entitled to such a scale.
The High Court cannot compel the government to provide a non-admissible pay scale in the matter of pay fixation.
Therefore, the Respondent is not entitled to a scale of 1400-2600 based on the government order.
