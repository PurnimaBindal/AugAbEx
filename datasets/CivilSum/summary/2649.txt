According to Section 2(e) of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, the definition of employee includes Head Masters/Mistresses and teachers are all considered part of the same class.
Therefore, Head Masters/Mistresses cannot make a gratuity claim under this Act, as per the judgment in Ahmedabad Pvt. Primary Teachers Association v. Administrative Officers and others, 2004(1) SCC 755, which determined that teachers are not considered employees under Section 2(e).
