Industrial Disputes Act of 1947 in Section 33-C(2) states that existing claims for salary of a higher post on the basis of equal pay for equal work cannot be granted if the claimant cannot prove the equality of work.
Moreover, if the petitioner was never appointed to the promoted post, they cannot claim any benefit of the higher pay-scale even if it was a mistake.
Therefore, there is no existing claim that can be maintained under Section 33-C(2) of the ID Act.
