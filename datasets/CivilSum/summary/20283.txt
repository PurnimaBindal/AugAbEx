Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 14 - Central Administrative Tribunal - Jurisdiction thereof - An applicant requested consideration for appointment as member of Board of Revenue, but a preliminary objection was raised by the State on jurisdiction grounds before the Central Administrative Tribunal.
The CAT upheld the objection and made additional observations about the constitution and appointment of members of the Board of Revenue, which was not raised in the initial application.
This decision was challenged and it was found that the CAT did not have jurisdiction, so it should not have commented on issues about the Board of Revenue.
The appeal was disposed of accordingly.
