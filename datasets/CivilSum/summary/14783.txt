The accused in a case conducted under sections 5(1)(d) and 5(2) of the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1947 was acquitted when bribe money was recovered from them during a raid.
The court found that the independent witness present during the washing process and other writings was not examined, which goes to the root of the case.
Additionally, the initial demand of illegal gratification by the accused from the complainant and the evidence of a shadow witness that no money was demanded in their presence was not proved beyond reasonable doubt.
The accused was only a recommending authority, and it was uncertain whether the recommendation would be accepted.
Therefore, there was no occasion for the complainant to pay any illegal gratification to the accused.
