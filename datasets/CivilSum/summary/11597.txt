According to Article 16 of the Indian Constitution, an officiating appointment does not guarantee the right to promotion.
The petitioner in this case is seeking regularization of his service due to his officiating appointment on a post.
However, the court held that only a substantive appointment creates a lien on a post, and therefore an officiating appointment cannot be a basis for directing Respondents to regularize the Petitioner.
Hence, the petition was dismissed as per Paras 5 and 6.
