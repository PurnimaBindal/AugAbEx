Constitution of India, Article 311 - Civil Procedure Code, Sections 9 and 11 - Jammu and Kashmir (Government Servants) Prevention of Corruption Act, 1962, Sections 17(3) and 20 - Dismissal from service/Enquiry/Res judicata - The appellant requested copies of proceedings of enquiry and enquiry report to prepare a response to the show cause, but they were not supplied.
As a result, the appellant was dismissed from service.
A writ petition against the order refusing to supply the documents was dismissed because the dismissal order had already been passed.
The appellant was advised to utilize any other legal remedy against the dismissal order.
Civil suit against the dismissal order was not barred by res judicata.
The Civil Court had jurisdiction to consider the suit since the writ petition did not result in a decision on merits.
The appellant was awarded a decree, as the dismissal order had violated the principles of natural justice.
The Order of the High Court was set aside, and the case was remanded for fresh decision.
