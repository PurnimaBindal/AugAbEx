Constitution of India, Art. 14 & 16 - Natural justice - Wrong fixation of salary - Recovery of - The recovery of wrongly fixated salary cannot be undertaken without following the principles of natural justice.
[Paras 6 & 7]
Constitution of India, Art. 14 & 16 - Wrong fixation of salary - Recovery of - It is not possible to recover salary if there is no misrepresentation on the part of the employee.
The order of recovery is not maintainable.
