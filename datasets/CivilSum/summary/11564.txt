According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 and Police Act 1861, Section 7 and 17, a daily wage employee accused of negligence during performance of duties may be terminated without violating Articles 14 and 16, as long as opportunity of hearing is provided.
Section 7 of the Police Act is applicable only in cases where dismissal is based on remissness or negligence.
