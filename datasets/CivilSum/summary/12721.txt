The validity of a fresh enquiry under Schedule 2 Item 3 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 is questioned in this case.
Even after being found not guilty of misappropriation charges by an enquiry officer, the petitioner was subjected to a fresh enquiry ordered by the management.
As per disciplinary proceedings regulations, there is no provision for review or reopening of enquiry.
Therefore, it is concluded that such actions by management to falsely accuse an employee should not be allowed.
The impugned order to hold a fresh enquiry cannot be justified and the petition is allowed.
