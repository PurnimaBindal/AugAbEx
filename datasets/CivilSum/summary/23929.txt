According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, recruitment rules for class III and IV service in the Subordinate Judicial Service in Maharashtra state include eligibility qualifications that allow for short listing of candidates.
Mere possession of higher qualifications than what is minimum prescribed for class IV post does not disqualify a candidate from being considered.
Higher qualifications cannot disadvantage a candidate and a criterion of this sort cannot be considered rational, as it amounts to a type of reservation.
The fourth criterion laid by the Advisory Committee was held to be unreasonable and it is no ground to deprive persons with higher qualifications from class IV posts.
Any discomfort in performing the duties of the class IV post cannot be used to deprive candidates of an opportunity.
[Paras 16 to 23]
