Constitution of India, Article 226 - State Bank of Bikaner and Jaipur (Officer) Regulations, 1979, Regulation 48 - Pension Scheme - Refund of Provident Fund - Interest - The petitioner is entitled to the benefit of the pension scheme starting from November 1, 1993 and has received arrears of pension since then.
However, there can be no interest at the rate of 6% charged from the date of retirement until October 31, 1993.
Furthermore, interest at a rate of 6% is only chargeable on the refund of provident fund from November 1, 1993.
The respondent's action in charging interest for the bank's share along with interest at a rate of 6% from the date of retirement until the pension scheme's subsequent date is discriminatory and arbitrary, and has been quashed.
