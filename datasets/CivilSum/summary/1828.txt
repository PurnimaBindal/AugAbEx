Constitution of India, Article 226 - Award - Challenge as to - The termination of service of the Respondent/Workman without a proper inquiry or hearing, after he reported back late from leave and submitted a medical certificate, was considered unlawful.
The Petitioner had granted the Workman leave without pay but issued a termination notice.
The Respondent was not provided with enough opportunity to present his case before the authorities, despite being granted leave for the period of his absence.
As a result, the Award was upheld.
