Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service of Employees) Act, 1974, Section 2-B - Guru Nanak Dev University Calendar Vol. IV-1999, Ordinance 16 - Suspension contemplated disciplinary proceedings - Suspension of Principal of College - Section 2-B of the Act does not require recital of allegations and reasons for suspension - Authority only needs to substantiate reason if order is challenged - Onus is on challenger to prove incorrectness of reasons if stated - Writ Court cannot evaluate reasons' correctness - Serious allegations of financial irregularities justify suspension.
