The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, along with Article 226 of the Constitution of India, deals with retrenchment.
The respondent had claimed retrenchment compensation and other benefits but was not permitted to rejoin her service after going with the Labour Inspector.
The Labour Court granted her resumption of duties with continuity and back wages but it was noted that disciplinary action could have been taken if she failed to submit a proper application for leave supported by a medical certificate.
The court gave directions for her to be reinstated with all consequential benefits after adjusting the amount earned by her in her current employment.
No interest would be given on that amount.
[Paras 21, 38, 41, 43 and 48]
