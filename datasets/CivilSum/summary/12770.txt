Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - No work no pay - An employee's transfer order was cancelled on the same day and the place of transfer was changed, but the petitioner was not informed of the corrigendum.
The petitioner was not permitted to join at the place of posting and the legal notice sent by the petitioner remained unreplied.
As a result, the petitioner remained out of duty for about 5/6 months.
The employer must make the payment of wages for that period and cannot take the plea of no work no wages.
[Paras 5 to 8]
