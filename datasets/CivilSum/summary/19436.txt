The mere adoption of service rules by the Electricity Board does not automatically make its employees employees of the State Government, according to Articles 309 and 311 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 5]
The protection of Article 311 is not available to employees of a statutory Board, according to Article 311 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 5]
Work-charge service employees of a statutory Board of the State cannot claim pension benefits for service rendered under the State because service under the State and Board are from different employers, according to Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
The rules do not allow for the condonation of gaps between service under the State and the Board.
If the gap cannot be condoned under the rules, the length of service cannot be considered for pension paid by the Board.
