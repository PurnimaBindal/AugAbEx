Constitution of India, Article 311 - Rajasthan Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1958, Preamble, Section 2(5) - Termination of services - An appellant challenged his termination order before an authority under Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1958, despite the fact that his employer establishment was registered under Factories Act, 1948 - The appellant was advised to seek relief under Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, but he chose to file an appeal and subsequent writ - Both were dismissed - It was concluded that the order of termination cannot be contested before the competent authority that operates under the Shops and Commercial Establishments Act.
