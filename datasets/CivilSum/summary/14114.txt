As per Bihar Pension Rules 1950, Rule 43(b), departmental proceedings can only be initiated against a retired employee with government sanction for events within four years of the date of proceedings.
In the case of the Petitioner, who retired in 1996, departmental proceedings were initiated in 1998 for events that occurred from 1992 to 1994.
Although the entire proceedings cannot be considered illegal, as events within four years of the date of the proceedings were covered, there is a possibility that the authority initiated the proceedings for the purpose of reducing the Petitioner's retirement benefits.
The Petitioner seeks the quashing of the departmental proceedings and the payment of retiral dues.
