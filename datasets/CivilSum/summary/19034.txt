The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 state that disciplinary enquiries must follow a certain protocol.
In a case where a subordinate was punished with the penalty of stoppage of four increments without being given a copy of the enquiry report and issued a show cause notice, the order inflicting major punishment was quashed.
The punishing authority is mandated to provide the delinquent official with a show cause notice along with a copy of the inquiry report before passing an order that inflicts major punishment.
