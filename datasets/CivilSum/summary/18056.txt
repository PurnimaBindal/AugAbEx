Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 - Ad hoc - Appointment/Regularization - The Petitioner was appointed as a Tehsil Welfare Officer on an 89-day basis and his name was registered with the Employment Exchange.
However, when regular appointments were made, his name was not considered as he was not sponsored by the Employment Exchange.
Nonetheless, as per the settled law in Piara Singh's case (1988(4) SLR 739), the Petitioner, having completed one year of service, is entitled to be considered for regular appointment.
Directions were issued to consider and regularize/appoint him on the day, if found fit, with all benefits.
