As per the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, a deputationist cannot claim absorption in the department where they are on deputation unless supported by statutory Rules or Regulations or orders.
The principle behind deputation is that the person can be repatriated to their parent department at any time.
There is no vested right to claim absorption, especially if the necessary qualifications are not met or if there has been misrepresentation regarding qualifications.
