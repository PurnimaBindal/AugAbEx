Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 2125 and 13.1 - Deputation - Promotion - Promotion during deputation to CID from Police Department - Deputationists are entitled to all benefits and promotion opportunities in the parent department.
Service on deputation in CID is to be treated as service in Police Department.
Deputationists can claim regular promotions only per terms in Police Department.
Ad hoc and officiating promotion while on deputation in CID does not provide any rights.
Repatriation is possible at any time, and reversion on repatriation does not violate any Rule or Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
Deputationists cannot claim to have superseded persons in the parent Department simply by virtue of promotions in CID while on deputation.
