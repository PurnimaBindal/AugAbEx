Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Service Rules, Schedule II, Article 226(1) - Age of Retirement - A chowkidar was supposed to retire on 31.3.1990 but was actually made to retire on 3131992 due to the fault of concerned officials.
The petitioner must be paid his salary for the period of his overstay as he had rendered his duties during that time.
His case for the overstay duration is to be treated as re-employment and the respondent is directed to release his pensionary benefits.
