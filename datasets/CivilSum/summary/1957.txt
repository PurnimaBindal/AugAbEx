The Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Sections 7(2) and 15(2) discuss Deduction of wages for absence from duty.
The authority is responsible for finding out if absence was justified and can determine whether or not to make a deduction.
Once it is determined that the person was absent and a deduction was made, the authority's jurisdiction ends.
It is up to the employer to justify the absence, and the authority cannot overturn the employer's decision to charge-sheet the employee.
If the authority is informed that a charge-sheet has been given to the employee for their absence from duty during the period they were claiming wages, they should direct the parties to the appropriate forum and not proceed with the matter.
