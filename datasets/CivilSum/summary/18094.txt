Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Admission - MD/MS/PG Diploma course - The petitioner and respondent, who both received 47 marks in the test, were ranked 31st and 24th, respectively, after the merit list was revised.
As per the rules, if two candidates receive the same marks, merit will be determined by the marks obtained in MBBS/BDS examinations.
The respondent received a Grade A MBBS degree from Russia where marks are not awarded and Grade A is given for marks between 70-84%.
In light of these facts, it cannot be argued that the respondent has been placed wrongly higher in merit.
