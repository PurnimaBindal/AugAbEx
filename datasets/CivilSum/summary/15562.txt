Bihar Service Code, 1952, Rule 97 - Suspension - If a departmental proceeding is withdrawn before the final order, the employee's salary during the suspension cannot be withheld or used for leave - A reasonable opportunity must be given and a proper departmental enquiry must be completed before any denial of salary or leave.
[Para 4]
Bihar Service Code, 1952, Rule 97 - Bihar Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, Rule 49 - A Government servant under suspension cannot have their admissible salary taken away simply because they were suspended during a departmental proceeding that did not result in a final order.
