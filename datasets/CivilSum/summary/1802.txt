Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 244(2) - Compulsory retirement - Sustainability - The government has the power to pass orders in public interest by considering ACR's and APAR's through a Screening Committee - The petitioner's first and second orders of compulsory retirement were revoked and quashed respectively, while the third order is currently under challenge - The petitioner fulfills the pre-requisites under Rule 244(2) - Though the petitioner has been punished with withholding annual grade increments, the government has the power to remove inefficient employees - Thus, the opinion formed on sound reasonings cannot be taken as an exception.
