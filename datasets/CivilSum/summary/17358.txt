According to Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, termination does not apply to those who resign from a current position in order to take a better one.
As a result, the respondents are required to reinstate the petitioner in his or her previous important position prior to commencing commercial apprentice training.
Nevertheless, the respondents are entitled to conduct a proper investigation into the suspected fraud that resulted in the petitioner's appointment as a commercial apprentice.
