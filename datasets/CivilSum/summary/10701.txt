According to the Administrative Tribunal Act, 1985, Section 14(2) and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10, if there is a dismissal of a peon and it is disputed, a reference under Section 10 can be made.
However, in light of the KVS v. Subhas Sharma case, where the judgement stated that service disputes concerning employees of KVS come under the jurisdiction of Central Administrative Tribunal, it was found to be sustainable.
