According to Section 28A of the Rajasthan Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1958, a petitioner's services can only be terminated on grounds of misconduct after a disciplinary enquiry has taken place.
However, in this particular case, the petitioner's services were terminated without such an enquiry, prompting the Authority under Shops Act to issue a direction for reinstatement with back wages.
The employer challenged this award and a compromise order was passed by a single judge.
The workman then challenged this order and it was modified, with the court ultimately ruling that the Authority's award did not warrant interference.
Consequently, the respondent was entitled to reinstatement with back wages and it was deemed futile to send the case back for decision on its merits.
