Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion - Confidential Reports - Adverse remarks - Representation Mala fide - Authorities failed to consider representation against adverse remarks made by the appellant and rejected it after promoting juniors - This gives rise to suspicion of mala fide intentions on the part of the authorities - Failure to periodically consider adverse remarks and neglecting representation in a timely manner constitute denial of equal opportunity for promotion - Appellant to be promoted from the date(s) private respondents were promoted and to any higher post they may have been promoted to in the meantime.
[Paras 6 and 7]
