Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Reference/Adjudication of dispute - Jurisdiction/Resignation - The parties did not agree that the petitioner had resigned voluntarily - The government has a limited scope and performs only an administrative function, and not a judicial or quasi-judicial one which is reserved for the Labour Court - Government cannot determine the disputed matter between parties - It cannot take upon itself the responsibility to decide the merits of the dispute.
(The citation for this information is AIR 1989 SC 1565, in Para 4)
