Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rajasthan State Industrial Development and Investment Corporation Limited Employees (Classification, Disciplinary Action Act and Appeal) Rules, 1979, Rule 14 - Alternate remedy of appeal - An alternate remedy of appeal must be efficacious to a writ petition; if the committee presiding over the appeal is the same as the punishing authority, it cannot be considered an effective recourse.
Thus, a writ petition may be maintained against the punishment order, regardless of the remedy of appeal under Rule 14.
