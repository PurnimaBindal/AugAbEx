"The forfeiture of admission fee in case of late surrender of seat, as per the Karnataka Selection of Candidates for Admission to Engineering, Medical and Dental Courses Rules, 1993, Rule 18, has been challenged under the Karnataka Educational Institutions (Prohibition of Capitation Fee) Act, 1984, Section 14 and Constitution of India, Article 226.
The court held that the purpose of forfeiture is to maintain discipline in the admission procedure and prevent misuse of admission orders.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to a refund.
[Para 9]"
