Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - Termination of Service - Teacher of privately managed recognised school - Institution must follow the regular procedure of enquiry and principles of natural justice before terminating the employment of its employees - High Court has the power to issue a writ of mandamus to any person in the event of injustice - The writ jurisdiction of the High Court is not affected by the Institution's lack of aid from the State - The petition has been granted.
