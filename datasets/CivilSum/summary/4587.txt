Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Disability pension - Claim - Petitioner's claim for disability pension was denied based on the decision of the Medical Board, which found the claimed illness not connected with military service.
The petitioner's response in Part I of the proforma was negative about incidents which took place in service, and the delay in raising the claim after 30 years further raises doubts about the correctness of the claim.
In light of these circumstances, an inference was drawn against the employee and the delay in raising the claim proved to be a handicap.
