Constitution of India, Article 226 - Andhra Pradesh Roads and Buildings Engineering Service Rules, Rule 3 - AP State and Subordinate Service Rules - Seniority - Inter se seniority - Dispute relating to inter-se seniority of direct recruits and promotees in the cadre of Deputy Executive Engineer - The Supreme Court had earlier held that the seniority of promotees for the period before 31-12-82 should not be interfered with - However, the Tribunal reopened the entire matter and directed that the service of promotees shall not be counted from the date of their entry into service - Such directions are not appropriate - The directions must be issued in accordance with the Supreme Court's decision.
