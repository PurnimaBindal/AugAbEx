Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination - Labour Court found that Section 25-F provisions were not followed - Workman had worked for 240 days in twelve calendar months prior to termination - No evidence that the finding is unreasonable or untenable - Termination is declared illegal and set aside.
