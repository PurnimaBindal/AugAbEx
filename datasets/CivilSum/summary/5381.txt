Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Backwages - The courts below directed reinstatement with 50% of back wages after an order of dismissal from service was set aside.
However, as there was a delay of 4 years and 4 months in approaching the Court and the employee was gainfully employed in the meantime, the impugned award of the labour Court was found to have no illegality.
