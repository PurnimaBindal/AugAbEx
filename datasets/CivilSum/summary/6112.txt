Section 10 of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970 deals with the abolition of contract labour.
In a particular case, the management decided to hire contract workers as casual workers, but the State Government referred the matter to the Advisory Board.
The Expert Committee found that no notifications under Section 10 should be issued to abolish contract labour, considering the circumstances of the Management.
The findings of the Experts Committee did not require any intervention, and thus, the Union's writ petition was dismissed.
