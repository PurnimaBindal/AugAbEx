Pursuant to the Panchayat Adhiniyam, 1981, Sections 83(1) and 83(2), the appellants were appointed as LDC by the Panchayat in February 1987.
However, the Collector cancelled their appointment under Section 83(1) without giving them a chance to be heard.
As a result, the order of cancellation passed by the Collector was overturned.
