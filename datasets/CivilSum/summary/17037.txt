Constitution of India, Article 226 - Kerala Education Rules 1959, Chapter XIV-A, Rules 1(1) Note (1), 43 and 51A - Appointment - Post of Teacher - A teacher referred to in Sub-Rule (1) also includes a Rule 51A claimant - Learned Single Judge correctly held that in matters of appointment to the post of a teacher, a claimant under Rule 51-A must be preferred over a non-teaching staff member with the prescribed qualifications.
