Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Evaluation of Answer sheet - The answer sheets of the petitioner of the mathematics paper were replaced with those of another candidate - The court found that this constituted a serious and dangerous fraud in answer sheet evaluation.
The Secretary of the Punjab School Education Board was ordered to file a first information report at the relevant police station to trace out the internal pages.
The petitioner was permitted to appear in the mathematics paper of the next examination without depositing any fee, and the marks obtained by him in the said examination shall be incorporated in the petitioners result.
The respondents were directed to pay the petitioner a sum of Rs. ten thousand for unnecessary harassment.
