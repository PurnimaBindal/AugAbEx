Advocates Act, 1961, Section 36-B - Disciplinary proceedings - A disciplinary committee failed to complete proceedings within one year of it being initiated against the petitioner by Bar Council of State on a party’s complaint directive.
As a result, the council lost its power to proceed in the matter and proceeding stood referred to Bar Council of India.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Adverse remarks - A writ petition was filed to expunge remarks of the High Court in another petition.
However, it was held that the writ jurisdiction of the High Court cannot be used when seeking expunction of adverse remarks made by a judge of the same High Court during the court's exercise of criminal revisional jurisdiction over anyone.
This relief could be granted by the court that made the remarks or the Apex Court.
