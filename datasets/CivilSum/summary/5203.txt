According to Kerala Unaided Professional Colleges (Admission of Students and Fixation of Fees) Regulations, 2002, Regulation 18, the government can claim up to 50% of seats in a medical college.
However, in unaided institutions, the management must have maximum autonomy over administration, appointment, admission, and fees charged.
Therefore, it is fair for the government to only have a 25% share in the absence of any right to 50% of seats.
[Paras 11, 15, 18, 23]

Under Kerala Unaided Professional Colleges (Admission of Students and Fixation of Fees) Regulations, 2002, Regulation 18, the fee structure in a medical college must be laid by the government after assessing the per capita cost.
Presently, the fee structure is considered an interim measure and cannot be deemed excessive under the circumstances.
A committee constituted under the Regulations will review and make a final decision on the matter.
[Para 29]
