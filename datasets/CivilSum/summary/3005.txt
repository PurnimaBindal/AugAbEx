The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 and various Acts, including the West Bengal Primary Education Act of 1973 and the Rural (Bengal) Primary Education Act of 1930, govern the appointment and regularization of Assistant Teachers.
A petitioner who had worked in an unsanctioned post for five years sought regularisation, but the court held that statutory rules clearly articulate the recruitment process.
As a result, the court cannot direct the respondents to violate these rules by regularizing the petitioner.
Furthermore, teachers appointed in violation of recruitment rules cannot claim any legal right.
The petition is dismissed.
