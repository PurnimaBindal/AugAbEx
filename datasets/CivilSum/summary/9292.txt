Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 39 Rule 1 read with Section 151 - Ad interim injunction/Admission/Reservation - Reservation is at the discretion of the authorities as an enabling provision and cannot be claimed as a right.
Authorities may choose not to provide any reservation and in such cases no claim can be made.
[Para 4]   B Civil Procedure Code, Section 151 and Order 39 Rule 1 - Ad interim injunction - Ad interim injunction granted based on principles laid out in Order 39 Rule 1 falls under the same provisions.
Just mentioning Section 151 CPC in the application does not necessarily mean that the order was passed under that section and is not appealable.
Objection overruled.
