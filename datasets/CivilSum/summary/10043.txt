Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Hostile discrimination - Superannuation - Enhancement of age - Retirement age increased from 55 to 58 years subject to State Govt.'s approval - Service conditions of Corporation employees can only change with Govt.'s permission - Majority of Govt. companies have a retirement age of 58 years - Court set retirement age as 58 years for Corporation employees, avoiding discrimination.
