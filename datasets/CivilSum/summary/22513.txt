Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work - The principle of equal pay for equal work cannot be implemented when two sets of employees in different organizations have duties and functions that are only partially similar without taking into account the qualitative commonalities.
The employees of Jal Nigam and Jal Sansthan have different roles and responsibilities which are qualitatively distinct.
Therefore, their claim for equal pay for equal work on the basis of discrimination is unfounded.
