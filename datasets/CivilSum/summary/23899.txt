Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - U.P.
Absorption of Government Servants in Public Undertakings Rules, 1984, Rule 5 - Uttar Pradesh Rajkiya Nirman Nigam Limited (Engineers and Architects) Rules, 1980, Rule 16(1) & (3) - Deputation - Absorption of deputationist - Generally a deputationist does not have a right to claim absorption, unless provided for in the Rules, but the deputationist has a right to be considered for absorption - If the employee opted for absorption under the Rule, was found suitable by the competent authority, and was allowed to continue for more than the maximum deputation period, he is deemed to be absorbed - The authority cannot adopt a pick and choose policy for absorption, and the inaction of the authorities will not take away the right of the employee under the Rules - Repatriation order quashed and the petitioner ordered to be adjusted at his proper place in the Corporation's seniority.
