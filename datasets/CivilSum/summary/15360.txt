Section 7(Q) of the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 states that a member is entitled to receive interest according to the rates determined by the Central Government in consultation with the Central Board under paragraph 60 of the Employees Provident Funds Scheme, 1952.
It is not within the provident fund commissioner's authority to deny the payment of interest to members based on the employer's failure to pay interest.
