Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical reimbursement - Private hospitals and nursing homes - The deceased, a member of Rajasthan Higher Judicial Service, suffering from Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG), received treatment from Escorts Hospital, New Delhi - Representations for medical reimbursement made by the petitioner's husband in his life time were not considered by the Govt. - Escorts Hospital was recognized by the State and there was no standard treatment available for diabetes in the State - The State was directed to reimburse the medical expenses to widow.
