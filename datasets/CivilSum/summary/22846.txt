Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Casual labourer - Termination of service - Award of reinstatement - High Court remitted matter to labour court - Subsequent dismissal of employee during pendency of dispute - First application for approval of termination dismissed on basis of High Court order and second for revival of earlier application also dismissed - Single Judge dismissed petition challenging order in subsequent application and matter is pending before DBSAW - Appellant can urge point regarding approval before DBSAW and get decision on merit - Remittance of matter to tribunal will amount to deciding matter pending before High Court.
