Legal proceeding - Fairness - Evidence - Claim of bias alone does not imply unfair trial - Defendant had full opportunity to defend himself - Tribunal failed to establish respondent's prejudice - Unsubstantiated allegations of vindictiveness do not invalidate the proceedings - Thus, Tribunal made a mistake in ruling the trial unfair.

Employment law - Punishment - Misconduct - Justification - When assessing the punishment for employee's misconduct, the whole context of the incident must be taken into account - The respondent caused the cancellation of a show and got into a fight with the security guards - The Labour Court should not have reinstated the respondent, given his role and consequences of his misconduct - Therefore, the ruling of the Labour Court is cancelled.
