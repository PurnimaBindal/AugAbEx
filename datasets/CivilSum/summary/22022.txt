Constitution of India, Article 226 - Date of birth - Retirement - Dock labourers - Labourers demanded correction in their dates of birth near their retirement - Their dates of birth were entered on guess by appearance at the time of recruitment relying on certificates issued by Panchayat Authorities - The High Court provided interim direction for Medical Board to determine age of workers - Such direction would prejudice the case of the employer - Interim order set aside.
