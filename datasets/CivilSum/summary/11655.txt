Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference - Delay and laches - Despite a delay of nine years in issuing a demand notice, relief cannot be refused if the termination is deemed unjustified.
However, the Labour Court may consider the delay and modify the relief by rejecting the grant of part of the back wages.
The petition has been successful, with back wages refused until the issue of the demand notice, but full back wages provided from the date on which the demand notice was issued until reinstatement.
