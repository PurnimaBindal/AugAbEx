Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, Rule 3(1)(iii) - Quantum of punishment - Natural justice - Punishment cannot be based or aggravated on past service record unless given notice and opportunity - Judicial review limited to decision-making process - Punishment of compulsory retirement on allegation of unruly behaviour based on past record without notice or opportunity not sustainable.
