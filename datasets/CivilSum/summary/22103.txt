According to the Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Section 2(s) and 10, a dispute arose regarding the termination of service of a "workman."
A reference was made, and the tribunal determined that the respondent was not a "workman" according to Section 2(s)(iii) of the Act.
This was because the respondent was mainly working in a managerial or administrative capacity.
The learned single judge and division bench ignored this fact, and since the respondent was mainly carrying out administrative or managerial duties, no reference could be made.
The tribunal's award could not be set aside, and the respondent's writ petition filed in the high court was dismissed.
The appellants agreed to honor the financial commitment made provided that the respondent vacated the premises belonging to the Corporation that he occupied, and amounts that were due to the respondent up to the date of termination were ordered to be paid.
