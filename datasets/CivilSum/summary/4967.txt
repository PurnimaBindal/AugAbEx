Under Articles 249, 252(1) & (2), 254(1)(2) of the Constitution of India, Parliament can frame an Act for the States on matters listed on the State List, but the State Legislature is also authorized under Article 254(2) to frame its own law on the same subject.
Therefore, while the Punjab Legislature had the jurisdiction to frame the Animal Sciences University Act, 2005, in the event of inconsistencies, a law made by Parliament holds greater weight than a State law, and the provisions of a State law that conflict with the Central law will be nullified.
However, if a law enacted by the State Legislature on a subject within the Concurrent List has the President of India's consent, it will prevail in the concerned State even if it has inconsistencies with the legislation passed by Parliament.
The Guru Angad Dev Veterinary and Animal Sciences University Act, 2005 does not violate Article 252(2).
[Paras 23, 26, 28, 29, and 42]

According to Entry 32 of the State List and Entry 25 of the Concurrent List, a State Legislature is fully authorized to legislate and create a university for establishing a University in the States.
Therefore, for establishing a University, the State Legislature has full power to enact an Act.
[Paras 33 and 34]

Section 7 of the Guru Angad Dev Veterinary and Animal Sciences University Act, 2005 lays down provisions for absorbing employees of the former university into the new universities on the basis of reorganization.
If the realignment and placement is on the same terms and conditions of employment as the result of departmental reorganization, it can not be considered a transfer in the strict sense, and no opportunity of hearing is required.
[Paras 34, 35, 37, 39, and 42]

The State Legislature's action to establish a separate University for Animal Sciences from the Agriculture University can not be considered arbitrary.
