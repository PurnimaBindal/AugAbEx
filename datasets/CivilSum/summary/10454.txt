Constitution of India, Article 226 - Withdrawal of suit - Termination of services - A suit for permanent injunction to restrain an employer from terminating the petitioner's service is withdrawn after a termination order was issued during the pendency of the case - A writ filed to challenge this termination order is deemed maintainable - Termination is seen as a separate cause of action.
[Para 9]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Termination - Principle of 'Last come first go' - Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution embody the doctrine of equality, with Article 14 being the general clause and Article 16 being one of the species of the doctrine of equality - It is invoked in matters of employment in public services - As per the doctrine, an employer cannot terminate a senior employee while retaining a junior employee from the same cadre - The rule can only be broken if the senior employee's performance was unsatisfactory and led to termination - In this case, the petitioner was confirmed as a peon, and the post of Chowkidar was abolished, but the petitioner's service could not be terminated, especially since those appointed as peons after him were retained - The termination was quashed and he was ordered to be reinstated with 25% backwages.
[Paras 12 to 14]
