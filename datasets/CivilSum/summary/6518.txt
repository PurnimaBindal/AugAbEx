The Constitution of India, Article 226 deals with examinations and unfair means.
A law student has been charged for attempting to use unfair means during an examination and was caught red-handed.
As a result, the student has been debarred from appearing at any subsequent examination for that year.
The court has held that such students cannot be shown any sympathy and there is no question of interfering with the impugned order.
Consequently, the petitioner's petition has been dismissed.
