Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Chitradurga Grameen Bank Conduct and Disciplinary Regulations, Regulation 33 - An employee facing criminal charges may request permission to engage an Advocate for their defense during disciplinary proceedings.
However, the court must determine that the charges are complicated before granting permission for legal assistance.

Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Chitradurga Grameen Bank Conduct and Disciplinary Regulations - Departmental Proceedings - During the pendency of criminal trials, it may be desirable to stay departmental proceedings if the cases involve identical and similar sets of facts and complicated questions of law and fact.
