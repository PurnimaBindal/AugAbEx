Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - The Petitioner, who was recruited as a Painter in the Indian Air Force, had completed their probationary period and received notice of termination, which was based on the ground that their appointment had violated the rules as the petitioner did not fulfill the requisite qualifications for the position and was underage at the time of joining.
The petitioner had also been absent for approximately a quarter of their tenure and had failed to qualify as a permanent employee.
Additionally, the petitioner failed to maintain discipline, despite being a member of a disciplined force.
As a result, the action of the respondent authorities was not considered arbitrary or unfair.
[Paras 5 and 6]
