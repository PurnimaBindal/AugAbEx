Constitution of India, Article 309 - Delhi Police Act, 1978, Sections 21 and 16 - Enquiry/De novo enquiry/Fresh enquiry/Principles of natural justice - The punishing authority has the power to order a de novo enquiry in cases where there was a serious defect in the initial enquiry, or if principles of natural justice were violated.
[Paras 9 and 10] B Charge-sheet/Vague charge-sheet/Principles of natural justice - A vague summary of allegations in a charge-sheet places the delinquent at a disadvantage, as it forms the basis of evidence and cross-examination of prosecution witnesses.
This amounts to a serious infirmity in the enquiry, and it is not illegal for a de novo enquiry to be ordered in such cases.
[Para 10] C. General Clauses Act, 1897, Section 21 - Competent Authority - The authority that is competent to pass an order also has the power to rescind, annul or revoke that order.
