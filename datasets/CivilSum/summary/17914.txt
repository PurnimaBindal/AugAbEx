According to the Constitution of India, Articles 162 and 16, ad hoc appointments should be made in accordance with subsequent instructions, which supersede earlier instructions.
In order to prepare a panel of teachers of different categories cadre-wise, a selection committee must be constituted and the names of candidates sponsored by the Employment Exchange Panel must be prepared in March every year.
It is not possible to consider the claim of the petitioner to prepare a seniority list of all ad hoc teachers at the District level based on their length of service in accordance with earlier instructions, as no direction can be issued for this purpose.
