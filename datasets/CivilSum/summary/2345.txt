Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1972, Sections 28, 32 - Incidental questions - Complaint of unfair labour practices - Only if the employer/employee relationship is undisputed or indisputable will a complaint be maintainable under Section 28 of the Act - If such relationship was previously acknowledged by the employer and is now disputed, it can also be resolved as an incidental issue under Section 32 of the Act.
