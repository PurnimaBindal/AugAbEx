Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Shiromani Gurdwara Parbandhak Committee Staff Service Rules, Rule 4 - Termination of Services - Natural justice - The termination of the petitioner's service was held to be in complete violation of the principles of holding a person guilty and principles of natural justice.
The petitioner was denied further opportunity and no inquiry was held in accordance with the law before the termination order was passed, which was in response to allegations of misconduct.
The petitioner had denied the charges and specifically pointed out that the alleged complainant-devotee does not reside at the given address and does not exist.
