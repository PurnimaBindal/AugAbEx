Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Enquiry Report - Preliminary Enquiry Report - Natural justice - Delinquent charged based on preliminary enquiry report without receiving a copy - Preliminary enquiry report considered important in such cases - If proceedings are based on a document, delinquent must receive a copy to avoid prejudice to his defence - Dismissal from service quashed with liberty to proceed in accordance with law.
[Paras 3 and 5]
