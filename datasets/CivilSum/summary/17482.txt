The Regional Provident Fund Commissioner ruled that the Punjab Tourism Development Corporation, which assumed responsibility for the Department of Tourism, was subject to the Employees Provident Funds and Misc.
Provisions Act, 1952 and not eligible for the infancy period under section 16(1)(b) of the Act.
However, the court found that since the Corporation was continuing the old establishment previously under the Directorate of Tourism, it was not a new establishment and therefore not eligible for exemption from the provisions of the Act.
