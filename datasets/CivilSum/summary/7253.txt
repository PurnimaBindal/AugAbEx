Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - State Bank of India Supervising Staff Service Rules, Rule 68(7)(i) - Banking Regulation Act, Section 10(1)(b)(i) - Criminal proceedings - Employees terminated on conviction, but can be reinstated on acquittal under the doctrine of fair play - Disability due to conviction lasts even after acquittal - Claims for arrears of wages during the termination period are not allowed since the conviction attaches a disability to the employee's continuing in service.
