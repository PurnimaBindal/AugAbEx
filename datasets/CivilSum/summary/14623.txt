The petitioner challenged an order from the Haryana Housing Board promoting respondent No. 3 to Executive Engineer, who was junior to the petitioner as Assistant Engineer and based on his better record.
Despite the petitioner's representation against a 'below average' report given for a specific period, which was rejected by the Board on 2161984, an inquiry was held which resulted in punishment for the petitioner due to adverse comments and allegations.
However, the punishment was set aside in appeal by the Chairman of the Board, rendering the basis for the rejection of the representation in 1984 moot.
The petitioner filed another representation for a confidential report from a later period, which is pending with the Board, and the Board has been directed to reconsider the petitioner's case for promotion in accordance with Yogesh Kumar Sethi's observations in CWP No. 2734 of 1991, which were made on 1911993 after disposing with two representations that were pending.
