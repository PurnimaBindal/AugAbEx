Constitution of India, Article 14 - Admission to MBBS Course - Eligibility - Candidates must pass 10+2 after a period of 12 years study, with two years of study in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology.
Once a candidate receives a certificate of passing 10+2 after completing the prescribed period of 2 years study with the prescribed subjects, they do not need a separate declaration or certificate of 10+2 and cannot be disqualified on that basis.
[Paras 10 to 12]
