State Bank of India Officers (Determination of Terms and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1979 - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 151 and Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 - Transfer - Mala fide - An individual does not possess a legal right to stay stationed exclusively at one location and transfer is an aspect of the service - Judicial intervention only warranted in case of transfer violation of lawful regulations or on grounds of mala fide - Burden of proof is significantly high on the accuser of mala fide.
