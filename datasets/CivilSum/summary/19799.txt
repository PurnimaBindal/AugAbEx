Constitution of India's Article 311 along with Haryana State Central Co-operative Bank's Staff Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1975's Rule 5(v) state that a probationer's services cannot be terminated through punishment while using probation as a veil.
In this case, the officer must be given a show-cause notice and an opportunity to defend themselves.
Any termination of service without following this procedure is considered illegal.
