Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation - Irregular appointments cannot be regularized solely on the basis of their duration, such as if they continue for over 10-15 years - When filling regular vacancies, the requisite positions must be advertised and filled in accordance with established procedures - The failure to follow these rules in making appointments renders them invalid, and no writ can be issued to maintain an illegality.
