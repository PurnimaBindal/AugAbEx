Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Rules for selection to MBBS and BDS Courses for 1997-98, Rule 3(b) - Admission - Discrimination - In order to be admitted, applicants were required to have both HSC and SSC exams.
However, an exception was made for children of Government employees regarding the passing of the SSC exam.
This rule is not discriminatory towards children of parents who are employed in private service, as Government employees have no say in matters of transfers and must accept their posting wherever they are sent due to exigency of service.
The petitioner cannot be equated with Government employees, and the object of the rules is to select the best students, making discrimination between Government and private employees irrelevant to the objective.
[Paras 3, 5 and 6]
