The principle of 'Industry-cum-Region' in wage adjudication before wages are revised must be considered to award fair wages, as per the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 3 Item 1, and Constitution of India Article 226.
The tribunal has also taken into account comparable concerns while fixing the wages and even if it has not effectively expressed itself to show that it considered the principle of industry-cum-region, the wages granted cannot be set aside, especially when they are not higher than the concerns on which the employer company relied.
[Paras 11,16,18 and 22] B.
The tribunal holds the jurisdiction to direct payment with retrospective effect from the date of demand for wage revision, as per the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 3 Item 1, and Constitution of India Article 226.
This is not improper when the financial capacity of the employer is not disputed.
