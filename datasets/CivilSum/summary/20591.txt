According to Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule III, Item I, the authority to issue a last pay certificate lies with Central Electrical Authority (CEA), if an employee was working in the services of Thermal Power Station under CEA.
However, administrative control of that Power Station was taken over by National Thermal Power Corporation (NTPC) from CEA in 1978.
Hence, NTPC has the authority to issue the last pay certificate for the year 1980.

According to Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule III, Item I, if an employee was working in the services of Thermal Power Station under Central Electrical Authority (CEA), administrative control of which was taken over by National Thermal Power Corporation (NTPC) from CEA in 1978, then fixation of the last drawn basic pay for the year 1980 should be done by NTPC and not by CEA.
Any attempt by CEA to fix the basic pay after its administrative control was relinquished would be illegal and cannot be sustained.
Hence, any reduction in basic pay of an employee on the basis of the order by CEA would be wholly illegal and set aside.
