Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(a) - Terms and Conditions of Employment, Clause 2 - Constitution of India, Article 226 - The petitioner refused to vacate their allotted quarter after the allowed period - Both parties are bound by the terms and conditions of service - Once the respondent-Management terminated the petitioner's occupation of the premises, their occupation would become that of a trespasser - The court held that eviction of the petitioner from the quarter would not alter a service condition - Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to any relief under Section 33(a).
