Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 36(4) states that a lawyer cannot appear before the learned Industrial Tribunal without the consent of the opposite party.
It cannot be assumed that the petitioner had given consent simply because they were present in person.
As compliance with Section 36(4) of the Act was not made, the respondent is not entitled to be represented through a lawyer.
