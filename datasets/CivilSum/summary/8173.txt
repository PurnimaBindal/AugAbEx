Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation in promotions - Catch up principle to be applied after accelerated promotion - Ajit Singh and Ajit Singh-II, 1999(7) SCC 153 observations - Seniority to be re-determined in the promoted cadre levels and reversions accordingly if required - Protecting promotions prior to 131996 - No disturbance in inter-se seniority for reserved candidates who are promoted through accelerated promotion - General candidates who were senior at Assistant Level and Superintendent Grade II before the reserved candidate moved to Level-4 will have to be treated as senior at Level-3 - Reserved candidates cannot count their seniority in the promoted category - Once there is no reservation beyond Level-3, promotion must be on modified seniority at Level-3 - Roster point promotees to be reviewed after 10.2.1995 - No reversion of erroneously promoted reserved candidates - Refixing of seniority at Level-4 by application of catch-up principle - No reservation beyond level of Deputy Superintendent - Reserved candidates to be treated as juniors to those promoted later but senior in lower level - No excess promotion ignoring the principle of seniority - Slide down for certain reserved category candidates promoted after 131996 - Revival of seniority list and refixing the same at the level where reservation is permitted before promotion to further level - Protection of promotions before 131996.
