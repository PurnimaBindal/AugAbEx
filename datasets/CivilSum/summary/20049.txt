Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Pension scheme - Options - Teachers of aided institution taken over by the Government must choose between the pension scheme and retiring at 58 or CPF scheme and retiring at 60 - No fault in the policy's discrimination - Retirees receiving CPF payments and retiring at 60 may re-deposit their amount with the Government to be treated under the Pension Scheme - Government directed to comply with this policy.
