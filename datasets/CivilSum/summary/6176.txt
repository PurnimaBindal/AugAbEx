Central Civil Services (Classification, Control & Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 15(2) - Natural justice - Departmental enquiry - The Disciplinary Authority is required to give the delinquent employee an opportunity of being heard compatible with principles of natural justice in a case where the authority disagrees with the findings of the Enquiry Officer and decides to give punishment on the basis of its own counter findings.
It must also record reasons while returning such counter findings and overriding the findings of Enquiry Officer.
