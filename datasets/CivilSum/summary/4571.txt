Under the Haryana Health Department Multi Purpose Health Supervisors and Multi Purpose Health Workers Group 'C' Service Rules, 1984, Rule 7, the appointment of Multi Purpose Health Workers requires a certificate course from a recognized university.
However, in this case, the petitioner had obtained a diploma from Rajasthan Vidya Peeth, which is a deemed university.
The State's rejection of this qualification is against the provisions of Article 254 of the Constitution and could keep out eligible candidates.
The University Grants Commission Act, 1956, Sections 3, 22, as well as instructions from March 18, 1975, recognize degrees and diplomas recognized by the Government of India as being recognized by the State.
Therefore, the petitioner, who secured more marks than the last selected candidate, should be appointed.
