According to the Constitution of India, in Articles 14, 16 and 226, it is required that equal pay be given for equal work.
The Salesmen working in the outlets of co-operative Societies in both rural and urban areas perform the same duties, functions and work.
Additionally, these posts are interchangeable, and so Salesmen working in rural areas should receive the same regular payment scales and conditions of service that their counterparts in urban areas receive.
[Para 16]
The Constitution of India in Articles 12 and 14, Local Bodies, and Co-operative Societies allow local authorities to set their own pay scale for their employees.
State Government Pay commission reports are not enforceable upon these bodies unless they choose to adopt them.
Local bodies are allowed to adopt the same pay scale as Government departments, and employees of local authorities are only entitled to arrears from the date the pay scale was adopted.
