According to Rajasthan Police Subordinate Service Rules, 1989, Rules 4, 8 and 11(a), the aim of the 1975 rules was to eliminate the minimum requirements of service rules to provide relief to deceased government employees.
Rule 4 had an overriding effect on rules, and Rule 8 reduced the minimum age to 16 years with no upper age limit.
The petitioner's application for the position of SI of police was rejected because they were considered too old according to Rule 11(a).
However, there is no age limit for appointment, and Rule 4 prevents such conditions from being imposed to deprive the incumbent of employment.
