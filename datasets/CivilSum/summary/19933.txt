The Border Security Force Act, 1968, Section 22, and the Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 157 state that a petitioner serving as a constable in the Border Security Force and remaining absent from duty for 4 days will be awarded 7 days of RI in custody of force.
This petitioner committed offenses while in custody, refused to take meals, and refused to pack drill in defiance of authority.
The order of dismissal was upheld because the punishment was not disproportionate, the petitioner was in uniform service in a disciplinary force, and there were no mitigating circumstances.

According to the Border Security Force Act, 1968, Section 22, and the Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 157, a petitioner serving as a constable in the Border Security Force and committing an offense while in custody will be awarded 7 days of RI and dismissed from service.
This petitioner failed to raise any contention before the Summary Security Force Court, did not intend to consult a lawyer or select a friend of his choice, and pleaded guilty.
The punishment was upheld.
