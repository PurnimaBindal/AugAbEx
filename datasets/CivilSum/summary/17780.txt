Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Termination - Appointment on trial/ad hoc basis made on recommendation of a Minister without considering the claims of any other eligible persons - Services terminable at any time without assigning any reason - If during the period of the trial, the authority found that the petitioner's job was unsatisfactory, the action taken was in strict conformity with the terms of the appointment - It cannot be labeled as arbitrary or unfair - Does not require interference in the exercise of extraordinary jurisdiction provided under Article 226 of the Constitution.
[Paras 4 and 5]
