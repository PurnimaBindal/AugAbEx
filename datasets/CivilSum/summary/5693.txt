Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pension Regulations for the Army, 1961, Regulation 173 - Family Pension - Special Family Pension - The opinion of the Medical Board of experts about the cause of death of the deceased is crucial for granting special family pension - Courts cannot disregard the opinion of experts for their own opinion - Provided proper materials are available, Courts must not interfere with the decision of authorities that the claimants are not entitled to special family pension.
[Paras 14 and 15]
