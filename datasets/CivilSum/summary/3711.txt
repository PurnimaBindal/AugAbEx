Rajasthan Municipal Subordinate and Ministerial Service Rules, 1963, Rule 27 - Selection against an advertised regular post cannot be deemed temporary, according to Articles 14 & 16 of the Constitution of India - Petitioner given appointment after due selection but only on a temporary basis for a fixed period - Later, services terminated and petitioner punished for litigating due process of law.
[Paras 16 & 17]  B Reservation of a single vacancy amounts to 100% reservation and is not permissible under Articles 14 & 16 of the Constitution of India.
