Constitution of India, Articles 311(2), Clause (b) of 2nd proviso - Dismissal under Article 311(2)(b) - A complaint was filed and a case was registered against a Head Constable - It was concluded that the impugned action could have been determined through a regular departmental enquiry and therefore it was not reasonably impractical to hold one - The order was set aside and respondents were given the liberty to hold a regular departmental enquiry.
