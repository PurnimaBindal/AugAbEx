The services of enrolled persons under the BSF Act are regulated by the provisions of the Act itself and the rules made thereunder, including the Border Security Force Rules.
Section 11(2) and Rule 177 empower the Commandant to dismiss or remove any person under his command, other than an officer or a subordinate officer, and is an independent power that is not related to the power of the Security Force Court for punishing offences like absence from duty without leave as per the Border Security Force Act, Sections 11, 19, and 62.
The Commandant's decision to terminate the respondent's employment after one year of absence from duty following the proper procedures under section 62 was found to be valid by the Union of India's appeal.
