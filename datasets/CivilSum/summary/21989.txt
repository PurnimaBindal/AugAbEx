Karnataka Public Works Engineering Department Service (Recruitment) (Amendment) Rules, 1985 - Retrospectivity of Rules - Promotion - Cadre - Promotion out of the cadre - Rule making authority should not be allowed to act in the past without discrimination - Rules made with retrospective effect for appointment to a higher cadre by transfer from a back date where the official has never worked, without any regard to the seniority of already working employees is discriminatory and violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
[Paras 5 to 7] B.
Transfer and Recruitment by transfer is normally made in the same cadre - Transfer in a different cadre must not breach Constitutional guarantee and rule of fairness for others - Appointment only on the basis of bare eligibility is neither reasonable nor fair.
[Para 6] C. Recruitment/Selection - Selection by test - Recruitment is comprehensive and includes any method for inducting a person in public service - Direct recruitment includes appointment by transfer.
