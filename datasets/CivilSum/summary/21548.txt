Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 14 and CCS (Pension) Rules, Rule 3(i), (ii) and (iii), and Rule 9 - Grave Misconduct - Inquiry - Respondent had provided no legitimate reason for his absence or refusal to join at Delhi - Inquiry Officer found that the respondent had purposely disobeyed Government orders and gave false excuses, including feigned illness and his wife's illness, to avoid duty - The Inquiry Officer also found that the respondent had premeditated his conduct and had already purchased property in London, indicating that he had no intention of returning to Delhi - As such, the Inquiry Officer found the respondent guilty of grave misconduct - The charge of grave misconduct has been correctly proven and the order of 18th December 1984 cannot be challenged.
