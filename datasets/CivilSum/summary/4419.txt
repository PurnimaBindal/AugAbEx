According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, a person who does not meet the qualifications required for the post cannot be appointed.

Under Article 16 of the Constitution of India, a person cannot be denied promotion on the basis of having a Prathama certificate instead of a Secondary examination certificate, if others have been promoted with the same certificate.
[Para 7]
As per Article 14 of the Constitution of India, an employee's qualifications are considered at the time of initial entry into service.
After gaining experience on the job, qualifications lose importance, and confirmation cannot be denied after a long period of employment for lack of qualifications.
