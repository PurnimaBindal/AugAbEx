Constitution of India, Article 16 - Temporary appointment - Re-appointment for a period of three months - Absorption - Termination - Stay of - College taken over by Govt - A temporary employee for a limited period cannot claim regular appointment upon takeover by the Govt. - The petitioner was not employed by the College on the date of the Govt.'s decision to take over - The petitioner is still employed due to a stay of termination - As such, the petitioner is not entitled to be absorbed into Govt. service.
