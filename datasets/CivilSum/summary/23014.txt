In Writ Petition (Civil) No. 1338 of 1990, under the Constitution of India Article 226, a second writ petition was filed with undue delay.
The cause of action for the petitioner arose in 1970, but the petition was not filed until 1990.
The petitioner's explanation for the delay was deemed entirely unsatisfactory, and the Tribunal upheld the dismissal of the writ petition.
