Army Act, Section 123 - Pension Regulations for the Army 1961, (Part 1), Regulations 3, 4, 5, 16-A - Cut in pension - Respondent retired after grant of certificate of satisfactory service with CAG and Public Accounts Committee making adverse observations leading to a cut of 50% in pension – Respondent argues principles of natural justice and misconduct needing to be established by departmental or judicial proceedings for a reduction in pension – Held, opportunity of hearing should be effective and not perfunctory, the decision-making process is improper and appeal is dismissed.
