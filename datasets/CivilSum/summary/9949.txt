The Labor Court found that the termination of a workman's services was invalid under section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act because the workman had worked for more than 240 days prior to termination but was not terminated in compliance with the Act.
The workman was ordered to be reinstated with full back wages, and when the parties settled on reinstatement with continuity of service, the period during which he had not worked was counted towards his service.
As a result, the workman had completed more than 240 days of service and the Labor Court was justified in directing his reinstatement with full back wages.

The petitioner worked for the Department of Animal Husbandry which was ruled to be an industry under section 2(j) of the Industrial Disputes Act.
