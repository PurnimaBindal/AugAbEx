Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation - Qualification - Ad hoc appointment - Simply because an individual is appointed in an ad hoc capacity in a post, that shall not bestow any right to be regularised against the post.
Court cannot instruct to regularise a person against a post for which he lacks the obligatory qualifications.
For the post of Librarian, a qualification of diploma in Library Science is required, and the petitioner was appointed on an ad hoc basis without the mandated qualification of diploma, and extension thereof time to time.
