Advocates Act, 1961, Section 35 - Misconduct of an advocate - Suspension - Practising under a changed name during a three year suspension is a serious offence - The Advocate responsible deserves to be penalized - The suspension period is excluded from the punishment and a fresh date for the three year suspension must be determined by the Bar Council.

Practice and procedure - Issues - Pleadings - Natural justice - Parties cannot object to the lack of specific pleading if they were conscious of the issue, went to trial, adduced evidence, and had the opportunity to cross examine witnesses on that issue.
