Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Termination - Enquiry - Natural justice - Petitioners who worked as Special Police Officers were terminated due to allegations of teasing a lady - No enquiry was conducted - Petitioners were not involved in fact-finding - It was decided that petitioners were entitled to a fair and reasonable opportunity as per the principles of natural justice - The challenged order was overturned.
