The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F, G and H, and Industrial Dispute (Central) Rules, 1957, Rules 77 and 78, cover termination of service for workmen.
In a case where the workman raised a dispute a decade after termination and a reference was made four years later, with the workman providing detailed monthwise working, the management failed to produce records and an adverse inference was drawn.
It was held that despite the delay, the period of working of the respondent was 271 days, and the argument of being prejudiced by delay and the burden to prove 240 days' working misconceived in the facts of the case.
Given the circumstances, compensation of Rs. 50,000 is granted instead of reinstatement.
