The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 governs the termination and reinstatement of employees, including the grant of back-wages.
However, in a recent writ case, a mutual compromise was reached between the parties involved, and a co-operative society has agreed to pay 50% of the petitioner's last pay drawn as compensation.
While the petitioner has been reinstated, they are not entitled to any other sum except for the compensation.
The workmen, on the other hand, are entitled to a payment of 50% of their last pay drawn as compensation due to the mutual compromise.
These decisions are in accordance with the Constitution of India, Articles 226/227.
