According to the UP Recruitment of Dependents of Government Servants (Dying in Harness) Rules, 1974, Rule 2(c) and Articles 16 and 309 of the Constitution of India, the word "Family" in Rule 2(c) encompasses a dependent divorced daughter who relies on her father for support.
Such a divorced daughter should be treated equally to an unmarried or widowed daughter as they are all considered to be their father's responsibility as part of his family.
