Co-operative Societies (Kerala) Rules, Rule 176 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10(1)(d) - An employee has the right to have their complaint reviewed by an independent forum, like a tribunal or labour court, with the necessary legal power, after they have tested all departmental procedures, if they are subjected to disciplinary action - Ruling: In this case, the employee was not given a chance before the Cooperative Arbitration Court because it was not notified; therefore, the referral of the dispute by the government before the Industrial Tribunal is valid.
