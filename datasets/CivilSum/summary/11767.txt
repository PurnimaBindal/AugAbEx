In accordance with Sections 2(oo) and 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the case concerns retrenchment due to the respondent-workman's abandonment of his job without notice.
However, as the respondent had voluntarily abandoned his position, it has been deemed a case of voluntary abandonment, hence not subject to the aforementioned provisions.
The petitioner's case has been allowed.
