Punjab Public Works Department (Irrigation Branch), Patwaris State Service, Class III, Rules 1955, Rule 12(a) - Appointment process involves passing of Patwar examination and registration of name on Circle Register.
Candidates from the register will be posted in permanent vacancies.
However, if a candidate remains unposted till the age of 25, his name will be removed from the pass candidate list and he will have to compete with fresh candidates.
