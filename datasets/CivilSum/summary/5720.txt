Seniority - Ad hoc service - In order for ad hoc service to be counted for the purpose of seniority, it must be shown that the appointment was against a regular vacancy.
1995(1) Recent Service Judgments 382 (FB), 1993(3) RSJ 113 and AIR 1993 SC 1564.
[Para 7]
Promotion - Past experience on ad hoc service - Seniority - In the event of a junior person being eligible for promotion due to past experience on ad hoc service, they should be promoted on an ad hoc basis until their senior attains prescribed experience to avoid supersession.
Seniority cannot be granted for temporary positions.
Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 100.
1986(4) SLR 1 referred.
