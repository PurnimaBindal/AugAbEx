Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Selection Grade - Petitioner, a lower division clerk promoted to upper division clerk on 14.12.1989 - A circular issued by the finance department stating that eligible employees could be granted selection grade as of 191988 - Petitioner denied selection grade on the basis of an adverse entry and appealed the decision - Court granted selection grade with effect from 16121989, the date of promotion to UDC - Petitioner appealed court decision, claiming selection grade from 191988 - Held, if the petitioner was fit for the selection grade, the date of promotion to UDC (16121989) was not applicable - Order of appellate authority without consequence and the date from which the selection scale is granted be fixed as 191988.
[Paras 3 and 6]
