Constitution of India, Article 311(1) - Punishing Authority - Enquiry Officer - Ideally, the enquiry should be conducted by a higher-ranking officer than the accused – However, if the enquiry officer is of the same rank or higher, and this does not harm the accused in any way, it does not count as an irregularity that would result in an injustice - The final order must be given by the appropriate punishing authority - The order handed down by the punishing authority was deemed valid.
