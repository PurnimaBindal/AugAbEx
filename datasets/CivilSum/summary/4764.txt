Constitution of India, Article 14 - Transfer/instructions - Change of Conditions of service - The petitioner was appointed as a Clerk with the condition that he would only be posted within the metropolitan area of Calcutta.
However, after the bank where he was employed (United Industrial Bank) was amalgamated with another bank (Allahabad Bank), the petitioner was transferred out of the metropolitan area.
This transfer constitutes a change in the conditions of the petitioner's employment and is therefore illegal and arbitrary.
The Allahabad Bank has no right to make changes to the petitioner's employment conditions, overriding the instructions of the Reserve Bank of India.
