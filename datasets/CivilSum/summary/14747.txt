Punjab Public Works Department (Irrigation Branch) Zilledars State Service Class III Rules, 1955, Rule 20 - Stop-gap arrangement/Reversion/Qualifications - A promotion made as a stop-gap arrangement may be allowed to continue for more than ten years.
However, reversion after ten years on the grounds of not possessing the required qualification is invalid.
[Para 6]
Promotion made on a stop-gap arrangement allowed to continue for more than ten years may be deemed to have been made by relaxation of qualifications in accordance with rules.
Therefore, reversion on the ground of not possessing the requisite qualifications is not valid.
[Para 6]
