The Constitution of India's Articles 226 and 300-A and the Pension Regulation Act, 1961 govern disability pensions.
In the case of a petitioner suffering from mental illness, specifically Schizophrenia, it must be noted that at the time of their discharge, the Medical Board had not mentioned the disease to be undetectable when they entered service.
However, the disease is deemed attributable to military service, and as such, the petitioner is entitled to a disability pension, including arrears for the 38 months before they filed their petition.
