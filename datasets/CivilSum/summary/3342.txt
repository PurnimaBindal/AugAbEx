Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 226 - Guru Nanak Dev University Calendar-1999, Vol.
I, Statute 74 - Absence from duty - Over-stay of leave - Indiscipline - Unjustified long over-stay of leave constitutes unauthorised absence from duty and a grave instance of indiscipline, which must be prevented - Petitioner sought extension of leave on multiple unjustified grounds after exhausting all available types of leave - Mere application for leave cannot rationalize unauthorised absence - Writ court denied the petitioner's request in light of their behavior.
