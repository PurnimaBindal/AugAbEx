In accordance with the Limitation Act, 1963 and Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 47 Rule 1, the time spent acquiring a certified copy of the judgement to be reviewed must be excluded when calculating the limitation for filing a review application.
It is also not necessary to file the certified copy along with the review application because it can be filed later on.
[Para 3]

When an order is based on admitted facts and documents, there is no need to frame issues for disputed questions of fact.
Therefore, the order made was neither erroneous nor contrary to any Service Rules.
As a result, the Revision Petition had no merit and was dismissed.
