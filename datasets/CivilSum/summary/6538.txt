"According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, a person who has committed forgery is not allowed to use the writ jurisdiction of the Court, and no sympathy should be shown to such a person.
The petitioner in this case forged her marksheet to show herself as higher in merit and eligible for admission.
However, if the forgery is discovered by the authority and the petitioner is declared disqualified from admission, the authority was justified and the petitioner cannot claim any harm done to her.
[Para 7]"
