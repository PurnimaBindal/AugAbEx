Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination of services - Petitioner claimed to have completed 240 days of service - Respondent failed to present supporting evidence - Labour Court relied on the statement of an Assistant Engineer who testified without any record that the appellant was only employed for three months despite refusal to summon records - The award of Labour Court was nullified, and the matter was sent for fresh decisions.
