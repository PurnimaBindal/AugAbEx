According to Paragraph 1221 of Punjab Police Rules, if an appellant is charged with absence, it is considered a stigmatic order if no opportunity of hearing was given to them.
It is also stated that in cases where a delinquent official is charged with absence, the punishment authority must provide an opportunity to the appellant before passing the order of discharge.
Ultimately, the Learned appellate authority's order from April 2nd, 2004 is set aside and the appeal is granted.
