According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, pay scales can be classified based on qualifications.
It is justified to grant different pay scales based on qualifications for a post.
If a post requires highly technical skills in engineering, individuals possessing a degree in engineering can reasonably be given a higher pay scale than those who were promoted or appointed based solely on their experience on lower posts, even if they lack the requisite qualifications.
