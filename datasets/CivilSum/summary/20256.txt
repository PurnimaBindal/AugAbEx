The appellant, who was appointed as a volunteer in Eastern Railway, was terminated when the scheme was withdrawn.
The tribunal directed that they should be treated as casual employees with temporary status from the date of the order.
However, the order was not a recognition of their right but a solution to the human problem.
They are not entitled to benefits from the date they acquired temporary status.
Furthermore, simply because employees of one division received the wrong benefit, it is not appropriate to extend the same benefit to another division.
[Paras 4 and 5]
