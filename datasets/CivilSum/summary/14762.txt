Constitution of India, Articles 14, 311 and 311(2) - Ad hoc Promotion/Reversion/Natural Justice - When promoting employees, correct facts must be brought to the notice of the authority.
If a mistake is found, the authority can rectify it and reconsider the promotion.
In the case of reverting a person who was wrongly promoted, no notice or opportunity of hearing is required.
Reversion due to rectification of a mistake or error does not involve Article 311(2).
