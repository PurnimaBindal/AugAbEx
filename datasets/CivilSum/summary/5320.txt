Constitution of India, Article 226 - Departmental/Criminal Proceedings - Termination - Alternative Remedy - Petitioner was dismissed after a departmental enquiry following an acquittal in a criminal case on appeal.
Petitioner challenged termination by way of writ, but the court held that since the dispute involves relief based on the general law of contract, the civil court has alternative jurisdiction.
Jurisdiction lies within the court since the Petitioner has not raised any plea involving enforcement of any right created under the Industrial Disputes Act.
[Paras 5 and 7]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Benefit of Acquittal - Departmental/Criminal Proceedings - Petitioner claims benefit of acquittal in criminal case on the same set of facts that were raised during departmental proceedings.
Respondent objects, stating that the acquittal was not based on merit, but rather fear of doubt.
The enquiry officer has found the Petitioner guilty in departmental proceedings.
The court held that the judgement of acquittal can help the concerned employee, but only if it contains a positive finding to dislodge allegations and charges leveled in departmental proceedings, and not otherwise.
The petition is dismissed accordingly.
[Paras 6 and 8]
