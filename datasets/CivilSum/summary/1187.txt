Haryana State Education Lecturers School Cadre (Group C) Service Rules, 1998, Rules 3 and 9 - Haryana State Education (School and Inspection Cadre) (Group B) Service Rules, 1998, Rule 9(1)(d) and (2) - Punjab Civil Service Rules Volume I Part I, Rule 3.12 - The petitioner challenged the deletion of his name from the seniority list of lecturers as he had been promoted to the position of lecturer on an ad hoc basis and then as Headmaster.
However, it was held that the employee acquires lien on the post only when he is confirmed and made permanent, thus, the petitioner did not acquire lien on the post of lecturer and his lien on the post of Master never ceased.
The petitioner's claim was hit by the principle of estoppel and delay and latches as he had accepted and enjoyed the fruits of the post of Headmaster.
No relief could be granted in the absence of a prayer to quash the promotion of alleged junior lecturers who were promoted as principal.
Petition dismissed.
