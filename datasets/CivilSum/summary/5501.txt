According to Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a workman is an employee who performs both manual and clerical work.
The salesman in IFFCO, whose job is neither clerical nor managerial, is considered a workman.
The finding of the learned single Judge that he is a workman is upheld.
[Para 6]
In relation to Section 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, termination on the ground of misconduct, such as mentioning wrong experience and conducting sales of urea on lower rates than prescribed, warrants restoration of termination.
The Labour Court ordered the reinstatement of the employee with 50% backwages, but the single Judge reduced it to 25%.
However, the matter concerning the employee's utter misconduct has not been given proper consideration.
Hence, the orders of the single Judge and Labour Court are set aside, and the termination is restored.
[Paras 7, 8, and 9]
As per Articles 14, 16, and 226 of the Constitution of India, experience is always to be considered on the date of application and not any date after the person has been allowed to join the service.
