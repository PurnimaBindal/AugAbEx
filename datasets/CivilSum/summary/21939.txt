According to Bihar Pension Rules, Part 2 Appendix 5-2(iii), a Government servant must complete 10 years of service in the State Government to be entitled to pension proportionately.
The respondent in the case was appointed as a Government servant in June, 1950 and sent on deputation to the Appellant-Board in August, 1959.
He was subsequently absorbed in the Board and retired from service.
The High Court directed the Board to pay pension and recover proportionately from the Government.
It was unclear whether the respondent voluntarily went on deputation.
The appropriate course would be for the respondent to make a representation to the Government, who would consider and dispose of it with a speaking order, according to the law.
