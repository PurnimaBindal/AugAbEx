Ashok Jindal, Advocate and additional standing counsel for UOI, argues that under Constitution of India, Article 16 and Army Instructions, a Non-Commissioned or Junior Commissioned Officer with 10 years' service and Matriculation qualification was eligible to be selected for permanent Commission (Special List) through SS Board.
However, the petitioner was not selected for permanent Commission (Special List) as Quartermaster, nor considered for it as Record Officer, despite applying for both.
The Final Selection Board rejected the petitioner's claim for selection as Quartermaster due to lack of experience.
The instructions state that a candidate is assessed for different posts based on one interview.
The respondents are directed to consider the petitioner's case for permanent Commission (Special List) as Record Officer, on the basis of the interview already held for the post of Quartermaster along with other relevant material.
