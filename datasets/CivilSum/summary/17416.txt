Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Past service - Employees of aided colleges - Transfer - According to the Rules, the seniority of lecturers in a college is determined from the date of their joining service.
This can be either through transfer or direct appointment after resigning from another college.
It must be noted that unless the management initiates the transfer, the transferee or direct appointee cannot surpass those who are already in service of the college.
This is because one may resign from their previous institute for various reasons that disentitle them of the benefit of their past service.
It is important to follow the Rules regarding seniority in service.
