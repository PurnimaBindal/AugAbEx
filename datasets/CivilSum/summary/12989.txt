Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - Examination - Special chance - Petitioner was unable to appear in two papers due to an accident and bed rest - Requested a special chance to clear the left over papers - VC denied permission to appear in supplementary examination - Court held petitioner was not at fault for being absent and permission was already granted to appear in supplementary examination based on peculiar facts and circumstances - Petitioner's appearance in supplementary examination to be viewed as regular.
