The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, and 39, allows for equal pay for equal work, but this doctrine cannot be applied if the educational qualifications or source of recruitment are different.
Individuals with varying qualifications cannot be considered equals, and maintaining different pay scales for positions with different duties is not a violation of Article 14.
Moreover, the doctrine of equal pay for equal work cannot be applied to provide relief to employees who are not legally entitled to it.
