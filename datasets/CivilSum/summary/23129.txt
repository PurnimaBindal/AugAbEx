Air Force Act, 1950, Section 189 - Air Force Rules, 1969, Rules 15 and 18 - Discharge from service - Discharge under the Policy for Discharging of Habitual Offenders under Rule 15(2)(g)(ii) of Air Force Rules as habitual offender - The Policy for Discharge aims to remove recurring offenders from the Air Force, as their past misconduct renders them unsuitable for further retention - The suitability for retention in the Air Force is determined based on the previous record of service and punishments imposed - Therefore, the discharge cannot be considered as a termination of service as a punishment for misconduct under Rule 18.
