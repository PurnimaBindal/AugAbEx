According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the appropriate government cannot deny reference on grounds of delay.
The government's function is administrative and not judicial or quasi-judicial, and it cannot delve into the merits of the dispute or make a determination of the lis, as this would exceed its conferred powers.
The issue of delay must be considered by the Industrial Adjudicator when answering the reference.
