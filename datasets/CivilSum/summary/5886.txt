According to Sections 2(oo), 10, and 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the applicant's appointment was conditional on the Governing Body's approval for six months.
However, the Governing Body did not approve the relaxation in appointment, and despite allowing the applicant to work beyond the period specified in the appointment letter, there was no valid order of extension provided.
Based on this, termination in accordance with the appointment letter cannot be considered as retrenchment.
The labour court's award, which determined the termination to be illegal and violative of Section 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act, should be quashed.
Even a workman can file a petition for the High Court to quash an award when there is relief granted in their favor by the Labour Court.
