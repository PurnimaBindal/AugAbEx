Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Orissa Ministerial Service Rules, 1985, Rules 6 and 11(1) - Selection - Advertisement indicated 15 vacancies - The select list contained the names of 66 successful candidates - 40 of those candidates were appointed - 14 candidates moved the Tribunal - The Tribunal found that selecting a panel of candidates comprising more than vacancies violated Orissa Ministerial Service Rules 1985, but the selected candidates should not be punished - the Tribunal ordered appointment of the left-over candidates - The Tribunal's decision was upheld.
