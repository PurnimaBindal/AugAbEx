Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Combined Service Examination Rules, 1989, Rule 15 - Appointment - Recruitment - Rule 15 amended - Recruitment to be made in accordance with Rule 15 - Candidates selected for a department should be appointed on existing department vacancies - If there are no vacancies or the candidate is medically unfit for that department, they may be adjusted in another department.
