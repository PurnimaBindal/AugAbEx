Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punishment and Appeal Rules, Rule 17 - Charge sheet - Voluntary retirement from service - A charge-sheet was issued under Rule 7 to a petitioner who had remained absent from duty from 351991 and he acknowledged it on 31101994 - The petitioner claimed that he had sent a letter treating it as a three-month notice of voluntary retirement, but it was not received by the respondents - Voluntary retirement was not accepted by the government as the petitioner had not completed the minimum of 20 years qualifying service on 3151991 and was charge-sheeted under rule 7 - Petition seeking voluntary retirement from service was dismissed.
