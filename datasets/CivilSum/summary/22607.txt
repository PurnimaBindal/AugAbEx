Cooperative Societies Act, (West Bengal) 1983 (45 of 1983), Sections 95, 97, 134 to 137, 25 and 87 - Cooperative Housing Society - A writ petition can only be maintained against a housing society if it can be proven that a mandatory statutory provision of a statute has been violated.
The fact that the society is governed by a statute alone is insufficient grounds.
A writ petition regarding the allotment of parking spaces to members of a housing society is not maintainable.
[Para 8]

Constitution of India, Article 19(1)(f) - Protection of fundamental right to hold property - The right to hold property cannot be protected if the title to that property is in dispute and is the subject of adjudication in legally constituted proceedings.
The question of whether rights in or to that property have been improperly or illegally infringed can only arise after the title has been established.
