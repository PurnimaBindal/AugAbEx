Indian Penal Code, Section 33 - Objectives and Scope.
[Paras 10 and 11] B Indian Penal Code, Section 33 - An employer has the discretion to take action against an employee, including dismissal or discharge.
- If the actions of the employer aren't approved by a tribunal, the employer is obliged to continue treating the employee as an employee.
[Para 16] C Employer and Employee - An employer's dismissal order is considered incomplete and changing until it's authorized by the tribunal.
- Whilst the employee-employer relationship can be terminated, the de jure association can only terminate when approved by the tribunal.
