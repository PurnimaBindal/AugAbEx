Article 16 of the Constitution of India is discussed in regards to employee promotions and allegations of infringement on promotion rights.
The appellant claims their juniors were promoted unfairly, but the record shows their cases were considered and they were found unsuitable.
It is determined that the constitutional rights of the appellants were not infringed upon and the judgement and order of the High Court is quashed with the appeal being allowed.
