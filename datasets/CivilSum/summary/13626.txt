Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Civil Service Medical Class-II - Members of service - Pay during post graduate course - All candidates undertaking post graduate courses must receive full pay during their program, without discrimination based on prior use of such facilities or length of service within the organization - The increased responsibilities of candidates during their course make it necessary for full salary to be provided, regardless of any other factors.
