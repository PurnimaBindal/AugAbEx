Constitution of India's Article 16 entails Service termination of a probationer.
The order of termination under challenge is only one line, labelled "Not sincere," which stemmed from misconduct and amounted to stigma.
The individual was not afforded the opportunity to defend themself.
Per AIR 2000 SC 1080, a probationer, like a temporary servant, is entitled to certain protection.
Principles of natural justice must be adhered to before the service can be terminated.
The writ petition was allowed, and the termination order was quashed along with consequential benefits [Paras 4 and 6].
