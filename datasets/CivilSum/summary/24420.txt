Under UP Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(g) and 6(N), an employee must have uninterrupted service for at least 240 days in one completed year to qualify for continuous service.
In this case, the respondent worked for only five months and is not entitled to any relief.
High Court judgment based on the Section 25-B definition is impermissible and has been set aside.

Despite this evidence, High Court concluded that due to unemployment, the respondent had no choice but to continue practicing for a short period, resulting in entitlement under Section 17-B However, if the payment has already been made, the respondent need not refund it under the peculiar circumstances of the case.
