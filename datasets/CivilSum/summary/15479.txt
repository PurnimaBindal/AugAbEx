Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 - Compassionate appointment - Married daughter - Claim - Permissibility - Deceased's married daughter applied for compassionate appointment, but was rejected - Tribunal denied it, stating that the widow was already receiving a monthly pension and the family had three residences - Contention that the daughter's husband was unemployed and her mother had to support them - Court held that married daughters are part of their husband's families and therefore are ineligible for compassionate appointment when their father passes away - The petition was dismissed accordingly.
