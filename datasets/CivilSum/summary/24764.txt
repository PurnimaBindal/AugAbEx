The Industrial Dispute raised by Co-operative Marketing Federation employees was declared to fall under unfair labour practices according to Schedule 4, Items 6 and 9 of the Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act of 1972.
Despite being declared permanent employees by an Industrial Tribunal, Marketing Federation and its Transferee society still fail to provide permanent status to the workers.
[Paras 8, 9]
According to the same Act and Schedule, the complaint of unfair labour practice regarding the denial of permanent status to the workers despite the Industrial Tribunal's award was dismissed on the ground of being filed beyond the 90-day limit from the dates of appointment letters.
However, the dates of service of the appointment letters were not proven, and the dismissal of the complaint without giving a chance to explain the delay was unjustified.
[Para 10]
As per Schedule 2, Item 6 of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, a permanent employee of Co-operative Marketing Federation working in its Cotton Department cannot be refused absorption by the new organization that acquired the Cotton Agency without any reasons.
[Para 12]
