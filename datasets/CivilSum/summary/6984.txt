Sections 75(1)(g) and 75(3) of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948 and Sections 2(k) and 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 were considered to determine whether a specific establishment was covered by the 1948 Act.
The jurisdiction of the Industrial Tribunal was questioned, and the tribunal ruled regarding the case under Section 33-C(2) while considering it as secondary to the main issue.
The ultimate conclusion is that proceedings under the 1948 Act are required for coverage of establishment under the Act as per its scheme.
The provision for Insurance Courts under the Act implies that jurisdiction of Civil Courts/Tribunals is impliedly barred.
Hence, the Tribunal had no jurisdiction to decide the coverage of the organization, and its order was made with manifest illegality.
