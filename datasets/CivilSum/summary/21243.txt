Constitution of India, Articles 320, 335 and 16(4) - Recruitment in State Medical services - The Selection Commission is empowered to pick the most competent and efficient candidates for the job.
It is not permissible for the Commission to prescribe minimum qualifying marks for shortlisting.
Only a certain number of candidates can be chosen based on merit.
Disqualifying candidates at the shortlisting stage based on minimum qualifying marks will deprive eligible candidates from participating in the selection.

Constitution of India, Articles 320, 335 and 16(4) - Recruitment in State Medical services - The Selection Commission has to follow the Policy matters of the State Government and the standards and conditions prescribed in the Service Rules.
They can only follow their own procedure for a fair selection in accordance with the Rules and Policy of the State.
The agency cannot prescribe standards different from those in the Rules and reservation policies of the Government for different categories.
The Rule making agency must ensure that the health services take care of people's lives.
