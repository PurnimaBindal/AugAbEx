The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, along with the Punjab Civil Services (General & Common Conditions) Rules from 1994, state that passing a Punjabi type test is necessary for direct recruitment.
However, this condition is not applicable to the appointment by promotion of clerks as per the 1994 Rules.
Additionally, passing the Punjabi type test cannot be a precondition for promotion.
[Paras 3 & 4]
