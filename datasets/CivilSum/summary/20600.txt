Sections 25-N, 25-O, 25-FF and 25-FFF of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Workmen options - Closure and transfer of Delhi industry to Baddi in Himachal Pradesh - Court orders workmen to be treated as continuous employees until the industry restarts at the relocated place - No need for workmen's options to join or not to join at Baddi - Non-response of workmen is irrelevant - Those who join at the relocated place would receive full salary and continuity of service, while those who don't join would receive retrenchment compensation under Sec. 25F(b) - Workmen who join would receive one year's extra salary to compensate for resettlement.
