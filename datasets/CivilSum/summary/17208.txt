Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Reversion - Petitioner reverted to the post of ad hoc clerks from Assistant - Both the petitioner and private respondent had been promoted as a clerk from class IV posts, as a peon - Later, the petitioner was promoted to the post of Assistant and was senior in the class IV seniority list - However, the private respondent claimed that the petitioner had passed matriculation after them and should be treated as junior, leading to the petitioner's reversion - Nonetheless, the petitioner was still eligible for the post of clerk when the first vacancy opened up, being senior in Class IV and having matriculation qualification - Thus, the petitioner is entitled to be regularized as a clerk from 381982 and the private respondents on subsequent dates - The order of reversion and cancellation of regularisation as clerk w.e.f.
3181982 is therefore set aside.
