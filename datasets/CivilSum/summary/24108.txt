Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10, 12(3) and 18(3) - Settlement - Pension Scheme - A clause of settlement that limits the benefit of pension to those who retire after 24-8-1996 is challenged - It is contended that all workmen belong to one class and there cannot be sub-classification among them - However, the settlement arrived at during conciliation proceedings is binding on all parties to the dispute - Unless the objectionable portion is shown to have outweighed all other advantages, the court will be slow to deem a settlement unjust and unfair - The fixation of a cut-off date for entitlement to pension cannot be deemed arbitrary or irrational, as it is imperative from a financial point of view - Consequently, the appeal is dismissed.
