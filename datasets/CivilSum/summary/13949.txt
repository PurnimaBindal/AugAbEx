According to Section 47 of the Persons with Disabilities Act of 1995, the provisions stated are mandatory.
An employee who incurs a disability after joining service may not have their service dispensed with merely because of their incapacity to work due to the disability.
It is not necessary for the accident resulting in the disability to have arisen out of or during employment, and it is enough if the employee acquires a disability during their service.
[Paras 5 and 6] B.
In addition, the provisions of Section 47 of the Persons with Disabilities Act of 1995 override Standing Orders that enable a company to discharge an employee if they are found to be medically unfit.
