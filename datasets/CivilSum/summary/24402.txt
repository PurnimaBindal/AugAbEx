Constitution of India, Article 309 - Service Rules - Draft Rules - The rule remains in force until it is repealed, replaced or amended even if it becomes unworkable - No promotion or appointment can be made in derogation to the existing rules just because draft rules are in the process of finalization - Draft rules cannot be followed when validly framed rules are already in force - Rule cannot be enforced retrospectively.
