Constitution of India, Article 311 - Joining time - Transfer from one place to another - Not relieved - Representation filed - Later on relieved - Joined after seven days - Issued chargesheet for remaining absent for seven days - Dismissal - The petitioner must have been given reasonable time to join - Following the principles in Civil Service Rules, the dismissal is quashed.

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Suspension - Dismissal - Joining at posting station after seven days from relieving station - Charged with remaining absent - Dismissed from service - The order of transfer made during the suspension period was not deliberated - No work be given during suspension - Only presence is required - A few days of absence can be treated as leave - The dismissal order is quashed.
