According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, (First Amendment) Rules, 1994, Rule 25, Note-I and Chapter VII, Annexure-A, as well as Punjab Financial Rules, Vol.- I, Annexure-A to Chapter VII Rule 73, there is no time limit for the correction of the employee's date of birth.
If an employee mistakenly stated their date of birth orally, they cannot be punished for delay and laches or the principle of estoppel as long as they did not use this mistake for their own benefit.
There is no time limit for correcting the date of birth as per para-materia to Note-I to Rule 25 of Pb.
CSR Vol-I and after the omission of the condition of limitation in Rule 73 of F.Rs.
