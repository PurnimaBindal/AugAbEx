Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Civil) Recruitment Regulations, 1965, Regulation 9 - Amendment in regulation to fill 75% of the posts of AE from among Degree Holders - Challenge as to increase of quota by Diploma Holders - Further amendment of Regulation 9 during pendency of writ contemplating 50% quota for direct recruits, 32% promotion quota for Diploma Holders and 18% for Degree holders - Court held that respondents did not act illegally in filling up posts of direct recruits from amongst degree holders and that the court would not interfere in exercise of writ jurisdiction.
