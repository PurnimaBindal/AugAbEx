Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2-A (2) (as incorporated by AP Amendment Act 34 of 1987) - Workman - Termination order - Appeal - Limitation - Jurisdiction of Labour Court - Under Section 2-A(2), workmen are allowed to approach the Labour Court directly to challenge dismissal, removal or discharge, instead of moving the State Govt. for reference - There is no time limit for challenging such orders - Section 2-A(2) can be invoked to challenge orders of dismissal even before the incorporation of the section into the Act, and any delay in challenging the order can be taken into consideration for determining relief.
