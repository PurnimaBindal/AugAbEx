In accordance with Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 18, common proceedings may be conducted when charges pertain to acts of omission and commission pertaining to one incident, even if two or more government servants are concerned, and even if one or two articles of charge are common, attracting the application of Rule 18.
Additionally, evaluation of evidence should be made with regard to the charge against the delinquent, and evidence against one government servant cannot be used to find another government servant guilty of misconduct.
Hence, common enquiry is permissible.
[Paras 18 to 20]
