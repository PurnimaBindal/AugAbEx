Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission/Education - State Government has the power to prescribe the method and procedure for admission to Medical Colleges through executive instructions - Judicial review of selection is only possible if there is a legal infirmity or if the criteria is unreasonable or violative of Article 14 of the Constitution - Merit of the candidates is determined based on complete years of service in rural or urban areas - Fractions of years cannot be considered for awarding marks - No legal infirmity in this criteria.
