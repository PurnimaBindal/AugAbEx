Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Remand - Selection - Promotion - If a selection or rejection made by an authority in selection matters is found to be incorrect, after the order has been quashed, the court must remand the matter to the relevant authority if it consists of experts and has considered various merits and demerits.
However, if a candidate is wrongly rejected, despite being found fit for promotion, the court may direct the authority to promote the petitioner once all facts are admitted, and remand in such cases is only a ritual and ceremonial act.
