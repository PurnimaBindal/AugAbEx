Constitution of India, Article 226 - Life Insurance Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1960, Regulations 16(1) and (2) - Employee promotion to a higher post after putting one year service within the period of two years - A continuous one-year work period is not mandatory for the promotion - The petitioner completed more than one year in a higher post of Assistant Divisional Manager before the promotion's extended period expired - The employee's taken Privilege Leave must be counted as duty - No distinction is made between probationer and approved probationer - Revision of petitioner's promotion ground is not valid since no adverse remarks on her performance in promotional post - All officers reported high performance ratings for petitioner - Directions have been given to consider the promotion of petitioner by relaxing regulations due to her excellent performance.
