Constitution of India, Article 136 - Advocates Act, 1961, Section 35 - Misconduct of an advocate - Removal from the rolls of Advocates - The proved misconduct involving litigation with personal interest and affecting 150 persons from the advocate's village has resulted in a debatable quantum of punishment - The punishment of removal from the rolls is deemed excessive due to its extreme impact - The court suggests a suspension of 7 years instead.
