Constitution of India, Articles 16 - Demolished Armed Forces Personnel (Reservation of Vacancies in the Punjab State Non-Technical Services) Rules, 1968, Rule 3 -War Service benefits/Notified vacancy - If the government employs someone under concession rules and proceeds with it, the employee is entitled to receive benefits for the war service period, even if the vacancy was not notified to be reserved - As a result, the petitioner was granted all benefits for their war service period.
