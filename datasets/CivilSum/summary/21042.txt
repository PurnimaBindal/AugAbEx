According to Rule 20 of the Punjab Industries Service (Class-II) Rules, 1992, promotion to the post of Assistant Director requires five years of service.
The petitioner, though performing the duties of an Assistant Director since 11/1977, was not eligible until 1980.
As a result, he must be ranked junior to Assistant Directors appointed in 9/1979 in direct appointments.
