Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Caste certificate - Not issuing caste certificate - Natural justice - The refusal to issue a caste certificate cannot be regarded as the annulment of an already-issued certificate.
There is no need for any show-cause notice or violation of natural justice principles.
Additionally, in rare instances, natural justice rules can be revised or disregarded.
