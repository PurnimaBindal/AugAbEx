Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Regularisation - Petitioner claims eligibility for regularisation according to Government circular - Govt. requires employee to have worked for 240 days a year without breaks of over 30 days for regularisation - Petitioner states he worked over 240 days during last five years - Respondents did not file written statement - Court orders respondents to consider petitioner's case for regularisation - Regularisation of petitioner's services if he fulfilled condition of working 240 days per year for last five years.
