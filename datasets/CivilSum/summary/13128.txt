Based on the Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311(2), and the Central Industrial Security Force Rules, Rule 34, an employee cannot be dismissed without a reasonable opportunity to defend themselves and the presentation of a charge sheet.
In a case where an employee was dismissed due to unauthorized absence, it was found that there was nothing on record to prove that a charge sheet was served, and the enquiry report did not recommend any punishment.
Therefore, the Disciplinary Authority did not adhere to the requirements of Article 311(2) and Rule 34, and the employee must be reinstated.
