Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Voluntary retirement/Withdrawal of request - An employee may withdraw their voluntary retirement request before it becomes effective, but this is dependent on the employer's acceptance - The act of seeking voluntary retirement is not compulsory for the employee, nor is acceptance compulsory for the employer - Therefore, if the employee changes their mind before the effective date, they may reverse their decision - The petitioner's petition has been allowed and they shall be considered to still be in service.
