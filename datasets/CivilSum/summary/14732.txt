Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Termination/Reinstatement/Voluntary retirement/Backwages/Legal representatives - The court overturned the dismissal order of the petitioner who was accused of absence from duty and ordered that the petitioner be treated as on duty.
However, the petitioner was not permitted to join the service even after giving three months' notice for voluntary retirement.
Consequently, the petitioner was deemed to have retired prematurely and, following the petitioner's death, the petitioner's legal representatives will receive all monetary benefits from the voluntary retirement.
