Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol. II.
(4th Amendment) Rules, 1984, Rule 914(1)(c) - Gratuity - Judicial proceedings - According to Rule 2.2(b), judicial proceedings include criminal proceedings for offenses such as cheating or forgery that may lead to conviction.
The employer is allowed to withhold the gratuity until the resolution of such proceedings, and determine if the employee caused a loss that necessitates reimbursement from the gratuity.
It is important to note that the gratuity cannot be withheld during any other judicial proceedings against the employee, and any compensation must include 12% interest for late payment.
[Paras 4 to 6] B.
While withholding gratuity payments under Rule 914, there is no consideration given to an employee's positive service record.
[Para 7] C. If a retiring employee is involved in judicial proceedings defined by the rule, the competent authority must withhold their gratuity.
No prior notification is required.
