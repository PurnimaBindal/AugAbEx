Constitution of India, Article 14 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Allegations against workman - Labour Court can only consider chargesheet allegations - Non-mentioning of particular charge does not always occasion failure of justice - Consideration of misconduct without notice can vitiate order if charge is separable from extra facts but punishment can still be upheld if originally justified.
[Paras 17 to 19 and 21]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Dismissal from service - Proportionality of punishment - Charge of armed entry, property damage, and confinement to Manager is sufficient to justify dismissal.
[Paras 19, 20 and 24]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Criminal and Disciplinary proceedings against the workman - Labour Court not bound by criminal court verdict but cannot ignore it - If evidence justifies dismissal order, it can differ from criminal court.
[Paras 21 and 22]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Settlement with co-workers - Settlement with some workmen is no ground for interfering with punishment of those who did not accept settlement, if serious charges are proved.
