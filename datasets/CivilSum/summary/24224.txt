The Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226, along with the Companies Act of 1956 and the Banking Regulation Act, including Sections 22, 35A, 3615, 15A, 18A, 18AA, and 46A are all relevant in the context of disciplinary proceedings and actions, particularly the dismissal of a bank employee.
While public banks are considered an instrumentality of the state and subject to the government's control, private banks running commercial activities are not subject to the same level of control.
Therefore, a writ petition challenging disciplinary actions against an employee of a private bank is not maintainable unless the petitioner seeks to enforce a statutory obligation upon the bank.
In those cases, a writ can be issued under Article 226 of the Constitution.
