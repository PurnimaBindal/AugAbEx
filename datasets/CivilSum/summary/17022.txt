Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Early retirement - Retirement benefits - Govt authorities must consider the petitioner's eligibility for entitlement before accepting the request for voluntary early retirement - Denying retirement benefits due to insufficient qualifying service is only justifiable if the employee misrepresented themselves - Mistakes in calculations by authorities cannot be used to deny an employee their dues - Only the official committing the mistake can be penalized - Govt officials must properly verify an employee's service record before granting permission for voluntary retirement since the record remains with them - Manorma Rai v. The Secretary to Govt., 2000(3) RSJ 89 followed.
