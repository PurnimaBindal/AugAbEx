Constitution of India, Art. 14, 16 - Right to employment - Character antecedents - Petitioner's father provided shelter to a Pakistan militant during his service - Petitioner's brother is also a Pakistan trained militant - Petitioner was arrested but released without any charges - The court held that despite the petitioner's family history, he cannot be denied employment after being selected as he cannot be punished for the actions of his father and brother.
