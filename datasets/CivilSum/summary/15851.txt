Constituttion of India, Article 226 - Clause 7 Category G-1 - Admission - Reservation - Defence Quota - The petitioner was not granted MBBS admission in the Defence Quota as per her merit, while respondents No. 8 and 9 were granted admission because they fell in category G-1 of clause 7.
The court held that the in-action referred to death or injuries sustained while fighting the nation's battle against internal or external forces.
On the facts, the admission given to respondents 8 and 9 was not proper, and respondent No. 8 must go, considering his compassionate case.
The admission granted to two respondents should not be disturbed.
However, the petitioner must be given admission forthwith, and in case she joins up within a week of receiving a certified copy of this order, no technical reasons should impede her from sitting for the examination.
As such, the petition is allowed.
