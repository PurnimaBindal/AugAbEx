The eligible persons affected by excessive reservation have the right to file a writ petition under Article 226 of the Constitution to challenge the same, as per the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226.
[Para 8]
Reserving seats for rural candidates without proper data as required by Article 335 of the Constitution and exceeding the ceiling limit of 50% reservation without compelling reasons is against the fundamental rights of others, as per the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16(2), 16(3) & 16(4).
[Paras 12 to 17, 19 and 20]
Reservation of 33% for women horizontally is permissible as long as it does not exceed 50% reservation, as per the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 15, 16(3) & 16(4).
