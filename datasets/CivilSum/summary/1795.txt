The frequent change of policy instructions regarding the correction of an employee's date of birth cannot be appreciated under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol. 1, Part I, Rule 2.5 and Punjab Financial Rules, Vol.
I, Rule 7.3 as amended on 21.6.1994.
An employee cannot claim the right to change the date of birth in their service record at any time, as it may adversely affect the rights of juniors awaiting promotion.
Belated claims should not be entertained, and courts will not issue directions to change an employee's date of birth unless there is conclusive proof, and rules must be strictly complied with to avoid prejudice to third party rights.
