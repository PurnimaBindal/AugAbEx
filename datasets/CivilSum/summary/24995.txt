According to Sections 3 and 114 of the Evidence Act, 1872 and Sections 10 and 121 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, photostate copies have evidentiary value.
However, in a case involving embezzlement of funds and a charge against an employee, photostat copies of entries in a shareholder's passbook were produced without the original and no explanation was given for its absence.
In this situation, as per Para 3, the photostat copies cannot be accepted without the original.
