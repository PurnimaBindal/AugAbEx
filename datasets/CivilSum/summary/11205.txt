According to the Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 226, a reserved post for the SC community cannot be filled by someone from the Kotegar Community, as they are not recognized as an SC Community.
The petitioner had previously benefitted from appointment with SC category, but is not entitled to a promotion in the same category.
However, the government order ensures that the benefits already attained under the SC category, which includes the initial appointment and the subsequent promotion, are still preserved.
