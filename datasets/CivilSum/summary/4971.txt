Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pendency of criminal case - Stay of departmental proceedings - Permissibility - It is at the discretion of the disciplinary authority to stay or continue proceedings in a case where an employee is facing criminal charges - The court cannot intervene in the discretionary orders, except in cases where the discretion has been exercised in a biased or unreasonable manner - The advisability and propriety of holding departmental proceedings need to be evaluated taking all relevant factors into account - In the present case, given the charges, it is deemed inappropriate to stay the departmental proceedings.
[Paras 11, 13 & 14]
