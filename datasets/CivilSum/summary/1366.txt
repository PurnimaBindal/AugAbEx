State Bank of India (Supervising Staff) Service Rules, Rule 51(3) - Domestic Enquiry - Limitation for Review - The reviewing authority of the bank must request for the records within six months of the final order in regards to the expiration of the sub-rule (3) of Rule 51 for review.
Moreover, the limitation for reviewing the order passed in domestic enquiries is six months.
