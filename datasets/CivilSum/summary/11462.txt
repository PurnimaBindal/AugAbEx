Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300-A - Pension Regulations, 1995, Regulations 28 and 29(5) - Voluntary Retirement Scheme (VRS) - The petitioners, who took voluntary retirement under the Scheme of 2000, cannot receive benefit from Regulation 29(5).
They must be regulated according to the spirit of the Scheme of 2000 as they form a distinct and separate class.
