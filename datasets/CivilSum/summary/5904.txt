Respondent- Chief Standing Counsel.
The petitioner and another officer serving on deputation are similarly situated.
While the other officer was absorbed in the department, the petitioner was repatriated to the parent department without any reasons given for the same.
The order of repatriation cannot be upheld.
This is based on Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311.
Support comes from 1991(1) SCT 317 and AIR 1985 SC 651.
