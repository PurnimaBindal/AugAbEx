Police Rules, 1960, Rule 359 - Absence from duty - Discharge from service - Natural justice - Respondent had discharged the petitioner from service without affording him an opportunity of being heard which is not justifiable action and the impugned order has been quashed.
[Para 6] B.
Practice and Procedure - Discharge - Natural justice - Employees are entitled to a reasonable opportunity to show cause.
They can deny their guilt and establish their innocence, which they can do only when they know the charge levelled against them.
[Para 6] C. Termination - Reasonable opportunity - When the Govt. employee is absent without leave, without reasonable cause - Termination of service without giving him an opportunity of show cause - is illegal.
