Constitution of India, Article 16 - Tamil Nadu State Higher Judicial Service Rules, 1982, Rules 2(b) and 7(b) - Seniority - The purpose of direct recruitment is to introduce new talent into the service to improve efficiency, and bar members choose direct recruitment to increase their chances of being elevated to High Court Judges.
To determine the placement of the appellant as District and Sessions Judges, Grade II, Rule 7(b) must be used to establish the inter se seniority of the appellants and promotees, based on their continuous officiation in the posts of District and Sessions Judges, Grade II.
