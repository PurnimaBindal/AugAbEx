Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 1 Rule 10 - Seniority - Delay and laches - Necessary party - A petitioner requested direction to promote him to leading hand in response from his junior's promotion date - In 1983, a seniority list was published and was not contested then - Five years later, the petitioner filed a Petition without including his alleged junior - The Court refused to address the petitioner's concerned after his alleged junior earned two promotions and dismissed the Petition.
