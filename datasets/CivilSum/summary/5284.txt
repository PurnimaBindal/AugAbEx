Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 9-A - Alternative job - Reduction in wages - Petitioner suffered from pre-existing bodily ailment and had an accident while working contributing to unfitness for job - Employer accepted request to assign lighter work subject to reduction in basic wage which petitioner did not consent to - Unilateral wage reduction without complying with Section 9-A is not permitted - Employer's option is to decline alternate job or grant alternate job subject to consent of workman to receive attached emolument - Directing employer to pay 50% of differential benefits petitioner entitled to prior to accident meets end of justice based on facts and circumstances.
