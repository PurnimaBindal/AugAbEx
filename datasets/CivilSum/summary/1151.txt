Constitution of India, Article 14 - Kerala Civil Judicial Service Rules - Selection - Qualification - Post of Munsiffs in the Kerala Judicial Service - The notification prescribes a minimum qualifying marks of 30% for viva voce test, which is considered legal.
Those who applied pursuant to the notification were aware of the conditions of selection.
Any person who applied and took the chance of being selected cannot question the legality of the selection process.
