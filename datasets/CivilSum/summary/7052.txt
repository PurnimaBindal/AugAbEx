Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Workman - Termination of service - Award of Reinstatement - Challenge regarding - Retrenchment compensation allegedly inadequate - Management claimed compliance with Section 25 - Labour Court did not clearly state how retrenchment compensation of 60 days was considered insufficient - Held, the Labour court erred by dividing the average monthly salary by 26 instead of 30, considering the workman only had to work for 26 days - The workman was paid wages for one month and one week day, which was considered an off day that would also be included in calculating the salary - Rule indicates that 15 days of average pay is to be given for each complete year - Award is overturned.
