Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment in Electricity Board - Apprentice trainees under the UP State Electricity Board disputed the advertisement issued by Electricity Board stating that even apprentices would need to be sponsored through employment exchange and appear in the written examination - Apprentices are permitted to be exempt from being sponsored through Employment Exchange, but they are not exempt from appearing in the written test, as declared in the Askand Kumar Srivastava case (1999 All.
LJ 770).
[Paras 10 &14]
