The University must mention in the advertisement for applications that qualifications must be met on a specified date.
[Para 11] C. Himachal Pradesh Universities of Agriculture, Horticulture and Forestry Act, 1986, Section 12(1) - The Rule's purpose for selecting a person for the post of Dean or Director of Research refers to three Scientists or Educationists with relevant experience from outside the University.
The expression "outside the University" does not exclude any person affiliated with the University in any capacity from the Selection Committee.
