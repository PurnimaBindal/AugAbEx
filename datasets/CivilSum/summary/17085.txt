According to Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, pay scale should be consistent.
In a situation where a workman designated as a Salesman was paid the salary of a Cashier, but management did not object for five years, it is reasonable to assume that they had accepted the workman as their employee and approved his proper salary.
Therefore, the management cannot dispute his employment after such a long period.
