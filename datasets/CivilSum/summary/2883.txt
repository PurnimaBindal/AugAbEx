Constitution of India, Article 16 - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 2(e), 14 - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rules 26(1), 49(1) - Central Government employees - Those who resign cannot be denied gratuity under Rule 26 of CCS Pension Rules as Rule 49 allows gratuity to those who retire prior to completing ten years of service - Rule 26(1) must be read in conjunction with Rule 49(1) so that a harmonious reading of both rules together can be achieved.
