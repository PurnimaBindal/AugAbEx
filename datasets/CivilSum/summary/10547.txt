The Industrial Disputes Act, 1894, Section 10(1)(c) details that disputed facts cannot be used as a reason for the government to decline making a reference regarding the Industrial Dispute/Reference.
The Labour Court has the sole authority to make decisions on whether the workman worked for 240 days with the management or not - the government cannot interfere in this matter.
Moreover, the government cannot decline reference of the dispute based on these disputed facts on their merit.
If there is a delay in the demand notice, it can be explained or may only involve a forfeiture of wages for that period.
