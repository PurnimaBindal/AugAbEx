Constitution of India, Articles 311(2) - Haryana State Electricity Board Employees (Punishment and Appeal) Regulations, 1990, Regulation 7(6) - Departmental proceedings - Punishment of dismissal - Challenge as to - Petitioner argued that combined show cause notice in respect of findings on charges proved and proposed punishment violates principle laid down in MD, ECIL's case - However, the proviso to Article 311(2) of the constitution does not require giving the delinquent an opportunity to represent against the proposed penalty, and this provision has not been found ultra virus.
Rather, it has been found that an opportunity must be given to the delinquent to represent against the findings recorded by Enquiry Officer before the disciplinary authority acts on the report.
The petition is dismissed.
