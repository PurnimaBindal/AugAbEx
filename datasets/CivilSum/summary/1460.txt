Industrial Disputes Act, Section 17-B - Gainful employment - Self- employment - If a workman is only engaged in self-employment to provide for himself and his family, it cannot be considered gainful employment - Without proof of gainful employment in an establishment, a workman cannot be denied the benefits of Section 17-B - Failure to comply with Section 17-B may result in the dismissal of the management's writ petition.
