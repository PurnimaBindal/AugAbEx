Sections 15 and 7(1) Explanation II of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936 state that deduction from wages on account of punishment for a good and sufficient cause cannot be held as unauthorised.
The authority under the Act cannot reverse the punishment order.
It operates like an executing court and lacks jurisdiction to see if the employer followed proper procedure or not while imposing the punishment.
Only if a punishment has no cause or reason can a claim be made under Section 15 of the Act.
According to Baldev Singh's case (Supreme Court), Nanak Singh's case, despite being contrary by a single judge of the High Court, does not represent the correct law.
