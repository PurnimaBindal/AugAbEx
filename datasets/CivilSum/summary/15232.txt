Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 9.18(2) - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rules 5.32 - Compulsory retirement of Sub Inspector Police - Invoke Rule 532 of Punjab Civil Services Rules Volume II and Rule 3.26(d) of Volume I for compulsory retirement without notice or consideration of representation, violating Note-II below Rule 9.18(2) of Police Rules - Notice and representation mandatory for natural justice - Compulsory retirement void due to arbitrary exercise of power and violation of natural justice principles.
