Haryana State Cooperative Bank's Staff Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1976, Rules 83 and 86 - Ad hoc appointment - Ad hoc appointment under rules 83 and 86, which the authority allowed to continue despite expiry of initial term, cannot be considered illegal or contrary to rules - Once authority allows employee to continue, they cannot deny benefit of continuity and length of service on the basis of notional breaks given by the authority itself to balance rules - Notional breaks cannot constitute break in service resulting in forfeiture of entire service prior to such breaks.
