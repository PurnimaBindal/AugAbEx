The Constitution of India, Article 30(1) and various rules governing basic school recruitment and conditions of service explain that minority institutions established by Jainis are entitled to protection and status under the Constitution.
Such institutions do not require prior approval of the Education Officer for removal of faculty by the management.
[Paras 18, 24, 30 to 33, 36 to 38, 51, 55 and 56]
While minority institutions have additional protection, they are not immune to necessary measures to regulate their functions.
[Paras 51 to 53]
The law established by a Division Bench of the same High Court must be followed by a single judge of the High Court, according to Articles 215 and 226 and Article 30(1) of the Constitution.
[Paras 15, 33 and 55]
