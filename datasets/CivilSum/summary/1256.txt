Constitution of India, Articles 16, 39(d) and 226 - Pay scale - Industrial Tribunal's Award - The petitioner-University has been directed to pay the employees of Shramik Vidyapeeth at par with its own employees.
- The principle of equal pay for equal work must be followed.
- Shramik Vidyapeeth is an autonomous body, with its staffing pattern and pay scales determined by the Central Government.
- The use of the petitioner-University's premises was temporary.
- The issue of appointment orders and adherence to rules and regulations did not make the appointees employees of the University.
- The respondents cannot claim benefits meant for the University's employees, and interchangeable transfers and appointments do not establish Shramik Vidyapeeth as part of the University.
[Paras 16 and 20]
