Admission to B.Sc.
Medical courses in different categories must indicate order of preference for various courses on the application form, as per Clause 12(g) of the prospectus for admission to para-medical Courses for Session September 1998 issued by PGI Chandigarh.
If a seat in the course of first choice is available, as per Clause 12(g) of the prospectus, candidate cannot be allowed to change the order of preference and must be first offered admission in course for which they have given preference at No. 1.
Petitioner was deprived of admission in his preferred course due to respondents being given admission in the course of their choice without adhering to the order of their preferences given in their application.
PGI is instructed to make room for the petitioner for admission in that course by cancelling admission of candidate lowest in merit or by creation of a seat.
