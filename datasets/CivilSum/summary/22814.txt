Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Dismissal of workman - Approval of Tribunal - Failure to comply with conditions in proviso to Section 33(2)(b) renders the dismissal invalid and the workman entitled to back wages - If approval is refused and not challenged, workman is deemed to have continued in service with entitlement to all back wages - No second application for approval is maintainable without compliance of earlier refusal order.
