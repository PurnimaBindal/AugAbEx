Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference of dispute to Tribunal - The government instructed BRP to employ at least one member from families displaced by BRP's acquisition of land.
In this case, two sons of a displaced person were already employed by BRP, and a third son applied for employment through the employment exchange but provided false information to meet eligibility requirements.
The offer of employment was revoked after the concealment of facts was discovered, and the individual was never employed.
The government later made a reference of dispute, which was challenged by BRP.
The court ultimately allowed the appeal and set aside the judgment of DB due to the claimant's dishonest actions.
