Constitution of India, Article 16 - Double jeopardy - The petitioner was charged with taking a library book and was debarred from using the library for six months as interim measure for initiation of disciplinary proceedings.
However, the punishment of stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect was awarded later during the disciplinary proceedings.
The court held that the petitioner cannot claim double jeopardy as the interim measure and the punishment were two separate proceedings.
[Para 71]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Disciplinary proceedings - Imposition of punishment - Principles of natural justice - The petitioner was charged with taking a library book and admitted to the offense.
The court held that for such a learned man in a high post, personal hearing or copy of the enquiry report is not required if there is no prejudice to the concerned person.
In this case, the petitioner's written admission was sufficient and personal hearing was unnecessary.
Therefore, the court concluded personal hearing is a myth in such circumstances.
