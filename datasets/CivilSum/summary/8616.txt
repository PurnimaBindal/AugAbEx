Section 100 of the Civil Procedure Code deals with the computation of pension and retirement benefits.
There was a question about whether the two years of extra service given to the respondent by court order should be calculated towards his pension.
However, the appellant did not bring up this issue in their appeal or before the trial court.
It is important for the appellant to be aware of their rights and act accordingly.
The findings of fact based on proper appreciation of evidence are not normally disturbed by a court in a regular second appeal.
The appeal is dismissed.

Correction in date of birth and repayment of salary are the issues addressed under Section 100 of the Civil Procedure Code.
The respondent worked by taking advantage of the decree passed in his favor for the correction of his date of birth.
The appellant paid him accordingly for the period he worked.
It is a settled principle of law that if an employee has actually worked and received salary under the order of court or otherwise, it is not fair or just to demand repayment of salary even if relief is declined later on.
