Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Qualification - Post of Radiographer - The usual rule is that equivalence of qualifications for the post of Radiographer should be determined by the appointing or competent authority when there is a question of equivalence of qualifications - There are no prescribed qualifications for the post of Radiographer by any statutory rules - Petitioners have failed to establish that they hold the required qualifications for the post of Radiographer so advertised by Board - As a result, the petitioner is not entitled to any relief.
[Paras 7 and 8]
