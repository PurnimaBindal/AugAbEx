Constitution of India, Article 311 - State Bank of India Act, 1955, Section 43 - Disciplinary proceedings and criminal proceedings - A Bank Official was accused of granting undue pecuniary benefits and loan sanctions to relatives - Simultaneous initiation of criminal prosecution and disciplinary proceedings - Official was subsequently acquitted of criminal charges with a clear finding that his actions were inadvertent errors - The official's misconduct was not willful or fraudulent - Therefore, the punishment of dismissal imposed in the disciplinary proceedings was inappropriate - The authority has been directed to reconsider.
