Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Advertisement - Selection/Recruitment - Selection beyond the advertised posts for prospective vacancies without advertising additional vacancies - Additional vacancies can be advertised before the selection is held - Future prospective vacancies cannot be advertised - Selection against the unadvertised vacancies is illegal - However, selection against the additional vacancies available and advertised before selection is valid - Vacant posts or vacancies arising subsequently should be notified to all those who became eligible after advertisement and before the occurrence of additional vacancies.
[Paras 9, 10 and 13]

Selection/Waiting List - Future vacancies - Recruitment - Waiting list is meant for filling up only left out vacancies for non -joining of selected candidates from selection list - Therefore, no appointment can be made from waiting list against the additional or future vacancies.
[Paras 11 and 13]

Constitution of India, Article 141 - The law laid down by the Supreme Court is binding on all selecting agencies and Authorities - All Courts subordinate to the Supreme Court and all authorities are bound to follow the mandate of Judicial pronouncements of the Supreme Court, as per Article 141 of the Constitution.
[Para 12]
