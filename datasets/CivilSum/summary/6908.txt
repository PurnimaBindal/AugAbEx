Constitution of India, Articles 16, 311 and 309 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control, and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 16 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1996, Rule 61 - Employee disappearance for more than seven years - Dismissal on the ground of absence.
The widow and child of the government employee who disappeared more than seven years ago are entitled to pension, retrial benefits and compassionate appointment.
An FIR on missing was lodged by the employer, and the employee was not traced despite the enquiry by CIB (CB).
Statutory presumption under Section 108 of Evidence Act was raised once seven years expired from the date when the employee went missing.
According to Rule 268-B, family pension would not await the expiry of seven years for raising statutory presumption.
The respondents are directed to release pension and other benefits from the date of completion of one year when the government servant went missing, with interest @9% per annum, and to consider the case of the son for compassionate appointment.
The order of removal against a person not alive is quashed.
