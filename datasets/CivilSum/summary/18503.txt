According to the Punjab Panchayat Secretaries (Recruitment and Condition of Service) Rules, 1979, regularisation was denied to the petitioner and termination was upheld.
The appointment of the petitioner was not made as per the rules/instructions and a similarly appointed junior has been serving for the past two decades.
Under the given circumstances, the claim of the petitioner is not valid.
However, the junior cannot be removed from service at this stage to prevent immense hardship to him and his family.
