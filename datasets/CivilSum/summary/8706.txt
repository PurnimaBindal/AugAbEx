Terminating an employee without conducting an inquiry on the basis of embezzlement and gross negligence allegations would amount to retrenchment under Sections 2(oo) and 25(G) of the Industrial Disputes Act.
Even if the worker served for less than 240 days, Section 25(G) recognition of ‘Last Come First Go’ must be followed.
As juniors were retained, the worker’s termination violated Section 25(G), and the labour court’s award was upheld.
