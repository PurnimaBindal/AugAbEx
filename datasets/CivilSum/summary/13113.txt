Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Central Civil Service (Fixation of Pay of Re-employed Pensioners) Order 1986 - Fixation of Pay - When a person who has retired before reaching 55 years of age and is re-employed, the rule is to fix his pay at the same stage of the last pay drawn before retirement.
The Rule does not mandate pay fixation at the minimum of the pay-scale for the post.
