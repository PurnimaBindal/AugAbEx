Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 226 - Selection List - Duration of select list - An appointee who was duly selected cannot be rejected on the basis that the duration of select list had lapsed during litigation - The authority cannot benefit from the delay caused by its own actions - The candidate successfully argued their case in court - The authority was defeated in the Supreme Court - The authority must adhere to the order.
[Paras 8 & 10]
