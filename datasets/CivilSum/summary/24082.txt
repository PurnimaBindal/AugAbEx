Medical Council Act, 1956, Sections 10A, 10B and 10C - Dentist Act, 1956, Sections 10A, 10B and 10C - New medical colleges must be approved by the Central Government, which has the power to grant or refuse approval, according to the institutions' compliance with mandatory laws and regulations.
To maintain the standards of Medical Education, institutions are required to submit a scheme that aligns with the Act and regulations.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Indian Medical Council Act, 1956, Sections 10A, 10B and 10C - Dentist Act, 1956, Sections 10A, 10B and 10C - High court does not possess the jurisdiction to direct the government to act in violation of the rule of law and procedures that are mandatory for the benefit of medical education.
A direction to increase seats, even if it is to adjust already admitted students, is not valid without a submitted scheme or consideration of the infrastructure available.
