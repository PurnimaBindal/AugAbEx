Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Fundamental Rules, Rules 53(1)(ii)(a) and 9(12) - Suspension - Subsistence allowance - Revision of pay during suspension - The suspended employee is eligible to receive a subsistence allowance based on the revised pay scale.
This allowance is necessary for sustenance during suspension since the employee may not be able to seek employment due to the continuation of the contract of service.
[Paras 9, 20, 25 and 26]
