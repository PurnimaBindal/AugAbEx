The appointment of the sixth respondent as Deputy Registrar in the Jamia Millia Islamia Act, 1988, is being challenged due to the Selection Committee's actions.
The respondent-university tried to justify their selection with grounds not mentioned in the minute of the selection committee.
Evidence provided does not reveal how many marks were given in the interview process, and the Committee was not authorized to relax the qualification requirements for a postgraduate degree.
The act of the Selection Committee is deemed as arbitrary, unjust, and unfair, and is therefore set aside.
