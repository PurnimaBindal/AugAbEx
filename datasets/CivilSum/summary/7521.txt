Sections 25-H and 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Retrenchment - Fresh appointment - If retrenchment of a workman is found to be illegal but he is not reinstated due to poor financial condition of the management, this is not a valid reason to deny him the benefits of Section 25-H at the time of making a fresh appointment to the same post - The workman will be deemed to be appointed with all benefits from the date of the fresh appointment.
