The decision by the appropriate authority to grant or deny a permanent commission to an officer is subject to review by the High Court under Article 226 of the Constitution of India because it can have civil consequences.
In the petitioner's case, the court considered the policy in effect along with other eligible officers.
The legality of the policy was not questioned, but non-selection is subject to review by the High Court.
The court cannot substitute its judgment for that of the selection board, and non-selection cannot be deemed arbitrary, illegal, or discriminatory.
[Para 9] B When confronted with a statutory complaint under Section 27 of the Army Act, 1950, the government must address it and dispose of it in accordance with the law.
This is not a mere formality.
Despite the lack of reasons given, the Central Government rejected the statutory complaint and confirmed the decision made by the Selection Board.
The selection process was not tainted by malice or arbitrariness, and the government disclosed its reasons in the counter-affidavit.
