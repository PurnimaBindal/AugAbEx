Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - The petitioner suffered an accident while on casual leave.
His disability was assessed at 60%.
The disability pension was declined due to the assumption that the disability wasn't caused by Army service since he was on casual leave.
However, the petitioner was still in service when the injury occurred.
It was held that the disability was a result of Army service and the petitioner is entitled to disability pension.
[Paras 5 and 8]
