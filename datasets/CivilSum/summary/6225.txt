The appointment procedure for contractual clerks was different from that for regular hands, and therefore, they cannot claim regularisation even if they have continued in such employment for long years as the original appointment was not made by following due process of selection under the rules.
This was held in reference to the 2006(2) SCT 462 (SC) case.
[Para 5]
The petitioners who were inducted in service as clerks on contractual basis were not subjected to the regular process of selection.
Requisition of names from Employment Exchange alone does not satisfy the requirement of Articles 14 and 16(1) of the constitution.
Consequently, the petitioners are not entitled to regularisation of their services.
This has been held in reference to the (1996)6 SCC 216 case.
