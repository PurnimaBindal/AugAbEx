Constitution of India, Article 226 - Calcutta State Transport Corporation Employees Service Regulations, Regulation 36 - Charge sheet - The validity of the charge sheet cannot be disputed merely because the charged employee has good defence against each of the specific charges.
Instead, the merits and factual truthfulness of the charges should be decided during departmental proceedings, not while determining the validity of the charge-sheet in writ proceedings.
The delinquent must still be given an opportunity to defend against the charges and there are no grounds to quash the charge-sheet.
[Paras 2, 3 and 5]
