Staff nurses who are allowed to join the B.Sc.
(Post Basic) Course at PGI are considered "on duty during the period of training and will be paid pay and allowances as per rules".
However, despite this condition, they were treated as being on leave and not on duty.
The petitioners had completed the necessary requirements for selection and had joined the course after being duly relieved by the authorities under whom they were working.
Previous courses had been approved by the government, and the government's repeated orders show that they have a continuous practice.
There are no grounds for departure from this practice, and there is no basis for distinguishing the case of the petitioners from that of other persons who have been deputed in earlier years.
The respondents' actions are discriminatory; the petitioners are required to suffer detriment and the respondents have promised a benefit that they cannot withdraw under the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226.
