Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 918 (as applicable to Haryana) - Compulsory retirement - Adverse remarks - Rule is meant for inefficient or corrupt officers, and Note 2 under Rule 918(2) only applies when the power is exercised - Appointing authority can retire an officer at 55 without reasons.
