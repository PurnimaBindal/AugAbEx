The petitioner, who had been working as a Clerk since 1971, had been assured by school authorities that he would be absorbed into the Class III post when it was sanctioned.
However, the petitioner was an approved Group-D staff member relying on a circular for the absorption of staff appointed before the norms for teaching and non-teaching staff were established by the government.
It was held that this circular could not be relied upon, and there was no rule that allowed for a change in the status of an employee.
Therefore, the petitioner could not claim relief for absorption as desired, as per the Constitution of India, Article 16 and 226.
