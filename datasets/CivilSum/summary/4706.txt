The petitioners in the Lecturer post selection cannot claim that their possession of the NET qualification made them eligible and should be appointed.
The notification allowing exemption from the NET/State Level Comprehensive Test was given and the interview criteria were based on it.
The petitioners did not object to the exemption at any point.
Even though they were unsuccessful in the interview, they cannot now argue that the NET test is a prerequisite and that the State Government was incompetent to allow the exemption.
Therefore, the petitioners are not entitled to any relief.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]
