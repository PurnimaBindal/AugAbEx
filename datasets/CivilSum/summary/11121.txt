According to Punjab School Education Board Act, 1969, Section 24, seniority and promotion are linked.
In the instant case, the appellant was selected for the Manager post and was confirmed from the date of joining the post.
The Board's resolution of March 21, 1979 stated that the 11th vacancy for the post of Superintendent was to be filled by promoting Managers in the Text Books Sales Depot.
The suit seeks a declaration that the 11th vacancy for promotion to the post of Superintendent falls in the share of Managers at roster point 41.
The court held that the Board is bound by the resolution dated March 21, 1979.
The suit was decreed, and the Board was directed to promote the petitioner to the position of Superintendent against the vacancy falling at roster point 41.
If that vacancy is not available, the petitioner is entitled to the next available vacancy in the Managers cadre.
[Para 10]

According to Punjab School Education Board Act, 1969, Section 24, resolutions are decisions and not proposals.
The resolution dated March 21, 1979, is also an executive instruction in the absence of rules and regulations.
The petitioner was eligible to be promoted and appointed against a vacancy of Superintendent from the quota meant for Managers when a vacancy at roster point 41 occurred.
The resolution dated January 24, 1984, is prospective and not retroactive.
[Para 8]

According to Punjab School Education Board Act, 1969, Section 24 (1), the Board could not refuse to enforce the executive instructions of the resolution dated March 21, 1979, only because it had not been notified under Section 24 (1) of the Act.
