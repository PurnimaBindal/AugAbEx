Madhya Pradesh Bhawan charged guests for lodging and boarding and organized systematic activities through cooperation between employer and employees, which suggests it can be carried on by a private agency.
Thus, the Bhawan is considered an industry under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and functions by the Madhya Pradesh Government cannot be considered sovereign.
