The petitioner is eligible to seek admission to the MA-I examination under Ordinance 16, as long as they possess a recognized university degree with at least 50% marks, and the Ordinance does not require any equivalence granted by the respondent-University.
The action of the respondent University in declaring the petitioner ineligible is in clear contravention of the Ordinance 16.
Respondents are directed to regularize her admission and allow her to continue her studies.
[Para 5]
The decision of the Academic Council that a BA degree of Punjabi University awarded after two years duration after passing the Gyani/Prabhakar examination be not recognized cannot override the Ordinances, as all Academic Council decisions are subject to the provisions of the Ordinances under Section 15 of the Guru Nanak Dev University Act, 1969.
The decision could only be implemented by amending the ordinances and not otherwise, and hence the decision of the Academic Council cannot be implemented.
