Constitution of India, Article 16 - UP Secondary Education Commission and Selection Board Act (Act 5 of 1982), Section 33 - Appointment - Filling up of short terms vacancy - The procedure for notifying a vacancy on an institution’s notice board is violative of Article 16 and deprives competent candidates of an opportunity to apply for the post.
However, the management has the power to fill up short term vacancies without prior approval from the District Inspector of Schools.
