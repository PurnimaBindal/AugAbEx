The Department of Revenue (Custom Appraisers Recruitment) Rules, 1988 - Promotion - Appraiser Post – Validity of 20% quota fixed for Preventive Officers (PO) compared to 75% quota fixed for Examining Officers (EO) - Post for different categories have different factors to be taken into account when setting quotas - Cadre strength is not the sole criterion for parity in promotion prospects among holders of feeder posts - Since POs have two ways of promotion, 100% to the post of Superintendent and 20% of promotion to Appraiser, and EOs have only one way of promotion with a quota of 75% for promotion to Appraiser, the two classes of people holding the two posts have different conditions of service and nature of duties, and can be treated differently by the promoting authority - No legal or constitutional infirmity in the lower quota fixed for POs compared to EOs for promotion to the post of Appraiser.
[Paras 15, 17, 22, 24] B.
Promotion - Although the right to be considered for promotion on a fair and equal basis without discrimination can be considered as a legal and fundamental right under Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution, chances of promotion themselves cannot be demanded as a matter of right.
