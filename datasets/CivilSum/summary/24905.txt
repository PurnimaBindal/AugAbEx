Constitution of India, Act, 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 15 - When the High Court interferes in an award passed by the Industrial Tribunal, it should not do so on overly technical grounds.
[Para 10]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10, 15 and 3 and Item 1 - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dock Workers (Regulation of Employment) Act, 1948, Section 2(b) - The question of whether the recommendations of a Wage Board for Port and Dock workers are applicable to bargemen, who may or may not be considered as dock workers, is an incidental question that the tribunal has jurisdiction to decide.
