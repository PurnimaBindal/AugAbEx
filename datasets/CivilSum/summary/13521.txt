Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Medical treatment - Reimbursement of medical expenditure incurred on By-pass Heart Surgery - Petitioner underwent open heart by-pass surgery at Escorts Heart Institute and Research Centre, New Delhi and submitted original bills, but the matter remained pending with departmental authorities - An amount equal to expenses for similar treatment at All India Institute of Medical Sciences was sanctioned - It was held that petitioner is entitled to reimbursement for the expenses incurred at Escorts Heart Institute and Research Centre, New Delhi - If payment is not made within the time stipulated in the order, petitioner will be entitled to receive payment of interest at the rate of 15% per annum.
