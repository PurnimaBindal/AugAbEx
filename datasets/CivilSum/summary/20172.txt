Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Industrial dispute - Pay scales and service conditions - A scheme has been formulated to streamline and revise pay scales and other service conditions for officers and employees in the General Insurance Company pursuant to the Central Act.
The scheme also includes a resolution mechanism for disputes.
Industrial Tribunals are tasked with adjudicating the reference and resolving the dispute in accordance with the principles outlined in the scheme, including determining whether the categorization of the workman is correct.
However, the tribunal does not have the authority to prescribe pay scales or classifications that would restrict its jurisdiction.
