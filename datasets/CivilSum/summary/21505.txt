Nagpur Improvement Trust Act, 1936, Sections 6, 4(1)(e) and 4(2) and Section 10 - Removal of Trustees - Ex-officio Trustees - Nominated ex-officio Trustees - The appointment and removal of ex-officio nominated trustees under Section 4(1)(e) and 4(2) is entirely dependent on the State Government - The doctrine of pleasure is applicable, and no principle of natural justice or opportunity for removal is required - Only if the removal is stigmatizing under Section 10, will the procedure under Section 10(3) apply, giving the incumbent an opportunity to respond - Section 6 does not have a corresponding provision, but the doctrine of pleasure is implicit.
