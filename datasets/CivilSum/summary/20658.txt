Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay protection/Absorption - Those who were working as N.D.S.
Instructors with the Central Govt. were temporarily appointed as PTE/IPEs in the Public Instruction (Education Department), Haryana on July 1, 1976, and absorbed permanently in October 1976.
As per clause 10 of their appointment letter, they were entitled to pay protection.
They were entitled to the same pay scale as available in the Haryana State and allowance as available in Central Government till their permanent absorption, and the state government's extra expenditures incurred would be reimbursed by the Central Government till they remained in the cadre of the PET only.
