The Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, outlines unfair labour practices and provides measures to prevent and remedy them.
When interpreting this social welfare legislation, the relevant provision should be construed to further its purpose.
Unfair labour practices include not only the ultimate action of dismissal, removal, or discharge but also any firm step taken towards that action.
The Labour Court has jurisdiction to entertain complaints based on the employer's present continuous action engaging in any unfair labour practices.
The Act provides both preventive and remedial measures, including the ability to pass restraint orders preventing such actions.
The Labour Court should carefully analyze complaints and only pass interim orders when a strong prima facie case is made.
