Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2, 25-G and 25-N - Writ petition - Employer contends that writ petition challenging retrenchment notices is not maintainable due to disputed questions of fact - However, employer-employee relationship is the subject matter of the writ petition itself, and there are no disputed questions of fact, only inferences to be drawn from admitted facts, so writ is maintainable.
