Constitution of India, Article 16 - J&K Forest Services (Gazetted) Recruitment Rules, 1970 - Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 24 - Arrears of pay - Notional promotion - Petitioners promoted as Deputy Conservator of Forest - But cannot claim arrears of pay on account of notional promotion - As they were appointed in 1984 and became eligible for the post only in 1990 after completing six years of service as Assistant - Junior officers were promoted in 1985 while petitioners were not eligible at that time - Eligible in 1990, but promotion depends on vacancy - Thus, they are not entitled to arrears of pay for the period they did not discharge duties of Deputy Conservator of Forest.
