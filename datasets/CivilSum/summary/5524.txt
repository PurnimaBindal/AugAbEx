Bombay Industrial Relations Act, 1946 - Misconduct - Workmen who are charge-sheeted must have an inquiry if they are found guilty of misconduct.
Punishment of dismissal may be imposed.
The trial court held that the enquiry was fair and proper, and the punishment was not disproportionate.
The court upheld the order of the trial court that reinstated the workman without back wages.
