As per Section 2(k) and 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, an industrial dispute refers to any conflict concerning workman or non-workmen regarding their service conditions, in which they have significant community of interest.
[Para 22]
In accordance with Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, the tribunal has granted the demand for revised basic scales and service increments after careful examination, taking into account the wages given to similarly situated concerns and the number of settlements and awards granted.
Therefore, there should be no interference with the revision of basic wages and service increments.
