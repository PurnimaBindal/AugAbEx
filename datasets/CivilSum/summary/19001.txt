Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Re-employment after retirement - Despite instructions banning re-employment issued on various dates, respondent No. 4, on the basis of State award and outstanding merit, was granted re-employment for one year starting 131999.
However, respondent's re-employment was in violation of the complete ban which was imposed against re-employment and further, he was granted re-employment on the basis of instructions which were already expired.
Therefore, the petition was allowed.
