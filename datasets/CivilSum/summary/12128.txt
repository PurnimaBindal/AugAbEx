The Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 1(3), 1(3)(c), 2(c) pertains to the claim of gratuity by an employee of Municipal Committee.
The employee's claim of gratuity was denied on the grounds that the provisions of the Act were extended to local Bodies from 2311982 whereas the Petitioner retired in 1981.
However, it was held that the provisions of the Act apply to all employees who retire after the enforcement of the Act, in view of the judgment of Division Bench in the case of Kundan Lal Narang upheld by the Apex Court.
Therefore, the Petitioner is entitled to gratuity with a 9% interest till the date of payment.
