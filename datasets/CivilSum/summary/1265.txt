Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Appointment by selection - Ban on fresh appointments - Termination of illegal appointments - Appointing authority estopped - If the competent authority conducts open selection through a test and allows candidates to join, complete training and continue in service for years, then the authority cannot declare appointments as illegal due to ban on fresh recruitments and terminate them on that ground.
[Paras 26 and 27]
