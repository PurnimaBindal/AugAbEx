[Paras 3 and 4] C. Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 17-B and 25-F - Award - Execution of the award - Jurisdiction.
This petition is actually an execution of the award date 6th November, 1985, which grants payment of back wages, bonus, leave wages, etc. to the workmen who were supposed to be reinstated with the benefits under Section 17-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
The benefits will be available to the workers now as the interim stay on reinstatement had been ordered by this Court during the pendency of C.W.P.
No. 1196 of 1986.
