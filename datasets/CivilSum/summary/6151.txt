As per the Constitution of India, Article 226 and Indian Medical Council Act, 1956, Sections 13(3) and 33(j), the petitioner, who completed an MBBS equivalent course in USSR, meets the necessary qualifications.
The foreign institution certificate states that the petitioner is entitled to professional services in relation to the awarded qualification, which falls under Part 2 of Schedule III to the Indian Medical Council Act.
The decision of respondent No. 2, requiring the petitioner to undergo one year further internship/training in India for enrolment and permanent registration, is illegal and contrary to the provisions of the Medical Council Act.
Respondent No. 2 must grant enrolment and registration to the petitioner.
