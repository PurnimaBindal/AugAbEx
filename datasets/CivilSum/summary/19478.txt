Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 137 - B-I test - Eligibility - Length of service - Petitioner was appointed on 341989 and qualified for the B-I test on 341992 by completing three years of service under the unamended Rule 137(b).
However, the said rule was amended on 891992 and changed the condition of length of service from three years to five years.
Consequently, at the time of the B-I test held on 18194, the petitioner had not completed five years of service as mandated by the Rule 137(b) and was thereby, ineligible to participate.
Moreover, it was ruled that one could not be selected for the promotion course for training, unless they had successfully passed the B-I test.
Therefore, the petitioner's plea for a special B-I test was dismissed.
