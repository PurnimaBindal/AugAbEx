Petitioners are challenging their service termination under Article 16 of the Constitution due to their appointment on an ad-hoc basis by an incompetent authority, not being paid and their services terminated after requesting release of pay.
In addition, they were appointed illegally by backdoor methods, violating Article 16.
It was confirmed that their appointments were indeed illegal, and they were rightly removed from service.
The authorities will pass appropriate orders regarding the payment of their salary.
