According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner was appointed on a daily wage basis in July 1990 and a policy was later implemented by the government stating that a daily wager who has completed three years of service is entitled to be regularised.
The petitioner claims to have been in continuous service for more than three years.
The respondent State is directed to consider the petitioner's case in light of the latest policy and the judgment passed in C.W.P.
No. 12367 of 97 titled Smt. Kavita Gandhi v. State of Haryana decided on 3091997.
