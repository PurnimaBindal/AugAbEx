State Bank of India Supervising Staff Service Rules, Rule 49(g) and Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India - Natural justice demands an opportunity for a hearing by the appellate authority - A non-speaking appellate order is not a violation of principles of natural justice if the authority considered all points raised in the appeal and examined all necessary materials on record.
