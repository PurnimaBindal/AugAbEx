The Constitution of India's Articles 226/227 and the CRPF Act, 1949's Article 11 dictate departmental enquiry procedures for cases of misconduct/neglect of duty.
A memorandum of Article of charges was served to the petitioner and other delinquents, but no response was submitted to the charge.
As a result, the petitioner was removed from service following the finalisation of the departmental enquiry.
There was no flaw found in the decision, and the petition was dismissed.
