Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1937, Rules 12.21 and 1624 - Order to discharge Constable from service due to poor performance based on CID antecedents report is not considered punitive or stigmatic - No need for a regular inquiry - Competent authority can choose to proceed with discharge or initiate an inquiry for imposing major punishment - Discharge upheld and Single Judge's order quashed.
