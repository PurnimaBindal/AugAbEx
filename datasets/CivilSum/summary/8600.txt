Constitution of India, Article 226 - Clauses 8 and 9 of Ordinance governing degree of Bachelor of Business Studies (BBS) of Delhi University - Petitioner challenged Ordinance ruling that denied permission to reappear in paper 3 of I semester - Petitioner sought directions to be allowed to attend classes and complete the course - The Ordinance limits appearances to two chances in failed papers of I Semester by regular students only - No such restriction exists for ex-students - Petition allowed with directions for petitioner to be readmitted to the course, permitted to take 4th Semester exams subject to minimum attendance criteria, and entitled to other benefits as per Ordinance and current practice for similar cases.
