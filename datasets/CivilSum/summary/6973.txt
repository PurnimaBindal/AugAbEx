According to Sections 25 and 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, a notice was issued by the respondent department prior to the termination of a workman's services.
The respondent argued that the notice was issued under Section 25-F of the Act.
The evidence on record shows that Section 25-N is not applicable in this case, and the petitioner failed to plead this argument with the Learned Labour Court.
As a result, the labour court non-suited the petitioner, but mentioned that the petitioner's case may be considered for re-employment in case of any future vacancies.
Consequently, there were no inaccuracies in the findings reached by the Learned Labour Court, and the petition was dismissed.
