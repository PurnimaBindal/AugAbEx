For Respondent: SK Bhanot, DAG, Punjab.
Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(b) - Dismissal from service/Enquiry/Dispensing with the enquiry - Orders of dismissal or removal from service passed by competent authorities under any of the Clauses under Article 311(2) are subject to judicial review.
If the court is convinced that the competent authority has exercised its powers without application of mind to the relevant considerations, such an order can be quashed.
The petitioners have been dismissed from service on the allegation that they had links with extremists.
It was for the authority to prove that it had sufficient and relevant material available for reaching such conclusion to its satisfaction.
The subjective satisfaction has to be recorded on the file that it was not reasonably practicable and possible to hold a departmental enquiry for valid reasons to prove the allegations.
However, the record was not produced despite directions by the court.
In the absence of such a record, it has to be held that the orders have been passed by the competent authority without application of mind.
The impugned order quashed with liberty to the authority to proceed in accordance with the law afresh.
[Paras 9 and 10]
