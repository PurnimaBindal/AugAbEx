As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, once an employee appointed on an ad hoc basis against an existing regular post is found to have attained uninterrupted ad hoc service which is subsequently regularised, their service will be considered as qualifying service for all purposes.
[Paras 6 to 8]

Under the Constitution of India, Article 226, the scope of judicial review is limited to patent infirmity or violation of well-settled principles of law.
