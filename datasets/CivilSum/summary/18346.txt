Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25FFF, 33(C)(i) - Industrial Disputes (Rajasthan Amendment) Act, 1970, Section 10(K) - Notice of closure - Restraining order - Failure of conciliation - Non-reference of dispute to Industrial Tribunal - A writ challenging the determination of the amount of wages under Section 33(C)(1) and a restraining order by the Labour Commissioner is not valid as parties have the option of remedying the dispute through adjudication by Industrial Tribunal.
Thus, the State Government is directed to make the reference.
