Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Section 124 - Seniority - Considered a condition of service - If a junior employee is temporarily placed in a higher position due to an investigation involving his seniors, he may later be asked to step down from the post - This would not be a violation of any fundamental rights - It is a temporary arrangement made for the exigency of service and does not create a right to hold a higher post.
[Paras 6 and 8]
