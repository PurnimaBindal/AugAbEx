Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Scope and jurisdiction - Labour Court can use its discretion under Section 11-A if the workman's defence in departmental proceedings is not considered - Bus Conductor argued that he didn't know about passengers sitting on the roof, but was caught issuing tickets to those inside the bus - Labour Court doubted his plea and correctly exercised discretion under Section 11-A - Labour Court's decision was legal.
