The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 state that pay should be equal for equal work.
Research Associates and Research Assistants at the University have identical educational qualifications, job-charts, and responsibilities.
However, Research Associates are placed on a consolidated pay of Rs. 1100/- per month, while Research Assistants receive a pay scale of Rs. 300-600.
This is a violation of equal pay for equal work.
Research Associates cannot claim to be placed on a time scale pay like their colleagues.
[Paras 10 and 11] B.
The Constitution of India, Articles 32 and 226 state that employees can make a grievance if what is due to them has been illegally withheld.
Although the University may argue that equal pay for Research Associates will create financial strain, this is not a tenable argument.
The University is directed to pay the difference with interest.
[Para 12] C. The ad hoc appointment of Research Associates of the University should be considered for regularisation to provide job security for long-standing employees.
Such a scheme would benefit both the University, who would gain experienced staff, and the employees who would gain job security.
