Selection List - The Selection Committee prepared a selection list for the post of Lecturer in Psychology, but failed to offer the post to the petitioner despite the candidate at No1 not joining the post and despite being the next candidate on the approved list if the post was vacated.
The college instead made an ad hoc appointment and later made a fresh selection for regular appointment, despite repeated directions to offer the post to the petitioner.
College directed to appoint the petitioner against the post, despite fresh regular appointment of the new candidate selected over and above the petitioner.
[Para 10] B. Estoppel/Selection - Although the petitioner participated in the fresh selection, she was not selected.
Nevertheless, the petitioner is not estopped from claiming appointment on the basis of her earlier selection.
