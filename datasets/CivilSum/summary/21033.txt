UP Intermediate Education (Amendment) Act, 1958 - Seniority - The appellant was appointed with the approval of Respondent No. 2-College effective on 171965.
The approval was granted on 2221966.
Respondent No. 3 was appointed on 971965 and approval was granted on 1781965.
The seniority lists of 1977 and 1984 showed the appellant senior to Respondent No. 3.
However, in 1989, the management treated the appellant junior to Respondent No. 3.
The reason being, the letter of approval to respondent No. 3 was issued prior to the appellant, and the Respondent No. 3 was shown senior in the seniority list of 1989 and appointed as Principal on an ad hoc basis.
The court held that there was no justification for the change of seniority after 24 years and there is no provision under which the date of appointment is to be considered as the date of approval.
[Paras 3, 4 and 6]
