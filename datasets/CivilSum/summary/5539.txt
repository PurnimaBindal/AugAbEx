Constitution of India Article 226 and Industrial Disputes Act 1947 Section 33-C(2) discuss the jurisdiction of the Labour Court.
The workman in this case filed a vague application for wages from 1981 to 1983 without specifying the month in 1981 in which he was due payment.
The application was filed in December 1991, and no evidence was produced to prove his working relationship with the petitioner.
Furthermore, the delay was not explained, and the issue of master-servant relationship was raised.
As a result, section 33-C(2) of the Act cannot be used, and the Labour Court cannot adjudicate the disputed question.
