Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Special allowance - Bi-partite Settlement - Bank employees contended that they checked or scrutinized all the schedules and/or vouchers received with the cheques and were entitled to special allowance as per para XIX(III) of Appendix `B' of 1st Bi-partite settlement.
The Tribunal awarded this claim solely based on affidavits without asking for any evidence, which is insufficient to determine this claim.
The Tribunal needs to examine the evidence on file to determine if the concerned workers fall under any category eligible for special allowance under the Settlement or the Sastry/Desai Awards.
As such, the award has been quashed and the case has been remanded to the Tribunal for a fresh decision.
