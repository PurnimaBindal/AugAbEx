Constitution of India, Article 136 - Part-heard matter - A High Court Bench who has not yet completed a hearing may or may not assemble to dispose of the matter as no date is fixed - The Senior Judge of the High Court may be asked to form the Bench if necessary - If for some reason the Bench is not available, another Bench should be formed to dispose of the matter within eight weeks - The Director of Extension may continue to guide certain students in research, although the university has the option of requesting other work from the Respondent or not.
