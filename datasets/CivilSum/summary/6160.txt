Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 11-A - Back wages - After finding punishment of removal from service unjustified by the Labour Court, High Court directed reinstatement with full back wages- However, before granting full back wages, the court must examine whether the workman was working gainfully during termination and whether payment of full back wages will be in public interest - The mere failure of the employer to produce evidence of gainful employment during that period is not sufficient grounds to grant full back wages.
