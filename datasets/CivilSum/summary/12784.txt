Examination/Unfair means - Talking - Merely talking to another student in the examination hall does not always imply the use of unfair means - It is unjust and arbitrary to ban the student only on that basis without providing any opportunity.
[Para 10]

Examination/Unfair means/Natural Justice - When handling cases of examinees resorting to unfair means in the examination hall, the Examination Committee functions in a quasi-judicial capacity - The Full Bench ruling in AIR 1973 All.
1 has reiterated the need to adhere to the essential principles of natural justice.
[Para 5]
