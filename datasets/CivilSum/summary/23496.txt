Constitution of India, Article 226 - Date of birth - Correction of date of birth at the end of service - Although there may be a special law for correcting the dates of birth for Government employees, courts cannot grant interim relief in such cases based on new material not presented at the time of entry into the service record.
A writ petition was filed to correct the date of birth at the end of service to avoid superannuation, but it would be imprudent to allow interim relief to such employees for continuance in service.
