Army Pension Regulations, Rules 173 and 186(1) - Entitlement Rules for Casualty Pensionary Awards, 1982, Rules 4, 5, 14 and 15 - The petitioner must receive service element disability pension for hypertension.
The condition of at least 20% disability is qualified by Regulation 186(1), so even if the disability is assessed less than 20%, the individual shall be entitled to service element of disability pension.
[Para 8]  B Army Pension Regulations, Rule 181 - A recruit is eligible for disability pension at the rates and under the conditions applicable to a Sepoy of the lowest group and cannot be denied the benefit merely because he was still a recruit.
[Para 8]  C Entitlement Rules for Casualty Pensionary Awards, 1982, Annexure III, Clause J - Constitution diseases - Hypertension is confirmed to not be a listed constitutional disease.
[Para 9]  D. Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 21 - Army Pension Regulations, Rule 173 - The claim of disability pension must not be rejected due to delay or laches, and the authorities cannot take benefit of their own wrong in giving a wrong interpretation to the provisions of the Rules.
