Constitution of India, Article 309, 14 and 16 - Instructions/Reservation - The Appellant's caste was notified as Backward Class after he joined service - Despite this, he became entitled to be promoted against the reserved point according to the roster point - However, reservation was later restricted to those with a certain income limit - The Appellant is entitled to be promoted against the reserved point where a vacancy had already accrued - Appeal allowed - Decisions of both courts below reversed.

Civil Procedure Code, Section 100 - Second appeal/Findings of fact - In cases where both the lower courts have denied relief based on evidence, and no substantial question of law is involved, there is little scope for intervention in a second appeal - But if the High Court believes that the findings of fact made by the lower courts were lacking in consideration of relevant evidence, or were arrived at by an erroneous approach, it is not prevented from re-examining evidence in second appeal.
