Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8 - Punishment - Natural Justice - Withholding of one increment as punishment - Plaintiff was found not guilty in the enquiry and was exonerated from the charges presented against him.
However, the punishing authority disagreed with the enquiry officer's findings and passed an order of punishment without giving the plaintiff an opportunity to respond by providing a copy of the note of disagreement and reasons.
The first appellate court's finding that the punishment order was illegal and invalid had no flaws.
