Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Railway Establishment Manual, Para 216 - Seniority - Ad hoc promotion - Para 216 of Railway Establishment Manual permits ad hoc promotion while regular selection is pending - Direct recruits are placed below promotees in seniority according to the mode of recruitment's shortfall - The rule is reasonable - Seniority for ad hoc promotion made during exigencies of service will count towards their seniority once they are duly selected later on.
[Paras 17, 19 and 24] B.
Ad hoc service - Seniority - Time gap arrangement - If ad hoc promotion is made as per rules during pendency of regular selection and followed by regular selection and promotion, entire period of ad hoc service counts towards seniority.
