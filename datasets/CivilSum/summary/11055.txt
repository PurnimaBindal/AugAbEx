Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Suspension - An employer has the right to suspend or terminate its employee, and a court can only intervene if the exercise of power is blatantly arbitrary or contrary to law - A daily wage laborer working for HUDA was suspended and no disciplinary proceedings were initiated for over five years - This caused the petitioner to suffer unnecessary harassment and financial loss, and wasted public money without any apparent purpose - The delay in conducting an inquiry was not explained - It can be assumed that no disciplinary proceedings were intended at the time the suspension order was issued - The respondents were instructed to reinstate the petitioner and conduct an inquiry if necessary, and to then determine the petitioner's arrears of salary - The suspension order was overturned.
