Rajasthan Police Subordinate Service Rules, 1989, Rule 26 - Promotion/Qualifying Test/Regularisation/Reversion - Petitioner promoted ad hoc as Sub Inspector with regular pay scale - Rule requires that passing the qualifying test is compulsory for regular promotion - Petitioner failed the qualifying test three times after getting opportunity, thus cannot be regularized - Reversion from ad hoc promotion for non-compliance of the rule is not illegal or against natural justice - No direction towards regularisation can be issued under Article 226.
Administrative instructions cannot supersede the rules - Instructions regarding regularisation of ad hoc employees - Passing of qualifying test is necessary, and regularisation cannot be claimed without meeting this requirement.
