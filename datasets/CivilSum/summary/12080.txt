Constitution of India, Article 226 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 13 - Appointment as Managing Director on tenure - Retention of lien on earlier post - Petitioner, initially appointed as Secretary and Financial Controller in DTTDC, was selected for the post of MD for a period of 5 years.
Though he terminated his lien from the earlier post after 2 years, he accepted the transfer of terminal benefits.
In light of these facts, it was held that the Petitioner cannot claim to retain lien with DTTDC since the post of MD of corporation is permanent and he was appointed on a tenure basis, and the outer limit of FR 13 applies.
Dr Kacker's case clearly covers the issue.
The Petitioner was fully conscious of his rights and exercised his choice to forfeit claim to his lien with DTTDC and secure all terminal benefits.
[Paras 19, 22 and 23]
