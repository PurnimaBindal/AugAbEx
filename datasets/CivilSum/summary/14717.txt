The respondents argued that the petitioner should have been transferred to the same post rather than a promotional post, but they didn't cite any supporting rules.
Despite this, it was held that a transfer can't be considered illegal or against the rules even if it amounts to a different post or promotion.
[Para 9] B The authorities violated the principles of natural justice by considering grounds not mentioned in the show-cause notice issued to the petitioner.
It was incumbent on the authorities to include all grounds in the notice to enable the petitioner to submit an effective reply.
