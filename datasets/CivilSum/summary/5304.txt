Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 - Deputation - Non-joining - Extension of - The Respondent was proposed for deputation, but due to not being relieved by the State Government, he was unable to join the post.
A fresh panel of officers was called for the deputed post, and the Respondent approached the Central Administrative Tribunal seeking an extension of joining time.
The OA of the Respondent was allowed, as there was no reason for the assignment to be cancelled.
However, the petitioners contend that the assignment was only for three years and that no officer can claim it as a right.
It was held that deputation means service outside the parent department on a temporary basis and cannot be made without the consent of the employee.
No deputationist has the right to be absorbed in the service of the borrowing department.
The Tribunal erred in passing such an order as nearly five years had lapsed from the date of the offer.
The petition was allowed accordingly.
[Paras 17, 18, 21, and 22]
