Section 10(1) of the Dentist Act, 1948, as amended by the Dentist (Amendment) Ordinance, 1993, and Section 28(5) of the U.P.
Universities Act, 1973, governs the admission and migration of candidates.
The Act imposes a complete embargo on institutions increasing their capacity in any course of study or training without the prior permission of the Central Government.
Following the introduction of Section 10-A by the Amendment Ordinance, 1993, the representative of candidates seeking transfer was rejected.
The transfer applications will now be considered in light of the amended statutory provision.
As a result, the High Court orders are set aside, and the respondents are directed to consider the applications for transfer according to the amended provisions.
