Constitution of India, Article 12 - State Agency - State Bank of India falls under the definition of State within the meaning of State under Article 12 of the Constitution.
[Para 2]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Compassionate appointment - State Bank of India has a policy for compassionate appointments - The policy takes into account the overall financial condition of the deceased's family, including service benefits, family pension, gratuity, provident fund, and income of other family members - Appointments cannot be made without open contest as it violates Articles 14 and 16 - If the family is able to sustain for a considerable time, it is presumed that there is no financial crisis - Claims for compassionate appointment cannot be deferred to await eligibility by a member/minor of the family - If the deceased was near superannuation, no appointment is offered on compassionate grounds - If the employee dies in the earlier stage of his career and the family is in penurious state, a delay in deciding an application for a compassionate appointment would not defeat the claim - Applications for compassionate appointments should be decided quickly.
