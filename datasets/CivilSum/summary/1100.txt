According to Section 10(3) and 10(4) of the Industrial Disputes Act, if a dispute regarding lock-out has not been referred to the Labour Tribunal for adjudication, the Government cannot issue a prohibition against lock-out.
Prohibition can only be issued if the matter has been referred to the Labour Tribunal.

As per Section 10(1), 10(3) and 10(4) of the Industrial Disputes Act, the entire dispute between the parties must be referred for adjudication in a composite reference rather than in pieces.
