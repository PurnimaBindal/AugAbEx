Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part I, Rule 25 (1994 Amendment) - Correction of date of birth - Constant policy changes by Govt. on this matter are concerning, but employees cannot change their recorded date of birth on their own accord - Courts will only order a change in date of birth with conclusive proof and adherence to the rules.
[Paras 10, 14 and 19]
