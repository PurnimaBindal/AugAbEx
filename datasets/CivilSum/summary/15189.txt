Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Dismissal from service - Disciplinary proceedings - Petitioner, an employee of State Electricity Board, was caught by the Board's Vigilance Cell accepting a bribe from a consumer.
An Inquiry Officer conducted the enquiry and the petitioner's unchallenged confessional statement was considered.
However, none of the witnesses said that they had seen him accepting a bribe, which did not provide any relief to him.
The penalty imposed on the petitioner was not disproportionate to the gravity of the proven misconduct.
[Paras 15, 18 & 19]
