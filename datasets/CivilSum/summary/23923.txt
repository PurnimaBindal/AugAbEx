Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference - Industrial Dispute - The exercise of power under Section 10 of the ID Act must be reasonable and rational - The appropriate government must consider whether an industrial dispute exists or is apprehended at the time of issuing the demand notice - A stale dispute cannot be referred under Section 10 of the Act - A reference made on a seven years delayed demand notice is unjustified and without jurisdiction.
