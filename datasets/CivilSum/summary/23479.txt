Constitution of India, Article 226 - UP State Tourism Development Corporation Rules, 1981, Rule 19 - Appointment to the post of General Manager - Rule 19 stipulates that appointments to Class I posts can be made only by selection or by deputation - If suitable candidates are unavailable, appointments may be made by deputation - The rule does not mandate appointment to Class I posts by means of promotion alone - The fact that promotion is one method of demonstrating skill and efficiency does not justify reading into the rule that appointment to the post of General Manager must be made by promotion and only by deputation in the absence of suitable candidates - This is a matter of policy to be decided by the Corporation - The court cannot substitute its own policy for the rule.
[Paras 9, 11, 13 to 15]
