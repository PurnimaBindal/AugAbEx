The promotion policy for industrial disputes settlement under Sec. 18 of the Act prescribes for a minimum percentage of marks in English for passing the test, which is binding on both parties unless revoked under the law.
The promotion policy stating minimum qualifying marks in English does not violate any constitutional provisions, and the petitioner cannot claim differential treatment.
