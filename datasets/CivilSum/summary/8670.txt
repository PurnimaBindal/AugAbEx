Civil Procedure Code, Order 41 Rule 33 - Power of Appellate Court - Janampatri - Not admissible in evidence - The person who creates a janampatri for another does not have knowledge of the date of birth of that person - They make the janampatri based on information provided by the person - Although the plaintiff was retired at age 55 instead of 58, it is fair to award them the pecuniary benefits for their three years of service since they retired long ago.
