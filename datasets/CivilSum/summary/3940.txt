Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control, and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rules 16 and 17 - Penalty of stoppage of increments with cumulative effect - The applicable law on the date of penalty must be followed - According to Rule 17, stoppage of increment was considered a minor penalty, and Rule 16 was to be followed for major penalties, but the judgment in Krishna Dutta's case took a contrary view - However, the judgment is prospective in nature and cannot be used to test the validity of penalties imposed before the judgment.
