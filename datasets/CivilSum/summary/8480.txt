Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221, and Rule 16.24 - Absence from duty - Discharge - Enquiry - Opportunity of hearing - The order of discharge was based on misconduct and was not a simple order of discharge; therefore, the impugned order should have been passed after conducting a regular inquiry and providing the plaintiff with an opportunity to prove his defence.
The first appellate court rightly quashed the order of discharge.
