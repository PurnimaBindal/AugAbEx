Challenge to the disciplinary authority's order to reopen an enquiry when the petitioner did not participate in earlier proceedings and sought participation.
The argument was that there is no provision for a de novo enquiry, so the communication is without jurisdiction.
However, it was held that the power to direct further enquiry is inherent with the disciplinary authority, as per the judgment in P Thayogarajan's case.
The disciplinary authority ordered a further enquiry in view of the previous manner in which the enquiry was conducted, which was seen as advancing the cause of justice and providing an opportunity to the petitioner as he earlier sought.
There was no illegality or irregularity in the enquiry which warranted any interference.
[Paras 8 and 9]
