The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 16 and 14, and Punjab Civil Services Rules Volume II Rules 511 and 512 address the issue of disability pension.
In the case of a Roadways bus driver who retired on medical grounds, he has the right to an alternative job if available.
If no job is available, he is entitled to compensation and disability pension.
Additionally, qualifying service of 10 years is not applicable for disability pension.
In the present case, the petitioner was only partially incapacitated, which means that the determination of his entitlement to pensionary benefits must be made in accordance to Rule 512 of the Civil Service Rules.
