Constitution of India, Article 226 - Madhya Pradesh Uchcha Shiksha Anudan Ayog Adhiniyam, 1973, Section 3(2) - Retirement benefits - Grant of - A petitioner retired as Deputy Registrar of University - The State Government made an order stating that University employees should receive retirement benefits equal to those of State Government employees - According to the Adhiniyam, the State Government has the power to discharge Ayog liabilities - Therefore, the petitioners are entitled to all retirement benefits in light of the government order.
