Constitution of India, Article 16 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 45-A - Overstaying in Government Quarter after retirement - Liability to pay penal rent - A retired government servant can occupy government accommodation for one month at usual rent and for four months at standard rent - A petitioner, who was a retired Additional District Judge and appointed as Chairman of Districts Consumer Forum, stayed in a government accommodation for more than four months - His application for continuation for government accommodation was denied by the High Court - Penal rent is demanded for the time that he exceeded the four-month limit, which is legal.
