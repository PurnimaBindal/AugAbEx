Constitution of India, Article 16 - Central Engineering Service, Class I, Recruitment Rule, 1954 - C.P.W.P.
Manual Volume I (1970 Ed.) Section 7 Para 13 - Promotion - The 1971 Resolution of the DDA for promotion to the post of Executive Engineer was validly amended to incorporate the procedure in paragraph 13 of the CPWD Manual.
This manual's procedure would apply for promotions to the CPWD's Executive Engineer post.
