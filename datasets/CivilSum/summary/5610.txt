Constitution of India, Article 311 - UP Fundamental Rules, 1973, Rule 56(6) - Compulsory Retirement - The petitioner was compelled to retire based on an adverse entry recorded many years ago that was unsupported by any other material.
This action amounted to digging up the past for material to use against the employee.
No prudent person could have formed the opinion to retire a person solely on this old entry.
Furthermore, during the petitioner's 27-year service, no other adverse remarks were recorded.
Forming an opinion based on an uncommunicated censure entry without considering the petitioner's long tenure was arbitrary.
[Paras 17, 19, 20 and 21]
