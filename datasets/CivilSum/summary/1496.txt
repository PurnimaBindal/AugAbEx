Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 37 and 39(d) - Punjab Educational Service, Class III, School Cadre, Rules, 1955 - Punjab Educational Service (Provincialized Cadre) Class III Rules, 1961 - The "doctrine of equality" dictates that equals must be treated equally, not unequally - Article 14 prohibits class legislation, not classification for rights of equality - Classification must be based on rational differentia and cannot be arbitrary - Qualifications, quality of work, and responsibilities are criteria for classification for grant of pay scales - Officers of the same rank performing dissimilar functions may not complain of dissimilar pay and discrimination - Service conditions of different Rules vary - Provincialised cadre is a diminishing cadre - State Cadre cannot claim parity with the members of Provincialised Cadre in the matter of pay scale and promotions - J.B.T.
Rajkiya Adhayapak Sangh’s case judgement cannot be read to lay down any law that State cannot discriminate between members of two cadres in the matter of grant of pay scales - The earlier judgement of Constitution Bench in Joginder Singh's case was not brought to the notice of the Supreme Court in J.B.T.
Rajkiya Adhayapak Sangh's case [Paras 1, 9 to 12, 14 and 23 to 29].
