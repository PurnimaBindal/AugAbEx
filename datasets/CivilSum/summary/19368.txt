The Promotion Policy for Punjab & Sind Bank (Officers) is governed by a number of regulations and acts, including Article 16(4) of the Constitution of India, Regulation 17 of the Service Regulations, and Sections 19 and 12(2) of the Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1980.
The policy is based on competition rather than seniority or reservations and allows all eligible candidates to compete on the merits.
The policy may be amended by the bank to get the best talent, but any changes cannot be made retroactively to affect already accrued rights to consideration for vacancies.
