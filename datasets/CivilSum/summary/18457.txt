Constitution of India, Article 21 - Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 33 - Reservation for the handicapped - Low vision and totally blind candidates are excluded from reserve category, unless exempted by State Government notification as per Section 33 of 1995 Act - No such notification issued by State - Instruction dt. 9.3.2001 not valid as it goes against the provision of Section 33 of the Act.
