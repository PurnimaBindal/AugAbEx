In regards to the recruitment of a Male Constable, the eligibility of a candidate is determined by the cut-off date, which for this case was the last date of receipt of the application form on 2452006.
Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 apply to this situation.
The petitioner in this case had appeared in the 10+2 examination but the result wasn't declared until 672006.
As a result, the petitioner did not possess the desired qualification on the cut-off date, and his candidature was rightfully rejected.
