Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularization - The petitioners are seeking regularization under respondent No. 2, who operates the establishment through a contractor.
However, the contractor has argued that he is no longer a contractor responsible for running the canteen on respondent No. 2's premises.
These are disputed factual issues that need to be proven with compelling and proper evidence.
The matter has already been referred to the labour court under sections 10(1)(c) and 10(1)(d) of the ID Act.
The court notes that the petition becomes meaningless since the labour court will handle the case promptly.
