Under Rule 18 of the Punjab State Planning Board (Class III) Service Rules, 1988, the competent authority is allowed to relax the promotion/qualifying test conditions.
In this case, the respondents were given a relaxation to pass the departmental Stenography test in English within one year of their promotion, with three chances to qualify.
The appellants cannot deny them promotion based on subsequent instructions withdrawing the relaxation after the respondents had already qualified the test, as per Constitution of India, Article 14 and 16.
