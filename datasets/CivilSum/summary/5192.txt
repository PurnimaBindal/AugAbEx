The Apprentices Act, 1961, Sections 2(aa) and 4(4), along with the Apprenticeship Rules, Rule 12(2), as well as the Uttar Pradesh Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(a) and (z), and 6N, were considered in a case regarding the appointment and termination of Apprentices and their classification as workmen under the Act of 1947.
The court held that, since no contract of apprenticeship or registered agreement, as required under the 1961 Act, was filed on record, the respondents in the case could not be considered as apprentices and must instead be treated as workmen within the definition of the 1947 Act.
