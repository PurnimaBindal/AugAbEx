According to Civil Procedure Code 1908, Order 23 Rule 1, as well as Articles 21, 32, and 226 of the Constitution of India, a subsequent petition with the same cause of action as an earlier withdrawn petition without liberty cannot be allowed.
This is because allowing such a petition would lead to malpractices like forum hunting and would never bring finality to a cause.
The decision of the Supreme Court in Sarguja Transport Service's case is an absolute bar to the entertainment of a subsequent petition, except in cases where it is in the nature of Habeas Corpus.
