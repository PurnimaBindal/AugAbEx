Constitution of India, Article 226/227 - Tikku Committee - Recommendations - The recommendations of Tikku Committee were implemented by the Government through an office memorandum issued on 14111991, stipulating that a Medical Officer must be promoted as Senior Medical Officer after completing four years of service, followed by promotion as Chief Medical Officer after six years of service.
The petitioners have requested that the benefit be extended retrospectively from the date of the OM's issuance, i.e. 14111991.
Representation was declined, stating that the date of implementation would be 2831995, the date of resolution of the Governing Body.
However, since no clear date is stipulated in this regard, the matter has been left with the Governing Body itself to clarify the position and decide on the effective date of the decision dated 2831995.
The Governing Body of the Institute has been directed to convene a meeting and make a clear decision.
