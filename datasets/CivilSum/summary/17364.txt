Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Education - Admission - Sports Gradation Certificate - Counter signatures of - Reservation of seats for sports persons in medical institutions - A claim for certification can be entertained with regards to residents of the State and Union Territory of Chandigarh, without a condition that the parents should belong to the Punjab State Services or be posted at Chandigarh Respondents are not justified in raising this objection as the condition only applies to sports persons who have participated in National Games, and not to those who have taken part in Junior or Senior National Championships.
