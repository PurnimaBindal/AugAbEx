The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - The promotion and arrears of salary - Engineers in Haryana Agricultural Service Class-I who are promoted retrospectively are entitled to arrears of salary and all other benefits that they would have been entitled to had they been promoted on the original promotion date - Imposing conditions on the arrears of salary etc. is illegal.
[Paras 14 to 18]
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion and equal pay for equal work - Punjab Agricultural Service Class II Rules, 1947 - Promotion to Haryana Agriculture Service Class-I - Agriculture Engineers - Promotions made on different dates - Promotion with retrospective date from which juniors were promoted - All those incumbents with the same qualifications, duties, and pay scale will be treated equally in terms of pay on the principle of equal pay for equal work - Both posts in different departments of a Government must have the same duties and responsibilities and be treated equally for all intents and purposes.
