Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 33 - Civil Service Regulations, Regulation 151 - Increment - Retirement - The last increment that is due to an employee at the time of their retirement cannot be forfeited if it was not released before retirement.
Additionally, an employee becomes entitled to an increment upon completing the preceding year, and cannot be denied the benefit simply because they are no longer in service on the day of payment.
There is no requirement for an employee to continue in service until benefits become available.
