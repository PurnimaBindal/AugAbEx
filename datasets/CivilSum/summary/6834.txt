Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F, Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14 and 16 - Daily wage employee- Retrenchment without payment of compensation - Labour Court has ordered reinstatement of workman with continuity of service after finding violation of Section 25F of the Act, but the award is set aside because workman's appointment only can be made as per Recruitment Rules, and violation of Section 25-F is not enough for reinstatement.
Also, ad hoc or daily wage employees do not have a right to invoke Articles 14 and 16.
However, petitioner has to pay a sum of Rs. 25,000/- as per the Court's order.
