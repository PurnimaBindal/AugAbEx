Constitution of India, Article 16 - Compassionate appointment - Pay protection based on revised pay scale - An employee who was retrenched on account of closure of a unit (Corporation) claimed protection of last pay drawn based on revised scale - However, since the revised scale of pay was never adopted by the Corporation at any point of time, the employee's claim for protection of last pay drawn based on the revised scale is not tenable - The employee is only entitled to protection of last pay drawn if the unit where they worked had adopted the revised scale of pay.
