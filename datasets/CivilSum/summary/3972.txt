Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Removal from service - The petitioner argued that the remedies are based on two different causes of action - However, the contention was found to be entirely misconceived - The petitioner had filed a writ petition challenging the punishment of removal from service, and then attempted to challenge the same punishment through an industrial dispute, on different grounds - The petitioner must not confuse different grounds as a different cause of action - It is impermissible to invent new grounds and elect a new forum after failing once in a particular forum - The petition was dismissed.
