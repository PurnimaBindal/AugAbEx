Article 14 of the Constitution of India outlines that selection and assignment of duties for the post of DG and IGP in the Karnataka State Police is by way of selection for promotion to a higher post.
Therefore, all eligible candidates must be considered in accordance with seniority.
B Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution state that as long as selection for appointment as DG and IGP is made fairly and upon consideration of service records of eligible candidates, a court has no right to interfere.
However, if selection is not made objectively and appears to be made mechanically, court interference becomes necessary to protect the rights of civil servants and ensure adherence to the rule of law.
