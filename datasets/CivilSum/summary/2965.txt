Under the Minimum Wages Act 1948, Section 1(5) and Tamil Nadu Catering Establishment Act, 1958, Section 2, canteen employees are subject to the Act, however, according to Section 2(1) of the Act, a restaurant or canteen operated by an educational institution is excluded from its provisions.
A Deputy Commissioner has granted minimum rate of wages to respondents working in a mess, holding that it falls under the definition of a "hotel".
However, the court has held that the term "mess" is excluded from the preview of the Minimum Wages Act, and the authority has committed an error in granting relief to the workmen.
Therefore, the petition is allowed accordingly.
