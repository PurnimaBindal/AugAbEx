Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1963, Section 69 - Limitation Act, 1963, Article 137 - Arbitration Award - Limitation - After the petitioner's two awards from 11121989 were not challenged, they were deemed final and exonerated the petitioner from their liability to deposit an amount due toward society after resigning.
Although a further award made on the same charge was challenged, it was also given in favour of the petitioner.
After a lapse of more than six years of passing the second award, appointing an Arbitrator and issuing a notice dated on 18101996 could not be allowed at that belated stage.
