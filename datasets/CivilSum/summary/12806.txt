Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 11 and 12, 1226 - Haryana Director General of Police, Standing Order 49 - Transfers from Commando Wing to GRP without his consent - The entire police force consists of General Police District and Railway Police District (GRP) - The Commando Force is part of the General Police District - Transfer from Commando Force to Railway Police only requires approval from the concerned Superintendent of Police or Assistant Inspector General of Police.

Head Note 20 does not prohibit a person over 30 years from being posted in the Commando Wing.
