The Workmen's Compensation Act, Section 10 (1) and Limitation Act, Section 5 allow for condonation of delay.
In a case where a claim petition was filed 60 days past the due date, an application for condonation of delay cited the appellant-claimant's illiteracy and assurance of compensation given by Railway as reasons for the delay.
The Commissioner rejected the application, but it was ultimately set aside on the grounds that the appellant-claimant had shown sufficient cause.
The matter was remanded back to the Commissioner for further consideration.
