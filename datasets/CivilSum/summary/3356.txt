Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Recruitment of Ex-servicemen Rules, 1982, Rule 2(c) - Ex-servicemen - If an Army member voluntarily retires from service, they should be considered as an ex-serviceman and cannot be separated from the category.
- Creating a subgroup without a rational basis is not allowed - Denying the petitioner's claim for a post reserved for ex-serviceman violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution and defeat the purpose of reservation.
