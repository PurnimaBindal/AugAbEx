Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 10(1) - Reference to Labour Court - Disputed questions of facts - The Industrial Tribunal can resolve the question of whether contract labourers working in the establishment of the principal employer should be automatically absorbed as regular employees following notification under Section 10(1) of the 1970 Act.
This decision should be based on the observations made in the Steel Authority of India case[Paras 3, 4, 5 and 8]
