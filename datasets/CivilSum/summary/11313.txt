According to Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 2125 and 1318, deputationists in CID from the Police Department do not have any right to be considered for regular promotion in CID, nor do they have any right for permanent absorption in CID.
Ad-hoc or fortuitous promotion made while on deputation is only for the term of deputation in CID.
The order of reversion and repatriation from CID does not suffer from any lack of jurisdiction.
In the interest of good administration, there needs to be some policy and guidelines on the subject of repatriation, and the cases of those repatriated should be considered by appropriate exemptions from technicalities.
State directed accordingly 1995(2) RSJ 229 overruled.
