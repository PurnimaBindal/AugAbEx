Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Section 15 - Railway Establishment Code, Volume II, Rule 2043 - Punishment/Suspension in lieu of dismissal/Subsistence allowance - The suspension period directed to be treated as suspension in lieu of punishment cannot be referred to as suspension during disciplinary proceedings and does not qualify for subsistence allowance.
[Para 4]
Railway Establishment Code Vol. II, Rule 2034 - Subsistence allowance - In case of suspension extending beyond 12 months, the employee is eligible for subsistence allowance of 75% of their wages only.
[Para 5]
Payment of Wages Act, 1936 - Section 15 - Jurisdiction of authority under the act - Authority not authorized to decide on subsistence allowance eligibility for period treated as suspension in lieu of punishment.
