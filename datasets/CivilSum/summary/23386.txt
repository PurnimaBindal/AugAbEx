The UP Intermediate Education Act, 1921 (as amended in 1976) and the Indian Constitution's Articles 14 and 16 dictate appointment and qualification rules.
For the position of Lecturer in Hindi, the required qualifications include an MA in Hindi.
The petitioner held an MA B.Ed and was not approved for the job due to insufficient qualifications.
However, a candidate with a BA B.Ed was later appointed and accepted, making the petitioner's appointment valid.
