According to Constitution of India, Article 226, gratuity cannot be withheld.
In this particular case, the respondents have withheld a portion of the petitioner's gratuity due to alleged excess payment of Border House Rent Allowance.
However, there is no evidence to suggest that the petitioner is at fault for this excess payment.
As a result, the decision to withhold the gratuity has been set aside, in accordance with the rulings in CWP No. 1141 of 97 dated 11497 and CWP No. 16258 of 96 dated 1497.
