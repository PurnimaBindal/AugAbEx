Army Regulations, Paras 70 and 108 (1987 Edition) state that promotions to the rank of Lt.
Colonel and above are made by selection.
Officers are screened by a Selection Board based on their overall career profile on individual as well as on comparative merit.
Promotion consideration is given three times: (a) Fresh consideration, (b) First Review, (c) Final Review.
Although ACRs for 10 years are taken into consideration, they are not the sole criterion for promotion, as other aspects are taken into account.
An officer who has been considered and graded unfit on all three occasions cannot claim promotion as a matter of right based only on seniority.
Petition dismissed.

Army Regulations for Army Act (1987 Edition), Paras 108 and 70 state that a Selection Board is constituted under the orders of the Chief of Army Staff.
The Chief of Army Staff is the competent authority to approve promotions up to the rank of Lt.
Colonel.
For all promotions to the rank of Colonel and above, the Central Government is the competent authority.
The Government can modify, review, and approve the recommendations of the Selection Board.
[Paras 6, 8 and 9]

According to Constitution of India, Article 226, the court is not to go over and above the proceedings of the Departmental Promotion Committees for selection to the higher posts.
The petitioner was considered in accordance with the regulations and instructions but found unfit, and no interference is called for.
