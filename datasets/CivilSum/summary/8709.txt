Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana Agriculture University Statutes, Chapter IV, Clause 4(2) - Promotion - Personal promotion - Seniority in the Cadre - When it comes to promotion, seniority is only applicable among those who belong to same service in one cadre.
Teachers promoted under `the Merit Promotion Scheme' to the ranks of Reader, Associate Professors, Professors only for the purpose of removing stagnation, will receive personal promotion.
Such promotions will not increase the strength of promotion cadre nor create any vacancy in the lower cadre from which promotion is made.
They will continue to perform the same duties even after the promotion.
Since the teachers receiving personal promotion do not become members of the cadre, they cannot claim seniority inter se with the direct recruits in the promotion cadre.
Their seniority will be assigned separately.
