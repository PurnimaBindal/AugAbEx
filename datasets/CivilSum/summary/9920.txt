Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Probation - Termination during probation - Misconduct - Enquiry - The petitioner's services were terminated during probation due to his misconduct, including unauthorised absence from duty, tampering with official records, and poor behavior with colleagues.
The court found that the true motive for termination was punitive and not a mere 'order of discharge simpliciter.'
The court can investigate the background of such orders and lift the veil to reach a just decision.
A termination based on misconduct, even of a probationer, is not a simple discharge but a violation of natural justice.
The delinquent must be given a chance to defend himself in such cases.
The order cannot be sustained, while the punishing authority is free to hold an enquiry and make an appropriate decision.
[Paras 6-13]
