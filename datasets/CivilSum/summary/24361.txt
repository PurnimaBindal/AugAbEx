Faridabad Complex (Regulation and Development) Act, 1971 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 19 - Punjab Civil Services Rules as applicable to State of Haryana, Rule 3.16 - Employees of Faridabad Complex Administration - Petitioners claimed retiral benefits/pension - Single Judge allowed petitions based on judgment in a suit that was upheld in second appeal - Pension scheme was introduced after the retirement of Petitioners in 1992 - Rule 3.16, which excludes employees of Municipalities from payment of pension, was not considered in the earlier suit - Division Bench of High Court held that petitioners were not entitled to the benefit of the scheme - Three Judges Bench of Supreme Court had taken a similar view earlier - Therefore, the action of respondents was not arbitrary, discriminatory, unreasonable, or violative of Article 14 or 19 of the Constitution.
[Paras 16, 18, 26 and 27]
Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 11 - Res judicata - Applicability - Appellants relied on a decision in a civil suit granting a similar benefit to similarly situated persons - They contended that the decision would operate as res judicata against the respondent - The court held that res judicata would not apply since the appellants were neither parties to the suit nor claiming through plaintiffs - Moreover, the doctrine of res judicata, as envisaged under Section 11 of the Code, does not strictly apply to proceedings under Article 226.
