According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, along with Air Force Instruction 19/62 Clauses 12 and 14 and a Circular from 16111989, the promotion from Flight Engineer-II to I has specific suitability criteria determined by the Chief of Air Staff or those under his authority.
These criteria do not establish extra requirements or make amendments to Air Force Instruction 19/62.
There is a distinction made between "Air Crew" and "Ground Tradesmen" in various circulars.
The High Court of Punjab and Haryana made claims without considering this distinction and the case was remanded to consider whether or not the circular dated 16111989 applies to airmen in the "Air Crew" category.
