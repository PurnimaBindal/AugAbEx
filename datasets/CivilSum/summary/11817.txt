Constitution of India, Article 226 - Haryana Aided Schools (Security of Service) Rules, 1974, Rule 7(6) - Resignation - Conditions of service - Rule 7(6) states that appointment orders must be issued in the form prescribed in Appendix B of the rules - Even if the appointment letter is not issued in the prescribed proforma, the petitioner's service conditions are still governed by the terms and conditions listed in Appendix B - The employee is allowed to withdraw their resignation during the period of notice required under the rules/terms and conditions - The petitioner withdrew their resignation within one month, therefore the resignation could not be given effect to, regardless of whether it was accepted prior to the one month period - Therefore, the petitioner is deemed to have continued in service and the acceptance of the resignation has been quashed.
