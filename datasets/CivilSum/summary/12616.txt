of India (Staff) Regulations, 1960, Regulation 3(j) - L.I.C.
of India Class 3 and 4 (Employees Promotion) Rules, 1987, Rule 2(h) - Promotion - Qualifying service - Petitioner, who was promoted as Section Head, sought reversion and transfer due to his ill-health after 4 months.
Later, petitioner was again promoted as Section Head, but when promoted to the post of AAO, he was not eligible because he lacked the required 6 years' of continuous service as Section Head.
Petitioner claimed that his service period rendered on the post of Section Head before his reversion should be counted towards his regular service as Section Head, and the plea was accepted.
[Para 10]

Reversion on request - Effect of reversion on request - Although petitioner requested for demotion and transfer due to his ill-health, he did not give up his service rendered on the higher post.
[Para 11]

Delay - Delay and Laches - As petitioner applied for promotion as soon as he became eligible and was only superseded later, the cause of action arose when his application and appeal were rejected.
Therefore, there is no delay in filing the petition.
