The Constitution of India, Articles 16(4), 341(1) and 366(24) dictate that 'Sunri' caste in the State of West Bengal is recognized as a Scheduled Caste, but not the 'Saha' caste, which the petitioner belongs to.
Despite having a Certificate of Scheduled Caste issued to the petitioner, he is not eligible for consideration as a Scheduled Caste.
[Paras 3 to 5]
According to the Constitution of India, Articles 341(1) and 366(24), the Court does not have the right to include or exclude a caste in the Scheduled Castes.
