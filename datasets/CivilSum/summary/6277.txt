Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11A - Disproportionate punishment - A workman's services were terminated for claiming disbursement of expenses based on fake G.Rs.
- The Labour Court found the punishment to be disproportionate to the charges - The fake G.Rs.
had been procured by another employee, not the respondent - The main responsibility of the alleged procurement of fake G.R was on the other employee, whose luggage was also transported with them - The termination was not justified for such a minor lapse - The court ordered for the respondent's reinstatement with full back wages - The High Court upheld the orders on appeal by the appellant.
