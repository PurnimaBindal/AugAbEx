Revision of pension for Personnel Below Officers Rank has been revised on recommendation of Group of Ministers.
The cut off date to pay revised pension is 112006 and revision is allowed based on anomaly that had arisen as on 111996.
Financial constraints cannot be used as an excuse as pensionary benefits are payable from the date of creation of anomaly.
The cut off date loses its reasonableness once the pre-111996 retiree's pension is redetermined with respect to the maximum scale of the post from 111996.
Thus, the plea of financial constraints for only the lowest paid employees is arbitrary and discriminatory.
The cut off date and clause (9) of the circular dated 1.2.2006 has been quashed.
