The Managing Director of the Punjab State Supply and Marketing Co-operative Services had the power to order the dismissal of an Accountant 'C' grade, as the Administrative Committee delegated this power to the Managing Director by resolution.
B.
The appointment of a Senior Officer as an Administrator of a Committee is not illegal or contrary to the provisions of the Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, and the orders passed by the Administrator are therefore valid.
C. Any appeals against the orders passed by the Managing Director had to be heard and decided by the Administrator, since the Administrator was discharging the functions of the Board of Directors, and hence, the orders passed by the Administrator were also legal and valid.
