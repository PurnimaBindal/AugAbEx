According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the Cooperative Society of the High Court Employees applied for 1-1/2 acres of land at Panchkula under the Group Housing Scheme 1990 (HUDA).
Despite the deadline having passed, the authority invited new applications without considering the petitioner Society's claim.
However, as the Society's eligibility is confirmed, the Authorities have been directed to allot the land to the society at Panchkula.
