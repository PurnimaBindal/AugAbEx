The Rajasthan Agricultural University Order dated 2871980 establishes the pay of government employees transferred to the University under the UGC scales of pay.
Despite this, dearness allowance was excluded from 111973 and 191976 for the purpose of equalizing pay scales with UGC pay scales.
The UGC pay scales had merged only dearness allowances with basic pay up until 131973, with additional dearness allowances payable under UGC scales of pay from that date onward.
Revised government scales, on the other hand, had merged dearness allowances as of 191976.
As such, the University's decision to exclude DA from 1173 to 1976 was made to equate the pay scales of Govt. servants with UGC pay scales, which had not been merged in revised UGC scales but had been in revised Govt. scales.
The Order dated 28780 of the University was upheld and that of the High Court was set aside.
