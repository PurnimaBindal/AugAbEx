According to Articles 12, 14, 16, and 226 of the Constitution of India, a petitioner who worked as a salesman in a Karnataka and Goa Sub-area canteen was terminated without an inquiry.
The canteen in question was established under Army order and is not controlled by the Central Government.
As the respondent-canteen has no public duty to perform, the petitioner cannot be reinstated by a mandamus order.
[Paras 11 and 12]
