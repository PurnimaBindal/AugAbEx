UP Regulation of Public Service Commission Rules, 1979, Rules 4, 5, 6 - Ad hoc anointment - Regularisation - Any ad hoc appointment made from the open market otherwise than according to the Rules will be regulated under the rules.
An in-service candidate with a lien in a post but working in another department on ad hoc basis cannot be considered a candidate appointed from the open market for regularisation required under the Rules.
The Respondent's contention that she must be deemed to be regularly appointed on an ad hoc basis is not tenable.
The Appellant directed to consider her case according to the Rules in the general cadre to which she would be entitled in her own turn had she remained in service in the general cadre.
