Article 14 of the Constitution of India - Recommendations of Departmental Promotion Committee - While the Appointments Committee of Cabinet isn't obliged to accept the recommendations of the Departmental Promotion Committee, they can't reject them without giving any reasons - Although reasons don't need to be communicated to the concerned individuals, the reasons for deviating from the recommended panel must be stated in the file, and provided to the court as and when required - In cases where recommendations have been rejected without providing any reasons, the Appointments committee must reconsider while consulting the Union Public Service Commission, and indicate any reasons for rejecting the recommendations.

The Administrative Tribunal Act, 1985, Section 19 - Appointment - Promotion - Deemed Promotion - The Tribunal's jurisdiction - If the Appointing authority rejects the recommendations of DPC without providing any reasons, the Administrative Tribunal can only instruct them to reconsider and record deemed promotion themselves - The Tribunal's order has been set aside.
