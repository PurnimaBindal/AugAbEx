As per the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, a Librarian at the Senior level in the Punjab Education Department (Colleges) may receive Selection Grade after completing 16 years of service.
The petitioner in this case was transferred from one college to another due to interference by the Managing Committee, and was eventually retransferred back to his original college.
This transfer should not have been used as a reason to deny him the benefit of Selection Grade.
As a result, the respondents are now directed to grant the petitioner Selection Grade starting from January 1, 1986, along with all arrears, within four months.
[Paras 6, 7 and 8]
