Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - Grant-in-aid Code/Disciplinary proceedings - If a writ petition is made against termination of services of a teacher of an aided school that is agreed upon by the Director of Education after disciplinary proceedings, it would be maintainable.
[Para 13]
Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - State - Mehar Chand Polytechnical Institution is an instrumentality of State as it is run with the funds of State and under a Statute of the State Government.
All employees of this institution are allowed to invoke Article 226 jurisdiction of the Court since the institution is a State as per Article 12 of the Constitution.
[Paras 21 and 22]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Contract of Service - Judicial actions to decide the reasonableness of the terms of a contract violating the provisions of Contract Act are allowed in contractual matters.
Clauses that allow service to be terminated by the employer by giving just three months' notice shall be struck down.
[Paras 23, 24]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Natural Justice/Opportunity of hearing - If termination of services is contemplated, an opportunity to be heard should be given, as per the Rules framed by the department.
Service conditions for promotion will be incorporated in the proforma at the time of promotion.
[Para 25]
Constitution of India, Articles 30, 14 & 12 - Termination of Services/Minority Institutions - Minority institutions have to comply with the Act provisions, which allude to an inquiry before terminating the services of a confirmed employee, and cannot act arbitrarily or violate Article 14 of the Constitution.
If there is a lack of statutes, an authority that is 'State' under Article 12 of the Constitution must follow its own rules and Principles of Natural Justice.
[Para 26]
