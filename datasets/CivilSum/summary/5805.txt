Under Section 12 of the Probation of Offenders Act, 1958, disqualifications attached to a conviction are removed but do not prevent departmental action being taken against an employee for conduct which led to their criminal court conviction.
In the case of Union of India v. Bakshi Ram, the Labour Court was not justified in setting aside the termination order based on the provisions of Section 12 of the Act.
The termination order was upheld because the workman, who stood convicted under Section 411 IPC and was released on probation under Section 4 of 1958 Act, could still face departmental proceedings despite the removal of disqualifications, as provided in the PSEB Employees (Punishment and Appeal) Regulations 1971, Clause 15(2)(d).
