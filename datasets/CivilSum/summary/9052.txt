The Army Rules of 1954, specifically Rules 34 and 36, state that in the event that observance of Rule 34 is neither possible nor expedient due to military exigencies or disciplinary necessities, the Officer convening the Court Martial can choose to dispense with it by making a specific declaration to that effect.
It is worth noting that Rule 34 is only of a directory nature, which means that failing to adhere to it will not nullify the Court Martial proceedings.
