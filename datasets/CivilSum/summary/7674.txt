According to Section 33C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a claim for difference in salary can be made.
Despite being designated as Beldar, there were categorical findings that the workman worked as a typist in order to regularize his service.
The employer did not present any evidence to refute this finding.
Additionally, the government paid the workman arrears of salary differences after a previous petition was dismissed in relation to a subsequent period.
Therefore, there is no justification to deny the workman the benefit of the difference of salary for the earlier period covered by the impugned order.
