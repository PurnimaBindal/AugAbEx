Constitution of India, Article 16, Article 226 - Dismissal from service for lack of eligibility - Cancellation of industrial worker's appointment due to inadequate qualification - National Trade Certificate granted by ITI used to secure position - Certificate later cancelled by ITI due to respondent obtaining admission by unfair means - Employment with Corporation cannot be claimed until cancellation of certificate is overturned by competent court - Corporation's dismissal order based on cancelled certificate deemed appropriate - High Court's decision to set aside dismissal order found to have jurisdictional error.
