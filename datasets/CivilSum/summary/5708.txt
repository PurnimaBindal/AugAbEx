Pension Regulations for the Army, 1961, Regulation 113(a) - Dismissal - Pension - An Ex-Havaldar petitioner was dismissed from service under the Army Act, hence not eligible for pension and gratuity based on previous service.
However, the petitioner can still make a representation to seek discretionary power of the President under Regulation 113 for receiving pension or gratuity.
[Paras 8 and 11]
