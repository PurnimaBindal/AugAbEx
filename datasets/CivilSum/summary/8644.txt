Constitution of India, Article 226 - Interim order - Arrears of salary - In a writ petition, an Angan Wari Worker sought a direction from the government to pay arrears of salary.
A single judge granted an interim order directing the State Government to pay arrears of salary before deciding on the writ petition's merits.
Such an order cannot be issued at the interim stage since it may amount to the grant of the primary relief claimed in the petition.
Interim orders like this seriously jeopardize public revenue and adversely affect the budgets of the Government and Local Authorities.
Thus, the order of the Single Judge has been set aside.
[Paras 5 and 6]
