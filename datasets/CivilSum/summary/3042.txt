Under Punjab PWD (Electricity Branch) Rules, 1952, Rule 9(i), the plaintiff was claiming that he joined earlier than the other PEPSU employees, and thus, he received promotions earlier than them which entitled him to be senior to them.
However, the Trial Court dismissed his claim, but the decree was reversed on appeal.
As a result, the petitioner was directed to draw up the seniority list all over again.
If the plaintiff's/respondent's seniority is to be disturbed, he shall be given an opportunity of hearing.
Therefore, the defendant/board must draw up the seniority list all over again as per the plaintiff's date of joining as LDC, date of promotion to the rank of UDC, Assistant and Head Clerk, keeping in view the date of joining and promotions of others.
Finally, the petition was dismissed.
