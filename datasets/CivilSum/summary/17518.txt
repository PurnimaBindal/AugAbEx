Under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, employees in the subordinate offices of the Health Department receive lower pay scales than those of Superintendents and Head Clerks in other Departments.
The post of Head Clerk was filled by those who passed through three channels of postings, but in the Health Department, Clerks were directly promoted to the position.
Upgrading Head Clerks to the status of Superintendents resulted in two categories of employees being unequally placed.
Providing equal pay scales would be violative of Articles 14 and 16.
The respondents have acted in accordance with good reasons in law and fact and so there is no need for interference under Article 226 of the Constitution, resulting in the dismissal of the petition.
