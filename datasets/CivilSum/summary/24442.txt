Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Competent Authority - Quantum of punishment - The enquiry officer cannot recommend a specific punishment in the absence of prescribed statutory rules.
- The competent authority is not obligated to follow the recommendations of the enquiry officer, and may form its own opinion on the appropriate punishment after examining the material available.
- The punishing authority is not obliged to impose only a minor penalty if serious misconduct has been demonstrated, regardless of the enquiry officer's recommendations.
- Unless the punishment imposed is extremely disproportionate, the court cannot interfere in the matter of punishment.
[Paras 10, 12, 14, 16, 18 and 19]
