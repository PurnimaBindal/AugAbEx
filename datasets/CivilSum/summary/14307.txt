The appellant, a constable, was dismissed under Rule 1638(2) of the Punjab Police Rules, 1934 and Article 311(2) of the Constitution of India for serious misconduct.
The charge sheet alleged that the appellant had entered the house of a Municipal Commissioner while drunk, misbehaved with the commissioner's family, and demanded Rs. 10 when asked to leave.
The dismissal was upheld as the appellant's misconduct was of a grave nature.
