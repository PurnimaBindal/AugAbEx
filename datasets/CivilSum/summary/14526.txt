Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.8(2) - Seniority - Pertaining to Head Constables - Promotion within one district is regulated by Rule 16.8(2) -A Writ of Mandamus was filed to direct fixation of inter se seniority with Lower School Course passers from other districts according to date of passing - The petitioner does not have the authority to claim seniority with those working in other districts - Petition dismissed.
