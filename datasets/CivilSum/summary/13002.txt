The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 226 provide that medical courses, including housestaffship and internship, should be available to all students regardless of their chosen branch.
However, the State has not introduced an internees scheme for homoeopathic students like it has for allopathic and Ayurvedic students, which is considered discriminatory.
As a result, the respondents have been directed to introduce Housestaffship for the petitioners.
