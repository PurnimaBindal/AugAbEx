Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) (Class-I) Rules, 1976, Rules 15, 9(5), 10 and 11 - Nomination - Nomination under Rule 15 is not a right but subject to eligibility and suitability under the rules - The Government and Commission can decline a name or reject a candidate if found ineligible - A candidate who does not meet the conditions of eligibility prescribed in Rules 9(5), 10(2) or 11(2) cannot be nominated under Rule 15(2).
[Paras 8 and 9]  B Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 32 - Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) (Class I) Rules, 1976 - Interpretation of statute and rules - Clear or unambiguous statutes must be given effect to irrespective of consequences - The interpretation that advances the object of the legislation should be preferred.
[Paras 7 and 8]  C. Constitution of India, Article 14 - Natural justice - Nomination - Authorities have the power to scrutinise and reject any nominations that do not meet eligibility or conditions in the rules - Rejected candidates cannot claim violation of natural justice principles for non-grant of hearing before rejection of candidature.
