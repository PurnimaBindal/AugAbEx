Constitution of India, Articles 226 - House Rent Allowance - City Compensatory Allowance - When a notification identifies a specific area for HRA/CCA, the Court cannot consider a notification issued for a different purpose and enactment.
[Para 8]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Conditions of service - House Rent Allowance - City Compensatory Allowance - HRA and CCA as part of employee's conditions of service but can be unilaterally altered in conformity with Article 14; rectifying a mistake in granting benefits to employees not covered by notified area doesn't violate Article 14.
[Paras 9 and 10]
Constitution of India, Articles 226 - House Rent Allowance - City Compensatory Allowance - Each city may have different criteria for spread-over of offices, pattern of development, and problems related to housing and habitation; yardstick prescribed in one city cannot be applied to all.
[Paras 10]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - House Rent Allowance - City Compensatory Allowance - Natural justice - Prejudice - Court need not necessarily interfere in cases of violation of natural justice; factors such as genesis of action, reasons thereof, and reasonable possibility of prejudice weigh with the Court before deciding whether to interfere.
[Para 11]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - House Rent Allowance - City Compensatory Allowance - Employees should not be penalized for overpayment due to financial sanction by Competent Authority and incurring additional expenditure towards house rent.
