Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ petition by the persons aggrieved - Maintainability - Petitioners claiming to be guardians of students of the respondent school and having standing to move the present writ - Seeking writ of mandamus to direct the authorities to take action in terms of an order of District Inspector of Schools (DIOS) - DIOS decision not likely to affect the student class in general - Petitioners lack standing to move the writ as aggrieved persons.
