The West Bengal College Service Commission (Amendment) Act, 1992, Sections 9A and 9B, along with Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 are applicable to the post of lecturer in Political Science.
The selection process for this post involves an interview that is worth 100 points.
The Petitioner claimed that this was against constitutional provisions, but the court disagreed, stating that it was not.
The Petitioner did not pass the interview with a minimum of 50 percent marks, so he was not empanelled.
Additionally, no one who scored less than the Petitioner in the interview was empanelled.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to any relief.
[Paras 20 to 24]
