Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Removal from service - Absence - The petitioner was removed from service due to being absent from duty without leave.
Even though the petitioner was not a habitual absentee, the punishment given was deemed too harsh and disproportionate to the gravity of the charge.
Thus, directions were given to modify the earlier punishment to a more suitable and lesser one.
[Paras 4 and 5]
