The petitioner's request for compassionate appointment as a dependent of a Government employee who retired on medical grounds was denied due to the employee's retirement at the age of 55.
The petitioner did not challenge the retirement order and therefore the petition was dismissed.
[Para 4]
The government's policy of providing compassionate appointment on medical grounds is criticized for providing employment on hereditary basis, which is contradictory to the constitutional principles of equality.
The Chief Secretary suggested that the government should review the instructions for such appointments.
