Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Discharge - Opportunity of hearing - The appellant was recruited as a constable on 21788 and was discharged on 22.2.97 under rule 1221.
The record clearly shows that the appellant had engaged in misconduct.
The order of discharge was stigmatizing and the respondent did not give an opportunity before passing the impugned order.
It was ruled that the respondent was obligated to provide an opportunity for the appellant before passing the impugned order.
Consequently, the impugned order of discharge was set aside.
