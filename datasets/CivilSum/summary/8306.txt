According to Bank of Baroda Services Regulation, 1979, Regulation 20(3)(iii), an employee who is undergoing disciplinary proceedings will remain in service even after their superannuation.
If an individual has retired and a show cause notice for dismissal is issued against them on the basis of a criminal case with pending corruption charges, they cannot be dismissed.
The argument that the pending criminal case constitutes disciplinary proceedings has been rejected.
[Para 15]
According to Bank of Baroda Employees Pension Regulations, 1995, Regulation 45, individuals who retired before the commencement of the Regulations can still have their pension withheld or withdrawn.
This does not constitute a retrospective application of the Regulations.
