According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 226 and 227, along with Railway Protection Force Rules from 1957, Rules 72 and 239, the contention of the petitioner is that Rule 239 rewards and awards must be taken into consideration before promotion or placement.
Rule 71 sets a maximum limit of 20 for the record of service, which is primarily physical rather than mental, and viva-voce generally stands as a result.
The High Court has already made a judgment regarding this matter and instructed the Chief Security Officer to reconsider the case based on service records as per the Rules.
The authorities have been ordered to consider all aspects based on the High Court's previous judgment within a month's time.
