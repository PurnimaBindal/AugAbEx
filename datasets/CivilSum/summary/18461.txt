Constitution of India, Article 226 - Recruitment and Promotion Policy, 1989 (As applicable to Uttar Haryana Bijli Vitran Nigam Ltd.) - Clause 19(b)(i) - Reversion - Petitioner was reverted from the post of Lower Division Clerk to that of Store Attendant on the ground that they were promoted inadvertently, as promotion channel in the store organization is different and the same is governed by Recruitment and Promotion Policy of 1992.
However, it was held that even though there is a different Recruitment and Promotion Policy in respect of store keepers, it does not debar Store Attendants from promotion as Lower Division Clerks under the Policy of 1989, as they are governed by both policies.
The reversion of the petitioner from the post of Lower Division Clerk is not justified and is quashed.
