Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation of service - Appointment as Craft Teachers in Tailoring Centre in the District by District Rural Development Agency - DRDA was set up in each district by the Central Govt. and expenses were shared by State and Central Govts.
- In 1984, Craft Centres were abolished and incumbents could have lost their jobs.
To avoid hardship, the State Govt. directed BD & POs to accommodate Craft Teachers in the Panchayat Samitis.
Petitioners were adjusted as Craft Sahayikas and received a fixed wage of Rs. 525/- pm which was later revised and raised to Rs. 800.
While instructions issued by the Govt. from time to time for regularisation of ad hoc employees are not applicable to persons working as Craft Sahayikas with the Panchayat Samitis, the petitioners are not entitled to regularisation of their services.
[Para 5]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Pay scale - Regularisation of service - Petitioners were appointed as Craft teachers by DRDA, but were later given appointments as Craft Sahayikas by respective Gram Panchayats.
They were only appointed as such in fixed wages and are not holding civil posts under the State.
There is nothing on record that suggests the posts of Craft Sahayikas and Craft teachers form one cadre or have a common seniority list, and it cannot be held that they are holding the same posts.
Mere fact that duties are similar does not entitle petitioners to same scale of pay as is being given to Craft teachers.
Therefore, their claim for being put at par with the regular employees working as Craft Teachers cannot be sustained.
