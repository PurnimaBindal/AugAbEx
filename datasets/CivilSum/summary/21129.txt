Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 136 - Appointment - Reservation - The appellant, who belongs to the Scheduled Tribes, was not appointed as a Lecturer (Psychology) against the reserved vacancy.
However, after pending appeal, she was later selected and appointed.
Despite lacking laboratory experience initially, she went on to acquire it and was ultimately eligible for appointment.
As the post was reserved for the Scheduled Tribes and she was a available candidate belonging to that category, her appointment cannot be deemed illegal.
