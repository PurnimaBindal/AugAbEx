Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Resignation - It is established that the employee stopped reporting for work after August 1983, indicating a desire to end the employment contract.
For over four years, the employee remained silent until 1987 when they attempted to withdraw their resignation letter from March 14, 1983.
Despite continuing to make representations, the last of which was rejected on June 13, 1990, the employee took no further action.
The writ petition was filed using the rejection as a cause of action.
The evidence shows that the employee had intended to terminate the employment relationship with the letter from March 14, 1983.
There is no possibility of being rehired after such a long gap simply because there was no formal communication accepting the resignation.
