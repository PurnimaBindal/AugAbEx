The appointment of a Vice-Principal In-charge of Evening Classes in Delhi University is subject to the selection process.
The governing body must make the appointment based on the recommendation of the Selection Committee.
The use of the word "wish" in the ordinance does not give the governing body the authority to act arbitrarily in making the appointment.
The governing body cannot claim absolute power to reject the selection made by the Selection Committee.
The petitioner is entitled to all consequential benefits due to the quashing of the governing body's order.
[Paras 9 and 15] B The Delhi University Ordinance XVIII(4)(4) prohibits arbitrary and capricious actions by the governing body.
The ordinance mandates that the appointment of Vice-Principal shall be made by the governing body upon the recommendation of the Selection Committee.
Any executive authority must act in a just, fair, and reasonable manner.
Arbitrary use of power is against the equality enshrined in Article 14.
An interpretation that would render the ordinance ultra vires of the Constitution must be avoided.
