Constitution of India, Article 226 - West Bengal Police Regulations 28(1) and 30(b) - Transfer - The transfer order was not violative of regulations 28(a) and 30(a) as Regulation 28(a) clearly sets a maximum posting period, and Regulation 30(a) only provides guidelines and not mandatory rules - Transfer is an incidence of service and not a condition of service - The Tribunal was correct to refuse to exercise discretion due to the limited scope of judicial review in transfer matters - No grounds to interfere.
