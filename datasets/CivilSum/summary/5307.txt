Section 10 of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970 pertains to contract labour employed for cleaning work in catering establishment and pantry cars of the Railways.
The Supreme Court issued orders abolishing the system and ordered the absorption of such cleaners in regular service.
However, three petitioners did not qualify for absorption and were subsequently rejected.
The District Commissioner Labour was tasked with adjudicating disputes and found that the petitioners were not employed on the date of the apex court's judgement, resulting in their rejection.
The petitioners' service was terminated/dismissed for misconduct without any show cause notice or domestic enquiry, violating the principles of natural justice.
The finding of the DC Labour was inconsistent, perverse, and unacceptable by any norm of law.
The respondents were directed to absorb the petitioners as cleaners with retrospective effect and provide all service benefits except back wages.
The writ petitions were allowed.
