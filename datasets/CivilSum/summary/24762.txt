Constitution of India, Article 226 - Evidence Act, 1872, Section 115 - Education - Admission - Admissions to Engineering Degree Course - An application by a Diploma holder who is working as an instructor in the Engineering College, made after the expiry of the last date for admission, was objected to by the Officer in charge of Admissions who claimed that the student had secured less than 60% marks in the Diploma Examination.
The application was later accepted after considering all the facts, including the above-stated objection.
However, the subsequent order of Dean putting the admission of the student in abeyance was held to be unsustainable.
