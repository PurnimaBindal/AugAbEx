The Railway Servant (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1968, Rules 9 and 14 state that termination without enquiry or opportunity is possible in cases of wilful absentism.
As per rule 14(ii), the disciplinary authority can forego an enquiry if it is not practical and provide written reasons for doing so.
In this case, it is evident from the record that all efforts were made to furnish the charge sheet to the petitioner but were unsuccessful.
Therefore, it is established that the removal order for the petitioner is justified under these circumstances.
