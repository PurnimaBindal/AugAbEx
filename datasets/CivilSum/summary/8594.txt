As per the Army Act of 1950, Section 191, Army Rules of 1954, Rule 13, Item III(v), as well as Army Regulations, Para 143, discharge on medical grounds may result in re-employment on an alternative job on medical grounds.
The petitioner was placed in the medical category `B' (permanent) and was subsequently discharged from service after completing a qualifying service of 10 years.
While he cannot again claim re-employment invoking his disability, as he has already availed this opportunity, he has not been found eligible for the job he is claiming due to his placement in Medical category `B'.
