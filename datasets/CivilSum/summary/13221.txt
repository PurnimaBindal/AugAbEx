Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2, Item 6 - Standard Code, Rule 50 - Deserter - Definition of - According to Rule 50, an employee who is absent from work for more than thirty days without any permission will be considered as a deserter - absence period of twelve days should be treated as leave without pay and cannot be included in the thirty days absence for Rule 50.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2 Item 3, Section 25F - Termination - Validity - The protection provided for workmen by Section 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act is intended to subserve a public purpose and should not be narrowly construed - Considering the petitioner as a workman within the meaning of Section 2(s), the termination of petitioner was in violation of the provisions of Industrial Disputes Act and is therefore set aside - The petitioner has been ordered to be reinstated with 50 percent back wages.
