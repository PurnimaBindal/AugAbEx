Constitution of India, Article 14 - Seniority - Absorption - Inter se seniority of employees absorbed in other government departments after being declared surplus from their original department - The seniority of these individuals is based on their absorption dates and cannot be based on their original department's seniority - If there was no policy to absorb all employees from the wound-up department, the senior employee retrenched and absorbed later would have a junior rank in the absorbing department.
