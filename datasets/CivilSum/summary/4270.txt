Service Law - Quantum of - Punishment - To determine the appropriate punishment, previous conduct and record must be taken into account.
Gurcharan Singh v. Punjab State, 1988-2 PLR 370.
[Para 4]
Service Law - Dismissal - A plaintiff made comments and shook a table but the allegations made were not serious.
After serving for 18 years with no previous complaints and being an ex-serviceman, dismissal was too severe a punishment.
Instead, the plaintiff should only have two increments stopped with a cumulative effect.
[Paras 4 and 6]
Service Law - Dismissal - Quantum of punishment - The High Court could intervene in the punishment given if the punishing authority did not apply its mind and ignored the nature and gravity of the allegations.
