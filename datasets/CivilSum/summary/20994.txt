Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination/Removal from service - Head Security Guard - Short absence from duty - Evidence - Appellant found not guilty - Appellant had gone to convey message to higher authorities to make a report about theft of coal - Despite being found not guilty, he was removed from service - Punishment of removal from service is excessive compared to the charge against him - The decision is overturned and the respondent is directed to reinstate the appellant without delay.
