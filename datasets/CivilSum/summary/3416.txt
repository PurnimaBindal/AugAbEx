Article 226 of the Indian Constitution grants disability pension, but a petitioner with an assessed disability of 100% by the Medical board only gets benefits for 50% of disability.
The petitioner argued that disease No1 worsened as a result of air force service, but there was no connection between service and disease No.2.
The Medical board assessed 50% disability for the first disease and 100% for the second disease.
The court held that it was a composite 100% disability and that the second disease was independent and attributable to air force service.
Therefore, the court ordered that the petitioner receive benefits for a 100% disability.
[Para 8]
The court ruled that the High Court has the right to assess the opinion of the Medical board independently rather than accepting it as binding.
The opinion of the Medical board is only persuasive and cannot go against the statute.
Therefore, the opinion of the Medical board cannot bind the High Court’s opinion.
