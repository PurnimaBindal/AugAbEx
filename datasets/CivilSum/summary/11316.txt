The grant of selection grade and promotion to a higher cadre or service should follow the same criteria, including the standard of confidential reports and passing of departmental examination.
A petitioner found fit and granted selection grade despite adverse remarks cannot have those same remarks used as a basis for stopping increments at the efficiency bar stage.
These adverse remarks are washed off after the selection grade is granted.
[Para 5]

While a rule requiring the competent authority to communicate adverse remarks within seven months is directory in nature, it should still be substantially complied with.
Failure to do so, as in the case where adverse remarks were communicated after 27 months, constitutes unreasonable delay and renders the remarks unreliable for use in withholding increments at the efficiency bar stage.
[Para 6]
