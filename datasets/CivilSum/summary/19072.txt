Constitution of India, Article 226 - Vacation of Govt. accommodation - Penal rent - After declining the petitioner's request for allotment of Govt. accommodation, he was advised to vacate the house he was occupying.
In the meantime, during the pendency of the writ petition, the petitioner was asked to pay market rent at a rate of Rs. 785/- per month.
House Allotment Committee had directed to pay him rent @ 50 times the normal rent which comes to Rs. 4900/- per month.
However, it was held that since the petitioner vacated the premises due to transfer and had been paying the market rent, the penal rent @ Rs. 4900/- per month was unfair.
Hence, it was fair to direct him to pay the market rent for the period he remained in occupation of said premises, considering that he was a clerk drawing a meagre salary.
