Constitution of India, Article 226 - Departmental proceedings - Criminal proceedings - Memo of charge and charge-sheet both are based on the same set of facts and evidence - Proceedings in both cases are identical without any differences - Charge in the criminal case is serious and involves complex questions of facts and law - Decided - Because both proceedings are based on identical questions of facts and the charges against the employee in the criminal case are of a severe nature involving complex questions of law and facts, the departmental proceedings will be put on hold until the criminal case has been resolved.
