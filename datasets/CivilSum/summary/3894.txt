As per the Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Sections 2(K), 2-A and 10, a dispute related to retrenchment, discharge, dismissal or termination of service must be resolved by conciliation, and unless it is resolved, it is deemed to subsist.
In the present case, the workman raised a demand for reinstatement after termination, and conciliation was filed.
The government adjudicated the dispute and concluded that it did not subsist.
However, the government should not delve into the merits of the dispute and take upon itself the determination of the Lis.
Therefore, the impugned order of the government is set aside, and directions are given to reconsider.
