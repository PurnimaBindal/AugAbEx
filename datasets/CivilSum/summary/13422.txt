Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Absorption - Casual Guards - Although appointed on a `no work no pay' basis, service was extended from time to time.
- They are currently contending for the absorption and regularisation of services.
- The engagement of casual guards was not on a regular basis, nor were they selected or appointed following the normal procedure of appointment.
- Therefore, there can be no regularisation.
- However, they may be considered for appointment along with other candidates within the normal procedure and may be given weightage for experience.
