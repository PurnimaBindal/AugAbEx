According to UP Police Regulations, Regulation 520 and UP Police Manual, Para 313, the Deputy Inspector General of Police posted at Police Headquarters has jurisdiction over the entire state and can transfer Inspectors or Sub Inspectors of Police from one range to another.
In a specific case, the petitioner, who was working as a Stenographer to the Superintendent of Police at Maharaj Ganj and had a pay scale equivalent to that of an Inspector or Sub Inspector of Police, was transferred to Radio Headquarters in Lucknow by the DIG.
The order for transfer was upheld.
