Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Conductor - Misappropriation charge - Labour Court found no illegality in enquiry - Penalty substituted from removal to 4 increment stoppage with cumulative effect - Acceptable judgment - Workman did not challenge legality of enquiry - Focus on loss of confidence, not misappropriated amount - Relying on BS Hullikatti's case, such conductors unfit for service due to financial loss to transport corporation - Labour Court overreached by applying Section 11-A of 1947 Act.
