The Haryana Veterinary Headquarter and Field (Group-C) Services Rules, 1999, Appendix-A, based on Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, mandates equal pay for equal work, and provides the same pay scale for the post of Mechanic Refrigerator whether the appointment is regular or temporary.
No further authority decision is necessary to grant any other pay scale, given there's no difference in the duties and responsibilities of temporary and regular appointees and the posts are transferable.
All are entitled to the same pay scales as per the rules.
