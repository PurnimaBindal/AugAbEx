Constitution of India, Article 311 - MP Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules 1966, Rules 15(3) and 15(4) - Removal/Disciplinary Enquiry - The enquiry report and show cause notice must be given to the petitioner in accordance with the provisions of MP Civil Service Rules.
Penalty of removal from service can be imposed, and a second show cause notice must be provided based on the enquiry report.
The order passed by disciplinary authority contained full reasons which were duly disclosed to the petitioner before taking impugned action and cannot be considered an unreasoned order.
The petition was dismissed.
[Paras 13, 14 and 17]
