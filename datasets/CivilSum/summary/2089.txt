According to Article 16 of the Indian Constitution and Sections 54(f) and 55 of the Calcutta University Act, an advertisement for the appointment of lecturers must be based on a resolution of the Syndicate that is final.
Section 54(f) establishes a minimum qualification only, but the advertisement in question fixed an essential qualification.
There were irregularities in complying with Section 54(f) of the Act, so the entire proceedings were quashed.
[Paras 5 and 6]

If the process of appointments is challenged and the appointment procedure is omitted, the High Court can still investigate all aspects of the case using its exceptional writ jurisdiction in accordance with Article 226 of the Indian Constitution.
