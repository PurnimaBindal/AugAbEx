Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provisions) Act, 1985, Sections 17 & 18 - Govt. company - Wages of workers of a Sick Industry - The State Government is not legally obliged to pay the wages of the workers of a company, even if it was a subsidiary of a government company.
[Para 14] B Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provisions) Act, 1985, Sections 15, 17 & 18 - Sick industry - Revival or winding up of - Any delay in deciding a reference received by the Board (BIFR) under Section 15 of the Act will worsen the industry's condition significantly, adding to its liabilities and making revival impossible - The Board must dispose of proceedings within six months.
