Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - Relocation of Residential School - The first respondent is the supreme body responsible for overseeing the establishment and operation of Residential Schools, and thus, is the most suitable authority to determine the appropriate location for running a school.
High Court is not authorized to challenge the decisions made by the competent authorities responsible for running Residential Schools.
The petitioner, Parent Association, failed to provide any definitive criteria for the respondents to establish the school, except highlighting the drawbacks of relocation.
Consequently, the petitioner's request for relief is unwarranted.
[Paras 5 and 6]
