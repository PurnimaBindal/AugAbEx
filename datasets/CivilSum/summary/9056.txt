Service Matter - Abolition of post - Termination/Discharge - Plaintiff sues for declaration that resolution passed by Sugarfed is illegal and void, and that he continues as an employee of Markfed - Plaintiff was appointed as a Plant Protection Expert for Markfed, but after six months, termination order was passed - Plaintiff's services were lent to Sugarfed during the appeal - Chief Minister ordered salary for gap period to be paid by Markfed - Plaintiff was held to be an employee of Markfed and entitled to consequential benefits - Both Markfed and Sugarfed should pay wages to plaintiff equally since he joined Sugarfed - [Paras 10, 13, 14, and 15] B. Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961, Section 69 - Order of termination was declared null and void, and plaintiff was entitled to be recalled - [Para 15] C. Service Matter - Suit for declaration - Plaintiff's cause of action was when his prayer to be recalled was declined by Markfed - Plaintiff had no other recourse but to approach the civil court - Lower Appellate Court rightly concluded that the suit is within limitation for Markfed - [Para 17]
