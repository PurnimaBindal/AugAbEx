The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Reservation - Admission to Medical Courses - It was argued that 75 seats out of a total of 150 should have been treated for the general category instead of just 47.
However, it was held that only 47 seats were allotted to the general category and an additional 22 seats were taken from the All India quota.
Additionally, vertical reservation is only applicable up to a maximum of 50%.
