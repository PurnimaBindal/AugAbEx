The Institute of Engineers (India) Calcutta is considered to be performing a significant public duty by holding the test of AMIE courses that are recognized as equivalent to a degree in Engineering.
As a result, a writ petition against the Institution is maintainable under Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226.
[Para 5]

Although the Examination Rules, Rules 36 and 37, do not provide for the award of grace marks, the observations made by the Supreme Court in Panjab University v. Sunder Singh, AIR 1984 SC 919 in the case of Post Graduate Classes should apply to AMIE Courses examinations as well.
