Oriental Bank of Commerce Employees (Conduct) Regulations, 1982, Regulation 3(1) - Disciplinary proceedings - Removal of delinquent officer - Despite police closing case as unfounded, acquittal in criminal case does not bar drawing up disciplinary proceeding against officer - Departmental proceeding requires proof by preponderance of probabilities, not beyond all reasonable doubt - Loss of confidence case necessitates judicial review only when decision is tainted by mala fide or violates principles of natural justice and prejudice is proven - Otherwise, decision must stand.
