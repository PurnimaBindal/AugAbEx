Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Part - 1, Regulation 173 - Disability pension - Claim of pension cannot be opposed on the grounds of delay and latches.
[Para 5]
Army Pension Regulation, 1961, Part - 1, Regulations 173 and 186(1) & (2) - Disability pension - Service element pension - Qualifying service - Even if the disability pension is discontinued due to non-completion of qualifying service on recovery from disability, the service element pension cannot be discontinued for a person granted both pensions.
