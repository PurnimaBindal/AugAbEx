Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India pertain to the seniority of employees.
Ad hoc service can be counted towards seniority after regularisation if the ad hoc appointment was made according to the rules and only formalities are left to be fulfilled for regularisation.
If the ad hoc appointment was made only as a fortuitous and stop-gap arrangement, then the employee will only be entitled to seniority from the date of regularisation.
However, the employee can count their ad hoc service for all other benefits.
[Paras 5 and 7]  B Article 226 of the Constitution of India states that a writ petition can be filed for the benefit of ad hoc service towards seniority after a lapse of 20 years.
However, various direct appointees may be affected if the benefit is allowed.
The petition may also be dismissed due to delay and laches.
