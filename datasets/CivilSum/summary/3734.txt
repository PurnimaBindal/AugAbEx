According to various rules such as the Haryana Municipal Employees, Pension and General Provident Funds Rules, 1993, Rule 2(1)(III), Haryana Municipal Service (Integration, Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1982, Rules 15(3) and 18, and Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume II, Part-I, Rule 316(a), previous retirees are not entitled to pension benefits.
The exclusion of some government employees from receiving a pension is explained in Rule 316(a), Note-I of Pb.
Civil Service Rules.
If the rules do not provide a particular benefit or exclude it specifically, the court will not issue a direction against the rule just because of hardship faced by the person.
Therefore, the court cannot direct payment of pension on such ground if the Rule does not so provide.
