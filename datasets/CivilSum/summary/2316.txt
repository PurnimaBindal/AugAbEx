Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Part I, Chapter XI, Rules 11.1(a) & 11.5 - Commutation of pension - The Punjab Govt. has issued instructions regarding commutation of pension for those who retire from its service on or after January 1, 1996.
They may commute pension equivalent to 40% of their basic pension and are eligible for restoration after 15 years from the actual date of commutation or when the commuted value is repaid by way of reduction from pension, not from the date of retirement or attaining the age of 70 in each case.
The commuted value for lump sum payment U/Rule 111(a) is to be calculated in accordance with the commutation table provided under Rule 11.5(2), using a factor of purchase given in the table of 12.
The Rules and instructions do not provide for application of multiplier of 15.
