Kerala State and Subordinate Services Rules, 1958, Rule 25(b)9 restricts candidates to apply to only one district for district-wise selection in response to the same notification.
However, this does not deny candidates the opportunity to apply to any post.
Candidates are free to choose any district, and the restriction is in place to avoid multiple applications and the futility of selection work if a candidate is selected in multiple districts.
Kerala State and Subordinate Services Rules, 1958, Rules 25(b)9 and 29 require the admission notice to be truthful, and candidates should not conceal any information.
Candidates cannot claim ignorance of the consequences of applying in response to the same notification in other districts, despite clear warnings.
Even if the seats are available, candidates' conduct does not warrant any relief.
(Paras 16 and 18)
