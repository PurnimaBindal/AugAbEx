The petitioners, who worked as Senior Lecture Assistants, claimed parity in pay scales with Laboratory Technicians and Assistants/Accountants.
However, the court found no evidence of similarity in the qualifications and nature of duties between these positions.
Despite the fact that these other positions were given identical pay scales, the principle of equal pay for equal work cannot be applied in this case.
As a result, the court dismissed the petition with the possibility of seeking remedies within the department.
