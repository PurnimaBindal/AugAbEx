Constitution of India, Article 16 - Recruitment - Section Process - Complaints about malpractice in recruitment process - The decision to scrap the recruitment process by the competent authority cannot be judged from judicial or even quasi-judicial standards.
Even a reasonable suspicion of corrupt means or malpractice is enough to scrap the recruitment process, and the need to maintain public faith in the process is also sufficient.
The competent authority does not need to hold a formal enquiry that follows the principles of natural justice.
The opinion of the learned single Judge that the decision to scrap recruitment was arbitrary cannot be justified.
[Paras 18 and 19]
