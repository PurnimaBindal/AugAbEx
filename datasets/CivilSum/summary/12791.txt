Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Compassionate Appointment - Financial hardship - Limitation - Delay in making request for compassionate appointment - When the father of the petitioner passed away in 1991 while serving LIC, his mother was gainfully employed by Punjab Govt. - Application filed by petitioner after 2-1/2 years - As per instructions of LIC, such requests are entertained only if made within one year from the date of death and aim to assist the bereaved family to overcome hardship due to loss of their breadwinner.
Therefore, petitioner is not entitled to compassionate appointment.
