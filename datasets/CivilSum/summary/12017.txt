As per the Constitution of India, 1950, Article 226, promotion to the post of Air Vice Marshal is branch specific, and therefore, the petitioner from the flying branch cannot be promoted to the post of Assistant Chief of Air Staff (Education) as it has to be occupied by an Education Branch officer.
Additionally, the court cannot direct the respondents to shift an officer from the flying branch to the Education Branch to create a vacancy.
There needs to be a clear vacancy before the petitioner's claim can be considered.
[Paras 9 and 10] B.
The scope of interference in regards to a claim for promotion is limited.
The government may utilize a vacancy in exigencies of service, but a writ court cannot direct it to do so without there being a legally enforceable right the petitioner can demand.
