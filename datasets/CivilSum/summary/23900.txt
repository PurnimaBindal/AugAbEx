Regional Rural Banks Act, 1976 - Regional Rural Banks (Appointment and Promotion of Officers and other Employees) Rules, 1988 - Promotion - The High Court ignored the counter affidavit filed on behalf of Bank, detailing respondents' service record and stating they were not found fit by Selection Committee, for the promotion to the post of Senior Manager/Area Manager.
Therefore, the impugned judgment of High Court has been set aside and the matter has been remitted back to High Court for a fresh decision.
