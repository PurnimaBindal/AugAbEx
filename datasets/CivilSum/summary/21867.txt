State Bank of Patiala, Voluntary Retirement Scheme, 2001, Clauses 5 and 8 - Withdrawal of the voluntary retirement offer - The right to withdraw can only be exercised within the timeframe provided by Clause 5 which is 15 days - Applicants cannot exercise this option within the employer's time under Clause 8 - Allowing such requests would result in the failure of the scheme because the bank cannot finalize the same - SBPVRS and SBIVRS are similar - The Supreme Court's ruling in Bank of India v. OP Swarnakar is applicable to both.
