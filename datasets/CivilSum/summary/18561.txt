According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, Multipurpose Health Workers should receive timely payment of their salaries.
Despite the fact that the petitioners are regular employees, the State Government has not paid their salaries on time.
The State Government is responsible for paying the salaries to the employees no later than the 7th of each month, regardless of the source of funds.
