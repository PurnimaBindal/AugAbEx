Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-B(1) - Continuous service - The benefit of Section 25-B(1) can be attracted by any uninterrupted period of service, and not a particular period, irrespective of whether it is from January to December.
[Paras 25 & 29] B.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-B(1) & (2) - Continuous service - The requirement of 240 days is mentioned only in sub-section (2) and not in sub-section (1).
Mere completion of 240 days of work within 12 continuous months may not entitle the workman for the benefit of sub-section (1).
The period of 240 days is not relevant for sub-section (1).
[Paras 27, 28 and 30] C. Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-B(1) & (2) and 25-F - Continuous service of 240 days - The computation of 240 days in Section 25B(2) is restricted to a period of 12 continuous months preceding the date of termination and not any other period.
