The Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rule 8 - Government Instructions - Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - If a loan is misutilised, recovery of a house building loan can be made - The petitioner was granted a loan for the purchase of a residential plot and construction thereon, but he purchased a commercial plot with the advance taken by him meant for the purchase and construction of a residential house - Mortgaging a plot does not absolve the petitioner from allegations of misutilisation of Govt. money - The petition has been dismissed.
[Para 6]  C. Loan - Misutilisation - Govt.
Instructions - If instructions had not been issued in the case of misutilisation of the loan, the Govt. would still be within its right to recover the amount in lumpsum - General instructions for the recovery of the loan in a lumpsum, along with penal interest, were issued by the Govt. in 1973 - Although the loan was not fully disbursed when the instructions became operative, the Govt. has the right to recover the amount in a lumpsum - The instructions would be applicable.
