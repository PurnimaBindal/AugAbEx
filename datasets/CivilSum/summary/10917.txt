Constitution of India, Article 226 - Central Civil Service (Conduct) Rules, 1964, Rule 3(1)(i) - Dismissal - Opportunity of hearing - Natural Justice - The Enquiry Officer exonerated the petitioner on one charge and proved the other charge partially - The Disciplinary authority disagreed with the conclusion reached by the Enquiry Officer and imposed the penalty of removal from service by holding both charges were proven against the petitioner - The petitioner argued that the non-furnishing of tentative reasons for disagreement recorded by disciplinary authority to the petitioner violated the principles of natural justice - The Court held that tentative reasons for disagreement recorded by the disciplinary authority were not given to the petitioner - The impugned order of removal from service is invalid as it was passed without observance of the principles of natural justice.
