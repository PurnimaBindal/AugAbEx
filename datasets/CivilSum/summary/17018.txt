According to Section 10 of the Dental Act, a graduate in Dental Surgery from any authorized institution in India included in Part I of the Schedule is entitled to registration with the Dental Council.
The petitioner holding a degree of Bachelor of Dental Surgery from Punjabi University, which is included in Part I of the Schedule, was refused registration on the grounds that the petitioner's college had provisional affiliation with the Pb.
Dental Council.
However, since the degree was awarded by the University and the College was also granted provisional recognition by the Council, the refusal of registration is invalid and illegal.
The petition was allowed with costs of Rs. 10,000.
