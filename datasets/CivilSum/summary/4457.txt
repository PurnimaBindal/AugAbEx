Constitution of India, Articles 309, 311 - U.P.
Temporary Government Servant (Termination of Service) Rules, 1975, Rule 2 - Termination - Petitioner's service was terminated without inquiry on the basis of being a temporary government servant - However, since the petitioner was appointed under compassionate grounds, the appointment should be treated as permanent under Dying in Harness Rules - Therefore, the termination is not justifiable.
