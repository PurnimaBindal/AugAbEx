Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Industrial Employment (Standing Order) Central Rules, 1946, Clause 14(4)(c) - Dismissal from service after conducting departmental enquiry - Employee not given report of Enquiry Officer after conclusion of enquiry and not given opportunity of hearing against proposed penalty before passing order of dismissal - Clause 14(4)(c) of the Standing Order not mandatory - Non-compliance with this requirement cannot be held to be more vitiating than non-supply of enquiry report - Employee can only be formally reinstated for limited purpose of enabling employer to proceed with enquiry from stage of providing copy of enquiry report - Employer can place employee under suspension for completing enquiry - On completion of enquiry, if employee exonerated disciplinary authority shall decide according to law how to treat intervening period from date of dismissal to date of reinstatement and consequential benefits - If employee found guilty, before taking final decision he must be heard on proposed penalty in accordance with clause 14(4)(c) of the Standing Order on the quantum of punishment.
[Paras 10 and 11]
