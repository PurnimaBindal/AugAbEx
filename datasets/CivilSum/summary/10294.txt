Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4(1)(c)(ii) - The Commissioner for Workmen's Compensation granted compensation to an individual assessed with a bodily injury of 15%, based on a percentage of disability of 100%.
However, the High Court established the disability at 50% for the Non-Schedule Injury after reviewing the facts.
