According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 300-A, pension and retiral benefits cannot be withheld due to lack of a no dues certificate or incomplete service book.
It is the responsibility of the office to issue the certificate and complete the book.
Any delay in releasing these benefits can cause hardships for the retiree and financial loss for the government, and if benefits are in fact delayed, the retiree deserves interest on the withheld amount.
