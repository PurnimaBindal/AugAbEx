Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana Municipal Service (Integration, Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1982, Rule 18 - Gratuity - The petitioner, who retired from the Municipal Committee in 1969, was denied gratuity at that time due to the withdrawal of the scheme.
However, their case warrants consideration in light of the decision made in CWP No. 2173-CIII (Misc.) of 1983 Udham Singh Chauhan v. Municipal Committee, Ambala (P&H).
The respondents are directed to review the petitioner's case for the award of gratuity in accordance with Udhan Singh's case.
