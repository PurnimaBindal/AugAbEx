Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Disciplinary authority - Compulsory retirement - The petitioner was accused of making a false LTC claim and not actually traveling, resulting in an order of compulsory retirement, which was subsequently upheld by the Appellate Authority.
The Appellate Authority also directed that the period between termination and reinstatement be treated as dies-non, a decision that should not be interfered with.
