Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - No promotion path exists from the position of Instrument Repairer - even if the petitioner is more qualified for a position other than that of an Instrument Repairer, he cannot claim any other position outside the promotion channel.
-The state holds full discretion in providing promotion channels - The petitioner may request the government to establish a promotion channel from the position of Instrument Repairer to the position of Foremen - The competent authority may consider the petitioner's request sympathetically.
