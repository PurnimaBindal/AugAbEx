Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 136 - Termination/Delay laches- The termination of the petitioner's services was challenged in 1989, 22 years after it occurred and without any explanation for the delay.
The petitioner invokes Article 14 on the grounds of equality and non-discrimination, citing the cases of other employees who were reinstated in similar petitions.
However, the petitioner's unexplained delay in approaching the court distinguishes them from those who sought relief in a reasonable timeframe.
Article 14 is an equitable principle, and any relief claimed on that basis must be founded on equity.
Granting relief in this case would be inequitable, and therefore, it is declined.
