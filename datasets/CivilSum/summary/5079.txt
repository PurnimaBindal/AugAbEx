Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11B - Misconduct - Criminal proceedings - Termination of services - Workman found guilty in departmental enquiry of serious misconduct of causing death of a person - Even if acquitted in criminal case, employer can proceed with disciplinary proceedings - Workman, a driver, caused accident and left injured person unattended to die - Reinstating such a workman with 40% back wages would be disproportionate - Labour court's order quashed.
