The Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Sections 2(12) and 38 are applicable to the petitioner who runs a petrol pump and a service station employing 7 to 8 persons and was called upon to contribute by the ESI Director.
The petitioner challenged the notification as invalid, but it was held that the notification did not amend the definition of factory but rather included establishments engaging in manufacturing with power employment of 10-20 persons.
As such, the notification is valid and enforceable.
