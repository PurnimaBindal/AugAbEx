Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Selection - Posts of Draftsman - The Selection Board advertised 21 Draftsman posts available in 2 departments.
Candidates were not required to specify department preference, and daily wage work in one department did not entitle the appellant to a job in that department.
Only the government as employer may allocate Selectees/Appointees to different departments.
[Paras 7, 8 and 11]
