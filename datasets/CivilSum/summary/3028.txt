Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Rajya Sabha Secretariat (Methods of Recruitment and Qualifications for Appointment) Order, 1974, Clauses 2, 3 - Promotion - Post of Senior Assistant - The petitioner argued that he has seniority over other promoted employees as he joined as an Assistant whereas they were appointed later - However, the court dismissed the argument stating that there is confusion regarding direct recruitment and promotion and the petitioner failed to account for the distinction between temporary and permanent employees - Respondent Nos. 2 to 6 were appointed through direct recruitment as Senior Assistants, while the petitioner was promoted to the same post later - The court held that the petitioner cannot claim seniority over others and hence dismissed the petition.
