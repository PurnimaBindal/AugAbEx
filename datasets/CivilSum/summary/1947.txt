Constitution of India, Article 226 - Alternative remedy of civil suit - Writ not maintainable - If someone decides to pursue the remedy of civil suit, they cannot later choose to pursue a writ under Article 226 of the Constitution for the same cause of action by withdrawing the suit for that purpose.
Writ petition is not appropriate if alternate remedy exists for seeking the same relief.
[Para 9]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Recommendations - Disbandment of Panel of Three Names - Once the person at No. 1 of the panel accepts appointment, the panel is disbanded.
No further appointments can be made to any future vacancies without following the selection process afresh.
Appointment from the disbanded panel will be void ab-initio.
