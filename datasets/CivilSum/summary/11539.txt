Constitution of India, Article 226 - Government of India letter No. 20(1)/98/D (Pay/Services) dated 3rd August 99 - Entitlement to Ex-gratia compensation in the event of Death due to accidental discharge of rifle while on duty - Court of Inquiry holds it is a case of Battle Accident - According to scheme provision to provide compensation disregarding deceased's personal default or contributory negligence - Petitioner must receive compensation along with 9% interest.
