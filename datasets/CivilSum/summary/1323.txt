The petitioner who was appointed as part-time on a non-sanctioned post under the U.P.
Intermediate Education Act, 1921, Section 7-AB, 7-A and Section 7-AA, cannot claim regularisation under the provisions of the UP Secondary Education Service Commission and Selection Board Act (5 of 1982), Section 33-A This is because Section 7-AB of the U.P.
Act No. 5 of 1982 makes it explicitly clear that its provisions shall not apply to part-timeers employed in institutions under Section 7-AA of the Act.
Therefore, the petitioner, who served for about 11 years, is not entitled to any relief.
