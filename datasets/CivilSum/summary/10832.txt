Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2 Item 3, Section 25F - Reinstatement - Daily rated employee - If a daily rated employee has completed more than 240 days in one calendar year, they are entitled to reinstatement in case of a violation of Section 25F of Act - In this case, new persons were engaged thereafter - The Labour Court initially held that the Petitioner was not entitled to relief, whereas a similarly situated workman was reinstated - However, the application of different yardsticks under the same facts renders the award illegal and unconstitutional - Hence, the Petitioner is entitled to reinstatement with continuity and full backwages.
