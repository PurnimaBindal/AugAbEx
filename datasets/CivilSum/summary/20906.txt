The Bombay Shops and Establishments Act, 1948, Section 7 states that companies dealing in Tea being sold in pushcart manually operated by a labourer, and sales offered in market from door to door must register.
Failure to do so can result in prosecution.
However, the matter is considered very old, and mobility of goods by push-carts is now a thing of the past.
The State has assured that companies would not be prosecuted, so the question of whether registration was necessary was not decided.
The matter is closed accordingly.
