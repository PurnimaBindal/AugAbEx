Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - U.P.
Recruitment of Service (Determination of Date of Birth) Rules, 1974, Rule 2 - Correction of date of birth recorded in an employee's service book - Correction is permissible even if the recorded date is based on presumption and is incorrect - Presumption is rebuttable - Correction's permissibility is subject to facts and circumstances of each case.
