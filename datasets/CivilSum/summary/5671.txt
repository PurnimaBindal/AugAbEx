Constitution of India, Articles 16, 14 and 311(2) - Absence from duty without permission - Holding of ex-parte enquiry - Dismissal from service retrospectively - The High Court determined that while the order of the first appellate court was not sustainable, the two parts of the order terminating services and giving retrospective effect to the order from the date of employee's absence were clearly severable.
The court found that assuming the second part of the order was invalid, the first part of the order terminating the services from the date the order was made could still be valid.
The court allowed the appeal and directed the department to hold a fresh enquiry against the appellant and pass a fresh order after hearing him.
