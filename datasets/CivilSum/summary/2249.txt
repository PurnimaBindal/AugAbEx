Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Sections 14 and 15 - Writ petition claim - State Administrative Tribunal has jurisdiction over the complaint of an affected person complaining about the State Govt.'s inaction to initiate recruitment - High Court lacks the inherent jurisdiction to entertain writ petition as court of first instance - Petition is not maintainable and is dismissed.
