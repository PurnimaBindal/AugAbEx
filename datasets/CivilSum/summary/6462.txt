Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Central Services (Medical Attendant) Rules, Rule 6(1)(A) - Entitlement to medical reimbursement - If the government recommends treatment at an approved institute, they cannot deny full reimbursement even if the charges exceed the approved package rate.
The government fixed the package rate charged by private hospitals, so there is no reason to argue for different rates to be paid.
