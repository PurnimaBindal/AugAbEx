Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Petitioner, who is a handicapped graduate in Agriculture Science holding 456% aggregate marks, is contending against the requirement of 50% to post of Trained Graduate Teacher in Social Science.
The contention is the exclusion of Agriculture Science subject in the post of TGT Social Science advertisement.
However, it is held that Agriculture Science is not an elective subject in the post and the petitioner is disqualified for not having the necessary 50% marks.
Furthermore, petitioner is unable to demonstrate any exemption in eligibility being a physically handicapped.
[Paras 5 and 6]
