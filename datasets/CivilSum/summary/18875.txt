Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - The petitioner was deemed medically fit upon joining the Air Force but developed a mental disorder due to the stresses of escort duty for a mental patient - Diagnosed as "Affective Disorder Mania" - The petitioner was invalidated out of Air Force service and received a 40% disability assessment - No evidence exists to show that the disease was constitutional - The respondent authorities were directed to release disability pension to the petitioner from the date of his discharge.
