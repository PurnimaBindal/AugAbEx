Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Section 2(s) - Workman - The question of the employee's status as a workman must be determined before any further relief can be granted by the Labour Court - The court must frame the question as a preliminary issue and make a finding before proceeding - The employee's right to interim relief is dependent on this finding.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Domestic enquiry - Any judicial or quasi-judicial authority must act with due diligence, fairness, objectivity, and must provide reasons for any actions taken, whether interim or final - Failure to do so cannot be allowed even if the respondent is ex parte.
