The All India Council for Technical Education Act, 1987, Sections 2(h) (g), 3, 10, 23(1) - All India Council for Technical Education Regulation, 1994, Regulation 2(2) [as amended by 1997 amendment] - Starting M.B.A. or MCA Course - The college was permitted by the university to start M.B.A./MCA Courses - Pursuant to 1997 amendment all Post Graduate courses in field of Technical Education are exempted from Regulation made by AICTE - AICTE issued order to University stipulating that admission should not be made without AICTE approval - AICTE has no power to direct University to get approval from AICTE for M.B.A./MCA Course as post-graduate courses in technical education are specifically exempted.
