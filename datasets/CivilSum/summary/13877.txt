According to Section 2 of the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights & Full Participation) Act, 1995, employees with Locomotor Disability cannot be prematurely retired without first examining if they can be rehabilitated in an equivalent or lower post, as long as they possess the necessary qualifications and medical fitness.
The respondent's decision to prematurely retire the petitioner based solely on an in-house medical examination was not proper.
The respondent cannot claim termination due to medical disability while also claiming insulation against the requirements of the Disabilities Act and their own resolution.
Therefore, the petitioner must report back for duty.
