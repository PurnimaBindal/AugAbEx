Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Termination from service - Management can terminate employment without issuing show-cause notice or providing opportunity of hearing if they are able to prove facts before the labour court.
The labour court's finding, supported by oral statements of witnesses, that the worker had committed embezzlement and made admissions, meant that no opportunity of hearing was required as Management is entitled to avail opportunity either at stage of termination or before Labour Court, due to the gravity of the worker's behaviour.
