Constitution of India, Article 14 - Correction of date of birth - Despite the petitioner consistently accepting and acting upon his date of birth being 16.8.51 by declaration in writing, appropriate authorities have discretion to decline the request for a change of date of birth, which cannot be faulted on the basis of arbitrariness or discrimination.
[Paras 5 and 10] B.
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Correction of date of birth - Latches - Laches is a material factor in accepting or denying relief in a claim for correction of date of birth, and the authorities declining to accept the petitioner's request after the lapse of over 30 years cannot be faulted.
[Para 5] C. Correction of date of birth - In service record - There is no one-size-fits-all formula for the correction of a date of birth in a service record, and the peculiar facts and circumstances of each case must be considered.
[Para 8] D. Correction of date of birth - A person cannot retract from a declaration of their date of birth made in their own handwriting upon joining service unless there were truly exceptional and compelling circumstances of a bona fide mistake.
