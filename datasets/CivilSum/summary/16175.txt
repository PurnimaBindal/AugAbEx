Constitution of India, Articles 300-A and 226 - Pension - The petitioner was granted pension under the Freedom Fighters Scheme by the Central Government on the recommendation of Delhi Administration.
In 1982, the State of Punjab announced that additional pension would be given to freedom fighters who were already receiving pension from the Central Government.
The petitioner fulfils the eligibility criteria under the 1982 Scheme and is receiving pension from the Government of India as he is a Sikh/other migrant who has settled in Punjab.
However, the meagre amount of pension granted by both the Central and State Government makes it impossible for any person to make ends meet.
If the petitioner has to shift to Chandigarh and stay out of the territorial limits of Punjab, they cannot be denied the benefit of Pension.
The petitioner is eligible for the grant of pension under the 1982 Scheme.
