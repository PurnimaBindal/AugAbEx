Punjab Jail Manual, Paragraph Cuccria, 576A - Accused convicted under Sections 302/307 IPC and sentenced to life imprisonment - Accused, being an income-tax payer and used to a better standard of life, is eligible for B Class facilities under Paragraph 576A The claim that Executive Instructions have superseded this paragraph and that the facilities have been withdrawn is invalid.
Paragraph 576-A is legally binding and cannot be superseded by executive instructions.
