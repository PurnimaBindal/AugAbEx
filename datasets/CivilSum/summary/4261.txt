According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India, when an employee's termination is based on a criminal conviction, the termination must be reversed if and when the conviction is set aside.
Even convictions for minor offenses must be disregarded unless the conviction renders the employee unfit to continue working.
The petitioner was not reinstated despite his conviction being set aside, and thus, was entitled to full back wages.
But, since the petitioner has already reached the age of superannuation, he can be compensated with the grant of 50% of back wages for the period he was kept out of the job illegally.
