Article 16 of the Constitution of India governs appointments and rules are not statutory.
The Board of Management can modify or change the rules in any manner and at any time.
[Para 13]
As per the Board of Management's competence to amend or alter rules, the qualifications for the post were duly revised.
The respondent No. 1 could not be considered for the post as he did not have the required qualifications.
