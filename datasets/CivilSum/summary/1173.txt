Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Senior grade promotion - High School Assistants - Qualifying service in the School Assistants cadre for senior grade is not based on service in lower categories like PD, LPS, or UPS Teachers.
The Kerala Pay Commission and Kerala Government Order of 16.9.1985 Clause (c) 10 provide for granting senior grades when no promotion opportunities are available.
Stage promotion to senior grade is provided after a specific length of service within a cadre.
