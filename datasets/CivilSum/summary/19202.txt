Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 138-A and 132 - Major punishment - Removal of name from Promotion List C-1 - The imposition of a major punishment results in an automatic removal of a person's name from Promotion List - The validity of Rule 138-A - Petitioner's name was removed from List C-1 due to punishment of stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect - Appellate and revisional authorities confirmed the orders - No defect in departmental proceedings - The Rule sets a uniform standard that a person who has received a major penalty is not eligible for promotion for six months, subject to good conduct - The provision is reasonable and not arbitrary.
