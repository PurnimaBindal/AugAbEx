According to Rule 43(a) and (b) of Bihar Pension Rules, 1950, the competent authority has the power to impose a 5% cut in the pension of a retiree on account of misconduct committed after retirement, however, the retention of a government quarter during extended periods of orders of the court cannot be considered unauthorized.
In this case, a mere retention of the quarter for 2 months more than the extended period does not warrant a cut of 5% on the pension.
The punishment is deemed excessive and charging a higher rate of rent during that period would meet the ends of justice.
