The Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 918, in conjunction with Rule 532-A of the Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, mandates that an officer other than those belonging to the IPS or HSPS who has completed 20 years of qualifying service may be compulsorily retired by the I.G.P.
without reason and without being eligible for special compensation.
An officer with a doubtful integrity record may be terminated in the larger public interest.
The petitioner, having been assessed as corrupt and found guilty of in-discipline, carelessness, and habitual absenteeism, was rightfully ordered to retire compulsorily in the larger public interest.
