Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, Sec. 3(b) - Punjab Civil Services Rules (Vol.I), Rule 8.137A - Maternity leave - Employees who give birth to a stillborn child cannot be classified as having an abortion or a miscarriage, and are therefore entitled to receive maternity leave instead of abortion leave.
[Para 5] B Maternity Benefits Act 1961, Sec. 3(b) - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Rule 8.137A - Maternity leave - An employee who has worked for one year and 11 months and 10 days, but who has been absent for six months and 27 days prior to giving birth to a stillborn child, is eligible for maternity leave and not abortion leave.
The maternity leave claimed by the petitioner cannot be treated as abortion leave.
[Para 5] C. Punjab Subordinate Court Establishment (Recruitment and General Conditions of Service) Rules 1997, Rule 14 - Termination of service of a temporary employee during the probation period - An employee who takes an abortion leave after giving birth to a stillborn child and who then remains absent for six months and 27 days is not entitled to receive termination of service.
District Judge's order may be appealed in the High Court.
