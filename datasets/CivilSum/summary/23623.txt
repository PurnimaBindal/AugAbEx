Reinstatement and re-employment of an individual is a right guaranteed under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226.
In a specific court case, the appellant was reinstated by court order, but went on to work for another employer.
The appellant is entitled to receive back wages as compensation until the day he obtained employment with the new employer.
[Para 3] B The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 11-B, specifies the appropriate punishment for various charges against an employee.
In a particular case, the employee was accused of falsely claiming Rs. 10 as rickshaw charges when he actually used a bicycle.
Dismissal was not a justified punishment for such a charge, and was therefore disproportionate.
