Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - The petitioner has been determined to have a disability of more than 20% by three Medical Boards and 50% by a Re-survey Medical Board.
However, the Chief Controller of Defence Accounts (Pension) has reduced the disability to 40% for a period of two years without any valid justification.
This action has been deemed illegal, arbitrary and baseless.
The respondents have been instructed to release the disability pension to the petitioner while evaluating it at 50%.
[Paras 4 and 5]
