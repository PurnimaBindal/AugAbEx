Kerala Education Rules, Chapter XXIV-A, Rule 9-A - Compassionate Appointment - Dying-in-harness scheme - The petitioner's father, who was a clerk, passed away on 31089.
However, the petitioner was a minor at that time.
She got married in 1993, and her husband is financially stable.
Furthermore, the petitioner's mother was a teacher at the same school and retired in 1999.
The petitioner was not dependent on her father on his date of death or at any point following it.
Thus, the petitioner is not eligible to claim employment under the dying-in-harness scheme.
