Constitution of India, Articles 311(2), 309 - Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules (1964), Rule 3(1)(i) to (iii) - Disciplinary enquiry - In the event that the enquiry is not completed within the time limit set by the High Court, the delinquent official must be reinstated - In the present case, the delinquent has taken advantage of this direction and has delayed the enquiry on various pretexts - Therefore, the ex parte order passed by the Enquiry Officer within the time limit on the basis of the material on record is not a violation of the principles of natural justice.
