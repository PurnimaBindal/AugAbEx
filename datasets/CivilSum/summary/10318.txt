Constitution of India, Article 226 - Removal - Departmental Enquiry - Natural Justice - Charges framed based on report of Investigation Officer - The bank cannot claim privilege of document and use it for its own purposes while denying it to the petitioner - It was held that non-furnishing of investigation report is fatal to the case - Enquiry conducted without furnishing investigation report is considered illegal and violates principles of natural justice - Therefore, the impugned order of removal was set aside.
