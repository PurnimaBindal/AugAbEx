Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Schedule 1, Part 2, Section 2 - Disablement - Loss of earning capacity - In a claim concerning an accident involving an auto-rickshaw where the vehicle turned turtle and the driver was injured, the Medical Practitioner assessed the percentage of disability at 40-45%.
However, there is no evidence that the workman is incapable of continuing to work as a driver or in any other capacity.
The Commissioner erred in assessing the loss of earning capacity at 100%.
