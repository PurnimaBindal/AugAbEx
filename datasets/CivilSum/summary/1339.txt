As per Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, Sections 2(8) and 52, in case of an employee's death, if it is due to an "employment injury", dependents benefits are given.
In a case where an employee died of `myocardial infraction', an ESI court had previously held that it was not due to an "employment injury".
However, it was later held that the death was indeed due to employment injury.
As a result, the appellants were entitled to all benefits that they were otherwise entitled to.
