Constitution of India, Article 226 - Increment - Selection Grade - A petition was filed for a direction to respondents to include ad hoc service for grant of proficiency step up by way of additional increments after 8/18 years of service - In light of the decision in Harjinder Kaur and others v. State of Punjab and others, CWP No. 11704 of 1989, the respondents were directed to consider the petitioners' claim after counting their ad hoc service for the purpose of granting senior and selection grades and proficiency step up as per Government instructions.
