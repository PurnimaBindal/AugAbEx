Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 311 - Border Security Force Act, Section 20(A) - Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rules 43 and 49 - Disciplinary proceedings - Punishment - Judicial review - The writ jurisdiction under Article 226 cannot interfere with disciplinary proceedings and punishment of a delinquent official found guilty of misconduct and assaulting their officer in charge, who received a fair opportunity to defend themselves and appeal their case.
