Constitution of India, Article 14 - Discrimination - Orders cannot be withdrawn after resignation acceptance - Contesting an election is not sufficient reason for resignation - If employees are allowed to resign for elections and rejoin after losing, it can cause mass involvement of government employees in politics - Discrimination only applies when equals are treated differently without rational basis - Previous order cannot be the basis for equality - Decisions in "State of Hy.
and another v. Ram Kumar Mann" and "Kartar Singh v. State of Hy.
and others" followed.
