According to Section 4(2) of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 and Section 2(b) of the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, the definitions of the term "wages" and "basic wages" respectively, have to be analyzed in their particular context as laid down by the legislation.
However, there is no significant distinction between the terms "wages" and "basic wages" in the context of excluding bonus from emoluments.
Regardless of their nomenclature, bonus cannot be included in the calculation of contribution to provident fund or gratuity, as it is excluded from the purview of both these Acts.
Therefore, it is inaccurate to claim that bonus is included in the concept of "wages" under the Act.
