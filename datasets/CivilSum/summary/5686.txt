Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Appointment -Confirmation - Termination - A provision in the appointment order that automatic confirmation in the service will not be granted - Issuance of confirmation letter by the company is a prerequisite to confirmation - During probationary/extended probationary period, an employee may be terminated without notice and without reasons - If confirmation is not granted after the probationary or extended probationary period, the probationer remains on probation and is not considered confirmed - The petitioner is not entitled to confirmation merely by virtue of the expiry of the probationary period.
