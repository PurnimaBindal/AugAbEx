The government has given legal standing to Respondent No. 3's status as XEN and recognized their duties as Executive Engineer.
The impugned order cannot be nullified due to the government's legal power under proviso to Rule 9(3).
[Para 10]
The Petitioner cannot challenge the relaxation of promotion experience given to Respondent No. 3 after taking advantage of the same provision - identical benefits have been given to both individuals and the relaxation was due to a lack of eligible persons.
The mere absence of reasons in the order is not enough to make the exercise of power invalid.
The petition is dismissed.
