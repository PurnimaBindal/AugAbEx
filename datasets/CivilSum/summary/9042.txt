The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection/Appointment - Merit List - The individual higher in the merit list has priority for appointment, even if a lower ranked candidate approaches the court for a direct appointment based on their own merit.
If the person lower in merit is appointed over the individual higher in merit, it would lead to perpetuation of inequality.
[Para 6 and 7]
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Delay and laches Appointment - Selection - Merit List - The petitioner, who was higher in merit, was ignored in favor of someone lower in merit who got relief from the court and was appointed.
The petitioner immediately filed a writ petition, which cannot be dismissed due to delay and laches.
[Para 5]
