Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Departmental enquiry - Natural justice - Senior Marketing Inspector dismissed from service for misappropriation - Despite not submitting reply to charge sheet, employer must hold an ex parte enquiry, provide copy of enquiry report and final show-cause notice with proposed action, and pass appropriate order after considering the delinquent's reply - Until then, the delinquent will be considered under suspension.
