Constitution of India, Article 216 - Bangalore University Regulation - Re-valuation Examination - Method for re-valuation- The method adopted for valuation applies to re-valuation when there are no separate rules or methods - Examiner appointed for re-valuation failed to award marks against answers but showed them in a separate list; method adopted is arbitrary and lacks rationality- University must appoint another examiner to re-value answer book of the petitioner accordingly.
