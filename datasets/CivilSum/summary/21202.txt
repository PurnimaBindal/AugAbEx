Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 48(1)(a) - Suspension - Pre-mature Voluntary retirement - The order of suspension must be served upon the employee to withhold the request for voluntary retirement - If the suspension order is misdirected or posted at any place of the employee's previous appointment, despite knowledge of his latest posting, the permission of voluntary retirement cannot be withheld.
