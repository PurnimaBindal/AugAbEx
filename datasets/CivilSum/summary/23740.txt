Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 39(d) - Equal pay for equal work - Language teachers in Vocational Education Institutes demand parity in pay with Lecturers in school cadre - Both sets of employees possess similar educational qualifications - State Government grants uniform pay hike to Post-Graduate Lecturers in higher secondary schools regardless of their academic excellence - Language teachers with Master's Degree entitled to same pay scale as school cadre lecturers with 2nd Class Master's Degree.
