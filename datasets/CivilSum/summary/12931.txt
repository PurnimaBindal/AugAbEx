Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 11 - Promotion - Petitioner claimed promotion by stating he cleared inter-departmental examination in 1992, but was issued a charge-sheet in 1993 and censured in 1998 - Procedure of sealed cover specified in OM dated 1491992 would not apply to a case where promotion is sought on basis of internal limited competitive examination - Held, instructions of 12.1.1988 and 1491992 do not provide for consideration of case of promotion upon imposition of penalty - Petitioner could not claim promotion on basis of inter-departmental examination - Director General of Personnel Instructions would not override instructions dated 1491992 issued by DOPT.
[Paras 6A and 7]
