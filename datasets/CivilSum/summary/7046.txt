Under the A. Pepsu Road Transport Corporation Employees Pension, Gratuity and General Provident Fund Regulations, 1992, employees must exercise the option to claim pension and refund their share of the contributory provident fund.
However, if there is no evidence on record to show that the pension scheme was circulated and noted by employees, the Corporation cannot deny the benefit of pension regulations for those who did not opt for it.
[Paras 8, 9, 11 and 12]
Although a suit claiming for pension was filed within 38 months of retirement, the arrears of pension payable may be curtailed to only three years and two months prior to the filing of the suit.
However, they will still be payable from the date of retirement which falls within 38 months.
[Para 16]
If a petitioner has already taken their Contributory Provident Fund, they cannot be denied a pension regulated according to the Pepsu Road Transport Corporation Employees Pension, Gratuity and General Provident Fund Regulations, 1992.
The Trial Court has already directed the plaintiff to refund their share of CPF with 9% interest.
