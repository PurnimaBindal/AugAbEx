Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Migration - Admission to MBBS, BDS/BAMS Courses - The Medical Council of India created an additional seat for the petitioner in the State Medical College at Amritsar and withdrew the same from the quota for the session which was to commence in the year 1993.
However, since the petitioner was allowed migration for the session commencing in the year 1992 by creating an additional seat, he should not be required to sit for the said entrance test.
[Para 3] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Migration - Joint test - The court in Pooja Ahluwalia v. State of Punjab has directed that 20% seats should be taken out of the purview of the joint test and filled by a committee keeping in view the peculiar facts of a particular case but subject to meeting prescribed conditions.
Since the petitioner's case falls under this category, the respondents cannot claim that he does not fulfill any other prescribed conditions.
The petition is therefore allowed, and the petitioner is directed to join the second professional course for the session 1992.
