Constitution of India, Article 226 - Standing Orders and Executive Instructions of DTC - Non-issuance of tickets - Misconduct - A conductor with DTC has been charged with non-issuance of tickets to four passengers who were caught by the checking staff - The conductor was charge-sheeted and found guilty after inquiry - He was removed from service but an industrial dispute is pending - Management sought approval of an Industrial Tribunal to remove the conductor from service - The Tribunal held that no misconduct was committed by the conductor - Standing orders or Executive instructions did not authorize the conductor's dismissal - The High Court upheld the order of the Tribunal - Attempts for an amicable settlement failed - Litigation lasted for 25 years - Rs. 75,000/- was awarded within two months as a full and final settlement of the claim, in addition to any previous amounts paid by court order.
