Constitution of India, Article 309 - Madhya Pradesh Class III (Non-Ministerial) Forest Service Recruitment Rules, 1967, Rules 11, 13 and 19 - Seniority - Post of Forest Ranger - The petitioner and respondent were recruited directly, not by promotion.
The direct recruits must complete two years of training before appointment.
The respondent underwent training for the period between 1979-81 and the authority treated the respondent as an appointee from 151980.
It was held that the respondent could not have been appointed in 1980 before completing his training.
Thus, the petitioner's stand that he is junior is outside the rules.
