Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Impartiality - Fairness in selection - Lecturer selection - Board members must be persons of eminence and impartiality - Their judgment on candidate suitability should be respected - Judicial review should only consider fairness of the decision-making process - If there is no indication of extraneous consideration, malice, or major irregularity that could affect fairness and objectivity, there should be no court intervention.
