Constitution of India, Article 300A - Revision of Pay and Allowances Rules, 1990 - Pension arrears - Interest - An Assistant Teacher in a school became eligible for a pension refund due to an excess amount drawn in the revised scale of pay, and approached the authority to ensure pension benefits were received, with the authority recovering the refundable amount from the petitioner's pension arrears.
The petitioner sought interest on the pension arrears, but the government committed no error as there was no valid reason to withhold benefits payable and no case made to direct the respondents to pay interest.
