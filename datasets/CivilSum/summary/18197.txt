Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Service (Medical) Attendance Rules, 1940, Rule 6 - Medical reimbursement - Cut off date - Petitioner received emergency treatment from Hero DMC Heart Institute, Ludhiana - Respondents' act of categorizing employees for medical reimbursement based on a cut off date is deemed unreasonable and petitioner's case is similar to another employee's - Order dismissing petitioner's claim is annulled.
