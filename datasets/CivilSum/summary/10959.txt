Constitution of India, Articles 14, 15 and 226 - Parity in pay scales - `Equal pay for equal work' - Petition denied for respondents claiming same pay scales and other benefits as regular Junior Medical Officers performing similar duties - Respondents appointed on purely contract basis rejected - Respondents have same qualifications and efficiently discharge their duties - Respondent-doctors took up employment on a contractual basis due to force of circumstances - Petitioners' appeal dismissed.
