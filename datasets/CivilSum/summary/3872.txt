Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay in Pension - Interest - Petitioner retired from service at superannuation age - Respondent failed to verify and forward pension papers - Tribunal ordered to verify and sign within seven days - Order complied after 23 months - Pension and gratuity granted after 30 months - Petitioner awarded 12% interest per annum for delayed period - Petition allowed.
