Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 14-B - Damages - Non-speaking order - Employer's contributions - Regional Provident Fund Commissioner imposed a flat rate of 100% damages for delayed payment of various items for the same period - However, the evidence shows that RPFC did not consider factors such as amount involved and duration of delay, number of defaults, period of delay, frequency of defaults and amounts involved - The impugned order to the extent it imposes penalty, is set aside - Case remitted to RPFC to consider the whole question afresh taking note of the norms set down.
