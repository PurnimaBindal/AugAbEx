The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947 pertains to a case of industrial dispute raised after 11 years with no explanation for the inordinate delay given.
The management raised a specific objection on the delay, and the Labour Court rightly found that the petitioner is not entitled to any relief.
A person who sleeps over their rights for an inordinately long time cannot normally claim sympathy of Court without giving a satisfactory explanation for delay.
It's important to note that proceedings in Court are time-consuming and public funds are spent.
