Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Benefit of grace marks - Petitioners in BAMS course claimed grace marks based on earlier syllabus/regulations - College changed affiliation and syllabus after admission - Court held that new regulations will govern petitioners' case - Petitioners cannot claim vested right based on previous regulations - Dismissed in accordance with Subash Chander and Munish Kumar Talwar's cases.
