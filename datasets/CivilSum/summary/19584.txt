The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 and the Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-F establish that if a permanent employee is being terminated due to a surplus of posts at a particular station, ad hoc appointees cannot be retained on the same or equivalent post and termination cannot occur without adhering to the requirements set forth in Section 25-F and 25-H of the Industrial Disputes Act.
Instead, the permanent employee should be placed in the position of the ad hoc appointees, or on any other equivalent or lower post if an equivalent post is not available.
