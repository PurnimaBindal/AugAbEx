Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - Limitation - A bifurcation was made to ensure the smooth functioning of the department - As a result, some applicants became junior to private respondents - However, the promotion granted to private respondents was based on a re-determined gradation list after the bifurcation - Thus, the promotion of private respondents was proper - Moreover, the applicants were aware of the anomaly in 1992 and 1994 but only filed an application in 1998 after their retirement - The Tribunal correctly dismissed their application and its order was proper.
