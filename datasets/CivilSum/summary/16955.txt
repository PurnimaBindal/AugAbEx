Central Civil Services Pension Rules, Rule 37 - Autonomous bodies - In order to qualify under Rule 37, control from the Government and ratio of finance are both considered - The Tea Research Association, which receives only 50% of its funding from the Government, is considered an autonomous body - Denying pensionary benefits to employees who have served such a body would be technical and unjust - The source of funding, whether direct or indirect, is irrelevant if it comes from the Government.
