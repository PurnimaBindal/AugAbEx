According to Article 309 of the Constitution of India, Rule 56(K) of the Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, voluntary retirement requests cannot be withdrawn except with the approval of a competent authority before the intended date of retirement.
If a request is made after one year of the intended date of retirement, it cannot be withdrawn.
