"The Haryana Service of Engineer, Class II, P.W.D.
(Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1970, Rules 6 and 7(3) state the eligibility for becoming a member of the service and receiving promotion.
According to Rule 7(3), individuals who have completed five years of service and hold AMIE qualifications from source 4 are eligible.
However, the five years of service should not necessarily be after attaining the AMIE qualifications.
Once these qualifications are met, individuals become entitled to be considered for promotion to Class from post.
[Paras 6 and 7]"
