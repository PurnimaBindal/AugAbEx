High Court has no authority over the decision of the Selection Committee regarding the eligibility of candidates for the post of Director - Court can only examine if there were any irregularities or illegal actions in the selection process - Mala fides must be proven to affect the selection of Respondent No. 3.
[Para 13]
Respondent No. 3 possessed all the required qualifications and experience as required by the Selection Committee - The court cannot determine from the minutes what was discussed during the meetings.
[Para 17]
The responsibility of deciding if a candidate meets the qualifications falls under the Selection Committee as they are experts on the matter - Mala fides must be proven for the selection process to be deemed illegal or irregular.
