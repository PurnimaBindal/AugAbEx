Constitution of India, Article 16 - Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, Section 45 - Rajasthan State Road Transport Corporation Employees Service Regulations, 1965, Regulations 106 and 116 - Transfer - Change of job position - It is acceptable to transfer within a single cadre to positions with different responsibilities - The exchange of jobs between the positions of Conductor and Booking Clerk within the same cadre is allowed - However, this cannot be done without taking into account their tenure on a specific job and the agreement between the Union - Instructions have been given to make permanent adjustments based on tenure for available positions.
