Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab State (Class IV) Service Rules, 1963, Rule 5(b) - Termination - Natural justice - Eligibility - The petitioner was appointed by the competent authority and did not commit any fraud or misrepresentation for securing the appointment.
However, he was terminated without being given an opportunity on the ground of being under age.
This decision was made without considering that he was not under age according to his date of birth at the time of appointment.
Furthermore, the termination was immediate upon change of appointing authority and the petitioner was not paid wages for the period he worked.
This lack of opportunity seriously prejudiced the petitioner's right and appears to be an act of bad faith.
As a result, the termination was quashed and the petitioner is entitled to all consequential benefits including arrears of salary.
