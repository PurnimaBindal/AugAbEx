Constitution of India, Article 226 - Age of superannuation - Central Government made recommendatory proposals to raise the age of superannuation from 60 to 62 years - State Government has no legal obligation to implement the recommendations and can choose to disregard them for valid reasons, including fiscal and relevant considerations - The issuance of a Mandamus to the State Government to implement the recommendations is not possible.
