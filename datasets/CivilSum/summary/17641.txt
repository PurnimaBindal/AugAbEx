Article 226 and 311 of the Constitution of India were utilized to conduct a departmental inquiry on a Head Constable of Police who extorted money from a colleague for obtaining a clearance certificate.
The punishment for the offense resulted in a forfeiture of five years of service and five increments as well as adverse remarks regarding dishonesty.
Despite the petitioner's appeals to the higher authority, a writ was filed and it was discovered that there was more than ample evidence available to establish the petitioner's lack of honesty in their role as a police official.
The charge of extorting money from a colleague was clearly demonstrated during the inquiry.
Though the punishment was lighter than anticipated, there were no flaws in the actions taken by the authority.
