According to the Pepsu Service Regulations, Class-III employees were required to retire at age 55, which was later raised to 58.
The post held at the time of retirement determines the age of superannuation.
Since the petitioners are members of Class III services, they have no right to continue till 60, and the action of the respondents in retiring them at 58 is in accordance with the regulations.
[Paras 10 and 12] B Even if an employee initially joins a Class-IV service, they cannot continue till 60 if they are holding a Class III post at the time of retirement, which has an admitted age of retirement of 58.
