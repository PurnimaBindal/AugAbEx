Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 14 - Compassionate Appointments - Death of an employee - Dependent of deceased employee making an application to appoint him on compassionate grounds under the Scheme then prevailing - Retroactive modification of scheme providing monetary package to dependents of deceased in view of compassionate appointment - Unlawful - A compassionate appointment promised in executive instructions cannot be retracted by a later executive command retroactively, even though competent authority has the power to change its policy - Any executive action should only be applied prospectively - Retrospective operation of amended policy - Arbitrary and a violation of Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
