Constitution of India Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Qualifications relaxation - Experience - Competent authority promoted petitioner from class IV to class III post of L.D.C.
in the quota for promotees - No one meeting the eligibility requirements available, so petitioner promoted in relaxation of experience - Higher authority did not approve the relaxation and reviewed the matter after three years, ultimately ordering petitioner to be reverted - This action deemed improper given the lengthy passage of time and petitioner's lack of fault - Higher authority should have relaxed experience requirement as petitioner had performed satisfactorily for four years prior to reversion order - Order overturned.
