The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227, pertains to regularisation of service.
In this case, some petitioners have completed more than 240 days in three years, not preceding the cut off date.
As such, respondents must consider their case.
[Para 3]

The principle of equal pay for equal work applies, and daily wagers (petitioners) have the same responsibilities and duties as their regular counterparts.
Therefore, they are entitled to receive the minimum of the pay scale with Dearness allowance.
