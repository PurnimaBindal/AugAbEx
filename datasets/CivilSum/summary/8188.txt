Arbitration Act, Section 20 - Limitation Act, 1963, Article 137 - Reference - Limitation - In 1989, the petitioner served notice calling the respondent to refer the matter to arbitrator - The cause of action for the petitioner arose for the first time on 23889 when the respondent replied notice - A petition under section 20 was filed in 1993, which is barred by limitation as it was filed after three years - Accordingly, the trial court dismissed the petition under section 20.
