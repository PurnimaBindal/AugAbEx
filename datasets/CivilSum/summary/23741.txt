Constitution of India, Articles 16, 229 - Punjab High Court Establishments (Appointment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1952, Rules 38, 8, 12 - Promotion - Out of turn promotion granted to a junior in view of his excellent track record and special qualification against newly created posts of Supdt.
Grade II and Grade I Although promotions were made conditional upon not making him senior to officials who were senior to him, this condition was imposed to protect seniority of officials in lower cadre and was found to be reasonable.
Ordered accordingly.
