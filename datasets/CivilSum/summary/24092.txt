Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Fairness - Relevance of Documents - Merely not supplying a document requested by a delinquent does not automatically count as a violation of natural justice; the delinquent must show that the document in question is relevant to the charge and has caused prejudice to the defence.
[Paras 8, 10 and 11]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Negligence - Incompetence or inaptitude may not always be misconduct, but in some cases negligence in duty can lead to misconduct resulting in heavy losses.
When the delinquent violates rules and ignores orders, this amounts to misconduct if it leads to significant losses.
[Paras 12, 13]

Constitution of India, Articles 136 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Findings - Supreme Court cannot examine findings of fact.
[Para 13]
