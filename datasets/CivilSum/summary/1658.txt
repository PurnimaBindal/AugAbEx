The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, outlines the objectives of Compassionate appointment and sets the duty of authorities to conduct an enquiry into the income of the bereaved family to determine their ability to sustain their livelihood.
If the family cannot handle a sudden financial crisis or destitution, eligible dependents are to receive Compassionate appointment immediately.
Denying relief to the remaining family in distress, because a separated son of the deceased was already employed, is not permissible.
Courts have laid down the law regarding the policy of Compassionate appointment, without which it would violate Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
