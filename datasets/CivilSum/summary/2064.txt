Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rule 7 - Charge-sheet - Enquiry - Dismissal - The disciplinary authority had imposed the penalty of dismissal from the service on the plaintiff after serving him a show cause notice - The departmental enquiry report was supplied to the plaintiff along with the show cause notice and it was not required by law to supply the preliminary enquiry report - The dismissal order was declared not illegal as the department had conducted a fair and regular enquiry and had given plaintiff full opportunity to defend himself.
