The AP State Road Transport Corporation Employees (Recruitment) Regulations of 1966 dictate that there should be a quota-rota rule in place between direct appointees and promotees.
The seniority of promotees will be fixed on the roster point of their quota when it becomes available, even if they were promoted prior to direct recruits filling those vacancies.
Rules 30 and 34 allow for temporary promotion but this does not entitle the promotee to claim seniority.
According to the minority judgment by Sinha J, if the quota-rota rule is not followed for a long time it will be deemed to have broken down and promotees will get seniority over direct recruits.
Regularising temporary promotions is permissible and allows promotees to receive seniority from the date of regularisation.
In interpreting the statutes, the court must choose the interpretation that promotes justice and equity and must avoid hardship when possible.
