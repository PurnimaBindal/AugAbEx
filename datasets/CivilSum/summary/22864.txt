The Constitution of India, in Articles 14 and 226, and the Payment of Bonus Act of 1965, in Section 22, specify that writs will not be maintained when an alternative remedy under the Industrial Disputes Act is available.
The scope of Article 226 is too broad for it to encompass all industrial disputes.
However, since non-payment of Bonus is considered an Industrial Dispute under Section 22 of the Bonus Act, the Government has been instructed to make a reference.
