Bank's Voluntary Retirement Scheme, Clause 10(j) requires individuals seeking voluntary retirement to produce a 'no objection certificate' from the Employees' Housing Co-operative Society, if they are an office-bearer or Director of the Society.
The validity of this requirement has been deemed not to infringe on the provisions of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
Additionally, this requirement does not warrant discretionary writ jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution.
