Enquiry - Delay in conducting enquiry - Charges of misconduct - Due to investigation by CBI, there was a delay in proceedings - In instances involving financial irregularities, obtaining evidence can take a prolonged period and it is common for acts of misconduct to surface several years after said irregularities - Therefore, delay by itself should not be used as grounds to dismiss a charge-sheet in such matters.
