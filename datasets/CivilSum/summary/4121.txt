The Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 state that the Central Industrial Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 47(2)(c)(i) allows for punishment of removal from service for grave misconduct, such as assaulting a senior person and using unparliamentary language against them.
Despite being a constable in a disciplined force, the petitioner showed serious and gravest misconduct.
The High Court, when exercising writ jurisdiction, does not examine disciplinary proceedings as an appellate court.
If there is some evidence of misconduct, the quantum is not considered a matter for the court to review.
Due to the gravity of the petitioner's misconduct, the punishment of removal from service cannot reasonably be considered arbitrary or disproportionate.
The opinion of the punishing authority that the petitioner is not a suitable person to be retained in service was upheld.
