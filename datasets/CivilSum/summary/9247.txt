According to Delhi Electricity Supply undertaking (DMC) Service (Control & Appeal) Regulations 1976, Regulations 7(4) - DESU (DMC) Service Regulations 1981, Regulations 2, 6 & 7, and Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, Sections 64 & 480(2), competent authority/major penalty/dismissal or removal from service cannot be exercised by someone not empowered to exercise statutory powers.
The powers of officers are limited and cannot exceed their jurisdiction.
As such, the General Manager is not empowered under the Regulations to initiate enquiry proceedings for major penalty.
Apart from this, when the Act prescribes a particular body to exercise a power, it must be exercised by that body, and cannot be exercised by others unless it is delegated.
The initiation of disciplinary proceedings and all subsequent steps taken by the General Manager are without jurisdiction and void-ab-initio.
[Paras 23, 26, 27, 31 and 32]

Article 311(1) of the Constitution of India states that the competent authority/major penalty for dismissal or removal from service should be issued by the officer holding the current duty charge.
Since at the stage of initiation of disciplinary inquiry, it cannot be postulated at the very outset whether there will be any punishment at all, and even if there is going to be any punishment, what particular penalty is likely to be imposed.
[Para 21]
