The Haryana State Electricity Board received a claim for regularisation and grant of pension from a work charge employee.
Two circulars from the state government dated 2381987 and 641990 were relied upon, with the contention that the circulars are not applicable to Board employees but only for government employees.
However, it was held that the Board's offer indicates that it either adopted the circular or made a policy decision to regularise work charge employees.
As a result, the direction was given to regularise the petitioner and pay pensionary benefits.
