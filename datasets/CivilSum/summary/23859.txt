Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination on account of conviction - Suppression of fact of conviction at the time of appointment - Non-disclosure of prosecution proceedings for an offense U/s 307 IPC not warranting termination - Aquittal of employee in criminal proceedings means no grounds to pursue termination order - Reinstatement with back wages granted, employee to be treated as fresh entrant from date of reinstatement order.
