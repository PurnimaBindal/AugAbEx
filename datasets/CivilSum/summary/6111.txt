As per the West Bengal Primary Education Act, 1973 and Recruitment and Leave of Teachers in Primary Schools in West Bengal Rules, 1991, individuals with a JBT/Primary Teachers Training Certificate can qualify for the post of Primary School Teacher, but they cannot claim extra credit for having a higher academic qualification, as per Rule 2 (m), (n), 6 and 9.
The Rules also state that there is no comparison between the two qualifications, as they operate in separate and distinct fields.
This ruling is based on the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
