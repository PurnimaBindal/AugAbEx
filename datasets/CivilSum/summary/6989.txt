Article 14 of the Indian Constitution applies to demobilized Indian Armed Forces personnel.
The Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) Rules of 1972, Rules 4 and 5 outline the reservation of vacancies for such personnel.
The Punjab Recruitment of Ex-Servicemen Rules of 1982, Rule 9, explains the seniority of military service and the reservation of vacancies.
The "old vacancies old rules" rule will apply, where the 1972 Rules would provide benefits of seniority for military service by giving a deemed date of appointment in Civil Service.
The 1982 Rules will still apply to vacancies coming into existence before the 1982 Rules came into force.
The service conditions of such appointees will be governed by the 1972 Rules and entitled to their benefits, while the 1982 Rules are not retrospective.

Article 14 and Article 226 of the Indian Constitution apply to demobilized Indian Armed Forces personnel.
The Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) Rules of 1972, Rules 4 and 5 outline the reservation of vacancies for such personnel.
The Punjab Recruitment of Ex-Servicemen Rules of 1982, Rule 9, explains the seniority of military service and the reservation of vacancies.
The concept of estoppel applies, where the implication and effect of the Rules have already been adjudicated and attained finality and implemented between the parties.
Therefore, it cannot be opposed in subsequent litigation by seeking a different interpretation to deny the benefit of the final judgment inter se the parties.
Once the old vacancies are governed by the old rules, it cannot be said that the old rules apply only for vacancies and not for other benefits available thereunder.
