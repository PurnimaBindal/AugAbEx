According to the Constitution of India, as per Articles 14, 16, and 226, the petitioner teacher who is a post-graduate and has a relevant degree to the teaching group should receive a higher pay scale.
The memorandum issued on 1221999 confirms the appropriate pay scale for teachers based on their qualifications.
Although a circular dated 1371999 was issued later, it does not deny post-graduate pay scale to teachers possessing a relevant PG degree.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to the post-graduate pay scale from their appointment date.
[Paras 10, 11 and 28]
