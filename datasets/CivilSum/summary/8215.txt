The Academic Council meeting decisions of Punjabi University (Patiala) dated 121999 and 941999 regarding Education and Admissions have been made.
These decisions include the classification of diplomas and degrees and the equivalence of diploma and degrees.
The decision dated 121999 states that the diploma course in Modern Office Management of Punjab State Board of Technical Education and Industrial Training is equivalent to graduation for the purpose of employment and admission to higher studies.
The later decision of 941999 classifies the diploma course into two categories based on eligibility qualifications for admission to diploma courses, but it only applies prospectively.
While the classification based on basic qualifications before diploma is valid, the University cannot change its decisions repeatedly while students who have already paid fees and missed their chances for admission to other courses suffer.
