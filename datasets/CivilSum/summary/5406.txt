Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1)(c) - Reference - Maintainability of - Workman raised the plea that notice was served to management before referring the dispute to Labour Court - The finding by the learned single judge that no opportunity of hearing was given to management is not factual - Thus, reference is maintainable - Order of learned single Judge and award of Labour Court are set aside.
