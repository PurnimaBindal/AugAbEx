The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, states that any order affecting the civil rights of a person must be fair and just.
In this case, while Municipal Authorities had ordered a promotion, the State Government later annulled the resolution without providing the person concerned an opportunity of hearing.
Given this lack of due process, the State Government's order must be set aside.
[Para 1]
As per the Madhya Pradesh Municipal Act, 1961 Section 94, Municipal Authorities may pass a resolution in favour of an employee.
However, in a situation where a resolution has been set aside by the State Government without affording opportunity of hearing, the order must be quashed.
The State Government should instead proceed with a proper enquiry and afford due opportunity to the concerned employee.
