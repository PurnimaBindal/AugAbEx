Constitution of India, Article 16 - UP Jal Nigam Service of Engineers (Public Health Branch) Regulations, 1978, Regulation 23(6) - Promotion - Seniority - Reckoning of - Adverse Entry - A representation was pending against an adverse entry on the date that the respondent was considered for promotion, leading to him not receiving the promotion - However, the adverse entry was subsequently expunged as his representation was accepted - Therefore, on the date of consideration for promotion, there was no adverse entry in his character roll - As a result, the High Court was justified in ordering that the respondent be entitled to reckon his seniority from the date that his junior was promoted upon his promotion.
