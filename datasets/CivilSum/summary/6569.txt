Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 310 - Army Regulations, Regulations 104(d), 105(f) and (g) - Resignation - An employee who has signed a contract of service with the government cannot be deprived of their right to resign - Division Bench of the High Court found valid reasons for resignation from Army due to family circumstances - Competent authority directed to make alternative arrangement and inform petitioner of accepted resignation date within two weeks - Authority cannot reject the resignation, only keep it in abeyance for a period of time - Rejection of resignation deemed arbitrary and illegal.
