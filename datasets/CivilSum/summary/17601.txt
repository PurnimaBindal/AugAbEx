According to Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, seniority cannot be disrupted without prior notice and a chance to be heard.
The petitioner was previously deemed senior to respondent No. 3 in all instances, but without warning or opportunity, this was changed and respondent No. 3 was placed above the petitioner in all positions.
The order is now quashed with the option to proceed again in adherence with the law.
