Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India are related to seniority and discrimination.
One petitioner, who is a driver in a factory run by administration, claims seniority at par with other staff drivers.
However, the petitioner is considered an industrial worker and extended all admissible benefits that apply to an industrial worker.
The petitioner was granted a higher pay scale under the assured career progression scheme and received the benefit of overtime allowance at a higher rate, which staff drivers did not receive.
As a result, the petitioner cannot claim seniority at par with staff drivers.
