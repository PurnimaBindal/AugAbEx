Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 2(b) - Basic wages/Arrears of wages - In case of revision of wages under the Industrial Disputes Act, the arrears of wages would be counted as a part of the contract of employment and hence be included in basic wages as per Section 2(6) of the Provident Fund Act.
