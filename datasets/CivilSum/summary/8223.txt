Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rule 4(iv) - Appellant/plaintiff was removed from service - Sued and won - Verdict later reversed without notice or opportunity to be heard - Ruling not supported by law, as fair hearing is necessary and fundamental.
Order cancelling previous one was not appealable, making it even more important to provide opportunity to be heard.
Appeal allowed, order of trial Court restored.
