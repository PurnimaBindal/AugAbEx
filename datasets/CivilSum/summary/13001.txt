The West Bengal Housing Board Rules, 1973, Rule 8, along with Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226, state that a petitioner has the right to be considered for promotion based on seniority-cum-merit.
However, the relevant recruitment rules were violated while promoting the respondent employees on an ad hoc basis.
Although a Committee was formed without the approval of the Govt. for regularisation of ad-hoc promotion, the formation of the Committee was considered wholly unauthorised and illegal.
Even though there was no power in Rules/Act to legalise this kind of irregular appointment, the regularisation of ad-hoc promotion was held to be illegal.
