Constitution of India, Article 227 - Revision - Suit for permanent injunction - A revision cannot be granted for the purpose of obtaining a direction to decide the application under Order 39 Rules 1 & 2 due to an F.I.R. registered in the police station - The petitioner has other available remedies, including the duty of the police to take necessary action - No grounds for revision.

Constitution of India, Article 227 - Applicability of - The jurisdiction under Article 227 is limited to exercising supervisory power with the aim of ensuring that inferior courts remain within their authority and do not exceed it - Solely an incorrect decision may not warrant the exercise of jurisdiction under Article 227, unless it leads to serious injustice.
