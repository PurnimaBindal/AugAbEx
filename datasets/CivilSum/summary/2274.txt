Constitution of India, Article 226 - Departmental Enquiry - Stay of - If departmental proceeding is stayed further proceedings would be unduly delayed - During pendency of criminal case - Entire defence disclosed in departmental proceeding - Criminal case already unduly delayed - Moreover, standard of proof, mode of enquiry and trial of both the cases are entirely distinct and different - No prejudice is caused to the petitioner, thus both proceedings initiated against petitioners could go on simultaneously.
[Paras 15, 16 and 17]
