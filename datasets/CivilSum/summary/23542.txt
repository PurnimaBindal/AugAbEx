Rule of Law - Justice must not only be done but also be seen to be done.
[Para 10] B Judicial propriety - To ensure public confidence in the judiciary's credibility and impartiality, litigants may expect that their matter should not be heard by a particular Judge if there is a reasonable basis for the expectation - The Judge, not influenced in any way in doing justice, should recuse himself from hearing the matter if his presence is likely to cause a reasonable apprehension in the litigant's mind - The impugned order is invalidated by a specific objection from the appellants concerning the respondent's case in the contempt petition where the order was made in his favour.
