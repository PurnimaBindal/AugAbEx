Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 162 - Promotion/Seniority - Executive Instructions dated August 17, 1983, related to Telegraphists for promotion to the posts of Lower Scale Grade Telegraph Masters (LSGTMs) - The Selection for the purpose of appointment on 17 85% upgraded posts of Assistant Telegraph Master (ATMs) as Lower Scale Grade Telegraph Masters (LSGTMs) had to be made from these working as ATMs on August 17, 1983 by using inter se seniority - The pay scale of LSGTMs would be given to the ATMs selected for promotion as LSGTMs but they wouldn't be senior to the Telegraphists who were promoted as LSGTMs subsequently - On promotion, the seniority of Telegraphists would be regained by them in the cadre of LSGTMs.
[Paras 10 and 11]
