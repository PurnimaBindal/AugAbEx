Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - Post of Divisional Manager - The adoption of different tests to prescribe requirements for promotion to two equivalent feeder cadres would be unreasonable and arbitrary.
Especially when these two feeder cadres are the only sources of recruitment to the next higher cadre of Divisional Manager.
The impugned rules that prescribe seven years of service as Assistant Divisional Manager for promoting to the post of Divisional Manager were quashed.
Directions have been given to prescribe a period of five years as the minimum required service for promotion from the Assistant Divisional Manager cadre to the Divisional Manager cadre.
