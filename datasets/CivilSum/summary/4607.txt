Education/Admission - Eligibility for admission - The only requirement for an eligible candidate is to have studied at least two school subjects at the first and/or second degree level.
It doesn't matter if these subjects were taught in college instead of school.
Excluding students who were taught senior secondary examination subjects in college from eligibility would be unjustified and unwarranted.
