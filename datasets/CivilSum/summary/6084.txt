Mr. Anoop Kumar Mehta, Advocate, is the respondent in the case involving the Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226.
The petitioner was transferred to a new location and reported for duty every day, but was not allowed to join.
The writ court later set aside his transfer, but the petitioner was still not allowed to join his previous department despite directions from the High Court.
Only after certain representations was he allowed to join duty.
According to the ruling, the employee is entitled to full salary and consequential benefits.
