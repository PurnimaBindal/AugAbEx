Constitution of India, Article 16 - West Bengal Rules for Management of Recognised Non-Govt. Institutions (Aided and Unaided), 1969, Rule 28(7) - Regularisation of Service - Long service is irrelevant for regularisation of a newly-created post which did not exist at the time of the incumbent's appointment - Since the petitioner was appointed without authorization to the post of Night Guard, his service cannot be regularized in that post or as a Class IV staff - Despite the petitioner's long years of service, a vacant post for Class IV staff is available in the school which needs to be filled through a new interview process that includes the petitioner and other candidates - Age limit shall not apply to the petitioner - The petitioner shall remain in service until an appointment to the said Class IV post is made.
[Paras 1 and 13]
