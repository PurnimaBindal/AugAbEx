Article 311(2) (a) of the Constitution of India allows for Provision to be invoked at any time if an employee is convicted of an offence that may lead to any of the major penalties mentioned in Article 311, and the order of removal from service passed on the basis of such conviction can always be revised if the government servant is later acquitted.
Suspension of sentence does not prevent action under Article 311(2) (a).
[Paras 6 to 10]
Delay in taking action under Article 311 (2) (a) is allowed if properly explained, and in the case of an employee convicted of corruption, action taken under Article 311 (2) (a) was held to be proper.
Delay due to a pending appeal against conviction and doubt as to whether the provision can be invoked during suspension of sentence are valid explanations.
