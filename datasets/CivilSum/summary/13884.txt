Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - West Bengal Co- operative Societies Act, 1983 - West Bengal Co-operative Societies Rules, 1987, Rules 2, 7(4), 14 and 48 - Dismissal from services - Temporary employee - Accused of defalcation and forgery - According to rules, an employee can only be dismissed after conducting an enquiry - Respondent cannot dismiss the petitioner without issuing a chargesheet and starting proceedings- Even though the petitioner was a temporary employee, they cannot be terminated without following proper disciplinary procedures - The dismissal order is invalid and quashed.
