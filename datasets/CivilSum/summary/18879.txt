Indian Forest Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1966, Rules 8 and 9 - Promotion to the post of Director Zoological Park - Appointment can be made from outside the cadre for a limited period of up to three months in the absence of eligible candidates from the feeder cadres, but prior approval from the Central Government is required - It is illegal to allow the appointee to continue indefinitely - The Government is instructed to consider eligible candidates within the cadre within two weeks.
