Rajasthan Civil Services (Medical Attendant) Rules, 1970, Rules 3(1)(ix), 4(4) and 5(2) - Constitution of India, Article 16 - Medical reimbursement - If a government employee receives treatment abroad where such medical facilities are not widely available, he or she is entitled to full reimbursement according to the fundamental right to health and medical aid.
