Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 19 - Memorandum of Settlement between Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited and Workmen, Paras 22-A, 241 - HPCL Employees Superannuation Benefit Fund Scheme Dated 13 May 1988, Clause 8(c) - Employee declared permanently disabled - Claim of compassionate appointment - Pursuant to Para 241 of the settlement, a dependent is eligible for a job commensurate with their experience and qualifications if they are permanently and totally disabled, and otherwise eligible - When the petitioner submitted their application, the settlement was in effect - While the application was pending, the employee superannuated - The claim was denied on the grounds that the employee was superannuated but not discharged, and respondents relied on clause 8 of the scheme to deny the claim - However, considering the circumstances and rules, the respondents are obligated to consider giving the petitioner a preferential appointment against a regular vacancy, provided everything else is equal, and with relaxation in age and eligibility criteria.
[Paras 22, 23, 26, 31]
