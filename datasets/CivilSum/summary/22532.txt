Factories Act, 1948, Section 2(n) Proviso (2)(ii) (as amended by Act, 1987) defines a 'deemed occupier' of a government-owned company, such as the Factory of Indian Oil Corporation.
The ultimate control of all affairs, including the opening and running of factories, lies with the Central Government.
If someone other than the Director is appointed by the Central Government to manage its affairs, they are considered the occupier for the purpose of Section 2(n) of the Factories Act.
