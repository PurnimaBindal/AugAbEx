The candidate in question was appointed to the Punjab Service of Engineers, Class II, PWD (Building and Roads Branch) without an education degree and was allowed to remain in this position for some time.
After attaining the qualifications necessary for the position of Assistant Engineer, he was considered for promotion.
There was nothing to suggest that there was a rule in place requiring minimum qualifications for the lower position at which the candidate was initially appointed and nothing indicating that the Board did not possess the power to relax educational requirements.
In fact, the Board's own actions would suggest that they had relaxed educational requirements and already qualified the candidate for the position in which he was initially appointed.
As such, his appointment cannot be questioned.
