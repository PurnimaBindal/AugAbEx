Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 2(a) and 7(3-A) - Gratuity - Calculation of continuous service - The Management argued that the petitioner was absent due to a strike, but failed to provide evidence that it was the workman's fault - As a result, the petitioner should receive gratuity for the strike period - In addition, interest at a rate of 10% pa. must be given on the unpaid gratuity amount.
