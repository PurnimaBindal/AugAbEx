The Bihar Non-Government Elementary Schools (Take Over) Act, 1976, Section 3, 4(2) and 8, and the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 were used to form the decision that an unaided school taken over by the government was classified differently than teachers of schools run by local bodies.
The different classes of schools were given different dates for absorption of deemed government servants, with teachers of unaided schools in a separate class.
This classification was found not to be in violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
