The Tribunal has found that the employer's decision to appoint respondents as Helpers rather than as Artisans constitutes unfair labor practice in the face of underemployment.
Under the Constitution of India, Articles 12, 14, 15, and 16, every instrumentality of the State is subject to all the constitutional limitations as the State itself, and the Rajasthan State Road Transport Corporation falls under this definition as an instrumentality of the State.
Since the respondents are employees of the instrumentality of the State, they are entitled to constitutional protection as per Articles 14, 15, and 16.
As a result, the petition has been dismissed.
