The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Regularisation of service - There was no assurance of regularisation of service at the time of temporary appointment on contractual basis - The petitioner was not included in the 51 shortlisted candidates for regular appointment because they had less experience compared to others - It was not arbitrary to not call them for an interview - There was no right to be regularised for the position of Assistant Engineer since appointments were made in accordance with the All India Officers Rule.
