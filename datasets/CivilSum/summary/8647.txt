Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Act, Sections 38(i) and Section 106 - Court martial - Desertion of military - A person absent from duty, who is not available to the Army Authorities, can be considered a deserter - Both terms apply to different contexts and circumstances - However, if the Army Authorities conclude that it was a case of desertion, the court cannot adjudicate that it was only an absence from duty - If desertion is determined by the authority, there is no requirement to comply with Section 106 of the Act - Summary court martial proceedings can be initiated to terminate or punish the service member under the Act.
