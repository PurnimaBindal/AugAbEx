The State Government is authorized to create rules and laws for Municipal Corporations, and with supervisory control, can allow the transfer of Corporation employees on deputation while preserving their lien and right to promotion in their parent Corporation, as per Madhya Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1956, Sections 58(5) and 167, Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, Schedule 7, List 2, Entry 5.
The power of State Government to transfer employees from one Corporation to another on deputation does not breach Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
