According to Section 2-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, if an employee is suspended or retired due to misconduct, they must be reinstated without an enquiry.
In one case, a petitioner requested that their dispute regarding retirement be referred to the Labour Court for adjudication by the State Government.
However, the request was rejected by the Government.
It was held that the Government's order cannot be sustained because they were not entitled to say that it was a case of retirement.
