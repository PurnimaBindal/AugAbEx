Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Backwage - The Labour Court granted 50% backwages and ordered for reinstatement with continuity of service after finding the termination order illegal due to technical issues.
It had exercised its discretion by considering the facts and circumstances of the case while awarding limited wages.
The High Court cannot interfere in the discretion exercised by the Labour Court concerning factual aspects.
