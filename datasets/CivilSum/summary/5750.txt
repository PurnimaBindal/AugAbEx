Modified eligibility conditions for MD course in surgery may be enforced against petitioner, even though he was allowed to take the entrance exam when the condition was in force.
Rule SRO-03 of 1989 required candidates to fulfill certain conditions to be considered eligible for selection and the Competent Authority was left to re-examine eligibility under the rule.
If the petitioner was found short of requirement, he could not claim admission despite his superior merit.
B.
The doctrine of estoppel or acquiescence cannot operate against a statutory bar, nor can higher merit cure ineligibility under the rules.
Since petitioner's eligibility for selection/admission was subject to fresh scrutiny, he needed to satisfy the requirements and could not invoke equitable estoppel or acquiescence.
The petition was dismissed.
