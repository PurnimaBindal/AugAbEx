Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2, Item 3 - Termination - An employer must prove that the employee workman had not completed 240 days of service - The Labour Court found that the required days of continuous service had been completed - There were no perverse findings.
[Para 10]  B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Termination - Withholding of best possible evidence - An adverse inference is made - The issue to decide was the actual date of entry of the workman - The Labour Court needed to see the attendance register for the relevant period - The employer failed to produce the relevant record - It was held that the petitioners consciously avoided producing the attendance register - The Labour Court was therefore justified in drawing an adverse inference.
