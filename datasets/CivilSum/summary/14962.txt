For Respondent Nos. 1, 4 and 5: Mr. RD Rane, AGP cites Constitution of India, Article 16 - Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1977, Section 9 - Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Rules, 1981, Schedule (B) Part III, Items (3) and (8) to argue that a full-time teacher teaching vocational subjects must hold a Diploma in Engineering, in at least 2nd Class in the appropriate branch and that the qualifications required for the said post are the ones prescribed in Schedule `B' Part II, Item (8).
It is held that the petitioner was the only qualified candidate for the said post by comparing the two qualifications.
