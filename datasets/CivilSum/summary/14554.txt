Recognition of degrees/diplomas from other states for appointment in the state of Haryana is based on reciprocity.
However, the state of Punjab does not recognize degrees/diplomas from institutions in Haryana, leading to the Haryana government's decision to withhold recognition of degrees/diplomas issued in Punjab.
In Civil Writ Petition No. 4999 of 1993, the court dismissed objections to this decision and ruled that degrees or diplomas in Art & Craft from the Department of Technical Education and Industrial Training, Punjab make a person eligible for consideration as an Art & Craft Teacher in Punjab.
Hence, qualifications obtained from that institution in Punjab should also be considered as recognized qualifications for appointment as an Art & Craft Teacher in Haryana.
