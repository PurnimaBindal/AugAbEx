The Voluntary Retirement Scheme dt. 29.8.1991 of Govt. of India, Ministry of Surface Transport allows Port Trust and Dock Labour Board to implement a VRS to reduce excess staff under specific terms and conditions.
Claim under the VRS is not absolute as the decision is up to the competent authority.
The court cannot force the authority to accept the VRS solely based on the Scheme.
The Board has the power to alter the scheme, and ineligible employees cannot challenge the scheme's modification.
The Writ Court can only direct the authority to consider the claims as per the Scheme but cannot require the grant of VRS.
