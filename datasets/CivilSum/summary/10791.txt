Jammu and Kashmir Civil Services Regulation, Vol.
I, SROs 321 and 514 - Unauthorised absence from duty - An order treating a period of absence as 'Dies-non' is not legally sustainable - Employee granted study leave of two years - Resume work after more than three years - Rights of the employee should be considered on the date of resuming duty - Employee is entitled to benefits as per the meaning of 'Dies-non' under SRO 321 - Retrospective application of SRO 514 is not justifiable.
[Paras 13 and 14]

Words and phrases - Word 'Dies-non'- Different meanings as per SRO 321 and SRO 514 - SRO 321 defines it as 'not qualifying for any remuneration' - SRO 514 defines it as 'not qualifying for any remuneration, pension, increments, experience, promotion, seniority' - Benefit cannot be denied based on SRO 514 as employee falls under SRO 321.
[Para 7]
