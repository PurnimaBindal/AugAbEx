Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, Section 10 - Bonus - Minimum bonus - If an industry has paid the minimum bonus to employees for the accounting year 1987-88, it must also pay the same for the years 1985-86 and 1986-87.
[Para 12]
Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 12 - Industry obligated to pay statutory minimum bonus under the Act - A writ of mandamus may be issued to a government undertaking to compel it to perform its legal duty and pay minimum bonus to its workers.
