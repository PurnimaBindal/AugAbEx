Chandigarh Administration Assistant Grade Examination Rules, 1989, Rule 2(c) - Punjab State Electricity Board Ministerial Service Class III Regulations, 1982, Rule 8 - Promotion to the post of Circle Assistant/Assistant Revenue Account - Written examination and experience - The employees of the Chandigarh Electricity Wing (Operation Circle) are governed by PSEB Ministerial Service Class III Regulations, 1985 until any new rules are framed.
They must appear in the Departmental Accounts Examination for Ministerial Establishment prescribed under the rules regulating their conditions of service.
They are not required to pass the Assistant Grade Examination introduced by the Chandigarh Administration.
Those who fail to pass the Departmental examination are not eligible for promotion solely on the basis of passing the Assistant Grade Examination.
