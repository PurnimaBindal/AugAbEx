Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226/227 - Writ - Maintainability - State - A writ against a society registered under the Societies Registration Act that runs an un-aided educational institution is not considered maintainable, unless the society does not meet the definition of 'State' as per Art. 12 - A body that is financially, functionally or administratively dominated by or under the control of the Government is considered as 'State' under Art. 12 - Control that is merely regulatory, under statute or otherwise, does not qualify for 'State' status.
[Paras 10 and 11]
