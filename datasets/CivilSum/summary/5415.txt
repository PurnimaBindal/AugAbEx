Constitution of India, Article 226 - Misconduct - Dismissal - Allegations of financial irregularities against petitioner, Assistant Manager of Bank - It has been determined, based on evidence, that he acted in a dubious and reckless manner regarding bank operations - The petitioner has been found guilty of numerous transactions and most of the charges have been proven - Interference from the Court in this case would negatively affect banking business, which relies on public confidence - Therefore, this is not a suitable case for intervention under Article 226 of the Constitution.
