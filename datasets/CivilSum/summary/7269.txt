The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947's Sections 2(oo) and 25-F, which deal with retrenchment, specify that compensation must be provided.
A genuine error in the calculation of retrenchment compensation should not result in an order of reinstatement with 30% back wages.
Instead, paying compensation of Rs. 25,000/- alone, instead of reinstatement and back wages, would be appropriate.
