Article 311 of the Constitution of India deals with punishment, efficiency bar, promotion and natural justice.
The act of allowing an employee to cross the efficiency bar has the effect of removing past misconduct and punishment.
Punishment cannot operate to the detriment of the employee's rights in the case of an instant nature.
Denial of promotion based on past conduct is invalid.
[Para 6] B.
According to articles 14 and 311 of the Constitution of India, adverse remarks in a confidential roll cannot be used to deny promotion unless the remarks were communicated to the employee concerned or the representation against such remarks is pending to decide whether the remarks were justified.
