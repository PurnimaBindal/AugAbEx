Constitution of India, Article 16 - Qualification - Selection for the post of Dental Officer - Minimum qualification required is BDS However, preference may be given to candidates with higher qualifications such as MDS.
A candidate with MDS qualification who was not selected for the position has no right to be selected based on their higher qualification.
They are entitled to only some additional weightage.
[Paras 7 and 9]
Constitution of India, Article 16 - Selection - Selection for the post is made by the Public Service Commission, and in the absence of any statutory criteria, the commission has the discretion to develop its own method of evaluation.
[Paras 7 and 9]
Constitution of India, Article 16 - Selection made by Public Service Commission - Viva voce and General knowledge awards of 20 marks each out of a total of 100 marks - unless mala fide is established, it cannot be concluded that the marks awarded by the commission were arbitrary.
