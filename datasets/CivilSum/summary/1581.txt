Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 36-A - Reference - Award - The Industrial Tribunal directed that the petitioner-Corporation should revise HRA and other allowances in accordance with orders of the State Government, under clause 17 of the settlement between the Trade Union and the said Corporation.
The Tribunal's exercise was constitutive rather than interpretive.
As such, the award of the Tribunal is patently invalid and beyond the jurisdiction of Section 36-A of the Act.
