According to Section 5 of the Haryana Private Colleges (Taking over Management) Act, 1978, the State Government is authorized to take over the management of college property for a maximum period of three years and is obligated to hand it over to a duly constituted Managing Committee.
If the term of taking over is extended after one year but not exceeding three years and the extension order is not challenged, there is no justification to interfere with the petition and it will be dismissed.
