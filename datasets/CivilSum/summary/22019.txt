Constitution of India, 1950, Article 16(4) - Reservation for OBCs - Creamy layer - Bihar Ordinance, 1995 and UP SC/ST and OBC Reservation Act, 1994 must exclude creamy layer from OBCs.
Economic and income criteria are applicable.
Mandal case guidelines state that IAS, IPS or All India Services members' children cannot avail reservation benefit.
Criteria for agriculturists can be based on their holdings.
Bihar and Uttar Pradesh added further conditions, which the Supreme Court declared arbitrary and violative of Constitution Articles 14 and 16(4).
Bihar Ordinance 1995's schedule III clause I is excepted, but Section 3(b) and Schedule II of UP Reservation for OBCs/SC/STs Act, 1994 are quashed.
