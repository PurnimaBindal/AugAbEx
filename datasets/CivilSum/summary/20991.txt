Constitution of India, Article 311 - West Bengal Service Rules, Part I, Rule 76 - West Bengal Services (Revision of Pay and Allowances) Rules, 1981 - Retirement - Group `D' employees opting for Group `C' employment - Group `C' employees retire at 58, while Group `D' employees retire at 60 - Opting for Group `C' employment means employees cannot return to Group `D' for retirement - Order for retiring opted employees at age 58 held valid.
