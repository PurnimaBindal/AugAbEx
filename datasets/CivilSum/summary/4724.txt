Post Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, Chandigarh Rules, 1967, Rule 7 - Appointment as an Acting Director of PGI as a temporary arrangement till regular selection of Director - According to the Rule, the senior-most professor should oversee the Director functions until the regular appointment, but the Institute may appoint another person for justifiable reasons, not exceeding six months - In this case, Institute decided to disregard all the candidates for regular selection to avoid controversy, including the senior-most professor - The court held that the reason did not go against legal provisions of Rule 7, nor did it bear any indication for or against any particular candidate - the appointment was valid.
