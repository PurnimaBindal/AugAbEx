Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Termination - Removal - Reduction in rank - The appellant, who held a post as a civilian in the defence service on a temporary basis and had their salary paid out of the Ministry of Defence's estimates, is not entitled to the protection of Article 311(2) of the Constitution or the Central Civil Services (C.C.
and A) Rules, 1965.

Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Central Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, Rule 5(1) - Termination - The "Appointing Authority" in Rule 5(1) of the Rules must refer to the authority who actually appointed the Govt. servant; as the appellant was appointed by the Director of DRDL, only the Director is competent to terminate the appellant's service.
The Deputy Director's termination order was without jurisdiction, and cannot be sustained.

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination - A valid payment is when the salary and allowance for one month in lieu of one month's notice are sent by money order on the day services were terminated.
[Para 11]

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, Rule 5(1) - Termination - In cases where the work and conduct of a temporary Govt. servant are not satisfactory, or if it is found that their continuance in service is not in the public interest on the grounds of their unsuitability, misconduct or inefficiency, the competent authority may either terminate their services in accordance with terms and conditions of the service or relevant rules, or it may decide to take punitive action against the temporary Govt. servant.
If the temporary Govt. servant's services are terminated in accordance with the terms and conditions of service, it will not visit them with any civil consequences.
