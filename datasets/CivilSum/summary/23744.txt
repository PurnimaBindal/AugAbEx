Under the Employees State Insurance Corporation Act, 1948, Sections 2(9), 12, 15, 17, 22, 75 and 76, and the Factories Act, Sections 2(n) and 7(1), the definition of "employee" encompasses the company's Managing Director if they are chosen to act in that capacity by the company and paid remuneration for the extra work.
The Managing Director would then be subject to the supervision of the Board of Directors.
As per Section 2(a) of ESI Act, the Managing Director would be considered an employee, and could be included with the remaining 19 employees to meet the criteria for establishment coverage under Section 2(12) of ESI Act.
