The Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970 Section 10 with a notification dated 9.12.1976 was issued by the Central Government, and the Security Guards Regulation Act, 1981, Section 2(i) prohibit the employment of contract employment for watching the buildings and security services.
The Govt. established agencies to provide specialised security services and surveillance cover to sensitive installations, which were not covered by the notification of 1976, and therefore any contract labor in this case is not abolished.
Moreover, the Govt. framed a scheme for the rehabilitation of ex-servicemen, of which it was mindful at the time of issuing the notification of 1976 and hence exempted specialised security services from the ambit of abolition.
Bringing these specialised services under the umbrella of abolition would defeat the purpose behind Security Guards Act and Rehabilitation Schemes for ex-servicemen and specialised services in the field.
[Paras 16, 18, 19 and 21]

The Ex-servicemen (Re-employment in Central Civil Services and Posts) Rules, 1979 apply only to ex-servicemen who have applied directly for recruitment for posts in Central Civil Services against the reserved post provided to them and not to those appointed through specialised agencies for providing security services.
