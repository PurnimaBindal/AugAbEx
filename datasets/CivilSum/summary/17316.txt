Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rule 7 - Promotion - Departmental proceedings - Enquiry - Haryana Government instructions dated 18.11.1971 Para 2(a)(iv) - Employees charged and undergoing departmental proceedings cannot be promoted until the enquiry is complete and they are found innocent - If no penalty is imposed or a minor warning is given, and the employee is suitable for promotion, they must be promoted retrospectively, even if temporary posts have to be created or juniors must be demoted - The mere findings of the enquiry officer declaring the delinquent guilty is not enough.
It is up to the responsible authority to make a final decision on punishment, if any.
