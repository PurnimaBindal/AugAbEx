Constitution of India, Articles 14(4) and 226 - Admissions to Post Graduate and Diploma Courses Rules, Rule 3 NB (1), Guideline 3 - Admissions/Reservation in admissions - Rule 3 NB (i) to the Admissions to Post Graduate and Diploma Courses Rules requires "separate reservation" in degree and diploma courses in the subjects where the number of seats in degree courses is four or more - However, if the number of seats available in a degree course is less than four, diploma seats may be added to the total number of degree seats in order to benefit the Backward Class for the purpose of earmarking the required quota in their favour - It is important to maintain the total percentage of reservation provided - NB (a) to sub-rule (5) of Rule 10 speaks of selections and makes reference to G.O.
