Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Termination - Misconduct - Domestic Enquiry - Natural Justice - Non-production of Inquiry Officer - Labour Court found that principles of natural justice were not followed during the domestic enquiry, and drew an adverse inference from the inquiry officer not entering the witness box to rebut allegations made by the workman.
The petitioner failed to demonstrate that the Labour Court's conclusions were inconsistent with statutory provisions, and therefore, there is no infirmity in the Labour Court's award.
