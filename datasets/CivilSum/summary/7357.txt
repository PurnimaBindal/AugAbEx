MV Vedachala, Advocate, argues that the Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, Sections 3 and 16(2), protects workers from being deprived of bonus benefits when a sister concern is established through the transfer of existing workers.
Simply appointing a few new workers and preparing separate accounts of profit and loss or failing to file a separate balance sheet is not sufficient grounds to deny workers their entitlement to bonus benefits.
Splitting assets and employees of a company to evade the provisions of the act is not permissible, and the act should be applied liberally in favour of workmen as it is a beneficial piece of legislation.
