Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Reinstatement - Compensation - If a termination is deemed illegal, the normal practice is to grant reinstatement with back wages.
However, in some cases, the Labour Court may award compensation instead of reinstatement.
In this case, the workman was terminated in 1976, and the Labour Court awarded compensation instead of reinstatement.
After 23 years, it would not be appropriate to upset this discretion and put the petitioner back in service.
Instead, the workman should receive a sum equivalent to 5 years' wages as compensation in lieu of the two years' wages awarded by the Labour Court for the ends of justice to be met.
[Paras 15, 19 and 21]
