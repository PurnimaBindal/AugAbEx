Constitution of India, Article 311 - Appointment - Probation - Termination - The managing committee of a school decided in a meeting on 1641991 that the petitioner's continuation of employment was not in the school's or students' best interest.
As a result, a resolution was passed and the petitioner was notified on 26.4.1991.
However, the termination order was stayed and conveyed to the respondent management.
To render the court's order futile, the termination order and resolution were apparently fabricated at a later stage.
Therefore, the petitioner was not notified of the termination order earlier.
The matter cannot proceed until a definitive finding is made as to whether the order of 1641991 was actually passed on that day or later.
The case is sent back to the record to make a definitive finding of fact and to offer the parties an opportunity to present evidence.
[Paras 1 and 2]
