Response: According to Nirmaljit Kaur, Advocate, the "Watch and ward" staff are subject to the same Army discipline as the other members of the Regular Army.
Therefore, the petitioner who was a "Watch and ward" staff member is entitled to pension and must receive the same increment as other members.
The court ruled that delay in approaching the court does not disqualify the petitioner from receiving the pension, but he will not be entitled to interest on the amount of arrears of pension.
