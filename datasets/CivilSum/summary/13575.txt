The trial court decreed the plaintiff's suit for a declaration that she was entitled to receive ad hoc family pension at the rate of Rs. 125/- per month from 30.9.77 and an enhanced ad hoc family pension of Rs. 150/- P.M. from 1.12.1979.
The plaintiff was also entitled to the arrears of pension from the due date with interest at 12% P.A.
from 4111986 until the date of payment.
The appeal of the State was dismissed, and the plaintiff's second appeal was upheld.
The courts below could not grant interest at the rate of 12% per annum under S 34 CPC from the date of decree until realization, and future interest could only be granted at the rate of 6% per annum.
The plaintiff was entitled to future interest at the rate of 6% P.A.
from the date of the decree of the trial court until payment and not at the rate of 12% P.A.
as granted by the courts below.
