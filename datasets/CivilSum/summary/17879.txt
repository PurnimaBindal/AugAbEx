Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection Grade - Assistants of both `B' Class and `A' Class offices must be placed in a single seniority list - Selection grade promotion must be granted in accordance with this seniority list - Assistants cannot choose to join either A or B class office - No advantage can be given to those working in `A' class office - Ruling of single Judge confirmed.
