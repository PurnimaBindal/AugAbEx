Rajasthan Service Rules, Rule 86 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 16 - Applicability - The service rules are applicable unless there is inconsistency with a special provision - The 1958 rules relate to misconduct by Govt. servants and penalty imposition - Rule 86's general provisions cannot be used to alter or modify orders issued under the 1958 rules by competent authorities.
[Paras 15 and 16]  B. Rajasthan Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rules 16(9) and (10) - Principle of Natural Justice - Full opportunity given by the Inquiry Officer - No personal hearing was given by disciplinary authority before passing the order - Non-observance of the principle of natural justice cannot be grounds for quashing a legal and just order passed by public functionaries while exercising their statutory powers.
