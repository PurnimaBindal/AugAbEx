Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Petitioners who completed the 2nd Professional Course of BDS were not given roll numbers for the 3rd Professional Course due to difference in batch systems - University's Rules and Regulations applicable to the course state it to be from June to June - Respondent argued that the petitioners did not complete 12 months for the 3rd course - Court finds that the petitioners were not of September 1999 batch, but regular students who missed their first examination - University aimed to save one year of students - Petitioners entitled to appear in 3rd Professional Course examination in June, 2000 - University cannot deprive the petitioners from issuing roll numbers or withhold their result.
