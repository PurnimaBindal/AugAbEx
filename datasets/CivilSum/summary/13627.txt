Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 39(d) and 226 - Pay Commission/Equal pay for equal work - Despite the report of the Pay Commission only being recommendatory, the Government cannot disregard it without proper consideration - Failure to consider the report may result in discriminatory and arbitrary actions - Any aggrieved parties may seek legal redress through the Court's writ jurisdiction.
