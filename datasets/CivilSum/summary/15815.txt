In accordance with the Rajasthan Recruitment of Dependents of Government Servants Dying While in Service Rules, 1975, Rule 5, and the Rajasthan Computer State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1994, Rule 6(i)(ii), a compassionate appointment as a programmer under the 1975 rules must be viewed as a substantive appointment as established by the rules of 1992, despite being described as temporary and not having any consequences.
Seniority should also be determined from the date of the initial appointment, which is considered to be a regular appointment under the rules.
If a petitioner has been wrongly screened under rule 6(i)(ii) by disregarding his regular appointment under the 1975 rules, his rights cannot be compromised solely because of the screening.
The petitioner is entitled to a suitable placement and any subsequent benefits.
