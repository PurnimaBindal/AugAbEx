Constitution of India, Article 14 - Rajasthan University Act, Section 23-A - Rajasthan University Ordinances, Ordinances 65(vii) and 257 - Recognition or equivalence of degrees or diplomas awarded by a University established by law - In the absence of any express provision to the contrary, degrees or diplomas conferred by a University established under a statute of India will be considered acceptable without any declaration of recognition or equivalence to those granted by any other University - These qualifications must be accepted as valid for all purposes where such a qualification is necessary and cannot be disregarded.
