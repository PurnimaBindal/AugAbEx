Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 39 Rules 1 & 2 - Interim injunction - Education institutions - While the principles of Order 39 of CPC must be taken into consideration, the Court normally should not interfere with the authority's decision at the interim stage - Courts should not issue any interim directions that amount to granting a final relief asked for - The High Court cannot normally grant a mandatory order even at the final stage - The High Court granted interim relief to run a Medical College despite specific refusal by the Govt. but such an order cannot sustain as Medical College cannot be established except with the previous sanction of Govt. - Extra-ordinary powers under Article 226 are to render justice in accordance with the law and mystery has no place in the judicial process.
