The petitioners are seeking promotion to the rank of Inspector in the Haryana State Crime Record Bureau on the basis that their juniors have been promoted.
They were initially recruited as Constables and eventually promoted to various positions, including ASIs and Sub Inspectors.
The petitioners claim that they were senior to all those who were later absorbed in the FPB However, their promotion claims were dismissed due to delay and latches, with their writ petition being filed after 15 and 7 years respectively.
