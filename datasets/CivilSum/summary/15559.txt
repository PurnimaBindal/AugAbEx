The Bhakra Beas Management Board has a joint seniority list that combines regular employees and those sent by transfer or on deputation from the Punjab and Haryana States.
However, the staff from the States cannot be considered employees of the Board as there cannot be two employers of an employee.
Joint seniority cannot exist between regular staff and transferee or deputationists, and the Board cannot promote employees who came from the States without ignoring its permanent staff.
[Paras 12 and 13] B.
The petitioner is challenging the joint seniority list that includes employees who were recruited directly by the Board and those working on transfer or deputation.
However, the transferee and deputationists are not necessary parties.
