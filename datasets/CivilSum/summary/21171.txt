The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226, dictate the appointment process for Assistant Engineers, including the requirement of minimum qualifications, such as a BE degree.
However, in this case, the petitioner provided false information regarding their BE qualifications in the writ petition in an attempt to commit fraud.
The High Court should not have accepted the writ petition of an ineligible person, and the appeal was granted to dismiss it.
This decision reflects the principle that individuals cannot benefit from their own fraudulent actions.
[Paras 9 and 10]
