Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. 2 Rule 22 - Gratuity - Leave encashment - Departmental proceedings - If the conditions stated in Rule 22 are not met, holding back gratuity and leave encashment is not justified - The retiree shall receive such delayed dues with 18% interest if not paid within 3 months - Enquiry must be completed by the authority within 3 months and payable dues of the petitioner with interest must be paid within a week.
