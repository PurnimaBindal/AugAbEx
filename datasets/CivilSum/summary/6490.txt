Constitution of India, Art. 226 - Writ - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Employees remain suspended - The Tribunal granted back wages to the respondents - The petitioner argued that it was up to the employer to decide whether or not to pay back wages for the period when an employee remains suspended and the Tribunal was not justified - However, the impugned order was not perverse - The period between the date of dismissal (including the period of suspension preceding such dismissal) and the date of retirement will be considered as duty for all purposes - Therefore, the respondents are entitled to full back wages.
