Constitution of India, Articles 226, 311 - Reinstatement - Back wages - A conductor who was dismissed from service due to shortcharging of fare was reinstated by the Labour Court but was denied backwages - High Court also denied backwages - It was held that backwages cannot be granted to an employee who has not worked and has charges of misappropriation proved against him - Granting backwages would lead to indiscipline in service, and the court cannot be a party to it.
