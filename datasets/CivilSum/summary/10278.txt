Constitution of India, Article 226 - Adhoc promotion - Reversion - After respondents 1 to 14 were promoted on adhoc basis as Junior Stenographers and cleared the test, the test was set aside and the respondents were reverted and the appellants were selected - Respondents filed writ to declare test valid and quash reversion, same was allowed - Subsequent promotees filed an appeal questioning the order of quashing reversion - Held, appellants' grievance appears to be premature and cannot be treated as direct and immediate to bring them in the bracket of ‘aggrieved persons’ to maintain this appeal - Appeal dismissed accordingly.
