Constitution of India, Articles 309, 226 - Recommendations of Beri Commission as approved by Committee of Secretaries - High Court cannot issue mandamus or high prerogative writ directing State government to comply with recommendations - Such direction would interfere with Governor's exclusive authority to frame rules under Article 309 - State has discretion to accept or reject Beri Commission report even if approved by COS - Appeal allowed.
