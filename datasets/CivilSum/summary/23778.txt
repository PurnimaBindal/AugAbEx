Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Model Service Rules for State Enterprises - The UP State Leather Development and Marketing Corporation Limited General Rules, 1981 - Appointment - Deemed Confirmation - Termination of services without Show Cause Notice - The appellant was initially appointed as Assistant Manager in the U.P.
State Leather Development and Marketing Corporation, then selected and appointed as Divisional Manager.
His services were ultimately terminated on the basis of the Board of Directors' recommendation due to a perceived deterioration in work performance.
However, the appellant was considered by the respondents to have satisfactorily completed his period of probation, and was treated as a confirmed employee with dismissal by giving 3 months' notice.
Despite this, the Rules were violated as no opportunity was given to explain his conduct before the decision to discharge was made.
The decision was therefore found to be in violation of principles of natural justice, and the impugned order of termination was set aside.
