According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner, a JBT teacher, claimed the benefit of Government instructions after her District Board school was taken over by the State Government.
The Government had granted a selection grade to teachers borne on the provincialised cadre who had completed 18 years of service on 1st Oct, 1974.
The petitioner's grievance was that she was being treated as if she belonged to the State cadre.
It was determined that until the entire provincialised cadre was exhausted or wiped out, such teachers should be considered part of the State cadre.
The Government is supposed to keep two separate cadres, i.e. Provincialised Cadre and State Cadre.
It was held in view of the D.B.
Judgment of High Court reported as Naginder Singh v. State of Punjab, 1990(1) RSJ 123, respondents cannot deny that the petitioner belongs to the provincialised cadre and had to be kept in that cadre at her due place in seniority.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to the benefit of Govt. instructions and must be given a selection grade on completion of 18 years service in provincialised cadre.
