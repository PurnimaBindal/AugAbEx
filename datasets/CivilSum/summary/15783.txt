Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Admissions - Entrance test for admission to Post Graduate Medical/Dental Courses in Government colleges in Punjab - SC candidates are entitled to reservation of seats - However, clearing the entrance test with floor marks is mandatory - Availability of seats alone is insufficient for SC candidates to claim admission without clearing the entrance test.
[Paras 2, 3 and 4] B Admissions/Education - Admission to Post Graduate Medical/Dental Courses - In-service candidates - Reservation for members of SC/ST in services under the State Govt. - Petitioners in-service candidates are not eligible to be considered against 60% of the quota reserved for such candidates in Post Graduate Medical Courses - Such courses cannot be deemed as services under the State Govt.
