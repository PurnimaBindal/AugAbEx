Section 16 of the National Counsel for Teacher Education Act, 1993 states that physical education CP Ed-DP Ed. courses in recognized institutions must conduct examinations.
The appellant enrolled in a C.P. Ed. course at a respondent college during the academic sessions of 1993-94, 1994-95, and 1995-96, but no examination was conducted by the board.
The college was later derecognized by the government after the enactment of the NCTE Act of 1993.
Despite this, the appellants filed a writ seeking directions for conducting examinations in 2006.
However, the provisions of the NCTE Act only apply prospectively to students who have taken the examination after the college's derecognition on August 17, 2005.
As the college was duly recognized before that date, the respondents are directed to allow the appellants to participate in the examinations for the courses of CP Ed and DP Ed. for the sessions of 1994-95 and 1995-96.
