Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - State Bank of India Officers Service Rules, Rules 67 and 68 - Departmental and criminal proceedings- It is not compulsory to stay departmental proceedings during the pendency of criminal proceedings on the same issue - Both proceedings can occur simultaneously - The purpose, procedure, mode, and standard of proof required in both types of proceedings differ- The decision whether the departmental enquiry would seriously prejudice the delinquent in defense at the criminal trial is always a question of fact in each particular case - The principles to be followed have been reiterated.
