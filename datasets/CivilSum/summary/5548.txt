Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Haryana Co-operative Societies Act, 1984, Section 102 - Jurisdiction - The operation of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 is not excluded by Section 102 of the Haryana Co-operative Societies Act - The jurisdiction of the Labour Court is not excluded - Petitioners never raised the objection on jurisdiction of Labour Court before filing the petition - Petitioner's objection of jurisdiction cannot be allowed now that they have lost.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination - Territorial jurisdiction - The territorial jurisdiction of the Court at Hissar is not excluded by an order of termination passed in the Head Office at Hansi and merely communicated at Meerut.
