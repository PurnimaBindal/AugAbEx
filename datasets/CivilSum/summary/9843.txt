According to Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India, the correction of date of birth is not a legal right, even if it was entered in the petitioner's own handwriting and served the bank with those entries without questioning it until reaching the age of superannuation.
The petitioner cannot challenge the entry of the date of birth in the service record at the end of his career.
The petitioner's right to correct his date of birth cannot be based solely on his Matriculation certificate.
[Paras 4, 9 and 10]
