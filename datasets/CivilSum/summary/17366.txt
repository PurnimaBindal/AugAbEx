The withholding of two annual grade increments with and without cumulative effect are not the same thing.
Therefore, it is held that clause (ii) of Order 36 does not include withholding of increments with cumulative effect, which is considered as a major punishment.
The order of the Tribunal in relation to forfeiture of wages during the period of suspension with a modification of withholding of two annual grade increments without cumulative effect is proper.
[Paras 3 and 7]

The writ of certiorari is not to be issued on mere technicalities or formalities.
As the Tribunal has done substantial and material justice in this case, there is no need for interference.
[Para 4]
