Constitution of India, Article 136 - Promotion - Fixation of basic pay - The respondent, who was previously an employee under Indian Telephone Industries in Category-H with a pay scale of Rs. 835-1419, was promoted as an Officer in Grade I with a basic salary of Rs. 2600.
It was determined that there was no reduction in the respondent's basic salary during the process of fixing their salary in their new role.
