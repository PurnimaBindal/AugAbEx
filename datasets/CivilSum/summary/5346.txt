Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 226 - Army Service Regulations, Regulation 529 - Custodial death - Compensation - Petitioner claims compensation for the death of her husband in custody - Foundation of the Petitioner's case is an affidavit stating the deceased was murdered - Officer giving the affidavit was involved in the espionage - Army authorities found no one responsible for the death - Inference of an inquest being done and the death being unnatural cannot be accepted without evidence to substantiate the claim - Petition dismissed accordingly.
