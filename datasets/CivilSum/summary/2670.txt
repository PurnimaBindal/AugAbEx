Sections 2(9), 2(13) and 2(17) of Employees State Insurance Act, 1948 - Definition of Employee - Technical and non-technical employees are not distinguished in the definition of employee - It extends to skilled, semi-skilled, clerical and employees performing manual functions - However, an all-encompassing definition would not have been included for the term "employer" if every person engaged by an employer had been intended to be covered - A general definition interpreted liberally would have sufficed.
