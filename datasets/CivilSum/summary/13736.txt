Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Criteria of selection - Once the selection process starts, the fixed criteria and selection norms cannot be changed - The selection agency disregarded the eye sight fitness criteria and got rid of the educational qualification criteria after the selected candidates failed in the fitness tests - Without scrapping the education requirement, none of the selected candidates would have achieved the required merit compared to the non-selected candidates who had higher qualifications - The candidates who were declared as failed appealed and got selected without any provision for appeal - The alleged appellate authority did not provide adequate reasons for the selection of candidates who were declared failed by the selection committee - This violated the prescribed selection criteria - The selection is nullified.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Bias - Territorial bias - The accusations of bias against one member of the Selection Committee cannot be accepted outrightly as he is not presumed to have influenced other members - However, in this instance, the said member received appeals addressed to himself and played a role in selecting all the candidates from his region who were declared fail in the physical tests without justification - The allegations of bias have been confirmed.
