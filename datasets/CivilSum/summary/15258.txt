Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-O(7) - Closure of undertaking - Definition of 'Exceptional circumstances' - A corporation in financial crisis without support from state and unable to stand on its own was considered an 'exceptional circumstance' justifying the closure of the undertaking.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-O, 25-O(1), 25-O(7) - Closure of undertaking - Exemption from provision of Section 25-O(1) - Failure to specify period as required by section 25-O(7) - The order to wind up was issued with approval of the concerned Minister, and the omission to specify a time limit did not render the exemption invalid.
