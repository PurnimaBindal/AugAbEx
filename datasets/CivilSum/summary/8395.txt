Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - De-recognition - Natural justice - An educational institution was de-recognized without a hearing, and no reasons were given or provisions made for existing teachers and students of that institution - This violated the principles of natural justice - The recognition was later restored - Respondents No. 1 and 2 filed a petition claiming salary - A single judge directed the appellants to release the salary to the respondents - The appellants appealed against this decision - They were directed to regularize the services of the petitioner-respondents, but no order was made regarding the arrears of salaries to the respondent-teachers, as they had already relinquished them.
