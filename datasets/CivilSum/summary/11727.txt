Constitution of India, Article 41 - Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Sections 32 and 33 - The State must focus political policies on reducing inequalities and increasing disabled individuals' access to education and employment opportunities - The 1995 Act mandates a 3% reservation for handicapped persons in direct recruitment for ministerial class I, II, and III posts.
[Paras 9-11] i) Section 32 - Examination of job requirements for suitable posts for the handicapped to effectively perform their duties - The identification of such posts cannot render the Act void.
[Paras 12 and 17] ii) Government holds the power to exempt establishments from reservation.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 41 - Handicapped persons must be allowed to apply for advertised posts - Individuals cannot be blamed for not indicating any physical handicap when even the regulations require physical fitness without defects.

Constitution of India, Articles 41 and 14 - Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948, Section 79 - Service of Engineers (Electrical) Regulations, 1965, Regulation 9 - The Act mandates a compulsory 3% reservation for handicapped persons - The regulations to the extent of repugnance will be void.
