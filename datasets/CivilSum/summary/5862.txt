Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - State - Instrumentality of State - Agency of Government - The definition of "instrumentality" or "agency" of Government is not limited to corporations created by statutes, but also applicable to government companies and societies.
Even a private body or corporation may fall under the definition of State if it is an agency of the Government.
A corporation fully financed by the State and under its control will come under the ambit of Article 12, and a writ petition is maintainable against such a corporation.
The tests to determine if a private body is an instrumentality of Government are the volume of financial assistance received from the Government and the quantum of Government control.
Cases like AIR 1985 J&K(FB), AIR 1975 SC 1331, AIR 1979 SC 1628, and AIR 1981 SC 487 have been followed in determining the same.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 39(a) and 41 - Termination - Rules and power to terminate the service of permanent employees with a simple notice - Any Rule or clause in the appointment order that empowers an authority to terminate the services of a permanent employee with a simple notice is ultra vires to Article 14 and violative of Directives Principles of State Policy contained in Articles 39(a) and 41.
Such service rules and bye-laws are liable to be quashed, and orders passed in such exercise of unguided power cannot be sustained.
Cases like AIR 1986 SC 1571, 1992(2) SCT 339 (P&H), and 1992(2) RSJ 383 have been followed.
