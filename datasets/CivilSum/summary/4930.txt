Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 12(3) - Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 47(1) - Discharge - The Petitioner was discharged from his service on medical grounds.
It is held that as per Section 47(1) of the Act, no establishment shall dispense with or reduce in rank any employee after acquiring disability.
The Petitioner is entitled to the job in the same cadre in which he was working at the time he acquired the disability.
