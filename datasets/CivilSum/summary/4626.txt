According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Section 19 of the Administrative Tribunals Act of 1985, if an Administrative Tribunal has been established to deal with the service matters of government employees, then any related matter cannot be presented before the High Court.
Both individual and group claims fall under the jurisdiction of the Tribunal, including matters related to enhanced working hours for employees.
This power is vested in the Tribunal under Article 323-A of the Constitution.
