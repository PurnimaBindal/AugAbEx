Constitution of India, Articles 124(4) and 124(5) - Judges (Inquiry) Act, 1968, Sections 3 & 4 - Judges (Inquiry) Rules, 1969, Rule 9 - The appointment of a Judge of the Supreme Court and the High Courts cannot be revoked during his tenure except in accordance with Article 124(4) and 124(5) of the Constitution and the applicable rules - Judges are not allowed to review the inquiry report before it is submitted to the Speaker of the Lok Sabha, but they can submit their response to the Parliament - The Inquiry Committee is not a Tribunal to be challenged under Article 136 of the Constitution and judges who are removed from service can only seek judicial review of the decision of the Parliament on the permissible grounds available to them.
