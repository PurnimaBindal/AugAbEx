Andhra Pradesh Ministerial Service Rules, 1966 (as amended in 1986), Rules 1, 3(17) and 4(2) - Promotion - Experience - Reversion - Watchmen in the Zilla Praja Parishad promoted to Junior Assistant positions before completing five years on the feeder post face the possibility of being reverted.
Reversions were made after proper notice, as these promotions are governed by Rule 3(17) and Rule 4(2), which do not require three years of service in the lower cadre.
