Constitution of India, Article 311 - Natural justice - Provision of documents and preliminary enquiry report - The petitioner did not request for a copy of the preliminary enquiry report from the enquiry officer; instead, he sought for the TA bills of his colleagues to demonstrate that they were also falsely claiming travelling bills.
These documents would not have been useful for his cross-examination of the witnesses or to prove his defence.
He used a first-class travel ticket issued in 1975 as proof of his travel claim in 1974.
The non-supply of these documents did not prejudice his defence, and thus there was no violation of natural justice principles.
[Paras 4 to 6]
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punishment - Extent of punishment - Unless it is found that the enquiry was breaching statutory regulations or the principles of natural justice, the court cannot intervene in the punishment order based on the proportionality of punishment.
