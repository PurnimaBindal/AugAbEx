Central Civil Services Rules, Rules 26 and 49 - Resignation - Entitlement for Pension - If an employee's resignation is accepted, by Rule 26, all past service is forfeited - No pension or computation of pension is allowed for that period under Rule 49.

Interpretation of statutes - All provisions of a statute should be read together - No part of it should be deemed superfluous - Court cannot add to a statutory provision which is plain and unambiguous - Language of a statute is key to understanding legislative intent - The Court must rely on what has been clearly stated, not what was intended.
[Paras 6 to 10]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Interpretation - Words and Phrases - "The question is not what may be supposed and has been intended but what has been said", "Statutes should be construed not as theorems of Euclid" "but words must be construed with some imagination of the purposes which lie behind them".
