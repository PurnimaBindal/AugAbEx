Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Family Pension - Re-fixation of - The family pension for the petitioner was required to be refixed as per the 4th Pay Commission recommendations - However, the respondents did not perform this re-fixation - The State's only defence that the petitioner did not ask for her right is not sustainable - It is the duty of the employer/State to promptly pay the proper dues to retired employees, their widows, and family members - The petition is allowed and the State is directed to pay interest at 10% on the delayed payment of arrears of family pension to the petitioner.
