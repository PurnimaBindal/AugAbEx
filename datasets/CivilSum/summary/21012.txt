According to Rule 173 of the Pension Regulations of the Army, 1961, a disability pension can only be awarded if the injury resulting in disability was caused by military service.
In the case of the respondent, who suffered a moderately severe injury, the medical board reported that the injury was not related to military service and he was diagnosed with a "Neurosis superimposed on an immature histrionic personability" by a psychiatric OPD.
Despite the recommendation to invalidate him out of service, the injury could not be attributed to service.
