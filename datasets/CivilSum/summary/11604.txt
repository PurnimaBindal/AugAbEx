The State of Haryana established a policy for the regularisation of employees who have completed three years of work and 240 days each year, according to the Indian Constitution's Articles 14, 16, 39, and 43.
The petitioner's services were terminated but were later reinstated with the continuity of service award, making them eligible for regularisation.
[Para 7] B The Industrial Dispute Act prohibits the denial of regularisation based on physical absence, and the petitioner was reinstated with continuity of service.
Respondents cannot refuse to consider the petitioner's eligibility for regularisation because of their absence.
