According to Section 4(6) of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, gratuity forfeiture is applicable for riotous or disorderly conduct.
However, the petitioners did not engage in such conduct by simply forcing other employees to leave their work station.
Therefore, the forfeiture of their gratuity was unjustified as there was no instigation of damage to machinery or creating a disorderly situation.
