Two employees who joined on the same day, with one joining in the afternoon and the other before noon, had their seniority determined by the managing committee of the College.
This determination was later accepted by the Director of Education, and an enquiry report by the Director is on record.
Therefore, the seniority fixed in this manner cannot be questioned.

The State Government has the final say in granting approval for a Lecturer position in colleges that receive grant-in-aid.
The decisions made by the State Government are binding on the College and its parties, unless found to be arbitrary or in violation of any rule.
