Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - A petitioner was declined a position as a Hindi teacher due to not meeting the qualifications of `Prabhakar' as specified in the advertisement.
However, the petitioner held a qualification of MA in Hindi and B.Ed.
which was higher than those notified in the advertisement.
The appointment cannot be denied on the basis of not having passed Prabhakar when candidates with BA in Hindi and B.Ed.
have already been appointed as Hindi teachers.
[Paras 5 & 6]
