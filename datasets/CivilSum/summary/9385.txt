Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Appointment subject to final decision of a pending writ petition in the High Court - The appointment would be subject to the doctrines of lis pendence and estoppel, and cannot take away the effect of the final order and the rights of the petitioner.
[Para 7]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Appointment subject to passing of the qualifying test of NET - A reasonable amount of time must be given to the appointee to pass such a test - Any time given less than the required amount must be deemed to be totally unreasonable - The termination based on that reason, despite the employee passing the examination before the termination date, is considered to be arbitrary and unjustified.
[Paras 9 to 11]
