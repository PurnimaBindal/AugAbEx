The West Bengal Home Guards Act, 1962 deals with appointment, deputation and absorption in the service of home guards.
The petitioners were initially appointed as home guards and later deputed to work for the ONGC for about three years and four months.
They are now claiming absorption and regularization in ONGC on the grounds that they worked there for a sufficient amount of time.
However, in the absence of any statute, statutory rule or policy decision, they cannot claim regularization in service.
The mere fact that a person is in continuous service does not guarantee regular appointment.
[Paras 6 and 8]
