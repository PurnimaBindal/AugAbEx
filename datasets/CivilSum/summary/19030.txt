Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - `Payment Seat' - A petitioner was admitted to an Engineering College against a payment seat.
The petitioner claimed that because he was dependent on an Armed Forces personnel whose father had died in action, he was entitled to admission for a free seat.
However, 80% of free seats were reserved for candidates who had passed their qualifying examination from the State of Haryana and the remaining 20% seats had been retained for Haryana residents.
Moreover, there was only one free seat for Ex-servicemen and one payment seat available in the college where the petitioner was admitted, and the petitioner has not passed the qualifying examination from Haryana State.
No deviation from the Admission Brochure provisions was permissible and no fault can be found with the admission process.
