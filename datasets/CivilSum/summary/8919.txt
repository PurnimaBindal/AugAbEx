Instructions on compassionate appointment under Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India allow any dependent child to apply if they have attained the age of majority within three years of the employee's death.
However, the authority must still verify whether the family of the deceased is still in distress and destitute.
If the family has been subsisting for more than 12 years, one of the dependents cannot automatically claim appointment without following the proper selection procedure and affording opportunities to others.
In a case where two brothers of the petitioner are already employed by the same employer, a claim for compassionate appointment after over 12 years cannot be accepted as it suggests that the family was not and is not in distress.
Therefore, the petition is dismissed.
