Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation/Promotion - Petitioner was promoted subject to the approval of the Public Service Commission.
However, the case forwarded to the Commission for approval was found to be incomplete.
Consequently, the Commission requisitioned the complete record repeatedly, but no explanation was given as to why the record was not sent.
As a result, directions ought to be issued to the government to submit the complete record within a stipulated period, and the Commission to pass appropriate orders within a month thereof.
