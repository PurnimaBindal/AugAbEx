The Co-ordinator cannot accept application forms delivered by post after the last date, even if the delay was caused solely by the postal authorities.
The rules mentioned in the information brochure/prospectus must be strictly adhered to, as any relaxation may give rise to illegal and arbitrary action.
The sender's communication mode becomes the post office's agent.
Only if the addressee has chosen registered post as the only mode of communication will the post office become the addressee's agent.
Thus, the decisions made in Saurabh Aggarwal and Anurag Sharma's cases have been overruled.
[Paras 6 to 11, 14 to 17, 25, 29, 32, 36, and 37]  B.
The Co-ordinator cannot entertain incomplete applications after the last date if it was specified that no incomplete applications would be entertained.
[Paras 16, 32, 35, and 37]  C. The Post Office does not serve as a common agent.
It functions as the communication mode of a party that chooses this method of communication.
[Paras 12, 25, and 26]  D. Admission brochures and prospectuses are legally binding and must be followed.
[Paras 6 to 11, 36, and 37]  E. If the authority makes an error, it does not provide the basis for other applicants to demand similar action, citing discrimination.
