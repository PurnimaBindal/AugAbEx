Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Air Force Act, 1950, Sections 39 and 73 - Absence from duty - Dismissal from service - Imprisonment - Quantum of punishment - Section 39 prescribes imprisonment for up to three years for an offence of absence from duty and Section 73 empowers the court martial to order dismissal from service - Court martial's order of dismissal for long unauthorised absence cannot be deemed unjust or unfair because delinquent's excuse of father's sickness is not sufficient.
