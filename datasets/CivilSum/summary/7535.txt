Under the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 1(3)(b), 2-A, 7-A, 19-A, a hotel unit of ITDC is not entitled to claim infancy benefits.
This is because the hotel's employees are subject to transfer between units and the hotel is under control, supervision, and management of ITDC.
Additionally, the accounts of the hotel form part of the balance sheet and profit and loss accounts of ITDC.
Therefore, ITDC and the hotel constitute one establishment for all practical purposes.
This was established in Para 27 and 28.
