Constitution of India, Article 14 - Admissions/Education - Change of speciality in MD Courses - A selected candidate's admission to different specialities is based on their preferred choice of three, with no limitations on the order of preference - The petitioner chose specifically to apply for MD (Pathology) and was accepted - She later sought to transfer into the speciality of 'Radio-diagnosis' by claiming a vacant seat, despite never having listed it as a preference - The authority is not obligated to offer her the seat before other wait-listed candidates in that speciality and she has no legal standing to demand the seat - The Article 14 rights of the Constitution were not violated in this case.
