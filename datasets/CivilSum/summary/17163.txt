Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 4 and 4-A - Compensation - Owner of truck cannot use non-compliance with Section 4-A as a means to deny claim of claimants that deceased was a worker under their employment - Testimony of driver and father of deceased stating that deceased was employed as a helper in the vehicle cannot be challenged as it was not contradicted during cross-examination - Investigator's evidence cannot aid the insurance company's defense - Insurance company is therefore liable to pay compensation.

Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Finding of facts - Age of deceased worker at time of accident is based on factual evidence and cannot be challenged in appeal.
