According to the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 and section 166 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, if a worker dies in an accident related to their job, they are awarded compensation and interest.
The liability for payment of interest on the compensation falls on the insurance company if the employer fails to make payment within the stipulated period.
This is a statutory liability, as stated in 1997(8) SCC 1 and distinguished in 2004 ACJ 452.
