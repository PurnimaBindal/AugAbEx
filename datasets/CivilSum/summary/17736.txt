Secondary Education Services Commission and Selection Board Act, 1982, Section 21 - UP Intermediate Education Act, 1921, Section 16(g) (3) - Promotion/Seniority - A senior teacher was asked to work as Principal as a transit provision while rules were being amended - Amended rule provided for filling the post of Principal by promotion on the basis of seniority from teachers/lecturers - Petitioner became entitled to be appointed as Principal under the amended rules - Resolution of the committee relieving her from the post of Principal was not approved - Managing Committee instructed to treat the petitioner as ad hoc principal until the resolution was approved.
