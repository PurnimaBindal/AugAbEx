Constitution of India, Article 16 - Stagnation increment - Employee requested reversion from promoted post - Clause 4 of Bipartite settlement states that employees who refused promotion after commencement of the settlement on 8.9.1983 cannot claim stagnation increment - Respondent previously received promotion - Court rules that since Clause 4(b) of the settlement does not address employees who sought reversion, respondent cannot be denied benefit of stagnation increment.
