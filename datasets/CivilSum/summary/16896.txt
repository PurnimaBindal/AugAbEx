The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21, dictate that compassionate appointments should be made.
The policy/circular letter dt. 851995 of Haryana Govt. on ex-gratia appoint of dependents of deceased employees while in service interprets "one step lower" as "one pay-scale below," so the pay scale of the deceased's post determines the pay scale of the post on which the dependent is appointed.
The date of death of the deceased is crucial and not the date of the appointment order.
In addition, if the pay scale of the post held by the deceased is revised after death, it does not entitle the dependent to claim the post or pay scale held by the deceased.
