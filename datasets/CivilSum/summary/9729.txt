Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14 and 16 - Qualifications - Establishment of qualifications for the Lecturer in Physical Chemistry post - It is an established legal principle that the prescription of qualifications and eligibility criteria is within the domain of the executive authority and/or expert body - The court lacks expertise and jurisdiction to investigate such matters - Having considered the issue, the University and expert body have prescribed the qualification for the Lecturer post, and it is not the court's place to replace it with its own opinion.
[Paras 5 & 6]
