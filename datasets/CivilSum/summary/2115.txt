Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Section 11(1) - Date of Birth - Compulsory retirement - In case of giving wrong date of birth at the time of selection, retirement will be compulsory- Discrepancy in Date of Birth present in School Leaving Certificate and Certificate issued by Village Chowkidar- It is determined that Village Chowkidar falls under the jurisdiction of the Rules laid down under the Land Revenue Act, and the certification issued by the Village Chowkidar is considered equivalent to that given by a Public Servant.
