The Punjab Municipal Services (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1975, Rule 22 - Power to relax qualifications for the Class III Executive Officer position is to be made with respect to a class or a category of people, not individuals.
Rule 22's relaxation applies to certain groups such as Wards of Freedom Fighters, Wards of those affected by terrorism and Wards of those who have done some outstanding civil social service.
The unqualified respondents were neither a class nor a category of persons who could receive the relaxation of their qualifications in terms of Rule 22 of the 1975 Rules.
The power to relax is not for individuals and, therefore, wasn't exercised in favour of the respondents.
Thus, they did not fall into any of the above three categories, and their appointment was quashed.
