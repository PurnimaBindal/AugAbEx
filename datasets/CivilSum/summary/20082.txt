Maharashtra Civil Services (Joining Time Foreign Civil Service, Payment during Suspension, Dismissal and Removal), Rules, 1991, Rule 72 - Prosecution proceedings under Section 309 IPC - Suspension during prosecution - Reinstatement after acquittal - The disciplinary authority has discretion to grant back wages, pensionary and other consequential benefits after acquittal, it cannot be granted as a matter of course - It would be detrimental to the maintenance of discipline to grant a person suspended on valid considerations full back wages upon acquittal - The appellant is not entitled to be treated on duty during suspension for pension, etc.
