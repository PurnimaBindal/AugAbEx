Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol.
I (Applicable to Haryana), Rule 48 - Note 3 - Interpretation of - The quality of work of an employee must improve annually during an efficiency bar review - The appellant had his efficiency bar stopped for ten years through one order just before his retirement - This violates Rule 4.8 Note 3 of the Rules - The order is quashed as a result.
[Para 6] B.
Service Matter - Declaratory decree - A suit for declaration is decreed - The necessary consequence is that the appellant is entitled to all financial benefits resulting from the declaratory decree - The order to deduct the amount is set aside.
