Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (XIV of 1947), Section 11 - Punishment of termination inflicted upon the petitioner for only one day's absence - The Court finds the punishment to be excessively severe and unthinkable - There are various reasons why a person may need to be absent for a day - Despite the petitioner's absence without leave, it does not necessarily justify such a drastic measure of terminating his employment permanently.
