Respondent's counsel, BC Prabhakar, argues under Article 226 of the Indian Constitution regarding a correction in the petitioner's date of birth on his school leaving certificate.
The court is strict about allowing challenges to date of birth, and any challenge assumes that the original records were incorrect and must be corrected.
In this case, the petitioner was asked for the original entries in the records to rely upon, but only a subsequent alteration was challenged, which is justified.
The action of the Board favoring the school leaving certificate over the personal records of the company which recorded the petitioner's date of birth is unacceptable.
Therefore, the alteration is liable to be struck down.
