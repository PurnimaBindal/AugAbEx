The Bihar Service Code, 1952, Rule 97 states that for the period of suspension, the salary of the suspended person should be withheld.
However, the respondent in this case retired while under suspension, and no order to impose punishment or withhold retirement benefits was made during the departmental proceedings.
As a result, the learned judge correctly directed the appellant to pay the respondent his salary for the period he was suspended.
[Para 7]   B.
According to Bihar Pension Rules, Rule 43, if no order is made to withhold pension and other retirement benefits, the impugned order that directs the payment of such benefits cannot be interfered with.
