According to Section 9 of the Civil Procedure Code, 1908, and Section 13-A of the Industrial Employment Standing Orders Act, 1948, any dispute under the Industrial Disputes Act must be adjudicated by forums created by the Act.
The jurisdiction of civil courts is therefore barred.
In this specific case, the time specified by clause 31 of the standing orders for correction of date of birth had lapsed, and the single judge granted relief on the basis of the decree.
Therefore, the suit for a declaration regarding termination of service is invalid and illegal.
