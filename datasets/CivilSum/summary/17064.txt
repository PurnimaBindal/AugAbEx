Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pension Regulations for Army 1961, Regulation 186 - Disability Pension - Service Element - Despite having a disability of less than 20%, the petitioner is entitled to receive the service element of disability pension - The respondents are instructed to release the petitioner's service element benefit - Only 38 months of benefit can be claimed prior to the filing of the writ petition.
