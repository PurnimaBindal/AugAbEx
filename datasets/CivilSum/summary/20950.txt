The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pension - Dearness Allowance - Re-employment - Classification - Pensioners who re-employ get Dearness Allowance added to their salary, while those who are not re-employed can be reasonably categorized - It is valid to deduct Dearness Allowance from the pension of re-employed ex-servicemen - The same rule applies to family pension.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pension - Re-employment of pensioner - Classification - Deducting the pension while fixing the salary of re-employed ex-servicemen who were in civil posts on 111986 is unconstitutional.
