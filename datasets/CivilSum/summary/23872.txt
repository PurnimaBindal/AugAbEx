Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation on regional basis - Admissions - 15% of MBBS course seats reserved for All India Pool and remaining 85% for Chandigarh local students pool - Candidates from local pool allowed to contest with All India Pool candidates - Local candidates pool filled up first separately to avoid encroachment of All India Pool seats - Authorities' action deemed fair and reasonable - Meritorious candidates of Chandigarh Pool not excluded.
[Paras 14 to 16]  B Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservation - Non-availability of reserved category candidates - Inter-changeability of reserved seats - Rule provides that if reserved category candidates are not available, the remaining reserved seats cannot be filled by the general category candidates - Total MBBS admission seats divided into two pools - If reserved category candidates are not available in one pool, the available candidates from the other pool have to be considered before offering reserved seats to general category candidates.
