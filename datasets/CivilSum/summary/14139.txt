Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19(4) - Departmental proceedings - Meaning of "would abate" used in Section 19(4) - The intent is to prevent duplication of actions by the applicant on the topic of the application U/S 19(1) - It does not cover departmental proceedings that are not part of the admitted application - The tribunal stopped the authority from conducting an inquiry into matters older than 4 years - The departmental proceedings against an employee cannot be regarded as proceedings for addressing the grievance concerning the issue of the application which attracts the Section 19(4) restriction on abatement.
