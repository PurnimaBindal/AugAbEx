Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Resignation - Withdrawal of - An employee is permitted to withdraw their resignation as long as there are no constitutional, legal, or contractual limitations imposed on them, even if the resignation has already been accepted by the employer - The petitioner submitted a letter withdrawing their resignation on the forenoon of 111998, before the acceptance of the resignation became effective and operative in the afternoon of 111998 - Therefore, the termination of the petitioner's service from 111998 is invalid and illegal - The respondents are to reinstate the petitioner into service and the petitioner is entitled to all benefits resulting from this decision.
