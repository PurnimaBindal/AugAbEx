Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ of mandamus - State cannot be compelled to appoint dependent of deceased Govt. servant on post of their choice - Appointment to same post as deceased is not a right.
[Para 6] B.
Constitution of India, Article 309 - Rajasthan Recruitment of Dependents of Govt Servants Dying while in Service Rules, 1975, Rule 8 - Compassionate appointment - Claimant claimed appointment to post of accountant held by his deceased father, but was appointed on post of Junior Accountant instead - Selection Committee, consisting of appointing authority and his two subordinate officers, interviewed the claimant to determine whether he would meet the minimum standards of work and efficiency required for the post of Accountant - There was no error in the judgment of the Selection Committee to determine that the claimant would not be able to maintain the minimum standards of work and efficiency expected for the post of Accountant - The appointment on the post of Junior Accountant was legal.
