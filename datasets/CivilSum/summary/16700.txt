In the case of a petitioner who is a Commander in the National Security Guard and whose minor son underwent open heart surgery at Escorts Hospital for Ventrical Septal Defect, the Central Government Health Scheme is required to provide full reimbursement.
The treatment was carried out at Escorts Hospital upon recommendation from a government hospital.
The petitioner cannot be denied full reimbursement merely because the charges incurred were above the package rate, and the respondent is directed to provide the full amount.
[Paras 4 and 5]
