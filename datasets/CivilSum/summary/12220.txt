Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provision Act, 1952, Section 1(3) Schedule I - Courier Service - Coverage under Act - The government included courier service as a specific entry in a subsequent notification, and it has a different status than a forwarding agent.
Therefore, the petitioner will be liable to be covered from the date of the new notification, despite respondents' argument that they were to be included in the term "forwarding agent" mentioned in the July 22, 1997 notification.
