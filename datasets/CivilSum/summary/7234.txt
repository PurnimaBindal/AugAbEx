The Annual Confidential Report that contained adverse remarks was set aside, but it was discovered that similar adverse remarks were previously recorded and repetitive punishment was given.
The Reporting Officer himself testified that the adverse remarks were recorded within the stipulated period and were based on plaintiff's work and conduct, as per government instructions.
The court cannot review the subjective satisfaction of the reporting officer, and interference from the court is only possible if there is evidence of rule/instruction violation or mala fides.
Since there is no such evidence, the appeal was dismissed.
