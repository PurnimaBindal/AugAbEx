"For Respondent: Randhir Singh, AAG, Punjab.
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection Grade - Length of Service - Transfer - Petitioner, an Art and Craft Teacher in the Punjab Education Department - Was transferred to a different District after completing qualifying service for the grant of Selection Grade - Despite the transfer, the petitioner should still be granted selection grade, as he had already become eligible before the transfer - The Respondents are directed to grant the petitioner selection grade.
