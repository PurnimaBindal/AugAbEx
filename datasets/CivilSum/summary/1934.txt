Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Dismissal - Ground of unauthorised absence from duty - Enquiry - The Disciplinary Authority dismissed the petitioner from service and designated the period of absence as "leave without pay" - The petitioner argued that the period of absence cannot be considered as a ground for dismissal as it was treated as "leave without pay" - However, it was held that the termination of service was legal and the regularization of leave without pay does not negate the dismissal order.
