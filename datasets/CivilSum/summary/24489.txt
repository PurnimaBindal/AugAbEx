Article 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India relate to eligibility criteria for admissions to Medical Super Specialties courses, including completion of the training period before the entrance test, and the rule is equally applicable to all.
Prospective provisional admissions given in the past cannot be made a ground to challenge the decision to have firm and clear eligibility criteria for future candidates in the interest of medical education.
The doctrine of legitimate expectation also cannot be applied when the decision was openly demonstrated in the prospectus to all.
[Paras 7, 8 and 9]
