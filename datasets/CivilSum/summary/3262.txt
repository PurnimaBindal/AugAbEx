Constitution of India, Article 14 - State Bank of India, - India Officers (Determination of Terms and Conditions of Service Order 1970, Paragraph 19(1) - Retirement/Extension in service after the age of superannuation - Employees cannot claim extension in service after the age of 58 years, as it is not a right, but only granted to promote the interest of the Bank.
Extension is not given with an aim to benefit the retiring employee.
