Minority Institutions have the freedom to run educational institutions, but the expression "Administration" used in Article 30 of the Constitution does not include mal-administration.
Termination of services of a teacher with 25 years standing without giving any opportunity and even without disclosing a reason for termination is not justified.
Termination of services affects not only the employee concerned but also his family/dependents on him.
Teachers appointed under minority institutions covered by Article 30 are entitled to the principles of natural justice equally as employees having statutory status.
[Paras 6 to 9, 11, 13 and 20]

A contract of service providing for termination of services by giving three months' notice or salary in lieu thereof is opposed to public policy and principles of human rights and natural justice.
Termination of a teacher with 25 years of regular service without reason and without supplying particulars of termination to the Deputy Director of Schools is illegal and arbitrary.
[Paras 17, 18 and 20]
