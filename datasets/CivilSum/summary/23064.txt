Indian Forest Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1966, Regulations 3, 5(4) - State Forest Service Officers - Promotion to IFS - The Selection Committee's evaluation shall not be easily challenged in court.
The respondent filed a petition for de novo selection and a declaration that the respondent should have been assessed as 'outstanding.'
After reviewing the respondent's ACRs, the tribunal found that there were significant irregularities by the Selection Committee and ordered a review.
The findings were upheld by the High Court, and no allegations of mala fide were raised.
The Selection Committee's methodology for grading eligible officers was meticulously followed, and there was no reason for the tribunal to interfere, as evidenced by the materials on record.
