Constitution of India, Article 226 - Jurisdiction - Army officer - Petitioner's claim for "Paschimi Star" 25 years after alleged participation in Indo-Pak War 1971 - Respondents denied the claim based on inordinate delay - Rejection deemed well-founded and on grounds of laches - No reasonable person can expect adjudication after a quarter-century lapse.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Honorary Commission - Army officer - Petitioner granted new retirement date due to late promotion - Consideration of Honorary Commission in last two years of service emphasized, as petitioner had been considered four times but not contemporaneously - Respondents directed to consider petitioner's case for Honorary Commission.
