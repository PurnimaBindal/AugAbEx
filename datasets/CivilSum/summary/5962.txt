Constitution of India, Article 12 - State - Analysis of whether a scheduled company governed by various provisions of Industrial (Development and Regulation) Act, 1951 can be considered an instrumentality of State as per Article 12 of the Constitution - Several tests provided - In cases where the Govt. has only a minority of shares and minimal representation on the Board of Directors - There is no significant control over the company's administration by the Govt. - Mere control of price and instructions to the Industrial sector and financial assistance through policies or Banks is not enough to qualify as an instrumentality of State under Article 12 of the Constitution.
