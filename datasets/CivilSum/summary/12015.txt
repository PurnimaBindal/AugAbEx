Articles 14, 16 and 226 of the Indian Constitution state that a regular appointment to a post under the State or Union cannot be made without issuing an advertisement in the prescribed manner, including inviting applications from employment exchange.
[Para 5]
According to Articles 14, 16 and 226 of the Indian Constitution, a petitioner who has been regularly selected as per the recommendations made by the Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC) cannot be denied appointment to a regular post of Lecturer in Library Science.
The appointment of Respondent No. 4, who was appointed as Lecturer in Library Science on an ad hoc basis only on a stop gap arrangement, is set aside and the authorities have been directed to appoint the petitioner as Lecturer in Library Science.
