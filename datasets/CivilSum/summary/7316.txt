Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Departmental enquiry - Natural justice - Dismissal as punishment - No violation of natural justice observed in enquiry, appeal or revision even if the inquiry officer was changed and findings recorded by another - Dismissal from services not disproportionate to the charge of moral turpitude against the petitioner, whose case was decided up to the level of D.G.P.
