Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural justice - Speaking order - For punishment, reasons must be recorded in support of conclusions - An order of punishment given to a Govt. servant affects their civil rights - If punishment is imposed after issuing charge-sheet and holding a regular inquiry by recording evidence, the action taken cannot be called purely administrative - While the authority who imposes a punishment is not required to write a judgment, the punishment order must include enough reasons for the conclusions and non-acceptance of explanation and pleas made in the representation of the culprit, so that they may challenge it effectively and be aware of the reasons.
