Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol. II, Rule 317-A - Pension - Qualifying Service - Individuals paid from contingencies can count half of their service as qualifying service, but only if the conditions in sub-clause (i) of Rule 317-A are met.
The stipulation that only half of the service counts towards qualifying service is arbitrary and lacks logic.
The phrase "half the period of service of such persons paid from contingency" in sub-clause (i) of clause (f) of Rule 317-A is illegal and therefore cancelled.
Additionally, individuals not responsible for a break in service can have it waived under Rule 423.
Petitioners are entitled to consider their whole services as qualifying service for pension purposes.
