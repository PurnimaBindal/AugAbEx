Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Petitioner received two promotions in April 1990 and February 1991.
He recruited constables in March 1991.
However, the respondent received complaints against him about some irregularities and illegalities committed.
He was served with a charge sheet dated 25996.
The petitioner challenged the holding of the departmental inquiry on the ground that despite receiving complaints, he was promoted.
It was held that the respondents were aware of the lapses committed by the petitioner yet did not take any action.
On the contrary, the petitioner was promoted in 1994, ignoring his misconduct.
The departmental action's delay has not been adequately explained, and six years is a reasonably extended period to postpone administrative action, particularly when the facts were already known.
The promotions achieved by the petitioner during this time amount to condoning his behavior by the respondents, and the delay/lapses in holding the inquiry are also grounds to quash the order criticized in the petition.
[Paras 7 and 8]
