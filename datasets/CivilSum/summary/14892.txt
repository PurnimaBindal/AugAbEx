Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. 1, Chapter VIII, Rules 8.116 and 8.117 - Earned leave - Government employees serving in vacation department may be eligible for earned leave for duties performed during vacation - If the employee was required to work during a full 60-day vacation, they can receive up to 30 days of earned leave - If the employee worked for a shorter period, they will receive a corresponding proportional amount of leave credits - They cannot claim earned leave for the entire period, regardless of the number of days worked during the vacation.
