Constitution of India, Article 14 - Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, 1957, Section 95(2)(b)(3) - Delhi Municipal Corporation Service (Control and Appeal) Regulations, 1959, Regulations 8 and 9 - Termination of services - Natural justice - Dispensing with holding of enquiry - Allegations of fraud in securing appointment - Termination deemed invalid due to lack of show cause notice and enquiry - No understandable reasons in the notice justifying the dispensing with the holding of enquiry - Employees were initially engaged on daily wages, then regularised and confirmed in regular service - Allegations of fake and fraudulent documents produced for appointment - No reasons recorded in the notice dispensing with the enquiry indicating any application of mind - Such an action by the authority cannot withstand legal scrutiny and should be quashed.
[Paras 34 to 38]
