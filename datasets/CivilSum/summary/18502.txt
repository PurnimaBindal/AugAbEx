According to the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 4(l)(b) and 4(l)(d), and the Kerala Service Rules, Rule 97(1) and 97(8), in cases of permanent total disablement, lump sum compensation and leave salary are provided.
However, it has been determined that while deduction can be made from half-monthly payments, leave salary cannot be deducted from the lump sum compensation payable under Section 4(l)(b) of the Act.
