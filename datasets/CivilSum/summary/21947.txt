The appellant applied for the position of Labour Inspector but was not promoted, despite being an Assistant in the same department, due to the filling of the post through both promotion and direct recruitment.
A roster with a quota was applied, and the appellant's claim for promotion was not granted.
It was held that the roster was only meant to be a guide for filling up the reserved quota for different groups, and once the quota was achieved for a specific community entitled to reservation, the roster would no longer be useful for that community as the guideline had already been followed.
