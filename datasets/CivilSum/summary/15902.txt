The Punjab Govt.
Notification No. 22/1/92-1 HB-III/3897 dated 2441996 issued by the Department of Health and Family Welfare, Clause 1(6) provides that candidates who have done post graduation as Registrars/Demonstrators are not eligible for appointment as Senior Residents when being promoted by selection from the posts of Junior Resident.
The respondent No. 3 did not complete his post-graduation in Paediatrics as Registrar, as he was declared Junior Resident and the posts of Registrars were abolished.
[Para 7] B.
According to the rule, registrars/demonstrators doing MD while working as such are not eligible for appointment as Senior Residents, but Junior Residents after doing the same course are.
The Court cannot interpret the rule in a manner that amounts to rewriting the language of the rule, as the Govt. had an opportunity to disqualify Junior Residents if they wanted to.
