UP Public Services (Reservation for Physically Handicapped, Dependents of Freedom Fighters and Ex-Servicemen) Act, 1993, Section 3 - Constitution of India, Article 15(4) - Admission - Educational Institute - Reservation - The Reservation Act does not automatically apply for admission in educational institutions - The government must independently apply its mind to provide reservation under Article 15(4) of the Constitution.

Constitution of India, Article 15(4) - Admission - Medical Course - Reservation - A petitioner who claims admission through reservation on the grounds of being a dependent of a Freedom Fighter produced a certificate that did not have the same form and language as the brochure but still met the criteria - The certificate cannot be rejected on the ground of being improper.

UP Public Services (Reservation for Physically Handicapped, Dependents of Freedom Fighters and Ex-servicemen) Act, 1993, Section 2(b) - Dependent - Definition - A candidate who is a son or daughter (married or unmarried), a son or a son or an unmarried daughter of a son, of a Freedom Fighter is a dependent under the Reservation Act - The candidate cannot be denied benefits on the basis of not being financially dependent on the Freedom Fighter.
