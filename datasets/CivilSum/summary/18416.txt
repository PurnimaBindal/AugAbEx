Rules for Recruitment to Faculty/Scientific/Technical/Ministerial and Other Posts, 1995, Rules 3 and 4 - Contractual appointee - Regularisation/permanent absorption - The order of appointment mentioned that the appointment is on a contract for five years - However, the rules permit making a contractual appointment - Therefore, even though the petitioner contends that they were compelled to execute the contract of service, they cannot claim an appointment for an indefinite period as they had accepted the aforementioned conditions - It was also held that the petitioner cannot be classified as a helpless Class IV employee who was unfairly compelled to accept the contract, which is advantageous to the respondent.
