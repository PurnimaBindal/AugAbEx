Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(s) & 25-F - Retrenchment - Compensation in lieu of reinstatement - Workmen who worked on a part-time basis for a total period of 2 years and six months, and were paid Rs. 200/- per month, were removed from service after a long period of 11 years had gone by - The stayed execution of the award during the pendency of the writ petition is a reasonable ground to refuse reinstatement and instead grant a lump sum compensation of Rs. 25,000/- - The award that directs reinstatement with back wages is modified to include a direction to pay lump sum compensation in lieu of reinstatement.
[Para 19]

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference - The industrial dispute referred is not just strictly a dispute but also matters that appear to be connected with it - The correct date of termination disclosed by the workmen is accepted as the basis for the adjudication of the dispute - This is not illegal.
