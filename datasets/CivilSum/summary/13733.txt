Govt. Order No. 179-GAD of 1994 dated 17-02-1994 grants ex-gratia relief of Rs. 2 lacs to the kin of those killed by militants.
According to subsequent Govt. Order No. 805-GAD of 1999 dated 15-7-1999, the amount was enhanced to Rs. 5 lacs for next of kin of all security forces, including those killed in operations who were permanent residents of the State.
The term "Security Forces" in this order includes the State Police Force.
The government has not yet made a decision regarding the enhancement of ex-gratia relief, and this has deprived the families of many brave soldiers who have lost their lives while fighting with the militants.
The State has a duty to come to their aid as soon as possible.
In this case, the petitioner's son was a member of the Police Force and was killed while fighting against militants.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to the payment of enhanced ex-gratia relief at the rate of Rs. 5 lacs.
The writ petition has been allowed with a direction to the respondents to release the balance amount of Rs. 3 lacs to the petitioner within two weeks.
