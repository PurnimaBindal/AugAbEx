The Constitution of India, in Articles 14 and 16, specifies the cut-off date for determining pay scales based on the principle of equal pay for equal work.
The equation of pay scales applies only from the date of the order, not from the date the pay scale was established.
The cut-off date is determined based on the specific circumstances of the case and is not arbitrary or in violation of Article 14 of the Constitution.
