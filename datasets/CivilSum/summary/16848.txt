Punjab State Education Class III (Primary School Cadre) Services Rules 1997, Rules 6 - Appointment to the post of JBT teacher - To be eligible to apply for the post, candidates must possess the minimum qualifications of Elementary Teacher Training Course or JBT Course or BA B.Ed.
- Diploma in Physical Education is not a recognized qualification for the post and was not advertised as such - Non-fulfillment of required qualifications can lead to termination of service.
[Paras 4 & 6]

Punjab State Education Class III (Primary School Cadre) Services Rules, 1997, Rule 6 - Qualifications for the post of JBT teacher - The minimum qualifications advertised were Elementary Teacher Training Course or JBT Course or BA B.Ed.
- Diploma in Physical Education is not mentioned as a qualification in the advertisement or in the rules - This qualification is relevant only to the post of Social Studies Masters and not the JBT teacher post - The two posts belong to different services and are governed by separate rules - A person with a physical education background may not be qualified to teach subjects such as Hindi, English, and Arithmetic to young children.
[Para 9]

Punjab State Education Class III (Primary School Cadre) Services Rules 1997, Rule 6 - Qualifications for post of JBT - The rule-making authority is best equipped to determine the qualifications necessary for a particular job - Courts do not have the expertise to prescribe qualifications for a post.
