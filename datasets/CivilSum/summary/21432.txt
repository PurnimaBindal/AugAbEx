Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14, 16 - Service Law - Screening Test - Selection process - All candidates were given the same time for preparation - Appellants appeared at the examination without any protest - Being employees at the time did not entitle them to ask for extended time - Therefore, appellants are estopped and precluded from challenging the selection process.
[Paras 13 and 15] B Andhra Pradesh State and Subordinate Services Rules, Rule 22-A, Sub- rule (2) - Reservation for women - Recruitment - Notification stipulated reservation for women at 30% - Subsequently increased to 33-1/3% with retroactive effect - The applicable rule would be the one existing at the time of selection - The preference for women candidates was in accordance with the rule - High Court erred in stating that reservation for women should have been confined to 30%.

Constitution of India, 1950, Article 309 - Recruitment and Conditions of Service - Amendment of Rules - Retrospective effect and retroactive operation - The rules prevailing at the time of notification should be applied in the absence of any rule with retrospective effect - If there is a rule with retrospective effect, the applicable rule would be the one validly brought on the statute book from an earlier date - The State can amend the rule with retrospective effect subject to constitutional limitations.
[Paras 10 and 12] D. Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14, 16 - Recruitment - Service conditions - The rules applicable for selecting candidates are those prevailing at the time of notification.
