Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab State Cooperative Supply and Marketing Cooperative Service (Common Code), Rules, 1967, Rules 15, 215, 217(e) - Disciplinary proceedings - Managing Director - Administrative Committee has the authority to delegate powers to Chief Executive Officer - No delegation of powers with retrospective effect allowed - Issuing the charge-sheet and appointing an Enquiry Officer by petitioner-Federation was without jurisdiction - Any order passed by the punishing authority was rightly set aside by the Revisional Authority - Impugned order allowing revision petition challenged on grounds of jurisdiction - Court dismissed the Petition.
