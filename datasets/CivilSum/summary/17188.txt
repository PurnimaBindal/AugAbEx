Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Termination - The petitioner was hired as casual labour on a daily wage basis for a specific term and her services were re-engaged as required.
The petitioner represented for re-engagement in preference to juniors, but it was held that there is no regular post.
The respondent is directed to consider engaging the petitioner in preference to freshers and juniors, as and when services of casual labour on an ad hoc/daily wage basis is required.
[Para 4]

Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Termination - The petitioner was a casual labour on a daily wage for a specific term and seeks re-engagement.
However, it was held that the petitioner has equally efficacious remedy available to her under the provisions of the Industrial Disputes Act.
[Para 3]
