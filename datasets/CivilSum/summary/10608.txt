According to the West Bengal Recognized Non-Government Educational Institution Employees' (Death-cum- Retirement) Scheme, 1981, Clauses 7(d) and 8 and the Constitution of India, Articles 14,16 and 21, only the period of service during which the petitioner worked as an approved teacher should be counted for determining qualifying service for pension benefits.
It cannot be argued that the period during which the petitioner worked as a member of the organizing staff should also be counted.
The scheme does not violate Art, 14, 16, 21 of the Constitution even if some employees are deprived of pension benefits.
