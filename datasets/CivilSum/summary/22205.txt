Recovery order issued by the labour authorities as the basis of a compromise award under Uttar Pradesh Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 6H and Payment of Wages Act, 1963 should not have been set aside by the High Court.
[Para 6]
Minimum Wages Act, 1948, Section 26(2) does not apply to employee of Cooperative Societies.
[Para 4]
Service conditions of employees of Cooperative Societies are governed by statutory rules under UP Co-operative Societies Act and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
Therefore, Industrial laws do not apply to these employees.
