Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - The company opened a new marketing office in Panaji (Goa) to promote sales and required experienced individuals to man the office.
In light of this, the plaintiff, who was a Sales Assistant with some experience, was transferred to Panaji.
The appointment letter clearly stated that the plaintiff could be transferred to any place in India and any section by the company, and the rights of both the employer and employee are governed by the terms of their contract.
