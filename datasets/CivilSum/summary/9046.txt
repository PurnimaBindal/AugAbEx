Constitution of India, Article 226 - Abandonment of Job - A workman was employed as Muster Roll Beldar.
Municipal Corporation lacked evidence to prove that he was employed for a specific period and abandoned his job.
Labour Court stated that he was not employed for a specific period.
Therefore, denial of employment to respondent by the Corporation for whatever reason shall have to be only according to law.
An enquiry should have been held against the workman if he had abandoned the employment.
However, that ground was not applicable in the present case.
The findings of the Labour Court were upheld.
[Paras 4 and 5]
