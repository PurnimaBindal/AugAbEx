Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(s) - Employee - Employee working as Deputy Manager (Finance) - Documents on record suggest employee is not doing manual work, and instead is primarily a consultant in risk management, involving inputs related to creativity and imagination - Therefore, it can be held that under the given circumstances, the employee cannot be called a skilled workman performing manual or non-manual work.
