According to Article 309 of the Constitution of India, along with Sections 2 and 3 of the Punjab Cooperative Societies Act of 1961, the Sub Divisional Magistrate, Chandigarh has the authority to exercise the powers of a Registrar for Cooperative Societies in his capacity as Joint Registrar.
The Punjab Government has authorized Joint Registrars to exercise all of the powers of a registrar through notification under section 3(2) of the Act, which is being adhered to by the Chandigarh Administration.
There is no delegation of powers when the powers are granted under the Act itself.
The Sub-Divisional Magistrate stayed the termination of a society employee within his jurisdiction under the Act.
