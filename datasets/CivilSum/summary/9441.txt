Constitution of India, Article 226 - Police Medal for Gallantry - Challenge of recommendation - An officer's challenge is based on the ground that the officer only sustained simple injuries.
However, the officer in question displayed exemplary gallantry and leadership of the highest order in a remarkably successful operation.
As such, the officer's case is covered under the rule, as the award was not conferred for the injuries alone but for bravery, leadership qualities, and gallantry displayed while keeping in view the risks incurred and the obligations and duties of the officer.
The decision is, therefore, not arbitrary or malafide and does not suffer from legal infirmity.
[Paras 14, 15, 16, 18 and 20]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Police Medal for Gallantry - Challenge of recommendation - The court establishes that judicial review of such an act is limited; it is not for the High Court to sit in appeal over the decision of competent authorities.
The court must only verify whether the decision was taken while keeping guidelines and material on record in view, irrespective of its sufficiency.
[Paras 19 and 20]
