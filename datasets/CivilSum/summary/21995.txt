State Bank of Patiala (Employees) Pension Regulations, 1995, Regulation 40(3) - Family pension - After reaching majority, the respondent applied for pension - The widow would receive family pension until her death or marriage - Despite being appointed in the same bank and receiving gratuity and provident fund of the deceased employee, the respondent's mother opted for her own pension and not family pension - The High Court wrongly directed the grant of family pension to the respondent.
