Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Adverse remarks - If communicated or uncommunicated adverse remarks are delayed and if such remarks were expunged by the reviewing authority, then these remarks are assumed to not exist - Furthermore, if an official was overlooked from promotion on the basis of such remarks, then the authority is obligated to consider his case from the stage he was ignored in the same way that his other counterparts were considered at that stage.
[Para 6]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Eligibility criteria for promotion - Adverse remarks - If an employee is ignored from consideration based on adverse remarks that were subsequently expunged for any reason, but eligibility criteria has been altered in the meantime that make him ineligible, such person cannot be ignored from reconsideration or deemed ineligible based on the new criteria - Rather, he has the right to be reconsidered according to the criteria that existed when he was unlawfully disregarded, and his juniors with the same eligibility were considered/promoted.
