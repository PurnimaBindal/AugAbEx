Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I, Part-I, Rule 75 - Suspension - Notice - The competent authority ordered the period of custody to be treated as leave of the kind due, but failed to issue notice to the petitioner as required by Rule 75 of Punjab Civil Services Rules - Issuance of notice is mandatory - The impugned order has been set aside.
