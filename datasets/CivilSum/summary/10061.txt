Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 25F, 25G and 25H - Retrenchment - The rule of last-come-first-go - Completing 240 days applies only to Section 25F and not Sections 25G and 25H - Despite the fact that the workman invokes the provisions of Sections 25G and 25H, the employer must still follow the principle of last come first go.
The workman is not required to prove the same.
