The Tribunal had the discretion to award appropriate relief to the workman based on the violation of principles of natural justice by the employer, and the employer did not plead financial incapacity or employment of the workman after termination.
Therefore, the Tribunal was justified in awarding back wages to the employee.
[Para 11] B The Tribunal and Labour Courts are aware of the serious consequence of termination of employment and the resulting sufferings of the employee.
The respondent workman's only alleged fault was absence from duty for two days due to sickness, and his past performance was not shown to be poor.
The Tribunal rightfully exercised discretion to award back wages to the workman based on the totality of circumstances, and there are no grounds for exercising certiorari jurisdiction to quash the award.
