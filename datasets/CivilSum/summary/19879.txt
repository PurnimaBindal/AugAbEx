According to Guru Nanak Dev University, Ordinance 10(j), 11 and 18, the assumption of using unfair means is not allowed and evidence must be presented.
The Unfair Means Committee is expected to act as a quasi-judicial authority and base their conclusions on facts and reasons.
It is important to note that a candidate's inability to secure good marks in some questions does not automatically mean they have used unfair means.
