Uttar Pradesh Public Works Department Subordinate Engineering Rules, 1951, Rules 12 and 16 - Expiry of select list - Appointment from select list - Candidates must be appointed within one year of the select list being published, after which appointments from the list are not permitted.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Advertised vacancies - Filling more vacancies than advertised violates fundamental rights under Articles 14 and 16 - Being on a select list does not guarantee appointment, nor can a candidate claim it as a right even if vacancies remain unfilled - Court will not require a state to continue making mistakes in appointments, even if errors have been made in the past.
