Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - The State Transport Corporation has a Disablement Retirement Benefit Scheme that offers suitable employment to one of the disabled drivers' sons, if available.
The petitioner accepted voluntary retirement under the promise that his son would be given a job.
For five years, his son has not received any employment.
Consequently, the Corporation has been directed to offer the son of the petitioner a job at the top of the list.
Furthermore, his qualifications will be considered later onwards for the suitable position.
