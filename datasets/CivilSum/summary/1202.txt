Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana Government Employees (Assured Career Progression) Rules, 1998, Rule 5 - ACP Scale - Seniority - Additional increments - Ad hoc and work charged services followed by regular service must be counted for the purpose of seniority and pension - However, it cannot be used for the grant of higher pay scale or benefit of Assured Career Progression Scheme upon completion of 8/18 or 10/20 years of service - The distinction made between the two types of services for the purpose of grant of additional increments is not valid.
