Constitution of India, Article 311 - UP Recruitment of Dependent of Government Servants (Dying in Harness) Rules 1974, Rule 1 - UP Temporary Government Servants (Termination of Service) Rules 1975 - Compassionate Appointment - Termination - The Petitioner's appointment was deemed permanent under the Dying in Harness Rules, despite being initially temporary and contingent on passing an examination.
The Rule provides for three chances to clear the examination before termination can occur.
As the Petitioner did not receive the opportunity to complete all three chances, his service could not be terminated.
