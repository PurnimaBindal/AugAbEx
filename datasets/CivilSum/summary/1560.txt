The waiting list for appointments has a validity period of one year, with the possibility of a six-month extension if requested by the government before the expiry date.
While the Rules of Business of the Commission are procedural and not mandatory like those under Articles 309 and 124 of the Constitutions of India and J and K, a small deviation from them will not invalidate the selection.
Furthermore, if future vacancies have arisen within the waiting list's validity period, and the government has requested an extension, the delay in granting the extension due to a missing file will not affect the selection and appointment of candidates for these vacancies.
[Para 14]
It is not necessary for the exact number of future vacancies to be specified.
If the existing ones have been advertised with a note that future vacancies within a year will be filled from the waiting list, this is sufficient notice.
Candidates who did not succeed in the selection process cannot claim a breach of Articles 14 and 16 due to the non-advertisement of future vacancies.
