Limitation Act, 1963, Article 113 - Period of limitation - Three years - Minimum time scale - A civil suit for a declaration that an order permanently bringing the plaintiff to the minimum of the time scale must be filed within three years as prescribed by Article 113.
In this case, the impugned order was made in 1982 and challenged in a civil suit filed in 1987, which was dismissed on the ground of being barred by limitation.
This dismissal was found to be legal.
