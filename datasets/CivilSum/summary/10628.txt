Central Industrial Security Force Rules, 1996, Rule 34 - Punishment of Censure - Allegation of misconduct - A petitioner accused a senior of engaging in corrupt practices - The inquiry officer later determined that the complaints were unfounded - Nevertheless, the petitioner was charged and subject to preliminary investigation without his knowledge - In the end, the penalty of 'Censure' was imposed - The burden of demonstrating that the petitioner's accusations were unfounded rested on the disciplinary authority, who failed to present evidence - As a result, the petitioner's appeal and revision were dismissed and the impugned orders could not be upheld without proof of guilt - The petition was therefore granted.
