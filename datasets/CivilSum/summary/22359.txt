Uttar Pradesh Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 6(6) - Civil Procedure Code, Section 152 - Review - Modification of the award - Jurisdiction of the Labour Court to modify its own orders - Section 6(6) of the Uttar Pradesh Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 corresponds to Section 152 CPC.
Its power is limited only to correcting clerical or arithmetical errors, or any accidental slip or omission, but cannot be exercised for merits, which only an appellate Court can do.
