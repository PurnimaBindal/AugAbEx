The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, Punjab Services (College Cadre) Class II Rules 1976, and Regularisation were all factors taken into account for the Post of Lecturers in this case.
The Petitioners were contracted with fixed salary, seeking regularization, equal pay for equal work, vacation salary, and the right to continue until appointment of regular incumbents.
The appointments of the Petitioners were not valid as no selection committee or criteria were disclosed, making their appointment against specified rules and norms.
The Petitioners are not entitled to the relief of regularisation as they have no right to the post, but they are entitled to receive payment for the summer vacations of this academic session.
The Government has been directed to make ad hoc appointments within 30 days if regular appointments cannot be made within that time.
