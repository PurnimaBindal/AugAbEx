Orissa Homoeopathy Medical Teaching Service (Methods of Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1980, Rule 3 - Selection - Criteria of selection - The appointment rule does not provide guidelines for determining the suitability of the candidates.
Members of the selection board base their assessment on the confidential character rolls of the teachers already in service.
If the rule doesn't have such guidelines, executive instructions under Article 162 may supplement it.
The selecting agency does not have the jurisdiction to establish their own criteria for selection unless specifically authorised by the rules.
It is the function of the rule-making authority, which is a legislative function, not executive.
The selection board acted without jurisdiction or authority, and the selection is quashed to be made afresh.
The government is directed to issue necessary instructions or amend the rules.
[Paras 32 to 36, 40, 44 and 45]
