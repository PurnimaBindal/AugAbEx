The All India Judges Association case had directed the State to implement retrospective promotions in the Higher Judicial Service since March 21, 2002.
The State did so via a notification published on January 9, 2007.
Civil Judges complained that this retrospective application affected their vested rights.
Data showed that out of 22 posts, only 19 were filled by promotion under the unamended rules, whereas 170 candidates were promoted under amended rules.
Additionally, 76 posts meant for departmental competitive examination were also added to promotees quota because there were no qualified candidates under merit quota.
Despite this, Senior Division Civil Judges were not found to be adversely affected by the retrospective amendment.
[Para 9] B When it comes to promotion in the subordinate judiciary, both seniority and merit should be considered.
The petitioner had no valid objection regarding the State's assurance that only merit wasn't being seen.
