Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rajasthan Educational Subordinate Service Rules, 1971, Schedule II - Recruitment - Qualifications - Post of Physical Training Instructor - The petitioner, who holds a Bachelor's Degree in Physical Education, was not considered, but persons who obtained degrees from the same universities were considered eligible and appointed - The petitioners argue that they are better qualified than those who were deemed eligible solely on the basis of their diploma in Physical Education - The petitioners contend that the non-consideration of their application is discriminatory - Therefore, the petitioners should be considered for the post of P.T.I.
