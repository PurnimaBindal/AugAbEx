Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rules 13 and 22 - Suspension - Alternative remedy - The Appellate Authority can determine the jurisdiction of the competent authority, legality, validity, and justification of the order under Rule 22 of the Rules.
Therefore, as an alternative remedy of appeal is available which is efficacious, a writ petition cannot be entertained.
