Letters Patent Appeal - Premature retirement - Re-employment sought - Denial of - Respondent sought re-employment after being denied premature retirement due to medical and domestic grounds - Appellant argues that Army instruction only allows re-employment for Army Officers who retire in substantive rank on super-annuation - Respondent's name transferred to regular reserve of officers but is not entitled to re-employment due to policy and special Army instructions - Recalling of respondent only applicable in case of emergency or war - Order of learned Single Judge set aside as appeal allowed.
