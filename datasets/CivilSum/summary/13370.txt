Constitution of India, Articles 300-A and 318 - Pension - Public Service Commission members' service conditions cannot be modified to their disadvantage after their appointment - Changes to pensionary/retiral benefits entitlements made by subsequent notifications will not apply to those appointed as members prior to the notifications - Any notification will only apply prospectively.
[Para 5]

Pension - Interest - Petitioner's pension and retiral benefits were delayed unreasonably and in violation of specific rules and laws - They are entitled to 12% interest on the delayed amount during the delay period.
[Para 6]
