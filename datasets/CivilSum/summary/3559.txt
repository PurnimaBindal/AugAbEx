Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 21 and 226 - Regularisation of services - Discrimination - Both the petitioner and one other person appointed as Class IV servants, but worked as House Keepers - The petitioner did not have their services regularised, although the other person's services were - Violation of Articles 14 and 21 of the constitution - The respondents ordered to appoint the petitioner as House Keeper with consequential benefits.
