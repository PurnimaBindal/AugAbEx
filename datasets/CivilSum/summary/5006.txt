Para 15: The Jammu and Kashmir government has the power to exercise Section 124 of its constitution, as outlined in the Jammu and Kashmir Civil Services (Contractual Appointment) Rules 2003, Rule 3, by converting ad hoc appointments into contractual appointments on a consolidated monthly salary equivalent to basic pay of the post.
The government's decision was held to be appropriate.
Para 6 and 7: The government's order regarding the conversion of ad hoc appointments into contractual appointments was applicable only to those appointed to nongazetted posts through due process of selection.
The petitioners were not recruited via the proper selection process and thus the government order was not applicable to them.
Therefore, there was no violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution.
