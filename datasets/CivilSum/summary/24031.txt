The Rajasthan Higher Judicial Service Rules, 1969, Rules 8(ii) and 15(ii), are found to be ultra vires Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution, and should be struck down.
The Rules exclude judicial officers from practice in other courts, and the object of the Rules, to recruit suitable and proper persons to the judicial service, is not achieved by excluding candidates on the grounds of lack of knowledge of local laws and regional language, thereby violating Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
Additionally, the doctrine of prospective overruling has been applied to hold the Rules as ultra vires, and it is recommended that a suitable examination be prescribed instead of excluding candidates on the grounds mentioned above.
