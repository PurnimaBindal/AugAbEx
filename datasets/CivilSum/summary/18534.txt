Constitution of India, Article 21 - Medical Reimbursement - Hospital recognized for specialized and general treatment but not for coronary bypass surgery - In this case, the words "specialized and general treatment" used for other recognized private hospitals also include coronary bypass surgery and must apply to the hospital in question.
Therefore, the appellant is entitled to reimbursement for the same amount prescribed by other recognized private hospitals for the same treatment.
