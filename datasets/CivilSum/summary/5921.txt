Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I, Part I, Rule 2.44(a) - Punjab Town Improvement Act, 1922, Sections 62 and 63 - Remunerations payable to the employees of Land Acquisition Tribunals - Remunerations paid under Section 63 of the Act are not allowances or concessions, but payments made in lieu of duties performed and special qualifications as a right available under the statute.
These rights cannot be curtailed without observing the principles of natural justice.
(1990(1) RSJ 131, 1991(3) SCC 263 and AIR 1967 SC 993 not attracted) [Para 8 to 13]

Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I, Part 1, Rule 2.44(a) - Punjab Town Improvement Act, 1922, Sections 62 and 63 - Remunerations/Reduction in remunerations with retrospective effect - No executive order having retrospective operation affecting the rights of the citizens can be passed - The executive does not have the jurisdiction to take away vested rights.
Orders passed to deprive of earned wages are without jurisdiction and vitiated.
[Para 14]

Pay/Revision of pay/Reduction of pay - Pay scales of all categories of employees were revised upwards on account of the rise in prices.
Remunerations granted under the rules were reduced in the case of petitioners with no clear rationale behind the orders.
The orders are arbitrary and liable to be quashed.
