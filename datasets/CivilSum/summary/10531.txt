For Charu Tuli, AAG, Punjab, according to Section 82(6) of the Punjab Reorganisation Act, conditions of service cannot be changed without previous approval of the Central Government.
The appellant in this case was entitled to retire at 62 years of age but was allocated to the State of Punjab and retired at 58 years of age under the Punjab Civil Services Rules, without prior approval of the Central Government.
The employee was never given the option to choose the service conditions under the Punjab Civil Services Rules and hence, the order of retirement at the age of 58 is considered illegal.
