The Constitution of India, under Articles 226 and 300-A, states that military service should be considered in pensionary benefits.
In this case, the petitioner was confirmed along with 86 other personnel, and the confirmation order was duly circulated.
The petitioner, however, claimed that he was not communicated the confirmation order, while none of the other confirmed co-employees made any such complaint.
The petitioner also failed to exercise the option within the granted time-frame.
Therefore, the order declining the benefit of counting military service is not to be interfered with.
[Paras 13 and 14]
