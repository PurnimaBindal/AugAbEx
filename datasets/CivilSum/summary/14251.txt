Under the UP Govt. Service (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1946, Rule 1 and Article 309 of the Constitution of India, a petitioner must be reimbursed for undergoing open heart surgery.
However, the petitioner in this case did not receive reimbursement for 6-1/2 years, and no reason was given for the delay.
Although the employee responsible was suspended, this did not resolve the issue.
The authority has been instructed to make the payment and provide a 12% interest.
[Paras 3 and 4]
