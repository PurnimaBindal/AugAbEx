Army Headquarter Policy, Para 4(m)(iv) - Review Medical Board - Request for - Rejected - Petitioner was initially rejected by Medical Board due to an ailment in both eyes, but was seeking a Review Medical Board after being selected in an interview and having a certificate from AIIMS stating the ailment is non-progressive - Respondents argued that Review Medical Board is not a right and only candidates with 100% fitness can be enrolled according to Para 4(m)(iv) - Although Para 12 confirms Review Medical Board is not a right, DGAFMS rejected the request with logical reasons and there was no irrationality or arbitrariness by the respondents - Petition dismissed accordingly.
