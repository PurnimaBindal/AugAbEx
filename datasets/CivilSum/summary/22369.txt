Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25-F - Completion of 240 days - Onus of proof - The workman must provide positive evidence to establish that he was continuously employed by the employer for over 240 days - Mere pleading and filing of affidavit is not sufficient to meet this burden of proof - It is incorrect to say that the employer has the burden of proving that the workman was not in its service for 240 days.
