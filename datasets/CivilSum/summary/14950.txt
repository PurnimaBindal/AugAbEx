Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Rules, 1996, Rule 266(3)(i) - Appointment - Eligibility - Post of Teacher Gr.III - The advertisement only required passing of secondary examination and did not mention English as mandatory.
Rules in force at the time of advertisement and application did not include English as one of the compulsory subjects.
Denying appointment based on Rule 266(3)(i) of Rules 1996, which included English as a compulsory subject, would be against Article 14 of the Constitution.
The petitioner is entitled to the appointment of Teacher Gr.III as per their selection.
