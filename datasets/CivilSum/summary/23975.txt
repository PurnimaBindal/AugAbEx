The Constitution of India, Article 32 - Age of superannuation - Enhancement - Judicial Officer - Supreme Court directions to frame Rules for enhancing age of superannuation of Judicial Officers - The judgment in All India Judges' Association Case does not automatically increase the age of superannuation.
It is subject to an evaluation of the judicial officer's continued utility to the judicial system and potential for continued useful service.
If found not entitled to extension of superannuation age, the officer would retire at the normal age or date of superannuation according to Service rules.
[Para 17]
The petitioner's further continuance in service would not be in public interest, according to the unanimous opinion of an evaluation committee consisting of eight Judges.
The petitioner has no inherent or fundamental right to continue in service beyond the age of 58.
Relief of enhancement of age of superannuation is not entitled to the petitioner under Article 32 of the Constitution.
