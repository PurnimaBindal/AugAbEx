According to Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, transfers can be made in public interest and should not be interfered with by courts on the basis of family disturbance or dislocation for employees with transferable posts.
These employees do not have a legal right to demand posting at a specific location of their choosing.
Interference with transfers is only permitted in exceptional circumstances, such as a violation of statutory rules or mala fides.
