Central Warehousing Corporation (Staff) Regulations, 1980, Regulation 21 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(1) - Compulsory retirement - The Screening Committee can review the work and conduct of an employee who is approaching the age of 50 years, but the order of compulsory retirement cannot be made until the employee reaches the age of 50.
The Review Committee found that the respondent/writ petitioner did not show any improvement in his performance, and passed the order of compulsory retirement after he attained the age of 50 years.
This order cannot be challenged.
