Haryana Municipal Act, 1973, Section 10(1) - Haryana Municipal Election Rules, 1978, Rule 70(4) - Election of President - Reservation of seat for SCs/BCs/Women - According to Section 10(1) or Rule 70(4), there is no language indicating that the reservation for Scheduled Castes etc. is limited to candidates elected from reserved constituencies - "members belonging to..." means only candidates who belong to Scheduled Castes/B.Cs/Women are eligible to contest for the reserved office of President, but a Schedule Caste candidate elected from a General Ward can also contest - After election, the President is the senior-most representative of the municipality and is responsible for the entire municipality - Constitution of India, Article 243-T  [Paras 9 and 12] B Haryana Municipal Act, 1973, Sections 278 and 10(1) - Haryana Government Notification dated 11.2.1995 U/s 278 of Municipal Act, 1973 - Municipal Elections - Election of President - Reservation - A government order U/s 278 of the Municipal Act can only remove difficulties and cannot be inconsistent with the statute - The government notification which deems a single Schedule Caste member elected from a reserved seat to also be elected as President is beyond the government's jurisdiction and amounts to amending Section 10(1) - Notification held ultra vires and election set aside.
