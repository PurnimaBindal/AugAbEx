According to Section 45 of the Road Transport Corporation Act, 64 of 1950, the Corporation cannot refuse pension benefits to an employee who did not opt for it, if the employee was taken back into service on court order.
The Corporation cannot argue that the employee manipulated records, and the office order states that an incomplete or conditional or ambiguous option will be deemed as opting for the pension scheme.
The Corporation's refusal to accept withdrawal of the option means that they cannot argue that it lapsed when the employee withdrew from the scheme.
Thus, the petitioner is entitled to the pension benefits under the office order provision.
