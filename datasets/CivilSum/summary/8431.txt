The writ petitioners-respondents had received two months of fire service and rescue training, but the petitioner failed the written examination for the post of fire operator.
All appointments must be made in line with recruitment rules and training alone does not entitle one to any appointment.
To obtain a writ of mandamus, the petitioner must prove their legal right and corresponding legal duties of the respondents.
[Para 7]
To be eligible for a civil post, one must pass the prescribed eligibility tests.
Without passing the eligibility test, directions for appointment are illegal.
