Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - U.P.
Recruitment of Dependents of Govt.
Servants Dying in Harness Rules, 1974, Rule 5(1) - Compassionate appointment - The period allotted for making a claim for compassionate appointment is 5 years.
If an application is made after 9 years, no direction can be given to respondent to consider the petitioner's claim after 16 years have lapsed.
