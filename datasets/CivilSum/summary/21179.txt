The legality of a strike in public sector undertakings was discussed under sections 22 and 23 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
Some undertakings were deemed public utility services, and did not issue notice under Section 22(a).
The strike was found to be illegal, and the employees were not entitled to wages during the strike period.
[Paras 21 and 23]
In Section 22 and 24 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the workman going on an illegal strike and subsequently being called off was discussed.
The lockout declared must be regarded as in consequence of an illegal strike, and cannot be regarded as illegal even if the provisions of Section 22 were not complied with.
Workmen were not entitled to wages for the lockout period.
[Para 24]
Under sections 18, 19, and 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the binding nature of an industrial dispute settlement was discussed.
A joint forum of five public sector undertakings demanded an increase of Rs. 30/- in the existing scale of pay, and a settlement awarding Rs. 25/- pm was reached in conciliation proceedings.
The tribunal refused to direct the adjustment of Rs. 25/- and held that the refusal of adjustment was proper.
[Para 28 and 30]
In Schedule 3, Item 1, and Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the minimum wages payable in 5 P.S.U's were revised by the tribunal.
The dearth allowance in one PSU was controlled by All India Consumer Price Index and others governed by local consumer price index.
Workers in the PSU controlled by All India Consumer Price Index were paid a City Compensatory Allowance.
The direction given by the tribunal that after pay revision they would not get City Compensatory Allowance was found to be improper.
