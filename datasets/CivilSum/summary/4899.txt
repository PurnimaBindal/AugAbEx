As per Article 226 of the Constitution of India, if a workman raises a dispute regarding termination before the Industrial Tribunal and is awarded reinstatement with continuity of service with full backwages by the Labour Court, the claim for compassionate/ex gratia payment must be directed to the competent authority.
The High Court and Supreme Court upheld an award in favor of a workman who unfortunately died shortly after joining the company, and it was decided that the competent authority must resolve the matter within four months in accordance with rules.
