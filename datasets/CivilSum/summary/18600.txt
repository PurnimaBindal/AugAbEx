According to Section 190 of the Air Force Act, 1950 and Para 121 of the Defence Service Regulations for Air Force, 1961, reservist pension can be obtained after completing a minimum qualifying service of 15 years.
The petitioner was discharged just 21 days before completing this period and there is a provision to condone a deficit in service for up to six months.
The impugned order which refused to condone the shortfall has been set aside, and the respondents have been directed to condone the shortfall and process the petitioner's case for reservist pension.
