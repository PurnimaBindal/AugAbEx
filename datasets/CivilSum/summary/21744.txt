As per Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a Selection Committee conducted interviews of 94 candidates and identified 8 suitable candidates for appointment as Plant Assistants.
However, 23 candidates were given positions as trainee operators instead.
The nature of work done by trainee operators and Plant Assistants is distinct.
Therefore, workmen who had accepted trainee operator positions for an extended period cannot claim higher emoluments since they were not treated as Plant Assistants at the time.
Only when their services were officially reclassified as Plant Assistant positions were they entitled to the benefits associated with that role.
