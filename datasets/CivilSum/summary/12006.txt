The Constitution of India, particularly Articles 226 and 311, together with the Railway Protection Force Rules of 1987, including Rules 151(1), 152(2), and 153, outline the disciplinary actions that can be taken against employees for gross misconduct, negligence of duty, and unauthorized absence from duty.
As per these rules, charge sheets must be issued, and quasi-judicial authorities must record reasons for their orders.
According to the present case, the inquiry officer's findings were without proper reasoning and hence cannot be upheld.
Therefore, the charge sheet and the inquiry proceedings were set aside.
