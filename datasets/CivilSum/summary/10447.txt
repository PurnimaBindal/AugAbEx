Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rules 48 and 49 - Pension - Voluntary retirement - Qualifying service - Retirement includes voluntary retirement - Pro-rata pension - There is no difference between ordinary retirement and voluntary retirement, provided that the employee has served for a qualifying period of service - Those who served for more than 10 years but did not complete 20 years of qualifying service are entitled to pro-rata pension - If employees became eligible for retiral benefits, DTC is estopped from denying any of the same.
