Constitution of India, Article 14 - Army Rules, Rule 13(A) - Departmental examination - Discharge from service - Natural justice Discharge from service due to failure to pass the departmental examination within the given time frame or chances - Subsequently passing the examination does not hold any value - Principles of natural justice are not applicable in this scenario - Following the principles of natural justice is not necessary if the situation remains unchanged.
