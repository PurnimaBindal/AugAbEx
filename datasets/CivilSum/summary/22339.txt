UP Public Services Reservation for Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and Other Backward Classes Act (4 of 1994), Section 4 - Benefit of reservation - If a candidate belonging to Naga Tribe which was tribe notified as Scheduled Tribe in State of Nagaland migrates to State of UP where said tribe is not notified as Scheduled Tribe, then the candidate cannot claim benefits of reservation as Scheduled Caste in State of UP [Para 14]
