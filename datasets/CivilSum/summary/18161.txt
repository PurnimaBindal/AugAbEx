The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 226, were referenced in a case involving the West Bengal Board of Secondary Education's mistake in a student's mark-sheet for the 2006 Secondary exam.
The petitioner's correct grade was 83, but due to the error, their mark-sheet displayed only 33 marks.
Despite all necessary precautions being taken, the error still occurred.
The Board was instructed to provide the petitioner with the correct mark-sheet, and the Secretary apologized for the mistake.
However, no compensation was awarded.
