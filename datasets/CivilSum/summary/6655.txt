Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 12 - Haryana Affiliated College (Security of Services) Act, 1979, rule 3, and Appendix-X - Retiral benefits - The petitioner, a retired clerk of Dev Samaj College for Girls, Ambala City, is seeking payment of the balance amount of gratuity and leave encashment, along with 18% interest per annum, on the grounds that the said college is affiliated with Kurukshetra University.
However, the court held that the respondent institution is not an aided institution and the affiliation of the college with the University does not entitle the petitioner to a regular pay scale.
The post of the petitioner was never sanctioned as an aided post by the University or State Government, and the college does not fall within the definition of State or instrumentality of State as required under Article 12 of the Constitution Therefore, the petition was dismissed.
