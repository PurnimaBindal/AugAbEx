As per Article 14 of the Indian Constitution, individuals who completed their education from 1st to 12th grade in rural areas are eligible for reserved seats in dental research courses, while those who completed part of their academic career in urban areas are not entitled to make this claim.
In addition, according to Article 226 of the Indian Constitution, candidates should not be allowed to join professional courses belatedly, and the date of admission must be strictly adhered to.
