Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection process - Participation - Petitioner not sponsored by Employment Exchange - Authorities accused of denying petitioner participation in the selection process - Petitioner argued in favor of authorities publishing employment notices to invite applications from all eligible candidates, instead of restricting the source of candidates to only employment exchanges - The petitioner did not challenge the validity of rules or the selection process, but only sought the permission to participate - The court held that the petitioner cannot participate in the selection process since he was not sponsored by the Employment Exchange.
