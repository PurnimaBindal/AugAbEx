Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education policy - Examination/Grace Marks - When dealing with education matters, academic standards must be followed as laid down by appropriate authorities - Decisions related to whether or not to consider the Madhyama Certificate issued by Hindi Sahitya Sammelan, Allahabad equivalent to or higher than +2 of 1st year TDS are policy decisions of State or authority vested with such power - Courts should only examine whether policy decisions or administrative orders are fair, rational and reasonable, based on relevant considerations and not mala fide, and serve public purpose or are not intended to benefit an individual or a group of persons.
[Para 10] B. Education/Recognition of degree/diplomas - The Madhyama certificate from Hindi Sahitya Sammellan Prayag, Allahabad was removed from recognized qualifications via notification on 2861985 - Qualifications and certificates obtained after 2861985 from Hindi Sahitya Sammellan Prayag, Allahabad are not valid - Individuals holding such certificates cannot be considered to have the required educational qualification.
