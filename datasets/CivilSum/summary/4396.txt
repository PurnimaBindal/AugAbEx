Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 14 - Disciplinary proceeding - Exoneration in enquiry report - Advice by UPSC and CVC - Disagreement note - Non-provision of UPSC's recommendation - Imposition of non-cumulative punishment of reduction of pay by two stages in pay scale for two years - Held, charged officer must have been provided with such material prior to finalizing punishment since disciplinary authority sought and acted on advice of UPSC to finally arrive at conclusion - Non-provision amounts to refusal of right to fair hearing - Orders nullified.
