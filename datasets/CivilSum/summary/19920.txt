Civil Procedure Code, Order 9 Rule 13 - Restoration of writ petition dismissed for default of appearance - A statement in an affidavit that the case was not noted by the counsel on the date listed for hearing is considered sufficient cause for condoning the delay.
[Paras 1 and 2] B.
Limitation Act, Section 5 - Condonation of delay - An unnoticed case by the counsel is deemed as a valid ground for condonation of delay.
