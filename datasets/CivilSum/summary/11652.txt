Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Termination of service - Appeal - Revision - The petitioner, a cooperative society clerk, had his services terminated without being given an opportunity to meet the eligibility and funding requirements.
Initially, the petitioner was appointed and approved based on the availability of funds, but his termination was challenged claiming that the rules cited were not adopted in accordance with the Act.
The revision was dismissed without allowing the petitioner to prove his grounds for termination.
The court found the order unsustainable and ordered the petitioner to be reinstated.
[Paras 23 and 24]
