Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25-F - Termination - Absence from duty - The petitioner-management argues that respondent No. 2's absence was voluntary and therefore he left service voluntarily according to a standing order - The petitioner's representative attended the Labour Court for two dates and then failed to attend - The reason given by the petitioner for not contesting the award was not sufficient - The standing order is not considered a law but is made for convenience of the party - The order of the Labour Court to reinstate the respondent with continuity of service and full back wages is upheld.
