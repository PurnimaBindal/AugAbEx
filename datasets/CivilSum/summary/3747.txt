In judicial review of an administrative order imposing punishment, the Court is not concerned with the decision itself but with the decision making process.
In this case, the petitioner failed to show how the order passed was illegal, arbitrary and without application of judicious mind.
The High Court in exercising its supervisory writ jurisdiction cannot act as an appellate court.
[Para 7]

When an inquiry is fair, in consonance with rules and principles of natural justice and the unauthorised absence of the delinquent is fully established, that would constitute a misconduct of a grave nature.
There was no violation of the principle of natural justice and full opportunity was granted to the delinquent to defend himself.
It is the disciplinary authority that has to award an appropriate punishment and the civil court cannot substitute its own view to that of the disciplinary authority.
As the delinquent in question belongs to the Police Services, discipline and obedience are of paramount consideration in the police force.
Termination on account of indiscipline or unauthorised absence is therefore not unjustified.
