The transfer of the appellant, who was a distinguished physicist, was ordered by the Director General despite the competent authority being the Secretary of Department.
The appellant did not comply with the transfer order, resulting in disciplinary proceedings and the termination of their services by ex parte order.
However, there was no delegation of powers for the transfer order, nor could it be implied.
As the transfer order issued by the Director General was contrary to the rules, it was considered non est in the eye of the law.
Therefore, the order of dismissal automatically falls and the appellant is reinstated.
[Paras 5-7]
