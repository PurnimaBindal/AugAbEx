According to Punjab Police Rules, specifically Rule 1221, a constable can be discharged from service even if there is an allegation of misconduct against them.
This is in line with the Full Bench decision of Sher Singh's case.
It's important to note that no opportunity of hearing is necessary.
Therefore, all lower courts were mistaken in thinking that the order is punitive in nature.
The order is non-punitive and serves as a lawful discharge during probation.
The appeal has been approved.
