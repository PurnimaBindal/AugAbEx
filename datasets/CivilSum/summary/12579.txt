Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Promotion from Grade II Coach to Grade I - The petitioner, a Grade II Coach, claimed that he should have been promoted to Grade I when he became eligible, or at least when his juniors were promoted.
However, his juniors were promoted, citing that he was not initially qualified for the post.
The petitioner, who improved his qualifications after joining the post and had no lack of efficiency or organizing ability, is entitled to parity of treatment with other Grade II Coaches in the matter of promotion.
[Paras 8, 9 & 12]
