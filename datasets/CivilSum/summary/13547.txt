Response : According to Ritu Bahri, AAG, Haryana, there is no rule that allows an employee to claim that their period of re-employment should be counted for the purpose of pension or other service benefits.
Without a rule in place, the court cannot issue a writ of mandamus to require the respondent to count the service rendered by the petitioner beyond 3061986 for pension and other post-retirement benefits.
Additionally, the petitioner was re-employed on July 1, 1986 and could not have been paid wages higher than those mentioned in the order for re-employment.
The petitioner's fixation of payment in the scale of Rs. 1200-2040 with a basic pay of Rs. 1200 could not be deemed arbitrary or capricious.
Lastly, the respondents deducted a sum of Rs. 18390 from the petitioner's gratuity without following basic principles of natural justice, which is declared illegal.
