Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2-A, 2(k), 10 and 36 - Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Section 20(2) - Reference - Unrecognised Union - A reference can be filed if the dispute falls under the category of Industrial Dispute as to retrenchment, discharge, or dismissal of an employee according to Section 2A.
There is also no restriction on an unrecognised union to champion the cause.
