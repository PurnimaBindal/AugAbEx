Haryana Provisional Veterinary Service (Class I) Rules, 1930, Rules 4, 13, 16 and 19 - Appointment/mode of appointment - The rules provide for two modes of appointment, and it is up to the State to choose which mode to use when filling positions.
This decision cannot be challenged in court based on a quota rule supplemented by instructions.
[Paras 2, 4 and 5]  B. Seniority/length of service - Seniority must be determined according to the point in time provided in the service rules.
If there are no such rules, then length of service, whether on probation or otherwise, should be considered for determining seniority.
