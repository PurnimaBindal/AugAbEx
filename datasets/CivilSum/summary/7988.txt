Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 25-F - Back wages - When an illegal and unjustified retrenchment occurs, the typical course of action is for the workman to be brought back with full back wages, unless the manager can provide compelling reasons to deviate from this rule - The workman is considered to be in a more difficult situation when it comes to hardship.
