VP Singh, Sr. Advocate with BS Mathur, Advocate, represented Respondent Nos. 2 and 3 in a case pertaining to the Delhi Civil Supplies Corporation Ltd, (Recruitment and Promotion) Rules, 1985, specifically Rule 18 that deals with seniority and deputation.
The rule states that seniority must be fixed in a particular grade as per instructions.
The term "grade" in this context refers to a class of employment, rank in the hierarchy of service, and pay scale.
Those appointed to a particular grade, either by absorption or otherwise, are entitled to seniority with reference to the date of their appointment to that rank and pay scale, regardless of whether they opted for 'A' pay scale or 'B.'
The petitioner's case was dismissed because the respondent had been drawing a higher salary all along and could not be punished for not opting for a particular pay scale.
