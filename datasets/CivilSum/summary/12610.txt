Tamilnadu Handicrafts Development Corporation Employees Service Rules, 1979, Rule (14) - Probation period/Termination - The rules state that probation period is six months but can be extended up to one and a half years.
Upon completion of the maximum probationary period, the employee's services are automatically confirmed, and no formal order of confirmation is required.
[Para 8]

Tamilnandu Handicrafts Development Corporation Employees Service Rules, 1979, Rule 2(14) - Notice or notice pay/Termination - According to the rules, the employee's services may be terminated with a notice of three months or corresponding salary in lieu of notice, which did not happen in this case.
The order of termination is in violation of the rules and can be quashed.
[Para 10]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Termination - While terminating the petitioner's services, the juniors were retained, causing the order to be bad in law.
Therefore the order has been quashed.
[Paras 9 and 10]

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Competent authority - Under the corporation's service rules, the Managing Director is the appointing authority for a Senior Sales Assistant.
The Sales Manager, who is below the rank of the appointing authority, terminated the employee's services; thus, the termination is unlawful.
