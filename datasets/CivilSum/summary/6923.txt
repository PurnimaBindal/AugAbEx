As per the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2 Item 6, a work charge Beldar was assigned the duties of LDC.
The workman claimed regularisation as LDC and equal pay for equal work.
However, it was held that since the workman never applied for or appeared in the selection for the post of LDC, recruitment to regular posts could not be made bypassing the constitutional scheme of recruitment.
As there are rules of recruitment for the post of LDC, the petitioner cannot be regularised.
The petitioner also cannot claim equal pay for equal work as Article 14 permits reasonable classification and the fact that the person has not gone through the process of recruitment may make a difference.
