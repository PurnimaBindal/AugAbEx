Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Qualification - Eligibility - Petitioner was appointed by considering JBT equivalent to OT - If a certain qualification is considered to be equivalent to another it would be applicable throughout the service - Order denying promotion on the basis of petitioner not being an OT is bad - Respondents directed to consider petitioner's promotion case for post of Sanskrit/Hindi teacher by recognizing her as qualified for which she had already appeared for an interview - If appointed, the appointment will relate back to the date when other teachers who were interviewed with petitioner were appointed.
