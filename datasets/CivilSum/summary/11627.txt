Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 24 - Promotion - Interim Relief - The Tribunal issued an interim relief ordering official respondents to consider the promotion of the applicant to the post of Chief Engineer within six weeks - However, an exparte interim order can only be passed for fourteen days under Section 24 of the Act - The mandatory order to complete the process within six weeks is not allowed - The order was set aside since it was not sustainable.
