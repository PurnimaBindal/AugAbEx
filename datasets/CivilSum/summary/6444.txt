Article 16 of the Indian Constitution discusses promotion and the grant of a senior scale.
When considering claims for promotion or senior scale, a person with a better record of service may be considered more suitable.
The efficiency bar, previous records, and qualifications should all be considered to avoid arbitrariness and illegality.
[Para 5]
Article 16 of the Indian Constitution also states that for the purpose of considering a claim for promotion, previous records of service are relevant.
This test is also applicable for the grant of a senior scale.
[Para 7]
In terms of promotion and grant of senior scale, previous records of an employee before crossing the efficiency bar can be considered.
This criteria is not similar to assessing suitability for an employee's retention in service.
