Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923, Section 23 - Compensation - Relationship of employer and employee - An issue does not need to be framed regarding the relationship of employer and employee if the employer has admitted the employment of the deceased - The case is remanded to determine the age, wages and award compensation.
[Paras 14, 15 and 16]  B. Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923, Section 2(e), 2(n) - Compensation - Order of employment - No requirement for written order of employment to be present in order to grant compensation - Clause (ii) of Section 2(n) clarifies the existence of written order of employment.
