The termination of a probationer's services is subject to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946.
If a probationer believes their dismissal was illegal, they may not approach a civil court but must instead go through forums created by the acts themselves.
A court's conclusion that a company failed to comply with the law does not automatically allow for civil court jurisdiction if there are no other authoritative pronouncements permitting such.
The trial court's finding that the termination was illegal was also overturned.
