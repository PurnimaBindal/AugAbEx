Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Quantum of punishment - Although the Court may hesitate to intervene in the discretion of the punishing authority, it cannot ignore the circumstances if the punishment is deemed excessive and disproportional to the charges.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Misconduct - An employee pursuing legal action against the actions of a competent authority cannot be considered as misconduct, and labeling it as such would be contemptuous on the authority's part.
[Para 7] C. Civil Procedure Code, Section 100 - Substantial question of law - The issue of whether punishment is disproportionate and unreasonable constitutes a substantial legal inquiry to warrant a second appeal.
