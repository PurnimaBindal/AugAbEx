Army Rules, Rule 13 Item III (V) - Army Act, 1950, Sections 20, 44 - Termination - Appointing an employee who doesn't possess requisite qualifications is against the law and that appointment can be set aside - Held.
[Para 11]
Army Rules, Rule 13 Item III (V) - Army Act, 1950, Sections 20, 44 - Termination - Authority is not required to proceed under Section 44 of the Act - Power comprised under Section 20 of the Act read with Rule 13 item III (V) is independent and not hedged by provisions relating to Court Martial, of persons subject to Army Act - Held.
