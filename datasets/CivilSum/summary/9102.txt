Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Reference - No limitation set for reference under Section 10 (1) of the Act - Article 137 Limitation Act does not apply - However, appropriate Govt. should refer dispute promptly - Govt. may reject belated reference.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section (10) (1) - Belated reference - Govt. has the right to decline a reference if it's delayed - Adjudicating authority can provide relief according to delay - Affected party can challenge the belated reference.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 (1) Reference - Petitioners served demand notice and raised an industrial dispute after a lapse of three and a half years and six years - State Govt. refused the reference on the grounds of delay - No illegality.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 (1) - Adjudicating authority cannot grant relief prior to the date of demand - State Govt. does not need to refer belated claims - Petition dismissed - Constitution of India, Article 226.
