The Indian Audit and Accounts Department (Administrative Officers) Recruitment Rules, 1964 does not specify the criteria that would make a Section Officer eligible for promotion to the post of Assistant Accounts Officer.
However, Executive Instructions have been issued that prescribe a minimum period of service as Section Officer to make him eligible for promotion as Accounts Officer.
Such instructions do not conflict with the Rules and are therefore considered valid.
[Paras 5 and 6]

The Comptroller and Auditor General of India is authorized to issue Administrative Instructions not inconsistent with the Rules for laying down criteria that would make a Section Officer eligible for promotion to the post of Assistant Accounts Officer.
[Para 7]
