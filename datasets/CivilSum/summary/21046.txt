Constitution of India, Article 136 - Termination - Full and Final settlement - An interlocutory application was filed by the appellant against his termination of service which was dismissed by the High Court.
On appeal, the Apex Court directed the respondent to pay Rs. 3,50,000/- in full and final settlement of all claims against them due to special facts and circumstances.
The order was misinterpreted by the appellant as setting aside the order of termination and reinstating him in service.
Consequently, he took proceedings for recovery of Provident Fund, Gratuity, and other retiral benefits.
A settlement was reached between the appellant and respondent.
In accordance with the settlement, the respondent paid a further Rs. 36000/- to the appellant and the appellant agreed to withdraw all proceedings relating to Provident Fund, Gratuity, etc.
The interlocutory application is now disposed of.
