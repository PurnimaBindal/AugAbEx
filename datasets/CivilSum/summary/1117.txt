Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 12 - Transfer - Challenge to transfer by way of writ petition - The petitioner is employed by J & K Bank and questions the maintainability of a writ against the bank - Whether the bank is considered an `instrumentality' of State under Article 12 - The Apex Court has a pending matter concerning the maintainability of the writ against the bank - However, a challenge to the transfer order is not sustainable.

Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Transfer of bank employee - Validity - The petitioner, an employee of a bank, was transferred from Delhi to Mumbai office - The respondents assert that the transfer is to MBD of the bank in Mumbai - There is a clear provision in the service conditions of the petitioner for his transfer - It is an admitted case that he can be posted in MBD of the bank in Delhi or Mumbai - The transfer order passed by the respondent is not in contravention of the terms of the petitioner's appointment.
