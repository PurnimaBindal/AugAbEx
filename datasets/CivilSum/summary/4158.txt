Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 10 - Apology - Contempt - Despite repeated judgments in favour of the employees, the State failed to comply with the Court's directions.
The State's case was lost up to the Supreme Court.
To remedy the delay, the newly appointed officer offered an unconditional apology.
If this apology is deemed sincere and bona fide, the Court may accept it and discharge the rule.
The Court is primarily interested in the compliance of its orders.
No punishment was given, but the petitioner was awarded Rs. 5000/- in costs to be recovered from the official at fault.
