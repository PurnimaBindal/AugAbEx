Bombay Civil Services Rules, 1959, Rule 152 - Suspension - Regularisation of suspension period - Acquittal - Appeal against acquittal pending - Appellant gave undertaking not to insist on regularisation until finality of the appeal - Requesting directions for regularisation while the appeal is pending - The prayer was rejected by the learned single judge - It was held that Rule 152 cannot be applied until the order of acquittal is final - It is not unlawful to request an undertaking not to insist on regularisation - The appellant cannot back out from his undertaking - A direction under Article 226 cannot be used in his favour.
