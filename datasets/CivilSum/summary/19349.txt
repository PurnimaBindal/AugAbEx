According to Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, if an employee falls ill at a location where there are no hospitals recognised by the Government of Rajasthan, they may seek treatment at any available hospital, which will be acknowledged as recognised by the State Government.
The State Government must reimburse the medical bills for such treatment.
The order, which denies reimbursement on the grounds that the hospital where the employee received treatment outside of the State was not recognised by the Government of Rajasthan, was rejected.
