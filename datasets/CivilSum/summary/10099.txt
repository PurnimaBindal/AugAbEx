As per Sections 2(j)(a), 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the petitioners who were appointed on daily wage basis for a specific project by the Language and Culture Department are not considered as 'workmen'.
Since their services were engaged for a non-industrial purpose, Section 25(F) is not applicable.
As a result, the termination of their services is considered legal.
