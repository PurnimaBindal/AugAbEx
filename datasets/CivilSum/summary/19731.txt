Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 - Premature retirement/Assessment of performance/Adverse remarks - Petitioner, a headmaster, faced premature retirement - His performance was always assessed as good, but downgraded due to the school's results - Petitioner was not informed of this downgrading - It is unjust to downgrade performance that was previously assessed as good or very good based on overall results of the school, which depend on other teachers' performance and students' capabilities - A head teacher should not be held accountable for poor school results and be considered useless.
