Workmens Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 4, 4A - Loss of earning capacity - Assessment of loss of earning capacity - The Commissioner rightly assessed compensation despite a report from the Medical Board declaring the respondent handicapped to the extent of 60%, as the report was not related to loss of earning capacity.
[Paras 7 and 8]

Workmens Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 4, 4A - Interest - Imposition of interest and penalty - It is not permissible to impose interest and penalty provided under section 4A while assessing compensation under the Act.
Such interest and penalty should be determined separately on further evidence.
[Para 9]
