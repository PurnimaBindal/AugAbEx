Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume II, Rule 5.18 Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo)(c) - Retirement - The petitioner, who worked as a driver, retired due to being unfit to drive heavy vehicles.
It was held that this was not a case of retrenchment as per Section 2(oo) of the Act.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo)(c) - Pensionary benefit - The Labour Court allowed for reinstatement with 50% back wages and post-retirement benefits based on the 50% wages.
It was held that the post-retirement benefits should be calculated based on the salary drawn on the date of superannuation.
[Paras 12 and 13]
