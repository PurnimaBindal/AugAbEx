Mr. YP Rao, Advocate, represented the Impleading Party Respondents No. 122-125.
The Constitution of India's Article 311 and 16, along with the Andhra Pradesh Public Health Subordinate Service Rules, 1964, were invoked to resolve the issue of Inter-se Seniority among direct recruits and promotees.
The Respondent Officers ranked below the appellants in the selection list of PSC, but were not to be appointed accordingly.
However, the State Government regularised their services as Health Inspectors in the cadre of APPHSS from the respective dates of their eligibility for promotion to that cadre on a temporary basis, by getting their names deleted from the selection list of PSC.
The Orders of retrospective regularisation of temporary promotees and temporary direct appointees as Health Inspectors along with the subsequent order made by the State Government in G.O.(Ms.)
No. 105 Health dated 2-2-1989 as to fixation of inter se seniority among them upheld.
