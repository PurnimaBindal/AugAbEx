Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Seniority - Delay and laches - The petitioner was passed over in 1975 after the final seniority test was issued - The petitioner filed a representation in 1988 and a Writ Petition in 1990 challenging the seniority and supersession, but the petition is liable to be dismissed due to the delay and laches - Settled seniority and promotions cannot be altered after such a long delay - Delay defeats equity as well.
[Para 5] B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Seniority/Promotion - The petitioner was not impleaded, and persons who were likely to be affected if the petitioner's claim were accepted were made senior and promoted - As a result of not impleading necessary parties, the petition is liable to be dismissed.
