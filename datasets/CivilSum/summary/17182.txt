Constitution of India, Articles 14, 39 and 226 - Equal pay for equal work - Petitioners hired on an ad hoc basis as Computer Programme Officer and Junior Computer Programmer at regular pay scales for six months - Extension granted with appointment on a contract basis with reduced salary - Salary cuts of Rs. 6400/- to 5000/- and Rs. 4400/- to 3200/- respectively despite serving with the same duties and responsibilities during the initial six months tenure - Such action was considered unfair, unjust, and exploitative - Petitioners entitled to receive wages equal to those earned by regular employees in the same role.
[Paras 7 and 8]
