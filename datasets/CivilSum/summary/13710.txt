As per the Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, in Sections 156 and 482, if a complaint is made to the police disclosing a cognizable offence, they are bound to register a case.
They cannot decline to do so on the grounds of an enquiry stating that there was no force in the complaint.
If the complaint discloses the commission of an offence, the Police should register the FIR and then proceed with it according to law.
They cannot avoid registering it and claim to have already enquired into the matter and found the allegations false.
