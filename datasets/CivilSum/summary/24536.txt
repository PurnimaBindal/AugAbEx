The policy for compassionate appointments in banks does not guarantee appointment as a right for applicants.
Instead, it is a compassionate measure that the competent authority can use to determine if the deceased’s family is in an unrecoverable financial crisis.
The authority must review the family’s terminal benefits, family pension, and other financial help, as well as its own resources of income before making the appointment decision.
As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, appointments must adhere to recruitment rules and meet the provisions of Article 14.
Appointments made outside the criteria of the policy cannot be used as a precedent for similar treatment by other applicants.
The court will not continue or support such injustice.
[Paras 24 to 30]
