Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rajasthan (Public Works Department) Work-charge Employees Service Rules, 1964 - Grant of semi- permanent status - No dispute exists regarding the right of workmen to be considered for grant of semi permanent status - While employer can deny or defer benefits as per Rule, once eligibility is acquired, benefits cannot be denied from the date of completion of two years' services - The Labour Court had not acted illegally in holding that the workmen were entitled to semi permanent status from the date of completion of two years' services from their initial appointments.
