Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Discrimination - Eligibility conditions - Time-bound promotions - The petitioner was denied first and second time-bound promotions solely because he had not passed Accounts Examination.
However, there was nothing adverse in his service record; meanwhile, the other three persons were granted both time-bound promotions without having passed the Accounts Examination themselves.
Therefore, the action of the authority was deemed to be arbitrary and discriminatory.
As a result, the petitioner was directed to also receive his time-bound promotions.
[Paras 6 and 7]
