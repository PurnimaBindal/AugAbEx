As per the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(j) and 10A, it was determined through the precedent set by a seven Judge Bench in Bangalore Water Supply and Severage Board vs. A Rajappa and Others, (1978)2 SCC 213, that the Telecom Department of Union of India is an 'industry' as it performs commercial activities and not sovereign functions of the State.
