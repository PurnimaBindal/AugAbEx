Legal Services Authority Act, 1987, Section 22(B) and (C), Chapter VI-A - Permanent Lok Adalat - Award by adjudication of dispute - Validity - Employee death case - Claim for group insurance policy - FIR lodged by brother of deceased claiming suicide - Police investigation reported as accident - Argument that award can be made only on settlement - Held, Permanent Lok Adalat has jurisdiction under sub-section (8) of Section 22-C when parties fail to agree and dispute does not relate to offence - Hence Permanent Lok Adalat was competent to make the award in this case.
