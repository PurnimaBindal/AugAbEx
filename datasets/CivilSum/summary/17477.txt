The Allotment Rules for Government Residences in Chandigarh state that autonomous bodies and public undertakings are not considered eligible offices, but employees who retain their lien on a post in an eligible office are entitled to occupy a government residence.
However, if an employee is permanently absorbed in an autonomous body or public undertaking, their occupation of the government residence becomes unauthorized and they must vacate the residence within the prescribed period.
The Rules do not require the competent authority to provide any opportunity for hearing before cancelling the allotment, and the occupant is liable to pay penal rent for the cancelled period.
