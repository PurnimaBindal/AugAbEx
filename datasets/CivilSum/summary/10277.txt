Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pay Parity - Post of Librarian - The petitioner was unable to present any evidence demonstrating that librarians are categorized as part of the teaching cadre.
As a matter of fact, it is essential to establish this fact.
Furthermore, the petitioner agreed to the appointment as a librarian with full knowledge that it carried a different pay scale than that of a teacher and that normal promotional opportunities may not be available.
Additionally, the petitioner's appointment does not suggest that the position of librarian was required to teach students library science as a subject.
Therefore, the court held that if a position is not part of the cadre, it cannot be included as such based on presumptions and conjectures of attendant circumstances.
As a result, the petitioner is not entitled to any relief.
[Paras 4, 5, 6 and 9]
