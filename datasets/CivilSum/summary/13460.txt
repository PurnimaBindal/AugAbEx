The Delhi Transport Corporation Pension Scheme specifies that retired individuals who have already received payment of PF and Gratuity are obligated to refund the employer's contribution to PF and excess amount of gratuity.
The reason being, as they have already used the employers' contribution, they cannot refuse to return it with interest.
However, there is no obligation to pay interest on the amount of gratuity refunded.
