Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation of Services - Haryana Govt.
Instructions dated 27.5.1993 - Regularisation of daily wage workers who completed 5 years of service on or before 3131995 - A deeming fiction of one year is given for 240 days worked in a calendar year - Interruptions on account of strikes, sickness, authorised leave, lock-out, cessation of work, and absence of workman due to accident should be ignored for calculating 240 days in a calendar year - The petitioner worked for more than 240 days in every year for more than 5 years without any break for more than 30 days at any time - The petitioner's denial of regularization due to only working for 4 years and 10 months is illegal and against the instructions - The petitioner's case has to be re-evaluated for regularization.
[Paras 8 to 10]
