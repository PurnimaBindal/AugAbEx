Constitution of India, Article 16 - Regularisation - The petitioner, identified as the Matron of the School, was appointed temporarily back in the year 1981 in disregard of recruitment rules.
Although the petitioner has no case for regularisation, leniency can be taken since there have been changes and such appointments cannot be ignored.
Thus, the District Inspector of Schools is instructed to consider the petitioner's appointment and provide a reasoned order.
