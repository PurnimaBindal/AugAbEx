According to Section 7 of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 and Section 22 of the Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provisions) Act, 1985, occupying a quarter after the service has been discontinued cannot be the basis for withholding the payment of gratuity.
The amount of gratuity that has been deposited in the court must be paid to the employee and the contention of the employer that the payment should be withheld since the case is pending with BIFR is not valid.
