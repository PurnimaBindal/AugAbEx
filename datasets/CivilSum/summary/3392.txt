Constitution of India, Articles 14/16 - Selection/Advertisement/Appointment - A college advertised a post for Principal with specific qualifications, but none of the applicants met the qualifications.
The Vice Chancellor rejected the name of Respondent No. 4, but they were still selected and appointed by changing the qualifications.
The court held that the qualifications in the advertisement could not be changed without readvertising the post, and since the qualifications were changed with the intention of favoring Respondent No. 4, the entire process was quashed.
The officer responsible for the action was ordered to pay costs of Rs. 5000/-.
(1972 SLR 706 : AIR 1980 SC 2141 and 1992(1) SCT 700 relied upon) [Paras 13, 14 and 15]
