Jammu and Kashmir Police Manual Volume I, Rules 187, 359 - Constable under trainee on probation - Discharge from service - Unauthorised absence grounds - Rule 359, governing departmental enquiries, is not applicable to probationers - The petitioner, who had been disciplined for frequent absences, was rightly discharged under Rule 187 - The competent authority acted fairly and appropriately, even when the principles of natural justice were not strictly followed - The petitioner's conduct during his probationary period suggests that he would not make a good police officer.
