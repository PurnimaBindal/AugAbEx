Punjab Police Rules, Rule 12.6 - Selection - Penal of selected candidates - Selection to the post of Inspectors of Police in Haryana - Requisition for 8 posts only - Board recommending names of 19 persons for appointment - Selection of more than required candidates was not legally sustainable.
[Para 10]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 12.6 - Selection - Selection and appointment to the post of Inspector of Police in Haryana - The Board cannot on its own relax the standards of physical fitness mentioned in the advertisement - Physical test could be prescribed to supplement the rules to make their applicability more uniform - Selection of persons who failed to qualify in all the five items quashed.
[Paras 11 and 12]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 12.6 - Selection - Interview - Selection and appointment to the post of Inspector of Police in Haryana - Misbehaviour by the candidate during interview - Board can take note of the misbehaviour and reject the candidate for appointment - Mere giving zero marks would amount to recommend his appointment on the basis of written test even if he is not found suitable in interview.
