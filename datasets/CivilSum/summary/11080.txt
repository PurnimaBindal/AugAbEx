Petitioner Tahmina Punvani advocates for the Constitution of India, namely Articles 14 and 16, in regards to a pay scale and promotion.
Laboratory Assistants in the Office of Directorate of Geology and Mining lack promotional avenues whilst their counterparts in Geological Survey of India (GSI) have two further posts.
Additionally, petitioners have the same nature of duties and educational qualifications as those in the GSI There is no justification for the disparity in pay scale and lack of promotional avenues.
The petitioner seeks directions to create promotional avenues and remove the disparity.
[Paras 1, 4, 9 and 18]
