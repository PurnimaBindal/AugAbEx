Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Cantonment Fund Servants Rules, 1937, Clause 5(b)(i) - Appointment/Regularisation - The termination of services of the petitioner, who was appointed against a temporary post for a fixed period through Employment Exchange after crossing the age limit prescribed in the Rules, and not regularizing their services by the authorities cannot be considered arbitrary as the competent authority refused to grant relaxation in the age limit - No relief can be provided.
