Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservation - Selection - Ex-serviceman - Discharge certificate - An ex-serviceman cannot be denied appointment as a general candidate once he has complied with the condition of producing his release/discharge certificate at the time of examination - In a case where the Commission recommended a candidate with inferior marks and who had not been released from the army, the action was arbitrary and violated Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - The Commission was directed to recommend the petitioner for appointment based on his merit.
[Paras 23(5) to 23(7) & 24]
