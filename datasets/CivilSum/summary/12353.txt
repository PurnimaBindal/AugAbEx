Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 18, Sch.
2 Item 6 - Bipartite settlement - Invocation of - The petitioner, an employee of a bank, was absent without permission or leave and was issued a notice by the Branch Manager.
When the petitioner did not respond, the clause of the bipartite settlement was invoked and the petitioner was declared to have voluntarily abandoned the job.
The petitioner was not given an opportunity to explain, which goes against the doctrine of fair play in action and the principles of natural justice.
