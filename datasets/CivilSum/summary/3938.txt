Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission - Eligibility - Course in D Pharmacy - The eligibility for admission to the course is based on passing the qualifying examination, with candidates who have taken the exam and are waiting for the result allowed to apply but required to provide the result at the time of joining admission/interview - Fulfilment of eligibility condition on the last day is mandatory, and the date of possessing eligibility criteria (passing the qualifying examination) cannot be the date of passing the supplementary examination - A candidate can only be declared to have passed an examination from the date the result is declared unless otherwise provided by any Act or Rules - The Division Bench of Punjab and Haryana High Court's decision in Arvind Kataria's case was deemed incorrect.
