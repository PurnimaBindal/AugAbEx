Compassionate appointment is not considered a right under the Constitution of India's Article 16 and 14.
These appointments can only be made if there are Rules/Regulations/Instructions in that respect.
The Board of Directors of Respondent-Corporation passed a resolution not to make compassionate appointments due to the precarious financial position of the corporation, until the policy was finalised at the Govt. level and implemented.
After an employee's death in harness, the petitioner requested a compassionate appointment but was rejected due to the resolution.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to a writ of mandamus.
