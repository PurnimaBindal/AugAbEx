Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - Refusing admission to part-time BTech course on the basis of the clause in prospectus which stipulates that only the date of marklist of diploma examination will be considered as the acquisition date of diploma, and experience will be counted accordingly - The clause in prospectus is entirely arbitrary and unlawful - Relevant date is the date of publication of result - Directive to respondent to consider petitioner's admission.
