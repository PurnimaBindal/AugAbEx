Tamil Nadu State and Subordinate Services Rules, General Rule 22 - Special Rules for the Tamil Nadu Revenue Subordinate Service, Rule 6 - Reservation in the matter of appointment on the post of Deputy Tahsildar - The retrospective operation given to the amendment introduced in Rule 6 of Special Rules by G.O.Ms.
No. 660 dated April 19, 1988 is invalid.
Before the impugned amendment, Rule 6 of the Special Rules made the provisions of Rule 22 of General Rules regarding reservation applicable to appointments to the category of Deputy Tahsildar.
The validity of the applicability of the provisions of Rule 22 of the General Rules is analyzed.
[Paras 11, 12 and 13]

Promotion - Reservations - Existing Rules providing for reservation in the matter of promotion can be continued for a period of five years - The appointments made on the post of Deputy Tahsildar cannot be questioned on the basis of the law laid down in Indira Sawhney's Case that the principle of reservation cannot be applied at the stage of promotion.
It is not necessary to go into the question whether appointment to the post of Deputy Tahsildar by transfer from Tamil Nadu Ministerial Subordinate Service amounts to promotion.
[Para 10]

Interpretation of Statutes - It is important to draw a distinction between a mere reference or citation of a statute into another and the incorporation of a particular provision of a statute.
While in the former case, any change in the referred statute by way of modification, repeal, or re-enactment will have an impact on the statute in which it is referred, but in the latter case, any change in the incorporated statute by way of amendment or repeal does not affect the incorporating statute.
