The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 39 & 226 - Petitioners are entitled to equal pay for equal work - They perform similar duties, functions and responsibilities as their regular cadre counterparts - They have been working equally for 7 to 11 years - State's argument that there are no sanctioned posts is irrelevant - Holding that petitioners are entitled to receive minimum pay scale with Dearness Allowance only - Effective from writ petition filing date.
