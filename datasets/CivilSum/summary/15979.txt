Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Pension - Petitioner had served under the State Govt. and then joined PGI, where he held different positions at different intervals until retiring on superannuation - PGI provided him with pension for service from April 1, 1963 to Jan 31, 1980 - The petitioner argued that he was entitled to receive pension even for the period from April 1950 to March 31, 1963, as there was no indication that his post did not qualify for pension - The court agreed, ruling that there was no rule stating that the post did not carry pension benefits and therefore the petitioner was entitled to arrears of pension from February 1, 1980, along with interest at a rate of 12% from the date of accrual until the payment was made.
