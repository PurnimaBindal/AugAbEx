The petitioners who were appointed as Drivers contractually have filed a grievance maintaining that their priority right to be re-employed was ignored by respondent No. 3 who appointed private respondents.
However, the case is not suitable for exercising jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution of India as there exists an equally efficient remedy under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
This was highlighted under the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 and the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(k), 10 and 25(H).
