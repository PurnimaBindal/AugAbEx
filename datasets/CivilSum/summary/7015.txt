Army Act, 1950, Section 22 - Retiral dues - Refusal of - Reason being employee was declared deserter - Decided, petitioner's husband did not return to duty but there was no intention of leaving the service permanently - Additionally, as nobody has any information about his whereabouts, absenteeism alone cannot prove him a deserter - Respondents failed to make plausible efforts in finding him - Petition granted.
