Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Scheduled Castes Land Development and Finance Corporation General Regulations, 1971, Regulation 4(d) - Reversion/Natural justice - Any order of reversion cannot be passed without providing an opportunity for hearing - All administrative orders with civil impact must comply with the principles of natural justice - Before revising seniority to an employee's disadvantage, a hearing must be given.
