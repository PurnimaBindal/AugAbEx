According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, petitioners who have worked as casual workers with periodic breaks for the past 22/25 years and have continuously fulfilled the duties of Group-D staff must receive wages at the same rate as regular Group-D employees.
Whether to grant the difference in wages from a specific date is left to the discretion of the court based on the facts and circumstances of each case.
Since the petitioners did not approach the court promptly, no satisfactory explanation was given.
As a result, the respondent has been instructed to pay wages at the minimum level of salary and allowances as applicable to the regular establishment's Group-D category on the principle of equal pay for equal work from the date of filing the writ petition.
