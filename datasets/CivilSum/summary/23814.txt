The Rajasthan Higher Judicial Services Rules, 1969, Rule 9(2) and the Constitution of India Articles 14 and 16 state that promotion to Rajasthan Higher Judicial Services should be based on availability of posts in the promotional quota.
The appellant in this case retired before promotional posts became available, even though the High Court had passed a resolution not to make further promotions from RJS until recruitment from the bar to RHJS was made.
The delay in recruitment caused imbalance in the service and it is not advisable to keep any posts in the judicial diary vacant for days.
Ad hoc promotion to officers in subordinate services is possible without their right to claim seniority over direct recruits.
The appellant's appeal was dismissed since no officer junior to him was promoted prior to his retirement on 31596.
