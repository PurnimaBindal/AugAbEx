Railway Protection Force Rules, 1959, Rule 44(9) - Disciplinary proceedings - Dismissal - The disciplinary proceedings against Rakshak resulted in dismissal, but the finding of the Enquiry Officer was based on surmises and conjectures.
The second show cause notice was deemed unacceptable as it implied that the finding of the disciplinary authority was final, rather than tentative.
Failure to provide the report of the Enquiry Officer violated the principles of natural justice.
Additionally, both the disciplinary authority and the appellate authority did not apply their minds properly.
Therefore, the order of dismissal needs to be set aside, and the appellant should receive full salary from the date of dismissal till reinstatement.
