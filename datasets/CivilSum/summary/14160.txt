The West Bengal School Commission Act, 1997, Article 226 of the Constitution of India, and Rule 28 of the Management of Recognised Non-Government Institutions (Aided and Unaided) Rules, 1969, explain the appointment of an Assistant Teacher and the use of a subject interview.
The District Inspector of School determined that the plea of the Managing Committee and the reasoning to have teachers appointed on the recommendation of the commission itself was not tenable.
The decision was ultimately resolved after being questioned before the writ court and Division Bench.
[Para 9]  B The process of preparing a three-man panel for the appointment of an Assistant Teacher, as outlined in Rule 28 of the Management of Recognised Non-Government Institutions (Aided and Unaided) Rules, 1969, should be given a liberal construction in light of the petitioner being the only suitable candidate found.
Preparation of such a panel is nearly impossible in such circumstances, and it is directed that the District Inspector of School approve a one-man panel as long as it is otherwise lawful.
