Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 21 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 12(3) - Compassionate Appointment - Applicants for compassionate appointments must apply within one year of the death of an employee - A wife of a deceased employee made a late application - A settlement between the Management and trade unions was entered into and had statutory force and was binding on the defendants - Defendants that made applications beyond the stipulated date were not entitled to compassionate appointment - Directions were given to consider the wife's case for a suitable post if she makes a further application as she underwent a medical fitness test and vocational training in mines.
