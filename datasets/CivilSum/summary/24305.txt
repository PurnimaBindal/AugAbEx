Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Freedom fighters pension and awards - Guidelines for proving status as a freedom fighter - Supporting evidence needed to make such a claim - Decisions of the Freedom Fighters Committee - Limitations on the writ Court's power to override committee decisions - If the claimant lacks required evidence and the Committee finds no entitlement, the High Court cannot override the Committee's decision and act as an appellate authority - Instead, the High Court may only remit the matter to the government for further consideration.
