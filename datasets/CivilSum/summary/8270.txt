Article 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India - Regularisation of Services - If Project employees of a Scientific research project are appointed on ad hoc basis till completion of project and no posts remain on completion of project, no order for regularisation can be made - Employees were not promised continuation of project or regularisation - No direction for regularisation can be issued.
[Paras 12 and 17] B Article 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India - Project employees - If some employees of a project are absorbed in other departments, efforts must be made to absorb others as well.
