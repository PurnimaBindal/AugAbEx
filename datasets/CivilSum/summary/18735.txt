Jammu and Kashmir Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rules 30 and 33 - Punishment of reduction to lower post is a major punishment.
The petitioner in this case has denied charges of dereliction of duty.
It is mandatory for disciplinary authority to either appoint an inquiry officer or conduct the inquiry themselves.
Imposing punishment without holding an inquiry is illegal since there is no evidence to support it.
The order of demotion is quashed and petitioners are to be restored to their original positions with all consequential benefits.
