Constitution of India, Article 226 - Discharge - Post of Research Associates - Petitioners terminated because project was closed - Not reappointed when Phase-II began - Job advertisement for same post not appropriate - Research Associates should be allowed to continue until the end of the project, unless there are issues of indiscipline, inefficiency, or non-suitability, and their services should be co-terminus with the project's lifespan - Job advertisement for Research Associate positions invalidated - Respondent instructed to allow petitioner to continue as Research Associates on Phase II of the project.
