Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F, Schedule 2 Item 6 - Award - Reinstatement with back wages - Termination found to be in violation of Section 25F and Service Rules - Workman provided affidavit stating lack of gainful employment - Employer failed to refute affidavit's contents - Therefore, the Labour Court rightfully awarded full back wages with interest at a rate of 9% per annum from the date the workman was not allowed to resume duties.
