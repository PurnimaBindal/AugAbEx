The plaintiff in a suit requested reinstatement with full back wages, arguing that his termination was illegal, void, and not binding on him.
However, it was determined that since he was an employee of a company constituted under the Companies Act, he could not seek declaration and demand reinstatement.
Instead, he could only claim back wages if the termination was unlawful.
As the termination of the plaintiff's services was in accordance with the terms and conditions governing his appointment, it was found to be lawful.
