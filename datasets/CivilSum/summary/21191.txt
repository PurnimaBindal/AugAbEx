The appellants worked in the Census Organisation from 1991 to 1994, with a break, and in the municipality from 1995 to 1997.
Although the job in the municipality was purely temporary and did not confer any rights on the appointees, the appellants were denied absorption as "surplus employee."
They approached the High Court for relief, seeking relaxation of age to the extent of service rendered so they could compete with others in the matter of selection and appointment.
The Punjab Government defined "surplus employees" as regular employees who faced termination due to the abolition of an organization, but the order of termination dated 1041997 termed appellants as "surplus employees" in view of the instructions of the Central Government, etc.
In view of the services rendered, the High Court found the appellants to be surplus employees and directed the Government of Punjab to allow them to compete with others by relaxing the age to the extent of services rendered.
