Constitution of India, Articles 29, 30 and 51A - Linguistic minority Private aided educational institutions - Local regional language as a compulsory subject - State regulatory power - A State is permitted to make the teaching of its regional language compulsory - Linguistic minority institutes are not affected by such a policy - They have the freedom to teach any other two languages of their choice - The negative right to exclude the learning of regional language cannot be derived from Articles 29 and 30 - Article 29(2) applies to both majority and minority groups.
