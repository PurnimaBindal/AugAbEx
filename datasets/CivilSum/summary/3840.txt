Under Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act 1947, the rejection of a claim laid after 13 years is allowed.
However, if the delay in laying the claim is properly explained and there is no prescribed time for the reference of a dispute, the matter needs fresh consideration by the government.
In this case, the petitioner had been making representations to the respondents who have absorbed similarly situated individuals, and there is nothing on record to show a denial of the fact of filing representation.
The explanation provided by the petitioner has not been considered, thus the petition is allowed accordingly.
