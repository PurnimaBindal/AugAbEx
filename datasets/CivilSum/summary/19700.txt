Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion - Eligibility - Promotion from Reporter to Supdt.
Grade I - Petitioners agreed to surrender their promotion right as Superintendent if Reporter posts are equalled to Private Secretary posts - Petitioners granted pay scale of Private Secretary and gazetted rank that goes with it - Petitioners accepting pay scales equal to Private Secretary or Superintendent Grade I does not mean they gave up their right to be considered for promotion to Superintendent Grade I [Para 6]
