Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Voluntary retirement - There's a difference between Voluntary retirement under the Conduct, Discipline and Appeal Rules and that under the special scheme - Voluntary retirement is an option available at the age of 50 years under the Conduct Rules, and at the age of 45 years under the Scheme - For those retiring under the Conduct Rules, they are eligible for benefits under the office order of the Company - However, those retiring under the Special Scheme aren't entitled to the benefits of revised pay scales - The High Court's order was reversed.
