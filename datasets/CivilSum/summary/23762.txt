Himachal Pradesh University Act, 1973, Sections 2(15) and 11 - Appointment of Teachers - Selection - Those appointed as Research Associates and Evaluators will be considered as teachers - Ordinance 35.11(b) provides for the advertisement of minimum eligibility qualifications - Appointment of persons in violation of statutory provisions affects the discipline and quality of education - Appointments by way of regularisation from lower posts are illegal, but may be protected in special circumstances.
