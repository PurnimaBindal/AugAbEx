Punjab Civil Services Rules (Volume I), Rule 2.2.
(b) (1) - Disciplinary proceedings after retirement - Disciplinary proceedings initiated before retirement shall be continued in accordance with applicable laws and rules; the retiree will be held responsible for the outcome of proceedings and any loss caused by them during their employment can be recovered from their salary arrears or any other dues; the recovery of salary arrears cannot be deemed ineffective as the employee had retired; if the recovery of salary is a minor penalty, it cannot be considered a major penalty due to the retirement of the employee during the pendency of the proceedings.
