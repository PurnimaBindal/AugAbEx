Rule 16 of Rajasthan Civil Services (Commutation of Pension) Rules, 1981, mandates medical examination before commutation of pension, and without it, payment cannot be made.
It has been established that this rule does not violate any provision of the Constitution of India.

In the order of compulsory retirement under Rule 172A of Rajasthan Services Rules, 1951, it was stated that payment towards earned leave would not be deducted from pension or gratuity.
Therefore, the order refusing payment for earned leave is not valid.
[Paras 13, 14 and 15] C. Recovery order passed under Rule 26A of Rajasthan Services Rules, 1951, for wrongly-fixated salary is not valid, as any benefits received without fault cannot be recovered.
[Paras 25 and 26] D. The recovery order was passed without fulfilling the principles of natural justice, Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, for the wrongly-fixated salary, and thus it is not valid.
