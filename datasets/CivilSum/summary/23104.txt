The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selections and promotions follow the rules and regulations under competent authorities - Courts are not allowed to create their own evaluation method - The court can intervene and give necessary direction when an officer is not selected or promoted contrary to law - Under Article 226 of the Constitution, if an officer is denied selection for any reason, the court has the right to interfere.
