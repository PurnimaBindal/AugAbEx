According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, as per Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part-I, while on deputation, an employee is entitled to be considered for promotions in his parent department along with other eligible persons, and he retains both his post and lien in the parent department while working elsewhere.
The employee should be granted any benefit that is provided to someone junior to him in his parent department.
If the employee is denied this benefit during his deputation, then it would be a violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
