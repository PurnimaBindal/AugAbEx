Recruitment - Vacancies reserved for unfilled posts of Ex-servicemen - Criteria for selection - The Selection Board should not rely solely on the certificate issued by the Sainik Board in determining if candidates are dependents of ex-servicemen and satisfy all qualifications, age, and other criteria - The certificate is only prima facie evidence - The Board has a duty to examine and call upon the candidates to prove that they are dependents of ex-servicemen and fulfill all requirements prescribed in the guidelines - Only then can the Board consider their cases against the unfilled posts reserved for ex-servicemen.
[Paras 5 and 6]
