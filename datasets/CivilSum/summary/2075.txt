Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Termination of Service - The language of discharge simpliciter - Labour Court can examine a discharge order's substance to confirm dismissal as a measure of punishment - The Labour Court has the power to quash an order passed without holding an inquiry or natural justice principles.
[Paras 4 and 6]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F, 25FF and 2(00) Retrenchment/Termination/Simpliciter discharge - An order of simpliciter discharge from service without complying with Section 25F is illegal - A workman terminated in such a way is entitled to reinstatement with full back wages.
[Para 9]
Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 25F, and 2(00) - Retrenchment - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pleadings - Violation of section 25F not taken before the Labour Court Order of termination otherwise found to be illegal - The workman can raise this plea in writ jurisdiction.
[Para 9]
Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10, 11 and 11-B - Relief - Justification of termination - Evidence can justify an order discharging a workman from service - The court has the power to grant compensation in lieu of reinstatement or refuse reinstatement if it goes against the management's interests.
