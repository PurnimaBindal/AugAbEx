CBI (Legal Advisors and Prosecutors) Recruitment Rules, 2002, Rule 1, in accordance with Constitution of India, Articles 309, 233(2) states that Public Prosecutors in CBI are not eligible for appointment as District and Sessions Judge due to their civil post under Union of India and inability to meet the criteria of practicing advocate under Section 2(1)(S) of Advocates Act, 1961.
