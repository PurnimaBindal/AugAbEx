When recording an Annual Confidential Report, the reporting officer's subjective satisfaction is taken into account, but they do not have the absolute right to negatively impact an officer's career.
[Para 7]
If a reporting officer has no basis for an entry claiming an officer's integrity is doubtful, it cannot be sustained.
[Para 7]
If an Annual Confidential Report is not recorded in accordance with rules, civil courts have the jurisdiction to take on the case.
