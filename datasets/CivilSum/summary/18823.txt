Appointing a candidate as a driver in APSRTC after empanelling him does not guarantee his appointment, as selection by the Selection Committee does not confer any right on him for the job.
The corporation has the right to investigate the authenticity of the driving license presented by the candidate.
The respondent correctly rejected the appointment after receiving a report from the Licensing Authority stating that no driving license was issued.
