Orissa Administrative Service Class-II Test (Appointment of Officers Validation) Amendment Act, 1992, Section 2 - Validity of Section 2 - The 1959 recruitment rules were not expressly or impliedly repealed by the Orissa Govt. resolution of December 1973 or Section 2 of the 1992 Amendment Act, both of which refer to the relevant rules.
[Para 18]  B. Orissa Administrative Service Recruitment Rules 1959, Rule 4 - Orissa Govt. resolution of December 1973 - The 1973 resolution invalidated only sub-clause (b) of Rule 4 of the 1959 rules, which ceased to have any application.
[Paras 20, 21]  C. Orissa Administrative Service Recruitment Rules, 1959, Rule 4(2) - "Year of allotment" - Even though direct recruitment may be the only source, the concept of year of allotment remains applicable in a more limited manner.
Seniority is therefore determined by allotment year.
[Paras 22, 23, 24 & 26]  D. Orissa Administrative Service Recruitment Rules 1959, Rules 2 & 4(2) - Merger of cadres - Seniority - Direct appointees are placed below original members by upholding the principle of year of allotment.
[Paras 27-29 & 37]  E. Constitution of India, Article 14 - Appointment - Advertisement - The selection and recruitment made pursuant to an advertisement will not be nullified by ambiguity in the post's nomenclature.
[Paras 33, 34, 35]  F. Constitution of India, Articles 32 & 226 - Judicial review of statutes - Hardship alone cannot be grounds for declaring valid legislation ultra vires unless it is held to suffer from the vice of discrimination or unreasonableness.
[Para 43]  G Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Seniority - Seniority is not a fundamental right but a civil right.
