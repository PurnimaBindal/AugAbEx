Constitution of India, Article 14 - Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, Sections 46, 51, 62 - Employees Insurance General Regulations, Regulation 76(b) - Commutation of benefit - Validity - Accident - Workman lost his left eye - ESI Corporation assessed liability at Rs. 263 per day - Payment of lumpsum against commuting benefit requested - Regulation 76(b) challenged - Provision of commuation of benefit available in case amount of benefit did not exceed Rs. 150 per day - Worker argued such provision was arbitrary - If benefit exceeds Rs. 150 per day or computation benefit did not exceed Rs. 10,000/-, Regulation 76(b) permits commutation - Regulation 76(b) is reasonable, legal and not arbitrary as it was intended to prevent periodic benefit payments for meagre amounts.
