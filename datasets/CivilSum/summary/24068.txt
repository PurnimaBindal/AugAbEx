Aircraft Rules, 1937, Rule 134(3) - Conditions of service - An employee can be required, at the time of employment, to agree to certain conditions and obligations, such as not being employed by any other organization or institution.
This does not violate Article 19(1)(g) or any provisions of the Contract Act, and the rule is therefore valid.
[Paras 15 and 17] B Aircraft Rules, 1937, Rule 134(3) - DGCA circular No. AIR/3/93 dated 25.2.1993 - Validity of the circular - Permits issued under Rule 134 are subject to the conditions imposed therein, including the additional conditions that may be imposed by the Govt. or DGCA from time to time.
The circular was issued with the necessary power and authority under the Air Craft Act or the Rules.
