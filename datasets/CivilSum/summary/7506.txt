Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary proceedings - Punishment of three increments stoppage with cumulative effect - The argument that punishment of the Petitioner is not sustainable because he was acquitted in a criminal case based on the same facts and where the same witnesses deposed is dismissed.
The departmental inquiry was not affected by any illegality or irregularity, and both departmental inquiry and criminal trial function in different fields.
The acquittal in the criminal case cannot negate the conclusions of the departmental inquiry.
