Article 226 of the Constitution of India deals with writs, specifically back wages.
An Industrial Tribunal awarded reinstatement and back wages, but it was held that Section 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act was not complied with.
Reinstatement was still upheld, but back wages for the workman were not sustainable.
Any payment received by the workman during the proceedings in the form of a subsistence allowance cannot be adjusted, and the petitioner is directed to report for duty.
[Paras 3, 5 and 6]
