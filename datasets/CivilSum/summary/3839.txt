Constitution of India, Article 309 - UP Public Service Commission Combined State/Upper Subordinate Services Examination Rules, 1994, Rule 2 - Select List - Petitioners demand their names to be forwarded to State for filling unfilled vacancies - The waiting list declared by Clause 5 of GO is valid for 1 year and can be extended upon request from department - Only 3 vacancies remain unfilled, hence contention that waiting list should continue is rejected - Waiting list is deemed exhausted after 1 year without any further intimation from department - Petition dismissed accordingly.
