The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, specifically Sections 2(oo) and 25-F, addresses retrenchment.
In a case concerning casual workers hired by a cooperative bank for a limited period and paid daily wages, it was found that the workers did not go through a selection process and were not given appointment orders.
When the employer chose not to renew their contract, it was determined that this did not constitute retrenchment under the jurisdiction of the labor court.
