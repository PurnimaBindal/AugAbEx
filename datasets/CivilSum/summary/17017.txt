According to Army Pension Regulations, Regulations 173(A) and 179 and Army Service Pension Rules, Annexure II, Para 7, if a soldier is treated for a disease during service at the command hospital and is invalidated on medical grounds due to a "Chronic anxiety state" that is attributable or aggravated by army service, the onus is on the Army authorities to prove that the disease was not caused by the service.
If there was no note of any indication of the disease when the soldier entered the army service, it cannot be considered as being constitutional in nature.
Therefore, the authorities must release the soldier his disability pension.
