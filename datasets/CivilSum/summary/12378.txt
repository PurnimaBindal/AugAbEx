Constitution of India, Article 226 - Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993, Sections 2(d), 12(a)(i)(ii), 14(1), (2), 15, 16 and 17 - The Act empowers the Human Rights Commission to call for personal affidavits of Police Officers against whom no violation of human rights has been alleged, to take cognizance of complaints alleging violation of human rights - Additionally, it grants the Commission powers to summon any person required to complete its enquiry and carry out the purpose of the Act - The Commission has the right to take cognizance on complaints and can call for personal affidavits of Police Officers - No sweeping direction can be given to cripple the functioning of the Commission under Art. 226 of the Constitution.
