In accordance with the Contact Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 10, regularisation of contract labourers is the responsibility of the appropriate government, not the court.
When a contractor is involved, there is no employer-employee relationship between the parties, so a court cannot order the regularisation of contract labourers.
Only the appropriate government has the authority to abolish contract labour through a notification under Section 10(1) of the Act.
[Paras 6 and 7]  B The Managing Director of a company cannot be considered a "State" in relation to the regularisation of contract labourers, and as neither the contractor nor the principal employer were named respondents, the writ petition is invalid.
