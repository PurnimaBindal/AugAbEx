Constitution of India Articles 12, 14 and 226 - Haryana State Agro Industries Regulations of Delegation of Powers, Clause 3-Articles of Association of Haryana State Agro Industries, Clauses 119 and 120 - Competent authority/Appointing and punishing authority - Jurisdiction - The dismissal of Petitioner Accounts Officer, who was appointed by the Board of Directors and is in the pay scale of Rs. 500 - 1000, was done without authority by the Managing Director.
The Managing Director did not have the power to issue a charge sheet or show cause notice for major punishment, as he was not specifically authorized to exercise the power of dismissal for officers with a pay scale of more than Rs. 250-750.
