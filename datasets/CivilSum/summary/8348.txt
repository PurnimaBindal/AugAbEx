Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, Section 13-A - Grant of Interim Relief - The power of the Labor Court is restricted to determining the application or interpretation of standing orders referred to it.
The Labor Court is not authorized to grant relief in enforcing the rights and responsibilities created by standing orders in cases of illegal lockouts and work suspensions.
During the pendency of proceedings, the Labor Court was rightly denied interim relief.
