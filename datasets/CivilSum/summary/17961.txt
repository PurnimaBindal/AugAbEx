Constitution of India, Article 16 - Selection Grade - Provincialised - Cadre and State Cadre - JBT teacher on Provincialised Cadre - On his own request, the petitioner transferred from one district to another district.
However, simply because of a transfer, the petitioner cannot be taken to the State Cadre, and will receive the selection grade based on seniority in the Provincialised Cadre.
