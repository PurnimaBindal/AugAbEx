According to the Constitution of India, in Articles 16 and 226, the Punjab Government has derecognized the ETT qualification passed from Chandigarh for the appointment of ETT teachers under the Sarv Siksha Abhiyan.
Despite petitioners claiming that ETT qualification from UT Chandigarh had been recognized by Punjab Govt., the advertisement had only been restricted to those who had passed ETT from Punjab.
However, a notification from Punjab Government issued on December 19th, 2005, shows that candidates who have passed Punjabi as one of the languages are eligible for appointment.
It has been determined that candidates who have passed the ETT diploma course from UT Chandigarh will be treated equally to those who obtained the ETT course from Punjab if they have passed Punjabi as one of the required languages.
