Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - The Punjab State Electricity Board (Revised Pay) Regulations, 1988, regulation 8 - Increments - Regulation 8 of the Regulations state that employees promoted after their publication are entitled to two additional increments along with their routine increments.
However, those who were promoted earlier are not eligible for this benefit.
These Regulations only apply to promotions made after their publication and not to past promotions.
