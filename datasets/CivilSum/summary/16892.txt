Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Section 25-f - Break in service - Termination on the ground that there was break of more than one year in service - The temporary status has been regularized for the respondent according to para V of the Scheme, and since there is no non-availability of work or requirement for service, retrenchment due to an event that took place years ago is unnecessary.
