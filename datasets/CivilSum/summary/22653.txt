As per Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, proceedings under the act are considered execution proceedings.
These proceedings require prior adjudication before determining and enforcing a right.
[Para]
Under Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, payment of overtime wages is claimed by appellants who are prima facie managers.
Therefore, determining their status in Proceedings under Section 33-C(2) falls outside the jurisdiction of the Labour Court.
Reliance on documents filed by appellants alone is not adequate to establish the factual basis of the issues involved in the proceedings.
