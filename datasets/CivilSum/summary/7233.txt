Army Act, 1950, Section 191 - Army Group Insurance - Benefit of insurance cover - The petitioner's husband was a member of the Army Group Insurance Scheme and made regular contributions.
When he did not return to his unit after taking casual leave, he was not heard from by his family or Army authorities for seven years.
Under Section 108 of the Evidence Act, it is presumed that he is dead.
However, the respondents withheld the insurance cover amount and required the petitioner to obtain a court order for presumptive death.
This is not sustainable and the respondents must release the insurance cover amount.
