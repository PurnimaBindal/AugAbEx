Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Natural justice - Termination of services taking into consideration adverse past record of service - While it is true that past service record should not be taken into account as punishment for proven guilt in departmental proceedings, in cases where an employee argues their record has always been upstanding, the punishing authority must investigate and verify this claim.
If it is discredited, the employee cannot later claim that the order is based on their past adverse record.
In this specific case, where the petitioner had been found guilty of rash and negligent driving resulting in a fatality and deemed unfit for service, previous conduct must be taken into account and the termination order remains valid.
[Paras 4 to 6]
