Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - If a person is ignored arbitrarily while promoting his juniors, he is entitled to notional promotion, seniority and arrears of wages from the date his juniors were promoted- If vacancies are available for promotion on the seniority cum merit basis, filling up of the posts by transfer of juniors while ignoring seniors is a malafide act by the authority - This action leaves the posts vacant at the places where the transferees come from - High Court orders giving benefit from retrospective dates have been upheld.
[Paras 2, 8 to 10]
