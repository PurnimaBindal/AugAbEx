Constitution of India, Article 16 - Kerala State Subordinate Services Rules, 1958, Rule 17-A - Reservation/Appointment - Rule 17-A provides for direct recruitment from the open market, not limited to any particular section of the public, with reservation for SC and ST candidates under Rules 14 and 17.
Reservation can be implemented during transfer or promotion, as well as during initial hiring.
However, appointments under the sports quota and compassionate appointment of dependents of those who died while serving are not subject to these rules, although they are considered direct appointments under Rule 17-A.
