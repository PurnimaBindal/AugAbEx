Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Promotion - Petitioner seeks promotion from Baildar to Road Mate - Petitioner claims entitlement to be considered for promotion from the date of respondents No. 3 and 4's designation as Road Mate - Respondents No. 3 and 4 contend they were appointed as Mate which is a separate cadre from that of Baildar - Petitioner failed to prove respondents No. 3 and 4 were Baildars like petitioner and were junior to him - Petitioner is not entitled to relief.
