According to the Prevention of Corruption Act, Sections 7 and 13(2), a sanitary/health inspector working with the respondent was found guilty of demanding and accepting a bribe.
After being convicted and sentenced to two years imprisonment, the petitioner was dismissed from service in 1996.
The petitioner appealed against the conviction, suspending the conviction order.
Despite this, the department can still take disciplinary action and terminate the employee's services based on the conviction order from the criminal court.
