As per Section 3(8) of the General Clauses Act, 1897 and Sections 14B and 14C of the Delhi Rent Control Act, employees of Indian Railways are considered Central Government employees.
Although the management of railways has been delegated to the Railway Board by the President of India, railways are still considered a part of the Central Government under the General Clauses Act.
Therefore, Railway employees are entitled to the benefits of Sections 14-B and 14-C of the Delhi Rent Control Act.
