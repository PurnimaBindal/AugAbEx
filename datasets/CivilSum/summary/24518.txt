Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226 and 311(2) - Departmental Enquiry - Dispensing with the Enquiry - In order to dispense with an enquiry, certain conditions must be met, including that the punishing authority be subjectively satisfied and provide independent reasons or material - Threats or obstacles posed by the delinquent do not excuse a dispensation of inquiry - High Court can intercede if the dispensation order is illegal, arbitrary, or mala fide - Clause (b) of the second proviso to Article 311(2) can only be invoked when it is not reasonably practicable to hold an inquiry.
[Paras 4 and 5]
