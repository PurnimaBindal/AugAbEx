Bihar Pension Rules, 1950, Rule 43(b) - Pension and gratuity - Withholding of pension and gratuity during pendency of departmental or judicial proceedings - The Government is not authorized to withhold any amount of pension or gratuity unless there is a finding of grave misconduct or conviction against the retiring employee.

Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Bihar Pension Rules, 1950, Rule 43(b) - Bihar Service Code, Rule 5 - Leave encashment - Executive instructions - Withholding or leave encashment during pendency of departmental or judicial proceedings - Rule 5 of Bihar Service Code does not empower issue of instructions for withholding of leave encashment - Issuing executive instructions to withhold any amount of leave encashment is against Article 300A of the Constitution and without any authority of law, cannot be relied upon.
