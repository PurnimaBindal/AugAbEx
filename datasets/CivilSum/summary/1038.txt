Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Competitive examinations - Organized by Public Service Commission - Whether it is reasonable and fitting to exclude misleading or vague questions and not allot any marks for them, due to a defect or ambiguity in a key answer of a question during the examination - Single Judge deciding in the affirmative - However, decision referred to a Division Bench for further review.
