Selection grade grant - As per policy framed on recommendation of the second pay commission - Respondents referred matter again to the 3rd pay commission - It was held that there was no justification for referring the case for recommendation to the 3rd pay commission.
It should have been dealt with based on prevalent instructions, especially when similarly situated individuals were given benefits.
The Respondent is directed to decide as per instructions prevalent at the relevant time.
