The Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16, prohibits discrimination and estoppel against legal rights regarding pay scales.
The petitioners performed duties of a higher post but received lower pay scales due to an undertaking that waived their right to claim higher pay.
However, the right to receive fair pay is a fundamental right and of public policy.
The doctrine of waiver does not apply to constitutional policies.
Filing a form does not grant the state power to discriminate in violation of constitutional mandates, and there is no question of estoppel against the law.
