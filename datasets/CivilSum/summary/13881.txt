Advocates Act, 1961, Section 24 - Constitution of India, Articles 19(1)(g), 21, 25 - Enrollment of Advocates - Priests and nuns are eligible to be enrolled as Advocates, but they do not join priesthood or nunhood as a profession for earning.
While, other individuals who join the profession of divinity dedicate their lives to serve God and men.
Additionally, being a priest or nun does not constitute a profession.
