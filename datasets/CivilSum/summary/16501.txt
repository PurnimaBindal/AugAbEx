Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2A, 10, 25 - B(2)(a)(ii) and 25-F - Termination without initiation of disciplinary proceedings - Continuous Service - Deemed Continuous service - 240 days service - According to the tribunal, the expression "Actually worked under the employer" means all those days during which the employee was in employment of employer and for which he was paid wages, taking into account unpaid Sundays and Holidays at the time of calculating actual working days of respondent no. 3 in a year.
As a result, the tribunal determined that respondent no. 3 worked for more than 240 days in a year.
In arriving at its decision, the tribunal also considered factors such as Bonus, D.A, HRA, PF - These factors are not relevant to the counting of continuous service, and the tribunal erred in law.
The impugned award has been set aside and quashed, and the matter has been remitted.
