Constitution of India, Article 14 - Central Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19 - Selection - Appointment - Candidates belonging to BCs can't demand that the government appoint them against vacancies reserved for them if the selection exceeds 50% of their quota.
Instead, they must compete with other eligible candidates during the appointment process.
This approach aligns with the principles of Articles 14 and 16(1) of the Constitution.
