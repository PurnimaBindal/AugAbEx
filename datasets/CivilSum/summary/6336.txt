Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Punjab Service of Engineers (Electricity Branch) Recruitment Rules, 1939, Rule 4 - Electrical Inspector Class II Rules, 1979 - Promotions - Reservation in promotions - The reservation of a single vacancy in a cadre is prohibited, but the second vacancy can be reserved for a Scheduled Caste candidate and in case of non-availability, the reserved point has to be carried forward - In AIR 1981 SC 298, the Supreme Court ruled there can be no reservation in promotions, but that decision would apply prospectively and vacancies already accrued would continue to be filled on availability till 5 years from the date of judgement - The petitioner, a general category candidate, was promoted against a reserved vacancy and worked without any issues for many years - Reverting him now would cause undue burden - Thus, authorities are directed to create a new post for him and consider his claim independently to accommodate him to that post.
