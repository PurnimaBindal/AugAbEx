Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 311(1)/Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 / Promotion / Seniority / juniors - Passing Carrier Wave Course - The petitioner passed the Carrier Wave Course before the respondents, but the respondents were promoted to the rank of ASIs before the petitioner.
Promotions are not determined solely by seniority or date of passing the Carrier Wave Course, but rather through a departmental selection committee test.
The petitioner did not succeed in the selection test and cannot claim promotion over his juniors solely on the basis of being first to pass the course.
[Paras 4 and 5]
