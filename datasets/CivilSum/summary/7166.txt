According to Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the burden is now on the workman to prove that they were not gainfully employed during the termination period, rather than on the employer to prove the contrary.
The Labour Court deemed the retrenchment illegal and ordered reinstatement, but did not award back wages due to the short duration of the workman's service and the lack of any evidence of unemployment during the termination period.
