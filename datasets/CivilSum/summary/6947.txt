Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - West Bengal Public Distribution System (Maintenance and Control) Order, 2003, Para 23- Appointment of Modified Ration Distributor - Selection Process - Inquiry held against provisions of Para 23 - Power to inquire vested in sub-divisional controller - Conducted by inquiry committee on his behalf - Sub-Divisional Controller has power to make comments only - Controller made recommendation for appointment - Making recommendation for appointment of dealer only and not distributor a gross violation of statutory provisions - Inquiry report set aside.
