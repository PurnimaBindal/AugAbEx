Section 34 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 pertains to Cognizance of offences related to Unfair Labour practice.
The section is intended to limit the right of a workman, trade union or employer to make complaints under the act.
In order to avoid frivolous, vexatious or untenable complaints, Section 34 requires that complaints must have authorization from the appropriate Government before they can be taken cognizance of.
This authorization can be given by someone who is not a Government servant.
The phrase "or under the authority of" in Section 34 must also be interpreted accordingly.
