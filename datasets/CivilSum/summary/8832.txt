Ad hoc appointment of the appellant on a short term basis was terminated after obtaining an injunction for continuation after completion of term.
Both the suit and appeal were dismissed.
The Civil Court was wrong in granting the injunction because the appointment was for a fixed period only and continuing the illegality cannot be allowed.
[Para 3] B According to Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 of the Civil Procedure Code, 1908, an injunction must be granted according to the known principles of law.
Granting an injunction merely based on hardship does not suffice as there must be a substantive right that requires protection.
In matters such as termination or suspension of service, interim injunction should not be readily granted.
