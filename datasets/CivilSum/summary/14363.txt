Rajasthan Employment of Physically Handicapped Rules, 1976, Section 2(v) - Appointment for post of teacher in physically handicapped category - The Rules do not specify a particular duration for certificates from physically handicapped individuals - Therefore, the requirement of a 3-year certificate in the advertisement is invalid.
[Para 13] B. Estoppel - The petitioner was prevented from applying as a physically handicapped person due to a certificate that was beyond 3 years old, and therefore had to apply as a general category candidate on the next date of the selection committee - The respondent's plea of estoppel is not valid in this case.
