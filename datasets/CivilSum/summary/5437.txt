Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-G and H - Implementation of "Last come first go" principle - Hire and fire policy - Dismissing daily wagers without adhering to the "Last come first go" policy is akin to adopting the hire and fire policy - It is not mandatory for the workman to complete 240 days of service to be eligible for the "Last come first go" principle as laid out in Sections 25-G and H.
