Under the U.P.
Intermediate Education Act, 1921, Section 16E and Regulations framed under Regn.
33-A, selection of teachers can no longer be made by schools after the amended Act.
Only the Commission under the UP Secondary Education Services Commission and Selection Board Act, 1982 has the authority to select candidates for appointment.
The selected candidate must be appointed on the recommendation of the commission and it is not legal to regularize selection under Regn.
33-A It is advised to use recourse to Regn.
33 sparingly and not as a routine.
