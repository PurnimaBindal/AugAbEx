Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admissions - Education - Admission to MBBS/BDS courses for Medical College - Reservations made for dependents of ex-servicemen and dependents of those discharged from military service on medical ground - Despite producing a certificate of dependency on a disabled ex-serviceman at the time of interview, the petitioner was not considered for admission due to a failure to mention their priority number for that category on the admission form - The authorities' decision to deny admission to the petitioner, who attained a score of 581 and outperformed the respondent who received admission, was ultimately arbitrary and unsustainable - As such, the petitioner must receive admission, even if that requires the cancellation of admission granted to Respondent No. 2.
[Paras 8 and 9]
