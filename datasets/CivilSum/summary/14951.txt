The selection process for the post of Patwaris was found to be arbitrary and unfair.
The marks secured by candidates were entirely ignored, and a new criterion was adopted.
Only one person from the merit list was selected, and interviews were conducted within a week of issuing interview letters.
Moreover, 180 candidates were interviewed in one day.
Consequently, the court gave directions for conducting a fresh selection out of persons who appeared in the test.
