Sections 10 and 12 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Reference - The decision to decline the reference based on the grounds that the workman was appointed for a fixed period and his termination was not classified as retrenchment is not appropriate - It is within the purview of the Industrial Tribunal to resolve such disputes - The government cannot interfere with the Industrial Tribunal's duties - The dispute must be forwarded to the Industrial Tribunal for adjudication.
