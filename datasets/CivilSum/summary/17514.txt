Pharmacy Council of India's Rules and Regulations of 1981, Regulation 10 - Admission - Students entering the Pharmacy Diploma course are required to pass an entrance test.
Matriculate students must pass this exam for admission to the course.
Those who have passed 10+2 examination are eligible for admission to the second year of the course provided they pass the subjects prescribed for Part-I which they have not previously studied.
The second year results of the petitioners cannot be declared until their first year papers are cleared.
However, if they pass their re-appears, their second year results will be announced.
