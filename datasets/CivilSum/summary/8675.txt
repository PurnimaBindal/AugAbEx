According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the appropriate government has jurisdiction over references.
When a workman raises an industrial dispute demanding notice that his services have been terminated after working for five years, the government may decline to make a reference if the worker has not completed 240 days.
However, it is the Labour Court's responsibility to determine whether the workman completed 240 days based on the evidence presented by the parties.
As a result, the government has been ordered to make the reference to the Labour Court.
