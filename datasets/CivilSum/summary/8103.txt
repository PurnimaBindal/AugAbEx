Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-H - Termination of workman's services while retaining juniors - Merely denying and explaining the retention of a few juniors is considered admission - Workman does not need to provide additional evidence - The Labour Court's findings, stating that the workman was unable to prove the retention of his juniors, were found to be unlawful and overturned - The petitioner should be reinstated with 50% back wages, taking into account his short duration of service.
