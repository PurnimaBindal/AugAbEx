Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Regularisation - Bipartite settlement dated 541995 between the FCI and FCI Workers Union - Workers who were paid wages for nine months during the preceding 12 months before April, 1995 are eligible to remain under the current direct payment system and will receive minimum wages notified, including those on medical leave or not paid due to injury or sickness incurred while working for the Corporation - Denying these workers the benefits of the settlement would be unjust and a hindrance to the cause of justice - FCI authorities are instructed to provide minimum guaranteed wages to these workers, similar to those paid to other workers.
