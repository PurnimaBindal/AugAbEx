The AP Administrative Tribunal Order (1975), Para 6, states that an annulment of a tribunal's order by the State Government under Article 371-D(5) would be deemed null.
The order of the tribunal must be carried out, regardless of any subsequent interlocutory orders from the Supreme Court directing a fresh inquiry against the employee that has led to their dismissal.
The fact remains that if the annulment had not been enacted, there would have been no grounds for the appeal to the Supreme Court and no basis for the interlocutory orders.
