According to Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Sections 102 and 128 of the Haryana Cooperative Societies Act, 1984, the jurisdiction of the Labour Court or Industrial Tribunal is not removed in cases of disputes between a cooperative society and its employers pertaining to the establishment of the society.
The power to determine the dispute is not entirely vested in the Registrar and Section 128 of the Societies Act, which excludes such jurisdiction, is unconstitutional.
This interpretation is supported by AIR 1986 (P&H) 386.
