The Indian Audi and Accounts Department were split into Audit and Accounts Wings, in accordance with Pay Commission recommendations for Pay/Equal Pay for Equal Work in the Constitution of India's Article 14 and 16.
The second paragraph of the Pay Commission report specified that certain posts within the Accounts Wing should be declared as functional grades with higher pay scales for those posts identified in the future.
It is not possible to apply higher pay scales retrospectively, and there is a clear difference between the two categories.
The implementation dates for the recommendations differ, but it is not a violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
