Constitution of India, Article 309 - Rajasthan Municipal (Subordinate Ministerial Service) Rules, 1963, Rule 10 - Appointment - Promotion - The appointment and promotion quota for the post of Nakedar is fixed at 50:50 ratio.
Respondent No. 5 was appointed under the direct recruitment quota while the petitioner claimed appointment under the promotion quota.
As the petitioner's case does not fall within the quota of direct recruitment, he cannot challenge the appointment of Respondent No. 5 from that quota.
Furthermore, Respondent No. 5 was appointed against the reserved vacancies of ex-servicemen, and therefore, he has no entitlement to be appointed against those reserved vacancies.
The petitioner has no locus standi to challenge the appointment of Respondent on the post of Nakedar as he cannot claim appoint under either the direct recruitment quota or the reserved vacancies for ex-servicemen.
