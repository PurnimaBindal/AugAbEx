Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 311 - Termination - Charge of unauthorized absence - Violation of principles of natural justice - No enquiry held or opportunity of defence given - Argument that holding of enquiry was not necessary as petitioner was temporarily assigned - Decision: Termination order was invalid and cancelled because it was not in compliance with natural justice principles, and a new order may be issued only if an inquiry is conducted and the rules of natural justice are followed.
[Paras 22 and 24]
