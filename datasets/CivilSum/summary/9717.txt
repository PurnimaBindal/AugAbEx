Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol.
I, Rule 7.3(1) & 7.3(2) - Misconduct - Punishment of dismissal - Reduced of punishment to forfeiture of salary for the period delinquent remained out of service and the period treated as leave without pay - If the delinquent is reinstated after being fully exonerated from the charge, a show cause notice under Rule 73(2) is required.
The employee will then become entitled to full pay and allowances.
However, if the punishment of dismissal is reduced by way of censure and restricting benefits of suspension period or otherwise by the appellate authority but the employee was not fully exonerated, the case is covered under Rule 7.3(1) and no further show cause notice is necessary.
The order was fully justified after conducting Departmental enquiry and affording full opportunity.
[Paras 7 & 8]
