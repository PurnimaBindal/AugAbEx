The VSNL made an error in payment of Dearness Relief on Pension/Family pension to retirees who were already receiving Industrial Dearness Pattern.
This resulted in such employees receiving double benefits of DR. Employees of IDA pay scales with IDA pattern of DR were not eligible for pension calculated on IDA emoluments with CDA pattern of DR.
The Constitution of India, 1950, Article 226 allows judicial review but does not confer any right to any party and it can be corrected if there is a bona fide mistake.
The court stated that compassionate allowance can be granted at the discretion of the employer but no writ or direction should be issued to compel the exercise of such power as there is no right in the employee that can be protected or enforced.
Dearness Relief is granted to Government Servants as a matter of grace and not a vested right, therefore, it cannot be claimed against the Government.
The Government's prerogative in terms of Rule 55-A of the CCA (Pension) Rules, 1972 grants DR at particular rates and subject to specific conditions.
