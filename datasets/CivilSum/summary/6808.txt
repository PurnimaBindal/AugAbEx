Constitution of India, Article 311-A - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2, Item 6 - Superannuation age - Date of birth - Calculation of age - Fractions of a day not considered - A fraction of day counts as complete day - Day of birth counted as full day - Specified age attained day before anniversary of birth day - For instance, if an individual's date of birth is March 1st, they will attain the specified age of complete year for the purpose of superannuation on February 28th of the year of retirement.
