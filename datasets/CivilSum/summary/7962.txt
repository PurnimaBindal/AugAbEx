Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Termination - Forged caste certificate - In 1984, the Appellant had his services as a Constable terminated due to providing a forged caste certificate, as indicated in the rules set forth by the Constitution of India in Articles 16 and 226 - The Appellant was acquitted of this crime in 1997 - After being acquitted, the Appellant sent a notice to the respondent to reinstate him - However, the Petition was filed three and a half years after acquittal and sixteen years after termination, and was dismissed by a single Judge on the grounds of delay - The order has been challenged on the grounds of illegality and ex facie bad - The court held that the mere pendency of the criminal case would not be something that precludes challenging the termination order - However, since the Petition was filed so long after the incident, the single Judge was justified in dismissing it due to delay, and therefore the appeal was also dismissed.
