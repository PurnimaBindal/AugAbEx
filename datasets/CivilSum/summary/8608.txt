Constitution of India, Article 226 - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 2A(1) - Continuous service - Despite being absent without leave for a few days, workers cannot be deprived of gratuity by the employer - Imposition of fine does not break the continuity of service - No order was passed by the employer stating that the absence was to be treated as a break in service - The employees in this case were in continuous service and were entitled to gratuity - Section 2A(2) only applies when the employee's case does not fall under Section 2A(1) - The employees here were covered under Section 2A(1), so the question of counting actual working days does not arise.
