Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(s) - Apprentices Act, 1961, Sections 2(aa) and 18(a) and (b) - Workman - Definition - Apprentices cannot be included as workman unless they meet the requirements mentioned in Section 2(s) of 1947 Act by working in specified capacities.
[Para 34] B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(s), 25F - Life Insurance Corporation of India (Apprentice Development Officers) Recruitment Scheme, 1980, Clause 6.1 - Apprentices Act, 1961, Sections 2(aa) and 18(a) and (b) - Termination of services of Apprentice Development Officer - The employee could not prove his status as a workman and that he performed any skilled, unskilled, manual, technical or operational duties.
A workman under Section 2(s) must establish he is not governed by Apprentices Act and working in capacities mentioned in the definition.
Hence the employee was not a workman under the meaning of Section 2(s) of Industrial Disputes Act.
