Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(s) - `Workman' - Meaning of - Determining nature of work based on allowances received is risky - The nature of work is determined by the responsibilities that come with the position - The petitioner's role was to prepare vouchers/details of cheques and had no managerial/administrative power - Respondent No. 2 did not provide evidence of the specific work the petitioner was assigned - Therefore, the petitioner qualifies as a workman under Section 2(s) of the Act - The case has been sent back to the Labour Court for re-evaluation.
