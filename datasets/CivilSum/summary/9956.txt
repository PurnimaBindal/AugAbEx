Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Promotion - Transfer - Transfer from one branch to another in the same department - The petitioner, who works at the Haryana Roadways, Rohtak Depot, was transferred to Central Workshop, Rohtak - Two branches can transfer posts inter se - A common seniority list is used - The order did not specify that the person would lose seniority or other benefits of his service on transfer - Juniors to the petitioner who were mechanics working in the Haryana Roadways, Rohtak Depot, were promoted as Head Mechanics - The petitioner was ignored only because he was sent to Central Workshop, Rohtak at his behest - The authorities' actions were discriminatory and illegal - The petitioner was still entitled to be considered for promotion along with his juniors in the Haryana Roadways, Rohtak Depot.
