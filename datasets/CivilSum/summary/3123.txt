Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974, Section 74 - AP Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Rules, 1976, Rule 4 - Promotion - Qualification - The petitioner for the post of Senior Environmental Scientist was not only senior but also well qualified, possessing all the requisite qualifications.
However, the 2nd respondent did not possess the necessary qualifications required for the promotion.
The rules have not been amended to consider the diploma in AIC as equivalent to M.Sc.
Chemistry.
Therefore, the 2nd respondent is not entitled to claim the promotion.
According to the Recruitment Rules, a person is not entitled to promotion or appointment if their claim for promotion is based on a qualification that is not listed in the rules.
[Paras 13 and 16]
