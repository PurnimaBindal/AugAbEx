Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions (Recognition, Grant-in-aid and Service Conditions etc.) Rules, 1993 - Employees of aided institutions are entitled to the same benefits as employees of Govt. Educational Institutions, including equal pay for equal work, selection grade, and retiral benefits.
[Paras 6 and 7]
Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions Act, 1989, Sections 21 and 19 - Employees of aided institutions have the right to maintain claims for equal pay for equal work, selection grade, and retiral benefits, and the Tribunal has jurisdiction to entertain such claims.
[Paras 11 to 13]
