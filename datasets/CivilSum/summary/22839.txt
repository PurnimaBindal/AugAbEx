The legislature can only validate acts that suffer from irregularity, not illegality, and appointments made illegally cannot be validated by a validating Act.
[Paras 7-9]
The legislature can remove infirmities through retrospective validation, but cannot override a court judgment.
[Paras 12, 16, 22, 23]
The court can only direct regularisation in exceptional circumstances, but cannot perpetuate deliberately committed illegalities.
[Para 18]
Legal consequences cannot be deemed, only facts can be deemed, along with the consequences that follow.
[Paras 20, 21]
