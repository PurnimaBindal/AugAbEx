The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 918 (as applicable to Haryana) - Compulsory retirement - Confidential reports - Adverse remarks prior to promotion - For the purpose of compulsory retirement, Mere promotion does not remove the impact of adverse reports relating to the period before the promotion.
Reports prior to promotion cannot be exempted.
1998(1) RSJ 73 followed - If doubts about integrity arose after the promotion, it is the subsequent entry that would dominate, and the fact that he was promoted would lose its impact and value.
