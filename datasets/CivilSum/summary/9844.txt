The petitioner was reverted from the post of Director General of Quality Assurance after allegations of accepting sub-standard material.
The petitioner claimed to have been released from the Army upon being seconded by Director General Quality Assurance, but the court held that Regulation 409 applies to military personnel even on detached employment.
The court only needs to consider whether a prima facie case has been made by the petitioner for a stay of the reversion order.
The stay application was deemed without merit.
[Paras 17, 18, 24, and 25]
Army personnel who are seconded for duty to civil departments, permanently or temporarily, are subject to Defence Service Regulations, 1962, Regulation 409.
