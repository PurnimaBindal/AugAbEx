Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 136 - Seniority - The transfer order stated that respondent's seniority would be reckoned in a certain manner, and therefore, the High Court was wrong in considering the transfer as due to administrative exigencies or the creation of a new division, and that it should not affect his seniority to his disadvantage - The order of the High Court is overturned.
