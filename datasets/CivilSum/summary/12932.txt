Referring to Section 18(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Rule 23(2) of the State Bank of India Employees Pension Fund Rules, this case involves retrospective revision of pay scale for the petitioner and the non-revision of pension.
The petitioner retired on February 28, 1993, with a revision of pay scale commencing from November 1, 1992.
The court held that the respondents must correctly fix the petitioner's basic pension on the revised basic pay due to the natural consequence of him being paid the difference of basic pay after settlement.
[Paras 10 and 15]

This case regards a claim for pension based on the revised pay scale, where the petitioner filed a writ petition after 8 years from the date of retirement and 7 years from the bipartite settlement under which pay scales were revised.
However, since the petitioner made a number of representations and the claim was denied only in May 2001, with the writ filed in June 2001, the petitioner cannot be blamed for the delay.
[Para 14]
