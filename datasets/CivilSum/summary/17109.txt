Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Medical Treatment - Reimbursement of expenses - The petitioner was diagnosed with chronic renal failure and certified by P.G.I.
to need lifelong dialysis - Despite getting dialysis at a cheaper price from Chandigarh Medical Centre, the respondents refused to reimburse the expenses claiming that the petitioner needed to first go to P.G.I.
for certification - Court held that Hemo-dialysis is a life-saving procedure and assistance from the respondent to call petitioner to P.G.I.
is not necessary - Since Chandigarh Medical Centre is charging less than P.G.I., petitioner's claim is allowed without needing a certificate from P.G.I.
- Respondent directed to clear all pending bills of petitioner.
