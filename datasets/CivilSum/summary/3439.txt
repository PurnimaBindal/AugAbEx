The Constitution of India, Articles 12, 16, 19, 39(a), 41, 226 pertains to the issue of regularisation of contractual appointees.
The petitioners were selected in the campus selection process and were the best candidates amongst the lot.
However, their appointment was not made on a regular basis due to a ban which has now been withdrawn.
It was held that the petitioners have a fundamental right to work, which cannot be ignored.
Several other similarly situated individuals have already been regularized.
Directions have been given to examine the claim of the petitioners afresh while considering their preferential rights, experience, and unblemished record.
[Paras 16 and 19]
