The petitioner in this case was claiming retiral benefits that were being denied to them.
The respondents had objected to the maintainability of the writ, stating that their bank did not qualify as a State under Article 12.
However, it was held that even if a society is not classified as a State, a writ would still be allowed for the enforcement of a statutory public duty.
In this case, the respondent's objection was dismissed, as the petitioner was allowed to retire without any proceedings being drawn up against them for alleged misconduct.
Therefore, the recovery of the loan cannot be made, as it is against legal principles and without jurisdiction.
The petition was consequently allowed.
