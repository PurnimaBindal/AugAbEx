The petitioner is claiming compassionate appointment under the Ex-gratia Grant scheme after the death of their family member while in service.
However, the court notes that compassionate appointment cannot be claimed as a right, and there is no evidence to prove that the family is facing hardship due to the employee's death.
The Ex-gratia scheme is meant to provide employment as a way to mitigate hardship, not as a guarantee.
The petitioner, who has three brothers already in service and has been cultivating ancestral land, is not entitled to a compassionate appointment, and thus the petition is dismissed.
