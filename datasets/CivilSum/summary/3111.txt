Civil Service Regulations, Rule 40 - Seniority - Promotion - The petitioners were prevented from joining on the designated date after their study leave.
However, they were entitled to promotion from 6.5.87.
It was the respondent-University's fault for the delay and the impugned order did not correct any errors but instead committed an error that deprived the petitioners of their rightful promotion.
Thus, the petitioners will be considered as having been promoted from 6.5.87.
