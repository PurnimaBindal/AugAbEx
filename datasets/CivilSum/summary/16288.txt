Punjab Police Rules, 1934, 138 and 139 - Promotion - Promotion Authorities are required to send every Head Constable on list 'C' to complete the Intermediate School Course in order of seniority, beginning with confirmed Head Constables.
Those who successfully complete the Course are eligible for seniority benefits and promotion to higher ranks in accordance with Rules 138 and 139.
[Para 3] B. Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 138 and 139 - Seniority - Seniority of Head Constables is determined by Range, not by State, as per Rules 138 and 139.
