Constitution of India, Article 226/227 - Selection - Appointment - Petitioner chosen but not appointed - Two out of four selectees appointed - Respondent No. 5 was ineligible for not satisfying the necessary qualifications - The appointment of the private respondent was void ab initio because of this ineligibility - Despite the private respondent having already retired, the benefits taken by him cannot be recovered - The petitioner had suffered serious prejudice due to the official respondents' arbitrary actions and the selection process manipulation - The petitioner is entitled to the appointment on the principle of "Actus curiae neminem gravabit" - To meet the ends of justice, the petitioner should be regularised as an Associate Professor with all consequential benefits from the date of the private respondent's superannuation since currently employed on an ad hoc basis.
