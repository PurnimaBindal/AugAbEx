Limitation Act, 1963, Articles 113 - Termination of service - Knowledge of termination order - Plaintiff filed a suit in 1986 for a declaration that he is deemed to continue in service with benefits, and termination order is not binding on his rights - Plaintiff claimed the suit is within limitation from the date of his knowledge of termination - Defendants refused to allow plaintiff to join despite his representations - Correspondence between plaintiff and defendant authorities show that plaintiff repeatedly requested to join and represented against his termination - He cannot argue that he had no knowledge of the termination order and file suit after a lapse of over 26 years.
