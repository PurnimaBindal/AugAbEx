Bihar State Universities Act, 1976, Sections 2(c), 2(i), 2(s) - Appointment - Promotion - Qualifications - Eligibility - The appointment/promotion to the post of Principal of an affiliated college requires specific qualifications along with teaching experience as a lecturer in an affiliated degree college for twelve years.
Such experience must be after acquiring statutory qualifications for a lecturer and in an affiliated college.
Any experience in the same college before grant of affiliation cannot be considered.
The petitioner did not possess the required qualifications of a lecturer at the time of her appointment, nor did she have twelve years of teaching experience in an affiliated degree college when promoted to Principal before its affiliation.
The condition of affiliation states that the teachers of the college must have statutory qualifications, as well as the Principal.
Thus, the Committee of the College was within their rights to reconsider her appointment and replace her with a qualified Principal after the grant of affiliation.
