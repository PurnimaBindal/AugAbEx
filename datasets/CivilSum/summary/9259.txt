Constitution of India, Article 311 - Natural justice/Termination - The termination of a Class IV employee from service for wilful absence without opportunity for a hearing breaches principles of natural justice.
Regulations governing employee conduct in non-government affiliated colleges were framed by Kurukshetra University, which made provisions of the Haryana Affiliated Colleges Act available to non-affiliated employees.
The Act outlines definitions, denoting an "employee" as a person employed full-time by an affiliated college.
Section 7(i) requires that no employee be dismissed, removed, or reduced in rank without first being informed of the charges against him and given an opportunity to respond.
In accordance with the Act, no charge-sheet was provided to the plaintiff, and he was not given adequate opportunity to respond.
His opportunity for defense could not be considered effective, and the college acted in violation of these provisions.
No chance to be heard was given to the plaintiff before the order was made to terminate his service, thereby casting a stigma on him.
The termination order cannot be upheld.

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Limitation Act, 1963, Article 113 - Cause of action/Limitation - A suit was filed by the plaintiff regarding his termination from services after he learned of the order dated May 17, 1978, terminating his services on November 17, 1979.
The right to sue accrued on November 17, 1979, and the suit was filed within the limitation period from the date of cause of action, on September 21, 1981.

Limitation Act, 1963, Article 113 - Void order - The order to terminate services that was passed in violation of statutory rules and principles of natural justice is non-existent and void in the eyes of the law.
This falls under Article 113 of the Limitation Act.
