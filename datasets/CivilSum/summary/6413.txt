Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 310 - Voluntary retirement - Withdrawal of voluntary retirement - The withdrawal of voluntary retirement is only allowed before its acceptance - An unconditional request for voluntary retirement alleging only harassment is not considered as conditional or under duress - Mere issuance of official memos without any further action does not amount to harassment or coercion - The withdrawal of retirement after acceptance and being relieved from duty is not withdrawal, and it is considered an option that was exercised voluntarily.
[Paras 15 & 16]
