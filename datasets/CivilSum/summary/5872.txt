Regular enquiry - Regular enquiry can be ordered for the same charges even if an employee is exonerated after a preliminary enquiry, if the competent authority feels that the findings of the preliminary enquiry conduct are perverse.
[Para 24]  C Constitution of India, Article 14 - Principle of Natural Justice/Regular Enquiry/Delay in holding regular enquiry - A delay in holding a regular enquiry is a violation of principles of natural justice.
An employee who was previously exonerated and then promoted, was subject to a delayed regular enquiry initiated without cause.
Thus, the enquiry is considered vitiating.
[Para 29]  D. General Insurance (Conduct, Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1975, Rules 25 (9) and (10) - Regular Enquiry/Material Documents/Non production of material documents - Failure to produce material documents during a regular enquiry for an employee being investigated is a violation of principles of natural justice.
The petitioner in this case was exonerated after a preliminary enquiry and summoned the file of preliminary enquiry, but it was not supplied.
The department's failure to supply the file led to serious prejudice, rendering the enquiry to be considered vitiating.
[Paras 30, 31, 35 and 36]  E. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Finding of fact - If a finding of fact has been arrived at as a result of misreading of evidence or is considered to be perverse, it can be agitated in writ petition.
