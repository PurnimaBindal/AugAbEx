According to the Co-operative Societies Act, any deduction from salary can be made, but it is not allowed to insert, interpolate or substitute any word or expression into the provisions to expand the meaning of "salary."
Therefore, recovery from DCRG is illegal.
[Paras 22, 24 and 25] B.
To effect any recovery from DCRG, written consent from the employee is necessary.
Without such consent, any recovery will be considered illegal.
