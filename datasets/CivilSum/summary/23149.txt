According to Article 310 of the Constitution of India, Section 2(n) of the Andhra Pradesh Agricultural University Act, and Regulations 3 and 33 of the Andhra Pradesh Agricultural University Regulations, the duties of a Physical Director include teaching students skills and techniques for various games.
In the UGC letter, the post of Physical Education Director is classified as that of a teacher, and therefore entitled to retire at the age of 60 years as applicable to teaching staff.
Recognition as a separate teacher post is not necessary.
