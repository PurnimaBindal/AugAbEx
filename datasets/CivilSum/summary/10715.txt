The Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 and 309, as well as the Indian Railways Establishment Code, Vol.
I, Rule 103(5), govern pay fixation, notification operation, and claim for running allowance.
In this case, the petitioners retired from service after 1988, and the notification dated 5121988 was deemed effective from the same day.
The petitioners are therefore bound by the aforementioned notification, and it was right for the OA of petitioners to be dismissed.
