Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Evidence/Termination/Charge of embezzlement - The termination based on the charge of embezzlement was invalid as it was not the basis for termination - The petitioner was acquitted of the charge by a Criminal Court - bare statement of a person cannot be used as proof for charges - The termination was quashed and the petitioner received full back wages as well as reinstatement to their previous position.
