The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb), 25-G and 25-H govern termination from service.
In this case, the Respondent was appointed as a Pump driver for six months under a scheme, and his services were terminated when the scheme came to an end.
As the Respondent was appointed for a fixed period, the provisions of Section 2(oo)(bb) were attracted.
Even if there was a violation of Section 25-G or 25-H in terminating the respondent's service, compensation, instead of reinstatement with back wages, should be directed.
The award of reinstatement with backwages was set aside, and a direction was issued to pay compensation to the respondent.
