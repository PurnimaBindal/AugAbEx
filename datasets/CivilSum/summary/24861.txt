Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and Laches - The seniority list was created in 1971, but since the appellant was continuously serving as a District Public Relations Officer, no prejudice was caused to him.
The Government gave the appellant proforma promotion around August 7, 1973.
The Government accepted the representation of respondent Nos. 4 and 5 by the impugned order on April 28, 1982, and directed that the inter-se seniority of the appellant and of the said respondents be determined on the basis of their substantive ranks on November 1, 1966.
The order further directed that respondent Nos. 4 and 5 would rank senior to the appellant.
The cause of action for the appellant to file the writ petition arose after 1982, and there was no unreasonable delay on the part of the appellant to challenge the impugned order and the final seniority list.
