The AP State Road Transport Corporation Employees (Service) Regulations, 1964, Regulation 25 and the instructions of the Transport Department violate the equality principle enshrined in Articles 14 and 16(1) and (2) of the Constitution.
The regulations provide for preferential treatment to the sons and daughters of deceased or retired as well as in-service employees for compassionate appointments.
The Transport Department supports this policy by giving preference to the wards of serving employees if they have completed 25 years of service.
However, this classification does not have any reasonable nexus with the object to be achieved and is discriminatory.
