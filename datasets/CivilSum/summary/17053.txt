The J and K Civil Service (Temporary Service Rules), 1961 along with Articles 14, 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India are discussed in relation to ad hoc appointments and subsequent regularization.
A petitioner in this case was initially appointed as a chowkidar but was promoted to Jr. Assistant in an ad hoc manner.
The petitioner is now seeking promotion to Jr. Assistant on a regular basis.
The court notes that prolonged ad hoc appointments are discriminatory and directs the respondents to consider the petitioner's case for regularization against the post he has held for over nine years.
