Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Rules 7.3(1, 2, 4), 75 - Suspension - Allegation of embezzlement of money - Reinstated in service after acquittal by trial court due to insufficient evidence - Suspension period treated as leave - Acquittal makes the suspension entirely unfair, and no action could have been taken to withhold his pay during suspension.
