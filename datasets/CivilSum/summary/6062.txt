Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 14 - Departmental proceedings - De-novo enquiry - The second enquiry initiated against the delinquent after the first enquiry was set aside on the technical ground of violating the principles of natural justice does not suffer from any infirmity.
[Para 11]
Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 14 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19 - Departmental proceedings - De-novo enquiry - Instead of quashing the entire proceedings, the proper course for the Tribunal to adopt after the first enquiry was set aside on technical ground of violating the principles of natural justice is to remit the case to disciplinary authority to proceed afresh, adhering to the principles of natural justice.
[Para 11]
Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 14 - Departmental proceedings - Delay in initiating enquiry - The delay in itself is not enough to set aside the charge sheet unless it is established that it will prejudice the delinquent in defence.
