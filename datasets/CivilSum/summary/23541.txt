According to Rule 7(4) of the All India Institute of Medical Sciences Rules, the appointment of the Respondent as the Director ended on 14th October 1995 unless his tenure was expressly extended.
He cannot continue as the Director unless he is appointed under Rule 7(4) by the President or Institute Body to make an interim arrangement for six months or till the regular Director is appointed.
As the Appellant was the next senior most in order, the President was justified in appointing him as the interim Director pending appointment of Director or for six months whichever may be earlier.
Therefore, Respondent would not be considered appointed even as an interim Director under Rule 7(3) of the Rules, as he had already held the office of the Director, and the Institute Body had decided not to re-appoint him except through the process of selection.
This appeal has been allowed.
