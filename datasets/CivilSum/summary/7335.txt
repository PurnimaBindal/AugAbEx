According to Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act and Section 3(5), 28, and Schedule IV, Item 1 of the Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, "trainees" are covered under the definition of an unfair labour practice.
A complaint was made that "trainees" were dismissed without due process and without receiving any compensation.
The Industrial Court held that the complaint was not maintainable before the Labour Court, but this view was deemed erroneous.
The Labour Court has the jurisdiction to entertain and decide the complaint.
