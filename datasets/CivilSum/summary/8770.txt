For Respondent No. 1, Miss Jaishree Thakur, DAG, Punjab, argued that the court cannot issue a writ forcing the government to declare any caste as Scheduled Casts or Scheduled Tribe.
The Central Government has not issued any order under Article 342 of the Constitution regarding the State of Punjab, so the mere inclusion of a community in the category of Scheduled Tribes by the State will not entitle the candidates of that community to claim the benefit of reservation.
Moreover, the Bauria caste included by the State of Punjab in the List of Scheduled Tribes under Article 341 cannot claim the benefit of reservation unless a Presidential order is issued under Article 342.
Miss Jaishree Thakur also emphasized that the need to declare a caste as Scheduled Tribe and make a reservation for its members is a matter to be decided by the Government, and the court has no jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution to compel the government to make a reservation.
