Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Termination - The petitioner filed a writ petition seeking direction against the termination of services as a temporary employee before regular appointment was made - The court did not issue a stay -Termination was effective while petition was still pending - Termination order was placed on record without amending the petition and not challenged in an independent petition - The order cannot be quashed.
