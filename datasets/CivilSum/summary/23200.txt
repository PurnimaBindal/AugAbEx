Haryana Cooperative Societies Act, 1984, Sections 34 and 34A and Provisos thereto - Proviso - The meaning, scope, and role of a proviso - A proviso cannot have an overriding effect over the substantive provision to which it is added - A proviso is added only to control or expand the scope of the main provision as an exception to a limited extent, avoiding ambiguous meanings and interpretations - A proviso cannot override or extend beyond Sections 34 and 34-A of the Act.

Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, Sections 4 and 6 - Staff Service Rules of the Haryana State Co-operative Land Development Bank Ltd, Rule 21 - Haryana Cooperative Societies Act, 1984, Sections 34 and 34A and Provisos thereto - Bonus - The Primary Banks of Haryana State Co-operative Land Development Bank Ltd keeping separate books of accounts and maintaining separate Balance Sheets of profit and loss are considered different cooperate entities with their respective registration or incorporation and are treated as separate undertakings to its apex Bank for the purpose of Bonus Act - The employees of Primary Banks are not eligible for parity of Bonus with the employees of the apex Bank.
