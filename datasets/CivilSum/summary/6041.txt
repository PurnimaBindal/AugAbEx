The State Government issued a circular letter granting JBT Teachers who obtained a BA qualification before March 8th, 1990, the benefit of a higher pay scale.
The petitioner was granted this higher scale by the Headmistress despite possessing the higher qualification before joining the service.
The benefit cannot be withdrawn simply because the petitioner had acquired this qualification before entering service.
The circular does not explicitly restrict the benefit to those who obtained the qualification while in service.
Moreover, if the benefit was to be given only to those who obtained higher qualification while in service, it would result in discrimination among JBT Teachers.
[Para 5]
The purpose of encouraging teachers to have a higher qualification is to impart better education to students.
Whether a teacher obtained that qualification before joining service or acquires it afterward is immaterial.
It is not a new question whether a teacher possessing a higher qualification before joining a service is entitled to the benefit of a higher pay scale.
