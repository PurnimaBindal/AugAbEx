State Bank of India (Supervisory Staff) Service Rules, Rule 20 - Departmental proceedings - Dismissal - The Disciplinary Authority imposed a penalty on the charge of indiscriminate grant of overdrafts, cash credit limits, and malpractices, despite claiming to have acted on the advice of the Central Vigilance Commission.
The rules state that an opinion from the Commission must be sought.
As a result, under the given facts and circumstances, the order of dismissal cannot be considered excessively harsh.
[Paras 13, 7]

State of Bank of India (Supervisory Staff) Service Rules, Rules 19, 20 - Disciplinary proceedings - Continuation after superannuation - The rules specify that disciplinary proceedings can be concluded by the authority that initiated them at the discretion of the Managing Director, even after the employee reaches the age of superannuation, as long as the proceeding was initiated before the employee ceased to be in service.
Therefore, the continuation of proceedings after superannuation was legal and justified.
[Para 7]
