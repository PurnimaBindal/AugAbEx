Employee termination - An employee's service can only be terminated once, and simply having an appeal option does not mean the employee remains suspended.
[Para 13]

Dismissal from service - Provisional pension grant - Interpretation of Punjab National Bank (Officers') Service Regulations, 1979 and Punjab National Bank (Employees') Pension Regulations, 1995 - Regulation 22, clause (1) applies when dismissal or removal occurs, and clause (2) only applies during service interruption.
[Para 12]

Order of dismissal - An employee's employment ends at the original order date, not the date of appeal decision.
