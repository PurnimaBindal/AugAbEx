Constitution of India, Article 16 - Bihar Pension Rules, Rule 43(b) - Pensionary benefits - Recovery from - The Petitioner's pension was fixed at a lower stage and recovery was ordered under the Bihar Pension Rules, Rule 43(b).
The contention was that Senior Selection Grade and pension were wrongly granted.
However, it was held that after the retirement of the Petitioner and more than 3 years of promotion, the Respondents cannot question the legality and propriety of the promotion nor lower down his last pay drawn.
