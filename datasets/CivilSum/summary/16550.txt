The petitioner was appointed on compassionate grounds as a clerk on 2071985 despite not meeting the required qualification.
The university relaxed the conditions imposed at the time of her appointment and regularised her service from 26.2.2002.
However, even after 20 years, the petitioner did not fulfill the requisite qualification.
Therefore, there is nothing more the petitioner could ask for.
Additionally, the petitioner has not shown any legally enforceable statutory right for her services to be regularised from the date of her initial appointment.
Consequently, the petitioner is not entitled to any relief.
