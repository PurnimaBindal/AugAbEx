Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Arrears of Wages - Recovery of - Jurisdiction of Labour Court - A claim for salary that the employer has not recognised or adjudicated cannot be decided by the Labour Court.
The Court cannot first determine the workmen's entitlement to certain benefits, then compute the benefits, and then adjudicate under Section 33-C(2).
Section 33-C(2) applies only when the entitlement has been previously adjudicated or recognised by the employer.
The decision in Municipal Corporation of Delhi v. Ganesh Razak, 1995(1) SCT 408 (SC) is followed.
