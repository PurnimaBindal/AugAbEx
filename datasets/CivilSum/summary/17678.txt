Constitution of India, Article 224 - Pleadings - Replication - A claim not included in the writ petition but added on replication should not be used against the respondents without giving them a chance to counter it - The petitioner should instead file the additional claim with the appropriate authority.
[Para 5] B Arrears/Interest - Increments - When an employee becomes entitled to cross the efficiency bar, the arrears of increments that become due and are paid late will accrue interest at a rate of 12% per annum.
