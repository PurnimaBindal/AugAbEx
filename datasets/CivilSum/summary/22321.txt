Under Industrial Dispute Act 1947, Section 25-H, the claim of retrenched workmen for preference in matter of employment has been upheld.
The 111 workmen who were retrenched before the enactment of Cooking Mines (Emergency Provisions) Act 1971 and before the colliery was nationalised are entitled to re-employment.
The learned Tribunal directed that the 111 workmen in question should be considered for employment as and when any recruitment is done, and refusal to employ them is not justified.
Statutory obligations mandate the re-employment of retrenched workmen, despite operational and financial problems encountered by the colliery.
