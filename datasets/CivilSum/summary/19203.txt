Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 21(1)(b) - Embezzlement - Appellate Authority - Jurisdiction to enhance the penalty - The appellate authority is allowed to either confirm, enhance or set aside the penalty imposed by punishing authority - With rule 21 in place, penalty can also be imposed in case none has been previously imposed - Notice must be given before enhancing the penalty - Penalty of censure was given to petitioner by punishing authority - Appellate authority enhanced the penalty - A reasonable opportunity was provided - Ruling states that the Appellate authority can indeed enhance the penalty under rule 21 - The action taken is fully in compliance with rule 21.
