Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural justice - Adverse entries - The recording authority can downgrade confidential reports if warranted in a given case, with reasons for such action to be recorded - Downgrading from 'Very Good' to 'Good' may not be considered adverse, but reasons for the change must be provided to the officer concerned - Normally, positive entries should not be downgraded to adverse, but in exceptional cases, it may be necessary and must be communicated - Even a positive entry can be considered adverse in some situations - Any downgrading made without reasons recorded and only through comparison is not sustainable.
