According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, promotions to the posts of Assistant Unit Officers and Assistant Entomologist Officers, and further promotion to Assistant Malaria Officer, must be based on seniority.
The petitioners in this case were offered a promotion to Assistant Unit Officers on the seniority-cum-merit basis but refused it, which made them junior to those who accepted the offer and were promoted to the higher cadre.
Meanwhile, the private respondents were directly recruited as Senior Laboratory Technicians.
As promotees and juniors in the feeder cadre, the petitioners cannot claim seniority and promotions over the private respondents" for the post of Assistant Malaria Officer.
[Paras 12 to 14]
