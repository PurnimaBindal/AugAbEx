Constitution of India, Articles 14, 31-A and 300-A - Pension - Natural Justice - Withdrawal of pensionary benefits - Petitioner's period of service in a private institution was regularized and treated as qualifying service for pension, subject to depositing the amount of CPF in the State Treasury - Petitioner did deposit the amount prior to his retirement, but order was later cancelled by the administrative department for alleged late deposit - Held, benefit once given with Hon'ble Governor's approval cannot be withdrawn without notice and similarly situated persons should be treated equally - Respondents directed to pay revised pension and gratuity.
