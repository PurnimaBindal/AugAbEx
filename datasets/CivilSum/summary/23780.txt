Constitution of India, Articles 16, 311(2) - Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1970, Section 19 - Union Bank of India Officers Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976 - Dismissal from service - The respondent was promoted to Bank Officer from the post of Clerk in 1988, but then in 1989, irregularities committed by him prior to his promotion and thereafter led to his suspension and charge-sheeting.
During the inquiry proceedings, the respondent attended on a few dates before it continued ex-parte.
The respondent was found guilty of the charges, and the disciplinary authority awarded him the punishment of dismissal.
The respondent's appeal was dismissed under Regulation 17.
The High Court allowed the writ petition, which set aside the order of dismissal from service in the assumption that a copy of the enquiry report not served on the respondent, directing to supply its copy, and then the disciplinary authority to consider the representation.
However, this was factually incorrect.
First charge-sheet related to the period before his promotion in 1988, and the remaining three for the period thereafter.
All the four charge-sheets relate to serious misconduct.
The High Court failed to apply its judicial mind to the facts and circumstances of the case and erroneously concluded that non-supply of the enquiry report/findings has caused prejudice to the respondent.
Therefore, the High Court's orders were set aside, and the order of dismissal of the respondent was confirmed.
