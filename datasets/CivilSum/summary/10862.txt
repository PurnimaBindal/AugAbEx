The Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16, 21, and 226 explain compassionate appointments, including eligibility, delay, and the rights of minors.
Those who are not eligible for appointment cannot claim the right to seek appointment later.
However, the dependent or heir of a deceased employee can be appointed if they are eligible and apply within a reasonable time.
This should not be viewed as reserving a post for future use.
