Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Appointment - Ban on appointment - Selection of Constables in Haryana Police - Despite a ban on new appointments, selection was made for Constables in Haryana Police - Appointments were withheld due to the ban - Selection of petitioners was not cancelled - After the ban was lifted, over 600 constables were recruited - Petitioners who were selected earlier are entitled to be appointed as the ban on selection does not imply automatic cancellation of selection.
