Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Education - Institute of Technical Education - Larson and Toubro Institute of Technology, catered to by a public limited company, is only for the children of its employees - though not an engineering college, the institute is run as a polytechnic - The State Government has set out the conditions for admissions which include following a strict merit and reservation policy - The All India Council of Technical Education and the State Government have both approved the continuation of the institute - The scheme of the institution is deemed related to its objective and therefore upheld.
