Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F, G, and H - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination of daily wager's service - Respondent was appointed temporarily for 4 years - Claim that honorarium couldn't be paid and services terminated due to financial crises and unavailability of a vacant post - Labor Court found that the respondent was a regular employee without a seniority list, notice or salary in lieu or compensation and thus, his service was illegally terminated - It is held that based on the Labor Court's well-reason findings, no interference under Article 226 is necessary.
