Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation - Part time workers - Non-availability of post - Age limit - Eligibility - Petitioners, who have served as part time workers as Sweepers and Kahars for over a decade, seek the regularisation of their services - Their claims have been rejected on grounds of eligibility, age limit, and non-availability of post despite similar employees having received regularisation - The court holds that the respondents have failed to provide sufficient evidence of the eligibility required for the post - The question of age limit cannot be used to deny the services of the petitioners, who have served for an extended period - The plea of non-availability of post is not tenable as the petitioners have served for over 10 years, and the respondents had a policy in place for regularisation - Therefore, the Court orders the respondents to regularise the services of the petitioners from the due date.
