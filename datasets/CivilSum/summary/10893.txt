Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Unfair Labour Practice - The respondent completed 240 days of service in the year preceding date of his termination via various orders of appointment, yet no legal reason for his discontinuation was provided - The practice of employing by such orders and terminating their service by non-renewal of order is deemed to be unfair labour practice by the labour court - The court's decision to uphold an award of reinstatement with continuity of service and backwages for the respondent.
