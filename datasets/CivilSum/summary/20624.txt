Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F - Contract of service - Non-renewal of contract - If a workman's contract was for a specific period and purpose and has ended, then their disengagement does not count as retrenchment.
Complaining about non-compliance with Section 25-F is not necessary in such a case, and they are not entitled to any relief.
