The Haryana Transport Department (Group `C') Haryana Roadways Service Rules 1995, Rule 9 - The Haryana Transport Department (Group `B') Service Rules, 1992, Rule 9 - Promotion - Recruitment by promotion to the post of Superintendent from Statistical Assistant or Assistant - Eligibility - 12 years' experience - Only 12 years of experience as Statistical Assistants or Assistants in the Roadways can be counted towards promotion as Superintendent, not experience in other departments prior to joining Haryana Roadways.
