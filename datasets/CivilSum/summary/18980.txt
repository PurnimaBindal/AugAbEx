According to Section 27 of the Orissa Education Act, 1969 and Rule 1 of the Orissa Education (Recruitment and Conditions of Service of Teachers and Members of Staff of Aided Educational Institutions) Rules, 1974, the appointment to the post of Hindi teacher requires approval from the appropriate authority.
However, there is no evidence on record indicating whether there was a sanctioned post for a Hindi teacher at the school in question, nor is there evidence that the appointment of the petitioner was made in accordance with the rules and due process.
Additionally, there is no material to demonstrate that the petitioner's appointment was properly intimated to the appropriate authority for approval.
Finally, since the management of the school was not made a party to the proceedings, the impugned order is considered appropriate.
