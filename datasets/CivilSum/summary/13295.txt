Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 173 - Govt. of India, Instructions AO 46/80 Clause 2(b) - Disability Pension - Shelter appointment - The petitioner's disability was assessed to be between 8 to 10% at the time of his discharge for refusing to continue serving in the Low Medical Category.
As he declined to accept shelter appointment, he is not entitled to disability pension or shelter appointment.
All conditions specified under Regulation 173 must be satisfied cumulatively.
