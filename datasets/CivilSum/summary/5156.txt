Constitution of India, Articles 20(2), 311 and 226 - Rajasthan Civil Services (C.C.A.)
Rules, 1958, Rules 16 and 19 - Double Jeopardy - Embezzlement - Petitioner subjected to departmental enquiry and had one increment stopped - Later, the petitioner was convicted by a court for the same offence - Petitioner dismissed from service - Ruling determined that this does not constitute double punishment and is therefore not in violation of Article 20(2).
