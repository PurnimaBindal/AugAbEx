Haryana and Punjab Agricultural Universities Act, 1970, Section 15 - Punjab Agricultural University Statute, Clause 2 - Appointment of Vice Chancellor of the University - Procedure for appointment - The Board of University has the power and discretion to follow one of two procedures for appointing a Vice Chancellor: either finalize the name of a candidate themselves or delegate this task to a screening committee which may recommend at least three names to the Board for selection.
The Board is not required to follow any prescribed procedure and can select a candidate without one.
The appointment made by the Board cannot be invalidated due to the lack of a specific procedure.

Haryana and Punjab Agricultural Universities Act, 1970, Section 15 - Punjab Agriculture University Statute, Clause 2 - Appointment of Vice Chancellor of the University - Procedure for appointment - Agenda of the meeting of the Board - The meeting of the Board was not held without an agenda on the issue of appointment of VC The agenda item specified that in case the current Vice Chancellor resigns, the Board would consider appointing a successor.

Haryana and Punjab Agricultural Universities Act, 1970, Section 15(4) - Punjab Agriculture University Statute, Clause 2 - Appointment of Vice Chancellor of the University - Procedure for appointment - Process of appointment - The Board of University can initiate the process of appointing a Vice Chancellor even in advance when a vacancy is likely to occur.
If the Board received the resignation of the current Vice Chancellor and convened a meeting to consider this and also the names of potential successors, it is not considered a hasty decision.
