ROPA Rules, 1981 - Equal pay for equal work - Petitioners' claim for upgradation to scale 10 with others denied despite same qualifications - Exclusion based on discrimination without tangible reason cannot be policy decision within domain of court jurisdiction - Govt. must give same scale as was given to counterparts for same duration with all benefits (Para 12 and 15).
Equal pay for equal work - Comparison of responsibilities and duties of various posts left to experts - Recommendation based on job evaluation - Court cannot undertake complicated task - Further grant of scale is policy decision outside court's jurisdiction (Paras 3.2 & 7).
