Madhya Pradesh Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1966, Rule 10 - Retired employee - Disciplinary proceedings - Order for loss to State - Penalty not minor - Notices issued in April 2000 for shortage from 1977 to 1983 - Penalty imposed not minor according to Rule 10 as the Petitioner had retired - Enquiry is obligatory since accusations were not admitted - Notices issued in contravention of Rule 9(2)(b) which requires sanction of Governor for instituting proceedings against retired employee - DFO lacked jurisdiction in instituting proceedings - Order quashed.
