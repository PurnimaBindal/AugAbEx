Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Appointment - Post of Assistant Professor Neurology - Criteria for Eligibility - Three years teaching experience as Lecturer in Neurology – Ad hoc experience not to be considered - Minimum qualification for the Post of Lecturer Neurology as per the Medical Council of India is the degree in Doctor of Medicine Neurology - Petitioner doesn't meet the qualification - Therefore, ineligible for the post of Lecturer Neurology - Petition denied.
