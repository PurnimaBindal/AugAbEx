According to Sections 23 and 32 of the Workmen's Compensation Act and Rules 28 and 32 of the Workmen's Compensation Rules, 1962, the Commissioner does not have the authority to issue an interim award that orders the payment of an amount as an interim award.
The Act and the Rules do not provide for an interim award, instead, Rule 32 permits the issuance of a judgment.
The Act fixes liability under Section 3 and quantification under Section 4, in line with the provided schedule.
