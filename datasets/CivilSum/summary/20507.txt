The Constitution of India, Article 136 and the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(j) refer to industry.
A Tribunal found that Telecom Department is not considered an industry, but this finding was deemed improper by the Supreme Court in General Manager, Telecom v. A Srinivas Rao and others, 1997(8) SCC 767.
The Supreme Court held that the Telecom Department is an industry, and as such, the matter was remanded to Labour Court for fresh consideration.
