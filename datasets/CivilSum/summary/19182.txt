According to Section 2 of the Police Act, 1881 and Regulation 525 of the UP Police Regulations, a Police Constable can be transferred from the Civil Branch to the Armed Police by the Superintendent of Police as per prior authorisation by the Inspector General of Police.
A Constable who has served for 22 years in the Civil Branch cannot be exempted from transfer to the Armed Branch.
The Regulations empower the Superintendent of Police to transfer such Constables with the sanction of the Inspector General of Police.
