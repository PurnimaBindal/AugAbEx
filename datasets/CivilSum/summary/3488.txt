Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F and 25-F(a) - Workman - Retrenchment - Validity - Violation of Sections 25-F, 25-F(a) - The management is found to have issued a general notice to workmen stating that their services would cease 7 days from the date of publication, which is a violation of the mandatory provisions of Section 25-F(a) that requires a workman to be given one month's notice indicating the reasons for retrenchment and payment of wages for the notice period.
The notice provided by the management rendered the retrenchment illegal and violative of the provisions of Sections 25-F and 25-FA of the Act.
[Paras 11 to 13] B.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination held illegal - Payment of back wages - Workmen failed to plead that they were not gainfully employed during period of termination - The nature of the job performed by the workmen makes it difficult to believe they were sitting idle during the termination period.
As the management retrenched workmen after need-based assessment and on the direction of the Apex court, reinstating workmen would not be appropriate, given that 14 years have passed since the termination.
Instead, the interest of justice shall be met if lump sum compensation of 1 lakh is paid to each workman.
