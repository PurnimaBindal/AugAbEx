Factories Act, 1948, Section 46 - Contractual workmen - A government corporation running a factory canteen as per statutory requirement is permitted to run it through a contractor.
Employees of a contractor are treated as employees of the corporation for all purposes.
[Paras 6, 7 and 8]

Factories Act, 1948, Section 46 - Contractual workmen - A government corporation running a factory canteen as per statutory requirement may do so through a contractor.
The workmen of the contractor are treated as employees of the principal employer (Government Corporation).
According to a High Court order, the employees of the Contractor must be treated as employees of the corporation and their services must be regularised.
For the purpose of absorption, the age of the workman should be considered when they were initially engaged by the contractor, not from the date of the absorption order issued by the High Court.
[Paras 8, 9 and 10]

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25N, 25H and 25S - Retrenchment compensation - A government corporation engaged a contractor to run its factory canteen.
According to a High Court order, workers must be treated as employees of the corporation.
Such employees will be subject to Sections 25N, 25H, and 25S for retrenchment compensation.
[Para 14]
