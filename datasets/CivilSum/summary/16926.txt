The Constitution of India 1950, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation of service - The petitioner was denied regularisation of their services due to a two month break in service.
However, since this break was due to the illegal termination of the petitioner's service, directions were issued that the petitioner's case be considered for regularisation in accordance with policy, notwithstanding the objection regarding the break in service.
[Para 4]
Break in Service - If the break can be attributed to the petitioner, it cannot be used against them.
