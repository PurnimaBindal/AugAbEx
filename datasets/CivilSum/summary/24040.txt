Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Qualifications - A candidate was selected for the position of Assistant Professor in Neuro Surgery based on specific requirements found in the advertisement, which included preference being given to those with a degree in super speciality, research work, and working experience.
The appellant, who held a degree of M.Ch.
in the super-speciality of Neuro Surgery and possessed all the preferential qualifications, was chosen as the most meritorious selection by the Selection Committee and was placed at No. 1.
Although the High Court wrongly gave preference to a degree-holder in MS (GS), the appellant was correctly appointed as Assistant Professor.
