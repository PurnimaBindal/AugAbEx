Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) & (5), 239, 239 to 241 - Reservation in Union Territories - The right of migrants to Union Territories to be considered in reserved categories is acknowledged - The President of India administers the Union Territory through an administrator - The administrator has the power to make regulations and can repeal or amend any Act or law applicable to the territory with the same effect as an Act of Parliament - The administrator exercises the authority of Central Govt. under Article 239 - The benefit of reservation for Scheduled Castes or Scheduled Tribes recognized by the Union Territory can be extended without infringing on Article 16(4) - The UT of Pondicherry's policy of considering all SC/STs for reserved posts irrespective of their States is lawful - Selection for reserved posts for SC/STs, including migrants, is not violative of constitutional or other legal provisions - Judicial pronouncements on State policies cannot be applied to U.Ts.
- All SCs/STs mentioned in Presidential Orders or recognized for that purpose are entitled to consider for reserved posts.
