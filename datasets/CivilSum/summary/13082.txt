Petitioners who have been volunteers in Bihar Home Guards are seeking direction to be appointed as constables, but the number of positions available in the Police Force is limited, compared to the more than one lakh enrolled volunteers within the state of Bihar.
Since there are only a few permanent positions available, appointing volunteers would defeat the concept of voluntary service.
Thus, the court cannot grant the relief sought by the petitioners, according to Bihar Home Guards Act, 1947, Section 13(2) and Bihar Home Guard Rules, 1953, Rules 4, 5, 11, and 12.
