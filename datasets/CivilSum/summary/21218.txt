Constitution of India, Article 16 and 136 - Necessary party - Practice and procedure - If inter se seniority between the appellant and another employee of the department is questioned but that employee is not impleaded as a party, then the question cannot be examined in the absence of the affected/necessary party.
Therefore, it would be open to the appellant to approach the High Court and file a fresh writ petition impleading the affected parties.
The appeal is dismissed.
