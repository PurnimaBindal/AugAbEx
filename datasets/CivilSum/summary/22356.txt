Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 18(1) - Settlement - Binding effect of - Estoppel - Only parties to a settlement entered into by agreement are bound by it, not all the workers of the establishment who have neither signed nor accepted the same in writing - Sub-sections (1), (2) & (3) of Section 18 deal with different situations.
[Paras 7 to 9]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(k) and 10(1) - Industrial dispute - In order to constitute an industrial dispute, a claim must be raised between the two parties - The mere non-grant of a benefit for some reason does not qualify as an industrial dispute and no reference can be made under Section 10(1) by the government in such an absence - A High Court can quash a reference made in the absence of any industrial dispute.
