Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Representation - Consideration of representations - Petitioner requesting consideration of his plea for compassionate appointment based on Govt. instructions - The Competent Authority has a responsibility to make a decision on the request/representation, and thus the Court requires notice to the authority before issuing a writ of mandamus - Deciding on such demands may save significant resources - Respondents instructed to review the petitioner's case according to the instructions.
