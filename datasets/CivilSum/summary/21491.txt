Andhra Pradesh Civil Service (Executive Branch) Rules, 1992, Rule 22(ii)(e) and 33 - Seniority - Appointment to the post of Deputy Collectors - The rules state that 2/3 vacancies are filled through promotion and 1/3 through direct recruitment, with reservation for SC/ST categories.
Due to a shortage of ST candidates for promotion, 12 ST vacancies were carried forward to be filled through direct recruitment.
The Public Service Commission was unable to conduct the recruitment in time, resulting in the appointment of the contesting respondents after the general recruitment candidates.
They are not entitled to be placed above those appointed on 9.12.1993.
