Punjab Police Rules, Rule 16.2 and 16.2(1) - Dismissal from service - Unauthorised absence for more than 147 days - The concerned authority had applied its mind to the gravest nature of mis-conduct as well as incorrigibility - Unauthorised absence from duty is considered the gravest act of misconduct - Court cannot interfere in quantum of sentence.

Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Unreasonable delay - Court cannot interfere in its writ jurisdiction - Petitioner waited for five years to approach the court - Held - Unreasonable delay in approaching the court under its writ jurisdiction.
[Para 12]
Constitution of India, 1950, Article 226/227 - Court need not go by the language of order - May consider the matter in larger perspective by taking into account the record of the case - Non-application of mind plea can be made without strict adherence to the orders passed, but by considering official records produced during the hearing.
