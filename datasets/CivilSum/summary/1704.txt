Section 85-B of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948 and Regulations 31-A and 31-C of Employees State Insurance (General) Regulations, 1950 stipulate that employers are required to pay their employees' contributions.
In case of failure or delay in payment, the employer is liable to pay interest and/or damage for each day of delay.
Such delays are considered as `failure to pay' and damages are levied on specified rates.
Moreover, it has been held that the quantum of damage, which ranges from 5% to 25% for different defaults, is not unreasonable or excessive.
