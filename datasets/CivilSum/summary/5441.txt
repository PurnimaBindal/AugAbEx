According to Section 2(j) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, whether the Agriculture department is an industry or not depends on the functions discharged by it.
If there is no sufficient material on record to prove that it exclusively carries out sovereign functions, it is considered an industry within the definition of Section 2(j).

Sections 10 and 11-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 specify that delay in demand notice does not affect back wages.
In the case of a petitioner who previously filed a writ petition but was relegated to the remedy under the ID Act, full back wages cannot be denied.
[Para 10] C. Article 14 of the Constitution of India mandates natural justice in cases of recovery.
A Labour Court ruling directing the recovery of back wages from the pay of an officer without the officer being notified and given an opportunity is not valid.
The employer of the officer can issue a show cause notice and recover the amount after giving him an opportunity.
