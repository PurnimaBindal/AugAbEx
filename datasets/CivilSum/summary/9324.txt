Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Section 10 - Reinstatement - Back wages - The Labour Court granted the relief of reinstatement with continuity in service and ordered 50% back wages to be paid.
However, if the termination is found to be illegal, the workman is entitled to full back wages.
The normal rule for reinstatement is to also grant back wages, and any party objecting to it must provide evidence of circumstances requiring a departure from this rule.
