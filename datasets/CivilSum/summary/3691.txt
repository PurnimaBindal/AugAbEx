Education - Admission - Counseling for admission to Medical Courses - The absence of a register for candidates to mark their presence at counseling can allow candidates fighting litigation in court to claim they were present - Managements cannot allege that candidates were not present at counseling.
[Paras 17 & 24]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Admissions - Education - Providing reservations for the wards of employees under different names violates the constitution and ignores merit - This practice is illegal, unconstitutional, and contemptuous.
[Paras 18 to 20]
Education - Admissions of MBBS Courses - Cut off date - Admissions must be completed on or before the cut off date per Supreme Court directions - Shifting and transfers of already-admitted students before the cut off date do not affect the cut off date.
[Paras 23 to 25]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Natural justice - An independent government agency conducting an inquiry into fraud and irregularities in admissions, followed by another inquiry based on representations from affected persons, does not violate principles of natural justice - This amounts to substantial compliance with natural justice.
[Para 27]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Equity - Fraud in admissions to Medical Courses - Equity acts on conscience - Fraud vitiates everything - Both parties must have equity - There can be no equity in protecting or promoting fraud.
