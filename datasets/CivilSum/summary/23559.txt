According to the Bihar State University (Constituent Colleges) Service Commission Act, 1987, Sections 6 and 58(10) (as amended in 1993) and Bihar Inter University Board Act, 1981, Section 5(2), provisional promotion must be approved and is not automatic.
The eligible teacher's service records must be properly assessed by the competent Committee.
Temporary promotion is not valid for more than six months unless recommended by the College Service Commission, and if no recommendation is made, it cannot be taken into consideration for appointments to the Commission.
Appellants were given time-bound provisional promotion under a scheme, but without recommendation and approval under Section 58(10), they cannot be considered for appointment as members of the College Service Commission.
