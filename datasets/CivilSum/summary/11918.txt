Constitution of India, Article 16 - Transfer - It is essential to ensure that transfer orders are legitimately issued based on administrative power in the public interest, for administrative emergencies, and in the needs of public service - They must not be employed for objectives entirely unrelated to the above concerns or for settling individual scores among sponsors who assume civil servants under their wings - Transfers influenced by the weight of patrons that determined the fate of parties are not acceptable- The order challenged cannot be supported.
