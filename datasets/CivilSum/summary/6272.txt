University Non-teaching Employees (Terms and Conditions of Services) Rules, 1971 - Seniority - When the determination of seniority is challenged, the parties likely to be adversely affected do not need to be present - Their absence will not affect the acceptability of the writ.
[Para 13]

University Non-teaching Employees (Terms and Conditions of Services) Rules, 1971 - Promotion - Superintendent post - The Executive Council has the authority to establish service conditions, including those relating to promotion - Other needs fall under the domain of administrative policies and are not subject to judicial review unless found to be arbitrary, unfair, or unreasonable.
[Para 15]
