Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Dismissal - A constable was charged for damaging the police department's reputation by beating a student with a chappal and drinking alcohol.
The petitioner was not examined medically, and there was no direct or indirect evidence that they had consumed alcohol.
The disciplinary authority could not make a decision based on hearsay evidence, and the dismissal order was invalidated.
[Paras 3 and 7]
