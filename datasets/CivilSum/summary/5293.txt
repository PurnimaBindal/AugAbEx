Medical Council Act 1956, Sections 10A and 33 - Establishment of Medical Colleges Regulations 1999 - State cannot impose a condition for grant of Essentiality Certificate for Medical Colleges as it is completely denuded of its power to legislate in this matter.
The Parliament has made exhaustive provisions for establishing medical colleges.
Therefore, such action is ultra vires.
[Paras 26, 27 and 31] B.
Establishment of Medical Colleges Regulations, 1999 - Availability of infrastructural facility for medical college - Applicant seeking permission to establish a medical college must have a hospital with 300 beds with infrastructural facility to develop it into a teaching institution.
Respondent obtained an essentiality certificate even though they did not possess such a hospital.
Granting an essentiality certificate without meeting the necessary requirements is a violation of the 1999 Regulations and cannot be upheld.
