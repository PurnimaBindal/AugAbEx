Constitution of India, Articles 14, 311 and 226 - Disciplinary and Criminal proceedings - Departmental inquiry may be stayed if there is a likelihood of prejudice due to disclosure of defence and examination of the same witnesses - Allegations of corruption - Disciplinary and criminal proceedings serve different purposes and have different procedures - Disciplinary proceedings aim to remove bad elements from the administrative machinery, while criminal proceedings aim to punish guilty parties - Both proceedings may proceed at the same time unless the charges are the same and involve complicated legal or factual issues.
