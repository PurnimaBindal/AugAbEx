Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Service matter - Selection Process for post of non teaching member - An appointment of an external expert was challenged by a member of the appointing managing Committee - The managing Committee is the appropriate authority to make a decision for concluding the selection process, while the district inspector is to give approval - The court held that a member of managing committee cannot challenge its own decision in writ, and petitioners who are not personally aggrieved cannot challenge the decision of the managing committee on the ground of validity, propriety, or reasonableness - The petition was dismissed.
