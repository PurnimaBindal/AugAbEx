Constitution of India, Article 226 - Misconduct - Quantum of Punishment - Interference with - When reviewing disciplinary action under Article 226, the High Court cannot act as an appellate authority on merits.
Interference with the punishment imposed by the disciplinary authority can only take place if the High Court finds that the punishment is shockingly disproportionate to the gravity of the misconduct committed by the delinquent employee.
