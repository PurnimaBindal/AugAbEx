Article 136 of the Indian Constitution provides for departmental proceedings that can be challenged for delay and laches.
In this case, departmental inquiry was already underway and a charge sheet had been laid for prosecution of offences such as embezzlement and fabrication of false records.
However, the tribunal quashed the proceedings, citing that it would take a long time to detect the embezzlement and fabrication which should be done in secrecy.
This decision was deemed a grave error by the appellate forum, who set aside the order and allowed the department to continue the inquiry.
