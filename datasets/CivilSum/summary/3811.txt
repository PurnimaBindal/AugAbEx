According to Articles 14 & 16 of the Constitution of India, a civil servant can request a correction of their date of birth provided they have conclusive proof that their recorded date of birth is incorrect, or if a legitimate and bona fide error has occurred.
[Para 7]
The Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I, Part I, Chapter II and the Punjab Civil Services (First amendment) Rules, Vol.
I, Part I, 1994 stipulate that an employee can apply for a change of date of birth within two years from the amendment's enforcement.
Even if the amendment is subsequently withdrawn, this does not restrict the correction of date of birth.
The decision of the administrative authorities granting or rejecting a request for date of birth alteration may be challenged in a competent Court, as administrative law does not prohibit the jurisdiction of Civil Courts.
