Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 33(2)(b) and 10(1) - Termination of services of workman during 'pendency of Industrial Dispute' - High Court's interim stay of reference order of appropriate Government - Complaint U/s 33(2)(b) not maintainable as no 'industrial dispute or proceedings pending before the Labour Court/Tribunal' due to stay of reference - Workmen may seek other remedies under the Act but not U/s 33(2) - With stay of reference order, Tribunal lacks jurisdiction to entertain application U/s 33(2)(b).
