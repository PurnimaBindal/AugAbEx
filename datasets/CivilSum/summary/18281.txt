Punjab Civil Services, Rules, Volume II, Rule 25 - Termination of service - Grant of retiral benefits - Appellant claims entitlement to retiral benefits as per Rule 25 since service was terminated and not dismissed - However, Rule 25 specifies that no pension is payable to an employee dismissed or removed for misconduct, insolvency, or inefficiency - As the appellant's absence of 9 years without leave is not denied, the use of the word "termination" in their case disqualifies them from receiving pension benefits.
