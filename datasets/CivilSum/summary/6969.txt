Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A, Schedule 2 Item 6 - Termination - Ground of mis-appropriation - A Front Office Assistant who took money from a guest to engage a taxi and misappropriated some amount was terminated as it was not logged in the book - Even though the guest and taxi driver were not examined, the complaint was backed by other documentary and oral evidence - The loss of confidence in the employee was the primary factor considered for termination.
[Paras 31, 36, 40 to 42]
