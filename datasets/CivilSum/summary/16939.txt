Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Natural justice - Back wages - Reinstatement - If the disciplinary authority did not provide reasons for disagreeing with the findings of the enquiry officer who had exonerated the delinquent, and termination orders are overturned, the delinquent is not entirely relieved of the accusations.
In such a scenario 'no work no pay' doctrine would be valid for the delinquent to reject his entitlement to back wages.
