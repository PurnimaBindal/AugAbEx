Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part 1, Rule 25, Note 1 - Evidence Act, 1972, Sections 32(5) & 35 - Date of birth - Correction of - Horoscope - Although it is an accepted practice to use horoscopes to prove age, it is considered weak and unreliable in most cases.
The age of an individual as stated in the admission register of the school is more authentic and reliable unless it can be disproven.
[Paras 9 to 13]

Civil Procedure Code, Section 100 - Substantial question of law - In second appeal, ignoring relevant materials while relying on irrelevant ones, like a horoscope over an authentic school record, constitutes a substantial question of law.
[Paras 12 to 14]
