Jammu and Kashmir Constitution, Article 126 - Jammu and Kashmir Civil Service Regulations, 1956, Article 207 - Jammu and Kashmir Government Order dated 8101963 - The service conditions of a Government undertaking cannot be changed by an executive order, nor can a company take over a government undertaking without following the proper procedure, as appointment to a post or Government Office cannot be determined by consent or orders but by statute or statutory rules.
Article 207 of 1956 Regulations deals with the conditions of grant of pension and the consequences of a permanent post being abolished, It is erroneous to say that an order under Article 89 of Articles of Association of the Company that has no statutory force could abolish a permanent post.
Government servants governed by 1956 Regulations still hold the same status and have not become employees of the Company.
[Para 17 to 22]
Instructions- Government Orders under Article 89 of Articles of Association of the Company- Government Orders under Article 89 only consist of instructions that have no legal force, so they cannot bind the employees or abolish the posts of a government undertaking.
