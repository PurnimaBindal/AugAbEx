Pension Regulations under Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1980, Regulations 28, 29 - Pension Scheme included in 1995 - Plaintiff did not opt for pension but continued to be CPF optee and later opted for VRS - Bank issued circular before acceptance of application, stating that eligible employees opting for voluntary retirement would also receive benefits of CPF, Pension, gratuity and leave encashment - Plaintiff denied pension - Court held that since petitioner did not exercise option, he continued with pensionary benefits under CPF, making him ineligible for pension either under Regulation 28 or 29 - VRS did not independently provide for pension; it was for workforce rationalization - Therefore, the question of plaintiff or any other VRS optee who had not opted for pension in the first instance of 1995 cannot arise.
