Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-B - Payment of full wages to workman filing proceedings in higher courts - The employer's liability to pay full wages has to be enforced through the Court, and the employer is not obliged to pay without orders of the Court.
Unless the workman satisfies the Court that he was not employed elsewhere and claims it, no order can be granted.
[Para 4] B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Relief can be granted under Section 33-C(2) only when an existing right is shown.
The employer had disputed the workman's entitlement on account of certain laws and facts, and this question requires determination.
There are no grounds to vacate the interim order since nothing was shown to indicate that the workmen were entitled under any award or anything that is not disputed.
