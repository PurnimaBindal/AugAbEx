According to the Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 9(2)(a) and (b) under Visakhapatnam Port Employees (Classification, Control and Appeal) Regulations, 1969, departmental enquiries can be initiated after retirement within four years of the events that took place.
Therefore, the claim made by the petitioner that the proceedings initiated after retirement were illegal is not valid.
The objective of rules 9(2)(a) and 9(2)(b) is to enable the continuation of departmental proceedings to recover any charges causing financial loss or involving defalcation of the amount from the pensionary benefits or from the pension of the delinquent officer.
As a result, the writ petition has been dismissed.
