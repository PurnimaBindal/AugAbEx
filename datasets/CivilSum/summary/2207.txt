Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Disciplinary Proceedings - Labour Courts have jurisdiction - A charge of misconduct against workmen in relation to a gherao of MD and Production Manager was established with evidence - The disciplinary proceeding's findings were reasonable - The Labour Court lacked the power to interfere with the findings of misconduct and exceeded its jurisdiction - The Labour Court's order was overruled.
