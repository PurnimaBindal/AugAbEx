Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act regarding wages and equal pay for equal work stipulates that when a petitioner is reinstated to the same status from which he was previously thrown out of service, he is entitled to the same wages as those paid to other workers of the same status at that time, even if there have been any changes in wages during the intervening period.
Furthermore, he is entitled to any enhancements in wages, including all allowances available to that post.
[Paras 5 and 6]
According to Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947 and Sections 18 and 19 of the Apprenticeship Act, apprentices are classified as trainees and not workers.
Thus, the definition of workman as outlined in the Apprenticeship Act is applicable.
If an apprenticeship period expires and the trainee continues in service, he becomes a workman.
