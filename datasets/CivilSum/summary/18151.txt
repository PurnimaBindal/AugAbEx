Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Appointment - Selection of petitioner as casual labourer through Special Recruitment Drive from amongst SC category - Police verification and medical examination already completed - Army HQ cites non-grant of sanction as reason for not issuing appointment letter - However, petitioner was selected through Employment Exchange where requisitions are sent after posts are sanctioned - Army HQ's letter does not accurately reflect situation, as petitioner was selected despite their claim that no one was available - Court directs petitioner's appointment with seniority and other benefits, except for arrears of pay and allowances.
[Paras 5, 8, and 9]
