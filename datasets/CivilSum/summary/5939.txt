Bye-Law 32 of the Haryana State Industrial Development Corporation Employees Service Bye-Laws requires the Corporation to give permanent employees three months notice or pay in lieu thereof upon termination, but this clause is deemed arbitrary and violative of Article 14 of the Constitution as per AIR 1986 SC 1571 followed.
In Para 5, it is stated that the Haryana State Industrial Development Corporation qualifies as a state under Article 12 of the Constitution.
