Article 226 of the Constitution of India, Sections 10 and 11 of the Post Graduate Institute of Medical Education & Research, Chandigarh Act, 1966, and Rule 7(3) of the Post Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, Chandigarh, Rules, 1967 govern the appointment of the Director of the Institute.
The Institute's President is authorized to create a broad-based Special Selection Committee tasked with recommending a candidate.
The committee's only function is to make a recommendation, and the appointment can only be made with the Central Government's approval of the Institute's recommendation.
The constitutionality of the committee is upheld.
