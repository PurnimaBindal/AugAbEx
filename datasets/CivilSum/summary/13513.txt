Constitution of India, Article 226 - Deputation - Writ jurisdiction - A deputationist does not have an indefeasible right to continue on deputation for an unlimited duration - Deputation is allowed for a maximum period of 4 years as per the Govt. Circular - In this case, the petitioner has already served on deputation for more than 8 years - A deputationist does not have any legal or fundamental right to be absorbed on the deputation post - Further, in case the deputationist is eventually ordered to be repatriated to their parent department, there is no basis for them to lodge a complaint.
