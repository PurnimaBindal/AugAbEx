The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 21, and 226, outlines compassionate appointment policies.
In accordance with State policy, only 5% of vacancies are reserved for compassionate appointment.
The authority cannot be directed to relax this non-statutory administrative policy, which is solely in the public interest.
Such appointments are meant to financially assist the bereaved family of a deceased Government servant and may deviate from Article 14's equality clause.
Therefore, policies limiting such appointments are justified.
