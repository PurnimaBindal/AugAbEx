Anganwadi workers engaged under welfare schemes and projects that follow no procedure for recruitment under any service rules framed under Art. 309 do not enjoy the same rights and protections as government servants under the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, since they do not hold any post under the State or perform any governmental functions.
Despite being subject to governmental control, they cannot be considered 'Government servants' and are not entitled to Art. 14 and 311 protections.
Thus, the Administrative Tribunal lacks jurisdiction to hear their cases.
