Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1)(d) - Reference - Settlement - Challenging the fairness of a settlement requires a separate industrial dispute - When the concerned workmen entered into settlements with the employer-transporters, the matter was brought to the attention of the Industrial Tribunal - However, both the Tribunal and the High Court concluded without evidence that the settlements were unfair - As a result, their orders were overturned.
