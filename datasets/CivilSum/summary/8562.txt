Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Selection post - Filling of vacancies - Post - Senior Assistants in District Scheduled Caste Co-operative Society Limited registered under the AP Cooperative Societies Act - Merit-cum-seniority basis must be used when filling the Senior Assistant post - The post of Senior Assistant cannot be considered a selection post - The temporary Engineering wings should retain their staff as long as the District Societies earn their expenses - The Corporation's claim that services of Engineering Wing employees cannot be regularised as a result of Engineering Wing's temporary status was rejected - Respondents were directed to regularise the service of the Engineering Wing from the date of appointment with all associated benefits, including promotion.
