According to Section 10 of the Medical Council Act of 1956, Government approval is required for Medical Colleges.
Any institution that fails to fulfill Section 10 conditions or does not allow inspection by authorities will be closed, and its students will be adjusted to other Colleges.
Both the Medical Council and the Authorities of Directorate of Health are directed to adjust the affected students in other Medical Colleges.
