The Entitlement Rules to Casualty Pensionary Awards to the Armed Forces Personnel, 1982, Rules 4, 5, 6, 9 and 14 state the provisions for disability pension.
The case pertains to a petitioner who developed 'Schizophrenia' while in service of Indian Navy.
The petitioner had a fit mental condition when he joined the service and was found behaving abnormally when he had completed 2 years of service.
Precipitation/hastening of the disease took place during service on the ship.
Although there is no clear finding that the disease was not aggravated/precipitated due to naval service, the petitioner's disability pension was rejected citing constitutional disease as the reason, without proper medical examination report.
Rule 9 states that in such cases, the claimant should be given the benefit of reasonable doubt more liberally in field/afloat service cases.
Hence, the petitioner is entitled to the pension.
