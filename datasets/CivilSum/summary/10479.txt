Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Adverse remarks - Non communication of - Direction to consider for promotion - The petitioner was deemed unfit for promotion as a result of adverse remarks that were not communicated to them.
A single judge concluded that such uncommunicated remarks could not be used against the petitioner.
However, there was no specific contention in the petition stating that uncommunicated remarks could not be taken into account.
There was a vague claim in the affidavit to that effect.
Even uncommunicated remarks can be taken into consideration.
Although promotion cannot be granted with retroactive effect, it can be awarded from the date of the actual promotion order.
The respondents are directed to reconsider the petitioner's case.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 - Res judicata - Promotion - An earlier writ petition directed the consideration of the petitioner's case for promotion but was deemed unfit and superseded.
The petitioner filed a subsequent writ petition against the supersession, which is not barred by the principle of res judicata.
