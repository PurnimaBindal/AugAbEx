According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, the appointment of a Superintendent of Sessions Court requires a recommendation from the District and Sessions Judge.
However, the District and Sessions Judge has no involvement in the actual selection and appointment process, except to forward eligible candidates to the High Court.
The High Court is solely responsible for selecting and appointing an eligible candidate as Superintendent, as it is a state cadre.
Therefore, the consideration of eligible candidates by the District and Sessions Judge does not fulfill the legal requirements of the relevant rules.
