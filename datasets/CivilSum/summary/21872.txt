Civil Procedure Code, Order 9, Rule 13 and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 deal with the setting aside of ex-parte award of Labour Court.
The Labour Court has the power of setting aside within a limited time frame of thirty days from the date of publication of the award which is before it becomes enforceable.
After this time period, the court becomes functus officio and loses jurisdiction in the matter.
