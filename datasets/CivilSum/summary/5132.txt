The Swatantrata Sainik Samman Pension Scheme, 1980, Paragraphs 4 and 9, provide freedom fighters with a pension.
In dealing with a freedom fighter's case, respondent authorities failed to understand the basic principles and objectives of the scheme to benefit sufferers in the freedom movement.
Despite sufficient evidence on record that the petitioner went underground and was declared an offender, the respondent should grant the pension to the petitioner as recommended by the State Government.
