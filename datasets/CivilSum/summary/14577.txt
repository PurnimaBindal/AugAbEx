The Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Regularisation of services/Casual labour - Number of working days - It is not fair to engage casual labour on stop gap, ad hoc or casual basis for a long period and not allowing them to complete 240 days in each year to qualify for the benefit of policy of regularisation - The practice of keeping causal labour for a prolonged period without regularising and absorbing them in regular establishments is discouraged in many judicial pronouncements - Senior casual labourers should be put on a gradation list and engaged against regular work so that they are entitled to be regularised early and absorbed in regular staff.
[Para 5]

The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation - Disputed facts - Adjudication of the matter is not possible in the writ jurisdiction when there is serious dispute regarding the facts as to whether the workers have actually worked for a particular number of days or not and the records are not before the court for a thorough study.
[Para 5]
