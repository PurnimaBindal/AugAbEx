When working as Deputy Commissioner of Police's Gunman, the respondent was found heavily drinking while on duty and wandering the market with a service revolver.
As per Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2(1), such an act amounts to Gravest misconduct, which is a dismissal from service.
[Para 9]
Punjab Police Rules, 1934, define the term 'Misconduct' with context, the delinquency in its performance, and its effect on the discipline and duty's nature.
It includes a singular act or repeated acts.
Furthermore, the word 'or' in the rule cannot be interpreted as 'and.'
