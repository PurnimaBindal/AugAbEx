According to Articles 226 and 14 of the Constitution of India, when an employee submits a resignation with the intent for it to be effective from a future date, the request remains incomplete until that date.
This means that employers are not allowed to relieve the employee before the date fixed by the employee.
If the employer chooses to accept the resignation before the intended date, the employee is still entitled to withdraw their request.
[Para 5]

As per Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, if an employee resigns with the intention of starting a legal practice and is subsequently relieved of their duties, they are not entitled to claim arrears of salary.
This is because the employer should not be burdened with the responsibility of paying the employee's salary while they are practicing as an advocate.
In this particular case, the appeal was partially accepted because the employee had been granted a license from the Bar Council and started legal practice.
