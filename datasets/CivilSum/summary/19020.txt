Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Maharishi Dayanand University Ordinance, Clause 6, Rule 11 - Examination - Re-appear - Petitioner was supposed to take reappear in two papers of 3rd semester examination of BE degree course.
However, after clearing one of the reappear subjects, he was informed that he only had to reappear in one subject.
Acting on this communication, he only took reappear in one paper.
Later, he was informed again that he had to take reappear in the 7th semester as he had not cleared the 3rd semester.
The University ordinance cannot permit the University to benefit from its own mistake and deprive the petitioner of one chance of reappear.
The respondents have been directed to allow the petitioner to take the examination of the 7th semester.
