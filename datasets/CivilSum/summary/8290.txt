The plaintiff's services were terminated orally, and the trial court ruled that the termination was illegal, ultra vires, and arbitrary.
The state's first appeal was dismissed, and the appellant argued that the respondent was not a regular employee but only a contractual one.
However, the department's notional breaks were deemed mala fide, and the respondent was found to be in continuous service from 22186 to 111187.
As a result, the plaintiff is entitled to benefit from seniority, regularisation, and continuity in service starting from 11111987.
