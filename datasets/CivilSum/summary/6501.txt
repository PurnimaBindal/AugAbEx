Constitution of India Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Cantonment Fund Servants Rules, 1937, Rules 5B (1 & 2), 8(2) and 53 - Termination - Natural Justice - Age limit - Rule of relaxation - Petitioners who belonged to Schedule Caste were terminated for being overage at the time of their appointment, despite not being granted relaxation as similarly situated teachers were - No show cause notice was given - The petitioners were selected and appointed as Assistant teachers after being sponsored by the employment exchange - The Board is empowered to relax age up to 30 years and in case of SC it can relax up to 5 years - The action of respondent was arbitrary, discriminatory and violative of principles of natural justice - The impugned order of termination is set aside.
[Paras 11, 14, 15 and 10]
