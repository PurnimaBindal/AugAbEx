Regional Rural Banks (Appointment and Promotion of Officers and other Employees) Rules, 1988, Rule 5 - Regional Rural Bank Act, 1976, Sections 17 and 29 - Promotion - Criteria of seniority-cum-merit - The bank has established a requirement for promotion where only those employees who secure 45 marks out of 60 shall be promoted.
It has been held that this circular does not refer to minimum marks being related to certain criteria, and there is no stipulation of obtaining minimum marks as a mandatory measure.
Prescribing a percentage of 75% as a measure to assess merit becomes a focus on merit-cum-seniority, rather than seniority-cum-merit, where a greater emphasis is on seniority although it is not the determinative factor.
Consequently, the appeal has been allowed.
