Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Reservation - Ex-Servicemen - Post in HCS (Executive Branch) and Allied Services - Haryana Government Instructions dated 6372 - Instructions dated 6372 applicable for filling posts of class I and class II reserved for ex-servicemen without reference to any competitive examination.
However, since posts in HCS (Executive Branch) and Allied Services are filled through competitive examination with interview, petitioner cannot demand applicability of instructions.
