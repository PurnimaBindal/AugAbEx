According to Sections 29 and 29A of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 and Section 9 of the Civil Procedure Code, 1908, the civil court does not have jurisdiction to execute an order of an erstwhile administrative tribunal.
The responsibility of executing such an order only lies with the tribunal itself.
The term "proceeding" mentioned under Section 29 must be interpreted with reference to the context where it occurs, and could mean a course of action for enforcing a legal right, such as the realisation of a judicial adjudication that has attained finality.
Although the term "proceeding" has not been specifically defined under Section 29, it is a comprehensive term.
