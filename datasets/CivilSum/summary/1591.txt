Legal Services Authority Act, 1987, Section 21 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 9 Rule 13 - An ex parte order by the permanent Lok Adalat of the High Court - The respondent did not appear, nor did they instruct their counsel, in response to the notice of the permanent Lok Adalat for the actual date - The orders of the Lok Adalat were passed as per rules and instructions of the respondent, and no fault can be attributed to the Court - The order of the Lok Adalat is upheld.
[Para 10]  B Legal Services Authority Act, 1987, Section 21 - Pension and Family Pension - The husband of the petitioner retired from Municipal Service and was entitled to pension as per his option under the rules - After his death, his widow was entitled to Family Pension under the scheme - It was the Committee's obligation to fulfil this obligation - Pure public servants have a civil right to retiral benefits, and this benefit also enures for the benefit of the family after the death of the public servant - If the Committee did not fulfil its obligation, Lok Adalat was right and justified in directing the Committee to pay the same within three months with 12% interest.
