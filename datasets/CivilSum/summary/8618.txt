Constitution of India, Article 14 - Admissions - Education - Admission form reached in time but there was no one to receive it, therefore, admission wrongly cancelled - No fault of the candidate - Petitioner's admission must be reinstated.
[Paras 13 and 14]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and laches - Education - One-month delay in filing writ petition after admission cancellation is not sufficient grounds to deny relief based on delay and laches.
