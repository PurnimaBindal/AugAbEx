Advocates Act, 1961, Section 35 - Misconduct - Punishment - Natural Justice - The appellant had his license to practice suspended for three months, but not for being found guilty of professional misconduct in response to a complaint made by the respondent.
Instead, punishment was given for allegedly making a wrong statement before the High Court regarding the dismissal in default of proceedings for professional misconduct.
The appellant was not given a due opportunity to be heard before punishment was given.
Additionally, the appellant was not notified that making a false statement before the High Court would be treated as an act of professional misconduct.
This failure to give notice has harmed the appellant's defense and resulted in a failure of justice.
The order of punishment must therefore be overturned.
