The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 and the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 25-F both apply to the termination of employment.
Whether or not the petitioner has been legally retrenched is a combination of a factual and legal issue that requires the examination of evidence submitted by the parties.
While the appropriate government must assess a worker's case to make sure it is not frivolous, it cannot independently resolve disputes.
That function is reserved for the Labour Court and Tribunal under the Industrial Disputes Act or similar legislation.
The respondent is instructed to refer the Industrial Disputes raised by the petitioner to the proper Labour Court for resolution.
