Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1958 - Overtime allowance - Petitioners received overtime allowance as the Shops Act was applicable to their godowns - No recovery can be made without allowing the petitioners to present their case - The benefit granted cannot be withdrawn without adhering to principles of natural justice - The respondents' actions breach natural justice principles and cannot be upheld - The order seeking to recover paid overtime allowance from petitioners is annulled.
