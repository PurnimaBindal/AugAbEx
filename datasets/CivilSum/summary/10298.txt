The petitioner argued that the charge sheet in the Railway Protection Force Rules, 1987, Rule 153(8) is inappropriate due to stigma, and that their right to defend by a person of their choosing was restricted, with relied-upon documents not delivered.
As such, the entire disciplinary proceedings, including the charge-sheet, have been set aside and quashed.
However, the authorities may initiate the proceedings once more, starting from the stage at which they were quashed.
