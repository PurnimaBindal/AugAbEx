Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Recognition of qualifications - Qualifications/Selection - Appointment - The petitioner, a Hindi Teacher selected by the Service Selection Board, Punjab, was denied an appointment letter due to a diploma in OT from Gandhi Hindi Vidyapeeth, Prayag (UP) which was not recognised by the Punjab State.
However, since the Punjab State had already appointed a number of individuals with the same qualifications, this amounts to deemed recognition of that diploma.
