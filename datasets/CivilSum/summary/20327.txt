Constitution of India, Article 16 - Railway Services Pension Rules, Chapter III, Rule 22 - Qualifying service for pension - The petitioner/respondent served under the Government of Orisa for 10 years, under the Government of Chandigarh for 3 years, and in Railways for 11 years, making for a total service of 24 years, 10 months, and 21 days.
Despite being given benefit for the purpose of retiral benefits and allowed pension etc., he was denied complimentary railway passes on retirement on the grounds that his service with the Railway was less than 20 years.
His application for complimentary Railway passes was eventually allowed.
[Paras 3 and 5] B.
Railway Servants (Pass) Rules, 1986, Rule 2(h) and Schedule IV under Rule 8 - A person must be a member of the service of Railway and have 20 years of service on the railways to be eligible for the post of complimentary pass.
Since the respondent only served the railway for 11 years, he does not qualify for complimentary railway passes upon retirement.
