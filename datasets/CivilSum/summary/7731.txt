The prosecution failed to present any relevant evidence or witnesses during the departmental enquiry for defalcation charges against the delinquent, resulting in a violation of natural justice.
Moreover, the delinquent was not given an opportunity to cross-examine or show cause, making the punishment order unsustainable.

The punishing authority relied on the past record of the delinquent without framing any charges or providing an opportunity to explain, which violated the principles of natural justice.
Since the order was solely based on the past record, it cannot be sustained.
