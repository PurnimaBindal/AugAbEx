Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Community certificate - Disciplinary Proceedings - Suspension - Allegation of securing appointment on basis of false community certificate - Suspension placed on petitioner due to criminal investigation by police after respondents failed to refer matter to District Collector - Initiating departmental proceedings based solely on own officers' enquiry of community certificate verification deemed unsafe - Respondents may only initiate disciplinary proceedings after cancellation of community certificate by District Collector - Order of suspension nullified, but does not prevent respondents from requesting enquiry and report from District Collector.
[Paras 13 and 15]
