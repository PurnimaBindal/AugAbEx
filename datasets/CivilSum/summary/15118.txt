Constitution of India, Article 226 - Discharge - The petitioner was appointed as a temporary Reception Officer in Haryana Vidhan Sabha for 89 days.
However, his services were dispensed with during the period of probation on account of unsatisfactory work and misconduct.
He also failed to report to work and left without taking leave from the authorities.
The Speaker, who was the competent authority, formed an opinion based on relevant material to discharge the petitioner during the probation period.
The order of discharge was held to be proper.
