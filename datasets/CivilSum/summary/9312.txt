The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 2(ra) and Section 25F - Unfair Labour Practice/Contract of Service/Retrenchment - Non-renewal of contract - Non-renewal of employment contract cannot be deemed unfair in cases where work continues; employee must prove that the employer intended to deprive them of benefits and engaged in unfair labour practices.

Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(oo) and 25F - Retrenchment/Contract of Service/Non-renewal of contract of service - Workman engaged for a fixed period - Further extension for another fixed period - Termination of service for non-renewal of the contract is not covered within the ambit of retrenchment - Provisions of Section 25F in such cases would not apply.
