Bar Council of India, Rules, 1975, Rule 1(1)(c) - Advocates Act, 1961, Section 7(i), Section 24(1)(c)(iii) and Section 49(1)(d) - Enrolment as Advocate - Qualifications - Non collegiate LLB degree - Ineligibility of non-collegiate LLB degree holders for enrolment as Advocates - The requirements of Rule 1(1)(c) are not in opposition to the provisions of the Act but rather amplify them - The vires of Rule 1(1)(c) regarding the provision of the Act are in favor of the rule - Rule 1(1)(c) requires completion of a law course with regular attendance at college recognized by a University - Private and non-collegiate degrees as well as degrees from unrecognised colleges are not qualified for enrolment.
