According to Section 2A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, if a union withdraws its support for an individual worker's case, the dispute cannot be considered non-existent under the law.
The deeming effect given to a workman under section 2A of the Act applies to the entire duration of the dispute, irrespective of the support of the Union.
[Para 6] B.
If the cause of action is in personem, even if a workman or union represents it, it cannot be considered a collective dispute.
The impact of a collective dispute must be applicable in rem.
