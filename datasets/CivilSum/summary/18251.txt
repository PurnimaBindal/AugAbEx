Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300-A - Voluntary retirement - Retiral benefits - Pension - Discrimination - Petitioner, who was sent on a deputation to Corporation, was absorbed by the Corporation but chose voluntary retirement - The petitioner should not lose the right to a pension - Petition was filed over a year after retirement, but it is not hindered by delay or latches - The denial of pension is considered as a continuous cause of action - The petitioner is entitled to a pension at the same rate as those in a similar position - Corporation should be directed to release full pension and other retiral benefits to the petitioner.
