According to Section 10 of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, labourers engaged for work of a non-notified establishment under the Act, such as a Municipal Council, cannot claim regularisation.
This is especially true for contract labourers who were appointed directly by the Council and whose work was of a casual nature, lacking documentation indicating otherwise.
