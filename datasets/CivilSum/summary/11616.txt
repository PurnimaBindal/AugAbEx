SBI officers Service Rules, Rule 47 - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - Challenged - Petitioner challenged his order of transfer under the transfer policy and alleged mala fide - Vague argument made to show mala fide - Petitioner claimed son is mentally retarded under paragraph 35 of the Transfer Policy - Respondents argued the transfer was due to administrative exigencies and son was married and not dependent - Rule 47 allows for the transfer of any officer to any place - The court held that the son of the Petitioner is not dependent and transfer was due to administrative reasons - No mala fide was found as the Petitioner did not provide specific allegations - Therefore, the Petition was dismissed.
