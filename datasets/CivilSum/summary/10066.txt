Punjab State Electricity Board Services of Engineers (Civil) Regulation Rules, 1965, Rules 7-a(i) & (ii) and 9 - Time bound higher pay scale - PSEB Finance Circular dated 24.5.1990 for the grant of next higher pay scale after 9 and 16 years service to AEs/AEEs - The circular dated 24.5.1990 applies to diploma holder AEs as well, as they will be eligible for the next higher scale after 9/16 years service, regardless of their mode of induction to the cadre or their degree qualifications.
Promotees are considered as direct recruits and are not required to possess degree/AMIE qualifications.
