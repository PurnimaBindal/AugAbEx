Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(4) - Industrial Disputes Central Rules, 1957, Rule 61(2) - Order of being 'protected workmen' - Whether applicable retrospectively - Employer failed to comply with mandatory requirement of responding within 15 days period as per Rule 61(2) of 1957 Rules - Labour Court granted respondent 'protected workman' status from application date - Meanwhile, respondent's termination of service held - Decision states that since employer didn't reply to request under Section 61(2) within 15 days, workman can be considered as having protected status after such period - Labour court's order upheld.
