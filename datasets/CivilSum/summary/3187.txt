Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 38 - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 54(14) (b) - Family pension - Definition of family for an unmarried Govt. employee - Rule that excludes father and mother from the definition of "family" is unreasonable - Govt. servant has a duty to support and maintain his aged parents - Neglect of parents is considered to be misconduct - Parents of a deceased unmarried Govt. servant should be entitled to family pension in the absence of other family members - Rule needs to be amended suitably - The petitioner's father should receive Family Pension within two months with arrears.
