Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Screening committee - Short-listing - Post of Lecturer - University established a Screening Committee to shortlist candidates for the post of Lecturer - Screening Committee increased the academic qualifications to reduce the number of candidates and meet the viability criterion - Petitioner did not meet the required standard - Petitioner did not identify any procedural irregularity or illegality during the selection process - Petitioner's request to participate in the interview cannot be entertained.
