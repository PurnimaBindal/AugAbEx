Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 19(1)(b) - Contempt - Sentence - Remitting of - Changed circumstances - The appellant had been sentenced to pay a fine of Rs. 2,000/- and to undergo detention in a civil prison for 15 days for having committed Civil Contempt.
The appellant had not paid dues of its employees and willfully flouted interim orders passed by the school tribunal.
At the time of sentencing, she was 70 years old and now, at the age of 83 years, she has pleaded for remittance of the sentence of detention in civil prison.
Considering the change in circumstances, the sentence is remitted.
