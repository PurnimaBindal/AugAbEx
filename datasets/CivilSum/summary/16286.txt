The petitioner in Punjab Civil Service Rules Volume - I, Part-I, Rule 3.26(d) - Civil Services Rules Volume-II Rule 5.32 (as applicable to the State of Haryana) requested for early retirement based on instructions that mandated good or above reports for 70% of the last 10 years' service.
However, the instructions cannot be enforced after KK Vaid's case, and the order has been overturned.
