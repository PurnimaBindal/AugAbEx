Constitution of India, 1950 Article, 226 - Compassionate Appointment - Petitioner requests compassionate appointment in lieu of her deceased husband - Petitioner's husband was suspended after a criminal case was registered against him, but the charges were later dropped upon his death in custody - Petitioner claims entitlement to compassionate appointment, citing Para 13 of the scheme - The scheme allows the bank to make such appointments at its discretion, but prior concurrence of the Central Government is required in cases where the deceased was involved in criminal or disciplinary proceedings - Petitioner's request was declined by the Central Government - Petitioner received retiral benefits and monthly pension - Court rules that relief cannot be granted, as the family is capable of meeting the financial crisis - Petition dismissed accordingly.
