The Industrial Disputes Act 1947 under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, Section 10 states that an award by the Labour Court ordering the reinstatement of a workman with full back wages without resolving the management's objections on matters such as delay, employee-employer relationships and the fixed term of appointment is invalid when there is no proof that the workman's appointment was valid.
The Tribunal's decision seems to be based on no factual evidence, hence the award is quashed.
Finally, the case has been referred back to the Labour Court for reconsideration, with both parties receiving sufficient opportunities to present their points.
