Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Classifying pensioners by setting a cut off date on changes to the pension scheme - The classification can only be between pensioners and non-pensioners - Those who retired before the pension scheme was in effect will not receive any pension even after the scheme is changed without specific provisions - However, there can be no acceptable classification for implementing an increase in pension through amendments - All current pensioners will be entitled to the same benefits from the increase.
[Paras 9 & 10]
