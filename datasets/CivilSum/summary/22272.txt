Constitution of India, Articles 15(3) and 16 - Reservation of posts for women - Article 15(3) supports making special provision for women in employment or posts under the State - This power is not affected by Article 16 - The State may use affirmative action or reservation to enhance women's involvement in activities under its supervision.
[Paras 6, 7 and 8]
Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservation - A separate quota for a special category of persons is typical in reservation policies - Reservation is intended to safeguard the weak category against competition from the open category candidates.

Constitution of India, Articles 15, 16 and 309 - A.P.
State and Subordinate Service Rules, Rule 22-A - Rule 22-A of the A.P.
State and Subordinate Service Rules that mandates 30% preference for women in direct recruitment is valid and reasonable - It does not violate Article 15(3), 16(2) or 16(4).
