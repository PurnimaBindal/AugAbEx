A.Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 616-B(iii) - Gratuity - Mother's claim - Even if the widow has not made a claim, the mother of the deceased employee cannot be denied her gratuity claim.
[Para 7]
B.Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 27 - Ex-gratia grant - Mother's claim - According to the annexure list in Rule 27, the mother of the deceased employee is entitled to claim ex gratia grant.
[Para 8]
C.Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rules 616-B(iii) and 2.7 - Indian Succession Act, 1925, Section 381 - Succession certificate - If a succession certificate has been issued by a court after giving notice to the general public under Section 381 of the Succession Act, 1925, then the Service Rules authorities cannot disregard the certificate even if their own Rules specify eligible persons.
[Para 7]
