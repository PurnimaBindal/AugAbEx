Notories Act, 1952, Section 5(2)(as amended) - Notaries Rules, 1956, Rule 8(4-A) Second proviso - Renewal of Notary Certificate - The renewal of Notary Certificate cannot be rejected based on the number of Notaries appointed before May 9, 2001.
The quota fixed by the state is only applicable for fresh applications received after that date.
However, the certificates issued prior to that stand saved by sub-rule (4-A) itself.
