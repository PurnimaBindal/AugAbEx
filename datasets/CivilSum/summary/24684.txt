Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - U.P.
Municipalities Act, 1916, Section 71 - Natural Justice - No one should be condemned without hearing as it is an elementary principle of natural justice - The right to hold the post of Tax Inspector was conferred upon the appellant by the order of appointment, which cannot be taken away without providing an opportunity of hearing - Any order that violates principles of natural justice is void.
