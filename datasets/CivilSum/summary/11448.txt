Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pension - Qualifying Service - For counting qualifying service for pension, the service rendered by a deceased employee on work charge basis prior to regularisation must be added - An affidavit given by deceased employee that a loan taken by him be adjusted from EPF does not mean that he had given up his right to pension - The widow is ready and willing to deposit the remaining amount of EPF with interest - It is concluded that pensionary benefits should not be denied to an employee based on technicalities - The court has issued directions to release pensionary benefits to the employee's widow.
