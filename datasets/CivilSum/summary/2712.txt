In order for the appropriate government to have jurisdiction to refer a dispute to a Tribunal/Labour Court to adjudicate, a workman must show that his individual dispute for regularization was sponsored or espoused by a Union of Workmen as per the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 2A, 2K.
Without evidence of espousal, the Tribunal loses its jurisdiction to adjudicate the dispute and the award is set aside.
[Paras 10, 13, 14 and 18]
