The respondents' pension was withheld due to recovery of damages for unauthorised occupation of railway accommodation.
However, Rule 9 of CCS Rules requires compliance with a prescribed procedure before pension can be withheld.
The 1971 Act provides a procedure for recovering assessed damages.
The tribunal quashed the impugned order, stating that damages assessed by the estate officer can be recovered in accordance with law.
Therefore, the application was dismissed.
