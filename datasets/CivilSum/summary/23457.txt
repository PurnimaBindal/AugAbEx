The Central Administrative Tribunals (Salaries and Allowances and Conditions of Service of Chairman, Vice - Chairman and Members) Rules, 1985, Rules 3 and 8 state that pension of a Member of Tribunal re-employed after seeking retirement from the post of Senior District Judge to Member of Central Administrative Tribunal cannot club two services for the purpose of fixation of pension.
The appointment as a judicial member of the CAT is independent judicial service.
Appointment in the CAT is on tenure basis governed by Rule 8 of the Rules of 1985.
Therefore, re-employment as a Member of the CAT after retirement as Senior District Judge from the State Judicial Service is not a re-appointment on a post in connection with the affairs of Union Government.
Consequently, the Central Civil Service (Fixation of Pay of Re-employed Pensioners) Order, 1986 is not attracted.
