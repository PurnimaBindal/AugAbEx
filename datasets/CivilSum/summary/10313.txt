"As per Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, gainful employment is not presumed for a workman who was able to subsist along with his family during an idle period.
It is the employer's responsibility to prove and plead that the workman had gainful employment.
One cannot assume that a man who is supported by his father can be considered as gainfully employed.
In a specific case, a workman held by his father was not deemed gainfully employed, and therefore he is entitled to full back wages instead of being limited to 25%.
[Paras 4, 5 and 6]"
