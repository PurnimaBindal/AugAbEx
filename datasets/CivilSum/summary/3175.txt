Constitution of India, Articles 311, 300 and 226 - Maharashtra Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1982, Rule 27(2)(b) - Pension - Departmental proceedings after retirement - Initiation or continuation of proceedings is only possible on service of charge sheet - Rule states that charges cannot relate to more than four years prior to retirement and must be issued within six months of retirement - Allegations against petitioner are from 1975-77, but retired in 1986 with no charge sheet issued within four years of retirement - Withholding of pension and provision of provisional pension is unfounded - Unfair for department to continue with proceedings on old allegations in 1977 letter - Delay unreasonably long and unexplained - Petition allowed with costs of Rs. 5,000/-.
