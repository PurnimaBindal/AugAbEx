Allahabad Bank (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976, Regulations 4, 5(1), 6, 7, 8 and Schedule - Disciplinary proceedings - Penalty - Dismissal from service of the Bank - The Assistant General Manager of the Patna Division could pass a penalty order against the respondent in question, who was transferred during the disciplinary proceedings.
Both the Deputy General Manager and Assistant General Manager are competent disciplinary authorities who can initiate and finalize disciplinary proceedings for officers of different scales.
The High Court's opinion that the change in disciplinary authority would negatively impact the delinquent's right to appeal was found to be erroneous.
The court quashed the High Court's order.
