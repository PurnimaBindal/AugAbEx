The petitioner applied for acceptance into the Voluntary Retirement Scheme and opted to receive a refund of their contributions, including interest.
Clause 6 of the scheme allows for withdrawal within 7 days of its closure.
The petitioner's request to withdraw was made on 23.12.04, well after the deadline of October 7, 2004.
The court held that the corporation was not bound to accept the withdrawal request under the VRS and that the petitioner had already received the credit balance even after their withdrawal application.
Therefore, the doctrine of estoppel applies, and the court cannot rewrite clearly worded terms of the contract.
