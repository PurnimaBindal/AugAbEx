The petitioner had been transferred four times except for the impugned order, which was the only actual transfer.
Hence, it can't be considered as a case of frequent/successive transfers under Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 5]
The validity of a transfer order under Article 16 of the Constitution of India depends on whether it mentions public interest or administrative exigency.
If the order lacks such a mention, the government needs to satisfy the court that those conditions are fulfilled, but it won't make the order invalid automatically.
