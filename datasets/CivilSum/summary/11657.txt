According to the Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1977, Section 4(6) and the Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 33(5), an employee who is detained in police or judicial custody will be suspended, but the suspension will end once the employee is released.
The Education Officer's sanction cannot extend the period of suspension beyond what is specified in the rule.
