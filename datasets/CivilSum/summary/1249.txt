Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 311(2) - Banking Staff Bipartite Settlement, Clause 19.3(c) - Criminal and disciplinary proceedings - Benefit of doubt - If acquitted in a criminal proceeding due to lack of evidence, the mention of 'benefit of doubt' is unnecessary and meaningless - This type of acquittal is an honorable acquittal and should be thoroughly considered by the punishing authority to determine if the charge is supported by any incriminating evidence - An employee cannot be punished solely based on the observation of 'benefit of doubt' made by the criminal court or inquiry officer without any foundation.
[Paras 7 to 20]
