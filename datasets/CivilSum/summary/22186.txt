The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2 item 6 and Punjab Agricultural University Act, 1961, Section 26 relate to voluntary retirement for an individual who has held a Professor position and lost the confidence of the University.
The qualifying service period for voluntary retirement eligibility is 20 years and in this particular case, the computation of the qualifying period includes the period during which the appellant was deemed to be in service by the Single Bench.
However, the appellant is not entitled to any back wages during this period, but it will be treated as leave of kind due to the individual.
