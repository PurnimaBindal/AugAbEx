The Haryana Municipal Service Integration Recruitment (Conditions of Service) Rules, 1982, in accordance with Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, require equal pay for equal work.
The role of Assistant pump operator was upgraded to the position of pump operator, and duly matched with increased pay.
Similarly situated tube well drivers/pump operators were also given the benefit of higher pay.
The Respondent is directed to provide the petitioner with the pay scale of a pump operator, starting from the date the post of Assistant Pump Operator was upgraded to that of a Pump Operator.
