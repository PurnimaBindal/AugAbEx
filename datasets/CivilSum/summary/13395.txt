Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Punjab Naib Tehsildars Class III Service Rules, 1984 - Transfer - A member of Punjab Naib Tehsildar Service challenges respondent No. 2's authority to transfer his services outside his cadre and non-payment of salary as violative of his fundamental right - The transfer is considered as termination - The order placing services at the disposal of Director, Consolidation of Holdings is found arbitrary and is liable to be set aside.
