Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I Part I, Rule 7.3(A)-(3) - Dismissal, removal or Compulsory retirement from service - Setting aside of - Gap in service - An employee who has had such an order set aside by the court on merits would be treated as on duty for all purposes entitled to full pay and allowances for the intervening period.
The principle of "No work no pay" cannot be applied in situations where the employer is the one who deprived the employee of their duty.
