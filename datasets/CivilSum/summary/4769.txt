Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections (18(1), 25-N, 25-O - Settlement between workman and management - The settlement is void ab initio and against public policy as permission was not sought from the State Govt.
The compensation accepted by the workmen under the settlement will not preclude them from enforcing their legal and fundamental rights.
The settlement is not binding on workmen as it is violative of Sections 25-N and 25-O of the 1947 Act.
Compliance with the mandatory provisions of Section 25-O of the 1947 Act is necessary for a valid closure of the factory.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ by Union - The writ is maintainable on behalf of petitioners for enforcement of legal and fundamental rights.
Registration of the Union is not necessary for espousing the grievance of its members, in view of the law laid down by the Apex court in the Chairman SBI case.
