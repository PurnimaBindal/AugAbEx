Employees State Insurance Corporation (General) Regulation, 1950, Regulation 40 - Refund of contribution - If an employer remits contributions as per the rules, and the government exempts the establishment with retrospective effect, then the remittance made by the employer is not considered as 'under erroneous belief'.
However, the Insurance Court may award reasonable interest on the refund amount.
Regulation 40 applies only when a person makes a contribution on an erroneous belief that it was not payable by them.
