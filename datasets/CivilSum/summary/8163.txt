Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Dismissal - Reinstatement - Backwages - A labor court found that the workman charged fair from 13 passengers without providing them tickets and defrauded the employer of Rs. 2730 - Despite having given the workman ample opportunity during the inquiry, the Labor Court replaced the dismissal with a loss of 70% backwages and ordered reinstatement - The order of the Labor Court is maintained due to special circumstances, but the workman will not be entitled to any backwages based on the given charges.
