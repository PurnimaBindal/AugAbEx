Petitioner, a Brigadier in the Army, claimed entitlement to the rank of Lt. General, as officers in the Army are promoted through three stages known as Fresh cases, First review and Final review cases which involve two streams of promotion - Command Stream and Staff Stream.
Within a batch of officers promoted together, Command Stream officers are placed senior above Staff Stream in seniority.
Where officers of different batches are promoted together, those promoted from earlier batches are placed senior and maintain that seniority.
Petitioner belonged to the Staff Stream and was promoted in the 1956 batch, but was placed below officers from the Command Stream promoted in the 1957 batch.
The principle of batch-wise seniority between officers promoted together requires petitioner to be placed above those promoted from the Command Stream in 1957, and entitled to be selected as Lt. General from the due date.
