Admission/Concealment of facts - Cancellation of candidature due to concealment of facts - The petitioner failed to disclose during 10+2 admission that she had not passed 10+1.
The admission was mistakenly granted since it went undetected at the appropriate stage.
The conditions of eligibility clearly mentioned 10+1 requisites including qualifications.
At the time of seeking admission, the candidate has presumed knowledge about admission.
The Board cannot be blamed for not bringing it to her notice.
A student bears the responsibility of admission.
She cannot put the blame of her own calculated wrong upon the authority and ask for relief, therefore.
[Paras 3 to 6] B.
Regular courses - Open School Scheme - One could take admission to 10+2 only by passing the regular course of 10+1.
Candidates who pass regular courses are eligible for 10+2 admissions, but those who pass 10+1 from the Open School Scheme are not eligible as Open School Scheme is not equal to Regular courses.
