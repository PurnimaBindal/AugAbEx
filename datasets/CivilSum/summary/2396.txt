The petitioner was convicted of violating Army Headquarters' purchase procedure by making a local purchase of 3140 cakes of soap toilet, which could only be procured by the unit through the Canteen Store Department.
Despite the petitioner's defense that the material was purchased at a lower price, the General Court Martial found the charge proved.
The sentence was reviewed by the confirming authority and a post-confirmation petition was dismissed by the government.
The Court found no issues with the Court Martial proceedings and dismissed the writ petition.
[Paras 12 and 13] B The Court in a Writ Petition cannot act as an Appellate Authority or interfere with punishment imposed unless the punishment is shockingly disproportionate to the charge.
