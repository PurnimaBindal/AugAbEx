Constitution of India, Article 14 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 80 - Limitation/Void order/Termination of services - Orders imposing punishment contrary to mandatory provisions of law or natural justice are considered void - Such orders may be disregarded by the Government Servant, who is then entitled to claim his back wages - However, the claim for back wages beyond 38 months is barred by limitation.
