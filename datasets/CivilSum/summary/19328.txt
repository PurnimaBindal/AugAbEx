Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Reservation - Posts of Carpenter Instructors - Although no post was reserved for the backward class, one post was reserved for Scheduled Caste and one for Ex-Servicemen out of four advertised posts - Even though the petitioner was at No. 2 in the merit list, he would be considered as a general category candidate as there was no reservation for backward class - The respondents cannot deny appointment to the petitioner and persons at No. 3 and 4 on the ground that recommendations were made against the backward class category as there was no reservation in that category - The respondents are directed to appoint the petitioner to an available post of Carpenter Instructor or any post that may become available immediately after the person at Sr. No. 1 is appointed.
[Paras 4 and 5]
