Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 18 - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 4 - Payment of Gratuity Rules, 1973 - Gratuity - Voluntary retirement - The Gratuity is typically given only once on retirement, based on the employee's salary on that date.
If an employee has already received their gratuity based on their salary at retirement, the transaction is considered complete and cannot be reopened later for persons who retire subsequently unless there are provisions allowing for retrospective enhancement.
