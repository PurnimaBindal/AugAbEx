Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Civil Services Rules (Volume-II), Rule 3.17(A) - Pension - The petitioner, who was convicted and dismissed from service, cannot count his service prior to dismissal for pensionary benefits because removal from service due to misconduct results in complete forfeiture of past service.
The petitioner, who was re-employed, is not eligible for such benefits.
[Paras 5 and 6]
