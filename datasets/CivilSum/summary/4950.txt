Constitution of India, Article 226 - Seniority - Seniority List - Although some parties may not be joined, the writ petition can still be maintained if their cause is appropriately presented before the Court - The dismissal of the petition by the Tribunal on the basis of non-joinder of parties, especially considering the petitioner's statement that no relief is sought against certain individuals on the seniority list, is not reasonable - The Tribunal is instructed to render a finding on the merits, with reference to JT 1996(2) SC 374.
