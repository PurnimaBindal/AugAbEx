Constitution of India, Article 226 - Backwages - Reinstatement - If a termination order is overturned due to a lack of natural justice, Article 226 dictates that the court should not grant backwages and the worker should instead seek redress under Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, which will allow the employer to make the case that the worker gained employment during the period of termination - The right of the employer cannot be infringed upon by providing backwages in a writ petition.
