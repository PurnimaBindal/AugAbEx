Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - The Selection Committee appointed the petitioner as the Principal of Private Aided College (Respondent 4), and while the University had given their approval, no official order had been passed even after the petitioner completed their probation.
The petitioner was informed that their services could not continue without approval from Respondent 3.
However, Chapter XX of the Punjabi University Calendar, Volume III Part II Rule 4 does not require Respondent 3's approval as a condition precedent to a valid appointment.
The University cannot use the non-communication of Respondent 3's approval as a reason to treat the appointment as irregular, and the presence of Respondent 3 in the Selection Committee should be treated as his approval.
Therefore, taking any adverse action against the petitioner would be unwarranted, and the petition is allowed.
