Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 16 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I, Part I, Rules 51 and 55 - Petitioner employees receiving excess House Rent Allowance due to their shared accommodation with their husbands - Petitioners directed to repay the excess amount as HRA is meant to compensate for actual expenses and not act as a source of profit.
[Para 2 and 8] The petitioners have no rights to claim HRA as they have not hired any accommodations nor are they paying any rent.
As such, the claim cannot be sustained.
