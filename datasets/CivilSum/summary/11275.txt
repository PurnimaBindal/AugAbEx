Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Principles of Natural Justice - Violation - The petitioner argued that the decision made by a different person/authority than the one who granted the opportunity of hearing is a violation of Natural Justice and Para No. 1914 of Bipartite Settlement between the bank and its workmen.
As a result, the impugned order is quashed due to non-compliance of Natural Justice and violation of Para 1914 of Bipartite Settlement.
The case is remitted to the Deputy General Manager (appellate authority) of Punjab National Bank, Zonal Office, Chandigarh, who will hear the petitioner personally with his counsel (if any) before reaching a decision and providing a well-reasoned explanation.
