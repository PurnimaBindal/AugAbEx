The West Bengal School Service Commission Act, 1997, and Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India discuss transfer policies for teachers.
The district inspector of schools has the power to approve group transfers of teachers in question.
However, for a writ petition seeking mandamus, the petitioner must be a person who has suffered harm.
The petitioner in this case cannot be classified as such based on the order from the DIOS, and it appears that she is advocating for a public cause.
The decision of DOPS dated 472004 was challenged in February 2006, but the petition is vulnerable to dismissal on the grounds of an unreasonable delay.
