According to Constitution of India, Article 226, respondents 2 and 4 were found to possess requisite qualifications and experience for the post of Education Officer.
In fact, their academic qualifications were deemed superior to the petitioner's qualifications, as they had secured first division in their masters degree, while the petitioner obtained only a second division in their master's degree.
The petition was ultimately dismissed as no infirmities were pointed out in the selection process.
[Paras 14 and 15]

The petitioner filed a writ petition challenging the selection process for the post of Education Officer after almost 5 years of delay.
No explanation was provided for this delay, and respondents 2 to 4 were already appointed to the position and even promoted to Deputy Secretaries to the Govt.
Therefore, the delay in filing the petition cannot be condoned and was deemed to defeat equity.
The petition was dismissed.
[Para 13]

Delay in filing a petition may defeat equity.
