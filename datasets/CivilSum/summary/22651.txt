Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, Schedule 2 Item 3 - Domestic inquiry - Evidence - Unlike Civil Court yardstick, provisions of Evidence Act are not applicable to domestic inquiries, as logically probative material including hearsay can be considered.

Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, Section 11A, Schedule 2 Item 3 - Finding of Inquiry Officer - In a case where witnesses testified to the delinquent's confession without being cross-examined and no plea of duress was raised, interference by the Labour Court with the Inquiry Officer's finding of misconduct is inappropriate.
