Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Anomaly - Rectification of anomalies - Equal pay for equal work - Rectification of anomalies is the correction of a mistake made at an earlier stage and it always applies retrospectively from when the mistake occurred - Employees should receive arrears of pay in the corrected pay scale from that date - It should not be considered a revision of the pay scale.
[Paras 9 & 10]
