Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Factories Act, 1948, Sections 51 and 59 - Wages for overtime - Jurisdiction - The company denied the workman's claim for overtime wages by disputing their right to it - This claim was not incidental to a benefit claim and had no prior adjudication or recognition - Therefore, the Labour Court cannot determine the workman's right to overtime wages and the company's liability - Order of Labour Court is overturned.
