Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39(d) - Equal pay for equal work/Work charged employees/Pay scale/Equal duties - Rules provide pay scales without any differentiation, but gradation in service is permissible based on factors like length of service and qualifications.
However, discrimination on grounds of regular or work charge establishment is not allowed under Articles 14, 16 and 39(d) of the Constitution.
Different pay scales would violate the equality clause.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Bona fide pleading - Affidavit - Court found that a supplementary affidavit filed by JN Kochhar was false and misleading.
The NHPC Personnel Manual provides a grade of 550-900 for Grade II foreman and 650-1200 for Grade I foreman.
Kochhar had ignored his previous affidavit wherein he argued that work-charged employees were not entitled to senior grade of Rs. 650-1200.
Filing such a false affidavit was a grave error on the respondent's part.
