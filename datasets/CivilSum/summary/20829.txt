According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, promotion and retirement are addressed.
Respondent No. 1 was legitimately promoted and worked in the promoted position.
However, the position was later reserved for a specific group, and Respondent No. 1 is nearing retirement.
As a result, the respondents have been instructed to establish a supernumerary post to allow the first respondent to continue serving in the position until retirement, with all of the job's benefits.
