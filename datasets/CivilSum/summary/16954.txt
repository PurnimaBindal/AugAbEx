Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol-I, Part I, Rule 3.26(d) (as in Hy.)
- Punjab Police Rules, Rule 9.18(1) (as applicable to Hy.)
- Compulsory retirement - The concept of public interest is not defined in either of the Civil Services Rules or the Police Rules, and it is generally a fact-specific inquiry.
If a public servant is found to have engaged in corrupt activities such as accepting bribes and apologizes or admits to such conduct, it is against the public interest to continue their employment.
As a discipline, the police department wields significant power which must be honestly exercised, and therefore, a police officer found to be corrupt should be compulsorily retired in the public interest.
