Petitioner R Desabandhu, Advocate and respondent N Paul Vasanthakumar and S.T.S.
Murthy, Government Advocates cited Constitution of India, Article 311 and Tamil Nadu Recognised Private Schools (Regulations) Act, 1973, Section 22(3)(b) and argued against an ex-parte enquiry on the grounds that the petitioner had not abandoned the enquiry and that the Enquiry Officer failed to issue a notice to confirm.
A dispute arose over the competence of the Enquiry Officer to proceed with the enquiry, leading to the inquiry being concluded without deciding on the issue of competence and rendering the enquiry invalid.
