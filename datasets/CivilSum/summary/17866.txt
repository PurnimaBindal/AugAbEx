Ordinances and Outlines of Tests, Syllabi and Course of Reading for First, Second and Final Professional MBBS, 1991, Ordinances 11(b)(i) and 11(b)(iii) - Grace Marks - If a candidate fails in two subjects and subsequently re-appears, they are entitled to one percent of the marks in the subject they re-appeared in and not the total aggregate marks of all the subjects.
