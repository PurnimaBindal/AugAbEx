Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion/Reversion - Seniority - The departmental promotion committee erroneously considered the petitioner outside of the consideration zone due to a mistaken date of appointment as April 30, 1969.
However, the petitioner was actually appointed on April 30, 1970.
After correcting the mistake, the petitioner's promotion was reverted.
The petitioner cannot benefit from the Promotion Committee's mistake, and the petition was dismissed.
