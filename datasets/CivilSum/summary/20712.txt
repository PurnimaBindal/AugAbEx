Rajasthan Mines & Geological Service Rules, 1960, Column 6 - Promotion - Experience - Post of Assistant Mining Engineer - The appellant is claiming direct promotion to the post of Assistant Mining Engineer after acquiring an Engineering degree, but promotion to this position requires holding a post of Mines Foreman Grade I, Head Draftsman, or Senior Surveyor.
Since the appellant did not hold any such post, they cannot be promoted to the post of Assistant Mining Engineer.
[Paras 9 & 11]
Promotion - Eligibility and Experience function differently - A channel must exist for filling a post through promotion.
In the absence of a channel, promotion cannot take place.
[Para 10]
