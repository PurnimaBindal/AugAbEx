Kerala State Subordinate Service Rules, 1958, Rules 9(a)(1)(iii) and (e) - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation - Temporary appointment/Power to appoint - Rule applied only in cases of temporary appointments made during times of emergency - No emergency circumstances or temporary posts were expressed - Employees who continued for many years cannot be considered as emergency appointments - Past policies of the Government demonstrated a desire to regularize after two years - Consideration of factors such as length of service, termination of poor bread-winners, consequences on settled families, age barrier for future job applications - Ordered that those who had been in service for some time be regularized.

Constitution of India, Article 309 - Rules framed under Article 309 - Adoption of Rules - Statutory force of Rules extends only to employees for whom they were created - Rules adopted through a resolution by a different department do not carry statutory force for the employees of the department.
