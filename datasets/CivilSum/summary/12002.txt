According to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F, a workman who has worked for a short duration without appointment and did not comply with regulations does not have the right to continue in service, even after serving for 240 days.
In such cases, the award of back wages is set aside.
On reinstatement, the workman's status would be the same as it was before termination.
