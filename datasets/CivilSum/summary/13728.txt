Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Compassionate appointment - Bank - The employer authority tasked to protect the welfare of the family members of the deceased employee should not adopt a rigid formula when considering each case for a compassionate appointment - Refusal to grant a request for compassionate appointment on the basis of the meager amount of family pension of Rs. 812/- pm will be in violation of the principles of natural justice - Some practical approach should be taken by the authorities - Reconsideration has been directed.
