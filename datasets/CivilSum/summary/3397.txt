Jammu and Kashmir shops and Establishments Act, 1966, Sections 5, 11 and 48 - Over-time allowance - Govt. authorized to partially relax provisions for specific group of workers - Workers can be classified based on the nature and place of their duties for overtime allowance - Partial relaxation within govt's power if provision can be relaxed - Larger power includes ability to exercise smaller power - Justification for exemption from double wages for overtime work for employees in ports, godowns and depots compared to office workers due to unequal working conditions.
