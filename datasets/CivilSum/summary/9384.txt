Constitution of India Article 16 - Medical reimbursement - A retired Chief Justice of High Court was denied reimbursement for getting a cypher stent during Angioplasty as the prior approval or recommendation from a cardiologist at the Govt. hospital was not obtained.
The court held that it is the responsibility of the operating physician/surgeon to use their discretion during such emergencies and that they should not stop the process until formal approval is received.
The court deemed the authorities' hypertechnical and insensitive approach as unreasonable and directed them to make payment within 30 days.
[Paras 1 and 2]
