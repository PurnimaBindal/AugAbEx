The petitioner challenged the order of transfer for appointment to the post of Principal, since the post had been advertised for direct appointment.
However, it was found that Section 16 of the Commission Act allows for appointment by way of transfer, even if the post has been advertised but not filled by selection for a long time.
The commission is not disentitled to fill the post in any other permissible mode, and the right to appointment is available only if made by direct recruitment.
The petition was dismissed.
[Para 5] B.
The provisions of taking consent of two managing committees for transfer of teachers were made under pre-existing rules, and if amended to do away with such consent, no exception to that rule can be taken.
The managing committee has very limited power in the appointment process, with the commission having the primary role in making appointments and filling vacancies.
