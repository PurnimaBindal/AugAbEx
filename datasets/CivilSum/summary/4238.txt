Rajasthan Medical Service (Collegiate Branch) Rules, 1962, Rule 20 - Select list - Reserve list - RPSC must forward a merit and reserve list to the Govt. - If the Govt. requests names from the Reserve List within six months of the original list being sent, RPSC must recommend names from the Reserve List in order of merit - RPSC cannot decide whether vacancies are available for filling or not - This is the Govt.'s responsibility - Any challenge to the Govt.'s decision must be pursued through appropriate proceedings - RPSC's decision not to recommend a name from the Reserve List has been quashed - RPSC has been directed to send names of candidates on the Reserve List in response to the Govt.'s request.
