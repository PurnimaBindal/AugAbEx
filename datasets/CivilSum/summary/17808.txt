Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Postgraduate Institute of Medical Education and Research Chandigarh, Rules, 1967 - Equal pay for equal work - Scales of pay - Service jurisprudence recognizes the principle of equal pay for equal work, therefore, it is not permissible to discriminate among employees in the same cadre who are performing the same duties on the basis of their initial pay scale or whether their pay scale has been revised.
