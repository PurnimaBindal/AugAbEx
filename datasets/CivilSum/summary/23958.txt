Advocate's lien on litigation papers of his client for an unpaid fee amounts to "professional misconduct" under Section 35 of Advocates Act.
The Bar Council of India Rules do not cover any lien on the litigation files kept by an advocate till the payment of his fee by his client.
[Paras 13 to 20, 24, 25, 38, 41 and 42] B Refusal to return files to the client when he demands the same is misconduct under Section 35 of Advocates Act and makes the advocate liable to punishment.
[Para 24] C. The advocate cannot be inflicted with harsh punishment, if he holds a bona fide belief, on account of decisions of certain Courts, that he has a lien on the papers of the case of his client and refuses to return the same till he is paid his dues.
A reprimand held to be sufficient in the interest of justice.
