Article 16 of the Indian Constitution - Family Pension - The husband of the petitioner filled out an option form before his death.
Whether or not it was accepted by the appropriate authorities in time is irrelevant.
Thus, the respondents are directed to grant family pension to the petitioner.
[Paras 2 and 3] B.
Article 16 of the Indian Constitution - Family Pension - Interest - There is no valid justification for not giving the benefit to the petitioner.
The amount has been unnecessarily withheld for over 22 years.
Consequently, the petitioner should be paid interest at a rate of 10% on the amount due from the date of her husband's passing until the amount is paid.
The petitioner shall also be paid Rs. 10,000/- as a cost.
