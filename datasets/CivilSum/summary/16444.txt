Government employees do not have a guaranteed right to stay at a particular post and must comply with transfer orders, unless the order is mala fide or made by an incompetent authority.
[Para 5]
The petitioner raised a grievance about the promotion of another employee to the post of security officer, but their subsequent transfer order was made in the interest of administration and cannot be considered mala fide.
[Para 5]
The registrar, with the approval of the vice chancellor, passed the transfer order for the petitioner's auxiliary staff cadre post, making the petitioner's claim of an incompetent authority null and void.
