Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - All India Service Act, 1957 - Indian Police Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1955 - Selection - Post of IPS Officer - The first respondent held higher posts than Deputy Superintendent of Police but was not included in the select list of 1982 to 1991 due to a grading of "good."
The select committee failed to take into account the first respondent's higher posts in their grading.
The Tribunal was justified in directing the writ petitioner to reevaluate the first respondent's service according to the formula from the Full Bench decision in SS Sambhus's case.
