Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Provision for appointment on compassionate grounds - Permissible to appoint a dependent on compassionate grounds even if another son/dependent of the deceased employee is already employed by the Board - Official memorandum does not exclude compassionate appointment to a son of the deceased employee who was already employed by the Board.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and Laches - Appointment on compassionate grounds of dependent of deceased employee - Relief can be sought within five years from the date of death in accordance with the relevant official memorandum - If writ petition seeking relief filed within five years, it cannot be dismissed on grounds of delay and laches.
