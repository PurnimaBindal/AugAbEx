Education Rules, 1959 (Kerala), Chapter XXXI, Rules 1, 2 and 44A - Degree - Withdrawal of Approval - Validity of same - The Appellant argued that the Respondent was ineligible for appointment as Head Master due to the derecognition of the B.Ed degree course of Anna Malai University by Kerala universities.
However, the Respondent obtained the degree before it was invalidated by the University.
It was decided that the subsequent withdrawal of approval of a degree does not invalidate degrees awarded by a University earlier.
Therefore, the Respondent's appointment was deemed appropriate.
