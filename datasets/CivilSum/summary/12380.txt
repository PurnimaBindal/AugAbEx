The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25-F, deals with Termination and Reinstatement, along with Back Wages.
The petitioner challenged the award of the reinstatement of the respondent-workmen with full back wages, arguing that the award was based on the false statement of the petitioner's witnesses who claimed that the workmen had abandoned work.
The workmen had been working for over 15 years, and the termination amounted to retrenchment without complying with the provisions of Section 25-F As a result, the award was modified, and the respondents were ordered to be paid 75 per cent of the back wages.
