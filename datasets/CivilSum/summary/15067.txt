Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, Regulation 173 - Disability Pension - Despite having been operated upon for Acute Appendicitis and developing Broncho Oesophageal Fistula, the petitioner's claim for disability pension was rejected as not attributable to military service - Medical Board assessed his disability at 60% - However, the complications arising from surgery carried out upon petitioner in Army Hospital are certainly contributory to other diseases - Therefore, it cannot be definitely stated that said disease is neither attributable to nor aggravated by military service - Accordingly, respondents were directed to reevaluate the petitioner's case for granting of disability pension - Arrears recovery, however, be limited to a period of 3 years immediately preceding filing of petition.
