The Constitution of India, Article 16 - Reservation - Without any material evidence, it is improper to deny the appointment of a candidate who was selected for a post that was later designated as a reserved category position.
[Para 9] B The Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - If a candidate is placed on a select list to be appointed for a vacancy likely to come up in the near future, but a ban on appointments is placed before the vacancy occurs, the candidate is entitled to the appointment as soon as the ban is lifted.
