J&K Police Manual, Rule 400 - Special promotion - Out of turn promotion - Rule 400 specifies that special promotions will be considered only for those who have been awarded the President's or Police Medal.
However, there is an incentive policy in place to encourage local police to perform better in anti-militancy operations.
This policy provides out of turn promotions for consistent and exceptional performance on the military front.
The cases are to be considered by a DPC and then put forward for approval by the Home Minister through the Chief Secretary and approved by the Chief Minister.
This policy has been misused extensively, with promotions granted at the officers' level at their own discretion.
Despite the policy prohibiting such promotions for ministerial staff, even clerks and stenos have been given such promotions.
The deserving cases have not even been considered and recommended.
Directions have been issued to further process deserving cases.
[Paras 19, 20, 25 to 27 and 30]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - J&K Police Manual, Rule 400 - Judicial Review - Special promotion - Out of turn promotion - Rule 400 provides to consider for special promotion only those awarded the President's or Police Medal.
However, the out of turn promotions have been granted arbitrarily, ignoring the really deserving persons.
The Writ Court cannot order promotions itself.
It is only within the domain of DPC to assess the merits as per the policy and orders passed by the Competent Authority.
The Court can only interfere if there is totally unfair consideration and arbitrary exercise of power.
[Paras 28 and 29]
