The delay in conducting disciplinary proceedings is a violation of Articles 226 and 311 of the Constitution of India.
The charges in question date back to 1989, but the authorities failed to issue a chargesheet or complete an inquiry despite a directive from a 1995 writ petition.
The tribunal found that more than 14 years had passed since the alleged incident and the authorities had not yet decided to issue a charge memo.
As a result, the tribunal deemed the inquiry to be flawed, leading the court to dismiss the petition.
