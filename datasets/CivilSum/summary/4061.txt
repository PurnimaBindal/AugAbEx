Constitution of India, Article 14 - Appointment - Petitioners, claiming appointment as Urdu Teacher on the basis of possessing the prescribed qualification, argued that candidates with lesser qualification have been selected.
Official respondents failed to file a reply, and non-official respondents received an ex-parte ruling.
After reviewing decisions in SWP No. 500 of 87 and SWP No. 851 of 88, the court orders the respondents to appoint petitioners as teachers against available vacancies without disturbing the appointment of respondent teachers.
[Paras 10 & 11]
