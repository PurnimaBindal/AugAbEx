Constitution of India, Article 226 - Air Force Act, Sections 39 and 119 - Air Force Rules, 1969, Rules 39 and 43(2) - District Court Martial - Quashing of - The petitioner could not point out any violations of the provisions contained in the Air Force Act and Air Force Rules.
- The petitioner had a reasonable opportunity to prepare his defence and participated in court martial proceedings, cross-examining prosecution witnesses and presenting evidence in his defence.
- Therefore, no interference is necessary in the impugned order as natural justice had been served.
