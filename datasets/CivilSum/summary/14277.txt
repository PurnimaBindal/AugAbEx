Constitution of India, Article 226 - Mormugao Port Employees (Classification, Control and Appeal) Regulations, 1964, Regulations 9(ii) and 12 - Minor penalty - The penalty for withholding two annual increments with cumulative effect imposed after a departmental enquiry should not include the period of suspension as not on duty - The petitioner is entitled to full pay and allowances during the period of suspension - The order of the disciplinary authority is set aside as the period cannot be treated as not on duty.
