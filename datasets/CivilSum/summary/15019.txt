Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 21, and 226 - Syndicate Bank Special Leave Scheme, 2004, Clause E - Rejection of leave - The Bank rejected the appellant's leave request due to administrative exigencies, and similar special leave requests were denied to others on the same grounds - The Bank's reasons for denying special leave were disclosed and not irrelevant or unreasonable, and the Court will not interfere with the respondents' action as it was not arbitrary or illegal.
