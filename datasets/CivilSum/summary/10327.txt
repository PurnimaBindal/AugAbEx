Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 39(d) - Equal pay for equal work - The appellant-Corporation argued that the respondent, who was appointed part-time chowkidar, was not entitled to the pay-scale of a regular chowkidar - However, the trial court and first appellate court both found that the respondent was performing duties for 8 hours a day and thus entitled to the pay-scale - The duty register also supports the respondent's claim - The lower courts did not err in finding that the respondent is entitled to the pay-scale of a regular chowkidar.
