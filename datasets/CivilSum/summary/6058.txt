The employee who reached the 6th stage in terms of Rule 7 and Rule 15 is entitled to one additional increment in the revised pay scale.
The revised pay scales were designed to acknowledge both the existing pay and length of service.
If only actual earned increments were taken into account, the entire scheme would become redundant.
The bunching should be made on the basis of each four of the earned increments, and after that the stages in the existing scale needs to be considered.
[Paras 13 to 16] B.
According to Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 309, instructions should not be issued contrary to statutory rules/schemes.
Haryana Govt. instructions dt. 22.8.2003 that stated the Govt. employee will get one increment for every three `actually earned increments' in the pre-revised scale are invalid as they are contrary to the Revised Pay Rules, 1998 scheme.
