Before the introduction of Section 31-A in the Uttar Pradesh State Universities Act, 1973, teachers were appointed by direct recruitment and promotion from a lower teaching post to a higher teaching post was not allowed under Section 31 of the act.
Hence, teachers who were promoted under the personal promotion scheme before the approval of Section 31-A cannot be granted the benefit of seniority for that period.
Seniority would only count from the date of the promotion being legalized under Section 31-A [Paras 13, 14 and 16]
