Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Post of Graduate Teacher - Contention that having appeared in interview petitioner is estopped from raising plea of bias - Selected candidate related to one of members of Selection Committee and subject expert - Contention that no other subject expert was available in school - Held, plea of estoppel is not tenable because petitioner could not have known about relationship of a subject expert with selected candidate - Doctrine of necessity cannot be invoked in absence of sufficient material to show that despite all efforts other selection committee could not be constituted.
[Paras 6, 7, 8, 10]
