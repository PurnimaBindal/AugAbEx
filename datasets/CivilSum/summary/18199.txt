Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of Deceased Government Employees Rules, 2003 - Eligibility - Relevant date - The petitioner was denied compassionate appointment on the ground that the dependent had exceeded the upper age limit of 30 years - However, rules were amended on 17122004 and the relevant date for eligibility purposes should be the date of death of the deceased employee i.e. 14.3.2005 - Petitioner is now eligible for consideration of appointment.
