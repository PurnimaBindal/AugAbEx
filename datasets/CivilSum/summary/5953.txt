Under Section 14(1)(b) of the Specific Relief Act, 1963 and Article 311 of the Constitution of India, contract of special service cannot typically be enforced specifically, nor would a court grant a declaration of employee status - this claim is not covered under Article 311 or the Industrial Disputes Act.
Further, an employer is not legally bound to retain an employee who does not wish to remain employed.
