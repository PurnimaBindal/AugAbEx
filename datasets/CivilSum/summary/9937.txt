Constitution of India, Article 226 - Banking, Regulation Act, 1949, Section 45(1) - Amalgamation of Banks - Regularisation of services - The resultant bank is bound to absorb workers who were eligible as per the Industrial Tribunal's absorption award against one of the amalgamated banks - Recruitment from outside is allowed, but eligibility claims must be considered - Ineligible persons cannot claim absorption.
[Paras 29-33]
