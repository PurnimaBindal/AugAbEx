It is contended on behalf of the appellants that under Constitution of India, Article 311 and Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2(2), the finding of the Additional District Judge on the question of Limitation is patently illegal.
It is argued that the judgment should have examined the plaint to determine the cause of action and then determined the correct article of the Limitation Act applicable to the case.
Additionally, objections have been raised against the observations of the first appellate Court allowing the competent authority to inflict only minor punishment on the plaintiff in certain conditions and the direction given regarding the payment of salary and other emoluments.
The High Court is criticized for lightly brushing aside the question involved in this suit and the case is to be remitted for fresh disposal according to the law.
