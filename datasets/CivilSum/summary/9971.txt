The employer seeking permission under Section 33 of the Industrial Disputes Act after a disciplinary proceeding can't be rejected by the Conciliation Officer just because the suspension allowance was not paid to the suspended employee.
Before rejecting the permission, the Conciliation Officer is required to determine the quantum of compensation and the duration in which such compensation needs to be paid.
It is mandatory to pay subsistence allowance as per standing orders and if such orders are absent, the authority before whom the application is submitted should fix the amount and duration of allowance.
It is not permissible for the authority to reject the permission without determining the compensation and allowance.
