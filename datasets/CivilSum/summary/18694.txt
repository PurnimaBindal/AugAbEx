Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Education - Professional courses - MBBS/BDS - Change of courses or institutions - Prospectus issued - Clause 18 deems petitioner ineligible for admission - Denied admission despite passing entrance examination because of previous admission to BDS course - Participation in entrance test without objection - No entitlement to change course midway - Allowing such changes would result in wasted seats and disadvantage for others - Case dismissed.
