The petitioner in this case remained a part of the Employee's Provident Fund Scheme until retirement without any objection.
As such, it can be inferred that he did not opt for the pension scheme and therefore his claim is groundless.
Without evidence of having opted for the pension scheme, the petitioner is not entitled to it.
However, being governed by Punjab Civil Services Rules, the petitioner is entitled to gratuity as per these rules.
