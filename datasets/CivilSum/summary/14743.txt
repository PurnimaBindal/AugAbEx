The Conduct of Discipline and Appeal Rules, 1978 framed by Coal India Ltd, Rules 211, 247 and 248, Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - The Rule specifies that if an employee is detained exceeding 48 hours in a criminal case or otherwise, he would be deemed to be under suspension - Deemed suspension would end if the employee was released on bail - If the authority wished to keep the employee under suspension, it had to issue a fresh order.
