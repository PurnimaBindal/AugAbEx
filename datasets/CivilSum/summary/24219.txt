Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 6(2)(b)(bb) and (c) - Appointment of chairperson and vice chairperson of the Tribunal - Judicial and Administrative members - Those administrative members appointed from administrative services have specialized knowledge in administrative matters and judicial experience, therefore they are well-equipped to dispense speedy and efficient justice in administrative service matters - It's incorrect to suggest that only members from judicial background are competent to hold these posts - A judicious mix of judicial members and those with grass root experience would serve the purpose better - It will be not appropriate to hold that the appointment of Vice Chairman should be made only from the persons mentioned in Section 6(2)(a) - Provisions of section 6(2)(b)(bb) and (c) are not ultra vires.
