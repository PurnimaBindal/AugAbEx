According to Rule 2 of the Post and Telegraphs Extra-Departmental Agents (Conduct and Service) Rules, 1964, the appointment of respondent No. 4 on compassionate grounds was challenged because the requirement of local residence was relaxed.
Rules do not allow for the relaxation of local residence qualifications, so it was held that respondent No. 4's appointment was in violation of the rules.
The petitioner, who was provisionally appointed to the post of ED BPM by the Inspector of Post Office and met the eligibility requirements including local residential qualification, should be considered for the post instead.
The appointment of respondent No. 4 was quashed, and the respondents were directed to consider the petitioner for the appointment.
