Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Leave vacancy - Appointment against leave vacancy - Temporary post - Appointment against a leave vacancy is considered as temporary - Order of permanent appointment issued by management was corrected by the competent authority - This modification was not challenged and became final - The appointee against leave vacancy was not entitled to question the modified terms of her appointment when the regular incumbent of the post returned from leave - The order of the Tribunal setting aside the modification in the appeal of the regular appointee was held illegal and without jurisdiction - The petitioner was entitled to salary only for the period she remained unemployed and to count such period for pension and other retiral benefits if any in the changed circumstances relief moulded - However, the petitioner already joined another institution - The management may or may not relieve the appointee against leave vacancy.
