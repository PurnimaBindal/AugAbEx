Misconduct of short charging fare from 35 passengers led to the dismissal of the conductor in question after being found guilty of similar offenses 36 times.
The principle of res ipsa loquitur applied to the situation, where the act of charging less fare was either dishonest or grossly negligent.
The corporation decided that the respondent was unfit to be retained as a conductor and should not have set aside the dismissal.
Although the respondent has already superannuated, the order of reinstatement is not valid, and the respondent is not entitled to any back wages.
However, the respondent is entitled to retiral benefits.
