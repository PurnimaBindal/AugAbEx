Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Selection - The appellants knew the Committee members and the appointment would be through direct recruitment.
They participated in the interview and accepted the Committee's jurisdiction for the purpose of selection.
Upon not being selected, they filed writ petitions to challenge the selection.
However, they did not object to the Committee's composition during the interview and had a chance at being recommended.
Thus, they cannot now question the Committee's constitution.
