Constitution of India, Article 16 - Regularisation of services - Adhoc appointment on a consolidated compensation on contract basis for a limited period - The right to remain in post expires after the contractual period - The services of the respondent being continued on an 'ad hoc' basis for more than a year does not entitle her to regularisation - The order of the High Court to regularise her services is set aside.
[Paras 20, 23, 24]
