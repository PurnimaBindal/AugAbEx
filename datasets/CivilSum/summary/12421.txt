Constitution of India, Article 311 - Natural Justice - Absence from duty - Removal from service due to unauthorised absence from duty is considered as discharge from service and falls under Article 311, even if the Rule states automatic discharge for that ground - The person affected must be given a chance to explain why they were unable to join their duty during the alleged absence - An order passed without giving such an opportunity is a violation of the principles of natural justice as well as Article 311 of the Constitution.
