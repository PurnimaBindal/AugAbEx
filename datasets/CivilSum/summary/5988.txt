Bhim Sen Sehgal, Advocate argues for the petitioner and SK Pipat, Sr.
Standing Counsel, Union of India & Joginder Sharma represent the respondent.
The case revolves around Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 and Pension Regulation of Army Rules, 1961 (Part-I).
The petitioner, who was discharged from Army service due to his 40% disability as a result of "Sensory Neural Deafness", claims that this disease was attributed to his military service and became deaf while dealing with high thunder and loud voice equipment.
The petitioner alleges that other individuals suffering from the same disease were granted disability pension and that his case represents a hostile discrimination and offends Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
Consequently, the petitioner demands that the respondents grant him the disability pension along with arrears with an interest rate of @12% and costs of Rs. 5000/-.
