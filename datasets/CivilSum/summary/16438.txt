Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Railway Establishment Manual, Rule 303 - Departmental examination - Promotion by passing of departmental examination - Seniority - Length of service - Provisional appointment - Clearing compartments - Passing mean, after clearing compartments - If compartments are not cleared, provisional appointment will be terminated - Appointment cannot relate back to provisional appointment but will commence from the date of complete passing of the departmental examination - Seniority will commence from that date and candidate cannot be made senior to those fully passing departmental test in the first go in the same batch.
