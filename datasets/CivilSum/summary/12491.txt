For Respondent No. 1 :- V.N.
Lokur, Advocate.
A.
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Departmental Enquiry - Criminal proceedings/Acquittal in criminal case - Departmental proceedings cannot be held simultaneously with criminal proceedings on the same charges and with the same witnesses - Enquiry Officer failed to give weight to order of acquittal in criminal case - Enquiry report and subsequent dismissal of the petitioner is illegal - Petitioner must be reinstated with full back wages and benefits.
[Paras 1 to 3] B.
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Departmental Enquiry - Hearsay evidence - Hearsay evidence is not a substitute for direct evidence - It can only be admitted in departmental proceedings subject to scrutiny and with the ability to discredit its credibility - Inconsistent evidence cannot be considered as credible.
[Paras 4 and 5]
