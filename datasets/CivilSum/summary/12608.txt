As per Section 1(4) of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, it applies to establishments with twenty or more workers.
Section 2(e) defines the term "establishment".
The State Public Works Department deploys workers supplied by contractors at buildings in different locations to man the lifts.
Neither the department falls under the definition of an establishment nor is the contractor required to obtain registration under the Act.
Therefore, criminal proceedings instituted against the petitioners are unfounded and have been quashed.
