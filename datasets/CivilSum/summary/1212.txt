Army Act, 1950, Section 192 - Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, Regulation 173 - Disability pension - Army personnel on casual/annual leave - Medical Board assessed petitioner's 60% disability caused by huge steel beam and cemented stone falling from a house's roof - Held, that petitioner was not performing any wrongdoings and the accident was beyond his control - As per Apex Court's settled law, a person on casual/annual leave is deemed to be on duty and there must be a visible link between normal living of person subject to military law while on leave and injuries suffered by him - As leave is discretionary of authorities, a person on annual leave is bound by Army Act and can be recalled at any time - Respondents instructed to consider and grant disability pension to petitioner.
