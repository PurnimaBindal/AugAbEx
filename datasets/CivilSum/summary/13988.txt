Constitution of India, Article 21 - Army Act, 1950, Section 192 - Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 2 - Disability Pension - Even though the appellant was boarded out of the army due to illness with medical category E and assessed at 100% disability, he was denied disability pension for years, which is a continuous wrong that violates Article 21 of the Constitution of India - The appellant is entitled to receive disability pension from the date he was discharged from service.
