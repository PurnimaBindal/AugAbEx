Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 28 - Jurisdiction - Service matters - The High Court does not have jurisdiction over service matters referred to in Section 28 of the Administrative Tribunals Act - The High Court made a decision without jurisdiction even if the concerned party did not object - The Supreme Court can hear the objection for the first time - If approached within 15 days, matter referred back to Tribunal for adjudication afresh - Tribunal instructed to disregard question of limitation.
[Paras 3 and 5]
