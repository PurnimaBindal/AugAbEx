The Petitioner-Company contested an order of PF Commissioner, which stated that the establishment was covered by section 2-A of EPF Act, 1952, from its inception and was not entitled to a infancy period benefit under the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 2-A The material that was on record was insufficient to show the functional integrality between the two companies.
The Petitioner company was not dependent on the other company, and if the other company closed down, the former would be able to survive alone.
Therefore, the Petitioner company could not be covered under section 2-A of the Act and would be entitled to infancy benefit of three years.
