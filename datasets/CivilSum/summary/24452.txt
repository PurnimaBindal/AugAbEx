Constitution of India, Article 226 - Interim orders - Enforcement of statute - Court-issued interim stays do not automatically delay the implementation of statutory provisions challenged in litigation post-dismissal of writ petitions - In other words, irrespective of the interim stay, the provisions will apply retroactively.
[Para 5] B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ of mandamus - Direction - Court mandates should be specific to avoid misinterpretation by authorities and unnecessary adjudication of legally or factually invalid claims - If claims are time-barred, the court should not instruct the authority to consider them.
