Constitution of India, Article 136 - Limitation Act, 1963, Section 14(1), Articles 58, 113 - Removal from service - Appeal - Limitation - Appellant, who was terminated from their banking job, filed appeals with Local Board and under Section 41(2) of the Tamil Nadu Shops and Establishments Act, 1947, both of which were dismissed - The Supreme Court upheld an earlier judgment, which found the Tamil Nadu Shops and Establishments Act, 1947, was not applicable to nationalized banks - Appellant subsequently filed a regular suit for a declaration that their removal from service was illegal, ultra vires and invalid - Held that the proceedings before the Deputy Commissioner of Labour (Appeals) constituted under Section 41(2) of the Tamil Nadu Shops and Establishments, Act, 1947, to hear and decide the appeal, were of a civil nature - Period of time from date of appeal under Section 41(2) until dismissal had to be excluded for computing the period of limitation for filing the suit for declaration.
