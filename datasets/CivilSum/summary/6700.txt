Homeopathy Central Council Act, 1973, Sections 15(1) and (2) 21(1), Schedules 2nd and 3rd - Homeopathic Physician - Eligibility for - Recognized qualifications - Inclusion in State-Register - A person can be included in the State-Register if they possess any of the qualifications listed in the 2nd or 3rd schedule of the Act.
No other qualifications are necessary.
If a person has a recognized medical qualification and their name is in the State-Register, they can be appointed as a Homeopathic Physician in Govt. service.

Homeopathy Central Council Act, 1973, Section 15(1) and (2) and 21(1), Schedules 2nd and 3rd - Homeopathic Physician - Eligibility for - Recognized qualifications - The Four Years diploma awarded by the Rajasthan or Punjab Council of Homeopathy System has been recognized by State of Punjab as a valid qualification for appointment to the Govt. service.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Homeopathy Central Council Act, 1973, Sections 15(1) and (2) and 21(1), Schedules 2nd and 3rd - Selection for Homeopathic Physician - Eligibility for - Recognized qualifications - Candidates participating in the selection process cannot challenge the validity of prescribed qualifications.
If they have an issue with the validity of qualifications, they should challenge it prior to participating in the selection process.
[Para 16]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Homeopathy Central Council Act, 1973, Sections 15(1) and (2) and 21(1), Schedules 2nd and 3rd - Selection for Homeopathic Physician - Criteria for selection by interview only - Waitage for higher qualifications and experience - Selecting authority need not sub-divide total marks under different heads if the selection is based on interview alone.
Marks of interview need not be restricted within the maximum limit prescribed by Supreme Court in Ashok Kumar Yadav's case.
[Para 19]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Homeopathy Central Council Act, 1973, Sections 15(1) and (2) and 21(1), Schedules 2nd and 3rd - Selection for Homeopathic Physician - Criteria for selection by interview only - Waitage for higher qualifications and experience - Marks should ideally be awarded for higher qualifications, experience, or any meritorious achievement of candidates.
However, if no marks are awarded for a particular event to all the candidates as a uniform criteria, that does not render the entire selection process arbitrary or invalid.
