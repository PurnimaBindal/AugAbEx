Pension Regulations for the Army, Rule 183 - Constitution of India, Article 16 - A member serving in the Army who has a disability below 20% is not eligible for disability pension, but may receive service pension.
This was established in the case of Sigmn Amarjit Singh v. Union of India, Civil Writ Petition No. 12311 of 1996, decided on 2721997.
