Army Rules, Rule 13(3)(III)(v) - Discharge from service - Punishment - Rule 13(3)(III)(v) of Army Rules allows for simplicitor discharge without punitive action.
Discharge under this rule is different from dismissal from service.
[Para 6]

Army Rules, Rules 13(3)(III)(v) and 74 - Discharge from service - Punishment - Rule 13(3)(III)(v) of Army Rules, along with a policy letter from August 14, 1984, are meant to remove undisciplined individuals from Army Service who refuse to improve despite repeated warnings and punishments.
[Paras 8 & 10]

Army Rules, Rules 13(3)(III)(v) and 74 - Discharge from service - Punishments under Rules 13(3)(III)(v) and 74 of Army Rules do not constitute double jeopardy.
Army personnel who repeatedly engage in undisciplined behavior and become habitual offenders must be removed in the Army's interest to maintain discipline.
[Paras 11 to 13]
