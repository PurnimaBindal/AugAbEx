While activities that fulfill Directive Principles of State Policy are considered welfare activities, they do not qualify for exemption from the Industrial Disputes Act.
Activities of the government or statutory bodies are not exempt either, so the plea that the Board's activities are in pursuance of Directive Principles of State Police has no bearing.
