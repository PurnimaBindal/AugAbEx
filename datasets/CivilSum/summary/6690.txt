Govt. employees are not eligible for House Rent Allowance (HRA) if they live in a government allotted accommodation that has been given to their spouse or parents/child as per Punjab Govt. letter No. 956-FCW-65/1548 dated 20265, according to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
HRA cannot be withdrawn by either spouse if one of them has been given Govt. accommodation.
[Paras 12 and 13]
Even if Govt. employees are not eligible, if they withdraw HRA, the amount can be recovered from them in monthly instalments in accordance with Govt. instructions of 1965, as per Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 16]
