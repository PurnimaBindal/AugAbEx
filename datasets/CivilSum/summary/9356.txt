State Bank of Hyderabad (Officers) Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 68(2)(xii) - Appointing Authority - Jurisdiction - On the date the dismissal order was passed, the Appointing authority passing the order was legal.
Even if the appointment was made by a different authority, the order still stands legal.
