Constitution of India, Article 30(1) - Minority institution - A school established by "Arya Samaj" which is a part of the Hindu religion cannot be considered as an institution established by a minority community - thus not entitled to protection under Article 30.
Even if the State Government had declared it as a minority institution in 1953, based on the relevant material on record, the Court can declare that it is not a minority institution.
