The jurisdiction of civil courts to deal with service matters has not been taken away by Rule 34 of CCS (CCA) Rules and therefore the Ld.
Single judge of the High Court has been held to have failed to exercise his jurisdiction by non-suiting the appellant on the grounds that a declaration U/s 34 of the Specific Relief Act cannot be made in service matters of Central Civil Servants due to Rule 24 of CCA Rules - case remanded for further proceedings.
[Paras 5 and 6]
When it is found that an appellant did not exhaust departmental remedies before approaching the court, the court should either dispose of the matter on its merits or direct the employee to the remedy instead of non-suiting the appellant at the appellate stage after a long litigation.
