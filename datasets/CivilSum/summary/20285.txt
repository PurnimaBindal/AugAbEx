According to the Andhra Pradesh Deputy Superintendents of Jails Service Rules, 1974, Rule 4, probation is only deemed when there is a substantive appointment, not an officiating or ad hoc appointment.
Such appointments cannot be taken as deemed probation, as per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 &16.
[Para 8]

If no eligible departmental candidate is available for promotion to the posts of Deputy Suptt.
Jails, direct recruitment may be initiated per the Andhra Pradesh Deputy Superintendents of Jails Service Rules, 1974, Rule 4.
The mere officiating appointment of a departmental candidate until the recommendations of the Service Selection Commission will not create any right to the post for regular promotion to the post meant for direct recruits.
Such posts go out of the reach of the authority competent to promote and fall within the jurisdiction of the Commission, according to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16.
