According to Rule 7(b) of the Army Regulations from 1961, disability pension may be given to soldiers who suffer from disabilities that are attributable to their military service.
The respondents were unable to prove that the Petitioner's disability was not due to military service, and the Petitioner was able to demonstrate that the disability did not exist before entering service, with the Medical Board acknowledging this fact.
As a result, the Petitioner is entitled to a disability pension of 9% per annum.
