Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-B - Challenge to award of reinstatement with backwages - Management must provide specific details regarding gainful employment of workman in order to meet requirements of Section 17-B - Failure to do so leads to workman being entitled to benefit under Section 17-B when award is challenged and stay is obtained - Workman must be paid last drawn wages as directed.
