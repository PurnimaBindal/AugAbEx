Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F and 25-G - Termination - Violation of provisions - Award of reinstatement - Challenge as to - Dispute regarding completion of 240 days - The Department argued that records were destroyed over five years ago, handicapping the workman's ability to prove from the petitioner's kept record.
The Labour Court concluded that due to a lack of orders of record destruction and certification to this effect, an inference can be drawn that the record was not produced deliberately.
Also, the department's witness admitted a stamp on the attendance card and relied on the ALC report to conclude that the workman has completed the required 240 days.
