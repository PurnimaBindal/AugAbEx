Articles 12, 226 - Co-operative bank - Writ against - Maintainability - Petitioner challenged the order of termination by the cooperative bank through a writ petition - The cooperative bank is considered a private bank and registered as society - As per the decision of `Federal Bank Ltd v/s Sagar Thomas', a mere discharge of a public function by a private banking company would not make it amenable to writ jurisdiction as it would not fall within the ambit of the instrumentality of State or other authority.
