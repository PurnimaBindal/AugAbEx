Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 Reference/Delayed demand notice - According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, if a workman's termination is bad in law but they delay in setting up a claim in court, their claim may be rejected, even if the general law provides for a three-year limitation period - The management will face adverse consequences due to the delayed claims of the workers.
[Para 91] B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F Retrenchment Compensation/Short payment of retrenchment compensation - In cases where the management technically violates Section 25F of the Act, the workman will not automatically be entitled to reinstatement unless it is proven that the retrenchment was unjustified.
[Para 10] C. Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-B - If an award's operation is stayed and a petition is allowed against the workman, the worker cannot be deemed to have worked and is not entitled to wages.
