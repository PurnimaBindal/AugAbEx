Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Transfer - Transfer policy - The transfer policy of the State for transfer and posting of its employees from one place to another is not enforceable and does not alter the service conditions of the employees - Govt. servants cannot claim to remain at a particular place or post of choice.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Transfer - Couple case - Transfer policy - The transfer policy of the State for transfer and posting of its employees from one place to another is not enforceable and does not alter the service conditions of the employees - Husband and wife in Govt. service may be posted at the same station even if their departments are different, but their choice is only taken into consideration in light of administrative needs - Couples cannot claim to remain at one particular place together in service as a matter of right.
[Para 7]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Transfer - Transfer policy - An order of transfer of a Govt. servant can only be challenged on the ground of malafide - The allegations cannot be entertained to challenge the transfer if there are no allegations against any particular officer or without impleading such officer as a party.
