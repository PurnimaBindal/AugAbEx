The Maharashtra Civil Service (Pension) Rules, 1982, Rule 27, specifies forfeiture of entire pension and gratuity for misconduct in departmental proceedings.
A Block Development Officer who failed to implement a government scheme in their block, causing loss to the government and administrative lapses, was charged and charged were proven in an inquiry.
The punishment was imposed in compliance with Rule 27 procedures, with no interference.
[Paras 9, 13, 15]

In the case of misconduct and non-supply of a copy of the preliminary report in departmental proceedings, a copy of a document not relied upon does not have to be supplied to the delinquent officer.
A report of the inquiry officer was in fact supplied to the delinquent officer, and they were able to cross-examine witnesses on that basis.
Therefore, the delinquent officer was not prejudiced by the non-supply of the preliminary inquiry report.
[Paras 6 & 9]
