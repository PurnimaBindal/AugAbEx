According to Constitution of India, Article 226 and Army Act, 1950, Sections 49, 56(a) and 60, the charge framed against the accused cannot be challenged in the complaint filed under Section 49, and cannot be allowed to be challenged for the first time in a writ petition.
[Paras 9, 10 and 12] B The Writ Petition challenging court martial proceedings is liable to be dismissed if filed after more than 6 years of punishment imposed, with regards to Constitution of India, Article 226, Army Act, 1950, Sections 49, 56(a) and 60, on account of delay and laches.
