Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 10 - Absence from duty - Penalty of stoppage of increment - Petitioner's explanation that they were threatened by terrorists and hiding in a nearby brick kiln was rejected by the department - Based on the Enquiry Officer's report, three annual increments with cumulative effect were stopped - A show cause notice was served under Rule 10 and two annual increments were subsequently stopped - However, no regular enquiry was held for the two increments - The Tribunal correctly restored the two increments, but the award for stopping three increments was upheld.
