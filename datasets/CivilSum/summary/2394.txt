After succeeding in the exams held for the promotion to the post of A.S.I.
of Police, some candidates filed a writ petition alleging malpractices.
The Single Judge ordered a review committee to consider the allegations, and the committee declared some previously passed candidates as failed for not fulfilling eligibility criteria.
The State ordered promotion for all candidates entitled based on seniority in the feeder cadre inter se, but the promotion would not come with backwages.
