Constitution of India, Articles 226, 309 - Maharashtra Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1982, Rule 10 - Compulsory retirement - A Judicial Officer has claimed that their compulsory retirement should be seen as punishment - The Review Committee found that the petitioner's compulsory retirement was necessary in the interest of the public after considering all evidence on record - The recommendation of the Review Committee was accepted by the committee of Administrative Judges, who ordered the compulsory retirement - As a result, the order is valid and the writ petition is dismissed.
