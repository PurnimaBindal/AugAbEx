Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab State Electricity Board Main Services Regulations, 1972, Regulation 7 - Conviction in criminal charge - Dismissal from service - It is not automatic - There needs to be an assessment by the competent authority who must consider the judgment of the criminal Court along with other available material to reach a definite conclusion that the employee is no longer desirable to retain in service due to the conviction - Therefore, immediate dismissal based solely on a conviction cannot be upheld.
