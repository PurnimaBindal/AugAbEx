Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 39 rules 1 and 2 - Injunction - Defendants are restrained from holding a demonstration, picketing, dharana, and gherao inside the plaintiff's premises.
They will not hold a demonstration within the radius of 100 meters.
This injunction is granted because if defendants are allowed to make inciting speeches, this will disturb the functioning of the hotel and peace of the inhabitants.

Constitution of India, Article 19(1) (c) - Interpretation - The interpretation of Article 19(1) (c) is that the trade unions do not have a guaranteed right to an effective collective bargaining or to strike.
The unions have a right to demonstrate their point of view and discuss their problems, but it must be within the framework of the law, should not lead to violence, and misrepresentation of law and order in the society.
The evidence shows that the defendants indulged in agitation, which may be akin to violence, and this is supported by photographs placed on record.
Therefore, the defendants are restrained from demonstrating, holding dharans, gheraoing, or any sit-in within a radius of 50 meters as per the Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 39 rules 1 and 2.
