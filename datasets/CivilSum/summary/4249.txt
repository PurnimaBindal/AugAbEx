According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Rule 7.3, Note 9 - Suspension - Criminal Proceedings - Employee who was suspended due to criminal proceedings and was later acquitted from those charges must be reinstated immediately and should receive full salary from the date of acquittal till date of reinstatement.
If the reinstatement is delayed, he or she should also be entitled to suspension allowance at an enhanced rate if suspension continues beyond six months.
Additionally, the Competent Authority is obligated to make an order that outlines the treatment of the suspension period.
