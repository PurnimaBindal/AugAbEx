Punjab Educational Service, Class III School Cadre Rules, 1955, Rule 11 - Selection grade for JBT teachers on reorganization of districts in Haryana - The lower appellate courts held that the selection grade should be based on the total cadre strength rather than just permanent posts.
The well-reasoned judgment of the lower court cannot be faulted.
Consequently, RSA is dismissed and the parties must pay their own costs.
[Paras 5 & 15] B Punjab Educational Service, Class III School Cadre Rules, 1955, Rule 11 - Reorganization of districts in the state - Inter-district transfer of teachers - Seniority of teachers should be based on continuous length of service, as provided in the rules.
Any instruction to the contrary cannot override the Act.
The seniority list published on 1591973 was not in accordance with the rules and therefore must be quashed.
The lower appellate court judgment is free of error.
Consequently, RSA is dismissed.
