Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Interview - Selection - Policy guidelines - Posts of Veterinary Dressers - Although no academic qualifications are required for recruitment to the posts, guidelines provided that selection be based on assessing a candidate's knowledge of regional language, rural management, and cleanliness of animals - These guidelines were accepted by the government - The petitioner cannot claim any preference in the matter of selection and appointment based solely on better academic qualifications - The selection made does not violate any law or constitutional provisions.
