Constitution of India, Article 226 - University Calendar, 1986, Volume II, Regulation 23 - Enquiry - Termination - The petitioner was terminated on the grounds of entertaining an admission form after the last date and wrong totalling of marks obtained by a candidate.
However, there were other officers involved in dealing with the admission form and according to Regulation 23, admission form could be entertained with a late fee.
Additionally, the mistake of wrong totalling was not only missed by the petitioner but also by two scrutineers who rechecked the result sheet.
Furthermore, the candidate did not receive any benefit from the wrong declaration of the result as it was later cancelled.
Consequently, the impugned order of termination was quashed.
[Paras 11, 16, 18 and 19]
