According to Rule 19 of the State Administrative Tribunal (Procedure) Rules, 1989, as specified in the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 (Act 13 of 1985), the Tribunal has no power to grant condonation of delay for review applications filed beyond the prescribed 30-day period.
Section 5 of the Limitation Act cannot be used to circumvent this requirement, and failure to comply with this rule will result in the Tribunal having no jurisdiction to oversee the matter at hand.
[Paras 13 and 14]
