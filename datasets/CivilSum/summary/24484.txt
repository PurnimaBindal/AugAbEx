Per Majority View, the appointment of civil servants made in violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India would be void.
However, the competent authority must take into consideration the foundation of facts established before arriving at such a finding.
If appointees complete a few years of service, their service cannot be terminated without complying with the principles of natural justice and without sufficient material to satisfy that the selection process was tainted.
If the State is satisfied that the officers in the majority have been found to be part of a fraudulent purpose or the system itself was corrupt and such findings are arrived at, all appointments traceable to the officers concerned could be canceled.
However, there must be a full effort to separate the innocent people from the tainted ones.
With regards to the appointment of judicial officers, no irregularity is expected to occur in the matter of allotment of marks at the interview in the presence of High Court Judges by the other members of the Commission.
The appointment of Constitutional authorities must be after a meticulous inquiry and scrutiny of the antecedents of the person concerned.
Only people of high integrity, merit, rectitude, and honesty should be appointed to these constitutional positions.
