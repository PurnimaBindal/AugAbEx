Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-B - Application for payment of wages pending proceedings - An application for such payment can be entertained after the decision of the writ petition, as the amount is paid to the workman for maintenance purposes - However, Section 17-B does provide that the workman cannot claim the amount after the writ petition before the High Court is over - The employer is directed to pay the workman the amount of wages last drawn at the time of termination.
