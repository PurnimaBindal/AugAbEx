According to the Haryana Fisheries Department (State Services Group-C) Rules, 1979, pay scales of lower post have been made equivalent to the pay scale of the higher post.
A petitioner represented to the government of Haryana, claiming that officers lower in post had been given an equivalent pay scale as higher posts due to the revision reflected in the First Schedule of the Haryana Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1987.
The state supports the petitioners, but it is not clear what the government weighed to equalize the scales of both posts, since Fisheries Officer is a promotional post.
Importing revisions to scales by the principle of equalizer is unsustainable, unjust, and unfair.
Therefore, petitioners' category will be entitled to the revision of the pay scale.
