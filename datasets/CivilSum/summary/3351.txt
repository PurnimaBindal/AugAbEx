Constitution of India, Article 311 - Enquiry report - The Punishing Authority is not obligated to abide by the recommendations and findings of the Enquiry Officer - The authority can come to its own conclusion based on the same facts and evidence without conducting a fresh investigation.
[Paras 5 & 6] B.
Civil Procedure Code, Section 100 - Second appeal - Departmental Proceedings - The High Court cannot scrutinize the amount of evidence in favor of a punishment order passed by a punishing authority - Nor can it discard the same evidence by forming a different opinion.
