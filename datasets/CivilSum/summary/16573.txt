Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 300-A - Payment of Gratuity Act, Section 4 - Gratuity - The petitioner has served for over 18 years and, as per the Payment of Gratuity Act, an employee is entitled to gratuity after five years of service.
The court directed for the payment of the entire gratuity to be given to the petitioner with an interest rate of 9% per annum.
