Constitution of India, Articles 12, 14, 16 and 226 - Bank policy matters - Transfer policy with incentives for experienced transferees at difficult stations - Policy distinguishes between new recruits and those with experience - New recruits cannot choose their station, while experienced transferees can avoid transfers to difficult stations on various grounds - Incentives for experienced transferees do not discriminate and improve bank efficiency - Court cannot interfere with bank policy and cannot direct substitution - Bank policy upheld, High Court order set aside.
