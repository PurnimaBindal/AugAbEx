Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 r/w Section 151 - Injunction - Retirement Date - In case of an injunction granted, the defendants were forbidden from retiring the plaintiff based on the date of birth stated in his service record - High Court vacated the stay order - Petitioner was subsequently retired on the relevant date - Respondent to receive all retiral benefits up until the date of retirement, and may be entitled to consequential service benefits if they succeed in their suit - Directed respondent to apply for release of retiral benefits, and ordered the trial Court to expedite the suit's decision.
