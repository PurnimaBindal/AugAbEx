Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(b) - Punjab Police Rules - Dismissal - Dispensing with enquiry - The plaintiff was dismissed from service by SSP under pretences of certain allegations of dereliction of duty to aid extremists with no enquiry conducted.
The First appellate Court recorded that the SSP was satisfied, from the material before him, that it was not feasible to hold a departmental enquiry due to a serious internal disturbance in the State of Punjab because of terrorism.
The order of dismissal passed against the plaintiff was legal, and no interference is necessary.
