Constitution of India, Articles 187,309,311,16 and 14 - Revision of pay scales - The Post of Assistant Information Officer, Punjab Vidhan Sabha was originally equated to that of Deputy Superintendent Grade `A' in 1962, after consultation with the Chairman of the Punjab Legislative Council and the Speaker of the Punjab Vidhan Sabha - However, a notification dated February 12, 1971 was issued by the Punjab Government withdrawing this equation.
The said notification was not issued in consultation with the Speaker of the Assembly, and as such, the earlier order could not be withdrawn - It was ruled that the petitioner will be entitled to the pay scale at par with Deputy Superintendent Grade `A' (now Superintendent Grade II) consistent from 121986 - Arrears of all benefits including salary will be restricted to a period of 38 months prior to the date of filing of the writ petition.
