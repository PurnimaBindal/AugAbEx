Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Factories Act, Section 46 - Andhra Pradesh Factories Rules, 1950, Rules 65 to 71 - Writ jurisdiction/Regularisation - The Rules created in public interest impose a public duty on every management to provide a canteen - All consequences of the canteen duty are deemed to be a public duty for management - The provisions require the appellant to appoint the necessary personnel for the canteen work - Workers employed in the canteen are considered the employees of the management - The single Judge made the correct decision and issued necessary directions in favour of the employees - Petition dismissed.
