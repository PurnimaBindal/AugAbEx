In accordance with Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 226, a probationer may be terminated for unsatisfactory performance during probation.
A communication of dissatisfaction regarding performance does not equate to a charge.
An order of simple discharge as 'services no more required' does not require an enquiry or hearing and is not stigmatic or punitive.
Such an action does not fall under Article 311 of the Constitution.
[Paras 16 to 29] B.
In terms of Confirmation and Probation Rules, there is no automatic confirmation upon expiry of probation.
Therefore, termination after probation cannot be deemed as improper.
