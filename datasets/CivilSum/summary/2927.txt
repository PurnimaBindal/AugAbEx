Railway Servants (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1968, Rule 9 - Disciplinary proceedings - It cannot be claimed that transferring an employee during pending disciplinary proceedings is a punishment in itself.
However, if the disciplinary authority believes that transferring the employee is necessary for a fair proceeding, the transfer should not be considered a punitive measure unless there is proof of mala fide or a breach of statutory provisions.
Courts should not intervene with a transfer made in the public interest or for administrative purposes.
