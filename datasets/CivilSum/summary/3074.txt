The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F, and TN Industrial Establishments (Conferment of Permanent Status to Workmen) Act, 1981, Section 3(1) states that retrenchment cannot take place without proper compliance of the provisions.
The daily wagers were paid wages at the end of the month and provided their signatures on vouchers which were entered into the account books.
However, their services were terminated without following Section 25-F, which amounted to retrenchment.
Despite notice, the management failed to produce vouchers and account books.
As a result, the daily wagers were entitled to reinstatement, but back wages were not granted due to a lack of evidence regarding their employment status during the said period.
