According to Rule 16 of the UP Contributory Provident Fund-cum-Pension Insurance Rules, 1984, if an individual exercises the option for reduced pension, they cannot later seek to incorporate any conditions not included in the option.
Therefore, the appellant, who had previously opted for reduced pension with the acceptance of the government's contribution to his provident fund, could not claim that the reduced pension was for a limited period until the amount of the government's contribution was repaid.
As per the government's order of July 20, 1985, the appellant became entitled to full pension only from August 1, 1985, and not before that.
