Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Right to life/Right to livelihood/Disablement/Alternative appointment - The right to life includes the right to livelihood as a fundamental aspect protected under Article 21 of the Constitution.
When an employee is disabled and unable to perform their duties, the employer must make reasonable efforts to adjust them to a suitable alternative post, even if some qualifications are relaxed.
In adjusting to an alternative post, the employee's pay drawn from the previous post must also be protected.
The State of Haryana was directed to adjust the petitioner, who was disabled from the post of Sub-station Attendant in the pay scale of Rs. 1400-2300.
They were directed to adjust him as an LDC by relaxing the typing qualifications and protect his pay drawn from the previous post as a Sub-station Attendant.
The State was also directed to pay all the arrears of salary to the petitioner, as it was not justified in offering the post of Carrier Attendant to the appellant.
The appeal was allowed.
