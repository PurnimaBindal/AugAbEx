According to Jammu & Kashmir Police Manual, Rules 145 & 185, a candidate found involved in anti-national and anti-social activities or detained in cases against him cannot be considered eligible for recruitment as constable due to character verification.
Even if proceedings against him are quashed or he is acquitted later, he cannot claim to be suitable or the charges false.
