According to Jammu and Kashmir Constitution, Section 126; Jammu and Kashmir Civil Service Regulations, Section 128; and Jammu and Kashmir Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 33(1) and 33(2), an employee can be terminated for absence without leave.
In this case, the petitioner, who was in charge of stores at Leh, left the job during an investigation into store shortages and was later arrested after three years for a criminal case related to the shortages.
Despite notices, the petitioner did not return to work and his services were ultimately terminated.
The learned Single Judge correctly found that the petitioner abandoned the job without any intention of returning and therefore was not entitled to an enquiry before termination.
