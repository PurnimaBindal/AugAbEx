The petitioner claimed that he should be deputed along with other staff for the Haj Pilgrimage to Saudi Arabia because his name was included in the list forwarded by the Divisional Commissioner to the Government, but it was not shown that his name was approved.
There was also no document on record to show that the petitioner's name was included in the list of staff.
The court found that merely proposing the petitioner for being deputed as part of the staff does not violate any legal right of the petitioner and therefore, he is not entitled to any relief.
