The petitioner was granted study leave and permission to undertake a BP Ed. course at a Physical Education College by the government, as per the Study Leave Rules, 1979, Rules 61 and 73.
The Director, Youth Services and Sports has no right to cancel the study leave and permission, as per Constitution of India, Article 14.
[Paras 4, 5 and 6] B.
Once study leave and permission to undertake training have been granted, a right has accrued to the petitioner.
Cancelling this permission without notice and a full opportunity for a hearing is a violation of natural justice, as per Constitution of India, Article 14.
[Paras 6 and 7]
