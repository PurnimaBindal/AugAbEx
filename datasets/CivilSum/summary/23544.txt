Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment/Panel - Single Judge quashes appointments of untrained teachers, but Division Bench upholds and directs preparation of panel - Minor irregularities in panel preparation deemed acceptable - However, panels prepared without following court directions in certain districts are quashed - Appointments must be made from the panel regardless of age limit - If appointments have already been made, petitioners can seek remedy in appropriate forum.
[Paras 13 and 14]
