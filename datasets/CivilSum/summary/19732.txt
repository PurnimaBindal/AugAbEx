Constitution of India, Article 14 - Regularisation of services - Relationship of employer and employee - The room or office where an employee is located is not the determinative factor in the relationship with their employer.
Rather, it depends on the offer of appointment and acceptance.
In this particular case, the petitioner had no relationship with the PGI and therefore has no claim against the institution.
The petitioner was initially appointed on ad hoc basis for a fixed period, and later joined as Record Clerk under a project of the Medical Council.
The appointment was made by the principal Investigator on behalf of the Director General of the Council.
