Constitution of India, Article 226 - Reversion - Ad hoc promotee - The petitioner was appointed as a cleaner and later promoted to higher posts of clerk and clerk-cum-typist on a purely ad hoc basis for a fixed term.
Such promotions are terminable anytime, as there is no right conferred to the petitioner to continue in the post.
The order of reversion to the substantive post of the petitioner is not illegal.
[Paras 7, 8 and 9]
