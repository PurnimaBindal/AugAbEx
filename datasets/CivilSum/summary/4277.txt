Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8(8) - Departmental Enquiry/Assistance of co-worker - Whether the Rule is mandatory - The rule is not mandatory but directory, and non-compliance does not invalidate enquiry proceedings.
However, in some cases, where education is lacking, assistance from another co-employee may be a mandatory requirement.
Failure to inform the accused employee of this entitlement does not invalidate the enquiry unless prejudice can be proven.
1992(1) SCT 258 (P&H) : 1992(1) RSJ 462 Hans Raj Gupta v. State of Punjab.
Overruled.
[Paras 22, 23, 25 and 26]
