The denial of regularisation cannot impact the granting of pay for the post of Garden Chaudhary according to Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
It is held that the terms of reference should be broadly construed and the workman should not be divested of the right to claim lesser relief, even if their original claim was for regularization.
Therefore, the plea is rejected.
[Para 5]

The grant of pay for the post of Garden Chaudhary is sustainable under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
The tribunal found that the workman was working as a Garden Chaudhary based on a review of documentary evidence.
The contention that these documents were issued at the workman's request is rejected, as official documents cannot be ignored based on a false claim.
Therefore, the pay for the post was rightly granted.
