Constitution of India, Articles 310 and 14 - Compulsory retirement - Annual Confidential Reports - Appreciations - Competent authority to record ACRs - In determining compulsory retirement or retention in service, only the ACRs recorded by competent authorities are to be considered, not appreciation letters obtained from others that are inconsistent with the work and conduct reflected in the ACRs.
