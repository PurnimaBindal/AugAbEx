Constitution of India, Articles 136 - Migration - Admission - MBBS Course - Admission to the Second Professional MBBS course in MD University by migration from other universities - Some petitioners challenged the validity of migration granted to certain candidates who were admitted to the MBBS course via MD University - There is a difference in the principle followed by MD University for considering the cases of migration of candidates for MBBS Course from the BDS Course - Candidates' cases were examined by a Committee and its recommendations were accepted by the University - Committee members were not accused of bias or animus - The Court deemed it unfair to set aside the Committee's selections - Candidates selected by the Committee for migration to the MBBS course are entitled to pursue their studies in accordance with University rules.
