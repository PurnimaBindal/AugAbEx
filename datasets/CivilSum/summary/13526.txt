Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Section 3 - Confidential reports - Performance - The appellant received one very good and seven good reports, as well as two average and one below average reports.
The reporting officer marked him as "Very Good," but the accepting authority changed it to "below average" solely because of his lack of contribution to family welfare as a Revenue Patwari.
Apart from this, there were no negative comments regarding his performance.
Even the instances where he was marked as "average" can be attributed only to punctuality.
There is no evidence to suggest that he was an ineffective worker who needed to be dismissed.
His performance had not degraded to the point where it was necessary to relieve him in the public interest.
The decision to retire him prematurely was not fair or genuine.
As a result, the appellant will be considered as having retired on the date of superannuation.
[Paras 3 and 4]
