The Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 2(d)(e) and (f), 5-D, 30 pertains to handling labourers working in PUNSUP and Markfed.
The issue of shifting liability under the Act on contractors has been raised, with the contention that PUNSUP and Markfed, being principal employer, must comply with the provisions of the Act.
However, in accordance with clause (3) of Section 30, the responsibility of the principal employer is to pay both contributions payable by himself in respect of employees directly employed by him and also employees employed through contractors, as well as administrative charges.
Therefore, any action taken in this regard is unsustainable.
