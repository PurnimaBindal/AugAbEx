Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 19 - Dismissal without enquiry - Petitioner Head Constable dismissed from service on accusations of raping a minor girl without holding an enquiry - Held, that the decision not to hold a departmental enquiry must not be left to the authority concerned - There must be written reasons that it was not reasonably practicable to hold an enquiry - Only with materials for some good reason recorded by authority in writing can an enquiry be dispensed with - Disciplinary authority should not forego a disciplinary enquiry readily or thoughtlessly nor over reason the requirement of Article 311(2) - The impugned order's satisfaction recorded by the authority has no support from materials produced to dispense with the enquiry enjoined in Article 311(2) of the Constitution - Impugned order set aside, leaving it open to the department to initiate a departmental enquiry if advised - Petition allowed.
