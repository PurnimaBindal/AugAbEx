Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab Tehsildari Rules, 1932, Rule 5(2) - Promotion - Rule 5(2) of the Rules stipulates that only those Naib-Tehsildars who have passed the departmental examination are eligible for promotion to Tehsildars - Petitioners did not fulfill the condition for promotion despite passing the Tehsildari Departmental Examination and working as Naib-Tehsildars for less than three years - Petition dismissed.
[Paras 3 and 4] B.
Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab Tehsildari Rules, 1932, Rule 5(2) - Promotion - Qualification - Minimum three years of experience as a regular and full-fledged Naib-Tehsildar after completing training and passing the Departmental Examination of Naib-Tehsildar is a requirement for promotion to the post of Tehsildar - Privare respondents who fulfilled the eligibility condition were promoted in accordance with the Rules, while petitioners did not meet the minimum requirement - Petition dismissed.
[Para 4] C. Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab Tehsildari Rules, 1932, Rule 5(2) - Promotion - Qualification - The prescribed qualifications for a post typically represent the minimum eligibility criteria and not the maximum - Petitioners were not eligible for promotion to the post of Tehsildar due to their failure to meet the minimum qualifying standards.
