Constitution of India, Article 311 - Reversion and reduction in rank - Differentiating between the two - Not all reversions constitute reductions in rank - An individual who is temporarily working on a higher post can be reverted to their original post for valid reasons without being considered a reduction in rank.
[Para 6]

Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 311 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 11(vi) - Civil Service - Statutory interpretation - Reduction in rank - Definition of penalty - Reduction to a lower time scale, grade, service, or post that an employee has never previously held is not permitted, even if the rule does not state such limitations, it must be interpreted in this way.
