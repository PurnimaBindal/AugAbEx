Appointment - Pay - Seniority - Past service - The petitioners accepted the clean and unambiguous conditions stipulated in their appointment orders without protest and joined posts in the pay scale stipulated therein.
There is no stipulation in their appointment letters that their pay and seniority will be fixed on the basis of past service rendered by them.
The petitioners cannot, therefore, claim that they joined the service of Respondent-NJPC on the basis of legitimate expectation that their past services will be counted for the fixation of pay and seniority.
[Para 13]
Appointment - General category - Petitioners cannot compare themselves with state government employees as well as seconded employees of HP Respondent-NJPC is pay Hill Compensatory Allowance to its employees as per rules and regulations.
Petitioners have been categorized under the workman category based on their qualifications and experience.
Thus, petitioners cannot be held entitled for their placement under categories of supervisors as well as executives as those categories are quite different from the categories against which the petitioners were appointed.
The petition is dismissed.
