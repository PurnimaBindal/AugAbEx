Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 12.15(1) - Appointment as Constable - Qualification for eligibility - Prospective constables must have a minimum height of 5'-7" along with a chest measurement of 33" with expansion of 1-1/2" - This requirement is reasonable for the post of constable in the police force - Failure to meet this condition renders a person ineligible to enter the police force - The requirement is mandatory and not directory.

Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 12.15(1) - Appointment as Police Constable - Petitioners were appointed as Constables after qualifying a physical test and a medical examination - However, they did not fulfil the requirement regarding chest measurement - Their appointments were cancelled - The legality of the action has no issue - None of the petitioners fulfil the prescribed standard - Those who fail to meet the eligibility criteria are not entitled to continue in their posts - Therefore, the termination of petitioners' services was correct.

Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Opportunity of hearing - An opportunity of hearing is provided to ascertain the factual position - Where there is no factual dispute, it serves no purpose to direct the respondents to hear the petitioners.
[Para 7]
Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Opportunity of hearing - Normally an opportunity of hearing is necessary before passing an order that adversely affects a person - However, such an opportunity is necessary only when a person disputes the basis of the action.
