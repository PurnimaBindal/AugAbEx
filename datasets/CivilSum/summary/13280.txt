Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-F - Termination - The respondent-workman had worked for 240 consecutive days within the preceding 12 months as per the provisions of Section 25-B, and was terminated without any notice or compensation.
The petitioner violated the provisions of Section 25-F of the Act.
The award passed by the Labour Court has no infirmity.
