Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion - Reversion by option - Petitioner had been transferred to a promoted post and opted for a re-transfer despite the possibility of being reverted to Clerk.
Following the lapse of one year, the petitioner is entitled to be re-promoted to the post of Accountant as a matter of right, and authorities must promote him from the date the reversion period concluded.
As such, the petitioner is directed to be placed in the Accountant cadre from the end of the one year period with the salary of that post, effective from the date of his representation regarding this matter.
