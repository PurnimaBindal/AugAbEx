Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, Section 20 - Employee of Co- operative Society - Control and aided by government - Whether a public servant - High Court quashed criminal case against employee holding him a non-public servant - According to the court, when defining a public servant, a purposive approach should be adopted to achieve the intention of the legislature - Therefore, even if not an office-bearer, such an employee is still considered a public servant.
[Paras 8, 12, 15]
