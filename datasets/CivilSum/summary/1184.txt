Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(k) and 18 - Settlement - A meeting between the Workers Union and the Management resulting in minutes is not considered a settlement according to section 18 - Additionally, this meeting is not able to override the decision of the Management to hold the election of the Union nor can it be classified as a settlement under legal definition - The meeting also cannot render the election of the majority Union or the recognition given to that union as invalid.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Res judicata - A writ petition challenging the validity of an election that has already been dismissed in a civil suit and a previous Writ Petition is considered res judicata - The petitioner's conduct disqualifies them from the discretionary relief given under extraordinary jurisdiction.
