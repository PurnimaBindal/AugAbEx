Constitution of India, Article 311 - Retirement - Pension - The Department is neglectful for allowing the petitioner to continue in service beyond the date of superannuation - No blame can be put on the employee for not informing the department regarding superannuation - The employer is responsible for retiring the employee on the relevant date - Pension and other retiral benefits must not be withheld on that ground.
[Paras 4 and 5]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Pension - Retiral benefits - Pension is a payment for rendered services rather than a gift - Delaying the payment of pension is not desirable.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 311 - Pension Rules - To avoid irritation to the retiring employees, State Governments must create specific, fair, and reasonable Pension Rules.
