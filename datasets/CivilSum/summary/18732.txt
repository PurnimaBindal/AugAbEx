According to the Central Architect Service Group A Recruitment Rules, 1989, Rule 7(5) regarding promotion through the roster system, a Deputy Architect can only be promoted to an Architect if the authority operates the roster to fill available vacancies in accordance with the prescribed ratio under the rules and the officer falls within the consideration zone.
If the officer does not meet these criteria, promotion cannot be claimed.
Additionally, diverting vacancies from a particular year to another cadre in order to balance the ratios under the rules does not automatically entitle an officer to promotion.
