The Punjab Civil Services Rules (as applicable to Haryana) Vol. 1, Rule 44, the Haryana Primary Education (Group `C') District Cadre Service Rules, 1994, the Haryana Panchahati Raj Act, 1994, Section 21, the Haryana Municipal Act, 1973, Section 66-A, the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 24G, and W, and the Haryana Zila Prashad Primary Education Service Rules, 2003, Rule 13 are all relevant to the case being discussed.
The petitioners have alleged hostile discrimination due to the fact that, although their qualifications and mode of selection are equivalent to those under the 1994 Rules, they are being treated as a separate class due to their status under the 2003 Rules.
On examination of the rules, it has been determined that there is no significant difference between the Government JBT Teachers and Zila Parishad JBT Teachers with respect to their qualifications, mode of appointment, pay scale, or performance of duties.
Therefore, it would be inconsistent with the provisions of Articles 14 and 16(1) of the Constitution to treat them as separate classes.
The condition imposed on the petitioners to complete graduation within 5 years and to be considered fresh entrants is considered unreasonable and discriminatory, and has been declared ultra vires of the Constitution.
