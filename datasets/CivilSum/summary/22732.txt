Constitution of India, Article 317 - Public Service Commission - The President can refer an enquiry to the Supreme Court to report whether a member of the Public Service Commission should be removed from office based on their conduct - A member of the Commission denied approaching the Chairman to influence the results of his nephew's examination, but admitted to not formally declaring his nephew's examination to the Commission - The Court found, based on evidence and testimony from the Chairman and Secretary of the Commission, that the member did in fact approach the Chairman to influence his nephew's result - The reference was answered in the affirmative.
