Army Pension Regulations, Regulation 173 - Disability pension - Disability attributable and aggravated in service - The petitioner was declared medically fit during his annual medical examination, but later developed severe headaches, giddiness, and palpitations due to the stress and strain of his duties and sub-zero temperatures while serving in the "Y" Communication Zone Signal Regiment stationed in J&K.
Although he was treated at various Army and Civil Hospitals, he was eventually diagnosed with an "Anxiety State" and discharged from the army on medical grounds.
However, the disease was found not to be attributed to military service, as the term "Anxiety State" is not interchangeable with "Anxiety Neurosis."
Additionally, merely exhibiting anxiety is not sufficient to qualify for disability pension - there must be a direct connection between the disease and military service.
Anxiety is an inherent part of life, and even healthy individuals may suffer from it due to life's complexities.
