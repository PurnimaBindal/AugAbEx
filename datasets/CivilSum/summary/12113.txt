Constitution of India, Articles 226, 311(2) - Dismissal from service - Ground of conviction - Petitioner found guilty of offence involving moral turpitude in criminal revision filed after order of conviction stood dismissed - Although petitioner is to be treated similarly to civil servant, they cannot claim greater protection than is afforded by Article 311(2) - Therefore, the competent authority was within its rights to dismiss petitioner due to their conviction on a charge involving moral turpitude.
