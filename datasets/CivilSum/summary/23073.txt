The Army Act of 1950, sections 61, 71, 72, and 73, provides for leniency in punishment for accused Army jawans.
A court martial awarded punishment of 3 months imprisonment and dismissal from service for carrying eleven bottles of rum while passing through a prohibited area.
The Supreme Court ruled that the punishment was too severe for the nature of the offence.
Hence, it set aside the punishment and remanded the matter back to the court martial for awarding lesser punishment.
It is to be noted that the punishment awarded by the court martial shall not disqualify from service.
