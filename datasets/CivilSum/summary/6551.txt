The Hindustan Aeronautics Workers Union, in light of an All India 'Bundh' call by opposition political parties, have encountered transport problems resulting in absence from duty.
Management is unwilling to regard this absence as 'special casual leave', as workers did not issue a call for the bundh but merely participated in it.
Despite this, the workers are regarded as the victims of the bundh, and the management has been instructed to evaluate the situation and issue suitable orders.
