Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Reinstatement - The employer was unable to demonstrate that the workman had not completed 240 days of service, nor was it proven that the workman's termination was done to make way for regularly appointed candidates.
The workman's entitlement under the Industrial Dispute Act cannot be revoked.
The labour court's award that called for reinstatement with 25% of back wages was confirmed.
