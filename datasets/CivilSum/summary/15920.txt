Constitution of India, Article 226 - Policy Decision - Pay scale - Higher standard pay scales - JBT teachers under Group `C' category are entitled to higher standard pay scale of Rs. 1400-2600 after ten years and Rs. 1600-2660 after 20 years of regular service - Ineligible for the higher standard scale if promotion is foregone or reversion is sought - Subsequent instructions cannot take away accrued rights - Petition dismissed.
