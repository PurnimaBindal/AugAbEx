Constitution of India (J&K), Artilcles 14, 16 and 311 - Appointment - Regularisation of service on justice - Eligible appointees prior to ban on ad hoc appointment - Can be regularised if they satisfy certain conditions - Thirteen petitioners and one additional petitioner had their services terminated illegally without proper inquiry into legitimacy of their appointments or compliance with principles of natural justice - Inadequate consideration was given to another petitioner - Termination orders have been quashed.
