Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 268-H - Pension - Cut off date - The government aimed to implement a more generous pension scheme for employees in service on 2921964 and retiring thereafter.
The liberalized pension scheme under Chapter XXIII-A commenced in March 1964, and the date 2921964 in Rule 268-H under Chapter XXIII-A was not arbitrary.
This cut off date mentioned in Rule 268-H is not in violation of Article 14.
The state government can introduce different retiral benefit schemes for Govt. employees based on their date of retirement, as they took into account all relevant factors and the conditions of retirees who retired prior to Rule 268-H [Paras 23 to 27]
