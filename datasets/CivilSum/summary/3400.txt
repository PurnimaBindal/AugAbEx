Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Swatantarta Sainani Samman Pension Scheme - Freedom Fighter Pension - Eligibility for - Certificate of co-prisoner Freedom fighters - The fact that the petitioner was issued many co-prisoner certificates in the past by his fellow freedom fighters is not enough to deny his entitlement, unless it is proven that false certificates were being issued - The petitioner's application was verified on an individual basis and he had already been granted numerous benefits as a Freedom-fighter - Rejecting his claim simply because his co-prisoners indiscriminately issued such certificates would be unjustified.
[Paras 15 to 17]
