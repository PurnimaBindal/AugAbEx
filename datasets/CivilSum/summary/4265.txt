According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, the Court cannot declare a petitioner suitable for a post and direct for appointment if not found suitable by the Selection Committee.
This applies to the case of a Petitioner applying for the post of Pump Operator Mechanic (Electrical) but who was not found suitable by the Selection Committee.
[Para 8]
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 provides for the right to consideration for regularization on fixed wages for more than 6 years.
The State is therefore obligated to consider individuals for regularization as per their suitability and qualifications for any post.
[Paras 12 and 16]
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 provide equal opportunity to all individuals to be considered for a post as per their suitability and qualifications.
However, authorities have misused their power by appointing their kith and kin, disregarding the claims of more qualified and suitable persons.
If all eligible and suitable persons can compete, employers can select the best possible candidate.
State Governments are bound to implement this scheme of the Constitution.
