Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Employees' Pension Scheme, 1995, Para 6A - Family pension - Petitioner's husband, a member of Provident Fund Scheme of 1952, submitted resignation and applied for withdrawal of Provident Fund benefits but passed away before receiving the amount - Petitioner submitted application for release of Provident Fund dues and family pension, but the latter was denied - Ruling held that since the husband did not actually avail the Provident Fund benefit, the petitioner is entitled to receive family pension.
