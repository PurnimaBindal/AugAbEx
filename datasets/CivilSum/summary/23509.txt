As per Section 6 of the Administrative Tribunals Act of 1985, the Chairman and other members of the State Administrative Tribunal are appointed according to the Supreme Court's directions in SP Sampath Kumar's case.
The Selection Committee for the appointments must be high powered and include the Chief Minister, Secretary of Law Department, and the Chief Justice of the State High Court.
The directions given to form the Selection Committee were advisory and not mandatory.
The government followed the selection process and made the appointments with approval from the Chief Justice of India, rendering challenges on that basis moot.
Additionally, the fact that the Chief Justice of India did not head the Selection Committee is not significant since he ultimately had to approve the appointments.
