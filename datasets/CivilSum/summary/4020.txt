The appointments of various individuals are listed for State Board No. 1, Director of School Education & DPSC 24-Pgs.
(North), and DPSC (Hooghly) & Chairman, DPSC (Hooghly).
The petitioners are requesting the relaxation of the age-bar for appointment under Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
The Supreme Court had previously directed authorities not to debar members of the petitioner samity who had crossed the age bar, but this only applied to the initial panel and not subsequent panels.
The previous direction was made under the impression that a panel had already been prepared, which was not the case, and the undertaking of the Deputy Secretary was also subject to panel preparation.
Therefore, the age bar cannot be condoned, but members of the petitioner samity who are otherwise qualified and not barred by age should be called for interview by the respondents.
