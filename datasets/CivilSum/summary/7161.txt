Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Claim - Petitioners and private respondents worked in the same department, but petitioners joined earlier - Even though private respondents were promoted earlier as Traffic Inspectors, petitioners became senior on the basis of Apex Court order - Private respondents promoted to officiate on higher post of Traffic Supervisor - As promotion orders did not mention promotion of private respondents to reserved post, petitioners should have been promoted as they were senior at the time of private respondents' promotion.
[Paras 10 and 12]
