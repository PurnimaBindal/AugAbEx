According to the Constitution of India, particularly Articles 14 and 16, employees absorbed by various corporations on the option basis are entitled to a monthly pension and medical allowances.
However, after the petitioners received benefits for some time, their medical allowance payments stopped.
The petitioner association subsequently filed a writ petition and it was determined that withdrawing benefits after having given them is against the principles of natural justice.
