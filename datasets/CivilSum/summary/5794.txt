Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution deal with Officiating Service and Ad hoc service and give Seniority priority to those Srvice men who hold the post in declared legitimate manner.
Promotions made in de hors the rules were declared illegal by the High Court; however, the promotee is still allowed to continue holding the post and never reverted.
That said, such service on promoted post is merely officiating and cannot be counted towards seniority or any other benefits.
[Paras 8, 9 and 10] B.
As per the Punjab Industries Service (State Service Class II) Rules, 1966, Rules 9 and 11 read with Rule 3 states that the Government is allowed to create a new post, include and exclude any post from the cadre of Service and miscellaneous posts given in Appendix 'A.'
The post of a Functional Manager and General Manager created in exercise of power under Rule 3 cannot be treated as an ex-cadre post to confer any additional advantages in favor of those holding such posts.
Moreover, Rule 11 in such cases will be applicable, and a joint seniority list of Assistant Directors and Functional Managers must be prepared.
Promotion given by giving special benefits treating such posts as ex-cadre posts are illegal.
