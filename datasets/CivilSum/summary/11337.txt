Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admissions/Reservation - Wards of employees - Admission reservations for an institution's employee wards are not permissible and any such reservations are considered void and cannot be enforced through a writ of mandamus - This has been followed in AIR 1984 Punjab and Haryana 278, 1990(4) SLR 4, 1990(1) PLR 490 & 1991 (1) SLR 741.
