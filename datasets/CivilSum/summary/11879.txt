According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, a compassionate appointment policy has a limitation for claiming the appointment on compassionate grounds.
If the family of the deceased consists of minors or non-eligible persons for appointment at the time of the death of the employee, the claim can be accepted any time within three years from the death of the employee.
However, in a recent case, the Single Judge granted relief and held that the limitation shall run from the date the minor attains the age of eligibility.
The court cannot re-write the policy itself to make further provisions in it if within the period prescribed in the policy none of the family becomes eligible for appointment.
The Order of the Single Judge was set aside and the appeal allowed dismissing the writ petition.
