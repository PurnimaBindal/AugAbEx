Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Appointment - Advertisement of posts - A post of Associate Director (Farm Advisory Service) was advertised by the Haryana Agricultural University which is equivalent to a professor and different to the post of Associate Director (Training) - The University appointed Respondent No. 3 to the post of Associate Director (Training) which was never advertised after the appointment of candidates at No. 1 - The appointment made without advertising such a post amounts to denial of equality of opportunity to all eligibles, which is violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
Appointment of Respondent No. 3 is quashed.
