UP Police Regulations, Para 541 and Constitution of India (Articles 14, 16 and 311(2)) - Confirmation and probation of the police officers - The UP Police Regulations, Para 541 is silent about the maximum period of probation extension - The probation period shall be deemed to be extended if there is no such specification - Probationers can receive permanent status only after a confirmation order is received.
[Paras 9 and 10] B.
Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Probationer - Temporary Govt. servants - The temporary employees and probationers who have no right to hold the post, are equally entitled to the protection of Article 311(2) as the permanent employees.
[Para 11] C. Constitution of India (Articles 311(2) and 14)) - Termination during probation - An innocuous order can be deemed punitive, and justified or not, depending on the circumstances of the case - The enquiry should be held to assess the suitability of probationer for the role and not to determine his misconduct without any allegation.
[Para 13] If the enquiry is only held to assess the suitability of probationer, it will not be considered as a punitive act.
If, on the other hand, there are allegations of misconduct, and an enquiry is held to confirm the allegations, then the termination would be based on misconduct and not mere "motive."
[Para 27 and 28] No penalty should be levied without proper inquiry under the prescribed procedure; following the procedure is mandatory in the case when the employee's probation is proposed to be terminated.
