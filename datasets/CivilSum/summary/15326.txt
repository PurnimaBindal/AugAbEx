The petitioner, who was placed under the "CEE" (Permanent) medical category and invalidated out of service, was denied disability pension despite a recommendation from the Medical Board.
However, the petitioner is entitled to a re-assessment by a Re-survey Medical Board to challenge the view taken by the Release Medical Board.
The Respondent has been directed to convene a Re-survey Medical Board to finalize the petitioner's case for the grant of a disability pension.
