Article 226 of the Constitution of India - Withdrawal of voluntary retirement - The government cannot order the voluntary retirement of an officer who has withdrawn his request before the stipulated date.
The petitioner was entitled to remain in service after making an offer of voluntary retirement, but was instead suspended and threatened with disciplinary action.
He had the right to withdraw his offer at any time prior to the stipulated date.
The government did not convey any order declining the petitioner's request to withdraw his offer of voluntary retirement.
Petitioner's retirement order with retrospective effect from April 30, 1993 was illegal and could not be sustained.
[Paras 6 to 10]
Article 226 of the Constitution of India - Premature retirement/voluntary retirement - The petitioner was suspended and being paid subsistence allowance when the government retired him with effect from April 30, 1993.
The government could not have compulsorily retired him before removing the cloud over him.
Their decision to retire him was therefore considered dubious.
However, the impugned order was quashed and the petitioner will be deemed to have been in service until his superannuation date on November 30, 1993.
