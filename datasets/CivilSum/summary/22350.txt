According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, seniority is important for ad hoc appointments as Assistant Engineers, with regularisation only possible through relaxation in conditions of service.
Seniority should only be determined from the date of regularisation, with officiation in ad hoc posts not being taken into account for seniority.
This is further clarified in JT 1990(2) SC 264.
[Paras 17, 18, 21 to 25]
