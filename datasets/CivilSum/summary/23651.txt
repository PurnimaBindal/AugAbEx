Family Pension Rules 1964, Rule 801 - Rules do not allow for the nomination of a beneficiary by the deceased employee - Therefore, the employee cannot dispose of the pension through a will - The widow of the deceased employee has the right to receive the family pension - The appeal has been granted.

Family Pension Rules, 1964, Rule 801 - The Railway Quarter was allotted to the deceased and family pension was not given to the widow - They may evict the widow by following the rules only after paying the arrears of family pension to the widow - Rent must be charged at the rate at which the quarter was let out to the deceased - The appeal has been granted.
