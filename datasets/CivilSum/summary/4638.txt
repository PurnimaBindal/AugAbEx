The Government Residences (Chandigarh Administration Pool) Allotment Rules, 1972, Rule 26 under the Constitution of India Articles 14 and 226, focuses on the allotment of government accommodation to government servants.
Journalists and Press Correspondents are not covered in the ambit of these rules and therefore are not entitled to government residential accommodation.
Allotment in their favor lacks legal sanction and the discretion in the rules has to be exercised in the public good.
Moreover, such allotment cannot be made arbitrarily as it must follow the principle of reasonableness and be absent of arbitrariness according to our constitution.
Any allotment in favor of journalists and Press Correspondents will be regarded as a bounty from the Government, as the residential accommodation is scarce and rents are high in Chandigarh.
Therefore, the government must take necessary steps and refrain from making any further allotment of accommodation in favor of journalists and Press Correspondents.
