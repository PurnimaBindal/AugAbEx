Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Termination - A probationer's services can be terminated for unsatisfactory service during an extended period of probation- A termination order that does not indicate any stigma must be considered by the learned Single Judge - The learned Single Judge was required to consider the order of termination and not the reasons for the action taken by the employer - The order of the learned Single Judge was set aside.
