Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - Employee transfer from Panchayat `A' to Panchayat `M'- Cancellation of transfer order due to financial constraints - The employee has no legal right to continue at place `A' indefinitely when that Panchayat does not intend to take the employee’s services - The Court cannot interfere under Article 226 of the Constitution.
