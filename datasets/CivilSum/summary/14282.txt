Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Posting - A constable in BSF, who was posted in Jammu and Kashmir, requested for cancellation of transfer due to psychiatric ailment - Petitioner claimed entitlement to remain in his parent unit in Delhi as per Posting/Transfer and Tenure Rules of the Govt. of India - The court determined that the respondent's order for posting was a normal course of functioning with no illegality or irrationality - It was not within the purview of the court to interfere with administrative functions, and thus dismissed the petition.
