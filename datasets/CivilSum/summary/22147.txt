Army Rules, 1954 Rule 13-A - Army Act, 1950, Section 19 - Defence Service Regulations, Part 79 - Compulsory retirement - Retirement of an Army personal for failing to pass Part B and Part D examination within the specified duration is in accordance with Section 19 of Army Act and Rule 13-A of Army Rules, as stated in the amended Army Instructions from 20.8.1999 (applicable since 24.4.1998) - Those who did not pass Part B examination within 13 years timeframe were held responsible as per Rules - The fact that the final order was issued only in 2001 does not alter the situation - The revised provisions can't be put into effect retrospectively.
