Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation in admissions - Petitioner contends that reserved seats for army personnel exceed 50% quota or combined with seats for Punjab residents exceed the quota and are illegal - Court holds that reservation of 9 seats for Punjab residents (15%) is permissible as it is based on region - Remaining 85% of seats are not reservation, but a source of recruitment based on the objective of benefitting the wards of the Army Personnel - Distribution of seats is valid and not related to any caste, region, religion, province or zone.
