As per the instructions dated January 11, 1971, the petitioner, belonging to a backward class, was entitled to be considered for the post reserved for backward classes at roster point No. 15, which fell vacant in August 1966.
However, this post was filled up by promoting a general category candidate.
The petitioner claimed promotion against the same post, but the instructions clearly stated that cases of promotions made before September 19, 1967, would not be reviewed.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to any relief.
