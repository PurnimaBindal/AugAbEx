The Industrial Descartes Act, 1947, Section 22(2)(b) and the Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 were referred to when a conductor working with the State Road Transport Corporation was discharged from service by the managing director of the corporation.
While an industrial dispute was pending before the tribunal, the corporation moved an application u/s 33(2)(b) for approval of the action taken.
However, the tribunal rejected the application because the proviso to Section 33(2)(b) had not been complied with and the application was not maintainable due to a delay of ten days in making it.
The employer had not explained the delay and all three conditions for simultaneous compliance - namely, dismissal or discharge, payment of wages and making of an application for approval - had not been met.
The employer must also explain any delay to show that it was not deliberate.
The interference was not warranted.
