Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 21 - Employees Family Pension Scheme, 1995, Para 12(4) - VRS - Pension - Revised pension scheme - A petitioner who took voluntary retirement at the age of 52 years and was a member of Family Pension Scheme, 1971 must have their pension determined in accordance with Para 12(4)(a) and (b) of Family Pension Scheme, 1995.
The respondents have been directed to refix the petitioner's pension.
