Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Stay of - Petitioner seeks stay of departmental proceedings due to ongoing criminal action relating to the same incident, and presence of common witnesses.
Petitioner has already provided explanation in departmental proceedings, and disclosure of defence will not impact criminal trial.
Criminal proceedings aim to punish accused under IPC, while departmental proceedings aim to take action against an erring official under standing orders or other enactment.
Enquiry officer has already been appointed and enquiry is almost complete, hence, staying departmental proceedings is not advisable.
