Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - The petitioner, a casual worker on daily wages, demands the salary of a temporary worker on the basis of equal pay for equal work - The Labour Court allowed the application under Section 33-C(2) for the salary and bonus difference claimed by him, which is not appropriate - The court held that when a right has not been determined yet, Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Dispute Act cannot be applied to the case - The order of the Labour Court has been canceled.
