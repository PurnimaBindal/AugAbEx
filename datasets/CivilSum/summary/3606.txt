According to the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4(1) Explanation II (as amended by Act 46 of 2000), the rate of compensation for an accident must be determined based on the date of the accident, not the date of adjudication of the claim.
Even though the Act was amended on 8122000 to substitute the words "two thousand rupees" with "four thousand rupees," this amendment cannot be applied retrospectively since the accident occurred on 17-8-2000.
