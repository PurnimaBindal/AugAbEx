Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Adverse remarks - Annual Confidential Report - Promotion - The number of ACRs taken into consideration for promotion - Promotion can be denied due to remarks in the ACR depicting non-cooperation with colleagues.
Other remarks in the ACR show 'average', 'good', and 'very good' behaviour.
After evaluation in the last ACR, the petitioner's behaviour with colleagues is improved and recorded as satisfactory.
Nothing of grave nature can be found in the ACRs.
Directions have been given to consider promoting the petitioner.
The rejection of representations of adverse remarks has been quashed.
