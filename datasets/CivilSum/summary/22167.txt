Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - Executive Office of Nagar Palika - Petitioner holding transferable post - The transfer is an administrative decision made as an exigency of service.
Interference by Courts with transfer orders should be very rare.
Held, inter alia: (i) Courts of law should not ordinarily interfere with an order of transfer issued to an employee as it is part of their service conditions, unless the order can be proven to be mala fide or that such transfer is prohibited by the service rules or that the authorities who gave the orders were not authorized to issue them.
(ii) Even if the appellant's allegations are correct that he was transferred at the recommendation of an MLA, this would not necessarily invalidate the transfer order.
(iii) Not all transfers at the instance of an M.P. or MLA would be vitiated; it depends on the facts & circumstances of each individual case.
[Paras 7 and 8]
