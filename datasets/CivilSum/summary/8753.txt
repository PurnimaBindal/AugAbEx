Under Army Act, 1950, Section 19 and Army Rules, 1954, Rule 14, Singla Judge ordered for full back wages and all other benefits to be paid to respondent/writ petitioner from the date of his removal from service till his retirement on superannuation, along with pensionary and other retiral benefits, after setting aside an order of dismissal due to a misconduct incident that allegedly occurred on 591983.
No trial by court martial by any person subject to the Act for any offence shall be commenced after expiry of period of three years from the date of the incident stated in Section 122 of the Army Act.
Ultimately, the dismissal order and show cause notice were declared illegal and without jurisdiction.
The Single Judge's conclusion that Rule 14 could not have been invoked was upheld.
