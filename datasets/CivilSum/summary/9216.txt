Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Reference - Delay - The award of Labour Court was challenged via writ, in which the Presiding Officer ordered the reinstatement of the workman with continuity of service and also held him entitled to back wages from the date of reference.
The workman had been involved in a theft case, with an FIR registered against him.
The Management could have proceeded against the workman departmentally and, after holding him guilty, terminated his services.
This was feasible even before the Labour Court.
The delay in seeking reference assumes significance since the Management may not be left with any evidence to prove the guilt.
Re-opening the case after a long time without giving any explanation of delay would be too inequitable and disadvantageous to the Management.
As a result, the award of the Labour Court was quashed.
