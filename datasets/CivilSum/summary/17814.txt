Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment as ASI against reserved vacancy for ex-serviceman - The benefit of reservation cannot be denied to the petitioner simply because they have previously taken advantage of it in their appointment as ASI.
They are entitled to be considered for the post of Inspector of Police against a reserved post for ex-servicemen.
[Paras 3 and 4] It is important to note that if a person takes advantage of reservations for a certain post, they cannot be denied the same benefit for a post for which they are actually qualified.
It should also be noted that even if a person applies for a lower post to make ends meet, they should still be eligible for reservations for the post they are qualified for.
