The Haryana Police Clerical (State Service Class III) Rules, 1977, Rule 9 r/w Appendix `B' - Promotion - Seniority-cum-fitness - According to seniority-cum-fitness basis, the senior person should be promoted despite the better merit of a junior, unless the senior person is found unfit.
Competitive consideration of all eligible persons is absent under this promotion method.
[Para 5]

Promotion - Criteria - Seniority-cum-merit - According to seniority-cum-merit basis, the seniormost employee should be considered for promotion and if found fit, should be promoted, unless unfitness/unsuitability is established.
[Para 6]

The Haryana Police Clerical (State Service Class III) Rules, 1977, Rule 9 r/w Appendix `B'- Promotion- Test - The promotion test is not a requirement under the scheme of the 1977 Rules.
Unless there is an allegation of unfitness to be promoted, the respondents cannot supersede petitioners on the ground that they could not clear the promotion test.
[Para 7]

The Haryana Police Clerical (State Service Class III) Rules, 1977, Rule 9 r/w Appendix `B'- Promotion - Test - Respondent No.2 DIG Police has no power to make or amend the statutory rules.
By adding a promotion test as a condition of eligibility, Respondent No.2 has effectively amended the 1977 Rules.
As such, his action is without jurisdiction.
[Para 8]

The Haryana Police Clerical (State Service Class III) Rules, 1977, Rule 9 r/w Appendix `B'- Promotion - The petitioners are senior and should be promoted as Clerks, effective from the date on which juniors to them have been promoted.
