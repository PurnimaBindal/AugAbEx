Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8 - Termination of service - Role of Inquiry Officer - Cross-examination by Inquiry Officer - Imposition of major punishment in violation of procedure - When the Inquiry Officer serves as both prosecutor and adjudicator, the defendant is likely to be prejudiced - An acting judge cum prosecutor is unlikely to be impartial - Cross-examination of the delinquent by the Inquiry Officer - An irregularity that can prejudice the delinquent - Punishment order based on such an inquiry is unsustainable.
