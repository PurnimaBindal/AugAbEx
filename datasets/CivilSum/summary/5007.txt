Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary Enquiry - Show cause notice - A bus conductor was accused of selling forged tickets to passengers - Following an enquiry, a show cause notice was issued and an order of removal was passed - The Labour Court found that the conductor was not responsible for getting the tickets printed and set aside the dismissal - Despite this, it was noted that the charge of selling forged tickets was still serious - An award that imposed a minor punishment was ultimately quashed.
