As per Section 10 of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, the Central Advisory Contract Labour Board did not suggest getting rid of the contract labour system for trolley retrievers, and their decision can be challenged.
It seems that the work these retrievers do is vital for the airport to function smoothly, with flights coming and going throughout the day, and it is a permanent job.
Pending the final decision of the writ petition, the petitioners have been granted status quo for their services.
