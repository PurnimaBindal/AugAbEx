Ex-Servicemen (Re-employment in Central Civil Services and Posts) Rules, 1979, Rule 6(3) - Appointment to any Group C Post - Amended eligibility rules state that matriculate candidates with at least 15 years of service in Armed Forces can be considered for any Group 'C' post requiring graduation as essential qualification and no technical or professional experience - Respondent applied for Data Entry Operator, Grade B - Prescribed qualification was graduation with Mathematics or Statistics - Work experience of technical or Professional nature not essential - Respondent successfully passed written and viva voce tests - High Court ordered petitioner to accommodate the respondent on a suitable post within a specified period and the petitioner assured to comply with the order - Appeal dismissed with costs Rs. 50,000/-.
