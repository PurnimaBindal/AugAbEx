Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Disciplinary proceedings - Tribunal’s jurisdiction - Workman accused of neglect of duties - Dismissal of workman - Misconduct unrelated to pending dispute - Tribunal needs to assess if employer has established prima facie case for the intended action against workman - Verdict states that it is not the Tribunal’s role to evaluate the appropriateness or sufficiency of the penalty - This falls under the prerogative of the management.
