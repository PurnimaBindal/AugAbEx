Constitution of India, Article 311 - Suspension/Departmental Enquiry/Preliminary Enquiry - Depending on the facts and circumstances of each case, an employer may or may not conduct a preliminary inquiry before taking any action of suspension.

[Para 13] C. Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Suspension/Appointing Authority - There is no rule that mandates that only an Appointing Authority may take an action of suspension as it is not regarded as dismissal, removal or reduction in rank.
