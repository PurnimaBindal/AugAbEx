Appointment to the post of Clerk was made in excess of advertised posts, with the selection process suffering from allegations of irregularities.
The Registrar Cooperative Societies rescinded the appointments on the grounds of non-compliance with statutory provisions and tampering with the answer papers and marks list in the written test.
However, the Bank and affected parties were not given a copy of the report of the Public Inquiry Commission, which the Registrar and Division Bench of the High Court relied upon.
As a result, the appointment was considered a gross violation of the principle of natural justice.
Despite this, the appointment made was held to be valid, with appeals being allowed.
