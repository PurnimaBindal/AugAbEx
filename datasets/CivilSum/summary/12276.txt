Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947) Schedule 2, Item 6 - Banking Companies (Acquisition & Transfer of Undertakings) Act (5 of 1970) Section 19 - Removal from Service - Quantum of punishment - Delinquent given sufficient chance to defend against charges of committing financial irregularities - Found guilty of imputations such as lack of integrity, honesty, and devotion, and acts highly prejudicial to Bank's interest - Argument of disproportionate punishment with respect to offence rejected - Punishment considered justified due to the serious nature of the allegations which involve financial irregularities - A bank relies heavily on public trust, and if that trust is breached by employee dishonesty, the interest of the bank will suffer - Hence, no room for leniency against such acts.
