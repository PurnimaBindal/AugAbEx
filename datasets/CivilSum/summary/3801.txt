The Andhra Pradesh Co-operative Societies Act of 1964, Section 61 and Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 11-A, Schedule 2, Item 6 was violated by retrenching a co-operative society employee without notice and retrenchment compensation.
The Labour Court found this to be illegal.
The court directed the junior employees to be retained and ordered the employee to be reinstated and absorbed, which was not perverse.
[Paras 5 & 6]
The Andhra Pradesh Co-operative Societies Act of 1964, Section 1 does not specifically exclude the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 11-A, Schedule 2, Item 6.
[Para 6]
The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 11-A grants the Labour Court jurisdiction to order the absorption of an illegally retrenched employee.
The term "such other relief to workman" includes relief of absorption in regular service.
The Labour Court's directive was not without jurisdiction.
[Para 6]
