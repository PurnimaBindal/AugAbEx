The Posts and Telegraphs Extra Departmental Agents (Conduct and Service) Rules, 1964, Rule 18 states that those holding an elective office are disqualified from appointment to the post of EDBPM.
In this case, the Petitioner's appointment was set aside by the Tribunal because they were holding the position of President of a society on the date of appointment.
However, it was determined that the term "elective office" in the relevant notification refers to offices related to political parties or organizations.
As a result, the order of the Tribunal was found to have legal flaws and the Petitioner's petition was allowed.
