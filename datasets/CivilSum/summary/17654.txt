Punjab University Celandar, Volume-I, Chapter VIII(E), 1989 Edition, Regulation 23 - Probation/Confirmation/Temporary appointment - Regulation 23 applies only in cases where a teacher is appointed on probation for a permanent vacancy.
If appointed against a temporary vacancy, the teacher remains temporary until the post becomes permanent and can only be confirmed from the date the post becomes permanent and their work and conduct found to be satisfactory.
Therefore, Regulation 23 is not applicable in cases where a person is working against a temporary post.
[Para 7]
Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Act, 1974, Section 2-A - Probation - Section 2-A of the Act applies when the post is likely to exist for more than one year and provides for a one-year probationary period.
However, if the post is not expected to exist for more than one year, the section has no application.
In this case, the college has already received communication that admission in plus-I classes will be discontinued from the next session.
This means that the services of the petitioner were extended before the expiration of one year, but it does not imply that the petitioner was appointed against a vacancy likely to exist for more than a year.
Therefore, Section 2-A of the 1974 Act does not apply.
[Para 8]
Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Act, 1974, Section 7 - Retrenchment/Re-employment - Section 7 of the Act applies when an employee has been relieved from an affiliated college as a result of retrenchment, and the employee receives preference for appointment to future vacancies.
However, the petitioner was not retrenched, but their services were dispensed with in terms of their appointment.
While their writ petition was pending, the college offered the petitioner a fresh appointment, which the petitioner would accept, provided that the order of termination was first set aside.
This condition was not acceptable to the college, and as a result, the petitioner was not entitled to the benefit of re-employment when their writ petition was dismissed.
