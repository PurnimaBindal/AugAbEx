According to Section 30 of the Workmen Compensation Act 1923, an appeal cannot be made without a certificate from the commissioner regarding the deposit of the payable amount.
Despite injuries sustained by workmen during employment, the employer's writ petition is not maintainable since it does not conform to the conditions set out in the Act.
The deputy commissioner awarded compensation to the workmen, and the employer was required to deposit the amount before proceeding with an appeal.
The current petition was filed to avoid the compulsory deposit and was consequently dismissed.
