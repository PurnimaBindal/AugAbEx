MP Nagariya Sthawar Sampatti Kar Adhiniyam, 1964, Sections 4 and 33 - Levy of tax on immovable property - A building used for charitable purposes, such as running a school by a Trust on no-profit no-loss basis, is exempted from levy of tax under the Act.
However, the exemption depends on the building being used solely for charitable purposes.
Assessment order was quashed in this case since the building was being used only to run a school and income from it was being spent solely on its upkeep.

Civil Procedure Code, Section 92 - Trust - A writ filed by the Secretary of the Trust in the interest of the Trust - The death of the Secretary through whom the petition was filed is immaterial since the institution is a corporate one and the death of its Secretary does not end the institution.
The Implementing authority will comply with the judgment.
