Punjab Municipal Employees Pension and General Provident Fund Rules, 1994, Rules 3 - Pension - The petitioner retired from service on 31.10.1992 and was declined pension due to failure to exercise their option within a stipulated period.
However, there was no provision under the rules indicating how long retired employees had to exercise their option and the condition of exercising an option was never personally notified to them.
Therefore, the authorities were not justified in their objection and an opportunity was not provided to the petitioner to opt for the benefit of the Rules within the stipulated period.
The authorities are obligated to disburse the pensionary benefits to the petitioner, as expected under the Rules.
The writ has been granted.
