According to the Pension Regulations for the Army 1961, Regulation 173, Appendix II, disability pension must be determined based on the report of the Medical Board.
Even if the list of diseases in Appendix II is not exhaustive, the board still has the power to grant relief to Army personnel afflicted by other diseases that developed as a result of their employment and cantonment environment.
If a re-survey Medical Board doesn't consider all relevant factors in assessing a 60% disability as being unrelated to military service, the Constitution orders the formation of another Re-survey Medical Board to review the attributability of that disease to service.
