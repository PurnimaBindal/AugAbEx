Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Public Works Department (Buildings and Roads Branch) Sectional Officers Service (Class III) Rule 1964, Rules 14 and 19 - Natural justice - Crossing of efficiency bar - The departmental examination must be regularly conducted as a prerequisite for the implementation of Rule 14 of the 1964 Rules - It is unjust for employees to suffer the consequences of the management's failure to hold the examination regularly and it is not acceptable to withhold promotions - If there is no test conducted, employees are entitled to annual grade increments and are able to cross the efficiency bar in the due process.
