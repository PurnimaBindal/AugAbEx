Prevention of Food Adulteration Act, Section 16(1) (a)(i) - Accused convicted after Bura sugar sample found adulterated.
The rule for Bura sugar changed subsequently with a new standard prescribed.
If the new standard is used, then the sample of Bura sugar is not adulterated.
Hence, the new standard should have retrospective operation.
Based on the 1974 PFA 21 judgment, the accused is acquitted.
