Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 7 - Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 10(c) - Dispute for regularization of contract workers in an establishment - If a notification under CLRA is not issued, a reference would still be made under the Industrial Disputes Act.
It is not permitted in law to decline a reference on the grounds that the union could not substantiate their contention, as that would be acting in issue and forbidden by law.
