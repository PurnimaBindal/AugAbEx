The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 21 address regularisation of service and family pension, respectively.
Court orders mandated that a workman be reinstated with back wages and the government instructed that those who met a certain service period and were employed on May 7, 1993, should be regularised.
The workman was still alive when the courts and government issued these orders, so even if the management did not reinstate him, he would still be considered employed on May 7, 1993, and eligible for regularisation.
As a result, the workman's wife and children are entitled to family pension.
