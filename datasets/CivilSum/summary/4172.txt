Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Haryana State Central Cooperative Banks' Staff (Common Cadre) Rules, 1975, Rule 9.6 - Termination - Reference - The deceased's services were terminated by the Bank, but no retrenchment compensation or notice pay was provided - The Labour Court concluded that while the deceased did not have the necessary qualifications for the Secretary post, the Bank could not direct the President to terminate his services without compensation - With the enforcement of Rules, 1975, the Bank became the employer for all cooperative society employees - Thus, it was the Bank's obligation to pay retrenchment compensation to the deceased - The impugned award from the Labour Court was not faulty.
