Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10(1)(c) - Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 - Reference/Res judicata - Withdrawing a civil suit with permission to file anew on the same cause of action - Reference under Section 10(1)(c) is not barred by res judicata as no determination of rights on merits occurred.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(s) - Workman - Managerial capacity - Secretary - Whether a workman is considered within the definition of 'Workman' or not depends on the fact in what capacity he was discharging his duties, which cannot be raised first in writ jurisdiction if not taken in the labour court.

Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Delay - Petitioner filed suit and withdrew to file anew on the same cause of action - Filed demand notice after about 2-1/2 years of withdrawal of suit - The delay at most would disentitle the petitioner to back wages for that period but from the date of demand notice.
