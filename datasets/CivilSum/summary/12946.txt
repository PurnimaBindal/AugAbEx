Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - Pay scale - Post of Mechanical Fitter - Despite a condition that the petitioner will be paid the salary of a Fitter Coolie, he was promoted in the public interest to the post of Mechanical Fitter - According to the law, if an employee is assigned higher duties, he should receive the pay scale of that higher post - Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to the pay scale of Mechanical Fitter.
