Constitution of India, Article 16, 32 and 226 - Petitioner seeking promotion to the post of UDC - No adverse entry until the date of consideration - Petitioner filed case after 9 years of supersession - Respondents contend that petition was filed after a long delay and that the 15 LDCs who superseded the petitioner have not been made a party - Delay and laches cannot prevent the petitioner from establishing a right that has already accrued to her - Fundamental right of equal opportunity under Article 16 is guaranteed by Article 32 of the Constitution - Relief will not affect seniority of those already promoted - Court orders re-consideration of the case of Petitioner after giving an opportunity for hearing and perusing ACRs - Petition allowed.
