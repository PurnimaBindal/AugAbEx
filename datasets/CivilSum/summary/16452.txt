In accordance with the Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, and the Central Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, Rule 5(1), the service of an under-trainee constable may be terminated if they are suffering from tuberculosis and require continuous treatment as directed by a Medical Board.
In light of the relevant rules and particular circumstances, it was determined that the orders did not violate any regulations and did not require interference.
