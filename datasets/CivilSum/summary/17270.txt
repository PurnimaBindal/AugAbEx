Maharishi Dayanand University Statute 38, Clause 25(1) - Governing Body meeting - If a meeting of the Governing Body of the college is held in the absence of the Vice Chancellor's nominee, the said meeting is invalid, according to the statute.
However, if the nominee confirms his or her presence in writing and is unable to attend for any reason, the Governing Body meeting held in his or her absence will be valid.
In this case, the nominee of the VC proposed the holding of the meeting and confirmed his attendance in writing.
Therefore, the proceedings of the meeting are considered valid, and the cancellation order of the VC has been quashed with a cost of Rs. 5000/- to be paid by the VC out of his own pocket.
