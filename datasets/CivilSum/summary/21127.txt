Constitution of India, Article 16 - Dena Bank (Officer's) Service Regulations, 1979 - Pay - The appellant, who was appointed as an Economic Officer of the Dena Bank in the pay scale I as laid down by the Pillai Committee, is entitled to receive a salary of Rs. 700-800 along with other allowances, perquisites and benefits, in pursuance of the appointment letter dated 26th May, 1978.
Although the Pillai Committee recommendations relating to pay of Bank Employees and their fitment were formally made effective from 1st July, 1979, they had come to be implemented w.e.f.
1st May, 1978.
However, the appellant is not entitled to any higher emolument on the score of fitment, but is entitled to receive more D.A.
as per Chart I given in the booklet titled "Pay Scales, Allowances and Pre-requisites of Officers in the Nationalised Banks".
[Paras 8 and 9]
