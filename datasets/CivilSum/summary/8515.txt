According to Section 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act, if a judicial officer is held in contempt of court, they may transfer the case and the proceedings will be stayed during the pendency of the application.
In this case, despite the Distt.
Judge's written notice of the transfer application, the Additional Distt.
Judge proceeded with the appeal and handed down interim orders that were detrimental to the petitioner.
The Additional Distt.
Judge was found guilty of willfully violating the orders of the Distt.
Judge and was fined Rs. 1000/-.
It was further ruled that no judicial officer should have an interest in hearing a particular case and should avoid such controversies.
