Constitution of India, Article 16 - When are departmental proceedings initiated against an employee to be stayed due to criminal prosecution for the same cause of action - The petitioner do not have the right to demand that the enquiry proceedings against them be put on hold until the conclusion of the criminal trial - The criminal and disciplinary proceedings have different objectives and approach - In disciplinary proceedings, the question is whether the respondent is guilty of conduct worthy of removal from service or a lesser punishment - In criminal proceedings, the question is what sentence should be imposed if the crime is proven - The standard of proof, mode of enquiry, and rules governing the enquiries and trials are different.
[Paras 9 and 13]
