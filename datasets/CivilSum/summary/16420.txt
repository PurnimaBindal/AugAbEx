Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Delay in release of retiral benefits - Claim of interest - The court held that the respondents should have clarified the nature of service rendered by the petitioner beforehand, according to instructions indicating that pension cases of retiring employees must be initiated six months prior to retirement to ensure payments are made on the retirement date.
The court granted interest at a rate of 9% from the date of accrual until the payment date.
