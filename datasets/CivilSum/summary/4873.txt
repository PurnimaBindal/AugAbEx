Army Regulations, Regulation 163 and Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural justice must be given when promoting a junior over a senior employee, even if there is no provision for notice and hearing - A breach of natural justice in the granting of ante dated promotion and seniority cannot be sustained.
[Para 7]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Administrative action must be taken in a timely fashion - A delay of 10 years without explanation in granting ante dated seniority and promotion resulting in the supersession of a senior employee cannot be sustained.
[Para 8]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, and Army Regulations, Regulation 163 - Declaration of unsustainable promotion and seniority post retirement - Only notional promotion and consequential financial benefits can be awarded to the petitioner for the extended period of service on actual promotion - Any adverse orders against wrongly promoted employee after retirement would be unfair and unjustified.
