Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules, 1957, Rule 10-B, Clauses 8 and 9 - Recording of evidence - Despite four opportunities, the workman did not lead his evidence - In turn, the Labour Court closed the evidence of the workman and set the case for arguments - Although the application for review of the order was dismissed - The penalty orders of serious consequences in procedural law should normally be proceeded by orders of lesser gravity - Therefore, it was held that the workman could have been granted another opportunity with the caveat that costs would be issued.
