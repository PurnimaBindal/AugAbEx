The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 310 and 226, and the Army Regulations, Vol.
I, Paras 104(d) and 105(f&d) deal with the issue of resignation.
A petitioner who had served in the Army as a doctor submitted his resignation on family grounds after years of service.
The request was rejected twice on the ground of exigency of service, which can only be a ground for keeping the application in abeyance.
The third application was also rejected on the same ground without any disclosure of efforts made to find a substitute or how long it would take to make an alternative arrangement.
The authorities have been ordered to reconsider the request sympathetically in accordance with the regulations.
