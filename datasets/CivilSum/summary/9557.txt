Petitioner Mr. PK Mutneja, Advocate, Respondent No. 1 Mr. SK Bhanot, D.A.G., Punjab, Respondent No. 2 Mr. JS Narang, Sr. Advocate with Mr. Baljinder Singh, Advocate, Respondent Nos. 3 to 8 Mr. Sunil Chadha, Advocate, and Respondent No. 9 Mr. Rajiv Raina, Advocate, are involved in a case related to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
According to Section 25-O(1) of the act, closing down of the factory without applying for permission to close down the undertaking is illegal.
Thus, the closing down of the undertaking is the foundation of settlement, and if the foundation itself is non-existent, any settlement arrived at is also void.
Moreover, according to a conjoint reading of Sections 25-J, 25-N, and 25-S, any industrial dispute related to retrenchment of a workman employed in an industrial establishment shall be settled only in accordance with the provisions of Section 25-N Also, before an employer decides to close down his industrial undertaking or retrench the employees, any settlement in contravention thereof is not binding on the workman, according to Sections 25-O(1) and 25-N(1)(b) of the act.
