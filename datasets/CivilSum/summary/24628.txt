Constitution of India, Articles 14, 300-A and 311 - Civil Services Pension Rules, 1972, Rules 8 and 9 - Illness of wife leading to absence from duty - Disciplinary proceedings - Voluntary retirement - No finding of grave misconduct - Validity of order withholding gratuity and pension considered - Ruling - Order is not valid.
[Paras 9 and 10] The withholding of pension as a punishment is illegal under Civil Services Rules 1972, Rules 8 and 9, in the absence of any findings of misconduct.
