Punjab State Supply and Marketing Co-operative Services (Common Cadre) Rules, 1967, Rule 214 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 39 Rules 1 & 2 - Temporary injunction/Departmental proceedings - The plaintiff's recovery orders were disagreed with by the punishing authority after an enquiry - Temporary injunctions should only be granted if there is a prima facie case, balance of convenience, and irreparable loss - It should be determined on the merits of the case whether Rule 214 of the Rules has been complied with - There is no basis to grant a temporary injunction - The court's preceding orders are annulled.
