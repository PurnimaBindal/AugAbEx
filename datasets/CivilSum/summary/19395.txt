Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 7 - Embezzlement - Dishonest intention - The petitioner was accused of making cutting and over-writings in the auction register with dishonest intention, but there was no charge of actual embezzlement - The enquiry officer acquitted the petitioner of any dishonest intention, yet the punishing authority awarded punishment without returning any finding of dishonest intention - The order of punishment was deemed illegal and must be quashed.
