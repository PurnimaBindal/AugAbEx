Rajasthan Judicial Service Competitive Examination - Examination/Syllabus/Language paper/Proficiency - Questions out of syllabus - Questions such as essay, expansion of an idea, precise writing and translation are not foreign to the language.
Instead, they are included in the language paper to assess a candidate's proficiency in utilizing language, style of writing, and expression skills.
Hence, it is not appropriate to consider such questions as out of syllabus.
[Paras 3, 4 and 5] B Syllabus/Questions out of syllabus - Mathematics - Arithmetic, Algebra and Geometry are different branches of mathematics, which is the science of space and numbers.
While Algebra, Geometry, and Statistics are not the branches of Arithmetic, it is incorrect to ask questions related to Algebra, Geometry and Statistics in a paper concerning Arithmetic.
