Constitution of India, Article 16 - Transfer Seniority - Reckoning of - Petitioner initially working at Primary Health Centre - Transferred to the Medical Education Department dated 831983 which provided that he shall retain his lien and promotional prospects - Petitioner argued his name was missing from the seniority list - It was submitted that his service in Medical Education Deptt.
was liable to be reckoned from the date of his joining there to avoid forfeiture of his services rendered in the government - Court held that the petitioner was entitled to be treated in service of the Medical Education Deptt from 2331983 for all purposes related to his service benefits including determination of his seniority and that seniority should be computed from this date and not from the date of order shifting his lien.
