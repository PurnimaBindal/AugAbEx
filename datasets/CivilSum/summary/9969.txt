Sanjeev Manrai, AAG, presents the Punjab Civil Service (Executive Branch) Rules, 1930 (as applicable to the State of Haryana), along with the Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311.
The case involves adverse remarks and vacancies belonging to Register A-I covering District Revenue Officers/Tehsildars/Naib-Tehsildars notified for appointment to H.C.S.
(E.B.).
The petitioner had expunged adverse remarks against him, and so he has the right to be reconsidered for being brought on Register A-I w.e.f.
the date when he was ignored for the same.
In case the petitioner is found suitable for appointment, the persons already appointed including the respondent would not be disturbed, and they would continue to hold the posts.
The State Govt. would create a supernumerary post in that eventuality.
[Paras 6, 7 and 91]
