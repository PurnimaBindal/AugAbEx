Deceased, who was an operator, was instructed by his employer to attend a fuse call that was not in his job description, resulting in his death due to shock.
According to the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 3, the employer is responsible for providing compensation in such cases.
Although the deceased's duty was to take readings at the control room and not attend fuse calls, if he had acted on instructions given by his superior in good faith to meet an emergency that resulted in his death, the accident would be considered to have occurred during the course of his employment.
The claimant is thus entitled to compensation.
