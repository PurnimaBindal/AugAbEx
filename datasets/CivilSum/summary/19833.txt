Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Reversion to General Cadre - Experience - The petitioner was initially selected as Registrar Urology and later as Senior Lecturer (Surgery).
The government tried to revert him on the basis of his experience in Urology, which the petitioner countered by stating that there was no Urology department in the institution and he only worked as Registrar (Surgery).
The petitioner fulfilled all qualifications for Senior Lecturer (Surgery), and cannot be reverted to the General Cadre from the teaching cadre.
