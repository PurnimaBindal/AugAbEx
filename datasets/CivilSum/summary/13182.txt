Constitution of India, Article 226 - Person with Disability (Equal Opportunity Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1996, Section 39 - Education - Admission - Reservation - Petitioner argues for admission based on reservation - Respondents claim that Pre PG examination and PG courses are under ordinance with no reservation - The Act mandates that institutions reserve not less than 3% seats for persons with disabilities - Institutions have a legal obligation to fulfill this mandate - Learned single judge was right to direct authorities to give benefit of reservation as required.
