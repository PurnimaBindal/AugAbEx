In the context of admission to engineering courses, the petitioner submitted a certified copy of their 10+2 certificate instead of the original.
Without giving the petitioner an opportunity to produce the original and without verifying the copy with the university, admission was denied.
The college also did not offer provisional admission pending the production of the original certificate.
This denial of admission was deemed unfair and the Regional Engineering College in Kurukshetra was ordered to grant admission to the petitioner even if it required a lower-ranked candidate to make room.
