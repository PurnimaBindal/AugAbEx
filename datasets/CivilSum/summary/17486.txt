According to the Constitution of India's Articles 311 and 16 regarding seniority on rejoining after accepting resignation, a petitioner appointed as Clerk on 15970 had her resignation accepted on 26.10.77 and was relieved of her duties, but later withdrew her resignation on 16.11.77 and was allowed to rejoin.
However, the Milk Commissioner Punjab could not permit her to withdraw her resignation as it had already been accepted and she was relieved from 26.10.77, meaning she could only be considered a fresh entrant and could not claim seniority as clerk from her original appointment date of 15970.
