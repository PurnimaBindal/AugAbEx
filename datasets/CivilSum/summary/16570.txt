Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - The Industrial Tribunal rejected an application filed by the employer under Section 33(2)(b) of the Industrial Disputes Act seeking approval of dismissal of a workman - The Tribunal found the departmental enquiry to be unfair and that compliance of section 33(2)(b) of the Act had not been made - Therefore, the Tribunal should have allowed the employer to prove the charges against the workman - The employer challenged the Impugned order which was quashed and matter remanded back to the Tribunal.
