According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Article 137 of the Limitation Act, there is no limit on raising demands by workmen and seeking references under the Industrial Disputes Act.
The court cannot interpret or make law by applying any provision of law applicable to the proceedings under the ID Act.
For delays, the court may apply a reasonable cut on back wages but cannot set aside full bench judgment of Punjab & Haryana High Court.
[Paras 10 and 12] B.
If the management has not pleaded delay to the Labor Court and no issue has been framed, the High Court cannot deprive the workman of the benefits under the ID Act.
The I.D.
Act exists to protect workmen and the fight between the workman and management is not one between equals.
