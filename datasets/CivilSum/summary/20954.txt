Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 2(oo) Clause (bb) and 25-F - Retrenchment - The respondents only worked during crushing seasons - They were hired for the season and ceased work when it ended - The question of whether this amounts to retrenchment arises - However, since the work is only seasonal, the respondents cannot be deemed retrenched.
