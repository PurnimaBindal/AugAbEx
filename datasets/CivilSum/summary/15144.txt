MTNL (Conduct, Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1998, Rule 5(43) - Departmental Proceedings - Validity of - Misconduct - The jurisdiction to initiate departmental proceedings lies with MTNL in the case of an employee whose services have been absorbed, even if the alleged misconduct pertained to the period before he was absorbed by MTNL.
Rule 5(43) gives MTNL the power to initiate departmental proceedings, and this power has to be given the widest amplitude.
The fact that MTNL noticed the alleged misconduct subsequently indicates a correspondence or communication between DOT and MTNL.
Therefore, the initiation of departmental proceedings cannot be considered improper.
[Paras 10, 11 & 12]
