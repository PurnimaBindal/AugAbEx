According to the rules outlined in the Punjab Public Works Department (Irrigation Branch), Circles' Clerical State Service, Class-III Regulations, promotion must follow circle-wise, even if a joint seniority list is issued by the Chief Engineer.
This seniority list cannot replace the Rules established by the Governor under Article 309 of the Constitution.
In this case, the petitioner was unable to apply for a promotion to the post of Accounts Clerk as no vacancies were available in his circle.
Therefore, the Court does not see any legal violation of the petitioner's rights and dismisses the petition.
