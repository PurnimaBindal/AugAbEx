Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reimbursement/Medical treatment - Approved institutes - Approval of Medical Board - Treatment does not have to be obtained in Government institutions with prior approval of the Medical Board - The petitioner received treatment for their heart disease in an approved institute as instructed by the Government - Denial of reimbursement on the basis that such treatment was available in state institutions is completely arbitrary and unfair - Waiting for admission to a Government institution for treatment of a disease like a heart attack is not feasible - Respondents directed to reimburse amount with 12% interest - Petition allowed with costs of Rs. 1000/-.
