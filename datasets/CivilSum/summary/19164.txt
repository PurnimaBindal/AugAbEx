Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Release of pensionary benefits - The petitioner was charge-sheeted and underwent an enquiry that resulted in a 10% cut in pension to be received with effect from 171992, because of his alleged failure to object to the method of purchasing medicines as an Accounts Officer.
Meanwhile, the Civil Surgeon, who was responsible for the purchase, was not given any enforceable punishment.
The court allowed the petition, quashed the impugned order, and remitted the matter to the competent authority for a fresh decision to be made after hearing the petitioner in accordance with the law.
