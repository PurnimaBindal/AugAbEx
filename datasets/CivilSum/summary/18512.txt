Constitution of India, Article 226 - Voluntary Retirement - Retiral Benefits - Delay - The department is unable to prepare pension papers in advance for voluntary retirement cases - In case of delayed preparation of pension papers and payment of retiral benefits, a reasonable time limit is one year from the acceptance date of notice of voluntary retirement - Employee complaints about delay in payment of retiral benefits are only valid if the delay is beyond one year from retirement date.
