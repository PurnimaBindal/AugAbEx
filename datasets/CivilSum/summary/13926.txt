Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Section 15 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Order of payment - Challenge as to - The awarded amount of leave with wages, retrenchment compensation, bonus and amount payable in lieu of notice period - It is argued that the order is beyond the scope of jurisdiction as per the proviso to sub-section (3) of Section 15.
However, since there is no dispute that no retrenchment compensation or one month's wages in lieu of notice was paid under Section 25-F and what has been awarded is only actual wages or dues and nothing under sub-section (3), no interference is necessary.
