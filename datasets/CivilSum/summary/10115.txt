Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination of services - Absence from duty - The respondent was appointed as Khalasi on 1221980, however, absented from duty effective 20.10.1981.
After nine years of absence, the Railways were approached for reinstatement on 14101999 as the employee's address was not available for notice.
The tribunal's order for reinstatement with all benefits was deemed unreasonable.
