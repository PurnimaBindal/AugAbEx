The termination order passed by the Deputy Director, as the authenticating officer under the Authentication (Orders and other Instruments) Rules 2002, for termination of a Probationer Public Prosecutor in the CBI is deemed valid, as per Rules 2(1), (4), Schedule I of the same legislation and upheld under the Constitution of India, Articles 311, 77(2) - Transaction of Business Rules, 1961, Rule 3 - Central Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, Rule 5(1) - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 14(2) - Ground of order not by competent authority basis of challenge was not deemed valid, and the Tribunal's order was overturned.
