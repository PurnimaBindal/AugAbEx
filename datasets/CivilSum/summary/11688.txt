Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Part I, Rule 22 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8 - Enquiry after retirement - Rule 22 of Punjab Civil Services Rules outlines the procedure for dealing with a retiring employee who has pending disciplinary proceedings and is convicted of a serious crime or found guilty of grave misconduct.
If this rule is not invoked at the time of retirement, disciplinary action cannot be taken under Rule 8 of the Punjab Civil Service (P&A) Rules.
A disciplinary inquiry initiated against the plaintiff after his retirement under Rule 8 for imposition of major penalty has been deemed unsustainable.
