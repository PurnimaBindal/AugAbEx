Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Excise and Taxation Department (State Service Class-II Rules, 1956, Rules 5(c), 6 and 12 - Appointments and promotions - Seniority - Quota and Rota system between the promotees and direct recruits - The promotion and inter se seniority between direct recruits and promotee Taxation Officers must adhere to the quota and rota system - The rules must be applied to "posts" or "vacancies" wherein the "vacancy" is considered a "post" - The appointment of a direct recruit against a post meant for promotees or a promotee against a post meant for direct recruits means that the appointment will not confer any benefit under Rule 12 for seniority or promotion - Persons working against such a post have to be pushed down in seniority to a place where a post in their own quota fell vacant - The length of service has to be counted from the date the post becomes available in their own quota - There is no provision for adopting the rotational system in the Rules for filling the post from the source meant for promotees and direct recruits - The post falling vacant in a quota is not permissible to be rotated for the same quota - The Rules provide only for a 50:50 quota and source by promotion and direct recruitment and must be applicable from the beginning - Service rendered on the post meant for the other quota will not count towards seniority but will be considered as experience - The quota rule cannot be termed broken in the absence of any rota rule - Seniority must be recast in accordance with the judgment of the Supreme Court in Suraj Parkash Gupta's case after giving an opportunity for representation and hearing to the affected persons.
