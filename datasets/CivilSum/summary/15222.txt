Constitution of India, Article 226 - Nomination of Medical Officer - Post Graduate course - Non-payment of salary - The petitioner qualified for the post graduate course of MD after the Punjab government waived the one-month shortfall of eligibility criteria, subject to the petitioner fulfilling the obligation to serve the State for five years and the interim period by signing a bond - The respondents argued that the petitioner's service fell short for nomination - The court held that since the shortfall had been waived, the respondents cannot act against the petitioner's interests - The respondents' actions were without any rational basis - The court gave directions to release the petitioner's salary.
