Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - The petitioner was appointed in 1977 and subsequently terminated from service in 1985.
The pay of three months was given to the petitioner in lieu of three months notice.
In light of financial constraints, an offer was made to pay the petitioner 25% back wages up to date and also agreed to rehire him.
The petitioner accepted the offer.
As a result, the respondent was directed to pay the petitioner 25% back wages and reinstated him.
[Paras 5 and 6]
