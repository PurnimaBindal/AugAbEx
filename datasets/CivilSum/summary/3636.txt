The petitioner in the case of Compassionate appointment was offered a class-IV post instead of the class-III post for which he had applied without waiting for vacancy on the latter.
However, the acceptance of the class-IV post does not prevent the petitioner from claiming the class-III post in case a vacancy occurred later on, as per Uttar Pradesh Basic Education Act Section 12 (1) and Constitution of India Articles 16 and 226.
