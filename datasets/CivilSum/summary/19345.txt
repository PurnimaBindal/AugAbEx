Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Post of senior lecturer - In the absence of Recruitment Rules, the filling of senior lecturer positions is done on a temporary basis from senior lecturers of +2 schools based on their seniority - The respondent's explanation for the stop-gap arrangement is reasonable and satisfactory - The petitioner cannot be considered for the temporary positions as it was not a part of his eligibility criteria - The petitioner's right for consideration would arise only after the recruitment rules are in place and the substantive posts are being filled - The petition is dismissed on the grounds that the petitioner has not been wrongfully excluded from consideration since he was not eligible for the temporary positions.
