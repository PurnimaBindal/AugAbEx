The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947 outlines sections 25-F, 25-FFF, and 2(j) regarding the closure and retrenchment of establishments and industries.
Section 25-FFF specifically addresses compensation for workmen in case of the shutdown of an undertaking, which falls under the umbrella of an industry with a restricted meaning.
This means that even if an undertaking is a part of an industry, it can be closed without shutting down the entire industry.
In a case involving the closure of a vocational training center, which was considered an undertaking of the District Red Cross Society and had received state aid, the services of all employees were terminated.
The petitioner fell under the provisions of Section 25-FFF, and it was not necessary for the entire activity of the Society to have come to an end for the section to apply.
Therefore, the court found the retrenchment to be valid.
