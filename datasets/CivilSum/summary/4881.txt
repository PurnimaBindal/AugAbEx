In accordance with Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 6.16(a)(b) & (1) and Haryana Govt. instructions dt. 22111991, past service rendered in Panchayats and Panchayat Samities counts towards qualifying service for pension.
Once an employee has rendered total qualifying service for family pension, their dependents are entitled to family pension according to the rules.
Zila Parishad/Panchayat Samiti employees are considered to be Govt. servants for this purpose.
