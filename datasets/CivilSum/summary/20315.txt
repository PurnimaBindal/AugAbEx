Constitution of India, Article 311(2), 311(3) and Proviso (h) - Enquiry - The decision of the concerned authority to dismiss an employee without an enquiry is final - If dispensing with the enquiry is not reasonably practicable, it can be justified based on the facts of the case.
For instance, if witnesses are unable to give evidence due to the terror of the delinquent, in light of the prevalent situations in the State, and the complainant and others are visibly terrified and intimidated, then it cannot be deemed unreasonable to dispense with the enquiry.
