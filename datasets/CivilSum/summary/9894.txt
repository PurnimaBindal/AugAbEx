The Labour Court had awarded that the workmen are eligible for full back wages and continuity of service which was challenged by the State in light of Articles 16 and 14 of the Constitution of India and the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
The civil court had also declared the orders of termination of workmen as illegal.
As a result, the workmen were granted the relief of reinstatement with continuity of service.
However, two of the petitioners gave up their claim for back wages and the third petitioner passed away.
As per the court's direction, the two petitioners are entitled to the benefit of continuity of service while the widow of the third workman is to be paid Rs. 50,000/- as a full and final settlement of the claim.
