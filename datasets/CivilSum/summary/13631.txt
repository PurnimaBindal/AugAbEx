Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 21 and 226 - Admissions /Qualifications/ Eligibility /Reasonable differentiation/University matters/Categorisation - The categorization of graduates for admission in LL.B.
based on differentiation between those who studied for 14 years versus those who studied for 15 years for their graduation is not arbitrary or fanciful - It demonstrates a rational and intelligible connection to the objective aimed at - The differentiation does not indicate inequality - The court lacks competence to judge such matters and, as such, should not evaluate the decision of the University.
[Paras 5 and 6]
