Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Kerala Police Act, 1961, Section 69 - Inter-departmental transfer - Police Constables - An option is provided for transfer from Armed Reserved Battalion to District Armed Reserve and then to local police - Petitioners opted to remain in the battalion and received a promotion there - They later submitted options to transfer to a different unit - However, this would require a reversion to the post of constable and a consequent reduction in emoluments - As per service conditions authorized by the Rules, interdepartmental transfer leads to reversion, and therefore the petitioners have no legal right to insist on continued higher emoluments despite their reversion.
