Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 10(1) - Suspension due to disciplinary proceedings and criminal case involvement - Suspension of a government servant can be based on multiple grounds, as stated in Rule 10(1) of CCS (CCA) Rules, 1965, since both criminal and disciplinary proceedings can occur simultaneously.
