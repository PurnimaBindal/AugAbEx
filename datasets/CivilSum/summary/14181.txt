Administration of Punjab War Heroes Families Relief Fund Rules, 1999, Rules 2(d), 3 and 10(b) - The Rules aim to support the families of those who die while performing their duties and offer certain benefits to them - The Rules cover accidental death of Personnel of Armed Force in Medical Group while on duty - Authorities have to determine whether the individual died in an accident in the course of performance of bona fide military duty - The expression "Battle or Physical Casualty" is not recognized by the Rules - The provisions of rule 2(d) clearly cover death due to accident - Interpretation of the Rule excluding "Death due to Physical Casualty" would violate the plain language of the Rule - The Rule must be construed liberally - Authorities ordered to grant entire benefits under the Rules to the petitioner widow, including Rs. 5 lakhs for the house and Rs. 2 lakhs by way of ex-gratia grant - The petitioner also awarded costs of Rs. 5000/-.
