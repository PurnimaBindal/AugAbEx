Rules state that selection for Admission/Education through entrance tests requires a minimum of 50% marks for general category candidates and 40% marks for SC/ST candidates.
Criteria prescribed by the rules are valid and the Court cannot direct the State Government to fill vacant seats with candidates who do not meet the minimum qualifying marks.
The Court can only direct the adjustment of the maximum number of candidates.
The State Government must follow the regulations prescribed by the Medical Council of India to maintain the standard of education.
There is no justification for issuing further directions.
Candidates who did not qualify for the earlier two sessions after already appearing in entrance tests cannot be directed to be admitted in the 1992 session simply because a few seats were left vacant.
JT 1992(4) SC 575 is followed.
