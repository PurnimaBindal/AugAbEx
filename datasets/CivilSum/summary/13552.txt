Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo), 25-F and 25-H - Retrenchment - Re-employment - Closure of establishment - The loss of employees' jobs due to closure is considered retrenchment - However, if the establishment reopens under new management, Section 25-H is not applicable and re-employment benefits will not be available - After nationalisation, there is no employer-employee relationship between the retrenched employees and new management - Appointing new employees based on eligibility does not constitute discrimination or a violation of Section 25-H or the Coking Coal Mines (Nationalisation) Act, 1972, Section 17.
