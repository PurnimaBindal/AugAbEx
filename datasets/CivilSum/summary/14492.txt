The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection/Appointment - Cancellation of appointments - Any selection that violates the prescribed procedure cannot be sustained and must be invalidated - Individuals appointed through such illegal selection cannot claim entitlement to their positions - Orders cancelling these appointments are valid.
[Para 13]
The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Natural justice - Applicability of the principles - The principle of natural justice is not a rigid formula - Its applicability depends on the specific facts and circumstances of each case - It is not necessary for it to be followed in every instance.
[Paras 14 & 15]
