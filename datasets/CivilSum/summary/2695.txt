Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 51(2) - Objecting to jurisdiction of Court Martial - Objection rejected - Natural justice - Must record reasons for overruling special plea as to jurisdiction - Recording of reasons is not just a statutory requirement, but also a requirement of natural justice - Even if a status does not require recording of reasons, it must be read into every rule as inherent - It allows the person affected and the court of appeal to legally and validly challenge the basis of the rejection order - The basic principles of natural justice are not denied to persons subject to the Army Act, even if their Fundamental Rights are excluded - Rule 51 allows the person concerned to lead evidence to establish their preliminary objection as to jurisdiction and the order is subject to appeal - Reasons must be recorded while declining the objection - The Court Martial is directed to record reasons.
