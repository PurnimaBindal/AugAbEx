According to Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, an application for competency was made and the preliminary objection made by the petitioner- Corporation was rejected by the Labour Court.
The order was not challenged and the reference was not made.
The respondent workman only had the option to move an application, which he did, and it is maintainable.
[Para 6]
The workman was not performing duties of a Tester-cum-Gauger and therefore, was not entitled to be placed in Grade IV or Grade V No such plea had been raised before the Labour Court which makes it impermissible for the petitioner to raise this plea for the first time.
[Para 7]
Under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, it is not valid for a workman who is not qualified for the post to be promoted to a higher grade.
Therefore, it cannot be permitted for him to contend that he is not qualified for the post.
