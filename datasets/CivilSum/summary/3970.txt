Constitution of India, Article 226 - Contract Labour Regulation and Abolition Act, Section 10 - Notification under Section 10 of the Act - Board Formation - Interim measures till the notification and report of the Board to determine which workers can be absorbed permanently in the establishment - The establishment is prohibited from replacing workers until the Board returns its results - Any workers who are replaced or removed before the notification and report of the Board will not be eligible for benefits - The establishment must not replace workers and may only terminate those whose contract of work is over and no further assignments are available.
