Constitution of India, Article 226 - Statutory Regulations for Medical Services and Armed Forces, 1983, Regulations 421, 422 and 424 - Invalidation from service - The Medical Board's opinion carries great weight once it expresses it, and allegations in the writ application are largely inferences drawn by the petitioner himself from private doctors' prescriptions - even though allegations regarding treatment at Command Hospital demonstrate that cyclothymic traits were recognized in the petitioner, and that the petitioner was advised to be kept under observation in a low medical classification.
The action of the respondents in referring the petitioner for medical evaluation and constituting an IMB was therefore supported by the record, and the finding of fact reached by the IMB was not subject to interference in exercise of power of judicial review under Article 226 of the Constitution.
