According to Army Act 1950, Section 20 and Army Rules 1954, Rule 17, an employee may be dismissed from service on the basis of misconduct.
In the case mentioned, the petitioner was dismissed from his service under section 64E of Army Act due to misconduct, but the Director of Judge Advocate General advised not to frame charges against him as there was a lack of evidence.
The Disciplinary authority cannot invoke Section 20 and Rule 17 of the Act and Rules on the Administrative side if there is no recommendation to Military Intelligence Directorate regarding strong suspicion against the petitioner and in the absence of adequate evidence.
The petition was subsequently dismissed due to lack of evidence.
