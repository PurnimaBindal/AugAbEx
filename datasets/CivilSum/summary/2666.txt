Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.24 - Prevention of Corruption Act, Sections 7, 13 - Departmental proceedings - Criminal proceedings - Double jeopardy - Departmental proceedings cannot be put on hold indefinitely, especially when serious charges of corruption are made against the employee, as the criminal trial may take its own time to conclude - Both proceedings can carry on simultaneously as there are no complicated questions of law and fact involved in the criminal trial.
