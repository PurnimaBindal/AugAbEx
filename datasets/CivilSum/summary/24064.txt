Constitution of India, Articles 311, 226 and 32 - Army Act, 1950, Sections 71, 72, 63, 57, 45 - Army Officer - Charge of very serious misconduct - The punishment can range from 7 to 14 years of conviction sentence with dismissal from service - Only dismissal from service is considered to be a lesser punishment - For disobedience of order and falsifying official records, dismissal from service cannot be termed disproportionate or harsh so as to attract judicial review.
