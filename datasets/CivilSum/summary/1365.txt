The order regarding pensionary benefits for an employee who was removed from service as a punitive measure and then given a fresh appointment after 10 years is vulnerable and deserves to be quashed.
The Tribunal cannot consider the past service as continuous for the purpose of pensionary benefits.
Instead, the matter must be remanded to the Tribunal to determine the question of the employee's completed 10 years after the fresh appointment.
