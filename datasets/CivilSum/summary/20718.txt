The appellants' ad hoc appointment as Assistant Inspectors (Factories) in 1963 was due to there being no qualified persons available and regularisation of their services was dependent on a notification issued by the State Government on 17.8.1966 that permitted local candidates to be regularised provided they were not disqualified for appointment under Mysore State Civil Service (General Recruitment) Rules, 1957 on the date of their appointment.
However, since the appellants did not possess the requisite qualification for appointment as Assistant Inspectors, they were disqualified from appointment and could not have been regularised under the 1967 order issued by the government.
The Tribunal upheld appellants' regularisation in 1970, but also noted that an Assistant Inspector could be promoted as Inspector only if he had completed three years' regular service, so the appellants could not have been promoted senior to the respondents.
