Constitution of India, Article 226 - J&K Civil Services (Decentralisation of Recruitment to Non-gazetted Service) Rules, 1969, Rule 12 - The petitioner was transferred to the Food and Supplies Department while retaining their lien in the parent department, but their name was incorrectly listed in the seniority list.
When the petitioner represented, the parent department terminated their lien, but there is no explicit order indicating their absorption into the Food and Supplies department.
Therefore, the petitioner cannot claim the right to be considered for promotion in the Food and Supplies department.
