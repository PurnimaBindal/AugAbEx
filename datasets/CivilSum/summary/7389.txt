Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Discrimination - Equal pay for equal work - Employees who are in a similar situation must be treated equally, including receiving the same pay-scale - Giving different treatment amongst equals violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Seniority - Current duty charge against existing vacancy - The employee holding current duty charge of a promotion post cannot be denied the pay of such post - Vacancies should be filled by considering and promoting eligible and entitled persons, instead of giving current duty charge to a person on a lower pay-scale.
