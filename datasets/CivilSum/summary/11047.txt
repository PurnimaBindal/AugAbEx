Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2-A - Abandonment of service - Despite wanting to return to work after receiving treatment, the management refused to let the petitioner resume their duties - Although the petitioner requested that Regional Director, ESI evaluate and issue the necessary certificate, no action was taken - When abandonment is claimed, it must be proven to have been voluntary and wilful, which cannot be said of the petitioner - The petitioner should be considered to have been continuously in service and reinstated with all applicable benefits.
