Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Leave of kind due and extra-ordinary leave - Discrimination - The rule or policy in effect at the time of leave being granted governs its granting - If an employee was not entitled to the specific kind of leave on the date of grant, an illegal granting of the leave to someone else will not give the employee the right to claim discrimination through a writ of mandamus - The equality doctrine of Articles 14 and 16 is meant to prevent future illegalities, not repeat past ones.
