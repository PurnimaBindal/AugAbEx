Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Civil Service (Punishment & Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 21 - Natural justice - Departmental enquiry against an Officer - The official was punished with stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect, which is considered a minor punishment under the rules.
The rules do not require a personal hearing for the inflicting of minor penalties.
A personal hearing is only necessary when imposing the major penalties specified in clauses (v) to (ix) of Rule 5 or enhancing the penalty imposed by the order to any of the specified penalties.
