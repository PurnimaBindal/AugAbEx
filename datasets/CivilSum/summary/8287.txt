The reservation of a single post of Professor in English for Scheduled Tribe candidates by the respondent-university is being challenged on the basis of Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16 and 226 regarding Appointment and Reservation.
The challenge argues that it would essentially result in a 100% reservation in favour of Schedule Tribe candidates, rendering the impugned notification violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution and unsupportable.
