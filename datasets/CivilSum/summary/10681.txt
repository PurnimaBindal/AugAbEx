According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, compassionate appointment claims can be declined if the deceased did not die due to work-related reasons outlined in the scheme.
In a specific case, a wife received Rs. 37 lakhs and a monthly pension of Rs. 4,300, indicating that the claimant cannot be considered to be in financial crisis.
Compassionate appointment is not a right that can be claimed, nor is it a source of recruitment.
As such, the rejection of the claim is upheld.
