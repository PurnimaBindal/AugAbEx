The Indian Constitution's Article 311 discusses probation for appointments, and stipulates that the total period of probation cannot exceed 4 years, except for extraordinary circumstances.
As per the rules, upon the completion of two years of probation, an appointment must be confirmed.
If the probation period is extended beyond four years, the employee must be deemed confirmed and cannot be discharged by a simplicitor order.
This ruling was referenced in AIR 1968 S.C. 1210.
