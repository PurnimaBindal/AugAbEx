The amending Act that restricts the grant of bonus to public sector bank employees except as according to the Payment of Bonus Act is one that falls within the jurisdiction of Parliament.
This is based on the Constitution of India, Article 245 as well as the State Bank of India Act, Sections 7(1) and 43-A and the Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, Section 2.
[Paras 36 to 39]

While the Legislature is allowed, within its jurisdiction, to create retrospective laws that render judicial decisions ineffective, vested individual rights cannot be infringed upon by such laws.
The enactment of a law that invalidates a court decision that settles personal rights would amount to the Legislature acting in the capacity of a court or tribunal, which is against the law.
