Punjab School Education Board Act, 1969, Section 10(A) - Election Code of Conduct - Appointment Violation of Code of Conduct - Removal of Member and Vice Chairman of the Board - The Vice-Chairman was appointed by the State Government on the same day the Code of Conduct came into force - There is no evidence that the petitioner motivated his appointment or had knowledge of the Code of Conduct - The show cause notice did not contain the allegation that the petitioner motivated his appointment - No ground under Section 10(A) of the Act contemplates the removal of a Member of the Board - No misconduct or violation of the Election Code of Conduct was proven, thus the Order of Removal of the petitioner is illegal and arbitrary.
[Paras 10 and 11]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Necessary parties - Lis pendense - The writ petition filed challenging the removal of the petitioner from the post of Vice-Chairman and Member of the Board - During the case, after notice and written statement, a new person was appointed to the post - That appointment will always be subject to the writ petition's decision, even without any court order - These actions are hit by the doctrine of lis pendense, even if the appointed person is not made a party to the petition.
