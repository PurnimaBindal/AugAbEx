Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission in MBBS Course - The prospectus for admission to the MBBS Course (1997-98) amended the criteria for selection to the course.
25 seats were filled based on merit in the PMT at the Sri Guru Ram Dass Institute of Medical Science and Research Amritsar.
Four of the remaining 25 seats were Donor Body seats to be filled based on selection by SGPC If any seats remained unfilled, they would be shifted to the general category.
The petitioner was placed at merit No. 703, and two Donor Body seats fell vacant to be put in the General category.
However, these two seats were given to the NRI/NRI sponsored candidates, Respondents No. 3 and 4, who did not fulfil the qualifications for filling Donor Body seats.
The petitioner's claim that he stood at merit No. 703 has not been denied, whereas Respondents No. 3 and 4 had merit No. 3225 and 5538 respectively.
The court allowed the petitioner, and admission of Respondent No. 4 was quashed.
