As per the Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, Section 32(IV) and the Haryana Housing Board Act, 1971, Section 3(3), the Haryana Housing Board qualifies as an establishment under the State Government and is considered a 'local authority' under the meaning of Section 32(iv) of the Bonus Act.
Thus, the provisions of the Act apply to the Board, which is under the administrative and financial control of the State Government.
