Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8 - Stoppage of increment with cumulative effect - Major penalty - The stoppage of increment with cumulative effect is considered a major penalty and must follow the regular procedures of Rules 8 and 9 before imposition.
Failure to follow these procedures will result in the penalty order being null and void.
