The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) stipulates equal pay for equal work.
In this case, the employee's claim for equal pay was allowed by the labor court.
However, an application under Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act is considered an execution proceeding, and a claim based on equal pay for equal work is not a pre-existing right that can be executed under this section.
Thus, the application under Section 33-C(2) was found to be misconceived.
