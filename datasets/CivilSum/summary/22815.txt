Border Security Force Act of 1968, Sections 3(2) and 77 - An officer who is on deputation and serving in the Border Security Force - The Border Security Act applies to the officer - Once repatriated to their parent department, the BSF Act no longer applies after six months.
[Para 9] C Border Security Force Act of 1968, Section 80 - An officer who has committed an offense in the BSF - The discretion to proceed or not to proceed under the BSF Act lies with the Director General or other specified officers where both Criminal and Security courts have jurisdiction over the same offense - Section 80 applies only to persons who are "serving" in the BSF and not to those who cease to be officers in it.
[Para] D. Border Security Force Act of 1968, Sections 80, 77, 3(2), and 81 - An accused officer on deputation in the BSF who committed an offense - There has been a fact-finding inquiry, but no trial has taken place - The officer is repatriated to their parent department - After six months of repatriation, the officer is no longer subject to the BSF Act - The criminal court may take cognizance of the offense committed by the accused officer while serving in the BSF.
