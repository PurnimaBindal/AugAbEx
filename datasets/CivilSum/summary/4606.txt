Constitution of India, Article 226 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 14 - Termination - Delay/ Laches - The delay that arose from the lack of a termination order from the respondent college authority cannot be solely attributed to the petitioner.
Furthermore, the decision to terminate the petitioner's services was only disclosed in a reply to a pleader notice, after the petitioner had already filed a Writ Petition.
Thus, the illegality of treating the petitioner as out of service without a termination order cannot be defended based solely on laches.
