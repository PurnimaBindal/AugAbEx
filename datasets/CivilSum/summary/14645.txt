According to Ordinance 13 of Punjabi University, Patiala, individuals cannot be promoted to attend the second professional course until they have passed all the subjects of the first professional examination.
This amendment was added on 17.5.1992 and has overruled previous cases such as Harvinder Singh v. Principal, Guru Gobind Singh Medical College, 1992 (1) PLR 23 while following and upholding Anita Virdi v. Medical College, Patiala CWO No. 2559 of 86.
