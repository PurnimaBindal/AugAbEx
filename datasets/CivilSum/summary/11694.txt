Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 45-A and 75 - Ex-parte Order - Setting aside of ex-parte orders - Natural justice - If an employer initiates proceedings to set aside an order passed ex-parte against them under Section 45-A of the ESI Act, based on the grounds that they were not served with the notice to show cause within reasonable time, the authority is required to consider and decide such an application and issue a fresh order after providing a full opportunity to the employer if it is determined that proper notice was not served - Remedy under Section 75 of the Act does not prevent the competent authority from setting aside an ex-parte order and issuing a new one while granting the employer an opportunity to be heard in accordance with the law.
