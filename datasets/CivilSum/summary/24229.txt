Constitution of India, Article 14 - Disciplinary proceedings - Natural justice - A person who refuses the opportunity given to them cannot later claim that the opportunity was not given and cannot challenge the final order on the basis of natural justice being violated.
[Para 7]

Constitution of India, Article 14 - UP State Universities Act, 1973, Section 66 - Departmental proceedings - Suspension allowance - Non-payment of suspension allowance - If an employee was unable to conduct and defend themselves properly during proceedings due to non-payment of subsistence allowance, then they can raise objections of prejudice - Non-payment of subsistence allowance alone is not enough to render the proceedings void for violation of principles of natural justice.
[Paras 7 to 9]
