The appellant was discharged from the military for participating in clandestine petrol selling, and although the order of discharge mistakenly cited Section 20 of the Army Act instead of Section 22, the order was not invalidated.
This is because the source of power can be traced to existing law even if it was not specifically referred to or if a wrong provision was cited.
In the end, the order cannot be considered punitive or detrimental to the appellant's future chances of obtaining civil service employment.
