Rajasthan Public Service Commission (Conditions of Service) Regulations, 1974, Regulation 4(2) - Chairman of the Commission - Equal pay for equal work - A member of the Commission who officiates as Chairman is entitled to an additional payment of Rs. 200/- per month on top of their own salary as a member - Merely performing duties of Chairman along with usual duties does not make one a Chairman - Incorrect payment of Chairman’s salary cannot be used as a precedent - Regulation 4(2) is legal - Claiming regular salary of the Chairman while officiating cannot be accepted.
[Para 13]  B Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 250 - Government servant - Rajasthan Public Service Commission (Conditions of Service) Regulations, 1974, Regulations 4 and 5 - Emoluments - Retirement - Pension - Appointment to Public Service Commission deems one to have retired from Government service from date of application/appointment to the Commission - Emoluments last drawn as a Government servant only counted until the date of retirement from Government service prior to becoming a member of the Commission - Member of Commission is a Constitutional functionary and not a Government servant or sanctioned post in any cadre.
[Paras 14, 15, 19 & 21]  C Constitution of India, Article 319(d) - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 250 - Government servant - Office of the Member of the Public Service Commission is not considered employment under the State Government as the Member is a Constitutional functionary - Emoluments of the Member cannot be counted as emoluments of a Government servant.
