According to Articles 14 and 16 of India's Constitution, there are certain qualifications and special increments that can be given to employees based on their classification.
However, there may be a differentiation on the ground of expenses on training, as those who acquired qualifications at the government's expense may not receive the same benefits as those who acquired their qualifications at their own expense.
This classification has been justified since those who acquired qualifications at the government's expense and also had the benefit of salary cut-off date from the date of decision to grant advance increments were not held to be arbitrary.
