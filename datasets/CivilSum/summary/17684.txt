The State Government exceeded its jurisdiction in exercising its powers under S.19(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 when it declined to make a reference, having essentially adjudicated upon the dispute and determined the merits of the case by considering the alleged misconduct of the workman.
As per the provisions of S.11-A, the workman can argue before the adjudicating authority that the dismissal was disproportionate to the charged levelled against him, but this opportunity can only be availed if the State Government makes a reference.
The State has no discretion in cases where S.11-A is applicable, and it must refer the industrial dispute under S.10(1) of the Act.
The order of the State Government declining to make the reference is quashed since it usurped the functions of the adjudicating authority while exercising its powers under S.10(1) of the Act.
