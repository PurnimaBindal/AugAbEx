Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 state the principle of Equal pay for equal work - Same eligibility should lead to the same pay scale.
However, different pay scales may be present based on factors like responsibility in job, duties to be discharged, etc.
Classification of posts and prescribing pay scales for several posts are two different and distinct functions.
This principle is supported by the 1997(1) Supreme Court Service Law Judgments 598.
