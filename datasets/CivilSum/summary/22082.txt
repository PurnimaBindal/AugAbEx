According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 and Uttar Pradesh Industrial Disputes Act, Section 4(k), university-managed cafeterias are necessary facilities for campus residents.
A combination of implicit obligation and university control and supervision tests are satisfied, which frees students from legal responsibility.
The Constitution guarantees equal pay for equal work, and ensures economic justice to casual, daily, and nominal-wage workers.
Social and economic justice must be provided promptly, without considering financial constraints.
Therefore, the university is directed to regularize cafeteria workers' services and pay them a regular pay scale, even if a new cadre has to be created for them.
