Army Pension Regulations, 1961 (Part-I), Regulation 155(b) and Rules - Reservist Pension - If the individual was discharged from the Army at their request before fulfilling their term of engagement, but had qualifying service towards a pension, they are entitled to receive it regardless of Clause (b).
Discharge on request, voluntary retirement, or redesignation with qualifying service cannot serve as grounds for denying the right to pension.
