Article 226 of the Constitution of India is applicable to the pension case of an ex-Havaldar petitioner who served in the Army for over 19 years.
Although the petitioner's war service and short service were not considered countable towards pension/gratuity, it was determined that the petitioner had completed more than 19 years' of service and was therefore entitled to pension under the regulations.
[Paras 2, 3, and 6] B.
After being discharged from the Army without being given a pension, the petitioner filed a writ in the year 1993.
Although the respondents pleaded delay and laches, the Court directed the grant of pension within three months, repelling the plea of the respondents regarding delay.
As the respondents had wrongly withheld the pension, they cannot be allowed to take advantage of their own wrong.
