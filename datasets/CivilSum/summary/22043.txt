Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16(4) and 335 - Reservation - Dereservation - Single post - Roster can be maintained for filling up a single post in the cadre and providing reservation benefits to Dalit communities - Any orders or directions to cancel reservation for such posts would contradict the constitutional mandate - It is established that no order or direction can be given to stop an authority from fulfilling its legal obligation - Any such directions would be considered null and void - Consequently, individuals from the general category cannot demand for dereservation of a reserved point for general category candidates - The roster system for a single point post does not breach Article 16(1) of the Constitution.
