Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection Grade - JBT teachers of Haryana Education Department - Selection Grade posts - Instructions dated 23.7.1957 - According to these instructions, Selection grade should be granted on 15% of the total posts, including temporary and ad hoc posts.
Furthermore, all individuals who become entitled to it as per the seniority list must be granted the Selection Grade, regardless of whether they claimed it or not.
