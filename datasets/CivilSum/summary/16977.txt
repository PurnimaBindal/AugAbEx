The termination of workmen without payment of retrenchment compensation was found to be in violation of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F and 25-J, as determined by the Tribunal.
The Tribunal held that Chapter 5-A of the Act had overriding effect on any inconsistent provision in any other law, in accordance with the law.
Therefore, the award of the Tribunal should not be interfered with.
