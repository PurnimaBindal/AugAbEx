According to Sections 45-A (2), 75, and 77 of the Employees State Insurance Act, the Employees State Insurance Court does not have the power or jurisdiction to grant an application for re-inspection of records for which the best assessment has already been passed.
Even if proceedings are underway under S. 75 r/w S. 77 of the Act and evidence has been adduced by the involved parties, allowing the respondent-employer's application for re-inspection of record in order to set aside the best assessment order and re-open the entire case is not justified.
