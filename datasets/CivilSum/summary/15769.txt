Jammu and Kashmir Reservation Rules, 1994, Rule 13 - Posts meant for reserved category remaining unfilled - Backlog - Clearance of - To clear backlog, under normal circumstances, 42% of posts meant for reserved category can be filled with the option of filling up to 50% of the posts - At least 50% of posts in a particular selection must be open category - A candidate selected falling within the 50% limit of a reserve category is eligible for appointment.
