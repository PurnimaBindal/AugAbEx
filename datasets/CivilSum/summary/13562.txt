Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - Superannuation/Extension in service - Calcutta Metropolitan Development Authority (CMDA) serves a public purpose.
CMDA appoints persons on deputation from Government departments and sometimes grants them extensions after the age of superannuation.
However, one such extension has been given even though a criminal case under Sections 420 and 120-B IPC are pending against the employee.
This suggests such persons are being retained for ulterior reasons rather than for public purpose.
Therefore, the Authority is directed to restrain from granting any extension after the age of superannuation to such persons in any manner whatsoever.
[Paras 3 to 8]
