Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeals) Rules, 1970, Rule 8(8) - The Enquiry Officer failed to ask the petitioner whether he wanted assistance from a co-worker.
Despite this, there is no justification for dismissing the award due to non-compliance of Rule 8(8) since the petitioner has not proved that he was unable to defend himself without assistance.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section - 11A - Jurisdiction - Embezzlement - The Presiding Officer made a mistake in examining the punishment issue.
He found the petitioner guilty of embezzlement, even though he had not been charged with it, nor had the Enquiry Officer found him to be dishonest.
The approach of the Labour Court was clearly incorrect.
Thus, the tribunal set aside the impugned award, ordering the Labour Court to reconsider the issue of punishment in the light of Section 11A [Para 17]
