The State Transport Corporation did not have any prescribed statute for Punishment and Appeal Rules for its employees.
However, the Corporation took disciplinary action against its employees under State Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules applicable to State Government employees.
Even in the absence of the Rules, the Corporation could legally initiate disciplinary proceedings against its employees by conducting a consistent enquiry with the principles of natural justice.
A Conductor who committed misappropriation of public money found the punishment of removal from service proportionate to the severity of the offense.
As per the Constitution of India, Article 311, the quantum of punishment is determined based on the offense and cannot be contested as disproportionate.
