FCI Staff (Regulations), 1972, Regulation 15(2) - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Probation - Extension of - Petitioner promoted as Deputy Manager on probation for one year from the date of assuming charge, which can be further extended to one more year.
The probation period of Petitioner was extended to another year on the expiry of the first year of probation.
The order challenged - Contention that order is illegal and without jurisdiction - Regulation 15 states that every person appointed to any post shall be on probation for one year from the date of appointment which period can be extended for not more than one more year - Held, since Petitioner has completed only one year of probation and no specific order confirming his services has been passed, no illegality has been committed by extending the probation period of in consonance with Regulation 15 and circulars.
Thus, the petition is dismissed accordingly.
