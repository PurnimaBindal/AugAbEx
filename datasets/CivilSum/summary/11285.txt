Constitution of India, Article 226 - Malpractice Rules, 1981, Item No. 3 - Malpractice in Examination - Natural Justice - The petitioner was caught copying from forbidden material on his answer sheet, leading to the cancellation of his performance and his debarment from two subsequent examinations.
He was never given a show-cause notice to explain himself, and fairness demands that he should have been given further opportunity to do so before punishment was inflicted.
The entire procedure adopted by the respondent is arbitrary and violates the principles of natural justice, leading to the impugning of the order.
[Paras 4 & 12]
