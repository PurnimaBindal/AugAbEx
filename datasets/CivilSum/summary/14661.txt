The Government of Punjab has issued instructions to provide employment to the dependents of deceased government employees on a preferential basis, even if it means creating new supernumerary positions.
The aim of these instructions is to address the difficulties faced by dependent family members after the breadwinner's death.
In such appointments, even those retrenched employees who are waiting to be employed under Section 25-H of the Industrial Disputes Act must be overlooked.
[Paras 6, 7 and 8]
Under Section 25-H of the Industrial Disputes Act, an individual retrenched under Section 25-F does not have the right to be regularly employed.
Their only entitlement is to be considered for reemployment in due order under Section 25-H of the Act.
[Para 9]
