The instructions dated 30.10.87 regarding leave vacancy appointments, as per Articles 16 and 226/227 of the Constitution of India, must be strictly construed as a concession for the incumbent appointed against the leave vacancy.
These instructions do not give the appointee the right to claim regular appointment against any other post.
Management erred in converting the petitioner's appointment against a leave vacancy to a regular appointment.
[Para 7]

Instructions of 8th April, 1991, regarding the appointment against a leave vacancy, refer to those vacancies that were earlier leave vacancies and may have become regularly vacant due to the incumbent not rejoining.
As the case of the petitioner is not the same, the non-approval of regular appointment by the Govt. rendered it meaningless.
[Para 7]

Despite the petitioner not being paid the salary for the period he worked due to the stay order of the Court, Management is directed to pay the entire arrears to the date when he was relieved.
