According to Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 12.21 and 1624, being absent from duty is a misconduct that can result in an employee's discharge from service.
When a plaintiff was discharged from police service due to his absence from duty, he raised a civil dispute as no enquiry had been held into the charge.
The appellate court allowed the appeal, but the High Court held that the impugned order of dismissal attached a stigma, since absence from duty is a misconduct.
The order was penal in character, and the appeal was eventually dismissed.
