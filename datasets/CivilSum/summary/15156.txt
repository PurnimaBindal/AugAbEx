According to Delhi University Act, 1922, Statute 37, seniority for Professors should be determined by length of continuous service in grade or posts.
Despite the appellant not being selected against direct recruitment, they were later promoted as Professor under MPS-1983.
However, the resolution passed by the Executive Council in 1999 cannot be used to alter the principle of seniority laid out in Statute 37.
Additionally, the resolution cannot override the substantive provision of the statute since it was passed after the fact.
[Paras 17, 21 & 23]
