All India Services (Death-cum-Retirement Benefits) Rules, 1958, Rule 6 - Pension and gratuity - Misconduct during service - The Government is authorized to withhold or withdraw pension, or a portion of it, if a pensioner is found guilty in a departmental or judicial proceeding for serious misconduct or for causing pecuniary loss to the Government due to negligence or misconduct during service or after retirement - Proven serious misconduct is enough to take action under Rule 6 - General Clauses Act.
