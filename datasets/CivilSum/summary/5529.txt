According to Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Sections 8(4) and 8(5), suspension of petitioners could not remain in force after 15 days if Directorate of Education refused permission for it.
As a result, petitioners are entitled to salary from date of suspension till date of reinstatement and have the right to join their duties.
However, they were prevented from doing so due to injunction order passed against them by school management.
[Para 11]

In case of management disbanding classes XIth and XIIth without approval from Directorate of Education, their action is not accepted.
After taking over management, Directorate of Education restarted these classes and did not consider petitioners surplus.
Upon reinstatement, petitioners are entitled to salaries for intervening period.
