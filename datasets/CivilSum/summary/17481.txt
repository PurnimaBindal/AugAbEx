Constitution of India, Article 226 - Public Interest Litigation - Petitioners fighting for public cause without any personal interest - Raised issue of illegal appointments made by Municipal Committee to State Government - Government failed to take action despite evidence of illegality - Petitioners filed writ petition in public interest and cannot be dismissed on basis of delay and laches.
