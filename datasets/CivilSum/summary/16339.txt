Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, Section 45 - UP State Road Transport Corporation Employees (other than officers) Service Regulations, 1981, Regulations 11 and 12 - Evidence Act, 1972, Section 115 - Training/Appointment/Estoppel - The Corporation had promised to give preference to the petitioners who were given clerical and conductor training while making regular appointments - However, the 1981 Service Regulations did not provide any preference to the apprentices - The Corporation is bound by the principle of promissory estoppel since the petitioners did not apply for jobs elsewhere due to being given a promise of appointment - As a result, all those who received training before the regulations came into force should be appointed provided they meet all other eligibility requirements.
