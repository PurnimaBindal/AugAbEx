Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(C)(2) - Recovery of Dues - Revised wages - Claim for - Even though petitioner resigned before completion of training period of 18 months, the settlement between employer and employees reducing training period to 3 months and upgrading workers warrants said grade to not be denied to the petitioner - The rules themselves make no distinction on continuation in service and are retrospective - Therefore, workman is entitled to claim the same benefits as his counterparts.
