Constitution of India Article 226 - Doctrine of necessity - Disciplinary proceeding - If no other authority is able to act as the disciplinary authority and appellate authority, doctrine of necessity may be involved - Even if Natural Justice has to give way to necessity, the machinery of justice or Administration must not break down - The Tribunal cannot give such direction if there is no prejudice or there is no other authority to discharge the function - Order of Tribunal set aside and remitted for consideration afresh since the question of jurisdiction was not raised before the Tribunal.
