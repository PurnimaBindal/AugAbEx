Admission - Even if a party was not confronted with a statement, their clear admission can be used as substantive evidence against them.
Such admissions may not be conclusive but can be used to derive conclusions about the matter.
[Para 10]

Possession - The trial court found that the plaintiff and their brothers were mortgagees in joint possession of the land and became joint owners, while rejecting the respondent's claim of an oral sale by the original owner.
The lower appellate court reversed the decision and affirmed the trial court's judgment.
[Paras 8, 12 and 14]
