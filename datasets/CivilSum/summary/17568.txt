Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Pay scale - Qualifications - Untrained teachers in Haryana Government schools can receive a higher pay scale based on their higher qualification of Prabhakar/Gyani with JBT.
The cut-off date for entitlement to the higher pay scale is 931990.
Those who acquired higher qualifications before this date are eligible for the higher pay scale.
Authorities are directed to identify eligible persons and provide relief within 15 days from receiving orders.
