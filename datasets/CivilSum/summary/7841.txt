Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part I, Rules 44, 413, 416 and 422(i) - Higher Pay Scale - An employee holding both his own post and a higher post shall receive the pay of the higher post.
The petitioner was not given a reason for being denied the higher pay scale.
The letter of appointment stating they would discharge functions of the higher post in their own pay scale holds no weight.
