Constitution of India, Article 226 - Ad hoc appointment - Termination - Mala fide - Despite an interim direction for appointing petitioner, court held that such directions of an interlocutory nature did not grant petitioner the right to continue in service indefinitely - College's decision to discontinue classes based on government directives made four years prior was not motivated by malice - Petitioner's termination was based on a decision made by the Managing Committee to relieve the junior-most Lecturer in English, after considering input from the President, General Secretary, and Principal of the College - Impugned order could not be found to lack jurisdiction - Petition was dismissed.
