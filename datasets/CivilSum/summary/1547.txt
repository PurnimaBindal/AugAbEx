According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India, if a probationer is found to be unsatisfactory during their probation period, their services can be terminated.
A screening committee determined that the services of the individual in question were unsatisfactory, but there were no allegations of wrongdoing or malicious intent.
The decision to terminate their services was made consciously by a properly constituted screening committee and no faults were found with the order.
