Section 10(1) of the Contract Labour Regulation Act and Sections 10 and 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971 state that the Food Corporation of India (FCI) should not employ contract labour as per the direction of the court.
As an alternative, FCI has opted to hire the services of government employees like SPOs/Home Guards.
However, it has not been determined whether this method would constitute as contract labour or not.
Since there is no clear finding against this method, it cannot be concluded that FCI is engaging SPOs/Home Guards as contract labour.
