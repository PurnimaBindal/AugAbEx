According to Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 9, Rule 13, an order can be recalled.
However, in a case where the advocate on record was unable to attend the proceedings due to illness and the vakalatnama was filed after the order was passed, it was contended that notice was served despite record showing otherwise.
Upon examination of contradictory evidence, the documents tendered by the advocate were found to be inadmissible and a sympathetic view could not be taken to prevent the abuse of the court's process.
The application was therefore dismissed.
