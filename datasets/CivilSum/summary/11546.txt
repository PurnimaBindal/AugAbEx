Standing Guide on ACR's (Punjab), Paras 5, 6, 6A and 7 - ACR - Adverse remarks - According to the guide, adverse remarks are conveyed after 2 years and their sustainability must be evaluated.
The correctness of a reporting officer's subjective opinion cannot be questioned by civil court, particularly when there is no evidence of prejudice due to a delay in conveying the remarks.
