Punjab Revenue Patwaris Class-III Service Rules, 1966, Rules, 4, 7, 8, 9 and 10 - Determination of seniority - Benefit of ad hoc service - Patwaris appointed on ad hoc basis through Department Selection Board - If appointments were not made on recommendation of the SSS Board or the relevant posts were not advertised through public advertisement, then it cannot be considered in accordance with the rules - Petitioner appointed outside the rules and cannot receive benefits of ad hoc service - Seniority is determined from the date of regularisation - Petition dismissed.
