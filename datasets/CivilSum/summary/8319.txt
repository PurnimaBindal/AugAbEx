Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Regularisation of Temporary Employees - Cut off date - Government of India gave instructions to regularise all temporary employees -Bank directed its branches that those who completed 90 days of service on or after 111982 should be regularised- The respondent worked for 90 days prior to 111982 - Regularisation was denied because their 90 days of service was completed before 1182- Held illegal- The words "on or after" 1182 implies the scheme also pertains to temporary employees, including the respondent, who have completed 90 days of service even before 1182.
