Letter Patent Appeal, Clause 10 - Intra Court appeal - Contempt pending - Whether maintainable - According to JS Parihar and Chunilal Nanda's case judgments, intra court appeal may be filed in cases where a learned Single Judge initiates contempt proceedings.

Claim of Regularisation - Direction in writ petition to consider claim for regularization - Contempt Court modified directions to regularize respondents without putting forward excuse of quota or quota within quota - Contemptibility of modified directions - As per the State's affidavit that part-time employees will be regularized strictly according to seniority and within quota, and service will not be terminated except for misconduct, delinquency, or incapacity, grievances of the respondents will not stand - Respondents have liberty to file a representation to make a grievance, which shall be decided by speaking order after the opportunity of hearing.
