The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo), 2(oo)(bb), 10 and 25-F and Constitution of India, Articles 311(2) and 16 are applicable in the case where Respondent No. 2 was terminated during his probation period without explanation.
The labour court held that the order was 'bad in law' due to Section 25-F not being complied with by the management.
On the other hand, the High Court's ruling claims that if the contract of employment between the employer and the workman is not renewed on its expiry as the stipulations in section 2(oo) (bb) of the ID Act, it does not amount to retrenchment.
In this case, since the workman's contract was not renewed at the end of his two-year probation period, Section 25-F does not come into play regardless of the fact that he served a few days more than the prescribed duration.
This outcome activated the provisions of Section 2(oo)(bb) of the ID Act, and the respondent's services could be justly terminated according to the contract of service.
