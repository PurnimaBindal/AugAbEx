Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 2(j) - Delay and laches - Industrial Dispute - A workman cannot raise an industrial dispute 10 years after their termination, unless a satisfactory explanation for the delay and subsistence of the dispute is provided.
The Government and Labor Court must take this into account before making or entertaining any references.
