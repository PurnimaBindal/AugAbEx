Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Indian Police Service (Appointment by Promotions) Regulations, 1955, Regulation 9(1) - Petitioner, a member of Rajasthan Police Service, being considered for promotion to Indian Police Service - Candidate recommended by Review DPC but couldn't be appointed due to retirement - Respondent within the zone of consideration and ranked No. 3 in order of merit and seniority against vacancies of year 1993-94 - Delay in convening DPC causing lapse - Tribunal's findings are just and in accordance with the Regulation - Respondent entitled to promotion from date his junior was promoted with all consequential benefits, period from superannuation to appointment as IPS to be treated as `Dies non'.
