Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability pension - A petitioner who was training as a recruit fractured the Middle Phalange and was invalidated out of military service.
CCDA(P) decreased his disability to less than 20%, and his claim for pension was denied because he was not sent to a higher medical board before the Chief Controller of Defence Accounts (Pension).
It was held that the accounts branch responsible for the pension cannot override judgments made by medical experts without referring the matter to a higher medical board.
As a result, disability pension was granted for the petitioner.
[Paras 2 & 3]
