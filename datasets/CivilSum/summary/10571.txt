Constitution of India, Article 16 - Seniority - Quota rule was not fixed before March 10, 1978 when it was introduced - Seniority for those appointed prior to this date was determined by continuous appointment without consideration of quota - Those appointed after this date may have their seniority impacted by the quota rule.
