Constitution of India, Articles 254 and 311 - Maharashtra Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1982, Rule 27(3) - Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 468 - Validity of sanction for prosecution - Proceedings cannot be instituted against a retired Government servant after more than four years - Rule 27 bars institution of proceedings in such cases - Criminal Procedure Code does not prescribe any limitation for offences charged against a Government servant - Despite this, the provisions of Criminal Procedure Code hold validity in sanction for prosecution.
