Section 22 of the Army Act, 1950 - Withdrawal of release application - A Lieutenant in Military Nursing Service filed for release citing family problems but later withdrew the request stating the problems no longer existed.
However, an order for release was still issued based on assumptions that she couldn't concentrate on her work.
This order was incorrect since the petitioner withdrew their request before its acceptance, leaving nothing for the authority to base their decision upon.
