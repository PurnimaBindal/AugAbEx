Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 11 - Res-judicata - The Tribunal should not have applied the principle of res-judicata.
If compulsory retirement had been ordered instead of removal petitioner may have still been entitled to retiral benefits and other terminal benefits.
The order rejecting second application due to res-judicata should be overturned.
