As per the Contract Labour Regulation and Abolition Act, Sections 14 and 35 and Andhra Pradesh Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Rules, Rule 25(v)(a), the licence for contract labour can be revoked if certain conditions are breached.
A paper mill that employed two different sets of security staff, one directly by the company and the other by a contractor holding a licence, did not pay both parties similar wages as was required by Rule 25(v)(a).
Specifically, the workmen employed by the contractor were paid lesser wages than those employed by the company, which is a contravention of the aforementioned rule.
Therefore, the licence of the contractor is liable to be cancelled under Section 14 of the Act.
