Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226 and 136 - Promotion - Eligibility - A petition for promotion from Senior Lecturer in Medicine to Assistant Professor (Medical) under the Punjab Medical Education and Service (Class I) Rules 1978 was disposed of with the direction to consider the petitioner's claim against the next vacancy as and when it arises - However, a subsequent petition was not maintainable when the petitioner was testopped from challenging the promotion of other individuals in previous petitions - On merits, the petitioner was not eligible for promotion to the post at the time of earlier consideration, and those who were already working on an ad hoc basis against the posts were entitled to be adjusted.
[Paras 2, 6, 7 and 8]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Promotion - Promotion from back date - The petitioner did not receive promotion from the date in which they claimed to be considered until their actual consideration date - The petitioner would not gain any substantial advantage in case prayer for promotion from a back date is granted - As such, no futile writ would be issued in the exercise of jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution - Consequently, the writ was declined.
