According to Section 14 of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, an earlier special law can only be altered or repealed by a later general law through an express provision.
Moreover, despite the provisions of Section 14, the Payment of Gratuity Act cannot supersede Section 16 of the Working Journalists and Other Newspaper Employees (Conditions of Service) and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1955.
Therefore, the ruling that the Gratuity Act applied in the case of respondent No. 1, a working journalist, was incorrect as his eligibility for gratuity was to be determined in accordance with the special statute of the Journalists Act.
