The Constitution of India's Articles 14 & 16 indicate that compassionate appointments are intended to ease the burden caused by the death of the breadwinner in the family.
Mere death of an employee in harness is not sufficient to provide a source of livelihood to their family.
To consider compassionate appointment, the financial condition of the family must be evaluated by the authority to help them overcome financial difficulties.
The petitioner was not entitled to compassionate appointment as he remained silent for 8 years.
