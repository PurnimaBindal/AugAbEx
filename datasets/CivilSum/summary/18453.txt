Constitution of India, Article 16 - Service Rules (Kerala), Part III Rule 3-C - Pay and allowances - Recovery order when employee receives excess payment due to error in pay fixation - No illegality - Argument that error not caused by employee, therefore government not entitled to refund - This argument is not supported - If an official is denied benefits, they have the right to demand payment - Correspondingly, they are obligated to repay any excess funds received.
[Paras 9, 10 and 11]
