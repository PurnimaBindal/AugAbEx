The Rules and Regulations for the Post Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, Chandigarh, as well as the related Act, require appointment through competitive selection.
The institute's national importance necessitates the highest standards for medical facilities.
Ad hoc appointments made through Entry 61 of Schedule I to the Regulations lack the necessary competitive selection procedure and cannot lead to regularisation of services.
Ad hoc appointees only have the right to compete in regular selections.
[Paras 7, 9, 10, and 15] B.
Despite litigation between competing candidates causing delays in a selection, the competent authority must still implement the decision.
It cannot postpone its obligation to await the outcome of litigation.
