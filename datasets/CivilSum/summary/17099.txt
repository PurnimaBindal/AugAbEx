Pension Regulations for the Army, 1961, Part-I, Paras 132, 173-A, 183 - Army Rules, Rule 13(3) - Disability pension - Qualifying service for disability pension - If an individual is discharged from service under Army Rule 13(3), Item III(v) in medical category "BEE" (permanent) without being provided with a "sheltered appointment", he will be deemed to have been discharged from military service under Para 173-A of Regulations and entitled to "Service Element" of disability pension despite not completing 15 years of qualifying service - The authority was instructed to grant disability pension to the petitioner.
