Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19 - The appellants complained of a notification amending the Rule - The validity of the retrospective nature of the amendment introduced by the impugned notification had already been considered by the Full Bench of the Tribunal and was found to be violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - The appellants sought the same relief on the basis of the Tribunal's Full Bench judgment - The application was dismissed on the grounds of delay and the tribunal refused to condone the delay - Held, it was a suitable case for the condonation of delay - The order of the Tribunal was set aside, the delay was condoned, and the appellants were deemed entitled to the benefit of Full Bench judgment and the same relief.
