Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission - Provisional admission/Examination - Students in the open school for + 2 examination were only taught four subjects up until the session of 1991-92.
In that session, provisional admission was allowed by the University for students who passed a fifth subject.
The situation was the same in the following year.
Petitioners who wish to be admitted in the 1992-93 session and are in the same situation as those in the previous session should be allowed the same benefit.
The petitioner placed in a compartment for one paper is not better off than others in the same situation.
Therefore, they should be allowed to pass one subject like those in the compartment and be given the same chance to pass the compartment, and do so with full chances, to receive provisional admission.
[Paras 6 to 8]
