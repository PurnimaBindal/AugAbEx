Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Ad hoc appointment - Ad hoc appointment grants no right to permanent appointment and can be terminated at any time.
Ad hoc appointees can apply and compete for departmental positions but cannot receive preferential treatment.
[Para 13] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Promotion - Eligible persons have the right to be considered for promotion, but the competent authority must determine fitness and suitability according to established rules.
The Tribunal cannot usurp the authority's role, and any decisions beyond its scope can be overturned by the writ court.
