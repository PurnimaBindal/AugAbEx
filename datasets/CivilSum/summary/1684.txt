Delhi High Court Establishment (Appointment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1972 Items 4 & 5 of Schedule II and Rule 7 - Promotion by selection/Select list/Amendment to rules - If the number of vacancies available at the time of advertising the vacancies were not specified and the selection committee prepares a list of all successful candidates, only as many candidates can be appointed as the number of vacancies available or that came into existence until the panel remained active - All candidates are not entitled to appointment, and once Rule 7 changes, the existing quota for promotees shall adjust accordingly.
However, directions were issued to work out the vacancies that came into existence until the panel was alive and appoint those from the panel accordingly.
If no life span is stated, the panel can continue indefinitely and eventually scrapped at any time.
