Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 309 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Appointment and other Service Conditions of Employees of Private Institutions and other Establishments Taken Over by Government) Rules, 1977, Rule 5(3) - Retiral benefits - Respondents paid Petitioner an amount to which they were not entitled - Court held that respondents cannot recover the amount as Petitioner has retired.
