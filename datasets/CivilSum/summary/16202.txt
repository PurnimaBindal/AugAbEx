Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 12 - Conciliation Officer only has limited jurisdiction and cannot discharge judicial or quasi-judicial functions – he acts purely as an administrator; he does not have the authority to issue injunctions or stay orders.
[Paras 5, 7, 8 and 9] B.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25(T) and 25(U) - Conciliation Officer cannot issue ad interim orders with civil consequences, like injunctions in cases of unfair labor practices.
[Para 10] C. Industrial Disputes Act-, 1947, Section 12 - The powers and duties of the Conciliation Officer are clearly defined by the Act, and no other interpretation is necessary.
Courts cannot order the Conciliation Officer to act in a certain way if it is not provided for in the Act.
[Para 12] D. Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 12 - Settlement agreements reached before the Conciliation Officer cannot be classified as orders or awards.
