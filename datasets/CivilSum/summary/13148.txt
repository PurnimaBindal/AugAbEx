Article 16 of the Constitution of India deals with the topic of Compulsory Retirement and Adverse Remarks.
The fact of recording or conveying adverse remarks must be proven on record.
There is no evidence to support the observation made by the Screening Committee.
The Petitioner has earned a promotion.
Therefore, it is held that any adverse report loses its impact after receiving promotion on merit.
Consequently, the decision of Compulsory Retirement cannot be maintained under the circumstances.
[Paras 7 and 8]
