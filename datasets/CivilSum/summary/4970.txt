Sections 7-A and 14-B of the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act (19 of 1952), along with Section 22 of the Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provisions) Act (1 of 1986), dictate the determination of moneys due from an employer.
If an employer company is declared a sick industrial company under SICA and a rehabilitation scheme is formulated for payment of the amount to provident authorities in respect of an earlier period, the scheme can be accepted by said authorities without the consent of BIFR.
In such a scenario, Section 22 of SICA, which contemplates the suspension of legal proceedings, is applicable.
