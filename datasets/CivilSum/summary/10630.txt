Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol I, Part I, Rule 4.2-A - Counting of length of Service for pension - Officers can add actual period by which his age at the time of recruitment exceeds twenty-five years or a period of five years to their service qualifying for superannuation pension, in case of appointment to posts for which specialist qualification was essential.
However, this rule does not apply to officers seeking voluntary retirement.
