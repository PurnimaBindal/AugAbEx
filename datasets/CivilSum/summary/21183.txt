Section 1(3)(b) and 6 Entry 24 of the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 was amended by a notification dated April 30, 1962.
It covered the manufacturer of sweetmeats who sells his own produced sweets.
A demand for monthly deductions from 1986 to 1991 would cause immense hardship to the workers.
In the interest of justice and the unique circumstances of the case, the Act should be applied prospectively.
