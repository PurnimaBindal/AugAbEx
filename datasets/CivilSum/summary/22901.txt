Constitution of India, Articles 16, 39(d) - Rajasthan Civil Services (New Pay Scales) Rules, 1969, Schedule - Rajasthan Education Subordinate Service Rules 1971, Rule 1 - Equal pay for equal work - Pay scales of trained and untrained teachers performing identical duties - Rules that make a distinction in their pay on the basis of educational qualifications are not discriminatory - The qualification of being trained enables teachers to perform their duties better, justifying the granting of a better scale of pay.
