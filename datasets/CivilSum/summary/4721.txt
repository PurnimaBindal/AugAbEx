Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part-I, Rule 4.8 - Increment - Annual increments are typically given, unless an employee has been found guilty of misconduct.
In such cases, a prescribed procedure must be followed to provide the employee with an opportunity to respond before withholding the increment.
[Para 9]

Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part-I, Rule 4.8 - Efficiency Bar - Natural justice - Crossing the Efficiency Bar and receiving increments thereafter is not an automatic process.
The authority must assess the employee's suitability and fitness based on their service record.
If there is any adverse entry, the employee is given an opportunity to represent themselves and their representation is considered before a decision is made.
No opportunity of hearing is required at the Efficiency Bar stage.
Stoppage at the Efficiency Bar is not considered a penalty or taking away an employee's accrued rights.
[Paras 10 to 13]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Efficiency Bar - Opportunity of hearing - Promotion and the Efficiency Bar are similar processes, and no one can claim an opportunity of hearing for either.
[Para 12]

Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14 and 16 - Natural justice - Delay - As per the D.B.
Judgment in Gopi Ram v. Registrar, Coop.
Societies, it is necessary to grant an employee an opportunity to respond if their claim for crossing the Efficiency Bar is being considered.
A Single Judge is typically bound by a larger Bench decision, unless the issue is referred to a larger Bench.
However, any delay or laches can defeat a relief claim on the grounds of natural justice, eliminating the need for further referral.
[Paras 17, 19 and 20]
