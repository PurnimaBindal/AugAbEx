Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Civil) Regulations, 1965, Regulations 7, 10 - Higher Scale - Claim to - Circular to place Assistant Engineer/Assistant Executive Engineer on higher scale on completion of 9 and 16 years of service - Petitioner was granted scale after 9 years but was subsequently denied the further scale - The ground of getting three promotions in his service career and not being a direct recruit was cited - However, it was held that since the petitioner on promotion got the same position and status as direct recruit on obtaining his degree, 16 years of service must be counted from the date of joining as Assistant Engineer.
