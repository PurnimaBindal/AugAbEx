Bombay University Act, 1974, Sections 2(30), 42 - Retiral benefits - Part-time Lecturers are not entitled to retiral benefits according to the Govt.
Resolution dated 21.7.1983, which applies only to full-time teaching staff.
Even if the teaching hours or total emoluments become equal to a full-time Lecturer, part-time Lecturers will not hold equal status to full-time Lecturers if their teaching hours are clubbed across two institutions.
The classification between full-time and part-time employees is legally permissible and justified.
