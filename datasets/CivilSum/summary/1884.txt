Constitution of India, Article 14 - UP Legal Remembrancer Manual, Para 706 - Standing Counsel - Termination - State Government holds sole discretion in appointing Standing Counsels - Appointment of any legal practitioner as a District Government Counsel constitutes merely a professional engagement - As such, the State Government is empowered to disengage a Government Counsel without cause or cogent reason, without the disengagement being seen as arbitrary or violating Article 14 of the Constitution - Consequently, the State Government's policy cannot be considered arbitrary.
[Paras 8, 11 and 15]
