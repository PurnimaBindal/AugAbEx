Jammu and Kashmir Civil Service Regulations, Articles 177-A and 226(1) - Pension - Regularisation of temporary daily labourers - Cabinet Decision No. 119 dt. 14.3.80 specified that only those who worked as temporary or permanent daily labourers for at least 30 years were eligible for pension, and the petitioner did not fulfill this condition - Therefore, the petitioner cannot receive pension under Article 177-A [Paras 6, 7, 8 and 11]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Delay and laches - Pension - Premature retirement - Petitioner challenged order of retirement after eight years to claim pension - However, since the petition was filed after a significant delay, it was dismissed.
