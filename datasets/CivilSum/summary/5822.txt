Jammu and Kashmir Engineering (Gazetted) Service Recruitment Rules, 1973 - Promotion - Promotion to the post of Chief Engineer is a selection post - Selection is based on merit and suitability, not seniority.
[Para 12]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Selection - Court intervention - Court can only intervene in promotion matters if selection process appears to be unfair, perverse, mala fide or biased.
[Paras 12 and 26]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Promotion - Selection must be based on real, valid and fair merit despite the pick and choose - Violating Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution if based on irrelevant, inadmissible and non-existent grounds.
[Paras 12 and 26]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Consideration - Doubt regarding the proceedings of selection committee - Record not produced - Presumption is that the committee proceeded to make selection without any standard, norm or criteria arbitrarily.
[Paras 14 and 21]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Promotion - Annual confidential reports - Competent authority - ACR must be recorded by the competent authority as per rules.
Entries recorded by an authority other than the competent authority, cannot be considered to the prejudice of the officer especially when representation under rules is pending against the same before the competent authority.
Promotion cannot be denied on that basis.
[Paras 15, 17 and 19]

Criminal Procedure Code, Sections 193, 195 and 209 - Constitution of India, Article 226 - False affidavit - False statement - A person who gives false statement or files false affidavit in the court, can be punished for the charge under Sections 193 and 209 of Cr.PC  Chief Secretary of the state filed two contradictory affidavits against the official record amounting to false evidence and dishonestly making a false declaration before the court - Directed to be prosecuted in accordance with law.
[Paras 22 and 23]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 and 226 - Consideration - Promotion - Non-consideration of an eligible person violates whole of the selection process.
[Para 27]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Petitioner ignored illegally - Posts available - Without setting aside the selection if - the petitioner can be accommodated - Need not to quash the whole selection.
[Para 27]
