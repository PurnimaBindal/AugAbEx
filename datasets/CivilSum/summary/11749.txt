Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Pay fixation - The petitioners grieved that their case was neglected by the Pay Commission.
Although this Court cannot provide a remedy, a sub-committee comprised of the Secretary of the Finance Department, Secretary of the WB Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribes Welfare Department, and Chairman of the first respondent has been formed to address their grievances and issue an appropriate order in the interest of justice.
