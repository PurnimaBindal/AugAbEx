Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 226 - Speedy trial - Disciplinary enquiry - It is necessary to commence and conclude enquiries or prosecutions in a reasonable time frame - Even if the enquiry was justified, an eight-year delay alone would hinder the petitioner's defense - The delay would make it difficult to recall what happened and produce necessary documents and witnesses - It was concluded that gross and inordinate delay in commencing and concluding the enquiry is fatal to proceedings - The impugned order was set aside due to the delay.
