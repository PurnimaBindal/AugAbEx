Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - Retirement - A son of a retiree who takes voluntary retirement on medical grounds is entitled to a compassionate appointment as per the existing policy - The applicant's request was considered by the appointing authority and recommended for appointment to the Commissioner - However, the refusal of the appointment due to a change in policy is not proper - Applications that were already filed before the change in policy should be considered in accordance with the law in force on the date when the right of appointment accrued - Since the appointing authority has already considered and recommended the applicant for appointment, there is no need for such an authority to reconsider - The petitioner is directed to be appointed in the vacancy of his father.
