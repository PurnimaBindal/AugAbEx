The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 310 focus on compulsory retirement based on adverse confidential reports.
A compulsory retirement order can be passed on the basis of adverse entries in ACRs.
The mere fact that the petitioner met the eligibility criteria to appear in the S.A.S.
Part II examination and was allowed to appear does not mean that the adverse confidential reports lost their impact.
The Promotion Committee did not promote the petitioner on the basis of his S.A.S.
examination, even after considering his entire service record.
[Para 7] B The Constitution of India, Article 226 discusses the issue of mala fide, but allegations of mala fide are not supported by full facts and particulars.
Therefore, the order passed against the petitioner cannot be deemed to have been passed mala fide.
