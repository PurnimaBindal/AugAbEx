Constitution of India, Article 226 - Food Corporation of India Staff Regulations, 1971, Regulation 22(2) - Compulsory Retirement - The competent authority may examine the employee's past service record, regardless of the promotion they received - The petitioner has received multiple punishments that discredit them - The order of compulsory retirement is not a punishment, but one passed in public interest and is not vindictive, arbitrary, or mala fide - The High Court cannot act as a court of appeal over the authority's judgment, which alone has jurisdiction over employee records.
