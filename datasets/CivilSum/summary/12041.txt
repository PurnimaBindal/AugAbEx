Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - West Bengal Primary School Teachers' Recruitment Rules, 2001, Rule 8 - Selection - Employment exchange - Post of Primary School Teacher - The council must follow the provisions of the West Bengal Primary School Teachers' Recruitment Rules, 2001 for the selection process of the Post of Primary School Teacher.
A statutorily obligated employment notice must be published by the council inviting applications from all eligible candidates.
The Rules mention the number of candidates sponsored by employment exchanges.
However, the contention that Employment Officers of exchanges have to sponsor names of all eligible candidates once a requisition is received is untenable.
Employment officers are obligated to sponsor names strictly according to the requisition of the employers.
The petition has been dismissed.
