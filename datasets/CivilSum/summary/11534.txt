Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Regularisation - Continuity of Service - Haryana Govt. Policy dt. 7.3.1996 & 18.3.1996 - Reinstatement with continuity of service - When the Labour Court awards reinstatement with continuity of service for a worker, the worker is deemed to have been in continuous service and entitled to be regularised in accordance with the policy dt. 7.3.1996 & 18.3.1996.
