Army Act, Sections 43 and 122(iv) - Army Rules, Rule 13(iii)(v) - Army Regulations, 1987, Regulations 465-A and 466 - Limitation for taking cognizance of offences - Allegations of irregularity and malpractices in Army recruitment - The prescribed limitation of three years for taking cognizance under Section 122(iv) does not apply under purely administrative action taken under Section 13 - Powers under Rule 13 for administrative action differ from those for court martial proceedings, and both options are available to competent authority.

Army Act, Section 43(a) & (b) - Scope and applicability - Irregularities in recruitment - Clause (a) applies to those who enroll without obtaining discharge from the relevant corp or department, while clause (b) applies only to authorized enrollers engaging in malpractice - Enrolling persons guilty of misrepresentation can be discharged using Rule 13.
