Constitution of India, Article 226 (W) - Management Rules 1963, Rule 28(8) - Termination - Leave - The petitioner returned from abroad following an extraordinary leave of about four years, and no formal proceedings were initiated against the petitioner.
Neither disciplinary proceedings nor the appointment of an enquiry officer was made.
It was held that the termination order passed without following the stages of Rule 28 was not sustainable, therefore, the order was set aside, and the authorities were directed to provide the petitioner with incremental and leave salary benefits.
