In the case of a doctor prosecuted under Section 161 of IPC and Sections 5(1)(d) and 5(2) of Prevention of Corruption Act who was acquitted on the grounds that he was not a public servant as defined under the Employees' State Insurance Act and Indian Penal Code, it was held that Panel Doctors under the ESI scheme are not considered public servants because they are not paid by government funds, nor are they performing any public duty.
They are under a contract with the corporation, and therefore the order stating that the Panel Doctor is not a public servant does not require interference.
