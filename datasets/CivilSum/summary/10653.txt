Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Reversion - Post of Platoon Commander - Petitioner who had been promoted as Platoon Commander was reverted back - Respondent No. 1-Commandant General passed impugned order - The order of reversion is now nullified due to previous stay order and the impugned order of reversion has been quashed.
The petitioner shall be deemed to have been working on the post of Platoon Commander assuming the order of reversion was never passed.
[Paras 2 and 3]
