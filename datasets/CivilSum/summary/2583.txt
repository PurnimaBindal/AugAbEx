Order 5 Rule 2 of Civil Procedure Code (5 of 1908) states that 'due service' refers to the service of summons along with the plaint.
The Legislature has used the word 'due' intentionally.
The attachment of a copy of the plaint or a concise statement is mandatory along with summons.
Further, it is incumbent upon the process server to inquire about the exact identity of the respondent before recording a report of refusal of summons.
[Paras 13 and 17]

As per Second Proviso of Rule 13 of Order 9 and Rule 2 of Order 5 of Civil Procedure Code (5 of 1908), the proviso would be applicable in cases where the plaintiff can affirmatively prove that the defendant knew the exact claim made in the plaint, and the Court is satisfied with such evidence.
The Court needs to consider the quality of the evidence before it can refuse to set aside the ex-parte decree.
In certain circumstances, the defendant cannot argue there was an irregularity in the service of summons.
[Para 15]
