Punjab Naib-Tehsildar (Class-III) Service Rules, 1984, Rules 7 and 15 - Conditions of appointment as Naib-Tehsildars - Appointment letters issued without the necessary condition of passing departmental examination within 2-1/2 years from the date of appointment - Appointments defective.
[Para 6]
Punjab Naib-Tehsildar (Class-III) Service Rules, 1984, Rules 7 and 15 - Appointment - Eligibility - Feeder cadre - Appointment as Naib-Tehsildar - Appointments made from outside the eligible candidates list without proper reasons provided - Such appointments violate rules and principles of natural justice and should be quashed.
[Paras 7 and 9]
Punjab Naib-Tehsildar (Class-III) Service Rules, 1984, Rules 7 and 15 - Appointment - Promotion - Qualifications - Eligibility - Appellants already passed the departmental examination for promotion to the post of Naib-Tehsildar - Experience of five years as Kanungo not completed - Promoted in their own scale till they complete five years of experience - There is no bar in continuing, on the post of Naib-Tehsildar after passing departmental examination in the pay scale of Kanungo till completion of five years' experience - Authority had no jurisdiction to revert them after completing five years in the pay scale of Kanungo from the post of Naib-Tehsildars.
[Paras 8 and 9]
Punjab Naib-Tehsildar (Class-III) Service Rules, 1984, Rules 7 and 15 - Discretion - Appointment - Promotion - Discretion used arbitrarily in violation of rules and natural justice is not acceptable.
