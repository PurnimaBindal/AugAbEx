In Writ Petition No. 24901 of 1999  In WP Nos. 35485 of 1998  In Writ Petition No. 24901 of 1999  Contract Labour (Registration and Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 10(1) -Prohibition of employment of contract labour - The respondent-University claimed that the entrustment of Watch and Ward duties to contractors was not illegal because Section 10(1) of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act was not applicable to them.
They argued that the University is an industry, not a factory, and that G.O.Ms.Nos492 and 107 are not applicable to them.
