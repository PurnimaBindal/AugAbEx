The A Demobilized Armed Forces (Reservation of Vacancies in the Himachal Pradesh State Non-Technical Services) Rules, 1972, Rule 5(1) (as amended in 1974) state that an ex-commissioned officer, upon appointment as an ex-serviceman, will be assigned a notional date of entry into service which will also reflect in the seniority list.
The Tribunal was incorrect in only giving the benefit of the actual period of service during the emergency to the ex-servicemen for seniority under rule 5(1).
