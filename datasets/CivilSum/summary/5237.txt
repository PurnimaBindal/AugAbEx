Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(P), 12(3), 33 and 33(A) - Dispute pending - Unpaid holidays - Increase of - Permissibility - The employment of daily rates employees is presumed to be for the day, making it illogical to deny payment for forced holidays.
Therefore, awarding wages for forced holidays is justified, as no application was made to seek such permission.
