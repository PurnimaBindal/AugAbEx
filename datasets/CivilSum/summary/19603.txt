According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, additional Peon-cum-Chawkidar posts can be created when vacancies exist, and approval from the Board of Directors is necessary.
However, in the case that the Board of Directors is non-existent, proposals made by the Bank Administrator can be deemed as the Board's decision.
The Registrar Cooperative Societies, Punjab has been instructed to sanction the bank's proposal.
