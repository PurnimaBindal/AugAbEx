Rajasthan Police Service Rules, 1954, Rules 28A and 33 - Selection Scale/ Promotion - The appointment to the post in the Selection Scale is considered to be promotion and selection on merit, and seniority in the cadre has to be decided from the date of promotion to the selection scale.
The High Court's decision that appointment on the Selection Scale doesn't constitute promotion under the Rules was found to be incorrect.
[Paras 8, 10 and 11] B Promotion - Words and phrases - Promotion means not only advancement to higher position or rank but also higher position, honor and higher grade.
[Para 8] C Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservation in promotions/promotion - As per the Supreme Court's decision in Indira Sawhney's case, Reservations in promotions are allowed for five years from the date of that decision.
