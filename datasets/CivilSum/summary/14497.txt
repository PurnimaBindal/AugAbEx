SK Bhanot, DAG, Punjab, arguing based on Constitutional articles 14 and 16(4) as well as Punjab Civil Services Rules, presents that a candidate not falling in the reserved category cannot be promoted against a reserved point and that the petitioner is entitled to be considered against the reserved point that he was deprived of.
It is further indicated that the promotion of Respondent No.4 against the reserved point is quashed, and that the petitioner's claim against the reserved roster point must be considered.
