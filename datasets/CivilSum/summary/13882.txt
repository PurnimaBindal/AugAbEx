Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25F - Refrenchment-Deemed continuous sevice - If a workman has worked for not less than 240 days in a period of 12 preceding months, they are deemed to have completed one year of service.
The petitioner in this case has worked 240 days in the last 12 months and is entitled to the benefit of deemed continuous service.
