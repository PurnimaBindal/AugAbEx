The petitioner was promoted from the rank of Head Constable to ASI in his own rank and pay of Head Constable under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
However, his rank of ASI was withdrawn after he was conveyed adverse remarks and his representation was rejected.
It was held that the report was legal and tenable and the High Court cannot review the remarks recorded by the competent authority.
The petitioner was granted fortuitous promotion with a condition that he would be reverted back to the rank of Head Constable if his work was not found satisfactory, and the reversion was in strict conformity with the terms of his appointment.
[Paras 8, 9 and 13]

The petitioner contended under Punjab Police Rules, 1448 that it was incumbent upon the authority to keep him informed about his defects.
However, it was held that the petitioner was aware of his defects, as he had represented against those remarks.
The remarks recorded against the petitioner and the orders rejecting his representations were found not to be vitiated.
[Paras 11 & 12]
