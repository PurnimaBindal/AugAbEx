According to Section 10(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, the Government cannot decline to refer a dispute for adjudication by deciding that dispute itself.
The Government's role is to only ensure the existence of an industrial dispute and cannot make decisions based on the evidence.
Any questions regarding the absence or abandonment of service must be decided by the Labour Court based on the evidence provided by the parties involved.
Therefore, if a dispute exists, the Government must make a reference as required by law.
