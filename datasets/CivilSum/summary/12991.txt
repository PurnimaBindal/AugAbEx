Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25F - Termination of service - A messenger who was employed on 23487 was removed from service in April 1998 without retrenchment compensation.
The workman had completed 240 days in service, and there was nothing to show that the need for the workman was over, so compliance with Section 25F was necessary.
The order of termination was set aside for being passed in violation of Section 25F [Paras 3 and 6] B.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(j) - Industry - If an industrial establishment maintains any office anywhere singly or jointly with any other industrial establishment, and that office is under the supervision or charge of an establishment, such establishment shall be considered an industry within the definition of Section 2(j) of the 1947 Act.
