Correction of date of birth in service record - Petitioner's school certificates and service record differ on date of birth - Date of birth was entered in service record based on Medical Certificate without asking petitioner to provide proof - Best evidence for age of educated person is Matriculation Certificate, but petitioner is not a matriculate and school certificates can be relied upon - Direction given to correct date of birth in service record according to school certificates.
[Paras 7 and 11]  B.
Application of Rule 56 Note-6 of Fundamental Rules - Petitioner appointed before Rule 56 Note-6 came into force - Rule 56 Note-6 requires application for correction of date of birth within five years of December 15, 1979 - Petitioner made representation in 1976 when he found out about wrongly recorded date of birth - Respondents cannot discriminate between employees who apply for correction before or after December 15, 1979 - Action of respondents would violate Article 14 of the Constitution [Para 10]
