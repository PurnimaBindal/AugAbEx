According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, section 31-B of the UP State Universities Act 1973, and Sections 12 and 16 of the U.P.
Higher Education Services Commission of Act 1980, ad hoc appointments must follow the rules and regulations with respect to qualifications and authority.
Any appointments made in violation of these regulations, cannot be regularised.
Such appointments are void under Section 12 of the Act.
However, if the appointee has acquired necessary qualifications, like a PhD, and has become eligible for the position of lecturer, their case may be reconsidered for relaxation of age limit based on their long service and good results.
