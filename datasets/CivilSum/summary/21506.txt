"The Army Act, Sections 52(a) and 164(2), combined with Pension Regulations for the Army, 1961 (Part 1), Regulations 16(a) &113(a), state that if a military official is dismissed from service under Section 52(a) of the Army Act, they are no longer entitled to pension or gratuity for their previous service.
Additionally, no presidential order is necessary to forfeit pensionary benefits in these instances.
