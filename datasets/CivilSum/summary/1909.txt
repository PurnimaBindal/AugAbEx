Kerala Service Rules, Chapter VII, Rule 57 - Provisional driver - Service terminated - Ground of being detained in custody on criminal charge - Reinstated after his conviction was set aside - Determines if entitled to pay and allowance for the period absent- According to Rules 56, 57 pay and allowance for such period granted only if acquitted of blame - Petitioner was not acquitted of blame - Authorities were within their rights to deny pay - Appeal dismissed.
