The appellants were suspended on 1021983 and 811987 due to charges of defalcation of property.
However, there had been no progress made in the criminal cases against them, and they had been under suspension for twelve to fifteen years.
Based on this, it was held that the continuation of their suspension is arbitrary and unjustified, and they cannot be asked to wait until the criminal case is disposed of.
As a result, the orders of suspension were set aside.
