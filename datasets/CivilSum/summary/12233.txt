Constitution of India, Article 309 - Central Services (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1944, Rules 3 and 6 - Medical reimbursement - For open heart surgery, actual expenses or package rates can be reimbursed - The petitioner is entitled to full reimbursement for expenses incurred at Escorts Heart Institute & Research Centre, New Delhi - The state is responsible for bearing the expenditure of government servants - An office memorandum on reimbursement of package rates was valid for two years only, while the subsequent memorandum recognized private hospitals and diagnostic centres under CGHS scheme for specialized treatment and package ceiling rates.
