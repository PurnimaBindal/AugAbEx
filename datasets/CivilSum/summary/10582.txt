Constitution of India, Article 311 - Departmental Enquiry/Termination/Charge-sheet/Natural Justice/Punitive order/Innocuous order - The petitioner was charge-sheeted, and their reply to the charge-sheet was deemed unsatisfactory.
A final show cause notice was issued, proposing termination of services as the penalty.
Services were terminated without conducting an enquiry.
The order of punishment went beyond the charges made in the charge sheet, and the petitioner was not given a chance to explain some of the other allegations.
These orders are illegal and violate principles of natural justice.
The order was set aside, and the petition allowed.
[Paras 3 & 4]
