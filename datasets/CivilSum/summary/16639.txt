Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - Mala fide exercise of power - The petitioner, a Senior Specialist, contests the transfer of respondent No. 4, a Junior Specialist, to the post meant for a Senior Specialist.
The petitioner was then transferred to accommodate respondent No. 4 which the court determined as a transfer suffering from mala fide.
As a result, the petitioner's transfer cannot be sustained and was quashed.
[Paras 8 and 9]
