A Bank employee was convicted under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, but paid the amount to drawee despite the conviction.
The employee was discharged on the grounds that the offence involved moral turpitude, but the order was set aside.
The question whether an offence involves moral turpitude must be decided on the facts of each case.
On the facts, it was held that the offence against the employee did not reflect moral turpitude.

According to Section 415 of the Indian Penal Code, an offence will only involve moral turpitude if the person had the intention to kill.
For example, causing the death of another by rash and negligent driving will not involve moral turpitude.
