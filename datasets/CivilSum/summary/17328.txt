Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311(2) - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Discharge - Absence from duty - The petitioner, who served as a police constable from November 1989 to December 11, 1991, was discharged from service due to habitual absenteeism.
The order of discharge was made as a punishment without providing any charge-sheet or inquiry, and thus, is subject to being quashed.
