Constitution of India, Article 16 - Medical Reimbursement - A petitioner experienced a heart attack and was hospitalized at an approved facility - The petitioner paid for the hospital bill - Later, the petitioner joined the CGHS scheme and was issued an identity card by the MCD in order to receive treatment for themselves and their family under the monthly pension program - Despite submitting bills to the MCD, reimbursement was refused - The court held that the MCD must reimburse the medical bills.
