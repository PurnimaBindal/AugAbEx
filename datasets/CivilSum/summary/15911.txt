According to the Constitution of India, Articles 311, disciplinary action cannot be taken against an employee who has been allowed to retire voluntarily, unless the rules governing the employment state otherwise.
If an employee is issued a charge sheet and then allowed to retire voluntarily, it is considered a waiver of disciplinary proceedings and proceedings cannot be processed further.
[Paras 4 and 5]

If an employee applies for voluntary retirement and it is accepted, they will only be deemed to have retired from the date specified in their application, not from the date of acceptance.
[Para 6]

In accordance with the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311, issuing a vague charge sheet just before an employee's retirement and allowing them to retire unconditionally is a violation of natural justice.
A charge sheet should include particulars of misconduct, names of witnesses and instances of the misconduct.
Such charges cannot be sustained and proved after a lengthy period of time.
As a result, the authority has been restrained from proceeding with the charge sheet.
[Para 7]
