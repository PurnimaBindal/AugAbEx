According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, JE petitioners are entitled to Travelling Allowance.
The petitioner in this case was granted permission by the University to participate in a championship in Durban and made a claim for a total of Rs. 75,357/- for his travels.
Although an amount of Rs. 37,000/- had been paid in advance, the remaining amount of Rs. 38,357/- was deducted from his retiral benefits.
The petitioner claimed that this was unjust as he trusted in the University's approval for the travel.
As a result, the respondents were directed to pay the petitioner the full amount of Rs. 75,357/-.
