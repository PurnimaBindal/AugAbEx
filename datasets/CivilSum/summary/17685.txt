The termination of services based on the Departmental test is allowed under Articles 14 and 311 of the Constitution of India, and failure to clear the test can result in removal from service.

Chapter V-B of the Industrial Disputes Act deals with retrenchment only in industrial establishments defined under Section 25(L).
Section 25-N does not apply to Government Corporations as such corporations are not covered under Section 25-L (a).
[Para 6]
According to Rule 76 of the Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules, 1957, retrenchment must comply with the provisions of Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act.
Even if the notice sent to the appropriate Government exceeded the period of three days, it does not invalidate the retrenchment.
The notice provisions are not mandatory unless they have caused prejudice to the workman.
