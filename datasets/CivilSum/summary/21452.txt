Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 15(4) - Reservation in admissions - Admission criteria for Post Graduate Courses in Medicine and Surgery - Reserved seats for Dalit, Tribals and OBCs - MBBS qualifying marks of 35% in admission tests is required - The Govt. criteria cannot be altered - If reserved category candidates do not meet the minimum qualifying marks, the seats should be opened to the general category -The importance of merit in higher disciplines should not be ignored -Strict selection on merit for post graduate medical courses like MD, MS, and the like.
