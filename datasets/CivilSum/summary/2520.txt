Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Seniority/Promotion - Various co-employees make representations for their seniority to be refixed above their juniors and for the promotion to be ante-dated if applicable - Competent authority acts arbitrarily by granting relief to one employee and keeping the others' claims undetermined - The authority must consider all representations and decide accordingly - The petitioners' claims must be considered and if found entitled, relief must be granted from the due dates.
[Paras 5 to 9]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and laches - The High Court should not exercise its certiorari jurisdiction in stale claims but relief cannot be denied to similarly-situated persons simply because some have approached the court at a particular point in time - The plea of delay and laches cannot be used when the competent authority itself granted relief after a delay of 4-5 years to other employees with pending representations - Similar relief must be granted to other similarly-situated persons.
[Paras 6 and 7]
