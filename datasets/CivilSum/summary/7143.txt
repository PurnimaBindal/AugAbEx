Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965 - Military service - Benefit of military service - Entitlement - The petitioner served in the army between 11.4.1961 to 271976 - According to the 1965 Rules, only those who enrolled during the proclamation of the National Emergency (26.10.1962 to 10.1.1968) were entitled to receive benefits - Since the petitioner had joined the army before the proclamation of emergency, the petitioner is not eligible for the benefit of military service under 1965 Rules.
