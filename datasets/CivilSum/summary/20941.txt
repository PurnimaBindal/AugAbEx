Constitution of India, Article 16 - Pleadings - The petitioner was promoted to Excise and Taxation Inspector on probation and subsequently passed the required departmental examination.
Despite others who did not pass the examination being confirmed, the petitioner was not confirmed in the role.
The petitioner was granted relief of confirmation, however, the appointments of those who did not pass the exam could not be invalidated as it was not within the scope of the case.
[Paras 6 and 7]
