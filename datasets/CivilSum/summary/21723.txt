Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punishment - Quantum of punishment - Reversion - Misappropriation of large amount of stocks and repeated transgressions - Proven charges - Reversion punishment is too lenient considering the seriousness of charges and scale of misconduct - Disciplinary authority has the power to impose appropriate punishment based on gravity of misconduct - The High Court's reduction of punishment from reversion to only two increments stoppage was incorrect.
