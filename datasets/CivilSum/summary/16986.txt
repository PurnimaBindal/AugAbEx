Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Reversion - Options - Acceptance of options - Cancellation of options after acceptance - Change of cadre - Cancellation of options after acceptance and change of cadre to technical posts - Any reversion by cancellation of options must adhere to the principles of natural justice - Options that have been repeatedly invited and accepted cannot be viewed as inadvertent or mistaken - Therefore, the reversion and cancellation of options contravene Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
