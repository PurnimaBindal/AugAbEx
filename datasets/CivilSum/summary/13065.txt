According to Rule 5(2)(i) of Punjab Trust Service (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules 1978 and Section 17 of Punjab Town Improvement Act 1922, only direct recruitment, promotion or transfer can be used to make appointments under Rule 5(2) of Municipal Service Rules.
However, the petitioner was absorbed in the Municipal Service after being declared surplus from Improvement Trust without following the proper procedure.
Therefore, the petitioner was never permanently absorbed in the Municipal Service and remained an employee of the Trust from the beginning.
Due to this, the petitioner was not considered for promotion to the post of Executive Officer.
