According to Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226/227, corporations or bodies created under statute are considered to be State instrumentalities or agencies.
The MP Rajya Bhandar Grah Nigam, as a result of its functions and powers of the Registrar, Cooperative Societies, is an instrumentality of State that is subject to writ jurisdiction.
[Para 11]
Per Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311, the court has the jurisdiction to determine if findings were based on some evidence, not whether the evidence was satisfactory or not.
The court can intervene in cases where evidence is absent.
Findings that are based on hearsay evidence, suspicion, and surmises are deemed to be illegal.
[Paras 12, 15 and 16]
