The Appellant had requested transfer to the General Surgery Department, but was instead ordered to work in the Plastic Surgery Department and deemed to have gained teaching experience in the General Surgery Department.
This order was not requested by the Appellant.
Even if the Appellant had requested this, it was improper and illegal for the Government or authority concerned to comply.
This order is unjust and must not affect the seniority of the Appellant.
