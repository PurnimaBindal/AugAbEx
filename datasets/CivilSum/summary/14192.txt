Haryana Revenue Department District Subordinate (Group-C) Service Rules, 1988, Rule 9 - Promotion to the post of Assistant - Petitioner denied promotion for not passing type test and junior candidates were promoted - Petitioner argues there is no requirement in statutory rules for type test but his appointment letter outlines the condition that he must pass the test to earn increment and be governed by Civil Service Regulations and Instructions - The latest instruction by Chief Secretary makes passing type test essential and petitioner is bound by it, but he has not challenged the clauses in the appointment letter - Ruling states that respondents 1 and 2 have the right to consider petitioner for promotion and take into account all relevant factors, including his non-passing of the type test.
