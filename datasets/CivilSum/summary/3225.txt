According to the Limitation Act of 1963, Article 67 to the Schedule and Section 3, any suit filed that is time barred must be dismissed, even if the limitation is not set up as a defence by parties.
This was highlighted in the case where an appellant did not have any opportunity to set up limitation as a defence to a suit since a decree was passed ex parte against him.
It is important for trial courts to consider the question of limitation even without any defence set up by defendants, especially when dealing with the suit ex parte.
