According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, an ad hoc appointment should not result in reversion without proper consideration of natural justice principles and qualifications.
The petitioner was promoted from Peon to Clerk without any mention of a specific time period, allowing them to serve as a Clerk with requisite qualifications and earned increments for over six years.
The authorities cannot revert the petitioner now for lacking typewriting skills or claim the initial appointment was just a stop gap arrangement.
Any such reversion would violate the principles of natural justice and Constitutional Articles 14 and 16.
