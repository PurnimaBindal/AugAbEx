Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Voluntary Retirement - Absent form duty - Enquiry - Holding of - Despite giving notice of voluntary retirement, the Petitioner was absent from duty and subsequently issued a charge sheet leading to termination of his services.
Petitioner's contention that no enquiry was held was dismissed and the reason for not holding an enquiry was deemed unacceptable as an enquiry could have been conducted ex-parte.
Consequently, the order of termination was quashed.
However, considering the fact that the Petitioner remained absent from duty after giving notice of voluntary retirement, he is not entitled to wages for the intervening period.
Petition was allowed partly.
