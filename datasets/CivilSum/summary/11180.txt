According to Conservation of Foreign Exchange and Prevention of Smuggling Activities Act, 1974, Section 3(1), a document is considered relevant and material if it has the potential to sway the opinion of the Detaining Authority in one direction or another.
[Para 11]
In a case where the Detaining Authority considered irrelevant documents such as the detenu's divorce deed, an application for grant of `B’ Class in jail, and receipts of gifts and Istridhan items, the order of detention was set aside since it demonstrated a lack of careful consideration on the part of the Detaining Authority.
