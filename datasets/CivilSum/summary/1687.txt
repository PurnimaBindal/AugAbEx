Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I Part I, Note 9, Rule 44 - Fixation of pay - Petitioner was a direct recruit to the Superior Judicial Service.
The petitioner held a permanent post of District Attorney and received a basic pay of Rs. 1930/- plus Rs. 200/- in special pay.
Upon selection, the petitioner was appointed as Addl.
District and Sessions Judge and sought protection for special pay of Rs. 200/-.
The court found that there is no rule stating pay of a member of service on joining Superior Judicial Service be fixed like direct recruits.
Petitioner held entitled to pay fixation under Rule 44 of PCS Rules, Vol.
I part I and special pay of Rs. 200/-.
Respondents directed to pay entire arrears that may be due on the basis of fixing the pay.
[Paras 8, 9, 10 and 11]

Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 277 - Delay - Cause of action - Representation filed in 1983 - Decided in 1991 - Writ filed in 1991 - Petitioner argued the cause of action arose when the representation was decided.
The court found no delay in filing the writ petition.
