The petitioner's claim for promotion to the post of Senior Inspector of Factories was carefully considered but ultimately denied based on the determination that he was not suitable for promotion.
As he has already retired from service, no further action can be taken through writ proceedings.
[Paras 5 and 6] B.
It must be noted that a civil servant has no inherent right to promotion, but is simply guaranteed the opportunity to be considered fairly.
This guarantee is provided under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
