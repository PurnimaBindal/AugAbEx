Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Section 15(2) - Rajasthan State Road Transport Works and Workshop Employees Standing Orders, 1965 - Deductions - A bus conductor lost un-used tickets and way-bill - An inquiry was held under the Standing Orders and orders were passed to deduct the loss from his pay - An appeal under the Payment of Wages Act set aside these orders - The deductions made were not covered by Sections 7(2)(c)(h) and 10 of the Wages Act - The Wages Act's jurisdiction is limited, and the deductions made under Standing Orders fall outside its jurisdiction - The Payment of Wages Authority has only jurisdiction to hear and resolve claims arising from deductions from wages or delay in wage payment that are not permissible under the Act of 1936 - The Payment of Wages Act does not apply to cases where an employer withholds wages due to an employee's negligence, misappropriation, or default.
