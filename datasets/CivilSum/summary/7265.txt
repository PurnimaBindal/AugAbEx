Regularization - The Supreme Court did not explicitly reject the claim of regular pay scale for respondents whose service was regularized from the date of their initial appointment.
However, the court denied a similar claim to another person.
The respondents accepted other benefits and terms of their appointment without objection and only raised this claim after 5 years.
As such, the terms of their appointment, which they accepted without reservation, bind the respondents.
Even if such a contention were raised, it would be deemed rejected in light of the decision in the case of "SBI v. Ram Chandra Dubey" and the specific rejection of similar claims.
