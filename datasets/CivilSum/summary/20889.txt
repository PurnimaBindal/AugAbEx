The Haryana and Punjab Agriculture University Act, 1970, Statute 16(11) and the Constitution of India, Article 14 clarify the determination of the study period salary and allowances.
Lecturers at HAU who were selected to undergo PhD Course in 1978 were permitted as in-service candidates according to the leave of the kind due to them, and pursued the course until 1980-81.
Despite not being paid leave salary, they cannot have a higher right than what was permitted to avail of.
Therefore, they are not entitled to salary and allowances, though other teachers, after lifting the prohibition, were permitted to undergo the course of study with full pay and allowances.
There was no individual discrimination or arbitrary or unjust action violating equality enshrined in Article 14.
Statute 16(11) of the Act is inapplicable.
(Paras 3 4 & 5)
