Central Reserved Force Act, 1949, Section 11(1) - Central Reserved Force Rules, 1955, Rule 27(a) - Punishment of pre-mature retirement - Rule 27 can impose the compulsory retirement punishment after charges are proven in an inquiry, and even though Section 11(1) does not mention this punishment, Rule 27 should not be disregarded.

B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Central Reserved Force Act, 1949, Section 11(1) - Quantum of punishment - Wednesbury principle of proportionality would apply if the inquiry procedure was irregular or the principles of natural justice were violated, leading to great prejudice or absurdity committed by the authority - within the limits of judicial review, the punishment cannot be dismissed for disproportionality when there is no such deficiency.
