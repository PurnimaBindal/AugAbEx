Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment on compassionate grounds - A minor petitioner's father died while in service of Electricity Board - Instructions issued on 2731991 said that a minor who attains the age of majority can be considered for a job on compassionate grounds if an application has been submitted within three years by their guardian after their father's death - The Board was obligated to reconsider granting the petitioner a job upon his attaining majority, especially since an application had already been filed within the specified period - Being rejected at the relevant time is not sufficient grounds to deny the petitioner relief.
