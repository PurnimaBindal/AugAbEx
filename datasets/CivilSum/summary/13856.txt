According to Delhi Education Act, 1973, Sections 20 and 24(4) pertaining to Appointment of Assistant Teachers, a selected candidate was not given an appointment letter by an aided school.
Therefore, the Directorate has given directives to issue an appointment letter and to consider notional benefits for the petitioner.
Action has been taken to ensure that the appointee receives the proper compensation.
