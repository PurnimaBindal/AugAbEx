Constitution of India, Article 16, 226 - West Bengal Primary Education Act 1973, Sections 2(vi) and 60(1)(k) - Appointment - The Director was instructed to appoint the Petitioner by the High Court, but the Council objected to the binding effect of such an order.
The Director is not required to meet the Council's standards for any appointment, as the State has the authority to verify the Council's right through the Director.
As a result, the contested order stands in force.
