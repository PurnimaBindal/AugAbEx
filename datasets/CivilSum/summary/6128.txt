Constitution of India, Articles 226, 309 - Pension Regulations Governing Arms Force, Reg.
113, Appendix II Clause 7 (a) and (b) - Disability pension - The petitioner was discharged from the Army due to non-organic psychosis and assessed at 20% disability - It was determined if the disease was attributed to or aggravated by military service - There is no evidence that the disease was noted at the time of the petitioner's acceptance for military service - The Medical Board did not state that the disease could not have been detected prior to acceptance - Therefore, the legal assumption that the disease arose during service applies - The petitioner will be considered for disability pension - The writ petition has been granted.
