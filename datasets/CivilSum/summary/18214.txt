Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection and Appointment of Anganwari Workers - Experience - Emphasis must be placed on giving preference to experience and daughter-in-law of the village as opposed to the unmarried daughter - Priority should be given to more experienced candidates rather than those with a familial relationship - Candidates with more experience should not be overlooked in favor of a daughter-in-law with less experience.
