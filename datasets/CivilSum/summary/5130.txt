Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226 - Rules for Compassionate Appointment, 1996, Rules 5, 10(3) - Termination - Compassionate appointee - Concealing fact of elder brothers being already employed in Education Department leads to termination - An ameliorating relief should not be taken as opening an alternative mode of recruitment to public employment - Since appointment of Petitioner was against rules, there is no illegality, irregularity or infirmity in impugned termination order.
