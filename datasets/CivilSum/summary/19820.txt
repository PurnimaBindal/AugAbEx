According to the Constitution of India, Article 16, equal pay for equal work is mandatory.
The petitioners were selected in 1979 as Managers (Rice Mills) in HAFED with a pay scale of Rs. 700-1250, and the respondents were selected for the same posts and carrying out the same duties and responsibilities in 1982 but with a pay scale of Rs. 800-1600.
Therefore, the petitioners are also entitled to the same pay scale of Rs. 800-1600.
Denying the same pay scale to equals in the same cadre with transferability of officials violates Article 16 of the Constitution.
