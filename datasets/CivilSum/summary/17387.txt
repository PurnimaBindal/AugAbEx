Army Pension - Civil Service benefit toward Army Pension - An Army officer who starts working for a civil organization after retiring from the Army is not entitled to receive pension based on the pay drawn in that organization.
Instead, the last pay drawn from their service in the Army will be considered as per Note 5 below Para 3 of the Government of India, Ministry of Defence letter dated 30101987.
When an officer serves in a civilian organization, only their basic pay plus NPA and rank pay, if any, will be treated as emoluments for pensionary benefits.
