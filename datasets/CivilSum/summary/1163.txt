Anjali Chowdhury, Advocate asserts that under Constitution of India Article 226, Writ jurisdiction may not compel the authority to exercise its discretion in favor of a petitioner claiming relaxation of the normal rules.
Moreover, the authority must reasonably exercise that discretion, which in this case, has not been shown to be exercised in any unreasonable manner.
As such, the writ court will not mandate the authority to grant approval as prayed, and has only directed respondents to give pay scale to petitioner from 29488.
