The challenge to the selection of respondent No. 5 for the post of Reader in Engineering and Technology is based on the ground that he lacked the requisite experience on the cut off date.
However, despite this, he was called for an interview and then selected.
The decision is that a person who has been selected without meeting the necessary qualifications cannot continue in the post just because litigation has delayed the process.
Therefore, the selection of respondent No. 5 is set aside.
