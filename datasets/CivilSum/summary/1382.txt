Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Qualifications - The employer's management has the authority to set a bar for overqualified individuals - The prescribed qualifications can depend on the employer's specific needs - When an employer sets a minimum qualification requirement, they can also declare overqualification as a disqualification for appointment - Based on the petitioner's higher educational qualifications, the respondents disqualified them for the post of messenger - The prescription of over-qualification as a bar for recruitment cannot be considered arbitrary or a violation of Articles 14 and 16.
