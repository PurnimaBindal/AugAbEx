Constitution of India, Article 16 and 342 - Presidential Scheduled Tribe Order, 1950 - Reservation - Reorganisation of States - Identification of disadvantaged classes for grant of benefit of reservation on reorganisation of States - To prevent automatic deprivation of reservation benefits, scrutiny committee must investigate if already identified scheduled tribes in a particular area are still entitled to such benefits - Referral to the scrutiny committee must be made before any decision regarding reservation is made.
[Paras 5 to 7]
