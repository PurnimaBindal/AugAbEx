Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 and 227 - Advance Increment - Recovery of excess amount paid - Recovery of excess amount paid to an employee, claimed to be a mistake, can't be sought after six years, especially when earlier proceedings for recovery against other teachers have been dropped as per High Court's judgement.
