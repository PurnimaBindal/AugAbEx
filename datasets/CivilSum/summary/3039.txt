The petitioner is seeking grant of Pensionary benefits or payment of employer's contribution towards CPF for the service period from 12/4/1951 to 31/12/1987.
However, the petitioner became a member of CPF scheme by his own violation and is not entitled to the relief of pension.
Additionally, since CPF scheme was introduced in May 1960, the petitioner cannot claim CPF benefit from 1951.
Therefore, the prayer of petitioner is declined and the petition is dismissed.
