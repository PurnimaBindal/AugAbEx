As per the court ruling, the petitioners who were appointed as Taxation Inspectors and were entitled to entry in service from 161993, have claimed for notional fixation of pay.
The Selection Board gave appointments to dependents of Ex-servicemen against the post of Ex-serviceman, leading to injustice to the petitioners.
It has been held that their pay can be fixed notionally from the said date, however, they will not receive actual financial benefits until the date of their joining the service.
[Paras 8 and 9]
