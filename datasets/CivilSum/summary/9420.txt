The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, and the Pharmacy Act, 1948, Section 32B, provide for dismissing employees with natural justice.
The eligibility of a salesman at the time of appointment must be considered when examining misconduct claims.
The mere opinion of the Council is not enough to prove misconduct without further inquiry into the employee's eligibility.
Without any evidence, the dismissal order is void.
