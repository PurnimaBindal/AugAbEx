Constitution of India, Article 226 - Haryana Canal and Drainage Act, 1974, Section 17(i)(c) - Challenge to order of Chief Canal Officer shifting area from one outlet to another - Divisional Canal Officer and Superintending Canal Officer rejected claim of respondents 1 to 3 on the grounds that their case pertained to sharing of water course and thus, consent of shareholder of proposed outlet is required - Respondents 1 to 3 claimed shifting of area from one outlet to another, so no provision exists for obtaining consent of shareholders of proposed outlet - Order of Chief Canal Officer was based on actual consideration and as the best judge in such matters, no interference is allowed under Article 226 of Constitution of India.
