According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, teachers who acquire higher qualifications should receive advanced increments.
A distinction between those who acquire higher qualifications during service and those who have them before joining service is considered arbitrary, irrational, and unrelated to achieving the intended objective.
The acquisition of higher qualifications by any teacher serves the same purpose, regardless of whether it was obtained before or during their employment.
Denying senior teachers the benefit of higher pay while granting it to junior teachers based on the same qualifications is unfair.
