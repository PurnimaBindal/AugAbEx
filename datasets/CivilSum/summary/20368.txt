Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Railway Establishment Manual, Rule 3511(c) - Pension - Regularisation - Termination of services of ad hoc employee - Re-instatement in the status of regular employee - Qualifying service - Consideration of condition in the Rule for regular appointment after medical examination - Medical examination undergone after years of re-instatement - Regular employee status can only be granted from date of medical examination - Benefit received already exceeds entitlement - Initial appointment cannot be used for granting regular employee status - However, entitled to pension benefits through temporary service as qualifying service.
