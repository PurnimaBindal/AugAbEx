Constitution of India, Article 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Dismissal - Re-instatement - The disciplinary authority should reserve the punishment of dismissal for extreme cases and it should be moulded with humanism and understanding.
In this case, the Labour Court found the punishment of dismissal to be harsh, unjustified, and disproportionate to the charge levelled against the workman.
The Labour Court ruled in favour of reinstating the workman with 50% of back wages, and the petitioner corporation was unable to point out any infirmity in the impugned award.
As a result, the petitioner corporation is directed to implement the award passed by the Labour Court within three months.
