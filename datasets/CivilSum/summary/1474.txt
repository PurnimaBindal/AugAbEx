According to the Constitution of India Article 141 - National Council of Teacher Education Act, 1993, Section 16 - National Council of Education (Determination of Minimal Qualifications for Recruitment of Teachers in Schools) Regulations, 2001, Regulation 4 - Qualifications for Assistant Teachers of Primary Education are specific, therefore recruitment of teachers based on any other qualifications is illegal and arbitrary, and violative of mandatory rules and court directions.

The Constitution of India Article 226 - National Council of Teacher Education Act, 1993, Section 16 - National Council of Education (Determination of Minimal Qualifications for Recruitment of Teachers in Schools) Regulations, 2001, Regulation 4 - The qualifications prescribed for Teachers are based on academic experts’ opinions and hence the court will not interfere with these expert opinions needlessly.
