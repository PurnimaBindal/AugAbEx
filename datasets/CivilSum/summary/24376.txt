Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination of service - A civil suit brought by a bus conductor who was appointed ad hoc for two years is not maintainable as employees of the Corporation are not civil servants and are not entitled to protection under Article 311 - The proper remedy for the employee is reference under the provisions of the Industrial Dispute Act - Reinstatement by Civil Court is not proper - Order set aside.
[Paras 16 to 18, 21 & 22]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(k), 10, 12, 18, 19, 31(2), 33(1)(a), 32-C(1) and 33-C(2) - A bus conductor, who is governed by Standing Orders of Rajasthan State Road Transport Corporation, can seek remedy under the Industrial Disputes Act for termination of service - Workshop employees are also governed by the terms of appointment.
[Paras 15 & 16]
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Enquiry before termination - It is not necessary when services were terminated simpliciter and do not contain any stigma under the law.
[Para 19]
Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 9 - Jurisdiction of Civil Court - The jurisdiction cannot be conferred by any order of Court if the Court has no jurisdiction.
