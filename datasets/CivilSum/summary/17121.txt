Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311(2) - Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 7 - Natural justice - Enquiry report - Imposing punishment different from the findings of the enquiry officer without notifying the employee of any reasons or grounds is a violation of natural justice - As such, the actions of the punishing authority have been deemed to violate natural justice principles - The order for punishment has been accordingly nullified.
