Constitution of India, Article 226 - Ex-serviceman - Civil employment - MCD does not recognize Petitioners' degrees/certificates attained during army service, disregarding several authoritative judicial pronouncements.
Court orders respondents to treat certificates of Petitioners as equivalent to civil degrees as per NCO code 13410 and issue letters of appointment.
Petitioners are entitled to notional seniority and fixation of pay from the date other individuals were appointed.
