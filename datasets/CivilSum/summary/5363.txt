The Non-payment of contribution to the Provident Fund by an employer in respect of workers employed in an industry is categorized as an economic offence under the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act.
Moreover, the limitations mentioned in Chapter XXXVI of Criminal Procedure Code are not applicable to this type of offence, despite the fact that the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 is not included in the schedule of the Economic Offences Act.
[Para ?]
Failure to pay contributions to the Provident Fund by an employer is considered an economic offence.
Even if proceedings for 10 years are pending, the accused is not entitled to discharge due to the delay in proceedings, as per the ruling in 1996 (2) RCR (Criminal) 419 (SC).
[Para 12]
Non-payment of contributions to Employees' Provident Fund results in an economic offence that cannot be overlooked due to the fact that one of the accused remained absconding.
Therefore, the accused is not entitled to discharge despite the delay caused by the proceedings.
The magistrate has been directed to complete the proceedings in six months.
