According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, a probationer's service can be terminated if they were appointed on a temporary post.
If there is no record of explicit confirmation, the employee cannot claim deemed confirmation upon completion of probation.
The decision to terminate the service in such cases cannot be considered as punitive or stigmatic, nor can it be said to be a mala fide exercise of power.
Additionally, the respondents have taken the stand that the Petitioner in this case does not have all the required qualifications for employment.
