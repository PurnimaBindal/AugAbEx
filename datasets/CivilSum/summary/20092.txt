Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16(4) - M.P. Police Regulations, Section 70 - Promotion - Relaxation in qualifying marks - Relaxation is only available for Dalits and Tribes reserved category candidates who do not qualify the qualifying test up to the required number of candidates, not for general candidates - The authority also has the power to withdraw the relaxation - Candidates who qualify due to relaxation do not have any vested rights - In case of any mistake, the authority can withdraw the concession at any time - Therefore, the authority rightfully withdrew the relaxation after realizing the error.
