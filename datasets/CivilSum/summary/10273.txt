Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14, 16 - Defence Services Regulations, Regulation 628 - Family pension - Second marriage by an Army man - The authority is justified in refusing grant of such benefit on mere asking of a stranger claiming to be the widow of a deceased defence personal who allegedly died 40 years ago if there is no marriage certificate or any other evidence of second marriage of an Army Official nor there is any entry in the Army record to that effect - Writ court cannot enter upon a consideration of such belated events especially when there is no evidence at all - Action of the authority cannot be said to be arbitrary.
[Paras 4 and 5]
