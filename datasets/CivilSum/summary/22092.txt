Constitution of India, Article 136 - Misconduct - Termination - If an employee is found guilty of misappropriation, the appellant-corporation may lose confidence in the employee.
Thus, it would not be fair for the court to substitute its own finding and confidence over that of the employer's decision to not reinstate the employee.
Additionally, due to the seriousness of the offence, the Labour Court cannot use its discretion to alter the punishment.
[Paras 7 and 8]
