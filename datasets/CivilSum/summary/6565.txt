Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Award of Labour Court - Judicial review - Presumption of guilt - Even though the High Court will not act as an appellate jurisdiction over the proceedings of the disciplinary authority and the Labour Court, the court will still interfere if the punishment order is based upon a presumption of guilt without any proper evidence - The court cannot simply presume guilt without any evidence to support it - The allegations against the petitioner only involve having spare parts in their almirah in the workshop which weren't immediately deposited back in the store after completion of the job - No presumption of guilt can be made unless the spare parts were found on the petitioner when leaving the workshop with an intent to steal them.
