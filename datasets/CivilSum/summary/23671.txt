Constitution of India, Articles 226, 371D - A.P.
Educational Institutions (Regulation of Admission) Order (1974), Para 11(c) - Education - 'Part-time course of study' - Duration of hours of study and syllabus are the same for students enrolled in evening law college as for students enrolled in day college - Students receiving the same degree as day college students cannot be considered part-time students - Whether a course of study is part-time or full-time depends on the content, tuition, and duration of the course - Local candidates may be given reservation as per the order.
