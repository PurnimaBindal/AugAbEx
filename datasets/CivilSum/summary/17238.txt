According to the Constitution of India, namely Articles 16 and 226, the petitioner, an employee of the first respondent School, claimed salary for her leave period due to breast cancer.
However, her claim was not considered as she did not provide proper medical certificates.
The petitioner's grievance was that she had not been paid for nearly 12 months after recovering and resuming her duties.
As a result, the court directed the MCD to pay the petitioner her salary and bonus in accordance with the rules.
