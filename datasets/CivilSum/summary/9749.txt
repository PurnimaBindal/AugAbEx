Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part-I, Rule 8.126 - Study leave - Cancellation of - Estoppel - Post Graduate Medical Course - If study leave is granted by the authority under the Rules and instructions, it is deemed that all requirements have been fulfilled and all aspects and facts have been considered - It cannot be cancelled after the completion of the employee's studies or converted to extraordinary leave that disadvantages the employee - Doctors from the same service who complete their post-graduation from Medical Colleges in the State or outside are required to complete three years of service instead of five years to be eligible for such courses.
