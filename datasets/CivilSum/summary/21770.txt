Government Servant Conduct Rules, 1956, Rule 24 - Movable, immovable and valuable property - When a Govt. servant enters into transaction of movable or immovable property the procedure indicated in the service rules therein, i.e. no Govt. servant shall except with the prior knowledge of the appropriate authority acquire or dispose of any immovable property by lease, mortgage, purchase, sale, gift or otherwise, either in his own name or in the name of any member of his family shall be followed - Obtaining the previous sanction of the appropriate authority is necessary for transactions that are not conducted through a regular and reputed dealer.
[Para 6] B.
Removal from service - A court does not have the authority to question the imposition of punishment on a Govt. servant - It is the disciplinary authority's responsibility to determine the appropriate penalty for the proved misconduct - Even if only one of the allegations is held valid by the disciplinary or appellate authority and is sufficient to impose a penalty, the court will be hesitant to interfere with that portion of the order - The order of removal does not malign the respondent's reputation to allow him to seek employment elsewhere - The High Court was completely wrong in overturning the order - The appeal was granted.
