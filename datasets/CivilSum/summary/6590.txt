The Constitution of India, Articles 30, 32 and 226 pertain to private schools and minority institutions.
Private schools established under the statute are amenable to the writ jurisdiction of the High Court.
This position has been followed in the cases of Miss Raj Soni v. Air Officer In charge (AIR 1990 SC 1305) and Frank Anthony's case (AIR 1987 SC 311).

Section 10 of the Delhi School Education Act, 1973, pertains to the Appropriate Authority.
The Appropriate Authority is competent to consider the contravention of the provisions of Section 10.
If the employees of the schools governed by Section 10 of the Act are not paid the emoluments equal to the employees of the schools run by the appropriate authority, the authority is competent to enforce the same.
However, equal emoluments mentioned in Section 10 do not mean equal basic pay and monthly salary drawn.
The authority has to take into consideration total emoluments paid in different manner including yearly bonus etc if any.
