Disciplinary proceedings against an employee that led to a Labour Court awarding reinstatement are not subject to the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 10.
Instead, the employee should turn to the Asstt.
Registrar, as provided under Section 55(2) of the Madhya Pradesh Co-operative Societies Act, 1960.
As a result, the award passed by the Labour Court must be set aside.
