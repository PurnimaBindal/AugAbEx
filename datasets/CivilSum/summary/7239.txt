Employees State Insurance Act (34 of 1948), Section 75 - Two factories running from the same premises, one owned by the father and the other by his son.
There is no functional integrity or interdependence between the two factories, and there is no unity in the matter of ownership, control and supervision.
Both establishments maintain separate books of accounts, separate balance sheets, and are assessed separately by the Income Tax Department.
Thus, both factories cannot be clubbed for the purposes of the ESI Act.
