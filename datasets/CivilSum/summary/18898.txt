Army Regulations, Regulations 185-A & 143 - Disability Pension - Sustaining disability - Re-evaluation of extent of disability - If the percentage of disability does not change after successive Medical Boards, then the disability pension will remain for ten more years - After that period, the pensioner needs to undergo reassessment of the disability by the Medical Board and if he fails to do so, authorities may withhold his pension.
