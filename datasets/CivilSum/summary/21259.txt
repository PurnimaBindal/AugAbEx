Under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, candidates who were eligible up to 1991 and for the vacancies available in 1991 must have their claims considered.
However, vacancies arising after 1991 should not be taken into account.
Candidates who become eligible later on have the right to be considered for later vacancies.
[Para 1]
Marks are allotted based on records of service and interview, with 50% weightage given to the latter.
No written test is involved and the interview marks are not excessive under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
