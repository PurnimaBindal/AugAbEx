Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16(4) - Selection - Appointment - Selection alone does not guarantee the legal right of appointment - The court cannot enforce appointment if the appointing authority has valid reasons to withhold it after selection - The selection committee found merit in the representation of Scheduled Caste candidates and withheld the appointment of the petitioner as the post should have been reserved for them - The petitioner's claim for appointment based solely on the selection is not sustainable.
