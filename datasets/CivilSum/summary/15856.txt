Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Regularisation - The petitioner, who was appointed on an ad hoc basis for 89 days against vacant posts, joined service on 1st Feb, 1990.
Her services were extended from time to time until the end of July 1994.
The Punjab Govt. issued instructions for the regularisation of services of ad hoc/temporary Class III & II employees who have put in two years of service as of 27th March 1992 and were still in service on that day.
Instructions were also issued for the regularisation of the service of ad hoc/temporary employees who completed a minimum of 240 days of service as of 311294 and were still in service on that day.
The petitioner who satisfies the requirements of instructions was ordered to be regularised.
[Paras 3, 4 and 6]
