Under Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rules 4(ii), military service benefit and seniority must be taken into consideration for promotions - Deemed date of promotion must be given after beneficiary of military service passes test - If a petitioner fails promotion test much later than a junior who was promoted earlier, petitioner will get deemed date of promotion one year later - Once notional promotion is granted, the person is entitled to all consequential benefits regardless of whether they actually worked in position or not.
