Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - BackWages - In case of illegal termination, the workman must receive full back wages upon reinstatement and any attempt to object must be supported by proper evidence - The absence of malice in the wrongful termination cannot be used as a reason to deny the workman their entitled back wages - Based on 1979 PLR 720 (FB), the award has been modified accordingly.
