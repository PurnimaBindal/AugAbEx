According to the Bihar Pension Rules, 1950, Rules 43(b) and 139, the Apex Court has ordered the petitioner to receive pension on his superannuated scale of pay as a Superintending Engineer.
Although the petitioner was entitled to 100% pension, the department provided provisional pension due to the direction of the Apex Court.
The department is correct to specify the pension as provisional because it was to be revised after the petitioner's acquittal in the criminal case.
[Para 9]
The Bihar Pension Rules, 1950, Rules 43(b) and 139 state that proceedings under Rule 43(b) of Pension Rules can take place after the petitioner's retirement.
However, the proceedings should not be regarding a matter more than four years before the initiation of proceedings.
The department has been directed to make a final decision within four months.
