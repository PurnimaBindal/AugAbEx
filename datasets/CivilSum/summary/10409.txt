Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10(1) - Delay and laches - Reference - Order of rejection of claim to make a reference by the appropriate Govt. challenged after a delay of over four years - The petitioner sought to excuse the delay on the basis of ignorance of law and illiteracy, stating that they were unaware that the order could be challenged in a writ petition - However, such an explanation could not be used to justify a delay and would set a problematic precedent - Ultimately, the petition was rejected as belated.
