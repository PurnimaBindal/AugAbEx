Clause 10 of the Letters Patent Appeal suggests that during a reference under Section 10(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, while making a decision on matters "incidental thereto," the tribunal can delve into matters such as the relationship between the master and servant.
The Single Judge was right in dismissing the writ petition based on the grounds that the tribunal could investigate the relationship between the employer and the workman.
The appeal was dismissed, and this conclusion was based on several previous cases.
