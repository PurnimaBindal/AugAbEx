Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Quasi-judicial Authority - Disciplinary Authority exercises quasi-judicial powers - It is required that the order should be a speaking one - The disciplinary authority must have objective satisfaction about the guilt and not subjective.
[Para 4]
Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Disciplinary Proceedings - On alleged misbehavior and disobedience, the delinquent should be given copies of the Proceedings - Failure to provide copies would amount to denial of reasonable opportunity.
[Para 5]
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary Proceedings - Alleged intentional absence - If extraordinary leave is granted during a particular period, then the public servant is permitted to be absent from duty during that period.
