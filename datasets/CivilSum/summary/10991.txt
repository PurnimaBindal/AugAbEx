Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Cancellation of candidature - Petitioners who were appointed as Gram Sachivs against posts reserved for Ex-Servicemen and their dependants had their services terminated due to failure to produce eligibility certificates - The question of eligibility as a dependant of an Ex-Serviceman is determined by whether the person has income from other sources such as agriculture, trade, property, bank balance etc. - The Board was required to examine whether the agricultural land owned/or inherited could provide a perpetual source of income to the candidate - Without reaching such a conclusion, the Board could not cancel the candidature - The order terminating services of petitioners was declared illegal and quashed.
