The petitioner, an employee of a Cooperative Bank, was terminated from his services while still in probation on the charge of embezzlement.
Upon further investigation, the petitioner's service termination was found to be a punishment for being found guilty of embezzlement without any enquiry.
The petitioner is entitled to emoluments from the date of termination until the date of award along with interest at 18% as per AIR 1980 SC 1896.
