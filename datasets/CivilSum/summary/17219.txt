Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination from service - Validity of - A Chowkidar on daily wages in the newly created Sub Treasury Office was appointed by a written order.
However, his services were later dispensed with by an oral order, which is illegal and arbitrary.
The services of the petitioner could only have been terminated by a written order by which he was appointed.
As a result, the action of the respondents cannot be maintained, and the petitioner must be reinstated in service with full arrears of salary.
[Paras 5 and 6]
