According to Rule 162 of the Punjab Police Rules, 1934, a police constable can be dismissed from service for absence from duty.
In this case, the appellant was dismissed from service for being absent from duty for about seven months and was found to be a habitual absentee.
The courts upheld his dismissal, stating that such a long absence by a police constable on guard duty constituted the gravest act of misconduct.
