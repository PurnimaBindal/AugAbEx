Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Service of Engineers Class II PWD (Public Health Branch) Rules, Rules 11 and 12 - Probation period/Seniority - Rule allows for a one-time extension of probation period for six months and a declaration of satisfactory completion - Extension must be granted before or shortly after the end of probation - However, extending probation six years later and declaring successful completion is arbitrary and has been overturned.
