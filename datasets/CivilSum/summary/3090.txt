Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 39 Rule 1, 2 and 115 - Interim injunction - Selection - Appointment - A person who was not selected for the post of lecturer filed a suit challenging the selection process and selection committee members on various allegations.
The court determined that granting an interim injunction would have serious consequences, including preventing selected persons from being appointed and causing harm to students who were not parties to the suit.
The court therefore held that it would be inappropriate to grant interim relief which amounts to granting final relief at the interim stage.
The court further stated that depriving a selected person from joining will have significant negative effects, whereas an individual who was not selected could receive monetary compensation if their lawsuit succeeds.
