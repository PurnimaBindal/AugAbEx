According to Army Pension Regulations, Part-I 1961, Regulation 316, eligibility for mustering-out pension includes certain categories of discharge.
If a petitioner was not discharged from the Army due to being supernumerary in the cadre and opted to quit instead of accepting reversion to a lower rank, they do not meet these requirements and are not entitled to a mustering-out pension.
