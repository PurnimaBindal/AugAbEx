The Uttar Pradesh Services of Engineers (Buildings and Roads Branch), Class-II Rules, 1936 (as amended in 1969 and 1971), Rule 23(d) Note (1) specifies how the seniority between direct recruits and promotees should be determined.
In accordance with the rules, promotees are entitled to benefit from their service in officiating capacity towards seniority, and their seniority is counted from the date of continuous officiation rather than the date of approval by the Service Commission.
The seniority list of 1995 prepared by the government of UP pursuant to the directions contained in D N Saksena and V K Yadav's cases is in accordance with the rules and any officiating service before confirmation or approval will count towards seniority.
Therefore, the confirmation/approval will relate back to the date of appointment may be ad hoc or officiating.
[Paras 3, 11, 12 and 26]

The challenge against the seniority list is only on the interpretation of rules and general principles of seniority without any individual grievance against any particular person.
Any individual claim still remains open to adjudication.
Direct recruits filed a petition against the principle giving benefit of their officiating service towards seniority to promotees, and the promotees are held not necessary parties.
[Para 10]

The government's conduct during the case was highly questionable.
The government supported the seniority list between direct recruits and promotees before the High Court and opposed the writ petition, but actively supported the case of writ petitioners in the Supreme Court.
This conduct is deemed to be reprehensible and against the interest of the administration.
[Para 27]
