Constitution of India, Article 309 - UP Subordinate Offices Ministerial Staff (Direct Recruitment) Rules, 1985, Rule 4 - UP Absorption of Retrenched Employees of Government or Public Corporation in Government Services Rules, 1991, Rule 2 - Retrenchment - Petitioner, a temporary employee who worked for four years and was terminated as per the appointment letter which stated the appointment would come to an end automatically after one year - The termination was due to terms and conditions of employment under a contract of service and therefore cannot be considered as retrenchment.
[Paras 27 and 34]
