Constitution of India, Article 226 - Relief - In order to seek relief from the High Court under Article 226, a party must act in good faith and provide true and full disclosure of all relevant facts.
They must not attempt to mislead the Court and their conduct must be honest.
Failure to do so may result in the High Court declining to exercise its extraordinary jurisdiction in favor of the guilty party.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Ad hoc appointment - Regularisation of service - The regular mode of recruitment through a properly constituted selection agency is required in order to ensure the most meritorious persons are appointed in public services, and the claim of regularisation of service of ad hoc appointees is an exception to this.
[Para 8] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Ad hoc appointment - Regularisation of service - Regularisation of service for those who are inefficient or have a service record full of adverse remarks would be detrimental to the minimum requirements of efficiency in public service.
The Court cannot order regularisation of service for individuals such as the petitioner who consistently receives adverse reports.
The plea to quash termination of service or to order regularisation of service based on length of service is untenable.
