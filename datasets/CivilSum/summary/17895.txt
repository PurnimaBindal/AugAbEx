Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Absorption - Clerk and Peon in the Homoeopathic Dispensaries - The Government has taken over the dispensaries and only staff working in dispensaries are eligible for absorption - However, the petitioners who were employed in connection with the working of dispensaries argue that they are also entitled to be absorbed in Government service - Petitioners held to be staff of the dispensaries when they were taken over.
