Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education/Examination/Result - The petitioner was initially shown as having passed the Arts and Crafts diploma exam, but it was later discovered that she had actually failed.
Due to this error, she missed the opportunity to appear for the exam a third time.
The authorities were instructed to allow her to take the exam once again to use her remaining chance, but her request to be declared a pass was denied.
