Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation - Retrenchment - Mere completion of 240 days of work by a temporary employee is not sufficient for regularisation in service.
All conditions prescribed for regular appointment must be met, including an existing vacancy and following the correct procedure.
Nonetheless, this requirement does not exclude the worker from the protection provided by Section 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act.
