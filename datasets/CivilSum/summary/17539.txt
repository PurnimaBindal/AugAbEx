Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Appointment - Compassionate appointment - Application of mother for giving appointment to her second son on compassionate ground rejected on the ground that brother was not dependent on the deceased-employee - Appeal against - Govt. Circular provides that employment would be given to a son/daughter/near relation of a Govt. servant who dies in harness leaving his family in ``immediate need of assistance'' - Govt. servant reported to be missing since 1985 and after lapse of 11 year it cannot be said that the family requires ``immediate need of ``assistance'' - Further Govt. Circulars specifically speak of actual death of a Govt. employee while in employment and not presumed death - Held, second son of appellant not entitled to derive any benefit out of circulars - Appeal dismissed.
