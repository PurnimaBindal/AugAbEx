Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) Reference - Natural Justice - If the appropriate Government declines a reference to an industrial dispute and then exercises its power for the second time, natural justice principles must be adhered to and parties must be heard.
[Para 8] B.
Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, Section 10 - Evidence - Pleadings - If a point that goes to the very jurisdiction of the Court is not raised before the Labour Court, it can still be raised for the first time in the writ jurisdiction.
Additionally, if an authority had no jurisdiction to hear a matter and make an order, failing to raise that question before the authority doesn't give it jurisdiction.
This issue can also be raised for the first time in the writ petition.
[Para 11] C. Privilege - Secret record - Evidence - The record of Sales Tax authorities isn't sacrosanct and can be reviewed in court.
