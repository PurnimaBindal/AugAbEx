Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Education - Examination - The petitioner was not found to have withheld any information from the respondents.
Although he had defaulted in clearing 50% of theory papers, he was allowed to take seventh semester examination and attended eighth semester classes for a short period due to a mistake, which was admittedly on the part of the respondents.
The petitioner cleared more than 50% practical papers and, therefore, should not be debarred from taking the seventh or eighth semester examinations on this count.
The petitioner cannot be held liable for the admitted mistake made by the respondents.
The petition has been allowed with discretion to declare the petitioner's result of all semesters, and attendance for the eighth semester is to be tabulated from the date of admission.
