Kerala Service Rules, Part 1, Rules 56B(i)(a) - Kerala Civil Services (Classification Control and Appeal) Rules, 1960, Rule 18(b) - Suspension - Pay and allowances during suspension period - If a government servant is suspended pending a disciplinary enquiry that is either initiated and proceeded with parallel to proceedings before a criminal court or initiated and completed after completion of proceedings before a criminal court, the mode of treatment of the period of suspension will be governed by Rules 56 and 56B, and Rule 18(b) will not apply.
[Para 18]  B Service Rules (Kerala), Part 1, Rule 56B(v) - Suspension - Determination of pay and allowances for the period when a government servant was kept out of duty due to his suspension - The concerned government servant is entitled to notice of the proposal by the competent authority.
