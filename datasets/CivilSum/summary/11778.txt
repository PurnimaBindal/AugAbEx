Punjab Housing Development Board Act, Section 99 - Ad hoc Promotion - Departmental Examination/SAS Examination - Regularisation - Petitioners promoted to Section Officers (Works) on ad hoc basis subject to passing of departmental/S.A.S examination - Reverted from the posts of Section Officer (Works) to Senior Assistant (Accounts) due to failure to pass the examination - Cleared examinations required for regular promotion - The petitioners, who failed to clear departmental/S.A.S examination by non-appearance in 3 chances available to them, lost their right to hold the posts of Section Officer (Works).
- Directed that if during the pendency of writ petition, any petitioner cleared SAS/departmental examination, then instead of reverting the respondents should adjust him against vacant post of Section Officer (Works) - Petition dismissed.
[Paras 15 and 17 to 19]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Order of reversion - Discrimination - Held, Petitioners cannot succeed in their plea of discrimination on the ground that some of persons selected for appointment promotion being made to await posting while others who had not passed departmental examination being continued on higher posts - Court cannot invalidate or quash an order which is otherwise legal and proper merely because in some other case similar order had not been passed.
[Para 16]  C Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume-I, Part-I, Note 4 of Rule 49 - Annual increment - passing of departmental/SAS Examination - Board can lay down requirement of passing of departmental/S.A.S examination as a condition precedent for grant of annual increment to an employee because successful clearance of departmental examination adds to the efficiency of employee.
