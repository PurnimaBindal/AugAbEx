Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 34 - Writ Jurisdiction - Disciplinary Proceedings - Suspension - Premature writ petition - An employee of an Agricultural Co-operative Society, who is under suspension and facing a disciplinary enquiry, should proceed with the enquiry before invoking the writ jurisdiction of the court - The petitioner can approach the court only when it could be stated that the enquiry proceedings were conducted unfairly or with prejudice against him - The delinquent cannot avoid the domestic enquiry and immediately resort to the court invoking its writ jurisdiction.
[Paras 7 and 9]
