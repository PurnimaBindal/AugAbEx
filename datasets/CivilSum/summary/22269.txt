A Commandant (Selection Grade) cannot benefit from a higher age of retirement under Rule 9 of Border Security Force (Seniority, Promotion and Superannuation of Officers) Rules, 1978 and will retire at the age of 55, not 58.
The term "Selection Grade" involves an element of selection because employees do not automatically get promoted to the selection grade pay scale.
Selection grade posts are created to provide relief to those who have limited opportunities for promotion to higher posts.
Higher pay or pay scales are granted in the same post.
Therefore, selection grade posts cannot be considered higher posts for the purposes of Rule 9, Border Security Force (Seniority, Promotion and Superannuation of Officers) Rules, 1978.
