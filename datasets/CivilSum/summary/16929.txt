Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Regularisation of Contractual employees - The petitioner's service were regularised on March 1, 1997, as per Government policy that permits the regularisation of employees on a contractual basis at the fixed salary of Rs. 1400/- having worked more than 240 clear days in a calendar year, during the last two years against available vacancies.
Nevertheless, the Respondents de-regularised the petitioner's services on August 26, 1998, on the ground that his services were regularised inadvertently in excess of vacancies.
However, during the pendency of the present petition, the petitioner's services were again regularised on March 1, 1997.
Hence the petition rendered infructuous.
The petitioner has requested that his claim for regularisation w.e.f.
December 15, 1995, when his junior was regularised, be examined by the respondent within three months and benefits, if due, be granted.
[Paras 5 and 6]
