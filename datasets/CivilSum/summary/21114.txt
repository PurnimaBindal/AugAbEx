Advocates Act, 1961, Section 36B(1) - Professional misconduct - Negligence of Government Pleader causes client to suffer ex-parte decree - Negligence does not have mala fide or deliberate inaction - Negligence without moral turpitude or delinquency may not qualify as professional misconduct.

Advocates Act, 1961, Section 36B - Professional misconduct - Whether negligence of an Advocate qualifies as professional misconduct depends on each case's facts - Dereliction of duty towards client or case constitutes professional misconduct - Negligence without moral turpitude or delinquency may not qualify as professional misconduct.
[Paras 12 to 14] PK Khandekar v. Bar Council of Maharashtra and others, AIR 1984 SC 110 at 113.
