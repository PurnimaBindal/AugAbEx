The petitioners in this case were initially appointed as Basic Health Workers in the Health Department of the former State of Joint Punjab, but after the reorganization of the State of Punjab, they were allocated to Haryana and allowed to work as Health Inspectors.
According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226, officiating employees are entitled to pay scale corresponding to their position.
Therefore, the petitioners are entitled to pay scale admissible to a Health Inspector.
The respondents are ordered to pay the petitioners their arrears of salary within six months.
