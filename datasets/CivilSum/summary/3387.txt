RN Raina, DAG of Haryana, argued that according to the Haryana Service of Engineers Class II, P.W.D.
(Irrigation Branch) Rules of 1970 and Article 226/227 of the Constitution of India, he was eligible for promotion to the rank of S.D.O.
based on the fact that he had passed the AMIE exam in 1979.
He claimed that his declaration of result of examination of AMIE would relate back to the year in which he passed the examination in 1979 and cited the P.W.D.
Manual to support his claim.
The court found his argument to be valid and directed the respondents to consider Raina's case for promotion.
If respondents come to the conclusion that the petitioner does not have the right to be promoted at par with other promotees, a reasoned order must be passed and communicated to the petitioner.
