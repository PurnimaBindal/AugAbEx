Industrial Drugs and Pharmaceuticals Ltd.
Conduct, Discipline and Appeal Rules, 1978, Rule 30A - Departmental proceedings - Amended provision - Applicability - Despite the notice not explicitly indicating that it was in reference to amended Rule 30(A), the proceeding was still valid as the reply from the accused suggested they were aware of it.
High Court erred in dismissing the proceeding on this basis.
[Para 11] B Fresh departmental proceeding - Two earlier proceedings pending - Sustainability - Employer had the right to initiate a fresh departmental proceeding based on an amended provision, even if two earlier proceedings were pending, provided that the new charge-sheet did not indicate that the previous proceedings were disregarded.
