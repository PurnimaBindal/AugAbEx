According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226/227, equal pay for equal work must be provided.
However, in a case where non-diploma holder road inspectors were claiming the same scale of pay that diploma holder road inspectors were receiving, it was found that the basis for fixing different pay scales for the two categories of road inspectors was that diploma holders possess technical qualifications, which places them in a higher pay scale, while those without such qualifications are placed in a lower pay scale.
As a result, the doctrine of equal pay for equal work was not attracted and the petition was dismissed, as seen in V Marekendeya v. State of AP, 1989(3) SLR 37.
