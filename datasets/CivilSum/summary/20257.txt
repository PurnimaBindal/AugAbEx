The Govt. Order dated 821983 stipulates pay fixation on re-employment after retirement from defence services in accordance with Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
The minimum pay scale will be awarded to ex-servicemen on re-employment in addition to their pension.
The term "pension will be ignored while fixing the pay on re-employment" means this.
[Para 4]

The Govt. Order dated 25.11.1958 cites Article 14 of the Constitution of India and explains pay fixation on re-employment after retirement.
The term "hardship" is only applicable when the ex-serviceman's pay is fixed in the time scale at the minimum level and his pension counted together is less than the total salary he was receiving before retirement.
The term "hardship" is not applicable when the ex-serviceman's pay and pension exceed the amount of pay he was receiving previously.
