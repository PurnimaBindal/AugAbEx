The officer who ordered a transfer can do so for administrative reasons, and as long as no rules are violated, the court cannot interfere in such matters.
A transfer made to ensure peace in the workplace cannot be considered malicious, even if the officer making the transfer did not have the power to do so.
Even if an officer is absent and then returns to duty after a court order, they are not entitled to full back wages.
Additionally, a tribunal cannot grant continuity in employment if the officer was absent for six years.
