Karnataka Selection of Candidates for Admission to M.Sc.
Nursing Degree Course Nursing Rules, 1995, Rule-4 (As amended on 1010200) - Constitution of India, Articles 19, 226 and 227 - Selection of candidates for admission based on merit regardless of service status was the norm before the amendment - Post-amendment selection of in-service candidates only based on seniority - This change is not unreasonable, arbitrary, or violative of Article 19, as experience is a nurse's merit.

Nursing Degree Course Nursing Rules, 1995, Rule 4 (As amended on 10102000) - Amendment with retrospective effect - Selection process already initiated before Amendment - Retrospective statutory rule only when expressly or impliedly stated - Completion of the process should be undertaken with existing provisions.
