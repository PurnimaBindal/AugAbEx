Kashmir and Jammu Universities Act (24 of 1969), Sections 40 and 36 - Termination of service - Ad hoc employee - Tribunal must provide natural justice - Petitioner-Lecturer in University issued notice of alleged misconduct - Termination cannot be attributed to unsatisfactory service alone - Allegations do impart a negative reflection on employee - In such cases the court must examine the real basis for termination - As this is not a situation of termination simplicitor of an ad hoc employee - Termination without enquiry is vulnerable to being cancelled.
