I, 1989, Regulation 14 - Cancellation of examination - The Vice-Chancellor has the power to cancel an examination in emergent matters and can seek approval in the next meeting of the Syndicate - After receiving complaints regarding irregularities in the examination, the Vice-Chancellor found that the allegations made in the complainants had prima facie credibility - The Vice-Chancellor had a duty to investigate and take action to remove any biases in the examination process - The action of cancelling the examination taken by the Vice-Chancellor was approved by the Syndicate and was legal.
