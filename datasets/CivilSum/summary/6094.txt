Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb), 25F - Adhoc appointee - Termination - Argument that non-renewal of contract does not qualify as retrenchment - No specified duration for contract - Employee has served 240 days consecutively - Decision: Section 2(oo)(bb) is inapplicable in absence of fixed term - Therefore non-adherence to section 25F makes termination invalid.
