The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Voluntary Retirement - Option - Withdrawal of - If employees request the withdrawal of their option after the specified period in the application and their request is accepted, the retirement will become active automatically from the date of notice expiration.
The contention that the lack of communication of the order would invalidate the acceptance is not valid since there is no requirement for such communication.
