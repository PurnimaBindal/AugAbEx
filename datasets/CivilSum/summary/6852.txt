Under the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 7A and 16, the contribution under the Act during the infancy period would be of benefit.
The appellants pleaded for separate incorporation and registration under the Indian Companies Act and other enactments, but the RPF Commissioner held that there was functional integrity and unity of purpose among the three companies.
He dismissed the argument that separate incorporation and registration of these companies under different enactments would make them different units.
The Commissioner had ample evidence to pierce the veil and read between the lines and was well within his rights under section 2A read with Section 7A of the Act to arrive at this satisfaction while deciding the claim of infancy made by the appellants.
