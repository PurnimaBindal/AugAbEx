The Factories Act, Sec. 46 states that for the purpose of the act, workmen of a statutory canteen are workmen of the establishment only and not for all purposes under the Constitution of India, Article 14.
[Paras 17 and 22] B.
If the canteen workers are under direct control of the management and paid through contractors but not engaged solely for the purpose of record, then for all purposes, they are considered to be under the direct employment of the management as per the Constitution of India, Article 14.
[Paras 26 and 27] C. While the initial appointment of canteen workers may not have been in accordance with the rules or policies of recruitment of an instrumentality of state and required to conform to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution, directions for the regularisation of workers are not made on the basis of any statutory right but as a means of eradicating unfair labour practices, undoing social injustice, and as a measure of labour welfare.
