Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Punishment - Substitution of, by Labour Court - Jurisdiction - Termination of services of employee - Although the Labour Court deemed the employee ineligible for any benefits, they still ordered the employer to hire them in a non-driver position for humanitarian reasons- this was not appropriate.
When the Labour Court deemed the employee's termination as legal and valid, it went beyond its jurisdiction by substituting the punishment.
