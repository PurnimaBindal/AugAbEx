The Punjab Private Managed Recognised School Employees (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Section 8(3), and the Punjab Privately Managed Recognised School Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 6 specify that appeal can be made to the Tribunal under Section 8(3) of the Act not only against dismissal, removal, or reduction in rank but also against termination except where the service ends by efflux of time.
Despite the availability of an alternative remedy, the jurisdiction of Court under Article 226 of the Constitution is not ousted.
[Paras 6 and 7] B.
In the selection process under the Punjab Private Managed Recognised School Employees (Security of Service) Rules 1981, Rule 6, the presence of the BEO in the Selection Committee is necessary.
If the BEO is duly called, but he fails to attend the meeting or does not inform beforehand, the remaining members have the power to proceed with the selection.
[Para 7]  C The selection process can be held on a working day or a holiday.
The instructions to hold the selection process on a particular day are directory and not mandatory.
Any deviation from this rule does not make the selection void.
[Para 7] D. To be eligible for a particular post, the experience counts from the date the person acquires eligibility qualifications.
This rule is applicable under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
[Para 10] E For the promotion from Teacher to Principal under the Punjab Private Managed Recognised School Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 6, the experience of teaching is essential.
The rule does not mandate that the teaching experience should be only on the post of Master or Mistress, as both of them are equivalent.
