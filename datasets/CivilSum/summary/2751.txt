Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Rajasthan Non - Government Educational Institutions Act, 1989, Section 18, Proviso (iii) - Termination of posts - Employees' service can only be terminated after prior compliance and approval of Section 18 of the Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions Act - Section 18 compliance is mandatory, even if the services are terminated due to post abolishment - Order passed by Tribunal and upheld by Single Judge has no infirmity.
