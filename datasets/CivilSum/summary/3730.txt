The plaintiff in a suit for a declaration could not receive the pay scale of the post of Accountant-cum-clerk under Grant-in-Aid Rules, Rule 15.6 until he met the eligibility qualifications on 8.11.1969, after the rules were applied on 30.11.1967.
However, once the plaintiff met the required qualifications, it was legal to fix the pay from that date forward.
[Paras 7 & 8]
In a regular second appeal, it is necessary to have a substantial question of law for jurisdiction under Section 100 of the Civil Procedure Code to apply.
