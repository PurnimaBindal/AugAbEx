The Departmental enquiry rules require that a copy of the inquiry report must be given to the delinquent, providing him the opportunity to make representation.
This must be done before an order of removal could be passed against him, even if there is a statutory rule saying that it shall not be necessary to give the member of the force any opportunity of making representation on the penalty proposed.
This is stated in Central Industrial Security Force Rules (1969), Rules 29A and 34(10)(ii), and the precedent of 1991(1) SCT 111 is relied upon.
