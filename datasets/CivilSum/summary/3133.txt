As per Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Promotion - Delhi University Act, Section 2(f), sub-sections 6 and 7 of Section 4 - Statute of Delhi University, Statutes 6(2), 8 and 37 - The Petitioners who were directly appointed as Professors in the Department of Education, by open selection for the projects under the centrally sponsored scheme - are considered employees of the University, since these posts were created by the University.
The inter-se seniority has to be counted from continuous length of service, and since the Petitioners have seniority over Respondent No. 2 as Professors, the appointment of Respondent No. 2 as Head of the Department based on alleged seniority is not valid.
