Constitution of India, Article 226 - Absorption - The recruitment of a post via absorption or other means should adhere to Rules - An appointment that violates the said Rules has no validity - The recommendation of an individual, who is not an appointing authority, such as the District Collector, to absorb someone into a vacant post of organizer which defies the prescribed procedure is legally null and void - The Administrative Tribunal has no jurisdiction to pass an order directing the absorption of the employee as an organizer - The Appointing Authority appointing the employee as Metron, lacking a vacancy for her in the capacity of Organizer pursuant to her willingness, does not entail a legal right for the employee to claim the post of Organizer - The impugned order of the Administrative Tribunal which directs the compliance of its earlier order to absorb the employee into the post of Organizer in another application filed by the employee is not sustainable.
[Paras 8 and 12]
