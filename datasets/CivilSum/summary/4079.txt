Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Equal Pay for Equal Work - The petitioners, Assistant Field Investigators, argued for pay parity with Field Investigators because they perform similar work and their duties are interchangeable.
The respondent's committee also recommended that Assistant Field Investigators be granted equal pay.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to equal pay with Field Investigators.
