Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961, Section 26(2)(b) - Punjab State Co- operative Supply and Marketing Federation Employees (Common Cadre) Service Rules, 1990, Rules 21, 80-B and 80-C - Government nominated member of the Board of Directors - Placed him to be appointed as Managing Director by Board - The state had the power to choose either the Managing Director or the Chairman - Subsequently, a Chairman was also appointed - As a result, the Managing Director could no longer function - His service was now at the disposal of the Board of Directors - Although the order was not bad in law, it was in accordance with Common Cadre Rules - The Chairman could not act as the Principal Executive Officer since he was neither a member of IAS nor a Joint Registrar or an Additional Registrar - Until a decision was made regarding the appointment, the Managing Director would continue to act.
