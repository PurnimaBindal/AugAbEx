Constitution of India, Article 311 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 13 - Suspension - Deemed suspension - An order of Appointing Authority shall deem a government servant detained in custody for more than 48 hours to have been suspended from the date of detention, and will remain suspended until further orders.
The Appointing Authority must consider the continuation of such deemed suspension, even if the revocation order was canceled.
If there is no evidence or averment that the guidelines and instructions issued by the Government have not been followed, it cannot be claimed that the suspension order or the order of cancellation of revocation of suspension has any flaws.
[Paras 6, 9 and 10]
