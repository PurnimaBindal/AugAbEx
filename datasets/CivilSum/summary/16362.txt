Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 - Promotion - Reversion - Ground that petitioner lacks experience in field of administration as per rules - Petitioner served as Personal Secretary to Chairman - Experience of similarly situated individuals treated as in the field of administration - Service as Stenographers considered as in the field of administration for promotion as Section Officers - Court ruled that the respondent cannot apply different standards of service jurisprudence to the same description of field of experience - The service of the petitioner must be considered as service in the field of administration - The order has been quashed.
