The General Insurance (Employees) Pension Scheme, 1995 and the General Insurance Employees Special Voluntary Retirement Scheme, 2004 along with Clauses 2, 14, 29, 30 and the General Insurance Business (Nationalisation) Act, 1972, Section 9, 17A were examined.
The scheme required 10 years of service and the age of 40 for eligibility for pension claim.
However, officials seeking voluntary retirement were excluded from the administrative instructions stipulating 20 years of qualifying service.
The rejection of pension claim due to not fulfilling 20 years of service was deemed illegal and not tenable in law.
