Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Admissions - District wise selection - District-wise selection may lead to discrimination - Candidates may be rejected from other districts despite being better qualified, while less qualified candidates from the same district may be selected - A notification limiting eligibility to residents of a specific district is unconstitutional and goes against the Supreme Court's decision.
