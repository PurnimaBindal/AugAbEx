According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India, Discipline and Appeal Procedure (Sch.
A) Clauses 10, 22 and 45, a conductor was charged for assaulting a driver and being under the influence of liquor.
However, misconduct as regards the assault was not proved, and the corporation failed to prove that the conductor was under the influence of alcohol.
Consequently, the punishment of withholding 50% back wages was considered appropriate.
[Paras 8 to 12]
