Constitution of India, Article 226 - Abolition of Post - Mala fides - The petitioner was employed as an Accounts Executive on 16.7.93, but his job was ended as a result of the post's termination - According to the petitioner, the position was only abolished to cancel his employment - It was alleged that the 3rd respondent was working in bad faith - Both the financial advisor and the petitioner had comparable qualifications - The reasoning behind the third respondent's decision to abolish the post was not disclosed - The elimination of the post was deemed to have been carried out in bad faith.
