Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 309 - U.P.
Recruitment of Dependents of Government Servants Dying in Harness Rules, 1974, Rules 5 - Termination of services - Compassionate appointment - The petitioner was appointed on compassionate grounds after his father passed away, according to policy and rules.
To terminate his employment without allowing him a hearing was deemed illegal and the termination order was quashed.
