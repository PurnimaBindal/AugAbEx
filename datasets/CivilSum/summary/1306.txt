The Contract Labour (Regularization and Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 110, and Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226, deal with the regularization of contract workers.
The petitioners seek directions for their absorption on a regular basis, claiming that they have been working for over three years through a contractor on work of permanent and perennial nature.
However, the high court cannot delve into disputed questions of fact in exercise of its extraordinary jurisdiction, and these questions should be resolved through raising industrial dispute and seeking reference to labour court.
[Paras 2, 6, and 10]

Under Section 110 of the Contract Labour (Regularization and Abolition) Act, 1970, and Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226, the petitioners also seek directions to the appropriate government for issuance of a notification.
The court gives directions to the government that if the petitioners seek this remedy and the government does not initiate this exercise, action shall be taken as per law contemplated under Section 10(2) of the Act.
[Paras 7 and 11]

The petitioners seek interim protection to continue as contract workers when the matter is referred to the appropriate government under Section 10 of the Act.
However, the court explains that normally it does not pass interim orders in such cases, and an exceptional case has not been made out.
Therefore, interim protection cannot be granted.
[Paras 8 and 12]
