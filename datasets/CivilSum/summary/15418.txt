According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, a permanent injunction can be granted to restrict the respondent from cutting and removing Kikar trees.
If the trees have been planted on someone else's land, that person becomes the owner of the trees and the planter has no right to remove them.
The court upheld the decision to grant only 15% of the value of the trees to the person who planted and maintained them.
