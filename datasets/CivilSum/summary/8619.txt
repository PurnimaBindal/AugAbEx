Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.38 - Departmental enquiry - Sanction of Departmental enquiry - The petitioner was dismissed from service due to misconduct.
However, the sanction for the initiation of departmental enquiry was granted by the District Magistrate without applying the mind or showing any special reason.
Furthermore, the respondents were unable to provide any reason for the grant of sanction for a departmental enquiry.
As a result, the petition was awarded with all the related reliefs.
[Paras 2 and 8]
