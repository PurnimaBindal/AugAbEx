The Punjab Government Instructions dt. 13.2.1995 for reimbursement of medical expenses under the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 215 have been declined based on subsequent instructions dt. 31121997, which provides only Fixed Medical Allowance, instead of outdoor treatment.
Even though the LPA is still pending against the single judge's judgment that granted reimbursement under similar circumstances and two other single bench judgments, the petitioner cannot be declined relief on the basis of mere pendency of the LPA They must be directed to reimburse the medical bills.
[Para 7]

The Punjab Government Instructions dt. 13.2.1995 for reimbursement of medical expenses are held to be discriminatory towards outdoor patients as compared to indoor patients and against the principle of equality under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
The instructions dt. 31.12.97 form an artificially carved out classification between equals and cannot be sustained as it does not satisfy the ingredients of "Reasonable Classification."
