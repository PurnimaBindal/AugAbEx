Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 9-A and 25 - Retrenchment - The competent authority allowed retrenchment of 19 workers.
Instead of referring the matter to an authority, the remedy of moving the High Court under Article 226 of the Constitution was chosen.
The appellant raised questions about the non-compliance with the mandatory provisions of Section 9-A of the Act.
After reviewing the matter, the contention was rejected.
Therefore, the appellant cannot now claim that the High Court should not have examined the merits of the said contention.
