According to Punjab Police Rule, 1934, Rule 162, members of the police force must abide by the rules and orders issued to them.
Taking alcohol while on duty and remaining absent constitutes the gravest misconduct, leading to dismissal under Rule 162.
When the competent authority is considering the punishment, the record of service is taken into account.
The punishing authority cannot be presumed to be not conscious of the length of service of the delinquent when awarding the punishment.
[Paras 6, 7 and 8]
As per Article 14 of the Constitution of India, during disciplinary proceedings, the past conduct, record of service and punishments of a petitioner found guilty of repeated misconduct can be taken into consideration.
The petitioner cannot claim that his previous record of service has been taken into consideration in violation of principles of natural justice as he was given the opportunity of cross-examination.
[Para 12]
When a punishment order is passed, and the appeal is dismissed, a writ petition challenging the punishment order filed after five years is liable to be dismissed on the ground of delay and laches.
[Para 14]
