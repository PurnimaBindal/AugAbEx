Jammu and Kashmir Constitution, Section 126(2) - Indian Constitution, Article 311(2) - Dismissal - Termination of services - Reinstatement - The petitioner's services were terminated due to absence from duty, but there was no inquiry held and the order of removal was retrospective.
The petitioner's past record was taken into account without his consent, which is not acceptable.
Unless, under the given circumstances, holding an inquiry is not possible, it cannot be dispensed with.
Mere absence from duty would not be sufficient ground to invoke such provision, and therefore, the order for reinstatement was given.
[Paras 12 to 15]
