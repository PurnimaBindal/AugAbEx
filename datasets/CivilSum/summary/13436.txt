Indian Medical Council Act, Sections 13(3) and 25(1) - Eligibility Criteria/Registration - Petitioners were denied registration under Section 25(1) of the Act because they did not meet the eligibility criteria for admission to the MBBS course.
However, they were admitted to recognised institutions after successfully competing pre-medical examinations by those institutions in then USSR According to section 13(3) of the Indian Medical Act, qualifications granted by the Medical institutions outside India which are included in Part II of Third Schedule are recognised medical qualifications for the purpose of this Act.
Therefore, anyone who successfully fulfils the eligibility criteria and completes medical education from a recognised institution and thereafter completes the requisite internship is entitled to registration with Indian Medical Council.
The petitioner fulfils all eligibility criteria and hence is entitled to registration.
