Section 16 of the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 cannot be applied retrospectively - Benefits guaranteed under the unamended provisions are saved by the amendment - Those who received a 3 year "infancy period" exemption will continue to enjoy the same irrespective of the amendment.
[Paras 9 to 12] B.
According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the interpretation of statutes is crucial - The amendment of Section 16 in the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 is always prospective unless specifically or implicitly applied retrospectively - The amendment cannot take away vested rights unless the legislation explicitly states that it does - When the statute's language is clear, no interpretation is needed - The interpretation of the statute must uphold the legislative intention without violating the language used in the statute.
