Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Haryana Revenue Group 'B' Service Rules, 1988, Rule 9 - Selection - Age relaxation - Selection for B class Tehsildar's post - Age limit set at 40 years - If no eligible candidate available within the age group, relaxation of age up to 45 years is permitted.
In the case of further non-availability of candidates, age relaxation can be raised up to 48 and 50 years, respectively.
This is a legitimate relaxation made for valid reasons and not an arbitrary or unjust action.
