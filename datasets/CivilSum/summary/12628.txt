Haryana State Co-operative Staff Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1981, Rule 9.12 - Haryana Co-operative Societies Act, 1984, Section 31 - Managing Director of a Co-operative Society - The State Government has no power to remove the existing Managing Director to create a vacancy for someone else under Section 31 of the Act- only the prescribed rules can be used for such a removal - the removal order has been quashed - the petitioner should continue as Managing Director.
