Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 32 - Allotment of shops/stalls - Discretionary allotments - Misuse for public authority - Misfeasance in public office and misuse of power by a public officer is actionable in tort - Public functionary must use its power transparently for bona fide purposes only - Discretionary allotments of shops/stalls by the Minister of urban Development in violation of the allocation policy - Allotments made to minister's relations, personal staff, or friends - Arbitrary exercise of discretion through power misuse - Minister held liable for exemplary damages - Show cause notice issued to the minister regarding awarding of damages.
