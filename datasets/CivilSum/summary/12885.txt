Challenge to the withdrawal of regular pay scale for the post of Black Smith under Article 16 of the Constitution of India.
Petitioner was initially appointed as a Welder and, benefitting from the Singh Sancheti Committee's recommendation, is entitled to pay fixation and regularisation based on their performance in a particular trade.
The Welder and Blacksmith posts are in the same trade and cadre, and the Fixation Committee considered the petitioner's case for fixation/regularisation on the post of Black Smith based on the recommendation of their immediate superior.
The order granting fixation on the post of Black Smith cannot be quashed just because the appointment order initially referenced the respondent as casual labour.
[Paras 11, 12]
