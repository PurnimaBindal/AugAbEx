Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Andhra Pradesh Dairy Development Co-operative Federation Limited (Conduct, Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1983, Rule 35 - Punishment - Discrimination in awarding punishment - The punishment given for failure to prevent spoilage of cattle feed was not applicable to the petitioner since the feed was beyond his control - Other similar lapses had been overlooked in the past due to unforeseen circumstances and the feed being beyond human control - The appellate authority did not apply its mind as mandated under Rule 35 of the Discipline and Appeal Rules - The punishment has been deemed illegal and quashed.
