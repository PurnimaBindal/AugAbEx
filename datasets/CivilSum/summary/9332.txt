According to Rules 37(2) and 40 of the General Insurance (Conduct, Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1975, insurance employees may have their services terminated for issuing ante-dated insurance cover.
If the party holding the cover files an insurance claim in civil court and the court determines that the cover was not ante-dated, the authority is required to reinstate the employee.
In this case, the petitioner was declared to be continuing in their service with all consequential relief by way of arrears of salary etc. [Para 5]
