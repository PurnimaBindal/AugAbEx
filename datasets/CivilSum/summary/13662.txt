Punjab Education Board (Penalties for Misconduct and Use of Unfair Means in the Matriculation and Higher Secondary Examinations) Regulations, 1979, Regulations 9(a), 10, 11(i) and 19 - Unfair means/Natural justice - Simply following natural justice is not sufficient to ensure the fairness of findings arrived at by an unfair means committee.
