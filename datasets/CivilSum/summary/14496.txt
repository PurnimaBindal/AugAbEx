Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Pay/Pay scale - The petitioner, who is a Draughtsman in GREF, claims that his unrevised pay scale of Rs. 205-280 prior to Jan 1, 1973 should be revised to Rs. 425-700 w.e.f Jan 1, 1973, citing a decision by the Ministry of Finance dated Sept 11, 1987 r/w Ministry of Defence Order dated August 4, 1988.
This decision has already been implemented by the Ministry of Defence in the case of Draughtsmen of the MES and by the Govt. of India in the Ministry of Finance for similarly situated persons in the CPWD, as directed by the Award of the Board of Arbitration.
Failure to implement this decision would lead to discrimination against the petitioner.
The respondents have been directed to fix the petitioner's pay in accordance with the decisions of the Govt. of India.
