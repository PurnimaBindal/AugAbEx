According to Section 2(15-B) of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, if an employee sustains an injury and a medical board assesses his disability at 40%, and he is unable to perform the duties that he was previously capable of doing, then he is entitled to the benefit of permanent total disablement.
It is important to note that whether or not the employee suffered permanent total disablement is a matter of fact.
