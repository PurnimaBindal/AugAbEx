According to Army Act, 1959, Section 20(3) & (7) and Army Rules, Rules 17 & 182, the competent authority cannot act outside the statutory rule merely based on the seriousness of allegations.
The procedure prescribed under Rule 17 for dispensing with the show cause notice and enquiry must be followed, or else the dismissal order cannot be sustained.
In this case, the procedure was not followed, and the competent authority did not provide any reasons for dispensing with the requirement of a show cause notice.
Additionally, no report was sent to the Central Govt. in terms of the proviso to rule 17, which constitutes a flagrant violation of the mandatory provisions of the Rules.
