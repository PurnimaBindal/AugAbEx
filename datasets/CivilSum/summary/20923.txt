Constitution of India, Article 136 - Fisheries (Subordinate) Service Recruitment Rules, 1979 - Recruitment - Locus Standi - Posts of Junior Research Assistant and Surveyor - Dispute relating to advertisement for filling up three posts by direct recruitment - 75% of posts to be filled up by promotion and 25% by direct recruitment - The appellant, who was serving as a Supervisor in a lower grade, applied for the same posts but was unsuccessful in getting appointed - He challenged the appointment of others on the ground of violation of quota rules - However, since he himself applied for the same posts, he cannot contend that there had been any violation of quota rules - Additionally, the appellant did not possess the requisite qualification for direct recruitment, and thus, has no locus standi to assail the appointment made in favour of others - No interference.
