According to Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, (XIV of 1947), termination of services was discussed.
As stated in Section 2(oo)(bb), this applies only to employment for a specific period of time.
The respondent worked as a "security supervisor" for over ten years, so Section 2(oo) does not apply.
Therefore, the respondent is entitled to remain in service until he reaches 60 years of age.
The presumption of non-applicability of Section 2(oo)(bb) is incorrect in situations where work is continuous as there is no evidence that the work for which a worker was employed had ended.
