The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Allotment of Govt. Accommodation - The Government must establish specific regulations, standards, and clear instructions for the allocation of Govt. accommodations in order to avoid the misuse of discretion by Government authorities - The principle of fairness must be included in the exercise of such powers by relevant authorities - Any exception to the rule must be based on a valid reason - The Government has been instructed to develop regulations for the allocation of Government accommodations at the District level.
[Paras 5 & 6]
