Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 14 - Compulsory retirement - The petitioner filed a case challenging the order of compulsory retirement.
The Trial Court discovered that the departmental enquiry was conducted in violation of natural justice principles and the case had no evidence.
Further, the Trial Court overruled the compulsory retirement order and directed full back wages.
The State Government did not submit the enquiry file in evidence before the Trial Court.
With no merit in the appeal, it was dismissed.
