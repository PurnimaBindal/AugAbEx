Haryana Excise and Taxation Inspector (State Service Class III) terminated after failing departmental examination - Ordered to be quashed on grounds of discrimination under Articles 14, 16, and 311 of the Indian Constitution - Petitioner passed remaining exam papers during litigation - Entitled to be considered in service before date of termination - Granted all consequential benefits.
[Paras 7, 8, and 9]
Haryana Excise and Taxation Inspector (State Service Class III) Service Rules, 1969, Rules 12 and 14 provide six chances for employees to appear in departmental examination within three years - Petitioner denied a chance to appear after the three-year time limit without reason - As other employees were granted time exceeding the Rules' limit, denying the petitioner was discriminatory under Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution - Petitioner entitled to appear in the exam after the expiry of three years.
