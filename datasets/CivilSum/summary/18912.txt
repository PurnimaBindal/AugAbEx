Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Disability Pension - The petitioner's disability, assessed as 20% by the Medical Board, resulted in the award of disability pension.
However, without consulting the petitioner, the Chief Controller of Defence Accounts (Pensions), Allahabad, unilaterally lowered the disability to less than 20% and ceased to pay out the pension.
This was found to be unjustified - the Accounts Branch cannot simply disregard the Medical Board's assessment of disability without referring the matter to a higher authority.
The court allowed the appeal and ordered the respondents to pay the petitioner's disability pension.
