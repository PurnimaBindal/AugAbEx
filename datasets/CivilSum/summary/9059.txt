West Bengal Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1971, Rule 7(3) - Criminal case - Deemed suspension - The suspension deemed for individuals involved in criminal cases is not automatically lifted by an acquittal or discharge - An order from the authority concerned is necessary for lifting the deemed suspension - Continuing such suspension is unjust as it can be left indefinitely to the departmental authority's whim and caprice.
