Constitution of India, Article 14 - Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948, Section 10(1)(e) - Removal of Member of Electricity Board - The Government must provide charges against a member of the Board seeking to be removed, allowing them an opportunity to defend themselves and cross-examine prosecution witnesses.
The Government must supply the entire material relied upon against the member with the show cause notice, supply a copy of the enquiry report, and allow the member to represent against it.
Principles of natural justice are inbuilt in every procedure for administrative action, even if not specifically prescribed.
If action is taken in violation of these principles, it cannot be sustained.
[Paras 11 to 15]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948, Section 10(1)(e) - Removal from service - Natural justice - Where merely an opportunity of hearing has been denied, the court can ask the person what prejudice has been caused to them on that account.
However, this theory cannot be applied to a case where action has been taken in total disregard to the fundamental rule of natural justice.
[Para 16]
