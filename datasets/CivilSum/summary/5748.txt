Selection and appointment for the posts of Registrars, including that of Radio Diagnosis and Imaging, are governed by Articles 226/227 of the Indian Constitution.
The Court has no power to direct appointment after a candidate is selected, as selection does not confer the right to appointment.
The advertisement for selection is an offer, and selection made is only a recommendation.
The State may choose not to make appointments based on selection, but it is their duty to try and get the best available talent, especially in medical colleges.
The State may re-advertise a post after selection if they feel that they have not attracted the best talent.
No case has been made that someone equally qualified was appointed from the selection list.
Petition dismissed.
(Referenced AIR 1984 SC 1850) [Para 5]  B Applicants for selection and appointment do not acquire any right, even after selection.
