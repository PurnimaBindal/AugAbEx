Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admissions - Reservation in admissions - Passing of PMET in general category - Candidates who pass the entrance test in general category may still claim the benefit of reservation - The passing of entrance test is merely a requirement for eligibility and consideration in comparative merit - Admission entitlement for candidates to reserved seats to be determined during counselling - Therefore, a reserved category candidate should not be denied their reserved seat just because they indicated their category as general on the entrance test form.
