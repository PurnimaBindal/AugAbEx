Punjab Roadways (Ministerial) State Service Class III Rules, 1977, Rules 8, 9 and 22 - Merit cum seniority - The government cannot relax eligibility conditions for direct recruitment and give further promotion under Rule 22, as seen in the case of the petitioner appointed as an ad hoc Legal Assistant.
The High Court's conclusion that the petitioner cannot continue unless there is a regular recruitment is correct.
