Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination - Violation of provisions - Award of reinstatement - Labour Court denied backwages and continuity - Single Judge granted benefit of continuity in writ by workman - Petition and writ appeal by respondent against award was dismissed by High Court - Writ appeal against order of Single Judge was allowed and the entire award was set aside - Appellant failed to bring to notice previous dismissal of appeal - Challenge on grounds of res judicata - Division Bench already concluded the issue of reinstatement - Order of High Court set aside, and Single Judge's order affirmed.
