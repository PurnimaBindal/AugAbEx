Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservation - Change of option - Admissions - Admission to MBBS Course - Candidates who choose to compete in General Category cannot switch to reserved category after not making the general merit list - The petitioner was ignored for admission in the reserved category when the respondent's option was changed - The respondent's admission needs to be cancelled and the petitioner should be given admission against the last seat in the reserved category.
