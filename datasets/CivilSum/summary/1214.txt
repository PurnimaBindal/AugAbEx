Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference - Pleadings - Procedure - If the dispute between the workman and the management raised before the Labour Court has not been cited specifically and contested, it is assumed that the parties have joined the issue and submitted to the jurisdiction of the Tribunal on the same - Consequently, the award cannot be challenged later on the grounds of competency of the Tribunal or the reference.
[Para 10]  B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11A - Dismissal from service - Proportionality of punishment - Pressuring the management to get the attendance of workers marked is not a major misconduct warranting dismissal - Dismissal from service considered excessive and severe.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Words and Phrases - "Denial of employment" - Dismissal or Termination - "Denial of employment" is a generic term which includes a variety of categories such as "dismissal", "termination of services", "retrenchment", "discharge", "loss of lien", "removal from the rolls", "superannuation", etc. [Para 11]
