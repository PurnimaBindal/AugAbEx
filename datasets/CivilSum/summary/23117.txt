Constitution of India, Article 16 - Karnataka Administrative Services (Tehsildars) Recruitment (Special) Rules, 1975 - Selection and appointment of applicants - Only eligible applicants on the date of advertisement shall be considered for selection and appointment - The amended rules, if applied with retrospective effect, shall govern selection and appointment - Otherwise, prevailing rules on the date of advertisement shall regulate the same - Applicants do not have a vested right for selection/appointment.
[Paras 11 and 13]
