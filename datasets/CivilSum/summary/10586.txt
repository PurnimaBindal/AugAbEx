According to the Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 320 regarding appointments and merit lists, the petitioner ranked first in the merit list created by the Haryana Public Service Commission, making him eligible to be appointed first.
The Commission's role is only to select and arrange the merit of selected candidates in order, without the ability to make further selections or withhold names.
The government is responsible for making appointments strictly according to the merit list's order, and it is only the government's decision to leave vacancies unfilled, but only with valid reasons.
