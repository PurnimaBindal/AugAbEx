Explanation of Domicile - Domicile of choice is a concept in private international law that identifies a person, in cases with a foreign element, with a territory subject to a single system of law, which is viewed as his personal law.
Domicile of origin is attributed to each person at birth by operation of law, and it is not decided by the place of birth or the place of residence of the parents.
A person's domicile may be transmitted through generations.
The domicile of origin lasts until the person acquires a domicile of choice in another country.
The domicile of choice is a combination of residence and intention.
A person's residence should be combined with the intention to reside permanently or for an unlimited amount of time in a country.
It is immaterial for this purpose that the residence is for a short duration.
In a country like India, there is no State-wise domicile within the territory of India, and a citizen of India is domiciled in the whole of this country.
The concept of 'domicile' is, therefore, inapplicable to the construction of the relevant provisions, having no foreign element, i.e., having no contact with any system of law other than Indian.
