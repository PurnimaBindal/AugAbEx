The government equated the pay scales of Assistant Surgeons and PCMS-II Doctors.
However, they did not produce the letter equating the pay scales.
The Writ petition was dismissed for lack of equation orders.
The petitioners succeeded in tracing the order on the office file and wanted a review of the dismissal order of the Writ Petition.
The State claimed privilege, but it was overruled.
There is no privilege on orders passed by the Minister.
The review was allowed, and the petitioners are entitled to a pay scale equal to the PCMS-II Doctors.
[Paras 3 and 4]
The Evidence Act's Section 115 states that legally speaking, no privilege to the order passed by the Minister could be claimed.
The privilege could only be claimed regarding office notings to protect the Officers of the State from giving an independent opinion on matters involving the State.
