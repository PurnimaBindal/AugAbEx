Constitution of India, Article 226 - Re-organisation Act, Section 82 - Pensionary benefits - A petitioner who was appointed as Clerk in Bhakra Dam Administration was transferred to BBMB on re-organisation of the State of Punjab.
He continued to work in BBMB until the date of his retirement; therefore, employees of the Punjab Government who retired from BBMB will be eligible for pensionary benefits based on their last pay from BBMB.
The decision in SLP No. 10098 of 1997 was referenced.
