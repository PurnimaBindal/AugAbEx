Constitution of India, Article 1 and 226 - Admission/Merit list - Combined merit list - The University of Lucknow must provide a merit list for each college from institutional candidates in that specific college, but a combined merit list must also be prepared for all colleges in the state for admissions to the Post Graduate Medical Entrance Examination.
The University is directed to advertise admission again and hold a combined test to prepare a combined merit list for all colleges in the state.
Merit cannot be sacrificed for convenience, and there can be no vested right in seeking admission in a particular college.
Rule (G) (ii) of the guidelines issued by the University is struck down.
All admitted candidates will receive specialties of their choice in all medical colleges, even if they have passed their MBBS course from medical colleges under other universities.
