According to Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, if a workman claims arrears of wages due to the non-granting of increments and the management cites orders passed against the workman to justify deductions made on account of stoppage of increments, until the orders of such stoppage, whether legal or void, are set aside within the period of limitation, the Labour Court cannot use Section 33-C(2) to grant arrears of wages for non-granting of increments.
Additionally, proceedings under this provision are of an execution nature, and therefore, the application cannot adjudicate the legality or illegality of orders of stoppage of increments/terminating.
