Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Termination - The third respondent was appointed as a Fitter on a temporary basis for two years, until December 31, 1984, and agreed to the terms and conditions of employment.
The appellant sent a warning letter to the third respondent on December 14, 1984, for assembling and addressing workers for trade union purpose, and the third respondent replied to the letter.
On December 28, 1984, a letter terminating his service was given, effective December 31, 1984, with no stigma on the third respondent.
The appellant was entitled to end his temporary employment after the two-year period, and the High Court was not justified in concluding that the services were terminated due to the third respondent's misconduct.
The High Court's order was set aside.
