Constitution of India, Articles 226, 245, 246 and 309 - Retirement - Extension in the age of retirement - The petitioner, who initially worked for the Govt. of India and later transferred to the State of Punjab, requested an extension in his retirement age citing amendments made in the service rule of Govt. of India - However, since the petitioner was absorbed by the State of Punjab, the relationship between him and the Govt. of India no longer exists - Therefore, the petitioner cannot claim extension of retirement age based on amendments in the service rules of Central Govt. employees.
