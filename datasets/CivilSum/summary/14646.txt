In-service candidates admitted to MD courses are entitled to full salary for the duration of the course, even if they fall short of a few days in rural service.
In one case, a petitioner was refused admission but was later allowed provisional admission and completed the course during a writ petition.
Despite a compassionate view being taken, the petitioner is entitled to full salary as an in-service candidate for the period of the course.
[Paras 5-9]
