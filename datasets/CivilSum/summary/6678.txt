Compassionate Appointment - Family Pension - Delay - The petitioner's claim for compassionate appointment was rejected due to the family's sufficient means for subsistence and the belated submission of the application.
However, the family pension cannot be taken into account as a determining factor in the family's financial position for the purpose of compassionate appointment as the pension is earned by the deceased employee while in service.
The rejection of the claim is unreasonable as no member of a deceased employee's family would ever be eligible for compassionate appointment if the family pension was the criterion.
The application was filed immediately after the petitioner attained majority.
