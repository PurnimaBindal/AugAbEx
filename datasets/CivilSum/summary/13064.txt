The University declined the petitioner from sitting in the 1st year MBBS examination, claiming that she hadn't undergone the necessary training from that college.
However, following a court order, she was allowed to sit in the examination, and the results were declared.
Subsequently, the college's affiliation was transferred to another university, and the petitioner was allowed to take 2nd, 3rd, and 4th-year examinations, with her results declared and her internship completed.
As the University's refusal to issue her the marksheet for Ist year examination was unjustified, it was directed to hand over the marksheet to the petitioner.
