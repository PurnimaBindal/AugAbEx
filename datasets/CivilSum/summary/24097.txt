Constitution of India, Article 310 - UP Fundamental Rules, FR 56 - Compulsory retirement - Adverse entries - State Govt. has the power to retire any govt. servant on completion of 50 years of age or qualifying serving - Old and stale adverse entries can still be considered - The only challenge the employee can raise is on the grounds of arbitrariness and mala fide exercise of power - Adverse entries regarding integrity and entire service record can be taken into consideration for compulsory retirement, with emphasis on the later entries in the character roll - A 10-year-old entry regarding integrity is not enough to set aside the retirement order.
[Paras 15 and 16]
