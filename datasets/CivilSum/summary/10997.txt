Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Transfer - Scope of the Court's Jurisdiction - Mala fide transfer - Mid-term transfer - An employee in a transferable cadre post has no right to remain in a specific place - The court will not intervene unless the transfer is shown to be mala-fide or motivated by improper reasons - The policy on transfers is designed to guide the authorities in regulating transfers - Imparting immunity against transfers would restrict the administration's right to manage in compliance with administrative demands - The transfer order can only be challenged if it is determined to have been issued for an improper purpose or to seek revenge - However, the employer can be directed to follow its policy, and only a competent authority may issue an order - The employer must consider grounds of hardship or family circumstances, and if feasible, provide accommodation at the location of the current posting - The authority has been instructed to reassess the situation based on the policy and petitioner's request and withhold the order before making a fresh ruling.
