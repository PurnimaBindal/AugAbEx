Employees' Family Pension Scheme, 1971, Paras 18 and 19 - Family pension - When reinstated in service with continuity of service and without backwages, employees are considered to be in continuous service without any interruption.
As a result, they cannot be denied the benefit of family pension solely because they previously withdrew benefits and had less than 10 years of service.
Additionally, employees who were members of the scheme prior to their termination will remain members after being reinstated.
