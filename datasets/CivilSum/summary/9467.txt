When considering an Army officer for promotion to higher ranks, a Selection Board must be consulted, and if the Board determines that the officer is not suitable for promotion, a court cannot overrule that decision.
The Board's assessment can only be challenged if there is evidence of bias or irregularities.
[Para 10] B.
If an individual is accused of mala fide behavior in relation to an Army officer's promotion, that person must be named in the proceedings; otherwise, accusations of wrongdoing will not be considered.
