Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection and appointment - Validity of the select list - The select list remains valid only until either the advertised vacancies are filled or one year, whichever comes first, unless otherwise specified - No appointments can be made after the panel's life has expired - Note that selection does not automatically translate to the legal right to be hired.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Postal delay - Selection and appointment - Delay in postal department - Even if an appointment letter was sent by ordinary post, a legal presumption of delivery arises - Postal delay, by itself, may not be considered grounds for a lenient view.
