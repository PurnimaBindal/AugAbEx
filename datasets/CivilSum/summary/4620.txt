Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Social Welfare (State Service Class III) Rules, 1973, Rule 9(1) - Promotion - Request to overturn promotion of respondent No3 to the position of Superintendent - Claim that the respondent did not have the required eight years of experience as an Assistant, therefore was ineligible for the promotion - The rules recognized two posts to be equivalent to the position of an Assistant, and there was no dispute that the other two posts held by the respondent fell under this category - Whether an occupant of an equivalent post could be considered for promotion even if the post was not explicitly mentioned in the rule - The court held that only those occupying the posts explicitly stated in the rules could be considered for promotion to the post of Officer Superintendent - The direction was issued to consider the petitioner's case for promotion to the post of Office Superintendent, a day before respondent No. 3 was promoted.
[Paras 3 and 4] SB Mathur v. Hon'ble the Chief Justice of Delhi High Court and others, 1988 (4) SLR, 505.
