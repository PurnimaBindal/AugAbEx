Sections 7, 28, 32, and 59 of the Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971 and Section 2(k) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 are relevant in determining the scope of the Maharashtra Act.
Complaints under the Maharashtra Act are only maintainable if the complainant has a locus standi and has an established relationship with the employer.
In the case of a dispute regarding the relationship, the Labour Courts under the Maharashtra Act have jurisdiction to adjudicate on the question of relationship.
Workmen must raise an industrial dispute under the ID Act.
The fulfilment of conditions for maintaining a complaint under the Maharashtra Act is valid and justified due to the limited object and purpose of the Act.
The scope of Section 2(k) of the ID Act is wide and must be taken into consideration with the Contract Labour Act and Maharashtra Act.
