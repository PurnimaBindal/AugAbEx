Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - An employee was transferred by a Superintending Engineer from one circle to another circle.
A suit filed by the employee was dismissed by a trial court.
The District Judge allowed the employee's appeal, stating that no service rules allowed the S.E.
to transfer an employee to another circle.
The State filed a Regular Second Appeal with no stay of action.
The employee remained working in the same circle for 12 years until the appeal was ultimately dismissed as infructuous.
