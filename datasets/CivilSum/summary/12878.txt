Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Workman status during pending proceedings - If a dismissal order is pending approval before the tribunal and the employer is declared as a Relief Undertaking, the workman cannot be left without remedy - The dismissal order should be deemed inoperative until the conclusion of the proceedings - Therefore, a writ petition is permissible.
[Paras 8 to 11]
