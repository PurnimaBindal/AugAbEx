Railway Manual, Rule 2505 - Casual Labourer - Termination of service - The Appellants claimed that the respondent had been absent for more than the prescribed period, and therefore his service had been terminated - Respondent produced no evidence to support his case or rebut that he had been engaged for project work - The High Court misinterpreted Appellant's reply and was mistaken in interfering with the findings of the Tribunal that the respondent had abandoned his position - The appeal was allowed.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-B and F - Casual Labourer - Termination of service - The Respondent had completed 240 days of work and contended that Section 25-F should have been applied - However, as the initial burden to establish continuous work of 240 days was on the Respondent and this had not been discharged, the Single Judge could not have made such a finding.
