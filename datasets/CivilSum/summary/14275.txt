Constitution of India, Article 226 - Maharashtra Civil Service (Pension) Rules 1982, Rule 67 - Pension - The petitioner was permanently absorbed into Maharashtra Industrial Development Corporation service on 19572 and was paid gratuity and lumpsum instead of pension according to his option on the date of his superannuation.
It has been held that the petitioner is eligible for the restoration of one-third of his pension from May 1987, which is 15 years after his absorption into the Maharashtra Industrial Development Corporation.
