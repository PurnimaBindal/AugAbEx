Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F - Abandonment of job - Leave - Disablement - A driver workman sustained disabling injuries and was bedridden.
As such, the employer cannot consider it as abandonment of job.
Additional compensation, notice, or enquiry must be given before the workman's removal.
The workman was awarded 50% back wages and reinstatement, which should not be contested.
However, if the employer offers an alternative job, the workman may accept it since his services cannot be terminated without due process of law.
