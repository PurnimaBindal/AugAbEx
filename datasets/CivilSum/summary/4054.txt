Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab National Bank Officers Employees (D&A) Regulations, 1997, Regulation 6 - Departmental enquiry - Enquiry proceedings must be conducted fairly and in accordance with law - Enquiry officer's conclusion that the charge is not proved by evidence without assigning any reason is not sufficient - In addition, the defence of the petitioner must be taken into consideration and any guidelines relied upon must be placed on records - Thus, the enquiry officer needs to reconsider the matter.
