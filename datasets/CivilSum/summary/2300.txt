Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 & 39(d) - Equal pay for equal work - State Government's decision to prescribe a higher pay scale for employees of Civil Secretariat Vidhan Sabha, Raj Bhawan and offices of Financial Commissioners is based on the qualitative difference in the nature of functions and duties performed by such employees as compared to those employed in other offices like Directorate of Public Instructions - These offices are involved in laying down policies that are required to be executed by offices falling in the second category - Employees of Civil Secretariat shoulder a higher degree of responsibility than those employed in the office of the Director of Public Instructions, etc. - Thus, the classification made between employees employed in Civil Secretariat etc and other offices is reasonable and does not violate the doctrine of equality - Moreover, the decision of the State Government is based on the recommendations of Pay Commissions.
[Paras 22, 23 & 24]
