Under Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Chapter IV, Rule 4.2(1)(b), the qualifying service for government employee pensions does not include service rendered in Zila Parishads, as Zila Parishad employees are not considered government employees.
However, even if an employee was regularised in government employment at a later date after taking over from Zila Parishad, their regular service starts from the date of regularisation.
In this specific case, the petitioner was regularised by Zila Parishad on 22.6.1977, and then again regularised in government service on 1581972.
Despite the lack of terms and service records provided by the management, an adverse inference will be made against them, and the petitioner should receive minimum pension in the interest of justice.
