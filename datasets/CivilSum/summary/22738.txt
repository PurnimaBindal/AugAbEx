Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 - Compulsory retirement - Premature Retirement - Adverse entries - Promotion - Uncommunicated adverse entries in confidential record of service - The competent authority holds the absolute right to retire any government employee upon completing the required service for that purpose, however this decision must be made in the interest of the public.
Any uncommunicated adverse entry prior to promotion or crossing the efficiency bar can be taken into consideration for the overall performance evaluation of the employee during their entire tenure of service.
The entire record of their service includes any uncommunicated adverse entries.
[Paras 4 and 9]
