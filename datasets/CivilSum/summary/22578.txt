Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - Companies Act, Sections 617 & 619 - Instrumentality or agency of State - A company established, funded, and controlled by the Government, with over 97% Govt. share capital financed by Govt. institutions discharging duties related to public functions of the State, and under the control and supervision of State Machinery, qualifies as an instrumentality or agency of the State - Although the physical form of the company may be a cover for the Government, the Court can still pierce the corporate veil of the entity to reach this conclusion - However, this does not negate its independent entity status distinct from the State or make it an agent of the State for all purposes, including binding said instrumentalities for all acts, liabilities, and obligations of the State Government.
