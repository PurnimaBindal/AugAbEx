Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Pension Regulations for the Army 1961, Regulation 173 - Disability pension - Petitioner was discharged from military service with 30% disability due to personality disorder.
His claim for disability pension was rejected on the grounds that it was not caused by military service, as no note was recorded at the time of his entry into service suggesting he had such a disability.
However, it was presumed that he suffered the disease on account of his military service and the petitioner was ultimately granted disability pension.
