Letters Patent Appeal, Clause 10 - Punjab Service of Engineers, Class I, PWD (B&R) Rules, 1960 - Promotion - Pre-requisite condition for promotion - The condition for promotion is eight years of service and passing the departmental examination which the petitioner satisfied as of 1981 - The Screening Committee rejected his case for promotion in 1989 due to a pending enquiry - However, the Screening Committee had ignored punishments awarded to other cases more than five years prior to their promotion - Therefore, any punishment awarded to the petitioner should not stop him from getting the promotion.
