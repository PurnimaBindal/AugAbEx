Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Fundamental Rule 56(b) - Interpretation of the term "industrial or work charged establishment" - Municipal Corporation of Delhi deemed to be an industry as its primary functions, including construction works, printing and publishing, and operating hospitals, are predominantly industrial in nature.
[Para 16] B Fundamental Rule 56(b) - Definition of a "workman" - A monthly-rated manual worker who is predominantly engaged in manual labor in an industrial or work charged establishment, and skilled or otherwise.
[Para 17] C. Constitution of India, Article 311 - Fundamental Rule 56(b) - Definition of an "artist" - The retirement age of 60 does not apply to a skilled Cameraman who is regarded as an artist due to the artistic skill and imagination required in their work defying the mere mechanical operation of a camera - Therefore, they do not fall under the category of artisans or workmen as defined in FR 56(B), and their retirement at the age of 58 is justified.
