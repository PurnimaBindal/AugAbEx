Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Examination Bye-laws, Clauses 74 and 101 - Education - Examination - The petitioners, who were initially placed under compartment in the matriculation examination but later declared successful in both the examinations of 11th class, were denied examination forms for the final examination of 12th class on grounds of certain instructions.
The Court held that such instructions created an unreasonable restriction on the right of students to take the final exam for which they are eligible, and were contrary to the bye-laws, which clearly permit taking of examination and admission to next course.
Consequently, the Court set aside the instructions and allowed the petitioners to take the exam of 12th class.
[Paras 14, 20 and 22]
