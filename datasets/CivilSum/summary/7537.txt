According to the Punjab Civil Services Rules (as applicable to Haryana) Vol. 1, Rule 44, a promotional post cannot have the same pay-scale as the feeder cadre post.
In the case of Personal Assistant, which was a promotional post from the feeder cadre of Senior Stenographer, the pay scales were replaced as per the recommendations of the 4th Pay Commission.
The Delhi Development Authority (DDA) had uniformly fixed the date for the replacement pay scale for both categories as 1196.
However, the date fixed by DDA was altered by judicial verdict due to which Senior Stenographers got the replacement pay scale w.e.f.
1186 but the effective date for the replacement scale remained as 1196 for Personal Assistants.
This led to an anomalous situation where the pay scale of Senior Stenographer and Personal Assistant remained the same for ten years.
The DDA was directed to grant the replacement pay-scale to Personal Assistants w.e.f.
1186.
[Paras 12 to 16]
