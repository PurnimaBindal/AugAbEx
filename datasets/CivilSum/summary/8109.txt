Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Sections 25F, 25G and 25H -Daily Wager - Allegation of Non-Compliance - 240 days - The petitioner argues that the respondents violated provisions of Section 25G and 26H of the Act and that junior workers were retained after Appellant's removal.
After reviewing the relevant record and evidence, the Labour Court concluded that the petitioner had not completed 240 days of work in a calendar year and, therefore, compliance with Section 25-F was not necessary.
The appellant could not demonstrate a violation of Sections 25G and 25-H or the retention of juniors, and as a result, no interference is warranted.
