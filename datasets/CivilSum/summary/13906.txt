Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 3-A and 4(b) - Enhancement of compensation - After a workman who was a driver by profession met with an accident, and had his right arm amputated above the elbow, it was determined that he suffered 100% disability due to the total loss of earning capacity.
The compensation was then increased accordingly.
[Para 6]

Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4(a) - Interest and Penalty - Following an accident that occurred on January 11, 1991, an award of compensation was announced on January 31, 1994.
The workman is entitled to a grant of 12% interest per annum from the date of the accident until the amount is deposited, and will also receive a penalty of up to 35%.
