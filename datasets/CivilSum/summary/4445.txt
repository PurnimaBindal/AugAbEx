As per the Constitution of India, Article 311, false caste certificates are subject to scrutiny.
Christian converts from Scheduled Caste to any other backward classes are categorized as 'other backward classes' in their caste certificate, according to the government's policy.
However, the petitioner had originally belonged to the 'Lohar' community included in the Scheduled Tribe but converted to Christianity.
The caste certificate did not specify his caste, and thus the order of removal was liable to be set aside.
