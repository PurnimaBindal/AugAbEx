Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 18(1), 18(3) - Equal pay for equal work - Eligibility - Settlement - Petitioners were placed in the pay scales according to their eligibility as per the settlement between the workers' union and the management, which did not entitle them to be fixed in the regular time scale unless the settlement conditions were met - The doctrine of equal pay for equal work cannot be applied - The settlement is binding between the parties.
