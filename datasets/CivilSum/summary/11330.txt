Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - State Electricity Board Service Regulations, Part II, Clause 40 - Transfer - The validity of transfer orders of employees to newly formed circles is based on the principles of uniform application.
The decision to continue employees on an ad hoc or temporary basis is a matter of administrative expediency.
Therefore, the transfer orders and allocation are deemed justified.
