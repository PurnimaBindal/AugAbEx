According to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(j) and Bharat Bhawan Nyas Adhiniyam, 1982, Section 2(a), Bharat Bhawan Nyas Trust, an institution promoting art and culture, is not considered an `industry'.
The trust is engaged in the promotion and preservation of artistic talent, which does not involve large-scale production requiring the cooperation of employers and employees.
As no goods or services are produced, and the acting is not done for the business of another, the members of the trust are not considered `workmen'.
