According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, promotions are made from Lecturers to PES Class II.
The petitioners, who were provisionally appointed as Headmasters from their posts of Lecturers, were denied promotion on the grounds that they were no longer in the cadre of Lecturers and were therefore not in the line of promotion.
However, the petitioners never severed their connections on the posts of Lecturers and as such, consideration for promotion to PES Class II cannot be denied.
In fact, they would be deemed to be promoted from the dates the promotions were made.
