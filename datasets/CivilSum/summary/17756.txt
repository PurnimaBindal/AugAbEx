Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I, Part I, Rule 4.4(b) - Pay - Discrimination - Appointment on the post of Vaidya (Ayurvedic Medical Officer) on temporary basis on recommendation of Employment Exchange - Selected by HSSS Board for regular appointment - The petitioner was not granted benefit of ad hoc service towards increments due to the reason that he was not in the government service, but the persons similarly situated have been granted such benefit by the government - Respondent's order to recover amount and withdraw ad hoc service benefit is held to be violative of Article 14 of the Constitution as the petitioner has been treated differently than others.
