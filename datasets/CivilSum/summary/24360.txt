The fact that the Calcutta High Court granted additional special pay of Rs. 50 per month to Assistant Registrars (Courts) on Appellate Side does not affect the Recording Officers (Courts) on Original Side since it is entirely a matter of assessment by the High Court as to equivalence of work done by the two categories, and such assessment must be left to the judgment of the employer, which is the High Court.
