Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection/Appointment /Selection list - Selecting does not guarantee the right to appointment - If appointing authority determines to scrap and remake a panel based on appropriate grounds , this is not unlawful unless proven to have violated the law or has been unjust in some way - Selection list cannot be kept active beyond six months - No out of turn appointments permitted - This is not an invalid decision.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection/Appointment / Selection list/Advertisement - After six months, selection panel may be scrapped - Number of posts under the state is restricted - Advertisements must be released periodically, allowing qualified persons to compete - If the panel is active for too long candidates may be denied the opportunity to compete.
[Para 8]
Constitution of India, Article 309 - Policy decision/Competent authority - Individuals such as the Vice Chancellor or Registrar, who are authorized to recruit clerks, are empowered to decide how many spots should be filled as well as the time frame for which a certain panel should stay active.
