Constitution of India, Article 226 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1964, Rule 3(1)(i) and (iii) - Disciplinary proceedings - A Postman stamp vendor facing disciplinary proceedings for misconduct has had his plea for deferring the proceedings until after the conclusion of criminal proceedings rejected by the Tribunal, who found no flaw in their previous orders.
The evidence presented in both proceedings differed, making the change proved on the basis of material evidence.
