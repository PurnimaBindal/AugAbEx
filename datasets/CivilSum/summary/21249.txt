Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 10 - Central Civil Services Rules (Pension Rules), 1972 - Provisional Pension - Retiral benefits - An employee who is placed under suspension and retires provisionally while disciplinary proceedings are ongoing is entitled to payment of provisional pension and release of their provident fund, but they must await the decision on the enquiry before the release of other retiral benefits.
