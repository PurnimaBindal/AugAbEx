Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission in professional courses - Reservation for Sports category - Merit and weightage - Academic merit must not be completely disregarded - Candidates seeking admission in professional courses on the basis of their sports achievements must meet a minimum academic standard - Those who fail to meet even this basic standard in academic/entrance test cannot claim admission solely based on sports achievements - Establishing such minimum standards as a part of admission policy at any stage does not amount to modifying the conditions after issuance of admission programme - The prescription of basic criteria for admission is not equivalent to any changes.
In Amandeep Sahota's case, a specific allegation had been made that the criteria was changed later to help certain individuals, but setting basic criteria does not equate to a change.
