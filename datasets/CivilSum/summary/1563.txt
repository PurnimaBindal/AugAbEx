Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Administrative Tribunal Act, 1985, Section 19 - Equal pay for equal work - Teachers - Service conditions - Lecturers working on contract basis in Government and Central Polytechnics, Chandigarh are entitled to receive full salary in the regular pay scale, as regular appointees, since they have been discharging similar duties - The mode and manner of their recruitment does not determine their right to be paid in a particular pay scale as per the doctrine of equal pay for equal work - Seniority, confirmation, and promotion are factors that may affect their conditions of service but not their right to receive full salary in a regular pay scale.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administrative Tribunal Act, 1985, Section 9 - The High Court can issue a writ of certiorari to correct the errors of jurisdiction committed by the Courts or Tribunals and to ensure the principles of natural justice and legality are followed - It cannot reopen the findings of facts by reappreciation of evidence unless the orders suffer from an error of law apparent on the face of the record or it refused to admit admissible and material evidence or had erroneously admitted inadmissible evidence which influenced the impugned finding.
