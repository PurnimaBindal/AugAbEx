Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Natural justice - Correction of mistake - Non-observance of principles of natural justice must cause prejudice before court can quash order; mechanical quashing of an order may lead to the restoration of another unlawful order - Correction of a mistake in the seniority list that does not cause any prejudice to the employee may be carried out without issue - Even post-decisional hearing does not warrant a writ if the affected party cannot prove the outcome would have been different.
[Paras 10 to 15]
