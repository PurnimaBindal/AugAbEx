Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural justice/Departmental enquiry - The delinquent was given several opportunities, with evidence being struck off and arguments being heard ex parte after failing to appear multiple times.
It therefore cannot be claimed that proper opportunity was not provided during the proceedings.
[Para 6]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Departmental proceedings/Quantum of punishment - In a case concerning a petitioner and Executive Engineer in PSEB, departmental proceedings were conducted resulting in a punishment of reduction in rank without reference to Regulation 26 related to quantum of punishment.
In the peculiar facts and circumstances of the case, the punishment was modified to stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect.
[Paras 7 and 8]
