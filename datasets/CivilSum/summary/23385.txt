Constitution of India, Article 14 - Appointment - Eligibility - The candidate must hold a Law degree on the last date, i.e., 2081988, to apply.
Although the respondent's law examination results were declared in October 1988, she was not eligible to apply.
The High Court directed the PSC to interview the respondent, but the PSC was right in refusing to call her for the interview.
The approach of the High Court could not be justified on any rule or practice or equitable considerations.
Therefore, the appeal is allowed.
