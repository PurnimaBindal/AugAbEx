Kerala Services (Vigilance Tribunal) Rules, 1960, Rule 59 - Pension - Retirement of Government servant - Reduction in pension - It is within the Government's power to reduce pension if they feel that the employee's service was not satisfactory without any requirement for proposal from subordinate authority.

Kerala Services (Vigilance Tribunal) Rule 59, reduction in pension - The employee must be given a chance to explain before reducing their pension.
