Civil Procedure Code, Order 7 Rule 11 - Rejection of plaint - Suit for injunction restraining defendant employer from proceedings against the plaintiff to terminate his service and grant of compensation and other reliefs - The termination of services on allegations other than those in the earlier charge-sheet under challenge in the suit renders the suit without any cause of action for a successful outcome.
Hence, one cannot receive any meaningful relief without setting aside the termination order.
The court can return the plaint on such grounds at any stage as it is infructuous, but the liberty to refile the suit is available, challenging the subsequent orders claiming all the relief.
[Para 15]
