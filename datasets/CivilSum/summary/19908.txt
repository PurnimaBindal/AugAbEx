Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection/Merit List/Appointment/Requisition of posts/Reduction in the number of posts/selection List/Lapsed selection List - Various departments can request the SSS Board for requisition of posts and a common selection list.
If the demand for posts is reduced, the recommendations made may be sent back resulting in candidates with lower merit being appointed while better qualified ones are not.
To ensure appointment in order of merit, the Board must first ask the department to confirm the need at the time and recommend the required number of candidates according to merit.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Clerks/Qualifications/Instructions of the Haryana Govt. to the SSS Board - All departments have the same qualifications for the post of clerks and a common selection list.
