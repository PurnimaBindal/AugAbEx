Constitution of India, Article 14 - Special increments/Jurisdiction - The petitioner was granted the benefit of three advance increments during her appointment by the Board of Directors, but after 11 years, the Managing Director ordered the recovery of the amount paid to her on the basis of these advance increments when the petitioner had retired.
This order was without jurisdiction as the Managing Director could not revoke benefits granted by the Board of Directors.
The Directors ordered not to make any recovery from the petitioner based on Managing Director’s orders, and the orders were quashed.
