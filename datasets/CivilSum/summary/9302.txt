Kerala Govt. Presses Subordinate Service Special rules, 1959, Rule 31 - Seniority/Promotion/Provisional Promotion/Eligibility - Provisionally promoted juniors due to the absence of fully qualified senior personnel - Qualified seniors were able to pass the test until the occurrence of regular vacancies - Provisional promotion of juniors in the past is not relevant - All qualified on the date of regular vacancies must be considered for regular promotions based on seniority.
