Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Pension and gratuity - Disciplinary proceedings - The petitioner, an Executive Engineer, received two charge-sheets shortly before their retirement and half of their gratuity was withheld.
Despite being cleared by an Enquiry Officer, the competent authority did not act for two years.
As a result, the enquiry report has been accepted and all the retiral benefits of the petitioner will be released with 12% interest.
The petitioner was awarded Rs. 5,000/- as compensation for harassment, and the official at fault for the delay was directed to bear the liability of interest.
