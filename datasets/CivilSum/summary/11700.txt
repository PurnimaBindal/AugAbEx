Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Pension Regulations for Army, Regulation 173 - Assessment of disability for disability pension - Petitioner's claim for disability pension was denied on the basis that the disease "Schizophrenia" relates to bodily structure - However, since there were no signs of disease at the time of joining the Army Service, it was deemed improper - It was held that the petitioner is entitled to receive disability pension in accordance with the relevant rules.
