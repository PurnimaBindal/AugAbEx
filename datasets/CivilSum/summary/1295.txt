Army Rules, 1964, Rules 11(2) and 13(3) - Discharge from Army service on medical ground - Compulsory discharge as an unwilling personal - Medical advice suggesting not to depute personal to high altitudes - Personal requesting modification of transfer orders - Suo motu action taken by authority recommending discharge of unwilling personnel without notice - Discharge order violates natural justice principles - Order cannot be sustained - Discharge order noted but to take effect after six months - Cancellation request filed before effective date prevents implementation.
