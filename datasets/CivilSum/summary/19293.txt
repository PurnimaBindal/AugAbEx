The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311, along with the Osmania University Act, 1959, Section 2(m), dictate that a person must be declared a Professor, Reader, lecturer, or any person offering instruction in order to be classified as a Teacher.
Additionally, not every person who provides instruction can be considered a Teacher.
As per Section 2(m), the term Teacher must properly adhere to a specific description.
As such, a request to keep a person in service past the age of 55 cannot be granted if they do not satisfy the definition of a Teacher as outlined in the Act.
The respondent, regarded as an Instructor in manual carpentry, cannot be considered a Teacher and thus, cannot claim a superannuation age of 60 years.
