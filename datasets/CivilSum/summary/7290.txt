Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - U.P.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 6(2-A) - Disciplinary proceedings - Dismissal from service - Charge against employee that he misbehaved with his senior and threatened him to kill - Proportionality of punishment - Reinstatement awarded by Labour Court and punishment of non-payment of wages for period between date of dismissal till enforcement of award - Punishment deemed not to be arbitrary.
