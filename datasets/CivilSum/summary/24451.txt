Indian Police Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1955, Regulations 5(5) and 7(3) - Criminal proceedings - Definition of pendency of proceedings - The Regulations state clearly that proceedings are considered pending once the charge-sheet is presented to the court, not just when charges are framed- The inclusion of the officer's name in the list is provisional - An interpretation that requires charges to be framed for proceedings to be considered pending would be dangerous.
[Paras 9, 11, 15 and 16]  B Constitution of India, Articles 41 and 266 - Statutory interpretation - When a statute's language is clear, courts cannot assign a different meaning to it just because the provision is harsh or other meanings may be possible - Courts must avoid interpreting statutes in a way that undermines their intended object as set out by the Legislature.
