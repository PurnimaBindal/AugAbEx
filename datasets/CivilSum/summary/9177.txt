The parties representing the respondent are M Mridul Sr Advocate, along with RS Saluja and Suresh Kumbhat.
The case in question involves Section 2(g) of the Trade Union Act, 1936, and Section 2(k) of the Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, with regard to the jurisdiction of a civil court and a dispute between two fake unions and their ability to represent the interests of workmen.
It is determined that there is no industrial or trade dispute as defined under Section 2(g) of the Trade Union Act, 1936.
Therefore, a suit for declaration and mandatory injunction in this case would be triable by a civil court.
