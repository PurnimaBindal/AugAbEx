In WP No. 731 of 1996, the petitioner was dismissed on the charge of misconduct.
The Labour Court found the delinquent guilty, but the dismissal from service was considered an extreme reaction.
The court deemed the reinstatement of the petitioner with 50% back wages justified.
There was no evidence supporting the claim that the delinquent was selling crackers during leave.
The Labour Court's order was upheld, citing that there were no grounds for the charges and that the delinquent disobeyed instructions by not wearing a uniform.
