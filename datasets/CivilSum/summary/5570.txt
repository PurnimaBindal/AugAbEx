Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 - Res judicata - Services of workman terminated without notice, chargesheet or compensation - Referral of first case declined by Labour Court on the grounds that PWD is not an industry - Fresh referral of second case - Labour Court ruled that principle of res judicata does not apply - Second referral is allowed - Decision on a point of law which is incorrect will not be considered res judicata.
