The issue of whether a Government servant should also be considered a workman under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, as defined in Section 2(s), must be evaluated based on the facts of each particular case.
This question was not expressly raised before the High Court, and the Apex Court ultimately chose not to address it.
However, the Labor Court's decision was partially modified, with the back wages being reduced by 50%.
