Constitution of India, Articles 16, 31-A and 300-A - Family Pension - Transfer - If an employee has rendered the qualifying period of services in the previous department but is later transferred to a department where they are incapacitated from rendering service due to ill-health, their legal heirs are still entitled to the family pension - The transfer of an employee from one department to another does not disqualify them or their legal heirs from receiving pensionary benefits if they have completed the necessary qualifying period of service.
