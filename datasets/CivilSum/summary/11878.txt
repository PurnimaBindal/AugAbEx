Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Sealed cover procedure for promotions during disciplinary proceedings - The mere contemplation of proceedings is not enough to justify the use of the sealed cover procedure and deprive the employee of the benefits of the recommendations of the DPC.
The employee must be issued a charge-sheet.
The petitioner is entitled to the benefits from the date of the DPC recommendations.
[Paras 1-4]
