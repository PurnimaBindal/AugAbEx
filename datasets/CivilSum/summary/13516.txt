Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - West Bengal Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Reservation of Vacancies in Service and Posts) Act, 1976, Section 8 - Certificate of Scheduled Caste - Despite the petitioner's subsequent struggle to obtain a Scheduled Caste certificate, the certificate he submitted at the time of his entry into service was validly issued by the Metropolitan Magistrate Calcutta, and it cannot be deemed unreliable.
It would thus not be fair to terminate his services on the basis of a later report from the S.D.O.
