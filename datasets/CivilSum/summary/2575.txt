Canara Bank Officer Employees' (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976, Regulation 7 - Disciplinary authority must provide reasons in speaking order only if it disagrees with the enquiry officer's findings.
In such cases, the authority must record its own findings on each charge.
However, it is not required to do so if it agrees with the findings and simply confirms them.
[Paras 12 and 16]
The order of the disciplinary authority that violates the rules merges with the order of the appellate authority.
The appellate authority must provide elaborate reasons and findings on each charge and its order can be challenged.
However, the order of the disciplinary authority cannot be challenged on this ground.
[Para 17]
Orders of punishment based on the evidence on record cannot be challenged in the writ jurisdiction of the High Court by way of reappraisal of evidence under Article 226 of the Indian Constitution.
[Para 19]
