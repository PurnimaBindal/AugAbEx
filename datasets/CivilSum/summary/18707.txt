Constitution of India, Article 226 - Seniority - Calculation of - Petitioner requests seniority from date of initial appointment - Municipality takes over the Educational Institution where petitioner works - Respondents refuse promotion to Petitioner by calculating his seniority from date of takeover - In light of similar petition of employees of the same school being allowed - Respondents are directed to calculate seniority of petitioner from date of initial appointment and provide him with consequential relief.
