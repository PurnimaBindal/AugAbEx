Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(j) - Determining if an establishment is an 'industry' - A tribunal ruled that an establishment that runs a diagnostic laboratory, an X-Ray unit, and earns from donations and service charges is considered an industry.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(s) - Temple priest as a 'workman' - A pujari of a temple, who does not perform manual, skilled, unskilled, technical, supervisory, operational, or clerical work, cannot be classified as a workman.
[Para 7] C. Employees of Industry - Retirement age - Model Standing Orders - If a tribunal considers an establishment as an industry, then it must apply the Model Standing Orders, which may include mandating retirement at the age of 58 years after payment of dues.
