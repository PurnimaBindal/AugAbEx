Under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, the recruitment must be limited to the number of vacancies advertised for.
When additional vacancies arise after the panel is drawn, a fresh requisition must be sent.
Making appointments to subsequent vacancies out of an earlier panel would violate the Fundamental Right under Article 16.
Therefore, the appeal for the appointment of candidates for additional vacancies was dismissed.
