A, Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination of services - Anonymous complaints received 6 years after appointment that petitioner does not belong to Schedule Caste Community - Upon investigation, the District Magistrate found that the petitioner does not belong to the scheduled caste and terminated his employment for procuring employment with a false caste certificate - However, the petitioner was not given an opportunity to present evidence to prove the correctness of his caste certificate - Thus, the petitioner was entitled to a proper opportunity to explain himself and the order of termination has been quashed.

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Reinstatement - Back wages - No evidence of the petitioner being gainfully employed during the period of termination (10 years) - As compensation, the petitioner has been awarded 25% of total salary from the date of termination till reinstatement.
