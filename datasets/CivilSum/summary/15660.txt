Constitution of India, Article 311 - All India Services (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1969, Rule 3(3) - Suspension - Continuance of - The petitioner has been suspended for 2-1/2 years, which exceeds the 90-day outer limit set by the rule.
The extension order for the suspension period was not served, and no special circumstances have been communicated for not initiating disciplinary proceedings.
The competent authority did not review the need for the suspension even after the order of the Tribunal.
The suspension order cannot continue and needs to be revoked.
