The Army Act, Section 39(A) - Army Rules, 1954, Rules 22, 115 - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Unauthorized absence - Discharge/ Dismissal - Petitioner contends that plea of guilt was entered, but the plea of guilt was not entered by the accused or recorded as per Rule 115.
The plea of respondents is not sustainable under the circumstances and the SCM sentence is set aside.
[Para 13]
The Army Act, Section 39(A) - Army Rules 1954, Rules 22, 115 - Unauthorised absence - Discharge/ Dismissal - Sentence - Proportionality of - Petitioner left on leave sanctioned by immediate officer - As a young recruit of 20 years, it is held that the sentence is disproportionate due to the absence not being unauthorized or overstayed.
The petitioner should not have been deprived of his livelihood.
