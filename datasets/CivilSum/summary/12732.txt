Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 918(1)(c) and 163(1)(b) - Premature retirement - Stigmatic order - A pre-mature retirement order that is stigmatic in nature and passed without holding any enquiry cannot be sustained.
The order accusing the person that their services are no longer required due to a chequered service record has penal consequences.
Such an order passed without following the principles of natural justice and not in public interest is liable to be quashed.
