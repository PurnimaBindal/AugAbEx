Equality clause of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India protects seniority as a recognized civil right of employees.
Seniority holds an important role in promotions when it is based on seniority-cum-merit.
However, promotion on the basis of seniority alone is not an entitlement.
[Paras 9, 10 and 20].
B.
In accordance with Regulation 13(ii) of the Punjab State Education Board (Employee Service) Regulations, 1988, the requisite basic qualifications for promotion to the post of Deputy Director Punjabi Cell are that the employee should be Assistant Director who studied Punjabi up to BA The petitioner has the same qualifications as those required for being appointed as Assistant Director 20 years back.
As he is eligible and the senior-most employee, he must receive the promotion first.
The rule contemplates promotion on the basis of principles of seniority-cum-merit, and seniority will prevail subject to eligibility only.
[Paras 12 to 17, 20, 21 and 23].
