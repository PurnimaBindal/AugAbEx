Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Indian Army Rules, 1954, Rule 13 - Religious teacher (Granthi) working in Indian Army discharged for failure to obey Commanding Officer's orders to load Guru Granth Sahib, Mandir and CSD canteen in a vehicle and carry to exercise location - Petitioner argued that he only pointed out that Guru Granth Sahib should not be carried along with CSD canteen and that was keeping in view religious principles and respect of Guru Granth Sahib - Court of Inquiry vitiated; Commanding Officer assumed petitioner had failed to obey his command unfairly and unreasonably - No material on record to substantiate the charges against petitioner in the Show Cause Notice - View taken by respondents is perverse - Set aside - Petition allowed.
