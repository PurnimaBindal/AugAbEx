The Administrative Tribunals Act of 1985 states in Section 14 that the Tribunal does not have jurisdiction over matters related to employees of Mahanagar Telephone Nigam Limited (MTNL) without a notification from the Central Government extending its jurisdiction.
The fact that an employee maintains lien on a post in the parent department on which the Tribunal has jurisdiction is irrelevant to confer jurisdiction on the Tribunal.
The petitioner, who was suspended by the Nigam authorities without consultation or approval of his parent department while working on deputation, is not entitled to approach the Tribunal.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]
