Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination - Notice - The petitioner, who was appointed as a daily wage worker for a period of 89 days, was disengaged after the completion of his first tenure due to a shortage of funds and work.
Although the petitioner was later re-engaged due to interim court orders, he had not completed 240 days of regular engagement in a year.
Hence, his disengagement did not violate Section 25-F of the Act, and no notice was required for his disengagement.
[Paras 8, 9 to 10]
