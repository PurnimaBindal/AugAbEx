Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Transfer - Security to life due to threat perception and personal problems, though employer's concern, cannot alone be a cause to cancel the transfer order.
An accusation of mala fide with proof or violation of policies must be considered before striking it down.
Transfer is a usual aspect in employment, and the responsibility of securing the employee's life lies with the employer or the State.
