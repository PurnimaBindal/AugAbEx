Specific Relief Act, 1963, Section 21 - Agreement To Sell - In case of failure in completing performance, or performance becoming impossible due to an acquisition of land during litigation, the vendor is entitled to compensation for damages - The awarded compensation to vendor includes the money value of services, time, and energy expended by the appellant in pursuing claims for compensation or expenditure incurred by him in litigation that culminated in the award.
