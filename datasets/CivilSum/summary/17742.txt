Constitution of India, Articles 12, 14 and 226 - Appointment/Approval/Selection - Approval of Vice-Chancellor required for selection of Lecturer in Private College - Nominee of Vice-Chancellor showing favoritism to a particular candidate and not agreeing with other members of selection committee - Vice-Chancellor cannot solely prevail over all other members - Selection made by majority must be approved by Vice-Chancellor unless there is some defect in selection committee - Refusal of approval without stated reason violates Articles 14 and 16 - Petitioner deemed appointed with all consequential benefits.
