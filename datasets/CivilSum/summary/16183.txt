Constitution of India, Article 14 - Appointment - Qualification - Due to shortage of graduate Mathematics teachers, Government relaxed qualifications and allowed managers to hire Maths teachers as long as they adhered to stated conditions - A Physics major with Mathematics as a minor could be appointed as a Mathematics teacher - The petitioner, possessing a Chemistry major and Physics minor along with a B.Ed in Natural Science and Physical Science, is entitled to have her appointment approved by relaxation of subject requirements as she is in a similar situation.
