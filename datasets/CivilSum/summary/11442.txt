Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Administrative instructions and policies - Limits of giving directions in writ jurisdiction - A court cannot issue directions that contradict the rules and regulations, and appointments to public services must follow the rules and policies set by the State.
If there is a conflict between administrative instructions and statutory rules, the instructions/policy must yield to the statutory rules.
When exercising writ jurisdiction, the Court cannot instruct the executive authority to disobey the law.
