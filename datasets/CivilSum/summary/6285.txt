Army Pension Regulation, Regulations 27(C) and 173 - Disability pension - Disability attributable and aggravated in service - Constitutional disease - The appellant had chronic Suppurative Ottis media, an inflammation of the left ear which can result from unresolved acute, ottis media and characterized by periods of exacerbations and spontaneous remissions - Appellant was examined during a remission period, and chances of a missed diagnosis due to it being a constitutional disease cannot be overlooked - Therefore, the appellant was rightfully denied a disability pension.
