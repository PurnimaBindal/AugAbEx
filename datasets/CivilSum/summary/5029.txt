Promotion - Departmental Exam - Selection - Post of Head Clerk - To qualify for the written test, a candidate must score 40% in each paper and 45% aggregate as per norms, and the selection and appointment of those who did not score at least 40% in each paper violates the condition of minimum marks and cannot be upheld.
[Paras 21, 22 and 24]
Appointment - Selection - Selection or appointment must be made in accordance with the rules/norms in force at the time of the initiation of the selection process.
[Para 20]
