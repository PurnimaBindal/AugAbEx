Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularization - Equal pay for equal work - Petitioner Union members working for years on consolidated pay requested regularization and regular pay scale - Respondents argued they are not government employees because they are paid by Non Public Funds and not recruited against regular government vacancies - Held that employees cannot claim benefit of the same pay scales as defence civilian employees because they are not government employees - Also held that since the employer is not a ‘State’ or ‘Authority’ under Article 12, the petition is not maintainable - No order required for regularization as respondent stated petitioners are already regular employees - Petition disposed accordingly.
