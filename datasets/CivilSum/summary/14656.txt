The Punjab Printing Subordinate Service Rules, 1943 have been replaced by the Punjab Printing and Stationery Department (Class III) Industrial Service Rules, 1964, which state that minimum qualification of matric is required for promotion.
However, officials employed prior to the promulgation of these Rules were denied promotion on this basis.
The petitioners argue that this is discriminatory and violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
A mandamus has been issued to the respondent to consider their case for promotion on a seniority-cum-merit basis, and they should not be denied promotion on the basis of their lack of matriculation.
