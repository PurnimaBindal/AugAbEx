Constitution of India, Article 16 and 309 - Conditions of Service - Promotion - The Rules provide power to the Government to grant more chances for passing an examination in any individual or class of cases.
The Government could relax or dispense with the requirements of any rule that regulates "the conditions of service of government servants or any class thereof" if it causes undue hardships in any particular case, including relaxing conditions prescribed for promotion since promotion is a condition of service.
The restriction on the exercise of the power or discretion does not exist.
Persons who have not exhausted all of their chances have the right to have their case considered for promotion, even if the departmental examination is not held regularly.
The executive instruction may supplement but not supplant the statutory rules under Article 309 of the Constitution.
The High Court was in error in ignoring this well-accepted principle.
