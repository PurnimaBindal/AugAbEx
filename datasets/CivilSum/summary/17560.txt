According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India, suspension should occur only when the employee's presence in their position would interfere with an ongoing investigation.
In the case of a driver for the State Transport, who is required to give preference to cross vehicles carrying VIPs, police, and ambulances by stopping their vehicle on the left-hand side of the road, there are many attendant circumstances that must be considered before executing these maneuvers on a busy street.
Furthermore, any malice on the driver's part in purposely slowing down the vehicle must be proven in a departmental enquiry, as there is no prima facie evidence to sustain his suspension until the matter has been fully investigated.
The suspension ordered due to the driver's failure to give way to the Minister's car and refusal to speak to the Minister while traveling on a bus cannot be upheld, though the situation calls for an enquiry.
