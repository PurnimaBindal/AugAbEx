Constitution of India, Articles 136 and 311 - Termination - Adverse inference drawn - Appellant, a police constable, dismissed from service without explanation - State fails to produce relevant record - High Court concludes that order is not penal as it does not cast any stigma - But, since non-production of records, adverse inferences drawn - Order of termination becomes penal in nature and is therefore invalidated - Period to be counted for seniority, but not for payment of remuneration upon reinstatement - Petition disposed of accordingly.
