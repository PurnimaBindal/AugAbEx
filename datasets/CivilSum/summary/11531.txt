As per Section 173 of the Army Act, 1950 and Regulation 173 of the Army Pension Regulations, 1961, denial of disability pension cannot be based on the ground that the petitioner was not discharged due to low medical category.
The release medical board had assessed the petitioner's disability at 40%, and he had served for 17 years.
Furthermore, the regulations provide that the disease which led to discharge must be considered to have arisen in service if no note was taken at the time of recruitment.
Also, it is undisputed that the petitioner's medical category was more than 20%, thus sustaining the denial of pension is not justified.
