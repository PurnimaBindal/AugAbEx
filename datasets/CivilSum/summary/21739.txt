Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 136 - Appointment - Ad hoc service - Regularisation - Seniority - Promotion - The appellant received the benefit of the ad hoc service he had rendered earlier to his regularisation, which resulted in his promotion and seniority being fixed.
However, the respondent was placed under similar circumstances but was denied this benefit.
The High Court held the promotion of the appellant to be based on the wrong seniority and set it aside.
It was found that the uneven treatment shown to the respondent in the matter of seniority was not correct.
The order of the High Court was proper.
