Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, Regulations 6(c) and 48(a) - Army Act, 1950, Section 192 - Pension - Disability pension - The petitioner was granted 15 days of casual leave during his recruitment training, and was injured in a train accident on his way home, resulting in medical discharge - The petitioner is eligible for disability pension as the injury is attributable to military service.
