Punjab Civil Services Rules, Chapter II (General Provision Relating to Grant of Pensions), Rule 2.2(b) and Rule 2.2(c)(1) - Retiral benefits - The petitioner was accused of embezzlement, which led to departmental and criminal proceedings.
The petitioner received a minor punishment in the departmental enquiry, while criminal proceedings were still ongoing at retirement.
Despite this, retiral benefits cannot be withheld.
