A.P.
(Roads and Buildings) Engineering Service Rules, 1965, Rules 3 and 23 - A.P.
State Subordinate Service Rules, 1962 - Seniority and promotion - Direct recruits and promotees - If promotees are inducted in excess of the quota and regularised in respect of posts that are beyond the limit, they are not allowed the benefit of seniority over those directly recruited within their quota without disturbing their regularisation.
The Supreme Court's orders in the Siva Reddy case are seen as just and equitable as they did not disturb regularisations that were in defiance of quotas, and instead maintained the balance by providing appropriate relief.
No rights accruing to promotees were disturbed.
