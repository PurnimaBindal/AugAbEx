The Punjab State Electricity Board Ministerial Establishment Departmental Accounts Examination Regulation, 1988, Regulation 9(4), as per Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India, had issued an order debarring the petitioners from an exam for two years without giving them an opportunity to be heard.
Later, the exam was cancelled, and the petitioners challenged the order on the grounds of no fair hearing.
The interim order allowed the petitioners to sit in the exam, and it was decided that it would be fair to declare the result on merit.
Therefore, directions were issued to declare the result accordingly, as it would serve the ends of justice.
