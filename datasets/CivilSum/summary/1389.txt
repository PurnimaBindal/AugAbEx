Constitution of India, Articles 38, 42, 47 and 226 - Justice - Administration of justice - Judiciary - Governments must provide necessary basic facilities, such as proper accommodations in the courts, residences for judges, and other amenities, to ensure efficient and effective discharge of their duties.
Adequate accommodation and facilities, including air-conditioning of court rooms, should be provided to judges, the bar, and the public without any unreasonable delay.
