Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pension Regulations for the Army, 1961, Regulations 213, 216 and 219 - AI 51/80, Paras 6, 7 and 8 - Family Pension - Eligibility - The petitioner's husband did not qualify for special family pension and the petitioner did not receive it either.
She received only ordinary family pension.
The pension granted will be paid to the widow until her death or remarriage, and then to the children in order of priority.
The exception in Regulation 219, which allows a widow to receive special family pension if she remarries her deceased husband's brother, does not apply in this case.
