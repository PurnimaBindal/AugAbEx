Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disablement - Petitioner requested 50 days leave before reaching the age of superannuation due to medical reasons hoping to be declared medically unfit to continue in service - Petitioner seeks compassionate appointment for his ward - Respondents argued that the rule requires disability to be shown 2 years prior to age of superannuation in order for benefit to be received - It is assessed that reasonable time depends on individual facts of each case when no specific rule is in place - The rule's main purpose is defeated by respondents' delay and inaction at every stage - No action was taken to get petitioner examined despite knowledge of their approaching age of 80 - Given time was reasonable - Court directs to appoint petitioner's ward, pending eligibility.
