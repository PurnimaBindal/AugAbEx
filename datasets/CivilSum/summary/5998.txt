Constitution of India, Article 226 - Clerks of Government schools - Petitioners, who are directly recruited clerks of State cadre, seek parity of pay with clerks of provincialised cadre - Principle of equal pay for equal work - Respondent state argues that employees of provincialised cadre, which is a diminishing cadre, get promotion after 20/25 years and are given personal grade - High Court has already upheld the decision as constitutional and has stated that the principle of equal pay for equal work is not applicable since the two sets of employees belong to distinct cadres and are governed by distinct service rules - In accordance with the judgment of High Court in Krishan Kumar's case, the petition is dismissed.
