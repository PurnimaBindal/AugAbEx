The pensionary benefits of the respondents were calculated according to the prevalent rules at the time of their retirement, including a state government notification from July 9, 1985.
This notification stated that any DA and adhoc DA sanctioned up to consumer price level index No. 568 would be considered dearness pay for pensionary benefits, for employees who retired on or after March 31, 1985.
However, the respondents did not receive this benefit, and the High Court ordered the State Government to pay all dues based on this notification.
It was later determined that the respondents were not entitled to benefits under this notification since the benefits were not available to retiring employees at the time of their retirement due to a change in rules.
As a result, the High Court's decision was overturned.
