Army Act (46 of 1950), Sections 191, 192 - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 13(3)(iii)(i) - Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, Regn.
183 - Disability Pension - The service element of disability pension must be granted even if an individual is not eligible for service pension due to insufficient service.
Denial of service element of disability pension to the petitioner, who was entitled to it throughout his discharge from service irrespective of his degree of disability, was deemed to be improper.

Army Act (46 of 1950), Section 192 - Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, Regn.
183 - Disability Pension - A discontinued disability pension due to resurvey must be reviewed by a medical board upon submission of a medical certificate showing a substantial increase in disability.
The failure of the respondent Authority to arrange for a Review Medical Board for the petitioner for more than 10 years was regarded as improper.
