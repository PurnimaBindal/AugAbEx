Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 14 - Reservation for Scheduled Castes - Recruitment of police constables - Government instructions making relaxation in chest and height measurements in respect of Schedules Caste candidates - The government failed to give relaxation to Scheduled Caste candidates despite the instructions on the ground that candidates did not apply for relaxation, which is an invalid stand taken by the government - Candidates are not required to apply for relaxation - The government has been directed to make recruitment after giving relaxation in chest and height measurements.
