The implementation of an ex-parte award of the Labour Court cannot be challenged on merit by the management when the beneficiary moves court for its implementation, even if it has not been set aside by a Division Bench of the High Court.
[Paras 2, 4, and 5] B.
In a Writ Petition seeking relief in the actual terms of an award of the Labour Court, the petitioner cannot get relief merely by prosecuting the officers under the Industrial Disputes Act, and the High Court is well within its jurisdiction to issue appropriate relief in the form of writs.
