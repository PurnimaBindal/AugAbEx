Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Seniority cum Merit - Merit cum Seniority - The promotion policy for officers of the National Insurance Company, dated 1421990, follows the criteria of seniority cum merit by fixing only 42 marks out of 100 for seniority.
The remaining 58 marks are assigned for other merits, which do not eliminate seniority altogether.
The right to promotion is not absolute, but only to consideration for promotion.
The principles of seniority cum merit and merit cum seniority are different, with the former emphasizing seniority and the latter emphasizing merit.
Nevertheless, seniority alone cannot be the sole consideration in seniority cum merit, as administrative authorities have discretion to set norms taking into account the nature and responsibilities of the posts and balance other merits with seniority.
The promotion policy for officers of the National Insurance Company is legal and non-arbitrary.
