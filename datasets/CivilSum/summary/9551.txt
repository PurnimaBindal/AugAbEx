Constitution of India, Article 16 - Suspension - Subsistence Allowance - Eligibility for - The petitioner, who served as Headmaster of an aided institution, was suspended and his request for subsistence allowance during the suspension was denied by the tribunal, which argued that the State Government was responsible for paying the allowance.
However, it was determined that the primary responsibility for paying subsistence allowance lies with the school's management.
The management cannot avoid its legal obligation to pay the allowance and shift the responsibility to the government.
