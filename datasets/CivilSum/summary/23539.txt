Central Industrial Security Force Act, 1968, Sections 5 and 22 - Central Industrial Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 11 - Resignation - A Sub Inspector in Central Industrial Security Force resigned and the Deputy Inspector General of the Force accepted the resignation - the request to withdraw the resignation and being re-enlisted in the Force was declined - The DIG was authorized by law to appoint Inspector in the Force, thus, competent in accepting the resignation - As such, the respondent's resignation could not be revoked afterwards.

Central Industrial Security Force Rules, 1969, Rules 58 - Re-enlistment - A Sub-Inspector in Central Industrial Security Force submitted his resignation - The DIG declined his request to withdraw his resignation and instead be re-enlisted in the Force - It was not an arbitrary act.
