Constitution of India, Article 226 - A circular was issued by the Government directing the management of a private ITC to pay at least 2/3rd of the salary that is being paid to Government Teachers - The management cannot challenge the decision of the Government as a representative of the management participated in the discussion before the decision was taken - The High Court has the authority to direct private educational institutions to pay salaries at the same rate as those issued to Government institutions - There is no interference called for under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
