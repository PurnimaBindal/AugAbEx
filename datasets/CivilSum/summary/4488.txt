As per schedule 2, item 3, section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act and section 18 of the Kerala Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, termination that does not amount to retrenchment under clause (bb) of section 2(oo) of the Act is allowed.
In the case of a watchman employed on a probationary period of six months whose post later got abolished, the company offered one month's salary in lieu of notice as termination on the ground that their godown was insured and no longer required a watchman.
The court found no evidence of mala fide intentions or a colorable exercise of power in the case, and thus, the provisions of section 25-F do not apply.
