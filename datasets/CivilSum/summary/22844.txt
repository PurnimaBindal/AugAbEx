Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Labour Court's discretion under Section 11-A - Limited jurisdiction - Loss of confidence in workman - Dismissal from service - Employer justified in inferring loss of confidence - Dismissal cannot be disproportionate - Section 11-A's exercise must be based on justifiable grounds and acceptable material - Section 11-A cannot be invoked without findings to the contrary.
