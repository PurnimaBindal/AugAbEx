Directions have been given under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 for the full identification of workmen, with one employee furnishing a list on behalf of themselves and others claiming to be real workmen.
The Chief Judicial Magistrate submitted a report regarding these real workmen, with various workmen filing a proforma for identification before the Tribunal.
The management has faced difficulties in this process, leading to the issuance of a fresh proforma for identification and certain modifications being carried out.
The union leader and claimants against whom the Chief Judicial Magistrate submitted a report have been held not entitled to raise a claim before the management, with other directions also being issued.
