Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reinstatement - Judicial review - The Labor Court found a termination of a workman to be illegal, ordered reinstatement with continuity of service and full back wages, despite the employer's representative being absent on the date of hearing and evidence being recorded ex parte.
However, the Labor Court failed to record a finding that the workman was employed in an industry and the termination was violative of rules of natural justice or mandatory provision of Section 25F of ID Act.
As a result, the impugned award suffers from an error of law and is liable to be quashed.
The Labor Court/Industrial Court is required to pass a speaking order, as cited in AIR 1970 Guj.
