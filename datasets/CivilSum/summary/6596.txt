Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Selection - Relaxation of qualifications - The advertisement must clearly disclose that the Selection Committee has the power to relax qualifications/experience, taking into consideration the relative merit of the candidates.
If this power is present in the selection process, it will ensure a fair and unbiased selection.
Such a selection and procedure will not be deemed arbitrary or in violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
If a candidate has been selected by the selection agency using its power of relaxation of qualifications, the appointing authority cannot refuse the candidate's appointment on the sole ground of not fulfilling the prescribed qualifications.
