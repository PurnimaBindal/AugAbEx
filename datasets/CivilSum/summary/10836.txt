Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical expenses on outdoor treatment - Reimbursement of - According to government instructions, only up to Rs. 6000/- per year can be reimbursed - However, government instructions that do not allow for full reimbursement of outdoor medical treatment can be quashed - Petitioner is entitled to full reimbursement for outdoor medical treatment - Decisions in "Renu Saighal v. State of Haryana" 1998(4) RSJ 557, "Kuldeep Kumar v. State of Haryana" 2002(3) RSJ 115, 1998(3) RSJ 705, and 1994(4) RSJ 387 have been followed.
