Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Group Insurance Benefits - The petitioner's husband was serving in the Army and went on leave in 1992, but was not heard from for over 7 years.
In such circumstances, it is reasonable to assume that he is deceased.
The Hon'ble High Court directed the release of other benefits to the petitioner, treating her husband as expired in the eye of the law.
It would be unfair to direct the petitioner to go to civil court to obtain a decree declaring her entitlement to Army Group Insurance when other benefits are being released.
The impugned order was quashed and directions were given to release the benefit of Army Group Insurance to the petitioner.
