Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Haryana Public Works Department, Public Health Branch, Junior Engineers (Group-C) Service Rules, 1986 - Promotion - Amendment of Rules - Existing vacancies - The fundamental principle of law dictates that amendments in rules or legislation made by the Government must be applied prospectively, unless there is a contrary intention - The amended rule was introduced in 1986 - Candidates who had already earned the right to be considered for promotion cannot be unfairly denied this right under the amended rules.
