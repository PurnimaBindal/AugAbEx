As per Constitution of India, Article 226 and Tamil Nadu Private School (Regulations) Act, Regulation 18(ii), petitioners are entitled to a regular basic scale of pay with DA and other attendant benefits, at rate fixed for Matriculation School.
The School of first respondent, which was upgraded and appointed on the undertaking they would pay the scale of pay as notified by the State Government, must pay according to Regulation 18(ii) for its Teachers as notified by the State Government for identical posts in its service.
The respondent is directed to implement the pay scale revised from time to time by the State Government for teachers employed in Government schools for writ petitioner from the date when the school was upgraded as Matriculation School.
