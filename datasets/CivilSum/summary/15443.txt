Medical Reimbursement - For kidney transplantation - Also for outpatient treatment after discharge from hospital - Based on decision in Ravi Kant v. State of Haryana, 1998(4) SCT 206, the respondents are instructed to reimburse the petitioner for their outstanding bills within two months.
However, reimbursement for medical expenses incurred as an outpatient will only be granted if certified by an authorized medical attendant as necessary for the petitioner's survival and without any substitute medications.
