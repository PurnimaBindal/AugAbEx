Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Seniority - Seniority list must be published every two years according to the 1999 policy - Promotion given based on seniority list of 1985 - Challenge made to seniority list of 1999 on grounds of delay and laches - Final seniority list of 1999 not subject to challenge - Promotions based on 1985 list not disturbed - Officers affected by non-finalization of seniority list for 14 years will receive deemed date of promotion and their seniority as Assistants will be counted for all future promotions.
