Constitution of India, Article 16 - MP Subordinate Agricultural (Non-Gazetted) Executive Service Recruitment Rules, 1971, Rule 13 and Schedule IV - Promotional avenues - The government has an obligation to provide promotional opportunities to its employees, including the petitioner soil surveyor who has served for three decades.
The petitioner should be promoted and the rules should be amended to promote soil surveyors.
Cited cases include AIR 1990 SC 311 and 1988 SCC (L and S) 927.
