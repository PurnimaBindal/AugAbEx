The Punjab State Electricity Board Services of Engineers (Civil) Recruitment Regulations, 1965, Regulation 2(9) mandates the determination of seniority based on the date of attaining qualifications rather than the completion of five years of service for 12-1/2% of Asstt.
Engineers (Civil) posts reserved for those who possess AMIE/BE qualifications and five years of experience as Engineering Subordinates.
Despite this, some juniors may become senior under the rules.
However, once seniority is determined in accordance with the rule on the basis of passing AMIE examination, it cannot be set aside just because some juniors become seniors and are entitled to be considered for promotion first.
As held in Ashok Kumar Sehgal's case, this action is in accordance with the rule.
Unless the Rule is challenged, seniority cannot be set aside on this ground.
