According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India, a major punishment of stoppage of four increments can be imposed.
However, in the case of allegations of embezzlement and irregularities against a Senior Auditor in Dairy Development Federation, the punishment of stoppage of four increments without a regular inquiry is considered technically invalid and vitiated.
The authority has ordered a regular inquiry into the charges and to award punishment commensurate with the established misconduct within three months.
