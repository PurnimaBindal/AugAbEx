Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, Rule 5 - Termination - Estoppel - If the respondent selected the petitioner despite the Competent Authority's remarks that he was "under height but fit," can the respondent terminate the petitioner's services on that basis?
The petitioner's services were terminated due to the medical reason that he was found to be 1 cm short.
However, the petitioner was selected despite the aforementioned remarks and almost finished with training.
Thus, the objection on his height cannot be sustained, and the principle of estoppel applies to this case.
The impugned order is set aside, and the petitioner is deemed to be in service.
[Paras 7 and 15]
