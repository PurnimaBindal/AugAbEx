Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Indian Medicines Central Council Act, 1970, Section 22 - Indian Medicine (Post-Graduate Education) Regulation, 1979, Regulation 6 Admission - MD Course - Criteria for admission to Post Graduate Course - Eligibility criteria for MD course continue to be BAMS, and instructions containing non-eligibility for three successive failures are not repugnant or contradictory to the qualification for admission to MD Course.
The university can adopt courses according to settled law on the point, and restriction of three successive failures appears to be a reasonable measure to shortlist eligible candidates.
[Para 7]

Furthermore, in the case of admission to MD (Ayurveda) course, the university applied the instructions debarring the petitioner on the ground that she had three failures in BAMS, which applies to all candidates.
Since the University allowed admission in 1992-93 session to another candidate, who had three failures, denial of the same to petitioner would result in discrimination.
Hence, the court directed the university to consider the petitioner's case for admission to MD (Ayurveda) in academic session 1993-94, strictly on merits and by disregarding her failures in BAMS [Paras 8 and 9]
