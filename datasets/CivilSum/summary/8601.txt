Constitution of India, Article 311(2) Proviso (b) - Army Act, 1950, Section 19 - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 14 - Dismissal from service - A man was dismissed from service without a formal enquiry due to the subject matter being considered immoral and he being deemed a potential threat to the security of the state.
