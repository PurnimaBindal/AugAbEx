According to Sections 21 and 19(1) of the Administrative Tribunals Act, the jurisdiction to condone delay is limited and there must be sufficient grounds for the Tribunal to do so.
The filing of repeated representations will not enlarge the period for filing an application and cannot be considered a valid reason for delay.
The Act was enacted to ensure speedy resolution of service disputes and a shorter period of limitation was provided for that purpose.
Therefore, entertaining belated claims would defeat the purpose of the Act.
