According to the Kerala Agricultural University Act, 1971, Section 43 and the Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules, Rules 14 and 17, if there is a single post and vacancies arise successively, communal rotation can be used for reserving the position.
If the lone post was initially filled from the general category, subsequent vacancies must be filled from reserved category candidates through communal rotation.
