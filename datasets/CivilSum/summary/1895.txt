Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 33(2)(b), 33-A and 10 - Dismissal and termination from service - If approval of the Tribunal for dismissal or termination is refused, the workman is entitled to full back wages and reinstatement as if he had remained in service and termination order was never passed.
Even if approval is granted, a workman can still claim adjudication under Section 10.
An application under the proviso to Section 33(2)(b) is a separate and independent proceeding.
If approval is refused by the Tribunal, the action of discharge or dismissal already taken by the employer would be void.
If approval is granted by the Tribunal that would not validate the action of discharge or dismissal.
[Paras 6 to 11]

Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 33(2)(b), 33-A - Prosecution for violation of provisions of Section 33(2)(b) - Only two kinds of punishment are subject to the above conditions and they are discharge and dismissal.
[Para 3]

Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 33(2)(b), 33-A - Payment of wages during pendency of proceedings - If a workman refuses to accept wages, the employer is required to tender them to the employee.
If the workman chooses not to accept, he cannot later complain that there was no payment of wages to him by the employer.
