Trade Unions Act (16 of 1926), Section 2(h), 2(9) - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(s) - Trade Union - The right to form - Officers and supervisory staff resolved to form a trade union - Registration was denied on the grounds that applicants are not workmen - However, it was held that the term "workmen" encompasses all persons employed in a trade or industry - As Section 2(h) places emphasis on the purpose for which the trade union is formed - Any group of employees is eligible to be registered as a trade union under the Act.
