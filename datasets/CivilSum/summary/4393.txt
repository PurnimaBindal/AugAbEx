The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Policy decisions - A policy was implemented stating that only one member from each family whose land was acquired would be given appointments - This policy was challenged and struck down by the High Court, which ruled that all persons whose lands were acquired, regardless of the area, were entitled to a job - The court also noted that the revised policy could not be applied retroactively - The petitioners cannot claim appointment based solely on the grounds that their lands were acquired.
