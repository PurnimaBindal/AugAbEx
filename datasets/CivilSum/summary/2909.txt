The Rajasthan Employment of Physically Handicapped Rules, 1976 states that a physically handicapped person can be regularly appointed by the appointing authority on any post in accordance with the rules.
Furthermore, in this peculiar case, the termination order cannot be sustained as the petitioners possessed the required qualifications at the time of their appointment and have been working regularly since 1992.
Therefore, the petitioners and the RPSC are entitled to regularization.
[Paras 3, 21, 22, 24 and 25]
