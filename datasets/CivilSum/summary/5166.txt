Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 deals with termination and appointments of class IV employees in Banks are governed by statutory rules.
Casual engagement for intermittent period without proof of receiving salary for 240 days does not amount to retrenchment under Section 25-F of the ID Act.
Disengagement from temporary work on daily wages or monthly consolidated pay does not attract Section 25-F [Paras 14, 15 and 17]
