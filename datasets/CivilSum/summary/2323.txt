The Service Regulations of the Bank allow for the commencement of departmental disciplinary proceedings even after acquittal in criminal proceedings, and therefore, the punishing authority is not without jurisdiction.
[Para 18]
Delay in commencing disciplinary proceedings after acquittal in criminal proceedings is not fatal to the proceedings, especially if the delay is not attributable to the employer.
[Para 19]
The quantum of punishment is a managerial decision left to the employer, but the procedure laid down in law must be followed.
