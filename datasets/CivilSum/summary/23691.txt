The Medical Council Act of 1956, Sections 11 and 16, along with Articles 32 and 226 of the Indian Constitution, govern the admission process for medical colleges.
Seats allotted for the All-India quota are filled by local students chosen by the State Government.
According to the Supreme Court, the State Government can increase the number of seats and absorb the additional students.
Despite this, the Indian Medical Council cannot be forced to abandon its legal obligations and permit seat increases without careful consideration.
