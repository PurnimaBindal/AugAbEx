The Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 2(e) and 2(s) - Meaning of the word 'Employee and Wages' - If an employee receives an honorarium for his service, it is considered emoluments paid for work and is included in the term 'Wages' - The petitioner resigned after eight years of service and is entitled to payment of gratuity under section 4(1)(b).
