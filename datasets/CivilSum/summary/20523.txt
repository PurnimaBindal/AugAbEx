Constitution of India, Article 16 - Compassionate appointment - Discrimination - Appellant and respondents were appointed as clerks under compassionate appointment, despite being eligible for the post of Sub-Inspector of Excise.
Later, the respondents were promoted to Sub-Inspectors, while the senior appellant was overlooked.
The Tribunal initially quashed the appointment of respondents but refused relief to appellant, citing delay in filing the case.
The review Tribunal did not interfere with respondents' appointment and did not grant relief to the appellant.
The order was challenged, and it was held that the appellant must be considered for the post of Sub-Inspector, and discrimination against the appellant cannot be justified.
The order of the Tribunal was set aside, and the State was directed to consider the appellant's case for the appointment of a Sub-Inspector of Excise.
The appeal was allowed accordingly.
