Haryana Public Service Commission (Conditions of Service) Regulations, 1973, Rule 5 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol-II, Rules 4.19(b) and 616(2) - Resignation - Pension - If resignation is due to domestic problems after completing qualifying service, it cannot be used as a means to avoid misconduct punishment.
Instead, it will be considered a voluntary retirement with proportionate pension and retirement benefits.
