Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996, Section 15 - Delhi School Education Act, Section 8(3) - Termination - Arbitration Clause - An employee who had been dismissed filed an Appeal to Tribunal - When petitioner filed application to reference dispute to Arbitrator under service rules' arbitration clause, his application rejected by Tribunal - However, Section 8(3) gives right to Tribunal to hear such appeals and Section 15 provides for hearing of appeals by arbitrator - It was decided that once remedies provided under section 8(3) or 15 are exhausted, the opposite party cannot scuttle that right by raising objection that right should have been exercised in the manner provided in an alternative provision - As both provisions serve the same purpose.
