Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 151 - Disability pension - Benefit withdrawn - Petitioner, a discharged Jawan found to be medically unfit, received disability pension from 11.5.49 to 12.5.53 until the benefit was withdrawn - Despite a resurvey Medical Board assessing the disability at 40%, the Controller Defence Account (P), Allahabad, at his own level, treated the appellant's disability as less than 20% and denied him any element of disability pension - In 1989, the appellant challenged the controller's decision, which was overturned, and the respondent directed to pay the disability pension to appellant from 1988 in accordance with law.
