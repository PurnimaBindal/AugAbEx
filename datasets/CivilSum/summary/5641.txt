Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admissions - Reservation - Reservation on institutional basis - Clause 1(b) in the Chandigarh Administration's Notification stating that only students who have completed 11th and 12th from Chandigarh recognized schools and colleges may be admitted to Government Medical College for the MBBS course - This 100% reservation on institutional basis is arbitrary and discriminatory and violates Article 14 of the Constitution - The condition of reservation on institutional basis in clause 1(b) of the Notification is ultra vires and has been struck down.
