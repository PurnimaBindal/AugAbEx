Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 335 - Reservation - Relaxation in eligibility conditions - Reservation in promotions - Indira Sawhney's case decision states that efficiency of administration should not be compromised - Qualifying standards cannot be relaxed in promotions where qualifying exams determine fitness of candidates - Tribunal was mistaken in believing Indira Sawhney's case permits such relaxations - Even with reservations in promotions, lower qualifying marks or evaluation levels are not allowed under Article 335.
