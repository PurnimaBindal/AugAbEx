Rajasthan Civil Services (Accounts Services) Rules, 1954, Rules 7(3) and 9 - Determination of quota meant for promotees and direct recruits - The 50:50 ratio is only applicable at the entry point for both directly recruited and promoted - All further promotions must be free of discrimination between the two classes - It is illegal to maintain the 50:50 ratio in all service cadres.
