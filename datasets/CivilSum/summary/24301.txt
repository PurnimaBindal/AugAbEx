Article 14, 16, and 311 of the Constitution of India and the UP Temporary Government Servant (Termination of Services) Rules, 1975 - The principle of 'Last come first go' applies only in case of retrenchment and not in the case of termination of a temporary servant on account of unsatisfactory work and conduct.
Juniors may be retained over seniors based on efficiency and suitability, which does not violate the principle of equality under Articles 14 and 16.
It is erroneous to hold that an order of termination on account of unsatisfactory work and conduct is necessarily punitive in nature.
[Para 5]

Article 14, 16, and 311 of the Constitution of India and the UP Temporary Government Servant (Termination of Services) Rules, 1975 - Temporary government servants can be terminated according to the contract of service or rules without a regular enquiry and detailed reasons.
The termination order is not conclusive and the court can review whether it was punitive or not.
However, a temporary servant does not have a right to hold the post and, therefore, does not suffer any evil consequences.
