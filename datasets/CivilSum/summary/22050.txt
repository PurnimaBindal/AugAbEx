Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab University Act, 1961, Section 5(3)(a) - Reservation - Admission to LLB course - Admission based on merit - Candidates passing qualifying examination from the same university are eligible to add an additional ten percent marks in their admission application - The reservation was university-wise and therefore deemed permissible - However, the addition of ten percent of marks is regarded as too high and should not exceed five percent - The rule for the addition of marks can be modified accordingly - The court cannot provide relief to the appellant at this late stage and the order is affirmed.
