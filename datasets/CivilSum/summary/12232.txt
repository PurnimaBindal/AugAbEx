Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 2(1)(n), 3 and 30 - Employer-employee relationship - The deceased worked for the respondent for over 4 years and sufficient evidence exists to show this relationship.
Furthermore, the documents confirm that the person died in an accident.
The commissioner's finding that the deceased was an employee and the accident occurred during work was not perverse or an impossible inference.

Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 3 and 12 - Compensation - Necessary party - In the case of an accident that takes place while the work of cutting trees is being undertaken through a contractor, claim proceedings can be held against either the principal employer, the contractor, or both.
Non-impleading of the contractor does not affect the claim proceedings.

Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4(1)(a) - Quantum of Compensation - Applicability of amending Act 30 of 1995 - The provisions of Section 4(1)(a) at the time of accident, which computed compensation based on 40% of monthly income, applies.
The commission's quantification of 50% of monthly income is erroneous as the application was made prior to the amendment.
Quantification after the amendment is correct.
