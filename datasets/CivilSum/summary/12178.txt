According to Sections 4 and 9 of the Public Premises (Eviction of Unauthorised Occupants) Act, 1971, the petitioner who was dismissed from service was not entitled to continue occupying government accommodation.
The petitioner was duly served with a notice of eviction, but failed to appear before the Estate Officer on the scheduled date.
Despite an appeal under Section 9 being dismissed, the court cannot interfere with the order of eviction on grounds of not serving as a court of appeal.
The writ jurisdiction of the court only ascertains whether the principles of natural justice were followed, there was a fair opportunity given and the authority below exceeded its jurisdiction or not.
