Notification for Admission to MBBS/BDS Courses in State Medical Colleges and State Dental Colleges states that sportsmen and sportswomen in the reserved category will be admitted based on their gradation done by the Department of Sports, Punjab.
To be eligible for admission, they must secure a minimum of 35% marks in the P.M.T.
The High Court's decision to direct a different procedure than the one notified by the State Govt. for admissions is not justifiable.
[Para 6]
Two percent of seats in State Medical Colleges and State Dental Colleges are reserved for sportsmen and sportswomen.
The State Govt. must decide the methodology for admission to these reserve seats, as it is the function of the executive to lay down the procedure for admission to reserve categories.
