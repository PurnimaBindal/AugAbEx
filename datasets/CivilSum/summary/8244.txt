Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Cochin University of Science and Technology Act, 1986, Section 7(2) - Appointment - Reservation - The mere fact that the petitioner participated in an interview will not prevent them from challenging the University's action in treating a single post of Executive Engineer in a cadre as reserved.
Such reservation violates constitutional provisions in Articles 14 and 16.
The University's contention of estoppel and waiver will not hold good.
[Para 8] B Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Cochin University of Science and Technology Act, 1986, Section 7(2) - Appointment - Rule of reservation - One solitary post cannot have 100% reservation and dissimilar posts cannot be grouped together for the purpose of applying the rule of reservation.
[Para 9] C Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Cochin University of Science and Technology Act, 1986, Section 7(2) - Appointment - Reservation - The constitutionality of Section 7(2) does not prevent the petitioner's claim from being considered on its merits.
The contention that the petitioner is not entitled to any relief because they failed to challenge the vires of Section 7(2) is not sustainable.
