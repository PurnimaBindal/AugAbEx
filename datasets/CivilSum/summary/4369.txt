Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Probation period/Extension of probation period - The appointing authority has the power to extend the probation period if they are dissatisfied with the probationer's work - However, there must be evidence to support the extension, reversion, or confirmation - An extension of probation based on punitive allegations will be considered mala fide - Courts can examine the official record and affidavits to determine the legitimacy of the extension.
[Paras 11 to 14]  B. Departmental Circular/Departmental Instructions/Deemed confirmation - An official circular requires six-monthly reports to be submitted within fifteen days, but this is not mandatory - Failure to submit the reports within this timeframe does not result in deemed confirmation.
