Constitution of India, Article 311(2), 2nd Proviso (a) - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2 - Dismissal from service - Conviction on criminal charge - Moral turpitude - Punishing authority did not properly assess whether the petitioner's criminal conviction under Sections 326/324/34 IPC involved moral turpitude - The punishment order is invalidated.
