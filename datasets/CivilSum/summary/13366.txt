Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Change of subject - The petitioner was enrolled in the MD course in Dermatology.
The petitioner argued that he was forced to pursue the course in Dermatology.
The respondents disputed the claim and noted that the petitioner was admitted to the speciality he selected according to his ranking on the merit list.
They also asserted that no general category candidate was granted admission to MD (Medicine) as claimed by the petitioner.
Once the petitioner selected MD (Dermatology), he could not change to another speciality even if openings became available in the future.
[Paras 4 to 7]
