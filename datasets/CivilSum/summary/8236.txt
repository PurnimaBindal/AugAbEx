The Suit for declaration was issued a decree stating that the plaintiff should be considered for promotion in the Assistant grade.
The plaintiff sought to execute this decree, but the defendant raised objections that he was unable to promote the plaintiff due to seniority-cum-merit principle.
The judgment was held that since the decree was passed by the Court in disregard of the rules and instructions, it was null and void, and cannot be executed.
Consequently, the execution petition was dismissed.
