Constitution of India, Article 226 - Evidence Act, 1872, Section 3- Award of Tribunal - Superannuation - Date of birth - The Indian Evidence Act does not apply to Industrial Tribunal proceedings, only principles for recording and re-appreciating evidence can be applied - Petitioner retired on 3151987, as he had given a date of birth of 18.12.1928 at the time of appointment - The Tribunal's decision to superannuate the petitioner from the given date was supported by evidence presented during proceedings - Accordingly, the Tribunal's award was upheld.
