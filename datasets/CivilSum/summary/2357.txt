UP Secondary Education Services Commission and Selection Boards Act, 1982, Section 18 - Selection/Appointment/Recruitment - During a ban on appointments imposed by the State Government, petitioners were appointed by the management of a state-aided private school and claim salary for the period - The state is responsible for paying the salary - However, the petitioners are not entitled to any relief - Selection and appointment during the ban period does not confer any rights on the petitioners - The state cannot be held liable for payment of salary.
[Paras 12 and 17]
UP Secondary Education Services Commission and Selection Boards Act, 1982, Sections 18 and 33C - The ban on recruitment was lifted on 2691991 - The petitioners claim regularisation thereafter under Section 33C of the Act - However, recruitment and appointment was banned during the relevant period - The order lifting the ban clearly stated that the selection made during the ban shall be treated as cancelled - Appointments made during the period of the ban are invalid and do not survive - Section 33C does not provide any benefit to the petitioners - The writ petition is dismissed.
