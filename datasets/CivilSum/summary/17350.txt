The policy decision that set the age limit at 43 years as of July 15, 1997 was challenged in a case where petitioners were denied admission to a course that would have allowed them to pursue higher assignments.
An order that was issued in August of 1997, but made effective from July, was deemed invalid due to retrospective nature and vagueness.
The decision to use a cut-off date of July 15, 1997 was also deemed invalid as there was no clarification on its relationship to the matter at hand.
