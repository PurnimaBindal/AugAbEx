According to the majority view, the decision in Saurabh Chaudri's case will only apply prospectively and not affect the admission process that was already underway when the judgment was announced.
Clarification on the implementation of the directions is necessary because there was no clarification on whether the judgment should be applied retrospectively or prospectively.
[Paras 4 to 6]
The modification of a judgment by a different Constitution Bench in exercise of jurisdiction U/s 142 is not legally permissible by way of simply an application for modification.
Only a review application may be the proper remedy, and that is at the behest of parties to the petition and not strangers, and immediately after the judgment, not at the behest of those who took chance on both sides despite directions of the court and failed to make the grade without protection of reservation.
Meritorious students cannot be made to suffer for lesser merit.
