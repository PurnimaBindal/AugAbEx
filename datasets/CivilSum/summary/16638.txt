Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability pension - The petitioner, who was discharged from service on compassionate grounds at his own request, challenges the non-grant of Disability Pension.
They argue that as the petitioner was ultimately discharged after 1-1/2 years from the date he made the application to discharge him, he should be entitled to the pension.
However, at the time of discharge, the petitioner had only completed services for about 9 years, and therefore is not eligible for the pension.
