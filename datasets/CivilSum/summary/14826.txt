The petitioner in the Bachelor of Engineering course was not admitted against the seat earmarked for NRI, but was instead admitted via telephonic directions from the Registrar of the University.
The petitioner willingly undertook to prosecute studies at their own risk and responsibility.
However, the petitioner was not admitted in the normal course based on merit secured in the CET conducted by the university.
The University did not commit any illegality by declining approval to the then Registrar's patently illegal action to secure admission for the petitioner, bypassing the merit of other candidates.
As a result, a writ of mandamus cannot be issued to direct the respondents to indirectly regularize the petitioner's admission.
