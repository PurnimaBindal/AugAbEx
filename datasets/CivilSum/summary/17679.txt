Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Transfer - Stayed - Transfer order cancelled - The petitioner's affidavit states that her husband suffered two consecutive heart attacks and requires constant medical check-up and emergency medical aid - Medical certificates issued by institutes in Chandigarh and New Delhi were provided - The petitioner expressed concern about being transferred again - The court held that the State should consider the difficulties expressed by the petitioner, as the State Govt. is responsible for the welfare of its employees.
