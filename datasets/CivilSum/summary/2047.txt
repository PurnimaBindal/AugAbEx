Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(oo)(bb) - Retrenchment - Non-availability of work - Termination of workmen's services due to non-availability of work will be considered retrenchment - Compliance with Section 25F provisions is required.

Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(s) and 25F - Retrenchment - Daily wage workers - Every employer must comply with Section 25F of the ID Act if a workman's services are terminated after completing 240 days of work within 12 months before the termination date, regardless of their status, appointment, or being a temporary or daily wager.
