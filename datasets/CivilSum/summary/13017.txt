The petitioner argues that they should receive the benefit of selection grade from the date of initial appointment rather than the confirmation on passing the efficiency test as done by the respondents.
The tribunal has rejected the appeal on the grounds of limitation, but as the petitioner continues to be deprived of a higher salary, the cause of action is recurring.
Thus, the rejection of the appeal due to delay is erroneous.
[Para 11] B.
The petitioner has been granted selection grade from the date of confirmation on passing the efficiency test.
However, based on the judgment of a larger bench in the "State of Rajasthan v. Farooq Ahmed" case, the initial service on appointment as per rules should be counted for the grant of selection grade.
