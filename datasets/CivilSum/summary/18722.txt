Border Security Force Rules, Rule 26 - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Retirement - Petitioner's retirement under Rule 26 of BSF Rules challenged on grounds of violation of principles of Natural Justice - Board of officers recommended retirement on account of unsuitability due to Petitioner's previous conduct of earning six punishments in four years of service - Show cause notice issued and compliance with Principles of Natural Justice observed - No further enquiry required as order not due to misconduct but due to unsuitability for continued service - Petition dismissed.
