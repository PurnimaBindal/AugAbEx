Haryana Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Section 7 - Haryana Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Rules, 1980, Rules 3, 4, 6 and 7 - Rules 4(4) - Dismissal from service - The Governing Body of the College found the employee guilty of misconduct after considering his reply and delivered a punishment.
Although the decision to give a hearing was not specified in the Rule, the authority must conform to its own resolution once having concluded that the delinquent deserves hearing to execute an unbiased punishment order.
However, the Chairman alone could not exercise this power, and other members of the Governing Body might have had differing opinions from the chairman.
This breach of procedure invalidated the order of punishment.
[Paras 9 and 10]

Haryana Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Rules, 1980, Rule 3(1) - Recording of Reasons - Rule 3(1) of the Security of Service Rules, 1980 mandates that the competent authority record good and sufficient reasons for imposing any penalty specified in that Rule.
The authority must communicate this reason to the affected employee to reach a conclusion.
The explanation of the petitioner was not considered by the authorities under the Act, and no reason was given for declining it.
This violates the order of punishment.
[Paras 10 and 11]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Speaking order - Quasi-judicial orders - A quasi-judicial authority is required to provide a speaking order under the principles of administrative law.
A public authority must act judicially in exercising its quasi-judicial powers.
[Para 10]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Misconduct - The petitioner was accused of taking liquor and then misbehaving with the guardian of a girl student while under its influence.
The incidence allegedly occurred during a holiday, and the college was closed due to a strike.
The punishment order ignored years of unblemished service and did not consider important factors, including the lack of information about the petitioner's misbehavior.
This invalidates the orders of the authorities as they are not based on good and sufficient reasons as stipulated in the Rules.
[Paras 12, 13, and 14]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Termination of service - Termination of an employee's service cannot be done with retrospective effect, making the termination order invalid.
