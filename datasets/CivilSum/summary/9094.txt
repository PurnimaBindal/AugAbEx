According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 and the Family Pension-cum-Gratuity Rules, Rule 20(bb) & (bbb), all pensioners are part of the same class and therefore entitled to the benefits of the liberalised scheme.
Any discrimination made amongst pensioners based solely on their retirement date, without any reasonable criterion, is subject to be dismissed under Article 226 of the Constitution Hence, it is impossible to argue that a writ petition is not maintainable and that no fundamental right has been violated.
