Article 311 of the Constitution of India and Article 7 of the Limitation Act, 1963 state that arrears of salary may only be paid for up to three years.
However, a suit to recover the entire amount can be filed when it is known that the salary will not be paid for the entire period.

Under Section 34 of the Civil Procedure Code, 1908 and Section 6(2) of the Interest Act, 1978, interest pendente lite can be awarded by the Court from the date of demand if other requirements are met.
