Constitution of India, Article 226 - House Rent Allowance - Recovery - Teachers posted within 10 miles of International Border with Pakistan given rent-free accommodation or house rent allowance equal to 125% of their pay without receipts - Cities/towns re-classified for grant of house rent allowance - Employees entitled to continue receiving higher rates until adjusted in new rates - Apex Court interpretation in SLP 9149 of 1992 grants protection to petitioners - Respondents not allowed to make recoveries of house rent allowance.
[Paras 2 and 3]
