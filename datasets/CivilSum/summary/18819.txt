The Constitution of India, Article 226 and the Army Act, 1950 are involved in this case where the petitioner who was successful in NDA was rejected by the Appeal Medical Board and denied the opportunity of Review Medical Board.
The court held that Review Medical Board against the Appeal Medical Board is not a matter of right and it is up to the Higher Medical Authorities to decide.
The writ petition is dismissed as no legal right of the petitioner has been infringed.
