Constitution of India, Article 226 - Departmental Enquiry - Natural Justice - If the disciplinary authority disagrees with the inquiry authority on any charge, the disciplinary authority must record its tentative reasons for the disagreement and provide the delinquent officer with an opportunity to respond before recording its findings.
[Para 8]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Departmental Enquiry - Natural justice - The petitioner was charged with wilfully remaining absent and disobeying orders and directions from higher authorities.
The inquiry officer did not find the petitioner guilty of the charge for remaining absent, nor did they record a finding of guilt for disobedience of any orders or directions.
However, the disciplinary authority, without passing any order of disagreement, found the petitioner guilty of remaining absent from duty.
This violated principles of natural justice and the impugned order of punishment was quashed.
