Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Industry - Sovereign functions of the State - Water supply & sewerage activities - The State must perform inalienable and sovereign functions, and no other person or agency - However, the function of preparing schemes for water supply and sewerage facilities can be undertaken by a private agency - The functions of such Board are not sovereign and can be covered under the definition of `Industry' - This decision was followed in Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board v. A Rajappa and others, AIR 1978 SC 548.
