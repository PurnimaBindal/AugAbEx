Delhi Sikh Gurdwara Act, 1971 - Indraprastha Vishwavidyalaya Act, 1998 - Tenure of service - Enforcement of - The petitioner's service was extended for a term of five years, redesignating him as Director General.
His contract terms limited his employment to until he will be 65 years old.
He superannuated before the five-year extension had ended.
The University statutes and UGC guidelines do not apply to him, and his case is contractual in nature.
Therefore, his enforcement cannot be made under Article 226, and he must seek damages for breach of contract.
