Central Civil Service (Conduct) Rules, 1964, Rule 3(i)(ii) - Central Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 14 - Resignation - Enquiry - Dismissal - A DSP who resigned from his temporary position while willfully absent from duty was dismissed after the inquiry officer deemed his misconduct proved beyond doubt.
The petitioner's argument that he believed his resignation had been accepted, and that his dismissal was disproportionate to his alleged misconduct, was challenged by the respondent, who contended that the petitioner remained in employment unless his resignation was accepted, and who observed that the petitioner had joined another company immediately without refunding the three months' pay and allowances.
The court ruled that the petitioner had disentitled himself from relief due to his conduct, and dismissed the petition as a result.
