Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 6-N - Fixed term appointment - Termination - Notice - Compensation - The petitioner was appointed as a daily wager but was permitted to continue for 26 months, making notice and retrenchment compensation necessary.
The termination of services without compliance of Section 6N is held to be bad in law.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 4(k) - Termination of services - Back wages - Delay in making of reference - A reference was made after a lapse of 8-1/2 years, therefore, 50 percent of back wages are allowed from the date of reference order.
