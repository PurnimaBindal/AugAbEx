The Army Act, Section 19, Army Rules, Rule 13-A, and the Constitution of India, Article 226 all pertain to promotion examinations.
In a case where the petitioner failed to pass this examination, a show cause notice for compulsory retirement was issued, followed by orders to retire from service.
The petitioner attempted to rely upon the review policy, which allows for additional time to pass promotion exams.
However, the show cause notice and order for retirement were issued prior to the issuance of the revised policy letter, so the petitioner cannot be considered covered by the policy.
