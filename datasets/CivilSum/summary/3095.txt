Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226 and 311(2) - Termination of service - Disciplinary action - The disciplinary authority can terminate the service of a convicted employee even if an appeal against the conviction has been admitted in the appellate Court and the sentence has been suspended.

Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Sections 389 and 482 - Termination - Stay of conviction - The appellate court has the power to stay the effect of conviction under section 389, but it should be exercised in rare cases and there must be a separate application for suspension of conviction.
In cases of moral turpitude conviction, notice should be given to the disciplinary authority.
