In accordance with Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 317(ii) and Article 226 of the Constitution of India, work charge service petitioners must receive regular pay after subsequent regularization, including work charged basis service when computing total service for granting increments and higher pay scale in accordance with State policy.
Failure to do so would be unjust and unfair, and the service on work-charge basis must be counted so long as it is satisfactory.
