According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 16 and 226/227, a job linked vocational course in LIC is offered for apprenticeship appointment.
Students who achieve Grade B2 or above in vocational subjects will be considered for appointment in LIC, subject to an interview and Medical Examination, as stated in the Examination Rules of CBSE.
One petitioner argued that she did not meet the scheme requirements since she did not obtain a B Grade or above in the Life Insurance paper on her first attempt.
However, this plea was not considered since the scheme is linked with CBSE and their Rules provide for improvement in performance.
LIC is also bound by CBSE Rules, which the petitioner has met, and therefore, has met all the criteria required for the apprenticeship.
