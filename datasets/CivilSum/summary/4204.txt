Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Natural justice - Dismissal from service - Delinquents requesting legal assistance - Enquiry held by the District Judge - Serious allegations of misconduct and misbehavior of oral nature with officials including the District Judge itself - Request for permission to have an advocate present not granted - This refusal is a violation of the principles of natural justice - The enquiry report prepared on the basis of a violation to principles of natural justice cannot be relied upon - An order of dismissal from service based on such an enquiry report is liable to be quashed.
[Paras 6 & 7]
