Railway Services (Pension) Rules, 1993, Rules 37, 49 and 50 - Railway Establishment Code (Part II), Rule 1303 - Pension - Subsistence allowance - Suspension period - Compulsory retirement during suspension - Emoluments for fixation of pension - Rule 37 gives suspension period as qualifying service and Rule 49 refers to `basic' pay - Rule 1303 refers to `amount drawn' - If full basic pay is the monthly "emoluments", then 75% of full basic pay will be `emoluments' for a person under suspension - Pension will not be fixed based on last pay drawn, but on revised subsistence allowance drawn during the last 10 months.
[Paras 20 to 30] B.
Pension - Subsistence allowance - `Emoluments' - Subsistence allowance during suspension will be considered last drawn emoluments for the purpose of pension.
