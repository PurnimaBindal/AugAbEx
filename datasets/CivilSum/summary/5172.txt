As per the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 12 and 226, "Markfed" qualifies as a "State" and therefore falls under the ambit of writ jurisdiction.
[Para 11]
According to the Punjab State Supply and Marketing Cooperative Services (Common cadre) Rules, 1967, Rule 215, the Managing Director of Markfed lacked the authority to pass the impugned order of dismissal against the petitioner.
Also, since a co-employee dismissed under the same order was reinstated, the petitioner must be granted the same benefits.
[Para 13]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10, specifies that delay alone cannot disentitle a petitioner from seeking relief; the matter of delay should be considered in light of the specific facts and circumstances of each case.
Since a co-employee dismissed under the same order was reinstated, it would be unfair to deny the petitioner the same benefit solely on the grounds of delay.
However, the relief granted to the petitioner may be molded by depriving him of back wages for the time period in which there was a delay in raising the dispute.
