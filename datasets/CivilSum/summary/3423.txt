Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Discrimination - Appointment for one sanctioned post of BEd.
School Assistant - The Respondent No. 4, who is a Mathematics teacher, was appointed to the sanctioned post, despite being nine years junior to the Petitioner, who is a science teacher - However, it is established that the sanctioned post of BEd.
Assistant can be filled by a science teacher - Therefore, the principle of seniority must be observed by State authority and aided educational institution in appointing to the sanctioned post - Hence, mandamus is issued to respondents ordering the appointment of the petitioner to the post.
