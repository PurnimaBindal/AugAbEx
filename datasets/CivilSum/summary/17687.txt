Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Opportunity of equality - The petitioners and respondents No. 4 to 20 provided their services during the Govt. employee strike with the assurance of absorption into Govt. service - Petitioners were not called for screening committee while respondents No. 4 to 20 were, resulting in deprivation of the petitioner's right to equal opportunity - Discrimination between similarly situated employees for one purpose is not acceptable - The petitioner's fundamental right to equality has been violated due to discrimination against respondents No. 4 to 20 - The respondents have been directed to re-convene the screening committee and interview the petitioners for selection.
