Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 115 and Order 39, Rules 1, 2 and 4 - Contract Act, 1872, Section 17 - Revision - Superannuation - Salary - A suit was filed for the declaration that the petitioner had the right to continue in service up to 60 years, and the order that retired her at 58 years was unlawful.
An interim injunction was obtained that prevented her from being removed from service at the age of 58 years, and this order was extended from time to time, until ultimately she crossed 60 years in service.
A claim was made for salary for the period beyond 58 years of age.
Ultimately, she was ordered to either receive payment, or to receive a deposit in court for payment within one month, of salary and allowances for two years, subject to her furnishing security for restitution in the event of ultimate dismissal of the suit.
