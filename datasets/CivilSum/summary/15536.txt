Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pension - Interest on arrears of delayed pension - An employee who was deprived of his pension for 11 years is entitled to 12% interest on the arrears that were paid after a long delay.
The government illegally withheld the pension and used the amount.
The authority is responsible for initiating the pension process of an employee six months prior to their retirement.
This ruling follows AIR 1985 SC 356.
