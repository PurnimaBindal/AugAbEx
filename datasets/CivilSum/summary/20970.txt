Section 2(a)(i) of the Mines Act, 1952 defines "mine" as only pertaining to the extraction of ores and not other parts of the establishment.
The appropriate government in the Industrial Disputes Act is identical to the one defined in the ESI Act.
A dispute concerning the Head Office of the mine is not considered a dispute regarding a mine as defined under the Mines Act.
The notification issued by the Government of Goa, Daman and Diu under the ESI Act was deemed appropriate.
