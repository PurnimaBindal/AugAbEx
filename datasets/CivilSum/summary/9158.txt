"Article 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India address regularisation and retrenchment.
The petitioner in this case was retrenched in accordance with Section 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act before completing five years' ad hoc service, and therefore is not entitled to be regularised by being put back in service.
The decision in State of Haryana v. Piara Singh and others, (1992(3) SCT 201) was followed.
[Paras 4 and 5]"
