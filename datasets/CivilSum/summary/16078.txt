Constitution of India, Article 226 - Absorption - Rehabilitation - The petitioner was not absorbed due to not having a conductor's licence at the time of nationalisation.
However, the petitioner was qualified as a Checker and had a practical knowledge of the conductor job.
The Checker position is equivalent to that of a Ticket Inspector of APSRTC.
The petitioner obtained a conductor's licence and was prepared to work as a conductor when the route was nationalised.
He was fully eligible for the post of conductor and entitled to be absorbed in that position.
The court ordered petitioner's absorption as a conductor as he was displaced due to the nationalisation of the bus route.
