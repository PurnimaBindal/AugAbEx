The petitioner, a Constable, was dismissed due to unauthorized absence from duty.
During that period, he was found traveling in a first-class compartment with a woman and fought with students in the same compartment, resulting in delaying the train by 40 minutes.
The petitioner later claimed to be "sick" during that period.
Despite not receiving a copy of the documents, the petitioner did not raise any objections.
The court held that the facts of the case did not fall under Section 42 and dismissed the petition.
