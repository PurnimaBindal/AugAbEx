Employees State Insurance Act, Section 1(4) - Establishment /Shops & Establishments - Shops with 20 or more employees are considered 'shops and establishments' if they conduct systematic commercial or economic activities.
The Act aims to provide welfare and should encompass as many establishments as possible to achieve its objectives.
The notification dated 18.9.1974 that extends provisions to shops and establishments is validly upheld.
[Paras 15, 22 and 23]
