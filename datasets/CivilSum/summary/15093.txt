Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ Jurisdiction - Bias - The Deputy Chief Engineer, who made the order of final assessment on 2132003, later affirmed the order of assessment on 10.2.2005 in the capacity of appellate authority, after being promoted as Chief Engineer - Assessing and appellate powers cannot be exercised by the same person - Bias and malafides do not need to be specifically proved in such cases - The petition has been granted.
