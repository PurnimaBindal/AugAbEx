Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Bombay Home Guards Act, 1947, Section 2A - Termination of services - Commandant, not a Govt. servant, is not entitled to protection of Article 311 - Order of termination passed without issuing show cause notice - Natural justice requires opportunity of hearing to be granted - Allegations of misappropriation of funds - Order liable to be set aside - Respondent may proceed against petitioner according to law.
