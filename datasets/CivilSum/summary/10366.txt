Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Advertisement - HCS (Executive Branch) - In 1992, an advertisement was given for 62 posts, but 12 more posts became available before the declaration of the result of the written examination held in 1996.
Although the number of vacancies was increased to 74, the commission did not issue any corrigendum to increase the vacancies.
The petitioners' names appeared in the selected list, but they were not appointed because no corrigendum was issued.
The increase in the number of posts long after the advertisement in 1992 means eligible candidates cannot be deprived of the opportunity of applying for vacancies that had arisen thereafter.
The selection process by way of requisition can be started for clear and anticipated vacancies but not for future vacancies.
[Paras 11 & 12]
