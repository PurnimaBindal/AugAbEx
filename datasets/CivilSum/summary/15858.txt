Contempt of Courts Act, Sections 11 & 12 - Admissions - Reservation - Merit list - A writ petition required admission of a student to achieve 50:50 reservation of seats - The college admitted the top student on the left out candidates' merit list - The petitioner was not the top student - As a result, she had no entitlement to claim the seat - Therefore, no contempt was committed by the institution.
