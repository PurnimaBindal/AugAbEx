According to Section 8(4) of the Delhi School Educational Act, the Managing Committee of a recognised school must obtain prior approval from the Director before suspending any employee.
In this case, the petitioner was promoted to Principal but was then suspended without prior approval from the Director.
The Director refused to approve the suspension and ordered for the petitioner's reinstatement.
This was upheld by the court, and the petition was allowed under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India.
