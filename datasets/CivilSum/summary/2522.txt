The Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 state that the transfer of a factory from one company to another company is not within the jurisdiction of writs, as the two companies are not considered a State or Authority within the meaning of these articles.
Should there be any question of permission required in such a matter, it is up to the Government of India or the Government of West Bengal to consider and make a decision in accordance with the law.
The court cannot preempt the government's decision or direct the government not to grant permission for the transfer in advance.

The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(cc) states that closure refers to the permanent closing down of a place of employment or a part thereof.
It is not even remotely concerned with the question of change of employer.
Transfer of a factory from one owner to another does not necessarily constitute an act of closure; a mere change of ownership does not imply the closure of the factory.
When a factory is transferred as a going concern, there cannot be any question of the act of transfer being an act of closure.
Any relief for retrenchment or closure, if applicable, would lie under the Act and not in writ jurisdiction since neither Pfizer Ltd nor Torrent Pharmaceutical Ltd is a State or Authority as per the Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226.
