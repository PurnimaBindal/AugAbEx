The Prathma Bank (Staff) Service Regulations, 1980, Regulations 8(3) and 10(2) and the Regional Rural Banks Act, 1976, Sections 29 and 30 address the termination of services of a probationer.
If a probationer's services are terminated during an extended period of probation without any impelling ground, the appointing authority cannot use Reg.
10(2) to pass a termination order.
Additionally, Rule 8(3) gives the appointing authority the power to end the services of a probationer, and no protection can be given to the probationer.
As a result, it was decided that the termination order issued under Reg.
10(2) was ultra vires, and the appointing authority cannot argue that the order was permissible.
