Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 311 and 226 - J&K Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 14 - J&K Public Service Commission (Limitation of Functions) Regulations, 1957, Rule 4 - Ad hoc arrangement - Ad hoc appointment - Ad hoc promotion - Discontinuation of an ad hoc arrangement by appointment or promotion to a higher post is automatic after the prescribed period and does not violate Articles 14, 16 or 311; the rules prohibit continuation beyond the time limit - Ending such an arrangement does not infringe on any right or principle of natural justice - Even if similar arrangements have previously exceeded the prescribed period, discontinuation must be carried out in compliance with the rules.
