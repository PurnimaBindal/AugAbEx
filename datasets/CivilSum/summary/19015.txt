In accordance with Article 16 of the Indian Constitution, the principle of 'first in last out' must be followed when terminating an employee's service.
However, a petitioner who was appointed as a Lekhpal had their service terminated while the services of a junior employee were not.
This violates the rule of first in last out and last in first out, and there is no valid reason for the petitioner's service termination.
As a result, the order in question has been invalidated.
