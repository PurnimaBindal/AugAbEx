Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Assam Police Manual, Parti III - Rules 11(X) and 66 - Appointing Authority - The appellant was appointed by the Principal of Police Training College but was dismissed by the Superintendent of Police who was a co-ordinate authority.
The contention that the order of dismissal was illegal due to being passed by a subordinate authority was rejected.
[Para 9] The court cannot question the relevance of the reasons given by the disciplinary authority for dispensing with a departmental enquiry.
