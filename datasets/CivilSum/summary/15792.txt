Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Extension of service - Benefit of Military Service accorded to second respondent - Petitioner can no longer contest the seniority of the second respondent who was promoted to Inspector General (Prisons) by the Departmental Promotion Committee - Petitioner failed to demonstrate that the promotion of the second respondent was not justified - State Govt. extended services of second respondent for an additional year due to the sensitive nature of the post and administrative demands - State Govt. had the authority to extend the service for one more year and this cannot be considered illegal.
