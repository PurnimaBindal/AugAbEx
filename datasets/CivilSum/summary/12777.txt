Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation for sports category - A benefit must be given to those who participate in activities organized by North Zone.
If a candidate is admitted without certificates indicating North Zone participation, admission need not be upset but the respondent University must admit the candidate who brought the case.
