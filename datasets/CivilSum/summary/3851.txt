As per Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the High Court is not bound to issue a writ of mandamus even if a violation of a right, or even a fundamental right, is complained of regarding retiral benefits due to overstayal in official accommodation.
In cases where the petitioner's own conduct contributed to the problem, the Court may refuse to intervene.
The petitioner may pursue ordinary legal proceedings in Civil Court.
The Court found the petitioner's conduct wanting in the past, potentially causing harassment and embarrassment to the employer after many years of retirement, and declaring the accommodation to be his own.
