Constitution of India, Article 16 - HP Secretariat Class III Service (Recruitment, Promotion and Certain Conditions of Services) Rules, 1973 - Seniority - Clerk in H.P.
Secretariat selected as Assistant in the office of Resident Commissioner in Delhi in 1974 - Integration of employees in the office of Resident Commissioner with the employees of Secretariat done in 1984 - Tribunal granted seniority to appellant from 1980 on equitable considerations due to the delay by government authorities in confirming the post, although services rendered by the appellant before 1984 cannot be counted - Tribunal's order upheld, with no justification for interference.
