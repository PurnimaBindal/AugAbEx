Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 6.24(2) - Pension and Gratuity - Benefit of Revised Pay - Petitioner's pension increase due to revised pay scales not actually received - Argument that petitioner did not join duty before retirement because he was on leave preparatory to retirement - No rule or regulation requiring employee on leave preparatory to retirement to join duty before retirement - Petitioner's case falls under note given underneath sub-rule (2) of Rule 624 of Punjab CSR Volume-II - Ordered to issue revised pension and gratuity - Petition granted.
