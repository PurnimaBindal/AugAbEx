J&K Civil Services Rules, Vol-I, Art. 128 - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Premature retirement - Overstayal of leave - Abandonment of job - Natural justice - Employees cannot be presumed to have abandoned their job without notice and opportunity for an inquiry in accordance with the Rules, even if there are rules or contracts providing for automatic termination due to absences.
Furthermore, services cannot be terminated in violation of principles of natural justice.
[Paras 7 to 9]
