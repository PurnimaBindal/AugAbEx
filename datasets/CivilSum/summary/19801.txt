Cantonment Fund Servants Rules 1937, Rule 11 - Pay - Dismissal - Removal - Jurisdiction - According to the First Proviso to rule 11 of the Cantonment Fund Servants Rules, the Executive Officer has the power to exercise the Board's powers over servants he appointed or those receiving pay below Rs. 100/- per month, in regards to dismissal - For this purpose, pay means basic pay only - Therefore, the Executive Officer can pass a dismissal order.
[Para 4]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Cantonment Fund Servants Rules, 1937, Rule 11 - Enquiry/Dismissal/Punishing Authority - The Executive Officer had the authority to act as the complainant, witness, enquiry officer, and punishing authority for an employee's misconduct - However, it would have been better to follow Rule 11 and have the Board itself take action in this case - Despite having the power to dismiss, the Executive Officer's punishment order was quashed and the petitioner was reinstated with full back wages.
