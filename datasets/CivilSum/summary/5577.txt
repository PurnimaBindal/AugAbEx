Vijaya Bank Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1981 - Vijaya Bank Officer Employees (Conduct) Regulations, 1981, Regulation 3(1) and Regulation 24 - In case of a disagreement with the enquiry officer regarding the delinquent's favorable findings, the disciplinary authority is not required to give notice to the delinquent.
There is no entitlement to a fresh notice.
[Paras 17,19 &10]
