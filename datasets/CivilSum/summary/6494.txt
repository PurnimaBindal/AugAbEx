The University issued a corrigendum to conduct a fresh selection for newly sanctioned seats at a Dental College, to which the petitioners had already qualified for and some were even admitted.
The Information Brochure stated that the admission process would be provisional and the number of seats were subject to change.
The Supreme Court's decision in the case of "Medical Council of India v. Madhu Singh and Others" supports the University's decision, therefore, it cannot be considered arbitrary or unjustified.
