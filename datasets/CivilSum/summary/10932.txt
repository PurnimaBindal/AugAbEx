Constitution of India, Article 20(2) - Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 7 - Termination - An employee was penalized for driving a bus rashly, resulting in two deaths and injuries to others.
As a result of a claim for compensation, the employee was brought to the minimum of the pay scale for four years.
The employee's services were later terminated for further reckless driving under sections 279, 337, 338, and 304-A of I.P.C, which resulted in the employee being punished twice for the same act.
The court held that the termination order was illegal, void, and against natural justice, and the order was set aside.
The employee is entitled to all benefits, such as back wages, seniority, and increments, with the exception of the previous punishment's order.
