According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rules 2.2(b) and 2.2(c), as well as Punjab State Electricity Board Employees (Punishment and Appeal) Regulations, 1971, Regulations 5 and 8, the retirement of an employee cannot be subject to departmental proceedings without the government's approval.
Furthermore, such proceedings cannot relate to events that occurred more than four years prior to their initiation.
