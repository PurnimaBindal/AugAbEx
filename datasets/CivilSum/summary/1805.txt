Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Absence from duty - Ex parte disciplinary proceeding conducted - Order of removal from service and treating absence period as leave without pay passed after show cause notice and filed application under Section 33(2)(b) for approval of the action proposed - Approval refused on the ground that once absence is treated as leave without pay, there is no misconduct left - It is the responsibility of the employee to establish no negligence or lack of interest, unless the leave is sanctioned and granted and absence being undisputed - Although treating the absence period as leave without pay does not wash away the guilt after holding the workman guilty of misconduct, the Labour Court erroneously refused approval on that basis - However, the workman is entitled to wages till the date of declining the refusal or stay of such order by the High Court.
[Paras 14, 20, 25, 27, 29 and 30]
