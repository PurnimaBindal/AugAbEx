Regularisation of Services - The court has ordered that Respondents previously appointed on an ad hoc basis, should have their retrenchment orders set aside and their services regularized.
Some of the Respondents are senior to those who were already given orders of regularisation, which has already been confirmed by the Supreme Court.
The Respondents are entitled to having their services regularized, since previous orders by the Apex Court have already been finalized.
It has been directed that the Appellant Corporation should regularize their services, and the direction for contempt made by the High Court should not be enforced.
[Paras 4 and 6]
