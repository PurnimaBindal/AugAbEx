According to the Constitution of India's Article 16, handicapped employees' retirement age is addressed.
The Haryana Government has laid down instructions on the retirement age of Class-III disabled employees with 70% disability, stating that it should be 60 years instead of 58 years.
Once the authorities of the Cooperative Department adopt these instructions, an employee covered by them cannot be retired before the age of 60.
The authority has been directed to reinstate the petitioner to be retired on attaining the age of 60 years.
