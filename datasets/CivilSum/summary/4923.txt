Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 and 311 - Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rules 4 and 7 - Punishment - Order of recovery - The petitioner, a Record Keeper, along with three colleagues, was responsible for mishandling and delaying the gratuity case of two employees.
A punishment of recovering 1/41th of the interest amount was imposed on him, to be collected in 24 monthly instalments, following an Enquiry Officer's investigation and dismissal of the petitioner's appeal.
The court cannot overrule the Enquiry Officer's conclusions as an appellate court by reconsidering the evidence and arriving at a different finding than the Enquiry Officer.
