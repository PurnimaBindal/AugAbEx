Constitution of India, Articles 14, 310 - Army Act, Section 18 - General Court Martial proceedings - A subsequent writ was filed in the High Court, challenging the judgment of the Full Bench in the case of 'Ex Maj. NR Ajwani v Union of India' as a fresh cause of action.
The High Court granted LPAs on the basis that the respondent withheld material facts justifying action and passed an order for extraneous reasons under the innocuous form of an order of termination.
However, the ground indicated in the writ petitions was not seeking review and High Court had not explained how concluded matters could be reopened.
Persons responsible for alleged manipulation or false implication were not impleaded as parties and writ petitioners had not established conclusions by evidence.
The matter is to be remitted to the High Court for fresh hearing after deciding the preliminary objection raised by appellants about the maintainability of writ petitions.
