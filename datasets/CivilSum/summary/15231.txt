Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Rules, 1996, Rules 289, 290 - Transfer - Sustainability - The State Government instructed Vikas Adhikari to relieve Gram Sewaks to join their new assigned location.
However, if the position is already filled, the employee with longer standing in the position should be relieved, as per Rule 290.
It was held that the transfer order, not meeting the pre-conditions of existence of a vacant post, is a violation of statutory rules, is illegal, and is liable to be rejected.
Additionally, the competent authority alone can apply its mind and pass a transfer order, not a delegated authority.
Thus, the transfer is quashed.
[Paras 13, 15, 16, 19, 21]
