Consumer Protection Act, 1986, Section 16 - Constitution of India, Articles 217 and 223 - Appointment of President of State Commission - The term 'Consultation' in this context refers only to a consultation with the Chief Justice of the High Court, and there is no need to consult with the collegium.
This overruled the decision in AIR 1996 SC 1308.
[Paras 22, 27 and 28] B.
Consumer Protection Act, 1986, Section 16 - Appointment of President of State Commission - The consultation required in this context cannot be equated with the consultation process required for the appointment of a Judge of the High Court.
[Paras 17 and 24] C. Consumer Protection Act, Section 16 - Constitution of India, Article 223 - When appointing the President of the State Commission, consultation with the 'Chief Justice' can be satisfied by consultation with the 'Acting Chief Justice'.
It is not acceptable to assume that an acting Chief Justice is unable to perform the duties expected of them.
