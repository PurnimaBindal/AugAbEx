The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, sections 2(A) and 10 state that individual disputes regarding termination of service would always be considered an industrial dispute.
Despite a workman not completing 240 days of continuous service, the appropriate government should not refuse to refer the matter for adjudication.
The decision is beyond the scope of section 10 and the government should not go into the merits of the controversy.
It falls within the scope of the authority constituted under the Act.
The fact that the workman can claim that Sundays and paid holidays should also be included needs to be taken note of to demonstrate that the government's order is not valid.
Therefore, direction has been given to the State Government to refer the matter for adjudication and pass appropriate orders.
