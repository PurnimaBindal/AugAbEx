Rajasthan Educational Subordinate Service Rules, 1971, Rule 9(3) - Appointment - Post of Teachers Grade III - Candidates appointed from same select list as petitioners against declared vacancies on direction of High Court after select panel validity had lapsed - Petitioners disentitled from challenging non-selection after delay of six years - Their plea that candidature should have been considered along with others not valid, per Apex Court judgment in J Ashok Kumar case.
[Para 10]
Appointment - Candidates from select list - Previous direction of High Court to consider petitioners' candidature must be obeyed as given, but non-consideration of their case cannot be faulted.
