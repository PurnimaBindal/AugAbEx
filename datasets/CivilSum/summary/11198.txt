The petitioner requested a high pay scale based on his acquired qualifications with due permission while serving.
According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, it is up to the government to determine whether the petitioner should receive high pay equal to those with higher qualifications.
[Para 12]

When statutory Service Rules regulate service conditions, rights and privileges must follow those Rules.
This applies to the Haryana Service of Engineers Class II, Public Works Department (Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1970, Rule 16(1) regarding promotions.
[Para 13]

The Haryana Service of Engineers Class II, Public Works Department (Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1970, Rule 16(1) allows officials selected for the course to take study leave and be granted leave of the kind due to them.
The petitioner took advantage of this policy but cannot interpret it differently.
Whether the study period should be viewed as on duty or otherwise is at the government's discretion.
