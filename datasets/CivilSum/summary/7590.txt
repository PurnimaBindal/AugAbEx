Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 & 11A - Delay - Service of Demand notice after a significant 9 years delay - The management raised a specific question before the Labour Court, and the workman did not dispute it - The Labour court cannot leave such an issue unanswered - As there is no reasonable explanation provided for the considerable delay, the claim of the workman should be dismissed - The Labour Court is not authorized to determine on its own the justification of delay - Such an award is not sustainable.
