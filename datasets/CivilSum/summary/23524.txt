Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Additional charge of administrative duties - The seniormost doctor in specialist Grade-I performs additional administrative duties as Medical Superintendent which is purely an administrative function - There is no specific cadre post as Medical Superintendent of the hospital under the administration of Delhi Municipal Corporation nor it is a promotional post - When another senior doctor in the specialities is appointed in the exigency of Medical Services, that senior doctor is entitled to perform the administrative functions in addition to his duties towards medical services of the Corporation - Medical Superintendent and other specialist Grade-I remain in the same grade and draw the same pay scale except a special allowance - The petitioner cannot claim as a matter of right to continue as Medical Superintendent once appointed.
[Paras 17, 18 and 20]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administration action - Fairness of administrative action - Court will not interfere if the administrative action is not unfair, arbitrary, lacking in reasonableness and tainted with mala fide - Administrative action cannot be allowed to sustain when taken without any reasonable basis and not in the exigency of administration but merely on the caprice and ipsi dixit of the concerned authority and actuated by mala fide intention - Such action is violative of Article 14 of the Constitution.
[Para 20]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Necessary parties - Mala fide - Allegations of mala fide levelled - Persons concerned not impleaded as parties - Such allegations cannot be gone into in the absence of the persons concerned.
