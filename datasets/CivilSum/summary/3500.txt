Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - West Bengal Board of Secondary Education Act, 1963 - Appointment - Regularization of service - Appointment of organizer staff is against the Act - Appointment made by a managing committee that is not validly constituted lacks authority of law - If the initial appointment is unlawful, it cannot be approved or regularized at a later point - The appeal is dismissed due to the failure to prove a legal right for regularization of the alleged appointment.
[Paras 26 to 28]
