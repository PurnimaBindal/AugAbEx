Panjab University Calendar Vol. III, Chapter 14(g) Para 76(19), Page 249 - A student of the 3rd Semester of law may migrate to another college affiliated with the same University.
However, the contention that migration is only allowed if the petitioner pays the entire fee of the 3rd Semester and becomes a student of the college is unreasonable and not required by law.
The petitioner should not be asked to pay fees for both the 3rd and 4th Semester in the previous college and then pay the same amount again to Panjab University.
The court allowed the petition.
[Paras 7, 8 and 9]
