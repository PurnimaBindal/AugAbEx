Article 226 of the Constitution of India pertains to retirement based on date of birth.
In a case concerning a petitioner, their date of birth was recorded in the service book based on a certificate from the Lambardar of the village.
However, the respondent changed their date of birth on the basis of a certificate from a civil surgeon who determined their age based on appearance.
The court quashed the order of retirement, citing that it was based on the petitioner's appearance.
The court also directed the petitioner to be re-examined by a civil surgeon, including an ossification test, and to convey their age to the department.
It was noted that the respondent can approach the Court again to raise any relevant points.
