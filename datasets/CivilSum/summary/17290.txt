The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, state that pay revision and increments must be given.
Petitioners who worked as Data Entry Operators in the Computer Centre had their pay revised on the recommendation of the Central Government, effective from 1191989.
However, the decision of the Goa Government to implement the scale from 15.1.1992 is arbitrary, and there is no justification as to why pay scales were not given effect from 1191989.
The petitioners are entitled to an increment in the revised pay scales based on the date on which the increment was due to them in the old scale, and they do not need to wait for a period of 12 months.
