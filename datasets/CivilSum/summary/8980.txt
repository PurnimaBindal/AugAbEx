The appointment of an employee on an ad hoc basis cannot continue past the maximum period specified in the appointment letter.
Sant Ram Behl v. State of Haryana, 1992(1) SCT 534(P&H) (FB) : 1992(1) RSJ 761 Foll. [Para 8] B Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - An employee appointed on an ad hoc basis has no right to continue in the post he was appointed to.
[Para 8] C If the replacing of one ad hoc appointee with another is to occur, then the petitioner will be appointed to the post as an ad hoc employee instead of another candidate until a regular candidate is chosen.
The petitioner has no right to remain in the position after the appointment term ends on 2.10.1994.
It is directed that respondents expedite the selection process and appoint a duly selected candidate on a regular basis.
