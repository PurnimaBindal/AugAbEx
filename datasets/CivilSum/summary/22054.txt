A workman governed by the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 cannot claim lay-off compensation under Section 25-C after a settlement is reached during conciliation proceedings by a union, according to Sections 25-C, 12(3) and 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
Settlements restrict the right of lay-off compensation under the first proviso to Section 25-C and such settlements are binding even for future workers, as per Section 18(3)(d).
Since a lay-off imposed by an employer affects the entire body of workers, their grievance regarding lay-off compensation cannot be an individual grievance.
A settlement arrived at during conciliation proceedings becomes an agreement between the parties and all workers are bound by that.
Additionally, no individual worker is entitled to claim additional benefits beyond the settlement under Section 33-C(2) of the Act.
In terms of Section 12(3) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, all settlements are based on written agreements between the parties.
However, all agreements may not necessarily be settlements until the procedure for giving them status of settlements is followed, and they may not have binding effect as settlements until then.
