Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39(b) - Equal pay for equal work - An employee who was given an appointment but not given a regular grade due to a pending complaint filed in 1973 - Denial of regular grade based on this complaint is not valid when similarly situated employees were given the grades - Direction given for the concerned employee to receive the regular grade in the same way as those who were similarly situated.
[Paras 7 to 10] Note: Opinion expressed in Amresh Kumar v. The Dena Laxmi Bai National College of Physical Education and ors., 1995(1) SCT 92.
