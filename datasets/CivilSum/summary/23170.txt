Bank of India Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976, Regulations 7 and 24 - Disciplinary proceedings - Enquiry report - If the Disciplinary Authority disagrees with an enquiry report, the Authority must record reasons for the disagreement - If there is sufficient evidence on record, the Authority can record its own finding, but if not, it must remit the case to Enquiry Officer for further enquiry and report.
[Para 10] B Departmental Enquiry - Evidence - Departmental enquiry proceedings are not subject to strict rules of evidence - Allegations must be establishable by evidence on which a reasonable person could arrive at a finding supporting disciplinary authority's charge - A court can only interfere if there is no evidence to support the finding, or if the finding could not have been arrived at by a reasonable person on such evidence - Court's powers of judicial review within writ jurisdiction is limited.
[Paras 11 and 12] C. Promotion - Disciplinary proceedings - Criminal proceedings - Sealed cover procedure - Sealed cover procedure is only applicable if the charge-sheet has been delivered or if some criminal proceedings are pending - Allegations relating to an earlier period cannot be considered - Bank employee, having been acquitted from charges, is entitled to promotion from the date of his entitlement, as the sealed cover procedure could not have been invoked in this case.
