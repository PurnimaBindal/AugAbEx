The petitioner in admission to a post graduate degree/diploma course was denied a weightage of 5 marks due to an improperly formatted domicile certificate and it not being issued by the General Assistant to the District Magistrate.
However, the certificate produced was similar to the required format with minor changes, and since the office of General Assistant to the Deputy Commissioner was renamed as City Magistrate, the certificate issued by the City Magistrate can be considered to have been issued by the General Assistant to the Deputy Commissioner.
Therefore, the petitioner should be allowed to proceed with their admission.
[Paras 5, 6 and 8]
