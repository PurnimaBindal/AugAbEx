The Payment of Gratuity Act, Section 4, 7(7), 4(6)(b), 2, 2(a)(1)(b) and Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 21 regulate the payment of gratuity and interest.
A respondent filed an application under Section 4 of the Act for payment of gratuity with interest.
The Controller authority appointed by the State rejected it on the grounds of delay.
However, the Appellate Authority allowed the application and directed the Controller Authority to reconsider relevant issues.
The Controller Authority later condoned the delay and held the respondent entitled to receive gratuity along with interest.
This decision was challenged and it was argued that the Controller Authority had no jurisdiction to decide the matter.
The court held that, on an appropriate interpretation of provision of Section 2 of the Act, the authority did not have jurisdiction to decide the matter.
Therefore, the orders passed were quashed, and the respondent's application was transferred to the controlling authority appointed by the Central Government for decision.
