argue that disability pensions may only be granted to individuals who are invalided from service on account of a disability attributable to or aggravated by military service and assessed at 20% or over, unless specifically provided otherwise.
The petitioner was medically discharged with a disability re-assessed at less than 20% (11-14%), resulting in the discontinuation of their disability pension in accordance with Army Rules.
The court finds no fault with the impugned order and dismisses the petition.
