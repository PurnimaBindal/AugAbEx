Industrial Disputes Act, Section 33-C(2) - Computation of wages - Application for - Dispute regarding employment status of Anganwadi workers/helpers - The very status of the appellants as Anganwadi workers is under dispute, and until this is resolved, there can be no claim for wages at the specified rates under Section 33-C(2).
Hence, the applications are deemed not maintainable.
