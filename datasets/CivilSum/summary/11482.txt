The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb), 17(B), 25B, 25F states that termination of service for workman must meet specific requirements.
In this case, the labour court ordered that the workman was to be reinstated since the provisions of the Act were not complied with at the time of termination.
The petitioner's contention that the services could be terminated due to the workman being appointed on probation on a temporary basis and being junior-most is invalid since there is no evidence to show seniority and is therefore deemed an unfair trade practice.
The award is upheld.
