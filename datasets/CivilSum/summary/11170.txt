Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Natural Justice Absence from duty - Willful absence - Termination - Enquiry - Appellant failed to attend duty - Registered notices and newspaper notices issued - Petitioner summoned to attend duty but did not respond - Termination order passed without following the principles of Natural Justice - No charge sheet or enquiry held - Termination invalid and against principles of Natural Justice - Termination quashed.
[Para]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Back wages benefits of service - Willful absence - Equity - Plaintiff not entitled to back wages and benefits of past service.
[Para 9]
The decision to quash the order was made based on legal grounds only.
The plaintiff was absent willfully for a portion of time, and it would be unfair to pay him for the period of absence and any period following termination until his reinstatement.
Therefore, the plaintiff will be reinstated without receiving any back wages.
