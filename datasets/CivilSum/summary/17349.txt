According to the Constitution of India, Article 14, Navy (Pension) Regulations of 1964, Regulations 120, 123, 124, and 125, the Entitlement Rules to Casualty Pensionary Awards to the Armed Forces Personnel of 1982, and Para 13, if an armed forces personnel takes casual leave or short liberty while on active duty, they are still considered to be on active duty.
Under the Government of India Notification No. SRO 8-E dated 5121962 issued under Section 9 of the Army Act, the father of a battle casualty who died during such a period is entitled to receive the grant of a special pension.
