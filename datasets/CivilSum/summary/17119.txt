Under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, eligibility for a Disability Pension is discussed - The petitioner had served in the Army after being medically examined and deemed fit - After being discharged, a Medical Board evaluated the petitioner's disability as 40% permanent - The respondent authorities had failed to provide evidence of the petitioner's unspecified psychosis during enlistment - It was ordered that the petitioner should receive disability pension from the date of his discharge.
