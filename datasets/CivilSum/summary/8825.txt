Bombay Primary Education Rules, 1949 - Appointment - The petitioner was selected for the position of Assistant Teacher with only an S.S.C.
qualification.
Later on, the petitioner improved her qualifications by graduating and passing the B.Ed.
examination in 1978.
However, a government circular from 1976 stated that individuals with a Bachelor in Education degree should not be appointed as primary teachers.
The court ruled that the petitioner's B.Ed.
degree was on par with the PTC qualification and could not be withheld from her.
[Paras 2 and 6] B.
Termination - Natural justice - The Director of Education made observations against the petitioner's appointment while deciding another teacher's case.
The petitioner was terminated without notice or a hearing, in violation of the principles of natural justice.
The termination was quashed by the court.
