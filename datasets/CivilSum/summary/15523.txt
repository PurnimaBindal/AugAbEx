Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Reservation - Petitioners and the Respondent had joined as Inspectors and were later promoted to Deputy Director and Joint Director respectively on the basis of reservation; Respondent was promoted earlier than Petitioners - Respondent subsequently was promoted to Additional Director Factories prior to Petitioners based on the principle that he had joined as Joint Director before them.
Petitioners argued that no panel was formulated nor any DPC was constituted.
The court found that Respondent was appointed without following the rules and procedure.
Respondent retired in May 1999 and Petitioner No. 1 was appointed by DPC in April 2001.
Petitioner No. 2 had passed away.
The court directed the respondents to consider 31st May, 1999 as the effective date of promotion for Petitioner along with corresponding financial benefits.
