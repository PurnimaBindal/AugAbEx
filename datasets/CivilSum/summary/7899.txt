Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Reappraisal - The Petitioner was empanelled by Promotion Board but later was de-empanelled due to negative traits in a fresh ACR - Negative comments given to the Petitioner are not to be communicated - The decision in UP Jal Nigam case is not applicable to this case of defense services - Since empanelment was subject to continuity of performance, no grievance can be made.
