Constitution of India, Article 16, 226 and 311 - Promotion - Confidential reports - Adverse remarks - Petitioner, who was passed over for promotion from Inspector Grade II to Inspector Grade I in favor of his juniors, was aggrieved - Petitioner argued that he was to be considered for promotion within the same rank, not from one rank to another - Adverse remarks relating to the year 1981 were not communicated, so they could not be considered against the petitioner - Adverse remarks for the year 1982 were not communicated in full, and after a considerable lapse of time had remained unexplained, so they may be used to ignore the petitioner - The petitioner was wrongly ignored and left out of consideration for promotion - Respondents have been instructed to consider the petitioner for promotion while disregarding Confidential Reports for the years 1981 and 1982.
