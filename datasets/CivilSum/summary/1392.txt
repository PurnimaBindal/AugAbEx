Ritu Bahri, AAG, Punjab, argues that the denial of monetary benefits to the petitioners despite their retrospective promotions is without legal or constitutional sanction and infringes upon their right of equality before the law.
The Punjab Government National Emergency (Concessions) Rules, 1965 state that the petitioners are entitled to the benefit of military service for purposes of seniority, increments and pensions, and by passing orders, the respondents have given them benefit of retrospective seniority and promotion to higher posts with retrospective effect.
The court has directed respondents to pay all monetary benefits to which the petitioners are entitled as a result of their retrospective promotions.
