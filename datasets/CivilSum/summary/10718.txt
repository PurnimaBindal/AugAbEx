Constitution of India, Article 311 - Bank of Baroda Officers Employees' (Discipline & Appeal) Regulations, 1976, Regulations 4, 7 and 5(3) - Dismissal from service - The appellant was dismissed from service due to allegations of dishonest transactions that were proven by the Enquiry Officer's report.
The Disciplinary Authority agreed with the Enquiry Officer's report and issued the order of dismissal.
Despite the appellant's appeal, the penalty of dismissal was deemed justified based on the seriousness of the charges.
