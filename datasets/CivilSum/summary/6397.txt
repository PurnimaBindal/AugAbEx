Police Act, 1861, Section 7 - Misconduct - Dismissal of a Constable due to misconduct - The lady constable was dismissed on charges of being found drunk and sleeping with a guest in the same bed at her residence.
However, there were no allegations that her behavior had any impact on her official function or that she was on duty during the relevant time.
Therefore, her conduct in her private life cannot be regarded as misconduct, and her dismissal was set aside.
