According to Section 13 of the Army Act and Army Rule 13(3)(iv), an individual can be discharged from service based on recorded answers against questions contained in the enrolment form.
If an appellant makes a false statement at the time of enrolment, such as claiming that no criminal case is pending against them, and this is later found out to be untrue, then a discharge is warranted.
Discrepancies between the enrolment form and counter affidavit are not sufficient grounds to contest a discharge, nor is there reason to doubt the correctness of the notice serving process.
This results in the dismissal of the appellant's appeal.
