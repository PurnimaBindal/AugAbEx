Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 173 and Para 7(b) of Appendix II - Disability pension - Military personnel with epilepsy and no pre-existing disease at the time of entry cannot be denied disability pension if it can be attributable or aggravated from military service - Instant treatment of any decease or abnormality of physical condition in the sanitised atmosphere of cantonment cannot be inferred.

Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 173 and Para 7(b) of Appendix II - Disability pension - Delay of 20 years cannot be a ground to deny disability pension - Benefit may be limited to a period of 38 months for arrears only.
