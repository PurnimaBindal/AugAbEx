Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Act, 1979 - Appointment - Approval of - Qualification - Termination - Post of Hindi Teacher - The petitioner claims to have been qualified for the position of Hindi Teacher as per the requirements of the job, which were Matriculation, Prabakar and OT The District Education Officer denied approval on the grounds that the OT qualification acquired by the petitioner was not recognised by the Government.
The respondent's argument is that the petitioner has passed the LT Examination which is not equivalent to the OT certificate recognised by the Government.
The petitioner's failure to challenge the termination order means the order is not liable to be set aside.
Additionally, the petitioner failed to demonstrate that the LT qualification was equivalent to the OT qualification recognised by the Government.
As a result, the petitioner is not eligible for any relief.
[Paras 8 to 10]
