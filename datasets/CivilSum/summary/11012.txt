As per Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act, a `workman' is someone who is under the control and supervision of the employer.
In the case of Haryana State Handloom and Handicrafts Corporation, they set up factories and training centers and provided a market for the goods prepared by trainees at home.
However, the corporation had no control or supervision over their working.
When the corporation faced losses, it closed down certain units, leading to the weavers seeking benefits of retrenchment.
However, they were not considered `workmen' as they did not satisfy the tests laid down by the apex court in various decisions.
