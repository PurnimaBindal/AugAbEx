Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 311(2) - Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 7(6) - Natural justice - For fair treatment, employees must be provided with a copy of the inquiry report and any dissenting note from the punishing authority - In cases where the inquiry officer and punishing authority are different individuals, show cause notices must be accompanied by a copy of the inquiry report and any dissenting notes so that the employee can effectively defend themselves.
