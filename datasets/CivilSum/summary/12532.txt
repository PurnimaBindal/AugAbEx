In accordance with Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Oil India Limited Executive Conduct, Discipline and Appeal Rules, Rules 523 to 27, disciplinary proceedings and the imposition of major penalties on a petitioner must be carried out by the competent authority, who in this case is the Chairman and Managing Director; the General Manager, being a subordinate, is not authorized to take such actions, thus making the departmental proceedings and punishment order passed on the basis of such inquiry void ab initio.
[Para 14] B.
As per Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 311 - Oil India Limited Executive Conduct, Discipline and Appeal Rules, Rules 5, 23 to 27 and Companies Act, Section 617 - a person holding a public post can only act as an Inquiry Officer while serving as a public servant, and therefore, after their superannuation, cannot act in the capacity of Public Servant; thus, re-appointment on a contract basis to the same post and with the same designation does not change their status as per the law.
