The Tribunal determined that the misconduct did not warrant the punishment of dismissal and as a result, the Petitioner was reinstated without backwages and the stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect.
However, the Petitioner was content with receiving 25% of backwages.
The award was modified to reflect that the Petitioner is entitled to 25% of backwages from the date of dismissal until their reinstatement, and the order of the Learned Tribunal to stop two increments with cumulative effect was overturned.
