The person's right to receive a pension is considered property under Article 19(1)(f) and Article 31(1).
Although it is not saved by Article 19(5), the State cannot withhold it by mere executive order.
[Para 5] B.
Retirement benefits, including pension, are an earned right that cannot be denied and become legitimately claimable immediately after retirement.
The attitude of indifference towards the petitioner's lack of retirement benefits cannot be forgiven.
The employee must receive adequate compensation, and their retirement benefits must be paid within three months after retirement.
