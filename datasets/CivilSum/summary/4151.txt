The State Government has amended the Mizoram Public Service Commission (Limitation of Function) Regulation 1994, withdrawing all cases of appointment for Group B (Non-Gazetted) Posts from the purview of Public Service Commission.
The Government has the power to hand over and withdraw subjects from the MPSC and other authorities, and this power does not get exhausted.
[Para 5]

Recruitment in Group B non-gazetted services can be made solely on the basis of viva voce, in accordance with Article 16 of the Constitution of India.
