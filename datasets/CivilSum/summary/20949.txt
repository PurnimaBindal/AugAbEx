Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Ad hoc and temporary service - Fortuitous appointment - An employee seeking regularisation from the date of initial fortuitous appointment was granted relief by Civil Court and High Court approved the order.
However, both courts committed error in fixing seniority from the date of fortuitous appointment instead of proper selection.
Seniority must be fixed from the date of proper selection.
