Constitution of India, Article 16 - Kerala Transport Subordinate Service Special Rules, Rule 4(b), Note 2 - Appointment - Selection - Post of Assistant Motor Vehicle Inspector - The petitioner did not meet the essential qualification on the last date of application submission but acquired it afterwards.
An unqualified candidate cannot claim appointment solely based on their community.
The inclusion of the petitioner's name in the ranked list does not guarantee the right to appointment unless they meet the required eligibility conditions for the post.
