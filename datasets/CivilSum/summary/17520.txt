Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Ad-hoc appointment against leave vacancy - Teachers/Lecturers appointed against leave vacancies only for the period the classes/studies take place at the institution - Regular teachers are entitled to their salary and all emoluments while on leave - It is not unlawful to restrict appointments against leave vacancies only for the period the classes are held - The institution cannot be forced to pay double salary during the off season.
[Para 2] Rattan Lal and others v. State of Haryana and others, 1985(3) SLR 548.
