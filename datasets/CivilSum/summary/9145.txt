According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, Rule 6 of the Gujarat Civil Services Classification and Recruitment General Rule, and Rule 5 of the Gujarat Service of Engineers Class I Rules, 1979, a person serving as a Supervisor in the service, who applies for appointment to a higher post, is entitled to age relaxation.
The expression "only such posts" in Rule 8(5) of General Recruitment Rules cannot mean that the same qualification is required for both the post held by the applicant and the higher post applied for, as per the 1986 GLH 352 ruling.
