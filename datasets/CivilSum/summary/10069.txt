Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment to the post of clerk in 1981 and seniority fixed - The petitioner is barred from challenging the seniority at a belated stage - Things that have been settled cannot be unsettled, especially given the inordinate delay in raising a challenge to the seniority list - Such a challenge could potentially cause prejudice to those who have already taken the seniority.
