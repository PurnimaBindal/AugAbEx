Constitution of India, Article 16 - Termination of Service - Loss of Confidence - A report of embezzlement against a delinquent must be enquired into and the delinquent must be given an opportunity to respond.
If a finding is recorded that the person is not fit to be retained, the employer is not obligated to reappoint the person.
In the case where a Single Judge does not interfere with the finding of the inquiry officer and declines to compel the employer to retain the petitioner, the view of the Single Judge does not require interference.
[Paras 3 and 5]
