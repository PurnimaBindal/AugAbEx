Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 227 - Retiral benefits - Discrimination - Notification allowing benefits to retirees retiring after 3131985 - Petitioner who retired prior to 3131985 seeking benefits under the orders on the basis of parity - Held that employees retired before and after 3131985 constitute different class of employees - Challenge to the action of the respondents in not allowing the benefit and fixing of cut off as invalid, arbitrary and not sustainable rejected.
