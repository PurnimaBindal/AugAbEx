The Punjab Civil Medical Service's in-service candidate quota for the MdE Post Graduate Degree Course cannot include a medical officer working at Punjab Agriculture University Ludhiana, as the University's autonomous status means the State Government does not have control over its employees and its service rules cannot be treated as government service.
[Paras 6 and 9] B.
The government has the power to make reasonable classification and relaxation of conditions for admission to the MdE course based on rural service, which aims to encourage qualified doctors to serve rural people.
The petitioner, who did not complete three years of rural service, is not entitled to admission.
[Paras 7 and 8] C. The reservation by relaxation of the requirement of three years rural service for those who served in Border Areas for admission to the Post Graduate MdE Degree Course does not give others the right to claim similar relaxation.
