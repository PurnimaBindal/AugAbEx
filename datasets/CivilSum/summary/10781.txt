Constitution of India, Article 226 - Exam for improvement - Permission to appear - The Petitioner's request to take improvement exams in all subjects including an additional paper was declined by the Respondents citing rules that allow for improvement exams to be taken either in all or an additional subject.
However, the court held that the Petitioner is entitled to take exams in all subjects, as per Rule 6, which only restricts a candidate from changing their additional subject in the succeeding year.
