Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Writ jurisdiction - The Labour Court based its finding of fact on record and it was not unjustified or without evidence - The High Court cannot reevaluate evidence that has already been taken into account by the Labour Court - The High Court should not act as an appellate court over the Labour Court's factual findings while exercising writ jurisdiction under Articles 226/227 of Constitution - No need to interfere with the award granted by the Labour Court.
