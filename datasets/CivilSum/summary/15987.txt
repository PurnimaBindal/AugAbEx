Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Instructions - Seniority - Promotion - The rules may be substituted with instructions by the competent authority if the rules do not specify the promotion criteria or if the authority wishes to prescribe certain eligibility conditions for promotion - Once instructions are issued, they can be altered by issuing subsequent instructions - Subsequent instructions relaxed the requirement of three years' experience, and the petitioners were promoted accordingly - The promotion orders cannot be revised subsequently to only be effective from when the promotees completed three years of service - Actions taken by the competent authority in relaxation of conditions are final.
