Article 14 of the Constitution of India deals with selection and ad hoc appointments.
In the case of a petitioner applying for the post of Research Engineer at a university, he was properly considered by the Selection Committee but not recommended.
His appointment on an ad hoc basis did not give him an indefeasible right to be appointed on a regular basis to the post.
[Paras 5 and 7]
Article 226 of the Constitution of India states that appointments to senior faculty positions in a university must be made by proper selection.
If appointments are made without proper selection by advertising the posts, it can be considered illegal and may be questioned.
However, candidates in the future cannot claim as a right to do away with the process of selection by advertising the posts.
[Para 8]
Article 226 of the Constitution of India also deals with selection and selection committees.
A petitioner who competed without any protest regarding the constitution of the Selection Committee cannot challenge it after finding himself not selected.
A point that was not raised in the writ petition cannot be allowed to be raised in arguments.
[Para 9]
