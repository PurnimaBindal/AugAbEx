Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 311 - Mecon Service Regulations Regulation 8.1.5 - Absence from duty/Termination/Automatic termination/Natural justice - Even though an Engineer in the Mecon Metallurgical & Engineering Consultants is not a Civil servant and provisions of Article 311 of the Constitution do not apply, Mecon Metallurgical and Engineering Consultants (India) Limited is considered a State under Article 12 of the Constitution and cannot exclude principles of natural justice - Termination without opportunity to show cause in compliance with minimum requirement of natural justice, even if the service regulations allow automatic termination on overstay of leave or unauthorised leave, is illegal - Regulation 8.1.5 merely provides for termination on the grounds of unauthorised absence but does not exclude the principles of natural justice - Order quashed.
