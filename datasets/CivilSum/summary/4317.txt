The claim for selection grade by Library Restorers relying on circular letters No. 8/30/78 -FR/5/1845 dated March 3, 1980 and 7/25/84-5FR/11160 dated October 29, 1984, which are just guidelines for Heads of Departments, has been dismissed as they do not confer any rights to employees for the granting of selection grade.
The previous ruling in Chaman Lal's case (L.P.A No. 117 of 1990 decided on April 3, 1991) stands overruled.
