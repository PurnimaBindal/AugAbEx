Constitution of India, Article 226 - West Bengal Service Rules, Part I, Rules 42 and 55(4) - Locus standi - The petitioners who challenged the order dated 29th April, 1993 regarding personal pay have no locus standi as they are neither directly nor indirectly affected by the impugned order.
The legality of the action can only be challenged by the aggrieved person.
Third party cannot question the correctness of the action.
The tribunal's findings that the petitioners had no locus standi to maintain the writ petition were upheld.
