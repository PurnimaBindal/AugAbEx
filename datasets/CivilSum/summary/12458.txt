Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25N - Retrenchment - Compensation - The Petitioner was not compensated for the period of notice before retrenchment as required by Section 25N The retrenchment orders of various other workers at the same workplace were set aside by the Labour Court.
The Respondent did not file a written statement despite the case being pending for over eight years, indicating negligence in defending the case.
The State Government's permission was not obtained as required by Section 25N and the Petitioner was not paid any salary or compensation.
The retrenchment order was revoked after the Petitioner agreed to the Respondent's offer to forgo backwages.
The Petitioner is entitled to receive salary from the date she resumes work.
