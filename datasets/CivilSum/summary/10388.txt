The orders declaring him a 'deserter' and dismissing him from service after three years of desertion are declared invalid as he had no intention of not returning to his unit against any service conditions.
The husband's absence from service due to his mental illness from a particular time until completion of his full service was beyond his control, therefore he is entitled to all the benefits of his full service.
The widow is also entitled to family pension according to the rules.
