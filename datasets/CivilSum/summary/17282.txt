Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Medical Registration - Cancellation of - Domicile certificate - Genuinity of - A petitioner who completed a four-year BAMS Course in 1990 and registered as an Ayurvedic Practitioner in 1991 had their candidature rejected in 1993 due to a suspected forged domicile certificate.
The certificate was later verified by Halqa Patwari and Tehsildar as genuine.
Additionally, exam cancellations cannot be made after the completion of a course.
As a result, the petitioner has been directed to receive their BAMS degree.
