Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) & 25-F - Completion of 240 days in terms of Section 2(oo) - The workman must demonstrate the completion of 240 days - The employer provided Muster Rolls only for the period when the workman actually worked - Failure to produce Muster Rolls for any other period, without any court direction, will not be viewed negatively against the employer.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(ra) - Unfair Labour practices - The Labour Court must be informed and given evidence of such practices - Filing a writ petition for this reason cannot be done for the first time.
