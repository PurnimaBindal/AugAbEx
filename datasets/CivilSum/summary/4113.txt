Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - A.P.
Judicial Ministerial Service Rules, Rule 8 - Promotion - Selection - The reasons for neither selection nor non-selection must be provided - Suitable candidates for promotion to the position of Head Clerk in Distt.
Munsif's Court under Category 3 of Division IV are selected on the basis of merit-cum-ability, with seniority being considered only in instances of equivalent merit and ability - Interviews are the only means of selection - Candidates have no inherent right to promotion but are obligated to be considered - Despite being senior, the petitioner was not selected while his junior was - There is no evidence to suggest that the petitioner has a more distinguished or superior service record than his chosen counterpart - The petitioner demanded money from complainants, resulting in him being chastised with a stoppage of one increment - There was no evidence of misconduct against the selected junior - The petitioner cannot demand equality with his junior and cry discrimination - Those found guilty of discipline violations must be treated differently from others.
