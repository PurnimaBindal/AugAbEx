The respondent-plaintiff had been performing the duties of DRRO/PRO from May 5th, 1982, and had been promoted to the position of DPRO since June 9th, 1989.
The plaintiff claimed promotion and salary from May 5th, 1982.
It was ruled that the plaintiff is eligible to receive the salary of the DPRO post for the time that he performed the duties of that post, but he would not be conferred with the status of DPRO.
The decree of the Additional District Judge was modified to the extent that the plaintiff would not be considered promoted to the DPRO post from May 5th, 1982, but he would be entitled only to the salary of the post during the period he discharged his duties.
