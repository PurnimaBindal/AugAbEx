According to Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 13.8, the name of a petitioner cannot be removed from promotion list C-II without any opportunity of being heard.
This is in violation of the principle of Audi alteram partem, as the petitioner was not given the opportunity to be heard before being removed from the list.
[Paras 14 to 16]
As per Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 13.8, a constable brought on List C-II cannot be promoted to the rank of Head Constable beyond 10% of the quota of the Cadre.
[Paras 14 to 16]
Promotion beyond the quota is not permissible as per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, and Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 13, 21 and 18, 8(1) & (2), Lists C-I and C-II.
The rules prescribe a maximum quota for promotion among Constables who do not fulfill the prescribed conditions for promotion.
Candidates available on promotion List C-I have a legitimate expectation of promotion to the rank of Head Constable, and this cannot be encroached upon by exercising power under Rule 1321 in excess of the prescribed quota for those on List C-II.
[Paras 7 to 13]
Bringing a candidate's name on Lists C-I and C-II does not amount to promotion according to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, and Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 13, 21 and 18, 8(1) & (2), Lists C-I and C-II.
Rules provide different quota from those on Lists C-I and C-II.
The quota rule is to be followed at the time of promotion only.
The rules also prescribe considerations for bringing on List C-II.
The name of a candidate cannot be removed from List C-II merely for being in excess of the prescribed promotion quota for the same.
If the name is to be removed for any ground, the candidate must be given the opportunity to show cause.
