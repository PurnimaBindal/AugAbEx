As per Section 23(1) of the All India Council for Technical Education Act, 1987, guidelines have been issued allowing for lateral entry of diploma holders at the second year, third semester level in Engineering Degree Programs.
The appellant-College in the State of Himachal Pradesh has not yet adopted a comprehensive scheme for admitting diploma holders to the degree program in the second year.
The High Court had directed the appellant to admit respondents, who are diploma holders, to the second year of the degree course, disregarding the necessary concomitants prescribed by AICTE.
This direction goes against AICTE's guidelines and is therefore set aside.
