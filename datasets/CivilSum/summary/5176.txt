Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961, Section 79 - Service matters of conditions of service of employees of Society - Advance notice - If the subject matter of a dispute in a suit does not concern the business of the society, advance notice under Section 79 of the Act is not mandatory.
[Para 11] B. Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961, Section 55(1)(b) - Reference of disputes to Arbitration - Service matters of conditions of service of employees of Society - When a matter does not concern the business of the society, it is not liable to be referred to arbitration.
Under Section 55(1)(b) of the Act, civil suits are not barred and demands for dearness allowance would fall under the category of matters that "touch upon the business of the Society."
