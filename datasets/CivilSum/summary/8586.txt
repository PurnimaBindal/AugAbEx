Indian Overseas Bank Officers Employees' Discipline and Appeal Regulations, 1976, Sub-regulation 6 of Regulation 6 - Enquiry - If enquiry officer takes on the role of presenting officer, enquiry is flawed - The Enquiry Officer cross examined and asked leading questions of witnesses, demonstrating that they were acting not only as enquiry officer but also as prosecutor - demonstrates bias, which is against the rules of natural justice and fair play - The disciplinary authority based its action on such a report of flawed proceedings, therefore the action should be nullified.
