The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Temporary promotion - Reversion - Reversion of a temporary promotion that was not made according to the seniority rules does not violate any rights under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
[Para 11]
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Quota for promotion - Temporary promotion of a candidate to a post outside of their quota - Reversion of such a promotee does not constitute a violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
