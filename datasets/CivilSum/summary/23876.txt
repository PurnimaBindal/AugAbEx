Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Uniform allowance - Judicial Officers - The reasons for providing uniforms for peons, chaprasis, police constables, drivers, etc. are entirely different than for Judicial Officers as they occupy a different and identifiable position.
Uniforms may be prescribed for some decorum and dignity of the court.
Uniform allowance is not an essential condition of service for Judicial Officers.
[Paras 2 and 9]

Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 226 - Conditions of service - Judicial Officers - Uniform allowance - The service conditions and pay and allowances are to be determined by the expert body set up as National Judicial Pay Commission.
The State can grant special allowances to Judicial Officers as a matter of policy, but the High Court cannot issue a writ of mandamus to direct the State to grant kit maintenance allowance or revise it, as it is not an essential condition of service.
[Paras 7 to 10]
