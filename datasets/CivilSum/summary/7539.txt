Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25F - Termination - Retrenchment compensation - For Section 2(oo)(bb) to apply, there needs to be a contract between the management and workman, which includes an offer and acceptance - Daily wage workers whose services are extended without proper communication or completion of paperwork do not have a valid contract - The labor court was justified in ruling that retrenchment violated Section 25F [Paras 8 and 9]  B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(1) and 25F - Delay and laches - An 8-year delay in issuing a demand notice does not justify challenging the reference of a dispute to the labor court - A writ petition against the validity of the reference order due to delay cannot be filed after a significant period has passed.
