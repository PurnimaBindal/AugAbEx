The certificate of deposit of the amount payable under an award must be included when appealing under sections 8 and 30(A) proviso of the Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923.
The powers of the appellate forum under section 30(A) undermine the deposit and disbursement contemplated under section 8.
The appellate jurisdiction controls the deposit and disbursement, and there is no inherent right of appeal except the one created by the statute.
[Para 10]

The appeal shall lay under Section 30(1) Third proviso of the Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923 when it is entertained for consideration, and not at the stage when the memorandum of appeal is presented.
The expression "lie" indicates "to entertain" or "to admit to consideration."
[Para 13]

The question of non-compliance of the proviso arises when there is tangible proof of the deposit required under the law, and the dispute is examined judicially by appellate court, not at the stage of presenting the memorandum of appeal.
The appeal under section 30 will not lie unless there is a substantial question of law.
[Para 19]

The memorandum of appeal contains the grounds on which the judicial examination of the dispute is invited in Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923.
[Para 19]

Before the appeal is entertained for consideration, there should be a deposit of the amount awarded by the Commissioner under Section 30(1) Third proviso of the Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923.
The appellate court under section 30(A) can deal with it.
