Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rule 5 - Retiral benefits - Suspension at the time of retirement - In the case of an employee who has already been acquitted by a Special Judge in an alleged corruption criminal case and whose ACR has also been expunged, no cut can be imposed on his pension or his retiral benefits cannot be withheld on the grounds of any loss caused to the Government.
