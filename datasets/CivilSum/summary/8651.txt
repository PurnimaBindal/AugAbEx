Reserve Bank of India Act (2 of 1934), Section 58 - Reserve Bank of India Employees' Housing Building Loan Rules, 1960, Rule 9(viii), (ix) - Reserve Bank of India (Staff) Regulations (1948), Rule 47 - Dismissal from service - The misconduct of submitting a false certificate for obtaining a housing loan and misusing the loan is valid and punishable by the loan rules through a dismissal from service.
Such loan rules are not against the Staff Rules and cannot be considered violative of Article 14.
