In the case of misconduct involving forgery of a license, and where the delinquent acted intentionally, the appropriate punishment is dismissal or removal from service.
[Para 10]

Forgery of a license with the intention of helping someone gain unlawful profit or causing loss is a serious matter.
Misconduct of such magnitude cannot be ignored on mere assumptions.
In cases like these, equity has no role.
[Para 9]

Under the Central Motor Vehicle Rules, the issuance of a forged license is a grave matter and cannot be dismissed simply based on the Tribunal's assertion.
The decision of the Tribunal was overturned.
