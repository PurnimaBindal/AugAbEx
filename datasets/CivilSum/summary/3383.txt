Punjab Public Works Department (Buildings and Roads) Branch Sectional Officers (Engineering) Service (Class III) Rules, 1964, Rule 11 - Punjab Service of Engineers, Class II, PWD (Buildings and Roads Branch) Rules, 1965, Rule 6 - Seniority - Promotion - When preparing the list of suitable candidates for promotion, seniority within the cadre of Overseers/Sectional Officers/Draftsmen must be taken into account - Seniority is determined by the date of continuous appointment, not the date of qualifying the examination of AMIE - Acquiring AMIE qualification only makes a person eligible to be included on the list, but all eligible persons must be considered in the order of their seniority as per the relevant rules, not based on the date of acquiring the prescribed qualification.
[Paras 9 to 13, 16 to 18]

Punjab Service of Engineers, Class II, PWD (Buildings and Roads Branch) Rules, 1965, Rule 9(3) - Committee formation to consider the promotion applications - It is not mandatory for the committee to meet annually - The committee is responsible for arranging the names of suitable candidates in the order of seniority.
