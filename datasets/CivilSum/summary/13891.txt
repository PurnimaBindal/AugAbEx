Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Break in service - While serving as a laboratory attendant, the petitioner requested leave in 1984, and was unable to return to work for 3-1/2 years due to unavoidable circumstances.
Despite repeated applications, he was allowed to return to work in 1991, with that period of absence treated as dies non.
The order was deemed punitive and could not have been issued without a departmental inquiry.
Thus, the impugned order has been set aside.
