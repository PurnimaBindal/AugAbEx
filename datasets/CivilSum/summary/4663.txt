As per Section 192 of the Army Act, 1950 and Rules 7(b) and 13 of the Army Rules, 1954, a claim for disability pension was filed by the petitioner, whose disability was assessed by the Medical Board as 20% for life.
The petitioner had served for 26 years and was fit at the time of enrollment.
The disease was diagnosed as Thoracic Outlet Syndrome (Right) and was contracted during service.
The disability is on account of stress and strain of service and, thus, will fall under Rule 13(III)(ii) of 1954 Rules.
Since the disease was contracted in service over which the petitioner had no control and was attributable to conditions of service under Rule 7(b), he is entitled to disability pension with arrears.
