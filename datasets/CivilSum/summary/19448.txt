The Punjab Agricultural Service(Class-1) Rules, 1947, Rules 2,3 & 10 specify that promotion from Class II to Class I service is based on seniority, qualifications and experience of a particular line.
Petitioners on the post of Hydrologist are not eligible for promotion to the post of Joint Director of Agriculture because only those with a degree or diploma in Agriculture and specified experience can fill the post of Deputy Director of Agriculture.
Merely being senior in the gradation list of the Haryana Agriculture Service Class I Officers and possessing an identity of pay cannot qualify them for promotion.
[Paras 10 & 12]

The Punjab Agricultural Service (Class-I) Rules, 1947, Rule 9 requires approval of the Service Commission for appointments.
While recommendations were made to the Service Commission, approval was not received.
Appointments made in the absence of approval cannot be treated as nullity for the purpose of considering experience for promotion.
[Para 8]

In case the rules do not specify qualifications for a particular post, executive instructions can be followed to fill in the gaps.
[Para 10]
