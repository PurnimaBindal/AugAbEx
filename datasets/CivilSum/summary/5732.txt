Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Management of non-Government Institution (Aided and Unaided) Rules, 1969, Rule 28(8) - Termination of services - Overstayal of leave - Absence from duty - The service of a teacher cannot be terminated for treating them absent despite their continuous requests for extension of leave.
If no proof exists that the teacher has joined service elsewhere and if no procedure has been prescribed under the Rules for terminating services of an approved teacher, the termination must be set aside.
The teacher must be treated to have continued in service for all purposes, except that they only will receive half arrears of salary because they did not work during that period.
