Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 19 - Non-production of Residence Certificate - Selection cancelled - The non-production of a certificate of bona fide resident of Rajasthan is not permissible - It was held that once the petitioner was selected, knowing fully well that she is not a resident of Rajasthan - The notification only says that residents will be given priority and nothing more - Being a resident is not a condition of appointment - The order cancelling the selection was quashed.
