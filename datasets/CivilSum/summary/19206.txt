Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Compassionate appointment - The petitioner's deceased husband held the post of Multi-purpose Health Worker (Male) in the pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040 - The petitioner was offered employment as a class IV employee in the pay scale of Rs. 750-940, but she requested employment on a post carrying one scale lower than that of her deceased husband - The court stated that the instructions issued by the Government for employment on a post one scale lower than the deceased employee's post apply, and given that others have been employed on Class III posts, the respondents must consider and decide the petitioner's application for compassionate appointment on a Class III post with the pay scale of Rs. 950-1500.
