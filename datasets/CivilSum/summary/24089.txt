The interpretation that a single individual funding an institution does not qualify for protection under Article 30 of the Constitution of India is incorrect, according to Kerala Education Rules, 1959, Rule 44 and the Constitution's Articles 31 and 226.
Whether or not the school was established by the minority community is a question of fact, which should be left to the enquiry of fact-finding authorities and not the High Court.
