Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25 - Abandonment - The workman can be terminated on grounds of abandonment, but can be reinstated with 50% back wages if it is found that they did not abandon their job.
Whether the respondent-workmen stopped working is a matter of fact, which the Labour Court found in their favor, and the court does not see any issue with this finding.
The petitioner's counsel could not present a case that falls under the jurisdiction of this court under Art. 227 of the Constitution of India.
