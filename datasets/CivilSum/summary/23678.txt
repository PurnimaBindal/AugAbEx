Constitution of India, Article 309, Proviso, Article 136 - UP Temporary Government servants (Termination of Service) Rules, 1975, Rule 3(1) - Termination of services of temporary Government servant - The authority's power to terminate the services is disputed due to the High Court's decision to set aside the termination.
The employee was in service for more than 28 years.
Even if the point is found in favour of the State Government, it is too late to allow the Government to terminate his service.
Therefore, without deciding the validity of the termination order, the State Government directed to regularise his services and provide all benefits, including promotion.
