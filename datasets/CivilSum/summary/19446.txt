Constitution of India, Articles 311(1) and 309 - Educational Ministerial Service Cadre Appointment, Promotion and Transfer Rules, 1974 - Suspension - Competent Authority - The power of suspension rests solely in the committee provided in the Rules, under the Chairmanship of the Regional Deputy Director of Education - An order of suspension given by the Collector-cum-District Officer, who was neither an Appointing Authority nor the Disciplinary Authority under the Rules, was made without jurisdiction.
