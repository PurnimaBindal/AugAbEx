The plaintiff/respondent was originally to retire at the age of 58 years as on 31.8.1994, but received a stay order that was later vacated by the Court of ADJ on 8.1.1996.
Despite filing a revision against this order, the operation of the 8.1.1996 order was not stayed.
If the plaintiff worked beyond 31.8.1994 and received salary due to the stay order, he must account for the excess period worked.
Whether the plaintiff, who worked until the age of 60, is entitled to wages for the excess period is now a point to be adjudicated by the trial court.
The ADJ has directed the court to dispose of the suit after framing proper issues.
