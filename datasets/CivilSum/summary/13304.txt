Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Transfer - Indiscipline - The competent authority can transfer an employee who creates indiscipline and disrupts the working of a government department, even if other co-workers are involved - The Court cannot interfere with such a transfer, even if there is some deviation from transfer policy - The transfer is not arbitrary or mala fide.
[Para 5]  B.
Transfer - Competent authority - If a higher authority requests a transfer for the interest of administration, the competent authority can make the transfer without being considered outside their jurisdiction.
