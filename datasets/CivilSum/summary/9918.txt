Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Reference - Industrial dispute - State Govt. cannot decline to refer dispute for adjudication by making the decision itself - It is the responsibility of the State Govt. to confirm whether there is an industrial dispute - Inquiry cannot be made on the basis of inquiry and the reference to the Labour Court cannot be declined or rejected - The case of workmen being wrongfully terminated are contested questions of fact that only the Labour Court can decide after recording evidence from both parties - The State Govt. cannot investigate and reach a decision on its own - Orders of the State Govt. refusing to refer the dispute to the Labour Court are invalid.
