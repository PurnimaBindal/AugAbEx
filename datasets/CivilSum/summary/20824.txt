Constitution of India, Article 32 - Regularisation - Petitioners Class III and Class IV employees on daily wage basis in Irrigation Department being paid wages as per the minimum wages prescribed by the Government - Working for more than ten years - Seeking regularisation and emoluments as were being paid to regular employees - State Govt. prepared a Scheme for Betterment (Appointment) Regularisation of Muster Roll/Daily Wage Workers in the State - Scheme modified with effect from January 1, 1994 to implement paragraphs 1 to 4, as the State cannot bear the financial burden of arrears of pay - Writ petition resolved under the aforementioned conditions.
