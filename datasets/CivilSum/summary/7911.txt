Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Misconduct - Suit challenging order of dismissal and setting it aside as null and void - Whether seeking enforcement of consequential relief through writ petition under Article 226 is maintainable - Held it is not a mere execution petition and is therefore maintainable - Respondents, who had not acted, cannot require petitioner to seek execution from trial Court as order is self-speaking - Petition allowed - Respondent ordered to reinstate the petitioner immediately with all consequential benefits.
