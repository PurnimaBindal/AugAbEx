Central Board of Secondary Education Bye Laws, Paragraph 7.4 - Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14, 16 - Education Code for Kendriya Vidyalaya, Chapter - Admission of a boy to class XI in the same school based on his performance in class X examination - Despite not meeting the cut-off marks, the student should not be denied admission, as it would be unreasonable and unjust to do so.
The school should also take some responsibility for the student's poor performance.
[Paras 3, 13, 16 and 18]
