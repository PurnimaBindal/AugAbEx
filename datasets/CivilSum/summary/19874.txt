Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 Haryana Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Section 16 - Haryana Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Rules, 1980, Rule 4(14) - Natural Justice/Cross-examination - The Petitioner was denied the opportunity to cross-examine the Prosecution witnesses, who also happened to be members of the Committee finding the Petitioner guilty.
The Petitioner's defense was thus prejudiced, which amounts to a violation of the principles of natural justice.
