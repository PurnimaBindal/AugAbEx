According to Section 48 of the Gujarat Municipalities Act, 1963, there are two types of suspensions: suspension by way of penalty or punishment and suspension pending departmental inquiry.
The former requires the observation of the principle of natural justice, while the latter is preventive in nature and does not require the application of natural justice principles.
In case of preventive suspension, the officer may be suspended even while on leave and is not entitled to notice.
However, the appellant in question was placed under suspension during his leave without any show-cause notice, which was not in accordance with the rules.
