Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India regarding deputation and permanent absorption state that a deputationist does not have the right to continue in the borrowing department.
[Para 7]
Repatriation from deputation does not affect an employee's civil rights.
[Para 7]
Natural justice does not require an opportunity for an employee to be heard before an order of repatriation is passed.
[Para 7]
Inter-departmental communications do not confer any rights on an official, as the orders issued by competent authorities are the only things that can create rights in their favor.
