Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Regularisation - Reserved vacancy - Regularisation of services as Junior Hindi Translator - Reservation principle applicable - When a vacancy occurs in the initial recruitment year and the roster point is for a reserved category, it must be treated as unreserved and filled accordingly.
The reservation should then be carried forward to subsequent years.
If there is a vacancy in the subsequent recruitment year, it should be treated as reserved.
In this case, two posts were created in 1990 and 1993.
According to the reservation principle, the post created in 1990 was treated as unreserved because only one post was created.
The next post created in 1993 will go to the reserved category because the reserved vacancy was carried forward to this year.
Hence, the petitioner who was working on this post on a causal basis could not be absorbed since the vacancy was meant for the reserved category.
