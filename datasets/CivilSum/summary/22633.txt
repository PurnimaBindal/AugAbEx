Under Articles 226 of the Constitution of India, budlee sepoys seeking absorption/regularisation must show that they have worked for more than 240 days, and the onus is on them to establish this.
The High Court was not correct in assuming that the respondents had satisfied this requirement without proper evidence.
[Para ] B The Constitution of India allows for the regularisation/absorption of Budlee sepoys under two conditions: having completed 240 days of work in a year or block of 12 months and having vacancies available.
This case concerns entitlements under the settlement and scheme, and while budlee workers differ from temporary workers, they are both subject to the scheme's policies.
As long as the scheme remains in place and its policies do not change, budlee workers who have completed 240 days of work will have their cases considered based on its provisions.
