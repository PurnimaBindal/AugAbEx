Under the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 311 and 226/227, and according to Rule 1221 of the Punjab Police Rules, 1934, an employee must be given an opportunity to address concerns related to their alleged willful absence from duty through a show cause notice before formal discharge.
However, in this case, no such notice was given, rendering the order of discharge equivalent to an order of dismissal.
Since such an order is stigmatic, it cannot be passed without a regular enquiry.
Consequently, the order of discharge has been set-aside, and the petitioner is deemed to be in service, entitled to all benefits related to their service from the date of the disputed order of discharge.
