Constitution of India, Articles 311(2) and 226 - Enquiry - Judicial review - Dispensing with the departmental enquiry - The satisfaction of the President or the Governor under Clause (c) of second Proviso to Article 311(2) of the Constitution of India is subject to judicial review and is justiciable - No evidence presented to demonstrate that the grounds for forgoing the holding of the departmental enquiry existed and were available to the satisfaction of the competent authority to trigger clause (c) - Dismissal order open to being nullified.
