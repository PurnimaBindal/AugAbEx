Constitution of India, Articles 136, 226 and 311 - Termination of services/Enquiry report - The High Court ordered the reinstatement of a delinquent who had been terminated by the punishing authority, despite the enquiry report being in favor of the delinquent and adverse remarks made by another officer which were not subject matter of charges being taken into consideration.
However, the Supreme Court set aside the High Court orders and directed the punishing authority to conduct a fresh enquiry based only on the material proved in the enquiry.
[Para 6] B.
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Enquiry report - According to the Supreme Court decision in Mohd.
Ramzan Khan's case, disciplinary proceedings are vitiated if a copy of the enquiry report is not supplied before the punishment order is passed.
However, punishment orders passed before this decision are not considered vitiated on that ground.
Union of India v. Mohd.
Ramzan Khan 1991 (1)SCT 111.
