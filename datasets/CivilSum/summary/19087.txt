Constitution of India, Article 311 - Dismissal - The petitioner, who was working as a lecturer in History, was dismissed from service without holding any enquiry upon his conviction under Sections 304 and 34 of IPC and under sections 25/27 of the Arms Act.
The court held that a person cannot be dismissed from service just because he is convicted.
The dismissal can happen only on the ground of conduct that has led to the conviction on a criminal charge.
Since the order of dismissal did not make any reference to the conduct, it was set aside and the court ordered for reinstatement.
