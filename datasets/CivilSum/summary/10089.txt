The reversion of the appellant from Sanitary Supervisor to Sanitary Beldar based on the ground that the qualification of Prathma Examination was not equivalent to Matriculation Standard was deemed incorrect by the court.
The court stated that even though Chandigarh Administration did not recognize Prathma Examination as equivalent to matriculation, it does not mean that the appellant was ineligible for the post.
The court set aside the order of reversion, and also pointed out that the appellant had cleared the 10+2 examination from an open school which is equivalent to Matriculation Examination.
