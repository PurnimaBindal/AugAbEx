Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - National Council for Teacher Education Act, 1993, Sections 14, 15 and 16 - Admission - Cancellation of - Eligibility - Admission to the teacher training course by NCTE - Admission can be canceled if eligibility is not fulfilled, which depends on obtaining 45% marks in Senior Secondary Examination - In this case, the petitioners did not fulfill the eligibility condition of 45% marks - NCTE has the right to determine this minimum percentage for planned and coordinated development of the teacher education system - The action taken by the respondents is not arbitrary.
