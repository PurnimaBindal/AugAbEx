According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner's appointment as a class IV employee on daily wages was not done through the proper procedure, and therefore cannot be regularized in the service solely based on their long-term work in the position.
The fact that some other employees have received benefits in an illegal manner does not give the petitioner any right to claim those same benefits.
This was found to be an unsustainable claim.
