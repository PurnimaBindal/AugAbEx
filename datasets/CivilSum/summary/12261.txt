CCS (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rules 49, 59 - Navy Pension Regulations, 1964 - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pro rata pension - Claiming of - Petitioner, who was enrolled in Indian Navy from 1963 to 1973, filed a representation for grant of Pro rata pension in 1999, relying on a notification of Government of India and Rule 49 of CCS (Pension) Rules - However, the Court held that the specific provision in Navy Pension Regulations provides for 15 years of qualifying service for pension and the Petitioner has no merit for his claim - Furthermore, the Petitioner's delay in approaching the Court and unwillingness certificate signed by him were also taken into consideration - The Petition is therefore dismissed.
