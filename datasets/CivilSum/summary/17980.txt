Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay/Equal pay for equal work - Finance Department's stance - The post of Assistant Registrar-Cum-Managing Officer is equivalent to Tehsildar (Sales) and Tehsildar - The posts entail identical duties and responsibilities - They require the same level of qualifications - Equal pay is justified for personnel serving in similar posts, along with identical revised pay scales - When such situations arise, the recommendations of the Pay Committee should be heeded, rather than the Finance Department's opinion, which is irrelevant.
