The petitioner in this case was subjected to pre-mature retirement due to adverse remarks in his confidential reports, despite earning 61% good or above reports and only three 'Average'.
However, his recent reports were 'Good' to 'Very Good' including those recorded after passing of impugned order of premature retirement.
It was also indicated in the summary put up before the officers' Committee that he had secured 80% good or above reports.
The order of premature retirement was quashed because adverse remarks once expunged cannot be read to hold the employee as dishonest or inefficient.
Additionally, it cannot be said that he had become a dead-wood and that it was in the public interest to retire the petitioner prematurely.
