As per the Employee Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 17(3)(a), 27-F, exempted employees are not required to pay inspection charges, but in case of a dispute, the charges must be calculated based on a salary of Rs. 2500 per month, instead of the total salary.
In a case where the respondent claimed charges on the entire salary, it was held that their decision was illegal and arbitrary, as they failed to distinguish between administrative and inspection charges, which are governed by different provisions of the Act.
This ruling is supported by the plain reading of the notification dated 1791964.
