The Tribunal did not become functus officio to entertain the application to recall the award as it was made within 30 days of the publication of the award.
In terms of Section 17 of the Industrial Disputes Act, the award becomes enforceable after 30 days, and Section 20(3) provides that proceedings before the Tribunal continue until the award becomes enforceable.
[Paras 16, 17] B.
The Tribunal cannot recall the award unless it committed a procedural illegality or mistake.
Since the recall of the award was not sought on these grounds, it was not permissible to review on the merits of the case in the absence of a provision in the Act conferring such power either expressly or by necessary implication.
[Paras 20, 21] C. The settlement was reached in the course of conciliation proceedings with the assistance and concurrence of the conciliation officer, and there is no doubt about it.
The conciliation officer intervened when there was considerable labor unrest and brought the parties to the negotiating table to work out the settlement, which was signed by the conciliation officer and the parties involved.
