The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Uttar Pradesh State Universities Act, 1973, Section 31 - Ad hoc promotion to the post of Principal - According to statute, the most senior teacher was to take over as Principal until a duly selected Principal was appointed - The petitioner's claim for promotion was denied due to his absence for a certain period - The order cannot be justified and is therefore repealed.
