Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F - Regularisation - Contractual Employees - According to section 25F of the Act, regularisation can only occur if employees have worked for more than 240 days with no violation of the provisions.
Respondents argue that the work is of a fluctuating nature and that appointments were not made in accordance with recruitment rules.
The court held that regularisation cannot be claimed without following the prescribed procedure laid out in recruitment rules and any violations can be raised by petitioners in the appropriate forum.
