Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - India Literacy Board, registered as a Society under the Societies Registration Act, is raising a preliminary objection regarding the maintainability of a writ petition against it, as it is not an agency or authority within the meaning of Article 12 of the Constitution.
This question must be answered prior to deciding the writ petition's merits and granting any relief to the Board's ex-employee.

Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - In the case of a contractual appointment of an employee of a Society for a fixed term, no mandamus can be issued for continuing their employment.
[Para 15]
Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - The principle of no work no pay applies if an employee was not on duty after the expiry of their appointment term.
Therefore, they cannot be awarded back wages.
