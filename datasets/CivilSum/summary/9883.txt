According to the Haryana Veterinary Service (Class-I) Rules of 1993, the qualifications for the post of Deputy Director (Feed and Fodder) are different from the qualifications for the post of Deputy Director (Veterinary Wing).
As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, equal pay for equal work is mandated, but the classification for granting pay scales based on qualifications is justified.
Despite similar job duties, the minimum qualifications for the ex-cadre post of Dy.
Director (Feed and Fodder) are different from those required for the post of Deputy Director (Veterinary Wing), and therefore, the petitioner cannot claim the latter's pay scale.
[Paras 6 and 9] B.
Classification of pay scales based on qualifications for equal pay for equal work is justifiable.
