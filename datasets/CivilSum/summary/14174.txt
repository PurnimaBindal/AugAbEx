Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Abandonment of job - Conciliation of proceedings - In the case of a workman who immediately serves a demand notice against his termination and the employer does not offer him his joining, it cannot be presumed that the workman abandoned his job.
Therefore, the employer cannot object to the granting of relief of continuity of service and full back wages.
