The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural justice/Deregulation - Show-cause notice - The petitioners had their services regularized based on the Haryana Government instructions dated 27593 but had their services deregularized due to subsequent instructions dated 18394 and having breaks for more than one month in their service.
Once their services were regularized, they acquired valuable rights that could not be taken away without complying with the principles of natural justice and issuing a show-cause notice.
Thus, the orders were deemed illegal and unfair and were quashed.
[Para 5]
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularization of services - When the services of workers are regularized based on the instructions dated 27593 of the Haryana Government, instructions dated 18394 cannot be applied to deregularize their services since instructions cannot be given retrospective effect.
[Para 7]
Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-B - Break in service - Regularization - If the interruption in service is due to sickness or authorized leave, accident, a legal strike, or a lock-out, such interruptions cannot be considered a break in service, so employees cannot be disqualified for regularizations in service.
