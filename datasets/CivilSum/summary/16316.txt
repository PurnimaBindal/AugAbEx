The reversion orders made by the Respondent against the Petitioner based on a decree obtained by the Private Respondent have been invalidated.
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 and natural justice necessitate that notice and a hearing be provided before reversion is ordered.
The Petitioner had not been party to the decree, and had even been promoted and confirmed without issue.
Given that the promotion had not been challenged, the reversion could not be conducted without notice and hearing, and has consequently been quashed.
