The Constitution of India in Articles 14 and 16 indicates that physical tests should apply to the selection and appointment of Constables.
However, in this case, the petitioner's performance was above the prescribed standards, making it unfair to exclude them from appointment solely on the basis of failure to qualify physical tests.
Additionally, the petitioner's performance as certified by the Director of the National Institute of Sports contradicts the respondent's claims.
Therefore, the respondents should review the petitioner's appointment claims based on the report from the Director of the National Institute of Sports.
