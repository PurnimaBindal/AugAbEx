Constitution of India, Article 226 - Gujrat Secondary Education Act, 1972, Section 38(1) - Jurisdiction - A complaint cannot be made challenging a decision of an educational institution for not giving an appointment order, by invoking Section 38(1) of Gujarat Secondary Education Act, if the person has not been given the appointment order - As per the ruling, the person cannot invoke jurisdiction under Section 38(1) of the Act until an appointment order is given - Thus, the Tribunal does not have the jurisdiction to decide such disputes.
