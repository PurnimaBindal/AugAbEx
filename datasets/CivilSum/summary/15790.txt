According to Section 31(11) of the U.P.
State Universities Act, 1973, to become a lecturer, approval of the Vice-Chancellor is necessary.
Although management approved the petitioner's selection for the post, the Vice-Chancellor did not accord approval.
The deeming provisions in the proviso to Section 33(11) cannot be invoked as the selection was disapproved by the Vice-Chancellor.
Therefore, the petitioner's claim for the post of lecturer cannot be accepted.
