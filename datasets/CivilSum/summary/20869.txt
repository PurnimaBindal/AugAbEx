Maharashtra Medical and Research (Recruitment) Rules, 1986 - Temporary appointment - Regularisation - The appellant, who was appointed temporarily for a permanent post, cannot be regularised under any other equitable principle since the rules of selection through Commission cannot be substituted by humane consideration - Hence, the appellant is not entitled to claim that she should have been deemed to have been regularised, and her claim that she stood regularised under 1975 Resolution cannot be accepted.
