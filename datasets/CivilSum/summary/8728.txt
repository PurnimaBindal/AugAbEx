The employee was suspended in 1982 in contemplation of departmental enquiry, and an FIR was registered against him under IPC sections 409, 468, and 471.
Despite the fact that the appropriate authority can suspend an employee in contemplation of a departmental enquiry, they cannot subject him to prolonged long-term suspension of approximately 13 years.
The employee was acquitted by the Magistrate in the criminal case, but he remained suspended for over two years.
The order of suspension has been set aside, and the respondent has been directed to finish the departmental proceedings.
[Paras 4 and 5] B.
The court should only use its jurisdiction to quash enquiry proceedings in the rarest of rare cases.
In this case, the allegation against the petitioner pertained to the crime of forgery, and although approximately 13 years have passed, the departmental enquiry has also been completed.
As a result, it is not an appropriate case to quash the enquiry proceedings.
