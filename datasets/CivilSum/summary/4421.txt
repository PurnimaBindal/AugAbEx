Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admissions/Education/Reservation in admissions - The intention of the instructions is to provide reservation for the children/ward of those whose death or disability is attributable to war casualty - The University is not bound to prepare a common list of all wards of such ex-servicemen to be considered jointly - University was justified in granting admission to those whose fathers made supreme sacrifices for the country and were killed either in operation or in war in preference to those whose fathers were relieved just on compacianate ground on account of ill health or were disabled in some accident etc. [Paras 7 and 8] B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 39, Rules 1 and 2 - Academic matters of University - Admissions/Education/Reservation - University's decision that the respondent was not entitled to admission under the reserved category was based on sound reasons - Court should not interfere in academic matters and should not supplant its own view over the decision of University on factual position - Interference must be slow.
