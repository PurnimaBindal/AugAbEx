Regularisation of service under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India is not applicable if the petitioner has a break in service lasting more than one month.
It is not acceptable to divide one month into two different calendar months.
The petitioner fails to meet the criteria for regularisation, and there is no merit to the case.
As per Words and Phrases, "One Month" is controlled by the expression "at a time" and thus refers to one calendar month.
