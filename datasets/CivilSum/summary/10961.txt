Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Rules 326 and 532 - Premature retirement - A petitioner, who was a teacher, retired early on the basis that her ACRs didn't fulfill the required 70% good or better report - Nonetheless, the trend of ACRs from 1987 to 1991 was consistently on the rise - During that period, the petitioner was given either very good, good or outstanding reports - The criteria of 70% good or better reports within the last 10 years is unreasonable - The reports from the whole service record should be evaluated, with special attention given to the last 5 years - Moreover, the petitioner was even found fit to cross the efficiency bar with effect from 1992 - Meaning she was not unfit for duty - The order passed seems baseless and not for the larger interest of the public - It is therefore quashed.
[Paras 19, 21, 22 and 23]
