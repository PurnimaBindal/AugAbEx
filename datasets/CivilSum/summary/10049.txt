Indian Red Cross Society and St John Ambulance Association, District Branch, Yamuna Nagar Staff Rules, 2002, Rule 11 - Extension in service - Cancellation of extension - Payment of wages for extended period - An employee whose service period is extended beyond their prescribed retirement age and is recommended for payment of salary cannot be denied their wages for the duration they worked due to the extension being cancelled.
As per Rules, they are entitled to post retiral benefits.
[Para 6] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - Red Cross Society - Employees of the Society have the same entitlements to post retiral benefits and gratuity under the Gratuity Act as State Government employees, including interest on delayed payments.
A writ petition can be filed against the Society for these purposes.
