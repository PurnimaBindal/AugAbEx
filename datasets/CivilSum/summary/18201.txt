Under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 3, Item 1 and Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, 1957, Sections 92 and 98, carpenters whose post isn't that of the Senior Carpenter, and hence don't require any essential educational qualification, have been granted a revised pay scale.
The government order allows skilled workers drawing wages in the Rs. 260-350 pay scale to receive a revision of pay scale.
Although the post of carpenter is in the scale of Rs. 210-270 and senior carpenter is in the scale of Rs. 260-360, the worker in this case is not considered a skilled worker and should not have received the revised pay scale according to paras 14, 15 and 17.
