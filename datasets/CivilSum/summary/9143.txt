Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 115 - Revision - The executing Court cannot award interest if it was not awarded to the respondent under the decree.
Executing Court cannot modify or add to the terms of decree based on its own discretion.
[Para 2]

Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970 - The termination order was null and void and the arrears of salary were paid to the claimant.
The executing Court allowed interest from the date of filing the suit till the passing of the decree and until the amount was realized.
The order of the executing Court as to interest cannot be sustained and is quashed since interest was not awarded to the respondent under the decree.
[Paras 1 and 2]
