The policy of regularisation of services as per the Constitution of India 1950, Articles 14, 16 and 226, is free for the government to formulate.
The State is not legally obligated to regularise the services of employees with pending disciplinary proceedings.
The welfare policy of the State cannot be stretched to mean that even a person guilty of misconduct should be regularised.
[Para 11]  B.
During the period of contractual or daily wage services, the conduct of a person claiming regularisation who is a daily wager would be a relevant factor.
If his services are unsatisfactory, the State is not legally obligated to regularise the services of such employees with pending disciplinary proceedings.
[Para 7] C. The subjective satisfaction of satisfactory performance is the domain of the employer.
It cannot be subjected to judicial review.
