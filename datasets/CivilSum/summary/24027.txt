Section 100 of the Civil Procedure Code states that in second appeals regarding quantum of punishment, interference by the High Court by reappreciating evidence is not allowed.
In a case where a bank cashier has been found to have grossly derelicted his duties and negligent with five charges upheld by the High Court, it is held that the High Court cannot interfere with the quantum of punishment.
The High Court cannot come to a different finding based on the same materials that the enquiry officer used to arrive at his conclusion.
The judgment of the High Court is vitiated and cannot be sustained.
