The grant-in-aid scheme for non-Government law colleges has been withheld while it has been extended to recognized private professional colleges in other faculties.
This selective discrimination was found to be unfair by the High Court, which ordered affirmative action be taken through the extension of the grant-in-aid scheme to all recognized private law colleges.
[Paras 11, 10, 13, 15 and 21] B.
The Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 39-A mandates that the State provide grant-in-aid to recognized private law colleges on par with other faculties.
This duty cannot be compromised in any manner.
[Paras 17 to 19] C. The petitioner has the right to seek a writ of mandamus based on a directive principle contained in the Constitution.
