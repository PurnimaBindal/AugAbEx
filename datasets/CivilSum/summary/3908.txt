The definition of workman according to Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 includes part time employees.
[Para 6]
If a part time employee has worked for more than one year, their termination must comply with the mandatory provisions of Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
[Para 7]
In the case of a part time employee, if a Labour Court determines that no show-cause notice or pay in lieu of notice was issued, there can be no interference under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
