Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservation for Backward Classes - Roster system - 100 point roster - Purpose of roster - BC candidates will have their first reserved point on the roster at the 10th point - The first 10 vacancies are taken as one unit - During the initial stage where only 7 posts are created, no BC candidate is entitled to promotion - If a vacancy is later created by retirement or increase of strength or any other reason, the 10th vacancy on the roster may be filled by a BC candidate - It is not accurate to say that no BC candidate can benefit from the 10% reservation policy when there are only 7 posts.
[Paras 3 and 4]
