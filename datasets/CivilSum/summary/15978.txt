Punjab Soil Conservation and Engineering Service (Class II) Rules, 1974, Rule 8 - Promotion - Amendment of Rules - Agricultural Inspectors, Agricultural Inspectors (Soil Survey), Soil Conservation Inspectors, Soil Survey Assistants and Sectional Officers have been placed in one cadre and are interchangeable and intertransferable - Equal pay for equal work principle applies to all categories - Their pay scales and opportunities for promotion to Class-II service should be equal - Prescribing different pay scales for promotion to Class-II service without amending the rules is not justified - Sectional Officers (now designated as Junior Engineers) should be entitled to the same pay scales and promotion to Class-II posts as other categories in the cadre - Government's action held unjustified and violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.

Constitution of India, Article 309 - Amendment of Rules - Statutory rules framed under Article 309 of the Constitution cannot be amended by executive order or instructions to change service conditions.
