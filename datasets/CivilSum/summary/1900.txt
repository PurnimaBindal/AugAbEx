Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227, 14 and 16 - Regularisation of Services - Grant of regular pay scale - Equal pay for equal work - Petitioners working as Pump Operators in PWD on daily wage basis - Their duties are inter-changed with regular counterparts - The petitioners are eligible to receive the same salary and allowances as their regular counterparts holding similar positions - The court has allowed the petition and directed the respondents to regularise the services of the petitioners and pay them the minimum of the time-scale of the post of Pump Operator until they are regularised.
[Paras 3 and 14]
