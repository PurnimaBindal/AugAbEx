Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Cut off date - Selection - Eligibility for selection to the post of Lecturer - Those who obtained a degree of MPhil before 31.12.1992 are exempted from passing the eligibility test conducted by UGC or C.S.I.R.
- The fixed date has no connection to the objective aimed to be achieved - All MPhil degree holders are eligible for the exemption.
