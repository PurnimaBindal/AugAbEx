Under the Administrative Tribunals Act of 1985, Section 22, an order that has been affirmed in appeal may not be reviewed by a former authority.
The doctrine of merger applies in such a situation, where the appellate order becomes effective.
Therefore, the Tribunal erred by reviewing its order based on the premise that the original order was passed by an incompetent authority.
[Para 10] B.
The scope of review under Section 22 of the Administrative Tribunals Act of 1985 does not include grounds that were not previously urged.
While the power of review is intended to rectify manifest errors, it does not permit a roving discussion that resembles an appellate court.
