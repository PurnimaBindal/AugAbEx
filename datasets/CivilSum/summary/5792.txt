Army Regulations, 1962, Regulation 60 - Promotion is different in Defence services and in civil services - Promotion in civil services is given to remove stagnation and increase efficiency whereas in Defence services the promotion is based on selection - Promotion to the rank of Colonel in Defence services requires experience, exposure and appropriate review - Junior officers can be selected in the interest of service.
[Paras 8 to 11]
Army Regulations, 1962, Regulation 60 - Army Act, Sections 191 and 192 - Notification of Rules - If the Rules are not notified under the Act but followed equally for all, these are treated as departmental instructions - Rules without notification have statutory force.
[Para 12]
Army Regulations, 1962, Regulation 69 - Promotion - Consideration - Chances for consideration - New criteria confining the consideration of an officer to 3 chances only is not inconsistent or contrary to Regulation 69 so far it is applied uniformly and equally to all as a policy - A policy can be changed according to the requirement of circumstances - It is within the competence of the authority to change it.
[Paras 13, 14 and 15]
Army Act and Regulations - Statutory complaint - Representation - Reasons - Adverse remarks - Statutory complaint against adverse remarks need not disclose the reasons - The authority must act in a fair and just manner.
