Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 309 - Punjab Govt. National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rule 4 - Promotion - Benefit of Military service - Petitioner, who was exonerated of all charges and reinstated with back wages and benefits, was given the benefit of military service and his seniority was fixed based on his date of joining.
Additionally, the petitioner's promotion request should have been considered given the promotion of junior clerks over him.
As a result, a mandamus was issued requiring the respondents to fix the petitioner's seniority based on his date of joining and consider him for promotion as Assistant/Accountant/Junior Auditor.
Following his promotion, he would be entitled to all benefits including salary arrears.
