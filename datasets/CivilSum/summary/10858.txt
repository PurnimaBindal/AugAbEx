Constitution of India, Article 226 - Adverse remarks - Reliance - Permissibility - Petitioner requests quashing of adverse remarks on moral character and reputation - No justification given for the basis of these remarks - No information was given to petitioner to explain or confront, according to the reporting officer - Held, reporting officer should share information with concerned officer before forming an adverse opinion for it to be confronted before being recorded - Thus, remarks cannot be accepted without sufficient basis.
[Paras 7 to 10]
