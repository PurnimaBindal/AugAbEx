Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - Transfer out of District - Competent authority - In regards to the transfer of a Sub-Inspector of police from West Bengal to a district different from their appointment prior to completion of 7 years, it is necessary to consider all matters related to the competency of the DIGP In the interest of justice, the petitioner has been ordered to be transferred back and the matter should be reconsidered while taking into account the requirement of exigencies of service.
