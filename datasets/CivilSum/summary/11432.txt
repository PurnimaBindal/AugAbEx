Army Pension Regulations 1961, Rule 173 and Para 7(b) of Appendix II - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 13(3)(iii)(b) - Eligibility for disability pension - A person who dies in uniform while actually engaged in action or activities related to military service must be considered to have died due to injuries attributable to military service for the purpose of eligibility for disability pension.

Army Pension Regulations 1961, Regulations 216 and 219 - Disability pension to widow - Remarriage by widow - If a widow remarries within the deceased husband's family and continues to live with him in commune or support other living heirs, she would be eligible for family pension according to Regulation 219's proviso.
