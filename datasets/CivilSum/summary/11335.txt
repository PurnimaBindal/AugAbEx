Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Prevention of Corruption Act, 1947, Section 13(1) - Punishment and Appeal Rules, Rule 7 Compulsory retirement - The petitioner was exonerated from all charges in a departmental inquiry before he was served a notice of retirement and the criminal case relates to the same charges as investigated in the departmental inquiry - The punishing authority agreed with the findings of the Inquiry Officer but decided to administer a punishment of warning as the petitioner had violated Govt. instructions - The petitioner could not be compulsorily retired based on the minor punishment of warning/censure received in a departmental proceeding as per instructions issued by the respondents themselves - The order of compulsory retirement and notice was quashed as it was found to be arbitrary and based on no evidence.
