Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Disability Pension - The petitioner, who was discharged from Army Service on medical grounds, was paid a disability pension of Rs. 10/- per month from 9101945 to 31.10.1945 and Rs. 15/- per month w.e.f.
1.11.1945 for lifetime under the amended provisions.
Later, his disability was reduced to less than 20%, and the disability pension was stopped.
The petitioner made various representations for re-survey medical board, and it is held that his claim to be subjected to a re-survey medical board has merit as he was granted disability pension for livelihood.
[Para 8]

Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Disability pension - Stopping of - Representations - Writ petition - The petitioner's claim to pension is a recurring cause, and therefore, not strictly hit by the principle of limitation or laches simplicitor.
It is held that relief to petitioner cannot be denied merely on this ground.
