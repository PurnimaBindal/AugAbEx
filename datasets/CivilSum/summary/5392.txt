Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Delay and laches - Explanation of delay - The Government may refuse to make a reference of a stale claim if the workman causes an unjustifiable delay, allowing the employer to change its position.
The Court may also refuse to entertain the writ petition and exercise its jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution.
In this case, the petitioner made a demand notice in 1982 and approached the Court in 2000, resulting in the dismissal of the petition as belated.
