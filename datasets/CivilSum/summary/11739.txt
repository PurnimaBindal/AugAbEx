The Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16 and 226, along with the Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950's Section 46(1)(c), all pertain to misconduct and disciplinary proceedings.
The petitioner was removed from service due to presenting a non-genuine school certificate during recruitment, something considered as misconduct, after a regular departmental inquiry took place under statutory regulations.
Additionally, an industrial dispute was previously raised against the proceedings, but it was dismissed after it was not pressed.
As the orders have become final, the petitioner cannot approach this Court indirectly to seek relief for reinstatement.
