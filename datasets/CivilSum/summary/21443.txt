In Civil Appeal No. 5206 of 1995, arising out of SLP (C) No. 7372 of 1995, involving Constitution of India, Article 16 and Punjab Civil Secretariat (State Services Class III) Rules, 1963, Rule 8 regarding promotion from Assistant to Superintendent Grade I in the office of Advocate General Punjab, Rule 8 is also applied to the Secretariat Service.
The practice of accepting such Rules in the office of Advocate General has grown, and there is no justification to deny the same benefit to persons in allied services in the office of Advocate General which is part of the wing of Govt., unless a discernible differentia touching the nature of service is shown.
[Paras 5 and 6]
