According to Para 423 of Regulations for the Medical Services of Armed Forces, 1962, a disease which caused an individual's discharge or death will typically be deemed to have occurred during service if it was not noted at the time of acceptance for service.
Even though the petitioner's claim for a disability pension was initially rejected on the grounds that epilepsy was not attributable to military service, epilepsy is a disease that can be linked to military service, and the petitioner was allowed to appeal.
