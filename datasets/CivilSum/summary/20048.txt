Constitution of India, Articles 136 and 226 - Date of birth - Delay and laches - A petitioner who worked as an officer in All India Civil Services requested a correction of her date of birth after 18-1/2 years of her appointment - The court found it difficult to believe that an educated person would remain silent for such an extended period - As a result, the order of the Tribunal directing the correction of date of birth after such a lengthy time was set aside.
