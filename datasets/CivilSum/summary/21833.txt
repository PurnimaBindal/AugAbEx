Constitution of India, Article 136 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Dismissal - After a Division Bench found the punishment of removal to be too harsh, the case was remanded for re-decision on the appropriate punishment.
The resulting decision called for the punishment of one increment stoppage and full back wages, which was challenged, leading to another remand.
The High Court ultimately suggested the punishment of removal, which was deemed unsustainable, resulting in the order being modified to determine whether any suitable punishment short of removal could be imposed for the alleged misconduct.
The decision also outlined the consideration of evidence regarding the appellant's gainful employment, and the matter was remanded back to the Labour Court for further judgment.
