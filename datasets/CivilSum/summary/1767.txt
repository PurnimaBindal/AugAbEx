The petitioner applied for the post of Primary Teacher but submitted an OBC certificate from another state.
The court held that a person belonging to OBC on migration is not entitled to benefits or concessions admissible in their State of origin in the State they have migrated and cannot take a different view due to the judgment of Supreme Court in MCD v. Veena.
[Paras 9, 10]

The selection and appointment for the post of Primary Teacher began in 1996-97, but the petitioner approached the court in 1999.
The court held that the petition is hit by delay and laches, and interference at this stage would upset third-party settled rights arising from the appointment, which would be against public interest.
[Para 11]
