Constitution of India, Article 226 - Waiting List - The waiting list cannot be used as a permanent source of recruitment for filling vacancies that are not advertised.
A candidate on the waiting list only has a limited right, which is acquired only if the selected candidate does not join for some reason and the waiting list is still in operation.
A candidate included in the waiting list cannot claim appointment on the grounds that vacancies were not properly worked out.
