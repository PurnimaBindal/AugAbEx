Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Selection started - Dying in harness category - Even if the selection process has already begun before the death of an employee, a vacancy cannot be filled under the dying in harness rules - Subsequent developments cannot override this unless there is a specific provision - Even changes in the law cannot overturn this situation.

Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Petitioner not sponsored by Employment Exchange - Despite not being sponsored by an employment exchange, a petitioner who competed with all candidates and topped the interview list cannot be denied the right to appointment - Unless specifically prohibited, candidates who are not sponsored by employment exchanges must be provided with equal opportunity for employment as guaranteed by Article 16 of the Constitution [Paras 6 and 7]
