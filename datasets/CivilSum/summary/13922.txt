Employers' Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 14-B - Provident Fund - Deposit in the Bank bona fide - Penalty - Before imposing the penalty, the authority must confirm that the employer's act was contumacious in nature - If the employer deposited the contribution in the Bank bona fide and not with the Regional Provident Fund Commissioner, this act cannot be considered contumacious in nature to attract the penultimate clause.
