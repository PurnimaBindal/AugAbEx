According to the West Bengal College Service Commission Act, 1978, Sections 7 and 8, as well as the University Grants Commission Act, 1956, College lecturers must possess certain qualifications to be appointed.
The University Grants Commission is authorized to establish these qualifications for academic or other eligible appointments of College Teachers.
The University is not allowed to waive the prescribed qualifications for anyone unless there are provisions under the Act that authorize it.
