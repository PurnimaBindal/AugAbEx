Constitution of India, Article 16 - UP Non-Technical (Class II) Services (Reservation of Vacancies for Demobilised Officers) Rules, 1973 (Rules reissued in 1980), Rule 5 - Military service benefit/Seniority - Those officers who were recruited to the Army between November 1, 1962 to January 10, 1968 and on December 3, 1971 and released at any time thereafter are entitled to Military Service benefit, recruitment and seniority - The appellant, who was commissioned on September 22, 1974 and released on November 30, 1979 and appointed to the civil post of Deputy Collector in 1979, is entitled to claim seniority as per 1980 Rules.
