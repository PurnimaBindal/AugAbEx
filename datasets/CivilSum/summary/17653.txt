For Respondent: Ritu Bahri, AAG, Haryana.
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Removal from service - Adequate grounds - Vague charge - The delinquent was accused of purchasing articles from sources that were not approved and without authorization from the competent authority, despite having acquired them at the lowest prices through tenders - No information was given about the approved source or the competent authority, and no malice was attributed to the petitioner - The charge is indistinct - The order removing the petitioner from service goes beyond the scope of the charge - The order is overturned.
[Para 5]
