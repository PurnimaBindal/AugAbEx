Haryana Aided Schools (Security of Service) Act, 1971, Sections 3(3) & 3(4) - Appeal - Reinstatement - Stay - The order of termination of services of the Principal is not approved by the District Education Officer - The school has filed an appeal before the Director of Education - Without getting a stay on the current order or any other favorable order, the school is obligated to appoint the petitioner as Principal and pay salary.
[Paras 4 & 5].
