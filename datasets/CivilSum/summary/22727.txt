Constitution of India, Article 372 - Jind State Civil Service Regulations, 1945 - Age of superannuation - Retirement - Jind State Civil Service Regulations relating to service conditions and superannuation ceased to be valid once Jind State was taken over by Pepsu State in 1948.
As per Article 372 of the Constitution, the rules could be modified by the Pepsu State.
The covenant entered by the Rulers of the States was an act of State, hence the employees of the former State of Jind were not entitled to their prior rights, including service until 60 years of age.
The High Court's decision was wrong.
