The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 state that time bound promotion and higher pay scales should be granted based on qualifying service.
Haryana Government Finance Department Letters dated 781992, 821994 and 291219985 clarify that the time bound promotion and increments are available even after promotion from Class-D.
Completing the qualifying service on Class-C automatically entitles an employee to a higher pay scale.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 guarantee natural justice for time bound promotion and higher pay scales.
Withdrawing the financial benefits already granted by competent authority is not permitted without affording an adequate opportunity and results in civil consequences.
