Constitution of India, Articles 141 and 226 - Judgments and precedents - The purpose of decisions is to clarify, not to define - Judges interpret statutes, not judgments - The words of judges do not become statutes - Courts should not rely on decisions without considering how they apply to the specific case.

Constitution of India, Articles 141 and 226 - Adverse remarks - Communication of adverse remarks - Procedure for recording and communicating adverse remarks - Not all unpleasant entries are considered adverse - The procedure determines which entry is considered adverse and must be communicated.
