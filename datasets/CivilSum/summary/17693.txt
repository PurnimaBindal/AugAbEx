Anupam Gupta, Advocate, cited Punjab University Calendar Volume III(1990), Rule 4- Migration-Eligibility-Students of LLB in Guru Nanak Dev University seeking migration to Punjab University LLB 1st year, 1st Semester class.
The candidate is admitted on the basis of Master's Degree.
The condition of securing marks not less than that of the last admitted candidate is mandatory only if the admission is sought on the basis of a graduate degree.
The condition cannot be imposed on the candidates admitted based on Master's degree unless the rule is amended.
Since the petitioner is eligible and seats are available, she is entitled to migration.
