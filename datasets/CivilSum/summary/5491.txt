Constitution of India, Article 226 - Central Civics Services (Pension) Rule, 1972, Rule 48 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56 - Compulsory retirement - The petitioner challenges the order of compulsory retirement based on the fact that the material relied upon by the competent authority was never communicated to him, and as such, the order is vitiated.
He also questions bias and the lack of an opportunity to defend himself.
However, the court held that uncommunicated adverse remarks can be relied upon when passing an order of compulsory retirement.
Such an order is not a punishment, but instead an opinion formed in the public interest, and thus, the principles of natural justice have no place in this context.
The government must consider the entire record of service before making a decision.
In this case, considering the petitioner's adverse remarks and previous indictment/punishment, the order of compulsory retirement was upheld, and the petition was dismissed.
