The Commission prepared separate merit lists for each category.

Only thirteen times the reserved vacancies candidates of Scheduled Castes were included in the merit lists from the qualifying candidates as per their merit.
No separate merit list was necessary at the stage of preparing a list of qualifying candidates for the main examination, as the separate merit list is required only at the time of the result of the main examination declaring the selected candidates for appointments.
