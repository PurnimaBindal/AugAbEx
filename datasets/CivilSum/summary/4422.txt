Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970 - Section 10 - Food Corporation of India is allowed to employ watchmen as contract labour - According to the ruling, the Corporation is not prohibited from employing contract labour to work as watchmen.
[Para 20]  B Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 10(2) - Necessary - Requirements must be met before a notification can be issued.
[Paras 11 and 13]  C Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 10(2) - Necessary - Punjab Government had not made a decision to abolish the employment of contract labour in offices of Food Corporation - The notification issued on 9.12.1976 only applied to establishments for which the Central Government was the appropriate Government - It could not be automatically extended to establishments for which the Central Government was not the appropriate Government on the date of the notification.
