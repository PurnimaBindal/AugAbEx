In accordance with the Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Sections 28, 30 and 44, the power of the labor court is limited to only determining if the evidence provided supports the conclusion of the inquiry officer and justifies the punishment.
Labor courts cannot act as appellate authorities to reassess the entire evidence and material before the inquiry officer.
[Para 5]

The Industrial Court has the power of revisional jurisdiction in accordance with the Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Sections 28, 30 and 44.
However, it must exercise this power of superintendence if there is an apparent error of law on the face of the record, without exceeding its limits of jurisdiction in reassessing the entire evidence and material before the inquiry officer.
[Para 5]
