Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Section 11 - Misconduct - Any misconduct committed prior to joining the Force cannot be a punishable offense under the Act - As such, persons cannot be punished under Section 11 for such act and conduct.
[Para 5] B Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Section 11(1) - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Rule 27 - Minor punishment - Section 11(1) of CRPF Act pertains only to minor punishments - Therefore, the punishment of compulsory retirement, which is considered a major punishment under Rule 27 of CRPF Rules, can only be imposed according to the prescribed procedure for that purpose and cannot be imposed under Section 11(1) of the Act.
