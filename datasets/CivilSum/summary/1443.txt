The Tamil Nadu Special Police Subordinate Service Rules, 1978, Rule 14(b) Explanation 1 requirement for candidates to disclose any criminal case that they are involved in is constitutionally valid.
This Rule is founded on an intelligible differentia that has a nexus with the objective of scanning and classifying individuals involved in criminal cases.
The object is achieved by maintaining the purity of the administration by identifying them at the threshold of entry into service.
The employer is entitled to prescribe qualifications and disqualifications for any appointment.
(Paras 11 to 13, 19 and 40)

Denying promotions due to punishment or conviction for misconduct does not amount to double jeopardy.
The principle is not applicable to service jurisprudence, and even an acquitted person in criminal proceedings on the ground of benefit of doubt can be subjected to disciplinary action.
(Paras 28 to 30)

Failure to disclose criminal proceedings pending in the application form for recruitment disqualifies a candidate.
Even if acquitted subsequently, the candidate remains disqualified for the current and future selections.
(Para 35)
