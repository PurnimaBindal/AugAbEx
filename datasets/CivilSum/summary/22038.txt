Constitution of India, Article 311 - Delhi Police (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1980, Rules 25(1)(e) and 25(1)(f) - Remand of case - Punishment - When an appellate authority remands a case for further inquiry, the sub-rule under which the appeal was disposed of must be mentioned in the order.
However, if the same order is justified under the law, the failure to mention the provision in the order will not invalidate it.
