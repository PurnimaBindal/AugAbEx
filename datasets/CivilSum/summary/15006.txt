Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19 - Absorption in service - Relaxation of age - The respondent was rejected for a claim to be absorbed in a Group-D post on the basis of being over aged - The petitioners did not follow their own guidelines - The Rules have a provision for relaxation of age - The respondent met the conditions for such relaxation - The claim of the respondent was wrongly rejected - The Tribunal's decision to allow the respondent's application for absorption was proper and justified.
