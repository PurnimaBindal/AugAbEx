Constitution of India Articles 14, 23 and 226 - Salary - Posting and transfers - Government servants cannot be posted to cash or other positions of greater responsibility without proper orders - Humiliating lower staff and depriving employees of wages violates fundamental employee rights - Violations entitle citizens to compensation and responsible parties must be held accountable - Officers directed to pay costs and release all dues, state instructed to take legal action - Petitioner treated as on duty for entire period not allowed to work/join.
