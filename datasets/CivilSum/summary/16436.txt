Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 38 - Compassionate appointment - Scheme for Appointment of Dependents of Deceased Employees on Compassionate Grounds - Applicant's eligibility for employment - Determination of sources of income of bereaved family - Competent authority's recommendation that income from all resources of the declining was insufficient for its sustenance - Bank denial of request based on subsequent events not known at original consideration by competent authority - Decision, that while compassionate appointment cannot be claimed as a right, the discretion to deny it cannot be exercised without any rationale basis ignoring the ground realities and large size of the family, if the applicant is eligible for employment.
