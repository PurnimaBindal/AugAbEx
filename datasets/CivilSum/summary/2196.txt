Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Natural Justice - Inquiry report - Non-compliance with principles of natural justice - Failure to supply copy of inquiry report - Even if not provided for in service rules, principles of natural justice require that a copy be supplied to the delinquent - Imposing punishment without supplying it cannot be sustained.
[Paras 11 and 12]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Natural Justice - Inquiry report - Prejudice caused by non-supply of copy of inquiry report - Inquiry report exonerating the accused - Authority differing with findings of inquiry officer and holding accused guilty of charges - No evidence in support of charges - All prosecution witnesses and evidence in favour of accused - Non-supply of report prejudicial to accused - [Paras 13, 15 and 16]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Natural Justice - Speaking order - Imposition of major punishment on employee via a non-speaking order cannot be sustained, especially in the absence of evidence supporting the charges - Authority must furnish inquiry report to employee and pass a well-reasoned order to support its findings.
[Para 18]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Natural Justice - Inquiry report - Remanding case to punishing authority to proceed afresh - Not always justified - Where punishment order suffers from various infirmities and lack of evidence, it isn't fair to relegate employee to punishing authority after 12 years of litigation - Order of punishment quashed.
