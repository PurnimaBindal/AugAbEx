The Punishment imposed on an employee in response to their misconduct should be closely related to the misconduct committed, as per Constitution of India Article 311(2) - Misconduct - Natural justice - Allegations of not investing arrears of dearness allowance of employees in purchase of national savings certificates due to non-supply of requisite information by officials concerned - Reversion which is stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect and reduction in pay scale for 2 years is against the rule of natural justice.
[Para 8]
Constitution of India Article 311(2) - Misconduct - Enquiry - Natural justice dictates that it is against the principles of natural justice, for the punishing authority to conduct both the preliminary enquiry, frame the chargesheet, conduct the regular enquiry and to inflict punishments himself.
