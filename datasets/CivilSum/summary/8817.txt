Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Retrenchment - Termination - The Labour Court had to determine whether the management's termination of a worker by abolishing his post, without following the ID Act or providing an opportunity, was justified.
The Labour Court ruled that the termination was justified because the worker's post was abolished.
However, the worker was actually holding a different post than his original appointment.
The non-speaking award, which did not provide any reasons, was deemed invalid and sent back to the Labour Court for a fresh decision with full opportunity for both parties.
