Articles 14, 16 and 226 of India's Constitution - Special pay - Special pay is granted based on duties, responsibilities and qualifications - Not every employee in the same pay scale is entitled to equal special pay - Only those with additional responsibilities or difficult job tasks are paid special pay - If Mandal Revenue Officers receive special pay on the Pay Revision Commission's recommendations, employees such as Head Clerks, Additional Head Clerks, Central Nazirs and Sheristadars of the judicial department holding the equivalent position in the same pay scale cannot claim special pay solely on the ground of having equal pay scales.

Articles 14, 16 and 226 of India's Constitution - Special pay - Allowance - Equal pay for equal work - Head Clerks working in all courts will receive a supervisory allowance of Rs. 50/- recommended by the Pay Revision Commission - Restricting the allowance to certain courts' employees is arbitrary and discriminatory - However, posts of Nazirs and Sheristadars in the same pay scale with Head Clerks only receive the allowance attached to that position when they perform the duties of Head Clerk.
