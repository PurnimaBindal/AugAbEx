According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 and the Junior Telecom Officers Recruitment Rules, 1996 and 1999, there are specific quotas for direct recruitment and promotion.
The Board of Directors of BSNL cannot simply divert posts from the direct recruitment quota to the promotion quota without proper justification.
In this case, such a decision was made without valid reason, and the Board must restore the direct recruitment quota posts.
