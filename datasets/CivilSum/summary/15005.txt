Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - Petitioner relieved of his duties despite the absence of a transfer order and in compliance with the transfer order of Respondent 5, who was transferred to a vacant post in his personal interest - No public interest was at stake - The Superintending Engineer should have sought advice from the office or refused the joining of Respondent 5 once there was no vacant post available at the petitioner's place - The petitioner's relieving was deemed illegal - The petitioner must receive the salary for the intervening period- The court ordered costs of Rs. 10,000/-.
