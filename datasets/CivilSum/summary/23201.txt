Constitution of India, Article 165 - General Clause Act, 1897, Section 13 - Additional Advocate General in High Courts - The post of Additional Advocate General is not a constitutional office and they are not authorized to perform any constitutional or statutory functions.
The appointment made by the State is only in the exercise of its executive power.

Constitution of India, Article 165 - General Clause Act, 1897, Section 13 - Additional Advocate General in High Courts - The provisions of the Constitution should be construed having regard to the expression used therein.
If the meaning of the words is plain and unambiguous, the Courts are bound to give effect to that meaning, irrespective of the consequences.
