Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 - Res judicata - Delay - Disciplinary proceedings - The petitioner faced charge-sheet and punishment, but that was quashed by the Civil Court whose judgment and decree were not challenged - It was held that no fresh charge-sheet could be issued on the same allegations - Moreover, no stale allegations could be brought up at the time of retirement of an employee - The authority was directed to release all retiral benefits of the petitioner with 9% interest.
