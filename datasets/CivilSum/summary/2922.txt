Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2 (KKK), 25C - Lay off - Deputy Labour Commissioner grants permission for lay off - Single judge stays permission - Balance of Convenience favors employer as Petitioner can be compensated in future - Thus, stay order must be set aside.
[Paras 9 and 10] B. Allahabad High Court Rules, Rule 5 - Special Appeal - Stay order affects vital and valuable right of Appellant - Since order under appeal resembles a judgment, Special Appeal is maintainable.
