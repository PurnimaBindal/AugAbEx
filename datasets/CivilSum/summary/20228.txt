The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300-A - Pension - Dearness Relief - The denial of Dearness Relief on pension after re-employment is justifiable because payment of Dearness Relief twice would occur.
[Para 3]
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Policy - Discrimination - Ex-servicemen face different and disadvantageous treatment from re-employed civilians concerning the payment of Dearness Relief - The government is to reconsider the policy.
[Para 4]
Recovery - Excess payment of Dearness Relief - To prevent hardship for employees who have received payments in due course, the government has been restrained from recovering excess payments.
[Para 5]
Armed Forces - Naval Forces - The term `Armed Forces' in the Letter dated 28101975 and office Memorandum dated 181975 of Ministry of Defence includes Naval Forces too.
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 162 - Service conditions - Instructions - In situations where the service conditions are not regulated by statutory rules, it is an established fact that service conditions may be altered by executive instructions.
