Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.1 - Police Act, 1861, Section 46 - Suspension - Payment of subsistence allowance - Officer who remained absent from duty for 11 months was suspended - The officer must attend roll call and be available to the authorities during the period of suspension - Not receiving subsistence allowance during the suspension period does not give the officer the right to be absent from duty - The officer was removed from service for willful absence and the punishment was converted to compulsory retirement.
