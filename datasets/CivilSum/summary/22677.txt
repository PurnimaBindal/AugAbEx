Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab Seperior Judicial Service Rules, 1963, Rule 8-A - Seniority/Reservation - In cases where no scheduled caste candidates are available for a reserved vacancy at a particular roster point and it cannot be carried forward, if filled by other categories, later eligible candidates of the previous category cannot claim seniority against those roster points.
