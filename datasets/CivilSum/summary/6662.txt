Constitution of India, Articles 226, 300-A - Andhra Pradesh Revised Pension Rules, 1980, Rule 9 - Pension - Recovery from pension - The proposed action to recover from the pension of the petitioner is illegal as there is no evidence to suggest that any inquiry was ongoing against him prior to retirement.
Moreover, no charge sheet was issued and it cannot be determined that any departmental proceedings had been initiated against him on the date of his retirement.
