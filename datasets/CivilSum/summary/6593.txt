The Constitution of India, Article 14 - Natural justice - Enquiry - Employers cannot alter the date of birth in an employee's service record without proper enquiry and giving proper opportunity to the employee concerned.
An enquiry is not required when declining the request of the employee for alteration of date of birth, except when the employee is entitled to such alteration under the service rules or contract of his service, or when the alteration is based on court directions or accepted by the employer.
[Para 5]

The Constitution of India, Article 14 - Date of superannuation - Increments - Leave encashment - If an employee continues in service after the date of superannuation due to some dispute or doubt with regard to the date of superannuation, he is not entitled to any increment or leave encashment for that period.
This is because the employee had no right to remain in service for that period, and it cannot be considered as authorised service.
The employee will only be entitled to benefits up to the determined date of superannuation.
