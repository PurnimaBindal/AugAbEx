The Post of Resident Director (Foreign Office) (Level-I) is a Selection Group under Constitution Articles 16 and 226.
To be eligible for Level-I, officers must be in the pay scale of 4100-5300 with two years of service in the grade.
The selection is challenged on the grounds that ad hoc employees have no right to the post they are holding.
The court holds that the requirement of two years' service in the grade cannot be met with ad hoc service so the selection is set-aside.
[Para 5]

According to Constitution Article 226, the evaluation of the relative performance of candidates is the function of the Selection Committee.
However, the court has jurisdiction to interfere when the Committee exceeds its authority and selects an unqualified candidate not before it for selection to Level-I Post.
As a result, the selection is set-aside.
