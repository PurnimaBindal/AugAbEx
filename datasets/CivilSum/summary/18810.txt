Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 and 227 - H.S.E.B.
Employees (Conduct) Regulations, 1984, Regulations 19 and 20(1) - Removal from service - The petitioner was accused of inciting employees to participate in a strike, while others who actually took part had their cases withdrawn.
The petitioner was treated unfairly compared to other employees and therefore the order of removal from service was quashed.
The petitioner was ordered to be reinstated, with continuity of service and all other service benefits.
