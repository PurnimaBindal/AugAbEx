Bombay Civil Services Rules, 1959, Rule 161 - Compulsory retirement - Public interest - Service record - Mere involvement in a criminal case is not enough to justify compulsory retirement, especially if there are no adverse entries in the employee's work and conduct records.
The order for retirement must be based on public interest and not passed as a punitive measure.
[Paras 21 and 22]  B. Bombay Civil Services Rules, 1959, Rule 161(1)(aa)(i) - Compulsory retirement - Competent authority - The appointing authority has the jurisdiction and authority to decide on compulsory retirement, not the Review Committee.
The Committee should not make conclusions on an employee's efficiency or integrity without material in their service record.
[Para 20]  C Bombay Civil Services Rules, 1959, Rule 161 - Compulsory retirement - Service record - Annual Character Roll - The efficiency, honesty or integrity of an employee is discerned based on their ACRs from the beginning of their service until the decision for their retirement.
This helps determine whether their compulsory retirement is in public interest for maintaining efficiency in the administration.
