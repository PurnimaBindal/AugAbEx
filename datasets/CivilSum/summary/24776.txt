According to the Employees Provident Fund and Misc.
Provisions Act, 1952, Section 16(1)(b)(c) and (d) (as substituted by 1988 amendment) as well as the Rajasthan Non- Government Educational Institutions Act, 1989, establishments belonging to or controlled by Governments are exempt from certain regulations including educational institutions that are recognized or aided by the State of Rajasthan.
These institutions are entitled to exemption under Section 16(1)(b) of the EPF Act, 1952, and the 1952 Act itself provides for exemption for establishments that have their own equivalent schemes or rules providing equal benefits as those provided by the Act.
The State Act is considered a complete code, and the government has substantive control over institutions even if they are not owned by the government.
The MP Shikshak Congress v. RPF Commnr.
case is distinguishable from this Act since there were no similar provisions in the State Act, but in this case, the Rajasthan Govt. has made parallel provisions in its Act and has full power of superintendence and authority to direct, restrict or regulate the workings of educational institution and has issued a notification in exercise of its power under Section 17 of the EPF Act, 1952 exempting these institutions.
