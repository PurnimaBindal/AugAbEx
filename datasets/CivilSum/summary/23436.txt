Constitution of India, Article 309 - Delhi Higher Judicial Service Rules, 1970, Rules 2(b) and (d) - Cadre Post - All posts in the service, whether permanent or temporary, are considered Cadre Posts.
The definition of 'Cadre Post' in Rule 2(b) means any post specified in the Schedule and includes a temporary post carrying the same designation as that of any of the posts specified in the Schedule.
Therefore, every promotee who holds the post of an Additional District and Sessions Judge in the Service is the holder of a Cadre Post, regardless of whether the post is permanent or temporary.
Direct recruits, on the other hand, hold cadre posts in all events because they can only be appointed to substantive posts in the Service on a permanent basis.
[Paras 19 to 21]

Quota rule - One third of the substantive post in the service must be reserved for direct recruits in order to rotate vacancies between direct recruits and promotees.
