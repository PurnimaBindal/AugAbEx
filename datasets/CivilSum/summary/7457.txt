Swatantrata Sainik Samman Pension Scheme - Eligibility for benefit - The petitioner was imprisoned for only four months, falling short of the requirement of six months as per the scheme.
Though Surja's case allowed for the sentence of six months being the key factor, the subsequent decision in Manohar Lal Azad's case stated that six months of incarceration is necessary, rendering the petitioner ineligible for the benefit.

Swatantrata Sainik Samman Pension Scheme - Recovery of withdrawn benefit - The petitioner, being 94 years old, did not misrepresent and was given the benefit when Surja's case was the settled legal position, hence the recovery cannot be made, particularly without evidence of the petitioner possessing assets to repay.
