Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 16 - Suit for declaration - Jail Warder - Termination of service - Challenge as to - The appellant claimed that no opportunity of hearing was given and that his appointment was subject to the condition of character verification.
Even though the High Court reduced the sentence, it was held that since he is a member of a disciplined force, he was expected to prevent the commission of crime.
In a departmental proceeding, a delinquent can be removed on the grounds of misconduct which led to his conviction.
The termination of the appellant was thus upheld.
