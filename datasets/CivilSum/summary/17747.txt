Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Aptitude test - Seniority - Settlement - Post of Advanced Ledger Posting Machine/Advanced Electronic Accounting Machine Operator (s) - Under the settlement, people who passed the aptitude test would be placed on a panel, which would be three times the amount of vacancies - The claimant's only grievance is that his name should have been ranked higher than those who passed the test later, which is unfounded because the selection was not for a promotional post, but for operating certain machines with a special allowance - Operators for these machines were selected from the clerical cadre based on seniority, where the petitioner is ranked lower than the respondents - The petitioner has no legal ground for a writ of mandamus - The petition is dismissed.
