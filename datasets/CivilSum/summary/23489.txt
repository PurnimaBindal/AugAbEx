Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 311-A - Dismissal from service/Pension - Post-retiral dismissal order - The competent authority cannot dismiss an employee from service after their retirement date.
However, they may note in the order that the employee would have been dismissed if it had been before their retirement.
If the rules permit, the disciplinary authority may also take actions such as withholding or forfeiting the employee's pension.
[Paras 3, 4 & 5] B Punjab and Haryana Civil Services Manual, Vol. II, Rule 22 - Central Civil Services Pension Rules, Rule 9 as has been made applicable to the staff of the High Court of Punjab and Haryana - The Chief Justice of the High Court has the power to take action under both Rule 22 of the Punjab and Haryana Civil Service Manual, Vol. II and Rule 9 of the Central Civil Services Pension Rules against an employee who has received a punishment order after they have retired or during the period of re-employment after retirement i.e. extended period of service.
