Section 24 of the Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, and Rule 36 of the Bar Council of India Rules pertain to appointment of Law Officers.
The Learned District and Sessions Judge did not issue a notification to call for bio-data from eligible candidates for the position.
Instead, he provided information to the President of the Bar Association, and some advocates submitted their bio-datas accordingly.
The Learned District Judge assessed the eligible candidates and prepared a panel, from which one candidate was appointed as Law Officer.
Despite the method being different from the one prescribed in Rule 36 of the Bar Council of India Rules, the panel formed by the Learned District Judge could not be deemed invalid.
