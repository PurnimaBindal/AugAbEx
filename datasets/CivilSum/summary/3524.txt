Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Absence from duty - Dismissal from service - Condoning or regularizing an absence period by treating it as leave of any kind or leave without pay under the authority, results in the allegation of misconduct on account of absence no longer being sustained.
Whether the decision to treat the period of absence as leave is made prior to or during the punishment order being passed, the punishment of dismissal cannot be justified - even while treating the absence as leave of any kind.
Although the employee will not be entitled to back wages for the period of absence, they will be entitled to full back wages for the period they were kept out of service on account of the termination order.
[Paras 5, 6, 10 and 11]
