Fundamental Rules, Rule 22(1)(A)(1) (previously FR 22-C) - Pay fixation - Promotion - Promotion to the post of Junior Engineer (Grade I) - Increased responsibilities - The appellant was promoted to Junior Engineer based on an agreement between the Junior Engineers Association and the Central Government.
The applicability of FR 22-C at the time was being considered, and this was finalized in accordance with the agreement.
The appellant failed to provide any evidence showing he had taken on additional responsibilities during the time he received the Junior Engineer (Grade I) scale, so there was no justification for him to claim benefits under FR 22-C.
