Service Regulations, Part II - Regularisation - A scheme was implemented for regularisation of workers employed as casual labour, village electricity workers, or contract labour - The non-regularisation of petitioners based on being minors at the time of initial engagement and over-aged during consideration of regularisation - There is no minimum age limit for appointment in the Service Regulations - There is no violation of public policy or irregularity upon initial appointment - Petitioners are not disqualified due to the absence of an entry age limit - Directions are given to review petitioners' case for regularisation.
