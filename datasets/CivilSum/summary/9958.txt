The writ petition challenges the detention of a petitioner under close arrest and his continued detention in accordance with the Army Act, 1950, Sections 3(1) and 9, and Army Rules, Rule 27(3), regarding active service.
The petitioner, who was due to retire upon reaching the age of superannuation, continues in active service due to 'Operation RHINO.'
As such, the Army Rule would not be applicable, as it is only applicable in cases where the petitioner is not on active service.
It was held that there was no requirement for the continued detention of the petitioner for more than two months without the sanction of the Chief of Army Staff or more than three months without the approval of the Central Government.
