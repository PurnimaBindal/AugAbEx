Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Section 2(s) - Workman - Petitioner's service terminated in a supervisory capacity - Labour court found petitioner not a workman under Section 2(s) - Court holds labour court's decision not perverse nor based on no evidence - Court cannot substitute its view for labour court's - Evidence suggests petitioner was in a supervisory capacity - Court cannot interfere with well-reasoned finding of fact - Petition dismissed.
