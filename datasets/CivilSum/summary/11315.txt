According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, the Petitioners were first hired as Packers (Class IV) with a Pay Scale of 300-430, later becoming Central Sterilization Supply Department Attendants (CSSD Attendants) with a Pay Scale of Rs. 400-600.
While the Packers' Pay Scales were revised to the scale of Rs. 750-940 and later to Rs. 950-1400 by the Public Health deptt.
of Haryana Govt., the CSSD Attendants' pay scale was never revised despite their higher job position compared to Packers - who are the feeder cadre of C.S.S.D.
attendants.
The high court, in its judgment, revised the pay scale of operation theatre attendants to Rs. 1200-2040 from 1194.
The Petitioners argue that as C.S.S.D.
attendants, they are just as entitled to the same scale of Rs. 1200-2040 w.e.f.
1194.
