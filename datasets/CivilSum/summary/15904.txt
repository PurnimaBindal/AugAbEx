Constitution of India, Article 309, - Rajasthan Subordinate Accounts Service Rules, 1963, Rules 6(iii) and 23(iii) - Appointment - A petitioner applied for the post of Junior Accountant among the Ministerial staff, but the reserved quota for Ministerial staff was already filled.
Although the petitioner's name was on the empanelment list, it was not found on the merit list.
Empanelment does not grant a vested right to the petitioner, who cannot claim benefits both as a reserved Ministerial staff candidate and as a general candidate.
This is because of empanelment, which came after more than six years.
