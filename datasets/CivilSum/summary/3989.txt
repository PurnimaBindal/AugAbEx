Constitution of India, Article 226 - Indian Penal Code, 1860, Section 304 - Court Martial Proceedings - Conviction - Dismissal - The petitioner was charged with culpable homicide not amounting to murder during Court Martial proceedings and was convicted and sentenced to rigorous imprisonment for a period of 10 years, as well as dismissal from service.
While a scuffle occurred between the deceased and petitioner and a bullet fired in the process, the Court Martial did not make a definite finding that the petitioner was responsible for causing the death of the deceased.
Conviction for the offence under Section 304 IPC can only happen if the act causing the death was done with the knowledge that it would cause death, and without recorded evidence that the trigger was pulled by the petitioner, it cannot be held that he was responsible.
Therefore, the conviction and sentence imposed on the petitioner, as well as the order for dismissal, are to be set aside and the petitioner is directed to be released immediately, with the direction for the respondents to take him back into service, with the time spent in jail counting towards his duty.
