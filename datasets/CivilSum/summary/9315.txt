Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11 - Quantum of punishment - A Conductor in Punjab Roadways was charged with allegations of misconduct, specifically not issuing and wrong punching of tickets after collecting money from passengers.
The Enquiry Officer found that the misconduct was due to heavy rush and not intentional, and exonerated him.
However, the Punishing Authority disagreed with the Enquiry Officer's findings and terminated the petitioner's services without providing any reason.
The Labour Court held the petitioner guilty of wrong punching of tickets, but quashed the punishment and forfeited past service and back wages.
The Enquiry Officer found that the charge of wrong punching was due to heavy rush, and the labour court did not provide any positive findings.
As a result, the petitioner is entitled to full back wages and continuity of service.
