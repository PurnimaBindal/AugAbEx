Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dismissal - Disciplinary Proceedings - A petitioner was found guilty of several charges, causing the mill to suffer significant losses due to misconduct, resulting in a finding of guilt.
The fact-finding on charges against the petitioner revealed that during their tenure, the mill's performance drastically declined, resulting in significant losses.
It is important to note that a decision based on facts cannot be interfered with in writ jurisdiction.
