The Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 1(3)(b) - Stone quarries - Establishments that quarry stones are within the scope of the Act.
[Para 2]
The Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 1(3)(b), 4 and 7(i) - Mines and Minerals (Regulation and Development) Act, 1952 - The appellant is quarrying stones under a mining lease and cutting or chipping them to the appropriate size before marketing.
However, running a stone quarry is the primary activity, and marketing is a subsidiary and incidental activity.
The High Court properly determined that the appellants are engaged in the manufacturing process, and the establishment is subject to the Act following the issuance of notifications on 12.2.77 and 19.2.77.
