Constitution of India Articles 14 and 16 - Admissions/Education - Age limit - Despite no age limit having been prescribed in the prospectus at the time of issuing, the formulation of admission policy was within the university's power.
Furthermore, the University did not prescribe any upper age limit.
However, the Admission Committee of the College decided to establish an age limit subsequently by framing guidelines.
Hence, the petitioner cannot be barred from admission due to being over-aged.
