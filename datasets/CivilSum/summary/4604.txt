Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Railways Establishment Code, Vol. II, Rule 2423-A - Retrial benefit - The question of whether a cut off date for certain retiral benefits violates Article 14 of the Constitution, was raised - According to the Tribunal, officials can attain weightage in qualifying service for pension computation if the post to which they were appointed required postgraduate research, specialized qualification, or experience in professional or technological fields, and had an upper age limit of more than 25 years - However, the Tribunal's decision to grant relief was based on the view that the cut off date was arbitrary, and that although Assistant Surgeons did not meet all the conditions, the same relief was applicable due to promotion - This decision was improper, as cut off dates are necessary when granting retiral benefits to certain employees - Therefore, the Tribunal erred in granting the benefits to respondents.
