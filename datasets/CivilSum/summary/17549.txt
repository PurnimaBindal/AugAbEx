According to Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India and the Punjab Agricultural Service (Class II) Rules of 1974, Assistant Geologists in the Agricultural Department were promoted to Geologists Hydrogeologists and their posts were granted the same scale of pay as Assistant Engineers/SDEs.
The Geologists in the Agriculture Department were also given the pay scale at par with Xens in the Irrigation Department.
Therefore, the petitioners were entitled to the benefit of senior scale and selection grade after 8 and 18 years of service respectively.
The court issued a mandamus in their favor for the grant of the senior scale and selection grade from the date of completion of the required years of service.
