According to the Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 9, directing de novo inquiry is not permissible, and a further inquiry may only be ordered to remove any defects in the initial proceedings.
The order to direct de novo inquiry may pose significant risks and consequences to the delinquent, and may be quashed with the liberty to proceed from the stage where any defect occurred in the proceedings.
[Para 9]
If charges against a public servant are dropped, then the order of exoneration is quasi-judicial in nature and cannot be varied without the relevant statute or rules authorizing such a variation.
[Para 9]
According to the Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 9, in order to ensure the fairness of administrative action, the punishing authority cannot participate in the proceedings as a complainant, witness, or party to the decision-making process.
The President of the Municipal Council is presumed to have considerable influence in the Council's decision-making process, and if they have an interest in the decision, it can reasonably be inferred that there is a real danger of bias.
