Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Transfer - Petitioner was stationed at one place for 23 years - Employees do not have the right to remain at a specific location - The authority is responsible for determining if a person should be transferred considering the requirements of administration - No malintent has been shown - The transfer was made in the public interest and to meet the needs of the administration - The order does not violate any statutory regulations - The petition was dismissed.
[Paras 4 and 5]
