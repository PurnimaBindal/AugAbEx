Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of Deceased Government Employees Rules, 2003, Rules 3(e), 8(e) and 13 - Compassionate appointment - Age limit - Despite the age limit of 25 years incorporated in Rules 8(e) and 13 for a married person to be considered for compassionate appointment, the claim cannot be rejected solely based on age - If the applicant meets all other conditions, their claim must be reviewed on its merits without consideration of age.
