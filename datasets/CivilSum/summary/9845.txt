Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation - Petitioner appointed by Hostel Committee - Hostel Committee passed a resolution to regularise the services of a petitioner - Hostel is attached to the women's college of TTD Devasthanam - Management refused to regularise petitioner's services on the basis of violation of Rules - Court ruled that since the services of some persons appointed after petitioner were regularised, there is no ground for denying petitioner's regularisation - Respondent instructed to regularise petitioner's services.
