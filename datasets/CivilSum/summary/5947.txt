According to Section 31(q) of the Haryana Agricultural University Act, 1970 and Clause 20(9) of the Statutes under Haryana Agricultural University Act, 1961, an employee cannot be declared guilty of misconduct if they overstay without an application for 1-1/2 years, even though their actions are not appreciated.
The University's action of declaring the employee's post vacant without giving them a chance to explain themselves violated the principles of natural justice.
As a result, the employee is reinstated with fifty per cent back wages.
