Section 11 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 pertains to ex parte awards and their setting aside.
Although the application to set aside the award was rejected on the basis that the Labour Court had already passed an award and was functus officio, this decision was not appropriate.
This is because Section 11 provides ample powers to Tribunals to adopt their own procedures in order to ensure justice.
This includes the authority to conduct adjudication on existing industrial disputes.
As a result, the application to set aside ex parte award was granted, and the reference was restored.
The matter was remanded to the Labour Court for new judgment, as seen in the Grindlays Bank Ltd v. Central Government Industrial Tribunal and others, 1980 (Sup.)
SCC 420 case.
