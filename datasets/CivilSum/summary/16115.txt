The departmental enquiry against a Government servant must be completed promptly, as delay will vitiate the proceedings.
[Para 5] B.
The onus of proving charges against an employee is upon the prosecution and a mere letter cannot be used as evidence unless the signatory is available for cross-examination.
[Para 6] C. If a charged employee is not provided with a list of witnesses and documents against him, and not given a chance to lead evidence in his defense, the inquiry will be considered invalid.
[Para 6] D. The enquiry officer must discuss all charges and evidence presented, as failure to do so will vitiate the proceedings.
