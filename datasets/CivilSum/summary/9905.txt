According to Articles 16 and 226 of the Indian Constitution, in regards to Writ Jurisdiction/Select List, while being empanelled in the list for appointment to the post of Lower Division Clerk, it should be noted that it does not guarantee any right to be appointed to the post.
Thus, a writ of mandamus claiming appointment is not maintainable.
[Paras 7 and 9]
As per Article 16 of the Indian Constitution, regarding Seniority and Empanelment, a petitioner cannot claim seniority over candidates who were appointed to a post earlier than the petitioner simply based on being empanelled earlier.
[Para 6]
