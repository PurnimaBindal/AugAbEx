Constitution of India, - Article 226 - Date of Birth - Petitioner, retired on the basis of the date of birth initially entered in service record, filed affidavit for change of date of birth after 20 years and correction made in record on the same day.
Court held that allowing change of date of birth on basis of employee affidavit would open flood gates of fraudulent changes.
Court found the affidavit to be insufficient evidence and concluded that the entry of date of birth was manufactured and concocted by petitioner for his own benefit.
The petition was dismissed.
