The Circular dated 931990 hinders the right of teachers to receive higher pay after acquiring higher qualifications - the distinction between teachers appointed as Masters with higher qualifications and those who acquire them while in service is unjustified and the State Government's failure to grant the grade of Masters to the latter violates Articles 14 and 16 - the Respondents are directed to provide petitioners with the pay scales of Masters as soon as they acquire the required higher qualification.
