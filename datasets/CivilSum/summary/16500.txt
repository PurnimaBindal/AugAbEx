Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate Appointment - Delay - The petitioner's claim for compassionate appointment is dismissed due to delay in application.
Premature retirement on medical grounds of the petitioner's mother, while the petitioner's father is already a pension holder, does not automatically entitle the petitioner to such appointment.
Financial condition of the family is the sole consideration for such appointments.
Held, since the petitioner is not suffering from extreme financial hardship, delay alone defeats the claim for such appointment.
