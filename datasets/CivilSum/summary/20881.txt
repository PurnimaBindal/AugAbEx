The Madhya Pradesh Industrial Training (Gazetted) Service Recruitment Rules, 1985, Rules 13 to 18 Schedules III and IV prescribe the mandatory eligibility qualifications required for appointment to a post - Experience, regardless of its length, cannot substitute for the degree and diploma requirements - Merely having experience as a principal on an ad hoc arrangement without the required Engineering qualifications also renders a person ineligible for appointment.
[Paras 18, 21, 23, 29, 30 and 32 to 34]

An academic qualification in the particular trade or line is necessary for appointment to a post requiring it - Mere experience is no substitute - An example is given that a Compounder cannot claim a post of Doctor solely on the basis of experience.
[Para 34]

Government service is conferred by the Rules and is much more than a contractual relationship - Any terms and conditions that contravene these Rules are inapplicable - A person appointed on an ad hoc basis without the prescribed qualifications cannot claim any status against that post - The Court cannot contravene the Rules by conferring a status solely on human approach.
[Paras 24 to 27 and 34]

Government Service cannot be claimed without meeting the requisite qualifications and following the established procedure for appointment - Courts and tribunals exist to ensure adherence to the Rule of law and cannot relax the eligibility qualifications.
[Para 34]

The concept of "human approach" in preferring a selected candidate over an unqualified person cannot be invoked at the cost of the duly selected candidate.
[Para 31]
