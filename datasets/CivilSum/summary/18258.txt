"According to Article 226 of the Indian Constitution, an employee does not have an enforceable right to a departmental quarter until it has been officially allocated to them.
Merely because an employee was not paid their House Rent Allowance (HRA) does not make unauthorised occupation of a quarter valid.
If an employee is found to be occupying a departmental quarter without authorisation, they can be dismissed from their job.
In this case, the employee was directed to vacate the quarter by management but failed to do so.
Disciplinary proceedings were initiated against the employee for misconduct and, after being given adequate opportunity to provide a hearing, he was dismissed from service.
The management's action was found to be valid.
