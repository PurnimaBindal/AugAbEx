According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 2.2(c)(1), an employee who is retiring during pending Judicial Proceedings or has an investigation instituted after their retirement is entitled to provisional pension until final orders are passed by the Court.
Once the trial Court passes a final order, the State's liability to pay provisional pension automatically terminates as the employee was entitled to only provisional pension and not pension during judicial proceedings.
[Paras 5 and 7]

Withholding pension during judicial proceedings against a retired employee does not require a personal hearing, but providing a show cause notice will be sufficient to meet with the principles of natural justice, as per the Principles of Natural Justice.
[Para 9]
