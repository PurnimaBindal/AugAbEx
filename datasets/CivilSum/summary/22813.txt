Constitution of India, Articles 226 - Appointments - Allegation of forged certificates - A non-affected party can complain to competent administrative authorities if selected candidates obtained appointments through forged certificates - Only the competent authority has the power to investigate and take appropriate departmental and criminal action - Filing a writ petition is not an effective solution - Appointees who have been in service for over 21 years, and have received promotions, cannot be disturbed by a person alleging forgery.
