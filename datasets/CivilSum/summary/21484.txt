Section 4(6) of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 is supplemented by the Coal India Executives Conduct, Discipline and Appeal Rules, 1978, Rules 27 & 342 - Disciplinary proceedings - Pre-mature retirement during disciplinary proceedings - Continuation of disciplinary proceedings after retirement - Waiver of right to continue proceedings if retirement is permitted without extending service for the purpose.
[Para 9]  B Withholding of Gratuity is governed by both Section 4(6) of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 and Coal India Executives Conduct, Discipline and Appeal Rules, 1978, Rules 27 & 34.3 - Gratuity can only be withheld if the conditions of Sec. 4(6) of the Act are satisfied, and only when termination is caused by conduct considered riotous or disorderly, or an act of violence or conviction involving moral turpitude.
