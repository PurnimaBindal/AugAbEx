Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Natural justice - Termination - Petitioner was charge-sheeted and given five days to respond - Nevertheless, the respondent terminated his employment before the stipulated time frame and based on the petitioner's alleged lack of qualification - This act is indicative of hostility and the ensuing termination order is invalid.
[Para 6]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Relaxation of qualifications - The petitioner applied for a post that required qualifications he did not possess - However, he was interviewed, selected, and assured of the relaxation of qualifications based on his experience - He resigned his earlier job, joined the mill, and received regular increments, special increments, and revised pay scale - The mill repeatedly advertised the same position and selected candidates, but the petitioner was never asked to quit - Using the clause in the appointment letter to terminate the petitioner's services after more than three years is illegitimate and violates the principle of natural justice.
