The police regulations state that absence without leave, overstaying leave, and other forms of misconduct can lead to major punishments.
The term "misconduct" is not specifically defined, so it must be given a general meaning.

When misconduct is not defined in service rules, it must be given a general meaning.

The High Court/Tribunal does not have the power to substitute its own penalty; however, if the punishment imposed shocks the conscience of the court, it can modify the relief appropriately.
In exceptional cases, the court can impose a penalty with supportable reasons.
