Constitution of India, Article 300-A - West Bengal Services (Death-cum-Retirement Benefit) Rules, 1971, Rule 100(1) - Payment of Arrears of Pension (Nomination) Rules, 1986, Rule 4 - Retiral benefits - Pension - Petitioner's application for retiral benefits was rejected by the Tribunal as the deceased employee had nominated his illegally married third wife which was not permissible as per Rule 4 of Payment of Arrears of Pension (Nomination) Rules, 1986 and the deceased had suppressed true facts regarding his family.
Although the petitioner had claimed being a nominee for retiral benefits, the Tribunal considered the petitioner as a stranger to the family of the deceased constable, and therefore, could not be considered as the nominee for retiral benefits.
