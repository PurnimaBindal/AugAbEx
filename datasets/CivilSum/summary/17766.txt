Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Services of Employees) Act, 1974, Section 4 - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Termination of services - Work and conduct - If termination of a college teacher's service during the sanctioned period granted by the DPI was due to inadequate work and conduct, then proper procedures must have been followed under the law.
Even freeing students from their studies before the sanctioned period ends is not grounds for dismissing the teacher's services.
