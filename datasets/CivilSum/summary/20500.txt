Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 300-A - Pension - Calculation of pension - The last ten months' average salary drawn by an employee must be taken into account, in addition to length of service, for computing their pension as per Govt. decision.
The liberalised pension scheme principle from Nakara's case cannot universally extend to mean that the last emoluments of every retiree in the same rank are the same.
The Government rules in force at the time of retirement should determine the calculation of all emoluments.
