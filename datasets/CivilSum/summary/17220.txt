Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 - Pay Scales - Parity - Steno Typists claims to be at par with Sub-Divisional clerks - Both posts require matriculation qualification - Same pay scale was applicable for both posts prior to 111986 - Both posts were eligible for promotion to the next higher post - Determination of duties associated with each post and the pay scales for employees falls under the jurisdiction of expert committees like the Pay Commission - Appellants cannot simply claim parity due to similar pay scales prior to 111986 - Parity in pay scales can only be established if the government had consciously decided to equate the two categories in the past - No such decision was made in this case - The appeal has been rejected.
