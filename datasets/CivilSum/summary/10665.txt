Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Pension - Family pension - Delay and laches - The delay and laches cannot be used as grounds to deny the claim of pension, especially when the authority itself is responsible for the delay.
[Para 14]  B. Rajasthan Work-charged Rules, 1964, Rules 3(3) & 22-A - Pension - Family pension - Qualifying service - If a work-charged employee completes qualifying service for a permanent status, but no order is passed by the competent authority, the employee or his/her dependents cannot be deprived of pensionary benefits.
The authority is directed to pass appropriate orders for the grant of permanent status to the deceased employee and family pension to his widow.
