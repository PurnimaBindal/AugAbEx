The Petitioner in this case is seeking promotion in the Army despite receiving severe displeasure in their ACR's for the years of 1991-1992 and 1992-1993.
Despite being considered for promotion on three separate occasions in the past, they were deemed unfit.
The Petitioner is requesting that the remarks of their Reporting Officer be expunged from their record to aid in their promotion.
However, it was held that the Respondent acted in accordance with the Army Act and Rules in their decision not to promote the Petitioner.
[Paras 19, 22 to 26]
