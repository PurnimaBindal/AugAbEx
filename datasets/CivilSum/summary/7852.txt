The Gujarat Silk Textiles Mill Undertakings (Nationalisation) Act, 1986, Sections 2(h), 3, 4(6), 7 Schedule 2 and Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 4 are both relevant to determining liability for the payment of gratuity to workmen.
The IInd Schedule of the Gujarat Silk Textiles Mill Undertakings (Nationalisation) Act provides for the payment of all dues of employees of a sick unit during both the pre and post-takeover management period.
Consequently, the corporation that took over the mill is liable to pay gratuity to workmen under the aforementioned acts.
