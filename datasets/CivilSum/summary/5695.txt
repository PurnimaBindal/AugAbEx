Contempt of Court Act, 1971, Sections 2(b) and 12 - Contempt of court - Order of suspension and compulsory retirement set aside by a single Bench - Order of single bench confirmed by division bench - Petitioner argues that they cannot be deemed suspended after having stood superannuated and compulsory retirement being set aside - Court holds that a separate order under sub-rule 5 of Rule 10 of CCS (CCA) is required for deemed suspension - Petitioner entitled to wages for the period of suspension - Consequential benefits not paid by respondents - Prima facie case of contempt made out - Contemners given two months to pay wages due to the petitioner, failing which they would be held guilty of contempt and punished in the interest of justice.
