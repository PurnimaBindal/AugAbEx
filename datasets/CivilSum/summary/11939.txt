Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226/227 - Ad hoc appointment - Petitioners who are serving as Lecturers on an ad hoc basis have requested to continue working until regular recommendees from HPSC join the posts - The Supreme Court has allowed ad hoc teachers to continue until persons recommended by the Public Service Commission take over - Thus, the respondents cannot terminate the petitioners until the regular recommendees from HPSC take over.
[Paras 2 and 3]
Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 16 - Ad hoc appointee - One of the petitioners has been holding the post, but a recommendee has joined in - An ad hoc appointment is still working due to the stay order granted by the Motion Bench - Since the recommendee has joined the post held by the petitioner, there is no further right for the petitioner to continue their service.
