"In case of a dispute between two factions claiming to be office-bearers of a union, the Registrar of Trade Unions cannot adjudicate on which group was validly elected or direct the office-bearer to hold an election or nominate a nominee to oversee the election.
Under Section 28 of Trade Unions Act, 1926, the Registrar has no jurisdiction to do so.
Such disputes must be resolved by filing a suit before a civil court.
[Para 9]"
