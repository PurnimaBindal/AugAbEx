According to Section 1(3) of the MP Industrial Disputes Act, 1960, and Section 21 of the M.P. General Clauses Act, 1957, the State Government cannot delete industries from the list of industries specified under the Act as it is not within their power.
In other words, the power conferred upon the State Government under Section 1(3) does not include the power to amend or rescind the notification issued in exercise of power under Section 1(3) declaring the specified industries.
