Prasar Bharati (Broadcasting Corporation of India) Act, 1990, Section 11 - Absorption - Retirement - Options for absorption of Doordarshan and All India Radio employees in Prasar Bharti - The Memorandum dt. 25.2.1999 only applied to existing employees and benefits were only intended for those currently employed, not retirees before 25.2.1999.
The petitioner retired before the existence of Prasar Bharati and was not entitled to the benefits of the Memorandum.
