Constitution of India, Article 309 - Education Rules, 1959 (Kerala) Chapter XIV-A, Rule 43 proviso and Chapter XXI - In filling a vacancy in the cadre of HSA, the ratio of 1:1:1 in core subjects does not necessarily have to be strictly followed.
If minimum subject requirements are met, a UPSA who is a trained graduate should be given promotion, ignoring the 1:1:1 ratio.
To fully effect the proviso, however, one must examine whether there are High School Assistants in the vacancy's subject area who can teach the total number of periods required.
If so, the qualified UPSA will be promoted, running counter to the very rule itself.
The proviso cannot nullify or run counter to the rule, nor can it negate the rule's scope.
As a result, the proviso to Rule 43 must be read down.
[Paras 12 and 15]
