Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 25-F and 25-H - Retrenchment - Seasonal workers - Co-operative Sugar Mills daily wage workers who work during crushing seasons - Did not complete 240 days of work in one calendar year - Can't enjoy the benefits of Section 25-H - Court ordered the respondents to maintain a register and prepare seniority in order to recall the retrenched workers according to their seniority in the crushing seasons.
