Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Delay and laches/Seniority - In 1977, a seniority list was created showing the private respondent as senior to the petitioner.
However, in 1991 the petitioner filed a petition challenging the seniority of the non-official respondent.
The petition failed to disclose these facts and was deemed liable to be dismissed on the grounds of delay, laches, and concealment of facts.
