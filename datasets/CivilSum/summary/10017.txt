Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 21 - Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of Deceased Government Employees Rules, Rule 2006 - Compassionate appointment - Eligibility for compassionate appointment - Dependents of the deceased are not entitled to compassionate appointment if they have survived for a reasonable length of time without assistance or employment - Petitioner received compassionate appointment after 8 years of the death of adoptive mother, despite not being legally entitled to be adopted by the deceased or considered a dependent - His claim for a higher class post was dismissed as an attempt to take undue advantage of the authority's leniency.
