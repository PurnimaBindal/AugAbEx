Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ - Res-judicata - When a writ petition against an order of the Tribunal is dismissed, all contentions are examined and the principle of double jeopardy is applied - If a petitioner files another writ petition after dismissal or review application, he cannot challenge the Tribunal's order again - Res-judicata is not a mere technical rule but seeks to end litigation and save litigants from further harassment.

Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 22(iii)(f) - Tribunal - Power of review - If the Tribunal decides a review application, it cannot entirely re-examine or recall its previous order, but has limited power as specified in Section 22(iii)(f) of 1985 Act - Similar to a Civil Court's power under Order 47 of Code of Civil Procedure, 1908.
