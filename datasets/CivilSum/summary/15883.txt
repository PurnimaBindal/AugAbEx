Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Haryana State Cooperative Land Development Bank Limited, Staff Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1974 - Promotion - Promotion chances - Law officers - Promotion to the post of Assistant Secretary (Law) - Including other posts in the feeder cadre may reduce the chances of promotion but cannot eliminate them - Legitimate expectation of some promotional avenue exists for those who join the service, but promotion cannot be claimed as a right - Therefore, if the best judge of need and facts considers it appropriate to amend the rules by including other cadre posts in the feeder cadre, the rule cannot be invalidated solely because the chances of promotion of petitioners have been diminished.
