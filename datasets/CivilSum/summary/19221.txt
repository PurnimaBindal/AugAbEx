Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution state that every public employment post should be filled considering all eligible persons.
Compassionate appointments are an exception, but they must only be exercised in rare cases to help those in distress.
The petitioner's mother is already employed in the bank and receives a good salary, so it cannot be said that their family is facing financial trouble.
Compassion should aid those in need, not greed.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to enter the service of the bank through compassionate appointment without contest.
[Paras 4 and 5]

Despite a mistake being made when it was written that the petitioner's claim would be considered when they come of age, the rule of equitable estoppel is not applicable.
The petitioner's mother is already employed in the bank, so there is no indefeasible right conferred upon them to be employed by compassionate appointment.
