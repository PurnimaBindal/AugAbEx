Medical Council Act, 1956, Section 11 - Recognition of medical qualifications granted by Universities or Medical Institutions in India - Any medical qualification granted by a University or Medical Institution in India, listed in the first schedule, will be considered a recognized medical qualification - Universities and Institutions not listed in the first schedule can apply to the Central Government for recognition - The first schedule may be amended by notification after consulting the council.
[Para 6] B Medical Council Act, 1956, Section 11 - Recognition and de-recognition of medical degrees - The petitioner obtained a Master of Surgery degree from Punjab University, which is listed under the first schedule - The candidate need not have studied in a recognized institution - As long as the degree is from a recognized university or institution, including those listed in the schedule, the Medical Council cannot de-recognize it.
