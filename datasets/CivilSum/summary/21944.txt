Regarding Short Service Commission benefits, a grievance was filed by personnel regarding their seniority and placement in the department's gradation list.
However, the appeal was dismissed due to the fact that during a period of over a decade, these personnel were consistently treated as junior to the aforementioned persons.
As a result, their inter se rights have already been established and should not be re-opened after such a long period.
This is in accordance with the well-established principle in service matters that seniority must not be tampered with after a reasonable amount of time has passed, as it results in an unjustifiable disturbance of the settled position.
