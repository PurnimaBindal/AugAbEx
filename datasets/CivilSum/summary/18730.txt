Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Officiating against a higher post carrying higher pay without specific appointment - If an employee is assigned to perform duties on a higher-paying post than the one they were appointed to, the employer cannot avoid paying the higher salary on the grounds that no specific order or joining report was issued.
[Paras 9 and 10]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Regularisation - Fixed-term contractual appointment - An authority cannot be compelled to regularize the services of a fixed-term contractual employee after they have completed their term and are no longer in service.
