Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation - Part-time employees working as sweepers or peons in various schools under the education department - The Respondent-State has created a policy for regularisation of Class IV employees in the department - The Petitioners should receive the same relief as in the case of Dharam Pal v. State of Haryana and others, CWP No. 193 of 2001, decided on 2132002.
In that case, after a detailed discussion of the State's regularisation policy, the court issued limited relief to the petitioners.
