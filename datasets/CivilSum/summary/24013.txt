are entitled to retain their services in the said branches after appearing in another competitive examination and being appointed in Irrigation Department against permanent positions by way of transfer - It does not constitute direct recruitment to break their seniority from previous service - As per G.O.
dt. 19.10.1968 and Memo dt. 17.8.1974, such an allocation is deemed to be only transfer and their previous services must be counted for the purpose of seniority - VK Shrotriya v. State of UP, 1998(3) 397 applies whereas State of Gujrat v. GC Desai, 1974 (1) SCC 188 is not applicable to the scenario.
