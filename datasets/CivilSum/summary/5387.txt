Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Casual Labour - Regularisation - Petitioners sponsored by Employment Exchange - Even though the petitioners were hired casually, they were provided with benefits such as various types of bonus and were assigned to jobs that required work to be done forever - A Scheme was directed to be created to adjust the petitioners according to the statutory rules in effect at that time and to absorb them.
