The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, Sections 92, 97 and 480 - Delhi Municipal Corporation Service Regulations, 1959, Regulation 488 - Seniority of adhoc appointments- In the absence of specific rules to the contrary, Delhi High Court consistently views that adhoc appointees who are later regularised should have their service counted for determining seniority.
