The Liberalised pension scheme of Rajasthan Government under Constitution of India, Article 14 involves the fixation of pension based on the last pay drawn.
The government has adopted an ad-hoc formula for this purpose, as it would be a lengthy and difficult process to gather records from 36 months ago.
The ad-hoc formula implemented by the Rajasthan Government is legally valid and correct, and the High Court was incorrect in striking it down.
This judgment has been overturned, and the notification of the ad-hoc formula upheld.
