Army Pension Regulations, 1991, Paragraphs 173 and 183 - Entitlement Rules for Casualty Pensionary Awards, 1982, Rules 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 14, 15 and 16 - Disability pension - "Generalized Seizure" is not eligible for disability pension as it cannot be claimed to be a constitutional disease.
Non-detection of the disease at recruitment stage due to lack of necessary technology or apparatus does not constitute a legal defence.
The burden of proof lies on the authority to prove that the disease is not attributable or aggravatable to military service conditions.
If a disease that is not recorded during recruitment stage leads to discharge on account of disability, it is deemed to be related to military service conditions.
[Paras 9 and 10]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Pension Regulations, 1991, Paragraphs 173 and 183 - Regarding Disability Pension, the opinion of the Medical Board should be respected.
However, if the findings of the Medical Board are contrary to statutory provisions or perverse, the writ court has the authority to interfere.
