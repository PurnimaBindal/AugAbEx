Oriental Bank of Commerce (Officers) Service Regulations, 1982 - Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1980, section 12(2) - Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 14 - Grant of Pay scale/Postings and transfers - Establishment Circular No. 37/7 dated January, 24, 1980 - Appointment and transfer in Rural, Urban, Semi-Urban centres - The choice of posting is the sole responsibility of the employer, and an employee has no say in it, regardless of his/her seniority - Clause (III) of Establishment Circular 37/7 defies reason, as it discriminates aginst Managers who happen to be posted in Rural Branch, but it does not require other officers, posted in Urban Branch, to meet a target - This stipulation is discriminatory, arbitrary, and illegal - As a Nationalised Bank, the Bank's actions are subject to Articles 12 and 14 of the Constitution - An officer cannot be deprived of higher pay scale to which he/she is otherwise entitled merely on the ground that he/she is posted in a Rural or Urban Branch [Paras 6, 8 and 9]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Delay and laches do not absolve the Bank of the responsibility to provide its officers with the entitlements [Para 7]  C Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 14 - Service conditions/Policies regulating the service conditions of staff of the Bank - Posting and transfer is the Employer's sole prerogative, and business procured by an employee has nothing to do with his/her grade [Paras 8 and 9]
