The Constitution of India, along with Articles 14, 16, 19, 341 and 342, as well as the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order, 1950 and the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950, address the issue of Reservation/Migrants from one state to another state.
Para 14 states that migrants are not eligible to receive the benefits of the reservation in states they migrate to from their original state, and they should ensure that they make way for the disadvantaged and disabled individuals in the new community who suffer from disabilities.
