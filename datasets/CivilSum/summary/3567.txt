Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, Section 7 - Public servant - Suspension - Delinquent tried on corruption charges - The mere delay in trial cannot be a sufficient cause for issuing the reinstatement order.
The Tribunal's decision to cancel the suspension and order reinstatement was erroneous.
As a result, the order of the Tribunal was revoked.
