The promotion of respondent No. 9 to the post of Vice Principal is under scrutiny, with the petitioner contesting it and arguing that he should have been selected instead due to his seniority.
The concerned authority downgraded the petitioner from 'outstanding' to 'good' in his personal file, but did not provide any reasons, nor did they communicate the downgrade to him.
As a result, the recommendation of MPSC to promote respondent No9 and the order promoting him to the said post are invalidated.
[Paras 14 and 15]
