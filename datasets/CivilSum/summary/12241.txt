Punjab Police Rules 1934, Rule 162 and Constitution of India 1950, Articles 311 and 226/227 - Disciplinary proceeding - Dismissal from service - According to Para 4, the petitioner was not given the chance to have a personal hearing even though the note mentioned in the reply to show cause notice indicated that one would be given.
The enquiry report was also delivered after the order of dismissal was passed.
The court found that non-supply of the copy of the enquiry report from the record meant the petitioner was not able to properly defend himself, and as such, suffered prejudice to his right of defence.
