The Policy guidelines of Ministry of Defence dated 2011979 on Voluntary retirement from Army state that the competent authority has discretion in accepting or rejecting requests and must do so within laid down parameters and guidelines.
While the guidelines are not exhaustive, many other grounds may also constitute extreme compassionate grounds.
The application cannot be rejected on the grounds that it does not satisfy the stated grounds, or that voluntary retirement cannot be claimed as a right.
Defence personnel are to be treated like human beings, not slaves, and their requests are to be considered subjectively.
[Paras 14 to 16] B.
Voluntary Retirement cannot be rejected on the ground of shortage of Officers of that Rank.
In such cases, the application can only be kept in abeyance and cannot be rejected.
