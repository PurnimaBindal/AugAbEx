Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-B - Evidence Act, 1872, Sections 101 and 102 - Back wages - If an employee's termination is found to be unjustified, he is typically entitled to full reinstatement with back wages - The burden of proof lies with the company to demonstrate that the employee was not employed - The employee cannot be asked to prove this.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-B - Back wages - Legal Representative - If a workman passes away during a pending writ, the legal heirs are entitled to receive payment due to the workman pursuant to Section 17-B of the Act - This is applicable from the initiation of the proceeding until the workman's death.
