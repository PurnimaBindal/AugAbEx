Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission - MBBS Course - Local quota seats - The petitioner provided proof of being a local resident through her father's election card, electricity bill and passport - However, the college demanded proof of payment of House Tax for the last three years - Admission Rules state that any one out of several documents including Ration Card, Electricity Bill, Passport, Election Identity Card and Gas Bill can serve as sufficient proof of being a local resident - It was held that the college's insistence on House Tax bill for the last three years is not consistent with admission rules.
