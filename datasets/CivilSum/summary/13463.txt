(Class-III-Technical) Service Rules 1963, Rule 6 - Increments - The BAMS/GAMS and BIMS degrees are considered to be equivalent qualifications for appointment, and those performing equal duties fall under the same cadre.
A qualification that is treated as equivalent for the purpose of initial appointment cannot be discriminated against when it comes to granting advance increments, as it is arbitrary and against Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
However, in this case, the petitioner who did not possess the BAMS/GAMS/BIMS degree was not treated as equivalent and did not receive advance increments, while those possessing the degrees did.
The orders were set aside and the appeal was accepted.
