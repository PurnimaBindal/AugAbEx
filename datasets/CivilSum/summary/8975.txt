Judicial review in respect of disciplinary proceedings is not forbidden by Article 311 of the Constitution, and if orders are unsustainable and courts below do not scrutinize them properly, the court can intervene even in a second appeal.
(Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Right of Judicial review - Civil Procedure Code, Section 100) [Paras 8 and 9] B.
If disciplinary proceedings are commenced without proper scrutiny of material and the employee's prima facie case is not considered, the non-application of mind at this stage is a serious legal infirmity that renders the entire proceedings arbitrary and capricious and subject to being quashed.
The employee is also entitled to all consequential benefits such as pay, promotions, and increments.
(Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Dismissal from service) [Paras 9 to 12]
