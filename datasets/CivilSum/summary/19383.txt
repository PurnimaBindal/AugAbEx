According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 12.2, it is the responsibility of the Head of the Department to maintain the service book of their employees.
The employee concerned should have access to their service book, and if it is misplaced, the department must take immediate action to reconstruct it.
The petitioner in this case was not granted increments, promotions, or pay scale revisions due to the unavailability of their service book for 23 years.
The respondents have been directed to reconstruct the petitioner's service book, refix their salary with all due increments, grant revised pay scales, consider them for promotion from the date their junior was promoted, and pay all arrears of salary with 12% interest from the due dates.
