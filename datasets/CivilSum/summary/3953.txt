The age of superannuation for university teachers is fixed at 60 years under Statute 16.24 of the First statute framed under the UP State University Act, 1973, and can only be changed by amending the statute in accordance with Section 50 of the Act.
Any direction by the Central Government to increase the age to 62 years and provide re-employment till 65 years is illegal as it goes against the legislative enactment.
The High Court also cannot issue directions to increase the age of superannuation.
[Paras 10, 11, and 22] B. Fixing the age of superannuation for university teachers falls under the purview of Entry 25 of List 3 and has no relevance to the co-ordination and determination of standards of education.
