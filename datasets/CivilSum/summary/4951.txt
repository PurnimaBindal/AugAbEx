Punjab Privately Managed Recognized School Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981 - Delay and Laches - Appointment to the post of Head Master - The appointment cannot be contested after a 10-year period - Despite the respondent's lack of teaching experience, it would not be fair to overturn the appointment at this late juncture - The Court must take into account the law of limitation as well as the principles of delay and laches when determining whether to grant or refuse relief.
