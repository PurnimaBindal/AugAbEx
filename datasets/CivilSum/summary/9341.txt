Section 123 of the Evidence Act, 1872 pertains to privilege of documents.
Documents pertinent to the departmental proceedings initiated against an employee under Service Rules, including the notings made on them, cannot be claimed as privilege.
A petitioner requested the production of their representation made to the Chief Minister, but it is not maintainable under any Service Rule.
The representation cannot be considered as a departmental proceeding and therefore, the state has the right to legitimately claim privilege against the document.
