Pepsu Service Regulations, Regulation 2.28 - Superannuation - Age of superannuation - Inferior Services - The age of superannuation for inferior services in the former state of Pepsu was 60 years - The petitioner, who was appointed as an untrained teacher in 1954, may have been part of an inferior service - However, when he accepted a higher pay scale as a graduate teacher in 1961, his status changed and the age of superannuation became 58 years.
