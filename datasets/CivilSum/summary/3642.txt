Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Natural Justice - Disciplinary proceedings - Appellate authority - The disciplinary or punishing authority cannot sit as the appellate or reviewing authority for their own orders, as it violates the principle that a person cannot judge their own case - Justice must not only be done but should also be seen to be done - allowing the punishing authority to become part of the appellate forum would infringe upon the principles of natural justice and would not be supported - if there is a technical error, it can be addressed by sending the case to an impartial appellate forum for a fresh decision.
[Paras 1 to 3, 5, 9, & 10 to 13]
