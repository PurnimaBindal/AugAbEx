Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural Justice/Admission of guilt/Dispensing with the enquiry - The dismissal order for an employee based solely on their statement cannot be justified under the provisions allowing for the dispensation of an enquiry on admission of guilt, as the admission must be clear and unambiguous - The dismissal without an enquiry violates principles of natural justice.
[Paras 9, 10 and 12]
Constitution of India, Articles 226 - Alternative remedy - If an employee's dismissal order is found to violate principles of natural justice by not holding an enquiry, they are not necessarily required to pursue an alternative remedy - The availability of an alternative remedy is not an absolute requirement for a petition under Article 226, but rather a rule of convenience and discretion.
[Para 13]
