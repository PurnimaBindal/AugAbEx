Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Jammu and Kashmir Cooperative Non-Gazetted (Subordinate) Service Recruitment Rules, Item XI to the Schedule - Selection - Appointment - Qualifications - A Graduate with Higher Diploma Course is required for the position of Cooperative Supervisor.
The candidate who is selected must provide proof that they possess the qualifications prescribed for the position before they are issued the appointment letter.
The appointing authority cannot be forced to issue an appointment letter if the candidate fails to produce the original certificate of the required qualifications.
[Paras 4 and 5]
