Constitution of India, Article 226 - Voluntary Retirement - Invoking the voluntary retirement scheme - A petitioner who submitted an application for voluntary retirement because of defamatory allegations against him requested that his application be accepted even if he was found not guilty in a departmental inquiry.
After the petitioner was cleared of charges, he sent another letter renewing his request for voluntary retirement and was subsequently relieved.
However, he filed a petition seven months after his retirement seeking settlement of his accounts, and requested the court to quash the order accepting his application for voluntary retirement.
The court held that the petitioner was not entitled to any relief.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - General Employees Service Regulations, Regulation 12 - Voluntary Retirement - Notice - If an employee asks for waiver of notice period and the employer accepts this request, such waiver would be permissible under the law because it was initiated by the employee and was not caused by the employer.
