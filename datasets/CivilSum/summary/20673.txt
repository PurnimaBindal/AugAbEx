According to the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 2(a) and Section 14, as well as the Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force Act, 1992, Sections 8, 2(1)(p), 2(1)(j), 153(1)(d), lower division clerks are not included as enrolled persons under section 2(1)(j) and are not considered members of the 'armed forces.'
The recruitment of lower division clerks is governed by the Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force (Lower Division Clerks) Recruitment Rules, 1973, which continue to apply by virtue of Rule 187 of the 1994 Rules.
As a result, the Administrative Tribunal has the authority to hear issues pertaining to their service.
