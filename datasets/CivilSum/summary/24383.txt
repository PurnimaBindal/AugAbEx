Karnataka Civil Services (Appointment on Compassionate Grounds) Rules, 1990 are to be followed strictly for compassionate appointments after the death of an employee.
The family's financial condition and the rules must be taken into consideration, and the rules do not allow for condonation of delay.
1996(4) SCT 63 (SC) and 1994(2) SCT 548 (SC) are cited to support this.
[Paras 8, 10] B Compassionate appointment should not be considered a right, and death-in-harness schemes cannot be applied uniformly across all types of posts.
The claim for compassionate appointment is made on the basis of the sudden financial crisis that arises when an employee serving the State dies while on duty.
