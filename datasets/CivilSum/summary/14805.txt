As per Section 192 of the Army Act (46 of 1950) and Para 173 of the Army Pension Regulations, if a petitioner enrolled in the Army and is later found to have a disease that emerged during their employment, the employer is responsible for providing medical care during service and if found unfit for military service, disability pension must be given.
In this case, the petitioner had undergone a medical test at the time of enrollment, and diabetes was detected 16 years later.
This disease is deemed to have developed during the petitioner's tenure in the Army.
