Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 9 Rule 7 - Ex parte order - Setting aside of - The lower court did not accept the reason for default in appearance given in two different affidavits, with one mentioning illness due to cold, headache and fever and the other mentioning dysentery, showing a contradiction in the nature of illness - As the reason for default is found to be untrue, it cannot be considered as good cause and the plea for setting aside the ex parte hearing order cannot be granted.
