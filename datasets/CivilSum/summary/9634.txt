Central Civil Service (Classification and Control Rules) Rule 15 - Enquiry report - The disciplinary authority is not bound by the Enquiry Officer's opinion in a departmental enquiry - The disciplinary authority may agree or take contrary view.
[Para 12]
Central Civil Service (Classification and Control) Rules, Rule 15 - Preliminary enquiry/Principles of natural justice - Statements recorded in the preliminary enquiry are to be taken into consideration by the Punishing Authority, but not shown to the delinquent official - Witnesses must be examined in the presence of the delinquent official after giving advance notice - Action held illegal.
[Para 13]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Natural justice - The disciplinary authority taking into consideration report of a post-facto enquiry not produced in the enquiry - Findings without notice to the delinquent cannot be taken into consideration - Action violative of principles of natural justice.
