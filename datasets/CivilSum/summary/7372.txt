According to the Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226, a State lacks the definition of an unaided affiliated college and therefore, is not subject to writ jurisdiction.
Nonetheless, if a privately managed institution breaches its constitutional duty, a writ of certiorari can be enforced and hence maintainable.

The Punjab University Calendar, Chapter VIII-A, Regulation 12, and Regulation 131 of Vol.
I, along with the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 2(3), states that a teacher is not considered an employee, and therefore, not applicable for gratuity.
If the University's instructions are violated, a teacher's status remains the same under the Payment of Gratuity Act.
