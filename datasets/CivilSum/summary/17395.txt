The petitioner claimed that their transfer was motivated and mala fide because it was allegedly asked for by the Minister.
However, it was held that interfering with the administration of the government under Article 226 of the Constitution in such circumstances is an unwarranted exercise of jurisdiction.
It was further noted that when posting orders are kept in abeyance at the instance of the Minister, who has a duty to look into public work, no interference is necessary.
[Paras 1 and 5]
