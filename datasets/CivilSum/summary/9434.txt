The Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act of 1946, Clause 28-A - Mala fide Transfer - Petitioner contends that the Standing Orders allow for transfer within sections and departments - Petitioner was transferred from one unit to another, which was later closed down - Order 28-A of Standing Orders does not prohibit transfer outside the unit - The fact that the unit was later closed down does not suggest any mala fide.
[Para 12]

Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 2(cc) - Closure notice - Challenge in writ challenging transfer - It is held that closure notice cannot be challenged in a writ challenging transfer as it must be adjudicated under the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947.
[Para 12]
