Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Dismissal - Substitution of penalty by Labour court - Accused of failing to issue tickets - Workman previously fired for similar misconduct - The Labour Court converted the penalty to discharge without back wages - The penalty of discharge cannot be considered an imposition - the fact that the Labour Court exercised power under Section 11-A suggests that the misconduct was proved to some degree - The workman is also penalized with a forfeiture of 25% gratuity - The order is modified accordingly.
