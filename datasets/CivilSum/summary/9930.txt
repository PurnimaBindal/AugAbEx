Kerala State and Subordinate Services Rules, 1959, Rule 8 Fourth Proviso, Part II - Seniority - Member of service - Leave without pay - Probation - An employee who takes up new employment after availing leave cannot receive double benefit from service and leave - Those who take leave without pay during probation cannot be ranked senior to those who complete their probation.
[Paras 7 to 9] B Kerala Government Servants' Application of Posts (Private Employment and Government Service) Rules, 1958, Rule 3(B)(4)(B)(iii) (1)and (2) - Leave without pay - 'Dies-non' period - The period spent on leave without allowances does not accrue service benefits - The officer forfeits benefits accrued prior to leave and is deemed a new entrant on return - Appointment on non-selection posts can only be made after leave subject to vacancy - An official who leaves for new employment is not entitled to service benefits.
