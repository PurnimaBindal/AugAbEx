Constitution of India.
Article 226 - Misconduct - Punishment of forfeiture of one year approved service - Misconduct can be based on omission of duty as well as a positive act - Petitioner, a Police Inspector, failed to seize tainted money during a raid despite it being an important piece of evidence, resulting in the accused being acquitted - The petitioner's plea that it was not part of his duty to seize currency notes once accused declined to accept bribe money is not valid - Punishment imposed is upheld.
[Paras 2, 3 & 4]
