The respondent, who served as an Assistant Professor in the Institute and performed skilled and technical work, was considered an "employee" under Section 2(e) of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972.
[Para 13]
As per Section 4 of the Payment of Gratuity Act, eligibility for gratuity requires 5 years of continuous service.
The Act was made applicable to the petitioner-Institute in 1997, and the respondent superannuated in 2001.
The entire period of his service should be considered for gratuity payment, thus making the respondent eligible.
[Paras 15, 16]
