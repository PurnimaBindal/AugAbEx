Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rule 7 - Inquiry - Departmental proceedings - Delinquent not supplied copies of documents produced in the inquiry by the Presenting Officer - Delinquent not given the opportunity to cross-examine any witness - Procedure adopted not known to any law - Punishment order passed on the basis of such inquiry violates natural justice and Rule 7(2) - Order quashed.
[Paras 13, 14, 15 and 17]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punishment - Discrimination in awarding punishment - Three officials charged with same allegation - Enquiry Officer records a finding that charge is fully proved against petitioner - Other delinquents have lesser punishment - Conduct of punishing authority reflects unfairness and bias.
Punishment order vitiated.
[Paras 12 and 17]  C. Constitution of India, Article 311 - Bias - Enquiry Officer uses personal knowledge for recording findings adverse to the petitioner - Conduct of proceedings against the petitioner not fair.
