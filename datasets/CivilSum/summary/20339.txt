Central Administrative Tribunal (Procedure) Rules, 1987, Rule 24 - Scope of - When the Tribunal deals with applications for contempt issued beyond the original order, it cannot issue directions to the revisional authority regarding the manner in which order has to be made.
If the original order was set aside and the case remitted for fresh consideration, the revisional authority can pass an appropriate order in accordance with law after considering all aspects.
Therefore, such directions do not fall within the scope of Rule 24, and the order made by the Tribunal shall be quashed.
