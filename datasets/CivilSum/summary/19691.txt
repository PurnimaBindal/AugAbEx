The petitioner applied for admission in MBBS course, but despite making it to the merit list, she was unable to secure a seat.
However, there was a vacant seat available in the BDS course and the petitioner claimed that if the remaining seats in this course were exhausted against the remaining merit list, she would be able to secure admission.
In response, the authorities were directed to consider the petitioner's claim against the vacant seat while giving equal consideration to other eligible candidates in the merit list after duly notifying them.
