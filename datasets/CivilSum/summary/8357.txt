Constitution of India, Article 226 - Permission to establish a dental college - Granting - In consideration of eligibility and qualification criteria, Respondent No. 1 forwarded an application from the petitioner to Respondent No. 2 for the grant of permission.
The objection for non-granting of permission to start college was not based on compelling reasons.
Respondent No. 2 did not provide tangible evidence about why recognition should be withheld for sixty students annually.
Therefore, Respondent No. 2 was directed to allow the petitioner to admit sixty students for the next academic year.
[Paras 13, 15 & 16]
