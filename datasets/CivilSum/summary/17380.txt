Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Seniority-cum merit - Selection will be based on comparative merit of candidates who achieve the bench-mark of "Very Good" or "Very good plus good" - Reserved posts will be available for SC/ST candidates - Candidates who achieve the bench-mark of "Very good" will replace seniority of candidates who achieve only the bench-mark of "Good" - Reserved candidates with an even bench-mark of "Good" will not be replaced by juniors with bench-marks of "Very good" or "Outstanding" - Those not falling within the number of vacancies available are not entitled to be put on the panel solely based on their right to be considered in the reserved category.
[Para 10]

Constitution of India, Articles 226 - Selection - Bench-mark - Confidential Reports - The recording of confidential reports of a candidate who was not selected is not within the scope of writ jurisdiction - Those who wish to have the reporting authority's correct recording must approach the authorities.
