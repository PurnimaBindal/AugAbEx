Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission - Cancellation of - LL.B Course - The petitioner qualified for the course entrance examination, but failed to submit a detailed marks sheet of BA final by the stipulated deadline, resulting in automatic cancellation of candidature.
Despite being unable to submit the marks sheet due to illness, the University or College must adhere to the prospectus for that particular year, making it justifiable to deny the petitioner admission.
