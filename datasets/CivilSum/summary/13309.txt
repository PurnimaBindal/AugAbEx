The admission of respondent No. 3 in the MBBS course has been challenged on the grounds that it was not in accordance with the rules and prospectus governing admissions under the reserved categories.
While respondent No. 3 claimed admission under the category of "terrorist affected family", it was found that her father was still alive and therefore she cannot be held to be a dependent of a terrorist-affected family.
Additionally, the certificate produced by respondent No. 3 was not in accordance with the prescribed proforma.
The action taken by the respondent authorities in admitting respondent No. 3 was deemed to be illegal, arbitrary and violative of Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution.
The petitioner was given directions to be admitted to the MBBS course as per his merit in the category of November 1984 Riot affected/Displaced persons.
The admission of respondent No. 3 was cancelled, but the respondent authorities were given the option to consider sympathetically and on compassionate grounds for accommodating respondent No. 3 against any vacant seat.
