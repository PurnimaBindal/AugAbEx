Persons working as Progressors in colleges affiliated with Gujarat University must retire at 58 years of age, as governed by Rule 161 of Bombay Civil Services Rules, 1959, according to the Constitution of India, Article 309 and Gujarat University Act, 1949, Section 33(5)(j).
The resolution of Gujarat University has no legal authority over Statutory Rules and Regulations, and those employees are not entitled to its benefits.
