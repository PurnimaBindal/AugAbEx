Constitution of India, Article 14 - ACRs.
- Natural justice - Down graded annual assessment reports cannot be used to refuse crossing of efficiency bar without notice or show cause, as this violates principles of natural justice.
[Para 8]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - ACRs.
- Down grading of annual assessment - If reports are not available, an employee's assessment cannot be downgraded or read against them.
[Para 8]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - ACRs.
- Down grading of annual assessment - Efficiency Bar - An employee is considered to have good reports to the extent of 50% if they are allowed to continue after the age of 55.
[Para 9]
Constitution of India, Article 14 - Higher standard pay scale - A.C.Rs.
- Down grading of annual assessment - Efficiency Bar - If the order refusing to cross the efficiency bar is found illegal and quashed, the employee becomes entitled to be considered for the higher standard pay scale.
