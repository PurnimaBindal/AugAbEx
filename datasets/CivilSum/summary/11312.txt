The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Pay - Arrears of Pay - Compulsory investment of emoluments - Retroactive cut-off date - The government's instruction to deposit 50% of arrears of pay into National Savings certificates seems unfair to college teachers who retired after 31.3.1990 - Those who retired before that date were exempted from the order and can receive the entire amount in cash - Classifying retired employees into two categories and setting a cut-off date is arbitrary - All retired employees should receive the entire amount of arrears in cash.
[Para 5] B The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Evidence Act, 1872, Section 115 - Pay - Compulsory investment of emoluments - Estoppel - College Teachers Federation agreed to the Government's proposal for compulsory investment of 50% of arrears of pay in National Savings Certificates - However, an individual member of the Federation has the right to claim cash payment of the entire amount due to them - No agreement can override an individual's personal rights.
