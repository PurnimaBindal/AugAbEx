Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.3 - Continuity of departmental proceedings - Challenge as to - Criminal trial pending - Allegation of shortfall in empty cartridges weighing 8 quintals 12 kgs - Two FIR's registered - Exceptional circumstances can cause stay of departmental proceedings - Factors such as prejudice and possibility of undue delay - Delay not acceptable under Capt M Paul Anthony's case - Therefore it would not be appropriate to stay departmental enquiry - Rule 16.3 would not apply as trial has not concluded in petitioner's acquittal, also rule does not bar concurrent holding of departmental enquiry.
