The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 and Proviso to Rule 27(a) of Kerala State Subordinate Service Rules, 1958 discuss the idea of transfer on request and the resulting seniority among L.D.C.s who are transferred on request.
The general rule in service jurisprudence is that if a government servant holds a particular post and is transferred to the same post in the same cadre, their length of service will remain.
However, a transfer on their own request means they forego their seniority until the date of transfer and will be placed at the bottom below the junior-most in the category in their new cadre or department.
Thus, they cannot upset the seniority and promotion prospects of others in that cadre or department.
The proviso to Rule 27(a) clearly states that the seniority of a transferee shall be determined with reference to the date of joining the new unit or department, and they cannot claim benefit of their past service for seniority in the new unit or department.
This rule applies to all and does not differentiate between those with state-wide or district-wise promotion posts.
