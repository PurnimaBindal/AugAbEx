Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Compassionate appointment - Guidelines for eligibility - The aim of compassionate appointment is to help the family of the deceased overcome their immediate financial difficulties.
This practice provides an exception to the general rule of open competition, as outlined in Articles 14 & 16 of the Constitution.
The employer is responsible for setting guidelines for determining who is eligible for compassionate appointment.
This includes taking into consideration the financial resources available, including terminal benefits and family pensions.
It's important to note that compassionate appointment cannot be demanded as a right or as a reservation.
