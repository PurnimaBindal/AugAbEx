Constitution of India, Article 14 - Transfer - Cancelling a transfer without giving any necessary reasons to an employee who already joined the new place is unfair - The government has the power to transfer and assign employees when it is necessary for administrative reasons and public interest, but it must be done in a just and reasonable way and not arbitrarily - Revoking a transfer order without proper justification cannot be upheld, and authorities cannot provide new reasons through an affidavit.
[Paras 6, 8, 10 and 11]
