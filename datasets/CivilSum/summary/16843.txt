The conditions for study leave were not challenged and therefore cannot be quashed.
As the delinquent already agreed to the conditions, no relief can be given that would effectively nullify them.
[Paras 8 and 9]
Rule 8.126 of the Punjab Civil Services Rules as applicable to Haryana imposes a duty on the government to grant study leave to employees who complete special courses of instruction or training.
This rule does not constitute discrimination.
[Para 10]
The Haryana Services of Engineers Class II Rules, 1965 do not provide for out-of-turn promotion on the basis of acquiring higher qualifications.
Seniority-cum-merit is the criteria for promotion.
