Constitution of India, Articles 233 and 236 - All India Service (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1954, Rules 4, 7 and 27 - Medical reimbursement - Delhi District Judge - Indian Administrative Service - In accordance with Art. 233 and 236, the post of Delhi District Judge is equivalent in status to that of the Indian Administrative Service - Consequently, All India Services (Medical Attendance) Rules apply - District Judge is eligible for medical expense reimbursement in accordance with Rule 4 and Rule 7.
