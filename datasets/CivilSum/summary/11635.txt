Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Schedule II Item 6 - Road Transport Cooperation Act (64 of 1950), Section 45 - Resignation - Acceptance of - Disciplinary proceedings against petitioner pending - According to the law, resignation cannot be accepted during disciplinary proceedings of employees who have committed grave misconduct as they cannot be allowed to short circuit the consequence.
However, since there was only a simple charge against the petitioner, acceptance of resignation during pendency of disciplinary proceedings is proper.

Therefore, no case was made out to uphold the claim of the petitioner.
