The penalty for non-vacation of official accommodation on transfer and reallotment of accommodation in another pool is 50 times the normal rent.
However, even though the petitioner was entitled to an alternative accommodation, it was occupied by another officer who was unwilling to vacate it.
Both officers went on occupying alternative accommodations allotted to each other, causing no loss to the department.
The court found no grounds to impose a penalty, and authorities should solve such minor problems at their level.
The order of rent penalty was quashed, and the petitioner was instructed to vacate the house when alternative vacant accommodation in the other pool was available to him.
[Paras 4, 6 to 9]
