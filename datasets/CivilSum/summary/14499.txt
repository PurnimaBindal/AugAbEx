In accordance with the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309, the Punjab Subordinate Agricultural Service Rules, 1933, Rule 7, and a 1983 memo from the Punjab Govt. Agriculture Department, the Department of Horticulture was established in 1979 and employees in the Horticulture Wing of the Agriculture Department were transferred to that department.
The petitioners, who were appointed as Agri.
Sub Inspectors in 1968 and 1970, are entitled to promotion as Agriculture Inspectors and as Horticulture Inspectors based on their qualifications before the change in qualification requirements instructed by the Government.
All posts created prior to the change in qualification requirements will be filled based on the qualifications required prior to the change for promotions.
