Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 9.18(2) - Compulsory Retirement - Scope of interference in writ jurisdiction - Although limited, the power of judicial review allows the High Court to interfere if it concludes that the authority exercised its power in an arbitrary or non-applicable manner - Completion of 25 years qualifying service alone cannot justify forcing an employee to retire, as they still have the right to serve up to the date of superannuation - Furthermore, adverse reports that were expunged or those that rated the Government servant above average cannot be used to support a public servant's compulsory retirement - Lastly, mere absence for a period for which only a warning was issued does not make a public servant incompetent or unfit to perform their duties, hence ordering their retirement in such a case is not valid.
[Paras 9 and 10]
