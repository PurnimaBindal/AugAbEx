According to Section 2(s) of the Industrial Dispute Act, a Private Ltd Company employee who is engaged in sales promotion and advertisement, as well as supervising the work of local salesman, should possess an imaginative and creative mind that is not of manual, skilled, or clerical nature.
However, termination of services of the employee is not covered by the Industrial Dispute Act as the employee does not come within the definition of a worker.
[Para 3]
The Industrial Dispute Act, Section 2(s) defines an industrial worker as an employee who operates, maintains, or repairs any facility or machinery.
In the case of a Private Ltd Company employee engaged in sales promotion program, the employee is not covered by the Industrial Dispute Act if their services are terminated before the Sales Promotion Employees (Conditions of Service) Act, 1976 comes into force.
[Para 3 and 4]
If an employee is terminated before the Sales Promotion Employees (Conditions of Service) Act, 1976 comes into force, the benefit of the said Act is not available to the employee.
However, leaving the employee without remedy is not just, and the company must ensure that the terminated employee is paid for their services, even if the termination happened 16 years ago.
The company is directed to pay three years' wages to the employee.
