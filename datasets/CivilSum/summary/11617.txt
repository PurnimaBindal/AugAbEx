According to the Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and Haryana Civil Services (Assured Career Progression) Rules, 1998, Haryana Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1998, Rule 2(2)(h) and Rule 3(m)(c) and (d), and ACP Rules, Rule 5, Pay scales, a petitioner argued that a person in the same cadre should not be discriminated against based on their source of recruitment, and the court agreed.
The court held that a petitioner cannot be given a lower pay scale than the pay scale being given to persons in the same cadre who are recruited directly.
