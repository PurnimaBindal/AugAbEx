Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delhi University Act, Section 7(a), Statute 11 (G) or Statute 8(xviii) - Alternative remedy - A petition that has been entertained and heard on its merits cannot subsequently be rejected solely on the ground that a statutory remedy was not availed of prior to filing the petition.
