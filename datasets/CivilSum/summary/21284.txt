Education - Policy of reservation in admission to medical courses for the year 1994-95 - Determining whether horizontal reservations are overall or compartmentalised reservations - Compartmentalised reservations occur when seats reserved for horizontal reservations are divided proportionately among social reservation categories and are not inter-transferable - Notification should specify number of seats reserved for horizontal reservations in each social reservation category - Paragraphs 17, 19 to 22.

Constitution of India, Article 15(1), (4) - Reservations in favour of socially and educationally backward classes of citizens - Reservation of seats in favour of candidates belonging to hill areas and Uttaranchal are reservations within the meaning of Article 15(4) - Separate horizontal reservation of 6% seats in favour of candidates from hill areas and Uttaranchal in addition to 27% reservation in favour of other backward class candidates is illegal - Paragraph 9.
