Constitution of India, Article 311 - Second punishment - In cases where subsequent charge-sheet proceedings were put on hold, and previous proceedings had already resulted in dismissal of the delinquent, the subsequent proceedings will not have any bearing on the punishment order.
[Para 3] B. Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2(3) - Notification - The purpose of the rule to publish dismissal order in the Police Gazette is to prevent re-employment of a troublesome individual.
While the rule is mandatory, non-compliance does not entitle the delinquent to relief nor impact the validity of the dismissal order.
