Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate appointment - Petitioner's father, a permanent primary teacher, was murdered - Petitioner's mother immediately requested compassionate appointment for one of deceased's children - Request ignored - Eldest son's application for compassionate appointment rejected after two years on the ground that it should have been made within two years of death - No rule shown to bar appointment after two years - Respondents did not act fairly - Decision to reject application for compassionate appointment overturned.
