Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3.1(a) - Premature retirement - Adverse entry in Confidential Reports - Integrity doubtful - Petitioners with adverse reports must be allowed to cross Efficiency Bar - Petitioner received a below average honesty rating in the 1985-86 confidential report and was not allowed to cross Efficiency Bar for the same reason - However, petitioner was allowed to cross Efficiency Bar w.e.f.
131988, thereby washing away the adverse entry - Premature retirement not justified in this case.
