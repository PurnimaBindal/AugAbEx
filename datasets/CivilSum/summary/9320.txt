Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Probationer/Misconduct - The services of a probationer cannot be terminated based on misconduct unless the provisions of Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act are followed.
[Para 4]

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Misconduct/Enquiry - A probationer's services cannot be terminated without conducting an enquiry for allegations of misconduct.
The management can justify their order before the Labour Court and if granted permission, evidence can be taken and the Court must record its findings.
In a case where no findings were recorded, it was remanded back with a direction to record its findings.
