Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 162 - West Bengal Board of Secondary Education Act, 1963 - Recognition of school - School proposal rejected - The rejection is based on the circular letter dated 26478 that prohibits recognition of such schools and states that schools can only be started under instructions from the Govt. or the board - The circular does not have the force of law - School has already exceeded the required roll strength as prescribed - The circular also fails to satisfy the requirement of Article 162 of the Constitution of India - The instruction under the circular is deemed to be vague - The Single Judge's order granting recognition of the school's application is upheld.
