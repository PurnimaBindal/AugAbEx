Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226/227 - Standing Order - Promotion - Absorption - Petitioners - SPOs claim for promotion to Constable after juniors' promotion - Standing Order mandates no direct recruitment of Constables - But appointments should first be made as SPOs - Minimum service period for absorption as Constable reduced to six months - "seniority and suitability" criteria to be followed for absorption - Unscheduled SPOs who have completed six months' service and satisfactory conduct can be absorbed - Competent Authority to consider SPOs' claims in seniority order - Directed District Superintendent of Police to consider petitioners' claims for absorption from date of juniors' appointment.
