Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(3) - Karnataka Industrial Disputes Rules, 1957, Rule 61 - Transfer pending reference - Legitimacy - Order to transfer workers does not infringe upon the terms of service - It cannot be claimed that the respondent was a protected laborer and that the transfer order was illegal - A transfer is a part of the job - Thus, the order of transfer is not invalid due to lack of permission under Section 33 of the Industrial Disputes Act.
