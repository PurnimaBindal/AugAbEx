The plaintiff respondent, an accomplished sportsman with exceptional performances in National and International events, is claiming five increments as an incentive under Clause 4 of Govt.
Instructions issued on 24.10.90.
As he also won a gold medal after joining the university's service, he is eligible for additional increments as per Govt. instruction from the date of issuance until his retirement.
He is also entitled to enhanced pensionary benefits by re-fixation of his last drawn salary on the day of his retirement after the addition of these additional increments.
