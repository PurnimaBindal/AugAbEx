According to the Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Aided School Employees (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Section 7, employees of privately managed (aided) schools are entitled to equal pay scales to the employees of Govt. Schools of Punjab Govt., but they have not been extended all other equal benefits by virtue of the same Act.
The Punjab Govt.
Instructions dated 1121988 for grant of proficiency step in increments after 8 years and 18 years have not been made applicable to the employees of aided schools.
Therefore, these employees cannot claim the benefit of proficiency step up equal to the employees of Govt. Schools.
