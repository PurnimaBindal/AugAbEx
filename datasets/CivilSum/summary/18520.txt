Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Punjab Re-organisation Act, 1966, Section 58, Fourteenth Schedule, Clause 5(1) - Punjab Government Notification dated 3181989, Clause 10 - Liberalized pensionary benefits - Liability of states to pension - Section 58 of the Act apportioned existing liability of pensions between the successor States, but not the future liability regarding those retiring after 1111966 - Successor states are free to provide additional benefits to their own pensioners - Only benefits available on 1111966 are protected - Subsequent Notification by State of Punjab has granted liberalized pensionary benefits to its pensioners, while the State of Haryana has not - Therefore, Haryana pensioners cannot demand the same benefit as it is a matter of policy for each successor state.
