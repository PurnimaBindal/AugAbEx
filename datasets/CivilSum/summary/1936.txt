Written statement/objections - Delay in filing objections - Filing objections on time in the Registry but late submission before the Judge - Court allowing time to file objections - These objections cannot be considered time-barred.
[Para 8] C J&K CSR Rules, Rule 6.4 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Date of birth - Delay in requesting change - Entry of date of birth in service record made on the basis of material provided by the employee itself - Challenging such an entry at the end of the service suffers from laches - This entry is of utmost importance and is presumed to be within the notice and knowledge of the employee concerned - Such a belated claim is considered to be made to take undue benefit of extension in service.
