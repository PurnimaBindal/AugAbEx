Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 13.17(1) - Adverse remarks - Only an officer who has worked with the subordinate for at least three months can record remarks in the annual confidential report - Adverse remarks made by an officer who did not supervise the subordinate for the required period cannot be used to justify the subordinate's early retirement - The State's appeal was dismissed and they were required to pay 2000/- rupees for costs.
