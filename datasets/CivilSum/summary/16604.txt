Prevention, Punishment and Procedure Concerning Cases of Misconduct and Use of Unfair Means in or in relation to Examination Rules, Rules 10(u) and 11(v) - Impersonation - A candidate who impersonates in an exam cannot be failed in their own exam which they had already cleared - Even if the impersonation was done out of sympathy for another candidate, the impersonator's own exam cannot be cancelled - According to Rule 11(v), withholding their degree for a period of five years could be an adequate punishment.
