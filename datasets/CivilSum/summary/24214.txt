Under the Employees' State Insurance Act 1948, Section 1(4) Proviso, Toddy shops in Kerala are not exempt from the application of the ESI Act, even though they are regulated by the State Government through the Abkari Act.
This is because the Toddy Workers Welfare Fund Scheme and the Toddy shops are not covered under the Proviso to Section 1(4) of the Act.
The Kerala Toddy Workers Welfare Fund Act and its associated scheme also do not provide exemption from the ESI Act.
