Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Principles of natural justice - Wednesbury principle - An administrative decision's unreasonableness does not count as a violation of the Wednesbury principle unless it is based on irrelevant considerations while ignoring significant material and resulting in an absurdity.
