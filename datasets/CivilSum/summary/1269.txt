According to Section 4(1) of the Workmen's Compensation Act, a claimant who was working as a truck driver and became permanently disabled due to an accident, resulting in the total loss of their earning capacity, should be assessed an appropriate compensation.
[Paras 17 and 18]
In accordance with Section 30 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, depositing the compensation amount and presenting a certificate of such deposit are essential prerequisites for appealing against an award of compensation.
The failure to do so renders the appeal invalid, and it is not considered a mere irregularity.
[Para 19]
