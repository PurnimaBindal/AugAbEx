Constitution of India Articles 14 16 300-A and 310 - Retirement - Nationalised corporations - Employees classified for retirement based on recruitment date, with 60 years for pre-nationalisation and 58 years for post-nationalisation employees - Classification considered reasonable under Constitution Articles 14 and 16, and not arbitrary - No appointment letter condition specifying retirement at 60 years - No legal entitlement to retire at 60 - Corporation not obliged to continue petitioner until 60 - Writ of mandamus cannot enforce petitioner's employment until age 60.
