The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(s) - Shop Manager - Maintainability of reference - According to an agreement, the respondent was appointed to the managerial cadre from the post of clerk and was performing managerial duties with independent decision-making authority - Held, as the respondent was promoted to the managerial cadre and was carrying out supervision and management duties, the reference could not be maintained at the respondent's behest.

Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Misconduct - Termination of service - Quantum of punishment - The respondent approved payment to a contractor under false pretenses of completion of work - The labor court ordered reinstatement, finding that a higher officer had knowledge of wrongdoing and that the respondent had refunded the money - A senior officer ignoring a subordinate's misconduct does not make the misconduct acceptable - Refunding the money does not exempt the employee from punishment in cases of misconduct based on fraud - An authority can dismiss an employee if they deem them untrustworthy based on undisputed facts.
