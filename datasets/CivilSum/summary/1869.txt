Constitution of India, Articles 16(4), 15(4), 46 and 341 - A Scheduled Caste couple who converted to Christianity have a son who converted to Hinduism at age 14 - Son is presumed to be Scheduled Caste under Section 114 of Evidence Act and entitled to reservation benefits.
AIR 1976 SC 1904 applied.
