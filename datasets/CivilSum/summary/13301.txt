Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delhi School Education Act, Section 8 - Delhi School Education Rules, 1973, Rules 117(b) and 120 - Disciplinary proceedings - Enquiry - Show cause notice - Filing a writ petition against a show cause notice is not admissible - As other remedies are open to a competent authority's decision, the writ can not be heard - It is therefore premature to file the petition, and it must be dismissed.
