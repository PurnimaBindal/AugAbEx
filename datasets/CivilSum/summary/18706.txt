According to the Delhi School Education Rules, 1973, Rules 96(3) (b), 104 and the Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Rule 98(4), a petitioner was appointed by Selection Committee as TGT but after four months, their service was terminated due to being overage at the time of appointment.
However, a notification and circular allows for a relaxation of two years of age and a number of other persons were appointed by Selection Committees with the age relaxation.
The court held that under Rule 98(4), approval of appointment to the petitioner is deemed granted by the Director if not objected to within 15 days.
Therefore, the order terminating the services of the petitioner was not sustainable.
