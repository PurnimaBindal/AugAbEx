Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion/Consideration - The petitioner was passed over for promotion in favor of a junior colleague - The petitioner had the right to be considered for promotion alongside his junior - This action is a violation of Article 14 of the Constitution - The petitioner had no misconduct charges against him - However, he retired during the pendency of the case - Therefore, he is entitled to receive arrears of a higher salary for the higher post, along with all the other benefits and pension on the basis of higher pay-scale of the higher post.
