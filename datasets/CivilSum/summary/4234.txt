Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Delayed challenge to promotion order passed in 1990 - No explanation for delay and laches - Repeated representations will not affect the limitation for filing a civil suit - Writ petition is liable to be dismissed for delay and laches.
[Paras 14, 15, 25]

Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservation - Roster system - A 100 point roster must be maintained under the reservation policy in Punjab Medical Services Class-I - The percentage of reservation cannot be overlooked while giving effect to the roster and the carry forward rule - Once the percentage of reservation is fulfilled, appointments cannot be made solely based on the reservation to Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes merely because a particular vacancy falls at a particular reserved point of roster.
[Para 18]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Concealment of facts - Relevance of facts - Litigants are required to trace and plead all relevant facts at the time of filing a claim - A petitioner who does not reply to a notice cannot plead ignorance of the fact - The petitioner is guilty of concealing facts and is not entitled to any relief under Article 226.
[Paras 22-23]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Promotion - The date of a basic order of promotion cannot be challenged - Persons becoming juniors cannot challenge subsequent orders determining the date of commencement of such order.
[Para 24]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disputed facts - Writ jurisdiction cannot decide serious disputes on facts.
[Para 25]

Punjab Civil Medical Service (Class-I) Rules, Rule 3, Appendix-A - Service - Post of Director Health Services - No notification for the creation of the post of Director ESI - The department did not fund the post - The post was funded by the ESI and the Central Government - There is no automatic creation of a post and inclusion in the cadre.
[Paras 29, 31, 33]

Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservation - Single cadre post - There can be no reservation in favor of Scheduled Castes or Scheduled Tribes against a single cadre post.
