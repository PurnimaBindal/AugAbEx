The petitioner had a shortage in attendance, falling short below 50% when he was expected to attend at least 66% of the lectures.
The reasons given by the petitioner were found to be untrue, and the court would not be lenient in this matter to avoid promoting indiscipline in the educational atmosphere.
The atmosphere in educational institutions is already deteriorating due to strikes, teacher-student relations, and careless student behavior.
Showing leniency would only encourage students to continue to miss their courses regularly without consequence.
