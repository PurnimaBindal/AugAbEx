Under the Police Act and Articles 311 and 309 of the Constitution of India, police personnel seeking premature retirement under relevant Police Rules must give three months' notice and may be eligible for pensionary benefits.
In this case, the appellant sought premature retirement due to ill health and gave the requisite notice, but also absented himself from duty without leave.
The police authorities did not pass any order within the three month notice period, nor did they address the absence without leave.
Consequently, the employee could be deemed retired from service from the end of the notice period.
Later orders of suspension and dismissal are therefore invalid and the appellant is entitled to his pensionary and other retirement benefits according to the relevant Rules.
