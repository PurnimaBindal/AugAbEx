Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21, 19 and 309 - Book of Financial Powers, (Second Edition of 1990) Page 175, S. No. 7-B and head (ii) at page 176 - Text Books Discount on text books published by the Govt. - Public authorities entrusted with public offices have a duty to use their powers for the public good and not for their own benefit - Officers must understand their responsibility towards society and follow the law - A committee consisting of the Secretary to Government, High School Education Department and heads of departments of Higher Education will determine the rates of discount after consulting the Government of India/University Grants Commission and Federation of Indian Book Sellers and Publishers - The Director of School Education cannot set discount rates on their own and invite tenders for the purchase of books for government Libraries and educational departments - Tender notification invalidated.
