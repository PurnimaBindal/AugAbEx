Section 8 of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 discusses the age of retirement and term of office for Chairman, Vice-Chairman, and Judicial and Administrative Members of Central Administrative Tribunals.
Reappointment is possible, and those appointed must hold offices in some capacity prior to their term with the Tribunal.
The concept of security of tenure does not apply to those whose term is reduced and Section 8 cannot be deemed arbitrary or jeopardizing to tenure.
The section has been amended to allow reappointment of members beyond the first term of five years, with an outer limit of 65 years for Chairman and Vice-Chairman, and 62 years for other Members.
The Supreme Court's observations in Sampath Kumar etc's case still hold true despite the amendment.
B Ramamanjini & Ors. v. State of Andhra Pradesh's decision and judgment were reversed.
