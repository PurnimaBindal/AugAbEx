Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 173-A and Rules 1 & 2 of Entitlement Rules, Appendix II - Disability pension - Invalidation from service - When a soldier is permanently placed in a lower medical category and is unwilling to accept alternative employment or has been retained in an alternative appointment, he shall be discharged before the completion of his engagement and deemed to be invalidated from service - If the soldier was invalidated out of service at his own request without accepting an alternate appointment and was declared 'fit to be released from service in low medical category' by the Release Medical Board with a 20% disability, he is entitled to the disability pension and should not be denied the benefit on the grounds of refusing an alternate appointment.
