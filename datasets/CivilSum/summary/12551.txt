Under Punjab Co-operative Societies Rules, 1963, Rule 28, a petition at the time of promotion without having the required experience and qualifications can be promoted subject to relaxation by the Registrar Co-operative Societies.
The Registrar, Co-operative Societies declined to relax the qualifications, and as the petitioner is ineligible, they cannot continue on the promotional post.
It is open to the competent authority to see whether academic qualifications and experience which are required for a post should or should not be relaxed.

Even though the Haryana Co-operative Societies Rules, 1989, repealed the Punjab Co-operative Societies Rules, 1963, any action taken, order issued, and bye laws made under 1963 rules were saved.
The petitioner was promoted on 4.11.1991 to the post of Cane Manager subject to relaxation of qualification by the Registrar.
The Mill adopted the 1989 Rules on 27.4.1992, but the qualifications laid down on that date under Rule 29 of the 1989 Rules would not be applicable to the petitioner's case as they were promoted before the new rules adoption.
