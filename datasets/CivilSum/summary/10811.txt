Rajasthan Animal Husbandry (Subordinate Service) Rules, 1977 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 39(d), 226 - Holder of post - Difference of salary - Petitioner appointed as Sais made to work as driver - Later given formal charge of post of driver - Petitioner had necessary qualifications for appointment as driver - Therefore, Petitioner entitled to pay of driver from date of appointment on principle of 'Equal pay for equal work'.
