Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 14-B - Late remittance of employees provident fund contribution within the prescribed period of para 3810 of the Employees Provident Fund Scheme, 1952 - Imposition of penalty for recovery of principle amount after an extended duration.
According to the ruling, the competent authority must apply reasoned judgment after examining the facts of the case ensure principles of natural justice are followed, and a reasonable opportunity is provided for the affected party to be heard, as there is no timeframe set by legislators for initiating action for damages under section 14-B Neither delayed proceedings nor an inability to raise defences in High Court are grounds to argue waiver, which can only be proven to exist if there is an irretrievable prejudice to the defaulter.
In response to the notice, the defaulter must plead adequately and provide the necessary evidence to demonstrate their claims.
