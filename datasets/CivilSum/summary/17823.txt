Punjab Civil Service Rules Vol-II Rule 4.12 - A (as amended w.e.f.
26.4.85) - Pension/Training period/Qualifying service - The petitioner retired in 1982.
The rule was amended in 1985 to count one year of training period followed by regular employment towards qualifying service.
The amendment is not retrospective.
According to Justice SS Dewan (Retd.
C.J.)
v. UOI 1991(2) PLR 501, the petitioner is entitled to the benefit of the amendment from its effective date.
[Paras 4 and 5]
