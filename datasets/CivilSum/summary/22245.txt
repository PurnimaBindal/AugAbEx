Constitution of India, Articles 32 and 311 - Termination/Back wages - Illegal termination - An employee was terminated illegally and later reinstated with back wages, but the authorities' adamant behaviour caused wastage of public money by pursuing the litigation for 20 years, resulting in the loss of working life of the employee.
The officers responsible for the termination and those who pursued the litigation unnecessarily are required to pay back wages from their salary.
