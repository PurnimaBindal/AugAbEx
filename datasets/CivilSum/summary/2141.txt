Constitution of India, Articles 311, 14 and 309 - Rajasthan Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rules 16, 23 - Appellate order - Exoneration from charges - Appellate authority issued a fresh order upholding the punishment after 7 months without any notice - Court held that the authority had become "functions officio" after passing of the earlier order and had no power left to review it without notice - The order was set aside.
