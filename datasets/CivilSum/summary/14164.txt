Constitution of India, Article 226 - West Bengal Rules, Para 8, Sub Rule (ii) - Penalty - Withholding of increments - With cumulative effect - Petitioner received penalty of withholding of five increments from the disciplinary authority - Petitioner argued that the withheld increments should be released as the punishment's duration of five years has passed - Additionally, petitioner argued that penalty for withholding increments is not authorized by law and the period of withholding has not been clearly stated by the authority as required - Para 8 of West Bengal Rules outlines the penalties that may be imposed and Sub Rule (ii) provides for withholding of increments and promotions - The impugned order is precise and correctly implements the law - Due to the annual nature of increments, the punishment has a five-year validity - Cumulative loss of increments necessitates an investigation, which has been properly conducted - Therefore, there are no issues with the impugned order - As a result, the petition is rejected.
