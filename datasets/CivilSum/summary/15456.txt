According to the Anti Ragging Law, ragging of new students by seniors in medical and other colleges is a brutal and barbaric act.
The police should take strong and severe action against students involved in ragging, and those found guilty should be expelled.
To encourage freshers to come forward and give evidence without fear of being ill-treated, police authorities must provide them with protection.
Educational institutions must put a stop to this inhumane activity, and strict measures must be taken by State and police authorities to eradicate the evil of ragging in the future.
