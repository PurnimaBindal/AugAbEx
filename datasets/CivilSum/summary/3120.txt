Petitioner, the holder of the prestigious award Uttam Yudh Sewa Medal, received a punishment of "severe displeasure" after three years of alleged misconduct.
However, Section 122 of the Army Act bars even trial by court martial after the expiry of three years.
The order is too vague and does not reflect the application of mind.
It is also violative of the principles of natural justice and Section 122 of the Army Act.
The appropriate authorities have been directed to consider the petitioner's case for promotion to the rank of Lt. General.
