Delhi Police (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1980, Rules 15(1), 5(3), 16(i), 16(iii) - Disciplinary proceedings - The punishment of withholding one increment with cumulative effect - A preliminary enquiry report that was not supplied did not have an effect - Statements of witnesses clearly support the charge and full opportunity was given to cross-examine witnesses - It was not necessary to supply the preliminary enquiry report as it shall not form part of formal departmental proceedings record - The order upheld was in the absence of violation of any statutory provision or principles of natural justice.
[Paras 10 and 12]  B Delhi Police (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1980, Rules 15(1), 5(3) - Preliminary enquiry - Its scope - A preliminary inquiry is a fact-finding inquiry to decide whether disciplinary action is required against delinquent.
Its purpose is to establish the nature of default and collect evidence to facilitate regular departmental proceedings - The charge is not proved on the basis of a preliminary enquiry.
[Para 9]  C Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary proceedings - The scope of interference in the order of punishment - High Court does not exercise power of judicial review to correct errors of law or procedural errors leading to manifest injustice- Court cannot re-examine the evidence produced in departmental proceedings to come to its own independent conclusion.
