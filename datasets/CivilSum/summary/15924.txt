Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I Part I, Rule 3.26(d) - Compulsory retirement - An employee must receive notice of three months or pay in lieu of notice, and adverse entries beyond the 10-year period of service can not be taken into consideration - Promotions and higher pay scales may be granted if the employee has a record of more than 70% good entries in the last 10 years of service - Orders of compulsory retirement based on prior adverse entries are illegal.
[Para 6] B. Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I Part I, Rule 3.26 - Compulsory retirement with retrospective effect - An order for compulsory retirement with retrospective effect is illegal because it denies the employee their salary for that period - The employee is entitled to three months notice or pay in lieu of notice - Orders of compulsory retirement with retrospective effect are illegal and employees are entitled to be reinstated.
