Appointment of the Director (Administration) of UHBVNL falls under the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 226 and Articles of Association of the Uttar Haryana Bijli Vitran Nigam Ltd's Articles 30 and 31.
Fixed term appointments cannot be curtailed or removed on the grounds of post upgradation in the absence of appointment conditions.
Article 31(2) does not allow reductions in fixed term appointments and longer minimum tenures may be prescribed by the government.
However, the doctrine of pleasure cannot be used to curtail the post's statutorily fixed tenure.
