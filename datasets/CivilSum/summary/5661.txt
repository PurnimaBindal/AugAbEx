Constitution of India, Article 21 - Criminal Procedure Code, Section 300 - Unlawful detention - The petitioner was unlawfully held in custody for 24 days - He had already been tried and sentenced by a competent court for the same offence - He cannot be retried for the same offence - The petitioner deserves to be compensated for the illegal detention through damages - Officials responsible for the detention are personally liable to pay - The act of illegal detention was condemned.
[Paras 7, 10, 11 and 16]
