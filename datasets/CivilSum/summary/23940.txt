When it comes to candidate selection, the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 309 outline that the last date of applications and eligibility must be specified in the advertisement.
If there is no such date, the cut off date for eligibility and applications will be the last date for applications to be received.
It is important to note that candidates who did not have the eligibility qualifications until that cut off date will not be considered for selection.
In terms of eligibility for selection, confusion can arise regarding the last date for applications and the cut off date for eligibility qualifications.
However, if candidates acquire the eligibility qualifications after the last date for receipt of applications yet are still given appointment, removing them from service after two or three years due to a procedural defect on recruitment agency's part would cause significant injustice.
It is essential to protect their appointments and exercise power under Art. 141 in order to do justice.
