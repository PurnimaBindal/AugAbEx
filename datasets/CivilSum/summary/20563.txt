Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 47 Rule 1 - Review - The Tribunal lacks power to review orders which have been confirmed by the Supreme Court.
Exercise of the review power undermines judicial discipline and undermines the finality of the order confirmed by the Supreme Court.
[Para 4]  B Res judicata - Dismissal of S.L.P.
without speaking order does not constitute res judicata - The principle of res judicata prevents parties from re-litigating the same issue once it has been decided.
This principle is grounded in public policy and aims to prevent vexation.
