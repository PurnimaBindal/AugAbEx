Article 226 of the Constitution of India - Back Wages - Payment of salary by employers for employee services rendered - `No work no pay' principle applies when an employee fails to provide service - Employers may be required to pay salaries if they are responsible for the employee not being able to render services - If employers are not responsible for the employee's inability to provide service, there is no obligation for employers to pay wages.
Reference made to AIR 1997 SC 1802.

Article 226 of the Constitution of India - Back Wages - Employee found guilty of taking a bribe and sentenced to imprisonment and a fine - Dismissed from services by employers - Acquitted by the High Court at a later date - Employers cannot be held responsible for the situation that led to the employee's dismissal - The petitioner did not provide service from the date of dismissal until the date of acquittal - The petitioner is not entitled to wages for this period, and the employer is not required to pay them.
[Para 6] Case referred to: Ramchandji Chaturji Thakore v. Superintendent Engineer, Gujarat Electricity Board, Himmatnagar (Gujarat) and another, 1997(1) S.C.T.
