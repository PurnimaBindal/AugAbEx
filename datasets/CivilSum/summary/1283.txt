Under the Constitution of India, Article 226, an educational institution such as the Army College of Law falls under the definition of "State" and is subject to writ jurisdiction of High Court.
[Para 15]
As per the Constitution of India, Article 14, eligibility conditions must be fulfilled before the date of interview, but if an interview is conducted after the result of a compartmental examination, the candidate cannot be ignored and must be considered based on merit.
Merit cannot be ignored simply because the candidate passed the qualifying examination later, especially if no such condition is stated in the information brochure.
[Paras 17 to 19]
If some candidates are ignored on the wrong interpretation of terms and conditions of admission and others admitted against the seats as per their merit, those who have completed a substantial part of their course cannot be displaced.
Therefore, even if a petition has merit, no substantial relief can be granted as those who have pursued their studies since long cannot be displaced at that stage.
