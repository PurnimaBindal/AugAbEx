According to the Constitution of India, Article 16, equal pay must be given for equal work.
Senior Auditors in the office of Accountant General and Assistants in Central Secretariat receive similar pay.
As the pay scale of assistants has been increased, Senior Auditors cannot claim the same level of pay because historically, auditors and assistants have been treated equally in terms of pay scales.
[Para 5]  B The Constitution of India, Article 16 states that equal pay must be given for equal work.
In cases where two posts that carry the same pay scales, an increase in pay for one post cannot result in an increase for the other post to the same level.
