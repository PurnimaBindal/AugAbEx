According to Sections 2(9) & 46 of Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, an employee who crossed the wage limit of Rs. 1000 in September, 1984 and died in December, 1984 but worked for less period and received wages of less than Rs. 1000 in November can still be entitled to benefits from the scheme for the corresponding benefit period due to their contributions from an earlier period.
