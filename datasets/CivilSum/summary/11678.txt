Pension Regulations for Army, Part-1, 1961, Regulation 173 - Disability Pension - The findings of the Medical Board recommended by the Medical Board must be followed by the Executive authority of CDA, and the review can only be carried out by the Appellate Medical Board or re-survey medical board- The CDA authorities cannot overturn the Medical Board's findings for their own purposes.
[Paras 6, 7 and 8]

Retrospective Orders - When a benefit is granted to an individual under specific laws or regulations, it cannot be withdrawn with retrospective effect, and any executive order would only have prospective impact.
[Para 9]

Natural Justice - Disability Pension - Once the Medical Board has deemed someone eligible for disability benefits and the authority has acted on it, such benefits cannot be withdrawn without giving the person in question a chance to respond.
[Para 6]
