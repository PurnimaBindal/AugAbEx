According to Section 36 of the University Act, 1974 (Kerala), the University has the authority to hire a woman coach for the women's team without needing to create a new Ordinance or modifying an existing one.
The position of coach is not limited to only male or female coaches.
Furthermore, it can be assumed that the University had already considered the appointment of a female coach for a specific sport or game when making the First Ordinance in 1978.
