Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination - Competent authority - The petitioner was appointed as a teacher on a temporary basis by the Headmaster.
The teacher holding temporary charge of the Headmaster cannot terminate the petitioner's services.
[Para 8]
Constitution of India Articles 14, 16 and 311 -Termination - Enquiry - The petitioner was appointed on a temporary basis for 6 months.
His services could not be terminated as a punishment without conducting an enquiry.
