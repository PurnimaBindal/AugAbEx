The punishment given to a police officer for misconduct must be proportionate to their guilt under Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India - if a policeman causes the death of someone due to their misconception of duties, it is a serious crime - punishment of dismissal cannot be seen as disproportional, and Article 226 of the Constitution does not require interference.
[Paras 9-12] B According to Bombay Police (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 2 and Section 25 of the Bombay Police Act, 1951, the District Superintendent of Bombay Police is authorized to take disciplinary action against and dismiss a Head Constable - therefore, the dismissal order of a Head Constable passed by the District Superintendent is valid and not out of his jurisdiction.
