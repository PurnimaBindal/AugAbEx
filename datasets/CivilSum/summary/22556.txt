The High Court directed the appellant to admit the respondent-petitioner to MS (G&O) even though the course had already started due to a seat being vacant from a candidate's resignation.
However, the appellant declined the claim for admission as three years minimum training is required for the award of post-graduate degree of M.D./MS, which the respondent did not possess.
Furthermore, the directions given by the High Court went against judgments in Dr. Anupam Gupta's case and were subsequently set aside.
