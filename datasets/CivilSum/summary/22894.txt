MP Public Works Department Engineering (Gazetted Service Recruitment) Rules, 1969 - Constitution of India, 1950, Article 16(4A) (77th and 85th constitutional amendment) - Seniority - General Category Candidate and Reserved Category Candidates - Promotion - An appellant was not preferred on basis of exceptional merit but appointed through reserved quota - In this case, seniority from the feeder post will be carried forward - There will be no unfair advantage given to the appellant for his earlier promotion under the reserved category - Decision made according to legal status prevailing at relevant time - Cannot extend retrospectivity beyond what has been given by parliament - Issue occurred in 1985, constitutional amendment retrospective from 1761995 only - Government's 1975 circular stated that incumbent cannot claim seniority based on accelerated promotion under reserved quota - Incumbent not eligible for seniority.
