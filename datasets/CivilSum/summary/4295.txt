Industrial Disputes Act, Section 12(3) - Civil Procedure Code, Section 9 - Jurisdiction/Settlement under Industrial Disputes Act - A suit under the general law is not prohibited, unless the dispute is not specifically covered under the settlement between the management and the workers union - If an employee is seeking correction of their date of birth in their service record and consequential reliefs of salary and date of superannuation, and they retire during the pendency of the suit for declaration, the only relief which can be granted by the civil court is 'correction of date of birth' - Consequential reliefs must be sought from the proper form.
