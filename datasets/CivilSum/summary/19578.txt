Privately managed schools have the discretion to prescribe higher qualifications for the appointment of Librarian, beyond those required by Affiliation Bye-laws.
This is to ensure that academic standards are maintained or improved.
The Education Board directed the management of a school to appoint a qualified Librarian in the prescribed pay scale, but the management is not bound by the Bye-laws and can set higher qualifications.
It is established that employers can require qualifications that exceed the Rules/Bye-laws.
