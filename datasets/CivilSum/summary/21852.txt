Rajasthan Administrative Service (Emergency Recruitment) Rules, 1976, Rule 25 - Seniority of Emergency Recruits - Article 14 and 16 of the Constitution - Discrimination - Emergency recruits to the Rajasthan Administrative Service form a distinct class - The Rule providing past service towards seniority can be deemed discriminatory - Despite remaining on the statute book for over a quarter-century, due to the dwindling number of emergency recruits, striking down the rule, if any invalidity can be established, would be inappropriate.
