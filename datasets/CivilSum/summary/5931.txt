According to the Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226, and the Societies Registration Act, 1860, the Indian Association for Cultivation of Science is an association registered under the Societies Registration Act, dominated by private persons, and free to accept grants from the governments while being answerable to them in respect of the grants received.
Its accounts are required to be audited by the Comptroller and Auditor General of India as long as it continues to receive grants from the government.
However, receiving grants is not enough to determine if it is an 'authority' under Article 12, and the society cannot be considered an 'Authority' or Instrumentality of the 'State' under Article 12 of the Constitution Therefore, a writ of mandamus cannot be issued to enforce private duties and obligations.
