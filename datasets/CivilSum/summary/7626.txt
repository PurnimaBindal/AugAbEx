Civil Procedure Code, Section 9 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(s) and 10 - Industrial Dispute - Civil court jurisdiction not completely barred - If the claim is based on a breach of industrial law, the remedy falls under the Industrial Disputes Act - However, if the claim is based on a general law contract, the civil court can still adjudicate regardless of it also being an industrial dispute - This interpretation is supported by the AIR 1995 SC 1715 case.
