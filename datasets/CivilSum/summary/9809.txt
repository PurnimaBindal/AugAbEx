According to Sections 25-F and 2(oo) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a workman can only be retrenched after completing 240 days of service.
In this case, the workman completed more than 240 days of service under different authorities under the Chief of the Department.
Therefore, his retrenchment violated Sec. 25-H and he should be reinstated with continuity of service and all other consequential benefits with 75% back wages.
[Paras 3 and 6] B.
Under Sections 25-F and 2(oo)(bb) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a workman must serve a demand notice after his removal from a job.
In this case, the workman served a demand notice immediately after his removal but was not called upon to join.
Therefore, it cannot be presumed that he abandoned his job.
[Para 4] C. The question of whether an employer's establishment is an industry cannot be entertained for the first time in a writ petition.
Therefore, the plea that the employer's establishment was not an industry under Constitution of India, Article 226 and Sections 25-F and 2(j)(k) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, cannot be considered.
