The respondent employer failed to justify deviation from the rule of "first come and last go" in terminating the services of the appellant who was appointed as typist/clerk by the Municipal Board.
The High Court erred in its ruling that the rule of seniority does not apply to daily wage workers.
The violation of section 6-P of the UP Industrial Tribunal Act, 1947, warrants the appellant's entitlement for reappointment.
