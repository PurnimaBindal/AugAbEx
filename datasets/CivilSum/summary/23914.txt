Constitution of India, Article 136 - Haryana Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Rules, 1993, Rule 7 - Appointment - Post of Principal - Respondent No. 4 was selected by the Selection Committee in accordance with Rule 7.
However, the Managing Committee did not provide any letter of appointment to Respondent No. 4.
The High Court concluded that the selection was conducted in adherence to the relevant rules and that the appellant's reasons for not appointing Respondent No. 4 were unreasonable.
Consequently, the court directed the respondents to appoint respondent No. 4 as Principal.
It was imperative to accept the appointment made by the selection committee, as the relevant rules did not permit the Managing Committee to challenge such selection.
The High Court's directive for appointing Respondent No. 4 was legitimate.
