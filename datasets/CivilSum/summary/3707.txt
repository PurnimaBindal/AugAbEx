Constitution of India, Article 16 - Length of Stay as Basis for Transfer - Transfer is a service incident based on the length of stay at a location - Courts cannot interfere with transfers made for administrative exigency unless it is made with the intention to punish - Power of transfer must be used honestly, in good faith, and reasonably - If the power is used for external purposes for achieving any alien purpose or for an oblique motive, its use shall be rejected by the Courts.

[Para 14]  C Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 19(1)(c) - Transfer of an Association's Officer - No illegality.
[Para ]  D. Constitution of India, Article 16 - Transfer of Employees - When a transfer order does not result in alteration of service conditions that disadvantage the employee, the order is not even appealable.
