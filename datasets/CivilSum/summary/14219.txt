According to the Constitution of India, under Articles 14 and 16, promotion of an eligible junior employee without vacancy and subsequent reversion due to lack of vacancy cannot be considered supersession.
If a senior employee becomes eligible before a vacancy becomes available, the senior must be considered first.
If the junior was earlier promoted without a vacancy, his reversion becomes inconsequential.
The petitioner is entitled to promotion from the date his junior was promoted, with arrears of salary and 12% interest if found fit as per his service record on that date.
[Paras 10, 12 and 13]
