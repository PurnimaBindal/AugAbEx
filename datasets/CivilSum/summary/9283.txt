The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) states that the Government does not have the jurisdiction to consider the merits of a case for making a reference to the Labour Court.
After considering the merits, the Government declined to make a reference, but this decision was deemed improper as it is the Labour Court's responsibility to decide the matter on merits.
The Government's function is administrative, not juridical or quasi-judicial, and has been directed to reconsider its decision.
