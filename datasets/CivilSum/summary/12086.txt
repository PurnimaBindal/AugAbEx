The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Withdrawal of selection grade - Recovery - Removal of selection grade and recovery based on it has civic consequences - It is not acceptable without following the principles of natural justice and issuing a show-cause notice.
[Paras 16 & 19]  B The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection grade - Ad hoc service - The calculation of service for determining the selection grade must be from initial appointment rather than from the date of training.
