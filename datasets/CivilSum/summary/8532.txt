Constitution of India, including Articles 14, 16, and 226, addresses pay scale and stipulates that "equal pay for equal work" must be maintained.
Furthermore, although job evaluation, qualifications, and other relevant factors must be taken into account when fixing the pay scale, it is not appropriate for a writ court to take on the role of an expert committee.
When comparing job performance, the relevant factors extend beyond the quantity of work and also include experience, qualifications, and mode of recruitment.
