Article 226 of the Constitution of India stipulates that pensionary benefits cannot be directed to be paid in instalments, and any order to pay commuted value of pension in instalments would be considered arbitrary.
[Paras 4 and 5]
The pensionary benefits due to retired employees of K.S.R.T.C are subject to K.S.R.T.C rules and any government order will not apply to the organization.
Additionally, executive orders cannot be issued with retrospective effect.
