Compassionate Appointment - Suppression of facts - Termination of Service - Natural Justice - The respondent's father died while in service, and the respondent applied for a compassionate appointment.
However, the respondent failed to disclose the fact that his mother and wife were already in service.
The department terminated the respondent's services, and the court held that there was no need to issue a show cause notice.
[Paras 6 and 7]  B Natural Justice - The principles of natural justice cannot be applied rigidly but depend on the circumstances of each case.
In order to successfully complain of a violation of natural justice, one must prove that they were prejudiced by its absence.
