The writ petition filed in the High Court on 15121992 challenged the orders of stoppage of increments passed in 1977, 1978, 1979, 1980, 1981, and 1982 under the Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Writ - Delay and laches.
The disciplinary authority may have considered the punishments for the purpose of granting promotion.
Granting promotion would result in great hardship for other employees who had performed better than the petitioner.
The writ petitions were dismissed due to delay and laches, and the court correctly exercised its discretion.
[Para 17]  B The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ delay and laches, is merely a fetter to the exercise of discretion by a court and is not an absolute bar to belated petitions.
