Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 - Selection on merit - Criteria of selection - Selection and appointment to the post of Gram Dak Sevak Branch Post Master (GDSBPM) - Eligibility and merit conditions - Candidate's income and availability of sufficient space for Branch Post Office must be considered - Only securing higher marks in Matriculation is insufficient - Petitioner selected on merits and working for a year, with no challenge to appointment or complaints on work or conduct - Notice of termination based solely on not having highest marks in Matriculation considered unjustified and quashed.
