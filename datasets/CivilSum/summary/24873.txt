Working Journalists and Other Newspaper Employees' (Conditions of Service) and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1955 - Beneficial legislation - Interpretation of Statutes - The Working Journalists Act is a beneficial legislation aimed at improving conditions of service in newspaper establishments.
Even if different constructions of the Act are possible, the one that advances its objectives and supports employees for whom the Act is intended must be accepted.
The law reports published by the first respondent are newspapers and their employees should be given the benefit of orders passed by the Central Government based on recommendations from the Palekar Award.
According to Section 2(b) and 2(f) of the Act, the contents of law reports constitute news for subscribers and readers.
