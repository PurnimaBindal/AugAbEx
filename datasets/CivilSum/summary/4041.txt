Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - MBBS course - Sportsperson category - Petitioner attempted to obtain admission under the sportsmen category using his grade `B' certificate based on his representation of India in an international tournament - Admission was declined - Private respondents, who were awarded a silver medal in a local tournament, were also given grade `B' certificates - Instruction and clause 52 of the prospectus stated the approach for determining proficiency inter se between various sports categories - According to the approach, a position holder falling in `B' category would have precedence over a mere participant though in International event - As a result, the petitioner is not eligible for admission.
