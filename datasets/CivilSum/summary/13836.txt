According to Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, for admission into the MBBS course with reservation, a candidate's father, who is a Central Government employee posted in the Andaman Islands, can apply for his child if he/she has studied there for at least two years.
However, if the father is transferred and posted to Chennai, the child no longer qualifies for the reservation benefit.
In this case, respondent No. 5 is not eligible for the benefit of reservation, but the petitioner is entitled to it.
As a result, the authorities have been instructed to make accommodations for the petitioner.
