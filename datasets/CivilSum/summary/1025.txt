According to Delhi School Education Rules, Rules 98(4) and 151, the appointment of ad hoc teachers in the development fee category cannot be regularized.
Additionally, the claim for regularisation was made after a long delay of 30 years, and the appointment was not in accordance with the Act's provisions, nor was it for a sanctioned or vacant post.
Due to these reasons, the petitioners' claim for regularisation cannot be granted.
