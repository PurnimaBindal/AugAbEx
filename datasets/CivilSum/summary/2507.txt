Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol.
I, Part I, Rules 8.21, 8116 and 8112 - Leave encashment - Employees of Education Department and other departments are treated differently when it comes to cash payment for unused leave - Only earned leave, not other kinds like half-pay leave, can be encashed - Cut off dates are necessary when the government extends monetary benefits - Fixation of cut off dates cannot be considered arbitrary or discriminatory - Disparity has been removed from a certain date.
[Paras 5 to 8]
