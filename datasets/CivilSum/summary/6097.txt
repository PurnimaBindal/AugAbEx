The workload for the post of Lecturer in English is calculated by the principal as 66 classes/periods based on a letter from the government.
The workload for a teacher is 25 periods a week, and if the workload exceeds any multiple of 25 per week by a fraction more than 3, an additional post will be sanctioned for fractional excess.
The reason given in the impugned order that workload did not justify the third post of Lecturer in English is not proper.
