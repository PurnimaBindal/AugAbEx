Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Contract - Contractual Services - Repeated extensions of contract - It must be noted that an employee on contract basis has no right to claim appointment against regular vacancy, even if claiming regularisation after 8-9 years of service.
It is well-established that the formal offer and acceptance of a contractual service in writing is merely directory—not a mandatory obligation.
