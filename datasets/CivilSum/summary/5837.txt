Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Education/Degree and certificates - Admission granted on the basis of a fake certificate - Petitioner used a forged degree of Shiksha Shastri from Vanarasey Sanskrit Vishvavidhyalaya issued in 1987, which was not recognized by UGC - Even the institution was found to be non-existent on investigation - The use of such forged degrees cannot be allowed for the continuation in service as a trained teacher - The court cannot encourage or perpetuate fraud by issuing writs to allow the petitioner to continue with his degree, even with the decision of the Supreme Court in Ram Sukh v. State, (AIR 1990 SC 592).
[Paras 12, 13 and 14]
