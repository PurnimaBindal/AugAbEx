Army Rules, Rules 13(3)I(iii)(a) and 2(A) - Disability pension - A petitioner who was discharged from the Army and received a lower medical category was assessed with a disability of NIDDM 250(d) at 11 to 14% for five years.
The petitioner claimed disability pension and questioned the assessment of his disability in a lower category.
The authorities have been directed to bring the petitioner before a Resurvey Medical Board within two months after the expiry of five years of the earlier Medical Board survey.
If the petitioner's disability is found to be 20% or more, he will be granted disability pension.
A re-examination by the Survey Medical Board will become due after two months.
