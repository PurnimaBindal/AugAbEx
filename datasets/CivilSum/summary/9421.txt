Constitution of India, Article 16 - Compassionate appointment - Petitioner was a minor when his father died - The fact that his family has been able to support themselves for about eight years is sufficient evidence that they have some reliable source of income - Thus, compassionate appointment was correctly denied - Compassionate appointment is primarily meant to provide immediate aid to the deceased government servant's family to alleviate their financial difficulties.
