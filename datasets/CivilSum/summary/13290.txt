According to Articles 310 and 226 of the Constitution of India, an employee is estopped from changing the date of birth he declared at the time of entry into service.
The appellant claimed his date of birth was in 1941 during his retirement but he gave 1939 as his date of birth upon joining the service.
Even if he made a mistake about his date of birth at entry, he is still estopped from changing it after benefiting from that date.
[Paras 5 and 6]

Claims for changes in date of birth near retirement may be considered stale and should not be entertained.
[Paras 5 and 6]
