The selection and appointment of the petitioners were done by a properly constituted Selection Committee with the appointing authority.
However, the petitioners were relieved from their positions after just 1-1/2 months without explanation or any chance to show cause.
Any irregularities in the selection process must not lead to the petitioners' termination without the provision of natural justice, as it also reflects negatively on the committee members.
Hence, the petitioners should be deemed to be continued in their service.
[Para 9] B If the appointing authority serves as the Selection Committee's Chairman, presenting the minutes of the selection committee before any authority might not be mandatory.
