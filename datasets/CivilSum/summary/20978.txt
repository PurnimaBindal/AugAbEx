Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Army Instructions, 1969, 1976 and 1987 - Promotion - Previous practice - Appointment by promotion and selection - The Department followed the practice of appointing candidates in order of merit on the Board's recommendation until 1987 - However, from May 1987 onwards, a different procedure was introduced which did not apply to the 1986 quota - The respondents were not justified in denying the appellants Permanent Commission, which they were entitled to as per the practice adopted before 1987 - Respondents directed to allow Permanent Commission in the Army Medical Corps (Non-Technical) to the appellants from the dates other candidates of 1986 quota were promoted/selected.
