The amendment of pleadings sought under Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 6 Rule 17 cannot be declined on the grounds of mere negligence or mistake, as the rules of procedure are meant to facilitate the administration of justice.
[Para 8]
Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 6 Rule 17 is intended to aid, not obstruct, the course of substantive justice and provisions concerning pleadings aim to inform each side of the other's case, allow courts to determine the real issues and prevent deviation from the litigation's required course.
