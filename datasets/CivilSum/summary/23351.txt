In IA No. 1 And SLP(C) of 1993, Constitution of India, Article 14, Indian Medical Council Act 1956, Section 12 - Admission/Migration - Candidates cannot be admitted to a medical college in India by migrating from an unrecognised medical college.
The Medical Council is responsible for determining the equivalence of subjects studied with the subjects that are taught in the college from where one seeks to migrate.
A candidate who has a compartment and does not reappear for the exam shall be considered to have failed in that exam and cannot be granted admission to a higher class in accordance with the Regulations of Dar-es-Salam University.
[Paras 33 and 34]
