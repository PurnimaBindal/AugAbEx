Rajasthan Compassionate Appointment of Dependent of Deceased Government Servants Rules, 1996, Rule 5 - Compassionate appointment - Concealment of fact - Termination - If any member of the family of the deceased is already employed on a regular post, then no appointment can be made on the compassionate ground - Delinquent concealed the fact that his brother was in service when he applied for the compassionate appointment - Service of the delinquent rightly terminated for obtaining employment fraudulently and contrary to the rules.
[Paras 13, 14, 15]  B. Rajasthan Compassionate Appointment of Dependent of Deceased Government Servants Rules, 1996, Rule 5 - Compassionate appointment - Limitation for - Appointments on the compassionate ground must be claimed within 90 days after the death of the deceased - The purpose of the compassionate appointment is to help the family of the deceased employee through the sudden crisis that comes about from the death of the only breadwinner - Appointment made after the dependent reached the age of majority - Application made when the dependent was still a minor - Not justified - Not a fit case of `sudden crisis'.
