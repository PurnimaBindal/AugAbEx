Industrial Disputes Act, 1974, Section 10(1)(c) - Working Journalists (Conditions of Service) and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1955, Section 17(2) - Working Journalists (Conditions of Service and Miscellaneous Provisions) Rules, 1957, Rule 36 - Amount due to a Newspaper employee from his employer - Reference - The State Govt. where the Central or Branch office of the Newspaper establishment is located is competent to refer the matter to Govt. - The Management objected that Chandigarh Administration had no jurisdiction to refer the dispute or make a reference - However, it was the responsibility of the Management to prove that it did not have a Branch Office at Chandigarh - Since the Labour Court failed to determine whether the respondent - Management had a Branch Office at Chandigarh, the impugned award was set aside.
