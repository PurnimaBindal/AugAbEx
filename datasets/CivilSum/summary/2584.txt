Under Article 300A of the Constitution of India and various provisions of the Punjab Civil Services Rules, an employee who has worked continuously for an organization is entitled to pensionary benefits.
In a case where the petitioner was posted on an ad hoc basis with the Bhakara Beas Management Board and later allocated to the State of Haryana, he was rejected a pension by the Board on grounds that he did not remain in continuous service with them.
The court, however, upheld the petitioner's claim stating that although he had worked for the State of Haryana for a short period, he had nonetheless continuously worked with the Board and should receive pension based on his last pay drawn with them.
