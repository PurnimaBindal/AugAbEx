Constitution of India, Articles 21, 226 and 227 - Instructions of Government of Punjab, Department of Health and Family Welfare (Health Branch) dated 192000 - Medical reimbursement - Treatment expenses - Refusal of reimbursement on grounds of weak financial position - Corporation under State Government control - Financial difficulties cannot justify failure to issue necessary directions when violation of fundamental rights is at issue - Pensioners who are not paid legitimate dues such as medical reimbursement are not expected to enjoy life or feel secure.
