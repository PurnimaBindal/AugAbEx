According to Fundamental Rules, Rule 17(1), pay and allowances are granted from the date of promotion.
However, in the case that the petitioner expressed their disinclination to take up the post at the relevant time, they are not entitled to the benefits of the Apex Court's judgment in Janakiraman's case.
The petitioner was promoted from a retrospective date, and as a result, FR 17(1) permits them to draw pay and allowances attached to the post from the date of assuming their duties.
