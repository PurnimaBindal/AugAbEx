According to Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14, 16 and 226, policy decisions regarding the equation of posts primarily fall within the domain of authorities.
Terms and conditions of employment are to be controlled by the Board's circulars, and government circulars are not applicable unless adopted by the Board.
[Paras 7 to 8]
An individual working as a bus conductor in P.S.E.B.
sought pay parity with conductors of the irrigation department on the basis of government circulars.
However, since the Board did not adopt the circulars, policy decisions were not interfered with unless there was evidence of discrimination or arbitrariness.
