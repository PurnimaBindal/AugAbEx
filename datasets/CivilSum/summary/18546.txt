As per Article 309 of the Constitution of India and the Punjab Town Planning State Service (Class-I) Rules 1972, the Post of Chief Town Planner selection criteria is based on merit-cum-seniority.
Rule 20 of Punjab Civil Services (General and Common Conditions of Service) Rules 1994 amended upto 31.12.2001 also states that the provisions of the rules shall be effective regardless of any contrary rules regulating recruitment and conditions of service for the appointment to public service and posts in connection with the State's affairs.
The Respondent was found to be merit above the petitioner, and the selection was made through legal means.
