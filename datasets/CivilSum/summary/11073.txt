Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code Section 11(v) - Res judicata - If a writ petition seeking multiple reliefs is disposed of granting only one or a part of the reliefs sought, the remaining claims made in the petition are considered to be declined and cannot be re-agitated in a subsequent petition - No relief beyond the prayers made in the petition can be granted - An earlier petition challenging a termination order did not result in its quashing, and therefore the termination order cannot be challenged again.
[Paras 10 and 13]
