Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - TTD Employees Service Rules, 1989, Rule 9 - Public Appointment - Prescription of residency - A petitioner cannot seek appointment as a local candidate in TTD college, because the State Government cannot prescribe residence as a pre-qualification for appointment.
Moreover, TTD is not governed by Presidential order, which is not applicable to posts in TTD.
