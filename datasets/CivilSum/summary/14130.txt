As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, the criteria for the appointment of a Lecturer is that they must have passed the NET examination conducted by USC.
However, the petitioners who hold degrees in MBE were denied the opportunity to appear for the NET examination in subject code-17 - Management, but were allowed to appear for subject code-01 - Economics.
The comparison of papers revealed that except for one paper, all the other papers are common between the two subjects, whereas subject code-01 - Economics does not have a single common paper.
It is argued that the subject taught in MBE course aligns closely with subject code-17 - Management, while subject code-01 - Economics is far more distant.
Therefore, the correct approach would be to allow the petitioners to appear for the subject code-17 - Management exam instead of subject code-01 - Economics.
