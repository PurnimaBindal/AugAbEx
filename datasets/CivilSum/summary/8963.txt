The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 10(1)(c), specifies that in cases of termination of service, a reference may be made.
In this case, a workman who was appointed as Mortar Mate in P.W.D.
on work charge basis had his services terminated.
The Labour Court proceeded ex parte when the representative of the employer did not appear, and the application for setting aside the ex parte award was dismissed.
The petitioner argued that the P.W.D.
of the State is not an industry, and therefore the impugned order and ex parte award given by the Labour Court was without jurisdiction.
However, this plea was not taken before the Labour Court and there is no evidence on record to show the nature of activities of the Department.
The award was upheld, and the petition was dismissed under Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India.
