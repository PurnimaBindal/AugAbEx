Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Promotion/Punishment - Disciplinary proceedings - The junior of the petitioner was promoted, subject to the outcome of the pending disciplinary proceedings against the petitioner, which ultimately concluded with only a warning.
This warning does not constitute a punishment.
The promotion of the junior was conditional and since the petitioner was ultimately exonerated, he is entitled to be promoted from the date his junior was promoted, even though the petitioner had retired.
The authorities were directed to give him monetary benefit of the promotion.
[Paras 7 and 8]
