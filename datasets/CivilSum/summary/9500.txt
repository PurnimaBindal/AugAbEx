Chartered Accountants Act, 1949, Sections 21(4), 21(5) and 22 - Chartered Accountants Regulations, 1988, Regulations 43(8) and 190-A - Professional Misconduct - Reference - The respondent was found to have accepted full time employment without obtaining requisite permission despite holding a full time practice certificate - The respondent admitted to the mistake and apologized for his actions - It was revealed that the respondent had no professional work and was holding a whole time certificate for practice - The Council was correct in removing the respondent's name from the register of members for 6 months as misconduct had been clearly established.
