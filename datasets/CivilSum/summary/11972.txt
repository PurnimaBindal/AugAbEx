A ruling on the Jammu and Kashmir Hindu Succession Act, 1956, Section 6 and the State Bank of India Employees Pension Fund Rules, Rule 23(5) regarding family pension states that the mother, who is a Class (I) legal heir under the act, cannot be denied the benefit of family pension.
The respondent Bank's rule, which denies the mother the benefit of family pension, is considered discriminatory and violative of Article 14 of the Constitution.
As a result, the rules relating to the non-inclusion of mother in the list of legal heirs for the purpose of family pension is held to be violative of Article 14 of the Constitution and the provisions of the Act and Schedule attached thereto and are struck down.
The mother is thus included in the rules as one of the legal heirs for the purpose of family pension.
Additionally, the petitioner mother, being the sole legal heir and dependent on the deceased employee, is entitled to the family pension and other retirement benefits that shall be released in her favor within two months from the date, a copy of this order is made available to the respondent Bank by the petitioner.
The petitioner shall also receive arrears of family pension along with interest at the rate which is being paid by the respondent Bank on fixed deposits under the five-year Interest payable Scheme.
Constitution of India further states that pension is not a bounty, but rather property.
