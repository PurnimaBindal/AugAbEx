In the case of Education - Admission - MD/MS/PG Diploma/Degree Course, a petitioner argued that she ranked higher than the last candidate selected in the open category and was thus entitled to a spot in Radio-Diagnosis discipline.
However, her spot was given to Respondent No. 7, in the RBA category.
Petitioner filed a writ petition due to her being allocated a spot in dermatology.
It was argued that there was only one seat available in the reserved category and that this was given to Respondent No. 10.
There was no further seat available in that particular discipline.
The Court ruled that the seat in the Dermatology discipline in Govt. Medical College which had been allotted to Respondent No. 6 would have remained available for the candidate next in line in the order of merit, and could not be given to Respondent No. 6, who was reserved category candidate.
As a result, the Board could not have validly given the discipline of Dermatology to Respondent No. 6.
The discipline of Dermatology must be given to the petitioner if she is next in merit in the open category, and another seat must be allocated to Respondent No. 6 in the reserved category discipline in which she applied and was selected.
[Paras 11, 12, 13 and 14]
