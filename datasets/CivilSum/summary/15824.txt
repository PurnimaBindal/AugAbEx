Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Selection - Appointment - Post of Sanskrit teacher - The petitioner holds B.Ed.
with Sanskrit and Shastri in Sanskrit, but does not have LTC (OT) in Sanskrit from Haryana - It is argued that she lacks the essential qualification - However, individuals with B.Ed.
and Sanskrit are considered eligible for appointment as Sanskrit teachers - Failure to consider the petitioner’s qualifications is a violation of her fundamental right to equality - As a result, the respondents must appoint the petitioner as a Sanskrit teacher.
[Paras 3, 5, and 6]
