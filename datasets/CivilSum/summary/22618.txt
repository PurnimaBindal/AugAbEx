Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pay scales - Contract of private service - Extension of - The Court is not authorized to extend or enlarge the scope of contract of service - Private school teachers cannot be granted pay scales on the principle of parity of pay scale with State employees - Private employees do not have a legal right to claim recommended revised pay scales by pay commission, even if they were initially granted existing pay scales recommended by the pay commission - Getting the current pay scales shall not mean the guarantee of getting further revised pay scales.
[Paras 17, 10, 15]
