Constitution of India, Article 14 - Army Instructions - Increments - Re-employment - Increments on re-employment of Army Official - According to Paragraph 4 of the instructions 29.7.78, only the initial payment should be determined.
However, under paragraphs 9 and 12 of the instructions, after determining the initial payment, increments for the entire re-employed period must be added to the initial payment.
