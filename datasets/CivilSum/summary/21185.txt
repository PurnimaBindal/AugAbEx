In order to be eligible for promotion to the post of Superintending Engineer from Executive Engineers possessing a Degree with 6 years of experience, the experience gained after obtaining degree/qualifications as well as prior to that will be taken into consideration.
The phrase `with' means `as well' in this context.
The eligibility criteria can be fulfilled even if the requisite experience had been obtained before obtaining the educational qualifications.
[Paras 4, 7 and 8] B.
The term `with' means "accompanied by", "in addition", and "as well" when used to connect two nouns.
