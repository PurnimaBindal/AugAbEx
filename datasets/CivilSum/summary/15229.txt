Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Transfer - Mala fide transfer - If a transfer is made to specifically adjust an individual, it can be considered an abuse of power and can be challenged in a writ court - Transferring a junior cadre person to a senior cadre post, displacing a senior cadre person, is clearly a mala fide transfer and violates the law.
