The regularisation of a casual worker from a retrospective effect is not usually allowed, as it can disrupt the seniority of regularly appointed employees.
In this case, the respondent's claim is incorrect, as she was originally engaged as a casual worker before being registered with the employment exchange at a later date.
Furthermore, based on a previous legal ruling, daily wagers cannot expect to receive the same wages or regular pay scales as regularly employed workers.
