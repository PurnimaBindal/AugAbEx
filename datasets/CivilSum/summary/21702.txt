Constitution of India, Article 16 - Contractual appointees - Claim of regularization - Single Judge of High Court allowed petition - Ruling states that circular cannot override Article 16 of Constitution, therefore regularization cannot be granted under it.
- According to Uma Devi's case, contract labourers, casual workers, or ad hoc employees cannot be regularized outside of the rules for selection, even after working for extended periods.
