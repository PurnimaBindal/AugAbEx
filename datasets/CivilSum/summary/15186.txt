Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, Section 5(3) - Appointment as a casual bus driver - The petitioners argue that even though their designation is casual bus driver, they work as permanent bus drivers - The respondents already made 294 casual bus drivers out of 350 as permanent - The law does not prohibit the appointment of drivers as casual bus drivers - If standing orders do not indicate, prefixing the word "casual" before a designation cannot be used to argue that the appointee is not deemed an employee under either category mentioned in the standing orders - The respondent must create a scheme to give all benefits of permanent bus drivers to the petitioners.
