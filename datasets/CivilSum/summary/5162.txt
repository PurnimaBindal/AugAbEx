Constitution of India, Article 16 - Employee seeking compulsory retirement - The employee may withdraw the application before the prospective date, even if the authority had passed an order of retirement in the meantime.

Constitution of India, Article 16 - Resignation and its acceptance - Employees have the right to resign or seek retirement, subject to statutory provisions.
Resignations may be sought with immediate effect or prospectively.
If resignation requires acceptance, the Authority has to consider it and may refuse to accept it.
Once accepted, non-communication of said order to the resignor would be immaterial and employee cannot seek withdrawal of application thereafter.
Voluntary resignation is essential and cannot be obtained by coercion or fraud.
The employer cannot prepone the date of resignation, and prospective resignations may be withdrawn before the effective date.
