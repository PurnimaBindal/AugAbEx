The State's decision to absorb or not absorb an employee cannot be questioned by the Court.
However, if the Government decides to absorb surplus staff or allow them to carry their own pay grades, they cannot depart from that decision without rational justification or valid reasons.
[Para 5]
If the State Government instructs the absorbance of surplus staff from one department to another in the same or equivalent pay scale, arrears of pay cannot be claimed for more than 38 months before filing a writ petition, especially if such petition was filed after more than 20 years.
