Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 226 state that admission to a BTech.
degree course must adhere to the prospectus.
The petitioners, who were qualified under the prospectus, had their admission regularized.
The prospectus contains the force of law.
Respondents argued that the petitioners were admitted in contravention of the ordinance of admission.
However, when there are two conflicting provisions due to lack of coordination between State Government, University, and college, discretion must be exercised in favour of the petitioners.
The respondents' confusion cannot cause harm to the petitioners, and a direction was issued to regularize their admission.
