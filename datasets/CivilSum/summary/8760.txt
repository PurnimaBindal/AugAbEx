Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 25F - Limitation Act, Article 113 - Termination - Retrenchment - Limitation for raising demand for reference - to Labour Court - A workman cannot have an advantage over a government servant in challenging his termination/retrenchment within the prescribed limitation.
The Labour Court is not required to reject the claim as time-barred if the demand is not raised within the prescribed limitation for filing a suit in the civil court, as long as there is a reasonable justification for the delay.
