Under Section 33(2)(b) of the Industrial Disputes Act and Section 4 of the West Bengal State Tax on Professions, Trades, Callings and Employments Act, it is necessary to obtain approval from the Tribunal before discharging or dismissing a worker.
The relationship between the employer and the employee is not effectively terminated until this occurs, and the employer may therefore deduct the statutory tax from the one month's wage paid under Section 33(2)(b).
