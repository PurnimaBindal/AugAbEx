Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulations 27(c) and 48(a) - Disability pension - Review of recommendations of Medical Board - The pension authorities are not authorized to review the recommendations of Medical Board at their own discretion.
Regulation 48(a) is only applicable if no disability pension was granted upon retirement.
Regulation 27(c) can be used before the grant of disability pension for reviewing the recommendations of Medical Board.
Once disability pension is granted, it cannot be suspended or stopped without prior notice and an opportunity of a hearing to the pensioner.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Delay and laches - Pension - Entitlement to pension is established by the Rules and gives rise to a continuing cause of action every month.
In a case where the order to stop the disability pension was given without any notice or sufficient cause in 1957, challenged in 1997, the petition cannot be dismissed on the ground of delay and laches.
However, arrears beyond 38 months prior to filing the petition are considered to be time-barred.
The order to stop the pension has been quashed and the petitioner has been directed to receive the arrears with continuity of pension in the future.
