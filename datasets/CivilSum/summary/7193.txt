Life Insurance Corporation Act, 1956, Section 49 - Life Insurance Corporation of India Apprentice Development Officers Recruitment Scheme, 1980, Clause 6.1 - Apprentice Development Officer - Termination of service - Whether punitive - The petitioner did not disclose material information at the time of appointment - The appointment letter stipulated that the service can be terminated at any time without notice - The petitioner's conduct disentitles him from relief under the court's equitable jurisdiction - There was no inquiry and no finding of guilt, so the order cannot be deemed as punitive or stigmatic.
