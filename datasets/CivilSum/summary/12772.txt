The Railway Protection Force Rules of 1987, specifically Rules 146(2) and 147(iv) and (v), entitles the compulsory retirement of an employee for misconduct and absence from duty.
In this case, the petitioner was charge-sheeted for desertion from his duty causing the Railway property to be left unprotected and also for being absent from duty from 2081987 to 21.8.1987.
Although it is not disputed that the petitioner was absent during that time, he did produce a medical certificate from a private doctor confirming his illness.
However, the respondents' decision to impose the compulsory retirement on the petitioner was not justified under the given rules, as he was able to provide a medical certificate.
Therefore, the order of compulsory retirement is overturned, and the petitioner is to be reinstated in his previous post with continuity of service.
