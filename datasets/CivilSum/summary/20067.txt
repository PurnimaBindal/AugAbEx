Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Part-time employment - Regularisation - Despite being appointed on a part-time basis outside of the established rules, the petitioner hired as a Class IV employee in the office of Defence Accounts is not entitled to temporary status or regularisation, even if he has been working regularly for a long period of time.
To be considered for permanent employment, he must be appointed according to the established rules based on his merit.
