Constitution of India, Articles 21, 226 - Leave Regulations, 1983, Regulation 31 - Reinstatement after termination - Stay away period - Salary cannot be denied - The regulation relied upon in denying salary is not applicable for deciding the issue - Therefore, the impugned order cannot sustain - Furthermore, such a period cannot be treated as dies-non, as the principle act of reinstatement mandates the counting of intervening days on the setting aside of termination by court.
[Paras 13, 17, 18 and 20]
