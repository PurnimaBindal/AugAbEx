The Punjab Kanungo Services Rules of 1945, specifically Rules 7, 12, and 15, Appendix 'A', as well as Articles 14-16 and 226 of the Constitution of India, and Punjab Civil Service Rules set forth instructions for promotion, seniority determination, length of service, date of confirmation, discrimination/arbitrariness, members of service, and promotion from Kanungo to Naib Tehsildar.
These Rules provide for district-wise seniority, with no intra-district seniority required by the rules.
Punjab Civil Service Rules do not make any provisions for district-wise or intra-district Seniority, with rational criteria for determining qualification for promotion including district-wise length of service in order to avoid discrimination and arbitrariness.
