According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, a petitioner took illegal gratification from a truck driver, resulting in departmental proceedings.
Statements from seven witnesses were recorded, but those of the driver and cleaner were unable to be obtained since they were not found.
As a result, the petitioner was subject to a punishment of the loss of two annual grade increments with a permanent effect.
The act was deemed to have reflected poorly on the Police Department, but the appellate authority opted to be lenient and imposed minimal disciplinary actions.
