According to Rule 2 of the Punjab Document Writers Licensing Rules, 1961, legal practitioners are exempt from obtaining any license or maintaining any register prescribed by the rules.
They have the right to draft pleadings or any other document only after passing the paper of law of conveyance.
Any embargo on legal practitioners from scribing documents will go against the Rules and the Advocates Act, 1961.
[Paras 8 and 10]
Only the Inspector General of Registration is competent to issue instructions under The Punjab Document Writers Licensing Rules, 1961, which cannot be overridden by instructions issued by any other authority.
[Para 9]
Dismissing a writ petition in limine without passing any speaking order does not have any binding effect as res Judicata.
