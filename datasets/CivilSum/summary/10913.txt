Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 (as amended by Act 1994), Sections 2(e), 4, 5 and 14 - LIC Act (as amended by Act 1 of 1981), Section 48(2-B) - LIC of India Class-I Officers (Revision of Terms and Conditions) Rules, 1985, Rule 9 - Application of Payment of Gratuity Act - The Act allows exemption from establishments if LIC's gratuity benefits exceed those under the Act - But the later LIC Amendment Act cannot override the provisions of the Act, as stated in Section 48(2c) of the LIC Act.
