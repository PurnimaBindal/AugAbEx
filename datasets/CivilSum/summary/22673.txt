The Railway Protection Force Act, 1957, along with Sections 6, 9 and 21 read with Rule 20 of Railway Protection Force Rules, 1959, outlines the competent authority and delegation of powers for the appointment, dismissal, and removal of members of the Force.
It is stated that the Act proposes the delegation of power by the Chief Security Officer for appointment and removal by the Assistant Security Officer.
The order of removal passed by the Assistant Chief Security Officer is upheld, and it is determined that Rules 20 are not ultra vires section 6.
