Karnataka Employees Triple Benefit Scheme Rules, Rule 50(5) - Voluntary retirement - When an employee gives notice of voluntary retirement, it must be followed under the appropriate circumstances - If an employee is allowed to continue and is given additional responsibilities beyond the mandated period of the retirement notice, it becomes invalid - Any subsequent notice or request must be considered independently - This consideration has nothing to do with the earlier expired notice - If an employer accepts the notice before the mandatory period is over, it amounts to a curtailment of the notice period, which is not allowed under the Scheme and Rule 50(5)(c) - If an employee has already declared their intention twice not to continue in service, instead of an order of reinstatement, compensation should be awarded - However, appeal to the tribunal is maintainable if the voluntary retirement was against the rules.

[Para 17] C Karnataka Employees Triple Benefit Scheme Rules, Rule 50(5)(m) - Voluntary retirement - Prior approval of the State Government is necessary before issuing an order of acceptance for voluntary retirement - This approval cannot be accorded without verifying the service record of the Accountant General in terms of the employee's eligibility and entitlement - Non-compliance with Clause (m) of Rule 50(5) will lead to retirement orders being deemed void.
