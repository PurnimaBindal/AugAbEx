Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rules 3(1)(o) & 37-A - Commutation of pension - Dearness allowance - Restoration of pension after expiry of period of commutation - Upon restoration, pensioners must receive the same benefits as other Government employees - Dearness relief is granted to pensioners to compensate for rise in living costs - Anything not included in pension must be paid in full - Full pension commuters who do not receive monthly pension are not entitled to other benefits.
