The Delhi High Court has jurisdiction to hear the case of employees who migrated from Jammu and Kashmir to Delhi and received all their emoluments in Delhi, including their terminal dues.
The cause of action for claiming balance terminal benefits wrongly withheld also arises within the territorial jurisdiction of the Court.
[Para 16]
Withholding part of the gratuity for non-return of books by a professor who migrated from J&K to Delhi is considered arbitrary.
Delay in payment of gratuity and pension should result in interest and compensation.
Thousands of families were forced to migrate under compelling circumstances, which makes it unreasonable to expect the professor to return the books issued first before ensuring their safety.
