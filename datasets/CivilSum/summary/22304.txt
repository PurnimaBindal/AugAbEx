According to Rule 5.32B(2) of the Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol. II, an employee may voluntarily retire by giving a notice of retirement.
It is unclear from the language used in the rules whether prior permission to retire is required.
If it is, then issuing a notice or request alone will not grant automatic retirement.
However, if acceptance of the notice/request is not required, retirement will be deemed to have occurred on the date of expiry of the notice period.
The request for permission contemplated by the sub-rules is for exemption from the notice period.
The proviso to sub-rule 1 states that, if the appointing authority does not refuse to grant permission before the notice period expires, retirement will become effective from the date of expiry of that period.
