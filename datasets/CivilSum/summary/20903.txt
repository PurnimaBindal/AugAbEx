Maharashtra Service of Engineers Class I and Class II Cadre Rules, Rule 16(a) - Seniority - Junior Engineers - Work charged establishment service - Work charged establishment service is not counted towards seniority in the regular cadre.
However, the State Govt. has decided that a Junior Engineer with work charged service will be assigned a deemed date of appointment in the regular cadre one day prior to the date on which his immediate junior was appointed - Seniority must take into account both work charged service and deemed dates assigned in accordance with the Resolutions of the State Govt. [Paras 6 and 7]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Work charged service - Seniority - Usually, service in a work charged establishment is not taken into account for determining seniority in the regular establishment.
Work charged appointments are tenure appointments, and do not count towards regular establishment cadre service.
