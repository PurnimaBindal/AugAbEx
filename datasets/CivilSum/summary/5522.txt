Constitution of India, Article 16 - Compassionate appointment - Dying-in-Harness - Request for - Delayed - Petitioner's father, an Additional Chief Engineer in PWD, died in harness - 7 years after his father's passing, petitioners sought appointment under the said scheme for immediate relief - Benefit is not meant to be prolonged but rather immediate - Additionally, the petitioner comes from a prosperous family - No financial necessity in the family was disclosed - Thus, the petitioner is not entitled to such appointment.
