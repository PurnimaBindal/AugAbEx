Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Part -I, Regulation 173 - Disability pension - Injury suffered while on casual leave - Service attributability - A member of the Army Act and Rules that sustains injuries while on casual leave at his residence should be considered caused by and worsened by military service.
If invalidated on these grounds, he's eligible for disability pension - A claim of a disabled pension can not be considered inadmissible solely on reasons that he was not on active duty at the time of the injury, and being at home, the injury was not due to service.
