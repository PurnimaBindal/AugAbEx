Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 relates to reference and its challenge.
The petitioner Union raised a dispute seeking similar wages as paid to others in Indian Airlines, leading to termination of services of the concerned employee and seeking of reference.
The respondents claimed no connection with the employees in question which is a premature plea.
Therefore, the dispute must be referred to the appropriate court.
