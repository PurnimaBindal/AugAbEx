Section 36-B of the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 requires contractors to register with the Provident Fund Commissioner and provide a salary sheet and copy of Provident Fund contribution payment to have their bill cleared.
Petitioner contractors did not prove that this Act is not applicable to them or that their laborers are not employees of the Electricity Board in question, therefore the circular requiring registration is not arbitrary or invalid.
