The Punjab Medical Education Service (Class-I) Rules state that to be eligible for the promotion to the post of Professor in a speciality, a person must possess a certain qualification achieved after the requisite post graduate qualification in the speciality concerned.
Appendices A and B further clarify the rules related to eligibility qualifications and experience for promotion.
[Paras 14 and 15]

In addition to Appendix C's requirements, the rule-making authority's intention is expressed through the use of the word "requisite" between "after" and "post graduate" to indicate that teaching experience must be obtained after acquiring the necessary qualification.
[Paras 18 and 21]

Eligibility for promotion to the post of Professor is based on the date of the vacancy, not the eligibility that may have been acquired at a later date.
[Para 22]

Leave without pay and suspension periods cannot be counted as teaching experience for promotion purposes.
[Paras 22 and 23]

To modify a regular promotion order to an ad-hoc promotion, notice and an opportunity to the person concerned must be given.
Otherwise, the order will be considered invalid.
[Paras 24 and 25]
