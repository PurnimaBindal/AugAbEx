Constitution of India, Article 14 - Rajasthan Municipalities Act, 1959, Section 297 - Rajasthan Municipal (Pension) Rules, 1989, Rules 3 and 4 - Pension - Classification of employees - Classification based on retirement time - Not permissible - Employees belong to the same class for receiving pension - Classification violates Article 14 of Constitution, as the criteria is not rationally correlated to the objective underlying it - The criteria has been struck down.
