Constitution of India, Article 226 - Employees of corporation - Office memo reviewing rehabilitation scheme - Argument raised against altering scheme sanctioned by BIFR - 5 members of management staff file writ petition claiming right to compel appellant to keep Mills running - Court rules that petitioners failed to demonstrate infringement of any legal right and appellant has no intention to dispense with their services if they do not opt for VRS - Court finds no basis for grievance and rules that the case is not fit for discretionary jurisdiction.
