Constitution of India, Article 226 - Lower School course - The High Court set aside the dismissal order against the petitioners and reinstated them with continuity of service and 50% back wages, entitling them to other consequential benefits.
A benefit was that they were declared successful for the lower school course based on an earlier test, but were not deputed for the course as they remained out of service at that time.
Respondents argued that it was too late to send petitioners for the course as other successful candidates had already been sent.
However, the court ordered the respondents to depute petitioners for the course, and upon successful completion, they were granted seniority immediately above junior persons.
