Constitution of India 1950, Article 226 - Appointment - Lady Supervisor in Welfare Department - Petitioner Anganbari Sevika - The petitioner contended that juniors were appointed as lady supervisor ignoring circular and her seniority - Court held that suitability was the only criterion for appointment as per circular - Petitioner was not denied consideration for suitability - No relief granted, but authorities directed to fill up the post in accordance with law and petitioner's case be considered if eligible - Petition dismissed.
