Constitution of India, 1950, Article 124(4) - Removal of a Judge of the Supreme Court - In regards to a Motion of Impeachment, it was questioned whether members who abstained from voting should be seen as supporting the Motion - However, it was held that the voting procedure allows for abstentions and that abstaining or refusing to express an opinion cannot be interpreted as support for the Motion - Silence does not equal acquiescence - The petition was dismissed.
