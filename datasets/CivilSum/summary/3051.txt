According to AP State Warehousing Corporation Employees Regulations, 1965, Regulation 11, and the Constitution of India Articles 16 and 226, voluntary resignation can be withdrawn if a three months' notice period is given.
However, in a specific case, the petitioner submitted his resignation without giving the required notice and requested that it be accepted immediately.
He reiterated this request within a week, and the respondent-Corporation accepted his resignation, even though he had not given the legally mandated notice.
The Corporation relieved him of his duties before his withdrawal letter reached their office.
This action was found to be legal.
