According to the High Court, the actions of the appellant were discriminatory under Article 16(1) of the Constitution because they denied a similar opportunity to a Supervisor Grade 'A' who was eligible for promotion to the post of Chargeman Grade II.
The Court's directions to hold an examination for all eligible supervisors were not followed.
The Tribunal addressed this issue in fresh writ petitions by directing the appellants to re-fix notional seniority for supervisors Grade 'A' in ordnance factories and fix their pay-scale and benefits based on their dates of examination.
This decision was legally sound.
