The Air Force Act of 1950, Sections 23, 65, 71 and the Air Force Rules of 1969, Rule 18 state that an officer who conducts themselves in an unbecoming manner can be dismissed without court-martial.
This is because the authority granted under Section 20(3) of the Act and Rule 18 of the Rules is distinct and separate from the authority provided in the Act for matters related to court-martial.
