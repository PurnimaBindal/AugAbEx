The petitioner, who had been employed on a daily wage basis in the University and had been transferred to the work charged establishment after completing approximately 2-1/2 years of service, was terminated owing to various acts amounting to misconduct.
The termination was performed after holding a disciplinary enquiry in accordance with the terms of the appointment letter, which was determined to be lawful.
[Para 14] B.
While dispensing with a departmental enquiry, the decision cannot be dependent solely on the opinion of the relevant authorities.
In the event that the satisfaction of the concerned authority is questioned in a court of law, those who support the order must demonstrate that the satisfaction was based on specific objective facts and not on the whim or fancy of the concerned officer.
