Constitution of India 1950, Articles 14, 16 and 41 - Education - Admission - Eligibility - Brochure required a minimum of 50% aggregate marks in the Board examination - The Board also clarified that the petitioner had to pass all five subjects - Even though the petitioner had passed her 10+2 examination with six subjects and had secured less than 50% marks, she had secured 504% marks in the five subjects required - The eligibility condition required the petitioner to satisfy the minimum prescribed percentage - Held that the eligibility condition specified that the 50% aggregate marks had to be of all the subjects taken in the 10+2 examination.
[Paras 3 and 4] B Eligibility - Admission - The terms and conditions of the brochure and the Government's notified scheme are binding and must be strictly adhered to.
