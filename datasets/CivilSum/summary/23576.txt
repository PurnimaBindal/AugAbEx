According to the Bihar and Orissa Veterinary Service Class-I Rules, 1935, Rule 14 which is in accordance with the Indian Constitution's Articles 14 and 309, seniority within the service is based on the officer's date of substantive appointment, regardless of their pay.
However, a new cadre has been created without any basis, causing seniors to be placed in a subsidiary or secondary cadre, indicating a violation of the rules and a bureaucratic strategy to favor loyalists on secretariat interests.
It is important to note that vested rights cannot be taken away by executive fiat that violates statutory rules.
