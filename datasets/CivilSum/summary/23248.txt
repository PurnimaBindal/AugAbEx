Under the Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules of 1965, military service during a national emergency could be counted for pension as a civil servant.
However, this rule has since been repealed under Punjab Recruitment of Ex-servicemen Rules, 1982, Regulation 2.
A respondent who joined the Army in 1961 and served during the 1962 emergency is not entitled to have his military service counted for pension as a civil servant.
Although the respondent has been receiving a pension from the Army, he cannot claim a pension from the State due to being ineligible during that period and cannot benefit twice.
The Court held that only those individuals who had voluntarily offered their services for the defense of the country during the 1962 national emergency are entitled to the benefits of accelerated seniority and dual pensions, citing cases from 1994(2) SCT 68 and 1997(2) SCT 88.
