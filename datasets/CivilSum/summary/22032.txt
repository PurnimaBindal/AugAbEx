Constitution of India, Article 311 - Railways Services (Conduct) Rules, 1966, Rule 3(1)(i) and (ii) - Misconduct - Compulsory retirement - An order of compulsory retirement, which is not considered a punishment, is a prerogative of the government and should be based on observable evidence.
Its issuance is subjective to the satisfaction of the government and does not require any statement of explanation.
The principles of natural justice are irrelevant concerning the order of compulsory retirement.
[Paras 16 and 18]
