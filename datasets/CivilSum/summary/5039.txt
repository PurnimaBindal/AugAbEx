Claim of pay scale prescribed by revised pay scale rules cannot be barred by estoppel, as per Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16(4) - Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1988, Rule 8, and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 18 - Authorities cannot use such a plea when employee is claiming benefits as per Rules.

A promotion entitles an employee to two increments, even if the cadres are merged after promotion, according to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16(4) - Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1988, Rule 8.
