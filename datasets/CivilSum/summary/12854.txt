Haryana Cooperative Societies Act, 1984, Section 29(3) - Difference of opinion - The Registrar's nominee having a difference in opinion with other members did not require the matter to be referred to the Government as the Registrar was not authorized to nominate a person in his place.
Section 29(3) was not relevant.

Constitution of India Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana Cooperative Societies Act, 1984, Section 29 - Promotion/Qualifications - The petitioner was promoted to Deputy Chief Engineer, but one member of the Board disagreed, stating that the petitioner lacked the required qualifications.
However, several other persons holding the same position had the same qualifications.
Therefore, there was no justification to revert the petitioner.
