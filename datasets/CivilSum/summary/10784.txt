Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Seniority list and promotion - Challenge as to - The respondent has been appointed before the petitioner and is at the top of the seniority list.
The department did not make a distinction between Assistant Draftsman (Civil) working in Architectural, Building, or Road wing, and promotion is meant to be made from the role of Assistant Draftsman.
The respondent holds a Diploma in Architectural Assistant, and the petitioner holds a Diploma in Draftsman.
The official respondents believed that the respondent possessed the essential qualification, and no injustice has been done.
The respondent was adequately qualified to be promoted.
[Paras 6, 9 and 12]
