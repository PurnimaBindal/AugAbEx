Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Education - Professional education standards - Recognition and affiliation of educational institutions - Admissions to un-recognized and un-affiliated institutions - Students admitted to such institutions cannot be aided in regularizing their admissions or in obtaining recognition for their degrees or diplomas - No exemptions can be made for such students due to their innocence or inability - Students who feel cheated or damaged may sue such institutions for damages - However, courts cannot pass interim orders to perpetuate illegal activities contrary to the governing rules and laws.
