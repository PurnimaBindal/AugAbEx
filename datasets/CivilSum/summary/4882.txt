As per Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.-II, 532-A, Vol.-I, Part-I Rule 3.26(d) and Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Vol.
I, Rule 9.18, in cases where a Sub Inspector Police is reported to have doubtful integrity with average immediate preceding year performance, premature compulsory retirement can be considered by the competent authority.
The fact that a criminal case related to corruption is still pending in trial cannot be ignored.
The service record can be taken into account to compulsorily retire such officers in the public interest and in the interest of the police force.
