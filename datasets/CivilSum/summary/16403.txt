Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Pay scales - Revision of pay scales - Office Daftries and Press Daftries were initially placed on equal pay scales - However, during a later revision, only the pay scale of Office Daftries was revised while Press Daftries were ignored and not given any reason for different treatment - Such arbitrary and discriminatory action by the Govt. is highly unacceptable.
