Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Post of JBT Teacher - Approval - The petitioner, who was a B.Sc.
and B.Ed.
holder, applied for the post in response to an advertisement.
Her application was refused on the ground that she was not a J.B.T.
However, the petitioner was duly selected by the Selection Committee, and even the instructions showed that persons with B.Ed.
qualifications were to be accommodated after adjustment of persons with J.B.T.
qualifications.
As such, the petitioner cannot be held responsible for the inaction of the college authorities for not incorporating Government instructions in the advertisement for the post.
Therefore, directions have been given to grant approval to the appointment of petitioner against the vacant post of J.B.T.
Teacher.
[Paras 6 & 7]
