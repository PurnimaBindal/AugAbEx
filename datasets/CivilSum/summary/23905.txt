Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Madhya Pradesh Transport Department Subordinate (Class III-Executive) Service Recruitment Rules, 1971, Schedule VI, Rules 11-A and 20 - Appointment - Reserved category post - Departmental Examination - Candidates belonging to SC/ST category - Allowed relaxation in passing marks - Posts of Transport - Sub-Inspector - It has been established that 10% relaxation from general passing marks of 50% is permissible under Article 16(4) of the Constitution, for SC/ST candidates competing for reserved category posts where general category candidates are not competing.
