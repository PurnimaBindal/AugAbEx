Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Dispute of inter se seniority between petitioners promoted to the post of Agricultural Officers, Class II and respondents who were direct recruitees to the same post - Government must provide alternate redressal forum for such grievances - Tribunal's jurisdiction is limited - Chief Secretary of State must establish high power committee and petitioners should submit their respective representations.
