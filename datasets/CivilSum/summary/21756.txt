Notification issued under the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 1(5) does not exclude Advertising agencies, which utilise services of experts and sell those services to clients.
As such, Advertising agencies are considered as shops under the Act, and its premises are covered under the definition of shops.
[Paras 16, 17 and 18]  B.
Steamship companies that engage in a systematic economic or commercial activity, even if not carrying out stevedoring operations, are considered as shops under the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 1(5).
