Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 22(3)(f) - Central Administrative Tribunal Rules, 1987, Rule 17 - Review - Selection - The Central Administrative Tribunal needs to exercise caution when deliberating on cases, especially when reviewing the records maintained by selection committees.
The Tribunal previously received records that did not include tabulation or mark sheets, but only notes.
However, it was later discovered that these documents were available and only the notes were destroyed.
The Tribunal's decision to order a fresh selection without reviewing all available records and ignoring important facts presented in the review petition is not justifiable.
As a result, the review is granted.
[Paras 2 and 3]
