Punjab Police Rules, Rule 1638(2) - Departmental enquiry - Dismissal from service after misconduct proved - Respondent's suit is dismissed - Lower appellate Court deems departmental enquiry to be invalid due to contradiction with Rule 1638(2) - Correct interpretation of Rule is that offenders must be prosecuted or non-prosecution reasons must be recorded - Therefore, defects in order will not affect departmental enquiry action.
