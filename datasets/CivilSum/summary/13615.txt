The Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Scholarship - The petitioners, who are employed as whole-time Research Scholars/Fellows under Kurukshetra University with postgraduate degrees, have their scholarship funded by the Haryana Government.
Therefore, only the Government of Haryana shall determine the amount to be paid and the petitioners are not entitled to revised emoluments under the revised guidelines for the benefit of research personnel working on R&R Programmes funded by Central Govt. Departments/Agencies.
The instructions would only apply to those persons whose scholarship is funded by the Central Government.
[Para 4]

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Scholarship - As the funding agency is different for the petitioners, the question of equal pay for equal work does not arise.
