Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995 (Kerala), Section 39 - Reservation for physically handicapped persons in Govt. educational institutions - The government is mandated to reserve 3% of the total number of seats in Govt. and aided colleges - The reservation must be allocated from the overall number of seats and not in addition to any reserved seats for privileged categories.
