Constitution of India, Article 226 - Extension of Service - Natural Justice - Petitioner's request for extension of service denied due to delay in passing promotion examination - Policy requires airmen seeking extension to pass exam three months prior to regular service expiry - Extension of service is not guaranteed - Principles of natural justice are not applicable - No intervention.
[Paras 5 & 7] Case cited: JWO Shankar v Union of India, LPA No. 416/98 decided on 2491998.
