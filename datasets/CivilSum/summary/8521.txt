Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Trade Unions Act, 1926, Sections 4 and 8 - Recognition - Recognition of Union of employees enjoying support of majority - The Government's refusal to reimburse expenses related to conducting a secret ballot lacks rationality and does not promote healthy industrial relations between employer and employees - Respondents cannot efficiently carry out administration without a recognized collective bargaining agent - The Commissioner of Labour is directed to conduct the secret ballot with funds provided by the Government.
