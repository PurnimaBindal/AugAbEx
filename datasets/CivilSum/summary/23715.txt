The High Court dismissed a Judicial Officer for misconduct after evidence was presented about written complaints to the District Judge.
It was determined that the finding of guilt was not based on no evidence, as there was prima facie proof of conclusioin charges.
The punishment of dismissal from service was deemed necessary in upholding credibility and integrity.
Disciplinary proceedings are not subject to the same standards of proof as criminal trials.
