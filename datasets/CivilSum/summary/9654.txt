In accordance with Rule 326 of the Civil Service Rules, Volume I, Part I, the petitioner challenged the decision of the punishing authority to order compulsory retirement.
Despite no charges being proved in the enquiry report, the punishing authority passed the order without providing a dissenting note, based solely on recommendations from the Personal Committee.
However, guidelines issued by the state government of Haryana on November 19, 1991 state that compulsory retirement on attaining the age of 50-55 years must take into account the service record of the past ten years and there should be no entry regarding doubtful integrity.
Since the impugned order was passed without following these guidelines, and was intended to punish the petitioner rather than serving the public interest or administrative exigency, the petition is allowed.
