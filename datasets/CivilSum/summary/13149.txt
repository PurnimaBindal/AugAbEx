According to Section 12 of the Police Act, 1961, the government can recover wrong payments, such as double advance payments, made due to a misinterpretation of a decision taken by the Council of Ministers.
In this case, the government of Tripura had decided to offer incentives to employees who did not participate in a strike, but the petitioners - who were Sub Inspectors - were not entitled to double advance payment as they were posted at a Training Institute in West Bengal under its control.
The government is entitled to recover the payment made under a misconstruction of the decision, and if the amount is significant, it must be done through easy installments.
