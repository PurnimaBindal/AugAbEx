The petitioner expressed his unwillingness to attend the Military Course due to family circumstances and children's education, but later withdrew this unwillingness.
The respondents did not mention if another officer was selected to fill his place, and the petitioner stated that a seat had been kept vacant for him.
The court deemed the order to remove the petitioner from the course as unsustainable in law and set it aside.
