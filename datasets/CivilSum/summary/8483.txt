Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Reservation - Merit list - The court has directed to re-determine the waiting list and set aside the select list to the extent in excess of advertised posts.
However, it does not mean to re-determine the merit of candidates who were already appointed against available advertised posts and bring down candidates of higher merit below those lower in the select list.
An order of termination by change of merit is considered illegal and unsustainable.
As a result, the petitioner is deemed to be continuing in service.
