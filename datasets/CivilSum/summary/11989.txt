Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Reservation - Relaxations - Change of category from general to reserved - Prohibited - The selecting authority does not have the power to alter the classification of a general candidate to that of a reserved category - The only alternative for the authority is to advertise the remaining position in the reserved category and invite all eligible candidates - General category candidates will not be allowed to change to the reserved category after realizing that their name is not on the merit list of the general category.
[Paras 10, 11 and 14]
