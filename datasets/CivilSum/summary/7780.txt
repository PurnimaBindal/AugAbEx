Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Substitution of penalty - The Labour Court has the power to modify punishment.
- The scope of this power was tested in a case where a worker was found guilty of negligence.
- The Labour Court lowered the punishment for the negligence, saying it was not intentional.
- However, this decision was criticized, particularly because the employer had suffered a major loss in the international market.
- The Court held that the award of reinstatement with 75% back wages was disproportionate, and modified the award to only reinstatement without back wages.
