Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission Selection Process - Merit based Allocation - Reservation - A single seat for MBBS course is reserved for Malayalee origin candidates - Petitioner and respondent No. 5 applied for the same seat - Petitioner challenged the acceptance of respondent No. 5's local certificate, alleging influence of his father on obtaining it - The Court found that the local certificate of respondent No.5 was correctly accepted - Hence, there was no irregularity in selecting and placing respondent No. 5 on the merit list - The petition was dismissed.
