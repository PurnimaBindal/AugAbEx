Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission - Regulations for Admission to Post Graduate Medical Course for 1996-97, Regulation 6(ii) - Legality of regulation 6(ii) - Candidates who are studying PG courses and were previously admitted cannot take the entrance test unless they pay the bond amount and stipend received up to date - Court held regulation is valid and not arbitrary - Regulation imposes a reasonable restriction on students who wish to take the entrance test while studying PG courses in one subject.
