Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Punishment Embezzlement - Dismissal from service - The Labour Court found that charges of embezzlement levelled against the petitioner were established and proved in a proper and fair domestic enquiry.
The petitioner was not granted benefit under Section 11-A of the Act by the Labour Court.
Dismissal from service was deemed appropriate as embezzlement, regardless of the amount, is a serious offence and the punishment cannot be said to be disproportionate to the charge.
Thus, the amount embezzled (Rs. 1755) shall not affect the seriousness of the charge and the petition is dismissed.
