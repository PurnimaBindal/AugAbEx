In the context of the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 and the Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provisions) Act, 1985, Section 22 does not prevent the recovery of arrears of Provident Fund under Section 8-B of the 1952 Act.
Although Section 22 suspends certain legal proceedings against properties of industrial companies, it does not prohibit the use of coercive measures outlined in Section 8-B of the Act.
[Para 7] B.
A notice under Section 8-B of the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, requiring a petitioner to show cause as to why they should not be committed to civil prison in execution of a certificate for recovery of arrears of Provident Fund, cannot be quashed on the basis that the respondents should have first taken action for sale and attachment of the property of the establishment under sub-clause (a) of Section 8-B(i) rather than resorting to the mode outlined in sub-clause (b).
This is because sub-section (i) of Section 8-B prescribes alternative modes of arrear recovery on the basis of a certificate issued by an authorised officer.
