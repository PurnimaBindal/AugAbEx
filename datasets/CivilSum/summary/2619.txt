Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 309 - Rajasthan Rules for Payment of Grant-in-Aid to Non Government Educational and Cultural Institutions, 1963, Rule 3 sub-rule 15 - Confirmation - The petitioner was initially appointed on probation for one academic session and her probation was extended from time to time.
Although the petitioner claimed automatic confirmation after the two-year probation period, she did not file supporting documents.
The appointment letter clearly stated that the appointment was for a specific period.
The court held that the petitioner's appointment was not in line with the rules and she is not entitled to confirmation after the two-year probation period.
