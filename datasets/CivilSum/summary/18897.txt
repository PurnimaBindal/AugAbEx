Medical reimbursement - Maximum limit - Treatment at a non-accredited facility - A patient underwent a kidney transplant surgery - Despite being directed to AIIMS, the petitioner was unable to get admitted owing to a lack of beds and was placed 26th on the waiting list, and was treated at a private hospital - The court ruled that he is entitled to reimbursement equivalent to the applicable rate at AIIMS during the relevant period.
