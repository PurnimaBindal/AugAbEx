The petitioner appointed as coordinator and subsequently confirmed in the same post was not given the pay scale.
While the petitioner's name was included in the combined seniority list of another cadre, he had never worked on that post nor enjoyed the fringe benefits available to the cadre.
The petitioner's juniors were promoted to higher posts and scales, causing a delay in his claim for scale.
It was held that the petitioner should be given seniority from the day he was appointed as coordinator and his scale should be admissible from the date when his juniors were made eligible.
