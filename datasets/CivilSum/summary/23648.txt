The Constitution of India's 42nd Amendment states that the wage structure can be revised to the disadvantage of workmen if management proves to the Industrial Tribunal that the existing wage structure was well above the minimum level and management cannot pay existing wages due to financial stringency.
[Para 28]

It is not justified for an employer who cannot pay the minimum wage to engage labour and run the industry.
[Paras 12 and 13]

If the wage structure of an industry or employment is already at the level of minimum wage, no revision is permissible to the disadvantage of workmen according to the Constitution of India's 42nd Amendment.
[Para 28]

A workman's wage is no longer a contract between employer and employee and instead has the force of collective bargaining under the labour laws.
[Para 12]

If an Industrial Tribunal acts in total oblivion of the legal position and causes manifest injustice to workmen, the Supreme Court has jurisdiction to interfere according to the Constitution of India, Article 136.
[Para 35]

In a company operating a Dearness Allowance scheme to neutralize the cost of living, the company is not allowed to revise the scheme to the disadvantage of workmen even if they argue that the D.A.
Scheme was more beneficial than the D.A.
Scheme adopted by other industries and resulted in over-neutralization according to 1986(2) SCR 484 relied.
[Paras 29 and 30]
