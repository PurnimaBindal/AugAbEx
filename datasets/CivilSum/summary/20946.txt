Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 4(5) - Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960 - Scheme for payment of gratuity framed by the employer - Workmen can't choose both - They can opt for either benefit under the Scheme or the Statute - Blue pencil doctrine doesn't apply when choosing between the two - If the employer's Scheme is unacceptable, workmen can choose the Statute, but they must choose one.
