According to Section 3 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, if a workman dies while on duty, the employer is liable to compensate his dependents.
In a case where a taxi driver was killed by an unknown person while staying in a rented room with tourists, it was held that the accident had occurred in the course of employment since the workman was there because of his job.
Although the employer had not made any special arrangements for his stay, the deceased was still bound to find a place to stay and therefore the employer and the insurance company are both responsible for compensation.
