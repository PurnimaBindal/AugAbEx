Under Section 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a workman's services were terminated after being absent for two days without formal permission.
The Labour Court concluded that the departmental inquiry conducted was legally sound and substituted the penalty of termination with stoppage of five increments permanently.
Though the penalty was lowered to two increments with cumulative effect, the petitioner was given 25% of back wages.
However, the alleged misdemeanours are serious enough that even the reduced punishment was considered harsh.
