Under the Army Act, 1950, Sections 63 and 123(1) and Army Rules, 1954, Rule 16-A, disciplinary proceedings allow a person to be taken and kept in military custody under Section 123(1) if two conditions are met: if the person committed an offense under the Act while subject to it, and if the person has ceased to be subject to the Act.
The Petitioner had received a tentative charge sheet for an offense under Section 63 of the Army Act while they were in Army Service and subject to the Act.
However, they ceased to be subject to the Act after retirement under Section 2(2) of the Army Act.
Despite retiring, provisions of Section 123 of the Act could still be invoked against the Petitioner.
The Central Government has sanctioned their detention in military custody for more than three months, which does not violate Sections 101, 102, and 103 or Rules 23 and 27.
Therefore, the detention of the Petitioner in military custody after retirement is not illegal according to Constitution of India, Article 226.
