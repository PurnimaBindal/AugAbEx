In accordance with Constitution of India, Article 16, an employee could not achieve a corrected date of birth on the basis of a School Certificate that was created after the entry into service.
It is required to have a contemporaneous document that the employee was unable to produce at the time of entry into service due to uncontrollable circumstances.
[Paras 5 and 6]

The distinction between the School Certificates of a student who passed as a regular student and that of a private candidate was explained in terms of the possibility of concealing the real age in the case of a private student.
