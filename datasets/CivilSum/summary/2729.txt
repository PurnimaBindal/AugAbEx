The termination order was withdrawn, but the petitioner is claiming consequential benefits.
The High Court disposed of the writ challenging the termination as infructuous since the retrenchment order was withdrawn.
However, the respondents argue that the withdrawal order stipulates that the decision regarding that period will be made after the final order from the High Court is received.
It is established law that when a termination order is set aside for being illegal or without jurisdiction, reinstatement with full back wages is the norm, and there is no apparent reason for deviating from it.
As a result, the petitioner is entitled to receive their full salary and any increments earned during the period in contention.
