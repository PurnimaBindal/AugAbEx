Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Prevention of Corruption Act, Sections 7 & 13(1)(d) - Haryana State Electricity Board (P&A) Regulations, 1990, Regulation 7(2)(d)(ii) - Conviction on Corruption Charges - Suspension of sentence - Dismissal or removal from service on the basis of conviction - Automatic dismissal from service is a consequence of conviction for government servants convicted on criminal charges of corruption - It is not appropriate to retain such a person in service - Mere suspension of sentence does not void the sentence - The punishing authority need not wait for appellate court decisions in criminal proceedings to take action.
