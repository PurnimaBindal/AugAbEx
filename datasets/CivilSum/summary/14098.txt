Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Contractual appointment - A petitioner was appointed as a computer operator under the Special Assistance Programme for a fixed duration after a grant was received from the UGC.
The petitioner accepted the job of the University with complete knowledge of its implications, and bound himself by the undertaking he voluntarily gave.
According to the appointment order, the termination of petitioner is not improper.
[Paras 10, 12 and 14]
