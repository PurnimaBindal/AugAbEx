Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Regularization - The petitioner was appointed as a stenographer on a contract basis with a fixed term that was extended from time to time.
Despite putting in five years of continuous service and making several representations for regularization, the petitioner was terminated and refused any scope of future employment.
The contention was that no regular post of part-time instructor was available and the petitioner was also claiming equal pay for equal work.
The court held that since the initial appointment of the petitioner was not in accordance with the rules, he cannot claim equal treatment and regularization as a matter of right.
The ban sought to be put by respondents was set aside and the petition was disposed of accordingly.
[Paras 12 to 14]
