According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, reorganisation was implemented in January 1976 in accordance with consultant reports.
The report indicated that those handling personal matters were not necessarily required to be absorbed in the Personnel Management Cell.
A petition for joint seniority was filed in 1991 after many individuals had retired and experienced various transfers and promotions.
Due to the considerable time lapse, it would not be practical to merge the petitioner into the Personnel Management Cell or General Services Cell.
The court dismissed the petition, citing delays and laches.
