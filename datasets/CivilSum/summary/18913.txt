Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Swatantrata Sainik Samman Pension Scheme, 1980 - Petitioner claims pension under the Scheme of 1980 on the grounds of being dependent on her freedom fighter husband - Initially granted but later suspended - Deceased husband had undergone imprisonment in three camps, totaling 10 months - Petition allowed - Respondents ordered to consider petitioner's case under the 1980 Scheme.
