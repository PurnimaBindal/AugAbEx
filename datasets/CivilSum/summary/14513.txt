Respondent AG Masih, AAG, Punjab argued that the termination of services of the conductors of Punjab Roadways was necessary as they were no longer required, and the juniors were retained in accordance with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
The junior-most employee had to go first.
However, the respondents were directed to take the petitioners back into service with continuity of service but without back wages for the period they did not work.
