According to Section 33(2)(b) of the Industrial Disputes Act, the approval of the Labour Court is not a decision on the merits of any issue, but rather a determination of whether the management has complied with basic principles of the law before awarding punishment or changing service conditions.
Thus, the approval of the Labour Court does not preclude a workman from challenging any subsequent orders of the management, as the principle of res judicata does not apply.
