Section 85-B of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948 establishes that a penalty is to be imposed for delay in payment of contributions.
However, the amount of penalty can be reduced in certain circumstances, such as when the delay is due to changes within the employer's business.
In this case, the ESI court recognized the employer's contention that the delayed payment was due to a change in partnership and financial difficulties, and therefore ordered a reduction in the penalty amount.
This reduction is fully justified according to Para 7 of the text.
