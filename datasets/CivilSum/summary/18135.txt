The petitioner, a member of Central Government Health Scheme, sought reimbursement for medical treatment received by his wife in a private, unrecognized hospital in an emergency situation.
The petitioner argued that he was not expected to take his wife to a recognized hospital or get a referral through a CGHS dispensary given the circumstances of the situation.
The court deemed the hyper-technical stand taken by the respondents as unreasonable and unjustified.
The court found that the expenditure incurred by a government servant while in service or after retirement should be reimbursed by the state.
As a result, the court directed that the petitioner be reimbursed an amount equal to the rates of PGI, Chandigarh.
