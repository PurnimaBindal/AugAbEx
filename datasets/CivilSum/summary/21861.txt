Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Working Journalists and other Newspaper Employees (Conditions of Service) and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1955, Sections 3 & 11 - The board formed under Section 11 cannot replace the role of the labor court.
The main purpose of the board is to evaluate the working conditions of newspaper employees and establish the wage rate for working journalists.
Disputes related to the retrenchment of working journalists fall under the jurisdiction of the labor court.
