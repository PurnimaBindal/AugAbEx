Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 - A dismissed civil suit for the appointment of petitioner in Corporation on the ground of their land being acquired without liberty to file another cause of action - A subsequent writ petition is also dismissed as petitioner has no fresh cause of action - Any further notice issued by Corporation cannot give a fresh cause of action to file another writ petition - Such petition is liable to be dismissed as barred.
[Paras 6 and 8] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Land Acquisition Act, Section 11 - Indian Oil Corporation appointments must be made in accordance with the provisions of the Constitution - Posts cannot be reserved exclusively for those whose land was acquired and compensation paid - They are not in an advantageous position - The Collector cannot order appointments for those whose lands were acquired by the Corporation.
