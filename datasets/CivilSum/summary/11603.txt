The petitioners, who work as SDOs/SDEs/AEs in Corporation, seek a revision of their pay scale to match that of SDOs/SDEs/AEs in the Haryana Government departments.
The Corporation cannot argue that it is not in a financial position to match the pay scale only for certain categories of employees because this would be discriminatory and violate Article 14 of the Constitution.
[Paras 17, 18, 19] B.
The principle of equal pay for equal work can only be invoked if two posts are equated and it is shown that two sets of employees are doing similar work with similar responsibilities.
The fact that two departments had the same pay scale in the past does not automatically invoke this principle, which is not a fundamental right.
