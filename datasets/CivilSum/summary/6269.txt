Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana State Government Policy/Instruction dated 29.3.1991 - Post of Sub Divisional Officer in Panchayati Raj Department - Natural justice - Termination - Regularisation of terminated employee - Petitioner's services were terminated due to some omissions and commissions, but no details were provided and no disciplinary proceedings were initiated.
Additionally, no show cause notice or opportunity for a hearing was given before terminating the petitioner's services, making the termination order invalid.
As a result, the petitioner is entitled to be considered for regularisation under the Policy.
