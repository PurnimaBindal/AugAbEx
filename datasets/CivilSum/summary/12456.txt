Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Calcutta Metropolitan Development Authority Service Regulations, 1976 - Promotion - Typing test Promotion from category 'D' employees to Category 'C' employees i.e. typist- cum-clerk - Eligibility criteria - Knowledge of typing is an essential qualification - There is no provision for relaxation of the essential qualification for promotion - Employees of appellant are entitled to all facilities enjoyed by the State Govt. employees except pension - Fixing up of eligibility criteria cannot be dispensed with unless there exist some provisions - Essential qualification cannot come within the purview of the term 'facility' - Petitioners- respondents were promoted in terms of the judgment passed by the Trial Judge - If the petitioners-respondents pass the typing test, they would be promoted with retrospective effect, given their four years of prior experience - Promotion made during the pendency of an appeal, and no stay order passed by the appeal court, cannot be disturbed.
[Paras 5 & 6]
