The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, cover the topics of Promotion and Reversion.
A promotion committee deemed the petitioner eligible for promotion, and the competent authority promoted them.
The successor to the competent authority does not have the power to override these orders.
Additionally, there was no evidence to support the accusation that the petitioner had obtained their promotion through illegal means.
Therefore, the reversion order is both without jurisdiction and unlawful.
