Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Sections 19 and 24 - Interim injunction - In cases pertaining to recruitment process and selections, the Tribunal should only grant an injunction in the rarest of rare cases.
Mere establishment of a strong prima facie case, balance of convenience, and irreparable injury in the applicant's favour are not enough to warrant an interim injunction or stay.
