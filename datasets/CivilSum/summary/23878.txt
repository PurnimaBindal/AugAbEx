Letters Patent Appeal can be maintained against a judgment passed by a single bench under Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution.
[Para 16]
Termination of workmen's services immediately after the failure of conciliation without prior permission of Conciliation Officer U/s 33(1) is violative of Statutory provision of the Act.
The legal fiction introduced by the legislature in Section 20(1) should be given full effect.
[Paras 19 to 30]
The introduction of any rationalisation scheme by the employer should be after notice U/s 9-A Notice served after the introduction of such scheme is considered invalid.
[Paras 31 to 37]
Discharge of a workman from service without proper penalty and in undue haste amounts to unfair labour practice.
[Paras 39 to 51]
While hearing an appeal against a single bench in writ jurisdiction, a Letters Patent Bench can correct patent errors of law committed by the Single Judge.
[Para 52]
A workman who refuses alternative employment offered by the employer and gets his original termination annulled by the court is not entitled to full back wages but only a reasonable compensation.
