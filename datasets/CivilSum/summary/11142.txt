Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 discusses Reference/Absence from duty.
The Government cannot adjudicate the dispute if the workman is willfully absent from duty, as this goes beyond the scope of Section 10 of the Act.
All the Government is required to do is form an opinion about whether there is an adjudicatable dispute between the parties.
In this case, the reference was declined without forming an opinion, which was an action undertaken without jurisdiction.
As a result, the Government has been directed to refer the matter to the labour Court within two months.
