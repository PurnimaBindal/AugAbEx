Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Absence from duty - Dismissal from service - Natural justice - Ex parte enquiry - Service of Charge-sheet - Quantum of punishment - A Veterinary officer, who left his duty without permission and intimation and went abroad, was issued a charge-sheet on the charge of absence.
Despite efforts made, the charge-sheet could not be served, and the officer did not attend the enquiry proceedings, even after publication in newspapers.
However, the punishing authority made every effort to serve a show cause notice.
The officer was dismissed from service after holding an ex parte enquiry, which was not deemed bad or excessive.
It was concluded that a responsible officer who abandons his duties in an irresponsible manner cannot complain that dismissal from service on that ground was excessive.
[Paras 5 to 7]
