According to APSE Board Employees Disciplinary and Appeal Regulations, Regulation 11(b), an employee who has been in police custody for over 48 hours is deemed to have been suspended and is entitled to subsistence allowance, unless the employer permits the employee to resume duty.
The suspension will continue even after the employee's release unless an appropriate order is passed.
The employee is entitled to subsistence allowance regardless of whether they are under deemed suspension or suspension by appropriate order.
The respondents have been directed to pass a final order in disciplinary proceedings and to consider the petitioner's request for subsistence allowance.
