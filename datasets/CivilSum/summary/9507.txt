(Professional) course - Rule III-A of Prospectus of Department of Law, Kurukshetra University, for session 2000-2001, stated that only government regular employees could be admitted to evening seats - A petitioner, working on an ad hoc basis, was refused admission - The petitioner alleged discrimination - The University's distinction was deemed not to violate Article 14 or 16 - An ad hoc employee's status is a temporary arrangement, and length of passage does not give any service rights - The petitioner, being ad-hoc, was denied admission - The petition was dismissed.
