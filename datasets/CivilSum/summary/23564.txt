Constitution of India, Article 323 - Appointment - Selection of direct recruit members of the Bar as Additional District and Sessions Judges - A sub-committee suggested a procedure for selection, which was approved by the Full Court for future recruitment of members of the Bar as Addl.
District and Sessions Judges - Respondents were appointed in 1989 and are currently holding their positions - Court finds no reason to unsettle their selection and no tangible illegality in their selection and appointment - Petition dismissed.
