According to the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Sections 51 and 52, as well as the Code for the Physically Handicapped, 1985, Paragraph 55, Assistant Teachers working in Institutes meant for physically handicapped persons must attain the minimum qualification of holding D Ed.
However, the replaced Code of 1997 took away the facility of five years time for acquiring this qualification, which the Petitioners have claimed was denied to them after the petition was filed.
Despite a circular by Director Social Welfare that stated untrained teachers appointed after July 1993 would not have their services approved, the Court held that it was open to management to engage them during the normal period when they were pursuing the course, as it could not be said that the teachers were lacking bona fide or due diligence in pursuing the D Ed course.
Thus, the Petition was allowed accordingly.
[Paras 8 and 11]

The Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1977, Sections 2(20) and 3(1), applies only to recognized schools established by management other than the Government or local authorities, according to the Court.
Since schools for physically handicapped individuals fall within the control and jurisdiction of the social welfare department, they are not recognized by the Director of Education, Divisional, or State Board and are therefore not covered by the Act.
