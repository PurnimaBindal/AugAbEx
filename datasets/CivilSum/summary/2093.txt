Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Selection/Interviews - A rational criteria has been established to assess the merits of candidates, and 20% marks have been reserved for interviews.
The selection for Coaches is based on various qualities such as endurance, physical fitness, mental alertness, and quality of leadership.
Therefore, it cannot be considered excessive to reserve 20% marks in such cases.
[Paras 4 and 6]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 and 226 - Selection/Interview - The selection process has been challenged based on the allegation that selected candidates were awarded unduly high marks.
However, it is solely the function of the selection committee to award marks for interviews and the court cannot provide an alternative assessment.
[Para 4]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection/Eligibility - The selection of a candidate has been challenged on the basis of her allegedly invalid NIS Diploma and the claim that she did not pass her graduation while seeking admission in the NIS course.
However, the High Court does not have the jurisdiction to conduct such an inquiry under Article 226.
The selection committee relied on the certificates produced by the candidate before them, and therefore the selection process was not invalidated.
[Para 4]
