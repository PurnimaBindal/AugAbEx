The petitioner, who was enrolled as a Head Constable in the Border Security Force, has been retired on medical invalidation.
However, he claims that he was assessed as fit by a private doctor and an annual medical examination report classified him in a higher medical category, entitling him to continue in service.
The Medical Board declared the petitioner unfit for service and placed him in a lower medical category, and he was subsequently retired on medical invalidation with pensionary benefits.
The issue was whether the petitioner was denied the opportunity to make a representation against his placement in the lower medical category and retirement on medical invalidation under Rule 25, and whether his subsequent upgradation in the medical category entitled him to be retained in service.
It was held that the report of the Medical Board would prevail over routine annual medical checkups, and since the petitioner was not in the process of making a representation, passing of the order a day before the stipulated period cannot be said to have resulted in denial of opportunity.
