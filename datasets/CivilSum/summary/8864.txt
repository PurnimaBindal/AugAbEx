Under Section 30 of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, M/s. SN Roy and Abha Roy filed a grievance that they were fulfilling the same job responsibilities as regular employees of the appellant company.
They sought absorption into the regular establishment.
However, in order to determine the eligibility for absorption of the contract labour, it must be established whether their job responsibilities were considered of a perennial nature.
Appropriate authorities must decide who qualifies for absorption based on the nature of their job and only after fulfilling the prescribed procedures of the Act.
If there are disputes regarding regularisation, the appropriate government must handle them.
