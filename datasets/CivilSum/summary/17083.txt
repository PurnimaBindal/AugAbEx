Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - A petitioner was twice assessed by two Medical Boards and found to have a disability of 20%, which entitled him to disability pension.
However, before disentitling the petitioner, respondent No. 3 failed to examine the petitioner, and instead unilaterally formulated an opinion that superseded those of the Medical Boards.
This action was deemed illegal and arbitrary, and the impugned order passed by respondent No. 3 was set aside.
Consequently, the petitioner was granted disability pension.
[Paras 6 and 7]
