Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Casual labourers - Method of regular recruitment - Petitioners working as carpenters and painters for approximately a decade - Challenged the process of regular recruitment after participating in it - Court held that petitioners cannot claim the right to be regularised without undergoing recruitment process - Some petitioners already appointed based on complaints of arbitrariness or discrimination - Petitioners are free to participate in future recruitment processes.
