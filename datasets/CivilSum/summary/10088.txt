Army Act, 1950, Sections 8 and 20 - Dismissal from Service - Competent Authority - A Dafedar Clerk (non-commissioned Officer) was found unfit to be retained in service and dismissed by Brigadier working as the Zonal Recruiting Officer, as per the Notification prescribing Zonal Recruiting Officer as the competent authority to exercise power of brigade or equivalent Commander.
The Brigadier was competent to pass the order of dismissal.
[Para 7]

Army Act, 1950, Section 20 - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 17 - Dismissal from Services - Show Cause Notice - A non-commissioned Officer of Army charged for corruption and accumulation of assets disproportionate to his known income.
A show-cause notice was issued spelling specific charges against him.
However, the appellant failed to explain that assets possessed by him were not disproportionate to his known source of income.
Therefore, the action dismissing the appellant from services was justified.
[Paras 11 and 12]
