Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo)(bb) - Contract of service - Non-renewal of contract of service - Project employee - Petitioner, appointed on a contractual basis for a fixed term which was extended from time to time, can no longer claim retention in service once the project and extended term expire.
There is no evidence to suggest that the appointment was made against a permanent post or that the project was still ongoing.
