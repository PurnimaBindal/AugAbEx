The petitioner, who passed the Ayurveda Acharya examination in 1981 from the Nikhil Bhartvarshiya Ayurved Vidyapeetham in Delhi, is eligible for admission to the MD (Ayurveda) Course as the institution was duly recognised by Punjabi University at the relevant time.
The fact that the institution was derecognised only in 1990 means that denying admission to students who did the Ayurveda Acharya course prior to the derecognition is not legally justified.
As a result, the University is directed to consider the petitioner for the next academic session's admission to the MD (Ayurveda) Course as per his merit in written examination/interview under the provisions of Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India.
