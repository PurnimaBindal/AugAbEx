Central Public Works Department Assistant Engineers (Central Engineering Service and Central Electrical Service) Group B (confirmation and seniority) Rules, 1979 Rule 4 - Constitution of India Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority/Confirmation - Rules that provide seniority based on the date of confirmation are questioned - Confirmations made unequal and discriminatory, and promotions given against temporary posts - Rule violative of the principle of equal opportunity and circumvents the court's judgment - Rule declared inoperative and void as it violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
