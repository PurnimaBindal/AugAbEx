Constitution of India, Article 366 - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rules 3(1)(o) and 9 - Pension - Gratuity - The appellant, who retired as Under Secretary in the Ministry of Home Affairs, Govt. of India, is entitled to pension and the entire amount of death-cum-retirement gratuity, but they have been withheld permanently due to serious irregularities found during an inquiry held with the consultation of UPSC.
The term 'pension' includes gratuity and therefore, withholding of gratuity is justified.
