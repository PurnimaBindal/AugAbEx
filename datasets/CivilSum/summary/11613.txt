"The Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16 and 226 deal with regularisation, qualifications and eligibility for appointment, and the appointment of petitioners without fulfilling eligibility qualifications as Workship Instructors.
The petitioners have no right to remain on the posts until regular and qualified persons are appointed as per the management's decision to replace them with fresh Apprentices under the Apprenticeship Act Scheme.
[Paras 7 to 10]"
