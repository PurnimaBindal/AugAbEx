The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Charge memo - Service - According to service rules, the charge memo should be served on the delinquent either in person or by registered post.
The petitioner failed to comply with the government's rules and had the charge memo pasted on the door of the delinquent's residence, which was deemed acceptable.
[Para 5]

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Disciplinary Proceedings - National Promotion - Sealed cover procedure - In a case where a co-employee was not punished for the same misconduct, directions were given to consider the promotion of the employee using a sealed cover procedure.
The matter was dispensed with when the respondent employee retired during the pendency of writ proceedings.
If the employee is found not guilty in the inquiry, they are entitled to national promotion with all consequential benefits.
[Paras 5 and 6]
