Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Pension - Widow of a soldier who died in 1950 claimed pension - Army Authorities refused on the basis that old records have been destroyed - However, some details of service of the deceased are acknowledged - The loss of records is not the petitioner's fault - The offer of Rs. 750/- as lump sum was insensitive - Petitioner is entitled to pension from 22.9.77 with arrears and interest @ 12% and costs Rs. 5000/- - Payment to be made within one month as the petitioner is over 100 years old.
