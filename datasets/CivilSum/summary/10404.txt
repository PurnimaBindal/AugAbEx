Constitution of J & K, Section 126 - Dismissal - Petitioner is challenging the recommendations of I.G.P.-Respondent No. 3 to Government for his dismissal - The Government hasn't taken any action of dismissal against the petitioner - Thus, it is held that writ petition challenging the recommendation simpliciter of respondent No. 3 is premature - The petitioner has no present grievance that allows him to approach the Court for its ventilation.
