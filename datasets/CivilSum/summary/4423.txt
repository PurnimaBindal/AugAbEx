According to Constitution of India, Article 226, Change of date of birth in the service book is necessary when date of birth of the employee recorded in the service book is changed and re-changed.
It is not clear who made the changes and under what authority.
The matter cannot be decided without affording due opportunity to the parties for adducing evidence, which falls within the jurisdiction of the civil Court.
Therefore, writ jurisdiction is not the proper forum.
[Paras 12, 13 and 14]  B.
In accordance with Constitution of India, Article 14 and 226, when the service record of an employee shows that the first entry of date of birth was changed and was re-changed again showing it as originally recorded, no question of change of date being vitiated on account of non-issuance of show cause notice arises.
Therefore, no writ can be issued on that ground.
