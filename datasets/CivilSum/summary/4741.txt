Article 14, 16 and 226 of the Indian Constitution detail the principles of natural justice in the event of termination from a job.
When a citizen's rights are violated, they have the right to be heard regardless of the type of legal action being taken against them.
A regular employee cannot be terminated without providing them with the chance to acquire the necessary qualifications that they lack.
Any falsified findings that lead to termination would be unacceptable and an employee cannot be forced to undergo more litigation when they have been treated unfairly.
