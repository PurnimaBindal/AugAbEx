The respondent was appointed as a Class IV employee through compassionate appointment after her husband, who was working in a Class III post, passed away.
While she initially accepted the appointment, the respondent later claimed that she was entitled to the pay scale of Rs. 950-1500, which was one step lower than the pay scale her husband received.
However, based on a decision made by the Apex Court in SLP(S) No. 778 of 1999 on March 22, 1999, "one step lower" refers to appointment on a lower post.
Therefore, the respondent would continue to work in her prior Class IV post.
