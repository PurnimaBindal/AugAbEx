Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 - Challenge to transfer order - Delay and laches - Petitioner challenged transfer order from Oct 2001 in 2003 - While waiting, petitioner submitted representations - Argument that petitioner only learned about true legal position after reading writ petitions in Oct 2002 - Court ruled that submitting non-statutory representations does not excuse delay in filing writ petition - If petitioner wants a writ, they must come to High Court within a reasonable time, regardless of their knowledge of the law - Appeal allowed.
