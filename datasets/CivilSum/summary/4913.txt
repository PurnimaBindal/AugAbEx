According to Sections 2(k) and 47 of the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, the definition of "Establishment" includes registered companies.
If the Legislature had intended to exclude all Incorporated Companies, a Statutory Corporation would have been specifically mentioned, just as a Government Company is specifically included.

According to Section 47 of the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, if an employee acquires a disability during their service, their termination of service due to their inability to perform their duties is improper.
They are entitled to be shifted to a suitable post with the same pay scale and service benefits, including back wages.
