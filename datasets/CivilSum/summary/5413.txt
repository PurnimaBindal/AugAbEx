According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India and the Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, Regulation 173, if a petitioner is found suffering from Schizophrenia and permanently unfit for any form of Military Service, they are not entitled to disability pension without any evidence that the decease in question was attributed to military service.
The petitioner did not produce any material in rebuttal showing such evidence.
