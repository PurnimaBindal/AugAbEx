Under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16, an application for compassionate appointment cannot be rejected solely because the family of the Government Servant has benefited from welfare schemes.
Also, benefits received by the family of the deceased should not prevent a compassionate appointment.
When considering a request for appointment on compassionate grounds, officials must make an objective assessment of the family's financial situation, taking into account all relevant factors, such as assets and liabilities, the presence of earners, size of the family, ages of children, and essential needs.
[Paras 7 & 8] B Union of India and its functionaries had given assurance that as soon as the appellant reached age 18, his case would be processed for compassionate appointment.
Although the appellant's case was being processed for three years, no appointment was granted.
The delay in the appointment was caused by the respondents, and the appellant cannot bear the consequences of the authorities' mistake.
