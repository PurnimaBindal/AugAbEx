Constitution of India, Article 226 - Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Schedule 4, Items No. 1(a), (b) (d), (f) and (g) - Unfair Labour Practice - Dismissal - Domestic Enquiry - A bus conductor was terminated for theft of the petitioner's funds, but he challenged the validity of the investigation.
The concurrent finding of facts was that negligence was proven but that he was not deceitful.
Reinstatement of the complainant with a continuation of employment and 50% of back wages was lawfully directed.
