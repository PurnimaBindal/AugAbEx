Minimum Wages Act, 1948, Section 20(2) - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 2 - Overtime - Entitlement to - A work charge employee who had been regularized after completing 10 years of service filed a claim petition under sub-section (2) of Section 20 of the Act for working over office hours.
The prescribed authority allowed the claim.
However, the petitioner contended that the provisions of the Act were not applicable to the respondent as they were being paid wages higher than the minimum wages fixed under the Act.
The court held that if an employee is being paid higher wages than the minimum wages fixed under the Act and their wages are fixed by contract, they cannot claim any benefits under the Act.
Therefore, the prescribed authority had no jurisdiction to entertain such a case, and the petition was allowed accordingly.
