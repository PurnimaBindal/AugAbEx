In accordance with Industrial Disputes Act, Section 33-C(2), a claim may be made to compute and recover the difference in salary between a Beldar and Road Roller Operator for a particular period.
The Labour Court determined that the workman was actually performing the duties of a Road Roller Operator and thus is entitled to the pay allotted to such a position.
[Paras 3 and 4]
Under Article 226 of the Indian Constitution, Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act is taken into account.
When determining whether a worker had served as a Road Roller Operator during a certain time period, the deciding factor is the Labour Court's supporting evidence.
If the Labour Court rules in the worker's favour, then it becomes a matter of calculating wages owed.
Consequently, if the Labour Court finds in favour of the worker, then the petition is dismissed.
