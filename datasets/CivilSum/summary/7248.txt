Constitution of India, Articles 14, 15 and 16 - Education - Residential preference in admissions to MBBS courses - Those applying for admission in two States cannot be automatically excluded from consideration on the ground that they have applied in the same category in the other State - A person may validly be a resident of two States and availing of a benefit can only be said when he is offered and accepts it - Exclusion solely on the basis of applying in two States will violate Articles 14 and 15 of the Constitution.
