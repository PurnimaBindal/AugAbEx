Section 6-N of U.P.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 pertains to the termination of services of workmen.
In a case where a worker was terminated on the grounds of being absent from duty without any enquiry, the Labour Court found the termination to be illegal and ordered reinstatement.
The award of the Labour Court was upheld, and no interference was called for.
