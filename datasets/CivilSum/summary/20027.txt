The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 300-A, Railway Establishment Manual's Rules 2311 and 2318, and Manual of Railway Pension Rules' Para 801 discuss Family Pension and the entitlements of substitutes.
Rule 2318 ensures that substitutes receive all the rights temporarily employed railway workers get after six months of service.
The dependents of a deceased substitute who acquired a temporary service status cannot be denied family pension entitlements under Rule 2311(3)(b).
As a result, the Tribunal's order was overturned and the petition was granted.
