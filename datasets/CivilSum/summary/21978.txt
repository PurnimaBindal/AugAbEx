Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, Section 13-A - Interpretation of standing order - Validity of standing order - Clause 15(x) of the Certified Standing Order of 1953 of Calcutta Electric Supply Corpn Ltd. states that taking intoxicants or noxious drugs while on duty or being under their influence while on duty, is considered misconduct and can lead to dismissal.
Standing Order 15(b) provides for a proper enquiry and opportunity for the workman to defend themselves.
Despite the workman's reinstatement by the Labour Tribunal, their concern of being victimised cannot be considered unfounded.
Any challenge to the interpretation of such standing orders falls under the jurisdiction of Section 13-A of the Act.
