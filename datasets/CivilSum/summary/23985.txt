Constitution of India, Article 136 - Reinstatement - Date of Birth - Retirement - The appellant was awarded reinstatement on 251999, however, there was a dispute regarding his alleged illegal termination of service and the non-implementation of the award due to a disagreement on his retirement date.
The appellant claims his correct date of birth to be 611943 and that he would retire only in the year 2003.
The controversy about his date of birth was raised in a writ petition but was not considered by the High Court in its impugned judgment.
The High Court's judgment was overturned and the matter was remanded back to the High Court to determine whether the appellant retired on 611996 or at a later date.
