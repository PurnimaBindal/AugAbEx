Constitution of India, Article 16 - Seniority - Medical Social Workers in Hospital - The departmental selection committee selected Respondent No. 4 for the post of Medico Social Service Officer, despite the fact that the appellant was senior.
Respondent No. 4 was appointed on an ad hoc basis initially and then on a permanent basis.
However, this selection was not based on seniority and therefore, the order by which Respondent No. 4 was appointed on a regular basis is unacceptable.
The Tribunal's decision to uphold the hospital's action is set aside.
