Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 11A - Grave misconduct - Absence from duty - Quantum of punishment - Long unauthorised absence from duty of public utility services for three years despite notice and opportunities, cannot be taken lightly and must be considered serious grave misconduct, especially if there is a history of incorrigibility and habitual offense - Once any misconduct is proven, the punishment quantum is not up for debate in Court.
