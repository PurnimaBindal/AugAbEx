The non-medical technical staff and class IV staff are entitled to the scale of pay set by the government for their respective cadres.
The employees of the universities are not considered as government servants and are governed by their own statutes and regulations under respective enactments.
The High Court's decision to extend the benefit of UGC pay scales to non-teaching medial staff and class IV government employees was deemed unjustified and set aside.
The respondents could not receive a higher scale of pay than what is prescribed for their post.
