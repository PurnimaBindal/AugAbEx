Under Article 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India, Sections 2(k) and 12(3) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, promotion during an industrial dispute is prohibited, except during a settlement period agreed to under Section 12(3) of the Act, though such agreements prohibit workers from raising individual grievances as to promotion.
An industrial dispute cannot be raised for higher wages/promotion in such situations.
However, workmen working in higher cadres pursuant to a common Award passed by the Industrial Tribunal and holding those higher posts for nearly 7-1/2 years cannot be reverted.
