Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Natural justice - Ex-parte proceedings - The punishing authority must afford an opportunity of hearing and consider any submissions made before passing an order.
The principles of natural justice require the delinquent to be supplied with copies of any written arguments filed by the Presenting Officer.
Failure to do so results in a denial of reasonable opportunity and vitiates the punishment order.
Additionally, a request for personal hearing should be granted and ignored only after due consideration.
