Constitution of India, Articles 146(2), 311(2) - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(j) - Supreme Court Officers and Servants (Condition of Service and Conduct) Rules 1961, Rule 9 - Compulsory Retirement - A Joint Registrar within the Supreme Court was retired from his post, on the recommendation of a Judges Committee - The appellant was given feedback to improve his performance in the preceding year - The appellant argued that his case could not be reviewed as he was over 55 years of age - However, FR 56 can be enforced on Supreme Court employees according to Rule 9 - The cases of officers who have attained the rank of Joint Registrar are to be reviewed annually, as per Chief Justice policy - The order of compulsory retirement was based on the available facts and was not considered to be punitive or motivated by external factors - Provisions of Article 311(2) or 146(2) do not apply as the Appellant already qualifies for a full pension - The appeal is dismissed accordingly.
