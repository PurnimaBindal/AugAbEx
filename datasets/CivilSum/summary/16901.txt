Constitution of India 1950, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - One out of 17 posts was reserved for ex-servicemen - Petitioner placed in General Category and on waiting list - Claiming employment under reserved seat not possible as petitioner did not provide necessary certificate or apply in that category - The petitioner did not attach necessary documents with the application form, nor applied under the Freedom Fighters category.
Therefore, the petitioner's case has no standing and the petition has been dismissed.
