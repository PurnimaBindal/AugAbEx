Constitution of India, Articles 19(1)(9) and 226 - Police protection - An appellant may legally engage workmen of their choice for carrying on their business and it is not within the authority of the respondent union to prevent the appellant from doing so in the absence of any legal prohibition or agreement - The law enforcing agencies like police personnel have a duty to give protection to a citizen carrying on lawful trade or business - Protecting fundamental rights is not only limited to workers but extends to employers as well - The respondents' claim based on a practice cannot be justified.
