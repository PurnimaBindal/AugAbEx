According to Punjab Cooperative Societies Act, 1961, Section 26 and Punjab State Cooperative Banks' Staff (Common Cadre) Rules, 1970-71, Rule 14, Managing Director appointments are regulated by the Rules and are not influenced by Board of Directors' existence.
Managing Director is part of the Common Cadre and remains functional, even if the Board or the Administrative Committee is not present.

Federation of employees' office bearers are allowed to plead for their members' cause under Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226.
However, they are not authorized to tarnish the institution's image by making unacceptable allegations against the officers or to indulge in mud-slinging.
They do not have the permission to make irresponsible and reckless allegations.
