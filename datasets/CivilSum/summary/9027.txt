The Education Code of the Kendriya Vidyalaya Sansthan, Article 81(b) violates Article 311(2) - The power given to Commissioner under the education code to dispense with regular enquiry on account of serious embarrassment to girl students and their parents is not a permissible ground to dispense with an enquiry.
[Para 31]

Article 81(b) of the Education Code of the Kendriya Vidyalaya Sansthan gives the Commissioner the power to hold a departmental enquiry against an employee, which is not valid.
[Paras 30 and 35]

The word "reasonable" modifies the word "practicable," but it does not make it analogous with "expedient," as explained in the Education Code of the Kendriya Vidyalaya Sansthan, Article 81(b).
[Para 29]

The Commissioner's power to dispense with a regular enquiry on the ground of serious embarrassment to girl students and their parents, as provided in the Education Code of the Kendriya Vidyalaya Sansthan, Article 81(b), is not valid when there is no complaint from the girls or their parents that a regular enquiry would cause embarrassment.
The order of the Commissioner dispensing with the enquiry is set aside and the dismissal of the teacher is also set aside.
[Para 20]

The Education Code of the Kendriya Vidyalaya Sansthan, Article 81(b) provides power to the Commissioner to dispense with a regular enquiry, but the order to dispense with the enquiry should have been passed by the Commissioner himself and not by an office note.
The removal order is set aside.
[Para 12 and 13]

The Education Code of the Kendriya Vidyalaya Sansthan, Article 81(b) gives the authority the power to dispense with regular enquiry by specifying reasons in writing that it is not practicable to hold the enquiry.
The court cannot examine the adequacy or sufficiency of reasons, but it can examine that the reasons must clearly and unambiguously spell out that it would not be reasonably practicable to hold the enquiry.
