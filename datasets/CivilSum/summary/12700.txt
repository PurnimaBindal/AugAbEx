Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Refixation of pay - Proficiency step up benefits - Annual Confidential Report - Adverse remarks - In 1984, the petitioner crossed the efficiency bar, but did not receive proficiency step up benefits due to adverse entries in ACRs in 1987-88 and 1993-94, which were never communicated to the petitioner.
