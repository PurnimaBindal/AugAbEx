Delhi School Education Rules, Rule 96 - Selection of Teachers by Managing Committee of School as the most meritorious candidate at No. 1 - Appointed without an appointment letter - Complaints against Selection Committee to Director Education - Resolution to suspend its recommendations without approval of Director - After two days petitioner is requested to wait for clarification from the Directorate of Education - A complaint on mere allegations cannot negate the selection process - Once the recommendations are accepted the selection is final - Appointments must adhere to the law - The management is ordered to proceed to make appointments.
