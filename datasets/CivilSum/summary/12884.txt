Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II - Family Pension Scheme, 1964, Rule 4 - Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Claim of family pension - Deceased Government employee was regularized on 7.10.1980 with a condition to report for duty after being examined by CMO and subsequently joined duty on 8101980, but died on 4.2.1981 - Family pension was denied since regular service was less than one year, but Note below Rule 4 allows for grant of family pension even before one year continuous service if the deceased employee was fit as declared by the appropriate Medical Authority - The official work carried out by the respondents is presumed to be correct as the medical examination was a condition of appointment on a regular basis and the principles laid out in Ram Kali Devi's case apply to this case - The court gave direction to pay retirement benefits, including family pension, within 3 months.
