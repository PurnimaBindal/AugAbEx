Constitution of India, Article 226 - West Bengal Service (Revision of Pay and Allowance) Rules, 1990 - Pay parity - In the case of wireless helpers claiming pay parity with constables, although their case was referred to the Pay Commission, no report was provided to the court to demonstrate whether the issue was addressed or not.
The Tribunal failed to consider that no reason was given by the Home Secretary on why the recommendations of the Police Commissioner were not accepted.
The finding of the Tribunal and order of the Home Secretary rejecting the petitioners' claim are nullified, and the matter of determining the pay scale should be left to the appropriate authority with experience and expertise on the subject.
The State Government is directed to establish an expert panel to determine the equation of pay scales for wireless helpers and constables.
