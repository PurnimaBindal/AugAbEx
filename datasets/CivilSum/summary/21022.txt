Requisitioning and Acquisition of Immovable Property Act, 1952 (as amended by Act 68 of 1984) - Solatium and interest - The District Court is not authorized to modify the final award and provide solatium and interest using the authority given by Sections 151 and 152 of the Code of Civil Procedure.
This is due to the court's inherent lack of jurisdiction, and also because there is no provision for granting interest and solatium in the Requisitioning Act.
