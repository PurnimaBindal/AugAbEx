Constitution of India, Article 226 - Out of turn promotion - Sports quota recruit - Petitioner requests advantage under policy guidelines, as his team earned silver medal in National Championship.
However, according to policy, the petitioner not only needed to win a bronze medal but also have an individual performance supported by a coach's recommendation.
The court concludes that the petitioner cannot benefit from the promotion policy since there was no individual performance backed up by a coach's recommendation.
