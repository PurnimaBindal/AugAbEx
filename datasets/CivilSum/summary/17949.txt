Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 13.9 List-D - Promotion/Good reports/Average reports - Despite being on List-D, the petitioner was not promoted to ASI due to a supposed unsatisfactory service record - However, the petitioner eventually obtained promotion using that same service record - It is unfair to deny someone promotion solely based on their reports being less than satisfactory or average - The petitioner should have been promoted alongside their junior officers.
