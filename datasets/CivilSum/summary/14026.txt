Constitution of India, Article 226 - Selection - Necessary parties - The Court cannot pass any adverse ruling on selected candidates without hearing them, as they may be adversely affected if the petitioner's plea is accepted.
[Para 6]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - Selection - Admission cannot be granted to a petitioner for a professional course such as fashion designing at a belated stage, after the first year of the course has already been completed and exams have been taken.
[Paras 5 & 6]
