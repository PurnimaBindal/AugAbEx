Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education/Gold Medal - The University changed the criterion for awarding Gold Medal to Post Graduate students based on overall grade in both the years of study.
This decision was taken at the end of the academic career of students without giving them proper notice.
Although the court will not interfere in the decision of the University, the decision should not be applied to the batch of students of that academic session.
