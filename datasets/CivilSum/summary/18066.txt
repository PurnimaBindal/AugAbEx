Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226/227 - Ad hoc employee/Termination/Last come first go/Discrimination - The petitioner, who was appointed as a chowkidar on a 89-day basis and had worked from August 7, 1985 to November 16, 1988, was terminated.
Meanwhile, juniors were retained.
The court directed that the petitioner's case be considered for taking him back.
