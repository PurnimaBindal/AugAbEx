Constitution of India, Article 309 - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 416 - Civil Service Regulations, Regulation 351(A) - Gratuity - Deduction of amount from pension for loss caused by petitioner due to negligence - Deduction permissible even after retirement - Use of the term `terminated' under Section 4(1) includes termination by superannuation - Regulation 351-A allows deduction from pension - Petitioner given sufficient opportunity of hearing - No illegality or impropriety in deduction of 10% from pension.
