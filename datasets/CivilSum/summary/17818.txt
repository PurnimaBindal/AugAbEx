Constitution of India, Article 311(1)(b) - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1638 - Dismissal from service/Dispensing with the enquiry/Enquiry not reasonably practicable - The invoking of Article 311(1)(b) was made on the grounds of registered criminal cases against the official and subsequently withdrawn.
However, compliance with Rule 1638 was mandatory.
As such, the SP was obliged to refer the case to the District Magistrate for a conclusion on whether an enquiry should be held or not.
It was not automatic that Article 311(1)(b) would empower the Punishing Authority to not follow the statutory provisions of Rule 1638 for the defense's right to an opportunity.
