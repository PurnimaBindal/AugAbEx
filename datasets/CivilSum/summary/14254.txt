Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Whether an original driving licence was fake or not is a factual question - It is not permitted to raise such questions in appeal without a specific plea.
[Para 5]

Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4 - Liability of Insurance Company for Compensation - If a driver has a renewed driving licence, the owner may not know if the original driving licence was fake or not - Unless it is proven that the owner knew about the invalidity of the driving licence, the owner cannot be said to have knowingly allowed the driver to drive without a valid licence - The Insurance Company must pay compensation as there was no breach of policy.
[Paras 7 and 8]
