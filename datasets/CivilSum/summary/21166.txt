Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Compassionate appointment - Minor dependents - The purpose of the scheme is to offer financial aid to the family of a deceased Govt. employee - Delayed compassionate appointment requests are invalid - Authorities who create the rules, regulations or administrative instructions must ensure they comply with Articles 14 and 21 - Compassionate appointment cannot be claimed as a right, nor can it be reserved for minor dependents who attain majority.
