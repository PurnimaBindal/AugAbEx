Article 311 of the Constitution of India pertains to criminal trial and disciplinary inquiry in cases of suspension.
Employees cannot stay disciplinary proceedings even if they are suspended pending criminal trials without a court order.
This led to a case where an employee had his suspension revoked but had to undergo departmental proceedings due to an incident of misappropriation that happened thirteen years prior.
The employee filed a case to quash the disciplinary proceedings, which was successful.
[Paras 7 and 8]

Article 311 of the Constitution of India also speaks on suspension in cases of criminal trial and disciplinary inquiry.
It is possible to hold a disciplinary inquiry on the same charges even if the employee is facing trial in a criminal case, provided that each case is reviewed priorly to decide whether a disciplinary inquiry needs to be delayed until the verdict of the criminal court- provided that charges are the same and similar.
[Para 8]
