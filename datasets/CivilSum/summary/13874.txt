Industrial Disputes Act.
1947, Sections 33A, 33(2)(b) - Termination - The approval of an application for termination can be rejected due to technical reasons and it will have an effect on the reference.
The termination can be upheld if the enquiry is fair and proper.
If the workmen's application under Section 33A is rejected, they cannot claim reinstatement based on the rejection of the management's application under Section 33(2)(b).
[Para 20]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 33A, 33(2)(b) - Suspension pending enquiry - The term 'suspension pending enquiry' does not prohibit suspension orders before a charge-sheet is issued, especially in cases of serious incidents.
Thus, the suspension in question was not illegal.
If the suspension ultimately leads to termination, it is merged into the order of dismissal and no longer exists.
