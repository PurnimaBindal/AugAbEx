According to Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the term 'workman' does not include a trade trainee, therefore the petitioner cannot be considered a 'workman'.
As a result, the stipend paid to him cannot be considered as 'wages', and the order for his disengagement upon termination of the training contract is not illegal.
[Paras 7, 9, and 11]
