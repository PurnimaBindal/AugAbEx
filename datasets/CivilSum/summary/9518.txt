Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Ad hoc appointment - Selection for regular appointment - To be eligible for appointments to the posts of drivers in police department, candidates must follow the procedure prescribed by the recruitment rules for adjudging eligibility, fitness, and competitive merit - Appointees who serve merely as a stop-gap arrangement during elections are not considered members of the service and cannot claim a better right to regularly selected persons - Ad hoc appointees are required to make way for regular recruits.
[Para 4] B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Necessary parties - Selection - A petition filed to challenge the selection of regularly appointed persons to the regular posts without impleading them as parties is not valid - No order shall be passed at the back of such parties - The petition is liable to be dismissed.
