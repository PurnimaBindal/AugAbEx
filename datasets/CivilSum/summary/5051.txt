Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Sections 25-F, 25-B - Retrenchment compensation to daily wager - Calculation of continuous service period - days where employee did not receive pay due to holidays, contractual agreement, or statutory provisions should not be included in computing the continuous service period for the purpose of calculating retrenchment compensation under Section 25-F read with Section 25-B of the Industrial Disputes Act.
