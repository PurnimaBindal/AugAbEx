Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provisions) Act, 1985, Sections 16, 17 and 18 - Transfer of employees of sick company to transferee company - The approval by BIFR to sell four unviable units does not result in the transfer of employees of the sick company to the transferee company.
This approval cannot be considered as approval of the final scheme according to Section 18 - no scheme has been sanctioned and no Draft Scheme has been published.
Since the admitted corporation is still in operation and only one unit has been sold, the employees do not lose their status as employees of the sick company.
[Paras 16, 19, 21]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 23 - Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provisions) Act, 1985, Sections 16, 17 and 18 - Transfer of employees of sick company to transferee company - As per the mandatory requirement of the law, giving notice to employees and obtaining their option is necessary.
The impugned circular issued by the transferor company stating that employees of the sick company were transferred to the transferee company without obtaining their consent is arbitrary and violates Articles 14 and 23 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 23]
