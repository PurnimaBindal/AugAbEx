According to Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and the Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, if a settlement is made to pay bonus and has been upheld in earlier litigations, then the corporation is legally obliged to follow it.
[Para 7] B.
The commissioner held that the Municipal Corporation is not required to pay bonus under the Act of 1965 as it is not covered under it.
However, while passing the order, the objections filed were not taken into consideration and the Court opined that future benefits cannot be withdrawn in violation of principles of natural justice.
The facts, circumstances and observations of the earlier litigation must be considered while deciding whether to withdraw benefits in the future.
