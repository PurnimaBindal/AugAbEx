As per the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 3, Item 6, an employee who has been ordered to be reinstated but without backwages by the Labour Court and the employer revises the order and receives an interim stay in their favour, the employee is entitled to receive backwages during the period of the stay.
This is because the employee was kept out of service due to the stay order.
