Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 14 - Casual Labour - `Trained Pool Scheme' of Posts and Telegraph Department - The RTP employees are not on par with the casual mazdoors after attaining temporary status.
RTPs were given the same training as regular employees and were assured to be accommodated in future vacancies as regular employees in the manner set out in the Scheme.
Thus, they are better off than casual labourers and cannot be compared and cannot claim additional benefits on that basis.
