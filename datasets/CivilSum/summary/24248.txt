Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Seniority - Inter se seniority between Assistants employed in the office of Advocate General, Bihar State by way of test conducted by the AG and those appointed by way of general competition - According to the High Court and Supreme Court, those appointed from other sources are entitled to seniority fixation based on the principles laid down in the Bihar Govt. Circular dt. 30.3.1981, without any discrimination based on source of recruitment.
