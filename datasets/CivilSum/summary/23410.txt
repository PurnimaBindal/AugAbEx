According to Article 162 of the Constitution of India, Sections 191 and 192 of the Army Act, 1950, and Rule 16-A of the Army Rules, 1954, compulsory retirement can be enforced for Lt.
Generals in staff positions between the ages of 56 and 58.
The petitioner retired at the age of 57, and as such, their retirement is not illegal.
