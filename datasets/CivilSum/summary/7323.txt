Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion - Equity - Petitioners who held Class III positions were promoted to Clerks due to unavailability of clerks and a State Government embargo on new recruitment promoted posts.
Such promotions violating Constitution's Articles 14 and 16 - Court order to recover the remuneration from the promoted post was deemed appropriate - No favourable consideration possible in the petitioners' interest.
