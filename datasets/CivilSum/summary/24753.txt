Constitution of India, Article 32 - Writs - A petitioner who was offered a job in accordance with an earlier petition to rehabilitate him but did not report for duty - The petitioner had previously run a small business and was hesitant to make a decision between the two options - A direction was given that if the offered job was available, it should remain open until a specified date for the petitioner to join, and if the position was not available, the petitioner should be provided financial assistance of Rs. 20,000/- to start up his business.
