In accordance with the Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 3(4) and the Army Pension Regulations, 1961, a disability pension is applicable to certain cases of discharge from service.
Regarding a claim made by a petitioner who suffered from epilepsy during service, it was rejected due to the disease being deemed unrelated to service.
It was determined that epilepsy cannot be considered a valid disease for the purpose of receiving disability pension.
Therefore, the petitioner is not eligible to claim disability pension.
