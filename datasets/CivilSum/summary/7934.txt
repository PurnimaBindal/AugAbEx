The offence under Section 24 of the Border Security Force Act, 1968 cannot be dealt with summarily, and must follow the procedure provided in the Act.
The petitioner's prayer to quash the charge sheet and constitution of G.S.F.C.
trial was dismissed.
As the petitioner participated in GSFC without protest and did not file a post-confirmation petition, a writ is not a maintainable remedy.

According to Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India, a special law should be dealt with and things be done in the manner provided in the Act.
As there is an equally efficacious remedy available and the bar is mandatory, a writ is not maintainable.
