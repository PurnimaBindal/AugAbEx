Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. 1 Part I, Rule 4.4 - Advance increments on promotion - Pay fixation on post with higher responsibilities - Must be in the next higher time scale and higher than juniors in lower-cadre with higher scale due to regional distribution - Promotion to Central Head Teacher post from JBT and Head Teachers - If seniors cannot be promoted to Head Teacher but to Central Head Teacher, cannot be placed in lower pay/scale than JBT Teachers who were juniors - Senior cannot receive lower scale/pay than juniors in same cadre - Well settled principle in service jurisprudence.
