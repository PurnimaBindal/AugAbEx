Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 7-A(1) and 7(1) - Appeal - Applicability of the Act - If an order regarding the applicability of the Act to an establishment has not been appealed under Section 7(1), a consequent order determining the amount due against the establishment cannot be upheld as an appeal against the earlier order, which settles the establishment's coverage.
