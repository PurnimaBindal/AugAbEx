Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Regularisation - Back-door entry - The petitioner was appointed without undergoing the selection process - The petitioner resorted to back-door entry - The petitioner's entry is illegal and unacceptable - Regularisation cannot legalise the unlawfulness - Only irregularity can be regularised by regularisation - The petitioner cannot claim the right to regularisation of their service.
