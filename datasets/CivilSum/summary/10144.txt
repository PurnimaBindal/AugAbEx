Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation - The instruction of regularisation is applicable to individuals who were in service on 31.3.93 and had completed their five years of service on or before 31.3.93 - The petitioners only worked for 9-10 days in March 1993 and cannot be considered to be in service on 31.3.93 - Respondents 3 & 4 who were regularised met the criteria set out in the instructions - The action of respondents in not regularising the petitioners is not in violation of instructions and is not discriminatory.
