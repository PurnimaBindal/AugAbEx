Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rules 612, 421 and 4.23 - Pension/Qualifying Service/Break in service - Petitioner's ad hoc service in another department is not included in his qualifying ten years of service for pension - Rule 4.23 prohibits condoning breaks in service less than five years, therefore he cannot count that period towards qualifying service - Where rules specifically deny a benefit, it cannot be granted in equity or general law.
