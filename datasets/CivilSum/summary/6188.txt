Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, sections 2(oo) and 25(f) dictate that retrenchment compensation must be paid.
Simply claiming that an individual is medically unfit is not sufficient to terminate employment without offering retrenchment compensation.
Furthermore, being diabetic does not make a person unfit; evidence of continuous ill health is necessary.
As a result, the worker is entitled to full back wages and reinstatement.
