The cancellation of a compassionate appointment is upheld due to the appointment being in contravention of the rules and without approval of the competent authority.
The petitioner's appointment was made ignoring advice and with mala fide intentions, therefore the approach of the respondents cannot be considered arbitrary or unreasonable.

The petitioner's appointment obtained through manipulation for age relaxation to apply for a government job cannot be granted relief under the equitable and extraordinary jurisdiction of the court due to the dishonest conduct.
