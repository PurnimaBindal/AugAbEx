Constitution of India, Articles 14, 311 and 226 - Departmental Enquiry/Prejudice/Natural Justice - Conductor - Passengers' statements not recorded - Conductor denied chance to cross-examine them - Inspection report relied upon without recorded statements of inspectors in support - Such report deemed unreliable - Enquiry violated Rules and principles of natural justice - Non-observance itself a prejudice - Prejudice established without further proof - AIR 1981 SC 136 SL Kapur v Jagmohan cited.
