Compliance with the provisions of Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - is mandatory.
Non-compliance of these provisions makes the removal from service void.
Making an offer of reinstatement subject to surrender of back wages is against the law and unfair.
[Paras 3 and 4]
Workmen are entitled to reinstatement with full back wages where their termination is found to be illegal.
Forcing a workman to relinquish back wages in exchange for reinstatement is an example of unfair labor practice.
[Para 4]
Despite a 5-month delay in giving a demand notice, an illiterate workman is entitled to full back wages upon reinstatement.
The delay is not belated.
