According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India regarding promotions, the non-communication of punishment of censure and fine cannot be denied by respondents 1 to 3.
Adverse remarks in ACR also cannot be relied upon to deny promotion unless they are communicated to the person concerned.
The promotion of respondents 4 to 10 juniors to the petitioner has been challenged, and the respondents are directed to treat the petitioner as promoted from the date that respondents 4 to 10 were promoted.
