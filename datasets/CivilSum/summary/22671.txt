The Indian Administrative Service (Regulation of Seniority) Rules and All India Services Act regulate the seniority, year of allotment, and weightage given to promotee and direct recruit officers in the civil service.
The weightage is computed from the year of appointment and the service rendered up to 12 years in the State Civil Service as Deputy Collector or equivalent posts is given a weightage of 4 years.
In assigning year of allotment, fractions are ignored and seniority is variable by operation of law.
The proviso in the IAS (Regulation of Seniority) Rules limits the assignment of year of allotment and is consistent with Section 3(1A) of the All India Services Act.
The consultation process required between parties involved is to render consultation meaningful and need not be repeated before bringing the proviso as part of the First Amendment Rules.
