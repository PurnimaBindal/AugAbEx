Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - Tuition fee - Writ petition - Maintainability - A petitioner disputed an educational institution's decision to increase tuition fees.
The court held that writ petition was maintainable as the educational institution provided a public utility service directly linked to educating children.
[Para 16]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - West Bengal Societies Registration Act, 1961 - Delhi School Education Act, 1973 - Education - Tuition fee - A circular prescribes an increase in tuition fees.
A petitioner argues that the circular is arbitrary and in breach of natural justice.
However, the comparative fee of similar institutions shows that the fee specified in the circular is neither exorbitant nor arbitrary.
In fact, many other institutes charge higher fees the same as or above the fee stipulated in the circular.
Consequently, no interference is warranted.
[Paras 19, 20, 23 and 24]
