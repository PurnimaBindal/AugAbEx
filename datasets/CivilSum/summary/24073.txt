Constitution of India, Articles 14, 136 - Dismissal - Discrimination - High Court overturned order of dismissal for respondent - Other employees facing same charges for same incident only received stoppage of increment, but High Court found discrimination in this separate treatment - High Court gave similar punishment to respondent with added direction of 50% backwages - Court held that separate treatment would be discriminatory, and backwages are declined based on charges - Petition disposed of accordingly.
