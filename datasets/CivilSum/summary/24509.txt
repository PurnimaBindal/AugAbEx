Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Employees' State Insurance Act, 1947, Sections 40 and 41 - Contribution to ESI - Employers must provide adequate medical facilities - Recovery of employer and employee contribution was stayed during the writ petition if adequate medical allowance and facility was provided by the employer - While an interim order cannot delay Act implementation, ESI Corporation should not extract contributions for periods when no medical assistance was provided and the employer already was providing it - An employer acting in good faith under court orders cannot be burdened twice.
