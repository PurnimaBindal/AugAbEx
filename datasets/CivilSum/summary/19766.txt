The petitioner, a member of H.C.M.S.II, requested a 'No Objection Certificate' from the Government to attend a Post Graduate Course at a Medical College.
The request was declined because the petitioner had not completed the required three years of rural service and five years of regular service in HCMS-II.
However, the petitioner was admitted to one of the general seats after completing more than three years of rural service, proving the objection that he had not completed three years of rural service to be invalid.
The petitioner is eligible for a 'No Objection Certificate' for one of the seats reserved for HCMS-II members.
Article 226 of the Constitution of India was referenced.
[Para 4] B.
The petitioner, when seeking a required certificate was a regular member of H.C.M.S.II, having completed more than six years of service.
The expression "regular service" was defined by Hon'ble Mr. Justice MM Punchhi in his 1987 judgment in C.W.P.
No. 201 of 1987.
It is not disputed that this case is covered by the judgment.
Relevant articles of the Constitution of India are 16 and 226.
