Constitution of India, Article 136 and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(j) were cited in a case where a housing society was formed to provide personal services to apartment owners, and a Tribunal ruled that the society and its employees were an industry and workmen, respectively.
However, it was held that not all services rendered fell within the definition of workmen, and the society was not an industry.
As a result, the award was set aside and the appeal was allowed.
