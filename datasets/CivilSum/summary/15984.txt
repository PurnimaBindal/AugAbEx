According to the Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(b), Punjab Police Rules 1934, Rules 169 and 1624, and Police Act 1861, Section 7, the competent authority must provide reasons based on relevant material for dispensing with the enquiry, and unless it is shown that witnesses were not ready to come forward, enquiry cannot be dispensed with.
In this case, the competent authority did not apply its mind independently and relied on the report of a subordinate officer without verifying its correctness.
Furthermore, there was no material available to support the allegation that the delinquent was involved with terrorists.
Dispensing with an enquiry cannot be done lightly or arbitrarily, or for ulterior motives or to avoid enquiry.
Thus, the order of termination has been quashed with liberty to proceed in accordance with the law.
