Civil Services Regulations, Regulation 52 - Public Servant - Transfer - The employer holds the exclusive decision-making power for transferring a public servant from their current posting, including when, where, and at what time - A holder of a transferable post within the government has no guaranteed right to stay at any one location - Courts should not intervene with transfer orders made for public interest and administrative reasons, unless the orders violate a mandatory statutory rule or are based on malicious intent.
