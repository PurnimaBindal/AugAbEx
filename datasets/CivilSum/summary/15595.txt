Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Section 126 - J&K Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, Rule 33(1) - Unauthorised absence - Discharge - The petitioner was discharged from service due to unauthorised absence and taking a foreign assignment without permission.
The petitioner cannot claim lack of opportunity to show cause for the loss of appointment when he was not available at the address left with the authorities.
The impugned order of discharge from service is not illegal.
[Para 9]
