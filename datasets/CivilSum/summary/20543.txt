Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission to Medical Courses/MBBS - All India Quota - Reservation - 3rd counselling is not advisable - Reservation of percentage of seats for All India Students is not absolute - The goal was to attain only a general equality, not exact mathematical precision - If after 2nd counselling no adjustments are made, the seats can be offered to states or may remain vacant but 3rd counselling should not be allowed as it would cause an endless process that is not advisable for finalising admissions and mid-term admissions to medical courses.
