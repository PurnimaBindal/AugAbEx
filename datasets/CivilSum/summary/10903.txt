According to Section 3(1) of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, an accident must arise out of and in the course of employment to qualify for compensation.
Despite receiving an extra Rs. 5 for reaching work early to melt tar coal, the deceased was hit by a truck while on his way to work and passed away.
As he was not en route to his place of duty at the time of the accident, his death was not a result of a work-related accident and, as such, was not eligible for compensation under the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923.
Any claim for compensation must be made under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 against the tort feasor.
