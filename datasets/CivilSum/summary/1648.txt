Constitution of India, Article 311 - Double jeopardy/Criminal proceedings - A bus conductor allowed passengers to travel without tickets, leading to both departmental proceedings and criminal prosecution against him - He sought to delay the departmental proceedings until the criminal case was resolved, but this was considered an insufficient reason - The case's complexity will determine whether or not the departmental enquiry can be postponed due to the pending criminal case on the same charges.
