The Co-operative Bank must adhere to the reservation policy and maintain a 50% quota for vacancies in both the Apex Society and member societies, as well as the District Banks.
This includes the District Banks as they fall under the definition of "member society."
This is in accordance with the Co-operative Societies Act, 1969 (Kerala), Sections 80(4) and 187, and is further supported by Article 16 of the Constitution of India.
[Paras 10, 11 & 21]
