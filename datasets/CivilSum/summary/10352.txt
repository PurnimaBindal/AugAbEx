According to the Kerala Education Act, 1959, Section 36 and the Kerala Education Rules, 1959, Chapter XIV-A, Rule 45, regarding the appointment of a Headmistress, the petitioner was qualified at the time the vacancy occurred, whereas the third respondent only became qualified upon reaching the age of 50.
It has been established by the Full Bench that the five years of service requirement for graduate teachers as per Rule 45 is relevant.
Hence, respondents 1, 2, and 4 are instructed to consider the parties' rights based on the principles set forth by the Full Bench.
