Fundamental Rules, Rules 17, 24, 26 and 56 - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rules 5(2), 14, 33, 34 and 35 - Civil Service Regulations, Articles 151 to 154 - Grant of increment - An annual increment will not be granted to a Government servant who retires on the last working day of the preceding month and whose annual increment falls due on the first of the succeeding month for the purpose of pension and gratuity.
[Paras 9, 46, 51, 53 and 56] B Fundamental Rules, Rules 17, 24, 26 and 56 - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rules 5(2), 14, 33, 34 and 35 - Civil Service Regulations, Articles 151 to 154 - Revised rate of DA - A retired Government servant is not entitled for revised rate of DA unless he is re-employed.
[Para 57] C Fundamental Rules, Rules 17, 24, 26 and 56 - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rules 5(2), 14, 33, 34 and 35 - Civil Service Regulations, Articles 151 to 154 - Gratuity - Pension is invisible accumulated savings of a Government servant while in service, and is fixed taking into consideration the emoluments paid to the Government servant.
[Para 10] D. Fundamental Rules, Rules 17, 24, 26 and 56 - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rules 5(2), 14, 33, 34 and 35 - Civil Service Regulations, Articles 151 to 154 - Grant of Increments - Annual increments are given to officers with good conduct unless withheld as a measure of punishment.
