Article 226 of the Constitution of India - Writ jurisdiction - No prayer to quash charge sheet - Charge sheet cannot be quashed when there are no such pleadings made.
[Para 2]
Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India - Suspension - Justified if enquiry is ongoing - If proceedings not concluded within one year, petitioner entitled to subsistence allowance at the rate of 75% with arrears from the date one year completes - Calcutta Service Regulations, Regulations 50(c), (d) and 54.
[Paras 3 & 6]
Regulation 50(d) of Calcutta Service Regulations - Departmental enquiry - Ordinarily, enquiry proceedings should conclude within six months - However, the use of the word 'ordinarily' in the regulation implies that proceedings may continue after six months if necessary.
