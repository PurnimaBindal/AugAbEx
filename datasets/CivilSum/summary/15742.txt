The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, and 226, was invoked in a case regarding admission.
The petitioner had obtained a "Result-Later" status on their 10+2 Examination, but had later taken and passed the entrance test for a BTech.
course in Electronics, and had submitted a certificate proving their qualification.
Despite this, the petitioner's admission was cancelled due to not having passed the qualifying exam, an act which was later found to be unjustified as the petitioner had produced a certificate proving their qualification and provisionally admitted to the course.
