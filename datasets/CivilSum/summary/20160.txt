Constitution of India, Article 136 - Retirement - Promotion - Relief - The government declined promotion for the respondent, who was granted an extension of service for three months after retiring on 3091995.
This was due to an Office Memo dated 18.5.1977 which stated that a person should not be given extension of service for promotion to higher post if they are going to retire.
The Tribunal deemed the Office Memo to be administrative and inapplicable, and their decision was upheld.
However, since the respondent has continued to work on the promoted post, no interference will be made as they are due to retire soon.
