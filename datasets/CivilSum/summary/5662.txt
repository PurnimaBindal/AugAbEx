Constitution of India, Articles 16, 19 & 311(2) - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Disciplinary proceedings - Workman dismissed for theft of store items after a fair and proper domestic enquiry - Labour Court orders reinstatement without backwages - Both employer and workman challenge the order in High Court - Single Judge dismisses workman's claim and allows employer to quash order of reinstatement - Workman appeals Single Judge's finding on non-applicability of Section 11-A - Court holds that Labour Court can interfere under Section 11-A if punishment to workman is unjustified - Appeals dismissed.
