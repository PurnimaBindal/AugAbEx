In accordance with the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309, the selection process for existing vacancies will not be governed by the unamended Rules in operation before amendment.
Simply coming into existence of vacancies, advertising these vacancies, and being eligible under the unamended Rules does not create a vested right against the State for candidates.
The government is permitted to withdraw the selection process issued under the unamended Rules and hold selection as per amended Rules in vogue.
[Paras 12 and 13] B.
The creation of a new cadre with entirely different qualifications and the abolition of posts existing prior to amendment itself means that candidates who were eligible and had applied for the post under the unamended rules cannot be considered under the vacancies existing prior to amendment.
Notification of new posts for selection as per amended Rules does not violate any law.
