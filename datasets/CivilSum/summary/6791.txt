Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 300 - Pension - Qualifying service - Private service - Pension is available only if the qualifying service is rendered in Government Department - The petitioner was retrenched by his private transport company, then given fresh appointment in Haryana Roadways, and retired before completing 10 years qualifying service - He cannot claim private transport cooperative society service as qualifying service in the Government Department, even with relaxation.
[Paras 6 and 7]
Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 14 - Relaxation - Pension - The Government specifically rejected recommendations for relaxation of qualifying service condition for pension - There is no rule or instruction for relaxation - Writ of mandamus cannot be used to claim relaxation as a matter of right.
