Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 16.3 - Criminal and disciplinary proceedings - Dismissal from service on criminal charges - There is no legal restriction on two proceedings happening simultaneously - The outcome of criminal proceedings, if they occur after the disciplinary proceedings, will not affect punishment already awarded by the disciplinary authority - Both proceedings have different procedures and objectives.
[Para 11] B. Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.3 - Criminal and disciplinary proceedings - Dismissal of constable from service on the charges of misconduct absence from duty after committing of an offense of sexual abuse of girl which brought a bad name to the whole of police force - If the punishing authority has found the constable guilty of the charges, the absence of mentioning of incorrigible and grave misconduct does not affect the punishment of dismissal.
[Para 12] C. Punjab Police Rules, 1937, Rules 1624 - Disciplinary proceedings - Dismissal from service - Natural justice - Personal hearing - Not mandatory - Show-cause notice - A delinquent cannot complain about a lack of natural justice principles if they have received and admitted the show-cause notice.
