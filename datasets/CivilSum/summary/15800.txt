As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, and the Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 131, 13.3(2) and 138, the petitioner who joined as a constable in 1972, was promoted as Head Constable on 2111985 through the sports quota of 2% which was within the prescribed quota for the post.
The petitioner was entitled to be considered for confirmation after two years of service as Head Constable, which is the probation period.
Therefore, on completion of the probationary period, the petitioner has the right to be confirmed on the post of Head Constable.
