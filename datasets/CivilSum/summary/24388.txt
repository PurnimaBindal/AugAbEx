The petitioner, a member of IPS who was not considered for promotion to an ex-cadre post of Joint Secretary, cannot claim violation of Articles 14 and 16.
Since the post of Joint Secretary is not a promotion post from the cadre of IPS, those promoted from other sources cannot be alleged to be junior.
Additionally, the seniority is determined only between the members of one cadre.
Moreover, the petitioner was not available to be considered for promotion because of already holding a special post and no claim can be laid down against an ex-cadre post.
[Paras 3, 4, and 14]
