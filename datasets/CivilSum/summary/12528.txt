Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Back wages - Delay in making reference - Workman terminated on August 31, 1983 - Demand Notice served on February 21, 1984 - Reference made by State Government after 7 years - Although the Labour Court ordered reinstatement, they declined back wages due to the delay in reference - No delay occurred in demand notice - The time taken by the State Government cannot prevent the workman from receiving back wages - The delay was not the fault of the workman - The Award of Labour Court was revised to allow the heirs of the deceased workman's back wages from the date of the demand notice until the workman's death.
