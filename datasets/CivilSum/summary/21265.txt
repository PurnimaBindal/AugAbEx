Constitution of India, Articles 30, 14 and 15 - Grant-in-aid - Exemption from tuition fee - Un-aided part-time courses - State Government instructed to exempt children of green card holders who underwent sterilisation from tuition fees in regular and aided courses - Clarification later issued that exemption only applies to regular and aided courses - Explanation deemed justified - Institutions not receiving funding have right to recover tuition fees - Court cannot directly order Govt. to pay grant-in-aid for green card holders in unaided courses - Grant-in-aid is a State policy matter - Court errant in directing institution to exempt children from tuition fees.
