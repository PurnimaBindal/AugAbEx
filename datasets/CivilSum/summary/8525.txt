Constitution of India, Article 226 - Award of Labour Court denying backwages challenged - The Labour Court's decision to deny backwages to the petitioner was based on the premise that the petitioner did not assume their duties after being declared fit by a doctor.
However, it was revealed that the petitioner was on medical leave as per a certificate issued by a doctor working in the Employees' State Insurance Hospital.
The petitioner also expressed to the respondent corporation that the doctor's opinion did not accurately reflect their state of illness.
As no other doctor examined the petitioner, the reason given by the Labour Court for denying backwages cannot be justified.
[Paras 2 and 3]
