According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, if a candidate does not comply with the conditions of promotion orders, they will face the consequences stipulated in the promotion conditions.
In the case of promoting a clerk, it is subject to passing the type test, which the petitioner did not pass for about six years.
Their deformity of the right middle finger is not a valid excuse for failing to pass the qualifying type test for such a long period, and they cannot receive relief in the writ jurisdiction.
[Para 5]

Again, based on the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, reversion for not passing the qualifying test, despite being granted opportunities, is not discriminatory.
The fact that some other person in a similar situation has not passed is not valid grounds for granting a writ in favor of the petitioner.
Nonetheless, the authority may look into their case.
[Para 3]
