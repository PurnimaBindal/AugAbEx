The Medical Council Act, Sections 10A, 10B, 10C, the Karnataka State Universities Act, Section 53(10), and the Karnataka State Capitation Fee Act Section 41(b) were invoked in a PIL alleging that medical colleges in Karnataka were admitting students beyond the number fixed by the Medical Council.
The state government and colleges challenged the Medical Council's authority to determine admission capacity, but the court found that the Council can prescribe the number of students for each college.
Only the Central Government is empowered to approve an increase in admission capacity.
The court rejected the Division Bench's ruling that admission capacity must be determined as of June 1, 1992 and held that the number of admissions permitted by the State Government and universities prior to that date was permitted.
It cautioned against allowing an illegality to continue and clarified that all regulations framed by the Medical Council with Central Government approval are not merely recommendatory.
