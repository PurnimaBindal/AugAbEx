According to the Punjab Educational Service (School and Inspection) Group A Service Rules, 2004, Rule 5, seniority mandates that a member of the common cadre included at Sr. No. 3 of Appendix B of the Rules must be posted as a DEO.
The post of DEO is considered a superior post out of the common cadre, and posting to the same must be made on the basis of seniority.
It is not acceptable to appoint a junior-most person to such a superior post, as the mandate of the statutory rule cannot be ignored.
In addition, the Punjab Civil Services (General and Common Conditions of Service) Rules, 1976, Rules 9 and 10, prohibit mala fide transfers to avoid promoting senior-most individuals.
Therefore, a transfer order passed on flimsy grounds against the senior-most in the common cadre and a person 100 steps junior being posted to the superior post to be held by the senior-most is not sustainable, according to Paras 12 to 14 and 17.
