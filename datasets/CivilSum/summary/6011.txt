Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Dismissal - Writ Jurisdiction - Scope of - Petitioner who was dismissed from service, after an enquiry on charges of misappropriation, contended that defense was not duly considered and it is a case of no evidence - However, no legal infirmity or material irregularity of procedure in the conduct of an enquiry has been shown - Therefore, the jurisdiction of High Court under Article 226 is limited, and the court is not sitting in appeal - Hence, in the absence of any legal infirmity or material irregularity being shown, the conclusion of the enquiry officer based on some evidence will not be interfered with - Accordingly, petition dismissed.
