Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provisions) Act, 1955, Section 22 - Application for stay of proceedings declined - Reference pending before Industrial Tribunal - Petitioner is registered as a sick unit with BIFR - Only when there is an order for payment of back wages, the question of application of Section 22 may arise - At this point of juncture, the Tribunal has not yet made any order casting any financial burden on the petitioner - Therefore, the application under Section 22 of the Act was rightly rejected.
