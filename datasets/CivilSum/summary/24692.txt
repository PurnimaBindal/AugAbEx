Constitution of India, Article 311 - Gujarat State Electricity Board Service Regulations, Regulation 113 - Gujarat State Electricity Board (Conduct, Discipline and Appeal) Regulations - Incident of Service - The transfer of a Government servant from one place to another, who is appointed to a particular cadre of transferable posts, is deemed an incident of service.
It is essential to mention that no Government servant has a legal right to request to be posted at a particular place.
