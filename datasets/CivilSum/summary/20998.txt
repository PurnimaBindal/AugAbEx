According to Section 10(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, making or declining a reference is an administrative action without any adjudication required, even in the absence of notice or reasons.
Reasons must be recorded only when the government refuses to make a reference.
[Para 4]
As per Section 10(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, when an earlier application for a reference was rejected, and a second application filed, if the matter had already been settled between the workman and management, no industrial dispute exists, and no adjudication order can be made.
