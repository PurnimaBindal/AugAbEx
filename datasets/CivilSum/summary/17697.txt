Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Public post - In order to adhere to the equality clause within Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution, appointment to a public post as public employment must be advertised or called through Employment Exchange to allow for equal opportunity to all interested candidates - This applies to ad hoc temporary/casual appointments as well, which should not exceed 6 months - As the petitioner's appointment did not follow this procedure, no direction can be given to retain him on the post.
