Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - O.M.
dated 761988 and dated 10.9.1993 issued by the Department of Personnel and Training, Casual Labourers (Grant of Temporary Status and Regularisation) Scheme - The scheme aims to provide temporary status to casual labourers, daily rated workers and daily wage workers who have worked continuously for one year - it is a one time measure - those who had not completed one year of continuous employment by the eligibility cut-off date cannot be denied the benefit of the scheme - the objective is to regularize casual labourers after they have continuously worked for one year on work of regular nature - the terms 'casual labourers', 'daily rated workers' and 'daily wagers' are not distinct categories and all are eligible for the benefit of the scheme.
