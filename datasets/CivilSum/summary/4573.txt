Constitution of India, Article 16 - Tamil Nadu Payment of Subsistence Allowance Act, 1981, Sections 2(a) and 3 - Subsistence allowance - Claim for - Respondent's appointment as Secretary in Co-operative Bank - At time of respondent's suspension, he was earning a salary of Rs. 10,447/- per month - According to Section 2(a), a person drawing a wage exceeding Rs. 3,500/- per month and employed mainly in a managerial or administrative capacity is not eligible for subsistence allowance - Claim for subsistence allowance denied.
