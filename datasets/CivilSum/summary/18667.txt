The petitioner, who worked as a Selection Grade Lecturer in Mathematics and had 23 years of teaching experience and 17 years of research experience, was not called for an interview for the post of Professor due to not possessing the requisite qualifications as per the advertisement.
Therefore, he was rightly ignored.
[Paras 7 and 15]

The Selection Committee has the authority to re-examine the qualifications and experience of candidates.
[Para 14]

Employers have the absolute right to prescribe qualifications for job positions.
[Para 13]
