Constitution of India, Article 311 - Competent authority - Termination of services - An authority lower in rank than the appointing authority cannot pass a termination order for a Govt. servant - The I.G.P.
is the appointing authority for a Head Constable in CRPF - A termination order issued by a Commandant, who is equivalent to an SP, is invalid and cannot be upheld.
