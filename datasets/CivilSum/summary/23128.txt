Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Reinstatement - Termination - Backwages - The Labour Court had awarded the respondent reinstatement and backwages, but the respondent's services were terminated a second time in 1988, and this order was never challenged or questions by the respondent - The High Court's decision to award backwages until March 1994 was criticized for ignoring the valid order of termination - The order of both the Labour Court and the High Court were modified such that backwages only had to be paid up until the valid order of termination on April 18th, 1988.
