Apprentices Act, 1961, Section 3(d) - Regularisation of apprentices - Appointment letters of apprentices contain a clause that they may be considered for appointment on the same regular posts after successfully completing their training, subject to job availability.
However, if regular posts are not available, management is not obligated to offer employment.
Continuation of apprentices on the same posts with the same stipend after training does not guarantee absorption into regular service.
The Corporation declared posts surplus due to its losses and no regular posts were available when petitioners' apprenticeships ended.
Therefore, the petitioners have no legitimate reason to complain.
