Constitution of India 1950, Article 14 and 16 - Pay - Equal Pay for equal work - There is a disparity in the allowance for ration between combatised and non-combatised personnel in CRPF - Distinctions in terms and conditions of service, nature of work, and tenure of service exist between combatised and non-combatised personnel - They are both governed by distinct Rules - Combatised personnel are governed by CRPF Act and Rules, which are stringent, while non-combatised personnel are governed by Civilian law i.e., CCS Rules - They cannot be treated as equals - The principle of equal pay for equal work is not applicable.
