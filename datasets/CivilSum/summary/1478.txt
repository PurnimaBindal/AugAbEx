Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Act, 1950, Section 8 (2) - Premature retirement - Withdrawal of request - Army Officers can withdraw their request for premature retirement after approval or acceptance but prior to it becoming effective.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Act, 1950, Section 8 - Premature retirement - Withdrawal of request - Accepting post-retirement dues does not equate to accepting the benefits of voluntary retirement, and therefore, the retiree is not precluded from challenging their premature retirement.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Act, 1950, Section 8(2)- Delegation of powers - Premature retirement - Competent Authority - Withdrawal of request - The Central Government is empowered to evaluate requests for withdrawal of premature retirement applications for Army officers, and this authority cannot be conferred to the Army Head Quarters in the absence of statutory guidelines or regulations.
