The petitioner was initially denied promotion due to an adverse entry in his ACR, but this was later expunged.
Despite being promoted during the pendency of his case, the petitioner now claims arrears of salary from the date of the expunction of his adverse ACR, as he was denied promotion through no fault of his own.
The court ruled that it was appropriate to award the petitioner a lump-sum compensation of Rs. 10,000/- in lieu of wages for the period in which he was not permitted to discharge his duties until his actual promotion.
