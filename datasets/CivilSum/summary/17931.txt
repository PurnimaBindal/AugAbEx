Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Economic and Statistical Organisation (State Service Class III) Rules, 1963 Rule 10, Appendix 'A' - Punjab Economic and Statistical Organisation (Class III Service) Rules, 1980, Rule 10 - Seniority/Experience/ Separation of cadres - Pre-1980 Amendment, clerks and computers were one cadre, with inter-transferable posts.
After the amendment, seniority of both clerks and computers must be based on their total service period before separation of the two cadres.
Petitioners' cases must be considered accordingly.
Note 2 of Rule 10 struck down for being contrary to the main rule and violating Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
