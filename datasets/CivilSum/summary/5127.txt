Under Section 19 of the Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act (5 of 1970) and Regulation 7(2) of the UCO Bank Officer Employees (Disciplinary and Appeal) Regulations, 1976 pertaining to natural justice in departmental enquiries, if the disciplinary authority disagrees with the enquiry officer over certain charges, the employee must be informed of the disagreement and given seven days to make a submission.

According to Section 19 of the Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act (5 of 1970) and Regulation 7(3) of the UCO Bank Officer Employees (Disciplinary and Appeal) Regulations, 1976, multiple penalties can be imposed for a single violation.
The word "all" must be interpreted as it is and the word "a" should be interpreted as meaning more than one.
The dictionary meaning of "a" includes any or every.

Section 19 of the Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act (5 of 1970) and Regulation 4(e) of the UCO Bank Officer Employees (Disciplinary and Appeal) Regulations, 1976 state that if an officer is reduced to a lower grade, it means a reduction to the lower stage from the stage at which the delinquent officer was working at the relevant point of time and not on the lowest grade.
