The Andhra Pradesh DB was justified in upholding the termination of the General Manager (Finance) due to misconduct, which was based on a letter from the Commissioner of Handlooms and Director of Handloom and Textiles.
The termination order was not infirm and was innocuous in nature.
[Paras 3 and 4]  B While the court may not interfere with the termination of temporary, probationary, or tenure employees, an order of termination without any stigma, it is permitted to examine the circumstances leading up to the order.
If inefficiency was the real reason for the order, the court can interfere since appropriate procedures were not followed, but if it was just the motive, the court cannot interfere.
