The Plantation Labour Act, 1951 (amended by the Plantation Labour (Amendment) Act, 1960) Section 1(4), 1(5) requires estates measuring 10.117 hectares or employing 30 or more persons to comply with the Act.
The proviso to Section 1(5) is meant to save from the operation of this Act only those estates that were less than 10.117 hectares or in which less than 30 persons were employed.
The proviso is an integral part of Sub-section (5) and cannot be interpreted to mean the Amendment Act of 1960.
