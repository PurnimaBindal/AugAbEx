Haryana Education Code, Rule 4(2) of Appendix 27 - Termination - Simpliciter termination order - Jurisdiction - Affiliated school teacher - The rules do not specify the procedure for termination when a teacher's services are terminated by the management of a privately managed school - The DEO has no authority to overturn the management's order as it neither serves as a disciplinary authority nor an appellate authority - Although the DEO may initiate de-recognition of the school, the jurisdiction to set aside the simpliciter termination order passed by the managing committee is not within their purview.
