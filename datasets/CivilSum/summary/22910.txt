Rajasthan Mines and Geological Service Rules, 1960, Entry 6 in the Schedule - Promotion/Practical Experience/Experience - The rules require practical experience of five years, not just service.
Each Assistant Mechanical Engineer must have five years of practical experience to be eligible for promotion to the post of Deputy Drilling Engineer.
Not every Assistant Mechanical Engineer of five years standing can be promoted.
The petition was dismissed.

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Rajasthan Mines and Geological Service Rules, 1960, Entry 6 in the Schedule - Educational Qualifications/Practical Experience - Obtaining additional or better qualifications alone does not mean fulfilling the requirement of practical experience prescribed in the Schedule.
Acquiring better educational qualifications does not remove the necessity of practical experience.
