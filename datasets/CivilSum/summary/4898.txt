Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 311 - Calcutta Port Trust Employees (Classification, Control and Appeal) Regulations, 1987, Regulations 7(v), 711, 8 and 23 - Absence from duty - Petitioner was absent from work due to prolonged illness from 12111990, which led to disciplinary proceedings being initiated and a charge sheet being issued - The charge sheet was based on the wrong grounds, as it appears that the period of absence for the year 1994-95 had been double counted - The petitioner was wrongly accused twice for the same offense and was a victim of official bias - The charge-sheet was quashed and the petitioner's case was allowed.
