According to Section 11(2) of the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 1952 and Section 46(B) of the State Financial Corporations Act 1951, priority must be given to the payment of Provident Fund Contributions over all other debts.
As the statute created a first charge, dues under the Act shall be paid in priority against all other debts, especially when it pertains to protecting the terminal social security dues of workmen.
