UP Secondary Education Services Commission Act (5 of 1982), Section 18 - UP Secondary Education Service Commission (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 1981 - Ad hoc appointment of teacher - Section 18 and the UP Secondary Education Services Commission (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 1981, are both in the same field and part of one integrated scheme for the recruitment of required ad hoc teachers - The prescribed procedure under the Removal of Difficulties Order, 1981 must be followed - Appointment under Section 18 without following the procedure is considered void.
