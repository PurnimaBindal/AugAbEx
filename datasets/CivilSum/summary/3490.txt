The appointment of a writ petitioner as Joint Manager (General) on probation allows authorities to assess suitability within the period.
Termination due to unsatisfactory performance during extended period of probation does not constitute stigma, and continuation beyond probation does not grant deemed confirmation status.
Petition rightly dismissed.
[Paras 16 and 17]
Article 311 allows a probationer to be discharged before the period's expiry, but not to punish them for any misconduct.
Authorities can assess performance and terminate service accordingly.
