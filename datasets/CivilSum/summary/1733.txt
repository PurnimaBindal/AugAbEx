The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, specifically Sections 25-F and 2(oo), Exception (bb), were discussed regarding the termination of a daily wage worker's service after 240 days.
The ruling was that the worker's 240-day service did not indicate permanence, and their termination fell under exception (bb) of Section 2(oo).
Therefore, Section 25-F did not apply in this case, and the award was set aside.
