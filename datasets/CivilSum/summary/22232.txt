The pay scale in the Constitution of India, as stated in Articles 16 and 226, was revised by the government effective May 1, 1990.
Despite this, the claimant is seeking pay from January 1, 1986, when the 4th Pay Commission recommended a change.
The High Court granted the claim, stating that there was no issue with the decision of the government and that interference under discretionary jurisdiction was unnecessary.
Additionally, since the legality of the notification was not being contested, there was nothing to challenge.
