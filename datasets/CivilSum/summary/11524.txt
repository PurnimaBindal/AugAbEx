Constitution of India 1950, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Part-timers - Regularisation - Policy decision - Part time employees with five years of service are entitled to regularization - Denial of regularization due to insufficient years of continuous service and qualifications is not sustainable - Policy applies only to part-timers, not those who worked partly as part-timers and partly as full-timers - Employers, especially the Government, cannot use questionable methods to affect the lives of their employees.
