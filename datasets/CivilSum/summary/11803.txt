Constitution of India, Article 226 - Jammu and Kashmir Horticulture (Planning and Marketing) Gazetted Service Recruitment Rules, 1981 (as amended in 1990), Rule 11 - While determining whether a Master's degree with Agriculture Marketing as one of the subjects is better or equivalent to a Diploma in Agricultural Marketing, only experts in education field can make such findings, and not the court.
However, for the post of Assistant Grading and Marketing Officer, the essential qualification prescribed is Diploma in Agriculture Marketing.
Therefore, having a Master's degree does not make the petitioner eligible as an expert in Agriculture Marketing as required by the Government.
