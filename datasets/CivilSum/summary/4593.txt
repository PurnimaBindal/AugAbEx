Article 14 of the Indian Constitution, Section 58 of the Reserve Bank of India Act 1934, Regulation 3(3) of Reserve Bank of India (Pension) Regulations 1990, and Regulation 26(4) of Reserve Bank of India (Staff) Regulation, 1948, all pertain to the Pension Scheme for employees.
Regulation 3(3) of Pension Regulations excludes employees who were on leave preparatory to retirement before 1186 but retired after that date from the benefit of the Pension Scheme.
Regulation 26(4) of Staff Regulation contains a deeming clause for employees on leave preparatory to retirement that enables them to avail of leave in lieu of encashing the said leave period.
This clause has been included to allow the availing of earned leave but not to extend other benefits, as retirees cannot avail of leave after the date of superannuation.
Furthermore, Regulation 3(3) of Pension regulation is not arbitrary but specific to the Pension Scheme.

`Generalia specialibus non derogant' is a legal maxim that emphasizes the prevalence of specific laws over general laws.
Specifically, the Reserve Bank of India (Pension) Regulation would override Reserve Bank of India (Staff) Regulation, 1948 in matters regarding pension, as the former is specific to the Pension Scheme.
