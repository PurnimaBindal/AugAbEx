Railway Establishment Manual, Paragraphs 302, 214(c)(iii) & (v) & 213 - Promotion - Seniority - Ad hoc promotion without holding suitability test - Seniority in the cadre of Senior Clerks is regulated by Paragraph 302 - Promotion to the Sr.
Clerk cadre is non-selection but can only be made from the most senior candidate after passing suitability test according to paragraph 214(c)(v) - Ad hoc promotion made without suitability test - Ad hoc promotees made regular after passing suitability test - Relevant seniority is not backdated to ad hoc promotion date - Promotions without passing prescribed suitability tests are against the rule and cannot be included in seniority ranking except for pension purposes - In the absence of explicit rules, seniority procedures follow general principles established by the courts - Regular promotion cannot date back to the ad hoc period even if the Rule describes how to calculate seniority from the date of promotion.
