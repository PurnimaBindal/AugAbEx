Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Disability pension - Petitioner suffered injury and had an amputated finger while in the Indian Army - Despite this, the petitioner continued service in a sheltered appointment which was later discontinued due to unavoidable reasons - Disability was assessed at 15% below pensionable degree (20%) - Claim for disability pension was denied - Petitioner was offered a rehabilitation opportunity as a peon but declined - Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to disability pension.
