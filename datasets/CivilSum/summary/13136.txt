Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate Appointment - Eligibility for dependent members of families killed in terrorist violence - The petitioner's father passed away in 1990 and a request for appointment was made in 1998 - However, as eight years had passed since the father's death, it cannot be considered an immediate requirement for the family - Additionally, as the petitioner is married and not dependent, she is not eligible for compassionate appointment.
The ruling in 1994(4) S.C.C.
138 is relied on.
