Industrial Disputes Act, Section 11-A - Natural Justice - Jurisdiction - The Petitioner was penalized with dismissal and stoppage of three increments by the Labour Court for collecting fare without tickets - The Award has been contested on grounds of violating natural justice - However, the High Court has limited jurisdiction in interfering with the discretion of the Labour Court - There is no interference with the Labour Court's conclusion regarding the contravention of natural justice.
[Para 3] B Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Dismissal - Punishment - Departmental enquiry - The Labour Court has given the punishment of dismissal and stoppage of three increments to the Conductor who was found guilty of collecting fare without issuing tickets - However, the Division Bench believes that there needs to be stern punishment for such employees, and a departmental inquiry should be held to determine the appropriate consequences.
