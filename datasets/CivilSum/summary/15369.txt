Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Punjab Public Works Department (Irrigation Branch) Revenue Clerks State Service Class III Rules, 1956, Rules 2(h) & 10(2) - Promotion - Imposing upper age limit for promotion - This limits the right of departmental candidates to be promoted for the remainder of their service period and is therefore illegal and arbitrary, as the age limit applies only to direct recruits.
