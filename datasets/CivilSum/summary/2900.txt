Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, Sections 166 and 167 - Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4-A(3) - Compensation - If the workman causes an accident due to rash and negligent driving, the issue of vicarious liability does not arise when the claim is made by the tort-teasor or any other person claiming under the tort-teasor.
Though insurance companies are not liable under Motor Vehicles Act, compensation can still be assessed under Workmen's Compensation Act.
[Para 6]  B. Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4A - Compensation - Interest - Interest for compensation amount begins to accrue 30 days after the date of the accident and not from the date of quantification.
