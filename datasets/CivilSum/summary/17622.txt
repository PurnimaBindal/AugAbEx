Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Reimbursement/Medical treatment/Prior permission of Medical Board or Director Health - Emergency treatment - The petitioner underwent Coronary Artery Grafting Bye-Pass surgery from Escorts Hospital as an emergency case.
Due to his critical condition and the necessity of the surgery to save his life, the authorities cannot deny reimbursement of expenses on the grounds of lack of prior permission.
The petition is allowed, and the respondents are directed to make the payment within one month from the date of resubmission of bills.
