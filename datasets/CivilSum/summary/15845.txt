Qualification for promotion to a headmaster post from a teacher is not laid down by the State, according to a case involving the Constitution of India, Articles 226.
The petitioner was qualified for the promotion using a resolution that countered the respondent's submission.
[Paras 4 to 7]

In a case involving the Constitution of India, Article 226/227, a petitioner was promoted to a headmaster post over respondent No. 5 despite the latter's appointment being earlier.
This is because the petitioner was appointed against a higher grade than the respondent, and the impugned order was set aside.
[Paras 8, 9 and 10]
