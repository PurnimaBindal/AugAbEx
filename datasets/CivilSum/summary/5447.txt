Constitution of India, Article 226 - Karnataka Private Educational Institution Act, 1975, Section 8 - Termination of service - Validity - Services of the petitioner terminated - The petitioner was reappointed on a temporary basis and had executed a bond that required a three months notice to be given to him if his services were not required.
The Tribunal ruled that the petitioner was only a temporary employee who could not be a permanent employee until he was confirmed and his services could be terminated without assigning any reason.
The termination did not subject the petitioner to any stigma.
Hence, the finding of the Tribunal was valid and the appeal was dismissed.
