Constitution of India, Article 311 - Appointment of petitioner on probation - The probation period cannot be extended beyond three years under the rules - Once the three-year period has elapsed, the service of the petitioner cannot be terminated on the grounds of unsatisfactory service by a simple notice - The department is required to conduct a regular enquiry under the rules before terminating the services, as per AIR 1968 SC 1210.
