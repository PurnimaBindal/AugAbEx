The Municipal Officers and Servants Recruitment Rules, 1942, Rule 4 and the CP and Berar Municipalities Act, 1922, Section 9 state that a person convicted of a crime that amounts to moral turpitude is ineligible for appointment to such positions.
Despite this, the appellant concealed the fact of his conviction at the time of appointment and was later terminated when this fact was disclosed.
The principle of condonation of misconduct and the master's inability to go back on his election to condone and instead claim the right to dismiss the servant will be applied, and therefore, there will be no interference, and the appeal will be dismissed.
