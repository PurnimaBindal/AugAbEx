Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 162 and 1624 - Dismissal from service - Misconduct - Gravest misconduct - A constable who refuses to obey the orders of his superior Head constable and causes him injuries is guilty of gravest misconduct and may be dismissed from service - The length of the delinquent's service may also be taken into account while deciding on the punishment.
[Para 9]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural justice - If a delinquent has been served with a show-cause notice and charge-sheet, filed a reply and participated in the preliminary and regular enquiry, but failed to cross-examine any prosecution witness when given the opportunity, he cannot claim that principles of natural justice have been violated.
[Para 10]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 19 - Natural justice - Documents - If no demand for supply of documents, statements and reports recorded during the preliminary enquiry is made during the regular enquiry, and no prejudice is alleged or shown to have been caused by non-supply, the punishment order cannot be challenged on the ground of a violation of natural justice.
