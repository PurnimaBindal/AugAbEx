The petitioner was suspended from service on charges of illegal absence and financial irregularities, during which time she refused to turn over office records to the Acting Head-Mistress in accordance with the Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 16.
Two officers conducted an inquiry, but the petitioner did not cooperate or participate.
When asked to show cause, she submitted a response claiming that the complaints and allegations were manufactured documents.
Despite this, the enquiring authority found the charges established and proved, and a minor punishment of withholding two increments was imposed.
The petitioner challenged the punishment as having been awarded without holding a regular enquiry, but this argument was found unsupportable; the punishment imposed was not in violation of the principles of natural justice.
Therefore, there should be no interference.
