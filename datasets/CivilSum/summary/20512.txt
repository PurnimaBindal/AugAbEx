Constitution of India, Article 16(4) 77th amendment - Reservation in promotions - A.P.
Commercial Tax Subordinate Service Rules, Rule 5 - A.P.
State and Subordinate Service Rules, Rule 22 - The judgement in Indra Swhney's case stated that no reservation can be made where it is not specifically provided.
However, the 77th amendment in the Constitution has removed the basis of this judgement, allowing the state to make provisions for reservation by promotion if they are satisfied and form an opinion that Scheduled Castes or Scheduled Tribes are not adequately represented in promotional posts.
Thus, the rule of reservation can apply not only in initial recruitments but also in promotions.
Rule 22 of Service Rules also made a specific provision for reservation in direct recruitment, indicating that reservation under Articles 141B, 16(1), and 16(4) also includes reservation in promotions as well.
