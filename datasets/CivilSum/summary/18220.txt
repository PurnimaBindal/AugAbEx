Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Punjabi University Syndicate Proceedings dated 2162003, Clause 24.17 - Pension - Computation of qualifying service for pension - Government departments or Govt. owned statutory bodies must have reciprocal arrangements to give benefit of previous service at the time of retirement from the later served department - Even if an employee is issued pre-mature retirement orders, such an order must be treated and converted as relieving certificate - Any unfair action depriving employee of retiral benefits for long service must be challenged - Punjab University must transfer all retiral benefits of the petitioner to Delhi University.
