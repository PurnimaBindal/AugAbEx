The Employees Provident Fund and Misc.
Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 1(3)(a), 3(1)(b) and 16, the Rajasthan Non Government Educational Institutions (Recognition Grant in aid and Service Conditions etc) Rules, 1993, Rule 2(r) and (s), and the State Act of 1989, Section 2(p) and (q) all pertain to the applicability of the 1952 Act to non-government aided educational institutions.
However, section 16(1)(b) of the Act clearly states that it does not apply to cases where a scheme or rule is already framed by the State Government.
Therefore, in light of existing State Rules, the Central Act will not apply to such institutions.
