Constitution of India, Article 226 - Sufficiency of evidence - Writ jurisdiction - In a previous petition, the High Court directed the Government to consider the documents provided by the petitioner in support of their claim for Freedom Fighters Pension.
However, the Government concluded that the evidence was not sufficient to prove that the petitioner had suffered imprisonment and was therefore ineligible.
The High Court cannot determine that the evidence presented to the authority was sufficient to support the petitioner's claim and cannot assess the sufficiency of evidence.
