Constitution of India, Article 16 - Petitioner seeks stay of orders due to frequent transfer - Stay granted - MPs and MLAs cannot be held wrong for suggesting action against public servants as representatives of public - State has placed some facts and figures on the table to justify orders - Transfer is an exigency of the service and public servants have no vested right to remain posted at one place - Courts should not interfere unless orders are in violation of mandatory or statutory rules or established to be mala fide - As per State's submissions, reasons for sticking to one place are obvious - Petition dismissed.
[Paras 3, 8 and 9]
