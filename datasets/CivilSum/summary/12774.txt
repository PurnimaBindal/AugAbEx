The petitioner, who was invalided out of service on Medical Board's opinion with a disability of 30%, was denied the disability pension on the grounds that the disability was not related to military service and was assessed at less than 20%.
However, the decision in Gurmukh Singh v. Union of India and others (C.W.P.
No. 6789 of 1998 decided on 20th April, 1999) quashed the impugned order.
