Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 102, Order VII Rule 1 - Limitation Act, Article 67 - Plaintiff/respondent discovered error in date of birth in 1956 - Suit filed almost 30 years later - Trial Court decreed suit - First appellate Court agreed - However, both Courts failed to consider that Article 67 of Limitation Act specifies a 3-year time limit from the discovery of mistake to file suit - Therefore, suit was barred by limitation and could not be upheld - Appeal allowed - Judgment and decree overturned - Plaintiff's suit dismissed.
