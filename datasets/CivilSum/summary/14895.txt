Under the Constitution of India, as stated in Articles 14 and 226, the government has the authority to issue instructions for the development of specific subjects and to approve different pay scales based on academic qualifications.
Providing a higher pay scale to Sanskrit teachers who possess Shastri qualifications and a one-year Diploma in OT is lawful and legitimate, as a teacher with these qualifications is unquestionably more competent in teaching the Sanskrit subject.
Not all graduates with OT are considered trained graduates and, therefore, cannot be granted a higher pay scale.
