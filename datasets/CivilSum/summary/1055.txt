Constitution of India's Article 16 covers when posts are advertised and an examination is held.
In this specific case, the petitioner appeared in the examination, but the examination was later cancelled and the posts were re-advertised without any explanation given for the cancellation.
Despite crossing the age limit, the petitioner could not be held ineligible.
The authority is now directed to hold the test and provide appointment if found adequate.
[Paras 15 to 17]
