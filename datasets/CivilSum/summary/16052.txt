Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Pension - Gratuity and leave encashment - Disciplinary proceedings - Withholding of pension is not permissible due to the pendency of disciplinary proceedings - Authority directed to release the full pension within two months, otherwise, interest will be paid at 18% per annum - Gratuity and leave encashment may be withheld until the decision of disciplinary proceedings.
[Paras 2 and 3]
