According to Rule 6-A of the Central Information Service Rules, 1959 and Article 309 of the Indian Constitution, seniority and conditions of service are dictated by the Service Rules.
If these rules are absent, seniority is calculated based on length of service.
An official only becomes a member of the service once they are regularised on the post and their seniority is determined from the date of regularisation.
