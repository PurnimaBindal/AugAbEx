As per Section 2(3) of the Industrial Disputes Act (1947), `Industry' includes the Road Construction Department in the Public Works Dept.
(PWD).
The status of profit motive and gainful object is not relevant in determining the definition of `industry', and there is no differentiation between the public, joint private or other sector.
Thus, the Public Work Department cannot be excluded or exempted from the definition of `industry', and the characteristic test is the nature of activity considering the employer-employee relations.
