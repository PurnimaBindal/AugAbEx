The Constitution of India, Article 311 - Posts and Telegraph Extra Departmental Agents (Conduct and Salaries) Rules, 1964, Rule 6 - Natural justice - In cases where termination of services is based on material that is adverse to the employee, principles of natural justice must be observed - The employee's services were terminated due to misconduct resulting from submitting false income certificate for appointment - Termination is illegal.
