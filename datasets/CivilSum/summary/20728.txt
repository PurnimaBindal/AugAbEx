Constitution of India, Article 311 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19 - Legal Representative - Legality of dismissal from service - Legal heirs have the right to challenge the legality and validity of an order of dismissal from their father's service, as per two members of the Tribunal who differed in their views - though both were unanimous on the order of dismissal being proper.
[Paras 8 and 9] The Tribunal did look into the issues' merits in rejecting the application, but it is not necessary to examine the legal heirs' right to raise the issue once the dismissal order is deemed proper.
