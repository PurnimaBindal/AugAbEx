Payment of Wages Act, Section 17(1-A) - Appeal - Certificate of amount deposited - Appeal cannot proceed without certificate - Employer required to comply with provision - Worker not required to demand payment - Delay in payment or refusal to pay results in bar to appeal - Payment made later will not make appeal valid - Provision does not allow deposit after filing of appeal or condone delay - Distinction may exist when provision is "appeal shall not be entertained" or "no appeal shall be admitted."
