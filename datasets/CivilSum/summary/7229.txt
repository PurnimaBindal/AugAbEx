The Army Act of 1950, specifically Sections 39(b) and 39(a), and 106, outline the consequences of absence from unit lines without leave.
The punishment for this offense is dismissal from service.
The court held that this punishment is not disproportionate to the offense committed.
The serious nature of the offense, such as abandoning units lines without permission, justifies the imposed punishment.
Maintaining discipline is crucial in ensuring the efficiency and ability of an organization to perform their duty.
