Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Punjab Agricultural Produce Markets Act, 1961, Section 42 - Punjab Agricultural Marketing (Class II) Service Rules, 1988 - Promotion/Qualifications - Even if the qualifications earlier prescribed in the Rules are subsequently changed, promotions are to be based on the qualifications required on the promotion date - Changes to the requirements do not affect the petitioner's right.
