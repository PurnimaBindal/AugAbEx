The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, states that the onus of proving termination lies with the workman.
In this case, the Management had stated that the workman had abandoned his employment, but then offered employment during conciliation proceedings which the workman refused.
There is no evidence to suggest that the Management was aware of the address of the workman.
Additionally, the workman served notice of demand after one year and three months, and there is no reference to any evidence indicating that the workman had discharged the onus or that the Management had actually terminated his services.
Consequently, the Labour Court's award that the workman had been illegally terminated cannot be sustained and should be set aside.
