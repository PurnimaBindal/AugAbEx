Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Admission of guilt - Termination - Absence of inquiry - Reinstatement - The order of termination was based on incorrect grounds.
There was no admission of guilt on the part of the workman in question, and therefore no further inquiry was necessary.
Based on the evidence presented, the Labour Court found that the foundation for the order was unfounded.
Consequently, the award of the Labour Court was deemed legal and free from error.
