Section 7(3-A) of the Payment of Gratuity Act concerns withholding of pensionary benefits/gratuity.
If the grounds for withholding are non-vacation of a government quarter or delay in furnishing a 'No Dues Certificate', they are not tenable and do not provide a valid reason to withhold gratuity.
[Para 11]
Sub-section (3-A) of Section 7 of the Payment of Gratuity Act provides for payment of interest on withheld gratuity, except where the delay is the fault of the employer.
It is the employer's obligation to determine the amount of gratuity and give notice of the amount payable.
In this case, the respondents are directed to pay interest at a rate of 10% per annum from 30 days after superannuation until payment, as the conditions for withholding interest were not met.
[Paras 14 and 15]
