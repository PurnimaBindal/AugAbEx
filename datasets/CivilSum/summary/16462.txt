Haryana Aided Schools (Security of Service) Rules, 1974, Rules 3 and 7 - Appointment of teacher in aided school - Approval of Director Education is not necessary, as once a post is created, it can be filled in accordance with Rule 7.
[Para 7] B Haryana Aided Schools (Security of Service) Rules, 1974, Rules 3 and 7 - Abolition of post - Appointment of teacher in aided school - Reduction in posts and expenditure advised by financial circular of Govt. does not automatically result in post abolition, and approval for appointment cannot be declined on that basis.
