According to Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 52(1)(d), an adopted son or daughter can receive compensation as a dependent under the Act.
[Para 6]
The finding of the Commissioner that the claimant in a case was the adopted daughter of the deceased is considered a finding of fact based on evidence under Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30, and cannot be interfered with.
