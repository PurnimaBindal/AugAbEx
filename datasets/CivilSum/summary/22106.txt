Limitation Act 1963, Section 5 - Condonation of delay - Default of lawyer - The Corporation removed their counsel from their panel and hired a new one to file an appeal, with the previous counsel failing to properly inform them of the decision.
As a result, it was held to be a sufficient cause for condoning the delay in filing the writ appeal.
[Para 5]  B Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Section 11-A - Misconduct - Punishment - Unless the punishment awarded by the Disciplinary Authority is shockingly disproportionate to the proved charge, it should not be interfered.
The appellant was involved in more than 41 cases of the same nature and was found to be a habitual offender, with punishments awarded but never learning their lesson and being deemed incorrigible.
For these reasons, the punishment of dismissal awarded by the Disciplinary Authority in the light of their previous service record was fully justified.
