Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Discharge from service - A casual worker was discharged from service without conducting a departmental enquiry.
The appellant corporation argued that it was a simpliciter discharge, but it is in violation of Section 25-F of Industrial Disputes Act for non-compliance.
The impugned order is rightly set aside, and the respondent is entitled to continuity of service and reinstatement.
However, the backwages awarded by Labour Court are reduced from cent percent to 50%.
