Constitution of India, Article 226 - Caste of a person - Deciding factor - Unless there is evidence of a change in caste affiliation, a person's caste is determined by their birth.
[Paras 9 and 20] B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Benefit of caste - Economic status - A person's economic status cannot be used to deny them scheduled caste benefits if they belong to that caste.
[Paras 18 and 19] C. Constitution of India, Article 226 - High Court - Supervisory power - The High Court should intervene if the subordinate authority's decision is nonsensical and contrary to ordinary reasoning - The court cannot disregard flawed decisions made by the subordinate authority on the grounds of evidential conflict - Article 226 can be utilized to challenge Scrutiny Committee findings.
[Para 30] D. Constitution of India, Article 226 - Scheduled caste benefits - Marriages - A person who marries someone from outside the scheduled caste is still entitled to all benefits assigned to the scheduled caste.
