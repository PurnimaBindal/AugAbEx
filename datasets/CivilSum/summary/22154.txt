According to Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Section 18 of the Apprentices Act, 1961, apprentices are not considered "workmen" for the purpose of the Industrial Disputes Act, even if they may be treated as such.
This is because of the specific exclusion under Section 18 of the Apprentices Act.
It's also important to note that even if the term of an apprentice is extended, they will continue to be considered an apprentice or contractual worker under Section 2(oo)(bb) unless they are taken into regular service according to applicable rules.
As a result, Section 25F will not apply on their removal.
