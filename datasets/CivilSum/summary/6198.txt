Constitution of India, Article 14 - Haryana State Agricultural Marketing Board Service Rules, 1974, Rule 10 - Promotion - Eligibility - Date of consideration for promotion - When the State Government decides to fill a vacancy is the relevant date for considering eligible candidates for promotion - It is up to the State to determine when a vacancy is to be filled - In 1983, a vacancy was available and Respondent No. 2 was eligible, but consideration for promotion was not taken up until October 1990, when two additional candidates had become eligible by passing the AMIE diploma - Who was the seniormost person possessing all the necessary qualifications and experience at the time of consideration was to be determined - Even though the Petitioner had passed the AMIE diploma in 1990, if there was nothing against him and he possessed all the necessary qualifications and experience, he could be promoted - The date of Respondent No. 2's passing of the AMIE diploma in 1993 was irrelevant.
