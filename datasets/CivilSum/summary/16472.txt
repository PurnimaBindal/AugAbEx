According to the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 21, Disability Pension for hearing impairment is based on an attribution of disability to service.
Despite the lack of a recorded hearing defect or deficiency at enrollment, the petitioner's claim that he was posted to places where he developed hearing problems and frequently referred to hospitals for treatment was unrebutted.
The Medical Board's opinion that the disability was contracted in the course of service, as well as other facts presented, support the conclusion that the petitioner's disability was sustained during service, and thus attributable to and aggravated by his Force service.
The Authority has been directed to provide the petitioner's pension in accordance with his disability, with an added 9% rate of interest.
