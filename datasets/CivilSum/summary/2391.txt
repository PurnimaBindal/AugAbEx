Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 - Termination - Temporary injunction - The petitioner, who was appointed as a Hindi Teacher and possessed qualifications obtained from an institute not recognized by the State of Haryana, had their services terminated.
The Division Bench of High Court found that the petitioner's degrees/diploma/certificate were not recognized and that individuals possessing such qualifications are not entitled to appointment as Hindi Teachers.
However, a temporary injunction may be allowed to the petitioner.
