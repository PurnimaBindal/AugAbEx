The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 31-C, as well as Punjab Civil Services Rules Volume II, Rules 2.2(c) and 2.2.
(b), specify that pension and gratuity must be paid to employees who have been acquitted of criminal or departmental proceedings without justification.
The mere consideration of the possibility of departmental proceedings or the continuation of a vigilance enquiry cannot justify withholding retiral benefits.
Pension and gratuity are the property of an employee and should not be withheld without the due process of law.
Withholding of dues from an employee who is acquitted of charges against him is unjustified.
The authority must compensate by paying 12% interest from the date of the employee's acquittal to the actual payment.
