The State's policy decision to allot quarters to police personnel on a rotational basis for a period of three years was challenged in the High Court.
The court held that the decision was contrary to justness and fair play.
However, the court also recognized that it cannot direct or advise the executive in matters of policy, as long as those actions are within constitutional limits and statutory power.
The correctness of the reasons behind the government's decision-making process was deemed irrelevant in the context of judicial review.
