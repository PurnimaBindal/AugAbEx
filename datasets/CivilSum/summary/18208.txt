Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Revision of pay scales and special pay by Pay Commission - The State Government cannot make classification between retirees who have benefited from the Pay Commission report and those in service regarding the revision of special pay.
Even if the Pay Commission report is accepted and there is no classification present in it, the Govt. cannot make such classification by mere clarification especially when it is clear about the cut off date for the implementation of the benefit - Pre 111996 retirees entitled to revision of pension w.e.f.
111996 as per 4th Pay Commission - The Single Judge rightly quashed the clarification dated 19111998 of Punjab Govt. [Para 13]
