Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300-A - Pension - Qualifying service - Counting together State and Central Govt. Service - In case of resignation from State service before joining Union Govt., appointment in Central Govt. service is subject to acceptance of resignation by State and approval by higher authorities - Non-approval of higher authorities for over 33 years can be presumed as necessary permission - Employee cannot suffer for inefficiency of State Govt. officials - Petitioner entitled to benefit for past services rendered to State and computation of pension accordingly.
