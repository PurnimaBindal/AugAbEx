Karnataka Handloom Development Corporation Limited (Staff Regulations) 1979, Regulation 18 Clauses (iii) and (vii) - Probationer - Termination of service - Appellant contends that since according to clause (iii) the probation period cannot be extended beyond two years the appellant should have become a confirmed employee and an enquiry should have been conducted.
However, according to clause (vii) of Regulation 18, if there is no order of confirmation then the employee remains on probation.
The court must interpret all provisions harmoniously to give effect to the whole, and not consider clause (iii) in isolation to assume that the appellant should have become confirmed after two years of probation.
[Paras 11, 12, 14, 20] B Probationer - Order of termination - Not punitive - Prior to the termination, the probationer was given a show cause notice.
The mere presence of misconduct without being investigated does not necessarily make the termination order a punishment.
Mere background information cannot be considered a stigma.
