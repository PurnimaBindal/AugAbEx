Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Submission of false caste certificate - Disciplinary proceedings - Cancellation of caste certificate by Collector - Appellate authority may consider the subsequent cancellation of the caste certificate - Once the original certificate is cancelled, any other certificate obtained on the basis of that certificate would be non-existent - Under these circumstances, there is no reason to allow the respondent to continue in service - In fact, disciplinary proceedings might not even be necessary in such cases.
