Voluntary retirement under the Punjab Civil Services Rules Volume II allows for seeking retirement after 20 years of qualifying service, but it is not automatically accepted and requires approval by the appointing authority.
[Para 8]
The competent authority has the power to deny voluntary retirement, as seen in the case of a petitioner with pending disciplinary cases against them.
The decision is within the rules and not subject to judicial review unless there is evidence of malice or deserving circumstances.
[Para 16]
The government is responsible for providing retiral benefits in cases of voluntary retirement.
[Para 13]
A petitioner cannot claim they will not accept retiral benefits until disciplinary cases are resolved as this goes against the rules and no cut in pension can be imposed except as punishment.
[Para 14]
Acceptance of voluntary retirement notice is the duty of the executive, but judicial review is possible if malice or deserving circumstances are present.
