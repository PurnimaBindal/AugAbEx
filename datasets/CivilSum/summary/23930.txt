Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Punishment - The High Court can intervene with the quantum of punishment given by the punishing authority if it finds that the discipline imposed was outrageously disproportionate to the accusations established - A roadway conductor was charged with charging a lesser fare from passengers on disputed grounds - The Inspector did not record the passengers' testimonies nor did any passenger testify during the enquiry - The delinquent and Inspector's report were the only basis for the punishment of dismissal - The conductor did not pocket the money charged from the passengers - The High Court was correct in interfering with the quantum of punishment and ordering a reinstatement with only 25% back wages.
