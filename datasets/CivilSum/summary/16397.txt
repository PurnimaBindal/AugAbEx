Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Recording of Confidential Reports - Acceptance of fourth representation against adverse remarks after the first three having been rejected held illegal - Adverse remarks originally recorded in the confidential report expunged on fourth representation - Reconstruction of adverse remarks held legal and just -Direction to withdraw benefits given on account of expunction of adverse remarks- Respondents ordered to consider petitioner for promotion treating the adverse remarks as intact until it loses its sting.
