Constitution of India, Articles 309, 16 - Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1964, Rule 5 - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 26(4) - Resignation - Withdrawal of - Refusal due to participation in politics - The Head Mistress in a Primary School resigned to contest a Municipal Election but lost - She then applied to have her resignation withdrawal but was refused on the grounds of her political participation - The court held that at the time of participating in politics, the petitioner was not a government servant - Rejection of withdrawal of resignation was improper - Denial of reinstatement with the same status as other employees is arbitrary and discriminatory - The intervening period can be considered as 'dies non' and thus withdrawal of resignation is permitted.
