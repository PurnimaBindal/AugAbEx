Article 227 of the Constitution of India defines the scope of powers of superintendence, which can be exercised by a court if a statutory authority takes action that is not in accordance with the law.
This Court may exercise these powers if a Co-operative Society, not seeking a writ, approaches this Court as a petitioner to challenge an order made by the Joint Registrar (Audit) Co-operative Society.
The petition is considered maintainable under Section 93 of the H.P.
Co-operative Societies Act, 1993.
[Paras 15 to 17] B.
According to Section 93 of the H.P.
Co-operative Societies Act, 1969, promotion should be given to seniors, rather than create a situation where the junior is promoted first.
The petitioner argues that she was senior and entitled to all benefits granted to her junior when he was promoted.
However, the absence of the junior as a party respondent in this case does affect the maintainability of the appeal.
The appellate authority cannot make certain observations and findings without notifying the junior, affording him an opportunity to explain, and hearing his case.
The order issued by the Joint Registrar (Audit) is cancelled, and the authority is directed to take appropriate action in accordance with the law.
