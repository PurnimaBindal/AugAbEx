Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Cancellation of - Eligibility - Petitioners who were admitted to ETT courses had their admission cancelled as they were believed to be ineligible due to having completed a three year Diploma Course in Engineering instead of passing the 10+2 exam.
The respondents had deemed the 10+2 exam equivalent to the Diploma Course in Engineering since 1991, and many Diploma holders had been granted admission to the course.
The petitioners are seeking to be re-admitted to the ETT Course.
