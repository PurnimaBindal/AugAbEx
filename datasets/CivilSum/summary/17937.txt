Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation - Contract service - Petitioner was appointed on an ad hoc basis for 89 days and his appointment was renewed three times.
The petitioner sought to regularise his services, but it was held that his services were governed by a contract of service.
Therefore, the petitioner is not eligible for the regularisation of his services.
[Paras 5 and 6]
