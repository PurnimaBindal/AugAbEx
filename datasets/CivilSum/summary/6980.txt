The State government under Rule 3 of Kerala Service Rules has authority to withdraw or withhold parts of pension as a punishment for retired government servants regardless if there was any financial loss to the government due to the alleged misconduct of the pensioner.
An imposed cut of Rs. 50/- per month on pension for 10 years as punishment is not deemed illegal or unjustifiable.
