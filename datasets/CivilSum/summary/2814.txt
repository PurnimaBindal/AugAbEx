Constitution of India, Article 226 - Retrospective promotion - Petitioner appealed on denial of promotion to Chairman who ordered review DPC - Petitioner was promoted from the date of review DPC rather than the date when juniors were promoted - Tribunal upheld the petitioner's claim - The order was sustained as promotion given by review DPC would relate to the same period when earlier promotions were made.
[Para 7]  B Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Jurisdiction Scope - High Court cannot interfere under Article 227 unless finding or order is clearly perverse or patently unreasonable - Exercise of power under Article 226/227 should be done only when there is dereliction of duty and flagrant violation of law and in cases where grave injustice would occur unless Court interferes - It cannot be used as appellate or revisional forum.
