Constitution of India - Articles 311, 14 and 16 - Promotion - Central Excise & Land Customs Department Group 'C' Posts, Recruitment Rules - The petitioner's transfer from one Central Excise Collectorate to another did not entitle her to have her service rendered in the former Collectorate counted towards her seniority in the new Collectorate, nor did it allow her past service to be considered for eligibility for promotion to a higher cadre.
The Rule does not specify that the required 5 years' experience as UDC and 13 years composite service as UDC and L.D.C.
must be in one Collectorate, and does not indicate that the period of service requirement is not applicable for those transferred on their own request.
The applicant was permitted to be promoted to Inspector from a previous date and granted all relevant benefits.
