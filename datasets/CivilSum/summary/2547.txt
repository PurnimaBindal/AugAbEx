Article 14 of the Constitution of India - Promotion - Seniority - It is not sufficient for senior officials to have "satisfactory" confidential reports in their service record for promotion - Satisfactorily fulfilling the duties of a lower post does not mean that the official is fit for promotion to a higher rank - There are various other considerations for promotion in higher rank.
[Para 15]
Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India - Promotion - Fitness - Jurisdiction of the court - The court cannot assess the merits of candidates or determine their fitness for promotion - The promotion process falls strictly under the management's exclusive jurisdiction - Unless there are allegations of mala fide, the court cannot intervene in the decision of the promotion committee.
[Para 15]
Article 226 of the Constitution of India - Delay and laches - Promotion - Seniority - The promotion of juniors ignoring their seniors was challenged after more than 10 years following further promotions of both respondents and petitioners - The writ petition will be dismissed because of delay and laches.
