Constitution of India., Articles 226 and 311-A - Gratuity - Delay in payment of gratuity - Normally, the Court would give interest on late payment - But, the petitioner didn't leave official accommodation as per the requirement after retirement - This was a misconduct and the Government could charge market rent and take action accordingly - Hence, no relief can be granted under Article 226 - As the payment has already been made, no additional orders needed - Petition is dismissed.
