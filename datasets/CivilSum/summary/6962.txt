According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, replacing an ad hoc appointee with another new ad hoc appointee is not permissible.
If the post exists and the ad hoc appointment was made according to the prescribed procedure, the appointee may continue until the regular appointee takes their place.
They are also entitled to be considered for regular appointment, even if they become over age which should be condoned.
[Paras 11 and 12] B.
As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, employees who have served for more than ten years and whose initial appointment was not illegal, should be considered by a duly constituted committee for regularisation as a one-time exception.
Even if they become over age, that should be condoned.
