When appointing a Professor of Philosophy, the Selection Committee's function ends once the selection process is completed and proceedings drawn.
Each member of the committee has the right to their independent, unbiased and considered opinion of each candidate.
It is not acceptable to say, after signing proceedings, that they overlooked certain candidate qualifications.
This undermines the sanctity of the selection process and encourages candidates to meet with committee members to try and renege on previously made selections.
B When it comes to academic appointments and qualifications, courts should not ordinarily interfere with the decisions of academic authorities.
The decision of whether a candidate fulfills the necessary qualifications should be made entirely by the academic bodies and the concerned selection committees, which consist of subject experts.
C. In a representation made before the Chancellor, the respondent specifically raised the issue that the appointee did not possess the necessary specialization in the "Philosophical Analysis of Clauses" as one of the qualifications.
The representation was rejected, and it is held that the Chancellor must have examined the eligibility of the appointee and sought advice from experts before rejecting the representation.
