Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 52 - Accident arising out of employment - In absence of any rebuttal evidence, an accident taking place during employment is considered to have arisen out of employment - Death of workman due to furnace fall in factory premises - Employer's claim that it was not an accident but suicide by workman by jumping into furnace - Employer-produced witnesses did not testify to seeing the workman jumping into furnace - Presumption that the accident arose out of the employment can be made.
