Constitution of India, Article 226 - Judicial review - Limits of - High Court using power of judicial review under Article 226 of the Constitution can only assess the decision-making process used by responsible authority and cannot question evidence-based findings during disciplinary proceedings, solely because a different conclusion could be reached.
