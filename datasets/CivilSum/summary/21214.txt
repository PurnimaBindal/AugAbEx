Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-A(2) - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 3 and 4 - Definition of gratuity - The period of "completed year of service" and "continued service" in a seasonal establishment is calculated from the date of employment.
There is no provision for computing the continuous service of an employee from calendar year to calendar year.
The meaning of "completed year of service" is continuous service for a year countable from the date of joining employment, which should not be confused with the calendar year.
The General Clauses Act's definition of a year as a calendar year is not relevant to the concept of "completed year of service."
According to the Act, an employee's continuous service from year to year is computed from the date of joining the job.
It is totally inappropriate to regiment or streamlining the whole concept into calendar year apportionments.
