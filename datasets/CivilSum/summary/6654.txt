Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Electrical) Recruitment Regulations 1965.
Regulation 6(d) - Promotion - Promotion from Executive Engineer to Superintending Engineer – The criteria for promotion is based on seniority cum suitability.
The policy of promotion requires 70% good ACRs during the last 10 years.
The petitioner was not promoted because he could not achieve 70% good or above reports, while juniors to the petitioner were promoted.
The petitioner challenged the ACR and sought promotion from the date his juniors were promoted.
The ACR was recorded on 1141991 for the period 1.4.1988 to 21.1.1989 and conveyed to the petitioner in 1995.
It was held that stale communication could not be acted upon for examining the case for promotion.
The ACR was quashed, and directions were given to consider the petitioner's case for promotion and promote him to the post of Superintending Engineer from the date when the persons junior to him were promoted.
[Paras 9, 17, and 18]

Adverse Remarks - Judicial Review, Articles 226/227 - Recording of ACR is the subjective satisfaction of Reporting Officer.
Unless it is inherently arbitrary and written with a mala fide intention or with extraneous reasons, the recording of ACR cannot be set aside by the High Court while invoking the provisions of Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 19]
