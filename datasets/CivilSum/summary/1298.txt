Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33C(2) - Minimum Wages, Act 1948, Section 20 - Claim of over time wages - High Court directed authority to determine claim within six months of proceedings - An application under section 33C(2) made to Labour Court claiming from a back period is deemed maintainable, as the entitlement has already been adjudicated and mere calculation cannot be termed, determination of right.
