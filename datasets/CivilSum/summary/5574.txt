The Labour Court may set aside an ex parte award if it finds that the order to proceed without notice to the management was wholly illegal and against natural justice.
The High Court will not interfere with this decision.
[Paras 4 and 6]
The management filed an application for setting aside an ex parte award passed by the Labour Court, which was received by them after a delay of several months.
The Labour Court used its discretion to set aside the ex parte award, which was found to be just and proper.
