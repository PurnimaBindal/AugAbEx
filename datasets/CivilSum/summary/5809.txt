Constitution of India, Article 14 - Fifth Bipartite Agreement (Entered into between Indian Overseas Bank and its employees), Clause XVII-A - Voluntary retirement - Notice - Doctrine of reading down - Petitioner bank employee was absent from work without reasons for over 90 consecutive days - The bank issued a notice on 14693 for the petitioner to join duty within 30 days of the notice - The petitioner received the notice on 19693 and joined duty on 14793, not from the date of notice - If a clause stipulated in an agreement is violative of Article 14, it can be struck down - The impugned order is set aside - The respondent-Bank is directed to permit the petitioner to join duty, subject to the condition that the petitioner provides an explanation for their absence.
[Paras 11, 12, 14, and 15]

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Fifth Bipartite Agreement (Entered into between Overseas Bank and its Employees) Clause XVII-A - Voluntary retirement - The requirement of clause XVII-A of bipartite agreement that the delinquent reports for duty within 30 days of the notice - The date of notice must be read as from the date of receipt of notice; otherwise, Clause XVII-A will be liable to be struck down as violative of Article 14.
