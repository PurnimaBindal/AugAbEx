According to Sections 2(b) and 2(f) of the Working Journalists and Other Newspaper Employees (Conditions of Service) and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1955, "newspaper" includes law reports and the contents of law reports are considered "news".
The decision in AIR 1983 Bom 201 has overruled the decision in AIR 1960 Orissa 221.
B The Working Journalists and Other Newspaper Employees (Conditions of Service) and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1955, should be interpreted in favour of employees, as it is a beneficial legislation.
The Palekar Award benefits should be given based on this principle.
