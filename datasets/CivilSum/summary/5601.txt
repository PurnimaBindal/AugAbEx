The transfer of a Governor does not fall under the scope of Articles 310 and 311 of the Constitution of India.
The Governor serves at the pleasure of the President of India and the doctrine of Audi alteram partem is not applicable in the matter of a Governor’s transfer.
The welfare of the people is considered as supreme, as per the doctrine of "Salus-Populi-Supremo-lex".

The doctrine of pleasure is equivalent to "enjoying the confidence of the House" and has two limbs, one "express" which is vested upon the Government either statutorily or constitutionally and another "implied" which is collateral to the statutory and constitutional limb of the express powers.
