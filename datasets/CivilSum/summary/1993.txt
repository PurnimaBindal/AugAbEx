Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Recording of findings - The Labour Court should record its own findings on the basis of evidence available on the enquiry file, even if the departmental enquiry is deemed fair and proper.
- The proportionality of the quantum of punishment awarded by the employer must be considered.
- The Labour Court has the jurisdiction to either reduce or substitute the punishment.
- The Labour Court in this case failed to exercise its jurisdiction under Section 11-A of the Act.
- The case has been remanded to the Labour Court for a fresh decision.
