When promoting an Army Officer, the entire service record is taken into consideration, not just one confidential report.
[Para 7 and 8] B.
In a case where an Army officer has received an adverse confidential report, it cannot be considered arbitrary or mala fide if there is no evidence of animus on the part of the reporting officer.
The complete service record and statutory complaint are presented to the Chief of Army Staff for review, and if the complaint is found without substance, it will be dismissed.
The Central Government also has the authority to dismiss further statutory complaints.
