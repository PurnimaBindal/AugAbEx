According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, a member of the Contributory Provident Fund who received their entire amount upon superannuation in 1987 cannot claim pension benefits by seeking circulars issued in 1984 and 1987, as they were not brought to the petitioner's attention.
The petitioner shifted to the IDA pay pattern in 1979 to receive better pay, so they are not entitled to benefits that the Central Government provides to its employees.
Additionally, since the petitioner's last employer is no longer a government company, they have already received the benefits of the CPF scheme.
