Syndicate Bank Employees (Conduct) Regulations, 1976, Regulations 3(1) and 24 - Departmental Enquiry - Prior to initiating departmental enquiry proceedings against his own officer, the disciplinary authority must have subjective and objective satisfaction regarding the allegations of misconduct made against the employee officer - Utilizing the available material, the disciplinary authority must arrive at his own decision regarding the misconduct in question - It is not acceptable to initiate an enquiry on the basis of directions given by the Central Vigilance Commission or Central Government - If it is found that the decision to initiate enquiry proceedings was influenced by a third party, then it cannot be justified and is considered illegal.
