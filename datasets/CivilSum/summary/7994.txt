In the Contempt of Courts Act case, the contemners argued that they cannot be approved for service unless the posts they are supposed to be appointed to are sanctioned.
They also claimed that an appeal has been filed.
However, it was held that in contempt proceedings, the court's scope is limited to deciding only the issue at hand.
The contemners should request an extension of time or provide a case as to why compliance is not possible in order to show respect for the court.
A defence to contempt by affidavit alone is not acceptable.
Additionally, there is no appeal in the eyes of the law unless the delay is condoned or an appeal filed within time, with a stay obtained.
The contempt was disposed of without penal action, with the directing authority being instructed to proceed with the matter based on the roll strength in creating the vacancy.
Once the vacancy is created, priority should be given to the petitioner herein for approval of their service.
