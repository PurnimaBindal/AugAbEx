According to Article 309 of the Constitution of India, Rule 10 of the Central Civil Services Commutation of Pension Rules 1981 states that commuted pension should be revised and the petitioner is entitled to this benefit as he commuted his pension at a fixed amount.
The rule also provides for revision on the commuted value of pension, and the petitioner must receive an increase in pension with retrospective effect.
