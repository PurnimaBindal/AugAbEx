According to Regulation 19(3) of the Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation Servants (C&D) Regulations, 1971, when determining full back-wages to be granted as an award, it must be considered if the workman has gainful employment.
The Court ruled that income received from agricultural pursuits cannot be equated with income from gainful employment, as it includes self-employment generating income.
Therefore, the original award was modified, and the workman was granted 50% of the back wages.
