Constitution of India, Articles 141 and 215 - Binding Presidents - Reference to larger Bench - Prior to proceeding with a reference on a conflict between two co-ordinate or dissimilar benches, the larger bench must first determine whether there is a conflict.
[Para 13]
Constitution of India, Articles 141 and 215 - Binding Presidents - Reference to larger bench - Decisions made by a co-ordinate bench or larger bench are binding on subsequent co-ordinate benches or benches with fewer judges - It is recommended to maintain judicial discipline and confidence by not differing with the earlier view of a co-ordinate bench unless it cannot be sustained under any circumstances - In that case, it is acceptable to ask for a reference.
[Paras 13, 14, 18, 20 to 25]
