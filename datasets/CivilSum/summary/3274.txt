Interpreting Punjab Civil Services Rules, Pension Act, and Constitution of India, it is determined that the state is liable to pay 100% provisional pension to the retiree until the government sanctions or imposes any cut on the pension.
The mere pendency of an investigation or the state's possibility of withholding or withdrawing a pension is not sufficient reason to withhold it.
Disciplinary proceedings for departmental enquiry cannot continue indefinitely, although a lapse in time or the person's retirement during an enquiry does not inevitably result in the proceedings to be nullified.
Limitations for the commencement of enquiry proceedings after retirement does not result in a violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution, and any delays would have to be judged based on the facts and circumstances of each case.
