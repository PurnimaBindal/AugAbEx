As per Section 3 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, the driver is authorized by the owner to choose a cleaner.
Therefore, the driver acts as the owner's agent in regard to the cleaner.
This establishes the relationship between the injured claimant/cleaner and the owner.
After evaluating the evidence, the claim of the injured claimant/cleaner was found to be legitimate and thus accepted.
