Constitution of India, Article 16 - Equal pay - The claim for equal pay can only be upheld when it is proven that the individuals making said claim are performing equivalent work - This requires the demonstration that their qualifications, job requirements, duties, and extent of responsibility are either the same or similar in nature.
[Para 5] B Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226/227 - Equal pay - Clerks and others petition for equal pay scale treatment with storekeepers - This claim is based on the assertion that their respective positions can be interchangeable - No evidence is provided that storekeepers and clerks have identical responsibilities or job duties - In particular, individuals in charge of storehouses have much greater responsibilities and may be held accountable in cases of inventory discrepancies - The plea that the positions held by the petitioners and those of storekeepers are of equal rank and responsibility is not valid.
