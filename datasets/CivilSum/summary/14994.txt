Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Life Insurance Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1960, Regulation 38 - Suspension during criminal proceedings - Pay and allowances for suspension period - Pay and allowance entitlement not guaranteed in every case - If an employee was suspended due to involvement in criminal proceedings related to their duties or at the employer's instance and later acquitted, they may receive pay and allowances for the suspension and time spent in jail - However, if an employee was personally involved in committing a crime unrelated to their employment and not attributable to the employer, they would not receive any pay or allowances beyond the suspension allowance.
