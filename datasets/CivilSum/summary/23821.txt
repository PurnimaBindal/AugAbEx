The West Bengal Madrasah Education Board received approval to upgrade a junior high Madrasah to a high Madrasah after initiating various proceedings.
Subsequently, the management applied for approval of excess staff for the High Madrasah, but without waiting for the disposal of their application, they filed a writ in HC.
Although some teachers did not possess the required qualifications, the HC directed the authorities to approve the staff for the Madrasah.
The court held that it was inappropriate to substitute itself for the statutory authority and directed the concerned authority to decide on the approval of staff.
[Paras 26 and 29]
