As per the Constitution of India, Article 16 and NBCC (Recruitment) Rules, 1970, Rule 2(b), a petitioner who worked as AAO challenged the review of her ACRs by CMD while being considered for promotion to the post of Administrative Officer.
Despite being considered for promotion in 1993, the petitioner was not selected for promotion that year, although no irregularity in her ACR was recorded.
The NBCC erred in not properly recording the ACRs for the years 1992-93 and 1993-94, and thus could not have reviewed them.
The court directed the NBCC to hold a review Departmental Promotion Committee for the promotion of the petitioner to the post of AO in accordance with the law.
