"Haryana State Supply and Marketing Cooperative Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1988, Rule 15(c) - Compulsory retirement - Adverse reports - Despite having one average report and no bad or adverse service record, the petitioner faced an illegal order of pre-mature retirement based on their ten years of service which included one outstanding report, two very good reports, and six good reports.
This decision is invalid since any adverse entry loses its significance upon promotion to Manager 'A' Grade.
