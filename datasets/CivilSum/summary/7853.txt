"According to Section 45 of the Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, the Pension Scheme allows for withdrawal.
The petitioners initially chose to participate in the scheme but later withdrew and took advantage of the benefits offered.
The question arises as to whether or not the petitioners can be included in the scheme again.
The court has determined that the transaction has been completed and cannot be revisited.
Consequently, the court will not intervene in the implementation of the pension scheme as it has been formulated.
[Paras 13, 15]"
