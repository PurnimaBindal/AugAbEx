Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo) and Schedule II, Item I - Termination of services - Absence from duty - Natural justice - The question of whether termination of a workman's service would amount to retrenchment under Section 2(oo) of the I.D.
Act - The Labour Court held that the termination of services of the workman was legal - A single judge upheld the said order - The medical certificate to prove the illness was found unreliable - The Labour Court has completely disbelieved the medical and fitness certificates - Proper evaluation was lacking - The principles of natural justice were not followed - The Labour Court was directed to decide the reference afresh.
