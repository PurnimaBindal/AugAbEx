According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14, and the Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(bb), 2(oo) and 25-F, ad hoc or temporary employees termination must conform to the terms of appointment order under Section 2(bb) of the Industrial Disputes Act.
Such termination falls within the provisions of section 2(oo) and not Section 25F or 25G, without any requirement to issue notice or provide retrenchment compensation.
They are appointed as stop-gap arrangements until regular employees are appointed, and their appointment ends when duly recruited persons are available.
As such, there is no direction to regularise their services.
[Paras 3 and 14]
