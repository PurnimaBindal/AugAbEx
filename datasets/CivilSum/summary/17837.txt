Punjab Service of Engineers, Class I (Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1964, Rule 9 - Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion/Consideration for promotion/Service record - The petitioner was not considered for promotion on the due date, but was considered at a later date and found fit for promotion based on the same service record.
The grounds of bad service record were not available to authorities on the due date.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to be considered for promotion from the due date with consequential benefits.
