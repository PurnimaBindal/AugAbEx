Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation - Ad hoc appointment - Termination of services of an ad hoc employee on expiry of term of appointment or completion of work for the purpose of which appointment was made - The appointee has no right to claim the post - Appointment of petitioner was made until the regular appointee comes - Even though given a chance to compete, the petitioner failed to meet the selection criteria - Therefore, the petitioner is not eligible for reinstatement and regularisation.
