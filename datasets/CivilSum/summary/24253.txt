The All India Service (Discipline and Appeals) Rules, 1969, Rules 6 & 7 - Recruitment based on a fake caste certificate - Scrutiny Committee report confirmed the certificate was fake - The appointment made with the fake caste certificate was not valid in the eyes of the law, and therefore, the appellant has no claim to the post meant for Schedule Caste - Protection under Article 311 of the Constitution of India not applicable - Ruling based on 1994(6) SCC 241.
[Paras 13 to 16] B. Pensionary benefits - Recruitment based on a fake caste certificate - Valid and legal appointment necessary for entitlement to salary and pension after retirement - Benefits cannot be given on a fraudulent appointment based on a false caste certificate meant for Schedule Caste.
[Paras 18] C. Admission based on a fake caste certificate - Examination completed under interim court orders but certificate later found to be false - Result cannot be withheld or degree denied to the candidate - However, in future, he will not be entitled to any benefits flowing from the Schedule Caste certificate obtained fraudulently - Ruling based on 2001(1) SCT 383 (SC).
