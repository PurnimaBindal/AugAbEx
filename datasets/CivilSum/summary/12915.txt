Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 deals with the quashing of an award regarding the termination of services of a workman appointed on a purely temporary basis.
If the termination order is passed in accordance with the employment contract and falls under exception(bb) of Section 2(oo), the termination order is not void.
In this case, the award passed by the Tribunal was quashed.
