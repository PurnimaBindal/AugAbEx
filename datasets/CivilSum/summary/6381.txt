Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969, Section 13 - Correction in service record of date of birth - Petitioner received birth certificate with correct date of birth, but respondent relied on assessment of age by Medical Board - Determined that date of birth cannot be contested for purpose of prolonging service - Additionally, birth certificate obtained by Petitioner is not legally binding on the respondent as they were not involved in the issuing of the document.
