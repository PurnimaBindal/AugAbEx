According to the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Schedule 2, Item 3, store attendant petitioners who were charged with shortages of receipt of light diesel oil and not checking both the dip rod and loading points covered with lids may be reinstated in service.
In this particular case, the petitioner had been falsely accused of having a false bottom and an M-5 pipe (non-perforated) inserted in the perforated dip pipe and was made to take the blame for the illegal activities of the contractor.
The award of the Labour Court, which was based on no evidence, also failed to show any indication of how the management had lost confidence in the worker, and the management had an alternative method of checking the quantum of delivery.
Furthermore, there was no clinching evidence available, and hence the workman should be reinstated as relief.
