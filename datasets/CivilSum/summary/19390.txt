Regulation No. 20 of the Banking Companies (Acquisition and Control of Undertakings) Act, 1970 allows for the continuation of departmental proceedings against retired employees, even after the date of superannuation.
The petitioner challenged the validity of the amendment made to the regulation, but it was found that the amendment was made in accordance with the directions of the government and with the required approval of the Reserve Bank of India.
Therefore, the challenge to the amendment is without any basis.
