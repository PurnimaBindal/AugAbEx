Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - `Sealed Cover Procedure' - Post of Inspector - The petitioner had failed the list `E' Board test, and as a result, no decision to promote them could have been taken by the D.P.C.
The outcome would not have been different even if the list `E' Board proceedings had been kept in a sealed cover and presented before the D.P.C.
since the petitioner's promotion was always dependent on them successfully clearing the test.
There is no need for judicial review under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
