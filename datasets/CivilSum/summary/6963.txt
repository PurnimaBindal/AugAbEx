Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 311 - Delhi State Industrial Development Corporation Ltd. (Staff Service) Rules, 1978, Rule 44(iv), (viii) - Punishment of dismissal - Appellate authority reduced punishment to stoppage of six increments - Subsequent review of appellate order to dismissal after eighteen months - Holding that the petitioner's substantive right could not be defeated without observation of principles of natural justice leading to a miscarriage of justice - In addition, the appellate authority has no power of review under the Rules - Thus, the petition was allowed.
