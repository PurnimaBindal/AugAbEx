Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Para 173 - Pension Entitlement Rules, 1982, Rules 14 - Disability Pension - The petitioner served in the Army from 1982 to 1998 for a period of 16 years and was invalidated out due to Schizophrenia, after serving at hard stations.
Serving in such places can lead to stress and strain on the mind, resulting in transferable virus and disease, impacting the person's mental health.
As such, the petitioner is entitled to disability pension as per the rules.
