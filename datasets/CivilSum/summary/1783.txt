The transfer of employees from NTPC to BALCO violates rules within the Constitution of India and the Industrial Disputes Act.
However, the terms of agreement, appointment orders and undertakings given by petitioner were all the same, making them estopped from challenging their transfers.
[Para 25]
Despite claims of unreasonableness, arbitrariness, and bias, the petitioners failed to challenge the transfer of certain non-executives on a pick and choose basis.
Challenging these points at the final stage of hearing is not permissible.
[Para 33]
No changes were made to the conditions of service for employees transfered to BALCO.
If there had been changes, the employees would have been at liberty to make a representation.
As it stands, the terms of agreement are not disadvantageous to such employees.
[Para 35]
