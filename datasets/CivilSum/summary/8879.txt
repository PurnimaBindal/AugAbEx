Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Termination/Regularisation - Plaintiff had worked on work-charged basis from 1972 to 1978 in the D.C. Office, Sangrur - A Presidential Order issued on 351977 allowed for regularisation of work-charged employees on completion of one year service, subject to satisfactory work and conduct - To determine suitability for regularisation, the Competent Authority sought reports from officers who had been in charge of the office, and received one report stating that the plaintiff's conduct was unsatisfactory - The plaintiff's service was then terminated based solely on the report of a subordinate officer, with the intention of denying the plaintiff the benefit of the Presidential order - This order was both illegal and mala fide.

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination - Work and Conduct - The burden of proof lies with the Competent Authority when a termination order is passed on the grounds of unsatisfactory work and conduct, not the employee.
