The Army Act of 1950, Sections 23, 39(b), 106 and 116 along with Constitution of India, Articles 21, 226 were applied to a case regarding the discharge of a petitioner through court martial.
The issue of proportionality of punishment arose in the case since the petitioner pleaded guilty of overstaying leave and had been found guilty of a similar charge on a previous occasion.
The court held that evidence should have been taken on the quantum of punishment providing opportunity to the delinquent and gave directions to reconsider the punishment since the earlier punishment was deemed harsh.
