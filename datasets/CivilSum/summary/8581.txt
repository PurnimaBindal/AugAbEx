According to Rule 13 of the Rajasthan State Mineral Development Corporation (Recruitment and Promotion) Rules, 1980, the appellant did not have the required qualifications for the post of Accountant, and had not taken the necessary tests as outlined in the rules.
As no order for the relaxation of educational qualifications had been passed, it was determined that granting relaxed qualifications to one individual could cause serious harm to others, and therefore the appellant cannot receive any special treatment.
