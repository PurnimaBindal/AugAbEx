Rajasthan Education Subordinate Service Rules, 1971, Rules 10 & 10(v) - Age limit/Relaxation of age - Those temporarily appointed within the age limit are entitled to be considered for relaxation of age during regular direct appointment.
However, if already overage when appointed on a temporary basis, one is not entitled to Rule 10(v)'s relaxation.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Appointment/Selection - An eligible person only has the right to fair consideration for selection and appointment.
Even the selection doesn't automatically entitled one to appointment since persons in the select list may be left out for various reasons, which are explained.
