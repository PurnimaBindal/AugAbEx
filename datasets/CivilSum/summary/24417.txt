Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1977, Section 9(9)(a) - Reservation - In the absence of reserved category candidate, the post can be filled on an annual basis from candidates belonging to other categories.
However, if a candidate from the OBC category is appointed due to the unavailability of a BC candidate, the post is considered filled properly.
Additionally, the services of the OBC candidate cannot be terminated to make way for a BC candidate.
[Paras 15 to 18]
