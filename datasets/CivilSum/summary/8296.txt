Constitution of India, Article 226 - J&K Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rules 33, 35 - Termination of Services - Petitioner went on earned leave to visit his sister in the USA but his application for extension was denied after the stipulated leave duration was over, show cause notice was issued and his services were subsequently terminated - Petitioner argued that he was not served with a show cause notice and that no charges were framed against him prior to termination - Held, show cause notice was served, and when no facts are controverted, no charge needs to be framed - Appointing authority can then pass orders based on uncontested facts - Unauthorised absence led to the assumption that he abandoned his post.
[Paras 10 and 12]
