Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(3) and 9-A - Notice of change - Prohibitory orders issued against lockout - The notice of change in the conditions of service by the employer, fixing the ceiling on DA, did not include any mention of staff reduction - Notice of lockout on both demands was found to be illegal - There was no valid reason for the employer to raise a dispute regarding surplus workers in the lockout notice - The order prohibiting lockout was deemed legal.
