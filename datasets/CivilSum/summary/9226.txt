As per the Constitution of India, Article 14 and the Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rules 48-A(1) and (2), an employee seeking voluntary retirement must give notice as required by the rules.
In the case of petitioner Superintendent B/R Grade I who completed 20 years qualifying service, and gave the required notice, there was no communication regarding refusal of permission for voluntary retirement within the specified time frame.
Therefore, as per Rule 48-A(2) of the 1972 Rules, retirement shall become effective from the date of expiry of notice period by operation of law.
