Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Retrenchment of workmen - Award of reinstatement - Challenge as to - Respondent workmen were let go by Mill on the basis that the Mill was closed - Respondents were hired by Appellant Corporation through regular procedure on personal pay scale - Respondents were moved to a Mill as their service was transferable - The Labour Court determined that under the circumstances, the management of Mill cannot terminate respondents' service as they were not its employees.
[Para 4]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Retrenchment of workmen - Award of reinstatement - Grant of pay scale - Labour Court decided to give pay scale to respondents similar to the employees of Corporation - Respondents were appointed on terms and conditions including personal pay - Respondents claimed pay scale only after retrenchment - Under the circumstances, respondents cannot demand pay scale available to similarly situated workmen - Respondents may seek parity of pay with other workmen in future if permissible in law.
