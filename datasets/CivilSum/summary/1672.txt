The Post Graduate Institute of Medical Education & Research, Chandigarh, Act, 1966, Sections 31 & 32 as well as the Rules and Regulations of 1967 provide information on the appointment and powers of the Dean.
The Director is the overall authority of the institution, while the Dean acts as an advisor to the Director.
Despite being a Group A post, the powers and functions of the Dean are not described in the Rules or Regulations.
The appointment of the Dean is made by the Governing Body based on relevant factors, with merit being the primary consideration.
The Director is not solely responsible for the appointment, and allegations of bias or mala fide cannot be attributed to them alone.
[Paras 12 and 13]

According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, selection to higher posts takes into account various factors, including compatibility with colleagues and seniority.
Courts typically do not interfere with selection unless it is mala fide or otherwise illegal.
The recording of adverse remarks, even if later expunged, does not necessarily indicate bias or mala fide.
[Paras 13 and 14]
