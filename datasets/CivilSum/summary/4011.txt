Kerala Education Act, 1958, Section 6 - Execution - Sale of property in execution - The Kerala Education Act is a special law that prohibits the sale of property of aided schools.
The aim is to maintain and conduct schools for the public interest.
This provision will override the Transfer of Property Act, and even sales made through court auctions in execution of court decrees against the institution are included.
Therefore, the school property cannot be sold, not even in court auction.
[Paras 9 and 13]

Kerala Education Act, 1958, Section 6 - Alienation - Sale in execution by the Court - Section 6 of the Kerala Education Act applies to both voluntary and involuntary sales, including court sales that count as 'alienation.'
