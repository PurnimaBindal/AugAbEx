Indian Penal Code, Sections 376 and 342 - Acquittal in rape case - The testimony of the prosecutrix was not believed for various reasons, such as her claim that she was raped by multiple people in one night and had a miscarriage at four months pregnant, which medical evidence disproved, and her inconsistent statements regarding which accused committed the first rape.
[Paras 8, 12, 13, 14] B Indian Penal Code, Section 376 - The presence of injuries is not necessary for determining whether rape occurred and must be determined on a case-by-case basis.
In cases where rape is alleged to have occurred multiple times with multiple individuals but no injuries are present, corroborating evidence is unnecessary if the prosecutrix's testimony is credible.
[Para 6] C. Constitution of India, Article 141 - Precedents in criminal cases cannot be relied upon exclusively and must be considered in the context of the factual scenario.
In criminal cases, precedents regarding the appreciation of evidence are not particularly significant.
