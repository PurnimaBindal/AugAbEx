Regarding the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 25-F and H, an award has been challenged as to its denial of being an employer.
The peon in question did not bring the notice to the Labour Court, resulting in an ex parte award.
However, after five years, the award has been challenged with no mention of the date of knowledge.
The specific finding given by the Labour Court was that the workman worked with the employer for more than 240 days in a calendar year, and the termination was in violation of the provisions of the 1947 Act.
Based on this finding, the award to reinstate with 25% backwages is deemed correct and calls for no interference.
Additionally, the petitioner decided to file an instant petition instead of approaching the Labour Court for setting aside the ex parte award.
