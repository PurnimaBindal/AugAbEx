Constitution of India, Article 14 - Regularisation of service/Qualifications/Increments - A petitioner, who served as Head Master when the school was taken over by the Government in 1969 and was later reverted to the post of SS Master without any conditions, had his case for regularisation of services concluded four days before his retirement in 1986 – after 17 years of service.
The condition imposed on him to receive annual increments once he acquires qualifications of subject combination is therefore arbitrary and illegal since it is impossible for him to fulfill; it is also unfair to question his qualifications after such a long duration of service.
The petitioner is thus entitled to all increments and arrears of salary on that account; costs of Rs. 5000/- to be imposed.
[Paras 4 and 5]"
