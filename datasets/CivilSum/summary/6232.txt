The claim for treating the period of absence of the respondent due to incarceration in a criminal case on duty and paying full emoluments as per standing order of backwages is not valid.
The standing order only applies when an employee is suspended due to departmental or criminal proceedings, and there was no order from the management placing the respondent under suspension.
As the respondent secured re-employment on the condition of not claiming backwages, the management cannot be burdened with paying his salary for the period he didn't work.
