The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Army Act 1950, Sections 20(3), 105 and 106 - Natural justice - It is impermissible to declare an army personnel as a deserter and dismiss him from service under section 20(3) without conducting an enquiry in accordance with Section 106 and without issuing a notice under Rule 17 asking him to surrender or face dismissal.
