Constitution of India, Article 16 - Reservation - Reservation in matters of admission at Post Graduate and Doctorate levels and teaching jobs, for Scheduled Castes and Schedule Tribes - It is not mandatory to specify reserved posts in particular subjects - This is to ensure that candidates from Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes are not deprived of opportunities in case there are no qualified candidates in the specified subject - The principle of reservation should be based on the roster prescribed by statutory rules and the notification should mention the percentage of reservations.
