The Subordinate Service Recruitment Rules, 1981, Rules 3 and 12 and Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 govern promotion and seniority.
The petitioner was promoted against the post of Line Inspector in the unrevised grade of Rs. 220-430 rather than from the post of Line Inspector (Executive) as Meter Inspector in the grade of Rs. 280-520 as per rule.
The petitioner meets eligibility conditions, including being a graduate, having five years of experience as Line Inspector, and holding an ITI certificate.
The court has issued a mandamus for the petitioner to be placed in the grade of Rs. 220-430 as Line Inspector and also be considered for the promotional post of Meter Inspector if it is added to the services.
