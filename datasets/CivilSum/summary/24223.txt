Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal Pay for equal work - Determining whether two posts should carry the same pay depends not only on whether the work is equal, but also on other factors such as the nature and volume of work and responsibilities attached to the post - Stenography at higher level posts cannot be compared to that at lower level posts.

Review - Limited Scope of Review - The Court's authority in review is restricted, as it cannot re-examine the case and completely ignore the original order under review, as this would exceed its jurisdiction.
