The Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16, as well as Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 6.17(3) (revised in 1996), state that the family of the deceased son are entitled to receive family pension and death-cum-retiral gratuity.
Rule 16.7(3) excludes parents from those entitled to family pension, which has been deemed irrational, arbitrary and unreasonable in the case of State of Punjab & another v. Kharak Singh Kang & another, 1998(1) SCT 556.
