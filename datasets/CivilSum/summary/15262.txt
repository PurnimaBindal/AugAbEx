The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 15, and 16, underscores the importance of providing equal opportunities in appointments.
It is not considered discriminatory for General category candidates to have fewer chances to pass the examination compared to Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, and Other Backward Class candidates.
Under Article 16(1), equality of opportunity of employment refers to equality among members of the same class of employees, rather than between members of separate and independent classes.
Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and Other Backward Classes are considered separate and distinct classes from General class candidates.
