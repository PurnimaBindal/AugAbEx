Constitution of India, Article 311 - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, - Order 23 Rule 1 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Jurisdiction - Termination - Civil suit - The plaintiff cannot seek redressal from any other forum without getting the order of the civil court recalled if the suit is dismissed for non-prosecution as per Paras 4 and 5.
As a result, a reference under the Industrial Dispute Act is not sustainable.
