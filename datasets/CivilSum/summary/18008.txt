According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II Rules 5.18 and 326, if a driver has an accident while on duty and becomes disabled, he must be given an alternative job if he is no longer able to drive.
However, a driver's case was not considered for providing an alternative job and was instead retired under Rule 518 of C.s.R Vol.II.
The court has directed that the petitioner be taken back into service on a post that he is able to perform and with the same salary, along with back wages.
