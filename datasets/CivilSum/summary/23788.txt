The Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules, Rules 14, 15 and 17, along with Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 16(4) and 335, indicate that candidates who qualify for a reserved vacancy must still undergo a merit assessment.
Additionally, academic qualifications alone do not suffice for eligibility.
The High Court's order in this regard was not proper, but allegations of malafide and the University's failure to produce selection committee records meant that the court's direction for appointment to Junior Assistant Professor would not be interfered with.
