Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab State Cooperative Audit Service (Class-I) Rules, 1977, Rule 7 - Promotion - Petitioner promoted as Joint Chief Auditor - When the post of Additional Chief Auditor fell vacant, the petitioner was eligible for promotion to this post - Additionally, the petitioner was given the additional charge of Additional Chief Auditor, which means that his service period while holding the additional charge has to be counted towards his experience as Additional Chief Auditor - The government can keep a post vacant only for certain valid reasons - Petitioner ought to be considered for promotion as Additional Chief Auditor from the date the post fell vacant with all relevant benefits.
