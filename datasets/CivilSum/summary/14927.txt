Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 138-A(1) - Disqualification for Misconduct - Lower School Course - Major punishment inflicts bar on admission or retention in List B - Disqualification can be waived subject to special reasons recorded by SP and confirmed by DIG - The petitioner was ineligible to appear for the test because he had been found guilty of being under the influence of liquor while on duty - The petitioner was not even eligible to be included in List B without qualifying for the test - No special reasons justify the waiver of disqualification - The question of the petitioner being re-admitted under Rule 138-A(1) (b) does not arise - The petition is dismissed.
