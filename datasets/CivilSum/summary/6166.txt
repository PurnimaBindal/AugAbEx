Constitution of India, Article 14 and 16 - Ad hoc appointment - Regular appointment - Ad hoc appointees on an 89-day basis have no right to continue in service - The High Court directed for regular appointments to put an end to ad hocism - The petitioner was given a full chance to compete in the regular selection but failed to qualify - All those already working on an ad hoc basis were considered - The employer has the discretion to select candidates from any source including the Employment Exchange - A person who has been given a full chance to compete has no right to challenge the selection process - Selection was made without limitations and not vitiated - The petitioner, being a conditional appointee on an ad hoc basis, has no right to continue after the arrival of regular appointees.
