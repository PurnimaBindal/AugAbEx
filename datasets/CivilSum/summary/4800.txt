As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16(4) and the Punjab Police Service Rules, 1959, Rules 6 and 10, the ratio of 80:20% by promotion and direct recruitment is required to be maintained at the point of recruitment as well as at the point of confirmation and as per vacancies, not posts.
The broken rotation quota rule cannot be allowed to be continued forever, and the quote-rota rule prescribed by the service rules would be followed strictly.
Those promoted contrary to the rule and judgment of the Supreme Court in Paramjit Singh's case cannot claim any legitimacy upon the illegality.
The State Government shall finalize the seniority list within three months while considering the principles mentioned in para 19, such as assigning seniority in the ratio of 4:1 and considering the claim of all seniors for empanelment to the IPS.
The State Government shall not recommend any other member of the Service junior to Shri Lok Nath Angra for empanelment for appointment to the Indian Police Service.
