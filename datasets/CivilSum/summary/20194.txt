According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 309, 311, and 16, a railway employee who retired in 1971 did not initially opt for the pension scheme, instead choosing the Provident Fund System.
However, the CAT granted benefits to employees who retired between 1.4.1969 to 1471972 and had indicated a preference for the pension scheme either before or after retirement.
The union cannot resist the petitioner's claim when the Tribunal has already directed such benefits.
The tribunal passed an order in 1990 in favor of the petitioner who opted for the pension scheme, and the respondent should extend the same benefits as granted to others.
The respondent is directed to accept the petitioner's option and provide the benefits of the pension scheme, while the petitioner is directed to deposit the entire amount received from the Provident Fund System.
