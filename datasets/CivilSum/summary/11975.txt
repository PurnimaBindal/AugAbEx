Border Security Force Act, Sections 19A, 19(2), 22E, 26 - Border Security Force Rules, Rules 167 and 168 - Resignation without pensionary benefits - Challenge - The petitioner had cases registered against them under various sections of the BSF Act, received 4 bad entries, and had a habit of being absent - Despite their personal problems, they tendered their resignation during a trial and verbally requested the Commandant to accept it - After acceptance, the competent authority withdrew proceedings and did not have any motive to coerce the petitioner to sign blank papers - The impugned order is lawful and justified.
