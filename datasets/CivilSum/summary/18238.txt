Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Madhya Pradesh State Industries (Gazetted) Service Recruitment Rules, 1985, Rule 2 - Pay scale - The petitioner, previously holding the position of Inspector (Industries), was promoted to Asst.
Director Industries via the feeder post of Asst.
Manager Industries.
However, they were only granted the pay-scale of Rs. 5000-8000 instead of the sanctioned Rs. 8000-13500 for the Asst.
Director position.
It was ultimately determined that the petitioner is entitled to the pay scale designated for the position of Asst.
Director.
[Paras 5 and 8]
