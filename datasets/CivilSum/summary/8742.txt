Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F, 2(oo), 2(oo)(bb) - Termination - Petitioner's services terminated after expiry of his contract - Petitioner claimed retrenchment compensation and argued that the management violated Section 25-F of the Act - The Labour Court upheld the termination holding that Section 25-F was not applicable as the appointment was on a purely temporary basis - The petitioner's appointment letter stated that the appointment was temporary and his services were likely to continue until 25121989 - The case squarely falls under Section 2(oo)(bb) and the termination amounts to retrenchment under Section 2(oo) - The Labour Court rightly considered this aspect and the award does not warrant interference - The petition is dismissed.
