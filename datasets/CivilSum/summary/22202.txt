Constitution of India, Article 16 - Army Act, 1950, Sections 19, 192 - Regulation for Army (1987), Para 423 - Salary and allowances for suspension period - A petitioner who was dismissed from services after being convicted in a criminal case and reinstated after appeal and acquittal, is not entitled to any salary for the period he remained out of service.
The fact that he was acquitted does not automatically entitle him to receive the salary for the concerned period.
Besides, the period will not count towards pension.
[Paras 7 to 9]
