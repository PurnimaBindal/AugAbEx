According to Panjab University Calendar Volume II, 1995, Regulation 81 and 82, if a person fails to secure the minimum marks required in a sessional paper, they are not necessarily treated as having failed in the theory paper as well.
The petitioner in this case, who secured the minimum marks in the theory paper, would only need to re-appear in the sessional paper in which they failed to secure required marks.
It is the intention of the University Authorities to treat each paper as separate and distinct.
[Paras 4 and 5]
