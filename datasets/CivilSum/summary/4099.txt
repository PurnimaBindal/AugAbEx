According to Sections 2(i) and 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act as well as Section 63 of the Karnataka Agricultural Produce Market Committee Act, the Karnataka Agricultural Produce Market Committee qualifies as an 'industry' under the ID Act, which means that disputes between employees and the committee can be referred to the Labour Court/ID Tribunal and the Labour Court has jurisdiction to pass awards in these cases.
The petitioner's argument that the committee's work is sovereign and regal in nature, and therefore does not qualify as an industry, is not valid, nor is the argument that the fact that the officers' salaries are paid by the Consolidated fund disqualifies the APMC from being an industry.
The APMC Act does not have any provision that excludes the application of the Industrial Disputes Act.
