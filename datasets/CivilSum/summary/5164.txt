Article 226 of the Indian Constitution provides the provision of review when a bunch of cases is decided by a common order.
If a writ was decided wrongly in that common order due to different facts, a review application may be allowed.

Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution address reservation policies and the consequences for applicants who change their category from general to reserved.
Candidates who belong to the reserved category and initially applied under the general category cannot claim the benefits of reservation later on.
