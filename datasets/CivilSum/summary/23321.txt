Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - A candidate can only start the job on presenting a valid discharge certificate from their current employer - The respondent, an officer in Canara Bank and one of the candidates recommended by UPSC for the job of Assistant Provident Fund Commissioner in the Employees Provident Fund Organization, did not obtain a vigilance clearance certificate and Integrity certificate, which prevented the appellant from issuing the appointment letter without a relief order from the respondent's employer - The High Court's order directing the appellant to issue the appointment letter was unjustified and therefore, set aside.
