Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Qualifications - Promotion - The authority that is responsible for setting qualifications can also modify them - An amendment that widens the pool of eligible candidates for the position of General Manager can't be deemed arbitrary or outside the jurisdiction of that authority.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Merit - Selection for promotion - Promoting to General Manager of the Corporation by selection - The Promotion Committee scrutinized their education and professional qualifications, as well as their confidential report and service record, but did not find the petitioner to be the most suitable candidate among the available ones - It cannot be considered arbitrary or illegal to promote individuals who have a better service record and overall performance.
