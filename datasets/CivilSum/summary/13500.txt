Interpretation of Law - Statutes and rules should be interpreted based on the plain meaning of their words, unless there is more than one possible interpretation.
[Para 2] B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Industrial Establishment (National and Festival Holidays and Casual and Sick Leave) Act, 1965, Sections 3 and 5(2) - Compensation for workers who work on holidays - Workers who work on a holiday allowed under Section 3 are entitled to double their daily wage as compensation, either in the form of money or one paid holiday plus their daily wage for that day.
A Labor Court modified its award to state that workers can receive either double wages for the holiday or one average daily wage and a substituted paid holiday within 90 days, at their own discretion.
