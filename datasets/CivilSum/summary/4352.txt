Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 Maharashtra State Financial Corporation Staff Regulations, Regulation No. 3(2) - Natural Justice/Domestic Enquiry - The delinquent official was asked to give statement first, followed immediately by written statements of prosecution witnesses along with documents from the file.
The employee was also asked to cross-examine at the same time, causing a bad sequence of statement recording.
This violates the principles of natural justice as the charged official should not be examined first, thus the employee was not given a proper opportunity for cross-examination.
As a result, the punishment and the Award of Labour Court held for the enquiry fair and proper were quashed, and the case remanded back to the departmental authorities to proceed in accordance with the law.
