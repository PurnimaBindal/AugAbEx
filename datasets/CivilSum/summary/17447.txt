Punjab Police Rules, Rule 16.2 - Service matter - Alcohol - The consumption of alcohol by a constable who is not under the influence of alcohol does not constitute the "gravest act of misconduct."

Punjab Police Rules, Rule 16.2 - Alcohol - A respondent suspected of alcohol consumption was taken for a medical examination, which revealed that although he smelled of alcohol, his pupils and conjectives were normal, indicating that he was not under the influence of liquor.
