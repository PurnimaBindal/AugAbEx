The petitioner, who started on a temporary basis in 1950, had his service confirmed with effect from 1.4.56.
There is no evidence to suggest that he was a contingent employee before 1959, and his service book has been maintained since 1950.
Therefore, it is unfounded to say that the service he provided as a respondent from 1950 to 1956 cannot be included in calculating his pension.
