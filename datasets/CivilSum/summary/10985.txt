Haryana State Supply and Marketing Co-operative Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1969, Rule 23(a)(5) - Eligibility for Promotion - Managers `A' Grade can be filled by promotion from Field Officers and Managers B Grade - Senior Sales Officers with a higher pay scale remain eligible for promotion to Manager `A' Grade - Failure to consider the appellant for promotion despite eligibility ruled a violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - Executive order can correct wrongful promotion of a junior over a senior.
