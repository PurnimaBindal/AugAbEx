Constitution of India, Article 16 - Qualification for appointment - When inviting applications for appointment to a post, suitability and eligibility must be judged with reference to the last date of receiving applications, unless otherwise specified - An individual acquiring qualifications subsequently is not eligible.

Constitution of India, Article 16 - Order of appointment by authority - A tribunal invalidated an appointment without making a determination on the correctness of the claim of the individual concerned - The tribunal's decision was not justified - The matter was referred back to the tribunal for a new decision.
