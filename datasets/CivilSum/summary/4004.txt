According to Sections 2(l) and (m) of the Factories Act, 1948, the applicability of working hours is a point of contention for workmen working as Retail Outlet Technicians (ROTs), especially with regards to the number of hours they work.
However, it should be noted that attending to work at a petrol pump or retail outlet installations do not automatically classify ROTs as factory workers.
Even if employees of a company are assigned work at a factory for a certain period, they cannot claim allegiance of service at that factory since the provisions of the Factories Act are not applicable to them.
[Paras 20, 28, 29 & 30]
