Pension Regulations for the Army, 1961, Regulation 164 - Pension - The appellant was enlisted in the Army on 18740 and discharged on 21.2.54 for being surplus to the establishment.
He was denied service pension as he had not completed 15 years of service.
In the new case, the appellant claimed he is covered by Regulation 164 of the Pension Regulations for Army, 1961.
However, the ingredients of Regulation 164 were not mentioned, and as a result, the appellant is not entitled to any benefits.
[Paras 3 and 5]
