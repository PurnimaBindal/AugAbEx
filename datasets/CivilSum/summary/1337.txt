Constitution of India, Article 226 - Correction of Date of Birth - The petitioner did not challenge or question the date of birth that was recorded in their service record and continued to serve until their superannuation.
Towards the end of their career, the petitioner moved for a correction of their date of birth based on a medical examination and a certificate of having passed their primary examination.
However, such a practice was not deemed proper and was deprecated.
