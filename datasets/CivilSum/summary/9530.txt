Constitution of India, Article 226 - Andhra Pradesh Revised Pension Rules, 1980, Rule 9 - Andhra Pradesh Civil Services (classification, control and appeal) Rules, 1991, Rules 20 and 21 - Disciplinary proceedings - De novo enquiry - Prior to ordering a fresh enquiry, the disciplinary authority must record reasons for being unsatisfied with the previous enquiry report - Ordering a fresh enquiry may be necessary due to a material defect in procedure or if some important evidence was not available during the previous enquiry - The disciplinary authority cannot simply discard an enquiry report because it does not appeal to them on merits - An enquiry report that was submitted as a result of an illegal direction from the disciplinary authority is not legally valid.
