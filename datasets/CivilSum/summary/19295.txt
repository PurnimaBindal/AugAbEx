According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, the Selection Committee approved by the Board of the University is responsible for selection and appointment.
The petitioner in this case was placed at Serial No.5 and candidates at Serial Nos. 2 and 3 failed to join.
The Vice Chancellor does not have the right to refuse issuing appointment letters to the selected candidates that have been approved by the University Board.
Once candidates above the petitioner failed to join, he was entitled to the appointment.
