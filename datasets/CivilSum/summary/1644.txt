Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 14- Criminal Procedure Code, Section 300 - Double jeopardy - Acquittal - Criminal proceedings - Employee charged with the same offences in both Criminal Trial and Departmental Proceedings- Departmental proceedings cannot proceed if he is acquitted honourably on merits in the criminal proceedings - However, if acquitted on technical grounds like a defect in sanction to prosecute or of limitation, the competent authority has the discretion to still proceed to establish his misconduct and suitably punish him - If acquitted due to non-production of witnesses by the prosecution despite opportunities, it amounts to acquittal on merits, not just on technical grounds.
[Paras 10 and 11]
