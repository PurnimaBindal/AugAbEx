According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution, admissions for medical and MD courses should not require a viva voce test.
However, for admissions to post doctoral super-speciality courses, it is not always necessary to assess personality traits like in civil services if aptitude, academic performance, and capacity to work hard have already been established during MBBS and MD studies.
The decision of the University to cancel the viva voce test was considered authentic in order to ensure an objective selection process and eliminate any arbitrariness.
[Paras 10 and 11]
