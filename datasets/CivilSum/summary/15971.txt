According to the Constitution of India, as per Articles 14 and 16, employees of Trusts and Government Departments within the State of Punjab are entitled to equal pay for equal work.
Petitioners, who are Assistant Engineers/SDEs working in the Improvement Trusts in the State of Punjab, claim pay scales equal to their counterparts in the Government Departments of the State.
Previously, their pay scales were the same.
The State Government has given approval for the trusts to provide the same pay scales to employees as those revised and sanctioned by the State Government.
Petitioners are performing duties equal to their counterparts working in the Government Departments and therefore deserve equal pay as drawn by their counterparts in the P.W.D.
(B&R) Department of the State.
The fact that they sometimes issue cheques and maintain cash books in Sub Division does not constitute a significant difference in their duties.
[Paras 3 and 4]
