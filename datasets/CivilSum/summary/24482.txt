The appointment of adhoc apprentices made in violation of the provisions of Adhiniyam is void as per UP Industrial Disputes Act, Section 6-N and U.P.
Nagar Mahapalika Adhiniyam.
However, in considering the legality of termination and granting relief, the provisions of the Industrial Disputes Act need to be taken into account.
In this case, Section 6-N of UP Industrial Disputes Act was not complied with, and therefore, reinstatement cannot be granted.
Instead, compensation of Rs. 30,000/- should be paid.
[Paras 25 & 29]
