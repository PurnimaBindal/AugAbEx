According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, and Regional Rural Bank Act, 1976, Sections 17 and 29, the Regional Rural Bank (Appointment and Promotion of Officers and other Employees) Rules, 1988, Rules 3 and 12 state that promotions are based on seniority-cum-merit and the bank has the power to devise criteria for selection.
The bank must set benchmarks and give preference to seniority in the selection process.

In regards to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, and estoppel, candidates participating in the selection process must be fully aware of the criteria and are not allowed to challenge the selection process by claiming the criteria to be bad.
