Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Natural Justice/Enquiry/Dismissal from service - The High Court found that the enquiry leading to the delinquent officer's dismissal was incomplete and did not give the petitioner a fair and proper opportunity to defend themselves.
As a result, the authorities were ordered to conduct a new enquiry with full opportunity for the petitioner to defend themselves.
However, the enquiry officer did not provide a sufficient opportunity for the petitioner, allowing only cross-examination and defence witness examination, and submitted a supplementary report instead of a fair opportunity.
The dismissal order was quashed, and another enquiry officer was appointed to provide a proper opportunity for the petitioner.
[Paras 10 to 12 and 14]
