Under Sections 3(1) and 102 of the Punjab Gram Panchayat Act, 1952 and Section 197(1) of the Criminal Procedure Code, Sarpanch of village Gram Panchayat is regarded as a Public Servant.
For his trial, sanction under S. 197 Cr.PC is required.
[Para 8]
In the case of an ex-Sarpanch of village Gram Panchayat, who committed embezzlement while he was Sarpanch, his prosecution can take place even when he is no longer Sarpanch.
Sanction under S. 197 Cr.PC is not necessary in such cases.
[Paras 20 and 21]
When a Sarpanch of the village Gram Panchayat commits embezzlement, uses inferior materials in construction, and fabricates records, his act is directly related to his official duties.
Therefore, sanction for prosecution under S. 197 Cr.PC is required.
