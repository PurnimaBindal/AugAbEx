Evidence Act, 1872, Sections 67, 68 - Succession Act, 1955 (39 of 1925) Section 63 - Will - Burden of proof - The responsibility of proving a will rests on the individual who claims to be the propounder of the will - The propounder must account for any and all circumstances that may be considered questionable.
[Para 11]
Pleadings - The propounder of the Will has claimed that the testator left their house to them in recognition of their services, however, this claim is not mentioned in the plaintiff's statement - Absent from the plaintiff's claim, even significant evidence cannot provide proof.
