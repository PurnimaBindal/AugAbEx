Civil Procedure Code, Order 21, Rule 1 - Specific Relief Act, Section 34 - Declaratory Decree - Execution of - Back wages - When a declaratory decree is made, it merely declares the position of the decree holder with regard to the judgment debtor and does not as such direct the judgment debtor to perform any specific action - The executing court cannot take any step outside or beyond the terms of such a decree - Thus, the decree holder must pursue any further remedies available to him through his claim for declaration - As the Execution application did not contain any order relating to back wages, the executing court was correct in dismissing it.
[Para 4]  B Back wages - Gainful employment - The employer had presented the workman's passport, indicating that he was employed during his termination, with no contradicting evidence provided by the workman - As a result, back wages were lawfully denied.
