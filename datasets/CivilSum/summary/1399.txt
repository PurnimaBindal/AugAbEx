Article 227 of the Indian Constitution grants certiorari jurisdiction to the High Court, which may interfere with labour court awards only if they lack jurisdiction or violate rules of natural justice.
Even where there is an error of law, there may not be interference unless it is made evident.

Under sections 2(oo) and 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, fixed-term contracts are permissible, and appointments expiring after three months can continue without extension.
Termination after 240 days of employment is not covered under section 2(oo)(bb) of the Act, but compliance with Section 25-F is required.
[Para 7]  C Should a worker abandon their post, it should be demonstrated with material evidence, as per Industrial Disputes Act sections 2(oo) and 2(oo)(bb).
In this case, the worker did not voluntarily abandon their position.
[Para 8]  D If a dismissal breaches Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, the worker is entitled to reinstatement with back wages, unless the employer can prove that the worker was gainfully employed during the dismissal.
