Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Evidence Act, 1872, Section 114 - Appointment - Presumption - Recruitment to the post of Conductor after being sponsored by employment exchange - The petitioner was not selected for the position on the grounds that he did not have a subsisting employment exchange registration at the time of recruitment, despite persons who had secured less marks than him being selected.
However, the petitioner is entitled to selection on merit as his sponsorship by the employment exchange presumes the subsistence of his registration, and is therefore entitled to attendant benefits such as seniority, increments and terminal benefits.
No back wages are to be paid.
[Paras 1 and 2]
