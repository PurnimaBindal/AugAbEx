Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation of service - The appointment of the petitioner was not consistent with the requirements of Articles 14 and 16(1) of the Constitution.
Therefore, no mandamus can be issued to direct the respondent to consider the petitioner's case for regularisation as no legal right is vested in the petitioner.
[Para 6]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation - Writ of mandamus - A writ of mandamus cannot be issued to the State or instrumentalities of State to regularise an employee's service, unless the appointment was consistent with Articles 14 and 16(1) of the Constitution.
The appointment must have been made through an advertisement and considering competing claims of eligible persons in the market.
Any back door entry appointment cannot confer a right on an employee to claim regularisation.
[Paras 5 and 6]  C. Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ of Mandamus - In order to obtain a writ in the nature of mandamus, a legal right must exist, and only then can the respondents be asked to perform their legal duties.
