Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3(1)(b) - Compulsory retirement - The authorities must have sufficient material on record to conclude that it is not in the public interest to retain delinquent employees in service due to adverse remarks in their Annual Confidential Reports.
If a representation made by the employee is considered and rejected, and they are unable to prove that the entries were illegal or unconstitutional, the order of compulsory retirement is justified as being in the public interest rather than a punitive measure.
This is a well-established principle as per the 1992(2) SCT 92 (SC) case.
