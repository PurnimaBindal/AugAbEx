The Board has a formula for promoting technical and non-technical employees to the position of Junior Engineer, which involves promoting in a ratio of 1:1 from seniority lists partly maintained for both categories.
However, this formula discriminated against other categories and infringed upon their right of equality.
The Board was directed to discontinue this system of promotions and make all promotions from a common seniority list based on the common seniority of technical and non-technical staff.
[Paras 10 to 13] B.
A writ petition regarding promotions can only be dismissed due to delay and laches if the delay is abnormal and unjustified.
If the petition is filed immediately, relief can be granted with retrospective effect and the old position restored.
However, if there is abnormal and unjustifiable delay, the relief can only be prospective.
