Rajasthan High Court Ordinance, 1949, Section 18 - Air Force Rules, 1969, Rule 15(2)(g)(ii) - Policy for discharging habitual offenders among Airmen in the Indian Air Force - AOP approved the recommendation for discharge under Rule 15(2)(g)(ii) of the Rules - The airman's discharge was strictly in accordance with the instructions - The contention that the response to the show cause notice was not considered was wrong - Stressed that the underlying principle of the policy was to remove undisciplined individuals from the force, and that the discharge was not a punishment for misconduct.
