Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - Service element of disability pension - The petitioner served the army between January 23, 1969, to September 23, 1972 - Discharged in medical grounds with a categorisation of `EEE' - Received disability pension till August 20, 1974 - Despite having a reassessed disability of less than 20 per cent, the petitioner is eligible for the service element of disability pension as per Amarjit Singh's judgement - The petitioner can claim 7% pa interest on arrears of pension from the date of writ petition submission.
