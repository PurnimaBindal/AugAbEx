Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay scale - Senior employees in the same cadre performing the same duties should not receive lesser pay/pay scale than their juniors, unless valid reasons exist - Equal pay for equal work is a constitutional goal, although not a fundamental right - Failure to recognize this principle would lead to irrational classification - Article 14 should be interpreted with this principle in mind.
