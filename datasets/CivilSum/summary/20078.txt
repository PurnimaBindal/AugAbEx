Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservation - Appointment - Appointment to PCS posts - In the event that candidates belonging to BC-A from a specific zone are not selected, the reserved posts should be offered to BC-A candidates from other zones.
This approach aligns with the aim of providing sufficient representation in the service to the specific group in the proportion contained in the roaster, and the same principle applies to Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe candidates.
