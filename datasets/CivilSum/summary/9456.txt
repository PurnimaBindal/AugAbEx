The Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Act, 1994 states that eligibility requirements for the Teacher Grade III position include evaluating the candidate's merit.
A petitioner argues that his improved marks on a secondary examination in 1997 should be considered instead of the original examination he passed with a supplementary in 1983.
However, this argument is not considered valid as it would violate Article 14 of the Constitution of India and create inconsistencies in grading.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to any relief.
[Paras 15 & 16]
