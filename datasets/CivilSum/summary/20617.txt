Employees working in the state of Nagaland were not entitled to draw House Rent Allowance, as clarified by the order of the President of India, which specified that the entitlement was only for those employees who were within the eligibility criteria prescribed in the OM dated 281960.
The Tribunal's conclusion that all employees posted in Nagaland were entitled to rent-free accommodation was incorrect, and there needed to be a decision from the Government entitling them to house rent or rent-free accommodation.
