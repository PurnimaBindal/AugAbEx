Constitution of India, Article 13(4) - Rajasthan Police Service Rules, 1954, Rule 6, Item A - Promotion - Selection scale - When a senior scale officer is granted selection scale - No higher status or rank is given to his post, and nominating a senior officer to a higher pay scale does not constitute promotion.
[Para 13]
Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 309 - Rajasthan Police Service Rules, 1954, Rule 8 - Reservation - Granting of selection grade does not involve any appointment by promotion and there is no separate post in the selection scale under the rules - Thus, there is no question of any vacancy or reserved vacancy - A post must exist first to have a vacancy available.
[Para 22]
Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 309 - Rajasthan Police Service Rules, 1954, Rules 8 and 33 - Selection scale - Seniority - Posts of Additional Superintendents - Selection grade is granted only to a certain number of holders of the post, which does not count as promotion - Seniority is based on date of appointment as Additional Superintendent - If a junior officer is granted the scale over a senior officer, it will not affect seniority for promotion beyond the post of Additional Superintendent - An officer holding the selection scale will be considered at the original seniority in the senior scale when promotion is available, regardless of whether they received the selection scale earlier than a senior officer.
