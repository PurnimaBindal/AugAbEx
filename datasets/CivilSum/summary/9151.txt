The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Penalty/Natural justice - If there is only one permissible penalty and one possible conclusion based on undisputed or admitted facts, Article 226 of the Constitution does not require the court to interfere.
[Para 5]
UP Directorate of Education Ministerial Service Rules, 1983 - Promotion/Reversion/Natural justice - The District Inspector of Schools cannot appoint or promote candidates to the post of Senior Assistant (Superintendent Grade II), which can only be done by the Additional Director of Education (Secondary).
The Director of Education (Basic)'s order of promotion to this post on March 13, 1989 was without jurisdiction and the conclusion was clear.
There was no violation of natural justice requiring interference.
[Paras 4, 6 and 7]
