Constitution of India, Article 16 - Absorption in service - When a private school was taken over by the Government, Art and Craft teachers were not absorbed in service - Due to the subject of Art and Craft no longer being in the curriculum, three out of the five recruited teachers couldn't be absorbed - Learned Single Judge issued directions to absorb them on different posts in different schools, which was affirmed by the Division Bench - As the petitioners have been in service for the last 13 years and are now ineligible for any fresh appointment in any State due to their age, it would be unjust and unfair to uproot them.
