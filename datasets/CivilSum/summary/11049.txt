The AP State Road Transport Corporation issued a notification to recruit 400 conductors and appointed 255 candidates from the empanelled list, leaving remaining candidates without appointment orders.
Petitioners challenged this action, citing discrimination and deprivation of the right to livelihood as implicit under Articles 14, 16, and 21 of the Constitution of India.
The court agreed with the petitioners and directed the Corporation to appoint them and issue posting orders within two months as the action of the Respondents was discriminatory and violative of Articles 14 and 16.
