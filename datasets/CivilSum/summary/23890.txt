Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Natural justice - Reversion - The Court should refrain from making unnecessary observations on subjects not in issue before it - The authorities cannot take adverse actions against employees based on such comments made by the Court without providing adequate opportunity - The withdrawal of the petitioner's absorption as Head Master with retrospective effect, based on erroneous observations of the High Court and without giving proper chance, violates natural justice principles - Orders from the High Court and authorities are quashed.
