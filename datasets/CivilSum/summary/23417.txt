Haryana Government instructions No. 3442-3 G.S.-II/71/19169 dated 1371971- Appointment on compassionate grounds - The son of a deceased police constable appointed as Clerk on compassionate grounds - It is not justifiable to appoint him as ASI or Inspector as these were promotional posts.

Writ of Mandamus- High Court accepted respondent's claim and directed the State to appoint respondent within 3 months - However, the order of the High Court is not valid - According to the rules, the High Court can only direct the consideration of the respondent's claim - It cannot directly order for the appointment - This direction does not fall under the scope of Mandamus.
