Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part-I, Rules 4.4(a)(i), 4.14(2)(i) & (ii), 313, 314, 316 and 248 - Pay fixation - Seniority - Officiating service will count towards seniority and all purposes in the parent cadre - It is mandatory for the senior to receive a higher pay than the junior - A Govt. employee holding substantive and permanent post retains lien on it while holding another post in officiating capacity - He is entitled to all benefits of his substantive post - His junior in that cadre cannot be fixed in higher pay than him - Instructions are not valid if they are in conflict with the rules.
