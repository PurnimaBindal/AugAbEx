Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226/227 - Selection - Appointment - State Village and Industry Organizer - To be eligible, candidates must hold a graduate degree and possess at least five years of administrative experience, as well as experience in working on a responsible post related to Khadi and Village Industries.
Respondent No. 4 met the requisite qualifications for the post and was considered eligible.
The petitioner was also called to take part in the written test, but opted not to appear.
After considering the claims of all candidates, including the petitioner, the respondent was selected and appointed to the post based on their overall performance in both the written test and interview.
The petition was dismissed.
