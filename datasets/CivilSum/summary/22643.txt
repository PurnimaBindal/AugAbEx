Bihar Police Manual, Rule 663(d) - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 12 - Public appointments - Termination of service - The appellants were appointed as constables in violation of recruitment rules in 1990.
As such, their appointments were deemed nullities and are subject to cancellation.
Despite having served for 14 years, the Supreme Court dismissed their appeals.
However, the court suggested that their cases be considered for future appointments, and any age restrictions may be relaxed for them.
