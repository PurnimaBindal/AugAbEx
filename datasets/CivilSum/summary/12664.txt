The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 grants regularisation and pension rights to workcharge employees.
Employees must complete qualifying service in order to receive pension, but the state policy states that service of workcharged employees will be regularised after a certain period.
Failure of authorities to follow the policy will not prevent an employee who retires after completing qualifying service from receiving pension.
Such an employee will be deemed to have been regularised from the date when they were supposed to be considered for regularisation, and their entire service will be computed towards qualifying service when fixing and distributing their pension.
[Para 3]

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 state that age cannot be considered a bar for regularisation.
This was confirmed in the case of 2000(4) SCT 460.
Workcharge employees seeking regularisation should not be denied based on their age.
