In Section 117 of the Border Security Force Act, it is stated that an order of dismissal may be appealed.
However, it has been found that some appeals have been dismissed without a proper application of mind or consideration of the petitioner's arguments.
Therefore, such dismissals have been set aside and the respondent has been directed to provide a more reasoned order.
The petitioner has also been awarded costs of Rs. 5000.
