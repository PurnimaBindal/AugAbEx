Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Service of Engineer, Class-II, P.W.D.
(Building and Roads Branch) Rules, 1965, Rules 6(1) and 7(2) Promotion/Qualifications - The petitioner lacked certain qualifications at the time of his appointment as Draftsman, but was later promoted to higher positions.
After 25 years of service, the department cannot deny his promotion to Assistant Engineer on the basis of not having a diploma in Civil Engineering.
Rule 7(2) only requires 10 years of experience as Draftsman and qualifications only need to be met at the time of appointment to the post.
The department must evaluate the petitioner's fitness for promotion and grant him all benefits if deemed fit.
The petition has been granted with costs.
