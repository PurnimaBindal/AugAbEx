Constitution of India, Article 226 - Quashing - Jammu & Kashmir Rural Development Subordinate Service Recruitment Rules, 1990 - Quashing of order whereby two grades of Inspectors of Panchayat were created by respondents - Petitioners also demand "equal pay for equal work" - Respondents argue that since only petitioner 5 holds the post of Panchayat Inspector in grade I, the petitioners are not entitled to bring the petition - Court holds creation of two grades of inspectors of Panchayat to be valid - The principle of "equal pay for equal work" cannot be applied as the petitioners have not established themselves as equals - Petition is dismissed.
