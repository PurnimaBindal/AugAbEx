Civil Procedure Code, Order 2 Rule 2 - Subsequent petition - Although an earlier petition filed claiming continuation in service was rejected, a subsequent petition after termination of service claiming pension as per entitlement is not barred by Order 2 Rule 2 CPC since the claim or cause of action qua pension was not available at that time.
[Para 5]
Civil Procedure Code, Order 21 Rules 23 & 24 - Remand - An order of remand should be necessitated only if sufficient material is not available on record to dispose of the case finally without a remand, and if there is a pure question of law involved whether appellant is entitled to pensionary benefits or not based on a determination as to what should have been his date of retirement, the appellate court can dispose of the same.
[Para 6]
Jammu and Kashmir Civil Services Regulations, Volume I, Regulations 177, 177(A) and 226 - Jammu & Kashmir Work Charged Employees Service Rules 1972, Rule 15 - To be entitled to pension, an employee holding a substantive post on the permanent establishment on the date of retirement or holding temporary officiating, including temporary service, must have rendered service up to the age of 60 years.
If wrongly retired prior to that contrary to rules, he will be deemed to have retired at the age of 60 and his pension will be determined and computed accordingly.
