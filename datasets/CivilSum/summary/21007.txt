Andhra Pradesh (Andhra Area) Estate (Abolition and Conversion into Ryotwari) Act, 1948, Sections 5(2) and 11(a) - Suo motu power - Exercise of - Reasonable time - The reasonable time frame for exercising suo motu power will be determined by the unique circumstances of each case - In this case, the Settlement Officer unlawfully granted a ryotwari patta and subsequently, interested parties destroyed the records - The Director of Settlement utilized suo motu power to set aside the order granting the patta - The High Court erred in restraining the Director from exercising the power based on a chronological limitation alone.
