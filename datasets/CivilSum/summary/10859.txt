Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rules 17 and 23 - Discharge - Appeal - Reinstatement - Back wages - An order of discharge was challenged before an authority and through writ - During the appeal and writ petitions, the authority confirmed the petitioner's discharge - Later, the court directed the authority to decide the appeal - The appeal was dismissed, termination upheld, and it was challenged through writ - Although the writ was allowed, back wages were denied - The petitioner is entitled to back wages from the date of termination as the authority confirmed the petitioner, and therefore, the discharge order and appeal would not survive, and the impugned orders are not sustainable.
[Paras 5, 8 and 10]
