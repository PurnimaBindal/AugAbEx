Panjab University Regulation Volume III(2003) Chapter XVI - Rustication of students - Petitioners, students of MBBS, attacked another student with weapons, injuring him - Petitioners claimed that they were not given a chance to speak before the rustication order was issued - The court found that the petitioners were wanted in a criminal case and were not available - The college was entitled to infer the involvement of the petitioners and legally debar them - The writ court would not interfere.
[Paras 16 and 19]  B. Disciplinary proceedings - Criminal case - Past conduct - Criminal and departmental proceedings can proceed simultaneously - Proof required in disciplinary proceedings is not the same as in criminal proceedings - Departmental action may be drawn from evidence and circumstances based on probabilities - An action may be based on past conduct.
