The Industrial Disputes Act Section 10(1) outlines the function of the appropriate government in making a reference in cases of dispute.
The government's role is purely administrative and does not involve evaluating the merits of the dispute.
However, it is within their rights to see if a prima facie case of dispute referable under Section 10 exists.
If the government declines a reference on the grounds that the termination was in accordance with the law, the order would be without jurisdiction.
To rectify this, a direction was issued to pass a fresh order according to the law.
