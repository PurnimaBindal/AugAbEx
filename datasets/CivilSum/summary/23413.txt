Constitution of India, Article 14 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 - Reservation - Estoppel - Admission in Medical College - The Respondent-Trust funded the Hostel attached to Medical College, and the Government permitted the Trust to nominate students up to 10%.
However, the Government could terminate this arrangement at any time.
Just because the Government contended in 1974 that the arrangement was valid and binding, it doesn't mean that the Government cannot change its position when it realizes the arrangement is against Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
This was confirmed in the case 1993(3) SCC 645.
