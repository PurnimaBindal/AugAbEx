Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Seniority - Natural justice - "Audi Alteram Partem" - Petitioner's seniority was previously settled above respondent No. 4.
However, after five years, her seniority was lowered without providing her with a hearing.
The order that disturbed her seniority has been set aside, and both the petitioner and respondent No. 4 will have a hearing to determine seniority.
If it is determined that the petitioner is senior to respondent No. 4, she would be entitled to the senior scale given to respondent No. 4.
[Paras 4 and 6]
