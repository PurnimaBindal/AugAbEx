The appellant, a Stenographer Grad-II in Food Corporation of India's Eastern Zone, requested a transfer to the Southern Zone on 771975.
In writing, the appellant stated that he would not have a lien in the Eastern Zone and requested his seniority to be fixed in the Southern Zone from the date he joined.
However, the appellant was promoted to Stenographer Grade-I in the Eastern Zone in 1972.
As a result, the appellant is entitled to promotion in the Southern Zone from that date and will be considered to have been transferred as a Stenographer Grade-II.
