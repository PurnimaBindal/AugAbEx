Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Regularisation of Services - Equal pay for equal work - Petitioner, appointed as Counter Incharge Apprenticeship with Haryana Tourism Corporation Ltd, claims regularisation and pay scale of Rs. 1200- 2040 - However, the petitioner does not possess essential qualifications for the said post, and qualifications are laid down by an employer for a purpose - Mere continuation in service on stipend basis does not confer the right to claim regularisation - Petition rejected.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]
