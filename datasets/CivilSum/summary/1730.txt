In the case of unauthorised absence and disciplinary proceedings resulting in the punishment of removal, the proportionality of the punishment must be considered.
The petitioner in this case had 26 years of unblemished service prior to the misconduct and there was no charge of misutilization of funds or loss suffered by the corporation.
However, the petitioner did abstain from duty for a significant period of time and did not participate in the enquiry proceedings.
The punishment of removal was deemed harsh and wholly disproportionate, and was therefore modified to voluntary retirement with the condition of paying Rs. 1 lac as costs of enquiry proceedings.
The period from the date of punishment until voluntary retirement is to be treated as dies non.
