Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Education matter - Admission - The appellant requested admission to an Engineering course by claiming reservation benefits as a member of the Scheduled Tribe, but his claim was rejected upon verification of his Caste Certificate by the Scrutiny Committee.
The High Court upheld the decision and dismissed the writ petition.
The Court ruled that the appellant cannot claim any future benefits for being a member of the Scheduled Tribe and shall not retain any financial benefits of his education if any privileges were awarded by the State.
If the determined amount is paid by the appellant, he may retain his degree.
