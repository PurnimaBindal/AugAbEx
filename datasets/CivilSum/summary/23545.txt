Civil Procedure Code, Sections 9 and 11 - Rajasthan Shops & Establishments Act, 1958, Section 28-A - Res judicata - Orders passed by the Civil Court under Order 39 Rules 1 & 2 will not be considered res-judicata before the authority under the Act - These orders are not final and can merge in the final order - Appellate courts cannot allow plea of res-judicata if not raised before the trial court - The jurisdiction of the Civil Court or the Civil Code does not bar the authority under the Act - The authority has powers to determine fact and law and condone delay.
[Paras 10 and 12]
