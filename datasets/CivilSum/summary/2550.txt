Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Contract Act, 1872, Section 23 - Termination - The terms and conditions of the contract did not contain a clause regarding termination - The clause introduced much later had no mutuality and did not specify how the service could be terminated - The clause was void under Section 23 of the Act due to being an unconscionable bargain and violative of Article 14 of the Constitution.
[Para 28]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 311 - Termination - A confirmed employee must be given a reasonable opportunity before termination - The petitioner alleged that he was not given notice, a hearing, and was victimized without fault - These allegations were never rebutted - A confirmed employee cannot be terminated without following the principles of natural justice - Violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.

Delay and laches - Parties had been corresponding and making offers and counter-offers for considerable time - The petitioner had been pursuing the matter with vigour - Held, it cannot be said that he was guilty of laches.

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Superannuation - Re-instatement - The petitioner had reached the age of superannuation, therefore re-instatement cannot be ordered - Ordered that he would be entitled to all benefits which would have accrued to him had he been in continuous service of the Corporation.
