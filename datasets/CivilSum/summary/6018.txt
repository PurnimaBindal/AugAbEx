According to the recommendations made by the Medical Council of India on Post Graduate Education, attendance from training cannot exceed 20% and maternity leave cannot be used as an excuse for short attendance.
The rationale behind this rule is that the residency system course can only be benefited through strict adherence to the course schedule, and any deviation from this rule could have negative effects on course standards.
The University is not given the option to deviate from this rule even when it comes to maternity leave.
These recommendations are binding and cannot be overlooked.
