Constitution of India, Article 311 - Allahabad Bank Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976, Regulation 6(4) - Enquiry-Admission of guilt by the employee - The finding on each charge must be recorded - There is no provision that if the charges have been admitted, no enquiry will be held - Admission may not always eliminate the need for an enquiry - But even if no enquiry is necessary, the proviso to Regulation 6(4) must be followed strictly.
