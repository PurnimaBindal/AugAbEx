Bihar Finance Service (Appointment through Selection) Rules, 1990, Rule 5 - Appointment-Eligibility-Post of Commercial Tax Officer - Despite the appellant not being confirmed in a class III post on 1491, he appeared in the examination and clearly stated in his application that the matter of his confirmation was still pending with the State Government.
However, in 1994, an order of confirmation was passed with retrospective effect from 1121985.
Thus, it was held that the exclusion of the appellant from consideration on the basis of merit could not be sustained.
The Public Service Commission was directed to consider the case of the appellant and declare his result within two months.
If found successful, the appropriate appointment order was to be issued with a prospective effect.
[Paras 4 and 5]
