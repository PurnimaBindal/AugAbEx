Haryana State Supply and Marketing Cooperative Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1988, Rules 13 & 16 - Promotion - Seniority-cum-Merit - Promotion to the post of Assistant Secretary - When considering individuals for promotion, Rule 13 & 16 do not differentiate on the basis of pay scale - The person with seniority in the length of feeder cadre service has the better entitlement - Despite being senior, the petitioner was promoted after their junior, but still has a claim on the promotion date of their junior.
