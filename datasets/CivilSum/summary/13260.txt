Constitution of India, Article 226 - AP Revised Pension Rules, 1980, Rules 8 and 9(2) - Punishment - The rules state that action can only be taken against a retired employee in accordance with Rule 9(2) - Rule 9(2) allows the State to impose punishment and not the disciplinary authority - In this case, the punishment was imposed by the Chief Executive Officer, making it illegal - No inquiry was conducted prior to the punishment - The petitioner's punishment order is set aside.
