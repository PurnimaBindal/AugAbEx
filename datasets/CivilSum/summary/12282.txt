Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(oo)(a), 2(p), 12 and 25-F - Resignation - Settlement - Workers resigned during strike - Settlement arrived at between their Union and the management before the Conciliation Officer allowing to join only those who did not resign or whose services were not terminated - Petitioner admitting his signatures on the resignation but disputing it to be voluntary - No such dispute raised at the time of settlement - The dispute cannot be considered belatedly - Allegations of coercion or fraud should have been brought to the notice of the Conciliation Officer at the time of settlement - The award upholding the petitioner's resignation is upheld, and the petition is dismissed.
