The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21, 226, 309, along with the Army Act, 1950, Sections 48, 80, 81, and the Army Rules, 1954, Rule 14, pertaining to Red ink entries, discharge, show cause notice, and termination orders, have been violated in a case where the procedure was not followed and no reasonable opportunity was provided to give one's case before the termination order was passed.
The right to livelihood is included in the right to life under Article 21, making the termination order not sustainable.
