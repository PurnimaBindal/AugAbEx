Constitution of India, Article 311 - Natural justice - Enquiry Report - The petitioner was accused of marrying twice while his first wife was still alive - The Enquiry Officer found that the charge was not proven and no misconduct was committed - However, the punishing authority removed the petitioner from service based on the same findings, without giving any reasons for disagreeing with the Enquiry Officer's findings and without providing an opportunity to the petitioner - Even though the appellate authority issued a detailed order, it could not correct the flaw - The punishment order has been overturned.
