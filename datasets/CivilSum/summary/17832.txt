Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion/Reversion - It is mandatory to consider seniority while promoting or reverting - If a person is not found suitable for promotion once, he is still deserving of consideration every time his juniors are promoted - Failure to do so would render the action illegal and liable to be undone - If he has been promoted already and later if he is found unsuitable, he cannot be reverted until his juniors are in the higher posts without considering his case again.
