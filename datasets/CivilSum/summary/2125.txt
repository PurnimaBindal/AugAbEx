The petitioner in this case challenged a reduction in pay that occurred after 7 years of continuous service.
The petitioner, a cleaner/sweeper, was appointed based on the sanction of the Inspector of Schools, but without the approval of the Government.
The pay reduction to a lower consolidated sum was deemed illegal, especially since the petitioner was not afforded an opportunity of hearing.
The petitioner cannot be made to suffer for the fault of authorities, as a constitutional right to enjoy a scale of pay of Rs. 820-1175 is enforceable.
As a result, the petition was allowed.
