The seniority of direct recruits should be determined according to the order of merit assigned by the Commission and as per Rule 10 of the Punjab Educational Services (Class II) Rules, 1934.
No amount of administrative instructions issued by the government can give rise to a claim that seniority of reserved category employees should be determined ignoring their merit.
Therefore, the plea that seniority of the petitioners should be determined as per the roster points cannot be accepted.
[Para 9]

The conditions of service of general category and reserved category employees are regulated by the same set of rules.
Different provisions cannot be applied to the members of service belonging to the same cadre for the determination of seniority.
[Para 10]

The roster is only used to earmark vacancies for Scheduled Castes etc. After the selection process is complete, appointments are given to selected candidates from both the general and reserved categories.
The roster ceases to operate after the appointments are made.
Therefore, the plea of the petitioners that roster point should be read as a part of the seniority rule and they should be assigned seniority according to roster points irrespective of their merit position is not tenable.
