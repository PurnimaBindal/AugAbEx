According to Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, and Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol-II, Rule 2.2(b) and 9.14(a), an officer cannot be served with a charge-sheet for events that took place more than four years prior to the institution of departmental proceedings, if not initiated while the officer was in service, whether before his retirement or during his re-employment.
In this case, the petitioner was served with a charge-sheet for acts committed in 1983-84, long after his retirement in 1998.
Therefore, the charge-sheet cannot be sustained under the law, and the writ petition is allowed and the charge-sheet is quashed.
