Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary proceedings - Writ jurisdiction - Judicial review - The scope of the writ court's jurisdiction is supervisory in nature.
It cannot reassess evidence to determine if the Enquiry Officer's findings are correct or not, unless there is no evidence.
Even if some of the findings are unassailable, it is not for the writ court to consider whether those findings alone weighed with the authority in passing the final order of punishment.
[Paras 4 and 6]
