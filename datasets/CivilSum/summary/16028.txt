The determination of seniority for the cadre of Assistants should be based on the length of continuous regular service and not on the date of confirmation, according to Punjab District Subordinate Service Rules, 1942, Rule 16 and Haryana Revenue Department District Subordinate (Group C) Service Rules, 1988.
The seniority list and show cause notice that determined seniority based on date of confirmation are considered illegal and quashed.
If a rule has two interpretations, the interpretation that is constitutionally intra vires should be preferred over the one that renders it constitutionally infirm, in accordance with the principles of interpretation of statutes.
