Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary proceedings - Criminal proceedings - Stay of departmental proceedings pending criminal case - The petitioner, who is charged of receiving illegal gratification, filed a prayer to stay the department proceedings while the criminal trial is pending - However, it is held that allowing departmental proceedings to continue would not prejudice the petitioner as the standard of proof in both proceedings is entirely different - Moreover, charges against petitioner are serious and departmental proceedings against a member of disciplinary force like the Police cannot wait endlessly till the conclusion of trial - Hence, departmental proceedings cannot be stayed at this stage and doing so would only serve the interest of the accused.
