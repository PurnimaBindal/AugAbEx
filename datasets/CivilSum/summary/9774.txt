Section 33-C of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Claim for excess salary payable on promotion - A petitioner, who qualified as a Chartered Accountant and was promoted from the position of audit clerk, was placed at the beginning of the salary scale for fresh Chartered Accountants resulting in him receiving almost the same emoluments - Questions regarding whether the petitioner received an unfair deal as a Chartered Accountant or whether he ceased to be a workman are not within the scope of Section 33-C(2) of the Act - The application does not determine the petitioner's pre-existing rights and therefore does not fall within the ambit of Section 33-C of the Act.
