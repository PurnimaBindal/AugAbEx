Rajasthan Ground Water Board Services Rules, 1969, Rule 2(g) and Rule 5 - Rajasthan Public Service Commission (Limitation of Functions) Regulation, 1951, Regulations 11(l), (5) and (6) - Rajasthan Civil Service (Absorption of Surplus Personnel) Rules, 1969, Rule 7(3) and Rule 1(2) - The RPSC must be consulted for the promotion of an officer under the purview of the Commission.
With provisions of Sub Rules (1), (5) and (6) of Regulation 11 read together, so as Sub Rule (1)(b) is not rendered otiose and redundant, the Absorption Committee must consult the RPSC for the officer's suitability for promotion.
As the petitioner was not substantively appointed to the post of Assistant Engineer under the Ground Water Department without consulting the RPSC, his appointment to the position is invalid, and all subsequent orders based on his appointment are also invalid.
The authorities are directed to consider his case under the rules accordingly.
