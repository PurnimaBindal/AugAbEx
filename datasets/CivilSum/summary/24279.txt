The establishment of a university, including deemed universities, and the regulation of admissions and standards of higher education fall under the control of the University Grants Commission (UGC) as per Entry 66 of List I Meanwhile, the State Government has jurisdiction to frame its own regulations and conditions of admissions for institutions falling under Entry 25 of List III.
The State cannot exercise power over the admission procedure for deemed universities, as it is exclusively governed by Entry 66 of List I The UGC is not only for making grants but also has the authority to create a university and give it special status.
As for the concept of "coordination," it includes the harmonization of actions according to a certain design, scheme, or plan of development, and this would involve the process of admission.
The State is estopped from declaring that a Deemed University's essentiality certificate will no longer cater to local needs if its coverage is enlarged to inter-states, for this goes against the university's original purpose.
