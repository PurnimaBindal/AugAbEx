Indo Tibtan Border Police Force, Act, 1992, Section 74 - Indo Tibtan Border Police Force Rules, Chapter XIV, Rules 174 to 177 - Absence without leave - A court of enquiry is mandatory for absences without permission lasting 30 days or more, and is permitted to consider any document as evidence without formal proof, provided that reasons are recorded.
If the accused employee fails to reply to charges of unauthorised absence or resigns from service while in touch with the proceedings, the court of enquiry and competent authority may presume abandonment of job.
[Paras 10 & 11] B Natural justice - Notice - If an employee is in touch with the court of enquiry and receives ample opportunity to appear through appeals, the plea of actual service of notice is not available to them.
