Eligibility criteria for admission to B.Ed.
course states that only candidates with at least 45% marks in their qualifying examination are eligible to appear in the entrance test.
The petitioner had sat in the entrance test before the declaration of her B.Com.
result, in which she obtained 378% marks.
However, she later submitted her result to the college authorities and continued her studies.
Despite not knowing about her eligibility at the time of the entrance test, her application form was forwarded to the university and she was allowed to take the exam after obtaining a court order.
Her result has been declared and she passed in the first division.
The court directed that her result be declared on a regular basis and the petition was allowed on the basis of Constitution of India, Articles 226/227.
[Paras 5, 6 and 8]
