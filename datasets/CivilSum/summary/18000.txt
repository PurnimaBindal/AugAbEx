Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punishment order/Speaking order - The punishing authority is obligated to provide reasons for their decision to impose punishment on a delinquent official - Even when a petitioner files a reply proposing removal from service, a stoppage of one increment punishment cannot be imposed without substantial reasons - The Board did not provide any reasoning in the meeting minutes or the order itself - The delinquent official must be informed of the reasons for their punishment - Since no speaking order was passed, the punishment has been invalidated and the opportunity given to pass a new speaking order.
