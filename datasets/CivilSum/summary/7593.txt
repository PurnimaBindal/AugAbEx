Constitution of India, Article 21 - Central Services (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1944, Rule 2(e)(ii) & (iii) - Bronchitis and Asthma - Medical reimbursement for Oxygen Concentrator - The list of equipments and appliances specified in instructions dt. 31.5.1985 and in the rules is not complete - If a patient's Chronic Bronchitis and Asthma are declared dangerous to their life and the Head of the Department of a Govt. Hospital recommends an Oxygen Concentrator for life, there is no reason why the cost of the equipment cannot be reimbursed, as many other equipments already are - The Authority is directed to provide reimbursement.
