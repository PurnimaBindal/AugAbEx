Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Rajasthan Recruitment of Dependants of Government Servant (Dying while in Service) Rules, 1975, Rule 2(F) - Compassionate appointment - Petitioner seeks appointment in dying in harness category on basis of mother's death - Petitioner was a minor at time of mother's death - Father maintained and educated petitioner - Petitioner cannot be considered dependent on mother - Therefore, petitioner is not entitled to appointment.
