Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 and 227 - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 - Employee who was a bank employee and had taken a house building loan from the bank, found guilty of causing loss to Bank and retired compulsorily - Bank not paying the gratuity amount and adjusting it towards loan - According to the judgment, Bank's action of adjusting the gratuity amount towards the outstanding loan amount was lawful and an employee cannot enforce his right for post-retiral dues in writ jurisdiction in such circumstances.
Reference made to 2000(3) SCT 19 (P&H) case.
