Constitution of India, Article 14 and 16 - Admission in medical courses against free seats - The admission committee is the competent authority to make admissions and allot seats - The principal of the medical college cannot disregard the recommendations of the admission committee and adjust seats against free and paid seats at his own discretion - The college is required to refund the difference in fee charged from candidates who were adjusted against paid seats, contrary to the recommendations of the committee for free seats.
[Paras 11 and 12]
