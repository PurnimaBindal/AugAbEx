Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Promotion - Promotion is only a consideration and not a guaranteed right for an employee.
If the employee's service record does not show him suitable for promotion, he cannot claim promotion.
[Para 3]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion - An allegation of junior employees being promoted without proper documentation cannot be considered valid and is too vague to be accepted.
[Para 4]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Transfer - The order of transfer is made in emergencies of service and should not be questioned.
