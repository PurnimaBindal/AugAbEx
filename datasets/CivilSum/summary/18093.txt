Punjab Police Rules, Rule 1321 - Constitution of India, 1950, Article 14 - Withdrawal of Rank - Repatriation - The petitioner's rank was granted erroneously while on deputation and the impugned order does not disclose a reason for the same, therefore, the petitioner does not have a vested right to retain the rank.
The withdrawal of the rank would not be considered arbitrary by the government.
[Paras 8 and 11]

Punjab Police Rules, Rule 1321 - Constitution of India, 1950, Article 14 - Power of relaxation - Promotion - The order must state valid reasons for promoting a police official to a higher rank when exercising power under Rule 1321.
[Para 8]

Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14, 16 - Administrative Act - Bona fide mistake - The government has the authority to correct a bona fide mistake at any stage while following the principles of natural justice.
