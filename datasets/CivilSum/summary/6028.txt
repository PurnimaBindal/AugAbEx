Kerala Education Rules, Rule 51-B - Dying in harness - Compassionate appointment - There is no deadline for filing an application for the dying in harness scheme - Rule 51-B's purpose is to provide employment to dependents - Rule 51-B can have a future effect when it comes to submitting an application - Whether the death happened before or after Rule's introduction is irrelevant - The petitioner is validated in submitting an employment application.
