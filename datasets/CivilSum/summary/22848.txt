Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation of Service - Repatriation from Group `C' to Group `D' service - The appellants' last drawn pay in Group `C' post will be protected and the period spent in ad hoc Group `C' post will be counted towards requisite length of service.
Relaxation in age is possible if required.
