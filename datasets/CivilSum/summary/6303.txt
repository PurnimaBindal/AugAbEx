Punjab Punishment and Appeal Rules, Rule 9(4)(i) - Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Enquiry report - It is mandatory for the punishing authority to supply a copy of the enquiry report to the employee along with the 2nd show cause notice - This is a corresponding right and obligation - The judgment in Mohd.
Ramzan Khan's case does not apply - The order of punishment passed without a copy of enquiry report is illegal.
