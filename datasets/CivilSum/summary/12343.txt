The Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Sections 12, 13 and 25 mandates the Registration and Licencing of Contractor, with contravention of sections 12 and 13 resulting in penalty under sections 23 and 25.
In addition, workmen employed through unlicensed contractor in an unregistered establishment will be considered as the workmen of the principal employer.
