Constitution of India, Article 226 - Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 10 - Absorption of contract labour in regular service of principle employer - Notification U/s 10(1) prohibiting employment of contract labour does not result in automatic absorption under any provision or implication under the Act - It only allows the labour to pursue their right to be absorbed as direct employees and cannot be adjudicated on by the High Court on disputed facts - The appropriate course of action is to refer the issue to an industrial Tribunal for adjudication - High Court directions for absorption of workers as direct employees is not supported by precedent in Steel Authority of India's case 2001(7) SCC 1 : 2001(4) SCT 1, which overruled Air India's case.
