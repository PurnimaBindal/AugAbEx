Civil Procedure Code, Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 - Transfer - Cancellation of transfer - Petitioner was transferred to Sonepat College as a Lecturer in 1991, with the approval of the Director Higher Education and the University, as a mutual transfer from Rohtak College.
The petitioner worked at Sonepat College until 1997 when the Secretary of the newly elected Managing Committee cancelled the petitioner's transfer.
The cancellation was challenged on the grounds of mala fides and an interim stay was granted by the Trial Court and then vacated by the lower appellate court.
The order of the lower appellate court was set aside as the merits of the case are yet to be explored.
However, the Trial Court was directed to dispose of the suit itself expeditiously.
