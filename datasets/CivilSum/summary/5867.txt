Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Findings of Labour Court - The High Court can reverse the Labour Court's wrong findings in its writ jurisdiction, if it incorrectly appreciates evidence on record - Management presented the Muster Rolls to demonstrate that the workman did not complete 240 days of work, but the Labour Court still took an adverse inference and ordered reinstatement with continuity - The award of the Labour Court has been quashed.
