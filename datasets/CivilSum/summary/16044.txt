The case of Re-Evaluation falls under the jurisdiction of the 1984 Prospectus for M.B.B.S/B.D.S.
examination, which does not include any provision for the Re-Evaluation of Answer-Books, and nor is it covered by Ordinance Re-evaluation of Answer - Books Rule 1(b), as Medical College students are debarred from re-evaluation.
Also, since MBBS/BDS Entrance examination is a public examination, re-evaluation cannot be allowed of right, as it may cause uncertainties.
The petitioner's answer books were re-checked in accordance with the provisions of the prospectus, and no mistakes were found in the result, which was conveyed to the petitioner.
Therefore, the petition was dismissed, as per Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
