The Official Secrets Act (19 of 1923), Section 3(1)(c) and the Army Act (46 of 1950), Section 123 both concern mercy petitions.
In one specific case, a petitioner was found guilty of providing maps and other secret information to the enemy, thus endangering not only the lives of Jawans but also the country.
The petitioner was dismissed from service, and the rejection of their mercy petition and denial of retiral benefits were deemed appropriate.
