Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Casual workers - Regularization of services - "Canteen employees" - Single Judge affirmed the prayer for regularization of services - Bank provided free accommodation, electricity, kerosene, and fuel to the canteen committees run by the employees controlled by the Bank - Single Judge rejected the Bank's contention of having no say in the service conditions of petitioners engaged by various canteen contractors - Canteens were determined to be run by Bank - Order and judgment of Single Judge supported.
