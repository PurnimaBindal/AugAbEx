Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regular Pension - Commuted Pension - Despite a writ petition staying the order for recovery of special pay from the petitioner, the granting of regular pension and commutation of the same cannot be hindered - As the amount to be recovered has already been deducted, it is unjustifiable for the respondents to not pay regular pension and commute the same - A directive is given for the payment of regular pension, commutation, and payment to the petitioner.
[Paras 5 & 6]
