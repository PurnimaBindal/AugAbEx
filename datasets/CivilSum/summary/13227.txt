Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Higher pay-scale - Qualification - The petitioner was appointed as a Science Teacher but obtained a Master's degree in Bengali - Teachers who have attained higher qualifications in their respective subjects are eligible for higher pay scales - However, the petitioner has not shown any attachment to the Bengali subject - Therefore, they are not entitled to the benefit of a higher pay scale.
