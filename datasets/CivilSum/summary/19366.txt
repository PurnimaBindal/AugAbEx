The Prospectus of the Kurukshetra University's M.B.A. course outlines that rounding off the decimal percentage of more than 05 to the next higher integer is only applicable to determining eligibility to appear in the Entrance Test.
The petitioner who did not secure the minimum requirement of 50% marks in the Entrance Test cannot claim the benefit of Clause 2 of the Prospectus to round his marks to 50%.
[Paras 10, 11 and 12] B The Kurukshetra University only gave ad hoc additional marks in subject marks to those who had otherwise achieved the minimum requirement of 50% marks.
The petitioner failed to secure the necessary 50% aggregate marks, and no one was granted additional ad hoc marks towards the aggregate 50% marks.
As a result, the petitioner's discrimination allegations against the authorities are unfounded.
