The plaintiff-respondent was dismissed from service by an order passed by Senior Superintendent of Police, Hoshiarpur.
The trial court granted the suit by judgment and decree.
However, the defendants filed an appeal with an application under section 5 of the Limitation Act to condone the delay of 45 days.
The District Judge dismissed the application, and the appeal stood rejected.
Consequently, the defendants-petitioners filed a revision in the High Court.
The delay in filing the appeal was due to a difference of opinion between the LR and the Home Department.
The High Court condoned the delay and remitted the case to the District Judge to decide the appeal on merit in accordance with the law.
