Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Every individual has the desire to occupy a superior position - Promotion is a valuable right - Petitioner, who was senior to Respondent No. 5 as Safai Mazdur, should not have been denied promotion - The promotion of Respondent No. 5 happened without considering the petitioner's claim for promotion - The promotion of Respondent No. 5 violated Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
