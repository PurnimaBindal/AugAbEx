Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Integrity doubtful reports - Performance assessment - Specific mention of absence of corruption allegations means the authorities were satisfied that the officer's integrity is not in doubt - Grading above average not `C' - For promotion, the officer cannot be considered as having integrity doubts.
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Service record - In normal cases, five years' service record must be considered but eight years' record in case of `integrity doubtful' reports/entries.
- Consideration of eight years' service record without `integrity doubtful' entries vitiated the DPC proceedings - Authority directed to reconsider only five years' record without treating any entry as `integrity doubtful'.
