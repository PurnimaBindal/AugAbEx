Under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1978, Sections 3(2) and 2(9), and the Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Rules, 1981, Rules 5(1) and (5), only one head of school may be appointed by the management of a minority institution, and approval must be obtained from the Education Department even if the person being appointed satisfies the categories listed in Section 2(9).
In cases of Minority Institutions, the Education Department only verifies whether the person is qualified to be appointed the Head of the school.
Furthermore, the protection of the Act is available to person claiming to be entitled on the ground of seniority, as in the case of an Assistant Teacher promoted as Supervisor in an institution covered by section 3(2) of the Act.
The Tribunal's order directing an appointment based on seniority was upheld.
