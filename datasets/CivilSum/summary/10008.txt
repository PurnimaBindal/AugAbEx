A retired Brigadier was appointed as Secretary of Rajya Sainik Board after being selected and recommended by a high-powered Selection Committee.
The petitioner could only be terminated if the post was abolished or if the petitioner was unable to hold the post.
However, the petitioner was terminated without any reason or notice which was found to be violative of the Haryana Civil Service (Punishment & Appeal) Rules, Natural justice, and Articles 14, 16 and 311 of the Constitution.
Termination of temporary appointment before the period ends would prima facie be considered as punishment.
Under Article 310, there is no difference between the individuals holding permanent or temporary posts.
While instructions are to be followed, guidelines are kept in mind at the time of making appointments.
Service conditions cannot be changed to the employee's disadvantage.
Not in accordance with guide-lines appointment can bring a change in the service condition without affecting the tenure of appointment.
The post of Secretary of Sainik Board is to be manned by an officer of the rank of Brigadier duly selected by the selection committee.
The termination of the petitioner without any rhyme or reason was deemed arbitrary and violative of Articles 14 and 16.
It is essential to maintain public interest in all State actions, and the scope of Article 14 and 16 extends beyond complaints from public servants with rights to a post.
