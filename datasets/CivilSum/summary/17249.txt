Education - Admission - Re-valuation - Petitioners students of Govt. Polytechnic, studying for diploma in Civil Engineering and petitioner No. 3 for Electrical Engineering must have complete pass in all subjects of Second Semester as necessary pre-condition for admission to the 5th Semester - The success of all three petitioners in the revaluation is relatable to the original examination published in June 1997 - Revaluation does not count as re-appearance in the same subject, and the result of the revaluation must relate back to June 1997 when the entire examination results appeared - Respondents must declare results of petitioners of 5th semester examination, consider admission of petitioners to 6th semester, and recognize that petitioners are entitled to regular admission to the 5th semester.

Education - Admission - Re-valuation - Attendance - Failure of petitioners to complete the required 75% attendance does not disqualify them from gaining admission to 5th Semester - Petitioners' lack of attendance was not their fault but occurred due to the examiners' failure to evaluate their answer scripts properly initially - Since this fault was rectified by the University in the revaluation, petitioners are entitled to all the benefits that accrued to all candidates who passed in the results published in June 1997, and they can't be penalized based on their failure to meet the maximum attendance requirement - The lecturer permitted them to attend the classes, recognizing their success in the revaluation.
