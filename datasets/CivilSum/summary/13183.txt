The Labour Union of the Mill requested that Management be prevented from terminating the services of daily rated workers and from hiring contract labour in accordance with Section 10 of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970.
The settlement between the Union and Management regarding the employment of casual and contract Labour prevented any objections.
However, the terms of the settlement were deleted from the copy produced by the Union before court, and the Engagement of contract labour when work prohibited to be done by Contract Labour is not against Law.
The Petitioners did not come to Court with clean hands.
[Paras 14 and 17]
