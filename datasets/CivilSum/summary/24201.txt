Voluntary Retirement - Cut off date - The cut off date for calculation of benefits under the Voluntary Retirement scheme is the date on which applications of employees who have opted for voluntary retirement are accepted, not the date of their actual relief from service.

Voluntary Retirement - Pay for notice period - The rights and benefits available to employees under a particular VRS should be examined in light of the specific terms and conditions governing it.

Voluntary Retirement - Withdrawal of - Once an employee has accepted voluntary retirement in accordance with the scheme or rules, it cannot be withdrawn.
