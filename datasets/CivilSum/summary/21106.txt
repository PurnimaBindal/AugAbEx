"In accordance with the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, and 141, promotions with respect to reservations shall be determined based on seniority in roster points.
The seniority of reserved and unreserved employees in the lower category will be taken into account in the promoted category, despite a prior promotion through the reservation process.
The allocation of roster points is intended solely to ensure adequate representation of backward classes in various service levels.
RK Sabharwal and Ajit Singh-I cases were given prospective effect according to Ajit Singh-II and MG Badappanvar's cases.
It is therefore directed that the Tribunal reconsider the claims accordingly.
[Paras 1, 2, 14 to 17]"
