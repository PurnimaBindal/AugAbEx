Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Enquiry Officer - Bias - Dismissal - Enquiry Officer demonstrated his intention to act on behalf of department rather than to act as fair Enquiry Officer - No opportunity given to rebut correctness and genuineness of two documents - Held that fact finding report of Enquiry Officer is vitiated as charges not been proved - Report of Enquiry Officer holding petitioner guilty liable to be set aside - Petitioner reinstated with 50% back wages.
[Paras 12, 13 and 15]
Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Domestic proceedings - In domestic proceeding being quasi judicial proceedings, suspicion cannot replace proof.
[Para 12]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Domestic Enquiry - Writ jurisdiction - Writ court cannot appreciate or analyse evidence adduced in domestic enquiry but in certain cases, after going through materials if writ court finds that on the basis of material no reasonable prudent man can come to conclusion that charges have been proved, court will interfere with this report and enquiry proceedings.
[Para 14]
Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Natural justice - In departmental proceedings authority has to follow fair procedure conforming principle of natural justice at all levels and all stages - Orders of punishment set aside.
