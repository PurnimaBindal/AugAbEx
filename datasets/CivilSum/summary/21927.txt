Constitution of India, Article 16 - Casual Labourers (Grant of Temporary Status and Regularisation) Scheme of Government of India, 1993, Clauses 4 and 6 - Casual labour - Appointment - Retirement - Deemed regularization and grant of retiral benefits to casual labour - Not permitted - The respondent was a casual labourer who had acquired temporary status under the 1993 scheme, but the scheme specifically provided that the conferment of temporary status does not bring the worker into the permanent establishment.
The CAT's direction for regularization of the respondent and grant of retiral benefits was not proper, and the judgment of the High Court stands set aside.
Furthermore, the grant of compassionate appointment to the legal heir of the respondent does not improve the situation for the respondent.
