According to Sections 10 and 33-C(2) of Industrial Disputes Act, the Labour Court cannot judge on entitlement and rights of disputed parties that may fall under Section 10 adjudication.
The Labour Court can only calculate benefits based on already adjudicated rights or interpretations of rights in incidental proceedings.
Thus, the order to pay arrears of salary based on a reinstatement order and consequential benefits is within the Labour Court's jurisdiction under Section 33-C(2), as it does not require an adjudication of rights.
The previous decision by the Single Judge is overturned.
