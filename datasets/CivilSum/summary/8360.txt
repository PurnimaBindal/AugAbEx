Constitution of India, Articles 14, 15 and 226 - Admission - For Engineering courses - The decision of the Director Technical Education allowing the institute to fill up seats on a paid basis from students who did not appear in entrance tests or could not qualify for the same is being challenged - The Admission Brochure specifies that only students who take the entrance test can be admitted - Private respondents were not eligible as they did not take the entrance test, and merit in the entrance test is the sole criterion for admission - Any admission not as per the Admission Brochure must be considered arbitrary - The admission of the private respondent has been quashed.
