The Minimum Wages Act of 1948, Sections 3(i)(a) and 4(1)(iii) - Wages - Piece rate wages - It is not possible to compare wages fixed on a piece rate basis with daily wages, as piece rate workers can earn more money by working more hours and are not limited to specific hours of work.
Furthermore, when work is done on a piece rate basis, payment cannot be made on a daily wage basis.
[Paras 3 and 15]
The Minimum Wages Act of 1948, Sections 5(1)(b) and 5(2) - Minimum rate of wages/Consideration of objections/Reasons to be recorded - When considering objections against the proposed minimum wages to be fixed, the Advisory Board must record reasons for reaching its conclusion and reflect on all relevant considerations in a fair and reasonable manner.
Notification based on recommendation or opinion without disclosing any reasons cannot be sustained, and as such, is quashed.
[Para 11]
Articles 14 and 19(1)(g) of the Constitution of India - Minimum Wages Act, 1948, Section 5 - Revision of minimum wage rates - If minimum wage rates in adjacent localities are below what they should be fixed under the Act and Rules, it cannot be a reason to avoid revising the rates in a particular district.
This does not infringe on the constitutional rights of the petitioners but may be a ground for workers in adjacent localities to get their wages revised based on the impugned notification.
