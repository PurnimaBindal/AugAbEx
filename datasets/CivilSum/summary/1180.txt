The selection of candidates for the Junior Basic Training Course on the basis of district-wise reservation is not in violation of Articles 14 and 15 of the Constitution of India.
There is no requirement based on place of birth in the admission process for JBT Course, but the district-wise allocation is based on persons belonging to a specific district.
Prohibition contained in Article 15(1) is not applicable, nor is Article 29(2) as there is no denying the admission to JBT Course on the basis of language.
Though knowledge of a specific language is a factor that has been taken into consideration in allocating seats district-wise, the district-wise selection has been upheld.
[Para 18]

Even though the petitioners have challenged the basis for admission to the Basic Training Course, district-wise selection has not been set aside.
Although the challenge to district-wise selection has not been successful, it should automatically be thrown out.
Since a substantial part of the course is already over and three persons out of 1500 will not affect the entire scheme and object of the advancement of primary education.
The admission is not set aside.
