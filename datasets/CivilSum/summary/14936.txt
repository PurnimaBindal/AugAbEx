Tamil Nadu Industrial Establishments (Conferment of Permanent Status to Workmen) Act, 1981, Sections 3 and 2(4) - Workman/Permanent status - According to Section 2(4) of the Act, the term 'workman' defines both full-time and part-time workers without any differentiation.
As long as they complete 480 days of service within 24 calendar months, both types of workers are eligible to be made permanent.
