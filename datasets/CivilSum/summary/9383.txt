Punjab Civil Services Rules, Chapter II and Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, Sections 2 and 5 - Notification - Whether Board employees entitled to receive gratuity under 1972 Act or Civil Services Rules - It is held that Board employees cannot claim gratuity under 1972 Act as conditions of their service are governed by Civil Services Rules, Chapter II.
The notification exempting Board from provisions of 1972 Act can be issued by State Government since there is no information to show that the Board is operating in any other State for supply of electricity.
Board employees are benefited by payment of gratuity under Civil Services Rules.
[Paras 13, 14, 22 and 26].
Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, Section 2 and 5 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Chapter II - Notification - Whether exemption can be retrospective - It is held that exemption can be issued retrospectively under section 5 of the 1972 Act.
However, orders prejudicial to employees cannot be passed and exemption cannot relate to a date earlier to commencement of 1972 Act.
[Paras 17 and 20].
