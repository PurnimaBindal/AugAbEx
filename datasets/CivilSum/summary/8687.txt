The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, outline the criteria for examination, admission, selection, and awarding of marks.
If the Rules, instructions, or advertisement do not specify that a fraction of a mark of 05 or more should be rounded off to 1, the court cannot direct that rounding off be applied to make an ineligible or failing candidate eligible - the criteria must be followed strictly as prescribed equally for all similarly situated individuals.
Reserved category candidates cannot claim a relaxation simply by comparing themselves to General Category candidates, as the reserved category is considered a class apart.
