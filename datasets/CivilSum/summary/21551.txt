Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 3, Item 1 - Back wages - The Labour Court ordered respondent's reinstatement with full back wages after being discharged for carrying unbooked passengers while working as a bus conductor.
However, the corporation filed a writ petition because the respondent failed to report for duty all these years.
As a result, only 50% back wages will be paid from the date of discharge until the date he reports for duty.
Additionally, if the respondent fails to report to duty within three weeks, the order for 50% back wages will be cancelled.
