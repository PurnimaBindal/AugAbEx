Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - Admission - MBBS Course - PMT Examination - Candidature - Cancellation of - Despite being allowed to take the PMT examination, petitioner failed to submit an 'application form' under his signature and did not correct the incorrect facts prior to taking the exam - Therefore, the petitioner cannot claim promissory estoppel since no right accrues by appearing in the exam - Additionally, as the petitioner breached rules/terms of examination and conditions contained in brochure and application form, there was no irregularity in the action taken by the respondent in canceling his candidature.
