Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab State Electricity Board Service (Premature Retirement) Regulations, 1982, Regulation 3(2)(c) - Premature retirement - If no orders are passed refusing to grant premature retirement within a specified period in notice for premature retirement - Then employee will be deemed to have retired on expiry of period mentioned in notice - Regulation 3(2) (c) would come into play if no orders were passed within period mentioned in notice for premature retirement - Petitioner would be deemed to have retired on expiry of 3 months.
