Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Central Civil Services (CCA) Rules, 1965, Rule 14 - Enquiry/Dismissal from service/Natural justice - Charge-sheet - It is mandatory to follow the provisions of Rule 14 which mandate the holding of a domestic enquiry where the delinquent can defend himself and prove his innocence - Dismissal order passed without complying with the provisions of the Rules is illegal - As a result, the employee has the right to be reinstated with full back wages and all consequential benefits.
