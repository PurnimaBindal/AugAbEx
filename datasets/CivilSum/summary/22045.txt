The SSS Board must prepare a merit list for Class III posts and recommend candidates for appointment based on their qualifications and job requirement.
The executive can allot selected candidates in the particular categories of services in the order of merit as recommended by the Board and roster, subject to fulfilment of qualifications.
Arbitrary exercise of power in selection made by pick and choose is liable to be quashed.
[Para 4] B Options given in application forms do not guarantee selection or appointment to a specific post.
The government must prescribe an objective and rational method of allotting candidates to departments based on job requirement.
