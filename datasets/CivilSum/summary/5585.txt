The Constitution of India, Article 16 and the U.P.
State Universities Act, 1973, Section 21(3) establish parity of pay scales for instructors and lecturers.
However, the redesignation of the post of instructors to that of lecturers does not automatically provide this parity.
This is due to the qualifications required for each post and the recruitment process being substantially different.
Therefore, a person claiming parity must establish that the posts in question are similar in all respects.
Simply showing that the nature of their duties are similar to that of the holder of another post is not sufficient.
In this case, the claim of the petitioner cannot be substantiated.
[Paras 18 and 22]
