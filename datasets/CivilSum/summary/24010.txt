Constitution of India, Articles 226 & 311 - Government servant - Whether an individual is a servant of the State or not is determined by factual evidence - In the case of Kurk Amin on both commission and regular basis, their duties and responsibilities are the same as per the UP Zamindari Abolition and Land Reforms and Land Revenue Act - Once the High Court, after reviewing relevant record, has made a factual determination that Amins appointed on commission basis are government servants, then they are entitled to regular pay scales - This entitlement cannot be denied unless the factual determination is challenged.
[Paras 14 and 15]
