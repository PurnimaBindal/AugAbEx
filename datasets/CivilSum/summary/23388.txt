Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, Sections 17 and 23(3) - Bonus - Customary Bonus and Bonus under Bonus Act - differentiation - A bonus given by the company to employees at the rate of Rs. 105% of wages for nine years is deemed as a customary bonus connected to Puja paid under the agreement between employees and the company, and not a bonus under the Payment of Bonus Act - The employer has the right to subtract the amount of Customary Bonus from the bonus payable to employees under the Payment of Bonus Act.
