The petitioner, appointed as P.T.I.
with qualifications higher than the minimum, fears termination due to possessing higher qualifications but not the same - and therefore seeks a writ of certiorari to quash orders of termination already issued.
In CWP No. 3758, the DB held that a person in the post for some time cannot be terminated merely for having qualifications higher than the minimum - which aligns with the State's policy.
Therefore, the writ petition is allowed and the respondents are directed accordingly.
