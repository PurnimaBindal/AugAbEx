Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Ex-servicemen/Qualifications - Reservation for Ex-Servicemen - Ex-Servicemen are given a reservation for appointment to the post of clerk in `A' Class Office.
The prescribed qualifications for the Ex-servicemen is only matriculation whereas for the dependents of ex- servicemen being considered against the posts left out of the quota reserved for ex-servicemen, qualification of matric first class is prescribed equal to general candidates.
This condition is not arbitrary or unreasonable.
Dependents of ex-servicemen cannot be considered to be ex- servicemen and the benefit of reservation is given to them only to ensure that the reservation for ex-servicemen may not lapse due to non-availability.
