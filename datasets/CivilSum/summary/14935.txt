Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 300-A - Pensionary benefits to employees of provincialised Local Body Schools - Policy decision grants retired employees above 30 years of age and who retired from service before November 1, 1966 in areas within Punjab pensions w.e.f.
December 18, 1972.
However, the benefit was denied to one writ petitioner who had retired as Headmaster from an area that now falls under the UT of Chandigarh, not the re-organised State of Punjab.
The single judge ruled in favor of the petitioner's entitlement to pension under the policy decision.
The State Govt.'s decision to exclude employees based on their retirement area is irrational, unreasonable and discriminatory, and a departure from the principle of equality.
L.P.A. dismissed.
