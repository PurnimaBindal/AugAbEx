Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 137(4) required 50% marks in the assessment of service record, as well as the provision of maximum marks under the rule.
It was argued that the provision of maximum marks for a particular test was within the domain of the respondent and not arbitrary, but this argument was found to be untenable.
However, there was no issue with the fixing of 50 marks as maximum marks for the assessment of service record.

In the case of constables not being deputed for Lower School Training Course, if the petitioners sit for the test to be held in the future and are successful in being brought on the `IB-11' List, their result for the examination may be taken as if they had taken the examination held in October 1992.
If they are found to be higher in merit than the last candidate deputed for the course from their district, then they would be deputed for the course and given the same benefits as those who were deputed in 1992.
However, if their merit is lower, their result will not be considered as part of the 1992 examination.
