Pension Regulations, 1961, Rule 82 Part II - Pension Payment Instructions 1973, Para 291 - Army withholding pension on the ground of conviction and imprisonment - The withholding of pension was contested on the basis that authorities did not properly consider before holding the pension.
The orders lacked any indication of application of mind, resulting in the orders being invalidated.
The matter has been remitted for fresh consideration.
