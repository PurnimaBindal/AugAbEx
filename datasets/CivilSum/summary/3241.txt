Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C (2) - Mandate of equal pay for equal work – A workman, who claims wages equal to those of other employees performing similar work, is not required to provide details of the wages paid to him and others.
The management retains documented wage records of all employees, and acknowledges that no difference exists between wages, save for any interruption in service that must be established by the management.
Withholding the official record, leads to an adverse inference against the management, which permits the court to grant relief in favor of the workman, even if the law is not strictly followed.
