Central Civil Service (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 48 - Eligibility for receiving pro-rata pension on completion of 30 years deemed service - A respondent who was sent on deputation and later absorbed in a Public Sector Undertaking is entitled to pro-rata pension and DCRG based on their length of qualifying service until the date of absorption.
The Tribunal correctly found that the amount transferred to the respondent's account was their own GPF and not the Government's contribution towards CPF.
The petitioner had already allowed pension on completion of 30 years of deemed service.
The writ petition is dismissed.
