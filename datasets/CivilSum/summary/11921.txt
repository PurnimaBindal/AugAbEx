Under the Haryana and Punjab Agricultural Universities Act, 1970, Section 23, the Academic Council is authorized to determine the conditions of service and qualifications of University teachers.
The Vice Chancellor has discretion, with the approval of the Academic Council, to relax the prescribed qualifications.
Any additional conditions imposed by an unauthorized officer have no validity and should be disregarded.
[Paras 18 and 19]  B.
As per the Haryana and Punjab Agricultural Universities Act, 1970, Section 23, a candidate's performance is graded according to a percentage system or a point scale system of marks.
The petition in question received an 'A' grade with an OGPA of 478/700, which entitles her to 722% marks by conversion formula.
A candidate receiving an overall grade point average of 400/400 is considered to have earned 80% or above.
Therefore, the petition was fully qualified for appointment.
The action taken in regard to the petitioner was not legal or correct.
The appointment of respondent No. 4 was quashed and the petition was allowed.
