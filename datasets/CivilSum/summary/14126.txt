Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Deputation - Continuity of - The Appellant served on deputation to a Government institute but his services were later surrendered.
The Appellant argues that he should be allowed to continue like other employees.
Rule 14 states that unless an option is given a person shall be deemed to be on deputation.
No option was given by the Appellant.
The Respondents argue that the services of the Appellant were surrendered because of the misconduct of his wife, which can lead to the withdrawal of the services of any officer on deputation by the State Government.
The court held that the fact that others were allowed to continue on deputation does not give the Appellant any legal right to continue on deputation unless absorbed.
The appeal was therefore dismissed.
