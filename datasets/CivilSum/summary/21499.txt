According to the Constitution of India, Articles 243-Q, 243-T and 15(4), Gujarat Municipal Act, 1963, Section 33(3), and Gujarat Municipalities it is (Reservation of Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, Backward Class and women for office of President) Rules, 1994, Rules 2 and 3, even if a candidate belonging to a backward class has been elected from an unreserved seat, they are eligible to stand for the election for the post of President of Municipality which is reserved for a backward class candidate.
The Act and Rules do not contain any provision that only members elected from the reserved seats will be eligible to stand for selection of the President.
The roster requires the office to be filled by a person belonging to a particular category, not from a particular post.
The judgment in Saraswati Devi's case (1997) 1 SCC 122 has been overruled.
