According to the Life Insurance Corporation of India Staff Regulations, 1960, Regulation 19(1)(2), the retirement age for class III and class IV employees and transferred employees belonging to class I and class II is 60 years.
However, Class I and class II employees appointed to the service after 191956 retire at the age of 58 years.
Those who are promoted from class III to class I and II cannot claim the same retirement age because they were previously class III employees.
Regulation 19(2) will be applicable.
