Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Retirement - The respondent, a Pump Driver, claimed that he couldn't have retired at the age of 58 years as he was a class IV employee - The courts below granted his suit for declaration - The question of whether Pump Driver is a class III or IV post was debated and it was held that it is a class IV post - The revision of pay scale doesn't mean Pump Driver was raised to class III - The suit was rightly decreed.
