According to Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 131, Chapter XIII, athletes can be promoted within the sports quota.
The appellant was recruited as a Constable and subsequently promoted as a Head Constable within the sports quota.
Although he received additional promotions, his name was removed from the list of Head Constables while undergoing training to become an ASI.
However, the appellant's promotion was in accordance with the rules, and he should be recognized as a regularized Head Constable, with all the attendant benefits.
