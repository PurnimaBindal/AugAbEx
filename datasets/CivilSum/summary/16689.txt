Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Sustainability of change in designation - Despite the Petitioner's promotion as Assistant Foreman Overhaul Electrical (Heavy Plant), their designation was altered to Assistant Electrical Foremen - There was no prior notice or opportunity to be heard given - The promotion was regular and the order indicates the original designation - Therefore, the Petitioner shall be considered as promoted to the position of Assistant Foreman Overhaul Electrical (Heavy Plant) and shall continue to hold the same - They are also entitled to the pay scale associated with that position.
