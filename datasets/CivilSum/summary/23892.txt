For Respondent No. 1 :- In-person (N.P) A.
Judges in Subordinate Courts under Constitution of India, Article 235 - High Court has control over subordinate courts - High Court has the power to take disciplinary and administrative actions against subordinate judicial officers - Recommendations of the High Court are binding on the State/Governor - However, the formal order to give effect to such a decision has to be passed only by the State Government on the recommendations of the High Court - High Court cannot pass orders of dismissal, removal, reduction in rank, or termination of services of judicial officers.
[Paras 12, 15, 16 and 21] B.
Justice and equity as per Constitution of India, Articles 136 and 141 - Pre-mature retirement of judicial officers by the High Court - Recommendations sent during the pendency of controversy in the Court - The officials concerned are entitled to all benefits till the date of recommendations received by the Government to meet the ends of justice.
