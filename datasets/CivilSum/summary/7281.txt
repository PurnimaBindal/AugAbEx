Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Reinstatement - Backwages - Labour Court ruled that the termination of the workman's services was illegal, and awarded back wages up to 50%.
However, it was held that the workman is entitled to receive full back wages for the illegal termination of services.
The payment of full back wages was therefore directed.
