Constitution of India, Article 309 - Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, Section 34 - Rajasthan Road Transport Workers and Workshop Employees Standing Orders, 1965, Clause 18-D(1) - The power to compulsorily retire an employee upon attaining the age of 50 years or completing 25 years of service is not unguided and has adequate safeguards against arbitrary use of power.
The mere use of the term "absolute right" in Clause 18-D(1) does not render the rule ultra vires.
