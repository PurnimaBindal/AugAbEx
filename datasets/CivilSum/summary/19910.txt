Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 and 309 - Haryana Food and Supplies Department, State Services Class III Rules, 1968, Rule 2 and Sub Offices (Group C) Rules, 1982, Rule 9(C) - Appointment and Promotion - Quota Rule - According to Rule 2 of 1968 Rules, the post of Assistant Food and Supplies Officer in the Department was to be filled by promotion only - 1982 Rules were introduced that provided for promotion of 67% of Asstt.
Food and Supplies Officers and 33% of direct appointment to the same posts - The new Rules, 1982 with a quota for both promotees and direct recruits were applicable from 26th Sept, 1982 onwards and applied to vacancies that arose after that date at the ratio of 67% for promotees and 33% by direct recruitment on the basis of existing vacancies and not on cadre strength.
