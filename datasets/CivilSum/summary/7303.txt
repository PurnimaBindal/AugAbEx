Constitution of India, Article 226 - Karnataka Private Educational Institutions (Discipline and Control) Act, 1975, Sections 3 and 15 - Karnataka Private Educational Institutions (Discipline and Control) Rules, 1978, Rule 6 - Karnataka Education Act, 1995, Sections 88 and 145 - Appointment - Approval - Petitioner was selected in accordance with mandatory provisions and strict compliance with the Rules.
However, approval was denied on false grounds of manipulation of marks and excess teachers in the school, showing a lack of consideration from the authorities.
The denial of approval was unjustified, and the management was directed to reconsider the petitioner's case as per the Rules.
