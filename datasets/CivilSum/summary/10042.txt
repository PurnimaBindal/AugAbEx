As per Constitution of India, Article 300A, State Bank of India Act, 1955, Section 50, State Bank of India Employees Pension Fund Rules, 1955, Rule 7, 8, 20, 22, for pension fund entitlement, an employee must have completed 10 years of pensionable service counted from the date of confirmation to the date of superannuation.
Permanent employees can only become a member of the pension fund from the date of confirmation, and cannot count services prior to confirmation for pensionable service.
This holds true as per the rules and the petitioners cannot challenge this.
[Para 19]

Superannuation age for bank employees is fixed at 58 years, which is not increased by extension of service.
As per the State Bank of India Act, 1955 and the pension rules mentioned in A, an employee must complete 10 years of pensionable service counted from the date of confirmation to the date of superannuation.
In the case of the petitioners, their service was extended by 2 years, but this does not increase the superannuation age of 58 years.
