Constitution of India, Article 226 - Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Sections 10 to 12 - Resjudicata - Estoppel - Successive proceedings - In contempt proceedings, any new plea to avoid implementation of earlier orders cannot be raised once the matter has been concluded between the parties after considering rival contentions - The court must adhere to its earlier decision in subsequent proceedings on the same matters - If it was previously decided that Lab Assts.
can be promoted as Demonstrators and their claims were ordered for consideration, the claims cannot be rejected again on the grounds that they are not entitled to be considered for promotion as demonstrators - However, other eligibility qualifications of the Lab Assistants must be considered before directing the state to treat them as Teachers/Demonstrators - Therefore, the court can only direct for their consideration.
