Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal Rules) 1970, Rule 8 - Pension/Retirement/Enquiry/Delay - Natural Justice - The petitioner was issued a charge-sheet in 1987 alleging misconduct in 1976, and immediately retired after receiving it.
The petitioner filed a petition to quash the punishment of a cut in pension, as well as the initiation of proceedings due to unreasonable delay and non-compliance of natural justice.
The charge-sheet and punishment were ultimately quashed.
