Kerala Universities Act, Sections 57, 58 - Promotion - Post of Principal - Applicability of provisions of UGC Scheme - Management promoting Respondent as Special Grade Principal on directions by High Court to appoint a qualified and suitable incumbent - Appointment set aside by University Appellate Tribunal for not meeting requisite qualification under UGC Scheme of 1998 - Argument that private colleges are not subject to UGC regulations since State Government has not yet made amendment to University Statutes - Section 58 stipulating that teachers must have qualifications prescribed by academic counsel regulations - Since University Statutes have not been amended, college management not obligated to adhere to UGC regulations - Tribunal's decision overturned.
