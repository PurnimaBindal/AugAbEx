Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Cadre review - Petitioner requests direction to give revised cadre structure retrospective effect - Petitioners argue that cadre review proposals were developed in 1986, but restructuring did not happen until 15/7/1999 - Decided that cadre restructuring is a policy decision, with the ultimate discretion resting with the Government - In making this policy decision, the Government has the prerogative to set a date for implementation - Since the petitioners have no legal right to demand cadre review, they cannot claim retrospective effect for the restructuring of the cadre.
