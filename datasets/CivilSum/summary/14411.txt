Servants Classification (Control and Appeal) Rules, 1930, Rule 49-A - Suspension - Detailed charges or evidence are not necessary for an order of suspension, as some of the charges are sufficient to indicate a disciplinary enquiry is anticipated.
[Para 4]
UP Government Servants Classification (Control and Appeal) Rules, 1930, Rule 49A - Suspension - An order of suspension need not explicitly state that an enquiry is forthcoming; rather, suspension is allowed when the appointing authority believes that a disciplinary enquiry is warranted.
