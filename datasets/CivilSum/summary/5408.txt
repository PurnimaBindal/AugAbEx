Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 states that a workman must complete one year of continuous service and have worked for at least 240 days in order to terminate his service.
Hence, the order of termination issued in violation of this section was set aside by the labour court.
The petitioner is entitled to receive 60% of backwages from the date of termination until their date of death.
