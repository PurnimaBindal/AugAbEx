Workmen's Compensation Act, Section 10 - Compensation - Denied - The claim for compensation was denied by the respondents despite the appellant suffering a permanent loss of one hand, mental torture and a handicap due to an accident while working.
The only reason cited for denial was that the appellant was not employed with them and was used to consuming intoxicants.
However, the appellant is entitled to a paltry sum of Rs. 16,140/-, along with interest at the rate of 12% per annum from the date of filing the petition till the date of actual payment.
