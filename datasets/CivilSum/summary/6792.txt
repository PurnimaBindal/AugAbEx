The termination of the petitioner, a confirmed employee of UP State Leather Development and Marketing Corporation Ltd, was declared arbitrary and violative of Articles 14, 16 and 311 of the Constitution of India.
The provision in Service Rules that allows for the discharge of a permanent employee under the orders of competent authority on three months' notice or by giving three months' salary in lieu thereof was deemed ultra vires.
The Respondents were directed to reinstate the petitioner in service with back wages, increments, and other consequential benefits.
