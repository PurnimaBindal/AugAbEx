Army Act, 1950, Section 123(1) and (2) - Retirement - Dismissal of an Army Officer after retirement - An individual can be dismissed from service for violating the Army Act even after retirement at the age of superannuation, provided they are subject to the Act.

Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Pension Regulations for the Army, 1961 (Part I), Regulation 113 - Pension/Gratuity - A Junior Commissioned Officer of the Indian Army who has been dismissed from service under the Act cannot make any valid claim for pension or gratuity despite earning the minimum qualifying service period.
