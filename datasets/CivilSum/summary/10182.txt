The Payment of Gratuity Act, Sections 4(6) and 7(7) states that the forfeiture of gratuity is applicable in cases of termination of services.
However, it cannot be applied to cases of resignation.
A pre-deposit is required, and the waiver of pre-deposit based on the pendency of proceedings before BIFR is without merit.
Pleas not taken before the Controlling Authority cannot be allowed to be taken for the first time in a writ petition.
The order of the Controlling Authority should not be interfered with merely on the ground that the appeal was rejected due to a failure to comply with the provisions of Section 7(7).
