According to Army Pension Regulations, Rule 7(b), a petitioner discharged with a 30% disability and holding the category "CEE" is eligible for disability pension.
The burden of proving that the disability was not caused by service is on the authority, and if they fail to prove this, it is presumed that the disability is attributable to service.
The petitioner in this case was entitled to disability pension, as confirmed by a Re-survey Medical Board that determined the disability to still be at 30% and with no note of any disease at the time of entry into service.
