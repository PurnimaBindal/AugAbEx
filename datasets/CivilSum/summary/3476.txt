DB cannot refer a matter doubted by FB to a Larger Bench, but it is within the power of FB to do so.
According to High Court Rules and Orders, the Law of precedents and rules of business of High Court, Division Bench cannot refer a matter doubted by a Full Bench to a Larger Bench, and the decision of FB is binding on DB.
Only the Full Bench has the authority to refer such matters to a Larger Bench.
