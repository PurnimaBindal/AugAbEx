The appointment of a respondent on compassionate grounds has been rightfully cancelled due to the violation of policy.
The legality of compassionate appointment is dependent on the deceased government employee's entitlement, which is not applicable if one of their parents is alive and in government employment.
In this case, the respondent's mother was already in government service, which makes the appointment on compassionate grounds in violation of policy.
