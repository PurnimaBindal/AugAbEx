Constitution of India, Article 226 - Direct recruitment - Post of teachers - Petitioners challenged recruitment process on the ground that the advertised vacancies did not match the number of positions filled - Respondents argued that the advertisement had mentioned the potential for increasing or decreasing the number of vacancies - Courts rule that if the advertisement forewarns about potential changes in vacancies, then any appointment beyond the advertised number cannot be faulted by the authorities.
[Paras 11 and 12]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Selection Criteria - Allocation of additional marks - Petitioner contended that the allocation of extra marks to graduate candidates was arbitrary and discriminatory - Courts rule that the authorities' criteria for allocation of marks was reasonable and fair - Hence, the criteria did not violate Articles 14 or 16 of the Constitution of India.
[Paras 3 and 14]
