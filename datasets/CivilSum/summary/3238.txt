The West Bengal Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Reservation of Vacancies in Service and Posts) Act, 1986 violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
The petitioner, who has 22 years of service, made a representation for promotion to the available post, but authorities filled the post through direct recruitment and blocked his chances for promotion.
The direct recruitment, treating the single post as reserved for Scheduled Caste, is ultra vires and the petitioner had a legitimate expectation for promotion.
The selection has been set aside and the authorities have been directed to fill the vacancy through promotion.
[Paras 10, 16, 17, and 18] B.
The West Bengal Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Reservation of Vacancies in Service and Posts) Act, 1986 cannot reserve only one post in the cadre.
