Section 59(3) of the Mahatma Gandhi University Act, 1985, specifies that promotion is based on seniority-cum-fitness.
The promotion criteria does not allow selection and is intended to prevent any disputes, controversies, and discretion.
[Paras 21, 27, 29, 47]

In the absence of an actual post, retrospective appointment benefits cannot be granted.
[Para 36]

According to the circular of the University Grants Commission, only Senior Lecturers or Readers possessing over 55% marks on Master's Degree are qualified for promotion under Section 60 of the Mahatma Gandhi University Act, 1985.
As the respondent was hired as a Senior Lecturer after the deadline for submission of applications and has less than the minimum required marks, they are ineligible for promotion.
[Paras 32, 33, 37]
