The Delhi and Andaman and Nicobar Islands Civil Service Rules 1971, which includes Rules 3(2), 18, 30 and 31 and Schedule I (as amended), introduced the Post of Junior Administrative Grade from 111986.
In 1988, Amendments were introduced to regulate appointments and there was a claim that the 1988 Amendment only applies to vacancies after the Amendment was introduced.
However, the rule-making authority was legally able to provide for appointments to the Junior Administrative Grade after the Amendment was introduced.
The Amendment made provisions for such appointments and there was no legal issue with the provision.
