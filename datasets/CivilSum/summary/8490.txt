Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Rajasthan Police Subordinate Services Rules, 1989, Rule 39 - Termination of services - A SC candidate who was appointed as a police constable against reserved category on probation for two years had his services terminated on account of absconding from police training centre.
The order of termination, which was penal in nature, was impugned due to lack of a prior enquiry against the petitioner.
As a result, the impugned order was vitiated and liable to be quashed.
[Paras 15 and 16]
