The advertisement for the appointment of Assistant Sub-Inspectors of Police in Haryana was issued by HSSSB.
The selection procedure involved a written test and an interview.
However, there were serious allegations against the constitution, procedure and functioning of the Board.
Additionally, answer sheets of the written examinations were destroyed even before the results of selection had been declared.
The selection was not objective and fair, with one candidate interviewed singly on one day and no tabulation of final marks awarded to candidates.
Thus, the selection made by the Board was quashed due to these issues.
