The application for modification of an order dated 15.6.2000 regarding the admission of Homoeopathic Graded and Direct Degree course is being made by the petitioner college.
The order directed the college to withdraw their advertisement and only admit 25 students.
The application is based on an inspection that took place on 1482000, and the order was not subject to any further inspection by respondents.
The Petitioner Institute had already been granted permanent recognition by the Central Council of Homoeopathy.
If any facts that could result in the cancellation of such recognition had come to the attention of respondents, they should have notified the Central Council.
The respondents are directed to comply with the order dated 15.6.2000 in its letter and spirit.
