Constitution of India, Articles 311(2) and 309 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 19.1 - Conviction - Dismissal from service - The Apex Court in Tulsiram Patel's case clearly stated that if the disciplinary authority becomes aware that a government employee has been convicted, it must consider whether the nature of their misconduct justifies punishment and determine the appropriate penalty by examining the facts and circumstances surrounding the case.
By simply dismissing the employee upon conviction without conducting this analysis, the disciplinary authority has committed a legal error.
The penalty is set aside.
Moreover, as the petitioner is now 56 years old and is due to retire at the age of 58, it is just to direct that respondents pay the petitioner Rs. two lakhs instead of reinstating the petitioner.
