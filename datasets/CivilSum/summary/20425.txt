The Constitution of India, Article 14 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 22 - Ad hoc promotion - Pay stepping up - A respondent's request to step up his pay to the level of a junior was dismissed - The length of the promotion did not influence the issue of stepping up - As a result, the respondent was not eligible for the pay stepping up - The order of the Tribunal was overturned, and R Swaminathan's case was relied on - The appeal was granted.
