Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rule 7 - Charge sheet - Voluntary retirement - There are instructions, dated 16/17.12.1971, against private practice, and while an employee may serve a notice for voluntary retirement, this does not preclude departmental proceedings - The purpose is to ensure justice is served in cases of corruption, cheating, and serious acts of omission and commission.
If such individuals could avoid punishment by retiring, the administration would be ineffective - The court dismissed the petition.
