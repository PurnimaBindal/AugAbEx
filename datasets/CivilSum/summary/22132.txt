Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 25-N, 25-O, 25-J, and 18(3) as well as Contract Act of Section 23, mandate the prior approval and notice of the government for retrenchment on account of closure - Any retrenchment or closure that occurs without said approval in contravention to the provisions of Sections 25-N and 25-O would be void - As mandated by the law, any contract that goes against a mandatory statutory provision and is opposed to public policy would be considered invalid, despite its binding nature under Section 18(3) of the Industrial Disputes Act.
