Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Appointment Adhoc - Petition allowed - Petitioners appointed in various Mandi Samities in temporary capacity - Termination of Petitioners’ services challenged - Termination orders quashed - Board directed to formulate a policy to deal with terms of their service - Back wages denied - Similar cases allowed wherein directions were given to allow the Petitioners to resume their duty - Petitioners had no right to the posts according to respondents - Termination orders quashed citing arbitrariness, unreasonableness and arbitrariness.
