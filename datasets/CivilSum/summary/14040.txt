Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reversion - Natural justice - Non-speaking order - Petitioner, who was reverted from Head Constable to constable, issued a show-cause notice due to a change in his date of regularisation from 1991 to 1999 - A non-speaking order rejected the petitioner's reply to the show-cause notice, which was given on various grounds - The order that rejected the petitioner's representation is not sustainable as it is non-speaking.
