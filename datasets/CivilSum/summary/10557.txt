Punjab Civil Services Rules [Premature Retirement], 1975 Rule 3 and 2[1] - The Superintendent of Police has the authority to appoint an Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI) based on seniority on List D per Section 241 of the Government of India Act, 1935.
The Deputy Inspector-General of Police's approval is only needed in exceptional cases where seniority must be ignored.
There is also no requirement that the appointment must be made by the Deputy Inspector-General.
The Provincial Government has delegated its powers to the Superintendents of Police under Section 241[1][b] of the Government of India Act, 1935.
[Paras 9, 10 and 11]

Punjab Police Rules, Vol. II, Rule 13[9][4] - The appointment of the ASI is based on the Superintendent of Police's recommendation.
While the DIG Police may provide a recommendation, the actual appointment is done by the Superintendent of Police.

Punjab Police Rules, Vol. II, Rule 13[9] [2] - The Rules applicable to ASIs in Haryana greatly differ from those of Punjab.

Punjab Civil Services [Premature Retirement] Rules, 1975, Rules 3 and 4 - The premature retirement of an employee can be done in the public interest if their service record is bad, integrity doubtful, and performance inadequate.
In such a case, the order of premature retirement passed should be without any infirmity.
