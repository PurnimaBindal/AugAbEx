Section 33 of the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995 mandates that 3% of recruitment positions be reserved for handicapped persons.
A plea stating that there is a ban on filling back log vacancies and that compliance with this statutory regulation is therefore impossible is not tenable as the ban applies prospectively.
The court has given directions to fill up the vacancies.
