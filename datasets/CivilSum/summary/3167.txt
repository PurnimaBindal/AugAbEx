Constitutional of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admissions - Eligibility - Qualifications - Cut off date - Requisite qualifications and requisite eligibility are distinct terms - The prescribing of a minimum age limit for PMT exam eligibility is neither arbitrary nor discriminatory - The cut off date is necessary to establish age requirements - Unless the cut off date for minimum or maximum age eligibility is shown to be "wide of any reasonable mark" or chosen at random, it cannot be deemed arbitrary, capricious or whimsical - Upholding the minimum age of 17 years for PMT.
