Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Petition for revised pay scale benefit - Enquiry concluded - No final order as criminal case pending - After filing reply to memo of charges in disciplinary proceedings and disclosing defence, order can be passed during pendency of criminal trial - Standard of proof different in disciplinary proceedings from criminal case - Probability and preponderance required instead of proving case beyond reasonable doubt as in a criminal trial.
