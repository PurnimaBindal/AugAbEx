Constitution of India, Article 226 and 227 - Punjab Industries Non- Technical (Group A) Service Rules, 2005 - Punjab Civil Services (General and Common Conditions of Service) Rules, 1994, Rule 18 - Reconsideration of case for promotion - Downgrading of Annual Confidential Report - The downgrading of an ACR without any accompanying reason or justification is illegal and arbitrary, according to the decision in the UP Jal Nigam's case.
As a result, the respondent is directed to reexamine the petitioner's promotion in accordance with the rules.
