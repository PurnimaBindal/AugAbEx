Under the Post Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, Chandigarh, Regulations, 1967, Item No. 21 of Schedule I and in accordance with Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309, the Director of the Institution is authorized to grant higher initial pay but not advance increments.
A directive allowing advance increments exceeded the Director's authority and could only be allowed by the Governing Body.
The Director could have superseded his earlier order when it was discovered that allowing advance increments was beyond his power, and the petitioners had no grounds for a writ petition challenging the administrative order.
