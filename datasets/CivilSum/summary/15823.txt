The petitioner was promoted to Deputy Superintendent Grade II on an ad hoc basis and was later considered and promoted.
The respondent No. 3, who was appointed as Deputy Superintendent Grade II, was later shown senior to the petitioner on the gradation list.
The petitioner was also promoted as Deputy Superintendent Grade I based on seniority.
However, when respondent No. 1 claimed seniority over the petitioner in representation, the petitioner argued that the State Government's decision in giving seniority to respondent No. 3 over him went against the Punjab Prisons State Services (Class II) Rules, 1978.
Respondent No. 3 argued that it was instead covered by the Punjab Prisons State Service (Class II) Rules, 1963.
The court held that the Punjab Jail Manual served as guidelines rather than having overriding effect over the 1963 Rules, which were made under the authority conferred by Article 309 of the Constitution of India.
As such, the petitioner's plea was dismissed.
