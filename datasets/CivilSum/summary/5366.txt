Constitution of India, Article 311 - Suspension - Enquiry pending - Although subsistence allowance not paid during the period of suspension, it would not automatically render the consequent order of removal invalid - Respondent did not claim that he was prevented from attending enquiry proceedings nor that any prejudice was caused due to non-payment of subsistence allowance -Despite the non-payment of subsistence allowance, attendance of the enquiry was not disputed - Therefore, the non-payment of subsistence allowance must be coupled with real prejudice - Thus, the removal and dismissal orders set aside by the Tribunal are quashed.
