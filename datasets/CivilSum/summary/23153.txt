Andhra Pradesh Co-Operartive Societies Act, 1964, Sections 16, 116-B - Promotional post - Principle of reservation - Interpretation of Govt. notification on reservation - The notification makes it clear that the Governor's direction to follow reservation principle applies to all posts in all Co-operative institutions to be filled by direct recruitment, either temporary or regular basis - The High Court overlooked the later part of the notification while interpreting the expression 'appointment' - The principle of statutory construction mandates that all words used in a statute or notification issued under it must have full effect -Therefore, it is concluded that the Governor did not give any direction to apply policy of reservation in appointments to promotional posts - The appeal is allowed.
