Constitution of India, Article 309 - Punjab Civil Medical (State Service Class I) Rules, 1972, Rule 9-A - Promotion - Qualification - Post of Deputy Director (Health Services) - The rules that have been framed with the authority of law cannot be substituted by any government order, notification or circular - The DPC erred in recommending the promotion of respondent No. 3 despite not completing 10 years of service - The High Court was not justified in considering the PCMS Class II as PCMS Class I through a notification - As the promotion of respondent No. 3 is against the law, it must be set aside - The State is directed to appoint a fresh DPC to consider the case of all eligible candidates, including the appellant and respondent No. 3 in accordance with the service rules, completely ignoring the notification.
