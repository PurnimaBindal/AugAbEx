According to Regulation 173 of the Army Pension Regulations, 1961, a person released or discharged with a 20% disability as assessed by the Medical Board or Resurvey Medical Board is entitled to disability pension and must be periodically re-examined.
If the Chief Controller of Defence Accounts does not accept the recommendations of the Medical Board or Resurvey Medical Board, that person must be sent for re-examination.
However, holding a Resurvey Medical Board to reassess a person's degree of disablement every ten years is not favorable for either party.
In a specific case where the petitioner was found to be 20% disable, the authorities were directed to grant him pension dating back to the retrospective dates with 12% interest and to hold a Resurvey Medical Board to re-assess his disability.
