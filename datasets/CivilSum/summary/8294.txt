Constitution of India, Article 226 - Reinstatement - Notional Increments - Removal of petitioner as a disciplinary measure - Labour Court directed petitioner's reinstatement with continuity of service and full back wages - However, after reinstatement, respondent did not take into account notional increments that petitioner would have earned had he been in actual service - This is not acceptable - Petitioner cannot be denied notional increments due to his delay in approaching the Court - Direction given to calculate petitioner's wage while taking into account periodic increments that would have been earned by him.
[Paras 3 and 4]
