The Indian Police Services (Cadre) Rules, 1954, apply Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, and Rules 3 and 5 dictate cadre allocation of IPS officers, adhering to state-wise reservation policy for SC/BC/OBCs.
Only general insider candidate can be allocated to general vacancy by following the roster system; however, the 'outsider'-'insider'-'outsider' principle of allocation may be deviated from in some cases.
The principles of reservation do not always permit mathematical exactitude, and minor or occasional departure from the principle is permissible, though 'outsider' allocation of OBC candidate against a general vacancy may violate the legitimate expectations of a particular candidate.
A candidate selected to an All India Service has no right to claim allocation to a particular State or Joint Cadre of his choice, and such allotment is an incident of service.
Policy choices determine the distribution of candidates hailing from a particular State to other States of the Union.
The allocation of a candidate to one or another State as a result of various policy choices by the competent authority cannot be invalidated on the ground of non-fulfillment of expectation of a candidate unless the decision is arbitrary.
No wholesale or extensive review of cadre allocation is permitted after about five years after various candidates achieve different positions.
