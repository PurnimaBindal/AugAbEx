Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer of petitioner - Petitioner claims transfer was made to favor 2nd respondent and went against rule of spouses being posted together - Petitioner not given opportunity to present circumstances for staying in same place - Person affected by transfer at request of another has legitimate expectation of fair treatment - Guidelines for spouses working together create expectation of no unfair disturbance - Order violated legitimate expectation and was quashed.
[Paras 5, 6, and 9]
