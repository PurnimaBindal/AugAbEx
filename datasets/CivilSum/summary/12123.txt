Punjab Municipal Act, 1911, Section 51 - Punjab Municipal Employees Pension and General Provident Fund Rules, 1994, Rules 2(h), 2(j) - Qualifying service - Employees must restrict their period submitting to their CPF to be considered as qualifying for pension, without any additional consideration for the type of post held.

Punjab Municipal Act, 1911, Section 51 - Punjab Municipal Employees Pension and General Provident Fund Rules, 1994, Rules 2(h), 2(j) - Qualifying service - Any Executive instructions concerning qualifying service computation for pension that is contradictory to the Rule, is invalid - Qualifying service computation for pension does not need to start from employee's CPF contribution date or date of confirmation.
