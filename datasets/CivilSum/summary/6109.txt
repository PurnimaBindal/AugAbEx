Constitution of India, Article 226 - Date of Birth - Rectification of - Petitioner requested rectification of date of birth after being notified of retirement - The request was denied as the Petitioner had previously provided a birth date of 21/1/1932 - School records indicate the Petitioner's actual birth date is 8/6/1108 as per Malayalam Era - A corresponding date of birth in Christian Era is 21/1/1933 - It was determined that the competent authority could rectify the Petitioner's date of birth as a genuine mistake without constituting an alteration or correction.
