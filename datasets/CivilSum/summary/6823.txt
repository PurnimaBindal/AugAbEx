Workman's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4-A - Penalty/Delay in payment/Notice and hearing/Sufficient cause - Before imposing a penalty on the employer for not paying awarded compensation to a workman, the Commissioner must give notice to the employer to show cause - If the employer is able to provide sufficient cause for the delay upon receiving notice, the Commissioner can only order payment with 6% simple interest.
