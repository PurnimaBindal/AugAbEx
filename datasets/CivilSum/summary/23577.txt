In cases where more positions are filled than advertised, the court may provide relief that balances the interest of the State and those seeking public employment.
The appropriate relief will vary depending on the circumstances of each case.
[Para 12]
An advertisement for 62 vacancies should not result in more than 62 appointments.
However, vacancies that were likely to arise due to retirement could be considered.
It would be unfair to invalidate all appointments made above the limit of 62 once the selection process was completed.
However, appointments made for newly created positions against future vacancies will be invalid.
