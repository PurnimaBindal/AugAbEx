Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work - The petitioner, who served as Social Education and Panchayat Officer, was assigned the duty of the post of BD and PO and worked in that capacity until retirement.
Therefore, he should receive the pay and allowances of the higher post of BD and PO for the entire duration of his service in that position.
[Paras 1 to 3]
