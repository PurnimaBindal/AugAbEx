Constitution of India, Articles 32, 226, 227, 323-B, 334, 335 and 339 - Andhra Pradesh Land Grabbing (Prohibition) Act, 1982, Section 7 - Special courts - Validity - The special court formed under the Act must appoint a Chairman in consultation with the State's Chief Justice, although the other members are appointed by the Government without consulting the Chief Justice - The members of the special court do not have the same level of independence as members of higher judiciary - The power of judicial review is a crucial controlling factor for tribunals that may not function correctly - The special court does not constitute the judicial services of the State - The court is, in fact, a revenue-based administrative tribunal that has a mixed composition of Judicial and Revenue members - The appointment of retired District Judges and revenue members does not fall under Article 234.
