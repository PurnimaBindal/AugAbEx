Constitution of India, Articles, 14 & 16 - Delay and Laches - Family pension - Despite appeal being filed beyond the prescribed period and not being forwarded by the Record Officer to the concerned authority, petitioner was in regular contact with the respondent in pursuing her case.
Furthermore, it was communicated to the petitioner that her appeal could not be forwarded due to it being delayed.
As such, her cause cannot be hit by laches.
The petitioner is given one month to file a fresh appeal.
[Paras 2 and 5]
