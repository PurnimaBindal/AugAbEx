Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Recruitment to the post of Sub-Inspector from in-service candidates who were holding graduation qualifications and were within 30 years - Petitioner was not considered for promotion as he was not working in Police Department but was working in the Special Police Department - His case was not considered on merit as the same happened to be clubbed with other cases in which those officers had left the Police Department and were serving elsewhere - However, it was held that there is no impediment in his way to come back to the regular line of Police Department and that the petitioner is eligible for the post - Thus, the Public Service Commission is directed to consider his case according to Rules.
