Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admissions - Reservation for internal candidates - Institutional reservation - The reservation of 75% for internal candidates who passed MBBS from Banaras Hindu University is against the Constitution.
Excessive reservation in super-speciality Post-Graduate Courses narrows down the scope of choosing the best and hard-working candidates.
Institutional preference can be given only to the extent of 50% of successful MBBS students, which further requires examination by experts to be gradually reduced.
There is no justification for giving any institutional preference to any student in superspeciality courses.
The best talent should be produced by holding an open competition at the national level according to Paras 12 and 13.
