Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Uttar Pradesh Public Services (Reservation of Scheduled Casts, Scheduled Tribes and Other Backward Classes) Act, 1994, Section 3 - Appointment - Reservation - Domicile - Petitioner, an OBC from Madhya Pradesh, seeks the benefit of reservation in Uttar Pradesh based on a certificate issued by MP authorities – Court held that the 1994 Act only benefits residents of Uttar Pradesh, and therefore the petitioner cannot claim the benefit of the same.
