According to Section 20 of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, an appeal to the Administrative Tribunal is only maintainable after the alternative remedy of a Departmental appeal has been exhausted.
The petitioner, who was not considered for promotion, had filed a representation and sent a copy to the Financial Commissioner, who was the appellate authority.
The representation was rejected, not on the ground that it was filed before an inappropriate authority, and the alternative remedy of the departmental appeal was duly exhausted.
Therefore, the appeal to the tribunal is considered maintainable.
[Para 3]

As per Sections 20 and 21 of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, the limitation period begins from the date of the 'final grievance.'
In this case, the petitioner was not considered for promotion and had filed a departmental appeal, which was rejected and deemed as the 'final grievance.'
The word 'grievance' in Section 21(1) relates to the 'final order.'
Accordingly, the appeal before the tribunal, filed within one year from the date of the final 'grievance' or 'order,' is well within the limitation.
