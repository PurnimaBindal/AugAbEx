Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19 - Central Civil Services (Leaves Rules), 1972, Rule 20 - Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 47 - An employee was incapacitated for service in 1994, underwent re-examination by Medical Board in year 2002, and yet termination was ordered w.e.f.
13.8.1994 - The Tribunal dismissed claim of terminal benefits and compassionate appointment - The Tribunal's reasoning was that termination order dated 11.6.02 does not ipso facto create a right for deeming employee on duty from 1994 onwards - The argument presented was that employee was deemed invalidated on expiry of 6 months leave as per Rule 20 of 1972 Rules.
However, it was held that once termination is effected after enactment of 1995 Act, it is violative of the provisions of Section 47, as per which, service of employee acquiring disability during service cannot be terminated - There is no automatic cessation of service, and an order is required to be passed - Respondents are to treat deceased employee in service till the date of death i.e. 1372002 and pay all consequential benefits.
[Paras 5, 7 to 9]
