The Maharashtra Secondary and Higher Secondary Board Regulations, 1977, Regulations 9(2)(xviii) and 14, regarding unfair means in education and domestic enquiry, stipulate that legal assistance is excluded and only the standing committee may conduct an enquiry into unfair means.
These rules do not contravene natural justice principles.
[Paras 8, 9 and 17] B.
The enquiry report on the unfair means used by 283 students reveals that the enquiry officer can receive assistance from his staff for drafting the report and that it is unnecessary to detail all the admissions made.
The failure to record reasons in such circumstances is not a violation of the principles of natural justice.
The applicability of these principles depends on the facts of the case, the nature of the inquiry, and the effect of the decision on the person's rights and circumstances.
[Paras 18 and 22] C. Domestic enquiries are beyond the jurisdiction of the High Court and the Supreme Court under Articles 136 and 226 of the Constitution.
They have no authority to record their own findings and must uphold the decision of the standing committee if it is supported by the evidence on record.
[Para 10] D The Maharashtra Secondary and Higher Secondary Board Regulations, 1977, Regulations 9(2)(xviii) and 14, relating to unfair means penalties, allow the punishing authority to levy a lesser penalty than the maximum as warranted by the severity of the offense.
The mere provision of a maximum penalty does not make the rules invalid.
