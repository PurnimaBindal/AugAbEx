Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Stepping up of pay - Special pay - The principle of stepping up of pay applies only when all employees discharge the same duties and are under the same responsibility, and not in different circumstances - If a junior person receiving special pay or overtime is transferred to a different place, stepping up of pay does not apply - The withdrawal of such special pay without notice is not arbitrary or a violation of natural justice.
