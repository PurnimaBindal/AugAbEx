Constitution of India, Article 226 - HCS (Executive Branch) Rules, 1930, Rules 11, 17 and 20 - Seniority - Post of HCS (Executive Branch) - A learned Single Judge affirmed that the petitioner in a writ petition is entitled to be appointed retroactively to the post of HCS (Executive Branch) with accurate seniority as of the date when other batch candidates were appointed, and the petitioner should be accorded proper seniority as a direct consequence of his retroactive appointment and upheld by Letters Patent Bench - As a result, the respondent is directed to accord the petitioner the seniority that would have been given to the person in whose position the petitioner became eligible to be appointed.
