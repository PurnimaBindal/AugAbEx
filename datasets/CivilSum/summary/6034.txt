Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 1(5) and Proviso thereto read with Section 16(1)(d) - Establishment - Liability to contribute to Provident Fund - Conversion of old Firm into new establishment - The Act still applies even if the number of employees is reduced below 20 - However, a new establishment can be exempt from the Act for 3 years and until the number of employees reaches 20, if it has no relation to the old firm - Mere purchase of assets, insurance policy or re-employment of few workers does not prove the old firm still exists under a new name - Once the old firm has closed and its dues paid, the new firm is not liable for the old firm's liabilities.
