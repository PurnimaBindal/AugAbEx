For the Respondent: The Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 12.21 states that a constable can be discharged by the Superintendent of Police within three years of their enrollment, but only after an opportunity has been granted.
Petitioners were discharged for participating in activities that were prejudicial to the maintenance of discipline, and the findings cast a stigma on their career.
These orders could not have been passed except after holding an enquiry and affording the petitioners a reasonable opportunity of hearing.
The impugned orders are not orders of discharge simpliciter but have been passed as punishment.
Petitioners are not entitled to be reinstated, but instead will be deemed to be continuing under suspension.
Fresh orders may be passed after a new enquiry, and no salary or monetary benefits shall be given from the date of dismissal until the date of this order.
It is up to the disciplinary authority to decide what treatment the period of suspension should be met with.
