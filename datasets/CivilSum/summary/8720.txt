Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 16, 226 and 311 - Departmental enquiry - Natural justice - Delinquent officer not provided with copies of documents relied on by prosecution - Enquiry officer did not proceed further with enquiry after appointment - As a result, the petitioners' guilt or innocence could not be determined - The respondents are instructed to complete the enquiry within 4 months.
If they fail to do so, the enquiry will automatically be considered lapsed.
Any promotions made pending the enquiry or after the findings of the enquiry officer shall be subject to review based on those findings.
