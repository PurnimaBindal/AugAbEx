J&K Govt. Services (Prevention of Corruption) Act, 1975, Section 6 - Corruption case against a non-Gazetted Govt. servant - The only competent authority to establish a Tribunal to investigate and make a report on the case is the District and Sessions Judge - The Single Member Tribunal Anti-Corruption (Non-Gazetted) does not have the power to investigate and report on cases in Jammu Province.
