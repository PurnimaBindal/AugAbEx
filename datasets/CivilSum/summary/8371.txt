The plaintiff's claim for regularization of services was rejected due to not completing four years of continuous service as a pump operator as of 31.12.1981 and being reverted from the position of pump operator to T/Mate.
Although the plaintiff argued that he had been discharging operator duties from 111975 to 31.12.1981, he had been shown as working as a T/Mate or operator in the department record.
The plaintiff alleged that he had been paid as a T/Mate but was, in fact, working as an operator.
The graph proved that the plaintiff was working mainly as an operator.
The first appellate Court concluded that the plaintiff had worked for four years as an operator until 31.12.1981.
The State's appeal was dismissed.
