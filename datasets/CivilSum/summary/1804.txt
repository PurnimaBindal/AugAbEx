Constitution of India, Article 226 - Denial of 'other Backward Class' certificate - Ability to sustain - Petitioner is denied on the grounds of being in 'creamy layer'- Notification states that if one or both parents are Class I officer, the person is in 'creamy layer'.
Petitioner's father was a Class I officer.
Petitioner asserts that 'is' should not be understood as 'was', however it was held that words 'is and are' also cover those who have held a Class I post in the past.
As a result, the petition was rejected.
[Paras 17 and 20]
