Medical reimbursement for post-operative outdoor treatment is mandated by the Constitution of India in Articles 21 and 14.
The State is responsible for reimbursing its employees for follow-up treatment expenses and other outdoor treatment for chronic diseases.
Furthermore, if an employee cannot function without a particular medicine or treatment, it is the responsibility of the State to ensure that the employee remains healthy and able to work.
