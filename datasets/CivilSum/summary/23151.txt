Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission in Medical Colleges in Haryana State on the basis of entrance test - Reservation of seats for Backward classes - Candidate of Backward classes will be selected on the basis of reservation policy - If a reserved candidate secures a position on merit, the next reserved candidate should be given the available reserved seat - The High Court held that Sunil Yadav should be adjusted against the one seat in the general category and the reserved seat should be given away to the candidate in the reserved category who is next in merit - Sunil Yadav was granted admission in the general category seat in the Rohtak Medical College and his appeal was allowed.
