Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion/Seniority/Seniority-cum-merit - Instead of choosing between "good and bad", employers are required to select a person with proven merit and ability by applying the principle of seniority-cum-merit.
While seniority is important, the officer's performance must also be taken into account.
The petitioner was warned and advised multiple times to improve their work and conduct, and cannot claim promotion solely based on seniority.
