Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 16 - Recruitment Rules, 1940, Rule 14 - Suspension of an employee - The power to suspend is discretionary, and there is no deemed suspension under the rule - A Head Teacher was suspended during the investigation of a criminal case, and no disciplinary proceedings were initiated - The order of suspension was not challenged for approximately 65 years - As the petitioner's delay in challenging the suspension order was unjustifiable, it is held that the order cannot be quashed on the ground of the non-initiation of disciplinary proceedings.
