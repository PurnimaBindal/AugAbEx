Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion - Seniority - Petitioner's grievance that 2nd respondent was sent to Intermediate School Course and omitted from the list - Despite petition being appointed as Constable before 2nd respondent, the latter was promoted to Head Constable first, making him senior - Therefore, second respondent is senior to the petitioner due to the date of his promotion - The petitioner's complaint about the Intermediate School Course cannot be upheld given the second respondent's seniority.
