The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226/227 supports equal pay for equal work.
Despite some carpenters in Milkfed being paid in the higher scale of Rs. 510-800 (revised), they are those who have come from PDDC and their pay scale could not be reduced due to the terms of their absorption.
Other than that handful, all carpenters in Milkfed draw pay in the same scale of Rs. 400-600 (revised).
The petitioner was given the same pay scale of Rs. 400-600 (revised) as other carpenters in Milkfed and cannot claim to be paid in the higher grade as he competed for it and failed.
Therefore, there is no discrimination, and the principle of equal pay for equal work is not violated.
