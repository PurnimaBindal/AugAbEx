Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8 - Major Penalty/Termination of services/Terms and conditions of appointment/Natural Justice - If an employee has been promoted from class IV to Class III position, the authorities cannot terminate their services at any time without notice, especially when there is no such provision in the appointment letter for the promotional post - After initiating an investigation and leveling accusations against an employee, the authorities cannot simply drop the proceedings to issue an order to dispense with their services as "no longer required" - Such an order cannot be classified as being only of a simple nature - The plaintiff's service record was found to be average and good, and their work and conduct were deemed satisfactory - Confirmation was recommended - The order of termination was deemed to be illegal, arbitrary and in violation of principles of natural justice.
