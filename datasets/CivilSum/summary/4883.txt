According to Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, Sections 2(44), 298 and Delhi Police Act, 1978, Sections 28 and 29, occupants/allottees of industrial plots cannot challenge the award of parking contract.
The contention that MCD cannot convert road network into a parking site without prior approval of the government was rejected, as the corporation is under duty to ensure that all public streets retain their dominant characteristic.
Impugned contract may only operate in three parking sites earmarked in the plan produced, and not on internal roads of colony.
