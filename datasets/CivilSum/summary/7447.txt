Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Termination of service - Approval of termination granted in application filed under Section 33(2)(b) of Industrial Disputes Act - Labour Court overturned order of termination on ground that petitioner failed to examine any passenger as witness - However, it is not mandatory to examine passenger to prove conductor's guilt - Matter remanded to Labour court for fresh decision.
