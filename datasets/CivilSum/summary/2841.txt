Haryana Panchayat (State Service Group `C') Rules, 1979, Rules 7, 9, and 12 - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Appellants were not selected for Clerk positions, but Respondent No. 1 was selected and got promoted on 7121982 - State Government issued a notification regularizing ad hoc Clerks who completed 2 years of service on 1591982, Appellants got regularized from 1591982 since ad hoc Clerks could not be regularized before the notification issued on 311983.
- Clause fixing seniority of such ad hoc Clerks from an earlier date is ultravires Articles 14 and 16 of Constitution and violative of Rule 12 of 1979 rules.
