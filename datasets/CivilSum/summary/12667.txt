Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(q), 12(3), and 24(3) - Strike - Demonstrations by workers of Hospital - Hospital workers and managements cannot inconvenience, harass, or threaten management officers or visitors, or disturb patients' peace and solitude under the guise of rights of strike and lockouts - Workers can hold peaceful demonstrations according to the law without causing a disturbance, and management can peacefully do the same - Illegal acts in violation of procedures may lead to restraining the workers from holding demonstrations, raising slogans, blocking access, and shouting or creating a disturbance.
