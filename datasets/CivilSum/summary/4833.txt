Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Reinstatement - Back wages - Denial - Petitioner removed from service without following due procedure - Tribunal found petitioner had completed 240 days in previous year - Reinstatement ordered without back wages - Argument made for back wages due to violation of Section 25-F - Held, Reinstatement and back wages are not the same thing - Back wages are not automatically granted upon setting aside dismissal order.
