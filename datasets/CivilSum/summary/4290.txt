Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Writ jurisdiction for changing date of birth - Questions of authenticity of documents and other related matters - Depend on factual evidence needing examination and assessment - As a Court of record, the High Court cannot use its writ jurisdiction to adjudicate on these issues - A Civil Court of competent jurisdiction is the appropriate forum for seeking remedy.
[Para 8]  B Jurisdiction - Correction in date of birth - Petitioner should seek remedy through a civil suit, where the entire case can be evaluated with proper appreciation of evidence.
