Administrative Tribunals Act (13 of 1985), Section 14 - Promotion - Post of Junior Time Scale Grade of Indian Broadcasting Programmes Services - The employee was denied promotion through the sealed cover procedure, leading to a delay of four years.
Despite being eligible for non-selection promotion, the employee was not promoted.
The Supreme Court's mandate was ignored, and the Tribunal did not consider the employee's case for promotion, resulting in the denial of promotion being deemed improper.

Administrative Tribunal Act (13 of 1985), Sections 19 and 21 - Promotion - Post of Junior Time Scale in Indian Broadcasting Programmes Services - An employee filed an application before the Tribunal against dismissal on the grounds that it was time-barred and suffered from constructive res judicata.
The dismissal was found to be improper since the application was filed within one year and was based on the law declared by the Apex Court.
