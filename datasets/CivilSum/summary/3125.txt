Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and laches - Representation - Upon direction by the court to decide a pending employee representation on merit, subsequent dismissal of a petition challenging rejection on the grounds of delay and laches is not valid.
[Para 7]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Necessary parties - Military service benefits - Only those who have provided military service are considered necessary parties for claiming military service benefits.
[Para 8]
Military Service Benefits - Discharge on compassionate ground - The apex Court in Raj Pal Sharma's case struck down the rule barring Armed Force personnel who received discharge on request on compassionate ground from receiving military service benefits, thus regardless of the mode of discharge every Armed Force personnel is entitled to benefits.
[Paras 9 and 14]
Military Service benefits - Age relaxation - If an employee requested age relaxation on grounds of military service upon joining civil service, they are entitled to military service benefits and seniority in civil post.
[Paras 10 and 11]
Military Service benefits - Fixation of seniority - All previous service rendered in the same or equivalent post must be reckoned for determining seniority in civil post, after subsequent instructions to Ministry of Defence Headquarters by Ministry of Home Affairs.
[Paras 15 and 16]
Constitution of India, Articles 162 and 309 - Executive Instructions - If the Rules do not provide guidance, instructions may offer supplementary direction.
