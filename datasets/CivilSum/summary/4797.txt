According to the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, as well as the Apprentices Act's Section 22, and the Bhakra Beas Management Board Class-III and Class-VI Employees (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Regulations of 1994's Regulations 4 and 21, while it is not mandatory for the employer to provide regular job opportunities to apprentices trainees, trained apprentices are given preference over direct recruits whenever job opportunities are available.
They are also entitled to age relaxation for the duration of their apprenticeship if necessary, and the training institute must maintain a seniority list of trained individuals for preference purposes, which fulfils the mandates of Articles 14 and 16.
