Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-F - Retrenchment/Retrenchment compensation - In the event of retrenchment in contravention of Section 25-F(a) and (b) of the Act, order will be quashed and back wages could be claimed by the petitioner under Section 33-C(2) of the Act if termination is quashed in writ petition - Retrenchment compensation and notice pay offered one day after the order of retrenchment made effective.
