Constitution of India, Article 16 - Ad hoc appointment - Regularisation of service - Appointment of doctors as Assistant Medical Officers or Assistant Divisional Medical Officers on ad hoc basis - The expression "upto October 1, 1984" in the direction for regularisation does not cover appointments made on October 1, 1984.
The respondent was appointed on an ad hoc basis on October 1, 1984, and joined service on October 9, 1984.
Therefore, the respondent is not entitled to the benefit of the Court's direction in Dr. AK Jain v. Union of India, 1987 Supp.
SCC 497 regarding regularisation, which was not intended to benefit any ad hoc appointee who was not serving in the positions of Assistant Medical Officer or Assistant Divisional Officer on October 1, 1984.
