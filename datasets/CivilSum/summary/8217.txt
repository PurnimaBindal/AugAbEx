Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, Order 7 Rule 11 - Rejection of plaint - Jurisdiction - The plaintiff, an employee of State Bank of India who was dismissed from service, filed a civil suit.
The trial Court returned the plaint to be presented before a proper forum.
It was held that the plaintiff must seek relief from the civil Court under the Industrial Disputes Act, as the entire pleading twines around the awards and settlements under the Act, and not a single averment in the plaint expresses violation of the right of plaintiff under general or common law.
The jurisdiction of civil Court is clearly excluded.
Thus, the revision petition was dismissed.
