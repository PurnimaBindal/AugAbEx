Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Premature retirement from service - An order is passed in public interest - Paid pay and allowances, instead of three months' notice, is as per the Govt. resolution - A label used to describe petitioner as "dead wood" was written against the reasons of his retirement - No consideration given to the letters of appreciation which were received for petitioner's duties - Petitioner not given notice as per the Govt. resolution - The affidavit in reply did not demonstrate how petitioner's duties affected public interest - Impugned order has been quashed and set aside.
