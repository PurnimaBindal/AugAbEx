The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, which is a labor law, pertains to the case of Baildar as per which reinstatement with back wages was sought.
The appellant was wrongly burdened to provide evidence that the workman had not rendered services for 240 days.
Hence, the fact that the claimant was appointed to a sanctioned post was not substantiated by any material.
Official records, on the other hand, indicated that the workman had worked for 179 days.
Additionally, the High Court has arrived at an abrupt conclusion without analyzing the factual position.
Therefore, the matter is remitted back for fresh consideration.
