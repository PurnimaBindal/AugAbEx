The appointment and selection of a Lecturer in Environmental Science at the Universities of Kashmir and Jammu are governed by Sections 36 and 47 of the Universities of Kashmir and Jammu Act, 1969.
The fact that one member of the selection committee was related to one of the candidates does not necessarily mean that the selection proceedings were compromised.
Furthermore, the appellant's failure to object to this at the time of selection means that they are not entitled to any relief.
[Paras 5 and 6]
