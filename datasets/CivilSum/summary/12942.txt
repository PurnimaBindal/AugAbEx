Constitution of India, Article 226 - Claim of retrospective pay scale parity - Appellants argue for equal pay for equal work - As Section Officers in Delhi university-affiliated colleges with UGC grant-in-aid, they perform same duties as Government of India or UGC Section Officers - IGC approval and Committee recommendation bring petitioners' pay scales in line with UGC or Government of India counterparts from 1983 onwards - However, appellants entitled to pay scales at least from 1101978 when UGC approved their redesignation as Section Officers - No justification for withholding pay scale parity under the circumstances.
