Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Other Backward Class Certificate found invalid - The argument that the termination order was passed without receiving a valid OBC certificate from a competent authority is not valid since there is no OBC category in the State of Haryana.
Since the petitioner's selection against a vacancy reserved for OBC was null ab initio, the respondents did not commit any illegality in terminating the petitioner's service after issuing a show cause notice.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - OBC Certificate found invalid - The petitioner, who seeks an appointment in the general category, claimed that candidates with lower merit were selected at separate workshops.
However, there are no rules or instructions to show that the petitioner can change his category and seek consideration under it, or that a common select list is prepared for different workshops/divisions.
Thus this plea cannot be entertained.
