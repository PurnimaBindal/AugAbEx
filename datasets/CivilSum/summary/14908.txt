Haryana State Federation of Consumers Co-operative Wholesale Stores Limited, Rules, 1975, Rules 9, 9(a) & 9(b) - Appointing Authority - Managing Director - Board of Directors - The Managing Director is responsible for appointing employees with a basic salary of up to Rs. 250/-, not the Board of Directors.
As a result, any penalties imposed on such employees by the Managing Director are not illegal due to a lack of jurisdiction.
[Para 5]

Haryana State Federation of Consumers Co-operative Wholesale Stores Limited, Rules, 1975 - Appeal - Speaking order - Quasi-judicial authority - When the Registrar of Cooperative Societies decides an employee's appeal, he exercises quasi-judicial powers and is required to issue a speaking order.
In the event that the order is not well-reasoned, it is subject to being quashed.
[Para 6]
