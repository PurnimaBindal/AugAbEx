Constitution of India, Articles 16, 162 and 309 - Orissa Ministerial Service (Method of Recruitment to Posts of Lower Division Assistants in the Offices of Heads of Department) Rules, 1975, Rule 14 - Appointment/Regularisation/Relaxation of Rules - Regularisation of appointments obtained by disregarding the rules cannot be allowed through the provision of relaxation - Using the power of relaxation in such cases will lead to complete suspension of rules, and therefore, regularisation of appointments through relaxation will be deemed illegal.
