Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability pension - Petitioner enrolled in army on 11.12.84 - Medical authorities found no fault with his health at the time - After serving for 6 years and 6 months he was invalidated from service due to Cerebral Tumour - The question is whether this is attributable to army service or not - Court orders a new Medical Board to determine the connection and release disability pension if found related to service - Petitioner can approach High Court again if Board goes against him.
