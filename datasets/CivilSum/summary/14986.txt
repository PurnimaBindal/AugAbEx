Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 226 - Medical reimbursement - Deafness - 'Hearing aid' - Despite refusal of prior permission of the authorities, reimbursement must be given for unsuccessful treatment of deafness by surgery and Nucleus Cochlear Implant - The refusal to grant permission on the ground that "Hearing aid" by Cochlear implant is not reimbursable is arbitrary and contrary to the instruction of the Bank permitting reimbursement for "Cochlear Implant Surgery" - The petitioner's father did his best for his son - Rules and instructions must be liberally construed and technicalities avoided while dealing with human problems, as there can be no mathematical precision while dealing with them.
