According to the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Sections 40 and 2(17), and Indian Penal Code, Section 405 Explanation 2, and Section 406, if a Public Limited Company misappropriates employees' contribution, the company itself is responsible for prosecution, not its Directors.
Directors are not considered principal employers within the context of the ESI Act's Section 2(17) and Section 405 Explanation 2 of the IPC.
[Paras 3 to 7]  B The Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Sections 2(17) and 40, state that in cases where the owner of a factory is a company, it is the company that is the principal employer, not its Directors.
The principal employer can be either the owner or the occupier, depending on the facts of each case.
If there is an owner of the factory, that owner should be considered as the principal employer responsible for contributions.
