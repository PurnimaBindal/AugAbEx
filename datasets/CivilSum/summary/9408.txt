Article 16 of the Constitution of India covers eligibility, selection, and appointment criteria for college principals.
Specifically, the incumbent must have at least 5 years of teaching experience at the level of Head of Department.
Respondent No. 6 had been appointed as the Head of Department since 1993 and was promoted to a regular appointment in 2004.
Their service cannot be considered an ad hoc or stop-gap arrangement because of its longevity.
Furthermore, the eligibility conditions do not distinguish between regular and ad hoc experience.
The selection and appointment of respondent No. 6 were based on their necessary experience and not arbitrary, erroneous, or illegal, according to the respondent college.
