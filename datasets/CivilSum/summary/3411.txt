Constitution of India, Article 309 - Proviso - Department (U.P.
Sales Tax) Rules framed in 1973 - State Government Rules in 1994 - Both valid - Which Rules apply for Promotion: Rule 4 of 1994 or Rule 16 of 1973?
- Both have different criteria and cannot coexist - Later Laws Abrogate Earlier Contrary Laws - 1994 Rules are General Rules and 1973 Rules are Special Rules - Respondents directed to consider the case of petitioners in accordance with Rule 5(i)(A) of 1973 Rules by applying criteria of 1994 Rules.
[Paras 15, 16, 19 and 20]
