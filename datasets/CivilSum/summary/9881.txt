Constitution of India, Article 309 - Rajasthan Subordinate Offices Ministerial Staff Rules, Rule 25(10) - Specifies that LDCs appointed on ad hoc or daily wages basis between 111985 to 31.3.1990 and currently employed shall be appointed on regular basis when a vacancy opens up, subject to passing a performance test conducted by the relevant Head of Department within three years - After the State Government issued notification amending the 1957 Rules for regularisation, respondents were required to conduct the requisite tests - Directives issued for respondents to conduct tests as required.
