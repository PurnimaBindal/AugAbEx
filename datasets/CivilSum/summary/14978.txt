Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. 1 Part 1, Rules 47 and 48 - Efficiency bar - Annual Increments - An employee's efficiency bar can only be stopped if they are deemed unfit to perform their duties and their performance has been evaluated as below average, not solely based on negative remarks in their ACRs.
[Para 8]
Civil Procedure Code, Section 100 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. 1 Part 1, Rules 47 and 48 - Efficiency bar - Annual Increments - The question of whether there is sufficient evidence to stop an employee's efficiency bar is a matter of fact and cannot be revisited in a second appeal.
