Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Recruitment and Promotion Rules, 1977 (Modifications in the annexure attached to the Rules with effect from 17101981) Rule 7 - Promotion-Reversion - Appointing through promotion - Eligibility for the promotion to the post of Chief Mining Engineer starting from April 21, 1980 - With the modification of the relevant entry in the Annexure attached to recruitment and Promotion Rules on 17101981, the appellant became eligible for promotion - Regardless of whether the promotion would have been regularized by the Company from 17101981, the appellant was entitled to be considered for further promotion in the cadre of Deputy General Manager and later G.M.
(T) - Company always considered the appellant as eligible for being elevated to the higher posts of DGM and G.M.
(T) - Although the promotee lacked the required experience and possessed inadequate qualification - Under the modified criteria, he was eligible for appointment as Chief Mining Engineer on 17101981 - It would be unfair to reverse the appellant after so many years, especially when he acquired eligibility for promotion on the modification of the relevant entry in the annexure to the Rules - Technical objection dismissed.
