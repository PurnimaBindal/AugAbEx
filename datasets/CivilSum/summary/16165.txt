As per Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1988 and Article 16 of the Constitution of India, Rule 3(h), two posts for Factory Inspectors were upgraded to the level of Deputy Chief Inspectors of Factories, redesignated as Deputy Directors of Factories and the solitary post of Deputy Chief Inspector of Factories was upgraded to the level of Joint Chief Inspector of Factories, redesignated as Joint Director of Factories.
In addition, the cadre strength of Deputy Directors of Factories was of two posts, both of which were vacant on the relevant date and required to be filled by promotion.
Since there were no other persons belonging to Scheduled Caste category holding post of Deputy Director of Factories, the roster would operate according to Govt. instructions.
One of the posts would be filled from Scheduled Caste category and the Scheduled Caste candidate was rightfully considered and promoted against reserved vacancy for members of Scheduled Castes.
The petitioner was not entitled to promotion against the upgraded post that he was holding before its upgradation.
[Para 10 and 11] B Rule 3(h) of Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1988, and Article 16 of the Constitution of India were taken into consideration and not only the incumbents in lower ranks were promoted but they were also put on probation subject to the approval of PPSC Government's intention was to create new posts and as such, the upgraded post was deemed to be a promoted post.
