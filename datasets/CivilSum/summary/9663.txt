According to the Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 and the West Bengal Urban Public Distribution System (Maintenance and Control) Order, 2003, appointment of a Modified Ration Distributor must follow statutory norms and receive approval.
The selection process for the appellant finding the most suitable candidate occurred under norms from 1985.
However, a new selection process under the 2003 norms must occur after a declaration of vacancy, which did not happen in this case.
Therefore, the old vacancy cannot be filled, even though the appellant found the most suitable candidate 21 years ago, as that would unfair to others entitled to apply under the 2003 order.
Seeking approval for the appellant's appointment was without jurisdiction, and the appeal was dismissed.
