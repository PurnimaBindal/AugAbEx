Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 4 - Stoppage of increments/Major penalty - The penalty of stoppage of an annual grade increment with cumulative effect is considered a major penalty - The imposition of such penalty is possible without full compliance with the procedures prescribed for imposing major penalty under the Rules.
[Para 6] B. Suspension/Back wages - The employee, who is suspended and not acted against by the Disciplinary Authority and is subsequently reinstated without a condition, is generally entitled to all consequential benefits - However, some exceptional cases may arise where the competent authority may refuse to grant full wages on reinstatement - The authority has been directed to reconsider the matter and make a decision afresh.
