The Development officers of LIC are considered as workman under Section 2(s) ID Act as per LIC of India (Staff) Regulations, 1960, Regulation 14.
[Para 4]
If a disciplinary proceeding is initiated through a show cause notice and the workman fails to reply, the competent authority has the right to take suitable punishment measures.
The responsibility lies with the workman to provide credible evidence of their response being served on the competent authority.
In the absence of evidence, simple claims from the workman are insufficient.
This rule is specified under LIC of India (Staff) Regulations, 1960, Schedule III.
