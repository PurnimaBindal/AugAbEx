Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Retirement benefits - Interest - The petitioner retired on 31.10.2003, but the respondents did not release and pay his retirement benefits.
Interest is allowed for the delay in payment.
If the petitioner finds that his benefits have not been properly calculated, he can agitate the questions and initiate appropriate original proceedings for appropriate relief.
The respondents are directed to pay interest at a rate of 10% pa. to the petitioner for the delayed payment.
[Paras 3 and 4]
