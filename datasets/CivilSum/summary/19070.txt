The Indian Railways Act, 1890, Sections 82-A and 82-C, along with the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 2(d), relate to a claim petition.
In a specific case, where a claimant's daughter of 7 years of age died in an accident, the claim petition was filed.
The UOI contended that the mother could not be considered as a dependent of her deceased minor daughter, which was rejected.
It was held that Section 82-A casts no-fault liability on the Railway Administration and entitles a person who has suffered loss to maintain an action to claim compensation.
