Paragraph A of the text highlights the importance of adhering to the terms and conditions set out in the Bipartite Settlement, which includes the deemed abandonment of service for workmen who absent themselves from duty without sanction.
The enforcement of this settlement does not require a regular inquiry, if undisputed facts suggest the workman did not return to work or furnish an adequate explanation.
Paragraph B of the text asserts that the workman must provide evidence of not earning anything during their absence from work if they file a claim under Section 17-B.
This section can only be invoked if the workman submits an affidavit attesting to their unemployment.
