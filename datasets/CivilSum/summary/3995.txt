Constitution of India, Article 226 - Enquiry - Respondent No. 5/Chief Engineer, suspended, pending enquiry - Due to the suspension of Respondent No. 5, Respondent No. 4 filled in as Chief Engineer - Respondent No. 4 became the Enquiry Officer, but would only remain in the position until Respondent No. 5 was reinstated - Legal proceedings initiated by Respondent No. 5 challenged the appointment of Respondent No. 4 as Chief Engineer - The court ruled that Respondent No. 4 was biased and therefore could not make a fair decision, rendering his report against Respondent No. 5 invalid.
