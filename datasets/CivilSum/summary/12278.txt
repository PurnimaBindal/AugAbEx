Jammu & Kashmir Daily Rated Workers/Work Charged Employees (Regulation) Rules, 1994, Rules 2(b), 4 & 8 - Regularisation - Regularisation of daily Rated workers' services - Rule 4 specifies that one must have worked for a period of seven years continuously as a Daily Rated Worker to be eligible for regularisation - Casual labour and Daily Rated Worker are not different, with the nature of work and continuity of employment being the determining factors - If a worker continues for seven years, their employment cannot be termed casual - They fall under the definition of daily rated worker and are entitled to the benefit of the regularisation rule - If their services are terminated in violation of Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, they have the right to be reinstated with back wages.
