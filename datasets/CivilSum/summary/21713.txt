According to Sections 7 and 34(1) of Air Corporations Act, 1953, the Corporation has powers and functions which include reservation for SC/ST.
The Central Government's directions under Section 34(1) to provide reservation in appointments are not outside the scope of Section 34(1).
Even though Section 7(1) talks about the Corporation's functions and the latter part talks of its powers to be exercised for proper performance of its functions, sub-section (2) mentions the functions in sub-section (1) as powers bestowed upon the Corporation.
Thus, regulating the service conditions of its employees is not only a power but a function too of the Corporation.
Hence, the Central Government's directives to provide reservation in appointments are not beyond the ambit of Section 34.
