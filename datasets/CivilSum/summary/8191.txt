Fixed term appointment under Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F - Automatic termination by efflux of time - Mala fide termination - Unfair Labour Practice - Labour Court found that the workman's services were terminated by way of unfair labour practice and that the policy of the management was mala fide - Termination cannot be covered under Section 2(oo)(bb) and is in violation of Section 25-F [Para 4]
Back wages under Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - In case of forced unemployment, workman will be entitled to reinstatement with full back wages if not gainfully employed during that period - Principle laid down in Hari Palace's case 1979 P.L.R.
720 shall come into play.
[Para 6]
Photocopies of service documents under Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F - Workman can exhibit photocopies of service documents, and the management cannot object to it since they could have produced the originals themselves - No adverse inference can be drawn against the workman for non-production of originals.
