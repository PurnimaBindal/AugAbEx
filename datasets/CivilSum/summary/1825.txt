Constitution of India, Article 16 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(m), Note 6 - Date of Birth - Application for correction in date of birth made after 28 years of joining service - The rejection of the application was appropriate - An application for correction of date of birth should be filed within a reasonable amount of time as set by any relevant rule or order, or if no such time exists - A birth certificate that is produced belatedly cannot be used to correct the date of birth.
[Paras 38, 40, 36 and 43]
