Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 162 - Dearness allowance - Ad hoc interim relief - In 1972, ad hoc interim relief was granted and was in excess of dearness allowance calculated on an increase cycle of 8 points in the Consumer Price Index - Instead of ordering lump sum recovery of excess amount, the increase should be adjusted according to the pay-slab of a Govt. servant - The State Govt.'s decision to recover the excess amount in a phased manner is not arbitrary, unfair, unreasonable or illegal.
