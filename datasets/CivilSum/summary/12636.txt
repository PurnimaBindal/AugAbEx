Accountants of Haryana State Co-operative Apex Bank seek pay parity with Accountants of Haryana State Co-operative Land Development Bank Limited from a retrospective date - They are requesting the same scale of 2000-3200 given to Accountants in HSLDB w.e.f.
111986, whereas the same scale was given to petitioners w.e.f 551988 - The plea is that petitioners should be given the same scale of 2000-32000 w.e.f.
111986 as they perform similar duties in the field and office - However, it has been held that pay fixed in one society cannot be used as a basis for granting pay scale in another cooperative society - There cannot be any similarity in the nature of duties and functions of each organization - The working of each organization is separate and distinct with its own level of duties and responsibilities - The petition has been dismissed.
