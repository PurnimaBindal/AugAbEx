Article 341(2) of the Constitution allows only the President to include/exclude caste groups from the Presidential List, the State Legislature/executive has no power over the List.
[Paras 13, 15-17, and 23]

It is unconstitutional to sub-classify castes placed in the Presidential List as it violates Articles 14 and 16(4), which defines them as a homogenous class and prevents further micro-classification.
[Paras 19, 22, 27, 29, 32, 34, 36, 38, 58, 62, 62, 85, 91]

Andhra Pradesh Scheduled Castes (Rationalisation of Reservations) Act, 2000 is ultra vires as it is not a law governing fields in the legislative competence of the State legislature like education and State Public Services.
The Act is tested by the doctrine of pith and substance of its true character.
[Paras 23 and 27, 66, 70, 72, 75, and 80]

The Andhra Pradesh 2000 Act is declared ultra vires as it violates Article 14 of the Constitution by creating micr-classification of Scheduled Castes, which cannot satisfy the test on the basis of population ratio or Constitutional scheme.
[Paras 28, 30, 38, 39, 68, and 81]

The Andhra Pradesh Act of 2000 is unconstitutional as it violates the Constitutional scheme in recognizing class-based, not caste-based reservation, and cannot be used for political issues.
[Paras 37, 68, and 84]
