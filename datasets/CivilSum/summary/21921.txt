Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1970, Section 19(2)(f) - Punjab National Bank (Employees) Pension Regulations, 1995, Regulations 1, 3 - Pension scheme - Late exercise of option - Eligibility - The scheme was published in the Official Gazette and the option was to be exercised within 120 days from notification, as per Regulation 3(3)(b) - The contention that the law was not known and therefore the notification was not enforceable was dismissed - Individual notice was not required - Petition was dismissed.
