Constitution of India, Article 226 - Goa (Rules for admission for Post Graduate Degree Courses of the Goa University at Goa Medical College) Rules, 1998, Rule III - Admission - Eligibility - Post Graduate Courses - The petitioner received their MBBS Degree under the 15% all-India quota in the state of Goa and argues that all students under this quota should be allowed to participate and compete in the state where they studied without being subject to the rule of residence - Despite the rule requiring a 10-year residence in Goa, the respondents are directed to consider the petitioner for admission to the post-graduate course without reference to this requirement.
