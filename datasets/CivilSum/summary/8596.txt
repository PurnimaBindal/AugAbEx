Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300-A - Pension - Reduction in pension - If an individual is found guilty of causing a significant financial loss to the Government, reduction in pension may be considered - In this particular case, the petitioner was found guilty of fabricating a revised compensation award for non-existent tube-wells which resulted in a loss of Rs. 1,75,000/- - The loss was specifically attributed to him after a fair opportunity was given for him to defend himself - While a retiree is entitled to full pension, in cases where there is undeniable evidence of substantial financial loss caused by the petitioner, a reduction of up to 50% in pension may be justified.
