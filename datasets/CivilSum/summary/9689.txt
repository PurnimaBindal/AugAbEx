Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 and 227 - Regularisation of service - Qualification - Air Compressor Operator - The eligibility of an employee due for regularisation is based on prevailing conditions at the time of their initial appointment, rather than at the time when they are to be considered for regularisation.
No qualification was required at the time of the respondent's employment and settlement.
Therefore, the order refusing regularisation solely on the ground of lacking a prescribed qualification should be overturned.
[Paras 9 and 10]
