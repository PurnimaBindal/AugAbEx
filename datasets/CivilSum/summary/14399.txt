Constitution of India, Article 16 - Regularisation - The Recruitment and Promotion Committee recommended the regularisation of the petitioners from 271977.
The petitioners were appointed on an ad-hoc basis on 1111976.
Had they wished to protest, the petitioners should have done so at a later date of regularisation.
The Rules were followed for the regularisation.
[Para 2] B Delay and Laches - In 1981, a seniority list was released, while in 1988, a writ petition was filed to contest the date of regularisation.
The petitioners' claims that they learned of the seniority list before filing the writ petition were not credible.
[Paras 3 and 6] C. Seniority - The officiation associated with an ad-hoc position is not official under the Rules.
It should not be considered for seniority ranking.
Seniority should begin at the time of appointment and not confirmation.
