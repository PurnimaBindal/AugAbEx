Payment of Gratuity Act (39 of 1972), Section 4 - Death of an employee in harness - The petitioner, in this case, the wife of the employee, had occupied an official quarter beyond the stipulated period - Damages for the unauthorized occupation cannot be deducted from the gratuity payable to the petitioner - The gratuity is considered as a property - Any damages that may need to be recovered under the law can be done so following the procedure established by the law.
