The Constitution of India, Articles 16, 23, 39, 41, 42, states that equal payment must be given for equal work regardless of a temporary appointment and a fixed salary.
Regarding the fixed salary for the post of Instructor, the petitioner received a lower pay scale compared to instructors from other centers.
Even though instructors are working in different trades with different skills, they are considered equivalent in terms of their post.
Therefore, no one can be treated at a lower footing, and the payment of fixed wages less than the minimum pay scale is unconstitutional, violative of the principle of equal pay for equal work, and cannot be sustained.
