Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal posts/Revision of equal pay scales/Pay scales - When two posts are equated, if there is a revision in the pay scale of one post, then there should be a similar revision in the other post as well.
However, it should be noted that the rule applicable in the case of posts equated does not apply merely because the incumbents of the two posts were drawing pay in equal pay scale.
AIR 1989 SC 19 is followed.
