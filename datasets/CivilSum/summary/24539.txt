The policy of grant of Selection grade and Suppertime scale after 16 and 18 years continuous service in once scale/cadre is based on equitable principle to avoid stagnation, heart- burning, demoralization of employees.
Transfer of employee from one department to another would be immaterial if the selecting and appointing agency is the same.
Selection grade or Suppertime scale cannot be denied merely because the employee had worked in some other Govt. Deptt.
for some time.
For the purpose of grant of Selection grade & Suppertime scale, his service rendered in other department cannot be ignored especially when that department has been giving the benefit taking into account service rendered by its employees in the department refusing the same on that ground.
