Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Ad hoc appointment - Implementation of the principle of 'last come first go' - Termination of ad hoc appointees - The general rule of length of service determines seniority, but in cases of selection-based appointments, seniority is based on merit - The petitioners have not provided evidence of their appointment orders or the merit list used during their selection process - Therefore, it is not possible to determine their seniority - If the petitioners have not undergone a regular selection process or are not included in the list of selected candidates, they have no right to complain about the termination of their services.
