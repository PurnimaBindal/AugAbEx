According to the Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(s), 2-A and 10(1)(c), a workman who is employed as a contract labourer cannot be considered a direct employee of the management unless evidence suggests otherwise.
In this case, the petitioner worked for the management through a contractor and received wages from the contractor, as evidenced by wage slips.
Although the management signed the wage register for calculation purposes, it does not make the petitioner their direct employee.
The labour court made their decision based on evidence and there was no illegality found.
Therefore, the court dismissed the petition.
