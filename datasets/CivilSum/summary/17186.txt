Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Promotion - Post of Senior Stenographer - Petitioners, who are Head Assistants, contend that their seniority was ignored and were not even considered for promotion to the post of Senior Scale Stenographer, which was filled by a private respondent who was a Senior Assistant - Respondents argue that the private respondent was found suitable for the post by Departmental Promotion Committee due to the absence of Recruitment Rule - The court, however, holds that since the private respondent was a Senior Assistant, all Senior Assistants and Head Assistants had to be given an opportunity to compete - It is further inferred that in absence of Recruitment Rules proscribing the mode of recruitment, the procedure for such recruitment should be fair and reasonable - The petition is allowed, impugned order is quashed, and official respondents are directed to fill up the vacancy either by direct recruitment or by promotion, provided all those eligible possessing qualifications required for the post are given the opportunity to compete with each other.
