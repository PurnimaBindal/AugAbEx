[Para 5] B Admissions/Education - Admissions to Medical Courses of MBBS/BDS - As per rule, the Admission Committee is responsible for the admission process and the Principal cannot grant admission against free/paid seats at his discretion without the Committee's recommendation.
[Para 6] C. Admissions/Education - A petitioner granted admission against a payment seat in the second counselling cannot challenge the admission given to those higher in merit against free seats as she did not raise any grievances regarding her admission to other institutions in the petition.
