Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 25-F - Termination - If there is a possibility for reference regarding the validity of termination to be made to a Labour Court, the reference would not become invalid.
The Labour Court would still have the power to decide such reference, even if while examining the validity of termination it came to the conclusion that the termination is retrenchment.
[Para 16]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Conditions of Appointment and Service Regulations, 1952, Regulation 14(10)(c) - Termination - Admittedly, the workman had been in the service of DTC for 31 years as a regular employee.
Merely because the workman remained absent for some time unauthorisedly, it would not be deemed that he wanted to resign from his job.
The Labour Court rightly held the termination as illegal.
