The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226, 227 mention the claim for parity in the pay scale of Workshop Attendants/Laboratory Assistants.
However, different pay scales based on educational qualifications have been prescribed.
As per the proposition of law laid by the Hon'ble Supreme Court in 'State of Mysore Versus P. Narasing Rao,' unequals cannot be treated equally.
Therefore, the respondents are justified in their action.
