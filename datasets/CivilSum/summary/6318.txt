Rule 25-A of Rajasthan Civil Services Conduct Rules, 1971 and Rule 16 of Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958 discuss the principles of natural justice in cases of second marriage and the penalty of dismissal from service.
The petitioner's misconduct was not serious enough to warrant the maximum penalty of dismissal.
However, the disciplinary authority failed to issue a show cause notice, which is a clear violation of the principles of natural justice.
The authority acted mechanically in immediately terminating the petitioner's services without proper consideration.
