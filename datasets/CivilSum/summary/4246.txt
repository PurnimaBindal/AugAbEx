Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Qualifications/Appointment - Eligibility - Application for the post - Applicant possessing higher qualifications than those prescribed for the post - Applicant was rejected on the ground that the qualifications required for that post were lower than those possessed by the applicant - Guidelines disqualifying persons possessing higher qualifications and preventing them from applying for lower posts cannot be approved - However, if the petitioner does not have the required marks for the higher post, he cannot be debarred from applying for the lower posts - The rejection of his application is therefore deemed arbitrary.
[Paras 4 and 7]
