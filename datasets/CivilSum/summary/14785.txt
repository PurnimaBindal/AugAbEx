In a case under Section 409 of the Indian Penal Code, which dealt with criminal breach of trust by a public servant, the trial was prolonged for 12 years.
The order of acquittal of the accused was upheld on the sole ground of the violation of the Constitution of India's Article 21, which guarantees a speedy trial.
However, it is important to note that the acquittal will not have any impact on departmental proceedings.
This information was cited from the 1989 (1) Recent Criminal Reports 139 (SC).
