Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Casual workers - Absorption - Petitioners were initially taken in various divisions of the Andhra Pradesh State Electricity Board as casual labourers - After reaching an agreement, the Workers Union and the Electricity Board allowed the petitioners to apply for absorption - However, the respondents refused to absorb them considering their age, even though no age qualification was mandated for their employment as casual labourers - The age qualification cannot be applied to ex-casual labourers, who are being considered for regular absorption under an agreement - Therefore, the respondents have been directed to consider the cases of the petitioners for regular appointment without disqualifying them on the grounds of crossing the age of 25 during their engagement as casual labourers.
