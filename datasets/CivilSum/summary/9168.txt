Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Quota Rule/Appointment/Promotion - Selection for promotion made by selection committee with consideration of seniority - Promotions made in excess of promotee quota on temporary basis but continued - Eligibility and efficiency of promotees not in question - Seniority of promotees and direct recruits to be fixed from date of entry in cadre, not from date of vacancy or birth.
