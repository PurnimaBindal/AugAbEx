The Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 and Industrial Disputes Act of 1947 were implemented to address the issue of misconduct by a conductor who was consequently terminated.
The charges against the petitioner included allegations of misappropriation of money by issuing tickets of lower denominations or not issuing them at all.
The Inquiry Officer found no evidence of fraud but noted that if the bus had not been checked, the petitioner may have taken the money.
The petitioner was in the process of issuing tickets when the checking was conducted and no passengers boarded from that bus.
The checking team did not even check the cash of the conductor.
Based on these incomplete findings, the petitioner's livelihood cannot be taken away.
[Paras 10, 11 & 12]
