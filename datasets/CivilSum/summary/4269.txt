Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, Rule 5(iv) - Punishment/Stoppage of increments with cumulative effect - There is no provision for punishment of stoppage of increments with cumulative effect in the rules - Punishment of stoppage of increments with cumulative effect is to be treated as without cumulative effect.

Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Punishment - The penalty imposed must be proportional to the gravity of the misconduct - If the penalty is disproportionate to the gravity of misconduct and nature of charges and the procedure followed in departmental proceedings was not in accordance with the Rules for such penalty, the Court can reduce the penalty but not substitute it.
