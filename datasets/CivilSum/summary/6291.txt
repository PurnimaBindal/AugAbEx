Constitution of India Article 226 - Compassionate appointment - Right - The petitioner requested compassionate appointment nine years after his father's death.
The department had anticipated appointing the petitioner upon maturity.
However, since nine years had elapsed and no post can be reserved indefinitely, the petitioner could not demand compassionate appointment as a right.
The aim of compassionate appointment would be defeated if it was allowed to persist for too long.
As a result, the petition was dismissed.
