Constitution of India, Article 226 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(b) - Age of superannuation - Extension of - The petitioner, who superannuated as Senior Master Technician, was required to perform maintenance, detect defects, and repair test equipment while also performing manual labor tasks.
Despite being designated as a Senior Master Technician, the petitioner can still be considered a workman according to FR 56(b).
The petitioner is entitled to the extended retirement age of 60 under FR 56(b) and to consequential monetary benefits, even though reinstatement is not required since the petitioner is already over 60 years old.
