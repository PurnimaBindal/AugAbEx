The appointment of an unqualified candidate due to an error in the select list must be rectified, and the candidate may be removed after notice.
Such an appointment negatively affects not only those who were not selected but also those who did not apply due to lack of advertised qualifications.
To prevent fraudulent practices, the court cannot condone the appointment of unqualified candidates.
Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 226 govern the selection, qualifications, and appointment procedures.
