Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 14 - Education - Examination - Compartment - Candidates are given two chances to clear the compartment; the first chance is with the old syllabi and the second chance is with the new syllabi for the next session.
If a candidate fails both chances, they cannot challenge the validity of the Bye-Law.
No one has the right to claim two chances, as the University grants them as a concession.
The Board is not obligated to provide any additional chances after the main examination, and the court cannot rewrite the Bye-Laws of the University.
The order of the Single Judge directing the Board to allow the petitioner one more chance with the old syllabi is unfounded, as examination cannot be rendered invalid for a single student, and exam redo is required if the entire examination was invalid due to incorrect syllabi.
The court's jurisdiction is limited to the writ petition's scope.
As a result, the order is revoked, and the writ petition is rejected, allowing the appeal.
