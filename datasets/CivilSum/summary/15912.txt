The petitioner, who is claiming to be dependent on his deceased mother, a former government employee, applied for a compassionate appointment in accordance with government instructions, but his claim was rejected due to his failure to provide evidence that he was indeed dependent on the deceased employee and that their family income did not exceed Rs. 2500.
There was also a fabricated ration card on record suggesting a fraudulent attempt to secure employment.
As a result, the petitioner's claim for compassionate appointment was justifiably rejected, in accordance with the basic conditions described in the government's instructions, with Article 226 of the Constitution of India being applicable.
