The petitioner challenged the allowance of adverse remarks from 1967 and 69 when they were used to stop them from crossing the efficiency bar in 1975.
However, since they waited until 1981 to file the challenge, the petition may be dismissed.
[Para 10] B.
Stopping an employee from crossing the efficiency bar due to old adverse remarks is acceptable if those remarks are still standing.
Therefore, there was no issue with stopping the employee in this case.
[Para 4] C. Instructions provided by the government for recording adverse reports are not legally binding and cannot be challenged in court under Article 226.
[Para 13] D. Allegations of mala fide were not presented in the representation before the authority, and therefore cannot be brought up for the first time in writ jurisdiction.
