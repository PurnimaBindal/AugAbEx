According to Section 9 of Civil Procedure Code, 1908 and Schedule 2 Item 6 of Industrial Disputes Act 1947, a workman cannot approach a civil court for an injunction against termination of employment.
In this case, it was found that the civil court lacked jurisdiction and the proper forum for resolving the dispute is the one constituted under the Industrial Disputes Act.
