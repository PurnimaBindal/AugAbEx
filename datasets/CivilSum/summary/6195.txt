Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - If a question of fact raised before the Labour Court was not noticed in the Award, the High Court can take note of the unrebutted fact and return its independent findings and modify the award accordingly.
[Para 5] B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 25-F - When a workman is transferred resulting in reversion but is justified in refusing to join on the transferred post, it is neither a case of abandonment nor wilful absence from duty.
Further, if the employee presents himself and asks for permission to work on his post but is not allowed, the findings of the Industrial Tribunal that the workman severed his connection with the management are uncalled for and liable to be set aside.
Abandonment of service has to be a voluntary and wilful action on the part of the employee and where the employee merely refused to join duty at a particular place, it cannot be said that he has wilfully abandoned the service.
