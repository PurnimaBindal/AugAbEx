The proviso added to Section 9(3) of the UP Basic Education Act, 1972 provides an embargo against transferring a basic school teacher to a local body.
However, this provision cannot be applied to a clerk working in a Zilla Parishad.

The transfer of an employee cannot be said to have been wholly done at the dictate of a Minister since the board applied its own mind in the transfer even if it was initiated on the advice of the Minister (Basic Education).
Therefore, it cannot be considered that the transfer was due to the order of the Minister alone.
