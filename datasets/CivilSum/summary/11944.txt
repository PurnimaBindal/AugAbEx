Constitution of India, Article 14 and 226 - Promotion - Consideration for promotion - Army officer - Estoppel - In a previous petition, the petitioner claimed that he was unfairly overlooked for promotion due to irrelevant records, and the petition was granted because the respondents did not present any defense.
As a result, the respondents were instructed to reassess the petitioner's promotion case based on all the claims made in the petition, and his case cannot be refused arbitrarily, nor can subsequent rejection orders be based on grounds that could have been raised earlier.
The rejection order was overturned, and the idea of only three considerations will not apply.
The petition is allowed with costs worth Rs. 3000/-.
[Paras 6, 7, and 8]
