The principle of res judicata aims to prevent repeated litigation on the same cause of action and promote fair allocation of resources.
However, it does not apply to fresh causes of action.
In this case, the earlier petition was dismissed due to the reserved status of the posts, but later on, the government appointed general category candidates against the same posts, ignoring the petitioners who ranked higher in merit.
Consequently, the subsequent petition claiming appointment in order of merit cannot be barred by res judicata.
[Paras 5 and 6]
In terms of selection and appointment, Articles 14, 16, and 226 of the Constitution of India provide that those who rank higher in merit have a prior right to claim appointment.
