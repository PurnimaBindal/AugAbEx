Section 39 of the Punjab Municipal Act, 1911 states that the Municipal Committee is prohibited from appointing individuals to provincialised posts, such as the post of Inspectors, and therefore the appointment of Clerks to these posts was not valid.
Therefore, the Government's decision to annul the Committee's resolution was appropriate.
[Paras 7 and 8]

The Municipal Committee is only authorized to make appointments for the posts advertised, and any excessive appointments made above this number must be quashed, even if it means hiring individuals with lower merit.
[Para 8]

The appointments made by the Municipal Committee, for which it is the competent authority, do not require approval from the Government.
However, individuals appointed to these positions have the right to be heard before their appointments are annulled by the Government.
