Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 36(4) - Management engaged an Attorney to present their case in front of the Industrial Tribunal.
- The Union raised an objection on the first day of appearance in court against management's Attorney representation.
- As per the provisions under Section 36(4) of Industrial Disputes Act, management is not allowed to hire an Attorney.
Thus, the Industrial Tribunal appropriately accepted the Union's objection.
