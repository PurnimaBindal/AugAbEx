Constitution of India, Article 311 - If the Standing Orders of the employer do not require it, the delinquent should be given a copy of the enquiry report following the law laid down in Mohd.
Ramzan's case, which applies respectively to orders of punishment passed after 20th November, 1990, and the proceedings pending with regard to orders passed prior to that are not affected by the judgment.
[Paras 14 and 19]
Constitution of India, Article 311 - If non-supply of the copy of enquiry report causes prejudice to the delinquent, the punishment awarded will be vitiated.
The petitioner could not make an effective representation for non-availability of copy of enquiry report.
His right of defence stood materially prejudiced resulting from various discrepancies found in the charge-sheet and the enquiry report.
Order of punishment quashed.
