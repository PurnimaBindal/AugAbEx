Admission - Reservations - Weightage for University students - To gain admission to the MBA course, weightage will be given based on the percentage of marks in the qualifying test.
NRI candidates are not required to pass the qualifying examination, and the weightage is not required to be given to NRI or Sponsored candidates that are reserved.
A special rule made in their case overrides the general provisions related to the preparation of the merit list.
