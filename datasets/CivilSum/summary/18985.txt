Constitution of India, Article 226 - Minimum Wages Act, 1948 - Minimum Wages (Central) Rules, 1950, Rule 29 - Arrears of less wages - Workmen who claimed arrears of less wages were directed by the authority to receive payment with penalty - However, the application for setting aside the ex parte order was dismissed without proper discussion of the averments made in the application - This order was deemed improper and liable to be set aside - The matter was remanded back to the authority to decide the application on its merits.
