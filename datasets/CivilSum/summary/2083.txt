The Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Section 33(2)(b) read with Section 2(rr) clarifies the meaning of "wages for one month."
This includes the correct amount owed to the employee after any deductions at source or statutory deductions.
A small shortfall in payment due to a genuine mistake will not be considered a breach of the mandatory provision for approval under the section.
Since the provision lacks a non-obstante clause, it must be read in harmony with other laws currently in effect.
