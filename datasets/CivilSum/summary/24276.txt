Constitution of India, Arts.
16, 12 - Regularisation of Service - Temporary staff appointed on consolidated pay in the Nodal Centre of University claim for regularisation - State Govt's order prescribing 5 years service for regularisation cannot be applied as Nodal Centre is under the overall supervision and guidance of Central Govt. - Long satisfactory service not a ground for regularisation - However, an increase in consolidated salary is directed.
