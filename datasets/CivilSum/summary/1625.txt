Constitution of India, Article 226 - Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Article 103 - Alternative remedy - The availability of alternate remedies does not preclude the exercise of writ jurisdiction when the authority has acted without jurisdiction.
[Para 20]
Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Section 15 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2A - Retrenchment - Industrial dispute - The Payment of Wages Act Authority does not have jurisdiction over industrial disputes related to retrenchment under Section 2A of the ID Act.
An order issued by such an authority without jurisdiction can be invalidated.
