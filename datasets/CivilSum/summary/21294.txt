Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 11 - Punishment order - Departmental enquiry - Dismissal from service - Enquiry held by the management found defective - Management allowed to present evidence before Labour Court to validate the dismissal order - The Labour Court deemed the dismissal order justified after weighing the evidence - The employer's punishment order concerns them alone and does not require confirmation by the Labour Court to take effect - Unless overturned by a competent authority, the order remains in effect - The dismissal order's effect will go back to its date of issuance, not the date of the award by the Labour Court - The worker is not entitled to back wages until the date of the award.
The ruling in 1980(2) SCR 146 has been overruled.
