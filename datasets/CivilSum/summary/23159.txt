Judicial obstinacy is defined as a pre-conceived opinion or pre-disposition to decide a case in a specific way, which can lead to a lack of impartiality.
If a judge's decision is overruled by a higher court, they must submit to that judgment and cannot re-write the overruled judgment in the same case.
Doing so would demonstrate bias and egoistic judicial obstinacy.
[Paras 33 and 34]
