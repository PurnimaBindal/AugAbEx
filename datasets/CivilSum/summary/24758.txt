According to Section 6(d) and Schedule 3, Item 4 of the Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, co-operative societies must compute payment of bonuses to employees based on their gross profit.
This profit consists of the capital invested and amounts carried forward to Reserve Fund under Section 66 of Mah.
Co-operative Societies Act, with 85% of such capital being deductible.
