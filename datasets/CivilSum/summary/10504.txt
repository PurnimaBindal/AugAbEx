Constitution of India, Article 226 - UP Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 6-N - Termination/Retrenchment/Re-instatement - The Labour Court ruled that the termination was an illegal retrenchment but did not grant the relief of reinstatement - The workman may seek a writ jurisdiction for the relief of reinstatement - The petition was accepted - Petitioner entitled to reinstatement and full back wages - Arrears to be paid in two months.
