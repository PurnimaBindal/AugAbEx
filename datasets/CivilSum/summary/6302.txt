Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability pension - The petitioner, who joined the Air Force on 27121996, became invalided on the same day due to neurosis historical conversations with 40% disability.
He was placed in medical category 'EEE'.
Although there is nothing on record to indicate that the petitioner was suffering from any disability when he joined the Air Force, he served for 14 years, 4 months and 9 days.
It is thus difficult to conclude that the petitioner's disease was a constitutional one, and the respondents have been directed to release disability pension to him.
