Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rajasthan Subordinate Office Ministerial Staff Service Rules, 1957, Rule 15 - Writ of mandamus - Mandamus cannot be used to compel appointment or promotion of an aggrieved person unless there is a violation of Article 14 or 16 of the Constitution - No directions can be given for the creation of promotional avenues when the respondent has given no guarantee for it.
