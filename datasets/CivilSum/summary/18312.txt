The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309, along with the Bihar Police Manual, Rules 826 and 843, deal with compulsory retirement based on overstaying leave.
In a case involving a constable who had to remain on leave due to familial obligations, the authorities imposed a punishment of compulsory retirement without properly considering the circumstances.
However, the punishment was deemed to be excessive and in violation of the Police Manual, and as a result, the constable is entitled to rejoin duty and receive 50% of his salary from the date of his compulsory retirement.
