Civil Appeal Nos. 167-68 of 1985 Constitution of India, Articles 16, 136 - Kerala Ministerial Subordinate service Rules 1956 (as amended in 1980) - Seniority - The old rule was struck down, resulting in new rules being made in compliance with the direction issued by the High Court.
A seniority list was prepared and promotions were given based on the new rules.
As the validity of the new rules were not challenged and promotions were made accordingly, it is not necessary to examine the correctness of the High Court's judgment after such a long period of time.
