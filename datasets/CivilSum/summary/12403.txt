Constitution of India, Article 226 - Golconda Grameen Bank Staff Service Regulations, Regulation 13(2) - Seniority - Employee seniority determined by rank assigned at selection - Petitioners selected simultaneously with respondents but offered positions first through reservation, cannot claim seniority ignoring initial selection rank - Rule of reservation provides only accelerated promotion, not accelerated consequential seniority.
