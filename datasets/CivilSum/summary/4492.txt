Rule 3.26(2) & (3) of Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part I - Extension in service - Retirement - Extension can only be granted to a Chief Engineer for a maximum of three months after reaching the age of superannuation under specific circumstances.
These circumstances require recording sufficient reasons that the employee provides work impossible for any other successor to perform.
Extension should not be granted as a favour to the employee or at the pleasure of the Government.
[Paras 12 to 15]
In the case of a Chief Engineer getting an extension in service in violation of Statutory Rules, Article 226 of the Constitution of India allows for Public Interest Litigation (PIL).
Despite not having a direct interest in the matter, it is possible to file a PIL if there is evidence of large scale violations or abuse of power by the Government or inaction by the Government machinery.
[Paras 9 and 10]
