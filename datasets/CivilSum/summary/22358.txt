According to the Constitution of India, Article 226, the Madhya Pradesh Industrial Relations Act of 1960, Sections 31(3) and 61, and the Madhya Pradesh Industrial Employment Standing Orders Rules of 1963, Rule 2(i), workmen cannot be considered as permanent employees under Madhya Pradesh law if they have completed six months of satisfactory service, unless they have served in one or more positions with clear vacancies.
If there is no such clear vacancy, the Court cannot issue a directive to categorize the workman as a permanent employee.
