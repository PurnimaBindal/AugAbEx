Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 1(3)(b), 7(7) - Order of payment by controlling Authority - Appeal against by employer - The appellate authority has passed an order without any reasons, even though the question of the period of working was required to be considered - The applicability of the claim preferred by the workman in view of Section 1(3)(b) and (c) of the 1972 Act should also have been taken into account - The order is quashed and the matter is remanded for a fresh decision.
