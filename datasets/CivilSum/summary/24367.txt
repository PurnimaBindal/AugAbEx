Constitution of India, Articles 311, 16 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 12.21 and 1624(ix) - Probation - Police Constable on Probation - Dismissed from service for being absent from duty for 22 days without holding an enquiry - The order was not punitive - Probationer does not have the right to the post - The employer has the opportunity to observe the probationer's performance during the probation period and terminate their services if they are not suitable for the post.
