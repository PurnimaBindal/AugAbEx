Workman Dismissal - Labour Court found Domestic Enquiry to be vitiated - Management refused permission to present evidence - After discussing the fairness of the enquiry, permission was requested - Tribunal should have given the management the chance to adduce evidence after determining that the domestic enquiry was illegal and invalid.
This was based on the decision of the Supreme Court in the case of workmen of Firestone Tyre and Rubber Co of India Pvt. Ltd. v. The Management & Ors. [Paras 4 and 7].
