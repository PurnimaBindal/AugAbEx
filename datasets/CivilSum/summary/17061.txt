The petitioner, a teacher, was transferred from one school to another as part of the respondent state's policy to improve teacher services for students in different government schools.
This policy involved withdrawing excess staff from some schools and assigning them to others with staff shortages.
The transfer order was passed in the public interest and there is no evidence of jurisdictional issues or mala fides.
There should be no interference.
[Paras 2 and 3]
