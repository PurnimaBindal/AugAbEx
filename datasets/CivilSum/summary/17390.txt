Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Promotion - Candidates may send applications for promotion to the post of Senior Lecturer through the proper channel or directly to the Director, Research and Medical Education on the basis of seniority and merit - The purpose of inviting applications through the proper channel is only to check facts with the office - Even if a candidate's application via proper channel is late, their direct application received on time must still be considered - Any faults on the part of the office should not negatively affect the candidate's promotion.
