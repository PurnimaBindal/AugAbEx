Constitution of India, Articles 12, 14, 16, 38(1), 39(e) and 43 - Regularisation of services - Daily wage workers - High Court directions to a Govt. Company to frame a scheme for regularisation of daily wage workers with a term of over 5 years is in line with the Constitution under Articles 38(1), 39(e) and 43 - Govt. establishments have a constitutional obligation to consider the absorption of long-term workers and alleviate the difficulties they face while working on a daily wage basis - A Govt. Company may, however, establish the necessary regular workforce to accomplish this while developing the scheme - State Haryana v. Piara Singh, 1992(3) SCT 201 : AIR 1992 SC 2130 and Raj Narain Prasad v. State of UP, 1998(8) SCC 477 followed.
