Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 8 - Minor penalty - The petitioner, serving as a Superintendent, was penalized for failing to reply within the prescribed time resulting in a huge delay in filing the reply in Court - Respondent-State was penalized with Rs. 5,500/- to be recovered from the erring officer - A reasonable punishment was imposed halting only two increments without cumulative effect - No merit to the argument that no action was taken against other officers engaged in similar delays - Interference is not necessary.
