Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo), (bb), 25-F - Workman - Termination of service - Petitioner worked for two years - No notice or pay in lieu of or terminal benefits given nor provisions of 1947 Act complied - The service did not come to an end on the expiry of the contract - The Labour Court recorded finding of fact that the appointment was on contract and application for appointment was so made - In view of the finding of fact recorded by Labour Court and also the fact that when service is terminated on expiry of contract as per Section 2(oo) (bb) provisions of Section 25-FF are not attracted - This termination is not retrenchment as defined in 1947 Act - Hence, there is no infirmity or illegality in award requiring interference.
