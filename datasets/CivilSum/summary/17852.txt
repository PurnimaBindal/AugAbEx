The eligibility criteria for admission in MD courses in Medical College includes the condition that candidates who are admitted in the Diploma course are not eligible for admission to MD course later on, even if they had applied for admission to the MD course.
This condition aims to prevent wastage of seats due to surrendering seats after the finalization of admission.
The petitioner was deprived of admission despite being an eligible candidate.
The petitioner was eventually granted admission, but the illegally admitted candidate was allowed to continue the course because they had already undergone it for a considerable period.
This prompted the court to allow the petition.
