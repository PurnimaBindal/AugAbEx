Article 226 of the Constitution of India stipulates that in a departmental inquiry regarding reduction to lower rank, an opportunity of hearing must be given to the petitioner before passing an order.
Here, the Punishing Authority did not provide such an opportunity.
The Appellate Authority did not provide any reasoning for dismissing the appeal and as such it is a non-speaking order that should be quashed.
[Paras 7 and 8]
In a departmental inquiry under Article 226, a de novo hearing should be carried out if the petitioner was not given a proper hearing previously.
In this case, the petitioner was not given an opportunity of hearing by the punishing authority who initiated the inquiry about 24 years ago.
However, it is not justified to remand the case back to the punishing authority for re-hearing given that the petitioner has already retired from service during the pendency of the writ petition.
[Paras 9 and 10]
A non-speaking order without assigning any reasons is not sustainable under Article 226 of the Constitution of India and is liable to be quashed.
