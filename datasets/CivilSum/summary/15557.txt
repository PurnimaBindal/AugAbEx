Constitution of India, Article 226 - Extension of Service - It is not guaranteed that service will be extended beyond the initial term of engagement.
The decision to grant or deny an extension of service is up to the discretion of Air Headquarters or a similar authority.
Review by the judiciary is limited to determining if the discretion was used unfairly, discriminately, or with bad intentions.
The petitioner did not mention any violation of natural justice principles and therefore cannot be granted an extension of service.
