Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Rules, 1996, Rule 274 - Waiting list - Select list is not valid indefinitely - Select list exists for only one year as per rules - Petitioner applied after select list expiry - No right can be claimed - Validity of select list is to consider new candidates - Waiting list inclusion does not guarantee appointment - Fresh appointment without appointing waitlisted candidate is possible.
[Paras 21, 22, 23]
