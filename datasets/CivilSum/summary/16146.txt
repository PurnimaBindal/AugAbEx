Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Compulsory retirement - Stigma - Reasonable opportunity - State Award - An order of compulsory retirement cannot be passed without dropping charges - The petitioner, a Doctor and State award winner, was not afforded a reasonable opportunity of being heard before the order was passed - Violation of Article 311 - The petitioner's continuance in service is in public interest as she is not a dead-wood - Failure to consider petitioner's performance that led to her State Award led to arbitrary and unreasonable order - Violative of Article 14 - Order quashed and petitioner to receive all consequential benefits of pay and allowances with 12% interest.
