Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Selection - Prescribed qualification - A petitioner was refused promotion for the post of Lecturer because he did not have the required qualifications in September 1993.
However, he was later interviewed based on a circulated circular in October 1994 after his results had been declared.
As a left-out case, he was eventually promoted during the pendency of a writ petition, and the respondents were ordered to consider his appointment date as the same as those who were selected and recommended on 20111995, only for the purpose of seniority and fixing of pay without arrears.
[Paras 4 and 5]
