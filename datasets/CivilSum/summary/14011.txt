Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India state that the district or state of residence of an individual cannot be a basis for discrimination in the matter of employment under the state.
A petitioner who originally belonged to the concerned district but was not considered for appointment solely because her husband belonged to another district cannot be denied appointment.
This is especially true when candidates below her in the merit list were given appointments.
[Para 9 and 10] B.
A select list or waiting list that has expired due to no fault of a candidate cannot come in the way of appointment if the candidate was otherwise entitled to be appointed.
Denial of appointment to a petitioner who was selected for the post and kept on a waiting list is a violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
