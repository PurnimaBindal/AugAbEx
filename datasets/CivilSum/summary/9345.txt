Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 311 - Disciplinary proceedings/Criminal proceedings/Double jeopardy - No hindrance exists on initiating disciplinary proceedings as well as criminal proceedings on the same charges - Disciplinary Authority not required to wait for the criminal court's decision in every case - Charges in the disciplinary proceedings should be viewed from the perspective of discipline and integrity among the administration's staff, which may not be relevant in the criminal proceedings, and thus, a rigid formula that holds up disciplinary proceedings until the criminal proceedings conclude does not hold true.
