Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Non-supply of documents - The rules of Evidence Act do not apply in departmental proceedings - In cases where a rule of procedure is violated, the entire proceedings cannot be vitiated unless it is shown that prejudice was caused to the charged official - The documents produced in the inquiry proceedings were known to the delinquent and were listed - No prejudice can be said to be caused to him.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8(8) - Disciplinary proceedings - Assistance of co-employee - Permission of punishing authority - Not required - Proceedings cannot be held to be vitiated merely because the delinquent was not informed of his entitlement to assistance.
