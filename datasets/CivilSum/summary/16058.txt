Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - Handicapped persons - Policy of the State Government regarding transfer in couple cases and physically handicapped persons - Transfer order of the petitioner passed in consideration to accommodate her due to her handicap as well as that of her husband - Instead of accommodating the handicapped couple who have worked at the same place for 18 years, the orders were cancelled to accommodate another couple - However, the case of the handicapped couple should be given priority due to their hardship and in line with the Government policy for the handicapped employees - Therefore, the cancellation orders were quashed and transfer orders restored - The general principle of not interfering in transfers was set aside due to the unique circumstances of the case.
[Paras 6 and 8]
