Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work/Pay scales - Option regarding pay scales - The petitioner chose the pay scale of Rs. 90-140 at the time of appointment - Subsequently, the pay scale of Rs. 90-140 was revised to Rs. 150-350, and then to Rs. 480-760 - The petitioner can still claim any applicable pay scale on the basis of equal pay for equal work and is not debarred from claiming the pay scale of Rs. 480-760.
