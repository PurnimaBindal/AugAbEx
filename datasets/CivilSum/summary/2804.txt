Sections 2-A(2), 10 and 33(2)(b) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 allow for dismissal from service, and while an Industrial Tribunal may approve such dismissal, a Labour Court can still examine the validity of the termination/dismissal order in an application under Section 2-A(2) or Section 10 of the Act.
The Labour Court correctly held that if the enquiry is flawed, the management must provide evidence to support the dismissal order.
