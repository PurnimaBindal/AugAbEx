The writ of mandamus can be issued against any authority or person performing public duties, not just statutory authorities.
Schools performing public duty while imparting education also fall into this category.
[Para 8]
In cases where part of the cause of action has taken place, every writ court will have jurisdiction.
[Para 7]
If the alternative remedy under the Act is likely to frustrate the purpose and the authority under the Act is the same against which mandamus is sought, then such an alternate remedy is neither speedy nor efficacious.
[Para 9]
