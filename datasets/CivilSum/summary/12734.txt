Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission - Reservation - Relaxation for SC/BC - Admission in 5 year degree course of Veterinary Science - Eligibility is having 50% marks in 10+2 (Medical group) or equivalent - SC/ST can get admission in seats claimed by them in their category - In entrance test they are required to score minimum pass marks, which is 40% for SC/ST and 50% for general - A reduction of 5% in minimum marks is allowed - Petitioners No. 1 and No. 2 scored 395% and 3825% marks respectively - The petitioners are not entitled to double advantage and cannot claim a seat - University is responsible for vagueness in the prospectus and is directed to avoid ambiguity in the future.
