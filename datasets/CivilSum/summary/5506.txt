Constitution of India, Article 226 - Annual Confidential Report - Annual confidential reports are subjective assessments recorded in the shape of a confidential report.
Breach of government instructions while recording the report does not invalidate it.
[Para 10]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Annual Confidential Report - Adverse Remarks - Since the recording of an annual confidential report is a subjective assessment, a civil court cannot question its accuracy.
