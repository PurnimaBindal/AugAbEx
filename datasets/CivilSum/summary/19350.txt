Central Government Servants (Medical Attendance) Rules, Rule 5(1)(c) - Reimbursement - Medical expenses - Both husband and wife are Central Govt. employees - In case the wife holds a higher post than that of her husband and they jointly apply for the reimbursement of medical expenses incurred in the treatment of the husband's mother, the husband is eligible to receive reimbursement as a dependent of his wife - The joint application is deemed maintainable even if the husband has been declared as a dependent of his wife - The petitioner's dependent mother is also entitled to medical benefits and all bills will be settled in accordance with the rules based on the salary of the husband.
