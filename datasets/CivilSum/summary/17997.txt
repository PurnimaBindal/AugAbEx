The petitioner in the Army Service was downgraded in Medical category in 1987 and was deputed to Sri Lanka to face bullet in 1988.
After repatriation, he was asked to choose between promotion and honorary rank, and he opted for promotion which he received in 1988.
In 1990, he was discharged on grounds of disability which is deemed illegal since he was considered fit for promotion.
He was also given much difficult and responsible duties, and was entitled to be considered for Honorary rank when he was discharged.
The order of discharge has been quashed with all consequential benefits.
[Paras 7 to 10]
