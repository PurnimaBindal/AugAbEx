Constitution of India, Articles 315 and 226 - Public Service Commission - Commission should not bring politics into question for actions taken due to changes in ruling party, as it is a constitutional independent body.

Constitution of India, Articles 315 and 226 - Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, Section 2(c) - "Public servant" - Chairman and members of the Haryana Public Service Commission are public servants and have a duty to uphold the mandate of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - they are amenable to the provisions of the Act if corrupt and unfair means are found to be involved.

Constitution of India, Articles 315 and 226 - Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, Section 2(c) - "Public Servant" - Public Service Commission - To claim registration of FIR without conducting a preliminary fact-finding inquiry will be embarrassing.
[Para 13]
Constitution of India, Articles 315 and 226 - Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, Section 2(c) - Judicial review - Investigation by police cannot be equated to the power of judicial review under Article 226.
[Paras 15 & 16]
Constitution of India, Articles 315 and 320(3) - Power and action of the government cannot be by mala fide - Full bench in Jaskaran Singh Brar's case no such law has been laid down that State Govt. has no power to take out certain posts from the purview of the Commission.
[Para 17]
Constitution of India, Articles 315 and 320 - Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, Section 2(c) - Public Service Commission - Chairman and members of the Commission cannot claim immunity from the provisions of Prevention of Corruption Act merely because in the matter of removal they are not less than High Courts and Supreme Court Judges.
[Para 18]
Constitution of India, Articles 315 and 326 - Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, Section 2(c) - Public Service Commission - Commission cannot maintain a petition for the protection of its Chairman and members.
