According to Article 309 and 14 of the Constitution of India and the Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules (U.P.
), 1930, specifically Rule 49A pertaining to Suspension, if a government servant is detained in custody, they are considered to be under suspension without the need for an order from the appointing authority.
However, this deemed suspension only applies to the period of detention in custody and cannot be extended without an express order from the appointing authority.
The Rules provide for deemed suspension in cases of detention on criminal charges or otherwise, but this provision is not arbitrary.
The decision in 1995(1) E SC 329 (All) has been overruled.
