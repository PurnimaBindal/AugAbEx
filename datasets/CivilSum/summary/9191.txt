Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Scope - Only an existing right can be the basis of a claim under Section 33-C(2) of the Act.
[Para 3]

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Award - Challenge in writ proceedings impugned order of Labour Court awarding to respondent a sum on account of his claim for leave salary and pay in lieu of notice - The scope of Section 33-C(2) is limited - The right of benefit that is sought to be computed in proceedings must be an existing right, i.e., a right already adjudicated upon or provided for - The Labour Court exceeded its jurisdiction under Section 33-C(2) of the Act in adjudicating upon issues raised between parties and in making award in favor of respondent - Award set aside.
[Paras 3, 4 and 5]
