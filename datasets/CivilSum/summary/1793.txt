Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Award - Termination setting aside - Compensation in lieu of reinstatement - The employee was not appointed through a regular selection process for any permanent position - The Tribunal denied reinstatement on the grounds of illegality - However, the labour court rightly exercised its discretion under section 11-A of the Act - In such cases, where there is a long gap between termination and reinstatement, such as ten years in this case, the employee shall be awarded compensation in lieu of reinstatement.
