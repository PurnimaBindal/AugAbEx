The Punjab Services of Engineers (Class II PWD B&R) Rules, 1965, Rule 6 deals with promotions and reversions, and a petitioner who was promoted on an ad hoc basis challenged his reversion orders and sought regularisation of his promotion, but it was found that no vacancy had become available in his source even at the time of his reversion, making the reversion orders quite proper.
[Paras 12 and 14]
The petitioners who relied on instructions for seeking regularisation of his promoted post were informed that the instructions were meant for a different type of case and it was not the case of petitioner that he was the only candidate eligible for promotions.
Therefore, they could not receive any benefit on the basis of said instructions.
[Paras 15 and 18]
