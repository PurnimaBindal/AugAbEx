Termination of service - Maintainability - The respondent was appointed by a Selection Committee headed by their own father, making the selection and appointment of respondent void ab initio due to principles of natural justice - Therefore, it cannot be sustained in the eyes of the law.
[Paras 8, 10] B Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Services of Employees) Act, 1974, Section 8 - College affiliated with Punjab University - Jurisdiction of civil court - As Section 8 of the Act bars the jurisdiction of civil court, only the remedy provided under the statute can be availed.
[Para 12] C. Order of termination - Suit for permanent injunction - Since the plaintiff was no longer in service, they cannot file a suit for permanent injunction and can only file for a declaration.
[Para 13] D Suit for permanent injunction - Non-joinder of necessary party - Since 90% of the salaries of the staff of the College are shared by Punjab University, who was not impleaded as a party, the suit is bad for non-joinder of necessary party, and the impleadment of Punjab University in the suit was necessary.
