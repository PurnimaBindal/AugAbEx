The Head of the Haryana Transport Department is authorized under Rule 14 of Haryana Civil Services (P&A) Rules to examine and review any case where a subordinate has passed an order to penalize an employee, with the requirement of an opportunity of hearing if the penalty is to be enhanced.
Rule 35 of 1995 Rules extends the application of this rule to Group C employees of the Transport Department.
Therefore, the Financial Commissioner's exercise of suo moto review over the General Manager falls within jurisdiction.
In addition, an employee who obtains government service through a forged license and causes loss or damage to government property may face dismissal as a result of their misconduct, with no interference under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
