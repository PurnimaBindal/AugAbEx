The Karnataka Administrative Tribunal (Salaries and Allowances and Conditions of Service of Chairman, Vice-Chairman and Members) Rules, 1986, Rule 3 Proviso is being challenged for violating Articles 14, 16 and 39(d) of the Indian Constitution, and this challenge is not estopped by the petitioner having drawn their salary in accordance with the rules.
Section 35 of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 prescribes the manner in which payment of the salary to the Chairman, Vice- Chairman or Member is to be computed.
The interpretation of these Rules is the same in both English and Hindi, and the omission of the expression "pension equivalent" in the English text of the proviso to Rule 3 does not change the intent and purpose thereof to deduct "gross pension" including the commuted portion thereof in computing the actual amount payable to a Member or Vice-Chairman of the Tribunal towards their salary.
Additionally, words "Gross Pension" in the proviso to Rule 3 of the Rules will include all pension including commuted pension.
