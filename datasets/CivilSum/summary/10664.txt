Constitution of India, Article 14 - Pension - Promotion - Computation of reitral benefits - Eligibility qualifications - A long-serving employee who has been promoted and allowed to work for 15 years shall not be denied the benefit of the promotion post, including the retiral benefits.
The eligibility qualifications for promotion are to be determined at the time of appointment/promotion, and such a promotion is valid for all purposes.
The authority's denial of retiral benefits for the promotion post was deemed unconstitutional.
