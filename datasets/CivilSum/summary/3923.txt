Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Qualification - Post of Assistant Teacher in Primary School - The petitioners expressed their grievance that the marks obtained as per their qualifications were not added at the time of considering their appointments.
However, candidates with higher qualifications are eligible in all aspects.
It was held that the petitioners are entitled to relief under this petition, and a direction was issued to reassess the qualification in the light of this court's judgment.
If the petitioners are within the zone of consideration, they should be given appointments within four weeks.
