As per Section 6-N of the U.P.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, termination of service is allowed only if work has been carried out for not more than 240 days in the past twelve calendar months.
In this case, the provisions of Section 6N of the 1947 Act were not followed and the worker's termination is considered retrenchment.
Therefore, they are entitled to be reinstated with continuity and half of backwages until the date of reference and full thereafter.
