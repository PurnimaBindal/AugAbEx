The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(1) (c) and 18(3) deal with promotion.
In a case where several persons were promoted as Ferro Printers from the position of peon, two persons who were promoted were junior to a person whose case had been taken up by the Union.
The Labour Court did not have the right to set aside the promotion of these two people as they were not present during the proceedings.
However, the Labour Court could have included them as parties under S.18(3) and proceeded to make a decision, but it did not.
As a result, their promotions were voided due to their absence.
However, this part of the award cannot be validated, and the Labour Court ordered a declaration that the individuals were to be promoted from the same day that their juniors were.
The direction which annulled the promotion of two people without making them parties is revoked.
(Paras 4 and 7)
