Constitution of India, Article 311 - State Bank of Hyderabad (Officers) Service Regulations, 1979 - Enquiry Report - Charge sheet - Departmental enquiry - Reduction in rank - Petitioner, who worked as an officer in State Bank of Hyderabad, was subjected to disciplinary action that led to a reduction in rank; the Disciplinary Authority was different from the Enquiry Officer and punishment imposed on the petitioner was not one of dismissal or removal from service - The report of the inquiry officer was not given to petitioner prior to imposition of penalty; instead, the report was included in the order that imposed punishment on petitioner - It is held that it is not necessary for petitioner to prove that non-supply of the inquiry officer's report prejudiced him; Non-supply of Inquiry Officer's report itself nullifies the proceedings.
