According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 and Central Tibetan Schools Administration Staff Service Regulations, Regulation 39(a), persons holding posts of Assistants, Audit Assistants and Senior Stenographers in the Central Tibetan Schools are entitled to same pay scales as their counterparts in Govt. of India Services, which includes parity of pay.
Therefore, there is no justification for these employees to be denied the same pay scales and salary as other General Govt. Employees.
