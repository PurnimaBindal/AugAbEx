The finalised seniority of the appellant in the cadre of Sub-Inspector was determined on 21.1.86.
Rules issued on 29587 contained a saving clause stating that seniority determined in accordance with existing rules on the date of notification would not be re-opened.
The High Court gave directions on 8.7.1998 in a writ petition, which did not include the appellant, that the seniority list dated 21396 be quashed.
The High Court issued directions that the seniority of the appellant, as per the seniority list dated 21.1.86, could not be disturbed under the impugned order.
