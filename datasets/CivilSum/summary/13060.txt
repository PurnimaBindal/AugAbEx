Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation - Prohibition on casual appointments - Those Daily Wagers who were appointed despite the guidelines contained in J&K Govt. Order No. 26-F of 1994 and SRO 64 of 1994 are ineligible for being regularised in service - Working for a temporary status for a specific period does not provide the right to be regularised.
