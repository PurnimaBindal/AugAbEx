According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I Part I, Rules 51 and 55, house rent is a compensatory allowance that is paid only when an employee actually pays the house rent.
If both the husband and wife are government employees posted at the same station, the house rent allowance will only be payable to one of them or the head of the entire family if there are multiple family members employed in Govt. Service.
It is not admissible to all family members, but only to the employee who is actually paying it.
[Paras 6 to 8]

Under Constitution of India, Article 14, natural justice must be upheld when house rent allowance is withdrawn.
If husbands of the petitioners are already receiving the house rent allowance, petitioners are not entitled to it under any rules or instructions.
There is a specific bar for paying the allowance to another spouse.
If a writ is issued for compliance of principles of natural justice, no defense has been suggested and court will not issue futile writs.
[Para 10]
