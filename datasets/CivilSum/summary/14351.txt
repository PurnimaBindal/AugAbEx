Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Absorption - Promotion - Reversion to the post of Senior Assistant - At the time of promotion from Senior Assistant to Superintendent Grade-II, there were four posts of Supt.
Grade-II available in Directorate - Reversion to the post of Senior Assistant was not possible after promotion to one of these posts - The Court found that there was only one post of Supdt.
Grade-II in the Directorate and other posts had been created exclusively for the State Board of Technical Education - Therefore, the petitioner could not have been promoted against the solitary post of Supdt.
Grade-II.
