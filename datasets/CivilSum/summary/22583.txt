In accordance with the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311, Paragraph 521(4) & (6) of the Sastry Award and Desai Award discuss the issue of gross misconduct by a bank employee who included three unauthorised increments for himself, constituting not merely a mistake, but real misconduct.
Although it may still not be deemed a gross misconduct, the term is not to be taken in the abstract, as service conditions regulate it according to applicable rules.

In departmental proceedings, the punishment imposed should not typically be interfered with unless it is either impermissible or goes against the Court's conscience.
This principle follows from the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311.
