The High Court directed the appellant to fill vacant posts of Executive Engineer of the Civil Circle of the Electricity Department by putting the respondents in charge.
In accordance with this direction, the Government made a policy decision to reorganize the Department.
As a result of this policy decision, a D.P.C.
would be constituted to evaluate the relative merits of candidates in the Executive Civil Division category, followed by appointment in accordance with the Rules.
