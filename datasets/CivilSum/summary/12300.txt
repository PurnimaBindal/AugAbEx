Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 18(1) - Settlement - Validity - The petitioner-management alleged that Rs. 2500/- were paid to the respondent-workman as per the settlement between them.
However, the petitioner-management failed to present attesting witnesses of the settlement in court.
Despite an application made to examine the thumb expression of the workman, the learned labour court correctly rejected the application and passed an award in favor of the workman.
