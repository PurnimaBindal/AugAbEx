The Constitution of India, Articles 20(2) and 226 - Salary - The amount of salary payable to the petitioner who was absent without obtaining regularisation cannot be deducted from future salaries and censured by the respondent, unless the petitioner was notified of the order.
The order does not prohibit the petitioner from initiating a regular departmental inquiry against him.
The impugned order cannot be set aside on this basis.
[Para 12] B.
The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Disciplinary inquiry - The disciplinary authority disagrees with the findings of the inquiry officer and imposes a penalty of withholding salary increment.
The petitioner, who was absent and drawing salary, requested that the salary be deducted from future salary, and the order cannot be challenged on the grounds of failure of the disciplinary authority to give reasons for or against a decision.
