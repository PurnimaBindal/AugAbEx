In accordance with Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 and Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part I, Rule 3.26(d), the appointing authority holds the power to retire a Government employee at the age of 55 years after a notice of no less than three months, if it believes it is in public interest.
Adhoc employees are typically treated as regular Government employees in terms of their superannuation date.
There is no indication of how it was not in the public interest to keep the petitioner beyond the age of 55 years.
As a result, the petitioner is authorized to continue in service until he reaches the age of 58 years.
