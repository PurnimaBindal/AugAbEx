In Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226, disparity can only be proven when different pay scales are assigned to equal classes of employees.
If employees belong to different unequal categories, prescribing different pay scales at the time of pay scale revision is not considered discriminatory.
High Court's decision to strike down disparity between unequals was incorrect.
[Paras 20 to 25] B.
The power to specify a date from which the revision of pay scales or terminal benefits/pensionary benefits shall take place is permitted under Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, as long as the date is reasonable and does not lead to discrimination between similar individuals.
