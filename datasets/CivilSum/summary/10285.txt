Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Termination - Set aside - Consequential benefits - Petitioner's appeal is accepted by Additional District Judge who directed that punishing authority must revisit the case and pass a well-reasoned order after giving the Petitioner a personal hearing - However, the judgment lacks clarity regarding the period from the termination of the Petitioner's services until the date of judgment - The court concluded that once the termination order is set aside, the Petitioner will be deemed to be in service and hence entitled to all consequential benefits until a fresh order is passed - Appeal is disposed of accordingly.
