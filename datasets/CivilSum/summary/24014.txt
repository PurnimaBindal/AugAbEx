State Bank of India Officers Service Rules, Rules 49 and 50(1) - State Bank of India Act, 1955, Section 43(1) - Every officer of the Bank is required to be transferred to any office or branch of the bank or even to other organizations in India - It is considered as misbehavior if any officer disobeys the transfer orders - An officer cannot plead ignorance of transfer orders if he has been representing against them after being relieved on account of transfer - Non-joining of work by an officer is not legal and justified and hence the principle of `no work no pay' can be applied - The authority has the discretion to decide the matter as per the rules without any interference by the court under Article 226 of the Constitution.
