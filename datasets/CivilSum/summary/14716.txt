Punjab Punishment and Appeal Rules, Rule 8.8 - Natural justice - Reasonable opportunity - Departmental enquiry - The onus is put on the delinquent officer to disprove the charge rather than on the prosecution to prove the charge - Witnesses and documents relied upon must be supplied - Officer should be asked if they require assistance from a Government servant to defend themselves - If these rules are violated, the enquiry is vitiated and the order of dismissal must be set aside.

Punjab Punishment and Appeal Rules, Rule 8 - Suspension - If a suspended doctor does not receive their suspension allowance for some time, they may be forced to do private practice for survival - It cannot be assumed that the delinquent doctor is admitting to the charge of doing private practice.
[Para 12]
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Punishment and Appeal Rules, Rule 8 - Suspension - Delay of 11 years in conducting enquiry and taking action can be demoralizing for the Government servant and gives rise to feelings of prolonged disciplinary proceedings meant to humiliate the person, keeping them under suspension and holding a sword over their head.
