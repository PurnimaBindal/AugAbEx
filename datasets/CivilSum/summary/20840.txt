Sections 67, 82 to 84 of the Punjab Reorganization Act, 1966 - Liability apportionment between Boards after state re-organization - Section 67(3) defines "liable" and "liability," which covers employees, provident fund, pension, and claims arising from contracts and actionable wrongs.
Section 82 does not apply.

Sections 67(3), 82, and 83 of the Punjab Reorganization Act, 1966 - Chandigarh Electricity Department - Deputation of proportionate staff from Punjab and Haryana States - UT Chandigarh allowed members of the staff from both states to continue their postings on deputation from the Electricity Boards of Punjab and Haryana - Section 67(3), not Section 82, authorizes allocation of employees from erstwhile Board, and the Central Government cannot make decisions about such employees.
No deemed absorption under Section 83 can be claimed by employees, merely because when the successor states formed, they worked in the territorial limits of UT Chandigarh.
