The Labour Court found termination of the petitioner's service to be unjustified, and subsequently reinstated them with continuity of service and 50% back wages.
Denying regularisation on grounds of physical absence from work is not permissible, as per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 read with Article 226.
The court followed the precedent set by Deep Chand and Kavita Gandhi.
[Para ]
According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1940, the award for reinstatement with continuity of service and 50% back wages cannot be contested on the basis of the worker's physical absence from work.
The department cannot plead this as grounds for denying the award.
