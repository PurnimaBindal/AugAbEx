The petitioner, a member of the Armed Security Force, possessed a fake police identity card and was tried by a Summary Security Force Court, where he confessed his guilt and declined the support of any friend for his defense.
He was subsequently dismissed from service, but no prejudice was found to have been caused in the circumstances.
The court relied on AIR 1965 SC 247.

According to Article 33 of the Indian Constitution, the rights of members of the Armed Forces can be restricted or abrogated to maintain discipline and ensure proper discharge of duties of the said force.
The court relied on AIR 1982 SC 1413.
[Para 7]  C In the case of a member of the Border Security Force being tried for possessing a fake identity card, the period allowed to prepare the defense was reduced from four days to 24 hours in the event of a Summary Security Force Court trial.
However, no prejudice was caused to the petitioner.
The court referred to Border Security Force Act, 1968, Section 2(1)(a) and Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rules 157 and 63(6).
