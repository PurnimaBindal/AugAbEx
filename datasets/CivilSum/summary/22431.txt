University of Udaipur (Sukhadia University) Payment of Gratuity to Employees Rules, 1979, Rule 11 notes 1 and 2 - Rajasthan Services Rules, Rule 7(24) - Gratuity - Pension - Last pay drawn - As a separate legal entity with perpetual succession, the University of Udaipur is not bound by rules framed by the Government unless specifically adopted by it in accordance with the provisions of the Act by which the university was constituted - The university adopted the Sukhadia University statutes in 1980 and therefore the Payment of Gratuity Rules 1979 are applicable to university employees, with Rule 7(24) of Rajasthan Services Rules being used to compute last pay drawn, including pay, dearness allowance and ad hoc dearness allowance as amended from time to time - The university also adopted the 1987 State Services Revised Pay Rules, and when calculating total emoluments for the purpose of gratuity, both basic pay and dearness allowance must be taken into consideration.
