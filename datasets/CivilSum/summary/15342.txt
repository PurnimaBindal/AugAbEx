Article 226 of the Indian Constitution discusses misconduct and departmental inquiries.
However, failing to reach the target fixed in the Green Card scheme without evidence of work slowdown cannot be seen as a basis to conduct a departmental inquiry against the petitioner unless the scheme is incorporated in the Rules or Standing Orders and unless non-achievement of the target is considered misconduct in the Rules or Standing Orders.
[Paras 9 and 10]

Article 226 of the Indian Constitution also talks about transfers.
In a general order of transfer that only cites administrative exigency to deal with individuals facing departmental inquiries and suspension by competent authorities, it cannot be concluded that the orders were passed maliciously or in violation of statutory rules or to punish the petitioners.
The reasons given justify the exigency under which the transfer orders were passed.
[Para 15]
