Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 11(3) and 18(3) - Reference/Parties to the reference/Impleading new parties/Jurisdiction - The Labour Court has the authority to add new parties to a reference made to it by the Government in order to make a just and proper decision - Adding the Managing Director of a Corporation to an industrial dispute against the Corporation does not significantly increase the scope of the reference - Any objections to this are hyper-technical and should be dismissed.
