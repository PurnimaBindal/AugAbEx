Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservation - Scheduled Castes members may benefit from the special provisions made by the State if it so chooses under Article 16(4) but it is not mandatory - If the State government or appropriate authority decides not to make a reservation, the courts cannot compel it to do so - Reservation made by one State or authority will not bind another State or authority.
[Para 13]

Constitution of India, Articles 12, 16(4) and 226 - State - Courts cannot direct the University to make reservations in favor of Scheduled Castes members for appointments to teaching posts since the University falls under the definition of a 'State' under Article 12.
[Para 13]

Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 335 - Reservation - The appropriate authority can make a reservation as long as it is consistent with the maintenance of efficiency and high performance in education - If the authority feels that reservation would not be conducive to building excellence in education, it cannot be accused of violating the Constitution - The authority's action would be in accordance with the Constitutional Scheme.
[Para 14]

Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 335 - Reservation - Punjab Agricultural University is not obligated to make reservations for Scheduled Castes members for teaching positions, as the University has chosen to deny the benefit of reservation to backward classes of citizens - This action is not illegal or in violation of the Constitution - The mere existence of reservations for non-teaching posts does not necessitate reservations for teaching posts.
[Paras 14, 15 and 16]

Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 335 - Reservation - There is a valid classification between teaching and non-teaching posts - Reservation for one category of posts cannot lead to automatic reservation in the other.
[Para 16]

Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 335 - Reservation - The University was not bound to make reservations for the posts of Assistant Professor (Farm Management)/Posts of Assistant Professor (Agronomy) and District Extension Specialist (Agronomy), and both petitioners are eligible and entitled to be considered for the posts.
[Para 18]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - The petitioner is eligible for appointment to the posts of Assistant Professor (Agronomy) and District Extension Specialist (Agronomy), as per the formula laid down by the University.
