Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Apprentices Act, 1961 - Seniority - Fixation of - The petitioner in a case cannot count the period of apprenticeship training as part of their seniority.
It is considered to be prior to their entry into a separately sanctioned unit of a service or cadre.
This ruling was based on the interpretation of Paras 18, 20 and 24.
