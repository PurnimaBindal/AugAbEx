The petitioner who had opted to shift was given admission to the BDS course, however, their name was placed on the waiting list.
The petitioner contended that seats in the third counselling were not filled from the waiting list, but it was held that the third counselling was only for the MBBS course.
The court ruled that in the absence of any public notice to fill seats in the Dental course from the waiting list, the petitioner cannot claim any right to the seat even if it became available.
Furthermore, since each Dental college is a separate entity with its own rules, the case cannot be interpreted as shifting and not a fresh admission that could not have been made beyond the date fixed by the Dental Council of India.
