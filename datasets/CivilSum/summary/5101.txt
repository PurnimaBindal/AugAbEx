According to Section 20(3) of the Army Act, 1950 and Rules 13(3) and 17 of the Army Rules, 1954, if an official conceals their previous service and is discharged under Rule 13(3) for being unlikely to become an efficient soldier, they may be dismissed without an enquiry.
The respondent was given the opportunity to explain their situation, but according to Rule 17, no formal enquiry was necessary as long as the official was informed of the cause of action and given a reasonable amount of time to respond.
Therefore, the dismissal order was legal and justifiable.
