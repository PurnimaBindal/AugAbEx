The Punjab Government's policy for regularisation of daily wage workers is based on a completion of 10 years of service by August 31, 1992.
The policy aims to regularise the service of those who have completed 10 years of service.
The cut-off date was set to achieve this objective, and since the policy was a one-time policy, it cannot be used to deprive workers who have completed 10 years of service after August 31, 1992.
An employee who died after completing 10 years of service and whose case had been recommended for regularisation will be deemed to have been regularised.
[Para 5]  B A daily wager who has completed 10 years of service is entitled to regularisation under the state policy.
If the employee dies after completion of 10 years of service, his services will also be deemed to have been regularised.
The dependents of the employee cannot be denied the benefits of his status as a regular Government servant.
Application for compassionate appointment by the employee's widow is maintainable and must be considered on merit.
