Constitution of India, Article 14 - Admission/Weightage of Marks/Additional qualifications - The University is responsible for determining whether to prioritize additional qualifications for admission to a particular course - However, academic experts must decide whether weightage should be given for specific qualifications for admission to a specific course - This requirement is reasonable and not arbitrary.
[Para 5] B Constitution of India, Article 14 - Nagpur University Act, 1974, Section 24(xi)/Admission/Criteria of admission/Weightage of Marks/Additional qualifications/Jurisdiction of admission committee - The admission committee must not adopt criteria beyond the Rules or that provided in the prospectus - The University authorities are obligated to adhere to what is indicated in the Rules/Prospectus - Any attempt to bend the rules and apply a different formula would confer arbitrary power upon the authorities and could be challenged as mala fide - Such actions would not only be improper but also unreasonable and run the risk of conferring unbridled power on the authorities.
