Bihar Service Code, Rule 96 - Suspension - Subsistent allowance - Certificate of non-employment during suspension - Denial of subsistence allowance only if affidavit or attendance is not provided by the employee during suspension - Compelling the suspended employee to give regular attendance is not required - Only requirement is that the authority ensures the delinquent does not leave the head place quarters - The successor of a deceased employee can claim the amount due by providing an affidavit of non-employment of the deceased.
