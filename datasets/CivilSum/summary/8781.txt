Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Delay/laches in promotion - Discrimination issues - Seniority list created - Promotion provisionally given to employees ranked lower than petitioner - Petitioner's claim dismissed due to delay in subjudiced seniority list case - Petitioner was denied promotion due to different criteria compared to others - Discriminatory and goes against Articles 14 and 16 - Petitioner only asked for seniority in seniority list and date of allotment to be considered, without any need to dispute facts - Court directed respondents to consider petitioner for promotion based on his seniority in seniority list and date of allotment without considering delay as reason for declining claim.
