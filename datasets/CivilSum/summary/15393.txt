Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2, Item 3 - Disciplinary enquiry - Superannuation - Departmental proceedings may continue after superannuation - Charge sheet must be drawn before superannuation - If charge sheet is not served personally, it may be sent by registered post and affixed on the premises - In the case of the petitioner, he was not present at the time charge sheet was served due to availing leave travel concession - There is no illegality in continuing the proceedings after retirement as the charge sheet was drawn up before superannuation.
