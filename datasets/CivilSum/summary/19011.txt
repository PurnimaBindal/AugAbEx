Constitution of India, Article 226 - A constable in Punjab Police was absent from duty, leading to a Departmental Enquiry.
The Appellate Authority didn't provide the enquiry report to the petitioner before dismissing him.
It was held that the Punishing Authority had to supply the report of the enquiry officer and give the petitioner a reasonable opportunity to be heard, but they failed to do so.
The petitioner was therefore unable to defend his case effectively, and the impugned order was quashed.
[Paras 3 and 4]
