The advertisement issued by a bank inviting applications for various posts in the rural banks with a choice of any one bank violated Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution as it contained a restrictive clause that was not with any objective to be achieved.
The restriction cannot be imposed in the opportunity of employment even on the basis of residence in a specific area, and in this case, not even this object is sought to be achieved.
[Paras 11 and 16]
