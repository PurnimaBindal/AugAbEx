States Reorganisation Act, 1956, Sections 82 and 115 - Retirement - Conditions of service - Previously, the post of driver was categorised as an inferior (Class IV) post in the State of Pepsu.
When these employees were allocated to successor States, they were still considered to be holding an inferior post, meaning they cannot retire at age 58 under the Punjab Civil Service Rules as Class III or II employees.
Instead, they must retire at age 60.
Section 82(7) of the Act protects their service conditions, and any changes to their disadvantage must have prior approval from the Central Government according to Section 115.
