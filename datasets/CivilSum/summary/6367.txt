Constitution of India, Articles 14, 311 and 226 - Absence from duty - Break in service - Withholding of retiral benefits - An employee who goes on duly sanctioned leave to take a job abroad and keeps his lien intact is entitled to retain his retiral benefits, provided his applications for extension of leave were ignored and he wasn't asked to immediately return.
If on his return, he was allowed to join and placed on different positions in different Govt.
Departments until his exit from Govt. service, without treating the alleged period of absence from duty as break in service or fresh appointment, he should be treated similarly to other employees in a comparable situation who received benefits.
Rejecting his application for treating his absence period as extra-ordinary leave would amount to hostile discrimination.
