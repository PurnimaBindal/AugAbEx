The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 state that promotions or reversions must consider overall records rather than just past records, including adverse entries.
After being promoted, an employee's service record prior to that stage cannot be used against them.
Additionally, any adverse remarks recorded before crossing the efficiency bar also lose their significance and are nonexistent.
Thus, considering an employee's entire service record for various promotions and allowing proficiency step-up is completely arbitrary.
