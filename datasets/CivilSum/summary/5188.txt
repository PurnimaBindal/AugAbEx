The challenge to the constitutional validity of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 Proviso, under Constitution of India, Articles 14, 19 and 39A, must specifically plead and demonstrate transgression of constitutional principles or infringement of fundamental right; otherwise, the court will not declare the enactment unsustainable.
[Para 4] B.
The right to file an appeal is not a vested, fundamental, or constitutional right; instead, it is only a statutory right bestowed under the statute under which the order is passed.
[Para 5] C. The validity of the pre-condition to deposit compensation as a condition of appeal under Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30, is upheld under Constitution of India, Articles 14, 19 and 39A, because it is incorporated to safeguard the interests of helpless dependants of injured/deceased workman and does not violate any fundamental or constitutional right.
