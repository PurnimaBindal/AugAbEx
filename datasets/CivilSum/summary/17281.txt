According to Army Pension Regulations, Para 173, Rule 7(b), those suffering from mental disorders due to anxiety during their service are entitled to disability pension.
Even if there was no entry in the medical check-up before the service, Rule 7(b) presumes that the disease was attributable to the disability incurred during service.
In a particular case where the petitioner had developed Schizophrenia due to Anxiety when posted in a forward area, and was previously diagnosed with Neurosis, the court held that the petitioner is entitled to receive disability pension.
