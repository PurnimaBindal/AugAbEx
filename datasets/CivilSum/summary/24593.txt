Constitution of India, Articles 12 & 32 - Delhi Education Act, 1973, Section 8(1) - Discrimination - The petitioner claimed that the age of superannuation for teachers under Section 208 of the Delhi Education Code was 60 years which was not denied - It is necessary for all institutions to have a uniform rule for superannuation of employees - Employers cannot arbitrarily set the age for superannuation - As there was no regulation, bye-laws or policy-decision regarding age of superannuation by the respondent management, the petitioner was entitled to be retired at the age of 60 years - This was the age of superannuation which the management followed before the Act and Rules came into force.
