Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 46 - Reservation/Admission/Education - If a student is eligible for and applied for two reserved categories but is unable to secure admission on merit in one category, this does not imply that he or she cannot be considered in the other reserved category - Denying consideration in both categories would be a violation of Articles 16(4) and 46 of the Constitution of India, citing Indira Sawhney's case (Mandal Commission case) - AIR 1993 SC 447.
