According to Recruitment Procedure of 1993, Clause 4(g), there is no upper age limit to select a candidate for appointment as Assistant Teacher.
Although the petitioner sought cancellation of the name of a candidate who secured first position in the panel approved by the District Inspector of Schools, on the ground that the candidate was above 35 years of age prior to reference of his name by the Employment Exchange, it was held that the age for an experienced and highly qualified candidate is relaxable up to 40 years.
Hence, the candidate is entitled to be appointed as an Assistant Teacher as his age has been relaxed by the appropriate authority.
[Paras 17, 18 and 19] B.
The selection committee had no jurisdiction to allot any marks relating to qualification of MA which was not mentioned in prior permission order of District Inspector of Schools and also not mentioned by Employment Exchange officer while sponsoring the name, as per Clause 6(a) of the Recruitment Procedure of 1993.
Although the Employment Officer sent a letter to consider the qualification of MA of the petitioner long after the selection process was completed, the petitioner's name was sent by the Employment Officer mentioning the qualification of BA only.
