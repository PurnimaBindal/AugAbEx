Constitution of India, Article 226 - Date of birth - High Court's interference with the date of birth recorded in employer's service record, in a proceeding under Article 226 of the Constitution - In cases where the date of birth controversy of an employee has been raised long after joining the service and has already been determined by following relevant Service Rules of General Instructions - Unless there is an arithmetical or typographical error patent on the face of the record, the High Court in its extraordinary jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution should not interfere with the employer's decision.
