Constitution of India, Article 14 - Departmental proceedings - Natural justice - A fair and proper enquiry requires that the delinquent be informed of the material being used against him/her and given the opportunity to defend orally and with documentation.
Failure to do so violates the principle of audi alteram partem.
[Para 9] B Constitution of India, Article 14 - Departmental proceedings - Natural justice - Prosecution evidence - All documents relied upon by the prosecution must be disclosed to and produced for the delinquent.
Failure to do so renders proceedings invalid.
[Para 13] C. Constitution of India, Article 14 - Departmental proceedings/Defence witnesses/Enquiry Officer - When the delinquent official requests to summon defence witnesses and the representative of management refuses, the enquiry officer must act independently and request objections from management.
No reasons given for not summoning defence witnesses violates a fair and proper enquiry.
[Paras 14 and 15] D. Constitution of India, Article 14 - Departmental proceedings - Enquiry Report - The enquiry report must contain a discussion of the material on the file and reasons supporting the findings contained within it.
A report based on the opinion of the Enquiry Officer is arbitrary and jeopardizes an employee's valuable rights.
