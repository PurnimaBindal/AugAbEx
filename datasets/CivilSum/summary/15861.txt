Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 23 - Prohibition of `Begar' and equal pay for equal work - If an employee is not paid according to their work as per rules, it amounts to `Begar' and violates Article 23 - The petitioner was appointed as a driver but asked to work as a Legal Assistant without being paid as one - Though not forced to perform forced labor, the petitioner was made to work as a Legal Assistant without appropriate pay, constituting `Begar' - To avoid violation of Article 23, the petitioner must be paid as a Legal Assistant.
