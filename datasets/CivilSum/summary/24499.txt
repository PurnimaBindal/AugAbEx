Haryana Development and Regulation of Urban Areas Act, 1975 - Precedent - High Court directed Authority to take action according to an order from an earlier writ case - Order of High Court set aside because it did not explain how earlier case was relevant - Decisions should not be relied upon without considering how it fits the factual situation.
[Paras 6, 7, and 9] B. Precedents - Interpretation of statutes: Judges interpret statutes, not judgments - Words of statutes are not to be interpreted as statutes - Disposal of cases by blindly relying on a decision is improper, as circumstantial flexibility can make a world of difference between conclusions in two cases.
