The Rajasthan Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules 1958, Rule 17 explains the Promotion/Selection Scale and how it can be denied on the grounds of minor penalties.
The Constitution of India Articles 20 and 226 was also referenced in this case.
Despite the petitioner being considered for promotion but not found fit for it, there was no discrimination since any employee found guilty of misconduct can be treated differently.
As a result, the order was deemed constitutional.
