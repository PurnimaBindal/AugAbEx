According to Fundamental Rule 56(j), compulsory retirement is not a punitive measure.
The competent authority can utilize Rule 56(j) if they believe based on evidence that an employee's efficiency is deteriorating during their last term of service.
If the decision is made in good faith for the public interest, it cannot be deemed arbitrary or unreasonable.
The decision can only be challenged if there is evidence of malintent.
