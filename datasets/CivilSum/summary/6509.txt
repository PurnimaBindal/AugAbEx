Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Bribe - Corruption charges - Demand of money - Recovery of money - To prove the charge of corruption, it is necessary to show evidence of a demand of money - There is no proof on record of any such demand or recovery - Declarations of witnesses as hostile are insufficient - The presence of a demand is a key component - Without it, a finding of corruption cannot be supported.
[Paras 13 and 14]
