In this case, the petitioner missed four semesters due to illness and was denied readmission due to poor performance.
The Institute should exercise its discretion and power in favor of the petitioner for readmission.
However, if the rules of the Institute do not help, it will be an erroneous approach on the part of the High Court to impose its own power.
