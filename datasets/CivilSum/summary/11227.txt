Constitution of India, Article 226 - Non-speaking order of punishment - Sustainability - Respondent passed fresh order on appeal of petitioner on direction of High Court - Though order of removal was replaced by order of stoppage of four increments with cumulative effect, but no reasons are given - Held, since order is devoid of any reason and is non-speaking and violative of principles of natural justice, it is set aside - Matter remitted for passing of fresh order.
