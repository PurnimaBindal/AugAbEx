Delhi Agricultural Market Committee (Service) Regulations, 1978, Regulation 14(6) - Termination of Service - The petitioner was appointed on an ad hoc/temporary basis and was terminated due to concealment of his conviction under Sections 323 and 148 IPC.
Despite working for 3 years and 6 months, his right to be regularised had not yet crystallized at the time the impugned order was passed.
As a result, the relief of reinstatement cannot be granted to the petitioner even though he was convicted of an offence not involving moral turpitude.
[Paras 19, 20 and 21]
