Constitution of India, Articles 12, 14 and 226 - ICFAI University Act 2003, Andhra Pradesh (Telangana Area) Public Societies Registration Act, 1350f - Education matter - Admission - Use of `Unfair Means' in MBA 1st semester examination - The relevant paper was cancelled - Conditional Appearance in 2nd semester examination allowed - Although the Respondents successfully cleared the 2nd semester examination, they failed to fulfill the condition of passing the 1st semester examination paper - Due to this, they were denied registration to the 3rd semester - The decision was held not to be unfair, unreasonable, or arbitrary - Furthermore, the Respondents were not considered a state within the meaning of Art. 12, thus they were not answerable under the Writ Jurisdiction.
