Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Termination - Misconduct - Notice justice - If an employee is terminated on the basis of specific allegations of wilful absence from duty and failure to perform assigned duties, but no enquiry is held, the termination order may be considered nullity.
The punishing authority must issue a show cause notice to give the delinquent employee an opportunity to explain their absence.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Termination - Innocuous order of termination - Misconduct - If a termination is based on allegations of misconduct, a court can investigate to determine if there is a true cause for termination, or if the allegations only provided a motive.
If it is determined that the termination was based on misconduct, the court can consider it to be punitive.
[Para 7]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Temporary appointment - Ad-hoc appointment - Termination - Natural Justice - Employers are obligated to follow the basics of natural justice before terminating an employee's services as a punishment, regardless of whether the employee holds a right to the post.
