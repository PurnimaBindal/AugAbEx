Maharishi Dayanand University Service and Conduct Rules for Non-Teaching Employees, Rule 15 - Constitution of India, Article 16 - Ad hoc appointment - Adverse reports - Resolution - The petitioner in this case was not confirmed in her post and terminated.
However, the petitioner had completed her one-year probation period and no order was passed to extend it before the expiry.
Thus, it is deemed that the petitioner had successfully completed her probation, and confirmation must follow automatically.
Any termination after probation is illegal, and the resolution was quashed with reference to 1989(3) SLR 587.
[Paras 4 and 9]

Maharishi Dayanand University, Service and Conduct Rules for Non-Teaching Employees, Rule 15 - Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Misconduct - Termination - Natural Justice - If services of the petitioner be terminated on any ground of misconduct, then the relevant rules and the rules of natural justice must be complied with.
In this case, the petitioner was terminated on the basis of some adverse remarks recorded by the Dean of Students Welfare, which were never conveyed to the petitioner.
As the petitioner had completed more than one year of service before the resolution for not confirming her was passed, any action on the basis of adverse remarks should have been conveyed to the petitioner.
Thus, the resolution was quashed, and the petitioner was reinstated with all consequential benefits.
[Paras 7, 8 and 9]
