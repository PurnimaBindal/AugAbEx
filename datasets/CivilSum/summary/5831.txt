The Haryana Technical Education Department Group-B Service Rules, 1986, Rule 11 - Seniority - Ad hoc service - The meaning of "continuous service on any post in service" is service on a post after appointment according to the Rules, according to the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 226.
The Rules provide for appointment by selection through Public Service Commission, and ad hoc service without appointment as per the Rules before regularisation cannot be counted for fixing seniority.
