Payment of Wages Act 1936, Sections 15 and 17 - Constitution of India, Article 126 - Alternative Remedy - Workmen's application under Section 15 allowed by primary authority, followed by appeal from Management which was returned for compliance with Act - Revision filed against order - Petitioner filed writ challenging primary authority's order to ignore available alternative remedy when provision was not being followed - Court rules that compliance with statutory remedies is mandatory for the benefit of workmen, thus the writ is not maintainable.
