Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - All India Council for Technical Education Act, 1987, Section 10(e) - All India Council for Technical Education (Norms and Guidelines for Fees and Guidelines for Admission in Professional Colleges) Regulations, 1994, Regulation 9 - Reservation of seats - The wording of Section 10(e) and Regulation 9 implies that the reservation of seats is optional rather than mandatory - The A.I.C.T.E.
has not mandated colleges to reserve seats - The State Government cannot compel private colleges to reserve seats for specified classes as per the instructions circular dated 19399.
