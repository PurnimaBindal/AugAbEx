According to the West Bengal Service (Death-cum-Retirement Benefit) Rules, 1971, Rule 22, the state government abolished the position of Tahsildars and replaced them with Bhumi Sahayaks, absorbing the former into the new position.
The petitioners complied with Rule 22, surrendering their share of government contribution to Provident Fund, and as a result, argue that they cannot be denied pension and other benefits by excluding their service period as Tahsildars, since the service of both positions counts towards qualifying service.
This is in accordance with Articles 14, 16, and 226 of the Constitution of India.
