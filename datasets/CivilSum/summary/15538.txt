According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 2.2(b) concerning retiral benefits, the proper course of action for departmental proceedings after retirement is to follow Rule 2.2(b) of Pb.
CSR-II.
If there is a pending enquiry, the official is entitled to 100% provisional pension, commuted pension, and the amount of unavailed earned leave.
The only amount that can be withheld until the completion of the enquiry is the gratuity.
However, if the charge is related to embezzlement and the amount alleged to be embezzled is higher than the gratuity, the retiree can be asked to provide an undertaking with adequate surety for that amount.
