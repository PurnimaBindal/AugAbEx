Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Pension/Military service - Qualifying service - The petitioner, who was discharged from the Army after meeting the qualifying service requirement for a pension from the watch and ward wing, is entitled to the same Pension Regulations as those in the Indian Army infantry - The pay and allowances of the watch and ward wing staff are equivalent to those of similar ranks in the infantry - Authorities are ordered to pay the petitioner's pension with arrears, beginning from the date of his retirement.
