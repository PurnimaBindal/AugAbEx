Civil Procedure Code, Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 - Specific Relief Act, 1963, Sections 36 and 37 - Preventing Trade Unions from obstructing public officers and officials of management from entering the establishment and not holding demonstrations within 300 meters of the office premises - Granting an injunction in such a scenario is not a jurisdictional issue and does not completely bar Trade Union activities, as long as they can carry out their activities beyond the 300 meter radius from the office.
AIR 1969 SC 966 has been followed in this regard.
