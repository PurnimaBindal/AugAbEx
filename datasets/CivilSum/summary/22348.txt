Constitution of India, Articles 14, 19 - National Council for Teachers Education Act, 1993, Sections 14, 15, 32 - National Council for Teachers Education (Form of application for recognition, the time limit of submission of application, determination of norms and standards for recognition of teacher education programmes and permission to start new course or training) Regulations, 2002, Regulations 3, 5, 6 - Application for recognition - In order to start new course or training, certain regulations are imposed that require necessary documents and data to be submitted under a specific time frame - Requests for recognition can be denied if the application is submitted after the cut-off date - The Supreme Court held that it was obligatory on the party submitting the request to comply with these regulations in a timely manner in order to ensure the application receives proper attention from Regional Committees - Furthermore, refusal to grant NOC does not invalidate regulations, hence imposition of conditions on recognition cannot be considered unreasonable or arbitrary.

Constitution of India, Article 14 - National Council for Teachers Education Act, 1993, Sections 14, 15, 32 - National Council for Teachers Education (Form of application for recognition, the time limit of submission of application, determination of norms and standards for recognition of teacher education programmes and permission to start new course or training) Regulations, 2002, Regulations 3, 5, 6 - Application for recognition - In cases where recognition was granted without a similar condition, the court held that the situation in those cases was different - Moreover, the imposition of conditions on recognition was deemed legal and valid, and therefore any institution that does not comply cannot invoke Article 14 of the Constitution.
