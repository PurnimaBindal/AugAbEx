Madhya Pradesh State Industries (Gazetted) Service Recruitment Rules, 1985, Rule 6(2) - Recruitment- Preserving the proportion in the total cadre strength - The language of the Rule is lucid and transparent - The Rule does not deal with filling vacancies as and when they arise but with preserving the proportion between direct recruits and promotees at a 50:50 ratio during direct recruitment and promotions to the duty post cadre or strength.
