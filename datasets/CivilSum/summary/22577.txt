As per Section 16G(7) of the Uttar Pradesh Intermediate Education Act, 1921, the order of termination must be approved.
In this case, the order of termination was passed by the Managing Committee on 71177 and was approved by the competent authority on 24.4.80.
As a result, the appellant should be considered as continuing in service until 24.4.80 and should receive all pecuniary benefits available to them until that date.
