Under the Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Section 28(1), Schedule II, Item Nos. 1, 1(a), 1(b), 4, 4(a), Schedule IV, Items 3, 5, 6, 7, 9 and 10, a complaint of unfair labor practices is only maintainable if the provisions of the Act are enforced by workmen who have been admitted as such.
In the case of a dispute over whether employees are working for a company, an appropriate forum must first resolve the dispute.
Only after the status of workman is established can a complaint be made under the Act.
