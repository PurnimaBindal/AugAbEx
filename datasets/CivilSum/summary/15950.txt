Punjab Roadways (Workshop) State Service Class III Rules, 1977, Rule 7 Appendix 'A' and Appendix 'C' Column 2 - Appointing Authority - Mechanics - Seniority - The General Manager of each Depot has the authority to fill the post of Mechanic based on the claims of eligible persons within their Depot, rather than at the State level - Claims of eligible persons cannot be considered on the basis of seniority at the State level, if they are already being considered at the Depot level - Redesignation of posts for Revised Pay Scale Rules does not affect promotions under Service Rules - The petitioner's claim for promotion from Fitter to Mechanic to be considered within 3 months as per court order.
