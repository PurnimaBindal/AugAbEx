According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, and 311, any rule regarding promotion or restructuring of posts cannot unfairly affect employees who were already in service prior to the implementation of the rule.
In this case, the vacancies existed prior to the restructuring and the petitioners were promoted based on the already existing pattern.
Therefore, their reversion was illegal.
All existing vacancies on the date of restructuring must be filled according to the method in place before the restructuring.
The Tribunal's order is set aside and the petitioners should be considered validly promoted, restored to their previous positions, and given consequential benefits.
