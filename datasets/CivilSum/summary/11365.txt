Rajasthan Cooperative Societies (Compulsory Retirement) Rules, 1990, Rule 20(a) - Compulsory retirement - Committee recommendation - Under the 1990 Rules, no committee can be created to evaluate an employee's service record for the purpose of compulsory retirement decisions.
Any decision made on the basis of a committee's recommendation would be in violation of the rules.
Order of compulsory retirement quashed.
[Para 14] B Rajasthan Cooperative Societies (Compulsory Retirement) Rules, Rule 20(a) - Compulsory retirement - Competent authority - Only the Chief Executive Officer of the Bank is authorized to issue a compulsory retirement order.
Any such order passed by the Bank's Secretary would be without jurisdiction and not sustainable in law.
[Para 14] C Rajasthan Cooperative Societies (Compulsory Retirement) Rules, Rule 20(a) - Compulsory retirement - Registrar's Approval - The Rules of 1990 were said to have been authorized by the Bank on the basis of modal rules released by the Registrar under his power under Rule 41(1) of the 1966 Rules.
The decision of the Registrar obligated any decision regarding compulsory retirement made by the Board of Directors to be approved by the Registrar.
It also necessitated that the Registrar must approve an employee's compulsory retirement decision.
It was held that Rule 20(a) and the compulsory retirement order were in violation of the Rules and could not be upheld in the eyes of the law.
