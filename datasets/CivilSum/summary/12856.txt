According to Article 16 of the Indian Constitution and Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 122, confirmation and intermediate training course are determined by seniority.
A person cannot be confirmed earlier than their seniors unless there is a valid reason.
Even though the petitioner was called back from training due to seniority, it would be a waste of public resources to make him retake the course.
However, he will not be entitled to seniority or any other benefits based on his out of turn training.
