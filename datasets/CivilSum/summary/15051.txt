Provident Funds Act, 1925, Section 6A - Companies Act, 1956, Section 34 - Recovery of arrears of Provident Fund - According to Para 4, liability of one company cannot be imposed on another company, even if some directors are the same - This is because companies are separate legal entities under the Companies Act, and the Provident Fund Act does not allow for the liability of one company to be imposed on another company by lifting the corporate veil.
