According to Articles 311 and 226/227 of the Constitution of India, administrative order which results in civil consequences must follow the rules of natural justice.
(S L Kapoor v. Jagmohan, AIR 1981 SC 136 followed).
[Paras 10 and 15]

The Constitution of India's Articles 311 and 226/227 address reversion and natural justice.
In this case, the petitioner was reverted from the post of Enforcement Officer to the Accounts Department without being heard.
Therefore, the order of reversion cannot be sustained.
[Para 16]

Article 16, 226/227 and 311 of the Constitution of India, and Regulation 25 of the Punjab Scheduled Castes Land Development and Finance Corporation (Staff) Regulations, 1971, deal with inter se seniority and reversion.
Alteration of seniority and reversion has civil consequences and hence, must follow the rules of natural justice.
The concept of opportunity, being a fundamental requirement, has been extended to every action with adverse civil or penal consequences.
Reversion or alteration of seniority from a given rank without giving an opportunity, is violative of basic rules of natural justice.
