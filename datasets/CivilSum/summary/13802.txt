Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I, Part I, Rules 312 and 15 - Lien on post - Temporary appointment - Probation - Resignation during probation from a temporary appointment - There can be no lien on the post before confirmation and regular appointment.
If a person resigns during probation from a temporary appointment to join another department, they cannot claim lien and cannot return to the same position after termination of their services from the other department.
[Paras 3, 4 and 5]
