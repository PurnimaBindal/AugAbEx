The Army Pension Regulations of 1961, specifically Regulations 173 and Rule 7(c) & (b) of Appendix II, govern disability pensions and the eligibility of certain conditions.
In one case, a petitioner with "essential hypertension" was placed in a low medical category as a case of Schizophrenia Psychosis, and an Invalidating Medical Board concluded that the disability was not related to military service and did not recommend the grant of a disability pension.
The percentage of disablement was assessed at 40% for only two years.
As there was no evidence presented that the disease was linked to military service, the rejection of the claim for disability pension cannot be considered arbitrary or illegal.
