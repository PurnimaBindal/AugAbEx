Constitution of India - Article 226 - Regularisation of service - The petitioner approached this Court for the regularisation of his service, and the writ was disposed of, with directions to the respondents to consider the case.
However, the case was again rejected, citing non-completion of 240 days of work in each year.
The presence or absence of supporting documents showing the petitioner's work status is unknown.
As a result, disputed facts cannot be taken into account while using writ jurisdiction.
Thus, the respondents have been instructed to give the petitioner's case consideration.
[Paras 5 and 7]
