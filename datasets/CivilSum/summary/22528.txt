The State Bank of India Employees Pension Fund Rules, Rule 22 (as amended w.e.f.
2091986) - Pension - Eligibility - For one to be eligible and entitled to pension from the fund under the Rules, two conditions must be met: the individual must be a member of the fund on 22.9.1986 and must retire after completing 20 years of service on or after that date.
An appellant who resigned from service in 1984, withdrew his contribution with interest under the Rule prevalent on that date and retired only after rendering 20 years of service can no longer be considered an eligible member entitled to claim pension based on the 1986 amendment.
The appellant was not a beneficiary of the fund at the time of his retirement.
The ratio of DS Nakara's case does not apply.
1997(1) SCC 208 and 1997(9) SCC 606 followed.
[Paras 11, 12, 13, 15, and 21]
Pension/eligibility for pension - Amendment in pension Scheme or Rules - If a person is not eligible for pension at the time of his retirement, he will not be entitled to any enlarged benefit under any subsequent amendment.
However, if a person eligible for pension at the time of his retirement survives till the amendment, he will be entitled to get enhanced pension based on the new formula brought in.
The ratio of DS Nakara's case does not apply.
1997(1) SCC 208 followed.
