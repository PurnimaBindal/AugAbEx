Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Equal pay for equal work - Claim for difference in wages by an Octroi Peon who claimed to have worked as an Octroi Clerk - The fact of working as an Octroi Clerk deemed disputed - Whether the worker was entitled to receive a Clerk's salary was a serious disputed question of fact - The issue of whether the worker was required to perform the Clerk's duties was also in dispute - Matters not incidental or ancillary to the determination of the money claim - Not within the scope of Section 33-C(2) of the Act.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Limitation - Delay - Workman not entitled to make unreasonable and undue delay claims without plausible explanation under Section 33-C(2) of ID Act - Late claims can be rejected on delay and laches even if no limitation specified for the claim.
