The award given under Section 33-A cannot be considered as unfair as the employer had been given sufficient opportunity and had not appeared before the court.
[Para 7]
Sections 33 and 33-A provide protection to workmen from arbitrary actions by the employer during the pendency of proceedings and ensure that industrial peace is maintained.
Any actions altering the conditions of the workmen, pending proceedings, must be taken with the permission of the court/tribunal.
The complaint under Section 33-A will be adjudicated like a reference under Section 10(1).
[Paras 9 to 11 & 13]
The term "workman concerned in such dispute" under Section 33 is not limited to workmen directly involved in the dispute but includes all workmen who would be bound by the award passed under Section 10(1)(d).
[Para 13]
Termination of services of an active workman who had joined in pursuing the demands mentioned in Section 10(1)(d) pending before the Labour Court/Tribunal certainly amounts to "in connection with the pending dispute."
[Paras 14, 15]
The natural justice principle demands that termination alleging a workman of disobeying orders or not taking an interest in work cannot be sustained, unless a regular inquiry has been conducted.
