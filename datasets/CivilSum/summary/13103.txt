Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. 1 Part-I, Rules 8137(iv) and 4.9(b)(ii) - Increment - Petitioner who was on extra ordinary leave received increments but they were later withdrawn - Court held that the benefits could not be withdrawn without notice and ordered that the benefits already granted not be recovered - Principles of natural justice upheld.

I Part-I, Rules 8137(iv) and 4.9(b)(ii) - Increment - Extraordinary leave period for pursuing higher studies not counted towards increments - Appellant filed suit challenging the order almost nine years later - Court held the order null and void but limited the entitlement to arrears and benefits for only three years prior to the institution of the suit.
