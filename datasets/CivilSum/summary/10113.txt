Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule II, Item 6 - Regularisation of services - Contract employees at airport required relief of regularisation of services after their contract period ended - Contract employees were receiving payment without performing any work due to the absence of aircraft operations - It was decided that the employees cannot be paid salary for doing nothing and had to approach the relevant authority for their claims for absorption as regular employees - The decision of the single judge was dismissed.
