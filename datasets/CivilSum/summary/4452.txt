Civil Procedure Code, Order 21 Rule 12 - Grant in aid - Disaffiliation of affiliated institute - Staff salaries are affected by disaffiliation and grant in aid - Disaffiliation results in automatic discontinuation of grant in aid for staff salaries - Failure to pay salaries despite an undertaking does not count as contempt if no grant in aid was payable - If the contempt court acknowledges this, the executing court can refuse to execute the decree past the affiliation date.
