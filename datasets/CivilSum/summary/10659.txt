Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work - Qualifications - Conditions of appointment - Sewing Mistresses claiming pay scale equal to classical and vernacular teachers - It is not fair to prescribe different pay scales for incumbents possessing equal qualifications and in similar situations.
- Whether the appointment was made on temporary or regular basis or in relaxation of eligibility conditions, it should not impact equal pay for equal work - Amarjit Kaur v. State of Punjab 1988 (4) SLR 199 followed.
