The Rajasthan Civil Servants (Revised Pay-Scales) Rules, 1983 along with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India govern the pay scales of different categories of employees in various departments.
Though there was a revision of pay scales for certain categories of employees in 1984, the pay scale of Upper Division Clerks in subordinate offices was revised in 1995 after representations were made to the Anomalies Committee.
Notification was issued following the consideration of Anomalies Committee's recommendations.
The action of the government was found not to be violating Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
