Constitution of India, Article 226 - Contractual service - Renewal of contract - Regularisation of services - Interlocutory order - A petitioner filed a writ petition seeking to regularise their service, and filed an interlocutory application while the writ was pending.
The petitioner requested an interim order directing the respondent company to set aside their decision to decline to renew the service contract.
It was held that the decision of the company declining to renew the service contract was not the subject matter of the writ petition.
The petitioner prayed for the regularisation of their service in the writ petition, but the company cannot be compelled to take their services if they are not willing to do so.
