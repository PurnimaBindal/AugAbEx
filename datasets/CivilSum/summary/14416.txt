Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Suitability and fitness - Test and interview for promotion - Promotion was previously based on seniority-cum-fitness rule - Introduction of written test and interview to test suitability and fitness treating all eligible candidates equally - No discrimination occurred - Petitioner cannot claim denial of right of consideration for promotion on the basis of seniority-cum-fitness after availing the offer of being tested by a written examination and interview.
