Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Conduct of the parties - The petitioner was seeking admission for his son in Engineering College Kurukkshetra, under the non-payment seat reserved for children/grand children of Freedom Fighters.
However, even though the grandfather of the candidate was drawing Freedom Fighter pension from the Govt. Treasury in Haryana, the petitioner's application for a Freedom Fighter's certificate was refused on the ground of incompleteness.
Furthermore, despite the direction to not prolong the proceedings, the original record was not produced and the seats were filled without seeking any clarification from the court.
This was viewed as dishonest conduct by the authorities, and even though part of the course was already over, candidates with lesser merit were granted admission, while the petitioner was ignored.
Therefore, the respondents were ordered to reserve a seat for the petitioner in any Engineering College in the next session.
