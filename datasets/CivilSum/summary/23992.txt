Constitution of India Article 136 - Education - Homeopathic Medical College - The Central Council allowed the college to upgrade for starting a Degree Course.
The college subsequently advertised for admissions to the Diploma Course with a note mentioning that the course is likely to be converted to a Degree Course.
The Central Council objected, stating that only upgradation for starting a Degree Course is allowed and approval for upgrading the Diploma Course into a Degree Course is not given.
The State Council requested the Central Council's approval for upgrading the Diploma Course to a Degree Course.
However, the Central Council deprecated the approval of the State Council.
Students filed a writ for the conversion into a degree course, which was granted with certain directions.
The High Court did not interfere with the direction for allowing students to complete the required number of years or hours for acquiring a degree, but set aside the direction that apportioned the expenditure for the extra period of study between the college, Central Council, and State Council.
The entire expenditure for the Degree Course will be borne by the students.
