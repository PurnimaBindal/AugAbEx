According to Section 25(f) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a chowkidar's appointment may result in termination and subsequent reinstatement, along with back wages.
The Labour Court concluded that the respondent had worked for more than 240 days and that the provisions of the Act had not been complied with, thereby invalidating the termination.
The respondent was reinstated with back wages, and the award was upheld.
It is crucial to note that any workman whose service is thus illegally terminated will be entitled to full back wages.
