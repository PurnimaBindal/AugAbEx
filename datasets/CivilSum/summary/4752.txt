In accordance with Section 47 of the Civil Procedure Code and Sections 11 and 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act, an order that has been implemented resulting in the dismissal of contempt is not subject to execution application.
In addition, if an order passed in a contempt petition has not been challenged and becomes final, an execution application for the same order is not permitted.
[Para 7]
A dismissal of an execution petition cannot be appealed in accordance with Clause X of the Letters Patent Appeal and Section 47 of the Civil Procedure Code.
[Para 7]
When a contempt petition is dismissed and it is determined that the Court's directions have been fully followed, a fresh writ petition concerning the same order is not maintainable under Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Clause X of the Contempt of Courts Act.
This is because the directions cannot be issued again for implementation.
