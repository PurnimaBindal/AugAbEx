Petitioners were appointed under the condition of police verification regarding their antecedents, as mentioned in the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311, and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2, Item 6.
However, the police were not authorized to return findings on the issue, and their report that the appointees were not dependents of ex-employees only served as the basis for their termination.
The termination is unjust and penalizing, and goes against the principles of natural justice.
Determining the private status of employees was beyond the authority of the police, and it may impair their right to seek employment and cast stigma on their career.
The competent authority should have followed the procedure and returned its own findings.
