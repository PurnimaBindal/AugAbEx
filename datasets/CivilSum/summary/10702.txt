Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Electrical) Regulations, 1995, Regulations 9 and 10(7) - The de-recognition of a degree cannot be made retroactively, especially when the notification issued by the appropriate government states that the de-recognition only applies to immediate effect and is therefore prospective only.
The withdrawal of recognition with retrospective effect is not allowed.
