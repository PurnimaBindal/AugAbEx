Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab government National Emergency (Concession) Rules 1965, Rules 2 and 4 - Amended Rules for Military Service Benefits and War Service - A reasonable classification was made for those who were commissioned during the emergency and those who joined the army before the proclamation of emergency.
Those who enrolled or commissioned during the emergency joined the army at that critical juncture of national emergency and forfeited all other career opportunities, while those who joined before had already chosen their career path.
Benefits had been promised to those who heeded to the call of the nation during the emergency.
The differentia in rules is, therefore, intelligible and has a direct nexus to the objects sought to be achieved.
