Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Qualification - Experience - Petitioner seeking admission to M.B.A. (Part time) Course - The question arises whether or not the petitioner had the necessary two years of experience in the executive or managerial cadre - The job of the petitioner as Sales Supervisor holds an executive post - The respondents were not right in rejecting his claim - The petitioner receives direction for the respondents to consider his case for admission to the M.B.A. (part-time) course.
