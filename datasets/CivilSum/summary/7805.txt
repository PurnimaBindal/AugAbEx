Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2, Item 3 - Reinstatement with continuity in service and full back wages - Benefits of bonus/ex-gratia amount - Employees reinstated after being out of service are entitled to receive the same bonus/ex-gratia amount as other employees were paid during that period.
[Para 4]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2, Item 3 - Leave encashment - Reinstatement with continuity in service and full back wages - Leave wages - Full back wages for 12 months include leave wages, and no additional leave wages may be given.
[Para 5]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2, Item 3 - Uniform allowance - Reinstatement with continuity in service and full back wages - Employees reinstated with continuity of service are not entitled to claim a pair of uniforms for the period when they were out of service.
