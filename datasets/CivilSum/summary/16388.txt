Constitution of India, Article 226 - While appointed on a temporary basis as an Aerodrome Assistant, the petitioner was offered two options while on deputation - either to be absorbed in National Airport Authority or to return to the parent department - The petitioner had chosen to repatriate, but later challenged that decision on the basis of inadequate information - Court held that once an option is exercised, it is final and binding, and found no case made out.
