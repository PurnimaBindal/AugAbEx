Constitution of India, Article 226 - Uttar Pradesh Government Servants Seniority Rules, 1991, Rule 8(1) - Public Service Commission (Procedure) Rules, 1970, Rules 13 and 21 - By virtue of Rule 8, seniority can be given only from the date of substantive appointment - Court cannot direct fixing seniority ignoring the statutory rule.
[Paras 12 & 17]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Uttar Pradesh Government Servants Seniority Rules, 1991, Rule 8(1) - Uttranchal Subordinate Services Rules, 1951, Rule 5(a) - Seniority - Retrospective promotion and seniority - Cannot be granted - One cannot get seniority from a date when he was not borne in the cadre adversely affecting those appointed in the meantime.
[Paras 14, 15 & 16]

Constitution of India, Articles, 226 - Uttar Pradesh Government Servants Seniority Rules, 1991, Rule 8(1) - Quota rule - Seniority - The previous promotion would be regular only from the date of the vacancy within the quota and seniority can be given only from that date - The rule of quota being a statutory one, must be strictly implemented - It is impermissible for the authorities to deviate from the rule due to administrative exigencies or expediency - Any construction otherwise would be illegal, nullifying the force of statutory rules and would offend Articles 14 and 16(1) of the Constitution.
[Para 14]
