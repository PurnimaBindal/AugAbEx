Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 39(d) and 226 - Equal pay for equal work - Petitioner promotees seek to be paid same salary as direct recruits - Petitioners argue that they have requisite qualifications and similar responsibilities and functions - Respondents argue difference in pay scale due to different selection processes - Court held that employees with similar job functions cannot be denied equal pay based on different sources of recruitment - Since qualifications, responsibilities, and duties of promotees are same as direct recruits, denying equal pay scale is discriminatory and violates Articles 14, 16 and 39(d) of the Constitution - Petition allowed.
