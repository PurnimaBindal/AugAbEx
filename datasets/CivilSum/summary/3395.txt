Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Fixation of pay - Discrimination - The Appellants, who are Trained Graduate Teachers, are being paid the pay scale of primary teachers, while other teachers with the same qualifications are receiving pay scales applicable to Graduate Teachers.
The court finds this to be discriminatory and determines that the Appellants are entitled to enjoy the benefits of the scale of pay as applicable to trained graduate teachers of schools up to Class XII.
