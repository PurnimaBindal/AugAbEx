Constitution of India, Articles 226 - Dereservation of Posts - Petitioner's request to convert a post reserved for a Scheduled Tribe candidate into his favour due to it being unfilled was denied by the Tribunal.
The decision was challenged and it was held that whether a post can be dereserved or not is at the discretion of the concerned department or authority and no one has the right to claim an unfilled vacancy.
No court or Tribunal can direct a recruiting agency to fill a post by dereserving it.
Additionally, no de-reservation of a post can be done without prior agreement from the National Commission for SCs/STs.
[Paras 2, 4, 5 and 6]
