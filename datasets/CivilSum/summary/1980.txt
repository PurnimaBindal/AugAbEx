Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Selection - Bipartite settlement - Writ jurisdiction - A bipartite settlement between the management and union in 1967 stated that selection of Junior Operative Trainee would be conducted via interview only and this process was continued up until 1992 - In 1993, management introduced a system of written tests for recruitment - Petitioners were not successful in the written examinations and thus not called for an interview - They do not have the right to be appointed, as it is only conferred on eligible persons - The selection process is not justiciable unless there is illegality, irrationality or procedural impropriety.
