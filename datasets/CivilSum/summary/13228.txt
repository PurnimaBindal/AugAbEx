Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Reinstatement - Implementation of Award - Delay - A driver's services were terminated - Labour Court ordered reinstatement with continuity of service and full back wages - Rather than appointing him back as a driver, he was appointed as Assistant Foreman - He did not raise any dispute at the time - It is too late for petitioner to raise a dispute after about ten years that award is not implemented - Writ petition filed at belated stage dismissed.
