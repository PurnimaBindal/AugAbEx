Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Reversion - Appointment of Badli Fillers of Company as Clerk Grade II - Graduates from Maithili University, which is not recognized by University Grants Commission - Reverted as General Mazdoors due to lack of qualification in terms of Industrial Settlement - Reversion not considered as punitive or illegal.
University Grants Act, Section 22.
Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Termination - Graduate degree of Maithili University not recognized as University was not established by Central Govt. or State Govt. nor was it recognized by University Grants Commission - Termination of service of a person who obtained appointment on production of degree certificate alleged to be issued by Maithili University - Not considered illegal or improper.
