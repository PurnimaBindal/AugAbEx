Article 16 of the Indian Constitution - The Andhra Pradesh State Higher Judicial Service Rules, Rules 1 and 6 - Andhra Pradesh State and Subordinate Service Rules, Rule 10(a) (i) - Seniority - Quota Rule - Special Rules for appointment and seniority of District and Sessions Judges Second Grade - Seniority determined on the basis of length of service including temporary posts - Rule 6 of Special Rules operates independently and is not dependent on the proviso to Rule 2 - Seniority is not dependent on quota rule - Quota only applies to direct recruits against permanent posts.
