According to Constitution of India, Article 14, admission to Post-Graduate Degree/Diploma Courses in the specialities of MS (Surgery), MD (Tuberculosis and Chest) and MD (Psychiatry) should not be discriminatory.
The petitioner, who is fully qualified for admission and has an additional Diploma in Clinical Pathology and Micro-biology, was denied admission despite having more qualifications than those who were selected.
The reason being that he could only seek admission in the speciality in which he had done diploma.
However, a similar clause has previously been struck down as being discriminatory.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to admission.
[Paras 4, 5 and 7]
