Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Andhra Pradesh Agricultural (Produce and Livestock) Markets Act, 1966 - Andhra Pradesh Agricultural (Produce and Livestock) Market Rules, 1969, Rule 178 - Selection - The Agricultural Marketing Committee requires candidates for the post of Attender to be sponsored by the employment exchange in order to be considered - Rule 178 of the Rules is valid, requiring Agricultural Committees to notify employment exchange of vacancies for all posts, including Attenders, and select candidates from the employment exchange's list.
