Constitution of India, Article 14 - Voluntary Retirement - The petitioner, an SP of CID in Punjab, proceeded on leave during a time of terrorism and did not report back despite calls.
After requesting to retire, his offer was treated as a notice and accepted.
The petitioner later returned and requested to rejoin, claiming his offer was not a notice of voluntary retirement.
However, it was held that as a member of a disciplined force, he could not feign illness to avoid his duty at a time of need.
Additionally, after his request was accepted, he applied for his retiral benefits.
Thus, the petitioner cannot claim that his offer was not a request for voluntary retirement.
The petition was dismissed and he was charged Rs. 2000 as costs.
[Paras 19 and 20]
