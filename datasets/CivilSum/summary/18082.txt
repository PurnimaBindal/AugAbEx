The instructions stipulating that an employee needs to have received 50% good reports in the preceding 10 years to be eligible for promotion or selection are arbitrary, as per Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 162.
[Para 2]

According to Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 162, an employee becomes eligible for promotion and the grant of the selection grade once they have received a good report.
Average reports conveyed or considered as adverse cannot be used as a basis for denying promotion.
The appropriate directions have been issued, and arrears are to be paid with 12% interest within five months.
