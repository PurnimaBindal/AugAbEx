Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rules 19(ii) and 14 - Departmental proceedings - In case the holding of inquiry is not reasonably practicable, the competent authority must record its conclusions with sufficient reasons based on some material as to its satisfaction failing which the order of dismissal in the absence of any inquiry will be vitiated.
[Paras 12 and 14] B.
Back wages - Gainful employment - The employee has an initial onus to plead and prove their claim that they were not gainfully employed during the idleness period and the employer must place some material to show the gainful employment if the employee has fulfilled the onus - There is no normal rule of grant of full back wages if mere termination is illegal - Grant of back wages is at the discretion of the court.
