Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 47 Rule 1 - Review - Scope of - According to Order 47 Rule 1, a hearing may be permitted for the correction of an evident mistake or error on the record, or for sufficient reasons, but not solely because a party did not emphasize all aspects or could have made a stronger argument by citing authoritative precepts to obtain a favorable verdict, and it does not postulate re-hearing.
In the event of a miscarriage of justice or a serious or palpable error, the court may rehear the case under its full authority, even for new and substantial information or evidence that, after due diligence, was not known to the applicant.
