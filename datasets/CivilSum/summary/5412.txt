The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A states that in cases where a tribunal holds a domestic enquiry against a respondent workman that is found to be unfair and improper, a review may be sought by the employer to lead evidence in support of charges that were found to be proven in the enquiry.
However, in the case where the review of the order is not merely procedural but in fact, it is a substantive review, such review would be impermissible.
