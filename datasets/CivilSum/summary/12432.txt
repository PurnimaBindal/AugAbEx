Constitution of India, Articles 21, 300(b) and 311 - The petitioner, a 90-year-old woman, requested family pension due to her late husband's service in the police department.
The State Government issued a circular concerning minimum pension/family pension for State pensioners which fully addresses the petitioner's case.
The petitioner, a widow of a police officer who worked in the office of the Superintendent of Police and met the qualifying period for eligibility for grant of pension/family pension, must be granted relief of family pension.
If the petitioner's pension is denied, Article 21 - the right to life, including the right to earn a livelihood - would be explicitly violated.
The right to a livelihood would be meaningless without such relief.
The petitioner is entitled to family pension starting from the circular's date.
The petition is granted.
