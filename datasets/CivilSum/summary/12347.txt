Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 532-B - Voluntary retirement - Pension - Notice of voluntary retirement - The authority must permit employees with twenty years of qualifying service for pension to proceed on voluntary retirement - If an employee is asked to submit pension papers and allowed to retire, the authority cannot oppose their claim for pension and retiral benefits due to lack of qualifying service - Petitioner had completed 22 years of service before retirement, but his claim was rejected due to deduction of leave period - Such unfair actions by state authorities should have been disclosed to the petitioner prior to retirement.
