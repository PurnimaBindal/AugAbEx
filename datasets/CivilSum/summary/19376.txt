Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Classification and discrimination of pay scales - Meter Readers and Clerks in the Municipal Committees in Punjab - Employees of Urban Local Bodies had the same pay scale as Punjab Govt. employees - Revised pay scale rules adopted by the Punjab govt. - Meter Readers and Clerks had a common seniority list, equal pay and status, and interchangeable duties - Qualifications for both posts included matriculation with Punjabi, and no typing experience required for Clerk - The order of the Govt. of Punjab and Municipal Authorities withdrawing a higher pay scale for the petitioners/Meter Readers was quashed - Petitioners' names were to be restored in the combined seniority list with the Clerks and given pay scale of that category with arrears of the higher pay scale and 12% interest from due dates till the date of payment.
