Education - Certificates - The petitioner was denied admission to the Bachelor of Engineering course in Computer Sciences for not submitting their 10+2 pass certificate, despite providing a marks list.
However, other students with similar marks lists were admitted and the Education Board confirmed that no separate certificates were issued.
The denial of admission to the petitioner was deemed arbitrary, and provisional admission was allowed through an interim order.
The University was directed to declare their result.
