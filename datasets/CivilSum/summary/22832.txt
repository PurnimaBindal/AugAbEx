The Bombay Primary Education (Gujarat Amendment) Rules, 1978, Rule 34(1) & (3) discuss the superannuation age in relation to contractual service.
Rule 34(3) exempts those appointed on contractual fixed terms from retiring before reaching that age.
In this case, the appointment letters of the petitioners prescribe a particular retirement age as per the Leave Rules of the Institution, which also prescribe a superannuation age of 60 years.
Therefore, according to Rule 34(3), they are entitled to continue their service up to the age of 60 years.
