The Central Administrative Tribunal (Group B&C Miscellaneous Posts) Recruitment Rules, 1989, Rule 5(1) and (2) - Seniority - Length of service - Deputationist - Inter se seniority of deputationists must be determined based on their length of service, including their service on the equivalent posts in their parent department - Recruitment under Rule 5(1) permits the benefit of service on equivalent posts to be considered in determining length of service - The rule-making authority did not intend to remove this benefit from the deputationists.
[Paras 10 and 11] B Article 14 of the Constitution of India - Seniority - Length of service - Seniority is determined by length of service on similar/equivalent posts, including any held by deputationist in their parent department prior to recruitment - Total length of service is not necessarily relevant.
