Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule II, Item 3 - Termination of services - A workman was continued on temporary basis for 4-1/2 years and then had their services terminated not due to unavailability of work, but due to the possibility of claiming permanency - The Industrial Tribunal awarded renistatement and back wages which was upheld - The petition was dismissed.
