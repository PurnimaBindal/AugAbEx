Article 16 of the Constitution of India and Section 16E(10) of the U.P Education Act, 1921 state that appointment by promotion to L.T grade may be cancelled after a lapse of 23 years if the validity of the initial appointment as CT grade is questioned.
While the Director of Education has the power to cancel appointments, it must be exercised within a reasonable time.
In this case, cancellation was held to be improper as the appointment had been acted upon.
[Paras 4 and 6].
Section 16.E of the U.P Intermediate Education Act, 1921, and Regulation 6(6) of Chapter 2 states that if approval for a proposal for appointment as a lecturer is not given within stipulated 3 weeks period, deemed concurrence in favor of the appellant arises.
However, the appellant was not promoted, as the management promoted the respondent on said post.
The appellant is entitled to all retiral benefits on the basis of deemed promotion, but salary from the date of the respondent's promotion was approved had not been given.
