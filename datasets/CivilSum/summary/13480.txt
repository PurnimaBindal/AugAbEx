Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion/Revision - Sealed cover promotion - Ad hoc promotion subject to vigilance enquiry pending - While a vigilance enquiry was pending, the petitioner was promoted in regular manner on the recommendations of DPC despite being junior to Respondent No. 2 - DPC recommendations were kept under sealed cover due to Vigilance enquiry and could not be promoted till the sealed cover is opened - Respondent No. 2 given ad hoc promotion subject to result of Vigilance enquiry - However, action of the Govt in giving ad hoc promotion to the Respondent without opening the recommendations in the sealed cover and keeping him on ad hoc conditional promotion despite result of Vigilance enquiry against him and reverting the petitioner to make room for Respondent No. 2 was held as illegal and arbitrary - Reversion of the petitioner quashed.
