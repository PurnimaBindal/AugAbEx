Life Insurance Corporation Act (31 of 1956), Section 49 - Life Insurance Corporation of India (Agents) Regulations, (1972), Regulations 16(1), 17(1) - Petitioners are challenging the validity of Regulation 17(1) through which their services were terminated by the respondent Corporation - Regulation 17 permits termination of agency by notice - Petitioners argued that their marriage with regular employees of respondent Corporation is contrary to terms and condition of statutory Regulations - However, the agency termination has been rightly done in accordance with the observations made in Smt. Lalitha Devi's case by the Apex Court - The provisions of Regulation 17(1) are constitutional and not arbitrary or violative of any constitutional provision - The petition has been dismissed as a result.
