Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology in Patiala is considered a deemed University under the University Grants Commission Act of 1956, as stated in Section 3 of the Act.
As per the regulations of the university, the Master of Engineering Course has a duration of 1-1/2 years, and candidates must complete 10 papers (excluding seminars/design projects) from a list of approved subjects, with a maximum of 5 papers allowed per semester.
A petitioner in this case failed to attain the required Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 55 for the entire course, and subsequently filed a writ petition requesting the opportunity to improve his CGPA.
The interpretation of Regulation 7 states that for any reason a candidate may fail, they must be given further chances to pass a paper or improve their CGPA.
This interpretation also aligned with Article 14 of the Constitution, meaning that candidates who have failed due to their CGPA are not at a disadvantageous position compared to those who have failed in a paper under Regulation 13(a)(i).
Once a candidate has reached the required grade of 55 or above as outlined in Regulation 13(a)(ii), they cannot be allowed to further improve their grade as they would not be a failed candidate.
As a result, the respondent in this case was directed to allow the petitioner to sit in a paper/papers to improve his CGPA, and the petitioner was allowed to take the examination in pursuance of the court's interim direction.
If he made a grade of 55 or above as required under Regulation 13(a)(ii), he would then be declared pass.
