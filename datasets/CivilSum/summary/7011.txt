Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Retrenchment - Failure to comply with Section 25-F - Even if the workman accepted compensation, the employer's statutory obligation remains unchanged - Employer required to pay one month's salary in lieu of notice and compensation within the legally stipulated time - Workman has to be reinstated as per the 1984 RLR 981 case.
