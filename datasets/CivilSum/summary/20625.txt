According to Section 2(b) of the Contempt of Courts Act, 1972, wilful disobedience of court orders regarding financial adjustments, such as arrears of wages and additional benefits, may result in the court ordering payment.
However, if the non-payment is due to financial constraints, the court may order part payment and the remaining amount to be adjusted in the deposits of employees.
While the orders of adjustment may not fully comply with the orders to pay, they cannot be considered a wilful disobedience of the court orders.
