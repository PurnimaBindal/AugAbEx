Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - All India Institute of Medical Sciences Regulations, 1958, Regulation 26 - Adhoc appointment - Regularization - Seniority position - A petitioner working as nursing sister in AIIMS had been appointed as Staff Nurse on adhoc basis in 1975.
After applying against a regular vacancy, she was sponsored by employment exchange and recommended for regular appointment in 1976.
However, the letter of adhoc appointment did not indicate that the appointment was approved by competent authority and the initial appointment was not in accordance with recruitment rules.
The period of adhoc appointment cannot be considered for computing seniority, as it was not made as per rules, although it is short.
Therefore, the seniority position ascribed by the Institute through seniority list is legal and valid.
The petition was dismissed accordingly.
[Paras 19, 22 and 23]
