Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Delhi University Ordinance XII, Clause 3-A - Superannuation - Re-employment - The term "Re-employed" in the explanation to Clause 3-A refers to the re-employment of a Principal who has previously been a distinguished teacher, such as the Fifth respondent who has been teaching at the college.
It has been determined that a Principal can be re-employed after their superannuation up until they reach the age of 65.
