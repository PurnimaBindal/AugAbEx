The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule II, Item 6 deals with the regularization of services of daily wagers.
An award was passed for the regularisation of services with retrospective effect, but the employer challenged it.
The court stated that the mere continuation of long services did not warrant direction for regularization or absorption in the service.
The tribunal may direct the management to examine the cases of those workmen for regularization if they are deemed eligible.
The award was ultimately modified.
