The appointment of notaries was carried out in accordance with the rules stated in the Constitution, the Notaries Act, and the Notary Rules.
After a panel of 13 candidates was forwarded to the Commissioner, a panel of four names was sent afresh.
Objections were invited under Rule 6 but none were received.
All necessary procedural formalities were fulfilled and there were no illegality or procedural infirmity in the appointments.
The writ petition was dismissed.
[Paras 3 and 7]
The appointment of notaries fell under the jurisdiction of the State Government as competent authority under Section 3 of the Notaries Act.
Since the State was under President's Rule, the President of India made the appointments as the competent authority.
The appointments were deemed proper.
