Constitution of India, Articles 20 and 311 - Criminal proceedings - Departmental proceedings - It is generally not permitted to have simultaneous inquiry in both court and department on the same set of issues, as the party's evidence may be exposed and used against them in other proceedings.
However, if there is a difference in issues or if joint proceedings are ongoing against accomplices, the employer can proceed with the departmental enquiry.
