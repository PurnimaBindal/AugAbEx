Under the Haryana Assistance to Dependents of the Deceased Government Employee Rules, 2003, in accordance with Rules 3(K) and 18, a petitioner whose mother was a government servant and passed away when she was six years old is eligible for compassionate appointment if they meet eligibility requirements upon attaining adulthood.
The petitioner submitted an application for ex-gratia employment under the Scheme in 2004, and the State is required to appoint the petitioner to a class III position on a compassionate basis, as per Rule 18.
The State must also pay a cost of Rs. 10,000/-.
