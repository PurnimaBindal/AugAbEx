Criminal Procedure Code, Sections 468 and 472 - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 7(3) - Failure to pay gratuity within 30 days constitutes a continuing offense until payment is made - Section 468 Cr.PC limitation does not apply.

Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 7(3) and (9) - Non-payment of gratuity is an offense - Section 7(3) violation is punishable under Section 9 - Paying after prosecution initiation does not absolve liability, but may be a mitigating factor reducing the penalty.
