Constitution of India, Article 16 - Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules, Rules 14 to 17 - Appointment - Reservation for members of Ezhava community - The High Court held that eligibility for appointment against a reserved vacancy requires a merit assessment - The respondent, who belonged to the Ezhava community, was directed to be appointed to the post of Junior Assistant Professor in the Kerala Agricultural University since the university failed to produce records of the Selection Committee's fair assessment of the respondent - The Supreme Court found no reason to interfere with the High Court's direction in this specific case.
