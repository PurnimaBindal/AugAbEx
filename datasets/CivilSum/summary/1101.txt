The petitioner applied for a pension under the Freedom Fighters Pension Scheme, 1972 and was denied.
However, the Haryana Swatantrata Sainik Sanman Samiti recommended the State Freedom Fighters Pension be granted to the petitioner.
The Government of India refused the recommendation due to a lack of original documents proving imprisonment.
The decision was deemed violative of the petitioner's right to equality, as no evidence was provided to show distinctions in eligibility between state and central government conditions.
As a result, the petition was granted.
