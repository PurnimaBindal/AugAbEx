Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment on compassionate grounds - Termination/Natural justice - Justification of termination - A petitioner, who was appointed on compassionate grounds, had his services terminated without notice or opportunity given - However, he continued to work for 16 years - The court ruled that terminating him at this stage would be unfair and would subject him to a life of penury and destitution, which is not how the family member of a deceased employee should be treated - Therefore, the petition was allowed.
