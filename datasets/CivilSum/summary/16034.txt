Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 300-A - Army Act, 1950 - Pension Regulation 173 - Disability pension - Military service - The petitioner, a 27-year-old NK who had represented services teams at different levels as a heavy weight boxer, suffered pain in his chest during strenuous training which could have resulted from a blow he received at that time.
Medical reports corroborate this claim, and there is no evidence to suggest any other cause for his illness.
Therefore, the view that his disability is not attributable to military service cannot be sustained.
The rejection of the petitioner's claim for disability pension is deemed wrong, and the respondents are directed to determine the amount of disability pension he is entitled to.
