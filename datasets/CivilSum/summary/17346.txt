Jammu and Kashmir Police Manual, Rules 187 and 208(g) - Dismissal - Unauthorised absence - The petitioner was dismissed from their services due to unauthorised absence without an enquiry.
Rule 187 cannot be applied as the petitioner had worked for more than five years.
The impugned order has been nullified but the respondents may initiate an enquiry as per their discretion.
[Paras 4 and 5]
