Ad hoc appointment may count towards seniority if the appointee is later made regular following the regular procedure, even if the ad hoc appointment was made without following the prescribed procedures.
Seniority should be counted from the date of initial appointment.
Ad hoc does not mean temporary.
[Paras 7, 8, 9]

The length of probation period cannot be longer than the duration of appointment as the purpose of probation is to find the appointee's suitability for the post.
If found suitable, the appointee can continue in service.
Therefore, the duration of probation must be shorter than total duration of appointment.
[Para 5]
