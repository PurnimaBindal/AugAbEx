Constitution of India, Article 311 - Andhra Pradesh Education Act, 1982, Sections 79 and 83 - Resignation - Principal of a Degree College submitting letter of resignation - Petitioner did not send withdrawal letter before resignation was accepted - Managing Committee passed a resolution accepting petitioner's resignation with immediate effect - Petitioner was informed of the resolution - Resignation deemed effective from date of acceptance and could not be withdrawn thereafter.
