Central Board of Secondary Education Rules, Rule 36.1(iv) (a) - Examination/Use of Unfair Means/Incriminating material - Use of unfair means during the examination - The possession of undesirable paper in a pencil box by a student constitutes a violation of the Rule - The Rule applies without distinction to both bona fide and malicious possession of incriminating evidence - It is not necessary to demonstrate that the material was actually employed - The assumption is that the student has used an unfair means during the examination - The Rule forbids the carrying of incriminating data into the examination hall.
