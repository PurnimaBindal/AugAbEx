Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 136 - Equal pay for equal work - Daily wagers - Casual workers - The earlier Supreme Court decision that casual workers doing the same work as regular employees are entitled to equal wages and paid at the same rate has been affirmed.
The payment for identical work must be made on a daily rate basis, and there is no room for interference.
FCI's appeal was dismissed with Rs. 10,000/- fine.
