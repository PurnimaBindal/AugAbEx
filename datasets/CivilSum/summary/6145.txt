Constitution of India, Article 16 - Ad hoc appointment - Regularisation/Confirmation - The petitioner was initially appointed on an ad hoc basis on 28.2.1983 against direct quota.
The Recruitment Rules came into effect on 4121985, and regularisation in the post of Section Officer (Horticulture) is to be carried out under the Recruitment Regulations in force at that time.
However, the petitioner fails to meet the eligibility criteria in accordance with the Recruitment Rules.
Despite expressing inability to give concurrence to relaxation of the rules, the UPSC acknowledged that no direction could be given to regulate the petitioner in the post of Section Officer (Horticulture) given the laid down eligibility criteria in Recruitment Rules.
