Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Kanungos Service Rules, 1945, Sections 6 and 12 - Promotion - Eligibility - Departmental - proceedings - Departmental examination - Petitioner was not permitted to participate in a departmental examination due to pending departmental proceedings against him - However, after being honorably exonerated, he was allowed to pass the test - Petitioner's juniors were promoted and made senior before him because they passed the test earlier than him - Authorities were not justified in preventing the petitioner from appearing in the examination, solely due to the pendency of departmental proceedings - He ought to have been assigned seniority and given promotion above his juniors, assuming he passed the examination when his juniors were permitted to pass it.
