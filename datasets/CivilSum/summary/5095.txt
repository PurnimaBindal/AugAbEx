Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ jurisdiction - In terms of an administrative order, it is legal to withdraw or reduce allowances of employees of a corporation.
Administrative decisions are subject to judicial review, and if based on irrelevant considerations ignoring the relevant material, they must be termed to be perverse.
Therefore, a writ petition is maintainable.
[Paras 10 & 18]
Constitution of India, Article 162 - Punjab Small Scale Industries Corporation Article of Association, Articles 135, 80 and 81 - Policy decisions - The Board of Directors of the Corporation must make its own decision regarding the service conditions of its employees.
The instructions of the State Govt. are not binding, but may be considered along with its own rules and policies and other financial constraints.
A decision to withdraw or reduce allowances based solely on the directions of the State Govt. ignoring its own rules and policies, would be considered perverse and based on irrelevant considerations.
Such a decision cannot be sustained and must be directed to be reconsidered.
[Paras 15, 18, 19, 20, 22 and 23]
