The Selection Committee formed for the purpose of making appointments under articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India was not validly constituted due to the inclusion of an ineligible member.
The interviews conducted by this committee are thus considered invalid, rendering the entire selection process null and void.
[Para ]
A candidate was not admitted despite achieving the required minimum marks and being successful.
This was due to the negligence of the Selection Committee, resulting in the candidate losing a valuable year.
The candidate is entitled to damages for this negligence, with the compensation being equivalent to the salary payable to a primary teacher for one year.
