Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311(1) - D.D.A.
(Salaries, Allowances and Conditions of Service) Regulations, 1961, Regulations 15 and 19 - Removal - Petitioner was appointed to class IV post by order issued by Vice-Chairman but was removed from service by Director (personnel) who is authority subordinate to Vice-Chairman.
It was ruled that the subsequent empowerment made in favour of Director (Personnel) could not change the situation where the order of appointment was made by a higher authority.
Therefore, the impugned order of removal was set aside and the petitioner was ordered to be reinstated in service.
