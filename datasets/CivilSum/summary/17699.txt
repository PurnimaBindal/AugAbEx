Admission/Eligibility - The minimum requirement for admission to the Electrician Trade was to have passed matriculation or its equivalent, or be a 10th pass (under 10+2 system) with Science, Physics and Chemistry, but it is not reasonable to require a minimum of 33% marks in the subjects of Physics and Chemistry.
The petitioner was denied admission due to a non-existent factor in the eligibility conditions, leading to unfair discrimination against the petitioner.
