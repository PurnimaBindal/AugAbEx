According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, as well as Rajasthan Education Subordinate Service Rules, 1971, Rule 16 and Employment Exchanges (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959, Section 4, vacancies must be advertised and applications must be invited openly.
The manner in which the vacancies are advertised can be selected, but there is no choice in the matter of whether or not to advertise them.
However, selecting candidates only from those sponsored by Employment Exchanges violates Rule 16 of the Rules and Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
