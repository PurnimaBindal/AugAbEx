Employees (Conduct) Rules, 1966 - Dismissal order - Challenge as to - It is not acceptable for a person to be a judge in his own cause - No witness can certify the accuracy of their own testimony - Order of dismissal against the appellant is invalid as it was decided by respondent No. 3, who had a personal stake in the enquiry.
