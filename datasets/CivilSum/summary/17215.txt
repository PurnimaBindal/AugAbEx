Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Part I, Rule 4.12A - Qualifying service for pension - Class III and Class IV employees who undergo departmental training should be considered as undergoing qualifying service for pension - If an employee was unable to receive a post without required training, it will be assumed they underwent it, unless it is proven otherwise - Loss of service record or inaction of authority cannot be used as a reason for the employee's suffering.
[Paras 4, 9, and 10] B.
Pension - Delay and laches - Claim of pension cannot be denied purely based on delay, especially if the delay is recurring - If retiree had made representation and it was accepted but rejected by higher authority after a long time, the petition cannot be dismissed for delay.
[Para 8] C Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Part I, Rule 4.12A - The word 'may' used in Rule 4.12A has to be interpreted as 'shall', as otherwise the Government would have the discretion to grant relief to certain individuals while denying it to others in similar situations.
