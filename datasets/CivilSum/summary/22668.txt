UP Government Servants (Discipline & Appeal) Rules, 1999, Rule 13 (as applicable to UP State Industrial Corporation employees) - Retrospective application of Rules to Corporation - The UP Rules of 1999 were automatically applicable to Corporation employees, so there is no need to pass a specific resolution for adoption.
Therefore, any retroactive effect of the UP Rules of 1999 to the Corporation is not necessary.
[Paras 10 & 11]
UP Government Servants (Discipline & Appeal) Rules, 1999, Rule 13 (as applicable to UP State Industrial Corporation employees) - Revisional authority - Retrospective application of Rules to Corporation - The Corporation has adopted the procedure of the UP Rules of 1999, but not the authorities of the State.
This means that the Revisional authority prescribed for the State employees under the Rules adopted by the Corporation cannot have jurisdiction over the employees of the Corporation.
As an independent body, the Corporation needs to substitute and prescribe its own authority.
[Paras 10 & 12]
