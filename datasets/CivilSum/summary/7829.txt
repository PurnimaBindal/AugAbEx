Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules 1958, Rule 9(a)(iii) of Part II - Rule - Scope of - The application of Rule 9 is considered in a scenario where temporary employees are continuing beyond the term of their appointment.
The court held that Rule 9(a)(iii) of Part II of Rules and its proviso only apply when the services of provisional appointees are terminated during their appointment time to make room for PCS/Regular hands.
Hence, provisional employees do not have a legal right to continue in service beyond their appointment period.
