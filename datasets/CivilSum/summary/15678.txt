Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Electrical) Regulations, 1965, Regulation 10 - Promotion - In the case of a vacant Assistant Engineer position, if an employer decides not to fill the available position, it cannot be forced to do so by the writ court - However, if vacancies are regularly filled and there is no conscious decision to keep the position vacant, different considerations may apply - Given a specific averment in the petition that the Board has regularly filled such positions, it is deemed unjust and unfair for the Board to keep this particular post vacant since 1991 - The respondent is directed to fill the position without delay.
[Paras 9 and 10]
