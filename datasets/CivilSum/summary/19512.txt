According to Sections 1(5) and 2(1) of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, it is necessary to determine jurisdiction and the appropriate government.
In this case, Punjab Roadways in Chandigarh is not under the control of the Central Government.
Therefore, the State Government is the appropriate government for Punjab Roadways, Chandigarh, and can apply the provisions of the Act by notification.
However, the administration is not authorized to apply the Act's provisions to the employees of Punjab Roadways in Chandigarh.
