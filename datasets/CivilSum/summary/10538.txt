Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311(2) - Termination/Discharge from Service/Ad hoc Temporary appointment - Temporary or ad hoc employees are not entitled to be regularised into permanent positions and can be terminated by a simple order under relevant rules or terms of appointment - Even if they are not found to be satisfactory, the competent authority can terminate their services after holding a preliminary enquiry without punitive action - However, if a punitive action is taken, ad hoc/temporary employees are protected under Article 311(2) - The use of the words "Terminate" or "Discharge" is not necessarily punitive and only leads to civil consequences if it affects future Government employment opportunities - The Court can look at the background of such orders to determine their true nature.
