The Constitution of India's Article 14 and 16 - Verification of genuineness of degrees and diplomas - Refusal to appoint a candidate after clearing confusion surrounding his degree is a violation of his fundamental and legal right.

The Constitution of India's Article 14 and 16 - Selection - Ban on appointment - A candidate who has been duly selected cannot be refused appointment based on the later imposition of a ban on such appointments.
