Punjab Civil Service Premature Retirement Rules, 1975, Rule 3(i)(a) - Premature retirement/Integrity doubtful/Enquiry - No inquiry or show-cause notice is mandated by the rule - Crossing the efficiency bar does not completely erase adverse remarks - "Integrity doubtful" carries a ten-year service career penalty - The premature retirement of the petitioner based on an overall service record assessment is lawful.
