Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary Proceedings - Accused charged with misappropriation of Rs. 100 - Punishment of dismissal - Money refunded after filing FIR - Enquiry Officer concluded that refunding does not absolve accused - Held, reasoning was correct under the circumstances and charge cannot be declared as not proven.
[Para 2] B. Disciplinary proceedings - Three charge-sheets - Three writ petitions addressed by single order - Filing of three separate appeals - Held, appellant should have filed one consolidated appeal since order of dismissal could not have been made effective by thrice dismissal.
[Para 6] C. Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary Proceedings - Punishment of dismissal - Proportionality of punishment - Accused charged with misappropriation of Rs. 100 and negligence of duty - Held, considering small amount, gravity of misappropriation cannot be treated as less significant.
As punishment of dismissal deprives employer of 19 years of service left and benefits of 18 years of service rendered, punishment converted to compulsory retirement in the interest of equity and justice, relying upon judgment of Apex Court in Husaini's case.
