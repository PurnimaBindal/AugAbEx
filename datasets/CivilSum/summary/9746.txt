Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Retrospective promotion - Back wages - If an employee is entitled to a job or promotion at a particular time but is not offered it, they should not suffer any loss through no fault of their own - They should receive wages for that job or post from that date - The principle of no work no wages does not apply in this case - Late approval of the post shall not deprive the employee of the benefits of such an employment.
