Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Seniority - Officiating service - The petitioner, who has been working as Zonal Education Officer/Joint Director of Education on an ad hoc basis since 1989, had his services regularised in 2002.
It has been held that if the initial appointment procedure is followed, and the appointee continues in the post uninterruptedly until regularisation in accordance with rules, the period of officiating service must be counted.
The petition has been allowed.
