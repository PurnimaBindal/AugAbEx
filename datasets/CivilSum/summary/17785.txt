Evidence in a case of alleged rash and negligent driving revealed that the accident occurred after the claimant attempted to overtake the car, resulting in the loss of control on their moped.
The tribunal found no evidence of fault on the part of the respondent, and their decision was upheld.

A claimant under the Motor Vehicles Act sought compensation after incurring injuries during an accident.
However, the tribunal determined that the injuries were not severe and did not result in any significant economic losses for the claimant.
As such, they were not entitled to compensation.
