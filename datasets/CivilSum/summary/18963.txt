The Constitution of India, Article 20(2) and the Maharashtra Agricultural Produce Marketing (Regulation) Rules, 1967, Rules 46 and 102 govern promotions and double jeopardy.
The rules clearly state that a person being considered for promotion must not have any punishment recorded against them.
Despite facing departmental proceedings and being punished, the petitioner will not be subjected to double jeopardy if their case is not considered for promotion.
However, as a result of their punishment, they will lose their seniority and be placed at the bottom of the seniority list.
[Paras 5, 6, 9 and 10]
