Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10, 12(3), 33-C(2) - Voluntary Retirement Scheme - The Labour Court allowed an application under Section 33-C(2) granting benefits of the settlement after an increase in the age of retirement to 58 years claimed the benefits.
The single judge held the view of the tribunal was unsustainable as the settlement covers only cases of existing employees.
The benefit sought to be enforced is necessarily a pre-existing benefit following from a pre-existing right.
[Paras 8 and 12]

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(s) - Voluntary Retirement Scheme - Having resigned from service by Voluntary Retirement, the cases of such employees are not covered by expression "workman."
[Para 11]

Voluntary Retirement Scheme - The amount is paid for voluntary retirement in lieu of employee leaving service and foregoing all his rights or claim in the same.
Its purpose is to bring a complete cessation of jural relationship between employer and employee.
If an employee is still permitted to raise a grievance, the whole purpose of introducing a scheme would be totally frustrated.
[Para 10]
