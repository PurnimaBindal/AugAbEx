Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Compassionate appointment - All eligible persons must be given an equal opportunity to fill public posts, especially when there is high unemployment - Compassionate appointment should only be used in exceptional cases - If the family has already received a large amount of benefits from the deceased and the applicant and their sibling are already earning through self-employment, and there is no serious financial burden on the family, it is reasonable for the authority to reject the request for compassionate appointment.
