The Constitution of India, Articles 309, and 226 - Waiting list - Future vacancies - Policy under the rules - The Court must enforce the government's declared policy in accordance with the rules for filling future vacancies that arise within six months of selection from the waiting list, unless the policy contradicts the rules - The fact that the vacancies arose after selection is irrelevant in this case - The government is required to make appointments from the waiting list accordingly.

The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and laches - The Supreme Court made it clear that the appellants could file a proper writ petition before the High Court in its order dated 13.10.95 while disposing of the appeal of the petitioners - The writ petition filed by the petitioner on 2911996 cannot be rejected due to the time delay as per Para 3.
