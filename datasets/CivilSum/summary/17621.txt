Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Recovery - Loss to the Govt. - Negligence - The enquiry officer found that the petitioner was responsible for causing loss to the government due to his negligence.
Although the petitioner submitted his explanation via a show cause notice, proportionate loss was sought to be recovered from him without holding any further enquiry.
However, there were no details regarding the amount of loss or how it was apportioned.
The mere findings in an enquiry against a third person cannot be applied to other employees.
Additionally, the argument that each person was responsible for 25% of the total loss simply because there were four people involved is not valid.
The order of recovery has factual and legal infirmities and is related to a matter from 1976-78.
Despite the show cause notice having been issued in 1990, the order of recovery was quashed.
