Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 2(1)(d) - Dependent - Compensation can be awarded to parents of the deceased workman if they were wholly or partly dependent upon the deceased - The employer failed to prove that the mother was not dependent upon the deceased - Previous court cases support the mother's entitlement to compensation under the Act.
[Para 5] B Workmen's Compensation Act, Section 4 and 4-A - Penalty and interest - Employers who do not pay compensation within a month are required to pay penalty and interest on the owed amount.
