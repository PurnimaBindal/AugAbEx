Constitution of India, Article 226 - Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Section 103 - Alternate remedy - Maintainability of writ petition - Writ jurisdiction cannot consider factual aspects, such as those related to a preliminary investigation and an FIR filed against a petitioner for using forged Scheduled Caste certificates to obtain Govt. service - Recommended that petitioner pursue alternative remedies under the concerned Code.
