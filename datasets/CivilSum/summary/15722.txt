Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Natural justice - The petitioner was appointed as a Guest Relation Executive after careful consideration - He took on the role, but was later offered the position of Captain, which cancelled his appointment as Guest Relation Executive - Without any notice to show cause, the order to cancel the petitioner's appointment is in violation of natural justice principles - The impugned order is set aside.
