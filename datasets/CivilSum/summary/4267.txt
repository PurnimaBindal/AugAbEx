Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Sections 47 and 115 - Termination - Back wages - Jurisdiction - If the termination order is rendered invalid, it is deemed to have never been passed and the employee is entitled to continuing service and consequential benefits of salary.
Plea of jurisdiction cannot be raised again in execution if it has already been decided by Civil Court.
[Paras 6 to 15] B.
Service Matter - Promotion - Promotions are not a right and are not entitled by Govt. servants, who are only entitled to consideration for promotion when it becomes due.
Consequential relief does not entitle an employee to promotion or salary of the promoted post.
The employee will only receive salary from the date of termination.
