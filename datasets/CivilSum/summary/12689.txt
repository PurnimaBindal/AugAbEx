Constitution of India, Articles 14, 162 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - Reservation of posts for compassionate appointment on attaining majority by dependents - Impermissible - Concept of compassionate appointments are designed to help grieving families of a deceased employee overcome their sudden loss of earning member, not as an alternative to recruitment in violation of equal opportunity of all eligibles - Officials/authority who make assurances and reserves posts for compassionate appointment for extended periods, causing harassment for applicants, can be held personally liable for damages - Petitioner granted cost of litigation Rs. 5,000/- to be recovered from officials at fault.
[Paras 6 and 9]
