Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 24 - Management cannot take back employees without them undertaking improvements - Tribunal prohibits lockout - Workmen's violent and disorderly conduct leads to Dharna and obstruction - Employers must comply with labour laws but employees must work smoothly - Employers can refuse to take back employees who refuse to give undertaking.
