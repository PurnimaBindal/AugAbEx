According to Central Information Service Rules, 1959, Rule 6-A and Constitution of India, Article 16, seniority between employees of the same cadre must be determined based on the date of entry into the service - Direct recruits came to the service in the cadre in 1970 and were rightly placed above employees whose services were regularised in 1977, after remaining out of the cadre before regularisation.
The Court cannot interfere when action is in accordance with Rules.
