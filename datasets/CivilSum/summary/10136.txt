Workmen's Compensation Act (8 of 1923), Sections 3, 2(d) - `Dependents' - Claim of compensation by parents of deceased workman - Deceased was earning Rs. 700/- per month - Deceased was unmarried - Parents were dependent on his earnings - Parents are legal heirs of deceased and entitled for compensation.
[Para 9] B Workmen's Compensation Act (8 of 1923), Sections 3, 2(n) - Compensation - Death of employee of fire works shop due to sudden break out of fire - Employer's plea that 20 or more persons were to be employed to hold a person workman is not tenable and depends upon question of fact - Employer cannot raise plea at appellate stage - Order of Commissioner holding deceased as workman and allowing compensation is proper.
