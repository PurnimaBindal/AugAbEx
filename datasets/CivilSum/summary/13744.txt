According to Article 309 of the Constitution of India and Rule 103 of the Rajasthan Medical and Health Subordinate Service Rules (1965), a female government servant is entitled to three instances of maternity leave, regardless of receiving consolidated wages.
A circular released on July 23rd, 2004 stating that employees on fixed salary are not eligible for maternity leave goes against Rule 103 and can be invalidated.
