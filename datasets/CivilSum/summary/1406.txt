Constitution of India, Article 309 - Appointment/Equal Opportunity - The purpose of statutory recruitment and appointment rules enacted by the State Government under Article 309 of the Indian Constitution, corresponding to Section 124 of the State Constitution, is to provide equal opportunity for all eligible citizens and to draw out the best available candidates for public appointments by impartial selection committees.
[Para 4] B Constitution of India, Article 16(4) Reservation - The state is required to reserve certain posts for Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and other socially and economically backward classes.
[Para 4] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equality of opportunity - Articles 14 and 16 grant all citizens the fundamental right of equality before the law, and respondents cannot take actions that negate that equality granted by Article 16, preventing eligible citizens from being considered for public appointments on merit.
[Para 4] D. Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ of Mandamus, when can be issued - The issuance of a writ of mandamus requires the satisfaction of two conditions: the existence of a statutory right in favor of the petitioner and a statutory obligation on the part of the respondent against whom the writ is directed.
[Paras 4 and 7] E. Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Writ of Mandamus, when cannot be issued - A writ of mandamus cannot be issued in cases of ad hoc appointments made by an incompetent authority, when the period for which the appointment was made has expired, or when the appointment was made in clear violation of Articles 14 and 16.
Irregularly appointed civil servants cannot assert the right to hold the post.
