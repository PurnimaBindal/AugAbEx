Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India outline equal pay for equal work, but classification based on qualifications must be equal in all respects.
Possession of higher qualifications may be a valid basis for categorizing employees, and pay may vary based on academic qualifications, experience, skill, quality, or responsibilities of duties.
The court cannot interfere with an employer's decision to place employees in a particular pay scale, unless the classification is arbitrary, unfair, or irrational.
