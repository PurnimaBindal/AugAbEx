Constitution of India, Article 310 - Jammu and Kashmir Civil Service Regulations (Vol I), Article 226, 1st Proviso (Vol. II) Schedule II Entry 39 - Age of retirement - Superannuation age - If a respondent was appointed in an inferior grade, they were entitled to retire at the age of 60 years.
However, if they were holding a post not in the inferior grade and the statute provided a superannuation age of 58 years for that post, they will superannuate at the age of 58 years and not at the age of 55, even before reaching the age of superannuation.
[Paras 5 and 6]
