Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Challenge as to - Appointment on the post of Library Attendant in PGI - The petitioner, who was not selected and has challenged the selection of respondents, claimed that he is more qualified than the respondents but his plea was rejected - The selected candidates had the prescribed qualifications and marks were allocated according to the criteria - The petitioner cannot complain about being denied equal opportunity - The court cannot act as a Super Selection Committee - Therefore, the selection is upheld.
