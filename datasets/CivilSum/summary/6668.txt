Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Accident - Compensation - A claimant-respondent, who was a helper and sustained injuries including a fracture of the right leg and left hand, sought compensation after being injured while his truck was parked on the left side of the road due to mechanical trouble and was hit by another truck - The Commissioner awarded him a sum of Rs. 30,405/- - The Insurance Company appealed and the compensation was reduced to Rs. 23,500/- - The jurisdiction of the High Court was limited in the appeal.
