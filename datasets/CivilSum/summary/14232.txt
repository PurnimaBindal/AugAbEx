Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Punjab Kanungo Service (State Service Class III) Rules, 1976, Rule 8 - Promotion - Candidates must pass a departmental test for eligibility for promotion.
If a candidate does not fulfill this condition, but has passed some papers, they may still be promoted but must pass the departmental examination within the designated period.
If a competent authority has not issued an order relaxing this condition, ineligible candidates cannot continue holding the promotion post nor can other ineligible candidates lay claim to promotion on the basis of seniority.
Such individuals are liable to be reverted.
