Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rules 16, 86(3) - Unauthorised absence - Disciplinary proceedings - Termination - In case an employee is found guilty of willful absence beyond one month as per Rule 86(3), they can be terminated from service.
However, in a specific case, the Single Judge of the High Court substituted the punishment with compulsory retirement.
The absence of 3 years was borne from the record.
The court held that in view of facts, circumstances, Rules and law laid by this Court order of removal is the only proper punishment to be awarded and not disproportionate as alleged.
[Paras 9, 13 and 19]
