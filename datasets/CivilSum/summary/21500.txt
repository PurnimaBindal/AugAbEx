The appellant was compulsorily retired on the grounds of 'doubtful integrity' as determined by the Screening Committee.
Judicial review of the order imposing premature compulsory retirement is permitted only if it is arbitrary, mala fide, or based on no evidence.
The safety of a senior officer with an unblemished reputation is compromised when their integrity is declared doubtful solely based on statements of individuals prosecuted by said officer.
The order of premature compulsory retirement has been overturned.
