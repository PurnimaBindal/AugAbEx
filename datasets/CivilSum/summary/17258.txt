The petitioner challenged the deputation of respondents to an intermediate school course for the promotion to the post of A.S.I.
Petitioner was not considered while respondents were appointed as Constables subsequently.
Though the respondents earned promotion as Head Constables prior to the petitioner and they are senior to the petitioner.
Since the respondents remained in Patiala District whereas the petitioner belonged to Ropar district, and the seniority lists of Constables and Head Constables are maintained district-wise, there is no ground for petitioner to make grievance about sending respondents to such course.
[Paras 3 to 5]
