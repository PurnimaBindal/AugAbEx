Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Confirmation - Distinguishes between concept of regularisation and promotion - Promotion is based on assessment of relative merit from senior most personnel in lower cadre, whereas for confirmation and regularisation, only satisfactory performance and vigilance angle is considered - Petitioners are entitled to regularisation of their services due to uninterrupted officiation as Junior Officers for more than 7 years, and sudden conversion to Senior Clerks should not be allowed.
