Constitution of India, Article 226 - Reduction of posts - Termination - The termination of employees on account of a reduction in posts must take into account reservations for specific categories.
The government has made a policy decision to reserve a certain number of posts for a particular category.
In the event of a reduction in the number of posts, the number of vacancies for reserved categories will be reduced proportionately, and the quota for the reserve category must be considered for the remaining posts.
In this case, the petitioner's service could not have been terminated as he was within his quota of the category of ESM after the reduction of posts, and the quota for ESM was not taken into account while retaining personnel on posts of Forest Guards.
It is important to ensure that reserve categories are represented up to the fixed quota, and termination cannot be based solely on seniority.
Therefore, the termination is not sustainable and is set aside.
