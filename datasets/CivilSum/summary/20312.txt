The Education officer post was upgraded to Deputy Director and the Corporation requested that a government officer be transferred to fill the position in order to oversee the Corporation's educational standards.
However, the Tribunal cannot provide direction to create a post or specify minimum qualifications; that is the domain of the legal or executive policy of the Government.
Therefore, the Court or Tribunal lacks the power to issue such directives, and the challenged direction is invalid.
The appeal is granted.
