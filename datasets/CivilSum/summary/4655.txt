The Punjab Agricultural University Act, 1961, Section 14(b) and (c) outlines the Board of Management's power and functions.
The Board is authorized to hold, deal and transfer property of the University while the Vice Chancellor is the university's Principal Executive and Academic Officer.
If the Board approves the exchange of land with the Wakf Board, the Vice Chancellor cannot be held accountable.
The Vice Chancellor is only in charge of execution and any irregularities cannot be blamed on the Vice Chancellor.
