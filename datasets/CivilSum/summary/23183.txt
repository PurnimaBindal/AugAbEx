Constitution of India, Article 12 - Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, Section 3 - Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, Section 68-A - Andhra Pradesh Factories and Establishments (National Festival and other Holidays) Act, 1974, Section 11(1)(c) - The "establishments" under the control of the government in Section 11(1)(c) of the 1974 Act and Article 12 of the Constitution have the same meaning - State Road Transport Corporations established under Section 3 of the Road Transport Corpn.
Act are wholly controlled by State Governments - Establishing a corporation separate from the State Government does not necessarily mean it is not under the State Government's control - Section 68A(b) of the Motor Vehicles Act only states that a state transport service and undertaking may be carried out by the Central Government, State Government, or any corporation or company thereof - By virtue of Section 11(1)(c) of the 1975 Act, the provisions of the said Act would not apply.

Constitution of India, Articles 215 and 216 - Binding precedents and judgments - A decision made by a Full Bench is obligatory on smaller/single benches - A single Judge sitting singly cannot express a different view on the same point of law - Judicial discipline requires that he must either follow it or ask for a reference of the matter to a larger bench.
