Constitution of India, Articles 16, 21 and 226 - Appointment on compassionate grounds/compassionate appointment - Wife of deceased seeks appointment - Objections raised regarding applicant's property and service benefits of deceased husband - However, financial soundness of family is not a factor in compassionate appointments - The goal is to prevent hardship, insecurity and separation for dependents - Simply providing money will not console the family - Providing appointment can keep the person busy and provide stability - If no posts are available, supernumerary posts should be created - Application should not be rejected and employer is directed to consider the appointment within two months.
[Paras 5 and 6]
