According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the Petitioner seeks regularization on a higher post and pay scale with consequential benefits since he has been performing Lower Division Clerk duties for the past 21 years.
However, the respondents denied the claim as the Petitioner is not qualified and has not gone through a routine selection process.
Despite the Petitioner being a regularized RAT Gang Coolie, the Respondents contended that he was paid over time allowance for his duties in a diverted capacity, but there is no supporting document to show that the work they did in the diverted capacity was authorized by the competent authority.
Therefore, the court is not competent to examine the disputed questions of facts, which need to be decided by a more suitable forum, and the petition is disposed of accordingly.
