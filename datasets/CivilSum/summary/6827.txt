Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Haryana State Central Cooperative Banks Staff Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1975, Rule 5(iv) - Termination of workman appointed as Manager Cooperative Society - Reinstatement awarded after inquiry conducted on charges of misappropriation and embezzlement - Workman discharged without proceeding with the inquiry report - Court finds that the alleged embezzlement formed the basis of the discharge order which was not a discharge simplicitor but was punitive in nature - The order could not have been passed without holding a regular inquiry and giving the opportunity of a hearing in accordance with the law settled by the Apex court in VP Ahuja's case.
