Bombay Civil Services Rules, 1959, Rule 161(1)(aa)(i) - Premature retirement - Notices of premature retirement based on the recommendation of the Reviewing Committee - The notice must be issued by the Under Secretary and not by the appointing authority, pending departmental inquiry.
Adverse remarks considered for last 10 years, even which was not communicated within the prescribed period - It was held that the notice issued was as the communicating agency of the Govt. and hence, it was with the authority of the Govt. - Notices of premature retirement on the subjective satisfaction of the Govt. cannot be said illegal.
The principle of natural justice is not applicable in the case of compulsory retirement; hence, the Govt. is not required to give the option of voluntary retirement - Communicating adverse remarks to person is a directory provision hence Reviewing Authority considers the entire record of service - There is a failure to prove enmity behind passing of the impugned notice.
The impugned notice of premature retirement is not illegal.
