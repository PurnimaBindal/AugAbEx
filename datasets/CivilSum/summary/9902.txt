Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958 - Dismissal - Natural justice - Absence from duty - Medical ground - The willful conduct of an employee must fall within the ambit of willful or deliberate absence from duty for it to be a major or minor penalty as contemplated under the CCA Rules, 1958.
The petitioner informed the competent authority of their absence from duty on medical grounds due to their ailment and the need for bed rest, but leave was not sanctioned.
The petitioner's forced leave on medical grounds cannot be considered as an irregularity to attract even the imposition of a minor penalty, and the enquiry officer should formulate an opinion recording a finding - which was not done.
The petitioner is directed to be reinstated with all consequential benefits.
[Paras 8, 9 and 10]
