Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission - Admission declined due to absence in counseling - It was decided that counseling does not grant or reject the right of a candidate to admission - As soon as a candidate submits a bio-data in counseling, he is considered to be present in the counseling - Regardless of occasional absences, it cannot be claimed that a candidate did not participate in counseling at all - The University has been instructed to admit the petitioners to the course.
