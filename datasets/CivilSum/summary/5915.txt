The Post Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, Chandigarh Act, 1966, and related Regulations establish that selecting authorities are not always bound by their recommendations, but appointing authorities must have good reasons for not appointing recommended candidates.
If appointing authorities choose to make appointments, they cannot appoint someone who was not among the recommended candidates.
In this case, the Governing Body did not accept the recommendations of the Selection Committee and appointed someone who was not recommended.
This appointment was quashed as contrary to the law.
The writ petition was allowed, and the order appointing the individual was set aside.
The Governing Body was directed to appoint the petitioner in accordance with the recommendations of the Selection Committee.
The High Court has the discretion to grant relief to petitioner even if the proper writ or direction has not been prayed for.
[Paras 7-12]
