Pension Regulations for Army, 1861, Rule 173 - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 13(3) Item III(i) - Disability pension - The petitioner joined the army as a sepoy on 19968 and had a motor vehicle accident while on casual leave on 30.11.77.
His disability was assessed at 40% in 1979, but was discharged only on completion of 17 years of service under rule 13(3) Item III(i).
However, since his injury was not attributable to military service, the petitioner is not entitled to a disability pension.
