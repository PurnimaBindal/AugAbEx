"According to Articles 16 and 14 of the Constitution of India, a government servant who is a heart patient receiving treatment at a specialized hospital like the Escorts Heart Institute is eligible for full medical reimbursement.
If the required treatment is not available at a government hospital, then the government is obligated to reimburse the full amount paid to the specialized hospital.
This interpretation is based on the JT 1997(1) SC 416 case.
[Para 6]"
