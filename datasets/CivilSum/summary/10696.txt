Constitution of India, Article 226 - Sikh Gurdwara Act, 1925, Section 132 - Sikh Gurdwara Employees Service Rules, Rules 4 & 6 - The Service Rules framed under the Act are justiciable in a writ under Article 226 - Particularly where violations of the rules have taken place - The writ can be invoked by a person aggrieved.
[Para 5]

Sikh Gurdwara Act, 1925, Section 132 - Sikh Gurdwara Employees Service Rules, Rules 4 & 6 - Rules 4 and 6 are separate and distinct - Rule 4 applies to misconduct charges, while Rule 6 is to be applied in case of absence from duty for more than 10 days – Absence from duty is a misconduct and can lead to removal from service - The penalty of removal from service must be imposed with observance of principles of natural justice - The failure to comply with Rule 6 will also vitiate proceedings and orders.
[Paras 6, 7, 9]

Sikh Gurdwara Act, 1925, Section 132 - Sikh Gurdwara Employees Service Rules, Rules 4 & 6 - Extension of leave - Absence from duty as overstayal of leave - Rejection of leave extension and requirement to return to work constitutes an opportunity to join work and not an opportunity to explain the absence - Delays during travel are not a deliberate disobedience, if the employee is able to show quick attempts to join duty.
