The petitioner opted for VRS and subsequently wanted to withdraw his application.
However, the credited VRS benefits were never objected by him and it is evident from his statement of account that the credited amount was substantially used for making a fixed deposit and withdrawing some amounts.
In light of the decision in LPA 396/2001, PNB v. CS Sanon, stated on 13.2.2006, the petitioner cannot withdraw his VRS application.
