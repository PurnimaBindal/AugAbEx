The Govt./Society has the power of withdrawal of nomination from a non-statutory/honorary post of Chairman of Association of the Indian Council for Social Science Research on account of losing confidence in the office holder - The court cannot issue a writ of mandamus in such circumstances as there is no legal right and corresponding legal duty upon the respondent - As it was done in terms of non-statutory Memorandum of Association of the Society, it does not even attract principles of natural justice or procedure of administrative law of conducting proceedings.
[Paras 31 and 51] B.
In the case of withdrawal of nomination from a non-statutory office and where the Govt. exercises pleasure doctrine without giving any notice or hearing, no prejudice can be said to have been caused for non-observance of principles of natural justice.
Court shall not set aside an order only because the principles of natural justice have not been complied with unless a prejudice caused is shown.
