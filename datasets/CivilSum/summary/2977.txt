Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14, 15, 19(1)(g) and 21 - Transfer - Cancellation of - Gender bias - Mala fide - Cancellation of transfer after implementation - While the Constitution provides for equality between men and women and de jure equality to women, it does not necessarily accelerate de facto equality between the two - In this case, a Lady Officer with a small child was transferred far away - Despite relocating and vacating official accommodation without protest, her transfer was cancelled at the request of an individual who claimed that a lady officer cannot effectively execute the functions of such post - This action lacks fairness and does not serve the public interest, thus, the cancellation of transfer orders should be upheld.
