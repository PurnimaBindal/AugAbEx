The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 govern the provision of higher pay scales for teachers with higher qualifications.
In Punjab and Haryana, the pay scale of lecturers is only granted to those actually posted as lecturers, and there is no automatic entitlement to it for teachers in education departments who attain higher qualifications.
There is no Supreme Court ruling or government order indicating automatic entitlement to the lecturer's scale for teachers with higher qualifications.
Different sets of rules apply to the two posts.
Certain court cases have addressed this issue, but they do not establish an automatic right to the lecturer's scale.
Some cases have directed the government to grant higher grades to teachers who have acquired particular qualifications, while others have rejected the notion of automatic lecturer's scale entitlement.
