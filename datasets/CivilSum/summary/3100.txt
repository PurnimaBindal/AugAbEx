Constitution of India, Article 311 - Temporary appointment - The petitioner was appointed on a purely ad hoc and temporary basis and their service was governed by appointment letter rather than recruitment rules.
Even though the petitioner was appointed after a selection process, they cannot be considered to have been appointed on a substantive basis and their services ended automatically with time.
As a result, there is no question of termination of the petitioner's service.
[Paras 29, 31 and 32]
