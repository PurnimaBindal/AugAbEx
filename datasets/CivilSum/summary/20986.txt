Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - U.P.
Secondary Education Service Commission (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 1981, Rule 4 - Appointment - Promotion is not a right, but rather the right to be considered for promotion if the requirements of the law are satisfied - Rule 4 of the Order authorizes the authority to make an adhoc appointment until a regular appointment is made - Although the seniormost person can generally expect to be appointed, this is not the case if they are incompetent - In this case, the appellant was not found to be competent, and his relationship with teachers and other employees was unsatisfactory, which led the management to fear that he would be unable to work as part of a team - Since this could harm the interests of the institution, it was not wrong for the District Inspector of Schools to refuse him a promotion and for the High Court to reject his writ petition.
