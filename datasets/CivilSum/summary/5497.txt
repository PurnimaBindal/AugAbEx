The petitioners in a unit of NALCO, who were security personnel, sought affirmation of their status in NALCO under Section 10(1) of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970.
It was argued that the security service rendered by the petitioners was of a perennial nature and that material on the number of employees needed to be employed on a regular basis was lacking.
The Labour Commissioner was directed to conduct an inquiry and provide a report.
