Constitution of India, Article 309, Proviso - Ordnance Factories Services Rules, Rule 2.1 - Promotion - Panel of successful candidates - The Tribunal's directive to form a Departmental Promotion Committee in April 1994 and to consider confidential reports of eligible candidates for 1993 while disregarding those of 1994 for the employees retiring in that year was legally appropriate.
The appeals were thus dismissed.
