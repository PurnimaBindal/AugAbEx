According to Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221, discharge from service is authorized for lack of good performance during three years from enlistment.
Although the petitioner was previously discharged, he was re-enlisted after approximately a month and a half with the assurance of keeping up with good performance.
His re-enlistment was approved by the SP out of compassion.
However, he was again discharged under the same pretext within three years.
The three-year period would be calculated from his re-enlistment date, and the previous period cannot be considered to suggest that the discharge order was issued after the expiration of three years.
[Paras 7 and 9]
