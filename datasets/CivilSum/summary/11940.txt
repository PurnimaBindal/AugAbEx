Punjab Civil Services Pre-mature Retirement Rules, 1975, Rule 4 and amendment No. 29 of 1979 - Pre-mature retirement/Notice period - If an employee gives notice for not less than three months after completing 20 years of qualifying service, they can seek pre-mature retirement - The petitioner served notice seeking voluntary retirement - The petitioner later gave another notice seeking voluntary retirement with immediate effect, which clearly disclosed their intention to seek voluntary retirement by giving three months notice - The refusal to grant voluntary retirement cannot be sustained as the petitioner had complied with the rules and served the requisite notice - The petitioner shall retire with effect from the date they stopped attending duty after serving notice and be entitled to pensionary benefits according to the rules - The petition is allowed with costs of Rs. 2000/-.
