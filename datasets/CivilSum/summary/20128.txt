According to rules 47 and 48 of the Bar Council of India, a charge of professional misconduct is similar to a quasi-criminal charge.
It must be proven beyond a reasonable doubt, rather than just by preponderance of probabilities.
In this case, the complaint failed to prove that the appellant engaged in taxi business after being enrolled as an advocate.
Thus, the Disciplinary Committee made an error in finding the appellant guilty of professional misconduct.
