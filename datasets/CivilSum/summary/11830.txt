Termination - Civil suit - Plaintiff's lawsuit for a declaration that she cannot be terminated from employment and is entitled to reinstatement - Not viable - This is because the suit is against a private institution that is not covered under the definition of State under Article 12 of the Constitution nor does it have any connection with the State - Constitution of India, Article 12.
AIR 1976 SC 888 and AIR 1981 SC 122.

Termination - Civil suit - Reinstatement - The defendants cannot be directed to reinstate the plaintiff as an interim measure - If the plaintiff is seeking remedy, it must be in the form of damages.
