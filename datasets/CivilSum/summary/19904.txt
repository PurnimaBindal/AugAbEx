Constitution of India, Article 226 - Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 - Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1949, Rules 49-A and 59 - Authority under the Act/Authority conferred/Necessary qualifications - The Civil Surgeon has been granted powers of Licensing Authority under the Act, but if he does not possess the necessary qualifications, he cannot act as the Licensing Authority and refuse renewal of licenses, which would be considered a refusal without jurisdiction.
