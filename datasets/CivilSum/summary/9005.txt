According to Articles 14 and 311 of the Constitution of India, an employee's probation period can be extended and they can be promoted on probation.
However, this does not automatically lead to confirmation unless there is a specific ceiling provided in the service rules or contract of appointment.
Additionally, a lack of a provision for extension of probation does not exhaust the management's power to do so under Section 13 of the General Clauses Act.
In cases where an employee is promoted only on probation, confirmation should follow unless it is deemed confirmation.
If the probationer's work and conduct is found unsatisfactory during the extended probation period, a promotee may be reverted to their previous position and a direct appointee may be discharged.
