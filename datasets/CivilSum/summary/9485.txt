Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Grant of additional increment on completion of 8 and 18 years services and higher standard pay scale on completion of 10 years of service - The court held that employees cannot receive the benefit of adhoc service for the purpose of proficiency step up and higher standard of pay after 8 and 18 years of service.
Therefore, the petition was dismissed.
