Constitution of India, Articles 311-A and 266 - Gratuity - Loan - The amount of loan taken by the employee from his department during service shall be deducted from the amount of gratuity paid to him.
The order of deduction must be upheld by the Single Judge of the High Court.
However, the Division Bench of the High Court in a separate writ petition ordered refund of the deducted amount without any reason, thus ignoring the earlier order of the Single Judge.
This action by the Division Bench is considered illegal as they entertained the prayer and ignored the earlier order of the same court.
As a result, the Order of the Division Bench has been set aside, and the appeal is allowed with costs.
