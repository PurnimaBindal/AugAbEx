Punjab Municipal Employees Pension and General Provident Fund Rules, 1994, Rules 1(3) and (4), 2(1), 2(h) and 2(j) - Punjab Municipal Act, 1911, Section 31 - Qualifying service - Option - Payment of Pension and Provident Fund - According to the rules, qualifying service includes the employee's entire service under the control of the Municipal Committee, which includes the period for which the employee was contributing to and receiving payment from the Provident Fund.
There is no provision in the rules for excluding this period when calculating qualifying service for the purpose of pension.
The amount of Provident Fund paid to the employee, which he was required to deposit in the pension fund, can be adjusted from the amount he is entitled to receive as retiral benefits after considering his entire period of service from the date of his appointment in the committee.

Punjab Municipal Employees Pension and General Provident Fund Rules, 1994, Rules 1(3) and (4), 2(1), 2(h), 2(j) and 8(4) - Pension - Provident Fund - The rules state that Punjab Municipal employees are entitled to pension, for which Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol. II apply.
Rule 8(4) requires that when sanctioning pension, the sanctioning authority must ensure that the employee's contributions for their period of reckoning for pension have been properly credited to the fund.
This should not be interpreted to mean that qualifying service is to be reckoned only from the date contributions are made by the employee towards contributory pension fund.
[Paras 11 and 12]
Punjab Municipal Employees Pension and General Provident Fund Rules, 1994, Rules 1(3) and (4), 2(1), 2(h), 2(j) and 8(4) - Qualifying service - Pension - Interpretation of the statutory rule - The definition of "qualifying service" as contained in rule 2(j) of the pension rules is clear: the employee's entire service in Municipal service is to be computed towards qualifying service.
A rule cannot be interpreted to mean the period of service rendered from the date the employee starts making contribution towards pension fund.
