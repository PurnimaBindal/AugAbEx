According to Constitution of India, Article 16 and UP State Universities Act, Sections 13(6) and 8 as amended in 1991, Assistant Director is considered as a teacher employed by the University for imparting instructions, including a Director as well.
However, Vice Chancellor is not authorized to make the appointment of teacher of University under Section 13(6) (8) of the Act after 22111991.
[Paras 5 and 8]
As per Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 and UP State University Act Sections 2(19) 16(6), (8) - (As amended in 1991), Vice Chancellor does not have the power to appoint a teacher in the University under Sections 13(6) and (8) after 22111991.
The petitioners cannot be appointed by Vice Chancellor in exercise of aforesaid power, nor can they get the relief as asked for that the respondents basis of appointment made by Vice - Chancellor under Section 13(6) (8) of the Act Entitled to their salary for the period for which they actually worked.
