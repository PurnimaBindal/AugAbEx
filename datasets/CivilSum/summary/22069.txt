Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 136 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Procedure and pleadings - Reinstatement - Tribunals consider the case and pleadings presented by both parties - Inconsistent stands cannot be taken - Relief is granted based on the workman's case, not the management's defence - In one case, the workman claimed that he was appointed as Octroi Moharrir and not as Octroi Peon, which the management was able to prove was untrue - The award of reinstatement with full back wages without considering the management's stand was deemed unsupportable - Therefore, the workman was not entitled to any relief.
