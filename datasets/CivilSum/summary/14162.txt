According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India and the Punjab Civil Secretariat (State Service Class III) Rules, 1952, promotion to the post of Clerk reserves 20% of posts for seniority-cum-fitness based promotion from Class III employees whose pay scale is less than that of a clerk and Class IV employees, provided they have five years' experience on any Class III post.
However, the petitioner, who was a Class III employee, had not completed five years of experience by the cut-off date.
The cut-off date was extended due to the pendency of the writ petition, but the completion of five years' experience during that period alone does not entitle the petitioners to be considered for the said post.
Furthermore, since they had not applied for the post, they cannot claim the right to be considered.
Hence, the petitioner is not entitled to any relief.
[Paras 7 and 8]
