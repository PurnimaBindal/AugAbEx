The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Classification - The government has the power to make a classification, but each classification must be reasonable and have a connection with the objective it seeks to accomplish.
[Para 9] B The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Increment - Distinction in sports - According to the government circular dated Oct 14, 1990, a sportsman can receive special increments based on the distinctions he has achieved, provided the conditions for eligibility are fulfilled - The petitioner qualifies for eligibility criteria - He achieved national and international recognition as a sportsman between 1978 and 1989 - Thus, he is entitled to the grant of special increments from 24.10.1990 - The government's refusal to grant increments to the petitioner was illegal - The petitioner is entitled to the grant of increments as per the prescribed distinctions attained prior to 24.10.1990.
