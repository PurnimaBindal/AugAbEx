The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 state that removal from service due to theft is not an unjust punishment, and can only be modified in cases where the initial punishment is shockingly disproportionate.
Judicial forums should not extend generosity or sympathy in such cases.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 state that once the Labour Court passes an award based on factual circumstances, it should not be interfered with in an exercise of writ jurisdiction under Article 226.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 state that the past unblemished conduct of an employee is not a sufficient reason to avoid removal from service when they are found guilty of theft of their employer's property.
Employers will be justified in removing an employee in such cases, and judicial forums should not extend sympathy or generosity.
