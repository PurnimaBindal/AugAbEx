Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-B - Misconduct - Punishment - Punishment must be proportional to proven misconduct - A conductor in State Roadways was accused of not issuing a ticket worth Rs. 810 - There was no evidence to suggest that he habitually refused to issue tickets after collecting fares from passengers - The Labour Court's decision to reinstate him without backwages was upheld.
