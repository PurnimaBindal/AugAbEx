The appellant did not meet the requirement of three years of service when the respondents underwent training, according to Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 1922 and 137.
There is no flaw in the decision made by the authority or the Single Judge, and the appeal is denied.
[Para 4] B.
There is only one facility available for police training in Haryana State, and it can be referred to as either a school or a college.
Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1922 applies to this facility.
