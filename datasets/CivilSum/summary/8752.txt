Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Reinstatement - Back wages - Promotion - The doctrine of no work no pay applies only when the employee deliberately abstains from work, not in cases where illegal termination of workman is set aside - If the termination of an employee is declared illegal, reinstatement and promotions, if his juniors have been promoted, are in order - Arrears of salary of promoted post are due from the date the employee is found eligible for promotion - The benefit of salary of promoted post cannot be denied just because the employee did not work in the promoted post during the period of termination - Doctrine of no-work no-pay does not apply in this case - Relevant case law discussed.
[Paras 5 to 8]
