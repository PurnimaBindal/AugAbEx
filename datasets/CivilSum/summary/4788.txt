Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Act, 1994, Section 89(8) - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Rules, 1996 - Unauthorized transfer orders made within Panchayat Samiti by State Government - Such orders are beyond the powers conferred by the statute, and therefore, remain unauthorized - The State Government lacks the authority to issue such transfer orders - The transfer order made by State Government must be quashed.
