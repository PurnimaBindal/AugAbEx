The Constitution of India, Articles 16 & 311 - Confirmation - Probationer - Upon completion of government service probationary period, confirmation of an officer may not be necessary - The outdated confirmation rule allows room for executive authorities to act arbitrarily or with mala fide intentions, leading to unnecessary legal battles.
[Para 7] B The Constitution of India, Article 16 - Probationer - It is not necessary to confirm an officer who was appointed on probation after the probationary period has ended.
