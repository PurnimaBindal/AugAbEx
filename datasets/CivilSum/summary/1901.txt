Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(4), 10-B and 11 - Interim order - Transfer dispute pending before labour court - Reference seeks to determine propriety of management order - Management transferred respondent No. 2 during pendency of dispute - Labour court stayed transfer - Respondent No. 2 not excluded from reference as they are a union member - Tribunal's stay order not arbitrary in light of pending policy decision - Respondents will suffer irreparable loss if transfer is not stayed - Petition dismissed based on balance of convenience.
