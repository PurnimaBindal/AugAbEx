Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 2(1)(n) - Workman - Deceased employed by a Foreign Shipping Company through an Indian Company acting as an agent - The deceased was, without question, a crew member of the ship at the time of the accident - Consequently, the deceased was a workman and the provisions of the Workmen's Compensation Act are applicable.
[Para 6] B. Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 4 and 22-A, Schedule IV - Compensation - Jurisdiction - The Commissioner is authorized solely to grant compensation or order the payment of compensation as specified by the Act, and not beyond that limit.
