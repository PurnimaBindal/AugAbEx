Under Himachal Pradesh University's First Ordinance of 1973, Clause (m) and (n), specifically Ordinance 635, if a petitioner conceives of veneration concerning the purported use of unfair means by constituents and evaluators in exams, they cannot assert their right to seek relief by demanding jurisdiction under Article 226, and there is no breach of Article 14 or any other fundamental rights of the petitioner under the Constitution of India.
