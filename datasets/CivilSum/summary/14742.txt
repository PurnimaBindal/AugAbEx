Rule 11 of the Haryana Civil Medical Service (Class-II) Rules, 1978 outlines probation requirements, including the presumption of satisfactory completion if an extension order is not given before the extended period expires.
Additionally, termination of services as a probationer is not allowed after the probation period has expired with one month's notice.
Accordingly, the termination in question has been nullified.
