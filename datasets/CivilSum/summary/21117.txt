Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 15 - Constitution of India, Article 309 - Tamil Nadu Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1953, Rule 8, Rule 8(v)(a) - Tamil Nadu Pension Rules, 1978, Rule 9(1)(b) - Interference with three punishments for same charge - Respondent employed as Block Level Accountant charged for making unauthorized entries in the stock register of food-grain quantities; misappropriation of food stocks on a large scale; and causing loss to Government - Despite the severity of the charges proved, the punishment of stoppage of two increments without cumulative effect was imposed due to the accused retiring soon - Suspension was also directed to count as a period of service without pay - The Tribunal cannot interfere with the punishment unless it is shockingly disproportionate to the charge.
