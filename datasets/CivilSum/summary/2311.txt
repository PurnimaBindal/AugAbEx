The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2 Item 3 and the Evidence Act, 1872, Sections 3 and 106 govern the process of domestic enquiry with regards to conductors who are found in possession of excess cash.
The burden of proof shifts to the conductor to demonstrate that the recovered amount was not a pilferage.
