Maharshtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of service) Regulation Act, 1978, Sections 9 and 11 (2) (c) - Salary - Appeal - Arrears of salary - The Schools Tribunal cannot hear employee appeals for arrears of salary or direct payment of arrears prior to termination, dismissal, or removal orders.
[Paras 11 and 12]  B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33C(2) Workman - A Clerk working in a private School is considered a workman for the purposes of Section 33C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, and the Labour Court is capable of hearing claims under this act.
