The criteria laid down for passing the entrance test for admission to Post-Graduate medical courses are valid and not subject to challenge.
Admissions made on the basis of such criteria cannot be considered illegal once the court has upheld the validity of these rules.
The prescribed 50% marks for general and 40% marks for SC/ST category candidates are neither invalid nor illegal.
It is not possible for the court to issue a direction to admit those who do not secure the prescribed minimum marks.
The State Government can add to the eligibility criteria laid down by the Medical Council of India in order to maintain the standards of education for admission to Post Graduate medical courses.
The court cannot direct the State Government to fill up the vacant seats with candidates who did not qualify in the entrance test.
The State Government has already been directed to take necessary steps to accommodate the maximum number of suitable candidates by relaxing the requirements of minimum marks while ensuring the standard of education and regulations prescribed by the Medical Council of India.
