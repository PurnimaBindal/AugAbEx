The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 and Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 were used in the case of a co-operative bank employee who embezzled Rs. 47,000/-, repaid it a year later, and was later dismissed from his job after an inquiry.
The employer lost faith in the employee, making it difficult to argue that the dismissal was disproportionate to the charge.
