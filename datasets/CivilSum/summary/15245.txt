Constitution of India, Article 226 - Haryana Cooperative Societies Act, 1984, Sections 102, 103 - Claim of regular pay scale - Petitioner, who has served as a mechanic for weigh bridge on a fixed pay since 1978 and has successfully completed two years of probation, contends that no post of Weighing Scale Mechanic is available and that regular pay scales have been granted to other employees at par with employees of the Haryana Government.
The Additional Registrar directed the respondents to consider the petitioner's claim, but relief was not granted.
The court held that, since the grant of regular pay scales to other similarly situated employees is not controverted, the petitioner is entitled to regular pay scale and directs that it be granted accordingly.
[Paras 10 and 11]
