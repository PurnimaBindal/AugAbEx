Central Services (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1944, Rule 8 - Constitution of India, Articles 21, 41 and 47 - Medical reimbursement - The government's decision to limit the amount of medical assistance for different types of treatments and surgeries is inadequate and could result in denying proper medical reimbursement - Any rule, order or instruction resulting in such a denial should be ignored - The petitioner was reimbursed Rs. 1,50,000/- for the purchase of a pacemaker but is not entitled to reimbursement for room charges in a private ward.
