Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - Cancellation of - Opportunity of hearing - A petitioner's admission for a diploma in Pharmacy cannot be cancelled without a show cause notice or opportunity to be heard.
The petitioner challenged claims that any other candidate with better marks had previously been admitted and this was not supported by evidence.
The petitioner had already started attending classes, making his admission complete, and therefore cannot be condemned unheard.
As a result, the order cancelling his admission was quashed.
[Paras 7 and 8]
