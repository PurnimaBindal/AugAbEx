Constitution of India, Article 311 - Temporary appointment - Termination of temporary appointment based on allegations of accepting illegal gratification without inquiry - Issuing such a stigmatizing order violates the mandate of Article 311 and must be nullified without providing the delinquent with a full opportunity to defend themselves - Nevertheless, in light of the available evidence against the delinquent, only 25% back wages are to be paid with reinstatement.
