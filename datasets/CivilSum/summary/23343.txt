Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion/Pendency of Departmental - Proceedings/Minor punishment/Consideration - A Commercial Tax Officer sought promotion to the post of Assistant Commissioner of Commercial Taxes but was deemed unfit due to pending departmental proceedings culminating in a minor punishment.
While he cannot claim promotion as a right, he has the right to be reconsidered, and authorities have been ordered to review his case sympathetically.
