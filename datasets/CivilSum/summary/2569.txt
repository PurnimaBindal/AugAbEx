Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Master and servant - A retained practicing lawyer hired by a Company to represent its interests before Labor Court and Conciliation authorities is denied his request for an increase in emoluments, but is later allowed an ex parte application claiming allegedly due amounts under Section 33-C(2) of the Act - Petitioner-Company seeks setting aside of ex parte award before Labor Court, but also approaches High Court with writ petition impugning Labor Court's order - Court finds the ex parte order to be in error and quashes it - Relationship between the parties cannot be deemed one of master and servant.
