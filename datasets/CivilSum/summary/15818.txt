Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Charge Sheet - Departmental Proceedings - Enquiry - The principle is well-established that an unexplained delay will nullify the departmental proceedings.
DDA gave self-contradictory explanations in its counter affidavit.
Such explanations cannot be deemed plausible.
Moreover, there is no justification for the inordinate delay of 7-10 years in initiating departmental proceedings in each case and then not pursuing them.
Furthermore, the fact that the DDA changed Inquiry Officers without just cause also adds weight to the argument that the charge sheets must be quashed.
[Para 9]
