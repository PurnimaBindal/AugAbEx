Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - State - Cooperative Societies - Educational institution run by private management registered under the Co- operative Societies Registration Act, 1860 and affiliated to Panjab University U/s 27 of Punjab University Act - The rules of the University govern service conditions and appointments of the College staff, with appointments requiring the approval of the University - The University, however, has no control over the staff beyond that point - The College's funding does not come from the Govt. or public body - The amendment in the Service Regulations for equal age of retirement for men and women is challenged on the basis of retrospectivity - The management's actions do not involve any breach of public duty - Respondent-College is not considered as `State' for the purpose of maintaining of writ petition - Thus, the Writ Petition is held not maintainable.
