Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Being included in the select list does not guarantee appointment - The petitioner applied for the post of Legal Adviser, was interviewed, and placed in the select list - However, the Corporation did not make any appointments from the list and instead issued a fresh advertisement - The petitioner did not acquire an indefeasible right of appointment by being on the list - The Corporation did not use a pick and choose method and did not violate any fundamental rights of the petitioner.
[Paras 3 and 4]
