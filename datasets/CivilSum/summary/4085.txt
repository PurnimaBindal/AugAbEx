Constitution of India, Article 226 - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 2A and 4 - "Continuous service" - Lock-out declared by Co in which petitioners were working - Management taken over by appellant-Company - Petitioners claimed continuous service and for notional gratuity - Trial judge found petitioners in continuous service and allowed appeal - Gratuity is based on past service, not present, and Section 2A broadly defines "Continuous Service" - Section 4 requires gratuity to be paid on termination of employment after five years of continuous service - Lock-out does not end employer-employee relationship and bipartite agreement cannot override statutory right.
