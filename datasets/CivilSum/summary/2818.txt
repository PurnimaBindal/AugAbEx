Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1)(d) - Wages for lock out period - Workers' strike initially justified but became unjustified due to duration - Management lock out - Workers not entitled to wages if strike unjustified and lock-out justified - Tribunal apportioned blame between management and workers at 50 per cent - Valid reasons given for apportionment - No grounds for interference under Article 226 of Constitution.
