The Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16, the Civil Procedure Code, Sections 9 and 11, and the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10, all indicate that an alternative jurisdiction exists for workers who cannot avail of both labour court and civil court after invoking one - The principle of res judicata under Section 11 will apply to avoid vexation for the same cause of action.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 226, and the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11B, provide that writ courts have powers to issue equitable orders without procedural technicalities that affect dispensation of justice - If the charge of embezzlement against a worker was not proven and allegations were based on probabilities, the writ court can correct the situation.
