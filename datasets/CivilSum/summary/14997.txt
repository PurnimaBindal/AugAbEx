Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 50 - Army Instructions 14/77, Paras 5, 11 & 11(A) - Pension for officers of Military Nursing Service - Qualifying service - Rule 50 applies only to those officers who have served for at least 20 years and are eligible for pension - Officers of Military Nursing Service (Local) are subject to Para 5 and 11 of Army Instructions 14/77 for pension and gratuity - If the employee retires at age 55, the qualifying service must be 30 years, while if they retire at 58, it must be 33 - Payment of gratuity must be made according to Para 11 and pension according to Para 5 of Army Instructions.
