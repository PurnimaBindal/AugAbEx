The Institute of Management Sciences, recognised by All India Council for Technical Education, cannot change its admission criteria for MBA courses after the completion of the admission process.
Candidates who meet the criteria advertised must be considered for admission.
[Para 13] B.
The allotment of seats and students for MBA courses at the Institute of Management Sciences is the responsibility of the competent authority.
The management cannot fill vacancies in free or payment seats without prior approval.
However, admission against such vacancies from the waiting list based on the merit of the entrance test is valid, as per Unni Krishnan's case.
Admissions made by the management at its own discretion without an entrance test are not valid and violate regulations.
Refunds with interest must be issued but no penalty imposed on the management if the student knowingly participated in the illegal admission.
