The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 16 and 162, outlines the granting of increments including the Family Planning incentive.
Executive instructions were given to grant increments to those who underwent Family Planning operations and treat it as part of basic pay with consequential benefits.
The Commissioner's order to treat the increments of the petitioner as personal pay without being added to basic pay was deemed illegal and quashed.
It was held that the incentive should be added to the basic pay and shall not remain merely as an increment without any consequential benefits.
