Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Advance Increment - Acquisition of higher degree - Relevancy - The High Court is the best judge of whether acquisition of an LL.B.
degree is relevant to improving the efficiency of typists or other categories, and it is necessary for the government to reconsider the matter afresh if the High Court concludes that acquiring an LL.B.
degree would be useful and improve efficiency.
The government may also extend benefit prospectively.
[Paras 14 and 15]

Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14 - Benefit of advance increment - Acquisition of LL.B.
degree - Steno-typists are entitled to advance increments if acquisition of an LL.B.
degree is relevant to their functions, as stated in GOMs.
182 and GO No. 142.
[Para 21]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Acquisition of additional qualification - Advance increment - Whether an individual is eligible for an advance increment depends on their minimum required qualification for their position.
Junior Assistants who acquire an LL.B.
degree are entitled to an advance increment.
Those who acquire an LL.M.
degree are entitled to an advance increment if that degree is the minimum qualification required for their post.
Steno-typists who have already received an advance increment upon acquiring a higher qualification other than the minimum required are not entitled to another increment if they acquire an LL.B.
degree while working in the same category of steno-typist.
[Paras 22 and 23]
