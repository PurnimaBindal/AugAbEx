Constitution of India, Article 226 - Central Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1986, Rule 2 - Fixation of pay scale - Lineman in Electricity Department - According to the recommendation of IV Pay Commission, there has been a revision in pay scale - Classification of posts doesn't determine fixation of pay scale - The classification of posts and prescription of pay scales serve as two separate and distinct functions - The order of tribunal that fixed the pay scale based on classification of post was deemed improper - The petition was allowed.
