Rajasthan Financial Corporation (Staff) Regulations, 1958 - Regularisation - After completing the selection process and creating a select list, a petitioner who was appointed temporarily cannot be regarded as being appointed substantively, as per Article 226 of the Indian Constitution.
[Para 31] B Rajasthan Financial Corporation (Staff) Regulations, 1958 - Regularisation - A petitioner appointed on a temporary basis demanded to be treated as being appointed as a substantive staff member or to be regularised, but he did not meet the requirement of passing a graduation examination to be given an annual grade, as said in the appointment letter - Qualification of Madhyama Visharad does not count for all purposes; only for the certificate in Hindi level - As per condition in the appointment letter, he is not entitled to regularisation.
