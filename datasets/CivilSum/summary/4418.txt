Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Tamil Nadu Recognised Private Schools (Regulation) Rules, 1974, Rule 15(4) - Promotion - Basis - Merit and ability - For promotion to the position of Headmistress, selection must be based on merit and ability according to the rules - Both the petitioner and the 4th respondent are Post-Graduate Assistants, which is one of the feeder categories for the post of Headmistress - Seniority is only relevant if both candidates have similar levels of merit and ability.
