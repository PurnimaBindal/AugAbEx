Under Indian Constitution, Articles 226/227 govern the granting of disability pensions.
In the case of a petitioner who joined the Army in 1966 and was injured by hostile individuals while on leave in 1993, the Medical Board did assess his disability.
However, as the petitioner was discharged from the service in 1994 upon reaching the 28-year service limit, he is deemed ineligible for disability pension, and his petition was dismissed.
