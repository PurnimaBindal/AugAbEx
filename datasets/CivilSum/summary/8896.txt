Respondent AG Masih, AAG Punjab cites Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(c) and Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2 in this case of dismissal from service.
The allegations against the petitioner, made in unanimous complaints, were that he was extorting money from terrorists and the public under the threat of arrest and booking them in terrorist activities.
No reason was recorded for reaching the satisfaction that an enquiry was not possible and the complaints against the petitioner were not looked into seriously.
The acts attributed to the petitioner were found to be unrelated to the spirit and situation envisaged by sub-clause (c) of Article 311 (2) of the Constitution, and the order of dismissal was set aside.
Although the petitioner has since died, his heirs are entitled to all his service benefits consequent to the petition being allowed.
