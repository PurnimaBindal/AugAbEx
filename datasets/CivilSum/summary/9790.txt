Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Equal pay for equal work - Equating of posts and pay scales - The writ court's jurisdiction in equating pay scales - Haryana Govt. will consider and attempt to follow the recommendations of the Central Pay Commission, but it remains the responsibility of the Hy.
Govt. to assess whether a particular set of posts (such as Translators in Vidhan Sabha and Lok Sabha) can be equated - Even though the functions of two posts may seem identical or similar, there may be differences in the degree of performance and work quality - The granting of higher pay scales is influenced by several factors, including qualifications, nature of duties, and working hours - Expert bodies should determine it, and the court should usually accept their judgment regarding the nature of duties and responsibilities of posts.
