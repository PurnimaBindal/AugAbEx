Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rules 4 and 8 -Penalties and procedure followed thereof - The petitioner was accused of failing to exercise proper financial control on a subordinate, resulting in financial loss to the government.
After submitting a reply, the government imposed a penalty of stopping one annual grade increment without cumulative effect.
The petitioner challenged the order, claiming it violated the rules and principles of natural justice.
The government did not objectively consider the petitioner's representations before passing the order, nor did it record a finding that the petitioner's reply was unsatisfactory.
The order did not show the operating reasons for holding the petitioner guilty of the allegation, violating the basic principles of natural justice.
Consequently, the petition was allowed.
