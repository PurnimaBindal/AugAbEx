Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Contract Act 1872 - Waiver - Writ Jurisdiction - Alternative remedy - First respondent was terminated on the basis of absence and subsequently reinstated with back wages in an appeal.
An agreement was made with management, waiving some benefits awarded in the writ petition.
A writ was filed again, but the allegations in the writ give rise to disputed questions of fact that cannot be resolved in a writ application.
The writ petition was dismissed and the respondents are advised to seek an alternative remedy.
