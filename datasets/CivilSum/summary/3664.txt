The respondent was working as Senior Specialist Gynaecology and holding charge of PMO in a big hospital.
However, a transfer was made to accommodate a junior person from a smaller town.
The Single Judge declared it as mala fide, since the authority did not take into consideration that the private respondent's services were more essential in the bigger hospital and it would cause likely hardship and inconvenience to the people at large.
