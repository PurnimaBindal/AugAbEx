Under Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 616(2) pertaining to resignation and pension and other retiral benefits, employees who resign after putting in more than 10 years of service are entitled to the same benefits as those who retire prematurely.
The decisions in "State of Punjab v. Gurkirat Singh" 2002(3) SCT 623 & Ganga Bishan v. State of Haryana and others, 1994(4) SLR 59 have been followed.
