According to the Constitution of India, Article 16 and 226, candidates must meet certain qualifications to be eligible for the position of Constable in CRPF.
However, in one case, the petitioner was found to be .5 cm shorter than the required height of 165 cm, but a conflicting report by another doctor showed a height of 167 cm.
The concerned authorities were directed to measure again and, if the petitioner meets the height requirement, he would be eligible for recruitment pending his fitness in other areas.
