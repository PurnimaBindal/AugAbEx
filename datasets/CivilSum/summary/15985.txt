Constitution of India, Article 226 – Writ of mandamus – Admission – In 1985, the petitioner obtained an interim direction for provisional admission in B.Sc.
Agriculture subject to the judgement of the case – The writ was presented for a final hearing in 1985 – The petition was unopposed, and no reply was filed – Furthermore, the petitioner's counsel was absent – As a result, it is reasonable to assume that the writ is now obsolete and that admission has been completed.
