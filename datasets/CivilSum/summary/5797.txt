Syndicate Bank Officers (Conditions of Service) Rules, 1966, Rule 35(j) - Syndicate Bank Officer Employees' (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976, Regulation 12(a) - Suspension - Disciplinary proceedings - Suspension may only occur if there is an ongoing enquiry and the employee's continued service is not in the best interest of the bank - This does not include pending criminal cases or police investigations - If there is no ongoing enquiry, charging, or suspension order, then a suspension is not valid - Any suspension in violation of these rules is void and unauthorized.

Syndicate Bank Officer Employees' (Discipline Appeal) Regulations, 1976, Regulation 6(7) - Natural justice - Opportunity to defend - Suspension - Legal Practitioner appointed as presenting officer - The delinquent officer must be given a chance to choose whether or not they want to be defended by a lawyer - Failure to consider the petitioner's request for legal representation means the employee is unfairly pitted against a trained lawyer, which violates Regulation 6(7) and principles of natural justice - The entire punishment order is void and flawed.
[Paras 30 to 32]  C. Suspension - Application of mind - Charges against the petitioner are based on instructions that did not exist at the alleged period - The suspension order is therefore made without an application of mind and is invalid.
