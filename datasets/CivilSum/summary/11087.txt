Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Granting higher grade for higher academic qualifications - Delay and laches - The petitioner, who was appointed as a teacher in the Education Department starting on October 1, 1963, made a claim for a higher grade based on her academic qualifications.
She relied on a circular issued by the Government of Punjab on July 23, 1957 and argued that it continued to be applied by the Government of Haryana after November 1, 1966.
The objection of delay and laches was rejected, with the court stating that the respondents cannot take advantage of their own omission.
There was no legal justification to deprive the petitioner of the benefit of a higher grade.
The respondents were directed to grant the petitioner a higher grade, effective from October 1, 1963.
This ruling has been supported by Chaman Lal v. State of Haryana, 1987(4) SLR 4 and Punjab Higher Qualified Teachers Union v. State of Punjab, 1988(1) SLR 768.
