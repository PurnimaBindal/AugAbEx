The case concerns the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16.
The petitioner, who worked for 4 years with notional breaks as Library Assistant in the Panjab University, completed 240 days within a year.
The Court directed that her case be considered for regularisation from the date her services were terminated.
The Court relied on the case of Piara Singh and another v. State of Haryana, 1988(4) SLR 739.
