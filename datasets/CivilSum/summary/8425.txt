Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Back wages - Denial of - The petitioner's order of removal was quashed and the alleged forged ITI certificate was not found to be forged.
Despite this, the claim for backwages after reinstatement was denied.
It is a well-settled principle that payment of backwages is normally granted after reinstatement unless it is proven that the concerned employee was profitably employed during the period of his removal from service.
Denial of backwages cannot be based on mere speculation and conjecture.
