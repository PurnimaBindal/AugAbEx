Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Qualifications - Improving qualifications - In the case of promotion from Lineman to Junior Engineer on acquiring a degree of AMIE which was equivalent to a BE degree, the petitioner cannot claim any benefit of out-of-turn promotion as Junior Engineer, as the policy for giving the benefit of out-of-turn promotions has been withdrawn and the Board is taking steps to recall out-of-turn promotions that were wrongly made.
