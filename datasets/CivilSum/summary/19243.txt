Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 16 - Gradation List - Promotion - The Health Department circulated a Gradation List in January 1977 which showed the petitioners senior to the private respondents.
However, the official respondents later circulated a revised seniority list on June 13, 1977, which was ultimately scrapped on the advice of Legal Remembrancer and private respondents were reverted.
Then, a new Gradation List was issued on January 1, 1979, which showed the petitioners junior to the private respondents, and Shri Sukhminder Singh, Inderjeet Singh, and Sant Lal were later promoted to the post of Assistant Malaria Officers based on this list.
There were no statutory rules for promotions for the relevant time, and the promotion to Assistant Malaria Officer could be made from among the persons holding the posts of Assistant Unit Officers/Senior Laboratory Technicians/Health Supervisors/Investigators.
The contention of the petitioners that promotions to the post of Assistant Malaria Officer should have been made based on seniority alone was deemed meritless.
Petition dismissed.
