Constitution of India, Articles 16, 162 - Promotion - Posts in State Medical Health Department - The general policy of the Government is to follow the rule of seniority.
Deviating from this policy requires prior approval of the personnel department, law department, and cabinet sub-committee.
A government memo introducing merit and ability as criteria for promotion to the posts of Medical Officers and upwards without prior approval violates the general policy of promotion.
The department also relaxed the criteria only when promoting general candidates and prepared a separate list of candidates as per the roster.
The impugned memo is issued to deprive SC/ST candidates of promotion, frustrating the faith and credit accorded in the executive by the Constitution.
