The UP Secondary Education Services Commission and Selection Board Act, 1992, Section 33 - UP Secondary Education Services Commissions (Removal of Difficulties) Orders, 1st and 3rd - Ad hoc appointment - Ad hoc appointments made under the Removal of Difficulties Order - Appointments made for 6 months but allowed to continue beyond the period of 6 months till the regular appointee comes - Ad hoc appointee had the right to continue till the regular appointee joins.
[Paras 26, 27 and 34]

U.P.
Intermediate Education Act, 1921, Section 16-E - Regulation 10 framed under Chapter II - Selection - Appointment - If only one qualified candidate was available before the selection committee, it is not bound to select that very candidate and to recommend his name for appointment - Selection in itself inheres judging of merit and suitability - If the selection committee finds that the candidate although possesses all the requisite qualifications is otherwise not suitable for appointment to the post, it can reject that candidate.
[Paras 30 and 31]

U.P.
Intermediate Education Act, 1921, Section 16-E - Selection - Appointment of teacher - The Regional Deputy Director has no jurisdiction to make appointment on any post specified in the Schedule appended to the Selection Commission Act nor the management can make such appointments without the recommendation of the Commission except in the case of exemption made U/s 16.
U.P.
Intermediate Education Act, 1921, Section 33 - Ad hoc appointment - Natural justice - Ad hoc appointment made under the removal of difficulties order - The appointee cannot be replaced with the regular appointee without hearing and complying with the principles of natural justice.
[Para 34]

U.P.
Intermediate Education Act, Section 16-E - Appointment of teacher/Selection - The District Inspector of Schools or the Regional Deputy Director of Education has no jurisdiction to entertain the representation against selection and appointment of a teacher - The representation lies before the Director.
[Para 33]
