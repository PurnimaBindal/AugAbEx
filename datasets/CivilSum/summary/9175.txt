Prior to the enforcement of Amending Act 21 of 1973, the Punjab Agricultural Produce Markets Act, 1961, Section 20 did not allow for the transfer of class IV employees from one Committee to another.
However, following amendment, any member of service can be transferred anywhere within the State of Haryana.
Therefore, transfers that were previously challenged on the basis of there being no provision in the Act cannot be voided after amendment.
