Constitution of India, Article 311 - Departmental enquiry/Retirement/Retirement benefits - Departmental enquiry after 14 years of retirement - The disciplinary authority cannot proceed with departmental proceedings after an employee retires because they no longer have any control over them - Delaying proceedings with the intention of depriving the retiree of their retirement benefits goes against the principles of social and economic justice protected by the Indian Constitution.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Charge-sheet/Departmental enquiry/Allegations relating to the period prior to the promotion - The charges must have specific material particulars and can't be vague or indefinite - Departmental proceedings cannot be allowed to proceed if there are no clear allegations.
