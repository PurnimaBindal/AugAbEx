The Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Transfer - Issuance of transfer orders - It is established that after an order of transfer has been issued and enforced, no substantive part of it can be stayed or revoked.
[Para 12] B The Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Transfer - Order of transfer impacting seniority - Seniority cannot be permitted to be influenced by transfer without informing and giving the parties a hearing.
