According to MP Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1961, Section 2(2) and MP Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Rules, 1963, Rule 7, the MP Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act will apply to departments unless the government exempts them.
The Rules and orders issued under the Act would be applicable to and govern the P.W.D.
of the State, unless the government issues directions to the contrary.
