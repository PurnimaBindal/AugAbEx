Jammu and Kashmir Persons with Disabilities Act, 1988, Sections 2(d)(v), (j) & (p), 21 and 22 - Jammu and & Kashmir Reservation Rules, Rule 8 - Reservation for handicapped - If a candidate participates in the handicapped category and appears physically before the selection committee with a visible handicap, the non-production of a fresh certificate cannot be a reason for ignoring them.
Handicap is an eligibility qualification, not acquired like educational qualifications.
The state is responsible for identifying posts and making recruitment of handicapped individuals as per the rules.
