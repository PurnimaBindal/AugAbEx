Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1958, Rule 9(a)(iii) - Termination - Provisional appointment for 179 days - An argument was made that the service of seniors to the appellant should be terminated first based on Rule 9(a)(iii) - It was determined that Rule 9(a)(iii) pertains to the termination of provisional appointees during their appointment period - Seniority under the proviso to Rule 9(a)(iii) can only be considered during this time - As the appellants have served beyond 179 days, there is no application of the proviso to Rule 9(a)(iii).
