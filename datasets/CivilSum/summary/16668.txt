Rajasthan Services Rules, 1951, Rule 179 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1996, Rule 12(b) - Fixation of pay, promotion, selection grades, pensionary benefits - The Petitioner's qualifying service shall commence from the date he joined duties on a substantive basis in 1977, as his prior service under different Panchayats on ad hoc or contract basis was not in an officiating capacity - The expression 'qualifying service' should not be confused with 'years of service' - Consequently, the Petition is dismissed.
