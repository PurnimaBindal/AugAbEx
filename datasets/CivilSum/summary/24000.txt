Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 33-C(2) and 25-F & 25-FFF - Closure of Industry - Retrenchment compensation - Workmen are entitled to compensation in case of closing down - Taking over by a corporation cannot deprive workers of compensation - If U/s 33-C(2) application made, compensation calculated as per Section 25-F - If closing down due to financial difficulties, compensation calculated U/s 25-FFF.
