Constitution of India, Article 14 - Recommendations of Departmental Promotion Committee - The Appointments Committee of Cabinet is not obligated to follow the recommendations of the Departmental Promotion Committee, but they cannot ignore or reject them without giving reasons.
These reasons do not have to be communicated to the affected individuals, but they must be included in the file and provided to the court upon request.
If recommendations are rejected, the Appointments Committee must reconsider them in collaboration with the Union Public Service Commission and provide reasons for any deviation from the recommendation.
[Paras 14, 18, 19, 20, 21, and 24]

Administrative Tribunal Act, 1985, Section 19 - Appointment - Promotion - Deemed promotion - Jurisdiction of the Tribunal - In cases where the Appointing authority rejects the recommendations of the DPC without giving reasons, the administrative tribunal can only order a reconsideration by the Appointing Authority and require that the Appointing Authority record the promotion, even if deemed promotion in itself.
The order of the tribunal is set aside.
[Paras 14 and 21]
