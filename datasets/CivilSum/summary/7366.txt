Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F and 10 - Scope of interference in writ jurisdiction in the Labour Court award - An order of Reinstatement with full back wages and adequate and substantive reasons based on evidence on record is necessary if termination of services of a workman after completion of 240 days service is done without compliance of provisions of Section 25F There is no room for interference in writ jurisdiction.
