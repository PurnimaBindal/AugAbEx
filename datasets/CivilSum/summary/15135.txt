Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8 - Promotion - Misconduct - Reversion - On a provisional basis until approval of PPSC, the appellant was promoted on 14591 from the post of Senior Technical Officer Class II to Senior Technical Officer Class I for six months - However, due to his misbehaviour with an industrialist, the provisional promotion was not extended beyond 11.7.93 - An inquiry was initiated and charge-sheet was issued on account of his misconduct - After the appellant reverted to a Class II post, the government decided not to proceed with an inquiry as he had already suffered punishment by not being extended period of his ad hoc promotion to the post of Senior Technical Officer Class I beyond 12.7.93 - The appellant is entitled to receive revised pensionary benefit since he retired on Class II Post.
