According to the Border Security Force Act, 1968, Section 40 and Border Security Force Rules, Rule 157, failure to report to unit on time can result in a punishment of 7 days RI in force custody.
If the accused refuses to accept food and perform pack drill during his sentence, this can be considered a violation.
The petitioner was given a copy of the charge-sheet, list of witnesses, and record of evidence with exhibits but failed to nominate a friend of the accused or forward any request to engage services of civil counsel.
The provisions of the Act and Rules were not violated.
As a member of a disciplined force required to maintain discipline and obedience to any punishment imposed, the punishment of dismissal from services was neither excessive nor disproportionate to the offence committed.
