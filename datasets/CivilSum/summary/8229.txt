Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - Challenge to government servant transfers due to political pressure - Court suggests that government set specific period for postings to ensure stability - If transfer is believed to be politically motivated, civil servant can make representation to Chief Secretary to State Government who will decide within six weeks - State Government must also establish a Board similar to the Civil Services Board for Class I officer transfers and postings.
