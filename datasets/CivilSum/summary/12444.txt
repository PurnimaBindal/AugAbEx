Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 311 - Removal - Departmental Enquiry - Petitioner removed from service due to possession of employer's property without following proper charge framing procedure - Procedure adopted by disciplinary authority violated principles of natural justice and fairness - Enquiry Officers and disciplinary authorities should act judiciously - In this case, disciplinary authority failed to evaluate evidence and only paraphrased witness statements of petitioner - Order quashed and petitioner entitled to all benefits.
