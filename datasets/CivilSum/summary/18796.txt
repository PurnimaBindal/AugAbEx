According to the Constitution of India, Article 14 and Central Govt. Scheme 51 of 1980, Family Pension is available for those who were in service as on 111964, including the Service element and Disability element.
However, the widow of a disability pensioner whose pension was stopped in 1951 and who died in 1990 without challenging the stoppage of disability pension or claiming pension on the basis of service element, is not entitled to the family pension on the basis of either disability element or service element.
