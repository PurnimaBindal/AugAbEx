According to Section 2(9) of the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, if a principal employer hires a contractor to perform a job and the contractor hires independent workers to complete the job, the principal employer has the power to reject the end product created by the contractor.
However, this mere right of acceptance or rejection does not amount to supervision under Section 2(9), and as a result, the principal employer is not liable to pay contributions for the employees engaged by the contractor.
