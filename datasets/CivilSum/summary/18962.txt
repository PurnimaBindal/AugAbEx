Granting current-duty charge of a higher post to a junior officer and bypassing their seniors violates the equality doctrine under Articles 14 and 16 in the Constitution of India.
Giving juniors the opportunity to hold such positions for long periods is not justified if it's not meant to overcome a crisis.
The orders to give current-duty charge to juniors are unjustified, and seniors/petitioners should receive the same even if it needs to be removed from juniors due to the shortage of posts.
