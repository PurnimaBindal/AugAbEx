According to Sections 10 and 11 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the management must establish the ground of the workman's employment from the date of termination until reinstatement for back wages to be denied.
The workman's admission that his family supported him was not sufficient to decline back wages, and heresy evidence cannot be used.
The management did not challenge the reinstatement, and no serious allegations were made against the workman, so the award was modified to grant full back wages until the date of reinstatement.
