Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Benefit of two increments - Employees who have reached the maximum of their pay scale and also crossed point 21 or above before 111991 were only allowed one additional increment on 111991 - Petitioner in the pay scale of 1400-2600 was receiving the maximum pay of the pay scale i.e. Rs. 2,600/- on 111991 with the next increment being granted on 111992 by the Finance Department with a pay increase of Rs. 2,600/- to Rs. 2,660/- - the benefit of an additional increment would only be given in the scale and not beyond the maximum point of the scale - the petitioner cannot be granted the benefit of two increments.
