Under Rule 80-C of the Punjab Co-operative Societies Rules, 1963, delegation of powers by the Chairman with the prior approval of the Registrar does not apply to further sub-delegation.
The delegatee has no power or jurisdiction to further delegate such powers.
Any action taken by the Managing Director by exercising such powers will be wholly illegal and void.
Punishment imposed by the MD in exercise of such power cannot be sustained.
