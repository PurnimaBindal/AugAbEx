Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India are relevant to Punjab Service of Engineers Class-I Public Works Department, including the Irrigation, Public Health and Buildings and Roads branches.
A constitutional amendment made to the Irrigation Branch Rules to allow promotion to Class-I was declared ultra vires by the Supreme Court in TR Kapur v. State of Haryana, 1986(4) SLR 155, due to it being violative of Articles 14 and 16.
The amendment was also found to be invalid due to non-compliance with Section 82(6) of the Punjab Re-organization Act, 1966 because it changed conditions of service for diploma holders to their disadvantage without approval from the Central Government.
[Para 4] B.
A writ petition filed by degree-holder A.Es.
against the promotion of diploma-holding respondents to the post of Xens challenged the amendment made to the Irrigation Branch Rules in 1984, which made a degree in engineering an essential requirement for promotion to the post.
The rules were found to be invalid only retrospectively on the ground of violation of Articles 14 and 16, not prospectively.
However, their infirmity under Section 82(6) of the Punjab Re-organization Act, 1966 still remains.
As a result, the petitioners cannot derive any benefit from the amendment.
