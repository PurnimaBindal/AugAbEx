The Constitution of India, Articles 16, 132, addresses equal pay for equal work.
The respondent, a chemist in the Inspectorate of Factories, claimed pay equal to that of a chemist in the Department of Industries, citing said doctrine.
The Tribunal was directed to grant pay scale fixed by the Industries Department for the post of Chemist.
The two posts are separate cadres with different requirements and salaries, and the Tribunal's finding that the posts were similar was unsubstantiated.
While both posts had similar responsibilities, they were not identical.
The Tribunal's order was unjustified, and until it is shown that two posts are almost identical, relief should not be awarded.
The impugned order passed by the Tribunal has been set aside.
