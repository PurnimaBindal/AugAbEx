All India Institute of Medical Sciences Act, 1956, Section 4(e) - Composition of the Institute - Clarification on the composition of members specified in Section 4 of the Act with regards to the category of five persons listed in clause (e) - The Central Government is justified in nominating four persons from categories other than scientists and the fifth being a non-medical scientist representing the Indian Science Congress Association.

All India Institute of Medical Sciences Act, 1956, Sections 6(2) and 4(e) - Object of the Act is to promote high standards in medical specialities and treatment - The Chairman of the supreme body must be nominated by the Central Government, and in case of the Minister holding the position, they will continue until they hold office, after which the incumbent will be nominated for the residue period - The Central Government is empowered to nominate five persons under Section 4(e), who will be members of the supreme body of the AIIMS.
