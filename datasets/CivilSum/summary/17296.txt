Kerala Service Rules, Part III Rules 116(3) and 116(5) - Recovery from gratuity - Liability caused by a Govt. servant - Interest on dues of retiral benefits and on amounts recoverable - The retiree shall receive 8% interest if retiral benefits are withheld illegally, and a 12% liability on the due money.
- Higher interest rates apply when public money is involved.
