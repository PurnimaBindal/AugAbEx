The High Court Establishment (Appointment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1973, along with Articles 16 and 136 of the Constitution of India, were originally released on January 23, 1975.
However, an amendment was made on August 5, 1980, which went into effect on that same date and not retroactively to the original release date.
Consequently, the pay scale benefit cannot be given to Revisors before August 5, 1980.
