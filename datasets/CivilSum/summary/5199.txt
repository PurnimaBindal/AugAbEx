According to Constitution of India, Article 226 and Army Rules, 1954, Rule 13(3), a petitioner who is discharged from service due to lower medical category but retired as a Havaldar is entitled to pension and other benefits as there was no evidence of any proceeding to reduce the petitioner to the rank of Naik.
Moreover, the petitioner was not given an opportunity before being relinquished from his substantive appointment as Havaldar.
No procedures under Army Act, 1950 and Army Rules were followed for the reduction in rank.
[Paras 10 and 13]

It is stated in the Constitution of India, Article 226 and Army Act, 1950 that Army Personnel can be subjected to actions taken under Army Act, 1954 at any place where they are serving, even if they do not have easy access to jurisdiction.
It is also held that the Chief of Army Staff can be sued anywhere in the country.
[Para 11]
