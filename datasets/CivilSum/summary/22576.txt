Constitution of India, Article 226 - Freedom Fighters Pension Scheme (1972) - Pension - Grant of pension - Petitioners sought pension under Swatantra Sainik Samman Pension Scheme - Applicants must prove their sufferings of the type and period recognised by the scheme - Standard of proof required not equivalent to criminal cases 'beyond reasonable doubt' - If applicant's imprisonment for the nation's freedom cause is probable based on evidence, minor discrepancies and contradictions are not crucial to denying the pension - Denying claim due to hyper-technical reasons is deemed inappropriate.
