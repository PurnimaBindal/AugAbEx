Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Dismissal/Disciplinary proceedings/Reinstatement/ - Back wages - The order of dismissal was challenged on the ground that it was not issued by the Disciplinary authority rather by the appellate authority - The disciplinary proceedings originated from common proceedings which is against the circular issued by the respondent - The punishment awarded to the petitioner is disproportionate to the offence alleged - Circular 9 clearly indicates that common proceedings should be used as a last resort - Respondent was unable to explain why common proceedings were initiated when separate charge sheets were served and separate causes of action were alleged - It was not established how the petitioner was found guilty - Reasons were neither discussed nor proved - There was a non-application of mind on the part of the enquiry officers and the Managing Director who stepped into the shoes of the Disciplinary authority - Conducting a common enquiry and imposing different punishments were deemed bad in law.
