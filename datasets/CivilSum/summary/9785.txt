Under the Orissa Aided Educational Institutions (Appointment of Lecturers Validation) Act, 1998, Section 3(2) and other related laws, lecturers in approved posts are entitled to the benefits of the grant in aid, including arrears of salary from the date when the Validation Act came into force.
The petitioners were appointed against such posts and had their appointments validated by the authorities.
Despite the release of grant in aid to several lecturers, the petitioners claimed discrimination as they were not afforded the same benefit.
The college where they were appointed is an A-1 category non-government educational institution and is receiving government grant in aid.
