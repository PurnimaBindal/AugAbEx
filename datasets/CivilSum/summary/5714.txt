Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 and 311 - Judicial review - Compulsory retirement - Public Interest - Adverse remarks - Petitioner was forced to retire by the Managing Director of UP Jal Nigam, but the court found that the decision was arbitrary because the petitioner had an overall unblemished record and there was no material justifying the decision in the public interest - The court has the power to review orders and found the impugned order to be perverse and arbitrary, quashed - The petitioner is treated to have continued in service notionally until the date of his superannuation and the respondents must pay his emoluments in accordance with law from the date of impugned order until his date of superannuation as per departmental rules.
