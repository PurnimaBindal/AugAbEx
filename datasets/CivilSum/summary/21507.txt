Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Setting aside of selection - Natural justice - Setting aside of selection on the ground of irregularities - The decision of quashing the selection, either as a whole or of individuals, after notice to each affected person, depends on the particular facts of the case.
It is necessary to scrutinize the nature and extent of the illegalities and irregularities committed during the selection process to determine the appropriate course of action.
If the illegalities and irregularities are not widespread and pervasive, affecting the result, making it difficult to identify those benefitting unlawfully or wrongly deprived of selection, it may not be necessary to issue individual show cause notices to each selectee.
In such cases, the only alternative may be to cancel the entire selection.
The intention behind the irregularities committed is also relevant.
[Paras 8 and 12]
