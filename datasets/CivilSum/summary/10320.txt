The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-N and 25-F prescribe the procedure for retrenchment in establishments with more than 100 workers.
If the provisions of Section 25-N are not followed, the retrenched workman is entitled to reinstatement with full back wages.
[Paras 7 to 10]
According to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A, a workman whose retrenchment is found to be in violation of Sections 25-F and 25-N is entitled to reinstatement with full back wages, provided he was not gainfully employed during the termination period.
[Para 10]
