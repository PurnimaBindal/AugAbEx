According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, the Rules do not provide for revaluation of answer sheets.
The matter of providing revaluation in the Rules is a matter of academic policy and is not subject to court intervention.
Due to the large number of students taking the exam, the frequency of the exam, the number of failed students, and the potential consequences of allowing revaluation, it cannot be considered arbitrary or unreasonable to not permit revaluation at present.
