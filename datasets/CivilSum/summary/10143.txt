Medical Reimbursement - Rejection of - Claim that petitioner's wife was transferred at his request and that the hospital where treatment was obtained is not reputed - Concluded that the petitioner's plea cannot be regarded as an irregularity that impedes the processing of medical reimbursement that is otherwise legitimate and valid - The petitioner was ordered to have the matter reconsidered on a humanitarian basis, taking into account the relevant regulations, rules, as well as the High Court's judgment in CWP No. 423 of 2002.
