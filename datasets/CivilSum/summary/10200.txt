Sections 10 and 12(5) of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947 are referenced in the following.
It is held that the government should be cautious when attempting to decline a reference of dispute with a view to examine the demands.
In the given situation, termination was held valid by the Labor Commissioner which led to the withholding of the reference.
It is recommended that the dispute should have been referred to the Labor Court for adjudication on merits.
