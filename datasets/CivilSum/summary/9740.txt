Constitution of India, Articles 16, 39 & 226 - Regularisation - Daily Wage Employees - The engagement of the petitioner for a long period by paying a petty amount is in violation of Article 39 of the Indian Constitution - It cannot be said that there is no work when the petitioners are engaged for a long period and they were hired to attend seasonally - The petitioners have the right to regularisation of their services in the positions they currently hold.
