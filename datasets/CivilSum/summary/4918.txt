Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2, Item 3 - Back wages - Review - Writ Appeal filed alleging claim for back wages rejected by High Court - Although appellate court dismissed the appeal and allowed petitioner to approach appointing authority, it cannot delegate power of review - No statutory powers - Once court decides against back wages, no opposing view is permissible.
