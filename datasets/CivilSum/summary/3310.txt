Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Demobilised Armed Forces Personnel (Reservation of Vacancies in the Punjab State Non-Technical Services) Rules, 1968 - Military Service/Benefits of Military Service/Seniority/Civil Post/Emergency Period - Benefits of military service on a Civil Post may only be granted for the service rendered during the proclaimed emergency period, rather than for the entire service period.
