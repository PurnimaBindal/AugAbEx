Constitution of India, Article 16 - Casual Labourers (Grant of Temprorary Status and Regularisation) Scheme of Govt. of India, 1993, Clause 4 - Temporary status to casual labourers - To qualify for temporary status, casual labourers must have been employed on the commencement date of the scheme and must have worked for a minimum of 240 days/206 days in a year.
