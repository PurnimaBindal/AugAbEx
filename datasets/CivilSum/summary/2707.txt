Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Disobeyance of instructions - The petitioner was accused of disobeying the order of the Branch Manager by refusing to let a non-regular employee carry cash from the vault.
However, the instruction of the Branch Manager was not proper in light of circulars from the Head Office and Zonal Office of the Bank.
The single Judge correctly determined that no misconduct was committed by the petitioner, and this decision has been upheld.
