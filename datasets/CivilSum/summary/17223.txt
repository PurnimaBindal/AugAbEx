Constitution of India, Article 311 - Orissa Service (Rehabilitation Assistance) Rules, 1990, Rules 8(1)(b)(d) and 9(5) - Rehabilitation appointment - The petitioner's father had rendered 29 years of service, and due to incapacitation, he opted for compulsory retirement, thus having five years and ten months of service left to credit - The application for rehabilitation appointment for his son was rejected without following the procedure laid down under the rules - The plea that the son did not have five years of service by the date on which the application was made was given for rejecting the second application - It was held that it was not proper - The second application should be considered even if the employee did not have 5 years of service.
