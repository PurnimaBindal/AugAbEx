According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, 21, and 300A, along with the Railway Pension Rules and Railway Service (Pension) Rules, pensionary rights for family pensions are not dependent on regularisation.
In fact, even a casual temporary employee can be eligible for this benefit, as shown by the case of a widow of a casual labourer with temporary status who is entitled to a family pension.
