Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(s), 25B and 25F - "Workman" - Benefit of continuous service - Part time employee - Working on ad hoc basis - Workman employed by the appellant employer - The definition of "workman" under Section 2(s) of the Act includes part time workman - Thus, part time workman would be entitled to the benefit of continuous service as per Section 25B and 25F [Paras 8 to 16]
