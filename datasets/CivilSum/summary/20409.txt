Karnataka Civil Services (General Recruitment) Rules, 1977, Rule 8 - Reservations - Rota and quota system - Retrospective rules - Rules allowing promotional quota for SC/STs with immediate effect - Tribunal rules the retrospective application of the quota illegitimate and non-sustainable.
[Paras 4, 5 and 6]
Karnataka Civil Services (General Recruitment) Rules, 1977, Rule 8 - Reservation - Guidelines - Backlog vacancies - Rules providing reservation quota with immediate effect - Guidelines for identifying backlog vacancies and placement of officers not developed under Article 16(4) by the Govt. - Rule cannot be invalidated solely because guidelines are not available - Govt. instructed to create guidelines under three months and give appropriate promotion to officers - Promotions granted under the reserved quota to remain till the guidelines have been created.
