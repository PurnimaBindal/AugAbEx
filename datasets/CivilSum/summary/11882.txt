Petitioner accepted a condition of migration that requires him to attend classes for 18 months and appear in the 1st Professional of MBBS Examination, as stated in an affidavit he filed.
The petitioner cannot deny the validity of this condition and receive the benefits of the transaction without accepting its disadvantage.
The University may consider his representation sympathetically, but the court cannot grant relief solely because he is a brilliant student and wishes to avoid the condition of migration.
[Paras 6, 7 and 8]
