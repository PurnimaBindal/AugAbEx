The meaning of "capitation fee" is defined by Section 2(a) of the Maharashtra Educational Institutions (Prohibition of Capitation Fee) Act, 1987.
It refers to any amount, in cash or kind, exceeding the approved or prescribed rates of fees given in Section 4.
In the case of an unaided school under the management of a religious minority where the government does not regulate fees, the institution will not commit an offense even if it charges higher fees than the government-regulated rates.
The state cannot restrict the rights of religious minorities to run education institutions without aid from the government.
However, regulations can be made to ensure the quality of education provided.
Although the state can pass laws to prevent schools from misusing school administration for earning large profits by collecting unreasonable sums from parents in the name of fees or donations, religious minority unaided educational institutions are also protected under Article 30(1) of the Constitution of India.
