According to Section 2(ra) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, it is an unfair labour practice to deny regular pay scales to workmen who have not been regularised yet.
Only after being regularised can they claim regular pay scales at par with regular employees.
[Para 6]
If casual labourers have been employed for a moderately long period of about two to three years, it can be presumed that their services are required regularly.
The respondents have been asked to regularise the services of such workmen within three months.
[Para 7]
If workmen do not receive regular scales within three months after an award has been published, they can seek the remedy under Section 33C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
