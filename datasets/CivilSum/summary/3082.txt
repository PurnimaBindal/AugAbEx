Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Mark-sheet - Mistake - Rectification of - The university corrected its mistake in the petitioner's mark sheet for paper No. 11, changing the originally awarded 90 marks to 37 marks without informing the petitioner of the reason for the reduction or offering a chance for re-evaluation - This deprives the petitioner of the opportunity to exercise their right to seek re-evaluation and is a violation of natural justice - It is held that the petitioner must be given the chance to apply for re-evaluation.
[Paras 42-45]
