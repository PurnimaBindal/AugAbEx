Constitution of India, Article 309 - Rajasthan Employment of the Physically Handicapped Rules, 1976, Rules 2(iv), 14 - Premature Retirement on recommendation of Medical Board due to 100% blindness suffered during service - Claim of family pension by wife of petitioner - Husband of petitioner not adjusted on any other job as per Rule 14 - Service period of 2 years 10 months - Petitioner not receiving family pension due to omission of respondents to adjust husband under mandatory provisions of Rule 14 of 1976 Rules - Court rules that petitioner's husband should have been treated in service and his death considered as occurring while in service - Court directs the granting of pension from the date of death of petitioner's husband.
