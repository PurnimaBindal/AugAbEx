Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Act, 1994, Section 19(1) - Constitution of India, Articles 21, 25, 26 - Disqualification for contesting Panchayat election of a person having three children - Petitioner has one child from first wife, two from second and none from third - First two wives of petitioner are dead - Contention that having more than two children from different wifes cannot be disqualification - Having more than two children at the time of consideration of candidature for election will be considered - Explanation to Section 19 is not applicable to this case - Statutory limitations can impose restrictions on created rights.
