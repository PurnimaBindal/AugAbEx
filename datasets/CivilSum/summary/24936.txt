Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10, 18 - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 22, Rule 10 - Impleadment of a party in an Industrial Dispute - Parties cannot reopen proceedings that have already taken place as a matter of course after acquiring the rights and liabilities of another party - The impleaded party will generally be bound by the steps that were previously taken in the proceedings and will not be allowed to recall or cross-examine witnesses, except in the case of exceptional circumstances.
