According to the Government Residences (Chandigarh Administration Pool) Allotment Rules of 1996, there is no provision for retaining allotment by family members in service after the retirement of the original allottee.
Also, any applications made before the enforcement of the new rules should be considered under the new rules and not the old ones.
Mere application under old rules wouldn't guarantee the right to be considered.
Any orders from the Tribunal to consider applications under old rules are quashed.
[Paras 5 and 6]
Any allotment of government accommodation without following the rules is considered void and cannot be the basis for issuing a mandamus or direction under Article 226 of the Constitution to direct authorities to act in violation of the statute.
The High Court can enforce constitutional or legal rights but doesn't have the power to issue a writ for authorities to act against statutory provisions.
