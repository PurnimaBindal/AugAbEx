Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 233 - Pay - Chief Justice of High Court recommends to State Government to provide personal assistants and judgment writers attached to the Judges with the same pay as reporters in the Legislative Assembly, including the special pay of Rs. 100 per month.
A direction was given to implement the payment of the special pay of Rs. 100 only from 141984.
