Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission/Education - Entrance test - Merit - Qualifying exam - Holding an entrance test is the optimal way to evaluate the relative merit of students seeking admission to professional courses.
Petitioners seeking admission to a vacant payment seat are ordered to hold the test within a short time frame, and a slight delay in admission due to exam scheduling will not be critical.
However, the request for admission based on merit from a qualifying exam has been turned down.
[Paras 1 to 5]
