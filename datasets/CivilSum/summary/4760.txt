According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 16 and 226, appointments must be regulated.
In this case, the petitioners were appointed as ladies security searchers through an agency to work at the International Airport for Air India security.
However, when the agency was later covered into a private limited company, the petitioners wanted to be considered employees of their employer.
When Air India advertised for direct recruitment of security searchers, the petitioners applied for the positions, but were disqualified for being over-age.
The petitioners then filed a petition to claim regularisation, not against their employer (SS (P) Ltd.) but against Air India.
Ultimately, it was held that the High Court cannot issue a mandamus to deem contract labor as having become employees of the principal employer, and thus the petition was dismissed.
This follows JT 1991(4) SC 413.
