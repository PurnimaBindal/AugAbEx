Constitution of India, Article 14 - Family Pension Scheme of 1964 - Family pension - Interest on arrears of pension - The employer must release the family pension due to the widow of the deceased employee in accordance with the Rules, even if the employee does not specifically request it - A delay in releasing the pension cannot be used to deny the beneficiary the benefits she is entitled to under the Rules - However, interest on arrears may be denied as the only consequence of such a delay.
