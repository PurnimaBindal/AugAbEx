Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Education/Admission - The petitioner applied for admission in MD Pediatrics as the first preference and D.C.H.
course as the second preference.
The admissions were made according to the admission notice and strictly based on merit.
Although the petitioner claims admission in his first preference with the basis of reserved quota of scheduled castes candidates, he is rightly given admission in his second preference as he has no right to claim over candidates with higher merit.
[Para 9]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education/Admission/Concealment of facts - The petitioner was given provisional admission through an interim order, but he concealed some facts to obtain the order.
Upon discovering the true facts, the petitioner refused any relief.
Hence, provisional admission cannot be allowed to continue as it depreciates the practice of withholding facts before the court of law.
[Para 11]
