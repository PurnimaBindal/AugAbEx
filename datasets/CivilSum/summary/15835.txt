Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(b) - Dismissal from service - Dispensing with the enquiry - The punishing authority provided sufficient reasoning for their decision to dismiss the petitioner from service without conducting an enquiry, as they had material that showed the petitioner had links to terrorists and anti-national elements and was passing on police information to such groups despite being transferred and warned.
Reports from different officers also showed that the petitioner was incorrigible and had failed to improve despite repeated warnings.
The disciplinary authority had no choice but to resort to the extraordinary provision of law to dismiss the petitioner from service.
The petitioner cannot claim that there was insufficient material for the disciplinary authority to arrive at their decision and that they relied only on the ipsedixit of a Station House Officer.
