Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation/Education/Admissions - Reservation in admissions for physically handicapped persons - There is a 1% reservation for handicapped individuals and seats are divided equally among the disciplines.
However, no seat has been given to a handicapped person, which is a violation of Article 16 of the Constitution and the reservation policy.
Therefore, at least one seat should be reserved for a handicapped person in any of the disciplines.
