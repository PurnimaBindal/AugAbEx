Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Reversion - Petitioners promoted subject to verification of their certificates - Petitioners reverted on the basis that their passed examination was not recognised by the corporation.
The court determined that though the petitioners had put in 20 years of service on promoted posts, the examination passed for promotion was not recognised by the corporation, making them ineligible for promotion.
The court also recognized that the experience the petitioners gained during their years of service made them qualified for the promoted positions, despite any educational qualification shortfall at the time of promotion.
As a result, the petitioners were permitted to continue on their promotional posts.
Petition allowed.
