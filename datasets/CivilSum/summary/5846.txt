The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ jurisdiction - High Court not to interfere in the award of a Labour Court if there was no legal error, non-consideration of relevant material, or any unreasonable inference drawn in its findings of facts.
[Para 1] B The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 11 - Punishment/Minor misconduct - A conductor of a bus who does not issue tickets before the bus starts has committed a technical lapse, and the punishment of stoppage of two increments with cumulative effect is not lesser considering the facts of the case.
Thus, there should be no interference in writ jurisdiction.
[Para 1] C Back wages - Award of Labour Court - An employer must prove the re-employment of a workman to hold that the award of granting back wages was unreasonable.
