Haryana Health Directorate, Technical and Miscellaneous (Group-C) Service Rules, 1989 - Pay scale - Qualification - Carpenter post - Only those who possess qualification of Matric with ITI - Polythenic Certificate are granted Scale of pay of Rs. 1,200-2040 - Petitioner who does not possess academic qualification of Matric may be denied scale of Rs. 1200- 2040 - Not a violation of Articles 14 and 16 of Constitution.
[Paras 5 and 7]  B Academic qualifications - Equal pay for equal work is not absolute - Differential treatment on basis of academic qualifications, nature of duties, or other factors permissible - Persons possessing higher qualifications which have reasonable relationship with post's requirements may be given higher pay than those who do not fulfil such qualifications.
[Para 5]  C Academic qualifications - Equal pay for equal work - Doctors, Engineers, Technicians perform technical duties - Academic qualifications have reasonable nexus with duty performance - Relationship between academic qualifications and post duties should be decided by experts - Decision that minimum academic qualification of Matric for Carpenter post entitles holder to higher pay may not be questioned.
