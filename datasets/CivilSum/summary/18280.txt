Constitution of India, Article 226 - Reversion - The Petitioner was promoted from the post of Assistant to Section Officer.
However, the Petitioner was later reverted as four persons senior to the Petitioner in the cadre of Assistants acquired eligibility for promotion after the Petitioner's ad hoc promotion.
It was held that the order was not sustainable as the Petitioner attained eligibility for promotion much before the other Assistants.
The principle of "last come first go" should have been adopted.
As a result, the petition was allowed.
