The Constitution of India's articles 16, 48(5) and 309 and Central Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1997, Rule 14 are involved in the upgradation of pay scale for Senior Investigators working in Central Statistical Organisation.
This is recommended by 5th CPC, and the contention of the Respondents is that 5th CPC made no recommendation for the upgradation of pay scale of the petitioners and that creation of the Subordinate Statistical Service is a pre-condition for the grant of higher pay scale of Rs. 2000-3500 (replacement scale of Rs. 6,500- 10,500) for Senior Investigators.
However, the report of 5th CPC in para 8117 contains nothing regarding the condition of creating a Subordinate Statistical Service for higher pay scale.
Notification dated 30997 prescribes a pay scale of Rs. 6,500-200-10500 for Senior Investigators, and therefore, the petitions are allowed and the pay scale is upgraded.
