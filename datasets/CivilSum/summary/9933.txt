Delhi Road Corporation Act, 1952, Section 12 - Disciplinary inquiry - Competent Authority - Petitioners, who were Assistant Fitters and Bus Conductors appointed by the General Manager, were issued a charge sheet by the Depot Manager they were attached to.
Disciplinary inquiry revealed that they had committed misconduct, and were each served with a show cause notice from the Depot Manager, containing the possibility of being dismissed.
The notices were found to be valid and legal, and it is open to the petitioners to contest the punishment in their response.
Writ petition is premature and liable to be dismissed.
