Indian Succession Act, Section 372 - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 1 Rule 10 - Dowry Prohibition Act - Hindu Succession Act, Sections 14, 25 - General Provident Fund (Central Services) Rules, 1960 - Payment of Gratuity Rules, Rules 51, 52, 53 - Succession certificate in respect of deceased daughter-in-law - Claim of provident fund, Gratuity and Group Insurance - Claim by parents - Husband and wife both deceased without issue - Allegations of husband torturing wife and causing her to commit suicide - Trial Court held property to devolve on husband's relatives as per Hindu Succession Act, but Rules governing PF, Gratuity, and GIS determine surviving family members (the petitioners) as entitled to receive the benefits.
