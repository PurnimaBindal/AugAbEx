The Bihar Civil Court Staff (Class III and Class IV) Rules, 1992, Rule 7 require advertisements to be issued in two daily newspapers in addition to notices for Class IV posts in Civil Court appointments.
Despite High Court directives, no advertisements were issued and appointments were made without holding any interviews or consulting a Selection Committee.
The appointments were made under Rules 73 and 77, which were constitutionally struck down in other cases.
Consequently, appointments made in violation of norms and instructions of High Court were not made bonafide and were rightly set aside.
[Paras 11 and 13]
Even if the appointees have been working for 15 years, allowing them to continue in their positions would condone a gross irregularity in their initial appointment.
Furthermore, since the High Court allowed them to participate in a fresh selection procedure and granted age relaxation, there is no basis for the plea of continuation.
[Para 13]
