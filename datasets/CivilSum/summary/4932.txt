The validity of an amendment to Service Rules providing that direct recruit Assistant in District Revenue Establishment without a degree could be promoted over promotee Assistants was upheld as it does not violate Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309.
[Para 22]   B.
An amendment to Service Rules under Article 309 of COI that allowed for the consideration of qualified direct recruit Assistant in District Revenue Establishment for inclusion in Deputy Tehsildar list and placement above promotee Assistants with retrospective effect from 1978 was deemed valid.
