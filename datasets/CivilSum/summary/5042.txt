Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F - Retrenchment compensation - Contract of service - Unfair labour practice - Non-renewal of contract - Employers must prove that the case falls within the exception of Section 2(oo)(bb) when a workman has completed more than 240 days of work in a 12 month period before termination and there is no further available work or post for which the contract could be renewed.
Otherwise, the non-renewal of contract would be considered an unfair labour practice under Clauses 5(b) and 10 of Vth Schedule to the Act.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F - Back wages - Length of service - Retrenchment declared illegal on technical ground - Short-term contract of service - After 20 years of litigation, back wages will not be granted to a short-term employee for such a long period, but he must have remained employed somewhere during that period.
The order is for reinstatement without back wages and other benefits of length of service for the purpose of regularisation, considering policies that were in place during the employee's appointment and reinstatement.
