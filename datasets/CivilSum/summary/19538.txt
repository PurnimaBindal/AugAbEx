Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Municipal Service (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1975, Rule 10 - Probation/Confirmation - If probation period is extendable, there will not be an automatic confirmation.
The competent authority must pass an order of confirmation upon completion of the probation period unless the rules or appointment order specify specific provisions of automatic confirmation.
However, any order of extension or discharge must be passed within a reasonable time.
If the delay in passing such an order is adequately explained, it cannot be quashed on that ground.
In this case, the petitioner's latest confidential reports regarding his work and conduct during probation were received late, and his work and conduct were found unsatisfactory.
The petitioner was informed that his case was under consideration, and no automatic confirmation was granted.
