Article 226 of the Constitution of India discusses education policy and grace marks in examinations.
It is important to maintain academic standards set by appropriate authorities when dealing with education matters.
Grace marks should only be awarded as a concession to overcome candidate hardship and cannot be used to declare unsuccessful candidates as successful.
The provisions of regulations must be strictly adhered to and the court should be cautious when extending the concession of grace marks.
[Paras 6 & 13]  B Regulation 52(1) & (2) of the Maharashtra State Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Regulations specify minimum pass marks in each subject.
A candidate must obtain at least 52 marks in each subject of Mathematics and Science, and the clubbing of grace marks is limited.
The automatic condonation provision allows for only three grace marks in Mathematics, and additional benefit for passing in remaining compulsory subjects is limited to 20 grace marks in any one subject and three subjects overall.
