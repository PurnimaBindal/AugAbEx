In accordance with the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A, in cases of minor misconduct where the Labour Court approves the findings of misconduct after using its discretion, the workman is not entitled to any back wages.
[Para 9]
The Constitution of India, Article 226, and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10, dictate that writ Courts cannot interfere with the tribunal's findings of fact regarding a workman's misconduct unless it is a case of no evidence or the decision is clearly perverse.
