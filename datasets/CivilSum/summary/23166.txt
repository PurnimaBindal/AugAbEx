According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, and the Army Act, 1950, Section 3, Army Order No. 11 of 1987, Rules 1 and 2, classification and discrimination exist in the classification between officers of the Army based on their duties and job requirements.
The concept of equality has an inherent limitation, and if there is a rational classification consistent with the purpose, equality is not violated.
The classification is based on the requirements of the armed forces and cannot be considered arbitrary; their pay, perks, and privileges are different.
Study leave is only granted to Commissioned Officers because for Officers of other ranks, there are institutions of Army where courses are conducted to enhance their efficiency.
The object of granting study leave is to enhance the knowledge of Commissioned Officers, so it cannot be said that Army Order No. 11 of 1987 is founded on intelligible differentia and has no relation with the object.
Therefore, the Order is not violative of Article 14 of the Constitution.
