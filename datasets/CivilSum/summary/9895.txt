Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Reinstatement - Even if a Labour Court deems the termination of a workman's service as illegal, they may decline to grant reinstatement due to concerns of further industrial dispute - However, it is incorrect to assume that reinstatement will always lead to such dispute - Consequently, the petitioner has been directed to be reinstated with back wages - Petition granted.
