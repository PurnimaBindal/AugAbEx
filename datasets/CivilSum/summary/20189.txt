Constitution of India, Article 32 - Prisoner of war - Officially declared dead but suspected to be alive - Writ of habeas corpus - Soldier's name listed as prisoner in Pakistani jail - Photograph of soldier identified by released prisoners - BSF authorities bring up issue in meetings with Pakistani officials - Ministry of External Affairs directed to investigate for not only petitioner's family but also armed forces of country - Writ of habeas corpus not being issued but relief molded to achieve justice - Importance of delivering justice, not held back by technicalities.
