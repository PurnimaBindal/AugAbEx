Constitution of India, Article 226 - Up-gradation - Of Posts - The government clarified that the post of skilled categories must be upgraded according to the sanctioned strength, rather than the actual strength.
However, the Director General Ordnance Factory redistributed the posts based on actual strength which was found to be illegal by the Central Administrative Tribunal.
The order of the CAT and the Apex Court would operate as res judicata, and raising such dispute would go against the directive of the Central Government.
Therefore, the Writ was dismissed for being without merit.
