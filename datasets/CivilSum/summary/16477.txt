Army Regulations of 1987, Regulation 146 - Military personnel - Extension of terms of engagement - Personnel Below Officer Rank (PBOR) - PBOR's terms were extendable by two years based on option exercised by individual - Petitioner submitted 'Unwillingness Certificate to Service' for the extended tenure in August 2004 but submitted 'Willingness Certificate' in January 2006 - Screening Board considered 'Unwillingness Certificate' and petitioner was discharged from service - Discharge order was rightly issued as 'willingness/unwillingness once extended cannot be changed'.
