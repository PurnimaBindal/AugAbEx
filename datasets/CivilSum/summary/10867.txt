Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Recommendations of Selecting agency - Joining after selection and appointment - Seniority will be calculated from the date of joining and not from the date of recommendation by the recruiting agency, except where rules dictate otherwise - Appointment is made after selection and joining remains optional for the appointee.
[Paras 8 and 18]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority determined by rules - Service rendered in an autonomous body prior to joining the government department - Such service cannot be considered for seniority, unless rules permit so.
[Paras 8, 9, 10 and 18]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Eligibility - Consideration for promotion - If an officer was ineligible for promotion in previous years based on his ACRs and juniors were promoted, subsequent years' promotions will not entitle him to seniority from previous years.
[Paras 11 and 18]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Delay and laches - Promotion - Promotions made in 1981 and 1983 - Challenged in 1997 - Petition dismissed due to delay - Settled matters cannot be reopened by entertaining a belated challenge.
