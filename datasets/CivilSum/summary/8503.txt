Punjab Revenue Patwaris Class-III Rules, 1965, Rule 4 - Seniority - Ad hoc service - Only ad hoc appointments made in accordance with prescribed procedure would count towards seniority - Appointments made by departmental selection committee not in accordance with the Rules - Ad hoc service after appointment cannot be counted towards seniority over regularly selected candidates.
[Paras 16, 19, 20 and 22] B. Punjab Revenue Patwaris, Class-III Rules, 1965, Rule 14 - Probation - Deemed confirmation - Deemed confirmation after expiry of maximum period of probation prescribed in the Rule, even if no confirmation order is passed.
