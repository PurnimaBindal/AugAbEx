The selection process for the post of constable involves the verification of character and antecedents by local police and the provision of accurate information in the application form.
In a case where the appellant suppressed material facts in the application form and was found to be involved in criminal cases that were resolved through a compromise, they were denied the appointment.
This decision was upheld by the SCT in 1997(1) and 2001(1) based on the guilt of the appellant for providing false information.
