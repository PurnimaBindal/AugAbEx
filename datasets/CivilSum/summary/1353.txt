According to Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(3) and UP Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sec. 6E(2), Protected workman's services cannot be terminated without prior permission of the Tribunal before which the matter of lay off is pending.
The management must pay one month wages to protected workman before termination.
[Para 6]

In case of reinstatement directed by Labour Court and a stay of implementation of award by the High Court, the court is bound to pass an order based on the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-B Even if no application is made, the workman is entitled to the benefits of Sec. 17-B and full wages at the last drawn rate from the date of filing the writ petition.
