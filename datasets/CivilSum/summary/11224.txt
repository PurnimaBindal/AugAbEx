Sections 14 and 30 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 - Death of 19-year-old cleaner of a vehicle in an accident - Owner of the vehicle admitted to a master-servant relationship with the deceased - Deceased was getting a salary of Rs. 3000/- pm - Award of compensation of Rs. 3 lacs - The fact that the vehicle was insured with the insurer was not denied but the plea by the owner that the insurance company would only be liable if the employer has become insolvent was not found acceptable - Liability was rightly fixed on the insurer.
