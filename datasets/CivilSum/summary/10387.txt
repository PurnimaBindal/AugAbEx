The Petitioner was offered a post-graduate course in the first counselling round of admission.
Subsequent counselling rounds were held to fill vacancies in other courses, but the Petitioner was not excluded from participating.
It would be unfair to deny the Petitioner a legitimate chance to participate in these later rounds.
Because of their ranking, the Petitioner can stake a claim with other candidates who were called for the first time.
Refusing them would favor less meritorious candidates.
Therefore, the Petitioner, or others in the same position, may also be considered.
