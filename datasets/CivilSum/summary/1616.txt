Empanelment to the post of staff officer, instructors in Civil Defence Organization does not entitle respondent to be called on duty.
A panel may be kept prepared, but person when empanelled does not derive any right for appointment.
Appointment has to be made in accordance with law and with all pre-requisites complied with.
Court cannot direct creation of a post and cannot create a legal fiction to deem petitioners empanelled.
[Paras 24 & 32] B.
Creation of posts is within the domain of the State to determine services required for a particular job.
