Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation of services /Equal pay for equal work/Daily wagers - The High Court ordered to pay equal pay for equal work and regularise the services of the daily wage workers who had been working for more than 15 years, discharging similar duties to their regular counters.
However, it was held that the daily wagers are not entitled to equal pay and regularisation until regular posts are available, and the High Court has no jurisdiction to order regularisation as it is purely an administrative function.
[Paras 2 and 3]
