Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion/Recommendation for promotion - The Departmental Promotion Committee is a recommending body and cannot be given the power of promotion by the appointing authority.
The appointing authority must consider the cases of all similarly placed persons, including the petitioner.
The petitioner's case was not considered by the competent authority only because his name was not recommended by the Promotion Committee while his juniors were promoted.
However, there was nothing adverse against the petitioner.
Therefore, he should be promoted from the date his juniors were promoted.
