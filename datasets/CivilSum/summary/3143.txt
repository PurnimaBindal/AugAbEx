Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) Proviso - Dismissal from service - Approval of tribunal - To obtain approval of an action of dismissal or discharge taken during the pendency of dispute before the tribunal, the payment of one month's wages to workman and making of the application for approval must be within the same transaction or on the same day when the action was taken.
If the management cannot prove that the actions were taken on the same day, the request for approval is liable to be rejected.
Also, the mere receipt of an application for approval by the tribunal is not enough.
The date of posting cannot be presumed unless the receipt issued by the postal department is proved.
[Paras 6 and 7]  B Evidence Act, Section 114 - Presumption of posting - Postal articles - There is no presumption for the date of posting of a postal article.
The date of posting should be proved by providing a postal receipt issued by the post office.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Jurisdiction of Tribunal - The Tribunal's jurisdiction is restricted to determining whether the domestic inquiry followed the rules and principles of natural justice and if a prima facie case is made out for dismissal or discharge on the legal evidence.
An inference of mala fide can also be made if the punishment imposed is unduly harsh, severe, unconscionable, or shockingly disproportionate.
The Tribunal must also ensure that the conditions in the proviso have been satisfied and all the actions have been taken at the same time.
If the conditions are met, the Tribunal will grant approval, and it will relate back to the date of the dismissal or discharge.
Whether or not the conditions are met will depend on the facts of each case.
