Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 320 - Ad hoc Appointment (Public Service Commission Purview Posts) Regularisation Rules, 1979, Rule 1 - Regularisation - Ad hoc appointment - Adverse entry - Efficiency bar - The petitioner made a representation against the adverse entry and was allowed to cross the efficiency bar also - Representation remained undecided - The adverse entries prior to crossing the employee's efficiency bar stand washed out, and those against which the employee has made a representation and representation has not been decided cannot be considered - The petitioner was denied regularisation based on such entries prior to crossing their efficiency bar - The Commission's action was deemed arbitrary and illegal - The order of termination passed on the basis of non-recommendation of Commission was quashed - Commission was directed to re-decide the petitioner's case.
