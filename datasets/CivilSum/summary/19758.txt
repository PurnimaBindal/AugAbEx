Under Section 15 of the U.P.
Intermediate Education Act, 1921, the Managing Committee was authorized to terminate the petitioner's services, but the Manager's termination order was not within jurisdiction.
[Para 5]
The termination of the petitioner's services is a complete violation of Regulations and natural justice as he was not provided with the enquiry report or an opportunity of hearing.
Also, no other evidence was available to him.
This is in violation with Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
