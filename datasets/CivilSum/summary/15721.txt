Constitution of India, Article 226 - Voluntary Retirement - Retiral benefits - The petitioner's request for voluntary retirement and pension was accepted by the respondent Board - However, the respondent is now claiming that the petitioner was wrongly retired - The court held that the petitioner is entitled to receive pension and other post-retiral benefits.
The court relied on the decision made in Ganga Bishan v. State of Haryana and others, 1994(4) SLR 59.
