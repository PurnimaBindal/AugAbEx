Constitution of India, Article 226 - Letters Patent Appeal - The appellate court must not undermine the decision of a single judge exercising writ jurisdiction merely to obtain a second opinion, especially when the opinion of the first court is not incorrect - The single judge reevaluated the answer sheets and found no evidence of unfair means used by the students - The Education Board filed defective appeals which caused up to five years of delay - The appellate court refused to intervene.
