Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226, 162 and 309 - Discrimination - Promissory Estoppel - The Cabinet decision dated 10.6.1946 specified that the members of the West Bengal Higher Judiciary Service receive the same treatment as members of the Indian Administrative Service, including scale of pay - This decision has been in effect since 1949 and there is no rule or legislation contradicting it - Therefore, members of the West Bengal Higher Judicial Service should be treated at par with members of the Indian Administrative Service with regard to their scales of pay - The doctrine of promissory estoppel applies regardless of whether it is a State action - Prescribing different periods for the grant of Junior Administrative Grade and granting supertime scale from different dates to members of the two services is discriminatory and arbitrary.
