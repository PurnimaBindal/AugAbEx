The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25, along with the West Bengal Amendment Act, 1980, Sections 2(s)(iv) and 3 10(1)(d), are governed by the Constitution of India, Article 254.
A supervisor receiving a remuneration of Rs. 1,600/- is considered a workman under Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act, despite the previous ruling that the respondent was not a workman.
The services of the respondent could only be terminated in accordance with Section 25-F, making their termination illegal.
Additionally, in case of repugnance between Central Act and State Act, the Central Act shall prevail.
