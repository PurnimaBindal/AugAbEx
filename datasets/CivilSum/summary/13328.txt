The vires of Haryana Civil Service Revised Pay Rules, 1998, Rules 3(c) and 3(d) were challenged by JBT teachers who are petitioners.
Despite holding certain qualifications, petitioners were placed in a higher pay scale and are now deprived of the corresponding revised pay scale which they enjoyed before the rules were issued.
Their previous pay is protected, however, they are not entitled to any relief.
