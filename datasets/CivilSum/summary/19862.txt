Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Indian Penal Code, Sections 148, 149, 325 and 425 - Policy on removal from service on moral turpitude of ex-convicts - Offences alleged against an individual not listed in the policy cannot be considered covered by it - Therefore, Sections 148, 149, 325 and 425, which are not mentioned in the policy's list, cannot be used to justify removal from service - Any such removal would be illegal.
