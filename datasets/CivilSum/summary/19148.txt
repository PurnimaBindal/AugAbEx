Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Regularisation - Annual confidential reports - Adverse reports - Average - While an average report is not considered adverse, if candidates with better reports are present in a competitive selection process, then employers can select those individuals instead of those with average reports.
As long as no rules or instructions are violated during the selection process, candidates with average reports cannot question the selection of those with better reports.
