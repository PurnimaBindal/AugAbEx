Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - House Rent Allowance - House Compensation Allowance - Instructions - Govt. employees entitled to rent free accommodation entitled to House Rent Allowance on slab basis according to pay range and place of posting - Instructions applicable to police personnel - Petitioners police personnel who had been receiving house rent allowance as per valid orders dated 141979 - Orders modified in 1991, excess amount recovered from petitioners - Recovery held to be unjustified and arbitrary, revised House Rent Allowance held valid.
