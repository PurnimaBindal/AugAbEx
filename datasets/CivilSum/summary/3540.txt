The Shiromani Gurdwara Prabandhak Committee (SGPC) is not considered an "Industry" under Section 2(j) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
The spiritual, faith-based, and charitable activities carried out in the Gurdwaras under SGPC do not involve any commercial, manufacturing, or profit-making elements.
As a result, the establishment cannot be classified as an "industry," and the employees of SGPC do not work in a master-servant relationship, but instead, work with devotion for faith and religion.
They are paid remuneration solely to survive.
[Paras 6 and 10]
