Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. 1 Part 1, Rule 7.6 - Suspension - Retirement - Conviction - If a person is facing any inquiry or criminal trial, an order regarding superannuation is required - If the delinquent lost upto Supreme Court and his conviction is upheld, the order would be retrospective to the date of suspension - Only if the delinquent succeeds and is acquitted by the court, the issue of superannuation arises - If no order regarding superannuation was passed when the delinquent was suspended before superannuation, then the order of dismissal from the date of suspension is appropriate.
[Paras 17, 19 to 21]
Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. 1, Part 1, Rule 7.6 - Relationship of employee and employer - When an order of dismissal is passed, the relationship between an employee and employer ceases - Even if the conviction order is under appeal, the person serving the sentence cannot be considered to be under suspension and entitled to reinstatement after completion of sentence.
[Para 17]
