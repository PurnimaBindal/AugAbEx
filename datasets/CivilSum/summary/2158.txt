The Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, Sections 22C(1) and 22C(8) state that a matter will be decided on merit by Lok Adalat.
Section 22C(8) empowers Lok Adalat to make decisions on the merits, even when the parties do not reach a settlement.
In this case, Lok Adalat passed a reasoned order giving a categorical finding against the petitioner.
The High court cannot reappreciate evidence and make a finding different from the one given by Lok Adalat.
In addition, the validity of the Amendment Act has already been upheld by the Hon'ble Apex Court in SN Pandey's case.
The petitioner has no locus standi to challenge the provisions of Central legislation, and UT Administration Chandigarh cannot be considered an aggrieved party by any stretch of imagination.
Allowing states or UT's to argue that their own acts and laws are unconstitutional would be a dangerous course.
