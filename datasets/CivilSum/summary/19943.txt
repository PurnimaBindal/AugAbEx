Central Excise a Land Customs Department Group `C' Posts Recruitment Rule 1979, Rule 7 - Constitution of India, 1950, Article 14 and 16 - Promotion - `Seniority' - `Eligibility' - Respondents, who transferred from one collectorate to another, gave up their seniority to do so, as per the conditions set out - Consequently, they were placed at the bottom of the list of employees in the new collectorate and cannot complaint about any promotions given to LDC's higher on the list - Neither the respondents nor employees in the transferee department can claim seniority - The High Court was incorrect in equating eligibility to seniority.
