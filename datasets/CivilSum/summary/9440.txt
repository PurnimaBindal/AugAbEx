According to Rule 44(5) of MP Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1976 and Article 16 of the Constitution of India, a divorced wife of a government servant cannot claim retiral dues, including family pension.
The term "wife" has been interpreted strictly and does not include any divorced wife, as established in AIR 2003 SC 2103 : (2003) AIR SCW 104.
