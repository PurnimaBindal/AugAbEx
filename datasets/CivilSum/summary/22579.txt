Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10(1) - Civil Procedure Code, Section 9 - Bar of jurisdiction of Civil Court - Termination, dismissal or discharge of a workman gives rise to an industrial dispute.
Labour Court is a proper forum to adjudicate the same and can grant effective relief expeditiously.
The object of alternate forum is to provide speedy remedy.
When an independent alternate provision and forum have been established, there is an implied bar to the jurisdiction of the Civil Court.
Rajasthan State Road Transport Corporation, 1995(5) SCC 75 followed.
