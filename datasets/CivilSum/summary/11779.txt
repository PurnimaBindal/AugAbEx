Constitution of India 1950, Article 226 - Andhra Pradesh (Regulation of Appointments to Public Service and Rationalisation of Staff Pattern and Pay Structure) Act 1994, Section 7 - Service - Regularisation - GOMs 212 dated 22494 applies to all State Government-owned corporations and their employees, including those working in offices and establishments of factories run by the corporations.
However, the GO does not apply to workers in factories established by the State Government.
