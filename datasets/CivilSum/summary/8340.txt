Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - West Bengal Service Rules, Rule 55(4) - Pay parity - The petitioners were senior to respondent in Upper Division Assistant - However, petitioners were drawing pay less than him - Although the benefit of NISG scale was awarded to the respondent, it was not awarded to the petitioners - Therefore, respondent enjoyed higher pay in the feeder post - As per Rule 55(4), equal pay could not be granted if the pay drawn by Senior Officer in lower post before promotion was less than the junior - Thus, the petitioners were not entitled for pay parity - The Tribunal failed to consider this point - Consequently, the order of Tribunal in favour of petitioners has been set aside.
