Letters Patent Appeal, Clause 10 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 11-A - Punishment - Back wages - The Labour Court, while allowing the reference, modified the punishment imposed by the punishing authority and stopped four annual grade increments of the worker without cumulative effect.
The court ordered reinstatement of the worker with full back pay.
The Single Judge upheld the punishment but ruled that the worker would not be entitled to back wages up until the date of enforcement of the award.
The worker would, however, be entitled to back wages from the date of filing the writ petition.
The Single Judge considered all factors such as the length of service, the gravity of allegations, and the length of litigation since 1977.
As the discretion exercised by the Single Judge was appropriate, it cannot be deemed unreasonable.
The well-reasoned judgment should not be interfered with.
[Paras 8, 11 and 12]
