Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Dismissal Order - Susceptibility of - Petitioner punished with dismissal - Argument that Punishing Authority relied on documents from Disciplinary Authority and Central Vigilance Commission which were not provided, causing prejudice - Conclusion: Petitioner was prejudiced in their defence by withholding documents, therefore the dismissal order cannot stand - Petition approved accordingly.
