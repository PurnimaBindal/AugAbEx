In accordance with Article 235 of the Constitution of India and the Punjab Superior Judicial Service Rules, an Additional District Judge transferred as the presiding officer of an Industrial Tribunal still falls under the control of the High Court, remains listed in the seniority lists of judicial officers, and maintains their position in the department of judicial service.
They will have a superannuation age of 60 years, and will not be absorbed into any service with an age of retirement of 67 years.
Furthermore, Section 7-C of the Industrial Disputes Act does not dictate a retirement age, but rather establishes a condition.
Therefore, a judicial officer transferred as PO of a Labour Tribunal cannot request a superannuation age of 67 years.
