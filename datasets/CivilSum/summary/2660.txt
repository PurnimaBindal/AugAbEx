"Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Arrears of salary - The petitioner's husband, who challenged his termination, filed an application for release of arrears of salary and allowances, but it was dismissed, with the observation that he may file a civil suit for that purpose.
The widow filed a writ petition after her husband's death and reinstatement, seeking arrears of salary and allowance.
This cannot be dismissed on the same basis, as the court did not reject the merits of the claim and her husband was merely advised to pursue a civil suit.
[Para 7]"
