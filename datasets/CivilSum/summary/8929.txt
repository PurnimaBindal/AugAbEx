Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(1) - Award - Implementation of award - The Labour Commissioner has issued recovery certificates and executed the award, but the management has not implemented it.
The technical issue of which officer issued the certificates should not be used to deny the workman's rights.
Even if the order of the Labour Commissioner was set aside, the Court would still direct the management, which is a State, to implement the award.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(C) (1) - Award - Back wages - The computation of back wages is simply an arithmetical calculation, and the competent authority under Section 33-C (1) should go into it.
The jurisdiction of the authority would only be ousted if the right to claim the money was disputed.
It would be too harsh for the workman to be driven to another round of litigation before the Labour Court.
As long as the award made by the Labour Court stands, the management must implement it.
[Para 3]  C Back wages - Gainfully employed - The Labour Court has directed the reinstatement of the workman, but only with 50% back wages due to his admission that he represented workers of various companies before Labour Courts/Industrial Tribunals since 1988.
The award of back wages is discretionary and cannot be considered arbitrary, and there can be no interference with it.
