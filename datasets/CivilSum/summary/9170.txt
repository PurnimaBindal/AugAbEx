According to Articles 14 and 311 of the Constitution of India, in regards to Probation/Confirmation/Reversion, if there is no extension of the probation period nor any communication of unsatisfactory performance during this time, the employee is confirmed on the expiry of the probation period.
Additionally, a direct recruit cannot be reverted to a lower post even for unsatisfactory performance during probation.
[Paras 27, 37, 38 and 45]
