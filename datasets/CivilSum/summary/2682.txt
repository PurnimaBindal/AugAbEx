Constitution of India, Articles 14 to 16 - Interview - Commission's Authority - The Commission is authorized to prescribe minimum marks for candidate selection, and as long as the prescription is not arbitrary, questions of suitability cannot be raised.
In the given case, the petitioner failed to secure the prescribed marks and, thus, cannot claim entitlement to any relief.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 to 16 - Constitution of Selection Committee - Expertise of Members - Selection Committee Members are required to have expertise in the relevant field with no more than one member from the Bureaucratic wing.
Administration capabilities and subject expertise are two different skills that cannot be substituted for each other.
