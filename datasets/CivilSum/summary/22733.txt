The Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16, 39, and 32 stipulate the appointment and regularization of the appellants who were hired as LDCs in the hospital.
Their appointments were regulated by the Safdarjang Hospital Recruitment to Class III posts, Rules 1973, which enabled recruitment to those posts only through direct recruitment.
After fulfilling the necessary qualifications and being chosen by a duly constituted board, they passed the appropriate examination and typing test and served for periods ranging from 18 to 20 years on an allegedly ad hoc basis.
Subsequently, the rules were modified, and selection for appointment to the LDC position was handled by SSC.
Nevertheless, after completing the requisite formalities, their services were regularized before the orders of regularization were cancelled.
There is no claim that the appellants' employment was not in compliance with the recruitment rules.
Even after a long period of employment, appointments cannot be undone.
Even if an equitable and compassionate view must be taken, the court will protect such appointments.
People who have been in service for such a long time with satisfactory performance cannot be dismissed from their positions.
