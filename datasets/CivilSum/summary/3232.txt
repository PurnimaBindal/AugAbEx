I, as applicable to the State of Haryana - Rule 326(b) and (d - Compulsory retirement - Average reports - Public interest - Government instructions - Retention in service beyond 55 years - Officers with 70 per cent or above good record in the last ten years may be granted retention in service beyond 55 years - Rule 326(a) or (d) must not be infringed upon - If "average" reports are treated as adverse, it may be taken into consideration when deciding on compulsory retirement - The instructions are satisfactory - Compulsory retirement based on average reports may be ordered in the public interest.

Compulsory retirement - Confidential reports - Government instructions - The State issued instructions on August 13, 1983, that officers may be granted an extension beyond the age of 55 years if they have more than 70 per cent good confidential reports in the last ten years - These instructions are not contradictory to Rule 326(a) or (d) - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
