Prevention of Corruption Act, 1947, Sections 5(2) and 5(1)(e) - Criminal proceedings and departmental proceedings - Officer charged under Section 5(2) of Prevention of Corruption Act and also proceeded against departmentally - Departmental charge and criminal charge were the same - Officer cleared in departmental proceedings based on Central Vigilance Commission's report which was accepted by Union Public Service Commission - No evidence left to proceed with criminal proceedings as higher standard of proof required in criminal case than in departmental proceedings - Criminal proceedings dismissed.
