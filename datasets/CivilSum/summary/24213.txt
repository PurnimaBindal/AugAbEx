Sections 2(i)(iv) & 6-N of the Uttar Pradesh Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 states that workmen engaged in producing goods or services for a principle employer, even through a contractor, are employees of the principle employer and covered by the definition of `employer' U/s 2(i)(iv) of the UP Industrial Disputes Act.
The relationship of employee and employer is not solely based on the test of control.
If the employer fails to produce the necessary records, the Tribunal may draw an adverse inference against them.
