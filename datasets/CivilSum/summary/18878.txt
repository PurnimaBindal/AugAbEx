For the purpose of estoppel, the Articles 14 and 309 of the Constitution of India allow absorption of employees on equivalent posts in different departments.
Even though the petitioner accepted a clerk position with an equal pay scale to the one held in the previous department, he cannot claim the higher post due to a later revision of the pay scale.
