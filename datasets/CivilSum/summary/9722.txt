Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Electrical) Regulations, 1965, Regulations 97, 99 and 12 - Promotions - Inter-se Seniority - The case of Respondent No. 2, who completed their AMIE degree while in service, could be evaluated under Regulation 97 instead of Regulation 99.
The Board rightfully rejected Respondent No. 2's contention that they were not in service when they cleared the exam because they were on leave, as the relationship of an employee does not come to an end when on leave.
As per regulation 12, the Petitioners have a preferential right to be considered for promotion as Senior Executive Engineers over and above Respondent No. 2.
The Board is directed to examine the Petitioners' promotion case from the date when Respondent No. 2 was promoted if found suitable based on seniority-cum-merit criteria.
