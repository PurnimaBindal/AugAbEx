As per Section 252(A) of the Jammu & Kashmir Municipal Act, 2000 BK, in accordance with Jammu & Kashmir Municipality Subordinate Services Rules, 1970, Rule 6, a petitioner who is an employee of the Directorate of Local Bodies and has been transferred to the Jammu Municipal Committee on deputation can be repatriated at any time, unless legally absorbed in the service of the Municipality.
However, till the date of his repatriation, he would be entitled to seniority and all benefits given to his juniors and not be at a disadvantage because of his deputation in the Municipality.
[Paras 6 and 7]
