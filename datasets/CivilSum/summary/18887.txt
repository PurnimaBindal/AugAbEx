The compulsory retirement order based on an uncommunicated warning and a censor entry recorded a decade prior to the order violates Articles 226 and 311 of the Indian Constitution.
The disciplinary proceedings initiated were not considered during the screening process, and there is no evidence presented before the Screening Committee for the petitioner's compulsory retirement.
Thus, the compulsory retirement order is deemed arbitrary and illegal.
