Administrative Tribunals Act (13 of 1985), Section 14 - Appointment - Petitioner working on an ad hoc basis applying for the post of Medical Officer (Unani) in CGHS, pursuant to advertisement - The petitioner possesses the necessary qualifications provided in the Rules for appointment to said post by direct recruitment - Shortlisting criteria for interviewing general candidates included service rendered by the petitioner on an ad hoc basis - The petitioner has the right to be considered for said post, and their ad hoc service should be taken into account.
[Paras 6 and 7]
