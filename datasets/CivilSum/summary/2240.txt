Constitution of India, Article 226 - Retrial dues - Provident Fund - Gratuity - Government quarter - Employee who opts for VRS and receives all benefits, including the Provident Fund, cannot retain government accommodation and seek release of gratuity - Employer is not responsible for non-payment of gratuity if the employee retains Govt. accommodation unreasonably - Employee was held defaulter and retaining Govt. accommodation with a lock and key is not desirable - Arrears and gratuity were deposited by employer with appropriate authority and cannot be said to be withheld.
