The Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 4, 7 and 8 outline gratuity payment and the withholding of such payment.
Upon superannuation, some amounts may be withheld and the balance adjusted towards any pecuniary loss caused by the petitioner during their service.
However, this is not a valid reason to forfeit gratuity.
It was held that the petitioner is entitled to the balance amount along with interest.
