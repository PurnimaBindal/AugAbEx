Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Pay scales - Equation of pay scales - Revision of pay scales - If the Government or the competent authority has decided to level the pay scales, different pay scales cannot be assigned to those posts during subsequent pay scale revisions - Assigning a lower pay scale to one of those posts at any later stage would be unlawful and a breach of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constituion of India.
