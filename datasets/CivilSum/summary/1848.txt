Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Act, 1950, Sections 164 and 165 - Court-martial - Review petition - Alternative remedy - Army Officers subject to punishment under Army Act must utilize statutory remedy U/s 164, as it is more comprehensive - The availability of statutory remedy precludes court-martial review petitions under Article 226 unless there is a breach of natural justice or action outside the court's jurisdiction - U/s 164, court-martial proceedings can be reviewed and new orders issued - Writ jurisdiction limited to breaches of natural justice or jurisdiction - Exhaustion of statutory remedies required for writ petitions.
