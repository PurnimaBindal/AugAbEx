Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability pension - A petitioner, an officer in the Indian Army, was granted a disability pension for life at a rate of 30% due to a 40% disability caused by Amoebic Hepatitis.
However, when the disability was re-assessed to be between 11% to 14%, the petitioner became dis-entitled for such pension.
The petitioner was not examined by any higher medical board before the Chief Controller of Defence Accounts (Pension).
It was held that the accounts branch dealing with the pension cannot override the judgment of medical experts without making a reference to a detailed or higher medical board.
As a result, the petitioner was granted disability pension based on a 30% assessment of his disability.
[Paras 3 to 4]
