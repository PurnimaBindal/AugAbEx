Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Criminal and departmental proceedings - Simultaneous proceedings are permitted unless they are based on the same facts and charges or criminal charges of a serious nature are involved - In such cases, the departmental proceedings should be stayed until the conclusion of the criminal case.
However, if the criminal case does not proceed, the disciplinary proceedings can resume.
[Para 9] B Constitution of India, Articles 141 and 226 - Judgments and precedents - The theory of precedents states that a decision contains findings of fact, principles of law, and a judgment based on both - A decision is authoritative only for what it actually decides and its ratio of law, not what can be deduced from it logically - Observations made in arriving at the decision are not binding precedents, and only the principle on which the question was decided is binding.
