Constitution of India, Article 226 - Zoological Survey of India (Central Service Class I and Class II posts) Recruitment Rules, 1963 - Writ jurisdiction - The authority advertised for direct recruitment to the post of Scientist - SD - Applicants with 5 years experience in Faunistic Survey/Taxonomy or Doctorate in Soology were eligible to apply - Age limit mentioned therein was relaxable upto 5 years in respect of Govt. servants - The petitioner, who did not apply, could not challenge the relaxation of age - If they had applied, the authority must have considered their cases for relaxation - Hence, the petition was deemed unviable.
