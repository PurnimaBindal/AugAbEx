According to UP State University Act, 1973, Sections 2(18), 2(19) and 49(d) as well as UP Civil Service Regulations, Regulations 459 and 520 and Fundamental Rule 56(a), the superannuation age for University teachers is 60 years as per Statute 16.24.
However, Statute 1701 clarifies that this does not apply to teachers of State Government colleges or Local Authorities.
In a case where a teaching doctor in a Medical College was allowed to continue working beyond the age of 58 years, the High Court's decision was deemed incorrect.
