Border Security Force Act, 1968, Section 11 - Deputationist - Repatriation - Adverse remarks - The petitioner's service record showed an adverse remark for sleeping while on active duty by removing clips of battery, and this was viewed as reprimand-worthy conduct in a prestigious force.
Consequently, the penalty of reprimand for such behavior was deemed appropriate, and the order of repatriation was also considered valid and justifiable.
