According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, admission to the Diplomat National Board Course of Osmania Medical College in Hyderabad is subject to the doctrine of Promissory estoppel.
However, the notifications regarding the entrance test and interview failed to disclose the eligibility criteria for candidates, the number of seats available for each faculty, and the number of reserved seats for Schedule Castes/Tribes.
Furthermore, there are no proper rules or guidelines for admission to the entrance test, and the notifications lack primary requirements.
As a result, the entire selection process is vitiated for want of proper procedure and fair play.
Therefore, the court has directed the government to frame proper rules and guidelines for selection before issuing a fresh notification.
It is important to note that when public interest is involved, the courts cannot bind the government to hold on its promise.
[Paras 18 and 21]
