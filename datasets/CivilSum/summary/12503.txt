Bihar Non-Government Elementary Schools (Taking over of Control) Act, 1976, Section 4(2) - Pay - The State Govt. cannot modify the terms and conditions of service for a Headmaster of a school using executive instructions, as it requires changes in statutory provisions and rules framed by the State Govt.
Thus, the Petitioner is entitled to the same pay of BA trained which he was receiving before the school was taken over by the State.
