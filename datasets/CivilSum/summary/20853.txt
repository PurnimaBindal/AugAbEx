MP Fundamental Rules, 1960 - Absorption in other department - On voluntary transfer and absorption in another department, the lien in the parent Deptt.
will be lost, and the transferee cannot claim to go back to the department where he had lost his lien.
[Para 16]  B. MP Fundamental Rules, 1960 - Absorption in other department - If the transferee was granted notional promotion in the parent department after his transfer, he will still be entitled to arrears of salary from that department.
