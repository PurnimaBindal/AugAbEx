When conducting a judicial review of employee appraisals and gradings, the Court cannot interfere except in cases where the results have been affected by extraneous considerations or bias.
[Para 4]
The objective of employee appraisals is to provide an opportunity for improvement and attain efficiency.
Reporting officers must assess fairly and objectively, without prejudice or extraneous considerations, and act with highest responsibility to promote honesty, integrity, and excellence.
[Para 4]
If an employee against whom allegations of bias and mala fides have been made fails to deny or refute the allegations, they will be deemed admitted and the actions impugned quashed.
