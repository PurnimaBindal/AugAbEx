Regarding a service matter involving transfer and promotion, the transfer order of the respondent being transferred out of Kerala Circle cannot be considered in violation of any statutory rule or as having been made with malicious intent.
The respondent's argument that some of his juniors were allowed to remain in Kerala Circle, and that his transfer goes against the government's policy of posting husbands and wives in the same location as much as possible, is not valid as the respondent holds a transferable post and cannot demand to remain in Kerala Circle even after promotion.
He has no vested right to stay in the circle and cannot claim it as a matter of right.
