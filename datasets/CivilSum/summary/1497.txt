Section 1 of the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act (19 of 1952) states that the Act applies automatically when conditions are fulfilled, and doesn't require any authority's decision relating to the Act, such as the assigning of a code number.
Even if there's a delay in code number allotment, it doesn't exempt the petitioner from liability.
