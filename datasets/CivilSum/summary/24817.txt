According to the Constitution of India, particularly Articles 14, 300-A, and 309, as well as the Bombay Civil Services Rules and Rule 189, the State Government reserves the right to reduce or withhold a Government servant's pension, even after they retire.
However, a reduction of pension by 50% was deemed to be excessively harsh and disproportionate to the misconduct that was proven.
The State Government should have taken into account the respondent's retirement from service and the severe impact such a reduction would have on their standard of living.
