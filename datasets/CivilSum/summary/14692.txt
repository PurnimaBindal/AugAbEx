Constitution of India, Article 14 - Discrimination/Admission - Classification - The policy of the Indian government is first priority to students from developing countries, then to students from developed countries for admission.
If there are any unfilled seats, eligible students from developed countries can be considered based on the combined merit list.
This policy does not violate Article 14 of the Constitution.
[Paras 7 and 8]
Constitution of India, Article 77 - Conduct of business of Govt of India - Admission - Policy decision - Guidelines - The admission scheme is designed for students from developing countries and cannot be changed for individual needs.
The policy review can only be done by the Central Government.
[Paras 8 and 10]
Evidence Act, 1872, Section 115 - Promissory estoppel - Admission - The information brochure of Govt Institution stated that foreign nationals will be sponsored by the government, but candidates from developed countries are not eligible for sponsorship.
The guidelines and submission of fees do not become contractual bind.
[Para 9]
