Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F - Retrenchment - Compensation - The Labour Court may grant compensation instead of ordering reinstatement if the order of retrenchment is illegal and contravenes section 25F of the ID Act, following an assessment of the case's facts and circumstances.
[Para 5]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Retrenchment - Compensation - Workers must have worked for over 240 days in a calendar year.
A writ petition disputed against awarding compensation to a worker is unfounded and should be dismissed.
