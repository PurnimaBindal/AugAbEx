Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion - Eligibility - Juniors in Artisan Grade `C' can be promoted to Artisan Grade `A' directly, but the petitioner must pass a grade test for promotion.
A settlement was alleged to have waived the trade test, but the relevant copy was not brought on record, so the claim was not accepted.
The alleged upgraded juniors were of a different department and cannot be considered juniors to the petitioner.
There was no discrimination made against the petitioner in considering their promotion/upgradation to the post of Artisans grade `A'.
