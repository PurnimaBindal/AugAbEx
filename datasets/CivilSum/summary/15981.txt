Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Education - The Court granted the petitioner interim admission to the Post Graduate Course for Degree of Master of Surgery (Opthalmology) - It was found that even candidates who scored lower than petitioner were admitted to the course and allowed to take final examination - The respondents are instructed to release the petitioner's result and to award him the degree if he passes or to grant him another opportunity in accordance with the rules if he does not pass.
