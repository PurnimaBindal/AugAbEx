Punjab Civil Services (Revised Scales of Pay) Rules, 1979, Rules 3, 4, 6 and 7 - Pay fixation - The petitioners were promoted as Additional Distt & Sessions Judges in an officiating capacity from the posts of Sub-Judges.
Their pay must be fixed on the appointed date, taking into consideration their holding charge on each post, and they will be entitled to all benefits on their substantive posts.
The pay of juniors receiving lower pay scales than the petitioners was fixed at a higher rate, which violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
The petitioners are entitled to a higher pay rate than their juniors, based on their pay drawn on the appointed date, along with all other consequential benefits and arrears of pay, including increments to their Sub-Judge salaries as if they continuously held that post.
