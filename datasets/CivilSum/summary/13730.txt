Central Civil Service (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rules 27(1)(b) & 28 - Absence from duty - Break in service - Forfeiture of service - Pension - Employees may face punishment for over-staying their sanctioned leave without permission, but if this absence is treated as misconduct and punished while they are still in service, it will not result in forfeiture of past service - Rule 28 takes care of interruption periods and is automatically condoned once qualifying service commences.
