Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Unauthorised absence - Termination of service - A workman exceeded leave by 17 days and returned to work with a medical certificate from a private doctor.
The management refused to allow him to return until he produced a medical certificate from the ESI hospital, which he was not required to do.
The labor court concluded that the termination was illegal, as no law prohibits a person from seeking medical help from a private doctor, even if he is a member of ESI.
As such, Lumpsum compensation of one lakh rupees was awarded.
