The Respondent claims that he is entitled to be appointed to the post via the Public Service Commission's selection list, but there is a dispute regarding whether or not the list has lapsed.
The government argues that the list expired after one year.
As the matter is still undecided, the High Court should not require the government to implement an interim order as it could interfere with the main relief that may or may not be granted in the writ petition.
The High Court is urged to dispose of the writ petition as expeditiously as possible, preferably within six weeks.
