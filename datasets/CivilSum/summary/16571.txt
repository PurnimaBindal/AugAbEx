Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical reimbursement - Petitioner's wife taken to hospital for emergency cancer treatment - Reimbursement denied due to hospital not being recognized by Govt. of Haryana - Court held that during emergency situations, list of approved hospitals need not be considered - Treatment received from unrecognized hospital during emergency does not bar employee or dependent from claiming reimbursement under Rules - Petition granted.
[Paras 2 and 5]
