Maharashtra Civil Services (Regulation of Seniority) Rules, 1982, Rule 4 - Seniority list - Promotees and Direct Recruits to the posts of Sales Tax Inspectors - The first list was challenged by the promotees and was quashed by the High Court.
A fresh list was ordered and the second list was challenged by the Direct Recruits.
The second list was also quashed by MAT and a fresh list was ordered.
The orders of MAT were confirmed by the Supreme Court and a third fresh list was prepared.
Any further petition is barred by the principles of res judicata.
[Para 21] B Maharashtra Civil Services (Regulation of seniority) Rules, 1982 - Seniority list - Pleadings - Carrying forward the vacancies, not provided under Rules - Plea not raised in earlier proceedings - The contention is barred by the principles of 'Constructive Res Judicata'.
[Para 22] C. Quota policy for promotions - Promotions given in excess of Quota limit - The promotions were treated as temporary/ad-hoc promotions by the government.
Documents related to such a decision were not brought on record.
It cannot be said whether the promotions were regular or on an ad-hoc/stop gap basis.
[Para 24] D Promotion policy - Quota - Certificate with regard to promotion excess to the quota - Not produced in earlier proceedings - Not to be agitated in present proceedings.
[Para 24] E. Promotions - List/seniority list - Seniority list prepared by ignoring dates of selection of employees.
The judgment of MAT is to bind all similarly situated promotees.
[Para 25] F Quota policy for promotion - Promotions given in violation of rules - held - Rules must follow - The appellate cannot take advantage.
[Para 26] G. Fixation of seniority - Seniority list prepared by ignoring dates of selection of employees - The list is contrary to law, and it has been quashed by the High Court.
The appeal devoid of any substance has been dismissed.
