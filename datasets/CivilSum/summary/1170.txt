Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 39 (d) and 300-A - Karnataka Administrative Tribunal (Salaries and Allowances and Conditions of Service of Chairman, Vice-Chairman and Members) Rules, 1986, Rule 3 Proviso-Fundamental rights-Waiver-Concession - There can be no estoppel against the Constitution and no citizen can waive away the fundamental rights conferred upon them by the Constitution.
The concession made by a citizen, whether under a mistake of law or otherwise, cannot create an estoppel against them in any proceedings.
Olga Tellis v. Bombay Municipal Corporation, A.I.R. 1986 SC 180.
[Para 10] The Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Sections 31 (1) and 35- Karnataka Administrative Tribunal (Salaries and Allowances and Conditions of Service of Chairman, Vice-Chairman and Members) Rules, 1986, Rule 3 Proviso-Excess salary- Adjustment of pensionary benefits - The court cannot make up for any deficiency in words used by the legislature.
[Para 15] The petitioner is not entitled to a refund of the amount adjusted by the respondent as arrears of pensionary benefits towards excess salary.
The amount recoverable from the petitioner increased on account of further increase in pension authorized to him with retrospective effect.
Recovery of such excess payment made without deduction of the amount that also forms part of the gross pension was recoverable.
Hence, the question of refund of any amount with interest thereon does not arise.
