As per the Army Act, 1950, Sections 2(2), 153 and 163 and Army Rules, 1954, Rule 71, the Supreme Court has decided to remit the case back to GCM for passing the appropriate sentence.
The court declined to direct the Petitioner to present himself before GCM.
It was held that for passing the appropriate sentence, the Petitioner must be a person subject to Army Act, but by virtue of section 2(2), he has ceased to be so.
Therefore, the Petitioner needs to be reinstated for the removal of impediment.
