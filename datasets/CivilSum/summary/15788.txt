Under the Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Rules, 1981, Article 14 of the Indian Constitution, and Rules 31(2) and 41(5)(b), transferring a secondary school teacher to the position of Lecturer Junior College is not considered a promotion since there is no promotional channel for that transition.
Transferred teachers retain their seniority in the list of teachers, and bringing them back to their original post does not require a departmental enquiry or constitute a penalty.
