The review petition in regards to the dismissal case involved a reversal of the initial enquiry decision, leading to directions for reinstatement and back wages for respondent No. 1, but without any findings on the merit of charges against respondent No. 1.
The review petitioner argued for an opportunity to conduct a fresh enquiry, which was not dealt with in the judgment.
Additionally, citing another judgment, the review-petitioner claimed that an error had occurred in the previous directions of reinstatement.
Overall, the review petition was allowed and the review-petitioner was permitted to hold a fresh enquiry in accordance with the law.
