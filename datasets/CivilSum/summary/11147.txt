According to Constitution of India, Article 311 and Syndicate Bank Officers/ Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976, Regulation 6 and Syndicate Bank Officers' Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 53, an Assistant Manager in Syndicate Bank can be removed from service after disciplinary proceedings, and the 1976 Regulations are still valid despite the 1979 Regulations being in effect.

These amendments have been validly published by issuing a circular to all officers of the bank.
