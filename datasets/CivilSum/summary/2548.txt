According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311, and Annamalai University Act, Sections 2(f) and 19(3), reduction in rank applies only when a person appointed to a post is reduced in rank, but there was no post of Head of the Department.
Therefore, replacing a Professor with another Professor via transfer would not result in reduction in rank.
[Paras 10 and 11] B Annamalai University Act, Sections 33(4) and 19, state that a transferred Dean of the Faculty ceases to be the Head of the Department and cannot claim to be a member of the Dean of the Faculty.
