Constitution of India, Articles 14, 53, 77, 310 and 226 - Army Act, 1950, Section 18 - Court martial - Termination of Military Officers - Presidential orders - Doctrine of pleasure - Judicial review on Presidential order passed in exercise of Article 310 of the Constitution and Army Act, 1950, Section 18 - Army Authorities actions are subject to judicial review under Article 226 of the Constitution - No authority is above the law - Evidence must be produced to support actions taken - State action must conform to the rule of law and procedure - If actions are based on evidence, justified by no reasons, tainted by mala fides or taken in violation of principles of natural justice, they are not legally valid - When records are found to be modified, the court can interfere and authorities cannot claim protection of Articles 53 and 77 of the Constitution - Termination orders quashed.
