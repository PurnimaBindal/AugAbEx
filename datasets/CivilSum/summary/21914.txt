According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, reservations can be carried forward and exchanged between Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.
In 1985, the reservation for Scheduled Tribe candidates could have been exchanged for a Scheduled Caste candidate, as stated in Note (1) to paragraph 111 of the Brochure on reservation.
However, the petitioner was not given this benefit due to an erroneous view taken by the High Court that the post was not exchangeable in 1985 to appoint him as an Additional District Judge.
The petitioner had the right to the benefit of reservation by exchange.
