Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Post Graduate Institute of Medical Education & Research Act, 1966, Section 2 - Classification - Age relaxation - Selection of specialist doctors for the post of Professors in Govt. Medical College - The objective is to select the best candidates, and thus the government cannot distinguish between doctors from Govt.
Colleges and those from PGI, which is an institution of national importance - PGI doctors are renowned experts.
PGI is wholly financed and controlled by the Central Govt with members' service conditions governed by Parliament's statute - Even though they may not be covered under Article 311, PGI doctors are entitled to all benefits provided to Govt. employees, and the principles of natural justice under Article 14 apply to them - Different treatment, such as setting age limits for eligibility, cannot be given to them - Selections based on such criteria are struck down, and the Commission is directed to re-conduct selections without such distinctions.
