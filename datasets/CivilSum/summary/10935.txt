Constitution of India, Article 226/227 - Misconduct - Initiation of Enquiry - Enquiry Committee consisting of three members established - The Petitioner challenges the Enquiry report, as one member was not appointed in that capacity - Concluded that an unauthorized person acted as a member of the enquiry committee, who was not appointed by the competent authority - The inquiry report is invalid - Petition accepted.
