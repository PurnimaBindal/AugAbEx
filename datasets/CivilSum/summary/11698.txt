Constitution of India, Article 226 - Jammu and Kashmir Service Selection Recruitment Rules, 1992, Rule 13(ii) - Recruitment - Applications invited - Short listing - Short listed candidates called for interview - The petitioner contests the constitutionality of Rule 13(ii), claiming that it deprives similarly qualified persons of equal opportunity - However, it is deemed reasonable to shortlist candidates based on their higher eligibility qualification to achieve the objective of the Rule - This method is not only fair and just but also unremarkable - Therefore, the petition is rejected.
