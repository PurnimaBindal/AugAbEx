According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, the appellant is eligible for the post of Head Master due to his qualifications as a Telugu Panditt and the application of Special Rules for the Tamil Nadu Higher Secondary Educational Service.
The principle of equating the experience of a Language Pandit to that of a trained graduate has been applied in other schools of the State.
[Para 4]

The passing of accounts test prescribed in Education Service Rules is not a necessary qualification for promotion to the post of Head Master of an aided higher secondary school, as per GOMs.
No. 720 dated 2841981.
Therefore, the appellant cannot be deemed ineligible for the post of Head Master due to his lack of this qualification.
