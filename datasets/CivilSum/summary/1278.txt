The Industrial Disputes Act, under Section 10(1) addresses the issue of reference for industrial dispute.
It is important to note that delay and laches can complicate the matter and that the government is not obligated to refer every claim.
Any explanation for the delay must be satisfactory, and if disputed, will be evaluated by the Labour Tribunal.
In the absence of any explanation, the claim will be dismissed.
