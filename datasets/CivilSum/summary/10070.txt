Constitution of India, Articles 16, and 226 - Rajasthan State Electricity Board Ministerial Staff Regulations, 1962, Regulations 21 and 23 - Probation - Suspension - The respondent was appointed as an Accountant on 991977, suspended on 2741978, and finally reverted back to the position of accounts clerk on 2.3.1980 - The petitioner contended that having completed 18 months of the probation period, they could not be reverted and would be deemed confirmed - No provision for extension of the period of suspension - Employee should be deemed confirmed - The reversion and suspension without inquiry were deemed illegal.
