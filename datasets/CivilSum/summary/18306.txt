Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 68 - Interest on delayed payment of pension and provisional pension and gratuity - In cases where a delay occurs due to departmental proceedings against an employee, they cannot avoid responsibility for the delay and may not be granted interest on pension.
However, if there is an unexplained delay in payment of gratuity, interest @ 8% will be granted.
It is expected that the competent authority will sanction gratuity and commuted pension within three months of the conclusion of disciplinary proceedings, unless the officer is not fully exonerated.
