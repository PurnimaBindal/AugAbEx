The Constitution of India, Article 226 pertains to Termination of services and Appeal.
A Writ petition was allowed by a single Judge stating that the order passed by the Appellate Authority was not a speaking order.
The entire proceedings were placed before the Appellate Authority - the Board - which went through all charges and findings decided by the Enquiry Officer and decided to treat the punishment of dismissal imposed by the Disciplinary Authority as one of termination.
The entire proceedings became a part of the decision of the Board, while the only thing communicated to the employee was the ultimate decision.
The Order of the Board was considered a speaking order.
