Constitution of India, Article 14, 16, 226 and 311 - Writ Jurisdiction - Appointment - Termination - Govt. Pleaders appointed on a tenure basis - They should be allowed to complete their tenure irrespective of the change in Ruling Party unless there is a dereliction of duties - Government terminated the services of Govt. Pleaders and Assistant Govt. Pleaders without reason - Court can scrutinize whether valid reasons existed for the termination - Plea that termination is beyond judicial review is unacceptable.
