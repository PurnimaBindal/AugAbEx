The petitioners are claiming senior pay scale due to the merger of the posts of Assistant Store Keeper/Store Keepers with the clerks and joint security list being prepared for granting it since 111978.
Out of the 52 petitioners, 10 have already received the senior pay scale, whereas the remaining 42 will benefit after the publication of service rules.
According to Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 226, the Respondent No. 2 is instructed to determine the representation of the petitioners according to service rules.
