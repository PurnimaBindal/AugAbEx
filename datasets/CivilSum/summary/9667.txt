Letters Patent Appeal, Clause X - Project Manager of PUNTEX - Declared surplus - Absorption as Treasury Officer in lower pay scale - Regarding sustainability, it was contended that the respondent had accepted an offer of appointment in the lower pay scale.
However, the surplus employees were directed to be absorbed in Govt. service as a policy measure, and the employees did not voluntarily accept posts in the lower pay scale.
Additionally, the Govt. had laid down a policy of absorption with a stipulation that employees' last pay would be protected.
This matter is squarely covered by the judgment in the Balbir Singh case against which Special Leave Petition (SLP) has been dismissed.
[Paras 10 to 12]

Letters Patent Appeal, Clause X - Project Manager of PUNTEX - Declared surplus - Absorption as Treasury Officer in lower pay scale - With respect to arrears on protection of pay, the respondent claimed interest.
It was held that the RSA against the grant of interest @ 12% in a civil suit by similarly situated persons had been dismissed.
As a result, the respondent is entitled to interest @ 12% with arrears.
