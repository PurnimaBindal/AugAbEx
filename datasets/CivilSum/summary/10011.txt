Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Qualifications - The selected and appointed petitioner for the post of lecturer was found fully qualified on application and advertisement.
Changes in qualifications cannot be used to harm the petitioner, as it is well established that eligibility conditions for public service recruitment are judged based on fixed dates in recruitment rules or advertisements up to the application or consideration date.
Any approval to earlier appointments cannot be refused and appointments cannot be canceled based on subsequent changes.
Termination for insufficient approval was ruled illegal and voided.
The petitioner is to be reinstated with all associated benefits, including salary arrears.
