The Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Right and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 47 prohibits the removal or reduction in rank of a person with disabilities due to their disability suffered during their service.
The directive to remove a driver from their post in Haryana Roadways and offer them alternative employment on a lower post of helper-tyreman with lower pay and allowances made in violation of Section 47 cannot be excluded through administrative instructions alone.
As such, the driver should be reinstated to their previous position as driver with all associated benefits and costs of litigation.
