Central Reserved Paramilitary Force Rules, 1955, Rule 27 - Central Reserved Paramilitary Force Act, 1949, Section 11(1) - Minor punishment - Major punishment - The procedure for minor and major punishments is different in the Rules - Natural justice demands that, once a charge sheet is issued for imposition of minor punishment and the employee is informed that only minor punishment will be awarded, the defence strategy will correspondingly be to not meet the charges of major penalty - The punishing authority cannot then award major punishment - Removal from service cannot qualify as minor punishment - The employee's past record and length of service must also be considered.
