The Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 25-F and 2(oo), deals with retrenchment due to a decrease in work.
The law does not distinguish between bona fide or mala fide retrenchment.
As long as the management's action is considered retrenchment, the workman will receive all the benefits of Section 25-F Even if the workman is dismissed due to a decrease in work, they are still considered retrenched and are entitled to reinstatement with full back wages.
