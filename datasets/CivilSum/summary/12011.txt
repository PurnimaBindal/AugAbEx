Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - Rejection of claim on technical grounds - The rejection of the elder son's application for compassionate appointment due to being overaged is considered a hyper technical ground.
The younger son's application was made within the stipulated period and was ultimately rejected due to not being completed within a three-year timeframe and not attaining 18 years at the time.
However, rejecting the claim on hyper technical grounds is deemed unacceptable, thus leading to the order's setting aside.
