Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 39, Rules 1 & 2, Order 43 Rule 1(r) - Termination - Reinstatement - A plaintiff, who was employed by the State Govt. as Master on payment of Rs. 500/- pm was terminated from the services.
The Trial Court dismissed the application but the Learned District Judge allowed the appeal.
The Plaintiff's contention that his service was terminated on account of the abolition of the Handloom Centre on a lack of funds, which was without substance, as the Handloom Centre was still functioning.
However, the Appellate Court directed reinstatement as it was unfair and unjust to throw the plaintiff out of employment after he had continued in service until now.
