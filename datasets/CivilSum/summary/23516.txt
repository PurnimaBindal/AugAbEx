Under Article 16 of the Constitution of India as well as Punjab Food and Supplies Department (State Service Class II) Rules, 1966, Rules 6 and 10, promotion and seniority is decided by appointing promotees before direct recruits in excess of promotion quota, until all direct recruits are appointed.
In case of posts in excess to their promotion quota, those appointed by promotion have to give way to direct recruits and will be considered from the date and against the post falling to the share of promotees.
It is important to note that service rendered against the posts meant for direct recruits will not confer any right upon the promotees.
[Paras 10 and 11]
