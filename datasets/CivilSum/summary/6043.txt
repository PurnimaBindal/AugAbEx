The Competent Authority had the choice to initiate disciplinary action or discharge under Rule 1221 of Punjab Police Rules 1934, and the Respondents were not required to follow provisions of Article 311 or Chapter 16 of the Rules.
The orders of discharge do not have any penal consequences.
[Paras 7 and 11]  B.
The issuance of a notice to employees does not necessarily mean that the employer intended to punish them.
The employer has the right to seek explanations from employees to satisfy itself about their suitability before arriving at a firm decision.
[Para 7]  C The termination of services of an employee under suspension results in penal consequences, but in this case, where no order of suspension was passed, the action cannot be considered penal.
[Para 8]  D The fact that witnesses produced by prosecution turned hostile does not mean that there was no material before the authority to conclude that the petitioners were not likely to make efficient police officers.
The action of the authority cannot be said to be vitiated on account of lack of evidence.
