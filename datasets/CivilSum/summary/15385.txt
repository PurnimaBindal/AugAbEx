According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the petitioners, who are lecturers, are claiming consideration for appointment to the position of Assistant Education Officer.
Their argument is that Masters are being appointed to the position despite the fact that Masters and Assistant Education Officer positions have the same scale, whereas lecturer positions have a higher scale.
However, it has been held that appointing a lecturer to the position would not be considered a promotion to a higher post but rather a reversion.
