Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 226 - Reservation - Cancellation of Admission - Education - Scheduled Caste certificate - The petitioner provided her BC certificate and five additional certificates for entrance test admission, and was not objected by the authorities – the blank column indicating "document wanting" deemed all requirements complete and acceptable – thus, it is unacceptable for the respondents to revoke her admission as if no caste certificate was filed.
