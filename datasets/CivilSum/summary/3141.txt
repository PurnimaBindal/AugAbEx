The Andhra Pradesh Direct Recruitment for posts of Teachers (Scheme of Selection) Rules 1994, Rules 15 and 19, along with Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, dictate the selection process for teachers.
In the event of additional vacancies arising during the selection process, the government may opt to fill them from the list of candidates who passed the written exam and received qualifying marks.
It is not necessary to conduct a new written test and interview for each round of vacancies.
[Paras 19, 20 and 21]
