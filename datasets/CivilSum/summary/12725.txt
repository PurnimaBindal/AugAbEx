Constitution of India, Article 226 - Guidelines for Transfers - Nature of - Interference - Tribunal ordered for Respondent's transfer to be reconsidered despite finding no wrongdoing - Transfer was deemed to violate transfer guidelines - It was determined that the transfer guidelines are merely suggestive and are not obligatory - If there is no evidence of wrongdoing, the transfer order cannot be interfered with by the Tribunal given that it is a condition of service which employees are required to follow.
