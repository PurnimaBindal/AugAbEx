Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Removal - Respondent faced a departmental enquiry due to unauthorized absence from duty - Inquiry report was submitted, and a penalty of removal from service was imposed - Labour Court determined that the inquiry was fair and proper, but considering totality of circumstances, directed management to give respondent a fresh appointment - The Court exercised discretion and found dismissal improper, thus interference is unwarranted absent proof of arbitrary exercise of discretion.
