Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 define the term 'Workman', which does not include members of the petitioner-Sangam, who are claiming regularisation and permanent status as employees in the respondent-Corporation.
The court has ruled that there is no employment relationship or even a contract of employment between them and they do not meet the definition of workman.
Therefore, they are not entitled to a direction for regularisation and permanent status in the respondent-Corporation.
[Paras 15, 18 and 19]
