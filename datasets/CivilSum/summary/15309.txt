Constitution of India, Article 226 - Non-worker - Industrial dispute - The dispute raised by employers workmen in regard to non-employment of others who may not be their workmen at the material time is covered under the expression 'industrial dispute' and is therefore maintainable.
[Paras 1 and 2]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Employment - Denial of - The ground that the elder brother is earning a livelihood by driving a three-wheeler is not an official ground to deny employment, especially when the employment of the sibling is not with any government agency.
[Para 3]
