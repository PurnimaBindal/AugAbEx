Constitution of India.
Article 226 - Punjab State Tubewell Corporation - The petitioner, appointed as a silt observer on work charged basis in 1978, was retrenched in 1988 after completing work.
Despite not being covered by any government instructions, the petitioner continued to work on a work-charged basis from 1978 to 1988 and thereafter to date through court stays.
It would be appropriate to consider the petitioner's case sympathetically for appointment to the post of silt observer or any other post for which he may be qualified on a preferential basis in accordance with the law.
[Paras 4, 5 and 6]
