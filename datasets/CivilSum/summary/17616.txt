The petitioner was disqualified from appearing in any University examination for a period of two years due to alleged unfair means.
However, there is no concrete evidence of the petitioner's guilt, and his good academic record, neat handwriting, and methodical answers suggest otherwise.
While it is important to curb the social evil of unfair means in University examinations, innocent students' educational careers should not be marred without proper consideration of the evidence on record.
Therefore, the order passed by the committee disqualifying the petitioner is set aside.
