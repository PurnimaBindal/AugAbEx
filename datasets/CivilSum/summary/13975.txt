Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Abandonment of duties - The petitioner was absent from work since March 1999 and only submitted a leave application along with medical certificate until October 5, 1999.
The petitioner did not request for an extension of leave nor submit any medical certificate after that and also did not appear before the Medical Board.
As per certified standing order, the respondent was justified in striking off the petitioner's name from the company's roll due to voluntary abandonment of duties.
