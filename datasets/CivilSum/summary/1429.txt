Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 3(1) - Murder - Accident during course of employment of workman - Meaning of "accident" in Section 3(1) - Word not used in common parlance - The term covers any mishappening resulting into injury or death of worker - The act of murder of a truck driver enroute to consignment destination is covered under Section 3(1) of the Act - Insurance Co must pay compensation.
