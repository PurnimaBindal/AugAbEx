Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 109 - Tamil Nadu Agricultural Produce Market Rules, 1962, Rules 203 and 202 - Tamil Nadu Agricultural Marketing Subordinate Service Rules, 1989, Rules 2, 7 & 10 - Regularisation - Repatriation - Having been declared as Government servants by the Tamil Nadu Government, the appellant was transferred to another Committee, where he was confirmed, promoted, and made permanent.
His repatriation on the basis that he can return to his parent Committee is completely unlawful because the idea of a parent Committee and borrowing Committee became obsolete after the framing of the Centralized Marketing Subordinate Service.
