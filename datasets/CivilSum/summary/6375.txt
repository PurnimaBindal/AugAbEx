Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Pay scale - Municipal Corporation converted post of Clerk into that of Work Supervisor - Plaintiff sought pay scale of Work Supervisor based on Resolution - Appellants argued post conversion was for limited period only - Resolution did not indicate fixed duration of conversion - Plaintiff's charge as Work Supervisor was also withdrawn by another resolution - Court held that withdrawal resolution would only operate in the future - Plaintiff entitled to claim post of Supervisor for duties previously performed.
