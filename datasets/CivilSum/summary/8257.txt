A complaint of a second offence under Section 85(c) of the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, punishable under Section 85A, enjoys a limitation of over one year according to Sections 85(c) and 85A as well as Section 468(2)(c) of the Criminal Procedure Code, 1973.
Since the offence is a continuing one, any question of limitation against the complaint of the offence is not maintainable.
