Constitution of India, Articles 226 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Delay and laches - The discharge of a constable during their probation period is not subject to appeal under Rule 1221, and any appeal or revision attempted afterwards does not excuse the delay in filing a writ petition.
[Paras 9 and 14]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Punishment - A discharge with a simpliciter innocuous order during the three-year probation period cannot be interpreted as a punishment.
[Para 11]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Criminal proceedings - The discharge of a constable during their probation period due to involvement in a criminal case is not based on the criminal proceedings alone, but rather a justified reason for discharge.
Therefore the order of discharge is appropriate.
[Para 13]
