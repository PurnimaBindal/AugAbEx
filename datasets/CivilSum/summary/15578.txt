Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 220 - Police Regulations of Bengal, Regulation 746 - Appointment - Interview - The applicability of Regulations incorporating provision of interview to candidates who passed practical test under the Regulation - A circular indicating a mere proposal to amend the Regulation, but not its actual amendment - Hence, the State authorities would not have conducted interviews contrary to the provision of the Regulation with statutory force - Petitioners must be considered for appointment as per existing Regulation.
