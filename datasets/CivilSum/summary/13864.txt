The petitioner applied for the post of Primary Teacher as an OBC candidate after 15 days of the issue of the advertisement.
The petitioner was granted more time by the respondents to furnish the OBC certificate, and after obtaining a genuine OBC certificate, the petitioner submitted the same before the cut off date.
The respondent was directed to consider the petitioner's application as an OBC candidate.
