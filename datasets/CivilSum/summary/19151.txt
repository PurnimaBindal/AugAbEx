Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - Married daughter - Eligibility - Unless it can be pleaded and proven that a married daughter is still being brought up by her father with her husband earning nothing, she cannot be seen as dependent on her father after marriage - Compassionate appointment cannot be given to a married daughter as a dependent of her father - The appointment given in conflict with rules and instructions has been withdrawn rightfully.
