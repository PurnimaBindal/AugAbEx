Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19 - All India Service (Confidential Rolls) Rules, 1970, Rules 2(a), 2(f), 6A, 6B, 8 - Expunction of adverse remarks - Tribunal ruled that accepting authority is the relevant supervising authority and their remarks are the final in the hierarchy - Thus, the accepting authority is not required to record reasons for downgrading ACRs - Communication of adverse entries in ACRs is the only requirement as per the judgment in Bahadur Singh's case.
