Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation - Railway Manual and Rules - Appointment to the post of Mate in Railways is through promotion from the post of Gangman - Respondents appointed directly against Class III posts as Mates - This is not permitted - Continuation as Mates for a long time does not guarantee regularisation - Tribunal's direction to absorb respondents as Mates not justified - However, given that they have been Mates for 22 to 25 years, the Supreme Court refused to interfere with Tribunal's directions as it would be unfair to regularise them against Class IV posts of Gangman.
