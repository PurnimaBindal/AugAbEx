Tamil Nadu State Civil Service (Executive Branch) Recruitment Rules, Rule 4 - Promotion - Experience - Promotion to the post of District Revenue Officers from Deputy Collectors - An anomalous situation arises due to rules that require 'six years' experience to be read as six years experience as Deputy Collector, regardless of whether the officer gained the position through direct recruitment or promotion - The State must adjust the rights of officers accordingly - Appeals are permitted - The rules are judged to be discriminatory and against the intentions of Article 14 in the Constitution.
