Under Section 17(1) Second proviso of the Regional Rural Banks Act, 1976, the Central Govt. has the power to determine the remuneration of employees - Award of Justice Reddi cannot be interpreted to diminish the power of the Govt. or render the rule superfluous - Revision of pay structure can only occur after the Central Govt. exercises its power under the Act, which could result in disparity between pay-scales of commercial/nationalized banks and RRBs - The Court can intervene to issue appropriate directions if the Govt. fails in its obligation to maintain parity.
[Paras 14, 20 and 22] B.
Once the Govt. and the bank accept and implement the Reddi award, the argument regarding the financial condition of the bank cannot be considered for directing it to provide pay parity with other nationalized banks as the Tribunal had already deliberated on this point in consideration of the facts and evidence, holding that financial constraints are not a sufficient justification to deprive employees of the pay parity they are entitled to under the Act, and the award has not been challenged.
[Paras 5, 16 and 18] C. According to the Reddi Tribunal's award, the word "parity" means equality of condition or rank equivalent, position or equal value, indicating that RRB employees are entitled to claim the same pay-scales, allowances, and other benefits as employees of sponsor banks.
