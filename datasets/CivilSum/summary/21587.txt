Department Proceedings - Acquittal in Criminal Case - An acquittal of a delinquent does not necessarily absolve them from undergoing disciplinary inquiries, but where the charges are identical, the same inquiries are not usually taken.
[Para 27]

Department Proceedings - Principles explained - The acquittal of a delinquent does not preclude an employer from taking action through disciplinary authority, but the proceeding must not be initiated mala fide and must be conducted in accordance with the law and principles of natural justice.
Additionally, if the Inquiry Officer is biased, no action may be taken and a departmental proceeding may only be initiated if the department has evidence within its power and possession to prove the charges against the delinquent officer.
[Paras 21, 25 and 27]
