Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pension - Withholding of pension - The withholding of pension due to the non-handing over of articles at the time of retirement is not justified without a pending or contemplated inquiry.
Pension is not a gift, but an earned property regularly payable on account of services rendered.
While subject to some conditions, an employee cannot be deprived of their right to pension on mere allegations or minor misconduct, unless they are found guilty of some grave misconduct.
