According to Punjab Civil Services Rules (Punishment and Appeal) 1952 and 1970, the punishing authority may impose a minor punishment without utilizing the prescribed procedure for minor punishment if proceedings have been initiated under the provisions that allow for major punishment.
However, if the proceedings have been taken under provisions for minor punishment, only after complying with the provisions, can major punishments be imposed.
This is all in line with Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
