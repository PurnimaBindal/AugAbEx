The canteen workers of LIC are deemed employees of the Corporation due to various factors such as the canteen and its assets being owned by the Corporation, service conditions of the workers being controlled by the Corporation, and complaints against the canteen management being entertained by the Corporation.
Any disputes between the workers and the canteen management are also resolved with the intervention of the Corporation.
[Para 12] B.
The canteen workers, who are working in the canteen owned by the Corporation but run through an independent contractor, are to be treated as employees of the Corporation subject to certain criteria.
They are entitled to be permanently absorbed and regularised in the service of the Corporation in the minimum pay scale prescribed.
They would also be entitled to the benefit of continuous service in the Corporation as canteen workers for the purpose of retiral benefits and salary arrears from the date of filing the writ petition.
[Paras 13 and 14] C. The canteen workers' claims of being employees of the Corporation and the demand for a minimum pay scale equal to the workers of the Corporation inherently entail the claim of regularization, even if it is not specifically mentioned in the pleadings.
The pleadings have to be read and considered as a whole.
