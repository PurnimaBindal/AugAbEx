Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 22 - Legal heirs - Death of workman in accident - Claim by parents - Death of parents during pendency of proceedings - Elder brothers of deceased eligibility for claim - Decided, The dependents who were entitled to receive compensation on the date of the accident are the relevant ones, and if they are no longer alive, the legal heirs will receive the amount that was due to them.
