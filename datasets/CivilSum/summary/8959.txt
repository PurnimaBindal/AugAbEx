The Rajasthan Service of Engineers and Research Officers (Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1954, the Rajasthan Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rules 14 and 17, and Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India deal with promotion/minor penalty.
The Departmental Promotion Committee granted a sealed cover promotion to the petitioner during the pendency of departmental proceedings against him subject to the result of the inquiry.
After the inquiry, a minor penalty was imposed upon the petitioner, and the DPC reviewed its decision taking into consideration the punishment imposed and declined promotion holding him not fit for promotion.
The action was not violative of Articles 14 of 16 of the Constitution of India, and the petitioner cannot claim discrimination vis-a-vis his juniors or others who were promoted because they had no disciplinary proceedings pending against them.
The DPC was justified in withholding the promotion during the pendency of the inquiry and reviewing its decision after the imposition of punishment upon the petitioner.

Article 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India deal with equal pay for equal work, and the petitioner was directed to perform the duties of Executive Engineer while posted as Assistant Engineer.
As a result, he is entitled to the emoluments of the post of Executive Engineer for the duration of the time he was made to officiate on the post of Executive Engineer.
