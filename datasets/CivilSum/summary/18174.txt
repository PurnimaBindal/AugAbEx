Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Transfer - Re-transfer - Petitioner transferred to Delhi Collectorate with the condition that he retains lien in Chandigarh Collectorate until he is confirmed by Delhi Collectorate - Petitioner is seeking retransfer while claiming his lien in Chandigarh Collectorate, as he has not been confirmed by Delhi Collectorate at the time of filing the application for re-transfer to Chandigarh - The order that prevented petitioner from seeking transfer to Chandigarh Collectorate is set aside.
