The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Chapter VA and B, Section 2(cc), 25-F, K, N & O discuss Work Munishies and Termination of Service.
In a recent case, the petitioners were given one month's pay instead of notice upon the termination of their services.
However, they argued that since more than 100 workmen were working in the establishment, compensation under Section 25-N and the provisions of Chapter VB should have been applied.
They also brought up the fact that their service was liable to be terminated with 10 days' notice as per their appointment letter.
The Board of directors ultimately decided to retrench the surplus staff on projects, but the petitioners argued that no permission was taken from the government to close the department.
The court held that no permission from the government was needed since there was nothing on record to show that more than 100 employees were working on the project, which would have attracted the provision of Section 25-O and Chapter VB.
Additionally, Section 25-G did not apply since the persons were not shown as working on the same project, and the provisions of Section 25-N were not applicable since the appointment letter stipulated 10 days' notice and the petitioners were paid one month's salary.
The petition was subsequently dismissed.
