Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination - The President's order stated that Military Nursing Officers could be retained even after their marriage, with their performance reviewed periodically in the two years following.
However, the officer in question had her service terminated after marriage and was not given the opportunity to prove her efficiency in the two years provided by the order.
The direct order was issued for her reinstatement in service and to be provided with the opportunity.
