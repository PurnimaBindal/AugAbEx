Constitution of India, Article 300A - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Clause 4 - Family pension - The petitioner was appointed on 17.10.1979 and their service was regularised on 10.3.1995.
Despite their untimely death on 26111995, the petitioner's family pension was denied on the basis of non-completion of one-year regular service.
However, the widow is entitled to the benefit of family pension as work charge period must be included in qualifying service for pension.
The respondents have been directed to release family pension to the widow.
