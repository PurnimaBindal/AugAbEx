Article 16 and 311 of the Indian Constitution – abolition of post – termination – ad hoc employee –  The respondent, who was appointed on an ad hoc basis as a Steno-typist, cannot continue in the abolished post after the management passed a resolution terminating the post.
The High Court found the termination not in accordance with rules and directed an inquiry be conducted according to rules to arrive at a decision.
This approach is deemed wholly erroneous.
The existing holder of an abolished post ceases to continue from the date of abolition.
Thus, the termination of the respondent's service is solely due to the abolition of the post, and conducting an inquiry is irrelevant.
[Para 2] B Abolition of Post – Termination – Enquiry – As the respondent's termination is directly linked to the abolishment of their post, there is no need to conduct an inquiry as per rules.
