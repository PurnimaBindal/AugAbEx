Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10(1) - Reference - Jurisdiction of appropriate Govt. - Appropriate Govt. can only assess the prima facie existence or apprehension of an industrial dispute between parties, but cannot determine the merits of the dispute itself to decline the reference.
[Paras 4 and 5] Govt. declining the reference on the "no work no pay" principle is accepting the employer's plea that the workman did not work voluntarily, which requires evaluation of evidence.
