According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Section 6 of the UP Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a Labour Court found the termination of a workman's service to be illegal and awarded compensation.
The employer failed to comply with the provisions of Section 6-N and was unable to prove any manifest error or perverseness in the award, therefore, interference in the award was not deemed necessary.
