Constitution of India, Article 311 - Service record - Alteration of date of birth - An ex-serviceman who was appointed as a Constable - The Military discharge certificate indicated that his age was 22 years old as of his discharge date - The Authorities reckoned his date of birth as exactly 22 years prior to the date of his certificate - However, an alteration to the initial entry based on conjectures is not allowed.
