Bombay Provincial Municipal Corporation Act, 1949, Section 465(1) - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pension scheme - Revised pension scheme - The Revised Pension Regulations of Bombay Municipal Corporation specify a cut off date, and pensioners are required to apply before a fixed date without knowledge of the persons affected - Regulation 2(1)(iv) and (v) provide the cut off date, and only those who retired prior to 1st April 1971 are entitled to the benefit of the revised pension scheme - However, those who retired between 141971 to 3131978 are not entitled to the same and are excluded without valid explanation - Denial of benefit due to failure to apply within the given time frame was deemed arbitrary, particularly where the pensioner was aware of the timing set forth in the scheme - The cut off date was also considered untenable in violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
