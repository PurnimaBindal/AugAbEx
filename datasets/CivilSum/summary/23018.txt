According to Section 16 of the Factories Act, 1948, a canteen employee cannot claim regularisation if the canteen is run by a contractor and the management exercises no control or supervision over the contractor's employees.
The salaries are paid by the contractor, and the management has no say in the appointment or dismissal of the employees.
Therefore, these employees cannot be considered as employees of the management.
