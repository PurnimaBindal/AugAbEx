Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Punjab State Co-operative Sugar Mills (Common Cadre) Service Rules, 1981, Rules 252, 253 and 254 - Probation - Termination during probation - Termination during probation after holding preliminary inquiry - Non-punitive and non-stigmatic - Purpose of preliminary inquiry is simply to ascertain the facts - If the employer opts not to invoke penal provisions but instead decides to terminate the employee due to unsatisfactory performance during probation as per appointment letter and applicable rules, no regular inquiry is required and principles of natural justice need not be followed.
