Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, Rule 5 - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Rules 16 and 102 - Temporary employee - Termination - Stigma - Rule 16 of CRPF Rules does not provide for the allowance of one month's pay in lieu of one month's notice for temporary Govt. employees - Therefore, Rule 5 of CCSTS Rules 1965 applies to the members of the Force who are temporary Govt. employees - Order of termination is valid even if one month's pay and allowances are given instead of one month's notice, as no financial loss has been caused to the petitioner - Petition dismissed.
[Paras 9, 10 and 14]  B Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Civil Services (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, Rule 5 - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Rules 16 and 102 - Temporary Employee - Termination - Stigma - If the order of termination does not contain any statement casting doubt on the conduct or character of the Govt. servant, then the order is not an order of punishment - The order must be interpreted based on how a reasonable person would read or understand it - The impugned order is a simple order of discharge without any stigma attached to the petitioner's character or conduct - Petition dismissed.
