In W.P. No. 2373 of 2003, under Constitution of India, Articles 226, and the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule II, Item 3, it states that dismissal from service can occur due to misconduct, such as theft of company property.
However, if the workman is acquitted in a criminal trial on the grounds of turning hostile, then they cannot be dismissed.
While the workman had a past record of warnings and suspension, the order of reinstatement and 50% back wages awarded by the Labour Court is considered improper.
