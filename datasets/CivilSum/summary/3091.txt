Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion - Criteria for promotion - Seniority-cum-fitness - Service record - Promotion of an Army Officer from Lt. General to Army Commander - The senior-most officer eligible for appointment to the post of Army Commander is the petitioner - The criterion of seniority-cum-fitness must be applied - Comparative merit cannot be adjudged - The notion of fitness does not entail comparison between two persons - Fitness must entail physical ability, mental ability, personality, and consideration - It cannot be interpreted as merit-cum-seniority or seniority-cum-merit - Once seniority-cum-fitness becomes the standard, promotion becomes automatic - Service records are the only materials relevant to fitness - Other materials outside the service record are extraneous and must not be considered when seniority-cum-fitness is used - The GOC-IN-C records that the petitioner is "fit in every way to hold his office" and "fit for promotion to the next higher rank/appointment" - The Chief of Army Staff could not recommend the name of any other/junior for any other reasons in supersession to the petitioner - This action could be seen as discriminatory or mala fide, which could demoralize the armed forces and damage the national interest.
[Paras 59, 61, 66, 67, 68 and 75]
