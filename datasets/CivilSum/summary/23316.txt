According to Rule 20 of UP State Colleges Medical Teachers Service Rules, 1990 and Constitution of India, Article 309 and 136, the direct recruit for the position of Professor of medicine has to be treated as senior even if their appointment is delayed due to stay orders obtained by ad hoc professors in untenable petitions.
The delay in the appointment of the direct recruit cannot be attributed to them and in such cases, regularised promotions of other candidates cannot be treated as seniority.
This is applicable even in situations where the appointment of the direct recruit was blocked by the respondents by obtaining orders from the High Court.
