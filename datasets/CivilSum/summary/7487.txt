According to Sections 14 and 17 of the National Council for Teachers Education Act of 1993, the recognition of degrees and diplomas is laid out.
B.Ed.
Correspondence Course from Bhopal University and Kurukshetra University fall under this category.
The Govt. of India published regulations in the Govt. Gazette on 2421996, which needed to be followed for the recognition of degrees.
The process of recognizing degrees could not commence before that.
Those who enrolled in the session 1995-96 before the regulations were in force cannot be considered to have unrecognized degrees.
Therefore, they cannot be deemed ineligible for the position of teacher solely on that basis.
