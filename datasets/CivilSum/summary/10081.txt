Constitution of India, Article 14 - UP High Schools and Intermediate Colleges (Payment of Salaries of Teachers and other Employees) Act, 1971, Section 9 - UP Intermediate Education Act, 1921, Section 16-FF - Salary - The petitioner, who did not work in the school in the month preceding the month when it was brought in the grant-in-aid list, cannot claim approval of salary payment since her appointment is considered void ab initio due to the absence of her appointment letter in the writ proceedings.
This ruling is based on the rules described in Paragraphs 11, 12, and 18.
