Employee's State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 39(5)(a) - Employee's State Insurance Corporation Regulations, Regulation 31-A - Interest on delayed payments by employer - Employers cannot avoid their liability of paying interest on delayed payments by claiming ignorance about their establishment not being covered by the ESI Act.

Employee's State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 39(5)(a) - Employee's State Insurance Corporation Regulations, Regulation 31-A - Damages for delayed payments by employer - Imposing damages is generally done only when the person responsible is found to have acted deliberately or been dishonest or contemptuous toward the law or their obligation.
