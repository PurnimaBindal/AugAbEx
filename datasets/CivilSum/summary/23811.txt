The UP State Electricity Board Operational Employees Category Service Regulations, 1995, Regulation 4 and the Electricity Supply Act, 1948, Section 79(c) were used to amalgamate three categories of officers into one cadre.
Employees within this unified cadre were interchangeable, but this decision was delayed for some reason.
However, in 1994, the Board re-examined the matter and reiterated the decision of interchangeability of posts.
Under service jurisprudence, if a unified cadre is formed consisting of different categories of persons, then there is no bar for the employer to make transfers within the cadre, and no specific order of interchangeability is required under the law.
[Para 9]

The UP State Electricity Board Operational Employees Category Service Regulations, 1995, Regulation 4 and the Electricity Supply Act, 1948, Section 79(c) were also used to amalgamate posts.
The posts had similar qualification requirements, pay scales, and job functions, so it could not be said that the Board grouped dissimilar posts into a unified cadre.
There is no legal infirmity with the Regulation framed by the Board in bringing these posts into one unified cadre.
