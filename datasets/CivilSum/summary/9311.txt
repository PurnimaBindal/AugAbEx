Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 9 Rules 13 - Jurisdiction/Limitation - The labour court loses jurisdiction to handle applications under Order 9 Rule 13 CPC after 30 days from the publication date of the award.
[Para 4]
Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 9 Rule 13 - Sufficient cause - An application made on the grounds that the workman was unable to prosecute the reference due to his absence and that his representative's statement should not bind him is not reason enough to set aside the award when the same representative filed the application.
