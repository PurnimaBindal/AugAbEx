Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Guest faculty - Petitioners who worked as guest faculty teaching staff from December 2005 to March 2006 to cover the shortfall in teaching staff, can continue as guest faculty teaching staff till regular recruitment is done.
[Para 5] B Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Pay scale - Guest faculty teachers - Petitioners cannot claim emoluments equal to minimum wages being paid to regular teacher faculty because they were engaged without any selection process for limited periods in the day and for some days only.
