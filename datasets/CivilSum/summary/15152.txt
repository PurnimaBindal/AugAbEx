It was found that once recognition has been given by the Competent Authority and the apex body of teachers education, the State is bound to grant affiliation according to the provisions of Section 14(6) of the 1993 Act.
Additionally, under Sections 31 and 32 of the 1993 Act, the NCTE is competent to formulate rules and regulations.
The petitioners were not required to obtain an NOC as a result of the amendment.
