Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 14(1) - Appointment - After being offered a job as a postal employee in the Army Postal Service, the appellant was promoted to higher ranks, including that of Major and given temporary commission.
However, he alleged that he was entitled to the rank of Lt.
Colonel but was denied it.
He was later repatriated to the Postal Department and since he was a civilian employee, the Administrative Tribunal had jurisdiction.
