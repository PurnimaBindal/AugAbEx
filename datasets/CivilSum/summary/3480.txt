The petitioner, who was a temporary appointee on a temporary post as a Steno Typist in Court, had his appointment terminated due to the withdrawal of Court for want of vacancy.
The petitioner challenged the termination on the basis that he was discriminated against and claimed his appointment as a right.
However, the report by Judges stated that the petitioner's work and conduct were not satisfactory, and he was not sincere and irresponsible.
An enquiry report found the petitioner to be inefficient and unfit for service, but no penalty was imposed as the service was already dispensed with.
The petitioner's plea of discrimination was rejected as there was no complaint against other employees, and he could not claim parity.
Non-supply of enquiry report was deemed irrelevant as the punishment was not imposed on its sole basis.
