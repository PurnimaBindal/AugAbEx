Kerala Service Rules, Rules 31, 32 and 33 of Part I and III - Reckoning of past service in private colleges/schools - Government cancelled order approving counting of past service for service benefits - Petitioners argue entitlement to protected past service in aided schools for increments - Past service in private colleges/schools cannot be counted for increments in government service if rules do not provide for it - Erroneously granted increments can be withdrawn, but already drawn amount cannot be recovered - However, aided school teacher can count approved service in aided institution for pension on retirement as per Part III of rules.
