The petitioner applied for admission to MA under the category of BA (Hons.)
and was granted admission by the university.
The petitioner requested that his admission be regularised not on the basis of being a BA (Hons.)
student but as a BA (Pass) student.
The university held him ineligible only when results were to be declared that he applied as a BA (Hons.)
student.
University must declare the result of the petitioner and admit him to MA (Final) year since he fulfills the requirement of having a minimum of 40% in four subjects.
The rules specifically state that students holding BA (Pass) degrees are eligible for admission to Master's degree.
