Constitution of India, Articles 234 and 16 - Appointment on the post of Civil Judge - Reserved Category - The State Government must verify the Caste Certificate of the candidate - The petitioner throughout described himself as a member of the scheduled caste Mahar, but the enquiry regarding the genuineness of the caste certificate was held behind his back and without giving him an opportunity to present evidence - Directions have been given to the court to form a committee and conduct a fresh inquiry - One post should be left vacant until the inquiry is completed.
