Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Rajasthan Civil Services Annual Performance Appraisal Instructions, 1976, Instructions 3 and 16 - Adverse Remarks - If a representation filed against adverse remarks in Annual Performance Appraisal Report is rejected after four years without recording any reasons, it is mandatory for respondents to pass a speaking order while rejecting the petitioner's representation, as stated in Paras 7 and 8.
In such cases, Adverse remarks may be liable to be quashed.
