In accordance with the Constitution of India and the Border Security Force Act and Rules, any member of the Border Security Force can be transferred to any location in India or abroad.
After three years of service as a Sub Inspector, a pre-promotion test known as the E-List is required, which was not held in the Punjab Frontier in 1969-70.
This resulted in juniors to the petitioner being promoted or entitled to promotion.
The test was eventually held in Punjab in 1971, and the petitioner was promoted from December 16, 1971.
However, individuals from other frontiers who were junior to the petitioner and qualified the E-List test became senior to him.
The petitioner was entitled to seniority and promotions from the deemed back date of his first junior's promotion.
