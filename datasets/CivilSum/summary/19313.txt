Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Termination - Qualifications - The termination of the Steno-cum-typist for lack of necessary stenography qualifications was justified as the services were no longer required to appoint an appropriately qualified person for the job.
The appointment was for a regular post, but the appointment letter clearly stated that employment may end at any time, and there was no guarantee of regular employment.
The nature of the position indicated that the petitioner was required to be familiar with Stenography in English to work efficiently with the Presiding Officer of the District Forum.
As long as no stigma is attached, there is nothing wrong with terminating the petitioner's services.
[Paras 3 and 4]
