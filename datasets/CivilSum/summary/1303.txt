Guruvayoor Devaswom Act, Section 33, Constitution of India, Article 226 - Allegation of Illegal Appointment in aided or unaided schools by Petitioners - Govt. conducted enquiry and served notices on illegally appointed teachers - Grounds of violation of Natural Justice and inherent want of jurisdiction raised - Held that State can revoke authority granted if misused as carte balance and Rule of Natural Justice cannot protect right acquired by fraud - Right to be heard before adverse order not attracted as that stage has not arrived - Respondent ordered not to terminate services of Petitioners until final decision by authorities.
[Paras 2, 6, 9 and 10]
