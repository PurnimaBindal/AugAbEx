If an individual was entitled to be appointed or promoted to a post and it was found that others had committed fraud in the selection process, the individual will be entitled to all benefits as if they were duly appointed or promoted on the relevant date, including arrears of salary and length of service.
The government may take action against its erring officers in accordance with the law.
This is outlined in the Constitution of India, under Articles 14 and 226.
