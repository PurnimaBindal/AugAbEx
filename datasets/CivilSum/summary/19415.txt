Constitution of India, Articles 16 - Denial of regularisation of service - Adverse remarks were expunged - This means that they no longer exist and cannot be used as a basis for decision making on the performance of the petitioner - Even if other material was relied upon, a decision based on these remarks will be considered invalid as it is based on outside factors.
[Para 6]  B. Expunged adverse remarks - Petitioner's consideration for regularisation was tainted by these remarks - The Committee should not have considered this material - Therefore, the decision regarding the petitioner cannot be sustained and must be set aside.
