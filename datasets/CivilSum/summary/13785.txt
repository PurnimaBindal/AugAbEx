A Sailor who has been dismissed from service under Navy Act is not eligible for pensionary benefits; Regulations 69 and 78 of the Navy Pension Regulation, 1964 specify that.
However, the Petitioner can still make a claim for pensionary benefits by condoning the shortfall in qualifying service, under the proviso to Regulation 69.
The Government must consider and dispose of the petitioner's claim in accordance with the law.
