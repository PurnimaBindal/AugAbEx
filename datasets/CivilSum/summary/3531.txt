Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Compassionate appointment - Handicapped dependent - Ex gratia payment - Compassionate appointment should not be viewed as a guaranteed right to replace a deceased employee with one of their dependents - The objective of compassionate appointment is to aid a family facing sudden penury - Even handicapped dependents of said employee must compete with other candidates in their reserved quota - When ex gratia payment is provided, it cannot be replaced by a court order of appointment.
