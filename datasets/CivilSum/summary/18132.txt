In the education system's semester format, if a candidate fails a lower semester, they may be put on academic probation for a year.
However, once the student meets the required Cumulative Grade Point Average or higher in lower semesters, their probation period comes to an end, and they are no longer bound by the probationary conditions.
If they do not pass a higher semester, they do not need to repeat cleared semesters.
