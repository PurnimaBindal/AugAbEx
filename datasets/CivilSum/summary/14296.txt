The Municipal Act of 2008, Section 52 and Articles 14, 16, and 226 of the Constitution of India are mentioned regarding termination of appellants without natural justice.
The Executive Officer can make appointments under his authority, but recommendations for appointments under the competence of the Council or President of the Council.
The appellants were terminated without notice, clear violation of natural justice.
Thus, their termination was set aside.
