According to the Punjab Civil Services (Pre-mature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 2, the appropriate authority - meaning the authority competent to make the substantive appointments - is responsible for pre-mature retirement.
While there is a stipulation in the executive instructions that a single entry should be considered sufficient for ordering pre-mature retirement, this cannot be interpreted as a mandatory rule.
Rather, executive instructions are only 'guidelines,' and do not embody an inflexible rule that needs to be rigidly followed.
In the case of the appellant, it was necessary for the appropriate authority to objectively examine his service record and come to a finding one way or the other regarding his retirement.
Failure to do so renders the premature retirement order quashed, and the appellant is deemed to have continued in service with all consequential reliefs.
