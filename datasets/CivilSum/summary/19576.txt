Chaudhary, Additional Standing Counsel - State Bank of India Act, 1955 - Military service benefit - The Bank, being an autonomous body, holds the power to select officers and employees and decide the terms and conditions of their service - There are no rules or regulations governing the granting of military service benefit - No order has been issued specifying the benefit of military service to the fixation of seniority or promotion - The Bank had only decided to provide the benefit of seniority to Probationary Officers - The petitioner has no legitimate right to demand the benefit.
