Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Educational Services Class III School Cadre Rules, 1955, Rule 10 - Equal pay for equal work - Pay linked with qualifications - The Rule requires that "members of the service will be entitled to such scales of pay as may be authorised by the Government from time to time" - Instructions dt. 3051957 of the Punjab Govt. granted pay scale of category A to teachers on the basis of their qualifications - Benefit was sought to be withdrawn by instructions dt. 19.2.1979 - Govt. cannot discriminate in pay scales amongst teachers of the same category based on date of joining or acquiring qualifications - Regardless of date of qualification, teachers are entitled to pay scales according to their qualifications and placement in category 'A' & 'B' - Providing different pay scales for equals would violate Rule 10 of the Rules and Article 14 of the Constitution.
