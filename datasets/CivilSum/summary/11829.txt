The respondent’s promotion of the petitioner on compassionate grounds and their subsequent rejection of his request to be appointed as a Deputy Forest Ranger is legal and just.
Despite the fact that the petitioner's father was a Forest Guard, and he had attained the necessary qualifications to be appointed as a Clerk after a 14-year gap, there is no discrimination.
This action is not violative of the provisions of Articles 14 and 16 under the Constitution of India.
[Paras 9 & 12]

The petitioner was given an appointment as a Clerk on compassionate grounds.
Thus, the writ of mandamus cannot be issued as he has no right to claim appointment as a Deputy Forest Ranger, and the State is under no legal obligation to give him the same.
As such, the petition is dismissed.
[Para 11]
