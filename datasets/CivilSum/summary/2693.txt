Haryana Compassionate Assistance to Dependents of Deceased Government Employees Rules, 2003, Rules 2(iii), 3(e), 6(1) and 8 - Claim for compassionate appointment - Despite formalities being completed in compliance with policy from 851995, claim for compassionate appointment was rejected on the basis of 2003 Rules which only provide ex-gratia assistance of 25 lacs for employees above age 55 at time of death - Judgment in Abhishek Kumar's case does not establish that Rules existing at time of death will apply - Rules are applicable to all pending cases where 2003 Rules were implemented - As the petitioner was not eligible as per Rule 8, respondents are directed to offer option for ex-gratia compensation and furnish benefits within one month.
