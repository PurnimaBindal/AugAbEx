Rajasthan State and Subordinate Services (Direct Recruitment by Combined Competitive Examination) Rules, 1962, Rule 4 and Rule 11(1) and proviso added by Amendment Notification dated 11th Aug, 95 - Interpretation of notification - The contention of the petitioners is that they have already appeared for four exams before the notification of 11th Aug 95 and now they are eligible for one extra chance.
It is important to use the cardinal rule of construction of statute to give words their ordinary, natural and grammatical meanings.
The purpose of the notification is to provide an additional chance to the candidates.
The Court decided that the benefit of one more chance should be available not only to candidates who have taken four chances before the syllabus and scheme changed and one additional chance provided through the proviso to sub-rule (i) added via notification dated 15593, but also to those who have completed four chances after the syllabus and scheme changed.
RPSC is obligated to verify the petitioners' eligibility for one more chance.
