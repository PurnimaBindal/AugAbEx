The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 -  A petitioner is entitled to a 12% interest on delayed payment of pension due to principles of mutuality; a party should practice the same standard of conduct towards another in the same facts and circumstances.
The interest must be paid to the petitioner.
[Para 7]
Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, Para 5, Regulation 121 read with Regulation 126 - A petitioner who rendered service in the Indian army and territorial army is not entitled to weightage as a person in territorial army and, therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to a higher rate of pension.
