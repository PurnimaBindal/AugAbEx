Constitution of India, Article 310 - Dentists Act, 1948, Section 6(1) - Removal from the membership of the council - Doctrine of pleasure - Natural justice - Although the Dentists Act's proviso reflecting doctrine of pleasure is not equivalent to the President's pleasure under Article 310 of the Constitution, it must stand the test of reasonableness, fairness, and justness by incorporating natural justice - Removing a nominated member before the end of his term carries civil consequences - Administrative exercise of power without proper reasons, fairness, and justness is invalid and subject to being struck down - General Clauses Act, 1897,Clause 16.
