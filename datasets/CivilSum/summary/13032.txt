Haryana Fisheries Department (State Group "C") Rules, 1979 - Promotion - If there is no specified method to regulate promotions, the seniority-cum-merit principle will be used, and the senior employee carries merit unless found unfit or ineligible for promotion.
[Paras 10 & 11]
Adverse confidential reports - Promotion - Uncommunicated adverse remarks should not serve as a basis for promoting an employee - The reason for conveying adverse remarks should enable the employee to improve and reach expected efficiency - It is necessary to communicate ACRs promptly, and late conveyance is of no significance regardless of its period.
[Paras 9 & 11]
Haryana Fisheries Department (State Group "C") Rules, 1979 - Administrative instructions - Promotion - Criteria prescribed in administrative instruction issued under Article 309 of the Constitution will have the force of law if there is no specified method in the Rules - Govt. is entitled to release administrative instructions.
