The Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules from 1965 fall under the definition of 'law' in Section 2(g) of the Punjab Reorganisation Act, 1966, and thus are applicable in the UT Chandigarh services until modified by the Punjab Recruitment of Ex-servicemen Rules, 1982.
The High Court and the Tribunal's ruling that these rules do not apply in the UT Chandigarh has been overturned, and the Conditions of Service of Union Territory of Chandigarh Employees Rules from 1966, specifically Rules 2, 3, and 4, will now apply.
