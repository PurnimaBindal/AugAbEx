As per Constitution of India, Article 16, recruitment applications are invited for a post with prescribed qualifications.
If a candidate does not fulfill the necessary qualifications before the application closing date, but acquires them before the interview date, that candidate is not considered eligible for appointment.
[Paras 7, 8 and 9]
If a candidate did not have the required qualifications at the time of application, but had acquired them before the interview date and was still selected, their appointment would not be valid.
However, in cases where thirteen years have passed since the appointment, inter se seniority will not be disturbed.
[Paras 7, 8 and 9]
