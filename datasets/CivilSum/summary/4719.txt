Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay/laches - The petitioner in this case was stopped at the Efficiency Bar in 1969, but was allowed to cross it in 1989 with effect from November 1986.
Despite the cause of action accruing to the petitioner in 1969, he did not file a writ petition until August 1993.
The delay was deemed inordinate and the petitioner's claim that the action was violative of principles of natural justice was rejected on the ground of delay.
