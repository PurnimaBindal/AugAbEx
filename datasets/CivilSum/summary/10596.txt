Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Criminal proceedings - Acquittal in Criminal proceedings - Termination - The criminal court found that the petitioner was not responsible for the goods shortage reported on the relevant dates in MARKFED and yet was terminated.
The co-accused employee, who was in charge on these dates, has already been re-instated.
The constitutional rights of citizens under Articles 14 and 16 for equal treatment during employment and punishment apply equally.
The petitioner was therefore ordered to be re-instated with all benefits and arrears of salary.
