Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - The petitioner, who was found medically fit upon his enrolment in the army on 2471968, was boarded out from service on 31.7.1996 due to Hypertension.
Despite having a 20% disability assessed, the petitioner's claim for disability pension was rejected by CCDA (Pension) who did not examine the petitioner before making their decision.
However, as the 20% disability suffered by the petitioner is attributable to army service, the petitioner is entitled to disability pension.
The respondents have been directed to release the benefits of disability pension to the petitioner.
