Constitution of India, Article 226 - APSRTC Employees (Conduct) Regulations, 1963, Regulation 28 - Removal - Misconduct - The issued show cause notice on 241998, and the explanation of petitioner was received on 3041998.
The order of removal was passed within three hours, which is not enough time to consider all aspects and evidence.
Delinquent employee may be unintentionally prejudiced by such a short notice.
The order is set aside, and the competent authority is directed to consider the material placed on record and pass new orders.
[Paras 7 & 8]
