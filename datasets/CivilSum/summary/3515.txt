Departmental Proceedings - Stay of - Pendency of criminal proceedings - Disciplinary proceedings must not interfere with criminal proceedings, especially if they are founded on the same set of facts.
If the departmental proceedings are based only on a criminal complaint lodged by a rival union president facing prosecution for the same incident, it is preferable to postpone departmental proceedings until after the conclusion of the criminal trial, especially since no independent enquiry was initiated by the bank.
[Paras 14 and 15]  B. Departmental Proceedings - Stay of - During pendency of criminal proceedings - Departmental proceedings and proceedings in a criminal case can proceed simultaneously, though separately.
However, if they are based on the same facts and the criminal charge is serious and complex, the departmental proceedings should be stayed until the criminal case is concluded.
The nature of the offence, the evidence collected during the investigation, and the charge sheet will determine whether the criminal charge is serious and complex.
If the criminal case is delayed, the departmental proceedings can be resumed to be concluded as soon as possible.
[Para 11]  C. Departmental Proceedings - Stay of - Pendency of criminal proceedings - There is no formula for determining whether departmental proceedings should be stayed during the pendency of a criminal case.
The two proceedings can go on simultaneously unless they are based on the same set of facts and evidence.
