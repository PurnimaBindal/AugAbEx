Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - U.P.
Regularisation of Adhoc Appointments (on posts within purview of Public Service Commission Rules, 1979, Rule 4 - Adhoc appointment - Regularisation - Petition for regularisation of adhoc appointment on temporary posts - Public Service Commission advertises regular posts - Ad hoc appointees do not apply or file a petition for interview - Provisions of Rule 4 for direct regularisation not met - No relief can be granted to petitioners.
