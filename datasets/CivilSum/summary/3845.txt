West Bengal University of Animal and Fishery Sciences Act, 1935, Section 34(1), Clauses 105(3) and 108(2) - Appointment - Vitiation - Post of Lecturers for Veterinary Medicine and Public Health advertised - Clause 105(3) requires preparation of a panel of 3 candidates as per merit - Panel not prepared - Two posts to be filled - Selection vitiated for non-preparation of two separate panels.
[Para 7]

Reservation - University authorities arbitrarily and illegally reserved one post for SC candidate - Advertisement was bad in law since it failed to notify reservation of post subjectwise and for not indicating the particular post so reserved.
[Paras 8 and 15]

Age Relaxation - Selection Committee is empowered to recommend age relaxation for in-service candidate - Unless rules are amended conferring express power to relax the age, the resolution by the university cannot override the statutory regulations - Selection Committee can only recommend and cannot grant relaxation of its own accord.
[Paras 16, 17 and 21]

Written test - Interaction with the Selection Committee requires a written test - Selection based only on oral interview without a written test is contrary to the Statute - Therefore, it is declared illegal.
[Para 29]
