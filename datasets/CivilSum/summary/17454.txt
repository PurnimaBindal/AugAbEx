The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, discusses the rules for direct appointment, including appointments made in urgency.
In the case presented, the Municipal Authorities were forced to make appointments in the place of Safai Karamcharis who had gone on strike.
None of the petitioners offered to join, and it was not a case of the petitioners being ignored during the appointment of fresh individuals.
The court found that the action taken was not in violation of Articles 14 or 16, and furthermore, cannot direct Municipal Authorities to create more posts and adjust the petitioners.
