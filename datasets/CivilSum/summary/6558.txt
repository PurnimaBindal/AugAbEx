Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Civil Services (General and Common Conditions of Services) Rules, 1994, Rule 17 - Recruitment - Selection for the post of Ayurvedic Medical Officer - The Board rejected the candidacy of the petitioner due to the lack of a specified qualification, namely Matric with Punjabi.
However, the decision in Roopa Sharma v. State of Punjab and others, 1998(1) RSJ 494 led to the Board being directed to consider the petitioner's candidacy along with other eligible individuals.
