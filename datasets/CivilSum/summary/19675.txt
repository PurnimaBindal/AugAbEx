Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Family pension and arrears of salary - Opportunity of hearing - Employee had remained under suspension for a prolonged duration - However, even after over 9 years, inquiry proceedings had not been completed - The employee passed away during this suspension period - Since the petitioner-widow's interests were likely to be impacted, she should have been given an opportunity to be heard - The order treating the suspension period as 'leave of the kind due' cannot stand - The appropriate authority must determine the amount due to petitioner and issue orders regarding the payment of family pension after hearing her.
