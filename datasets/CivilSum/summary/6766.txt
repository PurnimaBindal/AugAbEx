Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - Revaluation - Effect of - Denial of admission to MBA course based on petitioner's passing of revaluation exam - Convenor's actions deemed illegal and obnoxious - Purpose of revaluation is to correct errors committed by Valuation Officers - Vice-Chancellor of respondent-University must take steps to ensure bright students' careers are not destroyed at the whim of Valuation Officers - Respondent directed to consider petitioner's case for admission to MBA course based on her rank in appropriate college, as long as she meets other eligibility criteria - If all seats are filled, University must create a supernumerary seat for petitioner, and if permission is needed from AICTE, the University shall request it.
