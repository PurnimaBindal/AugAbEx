Under Section 28(b) of the Administrative Tribunals Act of 1985, a workman employed by an Indian Council of Agricultural Research Central Government Undertaking can seek redress for a service-related dispute before either the CAT or the Industrial Tribunal.
[Para 7] B.
In examining a witness who had not supervised the probationer's performance, the Indian Council of Agricultural Research's management violated Rule 5 of the Temporary Service Rules of 1965.
As such, the Tribunal's order to set aside the termination and direct reinstatement of the probationer was proper and legally sound.
