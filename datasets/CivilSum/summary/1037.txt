According to Section 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, the Labor Court does not have unlimited power to interfere with the punishment of an employer.
In a case of misappropriation, the amount of money involved is not relevant, and thus, the reduction of punishment from dismissal to stoppage of three increments for a bus conductor who collected fare from two passengers but did not issue tickets was not proportional.
Consequently, the dismissal of the bus conductor was upheld.
