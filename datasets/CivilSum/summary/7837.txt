Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 1952, Section 7(1) - Employees Family Pension Scheme, 1971, Para 32 - Pensionary benefits - Denial of - Petitioner was not granted pension due to the fact that he did not serve for more than 10 years - Although the Labour Court had declared his termination illegal and ordered reinstatement, the Petitioner's past service prior to reinstatement was not taken into account - However, it is established that if removal is held illegal, then the past service of an employee must be deemed as continuous service.
