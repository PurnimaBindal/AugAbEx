In Section 8 of the Central Industrial Security Force Act (50 of 1968), dismissal from service is discussed in relation to unauthorized absence.
In this case, the petitioner did not inform his superior of his sickness and also did not provide a medical certificate.
The evidence on record only showed that the petitioner did not take any diet from the mess and absented from the unit without authorization.
Based on the appreciation of this evidence, the order of termination was found to not be disproportionate.
