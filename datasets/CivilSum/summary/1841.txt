Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Right of selected candidates - Eligibility - The mere act of selection does not grant an indefeasible right to appointment.
However, the appointing authority must make a genuine and well-reasoned decision not to appoint.
The appointing authority cannot arbitrarily disregard the recommendations of the selecting agency made on the government's requisition.
Even if there are complaints about some candidates' selection that are correct, the entire selection cannot be disregarded.
Only ineligible candidates will not receive appointment letters.
[Paras 10 to 14]
