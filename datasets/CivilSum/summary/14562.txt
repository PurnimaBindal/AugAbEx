The Punjab Government National emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rules 2 and 4 pertain to military service benefit.
The petitioner in this case was denied the benefit due to executive instructions issued after the proclamation of National emergency, which restricted the benefit only to those who joined the Armed Forces on or after that date.
However, denying the benefit on this ground is not acceptable as government cannot modify or abridge the provisions of Rule 2 through executive instructions.
The denial of the benefit was wholly irrelevant, and the court issued directions to grant the petitioner the benefit of military service after hearing all affected parties.
