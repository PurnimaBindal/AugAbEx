Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Reserved category - B Tech/BE Courses - In the event that certain reserved category candidates are selected on merit for B Tech/BE courses, they will occupy general category seats rather than reserved seats.
Then, general category seats must be filled first and respondents have not complied with this requirement.
This results in prejudice to the petitioner who can rightfully assert a violation of Articles 14 and 16.
