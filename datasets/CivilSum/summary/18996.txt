Under Rules 2 and 3 of the Karnataka Civil Services (Automatic Grant of Special Promotion to Senior Scale of Pay) Rules, 1991, and Rule 3 of the Karnataka Civil Services (Time Bound Advancement) Rules, 1953, selection time scales were introduced to employees who had completed ten years of service for the corporation in 1983.
The benefit was introduced in 1984 and the 1991 rules introduced a scheme for the grant of it to employees.
Pleas arguing that the benefit under the 1991 Rules would not be available to those receiving it under the 1983 Rules were rejected as unconstitutional according to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
