According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Section 2A of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 and Section 25FF of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, an employee should receive a continuous service to be deemed eligible for gratuity.
In a case where a private respondent had a break in service due to a company closure, it was held that the employee was not terminated and was still in continuous service despite the break.
The closure was not valid and did not render the employee's service terminated.
Therefore, the employee was entitled to their gratuity benefits under the Act of 1972.
