Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(C) - Indian Railway Estt.
Code II, Rule 2043 of (F.R.
53) - Subsistence allowance - The petitioner, while suspended, should have received subsistence allowance equal to 50% of their salary for the first 12 months.
This rate could be increased or decreased by up to 25% depending on the responsibilities of the delinquent official to prolong disciplinary proceedings against them.
In this case, there is no evidence that the petitioner was responsible for prolonging proceedings.
The Railway Authority has also admitted that the enquiry was delayed due to their own fault.
The order of the Labour Court, which awards only 25% of the wages after 12 months instead of 75%, is unreasonable and is not supported by any evidence or rationale.
Therefore, the order of the Labour Court has been modified.
