Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 2(9) - `Employee' - Definition - The work that an employee does is not limited to factory premises - Being in charge of supervising the work of others is an indicator of being an "employee" - Even if the supervision is not ongoing, it is sufficient - The petitioner, a match factory, claimed that frame-filling workers are not employees because they are appointed by the factory - However, the appellant had the power to supervise and reject work that was not done properly - This established a relationship of master and servant between the appellant and its workers - The ESI court ordered the factory to pay contributions, and this decision was upheld.
