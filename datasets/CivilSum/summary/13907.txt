Article 311 of the Constitution of India and Rule 37 of the Rajasthan Civil Services (Police Subordinate Service) Rules, 1989 state that probationers who fail to discharge duties satisfactorily can be discharged prior to completion of their probationary period.
[Paras 11 and 12]

The use of the word "Nishkashit" in the order does not necessarily imply dismissal.
The nature of the order should be determined based on the proceedings leading to it, and in this case, it was the outcome of an assessment of the petitioner's service, resulting in the conclusion that he was not suitable for the position.
Therefore, it was an order of discharge simplicitor.
[Paras 20 and 21]

An order to dismiss a constable during his probationary period can be issued by the appointing authority if it is determined that his or her service is unsatisfactory, without the need for an enquiry.
In this case, the petitioner's repeated absences were taken into account, and the appointing authority concluded that he was not suitable for the position.
Therefore, the order was the result of an assessment of the petitioner's service and not any ulterior motive.
[Paras 17 and 18]
