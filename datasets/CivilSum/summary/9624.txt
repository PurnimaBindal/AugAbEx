Constitution of India, Articles 136 and 226 Education/Diploma certificate - Recognition of Diploma Certificates issued by one state in another state - Previously-recognized Diploma Certificate in Fine Arts and Crafts Teacher Training Examination issued by the Department of Industrial Training, Haryana - De-recognized later on in the State of Punjab - Despite de-recognition, the petitioner’s claim for compassionate appointment as Arts and Crafts teacher cannot be denied as the diploma was recognized at the time of completion of the course and application for the job.
