Constitution of India, Article 226 - Hindu Adoption and Maintenance Act, 1956, Section 16 - Compassionate Appointment - Respondent claimed to be the adopted son of deceased employee - Adoption deed not signed by any person giving child in adoption nor registered - No presumption under Section 16 can be drawn relating to validity of adoption - Single Judge of HC rightly held respondent not eligible for compassionate appointment - Judgment of Single Judge upheld.
