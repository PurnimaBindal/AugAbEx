Constitution of India, Article 226 - J & K Daily Rated Workers/Work charge Employees Regularisation Rules, 1994, Rules 7, 8 - Regularisation - The petitioners, who were appointed as daily wage workers on 2161993, were not allowed to mark attendance by Respondent No. 2 and were later terminated without indication of the date of termination - As per Rule 8, employees who complete seven years of service are entitled to regularisation - The phrase "subsequent financial years" must be given full meaning - The Rule making authority intends to confirm and regularise any employee working as a Daily Rated Worker as Work Charge employees who has completed seven years of service and to permit them to continue working as such until they complete seven years of service - The employee can then be absorbed - The petition has been allowed.
