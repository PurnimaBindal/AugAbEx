Life Insurance Corporation of India Staff Regulations, 1960, Regulation 39(1)(f) - Removal from service - The petitioner is charged with submitting a false bill claiming reimbursement for availing LTC.
There is a letter written by the chief ticket inspector of Railways to LIC of India showing that the petitioner and his family had undertaken a journey in a train.
Upon inquiry by vigilance department from railway authorities, the letter was not found to be fake.
The disciplinary authority did not consider this material piece of evidence that was capable of upsetting the finding of the Inquiry Officer, and the order of punishment is vitiated.
[Paras 11, 15, 16 & 17]
