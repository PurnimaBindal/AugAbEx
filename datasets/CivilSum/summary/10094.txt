According to Article 311(2) of the Constitution of India, both temporary and permanent members of services are treated equally, regardless of whether they hold temporary or permanent posts.
Even though the petitioner's appointment as a Clerk was temporary, he fulfilled all the requirements of being a member of the State's civil service and held a civil post under the State.
This means that the provisions of Article 311(2) of the Constitution are pertinent to the petitioner.
