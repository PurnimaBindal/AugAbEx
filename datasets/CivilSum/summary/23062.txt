Associate Professor - Allegation of Discrimination after the denial to pursue PhD course - According to norms, permission can be denied if the staff strength in the department would go below 70% - High Court observed inconsistency in following norms in some cases - Court held that the wrong order cannot be a basis for equality - Non-following of norms in some cases cannot justify departure - Respondent neglected to continue program even after being asked to follow the High Court's order - High Court's decision is overturned.
