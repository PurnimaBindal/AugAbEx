The Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 states the appointment, qualifications, and pay scale of T Mates.
Although some were recruited with a qualification of ITI and some with the qualification of Matric with ITI, the Executive Engineer incorporated them in various notifications sent to the Employment Exchange.
The Superintending Engineer or Executive Engineer did not commit any illegality in granting them the benefit of pay in the grade of Rs. 950-1400 and Rs. 1200-2040.
Once the benefit of revised pay scales has been given to them, it is not open to the respondents to take any action for withdrawing that benefit.
The notices issued by the respondents, seeking revision of pay of the petitioners, were declared illegal and quashed.
[Para 7] B.
Even though statutory rules do not prescribe qualifications for recruitment to T-Mates, the appointments made by concerned Xen after sending a requisition to Employment Exchange or otherwise on the basis of specified qualifications is sufficiently indicative of the fact that qualifications have been prescribed by executive decision.
The mere absence of statutory rules cannot lead to an inference that no qualifications have been prescribed.
The Executive has the power to issue administrative instructions/orders for regulating recruitment and conditions of service of civil posts in the absence of statutory rules.
Article 166(3) confers such administrative powers on the Government.
