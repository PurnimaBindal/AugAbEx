Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1970, Section 19 - Promotion - Clerks of the Bank aspiring for promotion to the post of junior Management Grade (Scale I) - Settlement with Bank - Promotees and direct recruits must fill vacancies proportionately at 75% and 25% respectively - Bank will create a candidate panel for promotion consideration - The Bank cannot conduct new tests or create a fresh panel unless all candidates in the previous panel have been adjusted to the promoted post.
