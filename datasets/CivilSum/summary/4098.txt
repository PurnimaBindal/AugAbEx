Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Court Martial - Petitioner's award by Court Martial deemed disproportionate and unduly harsh - Petitioner's submissions were considered but ultimately it was up to the Court Martial to accept or reject them - Punishment has since been significantly reduced and no further interference is necessary - Competent authority had punished petitioner with reduction in rank, one year RI, and dismissal but later commuted to severe reprimand, set aside dismissal, and reinstated petitioner in service - Court held that HC or Tribunal cannot normally substitute their conclusion on penalty and impose a different penalty unless punishment shocks their conscience.
