Constitution of India, Article 226 - Increments - Petitioner was reinstated with continuity of service and no back wages - The period between 1211979 and 11.4.1983 is considered as on leave of the kind due - Respondent treated the period as leave without pay - The increment benefit was not granted - Petitioner was not informed about the impugned order altering the earlier order regarding increment - The impugned order is nullified.
