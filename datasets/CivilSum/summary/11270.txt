Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Interview for the post of Assistant Teacher - Holding the interview is valid and legal if the majority of members of the selection committee participated, despite no nominee being placed by Panchayat Samiti despite due notice being given.

Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservation in a single post cadre - Reservation on account of rotation of roster in a single cadre post is not permissible within the Constitutional framework as it could exclusively reserve the post for members of backward classes, resulting in the total exclusion of general candidates.
