Section 40(a) and 130 of the Army Act pertains to the Summary Court Martial.
If an accused admits guilt, one must receive punishment as determined by the Summary Court Martial.
However, it is important that the accused must be given an opportunity to challenge its constitution.
Failure to comply with Section 130 is an infirmity that can invalidate the proceedings.
As such, in the case AIR 1987 SC 2386, the order of conviction was set aside due to non-compliance of the mandate in Section 130, as noted in paragraphs 6 and 8.
