Industrial Disputes Act, (14 of 1947), Section 11-A - Quantum and proportionality of punishment - False declaration made to obtain employment - The workman submitted a false application, claiming work experience that he did not possess - Since the workman provided this information in the application and submitted it, it is his burden to prove he had work experience - The workman is found guilty of misrepresentation and considered unsuitable for continued employment - Dismissal from service is justified - The court cannot interfere or show undue sympathy in such cases.
