Constitution of India, Articles 14, and 311 - Punjab School Education Board Employees (Punishment and Appeal) Regulations, 1978, Regulations 5, 6, and 14 - Natural justice - The punishing authority must issue a notice of disagreement with the findings of the inquiring authority that favors the delinquent employee to comply with the principles of natural justice.
Failure to do so makes the punishment order unsustainable.
