Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 141 - Judgments precedents of the Courts - Retroactive and future effect of judgments - Reservation - In the public interest, courts only give prospective effect to judgments to avoid unsettled matters - R.K. Sabharwal and others v. State of Punjab, 1995(2) SCT 646 gave retroactive effect only because matter remained under consideration of Court from selection date - Selections and promotions/appointments made prior to Sabharwal's case would operate reservation rule based on total strength in cadre, and not just future vacancies.
