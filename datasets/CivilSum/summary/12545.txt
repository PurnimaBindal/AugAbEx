Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 311 - Embezzlement - Departmental proceedings initiated - An FIR alleging embezzlement of Bank funds was included in a charge-sheet served to the petitioner for the purpose of holding a departmental enquiry.
An Enquiry Officer was appointed, and most of the entries are beyond one year from the appointment of the Enquiry Officer.
Even if some entries are within one year, such timing would not make any difference.
This is because, as stated in the criminal case charge, no charge has been framed against the petitioner.
As a result, departmental enquiry proceedings cannot be stopped, and the inquiry proceedings should not be interfered with.
The petition is dismissed.
[Paras 4, 5, and 6]
