In accordance with Civil Procedure Code, Order 47, Rule 1, an application for amendment cannot be accepted before the passing of the order.
Moreover, new grounds are not allowed to be taken during the review application.
[Paras 8 & 9]
As per Civil Procedure Code, Order 47, Rule 1, an application for review is not maintainable if an appeal against the same judgment is already pending.
The review application will not be considered after an appeal has been filed.
[Paras 9, 10 & 11]
