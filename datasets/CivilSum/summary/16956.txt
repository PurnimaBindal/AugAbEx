Pension Regulations for the Army in 1991 (Part-I) - Special pension - Family pension - Suicide - A soldier who dies by suicide through consumption of poison and whose death is not due to or worsened by military service is ineligible for special family pension - Nonetheless, if other criteria are met, the ordinary family members of the deceased are entitled to the pension - The petitioners were deemed eligible for ordinary family pension, and the pension was paid - Cancellation of their pensions due to their ineligibility for special family pension was deemed illegal, and their pensions were reinstated.
