According to Section 3 of the Andhra Pradesh Public Employment (Regulation of Age of Superannuation) Act, 1984 and Rule 231 of the Hyderabad Civil Services Rules, the role of a Traffic Inspector Grade II in the Andhra Pradesh Road Transport Department does not qualify as that of a workman as per the definition under Rule 231.
As the duties of the Traffic Inspector Grade II are of a supervisory nature, an individual holding that position is not eligible to continue in service until the age of 60 years, as per Para 7.
