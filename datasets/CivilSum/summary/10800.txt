Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Regular study - Petitioner's admission to B.Sc.
(Nursing) Course rejected due to not passing 10+2 as a regular student but from National Institute of Open School - The term `regular candidate' doesn't imply that a candidate needs to attend classes physically every day - The term `regular' means complying with the law, rule or established practice - If multiple interpretations are possible, the court should prefer the one which supports the constitutional validity of the rule - Distinction between studying by going to school regularly or by studying at home is not based on any intelligible differentiation.
