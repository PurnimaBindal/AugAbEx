Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Disability pension - Discharge from Military service due to contracting eye disease Ani-sometropiat Amblypia - Under Rule 7(b) of the Defence Instructions governing the disability pension, which was amended by letter dated 1101951, there is a presumption that a disability is caused by military service unless a note or medical opinion expressly stating the absence of the disability due to military service is present at the time of military acceptance - No such documentation is available on record - The denial of disability pension on the grounds that the disability was not caused by military service is therefore illegal - The authorities are directed to provide the necessary benefits within two months.
[Paras 3 to 8]
