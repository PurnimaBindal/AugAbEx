Under the "Advocates Act, 1961" and the "Rules of the Bar Council of India", a petitioner who has completed a three-year law course at a Banglore University-affiliated college and holds a law degree from the same university cannot be denied enrolment just because the affiliation is not approved by BCI As long as the candidate fulfills the conditions specified in Sections 24(1) of the Advocates Act and is not disqualified under Section 24-A, he is entitled to enrolment, regardless of whether he meets all of the requirements prescribed by BCI.
