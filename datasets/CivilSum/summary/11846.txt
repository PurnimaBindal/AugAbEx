Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservation for Scheduled Castes - Roster points - It is the percentage of reservation that needs to be maintained, not the roster points.
The roster is meant to operate only in contingencies.
When the percentage of reservation is complete in a particular cadre, the roster will cease to operate.
No more benefits can be claimed on the basis of roster points by persons of Scheduled Castes because if many posts are vacated by General Category candidates and Schedule Caste candidates are given the benefit of reservation every time, the percentage of reservation will increase.
