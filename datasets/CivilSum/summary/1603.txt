Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate appointment - The Industrial Tribunal directed the appointment of the respondent on compassionate grounds due to the management's failure to communicate reasons regarding the status of the respondent's application.
However, the Tribunal did not examine the respondent's entitlement to the appointment and could not order the petitioner to appoint the respondent.
Consequently, the award's directive was overturned and modified, with the petitioner being instructed to review the respondent's application for compassionate appointment.
