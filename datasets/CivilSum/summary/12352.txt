Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10(1) - Reference - Delay and laches - Refusal of claim for reference of dispute to labour court due to unexplained inordinate delay of 12 years in raising a demand is not considered unjust or improper - Appropriate authority can take a fair decision if there is an unreasonable delay - Law takes care of vigilant and needy - Relief cannot be claimed by those who delay for years without any explanation - Reappointment for two weeks after more than 11 years does not allow the workman to challenge his 12-year-old retrenchment - Appointment for only two weeks and non-continuance of such appointment does not violate any law.
