According to Section 14 of the Juvenile Justice Act of 1986, if a juvenile is charged with an offense under Sections 450, 376, 506 I.P.C. & Section 3(1)(XII) of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, they are to be tried by a Juvenile Court as outlined in the Juvenile Justice Act.
