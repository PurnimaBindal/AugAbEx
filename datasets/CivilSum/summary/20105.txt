Fundamental Rules, Rules 22(I)(a)(1), 26 and 27 - Fixation of pay on promotion - Pay to be fixed one step above - Ad hoc or officiation - Anomaly in pay scale of junior and seniors due to application of FR 22 or due to officiating service in higher scale - According to Government instructions dated 421966, only anomalies arising from the application of FR 22 and not from officiating promotion should be considered, and pay should be fixed taking into account the pay drawn while officiating- Officiating promotions are given only in administrative exigencies and are essentially a local arrangement - Seniority on an all-India level cannot be implemented while making local arrangements for a short-term by giving officiating promotions - The pay increase of junior employees cannot be considered an anomaly due to FR 22 and is not covered under FR 22(I)(a)(1).
