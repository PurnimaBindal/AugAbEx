Rule 19(1) of the Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeals) Rules, 1965, regarding Dismissal and Probation, requires the determination of the matter till the appeal is disposed of, despite the fact that the petitioner was released on probation and the appeal against dismissal remains undecided after more than 22 years.
The controversy is liable to be re-determined.
Rule 19(1) of the Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeals) Rules, 1965, states that despite more than 22 years passing, the appeal remains undecided which warrants a direction to the authority to decide it within one month.
The respondents are to pay the petitioner Rs. 10,000/- for apathy in not deciding the appeal.
