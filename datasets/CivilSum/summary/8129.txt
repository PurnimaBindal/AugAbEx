Jammu and Kashmir Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 17(d) - Appointment - Character verification - The relevant agency must present material for competent authority to pass a speaking order on appointment - Mere registration of FIR and submission of report by the police against the candidate is not enough to deny public appointment - The police verification may contain relevant or irrelevant material, so the appointing authority must consider the full material and pass a final order to ensure that the candidate's fundamental rights are not violated in an arbitrary manner.
