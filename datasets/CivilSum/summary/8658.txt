Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 14 - Punjab Municipal Accounts Code, Chapter V, Rule V-7 - Equal pay for equal work - The Octroi Clerks in the Municipal Committee must receive benefits of National, Festival and Gazetted Holidays, Saturdays and Sundays off, Restricted holidays and lunch break etc. at par with the employees working in the office side - All Clerks and Peons are borne on common seniority lists and some of them work in the office while others are posted at barriers - The Octroi staff should receive ten compensatory holidays in addition to the normal casual leaves on a uniform basis to compensate them for national and Gazetted holidays - The Municipal Councils should rotate their employees from the office to the barriers and vice-versa as per the rules contained in the Punjab Municipal Code.
