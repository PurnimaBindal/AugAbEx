Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - CSTC Employees Service Regulations, 1960, Regulation 10 - Reducing a person's rank after giving them successive promotions and posting them in a particular rank violates natural justice - A person cannot be demoted without an opportunity to be heard or provided a show cause notice - In a case where a petitioner was promoted to the post of Charge Hand and remained in the position for an extended period, reduction in rank for the reason of incorrect promotion is deemed illegal - The order was quashed, and the petitioner directed to be reinstated to their original position.
