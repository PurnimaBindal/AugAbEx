Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work - Ration money allowance - Allowance attached to special duties - Ration money allowance is only available to CRPF personnel who opt for combatisation or work in operational and sensitive areas - Terms and conditions of service for combatised personnel governed by special Act and Rules while non-combatised staff governed by Rules under Article 309 of the Constitution - Special hazards attached to duties in operational areas - Non-combatant staff also paid ration money allowance in such areas - This policy is not discriminatory as those who don't opt for hazardous duties cannot claim the benefits attached to it - Unequals cannot be treated as equals and vice versa.
