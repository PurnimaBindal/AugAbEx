Under the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 Section 21, applicants who wish to set aside the confirmation of appointment must file their application within one year of the appointment of another person.
However, if a medical certificate is submitted, showing that the applicant was ill for a certain period, the delay in filing the application can be considered minimal by excluding that period.
In this case, it was held that the applicant presented sufficient cause for the delay and the appeal was allowed.
[Para 5] B.
According to Articles 311 and 14 of the Indian Constitution, the refusal to appoint a qualified candidate solely on the basis that their cousin brother works in the same office as a peon is an arbitrary exercise of power and violates Article 14.
The appeal was allowed.
