Constitution of India, Article 226 - Jurisdiction of High Court - It is held that the authority of the judiciary to review under Article 226 must not substitute the viewpoint of domestic Tribunals, but only review their decisions under exceptional circumstances.
If such circumstances exist, re-examination of quality and quantity of evidence is not a matter of concern.
Moreover, discretionary power should not be exercised when the Tribunal has already delivered justice via its evidence and natural justice principles.
