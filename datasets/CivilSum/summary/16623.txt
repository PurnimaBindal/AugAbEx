Constitution of India, Article 14 and 16 - Draft Rules - Eligibility - Appointment by promotion and direct recruitment - If the draft Regulations remove appointment by promotion and mandate filling posts solely by direct recruitment, no claim of appointment by promotion can be made contrary to the rules - The petitioner cannot raise objections of any injustice or partiality if he was eligible to apply for the advertised posts but chose not to.
[Paras 11, 12]
