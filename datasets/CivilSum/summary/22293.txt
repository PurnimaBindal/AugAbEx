Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion - Sealed cover procedure - An FIR was lodged against the respondent on 3091988, and the decision to charge sheet the respondent was made by the Departmental Authority on 20111989.
The Departmental Promotion Committee followed the sealed cover procedure on 23.11.1989 due to the disciplinary proceedings against the appellant.
The grievance that a charge sheet was issued to the appellant on 1.8.1990 long after the Departmental Promotion Committee met is not tenable since the charges were already crystallised in the FIR.
Thus, it was held that the sealed cover procedure was rightly followed in the case.
1991(3) SC 317 is distinguished, while JT 1983(2) SC 695 is relied upon.
