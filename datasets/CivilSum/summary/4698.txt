The Board's employees are subject to the Civil Service Rules under the Karnataka Urban Water Supply and Drainage Board Act, 1974, Sections 4 and 69.
An employee was sent to a State corporation in accordance with the Rules and the Board's discretion, under a 1976 Notification that applied CCR to Board employees, which is not illegal.
[Para 5]

The Karnataka Civil Services Rules, Rule 419, state that the expression "Govt. Servant" includes employees of the Board.
An order issued under the CCR that sends a Board employee to a Corporation on the grounds that he is not a Govt. employee subject to CCR is not valid, as per the Constitution of India's Articles 226 and 309.
