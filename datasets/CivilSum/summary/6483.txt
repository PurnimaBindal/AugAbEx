Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 4(5) - Gratuity - There is no limit on the rate and total amount of gratuity payable under the Act - The Act provides for a minimum rate of gratuity to be paid - Both employer and employee can agree to a higher rate - If the settlement does not limit the gratuity receivable, the Act cannot impose a limit.

Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 4(5) - Gratuity - The gratuity amount should be computed based on the wages legally payable to the employee, not on the actual wages paid at a lower rate by the employer.
