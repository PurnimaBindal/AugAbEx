Punjab Civil Service (Promotions of Stenographers and Steno-typists) Rules, 1961, Rules 2 and 3 - Promotion - The post of Assistant, Head Assistant, Deputy Superintendent and Superintendent are all available for promotion to Stenographers - If the administration wishes to determine principles of promotion for Senior Scale Stenographers to Superintendent, it must consult the Punjab Public Service Commission in line with Article 320 of the Constitution - A Senior Scale Stenographer with five years experience is deemed eligible for promotion to Superintendent - Rule 3 does not apply, and conditions of test and experience as Assistant are not required.
