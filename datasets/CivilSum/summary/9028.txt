According to Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, retrenchment can be done if an ad hoc appointment was made for 89 days, and termination is done in accordance with the terms of the appointment.
The appointment was just for a stop gap arrangement, as the petitioner did not work for 240 days.
The management was not found guilty of unfair labour practices and thus the termination was not in violation of Section 25-F [Para 6]

Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act states that backwages cannot be granted in cases where a demand notice was given after a long delay.
Granting of backwages in such cases is not justified.
