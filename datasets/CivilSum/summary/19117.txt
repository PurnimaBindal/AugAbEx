Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 3.17-A(f)(i) - Pension - Qualifying service - Joginder Singh's case (1998(1) SCT 795) struck down the words "half the period of service of such person paid from contingency" in Sub Clause (i) of Clause (f) of rule 3.17-A, which stated that the service period for which salary was paid from contingency fund will count towards qualifying service.
