Bombay Civil Service Rules, 1971, Rule 161(1)(a)(i) - Order of premature retirement with option to join at reverted post - A Forester petitioner was refused to join a transferred post as the officer against whom he was posted got a stay.
Later, the department gave an offer to join duty on a reverted post but the petitioner refused to accept reversion.
As a result, the order of compulsory retirement was passed and the petitioner challenged it.
The report by review committee was found to be mala fide, illegal, erroneous, unjust and not sustainable in the eyes of law as it contained no detail of consideration or any date.
The order was passed with an oblique motive to defeat the claim of the petitioner and the respondent could not point out any Statutory provision of law under which petitioner could be reverted.
As a consequence, the impugned order was quashed and the petitioner should be treated in service from the date of impugned order till the date of superannuation.
Additionally, arrears of salary along with 12% interest should be paid to him within three months.
[Paras 12, 13 and 14]
