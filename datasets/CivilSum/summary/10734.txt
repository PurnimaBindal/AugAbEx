Indian Contract Act, 1872, Section 11 - Contract of Service - A contract of service between an employer and employee is a matter between them - However, individuals appointed to government service receive both contractual and statutory protection, although their initial entry into service is the result of a contractual relationship.
[Para 12]
Indian Contract Act, 1872, Section 11 - Majority Act, 1875, Section 3 - Age of majority - Completion of Contract of Service - The day on which a person was born is to be included in the computation of their age as a whole day, and a person must attain the age of majority according to the law as per Section 3 of the Majority Act, 1985 to complete a contract of service.
[Paras 12 to 14]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rules and instructions - Interpretation of law - Employment before attaining the age of majority - Effect on age of retirement - Statutory rules cannot be superseded by any amount of executive order or instruction - If a clear rule exists that contradicts or is inconsistent with an instruction or order, such instruction or order will be invalid and have no legal effect in abridging the rights granted in the rule - Government employees who have completed 40 years of service or attained the age of 58 years must be superannuated in accordance with the existing rule and cannot be retained in service beyond that.
