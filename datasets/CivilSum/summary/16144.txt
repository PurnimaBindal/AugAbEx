The rejected representation for change of entry regarding the date of birth of the respondent has resulted in a civil suit, which the State Government chose to contest, maintaining their initial stand as per their affidavit.
Thus, there was no legal justification for the State Government to suddenly issue an impugned order that changed the entry and rendered the suit as infructuous.
[Para 6] B.
During the pendency of the civil suit, the State Government accepted the respondent's claim and changed his date of birth entry, allowing him to continue in service for another two years.
However, no administrative exigency or fresh material justifies the issuance of the impugned order, which has been deemed arbitrary and quashed.
[Paras 3 and 6] C. The impugned order is in violation of the principles of natural justice as neither the petitioner nor any other party to the civil suit filed by the respondent had been given an opportunity to be heard before it was passed.
Therefore, the order is liable to be struck down.
