The Textile Undertaking (Taking over of Management) Act, 1983, Section 3(7) Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10, 25-F and 33-C(2) - Textile Undertakings (Nationalisation) Ordinance, 1994, Section 5(3) - Liabilities prior to taking over - The N.T.C.
will not take liabilities relating to the period prior to take over - Section 3(7) of the Act is clear and does not allow for any interpretation - According to this section, the Central Government or Custodian will not be liable for any debts incurred by the textile company prior to the appointed day - The Labour Court's order for the payment of wages, bonuses, etc. to workers prior to the takeover is unenforceable against NTC - It is not valid to argue that the phrase "any liability" in Section 3(7) of 1983 Act does not include wages, bonuses, or arrears payable to workers.
