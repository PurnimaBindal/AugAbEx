Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Delay and laches - The fixation of seniority for Government employees is the responsibility of senior officers.
A petitioner belonging to a provincialized cadre is challenging the non-fixation of his seniority, and authorities are objecting on the grounds of delay and laches.
However, such an objection is not maintainable as it is the duty of the authorities to fix the seniority of the petitioner at the right time.
By failing to discharge this duty, they are shirking from their responsibilities under the Rules.
