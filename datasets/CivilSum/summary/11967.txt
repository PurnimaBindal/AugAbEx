Punjab Village Common Lands (Regulation) Act, Sections 5 and 13 - Jurisdiction - A civil court is able to hear suits for declaration when Gram Panchayat's transaction of exchange was made in violation of mandatory provisions of the law in contravention of Section 5, and the dispute is over a site belonging to the Gram Panchayat.
However, due to the provisions of Section 13, the suit is barred.
