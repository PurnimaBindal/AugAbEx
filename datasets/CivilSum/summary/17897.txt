Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Economic and Statistical Organisation (State Service Class III) Rules, 1963 - Punjab Economic and Statistical Organisation (Class III Service) Rules, 1980, Rule 10 - Seniority - Inter se seniority of Computors and Clerks - Prior to October 1980, Clerks and Computors belonged to the same cadre and had one seniority, subject to transfer at the discretion of the Department - Denying the seniority benefit earned by Computors in their prior service, while granting the same to Clerks, would violate Article 14 of the Constitution.
