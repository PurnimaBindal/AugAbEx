Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission to second year (III semester) of 4 year B.E./B.Tech.
Degree courses - Eligibility - The petitioner has met the requirement of obtaining 60% marks, and there is no stipulation that he must pass in either the first or second division for eligibility.
The finding of the learned Single Judge that the petitioner passed the diploma course in the second class and is placed in second class due to not clearing all papers in the first attempt is unsustainable, as per rules such candidates are to be placed in the second class even if they secured above 60%.
To determine eligibility for admission, the total marks of three years should be considered for determining the percentage and grading, and the petitioner is deemed eligible for admission.
The appeal has been allowed.
