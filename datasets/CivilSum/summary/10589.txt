Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission - Education/Classification of Service - PCMS Class II ad hoc service - The government previously regularized those who competed one year's ad hoc service - Sandhawalia Commission submitted report in favour of regularising ad hoc PCMS doctors, which the government accepted - Selection by a departmental Selection Committee is not a legitimate basis for classification - Classification depriving ad hoc doctors from seeking admission as 'in-service' candidates in 60% reserved quota for MD/MS course, held to be unreasonable, unjust, discriminatory and violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - Instructions quashed.
