Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Termination of services - Absence from duty - Delay and laches - Plaintiff, who had joined as "Trained Dai" on 641974 and proceeded on maternity leave on 14.6.1975, had remained absent from duty for 10 years even after the expiry of her leave - Therefore, the plaintiff had abandoned her job and was not entitled to relief in the form of regularisation of service and arrears of pay after 10 years.
