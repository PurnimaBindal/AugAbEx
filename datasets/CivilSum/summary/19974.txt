A person who secured employment against a reserved post with the help of a false Caste Certificate cannot plead equity - his services were terminated after 20 years when the falsity was discovered - the termination was upheld, and he deserves no sympathy from the court.
The very foundation of his appointment vanished - the court would not be justified in exercising equity jurisdiction in his favour.
(Constitution of India, Article 311, Articles 16 and 14).
The court relied on the 1995(1) SCT 261 (SC) case.
