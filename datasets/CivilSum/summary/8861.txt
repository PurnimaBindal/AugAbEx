Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 11-B - Termination - Reinstatement - The Labour Court did not grant continuity of service when ordering reinstatement.
The period spent out of service cannot be counted towards seniority.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Seniority - Regularisation - Length of service - The petitioner's services were terminated while on a daily wage basis.
The Labour Court did not grant continuity of service when directing reinstatement.
Excluding the non-working period, the petitioner did not complete the required length of service for regularisation.
The petition was dismissed as it was not fair to grant continuity and seniority for the period served after the demand notice was sent, which was 6 years after termination.
