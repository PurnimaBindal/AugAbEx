According to Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 9 Rule 13 and Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Sections 17, if a representative of the management becomes incapable of attending court due to an accident, an application for setting aside an ex parte award cannot be rejected for not mentioning the representative's name in the application.
Additionally, the application does not need to be supported by a medical certificate with specifications of the injuries.
The order dismissing the application is not proper, and the award has been set aside, with the employee being compensated with costs.

As per Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 9 Rule 13 and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17, an ex parte award may be passed in cases where the representative of the management is incapable of attending court after cross-examining some witnesses of the workman.
If an application for setting aside an ex parte award is rejected on the ground that some other person could have been deputed in place of the said representative, it is not proper.
As such, the award has been quashed, and the workman compensated with costs.
