According to Railway Protection Force Act, 1957, Section 9(1)(i), and Railway Protection Force Rules, 1959, Rule 44, dismissal from service is an appropriate punishment for negligence of duty when it comes to protecting railway property from theft.
The loss being prevented for the general public and the thieves abandoning the property does not justify reducing the gravity of the employee's misconduct on guard duty.
