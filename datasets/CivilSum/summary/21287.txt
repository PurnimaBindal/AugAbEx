The Madhya Pradesh State Industries (Gazetted) Service Recruitment Rules, 1965 and 1985 - Seniority - Direct recruits given higher seniority than promotees - Promotions from quota meant for direct recruitment against rules - One-day delay in direct recruitments made them senior to promotees.
[Paras 4 and 10]
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Seniority - Direct recruits entitled to claim seniority under the rules - Not necessary to have the affected persons or promotees as parties to the case.
[Paras 14 and 18]
The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Seniority - Delay in applying for final gradation list prepared on 23.12.1988 not considered as laches.
