Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14 and 16 - Cut of date for Pension/Retirement Scheme - Promotion of employees - Article 14 has no application as employees are not of the same class or homogeneous group.
Employer can validly fix a cut-off date for introducing any new pension/retirement scheme or for discontinuance of any existing scheme.
Discrimination arises only if the employer retrospectively fixes a cut-off date arbitrarily thereby dividing a single homogeneous class of pensioners into two group and subjecting them to different treatment.
