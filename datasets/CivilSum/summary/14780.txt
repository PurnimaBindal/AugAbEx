Section 35 of the Punjab Municipal Act states that a resolution passed by the Municipal Committee is required in order for the Executive Officer to file an appeal.
The appeal becomes maintainable as soon as the Committee passes the resolution, and no further authorization is needed from the Committee for the Executive Officer to carry out their administrative duties.
The Officer is bound to carry out the decision of the Committee, and no separate authorization is necessary.
[Paras 9 and 10]
