Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection for promotion - Service record - The competent authority shall present any adverse additional material to the selection committee for the committee to consider its effect.
This material cannot be deemed irrelevant and the selection cannot be invalidated for considering it.
[Para 13]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection for promotion - Favouritism - It is justified for the Tribunal/Court to set aside a promotion selection if there is rank favouritism or a blatant attempt to get a specific candidate promoted.
[Para 13]  C Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection for promotion - Locus standi to challenge - A candidate may have cause to challenge the appointment of a candidate placed below him in the selection panel if he did not challenge the rejection of his own name by the appointing authority.
