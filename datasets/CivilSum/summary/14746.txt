Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 139, 1310 and 1312 - Promotion/Cadre/Creation of new cadres - Two cadres were created for Head Constables, which was not authorized in the rules nor by a competent authority - The petitioners were overlooked and sent for training based on their supposed clerical position - The creation of these cadres holds no significance - The petitioners should have been considered for promotion to Assistant Sub-Inspectors at the same time as their junior Head Constables.
