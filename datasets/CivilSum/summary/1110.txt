The stay application to reserve a seat for a physically handicapped person in PG Medical Courses has been rejected by the court.
Even though the prayer in the stay petition and main petition are the same, the points raised by the writ petitioner can only be considered and decided in the main writ petition as it is pending.
The learned Single Judge has correctly rejected the stay application and has directed the registry to list the case before him for final disposal.
