Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Reserved Category - Qualification- Post of Primary Assistant Teacher - Despite securing more marks than selected candidates, a petitioner who had applied for the post of Primary Assistant Teacher under OBC Category was not given the appointment.
The reason being that she had not studied Hindi at Higher Secondary level - Petition was dismissed on the same grounds by a learned single judge.
However, the petitioner holds a Bachelor of Arts degree with Hindi, and there is no logical reasoning in excluding her.
There is no justification for the distinction made by ignoring a candidate with higher qualification, hence the action of ignoring the petitioner is unwarranted and without any valid basis.
The appeal was allowed accordingly.
