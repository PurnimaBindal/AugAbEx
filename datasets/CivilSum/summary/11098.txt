Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Railway Protection Force Rules, 1959, Sections 44 to 47 - Departmental enquiry/Natural justice/Appeal - Dismissal from service - The enquiry may be dispensed with if it is not practicable to hold one without any opportunity being afforded - The Appellate authority must consider all material on record and provide an opportunity of hearing before rejecting an appeal - Failure to do so would result in an order passed without proper application of mind - The order must be set aside and the Appellate authority must provide a speaking order meeting all points with reasons.
