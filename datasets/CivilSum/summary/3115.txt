"No appointment, promotion or transfer of Presiding Officer of Labour Courts, Industrial Courts or Industrial Tribunal under Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 or Bombay Industrial Relations Act, 1946 can be made without following provisions of Chapter VI of Constitution of India, as indicated in Articles 235 and 236, and in Sections 7, 7-A and 8 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Sections 9 and 10 of the Bombay Industrial Relations Act, 1946.
The decision in 1990(2) Gujarat LH 506 was overruled.
[Paras 5 &7]"
