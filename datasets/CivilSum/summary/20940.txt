Constitution of India, Article 136 - Promotion - Respondent No. 2 completed apprenticeship training as a lineman in 1985, despite lacking matriculation certification but having studied up to class VII standard.
The recruitment rules indicate he can become an assistant lineman within six years and a regular lineman within four years of that.
The Labour Court directed the appellant-board to treat him as a regular lineman by 15101996 and recommended he be given earlier promotion with all other benefits, but respondent No. 2 is content with being treated as a regular lineman from 15101996.
No intervention is necessary.
