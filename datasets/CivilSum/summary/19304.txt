Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Haryana Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Sections 7(2)(3)(4) and 10 - Termination - Managing Committee contested orders of Director, Higher Education, Haryana and the Appellate Order, which wrongly terminated services of Respondent No. 2 and ordered for his reinstatement - Respondent No. 2 accepted appointment as a Lecturer in another college after one year of his services being terminated by the petitioner - Respondent No. 2 is unwilling to go back to the previous college - Managing Committee offered Respondent No. 2 50% wages for the relevant period, without claim for any grant-in-aid from the State Govt. towards his arrears of wages for the relevant period - Respondent No. 2 agreed to the offer as full and final settlement of his dues from the college - Writ petition has become unenforceable.
