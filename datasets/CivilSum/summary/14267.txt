Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Qualification - The petitioner who passed a two-year teaching diploma course from the respondent university was subsequently found derecognized by the Government.
The petitioners were appointed as Assistant teachers in the respondent's school, but their application for approval of appointment was rejected.
The court held that if the course was recognized when the petitioner joined it, it would be unfair to deny them the benefits of such a recognized diploma at a future point.
The court also made reference to AIR 1987 SC 2027.
