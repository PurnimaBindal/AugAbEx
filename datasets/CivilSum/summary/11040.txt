Constitution of India, Article 16 - Ad hoc appointment - Regularisation of services - Executive instructions have been issued by the government to regularize ad hoc employees.
These instructions state that employees who have worked continuously for at least 5 years and are still working on 25.11.1993 are eligible for regularization.
However, it is important to note that these two conditions should not be read together as it would distort the scheme itself.
