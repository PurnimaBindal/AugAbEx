Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Rajasthan Subordinate Courts Ministerial Establishment Service Rules, 1986, Rules 15 and 19 - Anticipated vacancies - Select list - Merely being on the list does not guarantee appointment unless a less qualified candidate was chosen - Anticipated vacancies only include retirements, not deaths or administrative transfers - Candidates not offered employment in the recruitment year must reapply in subsequent years as the waiting list will not carry over.
