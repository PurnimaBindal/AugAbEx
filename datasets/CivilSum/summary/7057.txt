Constitution of India, Articles 15(4) and 16(4) - Appointment - Reservation - Reserved posts are only meant for members belonging to specified groups, but this benefit is in addition to existing rights including the fundamental right of equality.
Those belonging to SC, ST and OBC are free to occupy any posts, including unreserved posts, but they must prove their merit like any other citizen who is not entitled to benefits of reservation.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 15(4) and 16(4) - Appointment - Reservation - Insisting that a candidate belonging to OBC can only be considered for the post reserved for that category alone is improper.
Respondents need to make appointments based on dividing all posts into two categories: unreserved and reserved.
They must first appoint candidates based on merit for the unreserved category before moving on to the reserved category.
Non-consideration of a candidate for appointment against an unreserved post is unconstitutional, and respondents must consider cases of such candidates for appointment.
