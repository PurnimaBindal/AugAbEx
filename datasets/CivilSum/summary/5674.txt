Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300-A - Pension - Interest on delayed pension - In cases where the pension of a former government employee has been unreasonably delayed without any valid reason, they are entitled to receive interest on their overdue pension payments - A delay in sending retirement papers to the Accountant General's office without any explanation resulted in the State Government being instructed to identify the officials responsible for causing the delay and to recover the interest owed from them through an official inquiry.
