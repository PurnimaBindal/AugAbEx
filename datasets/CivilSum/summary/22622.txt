Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Karnataka Village Officers Abolition Act, 1961, Section 2(n) - Discrimination - Equal pay for equal work - Patels and Shanbhogues hold comparable offices and perform comparable duties - They are warranted the same pay and benefits - The State Govt. cannot differentiate between them with regards to pay and allowance as it would infringe upon Article 14 of the Constitution, especially since the State itself treats the two posts as equal for all other purposes.
