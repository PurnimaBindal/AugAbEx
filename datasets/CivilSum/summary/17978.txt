Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion/Adverse reports - The petitioner was passed over for promotion on the basis of expunged adverse remarks, while juniors were promoted.
As a result, the petitioner is entitled to be promoted from the date his immediate junior was promoted, with all related benefits, including salary fixation and seniority.
The petitioner must also be given 12% interest for the arrears owed and the respondent must pay costs of Rs. 3,000/-.
