Article 309 of the Constitution of India states that Rule 54 of Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972 provides family pension to the widow or widower of an individual until the date of death or remarriage.
The petitioner, who is a widower, is entitled to family pension without producing a succession certificate.
[Para 6]
The petitioner, a widower, is entitled to gratuity under Rule 50(6) and Rule 51(1) of Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972 in equal share with his sons as there is no nomination made.
The respondents' insistence on a succession certificate is not required.
[Para 9]
The petitioner, a widower, is entitled to get his share of provident fund even without nomination as it is for the purpose of receiving the money only.
This can be derived from Section 21 of Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, and Rules 5 and 33 of General Provident Fund (Central Services) Rules, 1960.
[Para 11]
The petitioner, a husband, is entitled to the amount under Group Insurance Scheme as per Rule 198 of HP Government Employees Group Insurance Scheme, 1984, with his sons in equal shares, even though no nomination was made by the deceased employee.
[Para 12]
A widower cannot be treated as a `person aggrieved' within the ambit of Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 as holder of civil post nor appointed to any civil service by State.
Therefore, he had a right to approach the court for redressal of his grievance.
