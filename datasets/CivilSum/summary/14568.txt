Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeals) Rules, 1970 Rule 2.2(b) states that one increment of higher responsibility must be given to promotees in order to promote J.B.T.
teachers as Head teachers.
Rule 2.2(b) uses the word "instituted" which may be interpreted to mean that a departmental proceeding cannot be considered as being instituted against an employee until the relevant Punishment Appeal Rules and Rule 2.2(b) of the Rules have been complied with.
The evidence shows that no charge-sheet was issued to the petitioner prior to or even after his retirement, and there was no pending enquiry against him nor was one instituted within the specified time frame.
Therefore, the respondents have unfairly denied the petitioner his rightful gratuity, which is a deprivation of his property right without justifiable cause.
[Paras 6, 7 and 8]


Although the right to property is not part of the fundamental rights under Part-III of the Indian Constitution, it is still considered a constitutional right.
Article 220-A stipulates that no person can be deprived of their property unless it is done in accordance with the law.
Denying the petitioner his rightful gratuity, which is unsupported by any provision or law, goes against Article 300-A Consequently, the respondents are required to release the amount of gratuity payable to the petitioner within a month.
[Paras 8 and 9]
