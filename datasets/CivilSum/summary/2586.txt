Constitution of India, Article 300-A - United Bank of India (Employees) Pension Regulations, 1995, Clause 29 - Pension - Qualifying service - Despite serving 17 years 10 months and 17 days, the petitioner was denied pension by the bank for not completing 20 years of service.
However, sub-clause (5) of Clause 29 of the Pension Regulations entitles the respondent to qualifying service of 2 years and 11 months and 9 days in addition to the actual service rendered, resulting in a total qualifying service period that exceeds 20 years.
As such, the petitioner is entitled to receive pension.
