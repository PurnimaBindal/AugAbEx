Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Rajasthan Technical Training Service Rules, 1975 - Qualifications of Diploma & degree courses in engineering - Comparison of diploma-holders with degree-holders is not allowed by law because they are different classes and this classification agrees with the equality clause under Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
Discrimination only violates the constitutional guarantee of equality if it's unreasonable.
The distinction between diploma-holders and degree-holders is a valid classification.
[Paras 6 to 11]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Rajasthan Technical Training Service Rules, 1975 - Diploma & degree courses in engineering - Classification based on educational qualifications for eligibility of promotion to a higher post is valid.
Fixing a different period of experience for promotion is permissible.
[Paras 12 to 16]
