Constitution of India, Article 311 - Suspension - Premature retirement - If an employee is prematurely retired while under suspension, it is not necessarily harmless as during suspension they do not receive full pay, increments, or certain benefits - Such an order carries a definite stigma and penal consequence, resulting in the loss of certain benefits such as arrears of salary - Any order of retirement must comply with both Article 311 of the Constitution of India and the Punishment and Appeal Rules - Premature retirement will be considered punitive and stigmatic unless the employee is reinstated and receives due benefits according to the law - The order has been quashed, and the petitioner is entitled to all consequential benefits.
