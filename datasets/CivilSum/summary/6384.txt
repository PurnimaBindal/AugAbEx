Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Supersession in promotions - Criteria of promotion "merit-cum-suitability with due regard to seniority" - In cases of "merit-cum-suitability" criteria, seniority only comes into play if the merits of two candidates are equal - It is the merit and suitability that should be the primary considerations - A junior employee can even be promoted ahead of a senior if their merit and suitability exceed the senior employee's.
