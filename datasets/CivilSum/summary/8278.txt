Constitution of India, Article 226 - Suspension - Subsistence allowance - The petitioner, who was appointed purely on an ad hoc basis with a consolidated salary, was placed under suspension due to their involvement in a serious case of corruption.
Their appointment was terminable without notice.
It was decided that the petitioner's services would be deemed terminated as of the date of the suspension order, and that the petitioner was not entitled to any subsistence allowance.
