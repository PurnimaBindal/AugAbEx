Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Termination - Criminal Proceedings - The petitioner was falsely implicated in a case and later acquitted.
He was asked to join his job while he was still in jail, and his services were terminated only because he failed to join.
The court held that it was very unreasonable to ask an individual to join while they were in jail, especially when the individual was kept in jail for no fault of their own.
As a result, the petitioner is entitled to be reinstated with 50% back wages from the date they requested to be reinstated.
