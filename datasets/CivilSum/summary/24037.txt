According to Section 122 of Army Act, 1950, with regards to Court Martial, if there is a delay in initiating disciplinary proceedings due to the delinquent officer creating a situation withholding the start of the trial, then that officer cannot plead the bar of limitation.
However, if the trial commences after the judicial order of restraint on Court Martial is vacated, it will still be considered a valid trial.
