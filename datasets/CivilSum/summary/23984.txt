The appointments of Primary School Teachers are governed by Rules framed by the Govt. under the Assam Elementary Education Act.
Petitioners admitted that the appointments were made hastily and without proper selection or interview process, and therefore cannot claim any right to the post.
[Para 17] B.
Continuation of appointments made without following the statutory rules is not valid, even if ordered by the court for a period of six months or longer.
Such illegally appointed persons cannot claim any right to the post, even for humanitarian reasons.
