Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 6 - Administrative Tribunal - Appointment of Vice-Chairman - The appointee, despite having crossed the retirement age for a High Court Judge, cannot be challenged in their appointment to the position of Vice-Chairman because retirement age is a condition related to the duration of office and not a qualification for appointment.
The appointee's age of 65 years meets the age of superannuation required for the Vice-Chairman position, and thus their appointment cannot be challenged for having crossed 62 years of age.
