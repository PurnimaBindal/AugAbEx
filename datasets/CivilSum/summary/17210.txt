As per Arts.
309 and 311 of the Constitution of India and Sections 10 and 12 of the UP Home Guard Adhiniyam, 1963, the position of Platoon Commander is a civil position subject to Art. 311.
The services of a Platoon Commander can only be terminated after following proper procedure, which includes providing an opportunity to be heard or conducting an enquiry.
