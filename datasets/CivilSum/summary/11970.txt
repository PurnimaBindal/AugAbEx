The Karnataka Education Act, 1995, Sections 89 and 146(3) and the Karnataka Private Education Institutions (Discipline and Control) Rules, 1978, Rules 3 and 5 state that it is the responsibility of the management to pay salaries to their teachers, even if the government has not released the salary grants.
Failure to comply is considered an offence and is punishable under the Karnataka Education Act, 1983.
