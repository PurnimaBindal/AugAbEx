According to Section 2(k) of the Industrial Disputes Act and Section 3 of the U.P.
Industrial Peace (Temporary Payment of Wages) Act, 1978, disputes regarding payment of wages during a period of labour unrest cannot be adjudicated upon by the Labour Commissioner.
Instead, the Labour Commissioner should recommend that the dispute be referred to the proper forum under the Industrial Disputes Act.
In this particular case, the government has been directed to do so, and the management of the establishment has been directed to make payment of 25% of the amount found due by the Labour Commissioner, with the rest to be adjusted later on.
