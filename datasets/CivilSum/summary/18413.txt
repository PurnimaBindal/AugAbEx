Kurukshetra, University Calendar, Volume-I (1997 Edition), Ordinance No. XVII, Clauses 2(2) and 13 and Note appearing at page 246 - Award of Medal - Eligibility for the Award - The merit secured by candidates in annual exams alone constitutes eligibility for Medal etc.- However, in exceptional cases of hardship, non-obstante clause contained in second part of the note at Page 246 can be invoked, provided that the candidate passes the examinations within the minimum course duration and offers documentary proof to substantiate his explanation for non-appearance, at the time of seeking exemption for the main examination and permission for appearing in supplementary.
Decision of awarding Medal to a candidate opting voluntarily for the supplementary without explanation as required by the Rule declared mala fide and arbitrary and set aside to be conferred upon the original candidate.
[Paras 8 to11]
