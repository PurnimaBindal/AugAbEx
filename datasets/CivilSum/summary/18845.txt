Constitution of India, Article 226 - Recovery - The petitioner's pay was allegedly fixed incorrectly, resulting in an overpayment of Rs. 98,671/-.
The petitioner objected to this and claimed entitlement to three increments, but her objection was rejected without explanation.
The respondents have been instructed to provide a clear explanation for their decision and review the petitioner's objections appropriately.
