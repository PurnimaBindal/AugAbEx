Constitution of India, Articles 16, 21 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - Dying in harness - The main objective of granting compassionate employment is to provide an opportunity to the dependent of the deceased employee - A delay in processing the dependent’s request for employment would defeat the purpose of the scheme - The court has ordered for the immediate employment of the petitioner in a suitable position that matches their qualifications.
