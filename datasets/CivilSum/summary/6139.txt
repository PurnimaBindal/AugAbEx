As per the Maharishi Dayanand University Act, 1975, Section 5, the Vice-Chancellor of MDU is not authorized to conduct an enquiry against a private college for misappropriation of funds.
It has been held that no clause of Section 5 empowers the University to institute such an enquiry into an affiliated college like petitioner, and therefore, the directions given by VC, MDU, Rohtak, without jurisdiction have been declared null and void.
