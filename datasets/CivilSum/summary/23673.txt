The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(18) and 2(p) and Income Tax Act, 1961, Section 89 are invoked for disputes related to HRA, Gratuity, Duty-allowances etc.
The settlement awards a lump sum amount of Rs. 50,000/- to each employee, which cannot be treated as complete arrears of salary.
The part of the lump sum apportionable to 'Gratuity' would be exempt from tax up to the permissible statutory limits.
The employer is unable to give a breakdown of the lump sum into its components.
Therefore, tax authorities are directed to determine the gratuity component and give its benefit to the employees.
