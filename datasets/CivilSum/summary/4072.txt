According to Constitution of India, Article 226 and Food Corporation of India Act, 1964, Section 12-A, as well as Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 22(1), employees of the State Government who have been taken in service of FCI as direct recruits and are governed by Staff Regulations cannot claim to be equal to employees covered by Section 12-A and cannot claim an enhancement in the age of retirement from 58 years to 60 years.
