Constitution of India, Article 311 - MP Judicial Services (Classification, Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1955, Rule 24 - Probation - Termination during probation - Deemed confirmation - There are two distinct lines of decisions on whether a probationer would be deemed confirmed on the expiry of the maximum probation period.
These decisions depend on whether the rules stipulate confirmation on expiry of probation.
If the rules don't provide such a stipulation, the probationer will remain on probation until a formal order of confirmation is issued.
However, where the rules provide for a maximum probation period, confirmation will be deemed.
