Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(C)(2) - Non-payment of traveling allowance - Direction for payment - Sustainability - The order of payment passed by the Labour Court under section 33(C)(2) is justified and needs no interference, as the petitioner did not make payment of traveling allowance for subsequent period even after the principal question of entitlement of the respondent was already decided by the Labour Court in an earlier reference.
