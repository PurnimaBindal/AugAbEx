Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 7-A - Constitution of India, 1950, Article 226 - Equal pay for equal work - Contract labour - The application of equal pay for equal work cannot be extended to contract labour.
[Para 8] B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 7A, 10 - Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 226 - Contract worker - Regularisation - The automatic absorption of contract workers is not permissible, as a labor union had made representation in 1987 for their regularisation but they were prohibited via notification in 1989; the industrial dispute raised in 2001-2002 cannot be justified after 6-7 years.
