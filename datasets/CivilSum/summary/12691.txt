As per the Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 226, 338(5), (6) & (8), the transfer of a government employee from one state to another falls under the purview of the Executive.
Guidelines regarding the posting of husband and wife who are both government employees at the same station do not grant the employee a legally enforceable right.
The employer may take into account the demands of the administration while considering such a request.
The power of transfer of a government employee lies solely with the Executive, and the court should not intervene unless there are mala fides or violations of statutory guidelines.
The petitioner's case may be considered as and when vacancies occur, with the policy and guidelines regarding posting of husband and wife at the same station taken into account.
