Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 138 - Police authorities have the obligation to send every Head Constable on List 'C' to attend the Intermediate School Course based on their seniority, beginning with confirmed Head Constables, then those on probation, and lastly, officiating Head Constables - Upon completion of the course, their seniority will be determined in accordance with Rules 138 and 139.
[Paras 1 and 2] B. Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 138 and 139 - Seniority - The Deputy Inspector General of Police for the Range, not the State Government, is the competent authority to establish seniority among Head Constables under Rules 138 and 139.
