Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Departmental Enquiry - The workman was terminated within 10 days of joining service after causing a fatal accident due to rash and negligent driving.
However, termination without an enquiry was deemed inappropriate and was set aside.
It is recommended that a proper enquiry be conducted by providing an opportunity for defense.
The matter has been referred to the disciplinary committee.
