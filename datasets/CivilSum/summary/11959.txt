Constitution of India, Articles, 14, 16 and 226 - Pay Scales - Tubewell operators in Irrigation Department claiming parity of pay scales with those working in the Public Health Department - Writ Petition allowed - State filed review application on the basis of new documents - Review application also dismissed - Court inclined to take a different view due to the existence of new documents, but the State had already conceded the claim in two other cases, so the Court refrained from taking a different view - L.P.A. dismissed.
