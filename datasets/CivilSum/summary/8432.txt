Constitution of India, Article 311 - Railway Servants (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, Rule 6 - Reversion - Suitability test - The Plaintiff was promoted on an ad hoc basis to the post of Material Clerk subject to passing of a suitability test.
Despite being called upon to pass the suitability test, the Plaintiff failed to attend and was subsequently reverted to his substantive post.
This reversion was not a penalty under statutory rules since it was not stigmatic and did not amount to a reduction in rank.
It is important to note that any length of service on the promoted post does not confer the right to continue on that post.
