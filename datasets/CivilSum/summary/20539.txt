The appellant's counsel argued that the Union of India only needed to "consider" his promotion to Assistant Sub-Inspector and Sub-Inspector after his next junior was promoted.
However, the appellant was found unfit for promotion after his case was considered.
The argument that he was considered but not promoted was not presented before the Tribunal, as it was not mentioned in the impugned order.
Had it been explicitly stated in the pleadings, the Tribunal would have addressed it.
If the Tribunal inadvertently ignored it despite the request being made, then a request for the reevaluation of the case should be made.
