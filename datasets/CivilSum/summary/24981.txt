Tiwari, Pinaki Misra, Mrs. Mamta Kachawala, and Miss Bina Gupta, all advocates, argued that the Order of termination does not carry any stigma or visit the appellant with any evil consequences, as the appellant temporary servant had no right to the post and could be terminated by the employer under the contract of service and service rules by giving one month's notice.
Article 311 of the Constitution of India on Natural Justice is not applicable in this case, and the Order is not open to challenge since it is not founded on misconduct.
