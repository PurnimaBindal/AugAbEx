The petitioner's father, in whose position the petitioner is seeking appointment, passed away in April of 1981.
The petitioner applied for the appointment in February of 1994 and filed a petition in July of 1997.
The petitioner's claim is deemed belated as the petitioner was only eight years old when their father passed away, and they are also ineligible under the rules of appointment on compassionate grounds.
