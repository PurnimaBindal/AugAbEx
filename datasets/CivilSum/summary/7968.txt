Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India pertain to promotion and supersession.
If seniority has already been established for a long time, it cannot be disrupted if someone is promoted late as that person cannot receive benefits that were accrued to others through promotion at an earlier date.

Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India state that if a specific relief was not granted in a previous legal matter, it is considered as declined and is subject to res judicata.
Thus, any future claims to that effect will be barred.
[Para 20] C. Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India deal with promotion and experience.
A notional promotion/appointment to a higher post does not provide the promotee with experience of that post, making them ineligible to claim benefits of such experience towards future promotions.
