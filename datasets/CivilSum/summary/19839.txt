As per Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India that address equal pay for equal work, regularisation, additional charge, and promotion, Retoucher Artist was assigned additional duties of Press Manager where he continued to work for two decades.
Therefore, he must receive salary and promotion to the position of Press Manager based on his experience for that role.
Furthermore, his services should be regularised as Press Manager.
