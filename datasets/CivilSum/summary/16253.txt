Sections 10 and 11-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Back wages/Reinstatement - The termination of services of the workman found to be illegal - The workman was ordered to be reinstated with full back wages - The management challenged the award because there was no termination order, and therefore, reinstatement with full back wages was not justified - If the management admits that the workman's services were never terminated, the workman is entitled to current wages instead of back wages - In the absence of any termination order, the workman continues to remain in service.
