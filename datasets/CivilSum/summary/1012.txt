Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - State - The Indian Institute of Bankers is not an instrumentality of the State for the purposes of Article 226 of the Constitution, as it does not fulfil any requirement to be considered the State.
A writ of mandamus can only be issued to a public authority created by a statute to perform its public duty.
[Paras 27, 28, and 29]  B Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - State - For an institute to be considered an instrumentality of the State, the government must have authority and control over the body, person, or authority.
Mere financial assistance, a monopoly status, or registration under some Act of the State does not make the institute an instrumentality of State.
