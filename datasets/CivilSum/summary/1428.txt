Central Civil Services and Civil Posts (Upper Age Limit for Direct Recruitment) Rules, 1998, Rule 3 - Recruitment - Age limit - Claim for relaxation of age limit - Post of Deputy Superintending Archaeologist - Respondent, having qualifications and experience required for the post, is eligible for the benefit of two year increase in the upper age limit as per Rule 3.
However, resorting to interviews for selection should not be arbitrary and should not take the post out of the purview of Direct Open Competitive Examination.
Additionally, the benefit of the upper age limit relaxation is already given to the post of Assistant Archaeologist.
