Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 226 - Admissions/ Education - Reservation - Merit - The admission brochure issued by the University Rule 231 states that if a reserved category candidate would have been admitted in the general category on his own merit, he cannot be considered for admission in the reserved category and must be treated as a general category candidate.
The occupied seat cannot be deducted from reserved category seats, and those who were below in merit but in the reserved category are entitled to be considered for those seats.
Admission granted to a general category candidate with merit below the reserved category candidate is against the rules and Article 16(4) of the Constitution.

Admission Brochure of Panjab University for admission in Architecture College, rule 1.3.5 - Waiting list - Reserved seats - The rule is exclusive to seats falling vacant due to students leaving the institution after admission before the last date for admission.
Such seats have to be filled up from the waiting list as per merit.
The rule does not apply to reserved category seats, nor does it have anything to do with the first date of the interview.
