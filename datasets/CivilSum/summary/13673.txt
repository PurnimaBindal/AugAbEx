Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rules 8, 9 and 10 - Punishing authority/Show Cause/Reasons to differ with finding in the enquiry report - Plaintiff was cleared in the enquiry report - Punishing authority proposed major penalty by differing with the finding without providing reasons for disagreement - Compliance with Sub-rule 4 of Rule 9 necessitated recording reasons of disagreement and informing the plaintiff to make an effective representation and reply - Non-compliance with this procedure is a breach of the rules - Penalty and proceedings would be unlawful and tainted.
