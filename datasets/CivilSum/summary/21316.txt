The Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 32 govern the revision of pay scale.
Public sector enterprise employees cannot legally demand wage revision when the company is incurring sustained losses and cannot afford to revise their payscales.
Employees of Government companies may not request the Central Government to bear the additional expenses incurred from revising payscales, as they aren't civil servants.
It is untenable to argue that the financial capacity of the employer or the unit's economic viability cannot factor into the revision of payscales.
