Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(a) - Conviction - Misconduct of employee leading to conviction - Charge of theft - Natural justice - Although principles of natural justice can supplement the law, they cannot supplant it - If the Rule can address the specific contingency, the principles of natural justice are excluded - The doctrine of natural justice does not apply when the authority believes that holding an inquiry would be inexpedient and against the security interests of the establishment - Continuing the offender in public service after a conviction for theft would demoralize the service - Therefore, it is in the public interest not to conduct any further inquiry and to terminate his services immediately.
