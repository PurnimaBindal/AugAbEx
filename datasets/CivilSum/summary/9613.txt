Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8(3) and Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 related to natural justice and charge-sheets.
Delinquent charges are not defined clearly, nor are the rules that they violate.
As a result, the enquiry based on such charges would be flawed, and the enquiry report and related punishment would be nullified.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 require proper evidence in an enquiry.
In the case of a conductor, witnesses, including eye witnesses, were not examined.
Additionally, no clear comparison of cash and tickets was provided, and no distinction was made between punched and unpunched tickets.
As a result, the enquiry report cannot be sustained, and the findings presented in it must be rejected.
