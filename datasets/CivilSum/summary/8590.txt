Special Civil Application No. 1872 of 1989 Constable (Armed Branch, Unarmed Branch and Women Branch, Recruitment Rules, 1979, Rule 2 - Mode of recruitment of constables - The transfer rule making authority has only one mode of recruitment, which is direct selection.
As there is no transfer mode of recruitment, petitioners' claim to transfer to the post of unarmed constable is not valid.
Previous transfers of armed constables to the unarmed branch based on their qualifications do not give the petitioners the right to transfer.
The government's circular that allows such transfers outside the established rules cannot be enforced.
