The petitioner dismissed the respondent due to misappropriation but Central Industrial Tribunal found the charges unestablished and deemed the dismissal as illegal With the appellant Bank challenging the award, the High Court passed an interim staying order, requiring the Bank to pay the wages last drawn by the respondent or what he would have earned if he had not been terminated Section 17-B of the Industrial Dispute Act, 1947 governs this matter [Para 3] B Section 17-B of the Industrial Dispute Act, 1947 mandates that the employer must pay the full wages last drawn by the workman in order to alleviate their hardship caused by the delay in implementing the award The payment is considered a subsistence allowance and cannot be recovered even if the award is later set aside by the court [Para 19] C.
