The Municipal Corporation of Calcutta Employees (Death-cum-Retirement) Benefit Regulations, 1982, Chapter 1 Regulation 1 - Retiral and pensionary benefits - There is no discrimination with the setting of April 1, 1977 as the date from which new Regulations were to come into effect for exercising an option under the Pension Scheme.
[Para 7]
Terminal benefits, as well as pensionary benefits, constitute conditions of service, according to the Constitution of India, Article 309.
[Para 7]
The employer, under the Municipal Corporation of Calcutta Employees (Death-cum-Retirement) Benefit Regulations, 1982, Regulation 1, has the power to revise salaries/pay-scales, as well as terminal benefits/pensionary benefits.
The employer also has the power to specify a date from which the revision of pay-scales or terminal benefits/pensionary benefits will take effect.
If such date is specified reasonably i.e. without discrimination between similarly situated persons, then the Court will not interfere.
[Paras 7 and 9]
