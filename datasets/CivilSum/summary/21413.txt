The Constitution of India, Articles 311, 310 and 300-A - Rajasthan Service Rules, Rule 244(2) - Compulsory retirement - An official Government Circular has clarified that the Rule only applies to Gazetted Government Servants, and cannot be used on those in Subordinate Services - As the petitioner was a Patwari, they could not be retired under this Rule, and the order of compulsory retirement was therefore quashed.
