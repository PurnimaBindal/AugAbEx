Constitution of India, Article 226 - BSF Rules, Rule 26 - Premature retirement - Employee of BSF was prematurely retired on the basis of service record that showed receiving more than one punishment during the service - His response to notice was taken into account - The authority has the right to decide whether an employee is unsuitable for service upon examining all available materials - The fact that petitioner received three punishments before his promotion would not invalidate the action of respondents - No intervention.
