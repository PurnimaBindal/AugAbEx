Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Termination - Embezzlement - A conductor was charged with embezzlement of Rs. 1890 by not issuing tickets against the fare charged, resulting in his services being terminated.
The Labour Court reinstated the worker with continuity of service and 50% back wages, however, the punishment substitution was very mild given the small amount of embezzlement.
As the award is from 1983 and the workman has already been reinstated, it would not be appropriate to impose further punishment of dismissal from service.
Therefore, after considering all facts and circumstances, the award of 50% back wages has been set aside.
