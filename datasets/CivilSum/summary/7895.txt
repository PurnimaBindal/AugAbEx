Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination of a probationer - The petitioner was terminated due to unsatisfactory performance, as stated in the appointment letter - The termination order did not require a reason to be assigned - The petitioner contended that it was punitive, arbitrary, and a violation of the principle of natural justice because no inquiry was conducted - However, it was held that the termination was not stigmatic on its face because it was a straightforward case of unsatisfactory performance, and therefore it was not unfair.
[Paras 8 and 9]
