Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Medical Education - BAMS professional course - It is mandatory to complete required semester studies to be eligible for the final Professional Examination of BAMS - The petitioner advanced to the third Professional Examination in Jan, 2007 without completing studies of 18 months, despite appearing in the Second Professional examination in Nov./Dec.
2006 - As the petitioner was ineligible for the third Professional examination, the court cannot issue a directive that conflicts with the university's Ordinance and the Medical Council of India's scheme of Medical Education, which may be harmful to the public interest.
