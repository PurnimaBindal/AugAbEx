Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Selection - Promotion through DPC - Committee assesses eligibility and service record - Criteria adopted must be fair and reasonable - No evidence of arbitrary or unfair criteria - DPC may select a junior if deemed more meritorious - Selection of such juniors is valid.
[Paras 6 and 7]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Necessary parties - Promotion - Supersession - Person promoted in supersession not impleaded - Challenging selection in this way is mistaken.
