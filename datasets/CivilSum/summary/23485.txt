Constitution of India, Article 311-A - Army Act, Section 71(h) - Pension Regulations, Regulation 16(a) - Pension - Although Section 71(h) of the Army Act and Regulation 16(a) of the Pension Regulations operate in different fields and are not inconsistent with each other, they deal with the punishment and retiral benefits respectively of officials who have been found guilty by the Court Martial - Regulation 16(a) comes into play only after the Court Martial has pronounced its verdict under Section 17 and is not contrary to any Act or Rule - Since the pension is provided under these regulations, it may be revoked for justifiable reasons under the same regulations.
[Paras 7 and 8]
