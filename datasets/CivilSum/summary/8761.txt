Army Act, 1950, Section 80 - Natural justice - Withdrawal of rank - Misconduct - After being given full opportunity during an enquiry, the petitioner was found guilty of misconduct of indiscipline.
The competent authority was authorized to impose the punishment of depriving the petitioner of his rank, which cannot be considered illegal or arbitrary.
[Para 8]

Army Rules, Rule 13(3) Item 3(v) (i) to (iv) - Discharge from service - Indiscipline - Discipline is of paramount importance in the army, and the petitioner's conduct was found undesirable on multiple occasions.
He was found guilty of intoxication and entering the quarter of another employee without specific permission of the authorities and taking liquor there with the employee and committing rape upon his wife.
The punishment of discharge from service under the residuary provisions of clause (iv) of Rule 13(3) was not illegal or arbitrary, and protection under other clauses of the Rule cannot be extended in such cases where the authority can take notice of any kind of undesirability of a person to be kept in service any more.
[Paras 10 and 14]

Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Army Rules, Rule 13(3) - Speaking order - Discharge from service - The communication of the order of discharge from service as "not desired" was not a speaking one on reasons.
However, the record shows that the facts of the case were examined before the impugned communication was sent, and thus it cannot be said that there was no application of mind by the competent authority.
[Para 11]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Mala fide - Necessary party - Pleadings - A person against whom allegations of mala fide are levelled is a necessary party, and if no such allegations have been made in the appeal and representations before the competent authorities to examine the same, such allegations cannot be heard in the writ petition for the first time.
[Paras 12 and 13]
