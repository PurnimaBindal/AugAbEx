Letters Patent Appeal - Clause X - Seniority - The seniority of the appellant was disrupted without any valid reason, due to a mere letter from a member of the Legislative Assembly to a Minister in Haryana Govt.
The appellant's seniority had been established and fixed for several years before this, and none of the other affected employees raised any objections.
The impugned order of the Single Judge was overturned, and the petitioner was directed to be considered senior to the respondent in the writ petition, with all consequential benefits to be granted to the petitioner.
The appeal was allowed.
