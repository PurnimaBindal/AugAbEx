Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Transfer - Writ jurisdiction - Bank employee - General transfers - Petitioner, a Bank employee holding a transferable post, was transferred along with more than 450 other employees - No specific allegation of mala fide - Transfers were made for administrative reasons - Transfer is a service incident - The court cannot intervene in the orders of transfers made on administrative grounds using its jurisdiction under Art. 226 of the Constitution.
