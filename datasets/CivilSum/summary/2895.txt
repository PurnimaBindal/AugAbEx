The Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 and Industrial Disputes Act 1947 entitle an employee to re-instatement with continuity of service and full back wages if demand notice is delayed.
The period of the notice begins on the day of issuance and not before.
In this case, the petitioner mistakenly filed a civil suit earlier without intention to delay, and therefore, is entitled to full back wages from the date of filing the civil suit.
