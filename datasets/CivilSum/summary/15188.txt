The UCO Bank Officer Employees’ (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations of 1976 were amended in 2002, stating that new evidence can be used to impose penalties.
After a petitioner was served with a charge sheet by the respondent-bank, the charge was not proved by the inquiry officer and the petitioner was exonerated by the disciplinary authority.
However, a show cause notice was issued by the reviewing authority and the pay of the petitioner was lowered for two years.
This action was deemed to be the reviewing authority wrongly exercising power of the appellate authority.
The impugned order, decided under the old Regulation 18, was set aside.
(Para 13-20)
