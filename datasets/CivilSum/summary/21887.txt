The order of repatriation pertaining to the appointment of Khalasi in the cadre of Clerks is subject to the principles of natural justice, which include giving employees an adequate opportunity of hearing prior to taking decisional action.
Failure to comply with these procedures renders the post decisional hearing bad in law, especially when the appellant was not provided with a copy of the complaint, charges were not framed, witnesses were not examined, and no Enquiry Officer arrived at a finding of guilt.
As a result, the appeal is allowed.
[Para 23] B.
Although the disciplinary authority arrived at a finding already, a post decisional hearing is not called for and is not contemplated in the law.
Further, the result of such a hearing was known beforehand, so there was no need for it.
