The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 - Judgments and precedents - The law must be used to interpret judgments, which are not the same as statutes - The ratio of law established in a judgment must be analyzed in conjunction with the surrounding factors and circumstances and may not be utilized in all situations.
[Para 17]
The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 & 226 - Classification - Comparable individuals must be treated similarly - One individual's court appearance does not imply that similarly comparable individuals should receive differing treatment.
