Constitution of India, Article 226 - Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1969, Section 19 - Compulsory Retirement - Misconduct - A Bank Manager charged with encashment of social security deposit receipt falsely declaring the receipt missing and presentation of an incorrect TA bill and reimbursement - Intention to defraud Bank not clearly proved - Possible that misconduct was due to misjudgment on delinquent's part - Punishment is disproportionate - Reduction to next lower grade with continuity of service and 25% of backwages is appropriate.
