Air Corporations Act, 1953, Section 45 - Air India Services Regulations, 1953, Regulation 46 (II) - Voluntary Retirement - Employees who are at least 55 years old have the right to retire voluntarily with three months' notice - The request to retire cannot be denied based on the training expenses, as it is an irrelevant factor - The petitioner must be retired and all retirement benefits paid.
