Article 226 of the Constitution of India deals with the quashing of an order and the sustainability of a prayer.
An order that did not exist on the date of judgment cannot be quashed, since the prayer had already become infructuous.
Additionally, no prayer has been made to restrain a person from functioning after the expiry of the order.
[Paras 5, 6]
Article 226 of the Constitution of India also deals with the grant of relief not prayed for.
The prayer is to fill a post as per the seniority list without challenging the seniority list.
When the final seniority list is issued, certain rights accrue to individuals.
If someone wants to challenge the seniority list, they must challenge it directly.
A direction by a Single Judge to refix the seniority amounts to disturbance of the seniority list, which cannot be done in the absence of a challenge to the seniority list.
A judge cannot ordinarily grant a relief that has not even been claimed by a party.
The order has been set aside.
[Paras 8, 10, 11]
