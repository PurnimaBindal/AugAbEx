Constitution of India, Article 311 - Grant-in-Aid to Non-government Educational, Cultural and Physical Education Institutions Rules, 1963, Schedule III Clauses (1) and (8) - The Respondent was appointed as Headmaster for a one-year term but his services were terminated on the grounds of unsatisfactory performance at the end of the probation period.
The allegation of termination being punitive in nature was rejected on account of no evidence of mala fides.
Furthermore, as the termination was not based on misconduct or inefficiency, the tribunal's recording of finding without any allegation/material on record had no factual foundation, and the plea was not agitated before tribunal order set aside.
Ultimately, the termination was upheld and the petition allowed.
