Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion - `Merit cum Seniority' - Petitioner working as Deputy General Manager seeking promotion to the post of General Manager - Post of General Manager - A selection post - Principle of merit-cum-seniority taken into consideration when filling this post - The most qualified or meritorious individual selected for promotion - If merit is primary, merit-cum-seniority rule is used - Even though the petitioner was duly considered, they did not rank high enough.
