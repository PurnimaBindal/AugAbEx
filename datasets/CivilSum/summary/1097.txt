Section 5 of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970 (37 of 1970) states that recommendation of subcommittee must be unanimous for the abolition of contractual labour, otherwise it cannot be annulled.
Petitioner has challenged the notification by Punjab Government prohibiting employment of contract labour based on this ground.
However, the court holds that the recommendation made by subcommittee, even if not unanimous, cannot be faulted as it has considered all aspects, and cannot be deemed illegal unless it is shown that the recommendation must be unanimous.
[Para 10]

In accordance with Section 10(1) of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970 (37 of 1970), petitioner challenges the notification by Punjab Government prohibiting employment of contract labour on the basis that the work was not specifically shown to be perennial in nature.
However, the court holds that since the authorities had knowledge of the perennial nature of work in the plant, and the evidence regarding the same was considered while taking the decision, the notification may not reveal specifically whether the work was of perennial nature.
[Para 20]
