The Punjab Police Rules of 1934 and Article 311 of the Constitution of India, specifically rules 1628 and 1632 regarding dismissal from service due to absence, were applied to a Punjab Police constable who was absent from duty for over 41 days, resulting in his dismissal from service in 1994.
His appeal and revision were both rejected, and his subsequent second revision in 2005 was dismissed due to gross delay in filing.
It was held that filing another revision does not excuse the delay.
[Paras 1, 2, and 5].
B.
Rule 1628 of the Punjab Police Rules gives power to the IG, DIG, and SP to review and modify awards given by their subordinates and give opportunity for the defaulter to show cause in case of enhancement of penalty.
As the penalty was not enhanced, there was no need for the petitioner to be given an opportunity for a hearing.
Thus, the petition was dismissed.
[Para 6].
