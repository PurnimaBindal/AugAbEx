The Punjab Dairy Development Non-Ministerial (State Service Class III) Rules, 1981 Qualifications/Pay scales - Qualifications for direct appointment to the post of Dairy Extension Assistants Grade I and Grade II provided in the rules are different - Although Assistant Grade II can become eligible for promotion to Grade I after some experience, they cannot claim pay scales equal to Assistants Grade I on this basis - The eligibility and pay scales for the advertised posts were also stated - The petitioner applied for the post of Asstt.
Grade II, and therefore cannot argue that they should have been given a different grade - Unless a legal right is infringed, no mandamus can be issued.
