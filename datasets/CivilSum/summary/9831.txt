According to Sections 2(aa), 18 and 22 of the Apprentices Act, 1961, the petitioners who were serving as apprentices were merely trainees and not considered as workers.
The letters of selection given to the petitioners mentioned that there was no job guarantee, and the contract forms signed by them did not contain any clause about providing employment after the apprenticeship period.
Therefore, the petitioners cannot demand to be absorbed in the employment of the respondents.
[Paras 13, 15 and 22]
