As per Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, a selection grade was given to a junior employee from 1st January 1986.
The petitioner's ACR for 1986 was taken into consideration, and they cleared the efficiency bar in 1988.
ACRs for 1978-79, 1980-81, 1982-83, and 1983-84 were not contested, and there is no evidence to suggest that the criteria for granting selection grade are the same for crossing the efficiency bar.
The Selection Committee did not make any mistake in denying the selection grade.
