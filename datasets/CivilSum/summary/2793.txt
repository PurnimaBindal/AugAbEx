The Administrative Tribunals Act of 1985, the Central Civil Services (CCA) Rules of 1965, and Article 226 of the Constitution of India are all relevant to the issue of the legality of lesser punishments for sexual harassment in departmental proceedings.
While someone aggrieved by such an order can approach the tribunal, a third party cannot, and should instead seek intervention through the writ jurisdiction of the High Court.
B.
The punishment of dismissal from service is not disproportionate or harsh when an allegation of sexual harassment is proved against a public servant.
However, a court should not ordinarily interfere in the punishment awarded by the employer, and the case at hand is remanded to the appellate authority for reconsideration.
