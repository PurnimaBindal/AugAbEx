Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Disciplinary proceedings - Dismissal - The charge-sheet against the petitioner was vague with no clear allegations, and witnesses in support of the charges were not examined.
Petitioner was not given the opportunity to cross-examine witnesses who had given their written statements.
The inquiry officer followed an unfair procedure.
The respondent took plea of alternative remedy available to petitioner.
The findings of the Inquiry Officer and the final order of termination passed thereon have been set aside.
The petitioner is entitled to back wages and subsistence allowance which he would be entitled to under the law if the respondent proceeds to hold a fresh enquiry.
[Paras 11, 13, 15, 16, 17, and 20]
