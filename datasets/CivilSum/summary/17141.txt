Based on Constitution of India, Article 16 and Air Force Instructions 1962, Paragraphs 12, 14 and 17, a person meeting the criteria of paragraph 14 cannot be denied promotion unless they have a disqualification rendering them unfit for a higher position, not based on their merit.
The respondent-petitioner met the requirements of paragraph 14 and administrative instructions cannot amend AFI 19/62.
As per the decision made by the learned single Judge, the respondent-petitioner should receive automatic promotion upon fulfilling certain conditions.
