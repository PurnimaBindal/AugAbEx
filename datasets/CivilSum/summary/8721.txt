Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 Section 25-F - Mala fide - Services of a sweeper in Punjab Roadways terminated, and Labour Court ruled that termination was prior to completion of 240 days with malicious intent, thus violating section 25-F of the Act.
However, the Writ Petition held that the termination prior to completion of 240 days does not necessarily amount to malicious intent, as mere vague allegations are insufficient; some material facts are necessary to show that the employer acted dishonestly.
The Award of the labour court was set aside, and the petition was allowed.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Reference - The delay of more than five years in raising a demand about the illegal termination of services is considered to be a form of abandonment of the claim under the Act.
Employers cannot be expected to keep the post vacant permanently.
