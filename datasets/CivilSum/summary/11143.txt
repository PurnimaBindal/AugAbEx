According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 4.23, competent authority has the power to condone service interruptions, not the Court.
[Para 8]
The plaintiff-appellant worked continuously from 10111964 as a work-charge employee and was allocated to the State of Haryana after the reorganisation of the Punjab State.
The appellant was promoted and his services were regularised in 1974.
The services rendered by the plaintiff-appellant from 10111964 to 21.11.1974 as a work-charge employee shall be considered for granting pensionary and the allied benefits, as per Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 4.23.
