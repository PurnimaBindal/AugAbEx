According to the Sri Krishnadevaraya University Act, 1981, Section 28 and Constitution of India, Article 16, Statutes of the University, Statute No. 16(1), a retired professor may sit on the Selection Committee for Appointment/Selection/Selection Committee/Member of Selection Committee.
The only restriction is for those holding a lower rank position than the post for which Selection is being made.
The constitution of the Selection Committee remains valid and recommendations made by the committee, as well as appointments, are also valid.
