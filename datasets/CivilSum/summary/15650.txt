Constitution if India, Article 226 - Prasar Bharti (Broadcasting Corporation of India) Act, 1990, Sections 4(i), 4(4), 6(3) - A petitioner was appointed as a part-time member on the Board, and although they were supposed to hold office for six years, section 6(3) requires that 1/3rd of such members must retire on the expiration of every second year - petitioner had no right to continue for the full term of six years - Other members retained for a longer term than petitioner were deemed to be better for achieving the objectives of the Board - No stigma attaches to the petitioner - The petition is dismissed.
