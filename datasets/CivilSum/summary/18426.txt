The Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, Appendix-II and Regulation 22 (as amended w.e.f.
16101987) discuss compulsory retirement from the post of Asstt.
Grade-I and specify the competent authorities.
The amendment is not retroactive, and the amendment of 1987, which specifies appointing authorities for various posts, is not relevant.
The competent authority for the post of Asstt.
Grade-I under the unamended regulations was the Sr.
Regional Manager.
If the employee claims to have been appointed by a higher authority, it will be a matter of pleading and consideration in the light thereof.
However, in the absence of such pleadings, such a factual plea cannot be considered.
