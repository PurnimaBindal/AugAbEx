Constitution of India, Article 226 - Reinstatement - Back wages - The petitioner, a Branch Manager, was charged with misconduct while serving.
The Departmental enquiry led to the petitioner's removal from service.
The High Court found the penalty imposed as disproportionate and ordered the petitioner's reinstatement with reasonable punishment.
No denial of backwages and benefits was made.
The petitioner is entitled to all benefits during the out-of-employment period, and the letter issued by the Bank denying these benefits was overturned.
The Bank took no steps to impose the appropriate punishment after the earlier writ petition's disposal, and it is their responsibility to investigate any lapses committed by the concerned officers in this regard.
