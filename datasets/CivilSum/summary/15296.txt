In accordance with Article 226 of the Constitution of India and the Delhi Municipal Corporation Act of 1957, a teacher who is also an Inspector does not stop being a teacher even after being promoted to an administrative position.
As such, retirement age for teachers is 60 years while it's 58 years for officers and other employees.
The petitioner should have been retired at 60 years, and consequential benefits have been granted.
