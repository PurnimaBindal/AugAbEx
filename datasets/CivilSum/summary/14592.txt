Haryana Civil Medical Service (Class-II) Rules, 1978, Rule 13 Efficiency bar/Rural service - Benefit of rural service cannot be denied for the purpose of clearing efficiency bar in view of Rule 13, regardless of the state in which services were rendered.
[Para 3] B Haryana Civil Medical Service (Class-II) Rules, 1978, Rule 13 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Policy of Government/No objection certificate/Rural service to state - The Government's policy is to grant no objection certificate for in-service candidates to pursue PG Course in Medical College against reserved seats, as an incentive to employees who willingly served in the rural area of Haryana - The state is responsible for meeting the expenditure incurred during the course - The decision to send an employee to a higher course is up to the state, under its own conditions - According to the rule, only rural service within the state is considered eligible for the grant of 'NO OBJECTION CERTIFICATE'.
