Indian Penal Code, Section 193 - Perjury - Age of Dr. Justice of India - The Respondent filed an affidavit claiming that the President of India had not determined the age of Dr. Justice AS Anand in accordance with Article 217 of the Constitution - The affidavit is believed to contain a false statement - Proceedings under Section 192 of the I.P.C. must be initiated.
