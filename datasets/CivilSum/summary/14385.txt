Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India relate to promotions.
A meritorious officer must be promoted to Middle Management Grade Scale II based on merit alone, and the petitioner was censured after being issued a charge sheet.
There was valid material to keep him ineligible, and he was given the opportunity to take the test in the next D.P.C.
However, he failed, and he cannot contest the promotion of the candidate who passed and was promoted based on merit.
The petition was dismissed.
