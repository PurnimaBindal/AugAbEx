The appellant's promotion is being contested due to a lack of success in challenging earlier reversions and not challenging the Railway Board's communication.
Despite this, the appellant argued against these reversions in person but the court cannot entertain this argument for the promotion of the third respondent.
This is due to the clear position outlined in the Railway Board's communication that the appellant has lost seniority to Govind Sahai.
