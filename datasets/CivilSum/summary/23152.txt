Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Sections 2(22), 39, 40 - Wages - Remuneration - Subsistence Allowance - Subsistence allowance is considered remuneration and therefore part of wages according to Section 2(22) of the Act - Such allowance is payable to the employee during suspension and keeps the employee on the roll of employment, fulfilling their part of the contract - Both the employee and employer are liable to contribute to ESI based on the amount of subsistence allowance.

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 2(22) - Lay-off - Suspension - The main distinction between lay-off and suspension is that during lay-off, the employee can work for another employer and would not be entitled to compensation - However, during suspension, the employee remains an employee and the employer remains their employer - This is evident in the case of suspension pending a departmental enquiry.
