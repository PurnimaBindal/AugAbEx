A Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 4, 22 - Compensation - Liability to pay interest - As per the law laid down by the Supreme Court in the case of Ferro Alloys Ltd, the insurance company and the insured employer must jointly make good a claim for compensation along with interest - Therefore, the order of the Commissioner stating that the insured should pay the interest amount is unsustainable.
[Para 7] B. Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4-A(3)(b) - Compensation - Imposition of penalty - A fresh notice for the proposed penalty was required to be issued even though the employer was proceeded ex parte for the award of compensation - Therefore, the order cannot be sustained.
[Para 8] C Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4 - Compensation - Determination of - The contention that the Commissioner has wrongly held the income of the deceased driver to be Rs. 3000 per month as the insured has stated it to be Rs. 1500 is not upheld - Payment of daily allowance to the driver/conductor is a matter of common knowledge - Therefore, if Rs. 50 is added as daily allowance to the salary, the income comes to be Rs. 3000 per month - Thus, the finding of the Commissioner is upheld.
