Admission to LLB (Hons) - The condition for candidates' place of posting to be within 45 kms of the university's Faculty of Law is challenged - A petitioner working as a Hindi Teacher in a Govt. School at 70 kms argues that the condition is arbitrary and has no objective nexus - The court holds that the requirement of the Bar Council of India for enrolment as an Advocate is to undergo 75% lectures in theory and practical subjects, and thus, the condition cannot be considered arbitrary or unreasonable - The distance limit is set to facilitate the movement of the candidate from their place of residence to the university for academic pursuits - Additionally, the University has the authority to set eligibility conditions based on an intelligible classification.
