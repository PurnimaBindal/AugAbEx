Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Compensation - Accident - Loss of earning capacity - The Insurance Company argued that the claimant's loss of earning capacity should only be 50% because they only lost one hand, but this contention was rejected.
The Commissioner examined the Doctor's evidence and appropriately determined that the claimant had suffered a 100% loss of earning capacity.
No intervention is necessary.
