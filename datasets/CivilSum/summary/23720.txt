The legislature and judiciary have to maintain balance in exercising their respective jurisdictions.
If a judgment of the court becomes ineffective due to the change in law on which it was based, there is no usurpation of judicial power by the legislature.
The Haryana Service of Engineers, Class-I, Public Works Department (Building and Roads Branch), (Public Health Branch) and (Irrigation Branch) Act, 1995, passed in exercise of its powers by the State Legislature of Haryana amending the Public Service of Engineers Class I Service Rules, especially when it is prohibited from repealing the judicial decision, have trampled upon the power of judicial review, transgressed its limits and trespassed into the field of judicial review - Giving declaration under the Act emasculating the vitality and efficacy of judicial review, is a colourable legislative exercise and fraud on the Constitution so far it has been given retrospective effect.
However, the prospective operation of the Act may be valid.
