Delhi School Education Act and Rules - Constitution of India, Article 15(1) - Promotion - Petitioner eligible for promotion to Lab Assistant - School claimed post to be filled by female staff only - No such provision in recruitment rules - School's reliance on resolution deemed unsupported - Resolution must have legal sanction or support under Act or Rules to be valid - Failure to meet this requirement would fall afoul of Article 15(1) of Constitution - Court directed school to consider petitioner's case.
