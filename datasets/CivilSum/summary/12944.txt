According to Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1977, Sections 9 and 11 and Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 12, in case of a dispute regarding inter se seniority between parties, the Education Officer has the authority to settle the dispute.
However, in an appeal under Section 9 of the Act by an affected teacher, the School Tribunal has the jurisdiction to determine the issue of seniority.
