According to Maharishi Dayanand University Act, 1975, Statute 38(1-a), closure or merger of university-maintained colleges, specifically the closure of Evening College, is under the purview of the Executive Council.
The University Evening College was started to cater to the requirement of employees but was found to be misused by admission of non-employees who failed to make the grade on merit for day colleges.
Those who wished to continue their studies were admitted to Distance Education without any additional charges and special arrangements were made for their classes.
Thus, the closure of Evening College did not infringe on the rights of the petitioners and the challenge against its closure was dismissed.
