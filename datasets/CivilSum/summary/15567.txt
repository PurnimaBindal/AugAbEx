Constitution of India, Article 16 - Selection - Post of Mazdoor - The petitioner was initially selected for the post of Mazdoor, but his selection was cancelled because he failed to mention his involvement in a criminal case.
Although he was later acquitted of the charges, his request to join duty was denied and his subsequent application to the Tribunal was dismissed.
The court held that since the petitioner had been acquitted in the criminal case, it was deemed that he was never actually involved in any criminal activity.
Therefore, he is entitled to be appointed as Mazdoor and a mandamus was issued to the respondent to appoint him accordingly.
