Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11 - Ex parte award - Possibility of setting aside - The Labour court has the authority to set aside an ex parte award if the party against whom the award is given can provide sufficient cause for their absence.
- The labor court's order that the court becomes functus officio after the award becomes enforceable is incorrect.
- The case is referred back for further consideration.
