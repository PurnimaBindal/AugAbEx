According to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Constitution of India, Articles 226/227, the jurisdiction and evidence are crucial when the High Court is concerned.
The evidence and pleadings need not be questioned by the High Court, even if the parties involved led evidence and knew about the point involved.
Provided that the point was not raised in pleadings, the court can still adjudicate the point.
[Paras 8, 9 and 10]
