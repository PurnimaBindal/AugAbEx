Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay - Re-employment of an Ex-serviceman petitioner as a Security Guard - Respondents' action in re-fixing the petitioner's salary by debiting enhanced army pension drawn by him is illegal and declared void - Respondents directed to pay petitioner the salary without considering the enhanced pension taken from the Army and to release all deducted amounts from the petitioner's salary.
