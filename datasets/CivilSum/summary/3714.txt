The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Part-time worker - Whether "workman" or not - Even though a part-time employee is a civil servant for purpose of the Article 311(2) of the Constitution of India, he cannot claim back wages on the basis of wages which a full-time employee is entitled to.
A part-time worker falls within the meaning of "workman" under Industrial Disputes Act, and he can be paid the wages only as had been paid to him as a part-time employee.
No sanctioned post available, and appointments not made by competent authority.
