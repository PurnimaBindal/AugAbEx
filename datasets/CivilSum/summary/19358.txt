The Payment of Wages Act, 1986, Sections 15(3), 7(2)(c) and 10(1A) allow an employer to deduct from an employee's wages only after properly determining the loss and considering the workman's contentions.
The management was found guilty of deducting wages without considering all possible reasons for the deficit in weight, and no order for deductions was passed in accordance with the law.
The deductions were deemed illegal, and the workman was ordered to be refunded the wages with 12% interest and with the right to proceed afresh according to the law.
