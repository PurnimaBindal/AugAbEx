Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 - Revision of pay scales and allowances - Writ Court jurisdiction in addressing allowance anomalies - Pay and allowances fixation is the concern of experts- The court examines proper procedure and process application - The Anomaly Committee headed by the Chief Secretary of the Haryana government clarifies anomalies - The Anomaly Committee has been directed to consider petitioners' complaints submitted through appropriate channels and make a conscious and cautious decision within a specified time frame while following the "audi alterum partem" dictum.
