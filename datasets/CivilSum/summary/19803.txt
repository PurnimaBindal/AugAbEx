Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay scales - In accordance with Article 12 of the Constitution, an autonomous body, like a Board, cannot discriminate amongst its own employees - However, the Board is not the State Government, and as a separate employer, it is allowed to fix different pay scales and other emoluments than the Government for its employees.
[Para 5]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Revision of pay scales - Anomalies - If a Board revision of pay scales causes anomalies in implementation, the Board is required to remove such anomalies from the date of the revision.
[Para 6]
Equal pay for equal work - Workers with different job duties and qualifications cannot claim pay scale parity with Teachers - The nature of their job duties are different, as are the qualifications they hold - Therefore, they cannot claim equal pay despite the principle of equal pay for equal work.
[Para 3]
Equal pay for equal work - Petitioners in the Panchayat Department cannot claim pay scales equal to a Clerk appointed in the Secretariat in the grade of Assistant - While one Clerk in the Secretariat was given a special higher grade due to the peculiar circumstances of their job, the qualifications, quality, and quantity of work are different, and thus the principle of equal pay for equal work does not apply.
