According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, an employee's work charged service can only be considered towards their pension if they were regularised in the same post.
A petitioner who left their post and received terminal benefits cannot take advantage of their previous ad hoc service and claim double benefits, even if they later rejoined the same department through another department.
This was discussed in paragraphs 10, 11, and 12 of the case.
