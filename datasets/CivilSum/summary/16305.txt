The petitioner claims reservation on the one post designated for those with specialized knowledge in Forensic Medicine, citing Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution which deal with Reservation/Fixation of Roster Points/Category/wise reservation.
The requisite percentage of reservation for the reserved category has already been provided according to Roster Points, and the court should be wary of interfering with the methodology of reservation unless it is flawed.
Category-wise reservation may lead to 100% reservation for Scheduled Castes and General Category candidates.
