State and Subordinate Services Rules, 1958 (Kerala) Rule 28(b)(i)(8)(a) - Promotion - Supersession - DPC can only rely on grounds referred to in a representation to justify supersession - Petitioner's grounds for supersession were found to be untenable, and the DPC justified supersession on another ground - The contention that the DPC should only consider whether a representation is to be allowed or not is not supported by Rule 28 - The process of selection is designed to be conducted in an orderly manner by rules, and not perpetuate mistakes making ineligible officers beneficiaries thereof - In case of omission by DPC to take note of a relevant factor, it can consider case from all aspects subject to observance of principles of natural justice.
