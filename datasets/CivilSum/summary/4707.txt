Kerala Civil Services (Vigilance Tribunal) Rules, 1960, Rule 8(3) - Vigilance enquiry - Addition or alteration of charges - Tribunal adds charges - Accused given chance for evidence - Tribunal's inclusion of charge justified or not - Ruling states that changing, adding, or amending charges by the Tribunal before passing a final order is a procedural matter and as long as the accused is given ample notice and adequate opportunity for defense, there will be no violation of equity and natural justice rules.
