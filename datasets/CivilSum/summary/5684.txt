Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Chapter XIII, Rules 131 and 138(2) - Promotion of Head Constables - Promotions to the position of Head Constable must be based on a combination of selection and seniority, with consideration given to efficiency and honesty.
Additionally, passing the Lower School Course is required.
In limited cases, Selection Grade constables who have not passed the Lower School Course but are otherwise deemed suitable may be promoted to Head Constable up to a certain percentage of available vacancies with the approval of the Deputy Inspector General.
[Para 2]

Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Chapter XIII, Rules 131 and 138(2) - Petitioner promoted as Head Constable on ad hoc basis, being posted on the specialised post of Photographer - Institution of Selection Grade Constables came to an end by issuance of a notification dated March 18, 1980; therefore, the word "Selection Grade" is not to be read in Rule 138(2) - Rule 13.8 enables the D.I.G.
of Police to waive the requirement of training completion, subject to the prescribed quota under Rule 138(2), and promote a constable to the Head Constable rank.
As a result, the petitioner's potential reversion should not be considered, even if the promotion was made on an ad hoc basis.
Such a promotion should be evaluated on a regular basis.
The reversion of the petitioner is not legally justifiable or sustainable.
