Constitution of India, Article 311 - Delhi Police Act, 1978, Section 147 - Delhi Police Punishment and Appeal Rules, 1980, Rule 2 - Proportionality of Punishment - Reduction in rank from Head Constable to Constable as punishment, pending restoration to former rank as determined by competent authority - Later corrigendum specified period of reduction as 5 years - Charge of inappropriate behavior towards superior officer and entering police station without helmet - Punishment deemed vague, uncertain, and arbitrary - Unable to withstand judicial scrutiny - Punishment reduced to reduction in rank for 2-1/2 years due to some evidence of misbehavior and disproportionate punishment.
