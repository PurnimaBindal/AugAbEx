Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311(2) - Suspension - Suspension, though a part of contract of employment and a right of the employer, must be justified and not used arbitrarily or for extraneous reasons - Prolonged suspension without justification is unlawful and a burden on public funds - The petitioner was suspended in 1984 due to a criminal case but was granted the benefit of the Probation of Offenders Act - Despite this, suspension continued for more than eight years - The court declared this continued suspension unlawful and ordered immediate reinstatement of petitioner.
