The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-FF - Transfer of establishment or management to another establishment or employer - Mala fide transfer of workmen - Unfair labour practices - If the transfer is en masse to absorb the existing workmen into another establishment with their work and with no change in service conditions, and based on an accepted settlement, it cannot be considered targeted to victimize a few only.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F, 25-FF, 25-FFF - Scope of - Retrenchment compensation - Change in service conditions - Transfer of establishment or management to another establishment or employer - Section 25-F is only to apply for the purpose of computing compensation, and the expression "as if" used in Sections 25-FF and 25-FFF merely envisages compensation computation according to Section 25-F Both Sections 25-FF and 25-FFF provide for payment of compensation only in the case of transfer or closure of an undertaking and for no other purpose.
Every workman who meets the requirement described in those sections will be entitled to compensation in accordance with that section.
