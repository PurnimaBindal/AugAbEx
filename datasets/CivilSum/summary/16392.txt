Constitution of India, Article 226 - Revision of pay scale - The government had decided to revise the pay scale of Assistant Sports Officers and Sports Coaches of Kurukshetra University, granting them the same pay scale as the Director of Physical Education.
However, there is no valid reason to deny the same scale to the petitioners working in Maharishi Dayanand University, Rohtak.
The respondents are directed to grant the revised pay scale to the petitioners from the date it was granted to Assistant Sports Officers and Sports Coaches of Kurukshetra University.
[Paras 2 and 7]
