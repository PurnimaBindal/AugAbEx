Constitution of India, Article 226 - Daily wages - Termination - Regularization - Petitioners were appointed on a daily wage basis and worked in place of permanent employees when necessary.
An artificial break was given after six months, and the petitioners were asked not to come to work.
The contention was that the respondents engaged in unfair labor practices by giving an artificial break in service and not regularizing services of pensioners.
The petitioners have completed 900 days in five years in view of the respondents' policy.
Since the factum of policy is not denied, the direction is given to respondents to ascertain the period of service with seniority and consequential benefits and to regularize the services of the petitioners.
The petition is allowed in accordance with the order.
