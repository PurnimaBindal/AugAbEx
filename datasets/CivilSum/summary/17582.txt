Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Regularisation of services - Promotion - Reserved vacancy - The seniority of a petitioner whose services have been regularized as a Clerk starting on January 1, 1980 should be determined from that point onwards - A petitioner who fails to indicate when a person of the general category was wrongly appointed against a point reserved for Scheduled Castes is not entitled to be promoted over other members of Scheduled Castes or Backward Classes who were actually senior to him - Only one vacancy occurred during the year 1993, and Respondent No. 3, who is admittedly senior to the petitioner, was promoted in that vacancy - Petition is dismissed.
