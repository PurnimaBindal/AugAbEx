Industrial Finance Corporation Act (15 of 1948), Section 43 - Regulations under, Regulation 33(1), Explanation III - Compulsory retirement - An employee who has crossed 50 years of age but not reached 55 may be compulsorily retired under Regulation 33(1).
While Procedure in Explanation III was not followed, it was held that the retirement is not invalid as Regulation 33(1) is separate and self-sufficient rule and Procedural formality explanation III cannot restrict the regulation's scope.
