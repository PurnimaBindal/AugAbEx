Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Bihar Pension Rules, 1950, Rule 2 - Pension - An employee appointed as Junior Instructor in 1964 who was promoted to Senior Instructor in 1973 and retired on 3111998 - Pay was revised w.e.f.
111996 after retirement - The appellant filed an application for re-fixation of his pay as on 3111998 and consequential revision of pension but it was rejected - The appellant is entitled to the benefits - The appeal is allowed.
