Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Rajasthan Education Subordinate Service Rules, 1971 - Appointment - Qualification - Post of Teacher Gr.
III - Petitioners who applied for the reserved quota of widow/divorced lady were denied appointment because they did not hold a certificate of Senior Secondary Examination, which was the minimum qualification required for recruitment, but instead held a certificate of Higher Secondary Examination.
However, the petitioners could not have passed the Senior Secondary Examination in 1986-87, when admittedly it was substituted in place of Higher Secondary Examination in 1989-90.
Therefore, the highest qualification acquired by the petitioners at the time should be considered equivalent to the Senior Secondary Examination, and they are entitled to be considered for appointment.
