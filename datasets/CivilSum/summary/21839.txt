Constitution of India, Articles 310 and 311 - Bombay Civil Service Rules, 1959, Rule 161(1)(aa)(i)(1) - When resorting to compulsory retirement and abandoning departmental proceedings against a delinquent, unproven allegations cannot be used as the sole basis for such retirement if there is no other material on the service record.
Principle to be followed reaffirmed: compulsory retirement must take into account the entire service record of the officer and should not be passed as a shortcut to avoid departmental enquiry.
In the present case, there were no adverse entries in respondent's confidential record and the Review Committee did not recommend the compulsory retirement of the respondent.
As such, the order of compulsory retirement was passed for extraneous reasons and the Division Bench was right in holding the impugned order was liable to be set aside.
