Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code 1908, Section 60 - Recovery from salary - Petitioner acted as a guarantor for loans taken by a few individuals - As per the agreement, if the payment was not made, the money could be recovered from his salary - Petitioner argued that the recovery was limited to the provision mentioned in Section 60(1)(i) of the Code - It was held that Section 60 of the Code applies to attachment and sale in execution of a decree and does not apply to the recovery pursuant to an agreement - Therefore, the recovery can be made as per the agreement.
