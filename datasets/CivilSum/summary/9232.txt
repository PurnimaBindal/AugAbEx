The Contempt of Courts Act, 1971 (10 of 1971) Sections 11 and 20 addresses Contempt of Court and touches upon Order of suspension of employee.
In a scenario where Decree of Civil Court quashes the suspension order with consequential reliefs and Petitioner is consequently not allowed to join office, a Contempt petition would not be maintainable.
The trial court's decree would effectively override the suspension and can be executed, therefore, making proceedings under the Contempt of Courts Act not maintainable.
