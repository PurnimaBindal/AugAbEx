An Army Officer had his name removed from the panel for promotion to the post of Deputy Manager (General), despite being empanelled and declared fit for promotion.
No reason was given for the removal and in the next selection, he was told his name would not be included without an explanation.
The Review Promotion Board was instructed to include his name in the first promotion panel and have his promotion date relate back to that time.
[Para 13, 14 and 15] B.
The petitioner challenged the denial of his promotion after being declared empanelled, but those who were already promoted junior to him are not necessary parties.
