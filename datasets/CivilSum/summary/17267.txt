According to Section 2(n) of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Head Constable under Railway Protection Force is not considered as a workman, thus an application for compensation cannot be maintained under the Act.
[Paras 9 to 13]

As per Section 10 of the Railway Protection Force, 1957, RPF personnel are considered railway servants for the purpose of the Railways Act, but they cannot be recognized as workmen under the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923.
[Para 13]
