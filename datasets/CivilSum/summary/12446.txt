Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Technical Education Act 1987, Section 3 - Approval of Educational Institutions - Approval subject to fulfilling prescribed conditions - Automatic discontinuation of approval - The paramount interest of students, rather than profiteering, is the primary concern of educational institutions - Non-grant of approval can be imposed if institution is found to be engaging in profiteering rather than catering to student interests - Commendations and orders of non-grant of approval due to failure to meet required conditions and provide amenities to students are reasonable - The institution may reapply for approval once all conditions are satisfied.
[Paras 8 and 11]
