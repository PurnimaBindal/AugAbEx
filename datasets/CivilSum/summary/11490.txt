Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Post of Principal of College of Vocational Studies - The required qualification for the position is a Masters Degree in the relevant subject - The purpose of the requirement is to ensure that the Principal can effectively teach in the college and impart education in the relevant subject - A candidate who is fully qualified to teach the relevant subject but lacks a Masters degree cannot be deemed ineligible to be appointed as Principal of the college.
