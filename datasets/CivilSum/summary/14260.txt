Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Compensation - The assessment of loss of earning capacity depends significantly on medical evidence of physical disability - If a finding is reached without the expert opinion of the doctor who examined the injured party, the case may be sent back for a fresh assessment - However, if a significant amount of time has passed, as in this case, the appeal may be concluded by directing the insurer to pay compensation.
