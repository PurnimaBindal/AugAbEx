Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 state that candidates on a waiting list for selection through a public commission have the right to be appointed within one year of the recommendations made by the commission.
However, there is no statutory rule governing this situation.
The waiting list's period of validity should start from the date of recommendations of the selection commission, not the date of selection.
The Commission must be consistent in its view to rule out any unfortunate situations for candidates.
Additionally, the selection commission and the government must be vigilant and must not allow inconsistencies between them in matters of selection and appointments.
The period of validity of the waiting list should be reckoned from the last date when the recommendation was made.
In this case, it would be unfair to compute the one-year period from the date when the first recommendation was made by the Commission.
The fate of the 11 appellants should not be jeopardized due to inconsistent and varying stands taken and moves adopted by the State Government and the Commission at different stages for different purposes.
