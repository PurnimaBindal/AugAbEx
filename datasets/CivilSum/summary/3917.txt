Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Limitation Act, Article 137 - Reference - Demand notice - Industrial Disputes raised by the workman under ID Act do not fall under the purview of Article 137 of the Limitation Act.
There is no particular limitation prescribed and the Tribunal can balance the equities by imposing a cut on back wages while granting relief.
[Paras 9 and 13]  B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25-F - Completion of 240 days - In cases where official service records of workmen are not produced by employers, adverse inference is drawn against the employer.
Here, the employer failed to produce the appointment letter and the workman pleaded a different appointment date compared to the employer's claims, thus adverse inference is drawn against the employer.
