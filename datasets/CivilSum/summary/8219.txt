Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-G - Length of Service - Discrimination - Fulfillment of 240-day requirement is not compulsory to trigger provisions of Section 25-G - If the junior employees were retained, termination of senior employee would not be considered valid.

Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 25-F and 25-G - Back wages - An illegally terminated worker is usually entitled to complete back wages - this is the standard - Any party opposing this must prove the necessity of deviating from it.
