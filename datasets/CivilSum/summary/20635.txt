Consumer Protection Act, 1986, Section 2(1)(o) - Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 75 - Jurisdiction of ESI Court - Consumer's dispute - Claim for damages for medical negligence of doctors of ESI hospital/dispensary - The consumer forum has jurisdiction over a claim for damages due to medical negligence beyond the power of Employees' Insurance Court.
[Para 21] B Consumer Protection Act, 1986, Sections 2(1)(d) and 2(1)(o) - Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 75, Sub-section (1) or (2) or (3) - 'Service' - Jurisdiction of consumer forum - Service provided by the ESI hospital/dispensary - The ESI scheme is an insurance scheme and the service given in the ESI hospitals/dispensaries to a member of the Scheme or his family cannot be treated as gratuitous.
The consumer forum has jurisdiction to adjudicate upon the case of the appellant as a consumer under Section 2(1)(d) of the Consumer Protection Act.
[Para 14,21] (iii) The jurisdiction of the consumer forum is not ousted by virtue of sub-section (1) or (2) or (3) of Section 75 of the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948.
[Para 21] C. Claim for damages for medical negligence - To make liable for negligence, claimant has to satisfy three ingredients of negligence, namely- (a) existence of duty to take care; (b) failure to attain that standard of care; and (c) damage suffered on account of breach of duty.
