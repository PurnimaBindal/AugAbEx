In accordance with the Jammu & Kashmir Liquor License and Sale Rules, 1984, the plenary power to grant licences under Section 20 of the Excise Act, 1958, has been exclusively given to the government by the legislature.
Delegation to the Commissioner is permissible through a simple executive order instead of any statutory regulation or Rules framed under the Act.
The authority has the inherent power to withdraw the delegation since the right to delegate is vested in them.
Thus, the withdrawal of delegation can also be done via a simple executive order.
[Para 5]
The government, which was able to delegate powers to the Commissioner, made the decision of delegation.
If the power was later withdrawn, it cannot be argued that the order impugned was legally infirm or ultra vires the Act or Rules.
