Co-operative Federal Authority (Business) Regulation, 1976, Regulation 68 - Disciplinary proceedings - The High Court has assumed that an oral inquiry is obligatory, even without referring to Regulation 68.
The Supreme Court is not willing to close the matter only on the ground that 16 years have passed since the start of disciplinary proceedings, especially when the appellant is not solely responsible for the delay.
The order of the High Court has been set aside, and the matter has been referred back to the High Court for the writ petition to be considered afresh, as well as for the records relating to disciplinary proceedings.
