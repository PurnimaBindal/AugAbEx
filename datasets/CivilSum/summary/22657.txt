Constitution of India Articles 14 and 16 - Annual Performance Appraisal (APR) - Guidelines for promotion to the post of Deputy Director, Indian Airlines in 1991 required consideration of APRs from the previous three years - Instead of APRs for the year 1987-88, 88-89 and 89-90, APRs from 1985-86, 86-87 and 87-88 were considered due to the Officer concerned resigning and not writing their APRs for 1988-89 & 89-90 - The decision to consider APRs from 1985-86 to 1987-88 was held valid under the circumstances.
