The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(1)(c) and 25-F, specify that the termination of an employee's services must comply with the mandatory provisions of S25-F According to the Labour Court, the respondents had worked more than 240 days within the preceding 12 calendar months prior to their retrenchment, and the mandatory provisions of S25-F had not been followed.
As a result, the termination of their services was deemed illegal, and they were entitled to reinstatement with full back wages.
The petitioner's argument that the termination of employment was covered by an exception contained in S. 2 (oo)(bb) of the Act was not accepted, as it was inserted into the Act after their termination.
The petition was dismissed, and the Constitution of India, Articles 226/227, was mentioned in the ruling.
