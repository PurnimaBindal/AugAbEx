Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Death - Accident arising out of and in course of employment - Deceased, a Coolie, fell from the Box of a Truck - Vehicle registered as "Box Carriage" - The argument that the deceased cannot be referred to as an occupant, covered under the policy, is invalid - Since there was no evidence to establish whether the vehicle was covered on all sides - It was decided that the compensation to be paid by the insurer is rightfully granted.
