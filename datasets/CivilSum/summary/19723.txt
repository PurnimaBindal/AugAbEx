According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 46-A(v), ex-servicemen must have their break of more than a year condoned by the government if they join civil services after being released from the military.
Haryana Government also issued instructions stating that the break in military service would be automatically condoned if it did not exceed one year and could be condoned up to three years.
The government has condoned the break in service, entitling the petitioner to the benefits of military service with all consequential benefits such as arrears of salary to be paid with interest at 12% pa.
