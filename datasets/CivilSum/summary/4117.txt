Prosecutors appointed under Section 25 Cr.PC are governed by Service Rules for service conditions, and not by Cr.PC The posting of Public Prosecutors for teaching in Police Training Colleges is not in violation of Section 25 Cr.PC The argument that Prosecutors cannot be assigned any other duty is erroneous.
[Para 14] B.
Senior Public Prosecuting Officers in Police Training Colleges are cadre posts that do not fall under outside cadre or deputation posts.
Consent from these officers is not necessary for their posting to other departments.
Instructing new members of the service is not only necessary but desirable for posting Senior Prosecuting Officers.
