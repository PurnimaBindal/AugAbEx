Constitution of India, Article 310 - Premature retirement - The competent authority and the Appellate Authority provided the employee with personal hearings and thoroughly examined his entire service record and therefore did not violate principles of natural justice.
[Para 6] C. Constitution of India, Article 226 - Decision of writ petition at motion hearing stage - The High Court must pass a speaking order even at the motion stage.
However, a detailed judgment like one passed in a regular hearing is not necessary.
The orders passed at motion stage need not necessarily record all submissions and authorities, whether relevant or irrelevant.
[Para 7] D. Constitution of India, Article 310 - Review - The scope of review jurisdiction is limited to where some material document was left out or discovered after the decision and could not be available despite due diligence, or where an error apparent on the face of the record is found.
It cannot review a decision based on its merits, which is possible only in appeal.
