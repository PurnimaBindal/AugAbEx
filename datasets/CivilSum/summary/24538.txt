Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 and 136 - Reservation - A general candidate admitted in a reserved category for an engineering course may not receive the benefit of such a course if he is found guilty of intentional misconduct or misrepresentation - However, if the candidate was under a bona fide impression and an interim order is issued in his favor, he can be allowed to complete the course, and if his caste certificate is cancelled later, he may not be considered completely guilty of fraud or misrepresentation - The Supreme Court can provide appropriate relief in such cases, and the doctrine of proportionality should be invoked - The petitioner has already suffered for three years in pursuit of the degree - To avoid wasting the significant expenses incurred by the government on the petitioner's education, the University is instructed to issue the degree on payment of Rs. one lac and with a further condition that the petitioner cannot claim any further benefits of reservation.
