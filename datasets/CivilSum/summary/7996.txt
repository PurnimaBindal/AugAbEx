Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compulsory retirement - Adverse remarks - The Petitioner was retired compulsorily at the age of 55 based on adverse remarks regarding integrity, which were given prior to the Petitioner's promotion.
However, as the Respondents did not retire the Petitioner upon reaching the qualifying service of 25 years, the full weight of the adverse remarks, even if they were of dishonesty, cannot be used to justify compulsory retirement at the age of 55.
Furthermore, the Petitioner's promotion would have an additional effect on the previously suffered adverse remarks.
The Petition is thereby allowed.
