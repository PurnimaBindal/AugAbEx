Orissa Aided Educational Institutions (Appointment of Teachers Validation) Act, 1989, Section 3 (as amended by Amendment Act 18 of 1989) - Amendment - The original condition for regularisation of services of ad hoc teachers, "who are continuing as such teacher," was changed by the amendment to "he was continuing as such teacher."
This amendment means that only teachers who were in service on the date the Act came into force are entitled to the benefits of the Act, rather than those who joined later and completed one year of service.
[Paras 23, 25 and 26]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation/Ad hoc appointment - The government made an act for the regularisation of services of ad hoc teachers on the fulfillment of certain conditions.
However, ad hoc appointments made without following the process of selection through the selection board are considered back door entries, which are detrimental to the standard of education.
Therefore, appointees cannot be made regular at the cost of subsequent regular appointees who fulfill the conditions for regularisation under the act.
[Paras 20, 21 and 22]

Orissa Aided Educational Institutions (Appointment of Teachers Validation) Act, 1989, Section 3 - Contingent appointments/Leave vacancies/Additional vacancies - The Validation Act does not protect contingent appointments, appointments made against leave vacancies, or against the workload of additional sections not approved according to the staffing pattern.
[Para 26]
