Constitution of India, Article 16 - ROPA Rules, 1980, Rule 5(28), 5(33) - Special pay - To fix the pay of a promoted employee, his pay in the lower post must be increased by one increment and fixation should be done at the next stage.
If the scale of pay of Supervisor Grade I and Supervisor Grade II is same, then technical pay or special pay should also be considered as a part of his pay while fixing his pay on promotion.
This is because both posts carry the same scale of pay.
