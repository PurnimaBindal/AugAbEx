Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Promotion - Ad hoc promotion - Interview and written test basis promotion selection - Govt. instructions dt. 1931976 that employees already working on ad hoc basis should not be declared unsuitable in interview while forming promotion panels - Supreme Court held such instruction to be not contrary to the Rules - DPC's action of keeping the petitioner below in merit to block regular promotion contrary to policy of Board and Supreme Court - If a candidate continuously working on the promotion post for over 20 years is reverted only because of lesser marks in regular selection, it's too harsh - Authority directed to promote the petitioner in the same panel with retrospective effect.
