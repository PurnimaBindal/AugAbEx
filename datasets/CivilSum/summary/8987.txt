According to Article 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, Punjab Service of Engineers (Electricity Branch) (Conditions of Service) Rules, 1939, Rule 7-A, the seniority of Assistant Engineers of Punjab Electrical Inspectorate Office should be based on the order of recommendations by the Public Service Commission.
The actual date of joining is irrelevant as long as it is within the designated or expanded time frame set by the competent authority.
The 1939 Rules have no bearing.
[Paras 15 and 16]

The Limitation Act of 1963, Articles 120 and 113, dictate that even an illegal or void order must be challenged within the prescribed period of 3 years to obtain the required remedy.
[Paras 20 and 21]
