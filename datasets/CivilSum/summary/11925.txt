The Writ Court does not ordinarily exercise its jurisdiction in triable limitation issues, and the petitioner should seek alternative remedies before the Civil Court.
[Para 5] B.
The petitioners, who work as Foremen, argued that the Board did not implement its decision to distribute posts of special Foreman between Foremen and Cable Joiners in accordance with their numbers, and that they have not received their fair share.
They have sought promotion from the date they became eligible for it, with all back wages and other relevant reliefs.
The Board acknowledges that Cable Jointers have not received their due share, pursuant to its selection, and directs that the posts of Special Foreman should be filled only from the category of Cable Jointers until their share is filled.
Until the Cable Jointers have received their fair share, no vacancies will be filled from the Foremen category.
