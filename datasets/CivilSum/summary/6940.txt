MAT No. 3452 of 2002 deals with the Applicability of Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 and West Bengal School Service Commission Act, 1997.
The selection process for the post of Headmaster started before the Act came into force.
The Court stated that the provisions of the Act and Rules framed thereunder have no retrospective effect.
The Court directed the authority to take a decision on the approval of a panel prepared by the Committee without being affected by the West Bengal Service Commission Act, 1997.
The selection process should be allowed to continue if it was initiated prior to the Act coming into force.
[Paras 16 and 17]
