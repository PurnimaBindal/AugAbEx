Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Central Civil Services (Leave) Rules, 1972, Rule 44(1) - Disability leave - Entitlement - An employee was assaulted by another employee and sustained injuries while present in School premises - The respondent became disabled due to the received injuries - This case is covered under Rule 44 of Central Civil Services (Leave) Rules, 1972 - Therefore, the respondent is entitled to the grant of special disability leave - The petitioner is directed to grant special disability leave to the respondent along with consequential benefits.
