Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Scope - The Minimum Wages Act entitles employees to claim minimum wages without adjudication.
However, the specific case of the petitioner's entitlement to salary for June 1994 to December 1994 depends on whether they worked during that time, requiring adjudication.
Therefore, their claim under Section 33-C(2) would not apply to this aspect and the matter has been sent back to the Labour Court for the application to be considered solely on the merits of the claim for minimum wages.
