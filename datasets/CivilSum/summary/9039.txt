Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2-A and 10(1) - Employer - Competent authority - Workman implicated three companies as sister concerns in the demand notice served - Later, in front of the Conciliation Officer, he claimed Ambala Bus Syndicate as his employer, but in the Labour Court, he did not make any such claim - The Labour Court did not give any finding on who was the employer responsible for termination and reinstatement of the workman but still ordered reinstatement - As a result, the Award of the Labour Court is flawed - Thus, the Award is annulled, and the case is referred back to determine the employer responsible for meeting the award.
