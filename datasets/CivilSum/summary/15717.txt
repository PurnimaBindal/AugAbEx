Guru Nanak Dev University Calendar, 1986, Volume II, Ordinances 10(j) read with 13 and Ordinance 10(u) read with Ordinance 11 - Examination - Unfair means - Private candidate - False representation - The petitioner was accused of using unfair means and obtaining admission on the basis of false representation while appearing for an examination after seeking permission from her employer.
After being disqualified for three years, the petitioner has now been exonerated of charges under Ordinances 10(j) and 13.
However, the charge of false information under Ordinance 10(u) read with Ordinance 11 was the only one proven against the petitioner.
This finding is also incorrect as two different certificates were to be filled in by the Principal of College (employer), therefore any incorrect information could not have been given by the petitioner.
Hence, the petition has been allowed.
