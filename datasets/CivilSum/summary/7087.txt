The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947 and the Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act of 1971, Section 11-A and 28 respectively, allow for habitual absenteeism to result in dismissal from service.
The petitioner in this case did not show improvement despite multiple chances to improve, and the punishment of dismissal was deemed appropriate.
