Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Village/Revenue Estate/Admissions/Education - Some seats in Technical Education Institutions situated in villages are earmarked for village residents who helped build the institution - The definition of 'residents of the village' includes residents of the whole Revenue Estate - Morni is a part of village Bhoj Jabayal and contains about 18 hamlets, including Sherla and Morni - The appellant, who lives in Sherla, one of Bhoj Jabayal's hamlets, is qualified for consideration for admission to the reserved seats allocated for residents of village Morni.
