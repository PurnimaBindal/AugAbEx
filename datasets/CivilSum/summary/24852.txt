Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal Pay for Equal Work - The Doctrine will only be applicable if there is no difference in the nature of duties performed - If two classes of people perform the same duties under the same employer, with the same level of responsibility and working conditions, the Doctrine will be applicable.
[Para 6] Additionally, temporary or casual employees performing similar duties are entitled to the same pay as permanent employees.
