Section 78 of the Bombay Industrial Relations Act, Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act's Schedule 4 Items 9, and Section 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act deals with the Jurisdiction of Industrial Court.
According to a complaint by Badli Workers, their employer was engaging in Unfair Labour Practice by refusing to continue them in the same department, however, the petitioner claimed that there was no assurance given to the workers.
To the court's verdict, there was no breach of any agreement, settlement, or award hence, the dispute does not fall under MRTU and PULP Act and has to be adjudicated under Section 78 of the Bombay Industrial Relations Act.
