In accordance with the Constitution of India, Article 311, an Executive Engineer was appointed Assistant Enquiry Officer.
The engineer acted as a witness and prepared the memo of charge-sheet against a delinquent employee who was to depose against the petitioner and support specific charges.
With the engineer's assistance, an inquiry report was prepared which led to a show cause notice being issued and the petitioner being removed from services.
The petitioner was not dealt with fairly, justly and properly, as the principles of natural justice were flagrantly violated.
As a result, the order of removal is liable to be quashed.
[Paras 19, 20, 21 & 23] B.
Although the petitioner is not entitled to full salary for the period he remained out of employment due to the quashed departmental enquiry and consequential orders, he is entitled to a compensation of Rs. 100 Lacks, including the expense of litigation for pursuing the case in court for the last 16 years.
Furthermore, the period from the date of removal to superannuation should be added to the qualifying service for the purpose of fixation of pension.
