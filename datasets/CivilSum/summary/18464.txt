Karnataka Govt.
Servants (Seniority) Rules, 1957, Rule 6-A - Seniority - Counting of previous service in defence - Rule 6-A only applies to a serving officer who was transferred or appointed to an equivalent post in State civil service at instance of State Govt. - Therefore, the petitioner discharged from defence when he opted in State Civil Service cannot receive benefit from this rule.

Reserve and Auxilliary Air Force Act, 1952, Section 5 - Period of reserve service - Whether can be counted as in-service - Being on reserve after discharge does not provide any lien over post - Therefore, period of reserve service cannot be termed as in-service, especially when reserve liability came to end before the officer joined State Civil Service.
