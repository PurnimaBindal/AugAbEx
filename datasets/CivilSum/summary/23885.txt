According to the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, the Official Liquidator's and Court Liquidator's staff, including those appointed under Rules 308 and 309 of the Companies (Court) Rules and the Central Company Law (Service) Rules, are entitled to equal benefits of pay and allowances.
They should also be permanently absorbed in regular service as Central Govt. employees with all consequential benefits, on the basis of equal pay for equal work.
The High Court's directions have upheld this entitlement.
The Govt.'s contention that these employees are not entitled to be absorbed/regularized because they were not employed by the Govt. in accordance with the rules and their appointments were only temporary and paid from the company's funds, were rejected.
These employees must also receive arrears of salary from the date of filing the writ petition and three years prior to that.
