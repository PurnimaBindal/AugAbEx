Constitution of India, Articles 12, 14, 16 and 162 - Scheme of regularization by State - Benefit sought by employees of Board - Sustainability - Respondents were employed on contract basis in two different schemes of Municipal Corporation, however, their services were terminated and their prayer for regularization was denied - It was held that a statutory board is an autonomous body and that they should apply the rules of recruitment laid out under statutory rules - Being a 'State' within the meaning of Article 12, they are bound to implement the constitutional scheme of equality - The State cannot issue directions that are inconsistent with the constitutional principles adumbrated under Articles 14 and 16 - The direction given by the Constitution Bench in Umadevi's case was only to regularize irregular appointees and not illegal ones - The judgment of the High Court was set aside.
