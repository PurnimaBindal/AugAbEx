Karnataka Civil Services (Appointment on Compassionate Grounds) Rules, 1996, Rules 5 and 9 - Compassionate Appointment - Application for compassionate appointment rejected due to delay in approach - Rules have a time limit of one year from the date of death of the Govt. servant who passed away in March 1994 - Delay caused by confusion surrounding the earlier rules being quashed and the matter reaching the Supreme Court - Plea that rules prescribing time limit are only directory in nature - Non-consideration of claim solely on basis of delay would result in miscarriage of justice - Directions given to consider petitioner's claim for compassionate appointment.
