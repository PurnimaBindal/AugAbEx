Constitution of India, Article 16 - Central Govt Service Medical Attendance Rules, 1944, Rule 3 - Medical expenses - Reimbursement of - The petitioner is entitled to reimbursement for purchasing a hearing aid on the recommendation of a specialist while under hospitalization in a recognized hospital.
The 1944 Rules prescribe hearing aids as a reimbursable item while the respondents do not have any guideline for admissible items.
The scheme must be interpreted beneficially and the principle of Contra Proferentum applies if doubt is raised by the respondent.
