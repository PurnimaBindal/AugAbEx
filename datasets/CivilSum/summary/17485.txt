The Punjab Civil Services Rules (as applicable to Haryana) and Industrial Disputes Act hold various provisions for removal from service and promotion of employees.
In one case, an enquiry officer relied solely on hearsay evidence to convict a petitioner of misconduct during training, resulting in an illegal punishment order.
The petitioner is entitled to be reinstated based on the unsustainability of the enquiry officer's findings.

A petitioner has the right to receive documents before the recording of evidence from witnesses in departmental proceedings, as per Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
Failure to supply such documents to the delinquent can cause prejudice.
[Para 19]

The failure to summon an official witness requested by a delinquent during departmental proceedings violates principles of natural justice as per Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 21]

Article 311(2) of the Constitution of India provides the right to a reasonable opportunity, which includes the ability for the delinquent to cross-examine witnesses and examine their own witnesses.
Failure to provide this opportunity vitiates the enquiry.
