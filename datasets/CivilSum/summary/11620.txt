Constitution of India, Articles 311 - Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 7 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume 11, Rule 2.2(b) - Pension - Withholding of - Penalty - The petitioner, a Junior Engineer, was issued two charge sheets before retirement, but no reply was filed and no departmental enquiry was conducted.
The respondents imposed a punishment of recovery to recover pecuniary loss caused by the petitioner's negligence and breach of order, resorting to procedure for major penalty, instead of providing adequate opportunity to represent before imposition of minor penalty under Rule 8.
The petition was allowed, as the petitioner had been given the impression that an enquiry would be held on issuance of the charge sheet, and the order of recovery was set aside.
