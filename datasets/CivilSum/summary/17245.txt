After being selected as a Traffic Inspector in APSRTC and completing a 12-month training period, the petitioner executed an agreement with the Corporation that required them to work for five years and return the stipend if they failed to meet the terms.
Although they were later selected as Junior Assistant, they cannot question the agreement they executed on their own free will and as a result, the Corporation is entitled to recover the stipend amount.
This information can be found in the Constitution of India, specifically in Articles 16 and 226, as well as in paragraphs 9, 10, and 11 of the text.
