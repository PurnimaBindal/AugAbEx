Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 162 - Appointment/Interview - Cut off date - Candidates should have at least two years of service left on the scheduled date of interview in order to be considered for calling for interview - Communication from one officer to another officer indicated that candidates with less than 2 years left over service will not be considered - Two circulars relied upon lack statutory or executive backing and do not lay down specific guidelines - Cut off date for computing two years service as the date of interview is not reasonable as the date of interview is uncertain - Date on which the Selection Board initiated action for filling vacancies is a reasonable cut-off date - Petitioner eligible for selection.
