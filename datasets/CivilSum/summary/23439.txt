Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - U.P.
High School and Intermediate Colleges (Reserve Pool Teachers) Ordinance, 1978, Section 4 - Appointment - Strike - When the appellant and others were recruited during a strike and all the teachers resumed work, their services were terminated - The Government passed an ordinance creating a reserve pool of teachers like the appellants and stating that they would be appointed to any vacancies which may occur anywhere in the State - Reserve pool teachers are qualified to be appointed to vacancies which will be filled by direct recruitment as they occur.
