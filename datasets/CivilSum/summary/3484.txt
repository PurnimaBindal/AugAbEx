The Punjab Ayurvedic Department (Class III Technical) Services Rules, 1963 and Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 are involved in vaidyas' claiming posts of District Ayurvedic and Unani Officers.
The action of respondents prescribing qualifications of degree with a course of five years is being challenged.
The contention is that at the time when petitioners passed the degree course of Ayurveda, the same was for less than five years.
However, juniors were promoted, and the court held that petitioners can't be discriminated merely because they obtained a degree at a time when the duration of the course was prescribed for promotion.
The action of respondents in promoting juniors is in violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the constitution, and it is illegal and arbitrary.
The court directed the respondents to consider the case of petitioners for promotion from the date their juniors were promoted, grant consequential benefits, and pay arrears.
