Punjab Irrigation Research (Class-II) Rules, 1963, Rule 9 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 309 - Selection/Age relaxation - Competent authority under the rules may exercise discretion to relax age limit for the selection of Assistant Research Officers in the Irrigation Deptt.
Punjab - Fair exercise of discretion in favour of a qualified candidate is not arbitrary and consequential selection and appointment are not illegal.
[Para 11] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection/Experience - If a candidate was appointed on fair considerations and has worked on the post for a sufficient amount of time, slight lack of experience on the feeder post should not be a reason for quashing the selection.
[Para 13] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection/Appointment - If some selected candidates have not joined, and posts are lying vacant, unsuccessful candidates who have a good service record and career in the department, but are far below in merit, should be considered against the vacant posts.
