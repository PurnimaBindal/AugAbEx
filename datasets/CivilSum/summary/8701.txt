Section 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F of Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 deals with retrenchment of a workman due to misconduct.
If a workman is terminated on grounds of misconduct without any enquiry into the matter, it amounts to retrenchment in violation of Section 25-F In such cases, the workman is entitled to reinstatement with full back wages as awarded by the Labour Court, which has been upheld.
