The Arbitration provisions under Sections 59 and 60 of the Delhi Co-operative Societies Act, 1972 allow for disputes to be referred for arbitration by the Registrar of Co-operative Societies.
This was confirmed in a case where a new Managing Committee of a Cooperative Society made a complaint to the Registrar about irregularities in the purchase of building materials by the previous Committee.
The Reference to Arbitration was held to be valid as the dispute fell within the ambit of Section 60, which is illustrative and not exhaustive.
