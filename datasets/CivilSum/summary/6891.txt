Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Contractual employees - Dearness allowance - Employees hired on a contractual basis for a fixed salary cannot demand allowances that are available to permanent employees - They cannot receive dearness allowance or be treated equally to permanent employees under the principle of equal pay for equal work - The responsibilities of a contract employee cannot be compared to that of a permanent employee - Even if there may not be a quantitative difference, there is a qualitative difference between the two positions.
[Paras 8 and 9]
