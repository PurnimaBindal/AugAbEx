According to Section 10(1)(c) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the appropriate Government cannot determine whether a workman is covered within the definition of Section 2(S) of the same act or not while making or declining to make a reference.
Despite a demand notice by the Junior Engineer of Sugar Mill, the reference was declined on the basis that the duties and functions were of an administrative nature.
This decision on the merits of the case fell outside the jurisdiction of the Government.
As a result of this decision, the Government has been directed to make a reference to the Labor Court within one month.
