The Assistant Superintendent position at the Quality Marking Centre (Textile) is considered a single post cadre, even though there are two posts.
One is meant to be filled by promotion and the other by direct recruitment.
Therefore, this is considered a single post cadre for both promotees and direct recruites.
As a result, there can be no reservation in a single post cadre.
[Para 6]  B.
The rule of carry-forward does not apply in this case, as it is a single post cadre.
Reservation is not permitted in a single post cadre, and the instructions dated 11101974 do not apply.
The decision of the Single Judge has been upheld, with reference to the Letters Patent Appeal, Clause X., 1981 (1) SLR 461.
