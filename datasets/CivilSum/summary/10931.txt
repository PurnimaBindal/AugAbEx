The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay scales - Revision of pay scales - Cut off dates - While the Government can exercise administrative power to grant pay scales from any date, this cannot be accepted without consideration - The State cannot arbitrarily set different dates for pay scale revisions among employees who are in the same situation unless there are valid reasons for different treatment - Usually, parity has to be maintained among similarly situated employees - Implementing revised pay scales for similarly situated individuals from different dates violates the fundamental rights of equality guaranteed under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
