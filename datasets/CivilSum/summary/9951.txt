When a selection is based on interviews and academic performance, reserving 50% of marks for viva voce test is acceptable under Articles 14, 16, and 226 of the Constitution of India.
The selection is not invalid due to reserving 50% of marks for viva voce tests.
[Para 9] C The publication of the entire result is not required for selection as unconstitutional under Articles 14 and 226.
Therefore, a non-publication of the entire result is not a reason to quash the selection.
[Para 11] D When the criterion for selecting meritorious candidates for a limited number of vacancies is observed, non-observance of the principle of interviewing a limited number of candidates for a limited number of positions cannot be used as grounds for invalidating the selection process under Articles 14 and 16.
[Para 12] E. The selection of candidates for more vacancies advertised violates Articles 14 and 16 if those candidates were not advertised for future vacancies-due to the potential for increased eligibility of some candidates for future vacancies who should have had an equal opportunity to compete for the positions in the first place.
[Paras 13 and 17] F. It is illegal to have different selection committees for selection to a large number of vacancies under Articles 14 and 16.
Although a little judgment variation is natural, it cannot provide grounds for challenging the selection process.
[Para 16] G If some candidates are alleged to be selected despite their ineligibility, the state has the right to take action after verification later on.
Selection cannot be quashed for that reason according to Articles 14 and 16.
