Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Naib Tehsildar (Class-III) Rules, 1984, Rule 7 - Pay scale - Promotion - Departmental examination - Petitioner denied additional chance to clear one paper out of eight due to death of a close relation because he failed to disclose the relationship; denial found to be legal.
However, denial of an additional chance to petitioner, when other persons were given the same benefit in similar circumstances, is deemed arbitrary and discriminatory.
