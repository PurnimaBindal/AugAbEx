In accordance with Section 3 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, compensation is awarded to a deceased conductor who suddenly collapsed while on his way to report for duty and ultimately died in hospital.
However, the incident did not occur within the place of employment but on the road.
The Commissioner, by extending the theory of notional extension, stated that the incident occurred during the course of employment, which is not correct.
It was concluded in Paras 4 and 8 that the Commissioner was not right in holding that the incident took place during the course of employment.
