Constitution of India, Article 14 - Selection/Promotion/Interview - Promotion by way of selection - Promotion from the post of Sub-Inspector to the post of Inspector Police - The Board of Selection gave selections taking 50% marks reserved for rendering allotment based only on the interview and the Petitioner, who was on CI List II, got nominated for promotion - As per the Supreme Court decision, interview marks cannot be reserved more than 15%, thus selection made by keeping 50% interview marks is defective - The Petitioner’s case was directed to be reconsidered as per the law laid down by the Supreme Court.
