Tamil Nadu Recognised Private Schools (Regulation) Act (29 of 1974), Section 22 - Evidence Act (1 of 1872), Section 115 - Estoppel - Dismissal of teacher of recognised private school on ground of misconduct - Enquiry - Request by teacher for educationists other than School Committee to conduct enquiry - Consequently, non-members were appointed to carry out the enquiry - Teacher was part of the process - Challenge to Committee's authority was not made - Thus, teacher is prevented from raising such issue now.
