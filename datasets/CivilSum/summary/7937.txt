Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14, 16 and 39 - Regularisation of service - Part time employees in the Education Department, who have worked for more than 7 years, are entitled to regularisation - Sub committee shall be appointed - Committee shall listen to part time employees in representative capacity - Claim cannot be denied on the basis of lacking essential qualifications - Directions issued to consider the case within 6 months.

Constitution of India, 1950, Article 226 - Delay & latches - The state has a responsibility to act promptly on its instructions.
[Para 10]

Constitution of India, 1950, Article 226 - Judicial Review - It is not appropriate to exercise power under Article 226 of the Constitution of India to determine the nature and quantum of work, as it primarily falls under the domain of the State and its specialised agencies.
