Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 47 and Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 state that an employee cannot be discharged, reduced from their rank or retired prematurely because of their disability.
The competent authority must accommodate them in the same pay on an alternative suitable post, even if a supernumerary post is to be created.
[Paras 9 and 12] B Under Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 and Disability during service, the opinion of a departmental medical officer cannot supersede an opinion given by the All India Institute of Medical Sciences, certifying a petitioner to be fit for doing a desk job.
Even if a second opinion is required, such an institution's opinion shall prevail.
[Para 8]  C The existence of an alternate remedy is not an absolute bar for exercising jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution, as per Constitution of India, Article 226.
