Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Pension regulation for Army, regulation 173 - Disability pension - The petitioner suffered a fracture of ankle and schizo Affective Psychosis while working as a sepoy.
He was subsequently discharged from service due to his disability.
It is not disputed that he was fully fit at the time of enrolment.
The respondents are directed to consider the petitioner's case for disability pension in accordance with the rules.
