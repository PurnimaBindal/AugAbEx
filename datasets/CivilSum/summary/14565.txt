Under Section 14-B of the Employee's Provident Fund & Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, a Provident Fund Code Number is required for depositing Provident Fund.
However, the non-allotment of a Provident Fund Code number by the authorities is not a valid reason for the non-deposit of Provident Fund for a long time.
The petitioner company, being a branch of its Principal establishment having a code number, could have made the deposit in the Principal establishment's code number.
[Para 7]

In the case of non-payment of Provident Fund by a sick company under Sections 17 and 22(1) of the Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provisions) Act, 1985, the Board of Industrial & Financial Reconstruction can halt penal action where the matter is pending decision in connection with the rehabilitation scheme.
However, if the Board does not exercise its jurisdiction properly, the court may set aside the impugned order and refer the matter back to the Board for re-consideration.
