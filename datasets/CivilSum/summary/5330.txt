Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Natural justice - Prejudice - While the punishing authority has the jurisdiction to disagree with the findings of the enquiry officer, it cannot impose the punishment of dismissal from service without giving the delinquent an opportunity to defend himself - Otherwise, it would be prejudicial to the delinquent - If the delinquent is given a chance to state his reasons for disagreement, he could convince the authority to rely on the findings of the E.O.
- The action taken by the punishing authority is therefore held as violative of the principles of natural justice - The authority is directed to restart the proceedings from the stage of notice of disagreement, after giving opportunity to the petitioner.
