The Petitioner, claiming to be a scheduled caste candidate seeking admission to Medical College and to be the adopted son of a Harijan, disputes his adoption as it is contrary to the law.
He later claims his adoption to be permissible by custom, though custom cannot be presumed.
The party alleging to be governed by a particular custom must plead and prove existence of such custom supported by evidence.
Custom cannot be proved in writ jurisdiction, applicable jurisdiction being the Civil Court.
