The age of retirement for employees under the State Bank of India (Subsidiary Banks) Act, 1959, Sections 11(1) and 63 is fixed at 58 years or 30 years of service, whichever comes earlier.
This is not an arbitrary regulation as the 30-year period of service is not insignificant for gainful employment.

The term "tenure post" in service jurisprudence refers to a fixed term during which an office is held.
[Para 8] C In accordance with Constitution of India, Article 16, the age of retirement for employees of nationalized banks is fixed at 58 years, while employees of the subsidiary banks of the nationalized bank are retired upon completing 30 years of service.
The employees of the subsidiary bank cannot claim equal status as those of the nationalized bank as the two are rationally differentiated on the basis of policy.
