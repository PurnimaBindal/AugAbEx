Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Admission - Migration - Migration should not be treated as equivalent to fresh admission on merit.
If multiple students are seeking migration for a limited number of seats, it should be granted based on merit, provided their circumstances are similar.
The rules of the university and guidelines provided by the Dental Medical Council of India must be followed in all situations, and the educational standards of both the college from which migration is being sought and the college in which it is being sought cannot be ignored.
