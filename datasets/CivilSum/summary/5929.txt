Constitution of India, Article 14 - The Haryana Food and Supplies Department Sub-Officer (Group C) Services Rules, 1982, Rule 11 - Seniority - Ad hoc Service - Strict adherence to specific statutory rules is required for the fixation of seniority of members of service - The term "member of service" has a specific meaning and applies to the seniority of regular appointees - If rules indicate that seniority should be determined according to merit by the Board, that determination should not be disturbed - Seniority must be fixed according to Select list and ad hoc service cannot be used for that purpose, although other benefits such as leave, increment and pension may be claimed.
