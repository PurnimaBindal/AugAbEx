Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Promotion/Promotion Policy/Eligibility - A promotion policy was established in accordance with an agreement with the employees union that included specific qualifications and standards.
Despite this policy, promotions were made based on recommendations from the workers union, damaging the working environment and rendering the promotions illegal.
However, all individuals in similar circumstances have the right to be considered for promotion using the same criteria, as mandated by law.
The bank has been directed to treat the petitioner's case in the same manner as their peers for promotion.
