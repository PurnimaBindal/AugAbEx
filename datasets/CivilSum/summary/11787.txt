Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Change in date of birth - Age of superannuation - Maximum tenure of service - Minimum age requirement - According to the rule, a person can work for only 42 years, within the age range of 18 to 60 years.
The retirement age is 60 years and one must have joined service at 18 years of age.
If an employee has completed 42 years of service, he cannot request a change in his date of birth to extend his tenure.
It can presume that the employee either provided incorrect date of birth information or is submitting false documents to support his case.
He cannot benefit from both ends under these circumstances.
