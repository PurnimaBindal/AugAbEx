Constitution of India, Article 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 29 - Award of Labour Court - Enforcement of the award - In a case where an application for enforcement of an award and payment of arrears due to the worker has been pending before the authority/Regional Labour Commissioner for over five years, and where no direction was issued by the Labour Commissioner, the respondents are directed to enforce the award within two months and make the payment with interest of 10%.
