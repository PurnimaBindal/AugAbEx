Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary matter - The conclusion of the authorities that the delinquent was guilty of misconduct is not flawed by any procedural error.
Article 226 does not permit interference in such cases.
The delinquent may argue that the statement of allegation was vague, that he was not provided with proper material, and that his statement was recorded before the prosecution.
However, his offer to be examined first is taken into account and it is found that he does not show how he was prejudiced.
Therefore, no interference is appropriate.
B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Quantum of punishment - In cases of misconduct, the punishment should be proportionate to the offense.
If the petitioner submits a representation about the severity of the punishment, it should be considered and dealt with according to the law.
