According to Article 30(1) of the Indian Constitution, the "Establishment" and "Administration" of Minority Institutions/Communities must be upheld.
However, in the case of an appellant-management appointed Headmaster disobeying R 44 of Chap.
XIVA-KER by not following the ordinary rule of seniority, the management is not protected under Art. 30(1) in administering the school.
There is also no evidence to suggest that the school in question was established as a minority institution.
In fact, it was established by an individual.
As a result, the minority community must have a role to play not only in the subsequent administration of the institution but also in its initial establishment.
In the original petition, the institution managed by the appellant was rightfully declared not a minority institution and not entitled to protection under Art. 30(1) of the Constitution of India.
