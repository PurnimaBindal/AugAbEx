Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference - The government is obligated to refer disputes to the proper authority for adjudication and cannot decline to do so simply because the demands mentioned in the demand note were deemed not liable for adjudication due to the petitioner's failure to attend to his duties despite reminders from management and through newspapers - The Labour Commission cannot decide disputed questions of fact between parties beyond its scope - The respondent government must make a new decision regarding the question of reference.
