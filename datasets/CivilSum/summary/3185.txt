UP Subordinate Civil Court Inferior Establishment Rules, 1955, Rule 12 - Waiting list operates only until notified vacancies are filled, not until the waiting list is exhausted - Revision of list does not mean it lasts until all candidates on waiting list are appointed.
[Paras 29, 30, 42 and 43]

UP Subordinate Civil Court Inferior Establishment Rules, 1955, Rule 12 - Reasonable dimension for waiting list is a moderate one containing a proportional number of candidates in relation to the notified and anticipated vacancies.
[Para 24]

Writ jurisdiction - Natural justice - Court need not issue writs on grounds of non-observance of natural justice where only one conclusion is possible and natural justice is not relevant - Courts should not issue unwarranted writs.
