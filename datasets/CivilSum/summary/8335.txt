Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11A - The respondent bus conductor was terminated by the Disciplinary Authority after a quarrel with passengers led to him stabbing one.
However, the Labour Court ordered reinstatement without giving reasons for its decision that punishment was disproportionate.
The court held that the Labour Court exceeded its jurisdiction by interfering with the punishment awarded without sufficient reasons and that its decision was perverse.
Therefore, it was quashed.
