Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 4(3) - Suspension - Remand by appellate or revisional authority setting aside the dismissal, termination or removal - The suspension will be considered as continuing from the date of original order of dismissal, removal or compulsory retirement and will remain active according to the Rule - Labeling the direction to extend the suspension as illegal is not valid.
