Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Post of Teacher Grade III - Qualifications - Selection - The petitioner fulfilled all the required qualifications for the post of Teacher Grade III when selected and placed on the waiting list on 15698 and later faced the de-recognition of one of her degrees on 271099.
However, this de-recognition does not disqualify the petitioner from her previous selection and juniority cannot be used as a basis for denial of appointment.
[Paras 6, 8 and 9]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Select list - Waiting list - Expiry of - The petitioner, who was selected and placed on the waiting list, cannot be denied appointment on the pretext of select list expiry or the post being filled by someone else.
If the fault for select list expiry lies elsewhere and the petitioner was otherwise entitled to the appointment, the expiry cannot be used to deny the appointment.
Denial of appointment despite candidate eligibility is violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
[Paras 12 and 13]
