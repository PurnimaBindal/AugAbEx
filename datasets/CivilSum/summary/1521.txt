In Section 11A of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, the quantum of punishment for dismissal from service without any major misconduct is discussed.
The act says that past minor punishments should not be considered in the future, especially if the allegations were not of a serious nature.
Therefore, punishing someone with dismissal from service for mere allegations of using abusive language is disproportionate.
It was held that depriving the employee of 60% of their wages would be an appropriate punishment given their past record and the misconduct.
