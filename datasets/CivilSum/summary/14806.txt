According to the Punjab State Electricity Board Security Force Service Regulations of 1976, Regulation 6(iii), deputation appointments cannot be counted towards the quota reserved for promotions from Security Guard to Assistant Security Inspector.
This means that despite filling vacancies through deputation for a long period of time, Security Guards are still entitled to promotion in accordance with the 40% reservation quota prescribed by Regulation 6(iii)(a).
Adherence to the quota rule is still achievable and mandatory, as stated in Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16.
