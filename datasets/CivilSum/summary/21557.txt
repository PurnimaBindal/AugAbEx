According to Section 11-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the Labour Court has the discretion to determine the punishment for employee misconduct.
In this case, the Labour Court chose to substitute the penalty of dismissal with a lesser punishment due to the mitigating circumstances of the charges against the roadways conductor.
The High Court approved the Labour Court's decision of reinstatement with a lesser punishment, and there is no reason to interfere with this outcome based on the facts of the case.
