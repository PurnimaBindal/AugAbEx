Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection/Selection List - Recruitment/Public Service Commission - Overage - Selection for anticipated vacancies by Selection Committee - Despite being overage, petitioners who were selected and had a selection list prepared by the Selection Committee for anticipated vacancies in 1986, but now await appointment due to the posts being within the purview of PSC, are allowed to compete before the Commission pursuant to directions issued.
[Paras 10 and 11]
