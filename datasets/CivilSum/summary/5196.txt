The scope and jurisdiction of Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 are quite limited and differ from those of Section 10.
Only claims that are undisputed and computable in terms of money are covered.
In a specific case where a workman's claim for wages during a period of transfer, relieving, and joining was disputed on facts and the transfer order still pending challenge through a writ petition, an application under Section 33-C(2) of the ID Act was not maintainable for such disputed claims.
As a result, the Labour Court's order was quashed.
