Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Holding current duty charge by a lower-post officer is not promotion, and no rights, equities, or expectations can be built upon it - Taking back such charge does not attract Articles 14 and 16 - But while giving the current duty charge of a higher post to a lower-post officer, the senior person should not be deprived of his legitimate right to hold the higher post.
