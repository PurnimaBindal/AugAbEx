Air India Standing Orders, Clauses 20 and 23(4) - Medical Benefit Scheme - Retirees are entitled to medical benefits under the fundamental rights of retiral benefits, as per the Supreme Court's decision in the case of Regional Director ESIC - All employees, including pilots whose licenses have been cancelled or those who weren't medically unfit, are entitled to avail the benefits of the scheme.

Air India Standing Orders, Clauses 20 and 23(4) - Air India Employees Passage Regulation - Free Air Passage - Although the regulation allows for the grant of free air passage, it cannot be claimed as a right by an employee who has been discharged of his duties for misconduct, especially if there is no legal enforceable right available.
