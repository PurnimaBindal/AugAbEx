You cannot withhold retirement benefits of an employee who is permitted to retire on superannuation, unless there was a criminal case or disciplinary enquiry pending at the time of retirement.
[Para 4]

According to Section 4(6) of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, gratuity cannot be withheld unless the employee's services were terminated due to any disciplinary charge for loss caused to the employer or for any offence involving moral turpitude.
Withholding gratuity of an employee who retired on attaining the age of superannuation is not legal.
Initiating a departmental enquiry for irregularities committed resulting in loss to the bank or an ongoing criminal investigation is not grounds for withholding gratuity once the employee is permitted to retire on superannuation.
