Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2 and 33(216) - Dismissal - Appellant dismissed from service - Respondent-Management's first application was rejected by the Tribunal for not tendering one month's back wages, as mandated by the Act - Second application was allowed by the Tribunal after the back wages were tendered - However, the Tribunal's decision to allow the second application and reject the first one was not justified, as non-payment of arrears of wages renders the dismissal order void and inoperative - One month's wages would include all prior wages and wages for the next month - Therefore, the application filed by Respondent-Management stands rejected.
