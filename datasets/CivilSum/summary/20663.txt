Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion - Conditions for promotion - The appellant, a constable, was not promoted to the post of Sub-Inspector on the basis of not meeting physical requirements.
However, the rules are silent on the physical requirement issue.
The Government allowed others below the required height to hold the post of S.I.
Nevertheless, the appellant's chest measurement shortfall has to be ignored, giving him the same benefit as others.
