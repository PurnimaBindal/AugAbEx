According to the Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1978, Section 16, Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Rules, 1981, Rules 3(4), 9(1)(a), and Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16(1), if the same management runs three schools, one of which is exclusively for girls, the post of Head Mistress in Girls School is restricted to the senior-most lady teacher.
The post of Girls School becomes a solitary cadre, which means there can be no reservation, as it would violate Articles 14 and 16(1) of the constitution.
