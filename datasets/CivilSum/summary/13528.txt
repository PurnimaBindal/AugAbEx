Constitution of India, Article 226 - Seniority - Employees of Punjab Roadways - Following the handover of buses belonging to Punjab Roadways to the Municipal Corporation, employees were also transferred - After a writ petition was filed, the government reversed policy and returned the bus routes to Punjab Roadways - The petitioners were re-absorbed into the Punjab Roadways - A direction was issued in the disposed writ petition requiring the petitioners to be assigned the seniority they previously held before their service was transferred to the Municipal Corporation.
