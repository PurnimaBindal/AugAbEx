Constitution of India, Articles, 14, 16 and 226 - Review petition - Challenge made to the correctness of orders of Division Bench, as well as to the arbitrariness and validity of the selection process - Only the Apex Court can address challenges to the correctness of Division Bench orders - Challenges to arbitrariness and validity of the selection process cannot be considered through a Review petition - Such challenges should have been made before the appropriate forum.
[Paras 10 and 15]
