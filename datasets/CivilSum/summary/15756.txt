According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, recruitment and promotion in the Punjab Service of Engineers Class II PWD (Buildings and Roads Branch) must follow the Rules governing service conditions.
Ad hoc promotion cannot be considered regular promotion just because of a long lapse of time.
Such promotions must make room for seniors becoming eligible for regular promotion even later on, and ad hoc promotees have no right to a regular post.
[Paras 4, 5 and 7]
