As per Section 29 of the Punjab Water Supply and Sewerage Board Act, the State Government can issue directions to ensure that the service conditions of its employees who were transferred and absorbed in the Board are not varied to their disadvantage.
In this case, the petitioners were allowed to hold the current-duty-charge of the post of Superintendent Grade-I without being promoted on a regular basis.
However, the court directed the Board to consider the case of the petitioners for promotions to Superintendent Grade-I within two months.
Additionally, since the respondent Board did not file a written statement even after being served, the averments made in the writ petition were deemed to be admitted.
