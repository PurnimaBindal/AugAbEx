Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume II, Part-I, Rule 3.17 - Pension - Aggregate/Qualifying service - The petitioner worked on a work charge basis from 2191970 to 1851986 and is now requesting that ad hoc service be considered qualifying service for pension - This plea has merit, as work charge service followed by regularisation should be counted as qualifying service and breaks should not be included in calculating aggregate service.
