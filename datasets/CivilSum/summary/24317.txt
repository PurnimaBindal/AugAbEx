The Orissa Superior Judicial Service Rules, 1963, Rules 5, 7 and 8, and the Constitution of India, Articles 162, 233 and 309, allow for administrative orders regarding appointment, promotion, direct recruitment, and quota rule.
Quotas can be established by the government, and a 25% quota has been fixed for direct recruits, which is not considered unreasonable.
The constitutional validity of the rules framed cannot be challenged simply because chances of promotion for promotees are reduced.
However, it may be unfair for judicial officers involving themselves in frivolous litigation.
