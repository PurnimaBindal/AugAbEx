Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - Mala fide - The petitioner contested a transfer order citing mala fide on part of the Principal and one teacher who had hostile relations with her - However, failed to include the District Education Officer who actually passed the order - No evidence presented to demonstrate how the Principal and one teacher could sway the District Education Officer in their favor - Allegation of mala fide by petitioner dismissed due to absence of necessary party - No relief granted to petitioner.
