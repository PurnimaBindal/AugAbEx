According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, and Reserve Bank of India (Staff) Regulations, 1948, Regulation 13(4), the fixation of pay during a transfer depends on the category of each respective position.
The position of Coin/Note Examiner and Clerk Grade-II are considered two different categories.
The petitioner was appointed as a Clerk Grade-II through a competitive examination consisting of recruitment tests and a viva-voce.
When the petitioner accepted the terms and conditions of being appointed as a Clerk Grade-II, it became a bilateral contract, and he cannot now claim to have been discriminated in any way regarding the fixation of his pay.
This case pertains to fresh appointment in clerical cadres, and the service conditions is governed by Regulation 13(4).
