Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Volume II Rule 13.7 - Service Record/Test - Examination of service record is a suitable test to judge the suitability of a person for promotion, and the examination must prescribe maximum marks to determine the required marks for obtaining 50% - [Paras 9, 13, 17 and 18].
B Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Volume II, Rule 13.7 - Interpretation of Statute - The intention of the Legislature is the predominant consideration for interpretation of statute, and the plain meaning of words is given unless it leads to absurdity or injustice - [Paras 11, 12 and 14].
C Punjab Police Rules 1934, Volume II, Rule 13.7 - Constitution of India, Articles 14-16 - A service record assessment is a form of test for comparing the suitability of two persons, and the meaning of the world 'test' should be contextualized according to the rule - [Paras 15 and 22].
