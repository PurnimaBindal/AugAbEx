Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 14 - Promotion of Surveyors and Agriculture Inspectors in Department of Soil Conservation to the post of Sectional Officers against 10% posts of the sanctioned strength - Government ordered promotions for those who had served more than 10 years - No promotions beyond the quota allowed - 12 Agri.
Sub Inspectors were promoted as Sectional Officers - Petitioner challenged the promotion of 12 Agri.
Sub-Inspectors as contrary to the instructions that only six posts could be filled by promotion among the Agri.
Sub-Inspectors and the remaining six posts must be filled by promotion among the Surveyors - Surveyor quota was already exceeded, making the six promotions invalid - Petition dismissed.
