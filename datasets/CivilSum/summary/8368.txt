According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rules 419(b), 4.19(a), and 317A(d) and a Haryana Govt.
Memorandum dated November 22, 1991, an employee of Zila Parishad can be absorbed into Govt. service and granted a no objection certificate for their resignation to join the Health Department.
This will entitle them to receive pension in accordance with the November 22, 1991 memorandum, as long as they did not resign without permission and for the purpose of joining another department.
Petitioner in this case is entitled to receive pension.
[Para 11, 13, 14, 15 and 16]

The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 300-A state that a petitioner is entitled to pension.
In this case, the petitioner retired in 1977 but only became entitled to pension based on instructions dated November 22, 1991.
He made various representations for the grant of pension which were only rejected in 1997.
Nonetheless, he filed a writ petition in 1997 itself and as such, cannot be dismissed on the grounds of delay.
[Para 16]

Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rules 3.17-A and 4.19(a) state that an employee cannot be denied pension if they were discharged from service during their probation period with the observation that their work conduct and behavior was not satisfactory.
This cannot be considered dismissal from service due to misconduct.
