Industrial Disputes Act, Section 11 and 11-B - Back Wages - If termination is found to be illegal, the norm is to order reinstatement with full back wages.
However, the Court has discretion to grant back wages as it deems fit.
Workman, as a union leader, appeared in Labour Courts as an Authorized Representative of workmen before and after termination.
It can be presumed that he earned something from this practice.
Therefore, the deduction of 50% back wages cannot be arbitrary.
A workman need not prove that he was gainfully employed during the termination period, and if a legitimate presumption can be drawn that he earned some compensation, the Court can apply a reasonable cut to his back wages.
