Constitution of India, Article 226 - Entitlement Rules for Casualty Pensionary Award, 1982, Rule 27(c) - Disability Pension - The petitioner was granted a temporary disability pension in 1962 at 20%, which continued until 1989.
However, the Chief Controller of Defence Accounts (Pension) later ordered that the petitioner's disability was reassessed at less than 20% and the appeal was rejected by the Government of India.
The petitioner was also ordered to pay Rs. 23,370 toward overpayment.
The impugned order was quashed, and the respondents were directed to continue disability pension even for the period beyond 2361989.
