According to the Food Corporation of India Staff Service Regulations, as amended w.e.f.
16.10.87, Compulsory Retirement can only be ordered by the Competent Authority.
Additionally, an order of promotion as Technical Assistant Grade I by the Zonal Manager cannot be sustained if it is passed by a lower authority.
This goes against Articles 226 and 227 of the Indian Constitution.
