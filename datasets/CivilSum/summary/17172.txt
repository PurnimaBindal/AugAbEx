Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Qualification - Relaxation of - Post of Air Condition Coach Attendant in Railway - The Chief Personnel Officer holds the power to relax educational qualifications after consulting higher authorities - The Railroad Administration considered the contesting respondents' service and experience when making the decision to relax education qualifications for the post of Air Condition Coach Attendant - The Tribunal erred in overturning the Chief Personnel Officer's exemption orders that granted relaxation to the contesting respondents.
