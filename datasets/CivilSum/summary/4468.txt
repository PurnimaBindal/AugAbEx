Limitation Act, 1963, Section 5 - Appeal - Delay - Condonation of - An appeal was filed after an inordinate delay of 201 days, on the basis of the contention that the delay was due to administrative reasons.
However, it was determined that officers responsible for filing the appeal did not take prompt action even after the decision to challenge was made.
This was not considered a sufficient cause or case of bonafide lapses, but rather a case of irresponsible behavior by a government functionary.
Thus, the delay could not be condoned.
[Paras 9 and 10]
