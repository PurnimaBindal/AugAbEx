According to Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Electrical) Regulations, 1965, Regulation 10(3)(c) - Promotions are based on qualifications - Rule prescribing Classification based on qualifications between Diploma Holder Junior Engineers and non-diploma holder Junior Engineers for promotion as Assistant Engineers - It is not in violation of Articles 14 and 16.
This classification based on qualifications has previously been upheld by the Supreme Court in P. Murugesan v. State of Tamil Nadu, 1993(2) SCT 416 following State of J & K v. Triloki Nath Khosa 1974(1) SCR 771.
A petition challenging Regulation 10(3)(c) and consequential promotion of Diploma Holders was dismissed.
The decisions in PSEB v. RK Sharma AIR 1987 SC 367, VK Vermani v. PSEB 1981(2) SLR 346 & S.R.
Sharma v. PSEB 1980(3) SLR 75 are held to be per incurium and Full Bench in PSEB v. Ashok Kumar Sehgal, 1989(4) SLR 437 was followed.
