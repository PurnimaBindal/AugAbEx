Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 311 - All India Services (Conduct) Rules, 1968, Rule 16(2) - All India Services (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1969, Rules 6 and 24(1) - Writ jurisdiction - Disciplinary action - Charge - Enquiry - Cancellation of enquiry - De novo enquiry - Principles of natural Justice - An enquiry officer carried out a perfunctory enquiry and on the basis of same, the State Government dropped the case with warning - However, there was no clear cut finding as to whether the petitioner was guilty of misconduct or was innocent - The fact that an enquiry is conducted is to find whether the delinquent is guilty or innocent and he could not be both - The Central Government stated that there should be definite findings as to whether the petitioner is guilty or not and warned him if he was guilty - There was no infraction of principles of natural justice in issuing the impugned Government order appointing an enquiry officer to conduct a fresh enquiry - The 2nd respondent has the power to cancel the earlier enquiry report and order a de novo enquiry - The order passed by the Central Administrative Tribunal has no error or infirmity.
