Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(oo)(bb) - Contract of service - Fixed term appointment - If the term of appointment was not fixed at the time of appointment or renewal, making it a fixed term appointment later on will not attract Section 2(oo)(bb) of the ID Act.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 227 - Regularisation - If the State has a policy of regularisation, the employees must meet the eligibility criteria laid down in it - A senior cannot complain of discrimination if juniors meet the eligibility requirements and they do not - Setting a criteria or test as a condition for regularisation is not arbitrary or violative of Articles 14 and 41.
[Paras 10 to 14, 16 and 17]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation - Qualifications can be relaxed on the basis of experience only if the employee has worked for a fair length of time and was eligible when appointed or where such a long spell of service can be said to be adequate experience - The court can issue a direction for regularisation only if there is no scheme for regularisation by the employer.
[Para 14]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Ad hoc employees must be allowed to continue until regularly selected candidates are appointed and cannot be replaced by another set of ad hoc employees.
[Para 18]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Petitioners who were appointed on an ad hoc basis are entitled to a salary equal to those regularly appointed to the same position.
