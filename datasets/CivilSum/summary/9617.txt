Constitution of India, Article 311 - Army Regulation 163(b)(iv) - Premature retirement - The petitioner had the option to continue in service for 26 years of pensionable service or until the age of fifty years.
The withdrawal of the option resulted in retirement at the age of fifty years.
The writ argues that an employee should be allowed to withdraw their option if it is unfavorable to them.
The petitioner is entitled to continue in service until the age of 55 years as per Army Regulation 163(b)(iv).
The impugned order of the petitioner's premature retirement at fifty years old was wrong, and thus quashed.
