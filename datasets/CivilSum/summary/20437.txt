Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Central Public Works Department OM No. A-26017/4/91/EC/VI dated 1681991 and OM dated 2731991 - Higher pay scale on completion of 5 and 15 years service - Previous service before deployment in CPWD - Employees transferred to CPWD from other departments as fresh entrants are not eligible for their previous service benefits for seniority and higher pay in CPWD - These benefits can only be availed by those who were already present within the department - Previous service benefits can however be claimed for TA, joining time pay leave and pension.
