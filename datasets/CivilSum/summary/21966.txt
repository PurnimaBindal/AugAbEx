Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Voluntary retirement scheme of Punjab & Sind Bank - Revocation of VRS offer - Estoppel - Once an employee has accepted and received payments/benefits under the VRS, they cannot go back on their decision and withdraw from it.
Though the bank can withdraw the offer before the acceptance, the employee cannot rescind their acceptance after due appropriation.
[Paras 3 & 7]
