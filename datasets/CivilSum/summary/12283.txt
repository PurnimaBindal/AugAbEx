Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) - Fixed term appointment - Automatic termination on completion of work - Although the workman was given an appointment letter for six months, he was appointed to work in some other place after completing the work - He continued working for more than 240 days - Once it is proven that the workman worked for more than 240 days, the fixed term appointment letter cannot be relied upon - If the termination is found to be in violation of the provisions of the ID Act, reinstatement with 40% back wages is justified.
