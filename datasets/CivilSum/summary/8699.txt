Under Section 52(J) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the Irrigation Department is considered an industry.
This classification is supported by the AIR 1988 SC 1182 case.
[Para 7]
According to Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a Beldar who had been retrenched, promoted to munshi, and subsequently reverted to Beldar, did not join his duties after the reversion.
As a result, he was terminated from employment.
However, the reversion was set aside and the Beldar was reinstated.
[Para 9]
Section 25 of the Industrial Disputes Act specifies that wilful absence from work is equivalent to abandoning the job.
Therefore, an individual who abandons their job through wilful absence is not entitled to back-wages upon being reinstated.
