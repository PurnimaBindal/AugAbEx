Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Volume II, Rule 162 (1) - Dismissal from service/Misconduct/Gravest misconduct - The dismissal of a police personnel can only be done if they are guilty of gravest misconduct, and not for a minor mistake or eve a single instance of alcohol consumption.
There should be evidence of creating nuisance or incorrigibility to prove their unfitness for the service.
Dismissing an employee without considering their length of service or claim to pension is an illegal and arbitrary act.
