Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) (Rajasthan) Rules, 1958 - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 170 - Pension - Departmental enquiry - Retirement of Government servant - The order of a cut in pension after a departmental enquiry into misconduct conducted after superannuation was upheld, despite the contention that a cut in pension could only be made if the delinquent Govt. servant had caused pecuniary loss and not otherwise, which was deemed not tenable.
