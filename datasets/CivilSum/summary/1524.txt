Constitution of India, Articles 164, 14 and 16 - Pay scales - Re-organisation of States - Punjab Govt.
Instructions dated 23.7.1957 - If a teacher's pay had already been re-fixed or ordered to be fixed in accordance with the instructions dated 23.7.1957 prior to the re-organisation of the states and the teacher was subsequently assigned to the state of Haryana, then the Haryana authorities were obligated to follow the pre-existing orders and adjust the teacher's pay accordingly - However, the arrears are limited to 38 months prior to the filing of the earlier writ petition.
[Paras 13 and 14]
