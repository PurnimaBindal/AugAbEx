Constitution of India, Article 310 - Promotion - Fixation of - Seniority - Fixation after applying two stream concept ('command and staff' and 'staff only') - Officers in 'command and staff' stream of same batch ranked senior to officers in 'staff only' stream- Officer of earlier batch selected in 'staff only' stream outranks officer selected in subsequent batch under 'command and staff' stream.
