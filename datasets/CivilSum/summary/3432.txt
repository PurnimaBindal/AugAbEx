According to Section 2(9) of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, the definition of "employee" includes branch offices and establishments engaged in the sale and distribution of goods manufactured by the company, and even if the sale of the company's products is limited at these centers, they are not excluded from the ambit of the "employee" definition since they serve to promote the sale of the products.
The word "includes" in the definition serves to expand the scope of the term "employee".
