According to Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947) Schedule 2 Item 3 and Constitution of India, Article 311, disciplinary proceedings must include giving the petitioner the opportunity to represent their case.
In this specific case, the disciplinary authority did not provide the opportunity and there were no reasons given for their findings.
Therefore, the order of dismissal was deemed illegal and quashed.
It is recommended that the competent authority pass a fresh order in accordance with the law.
