Constitution of India, Article 311(2), second proviso, clause (a) - Termination on the ground of conviction - Acquittal in appeal - Reinstated with back wages - If an employee was convicted for an offence committed by them in their personal life, then acquitted in appeal, they are entitled to reinstatement but not back wages.
The employer cannot be held responsible for the employee's personal conduct.
The situation may be different if the termination was due to the employee's acts and conduct while employed.
The order to grant back wages is set aside.
