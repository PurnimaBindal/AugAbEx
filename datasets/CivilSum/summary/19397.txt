Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) is interpreted in this instance.
The management pleaded that their workmen's service was not terminated and could return to work without back wages.
Workmen raised an issue of termination of their services with the government.
The government declined the reference due to their belief that the workers' services were not terminated since management said that they could return to work.
The law states that disputed questions of both fact and law should be left to the authority to decide reference.
The government decided the disputed question in favour of the management, which it cannot do when it exercises its powers U/s 10(1) of the Act.
The government's order cannot be upheld because they chose to decide disputes that were not their place to decide.
Therefore, the order of the Government is liable to be quashed, and the Government is directed to refer the dispute within two months.
