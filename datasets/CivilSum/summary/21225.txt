According to the UP Secondary Education Services Commission (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 1981, Section 33-B and Third order, regularisation of ad hoc employees is only applicable when their temporary services continue in a vacancy in accordance with Section 2 of the Act.
If an employee's services were terminated but continued only on the basis of an interim stay order, they cannot be considered to have continuously served the institution, making them ineligible for the benefit of the Third Order.
This is because they were not continuing their service by the management's order.
