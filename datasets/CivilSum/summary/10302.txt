The Punjab Civil Services Rules (as applicable to Haryana) Vol. 1, Rule 44 states that JBT teachers who are promoted as Head teachers must receive one increment of higher responsibility.
[Para 2]
The petitioner, who is an Assistant Computer Instructor with a diploma in Computer Programming and an MA in Hindi, was being paid a salary of only Rs. 2000/- PM, which was later increased to Rs. 2400/- PM.
The court deemed this to be unfair and arbitrary treatment, and enhanced the petitioner's salary to Rs. 5500/- PM, effective six months before the petition was filed.
The employer cannot take the plea that the petitioner was not qualified for the post, as the petitioner had been on the job for nine years and her work and conduct had been found satisfactory.
[Paras 5, 7, 8, and 9]
The petitioner had been holding the post of Assistant Computer Instructor since 1992, and the post still needed to be filled.
The court directed the respondent to consider the petitioner for Regularisation within 3 months in an appropriate pay scale, and create a regular post for the petitioner with emoluments not less than Rs. 5500 pm The respondent's plea that the benefit of regularisation not be given as the posts were sanctioned under a scheme was deemed untenable, as the number of Community Polytechnics rose from 35 to 400 over time.
