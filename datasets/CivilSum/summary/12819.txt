Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Indian Penal Code, Section 420 - Concealment of fact - The application proforma in the notification did not mention the requirement to state the number of attempts taken to pass the qualified exam - Therefore, there was no concealment of facts - The termination of service without an opportunity for a hearing or show cause goes against the principle of natural justice - As a result, the termination order and F.I.R. u/s.
420 were nullified.
