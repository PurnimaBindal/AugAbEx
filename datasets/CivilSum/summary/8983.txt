Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 18(3) - Reference - Alleged illegal termination - The Labour Court received an objection that the reference was not maintainable as the employer, namely, the proper party, had not been impleaded before it.
The Labour Court upheld the objection and declined to answer the reference on merits.
However, the Pepsu Road Transport Corporation filed a written statement on merits and contested the reference when issued a notice by the Labour Court.
It is the opinion that the Labour Court should have exercised its jurisdiction to summon the Corporation under S.18(3) if it had not been properly impleaded with a view to effectively dispose of the reference on merits.
The Labour Court was not justified in leaving the reference unanswered on merits on a hyper-technical ground.
