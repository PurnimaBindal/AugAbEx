The petitioner was denied compassionate appointment solely on the grounds that the Scheme was introduced after the death of his father in 1969, however, the petitioner became a major after 1975.
The case of the petitioner was evaluated by the respondents up until the medical examination level, as per a letter dated 13.3.1989.
Despite having his request rejected via a letter dated 221188, such a rejection letter held no consequence due to the letter from 13389.
Consequently, the petitioner was deemed entitled for compassionate appointment in accordance with the policy.
