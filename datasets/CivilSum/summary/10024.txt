The trial court dismissed the suit challenging the order of punishment, but the appellate court accepted the appeal, stating that proper enquiry had not been conducted and no presenting officer was appointed.
The appellate court's finding of fact that the punishment was illegal cannot be interfered with in RSA, especially since the impugned order relates to the year 1984, and interference after a period of 24 years would not be in consonance with justice.
