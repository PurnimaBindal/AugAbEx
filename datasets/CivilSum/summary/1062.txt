In accordance with Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, it is possible to apply to become a party to a reference.
In this particular case, several employees resigned from the respondent union and joined the petitioner union, and a settlement was reached with the company, but without resolving the dispute regarding permanency that was causing the problem, and the tribunal impleaded the petitioner union.
The single judge reversed the tribunal's order, which was not correct, as the fairness and reasonableness of the settlement have to be assessed in the reference.
Lastly, given that the subject of the dispute was left out of the agreement, the petitioner is a necessary and proper party.
