State Govt. has laid down criteria that inter-se merit of sportsmen for medical courses shall be judged on the basis of their performance in the +2 course only, and various distinctions attained in sports prior to joining or after completion of the +2 course shall not be taken into consideration.
This classification has a reasonable nexus with selecting the best students as well as encouraging sportsmen.
State Govt. can choose sources from which admission to medical colleges has to be made and it is not for the Court to lay down the criteria.
The State Govt. has the right to establish its own criteria for admission based on the experts' decisions.
[Paras 13, 14 and 15]
The Govt. has laid down new criteria in 1993 that distinctions in sports attained by a student during the +2 course shall be considered only and various distinctions attained in sports prior to joining or after completion of the +2 course shall not be considered.
This criterion is valid but should be applied for the admissions to be made from the year 1995-96 onwards.
A number of students who had attained the requisite distinction in sports prior to joining the +2 course may have not chosen to participate in sports while studying for the +2 course as they could not have imagined that the criteria which had been in force for more than a decade shall be suddenly changed and the distinctions attained by them would be rendered irrelevant.
[Para 17]
Govt. is bound to lay down a minimum standard of qualifying marks in the entrance examination for admission against the seats reserved for the sportsmen.
A student seeking admission against the seats reserved for sportsmen has to secure minimum 35% marks in entrance test before he becomes eligible for admission to medical course.
Admission cannot be made purely on the basis of achievements in sports.
