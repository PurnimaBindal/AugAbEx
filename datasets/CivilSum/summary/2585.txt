Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Haryana Horticulture (Group A) Service Rules, 1998, Rules 7 and 9 - Appointment - Eligibility - The appointment of the Respondent as Director (Horticulture) was challenged on the ground of lack of relevant experience in the post of Joint Director.
However, the Respondent holds the post of Deputy Director (Horticulture) in CPWD, which is equivalent to the post of Joint Director of Horticulture in the State of Haryana.
Furthermore, the Respondent has more than 5 years' experience working as a Director in CPWD, making the appointment legally valid.
