Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Writ of Mandamus - West Bengal School Service commission Act, 1997 - Regularization - Appointment - An appointment made without initiating recruitment process is considered an illegal appointment - This renders such appointments devoid of any vested or enforceable legal right - The mere availability of a vacancy is not grounds for regularizing an initial illegal appointment - The court held that there is no scope for issuing mandamus to legitimize an illegal act - The petition was dismissed.
