Constitution of India, Article 226 - UP Recognised Schools (Junior High Schools) (Recruitment and Conditions of Service of Teachers) Rules, 1978, Rule 10(1), 10(5), 10(5)(ii) and Rule 11 - Selection for Assistant Teacher Position - Selection Committee only prepared three selection lists instead of four - In following Rule 10(1), committee made mistake and appointment of No. 3 on list was erroneous - Under Rule 10(5)(ii), DBEO could only return papers to committee to prepare list according to Rule 10(1) - Court quashed appointment of candidate from No. 3.
