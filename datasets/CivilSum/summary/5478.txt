Rule 43 of Bihar Pension Rules, 1950 states that the State Government has the power to withhold pension and gratuity alone, and not other pensionary dues such as provident fund, unutilised leave, group insurance, for recovery of pecuniary loss caused to the government.
[Para 12]
According to Rule 27 of Bihar Pension Rules, 1950, the term ‘pension’ includes gratuity and not other pensionary benefits like provident fund, unutilised leave, and group insurance etc. [Para 12]
