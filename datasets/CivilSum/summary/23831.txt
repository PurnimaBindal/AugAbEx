Constitution of India, Article 309 - Emergency Commissioned Officers and Short Service Commissioned Officers (Reservation and Vacancies) Rules, 1967 - Scope and applicability - Seniority and promotion - The 1967 Rules were intended for application in Central Civil Services - Inter se seniority between direct recruits and promotee SCOs - Executive instructions were issued to give benefit of past service in Army service to ECOs, in the absence of a provision in the main rules - The seniority list prepared on the basis of these instructions is in accordance with law and in compliance with the directions of the Supreme Court in Sahi's case - The authorities are instructed to make promotions using this seniority list.
