The terms of settlement illustrate that the respondent bank only had a willingness to consider a policy for compassionate appointment of sons, unmarried daughters, and widows of employees who had died or taken early retirement.
No actual policy was formed, so the fact that appointments were given on compassionate grounds before the industrial dispute does not prove the existence of a policy.
The High Court was therefore correct in rejecting the appellant-workman's claim for compassionate appointment.
