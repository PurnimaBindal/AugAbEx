Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 36(4) - Industrial dispute - Advocate - Appearance of - A company filed Vakalatnama and its advocate later filed the reply and affidavit but no objection was raised.
The workmen's application under Section 36(4) was rejected by the Labor Court.
The contention that the order is contrary to provisions of Section 36(4) of the Act which does not permit any advocate or legal practitioner to appear in proceedings under the Act without the consent of the party and leave of the authority.
It is held that no party can adopt an unreasonable attitude to exploit the situation arising out of Section 36(4) of Act to deliberate disadvantage of the other side.
The labor court can overrule refusal of consent on merit independently while considering the question of grant of leave.
Hence, in the absence of any miscarriage of justice to warrant interference, the order of Labor Court is legal and proper and the petition is dismissed accordingly.
