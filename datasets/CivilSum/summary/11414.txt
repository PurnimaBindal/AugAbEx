Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Punjab Civil Services Rules - Age of retirement - Despite revision in pay scale of Pump Operators, the status of an Assistant Pump Operator (a class IV post) does not automatically elevate to Class-III post - Therefore, any class IV employee must be permitted to continue working until the age of 60 years as entitled - An order to retire the petitioner at the age of 58 years has been overturned.
