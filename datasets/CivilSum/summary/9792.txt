Constitution of India, Article 226 - Refixation of pay - The Division Bench confirmed the penalty of reduction to time scale against the petitioner, but quashed the initiation of a denovo enquiry and the show cause notice for the penalty of removal.
They considered the fact that the petitioner had already undergone imposed penalty and concluded that there cannot be any fresh order of penalty for the same offence.
The petitioner himself represented before the court that he had already undergone the penalty, and as a result, an order passed refixing his pay in terms of the issued penalty of reduction to the time scale of conductor must be given effect to.
