According to Section 10(1) of the Contract Labour (Regulations and Abolitions) Act, 1970, if a public sector undertaking is under the Central Government, then any notification issued by the Lt.
Governor is not valid.
In this case, since the Central Government has complete control over the petitioner, the appropriate government for them would be the Central Government.
Therefore, the notification issued by the Lt.
Governor is without jurisdiction and has been quashed.
[Paras 6 and 7]
