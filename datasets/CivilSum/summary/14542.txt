Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Police Act, 1861, Section 7 - Police Regulations of Bengal, 1943, Section 1 - Suspension - Misconduct - Charge-sheet - The suspension order must contain the contents of the charges given to the employee, including the words and ground on which it was passed as "gross misconduct and dereliction of duty," as per the suspension order.
The suspension order must not be passed with a closed mind and bias, and an inquiry must be conducted in accordance with the relevant provisions and procedures.
The employee will be given all opportunities to defend themselves, and the charge-sheet will also follow the suspension order.
This will ensure that no prejudice is caused to the employee.
