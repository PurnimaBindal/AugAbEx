Panjab University Act, 1947, Section 31 - Panjab University Regulations, Regulation 4.2 - Admission - Eligibility for admission - Admission to BA-I under the Regulations requires a minimum standard of 33% marks in the qualifying examination, which has legal backing under Section 31 of the Panjab University Act.
The University cannot go against these statutory provisions, even if it means denying admission to a student, and the court cannot force the University to approve an admission that goes against the Act and Regulations.
Students cannot continue their studies for years based on court orders while the case awaits decision, nor can they legalize an illegal admission.
Even the doctrine of estoppel does not apply when there is a deliberate violation of statutory provisions.
