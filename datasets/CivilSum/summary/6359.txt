Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Punishment/Proportionate punishment/Jurisdiction of the court to interfere - The court can interfere to reduce penalty in special circumstances where it is found disproportionate to charges levelled against delinquent official, but not when charges are serious - Bank Manager failed to perform onerous duty to deduct monthly instalments towards loan obtained; Dismissal from service is not disproportionate to the charges.
