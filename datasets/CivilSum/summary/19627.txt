In accordance with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India regarding qualifications and pay scale, the petitioner's appointment letter stated he would receive a running pay scale and be treated as a fully qualified teacher.
Other stipendiary teachers appointed with the petitioner were also given the running pay scale.
Based on Government instructions, the petitioner was entitled to be considered for regular appointment.
Since he was appointed on a regular basis and treated as fully qualified at the time of his appointment, along with other stipendiary teachers who were considered trained Sanskrit teachers, he cannot be later treated as unqualified or untrained for the purpose of pay scale.
