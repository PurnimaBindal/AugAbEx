Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I Part I, Rule 4.9 - Pension - Increments - Extra services after retirement - According to PCSR Rule 49, any duty in a post on a time scale should be counted for increments in that time scale.
If the PRTC Service Regulations do not have such a provision, PCSR Rule 49 applies.
In the case of the petitioner, who was re-employed and had no service termination due to fraud, that service should be reflected in salary/increments.
[Para 6]  B.
Limitation - Counting of past service towards salary and increments - There is no limitation if the plaintiff wants this service to be counted for computation of retiral benefits, but arrears beyond 36 months will not be granted.
