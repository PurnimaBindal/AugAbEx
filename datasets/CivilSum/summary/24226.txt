Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Contract Act, Section 2(g) - Voluntary Retirement - Offer of Voluntary Retirement - The scheme of nationalised banks and State Bank of India limiting employee withdrawal of their voluntary retirement offers is legally invalid - Request of voluntary retirement does not take effect immediately but in the future - The scheme lacks consideration and enforceable rights, making it void under Section 2(g) of the Contract Act - Employees can withdraw their offers for voluntary retirement - The clause in the State Bank of India's scheme that reserves the right to enforce employee offers upon rejection is valid due to the consideration it provides - Resignation by a constitutional authority is always unilateral and immediately effective.
