Punjab Civil Services, Rules, Vol.
I Rule 2.42 backs up this point.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol I, Rule 235 - Lien - Deputationist - Vacancy - Officiating service - This rule states that a person on deputation can return to the parent department at any point, and that they hold lien in their department's cadre.
Officiating service against such posts does not bestow seniority on the officiating person in the cadre.
