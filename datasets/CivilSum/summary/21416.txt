Constitution of India, Article 226 - Letters Patent Appeal - Writ appeal - The judgment of the Single Judge of the High Court recorded the statement of facts from the case file that the delinquent was proceeded against with the approval of the competent authority, and the order of dismissal was issued by the punishing authority after going through the file of the proceedings - Such statement of facts recorded in the judgment must not be doubted, and the order of the Single Judge cannot be set aside on that ground alone - The case must be remanded to be decided on the other submissions' merits since the Division Bench's order has been set aside.
