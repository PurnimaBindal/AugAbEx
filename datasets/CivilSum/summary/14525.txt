Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection/Appointment - Merit list - The petitioner was selected by Haryana SSS Board and ranked at Sr. No. 7 on the merit list.
However, candidates who were rejected during selection or did not compete were adjusted against the advertised posts.
The authorities' conduct was completely unfair.
Therefore, the court directed to consider the petitioner against an available post or create a post by terminating one of the juniors or ineligible persons.
