Constitution of India, Article 226 - Haryana Civil Services (Class-II) Rules, 1978, Rule 11(3) - A petitioner who was ignored for promotion to HCMS-I due to adverse remarks during probation has been confirmed and his probationary period was never extended.
The significance of such remarks has been lost, and the court has directed that he should be appointed to HCMS-I from the date his junior was promoted.
Additionally, seniority is to be given over his junior.
[Paras 5, 7 and 8].
