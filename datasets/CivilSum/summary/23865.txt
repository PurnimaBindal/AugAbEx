The Electricity Board hired a Contractor to maintain plant and stations, who employed 42 persons without being a Licenced Contractor under the Contract Labour Act, 1970.
The High Court set aside the termination of employees who had completed 240 days by applying the Principle of Lifting the Veil to reveal that the contract system was a mere camouflage and the Contractor was procuring labour for the Board from open market.
Promoting continuity of service, the court held there was a relationship between Board and employees.
[Paras 18 & 16]
The finding of fact arrived at by the labour court cannot be disturbed under Article 226, unless it is perverse or there is an error apparent on the face of the record.
