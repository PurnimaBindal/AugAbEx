Constitution of India, Article 16 - Kerala State Planning Service Rules, Rule 2, Note 2 - Age Relaxation - Candidates who are already in the service of the State Planning Board may be granted age relaxation - The term "in the department" cannot apply to "any department of Government" - Planning and Economic Affairs Department in the Secretariat is the administrative department of Department of Economics and Statistics and the Department of State Planning Board - As such, an employee of one department cannot claim to be a member of the other Department.
The word 'the' used before the word Department, with reference to which an application is invited, can only refer to that particular department, namely the State Planning Board - The relaxation of age can only be extended to candidates who are already in the service of the State Planning Board - This Court cannot broaden the scope of a rule when it is specific in meaning.
