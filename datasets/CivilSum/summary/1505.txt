During selection appointments, having higher qualifications doesn't guarantee better quality.
A person's experience, aptitude and performance in an interview are also important factors for consideration by the selection body.
The petitioner did not question the method of selection or the competence of the authority and selection committee members.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to any relief based on qualifications and the court cannot reassess the candidate's merit.
[Para 6]
The High Court cannot overturn the decision of the selection body.
Instead, it can only review the procedure adopted for the selection process.
Moreover, the High Court cannot impose its own opinion instead of the selection committee's on candidate's inter se merit.
