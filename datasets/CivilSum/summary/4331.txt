Article 311 of the Constitution of India, Section 25N of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and Rule 76-A of the Industrial Disputes Rules - Requirements for retrenchment - Employer must give three months notice in writing indicating reasons for retrenchment, or pay wages in lieu of notice - Employer must obtain permission from the appropriate Govt. or specified authority, and submit application in prescribed manner - In this case, management planned to retrench 20 workmen including five trainees, and sought permission to retrench five - Permission granted, but without reasons given for refusing permission to retrench all workmen - Order quashed.
[Para 4]

Under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the order passed under Section 25N of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, granting or refusing permission for retrenchment can be challenged in writ jurisdiction.
