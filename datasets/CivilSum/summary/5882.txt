Constitution of India, Article 311 - Misconduct - Disciplinary proceedings - The writ petition was allowed, and authorities were given the liberty to proceed afresh from the stage reached prior to the issue of the 2nd Show Cause Notice.
The Disciplinary Authority finally decided that the appellant is guilty of misconduct, and their finding relating to all charges appears to be conclusive and final, not tentative.
Under the provisions of Article 311, even at the 2nd Show Cause stage, a delinquent is entitled to represent both against the finding of alleged misconduct and against the proposed punishment.
