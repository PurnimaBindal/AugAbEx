Constitution of India, Article 14, 15 and 16 - Pre-medical entrance Test for admission to MBBS/BDS course - The minimum percentage of marks for reserved categories is limited to 40% and, as a result, the marks of handicapped category should also not go beyond 40%.
This fixation of minimum percentage is deemed to be discriminatory, arbitrary, illegal, and also in violation of Articles 14 and 15 of the Constitution of India.
