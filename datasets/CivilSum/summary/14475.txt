Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 12 and 25-F - Termination of service - Re-instatement - Back wages - If the termination of service is illegal, and there is a null order of retrenchment, the workman remains in service with all relevant benefits - Even if the worker obtained employment for survival purposes, wages earned during that period cannot be included in back-wage calculations.
