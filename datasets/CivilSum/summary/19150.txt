According to Constitution of India, Article 226 and Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 10(1), the government announced a notification to abolish contract labour for some job categories in AP State Electricity Board.
The petitioners, who were employed on a contractual basis, cannot claim the regularization if they were not enrolled on the date of contract abolition.
Only those employees who were working on the date of the contract's abolition are eligible to be absorbed on a regular basis.
