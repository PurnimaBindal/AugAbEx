Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Termination - Appointment - Cancellation of - Natural Justice - 'Audi alteram' - Petitioners appointed in a special recruitment in various division of UP Jal Nigam - Their appointments were rescinded by CE order - However, the orders of termination/cancellation of appointments were bad in law because the petitioners were not given any opportunity of hearing - Thus, such orders were quashed and petitioners to be treated as if in continuous service.
Reference to 1997(5) SCC 20.
