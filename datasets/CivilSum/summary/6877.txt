When considering promotions for Junior Engineer II, a court modified a decree to require promotion consideration for a junior who was previously overlooked.
Despite being regularized at a later date than private defendants, the court held that the respondent should be considered for promotion based on their earlier date of regularization.
If the respondent is promoted to JE I and II, the seniority of private defendants must be considered and fixed according to relevant service rules.
