According to the Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226, and the University Grants Commission Act of 1956, Section 26, seniority for Lecturers in University must count service rendered as Demonstrators.
The state government implemented a UGC scheme of pay revision in 1977 that provided parity between demonstrators and lecturers.
The government resolution dated 2721989, paras 12(a) and 13(a) details this requirement.
