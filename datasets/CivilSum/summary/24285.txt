The appointment of the Director of CBI is determined by the C.B.I.
(Senior Police Posts) Recruitment Rules, 1996, the Delhi Special Police Establishment Act, 1946, Section 4A, and the Central Vigilance Commission Act, 2003, Section 26, as well as the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16.
While seniority is a consideration when appointing the Director of CBI, ultimately merit, including integrity and experience in anti-corruption investigations, will be decisive in making the selection.
The Vineet Narain case of 1998(1) SCC 226 and Section 4A clarified that officers in the senior-most four batches, regardless of empanelment, are eligible for consideration for the post of Director, CBI.
Additionally, the post of Director of CBI is only a tenure position, and the officer may be transferred or returned to their original post after a designated period.
