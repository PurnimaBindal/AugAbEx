Haryana State Central Cooperative Bank Staff Service (Common Cadre) (First Amendment) Rules, 1999 - Validity of amendment - Registrar does not have the authority under Section 37 of the Haryana Cooperative Societies Act to make an amendment in the Common Cadre Rules with retrospective application of amendment - The retrospective effect of amendment cannot be sustained, and the notification of amendment is quashed.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana State Central Cooperative Bank Service (Common Cadre) (First Amendment) Rules, 1999 - Validity of amendment - Quota of promotions for different cadres fixed by 1999 amendment in the Rules results in supersession of seniors by their juniors and violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - The promotions made in quotas resulting in the consideration of juniors before their seniors cannot be sustained.
