Constitution of India, Article 16 - Recruitment by State - Selection Services Board - It is considered arbitrary and illegal to appoint selected candidates through a pick and choose method without preparing a merit list.
The court declined to interfere in the matter due to the absence of objective or rational criteria followed in recruitment.
In addition, the High Court's view that exercising an option was necessary is incorrect since those exercising the option do not obtain an absolute right.
