Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University Act, 1998, Section 26 - Selection - Constitution of Selection Committee - Petitioner, who was physically handicapped, appeared before the selection committee four times during 1999-2004 but was not selected because eligibility for the post does not guarantee appointment.
Suitability of the candidate must be assessed by the Selection Committee.
It is not considered that the exclusion of a member of SC, ST, or Physically Handicapped category in the Selection Committee led to the vitiating of the selection process.
There was no specific provision in the Statute for such a requirement.
[Paras 10 and 11]
