Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Transfer - Administrative exigencies - Malafide - Forest guard - The transfer of the petitioner occurred five times over two years - Normally, actions taken by the State are assumed to have been done in good faith with regards to the interests of good administration - Given the nature of the petitioner's role, it was reasonable for him to be transferred as per the exigencies of service - There is no evidence that the transfer was malicious simply because it occurred numerous times in the same district - Therefore, the petition must be dismissed.
