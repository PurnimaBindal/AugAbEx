Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Writ of Mandamus - West Bengal Board of Secondary Education Act, 1963 - Management of Recognized Non Government institutions (Aided and unaided), Rules 1969 - Recruitment - Appointment - Post of sweeper - Petitioner claims permanent appointment - The appointment as sweeper was illegal - As per the rules, no recruitment process was initiated while appointing petitioner - This is not a mere irregular appointment - It cannot be legalized by issuing mandamus - Therefore, the petition is dismissed.
