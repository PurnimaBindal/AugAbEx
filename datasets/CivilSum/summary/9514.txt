Motor Transport Workers Act, 1961, sections 2(f), 13, 15 and 26 - Tamil Nadu Transport Workers' Rules, 1962, Rule 26 - Overtime allowance - Hours of work - Definition of - The first respondent was employed for an outstation festival trip - There is no evidence that the employer had terminated the employees' duties after eight hours of minimum duty or ten hours of maximum duty or given any roster to engage employees - In the absence of such directions, it is considered that the employees have been engaged for the entire duration and have been at the employer's disposal for all hours - The Labour Court's decision to award overtime pay was upheld.
