The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, dictate the rules for selection, including the advertisement of posts.
If there is a variation in the number of posts advertised or existing posts and future vacancies, it violates Articles 14 and 16.
This includes the selection and appointment against future vacancies by those who become eligible after the advertisement but before the selection.
However, if the advertisement itself made it clear that vacancies may vary on account of vacancies arising until the date of selection, those who participated in the selection process but failed to get selected cannot challenge the selection on the ground of variation in the number of vacancies.
[Paras 13 to 16]
