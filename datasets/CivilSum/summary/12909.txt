The appellant, a Constable in Railway Protection Force, was found to have left his post without proper intimation and remained absent for 10 months.
Such conduct was deemed unbecoming of a member of disciplined force and departmental proceedings were conducted according to the RPF Rules.
Consequently, a major penalty of removal from service was imposed and no interference called for under the Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311.
