Constitution of India, Article 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F and 25-G - Termination - Reinstatement - A workman who worked as a daily wager for a short period and did not complain against the termination of his services, engaged in agricultural work on his own land, and had an unexplained delay of 8 years in raising an industrial dispute, did not object when invited by the petitioner.
The Labour Court failed to consider the plea of the petitioner of delay in raising industrial dispute.
The impugned award of the Labour Court directing reinstatement with 25% of backwages was set aside.
[Paras 28 and 29]
