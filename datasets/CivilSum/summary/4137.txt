Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Appointments - Discrimination - Compassionate Appointment - The petitioner who was appointed on compassionate grounds as an Excise Guard cannot claim a higher post on the grounds of equality before the law, as others were given higher posts.
If some individuals were unlawfully given benefits, this illegality cannot be extended to others, and the court will not perpetuate it.
Compassionate appointment is not an automatic right, and one cannot demand appointments other than the ones offered under the scheme.
[Para 8 and 11]
