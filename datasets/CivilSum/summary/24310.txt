As per the IAS (Appointment by Selection) Regulations, 1997 and Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, Non-State Civil Services Officers must meet the age bar for consideration based on the cut-off date and year.
Eligibility is determined from 1st January of the year in which the Selection Committee meets.
The petitioner was ineligible as he completed the age of 54 years on 2012001 and the eligibility was as on 112002 as per the proposals called for from various departments.
Therefore, he was not included in the list of names recommended for consideration.
The Regulation cannot be misread due to typographical errors in the communication, as it may hinder the entire selection process.
