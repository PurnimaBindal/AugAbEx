According to Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and the Kerala Cashew Factories (Acquisition) Act, 1974, retrenched workers are entitled to compensation.
The petitioner is seeking compensation for retrenchment, notice pay, and gratuity after the factory was taken over by the government.
Even though these disputes were not referred, it is clear that the government refused to do so.
However, if workers are still employed, the question of retrenchment compensation does not arise.
