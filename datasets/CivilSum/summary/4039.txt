Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Cut off date - Challenge to petitioner's eligibility for employment as Assistant Teachers and Nursery Teachers - Petitioners appeared for their B.Ed.
exam and its results were declared on 15th January, 1993 - According to advertisement, petitioners did not meet eligibility criteria - Challenge made to 1st January, 1993 being the cut off date for acquiring academic qualifications - Cut off date corresponds with age cut off and was fixed based on past issues and complaints - SSC argues that cut off date is not arbitrary - Petition dismissed.
