Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965 (As amended by Haryana), Rules 4 and 5 - Military service benefit - Qualifications - The petitioner was appointed as UDC in HSEB in 1975 against reserved vacancy for Ex-servicemen.
By virtue of his military service, he should have been entitled to a deemed date of appointment from 1969 as UDC.
However, the deemed date was only given from 1972 when he had passed graduation.
Denial of the deemed date of appointment on the ground that he acquired qualifications after the deemed date is not valid.
Petitioner is entitled to a deemed date of appointment with all consequential benefits from 1969.
[Paras 1 to 3]
