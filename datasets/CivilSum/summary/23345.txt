State Govt. is not justified in assuming that the Assistants Statistical Investigators who were appointed prior to a certain date and whose services had been regularized should be considered for promotion to the post of Statistical Inspector before others.
The Rules, as amended, must be applied and the promotion process must not be postponed until all such Assistants Statistical Investigators have been promoted.
Only then can the 8:2 ratio be applied to recruitment by transfer for the post of Statistical Inspector of Assistants who entered service in the Department of Statistics after a certain date.
