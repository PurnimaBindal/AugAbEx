The Tehsil-wise mode of selection of candidates for Distt.
Cadre posts is prohibited and illegal, violating Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
[Para 14]
The selecting agency must adhere to the criteria prescribed by the rules or the appointing authority when selecting candidates.
If the criteria do not allow for Tehsil-wise selection, the Selection Board cannot implement Tehsil-wise selection of candidates.
[Paras 14 and 15]
Candidates who participated in the selection process cannot challenge the selection by alleging that the selection criteria were faulty.
After an extended period of appointments, it would not be reasonable to overturn the selection and redraw the select list.
[Paras 16 and 18]
When candidates are selected after competitive tests, they cannot be re-tested and discharged for failing the trade test.
[Paras 19 to 21]
Reserved category candidates are not eligible for further age relaxation under the Act or Rules for Police Rules, Rules 176(2) and 182 recruitment, beyond what is specified by the Rules.
