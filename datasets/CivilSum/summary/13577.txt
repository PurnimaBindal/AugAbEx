According to the Constitution of India, Article 16 and Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 138(2) and 1318, promotion involves a two-year probation period and confirmation.
The petitioners have surpassed the required probation period by a considerable amount and there is no evidence to suggest that their conduct was unsatisfactory during this time.
As per the rules, the petitioners shall be considered confirmed on the post of Head Constables upon completing the two-year probation period.
