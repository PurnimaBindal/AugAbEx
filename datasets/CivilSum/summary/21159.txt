Constitution of India, Article 14 - All Government servants must be granted pension based on the same criteria - Jammu and Kashmir State Civil Service Regulations, Regulation No. 177, proviso 3rd - The appellants worked for the Government Transport Undertaking in Jammu and Kashmir starting in 1950 and then later with Jammu and Kashmir Road Transport Corporation for various terms - The State undertaking made a permanent department of the State Government on 941969 and conferred pensionary benefits from that date - On 961981, rules were amended to allow even temporary Government servants with 20 years of service on the date of superannuation to be entitled to draw pension - The appellants argue that some drivers in similar circumstances were granted a pension but they were refused - The court concluded that it is not justifiable for the State Government to draw a line between those who retired earlier and those who retired after 9th April, 1981 - Even in DS Nakara and Ors case, the court held that a criterion based on the date of enforcement of the revised scheme entitling benefits of revision to those retiring after a specified date while depriving the benefits to those retiring prior to that date was violative of Article 14 - The appeal was allowed.
