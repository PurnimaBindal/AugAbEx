Indian Broadcasting (Programme) Service Rules, Rules 7(2)(a), (b), 7(3) and Note 2 below Schedule IV and Schedule V, Serial No. 5 - Promotion to the post of JTS through selection and promotion - Note 2's use of "field of Selection" is for options to be exercised by feeder category personnel for the management or programme wings of JTS posts - "Selection" under Note 2 assesses feeder category suitability for the "Programme Wing" or the "Production Wing" while the DPC selects promotees from either wing - Note 2 has no relevance to the definition of "Promotion" in Rules 7(2) or at Serial No. 5 to Schedules IV and V - The decision of the Karnataka Bench and Ernakulam Bench of CAT that filling 50% of the quota for promotion to JTS posts by "seniority" is upheld, while the contrary decision of the Full Bench of CAT Jaipur is disapproved.
