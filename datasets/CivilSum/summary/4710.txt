Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - The petitioner serves as a temporary Assistant Teacher in English - The petitioner and the school request approval for appointment - The Director of School Education authorizes one additional post - However, as the petitioner's appointment was on a non-existent post, there is no sanctioned post for their appointment - Thus, the petitioner is not entitled to relief as prayed for.
