Articles 15(1), 15(4), 16(4) and 29(2) of the Constitution of India, along with the UP Public Services (Reservation for Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and Other Backward Classes) Act, 1974, permit residents of Uttarakhand and hill regions to receive 27% reservation quota since 1974, as they are socially and educationally backward, but the state may review the situation periodically, since the reservation cannot last indefinitely.

Articles 15(1), 15(4), and 16(4) of the Constitution of India, as well as the UP Public Services (Reservation for Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and other Backward Classes) Act, 1974, entitle residents of Uttarakhand and hill regions to preferential treatment for medical school admissions because they are considered backward, but not for agricultural college admissions; however, since the state has already acknowledged them as backward for one purpose, there is no rationale for denying the same benefits for another purpose, and the state must regulate future admissions accordingly.
