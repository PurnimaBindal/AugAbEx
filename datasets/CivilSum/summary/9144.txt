Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pension/Migrants from Bhawalpur State - Employees of the former State of Bhawalpur who joined the Central Government of India after 1581947 are entitled to full service benefits and can receive pensions - However, employees of the statutory Boards are not considered Central Government employees and may only be entitled to pension from the Board and not the Government.
