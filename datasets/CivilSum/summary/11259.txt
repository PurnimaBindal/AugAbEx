The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Andhra Pradesh State and Subordinate Services Rules, Rule 22 - Reservation for Ex-servicemen - The court has held that Rule 22 is simply an enabling provision, as no official notification has been issued to that effect.
This means that the court cannot direct anyone to give effect to the enabling provision, nor can the petitioner claim a legal right to issuance of writ of mandamus.
