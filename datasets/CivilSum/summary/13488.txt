Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Appointment - Approval - Petitioner was appointed by the Principal without seeking concurrence from the governing body of the College - Governing body refused to grant approval to the appointment - Petitioner received an ex-parte ad-interim court order preventing termination of services - University rules require recommendation of the governing body for appointment of lecturers - Principal's appointing the petitioner unilaterally is not in accordance with University rules - Petition was dismissed.
