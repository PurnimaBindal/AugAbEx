Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1)(d) - Disciplinary proceedings against a workman - The workman cannot claim that enquiry proceedings are vitiated if he voluntarily chose not to participate.
[Para 6]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1)(d) - Termination of services of workman after proper enquiry - If a proper enquiry has been conducted by the employer and supported by evidence, the Tribunal cannot interfere as an appellate authority.
[Para 7]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1)(d) - Use of abusive language by worker against a superior officer - This constitutes a serious breach of discipline and justifies termination of services without a lesser punishment.
