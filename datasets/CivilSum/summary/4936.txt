According to the Constitution of India, Article 16 and various acts and statutes, including the University Grants Commission Act and the Jai Narayan Vyas University Jodhpur Act, the appointment and promotion of the Head of Department is governed by certain rules.
Specifically, if a professor/reader/lecturer goes on leave for more than a month, the next seniormost professor/reader/lecturer shall be appointed as HOD for such period and such term will not be counted towards three years either for a regular or stop-gap appointee.
Thus, in the case of a petitioner appointed against a leave vacancy, they must make room for the regular incumbent on return from leave.
