The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 govern the selection grade and senior selection grade for lecturers working in government colleges.
In the calculation of qualifying service, the previous services provided by the lecturers in unaided private colleges before being taken over must be counted for granting senior scale/selection grade.
[Paras 10, 16 and 22]
According to the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, the period during which an employee was continuously working on his post cannot be regarded as a break in service solely because he was not paid his legally payable salary.
The duration must not be ignored when it comes to his/her service/qualifying service.
[Paras 17, 18 and 20]
Under the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, if the College's similarly placed employees are granted senior scale/Selection Grade by counting their previous service in private institutions taken over by the Government, then other employees cannot be deprived of the same benefit.
If they also fulfill the necessary criteria, they are eligible for the advantage.
[Paras 23 and 24]
