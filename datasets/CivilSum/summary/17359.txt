Constitution of India, Article 311 - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 170 - Departmental proceedings - Charge-sheet - Dereliction of duty - Charge sheet issued after four years from the incident cannot be sustained - Rule 170 requirements must apply in full - Charge sheet seeking prosecution for dereliction of duty after petitioner's retirement is unsustainable due to limitation prescribed by Rule 170.
