The appointment of LIC agents is governed by various regulations and rules, including the Constitution of India, Articles 16, 19(1)(g) and 21 - Life Insurance Corporation of India Act, 1950 Section 49 - Life Insurance corporation of India (Agents) Regulations, 1972, Regulations 5, 17 and 25 - Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1955, Rule 15.
There is a circular in place that only allows LIC agency to be given to the applicant whose spouse is not serving in State/Central Govt. or public undertaking.
However, it is argued that this circular has no legal force as it has not been published in the Gazette nor placed before both Houses of Parliament.
Furthermore, it is contended that the circular is in violation of Article 19(1)(g) and Article 21 as there is no provision in the rules of the Central Govt. that prohibits the spouses of public sector or government employees from being appointed as agents.
While there is a bar on employees to canvass or participate in the business of their spouses, the circular cannot be said to be valid without any amendment to the regulations and is contrary to the rules.
