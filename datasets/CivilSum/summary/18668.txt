Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 and 226 - The persons with Disabilities (Equal opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full participation) Act 1995 - While filling the post of clerks and peon, physically handicapped people other than the blind were considered - The visual handicap is considered to be 100% and the post of peon was originally meant for blind persons - With respect to academic qualifications, it is not fair for respondent No. 4 to choose a person with less disability than the petitioner - However, the counsel on behalf of respondent No. 4 argued that they selected the candidate in accordance with the advertisement and based on their overall performance and educational qualifications - The petitioner was not discriminated against and a suitable blind candidate was selected.
