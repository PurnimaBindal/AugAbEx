Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, states the consequences of promotion and reversion.
Train Clerks who were provisionally promoted to become Assistant Head Train Clerks and were eligible for promotion as Goods Guards, petitioned to revert back to their original post of Train Clerks while also applying for promotion to the position of Goods Guards.
The excess pay they had received was recovered.
Despite being fully qualified for the position of Goods Guards and even eligible for selection, their appointments were cancelled due to the Tribunal's error.
