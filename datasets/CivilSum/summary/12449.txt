The petitioners were appointed as clerks on ad hoc basis without being sponsored by employment exchange and not selected by SSS Board, but they were regularised based on Punjab Government instructions dated 22.5.1969, and therefore cannot claim seniority on the basis of their ad hoc service.
They are not entitled to count ad hoc service as per Chambel Singh's case.Full Bench.
