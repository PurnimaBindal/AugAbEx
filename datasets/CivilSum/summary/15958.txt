According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, promotion by selection should follow the principles of natural justice.
In the case of a doctor being promoted as a professor by selection, a reference against the promotion was made to the Chancellor, who considered representations that had civil consequences for the petitioner's rights.
Despite this, the promoted person was not given the opportunity to respond, leading to an unfair, unjust, and inequitable order from the Chancellor that violated the principles of natural justice.
