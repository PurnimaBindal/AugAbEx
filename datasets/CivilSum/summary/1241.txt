According to Section 20 of the Minimum Wages Act (2 of 1948) and Rule 25(2)(v)(a) of the Contract of Labour (Regulations and Abolition) Service Rules of 1971, the petitioner, who is a government company and public sector enterprise, is liable to pay minimum wages to contract labor.
Although a notification dated 1731993 prohibits contract labor, the respondents worked as laborers from 1993 to 1996 and received the benefit of this work from the petitioner.
Thus, the respondents are entitled to receive minimum wages as provided by Rule 25(2)(v)(a) of the Rules, 1971, and the petitioner-company is responsible for paying the wages as the contractors did not pay.
