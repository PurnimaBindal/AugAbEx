Admission in B.Pharmacy at the College of Pharmacy, Pushp Vihar, New Delhi under the University of Delhi is reserved for certain states.
The petitioner's name was recommended by the Commissioner and Secretary, Technical Education and instructions indicating reservation were provided on record.
Therefore, admission could not have been denied to the petitioner on the ground of unavailability of seats.
However, since the course has already commenced, the college authorities have been directed to consider the petitioner's case for the next session if eligible and according to law, while demonstrating the reserved seats to all eligible.
