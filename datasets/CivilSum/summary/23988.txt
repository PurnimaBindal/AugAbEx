The Bar Council of India Rules, 1961, Rule 49, and Sections 24, 28 of the Advocates Act, 1961 outlines conditions for eligibility for Advocate enrolment.
According to this law, a full-time employee, even if designated as a Law Officer, is not eligible to be enrolled as an Advocate if their duties are mainly ministerial rather than representing their employer in court cases.
The exception to this rule does not refer to any designation.
Employees whose work mainly or exclusively involves acting or pleading in court may be entitled to enrolment.
Law Officers whose service conditions only involve miscellaneous duties and disciplinary proceedings for working in the legal Cell of the establishment are not entitled to the benefit of Rule 49 or Rule 3 of 1979 Rules.
Ineligibility at the beginning is valid, and this action does not discriminate against the Government pleaders appointed to plead Govt. cases.
[Paras 16-23]

Enrolment as an Advocate and cancellation of seniority occur when a registration is cancelled as being opposed to the law.
Such enrolment is considered non est.
Therefore, the maintenance of seniority on the Council's rolls is not applicable.
