According to Punjab Civil Service Rules Volume I Part I - Rule 4.5 A(i)(a) - Fixation of Pay/ Probationer - Appointment in other service or cadre, the pay of a probationer on a new post cannot be less than what he was drawing on his earlier post where he holds a lien or would have held a lien.
[Para 6]
Punjab Civil Service Rules Volume I Part I - Rule 4.4 (a) - Pay/Fixation of pay - Appointment on a new post in other service or cadre, states that pay fixation after confirmation on the new post or other service or cadre depends on a finding that the new post carries duties and responsibilities of greater importance than the previous post and has to be recorded by the appropriate authority.
[Para 7]
If a petitioner alleges that his pay in the other service was not fixed properly, it cannot be looked into after a delay of five years according to Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 8]
