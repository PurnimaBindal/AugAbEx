Constitution of India - Articles 311 and 14 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 137 and 13.20 - List B-1 for Lower School Course required for promotion to the rank of Head Constable - The test requires 25% marks for interview and this requirement has been challenged - Courts have held that a varying percentage of marks is justified - However, a distinction has been made between interviews for educational institutions and selection to higher posts - This is to eliminate arbitrariness and to assess the suitability of a person for the higher post - The petition has been dismissed.
