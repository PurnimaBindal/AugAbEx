Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - The petitioner, who is a permanent resident of tehsil Naraingarh, had transferred his name from Employment Exchange, Naraingarh to Employment Exchange Chhachhrauli.
After being sponsored for the position of cattle attendant by the Employment Exchange Chhachhrauli, he was selected.
There was no objection taken from any side at that time, instead, the petitioner was allowed to complete his probation period of two years.
It would be unjust if now the petitioner's appointment is quashed on the grounds that the recommendation from Employment Exchange, Chhachhrauli was unfair.
Therefore, the impugned order of termination has been set aside.
