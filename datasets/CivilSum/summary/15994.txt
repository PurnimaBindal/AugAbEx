According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, 60 marks are allocated for the written test and 40 marks are designated for the interview.
Within the 40 marks for the interview, 20 are intended for assessing the intelligence of the candidate and the other 20 are intended for evaluating their job knowledge.
The employer's criteria for allocating these marks are considered to be in compliance with Article 16 of the Constitution of India.
