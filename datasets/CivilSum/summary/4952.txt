The principles of equal pay for equal work must be applied if a claim is made that pay scales are unreasonably equal, and the petitioner should receive a higher pay scale as applicable to another similar post.
These principles require the claimant to establish equality in both quantitative and qualitative respects, which may require expertise.
If classification made by experts is found to be reasonable and not arbitrary in violation of the equality clause, then the court cannot interfere with such findings.
