The departmental enquiry with regards to promotion was pending against the petitioner since 1989, but they were acquitted of charges in a criminal case in 1996.
However, the petitioner was not considered for promotion in 1997 like their juniors who received a promotion.
Another enquiry started in August 1998, but the respondents have been directed to conclude the pending enquiry and then consider the petitioner's case for promotion from December 1997 onwards, when their juniors were promoted.
Despite the later enquiry that began in August 1998, it should not affect the petitioner's promotion.
