According to the Constitution of India, as well as University Regulations, Regulation 30, re-examination of answer scripts has a time limit that is usually one month, but the interpretation of the word "ordinarily" means that the time limit can be extended.
In this case, due to the special circumstances surrounding the student's eligibility to appear in Part-II (Hons.)
examination, a re-examination was permitted even though more than one month had passed.
