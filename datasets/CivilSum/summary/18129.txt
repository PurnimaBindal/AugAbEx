Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 21 - Disability during service - Compassionate appointment of child of invalidated employee - The government's policy for compassionate appointment to a disabled person's child cannot be denied by offering a lower status and pay scale.
If the employee seeks voluntary retirement on that ground with a specific request to register their child's claim for appointment under the policy, it cannot be said that they waived their right by not accepting an alternate appointment and claiming retirement without any condition.
