Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Seniority-cum-merit - Selection - A petitioner was denied promotion because the DPC declared him “not yet fit” due to his “average” gradation - Mode of promotion is seniority-cum-fitness - Held, it is irrational to deny promotion solely based on an average gradation - Selection committee should explain their decision-making process for each candidate - Petitioner's junior's merit does not automatically disqualify him for promotion - Therefore, denial of promotion by DPC is illegal and unjust.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Seniority-cum-merit - Selection - In seniority-cum-fitness, senior officers have the right to promotion unless declared unfit due to an adverse record or if they do not meet certain conditions - In promotions by selection, merit is the only consideration and seniority only has relevance when the merit of two candidates is equal.
