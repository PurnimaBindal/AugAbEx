Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Claim for various types of compensation - Notice required before proceeding - Labour Court should not proceed ex parte against management based solely on lack of notice acknowledgment within 30 days - Since management was not served, the order of the Labour Court cannot stand - The court has directed the Labour Court to hear both parties and dispose of the application filed by the worker according to the law - Constitution of India, Articles 226/227.
