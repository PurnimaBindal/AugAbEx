According to Sections 4(6) and 14 of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, any deduction made from gratuity for residential accommodation charges after retirement under Voluntary Retirement Scheme is illegal and invalid.
[Para 15]

MAMC, being a Government company, is considered a 'State' under Article 12 of the Constitution of India.
As per the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 4(6), if any deduction is made from the undisputed amount of gratuity, it will amount to deprivation of property under Article 300-A of the Constitution.
Such deductions are arbitrary and a violation of the fundamental right granted under Article 14 of the Constitution.
