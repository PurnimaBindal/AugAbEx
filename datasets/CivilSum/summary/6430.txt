Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226/227 - Petitioners complain about their claim for parity of pay scale being declined - Evidence shows pay scale of Multipurpose Supervisor in Health Department and Sanitary Inspectors in Local Bodies were the same till 1983 - Petitioners claim declined based on wrong assumption of facts, impugned order set aside and respondents directed to decide the matter afresh in accordance with law.
