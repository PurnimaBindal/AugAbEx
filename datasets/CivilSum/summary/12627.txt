Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - State Bank of India (Supervising Service), Rules, Rule 50A(1) - Suspension - Effective date of suspension - Although the suspension order may be served later, it takes effect from the date it was passed.
[Para 8] B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Evidence Act, 1872, Section 115 - State Bank of India Officers (Determination of terms and conditions of Service) Orders, 1979 (DTCS Orders) - Retirement - Retirement age/Orders of retirement/Automatic retirement/Acquiescence/Jurisdiction/Extension: (i) If the rules provide a specific retirement date, the employer does not need to ask the employee to retire, and automatic retirement takes place if there is no extension in advance.
(ii) Once an employee retires, he or she is no longer a member of the service, and any acquiescence on the part of that person cannot confer jurisdiction on the Bank authorities.
Any order made after the retirement date is null and void.
If disciplinary proceedings are pending on the retirement date, the employee will be deemed to be in service only for that purpose until the proceedings are completed.
