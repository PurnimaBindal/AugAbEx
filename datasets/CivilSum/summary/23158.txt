Constitution of India, Article 311, 309 Proviso - Recruitment Rules for Post of Assistant Engineer in Public Works Department, Rule 11 - Promotion - To be promoted, a Degree-holder must have three years' service, while a Diploma-holder Junior Engineer must obtain a degree while in service - The three-year requirement is calculated from the date of degree attainment, rather than any earlier point - Service in the grade as a Diploma-holder before acquiring the degree is not considered service in the grade with a degree.
