Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Central Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 14 - Post Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, Regulations, 1967, Regulation 36 - Employees of PGIMER, Chandigarh, are not government servants, which means Article 311 does not apply to them.
Hence, principles of Natural justice in compliance of Article 311 do not apply to such cases.
[Paras 11, 12 and 14].
B Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 14 - Post Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, Regulations, 1967, Regulation 36 - Refusal of extension of study leave and non-joining of duty on expiry of study leave can lead to departmental action.
Interim relief of stay proceedings and direction to allow join duty held not justified as it was challenged on grounds of violation of principle of natural justice.
