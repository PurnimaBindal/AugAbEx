In accordance with Articles 14 and 311 of the Constitution of India, suspension, detention, and departmental proceedings may not be necessary in every case of employee misconduct.
It is only in rare cases of severe misconduct that may result in termination where suspension would be considered.
Furthermore, an inquiry may be conducted without immediately placing the employee under suspension.
A suspension order was overturned in this instance, with the respondents ordered to pay arrears within two months for the suspension period.
