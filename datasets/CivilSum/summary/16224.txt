Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment/Joining report - The petitioner who was selected as a school lecturer was supposed to join by 7991 which was a holiday and could not do so until 9991.
Despite holding a B.Ed.
certificate, he was not allowed to join on the ground of not producing it.
However, the court directed the respondents to allow the petitioner all the benefits from 9991 and the petition was allowed with costs Rs. 2000/-.
[Paras 2, 3 and 4]
