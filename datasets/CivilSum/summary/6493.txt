According to Sections 151 and 152 of the Civil Procedure Code, orders can be corrected in cases where ambiguity in the language of the order results in the loss of relief granted.
Corrections must be made to ensure clarity and consistency in the order.
Additionally, the relinquishment of a claim by one claimant should not affect the rights of others in the same petition or appeal.
