The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311, along with Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.28(3), require natural justice to be followed when imposing punishment.
In a case where a Punishing Authority reduced a petitioner's rank and an Appellate Authority enhanced the punishment to dismissal from service, the order of dismissal was quashed for violating Rule 16.28(3).
This was because no show cause notice was given as to why the punishment should not be enhanced, and no reasonable opportunity to be heard was provided before enhancing the punishment.
