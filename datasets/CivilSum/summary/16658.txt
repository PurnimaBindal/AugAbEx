Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Advertisement of eligibility conditions - Transparency - Relaxation of eligibility conditions after the deadline for applications is not allowed - Such relaxation would violate Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution by denying the right to compete and consideration to those who did not apply and would result in undue benefit to those who applied despite being ineligible under the original conditions - Selection made by subsequently relaxing eligibility conditions would not be a fair process in public appointments.
