AP State Road Transport Corporation Employees (Leave) Regulations, Regulation 39(3) - Unauthorised absence - Termination - Natural justice - Back wages - Petitioner, a driver, was removed from service in 1980 for unauthorised absence and approached the Labour Court in November 1987 after seven and a half years - Labour Court allowed 25% of back wages - When an order of termination is set aside, full back wages are usually granted, unless exceptional circumstances warrant denial of back wages - Petitioner's delay in approaching the Labour Court justifies the denial of back wages for the period from date of termination till he approached Labour Court - Half of backwages from 27111987 till date of reinstatement has been granted - Petitioner to have continuity of service but without any attendant benefits.
