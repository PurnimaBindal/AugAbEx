Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Suspension - Suspension period - Wages for suspension period - If charges are unproven, then suspension shall be considered unjustified, and the delinquent is entitled to full wages for the suspension period, and the suspension period should count towards qualifying service for all purposes - Additionally, the wages for the suspension period cannot be forfeited without providing a show cause notice to the employee - Any directions given by the authority to the employee to apply for leave due to the unjustified suspension period cannot be considered valid.
