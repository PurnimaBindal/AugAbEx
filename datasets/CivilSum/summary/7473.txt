Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equality of pay scales - Even if different sources of appointment exist for one cadre, different pay scales cannot be prescribed - All Gram Sachivs and Village Extension Workers in Haryana are entitled to equal pay scales and emoluments, regardless of their source of appointment - Discrimination on the basis of induction source cannot occur once employees are brought into one cadre.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Legal Services Authority Act, 1987, Sections 19 to 22 - Jurisdiction of Lok Adalat - Lok Adalats complement regular courts by settling cases through compromise or agreements - They cannot act as a regular court for adjudication - However, frivolous objections by the State are not expected when the settled law is consistent with the view taken by the Lok Adalts.
