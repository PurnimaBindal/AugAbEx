Confidential Reports - Guidelines for Adverse Reports - Instructions issued by the Haryana Govt. lay down that adverse reports must be supported by reasons and recorded by the officer who saw the work being done.
These should also be conveyed within the specified period to give an opportunity to the official to respond.
In the present case, the instructions were violated repeatedly, leading to the adverse reports being expunged.
[Para 5]
Representation against 'Adverse Reports' Speaking Order - Decision on Representations - The next higher authority should decide on representations against adverse reports, and must issue a speaking order.
The authority that recorded the adverse report must place the representation before the next higher authority.
