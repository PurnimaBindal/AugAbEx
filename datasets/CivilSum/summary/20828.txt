Kerala Service Rules 56 and 57 declare that when employees are suspended due to their involvement in a criminal case, salaries and allowances are withheld until they are acquitted.
In this specific case, the petitioner was under suspension until she was acquitted.
After her acquittal, a departmental inquiry was conducted under Rule 56.
During the suspension period, the petitioner was not given any salary except the suspension allowance, which is in line with the rules.
