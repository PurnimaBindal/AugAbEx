Civil Procedure Code, Order 39 Rules 1 & 2 - Employees State Provident Fund Scheme - Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Rules, 1971 - Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Act, 1970 - Interim stay on deduction of EPF contribution from the bill of Labour Contractor - The Scheme is a welfare legislation and should be interpreted to benefit employees - Nothing exempts short-term casual labour from the EPF Scheme - Even if the contractor is successful, a refund can be requested - Principal employer is obligated to make statutory deductions and cannot be prevented from doing so by an injunction.
