According to Punjab Civil Services Rules Volume-I, Rule 38 and Punjab Municipal Act, 1911, Section 236, the Director Local Bodies cannot annul the resolution of the Municipal Committee based on the argument that Punjab Civil Services Rules do not apply to Municipal Employees and that relaxation of age limit under Rule 38 was illegal.
The termination of services of the petitioners on the ground of age limit was not justified, as there was no provision for age limit in the bye-laws of the Committee.
The resolution was deemed unnecessary and an exercise in futility.
Therefore, the termination was quashed.
