Payment of Wages Act, 1936 and Standing Orders govern the employees of Hindustan Machine Tools Ltd - Management can deduct employee wages for absence from duty in line with Wages Act, but not as a penalty.
The provision states wages will only be paid for work done.
[Para 11]

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33 - Although the authority is bound to make a prompt decision, proceedings will not be voided merely due to them not being decided within three months.
The eight adjournments requested by the petitioner caused the delay, not the management and their actions were deemed appropriate.
[Paras 8 and 9]

Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 311 - Absence from duty is considered misconduct and is a matter for the appropriate authority to determine the appropriate penalty.
As the employer had already decided to dismiss the petitioner, and their actions were approved by the Tribunal, the High Court has no jurisdiction to intervene.
[Para 15]

The Standing Order specifically provides that employers must consider the gravity of misconduct, previous work record, and other circumstances when imposing a penalty.
The validity of this provision was not challenged, and the authority's decision was found to comply with it.
As a result, the petition was dismissed.
[Para 16]
