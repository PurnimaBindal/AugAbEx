Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 11 - Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules, 1957, Rules 9 to 30 - Practice and Procedure/Evidence/Pleadings/Issues - The Labour Court can make findings on matters not specifically framed as issues - The court is not bound by strict civil procedure and evidence acts, but it is the responsibility of the tribunal to resolve disputes between parties - In the absence of specific pleadings and issues not framed, the court can still make findings on the basis of evidence if parties were aware of the dispute and had ample opportunity to provide evidence - The 1991 Sher Singh v. Presiding Officer Labour Court decision was overruled.
