Constitution of India, Article 226, 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 33C(2) - Regular Pay Scale - If a worker is recommended to work on a higher post on a short-term basis, they are entitled to the remuneration of the higher position - Without proof of consent from a worker to work as an accountant without remuneration, the corporation's refusal to pay the higher wage is unreasonable - The application under Section 33C(2) of the Act was deemed maintainable and the petition was dismissed accordingly.
