Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 300-A - Promotion - Pension - Vacancy - If an individual was unlawfully superseded and deemed eligible for notional promotion with retroactive effect, they cannot be refused the benefit on the basis of a lack of vacancies at the relevant time, particularly after their retirement from service when they are only entitled to certain monetary benefits regarding their pension - There is no vacancy to consider, but the matter can be resolved by awarding notional promotion and recalculating their pension payments accordingly - Petition permitted.
