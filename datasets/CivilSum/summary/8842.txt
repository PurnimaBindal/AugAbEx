As per Section 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a request to introduce fresh evidence to support charges due to an improper enquiry must be made by the management at the earliest stage.
The management does not have an absolute right to request for fresh evidence at any point and it is not the duty of the Labour Court to advise the management to exercise its right.
Upon receiving such an application, the Labour Court will determine whether it was made without unreasonable delay and if any delay has been adequately explained.
