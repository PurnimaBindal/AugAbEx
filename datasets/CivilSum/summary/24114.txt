Under the Punjab Municipal Act (1911), Sections 38 and 38(6), the petitioner was appointed as a peon and eventually became an octroi Moharrir and then an octroi inspector.
However, after the amendment of Section 38, the petitioner was no longer found eligible for promotion due to lack of experience as a clerk.
Although the post of octroi inspector was abolished on July 22, 1980, the post of octroi moharrir was equated with the post of clerk.
The High Court dismissed the petition with a short and vague "Dismissed" order, so the case has been remanded back to be decided with a speaking order.
