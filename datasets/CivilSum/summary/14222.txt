Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 31 - Suspension - The petitioner was suspended in January 1996 but no allegations were made against him by the respondent authorities for almost four years.
No inquiry was conducted against him and he retired in October 1996.
Petitioner was not given any reasons as to why he was not charged with any offense.
The respondents are ordered to conduct a proper inquiry against the petitioner and complete it by August 31, 1999, otherwise he will be assumed to have been exonerated from all charges.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]
