Constitution of India, Article 311 - Natural justice - Termination from service - Haryana State Industrial Development Corporation Employees Service Bye-Laws, Bye-Law 32 - The petitioner, who was a confirmed General Manager (Finance) of the Corporation, was terminated from service on allegations of misconduct of absence from duty without proper enquiry invoking bye-law 32.
Settled principle of law is that an employee of State after confirmation cannot be sent out of service merely by giving notice or notice pay, such rule is arbitrary and violative of Article 14 of the Constitution.
Order of termination has been quashed and petitioner is entitled to all consequential benefits as if he has retired on the date of his superannuation and fixation of pensionary benefits accordingly.
[Paras 2 to 6]
