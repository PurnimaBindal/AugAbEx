Constitution of India, Articles 14,16 - Selection - Appointment / Retrenchment - The petitioner was selected as Nurse-Dal and put on the waiting list - Later appointed on a temporary basis as ANM as Nurse Dai with the condition of termination with one month's notice pay - She was then transferred on the same terms and ultimately retrenched due to the abolition of the post - As there was no regular vacancy, she cannot claim any right.
