Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural justice - Pay scale - Redesignation - The petitioner's basic pay scale of Rs. 1,200/- and designation of Plumber-cum-Fitter were withdrawn and replaced with Plumber in the pay scale of Rs. 800-1,150 without allowing an opportunity to the petitioner - It is not permissible to make any decision that adversely affects a person's rights without following the principles of natural justice - The order is invalidated and permission is granted to commence proceedings in accordance with the law.
