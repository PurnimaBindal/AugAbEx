Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and Laches - Appointment of petitioner as a Conductor on daily wages for one month - His services were terminated after this time - Services of a daily wager can be legally terminated after their specific term has expired - Furthermore, the petitioner has filed this petition six years after the fact - For both reasons of delay and laches as well as the merits of the case, the petition is dismissed.
