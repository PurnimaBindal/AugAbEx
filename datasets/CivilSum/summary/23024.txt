As per Section 2(S) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, placing a Gold Smith on a bank's approved list of appraisers for weighing and testing gold on a commission basis does not establish a master-servant relationship.
This is because the Gold Smith has a fair amount of freedom and responsibility over the manner of their work.
Hence, such a person cannot be considered as a workman and the relationship does not permit re-union in the event of disruption through a Labour Court in the same manner as a master-servant relationship.
[Paras 5 to 7]
