Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Section 126(2)(c) - Dismissal - Enquiry - Dispensing of - The petitioners were not shown to have indulged in activities that could harm the security of the state.
No material or record that would allow the competent authority to make a decision based on relevant considerations was presented to the court.
The state government did not put up any defense.
Since there is no proof of the petitioners' involvement in activities harmful to the security of the state, the dismissal order cannot be upheld.
The order of dismissal is quashed.
[Paras 11, 12 and 13]
