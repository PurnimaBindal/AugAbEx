Constitution of India, Article 226 - Indian Medical Council Act, 1956, Section 33 - Indian Medical Council Regulations, Schedule 1, Para 3 - Appointment - Qualification - Post of Senior Resident Doctor - The advertisement stipulated that the postgraduate qualification must be from a recognised university.
The petitioner's degree was not from a university recognised by MCI.
Merely being on the select list does not grant automatic right to appointment.
As the petitioner did not meet the condition stated in the advertisement, the respondent has the right to refuse her appointment.
