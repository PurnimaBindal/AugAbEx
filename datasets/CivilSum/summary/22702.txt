Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 266 - Education - Natural justice - Admission - A routine interim order was passed without hearing the State Government and had the effect of displacing a student from a private college when they requested admission to a medical college in another state - Interim order was set aside - High Court's decision was made without following proper legal principles for judicial review - Decision was set aside - The student must face the consequences of the invalidated orders since they requested them - Appeal was allowed and costs were awarded.
