Punjab State Supply and Marketing Co-operative Services (Common Cadre) Rules, 1967, Rule 217(e) - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 115 Revisional jurisdiction - Whether the authority under the Marketing Cooperative Services (Common Cadre) Rule, Rule 217(e) can interfere with the findings of fact upheld by the Appellate Authority and investigate the legality and propriety of the order - Appellate authority order under service rules is not final - Registry Cooperative Societies' revisional jurisdiction is not limited as is in Section 115 of the CPC - In service matters, the authority must assess the legality and propriety of the decision and may even reverse its findings of fact - Court limitations on revisional jurisdiction under the CPC do not limit the scope of revision under service jurisprudence.
