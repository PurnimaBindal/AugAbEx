Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - If a post is reserved for physically handicapped persons and they meet qualifications and suffer from the specified handicap, they have a right to submit an application and to be considered according to law.
It is not constitutional for an authority to consider an individual's application without giving notice of the vacancy and providing opportunities for eligible persons to compete.
[Para 6]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - In the case of the petitioner, a physically handicapped person, the competent authority informed him that posts would be filled soon through the SSS Board.
The petitioner was advised to apply for the post at the appropriate time.
The Board followed strict procedures by advertising the posts and considering all applicants.
The petitioner failed to apply and therefore could not have had his name recommended by the Board.
[Para 7]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - After an authority advertises posts, they are required to follow a process of selection that includes laying down criteria, conducting a written test and/or interview, and considering all applicants.
Candidates cannot avoid competition.
In this case, the petitioner only sought a "backdoor entry" into service, which the State Transport Commissioner did not permit.
The petitioner suffered no injustice that would require Court intervention.
[Para 9]
