Constitution of India, Articles 16, 21 and 226 - Salary - Work charged employees - The petitioner was appointed as a work charged Section Assistant and his services were utilized as a regular Section Assistant against the permanent post of Sub-Engineer Grade II, resulting in the petitioner being continued on the post with a higher pay scale.
However, his salary was stopped due to lack of approval from the Finance Department.
It was held that the government cannot deny the petitioner a salary raise on a technical plea and cannot make him shoulder higher responsibility without due compensation.
The order of stoppage of salary was deemed to be illegal.
[Paras 10 to 12]
