According to Section 7-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, in order for a dispute to be referred to a National Tribunal, the only grounds available to the Central Government are if the dispute involves a question of national importance or if industrial establishments of more than one state are likely to be interested.
Even if Medical Representatives work in more than one state, each case must be based on individual facts.
Also, the contention that adjudication of disputes by different tribunals could lead to a difference of opinion is not sufficient.
The dismissal of a group of workmen cannot be considered an important question of law or a question of national importance, and therefore the Notification was set aside.
