The regularisation policy of the Corporation stipulates that those appointed through Employment Exchange or open advertisement can be regularised after two years of service.
Those appointed through other means need to complete three years of service.
However, the petitioners were appointed on a temporary basis and do not meet the conditions for regularisation.
Therefore, their service cannot be regularised since the appointments were made without notifying the vacancies to the employment exchange or the general public and were made through a departmental committee.
