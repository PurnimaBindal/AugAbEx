Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300-A - Disability Pension - The petitioner's claim for disability pension was rejected as he was not considered to be on duty when he suffered an injury during casual leave while at home and also suffered from schizophrenia.
However, in regards to his claim for pension due to schizophrenia, it was noted that he was enrolled in service fully fit and after completing training he began displaying symptoms of schizophrenia.
It is presumed that he contracted the disease as a result of military service, and therefore he is entitled to receive disability pension on account of his schizophrenia.
