Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Scope of adjudication - The employer's denial of relationship and employment does not affect the jurisdiction of the Labour Court under Section 33-C(2) - The Labour Court determines eligibility based on workman's evidence - The Labour Court is authorized to adjudicate salary claims for existing employee rights - The jurisdiction of the Labour Court under Section 33-C(2) is not affected by an artificial plea of abandonment.
[Paras 19 to 21]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Civil Procedure Code, Order 9, Rules 7 & 13 - Ex parte proceedings - Scope of adjudication - If the management intentionally neglects to appear before the Labour Court, the order to proceed ex-parte cannot be considered illegal or without jurisdiction.
