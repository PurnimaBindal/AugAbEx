Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 100 - Railway Protection Force Rules, 1959, Rule 44 - Punishment - Non-disclosure of assets amassed during service warrants dismissal from service - Natural justice requires fairness in the action - The Court's role is to evaluate the manner in which action was taken, not the merits of the case - If the delinquent was given sufficient opportunity to explain but couldn't justify themselves and no injustice occurred, the court need not interfere with the punishment - A dishonest Govt. employee who violated conduct rules and committed serious misconduct does not deserve leniency.
