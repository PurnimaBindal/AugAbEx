The Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, Section 32(v)(c) pertains to the Water Supply and Drainage Board's profits.
The Board is a State-designed institution created to manage projects that the Government Department has failed to run properly, thus reducing pressure on the limited resources and revenue of the State.
However, the Tamil Nadu Water Supply and Drainage Board cannot fall under the exempted category under Section 32(v)(c) of the Payment of Bonus Act.
