The question being considered is how the petitioner should be compensated and how to measure it in terms of promotion.
The petitioner claimed he suffered physical and mental pain, loss of reputation, social acceptance, and financial damages.
It's impossible to determine what promotions the petitioner could have earned due to lack of decisive criteria.
The petitioner's grievance of being humiliated and looked down upon by peers and superiors has been rectified through the appellate judgement declaring his lower medical categorization as unjustified.
The promotion cannot be granted by adopting promotions by his batch-mates as a measure because it violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
