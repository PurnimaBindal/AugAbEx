The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Petitioners were employed as General Teachers on the basis of their National Trade Certificate in Cutting and Tailoring.
Nevertheless, the Supreme Court deemed that qualification to be ideal for the position of Craft Teacher, but not for that of general teacher, according to a verdict dated 16111992.
The High Court upheld the appointment of petitioners on 1131992 and its decision was never challenged.
Consequently, the termination of the petitioners' services was based on a circular dated 741994, which established the cut-off date as 6111985.
The termination order was then quashed and the appeals were granted.
