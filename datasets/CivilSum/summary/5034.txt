Constitution of India, Article 16 - Seniority - Inclusion of ad hoc service - Respondents appointed ad hoc for a fixed term with the condition that it would not contribute towards seniority - Ad hoc appointments made in violation of rules and respondents were not qualified to hold those positions at that time - Consequently, respondents were subject to the terms specified in appointment order and could not claim benefits in conflict with those terms.
[Paras 20 to 22]
