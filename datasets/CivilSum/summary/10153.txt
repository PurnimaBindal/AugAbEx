According to Sections 15, 21 and 22 of the Indian Medical Council Act, 1956 and Sections 2(7), (13) and (14) of the Delhi Medical Council Act, 1997, the jurisdiction of the Medical Council only covers registered practitioners.
The deletion of a name from the Register can only occur if the name is on the Register.
A doctor is subject to the jurisdiction of the State Medical Council only if they are registered with it.
Despite this, there is still authority to deal with misconduct issues or disciplinary issues if a doctor practices in a state without being registered with the State Council.
If a doctor is registered with the Medical Council of India, action can be taken against them and any complaints should be referred to the MCI.
