Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Retrenchment - Allegations made by claimant considered in cases of illegal retrenchment - Tribunal does not examine plea of employer that workman left on its own after completion of work - Discontinuation of employment at employer's instance not examined - Tribunal's order for reinstatement set aside, case remanded for fresh adjudication.
