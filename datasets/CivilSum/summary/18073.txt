According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 226, Policy Instructions dictate that Work charged and Regular employees should be treated equally when it comes to discrimination, Pensionary benefits, Family pension, and employment of dependents.
The State is supposed to act as a Model Employer, and no interpretation of Policy Instructions should exclude work-charged employees from access to Pensionary benefits and employment of dependents/sons.
A direction has been issued to release admissible Pensionary benefits and ensure employment of the son of the deceased.
