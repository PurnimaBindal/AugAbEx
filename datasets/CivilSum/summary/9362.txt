Civil Procedure Code, Order 17 Rule 3 - Evidence - Closing of evidence by order - If a party has deposited diet money and process fee for summoning witnesses, it is not the party's fault if witnesses cannot be served or examined.
Therefore, the court has no reason to close evidence in such cases.
The trial court should arrange for summoning the remaining witnesses through the petitioner's deposit of process fee.
[Paras 2 and 3]
