Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Section 4 - Selection - Medical examination - Appointment - Physical fitness - Writ jurisdiction - Difference between Medical Board and CMO opinions - If the Medical Board opined the candidate as fit, the subsequent contradicting opinion of the CMO without diagnostic reasons cannot be accepted - The writ Court must not shirk its duty if there is sufficient material for the fitness of the petitioner.
[Paras 14 to 20]
