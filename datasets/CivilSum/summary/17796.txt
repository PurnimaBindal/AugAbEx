Army Pension Regulations, Regulation 173 - Disability pension - Medical disability - Disease attributable to service - If a disease arises during service and leads to discharge, it will be deemed to have arisen in service.
This is unless there is evidence to suggest that the disease could not be attributed to service.
A petitioner who was discharged due to a disease must present material that shows that the disease was not related to service.
If they cannot, they are entitled to disability pension.
In this case, the petitioner had a 20% disability recorded in the disability certificate and the condition of Regulation 173 was satisfied.
