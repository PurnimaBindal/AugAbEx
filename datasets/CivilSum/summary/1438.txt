Constitution of India, Articles 14, 20(2) and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Double Jeopardize - It is prohibited to punish a person twice with the same allegations - If an employee has already undergone departmental inquiry and cleared of any wrongdoing, they cannot be retried for the same charges, even if the matter was adjudicated by the Court in favor of the employee.
This bar is complete.
