Punjab Police Rules, Rule 13.8 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Reversion - Repatriation - Absorption - A constable working for Punjab Police who was promoted to Junior Intelligence Officer in Intelligence Bureau, and who was later reverted and repatriated back to the parent department, has challenged the order of having to work under his junior as constable only - It was found that he had no adverse record during his tenure at Intelligence Bureau - Therefore, he is entitled to be promoted as Head Constable from the date his junior was also promoted in the Police Department.
