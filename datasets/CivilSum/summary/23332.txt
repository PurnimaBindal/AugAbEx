Andhra Pradesh Engineering Service Rules, 1966, Rule 4 - Preference in appointments/Administrative Efficiency - Preference should be given to post-graduates over simple graduates to benefit from their better qualifications, mental equipment, and quality of performance for greater administrative efficiency.
The classification on the basis of higher qualifications to achieve increased administrative efficiency is permitted under our constitutional scheme and treating post-graduates as a separate class is not a violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
[Paras 11, 12 and 13] B. Appointment/Selection - Minimum qualifications - The recruiting agency has the freedom to set minimum eligibility qualifications to narrow the pool based on higher academic qualifications, and this is not a violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
