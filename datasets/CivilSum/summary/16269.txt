The Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965 (as applicable to Haryana), Rule 2 - Military Service/Ex-Serviceman/Amendment - Individuals will only be entitled to Military Service benefits if they joined the Army during an emergency as determined by an amendment in 1976 - Individuals who joined civil services as ex-servicemen before amendment of the rules will be entitled to Military service benefits regardless of when they joined the Army - The petitioner joined the Army before the emergency and is thus not entitled to Military Service benefits since he joined after the amendment of the rules.
