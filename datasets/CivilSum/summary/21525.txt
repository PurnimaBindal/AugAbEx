Constitution of India - Articles 311, 14 and 16 - Pay structure - Bank stipulated pay grades and scale categorizations for its officers - After accepting the Pillai Committee Report and prior to the appellants' confirmation as per appointment letter, the Bank placed the appellants at the minimum of the revised scale effective from 171979 without requiring them to pass the Bank's confirmation test - Circular providing for the fitting of Management Trainees in the revised scale held neither arbitrary nor discriminatory - The appellants had no right to demand retention under the old pay scale.
