Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Advertisement of posts - Appointment to the position of a Professor - Appropriate conduct requires appointments to be made only for the advertised posts, except in cases of unusual circumstances and emergency situation - Doing otherwise violates the rights of fairness and equality among applicants.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection - Appointment - The Election Commission has directed that there be no announcement of any benefits to employees until the election is completed - However, this directive does not prohibit appointments based on selection processes - Thus, selection and appointment alone cannot be deemed unlawful on this ground.
