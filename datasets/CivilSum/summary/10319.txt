Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Writ petition - Maintainability of - The claims made by petitioners are disputed by third respondent and involve questions of fact - These can only be resolved through evidence provided by parties - If the petitioners want a remedy, they should pursue an Industrial dispute under Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Writ petition is not an appropriate path.
