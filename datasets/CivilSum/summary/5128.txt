Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, Rule 8(16) and 8(17) - Departmental Enquiry - Imposition of punishment - Respondent's non-compliance with provisions alleged - Respondent claimed he was not allowed to give statement of defence or produce defence witnesses - However, since the respondent was given an opportunity in accordance with rules, there was no illegality in enquiry proceedings.
[Paras 14, 15, 16 and 20] B. Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, Rule 8(18) - Departmental Enquiry - Non-compliance with provisions - Respondent claimed they were not questioned on circumstances appearing against them despite not examining themselves - However, since the respondent cross-examined witnesses and read their reply to the charge sheet in defence, no prejudice can be said to have been caused by the enquiry officer's failure to question them.
[Paras 17, 19 and 20]  C. Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules - Departmental Enquiry on a charge against a delinquent conductor of not issuing a ticket after taking money - Passengers not examined in enquiry - However, rules of evidence do not apply in departmental proceedings and even probable evidence can be relied upon - Hence, principles of natural justice were not violated by admitting and taking into consideration statement of ticket verifiers when imposing punishment.
