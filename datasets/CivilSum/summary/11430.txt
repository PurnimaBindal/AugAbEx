Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Post of JBT Teachers - The petitioners, who are residents of the State of Punjab, were not called for an interview because they had passed their ETT Course from the Department of Education, UT, Chandigarh, instead of an institution located in Punjab.
However, the advertisement did not violate any recruitment rules as candidates who passed their ETT Course from UT were deemed eligible.
The Court directed the respondents to interview the petitioners and consider their case.
