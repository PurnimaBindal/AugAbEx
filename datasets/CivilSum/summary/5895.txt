The termination of services of cinema theatre employees on account of misconduct failing under Rule 141 does not require one month's notice or pay lieu thereof, as per MP Shops and Establishment Act, 1958, Section 58(1) and MP Shops and Establishment Rules, 1959, Rule 14.
[Para 16]
According to Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo), every termination that is not by way of punishment, non-renewal of contract of employment, or continued ill health amounts to retrenchment and attracts the provisions of Section 25-F of the Constitution of India, Article 311.
[Para 20]
Termination of services on account of misconduct falls under the interpretation of Section 58(1) of unamended MP Shop and Establishment Act which states that services can be dispensed with without holding domestic enquiry.
However, it was open for the employer to prove the misconduct of the employee before the Labour Court if the termination was challenged.
Hence, it does not fall within the definition of retrenchment as per Industrial Disputes, 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25F [Paras 20 and 21]
In cases where the termination of service due to misconduct is not specifically mentioned in the termination order, as per MP Shops and Establishment Act, 1958, Section 58 (unamended), the employer should be permitted to lead evidence on the plea of misconduct, as per Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A [Para 26]
