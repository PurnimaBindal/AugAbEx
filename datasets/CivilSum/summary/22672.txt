Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Officiating promotion/Ad hoc promotion/Abolition of post - Reversion - Respondents who were ad hoc promoted to officiate as Junior Clerks in 1984 and were later reverted to their substantive posts due to abolition of the post cannot claim the right to officiate against an abolished post- The decision of the tribunal is overruled, and the order of reversion is upheld- The respondents cannot be accommodated against other posts.
