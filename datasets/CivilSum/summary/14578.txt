Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Age of superannuation - Promotion from Class IV to Class III - The petitioners were originally appointed as Patwaris with a retirement age of 60 years.
They were provisionally promoted to Naib Tehsildar and Tehsildar and received salary and benefits for those positions.
These benefits should be counted towards their pension and retirement package.
The age of retirement should not be different for persons holding the same positions, regardless of their initial appointment.
