The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309, the Electricity (Supply) Act 1948, and the Punjab Public Works Department (Electricity Branch) State Service Class-III (Subordinate Posts) Rules 1952 Rule 3-A(1)(2) state the rules for Promotion/Seniority-cum-Merit or Fitness.
The reservation is provided only when the posts are filled by promotion on the basis of seniority-cum-merit and not on the basis of seniority-cum-fitness.
No subsequent executive instructions can change the rules formulated under Article 309 of the Constitution [Paras 4 and 8] B.
According to Article 309 of the Constitution, Seniority fixed on the basis of instructions cannot be challenged.
If the instructions are not challenged, the promotion actions taken in accordance with the instructions cannot be challenged too.
