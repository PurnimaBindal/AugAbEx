Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pension - The petitioner, who was contributing to Provident Fund and was a member of Contributory Provident Fund scheme, is not entitled to pension benefits.
The benefits of the Contributory Provident Fund Scheme are intended to substitute for pension benefits, not to supplement them.
However, previous attempts to obtain pension benefits have been met with dismissal from both the High Court and the Supreme Court, with the latter granting the petitioner liberty to make a representation before the Lt.
Governor - a request which was ultimately denied.
As such, the principles of res judicata bar this petition.
Additionally, because there is no applicable pension scheme for private unaided schools in Delhi, the petitioner would not be entitled to pension.
