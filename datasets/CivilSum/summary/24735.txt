Constitution of India, Article 311 - Orissa Service Code, Rule 71(c) Adverse Remarks - If a representation is filed within time, the Review Committee must meet during the period between the communication of adverse remarks and the making of representation.
Failure to do so means that the Review Committee should have delayed consideration of the appellant's case.
[Para 4] B Constitution of India, Article 311 - Orissa Service Code, Rule 71(a) - In cases of premature retirement due to adverse entries, a representation can be made within six months.
The Review Committee cannot take into account such adverse entries until six months have passed.
