Haryana Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Sections 4 and 6 - Pay scales - Instructors working in affiliated colleges in Haryana claimed pay scale equal to demonstrators and government college instructors - Petitioner cited individual recommendations from university officials - Act states only State Government can prescribe pay scales and not university or UGC - State Government has prescribed equal pay scales for instructors in affiliated colleges and universities, thus action is fair - Petitioner cannot claim preferential treatment compared to government college counterparts.
