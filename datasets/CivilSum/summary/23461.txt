Constitution of India, Article 14 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 16 - Forgery - Forgery is legally considered an offense punishable under Indian Penal Code - Any conclusions drawn should not be made lightly based on assumptions and presumptions - The High Court issued a notification in 1994 which was changed in 1995 and could potentially benefit a certain group of employees - The petitioner, who was the Registrar of the Rajasthan High Court during the relevant period, had no record of personal benefits gained from the change - The logical exercise should be to see if any person benefited from the change and whether the petitioner had any connection with them - No one in the High Court considered this aspect, and there is no evidence that any candidate likely to benefit from the change was connected to the petitioner - As a result, all disciplinary proceedings taken against the petitioner were quashed, and the Full Court Resolution and order that placed the petitioner under suspension were also quashed - The petitioner was given an allowance of Rs. 10,000.
