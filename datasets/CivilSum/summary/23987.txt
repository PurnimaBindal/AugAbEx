Karnataka State Civil Services Act, 1978, Section 8 - Karnataka State Civil Services (Absorption of Persons working as Part Time Lecturers in the Karnataka Education Department of College Education) Special Rules, 1996, Rule 2(b)(iii) - Regularisation of services of part-time lecturers serving in different colleges since ten to twenty years - The lectures appointed with the requisite qualifications and not as a back-door entry - The absorption rules, framed in compliance with Section 8(3), have the force of law - One time regularisation/absorption under statutory rules is not opposed to any law or concept of equality before law - High Court's decision to strike down the rules on grounds of discrimination was erroneous.
