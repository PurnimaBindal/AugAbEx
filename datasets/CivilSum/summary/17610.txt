As per Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, the petitioner who was three years old when his father died while in service of HSEB, and was left at the mercy of relations, was eligible for a compassionate appointment.
The petitioner followed instructions and made the application, but the respondents alleged that such applications were not received, without filing an affidavit to prove it.
The petitioner cannot be faulted if an official failed to forward the application to the competent authority.
The impugned order rejecting the application was quashed and the authorities were directed to reconsider the petitioner's eligibility and suitability for a post.
