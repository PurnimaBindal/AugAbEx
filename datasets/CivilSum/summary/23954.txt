The Constitution of India under Articles 234 mandates consultation with the High Court for any Ex-Servicemen Reservation in Judicial Services Rules to ensure the independence of judicial services from executive influence.
The 1981 Himachal Pradesh Rules for Reservation in Judicial Services were framed without consultation with the High Court, which is a mandatory and meaningful requirement.
As such, the 1981 Rules are invalid and unconstitutional, and no benefit of military service can be given towards seniority.
However, the benefit of such service towards increments and pay fixation is still valid.
