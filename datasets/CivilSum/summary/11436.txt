Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Interview marks - Selection criteria - The selecting agency cannot be said to have much discretion if only 10 marks are reserved for the interview after allotting marks for various other purposes.
[Paras 3 and 4] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Estoppel - Selection - If candidates participate in the selection process despite knowing the selection criteria, they are precluded from later challenging the selection by claiming the criteria to be unjust.
