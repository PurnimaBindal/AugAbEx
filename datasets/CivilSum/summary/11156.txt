The petitioner applied to change from MS Surgery Group II to MD Medicine Group II but was granted admission in MS Surgery Group II.
When some candidates selected for MD Medicine Group II did not join and seats became available, the petitioner applied to switch to Medicine Group but was denied until they approached the Court.
Eventually, a seat was reserved for the petitioner and they were allowed to shift.
The Court found no delay and no grounds to refuse the claim, stating that the 30-day period provided only applies to normal cases.
