Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Haryana Forest Department (State Service Group 'B') Rules, 1980, Rules 6 and 7 - Promotion/Classification/Qualifications - Rule 7 of the Haryana Forest Department (State Service Group 'B') Rules, 1980 does not violate Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, even if it requires equal qualifications for promotion to Class II Service for both direct recruits and promotees - Providing eligibility conditions based on the duration of training or qualifications from an institute is not discriminatory or unjust - Specific qualifications for a post can be prescribed by the state, considering the responsibilities and requirements of the job and reasonable classification on the basis of qualifications can be made within a cadre for the purpose of promotion to the higher rank.
[Paras 16, 21, 22 and 25] Amendment of Rules/Qualifications/Recruitment/Vacancies - Revised higher qualifications for recruitment to a post can be prescribed by amending the Rules - However, existing vacancies on the date of amendment coming into force, have to be filled up from the persons eligible according to the unamended Rules without reference to the eligibility qualifications provided by the amended Rules.
