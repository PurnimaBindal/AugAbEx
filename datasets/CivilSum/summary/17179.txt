The Respondent-workman, who had previously been employed in Bhakra Beas Management Board, was retrenched and received retrenchment compensation and other terminal benefits.
He was then reemployed at the Mukerian Hydel Project with a fixed basic pay and was informed that no claim for an initial pay increase would be considered based on his previous pay at another establishment.
The workman agreed to these terms and joined as a work-charge workman.
The order of the learned Single Judge to grant pay protection was overturned.
