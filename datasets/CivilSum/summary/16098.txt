Respondents Charu Tuli and AAG, Pb, must provide a reasonable opportunity for lower subordinates, who have been enrolled, to count their previous police service for pension subject to Punjab Police Rules, 1934 Volume I, Rules 9.2(6) and 1224.
The interruption of service from 2641990 to 14.11.1990 was condoned, and therefore, the action of respondents under Rule 92(6) was in accordance with the law.
However, it has been found that the order by which the break in the petitioner’s service was condoned, has been cancelled without providing a due and reasonable opportunity, which is unjust and violates the principles of natural justice.
Furthermore, according to Rule 13.7(2) of the Punjab Police Rules, 1934, a constable who has passed the Matriculation Examination and has completed more than three years of service is eligible to compete and be selected for admission to the lower school course.
The petitioner had served as a constable and had completed almost four years of service, making him fully eligible for the course.
However, respondents ignored his rightful claim to deputation for the course, which is illegal.
