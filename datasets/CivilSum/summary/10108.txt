Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Post of Assistant Teacher - Petitioners are aggrieved that candidates below them in merit/waiting list received appointment - However, they fail to prove this claim - One appointment was made in compliance with the Supreme Court's order, while another candidate was included under a separate category - Petitioners cannot compare their case with the appointments of other candidates - Consequently, the order declining their appointment claim is valid.
