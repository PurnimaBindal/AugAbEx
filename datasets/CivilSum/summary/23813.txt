In accordance with UP Water Supply and Sewerage Act, 1975, Section 27, a post was created for the finance officer by Jal Sansthan on 16178.
The appellant was subsequently reverted from this post, however, the High Court made an error in referring to the amended provisions of Section 27 which were not applicable during 1978.
[Para 7]
The appellant was reverted from the post of finance officer which was created on 16178 in accordance with the unamended provisions of UP Water Supply and Sewerage Act, 1975, Section 27.
The amended provisions only require prior approval of the government for the creation of posts, not for appointment to posts created in Jal Sansthan.
The appellant was promoted on 7.6.86 and there is no issue with this promotion.
The reversion order is set aside as it is wholly unsustainable.
