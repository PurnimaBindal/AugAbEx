The selection of a PD Teacher based solely on interview performance is questioned by K Thankappan, citing the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16.
This process is seen as arbitrary and whimsical, with potential for manipulation, and must not only be fair but also appear fair.
AIR 1987 SC 454 is referenced as having deprecated the use of marks obtained in the viva-voca as the determining factor in selection.
The method used is deemed unfair, unjust, and vitiated by arbitrariness and is set aside.
