Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Mala fide - Plea of - Even though the Appellant accused the Chairman-cum-Managing Director of mala fide actions, he did not include him as a party in his writ application.
It was necessary to do so in order to adjudicate upon any purported malice of fact against him.
Moreover, the Competent Authority has the discretion to determine the nature of job that the concerned officer will perform.
It was not stated that power was used for an unauthorized purpose and there was no legal right, hence the appeal was dismissed.
