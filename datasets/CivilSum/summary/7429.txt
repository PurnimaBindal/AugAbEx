Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Compassionate Appointment - It is incorrect to claim it as a right - There is an objective of not offering a post to the dependents for the post held by deceased employee - The aim of the policy is to help in sudden financial crises due to the death of the breadwinner of the family - The concession cannot be granted regardless of the sound financial position of the family, as they can deal with the situation - Claims regarding compassionate appointments cannot be kept indefinitely - Making routine compassionate appointments will violate the equality clause in Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution and disadvantage those who genuinely deserve the appointment via the open market - Public offices cannot be treated as private property of their employees - Even an unreasonably delayed consideration of a claim does not confer any right upon the claimants - If the family was able to manage its financial responsibilities for a long time, the situation can no longer be considered as a financial crisis to be resolved by providing compassionate employment.
[Paras 9 to 14]
