The Subordinate Service Selection Board, Haryana - Selection - Appointment - Petitioners claimed that they were entitled to be appointed as JBT teachers on the basis of the selection made by the Board against posts that remained unfilled.
The court held that denying appointment to candidates whose names were included in the Board's list was arbitrary and unjustified.
The Board's failure to forward the entire select list to the department was also considered suspicious.
The respondents were found to have acted illegally by not granting appointment to selected candidates despite vacant posts being available.
The Director Primary Education, Haryana was directed to instruct concerned authorities to appoint selected candidates within three weeks to fill the vacant posts of JBT teachers that remained unfilled due to non-joining of prior appointees.
The writ petition was granted.
[Paras 9, 15 and 17]  B.
Subordinate Service Selection Board, Haryana - Selection - Appointment - It was concluded that there is no rule that requires the permission of Government before the Director can grant appointment to selected candidates against unfilled posts.
The Director is competent to approve the select list prepared by the Board and the appointments that are required to be made by concerned District Education Officers as per his direction.
The decision taken by the government not to fill up vacant posts and to re-advertise them was also deemed arbitrary and unjustified.
