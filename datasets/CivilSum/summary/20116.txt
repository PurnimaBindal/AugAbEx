Railway Establishment Manual, Para 303(a) - Seniority - A group of 29 candidates were selected by Railway Recruitment Board Jammu for the post of Depot Store-keeper Grade II on 11789 and were sent for training in four different batches.
Seniority was determined based on merit obtained at the end of the training period, in accordance with para 303(a) of the Manual.
Respondents No. 5 to 9 contended that they should be declared senior to others as they completed training in the first batch and earlier than other personnel.
However, CAT held that the amended Rule 303(a), which came into force on 5.5.90, would determine seniority, and Respondents No. 5 to 9 would rank senior under this rule.
However, it was held that as the posts had already fallen vacant prior to July 1989 and the selection process had been completed by the Railway Board on 11.7.1989, subsequent amendments to Rule 303(a) would have no application and the unamended Rule 303(a) as it stood on 11789 would govern the case of inter se seniority.
