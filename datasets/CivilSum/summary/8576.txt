According to the Constitution of India, Article 16 and the Apprentices Act, Section 22, apprentices who have completed training can only receive preference over direct recruits if other circumstances are equal.
In the case of a petitioner who completed one year of apprentice training and applied for the position of Assistant Engineer, he did not receive a call for an interview as he failed to meet the minimum marks requirement in the written test and oral interview.
Therefore, he cannot receive preference over other candidates as he does not have the same standing as them.
