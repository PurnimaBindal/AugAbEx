Under Section 28 of the Major Port Trust Act, (38 of 1961), Regulation 4 of the Paradip Port Trust (Adaptation of Rules) Regulations (1967) stipulates that retirement age for Port Trust employees may be reduced from 60 to 58.
Regulation 4 also allows the board to alter, amend or change existing rules without amending Fundamental Rule 56.
It was decided to reduce the retirement age due to excess manpower in the Paradip Port Trust to right-size the organization.
Additionally, the resolution of the board indicates that it is adhering to the policy decision of the Central Government.
