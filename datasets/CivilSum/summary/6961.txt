Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Reservation policy for dependents of Ex-servicemen - As per instructions dated 21.5.1979 and 22111980, dependent of ex-servicemen must be considered for the post after considering case of ex-servicemen.
These instructions were endorsed by Supreme Court in Dilwan Singh's case.
It was held in view of Supreme Court judgment that ex-servicemen must be appointed and the dependent of ex-servicemen can continue working.
Respondents are allowed to create supernumerary posts for dependent of ex-servicemen.
