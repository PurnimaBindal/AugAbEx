The constitutional articles 14 and 16 allow for the revision of pay scales even for temporary positions, such as the advertised post of a temporary Assistant Professor likely to continue into a new education cell at the Regional Engineering College in Kurukshetra.
The petitioner cannot be removed by the College without proper cause, and is entitled to the same revised pay scale as other similar employees.
Arbitrary harassment from authorities can create dangerous frustration.
The petition is allowed with a cost of Rs. 10,000/-.

The Regional Engineering College in Kurukshetra is accountable to both the Central and State Governments for financing and composition, and is therefore an instrumentality of the State.
It is amenable to writ jurisdiction.
