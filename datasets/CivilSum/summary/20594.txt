The Constitution of India, Article 14, states that if a confirmed employee is absorbed into another department of the same establishment, they will carry their period of regular service unless specifically provided otherwise.
A reversion order cannot be issued without notice and opportunity of a hearing.
[Paras 9 to 16]
According to the Constitution of India, Article 14, once an employee's services are regularised and confirmed, the terms of their initial appointment lose significance.
[Para 10]
If an employee is allowed to pass different trade tests and given promotions based on benefit from previous service rendered in another department, they cannot be reverted under the guise of correction of mistakes, without notice and opportunity of a hearing.
[Paras 15 and 16]
The Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 21, states that the limitation will start running from the date of return of the plaint, not from the date of the orders.
A delay of only one and a half month in refiling in the Tribunal cannot be condoned.
[Paras 19 and 20]
According to the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19, if a petition against reversion is filed based on the cancellation of earlier orders giving benefit to seniority, the service record of the employee should be investigated to determine the correct facts.
It is the duty of the Tribunal to do so.
