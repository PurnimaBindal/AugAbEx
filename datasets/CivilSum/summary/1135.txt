According to the Constitution of India, Article 16 and U.P.
High Schools and Intermediate Colleges (Payment of Salaries of Teachers and Other Employees) Act, 1971, Section 9, along with UP Intermediate Education Act, 1921, Section 7-A, the creation of a new post requires prior approval from the competent authority, and failing to obtain such approval disqualifies the Management from receiving reimbursement of salary for such teachers or employees.
