As per Section 19 of the Central Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 and Rule 11 of the Orissa Service Code, if a retired Govt. servant overstays in Govt. accommodation beyond the allowed period, they will be charged penal rent at five times the standard rent.
The Rule that allows for such a levy of penal rent at this rate was ruled by the Tribunal as justified, as it was incorrect to say that the Govt. was not entitled to levy this rent.
