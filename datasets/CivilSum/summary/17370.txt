Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Punjab Police Rules, Rule 12.21 - Discharge - While deputed for training to Haryana Armed Police, the petitioner was absent on two occasions, resulting in an order of discharge being served.
The order of discharge was simplicitor and there was no stigma attached.
The competent authority had given due consideration while passing the order of discharge, and thus no interference with it is warranted.
Reference of 1977(2) SLR 371 was made.
