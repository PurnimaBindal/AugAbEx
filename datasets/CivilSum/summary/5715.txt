Industrial Dispute Act, Sections 10 and 11-A - Permission to lead evidence to prove charges - Even if a Labour Court finds the departmental enquiry to be unfair, it will not impact the subsequent proceedings held to determine the misconduct charges.
[Para 5] B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 11-A - Punishment - The Labour court is allowed to take past misconducts and punishments into account while awarding or approving punishment, as these may have been mentioned in the show-cause notice during inquiry.
[Para 5] C. Constitution of India, Article 311 - Natural justice - Secondary evidence - In a case of embezzlement against a bus conductor, if passengers are not examined during inquiry, the Inspector who conducted the raid can refer to proceedings he had drawn at the time of inspection and tender the same in evidence.
The punishment is not vitiated unless there is mala fide on the Inspector's part.
[Paras 6 and 7] D. Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 11-A - Misconduct - If an employer's conduct during the inquiry is found to be unfair, the management can establish the misconduct afresh before the Labour Court, and the punishment will operate from the date of the Labour Court's award.
The workman is entitled to full back wages until the date of award by the Labour Court.
