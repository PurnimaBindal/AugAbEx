Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 1988, Rule 5 - Option - Usage - If circumstances alter after electing under the revised pay rules and lead to hardship among employees, the competent authority must allow them a chance to re-select - Once a general direction is given for relief to employees facing similar circumstances, departmental authorities should comply to avoid legal action.
