Bank (Employees) Pension Regulations, 1995, Regulations 2(4) and 29 - The pension scheme introduced from 1111993 is applicable to Bank employees and includes provisions for voluntary retirement.
A dispute arose over whether the Regulations apply to employees who retired voluntarily between 111986 and 31.10.1993 and the authorities attempted to make a distinction, but the High Court gave appropriate direction and interference is not necessary.
