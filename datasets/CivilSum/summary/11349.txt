Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 22 (b) - Pension/Retiral benefits/Compulsory retirement - The right to pension of an employee is protected by the Rules and cannot be taken away unless permitted - An employee who is compulsorily retired is entitled to full pension.
[Para 5]

Order of compulsory retirement in public interest does not cause any stigma and cannot be challenged under Article 300A of the Constitution of India.
[Para 5]

A claim for salary arrears for the period of 1980 and 1981 made in 1988 is not entertained due to the belated claim.
[Para 5]
