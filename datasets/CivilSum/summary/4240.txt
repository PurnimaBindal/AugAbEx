Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33 - Inquiry - Suspension - Dismissal - Workmen were dismissed without any inquiry almost three years ago - Employer has justified the dismissal before the Labour Court - However, even if the employer wins the case, they will have to pay wages until the date of the Labour Court's decision - If the employer loses, they will have to reinstate the workers and pay back wages, but even if the employer wins, they will still have to pay back wages because the dismissal order will not relate back to the original date - Petitioner-workmen must receive full salary from the date of dismissal until the date of the final award, if the reference is granted.
