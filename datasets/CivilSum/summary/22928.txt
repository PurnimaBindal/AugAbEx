According to Article 246 of the Constitution of India and the Karnataka Municipal Corporation Rules of 1977, as amended by the Karnataka Municipal Corporation Amendment Act of 1981, the State legislature has the power to create acts regarding absorption and appointment.
The Act was made and validated past acts and proceedings, removing defects declared by the court.
Therefore, the appointment of the appellant as Addl.
Chief Engineer is legal and valid.
Although the Act was not brought to the notice of the High Court when the direction to repatriate the appellant was made, this does not nullify the valid legislative action taken.
As a result, the order of the High Court and the consequent order of the Government repatriating the appellant from Corporation Service to State Service have been declared illegal, and the appellant is entitled to all consequential benefits, including all pensionary benefits from the Corporation.
