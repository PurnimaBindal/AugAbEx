Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Natural justice/Ex parte enquiry - Even though the petitioner requested an adjournment due to illness and furnished a medical certificate, the enquiry was not postponed - Despite an application to set aside the ex parte proceedings within three days from the date ex parte evidence was recorded, the enquiry report was submitted without considering the plea - The report was accepted and an order of removal from service was passed, despite the petitioner being provisionally re-instated after issuing show-cause notice - The order was in clear violation of the constitutional protection of opportunity to defend and proper enquiry - The petitioner suffered great prejudice - Although the plea of alternative remedy was taken at a late stage, it was rejected - The impugned order of punishment was overturned and the petitioner was entitled to all consequential benefits.
[Paras 2, 3 and 4]
