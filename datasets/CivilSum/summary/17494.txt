Articles 16 and 14 of the Constitution of India establish the Merit Promotion Scheme for Government and University College teachers starting from January 1, 1986.
To be eligible for the scheme, teachers must have twelve years of service as a Lecturer.
Private-aided College Lecturers were later added to the scheme on May 15, 1987.
On January 9, 1989, the condition for eligibility was increased to twenty years of regular service, but later reduced to sixteen years.
It was held that privately-aided College Lecturers cannot be denied access to the scheme due to the order issued on January 9, 1989.
The petitioners must be considered for the selection grade under the merit promotion scheme in compliance with the instructions dated May 15, 1987.
