The petitioner was permitted to transfer from Bangalore University to Punjabi University in Guru Gobind Singh Medical College, Faridkot for MBBS courses.
Despite being qualified for the course, the petitioner's admission was terminated without any notice and was labeled as provisional.
After disputing the annulment of his admission, the petitioner was allowed to take all of the exams and was awarded the final doctorate degree.
He even went on to register as a doctor with the Punjab Medical Council to practice medicine.
Since the mistake, if any, was not the petitioner's responsibility but that of the Colleges, the petitioner's admission needs to be regularized.
According to AIR 1984 SC 186, AIR 1986 SC 1448 & 1362, and AIR 1987 SC 2027, the petitioner is eligible to all advantages of the courses as a regular student, even though he was allowed to take all the exams under court orders.
