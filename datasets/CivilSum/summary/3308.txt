In Section 100 of the Civil Procedure Code of 1908, an appeal was dismissed because it was unsigned and no power of attorney had been signed in favor of the appellant's counsel.
The dismissal resulted from a mistake made by the counsel and the party should not be held responsible for this error.
The Registrar or the court should ensure that the defect is corrected and the appeal is remanded for a decision on its merits.
[Paras 2-4]
