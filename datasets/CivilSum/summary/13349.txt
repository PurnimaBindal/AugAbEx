Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Change in Discipline - Petitioner selected and enrolled in BE (Mechanical) at Rohtak - Challenged a provision of Brochure which disallowed a change in discipline - Later allowed course in Architecture branch at Mursthal, but continued at Rohtak - Withdrew writ petition and cancelled application to study at Mursthal - Present petition to challenge rejection - Held, that admission at Mursthal is null and void, and petitioner has a right to continue studies at Rohtak due to already studying for 2 years semester there.
