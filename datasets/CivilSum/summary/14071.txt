The Punjab Roadways Ministerial (State Service Class-III) Rules of 1977, Rule 9(1)(k), and the Punjab Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules of 1988 were amended to prescribe the revised pay scale for the post of Junior Auditor, which required recruitment by direct appointment from only second-class graduates or equivalent.
However, the amendment did not affect Rule 9(1)(k), which provided for promotional and direct appointments.
Therefore, rejecting a claim for promotion on the grounds that 100% direct recruitment is required after the amendment to the 1988 pay scale rules is not valid.
[Para 11] B.
The same rules apply to promotions based on quota and roster points for both promotees and direct recruits.
Existing vacancies on the date the rules were amended are governed by unamended rules, and those who had the right to be considered for promotion against such vacancies cannot be denied their rights due to a subsequent amendment to the rule.
