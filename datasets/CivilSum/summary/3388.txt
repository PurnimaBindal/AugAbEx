Constitution of India, Articles 1416 and 162 - West Bengal Services (Qualification Pay) Rues, 1985 - Advance increment - Additional qualifications - The petitioner is eligible to be considered for advance increments and qualification pay as per rules, as they have acquired Post Graduate qualifications of a specialised and technical nature required for efficient discharge of duties.
Denial of this benefit would be arbitrary, capricious and in violation of the provisions made in the Rules and Instructions of the Government and Article 14 of the Constitution.
