According to Section 14 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, a helper was killed and the vehicle hijacked by anti-social elements.
The court was not made aware of any contracts between the employer company and insurance company regarding liability of workmen.
The employer-company must bear the compensation for the deceased-helper, as fixed by the impugned order.
The appellant-insurance company is not responsible for indemnifying the same.
