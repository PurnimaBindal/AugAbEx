Punjab Police Rules, Rules 162, 1621, 1629 - Suspension - Unauthorized absence - The argument that employees during suspension cannot be required to perform duties and attend parades has been considered by the Apex Court in Dharam Singh's case, and it has been held that suspended employees can be required to mark attendance and perform duties as required - Any absence during suspension is considered as misconduct and can lead to appropriate punishment.
[Para 8]
Punjab Police Rules, Rule 162 - Unauthorized absence - Dismissal - Length of service must be considered while punishing an employee - When considering the length of service of the petitioner who had ten years of service, it is mandatory for the punishing authority to take Rule 162 into consideration - The punishment for absence during suspension can be reduced to compulsory retirement as per the parameters established by the Apex Court in Dharam Singh's case.
