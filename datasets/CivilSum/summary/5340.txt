Constitution of India, Article 16 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(a) - Superannuation - A government servant is obligated to vacate their office upon reaching the specified age of retirement.
They cannot remain in their post by claiming they were not informed of their retirement date.
If the government servant does not comply, they must return any benefits received after attaining their age of superannuation.
