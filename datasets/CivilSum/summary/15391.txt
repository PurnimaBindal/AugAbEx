Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2, Item 6 - Departmental enquiry - Bipartite agreement - The initiation of departmental inquiry cannot be barred by the pendency of another criminal case even if there was an acquittal in one case.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Departmental Proceedings - Judicial Review of - Findings of the disciplinary authority in departmental proceedings are usually matters of subjective satisfaction of the department authority.
The court should refrain from interfering unless there is a legal infirmity or error in the decision-making process.
