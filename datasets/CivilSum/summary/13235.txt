Constitution of India, Article 20 - Border Security Force Rules, Rule 43 - Promotion - Employee's claim of promotion already considered by DPC even after found guilty of misconduct - Promotion withheld - Procedure for withholding promotion under Rule 43 not followed - If there is no disciplinary proceeding pending against employee on date of promotion consideration then they are entitled to be promoted - Reprimand would only have prospective effect - Withholding of promotion after enquiry as per Rules permissible - Double jeopardy not allowed under Article 20 of Constitution of India - Appeal dismissed.
