The Common Entrance Test Prospectus for payment seats in the BDS course outlines admission norms and guidelines.
Regulation 5 specifies that the University is required to conduct C.E.T.
for both free and payment seats.
It is not appropriate for the State of HP to establish criteria for admission to payment seats based on the 10+2 examination as it would defy the scheme endorsed by the Supreme Court in Unni Krishnan v. State of AP (1993) 1 SCC 645 and the Court's direction in Manisha Gupta v. State of HP, CWP No. 1703 of 1996.
Out of the 21 available seats, respondents are directed to admit students selected from the CET's merit list, not from the 10+2 examination merit list.
