Admissions/Education - Equivalence of qualifications - Panjab University's Siksha Shastri admission requirement - Matriculation or an equivalent recognised qualification is a must - Petitioners passed Purva Madhayama which is a prerequisite to passing Uttar Madhayama - Since Mahrishi Dayanand University equals Purva Madhayama to matriculation, candidates who passed Purva Madhayama are deemed to have passed matriculation - Therefore, it is legally established that all candidates with Purva Madhayama have fulfilled the qualifications for admission to Panjab University's Siksha Shastri (OT).
