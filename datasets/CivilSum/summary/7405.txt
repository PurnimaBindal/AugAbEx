Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Haryana Education (College Cadre) Group B Service, Rules 2(d), 3 and 6 - Appointment of College Lecturers - The Principal of a Govt. College does not have the authority to regularly appoint Lecturers; the competent authority is the State Government - The writ Court, holding jurisdiction under Article 226, cannot rectify an irregularity - Writs cannot regularize appointments made by the Principal under Rule 6.
