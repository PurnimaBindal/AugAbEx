The Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act of 1970, sections 7 and 12, as well as the Security Guard Regulation Act of 1981, deal with the regularization of contract laborers.
The petitioners in this case were employed on a three-month contract, which could not be renewed because the contractor was not licensed.
However, they continued working under the respondents' administrative control and were paid directly by them.
The doctrine of lifting the veil should be applied in such cases, and when interpreting legislation concerning workers, the principles of justice, equity, and good conscience must be taken into account.
As a result, the workmen's employment should be regularized.
