Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 13.1 - Instructions - Promotion - Test - The petitioner must have passed the Radio Technician Grade Course to be eligible for promotion to Sub-Inspector - since the petitioner was unable to pass the course, they were not promoted along with other eligible candidates - selection is not solely based on seniority, merit is also a factor - tests are a recognized method for evaluating merit - even if the petitioner is governed by Punjab Police Rules, the instructions are not in conflict with Rule 13.1 - Therefore, the petitioner failed and respondent No. 4 was declared successful and promoted to Sub-Inspector.
