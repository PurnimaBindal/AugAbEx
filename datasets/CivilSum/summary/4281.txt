Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination - Natural justice - Regularisation of service - The petitioners were not bound by the judgment of an earlier Civil Writ Petition as they were not a party to it.
The respondents were therefore precluded from terminating the petitioners' services.
Other teachers in similar situations had been regularised, and the petitioners had a right to remain employed.
Consequently, their services could not be terminated without due consideration of principles of natural justice, including an opportunity to be heard.
The court directed that the petitioners be deemed to be in regular service based on the government letter.
