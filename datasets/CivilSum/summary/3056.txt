The Court received a writ petition about seniority and regularisation.
The grievance was that once regularisation was made from a retrospective date according to the Court order, the seniority should also be fixed from that date.
It is up to the concerned authority to examine whether ad hoc promotions of promotees in 1979 were against the promotion quota or made for a stop-gap arrangement.
The Court has issued a direction that the State respondents can make a fresh seniority list if advised to do so according to the guidelines given by the Court and Apex Court, or fill up Executive Engineer posts according to related service rules.
If there are some ad hoc appointees in those positions, they shall not be regularised, but their services shall not be disturbed until the seniority is determined.
[Paras 9, 11, 13 and 18]
