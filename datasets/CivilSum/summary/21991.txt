The selection committee must consider various factors to determine merit, and higher marks in the qualifying exam alone cannot be the determining factor.
The selection cannot be canceled based on this criteria alone.
The selection committee is presumed to have considered all relevant factors in their decision-making process.
The selected candidate had the necessary experience and submitted required documents within the prescribed time, while the non-selected candidate failed on both counts.
Thus, cancellation of selection is deemed illegal and arbitrary.
[Paras 13, 14 and 16]
In the absence of bias or favoritism, a selection made by a duly constituted selection committee cannot be canceled.
Anyone alleging bias or favoritism must prove it.
