Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administrative officer - Equal Pay Scale - Grant of - Petitioner argues that post of Ad.O is equivalent to post of Accounts Officer, but there is a discrepancy in pay scale - Petitioner has retired - Respondents acknowledge claim - Court holds that Petitioner is entitled to the same pay scale as that of Accounts Officer from the appointment date - Orders the fixing of the Petitioner's pay, payment of arrears of salary, and re-fixing of retirement benefits.
