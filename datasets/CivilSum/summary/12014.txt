Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Haryana State Electricity Board/Nigam (Punishment & Appeal) Regulation, 1990, Regulation 7 - Promotion - Denial - The petitioner was penalized with stoppage of one increment without future effect on 431999, two increments on 28121999 and one increment on 1212003.
While the two orders passed in 1999 had come to an end on 28122001, the stoppage of one increment imposed on 212003 remained in effect till 212004.
Therefore, there is no justification for rejecting the petitioner's promotion case after January 2004 in absence of a charge sheet and he cannot be disregarded for promotion.
The petitioner's promotion cases for the post of Assistant Engineer should be considered from the time his juniors were considered and promoted.
