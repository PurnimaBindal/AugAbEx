It cannot be concluded that the Labor Court did not exercise its powers judicially when granting an opportunity to an erring workman to reform and prove loyalty.
The court believed that justice should be tempered with mercy.
Furthermore, the fair and lawful enquiry as well as the finding that was not vitiated did not prevent the Labor Court from interfering with the order of termination under Section 6(2A) of the UP Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
This is based on the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A [Para 7]
