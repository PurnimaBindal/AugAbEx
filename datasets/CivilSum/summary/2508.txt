The Widow of a deceased Govt. employee was claiming the retiral benefits of her husband, but as she has never been an employee of the Govt., this is not a service dispute and therefore doesn't need to be referred to the Tribunal.
Instead, the High Court must entertain the petition under Art. 226 of the Constitution.
[Paras 4 to 6] B.
Unreasonable delay in paying the retiral benefits of a deceased employee to his widow without any justification is regarded as indulging in corrupt practice.
The Authorities are directed to release the benefits within 30 days from the date of order.
