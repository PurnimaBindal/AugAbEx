According to the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, judicial officers are eligible for rent-free accommodation.
In SD Anand's case, it was decided that Haryana State's judicial officers cannot be deprived of rent-free accommodation during their deputation in UT Chandigarh.
The Supreme Court's judgment in All India Judges Association case AIR 2002 SC 1752 considers all judicial officers to be entitled to rent-free accommodation.
