A.
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Industrial disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo) and 25-F - The Principle of 'last come first go' must be upheld in retrenchment or reversion cases, as per the Rules that provide for this.
To ensure fairness, a final seniority list must be prepared with objections from affected parties and reserving points for reserved categories before giving benefits of reservation.
Reversing or retrenching based only on tentative seniority and without objection calls, and retaining juniors while seniors are reverted, violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
[Paras 9 and 10] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 - Res judicata - When a petitioner files a suit for permanent injunction before receiving a reversion order, withdrawing the suit to challenge the order through a writ petition would not bar the petition under the principle of res judicata.
