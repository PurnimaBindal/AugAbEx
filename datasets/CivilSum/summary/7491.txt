The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 12.21 and 162 - Misconduct - Natural justice - Discharge from service during probation without taking any allegation of misconduct and punitive action - If a constable is discharged under Rule 1221, the procedure under Rule 162 is not applicable - No regular inquiry or compliance with the principles of natural justice is required - But, if there are specific allegations of misconduct even mentioned in the order of discharge, it is not a simple discharge, but a stigmatic one - An inquiry and compliance with the principles of natural justice is required, even for a policeman on daily wages.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 12.21 and 162 - Daily wagers - Discharge without punitive action due to alleged misconduct during probation - Even a daily wager cannot be discharged without complying with the principles of natural justice when there are specific allegations of stigmatic misconduct.
