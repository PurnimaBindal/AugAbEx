In case of a conflict between the date of birth recorded in the Birth Certificate issued by the Registrar of Births and Deaths and the entry of birth in the School leaving certificate, the entry in the Birth certificate will take precedence.
However, if the certificate is unreliable, suspicious, or appears to be manipulated, the party should approach the Civil Court to obtain a declaration.
As per the Passport Act and the General Clauses Act, the passport holder has the power to alter/amend the entries related to birth/place of birth.
It should be noted that the register of Births and Deaths is a public record, and a presumption of truth is attached to it.
