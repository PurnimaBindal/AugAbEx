Section 10-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Sections 34 and 36 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 outline that any agreement between workers and management to refer a dispute to an arbitrator must be published in the official Gazette, which is mandatory.
Failure to publish the agreement results in an unenforceable award by one of the parties.
Additionally, the provisions of Section 10-A(5) do not save the award.
Consequently, such an arbitration agreement and award cannot be classified as private arbitration under the provisions of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
