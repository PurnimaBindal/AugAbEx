Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Pay scales - Revision of pay scale - Posts and pay scales must be equivalent on the basis of duties and responsibilities - Any anomalies in pay scales must be removed - State cannot discriminate by fixing different pay scales and dates of revision for similarly situated employees - Removing the anomaly will be effective from the date it started - Milk Commissioner and Director Animal Husbandry carry equal duties and should receive equal pay - Arrears of pension will only be granted for 38 months preceding the date of the writ petition.
