Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 14 - Promotion - Qualifications - Retirement - Pension - The petitioner needed a training certificate in order to be eligible for promotion - The petitioner was not sent for training while others were - The respondents were ordered to send the petitioner for training, but he retired before that could happen - The petitioner was being unfairly held back, and so should be promoted even though he did not meet the qualifications - The petitioner is directed to be promoted one day prior to his retirement, and his retiral benefits will be refixed and released within six months.
