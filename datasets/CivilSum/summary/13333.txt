Haryana Government Law and Legislative Department State Service Group `B' Rules, 1979, Rule 7 - Promotion to the post of Superintendent (Legal) - The petitioner, who is a blind person, was appointed on the post of Legal Assistant, but is claiming promotion to the post of Superintendent (Legal) and questioning the legality of respondent No. 4's appointment on the ground that he does not fulfil the requisite qualifications for promotion.
However, the respondent is working on a cadre post, whereas the petitioner is on an ex-cadre post, which has been separately defined in the gradation list.
Therefore, the petitioner is not entitled to promotion.
On the other hand, the respondent possesses a BGL degree in law, which is an academic qualification for the purpose of promotion, and is therefore qualified as per the requirements under the Rules, and was correctly promoted.
[Paras 7, 9 and 10]
