Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pension Regulations for Army - Disability pension - A petitioner who suffered a bullet injury in the eye in 1965 and whose disability was assessed at 40% was discharged from service in 1983 in this case.
The petitioner was not paid disability pension, but it was held that they are entitled to it.
The respondents were directed to pay the petitioner disability pension along with interest at 12% per annum.
