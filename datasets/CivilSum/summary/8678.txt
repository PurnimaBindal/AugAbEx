Hissar Agriculture University Statute, Chapter IV, Clause 4(2) and Chapter IV-A, Clause (3) - Seniority of Teaching Staff - Merit Promotion Scheme - Promotion under this scheme is personal to the incumbent and does not affect the cadre strength of lower posts - This is to prevent stagnation and provide avenues for promotion - Promotees do not become members of the cadre and have no seniority vis-a-vis direct recruits - University directed to frame separate seniority for direct recruits and Merit Promotion Scheme promotees.
