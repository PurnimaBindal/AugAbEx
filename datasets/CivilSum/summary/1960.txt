Constitution of India, Article 227 - Workmen's Compensation Act 1923, Section 30(1)(a) - Alternative remedy - Writ jurisdiction - If there is already an alternative remedy of appeal under the Act, the court will not exercise its writ jurisdiction - It is not acceptable to use writ jurisdiction to get around statutory provisions and escape the need to follow the statutory remedy of appeal - Petitioner filed a petition under Article 227 of the Constitution instead of filing an appeal under Section 30 of the Act to challenge the order of the Commissioner, with no argument that the Commissioner lacked jurisdiction to pass the order - The matters brought up in the petition could be raised in an appeal - The petition is considered not maintainable.
