Punjab Public Works Department (Public Health Circle) State Service Class VI, Rules, 1983, Rule 8(3) - Punjab State Assistant Grade Examination Rules, 1984, Rules 4, 8 and 9 Promotion/Seniority - Promotions in the service are based on the principles of 'seniority-cum-merit' and 'merit-cum-seniority', with no person entitled to claim promotion on the basis of seniority alone - The most senior person fulfilling promotion eligibility conditions must be promoted unless unsuitable for the post based on their record - Selection based on merit can also occur in some cases - However, suitability/unsuitability on the basis of record is inherent for selection and no one can claim promotion as of right.
