Delhi Police (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 12 - Application of Rule 12 - Rule 12 applies in cases where a person was tried and discharged.
Since the respondent was not tried and acquitted by Criminal Court, the provision would not apply.
[Para 32]  B Indian Evidence Act, Section 25 - Code of Criminal Procedure, Section 162 - Confession made to police - Admissibility in disciplinary proceeding - The fact of making the confession is not disputed.
So far, the evidentiary value of said confession is concerned, Section 25 of Evidence Act and Section 162 of CRPC provides for an embargo as regard admissibility of a confession in a criminal trial, said provision per se has no application in departmental proceeding.
The Tribunal, as also the High Court, were not correct in arriving at the finding that said confession was not admissible even in a departmental proceeding.
The appeal is allowed.
