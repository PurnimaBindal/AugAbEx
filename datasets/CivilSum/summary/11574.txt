Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Reinstatement - If the termination of the petitioner's service by the respondent authorities is found to be illegal, he must be reinstated into service - Even if the Hosiery Goods Training Centre where he was transferred was closed down, it is necessary for the respondents to place him in a suitable position based on his qualifications - Accepting the post offered would not be considered as a new appointment within the department - He should be eligible for the service benefits from the year of his appointment.
