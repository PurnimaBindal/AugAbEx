Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 423 and Rules 7 & 8 of Entitlement Rules, Annexure-III to Appendix-II - Disability pension - Medical Board's opinion on disability's attribution or aggravation - Petitioner discharged from service and placed in category CEE (permanent) due to "solitary Generalized Tonic Colonic Seizure" with 20% disability - Medical Board declared it constitutional and not related to military service - If a disease or disability arises during service, it must be shown that conditions of service contributed or caused it - If the disease is constitutional, medical opinion under Rule 423(d) is final, subject to exceptions - The Pension Sanctioning Authority can still hold that the disease was connected to service - Annexure-III to Appendix-II of Pension Regulations guides the determination of aggravation of a disease - The presumption of attribution or aggravation only applies to diseases listed in Appendix-II when there is no evidence of stress or strain from military duty - Petitioner is entitled to disability pension since his disease falls under category-B in Annexure-III.
