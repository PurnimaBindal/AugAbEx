Constitution of India, Articles 311, 309 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 16 - Departmental enquiry - Appointment of Clerks and Drivers - A member of Selection Committee faced allegations of irregularities in selection and was punished with stoppage of increments - The member argued that he had fully complied with the roster system and his reply was not properly considered by the authorities - It was held that singling out the member for punishment without serving other members of the Committee was unjustified - Stoppage of increment as punishment was deemed improper.
[Paras 31 and 32]
