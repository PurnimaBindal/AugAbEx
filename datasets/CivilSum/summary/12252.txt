Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Indian Police Service (Regulation of Seniority) Rules 1954, Rule 3(3)(b) - All India Services (Conditions of Service Residuary Matters) Rules, 1960, Rule 3 - Seniority - Calculation - Crucial date - Petitioner seeks seniority restoration by computing officiating period from inclusion in select list rather than physically joining the cadre post - Respondent argues that individuals ranked above Petitioner in IPS list had commenced officiating on cadre post earlier than Petitioner - Tribunal rejected the Petitioner's claim, citing the date of joining cadre post as the crucial date for seniority calculation - Rule 3 of All India Services Rules cannot be relaxed as it is not a case of undue hardship, nor can equity be claimed on failure to get released - Petition dismissed accordingly.
