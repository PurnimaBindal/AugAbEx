The termination order passed on the ground of a plaintiff's conviction for criminal charges under Sections 409 and 109 of IPC is not violative of Article 311 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 3] B.
After an employee's conviction, the competent authority can take disciplinary action, which may include termination of services.
In a case where a Social Education and Panchayat Officer was convicted for embezzlement, the competent authority held that further retention of the employee in public service was not desirable and the decision was deemed legal.
[Paras 4, 5 and 6] C. Retrospective termination is not permitted.
Such an order will apply only from the date of passing the order.
[Para 6] D Any suit filed beyond three years from the date of passing an order of dismissal or termination of service as wrong, illegal or ultra-vires is time-barred and is governed by Article 113 of the Limitation Act.
[Para 6] E. A finding of fact in regards to the service of a termination order passed in 1977 cannot be interfered with in a second appeal challenging the order in 1988.
