Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39(d) - Equal pay for Equal work - Technicians and senior technicians working at Thermal Power Station at Panipat and Fridabad have pay scale disparities - The appellant could not demonstrate that the qualifications for the technician and senior technician positions at Fridabad and Panipat are different - All thermal plants in Haryana have a common employer after 1986 - Recruitment and promotion policies are now governed by the same set of rules regardless of whether the employees are at Fridabad or at Panipat - It was held that the plaintiffs are entitled to the same pay scale as their counterparts working at Panipat.
