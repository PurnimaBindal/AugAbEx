According to Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, the respondent was appointed as Director (Finance) of the appellant-corporation for a period of five years.
However, after being appointed as Director Finance of another corporation for two years, he was subsequently removed and is now not eligible for reappointment to the appellant-corporation, as he was not under a contract of permanent appointment or absorption into their service.
[Paras 13 to 16]
