Constitution of India, Article 226 - Removal from service - Denial of extension - Non-payment of pensionary benefits and gratuity - The petitioner joined the Air Force in 1984 and was enrolled in Defence Security Corps.
The petitioner was granted an extension until February 22nd, 1999, but was relieved on February 20th, 1999.
The petitioner was not able to complete 15 years of service to receive pension due to this action.
The petitioner claimed that he was not eligible for an extension due to four red ink entries.
The court held that once the petitioner was duly punished for the same acts and his case for extension was recommended after considering this aspect as well, then the order of discharge was arbitrary and illegal, passed mechanically without application of mind.
The petitioner is entitled to restoration of service with consequential benefits upon retirement as admissible.
[Paras 12, 17 and 18]
