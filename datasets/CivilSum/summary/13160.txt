The Constitution of India's Articles 14 & 16 stipulate equal work equal pay for JBT teachers with higher qualifications, and additional benefits are given to them.
Within one cadre, classification is made based on qualifications, and it is not impermissible to give differential treatment based on educational qualifications.
This action is not a violation of Article 14 nor of Article 16 of the Indian Constitution.
