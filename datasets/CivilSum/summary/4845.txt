Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Post of Assistant Teacher - A contractual teacher claimed age relaxation for the period he worked.
However, as he did not fall in any category specified in the advertisement for age relaxation, it was held that he was not entitled to seek age relaxation under the category of Govt. servant.
Furthermore, since he did not apply in pursuance to various advertisements issued from time to time, he cannot now claim privilege of age relaxation.
