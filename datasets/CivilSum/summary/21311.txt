The appellant argued that his service in the GREF should be counted for seniority purposes in the Provincialised Service of the State, as it was an integral part of the Indian Army and he was entitled to such under the Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965.
However, the court held that the appellant's service in the GREF could not be treated as service rendered in the "three principal wings" of the Armed Forces and thus, he was not entitled to count it towards seniority in the Provincialised Service.
The Punjab Rules 1965 were found to be inapplicable to the appellant.
