The UP Recruitment of the Dependents of Government Servants Dying in Harness Rules, 1974, Rule 5(i) and Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India were used to consider a case of compassionate appointment.
The claimant was the only heir of his deceased mother and filed the application after reaching the age of majority, but the State Government rejected the claim due to a delay of 5 years.
However, the rejection was improper as it was made without considering the hardships and without proper application of mind.
Therefore, the order was set aside.
