Under Section 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the termination and regularisation of "Safai Kamdars" who had been working for more than 9 to 13 years for the respondent Housing Board, which took over the colonies where they were working, was at issue.
The work being done by the petitioners was found to be of a permanent nature, and their services were not transferred, but instead oral notification was given that their services were not required.
The respondents' defence that the Corporation employees were performing the work was not justifiable, and retrenchment is not allowable when work is available.
Municipal Corporation Bhavnagar was instructed to engage their services, with 50% backwages also approved.
