According to Sections 2(j) and 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the District Industries Centre operated by the State Government falls under the category of industry because of its systematized activity to aid businesses and industrialists in addressing challenges and acquiring information from government and semi-governmental establishments.
Therefore, the termination of an employee's services without retrenchment compensation was considered unlawful, and the employee must be reinstated.
The question of whether the employee is entitled to back wages or not remains undecided and has been referred back to the Labour Court for limited issue determination.
