As per Section 33(2)(b) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, in cases of disciplinary proceedings regarding unauthorised absence, the delinquent worker should be given a chance to provide explanations, including medical certificates supporting their absence due to illness.
The Enquiry Officer must offer the worker an opportunity to refute the allegations against him.
If the worker accepts the attendance record and is satisfied, there is no violation of the principles of natural justice.
The Tribunal has been directed to reconsider the approval application made under Section 33(2)(b) by following the appropriate legal proceedings.
