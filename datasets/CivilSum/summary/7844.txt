Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Railway Protection Force Rules, 1987, Rule 162 - Probation - Criminal case - Termination during probation on account of registration of criminal case - not acceptable - Probationer can only be terminated on grounds of unsatisfactory services, incompetency, or unfitness - Discretionary termination during probation based on registration of a criminal case not related to official duties is not valid.
