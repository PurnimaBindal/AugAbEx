Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(s), 33C(2) - Andhra Pradesh Relief Undertaking (Special Provisions) Act, 1971 - Section 33C(2) compared to Voluntary Retirement Scheme - Interpretation - Lay off by management - Denial of lay off compensation - Payment of special compensation - Appellants chose VRS which did not include lay off compensation - Labour Court denied application under Section 33C(2) as not maintainable - Decision suggests that if claim for lay off compensation has no connection to VRS, proceeding under Section 33C(2) can be maintained - The significance of this case led to it being referred to a larger Bench.
