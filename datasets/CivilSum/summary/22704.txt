Under Section 40 of the Andhra Pradesh Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, termination during probation requires reasonable cause and cannot be carried out without cause.
The Bank terminated the services of an employee during probation without providing any reason or material to support the termination, therefore the finding that there was no reasonable cause for the termination cannot be set aside.
[Para 12] B.
According to Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, termination during probation after 240 days of service is illegal if the provisions of the act are not followed.
An employee appointed in the clerical grade of the Bank is considered a workman under the ID Act and their termination without following the proper procedures is rightly set aside.
[Paras 13 and 14] C. The Andhra Pradesh Shops and Commercial Establishment Act, 1966, requires that an employee be given one month's salary in lieu of notice along with the order of termination.
Failure to pay this renders the termination illegal.
