In accordance with Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 and Goa School Education Act, 1985, Section 11, a teacher for vocational courses appointed on purely temporary basis without any right to claim regularisation, confirmation or extension must have approval renewed each year for their continuation.
In the case of a fulltime teacher being appointed later as a Double Part Time Teacher, the petitioner has no legal right to be appointed as Full Time Teacher and is therefore ineligible for relief of mandamus.
[Paras 8 and 9] B.
A teacher appointed on a year to year basis for vocational courses, as per the Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 and Goa School Education Act, 1985, Section 11, has no claim to their subsequent appointment as a Double Part Time Teacher after 5 years of appointment.
Any petition raised after this point will be dismissed on the ground of delay and laches.
