The Constitution of India, Articles 16, 136, 226 - Appointment - The petitioner passed a written test and viva voce and appeared in the panel, but the panel was quashed.
The petitioner filed a petition against the quashment, which was allowed by the tribunal, but later set aside by the High Court.
The High Court held that it was not proper to interfere with the orders of the tribunal, as the inclusion of a name in the list does not confer any right.
However, the court stated that where the administrative authority makes an erroneous decision, the court of law can interfere.
