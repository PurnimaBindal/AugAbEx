The Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227, allow for the exercise of the Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.28, which contains provisions for the reversion of employees.
Such a case arises when a petitioner is found to have accepted illegal gratification and the reviewing authority, the Director General of Police, is made aware of this by a co-defaulter during the hearing of a revision petition.
As a result, an enquiry file is initiated, and relevant records are reviewed by the appellate authority, which leads to a show cause notice issued to the petitioner.
Despite being promoted to rank as a Sub-Inspector, the petitioner did not disclose this information in their reply to the show cause notice.
It is determined that the reviewing authority is entitled to reconsider the petitioner's case and revert them from the position of Sub-Inspector to Head Constable.
