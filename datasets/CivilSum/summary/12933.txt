Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 323(2) - Selection - The Election Commission's imposition of the Model Code of Conduct after the selection process will not affect the right of appointment for candidates chosen by the Selection Commission/Board.
Selected candidates have the right to be called for appointments once the term of the Code of Conduct is over and the authority decides to make appointments to the posts.
