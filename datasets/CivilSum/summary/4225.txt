Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dismissal - Successive petition - Res judicata - The Board of Management of University had initially passed an order of dismissal which was quashed by a Division Bench.
The order became final as no appeal was filed.
A fresh order was passed to terminate the service of the appellant without any new evidence or fresh enquiry.
The Single Judge in the second writ petition upheld the order of termination.
However, this decision was found to be erroneous as the Division Bench's decision in the appellant's earlier writ petition operated as res judicata.
Thus, the Board of Management was not open to act contrary to it and the Single Judge was bound by the decision of the Division Bench.
Consequently, the impugned order terminating the service of the appellant was quashed.
[Paras 7 to 10]
