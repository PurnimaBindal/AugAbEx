Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 100 - The High Court has a limited jurisdiction to interfere in concurrent findings of fact and judgment of the court below.
This means that the court can only interfere if the concurrent view is illegal, unsustainable, or if there is a palpably erroneous finding recorded.
