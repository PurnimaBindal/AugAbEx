Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work/Pay scale/Higher and lower post - To determine which post is higher and carries more duties and responsibilities, the maximum pay scale is considered - It is possible for an employee to receive more salary while working on a lower post than in their promotional post - According to Para 9, the pay scale of Rs. 3,200-5,600 is considered higher than the pay scale of Rs. 4,100-5,300 as the former offers further increments up to Rs. 5,600/-.
