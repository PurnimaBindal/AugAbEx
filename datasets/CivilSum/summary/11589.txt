According to Section 10(1) of the Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, a reference cannot be refused.
The petitioner claimed they were working on a regular basis, but this was disputed by the respondents.
Despite conciliation proceedings failing, the appropriate government refused to make a reference on the basis that the termination was in compliance with the terms and conditions of employment.
It was held that the order of the appropriate government in making a reference is administrative, and therefore cannot delve into the merits of the dispute and determine the lis.
The respondents were directed to refer the dispute for adjudication to the appropriate forum.
