Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 16 - Appointment - Carry forward vacancy - A candidate is claiming the appointment to the `A' Class Tehesildar position, which was reserved for backward classes and had become vacant due to another candidate's appointment to H.C.S.
(Executive) Branch as per the Court's judgment - However, another candidate from the same backward classes, but higher in merit than the petitioner, was offered the position of Assistant Employment Officer but did not join - Therefore, the question of offering her the `A' Class Tehesildar position does not arise - A vacancy on account of non-joining of a particular person cannot be carried forward if a person in the same category is available - Thus, the petitioner is entitled to the `A' Class Tehesildar post from the date the same became vacant on appointment of the candidate to the H.C.S.
(Executive) Branch.
[Paras 5 to 8]
