Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1638(1) and (2) - Dismissal from Service - Police officers who commit criminal offenses require an intimation of the offense to the District Magistrate - The Department must obtain the sanction of the District Magistrate before taking any departmental action - An order of dismissal passed in violation of Rules 16.38 and 1624 would be quashed.
[Para 15] B. Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.31 - Appeal - The appellate authority must provide a speaking order while disposing of an appeal under Rule 1613 - The order of an appellate authority that contains no grounds cannot be sustained.
