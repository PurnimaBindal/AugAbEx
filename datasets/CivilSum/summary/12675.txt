According to AP Revised Pension Rules, 1980, Rule 43 and Article 309 of the Constitution of India, pensionary benefits are only entitled to employees who have put in 20 years of service in an aided post.
The petitioner, who joined as a Physical Education Teacher in 1966, was granted voluntary retirement in 1991 but did not put in the required 20 years of service in an aided post and is therefore not entitled to receive pensionary benefits.
