Respondent Nos. 1 & 2 have been instructed via Government circulars dated May 9, 1985 and July 1, 1991 that an officer with 50% good or better than average reports in the preceding 10 years could be considered suitable for promotion if the claim for promotion to a higher post had to be considered prior to May 9, 1995.
However, if promotion has to be made after May 9, 1995, the officer should have 70% good or better reports during the preceding 10 years to be considered eligible for promotion.
The instruction does not imply that the record has to be divided into two water-tight compartments, which means that the reports prior to and after 1984-85 don't have to be considered separately.
The petitioner securing 70% good reports on the date his juniors were promoted is entitled to consideration for promotion.
The petitioner's claims for promotion should be considered cumulatively, despite pendency of disciplinary proceedings.
The mere pendency of the charge-sheets was not relevant when the impugned order was passed, and the petitioner was wrongly considered as ineligible.
The authorities have followed the same infirm procedure even for subsequent periods.
Respondents have been directed to reconsider the petitioner's claim for promotion from the date/s the persons juniors to the petitioner were promoted.
