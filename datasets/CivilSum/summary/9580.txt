Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, Section 7-A and Schedules II and III - The Industrial Tribunal has the power to decide disputes specified in the second or third schedule - A management challenged an award on the liability of providing uniforms and pads to workmen, arguing that no other similar factory provided these facilities - The court held that if the management had raised this point before the Industrial Tribunal, the workman would have had the opportunity to present evidence - The matter is remanded back to the Industrial Tribunal for fresh disposal with opportunity for both parties according to law.
[Paras 3 and 4] Case referenced: Tul-Par Machine & Tools Company, Faridabad v. Shri Joginder Pal, Workman, 1983(2) ILR 357 (Pb.
