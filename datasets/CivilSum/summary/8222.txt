Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 25-G and H - Implementation of "last come first go" principle, and failure to follow prescribed procedures under Section 25-G while terminating the services of a workman and retaining his juniors leading to his reinstatement with full back wages.
[Para 4] Also, a workman is entitled to reinstatement with full back wages despite being marked as absent if a new person has been appointed in his place, even if his juniors are retained.
