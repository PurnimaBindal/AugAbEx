Constitution of India, Article 141 - Precedent - Judicial discipline - For the sake of Judicial Hierarchy and consistency, courts must adhere to established practices and procedures.
If there is a difference between the views expressed by a previous Division Bench and Full Bench and the Supreme Court has established relevant principles for the same issue, it would not be appropriate for a single Judge to resolve the controversy.
Refer to a Division Bench instead.
[Para 7 and 8] B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - When it appears in Article 226 of the Constitution, 'Person' should not be construed in the parlance of a private person or authority, but should be read in relation to the phrase 'Person' or 'authority.'
The use of the word 'or' indicates that the term 'person' refers to a person in an ex-officio capacity in relation to an authority, and not simply an individual.
