Rule 2.2(b) of Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Part II - Minor penalties - Censure after retirement - Order of censure cannot be sustained, as punishment U/Rule 4 can't be given to an employee after retirement.

Limitation for filing a writ is the same as for suits - Petition for payment of salary in lieu of antedated promotion filed after five years of refusal is liable to be dismissed as barred by time.
[Para 10]
The entitlement to transfer travel allowance is subject to compliance with the transfer order - Failure to comply with it leads to ineligibility for the allowance.
