Constitution of India, Article 311 - Deputation - Reversion from deputation - Petitioner, a permanent employee of Deputation Department, was appointed to Archaeology Department by transfer with approval of Public Service Commission.
After 8 years, Archaeology Department reverted petitioner to Education Department to promote its own employee.
Despite holding lien in Education Department, petitioner's reversion is not affected by Article 311 of Constitution of India.
