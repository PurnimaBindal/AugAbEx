The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25-F govern retrenchment/non-renewal of contract of service.
Retrenchment refers to termination for any reason except for those specified in Section 2(oo)(bb), and exceptions to the general rule of Section 2(oo) cannot stifle the basic provision and object.
The non-renewal of fixed term, automatic termination upon completion of work or discharge due to non-fulfillment of a specified target may be exceptions to retrenchment.
If an employer repeatedly appoints a worker shortly after ending their last appointment, they cannot argue that the worker did not complete 240 days from the date of the last appointment until the retrenchment to avoid following Section 25-F's necessary implications.
Such a plea would deny all workers the benefits of Sections 2(oo) and 25-F of the Act.
