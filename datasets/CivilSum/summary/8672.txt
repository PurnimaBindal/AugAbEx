Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 16 - Termination of service - Reinstatement from date of termination of services - Denial of wages - An employee's services were terminated and the Labor Court ruled the termination invalid and ordered reinstatement with continuity of service but treated the period of absence as leave due and paid.
The court held that the employee is entitled to back wages unless evidence shows that they were gainfully employed during that period or for other valid reasons.
