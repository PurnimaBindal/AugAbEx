Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4(1) - Professional disability and physical disability - The claimant, who was a driver, was involved in an accident that left his left hand unfit for long distance driving.
It was concluded that physical disability and professional ability are not the same.
Despite the claimant's physical disability at 25%, the professional disability was deemed at 100%, leading to an increase in compensation to Rs. One lakh.
