The pay of T Mates working in the office of Executive Engineer Mechanical Division PWD B&R may not be reduced without giving them a chance to be heard pursuant to the principle of audi alteram partem, as guaranteed under Constitution of India 1950, Article 226.
The Order reducing their pay is therefore set aside, and the Respondent may pass any appropriate order in accordance with law and rules.
[Paras 5 and 9] B.
Whenever the rights of employees are likely to be adversely affected, it is mandatory for the State/Employer to give notice before passing orders as per the Constitution of India 1950, Articles 226 and 311.
As no such notice was given in this case, there has been a violation of the principle of audi alteram partem.
Thus, the order passed by the respondents cannot be sustained, as there is a serious violation of the basic rule of law.
