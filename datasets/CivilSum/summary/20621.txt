The Supreme Court Judges (Salaries and Conditions of Service) Act, 1958, Section 23C and the Supreme Court Judges Rules, 1959, Rule 5 states that retired Chief Justice and Judges of the Supreme Court can receive reimbursement of medical expenses from government doctors/hospitals or registered medical practitioners/private hospitals.
The Registrar General, with approval from the Chief Justice of India or his nominee, is the sanctioning authority for such reimbursement.
The extent of the benefit would be the same as that available to sitting judges.
