Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Sections 151 and 152 - The issue of maintainability of an application in a decided case - An application in a decided writ petition that merely seeks to pursue already granted relief without seeking fresh relief based on a new cause of action may not be considered not maintainable - In the case of applicants seeking for the holding of a supplementary examination for those who benefited from the previous judgment, the limited relief granted does not warrant the intervention of the Supreme Court.
