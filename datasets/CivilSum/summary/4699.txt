Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Fundamental right - Waiver of - Extension and regularisation of deputation period - Juniors in the parent department being promoted - The right to promotion cannot be deprived from an employee on deputation, who was detained by the competent authority - The employee cannot suffer a loss based on something he was not responsible for - One cannot be denied their fundamental right even if they waive it - If other employees received the benefit of an extended deputation period for promotion, equal treatment must be given to all.
