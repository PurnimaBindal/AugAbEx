Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Dismissal from service/Natural justice - A Turner working in a Gun Carriage Factory faced allegations of attempting to commit theft.
An enquiry was conducted, but during the proceedings, the petitioner was not given an opportunity to cross-examine witnesses examined by the prosecution.
As a result, the enquiry was flawed and the principles of natural justice were violated.
Consequently, the order of dismissal was overturned.
[Paras 4 and 5]
