BSNL Assistants Recruitment Rules, 2003 - State has the power to frame, modify, and amalgamate/bifurcate departments and create different categories of posts and cadres - Exercise of power cannot be questioned as long as rules are framed uniformly for employees falling in a class/category, even if it means amendment of recruitment rules.
[Paras 6 to 8]

BSNL Assistants Recruitment Rules, 2003 - Selection by competitive examination - Framing of new service rules cannot be challenged under Articles 14 and 46, as long as they do not render anyone ineligible - Competitive examination is a fair method of selection.
[Para 9]

BSNL Assistants Recruitment Rules, 2003 - Assurances for providing channels of promotions do not mean assured promotions - Provisions for competitive examination for promotion amounts to providing channels for promotions.
[Para 10]

BSNL Assistants Recruitment Rules, 2003 - Recruitment to vacancies existing prior to amended/new rules - Question of recruitment process arises only when selections are made - No cause of action when there is no move for selections.
[Paras 11 and 12]
