Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Rajasthan (Public Works Department (B&R) including Garden, Irrigation, Water Works and Ayurved Departments) Works Charge Employees Services Rules, 1964 - Regularisation of service - Semi permanent status entitlement - According to rules, a Workman can obtain semi permanent status after completing two years of service from initial appointment - Case referred to Labour Court to determine whether Workman was entitled to regularisation under the 1964 Rajasthan PWD Rules - Also included which date Workman would be granted semi permanent status - Labour Court has jurisdiction to determine date of entitlement - Burden of proving case is on management, even if they later grant status - Once Workman provides affidavit, management must prove objections.
