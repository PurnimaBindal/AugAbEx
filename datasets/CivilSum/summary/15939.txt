Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Municipal Corporation Act, 1976, Section 15 - Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Punjab (First Amendment) Rules, 1987, Rule VII, clause 39(1) - Superannuation age - The employer and employee can fix the superannuation age through an agreement, settlement, or award as per clause 39 of the amended provision - If there is no agreement, settlement, or award, the employee shall retire at 58 years of age - The retirement age is reasonable and consistent with other Class III employees and civil servants - Therefore, the corporation's decision to retire the petitioners at 58 years of age is legal and fair.
[Paras 6, 7, and 16] B Punjab Municipal Corporation Act, 1976, Sections 15 and 12-A - Model Standing Orders - Conditions of service - The Model Standing Orders, as amended by the notification of March 6, 1987, govern the petitioners' conditions of service - The appropriate government has the power to frame rules, including Model Standing Orders - Section 12-A states that the provisions of the Model Standing Orders apply from the date the Act becomes applicable to the establishment until certified Standing Orders come into operation - This ensures that employees' conditions of service are determined in conformity with the Model Standing Orders until certified Standing Orders are in place, thereby reducing uncertainty.
[Paras 9, 10, and 12] C. Punjab Municipal Corporation Act, 1976, Sections 12-A and 15 - Model Standing Orders - Temporary application of standing orders - The Model Standing Orders continue to apply until certified Standing Orders are in place - Their application is not temporary but continues for years - The Legislature has specified the duration of the Model Standing Orders' application from the date the Act becomes applicable until certified Standing Orders are in place - Therefore, the application of Model Standing Orders is not in violation of Section 12-A [Para 14]
