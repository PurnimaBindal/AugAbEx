Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Sections 28 and 30, Schedule IV, Items 5, 6, 9 and 10 - Unfair Labour Practices - Temporary employment - Badli workers - A practice of temporary employment that does not aim to deprive employees of permanent status and privileges is not unfair - However, employers must prioritize recalling and absorbing such workers based on seniority and eliminate any marginal disparities in benefits between permanent and temporary employees.
