The petitioner was ad hoc promoted to Clerk from Peon for six months and this continued for three years, but was later reverted due to the Haryana SSC Board not approving his appointment.
However, there was no condition that his promotion was subject to subsequent approval by the Board.
The petitioner argued that adverse remarks were never conveyed to him, and that these remarks cannot be used to pass the impugned order.
It was also mentioned that the period during which ACRs were good or adverse was not indicated.
The order cannot be sustained.
[Paras 4 and 6]
