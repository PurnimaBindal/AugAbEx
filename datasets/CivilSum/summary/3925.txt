Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 15(2)(b) and 17(b) - Statute Interpretation - Any order passed by the Court must be implemented and followed - It cannot be assumed that the legislature did not intend for orders made under the provisions to be enforced - The Trial Judge was incorrect in stating that orders made under Section 15(2)(b) were not enforceable.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Striking off defence - Jurisdiction - The management can still raise the plea of jurisdiction even if their defence is struck off - Striking off the defence only pertains to defences related to the issues on merits and not to jurisdictional issues.
