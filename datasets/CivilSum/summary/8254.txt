According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, the effective date of appointment or promotion must be considered when determining entitlements such as retirement benefits.
The Supreme Court has ruled that if an order of promotion is effective immediately upon issuance, the employee is entitled to its benefits regardless of whether they actually assumed the position before retirement.
However, the validity of the decision in Hawa Singh's case is uncertain.
This was held in the case of Dr. Amarjit Singh Ahluwalia v. State of Punjab, AIR 1975 SC 984.
