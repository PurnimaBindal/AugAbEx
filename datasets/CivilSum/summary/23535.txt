Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Karnataka State Civil Services (Classification and Scale of Pay of Non-Graduate Junior Engineers of Public Works Department) Act, 1979, Section 2 - Act validity - The bifurcation of the common cadre of Junior Engineers with retrospective effect is legally unsustainable - Those who were part of the common cadre before the Act cannot be disadvantaged by retrospective enforcement - Non-graduates should also be placed in Division I, and the bifurcation should only apply from the enforcement date of the Act.
