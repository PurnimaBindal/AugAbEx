The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2 Item 3 and Constitution of India, Article 226 provide that dismissal due to discrimination is illegal.
In a previous case, similarly placed charge-sheeted and dismissed workmen were reinstated on tendering apologies.
As no apology was sought from the Petitioners, the failure to make a similar offer has resulted in discrimination and mala fides.
Therefore, the Petitioners are entitled to reinstatement with 50% backwages.
