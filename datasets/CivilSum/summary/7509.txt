Rajasthan Police Subordinate Service Rules, 1989, Rule 4 - Correction of mistake - Entitlement for the post of Sub-Inspector - Petitioner assigned to wrong post and then corrected - Rectification order not a transfer order and follows natural justice.

Rajasthan Police Subordinate Service Rules, 1989, Rule 4 - Estoppel against statute - Correcting mistake of department - No entitlement conferred by mistake - Confirmation order passed during pendency struck down by lis pendence - Authority not estopped from correcting mistake.
