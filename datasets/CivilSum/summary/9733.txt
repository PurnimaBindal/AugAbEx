Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference of dispute - The government's scope of jurisdiction does not include adjudicating upon workman disputes.
Only the existence or apprehension of industrial disputes is required.
Whether there was compliance with the provisions of the Act, or the success or failure of the workman, is beyond the scope of section 10.
[Paras 1, 5, 6]

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F - Retrenchment - Estoppel - Waiver - Retrenchment compensation must be paid at the time of retrenchment.
Payment made later on is of no consequence unless it is shown to be made for a settlement of dispute.
The doctrine of acquiescence, waiver, or estoppel ordinarily does not apply to industrial adjudication, and non-disclosure of payments by the workman is immaterial.
[Paras 16 to 20]
