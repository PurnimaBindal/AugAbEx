The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226/227 - Ad hoc appointment - Additional increment - The petitioner seeks a direction for the respondents to grant them the benefit of an additional increment when they complete 8 and 18 years of service, taking into account the ad hoc service they rendered from the date of their initial appointment on an ad hoc basis.
The issue is resolved in Dharam Vir Garg v. State of Haryana (C.W.P.
No. 720 of 1994 D/d.
27.7.1994 - Petition allowed, direction issued to respondents to consider case of petitioner for grant of additional increment(s).
[Paras 2, 3 and 4]
