According to the Constitution of India, Article 226 and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1), when disputed facts are involved in an industrial dispute, the labour court is the appropriate forum to adjudicate upon them and not a writ petition.
[Para 22 to 26]
The High Court cannot direct the regularisation of canteen workers unless the canteen is statutorily an obligation of the State or its statutory body, as workers of a self-organised canteen run by the representatives of workers cannot be considered State employees.
The High Court also cannot direct the States to frame a policy of regularisation nor can it grant pay parity as regularisation cannot be adopted as a mode of employment.
