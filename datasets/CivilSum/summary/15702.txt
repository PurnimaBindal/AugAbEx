Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Ex-casual labour - Absorption - The scheme allows for an exemption for graduate ex-casual labourers in regards to the specific number of working days required for absorption - The scheme mandates higher working days for lower qualifications and lower working days for higher qualifications - The Court must interpret the scheme in a manner that furthers its benevolent purpose of accommodating ex-casual labour - The petitioner is eligible and must be appointed by the respondents.
