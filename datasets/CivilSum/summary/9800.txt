Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 226 - Re-employment - Claim for - Service of Petitioner terminated due to closure of mill - Petitioner seeks direction for re-employment - Court held that Article 21's right to life does not include the right to get a job - There is no fundamental right to get employment - Therefore, the Court cannot direct an employer to re-employ a terminated employee.
