In cases where an affidavit submitted by an employee-workman states that they were not employed during a writ petition, and there is no evidence to dispute the affidavit, the proviso to Section 17-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 is not applicable, and the employer must pay the workman wages due to them under the section.
[Para 4] B.
While a stay order on payments made by the employer does not affect the application of Section 17-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the employer may choose to reinstate the workman and pay their wages, subject to the final decision of the writ petition.
If the workman is reinstated and receives their wages, they will not be eligible for the benefits under Section 17-B [Paras 6 & 7]
