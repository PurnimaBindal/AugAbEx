According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Appendix 1 Para 4(i) Note 1 regarding Family Pension Scheme, 1964, an employee's dependents would still be eligible for Family Pension even if the employee passed away before completing one year of service, as long as the employee was medically examined and found fit before joining and produced a medical certificate of fitness.
The completion of one year's continuous service is not necessary for the granting of Family Pension under the Scheme.
