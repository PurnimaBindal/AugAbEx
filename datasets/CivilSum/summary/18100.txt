Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - The claimant was injured while driving, but there was no proof that the claimant was employed or receiving a salary from the owner of the vehicle.
Even though the claimant was mentioned as a driver in the DDR, it does not mean that he was employed as one.
As a result, the claimant is not eligible for compensation under the Workmen's Compensation Act.
1999(3) RCR(Civil) 601 : 1999(4) SCT 394 (P&H) was relied upon.

Civil Procedure Code, Sections 100 and 96 - If the findings of an authority are inherently perverse, it raises a substantial question of law.
In this case, the Commissioner's finding that the driver who was injured in a motor accident was employed by the owner was inherently perverse.
Therefore, an appeal can be made.
