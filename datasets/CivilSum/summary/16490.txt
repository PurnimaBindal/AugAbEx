Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 300-A - Freedom Fighter Pension - Petitioner's claim for Freedom Fighter Pension was rejected due to insufficient evidence.
However, the District Magistrate confirmed petitioner's genuineness as a freedom fighter, the State Government made a recommendation, and the Central Government issued an order to sanction pension.
The Authority failed to consider these facts before rejecting the claim.
As a result, the Competent authority of Central Govt. has been directed to give a fresh decision after considering all the relevant facts and circumstances.
