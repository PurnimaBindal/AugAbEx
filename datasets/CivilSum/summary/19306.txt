Constitution of India, Article 162 - Karnataka Municipal Corporation Act, 1976, Sections 82 and 84 - Appointment - The State Government has the authority to appoint Deputy Commissioner of the Corporation - The commissioner cannot make these appointments - Section 84 of the Act specifically prohibits the Commissioner from making appointments to the said post.
[Para 9]
Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act, 1976, Sections 82 and 84 - Karnataka Municipal Corporation Rules, 1977, Rule 26 - Validity - Rule 26 is in accordance with Section 82 of the Act and is therefore not ultra vires the Act or invalid - The government can validly refuse to approve the appointment of Deputy Commissioner.
