Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Haryana Warehouseing Corporation Employees Pension Regulations, 1996, Regulation 10(2) and (3) - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8 - Pension - A charge sheet issued after retirement that contains charges related to events older than four years cannot be sustained, and as a result, the charge sheets have been quashed.
