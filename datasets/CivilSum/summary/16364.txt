Constitution of India 1950 - Service law - Article 226 - Writ - Writing of fresh ACR - Petitioner's ACR for the year 1996 was invalidated due to not completing the mandatory physical service of 90 days - Fresh ACR was not written - Petitioner's batchmates were promoted - Court held that the petitioner should also be promoted with their colleagues.
