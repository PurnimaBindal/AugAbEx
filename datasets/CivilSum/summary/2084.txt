In the WP No. 1306 of 1991 and WP No. 1652 of 1991, AS Bobde Advocate-General, Mr. Ulhas G Kerkar (Asstt.
Govt. Pleader), and Mr. BP Pandya (Advocate) represented the State.
Ultra vires has been declared for the government order fixing new criteria without any proper application of mind regarding admission in medical colleges run by or under the control of State Govt, where the criteria of subject marks was holding field since 1971.
In regards to admission/education/estoppel, where the students continue with their studies under the scheme provided in the Prospectus of the institution, any subsequent change made in the Prospectus after that would not be applicable to the admissions already made.
The rule of promissory estoppel does not operate so that the admission rules once issued could not be altered subsequently by the Government.
Theory of promissory estoppel based on the elusive concept of preparation time for the qualifying examination would be unrealistic.
Duty of the Govt. to effect appropriate changes in relation to its executive institutions as and when the circumstances require, for admissions in the Post-Graduate Courses in Medical Colleges run by the Govt.
