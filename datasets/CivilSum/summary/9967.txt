The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, referencing Sections 10 and 25-F, addresses termination, re-instatement, and back wages.
A previous ruling from the Labour Court found that a workman was entitled to reinstatement due to noncompliance with Section 25-F.
This ruling was not contested, but the workman did not challenge the termination for over three years.
A demand notice was served and no explanation was provided that would warrant the grant of back wages for this period.
The award of the Labour Court was modified so that the Workman would not be eligible for back wages, but the time served would count towards continuity of service.
