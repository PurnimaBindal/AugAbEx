Punjab Police Rules, Rules 162, 1622, 1625 - Dismissal - Proportionality of punishment - Constable found intoxicated while on duty - The length of service, which is 22 years, was not taken into account - It was ruled that the authorities should have evaluated if a lesser penalty such as mandatory retirement was appropriate for misconduct considering the length of service - The verdict was converted to mandatory retirement.
