Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Misconduct - Absorption - Refusal to absorption on grounds of misconduct - An employee cannot be denied the benefit of absorption without a charge memo and enquiry for misconduct.
[Para 18]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Deputationist - Absorption - Refusal to absorption - It is not an absolute right of a deputationist to be absorbed in the borrowing department and it cannot be forced upon the department.
[Para 20]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Deputationist - Absorption - Refusal to absorption on accepted terms and conditions - The petitioner gave an affidavit that he would not claim any right of absorption if repatriated, so no direction can be given to absorb him in the borrowing department and he is estopped from making such a claim.
