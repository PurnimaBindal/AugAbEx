The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, address appointment procedures.
Promissory estoppel is also a factor when considering appointments to the Head of Department position.
Seniority is not lost by refusing the position in a previous year and eligible candidates have the right to be considered.
However, promissory estoppel does not extend to any additional rights.
As such, Respondent No. 4 is entitled to the appointment as Head of Department.
