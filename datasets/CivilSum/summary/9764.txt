The Indian Telephone Industries Ltd (Employees Conduct, Discipline and Appeal) Rules 1975, Rule 35(2)(a), which deals with the Retirement Age, can be taken back, for the tenure of service comes within the ambit of `terms of service' and can be altered without the consent of employee.
However, it can be challenged on the limited grounds of being violative of the Constitution, statute or rule having binding force.
[Para 2]
The circular rolling back of Retirement Age below Board level employees is valid, as Board level employees form a distinct and separate class that exercises different powers and functions than the contractual employees.
Hence, taking them as an exception cannot be deemed discriminatory or violative of Article 14 of the Constitution.
