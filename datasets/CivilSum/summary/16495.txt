Constitution of India, Article 226 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 11 - Termination of services - Back wages - An employee whose services have been terminated on allegations of furnishing a fake certificate, and whose allegations were deemed unsubstantiated, can successfully argue for his/her reinstatement and may also be entitled to back wages.
However, the employer cannot be compelled to pay for the period when the employee did not contribute anything to the organization.
As a result, the direction for payment of back wages was set aside by the tribunal.
