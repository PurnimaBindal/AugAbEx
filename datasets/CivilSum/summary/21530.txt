The Secretary of the department is the competent authority to transfer the appellant, and the transfer order passed by the Director General without delegation of power is without jurisdiction, thus a nullity.
Further orders of transfer passed without cancelling the null order will also be considered non est.
Dismissal order based on defiance of such null orders should also fall.
[Para 6]
A delinquent received no notice of an ex parte enquiry despite no effort being made to serve notice.
The enquiry officer had no justification to proceed ex parte.
Orders of dismissal based on invalid inquiry are considered invalid and non est.
[Para 7]
A dismissal order found invalid due to procedural infirmities and violation of principles of natural justice entitles a delinquent to reinstatement.
However, the delinquent is only entitled to 1/3rd of back wages due to the authorities' unfair conduct.
