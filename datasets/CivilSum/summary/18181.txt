Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination of services - Enquiry - Validity of - The enquiry proceedings resulting in termination of service of petitioner by way of punishment were quashed as the charge sheet issued by disciplinary authority was not sent to the petitioner and the disciplinary authority was not competent to make the final order.
Respondents were granted liberty to proceed afresh in the matter.
