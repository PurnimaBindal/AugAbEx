Constitution of India, Article 226 - Reversion - Promotion - The Petitioner began working as a work charge T-mate in 1969 and was later promoted as a work charge lineman and adhoc lineman in 1970 and 1979.
Other colleagues who started working with the Petitioner were promoted as Assistant Lineman and Lineman in 1995 and 1997.
The Petitioner was later reverted back to the post of Assistant Lineman in 1998.
The Court found that if the Petitioner had not been promoted earlier, without fault, then he would have been promoted to the position of Assistant Lineman and Lineman.
Therefore, the reversion is not sustainable.
