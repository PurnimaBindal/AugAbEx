Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Reservation - Despite being the sole candidate qualifying for the M.Sc.
(Physics) course as a Backward class candidate, the petitioner was not considered for the reserved category.
However, the brochure had reserved 27% seats for various categories, including Backward class candidates.
Moreover, the college had already granted admission to some of the reserved category candidates in the MA previous class.
Therefore, the plea that there was no reservation for Backward Class candidates is not tenable, and the petitioner should be granted admission to the said course.
[Paras 5 and 8]
