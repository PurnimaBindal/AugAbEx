Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Delay and laches - Promotion - ACRs - The petitioner's request for promotion was dismissed after 11 years, as it was considered to be invalid due to delay and laches.
[Para 15]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - ACRs - Downgrading of ACRs - The reporting officer, who downgraded an officer's ACRs, must give reasons justifying the decision.
The court cannot upgrade the ACRs itself, but may remand the case to the competent authority for reconsideration.
[Paras 17 and 18]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion - Consideration for promotion - The selection/recommendation of candidates for promotion falls within the managerial function of the competent authority/committee/board.
The court cannot act as an appellate authority, but may remand the case to the authority for reconsideration after correcting the candidate's grading if a mistake was made.
[Paras 22 and 23]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - The non-disclosure of bona fide reasons for ignoring a meritorious candidate creates doubts and negatively affects the morale of officers.
Therefore, there needs to be a method to apprise the concerned officer of the grounds for not promoting him/her and promoting someone else.
[Para 24]
