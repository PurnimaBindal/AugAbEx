Constitution of India, Article 226 - Age of superannuation - Interim injunction - Scientists working under ICAR approached Tribunal after proposed retirement at 60 - Tribunal restrained retirement, but impugned order not justified - Tribunal should have considered balance of convenience and irreparable loss - Permitted continuation beyond age of superannuation would usurp rights of others - Impugned order set aside.
