Section 36 of the Payment of Bonus Act, 1965 allows State Governments to issue exemptions.
In regards to the 2nd respondent Corporation, a Government order was issued suspending the Act and taking away the right of workmen to receive minimum statutory bonus without their knowledge.
However, the power of the Government is not absolute, and there was no basis for such exemption as no request was made by the Corporation and the financial position of the establishment wasn't taken into account.
The impugned Government order was made based on collateral considerations and is unjustified and ultra vires of the Act because no reasons were recorded for forming such opinion.
