According to the Rajasthan Civil Services (Medical Attendance) Rules of 1970, the State Government is responsible for reimbursing medical expenses incurred by its employees and their family members.
This includes cases where treatment is received outside of the State if a medical facility is not available locally.
In a recent case, the court ordered the State to reimburse the expenses incurred by a Government employee for his wife's medical treatment at the Escorts Heart Institute and Research Centre, where she was admitted in an emergency.
The court also criticized the State for denying the claim on the grounds that the employee did not seek clarification from a local hospital, and directed the State to reimburse expenses certified by the hospital.
