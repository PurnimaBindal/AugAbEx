Constitution of India, Article 14 - Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 7-A and 7-D - Right to appeal - The authority's action of coercive recovery without allowing one to exhaust statutory remedies of appeal under the Act by not providing the appellate authority and keeping posts unfilled is unfair and arbitrary.
The authority is restrained from effecting recovery until appeals are decided.
[Paras 5 & 8]
