Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Special pay - Pension - Upper Divisional Clerks who work on specific positions are paid special pay of Rs. 35/- - However, when promoted as Head Clerks/Chief Clerks, they are not given the benefit of special pay in pay fixation - To eliminate the pay anomaly without back wages, the Board of arbitrators decided to give notional pay - The appellants who were not in service when notional pay was given and never received special pay cannot claim the benefit of special pay or notional pay in their pension.
