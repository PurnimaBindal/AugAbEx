Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Miscellaneous application - The High Court has the power to treat miscellaneous applications as writs, which allows them to pass an appropriate order to avoid injustice caused by procedural technicalities or inaction of State authorities.
Thus, the High Court has the competence to exercise its powers under Article 226 by treating an application as a writ petition.

There should be no dispute regarding the entitlement of the father to family pension.
[Para 7] C. Constitution of Jammu & Kashmir, Section 124 - Jammu & Kashmir Civil Services Regulations, 1962, Articles 249-P, 2 & 9-N(A) - Ex-gratia relief - The enhanced amount of ex-gratia provided by a government order applies to pending cases as well.
Even if the ex-gratia was sanctioned much after the government order came into force, the benefit of the enhanced amount cannot be denied.
