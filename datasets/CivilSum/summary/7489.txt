Section 12 of the Contempt of Court Act, 1971, deals with non-implementation of directions.
In a particular case, the petitioner was promoted as per the directions but was denied consequential benefits.
The respondents relied upon a judgment of the Hon'ble Supreme Court in State of Haryana v. OP Gupta.
The petitioner, on the other hand, relied upon various judgments to argue that consequential benefits include arrears of pay.
The court held that the applicability of the judgment is a question that needs to be decided in appropriate proceedings but not in contempt proceedings.
Therefore, the denial cannot be termed wilful or intentional, and the rule is discharged.
