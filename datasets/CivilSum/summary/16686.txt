The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 state that bad service record of a deceased employee can be taken into account when denying compassionate appointment to his or her L.Rs./dependents.
Those with good service record should be given preference under this policy, which will serve as a deterrent against employee misconduct.
[Paras 9 and 11]  B. Compassionate appointment is not a punishment, so an employer's denial of such appointment does not need to follow principles of natural justice, according to Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
