Constitution of India, Article 226 - Anomaly in pay scale - Equating the pay scales of Haryana Agriculture Services Class I and Class II posts created an anomaly w.e.f.
1.5.1990 - Petitioners made a representation in 1994 - Respondents removed the anomaly in pay scales w.e.f.
1994 - Petitioners demanded higher pay scales from the date of anomaly till it was removed - The government stated that such steps were taken to make the minimum number of pay scales - The court held that the respondents cannot deprive the Petitioners of higher pay scale w.e.f.
1990 when the anomaly occurred, but since the Petitioners could not show any steps taken by them from 1990 to 1994 for the removal of the anomaly, the court is not obliged to grant arrears - relief granted is restricted from the date of filing this petition - directions given to the respondents to remove the anomaly notionally from 1990 onwards.
[Paras 1 and 3 to 6]
