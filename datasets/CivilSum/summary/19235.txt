Punjab Cooperative Societies Rules, 1963, Rule 46 - Appointments - Relations of members of Committee or officers of Society - Appointment of any relation of members of Committee or officers of a Society must be approved by the Registrar prior to appointment.
However, they may still be considered for appointment in the Bank.
A case wherein the Deputy Registrar was a member of the Committee and selected the son of a member of the Committee demonstrated tacit approval of his selection.
The Bank may still need to forward the case for approval of the Registrar, but the entire selection cannot be deemed invalid solely due to this reason.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection - Petitioners who were allowed to appear and considered for selection but did not perform well in the type test, which was the only requisite to pass, have no grounds for complaint.
[Para 15] C. Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservations - Selection - Selection for appointments in the Cooperative Bank is at the discretion of the Bank with regard to the reservation policy of the Govt.
In the absence of any Rule or instructions from the Bank asserting that the Govt.'s instructions for reservation in appointments are binding, the Bank has the power to make or decline reservations.
