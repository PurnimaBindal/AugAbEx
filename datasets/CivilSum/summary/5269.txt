Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Appointment - Fixed term appointment - Unfair conditions of appointment that impose limitations on tenure are deemed unreasonable and unacceptable by the court - Such terms have colloquially been referred to as "the Henry VIII Clause" - Acceptance of such terms does not operate as estoppel.
