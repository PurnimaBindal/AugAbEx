Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Double Jeopardy - Pendency of Criminal proceedings - Departmental enquiry - If criminal proceedings are pending for the same charge as departmental proceedings, the departmental proceedings must be put on hold until the criminal trial reaches the stage where the accused discloses their defense - However, the suspension order will remain in effect if the appointing authority chooses.
[Paras 9 and 10]
