Constitution of India, Articles 14, 311 and 226 - Pension and retiral benefits - Family pension - Disciplinary proceedings - The employee's date of birth was questioned in 1972 and then again in 1990-91 before his retirement.
Although a report came in favor of the employee in 1991, the competent authority did not pass any orders until 1994.
The employee died within two months of retirement, and his widow received a show cause notice without any documents.
The order passed was in violation of natural justice, as the authority was required to supply complete documents and reasons for differing with the report.
Pension and family pension are valuable rights, and the impugned order was quashed.
The respondents were directed to release all dues of the deceased to the petitioner/widow and family pension without any delay.
