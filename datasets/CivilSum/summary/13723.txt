Constitution of India, Article 14 - Probationer - Termination during probation on the ground of unsatisfactory performance - An employee who fails to demonstrate good results during probation may be terminated from service, even during an extended period of probation.
The probation period is used to evaluate the employee's work and conduct.
If the employee meets the required standard to the satisfaction of the employer, he or she acquires the right to confirmation.
However, being on probation does not provide a right to management to terminate an employee's services without proper justification.
Nevertheless, an employee cannot claim confirmation despite unsatisfactory performance.
