The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 state that the scope of judicial review in the matters of adverse remarks is limited.
However, if the remarks are recorded in an arbitrary manner or on account of extraneous considerations, the writ court can interfere.
In the case of a Deputy Commandant in CRPF receiving uncalled for adverse remarks on their work and conduct, despite their work and performance being commended, the appellate authority found mala fide of the reporting and reviewing authorities and expunged some of the remarks.
The remaining remarks were held to be recorded arbitrarily and quashed.

If an employee is ignored for promotion on account of adverse remarks, which are later found to be recorded arbitrarily and quashed, the employee is entitled to be considered afresh without such remarks.
