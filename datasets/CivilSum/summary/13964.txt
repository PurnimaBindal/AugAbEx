Under Section 2(n) of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, a casual worker who worked for only one day on a daily wage of Rs. 100/- is considered a worker and is entitled to compensation.
One does not have to be a permanent worker to be classified as a workman.
The compensation amount for such cases should be calculated based on monthly wages as outlined in Section 5.
Additionally, under Section 4(A)(3)(a), interest on the compensation amount must be paid at a rate of 12% per annum from the date of the accident.
