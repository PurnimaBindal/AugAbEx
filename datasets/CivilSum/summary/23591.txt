The National Bank for Agricultural and Rural Development issued guidelines for promotions under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule 2, Item 6, stating that promotions should be based on seniority-cum-merit.
However, a subsequent circular applied to all employees of Rural Bank, which required promotions to be based on a comparative assessment of performance appraisal of officers concerned.
The promotions made after consideration of such performance appraisal cannot be challenged on the grounds of violation of guidelines, hence the Ld.
single judge's judgment is set aside and the appeal is allowed.
