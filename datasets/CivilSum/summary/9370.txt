According to ESIA Sections 85(e)(ii) and CPC 1973 Sections 468(2)(b) and 472, failure to pay ESI contribution and submit returns within the prescribed period is an offence.
The accused did not comply with this, resulting in a limitation of only 6 days.
Although ESIA is a beneficial law for employee welfare, the court failed to condone the delay and set aside the accused's acquittal.
This is in line with AIR 1984 SC 1688.
[Paras 5 and 7] B.
Under ESIA Section 85(e)(ii) and CPC 1973 Section 468(2)(b), it is a continuing offence to fail to submit returns under ESIA within the prescribed period.
