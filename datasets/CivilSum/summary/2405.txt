Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(j), 2(s) and 25F - Discharge from service - The Institute employees meet the definition of "workmen" in Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act - The Institute is considered to be an industry - Mr. Respondent No. 2 worked as a welder instructor and his service was terminated voluntarily - A certificate of good conduct was issued at the time of termination - Mr. Respondent No. 2 received ex-gratia payment - He filed a claim for reinstatement with back wages about a year later after the termination was made, leading to an industrial dispute - The Labour Court ruled in favour of Mr. Respondent No. 2 - However, the High Court found the award of the Labour Court to be erroneous and perverse.
