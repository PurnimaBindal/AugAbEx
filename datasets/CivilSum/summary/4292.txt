When discharging an employee on the ground of inefficiency under Punjab Police Rules 1934, the material considered must be relevant.
If the material is found to be irrelevant, the court may not sustain the order.
[Para 4] B.
An order of termination, while innocuously stated, but establishes infraction of the provisions of Article 311(2) of the Constitution, the court can lift the veil to find out the real purpose of the order.
This is in accordance with Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221.
[Para 4] C. When the competent authority decides to discharge a police officer on the grounds of unsuitability under the relevant service rules, and the action is not punitive, it is not necessary to hold an enquiry and frame charges.
Simple discharge under the service rules cannot be termed as punishment even if some preliminary enquiry was held on some misconduct and dropped with the view not to take punitive action.
Provisions of Article 311(2) are attracted only when either of the three major punishments are intended to be awarded as provided in Article 311.
[Paras 4, 5, 8 and 11] D. If there is a direct nexus between the charge levied and the action taken, the order will be punitive in nature as provided by Rule 1221 of Punjab Police Rules, 1934.
This is in accordance with Article 311 of the Constitution.
