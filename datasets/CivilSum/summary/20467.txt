Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Rajasthan Subordinate Officers Ministerial Staff Rules, 1957, Rules 7(1)(d) and 15 - Promotion - Qualifying examinatiion - The petitioner was appointed to the post based on the result of a qualifying examination.
He not only passed the examination, but also received the highest marks.
The order of appointment was based on a competitive basis that was entirely different from the other 50% who were to be appointed based on seniority-cum-merit.
The court quashed the order cancelling the petitioner's promotion and entitled him to consequential benefits.
[Paras 9 and 10]
