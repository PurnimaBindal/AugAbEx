Fundamental Rules, Rules 9(21)(a) and 44 - Central Administrative Tribunal (Salaries and Allowances and Conditions of Service of Chairman, Vice Chairman and Members) Rules, Rule 6(i)(iv) - HRA - After reviewing the judgment of Supreme Court in Director, Central Plantation Research Institute, Kesaragad's Case, it has been determined that HRA is covered by the definition of compensatory allowance outlined in FR 44 and is therefore not considered part of pay.
[Para 11] B Central Administrative Tribunal (Salaries and Allowances and Conditions of Service of Chairman, Vice-Chairman and Members) Rules, Rule 12 - Swamy's FRSR Part V (HRA and CCA) - An Administrative Member of CAT is entitled to reimbursement of rent for private accommodations under certain conditions, including sending a proposal for approval of the rent to the Principal Bench if a suitable accommodation within prescribed carpet area and rent ceiling is not available.
[Para 12] C. Central Administrative Tribunal (Salaries and Allowances and Conditions of Service of Chairman, Vice-Chairman and Members) Rules, Rule 12(2) - Claim of 15% HRA - It has been held that if a Member is not provided accommodation or chooses not to avail of pool accommodation, they should be entitled to a monthly allowance equal to 15% of their pay.
