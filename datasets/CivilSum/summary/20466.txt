Indian Audit and Accounts Department Section Officer (Commercial Audit) Recruitment Rules, 1988, Rule 12 - Comptroller and Auditor General's Manual of Standing Orders (Administrative) Volume-J Paragraph 5.6.6 - Executive Instructions/Standing orders - Seniority of the directs recruits to the post of Section Officers - An executive instructions/Standing orders can be validly made and enforceable to fill the gap not covered by Rules and inconsistent with the Rules if framed, even if there are no statutory rules in force for determining seniority in a Service or even if they are silent on any particular subject.
