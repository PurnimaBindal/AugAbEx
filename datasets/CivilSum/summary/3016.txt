Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Scope - Labour Court orders reinstatement with continuity of service and full back wages, and granting benefit of pay scale - The order is challenged, but the Labour Court committed no error while exercising power under Section 33-C(2) and has sufficient power to grant interest - The court cannot reappraise evidence led before Labour Court - Therefore, no interference under Article 226 and 227 is necessary - Ad interim relief granted earlier is now vacated.
