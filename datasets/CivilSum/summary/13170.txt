According to Section 4A of the Workmen's Compensation Act 1923, the insurance company is obligated to pay interest on the award.
Despite any specific agreement between the insurer and the insured, the insurance company failed to deposit the sum within 30 days of the accident, making it liable to pay interest.
This liability still holds even if the insurance company appeals the decision while being fully aware of its statutory position.
