Refusal of legal assistance in departmental proceedings of Gujarat Panchayat Service (D and A) Rules, Rule 7(7), despite petitioner's request to engage a lawyer, was unjustified and arbitrary and against the rule, given the Law Graduate Presenting Officer's key role in multiple disciplinary proceedings.
[Paras 5 and 8] B.
In a case where petitioners were refused legal aid for a defence in departmental proceedings on the grounds that the case was not complicated, discovery of the fact that 16 witnesses were examined by the department against the petitioner and that the Presenting Officer was a Law Graduate showed the rejection to have been arbitrary and unjustified.
[Para 16] C In departmental enquiries, the Authority to grant permission of engaging a Legal Practitioner in defence lies with the disciplinary authority as per Rule 7(7) of Gujarat Panchayat Service (D and A) Rules.
It is not under the Enquiry Officer's scope.
