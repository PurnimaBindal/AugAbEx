According to Railway Establishment Manual, Paragraph 321 - Seniority - Inter-se- Seniority - the inter-se seniority in the Junior Planner cadre must be determined according to para 321.
The post of Junior Planner considers staff of different seniority units, and the total length of service in equivalent grade held by employees is the determining factor.
In this case, two appellants who held the post of welder since 4376 are contesting against a respondent who held the post of Springsmith since 18780.
Since the posts of welder and springsmith are in the same grade, the two appellants must be considered senior to the contesting respondent as they have longer length of service in the equivalent grade from which they were promoted.
