Constitution of India, Articles 136, 32, 226, 14, 16, 39, 41 and 45 - Provision of equal pay for equal work - Teachers of aided private schools are entitled to receive pay scales and other benefits equivalent to those of Government school teachers - The State Government has been instructed to reimburse the Additional Dearness Allowance in 35 instalments.
