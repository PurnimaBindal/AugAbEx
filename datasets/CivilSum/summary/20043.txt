Constitution of India, Articles 21, 136, and 141 - Appointment/Temporary appointment/Absorption - Age and physical fitness - A scheme was created by the state to absorb eligible workers who fulfilled eligibility requirements - However, some workers who worked for about 8 years and achieved some qualifications did not meet eligibility requirements - While the scheme cannot be faulted and no direct order can be given to absorb these workers, authorities have been directed to engage them first if work is available and consider them for absorption by relaxing the age limit.
