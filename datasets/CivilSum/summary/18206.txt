Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 91-B - Voluntary retirement taken by deputationist - The petitioner claimed entitlement to privilege leave for service rendered following fresh appointment in Federation - However, respondents computed privilege leave salary based on entire service - It was ruled that petitioner, despite being appointed pursuant to resolution by absorption, was not entitled to pensionary benefits beyond rules prescribed for transferred and absorbed employees - According to Regulation 12(i), privilege leave must be worked out as per RSR provisions and Rule 91-B of RSR specifies that unused leave salary cannot surpass 300 days in total.
