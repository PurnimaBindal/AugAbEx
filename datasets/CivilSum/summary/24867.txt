Civil Service Regulations, Article 459(L) - Validity - Compulsory retirement - According to Mr. Rao, adverse entries were not communicated.
However, the entries extracted were based on general assessment of performance, and the officer had already been informed of disapproval from years before.
The review proceedings followed the guidelines of the Government and were justified due to the responsibility of the officer's position.
Compulsory retirement does not imply misbehaviour or incapacity, as previously determined by the Constitution Bench in Shyam Lal v. State of UP & Union of India.
The power of compulsory retirement and its procedure have been approved through numerous decisions of the Court.
