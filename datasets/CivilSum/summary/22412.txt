Constitution of India, Article 16 - UP Recognised Schools [Junior HIgh School] Recruitment and Conditions of Service of Teachers Rules, 1978 - Rule 9 - Promotion - Seniority - Selection post - Headmistress should be appointed based on selection, after considering seniority of applicants in initial cadre of Assistant Teachers.
Appointments through selection do not depend on confirmation or length of service.
Seniority depends on length of service.
