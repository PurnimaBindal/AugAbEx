Constitution of India, Articles 311, 309, Proviso - Promotion - The argument by Food and Civil Supplies Department employees challenging promotions due to the abolition of the department and its integration with Revenue Department is invalid - Despite the abolition of statutory rationing, Food and Civil Supplies Department and Revenue Department remain separate government departments, and employees will still be subject to the rules of Food and Civil Supplies Department - Government Servant - Promotion.
