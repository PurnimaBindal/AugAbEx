According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, inter-State degrees and diplomas must be recognized for education purposes.
In particular, the State of Haryana has recognized diplomas issued by the J&K State Board of Education for higher education and recruitment for jobs, due to instructions issued by the Haryana Govt.
Furthermore, an eligible person cannot be denied employment solely on the basis of holding a degree or diploma from a different state or institution.
[Paras 14 to 23]
