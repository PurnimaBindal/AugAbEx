Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Companies Act, Section 617 - Dearness relief on military pension - Recovery on re-employment - The respondent company ordered recovery of dearness relief on military pension from the petitioner for the years 1983 to 2001 after the petitioner joined in 1983 - As the respondent is a private limited company and not a government company, the petitioner is not a holder of civil post under State and there is no relationship of master and servant - Therefore, the respondent is prohibited from withholding dearness relief and any recovery made shall be refunded.
[Paras 5, 7 and 8]
