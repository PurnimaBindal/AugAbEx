The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, mandates equal pay for equal work.
The petitioner, who was employed as a Helper but was unable to perform the physically demanding duties of the role due to a disability, requested to be assigned the duties of a Clerk-cum-Typist.
This request was granted on compassionate grounds, but his claim for payment as a Clerk-cum-Typist was deemed unjustified because the doctrine of equal pay for equal work does not apply.

The Constitution of India, particularly Articles 14 and 16, prohibit discrimination in promotion and require seniority-based promotion.
The respondent in this case, who was the most senior Class IV employee, was promoted to the post of Clerk-cum-Typist through the quota system.
The petitioner's claim of discrimination between him and the respondent was rejected, as he would also be considered for promotion when his turn comes.
