Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission/Education - Despite having no fault of his own, the petitioner was denied admission in B.Ed while candidates with lower marks were admitted - The petitioner has missed most of the classes and is unable to fulfil the attendance requirements - The court has a duty to uphold public interest and prevent public harm and has the power to issue writs - The court has directed for the petitioner to be admitted in the next session and receive compensation of Rs. 15,000/- for the loss of one year due to no fault of his own.
