Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Paras 173 and 173-A - Central Civil Services (Extra-ordinary Pension) Rules, Rule 38 - Disability pension - The rules do not specify any minimum service requirement for an army personnel to become eligible for disability pension, as long as the disability is attributable to service.
Even those who suffer disability shortly after joining can claim disability pension.
[Para 6]

Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Paras 173 and 173-A - Central Civil Services (Extra-ordinary Pension) Rules, Rule 38 - Disability pension - Paranoid Schizophrenia caused by military service - If the individual did not have any recorded disorder or disability at the time of joining service, the disease is considered to have occurred due to stress and strain of army service and hence attributable to service.
[Paras 6 and 7]

Constitution of India, Article 310 - Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Paras 173 and 173-A - Medical Board examinations of personnel for disability pension - In a case where the court orders repeated medical examinations until the final decision, the petitioner is considered to be still in the master-servant relationship until the final decision, regardless of the initial medical board.
[Paras 7 and 8]
