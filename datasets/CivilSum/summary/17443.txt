Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Resignation - Withdrawal of resignation - A notice of three months to quit is required - It is not possible to accept the resignation before the three month notice period has expired - The employee may withdraw their resignation at any point before the three months are up - If the withdrawal is made before the notice period is over, the management cannot accept the resignation and the employee retains their rights on the post - The single judge was incorrect in stating that the management could accept the resignation before the notice period had ended and that the employee could not withdraw it after acceptance.
