Punjab State Electricity Board Main Service Regulations, 1972, Vol-I, Part-I, Regulation 7.3(2)(4) - Suspension period - Leave of kind due - Suspension must be justified with proper charge proving and employee allowed to prove their innocence - If the employee is not proven guilty and suspension is not justified, they are entitled to all service benefits and the duration of the suspension must be counted as duty period.
