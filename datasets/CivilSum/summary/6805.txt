Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - Delay - After the death of her husband (who had been employed), the petitioner, along with her minor son, applied for appointment to her son on compassionate grounds as opposed to immediately applying for her own appointment - However, her decision not to apply for herself does not prevent her from representing her son for employment in accordance with government policy instructions - The appointment of a dependent son of the deceased cannot be denied.
[Paras 5, 6 & 7]
