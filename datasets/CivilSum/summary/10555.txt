Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 9-A - Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act 1946, Sections 4 and 5 - Service conditions - Alteration in service conditions - Absorption/Superannuation - Date of superannuation - Petitioners employed in a Government Department who have been absorbed in a Corporation are eligible for all benefits of Government service and may include their service in the Corporation for this purpose.
Their age of superannuation, however, will remain the same as in Government Service.
If the Corporation had included a stipulation in its rules that the age of such employees for superannuation would be the same as in Government Service, it should not be considered as a change in service conditions.
