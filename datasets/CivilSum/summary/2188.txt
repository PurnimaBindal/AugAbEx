According to Sections 4 and 2(e) of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, teachers of educational institutions cannot be regarded as employees under the act's definition of "employee."
This exclusion applies to individuals who do not perform skilled, semi-skilled, manual supervisory, technical, or clerical work.
As the job of a teacher is to impart education, it does not fall into the categories of skilled or unskilled manual, supervisory, technical, or clerical work.
