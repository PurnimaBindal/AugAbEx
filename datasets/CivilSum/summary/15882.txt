Constitution of India, Articles 311, 16 and 14 - Promotion - In the Animal Husbandry department, 20% quota is allotted for Agriculture Sub Inspectors to be promoted to positions such as Agriculture Inspectors and Agriculture Development Officer, however, the petitioner working as an Agriculture Sub Inspector in the Soil Conservation department was denied such quota - The respondents argued that the departmental rules and qualifications for Agriculture Sub Inspectors in the Animal Husbandry and Soil Conservation departments are different, and the nature of work is also dissimilar - As a result, the writ petition was dismissed.
