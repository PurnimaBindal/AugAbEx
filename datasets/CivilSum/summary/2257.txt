Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Andhra Pradesh State Electricity Board Regulations, Regulation 28(3) - Dismissal - Unauthorised absence - The petitioner was suspended in 1981 due to material shortage and was found guilty in domestic enquiry.
An order of recovery was passed and he was reinstated in 1987, but at a different station.
Upon receiving an order of reposting, he refused to comply and stated that he would abide by the decision of the court.
The principles of natural justice were not violated and Regulation 28(3) of the AP State Electricity Board Regulations was not arbitrary.
Therefore, no interference is called for.
