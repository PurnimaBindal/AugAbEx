Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection for teachers - Interview marks - The distribution of 50% marks for academic performance and 50% for viva voce in the selection of services does not exhibit any arbitrary behavior that warrants interference from the court.
Marks for the interview may vary based on the nature of the service and the age group of the candidates.
The allocation of 33% marks for the interview in teacher selection cannot be considered excessive.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection for teachers - Marks for higher qualifications - No additional marks should be awarded for higher qualifications than the ones set out in the rules for the selection of teacher roles.
