As per the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 16 and 226, qualifications and promotions are of utmost importance when it comes to appointments, particularly to Headmaster positions.
A petitioner who previously served as a BT Assistant, who then earned an MA degree, and was appointed as Headmaster was later set aside by the Secretary of the school as they deemed the petitioner unqualified for the position.
However, according to GOMs.
No. 720, an amendment made by GOMs.
No. 1657 on October 31, 1988, BT teachers who have completed a 10-month PG course conducted by the State Council of Education Research and Training will be considered for the position of Headmaster.
The impugned order, therefore, must be set aside since the required qualifications for promotion to Headmaster calls for a BT Assistant with no less than a decade of experience.
Thus, the petition is allowed.
