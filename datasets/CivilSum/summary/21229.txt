Under the Andhra Pradesh Part-time Village Officers Abolition Act, Sections 3 and 5, along with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, an employee was only entitled to gratuity under the Scheme if they retired at the age of 58 years, after giving notice.
However, upon abolition of the posts under the Act, the Scheme no longer applied, therefore the employee was only entitled to a refund of their contribution towards the family benefit scheme, plus 6% interest.
