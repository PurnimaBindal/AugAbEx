The Haryana Aided Schools (Security of Service) Rules, 1974, Rule 8(2)(3)(6) - Probation period / Deemed confirmation / Termination - Services could not be terminated for unsatisfactory completion of probation period - Termination must be for valid reasons - Probation period cannot be extended beyond three years - Termination order quashed - Employee deemed to be confirmed.
