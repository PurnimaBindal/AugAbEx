Constitution of India, Articles 226, 311(2) - Army Regulations, Regulation 423 - Dismissal - Benefit of Acquittal - Petitioner, an Indian Army personnel, was convicted of a murder case by a designated court but was later acquitted by the Hon'ble Supreme Court in appeal and was released on bail.
However, he was dismissed from service.
The petitioner seeks reinstatement with full back wages, contending that acquittal does not automatically lead to reinstatement, and Article 311 restricts the manner in which government servants can be dismissed, removed or reduced in Rank.
The first clause of Regulation 432 allows for punishment without an enquiry on grounds of conduct that lead to conviction.
The question is whether the petitioner is entitled to reinstatement given his conviction was set aside.
It was held that when a government servant accused is acquitted in appeal, the order of dismissal must be revised/reviewed by the disciplinary authority, and therefore the petitioner is entitled to reinstatement with continuity of service and back wages.
The petition is allowed accordingly.
