According to the Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227, the petitioner complied with note 72 of the B.Ed.
course prospectus by sending their admission form via Registered Post to the Coordinator, Entrance Test Panjab University.
The petitioner received their result card one day earlier and the registered cover was returned to them without any endorsement eleven days later.
The addressee denied this fact, but the documentation makes it clear that the admission form was sent and received back as stated.
The respondents rejected the petitioner's candidature because note 72 had been amended to require the admission form to reach respondent No. 3 within 10 days of the declaration of the result, which was not known to the petitioner.
However, there is no reason to disbelieve the petitioner's claim, and the petition is therefore allowed.
