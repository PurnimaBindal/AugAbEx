Punjab State Cooperative Financing Institutions Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1970-71, Rule 3 - Death of employee after serving for over 30 years - The dependants of the deceased employee are entitled to 20 months gratuity instead of 15 months gratuity.
Clause (b) of Rule 3, which allowed for discriminatory payment, has been invalidated.
