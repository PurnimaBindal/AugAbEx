Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay - Achievement of targets - An employee cannot be denied salary for not achieving a target if no such condition was set at the time of appointment.
[Paras 10 & 12]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation - Petitioners working for Indian Red Cross Society Haryana seeking regularisation of their employment - The court held that since they were working on a project and not employed by the State Govt., their request for regularisation cannot be considered.
