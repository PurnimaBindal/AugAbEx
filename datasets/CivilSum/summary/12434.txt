The petitioner, a widow and mother of an only unmarried son who died after retirement, was denied family pension based on Rule 54 of the Central Government Pension Rules, 1972, which excludes parents of an unmarried employee who dies from eligibility for family pension.
Two writ petitions were filed seeking a declaration that the mother and father of the deceased unmarried son are entitled to family pension under Rule 54 and quashing the intimation denying family pension.
The court held that Rule 54 does not include the mother of the unmarried son within the definition of family for the purpose of family pension and, therefore, the relief of family pension cannot be given to the petitioner.
