Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Reference - The appropriate Govt.'s scope of jurisdiction at the reference stage is not like that of an adjudicator - It cannot consider the merits of the case regarding the validity of retrenchment and compliance with the provisions of the Act - The Govt. does not have the authority to reject a reference on those grounds - The Labour Court will make that determination after the reference.
