Constitution of India, Article 14 - Recovery - Use of office car and purchase of furniture for office - The Chairman of a Co-operative Bank was accused of using office funds to purchase furniture and use official car for private purposes.
A report from the Joint Registrar contradicted this accusation and indicated that the purchases were made for official use.
As a result, the order for recovery of alleged unwarranted expenditure was set aside.
