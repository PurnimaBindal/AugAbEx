Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Award - Dismissal of claim on ground of territorial jurisdiction - The infringement of rights of the workman arose at Rajasthan where the workman was last employed.
Despite the claim raised at Delhi, where the Head office of management and Directors are residing and PF was deposited, the cause of action did not arise in Delhi.
The impugned award has no illegality, perversity or infirmity.
