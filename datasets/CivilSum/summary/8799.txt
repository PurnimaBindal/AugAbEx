As per Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, under Section 33-C(2), the Labour Court cannot allow any interest on arrears of wages or payment of leave encashment.
In this case, the workman was reinstated with full back wages and continuity of service, but there was no mention of payment of interest in case of non-payment or delay and no direction for payment of leave encashment in the award.
Therefore, the Labour Court, acting as an Executing Court, could not go beyond the award.
Additionally, the workman was not entitled to any leave encashment since they were paid the salary for the entire period from the date of termination of services to the date of joining.
As there was no specific order for leave encashment, the Labour Court could not require the management to make such payment while exercising powers under Section 33-C(2) of the Act.
