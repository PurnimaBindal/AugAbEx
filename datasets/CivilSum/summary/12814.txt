The Education Policy, as per the Punjab Civil Services Rules (as applied to Haryana) Vol. 1, Rule 44, states that academicians must define admission criteria that are objective and prioritize merit.
While it may not suit every group, uniform criteria that acknowledge higher qualifications are not considered arbitrary.
[Para 6] B.
As per Articles 14, 16, and 226 of the Constitution of India, candidates who possess only the minimum qualifications cannot claim admission as a matter of right.
Those with higher qualifications have priority in admission.
