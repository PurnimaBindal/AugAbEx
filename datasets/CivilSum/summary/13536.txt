Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Disability pension - Petitioner was discharged from the Army on completion of his period of engagement on 7.7.1969 and received a disability pension assessed at 30% after being examined by a Release Medical Board - Upon re-examination by the Survey Medical Board on 951988, his disability was assessed at 60%, but the CCDA (Pensions) only authorized the disability pension at the old rate of 30% - The petitioner argues that the CCDA's action was arbitrary and that the Re-Survey Medical Board's recommendations must be respected until a new Medical Board is appointed - The court ordered the allowance of the petition and directed the payment of a disability pension at the rate of 60% from 951988, along with Rs. 5000/- as costs.
