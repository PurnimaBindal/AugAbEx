Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Recovery - Natural justice/Opportunity of hearing/Speaking order - Recovery of excess amount paid as DA must follow the principles of natural justice, including the opportunity to be heard and a speaking order.
In this case, the order of recovery was passed without affording any opportunity, and the representation made against it was rejected with a cryptic and non-speaking order.
As a result, the orders are deemed illegal and set aside.
