Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Rule 180 of Army Rules deal with Departmental proceedings and judicial review of Court Martial.
If a delinquent is found guilty of grave and serious charges of misconduct and if Army Rules are strictly followed in the procedure, even dismissal cannot be considered as excessive and disproportionate.
[Para 6]
Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Rule 180 of Army Rules state that if the term of employment of the delinquent expires during the pendency of proceedings, the Court cannot order reinstatement.
