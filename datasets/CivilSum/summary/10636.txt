Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 4 and 22 - Compensation - In the case of loss of three fingers, the Commission has awarded compensation equating to 80% of earning capacity - The age of the applicant is irrelevant to the Commissioner - Compensation is based on index factors outlined in Section 4 of the Act - An individual cannot claim to be physically restricted to only one type of work - The Commissioner is obligated to determine disability at 80% as the law requires, regardless of discretion.
