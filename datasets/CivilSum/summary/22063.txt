Maharshi Dayanand University - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Education - Admission - The first respondent joined MD course in Anaesthesia of appellant University but fell short of the minimum training period requirement.
Despite their application for exemption being turned down, they moved a writ petition and were directed by the HC to be provisionally admitted.
However, at an interim stage, the Court stayed the operation of the order as the first respondent had already joined the course 15 days late and had taken leave of 42 days, making them short of the required training period by 57 days.
It is necessary to interfere to uphold the sanctity of the requirements of the Medical Council of India and the University.
These requirements are laid down to ensure that the full period of training necessary for acquiring the qualifications is completed and it is in the public interest that they are not lightly deviated from.
The University was not obliged to give exemption for the 30 days absence, as the leave it gave contemplated a full training period through repetition.
Therefore, the first respondent fell short of the required training period by at least 42 days and must appear and pass the next examination.
The appeal was allowed.
[Paras 2, 4 and 5]
