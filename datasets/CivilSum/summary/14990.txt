Constitution of India, Article 309 - Bihar Pension Rules, Rule 43(b) - Retiral Benefits - Family pension - Excess payment made to a deceased employee could not be recovered from legal representative - Impugned action of Respondents to adjust alleged excess payment in pension amount, not justified - Petitioner- wife entitled to draw her family pension amount on the basis of last pay drawn by her husband at the time of his death - Petition allowed.
