Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 33(2)(b) and 33-C(2) - Disciplinary proceeding - Dismissal - Dismissed from service - Approval of dismissal order not granted - Writ filed by management - Writ petition closed but not decided on merits - Company under new management - Application of workman under Section 33-C(2), dismissed - Writ by workman - Order of Labour Court rejecting approval of dismissal order is binding on new management - Workman to be treated as if on the company's rolls and entitled to wages from date of dismissal from service.
