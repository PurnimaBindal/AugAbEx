Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Education - Admission - The petitioner's registration to P.S.E.B was initially declined due to a compartment in class X while studying in Dehradun, which supposedly made them ineligible for admission in Class XI of Punjab State - However, it was deemed that schools situated in Dehradun and affiliated to CBSE cannot be treated differently than schools in the State of Punjab and UT - The location of the school was not significant as both sets of schools were affiliated to CBSE - The petitioner's provisional admission to 10+2 was to be made regular.
