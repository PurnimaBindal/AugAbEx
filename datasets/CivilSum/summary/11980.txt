The department's clarification as to the domicile of Chandigarh, issued in a letter dated 28.6.2007, goes against the provisions of the Punjab Panchayati Raj Primary Teachers (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules 2006, Rule 10(1)(b), which were framed under sections 196(2) and 227 of the Punjab Panchayati Raj Act, 1994, and is in stark violation of the same rule.
The guidelines for a 'candidate from Chandigarh' mentioned in the letter are wholly distinct from those mentioned in the rules; however, administrative instructions cannot supersede statutory rules.
Therefore, the directive cannot be enforced and candidates who satisfy the criteria specified in the Rules are qualified for appointment.
