Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 14 - Army Pension Regulations, Regulation 132 - Pension - The petitioner, who was recruited as a Sepoy in 1941 and discharged in 1959 after more than 15 years of service, is entitled to pension under Regulation 132.
The court directed the computation and payment of arrears to the petitioner within four months, with interest at 12% if payment is delayed.
