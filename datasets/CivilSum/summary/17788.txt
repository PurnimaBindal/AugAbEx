According to Punjab Cooperative Societies Act, 1961, Section 3(4) and Punjab State Cooperative Agricultural Service Societies Employees Service Rules, 1986, Rule 20, a petition can be filed under Rule 20 before the Registrar against the resolution of the society for the appointment of Secretary.
However, it is important to note that the Deputy Registrar has no jurisdiction to entertain any petition under Rule 20 and the order passed by him is subject to scrutiny by the Registrar under Section 3(4) of the Act.
The Registrar has the power to set aside the order of the Deputy Registrar and remand the case to him for fresh decision.
