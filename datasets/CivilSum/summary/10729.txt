Constitution of India, Article 311 - Haryana Civil Services (Punishment & Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 7(6) - Natural justice - Enquiry report - The principles of natural justice require that the enquiry report must be supplied to the delinquent as a part of natural justice.
When the enquiry officer and punishing authority are different individuals, the enquiry report must be provided to the delinquent prior to any opinion formation by the punishing authority regarding the correctness of the enquiry officer's findings and proposed punishment.
This is to enable the delinquent to represent against the enquiry officer's findings and convince the punishing authority of any illegality and irregularities committed by the enquiry officer.
However, Rule 7(6) only provides for the supply of the enquiry report along with the final show cause notice, which is in violation of the principles of natural justice.
As per the suggestion of the Supreme Court in Mohd.
Ramzan Khan's case, this rule is required to be amended.
[Paras 4 to 6]
