Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 21 - Medical reimbursement - Dependents are entitled to claim - Claimants must furnish dependent's certificate and show their dependency for medical expenditure - In the case of a retiree and pensioner of Central Govt, the husband cannot be considered dependent upon his wife - Therefore, the wife is not entitled to claim reimbursement of medical expenses on her husband.
