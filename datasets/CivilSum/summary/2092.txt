Municipal Corporation of Delhi Act, 1957, Section 98 - Seniority/Seniority List/Final seniority list - The final seniority list is finalized after objections are considered and is based on the quota-rota and service regulations approved by the Lt.
Governor of Delhi.
It cannot be challenged after many years as this would prevent any seniority list from being finalized and employees would be uncertain about their future.
[Para 17] B. Seniority/Length of service - In the absence of rules or orders, length of service is used as the basis for determining seniority.
