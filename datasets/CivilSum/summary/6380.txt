Article 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution state that there should be equal pay for equal work.
However, Library Assistants/Junior Library Assistants and Librarians have different qualifications, nature of duties and job responsibilities.
Therefore, it is impossible to equate these cadres for the purpose of pay scale.
[Para 3] B Article 226 of the Indian Constitution provides writ jurisdiction for pay scales.
Board of Management is an expert body that can decide on the equality of different cadres/posts by taking into consideration the relevant material, rules, law and job requirements.
Courts should not substitute their own opinion for that of the expert body when exercising jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Indian Constitution.
