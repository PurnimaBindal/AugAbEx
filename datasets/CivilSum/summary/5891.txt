argues that employees of temporary research projects cannot claim absorption and regularisation of their services against proposed projects, and must only compete with other candidates for appointment in new projects.
Additionally, ad hoc employees cannot claim regularisation of their services, but may be given age relaxation for their valuable experience.
Finally, temporary project employees cannot claim equal pay to regular employees, as there is a reasonable classification between the two.
