Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Charge-sheet - Prejudice due to delay - Charge-sheet was issued more than nine years after original default with no satisfactory explanation for delay - F.C.I only gave vague reason of inter-departmental correspondence delaying the finalisation - Petitioner's right to claim joint inspection of stocks is deprived - Possibility of stock pilferage and petitioner may not be able to produce evidence after a lapse of ten years - Proceedings and charge-sheet quashed.
