Guru Nanak Dev University Calendar, Volume II (1991) Ordinances, 10(j), 11 and 13 - Education/Examination/Unfair means - The Unfair Means Committee barred 45 students for 2 years for mass copying, based on the report of a subject expert who found they had mentioned figure "t" instead of "t2" in question No. 1(c) - Students denied the charge, and no incriminating material was found on them - Punishment order was passed under Ordinance 10(j), but since the case did not involve any material indicating help from an external source, the order was quashed - The decision in CWP No. 2450/92 Harjinder Singh & others v. Guru Nanak Dev University etc. was followed.
