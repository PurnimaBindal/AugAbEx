The research wing of the Government was abolished and its employees were integrated into the University under an absorption scheme.
The transferred employees became government servants in the University cadre and their pay was protected under the absorption scheme.
The University is bound by its own scheme.
[Paras 6 & 7]  B The Rajasthan Universities Act, 1974 requires recommendations from the Selection Committee for direct appointments, but not for absorption under the State Government's policy decision to transfer those working in the research wing.
[Paras 8 and 10]  C The petitioner was transferred to the University cadre based on a policy decision formulated by the Government.
The Government cannot act contrary to its own policy and the doctrine of promissory estoppel prevents injustice.
This doctrine is applicable against the state and state bodies as a defence or cause of action, and is not limited to contracts or estoppel.
