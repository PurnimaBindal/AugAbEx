Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 49(1) - Salary - Higher post - While working as Assistant Engineer, the respondent prepared an evaluation report in the absence of the Executive Engineer due to his superannuation.
The respondent is now claiming pay and allowances as applicable to an Executive Engineer for the said period.
However, just performing additional duties is not enough to warrant a higher status and additional pay.
