The appointment of a female principal in a women's college is justified under Articles 14, 15(3), and 16 of the Constitution, and Rules 5 and 8 of the Punjab University Calendar Volume III are not in violation of these articles.
Sex-based classification and discrimination between males and females are permissible, and the state has the power to make special provisions under Article 15(3).
The Court cannot sit in appeal against policy decisions made by the state, and the appointment of a female principal in a women's college is a reasonable classification with a nexus to the object sought to be achieved.
