Constitution of India, Article 16 - Mizoram Police Service Rules, 1986, Rule 25 - Seniority - Promotion - Rule 25(iii) mandates that the seniority of direct recruits and promotees is to be determined in accordance with quota and rotation - Therefore, rotation must be considered based on date of appointment and vacancy under the rules - Seniority was correctly determined based on respective appointment dates - Petitioner cannot claim right to promotion or seniority over those promoted by DPC recommendation - Petitioner can only be considered for promotion and seniority at a later stage of selection.
