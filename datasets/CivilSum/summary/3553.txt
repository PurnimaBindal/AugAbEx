Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Uttar Pradesh Selection Procedure for Direct Recruitment for Group `C' Posts (Outside Purview of UP Public Service Commission) Rules 2002, Rule 2 - Interview - Appointment for the post of Clerk-Cum-Typist - The maximum marks for the interview were fixed, with an emphasis on the candidate's personality.
The selection committee adopted a procedure to award interview marks only after the entire interview process was over.
However, in this case, marks were awarded to 50 candidates merely on presumptions, after a period of 8 years, which rendered the selection list liable to be quashed.
As a remedy, the Board was directed to conduct fresh interviews and award marks to the candidates immediately after their interview.
