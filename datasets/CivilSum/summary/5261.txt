According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution, regularisation must occur.
The petitioner in this case has been employed as a Music Teacher for approximately 15 years in Kendriya Vidyalaya and has all necessary qualifications.
Additionally, he was appointed after being properly selected by the selection committee.
Therefore, he deserves to be regularised in his job and receive compensation from the time of his original appointment.
[Paras 23 to 25]
