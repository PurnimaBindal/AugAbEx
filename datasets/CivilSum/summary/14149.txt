Army Rules, Rule 13A - Army Instructions (SAI) 1/S 1985 as amended by SAI 26/S 1989 - Retirement order - Compensatory chance - A petition filed by an individual ordered to be retired for failing to pass a promotion examination within the prescribed time has been dismissed after seeking a compensatory chance, on the grounds of being actively involved in operational duties before the test.
The petitioner contended that Rule 13A is ultra vires to the extent that the termination order is passed under this rule, and also sought the benefits of communication.
However, it was held that Rule 13A serves to weed out officers who do not meet the prescribed standards under the Army Act and Rules.
Therefore, no interference is required and the claim is untenable.
The benefit of communication cannot be given retrospectively, and hence the petition was dismissed.
[Paras 17, 19 and 21]
