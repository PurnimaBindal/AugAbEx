Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission - Pre-Medical Course - The petitioners, who obtained decent marks, were admitted to Diploma courses while those who secured lesser marks were admitted to MS due to reservation among Scheduled Castes (SC) - The AP Scheduled Castes (Rationalization of Reservations) Act, 2000 was struck down by Supreme Court as violating Article 14 of the Constitution - The Respondents are required to review the allotment made to SC candidates and readjust the seats based on their rank and marks.
