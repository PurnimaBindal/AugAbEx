Constitution of India, Articles 226 - Selection - Appointment - Petitioner was not selected for unregistered post of Junior Resident-III in surgery despite being more meritorious, qualified, and experienced than the selected candidate.
The post has remained vacant due to non-joining of one candidate, and there are only 21 days left to fill it.
Petitioner should not be deprived of the remaining days' benefit, and the respondent is instructed to consider the petitioner's claim for appointment and pass an appropriate order.
[Paras 7 and 8]
