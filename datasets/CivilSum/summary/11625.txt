According to Certified Standing Orders 30(d) and 30(g) in conjunction with Sections 10-A and 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, a worker who fails to report at the company's gate is not entitled to suspension allowance.
However, if the workman wants to claim the allowance, he should invoke Section 10-A of the ID Act rather than Section 33-C(2).
