The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 mention proficiency step up, seniority and ad hoc service.
This is taken into account for determining seniority and qualifying service for the grant of proficiency step up.
In the case of a Medical Officer PCMS-II petitioner, the respondents are directed to fix the petitioner's seniority by giving him the benefit of his ad hoc service.
Furthermore, the petitioner will receive the benefit granted in the Dr. Ashok Kumar Khullar and Dr. Gurdip Kumar Uppal v. State of Punjab case for the grant of proficiency step up.
