Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Regularisation - Equal pay - Petitioners claiming regularisation of service and uniform pay scale/grade - The State Government is responsible for deciding the payment of salary for Engineers based on their evaluation of work - The Petitioners are directed to supply fresh copies of their representations to the State Government along with supplemented representation within three weeks - The State Government will decide the representation of the Petitioners within four months of receiving it - If the State Government finds no force in the representation, then a well-reasoned and speaking order will be given to reject it.
