According to the Constitution of India, Articles 21, 226 and 311, the Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, and the Indian Penal Code, 1860, Sections 494 and 498A, the petitioner was suspended from service for performing a second marriage, during the pendency of a criminal case.
The petitioner filed an OA before the Tribunal which disposed of the same directing the respondent's to consider revocation of suspension order.
However, the respondent's retreated the order without any fresh grounds.
It is held that the application of mind by the competent authority is necessary for passing suspension order, and such orders should not be passed as an administration routine or an automatic order.
In such cases, authorities should consider relevant facts and circumstances, and take into consideration the public interest.
Since neither the need to continue the order nor any reason has been recorded, the petitioner need not be kept under suspension.
Therefore, the petition is allowed accordingly.
