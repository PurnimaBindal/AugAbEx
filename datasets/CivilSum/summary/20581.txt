When interpreting statutes and rules, the court should assume they are constitutionally valid unless evidence is found that violates the Constitution.
When possible, the court should constructively interpret different provisions of Acts or Rules to sustain them instead of simply striking down provisions.
The court should sustain provisions if different provisions of rules can survive by reading down a particular provision to clarify any ambiguities.

For determining seniority in new service, pre-existing length of service must be counted for persons being absorbed in permanent service who were on deputation from different sources.
[Para 6]
If taken literally, Regulation 9(2) may appear to disregard past services rendered by deputationists in their parent departments.
Rather, the total length of service of deputationists must be taken into account in determining their seniority at the Commission.
Regulation 9(2) was upheld by reading it down in accordance with this interpretation.
