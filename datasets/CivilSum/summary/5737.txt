Pepsu Road Transport Corporation (Conditions of Appointment and Service) Regulations, 1981, Regulation 17 - Age of retirement - Physical fitness - The age for superannuation has been set at 58 years in compliance with regulations established under the Act and certified under the Industrial Employment Standing Orders Act, 1946.
The validity of the regulation has been contested in light of the Supreme Court's ruling in British Paints (India) Ltd. v. Its Workmen, but this decision only applies to specific circumstances and does not affect the regulations established for the age of superannuation in service rules and regulations.
Furthermore, there is no evidence that the regulation conflicts with any law or Standing Orders, thus the petition ought to be dismissed.
