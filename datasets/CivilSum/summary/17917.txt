According to Army Pension Regulations and Regulation 173, disability pension is granted to individuals who are discharged due to a disease that arose during their service.
If there was no note of the disease at the time of acceptance for service, it is considered to have arisen in service.
The presumption can only be rebutted with proof that the disease couldn't have been detected during the medical examination.
In the case of the petitioner, who was discharged on medical grounds and fulfilled the 20% disability requirement, he is entitled to receive disability pension along with arrears.
