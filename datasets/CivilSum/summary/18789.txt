Constitution of India, Article 311 - Police Act (5 of 1861) Section 7 - Dismissal - Proportionality of - Police Constable found drunk, charged and dismissed - Petitioner pleads for forgiveness and promises good behavior - Ruled that while petitioner deserves punishment, dismissal is too severe - Decision therefore set aside - Back wages limited to 25% from dismissal to reinstatement.
