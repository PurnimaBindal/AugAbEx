The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation and Selection - Ad hoc service - Selected ad hoc employees cannot claim regularisation if their selection is declared bad and quashed by the court, even if they participated in regular selection - Employees who were not a party to court proceedings against the selection do not have the legal right to continue in service and can challenge the court order in appeal.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Writ proceedings - Parties and Appeals - A person affected but not a party to a writ petition is entitled to file an appeal under Article 136 of the Constitution.
