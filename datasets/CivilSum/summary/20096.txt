Constitution of India, Article 14 - Central Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19 - Selection - Qualifications - Damages - Eligibility - The prescribed qualifications for the post of Deputy Medical Superintendent are 15 years of experience and post graduation in Medicine - The respondent did not meet the experience requirement - The Tribunal's decision to exclude the respondent from being called for an interview was justified - The Tribunal is not authorized to award damages.
