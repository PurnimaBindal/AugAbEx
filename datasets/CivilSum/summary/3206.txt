Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rule 7 - Departmental enquiry - Delays in initiation of enquiry - As long as there is no evidence that the delay has caused the delinquent any prejudice, enquiry proceedings cannot be deemed invalid due to delays.
[Para 10]

Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rule 7 - Natural justice - Ex-parte enquiry - Delinquent's inability to participate in the departmental enquiry due to his financial difficulties as a result of non-payment of subsistence allowance during suspension - Holding ex parte proceedings in such circumstances makes the proceedings void.
[Paras 11 and 12]

Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rule 7 - Speaking order - Punishment order - Punishing Authority in disciplinary proceedings has quasi-judicial powers when passing punishment orders.
The authority has to make a reasoned decision, consistent with natural justice.
A quasi-judicial order must demonstrate the objective application of judgment.
[Paras 14, 15, 18 and 19]

Revisional/appellate authority - Representation/appeal - The Revisional/Appellate Authority is obliged to address the points raised in the appeal/representation and cannot dismiss it with a terse decision.
[Para 20]
