"According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, seniority, promotion and appointment cannot be denied to a petitioner who was selected in direct recruitment and found place in the select list.
If the appointing authority was at fault for delaying the petitioner's appointment, he must still receive the benefit of seniority as per the select list and be entitled to seniority and promotions based on his position in the list.
[Para 3]"
