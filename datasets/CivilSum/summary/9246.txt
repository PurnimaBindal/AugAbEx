Constitution of India, Article 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25(h) - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 11 - Res judicata/Retrenchment - After having been retrenched, a workman claimed re-employment at a time when the post was being filled afresh, but this claim was refused.
The workman challenged the order of refusal, arguing that an earlier order of retrenchment should not operate as res judicata.
The court held that the order of retrenchment was valid and that the earlier order did not prevent the workman from making a fresh claim.
