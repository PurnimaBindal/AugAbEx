Advocates Act 1961, Section 49(1)(c) - Professional conduct - Advocates must adhere to the Rules framed by the Bar Council of India's Preamble - An advocate's actions must be both lawful and moral, and what is acceptable for a non-professional bar member may be unacceptable for an advocate - If an advocate becomes aware that relief for their client can only be obtained through dishonorable means, they should refuse to do so, as it would be an abuse of the court's processes and an interference with the administration of justice - Advocates who engage in procedural maneuvering should be held accountable by requiring them to pay costs.
