According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, for an employee to avail the benefit of past service for pension, they must exercise that option within a year of reappointment.
Failure to exercise the option despite being aware of this rule means the employee cannot claim the benefit of past service.

The authorities responsible for framing administrative policies and instructions must circulate and ensure that all concerned parties are aware of them, in accordance with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
