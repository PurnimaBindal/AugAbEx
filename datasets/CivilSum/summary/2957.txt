According to the All India Council for Technical Education (Grant of approval for starting new technical institutions, introduction of courses or programmes and approval of intake capacity of seats for the courses or programmes) Regulations from 1994, the AICTE has the exclusive power to grant permission to start a new technical institution or to introduce new courses or increase of intake capacity of seats, and state governments cannot refuse a no objection certificate when AICTE has granted approval.
It is not necessary to obtain the approval of the state government, and any provision in the Statute of a University requiring NOC as a precondition for affiliation of such institution/courses would be repugnant to the provisions of the AICTE Act and hence void to that extent.
Also, if the institution has the infrastructural facilities, the University can be directed to grant affiliation.
Note to Clause 4 of Statute 38 of MD University Rohtak is repugnant to the central Act and is therefore also void.
