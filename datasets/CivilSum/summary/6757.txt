According to the Army Act, 1950, Sections 45 and 120 and Army Rules, 1954, Rule 130, a non-commissioned officer who is charged with molesting a soldier's wife must be dismissed from service after recording a summary of the evidence.
The petitioner's actions were deemed unbecoming of a gentleman, and incivility constitutes statutory misconduct.
Therefore, interfering with the order of dismissal from service is not appropriate.
