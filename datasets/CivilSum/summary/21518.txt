Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 30(1), 3(q), 13 and 26(2) - Qualifying service - Addition to qualifying service - Qualifying service is the "service rendered while on duty or otherwise which shall be taken into account for the purpose of pensions and gratuities admissible under the rules" as defined in Rule 3(q) - Anyone appointed to a service or post referred to in sub rule (1) of Rule 30 after 31st March, 1960, regardless of their prior governmental employment status, can receive additional qualifying service under Sub Rule (1) of Rule 30 - To deny the benefit under Rule 30 to those already employed in the government at the time of their appointment to the service or post referred to in Rule 30(1) would result in abnormal outcomes.
