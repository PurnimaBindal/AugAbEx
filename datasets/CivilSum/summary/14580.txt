Constitution of India, Article 16 - Medically unfit - Absorption - Reservation - To rehabilitate employees like drivers who become medically unfit for certain ailments, APSRTC reserves 10% quota in suitable posts after long service on their posts.
Such employees should be treated as one unit for their absorption in suitable posts, and their seniority should be prepared from the date on which they were declared medically unfit.
