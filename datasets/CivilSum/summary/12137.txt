Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Supersession - Criminal proceedings - Supersession during pendency of Criminal proceedings - The petitioner may be entitled to promotion from the date his junior was promoted, superseding him, if acquitted on merits in appeal after conviction by trial Court, where no evidence was produced to support the criminal charge before the court, and adverse entries in the service record were reviewed and assessed as Good, allowing for crossing the Efficiency Bar as well - All reasons for supersession completely eliminated.
