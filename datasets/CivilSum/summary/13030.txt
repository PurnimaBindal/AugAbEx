According to Rule 10 of the Punjab Civil Service (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, disciplinary proceedings state that punishment of stoppage of three increments must adhere to the principles of natural justice.
In a recent case, the petitioner was found guilty of helping an accused in a preliminary enquiry.
The accused was convicted before the impugned order was passed.
Although the rules do not require a regular enquiry for imposing a minor punishment, it mandates the issuance of a charge-sheet and consideration of a reply and discretion to hold an enquiry.
As the competent authority already formed an opinion based on the material on record, the show cause notice was considered merely post-decisional hearing and does not satisfy the rules of natural justice.
Additionally, when the complaint was entertained, the enquiry was held behind the back of the petitioner who did not get the chance to cross-examine the statement relied upon.
[Paras 5,6 and 7]
