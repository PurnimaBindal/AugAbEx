Constitution of India, Article 14 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Regularisation of service - Workmen who were coolies in Electricity Board claimed regularisation - The workmen argued that they worked for 240 days in a year but were not regularised as per office order dated 13.11.87 - The appellant claimed that the office order did not apply to that specific region - Explaining that the said office order giving benefits to a specific region is discriminatory, the respondents are entitled to regularisation of service.
