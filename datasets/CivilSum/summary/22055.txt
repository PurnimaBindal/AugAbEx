Constitution of India, Article 14 - Selection - Viva Voce marks - Interview - Service experts must determine criteria for viva-voce marks in selection posts based on job requirements, personality, and academic/professional requirements - Court cannot specify how many marks should be allocated for viva-voce test in recruitment for any particular public post - Criteria of selection for Chief Personnel Inspector in Railways fixed by experts with 50% marks for professional ability and no written test - Criteria not arbitrary or illegal - Selection upheld.
