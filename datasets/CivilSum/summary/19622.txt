Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 16 - Punjab Educational Service Class II, School Cadre Rules, 1955, Rules 7(b) and 9(d) - Appointment by transfer - Seniority - Rule 9(d) mandates the determination of seniority of an employee appointed by transfer based on their seniority in the previously held appointment in the cadre of that service.
The rule safeguards an employee's seniority who has been appointed by transfer under Rule 7(b) of the Rules.
