Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 and Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 11 deal with Second reference and Res judicata.
The Tribunal has earlier held termination as illegal and directed reinstatement of workmen in its earlier reference.
However, no order was passed for back wages and other consequential relief.
It is held that the second reference for consequential relief including back wages arrears etc. is barred by the principle of res judicata.
