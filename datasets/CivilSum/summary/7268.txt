The Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 2, and the Wages Act, 1936, deal with the issue of backwages for the termination of 301 workers in 1980.
The Industrial Tribunal awarded employment with backwages in 1990, but did not address the issue of gainful employment during the intervening period.
Gainful employment is an important factor to consider when awarding backwages.
The matter regarding backwages was remitted back to the Tribunal for further consideration.
