Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical Reimbursement - Petitioner's husband had heart by-pass surgery - Petitioner claims that her husband is a family member and therefore eligible for medical reimbursement - State's only defense is that the husband receives Rs. 250/- per month as pension from Central Government and is not entitled to medical reimbursement - In accordance with the High Court's decision in CWP No. 7477 of 1999, respondents ordered to reimburse husband's medical bill within two months.
