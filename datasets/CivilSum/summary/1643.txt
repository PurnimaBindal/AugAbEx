Constitution of India, articles 14 and 311 - Probationer - Termination during probation - Charges - An employee accused of certain lapses - Services terminated with the charge sheet itself in composite order - Such an action is not allowed by judicial norms and can be nullified by the Court - Such an order cannot be considered as an order simpliciter - The delinquent does not deny serious allegations of mala fide and not being given any opportunity to defend - Order is tainted and cannot be upheld.
