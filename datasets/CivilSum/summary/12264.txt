Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Reserve Police Force Act, 1949, Section 11(1) - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1995, Rule 27 - Disciplinary Proceedings - The petitioner was initially charge-sheeted under Section 11(1) for a minor penalty, but was later imposed with a punishment of compulsory retirement under Rule 27 of the CRPF Rules, which is a major penalty - This was found to be improper as disciplinary authorities are not allowed to convert a charge under Section 11(1) that is a minor penalty to a major penalty after the completion of an enquiry - Such an action would be a violation of the reasonable opportunity for defence and is against the provisions of Article 311 of the Constitution.
