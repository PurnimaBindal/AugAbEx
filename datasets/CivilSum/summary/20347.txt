Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226, along with the Central Secretariat Clerical Services Rules, 1962, Rule 11(3) read with the Third Schedule to the Rules, prescribe that change of cadre for an incumbent must be based on sound reasons.
Moreover, when a person is brought from one cadre to another, they must be treated as lowest in the cadre on that date, but they cannot be made junior to all those who were not even born in the cadre on that date.
The seniority of such incumbent must be determined based on the date they came to such cadre and cannot be disturbed without valid reasons.
