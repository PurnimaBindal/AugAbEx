Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 16(1) - Pay scales - Equal pay for equal work - Cooperative Society employees - Although Cooperative Society employees are not considered state employees, as per Article 12 and 16(1) of the Constitution of India, they are entitled to equal pay for equal work.
However, they must follow the rules of their respective societies for receiving wages instead of State Civil Services Rules.
