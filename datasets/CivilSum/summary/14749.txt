The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Education/Migration - Petitioners took an exam for admission to Medical College, Patiala, and listed preferences for two other colleges as well - Petitioners were allocated to Medical College, Amritsar - Subsequently, some seats became available at Patiala - Many students requested transfer to Patiala - As per the government's instructions, students were to be transferred in order of merit - Respondents, however, transferred students with lower merit - This was a arbitrary and violated Article 14 of the Constitution.
