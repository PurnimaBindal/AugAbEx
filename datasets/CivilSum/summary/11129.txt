Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservation in promotion - As per the Supreme Court's observation in Indra Sawhney v. Union of India, AIR 1993 SC 477 (Mandal Commission Report case), reservation in promotion is not illegal and cannot be stopped immediately - An amendment period of five years has been fixed for rules related to promotion reservation - Reservation in promotion shall continue to be followed until then.
Additionally, under Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16(4) - Roster System - Promotions - Reservation in promotions - A rule that reservation of a single vacancy amounts to 100% reservation is valid only when the appointment is made through direct recruitment - In promotions made according to roster points, this rule does not apply.
