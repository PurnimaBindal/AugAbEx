Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Termination - Petitioner was lawfully selected and appointed - Termination of petitioner's services to appoint respondent in accordance with a Single Judge's direction was deemed disruptive - Although only one post existed, petitioner's selection and appointment was valid, and his services could not be terminated due to wrongful denial of another's appointment - Court directed for petitioner's reinstatement as if his services were never terminated by creating a supernumerary post.
