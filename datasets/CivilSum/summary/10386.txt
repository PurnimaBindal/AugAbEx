According to Articles 226/227 of the Constitution of India, the petitioner had retired from Army service as Subedar on April 30th, 1997 and had been fighting for a promotion to the rank of Subedar Major after retirement.
While the petitioner was at Serial No. 52 in the Seniority list, those promoted to the rank of Subedar Major had been at Serial Nos. 35 to 40, with the latter being enrolled in services on May 28th, 1970, which was after the petitioner's enrollment.
However, those at Serial Nos. 35 to 40 had become senior to the petitioner due to their promotion to the rank of paid acting Naik in 1974/1975.
While the petitioner was also considered for promotion, they were not recommended for it.
The respondents had thus promoted the JCOs at S.Nos.
35 to 40 to the post of Subedar Major and the court found no infirmity in their action.
Consequently, the petition was dismissed.
