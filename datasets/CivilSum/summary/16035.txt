Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Classification - Pay scale - Qualifications - Higher pay scale on the basis of passing Subordinate Accounts Service (SAS) examination - The competent authority has the power to make reasonable classification on various considerations, but requiring SAS examination for higher pay scale is not justified and is illegal.
Even matriculates are eligible to appear in the SAS examination, but passing SAS examination does not make one better qualified than those who have passed B.Com.
examination.
All SAS and non-SAS candidates are part of the same cadre of Accountants.
[Paras 10 to 12]
