The Railway Protection Force Rules, 1958, Rule 44(7) and Article 311 of the Constitution of India cover charges against delinquent of connivance - In order to take action against a delinquent who has connived with another person, the charge of connivance must be proven.
If the charge of connivance is not proven, then any findings on new charges different from those mentioned in the original charge-sheet are not valid.
The delinquent also must have been notified of the new charges or reports on the new charges.
If this did not happen, any show-cause notice and enquiry report are not valid and the delinquent was denied reasonable opportunity.

Rule 44(7) of the Railway Protection Force Rules, 1958 refers to the term "consider", which involves due consideration and application of the material on record in a quasi-judicial manner - In order to act on the charges against a delinquent, the disciplinary authority must act in a quasi-judicial manner and pass a speaking order with reasons based on the material on record.
[Paras 26 and 27] C. When a procedure and manner for doing something is prescribed in statutory rules, the competent authority must act in accordance with the prescribed procedure and manner - If the authority does not follow the prescribed procedure, it violates principles of natural justice.
