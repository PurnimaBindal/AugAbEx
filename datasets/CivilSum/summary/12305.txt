Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Act, 1994, Section 89(8A) - Employees were asked to submit a bond or undertaking not to seek transfers for a period of 10 years as a condition at the time of appointment.
The condition of depositing Rs. 20,000/- with the state treasury in case of transfer request within 10 years was considered unjust, unfair and violative of Articles 14, 16 and 21 of the Constitution of India.
The court ordered that the bonds of undertaking stand discharged and the condition was invalid.
[Paras 12, 13 and 15] B.
The state government cannot unilaterally abandon the condition of a bond of undertaking furnished by employees.
Only employees can withdraw the bond, and not at the request of the state government.
The court held that the act of the state government was arbitrary, unjust, unfair and unreasonable.
