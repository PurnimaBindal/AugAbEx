Respondent Rajiv Atma Ram, Advocate, presents the Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Electrical and Civil) Regulations, 1965, Regulation 16(1)(a) - regarding seniority and the qualifications of AMIE degree in Engineering.
The Board utilized its power under Section 79 of the Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948 to amend the Regulation in 1981, specifying that seniority in the lower cadre would be sustained in the higher cadre and that promotees would not garner seniority solely on the basis of passing the AMIE qualification.
A notification was released in 1984 concerning the decision made by the Board, and in the interim, promotions were conducted based on the amendment.
It should be noted that the unamended regulations did not necessitate such a decision to be notified and that it was enforceable from the date of the decision.
The promotions based on the amendment in the regulations, as per the Board's decision in 1981, were held to be valid, and the amendment was enforceable from the date of the Board's decision before the amended rules came into effect.
