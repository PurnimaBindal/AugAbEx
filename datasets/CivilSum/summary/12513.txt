Section 13(6) of the Haryana and Punjab Agricultural Universities Act 1970 concerns the retirement of members of the Management Board.
The Board's official members are required by sub-section (6) to determine, by lots, which members will retire at the end of each year.
Failure to follow this procedure will result in the decision and all subsequent proceedings being considered invalid and liable to be quashed.
