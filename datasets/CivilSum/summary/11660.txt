Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Retiral benefits - Refusal - The granting of benefits is not a matter of grace or charity on the part of the government.
It is the employee's legally cognizable and judicially enforceable right to receive retiral benefits.
Therefore, it is not proper to deny an employee their retiral benefits despite their repeated representations.
The respondents have been directed to settle the petitioner's claim for retiral benefits.
