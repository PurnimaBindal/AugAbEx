Constitution of India, Articles 29 and 30 - Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Section 8(1) - Appointment - Institution administered by Linguistic Minority - Prescription of Malyalam as additional essential qualification - It is the constitutional right of a linguistic minority to insist on knowledge of their language, and the Delhi Education Act is subject to guarantees of constitutional rights of minority institutions.
Given that the Kerala Education Society is an aided institution subject to regulatory provisions contained in the Delhi School Education Act 1973 and the Rules made thereunder, the Management of the Institution is allowed to prescribe knowledge of Malayalam as an essential qualification for the post of Vice-Principal.
This is due to the fact that the Institution promotes Malayalam language, making it proper and desirable for administrative personnel to have knowledge of the language to better serve their responsibilities.
