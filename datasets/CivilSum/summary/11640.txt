Employees State Insurance Act, 1948 (Central Act No. 34 of 1948),Section 75(1)(g) - Contribution - Ceiling Limit - If an employee's salary is above the ceiling limit at the start of the contribution period, they will not be required to pay any contribution even if their salary decreases during the period - The clarification given by the Deputy Director of ESI was correct and was upheld by the ESI Court - The appeal has been dismissed.
