Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 300-A - Pension - Provident Fund - The petitioner failed to opt for pension scheme before retiring in 1972, despite 6 opportunities to do so.
In 1984, he requested pensionary benefits but was not entitled since he deliberately chose the Provident Fund scheme.
After 24 years, he cannot switch to the pension scheme and is not eligible for any direction of mandamus from authorities.
