The impugned judgment of the Tribunal, which dismissed the appellant's application for pay fixation as time-barred, is against the law, given that the claim to be paid the correct salary computed on the basis of proper pay fixation is a right that lasts throughout the entire tenure of service.
It is considered a continuing wrong against the appellant, giving rise to a recurring cause of action each time an improper salary was paid.
Although the recovery of arrears calculated on the basis of the difference in pay is time-barred, the appellant is entitled to proper pay fixation in accordance with the rules, and if his claim is substantiated, the cessation of a continuing wrong.
If he is entitled to any consequential relief such as promotion, it will also be subject to defences of laches or limitation.
The application cannot be considered time-barred insofar as proper pay fixation on the basis of a recurring cause of action is concerned, and it cannot take into account any other consequential relief beyond pay fixation as of 181978.
