Appointments made without advertising public posts and without considering eligibility and qualifications and not allowing all eligible parties to compete violate the guarantee of equality as defined in the Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16 and 226.
Correcting these appointments by terminating them without any opportunity to the appointees does not violate any vested rights and does not cause prejudice.
Courts cannot protect such appointments in exercise of their writ jurisdiction as they will not compel any authority to perpetuate illegality.
[Paras 5, 6, 9 to 11]
