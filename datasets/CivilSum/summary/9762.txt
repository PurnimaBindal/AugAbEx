The Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 2 and 4 - Assessment of loss of earning capacity - The prescribed percentage of 28% must be applied in the case of an injury falling under Part II of Schedule I - Commissioner cannot assess 100% loss of earning capacity for a scheduled injury.
[Paras 13, 15 and 17]  B. Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 2 and 4 - Definition of total disablement - It must be a disablement that would incapacitate a worker from performing all the tasks he was previously able to perform before the time of the accident.
