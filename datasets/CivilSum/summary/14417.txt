National Emergency (Concessions) Rules, 1965, Rule 4 - Military service benefit - Promotion - If an individual has already received increments and seniority benefits as a result of military service on their initial post, they cannot claim the same benefits on a higher post after promotion, unless it can be proven that a junior colleague was promoted from the seniority list ahead of them.

Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 - Res judicata - If the earlier suits did not involve the framing of issues, hearing of parties on any issue, or decision of claims, and one suit was dismissed as premature while the other was not pursued after employer abandonment, such issues cannot be considered barred by res judicata.
