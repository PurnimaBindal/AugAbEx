Army Pension Regulations, 1961 - Rules of Casualty Pensioners Awards, 1982, Paras 14 and 17 - Discontinuation of Disability Pension - Permissible if PCDA (P) Allahabad accepts disability at less than 20% - However, Supreme Court case of Ex-Sapper Mohinder Singh states that the opinion of Medical Board on issue of disability should be accepted until a fresh examination is done by Medical Board to reach a different conclusion - Therefore, petition is allowed.
