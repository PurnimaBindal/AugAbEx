Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Absorption - Seniority - Seniority of employees who have been regularized shall be counted from the date of regularisation and will be deemed to have entered into service from that date - Appointment made in contravention of recruitment rules is void ab initio.
[Para 4] B Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - West Bengal Death-cum-Retirement Rules - Absorption - Retiral benefits - The petitioner cannot be appointed to the State service unless the post is sanctioned - The petitioner is deemed to have entered service only from the date of his absorption because the State did not sanction the post of Tahasil peon prior to 1984 - The petitioner's service cannot be considered valid as Tahasil peon when he was under a contract of service appointed by the then Tahsildar on commission basis - The period of such service cannot be clubbed together for the purpose of computation of retiral benefits although a minimum of 10 years of regular service is essential for obtaining the said benefit - The question of whether the matter should be heard by a larger Bench considering its importance remains.
