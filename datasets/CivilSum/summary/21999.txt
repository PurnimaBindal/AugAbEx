The appellant, an Ex-Air Force Officer, was appointed as Junior Jailor temporarily and assumed office at a Jail camp.
On closing of the camp, he was again appointed as DPO Grade II.
Then, he was appointed as Deputy Superintendent of Jails and given the benefit of his service as Jailor as qualifying service for the same.
Later, he was promoted as Superintendent of Jails and placed above respondents Nos. 1 to 3 in the seniority list.
The Governor exercised his powers under Rule 47 and issued an order by relaxing Rules for regular appointment of the appellant.
This cured any defects that may have existed earlier in the matter of regular appointment of the appellant as Jailor and he was rightly placed senior to respondents 1 to 3.
[Paras 12 and 14]

The Governor can exercise the powers to relax the Rules conferred on him by Andhra Pradesh State and Subordinate Services Rules with retrospective effect in the interest of justice and equity, if required.
