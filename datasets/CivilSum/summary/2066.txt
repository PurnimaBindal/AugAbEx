According to Constitution of India, Article 311, termination of ad hoc employees at any time is in the nature of service as they have no entitlement to the post and cannot claim for the illegal orders of termination to continue in service.

As per the Constitution of India, Article 311, the appointment letter specifies the tenure of service, and the service can be terminated upon completion of that period.
[Para 11] C. According to Constitution of India, Article 311, termination of services is the discretion of the State Govt.
It is not a ground for termination that a post exists or some work is available.
Termination of a temporary person appointed on an ad hoc basis, as per the terms of the appointment, is valid.
