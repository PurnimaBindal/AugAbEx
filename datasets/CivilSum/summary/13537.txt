Constitution of India, Articles 300-A and 226 - Delay and laches/Pension - The employer's interest remains protected even if the retired employee delays claiming pension - The employee's right to claim the money due to him does not expire over time - In matters related to pension, delay cannot be used as an excuse.
[Para 5]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 300-A - Pension - Work charge service - Service on work charge basis followed by regular service should be taken into account for qualifying service - A rule that excluded such service was deemed invalid in the 1988 case of (2) PLR 223 (FB).
