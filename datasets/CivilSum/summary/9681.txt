The workman was terminated from service due to absence from duty, embezzlement and refusal to participate in an enquiry.
He was absent from duty from 1.9.93 till the time of termination on 2395.
Despite being duly informed, the workman failed to participate in the enquiry and did not submit a medical certificate from CMO to support his explanation for absence from duty.
In addition to this, he was also charged with embezzlement of 39 bags of urea, but he claimed it was a clerical mistake and deposited the amount equal to embezzled money.
However, the court held that the temporary embezzlement was equally serious and admitted, so no further enquiry was needed and his services were rightly terminated.
