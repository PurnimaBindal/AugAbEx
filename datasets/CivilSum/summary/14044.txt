Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions Act of 1989, Sections 2(q), 18 - Provisions - Applicability - The argument that Section 18 of the Act will not apply to the college since it is not affiliated with a university or any state government authority is not valid - The college is affiliated with CBSE Rajasthan and is a recognized institution, hence it is governed by the mandate of Section 18 of the Act.
[Para 7] B Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions Act of 1989, Sections 2(q), 18 - Termination - Validity - An order to terminate by a unanimous opinion of the Management Committee is not valid under Section 18, which mandates obtaining written consent from the Director of Education - If consent is not obtained, the order of termination cannot sustain under the circumstances.
