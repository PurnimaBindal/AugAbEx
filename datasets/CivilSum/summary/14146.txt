Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Eligibility - Age bar - Post of sub-inspector - A petitioner who knowingly applied for a post despite being over the age limit cannot contest his rejection solely based on that ground; the petitioner cannot claim relaxation of age for OBC candidates either as it was not in effect at the time of application.
[Para 5]

Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Eligibility - Fixation of cut-off date for determining age of candidates - The process of selection for posts take considerable amount of time due to a large pool of candidates; setting the cut-off date as the 1st of January of the following year or year when the ad was issued is not against the law.
