Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Termination of ad hoc employee - Absence from duty - Criminal proceedings - Acquittal in criminal proceedings - Accused employee can be reinstated after an independent inquiry into their suitability, even if acquitted - Ad hoc employees still have some rights - Automatic termination due to absence is illegal - Absence is not necessarily misconduct if a valid explanation is provided - Enquiry into the cause of absence is required, according to principles of natural justice - If the employee can justify their absence and the authority agrees to reinstate them, back wages may not be awarded - Direction given to conduct an enquiry.
