Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate Appointment - Termination of Services - Post of Craft Teacher - The condition requiring a widow to complete a full time diploma course of two years immediately upon her appointment is considered harsh and cannot be fulfilled.
It would cause her to take leave without pay which goes against the purpose of compassionate appointment.
In addition, a diploma course in cutting and tailoring for two years is not available.
The petitioner is not at fault for completing an available one-year diploma for which she was granted permission.
The termination of the petitioner has been overturned and the court has directed for her reinstatement.
[Paras 10, 11, 13 and 14]
