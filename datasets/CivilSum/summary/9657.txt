Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Disciplinary proceedings - Minor penalty of recovery - Challenge as to - The petitioner claimed that the appellate authority did not consider various aspects raised in appeal.
However, it was held that the petitioner had admitted acceptance of stack No. 2-D/1, and the penalty of recovery was imposed based on storage loss in said stack after considering his stand.
The disciplinary and appellate authority had also considered all arguments and passed a speaking order, without violating the principles of natural justice.
