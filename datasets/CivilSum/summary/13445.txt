Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - Challenging transfer order - The government argues against the challenge citing a pending departmental enquiry and the risk of witness tampering or record tampering - The tribunal has halted the transfer - It is determined that a transfer order may be invalidated if it is mala fide or in violation of statutory rules, neither of which exists in this case - Even without any allegations of mala fide against a specific officer, the transfer is not unreasonable and cannot be invalidated.
