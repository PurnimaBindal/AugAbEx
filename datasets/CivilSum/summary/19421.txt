According to the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, the Ashram that provides relief to destitute women and widows and is operated on a no profit and no loss basis is not considered a commercial organization.
Trainees who are paid nominal amounts for their work done are not seen as employees, and therefore, the provisions of the Act do not apply to the Ashram since there is no master-servant relationship.
