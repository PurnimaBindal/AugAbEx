In the context of education and admissions, reservation for teachers and their wards in professional courses does not violate Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
They are considered a separate class and the classification is based on intelligible differences that have a rational relationship to the objective of the rule, and therefore the rule is not invalid.
[Paras 13, 18 and 19]
In the case of admissions and merit-based reservations, a candidate who is not entitled to admission under the reservation system must demonstrate that their merit was higher than the last candidate admitted in order to be successful.
[Paras 16 and 19]
In the context of admission petitions that challenge the validity of admissions, it is necessary to implead candidates whose admission will be impacted if the petition is accepted.
Failure to do so will result in the dismissal of the petition.
[Para 17]
