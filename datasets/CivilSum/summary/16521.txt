Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Electrical) Regulations, 1965, Regulation 2(i)(a) - Promotion to the position of Asstt.
Engineer (Electrical) from Draftsman - Eligibility requirements - Passing of Departmental Accounts Examination is necessary, and the word `shall' must be interpreted as such - Relaxation of qualification is a privilege and cannot be demanded as a right.
