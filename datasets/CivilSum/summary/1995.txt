Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume 2, Rule 9.3 - Writ - Delay in pension disbursement - Interest on delayed payment is a part of the right to pension itself - Writ petition can be maintained for the claim of interest on delayed payments and other retiral benefits under Civil Service Rules.
[Paras 16 and 17]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pension - Disbursement - Delay in payment must be compensated - Generally, interest of 12% is awarded for the delay - Higher rate of interest up to 18% is awarded if circumstances warrant - Usually, pension disbursement should not take longer than two months from the date of retirement.
[Para 9]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pension - Delay in disbursement - Those responsible for the delay should be made personally liable for payment of interest to retiring employee - The working of erring officials is often indifferent despite the safeguards provided in the rules - Government directed to fix responsibility of officials in each case and recover the amount from them.
[Paras 18 and 19]
