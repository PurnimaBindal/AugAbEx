The Haryana Transport Department (State Service Class-III) Rules, 1983 - Qualifications - Pay Scale - A General Manager requisition shows that Diesel Pump Attendants have a higher education requirement than Battery Attendants, but no government decision has been made to prescribe such qualifications.
The rule requires only Matric for DPAs and Matric with ITI for Battery Attendants.
As a result, Diesel Pump Attendants are not entitled to a higher pay scale than Battery Attendants.
[Paras 11 and 12] B.
Equal pay for equal work - The similarity of the duties and responsibilities of the two different posts cannot be determined based on just a statement.
If the qualifications required for the two posts are the same, the government is unjustified in not providing both with similar pay scales.
The government directed to consider the grant of equal pay scales to ITI certificate holder Diesel Pump Attendants in the revised pay scales as given to Battery Attendants in Haryana Roadways.
