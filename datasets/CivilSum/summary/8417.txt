Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - Additional seats - Approval of - BCA and BBA courses - Instititutes were not permitted to increase the number of seats before 8299 - The mere fact that the matter was being considered did not grant the institute the right to assume permission had been given and grant admission against additional seats on 171298 - The permission granted by the Vice-Chancellor for admission to additional students for the Ist semester was not justified as the 1998 batch had already taken their examination by then - The institute was instructed to refund any fees charged from additional students - The university will have final say on the Institute's application for sanction of additional seats.
