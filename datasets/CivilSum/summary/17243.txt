Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Appointment - Doctrine of equality - Petitioner who lacked a necessary qualification for the position of Sanskrit teacher, and did not receive appointment - Although the petitioner disputed that similarly-situated candidates secured appointment, the petitioner was deemed ineligible for the position - The doctrine of equality enshrined in Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India cannot be used to force public authorities to make illegal appointments on the basis of precedent.
