According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, in order to be eligible for promotion, the appellant who was working in LT Grade as Hindi Pandit needed to possess the prescribed qualifications for the post of Hindi Pandit in the college which was upgraded from High School.
The prescribed qualifications required an MA in Hindi and a BA degree in Sanskrit, which the appellant did not possess, despite having a PhD in Hindi.
As a result, the appellant was deemed ineligible for the post of Hindi Pandit in the college and did not have entitlement to claim promotion against the said post since it was reserved for Scheduled Castes.
