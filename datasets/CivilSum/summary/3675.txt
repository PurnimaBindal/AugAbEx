Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Merger of establishments and cadres of employees of both - Seniority and promotion - Vayudoot employees absorbed into Indian Airlines with protection of basic pay - Claim by Vayudoot employees for seniority and promotion with Indian Airlines declined in absence of rules - Indian Airlines subsequently merges cadres and places Vayudoot employees at bottom of seniority of IA employees - Vayudoot employees challenge the merger, claiming seniority and continuation of service based on length of Vayudoot service - Court rules that merger is administrative function of employer and not jurisdiction of the court - Equivalence of ranks and status must be considered when merging different types of posts by examining relevant recruitment rules and equation of posts in equivalent categories according to qualifications, nature of duties, length of service and pay - Offer to merge Vayudoot employees on corresponding posts and assign seniority at bottom of IA employees' seniority without determining equivalence and equation of posts as per law is quashed.
[Paras 53, 54, 65, 69, 72 and 74]
