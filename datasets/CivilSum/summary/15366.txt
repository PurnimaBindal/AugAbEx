Arbitration Act, 1940, Section 30(c) states that the award must be informed and the arbitrator must specify his reasons for it.
If an award is passed without disclosing these reasons, it must be sent back to the arbitrator for a reasoned award.
This decision is based on Raipur Development Authority etc. v M/s. Chokhamal Contractors etc., A.I.R. 1990 SC 1426.
