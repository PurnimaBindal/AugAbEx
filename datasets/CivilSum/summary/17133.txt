Administrative Tribunal Act, Sections 20 and 21 - Railway Service (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Part V, Rule 80 - Jurisdiction - Tribunal cannot usually accept an application from an individual who has not already exhausted all available remedies under the relevant service rules to address their grievance - However, if an appeal is made and not resolved within six months, the Tribunal will have jurisdiction to hear an application if it is made within one year of the expiration of the six-month time frame.
