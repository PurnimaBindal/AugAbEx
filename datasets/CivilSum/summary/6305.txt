Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Probation - To determine if a termination order is a punitive measure, it must be established whether the alleged misconduct was the motive or foundation of the termination.
The petitioner received notices to show cause but with no appointed enquiry officer, chargesheet, evidence recorded, or finding of guilt, any notice cannot establish that the made allegations formed the foundation of the termination order and not the motive.
The record of the petitioner's performance over a year was evaluated to make the decision regarding his suitability based on his work and conduct.
Therefore, the decision to terminate the petitioner's services was not punitive in character.
[Paras 6 and 8] B.
Probation - The term "probation" refers to the period during which the person placed on probation's fitness and suitability are evaluated.
