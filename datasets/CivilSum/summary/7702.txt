Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation - Reinstatement with continuity - Effect of - An employee who is reinstated with continuity is considered to be in continuous service for all purposes - Seniority is to be determined from the date of the employee's original appointment as per the order of reinstatement - If employees appointed after that date have been regularised, the employee reinstated with continuity by court orders is entitled to be regularised from the date of their entitlement according to the State Government's policy - The absence of such employees is not considered willful but a compulsion imposed by the employer.
[Paras 6 & 7]
