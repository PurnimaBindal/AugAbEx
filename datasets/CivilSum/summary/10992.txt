Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary action - A notice required the petitioner to show cause as their caste had been denotified as Scheduled Tribe - The petitioner was hired on the basis of belonging to the Scheduled Tribe - The petitioner argued that they were considered an ST when they received their certificate - The department took a lenient view and did not issue punishment - After a gap of 20 years, a notice was issued to the petitioner - The charge sheet was rejected as the matter could not have been reopened, and it was not new information that was unknown to the respondents for the previous 21 years.
