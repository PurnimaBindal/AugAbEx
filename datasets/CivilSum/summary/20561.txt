Constitution of India, Article 311(2) Second Proviso, Clause(b) - Dismissal from service - Dispensing with the enquiry - Police officer - Threats to the witnesses - The punishing authority must be provided with reasons for dispensing with the holding enquiry for passing an order of dismissal from service of a Govt. servant to his satisfaction, as to why holding of enquiry was not possible - The court can examine such orders on the ground of malafide and extraneous considerations - Officer charged with extortion of money - Reasons recorded on the file establish that it was not feasible to hold enquiry as the delinquent was intimidating and terrorising the witnesses - Order of dismissal upheld; Order of the Tribunal set aside.
