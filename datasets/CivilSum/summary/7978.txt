According to Section 12 of the Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948, the Delhi Vidyut Board should act through its Board, and in line with its Constitution when competing interests of higher cadre employees are involved.
A senior and impartial officer should be nominated to represent the Board before the court.
[Paras 5 and 10]
The Delhi Vidyut Board's petition cannot be rejected for want of cause of action when it raises questions concerning the general practice and policy followed by the Board in filling the post by deputation, ignoring its own officers of the cadre of petitioners contrary to its own Regulations.
[Para 6]
One group of employees questioning the practice being followed by their employer, the Board, and another group having the same interest with the Board/respondent already impleaded do not need to be impleaded as parties.
The petition is against the Board, not the employees/deputation, and therefore their interests will rise and fall with the Board.
[Para 6]
The Delhi Vidyut Board Recruitment and Promotion Regulations permit filling up posts by deputation ordinarily not for more than three years.
The continuation of deputationists without any reason will prejudice the rights of persons in the cadre, but the discretion vested in the Board to fill up the post in relaxation of Rules does not mean in violation to the same.
With the prior approval of UPSC, and by recording sufficient and cogent reasons, only in exceptional cases can the deputation be extended.
[Paras 8 and 10]
When statutory rules are in operation, they have the force of the law and prevail over any administrative instructions.
According to Article 14 and 16 of the Constitution, along with Section 78A of the Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948, administrative instructions cannot override the statutory law.
[Para 12]
An expected change in the law is not a valid reason for ignoring or not following the present law.
According to Article 14 and 16 of the Constitution and Section 78A of the Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948, amendments to the rules should be followed as per the existing law.
