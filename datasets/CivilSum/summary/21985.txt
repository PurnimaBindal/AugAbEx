The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Inter-se seniority determination - Seniority determined by date of entry in service - Appellants became Assistant Engineer Class-II on 111969 - Respondents became Assistant Engineer Class-II before 111969 - Respondents rightly have seniority over the appellants - Period of apprenticeship is not a valid ground for challenging seniority, regardless of whether it was six months or one year.
