Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Enquiry - Merely delaying the issuance of a charge-sheet or concluding disciplinary proceedings - is not enough on its own to cancel the proceedings - However, if the accused official can prove that the delay has harmed them or deprived them of a fair trial - Disciplinary proceedings may be cancelled - Before claiming delay and laches as grounds for challenging the investigation, prejudice must be established.
