Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay scales - Classification of posts and pay scales - Classification of teaching and non-teaching staff - The State Govt. granted qualified and unqualified Physical Instructors the status of Teaching Staff - Once placed in a teaching post, one should receive a teacher's pay-scale, not a non-teaching staff pay-scale - The High Court made an error in categorizing a teacher with a non-teaching pay-scale - The Govt. authorized Graduate Lab Instructors to have teaching status, without financial benefits - This exercise of power is discriminatory.
