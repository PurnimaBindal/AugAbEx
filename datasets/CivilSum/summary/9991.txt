According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the State Government has the authority to make a reference in a dispute.
However, the State Government declined to make a reference without disclosing its reasons.
Meanwhile, the management and the labor court had taken opposite stands, and it would be for the labor court to decide which stand is correct after reviewing the evidence.
The court deemed the order of the Government to decline the reference as invalid and directed it to refer the dispute within two months.
The petition was granted with costs.
