The petitioner was appointed as Chief Project Manager in a Corporation for 2 years on a contractual basis.
Termination without notice or pay in lieu is no longer permissible.
If the parties are in an unequal bargaining position, the instrumentality of State cannot invoke terms of the contract of service to terminate an employee's services in an arbitrary manner.
The termination is illegal, and the petitioner is entitled to continue in service till his services are terminated per the law or his superannuation.
[Paras 29, 30, 32 and 33] B.
The services sought to be terminated is violative of rule of audi alteram partem, as the terms of the contract of service were invoked without complying with the principles of natural justice.
[Para 34] C Termination based on undisclosed reasons is improper, and charges of misconduct must be established via proper enquiry.
The petitioner is entitled to continue in service with all consequential benefits, pay, and allowances.
[Paras 35 and 36] D. The appointment letter had a clause contrary to the assurance given in the advertisement.
Such clauses cannot be invoked as it is violative of Article 14 of the Constitution.
