State Bank of India Employees Provident Fund Rules, Rules 7 and 8(b) - Provident Fund - Age limit - The employer's Provident Fund scheme sets an age limit for becoming a member, which should be based on reasonable grounds.
Fixing the cut off date as 111965 for employees who were confirmed earlier in bank service is arbitrary and irrational.
However, the aim of setting an age limit after 35 years is to ensure that employees can give a minimum of 20 years of service as per Rule 22 of the Pension Fund Rules.

State Bank of India Employees Provident Fund Rules, Rules 7 and 8(b) - Provident Fund - Age Limit - Ex-serviceman - Previously, ex-servicemen were excluded from the Imperial Bank of India Pension and Gratuity Fund Rules, but they are now eligible to be admitted to the SBI Employees Provident Fund as per the circular dated 8.4.1974.
Therefore, the age bar under rule 8A of the rules does not apply to them.
