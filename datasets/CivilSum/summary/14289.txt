Constitution of India, Article 226 - Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995 - Selection - Reservation - Physically handicapped persons not reserved any post - Petitioner did not request Board to make any reservation - Petitioner participated in competition but was not selected - Selected candidates were not included as parties - Appointed selected candidates - Fresh selection cannot be demanded from Board at this point.
