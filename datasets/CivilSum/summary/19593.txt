According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, there must be qualifications and equal pay for equal work.
The Ferro Printer post in the Public Health and Chief Architect Department of Punjab carries out the same functions and responsibilities as in other departments, and therefore, the petitioner should not be denied the same pay scale as other Ferro Printers in different departments of the Punjab Government simply because they are not matriculate.
