Constitution of India, Article 226 - Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1996, Section 2(e) - Contrubution to EPF - Despite the issuance of a demand notice to pay contribution towards PF and Insurance Fund, the company failed to pay - A show cause notice for arrest was then issued to the Executive Chairman of the company - However, the occupier of the factory is a different person - Thus, the show cause notice served to the Executive Chairman treating him as the employer is without jurisdiction.
