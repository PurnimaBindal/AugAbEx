State Bank of India Act, 1955, Section 45 - State Bank of India Officers' Service Rules, Rule 19 - Emergency Commissioned Officers (Reservation of Vacancies) Rules, 1971, Rule 6 - Retirement - Extension of service - The benefit of earlier Army service can only be extended for the limited purpose of seniority, pay and pension as per Rule 6 of the 1971 Rules, and cannot be used for other service conditions including extension of service for retirement.
