Gujarat Judicial Service Recruitment (Amendment) Rules, 1979, Rule 5(1) - Constitution of India, Articles 234 and 309 - Selection/Direct recruitment/Written test - The direct selection procedure involving a written test involves an elimination test as well - The written test is a more objective method and all candidates must undergo it - Holding a written test is not illegal if the Service Rules do not provide a specific method.
[Paras 1 and 10]
