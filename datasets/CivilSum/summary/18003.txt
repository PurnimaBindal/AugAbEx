Panjab University Calendar Vol. II, Regulations 4.1, 42 and 43 - Attendance of 66% Lectures/Education/Examination - A petitioner was not allowed to appear in an examination due to shortage of attendance - In case of shortage, only a 10% condonation is possible - Otherwise, the student has to repeat the course with no further chance of condonation - The petition was dismissed.
[Paras 10, 11 and 12] B. Education/Undertaking - The student is not permitted to join any course or service during the course and agreeing to this condition in the admission form is mandatory - In case of breach, the student may be prohibited from taking the examination.
