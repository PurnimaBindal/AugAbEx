The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 162, and 309 provide instructions for promotions, including eligibility qualifications and sources of promotion.
However, there are no provisions for promotions from Class IV employees to the posts of teachers, despite a Punjab Govt. Circular dated 25.4.1972 that opened avenues of promotion for stagnating Class IV employees.
The instructions use the term 'clerical posts' after the words 'Class III' and further use 'i.e., clerical etc.' which implies that only posts similar to those of clerks are included.
Rules framed by the Legislature under the Constitution cannot be supplemented with the instructions to create a new source of recruitment.
Consequently, the instructions do not intend to cover the posts of teachers to be filled in through promotion from Class III employees.
Judgment in Roop Chand v. State of Punjab, CWP No. 8189 of 1993 and Dharam Chand v. State of Punjab, CWP 4025 of 1988 held that earlier interpretations of the instructions were incorrect.
