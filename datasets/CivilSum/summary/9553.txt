Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Requisite qualification - The petitioner was terminated from their services due to not having the necessary qualifications for the position of Hindi Teacher, which required a BA with Hindi and B.Ed.
with Hindi as a teaching subject.
The petitioner had Honours degree in Sanskrit and Shiksha Shastri (OT Sanskrit) and attempted to stretch their qualifications by analogy, but as they did not meet the requisite qualification, the termination was justified.
