Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab Service of Engineers, Class I (Buildings and Roads Branch) Rules, 1960, Rules 6(b), 8 and 12 - Seniority - Promotional - Eligibility - Merit and suitability are the criteria for promotion to the post of Executive Engineer, and eligibility under Rule 6(b) alone does not entitle Class II Service members to promotion in Class I service.
Promotion must be carried out according to the procedures established by Rule 8, and seniority should be calculated under Rule 12 from the date of selection under Rule 8, not from the point of eligibility for promotion under the Rules.
