Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Reinstatement - Backwages - The Labour Court found that the Ist Respondent's termination was wrongful due to lack of notice, charge sheet or enquiry, and that the provisions of Section 25-F were applicable.
The Labour Court ordered reinstatement with full back wages, a decision which was upheld on appeal without interference.
However, because there was no evidence that the Ist respondent was not gainfully employed during the period between her termination and the raising of the industrial dispute, the award of full back wages was modified to only include the period from the date of raising the dispute.
