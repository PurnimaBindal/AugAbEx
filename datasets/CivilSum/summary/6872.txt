According to the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Schedule II, Item 3, Section 11-A, delinquent employees who are charged with unauthorised absence cannot challenge said charge sheet by claiming that it does not refer to remaining absence without permission.
The charge sheet's purpose is solely to inform the employee of the alleged misconduct in order to initiate disciplinary action.
B.
The treatment of absence as leave without pay does not exonerate delinquent employees of charges of misconduct.
This is exemplified in the Sardar Singh case, where habitual absence was a factor that weighed against the employee in departmental proceedings.
Therefore, habitual absence should be considered as a factor in determining an adverse conclusion against the delinquent employee in departmental proceedings.
