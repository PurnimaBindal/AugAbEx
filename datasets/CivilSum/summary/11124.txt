Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Lien/Absorption - If an employee is absorbed and confirmed in a permanent post in another department, their lien is automatically terminated from the previous post - If the employee declines an opportunity available to them on the post they hold suspended lien against, they will be presumed to have no intention to return and cannot make any grievances against the next person who takes the opportunity - They also cannot claim seniority over such person who gets promoted to the higher post.
