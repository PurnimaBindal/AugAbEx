Constitution of India, Article 226 - Voluntary retirement - Disciplinary proceedings - Petitioner applying for voluntary retirement - Respondents refused to accept the application - Two disciplinary proceedings initiated against the petitioner - Petitioner's legal right to go on voluntary retirement allegedly frustrated - Proceedings initiated held to be mala fide - Petitioner entitled to benefits of voluntary retirement - Impugned charge sheets quashed and respondents directed to grant all benefits to petitioner relating to his voluntary retirement.
