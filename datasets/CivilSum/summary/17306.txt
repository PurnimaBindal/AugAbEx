Under the Workmen's Compensation Act, Sections 3 and 4A, a workman who is injured on the job is entitled to compensation.
Here, a driver who sustained multiple injuries while working was awarded compensation against the insurer.
The appellant argued that the employer, not the insurer, was responsible for paying the penalty, and the court found in favor of the appellant.
As per the 1997(3) PLR 606 case cited in paragraphs 4 and 5, the award has been modified.
