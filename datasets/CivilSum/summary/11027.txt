The Government has an obligation to pay pension according to the Constitution of India, Articles 300-A and 226.
It would be unjust for the Government to take advantage of its own delay, even if a person caused delay due to ignorance or other reasons, and only interest can be denied on arrears.
[Para 6]
Regulation 179 of the Army Pension Regulations, 1961 compensates a person who has retired after completing his/her term but has a disease or injury resulting in 20% or more disability due to military service.
However, the authority has the discretion to deny compensation if the issue was not constitutional and not due to Army service.
The court should not substitute its opinion unless the authority's discretion was arbitrary and unfair, considering each case on its own facts.
