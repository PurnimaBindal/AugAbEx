Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Educational qualifications - Recognition and equivalence of inter-State degrees and diplomas - Evaluation of equivalence is the responsibility of each State and its agencies - If one State de-recognizes certain education diplomas or certificates from other States, the related candidates are disqualified, except in cases where the de-recognition is not applied retrospectively - Candidates who had already pursued their studies or obtained employment before the de-recognition date are not affected by it - However, candidates who enroll in a de-recognised institute after the cut-off date are no longer eligible.
[Paras 15 to 21]
