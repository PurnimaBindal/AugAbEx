According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the issue of overstay in government accommodation is addressed by Para 3 and Para 4 of the letter dated 1221992.
Para 4 applies to government servants who become Members of UPSC and have not availed benefits of retention of accommodation on superannuation from the government.
Since the petitioner is entitled to occupy government accommodation even after becoming a Member of UPSC like a regular government officer, the period of overstay is covered by Para 4 and respondents cannot charge damage rent.
