According to Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, and Sections 10 and 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, a workman appointed as part-time employee and asked to work for eight hours cannot be deprived of protection under Section 25-F of the Act, even if their services were orally terminated.
The Labour Court concluded that the workman was a full-time employee and the failure to comply with Section 25-F rendered the retrenchment order invalid, meaning the workman continued to be on service.
