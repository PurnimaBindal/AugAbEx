The State Bank of India Act, 1955, Section 43(1), and the State Bank of India Officers (Determination of Terms and Conditions of Service) Order, 1979, Paragraphs 6, 7 and 8, were examined with reference to the Constitution of India's Article 14.
The promotion of officers Grade I confirmed on or before 31121972 in Middle Management Grade Scale II and others in Junior Management Grade Scale I following the restructuring of the management was not discriminatory and did not violate Article 14.
Moreover, the restructuring of the management was a part of cadre adjustment process for minimising the number of cadres, and the merging of different pre-existing Grades would not result in any demotion or unequal treatment.
