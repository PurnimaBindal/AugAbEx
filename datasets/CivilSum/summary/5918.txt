[Para 2] B A writ petition under Article 226 of the Constitution of India is maintainable for any arbitrary or discriminatory unilateral change in the conditions of service of the staff employed in the family planning centers run by various organizations under the Scheme of the Government of India.
These centers are fully reimbursed by the Government for pay and allowances to their staff employees and are therefore covered within the meaning of 'State' or an 'Authority under the State'.
Their appointments and service conditions are governed by the policies and authority of the Government.
Various authorities of the Supreme Court have laid down the tests to satisfy the definition of a State Authority.
[Paras 5 to 11] B Petitioners who perform similar duties as the staff at other family planning centers, whose terms and conditions were equated with other government employees, resigned from their previous employment on the assurance of equal emoluments.
Any unilateral change in their service conditions would be an infringement of the principle of equal pay for equal work as well as the law of promissory estoppel, even if the service conditions have not been settled by way of a contract.
