Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Dismissal - Domestic enquiry - Natural Justice - In the first enquiry, Respondent No. 1 was denied the chance to lead his own evidence, and in the second enquiry, evidence of a witness was recorded behind his back and he was asked to give his statement first.
The Tribunal's decision to deny permission was upheld, stating that the inquiries were not fair and proper.
