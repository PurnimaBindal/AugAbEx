Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 166 - Project employees may be classified on the basis of places of posting, and those posted within a project area may receive special incentives like project allowance and basic amenities, such as free quarters, electricity, and water supply.
Those posted outside the area of the project may not claim the same benefits on the ground of discrimination, and employees posted within the area of the project may become a distinct class.
[Para 5]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 166 - The Minister of the Department is empowered under the Rules of business to issue instructions and withdraw special benefits, and the withdrawal of special benefits is not without authority.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 166 - Instructions made applicable only from the date of issuance, not with retrospective effect, and are not invalid.
Any defect in the instructions is cured.
