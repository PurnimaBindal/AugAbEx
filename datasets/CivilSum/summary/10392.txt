Constitution of India Article 14, 16 and 226 - Adverse remarks - In the plaintiff-respondent's annual ACR for the year 1993-94, adverse remarks were recorded without any basis and were subjective and biased against him, violating instructions - The plaintiff's entire service record was affected by this and he was labeled a burden to the department who needed to be weeded out - The contradiction in the observations between the plaintiff's previous "good" or "very good" records from 1982-93 and the adverse remarks is problematic - Such severe remarks should have some supporting evidence or be based on a specific event - The state's appeal was dismissed.
