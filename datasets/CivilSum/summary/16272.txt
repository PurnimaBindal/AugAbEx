The Punjab Trust Services (Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1978, Rule 5.2 - Quota Rule - Petitioner promoted in promotion quota on regular basis should not be reverted without being provided with an opportunity to comply with the principles of natural justice.
Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Rules provide for 50% quota to fill posts, each from direct recruits and promotees.
