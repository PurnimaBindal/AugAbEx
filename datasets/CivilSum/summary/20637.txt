Disciplinary proceedings after an employee reaches his age of superannuation can be continued if there are statutory rules.
A rule that enables the continuation of a disciplinary proceedings despite an officer's superannuation must be a statutory rule, and a rule applicable to disciplinary proceedings.
[Para 22]

According to the UCO Bank Officer' Employees' (Discipline and Appeal), Regulations, 1976, disciplinary proceedings cannot be initiated unless and until a charge sheet is drawn up.
[Para 21]

(i) Disciplinary proceedings cannot ordinarily continue after an employee reaches superannuation.
(ii) A statutory rule is needed to continue disciplinary proceedings after superannuation.
(iii) The rule must apply to disciplinary proceedings.
[Para 22]

Imposition of punishment in disciplinary proceedings can only be initiated under the UCO Bank Officer' Employees' (Discipline and Appeal), Regulations, 1976, and not the 1979 Regulations.
