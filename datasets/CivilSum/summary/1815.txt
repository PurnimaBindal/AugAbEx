Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Delay and laches - Promotion - The Court cannot interfere with the promotion of a co-employee after 15 years, as it would be a belated stage and a sound exercise of discretion for the Courts to refuse to exercise their extraordinary powers under Article 226.
Persons who do not approach the Court expeditiously for relief and who stand by and allow things to happen cannot put forward stale claims and try to unsettle settled matters.
