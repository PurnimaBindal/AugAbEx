Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Promotion - The Departmental Promotion Committee promoted the petitioner as Senior Foreman after deliberation - However, promotion order was withheld by the investigating authority, CBI, until the conclusion of their investigation - When promotion orders were finally issued, neither crime was registered by CBI police nor any charge memo was issued - The respondent was thus directed to promote the petitioner without any hindrance.
