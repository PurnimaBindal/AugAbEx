State Bank of India Act, 1955, Sections 43, 49 and 50 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - The Central Government, through Section 49 of the Act, is authorized to enact Rules to apply the Act - The Central Board can make Regulations - The service Rules of the Bank do not provide for military service benefit - The employer has the power to determine service terms - Only Rules, Regulations or Executive orders confer benefits on employees - The petitioners, who are not entitled to military service benefits, cannot receive mandamus without the benefit-granting policy - As the Rules framed by the Central government are for its own Civil Services' employees, Bank employees are not governed by these rules.
[Paras 9 to 12] B Released Emergency Commissioned Officers and Short Service Commissioned Officers (Reservation of Vacancies) Rules, 1971 - The Rules framed by the Central government were created for Civil posts - Bank employees are not the Central Civil Services employees - The Rules are not applicable to Bank employees unless the Bank adopts them for the purpose.
