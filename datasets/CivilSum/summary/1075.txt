Constitution of India, Article 226 - Award - Judicial discipline - Judges at a tribunal cannot ignore or rule against judgments of the Supreme Court or High Court that are cited.
Such judgments are binding on all judicial and quasi-judicial authorities subordinate to it.
Going against these judgments would violate judicial discipline and could even be considered contempt of court.
