Constitution of India, Article 226 - Calcutta and Suburban Police (Subordinate Ranks Recruitment, Conditions of Service and Discipline) Rules, 1962, Schedule II - West Bengal Government Servants (Death-cum-Retirement Benefit) Rules, 1971, Rule 10 - Departmental proceedings/Superannuation/Retiral benefits - There is no provision for proceedings and charge-sheet after retirement except in case of recovery of loss caused to Government.
With that being said, the proceedings are nullified and the petitioner's retiral benefits are to be released immediately.
