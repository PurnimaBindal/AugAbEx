According to Section 25B(ii)(iii) of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, the labor court determined that the workman had served the petitioner-management for over 240 days.
The contention that the termination was due to the completion of specific work was rejected and there was no evidence of abandonment of service.
Therefore, the impugned award ordering the reinstatement of service with full back wages is valid and there is no ground for interference.
