The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, Seniority, raises an issue of impleading all the parties that may lose their seniority if the writ petition were allowed.
The matter must be reconsidered in light of determining the respective rights of seniority in their presence.
[Paras 17 to 20]
Seniority inter se, though not a fundamental right, has civil consequences as a civil right and requires determination in the presence of those affected.
[Para 17]
