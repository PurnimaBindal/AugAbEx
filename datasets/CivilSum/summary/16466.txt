The Constitution of India, Articles 16, 14 and 39(d) - Disparity in pay scale - An unjustified and irrational classification based on source of recruitment - Violates Article 14 in conjunction with Article 39(d) of the Constitution of India - Both petitioner and respondent employees, who work in the same cadre and perform similar duties with the same level of skill, effort, and responsibility, should receive the same pay scale.
