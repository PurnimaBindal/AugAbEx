Punjab Educational Service (College Cadre) (Class I) Rules, 1976, Rule 10(2) - Promotion - Eligibility based on seniority-cum-merit or merit-cum-seniority - If seniority is specified for promotion, even a slightly more meritorious junior cannot skip promotion - The post of Principal is not among the scheduled positions under Rule 2-A of the 1962 Rules -  Thus, the notification issued by the Punjab Govt. on Dec 29, 2000 will apply to the post of Principal-  Unless the person is unfit for the position, promotions to the post of Principal must strictly be made based on seniority.
