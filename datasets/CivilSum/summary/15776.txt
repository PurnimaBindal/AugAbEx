Central Civil Services Pension Rules, 1972, Rule 49(3) - Calculation of length of qualifying service for purpose of pension - A fraction of a year equal to three months and above must be considered a completed one half year and counted as qualifying service.
[Para 19]
Central Civil Services (Leave) Rules, 1972, Rule 26 - If an employee takes earned leave during the first half of the year, it will be adjusted against the 15 days of earned leave kept separately in the account on the first day of the year, and the balance brought forward from the previous year will be kept intact.
Leave cannot be treated as lapsed.
[Para 20]
General Insurance Business (Nationalisation) Act, 1972 Section 17-A - General Insurance (Employees) Pension Scheme, 1995 - Retiral Benefits - Deductions - The petitioner gave an undertaking while taking loans that if any amount remained due at the time of retirement, it could be adjusted towards his retiral benefits.
It was held that the respondents were entitled to adjust the housing loan amount from the petitioner's retiral benefits.
