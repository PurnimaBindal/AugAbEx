The Haryana Irrigation Department Draftsmen and Tracers Group C Service Rules 1982 Rule 5 allows for reversion by subordinate authority but such orders must be sustainable.
While the Engineer-in-Chief is the appointing authority, the Chief Engineer passed the reversion order.
However, the respondent claims that the Engineer-in-Chief is appointed from out of Chief Engineers and thus, the power may have been delegated.
The court held that the delegation order did not authorize the Chief Engineer to exercise any power of Engineer-in-Chief, and therefore, the reversion order was without jurisdiction as the two posts cannot be considered the same.
[Paras 6, 7]

The Haryana Irrigation Department Draftsmen and Tracers Group C Service Rules 1982 Rule 5 permits reversion of posts declared surplus.
The petitioner argued that direct recruits who exceeded their quota should have been reverted instead of promotees.
However, the court stated that this objection cannot be entertained without the necessary party being impleaded, those who are likely to be affected by the succeeding of the petition.
[Para 5]
