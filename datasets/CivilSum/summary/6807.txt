Constitution of India, Articles 226, 14 and 16 - Regularisation - Ad hoc appointment - Right to claim regularisation of service cannot be solely based on two or three years of work - Regularisation should only be directed when necessary posts and funds are available and employee is required for the work - Conduct and nature of employee should also be taken into consideration by the employer - Single Judge order set aside - Petitioner's case to be considered for regularisation.
