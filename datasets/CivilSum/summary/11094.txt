Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rules 512 and 518 (as applicable to the State of Haryana) - The petitioner, who was declared unfit for the post of driver due to an issue with his eyes, was retired from service under Rule 518 without being considered for alternative employment under Rule 512 - However, the order of retirement has been quashed and the respondents have been directed to consider the petitioner's case under Rule 512 for alternative appointment.
[Paras 2 & 3] B. Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rules 512 and 518 - The petitioner was given an alternative appointment under Rule 512 due to being unfit for the post of driver - He cannot be retired under Rule 518 from the alternative appointment on the grounds of being unfit for the post of driver.
