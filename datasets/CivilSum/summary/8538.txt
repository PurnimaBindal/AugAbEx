The absence of profit motive alone cannot exclude the activity of Government from the definition of industry under Section 2(j) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
Zila Sainik Board is considered to be within this definition.

The duties of a welfare worker are similar to ministerial duties.
As Respondent No.1 is employed by Zila Sainik Board, they are considered a workman under Section 2(s) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
