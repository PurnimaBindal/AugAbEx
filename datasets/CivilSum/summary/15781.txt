Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Suspension - Show cause notice - Simultaneous suspension with show cause notice must be replied within 15 days - Permissible for suspension to take place with show-cause notice - The employer is not required to wait for a response prior to issuing the suspension order - The order cannot be challenged on these grounds unless mala fides are proven - The proceedings must be concluded within one year and if not, the suspension must be revoked.
[Paras 3 and 6]
