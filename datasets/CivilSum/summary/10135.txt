Punjab Forest Subordinate Service (Executive Section) Rules, 1944 - Compassionate appointee - Appointment as Deputy Forest Ranger - Cancellation of - Sustainability - Petitioner was appointed as Forester on compassionate ground and on representation was appointed as Deputy Forest Ranger on which post deceased was working - The respondents cancelled the aforesaid appointment - The petitioner lacks the necessary qualifications for the post of Deputy Forest Ranger according to the 1944 Rules - The instructions allow for the appointment of a candidate only one step below the post held by the deceased - It has been established that the petitioner is not eligible for the post of Deputy Forest Ranger under the circumstances, and his appointment as Forester is also unjustifiable in view of the law laid down by the Apex Court in the case of Umesh Kumar Nagpal - However, since the petitioner has been in his job for a period of 12 years, it would be unfair to declare his employment illegal at this stage.
