Punjab National Bank Officer's Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 20 - Voluntary retirement - Disciplinary proceedings - Charge sheet - Show-cause notice - For officers seeking to voluntarily retire, three months notice must be issued according to the Regulation, and permission from competent authorities is required - This allows the authority to investigate and evaluate whether there is any irregularity committed by such an officer - If any disciplinary action, show-cause notice, or explanation letter is pending against an officer, the request for voluntary retirement will be rejected due to mandatory provisions - Given that officers of the bank deal with public funds and hold a position of trust, if an officer commits an irregularity and subsequently wants to retire, the employer is allowed to reject the request - Even if the irregularity has gone unnoticed, the officer does not have an automatic right to leave the service - In such cases, the Bank Authorities cannot be directed to retire an officer who has requested voluntary retirement.
