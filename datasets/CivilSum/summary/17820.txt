Section 18 of the UP Secondary Education Services Commission and Selection Board Act states that if a vacancy cannot be appointed through the Commission, the position will be filled by temporarily promoting a teacher of similar qualification - The petitioner was promoted to the Lecturer in History position adhering to this rule - However, respondent No. 4 was appointed in the petitioner's place without notifying the petitioner or giving them an opportunity to contest this decision - This is a violation of natural justice, and the order is quashed, with directions given to reconsider respondent No. 4's representation after providing the petitioner with a hearing.
[Para 14]

The constitutional articles of 21 and 41 guarantee the right to livelihood and work - To deny an individual promotion to which they are entitled is to strip them of this right - This is a particularly egregious act given the state's obligation, as indicated by Article 41, to ensure effective provision of work - [Para 13]

The teaching profession is encompassed by the term "any occupation" outlined in Article 19(1)(g) of the Indian Constitution - This right, paired with that granted by Article 21, secures teachers' tenure and protects them from unjust deprivation.
