Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 11 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 33-C(2) - Res judicata - Stoppage of increments - Arrears of wages - The Labour Court did not have the authority to act as an appellate court and disregard the judgment and decree of the civil court.
The petitioner's claim for setting aside the order of stoppage of increments and arrears of salary on that account was dismissed by the Civil Court, and the decree and judgment of the Civil Court were allowed to attain their finality since they were not challenged on appeal.
The petitioner's appeal to the decree was the only available remedy.
The High Court also cannot intervene in its writ jurisdiction in a civil court decree.
As a result, the award of the Labour Court refusing the petitioner's claim was upheld.
