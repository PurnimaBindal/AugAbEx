The suspension of an employee cannot be prolonged on different grounds than those on which the order of suspension was passed as per Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 311.
Prolonging suspension without initiating proceedings for an unreasonable amount of time is a violation of the employee's rights under these Articles.
The court can entertain petitions challenging the actions of the authority in cases of prolonged suspension without any proceedings initiated.
[Paras 10-14, 20]
