According to Punjab Civil Services, Vol.
I, Part I, Rule 3.26(a)(c)(1) & 5.32, a petitioner who was a Superintending Engineer cannot be compulsorily retired at the age of 55 years, but according to said rules, he could have been retired at the age of 58 years.
[Para 13] B.
Rule 3.26(a)(c)(1) was amended on 23888, but since the petitioner was appointed as Superintending Engineer on 31578, the said amendment cannot be made applicable to him retrospectively.
