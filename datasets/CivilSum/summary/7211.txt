Delhi Electricity Reforms Act, 2000, Section 16 - Delhi Electricity Reforms Rules, 2001, Rules 2, 3, 4, 6 - Transfer and taking over of establishment with liabilities - The transferee establishment assumes past and future litigation when assets and liabilities are transferred - Past and future liabilities deriving from any pending litigation concerning employees, retired employees, consumers, or other parties will be included.
