Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punishment - Doctrine of proportionality - Scope of judicial review - The scope of judicial review is not directed against the decision but the decision-making process - The Court Martial authorities have discretion over the choice and quantum of punishment, but punishment should not be so disproportionate as to compel the Court to examine it under Article 226 of the Constitution - In some cases, the doctrine of proportionality may be considered under the scope of judicial review.
