The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Suspension - Criminal proceedings - The suspension of a government servant cannot be utilized as punishment for a criminal case against them, especially if no departmental inquiry has been conducted and the trial is still at an initial stage where the charges have not even been framed yet.
The discretionary power of suspension is to facilitate fair investigation or inquiry and should not be allowed to harass or punish a government servant before their guilt has been established.
Therefore, the suspension must be revoked.
[Paras 7 to 9]
