According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, universities have the power to include or exclude any game in the 'List of Sports Disciplines', and sportsmen cannot demand that a game be included or that once included, it cannot be excluded.
The exclusion of Shooting from the list by the universities and Punjab Engineering College adopting the same policy is valid, meaning that a petitioner who is a sportsman in Shooting has no basis for claiming admission under the sportsmen quota.
