Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - HPCL Employees Superannuation Benefit Fund Scheme Rules, Rule 8(b) - Appointment - Discharge from service - "Medical Fitness" - After the petitioner's father was discharged due to total disablement while in service, the petitioner was considered for employment in the Corporation at the entry level vacancy.
However, after a medical examination, the petitioner was not found to be fully fit and the Corporation expressed its inability to provide employment.
Rule 8(b) cannot be utilized to direct the Corporation to provide employment under Rule 8(a).
[Paras 6 to 9]
