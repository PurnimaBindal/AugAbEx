Rule 7 of J and K Public Service Commission (Conduct of Examination) Rules, 1973 states that a candidate must fulfill all the eligibility conditions for admission to the preliminary exam on the last fixed date.
The petitioner did not fulfill the eligibility criteria as he had passed his BA exam after the last date fixed.
Therefore, he cannot be considered as eligible for the exam.
The intention of the legislation was that the candidate must have eligibility until the last date of applications.
Even if the petitioner was allowed to take the preliminary examination, he has no right to it.
[Paras 7, 11 and 12]
