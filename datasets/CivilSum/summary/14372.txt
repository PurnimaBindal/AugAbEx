According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, selection must be fair, with candidates being selected based on written tests, interviews and higher qualifications.
A private, independent agency conducted the written test, while candidates were given a fair chance during interviews to demonstrate their general knowledge, adaptability and aptitude.
Selection was strictly based on merit, and even if marks were assigned according to the petitioner's suggestion, selected candidates still came out on top, disproving the petitioner's allegations of bias.
[Paras 6 and 8]
Candidates cannot challenge the constitution of the selection committee after the selection process, regardless of the outcome.
[Para 8]
In order to maintain fairness during the selection process, the selection committee was constituted only one day prior to the interview.
This limited the scope of canvassing and prevented undue influence from the various candidates, who were wards of department employees.
