According to Section 16(1) of the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, the Central Administrative Tribunal would have jurisdiction over a dispute concerning a claim to a civil post under the casual labourers (Grant of Temporary Status and Regularisation) Scheme of the Department of Telecommunications, 1989.
The scheme outlines the recruitment process for casual labourers to the Group D cadre in the Department.
This information is stated in Paragraphs 5 and 6.
