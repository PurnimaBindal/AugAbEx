The writ petition sought to finalize the seniority of members of Punjab Service of Engineers, Class II under Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226/227 and Punjab Service of Engineers, Class II, P.W.D.
B&R Branch Rules, 1965, Rules 6, 8 and 9.
The seniority had to be fixed after preparing a roster under the provisions of these rules, and a separate seniority list had to be made.
The Court directed the Govt to prepare a seniority list of members of Punjab Service of Engineers, Class II in accordance with the roster.
Moreover, anyone aggrieved of fixation of their seniority has a right to agitate either before Govt. or before this Court in accordance with laws.
