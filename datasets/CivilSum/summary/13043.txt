Constitution of India, Article 16 - Premature termination of deputation tenure - A petitioner who had gone on Deputation to DMRC (Delhi Metro Rail Corporation) while working in a Para Military Force seeks the next higher grade applicable in his parent department and the assistance of the Lt.
Governor and political functionaries to obtain a higher pay-scale.
However, his representations were not in compliance with proper channels, and the premature termination of his deputation tenure was deemed not arbitrary, especially since there were no disclosed facts or grounds revealing apprehension of bias or mala fide on the part of the respondent-authority.
[Paras 10 and 11]
