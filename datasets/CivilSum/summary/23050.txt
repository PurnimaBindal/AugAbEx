Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo)(bb) - Retrenchment - Fixed term appointment - Non-renewal of - If the term of a contractual workman is not renewed, it will be considered as retrenchment.
[Paras 9 & 10] B.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo)(bb) - Retrenchment - Completion of 240 days - The workman must prove completion of 240 days and cannot rely solely on an affidavit without supporting material.
The non-production of records by the employer does not result in adverse inference unless the workman discharges the burden of proof and onus shifts to the employer.
[Para 11] C. Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-H - Retrenchment - Abandonment - If the management raises abandonment of job as a defense against illegal retrenchment claim and is willing to reinstate the workman, the proper course is to consider him for appointment when the employer requires it.
