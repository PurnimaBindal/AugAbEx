The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, were invoked in the case of a petitioner who was denied admission to the Bachelor of Pharmacy program because he was not entitled to reservation benefits due to his father's retirement from the army prior to April 1, 1995.
The respondents argued that the eligibility criteria stated in the prospectus could not be contested, and the petitioner was also not eligible because he had not attended any school in Haryana.
Despite having read the prospectus and applied for admission, the petitioner's challenge to the denial of his admission was rejected by the court, resulting in the dismissal of his petition.
