Constitution of India, Articles 226/227, 14 and 16 - Appointment - The government of Haryana advertised 74 posts of Assistant District Attorney and the petitioner was placed on the waiting list.
All 74 candidates recommended by the HPSC joined their duties.
The petitioner argues that 30 posts had fallen vacant after the commencement of the selection process.
The court held that any further appointment beyond the 74 posts would be invalid since it would undermine the advertisement and the very right of the original candidates who would want to compete with subsequent applicants.
The petitioner could be granted employment if any of the recommended candidates do not join.
The petition has been dismissed.
