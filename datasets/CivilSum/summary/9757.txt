FCI Staff Regulations, 1972, Regulations 7(2), 59(2) - Enquiry report - Disciplinary authority - Dissenting view - Sustainability - The disciplinary authority must record tentative reasons for disagreement when it disagrees with the finding of Enquiry Officer on any article of charge, and give the delinquent a hearing when no reasons or opportunity have been given to them - The order is hence not sustainable.
