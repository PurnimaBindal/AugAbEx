Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Civil Services, (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rules 4 (vi) - The employer reserves the right to proceed against an employee on the charge of fraud/embezzlement in departmental proceedings, as well as criminal court proceedings - If departmental proceedings end before criminal proceedings and the punishing authority independently decides that the delinquent deserves to be removed or dismissed from service based on their misconduct, then an order of conviction/acquittal by a criminal court later on the same charges won't nullify that order of removal unless challenged independently - If the punishment order stands unchallenged, the employee cannot seek direction from the court claiming they should be deemed in service for all purposes based on their acquittal - Petition dismissed.
