Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I Part I - Premature retirement - Adverse remarks - Service Record - In order to decide whether to give compulsory premature retirement to an employee, the competent authority may look at their entire service record - The Daya Nand v. State of Haryana 1995(1) SCC 423 case decision reversed the KK Vaid's case decision - The petitioner in this case received 5 good reports during 11 years of service but also had below average performance for three years and average performance for two years - They were repeatedly given adverse remarks and faced punishments such as increment stoppage, censure, and suspension - Considering their entire service record, the authority's decision to give them premature retirement was justified - The judgment of the Single Judge was reversed and the appeal was allowed.
