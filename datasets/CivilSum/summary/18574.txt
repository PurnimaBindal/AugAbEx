Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 162 - Dismissal - Misconduct - The dismissal order was passed by the punishing authority based on the continuous misconduct of the petitioner and considering all relevant circumstances under Rule 162.
The order is legal.

Punjab Police Rule, 1934, Rule 1632 - Order of dismissal - Revision - Competent authority - The Deputy Inspector General of Police passed the order of dismissal and the Inspector General of Police is the competent authority to decide the revision as per Rule 1632.
There was no violation of the rule as the Inspector General of Police is the authority next above the prescribed Appellate Authority.
[Para 17]
Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 162 - Dismissal - Misconduct - Wilful absence from duty - Departmental Proceedings - A retrospective order of dismissal is not given merely because the delinquent was allowed to join during the pendency of the appeal.
If the Appellate Authority approves the order of the punishing authority, the dismissal will relate back to the date of the punishing authority's order.
