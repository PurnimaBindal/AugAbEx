When the Council and the Board do not recognize the vocational course as equivalent to any of the prescribed qualifying examinations, it must be held that the petitioners are ineligible for the course.

It is for each University or institution to decide the question of recognition or equivalence of an examination/course held by any other Board or university with the qualifying examination which constitutes the basis of eligibility.
The Court cannot sit in judgment over the decision of those Bodies in such matters as they are the best judges to decide such questions, considering the courses, the syllabus, the quality of teaching or instruction, and the standard of examination.
