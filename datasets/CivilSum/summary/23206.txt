State Bank of India Officers' Services Rules, 1992, Rules 33(1), 34(3) and 69(2) - Misconduct- Bank employees must adhere to the rules and regulations of the bank, and it is considered misconduct to act beyond one's authority - a charge of granting a loan to the appellant's wife under a scheme meant for educated unemployed was established - the decision to sanction the loan was not honest - the punishment of removal awarded by the Appellate Authority is appropriate given the facts and circumstances.

State Bank of India Officers' Services Rules, 1992, Rule 69(5) - Filing of appeal - The competent authority may, for good reason, extend the specified time for anything required to be done or condone any delay.
[Para 1]

State Bank of India Officers' Services Rules, 1992, Rules 69(2) and 69(5) - Enhancement of punishment - In a case regarding disbursement of a loan to the appellant's wife under a scheme meant for educated unemployed youth, the Appellate Authority considered the submissions made by the appellant and modified the proposed penalty of dismissal to that of removal based on the facts and circumstances of the case.
