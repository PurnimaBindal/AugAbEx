Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Workman - Award holding termination illegal - 40% backwages granted - Challenge regarding award conferment - Workman was called upon to perform his duties, but he failed to comply - Backwages awarded for the same time period too - Decided that tribunal's decision to grant backwages even when the workman did not join duty, is a valid cause for reducing the backwages award from 100% to 40% until the decision - Additionally, 9% interest awarded due to non-compliance of award.
