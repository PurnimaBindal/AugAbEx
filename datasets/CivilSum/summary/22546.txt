The Minimum Wages Act, 1948, Section 3 - Karnataka Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1961 - Commercial establishments dealing in import and export, clearance and forwarding of cargo, travel and tourism, imports, consolidations, and courier services registered as a "commercial establishment" will be covered by the definition of "shops" or "commercial establishment" as commonly understood because they engage in purely commercial activities for profit and function as a retail shop providing services.
[Paras 6, 8 & 25]  B Section 3, 5 and 27 of the Minimum Wages Act, 1948 - Applicability - Even if an employer pays more than the minimum wage, they are still subject to the law; if they ever pay below the minimum wage, they will be liable to legal action - The Act applies to all shops and commercial establishments where unorganized labor is employed or being paid below minimum wage rates.
[Paras 10 & 25]  C Minimum Wages Act, 1948, Sections 2(h) and 4 - Minimum wages - Dearness allowance - Variable Dearness Allowance is a part of minimum wages fixed under Sections 3 and 4 - Basic wages and other dearness allowance constitute minimum wages - If an employer already pays wages higher than the notified minimum wages the Act does not require they pay variable dearness allowance; neither does the competent authority need to bifurcate the cost of items considered when determining minimum wages - A lump sum is determined to provide adequate remuneration to the worker - The scheme or the act does not provide that the rates of minimum wages are to be split up based on the cost of each necessity considered when deciding the minimum wage.
