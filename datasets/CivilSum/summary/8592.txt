In accordance with Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1958, Rules 8, 16 and 18, when a service member is employed in another service through the government's recommendation or invitation, he does not lose his lien in his State Service cadre even if he is confirmed in the other service.
As a result, he can return to his parent department as well as be returned by the borrowing department.
Without the officer's consent, his lien may not be suspended or terminated.
Village Extension Officers of Rural Development Department who were appointed in the Co-operative Department in the service's interest did not have their lien terminated, and the order to return them to the parent department is not illegal.
(Paras 8, 9, 11, 19 and 26)

According to Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1958, Rules 24 and 16, an employee does not automatically confirm on completion of probation unless he confirmed in Rule 24 post.
(Para 13)

The Kerala Municipal Common Service, established under Section 90(2) of the Kerala Municipal Corporation Act, 1961, is a public service as per Kerala State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1958, Rule 8.
(Paras 32 and 33)
