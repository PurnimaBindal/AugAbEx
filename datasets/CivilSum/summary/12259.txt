Constitution of India, Article 226 - Stoppage of increments - Judicial review - Petitioner was suspended during the pendency of disciplinary proceedings and was found guilty.
As punishment, two increments were stopped.
The learned single judge opined that the suspension period of the petitioner should be treated as service for all purposes except for monetary benefit.
However, this decision was challenged as there was no finding that the disciplinary authority acted wrongly.
The court held that without such a finding, interference in exercise of the power of judicial review was unwarranted.
The appeal was allowed accordingly.
[Paras 5 and 6]
