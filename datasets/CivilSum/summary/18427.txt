Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Service Rules - The competent authority can make appointments either by direct recruitment or by promotion in the absence of service rules governing recruitment to the post.
Direct recruitment is made through State constituted agencies, while appointment by promotion falls within the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent authority.
[Para 7] B.
If there is no specific rule requiring the Public Service Commission/SSS Board's approval for promotions, no approval is necessary.
Post-dated approval will rectify irregularity, if any, and relate back to the promotion date.
[Paras 8 and 9] C. If the appointment by promotion is not defective or irregular, it is deemed a regular appointment for seniority and all purposes.
[Para 8] D. If the appointment by promotion is not defective or irregular, after about 10 years, it will not be appropriate for the competent authority to disrupt settled seniority.
[Para 9] E It is established law that when initial appointment is on an ad hoc basis, seniority will count only from the date of regular appointment.
