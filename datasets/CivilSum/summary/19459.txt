Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work - When pay scales of employees within an instrumentality of the State are equal to those of Government employees, pay scale revisions must be granted equally - Differences in duties may permit for distinctions in pay - However, if revised pay scales have been provided to almost all statutory body employees, specific cadres cannot be singled out and denied pay benefits.
