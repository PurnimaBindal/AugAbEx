According to Border Security Force (Seniority, Promotion & Superannuation of Officers) Rules, 1978, Rule 9, there is a distinction between Posts of Commandant and Commandant (Selection Grade).
A Comandant (Selection Grade) is ranked higher than a Commandant and the age of superannuation for both posts is different.
Commandants (Selection Grade) retire at the age of 58.
The petition has been allowed.
