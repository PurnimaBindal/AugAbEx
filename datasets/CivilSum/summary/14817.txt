Army Act (46 of 1950), Section 191 - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 2 - Confidential Report of Junior Commission Officer (JCO) - JCO received adverse remarks about his abrasive dealings with subordinates, but no written notice to improve was provided during the reporting period - Given the JCO's excellent service record, he could have been warned at most - As a result, the adverse remarks were nullified.
