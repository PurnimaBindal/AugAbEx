According to the Constitution of India, Article 227 and the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F, a respondent company is permitted to terminate seasonal employees.
In this particular case, the petitioner-workman was engaged for seasonal work only and had found alternative work elsewhere when the season restarted.
Despite being called back to work in the factory, the petitioner declined to return voluntarily.
Given these conditions, the Labour Court's decision to deny the petitioner reinstatement in the seasonal work of the respondent factory appears entirely reasonable.
