Constitution of India, Article 16 - Central Civil Service (Extra Ordinary Pension) Rules, Rules 6 and 7 - Disability Pension - The respondent was initially recruited and enrolled after being deemed physically and medically fit.
However, they later contracted an illness leading to disability after many years of service.
The appellant- Government failed to prove that the disability was not caused due to service.
The learned single judge was correct in awarding disability pension.
[Paras 8 & 9]
