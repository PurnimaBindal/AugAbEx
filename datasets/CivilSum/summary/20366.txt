Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Postal Manual Vol. IV, Rule 273 - Accountants examination held in 1992 - Petitioner passed the exam but had his candidature cancelled due to his classification as a PA of SBCO - No Rule states that SBCOs are ineligible for the Accountants examination - Postal Assistants and SBCOs allowed to appear and appointed as Accountants even after cadres were reorganized - Appellant's claim was hindered by an erroneous affidavit from an official representative - Appeal granted with costs and respondent directed to give appellant entitled benefits as a qualified accountant in 1992.
