Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 30 as amended through a notification dated 1951982 - Additional service - Qualifying service - The extension of this rule has been given to all scientific and technical staff, and even to those who retired from service after 31360 through a Notification dated 201087.
As a result, if eligible, all scientific and technical staff members are entitled to the benefit of Rule 30.
