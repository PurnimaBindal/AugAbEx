According to Section 26(1-D) of Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961 and Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India, only authorised persons are allowed to file a writ.
The person who filed the writ petition was not authorised on the date of filing, but was later authorised after 1-1/2 years.
The administration is not authorised to allow anyone to file a writ on behalf of the Society.
