MD University Rohtak, Leave Regulations, Clause 7(2) - Absence from duty - Employees called to join duty by a certain date - If employee did not join due to a holiday on the last day of calling, they must join on the next opening day, or face potential consequences after being given opportunity to explain.
When an employee fails to join, the authority's decision to declare the position vacant is not supported.
