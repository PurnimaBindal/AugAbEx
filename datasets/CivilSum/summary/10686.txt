West Bengal Panchayat Act, 1973, Section 224 - Petitioner challenging selection process and selection of respondent for the post of Panchayat Karmee - Selection Committee breached Second proviso to Rule 7(4) by creating two additional heads during the oral interview and allotting zero marks to petitioner, while respondent No. 1 was allotted 15 out of total 15 - Bias of members of Committee proved through its actions - Entire selection process, selection panel, and respondent No. 10's recruitment quashed and set aside - Written exam and interviews ordered de-novo.
