Petitioners claim to be similarly situated as Gopal Sahayaks with regard to training in Dairying and Animal Husbandry but were discriminated against by not being sent for training of Veterinary Pharmacists like Gopal Sahayaks.
The government's action of sending Gopal Sahayaks who could not qualify examination and ignoring petitioners and other qualified persons under the Scheme is challenged.
It is argued that the proper course for the government was to consider persons trained both under the Scheme with regard to training in Dairying and Animal Husbandry and the Gopal Sahayak Yojna and make selections, if necessary by formulating criteria therefor.
While the Gopal Sahayaks have already undergone training of Veterinary Pharmacists for a period of three weeks, it would not be fair to set aside the government's action to send them for further training.
Hence, the court directed the respondents to consider the claim of petitioners and other similarly situated persons trained under the Scheme with regard to training in Dairying and Animal Husbandry along with the Gopal Sahayaks for training of Veterinary Pharmacists.
This action is in line with Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
