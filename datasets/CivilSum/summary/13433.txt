Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Education - Admission - Reservation - The petitioner applied for the Elementary Teachers Training Course as a ward of a teacher.
Respondents Nos. 3 and 4 were selected based on higher marks for the reserved category.
Petitioner filed a representation claiming she had higher marks in the Entrance Examination.
However, the admission notice specified that the category applied for could not be changed later.
Furthermore, the petitioner had indicated in her application that she was married and no longer dependent on her mother, who was a teacher.
Even if she were considered a rural category candidate, she could not change categories.
[Paras 2 and 8]
