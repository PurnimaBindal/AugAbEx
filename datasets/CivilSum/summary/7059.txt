According to the Constitution of India, as well as the Punjab Police Rules of 1934, Rule 12.16(22), no waiting list can be prepared.
Therefore, no appointment can be made from any waiting list, even if it has been prepared in violation of Rule 12.16(22).
Additionally, the mere advertisement of vacancies or selection does not grant any rights to the candidates, unless they can demonstrate that they have been appointed.
