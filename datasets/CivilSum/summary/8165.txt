Constitution of India, Articles 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - The determination and computation of monies/benefit due from employers must be done based on record - In this case, the Labour Court awarded the entire amount claimed by employees without giving the employer a reasonable opportunity to adduce documentary or oral evidence to prove or disprove entitlement of employees to get money due - Consequently, the order of the Labour Court has been set aside and the employer is directed to be given the opportunity to adduce documentary and oral evidence in their possession.
