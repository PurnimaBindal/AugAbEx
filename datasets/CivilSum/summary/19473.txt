Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Age - Employment Exchange - The petitioner's case was not considered by the respondents as his name was not sponsored by the Employment Exchange due to him being overage (over 25 years), despite his name being registered with the Employment Exchange eight years prior.
However, the Employment Exchange has been directed to sponsor his name and the respondents have been directed to consider his case without age being a factor.
Additionally, there is a need to improve the procedure in the Employment Exchange.
[Paras 2 to 4]
