Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Counselling - The petitioner's sole grievance is that his chances of getting admitted into an Engineering College were affected by the counselling process which should have been conducted first in the General Category and then for the reserved category - The petitioner belongs to the Backward Class (B) category - It is not proper to disrupt the ongoing curriculum - The petitioner is directed that all general seats shall be filled first, and all reserved category candidates who scored well and are in the merit list shall be included - Thereafter, reserved category seats shall be filled and the petitioner will be called for counselling.
