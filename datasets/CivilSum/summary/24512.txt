UP Homeopathic Medical Service Rules, 1990, Rule 8 (Proviso) - Preferential qualifications - The addition of a proviso stating "preference will be given to degree holders" does not imply that degree holders will be selected before diploma holders.
Both qualifications will be considered equally, and if all other factors are equal, degree holders will receive preferential consideration.
If a diploma holder is selected over a degree holder based on relevant considerations, this is not unlawful.

Selection - The inclusion of an individual's name in a merit list does not guarantee an indisputable right to appointment.
[Para 17]
Discrimination - Wrong order - Simply because a wrong order goes unchallenged, others are not entitled to claim the same order on the grounds of discrimination.
