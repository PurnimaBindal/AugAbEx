Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - State Bank of Mysore, Officers Service Regulations 1979, Regulations 17(1), 17(2) and 67(g) - Writ jurisdiction - Misconduct - Punishment - The determination of whether an act of omission or commission constitutes mere irregularity or misconduct pursuant to applicable service rules depends on the nature of the act, the entrusted duties of the employee, and the potential harm to the employer - Misappropriation of bank funds with dishonest intent constitutes both an irregularity and misconduct - Only when a punishment appears to be so disproportionately arbitrary or whimsical may a court intervene - Punishment imposed on petitioner upheld, as it does not appear to be disproportionate to the severity of his misconduct.
