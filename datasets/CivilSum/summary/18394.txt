The Rajasthan Govt. deleted the provision of initial recruitment by transfer but the proviso to Rule 37(2) still maintains the provisions for inter-department transfer.
Under the Rajasthan Subordinate Offices Ministerial Service Rules, 1999, a transferred employee's lien remains intact in their parent department, and any condition stipulated by the transferring authority contrary to rules will not operate against the employee.
If an employee is repatriated, they must be taken back by the parent department, but if the borrowing department has been absorbing transferred employees, their experience gained during long stay in that department should be considered for absorption.
