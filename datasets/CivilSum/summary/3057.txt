Rule 16.2 of Punjab Police Rules states that dismissal can only be awarded for the gravest act of misconduct or cumulative effect of continued misconduct proving incorrigibility and complete unfitness for police service.
Punishment must be in proportion to the offence proved, and not every act of absence from duty or overstayal of leave can be considered as an act of gravest misconduct unless it caused a huge loss.
B In the case of a constable who had more than 20 years of service, an order of dismissal was held illegal, arbitrary and unsustainable.
C Punishment must be commensurate to rules, irrespective of specific plea raised by the delinquent.
The authority is bound to take care of rules.
