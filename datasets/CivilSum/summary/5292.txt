Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33C-(2) - Claim for overtime wages - Workmen filed the claim for overtime wages 18 years later, without providing any explanation for the delay - There is no provision to extend the limitation for filing a claim - The absence of a time limit does not mean that claims can be made after years or decades have passed - The claim for overtime wages lacks any oral or documentary evidence to support it and should be deemed completely false and groundless.
