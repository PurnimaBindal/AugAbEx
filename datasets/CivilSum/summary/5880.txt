Constitution of India, Article 311 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(c) - Compulsory retirement - The order of compulsory retirement for employees who have attained the age of 50 years should not be referred to a screening committee immediately, but should instead be done as soon as possible.
The word 'immediately' should not be interpreted rigidly but with reference to 'as soon as possible'.
