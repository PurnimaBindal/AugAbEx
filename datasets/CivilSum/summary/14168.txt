Delhi Water Supply and Sewerage Disposal Undertaking (Control and Appeal) Regulations, 1922, Rules 6 and 8 - Stoppage of increments with cumulative effect - Restoration of increments - The penalties for imposition of stoppage of increments as minor and major penalty are different - If increments are stopped with cumulative effect after inquiry, it is counted as a major penalty - The punishment order not specifically disclosing the duration of the penalty will not alter the nature of the penalty - The term "permanent postponement of the increments" indicates that the penalty is with cumulative effect and cannot be recovered.
