Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Natural justice - Family pension - The wife of a deceased person was granted with family pension in 1979, which was stopped in 1993 without any show cause notice or hearing opportunity being given - An order that violates the principles of natural justice can't be supported - Therefore, the respondents are directed to continue providing the family pension, along with any arrears with 12% interest and liberty to proceed in accordance with the law.
