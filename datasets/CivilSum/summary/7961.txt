Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Labour court - Powers of - The Tribunal has the power to re-examine evidence presented before an enquiry officer and has awarded reinstatement with full back wages as none of the charges against the respondent stood proved and the penalty could not be sustained.
The contention that the Tribunal had no jurisdiction to re-examine evidence was dismissed and the appeal was also dismissed.
