Refund of salary is required if an appointment letter contains a condition that an employee will be liable to refund entire salary upon resignation during probation period.
Such a condition is deemed to be against public policy, public interest and unequal, unfair, unreasonable under the Contract Act, 1872 and Article 14 of the Constitution of India.
Reasoning that the institute incurred expenses to finalize the selection process is not acceptable as normal administrative costs are inherent to any institute's recruitment exercise.
Thus, the respondent-institute is required to refund the deposited amount of the employee's salary.
[Paras 4, 5 and 6] B Detrimental contract terms and service rules violating the principles of distributive justice, parts III and IV of the Constitution, and against public policy and public interest are considered violative of Article 14 of the Constitution.
Refund of salary is required upon resignation during probation if such unconscionable, unfair, and unreasonable terms are present.
