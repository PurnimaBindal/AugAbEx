Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 18(3)(d) - Award of Industrial Tribunal - The award is binding - Principles of natural justice not applicable - Collective bargaining on behalf of all workmen - All existing and new workmen are bound by the terms of the award - No violation of principles of natural justice if union representatives were provided hearing and accepted the award.
