Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Reimbursement - Treatment - Outdoor treatment - Specialised treatment - Chronic disease - The petitioner and his wife required specialised treatment not available in the State due to their disc problem.
They were referred to AIIMS by the Director General, Health Service of the State and incurred expenses on various tests and x-ray laboratory tests.
The authorities cannot deny reimbursement of expenses on tests that are necessary to diagnose the disease and prescribe treatment if they have referred them to AIIMS.
The rules do not make any distinction between indoor and outdoor patients for reimbursement.
Any instructions that deny full reimbursement of medical expenses for outdoor treatment cannot be justified under Articles 14 and 21.
The instructions of the Hy.
Govt. dt. 17.7.1992 to that extent are declared unjustified and the authorities are directed to reimburse.
