The Constitution of India, Articles 32, 14 and 226 - Delay and laches - The principle of delay and laches applies to Article 32 petitioners as well - A pay scale revision was implemented in 1957, and disparity was removed in 1969 - However, petitions were not filed until 1991 under Article 32, requesting the removal of the disparity that occurred in 1957, and claiming arrears of salary - This grievance cannot be resolved after such a prolonged period - The financial burden associated with such a claim must also be considered.
