According to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, if an employee has a break in service and is reinstated by court order without back wages, the termination period must be treated as leave of the kind due.
If the court order is finalized and implemented, the employee must be granted leave of the kind due, thereby treating the employee as still in service.
Once leave is granted, the leave period cannot be disregarded when calculating the length of service for pension eligibility.
It is not permissible to deny an employee pension by ignoring the period of leave of the kind due and reducing their qualifying service below the required threshold.
