According to the Pension Regulations of Army, Regulation 173 and Rule 7(b) of Appendix II, a petitioner who was recruited in 1968 and was fixed in medical category `AYE,' and later developed hearing loss in 1994 due to the stress and strain of service, was subsequently downgraded to `CEE' (Permanent).
The petitioner applied for an extension of service in 1996 and was recommended as fit for further service by a senior ENT specialist, as well as by the Senior Medical Officer after re-examination.
However, the officiating S.M.O.
remarks were deleted and changed to "unfit" for service by the Senior Medical Officer after his return from leave.
The petitioner alleged mala fide intention on the part of the SMO, and was declined service extension due to the medical category not being attributable to service.
It was held that the hearing loss or disability suffered by the petitioner arose from and was influenced by service, therefore it was attributable to service.
The respondents have been directed to re-consider the petitioner's case for extension of service on its merits.
