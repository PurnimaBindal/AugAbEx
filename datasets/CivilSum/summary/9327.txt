Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 33(1)(a), 33(2)(6) and 33-A - Alteration of conditions of service - Complain under section 33-A when a dismissal order has been passed in contravention of Section 33(1)(a), writ petition not allowed.
[Para 7]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 33(1)(a), 33(2)(b) and 33-A - Complaint - A separate award by the Tribunal is necessary when a complaint under section 33-A against contravention of section 33(1)(a) is filed during the pendency or after termination of the proceedings before the Tribunal.
[Para 10]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-A - Complaint/Limitation - A complaint under section 33-A can be filed without any prescribed limitation.
In case of delay, pendency of a writ petition can be considered a reasonable excuse.
