Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Qualifications - Appointment by selection - An individual may be required to meet higher qualifications than those provided in the Rules - Mere possession of minimum qualifications is insufficient to guarantee appointment - The government has the right to choose the most qualified candidate - A writ of mandamus can only be issued if the petitioner has an enforceable legal right and the authority has a legal duty to enforce it - The petitioner, who possessed only minimum qualifications and experience as an in-charge of the institution, has no legal right to be appointed as a Principal, even under the doctrine of legitimate expectation.
[Paras 7 to 10]
