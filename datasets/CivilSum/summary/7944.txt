Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 - Junior - Drawing higher pay - Appeal against drawal of higher pay scale by Junior - Appellant denied additional increment as part of rectification of anomaly regarding juniors drawing higher pay scale after promotion on implementation of settlement between Management and employees - Appellant not included in group of beneficiaries - Respondent's classification in matter of rectification of anomaly is under-inclusive and therefore unconstitutional and violative of Article 14 of the Constitution - Appellant was discriminated against - Entitled to step up of salary from date junior started to draw higher pay - Appeal allowed.
