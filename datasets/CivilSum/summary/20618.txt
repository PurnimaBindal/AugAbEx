Constitution of India, Article 16 - Orissa District Revenue Service - Method of Recruitment and Conditions of Service Rules, 1983, Proviso to Rules (5) and Rule 6(1) - It is valid to extend the age limit up to five years for specific categories under government guidelines.
This power is not unguided.
However, Rule 6(1) is in violation of the prohibition under Article 16(2) as it invites applications from candidates of the district alone.
Selections made based on such applications cannot be upheld since no discrimination can be made on the basis of place of birth.
