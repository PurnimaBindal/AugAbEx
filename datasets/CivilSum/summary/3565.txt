Constitution of India's Article 226 allows for the termination of temporary services without prior notice.
The Bombay Shops and Establishments Act, 1948, Section 66(b) mandates no requirement of notice for temporary service termination, as demonstrated with the respondent's limited period leave vacancy.
The respondent's services automatically ceased at the end of this period, and the Bombay shops and Est.
Act, Section 66(b) notice was not necessary.
