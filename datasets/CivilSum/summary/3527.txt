Constitution of India, Article 14 - Adverse remarks - The accepting authority cannot downgrade ACRs from recommendations made by the Reporting officer without providing reasons for departure - Entries such as `very good', `shows interest in his work', `reliable', `shows compassion towards persons belonging to S.C./S.T.'
cannot be converted into unjust adverse remarks like `grown very fat and must work hard', `must learn art of discipline and respect his seniors' without evidence of unfitness, indiscipline, or disrespect towards seniors - If there is no indication why the accepting officer departed from the reporting officer's recommendations or why the performance was deemed unsatisfactory, then such unmerited adverse remarks cannot be upheld and must be rejected.
[Paras 17 & 13]
