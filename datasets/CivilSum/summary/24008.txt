Article 30 of the Constitution of India provides for the right of minority institutions to govern their own affairs, but the government can still create regulations to maintain educational quality and to establish qualifications and conditions of service.
These regulations must be beneficial to the institution and must not infringe on the right under Article 30.
[Para 5]

With the Uttar Pradesh Secondary Education (Services Selection Boards) Act of 1982 now in force, the provisions for termination in Section 16G(3)(a) of the 1921 Act are no longer applicable.
Approval of an inspector is not necessary nor legally valid in dispensing punishment when the Services Selection Board is able to discern.
An independent agency has been created to address the shortcomings of an inferior official to avoid arbitrary decisions without any guidelines.
