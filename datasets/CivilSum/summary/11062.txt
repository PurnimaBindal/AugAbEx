Appointment to public posts is governed by constitutional provisions in India.
Employers have a duty to consider all eligible candidates for public posts and must advertise vacancies, or send requisition to employment exchange, if they wish to make appointments without violating Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
[Para 4] B.
To comply with requirements of the Employment Exchanges (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959, appointments in the State Cooperative Bank must be advertised, or requisition to employment exchange must be sent, regarding vacancies existing for more than 89 days.
Failure to do so is a violation of the Act and the appointments are quashed.
[Para 5] C Favoritism and nepotism by bank authorities in appointing their own or officials' relations is a violation of bank rules.
Appointments based on such conduct are quashed.
[Paras 6 and 7] D. Appointments on public posts cannot violate the rules of the bank and constitutional provisions in Articles 14 and 16.
Officials who appoint relations over deserving candidates face action for violating rules and the constitution.
The bank is directed to take action against these guilty officers and a petitioner is awarded Rs. 10,000/- costs to be paid jointly by the appointees.
