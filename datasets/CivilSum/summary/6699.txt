Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Special Family Pension - Death of husband of petitioner while on-duty in Indian Navy - Medical Board holds cause of death (suicide) attributable to service - Officer in Command or Pension Sanctioning authority cannot disregard Medical Board's opinion in favor of their own without strong reasons to deny Special Pension - Medical Board's opinion must be given full weight.
