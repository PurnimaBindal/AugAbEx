Constitution of India, Article 136 - False and fabricated documents - The Plaintiff Protection Officer in Sugarfed was terminated when the post was abolished, but he produced a document in support of the contention that he was an employee of Markfed and was on deputation with Sugarfed.
The Additional District Judge and High Court in Second Appeal held that the respondent was an employee of Markfed and was on deputation with Sugarfed.
However, Markfed submitted before the High Court in Second Appeal as well as in Review Petition that the documents on which reliance was placed are fabricated, with the said contention of Markfed not being accepted by the High Court, and the Review Petition dismissed.
The Court ordered the District Court, Chandigarh to record a finding, and held that the suit filed by the respondent before the Trial Court was rightly dismissed.
As a consequence of the findings recorded by the District Judge, it must be held that the suit was barred by limitation.
Judgments of High Court and Addl.
District Judge set aside.
B Criminal Procedure Code, Section 195 - Production of forged and fabricated documents - Initiating criminal proceedings against respondent - The Registrar of High Court directed to initiate criminal Proceedings under Section 195 Criminal Procedure Code since it has been found that the documents that were produced by the plaintiff were false and fabricated documents.
