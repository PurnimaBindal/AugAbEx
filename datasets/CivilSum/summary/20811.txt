Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(a) - Punishment - Quantum of punishment - Dismissal from service for misappropriation of public money - No punishment other than dismissal is acceptable for corruption or misappropriation of public funds - Any leniency shown in these cases goes against public interest - The amount of money misappropriated doesn't matter, the act of misappropriation itself is significant - The Director Local Bodies' decision to reduce the punishment of dismissal to stoppage of increments has been overturned.
