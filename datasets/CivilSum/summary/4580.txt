According to Sections 2(b) and 6 of the Employees' Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, "Special allowance" is considered as part of the basic wages and not DA.
Section 2(b) includes all emoluments except those specified under clauses (i), (ii), and (iii), and DA is a component of wages.
Special allowances being a cash payment, it is considered part of the wages on which contribution must be paid under Section 6 of the 1952 EPF Act.
