According to Section 19(2) of the Workmen's Compensation Act (8 of 1923), a claim for compensation must be filed before the Workmen's Compensation Commissioner who will determine the liability of the employer after framing issues and recording evidence.
The findings of the Commissioner cannot be challenged in a Civil Court, and a suit filed by the employer to recover the amount paid to the claimant employee under the Commissioner's order is prohibited by Section 19(2).
The copy of the award indicated that the employer was very much a party before the Court of the Commissioner.
