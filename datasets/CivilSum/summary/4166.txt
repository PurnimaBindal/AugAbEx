According to Punjab Service of Engineers P.W.D.
Class-I (B&R Branch) Rules, 1960, Rule 15, passing the departmental exam or any other prescribed test is necessary before being considered for promotion to a higher post.
In this case, the petitioner had not passed the exam until May 1976.
Therefore, his claim for promotion to the post of Executive Engineer in 1975 is considered premature and untenable.
Punjab Service of Engineers P.W.D.
Class-I (B&R Branch) Rules, 1960, Rule 15 states that promotion is based on merit and suitability.
The petitioner's punishments in three different esquires have been quashed, which gives him the legal right to be reconsidered for promotion as of 821979 and all subsequent dates when juniors were considered.
However, the Screening Committee took unfavorable material into consideration in subsequent years, making their consideration unfair.
The case for petitioner's promotion to Xen as of 821979 is declared illegal and quashed.
The Respondent-Government is directed to re-consider the petitioner's case for promotion to Xen as of 821979 and all subsequent dates when juniors were considered.
