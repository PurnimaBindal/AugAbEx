Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2 and 25F - Haryana Cooperative Societies Act, 1984, Sections 102 and 128 - Jurisdiction/Workman/Labour Officer - The petitioner did not have any supervisory duties and worked under the management's direction without independent initiative.
The labour court found the petitioner to be a workman and the award was not deemed as perverse.
The High Court does not serve as a court of appeal.
The termination of the petitioner's services violated Section 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act.
The labour court's jurisdiction was not barred, and there is no need for interference.
The writ was dismissed.
