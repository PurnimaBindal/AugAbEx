According to Article 12 of the Constitution of India, the J&K Social Welfare Advisory Board is not regarded as a State under the definition of authority given in the Constitution.
The tests of being an instrumentality or an agency of the Government, as determined in Ajay Hasia's case AIR 1981 SC 487 and Shetty's case AIR 1979 SC 1628 are followed.
[Paras 1 to 5] B.
If a workman completes 240 days and then has their services terminated illegally, they are entitled to reinstatement with full back wages.
This is stated in Sections 25F and 25H of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and also in Articles 14 & 16 of the Constitution of India.
"Actually worked for 240 days" encompasses all of the days during which the workman was employed and paid wages under contract of service or compulsion of statute.
Sundays/weekly rests and paid holidays cannot be excluded when computing "Actually worked 240 days."
Even government employees, contract labor, seasonal employees, project/scheme employees may be ordered to be regularized.
