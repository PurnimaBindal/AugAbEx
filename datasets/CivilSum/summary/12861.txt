Constitution of India, Article 226 - BHEL Security Force Regulations, Regulation No. 13(b)(ii) - Date of birth/Medical Certificate - If an employee submits a medical certificate that contradicts the date of birth mentioned during joining, it is easy for them to procure such a certificate.
On the other hand, a Medical Officer cannot assess the exact date of birth and age.
Due to the lack of clinching evidence, the High Court cannot re-investigate the issue.
