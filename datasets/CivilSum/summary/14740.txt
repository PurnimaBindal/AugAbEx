According to Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3(i)(a), in cases of doubtful integrity, the entire service record of an official must be considered.
A single entry of doubtful integrity could result in premature retirement.
In this case, the petitioner received adverse remarks even after being promoted.
The order for premature retirement was upheld, and the judgment of the Single Judge was set aside.
[Paras 5, 6, and 7]
As per Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 and 4, when an employee files a representation against adverse entries, and a premature retirement order is passed during the pendency of the petition or appeal, the order cannot be deemed invalid.
The representation can be rejected before it is filed or even during its pendency.
[Para 7]
