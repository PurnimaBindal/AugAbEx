Under Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a petitioner whose services were terminated while working in an unaided school and reinstated to the same school is entitled to the same salary as employees at that time, according to Punjab Civil Services Rules.
[Para 5]
Management must pay arrears of back wages to a petitioner whose services were terminated, who was reinstated later and is entitled to the same salary as a Peon of an unaided school during that period, based on the Punjab Civil Services Rules.
