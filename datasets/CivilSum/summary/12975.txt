Constitution of India, Article 226 - Absorption - Writ petition - Maintainability of - A contractor who runs a subsidized canteen in Bank premises on a `no profit no loss' basis, and who has requested absorption as an employee of the Bank via petition, is not maintainable.
The appropriate course of action would be to raise an industrial dispute before the appropriate authority.
Furthermore, the employees of the contractor may be considered employees of the principal, but the contractor himself cannot be treated as an employee of the principal employer.
[Para 4] B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ Petition - Maintainability of  - When exercising power of writ jurisdiction, the court cannot entertain issues involving disputed questions of facts where an alternative remedy is available.
