Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Post of constable G.D. in B.S.F.
- The petitioner was qualified for appointment as a constable as per the advertisement, but became average and no clear vacancy was available.
Although he was kept on the list of selected candidates, this did not entitle him to seek a post if he was not eligible.
The court cannot issue a direction to appoint him after a lapse of nine years.
[Paras 4 and 5]
