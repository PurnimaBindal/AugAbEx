Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion - Rural Experience - Benefit of - The Petitioner was denied promotion due to not receiving marks for experience in rural branch.
According to clause 34, all employees posted in a rural branch on the cut off date of promotion should be given marks for each completed year.
The argument that the candidate must actually be working in the rural branch on the cut off date was rejected.
The court held that the weightage of working in a rural branch must be given irrespective of posting on the cut off date.
