Constitution of India, Article 226 - Selection - Confirmation - Termination - The Petitioner was offered an appointment after being selected and their service was confirmed after probation.
However, their appointment was subsequently cancelled due to a lack of prescribed qualifications.
The Petitioner argues that the authorities cannot invoke any conditions after confirming their appointment.
The court found that the appointment was irregular due to the breach of conditions, but it must be treated as regularized in light of the conduct of the respondents.
Furthermore, the court found that the removal order was in violation of Article 311 and thus unsustainable.
The Petition was allowed accordingly.
