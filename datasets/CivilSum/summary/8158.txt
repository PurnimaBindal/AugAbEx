Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 1624 and 12.21 - Discharge from service - Natural justice - Absence from duty - Discharging an official on the basis of an innocuous order of simpliciter discharge is not valid.
The court can scrutinize and reveal the true grounds for the discharge.
In service jurisprudence, absence from duty is considered as misconduct and warrants an enquiry before any decision is made.
Discharge based on such misconduct charges amounts to dismissal and cannot be passed without an enquiry.
Rule 12.21 should be applied instead of Rule 1624.
The order of discharge without any supporting evidence that the official is not likely to be efficient is unsustainable and is therefore quashed.
[Paras 7, 8, 9, 13, 15 and 16]
