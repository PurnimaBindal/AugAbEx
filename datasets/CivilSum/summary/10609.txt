Constitution of India, Article 311 - Tamil Nadu Police Subordinate Service (Civil & Discipline) Rules, 1955, Rule 3(b) - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 15 - In the case of bigamous marriage, a disciplinary authority imposed the punishment of removal of Constable, which was deemed not disproportionate.
The Tribunal confirmed the conclusions of the Disciplinary Authority but imposed a lesser punishment.
However, it was not proper for the Tribunal to interfere with the punishment, so the order passed by the Tribunal was set aside, and the order of the Disciplinary Authority was confirmed.
