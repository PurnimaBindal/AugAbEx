Indian Medical Council Act, 1956, Section 10A - Admissions to Medical Courses - Medical institutions must adhere to the norms stipulated by the Medical Council for standards and capacity, else admissions cannot be granted - The Central Government cannot authorize admission of students exceeding the number of seats permitted by the Council - Before applying for inspection to increase seats, the institution must first augment its infrastructure and facilities - Any permission granted by the Central Government not in accordance with the prescribed procedure under the Act will be invalid.
[Paras 3, 8 to 10 & 13]
