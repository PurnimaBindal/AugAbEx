Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Natural justice - Enquiry report - In case the disciplinary authority wants to disagree with a report submitted by the Enquiry Officer in favor of the charged official, it must record its reasons and issue a show cause notice to the delinquent, with reasons, to afford an opportunity to present their viewpoint and persuade the authority to accept the report.
Failure to provide such an opportunity before passing the punishment order will render it an illegality.
Merely confirming the punishment order at the appellate and revising authority level will not cure this defect as it is a patent illegality that cannot be ratified.
The order has been quashed with the liberty to pass a fresh order in accordance with the law.
[Paras 7, 8 and 9]
