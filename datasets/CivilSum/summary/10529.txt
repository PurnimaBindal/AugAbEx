In accordance with Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a workman who is gainfully employed on lesser pay than that given by the management is entitled to receive the difference in amount which should have been paid to them.
It was held that if the workman was gainfully employed for any duration, it cannot be claimed by the Labor Court that no wages shall be paid for that period.
The petitioner is therefore rightfully entitled to payment of the difference in amount.
