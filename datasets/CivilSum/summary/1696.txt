Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, Section 140(2) - Procedure - Substantive law - Section 140(2) involves substantive law instead of procedural law.

Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, Section 144, 140 and 217 - General Clauses Act, 1897, Section 6 - No-fault Liability - Repealing provision - The absence of a provision for exclusion of the application of Section 6 of the General Clauses Act by Section 217 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 implies that Section 144 applies solely to claims for compensation related to accidents that happened after 171989, and that cases before said time are governed by Section 92-E of 1939 - The increased rate of compensation that was implemented through the amended sections 140 and 144, which were effective since 14111994, cannot be used to apply to claims that stem from incidents that occurred before the aforementioned date - The right and obligation come into existence only on the date of the accident, instead of on the date when the court evaluates the claim.
