Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Civil Services Rules (Applicable to Haryana) Rule Section 532, volume-II, Rule 326 volume 1, Part 1 - Departmental Proceedings - Premature Retirement - Public interest - If an employee's integrity is doubted, they may be given premature retirement after a regular departmental enquiry because it is in the larger public interest to remove "deadwood" employees.
The petitioner in this case was given premature retirement after such an enquiry, and the order was upheld.
