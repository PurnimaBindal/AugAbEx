Constitution of India, Article 309 - Andhra Pradesh Civil Service (CCA) Rules, 1991 - Applicable to Government servants - Departmental enquiries cannot be conducted against employees of Cooperative Societies registered under Andhra Pradesh Co-operative Societies Act - Such societies have their own bye-laws that govern service conditions - The appointment of an enquiry officer by the Government, as per the impugned order, has been set aside - The respondent is free to proceed with the enquiry as per the Corporation's bye-laws.
