The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, outlines the conditions for promotion on probation and confirmation, including the potential for discrimination in the imposition of conditions.
For promotion to the post of Manager (Flight Operations), passing a test may be a requirement even if earlier promotions were made without this condition.
Such a circumstance does not constitute discrimination, as there were no such instructions previously.
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, also addresses the possibility of reversion from a promoted position if a probationer fails to fulfill a condition of confirmation despite being given extra chances.
A conditional confirmation can be revoked if the probationer does not meet the necessary requirements.
