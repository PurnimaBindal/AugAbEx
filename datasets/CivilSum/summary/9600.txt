The Constitution of India, Article 226, and Section 11(2) of the Navy Act, 1957 govern re-engagement of retirees.
The administration's conformity with the regulations and not unrestricted conduct is the court's domain to review.
The court cannot re-evaluate Navy Officers' performance for re-engagement.

The training period is counted for retirement purposes, according to the Constitution of India, Article 311-A, and should also be considered for pension purposes when completing 15 years of service from the Navy.
