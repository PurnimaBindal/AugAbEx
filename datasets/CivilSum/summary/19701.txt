Article 16 of the Constitution of India along with Rule 8 Explanation of the Haryana Agricultural University Regulations were examined in a case regarding seniority.
The question was whether the petitioner's service rendered on foreign service with the University can be counted when he was absorbed by the University.
The expression 'foreign service' signifies service in the University of an employee other than that of the University.
It was held that since the petitioner had joined the University after leaving the service of Govt. of India, his service rendered on foreign service must count towards his seniority.
[Paras 7 and 9]  B Rule 8 Explanation of the Haryana Agricultural University Regulations permit permanent absorption of persons initially taken on foreign service and also provide that such foreign service shall count for seniority.
The court opined that the effect and benefit of such provision cannot be denied merely because it may lead to some uncertainties and fluctuations in the seniority of the employees of the University.
