Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Civil Services (Revised Scales of Pay) Rules, 1979, Rule 2 Schedules B and C - Cadre strength - The merger of different categories into one cadre resulting in an increase in cadre strength cannot be deemed illegal and arbitrary unless expressly prohibited by the rules - Consolidation of various posts into one cadre does not constitute amending the regulations.
