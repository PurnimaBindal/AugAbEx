Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination/Probation - Termination of services during probation period as per appointment order conditions - Probationer's confidential reports and performance must be considered - The principle of tearing of the veil to uncover the real nature of the order applies only if there is a direct nexus between the charge levied and the action taken; overall performance assessment and employee action/inaction not sufficient cause - Competent authority may investigate complaints against probationary employees to assess performance.
