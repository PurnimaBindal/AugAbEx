The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 16 and 226, allows the government to issue orders regarding the Teacher-Pupil ratio of 1:40, and such orders are considered valid.
The Full Bench has further clarified that the ratio of students-teacher strength should be based on each individual standard/section as a unit, and the minimum number of teachers required should not be less than the number of standards/sections in a school.
Additionally, if the students' strength in a particular standard exceeds 60, an additional section requiring a second teacher is required, and if the strength reaches 100, a third teacher is required.
Even if the above ratio is maintained, if the teacher-students ratio 1:40 of the entire school requires an increase in teachers' strength, it has to be allowed.
However, the strength of teachers in no case should be less than the number of standards, including additional sections.
The school authorities should create additional sections in respect of any particular standard if more teachers are sanctioned, keeping in view the standard of education.
This ratio is not only applicable to Aided Schools or Government Schools, but also to Private Recognised Schools that require recognition.
The government may formulate appropriate norms in accordance with these observations and constitutional provisions.
