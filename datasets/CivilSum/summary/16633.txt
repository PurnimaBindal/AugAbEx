Constitution of India, Article 226 - Voluntary retirement - Withdrawal of offer - The petitioner requested permission to switch over to general provident fund scheme upon her voluntary retirement application - Her offer for voluntary retirement was later accepted, but her request to switch over to the provident fund scheme was rejected - The petitioner subsequently attempted to withdraw her offer for voluntary retirement, but the corporation rejected her request - The Scheme did not give the petitioner the right to ask her employer to rescind its decision to accept her offer for voluntary retirement.
[Paras 5 to 9]
