Constitution of India, Articles 141, 142, and 226 - Admissions/Education - Admissions in Govt. Medical and Engineering Courses - Following the scheme of Unni Krishnan's case, some petitioner-institutions continue making admissions under an old, illegal procedure.
State government permits these institutions through relaxation, but this does not match the judiciary of Unni Krishnan's case.
The State lacks capacity to delegate the selection power.
The State Government also cannot exempt the said judgment of the Court.
There can be no equity in perpetuating the illegality and violations of institutions and State authorities.
In any case, the Supreme Court has already negatived the question of equity in such situations, as in Chander Chinar Bada Akhara Udasin Society's case.
Institutions’ petitions are dismissed with expenses.
[Paras 11 to 13, 19 and 20]
