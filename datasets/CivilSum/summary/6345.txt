Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 36(2) and 36(4) - Representative - Appearance on behalf of the employer before the Labour Court - Even if an advocate represents the employer before the Labour Court, he/she does not appear as an advocate but as an office-bearer of the Association/Federation of which the employer/company is a member, and is allowed to appear in that capacity as a representative of the company - Such an appearance by an advocate on behalf of the company meets the requirements of Section 36(2)(a)(b) of the Act, and sub-section 4 of Section 36 does not apply.
