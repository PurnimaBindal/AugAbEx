Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) and 226 - Reservation - Bona fide mistake - The Public Service Commission of J&K had advertised a position for Lecturer in History specifically for the Socially & Educationally Backward Class candidates.
- The interviewees were required to produce a certificate of reservation at the interview - The petitioner did not have the necessary certificate at the time - While the petitioner mistakenly asked for a Backward Area certificate in a telegram, this did not change the circumstances - As such, the petitioner cannot claim consideration for the position.
[Paras 3, 4 and 5]
