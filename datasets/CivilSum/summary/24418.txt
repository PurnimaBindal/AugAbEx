According to Section 116-C of the Representation of People Act, 1951, a woman of high caste who marries a scheduled caste person does not automatically become a member of the scheduled caste and is not entitled to contest an election on a reserved seat.
[Paras 18, 23, 24, 26 and 32] B.
Even if a high caste Hindu woman marries a scheduled caste person and is accepted by her husband's family, she does not become a member of the scheduled caste unless accepted by the community of her husband.
Election to a reserved seat for scheduled caste women by such a woman is void, as acceptance by elders of her husband's family does not equal acceptance by the community of her husband.
