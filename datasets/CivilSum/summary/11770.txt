According to Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, claims for arrears of wages must be based on pre-existing rights.
A labor court's finding that a worker did not report for duty during a period of criminal trial, coupled with a written statement relinquishing the right to back wages upon acquittal, means that the worker is not eligible for back wages.
This claim cannot be made under Section 33-C(2) of the ID Act.
