Constitution of India, Articles 16, 14 - Indian Railway Establishment Manual, Sub-para (a) of para 2501 - Pension - Qualifying period - Petitioners initially employed as Project Casual Labour by the Railway Administration - Later absorbed on a regular temporary/permanent post - Whether services provided as Project Casual Labour qualify as part of qualifying service for the purpose of pension and other retiral benefits - Project Casual Labour benefits to be treated as temporary from 111981, with half of the service rendered as Project Casual Labour to be counted as per the order of October 14, 1980, following the scheme in Inder Pal Yadav's case.
