Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission - Reservation in admissions - Classification and categorisation - Quotas for different categories fixed in admissions to Post-graduate Diploma/Degree MDS courses - 50% of seats reserved for `in-service' candidates and remaining 50% for `non-service' candidates - Categorisation for allotment of seats with the goal to accommodate different categories cannot be considered as reservation - Final selection depending on the inter se merit performance amongst their own category candidates - Candidates of other categories have no right to claim seats allotted to other categories, even on the basis of their merit - Mere use of the word 'reservation' alone does not imply the entire mechanism of a protective reservation for BC or S.C./S.T.
citizens - Classification and categorisation are upheld.
