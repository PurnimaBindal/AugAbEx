Prisons Act, Sections 45 and 46 - Jail punishment - Judicial appraisal - When a case is sent to the Sessions Judge for judicial appraisal, the convict must be given an opportunity to be heard.
If the documents fail to show application of judicial mind, the description of jail punishment as having been judicially appraised cannot be used.
This interpretation is supported by 1990(1) Recent Criminal Reports 679 and 1991(1) Recent Criminal Reports 466.
[Paras 5 and 6]
