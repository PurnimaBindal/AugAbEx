Constitution of India, Article 14 - Jammu University Statute 7(a), 8 and 13 - Research scholar - Prohibition on part time employment during scholarship - Distinguishing between University/College teachers, other teachers and research scholars - rationale - University/College teachers have ample time for study and part time work - Only teach specific subjects - Teaching at the collegiate and university level is an aspect of further study and research - School teachers have less time available - Clear distinctions in classification exist - Students who enrol in the PhD programme are aware of the statutory prohibition on part time employment during the programme - They cannot challenge the provision - Statute 8(b) is not invalid.
