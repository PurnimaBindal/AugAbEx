Madhya Pradesh Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1966, Rules 9(2-a) and 5(a) and (b) - Suspension - The rule requires that a suspension order must provide reasons or accompany a charge sheet - The petitioner's charge sheet was served without any reasons 45 days after the suspension order - Even though the suspension order was illegal, since enquiry proceedings have now been initiated, the authority is still permitted to reconsider whether it is necessary to continue the suspension - The competent authority has been directed to reconsider the petitioner's suspension within 15 days.
