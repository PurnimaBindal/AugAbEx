Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 11 Rules 6 and 7 - Back wages - Employers must prove the issue of gainful employment of workman - Interrogating workman about assets, properties, and source of funds is irrelevant and scandalous - Labour court cannot issue such interrogatories without jurisdiction and non-application of mind.

Civil Procedure Code, Order 11 Rules 6 & 7 - Interrogatories - Court must determine interrogatories' relevance and lack of scandal before applying its mind.
[Para 5] C. Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 11-A - Reinstatement - Backwages - Normal rule is for full back wages when termination is illegal and workman is reinstated with continuity - Employer must prove deviation from this rule.
