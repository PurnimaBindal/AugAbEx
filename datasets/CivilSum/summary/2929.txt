The Delhi Police Act of 1978, Sections 21 and 147, and the Delhi Police Punishment and Appeal Rules of 1980, Rule 12, cover dismissal on departmental enquiry before being acquitted by trial court.
Petitioner seeks the benefit of Rule 12, which states that a delinquent cannot be punished departmentally if he is acquitted by the criminal court.
However, since the Petitioner had already been punished departmentally before his acquittal by the trial court, Rule 12 is not applicable.
