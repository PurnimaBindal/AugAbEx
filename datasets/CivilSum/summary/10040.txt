The Constitution of India's Articles 16 and 226 states that a temporary employee can be absorbed into regular service.
This was the case for a banking service appellant, who had served for more than 240 days within 12 calendar months.
The Bank's circular of 12th March 1991 affirmed that temporary employees who have served at least 240 days in a 12-calendar-month period after 1.1.1982 can be absorbed into available vacancies without any test or interview.
Consequently, the appellant's absorption claim cannot be denied, and the Bank has been ordered to permanently absorb them in the subordinate cadre.
