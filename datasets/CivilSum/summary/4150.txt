The Gujarat Water Resources Development Corporation Ltd Employees (Control and Appeal) Rules, 1964, Rule 4 - Suspension - Misconduct - Interference by the Court - The petitioner challenged the order of suspension on grounds of mala fide, but the allegations were found vague, indefinite and baseless.
The charge of mala fide cannot be sustained without a clear allegation against the Minister and in his absence, the order of suspension was held valid.
[Para 9] B The Gujarat Water Sources Development Corporation Ltd Employees (Control and Appeal) Rules, 1964, Rule 8 - Suspension - The petitioner challenged the order of suspension on the grounds that Managing Director was not competent to make the order.
However, the suspension order was held valid since the managing director was his appointing authority.
[Para 10] C. The Gujarat Water Resources Development Corporation Ltd Employees (Control and Appeal) Rules, 1964, Rule 5 - Suspension - Natural justice - An order of suspension made in contemplation of disciplinary proceedings does not require giving a reason beyond what has been given in the order.
There is no requirement to provide notice or opportunity of hearing to the petitioner.
Therefore, the plea that the order of suspension lacks reason and was made without notice or opportunity of hearing is not valid.
