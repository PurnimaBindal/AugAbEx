According to Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a workman needs to complete 240 days of work in the year preceding the termination of his service to be entitled to its benefits.
Even if the workman worked in three different provincial Sub-Divisions under the same employer, his period of service could be clubbed to calculate if he had completed 240 days.
[Para 10]
Workman's termination of services was held void-ab-initio as he was not offered compensation nor given notice as required by Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, before termination.
As a result, the petitioner was reinstated with full back wages.
