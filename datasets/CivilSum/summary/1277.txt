Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Schedule 2, Item 6 - Bipartite Settlement dt. 19-10-1966, Clause 195 - Second charge-sheet - The petitioner challenged the first charge-sheet issued for allegedly engaging in business outside the scope of duties as being vague.
The High Court allowed the petition and stayed further proceedings.
As there was a restraining order passed by the High Court, the Enquiry Officer did not submit any report.
Therefore, no de novo enquiry was conducted.
However, the bank can proceed to remove the defect by issuing a second charge-sheet to the petitioner, providing details of the allegations against him.
[Paras 21 and 22]
