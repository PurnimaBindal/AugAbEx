The appellant's claim for notional promotion to the grade of Rs. 250-380(AS) w.e.f.
611964 was considered by the Railway Administration pursuant to the direction of the Single Judge, and was granted notional promotion w.e.f.
15.2.1968.
However, it was found that the appellant had failed to raise any contention that he was eligible for notional promotion in the year 1964, and therefore, the plea was not accepted.
The Railway Administration's affidavit filed in 1984, which disclosed this fact for the first time, was accepted by the Single Judge in the absence of any challenge thereto, and as a result, the appeal was dismissed.
