According to Punjab Police Rules, Rule 1638(1), if a police officer commits a criminal offense in connection with official relations, the District Magistrate must be informed.
The District Magistrate will decide whether the complaint should be investigated by police or made over to the Magistrate.
Rule 1638 of Punjab Police Rules is mandatory.
[Para 7]
Under Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 173 and 19(2), if a police officer commits a criminal offense in discharge of official duties, the case will be registered and investigated by police.
If the order of the District Magistrate is not obtained under Rule 1638 of Punjab Police Rules, the report of the police investigation under Section 173 Cr.
PC will be quashed.
Relying on 1976 CLR 39(FB) and AIR 1955 SC 196, Rule 1638 is mandatory.
[Para 7]
According to Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 197(2), if a police officer commits a criminal offense in discharge of official duties, the State Government must accord sanction for prosecution, not the SSP (Appointing Authority).
In Punjab, the police cadre has been declared as Armed Forces by the Punjab Government.
In a case where prosecution against a police officer was quashed for non-sanction of the Government, there is no bar to launch fresh prosecution after valid sanction.
