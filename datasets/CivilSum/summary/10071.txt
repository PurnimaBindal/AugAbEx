Constitution of India, Articles 311(2) and 226 - Dismissal - Misconduct - The delinquent employee, who was a godown manager, sold a large amount of palmoline oil in the black market while also misappropriating the funds - The Tribunal reduced the penalty from dismissal to a lower time scale, stating that the employee was negligent and accepted his defence that the loss occurred due to leakage, which was a false claim - The Tribunal's order was overturned.
