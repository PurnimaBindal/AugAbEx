Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 14(2) - Ex-parte enquiry - Natural justice - The delinquent requested an adjournment of the enquiry proceedings on medical grounds, but the Enquiry Officer ignored the request and proceeded ex parte against the delinquent.
An enquiry report was submitted, finding the charge of unauthorised absence proved.
The authorities' actions were found to violate the rules and principles of natural justice.
