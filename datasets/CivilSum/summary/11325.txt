Constitution of India, Article 14 - House Rent allowance - Over payment of allowances - Government instructions - Government has the authority to withdraw the House Rent allowance concession at any time - Urban areas covered by the instructions provide House Rent allowance - Rural area allowance applies to those employed in rural areas - House rent protected rates for entitled individuals until they are merged into revised allowances and pay - Protection clause of instructions not applicable to those no longer entitled to House Rent allowance - The department can recover over payments exceeding the entitled amount for those only entitled to rural area allowance and receiving House Rent allowance - Such individuals cannot invoke the protection clause - Petitions dismissed.
