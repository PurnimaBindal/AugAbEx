Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Deputation - Circular specifies maximum period of deputation as three years for Junior Clerks, who may be repatriated after that time as per Chief Personnel Officer's approval - Junior Clerks approved for one-year posting as Meter Readers in the existing pay scale - Deputation period extended for maximum of two years - All conditions clearly specified at time of application - Petition challenging conditions dismissed as not justified after maximum extended deputation period.
