Enquiry - Termination - Natural justice - Order of termination issued without prior enquiry - Subsequent enquiry conducted and original order affirmed - Not in accordance with Rules and against natural justice to conduct enquiry after punishment imposed - Appellate authority should have set aside termination order and ordered enquiry, but did not - Reinstatement and arrears of salary denied to petitioner unjustly.
[Paras 3 and 4] B Natural justice - Every individual is entitled to a fair hearing - No enquiry conducted prior to termination order - Respondents' action unjustified - Workman entitled to reinstatement and arrears of salary - Board can conduct proper enquiry and take appropriate action.
