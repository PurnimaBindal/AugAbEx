According to Article 32 of the Constitution of India and Section 10(1) of the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, the petitioners being contract labourers, who have continuously worked for 10 to 20 years, should receive the same wages and treatment as regular employees, since they have been discharging jobs of recurring nature.
However, for certain employees, an industrial dispute was previously raised and settled through an award-making process.
For the applicants seeking the settlement of their grievances, they should have approached the appropriate authority under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
