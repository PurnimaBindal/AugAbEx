Army Act of 1950, Section 116 - Court Martial/Natural justice/Charges - The charge-sheet must specify the accused's role and what actions they failed to take in the situation - If the charge-sheet is vague or lacks material particulars, it can prejudice the convict - If the conviction is unsupported by evidence, the High Court can intervene under Article 226 of the Constitution - As per Paras 10 to 13, the Court Martial is invalidated and must be overturned.
