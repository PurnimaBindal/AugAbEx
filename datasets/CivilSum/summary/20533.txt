Constitution of India, Article 16 and 226 - Transfer - Challenged on the grounds of mala fides and participation in trade union activities - The tribunal held that the transfer order was made in a colorable exercise of power, and thus quashed the transfer - However, there was no material to justify interference with the mere order to transfer - The view taken by the tribunal was not justified and the impugned order of the tribunal has been set aside.
