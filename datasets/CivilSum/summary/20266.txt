The Tribunal has the power to address complaints related to civilian posts connected to Defence, but it was concluded that the Dhobis appointed to wash clothes of cadets at NDA are not considered to hold civil posts despite being paid salaries from Regimental funds.
The Commanding Officer acts as a trustee for Regimental funds and has some control over the Dhobis, but this does not mean that their posts are civil.
Therefore, the learned Tribunal's order was set aside.
