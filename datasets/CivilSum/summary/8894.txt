Charu Tuli, AAG for Haryana, argues that teachers should receive higher pay scale based on their qualifications as stated in the Constitution of India, articles 14 and 226.
She references Punjab Government instructions from 1957 that apply to all teachers in the Haryana Education Department.
According to these instructions, teachers are entitled to a higher pay scale from the date they acquire higher qualifications.
