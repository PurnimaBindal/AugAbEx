Constitution of India, 1950, Article 14 - Disciplinary proceedings - Dismissal of delinquent officer based on an enquiry without being provided an opportunity to defend or a copy of the enquiry report is improper - Parties agreed to give a fresh opportunity to the dismissed officer to file a show cause in response to a second notice and have the matter considered afresh on merits - As a result, the appellant bank was directed to initiate disciplinary proceedings from the stage of serving the second notice, allowing for the opportunity to defend and supplying the enquiry report - Failure to provide these requirements renders dismissal based on the enquiry liable to be quashed and set aside.
