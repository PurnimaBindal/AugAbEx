In accordance with the Punjab Water Supply And Sewerage Board Act 1976, the Punjab Municipal Corporation Employees Pension And General Provident Fund Rules 1994, CSR Vol. II, Rules 1(3), 5, and 8(2), CSR Vol I Part I, Rule 7.5(1), and Pension Rules 1994, Rule 2 Clause (g) and (i), the petitioner sought to receive the benefits of past service.
The petitioner argued that the government had previously provided pensionary benefits, but the respondents contend that the petitioner forfeited the benefits due to their resignation from Government service as stated in Rule 7.5(1) of CSR Rules.
The court concluded that the petitioner cannot claim benefit of past service as their appointment to the board was a fresh appointment, and thus the petition was dismissed as a result.
