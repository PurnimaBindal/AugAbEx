Constitution of India, Article 226 - Ad hoc employees of Government college - Claim of regularization - Salary for vacation period - Beldars, gardeners, peons and sweepers petition for regularization after being appointed on an ad hoc basis by the selection committee - College regularly terminates their service during school vacation period and reappoints them afterwards - It is argued that their salary is paid from amalgamated funds and their service is not required during vacations - The court finds that continuing the Petitioners on an ad hoc basis for an extended period of time without regularization is unfair as regular posts must have been vacated during this time - The court's directions are for the consideration of the Petitioners' case for regularization from the date that these posts became available without being influenced by the fact that they were paid from an amalgamated fund.
[Paras 7,8]
