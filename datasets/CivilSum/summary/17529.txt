Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Punjab Government National Emergency Concession rules, 1965 - Seniority - The petitioner had joined the army after the emergency had ended, so they are not entitled to the benefit of military service towards seniority.
However, the remaining petitioners who did render military service are entitled to the benefit and their claim should be considered according to the 1965 Rules.
The rejection of their claim was deemed illegal and arbitrary and ordered to be set aside.
[Paras 3 and 4] Rajender Kumar v. The State of Haryana and others, 1992(2) PLR 754.
