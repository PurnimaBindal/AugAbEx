Contempt of Courts Act, Sections 11 and 12 - Contempt of Court - Alternative relief - Medical bills - Non-compliance of orders of the Court - Interest of justice - The Court should prioritize achieving a larger cause and object over passing orders that may become un-executable or cause unnecessary delays - Although the court can decline to take action even if a prima facie case has been made out, contemners explained difficulties in obtaining sanction from the Finance Department for payment of medical bills in compliance with the court's orders - Despite being satisfied that contempt has been committed, the court dropped the contempt proceedings and granted further time for compliance within a specific framework - A report is to be submitted to the court and the petitioner was awarded costs of Rs. 5,000/- to be recovered from officials found at fault for causing delay.
