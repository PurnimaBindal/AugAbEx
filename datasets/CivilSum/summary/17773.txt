According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, the Punjab Medical and Health Department has established rules for Nursing Superintendents, Matrons, Sister Tutors, Public Health Nurses, Staff Nurses (Teaching) & Nursing Sisters, Public Health Nurses, Staff Nurses and Male Nurses (State Service Class III).
These rules, specifically Rules 6 and 17 of the Punjab Medical and Health Department, dictate that individuals who have obtained a diploma in general Nursing and Mid-wifery from the ancillary Medical Training School (Medical College Complex) Bakshinagar, Jammu and registered as Nurses/Mid wives with the Jammu and Kashmir Nurses, Midwives & Health Visitors Registration Council under the Jammu and Kashmir Nurses Midwives & Health Visitors Registration Act, 1956, are eligible for consideration for appointment under the Punjab Rules.
The State Government may establish qualifications for registration with the Punjab State Nursing Council, but they cannot be compelled to accept registration under any other council as a sufficient qualification.
However, if equivalent qualifications recognised under other Acts are not considered to be inferior in any way, the Government must consider their cases under the provisions of relaxation.
Therefore, the Government is instructed to consider the cases of the petitioner under Rule 17, instead of rejecting them, to determine whether they are entitled to some relaxation or not.
