The Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Sections 2(12), 2(17) and 85(a), (c) and (e) states that a complaint or prosecution must be sustainable.
However, the accused's ultimate control over the affairs of the Mill or if they are considered the Principal employer was not mentioned nor was it indicated if the concern falls under the term "factory" or if there are more than 10 employees.
Due to the lack of explicit recitals, the complaints are vague and not maintainable according to Paras 11A and 15.
