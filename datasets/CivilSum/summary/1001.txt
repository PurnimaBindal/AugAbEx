Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Police Act, 1861 - Section 7 - Absence from duty/Condonation of lapse/Punishment - The punishment given must match the severity of the charge - The petitioner was charged with absence from duty and was removed from service - However, the petitioner's absence of 57 days was condoned, and therefore their absence of 5 days loses its significance - The penalty of removal from service is not proportional to the charge - The case has been sent back to the punishing authority to assign a more suitable punishment.
