Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dentists Act, 1948, Section 10-A - Technical Education Institutions - Expert reports - Recommendations of the Dental Council of India - Academic matters :- According to Para 6, a technical college's permission to admit students and start classes is to be left to the concerned authority and interference from the High Court is unlikely unless the academic body's decision is unjust, unfair, or unreasonable, and suffers from mala fides and arbitrariness.
According to Para 8, in an academic matter of a technical nature, inspection reports by advocates who are not experts in the matter should not be considered the authority.
Instead, only reports of experts are to be considered.
