Constitution of India, Article 226 - Selection - Non-issuance of appointment orders - The petitioners, who participated in the selection process and were found suitable, did not receive appointment orders due to the ground that an amendment regarding relaxation of age was not carried out in recruitment regulations.
However, MCD had previously made a conscious decision to give age relaxation, and the non-appointment of the petitioners is due to MCD's own inaction or omission in amending regulations.
The decision is deemed strange and irrational, and the court therefore directs the issuance of appointment letters with all consequential benefits.
