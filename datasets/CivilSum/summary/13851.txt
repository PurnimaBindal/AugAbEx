Constitution of India, Article 311 - Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rules 7 and 9 - Departmental enquiry - The Petitioner was found to be consuming liquor on duty and habitually abusing others.
The punishing authority issued an order of removal from service, however, the appellate authority reduced the petitioner's pay scale until retirement.
Considering the petitioner's past record of misconduct, there are no grounds for interference.
