In regards to Disputes under the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 1(4), 74, 75 and Constitution of India, Article 226, it has been held that the issue of the Act's applicability to any establishment must be decided by the appropriate forum, rather than through extraordinary writ jurisdiction.
As such, the proper course of remedy is to approach the Employees Insurance Court and raise an industrial dispute, if possible.
