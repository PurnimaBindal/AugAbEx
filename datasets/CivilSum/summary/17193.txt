UP Intermediate Education Act, 1921, Section 16-FF - UP Intermediate Education Regulations, Regulations 5 and 20 - Vacancy - A vacancy falling within the 50% promotion quota does not require advertisement and will not be deemed surrendered unless no eligible candidate is available within three months of the committee of management decision to fill the vacancy through direct recruitment.

UP Intermediate Education Act, 1921, Section 16-FF(4) - Appointment - Approval - The petitioner's appointment for the post of Teacher was wrongly disapproved due to an erroneous view taken by authorities.
Section 16-FF(4) requires approval by the District Inspector of Schools, and once granted, the appointment would become effective from the date of approval.
