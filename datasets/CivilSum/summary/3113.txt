Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Advertisement - Educational qualification - Interview - Appointment/Selection - A total of 54 posts of Horticulture Development Officers were advertised by the Agriculture Department, with clear preference given to candidates having B.Sc.
Agriculture and MSc.
Agriculture qualifications, or B.Sc.
with a major subject of Horticulture.
Despite all petitioners having passed degree courses from different universities, they were excluded from appointment as they did not possess the specific qualification prescribed in the advertisement.
The court held that the petitioners had not established their entitlement regarding the essential qualifications, and as such, their petitions were dismissed.
[Paras 3, 7, 19, 22, 28]
