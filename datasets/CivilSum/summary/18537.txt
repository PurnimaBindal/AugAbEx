According to the Indian Constitution, Articles 14 & 21, Compassionate appointment should be given in urgent situations to prevent the family of the deceased public servant from falling into poverty.
However, it should be noted that compassionate appointment is not a guaranteed right.
In this case, the petitioner's father passed away in 1993, and the family was able to manage for nine years without him.
Therefore, the petitioner is not eligible for compassionate appointment.
