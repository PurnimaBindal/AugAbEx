Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 1624 - Natural justice - The delinquent, found guilty of misconduct for consuming alcohol while on duty, received punishment based on the findings recorded in the enquiry report.
The Tribunal cannot nullify the punishment unless the delinquent shows that he was prejudiced due to non-supply of the report before the show-cause notice or that it would have had a different effect if the report was supplied earlier.
The decision in State of Punjab v. Ram Singh, 1992(3) SCT 398 (SC) was followed.
The order of the Tribunal quashing the punishment on the ground of non-supply of the report before the show-cause notice is not sustainable.
