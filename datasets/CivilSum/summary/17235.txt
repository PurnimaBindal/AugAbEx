When the petitioner was appointed as a member of the KPSC, he was already holding a post in the super-time scale of the IAS.
Although there was a revision of the pay scale under the I.A.S (Pay) Rules in 1954, the petitioner is not entitled to it because it came into force after his retirement and he would have had to continue as an IAS officer.
This information is outlined in the Constitution of India, Article 318, Karnataka Public Service Commission (Condition of Service) Regulations, 1957, Clause 4.
