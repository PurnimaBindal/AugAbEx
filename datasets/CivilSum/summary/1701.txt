Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provisions) Act, 1985, Section 22 - Employees Provident Fund and Misc Provisions Act, 1952, Section 14-B (As amended in 1988) - Recovery of EPF Contribution - Section 22 of the SICA Act, 1985 cannot stay recovery proceedings for EPF contributions - Applications under section 22 of SICA that seek to stay recovery proceedings are not maintainable - The amendment in Section 14-B of EPF Act states that proceedings under the EPF Act cannot be stayed even if the company has approached the BIFR.
