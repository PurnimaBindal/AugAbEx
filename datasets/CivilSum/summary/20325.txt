Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Central Services Pension Rules, Rules 33 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 9(21) - Pay scales - Pension - A new formula for calculation of pension and revision of pay scales was implemented from 3061987.
Employees who retired from 111986 to 3061987 were given the choice to have their pension calculated under existing rules or under the revised rules, whichever was more advantageous.
However, the claim of employees to have new pay scales but old formula of calculating pension cannot be accepted, even if it is more beneficial.
The decision in 1997(3) SCT 722(SC) is distinguished.
