According to Rajasthan Civil Services (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1970, Rules 2(3)(vii), 3, 12, a government employee undergoing angiography and angioplasty in a private hospital during an emergency situation is entitled to reimbursement of medical expenses.
This is because the saving provision contained in Rule 12 relaxes the provisions contained in Rule 3.
The government cannot insist that employees only receive treatment in government-recognized institutions in such situations.
The reimbursement will be at the rates applicable in government-approved hospitals.
