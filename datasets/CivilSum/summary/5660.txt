Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 - Regularisation - Temporary appointment against leave vacancy - The respondent's services were terminated when the lecturer on leave rejoined the university - In response, the respondent filed a suit to prevent the university from terminating their services - The appeal ultimately granted the respondent's application - However, the specific questions at issue should have been addressed in the main suit rather than in the present proceedings - Therefore, the petition is allowed, the impugned order is set aside, and the plaintiff's application under Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 is dismissed.
