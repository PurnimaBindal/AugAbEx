Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F - Reinstatement of daily wager and seasonal worker - Burden of proof is on the workman to establish that they have worked for more than 240 days - Labour Court did not address the appellant's argument that the workman had not reached 240 days due to the end of the season - The order has been overturned, and the case will be reconsidered.
