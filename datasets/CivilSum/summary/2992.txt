Constitution of India, Article 226, 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25F - Termination of Workman - In this case, a workman was terminated due to her failure to pass a type test, despite having multiple opportunities to do so.
However, it was found that the condition of passing the test was relaxable and therefore the termination amounted to retrenchment.
As the provisions of Section 25F were not followed, the termination was declared void ab initio by the Labour Court, and this decision was upheld.
[Paras 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8 and 14]

Constitution of India, Article 226, 227 - Writ Jurisdiction - The scope of writ jurisdiction is limited.
The High Court will not interfere with a decision unless there is evidence of error apparent on the face of record, the decision is based on no evidence, or the findings of fact appear perverse.
In this case, the award of the Labour Court was found to be free of any such errors and the writ petition was dismissed accordingly.
[Paras 15, 16]
