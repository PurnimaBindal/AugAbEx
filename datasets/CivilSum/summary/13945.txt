Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - 5% posts of Assistant District Attorneys to be filled from amongst ministerial staff - Amendment to recruitment rules cannot be applied retrospectively to deprive eligible employees of their promotion rights against available vacancies prior to the amendment - Petitioners' case to be considered for promotion against 5% quota earmarked for ministerial staff out of total vacancies available prior to the amendment.
