(Gynaecology) and Diploma Course Group-I - Interview - Selected candidates for both courses were called for a single interview, without separate interviews being mentioned in the interview letter - Petitioners, who scored higher than some selected candidates for the diploma course, demanded admission to the diploma course - If a candidate applies for both degree and diploma courses, the interview marks for one course can be considered for the other - The respondents did not dispute the petitioners' higher marks, and so the petitioners were entitled to admission to the diploma course - The court directed the respondents to regularize the admission of petitioners who were admitted to the diploma course by virtue of an interim court order - The Constitution of India, Articles 226/227.
