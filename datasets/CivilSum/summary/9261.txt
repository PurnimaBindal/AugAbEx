Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Selection/Appointment Direct Appointment/Amendment of rules/Qualification/Eligibility/Advertisement of posts - The advertisement of posts only serves as a notice to the public.
Issuing this advertisement does not grant the right to applicants to be appointed or selected, and it can be withdrawn or altered at any time.
The qualifications may also be changed if necessary, and the authority may choose to readvertise the post accordingly.
Selection alone does not guarantee the right to be appointed, and if the appointment rules are amended before the process is completed, the appointments will be governed by the new rules.
There are rules regarding promotion of a person working on a lower post, but in the case of direct recruitment, the occurrence of a vacancy does not guarantee consideration unless the authorities decide to do so.
[Paras 7, 8, 11, 12 and 14]

Constitution of India, Article 14 and 16 - Appointment/Ad hoc appointment/Selection - Ad hoc appointments serve as temporary arrangements and do not grant rights against a post.
[Para 16]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Qualification / Eligibility - Comprehensive test - The State may lay down a uniform test of required minimum standard to ensure fair selection of personnel who pass the test.
This action cannot be considered arbitrary.
