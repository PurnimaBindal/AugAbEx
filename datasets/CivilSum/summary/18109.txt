The petitioner, who is physically handicapped with a degree of disability of 75%, was working as a Junior Engineer at Uttar Haryana Bijli Vitran Nigam.
The government's instructions from 31.01.2006 were adopted by the Nigam, which directed them to retain the petitioner in service until the age of 60 years.
The writ petition was successful.
