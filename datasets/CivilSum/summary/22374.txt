Civil Service Regulations, Regulations 351-A and 470 - Initiation of inquiry proceedings - Recovery - The government is legally authorized to recover any lost funds from the pension and gratuity of a guilty employee, once the delinquent is placed under suspension or charge-sheeted.
Even if a stay of suspension or superannuation occurs, the disciplinary proceedings are still deemed to have been initiated.
[Paras 9 to 15]  B Civil Service Regulations, Regulations 351-A and 470 - Sanction of Govt. - Unless the proceedings were initiated while the delinquent was on duty, an Authority other than the Governor must obtain Govt. sanction to initiate proceedings.
[Para 12] C. Civil Service Regulations, Regulations 351-A and 470 - Recovery from pension - Natural justice - To recover losses from a pension, the employee/pensioner must be given the opportunity to respond in a regular inquiry.
[Paras 15 & 18] D. Civil Service Regulations, Regulations 351-A and 470 - Recovery from pension - Initiation of inquiry proceedings - Regulation 351-A permits proceedings to be continued after an employee retires, provided that they were found guilty of misconduct either in departmental or judicial proceedings.
