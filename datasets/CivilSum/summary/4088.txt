When an inquiry authority's reports are not made available to employees before filing appeals, it raises the question of whether they should have been given a chance to be personally heard, even if there are no rules or regulations stating so.
In this case, the inquiry report was provided to the petitioners for the first time after the disciplinary authority's orders.
As a result, the appellate authority should have heard the petitioners in person before making a decision, which was not done.
Thus, the orders passed by the appellate authority were set aside, and they will need to rehear the appeals with petitioners being given a chance for a personal hearing.
This was outlined in the Constitution of India, Articles 226/227, and Punjab National Bank Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1977, Regulation 17(ii).
