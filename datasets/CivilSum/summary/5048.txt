Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission to B.Ed.
Course - The cancellation of admission that violates the principle of natural justice is set aside for granting admission as per the orders.
Directions are issued.
[Paras 18 and 21]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - The admission granted after authorities have conducted scrutinies - It is held that after conducting three scrutinies, the respondent authorities cannot take advantage of their mistake that results in miscarriage of justice.
[Para 14]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Natural justice - Administrative action - Applicability - It is held that the authority should have issued notice to the petitioner before taking any harsh decision.
