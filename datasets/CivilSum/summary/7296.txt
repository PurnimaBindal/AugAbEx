Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Gujarat Educational Institution (Regulations) Act, 1984, Section 5 - Termination of service - Natural justice - Opportunity of hearing - Petitioner was appointed after an interview - The respondents cannot claim that the petitioner was not qualified on the date of appointment, as they did not fulfill the requirements of qualification in the selection process - The order of termination is set aside because the opportunity of hearing was not given.
