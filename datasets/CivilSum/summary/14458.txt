YP Malik, Advocate for State Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Appointment - Post of Constable - Petitioners were selected for the post of Constable.
Although all formalities were completed, appointment letters were not issued.
Respondents claim that petitioners did not join when verbally asked to do so and that there was a general ban on appointments.
However, in light of the appointment of other candidates selected along with the petitioners, the argument for a general ban on appointments is not valid.
Without any evidence that the petitioners were verbally requested to join, it cannot be assumed as valid.
The offer of appointment should have been in writing.
The respondents are to appoint petitioners as Constables.
[Paras 2, 3, 4 and 5]
