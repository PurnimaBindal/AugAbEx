Tamil Nadu Warehousing Corporation General and Staff Regulations, 1965, Regulation 14(2) - Domestic Enquiry - Dismissal - The charges against the petitioner include misuse of office, irregularities in appointment, receipt of illegal gratification etc. - The petitioner did not explain after the charge memo was served - The petitioner failed to cross-examine all witnesses except for two on earlier occasions - The later request of the petitioner to cross-examine other witnesses was declined by the Enquiry Officer - The statements given by witnesses were true and voluntary - The contention of the petitioner that the Enquiry Report was not furnished before imposing a penalty is not acceptable as Regulation does not provide for its supply before imposing a proposed punishment.
- The domestic enquiry conducted by the Enquiry Officer was fair - No infirmity in the order of Disciplinary Authority was found, and the order was confirmed by the Appellate Authority.
