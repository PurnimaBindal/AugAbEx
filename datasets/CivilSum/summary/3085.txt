Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 226 - Model Standing Orders, Clause 14(4)(c) - Dismissal - Writ jurisdiction - Failure to comply with requirement of clause 14(4)(c) of Model Standing Orders while imposing punishment of dismissal - Resulted in violation of Article 21 of the Constitution of India and deprivation of livelihood for the petitioner employee - Employee has the right to approach the High Court under Article 226 of the Constitution of India for enforcement of fundamental rights under Articles 14 and 21 of Constitution of India.
