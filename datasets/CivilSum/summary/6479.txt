Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Petitioner's claim for promotion - Delay and laches - Petitioner contends that he fulfilled the benchmark for promotion - Petitioner was allowed to cross the efficiency bar by a civil court's order that set aside a prior order denying the same - Petitioner argues that the benchmark for crossing the efficiency bar and promotion is the same - Promotion was denied by an order dated 3011981 and efficiency bar by an order dated 151981 - The court held that the petitioner's contention could only be justified if the claim for promotion had arisen subsequent to the claim for efficiency bar - As the petitioner did not challenge the orders earlier, he cannot impugn the actions of the respondents after a decade - Hence, the petitioner's claim is beyond the limitation period.
