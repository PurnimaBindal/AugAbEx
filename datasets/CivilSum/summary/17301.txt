Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - Salary - Withheld - The petitioner claims she has not been paid regularly nor given posting in any ITI in Kashmir division, despite clear government orders - Withholding pay without justification is a form of punishment - It is improper to prevent a government servant from fulfilling their duties - Without proper posting, it is not appropriate to continue an employee - The petitioner should be given a posting as a Junior Instructor in accordance with their status, and their unpaid earned salary should be promptly disbursed.
