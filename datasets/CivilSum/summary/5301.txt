According to Section 3/4 of the Rajasthan Police (Incitement of Disaffection) Ordinance 1979, employees can be dismissed without enquiry.
The petitioner argues that similarly situated individuals involved in agitation were reinstated, and that they were acquitted in their criminal case.
However, there is nothing in the record that indicates that the petitioner's service record was inadequate.
In light of the facts and circumstances of the case, it is held that the order of removal cannot be justified, especially given that the petitioner has already surpassed the age of superannuation.
As a result, directions have been given to reinstate the petitioner with 50% back wages.
