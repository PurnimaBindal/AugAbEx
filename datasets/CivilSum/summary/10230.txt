Rule 5 of the Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970 pertains to punishment and double jeopardy.
According to this rule, the disciplinary authority can not only reduce the pay of the delinquent but also issue a direction regarding the increment that will accrue during the period of punishment.
Such punishment does not amount to double jeopardy.
[Paras 6 and 8] B.
The punishing authority in this case had given the delinquent an opportunity to be heard and had informed him of the fact-finding inquiry and its prima facie view.
Therefore, it cannot be claimed that the matter was prejudged.
