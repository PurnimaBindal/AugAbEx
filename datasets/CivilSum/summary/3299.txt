Constitution of India, Article 14 - Appointment on compassionate ground - Ban on appointments - Petitioner's husband was a Junior Assistant who died during service - Petitioner applied for appointment as a Clerk on compassionate ground - Ban on recruitments is not applicable in the case of compassionate appointments - Petitioner to be appointed within three months on a suitable post, as a vacancy arose after the ban and expenditure on such appointment will be less than what was being drawn by the deceased.
