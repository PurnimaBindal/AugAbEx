Jammu and Kashmir Civil Service Regulations Volume II Schedule II, entry No. 59 - Appointment - Inferior post - Retirement - Legality of - Petitioner was appointed as Bearer-cum-Chowkidar mentioned as Inferior post in Schedule II prior to 10101996 - He could have retired at the age of 60, but respondents-State Govt. retired him at the age of 58 years - The decision was illegal and set aside - Constitution of India, Article 226.
