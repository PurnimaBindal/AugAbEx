Suresh Monga, DAG, Haryana, cites Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 and Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 12.21 and 193, in a case regarding the discharge order of a Police Constable who failed to pass a certain test.
The Superintendent of Police recommended the case of the petitioner for another opportunity to pass the test.
The petitioner was deputed for further training when the next examination was to be conducted and continued in service until October, 1990.
The court orders that the petitioner be given another chance to pass the failed paper and if successful, deemed in service with no arrears paid if unsuccessful.
[Paras 2, 3 and 4]
