Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb), 25-F - Workman - Termination of Service - Award of reinstatement - Service terminated without complying with mandatory provisions - Labour Court found the termination to be in violation of Section 25-F of Act and ruled that the burden lies on employer to prove that the case does not fall under the exception clause - No evidence provided by department to support this claim - Case falls within the term termination due to absence of any information regarding period/nature of work in the appointment letter - Award is upheld.
[Para]
Award - Challenge by filing writ petition - Delay and laches - Writ Petition filed after 1-1/2 years of award - Petitioner did not pursue the matter in a timely manner and did not provide any explanation for the delay - Sadasivaswamy's case clearly states that the aggrieved party must move the court within six months or at most within one year from the date of occurrence of the cause of action.
