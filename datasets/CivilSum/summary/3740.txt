The Constitution of India, Article 226, calls for the regulation of services of contract labourers.
However, a writ petition cannot be maintained in a High Court under Article 226 of the Constitution.
Furthermore, no interim order may be passed to maintain the status quo regarding their employment with the employer until the dispute is decided by the Industrial court.
