The Tribunal directed that the post of Executive Engineer be filled in proportion with 2/3rd from Assistant Executive Engineers and 1/3rd from Assistant Engineers according to the rules, however, the rules do not require a consistent maintenance of the total strength of the cadre of EE's in such a manner that 2/3rd cadre is manned by those promoted from the post of AEE's and 1/3rd by those promoted from the post of AE's.
The expression 'posts to be filled' used in the rule indicates that wherever a selection is made to the post of EE, the criteria and essential minimum qualifying service must be strictly adhered to.
Any promotion in excess of the quota would result in the breakdown of the prescribed quota.
The ratio of 2/3rd and 1/3rd cannot be interpreted in a way that all 58 posts of EE's for the year 1997-98 schedule be filled in by promotion from the cadre of AE's only, which will break down the ratio prescribed in the rules, causing one cadre to get excess quota.
