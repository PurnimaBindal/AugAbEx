Constitution of India, Article 14 - Probationer - Termination of services during probation - Compliance with natural justice - Enquiry into the charges is essential - Even if some serious allegations are made against a probationer, termination during probation without any notice or enquiry cannot be invoked once a charge sheet is issued - The charge sheet must be taken to its logical end by conducting an enquiry and giving opportunity to the employee.
[Paras 6, 8, 11 and 12]
