Punjab Tehsildari Rules, 1932, Rules 8 and 6(i)(b) - Punjab Financial Commissioners Stand Order No. 12 para 37(ii) - Promotion to the post of Naib Tehsildars - The Standing Orders of Financial Commissioner found in the Land Administrative Manual serve as guidance on the subject matter, and if there are no eligible candidates from the Kanungos cadre for Naib Tehsildar vacancies, the Financial Commissioner has the authority to fill the positions on an ad-hoc/officiating basis by transferring Ziledars until eligible candidates become available.
