Constitution of India, Article 311 - Panchayat Samiti and Zila Parishad Rules, 1965 - Rule 3 - Appointing Authority/Competent authority - Appointment of Panchayat Secretary - Despite an irregularity in the appointment of a Panchayat Secretary made by the Panchayat Samiti under 1965 rules, the appointment was deemed valid as it was made and approved by the competent authority.

Service/Notice - Service of notice - Refusal to accept - The validity of a termination order cannot be established solely by a report of an interested peon that the official concerned has refused to accept the notice.
The absence of any reference to the notice or its refusal in the termination order suggests the possibility that the report was manipulated.
