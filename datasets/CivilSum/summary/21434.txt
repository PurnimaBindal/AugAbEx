The Indian Forest Services (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1966 states that the Selection Committee will choose candidates for IFO appointments.
For the years 2003, 2004, and 2005, the Selection Committee picked eight candidates.
However, they did not fill the one vacancy for 2006 because there was no proposal from the State Government.
After the parties reached a consensus, the Selection Committee was ordered to convene a meeting to consider the case of respondent-I.
These directions were issued in paragraphs 3, 10, and 11.
