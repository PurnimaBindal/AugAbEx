Section 115 of the Civil Procedure Code and Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India allow for disciplinary action against a judicial officer for an erroneous finding recorded in a judgment, which can be set aside in revision.
However, disciplinary action cannot be initiated unless there is more than just the erroneous finding, as clarified by the case reference of AIR 1996 SCW 2098.
