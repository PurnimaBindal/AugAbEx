The Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 2(e), 2(f) stipulate that there must be a relationship of master and servant between the employer and the employee, and that the requisite control of the master over the servant must be present.
The Punjab Khadi Mandal is not entitled to reject the cloth woven by members, and the members are entitled only to remuneration corresponding to the cloth woven by them.
Members are not compelled to take the yarn and do the work, as it is entirely their discretion.
There is no evidence to show any kind of supervision or control of the petitioner over the weavers.
[Paras 4 and 5]

Punjab Khadi Mandal is a non-profitable organization intended to help poor weavers have their cotton industries in their own villages.
Weavers take cotton yarn to their respective homes in villages where they weave cloth and bring it to Khadi Mandal, which pays them remuneration according to work done by them.
There is no control or supervision by the Mandal about the work done by weavers.
There is no question of rejection of cloth or supervision by petitioner over weavers, and there is no question of compelling them to do the work.
As such, held -Weavers who take the cotton yarn to their homes for weaving the cloth from Punjab Khadi Mandal are not employees of Khadi Mandal within the meaning of Section 2(f) so as to attract provisions of the Act.
The petitioner cannot be directed to contribute towards the provident fund.
[Paras 4, 5 and 6]
