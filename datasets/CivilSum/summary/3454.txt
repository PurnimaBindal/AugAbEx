Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, Hindi Sahitya Sammelan Act, 1962, termination, education, diplomas and degrees' validity - Hindi Sahitya Sammelan, Allahabad is not recognized or conferred with a deemed university status by any statute of the State of UP - Neither Hindi Sahitya Sammelan, Allahabad nor Hindi Sahitya Sammelan, Prayag is an established body for awarding degrees, diplomas, or certificates in any education - Any certificates or degrees issued by these institutions are fraudulent and cannot be used for securing public service jobs - Termination of services based on these false degrees is justified - U.G.C must take action against these bogus institutions and provide a report on their actions.
