Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 13.7 - Punjab Police Act, 1861, Section 12 - Promotion - Restructuring of cadres - Male and Lady Constables in Haryana Police were formed as separate cadres, but were later reconstituted into a single joint cadre, with Lady Constables, Lady ASIs, etc. to be placed according to their respective dates of joining and promoted in turn.
Any executive order which prejudiced an employee's rights could only be prospective in nature, and promotions made during this interim period could not be affected.
The decision to restructure the cadres and promotions conforming to established rules could only be annulled prospectively, and therefore the Order of DIG dated 971993 was held to be operative only in a prospective manner.
