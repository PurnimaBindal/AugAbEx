Transfer - Administrative grounds - Even officers and employees belonging to a region has no right to insist on being posted at a particular place - The respondent has a transferable post and has no choice in the matter of posting - The respondent's transfer to Dimapur was justified because he has no legal or statutory right to claim his post at Agartala.
[Para 7]
Transfer - Administrative grounds - Interference with the transfer of a public servant made on administrative or public interest grounds is only allowed for strong and compelling reasons rendering the transfer order improper and unjustifiable - The respondent's claim for a choice posting cannot be accepted - The impugned order of the Tribunal is not sustained.
