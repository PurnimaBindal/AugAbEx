Punjab Agricultural Produce Market Act, 1961, Sections 3(5)(e), 12(4) and 15 - Removal of Chairman of Market Committee on the ground that he was convicted in criminal offence under Section 307 IPC and Arms Act - Removal set aside - The said offences did not involve moral turpitude - A criminal case conviction in a Section 3(5)(e) is not a disqualification unless the offence involves moral turpitude - Moral turpitude is not involved in Section 307 IPC or even murder.
[Paras 8 and 9]  B. Punjab Agricultural Produce Market Act, 1961, Sections 3(5)(e), 12 and 15 - The meaning of moral turpitude - Offences under Sections 307 and 302 IPC do not involve moral turpitude - (1) The term "moral turpitude" refers to the baseness of character, which is a question of fact and depends on the public morals of the time, common sense of the community and context and purpose for which the character of offence is to be determined.
(2) The term "moral turpitude" is a vague one which means a conduct that is contrary to justice, honesty, modesty or good morals and goes against what a person owes to their fellow person or society in general.
The gravity of punishment is not considered when determining whether the misconduct involves moral turpitude or not.
