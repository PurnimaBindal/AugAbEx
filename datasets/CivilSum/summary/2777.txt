Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21, 73 and 162 - Employment in Govt. service against acquisition of land - Any assurance of employment for the successors of land owners can't be given as it would contravene Articles 14 and 16 by completely excluding others from the right to compete - Land owners are only entitled to compensation - The State can provide a reasonable policy for cases of hardship.
[Paras 19 to 22]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Delay and laches - A writ petition filed after nine years of land acquisition for employment against it is liable to be dismissed on the grounds of delay and laches.
[Para 25]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Territorial Jurisdiction of writ Court - The High Court Delhi held maintainable a writ petition alleging that employment assurances for family members of land losers were made by the respondent at their Head Office in Delhi where the cause of action arose.
