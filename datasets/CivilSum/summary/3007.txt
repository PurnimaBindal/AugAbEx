Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F and 25-G - Petitioners completed 240 days in a calendar year - Respondents unilaterally decided later to pay Rs. 35/- per day - Termination without following provisions of Section 25-F and 25-G of Industrial Disputes Act - The last order of termination was illegal and the petitioners shall be considered as continuous in service and should receive all the related benefits.
