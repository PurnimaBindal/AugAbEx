Constitution of India, Article 16 - Compassionate ground of appointment - Compassionate appointment is not a rule, but an exception to the normal mode of selection to any office under the State.
The scheme announced by the authority is in the nature of a concession.
As per amended rules, a minor adopted son of a deceased employee of the Electricity Board is not eligible for compassionate appointment.
