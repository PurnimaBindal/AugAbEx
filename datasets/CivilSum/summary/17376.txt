According to Section 4-A of the Workmen's Compensation Act, the legal representatives of a deceased worker who died in the course of employment are entitled to natural justice in the form of representation in any award given.
In the given case, an order was passed without giving the petitioner an opportunity to make a representation as to whether any amount was due to the Company.
This order is not sustainable, and the respondent is directed to give notice and hear the petitioner before passing any order, such as a garnishee order, against him.
No amount shall be disbursed out of the sum deposited by the petitioner with DC to claimants pursuant to the award passed against the Company.
