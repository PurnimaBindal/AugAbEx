Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(j) - Industry - Welfare functions of the state - As per the Constitutional mandate of Directive Principles to be a Welfare state, the functions of public welfare of the State is a sovereign function.
Irrigation and Telecommunication Departments do not fall under the category of an ‘Industry’.
[Para 3]
Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-f - Closure - Project workers - Daily wage workers working on a project do not hold any post or right to protection under the provisions of ID Act.
On the closure of the project, they are not eligible for any protection.
