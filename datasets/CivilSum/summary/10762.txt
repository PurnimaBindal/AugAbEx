Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised Pay Scale) Rules, 1969, as revised in 1998 - Rajasthan Agriculture Subordinate Service Rules, 1978, Rule 5 - Revision of selection grade - Amendment of rules and sustainability - Petitioners were granted selection grade in 1989, but became ineligible for promotion in 1991 due to newly prescribed educational qualifications - Held, the amendment cannot be given retrospective effect, and the selection grade granted under existing rules cannot be taken away as it would violate their crystallized vested right - No statutory rule or administrative order can eliminate such right and no new rule can affect or impair it - Thus, the action of taking away selection grade is arbitrary, discriminatory, and against Articles 14 and 16 of Constitution.
