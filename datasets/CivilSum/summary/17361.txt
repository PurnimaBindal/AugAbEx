Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Disability Pension - Claim for - Widow of Haveldar claiming disability pension - Petitioner's husband found fit and was enrolled - Despite being a normal person until 1989, he acquired an academic qualification of a law degree during his military career, and his work was found to be satisfactory - His disease, "psychosis," was found to be attributable to Military Service - While a person may become frustrated due to strict disciplinary service and afflicted with mental illness, the disease was not constitutional - The petitioner is entitled to disability pension from the date it became payable.
