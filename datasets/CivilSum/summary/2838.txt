The validity of Rules 7.5(1) of Volume I Part I and Rule 3.17-A(1)(v) of Volume II Part I under Articles 14, 21, and 300-A of the Indian Constitution is not ultra vires.
The classification between different types of retirement and resignations is based on rational thinking with a connection to their objective, and they have constitutional validity.
The objective here is to protect public interest by sustaining discipline in public service, and voluntary resignations are against public interest and cannot be granted pensions.
