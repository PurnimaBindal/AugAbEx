Constitution of India, Article 26 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Termination - Probationer - Termination of probationers on the basis of unsatisfactory service during probation is not equivalent to retrenchment and no inquiry is necessary in such cases - The Labor Court was mistaken in finding the worker's termination improper and ordering reinstatement with backwages when the corporation had presented sufficient evidence to justify it - The order to terminate was upheld.
