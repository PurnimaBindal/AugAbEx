Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - State Bank of India Act, 1955, Section 43 - State Bank of India Officers (Determination of Terms and Conditions of Service) Order, 1979 - Discrimination - Medical Officers, who form a separate class with different employment nature and hours, are not entitled to certain benefits available to regular employees of the Bank - Reasonable classification between two sets of employees - Petitioner's claim for benefits rejected - Articles 14 and 16 of Constitution of India not violated.
