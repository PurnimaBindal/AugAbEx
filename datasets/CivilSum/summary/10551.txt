According to Section 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, if a workman is removed from service after an invalid enquiry, and is subsequently reinstated with back wages, the Labour Court must determine if the workman remained unemployed during the idle period.
In a case where the Labour Court failed to provide any such findings while awarding back wages at the rate of 40%, the order regarding back wages was set aside.
