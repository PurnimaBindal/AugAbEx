Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination Retrenchment compensation - The Labour Court issued an award that a workman was terminated without following procedure of Section 25-F of the Act and ordered his reinstatement with continuity of service and 50% of back wages.
Management challenged this award and wants to terminate the workman's services.
If they do so, it would be regarded as an unfair labour practice.
Section 25-F(ii) of the Act would be applied and the workman would be entitled to retrenchment compensation.
