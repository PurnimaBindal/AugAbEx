Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Resignation - Withdrawal of acceptance - Validity and nature of resignation - If resignation is made due to employer-created frustration, its background, context, tenor, and language must be considered before determining its validity - Failure to do so renders such resignation meaningless - When a rule requires three months' notice or three months' pay deposit, an irregular resignation cannot be accepted without recording special reasons - Acceptance or refusal to withdraw such a resignation is illegal and arbitrary.
