Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Interpretation of - Bonus and incentive bonus - The workman can claim not only wages but also other benefits that are computable in terms of money according to any agreement, Rule or law - This means that the workman can claim Bonus, incentive bonus and Provident Funds as well under Section 33-C(2).
