According to UP Service of Engineers (Buildings and Roads Branch) Class II Rules, 1936, Rules 3(b), 23, 4, 19 and Constitution of India, Articles 309, 226, the seniority of an engineer appointed substantively to the temporary post in a cadre should be determined from the date of his appointment, regardless of whether he has been confirmed or not, and he should be considered a member of service.
