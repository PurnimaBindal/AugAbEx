According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India and Punjab Municipal Corporation Pension and General Provident Fund Rules, 1994, Rules 2(k) and 3, an employee of the previously existing Municipal Committee in Amritsar, which later became Municipal Corporation, Amritsar, is eligible for pension through the definition of "qualifying service" as per 1994 Rules used to calculate pension for such employees.
