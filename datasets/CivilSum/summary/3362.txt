Jammu and Kashmir Civil Services Classification, Control and Appeal Rules, 1956, Rule 14 - Adhoc Appointment - Natural Justice - Individuals who were appointed without following due procedure have no right to claim procedural safeguards when orders of termination are passed.
[Para 7]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Any appointment made without advertising the position and offering everyone a fair chance to participate is a violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
