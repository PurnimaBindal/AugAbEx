The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Appointment - Post of Head Constable - Candidature cancelled due to non-disclosure of involvement in a criminal case - Court held that the non-disclosure was an inadvertent error and not willful concealment, and therefore, the order was quashed and directions were given to proceed with the case of the petitioner.

The Constitution of India, Article 14 - Post of Head Constable - Non-disclosure of involvement in a criminal case - Candidature of other applicants with similar non-disclosure condoned, but petitioner's case was not - Court held that though Article 14 cannot be enforced as a negative covenant, the respondents' departure from their usual practice and approach in the petitioner's case lacked justification.
