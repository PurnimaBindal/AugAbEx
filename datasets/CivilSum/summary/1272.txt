Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-B - Conditions for awarding of wages - The fulfillment of Section 17-B's prerequisites obligates the Court to grant the wages last drawn by the worker from the date the employer petitioned the High Court or Supreme Court against the Labour Court's award in favour of the worker against termination, given that the worker filed a non-employment affidavit - The provision is compulsory.
[Paras 8 to 13] B Rajasthan High Court Ordinances, 1949, Ordinance 18 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-B - Judgment - Special Appeal - Letters Patent Appeal - Orders deciding a Section 17-B application from the ID Act is an appealable judgment under Ordinance 18 - A special appeal may be taken against such an order.
