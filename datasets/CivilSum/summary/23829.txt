Bihar Judicial Service (Recruitment) Rules, 1955, Rules 19 and 20 - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Bihar Civil Service (Judicial Branch) Ad-hoc Recruitment Rules, 1974 - Seniority - The appointees under the 1974 Rules cannot claim seniority over the appointees under the 1955 Rules against the already existing regular posts.
[Paras 12 and 17]  B Bihar Judicial Service (Recruitment) Rules, 1955, Rule 20 - Reservation - Seniority - The ad hoc appointees cannot claim seniority over the SC/ST candidates appointed against the already declared vacancies - On regularisation, the ad hoc appointees would rank juniors.
[Paras 18, 22 and 25]  C. Res judicata - Constructive res judicata - Seniority - The claim of the respondents as being senior to the appellants could not be rejected in the subsequent proceedings on the grounds of res judicata or constructive res judicata.
