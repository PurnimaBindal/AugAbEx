The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 state that ad hoc appointments for a limited period on a continuing post violate these articles.
An individual appointed on an ad hoc basis is entitled to hold the post until the regular appointee takes over and can compete in the regular selection process.
Additionally, termination of services based solely on the expiry of the fixed term when the post continues is arbitrary and unfair and can be quashed.
