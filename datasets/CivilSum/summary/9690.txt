The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(1), 18, 2(p) dictates that a settlement resulting in certain rights and liabilities cannot simply be an understanding.
[Paras 8, 9]
Under Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(1), 18, 2(p), the government cannot perform a judicial or quasi-judicial act when determining the lis of an industrial dispute.
Mere understanding or even settlement cannot amount to an adjudication of dispute.
[Paras 10, 17, 21, 22]
The change in wages amounts to a change in conditions of service for which workmen have the right to challenge.
Section 9-A cannot be violated by a change in wages without notice and a reference cannot be declined based on an alleged understanding.
