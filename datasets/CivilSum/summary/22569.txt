Constitution of India, Articles 226, 319 and 323 - Selection Commission - In case of any defect found in the conduct of selection, the Court can only set it aside and direct the commission or the appointing authority to redo the process of selection - Issuing directions to any other authority will be unconstitutional, as the Commission is the exclusive constitutional authority for selections.
[Para 5]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection and Recruitment - Lack of experience at the time of recruitment can be compensated by working on a post - Terminating the services of a candidate after years of experience on the grounds of lacking experience at the time of recruitment will have unpredictable consequences.
