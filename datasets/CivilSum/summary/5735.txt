The constitution of the Selection Committee under Article 226 of the Indian Constitution was challenged on the basis that the Member Secretary of the committee was not qualified as he was already nominated as a member of Rajya Sabha.
The teacher nominees or selected members were to take leave of absence during their term as Members but the resolution to ask the Member Secretary to take leave of absence could not be implemented due to a stay order passed by the court.
No arguments of bias or animus against the petitioner were made, nor was any material irregularity or proof of mala fides affecting the selection found.
Therefore, the decision of the Selection Committee cannot be interfered with, as the committees' actions were not at fault.
