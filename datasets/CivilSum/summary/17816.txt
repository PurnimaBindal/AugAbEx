Constitution of India, Articles 14,16 and 226 - Qualification - Appointment as JBT teacher Regularisation - The petitioner, who holds a two-year J.B.T.
Course qualification from the State of Rajasthan obtained prior to the date of de-recognition by the State of Punjab, must be considered as duly qualified and directed respondents to approve/regularise her case - Upon approval or regularisation of her services, she shall be entitled to all consequential benefits.
(Neelam Kumar v. State of Punjab, 1993 (1) RSJ 307 foll.).
