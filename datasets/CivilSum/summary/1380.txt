Employees' State Insurance Act (34 of 1948), Sections 53, 60 and 61 - Motor Vehicles Act (59 of 1988), Section 166 - Bar of benefits under other enactment - If an employee receives injuries while returning home from a factory in a vehicle provided by the employer, and the injuries could be considered to have been sustained during the course of their employment, employees of the factory covered under the Act must be aware that claimants availing ESI benefits from the factory are not entitled to compensation under the Motor Vehicles Act due to the bar contained in Sections 53 and 61 of the Act.
