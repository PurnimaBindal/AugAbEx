Andhra Pradesh Education Act, 1982, Section 89 - Termination - Appeal - If a Lecturer's services are terminated, an appeal may be made to the Director of Higher Education under Section 89(a) of the Act.
However, if the Director refers the appeal to the Government, their action is illegal, as the Director must hear and dispose of the appeal on its merits.
The Director is not authorized to refer the matter to the Government, and the Government is not authorized to hear the appeal.
This is because the right to appeal is a matter of substantive right vested in the aggrieved party and cannot be altered or scuttled by an executive action.
