Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 311 - Termination - Absence from duty - The petitioner's husband, a driver in Haryana Roadways, was injured in an accident and unable to perform his duty as a driver due to his fractured leg.
Despite his requests for alternative light duty being turned down and his subsequent absence from work, his voluntary retirement request was declined and his services were terminated.
The court found the authority's actions unjustified and substituted the termination order with acceptance of voluntary retirement, directing the release of retiral benefits and pension with arrears and grant of family pension to the petitioner.
