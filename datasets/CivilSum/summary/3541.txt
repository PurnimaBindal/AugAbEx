The Compassionate Appointment policy, framed by the State Bank of India in accordance with the Constitution of India's Articles 14, 21, and 226, has an objective of providing financial aid to the deceased employee's family.
The policy's goal is not to offer an alternative mode of employment or to create a new post, and appointments to public employment must follow Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
The authority can consider all sources of income, including interest on terminal benefits received from the employer, to determine whether the family has fallen into severe financial crisis when evaluating its financial position.
