As per Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, Govt. employees undergoing sterilization operation after having two or three surviving children are eligible for special incentives.
However, the condition of undergoing the surgery after the date of issuance of the orders does not provide a rational basis for classification.
Hence, all the individuals who have undergone sterilization or have a spouse who underwent the operation should be eligible for the special increment.
