Constitution of India, Article 14, 16 and 226 - Selection/Appointment - Screening test - Government employees of Haryana Roadways went on strike - After the strike was called off, dismissed employees were to be reinstated and striking appointees were to undergo screening tests, subject to eligibility - The petitioner had already been selected to the role, but was appointed during the strike on a contractual basis - To regularize the petitioner in their role, they cannot be required to take the screening test - The screening test is only necessary for those who were appointed without undergoing proper selection during the strike.
[Paras 2 and 6]
