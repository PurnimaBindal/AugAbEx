Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Osmania University Act, 1959 Section 38(1) - Conditions of service of University employees - Ensuring uniformity in service conditions of teaching and non-teaching staff can be a challenge due to the different nature of their duties and responsibilities - However, if it is necessary for the exigency of administration and impossibly impractical to maintain uniformity, the University has the authority to prescribe different age of retirement for the two categories of employees - Even though maintaining the same age of superannuation is not a compulsion under the law, the University is obligated to follow U/s 38(1) of the Act wherever possible and practical - The High Court's order to maintain the uniform age of 60 years is justified, and the University's appeal is dismissed.
