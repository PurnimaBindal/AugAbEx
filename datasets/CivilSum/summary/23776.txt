Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Uttar Pradesh Recognised Basic Schools (Junior High Schools) Recruitment and Conditions of Service of Teachers) Rules, 1978, Section 2(e) - Junior High School Payment of Salaries Act, 1978 - U.P.
Recognised Basic Schools, Recruitment and Conditions of Service of Teachers and other employees, Rules, 1975, Rule 10 - Payment of salaries to teachers in recognised schools - The Payment of Salaries Act, 1978 was introduced to address the issue of salary delays for teachers and non-teaching employees - The State's claim that the Act only applies to classes 6-9 and not classes 1-5 of primary sections is not valid - All teachers, regardless of the grade they teach, are entitled to the same payment treatment - Payment of Salaries Act, 1978 is applicable to teachers of primary sections from the date it came into force, but not retroactively - Teachers who have already received payment in accordance with the 1978 Act cannot be denied the same benefits.
