Railway Establishment Manual Vol.I Revised Edition 1989, Paragraphs 302 and 303 - Seniority - Promotion - Promotion to the post of Senior Clerk, which is a Class III post, must be done through selection.
Seniority in the categories of Class III posts, which are partially recruited through direct recruitment and partially through promotion, is determined from the date of joining the promotion post, not based on selection merit.
