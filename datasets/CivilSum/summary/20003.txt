According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India and Rule 8 of the Defence Quality Assurance Rules of 1979, a Junior Scientific Officer can be promoted to the Senior Scientific Officer position only if they have worked in regular service for three years and have a degree in Engineering.
The petitioners did not meet either of these criteria, as they were not Junior Scientific Officers on the date when the Rules were enforced and thus had no experience in the position.
Additionally, the requirement of possessing a degree for the Senior Scientific Officer Grade II post applied to both direct appointments and promotions, according to Schedule I to the 1979 Rules.
The plea to extend the same benefits to petitioners who were empanelled cannot be accepted since they could not be treated the same as those who were working as Junior Scientific Officers.
