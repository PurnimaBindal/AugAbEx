The petitioners in this case were part-time workers in libraries established by the government at Mandal Headquarters.
Despite being referred to as part-time in their appointment order, they were essentially working as regular employees.
The state cannot discriminate against these employees and must provide them with living wages on par with others in similar positions.
As a result, these employees are entitled to have their services regularized.
[Paras 11, 12 and 19]  B.
The third proviso to section 7(1) of the Andhra Pradesh (Regulation of Appointment to Public Services and Rationalisation of Staff Pattern and Pay Structures Act, 1994) which prescribes ten years for regularizing attenders working in libraries is considered unconstitutional under Articles 14, 21, 39(d) and 43 of the Constitution of India.
