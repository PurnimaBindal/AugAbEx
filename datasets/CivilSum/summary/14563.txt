Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(j) - Compulsory retirement - Public interest - Group Commandant of CISF - The petitioner was compelled to retire not for being useless to the service, but for being previously punished for various acts of misconduct.
The order was not based on any adverse confidential reports which could justify the official's removal.
The real intention of the authorities was to remove the petitioner discreetly.
Therefore, the order of compulsory retirement is unlawful and should be nullified, and the respondents should reinstate the petitioner with all the benefits.
[Paras 6 to 8]
