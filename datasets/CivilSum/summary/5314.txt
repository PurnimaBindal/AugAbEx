Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Equity - Admission to B.Tech.
Electrical Course - The petitioner attempted to mislead the authority by showing an incorrect percentage.
The university enforced the eligibility conditions against all concerned, and the petitioner misled the authority in getting admission.
Therefore, the petitioner is disentitled from claiming discretionary relief under Articles 226/227.
It is important for those who claim equity to present the Court with complete and true facts.
[Paras 12 and 13] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Cancellation of admission - The petitioner misled the authority in getting admission, and a show cause notice was issued.
The reply received was found unsatisfactory, and admission canceled as the petitioner failed to fulfill the eligibility condition.
It is not appropriate to exercise writ jurisdiction on misplaced sympathy.
The petitioner's application did not meet the prescribed percentage of the eligibility condition of a brochure.
Therefore, the Court dismissed the petition.
