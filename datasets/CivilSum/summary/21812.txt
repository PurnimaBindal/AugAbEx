The Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Act, 1970, Sections 10(1), 10(2) and 2(b) deal with the abolition of contract labour and the absorption of contract workers as permanent employees of the corporation.
The constitutionality of the Central Government's authority to prohibit contract labour from doing cleaning and sweeping work at the residential premises of staff or sports complex is being questioned.
The restricted scope attributed to section 10 of the Act given in Gammon may need to be decided independently, and a question has been referred to a Constitution Bench.
