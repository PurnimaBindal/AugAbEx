Constitution of India, Article 311 - Pepsu Service Regulations, Appendix No. 1 - Superannuation - The employee's retirement age is determined by the post held at the time of retirement.
If an employee initially joins a Class IV post with a retirement age of 60 years but subsequently holds a Class III post, he cannot continue until the age of 60 but will superannuate on acquiring the age of 58 years.
The determining factor is the status of the employee when the retirement order is passed.
A constable is considered a Class IV employee in the State of Punjab.
Therefore, the petitioner's retirement at the age of 58 years is appropriate.
