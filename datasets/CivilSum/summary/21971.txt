Under the Persons with Disabilities Act and Articles 16 and 39 of the Constitution of India, a policy decision was made to fill reserved posts for handicapped individuals through a Special Recruitment Drive.
However, the state has taken a conflicting stance by terminating the services of handicapped individuals.
This goes against both the policy decision and statutory provisions.
The state must fulfill their obligation to make reservations for handicapped persons and continue the services of those who were terminated with back wages and other benefits.
