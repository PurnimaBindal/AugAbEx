Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Rajasthan Transport Subordinate Service Rules (1963), Rule 2 - Recruitment - For the post of motor vehicle sub-inspector, the petitioners did not meet the required qualifications as stated in the advertisement - Specifically, they did not have the necessary licenses to drive a motor cycle, heavy goods vehicle, and heavy passenger vehicle - The court found that the petitioners should have properly read the conditions regarding educational qualifications before submitting their application - The court also held that it should not interpret or read between the lines of the conditions listed in the advertisement - Rejecting their candidature was deemed appropriate.
