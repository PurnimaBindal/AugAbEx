Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 36(4) - Pendency of proceedings before Labour Court - Employer represented by legal practitioner - Petition argued representative was not appearing as an Advocate but as a General Secretary of the Union - Lack of proof that he was on the payroll of the union makes it impossible to establish that he was an officer of the union - Therefore, he cannot represent the member of the said union - Order of Labour Court, disallowing the legal practitioner to appear on behalf of the employer, was validly upheld.
