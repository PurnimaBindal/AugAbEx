Constitution of India, Article 16 - Orissa Education (Recruitment and Conditions of Services of Teachers and Members of the Staff of Aided Education Institutions) Rules, 1974, Rule 2(h) - Appointment as teacher - The petitioner's provisional approval granted pursuant to directions issued by the High Court does not constitute appointment as a teacher as per the rules.
The approval only stands subject to appointment, which was not made in this case, rendering the order invalid.
