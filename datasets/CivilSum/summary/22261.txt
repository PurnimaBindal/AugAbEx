Constitution of India, Articles 16, 136 and 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2 - Termination of casual labour - A casual labour peon appointed on daily wages under Director Postal Services, Jaipur is entitled to be paid for days not worked (including Saturdays, Sundays and Gazetted Holidays), in the absence of material on record - However, the Tribunal's approach was unsound as no other basis for payment was examined and the issue of whether P&T Department qualifies as an industry was not discussed on merits - Therefore, the Tribunal's order was set aside and the matter was remitted for a fresh decision in accordance with the law.
