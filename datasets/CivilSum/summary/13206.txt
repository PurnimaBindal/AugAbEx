The petitioner sought a pro rata pension after serving in the army from 1972 to 1985.
However, the provisions of CCS (Pension) Rules and notification cited did not apply to army personnel, who are only governed by Pension Regulations for Army 1961 requiring 15 years of qualifying service for pension.
Additionally, the petitioner failed to provide an explanation for the inordinate delay, thereby resulting in the dismissal of the petition.
