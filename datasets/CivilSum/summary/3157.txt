Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal Pay for Equal Work - Operators working in Andhra Pradesh Beverages Corporation demanding pay scales at par with those of Govt. Department as per Pay Commission Report, 1986 - Different duties allocated to operators in different Departments of Govt. and Public Sector Undertakings - Complete parity cannot be assumed between operators - As an instrumentality of State, Corporation cannot deny right to equal pay for equal work - However, court cannot order equivalence of pay scales without adequate evidence of equivalence of duties - Equation of posts and pay are matters for executive and expert bodies - Corporation ordered to appoint Senior Officer to find parities and report, to be implemented retrospectively from petitioners' confirmation.
[Paras 2, 16, 17, 19 & 20]
