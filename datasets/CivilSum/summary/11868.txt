Constitution of India, Article 226 - Constitution of J&K, Section 103 - Competent authority - Ad hoc appointment - Post of Library Assistant - The Deputy Commissioner's ad hoc appointment of the petitioner to the post of Library Assistant is improper, as only the Competent Authority under the relevant regulations or rules has the power to appoint.
The petitioner's right will only accrue in her favor if the Authority concerned passes an order appointing her to the post.
If such an order is passed, she may then approach the Court under Article 226.
However, as of now, no such order has been passed, and therefore the Court is unable to aid the petitioner.
As a result, the petition is dismissed.
