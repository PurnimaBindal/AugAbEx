Constitution of India, Article 311(1)(b) - Departmental enquiry - Termination of services dispensing with the departmental enquiry - The competent authority must record its satisfaction based on objective fact and relevant material that it is not reasonably practicable to hold an inquiry before invoking the provisions of Article 311(2)(b) - In this case, the involvement of a police officer in a civil dispute of brothers did not warrant the application of these provisions - The authority did not appear to have made any independent enquiry, relying instead on recommendations made by other subordinate officers - The petitioner had a 36-year unblemished service record, and the authority was required to verify the allegations before passing the dismissal order - The Authority exercised the power under proviso to Article 311(2) without any application of mind - Therefore, the order of dismissal was set aside with liberty to hold enquiry in accordance with law.
