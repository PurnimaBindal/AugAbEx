According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India and the Central Civil Services (Extraordinary Pension) Rules, Rule 3-A, a constable of the Indo Tibetan Border Police was injured while returning to his duty on deputation to National Security Guard after a bus accident, to be treated as on duty.
The petitioner is eligible for disability pension and the respondents are ordered to consider his case.
