Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 39 - Equal pay for equal work - Qualifications - Revision of pay scales - Giving a higher pay scale only to those appointed on the basis of higher qualifications or with first or second class is not in line with the principle of "equal pay for equal work" - Employees who were appointed earlier and met the necessary qualifications at the time of appointment cannot be denied the higher pay scale for the same positions with the same duties - The authority must provide the petitioners with the revised higher pay scale from the applicable date with arrears.
[Paras 28 to 31]
