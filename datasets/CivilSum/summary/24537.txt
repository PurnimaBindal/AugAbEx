Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Natural justice - Departmental proceedings - The Bus Driver cannot argue that it can only be proved in a regular inquiry that there were passengers in the bus travelling without tickets since he did not stop the bus at the signal of the checking staff.
If the checking staff is prevented from entering the bus, the passengers cannot be checked until later, and thus administrative action can be taken against the driver for misconduct in not stopping the bus for checking.
[Para 8]  B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-B - Back Wages - Conduct of the workman - If a worker repeatedly engages in indiscipline and does not improve their conduct despite warnings, they should not be awarded back wages, even if their termination is found to be unjustified.
