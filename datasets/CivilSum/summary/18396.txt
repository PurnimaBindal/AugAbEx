The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, as well as Oriental Bank of Commerce Officer Employees (Conduct) Regulations, 1982, Regulations 4 and 8, deal with minor penalties and the challenge of such penalties.
The petitioner claims that their contribution towards PF and Gratuity were taken by the Respondents without any notice or opportunity.
However, according to Regulation 8, officials must be informed of their lapses in writing and given a chance to respond before a minor penalty is imposed.
As the bank did not follow this procedure, their action was deemed illegal and not justified, and the order was considered unsustainable.
