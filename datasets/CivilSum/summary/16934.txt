Constitution of India, Article 16 - Company Rules, Rules 1, 2, 5 and 6 - Probation - Extension of - Permissibility - A petitioner was promoted on 23101997 with a clause of probation for one year, and after completion of same no further order extending probation or confirming petitioner was passed.
However, on representation by petitioner, it was informed that his probationary evaluation report was far from satisfactory and thus extending probation for a further six months from 23101997.
Petitioner seeks confirmation on post from the date he completed one year probation.
As per Rule 2, probation cannot be extended to a period exceeding one year.
Rule 6 states that an employee rated below average if fails to show improvement can be terminated from service.
However, the petitioner never communicated any deficient rating, and the order extending probation has been passed in breach of Rule 2.
Hence, it is patently illegal and without authority of law.
Thus petitioner entitled to confirmation from 23101997, and the petition is allowed accordingly.
[Paras 6 and 7]
