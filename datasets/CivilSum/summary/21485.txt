The scheme for Rehabilitation under the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, for disabled individuals employed by a State Project Co-ordination Committee raised questions of employment status.
The terms and conditions of service for these employees were applicable to the State Government, even though the project was funded by the Central Government.
The Tribunal held that the Central Government was not the employer of these individuals.
After the project ended with no budgetary provision made, employees sought direction to either continue the scheme or be absorbed by either the State or Central Government.
The State cited financial constraints, but the responsibility to implement the 1995 Act ultimately falls on them.
Control of the projects was with the State Government, and the tests for determining the employer-employee relationship remained the functional, control, and organizational tests.
The service of these employees should be continued if projects are continued, or absorbed by the State, with temporary employment offered by the Central Government.
[Paras 11, 12, 17 to 19]
