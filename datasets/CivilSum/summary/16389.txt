Constitution of India, Article 172 - Executive action - Validity - State Government transferred all Govt. medical colleges to Autonomous Societies - Government retains overall control of the Societies - Recruitment of teaching staff does not require consultation with UPSC - The Board of Governors was constituted as the highest decision-making body - An order was also issued for the Government to frame model bye-laws - This was to be done by a committee nominated under a Government order, even before the foundation of the Society - The government's behavior was in violation of statutory provisions and the order was subsequently struck down.
