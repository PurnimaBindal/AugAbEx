Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 311 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control & Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 17 - The petitioners were subject to a minor punishment of stoppage of two grade increments without cumulative effect, despite no allegations of any misfeasance, misappropriation, or loss caused.
The Punishing Authority passed a non-speaking order and violated principles of natural justice.
The charge-sheet and statement of allegations did not involve any misconduct and could be cancelled at any time.
As a result, the charge-sheet and the order of punishment have been quashed, and the case of the petitioner for promotion to the post of Addl.
Chief Engineer will be reconsidered based on merit.
[Paras 19 and 20]
