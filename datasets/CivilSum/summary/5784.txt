According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the court must determine whether the petitioner has acted with gross negligence in pursuing their remedies in cases of delay and laches.
However, if the delay has not caused any harm to the rights of others, and it would be unfair to disrupt those rights, the objection of delay should not be considered.
[Para 4]
The Supreme Court case of Chaman Lal v. State of Punjab and the DB decision in Rattan Singh's etc. v. State of Haryana, 1995 (1)SCT 711 cover the claim for higher pay scale of teachers who acquire higher qualifications during service under Article 16 of the Constitution of India.
Additionally, the circular dated 931990 does not have retrospective effect.
Therefore, the petition is allowed.
