Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 and 310 - Promotion - Superannuation - When an employee accepts a promotion with the condition to revert back to their original position, and this conditional acceptance is not rejected, it is considered accepted with the conditions imposed by the employee.
The employer cannot later plead that the employee was not entitled to revert back, especially if they have continuously held their original position and have been paid the salary of that post.
If the age of superannuation for the promotion post was 58 years and the original post was 60 years, the employer cannot deprive the employee of retiring at the age of 60.
They will be deemed to have retired from their original post at the age of 60 and entitled to the consequential benefits except the salary for the period they do not physically work.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 and 136 - Scope of power of Supreme Court under Article 136 - Powers of the Supreme Court under Article 136 of the Constitution are unfettered and can be invoked as and when it reaches the conclusion that a person has been treated unfairly.
[Para 16] C Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 and 310 - Retirement - Date of superannuation - Conditions of appointment are either to be read from the appointment letter or from the record of posting and discharge of duties.
If the appointment letter does not indicate that the promotion was temporary or ad hoc or on a regular basis, and the post from which the promotion was contemplated was not abolished, then it will be deemed that the employee never accepted the promotion post, especially when it was contrary to their interest.
