Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation - Qualifications - Experience - Qualifications must be met at the time of induction into service but an employee cannot be denied regularisation and regular pay scale due to lack of qualifications after long experience on the post - Experience helps improve efficiency in service - Qualification requirements can be relaxed after allowing an employee to work on a post for more than 3 years.
[Paras 4 and 5]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regularisation - Discrimination - Relaxation of qualifications - If a junior employee has been given relaxation of the same conditions and regularised in service, the same benefits cannot be denied to the petitioner - Authorities directed to regularise the petitioner's services from the date his junior was regularised and provide all consequential benefits including arrears of pay and allowances.
