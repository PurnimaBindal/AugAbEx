Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 162 - Executive instructions of the State - All India GV Malvanker Shooting Championship, 1996 recognised by the National Rifle Association of India - Punjab Government instruction dated 481992 states the Championships organised by recognised national federations are accepted for admission to Punjab Engineering Colleges - The instructions of the Punjab Government will prevail over those of the Chandigarh Administration and National Rifle Association in matters related to Punjab Engineering Colleges - Once All India GV Mavlankar Shooting Championship 1996 is recognised by Punjab State, National Rifle Association authorities cannot reject the certificate after training, and the instructions of the Chandigarh Administration will not serve as precedent.
