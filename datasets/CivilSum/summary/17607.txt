Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Equal pay for equal work - Qualifications and eligibility - Promotion - Principle of 'Equal Pay for Equal Work' is not applicable unless the individual fulfills all required qualifications and is performing the same duties as regular incumbents of a higher post - A petitioner who was appointed as a Class IV Ward Servant in the ECG Department of PGI, Chandigarh and was performing the duties of Junior Laboratory Technician without possessing the necessary qualifications cannot demand the salary of the higher positions.
[Paras 7 and 10]
