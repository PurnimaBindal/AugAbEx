As per Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the appropriate Govt. for the purpose of reference is the State Govt. even if a company of the Central Govt., such as National Hydro Electric Project Power Corporation, is governed by its own constitution and has an independent legal entity.
This is because the corporation runs projects for its own purposes and, despite being owned entirely by the Central Govt., works under the authority of its own constitution.
