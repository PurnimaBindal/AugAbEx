The Jammu and Kashmir (Govt.
Servants Prevention of Corruption) Act, 1962 allows the findings recorded by the Commission to be used as the basis for passing an order of dismissal.
However, an order of dismissal was challenged on the grounds that the findings of the Commission were not made available.
The High Court concluded that the proceedings of the Commission were not liable to challenge in a civil court, but the Supreme Court remanded the case back.
In this case, appreciation of evidence was permissible, and the principle that findings are not to be appreciated and the High Court should not act as a court of appeal was held to be not applicable.
Ultimately, the delinquent's guilt was not established.
