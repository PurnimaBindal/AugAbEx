Punjab Tenancy Act, 1887, Section 84(4)(5) - Punjab Land Reforms Act, 1972 - Both the Commissioner and the Financial Commissioner have the power of revision under Section 84 of the Act regardless of whether they were approached by the department or any interested or non-interested party - Revisional powers provided by the statute are inherent and intended to correct errors committed by subordinate authority in passing orders under the Act.
[Para 9] B Revisional Jurisdiction - Jurisdiction exercised regardless of whether done suo motu or at the request of an aggrieved or concerned party - If the revisional authority determines after reviewing the records of a case pending or decided by the subordinate authority that the order needs modification or annulment, they may modify or annul the order within their jurisdiction - Punjab Tenancy Act, 1887, Section 84.
