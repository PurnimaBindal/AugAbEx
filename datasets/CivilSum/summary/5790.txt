Industrial Disputes Act (1947), Section 25F - Termination of service - Compensation - Workman's period of service for compensation calculation must be continuous - Any period of service, whether it was casual, temporary, regular or permanent, would be considered "employment" under Section 25F - Retrenchment compensation calculation can't ignore casual employment periods - Failure to comply with Section 25F - Ruling invalid due to this non-compliance.
