According to Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Schedule IV, Items 5, 6, 9 and 10, temporary employees are not entitled to the benefits of permanent employees.
However, an employee filed a complaint claiming permanency after having completed 240 days of service and the Industrial Court wrongly dismissed the complaint.
The employee is actually entitled to permanency from the date the complaint was filed.
