Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Interest/Res judicata - After disciplinary proceedings, the petitioner's salary was withheld.
However, this order was later quashed, but the salary was still unreasonably delayed.
A subsequent writ petition was filed and the petitioner was eventually awarded 12% interest.
[Paras 9 & 11] B. Gratuity - Delay in payment of gratuity - Since gratuity becomes due on the date of retirement, it is considered property from that date.
However, the payment was withheld due to disciplinary proceedings and the government utilized the amount during that time.
The petitioner is therefore entitled to 18% interest on the amount of gratuity for the period of delay.
