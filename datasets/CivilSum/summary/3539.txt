Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 - Premature retirement - Adverse remarks in ACR - Quarterly reports containing adverse remarks are not to be considered - Only annual confidential reports should be considered - A single entry regarding doubtful integrity in quarterly report serves as warning and advice for improvement - If complete annual report and subsequent ACRs are not produced despite repeated orders, adverse remark deemed to be not incorporated in ACR - Order of premature retirement based only on quarterly report cannot be upheld.
