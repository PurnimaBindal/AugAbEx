Gujarat Civil Service (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1971 : Rules 7, 18, 21 and 23 - The High Court has the authority to increase the punishment given by the District Judge to a Ministerial staff member following Rule 23 - The High Court's decision to increase the punishment falls under its administrative jurisdiction.

Articles 235 and 236 of the Indian Constitution dictate that the High Court has administrative control over both Judicial Services members and the Ministerial staff of subordinate courts - The ruling in 1977(2) SCC 12 is applicable.
