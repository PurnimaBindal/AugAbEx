Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2-J and 25-F - Termination - The Canal Patwaris petitioners were on work charge basis with their initial appointment being regularly extended with the last extension order expiring on a relevant date.
However, they were told to leave before the expiry of the last extension order, which goes against the mandatory provisions of Section 25-F, invalidating the impugned order.
[Para 1]
Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F and 10(1)(C) - Writ - Termination - Reference - Despite being eligible to seek reference under Section 10(1)(C) of the Act, the case of the petitioners remained admitted in the High Court from 1988 till date.
In light of the judgment in Phoolwati v. DC Rohtak by the D.B.
of High Court in 1986 PLJ 823, it would be unfair to send the petitioners to the Labour Court at this point, and hence the petition is granted.
