Article 39(d) and 14 of the Constitution of India is applicable to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33C(2) - Daily rated/casual workers performing the same work as regular employees are entitled to equal pay on the principle of equal pay for equal work - Article 39(d) is a fundamental right when read with Article 14 - Hence, the right to equal pay of these employees is their Constitutional right.
[Paras 5 and 6] A noteworthy point is that Daily rated/Casual workers are also entitled to the same wages as regular employees on the principle of equal pay for equal work.
B Section 33C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, governs Equal pay for equal work.
This provision allows an employee's guaranteed right to the principle from the day they were appointed, even if they are not claiming for the future, but also for the past.
[Para 22] C. Section 33C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and Articles 39(d) and 14 of the Constitution of India clarify Remuneration as the basic wage or salary and any additional emolument whatsoever payable either in cash or in kind.
Therefore, Daily rated casual workers are entitled not only to a salary equal to their regular counter-parts but also the allowances.
