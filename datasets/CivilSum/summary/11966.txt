Limitation Act, Section 5 - Condonation of delay - Explanation required - Official channels cited as reason for a delay of one year - AIR 1975 AP 109 states that the state cannot have special treatment in regards to the Limitation Act - Insufficient explanation for delay provided in case - Legislature grants no special limitation to the state - Application dismissed - Letters Patent Appeal also dismissed due to being time barred.
