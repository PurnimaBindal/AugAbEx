Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Education - Admissions - In the event that an eligible person was wrongly ignored in favor of an admission/promotion to a higher class due to a bona fide mistake of the institution, the wrongly ignored person may be given their due.
However, the admission of the person made by mistake should only be disturbed if it was obtained through fraud, or if they were ineligible or illegal.
