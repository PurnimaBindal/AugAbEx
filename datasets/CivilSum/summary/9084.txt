According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a dispute must be referred for validation, and in the context of termination of a workman's services, the dispute centered around whether the termination was justified or not.
In this case, there was no issue regarding the validity of the reference, and thus, the Labour Court could not reject the reference on the grounds that there was no dispute or that the reference could not be made.
As a result, the award was quashed and the case was sent for further review.
