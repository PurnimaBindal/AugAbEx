Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Compassionate appointment - The purpose of compassionate appointment is to aid the family in an unexpected crisis - Appointment on compassionate grounds is not a recruitment method, but a facility to swiftly rehabilitate the deceased employee's family and relieve them of destitution - Such appointment cannot be demanded as a right and must be granted within a reasonable timeframe.
[Paras 10, 15 & 17]
