The Notifications declaring certain posts in Labour Courts and Industrial Tribunals of Delhi Higher Judicial Service as 'cadre posts' were challenged on the grounds that they violate the rights of potential presiding officers.
However, it was held that the Notifications do not amend Sections 7 and 7(A) of the Industrial Disputes Act, and thus these 'cadre posts' can still be filled from those specified in the Act.
