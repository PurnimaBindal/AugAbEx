Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 - Airport Authority of India Act, 1994, Sections 1(3)(aa), 12(3), 9, 12(1), 12(2), 12(3), 12(3)(aa) - Challenge to action of privatizing airports - Petitioners contend that Section 12(3)(r) is inconsistent with Sections 12(1) and 12(2) - Held, Sections 12(1) and 12(3) are complementary and the authority can undertake privatization in its best commercial interest - No prejudice to legislative mandate of Section 12(1) - Petition dismissed.
