Air Force Promotion Policy of 1995, Clauses 10, 17 and 24 - Promotion to the post of Wing Commander - "Considerations" - According to Clause 10, an Officer who doesn't make a grade in three consecutive Promotion Boards must go before the Concurrent Review Board and will receive three more considerations.
If an Officer doesn't make a grade for promotion, including medical grade, after a total of six considerations, they are not entitled to any further consideration.
