Constitution of India, Articles 15 and 16 - Jammu and Kashmir Reservation Rules, 1994, Rule 38 - Regularisation - Temporary teachers in Adult/Non-formal education who are not part of regular teaching and do not hold any posts cannot claim regularisation of service - There is no provision for regularisation of this category of teachers in the Reservation Rules - Thus, they are not eligible for regularisation.
[Para 3]
Constitution of India, Articles 15 and 16 - Jammu and Kashmir Reservation Rules, 1994, Rule 38 - Equal pay for equal work - Temporary teachers in Adult/Non-formal education who are not part of regular teaching do not perform the same duties or hold the same posts as regular teachers of the Education Department - They cannot claim pay equal to that of regular teachers on the grounds of equal pay for equal work.
