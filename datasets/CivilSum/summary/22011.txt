The absorption of government servants in the Tamil Nadu Cooperative Milk Produce Federation which replaced the Tamil Nadu Dairy Development Corporation Ltd in 1981 left employees with no choice but to accept permanent employment with the Federation despite being government servants, and they cannot be deprived of their status as civil servants without their consent.
Terminal benefits are required to be paid to these employees, and GO 1921 provides reasonable benefits except for items 3(c) and 3(f), which have been struck down.
Respondent employees are entitled to family pension and future benefits of liberalised pension rules.
Other paragraphs in the case are upheld, and employees who retired after February 1, 1983 are deemed to have opted into the Federation and are entitled to terminal benefits according to GO 1921.
The appeal is allowed.
