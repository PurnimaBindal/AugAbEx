Constitution of India, Articles 311, 330-A and 14 - Border Security Force Rules, 1969 Rule 19 - Resignation - Pensionary benefits - A letter dated 4.11.1981 stated that under Rule 19, if resignation is accepted, BSF Forces members will not be entitled to prorata pensionary benefits unless resignation is deemed to be premature retirement and the corresponding benefits are allowed - This decision can be applied uniformly to all BSF employees - No pensionary benefits granted to any person who resigned under Rule 19 after the letter was issued but certain employees granted benefits before the letter - No discrimination.
[Para 5]  B Constitution of India, Articles 311, 300-A and 14 - Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 19 - Resignation - Pension Discrimination - A person who resigns under Rule 19 is not retiring from the post but can consider consequence of forfeiture of pensionary benefits - No claim of retirement or grant of pension for resignation under Rule 19 - No discrimination as some persons granted pension prior to Nov 4, 1981 but it does not confer right or claim for pension.
[Para 5]  C Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 300-A - Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 19 - Evidence Act, 1872, Section 115 - Resignation - Pension - Estoppel - No pensionary benefits granted when resignation accepted - Offer made to rejoin service but petitioner did not accept - No claim for pension benefits can be made and no estoppel applies to either party.
