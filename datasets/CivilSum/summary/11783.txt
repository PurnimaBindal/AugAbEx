According to the Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, under sections 2(e) and 14, as well as the Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, and the Electricity (Supply) Act of 1948 under Section 78-A, the employees of the Electricity Board are not considered to be government employees or hold posts under the State Government.
Although the Board has adopted Punjab Civil Services Rules, this does not make its employees government employees and they are still entitled to gratuity under the Act.
Application U/s 15 of the Act by the employees of the Board is therefore maintainable.
