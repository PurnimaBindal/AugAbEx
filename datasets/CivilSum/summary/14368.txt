Under Kerala University's First Ordinance of 1978, Ordinance 3, teachers may be appointed in colleges managed by private organizations based on their work load, with approval from the university.
The salary of such teachers will be provided by the government through the Director of Collegiate Education.
However, in case of doubts regarding the propriety of appointment, the Director of Collegiate Education takes the matter up with the university, whose decision is binding on the government.
