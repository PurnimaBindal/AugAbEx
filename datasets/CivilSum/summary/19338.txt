Constitution of India, Article 309 - Pay fixation for promotion - After being commissioned as a Flying Officer, the petitioner was entitled to ante dated promotion of two years, or two increments under Para 9 of Air Force Instruction 48 of 1975.
However, the Accounts Office denied payment on the grounds that the ante date period only applied to the time served as Pilot Officer before commissioning.
This interpretation would render the rule redundant, as every expression used must be given meaning and not be rendered useless.
The court ordered the Deputy Accounts Officer to refix the petitioner's salary, giving him two increments and paying him arrears within two months.
The petition was accepted with costs of Rs. 2,500/-.
