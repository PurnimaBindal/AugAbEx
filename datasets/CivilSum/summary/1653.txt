The State Authorities have a duty and obligation to protect and produce the service record of government employees in court when required.
Failure to produce the record will result in an adverse presumption against the state and in favor of the claimants.
[Para 8]

A work-charged employee is entitled to the status of a permanent employee after working for 10 years in the establishment.
Keeping an employee on a casual basis against a regular work charged post is arbitrary.
Once a worker has met the 10-year requirement, the employee is entitled to all benefits, including family pension.
[Paras 9 to 14B]

The Constitution of India acknowledges the assistance that the State should extend to its citizens in cases of old age, sickness, disablement, and unemployment.
Therefore, denying relief to an old illiterate widow of a government servant for the services rendered by her husband is unconstitutional.
Family pension was granted, along with arrears and an interest rate of 12% after 28 years.
