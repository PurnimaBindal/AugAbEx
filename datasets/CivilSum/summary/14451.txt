Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 14 - Suspension - Reinstatement - When an employee is suspended on the grounds of a criminal case, and is later acquitted, the suspension period must be deemed as on duty for all intents and purposes.
It cannot be considered as leave, thereby depriving the employee of wages for that period.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to all arrears of salary for the suspension period.
[Paras 5 and 6]
