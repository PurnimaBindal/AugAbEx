According to Section 4(6)(b) of the Payment of Gratuity Act (39 of 1972), an employee who has been dismissed for gross misconduct forfeits their gratuity.
The employee cannot argue that there was no specific order or separate proceeding for forfeiture of the gratuity.
Furthermore, even if the employee is acquitted in a criminal case, it will not affect the forfeiture of the gratuity.
