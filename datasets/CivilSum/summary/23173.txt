Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 311 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1930, Rule 49A(4)(a) - Suspension - After being convicted for misappropriation, the employee was dismissed.
Later he was acquitted and the dismissal was revoked.
However, he remained under suspension since the order indicated that enquiry would continue.
The High Court held that the employee was entitled to full salary since no decision had been made to hold a further enquiry.
However, this decision was set aside.
