Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 309 and 226 - Compassionate appointment/Disabled and invalid employees of Roadways Department - Haryana Government policy for readjustment of disabled employees on alternate posts or compensation if adjustment is not possible - Dependents can be appointed if post is available - Policies are independent and alternative, not cumulative - No double benefits can be claimed - Policies do not confer any indefeasible right - Govt. posts are not inheritable - Conferment of double benefits would be against public policy - Petition dismissed due to claim of compassionate appointment for sons after availing compensation under the other policy.
