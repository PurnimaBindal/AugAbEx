Under the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 3(1), 30, if a workman dies while on the job, it must be determined whether the death occurred due to an accident arising out of and in course of employment.
In a particular case, a workman suffering from a chest disease died of a heart attack while working a job that involved only switching on or off.
A doctor concluded that there was no stress or strain in his job, and therefore, the High Court rightly held that the death was not caused by any accident arising out of and in course of employment.
In such peculiar circumstances, the amount already paid shall not be recovered.
