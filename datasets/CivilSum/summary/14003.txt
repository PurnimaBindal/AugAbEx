According to Section 114 of the Evidence Act, 1872, there is a presumption of correctness in the residence certificate submitted by the petitioner who had applied for the post of constable under the category for residents of Chandigarh.
Even though his candidature was rejected on the ground that he did his Senior Secondary Examination from Haryana, he had applied under the category reserved for residents of Chandigarh for which only 12 seats were available.
It was noted that for the candidates from Haryana, 318 seats were available and thus, the petitioner did not gain any undue advantage by claiming himself to be a resident of Chandigarh.
