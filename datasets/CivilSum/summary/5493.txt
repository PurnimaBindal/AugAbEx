Bombay Civil Services Rules, 1959, Rule 152(3) and (5) - Pay - In cases of reinstatement, payment of salary for period of absence due to suspension, dismissal, etc. must be considered - When deciding not to grant full pay and allowances, principles of Natural Justice should be followed.

Bombay Civil Service Rules, 1959, Rule 152(3) - Pay - When a petitioner on corruption charges has their conviction reversed, the period of suspension may be considered spent on duty, and deemed promotion granted - The absence of pay and allowances for this period was deemed unjustified.
