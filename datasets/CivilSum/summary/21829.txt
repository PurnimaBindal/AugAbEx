Constitution of India, Article 32 - Beedi and cigar Workers (Conditions of Employment) Act of 1966, Section 1 - Writ petition by District Beedi workers' union - Complaint made by union regarding fabrication of employee records by Beedi manufacturing units, non-compliance with labour laws, etc. - The objections of the central government are invalid as it concerns the power of the state government - Supreme Court directs formulation of scheme by worker union and state government to implement labour laws, as per Para 4.
