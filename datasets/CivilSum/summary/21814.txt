According to Prevention of Corruption Act, 1947, Sections 5 and 6 and Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 173(2), CBI is not required to present the collected material before sanctioning authority if no case is made out against the respondent.
The Special Judge and High Court were incorrect in insisting that CBI needed to acquire sanction from prosecuting authority before seeking acceptance of the report under Section 173(2) Cr.PC for the discharge of the respondent.
