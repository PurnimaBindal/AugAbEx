Prevention of Corruption Act, 1947, Section 5(1)(c) - Criminal Offence - Departmental Enquiry - When a delinquent employee is acquitted by a criminal court, disciplinary proceedings cannot be initiated in normal course on the same charges and evidence - The case may fall under criminal law but the same facts may constitute misconduct under the Service Rule - Domestic enquiries do not follow strict rules of evidence as criminal cases do for a conviction.
