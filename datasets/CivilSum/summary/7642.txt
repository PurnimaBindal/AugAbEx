Petitioners who were appointed to the LDC post in 1985 were later appointed against vacant LDC posts as daily rated workers and were regularised in 1992.
In computing the period of service for grant of Selection grade, their service should be counted from the date they were brought on the regular cadre and fixed in the regular pay scale applicable to the post against which their appointments had been made.
This decision follows the ruling in 2002(2) SCT 635 (Raj).
