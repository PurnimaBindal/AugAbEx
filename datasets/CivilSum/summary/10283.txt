Constitution of India, Article 226 - Absorption - Seasonal workers - Petitioners seeking absorption and consequent regularization - Petitioners have performed seasonal duties for 5 years - While regularization cannot be claimed as a matter of right, Petitioners deserve sympathy for consideration - Age bar may be waived if necessary - Socialism is not just a concept, but must be practiced - Authorities must consider representations of Petitioners in light of this court's judgment - Writ Petition allowed with directions to consider Petitioners.
