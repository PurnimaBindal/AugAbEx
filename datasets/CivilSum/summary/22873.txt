Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11A - Workman - Disciplinary proceedings - Termination of service - The Labour Court's findings of fact cannot be reversed by the High Court - High Court found serious flaws in the charge of intentional injury - No FIR lodged, barrier not put up by corporation, and no medical examination conducted - The Labour Court did not assign a reason as to how the charges are proved nor did he take into consideration his power under Section 11A - The doctrine of proportionality applies in a case of this nature - Reinstatement of workman with 25% backwages is in the interest of justice instead of 50% as granted by High Court.
