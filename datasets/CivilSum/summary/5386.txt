Constitution of India, Articles 12, 14, 16, 30, 32 and 226 - State Bank of India Employees Pension Fund Rules - According to Rule 3(i)(a) of State Bank of India Retired Employees, Medical Benefit Scheme, Medical Benefit is only available to employees who have retired after 30 years of service - The petitioner was not permitted to become a member as they had not completed 30 years of service - The petitioner argues that this clause is arbitrary and discriminatory towards pensioners - This Court will not interfere with the decision made by the Bank as long as it is made in good faith and is reasonable - It is not normally in the Court's jurisdiction to weigh the pros and cons of policy, unless discriminatory or against any legal provision - More members being admitted may not be beneficial to the Scheme due to its contributory nature - Therefore, the Scheme does not differentiate between those who are similarly situated, and the petition is rejected.
