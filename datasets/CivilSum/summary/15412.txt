Sections 1(4) and 2(d), (e) & (o) of the Consumer Protection Act, 1986 - Pension disputes between pensioners and pension authorities do not fall under the definition of "Consumer dispute" or "Service" - The statutory forums do not possess any plenary or inherent powers like Civil Courts - Complaints can only be entertained if they fall under the jurisdiction of the Act.
