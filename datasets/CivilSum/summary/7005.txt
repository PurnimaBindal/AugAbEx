Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F, Schedule 2 Item 3 - Rajasthan Municipalities Act, Section 283 - Daily wage employee - Termination of employment - No backwages paid at 40% during reinstatement after previous award - Alleged agreement to waive backwages deemed invalid - No retrenchment compensation given on second termination - Petitioner entitled to reinstatement with arrears of backwages from earlier order and backwages as daily wage employee from date of earlier reinstatement till present reinstatement, as removal was not in compliance with Section 25-F [Paras 8 to 10]
