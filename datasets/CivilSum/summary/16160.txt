Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Promotion/Reversion/Fraud Misrepresentation - Manipulation of service record - The petitioner manipulated his service record to obtain higher pay scales for a higher post, to which he was never promoted - The Head office recommended his reversion to his original post - The petitioner cannot benefit from his own fraud - The government has the power to correct mistakes and grades without giving the employee a chance to be heard - The employee cannot use Article 226 for this purpose.
