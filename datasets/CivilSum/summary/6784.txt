Constitution of India, Article 226 and 227 - Petitioners, who were not deputed for Foresters Training Course whereas junior respondents 5 to 39 were deputed, had their names deleted from the array of parties on request - Respondents argued that petitioners were ineligible due to being under 45 years of age - Court ruled that no relief can be given as all seats in the Training College were already filled by junior respondents, whose names were deleted from the array of parties, and the court cannot increase the number of seats or nullify the order of deputation - Petition was dismissed.
