Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disallowed to join duties - Though the petitioner was selected as Lecturer by the Department Selection Committee, she was unable to take charge as she was declared temporarily unfit by the Chief Medical Officer due to pregnancy.
Despite being allowed time to join her duties by the Education Minister of Haryana, the petitioner was not allowed to join her duties due to a perceived lack of workload in certain subjects.
Even if there was no workload available, the petitioner's services would not have been terminated had she joined her duties by September 15, 1994.
In order to provide continuity of service, the services of a Lecturer are not to be terminated until recommendees of Haryana Public Service Commission join, and as a regular incumbent recommended by the Commission has not joined, the petitioner must be permitted to join her duties immediately.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]
