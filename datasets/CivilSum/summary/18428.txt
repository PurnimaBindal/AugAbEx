According to Rule 11 of the Rajasthan Medical & Health Subordinate Service Rules, 1965 and Regulation 46(2) of the Rajasthan Nursing Council Regulations, 1964, it is not necessary for a candidate seeking appointment for the post of Nurse to be registered with RNC as Nurse Grade `A' - Even if the registration certificate issued by RNC is not appended with the application, it cannot be rejected outright - The registration certificate is only required when the name of applicant is included in select list.
