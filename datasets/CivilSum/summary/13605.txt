Admission - Sports quota - Gradation certificate - Interview - Guidelines - The guidelines for admission to reserved categories of sports can be changed by the Chandigarh Administration after the examination process has commenced.
One official from the Sports Department is to be a member of the JAC to give admissions to various colleges in the state.
However, no official from the Sports Department had come with original gradation certificates.
The petitioner and other candidates in similar situations were neither interviewed nor admitted by the JAC.
Policy decisions for admissions to reserved categories have to be applied in the year of admission and not when a particular person participated in the event.
The benefit of participation in sports is given to those who participated within three years prior to the examination year.
Making the policy applicable to the year of participation would be unjust to other students competing in the same examination as different standards would be applicable to different candidates.
The same guidelines have to be made applicable in respect of reserved categories in an examination for a particular year, and they cannot be changed from student to student, making this finding unsustainable.
