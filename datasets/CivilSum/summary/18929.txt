According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 226/227, a petitioner was appointed as a lecturer in Political Science against a "lien bound" vacancy.
However, the private respondent had been on extraordinary leave for two years.
The respondents argued that since the private respondent had rejoined the department after their leave, the appointment of the petitioner was automatically cancelled.
It was agreed that the appointment of the petitioner was in fact lien bound.
Moreover, the private respondent had rejoined their duties after their leave had expired.
Given the private respondent's return to work, the petitioner's case for a writ of mandamus is no longer valid.
Consequently, the petition was dismissed.
