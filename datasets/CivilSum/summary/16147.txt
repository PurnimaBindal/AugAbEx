The petitioner was granted admission to BA Part I for Sessions 1990-91 but was unable to clear the compartment examination, and thus her candidature was suspended.
However, on representation, the petitioner was given a chance to appear in the compartment examination in September 1991 and was declared successful.
She was then permitted to clear the SSC Examination Part II.
Despite this, the University did not declare her result of BA Part I Examination held in April 1991, leading the petitioner to believe that the University's actions were arbitrary, unreasonable, and capricious, and in violation of Article 14 of the Constitution.
As a result, the order cancelling the petitioner's candidature and result of the BA Part I Examination has been quashed.
