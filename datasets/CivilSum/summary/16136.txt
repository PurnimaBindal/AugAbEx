Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 5 - Major penalties/Stoppage of increments/Departmental Enquiry - The stoppage of increments for an employee is considered a major penalty, and a regular enquiry must be held before imposing this penalty.
This has been followed according to 1991(2) SCT 30.
