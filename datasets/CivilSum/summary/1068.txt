According to Sections 11 and 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971 and Sections 17 and 33 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and Article 215 of the Constitution of India, an award can be stayed subject to the deposit of the awarded amount.
If the amount is not deposited, the workman may initiate recovery proceedings.
If the order is not observed, the order under Section 17-B will continue to operate, and the benefit of doubt will be given to the management.
The party may be punished if there is contumacious disobedience of a judicial order or if the contemnor takes advantage of the order while wilfully failing to fulfill all obligations cast upon him.
This was established in a writ petition where the party failed to deposit the awarded amount.
