Section 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971 deals with disciplinary proceedings for convictions, and if termination during an appeal does not amount to interfering with administration of justice.
Disciplinary authorities have the power to pass orders as they see fit.
[Paras 13 and 15]
Under Section 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, a cause of action for contempt cannot be initiated solely by asking, and the consequences of punishing for contempt, even with a summary procedure, are serious.
Courts have the discretion to decide whether to punish for contempt despite a proven offense.
