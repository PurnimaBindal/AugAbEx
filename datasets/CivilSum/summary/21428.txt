Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol. II, Rule 532 - Punjab Police Rules, Rule 18(1)(c) - The Authority issued an order of compulsory retirement on 7-9-1989, which was challenged in a writ.
The High Court directed the Authority to reconsider, and upon reconsideration, the Authority passed a new order of compulsory retirement on 6-11-1995.
However, the date of compulsory retirement would remain 7-9-1989 and not 6-11-1995.
