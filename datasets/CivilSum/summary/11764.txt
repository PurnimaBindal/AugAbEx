"Under the Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226, pensionary benefits cannot be withheld due to embezzlement of Government money.
Departmental proceedings were launched against the petitioner and charges of embezzlement were leveled but the petitioner was later cleared of these charges and no charge sheet was submitted after investigation.
Therefore, the petitioner cannot be prosecuted in any court for this offence and must be given directions for release of their pension and gratuity.
[Paras 5 and 7]"
