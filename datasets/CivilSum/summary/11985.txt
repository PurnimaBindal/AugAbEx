Constitution of India, Articles 166(1) & (2) - Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 7 - Punishment order - Communication of order - Notings on files - A mere noting or order on the office file is not considered a legal order until it is issued and communicated to the concerned person.
Additionally, if an opinion is expressed by an authority on the file without issuing a final order, it does not create any hindrance on a subsequent authority to pass an order as it may deem appropriate.
