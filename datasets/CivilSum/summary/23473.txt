Karnataka State Civil Services (Classification and Scales of Pay of Non-Graduate Junior Engineers of the Public Works Department) Act, 1975, Section 2(1)(ii) - Karnataka Public Works Department Service (Recruitment) Rules, 1960 (as amended w.e.f.
371969 on 911974) - The introduction of retrospective bifurcation of the common cadre of Junior Engineers into two cadres i.e. Division I and II is seen as discriminatory against the Junior Engineers (Division I) and in favor of non-graduate Junior Engineers and violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - Any amendment should be made prospectively in order to avoid such discrimination.
[Paras 7, 10, 12, 14, 15 to 17]  B Constitution of India, Article 309 - Amendments to Service Rules - The legislature is allowed to make retrospective changes to statutes but only to the extent that they do not violate principles of Articles 14 and 16 or cause discrimination.
