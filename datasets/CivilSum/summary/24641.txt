Delhi School Education Rules, 1973, Rule 126 - Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Section 2(d) - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 35 - Pension - Notification under, dt. 17-10-1975 of Delhi Administration - Pensionary benefits - The petitioner, a Primary teacher of Aided School who retired in 1977, was granted the option to receive pension and gratuity before retirement.
The benefit could not be rejected based on the fact that the modalities for granting pension were not finalised till 1988 or that approval of Government of India was not received.
Respondents are directed to pay pension to the petitioner.
