Constitution of India, Article 227 - Transfer - Challenge as to - The petitioner was transferred after being promoted 16 days earlier and the transfer pertains to a group of 129 people.
No mala fide or discrimination was alleged.
It was held that the general order of transfer cannot be considered mala fide or illegal since there was no case of mala fide, arbitrariness, accommodation of another person, or breach of rules.
The petitioner did not have a vested right to remain posted in the same district after their earlier posting.
Therefore, the petition was dismissed.

Constitution of India, Article 227 - Scope of jurisdiction - It was held that the High Court cannot interfere with the exercise of discretionary powers vested in an inferior Court or Tribunal unless the finding or order is clearly perverse or patently unreasonable.
