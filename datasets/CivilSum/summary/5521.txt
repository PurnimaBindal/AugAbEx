The maintainability of a writ petition under U.P.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 4-K and Constitution of India, Article 226 is being questioned in a case of the termination of two workers by a petitioner-company's union.
The issued reference order lacks notice to the petitioner-company and reasons, which is being challenged.
The petitioner is not entitled to question this legality due to the fatal omission of not impleading the union and two workers.
Furthermore, the raised inquiry can easily be brought up before a tribunal.
Thus, this case is not fitting for the court's discretion to be exercised in favor of the petitioner.
