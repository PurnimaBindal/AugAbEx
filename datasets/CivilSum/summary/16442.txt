Constitution of India, Article 309 - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rules 265, 268D, Chapter 23, 23-A - Denial of widowed mother's claim of pension as sole legal heir on the ground that rule 268D deems family pension to a mother of a deceased government servant inadmissible - Chapter 23 includes mother in the list of dependants, not repealed by chapter 23(A) - No separate nomination provision stated in chapter 23(A) - Thus, mother is a valid nominee to receive benefits under the rules, and clause 265(b), which only covers original family pension scheme contingencies, cannot be excluded, but must be a part of the scheme of family pension - Accordingly, the mother is eligible for family pension under 1950 rules.
