In accordance to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India regarding pay scale, Language Teachers in Vocational Education Institutions as well as lecturers employed in the School cadre should be receiving equal pay for equal work.
The Language Teachers have been granted the claim of pay scale from 2000-3000 to 2000-3500 as has been allowed for School cadre lecturers, following the decision made by the Supreme Court in JT 1997(5) SC 217.
