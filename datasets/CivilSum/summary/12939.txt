The Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of Deceased Employees Rules, 2003, Rule 3(d) and 8 - Compassionate appointment - Appointment - Communication - According to Article 166 of the Constitution, a communication must have two requirements to be considered a government order: (a) it must be expressed in the name of the Governor, and (b) it must be communicated to the person concerned.
Once the competent authority communicates the order, the appointment is complete and cannot be argued otherwise.
[Para 7]

The Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of Deceased Employees Rules, 2003, Rule 3(d) and 8 - Compassionate appointment - Appointment - Cancellation - When an appointment order is communicated, it is considered complete and cannot be cancelled without providing notice and hearing to the appointee as required by natural justice.
[Para 8]
