Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3(i) (a) - Judicial review of Screening Committee's order for premature retirement - No irregularities or allegations of mala fide found - No intervention by way of judicial review is necessary - Petition dismissed.

Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3(i) (a) - Premature retirement is not a punishment but based on appraisal of a Govt. employee's service record - All retiral benefits are given - Age of retirement for a Govt. employee is 58 years - Rule 3(1)(a) of the Rules, 1975 prepones the date of retirement based on performance evaluation.

Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 (i) (a) - Efficiency bar crossing principle loses significance as per Balkuntha Nath Dass v. Chief District Medical Officer, AIR 1922 SC 1020 - Petitioner's plea that only latest record should be considered is invalid - Even uncommunicated remarks can be taken into account - Subjective satisfaction of the Govt. is necessary for compulsory retirement and entire service record must be considered.
