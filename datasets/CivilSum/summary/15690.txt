Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Revised pay scale - Benefit of - Withdrawn - Natural justice - The petitioner's benefit of the revised pay scale was initially granted, then withdrawn after three years and later restored after protest - However, it was again withdrawn without giving him any opportunity to explain or informing him of the reason - This action is a violation of the principle of natural justice and is deemed unfair and arbitrary - Therefore, it cannot be sustained and is set aside.
