A person working as an apprentice without receiving any compensation and without being appointed by a competent authority is not considered a workman under the ID Act, even if the duration of their work continues for more than 240 days - Section 103 of the Factories Act provides a presumption which is only applicable for that Act and can be rebutted.
[Paras 12 and 13]  B The presumption provided in Section 103 of the Factories Act can be rebutted - It is only applicable for the Factories Act and not for Section 10 of the ID Act.
