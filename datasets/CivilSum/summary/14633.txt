Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 122 and 1318 state that seniority should be settled by the dates of confirmation for promotions.
The date of confirmation is crucial for Head Constables as it affects their further promotions and career advancement.
Altering the date of confirmation can impact seniority and career advancement, and the competent authority must provide an opportunity to petitioners.
An order passed in violation of natural justice principles is quashed.
