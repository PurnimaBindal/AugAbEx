Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - Delay in seeking - The petitioner sought promotion to Senior Assistant Accounts Clerk, requesting eligibility from the date of his junior colleagues' promotions - Allegedly, those colleagues were promoted in 1982 - In 1986, a seniority list was released - The petitioner only made his representation in 1995, after a 13 year lapse - His petition was dismissed on the grounds of delay and laches.
