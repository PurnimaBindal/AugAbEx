According to Civil Service Regulations in Articles 37-A, 37-B, and 37-C, the petitioner was appointed on a temporary basis and later transferred to another department on mutual basis.
Although the petitioner was then transferred again with a stipulation to retain lien in the parent department, she did not acquire any lien on the post in the parent department since it was a temporary position.
As a result, the petitioner is not entitled to a promotion in the parent department, and the question of her seniority must be resolved where she is currently working.
