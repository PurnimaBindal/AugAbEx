The Court upheld the petitioner's claim that they were denied promotion due to conflicting rules.
Though the Haryana Medical Education Rules were superseded by the Medical Council of India Regulations in 1998, regulations under Section 33 of the Act are binding and will prevail over rules framed by State.
[Para 14]

The Medical Council of India Regulations for promotion do allow for a relaxation in qualification if approved by the Medical Council.
The Court directed the appointing authority to reconsider the promotion of similarly situated individuals in accordance with rules and regulations.
[Paras 15, 16]
