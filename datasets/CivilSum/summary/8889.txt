Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8 - Disciplinary proceedings - Departmental enquiry - Delays in an enquiry are unacceptable - Charge sheets issued in 1975 and replied in the same year - However, competent authority decided to appoint Enquiry Officer in 1995, 20 years after the fact - Records crucial to the enquiry are unavailable, leading to prejudice against employees - Rights of the petitioners were jeopardised due to no promotions and crossing the efficiency bar for 20 years - Charges related to events 23 years prior, making them irrelevant - Charge-sheets along with the order appointing Enquiry Officer have been quashed, and now it is necessary to conduct enquiry proceedings within a reasonable time.
