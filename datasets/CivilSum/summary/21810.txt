Army Act, 1950 - Jurisdiction - Court Martial - Confirming Authority - The Court Martial initially found the appellant not guilty and sent the order to the confirming authority for confirmation.
However, the confirming authority remitted the matter back due to aspects of the case that were not properly considered.
Upon reconsideration, the Court Martial found the appellant guilty.
The appellant claimed that the confirming authority overstepped their jurisdiction, but it was held that they did not make any prejudicial findings and the Court Martial was free to decide while taking into account the basic features indicated by the confirming authority.
The Court Martial passed a reasoned order after providing the appellant with an opportunity to be heard and therefore no interference was necessary.
