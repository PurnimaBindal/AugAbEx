Constitution of India, Article 226 - Denial of disability benefits to a person on the basis that their disease is not attributable or aggravated by military service - Mitral Valve Prolapse-394 is a congenital condition, but it can worsen due to stress - The duty of a soldier in the Army is strenuous and difficult - The petitioner's disease is deemed attributable to their Army service - The respondents must provide disability pension to the petitioner within three months.
