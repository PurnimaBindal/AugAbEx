Delhi School Education Rules in 1973, Rule 104, stipulates that termination of employment may occur if the Director's proposal for a grant-in-aid is rejected for being over the age limit.
The petitioner in this case had been employed at the school since 1988 and was under the age limit of 25 years at the time of joining, which was when the school achieved recognition.
It was determined that the age bar specified was for recruitment and not regularization, thus Rule 104 would not apply to the petitioner's case as per paragraphs 12, 13, and 15.
