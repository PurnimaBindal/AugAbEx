The Payment of Wages Act, 1936, Section 15(1), and Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Sections 15(3) and 28, are in question with regards to jurisdiction.
The Payment of Wages authority is exempted from the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, despite the Municipality's plea that all service matters, including wages, must be adjudicated by Administrative Tribunals under the purview of Section 15(3) of the Act.
The authority under the Payment of Wages Act has jurisdiction to entertain the claim for wages filed by the 2nd respondent.
