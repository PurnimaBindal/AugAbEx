Constitution of India, Articles 136, 226 and 311 - Reinstatement - Back wages - Following the order of the Tribunal, the respondent was reinstated back into service and subsequently confirmed by a further order from the authorities with effect from 1st February 1986.
The authorities' later action cannot be considered a result of the Tribunal's order.
The Tribunal's direction that half of the back salary be paid is not fair as public money should not be spent to reward such deviant behavior, particularly by members of a disciplined force.
Therefore, the part of the Tribunal's order that directed back salary be paid to the respondent is set aside.
Furthermore, no back salary shall be payable to the respondent until the date of reinstatement, despite the retrospective confirmation.
