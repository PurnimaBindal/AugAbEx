According to Section 2(n) of the Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923, in the case of a workman in a bakery, if an accident occurs during the course of employment - for example, falling into a well while drawing water during duty hours - and such workman was serving tea as well, it cannot be concluded that the activity of drawing water is unrelated to the workman's duty, and hence, the accident cannot be considered as beyond the course of employment.
