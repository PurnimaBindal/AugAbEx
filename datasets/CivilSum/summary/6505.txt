Constitution of India, Articles 12, 21, 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 32(2)(b), 33(a) - Order under Section 33(2)(b) - Writ - Maintainability - Employer is required to treat employee as continuing and provide consequential benefits upon refusal of Tribunal to approve dismissal order - Petition by employee can be filed if such benefits are denied, as termination order is void ab initio - Single Judge dismissed employee's petition on grounds of alternative remedy.
