Payment of Wages Act, 1936 Section 17(1-A) - Limitation Act, Section 12 - Appeal - Time taken to obtain certified copy of order - Deposit of awarded amount - Time limitations for both deposit and appeal filing, and must be within the prescribed limitation - Producing certificate of deposit along with appeal, after the time limit has lapsed, does not count as within limitation - However, appeal limitation period starts after certified copy of order is received - The Appellate Court must take into account the time period spent obtaining the copy - The matter is remanded to the Appellate Authority.
