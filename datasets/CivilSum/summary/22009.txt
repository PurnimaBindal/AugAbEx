Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226, 311 - Departmental proceedings/Natural justice - Charges of misconduct and integrity and dishonesty - Employees who fail to respond to charges or show cause notice and do not participate in departmental proceedings without sufficient reasons cannot argue later that they were not given a reasonable opportunity to defend themselves and that it violated natural justice and fair hearing principles - Order followed all procedural formalities - High Court was not right in annulling the punishment order on the grounds of natural justice violation.
