Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 309 - Rajasthan (Recruitment of Dependents of Government Servant Dying While in Service) Rules, 1975, Rule 5 - Compassionate Appointment - The objective of providing compassionate appointment is to furnish financial assistance to the mourning family and is not an absolute right - If the applicant's financial status is stable, the rules can be denied to them - Both the applicant's father and mother were employed - The applicant's mother passed away, and the applicant is dependent on his father - Since the father is still employed, he is not eligible for employment under the rules - The applicant must demonstrate that he was solely dependent on his mother and not on his father for his upkeep.
