Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay scales - Equivalence of pay scales - Tailoring Mistresses are to receive a pay scale equal to that of the Classical & Vernacular Teachers, and they are also entitled to the same revised grade.
[Para 3]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection Grade/Seniority list/Cadre - The seniority list for Tailoring Mistresses is at the State level, while that of Classical & Vernacular Teachers is at the District level.
Although they may have equivalent pay scales, they do not form a joint cadre.
Tailoring Mistresses are not entitled to selection grade alongside the Classical & Vernacular Teachers.
Rather, they are entitled to the same selection grade in their own cadre according to their own seniority list to the extent of 15% of their posts.
Any mistakes can be rectified, but any payments already made cannot be recovered.
