Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Punjab Pollution Employees Service Regulations, 1980, Regulations 9 and 13 - Promotion - Post of Senior Environmental Engineer - In order to be eligible for promotion to the post of Senior Environmental Engineer, candidates must have the qualification of degree as laid down.
The petitioner, who only holds a diploma, cannot be considered for promotion.
