Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion - The appellant's request for promotion was not considered previously due to ongoing departmental proceedings against him.
However, after being cleared of the charges, the appellant is entitled to have his promotion request considered from the date that his juniors were promoted.
Despite his representation for promotion being dismissed due to failure to follow proper grievance procedure, the appellant is given permission to submit a new representation for promotion, and the respondents must make a decision within six weeks and provide an explanation.
