Constitution of India, Article 16 - Criminal Procedure Code, Section 300 - Double jeopardy - Charge of theft against an employee - Departmental and criminal proceedings initiated against the employee - Both actions are valid - Not a case of double jeopardy - Departmental proceedings and criminal proceedings are independent of each other and can be initiated against an employee.
The outcome of one does not affect the other.
[Paras 9 and 10]
