Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dentists Act, 1948, Sections 10A and 20 - Establishing and enlarging capacity of Dental Colleges - State Government's permission and certificate are necessary - An incomplete application to the Dental Council cannot be accepted without the State Government's certificate - The Court cannot direct the Dental Council to proceed with inspection and approval on such an incomplete application - Proceedings cannot violate statutory provisions and the State is a necessary party to such proceedings - Absence of the State cannot justify issuing directions for ultimate approval to be granted by the Central Government.
