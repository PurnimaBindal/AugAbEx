Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 226 - Admissions/Education - Doctrine of legitimate expectation - University students expected their diploma course examination to be held and the result declared before medical course admissions, as per past practice - Students expecting an extension of time to join medical course if results not declared before admissions closed - Examination for diploma course not held before medical course admissions closed - University did not grant extension of time, contrary to past practice - Cut off date for admissions fixed arbitrarily without notice to that effect - Doctrine of legitimate expectation applies - University cannot claim petitioners were ineligible for admission - No reason for the University to have deviated from past practice of granting extension of time in such a situation.
