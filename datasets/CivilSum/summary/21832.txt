The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(g) & 2(s) - Scope of interference in writ jurisdiction in the findings of fact arrived at by the competent authority or Tribunal - In situations where findings of fact were based on some materials and evidence relevant in the matter, the writ court is not supposed to disturb such findings merely for want of sufficiency or credibility of such evidence unless it is demonstrated that the findings were based on no evidence.
[Paras 17 and 21]

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(g) & 2(s) - Employee employer relationship - Canteen Workers - In order to safeguard the welfare of the workmen, the veil may be pierced to get at the realities of their employment.
No abstract principle or rule as a ready reckoner for all situations can be laid down to hold that canteen workers were employees of the Establishment.
Only some guiding principles can be indicated to be applied in the similarity or identity of facts of particular cases.
Findings of the Tribunal that the workers were employees of the Bank in its canteen entitled to be reinstated in view of the necessity of canteen, upheld.
[Paras 18 to 22]
