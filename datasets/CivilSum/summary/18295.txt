Rajasthan Civil Services (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1970 and Constitution of India, Article 21, state that a retired government servant is entitled to reimbursement of medical expenses.
In this case, the retired gov't servant was recommended for heart surgery in AIIMS but had to undergo surgery in Escorts hospital due to a doctors' strike.
He had to borrow a loan from a bank, and is therefore held entitled to a reimbursement of up to Rs. 1,20,000/- as well as interest at a rate equal to that charged by the bank (i.e. @ 12%).
[Paras 6 to 9].
