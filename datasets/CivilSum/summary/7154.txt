The Andhra Pradesh Education Act, 1982, Section 83, and Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, deal with the topic of retrenchment and provide an alternate remedy of appeal.
The respondents in this case were obligated to follow the provisions of Section 83.
The termination order was issued without adhering to the statutory requirements, and hence it was set aside.
The existence of the alternative remedy of appeal under the Act did not bar the exercise of jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
