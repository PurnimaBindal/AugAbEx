According to the Punjab Educational Service Class III School Cadre (First Amendment) Rules, 1995, Rule 2(2), as well as a Haryana Government Notification dated 1231976, JBT Certificate holders are equivalent to OT and have even been considered as such by both the High Court and the Supreme Court.
Since the notification has received judicial validation, JBT certificate holders must be considered equivalent to OT.
