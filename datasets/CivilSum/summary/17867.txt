Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 and 311 - Regularisation of service - Termination - The petitioner has served for over two years before filing a writ petition - The State argues that the petitioner's case for regularisation will be examined in light of state directives and the verdict in State of Haryana v. Piara Singh, 1992(3) SCT 201, and if the petitioner meets all the requirements mentioned in the instructions and judgment - Judged, The petitioner's services cannot be terminated until his case is reviewed for regularization.
