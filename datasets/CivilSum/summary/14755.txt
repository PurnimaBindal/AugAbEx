The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Selection - Medical Fitness - Eye Sight - A petitioner applied for the position of Assistant Sub Inspector (Technician) in the Police Service and was selected, ranking fourth on the select list.
The petitioner was referred for a medical examination to determine whether the defect of Myopia made him unsuitable for Police Service.
Medical experts found the petitioner fit for the job in question, and that Myopia with 25 DSP did not make him unfit for the job.
The petitioner is therefore eligible for appointment to the position.
[Paras 2 and 4]
