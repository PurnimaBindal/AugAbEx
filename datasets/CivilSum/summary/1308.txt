Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 - Transfer - Deputationist - Petitioner seeks cancellation of transfer allegedly on ground of malafide, against settled policy and guidelines - State Government has power to withdraw services of petitioner being on deputation - Petitioner transferred on request made to government by Chairman in interest of administration - Sufficient material not presented to prove malafide - Court upheld that transfer is a matter of incidence and should not normally be interfered with unless malafide or arbitrariness is proved or transfer is not in interest of public and administration - As transfer is in interest of public and administration and government's action is within its right, petition is dismissed.
