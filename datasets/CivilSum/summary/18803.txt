The Haryana Sate Electricity Board Conduct Regulations, 1984, Rules 19 and 20(1) state that disciplinary proceedings are to be conducted for employees who are found to engage in strikes or instigate others to strike.
In this case, the petitioner sent a fax massage to the Chairman of the Board expressing the sentiments of employees and cautioning against the potential risks of untrained and inexperienced personnel handling equipment.
It cannot be inferred that the petitioner instigated other employees to strike or worsened the power situation in the State.
The disciplinary action taken against the petitioner was found to be baseless and liable to be quashed.
[Paras 11 and 14]

The Haryana State Electricity Board Conduct Regulations, Rule 21 dictates that disciplinary proceedings should be conducted consistently and without discrimination.
In this case, it was found that the State authority had proceeded differently against the petitioner, who was charge-sheeted with the same allegations of misconduct as others, but was singled out for disciplinary action while the proceedings against others were dropped.
The petitioner was held to be discriminated against.
[Paras 15 and 16]
