Constitution of India, Article 226 - Haryana State Supply and Marketing Cooperative Services (Common Cadre) Rules, 1969, Rules 210, 213 and 217 - Termination - When a termination order is made under Rule 210 without punitive or stigmatic effect, no appeal or revision can be taken under the rules - This implies that the only remedy available is through a writ petition under Article 226 of the Constitution which must be filed within a reasonable time - The writ petition filed against the termination order passed in 1976 in 1983 was deemed tardy and subject to dismissal on the basis of delay and laches.
