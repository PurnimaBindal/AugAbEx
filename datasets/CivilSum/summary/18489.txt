Constitution of India, Articles 226 & 227 - Promotion - Reservation - Roster point - A member of the backward class category, respondent No. 2, was promoted at roster point No. 26 in the quota meant for that category, as no schedule caste candidate was available.
As posts belonging to schedule castes and backward classes categories are exchangeable and the roster point for the backward class category stood exhausted, the roster point at 26 cannot be offered twice to a member of the backward class category.
Sufficient material shows that respondent No. 2 belongs to the backward class, and there is no infirmity in their promotion.
