Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Reversion - Natural justice - 14 years ago, promotion from the role of Laboratory Attendants to Multipurpose Health Supervisors was granted - Petitioners now face reversion without any explanation or chance to defend themselves - The petitioners were allowed to continue on the promotional post for over 7 years thanks to an interim order in a writ petition - It would be unjust to revert them after 21 years without valid reasoning - The order of reversion has been invalidated.
