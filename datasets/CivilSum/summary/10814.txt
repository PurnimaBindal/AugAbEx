The Tribunal's finding that the petitioner's duties as a District Health Education-cum-information Officer were different from that of an Information Officer in Department cannot be challenged in writ jurisdiction, as per Articles 39(d) and 226 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 9] B.
The principle of equal pay for equal work cannot be stretched too far, as different States have different situations and financial conditions.
Therefore, even if a higher pay scale is granted to the post of District Health Education Officer in another State, parity of pay cannot be claimed with them under Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
[Paras 9 and 10] C. The petitioner cannot be given a higher pay scale solely on the basis of a recommendation made by a committee constituted by the State Government.
Mere recommendation is not law, as per Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
