Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 16, 2(2) state that dismissal from service is appropriate when serious charges, such as having links with criminal elements and involvement in criminal cases, are proved against an officer.
This conduct goes against the public trust in the disciplined force.
The punishment of dismissal from service is justified, and even if a revision petition against conviction is pending, the delinquent cannot be reinstated into service by nullifying the order of dismissal.
