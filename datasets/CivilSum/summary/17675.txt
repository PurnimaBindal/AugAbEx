Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Medical reimbursement - Reimbursement of expenditure towards medical treatment obtained outside India - If prior approval could not be obtained for treatment out of India due to emergent requirement of treatment - Employee is entitled to reimbursement - Instructions of Punjab Government.
The petitioner suffered an attack of left renal colic all of a sudden and had to be removed to hospital immediately and operated upon, thus satisfying the requirements for reimbursement.
The petition is therefore allowed.
