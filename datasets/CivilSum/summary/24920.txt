The dispute over the creation of the post of Professor in super speciality is irrelevant for deciding the appeal in the Supreme Court.
Contenders for the post cannot challenge the creation of a post as, unless the post exists, there is no scope for the contest over selection.
This objection can only be raised by the Central Government.
[Para 5]
Academic committees of experts and other authorities under the Act and Rules or Regulations must determine the job requirements and prescription of qualifications for the post of Professor in super speciality in Paediatric Gastroenterology.
While the court may be an expert of experts, it is proper to take note of its limitations while entering into the makers of expertise.
[Paras 8 and 9]
The creation of a post for Professor in the super speciality of Paediatric Gastroenterology was not mala fide as there was a proposal by the Government for quite some time to open the super speciality.
It was proper to provide for a Professor for that post.
