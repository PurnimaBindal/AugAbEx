According to the Rules for appointment of Teachers to primary/upper primary Govt. Schools of State of Kerala, TTC (Teachers Training Certificate) is the prescribed qualification and B.Ed.
diploma/degree is neither the prescribed qualification nor it is equivalent or higher qualification for the post of Primary Teacher.
Holders of E.Ed.
qualification cannot be considered for post of Primary Teacher who are required to teach small children.
Although B.Ed.
candidates may seem suitable, the training for B.Ed.
and TTC are totally different for teaching the children and students of different age group - Therefore, the object of Legislature in non-providing B.Ed.
qualification in such Rules is clear - However, it is a matter of education policy to prescribe qualifications of the teachers for a particular class of children.
Until the relevant provisions of the Act and Rules are amended, B.Ed.
candidates cannot be considered eligible for the post of Primary Teacher.
