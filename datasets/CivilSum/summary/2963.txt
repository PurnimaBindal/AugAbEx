Maharashtra Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practice Act, 1972, Section 24(1) - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 23 Clause (c), 24(2) - Strike - Lock out - Undertaking - Validity of - Strike was illegal due to non-compliance with the requirement of furnishing a 14 days notice.
Lockout caused by an illegal strike is absolved of illegality under Section 24(3).
Demand for undertaking was illegal and unjustified as it precluded workmen from agitating their claims before court and demanded a waiver of rights.
