Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Appointment - Post of Hindi, Punjabi/Art and Craft teachers in Govt aided schools - The approval of the appointment of the petitioners was declined due to certificates obtained from institutions located in Haryana not being recognized as equivalent to those in Punjab - The derecognition of such certificates would only apply prospectively - The respondents were directed to consider the petitioners' cases for appointment to posts if vacancies exist, otherwise, to create more posts - Relying on Neelam Kumari v. State of Punjab, 1993(1) SCT 770 (P&H)(FB).
