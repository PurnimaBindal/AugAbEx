Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Alleged misconduct leading to reversion - If the employer utilizes punitive provisions, even if they choose to exercise their power in terms of the appointment letter, and then proceeds to pass a reversion order, the order cannot be considered innocuous or without stigma - The employer must conduct a proper investigation and adhere to principles of natural justice - Any reversion order issued without these precautions cannot be upheld.
[Paras 11 and 12]
