Admission to MBBS & BDS courses in medical/dental Colleges in Haryana - Institutions must fill unfilled vacancies based on merit determined by the admission committee and communicated through the university, according to a notification from the Haryana Government dated November 7, 1996.
Institutions that granted admissions prior to receipt of merit list after third counselling and in violation of merit acted illegally.
It is only the Interview Board which would be the final authority to make selection as per merit determined on the basis of the result of the common entrance test.
Admissions granted in violation thereof cannot be allowed to sustain.
[Paras 8 to 11]  B.
The dentists act and regulations put in place by the Dental Council of India dictate admissions procedures and professional standards in medical institutions.
The regulations concerned with admissions are considered to be directory in nature and cannot supplant the procedures laid out in the information brochure.
[Paras 10 and 11]  C. Any admissions that violate merit, regulations, and settled law by the courts cannot be regularized by the courts.
Granting such candidates sympathy would nullify the procedure laid down in the information brochure for admissions.
