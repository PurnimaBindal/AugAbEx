Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab State Assistants Grade Examination Rules, 1984 - Punjab District Service Class III Rules, 1976, Rule 11 - Seniority - Promotion - Chances to clear examination - The petitioner and Respondent No. 3 both appeared in the Assistant Grades Examination.
The petitioner passed and was promoted while the respondent did not pass.
In the next examination, respondent No. 3 also passed and the Punjab State increased the number of chances to clear the examination.
However, seniority must be determined by continuous length of service and a failed candidate cannot be considered more meritorious than a person who passed on their first attempt.
Therefore, respondent No. 3 should not be senior to the petitioner.
