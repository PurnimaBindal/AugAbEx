Constitution of India, Article 226 - Mandamus - Writ of - A Petitioner claiming appointment as a Dental Surgeon, Class II, against one of the unfilled posts meant for the Scheduled Castes-A category is not entitled to any relief at this stage.
The petitioner failed to fulfil the condition of notice of demand, which he ought to have satisfied before invoking jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution for issuance of a writ in the nature of mandamus.
[Paras 3 and 5]
