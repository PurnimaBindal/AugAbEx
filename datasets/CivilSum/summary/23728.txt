Upon reading Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4-A(3) together with Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, Chapter XI, Section 147, it is evident that if an employee is injured in a motor vehicle accident while on duty on the vehicle owned by their insured employer, the insurer is liable to pay the compensation with interest under the Compensation Act.
However, U/s 4-A (3)(b), the penalty resulting from personal fault of the insured cannot be imposed on the insurer and must be paid by the employer.
Prior judgments holding insurance companies responsible for the penalty have been overruled.
