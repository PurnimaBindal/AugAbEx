According to Section 61 of the Punjab Wakf Board Act, 1954 and Regulation 24 of the Punjab Wakf Board Regulations, a transfer order of an Imam cannot be based on the import of the appointment order if the appointment letter does not mention that the Imam is liable to be transferred.
[Para 8]
The Secretary of the Board cannot make appointments or transfers of Class II employees of the Board without the approval of the Chairman or the Administrator as per Section 61 of the Punjab Wakf Board Act, 1954 and Regulation 24 of the Punjab Wakf Board Regulations.
[Paras 9 and 11]
Notice is mandatory as per Section 56 of the Punjab Wakf Board Act, 1954 only when the challenge is to an act done by the competent authority under the Act or Regulations.
No notice under Section 56 is necessary where the transfer order has been passed by an incompetent authority as stated in Paras 10 and 11.
