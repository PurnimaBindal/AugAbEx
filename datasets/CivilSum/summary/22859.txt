Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Nursing Council Act, Section 13 - Abolition of post - The sole post of Inspector of Nursing Council was abolished in a mala fide manner - However, appointment was made against a specifically created and sanctioned post, as the post of Inspector is necessary for the Council's statutory work - The termination of the Inspector was done abruptly during the pendency of a writ petition, without taking permission from the court - While the Inspector was reinstated, the post was not revived, making the actions of the Council a mala fide exercise of power.
