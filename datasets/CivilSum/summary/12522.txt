State Government Pleader BT Patil citing Constitution of India, Article 14 - Rules for Admission to Post-Graduate Courses 1991, Rule 6 - Admissions - Admission to PostGraduate Courses in Medical College - Vacancy - stated that the Rule 6 of the 1991 Rules mandates the dean of the college to advertise the vacancy before allotting a candidate to comply with the mandate of the Rule and Article 14 of the Constitution.
All eligible candidates must be given an opportunity to compete for the seat and the dean must verify the availability of candidates for the category.
Admission without advertising the vacancy violates Rule 6 and Article 14 of the Constitution, making it illegal.
