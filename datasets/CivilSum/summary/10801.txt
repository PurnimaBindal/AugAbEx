Constitution of India, Article 226 - Contractual appointee - Termination - Person holding post temporarily has no right to the post, and services can be terminated without reason under the terms of the contract or statutory rules.
Appointment extended with a warning to improve work, but the stipulation in the appointment order implies service can be terminated without notice if work or conduct is not found satisfactory.
The order is upheld.
