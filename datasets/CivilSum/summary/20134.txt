Central Civil Services (Fixation of Pay of Re-employed Pensioners) Order, 1986, Clause 11 - Indian Railway Establishment Code, Rule 1712(i) - Re-employment of pensioners - Clause 11 of the Order of 1986 cannot be overruled by Rule 1712(i) of Indian Railway Establishment Code.
Pension cannot be added to the pay for calculation of allowance.
[Para 4]
Indian Pass Manual, 1977, Rule 65(1)(b) - Railway Servants (Pass) Rules, 1986, Rule 16 - Adding of pension to pay to claim privilege of passes and Railway tickets - Pensioners re-appointed are not allowed to add pension to pay for determining the category of passes.
Only employees in Group `C' with pay of Rs. 2301 or above or those in the scale minimum of which is Rs. 2,000/- are entitled to first class passes.
Railway Servants (Pass) Rules, 1986 takes precedence over Pass Manual.
