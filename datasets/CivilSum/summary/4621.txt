Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Disciplinary action - Misconduct - The Court cannot review the penalty for proved misconduct.
The appellate authority agreed on the misconduct but found the punishment of dismissal to be disproportionate.
Therefore, the punishment of reduction in pay, withholding of increments, etc. was substituted.
The claim of the petitioner that the order of reinstatement proves innocence was rejected.
It is the disciplinary or appellate authority who has discretion over the penalty for proved misconduct.
[Paras 2 and 3]
