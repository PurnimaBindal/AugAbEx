Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Qualification - Post of Helper - The petitioner, who is duly qualified according to the requirements for the post of Helper recommended by the 3rd Punjab Pay Commission, had their writ petition granted.
The State of Punjab is bound by the recommendations of the Pay Commission once they are accepted.
The impugned order terminating the petitioner was quashed and the petitioner is entitled to all consequential relief.
