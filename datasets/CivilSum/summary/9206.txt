Ex-Servicemen (Re-employment in Central Civil Posts) Rules, 1979 - Constitution of India, Article 16 - Re-employment of Ex-Servicemen in Govt. Service - Ex-Servicemen, who receive military pensions, are not eligible for Dearness Relief during re-employment - Administrative orders that withdrew Dearness Relief from Ex-Servicemen pensioners during their re-employment are valid and reasonable.
