Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rules 5.32(8), 6.16(1), 6.16(A) and 6.16(2) - Pension - Premature retirement - The petitioner has been permitted to retire after completing 18 years, 5 months and 15 days of service in accordance with Rule 616(2), and is eligible for pension, gratuity, and the amount of money which is admissible under the Group Insurance Scheme.
Even if the qualifying service of 20 years is not met, the pension cannot be denied once premature retirement is allowed without any conditions.
