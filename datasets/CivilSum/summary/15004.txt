Constitution of India, Article 16 - Rajasthan Co-operative Societies Act, 1965, Section 32 - Temporary Class III employee in Bank - Cancellation of appointment - No opportunity of hearing given - It is argued that under Section 32 of the Act, the bank had the right to rescind the resolution passed by the Board without providing an opportunity, but the impugned order is held to be invalid for violating the rules of natural justice - It is irrelevant that the appellant appealed and was heard as the hearing by a higher authority after the decision would not cure the violation.
