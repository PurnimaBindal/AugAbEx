Army Regulations (1987 Edition), Para 108 Recommendations of Selection Board/Approval of the Central Government/Recordable Displeasure/Army Officer - The petitioner received recordable displeasure in 1983 which was later reduced to unrecordable in 1985.
Thus, there was no recordable displeasure against him.
Although the petitioner was found suitable for promotion in 1983, the government did not approve the Selection Board's recommendations and did not disclose any reasons for their decision.
The petitioner is therefore entitled to promote as Colonel from his due date.
[Paras 8, 9 and 10]
