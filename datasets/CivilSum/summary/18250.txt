Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - The employer is not required by law to have a scheme for employment on compassionate grounds.
Even when a decision is made to discontinue the scheme, it cannot be objected.
The petitioner cannot be granted relief of appointment on compassionate grounds, as steps were taken to reduce the number of existing employees.
[Para 5]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 - Gratuity - Adjustments of gratuity prohibited - The employer cannot adjust gratuity towards repayment of loans given to the deceased employee.
Therefore, the authorities are directed to release the amount towards the gratuity.
