Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Central Bank of India Officer Employees' (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976, Regulation 6(7) - Departmental Enquiry - Fair play - Right to legal assistance - Disciplinary authority must grant employee permission to engage a legal practitioner in cases where the charges are complex and numerous, and documents to be considered are voluminous.
Refusal of permission solely on the grounds that the presenting officer is not trained in law neither proper nor judicious.
Delinquent employees must have the assistance of legal practitioners in order to present their defense effectively.
[Paras 4, 5 and 6]
