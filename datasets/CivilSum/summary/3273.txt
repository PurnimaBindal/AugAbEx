Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(j) - Adverse entry - Confidential reports - When deciding to retire a government employee, more weight should be given to their recent confidential reports rather than earlier ones.
The employee in question had mostly good entries over ten years, and even in the relevant years leading up to the retirement order, their work was generally appreciated with no adverse entries.
Therefore, the compulsory retirement order was found to be in violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution and was quashed.
