Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 7-A and 10(1)(d), 2(d) and 2(rr) - Workmen - Wages - Commission.2(rr) - Wages - The commission paid to workmen on Deposit Collection is considered as wages, and it is linked to productivity and business promotion.
[Para 25]  2(s) - Workmen - For Deposit Collectors of a Banking Company - Despite no fixed hours of work and regulating their own hours due to the nature of duties, Deposit Collectors are accountable to the bank, and there is evidence of control by the bank over them - Hence, findings of the Tribunal that there is a relationship of master and servant and that they are employees of the Bank are not baseless or unsustainable.
B.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(s) - Banking Regulation Act, Section 10 - Collection agents - Regularisation of services - Equal pay for equal work - Deposit Collectors - Payment of commission on collections - Prohibition of employment of managing agents.
The proviso to Section 10 stipulates that commission can be paid to persons who are not in regular employment.
Deposit Collectors are not part of the regular employees of the Bank but meet the definition of workman under the ID Act.
Therefore, they are not entitled to be absorbed into regular service of the Bank, and neither a direction can be issued for the regularisation of their services nor payment of wages equivalent to the regular employees as there is no similarity in their duties.
