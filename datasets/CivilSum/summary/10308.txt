Constitution of India, Article 226 - UP Intermediate Education Act, Section 16-G - Application - Teachers working in recognized unaided institutions cannot be considered as regular teachers under Section 16-G and may only be part-time or honorary teachers.
The management that pays honorarium from its own resources may also dismiss them, and no writ of mandamus can oblige the management or principal to retain or remunerate a part-time or honorary instructor.
