Andhra Pradesh Revised Pension Rules, 1980, Rule 31 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 9(21)(a)(i) - Pension - Computation of - Emoluments - The computation of pension using emoluments does not include special pay or personal pay.
The incentive increments given to respondents are either special pay or personal pay and do not form part of pay under Rule 9(21)(a)(i), and should not be considered as emoluments for computation of pension.
Rule 31 of Pension Rules 1980 applies to all employees who were in service on that date, not just those who joined service after the revised pension rules came into effect - Rule 2 proviso is not concerned with the amendment of Pension Rules.
[Paras 4, 8 and 9]  B Constitution of India, Article 309 Proviso - Fundamental Rules, Rule 2 Proviso - AP Revised Pension Rules, 1980, Rule 31 - Pension - The proviso prohibits the modification or replacement of provisions of Fundamental Rules itself in exercise of power under Article 309 and does not reference any other Rule that a Governor could frame under the proviso to Article 309.
As a result, the proviso to Rule 2 of FR cannot affect the power of Governor to amend the Pension Rules in exercise of his power under the proviso to Article 309 of the Constitution.
