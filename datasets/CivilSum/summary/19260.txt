Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission to Medical college - Admission against payment seat - A candidate in the last merit order for non-payment seats who is not admitted but someone admitted against payment seat cannot claim fee refund only because a non-payment seat candidate leaves the course, until a new candidate liable to pay for a payment seat is admitted - Refunding fee to candidates admitted against non-payment seats due to dropouts will cause severe financial losses to the college, hindering its functioning.
