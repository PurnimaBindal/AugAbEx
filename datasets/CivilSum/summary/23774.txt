Constitution of India, Article 229 - Rajasthan High Court (Conditions of Service of Staff) Rules, 1953, Rules 2 and 2A - Appointment of Additional Registrars of the High Court - Appointment of ministerial staff of High Court Establishment - Jurisdiction of Chief Justice of the High Court and other judges - Appointment of judicial officers of Higher Judicial Services in the Establishment of the High Court - Jurisdiction of administration and appointments of administrative staff of the High Court is exclusively for the Chief Justice.
Judges of the High Court cannot alter the constitutional provisions or the rules made by the Chief Justice, nor can they suggest any constitutional amendment or amendment to the Chief Justice's rules.
They also cannot create any avenue of promotion from the Rajasthan Judicial or Higher Judicial Services to the establishment of the High Court.
Other judges or employees of the High Court cannot file a legitimate grievance.
[Paras 30 to 34]
