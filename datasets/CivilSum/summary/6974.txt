Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Recruitment of Ex-servicemen Rules, 1982, Rule 4 - Second appointment in Civil Services - The benefit of military service on subsequent appointment cannot be denied by way of clarification - The benefit should not be limited to only the first appointment, as the rules do not have any such conditions specified - Restricting the benefit to only one appointment is unreasonable and does not serve any purpose - The benefit of military service can also be availed on second appointment - The condition is only applicable to the wife or dependents of Ex-servicemen.
