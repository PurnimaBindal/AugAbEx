The Punjab Election Departmental (Group-B) Service Rules, 1963, Rule 6(3), and Rule 14, in accordance with Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India, provide that the promotion of an employee requires a certain amount of experience.
There is a minimum requirement of experience for promotion established by the rule-making authority, and it is assumed to be in the public interest.
Relaxation of this requirement is only permitted under specific circumstances as outlined in Rule 14.
The record does not demonstrate that Respondent No. 2 received a written order granting relaxation in the requirement of Rule 6(4) before his promotion, and as such, his promotion is illegal.
[Para 9] B Article 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India, Rule 6(3) and Rule 14 of the Punjab Election Departmental (Group-B) Service Rules, and a circular dated 1781971 regarding payment of emoluments to promoted employees who do not meet the minimum years of service requirement, were referenced in this case.
The circular is executive in nature and cannot be used to argue an implied relaxation of experience rules.
Furthermore, the circular pre-supposes the existence of a written order granting relaxation of experience requirements as outlined in recruitment rules.
Respondent No. 2 was not eligible for promotion as he did not meet the experience requirements specified in Rule 6(4).
