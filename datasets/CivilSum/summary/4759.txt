Constitution of India, Article 16 - Revision of pay scale - Petitioner Technical Officer - Despite the Government raising the pay scale of the petitioner's post to that recommended by the Pay Commission for the post of Assistant Director, the Government did not believe that the petitioner should receive the same pay scale as the Assistant Director due to the differing nature of their duties - The petition was dismissed.
[Para 10]
Constitution of India, Article 16 - Revision of pay scale - Pay Commission recommendations - The recommendations of the Pay Commission regarding revision of pay scale of Technical Officers to that of Assistant Directors are not necessarily binding on the Government, and certain factors may be considered such that the Government may refuse to accept the recommendations - Even a higher pay scale than the one recommended by the Pay Commission may be granted.
[Paras 8 and 9]
Constitution of India, Article 16 - Revision of pay scale - Posts of Technical Officers and of Assistant Directors - Due to the differing duties, requirements, and qualifications, the recommendations of the Pay Commission regarding the revision of the Technical Officer pay scale to that of the Assistant Director pay scale are only recommendatory in nature, and equal pay for equal work doctrine is not applicable - The Government may refuse the recommendations of the Pay Commission.
[Para 9]
Constitution of India, Article 16 - Pay scale - The doctrine of equal pay for equal work only applies when persons are placed similarly, and therefore does not hold good when two persons are differently placed - The pay would depend on the nature of duties, qualifications, and responsibilities attached to the post, and not on the quantum of work.
