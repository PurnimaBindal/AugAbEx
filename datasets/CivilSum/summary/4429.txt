The Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulations 216, 219 and 212 make a special provision for widows who remarry the brother of their deceased husband by way of local customs of the community.
Regulation 219 carves out an exception to encourage and provide support to such widows.
Even if a widow remarries the brother of her deceased husband, the pension would not be terminated because there can be no distinction made between a widow of a person dying by reasons attributable to or aggravated by military service and a person dying just while in service.
The problems faced by the widows are the same, and a contrary view would result in an arbitrary distinction hit by Articles 14 and 21 of the Constitution.
The principle shall equally apply to the ordinary family pension under Regulation 212 and not only to special family pension under Regulation 216.
