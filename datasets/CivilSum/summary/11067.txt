Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 14-B - Provident Fund/Penalty - Damages - Delay - Educational Institutions - Colleges - The Act was challenged in the Supreme Court with regards to its applicability to educational institutions, as prior to the Act, the University was handling provident fund accounts of college employees.
The Court dismissed the petitions and directed for arrears to be deposited immediately without any interest or damages.
The delay in transferring accounts was not the petitioner's fault and they were not in default of payment, hence no penalty or damages can be imposed.
Section 14-B only applies when there is default in payment by the employer.
