As per Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules 1958, Rule 16, a Departmental Enquiry must adhere to the principles of Natural Justice.
In this case, the Petitioner was removed from service on grounds of wilful absence from duty without being given an opportunity for defence or cross-examination.
The enquiry officer was also not aware of the rules for conducting an enquiry.
The order passed based on facts beyond the chargesheet or the enquiry cannot be upheld to the principles of natural justice and violates the right of the Petitioner to produce his defence.
Therefore, the punishment given to the Petitioner cannot be sustained.
As per Paras 17 to 19, the Petition is allowed accordingly.
