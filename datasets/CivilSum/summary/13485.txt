According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, seniority is based on regular appointment and not ad hoc service or stop gap arrangements.
Even if the ad hoc appointment was made in accordance with the Rules, it will not count towards seniority.
The petitioner's ad hoc appointment without being selected by a selection committee for a fixed period cannot be counted towards seniority.
