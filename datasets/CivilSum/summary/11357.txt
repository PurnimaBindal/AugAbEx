According to Article 14, 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India, as well as section 36 of the Electricity Act, 1910 and Rules 8 and 9 of the Electricity Rules, 1979 and the Punjab Electrical Inspectorate (State Service, Class II) Rules, 1979 Recruitment/Prescription of qualifications for recruitment to the post of Assistant Electrical Inspector, the qualifications prescribed under Rule 4B of the Electricity Rules of 1956 do not apply to direct recruitment to the posts of Assistant Electrical Inspector.
