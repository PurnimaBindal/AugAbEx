The transfer of an employee has been challenged under Constitution of India, Article 311 and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Schedule V, Item 7.
The industrial dispute was raised on the ground of mala fide act of victimisation.
However, the plaintiff did not claim that there was any violation of the terms of the contract of service.
An employee who initiates proceedings under the Industrial Disputes Act is not entitled to maintain a suit in the Civil Court.

An order of the trial court under Section 151 of the Civil Procedure Code, 1908 is not appealable.
However, a revision lies.
