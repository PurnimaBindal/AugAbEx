Constitution of India, Articles 311(2) - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rules 5(v), 8, and 9 - An individual who receives a major penalty will have their salary stopped 4 increments with cumulative effect - Rule 5(v) applies to major penalties - Rules 8 and 9 must be followed, which require a regular inquiry - Failure to comply with these rules results in an illegal and overturned decision.
[Paras 15, 16 and 21]  B.
Words and Phrases - When "inquire" is used in the context of departmental inquiry, it means an official investigation by a person with the power to compel testimony and seek the truth of accusations made.
