The Marathwada University Act, 1974, Sections 11(4) and 11(6)(a) - Education - Vice-Chancellor's powers- He holds a position that carries implied powers allowing him to take immediate action under Section 11(4) to put order and check malpractice.

The Marathwada University Act, 1974, Sections 11(6)(a) and 24(I)(xxix) - Disciplinary Action - The Vice-Chancellor's power to regulate the work and conduct of officers does not authorize him to take disciplinary action for removal from service.
[Para 19]
The Marathwada University Act, 1974, Sections 24(1)(xii) and 80 - Dismissal of officer from service - Delegation of power - The Executive Council failed to obtain prior approval from the Chancellor for delegation of power by the Executive Council, leading to the dismissal not being sustainable.
