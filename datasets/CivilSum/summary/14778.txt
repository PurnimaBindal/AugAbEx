Section 42 of the East Punjab Holdings (Consolidation and Prevention of Fragmentation) Act, 1948 is being challenged based on an order issued by the Director of Consolidation of Holdings in Punjab.
The Director issued an order for the partition of land after receiving an application from the proprietors.
However, the order has been quashed due to reliance on the 1997(2) PLJ 375 judgement.
