Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay scales - Petitioners serving as Librarians in private aided colleges - Director Public Instructions announced granting of revised higher pay scales, subjected to certain conditions - Pre-existing Librarians hired before December 31, 1972 were protected - However, those Librarians who were recruited after December 31, 1972 and still had the University Grant Commission's required qualifications were given the revised pay scales - The Petitioners, who were employed from 1959 to October 6, 1979 and had the required qualifications mentioned in the letter dated 21121989 were also eligible for the pay scales revision, i.e. Rs. 700-1600/2200-4000.
