"The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 and various regulations of the West Bengal Council of Higher Secondary Education lay out mandatory rules for admission to a particular stream of study, including conditions as to eligibility, change of subject, and compliance with essential conditions.
The authority cannot make any exemptions or exceptions to these rules, and the rule of estoppel does not apply if a petitioner was wrongly admitted.
[Paras 5 to 7]"
