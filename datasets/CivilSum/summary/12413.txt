The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo), 10 and 25-F requires employers to issue a notice/charge-sheet and pay retrenchment compensation if terminating an employee.
A recent Labour Court case held that a terminated employee who had worked for 240 days was entitled to reinstatement with continuity of service and full back wages.
The court deemed the termination retrenchment within the meaning of section 2(oo) of the Act, and found the impugned award was justified.
If the department has no vacancy against which the employee could be regularised, retrenchment in accordance with the law is legal.
The petition was therefore dismissed.
