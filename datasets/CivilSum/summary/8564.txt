According to Section 402 of the Companies Act, 1956, the Articles of Association state that a petitioner was appointed as Managing-cum-Chairman until the conclusion of the fifth annual general meeting of the defendant company by a resolution of Board of Directors on 6274.
However, the court appointed an Administrator of the company on 23575.
As a result, the petitioner cannot be granted salary beginning from 1675 onward since he had ceased to work in that capacity after the appointment of the Administrator, as stated in the suit.
