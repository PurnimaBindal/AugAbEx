Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical Expenses - Reimbursement - The Principal of a Government School underwent heart surgery at Batra Hospital in Delhi and submitted a bill for Rs. 1,18,000/-.
The State Government only paid Rs. 82,000/-, leaving a remaining amount of Rs. 36,000/- and Rs. 10,780/-, plus interest.
It was contended that Batra Hospital was not recognized by the State Government.
The court held that the claim would be decided in light of the instructions valid in 1993 and determined afresh by the government, while dismissing the petition for interest.
[Paras 3 and 6]
