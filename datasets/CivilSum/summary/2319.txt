Under Section 3 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 and Sections 124-A, 128 of the Railway Act, 1989, when granting compensation, it was decided that any compensation received under the 1923 Act should be deducted from what is granted under the 1989 Act.
Additionally, a beneficiary can only recover compensation under one law for claims related to the same accident.
