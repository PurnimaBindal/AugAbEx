The Army Rules, 1954, Rule 22 and the Constitution of India's Articles 226 and 311 are related to natural justice.
In this case, the petitioner was charged with theft under Rule 22, but an alleged statement he made during the hearing was not provided to him.
The charge was for stealing an almirah that it would have been impossible for one person to remove.
The authority showed a total non-application of mind, and the dismissal order has been quashed.
