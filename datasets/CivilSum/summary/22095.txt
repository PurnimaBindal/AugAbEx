According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, promotion and selection of Research Officers can be determined through merit-cum-seniority or seniority-cum-merit.
Seniority plays a secondary role in merit-cum-seniority, whereas if selection is seniority-cum-merit, merit will reflect only fitness and suitability.
The most senior candidate will be preferred only if two candidates possess equal merit.
