The denial of a claim for compassionate appointment was based on an order passed by respondents who assessed the assets and liabilities of the deceased.
While the provisions of the scheme were not challenged, it was held that the matter falls squarely under the judgment in Umesh Kumar Nagpal's case.
The objective of granting compassionate appointment is to help the family of the deceased to manage sudden crises, and not to offer post for post to a family member.
The respondents examined the petitioner's claim according to policy and reached a conclusion that does not require intervention.
