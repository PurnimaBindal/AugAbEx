Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - Public Authority - Public Duty - Judicial Review - The Writ jurisdiction can review the action of a person or authority performing public duty, including the enforcement of statutory rights such as the right to livelihood.
A government-owned company is subject to writ jurisdiction when the question of someone's livelihood arises.

Constitution of India Articles 12 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act: Sections 25F and 25G - Contract of Service - Retrenchment actions that violate Sections 25F and 25G of the Industrial Disputes Act are not purely contractual rights or obligations.
A workman has a right to enforce their statutory rights under Sections 25F and 25G of the Industrial Disputes Act, which are entirely statutory.
