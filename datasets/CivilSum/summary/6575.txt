Constitution of India, Article 311 and 16 - Admission to BE on basis of Joint Lateral Entry Engineering Test, 1994 (LEET-94) - Petitioners having Diploma in respective Branch of Engineering were admitted to third semester on being successful in LEET-94 - Result of 3rd and 4th Semesters not declared by the respondent-University - Petitioners not issued Roll numbers for 5th Semester examination - High Court directed Respondent-University to grant permission provisionally to appear in the fifth semester examination - Petition admitted for hearing and petitioners in the meantime completed all semesters - Result still not declared.
[Paras 2 to 5] After over a year of admission, respondent-University directed to shift petitioners to another Engineering College in Punjab where regulations permit lateral entry of such students to the third semester of the four-year Bachelor of Engineering Degree Course.
[Para 7] Court orders implementation of respondent-University's decision and directs university to declare results of petitioners for various semester examinations they appeared under court order or otherwise.
