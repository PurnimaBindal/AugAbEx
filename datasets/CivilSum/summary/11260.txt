According to Rajasthan State Electricity Board Ministerial Staff Regulations, 1962, Regulation 6, class IV employees who acquire higher qualifications are given reservation in promotion to the post of LDC.
An amendment to the regulation provides for promotion strictly on the basis of seniority.
The petitioner was promoted relying on the unamended regulation, but was later reverted after working as an LDC for 14 years.
The respondents argued that the promotion was made against the amended regulation, but it was held that reverting the petitioner after 14 years of service, with seniority as LDC intact, was not sustainable.
The petition was therefore allowed.
