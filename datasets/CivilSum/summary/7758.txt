Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pension - Petitioner requests pension for the post of Principal which was held under interim orders of court - Petitioner was denied promotion by members of DPC, relying on unconveyed adverse remarks - Adverse remarks were given after the fact - Petitioner sought legal intervention - Court ruled the by-passing of Petitioner in DPC was illegal - Therefore, Petitioner is entitled to pension of post from which he retired.
