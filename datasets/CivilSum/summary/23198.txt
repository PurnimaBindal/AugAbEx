Orissa Lokpal and Lokayukta Act, 1970, Section 3 - Appellant, who retired as Chief Justice of Orissa High Court, was appointed as Lokpal on 1781989 under Section 3 of the 1970 Act.
However, the Act was repealed by the 1992 Ordinance, and he ceased to hold the office of Lokpal.
The question is whether the appellant is entitled to any compensation for loss of salary for the remainder period of his tenure as Lokpal, which stood curtailed by reason of later enactment.
It is held that the appellant should be compensated for the restrictions attached and not for deprivation of the office.
The court has issued directions accordingly.
[Paras 8 and 9]
If the appointment is for a tenure and the post is abolished, then there would be no entitlement to compensation.
