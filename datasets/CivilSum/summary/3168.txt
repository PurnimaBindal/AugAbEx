Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate appointment - The rejection of the petitioner's application for compassionate employment on the basis of being time-barred is upheld.
It is held that the claim for compassionate employment is not a matter of vested right and cannot be enforced at any time.
The petitioner shall be paid ex gratia compensation of Rs. 25,000/- as directed.
The order of Single Judge directing employment with back wages is set aside.
[Paras 5 and 13]
