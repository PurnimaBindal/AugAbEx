Contempt of Court Act, 1971, Section 14 - Contempt of Supreme Court - The senior law officers, as well as the Medical Education Department officers, intentionally misinterpreted a Supreme Court order regarding quota in admissions to medical, dental and engineering courses.
Their conduct was found to be deliberate and aimed at achieving an illegitimate benefit.
They are guilty of Contempt of Court and their censured conduct should be recorded in their service record.
Their unqualified apology has been rejected and there was no room for bona fide error.
[Paras 8 to 10]
