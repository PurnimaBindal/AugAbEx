Rule 8.127 of Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume I Part I, deals with maternity leave on adoption of a child.
However, as per instructions issued by the Government of Haryana, maternity leave on adoption is not applicable to employees of Punjab.
The court cannot compel the State to issue a circular granting similar benefits to its employees when the rules framed by the State do not grant such relief specifically or even by necessary implication.
[Para 5]

Article 226 of the Indian Constitution deals with judicial review of matters related to State policy.
However, the scope of judicial review is limited, and decisions regarding State policy squarely fall under the domain of the government.
Normally, the court will not compel the State to take any action.
