Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination - The termination of the Assistant Government Pleader's services were based on allegations of misbehaviour made by other advocates and a report from a Special Officer.
Government Pleaders are not subordinate to the Special Officer who supervises their offices, and their appointments are treated as engagement of a Counsel by the State as a litigant.
The order of termination casts a stigma on the petitioner-appellant, therefore must be nullified.
The impugned order of termination was set aside.
[Paras 6 and 7]
