Section 48 of the Border Security Force Act is para materia to Section 71 of the Army Act, 1950.
The petitioner was tried by the security force court and dismissed from the service, but the judgment of punishment does not imply forfeiture of pension, gratuity, and other benefits.
As a result, even though the petitioner was dismissed from service, they are entitled to all of the benefits of a retired person.
