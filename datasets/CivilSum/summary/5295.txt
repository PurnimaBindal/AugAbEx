Constitution of India, Article 226 and 227 - Management Rules, 1963, Rule 28(8) - Absence - Transfer sought - Petitioner joined the institution with the intervention of a Special Officer appointed by the court.
The petitioner is now seeking financial benefits for the period of absence.
However, there is a dispute as to whether the employee attended work or performed the work allotted or not, and the reasons for the same.
In this regard, an inquiry needs to be conducted to establish any omission or commission from the employee's part before any deductions from wages can be made.
Therefore, the matter has been referred to the Director of School Education for decision and the provision of benefits if any.
