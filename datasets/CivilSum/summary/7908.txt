The Tribunal granted equal pay to the petitioners, which was paid to Delhi Police drivers.
This decision was challenged on the grounds that the conditions of service, educational qualifications, and method of recruitment differed between the petitioners and Delhi Police drivers.
Since the Union of India did not appear before the Tribunal, the matter needs to be considered afresh, and it was remanded back for a fresh decision.
[Paras 11, 14, 15, and 16]

The doctrine of equal pay for equal work, as mentioned under Article 39 of the Constitution of India, can be applicable when a court of law clearly finds that the duties and responsibilities performed by both parties are at par with all respects, including educational qualifications and experience.
This finding would enable them to receive the said benefit.
