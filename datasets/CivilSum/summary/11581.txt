Section 21 of the Administrative Tribunals Act 1985 and Section 5 of the Limitation Act 1963 - Terminal Benefits - Petitioner seeks terminal benefits after 25 years of death of employee - Application deemed maintainable as terminal benefits are a recurring payment and non-payment constitutes a continuous legal injury.
[Para 2]
Railways Act, 1989, Section 136 - Railways Establishment Manual, Rules 2311(3)(b), 2318 - Manual of Railway Pension Rules, Para 801 - Terminal Benefits - Grant of - CAT rejected claim on ground that deceased had not been regularly absorbed - Contention that as per rules benefit will not be given to person holding temporary position - Held, employee can receive benefits by way of concession, as Rule 2311(3)(b) is an artificial situation.
