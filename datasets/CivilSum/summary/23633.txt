Constitution of India, Article 300-A - High Court Judges' Rules, 1956, Rule 2 - All India Services (Death-cum-Retirement Benefits) Rules, 1958 - Death-cum-Retirement Gratuity - A retired High Court Judge who served prior to October 1, 1974 is eligible to receive the same Death-cum-Retirement Gratuity as an officer of the Indian Administrative Service in the rank of the Secretary.
However, if a High Court Judge did not complete seven years of service, they are not eligible for this benefit.
