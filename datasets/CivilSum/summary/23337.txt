The Indian Ordnance Factories (Recruitment and Conditions of Service of Class III Personnel), Rules, 1956, Rules 3(1), 8 and 12 discuss seniority and appointments by promotion, and the Director General Ordnance Factories issued an order granting ante dated seniority to the appellants.
However, the Supreme Court in Paluru's case has ruled that the appellants' claim in Civil Appeal No. 441/81, upon which they had based their claim, was not correctly decided.
Therefore, the appellants are left with no ground to sustain the order dated February 20/25, 1987, by which they were given ante-dated seniority.
As per the reasoning in the judgment of Paluru's case, the impugned judgment of CAT in this case is upheld, and the appeal is dismissed.
