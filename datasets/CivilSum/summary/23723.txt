Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Osmania University Act, 1959, Section 38(1) - The university increased the age of superannuation for teaching staff to 60 years, but non-teaching staff claimed for uniformity in superannuation age which was accepted by the High Court as per the provisions of Section 38(1) of the Act.
The decision of the High Court was considered valid, as the university is allowed to have different superannuation ages for different employees, as long as it is not impractical or undesirable to bring uniformity, the mandate of Section 38(1) should be followed.
