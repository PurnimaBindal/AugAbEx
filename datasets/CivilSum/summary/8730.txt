Civil Procedure Code, Section 47 - Reinstatement - Execution of decree of declaration - If a JD objects to the execution of a decree of declaration, the executing court must address the objection with a speaking order.
Simply overruling the objection with a cryptic order is unacceptable as the executing court must provide reasons for its decision.
The court is directed to issue a speaking order supported by clear reasons.
[Paras 4 to 6]
