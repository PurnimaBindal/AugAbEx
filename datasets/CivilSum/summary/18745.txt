Constitution of India, Article 16 - Selection - Suppression of material fact - When a petitioner's selection is being withdrawn due to a certificate relating to date of birth being found erroneous, the petitioner should be given an opportunity for a personal hearing to prove that the mistake was not intentional, but rather a typographical error.
