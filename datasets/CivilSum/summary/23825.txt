Constitution of India, Articles 233(1) and (2) - Advocates Act, Section 2(a) - Bar Council Rules, Rule 49 - Delhi Higher Judicial Service Rules, 1970, Rules 5 and 7 - Appointment - Recruitment to Delhi Higher Judicial Service invites applications from candidates who practiced as advocates - Government advocates were not called for an interview, but they should be allowed to participate in the recruitment process - An advocate employed by the Government or a body corporate as a law officer, even on payment of a salary, would not cease to be an advocate - The object of recruitment would not be defeated if these persons are allowed to participate in the recruitment process - As the recruitment process is complete, the appellants cannot be given relief - The selected candidates have been appointed, and as the appellants were not impleaded as a party, their appointment cannot be upset - A direction is given to process applications for the recruitment of candidates in the future.
