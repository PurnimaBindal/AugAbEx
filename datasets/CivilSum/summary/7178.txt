Delhi Police (Appointment and Recruitment) Rules, 1980, Rules 9 and 24 - Appointment for the post of Constable - Cancellation of candidature - Debarment on ground of `partial colour blindness' - Rule 9 does not supersede Rule 24 and Appendix - Rule 24 and Appendix are specific provisions which create an exception to general bar in Rule 9 - Colour blindness is a requirement for drivers and those controlling traffic only - Petitioner selected for post of constable was unjustly deprived of it due to debarring him on ground of colour blindness, which was contrary to Rule 24 read with appendix - Provisions barring recruitment to certain specific categories due to colour blindness have rational basis and differential treatment based on job requirement.
Citing 1994 Supp (2) SCC 52 [Paras 9, 12 and 13]
