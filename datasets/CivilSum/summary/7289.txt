Constitution of India, Article 309 - UP Absorption of Retrenched Employees of Government or Public Corporation in Government Service Rules, 1991, Rule 2 - Absorption - Retrenched employees cannot be absorbed if they were employed by an institute registered as a society, such as the Institute of Engineering and Rural Technology (IERT) - IERT is not an instrumentality of the State Government or a State Corporation, and its employees are not employed by the State Government - Therefore, retrenched employees of IERT are not entitled to the benefits of the rules - Any order granting such benefits would not be proper.
[Paras 13 to 16]
