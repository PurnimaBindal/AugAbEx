The profession of a Chartered Accountant is regulated by the Chartered Accountants Act, 1956, and the office of a Chartered Accountant is not considered a commercial establishment under the Jammu & Kashmir Shops and Establishment Act, 1966; as per Notification SRO No. 740 dated 13111978, it has been held ultra-vires.
Duty of Chartered Accountants is limited to verifying the records of business transactions.
