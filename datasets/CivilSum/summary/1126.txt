Constitution of India, Article 16 and 226 - Recruitment - Reservation - Roster Register - Petitioners challenge the orders of District Judge appointing some of them on an ad hoc basis and not appointing others to any posts despite being selected in pursuance of recruitment notification.
However, being empaneled or selected does not confer a vested right for appointment, especially when the District Judge's panel was not approved by the High Court on the administrative side.
The petitioners cannot claim appointment despite the wrong identification of vacancies for various social groups.
The committee's decision to remedy the situation arising out of non-maintenance of roster for a long time to ensure the rule of reservation is observed in its true spirit is reasonable.
