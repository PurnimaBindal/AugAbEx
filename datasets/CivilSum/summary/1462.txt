Border Security Force Act, Section 62 - Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rules 19(B), 36 - Unauthorised absence - Court of enquiry - Order of dismissal - The petitioner was absent without authorization for 174 days and did not challenge the proceedings of the enquiry.
The petitioner did absent himself on two earlier occasions and did not give any explanation for not joining duty.
It was held that even though the petitioner's wife was not admitted to any hospital, he did not join duty on the basis of her medical certificate advising home treatment.
The medical certificates seem to have been obtained in order to justify the absence.
Moreover, the petitioner is a disciplined force member, so a person who is a habitual offender may not be treated leniently.
