Army Pension Regulations, Regulations 173(A) and 179 - Army Service Pension Rules, Annexure II, Para 7 - Disability Pension - The Army authorities are responsible for proving that a disease suffered by an invalidated person was not attributable or aggravated by Army service once they are invalidated on medical grounds - The presumption is that any disease not noted at the time of entry into Army service was attributable to or aggravated by Army service - A case where epilepsy is rejected as a disability cannot be the sole basis for rejecting disability pension papers - Only when a person's disability is more than 20% can they receive disability pension - The authorities must release the petitioner's disability pension from 38 months prior to filing the petition.
