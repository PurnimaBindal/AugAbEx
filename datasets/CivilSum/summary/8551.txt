Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Rules for the Management of Recognised Non-Government Institutions (Aided and Unaided), 1969, Rules 4(a) and 5(b)(c) - Appointment - Empanelment - Despite one candidate producing a forged letter at the interview, the appellant who appeared in the same interview cannot be denied empanelment - The guilty party must instead face appropriate punishment - Holding fresh interviews would be unfair to the innocent interviewees - The old panel must therefore be sent back to the Selection Committee for the formation of a new panel based on the previous interview - This new panel must be approved by the District Inspector of Schools in accordance with the Recruitment Rules.
