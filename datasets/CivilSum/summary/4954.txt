Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Reference - Limits of Appropriate Govt.'s power and jurisdiction - Appropriate Govt. can only consider admitted or undisputed or undisputable facts to determine whether there is an industrial dispute and whether it should be referred for the sake of industrial peace and harmony.
The Govt. cannot overstep its limited jurisdiction by investigating the merits of a dispute or acting as an adjudicator on disputed or disputable facts, as this is the role of the Tribunal.
Here, the Govt. overstepped its jurisdiction by determining that the workers had abandoned their job, were contract labor rather than actual employees, and were seasonal labor.
Hence, the Govt.'s rejection of the reference was liable to be set aside.
