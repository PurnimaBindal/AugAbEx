Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1970, Section 19 - Leave encashment - Employee who was suspended during disciplinary proceedings and reinstated later is not eligible for privilege leave during the period of his suspension until reinstatement - Eligibility for privilege leave depends on actual service rendered by the employee - Therefore, the employee is not entitled for relief of leave encashment.
