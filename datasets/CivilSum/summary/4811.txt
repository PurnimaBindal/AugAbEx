Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F, G and H - Daily wage worker - Termination of service - Award denying relief - Claim of no retrenchment compensation paid, juniors working, and violation of principles of natural justice - Petitioner absent and left job - Labour Court ruled that management not required to serve notice since worker did not return after demand notice sent after 5-1/2 years and no reasonable excuse for delay - Section 25F, G, and H applied if petitioner willing to resume work - No time limit for reference under Act, but late reference invalid - Also, Section 25-F inapplicable to casual laborers.
