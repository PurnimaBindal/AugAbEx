Payment of Wages (Mines) Rules, 1956, Rule 22 - Complaint - The prosecution asserted that the complainants had not properly maintained the register in Form 'B' according to Rule 5A because photographs were not affixed under serial Nos. 97 and 98, and that the compliance report was not satisfactory.
However, Form 'B' in the First Schedule to the Mines Rules does not specify the requirement to affix photographs against serial numbers, so the register in Form 'B' could not be deemed to have been improperly maintained simply because photographs had not been affixed under serial Nos. 97 and 98.
Moreover, the complaint petition did not clearly state how the compliance report submitted by the employer was deemed unsatisfactory and the allegation to this effect was vague.
It was essential for the prosecution to identify the specific Officer in default, which was not averred in the complaint petition.
Therefore, the cognizance of the offence was set aside against the petitioners.
