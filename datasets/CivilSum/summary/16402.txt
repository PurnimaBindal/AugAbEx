The respondent's termination was due to a fabricated Certificate of BA Part III.
Their appointment in another department on compassionate grounds, at a non-existent post, based on the same qualifications was deemed illegal and arbitrary.
[Paras 4 and 5]

No direct appointment as Sub-Inspector under Punjab Roadways State Service Class III Rules, 1977 is possible, as it is neither a promotional post nor a post under those rules.
The only reason such a post exists is to adjust various cadres to remove stagnation in the higher scale.
Compassionate appointments do not apply to Sub-Inspectors and reservation policy is not applicable.
