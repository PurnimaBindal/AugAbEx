Constitution of India, Articles 16(4) - Reservation for OBCs and SC/ST - Rajasthan State and Subordinate Services (Direct Recruitment by Combined Competitive Examination) Rules, 1962, Rule 13 - Interpretation of proviso to the Rule - The qualifying mark relaxation is only provided for SC/ST candidates, not for OBCs - Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes are a separate class from OBC - The omission of OBCs in the proviso to Rule 13 is not a violation of Article 16(4) of the Constitution - Affirmative action and positive discrimination are built into the equality of opportunity in status - The state has the power to give special benefits to backward classes.
[Paras 13 and 14]
