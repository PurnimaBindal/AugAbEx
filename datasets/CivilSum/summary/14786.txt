The writ petition requests the quashing of an inquiry report and seeks reinstatement in a subsequent writ, with the withdrawal of the FIR and an order by the arbitrator concluding that the charges were not proven on reference.
It is held that two contrary findings by authorities in parallel proceedings must be reconciled, and the dismissal order was passed earlier, so the order by the arbitrator in the award and appellate authority was not given proper consideration.
Under these circumstances, the appellate authority directed to decide the matter afresh, taking into consideration the award, and the impugned orders were set aside.
