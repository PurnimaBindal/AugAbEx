Admission/Education - Admission to Master of Library and Information Science course - One seat reserved for a specific category was filled from the open category - Petitioner was provisionally admitted at the time of filing the petition - Course ended before hearing the petition - Court was supplied the result where the petitioner scored high marks - Admission, even if irregular, is regularised by the court - Provisional admission is regularised - petitioner deemed to have passed.
1992(3) RSJ 102 followed.
