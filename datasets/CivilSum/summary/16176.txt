Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Employment on compassionate grounds - Petitioner requests employment on compassionate grounds after his father's death - Request denied by authority due to petitioner's brother already being employed by the authority - Employment on compassionate grounds is not a right and is solely at the employer's discretion - Redress cannot be sought through writ jurisdiction for this matter - Adequacy of grounds cannot be determined through writ jurisdiction.
[Paras 7 and 8]
