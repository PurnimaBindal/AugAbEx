Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311(2) - Rajasthan Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1958, Section 28-A - Termination of services - Probationer - Natural justice - Services of probationer terminated for poor work without notice - Employees confirmed as regular/permanent have a safeguard of one month's notice or wages in lieu, whereas probationers do not have this protection - Probationary employees have temporary employment and can be terminated with or without notice - Such employees have no right to claim continued services.
