Petitioners sought reinstatement with backwages under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F.
They argued that the Respondent failed to provide work and serve notices under Section 25F of the Act.
However, the Respondent gave notice to the Petitioners informing them that there is no work along with a demand draft of amount towards bonus, service compensation, gratuity, casual leave and notice salary.
The court held that reading of notices indicate that one month's notice, salary, and compensation have been specifically given.
Therefore, said notice would be considered sufficient compliance of Section 25F of the Act and thus, the petition was dismissed accordingly.
