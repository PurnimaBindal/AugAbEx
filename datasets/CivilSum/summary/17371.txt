Constitution of India, Articles 310(1) and 311(2) - M.P. Municipal Act, 1961, Sections 19(2), 41(4) and 36 - Termination - Doctrine of pleasure - Termination of nominated Councillor - A person nominated to Nagar Panchayat as Councillor by the State Government is subject to the Doctrine of pleasure engrafted in Article 310, and holds office during the pleasure of State Govt. - However, the nominee's office is co-terminus with the pleasure of the State Govt. - Therefore, once steps are taken in exercise of Doctrine of pleasure, the nominee cannot raise any grievance, and the question of calling explanation, issuing show cause notice, or giving opportunity does not arise.
