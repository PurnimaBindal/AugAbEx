Constitution of India, Articles 311(2) - Natural justice - Hearing - Dismissal from service - The petitioner, who was working as a Bank Manager, was charge-sheeted and an enquiry was held against them.
The enquiry officer submitted a report that didn't comply with the bank's regulations, and the officer was directed to submit a more exhaustive report with reference to the relevant evidence.
However, before submitting the fresh report, the petitioner was not given an opportunity to be heard, which violated the principles of natural justice.
Denial of natural justice is itself a prejudice, and no proof of prejudice can be asked for from the person affected.
The order of dismissal and the enquiry report were quashed, with liberty to proceed afresh following the principles of natural justice, as per Punjab National Bank Officer Employees (D&A) Regulations, 1977, Regulation 4, and New Bank of India Officer Employees (D&A) Regulations, 1982, Regulation 6(21).
