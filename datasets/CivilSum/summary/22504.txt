Allegation of professional misconduct against an advocate by a complainant who was represented by the same advocate up to the appeal stage.
However, no objection was taken by the complainant.
As a result, the allegations were not proven.
[Paras 9 and 10] B.
In cases of professional misconduct by an advocate, the evidence adduced for an enquiry by the Disciplinary Committee should be beyond all reasonable doubt.
