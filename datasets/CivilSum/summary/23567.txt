Central Bank of India Officer Employees' 8 (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976, Regulations 24 and 3 - Misconduct - Dismissal - The respondent exceeded his authority by allowing overdrafts or passing cheques for substantial amounts - Although some of these actions did not result in loss for the bank, others yielded profits.
However, findings suggest that several advances and overdrawals allowed by the respondent beyond his authority have become irrecoverable.
The respondent's actions were not a single act, but rather a course of action spread over time and involving many transactions.
Any employee or officer of a bank should act and operate within their given jurisdiction to ensure discipline within the organization.
Exceeding one's authority is a breach of discipline and Regulation 3.
Such actions are defined as misconduct under Regulation 24.
The High Court was mistaken in determining that the respondent's conduct did not violate Regulations 3 and 24.
