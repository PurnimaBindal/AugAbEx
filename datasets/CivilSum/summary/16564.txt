Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - West Bengal Primary School Teachers' Recruitment Rules, 2001, Rule 8 - Employment Exchanges (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959, Section 4 - Selection - Participation in selection process - Candidates seeking post of Primary School Teacher must have their names requisitioned by employment exchanges as per Rule 8 of West Bengal Primary School Teachers' Recruitment Rules, 2001 - Only candidates sponsored by employment exchanges will be considered in selection process - Petitioners not sponsored by employment exchanges are not entitled to participate in selection process.
