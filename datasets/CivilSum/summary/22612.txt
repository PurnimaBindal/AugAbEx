In accordance with the Prevention of Corruption Act and Constitution of India, a public servant charged both criminally and departmentally for possessing assets disproportionate to their income was subjected to a departmental enquiry that resulted in their dismissal.
However, despite being acquitted in the criminal case, the order of dismissal was set aside.
[Para 21]
While under investigation for possessing assets disproportionate to income, a public servant was charged criminally and dismissed from service as a result of a departmental enquiry.
Despite being acquitted in the criminal case, the order of dismissal was set aside due to the overlapping of witnesses in both cases.
The appellant was entitled to pension but not back wages as they had already retired.
