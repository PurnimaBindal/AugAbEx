Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Sections 10(1) and (2) - Factors mentioned in the Act not to be considered by writ court - The responsibility of considering factors mentioned in Section 10(2) of the Act lies with the Appropriate Authority (Government) - The enquiry that considers these factors has quasi-judicial nature - Writ court shouldn't order absorption but direct the appropriate authority to follow the rules.
