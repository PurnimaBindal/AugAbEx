Constitution of India, Article 226 - Indian Penal Code, Section 325 - Probation of Offenders Act, Sections 43 and 12 - Dismissal from service - A petitioner was convicted by criminal court under section 325 of IPC and dismissed from service, but later released on probation after an appeal.
The dispute that led to the conviction was a sudden fight between two brothers, and the allegations against the petitioner did not involve moral turpitude.
The release on probation removed disqualification according to section 12 of the Probation of Offenders Act.
The State Govt. is required to reinstate the petitioner in service, with continuity of service and all consequential benefits; however, he is not entitled to wage from date of dismissal till his reinstatement.
