Industrial Disputes Acts of 1947, Section 11-B - Punishment - Quantum of punishment - Labor court replaced the termination of a bus conductor found guilty of embezzlement of RS. 615 with the punishment of stopping five increments with cumulative effect - Labor Court's award was implemented - It shouldn't be interfered by the court if the Labor Court has the discretion to substitute a lesser punishment, and justice has been done if it has been exercised judiciously.
