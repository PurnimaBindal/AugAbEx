As per Section 11 of the Regional Rural Banks Act, 1976, UCO Bank had a statutory obligation to appoint the Chairman of the Regional Rural Bank.
The petitioner, who was a Zonal Manager, was sent to the Rural Bank on deputation without his consent under the UCO Bank (Officers) Service Regulations, 1979, Regulations 23(V) and 50.
The Regulations do not require the consent of the transferee for deputation to the Rural Bank.
[Paras 11 & 12] B.
The posting of the petitioner as Chairman of the Rural Bank from the post of Zonal Manager did not entail any reduction in salary, perks or status.
He received deputation allowance over and above his salary, and the allegations of mala fide were not substantiated.
The transfer does not hinge on whether the post confers the same powers as the previous post held by the employee.
