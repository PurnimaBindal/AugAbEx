Orissa Aided Educational Institutions (Appointment of Teachers Validation) Act, 1989, Section 3 - Validation of certain appointments - The Petitioner's ad hoc appointment was validated and considered regular employment.
However, teachers appointed on the recommendation of the Selection Board or whose appointments were validated prior to the commencement of the Act cannot be placed below those whose services have been validated under the Validation Act.
This ensures that the teachers are not at a disadvantageous position.
While fixing inter se seniority, teachers whose services have been validated under the Act shall be placed above those whose services have been validated under the Validation Act.
Hence, the petitioner cannot be considered senior to those appointed on the recommendation of the Selection Board.
However, the petitioner's request for regularization of services in relation to break periods and vacations has been accepted.
