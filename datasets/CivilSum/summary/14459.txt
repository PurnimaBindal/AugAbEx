Punjab Conservation and Engineering Service (Class-I) Rules, 1978, Rule 8 - Appointment - Under the Rule, 50% of Divisional Soil Conservation Officer posts must be filled by direct recruitment, and 50% through promotion from Assistant Soil Conservation Officers.
Appointments of direct recruits were stayed due to promotees, until directions were issued to determine the number of vacancies for direct recruitment.
Appointment to those vacancies had to be made through the prevalent method when the vacancies became available, and subsequent instructions cannot replace statutory rules.
[Para 5]

Appointment - Selection - Withholding of appointment after selection for direct recruitment, due to a stay at the instance of ad hoc appointees holding the posts.
The delay cannot be attributed to the selected candidate.
[Para 5]
