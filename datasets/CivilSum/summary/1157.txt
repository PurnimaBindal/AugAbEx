Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Discharge from Service - Probation - If a Constable is found inefficient, the competent authority can discharge him from service - The competent authority concluded that the petitioner did not improve despite opportunities and is not likely to become an efficient police officer - The authority formed its opinion after considering various acts of absenteeism during the probation period - If the opinion is formed after consideration of factors, it cannot be arbitrary or perverse.
[Paras 10-12] B.
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 12.21 and Rules 16 - Enquiry - Natural justice - If the competent authority decides to discharge an employee under Rule 1221 based on allegations of absence, it must give notice to the employee and provide an opportunity to explain his conduct - No regular enquiry is necessary to form an opinion under Rule 1221 - Order of discharge does not indicate any misconduct and does not cast any stigma on the employee - Neither Article 311 nor Rule 16 applies - Order passed under Rule 1221 is valid.
[Paras 8, 13 and 14] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punishment - Discrimination in awarding punishment - Allegations of discrimination are not enough to prove the occurrence of discrimination - The petitioner must provide full facts of discrimination on record to enable the court to record sufficient findings.
