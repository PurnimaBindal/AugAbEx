Article 226 of the Indian Constitution pertains to Admissions/Education.
Universities and institutions implement their own programmes of studies, exams and admissions.
Courts cannot direct uniform programmes or time schedules throughout the country.
Each institution announces its own admission dates.
Setting cut-off dates is necessary to finalize admissions and cannot be considered arbitrary.
