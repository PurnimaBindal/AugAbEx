Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Appointment/Eligibility - Instructions for temporary appointments in place of striking Junior Engineers from amongst the 'Under-Employed Diploma holders' - The petitioners, who were previously work munshies, were already retrenched, making them ineligible for temporary appointment as Junior Engineers despite being diploma holders.
