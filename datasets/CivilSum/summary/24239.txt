The UP State Road Transport Corporation Employee (Other than Officers) Service Registration, 1981, Regulations 17(2) and 17(3) give discretion to provide alternative employment to drivers who become medically unfit.
However, this discretion must not be exercised arbitrarily but rather must be exercised reasonably and impartially.
[Paras 8, 9 and 15] B.
Guidelines for exercising discretion given to Authority under UP State Transport Corporation Employees (Other than Officers) Service 17(3) which provides alternative employment to disabled drivers.
