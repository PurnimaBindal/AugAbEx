According to Clause 2 of the UP Secondary Education Service Commission (Removal of Difficulties) (Second) Order, 1981, and Section 16-G of the UP Intermediate Education Act, 1921, the approval post of Assistant Teacher demands proper financial approval from the Committee of Management.
Though the petitioner has been given a short term appointment, the lapse of Management couldn't be taken adversely against her.
Therefore, refusal of approval along with the appointment is not sustainable.
