Punjab Recruitment of Ex-servicemen Rules, 1982, Rule 4(1) - Reservation of Ex-servicemen category - Amendment in Rule - The percentage of reservation for the Ex-servicemen category was reduced according to an amendment in the rules.
The earlier rule was superseded, and the amended rule substituted the words 14% for 15%.
The amended rule shows that 8% of posts are reserved for the Ex-servicemen (General) category to which the petitioner belonged.
The petitioner was not selected because their marks were less than the last candidate in the waiting list, and by no stretch of imagination could they be selected as an SS Master.
[Para 7] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Punjab Recruitment of Ex-servicemen Rules, 1982, Rule 4(1) - Writ of mandamus - Process of selection - The petitioner has no legal right to be selected as an SS Master against the Ex-Servicemen (General) category, and the selection process was not found to be illegal.
Therefore, a writ of mandamus cannot be issued to enforce something that is not a legal right.
