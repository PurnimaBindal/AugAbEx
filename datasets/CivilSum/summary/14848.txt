The Assam Medical Attendance Rules, 1976, Rules 11, 14, and 18, along with Articles 309, 14, and 16 of the Constitution of India, state that medical reimbursement is available to those who have incurred expenses on kidney transplantation.
The petitioner received the treatment in a hospital outside the State, without the prior approval of the Director of Health Services, however, the Government has the power to approve treatment in hospitals outside the State, even if those hospitals are not recognised.
Refusal to reimburse the petitioner is discriminatory and a violation of Article 14.
