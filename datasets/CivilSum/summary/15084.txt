Constitution of India, Article 311 - Removal from service - Member of armed forces - Petitioner's brother suffered a severe paralytic attack - Immediate superior officer refused to grant leave - Petitioner lost temper and raised their voice -  Court held that imposed penalty was shockingly disproportionate - In case of shockingly disproportionate punishment, the Court can adjust relief - Punishment of lowering to initial pay with withholding of two annual increments was imposed.
