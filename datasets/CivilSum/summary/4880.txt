Constitution of India, Articles 226, 311 - Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Section 103 - Appointment - Post of teacher - Under Rehbar-e-Taleem Scheme, appointments are made through a village level committee after assessing deficiency, drawing a panel of eligible persons, and submitting it through the Zonal Education Officer to DC, who along with Director School Education makes the final selection - The petitioner's name was above the private respondent on the panel - The respondents do not dispute the petitioner's merit - It is argued the petitioner was not suitable due to her being female and the area being militant prone - The court held that the DC cannot discriminate against the petitioner on the basis of sex, and the petitioner had a legal right to be selected due to her better merit - The selection is arbitrary and is quashed.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Post of teacher - Challenge to selection - Once a challenge to selection is made after appointment, it by itself contemplates challenge to appointment and consequent order - Non-challenging of appointment order cannot affect the maintainability of the writ petition.
