Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Seniority list - In 1976, seniority of parties was established - The respondent did not dispute his seniority status until 1994 - The Tribunal granted the OA claim - Examining the jurisdiction of the Tribunal, it was concluded that seniority is a civil right and should not be disturbed after a long period of time- The principle of law has been established that seniority list should not be changed, once settled- The delay in raising objections has negated equity - The contested order is vacated - Petition allowed as a result.
