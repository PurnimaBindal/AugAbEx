The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Evidence Act, Sections 45 and 47 - Expert Evidence - Handwriting expert - Reports and opinions of an expert are evaluated by the court independently to reach a conclusion.
The court cannot exclude expert reports based solely on past criticisms.
It is the court's duty to consider and form its own opinion, though the expert's credibility may be considered.
Even if the expert is unable to form a definitive opinion, the court cannot reverse findings of guilt based on the report.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Criminal proceedings - Disciplinary proceedings - Acquittal in criminal charge does not absolve misconduct charges.
The approach and objectives of criminal and disciplinary proceedings are different, with varying standards of proof.
An acquittal in criminal proceedings does not prevent the initiation of disciplinary action.
[Para 17]  C. The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Judicial review - Scope limited to correcting errors of law, procedural errors leading to manifest injustice, or violations of natural justice principles.
Writ courts do not act as appellate courts.
[Para 18]  D. The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Bank employee - Misconduct - Bank employees must exercise vigilance and care in handling public funds.
Negligence can result in action against a high position holding employee.
