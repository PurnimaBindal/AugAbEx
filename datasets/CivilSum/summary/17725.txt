According to Clause 8 of Chapter II of the Guru Nanak Dev University Calendar on Unfair means/Examination, a student who is caught using unfair means is allowed to answer the remaining questions on a fresh answer sheet and appear in the remaining papers until the competent authority finds him guilty and punishes him accordingly.
However, the clause does not prevent the authority from taking further action against the student once he is found guilty.
This clause does not follow the ruling of Manisha Sharma's case (1994(2) RSJ 573), which is overruled.
