According to the Constitution of India, as per Articles 14 and 16, a Government employee must adhere to a transfer order and cannot refuse it.
If the employee has a valid grievance, it is recommended that they represent their concerns after relocating to their new place of work.
[Para 10] B.
If a Government servant is absent from work, they may be punished with leave of the kind due for the duration of their absence.
This punishment is a lenient view taken by the authority.
It is not advisable for a Government servant to refuse to join their new place of posting in protest against their transfer.
