Constitution of India, Article 14 - Suspension - Interlocutory injunction - The Respondent, who was suspended pending departmental inquiry on the charge of misappropriation while discharging his duties as the Armed Police Sub Inspector, had his suspension order challenged and stayed by a Civil Court.
The State appealed, arguing that the Trial Court acted illegally by granting the injunction order as the suspension order was already in effect when the lawsuit and injunction application were filed.
The State also argued that the Trial Court erred in concluding that the order of suspension must state reasons in detail, as no such law exists.
[Paras 5 and 6] B.
Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Order 39, Rules 1 and 2 - Interlocutory order - Suspension - As per this rule, the subjective satisfaction of the concerned authority is sufficient to pass the suspension order, which is an administrative order impliciter.
An interlocutory order cannot be passed against an order of suspension.
The impugned judgment by the courts below was set aside as it cannot be sustained.
