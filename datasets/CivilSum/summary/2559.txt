The question of whether an authority is deemed to be a principal employer under the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act cannot be raised at the appellate stage if it was not raised earlier.
This is according to Civil Procedure Code order 41, Rule 1.
[Para 15]
The Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act defines "Workman" in S. 2(1)(i).
A workman who is employed as contract labour by a principal employer and hired through a contractor would not become a workman of the principal employer due to an alleged violation of the Act.
This is a question of facts.
