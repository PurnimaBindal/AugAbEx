Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11A - Compensation in lieu of reinstatement - Labour Court's jurisdiction - Dispute over whether the Labour Court has the power to grant relief other than reinstatement - Challenge to a compensation award made by the Labour Court - Section 11-A provides wide discretionary powers - Compensation in lieu of reinstatement can be granted in exceptional circumstances - In this case, the Labour Court was justified in granting compensation instead of reinstatement due to special circumstances - The award of the Labour Court was upheld.
