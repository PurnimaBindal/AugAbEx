Constitution of India, Article 226 - Gujarat Gram Panchayats Act, 1961, Section 206 - Deputation - Promotion - The petitioner, who was originally appointed in the irrigation department, opted for allocation in the Panchayat service, and after being repatriated, his order for allocation was restored.
The petitioner claimed entitlement to seniority and promotion and was promoted, but the order was not served and was later cancelled by the respondents.
The court held that the order cancelling promotion and denying benefits to the petitioner was arbitrary, illegal and unreasonable, and ordered the respondents to pass appropriate orders regarding benefits and promotion of the petitioner within three months.
[Paras 10 to 12]
