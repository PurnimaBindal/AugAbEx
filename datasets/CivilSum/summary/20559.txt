Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Migration - Admission - Education - While migration from one University to another in medical courses cannot be claimed as a right, the University cannot make arbitrary decisions regarding migration at the whim of the Vice-Chancellor or Principal of the concerned college.
Guidelines have been established for medical student migration and a committee has been formed for this purpose.
Vice-Chancellors cannot arbitrarily reject committee recommendations, though such recommendations are not binding.
If such recommendations are rejected, the Vice-Chancellor must provide valid reasons.
The trial court's order was restored, and the appellant was permitted to enroll in the Dental College in Rohtak.
If deemed necessary, the authority was directed to grant an additional seat."
