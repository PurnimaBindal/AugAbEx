Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Punjab Police Rules, Rule 9.18(i)(c) - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. 1, Part I, Rule 3.26(d) - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 5.32(c) - Confidential Report - Adverse remarks/Adverse reports - Pre-mature retirement - Natural justice - The petitioner was exonerated from charges by an enquiry officer who found no evidence, and was subsequently reinstated with period of suspension treated as duty.
However, the petitioner's representation against adverse remarks was not communicated to him, denying him the opportunity to prove his case.
This resulted in a material miscarriage of justice, as the action of the DG, Police was based on wrong assumptions and violated the principles of natural justice.
The retirement order passed on the basis of reconstructed adverse reports cannot be sustained, as the action of reconstructing the report vitiates the order.
