Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeals) Rules, 1965, Rules 10(2), 10(6) and 10(7) - Notification dated 242004 - Suspension - Review of Suspension - According to the notification, the suspension order was to be reviewed within 90 days, which should have been counted from the date of notification.
However, the enforcement of Amendment Rules was postponed, causing the old rules to remain in force.
Consequently, the order of suspension was reviewed much later than the stipulated 90-day period.
Because the Tribunal failed to consider the notification, it arrived at a wrong conclusion.
As a result, the Impugned Order was set aside.
