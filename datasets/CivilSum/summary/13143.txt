MMTC Employees Conduct, Discipline and Appeal Rules, 1975, Rule 25 - Principles of natural justice - Relieving an employee upon expiry of term does not amount to termination - Appointment extension subject to CVC clearance - Petitioner relieved without hearing - Order is not stigmatic - As the petitioner's term expired, they were relieved from service and not terminated.
