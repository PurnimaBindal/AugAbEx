Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(kkk) - Lay-off - The scope of "For any other reason" - Lay-off can be caused by any of the reasons specified in the definition or other reasons not specified in the section.
It need not always be for the reasons specifically mentioned, but it should be for similar or analogous reasons as stated in the definition.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F and 2(kkk) - Retrenchment/lay-off - Employers have the discretion to organise their business as they see fit - If the lay-offs or retrenchments are part of a bona fide organisation of the business, the worker cannot challenge the manner of organisation or the consequential lay-offs and retrenchments because no employer should have to carry on an uneconomic business, and the retrenchments need to be accepted.
