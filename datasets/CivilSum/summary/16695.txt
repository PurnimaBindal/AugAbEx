Punjab School Education Board (Employees Pension, Provident Fund and Gratuity) Regulations, 1991, Regulation 5, 6, 13 - Benefit of past service - Petitioner, who joined Health Department in 1962 and was later selected as Lecturer and sent on deputation as Dy.
Secretary in 1983 where he was absorbed, seeks to count service from 1962 to 1983 - However, Regulation 6(c) states that entire service of petitioner must be added to qualifying service on post requiring specialized qualification, as petitioner was appointed as Dy.
Secretary on the basis of specialized experience - Thus, the order of denial is against statutory Regulations.
