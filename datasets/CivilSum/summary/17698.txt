Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part-I, Rule 7.5 - Resignation - Withdrawal of Resignation - Election - The fundamental principle of the rule is to serve the public interest.
The competent authority may permit the withdrawal of resignation if the resignation was submitted for compelling reasons.
The official in question cannot be allowed to withdraw his resignation after losing the election as the submission of the resignation was not for compelling reasons.
Contesting Assembly elections while being in government service cannot be considered as proper conduct.
[Para 7]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Discrimination - Illegal orders - An accusation of discrimination cannot be made unless both cases' complete comparative details are present before the court.
Two wrongs do not make a right.
Perpetuating a wrong interpretation of the rules on the grounds of invidious discrimination is not permissible, even if a decision has been made contrary to statutory rules in a specific case.
