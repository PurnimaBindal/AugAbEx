Customs Act, 1962 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 3(c) and clause 7.2 - Rewards to informers and Government servants - The main issue is whether the IPS officer responsible for the detection and seizure of 900 contraband gold biscuits is entitled to a reward.
The question is also whether the restriction to officers above the rank of Assistant Collectors/Assistant Directors applies only to officers in the Customs Department and not to officers in other cadres.
Although the respondent was mainly responsible for the detection and seizure of the contraband goods, the plea for a reward was rejected.
The restriction that only Group A officers above the rank of Assistant Collector/Assistant Director will not be eligible for reward on the basis of value of seizure applies only to the Customs and Central Excise Department and not to other agencies, particularly State Police officials.
Therefore, the respondent is eligible for a reward.
