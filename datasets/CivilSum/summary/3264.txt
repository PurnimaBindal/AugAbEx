Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Reference - State Government has the authority to examine prima facie the merits of the case before referring it to the Labour Court/Industrial Tribunal.
The Government has the right to decline the reference if the dispute is deemed patent frivolous or perverse.
The State Government is not obligated to refer every dispute raised by the workman and can scrutinize the demand raised to determine its legitimacy.
