Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F - Termination - To be eligible for reinstatement, the workman must have completed 240 days of continuous service.
In this case, the Labour court found no evidence to support the workman's claim of termination without proper cause.
Therefore, the order of reinstatement is legal.
However, the workman is not entitled to back wages for the period he was not gainfully employed, but is entitled to receive 75% of back wages for the rest of the period.
