Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Punishment - Dismissal from service - Gravity and seriousness of proved misconduct cannot be ignored by the Labour Court when using their discretion to substitute a lesser penalty - The award of the Labour Court exercised under Section 11-A was rightly set aside in the case of embezzlement of public funds by a Punjab Roadways Bus Conductor, as the dismissal penalty imposed by the employer was justified.
