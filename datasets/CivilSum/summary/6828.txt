Constitution of India, Article 311 - Administrative instructions - Enforceability - Administrative instructions do not have statutory force, but they must be substantially followed.
Government officers must act in accordance with administrative instructions, as their actions are representative of the government.
Disregard of such instructions will invalidate any actions taken by the authority.
[Para 11]

Constitution of India, Articles 311 14, 226 - Expunction of adverse remarks - The petitioner did not receive any verbal or written communication from the reporting officer, and there is no evidence to support the adverse remarks.
The general assessment of the officer cannot be commented upon in a brief or casual manner.
The remarks are vague and cannot be sustained.
Therefore, the remarks have been expunged.
[Paras 14 to 16]
