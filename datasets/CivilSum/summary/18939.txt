Constitution of India, Article 226 - Gratuity - Delay - Interest - The petitioner retired on 7697 and received gratuity on 1952000 during the pendency of the writ petition.
The court directed the respondents to provide interest at 12% per annum on gratuity from 1.1.98 until payment to the petitioner.
[Para 6]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate Appointment - Ex gratia scheme - The petitioner retired due to medical unfitness after 23 years of service and requested employment for his dependent son under the ex gratia scheme.
The request was denied on the basis of his voluntary retirement.
The court directed that the son is eligible for the scheme and should be considered according to rules and regulations since both conditions were met.
