Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25F - Retrenchment - If an employee works consistently for 240 days under the same establishment, they are entitled to protection from retrenchment.
If the employee works in different branches of a corporation, they cannot count it as consistent employment for the purposes of computing 240 service.
Service in different branches and different places cannot be grouped for the purpose of Sections 2(oo) and 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act.
