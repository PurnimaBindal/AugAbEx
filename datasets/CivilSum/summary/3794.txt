Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - Eligibility - The qualification of a candidate must be considered on the date of advertisement instead of the date of the interview.
[Para 12]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Petition - Final disposal of - The court should settle the dispute as early as possible to inform similarly situated persons of their position in the law, and respondents should not deny legitimate claims.
[Para 11]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Selection process - Participation - A person who participates in and fails the selection process cannot challenge it.
