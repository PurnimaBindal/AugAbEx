Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Compassionate Appointment - The management and union must settle on a scheme for compassionate appointments - Scheme interpretation cannot vary from case to case - The petitioner's claim for appointment due to the death of his step-mother who was working with the management alongside his father (who is still employed) is valid - Similar cases have already received appointments - The management cannot use the fact that the petitioner is a step-son of the deceased employee to deny him appointment under the scheme.
