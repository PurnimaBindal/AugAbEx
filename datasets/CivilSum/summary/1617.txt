Constitution of India, Article 311 - Probation - Termination of Service - Stigma - The petition challenges the termination order after three extensions to the period of probation - Impugned order not innocuous one - No stigma cast on the appellant - The period of probation extended with the expectation that the petitioner would improve his performance, however, there was no improvement.
- Appeal dismissed.
