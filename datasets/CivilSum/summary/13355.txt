The petitioner, who began work as Medical Officer on an ad hoc basis in HCMS-II on November 20th, 1981 and was regularised on November 1st, 1991, received regular annual increments from November 1982 onwards, along with a scale increase from 3000-4500 w.e.f.
May 15th, 1989 to 4100-5300 w.e.f.
November 20th, 1993 after being in service for 5-12 years as per Government Circular dated June 2nd, 1989.
Consequently, the ad hoc service counts towards the award of monetary benefits if it is later regularised, allowing the petitioner to receive annual increments and selection grade.
