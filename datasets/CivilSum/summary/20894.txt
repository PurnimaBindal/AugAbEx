Constitution of India, Article 311 - Maharashtra Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1982, Rules 10(1) and 10(4) (a) (1) - Compulsory retirement - The circular of the government suggests that Class-I Gazetted Govt. servants' cases should be reviewed at the age of "50 - 55" years, and undoubtedly, the review has taken place at the age of 55 for the appellant.
The argument that there could be no review at the age of 55 because there was no first review before the employee reached the age of 50 years is not valid.
The Rules and the Circular do not forbid the review at the age of 55, and the authorities have enough evidence to compel the appellant's retirement.
