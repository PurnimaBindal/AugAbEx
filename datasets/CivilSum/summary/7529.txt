Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India state that if a probationer completes an initial period of six months and no maximum period of probation has been set, the presumption is that the probation period will be extended on completion, but not confirmed.
If the employee accepts the conditions of appointment, they cannot claim otherwise.
[Para 15]
Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India require that a probationer be given natural justice and that termination during probation without disciplinary proceedings cannot be punitive in nature.
Termination after completing the initial six-month probation period and after 5 years of an extension letter, without levelling any allegations or resorting to disciplinary proceedings, cannot be considered punitive.
Mere reference to correspondence and vague allegations of mala fide are not enough to invalidate the termination order.
