Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 17 - Termination order - Appeal - Limitation - The order dispensing with the petitioner's services was passed on 741984.
However, before the order could be served on the petitioner, an appeal was filed on 971984, in which the petitioner requested a stay of the order dated 4.7.1984.
Rule 17 stipulates that an appeal must be filed within 45 days of the date on which a copy of the order appealed against is delivered to the appellant.
However, this does not mean that a person cannot file an appeal if they become aware of the order before it has been served on them.
Therefore, the decision of the respondents to not consider the petitioner's appeal is not valid.
