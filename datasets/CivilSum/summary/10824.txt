Constitution of India, Article 226 - Minimum Wages Acts, 1948, Sections 14(1), 20(2) - Minimum Wages (Central) Rules, 1960, Rule 25 - Over time allowance - Claim of - Respondent, a Chowkidar of Training Hostel, sought benefit of an OM covering only those Chowkidars posted in field offices of CPWD under scheduled employment as listed under Act - Court held that since Training Hostel did not fall within meaning of term field office, the respondent could not seek the benefit of the OM.
