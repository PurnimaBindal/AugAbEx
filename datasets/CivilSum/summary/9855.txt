Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Regulations for Senior Secondary Examination, Clauses 3(g)(h) and 6(b)(ii) - Examination - Private candidates who did not have a required practical certificate were not allowed to take exams.
The rule was introduced in 1993 but not implemented until 1999.
Petitioners pleaded for notice before implementation, and the Board was directed not to insist on the certificate requirement for exams already conducted in March 1999.
However, the rule would be enforced completely for subsequent years.
