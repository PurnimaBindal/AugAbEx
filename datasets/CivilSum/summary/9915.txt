According to the Constitution of India, Article 226 and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1), the State Government cannot refuse to refer the dispute for adjudication by deciding itself the dispute.
Instead, the State Government must determine whether an industrial dispute exists and has no power to make an inquiry or reject or decline the reference to the Labour Court based on an inquiry.
In the case of wrongful termination, whether the workmen voluntarily absented themselves or abandoned the services is a question of fact that only the Labour Court can decide after recording evidence.
It is not the State Government's responsibility to examine the evidence and record a finding.
Consequently, any orders issued by the State Government refusing to refer the dispute to the Labour Court must be annulled.
