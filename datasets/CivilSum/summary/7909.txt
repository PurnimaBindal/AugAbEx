Authorities responsible for admission to MBBS/BDS/B Physio courses in government financed institutions must have the discretion to determine if physically handicapped candidates are capable of completing the program.
Additionally, the state government must make a policy decision to reserve 47% of seats for SC/ST and OBC and allocate 3% for handicapped students.
While admission for the current year has concluded, the government is instructed to implement this policy for future academic years starting next year.
