According to Section 33C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, the Labour Court has awarded the respondent No. 1 with the difference in the city compensatory allowance.
However, it is important to note that the Labour Court can only compute existing or determined relief, and has no jurisdiction to compute benefit which is yet to be determined.
Normally, an investigation of the nature of the determination of the workman's right to relief and the corresponding liability of the defendant is outside the scope of the Labour Court.
