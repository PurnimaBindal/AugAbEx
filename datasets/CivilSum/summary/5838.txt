The Constitution of India, Articles 39(d), 14 and 19, aim for equal pay for equal work and ensure employees of the same status, experience and seniority are given equal treatment.
[Para 3]
The Constitution of India, Article 14, strikes down arbitrary state action and guarantees fairness and equality of treatment.
Article 14 only permits reasonable classification in legislative or administrative mandates with a casual connection between the classification basis and object.
[Paras 8 and 26]
In the matter of ad hoc appointment, regularisation, and seniority, a corporation should form a regularisation plan with ad hoc appointments being used only when exceptionally necessary, and only after an employee has worked for over ten years should they be regularised.
When two employees are competing for a promotion, all things being equal, seniority must be given due consideration.
