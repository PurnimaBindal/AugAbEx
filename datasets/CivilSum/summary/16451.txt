Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Termination of service of a Radiographic technician on probation - Complaints of neglecting duty, committing serious irregularities, and not being regular in reporting for duties - Plea of deemed completion of probation - DPC recommended an extension of probation period by six months and later discharge - Director Principal accepted recommendations - Discharge order is based on recommendations of DPC and cannot be termed illegal or arbitrary - No need to hold an enquiry when work of probationer is overall unsatisfactory - Not conveying decision of DPC extending probation period would not mean petitioner is deemed to have completed probation especially when work and conduct was not satisfactory - No reason to doubt correctness of conclusion arrived at by DPC on basis of record placed before them.
