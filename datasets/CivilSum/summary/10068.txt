Benefits of Promotion - Promotion in fact and in law is an advancement for the accrual of benefits of the promoted post.
An individual must actually work on the promoted post before claiming any benefits that arise from it.
[Para 9]
Benefits of Promotion - Pendency of disciplinary proceedings - The petitioner was selected by an order that stipulated that the promotion order would be effective from 1905 or the date of assumption of the promoted post.
The petitioner was charged with misappropriation of public funds, and a decision was taken to take disciplinary action as far back as January 2005.
Due to the participation of several officers in the process, it was likely to take some time before any action could culminate in issuance of a chargesheet.
The non-issuance of a letter of posting and petitioner's non-joining created a serious impediment for the petitioner in seeking actual promotion.
Even if the DPC disciplinary authority did not have full knowledge of the disciplinary proceedings, it was justified in declining to issue an order of posting that would enable the petitioner to join the promoted post.
[Paras 6 & 9]
