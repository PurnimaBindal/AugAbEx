Constitution of India, Article 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination - Workman - Termination of service of a part-time employee - Workers paid on piece rate basis or job basis also qualify as "workman" - Worked 143 days on daily wages - Time spent on piece rate basis must be considered as part of employment for continuity of service - Termination is not valid.
