Under the Constitution of India's Article 16, The Released Emergency Commissioned Officers and Short Service Commissioned Officers (Reservation of Vacancies) Rules, 1971, Rule 6, an ex-Army personnel can claim the benefit of military service only if they establish that they were appointed against a vacancy reserved for an Emergency Commissioned Officer or a Short Service Commissioned Officer on joining a bank after being released from Army.
The Regional Manager of Bank stated that the petitioner was not appointed against any reserved vacancy, so they are not entitled to the benefit of military service when determining seniority.
