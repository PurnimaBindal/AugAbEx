According to Sections 14-B and 17-B of the Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 1952, when there is a default in payment of contribution, a penalty will only be imposed if guilt is present.
The transferee employer is responsible for paying the contribution for the period before the transfer, but they will not be held liable for any defaults committed by the previous employer during the period preceding the transfer.
