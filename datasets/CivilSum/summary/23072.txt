New Delhi Municipal Council Act, 1994 - Claim of Shiv Shankar Committee pay scale - The appellant argued that the post of Assistant Engineer (E&M) stationed in Auto workshop should be considered part of civil wing, despite respondents being posted there after promotion - However, the court found no evidence to support this claim and held that the respondents are entitled to Shiv Shankar Committee pay scale for being posted in the Auto workshop after promotion.
