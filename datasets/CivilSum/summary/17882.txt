Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226/227 - Promotion - Basic pay - The petitioner was promoted from the post of Bill Distributor to that of regular T-Mate, but his basic pay reduced as a result - The petition does not specify the basic pay scale for either post - The respondents are instructed to hear the petitioner and issue a speaking order - If the post of T-Mate is higher or equivalent in rank to that of Bill Distributor, then the action of the respondents in reducing the petitioner's basic pay would not be valid.
