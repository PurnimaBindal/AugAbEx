Constitution of India, Article 14 - Central Health Service Rules, 1982, Rule 4(10) (iii) - Promotion/Selection/Suitability - Selection for promotion based on suitability cannot be based solely on seniority or seniority-cum-fitness.
Departmental Selection/Promotion Committee must assess suitability based on guidelines provided.
Suitability refers to suitability for the post in question.
Court should consider merit and not just seniority in promotions to super-time grade posts.
[Paras 45, 46, 48, 49 and 51] B Central Health Service Rules, 1982, Rule 4(10) (iii) - Post/Common Post and floating Post-Difference between common post and floating post - `Common posts' result in an employee moving to a new position after promotion, while in `floating posts', the employee's existing position is upgraded.
[Para 27] C. Constitution of India, Article 14 - Central Health Service Rules, 1982, Rule 4(10) (iii) Selection Post/Non-Selection post - Selection posts are filled based on comparative assessment of suitability and merit, or continuous length of service, while non-selection posts are not.
