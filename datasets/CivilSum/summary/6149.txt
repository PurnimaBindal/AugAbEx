Constitution of India, Article 226 - General Provident Fund (GPF) - Withholding of - Petitioner, who was dismissed from service due to conviction in a criminal case, requested for release of GPF amount - GPF was not given even after notice of demand - Court held that GPF is petitioner's own property and there is no legal justification for withholding it - Respondents directed to pay the amount of GPF due to the petitioner with interest at 18%.
