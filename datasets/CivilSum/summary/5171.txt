Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Criminal and departmental proceedings - During criminal proceedings, delinquents must prove prejudice to the departmental enquiry proceedings - In cases where the evidence is not complex and the scope of the departmental enquiry is broader than the criminal proceedings, it is not appropriate to stay departmental proceedings - There is no set rule that enquiry proceedings must be stayed during criminal proceedings - Delinquents must expedite the enquiry proceedings as it is in their best interest - The defence that a public servant was falsely implicated and proper procedure was not followed is not enough to stay enquiry proceedings - If the delinquent already disclosed his/her stand, there will be no prejudice - Based on these factors, this is not a suitable case to stay enquiry proceedings.
[Paras 7, 8 and 9]
