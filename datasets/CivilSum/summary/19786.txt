Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 173 Pension/Disability pension/Discharge Clause 7(b) of Appendix II to the Regulations states that if a disability is not noted upon entry into service, it is assumed that any disability incurred during service is attributable to Army Service.
The petitioner experienced 40% disability of eyesight due to a grenade explosion during training and therefore cannot be denied disability pension for disability not being attributable to Army Service.
The petitioner is entitled to disability pension and the petition is allowed.
