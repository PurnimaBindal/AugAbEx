Sections 4(1) and 30 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 and Section 149(2) of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, permit insurers to raise only certain defences and not any others - Insurers cannot claim their appeal is maintainable if they are not aggrieved parties in respect of an award passed and if the driver or owner of the vehicle involved in the accident have not contested before the Commissioner.
