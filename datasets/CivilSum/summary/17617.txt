Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion - Adverse entries - Promotion to the Post of ASI - The Court concluded that adverse entries made by the reporting officer regarding petitioner's integrity were unfair after reviewing their service book and ACRs - Officer's integrity beyond doubt and no proof of communication of adverse entries - Ignored for promotion due to not having enough good entries - Respondents instructed to reevaluate service record and suitability for promotion, as well as promote from the date of junior promotions, if found fit, and provide consequential benefits.
