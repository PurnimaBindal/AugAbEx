Jammu and Kashmir Compassionate Appointment Rules, 1994, Rule 3 - Compassionate Appointment - There is no inherent right for an employee to request compassionate appointment - When a state decides to make a compassionate appointment, it must adhere to the rules - According to Rule 3, if an individual possesses qualifications beyond matriculation, they should be given a position other than Class IV - The respondents have been instructed to evaluate the appellant's request in accordance with Rule 3.
