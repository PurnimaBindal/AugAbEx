Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - National Education Policy, 1986 - Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas (JNV) - The objective of the scheme is to promote national unity and provide excellent education to virtuous students of weaker sections of the society - The scheme also aims to establish resource centers at the district level - It is essential for the teachers and management to take care of students during the initial period of migration - The locals and transferees should be motivated for assimilation in the new environment - It is the responsibility of elders, parents, and teachers to take care of the well-being of children - Education is an investment for the nation to harvest responsible adults and a well-functioning society - Developing a relationship between children from different cultural backgrounds requires a thoughtful approach for achieving the goal of an integrated nation.
