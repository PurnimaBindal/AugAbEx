Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Examination - Admission - Delay - Shortage of lecturers - Intermediate School Course - The respondents were delayed in granting admission to the petitioners for the Intermediate School Course, which they became eligible for after the writ petition was allowed - The petitioners were eventually granted admission, but it was long after the Course had begun, making them ineligible to appear in the final examination due to not being able to attend 75% of the lectures - The percentage of lectures attended by the petitioners should be calculated from the date they were allowed to join the Course, with them deemed to have attended all lectures between 15.6.1994 to 30.9.1994 - The court directed that the petitioners be allowed to take the final examination of the Course that had commenced on 15.6.1994 and that their results be declared by the relevant authorities.
