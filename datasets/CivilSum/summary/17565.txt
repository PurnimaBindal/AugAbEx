Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules 1960, (Kerala), Rule 10 - Suspension - The appointing authority or higher authority does not need specific empowerment to exercise power under Rule 10, while specific empowerment is required for other authorities - Suspension order by the Secretary to the Vigilance Department is a Kerala state order, and the Secretary was only authenticating the order of the Governor - Amendment to the Rules of Business is not empowerment as per Rule 10 - Government has the power to place the petitioners under suspension without any empowerment - Constitution of India, Article 166(2), 166(3).
