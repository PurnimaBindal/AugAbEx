Employee's State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 75 - Clubbing of business - Father and son carrying two business in same premises - Two firms have separate registration under sales tax, municipal licence, etc. - No evidence to show that employees of two firms were being interchanged - The Regional Director's order after clubbing both industries is deemed illegal - It is noteworthy that there is no law prohibiting father and son from doing two different businesses independently from the same premises, rather the consideration must be whether there is functional integration or proximity in the two businesses, to determine if the businesses are the two faces of the same business, after lifting the veil.
