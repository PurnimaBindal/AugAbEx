Charu Tuli, AAG, Punjab argues that the principles of natural justice are not relevant in the conduct of an examination, as the examination and declaration of results is not a quasi-judicial function.
Additionally, admissions made in excess of advertised seats cannot be challenged in the absence of affected candidates.
Furthermore, a court cannot direct re-evaluation of answer sheets, unless explicitly provided for in the examination scheme.
Finally, the burden of proof for charges of discrimination and arbitrariness lies with the party making the allegation.
