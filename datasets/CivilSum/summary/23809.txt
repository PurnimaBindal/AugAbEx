Foreign Allowance - Respondents who joined NBCC on deputation for overseas project and opted for Central PWD scales executed separate and individual contracts - Government revised pay scales and High Court directed NBCC to pay foreign allowance @ 125% - However, foreign allowance was not promised at the time of induction and is in nature of a residuary perk - Thus, it cannot be treated as salary or akin to deputation allowance - It will only be payable on original basic salary and not on salary after Fourth Pay Commission revision.
[Paras 27, 33 and 36]
