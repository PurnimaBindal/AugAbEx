Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Compassionate appointment - The claim made by the Petitioner was rejected due to their mother receiving terminal benefits, family receiving a pension, owning a house, and both sisters being married.
The High Court held that it would not substitute its opinion since the case was duly considered by the authority.
[Para 1] Additionally, since the purpose of compassionate appointment is to aid families in sudden crises and prevent them from falling into poverty, the rejection of the claim made by the Petitioner after their father's death in 1997 was considered appropriate.
