Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compulsory Retirement - The petitioner was in "good" standing for three years, "average" for two years, and "fair" for one year before being compelled to retire - The remark of "average" did not imply that the petitioner was a "dead wood," and it was not in the public interest to terminate their employment  - The impugned order was issued before the petitioner had a chance to effectively respond to the adverse remarks - Additionally, the Government's prescribed procedure for review was not followed prior to issuing the impugned order  -  The impugned order was not carefully considered, and was therefore overturned.
