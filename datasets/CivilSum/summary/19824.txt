Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 1215, 1221 and 1222 - Age/Discharge - The petitioner was discharged under Rule 1221 for being overage when he joined Bn.
HAP, even though he was originally recruited when he was under the age of 27 and was issued a certificate under Rule 1222.
The order of discharge is invalid since he cannot be considered a fresh appointee when he is transferred to Bn.
HAP.
[Para 5]
Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Discharge/Jurisdiction - It is only in cases where something against the person to be discharged has come on record suggesting inefficiency that Rule 1221 can be invoked for discharge.
Grounding the discharge order on being overage during recruitment is outside the jurisdiction of Rule 1221.
