The Indian Constitution, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Selection - Regularisation - A petitioner who is currently working as a teacher on an ad hoc basis has applied for a post that has been advertised for regular appointment, but has allegedly not been called due to ineligibility.
The respondents claimed that she was called but did not show up for an interview, however their stance has been changing.
This implies that the petitioner was not given a chance to compete or be evaluated.
The respondents must examine her claim on its own merits.
[Paras 7 to 9]
