Punjab Civil Service Rules, Vol. II (Part-I), Chapter 2, Rule 2, 2(b) - Recovery from pension - Departmental proceedings - The employer has the right to withhold pension or a part of it only if the pensioner is found guilty of gross misconduct or negligence during their service, and subject to specific conditions.
These conditions include instituting the proceedings while the officer was still in service, requiring prior sanction of the Government if the officer is no longer in service, and prohibiting the institution of proceedings if the alleged events took place more than four years before.
The proceedings are only deemed instituted upon issuing the charge-sheet, suspending the officer, or presenting a challan in criminal proceedings, but not by merely issuing a show-cause notice.
These principles also apply to employees of privately managed aided schools and colleges.
Additionally, it is considered unfair and arbitrary to hold a retired employee liable solely through the issuance of a show-cause notice.
Proof of misconduct is required before implementing recovery.
