Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Punjab Civil Service (Executive Branch) (Class I) Rules, 1976, Rule 8 - Eligibility for appointment to the Punjab Civil Service (Executive Branch) - Nomination for appointment to the Punjab Civil Service (Executive Branch) is available for members of different services, including District Development and Panchayat Officers and Block Development and Panchayat Officers - Appointment to Executive Branch service is not a promotion within a service - Upon appointment, one cannot claim pay parity with pay scales available in the feeder cadre of service - The government is not obligated to revise pay scales in its Executive Branch every time feeder cadre pay scales are revised - The status in the Executive Branch is highly regarded and compensation is less of a consideration - The petition has been dismissed.
