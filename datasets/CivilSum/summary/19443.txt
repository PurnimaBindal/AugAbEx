Constitution of India, Article 226 - University Grants Commission's guidelines - Petitioner entitled to benefit of senior scale in the cadre of Lecturers from date of completion of 8 years of service - Petitioner appointed as Lecturer post selection and met UGC qualifications - Two prior posts of Lecturer held by petitioner had similar qualifications and pay - Her resignation from previous college before joining new college is irrelevant as UGC guidelines allow for countable past service under certain conditions - Respondent No.2 had already ordered protection of petitioner's pay based on counting of past service - Respondents cannot deny senior scale to petitioner based on counting of past service - Such decision by respondents is illegal.
