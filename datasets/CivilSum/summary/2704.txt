Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Contract Labour (Abolition and Regulation) Act, 1970, Section 10(1) - Termination of services - Principal Employer - Contract Labour - According to the finding recorded by Labour Court, a workman who claimed to be appointed by management through a contractor and claimed the contract between the employer and the contractor as a sham contract, was not employed by the principal employer.
This finding was not seen as perverse.
