Evidence Act, 1872, Section 115 - Panjab University Act, 1947, Section 20(5) (Rules framed under) Rule 11.3 - Panjab University Calendar, Volume III Chapter XIV - Promissory estoppel - A Principal violated rules and policy decision by giving consent to an inter-university migration of an engineering student from Bhatinda to Chandigarh - The violation was not procedural or technical but went to the core of the institution - Therefore, the doctrine of promissory estoppel does not apply, and the Principal is not obligated to grant admission by migration in subsequent cases.
