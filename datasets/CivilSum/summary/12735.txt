Constitution of India, Article 311 - Bengal Police Regulations, 1943, Regulation 861(f) - Dismissal - Disciplinary Proceedings - Enquiry report - Show cause notice - The disciplinary authority did not come to a provisional finding of guilt based on the enquiry report but instead concluded that the charges against the constable were proven - The Superintendent of Police also did not issue a show cause notice - It was held that when a statutory regulation outlines the procedure for imposing major penalties, that procedure must be strictly followed - The impugned order was annulled and the respondent was instructed to pass the proper order in accordance with the law.
