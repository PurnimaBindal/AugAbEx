Service matter concerning seniority, promotion, and Senior Selection Grade is addressed in the following case.
The plaintiff, the respondent, was promoted to Clerk on 1121975 when 1972 instructions were in place.
The instructions did not state that the respondent or others in similar circumstances needed to pass a Punjabi test.
However, instructions issued on 2481983 do require a Punjabi typing test.
These instructions cannot be applied retroactively to the respondent's promotion on 1121975.
At the time of promotion, no condition was imposed on the respondent that his services would be regularized after he passed the Punjabi typing test.
The Government was directed to consider the case of the plaintiff-respondent for the grant of senior/selection grade.
The plaintiff-respondent will be deemed to be regularized with effect from the date of promotion.
[Paras 6 and 7.]
