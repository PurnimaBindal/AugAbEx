According to Sections 10 and 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, the Authority was directed by the High Court to provisionally admit the petitioner into the M.D.
Course.
However, the Authority ended up admitting another senior candidate who was unaware of the court order.
The Authority was not found guilty of wilful disobedience, but was ordered to admit the petitioner and create an additional post if necessary.
The High Court is authorized to correct any wrongs done to the petitioner and restore their status quo during the contempt proceedings.
