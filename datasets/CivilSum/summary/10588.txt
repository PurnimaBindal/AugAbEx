Punjab Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes Department (Class II) Service Rules, 1962, Rule 8(1)(g) - Punjab Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes Department (Class II) Services Rules, 1978, Rules 9 and 11 - The 1962 rules do not establish a quota for promotion - It is illegal to establish seniority based on a 25% quota for promotees and 75% for direct recruits - The 1978 rules set a quota rule of 50:50 - Those who were appointed under the 1962 rules are entitled for seniority from their date of appointment - Those appointed under the 1978 rules have to be given seniority based on quota rule and from the date of their regular appointment.
[Paras 5 to 8]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural justice - Seniority - Determining seniority without affording opportunity of hearing to the affected persons is illegal and violative of principles of natural justice - Not providing a proper opportunity to the affected persons to meet with the points raised in the representation of other affected persons and to meet with the same from the material on official record is improper - The seniority list is quashed.
[Paras 9 and 11]  C Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Ad hoc appointment - Regularisation of services - The petitioner was appointed as Superintendent in 1956 and promoted as District Welfare Officer on an ad hoc basis in 1972 - The Public Service Commission did not approve his appointment - There is nothing on record to show that the work and conduct of the petitioner were unsatisfactory at any stage - The petitioner is entitled to be treated as having regularised into service and for determining his seniority from the date of his initial appointment.
