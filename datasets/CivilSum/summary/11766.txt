Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Medical Service - The petitioner was promoted to the post of Assistant Professor, Paediatric Surgery, Rajendra Medical College and Hospital Ranchi, however, the promotion was later cancelled and respondent No. 6 was appointed in their place.
The petitioner was the top candidate with 10 points and relevant experience, while respondent No. 6 obtained seventeen points but had no experience as a Resident Surgeon.
The petitioner had the necessary qualifications and was rightfully appointed, and his appointment was never challenged by respondent No. 6.
The cancellation of the petitioner's appointment was unjustified and violated principles of natural justice, as a result of his petition, he shall continue to hold the post.
