As per Section 16-E of the U.P.
Intermediate Education Act, 1921, the appointment of Principal in educational institutions is outlined.
When the position of officiating Principal became available, the senior-most teacher, the petitioner, refused the appointment.
However, when a permanent vacancy arose due to retirement, the petitioner claimed their earlier letter refusing the position was only for the officiating position, and made a representation.
If District Inspector of Schools recalls his appointment order, the Committee of Management and the appointed person must receive an opportunity to be heard.
