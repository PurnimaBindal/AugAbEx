Constitution of India, Article - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Award - Executive Authority cannot review or remit decision by Quasi-Judicial Authority without limitations.
Quasi-judicial authority's decision cannot be remitted or reviewed by an executive authority without limitations.
The power of review can only be exercised by a court under specified limitations.
Section 6(4) of the Act does not impose any such limitations.
