According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, regularisation must be granted to eligible candidates.
The State of Haryana's Instructions dated 731996 and 1831996 state that regularisation should be granted to workmen who have completed three years of service.
In one case, a workman was denied regularisation based solely on a 41-day break in service that occurred after the completion of three years of service.
This denial is unjustifiable because the instructions apply to all workmen who have completed three years of service, regardless of the date they achieved this landmark.
The purpose of regularisation is to recognize the valuable experience that adhoc employees have acquired over time, and denying this recognition would only add to unemployment.
Jagdish Lal and others v. State of Punjab and others, 1988(2) PLR 43.
