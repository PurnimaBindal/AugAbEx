Rajasthan Subordinate Officer Ministerial Staff Rules, 1957, Rule 25(10) - Daily wage - Regularization - The Petitioner has been awarded the pay scale of the post of Store Munshi since 2481987 and has been working continuously.
Rule 25(10) provides for regularization of such persons.
It is held that the Petitioner is directed to be regularized from the date of his initial appointment if deemed suitable.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Award of pay scale of higher post - Regularization not ordered - The matter of regularization was not referred to the Labour Court.
It is held that under the given circumstances, no act of illegality or irregularity has been committed by the Labour Court in not passing an order of regularization.
