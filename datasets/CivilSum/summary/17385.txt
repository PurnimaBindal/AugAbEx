Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25 - Termination - `Last Come First Go' - Society cannot apply different standards to different individuals - Junior retained while petitioner was terminated - Impugned order violates principles of `Last Come First Go' - Seniority of respondent No. 3 counted from date of joining sugar mill, whereas petitioner's counted from transfer to Distillery unit.
The order cannot be upheld.
