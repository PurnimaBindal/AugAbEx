Punjab State Agriculture Marketing Board (Class II) Service Rules, 1988, Rule 38 - Constitution of India, Article 309 - Change of service conditions - Appointment by promotion/Qualifications - The petitioners were found ineligible for promotion under the newly amended Rules, but they were eligible under the unamended Rules that were in place at the time the vacancies occurred.
Therefore, the posts must be filled in accordance with the criteria that existed when the vacancies occurred.
Upon promotion, the petitioners will be entitled to all benefits, including arrears of salary, with interest and seniority, etc. [Paras 8 and 11]
