The Constitution of India, Articles 30-41 - Aided schools/Grant in aid/Gratuity - The responsibility of providing gratuity to their employees falls on every management, while the payment of grant in aid to privately managed educational institutions is a policy of the State Government.
In cases where privately managed aided schools are liable to pay gratuity, the Court has no jurisdiction to direct the Government to change its policy involving expenditure.
The court dismissed the appeal with cost, following the precedents of AlR 1965 SC 1196 and 1919(3) SCC 11.
