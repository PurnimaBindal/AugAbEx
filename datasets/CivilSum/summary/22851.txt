Criminal Procedure Code, Section 197 - Sanction for prosecution of Public Servant - If the Public Servant acts in excess of his/her duty during official duty, a sanction for prosecution is required, unless it can be proven that the Public Servant used corrupt means.
[Paras 11 & 12] B Criminal Procedure Code, Section 197 - Criminal complaint against Public Servant - Section 197 CrPC does not immediately apply to a complaint case.
