Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Regularisation - Absorption - Cadre post - Research Associates - Those appointed on contractual basis for non-cadre posts of Research Associates must have their services dispensed with in terms of their contract on completion or scraping of the Scheme, as there would be no such post in any cadre.
Moreover, they cannot be regularised in service, under any circumstances.
[Paras 5 to 11]
