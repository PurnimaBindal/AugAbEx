Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Wages - Non-payment of wages for working on Saturdays - The Punjab Mandi Board conducts a 5-day work week, but the petitioners were required to work on Saturdays on various projects undertaken by the Board - The denial of this benefit to the petitioners was ruled to be arbitrary - The petitioners are entitled to receive extra wages for working on Saturdays.
