Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Pensionary Benefits - Petitioner retired after reaching age of superannuation - Bank Authorities levied certain charges after retirement - Pensionary benefits withheld - Disciplinary proceedings cannot be initiated against a retired person unless specifically allowed by Service Rules - Bank Authorities' action is illegal - Petitioner can be charged with misconduct at best in accordance with the Rules, but no rule exists to reduce his pension after a departmental enquiry for misconduct - Notice to show cause is quashed - Release of pensionary benefit directed.
