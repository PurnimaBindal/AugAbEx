Constitution of India, Article 14 - Termination of services - Junior retained - Allegation of illegal termination and discrimination by a petitioner who abandoned his job - Petitioner claims to have worked off the record and produced official record in court which was found to be tampered with - Court issues notice of perjury and contempt to petitioner - Adjustment on the post by transfer or fresh appointment cannot be considered arbitrary or discriminatory - Petitioner's claim declined.
