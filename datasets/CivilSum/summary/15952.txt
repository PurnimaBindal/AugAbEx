The Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Seniority list - A Junior Engineer in PWD (B&R) Branch, the Petitioner, has filed a petition requesting that he may be declared senior to Respondents 3 to 8 - However, the seniority list shows petitioner as senior to the respondents - As the petition is wholly misconceived, it is dismissed - Additionally, as the Respondents have been unnecessarily involved in this court case, the petitioner is now liable to pay their costs, which amount to Rs. 5000/-.
