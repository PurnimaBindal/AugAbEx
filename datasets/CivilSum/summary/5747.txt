The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 309, concerns the prospective and retrospectivity of service rules.
If a process of selection had already begun for a particular post before the enforcement of new service rules, promotions and appointments to that post would not follow the new rules.
It's also important to note that the rules will not apply retrospectively.
This information is outlined in the Rules for Management of Recognised Non-Government Institutions (Aided and Unaided) 1969 in Rules 28(1) and 28(4).
