The charge-sheet that was challenged involved accusations of producing a forged caste certificate and a claim of double jeopardy.
The APMG directed the respondent to be treated as "OC" instead of Scheduled Tribe, prompting the State Govt. to dispose of the appeal and allow the department to take disciplinary action.
However, the issuance of a writ against the charge-sheet or show cause notice is not typically permissible as it does not give rise to a cause of action unless issued by a person without jurisdiction.
In this case, the charges had not been properly investigated and the respondent had not been exonerated, so double jeopardy is not applicable.
