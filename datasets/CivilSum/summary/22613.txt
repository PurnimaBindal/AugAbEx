When dismissing a person from service due to espionage activities under the Army Act 1950, Section 20(1) and Army Rules, 1954, Rules 17 and 177, the regular inquiry process may be dispensed with if there are sufficient reasons found and recorded on the file.
Following such procedure, an order of dismissal passed in exercise of power under Rule 17 and Section 20(1) of the Act cannot be questioned for want of jurisdiction or arbitrariness.
Merely because an inquiry was initiated under Rule 177 in the incident as a whole does not imply that a departmental inquiry was previously initiated and abandoned in order to invoke Section 20 and Rule 17.
