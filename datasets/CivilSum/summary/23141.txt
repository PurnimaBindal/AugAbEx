According to Punjab Police Rules 1934, in Rule 16.1 Column (6) of the table, various Superintendents of Police are listed as punishing authorities to pass dismissals.
In a specific case, the order was signed by SP Patiala but issued by Superintendent (Headquarters).
Therefore, it is necessary to ascertain if the dismissed person was working under the control of SP Patiala to determine if the order was passed by the competent authority or not.
The case was subsequently remanded to the High Court.
