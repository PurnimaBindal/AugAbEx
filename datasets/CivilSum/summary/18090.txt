Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Haryana Compassionate Assistance to Dependents of Deceased Government Employees Rules, 2003 - Petitioner, the widow of a deceased Head Constable in Haryana, submitted a claim for ex-gratia payment which was rejected on the grounds of being submitted too late - However, petitioner had submitted an application for compassionate appointment within two months of her husband's death as per instructions - The reason given for rejection is contrary to facts - The court has directed the release of ex-gratia financial assistance under the 2003 Rules.
