Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Re-employment after retirement - Jawaharlal Nehru University, Delhi Circular No. Aca.I/U/2(115) dt. 231998 which imposes superannuation age at 60 years and grants an option to apply for re-employment up to the age of 65 years was replaced by circular dated 15.6.2000 setting retirement age to 62 years and giving options to accept retirement at that age as regular employee or to continue as re-employed up to 65 years - The automatic order for re-employment expires on the acceptance of the option to retire at 62 years and be considered as regular employee - A Chairperson can be held only by a regular employee and not by a re-employed one, therefore, re-employment is only a concession on consideration and eligibility fulfillment and cannot be claimed as a right - The already re-employed person has the right to continue until the age of 65 years in the absence of any option or agreement to retire at 62 years - Presumption of such option cannot be merely based on ipse dixi of the University and re-employment needs to follow the Rule.
