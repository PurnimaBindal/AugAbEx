Pension Regulations, 1973, Regulation 173 - Military Service - Disability pension - Eligibility conditions - For disability pension, injury, disease or wound must have been caused on account of military service - If the disease is accepted to have arisen in service, the conditions resulting in the occurrence of the disease must be due to the circumstances of duty in military service - If such conditions are not present, injury or disease cannot be attributed to military service.
