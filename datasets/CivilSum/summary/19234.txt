Haryana Municipal Services (Integration, Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1982, Rule 10, Appendix `B' - Promotion - Clerical cadre - Individuals can be promoted from clerical cadre to Assistant after five years of service in clerical cadre - The position of Octroi Moharrir is considered part of the clerical cadre - Time served as both an Octroi Moharrir and clerk is deemed as experience/service in the clerical cadre for the purpose of achieving the five years of service necessary for promotion to Assistant.
