Industrial Disputes Act 1947 - Sec 10(1)(c) - Misconduct - Accusation of violent and disruptive behavior, including verbal abuse toward a superior officer - Domestic inquiry conducted - Preliminary issue ruled against workman - Is ruling proper?
Yes - Therefore, considering the nature of the workman's misconduct (specifically physical assault against the Manager on the premises) and the fair conduct of the domestic inquiry, we find no justification to interfere with the challenged award.
