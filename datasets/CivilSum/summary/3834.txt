Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Sections 4F and 6N - Settlement - Retrenchment - Agreement reached between employer and union - The statutory incident of retrenchment, retrenchment compensation, cannot be denied even by an agreement that mentions all other payments except for retrenchment compensation.
[Paras 9 and 10]
U.P.
Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Sections 4F and 6N - Constructive Res judicata - Validity of settlement challenged earlier - Workmen were not a party to proceedings - Principle of constructive res judicata will not apply when the question of retrenchment compensation was not raised and decided earlier and when Respondents were not a party to earlier proceedings.
[Para 15]
U.P.
Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Sections 4F and 6N - Reference - Scope of - Labour Court did not go beyond scope of reference when they awarded retrenchment compensation because the question of payment of retrenchment compensation was inter-twined to the question of valid termination.
