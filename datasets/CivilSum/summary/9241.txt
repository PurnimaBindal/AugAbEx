Constitution of India, Article 16 - Haryana Food and Supplies Department Sub-Offices (Group-C) Service Rules, 1982, Rule 11 - Seniority - Ad hoc appointment - It has been held that service performed on ad hoc basis will not be credited towards seniority for a person later appointed to a Civil Service cadre.
This decision was based on the case 1992 (2) SCT 330.
