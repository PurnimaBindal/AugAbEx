Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Haryana Aided School (Security of Service) Rule 1971, Rule 5-A - Aided school employees - Revision of pay scales - Gratuity - Arrears of pay on revision of pay scales with retrospective effect - In accordance with the decision made on December 7, 1998 by the Haryana Govt. to release revised pay scales and gratuity to the employees of aided schools from January 1, 1996, it is the obligation of the Aided School management to present a claim for all outstanding amounts due to their employees from the State Govt. and pay the same despite the fact that the money should be paid by the State Govt. - The State Govt. is not allowed to avoid its responsibility and liability to pay just because no direct master and servant relationship existed between the Govt. and the employee - If the Aided School fails to properly fulfill its duty, the State Govt. must take action against such institution under Rule 5-A of the Haryana Aided School (Security of Service) Act, 1971.
