According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the Government is not restricted in its power to refer industrial disputes, although it may be justified in rejecting a dispute that has been delayed without a rational basis.
However, if a worker diligently pursues their case and approaches the Conciliating Officer within a reasonable period, denying the dispute due to delay would not be reasonable.
