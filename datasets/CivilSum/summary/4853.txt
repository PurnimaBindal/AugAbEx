The Award allows application under Section 33-A holding dismissal illegal.
The contention that it was not compulsory to seek prior permission under Section 33(l)(b) and 33(2)(b) in view of the overriding effect of ESMA is disputed.
The wording of statutory provisions in the instant case does not permit Section 4 read with Section 12 of ESMA to be interpreted in a way that would override provisions of Industrial Dispute Act, on the contrary, it contemplates applicability of provisions of law which govern disciplinary proceedings.
