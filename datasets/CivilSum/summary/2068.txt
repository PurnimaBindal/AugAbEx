Constitution of India, Article 14 - Prospectus of Haryana Agricultural University, Session 1991-92, clause 29(i) - Admission - Weightage in marks for admission to Post-graduate Programme of M.Sc for the session 1991-92 of Haryana Agricultural University - The clause granting a weightage of 10% marks to candidates who studied in a village school for eight academic years and additionally passed Middle or Matriculation examinations as a regular student from the village school is deemed arbitrary.
A decision from 1980(1) ILR 493 (P&H) has been overturned.
