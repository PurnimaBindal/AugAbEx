Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, - Section 11-A - Compensation in lieu of reinstatement - The establishment cannot plead closure if it was available during the dispute before the labor court and the single judge.
The workman could have been retrenched after reinstatement, in accordance with the law.
Additionally, the establishment was not in severe financial crisis.
The presented facts are not subsequent events to modify the relief in Letters Patent Appeal - thus, the appeal is dismissed.
