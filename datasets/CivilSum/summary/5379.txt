The Writ Jurisdiction, as per Article 226 of the Constitution of India, does not allow the Court to interfere with the quantum of punishment based on the length of service once misappropriation and misconduct is proved, as sympathy cannot be shown to the petitioner.
[Paras 6 and 7]
Length of service cannot be a reason for imposing a lesser punishment on a delinquent employee for their misconduct, as it cannot be taken as a license to commit misconduct.
However, if an innocent employee is victimized or the punishment imposed is disproportionate, it can be grounds to set aside the punishment order.
[Paras 7.4, 75 and 76]
