Defence Services Regulations, Regulation 138 - Date of birth - Delay - Change in date of birth - Amendment in the Service Regulations - An employee who discovered that their date of birth was recorded incorrectly in their service book in 1970, and who should have retired as a Major-General on 2421994, applied to correct the record with Army Authorities only on 15101991.
The employee's correspondence with the authorities remained in doubt because he claimed to have been born in Pakistan.
A decree obtained against the University by the employee for correcting his matriculation certificate without the Department's involvement was found to be inapplicable.
The amended regulations came into force in 1979, but employees already in service could apply for correction within five years of their introduction.
The decree and instructions of 1990 did nothing to explain the employee's delay, and the decree was a decree in personnem, not a judgment in rem.
