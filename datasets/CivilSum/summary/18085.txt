According to the Haryana Co-operative Societies Act, Sections 69 and 115, and the Haryana Co-operative Societies Rules, Rule 110, a revision petition may be maintained before the Government against an order of the Registrar.
Section 115, which is similar to Section 69 of the Act, allows the Government to review and pass an order on any proceeding where no appeal can be filed.
When the Revisional Authority becomes aware of the legality or propriety of an order, it can exercise its power, regardless of whether it was at the instance of the interested party or suo motu.
If there is no application before the Registrar or appeal, then section 115 provides the sole available remedy to a person aggrieved by an illegal decision.
