Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rule 244(2) - Rajasthan Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1996, Rule 53(1) - Compulsory Retirement - The disciplinary authority concluded that the delinquent officers were responsible for forgery by interpolation of muster rolls without providing evidence to support their assumption.
The petitioners' representation against this assumption was rejected without justification.
Additionally, an order of recovery was passed against one of the petitioners despite no financial loss being incurred by the State Government.
As such, the order of compulsory retirement is invalidated.
[Paras 22, 23 & 24]
