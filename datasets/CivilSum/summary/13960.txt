Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - A petitioner was prevented from appearing in Part III examination because they did not pass BCA Part I and II one academic year before the Part III exam - The petitioner appeared in the exam under the protection of interim orders - There is a regulation that allows a candidate who passed BCA Part I to take Part III even if they have to retake three papers in Part II - The rules were amended - The amended rules will not be applied to the petitioner who appeared in the exam before the amendment and already satisfied the previous regulations - Thus, the petitioner is now declared eligible.
