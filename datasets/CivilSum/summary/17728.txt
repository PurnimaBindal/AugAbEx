Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part I, Rule 2.35, 311 to 317-A - Lien - Seniority - Petitioners - Head Constables continue their lien in the Armed Police - Seniority vis-a-vis others working in Armed Police remains intact - Claim of petitioners ignored on grounds they had been transferred to District Police - Right to be deputed to course before juniors - Not deputing petitioners for the course is violative of Article 16 of the Constitution.
[Paras 5 and 7]
Cadre - Lien - Seniority - Promotion - Person with lien on a post presumed to continue holding post despite being deputed elsewhere - Mere transfer cannot take away right to seniority and further promotion - Such action would violate Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
[Para 8]
Promotion - Junior Head Constables promoted to higher post considered in relation to petitioners' claim - No person junior to petitioners deprived of benefits simply due to attending course before petitioners - Petition allowed.
