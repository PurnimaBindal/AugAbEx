The regularisation and absorption of project employees, as well as the termination of their services at the completion of a project, fall under the purview of the Constitution of India's Article 226 and the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947's Sections 10, 25-K, and 25-FF.
Whether the workers can be adjusted or transferred to other projects operated by the construction company IRCON to become permanent employees of IRCON depends on whether there were vacancies available to continue a cadre in service, the nature of the work and of the project, and the existence of vacancies on the date of the workers' employment.
These questions involve factual disputes that cannot be resolved through writ proceedings but can be addressed through raising an industrial dispute.
