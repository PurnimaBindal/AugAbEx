Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Sections 2(i) and 47 - Termination - Medical Disability - An employer must not dismiss or demote an employee who develops a disability while in service - If the employee cannot be placed in any other position, they may be kept on a supernumerary post until a suitable post is available or until they reach the age of superannuation, whichever comes first - The termination of the employee on medical grounds was rightly overturned by the Tribunal.

Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Sections 2(i), 47 and 72 - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Termination - Medical Disability - The provisions of the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act are supplementary to and not in derogation of the Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules and any other law in force that benefits persons with disabilities.

Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 2(i) and 2(t) - 'Disability' - 'Person with disability' - Significance - The term 'mental retardation' denotes one of the disabilities covered under the Act - A 'person with disability' is someone who has at least a 40% disability as certified by a medical authority.
