The Food Corporation of India Staff Regulations, 1971 provides a statutory remedy of review which the petitioners are required to seek before approaching this Court under Article 226/227 of the Constitution of India.
They are directed to utilize this alternative legal remedy.
[Paras 4 and 5]
The Limitation Act, 1963, Sections 5 and 12 indicates that a delay in filing a review cannot be dismissed solely on the basis of limitation, especially if the petitioner is sincerely pursuing the case in the writ court.
