Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 124 and 1215 - Recruitment of Constables from SPOs - Selection - The prescription of written and physical tests to determine the eligibility of SPOs to be absorbed as Constables is not considered illegal or arbitrary.
SPOs cannot be directly absorbed as Constables without passing these tests.
The rejection of those who fail the tests is also deemed valid, and Balour Singh's case was cited in support of this.
