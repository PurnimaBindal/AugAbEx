Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 4(6) - SIDCO Employees Service Regulations, Regulation 114 - Gratuity recovery - Recovery via Regulation 114 can only be done through pay, not gratuity - Recovery from gratuity is only allowed upon employee termination - Regulations formulated by a company for gratuity recovery are invalid as gratuity rights are governed by Parliament's Gratuity Act - Employer can forfeit gratuity only as provided under Section 4(6) of the Act.
