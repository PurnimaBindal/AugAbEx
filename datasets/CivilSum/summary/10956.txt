Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Education - Admission - Post Graduate Degree Course - A petitioner who had served as a PCMS Doctor and had completed her MBBS applied for admission in the 40% fresh graduate quota for the consideration of her admission in the 60% quota.
Other candidates in a similar situation were granted admission in the 40% quota.
The Court held that although the petitioner had written "No" against the relevant column, she was eligible under the 60% quota and was granted provisional admission.
The petitioner successfully completed the course and the petition was allowed.
