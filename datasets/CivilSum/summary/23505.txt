The Rajasthan Administrative Services Rules state that selection for promotion should be based on service record and ACRs, but the rule does not specify how many ACRs need to be considered or what level of rating is necessary for selection.
It is up to the DPCs and other administrative authorities to interpret and apply the rule.
In this case, the DPC considered 7 years of service and the requirement of at least 5 very good out of seven ACRs.
The court cannot interfere with the selection process or substitute its own judgment, and the latter amendment to the rule cannot be applied retroactively.
