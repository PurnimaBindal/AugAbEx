Constitution of India, Articles 226 - Admission - Education - Admission to MBBS Course against the payment seats based on merit in Pre-Medical Test - Admissions made according to merit - Court cannot order creation of seats for admission - Petitioners not included in the merit list up to the last candidate admitted against payment seats - No orders can be passed without any candidates present violating the principles of natural justice - Courts must follow natural justice principles with as much rigor against executive and quasi-judicial authorities.
[Paras 6 and 7]
