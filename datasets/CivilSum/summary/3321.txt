Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Review or appeal against the judgment passed under Article 226 - Ministry of Home Affairs circular dated 27111998 entitling an officer to wear Ashoka Emblem and one star after 11 years of Medical Officer service and to wear Ashoka Emblem with two stars on completion of 16 years of service - Clarification letter-instruction dated 2392005, held not retrospective and to not take away the rights accrued under 1998 instructions - Executive orders cannot be made with retrospective effect - Judgment implementation cannot be undone by a prospective appeal or review request - Benefit of judgment can be claimed by similarly situated people until it is still in effect.
