Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Advertisement - Appointment - Posts of Principals in Senior Secondary Schools - A total of 18 posts were advertised, but the Commission recommended the names of 30 persons.
This goes against the rule that the Commission cannot select or recommend persons in excess of the advertised posts.
While recommending two or three extra candidates is permissible in case of a declined offer, varying the number at the Commission's own discretion may result in uncertainty and negate the guarantee under Articles 14 and 16.
