The petitioner sought the benefit of a judgment in a pay scale claim, but the Board restricted the benefit only to those who contacted the court.
The question of limitation was dismissed since the anomaly in pay scale is a recurring cause of action.
The Court ordered concerned authorities to implement the judgment not only for those who approached the court but also in every case involving a similar fact situation to prevent unnecessary court proceedings and discourage bureaucracy apathy.
The petitioner was directed to be placed in a pay scale of 6300-10700 from the date they joined the Board as a laboratory assistant with arrears.
