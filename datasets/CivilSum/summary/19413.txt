Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 Section 2(1) (n), Schedule 11(xxv) - Workmann Show-Driver - Driver of Government vehicle - A driver of a vehicle, whether it be a Government vehicle or a private one, is considered a workman under the Workmen's Compensation Act and is eligible for compensation under the Act in case of injury or death.
[Para 5]
Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 Section 4, Explanation 2 - Compensation - Claim - Pleadings - Determination of compensation - Compensation under the Act must be awarded in accordance with the prescribed scale in the Schedule to the Act, regardless of the claim made in the petition - Even if the claim is lower than the entitlement under the Schedule, the authority under the Act is not prevented from awarding compensation in line with the Act's provisions.
