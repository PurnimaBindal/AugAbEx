Kashmir and Jammu Universities Act, 1969, Section 36(A)(a) - Article 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India - Selecting Professors for Management Studies - Debarred Experts - An expert debarred from assoicating in one university's selection process cannot be automatically barred from all universities - Invalidating selection based on this ground is not permissible.
[Para 13] B Kashmir and Jammu Universities Act, 1969, Section 36(A)(a) - Article 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India - Selecting Professors for Management Studies - Visiting Member Experts of different Universities - The term "not connected with the University" cannot be interpreted so widely as to debar those who visit universities sporadically to deliver lectures.
[Para 14] C. Kashmir and Jammu Universities Act, 1969, Section 36(A)(a) - Article 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India - Disqualifications for Appointment to Diversified Area - The court cannot review the decision of a selection committee in matters of research and candidate selection - The selection process cannot be questioned once a candidate has been considered and rejected by the committee.
