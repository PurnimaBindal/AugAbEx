Constitution of India, Articles 14, 15(4) and 16(4) and Article 246(1) List-I, Entries 63 to 66, List-II, Entry 11, List-III Entry 25 - Indian Medical Council Act, 1956, Sections 19-A and 20 - Reservations Jurisdiction/Education/Reservations in Admissions/Admissions to Post-Graduate Medical Colleges - With the deletion of Entry No.11 from List II of the seventh schedule to the constitution and substitution of Entry 25 to List III by the 42nd amendment, the State has become an essential power to regulate admissions and policies consistent with Article 14 for the medical institutions it established and maintains - providing for reservations is within the State's competence - Admission to Post-graduate Medical courses is not a promotion or appointment and admissions are based on academic merit - The State's power under Article 15(4) cannot be overridden by Regulations made by the Indian Medical Council - Entry 25 of List III governs Admission of students to post-graduate medical courses.
