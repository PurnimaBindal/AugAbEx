The respondent was hired as a typist on a temporary basis until a regular post was filled, and an advertisement for the regular post was issued on June 4, 1996.
However, by the time the post was advertised, the respondent had exceeded the age limit of 25 years.
The respondent requested an age limit relaxation, but the fact that she had worked satisfactorily since January 1993 was not enough to justify it.
The decision of the learned single Judge that the age bar should not apply to the respondent due to her three years of work prior to the advertisement is unsustainable.
[Paras 3 and 4] (State of Haryana vs. Piara Singh, 1992(4) SLR 799: 1992(3) SCT 201 (SC))
