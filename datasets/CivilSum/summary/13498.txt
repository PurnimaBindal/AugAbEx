Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Review - Termination of services - A Vice-Chancellor of a University who is exercising powers under the University Act to consider approval for termination of services of a University employee does not act as a quasi judicial authority.
He cannot review his own order of disapproval of termination unless the Act has given express power of review.
The termination order stands quashed.
[Paras 10 to 13]
