Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Discharge - Termination - Reinstatement - The petitioners, Constables in the Punjab Police, were discharged from service after an accused they were guarding escaped and a case under Section 223 IPC was registered against them.
However, after filing petitions, they were acquitted in the criminal case and it was found that their case was no different from that of two other colleagues who were reinstated.
The objection of delay by respondents cannot be sustained, and thus, the petitions were allowed.
Orders of termination were set aside, and orders of reinstatement were given.
