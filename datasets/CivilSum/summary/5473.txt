Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Charter of demands by respondent union regarding wage scale, DA, LTA etc - In examining the financial capability of employers, relevant factors such as industry progress, future prospects, profit made, expected demand and burden and incremental increase of employer needs to be taken into account - Tribunal mechanically accepted figure submitted by union without considering written submissions by the company - Wages fixed by the Tribunal were much higher than those paid by comparable industry - Award of Tribunal modified.
