The Unfair Means Committee invoked the provisions of Guru Nanak Dev University Ordinance 10 (J) and II when disqualifying a petitioner for being found in possession of exam material in the exam hall itself.
After providing notice and a hearing, the committee passed the disqualification order, which was deemed valid due to ample evidence of misconduct under Ordinance 10(J).
It is therefore concluded that the decision to bar the petitioner from all exams was neither erroneous nor illegal.
[Paras 8 and 9]
