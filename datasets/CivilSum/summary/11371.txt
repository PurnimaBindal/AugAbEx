Constitution of India, Article 309 - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 68(5)(2) - Withheld pensionary benefits - Request for interest - Rule requires that interest be paid at a rate of 12% - It was determined that payment of interest is not a penalty but a typical capital accretion following the Supreme Court's ruling in Alok Shankar Pandey's case - A directive was given to award interest at a rate of 12% from the date of amount owed until payment.
