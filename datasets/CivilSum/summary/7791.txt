Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Whether or not service rendered by petitioners should be taken into account for grant of Senior scale/selection grade after 8-16 years of service despite a gap of one to two years in other aided institutions after closure of DAV College, Hasangarh - Court holds that conditions provided by State Government have been complied with in accordance with UGC guidelines notified on 822000 - Time taken for adjusting/absorbing petitioners (around two years for petitioners No.1 and 2, and around one year for petitioner No3) should not disadvantage them.
