Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Education - Rules of Admission, Clauses 6.4 and 73 - The Institute illegally granted admission to petitioners who had failed the Class IX examination to take the Class X examination - It was held that the petitioners were not entitled to take the Class X examination - The Board could not be directed to declare the result of the petitioners.
