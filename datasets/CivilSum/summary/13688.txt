Haryana Aided Schools (Security of Service) Rules, 1974, Rule 82 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Probation - The scope of jurisdiction under Article 226 is limited - Interference is only allowed when the authority acted illegally without jurisdiction or there was a mistake apparent on the face of it - No interference in firm findings based on evidence and record - If the employee was good during probation, he becomes entitled to confirmation, and his services cannot be dispensed with as a probationer after completing the probation period.
