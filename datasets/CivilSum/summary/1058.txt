Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 4(6) - Forfeiture of gratuity - Resignation and its acceptance - Effect of - If an employee resigns but their services have not been terminated based on a reason stated in the Act, they cannot be subject to forfeiture.
[Para 13]

Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Sections 4(a), (b) and 7 - Delay in claiming gratuity - Limitation - The employer cannot use limitation as a defence and must arrange to pay gratuity regardless of whether the employee has made a claim.
The employee can argue that the failure to pay their gratuity was a recurring and ongoing issue.
[Paras 10 and 12]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 - Object - This Act is considered a social welfare legislation and employers cannot prevent employees from receiving what they are owed.
[Para 11]
