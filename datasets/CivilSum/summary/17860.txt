State Bank of Patiala (Officers) Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 67(a) - Promotion - Disciplinary proceedings - Censure - The Bank's promotion guidelines don't prohibit officers under disciplinary proceedings to be considered for promotion - A censure order was issued against the petitioner after giving his response, concluding the proceedings - The mere existence of disciplinary proceedings should not be a reason to exclude an otherwise eligible candidate from promotion, and therefore the Bank was wrong to bar the petitioner from the promotional test.
[Para 4]
State Bank of Patiala (Officers) Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 67(a) - Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226/227 and 311 - Relief - Arrears of pay - The petitioner was wrongfully declared ineligible and debarred from taking the promotional test where many juniors were allowed to appear and were promoted to MMGS-II - He was eventually promoted through the seniority channel - In order to meet the ends of justice, he should be deemed to have cleared the test and promoted to MMGS-II as of August 1, 1985, without receiving any arrears of pay or other allowances for the interregnum period.
