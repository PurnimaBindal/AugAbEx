Constitution of India, Article 136 - Punishment - Serious charge of allowing a convict to escape - Removal from service as punishment - Tribunal remanded the matter after setting aside the orders - Directing that termination of service should not be imposed - However, tribunal was not sitting in appeal over the findings of the enquiry officer - There was clear evidence suggesting the guilt of two employees - Disciplinary authority is responsible for determining the appropriate punishment.
