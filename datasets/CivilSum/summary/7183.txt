Section 14B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, allows for full last drawn wages to be granted as interim relief by the Tribunal.
However, if the workman is not rendering any service to the management and there are no pleadings of gainful employment, the recent pronouncement by the Supreme Court suggests that the workman might not be entitled to full back wages even after reinstatement.
Hence, the workman is given only 50% of the last drawn wages as interim relief, and the Tribunal's order is modified accordingly.
