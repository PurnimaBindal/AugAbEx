Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Compassionate appointment - Assistance provided to dependents of employee dying in harness - Compassionate appointment can be granted only if the family is left in dire financial circumstances - In this case, the family received a lumpsum amount of more than Rs. 5,62,000/- as terminal benefits, the widow is receiving two pensions, and other family members are employed - Therefore, the family cannot be considered to be in dire financial need and does not require assistance in the form of compassionate appointment.
