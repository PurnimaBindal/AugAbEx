Employees Provident Fund Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 7-A - Appeal - Validity of Section 7-A - The absence of a provision for an appeal from the order of the Regional Provident Fund Commissioner does not make Section 7-A of the Act violative of Article 14 of the Constitution.
This is particularly because there is a provision for an appellate tribunal that is functional under the Act for the orders passed under Section 7-A of the Act.
