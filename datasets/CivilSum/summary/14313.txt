The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, relate to promotion and pay.
The petitioner was appointed as Technologist Grade II but was directed to handle work for the Bio-Medical Sub Division without extra remuneration when a vacancy occurred in Technologist Grade I Because the post became vacant in July 1991, only those eligible for promotion on that date can be considered.
The respondent must make regular promotions in accordance with the law, considering only those employees who were eligible on July 1991.
The petitioner is also entitled to pay for the higher post from the date he worked against that post.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]
