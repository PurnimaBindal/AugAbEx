Constitution of India, Article 16 - Interview - Appointment - Criteria for screening the applications and shortlisting the candidates - To exclude general category candidates who had not obtained 76% marks in the qualifying educational examination, was one of the required tests - As the petitioner did not secure even 60% marks, he was excluded - Adopting a justifiable criterion for shortlisting candidates to create a limited consideration zone from a larger eligibility zone cannot be deemed arbitrary or unlawful - Therefore, no relief could be granted to the petitioner.
