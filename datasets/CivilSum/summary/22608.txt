Employees working in aided private educational institutions in Rajasthan are entitled to the same leave encashment benefits as those working in government institutions according to Sections 2(r), 16, and 29 of the Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions (Recognition, Grant-in-aid and Service Conditions, etc.) Act, 1989.
Section 29, in conjunction with Section 2(r), defines "pay and allowances" and "salary" to include leave encashment, which is considered to be the amount paid for unused leave.
