Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 19(1)(f) and 31(1) read with Article 309 - Indian Railway Establishment Code, Vol. II (Fifth reprint), Rule 2544 as amended by notification No. GSR 1144(E) dated 5.12.1988 - Pension - Retroactive rule changes - Parliament has the power to make retroactive changes to the law but cannot take away already-accrued rights - Pension is considered the property of retired employees - Reducing a retiree's pension violates Articles 19(1)(f) and 31(1) of the Constitution, and changing the rules retroactively to take away his entitlement to a pension that he was already receiving is also unconstitutional and violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - Rule 2544's retroactive effect and the taking away of accrued and vested rights was properly overturned by the Tribunal.
