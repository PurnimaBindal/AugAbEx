Pension Regulation for the Armed Forces, 1961, Para 173 - Disability pension - Disability due to military service - While attending fire range classification involving sound process of high intensity, the petitioner suffered disability of deafness at 76% while on duty - The disability is definitely due to military service - Therefore, he is eligible for disability pension.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Limitation - Pension - Arrears of pension - The petitioner's claim for disability pension was ultimately denied in 1989 - The writ petition was filed in 1994 - Since the suit for recovery of arrears can only be filed within three years, the petitioner is only entitled to arrears for three years from the date of the writ petition.
