Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(oo)(bb) and 2(ra), Fifth Schedule, Item 10 - Fixed term appointment - Unfair labour practice - The management's appointment of a workman on a post for only 89 days, with the intention of continuing the employment, goes against the provisions of the Act and is an example of unfair labour practices.
The fact that the workman had not completed 240 days of service does not mean that they cannot seek protection under the Act.
