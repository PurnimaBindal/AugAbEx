As per the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 14(1)(b), an IPS Officer who has been sent on deputation to Bhakra Beas Management Board as Director (Security) is considered a Central Govt. employee and falls under the jurisdiction of the Tribunal.
This is because he is working in a statutory body established by the Central Govt. while still being an IPS Officer in his parent cadre.
The details of his amenable jurisdiction under CAT can be found in Paragraphs 7, 8, and 10.
