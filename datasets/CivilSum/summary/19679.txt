The respondents in this case are Varinder Pal Singh, AAG Punjab for No. 1 & 2, and Ashok Aggarwal Sr Advocate with Subash Goyal for No. 3.
The writ concerns a representation by the petitioner under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
The petitioner has requested that the DPI Punjab pass a speaking order regarding their representation.
If the DPI deems the DEO to be the appropriate authority to decide the representation, a copy of the order and representations must be transmitted by the DEO for disposal.
