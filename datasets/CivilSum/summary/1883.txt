Constitution of India, Article 226 - Misconduct - Disciplinary action - A leaflet containing derogatory and abusive language against the University was allegedly issued, an enquiry conducted, and petitioner removed from service.
The argument that certain documents were not supplied held that the complaint as to non-furnishing of documents is examined on the touchstone of prejudice.
As documents called for by petitioner have no relevance to charges, no prejudice has been caused.
[Para 17] B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - University Ordinance 74 - Misconduct proportionality of punishment - The order of removal passed on proving of charges after inquiry was challenged, and the quantum of punishment was challenged.
Ordinance 74 limits the power to award punishment of removal or dismissal only if the offense is that enumerated in clauses f to h. The held since the offense is not covered, the punishment is unsustainable and quashed.
The matter is remitted to respondents to award such other punishment as open to it in law.
[Paras 27 to 31] C. Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ Jurisdiction - Judicial Review - It is not the function of a writ Court to review, reappraise, or reconsider the evidence on the finding arrived at by the Enquiry Officer/Disciplinary Authority.
Since the conclusion arrived at receives support from evidence and material on record, and principles of natural justice have been complied with, no interference is required.
