Constitution of India, Article 226 - Revision - A respondent-workman was promoted as Assistant Tyreman on an ad hoc basis.
The reversion of the respondent workman to the post of Helper Tyreman was challenged.
The DPC considered the record of the respondent-workman after his promotion as Assistant Tyreman and not of the feeder post from which he was promoted.
The Labour Court rightly held that the revision was not proper.
[Paras 3 and 4]
