The Factories Act (63 of 1948), Section 2(k) and the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1958, Section 2(12) are applicable to determining whether a private hotel qualifies as a factory.
An order including a hotel under the 1948 Act was deemed sustainable, since it was found to be carrying out various manufacturing processes.
These included using a fridge to store food and drink, and employing a 1/2 horse power pump to supply water to rooftop tanks used by travellers.
The inclusion order under the 1958 Act was upheld.
