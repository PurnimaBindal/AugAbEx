Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Back wages - A worker was awarded Rs. 37,18770 as back wages by a labor court after considering rival claims.
The appellant, who had been taking a technical stand to thwart the worker's claim, had their writ petition dismissed by a Single Judge who criticized their fruitless litigation and wasting of public funds.
Despite this, the appellant filed an appeal, which was subsequently dismissed with cost.
