The Public Services Commission does not have the authority to recommend candidates beyond the number of vacancies advertised under Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 – Selection/Advertisement Vacancies.
Any recommendations beyond the number of advertised posts in the respective categories are liable to be set aside, as per Rule 9.
B.
The criteria for allocating marks to qualifications must be laid down by the Commission in cases where the selection is to be made only by interview under Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 – Selection – Interview – Interview marks.
Reserving 50% marks for viva voce in a selection by interview only is not arbitrary, and the selection cannot be held to be vitiated for that reason alone.
Rule 10 is followed by AIR 1994 SC 141.
C Higher academic merit is not the only consideration for selection under Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 – Selection – Merit.
A person with lower academic merit may perform better in the interview than a person with higher academic merit.
If candidates of lower academic merit are selected, selection cannot be held to be arbitrary, as per Rule 11.
D. An unsuccessful candidate who is entitled to apply for the post again if the selection is set aside has the locus standi to challenge the selection as per Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 22 – Locus standi.
Rule 12 defines this.
