All India Pre-Medical Examination Rules, Rule 4.4(iii) of Code 02 - Education - Admissions - Allotment of seats to MBBS Courses - CBSE, in collaboration with the Medical Council of India, has established eligibility criteria for allocation of seats to the MBBS course against 15% All India Quota - States and colleges cannot impose their own requirements and satisfaction based on their own criteria - Candidates who pass the entrance test against 15% All India Quota seats cannot be subject to the condition of passing the qualifying examination in the same attempt.
