Constitution of India Article 226 - Selection/Act and Conduct - Fraud - A candidate filed a writ petition asking the authorities to consider him in the physically handicapped reserved category.
An interim order allowed him to appear in the interview at his own risk and responsibility.
The candidate was then selected in the reserved category, giving the impression that the court had ordered to consider his case against a reserved vacancy.
The candidate's conduct was found to be dubious and fraudulent since he had no right to be considered in the reserved category and had failed in the written test.
Consequently, the selection of the candidate was quashed.
