Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Sections 14 and 28 read with section 3(q) - Civil Procedure Code, Section 9 - Jurisdiction - Service matters - Suit - for damages - Tortous acts - The plaintiff has filed a suit for damages against the defendant, who allegedly harassed and caused mental pain and injury to the plaintiff by acting mala fide with a vindictive attitude.
Additionally, the plaintiff alleges that the defendant tampered with official record to tarnish the record, image and career of the plaintiff.
The court has determined that the matter does not fall under any clause of Section 14 of the Administrative Tribunals Act and that the suit for damages is maintainable.
The court cannot determine whether the allegations are true or false at this stage, and has to rely on the plain reading of allegations in the plaint to determine the question of maintainability of the suit.
