Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Education/Diploma and certificates - A certificate course in Physical Education recognized by the Haryana Government at the time of admission to the course in the institution is subject to change during the pendency of the course.
When the certificate of the institution is no longer recognized by the government, students who joined the course when the certificate was recognized by the government are still entitled to all the benefits of the certificate issued to them on completion of the course.
The government cannot ask these students to surrender the certificate because it is no longer recognized.
The government of Haryana must recognize certificates issued to students who joined the course before de-recognition of the course, following AIR 1987 SC 2027.
