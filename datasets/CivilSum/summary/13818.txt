Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Permanent appointment - Claim for - The Managing Committee of School made the initial appointment of the petitioner as Night Guard without initiating a selection process or obtaining prior permission.
The appointment was never sanctioned by the Director of School Education or approved by the District Inspector of Schools, making it illegal.
The long tenure of the illegal appointment does not grant any right to seek permanent appointment to the post.
[Paras 7 and 8]
