Jawaharlal Nehra University Act, Section 3 - University - Definition of - Universities are corporate aggregates comprised of incorporated bodies - According to Section 2(2) of the Act, universities are considered a natural person.
[Para 29] B University - Objects of University - The Statutes by which they are incorporated reflect their objects and powers of its officers or its bodies and the manner of exercise of powers and passing resolutions - Universities have to have the objects for which they are incorporated and their articles & Memorandum of Association.
[Paras 30 to 32] C Jawaharlal Nehra University Ordinances - Ordinance 14(62) and (12) - Ordinarily - Clause (12) of Ordinance 14 permits relaxation of any provision at the discretion of the Academic Council - The provisions of Ordinance (62) and (12) have to be read together - The Ordinance is flexible and not rigid, so it doesn't exclude other meanings which can be ascribed to the word 'Ordinarily' in English language like normally usual practice or usually etc. - If a student's request is made to take the end semester examination before completing the prescribed period, relaxation of the rule is presumed if not specifically relaxed - The Ordinance is not fixed, rigid, inflexible or sacrosanct - The student cannot be held at fault and made to suffer, so the result has to be declared and she can take benefit of that.
