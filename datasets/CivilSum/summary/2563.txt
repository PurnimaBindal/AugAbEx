Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3(1)(a) - Adverse Remarks/Efficiency Bar/Promotion/Premature Retirement/Crossing of efficiency bar - When adverse remarks are recorded before the crossing of efficiency bar, they cannot be completely disregarded after the crossing of efficiency bar.
The adverse entries prior to crossing of efficiency bar would be included in the employee's record of service which can be taken into consideration while making decisions regarding retention in service or premature retirement.
Nonetheless, adverse remarks recorded before promotion to a higher post, particularly where promotion is based on merit and not solely on seniority, would lose their significance.
Promotion would nullify the effect of the adverse remarks against the government servant.
