Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admissions/Education - Recognition of education Institutions - Despite having their recognition withdrawn by the state authorities and a public notice about the derecognition, an educational institution continued to invite candidates and grant admissions - The petitioners who joined the institution without taking proper care cannot claim protection from the court on the basis of a bonafide impression that the institution was still recognised during the enquiry - Petition dismissed.
