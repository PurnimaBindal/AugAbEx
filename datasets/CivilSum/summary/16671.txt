Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation - Caste based reservation - Disallowed - Rajasthan Govt. circular dated 3042001 that implements reservation on the basis of caste wherein the candidacy of Para-Teachers would be selected solely from the castes for which the position of Sarpanch was reserved for the Panchayat, is unconstitutional and annulled - Selection must be based on merit exclusively.
