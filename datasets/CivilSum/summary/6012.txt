Advocates Act, (25 of 1961), Sections 7, 24 - Bar Council of India Rules, Rule 2, 17, Part IV - Enrolment as Advocate - Refusal of - Petitioners rejected as advocates - Main reason was the lack of approval from Bar Council of India for University Law College's five year course - Petitioners contended that Rule 17 of BCI Rules states that no college can teach law unless approved by BCI - Held, Rules 2(1)(b) and 17(1) only require approval by Bar Council for colleges which need to be affiliated with a University - Hence if the University itself is approved, any institution run by it does not need separate approval under Acts or Rules - Petition granted as a result.
