Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, which includes Sections 25-F, 25-H, and 25-N, covers Closure of unit, Retrenchment of all employees, and Re-employment.
It is the responsibility of the workman to prove that the unit from which he was retrenched had more than 300 employees on that date.
Employees working in the whole corporation cannot be considered for this.
If the entire unit has been closed and some juniors, including the petitioner, were retrenched, their re-employment in other units of the corporation against the call of fresh appointments do not violate Section 25-H 1992(1) SCT 136 was followed, and the petition was dismissed.
