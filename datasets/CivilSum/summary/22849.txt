Constitution of India, Article 215 - Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Sections 12, 14, 15 - An order passed by the court must be obeyed by the authority - Not following a court order may lead to the authority being held liable for contempt.
[Para 7]

Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Sections 12, 14, 15 - Constitution of India, Article 215 - Compliance with an order of termination from service does not amount to contempt, even if the authority has not paid the arrears of salary without a positive direction to do so.
