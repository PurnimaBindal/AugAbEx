Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural Justice - Departmental proceedings - Domestic enquiry - Advocate - Equiry Officer - The management's advocate may be appointed as an enquiry officer to investigate an employee, as long as this does not affect the fairness and objectivity of the proceedings.
Proceedings cannot be considered invalid simply because an advocate was appointed as enquiry officer.
[Paras 8 to 10]

Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 11-A - Preliminary issue - Validity of enquiry - Once a preliminary issue concerning the validity of the enquiry has been decided, the Labour Court may not look at the evidence on the enquiry file or allow the parties to produce further evidence on this issue.
Instead, the Labour Court must evaluate the evidence on its merits and allow parties to lead their evidence on merits even after deciding the preliminary issue to decide whether the punishment order was valid and justified.
[Paras 11 and 12]
