Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Punjab Water Supply and Sewerage Board (Service) Regulations, 1981 - Promotion - Seniority - Post of Executive Engineer - The only requirement for promotion to the post of Executive Engineer is that the candidate has served as S.D.E.
for at least 8 years in the execution and maintenance of Public Health Works.
The petitioner had worked for more than 8 years in the execution and maintenance of PHW when Respondent No. 3 was promoted.
While the Board could have refused to consider the petitioner's claim for promotion on the basis of his being a graduate in Mechanical Engineering, they could not have preferred Respondent No. 3, who was far junior, merely on the basis of his having a degree in Civil Engg.
Since the petitioner is senior and possesses the necessary qualifications under the Regulations, his grievance is well-founded and sustained.
The promotion and appointment of Respondent No. 3 to the post of Xen is set aside, and the Respondent is directed to consider the claim of both parties in accordance with regulations.
