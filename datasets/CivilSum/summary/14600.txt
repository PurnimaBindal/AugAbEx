Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 162.
- Dismissal from service/Absence from duty/Misconduct - Willful absence from duty for two months will be considered a misconduct that can lead to dismissal from service.
[Paras 5, 7]
Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 162 - Dismissal from service/Past service record - If an employee's misconduct is severe enough to warrant dismissal, and the punishing authority examines their past record and determines that they are incorrigible and not dedicated to serving the department honestly, the dismissal order will not be invalidated since the past record is only taken into account to determine if a lesser punishment is appropriate.
[Paras 7 and 8]
Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 162 - Dismissal from service - Provident Fund - A dismissed police service employee under Rule 162 may withdraw their Provident Fund and other outstanding dues.
