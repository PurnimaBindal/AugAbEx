The State Government has decided to fill Haryana Civil Services (Executive Branch) posts using special recruitment provisions under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, and Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) Rules, 1930, Rule 5.
The Haryana Public Service Commission was in the process of selecting candidates when new eligibility criteria were announced in a fresh notification.
Previous candidates have no right to claim appointment based on the earlier notification and the authority responsible for recruitment has the inherent power to revise and substitute the method if necessary for good reasons under Punjab General Clauses Act, 1898, Section 19.
