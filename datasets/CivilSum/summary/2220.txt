Disciplinary Proceedings - Despite an interim court order suspending enquiry proceedings, continuing disciplinary proceedings after the respondent's employment had ceased was permitted by the right of the public employer to conduct a departmental enquiry.
Furthermore, since there were no allegations of malice, illegality, or bias, the petitioner could not profit from the interim order that obstructed the course of enquiry.
[Paras 22, 23, and 26] B. Gratuity - In order to argue a claim on its merits, the petitioner must first approach the authorities established under the Payment of Gratuity Act, as a claim for gratuity relies on entitlement under that Act.
