Punjab Civil Service Judicial Branch Rules, Rules 7 and 8 - Petitioners applied for posts of Judicial Officers in the category of ex-serviceman - The claim for their appointment was turned down on the basis that they did not fulfil the obligatory terms mentioned in the modified rules - Contesting the applicability of the modified Rules, petitioners were of the view that the rights vested with them at the time of filing the application cannot be taken away by the amendment in Rules - However, it was established that un-amended Rules 7 and 8 were unworkable and hence modified Rules were executed to remove the lacuna - The amended rules were considered to have been in existence even prior to the advertisement - Since the appellants could not secure the essential condition of gaining 55% marks, including the viva-voce test, they could not be chosen even under un-amended rules - Accordingly, the approach by the authorities was appropriate and the appeal was dismissed.
