Orissa Rural Employment, Education and Production Act, 1992, Section 3 - Constitution of India, Article 245 - Mines And Minerals - Legislative Competence - Validity - The levy of tax on "mineral bearing land" and "Coal bearing land" under Section 3 of the Employment Education Act, read with Sections 2(a) (1) and 2(d), is beyond the jurisdiction of State Legislature and is considered ultra vires.
