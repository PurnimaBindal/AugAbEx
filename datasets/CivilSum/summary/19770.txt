According to the order dated May 10, 1967, officers and staff allocated to Haryana State Electricity Board are to follow the same service conditions as those in the defunct P.S.E.B.
However, the Haryana Board amended the Rules and reduced promotion chances for some officials who were supposed to be promoted according to the amended rules.
While the amended rules do provide avenues for promotion, chances of promotion are not considered a condition of services, and as such, there is no violation of the Act or Order dated 10.5.1967.
