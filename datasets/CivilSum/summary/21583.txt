Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-B - The State Government is responsible for interpreting the award in case of disputes - The Labour Commissioner is not a judicial authority.
[Para 15]  B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Bonuses are not considered wages - Allowances are included in the definition of wages.
[Para 17]  C Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - The Presiding Officer of the Labour Court is a judicial authority - The award must be clear and certain - The award should not conflict with any statute.
[Paras 18 and 19]  D. Payment of Bonus Act, 1965 - Bonuses do not include wages - It must be construed in a different sense - The Labour Court cannot determine bonus rights under the Payment of Bonus Act.
[Para 20]  E. Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, Section 22 - A claim for bonus can be raised only by raising an industrial dispute - An execution application is not appropriate.
[Paras 28 and 29]  F Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, Section 22 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33C(1) - The provisions of an application under Section 33C(1) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 may not be held to have any application if the existence of right is disputed.
[Para 30]  G. Labour Commissioners cannot state that bonus payments are considered deferred wages - Such a view is impermissible when disputed by the appellant - The judgment is set aside.
