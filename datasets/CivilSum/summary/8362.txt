In appointments made, the petitioners argue that the respondents have violated the directions of the Court, as stated in Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
A Division Bench of this Court had directed the respondents to engage out of the select panel prepared and approved on 201190, and to offer appointment to the petitioners according to their merit in the select panel.
The respondents were strictly following the seniority position in the panel, and the contention that they were engaging casual labour through contractors and private agencies was also rejected.
The agreement was entered prior to the orders of this Court, so there could be no inferred disobedience of the order.
As a result, the petition was dismissed.
