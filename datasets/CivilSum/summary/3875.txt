Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination of services of workman - Industrial dispute referred to Labour Court to decide whether workmen's termination of services was justified - Workman claimed she was retrenched, having worked for 1-1/2 years and drawing a salary of Rs. 275/- per month at the time of termination - Management argued workman had already collected her final dues, estopping her from raising dispute - Labour Court ruled in favour of workman, finding management's documents not proved according to law and the workman having worked for more than 240 days in the calandar year, making Section 25-F applicable - No errors found with jurisdiction or the award - Writ petition dismissed.
