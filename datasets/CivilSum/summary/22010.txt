Andhra Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation Employees (Classification, Control and Appeal) Regulations, 1967, Regulations 18, 20 and 21 - Suspension - Pay and allowance for suspension period - If an employee of Corporation is suspended pending investigation and trial in a Criminal Prosecution and the proceedings are terminated in favor of the employee, they are not entitled to full salary for the suspension period as a right.
However, under the regulations of the Corporation, the authority can make a decision that the employee was not entitled to full salary for the suspension period.
This decision must be backed with reasoning and the employee must be given the opportunity to respond.
