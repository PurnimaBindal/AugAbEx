Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Resignation - Termination - Back wages - If an order of termination or refusal to accept resignation before its acceptance is declared illegal, it is assumed that all consequential reliefs are granted, and the employee is entitled to full back wages.
[Para 9] B Constitution of India, Article 30 - Grant-in-aid - Back wage - While the State Government cannot be held liable for back wages until they were made aware of the management's illegal action and did not take any corrective steps, once they were aware and directed the management not to fill the position until a decision was made, the State Government is liable to contribute 95% of the arrears from that date till the employee's reinstatement.
However, the management will be liable for 100% of back wages from the date the petitioner withdrew their resignation but was not allowed to return until the Government was involved in the illegal action.
