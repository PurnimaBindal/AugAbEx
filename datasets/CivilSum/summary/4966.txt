Railway Protection Force Act, 1957, Section 21 - Railway Protection Force Rules, 1987, Rules 935 & 90 - Transfer - Rule 935 only prohibits problematic employees from being posted to sensitive posts - It doesn't limit anyone else - Employers decide where to post employees based on service and administrative exigencies, not on employee requests - There is no right for an employee to demand a particular posting - The order to transfer the appellant to a non-sensitive post cannot be considered as that of punishment invoking Rule 935.
