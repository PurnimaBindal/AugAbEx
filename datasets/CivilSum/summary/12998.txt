The Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Civil) Recruitment Regulations 1965, Regulation 9(1)(i) and (ii) state that promotion from Junior Engineer (Civil) to Assistant Engineer (Civil) is based on seniority acquisition of the qualifications of Diploma and AMIE degree.
The Board is required to prepare a list on the first day of January each year with all eligible candidates, and names of candidates with AMIE/BE qualifications are to be included below the names of earlier eligible candidates.
If there was no list prepared under Clause (i), the seniority of lower cadre is liable to be ignored.
Clause (ii) cannot be read in isolation, and if a list had been prepared every year under Clause (i), it is a continuous running list.
The name of a JE who acquires the requisite two qualifications is liable to be included at the bottom of the list, irrespective of their original seniority in the lower cadre.
However, if two or more officials fulfill both conditions on the same day, their seniority in the original seniority in the lower cadre will prevail.
[Paras 15 & 17]
