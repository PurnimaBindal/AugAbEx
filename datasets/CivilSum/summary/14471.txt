Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Ad hoc appointment - Government policy - Term of adhoc appointment cannot be extended - Services terminated - The order of appointment cannot limit the term of appointment to six months in an arbitrary, unreasonable and unconstitutional manner - Employees appointed on ad hoc basis can only be terminated on selective grounds such as unsatisfactory performance, abolition of posts, availability of regularly selected candidates or misconduct.
