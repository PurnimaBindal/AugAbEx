Constitution of India, Article 226 - Natural Justice - The principle that no person can be judge in his own case - Petitioners expelled from hostel based on report of enquiry committee - Respondent No. 2, who was the complainant, played a significant role in decision making process from the acceptance of the report of fact finding committee until the decision to expel petitioners from hostel - This kind of action shows bias on the part of respondent No. 2 and violates the principles of natural justice - The impugned order is quashed - Respondents can proceed with the matter from when the fact finding committee submitted its report without the involvement of respondent No. 2.
[Paras 15 and 19]
