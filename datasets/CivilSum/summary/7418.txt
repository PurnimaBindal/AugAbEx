Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Sections 2(5), 25-B, Schedule II, Item 3 - Appointment of a workman for 120 days - The workman's appointment was renewed for a further 120 days after completing the initial 120-day contractual period, but at no time did he work continuously for 240 days.
Upon termination of his service at the end of the contractual period, he would no longer be considered a workman.
It is challenging to determine the status of a workman based on provisions of the Act that require completion of 240 days of work, and therefore, the workman cannot be eligible for reinstatement and continuity of service.
