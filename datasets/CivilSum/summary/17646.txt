Who can be tried twice on the same charges under Indian law is governed by Articles 311 and 320 of the Constitution - A petitioner was charged with embezzlement, but the charges were not proven - Despite the enquiry officer's report, the Punishing Authority proposed dismissal, sought approval from the Public Service Commission, and issued a fresh charge sheet - The petitioner was found not guilty again, but the order of dismissal was passed on the approval of the Public Service Commission - The State cannot use the same charges to both punish and exonerate someone - The order of dismissal was overturned on these grounds.

Filing charges is governed by Article 311 of the Indian Constitution - If someone is recommended for dismissal by a Service Commission, the approval is not binding on the government - The Service Commission is only a recommending authority, and the government must act independently and without prejudice.
