Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Discharge - The police being a disciplined force, anyone who remains absent for over three months, without providing a reasonable explanation, deserves to be discharged from duty.
[Para 11]
Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Absence from duty - Discharge - The Superintendent of Police had the option to issue a charge-sheet to the appellant and proceed to take penal action when the latter remained absent from duty for three months without any explanation - The officer also formed the opinion that the appellant was not likely to make a good police officer and passed a simple order of discharge - The order did not cast any stigma on the appellant since no allegations were made against him and no enquiry was required to be held - Therefore, the impugned order of discharge was held not illegal.
[Para 7]
Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Absence from duty - Discharge - The provision in Rule 1221 is intended to provide an opportunity to the employer to watch the performance of a constable - If the constable performs up to expectations, he can continue in service, or else the Superintendent of Police may decide to pass a simple order of discharge or proceed to take penal action - In this case, the SP passed the impugned order of discharge in complete conformity with the provisions of Rule 1221.
