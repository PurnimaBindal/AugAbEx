The Air Force Act, 1950, Sections 16, 17 and 20(3), as well as the Air Force Rules, 1969, Rule 15, and Air Force Order, Order 227/77, along with Constitution of India, Articles 70, 162 and 311, were taken into account for a dismissal case.
The petitioner, a Sgt.
Education Instructor in Group II, applied for discharge from service on compassionate grounds due to family problems, but his application was denied.
After he refused to get himself attested, he was issued a dismissal order according to A.F.O.
227/77.
However, because Rule 15, which is statutory, specifically applies in this case, A.F.O.
227/77 is not applicable and the dismissal order was quashed.
