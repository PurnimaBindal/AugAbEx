According to Rule 7 of the Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules of 1952, the condition of a one-month notice may not be necessary if the employer has the option to terminate the employee by paying one month's salary as notice pay.
Termination without notice will not invalidate the order of termination, but it will make the employer liable to pay one month's salary.
[Para]
The court can examine the circumstances surrounding the termination of an employee if it appears that the employee was punished despite an apparently innocuous order of termination.
In this case, the petitioner was charge-sheeted and an inquiry was conducted.
Before the inquiry proceedings could be concluded, the petitioner was relieved and told that their services were no longer needed.
The order of termination cannot be upheld as the findings of the inquiry officer were not disclosed, and there were no allegations of poor work or behavior during the probation period.
