Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Locus standi - The widow and heirs of a deceased employee have the right to challenge a punishment order that was illegally passed against the deceased before his death, as they will suffer from the consequences of it.
[Para 2]
Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Termination - Absence from duty - Illness - Disciplinary proceedings - Despite the husband's prolonged absence from work due to illness and the supporting evidence of his illness and eventual death, the enquiry officer did not believe the documents and imposed the punishment of termination.
This was a clear instance of 'non-application of mind and lack of humanitarian considerations', and the termination was not appropriate.
The order was subsequently annulled, and the petitioner and other legal heirs are now entitled to all other consequential benefits, including compassionate appointment.
[Paras 4 and 5]
