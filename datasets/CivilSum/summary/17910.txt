As per the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, the eligibility to be considered for the post of Lineman is possession of a Matric or equivalent qualification along with the National Apprenticeship Certificate in the Lineman trade.
Post obtaining the National Trade Certificate, candidates are required to undergo a two-year apprenticeship under the Indian Apprenticeship Act.
However, none of the petitioners possess the National Apprenticeship Certificate and hence are not eligible for the said role.
Therefore, there is no issue with the decision of the PSEB in denying the appointment of the petitioners as Linemen.
