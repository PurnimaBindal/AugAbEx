Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Recruitment Rules, Clause 6(k) - Recruitment - Employment exchange referred a list of 20 candidates - Despite being senior, the name of respondent No. 6 was illegally left from the list.
The first list was corrected by the Employment Exchange and respondent No. 6's name was incorporated, while the name of one 'P' was deleted.
It was clarified that the Employment Exchange only corrected the first list and did not prepare a second list as alleged.
The court directed the respondents to call candidates for interview in terms of the revised list, which was held to be valid.
