Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 11 - Res judicata - Industrial Dispute - Civil Court has no jurisdiction to decide an industrial dispute, including a dispute between a workman and management in regards to an order of termination.
As such, the Civil Court has no jurisdiction to grant a declaration that an order of termination is invalid.
The decree and judgment of the Civil Court are not binding on the Labour Court and therefore do not operate as res judicata.
[Para 4]

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - The Labour Court has the power to direct the reinstatement of a workman and impose any appropriate punishment.
The Labour Court is not restricted from altering the punishment awarded by management.
[Para 5]

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - The Respondent No1 was not found guilty of misappropriation or embezzlement based on the evidence.
