Constitution of India, Article 226 - Date of birth - Retirement - Salary - Documentation proves that the petitioner joined Indian Army as a sweeper on 24466 and that his date of birth was 10.1.38.
Although the petitioner was supposed to remain in service until age 60, his date of superannuation was cut short to 31.1.98 due to the fault of the respondents.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to salary through 31.1.98 and should not be made to suffer for the respondents' lapse.
The respondents are directed to release the petitioner's salary through 31.1.98 after deducting any salary already paid, and pensionary benefits should also be released within two months, treating the petitioner as in service until 31.1.98.
