The prospectus for admission carries legal weight and the conditions stated within it must be strictly followed.
Failure to provide the necessary documents by the application deadline for consideration in the reserve category renders the candidate ineligible for any benefits.
However, the candidate may still be considered in the open category.
If an additional document not stated in the prospectus is required, the candidate cannot be excluded from their chosen category.
The prospectus only required a category certificate from the competent authority, and the college's additional requirement of an affidavit cannot be enforced.
The conditions stated in the prospectus are the only ones that can be enforced.
