Constitution of India, Article 16 - Voluntary retirement - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 48A - Voluntary retirement - Notice of voluntary retirement given by the petitioner was accepted but petitioner requested permission to withdraw - Holding permission cannot be withheld, it was found that the respondent's action of withholding permission was unjustified - As a result, the action was deemed illegal and quashed.
