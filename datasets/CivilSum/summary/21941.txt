Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Army Rules, Rule 13-A - Army instructions, Special instruction, Para 15 - Promotion - Technical Graduate examination - The petitioner received a show cause notice for termination under Rule 13-A read with para 15 of the special instructions for failing to pass the promotion examination within the period of service.
He claimed exemption from passing the technical promotion examination due to being a Technical Graduate, but this exemption was not granted to similarly situated officers.
As a result, the Authorities were within their rights to issue such notice under Rule 13-A, and the petitioner was not entitled to any exemption.
The order of the Single Judge was restored.
