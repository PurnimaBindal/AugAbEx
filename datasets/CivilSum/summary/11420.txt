According to Constitution of India, Article 311 and Police Act, 1861, Section 7, disciplinary proceedings can lead to dismissal from service.
A petitioner was charged with purchasing a motorcycle without permission of department and indulging with accused under NDPS Act, but the motorcycle was not in petitioner's name and no case was registered against them under NDPS Act.
In the departmental enquiry, no Presenting Officer was appointed and the Enquiry Officer acted as presenting officer.
As a result, the dismissal order was quashed and the petitioner was reinstated with full back wages.
