Criminal Procedure Code, Sections 378(3) - Speaking order - Appeal before High Court against order of Acquittal - High Court refused leave by order - "Heard Dismissed" - Order set aside - It is necessary for an order to include reasons in order for it to have clarity and indicate that the High Court has applied its mind, especially when the order can be challenged further.
This requirement is supported by the case 2004(1) RCR(Criminal) 304 : 2004(2) Apex Criminal 193 (SC).
