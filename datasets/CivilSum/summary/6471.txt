The Khadi Gramodyog Maha Vidyalaya Pension Scheme states that regulations applicable to employees of the board will also apply to employees of the Mahavidyalaya.
As a result, the benefits of the pension scheme for board employees will extend to Vidyalaya employees as well.
This is in accordance with the AP Revised Pension Rules, 1980 and the Andhra Pradesh Khadi and Village Industries Board Act, 1958, which are governed by Articles 226 and 300-A of the Constitution of India, as well as Regulations 7 and 9 of the Khadi Gramodyog Maha Vidyalaya Employees Recruitment and Service Conditions Regulations, 1996.
