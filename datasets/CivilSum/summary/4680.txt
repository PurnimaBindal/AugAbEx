Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Section 10 - Pay Parity - A recognized school must provide its employees with the same salary, allowances, and conditions of service as those of employees in schools run by the appropriate authority - The employee's financial difficulties are not a valid reason for relieving them of their statutory obligation to pay.
[Paras 18 and 21]
Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Section 10 - Pay Parity - The liability of a school to pay arrears of salary from the date of its recognition is absolute - However, arrears are restricted from the start of the academic year 2003-04 to avoid raising fees.
