Employees of autonomous bodies are not considered Govt. servants and any order imposing cut in the pension of a retired Govt. employee can only be passed by the President of India in consultation with UPSC, but this mention in Pension Rules does not apply to the employees of Boards that adopt such Rules.
The Board has the authority to pass necessary orders in the case of its employees, without having to consult UPSC.
The Board can substitute the `President' by `Board' and `Government' by `DVB'.
[Para 6] B.
Appeal against an order under Rule 9 cannot be heard by any inferior authority to the punishing authority.
If the punishing authority is the Board itself, there can be no superior authority.
Applying the `doctrine of necessity', the Board would be justified in hearing the appeal itself.
[Para 7] C. An order to cut the pension under Rule 9 can be passed even in the absence of any pecuniary loss caused by the misconduct of the retiree.
[Para 8] D. The punishing authority is not bound by the findings of the enquiry report and can differ from them.
[Para 9] E The Writ Court cannot act as an appellate Authority over the findings reached by the authorities, unless the same are based on no evidence or perverse.
