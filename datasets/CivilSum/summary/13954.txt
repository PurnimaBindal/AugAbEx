Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Act, 1979 - Punjab Privately Managed Recognized Schools Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 9(2) - Punjab Privately Managed Recognized Aided Schools Retirement Benefit Scheme, 1992, Clause 6(3) - Retiral benefits - Calculation of qualifying service - The employer must obtain permission to join the service of other schools - The objective of the rule is to inform the employer in advance about joining the service of another employer - Non-compliance of the rule cannot defeat the purpose of the act and rules intended to grant retiral benefits - The scheme of 1992 cannot take away accrued benefits under the act and rules - Employees cannot comply with conditions that did not exist at the time - Authorities instructed to calculate entire service and retiral benefits accordingly.
