Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 136 - Haryana Vidhan Sabha Secretarial Service Rules, 1981, Rule 10(2)(b)(i) - Promotion/Reversion/Probation/Equal pay for equal work - The appellant was promoted to Deputy Secretary and placed on one year probation.
However, his work was deemed unsatisfactory and he was subsequently reverted, though he was directed to continue with the same duties until further notice.
The order of reversion was upheld, but the respondents were instructed to pay the appellant the same emoluments he received as Deputy Secretary until he is relieved of the post's duties.
