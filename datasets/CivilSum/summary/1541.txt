Section 17B of the Industrial Disputes Act 1947 and Section 22 of the Sick Industrial Companies (Special Provisions) Act 1985 deal with Payment of Back Wages.
An employee who was awarded back wages by a labour court, had their award challenged on the grounds that a reference before SICA was pending, and the order by the labour court was barred by section 22 of SICA.
However, it was held that the embargo under section 22 of SICA is not an impediment against a claim for recovery of wages, since the industrial dispute raised by the employee is not a proceeding against properties of the company nor is a suit for recovery being barred under provisions of section 22 of SICA.
