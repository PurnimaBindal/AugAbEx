Constitution of India, Article 311(2), 42nd amendment - Natural Justice/Copy of Enquiry report - The Supreme Court's observation in the Ramjan Khan case is that, despite the amendment in Art. 311, the delinquent must be provided with a copy of the enquiry report and proposed punishment to meet natural justice - These observations are only applicable prospectively - The proposed punishment in the enquiry report was increased by the punishing authority - This cannot be challenged on the grounds of non-supply of the enquiry report.

Constitution of India, Article 246 - Central Industrial Security Force Act, 1968, Sections 22 and 9(3) - Central Industrial Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 49 - Delegation of powers - The Central Government has been given revisional powers under the Act, and has further delegated these powers under Rule 49 to the Director General - This action is considered a sub-delegation and has rendered Rule 49 ultra vires.
