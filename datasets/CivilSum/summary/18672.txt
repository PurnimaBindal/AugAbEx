Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Retiral Benefits - Interest - The petitioners retired from the services of Mata Gujri College, Fetehgarh Sahib and a notification was issued by the Punjab Govt. in 1976 stating that employees of privately managed aided institutions would be entitled to automatic pay revision and all the benefits equivalent to those of their counterparts working in the Government Institutions - Within one month of the order, Respondents No. 3 & 4 must pay all retiral benefits to the petitioners along with interest at a rate of 18% per annum.
