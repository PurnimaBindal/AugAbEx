The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947 outlines the scope of relief of back wages, which includes entitlement to increments, Dearness Allowance, and revision in pay scale for reinstated employees.
However, the rate of salary cannot be based on the date of termination.

The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 33C(1), raises doubts about the maintainability of applications for benefits such as bonus, leave encashment, Provident Fund Contribution, and perks.
The issue of maintainability remains undecided by the court.
