Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Joint writ petition - Seniority - Confirmation - The seniority list is determined by the date of confirmation, without considering continuous officiating service on the post of the concerned employees and basic grade seniority.
The authority is legally obligated to confirm at the appointed time.
The seniority list was decided to be revised.
Petitioners filed writ petitions jointly.
The seniority list was being challenged in its entirety.
All petitioners have a common cause of action.
Therefore, a joint writ petition can be maintained.
