Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 14 - Compassionate Appointment - The application for compassionate appointment cannot be kept pending indefinitely.
If the family can sustain itself for a prolonged period, it cannot be assumed that they are still experiencing financial distress.
Compassionate appointments cannot be claimed as a right and granted to a specific individual in violation of Article 14 of the Constitution because appointments in public positions must be made through open recruitment where all eligible candidates have a chance.
Moreover, compelling a public authority to appoint a particular individual would deprive it of choosing the most eligible candidate.
