Constitution of India, Article 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 33-A - Employees filed an application under Section 33-A claiming reinstatement and payment of wages due to a contravention of management's layoff action - Principle of res judicata applies to subsequent references under Section 10 of the Act - A proceeding under Section 33-A allows disputes over the legality of retrenchment to be addressed and can result in reinstatement if found to be illegal - Res judicata principles are applicable to the present case.
