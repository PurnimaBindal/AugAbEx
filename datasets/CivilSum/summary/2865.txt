Constitution of India, Art. 14 & 16 - Termination - Reinstatement - Back wages - Usually, a worker whose employment has been unlawfully terminated would be eligible for full back wages except for the time during which he was gainfully employed while unemployed - Employer failed to demonstrate workman's gainful employment during termination - Refusal to pay back wages is not justifiable.
[Paras 7 & 8]  B Constitution of India, Art. 14 & 16 - Back wages - Delay in issuing demand notice - Back wages may be decreased to the date of the demand notice if there is an inordinate delay in issuing the demand notice that is unexplained.
However, once an action is initiated within the period of limitation, the period preceding its initiation cannot be taken into account to determine the issue of delay and laches - Back wages must be calculated from the initial date of termination, not the date of the demand notice.
