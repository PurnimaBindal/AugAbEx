Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India and Section 115 of the Evidence Act stipulate that selection participation amounts to a waiver of the right to challenge the source of recruitment.
[Para 7]  B In a petition challenging selection, those recommended or appointed are necessary parties; the selection cannot be upset in their absence.
[Para 8]  C The purpose of the waiting list is to appoint individuals against unfilled positions.
The inclusion of a candidate's name in the waiting list does not guarantee an appointment, nor does it confer any legal right.
[Paras 9 and 10]  D Any vacancies must be advertised to provide an equal opportunity for eligible candidates to apply, and new openings cannot be filled from the waiting list without advertising them.
[Paras 11 and 12]  E. The life of the selection list is one year, after which it expires, and the court cannot infuse life into the dead selection list after the expiration of its validity.
