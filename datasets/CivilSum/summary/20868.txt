All India Services Act 1951, Section 3 - IAS (Appointment by Selection) Regulations 1956, Regulation 3(l) - Non State Civil Service Officer - Appointment to IAS - The Appellant was recommended for appointment by their parent department, however, no meeting of the State Committee for short-listing candidates occurred for two years.
A fresh recommendation was also not favorably considered by the State Committee as the Appellant became overage.
The Appellant's plea for a different cut-off date was not upheld, as no such duty is placed under Regulations for annual consideration for appointment by selection.
