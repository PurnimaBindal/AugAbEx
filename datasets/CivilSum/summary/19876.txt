According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol II, Rule 5.32 - A(C), premature retirement can only be enforced when an employee has received consistently bad reports for ten years leading up to it.
In the case at hand, the petitioner had received 10 good reports in that time, with only one average report, which did not mention anything negative pertaining to their integrity.
As a result, the order for premature retirement was nullified and the petitioner was entitled to all consequential benefits.
