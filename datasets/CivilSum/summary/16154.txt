The petitioner in the case was interested in many posts in the general pool and applied as a general category candidate without any mention of being from a reserved category, thereby waiving his right in the reserved category for backward class vacancies.
The government is not obligated to provide employment to every backward candidate nor can a supposed right be claimed.
Article 16(4) is not applicable to his case, and he cannot claim rights to reserved posts after waiving his rights in that category.
