Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Punjab Services (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1940, Rule 2(3) - Treatment - Reimbursement of medical bills - The reimbursement for out-door treatment cannot be limited to Rs 500/- pm - The Rules do not differentiate between outdoor or indoor treatment for the purpose of reimbursement - Instructions making such differentiation and fixing the limit as Rs 500/- p.m. are violations of Articles 14 and 21 of the Constitution - Supreme Court judgment in Ram Lubhaya Bagga's case is inapplicable - Para 3 of the instructions of Haryana Government dated 1181992 that denies the benefit of full medical reimbursement to an outdoor patient quashed - Government instructed to provide full reimbursement.
