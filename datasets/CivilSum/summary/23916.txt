Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Disciplinary Proceedings - Respondent No. 1 was put forward for promotion on 28.5.1998 - Disciplinary proceedings were initiated against him on 2871998 and he was suspended on the same day - The High Court determined that the suspension was an afterthought, ordered that the suspension order be disregarded, and allowed the disciplinary proceedings to continue - The High Court also ordered that the promotion panel should be considered valid - This was not appropriate - Respondent No. 1 was required to be suspended until the disciplinary proceedings were completed.
[Paras 3, 4 and 5]
