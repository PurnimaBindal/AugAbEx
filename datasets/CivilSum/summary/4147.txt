The petition challenging the selection of Respondent No. 4 as PCS (Executive Branch) after two years of finalization is subject to the ground of delay and laches.
[Paras 11 and 12]
Any person employed in executive branch can be recommended by any of the two ministers for selection if he is satisfied with his work and conduct.
[Para 13]
The petitioner's application for setting aside the recommendations of Respondent to PCS (Executive Branch) on the same grounds was rejected earlier and therefore he cannot file a fresh writ petition for the same relief.
[Para 14]
Mere recommendation and selection do not entitle a person to claim a writ of mandamus for appointment.
[Paras 15 to 18]
