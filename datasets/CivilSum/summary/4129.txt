Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Quantum of punishment - Removal from service - Charge of wilful disobedience of orders and wilful absence found to be true - Punishment of removal from service given - Various irregularities in the proceedings leading to violation of natural justice principles - Normally, if proceedings are found to be defective, a re-enquiry should be ordered; however, this would be even more harmful to the delinquent - Given the circumstances, the punishment of removal from service was deemed disproportionate to the offense - In order to serve the ends of justice, the court adjusted the punishment, changing it from removal from service to forfeiture of back-wages only, and ordering reinstatement with continuity for pension purposes.
