Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Vocation Education Scheme/Regularisation of part time teachers working in vocational education scheme - The idea that part-time vocational education teachers should be paid the same as full-time non-vocational teachers is unfounded - The decision to determine the type of staff required for a government scheme is the responsibility of the executive - It is unlikely that the court would interfere with the government's decisions unless the activity is permanent in nature - If the scheme is permanent, the government must absorb staff periodically after a certain length of service - The government of Karnataka agreed to regularize part-time lecturers after ten years of service - This process will be completed within six months.
[Paras 9 to 14]
