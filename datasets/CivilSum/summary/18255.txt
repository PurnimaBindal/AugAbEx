Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Rules for Fixation of Pay of University Employees, 1985, Rule 6(1) - Protection of existing pay - Petitioners were appointed as Section Officer on the condition of protecting their pay as Senior Personal Assistant - An audit objection regarding retiral benefits was raised after superannuation, claiming they cannot be released as per the last pay drawn by the petitioners - Rule 6(1) safeguards the last pay drawn by the petitioners - It was held that the single Judge's decision to release retiral benefits according to the pay last drawn was correct - There is no need for interference - The appeal is rejected.
