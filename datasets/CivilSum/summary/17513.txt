Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Resignation - Withdrawal of resignation before acceptance - Retrospective acceptance - An employee of the Government can only withdraw their resignation before acceptance by the competent authority if they remain in service awaiting this acceptance.
If, on their own volition, they unilaterally abandon their post and service after submitting their resignation and stay away from duty, they are not entitled to withdraw it at any time.
Consequently, their service and tenure stands terminated immediately regardless of when their resignation is accepted.
Retrospective acceptance of the resignation in such a case would be appropriate.
