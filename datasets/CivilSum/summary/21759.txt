Orissa Service Code, Rule 71(a) - G.A.
Department Circular No. 30495/GA dated 24111987 - Compulsory retirement - The government must form an opinion that a government officer needs to be compulsorily retired from service and must consider the entire service record, particularly the latest record, to make that decision.
Failure to consider the entire record objectively may result in arbitrary exercise of power.
In this case, the government only used a single adverse report from the relevant year to make the decision, without considering earlier or later reports.
This exercise of power is illegal and the order of compulsory retirement is set aside.
[Paras 4, 9, and 10] B.
Confidential Reports - Writing confidential reports is a significant responsibility for the reporting officer and can greatly impact the career prospects of subordinate officers/employees.
Reporting officers should provide fair, objective, dispassionate, and constructive comments while assessing the character, ability, and responsibility displayed by the concerned officer/employee during the relevant period.
Subordinate officers/employees should be encouraged to improve their efficiency in service and discharge of duty.
