Under the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 20(1) and the Constitution of India, Article 136, the Deputy Labour Commissioner in the State of Orissa has the authority to grant compensation for workmen's compensation.
This is due to a notification issued under section 20 of the Act that designated the Deputy Labour Commissioner as the Commissioner for Workmen's Compensation for the entirety of the State of Orissa.
