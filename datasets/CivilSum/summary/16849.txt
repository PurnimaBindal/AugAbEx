Central Civil Services Rules 1972, Rule 49(2)(b) - Pension - Provisional Death-cum-Retirement gratuity already granted - A plea by the respondent that complete particulars of the petitioner's service was not furnished must be rejected, as the department had complete particulars of his service if provisional gratuity had been granted.
[Para 7]
Central Civil Services Rules 1972, Rules 9(1) & 49(2)(b) - Proportionate pension - Minor penalty - The penalty of withholding one increment that was awarded to the petitioner is not enough to establish grave misconduct or negligence, so it will not affect the pension of the petitioner.
