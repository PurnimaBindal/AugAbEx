Respondent No. 2, represented by B M Lal, Advocate, invokes the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, specifically Section 2(oo)(bb), in support of their contention that the termination of the workman's contract was in accordance with the conditions of appointment.
The workman's position was comparable to that of a probationer, as she had agreed to the condition that her services may be terminated due to unsatisfactory conduct or work performance before the expiry of the appointment period.
The workman's unbecoming conduct has been established on record, and therefore, the award of reinstatement is unjustified and should be quashed.
