Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Appeal against penalty - Decision by same officer - Sustainability - Petitioner appealed against an order of a promoted Executive Engineer who himself entertained and dismissed the appeal - It is not permissible for one to be a judge in his own cause or his own judgments - In ensuring that justice is not only done but must appear to have been done, the respondent should not have decided the appeal himself - The order has been quashed.
