Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation - Petitioners served in class IV category posts for over a decade - Their petition for regularisation was rejected due to overage and non-availability of posts - Petitioners' case for regularisation cannot be turned down because of their overage when they were initially appointed - It would be unjust for respondents to deny regularisation of petitioners' services when they have allowed them to work for more than 10 years on same posts - Respondents directed to take necessary actions for regularising services of petitioners.
