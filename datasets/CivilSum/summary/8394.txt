According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 226 and 227, and the Employees' Provident Fund and Family Pension Fund Act, 1952, Section 7-A, a petitioner running a partnership firm and manufacturing ink disclosed that their establishment had a maximum of 17 employees.
However, a PF inspector's report stated that the petitioner had 16 direct employees and 7 contractor's workers.
As a result, an order was issued in the 7-A proceedings stating that the petitioner's establishment was governed by the provisions of the act.
The petitioner argued that their establishment and Umrao's establishment were two independent entities, but the court held that, based on certain facts such as the unity of purpose and similarity of business carried on by both, Umrao Ali was not an independent contractor and his workers were actually the employees of the petitioner.
Therefore, both establishments were considered one for the purpose of the act and the petitioner was governed by its provisions.
The petition was dismissed.
