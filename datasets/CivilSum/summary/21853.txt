Constitution of India, Article 226 - Classification - Cadre structuring - Rural Development Officers and Specialist Officers of the Bank categorized into different cadres based on duties and responsibilities - Although job duties may be similar, differences in quantum, quality and utility of work may justify such classification.
[Paras 15 and 17]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Classification - Equal pay for equal work - Principle of equal pay for equal work based on nature of work performed rather than volume of work - Qualitative differences in reliability and responsibility of duties may exist - Job functions may be the same but differences in tasks make a difference - Duties and responsibilities of Rural Development Officers and Specialist Officers of the Banks cannot be considered equal, therefore equal pay for equal work cannot be applied.
