Army Instructions 75/78 prescribe rules for Short Service Commissioned officers, and state that earlier service as a permanent commission officer cannot be counted towards the permissible tenure of 10 years for a short service commission.
In this case, the Petitioner had served as a permanent commission officer for about 5 years before resigning and joining the short service commission for 5 years.
However, the Respondents counted the Petitioner's earlier service in the permissible tenure, and gave him ante-dated seniority for pay and promotion on rejoining.
The court held that this contravenes the rules and is illegal.
[Paras 12, 13, 16 and 29]

The Petitioner argued that similarly situated persons were granted extension, while he was denied.
The court held that the order refusing extension to the Petitioner was discriminatory.
[Para 16]

The Respondents contended that the Petitioner is not entitled to extension because he misrepresented that he had been released from service earlier, whereas he had resigned.
However, the court held that the Petitioner was required to be released upon acceptance of his resignation, and it would be unfair to disentitle him to extension on this ground.
[Para 19]
