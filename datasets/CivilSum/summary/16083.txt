Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Haryana Education (College Cadre) Group A Service Rules, 1986, Rule 9(1)(b) - Pay scale - Professor's grade - Petitioner appointed as a Principal on 12281 by direct recruitment is senior to one who was given the Professor's grade based on the requisite 25 years of teaching experience - Arbitrary and unfair - The provision of giving the Professor's grade based on this kind of criteria creates an anomalous situation where a junior Principal gets a higher grade than a senior, which violates the equality clause of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - Respondent directed to give petitioner the Professor's grade from the same date as any of his junior was given that grade.
