Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226/227 - Intermediate School Course - Eligibility - The petitioner's eligibility is based on their confirmation date.
In another writ petition, a Head Constable ranks senior to the petitioner, but since they have been deputed for the Intermediate Course, the petitioner has a right to be deputed as well due to their seniority.
The official respondents are instructed to depute the petitioner for the Intermediate School Course.
