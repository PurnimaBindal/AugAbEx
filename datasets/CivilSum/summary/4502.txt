Rajasthan Educational Subordinate Service Rules, 1971, Rule 16 - Recruitment process requires publication of posts inviting applications from the general public.
Restricting publication only to the calling of names from Employment Exchanges is a violation of recruitment rules and the Constitution.
[Paras 9 and 14]  B.
Recruitment by calling candidates only from Employment Exchanges is unconstitutional, illegal, and unfair.
Employment Exchanges lack guidelines and machinery to test the merit of candidates, nor do they maintain any such list.
Appointments in one district cannot be restricted to candidates only from that district, and selection on District/Divisional level would be absurd.
[Paras 15 to 17]  C. The appointing authority for public posts is obligated to make publication of posts to the general public, with information of vacancy and number of reserved vacancies.
Only publishing information that names have been called from Employment Exchanges and only considering such candidates is unconstitutional.
