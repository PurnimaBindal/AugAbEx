Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 300-A - A.P.
Revised Pension Rules, 1980, Rule 9 - Pension - Retiral benefits - Withholding of - An employee of State Govt. who opted to be absorbed into service of Corporation is entitled to pension and retirement benefits as per Rules.
However, after imposition of punishment of reduction in pension and recovery of certain amount from terminal benefits, the petitioner retired during the pendency of enquiry proceedings.
As the relationship between the employer and employee ceases once the latter retires, disciplinary proceedings cannot be continued without any authority of law.
In such a case, the petitioner is also entitled to interest and directions must be issued to pay terminal benefits with interest at 12% pa from the date when they became due.
