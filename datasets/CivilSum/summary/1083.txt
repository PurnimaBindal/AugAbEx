Judicial review of a departmental inquiry where there was an 11-year delay in the supply of listed documents that were only given after court intervention.
The delay by the respondents was both unreasonable and blameworthy, and the inquiry cannot be allowed to go on indefinitely even if it concerns corruption allegations.
The inquiry was deemed unlawful and went against the petitioner's rights to fair and reasonable treatment from the state.
As a result, the charge-sheet issued to the petitioner was canceled.
