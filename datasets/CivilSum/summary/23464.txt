The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 41 emphasize that employment given on daily wages under Jawahar Rozgar Yojna by DRDA, which is meant to provide those below the poverty line with a livelihood, cannot claim regularisation since the scheme is not intended to provide more than what it already offers - regularisation will frustrate the scheme itself.
[Para 15]  B.
While the right to livelihood falls under the right to life in the Constitution of India, it has not been incorporated as a fundamental right due to the country's inability to guarantee it, and not because it is considered less fundamental to life - it has been placed under the Chapter on Directive Principles of State Policy, which mandates the State to make effective provisions for securing the right to work within the limits of its economic capacity and development.
