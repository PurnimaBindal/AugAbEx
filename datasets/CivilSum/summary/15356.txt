According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 2.2(b), an employee found guilty of accepting a bribe in departmental proceedings may have a part or all of their pension withheld by the Government.
Just because the Government did not experience any monetary loss does not mean that no deduction can be made from the employee's pension.
The 1996 (4) SCT 734 (P&H) case is distinguished in paragraphs 11 and 12.
