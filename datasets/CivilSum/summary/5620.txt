Contempt of Courts Act, Section 12 - The contempt court cannot grant any relief beyond the scope of the order that must be implemented.
If the order is implemented and there is no intentional disobedience, the contempt proceedings rule must be discharged.
If the petitioner is not fully satisfied with the order, they must seek their remedy or appeal.
The Contempt Court cannot add to the order.
The Writ court quashed the order of premature compulsory retirement and directed reinstatement with consequential reliefs.
The Contempt Court's direction to confirm and promote the petitioner from retrospective dates was beyond the scope of the order and the writ petition.
The Single Judge's order in contempt was without jurisdiction and wrong.
The order is quashed.
