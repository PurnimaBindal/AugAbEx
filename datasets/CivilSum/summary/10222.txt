Constitution of India, Articles 226, 311 - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 47 - Unexecutable Decree - Writ Petition - Maintainability - According to Para 7, it has been held that a writ petition cannot be used to exert, enforce or implement a right that has accrued to a party pursuant to a judgment, decree or order of a civil court governed and controlled by provisions of the code.
