Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 read with Article 21 - Appointment/Termination - Abolition of posts - Restructuring of the Statutory Corporation - Petitioners appointed as Managers in the Corporation after following the proper procedures, but had their service terminated due to restructuring - Posts of Managers not yet abolished - The court finds it unfair to terminate the petitioners' services when there are vacancies in the same position - Termination orders quashed and reinstatement ordered without back wages.
