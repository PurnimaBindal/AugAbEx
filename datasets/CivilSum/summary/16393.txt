The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, and 226, dictate the admission process for in-service candidates seeking entry to PG disciplines.
The appellant passed the entrance test and achieved a high rank, but was unable to provide the necessary service certificate for in-service candidates.
Despite being given four months to obtain the certificate, it was not produced, and therefore no relief could be granted due to a lack of vigilance.
