Under the Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rules 2 & 4, individuals who were enrolled or commissioned in Army service before the emergency was declared are eligible for military service benefits when they take up civil positions in Punjab, with regards to pay and seniority.
Nirmal Singh v. State of Punjab, 1991(1) SCT 35(P&H) : 1992(1) SLR 24 is overruled, while 1990(1) RSJ 85 and 1992(3) SCT 312 Moti Singh v. District & Sessions Division are followed.
