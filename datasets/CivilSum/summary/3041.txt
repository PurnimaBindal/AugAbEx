Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - The petitioner was working on probation.
However, the probation period was extended, and the petitioner's services were terminated during the extended period.
It was held that the respondents' actions were not arbitrary, and a writ can only be issued if they have violated any statutory or constitutional provision, which is not mentioned.
[Para 20]

Constitution of India, Article, 12 - Whether EAN-INDIA is a "State" or "another authority" within the meaning of Article 12 of Constitution of India amenable to the writ jurisdiction of this court - The Court held that EAN-INDIA is not a body covered under Article 12 of the Constitution, and therefore the writ petition is not maintainable.
