Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Regularisation - Confirmation to the Post of Principal of College - Petitioner, who was authorised to discharge routine work of Principal under a working arrangement, also served as the head of the Department of Pathology in addition to his substantive post.
During the relevant period, no one was promoted or confirmed to the post of Principal.
The petitioner claimed confirmation, but was not available for promotion due to retirement.
Juniors to the petitioner were considered and given adhoc promotion as Principals, however, the court held that the petitioner had no right accrued to him and his claim was not justified.
