Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(s) - Medical representative - Determination that medical representatives promoting product sales are not workman under the Act - Consequent invalidity of the entire proceeding in reference case and award.
[Paras 12 and 16] B Transfer - Challenge to transfer - Possibility cited by letter of transfer on request or mutual basis - Holding that even if provision existed allowing workman to refuse transfer would not be a right - Transfer falling under the service conditions and permissible unless carried out with malafide or against rules or was done by an authority lacking competence- Termed appropriate for the aggrieved party to seek resolution via higher authority rather than challenge.
