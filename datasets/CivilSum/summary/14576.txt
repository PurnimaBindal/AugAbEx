For Respondent No. 2: VP Sharma, Advocate.
According to Article 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, once seniority is established, it cannot be changed without notice, hearing, and proper opportunity for audi alteram partem.
Actions that result in a person's reversion without proper notice and a chance to comply with rules are liable to be quashed.
Additionally, Article 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India indicate that promotions must be based on seniority and promotion rules.
Accordingly, the court cannot issue a promotion directive to the petitioner if senior persons have not yet been promoted.
