Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Seniority - Transfer - Own request - The petitioner, who had requested a transfer from one circle to another, was aware that persons transferred at their request were placed at the tail end of the seniority list - Maintaining seniority on a circle-wise basis - Petitioner's claim for seniority cannot be upheld, and the petition is dismissed.
