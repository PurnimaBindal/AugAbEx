Gujarat State Civil Service (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1971, Rules 9 and 10 - Departmental Inquiry - Quashing - Seeking retiral benefit - Appellant had to superannuate on March 31, 1992 - He replied to show cause notice a day before his superannuation - Inquiry did not proceed further - Single Judge gave interim relief with conditions - Benefits granted upon security and undertaking - Respondent failed to complete inquiry despite direction - Appellant requested full amount - Petition dismissed by Single Judge - LPA concluded that Single Judge made no error in dismissing the petition - High Court is typically not authorized to quash a departmental inquiry under Article 226 of the Constitution of India - Appellant had received conditional benefit which cannot be relieved from or discharged from security - Respondent instructed to complete inquiry quickly.
