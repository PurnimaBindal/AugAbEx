The delay in filing a petition challenging a Labour Court Award without any explanation for the delay is not acceptable under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17.
The Court held the petition liable to dismiss on account of delay.
The State cannot deny an employee relief by allowing such a delay.
[Paras 12, 20, 22 & 25]
When scrutinizing the legality of a labour court award, writ jurisdiction is hardly extensive.
The court cannot examine the facts in great detail if the award is in accordance with the law.
[Paras 14, 18 & 19]
The state is obligated to carry out the orders of the court in accordance with the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17.
If a right exists, it must be enforced.
[Paras 21, 23 & 24]
