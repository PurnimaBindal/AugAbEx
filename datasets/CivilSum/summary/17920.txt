According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, discrimination in appointment to posts on compassionate grounds is strictly prohibited.
In this particular case, the petitioner's father passed away while serving as an Assistant Excise and Taxation Officer, and the petitioner himself holds a double MA and is seeking appointment as an Excise and Taxation Officer, which is a Class II post.
According to government instructions, appointments can be made on compassionate grounds for both Class II and I posts.
Additionally, there is no legitimate reason to deny the petitioner's appointment when previous individuals with lesser qualifications have been appointed to the same post.
It is unacceptable for the government to adhere to instructions only to a certain extent and arbitrarily deny appointments to higher posts than Class III and IV.
Such actions are in violation of Articles 14 and 16.
As a result, the respondents are obligated to appoint the petitioner to the post of ETO, for which he has the necessary qualifications.
