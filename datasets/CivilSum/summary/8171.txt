Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Resignation - Withdrawal of - It was necessary to convey that leave could not be granted if prayer for resignation was alternative one, otherwise the letter could not be considered as resignation.
Furthermore, the petitioner's continuous application for leave on subsequent occasions constituted withdrawal of resignation.
Unless leave is rejected, it cannot be assumed that the petitioner had abandoned service.
The Labour Court was incorrect in stating that the resignation was accepted and that the petitioner had abandoned service.
The petitioner's service was terminated, and the case was remanded to the Labour Court to make a decision in accordance with the law.
