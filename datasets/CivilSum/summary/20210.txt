Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Railway Board Circular dated 11.8.1992 - Discrimination - Gender discrimination - Railway accommodation - Regularisation of Railway accommodation - The regulation that distinguishes between married and unmarried daughters for eligibility of Railway accommodation violates Article 14 of the Constitution - Married daughters should be given the same eligibility as unmarried daughters - If all sons of a retired Railway employee are married and not employed by Railways, then the employee's married daughter should be entitled to regularisation of accommodation in her name.
