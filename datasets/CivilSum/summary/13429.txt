Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Rajasthan Service Rules, 1951, Rules 110 and 112 - Study leave - While the Rules only allow for half salary to be granted during study leave, there is no restriction on granting full salary to doctors on deputation - The respondent-State grants full salary to Veterinary and Medical Doctors pursuing post-graduate studies, but not to Doctors in the Ayurved Department - Failure to grant the same facility would be considered discriminatory.
