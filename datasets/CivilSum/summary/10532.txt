Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rules 511 and 512 - Premature retirement - Medical fitness - Partial incapacity - Retirement on the grounds of partial medical fitness, related to poor eyesight, and exposure to occupational hazards - In case of an employee who is likely to contract a disease or suffer incapacity due to their job, the employer must first try to provide them with alternative suitable employment - If no suitable position is available, the employee must be given an additional allowance along with their retirement benefits.
