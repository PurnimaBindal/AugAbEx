Army Act, Section 19 - Army Rules, Rule 14 - Termination from service - To terminate an officer's services or dismiss them from service, a show-cause notice must be issued, and the officer should be given all adverse materials against them - Non-compliance with Rule 14(2) of the Army Rules will vitiate any proceedings and actions taken by the authority - The petitioner was dismissed from service without adherence to Rule 14(2) - No adverse materials were supplied to the petitioner along with the show-cause notice - The order of dismissal is unsustainable, and the petitioner is entitled to all retiral benefits and other allowances until the date of superannuation.

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Army Act, Section 19 - Army Rules, Rule 14 - Special provisions - Since the defence service and civil services have an apparent distinction, the defence services are not covered under Article 311 of the Constitution - Therefore, Army Rules that provide less advantageous procedures to delinquent officers are not violative of Articles 14 and 311 of the Constitution.
[Paras 31 and 32] C. Army Act, Sections 19 and 45 - Army Rules, Rule 14 - The removal from service under Section 19 read with Rule 14 without a Court Martial cannot be said to derogate from Section 45 of the Army Act, as the two are mutually exclusive.
