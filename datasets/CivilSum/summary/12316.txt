Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Regularisation of services - Break in service - Regularisation of services of daily wage workers - Policy instructions dated 731993 and 3111996 of the Haryana Govt. - Proper justification must be provided to condone breaks in service on account of illness - If eligible under the instructions, the authorities cannot refuse regularisation of services - Respondents must consider petitioners' case for regularisation of services, ignoring the breaks due to illness.
