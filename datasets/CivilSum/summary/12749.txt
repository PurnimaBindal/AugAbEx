Constitution of India, Article 226 - Family Pension - The petitioner's eligibility for two family pensions, one from Govt. of India and another from the office of National Fertilizer Ltd - The petitioner's husband retired from the Army Service after serving a specific period, and later joined service in National Fertilizer Ltd and was eligible for the benefit of family pension based on the rules of National Fertilizer Ltd - According to the rules, an employee of National Fertilizer Ltd receives family pension - The court held that the petitioner is entitled to receive family pension from both departments because the source of employment is different.
