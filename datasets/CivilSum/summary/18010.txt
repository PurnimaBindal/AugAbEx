Constitution of India, Article 311 - Dismissal from service/enquiry/Natural justice - A Punjab State Warehousing Corporation employee was dismissed from service on allegations of defalcation of material from the godown.
A departmental enquiry was conducted, but no evidence was recorded, and the delinquent was not associated in the enquiry.
The conclusions were based on a police report and presumptions, but guilt needs to be proved by positive evidence.
The whole proceedings are tainted, and the dismissal order is nullified.
The petitioner is entitled to all the benefits of salary and service.
