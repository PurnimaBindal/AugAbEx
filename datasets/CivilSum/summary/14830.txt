According to Section 2(n) of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, daily wage workers are entitled to compensation.
However, there was a contention that employment should not have been considered casual.
It was ultimately held that since the Respondent was employed for four years, the employment cannot be defined as casual under the Act.
Consequently, the workman is entitled to compensation.
