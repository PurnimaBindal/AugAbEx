Warehousing Corporations Act, 1962, Section 42 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Central Warehousing Corporation (Staff) Regulations (1986), Regulation 39 (ii) - Removal from Service - Respondent claims discrimination because disciplinary authority removed them while only imposing punishment of reversion on similarly situated employees - Single Judge substituted punishment by reversion - However, different orders in cases of similarly situated employees are not sufficient grounds for issuing a writ on the basis of discrimination - The legality and validity of the order in other cases must be investigated before it is imposed on the respondent - Therefore, the Single Judge's findings are not sustainable and the petition is dismissed.
