Constitution of India, Article 226 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(j) - Compulsory retirement - Jurisdiction - Rule 56(j) allows competent authority to assess the complete performance of a Government official who has reached the age of fifty years.
If it is determined that allowing the person to continue beyond the age of fifty would harm the interests of the Department, then the authority can retire the person at the age of fifty.
Before taking such a drastic measure, the authority must have sufficient material on record.
The authority's discretion should be exercised prudently and not based on stray observations about the person's character and conduct.
The entire performance and material on record must be examined before deciding to retire a person compulsorily.
The authority must also consider reverting the person to the post from which they were promoted, with the necessary consent, and allowing the person to continue in the reverted position until the age of fifty-eight, if the person chooses to do so.
