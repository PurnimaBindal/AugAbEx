Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8 - Termination - Double jeopardy - When a delinquent is punished twice with different punishments for the same charge, it is considered double jeopardy, and the subsequent punishment order cannot be upheld.
[Paras 6 and 7]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reinstatement - Back wages - If an employee is found to have remained in gainful employment during their termination period, they may only receive continuity of service with back wages to the extent of the difference between their entitled wages and what they earned during their termination period.
