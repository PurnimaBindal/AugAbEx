Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311(2)(b) - Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 7(2)(b) - Removal - Discrimination - Petitioners, who were staff nurses, engaged in an illegal strike despite being debarred from doing so by notification - Even though other employees also participated, the petitioners did not report for duty when given the opportunity - The punishment of removal from service against the petitioners is valid, and cannot be said to be discriminatory even if no action was taken against others.
