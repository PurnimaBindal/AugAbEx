Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Compassionate appointment - Central Bank of India, Compassionate Appointment and Financial Assistance Scheme for the dependents of its employees dying in harness - Assessment of financial position and assets of the family - Officials cannot be selective in disclosing the assets of one family while ignoring those of another – it is known that Heads of Departments in government offices and Nationalized Banks are aware of the properties of their employees.
[Para 10]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 – Compassionate appointment – Central Bank of India, Compassionate Appointment and Financial Assistance Scheme for the dependents of its employees dying in harness – Assessment of financial position and assets of the family – Officials cannot discriminate in providing compassionate appointment based on changes to the scheme or the financial position of the family – if one family with more income can receive relief of compassionate appointment while another with very limited income has their application pending for a long time, the latter cannot be denied relief, especially on the ground of delay – petition granted with costs of Rs. 10,000/-.
