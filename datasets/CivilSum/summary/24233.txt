The Employees Pension Scheme, 1995, under the Employees' Provident Funds & Miscellaneous Provisions (Amendment) Act, 1996, was implemented on November 16, 1995, and its validity and scope cannot be reviewed judicially.
Once the government has been empowered to create an appropriate pension scheme and has exercised that power, the court will not review the statute or the scheme.
All members and their dependent family members are entitled to either pensionary or withdrawal benefits under the scheme, and there is no inconsistency in membership position.
In addition, it is incorrect to say that the government's contribution is notional - it is an investment and earns interest in accordance with statutory provisions.
The soundness of the scheme is not threatened by fluctuations in the interest rate, as the act takes care of such fluctuations, and there is no risk of the scheme failing due to this.
(Paras 16, 17, 21-28)
