Constitution of India, Article 226 - Madhya Pradesh Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1966, Rule 18 - Disciplinary proceedings - Joint trial - A member of the police force was subjected to a joint trial with another delinquent, with no prior sanction from the competent authority - It was held that joint inquiry violates the principles of natural justice - The punishment awarded as a result of the joint trial was invalidated.
