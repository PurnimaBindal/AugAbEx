Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Pension Regulations for the Army, 1961, Rule 173 - Entitlement Rules for Casual Pensionary Awards, 1982, Rule 12(d) - Disability pension - The question whether petitioner would be eligible for disability pension after suffering injuries while on his way from his duty station to his leave station on casual leave, is discussed.
The court found a close connection between the journey and the injury.
Medical Board at MH was directed to re-examine petitioner to determine his level of disability and release his disability pension accordingly.
