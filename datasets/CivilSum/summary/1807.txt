According to the Freedom of Information Act, 2003, Sections 3 and 4, and Article 19(1)(A) of the Constitution of India, and Section 123 of the Evidence Act, 1872, an individual has the right to information.
In a case where a petitioner seeks a copy of an appointment letter from a University to challenge an appointment, the University cannot refuse to provide the letter.
The authorities are obligated to provide the document if it has a nexus with a judicial remedy in accordance with the law.
Denying the document for the purpose of judicial review would be frustrating an individual's constitutional rights.
