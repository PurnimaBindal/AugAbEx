Constitution of India, Article 14 - Syndicate Bank Officers Employees' (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976 - Departmental proceedings - Termination - Natural justice - Competent authority has discretion to either supply copies of documents or allow inspection of record - Proceeding cannot be considered invalid merely due to non-supply of an irrelevant document without impacting the delinquent's rights.

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Syndicate Bank Officers Employees' (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976 - Departmental proceedings - Admission of charges - Parties are allowed to produce relevant evidence and examine any witnesses - Delinquent's admission or lack of dispute removes the necessity for evidence in reply to show cause/charge-sheet.
