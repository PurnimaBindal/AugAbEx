According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, as well as Sections 10 and 11 of the Hindu Adoptions and Maintenance Act, 1956, an adopted son is entitled to compassionate appointment.
Rejecting an adopted son's eligibility on this basis is improper.
An adopted son is entitled to compassionate appointment if he is otherwise eligible and is considered the son of the adoptive parents under Section 12 of the Act.
The GO's requirement that the adoption should have taken place at least five years prior to the employee's death is unsustainable.
Although the adoption deed states that the adoption had taken place seven years prior, there is no evidence to substantiate that claim.
As the petitioner's status is in dispute, it is not a question that can be adjudicated in a writ petition and must be resolved by a Civil Court.
Therefore, the writ petition is not maintainable and should be dismissed.
