Central Civil Service (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, Rule 5(1) - Temporary appointee - Termination - Concealment of criminal case - The appointment letter stated that service would be terminated in case of adverse verification of character and antecedents - The petitioner cannot argue that acquittal in a criminal case exempts him from termination, as he accepted this condition in the appointment letter and completed training - Additionally, the verification of antecedents clause was upheld in previous cases.
[Paras 10, 15-19]
