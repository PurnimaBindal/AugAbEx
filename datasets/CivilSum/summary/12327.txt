Mizoram Public Works Department (Group A Posts) Recruitment Rules, 1975, Rule 1 - Eligibility for promotion to the post of Superintending Engineer is a period of five years experience as Executive Engineer.
Petitioner and respondents 6 and 7 have worked as Engineers under PHE Department in the combined PWD/PHE Department.
The period of five years experience should be counted from the date when the Engineers concerned were permanently allocated to either of the departments.
Neither respondents No. 6 and 7 nor the petitioner acquired the eligibility criteria of five years in PHE Department when respondents No. 6 and 7 were promoted.
Therefore, the promotion of respondents No. 6 and 7 cannot be interfered.
[Paras 5 to 8]

Promotion - Eligibility - Discrimination - Respondents were promoted without having eligibility criteria.
The petitioner who had not completed the minimum qualifying service cannot claim discrimination merely because two of his seniors were promoted without having the required qualification.
The promotion of the respondents is evidently wrong, and therefore, the court cannot direct the Government to pass another wrong order to promote the petitioner.
[Para 11]

Promotion - It cannot be claimed as a matter of right.
It is the responsibility of the appropriate authority to consider and promote an officer to the next higher grade as per administrative necessity.
The petitioner has no right to enforce in a court of law to give him promotion with effect from the date he had acquired eligibility for consideration for promotion.
[Para 10]
