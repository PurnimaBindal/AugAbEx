Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 4A - Claim Petition - Limitation for amendment of claim petition - The legal heirs may still file an amended claim petition even if the original claimant has passed away during the pendency of the case.
As long as the original claim was already filed, the amended claim is within the limit.
[Para 3] B. Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 4A and 10B - Compensation - Penalty - Liability of insurance company to pay penalty - If the employer should take fault for the Insurance Company's inability to pay compensation on time, the Insurance Company is not responsible for paying the penalty for the delay in payment.
