Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) & 33-A - Approval of the Court - Workers are protected from mala fide action by their employers during legal proceedings - Employers cannot terminate employment during a civil suit without prior approval - A complaint under Section 33-A can be made by the worker.
[Para 10]

Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - High Court has jurisdiction over Tribunal orders - If the Tribunal's decision causes injustice and is not based on merit, the High Court can interfere in the interest of justice.
