According to Constitution of India, Articles 226/227, a petitioner was granted disability pension and service element after being released from Navy Service upon completion of contractual period of 10 years.
However, it was later discovered that the petitioner was not entitled to the service element benefit as he was not discharged due to medical reasons, but rather due to the end of the contractual period.
Therefore, the authorities rightfully issued an order for the recovery of the said amount.
Every pension order is subject to calculations, thus the pension order itself does not create estoppel against the decision made by the authorities.
[Paras 7 and 8]
