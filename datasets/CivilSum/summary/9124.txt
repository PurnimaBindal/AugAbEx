Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Reversion - An order of reversion found to be incorrect is to be set aside entirely and not partially or temporarily.
[Para 5] B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Delay and laches - Dismissing a petition due to delays and laches is a procedural rule and not a legal one.
If there is a valid reason for the delay, the illegal action cannot be upheld by citing laches.
If an employee has made repeated appeals without receiving a decision, they cannot be dismissed on the basis of laches alone.
