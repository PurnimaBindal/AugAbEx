The petitioner was appointed as Professor of Pathology on a regular basis with approval from HPSC.
Per an agreement between the University and the Government, those appointed on a regular basis with HPSC approval do not need to be regularized again.
However, in 1980, the Government of Haryana took over the College from the University and the petitioner was appointed on an ad hoc basis.
This appointment is bad in law and the petitioner should be considered a regular appointee to the post of Professor of Pathology from June 6, 1975.
[Para 3] Note that the petitioner could not be ordered to work on an ad hoc basis after being appointed on a regular basis with HPSC approval.
The post of Principal can either be filled through promotion or direct recruitment and the government has not yet decided which method to use.
The petitioner is currently acting as the Principal, but qualifications for the position will only arise when the post is filled on a regular basis.
It is not appropriate to direct the respondents to appoint him as the Principal of the college at this time.
The relief claimed by the petitioner is premature.
