The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 33(2), 33-A, 33(2)(b) and 31 - Violation of the provisions of Sections 33(2) and 33(2)(b) will entitle the workman to make a complaint to the authority before the dispute was pending, making the management liable to punishment.
While the complaint needs to be adjudicated as if the matter was referred to the authority for adjudication by following the procedure to decide a reference, if the action of the management is found in violation of the provisions of Section 33, it may make it liable to punishment but not the workman entitled to reinstatement ipso-facto.
[Paras 12 to 19]

If after adjudication of the complaint the workman is found guilty of tampering with the official record with the object of causing loss to the employer, such a workman cannot be imposed upon the employer in exercise of power under Article 226 of the Constitution.
[Para 20]

If the management is able to locate some important evidence after the enquiry report is submitted, the competent authority can ask the enquiry officer to look into it and give an opportunity to the employee concerned.
