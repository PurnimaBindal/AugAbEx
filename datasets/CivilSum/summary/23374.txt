Constitution of India, Article 311(2) (as amended by 42nd Amendment) - Disciplinary proceedings - An employee has the right to receive the report of the Inquiry Officer, even if the punishment imposed is other than dismissal, removal, or reduction in rank - Service rules that require an inquiry before punishment is awarded give the employee the right to receive the report, even if the disciplinary authority is not the Inquiry Officer - Denial of the report is a breach of natural justice - Court or tribunal should not automatically set aside the order of punishment if the report was not furnished - Right to make representation against findings in the report is essential to defense against charges - Prospective in operation from November 20, 1990.
[Paras 30, 31, and 33-34]
