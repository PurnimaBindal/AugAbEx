Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Haryana State Electricity Board employees (Punishment and Appeal) Regulations, 1990, Regulation 7(6) - Validity of Regln, 7(6) - Termination of services - Natural justice - The conclusion that second show cause notice with proposed punishment is necessary, as per Ramesh Kumar v. State of Haryana, contradicts the principles outlined in Mohd.
Ramzan's case and Karunakar's case - The regulations do not prevent the delinquent from receiving a copy of the inquiry report and submitting representation - The principles of natural justice require the punishing authority only communicate the inquiry report with the second show cause notice when proposing punishment - The matter referred to a Larger Bench.
