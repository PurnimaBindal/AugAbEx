According to Section 4 of UP Public Services (Reservation in favour of the Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe and other Backward Classes) Act, 1994, the percentage of reservation is only applicable to individual posts.
Clubbing together various posts under the general category of Group A or Group B, even if they have different responsibilities and pay scales, is considered illegal.
Instead, each category falling under Group A or Group B should be individually isolated and the reservation formula should be applied accordingly.
[Paras 11, 12 and 15]
