According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, if there is a dispute regarding promotion between two workers, it can be referred to an industrial tribunal.
A reference can result in the quashing of a promotion for a worker who was not a party to the proceedings.
However, if this occurs, the award can be set aside and the matter can be remanded back to the tribunal for a fresh decision after impleading the affected worker and hearing all parties involved.
