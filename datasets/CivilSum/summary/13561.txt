Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 311 - Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, Rule 49-A(2) - Suspension/Automatic suspension on arrest - The provision that automatically suspends an employee if they are arrested on a criminal charge and detained for over 48 hours is ultra vires Articles 14 and 21 of the Constitution - There may be circumstances out of the employee's control that prevent them from obtaining bail within two days of arrest - The rule has been deemed null and void.
