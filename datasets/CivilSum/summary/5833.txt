The SSSB of Haryana cannot select more candidates than the number of available posts in the advertisement, but a small percentage of candidates may be kept on a waiting list in case some candidates are unsuitable or decline the offer.
No large deviation from the advertised number of posts is allowed.
[Paras 8 and 19]
Even if the Subordinate Services Selection Board prepared a merit list with more candidates than posts, those selected have a right to representation.
A writ petition cannot be dismissed for not including all necessary parties.
[Para 13]
