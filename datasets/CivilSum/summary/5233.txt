According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(1) - Model Standing Orders, Clause 32 - Pending application for approval of dismissal of an employee - During the pendency of approval, the management has the right to suspend the employee but cannot deny him the subsistence allowance - If subsistence allowance denied, it will be considered as violation of natural justice - In Fakirbai Fulabai Solanki v. Presiding Officer and another AIR 1986 SC 1168 and Ram Lakhan v. Presiding Officer and others 2002(2) SLR 177, it was held that denial of subsistence allowance is a violation of natural justice - Par.
