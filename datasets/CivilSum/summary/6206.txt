Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination - Reinstatement - Back wages - Although the Labour Court reinstated a workman with continuity of service, they denied him back wages due to the letter sent by Employment Exchange informing the employer that the name of the petitioner was wrongly sent.
However, the order of the Labour Court denying bank wages is in contrast to the judgment laid down by the High Court in Hari Palace, Ambala City v. Presiding Officer and anr., 1979 PLR 720 followed in Kanwar Rohit v. The Presiding Officer, 1992(2) RSJ 425.
Therefore, in this case, the petitioner is entitled to back wages according to the order.
