As per Constitution of India, dismissal of a judicial officer charged with taking bribe cannot be disbelieved in writ jurisdiction if the charge has been proven after enquiry and opportunity given in response to a complaint.
Article 226 may be invoked by the High Court if prescribed procedure of enquiry is not followed or principles of natural justice are violated.
Court may interfere if conclusion is based on no evidence or is arbitrary or capricious, but adequacy or reliability of evidence cannot be questioned.
Court cannot act as an appeal court.
[Para 6] B.
A show cause notice was issued to a judicial officer charged with taking bribe, proposing penalty of dismissal, and the officer only appealed for consideration on humanitarian grounds.
The High Court's recommendation of dismissal to the Governor for approval is not vitiated for want of a copy furnished to the officer.
The officer failed to prove that the decision is arbitrary or capricious and no interference is called for.
