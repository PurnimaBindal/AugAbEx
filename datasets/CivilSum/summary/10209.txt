Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 2, Rule 2 - Termination - Suit for recovery - The terminated employee cannot claim reinstatement or a declaration of entitlement to reinstatement if the termination order is held null and void - Such a claim is barred under Order 2, Rule 2 - Even a fresh suit claiming such declaration would not be allowed.
[Para 2]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and Laches - A suit was decreed on 1.3.1979, declaring the termination null and void - However, a writ filed for reinstatement in Sept 1983 was highly belated and clearly barred.
