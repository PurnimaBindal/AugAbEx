Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Domestic enquiry - Failure to provide enquiry report - Whether it will invalidate the enquiry or punishment order - Decided that High Court must confirm if the employee was adversely affected before taking action - The court is then required to give the employee a copy of the report and a chance to demonstrate how their case was harmed due to the non-disclosure of the report - If an employee had an opportunity to contest the report before the appellate authority, and the authority upheld the punishment order, it cannot be said that any prejudice was caused.
