Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 227 - Dismissal - Ground of embezzlement - Petitioner claiming that enquiry was not in accordance with rules of natural justice - Petitioner admitting fairness of enquiry in civil suit - Held, Petitioner cannot demand equal treatment to co-accused who did not admit to fairness of enquiry - Additionally, petitioner's admission in civil suit overrides claim of improper enquiry.
