The Constitution of India in Articles 14, 16, and 21 - Medical reimbursement - Husband of petitioner suffers from chronic renal disease and receives indoor treatment from a nearest Hospital of Specialities - Authority pays for indoor treatment, but refuses reimbursement for outdoor treatment due to hospital not being recognized - Such action is held to be completely arbitrary, unreasonable, and unsustainable in the eyes of the law.

The Constitution of India in Articles 14, 16, and 21 - Medical reimbursement - Husband of petitioner suffers from chronic renal disease and receives emergency treatment from a nearest Hospital of Specialities - Authorities refuse reimbursement due to hospital not being recognized - However, it is held that reimbursement cannot be denied in cases of emergency, if treatment is taken from an institution not recognized by Govt, and should be paid at least at the AIMS/PGIMER rates - Life of a person cannot be valued in monetary terms.
