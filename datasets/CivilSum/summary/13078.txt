The Bachelor of Computer Application course in question is governed by Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India.
The petitioner was initially deemed ineligible during his second year of study due to failing his 10+2 examination.
However, he had been admitted based on his results as published in the Gazette, before receiving the failing mark sheet.
The College authorities were promptly informed of the discrepancy, and forwarded the mark sheet to the university, who did not raise any objection.
The court provisionally allowed the Petitioner to take the examination and declare the result, and it is held that this permission deserves to be regularized.
The respondents are estopped from questioning the Petitioner's eligibility to be admitted in BCA Part II, having previously allowed him to pursue his studies in the course.
