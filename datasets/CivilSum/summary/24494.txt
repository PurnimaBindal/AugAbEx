Constitution of India, Articles 14, 229 and 339 - Executive instructions - Rules established under the proviso of Article 309 of the Constitution may apply to High Court employees, but executive instructions do not apply when they are inconsistent with the Rules created by the Chief Justice of the High Court in accordance with Article 229.
[Para 12] C. Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection - Wrong Provision of Law - Superior Court is only concerned with whether the authority followed the correct jurisdiction and not if the wrong provision of law was used.
[Para 16] D.Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection - Criteria of selection - The promotion to Section Officer from Assistant is selected on merit - Candidates are prohibited from challenging the criteria after the fact - As long as the criteria are based on relevant considerations such as seniority, integrity, and devotion to duty, it will not be deemed illegal.
