Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection - Promotion - In order to pursue writ jurisdiction under Article 226 of the Constitution, individuals must demonstrate an infringement of their legal right - without the existence of a legal right, there can be no infringement of the right to promotion or consideration for promotion.
[Para 31]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - All India Institute of Medical Sciences Rules, 1958, Rule 7 - All India Institute Promotion from Assistant Professor to Professor - The decision of the Institute to provide lateral entry at Level II and Level III does not deprive a faculty member of their promotion to a higher level post because they will carry their lower post with them - By adopting the APS Scheme, the Institute shortened but did not abolish its ability to make direct recruitment to the faculty posts - The Institute chose to take recourse to lateral entry at higher levels of Associate Professor and Additional Professor only in exceptional circumstances - the right is only for consideration for promotion, so as long as that right exists, a member of the service cannot be aggrieved or have any locus standi to challenge the Scheme.
[Paras 27, 41, 43, and 57]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Judicial review - Writ jurisdiction - Administrative decision - The Court can only review the decision-making process and not the merits of the decision itself, except when the decision is vitiated by arbitrariness, unfairness, illegality, irrationality, or unreasonableness.
[Paras 52 and 53]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Writ jurisdiction - News items - Administrative decisions - The authenticity of newspaper reporting of the proceedings of the Governing Body of the Institute cannot be trusted - The employees of the institute cannot question the veracity of the decision taken.
[Para 79]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Writ - Maintainability - An administrative decision that is not final cannot be considered in contravention of any provisions of Rules, Regulations, or the Act.
[Para 78]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Open Global Selection - Selection of only those already in service of the institution - The fact that nobody from outside the country has applied doesn't mean selection was arbitrary or predetermined - colleagues in a small cadre can assess and estimate who will secure the highest merit - Unless some material or evidence is placed on record, the Court cannot interfere with the decision of the high-level Academic Committee not interested in any particular candidate.
[Paras 83 to 85]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Election Commission's moratorium - The promotions or direct recruitments of faculty members are not a policy matter of any State or Central Government that may be affected by the announcement of general elections by the Election Commission.
