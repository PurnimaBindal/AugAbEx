The High Court was wrong in awarding selection grade pay to all Delhi Higher Judicial Service officers based on the principle of equal pay for equal work.
The service conditions of Higher Judicial Services in each respective state demand a distinction between different officer grades.
The application of the equal pay doctrine would destabilize this vital distinction illegally, illogically, and inappropriately.
