The State Government's decision to withdraw the benefit of annual increment in time-scale of pay to subject teachers appointed on ad hoc basis is illegal and goes against the Fundamental Rules.
Ad hoc teachers who have been in service for less than a year are entitled to the benefit of annual increment as per FR 24 and 26.
[Paras 6, 7, 8 and 9] B Ad hoc appointees who were not selected through a proper selection process have no right to claim regularisation.
Only selected appointees are eligible for consideration for regularisation.
