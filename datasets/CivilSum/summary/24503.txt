Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Uttar Pradesh State University Act, Section 37(2) - Affiliation of Medical Colleges - Though it may not be appropriate, medical colleges that have repeatedly been granted temporary affiliation to the University on the basis of having infrastructure up to the required standard, and where admissions and exams are conducted under interim orders of the court, should not have their students deprived of consequential degrees upon course completion in the particular case's circumstances.
While the court cannot demand the University to grant affiliation, it can instruct it to consider possible affiliation.
