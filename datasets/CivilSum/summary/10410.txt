According to Rule 3 of Part III in Kerala Service Rules, Death-cum-retirement gratuity can be withheld.
However, in the case of an appellant who suffered a loss during a motor accident involving a vehicle owned by the Corporation, no liability can be determined without disciplinary proceedings.
The Corporation has a duty to quantify the amount withheld and inform the pensioner with an opportunity to explain themselves.
In this case, the Corporation failed to follow the proper procedure and cannot recover the amount from the appellant.
