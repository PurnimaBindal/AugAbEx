Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(c)(2) - Special allowance - Civil Court authority to entertain a suit for appellant's denied allowance - Defendant claims allowance is not payable under Chapter 5-A of the Act - Defendant attempts to obstruct claim with reference to disciplinary proceedings - If disciplinary proceedings are void for violating natural justice principles, Civil Court can nullify the order and provide plaintiff with consequential relief - Civil Court neglected to address jurisdiction issue, thus allowing suit to be maintainable - Judgment and decree of lower courts nullified - Trial Court must frame issues according to law and revisit the matter.
