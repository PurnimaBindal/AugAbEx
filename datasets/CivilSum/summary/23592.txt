Constitution of India, Article 226 - Speaking order - High Court dismissed the Writ Petition with only one word 'Dismissed' - The High Court is obligated to provide reasons upon dismissing a writ petition - A speaking order must be passed so that the petitioners can understand the grounds for which their petition was dismissed - The case has been sent back to the High Court to be resolved, with the opportunity for all parties involved to present their case, and for the Court to give a speaking order regarding the merits of the case.
