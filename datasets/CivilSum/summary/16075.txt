Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Income Tax Department (Inspectors) Recruitment Rules, 1969 - Promotion/Instructions - The Rotation of Posts between direct recruits and promotees - There is no procedure in the rules to govern seniority - The instructions of the government will be binding for the seniority determination in the absence of specific rules - Any change to the seniority determined in accordance with the instructions cannot be made at a later stage.
