Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 215 - Binding precedents - Necessary parties - Interpretation of Rules - If affected parties were not impleaded and a decision was made with a wrong interpretation of rules, it cannot be considered a binding precedent simply because no appeal was filed against it and it was allowed to become final - Affected persons who were not a party to the decision still have the right to dispute it and the Supreme Court can investigate its correctness under Article 141.

Haryana Technical Education Department (Group A) Service Rules, 1986, Rule 9(1)(c)(i) - Haryana Technical Education Department (Group A) Service (Amendment) Rules, 2001, Rule 2 - Promotion to the post of Principal on non-Engineering side of Polytechnics/Technical Institutions - Eligibility for - According to Rule 9(1)(c), the recruitment of the Principal of an institute offering diplomas in engineering trades can only take place through the promotion from Heads of Departments in Engineering - English teachers promoted as Applied Science's Heads of Departments are not eligible as English has nothing to do with Engineering knowledge and it is not included in the department's hierarchy - However, after the 2001 amendment, Applied Science has been classified as a non-Engineering side, and therefore, the Head of the Department of Applied Science is eligible for consideration to the post of Principal.
