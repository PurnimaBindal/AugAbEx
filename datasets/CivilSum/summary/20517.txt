Article 14 of the Indian Constitution relates to selection through interview, where the number of applicants is high and it is not feasible for the Board to interview each candidate.
In such cases, the selection board may limit the number of candidates to a reasonable number based on higher qualifications and relative merit in the qualifying examination.
[Para 9] B Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India are related to the selection process and the jurisdiction of the court in considering merit.
The court is not responsible for replacing its view with the opinion of experts in the field.
Instead, the court has to ensure that candidates are given adequate consideration and a complete opportunity to contest.
If more suitable and meritorious candidates with higher qualifications are selected for the teaching position, there is no room for complaint or interference by the court.
