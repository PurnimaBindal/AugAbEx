Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I, Part I, Rule 8.116(iii) - Petitioner's claim for leave preparatory to retirement for 180 days on full pay was rejected, as per instructions it was only admissible when petitioner was to superannuate from service.
However, as per Rule 8.116 of the Rules, the petitioner is entitled to this relief.
The court held that if an employee is entitled to a relief by statutory rule, it cannot be denied to them based on instructions that replace the rule.
The court directed the respondents to allow the petitioner to have leave preparatory to retirement for 180 days on full pay as per Rule 8.116(iii) of the Rules.
