According to Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 239 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 39 - Industrial Disputes Rules, 1957, Rule 2(f), even if the Central Government is the appropriate government in relation to a dispute arising within the jurisdiction of NCT, the State Government can also take action in accordance with Rule 2(f) of the Rules.
Furthermore, a petition challenging a reference filed after two years cannot be entertained on the ground of delay.
[Paras 24 and 27]

In relation to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F, the termination of a workman must comply with the mandatory provisions of the Act, as set out in Section 25-F otherwise it will be deemed bad in law.
[Para 25]
