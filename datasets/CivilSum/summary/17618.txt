Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Nomination - Engineering Course - Admission - The petitioner was recommended for nomination to a course leading to the degree of Bachelor of Pharmacy in Maharashtra but was not admitted to an Engineering Course due to prior admission in a Haryana Engineering College against a paid seat - However, as the petitioner withdrew from the Engineering College, the disqualification no longer exists - The petitioner is the only eligible candidate from Gujarat and the seat would be wasted if not nominated - The Respondents are directed to consider the petitioner's claim for nomination in the State of Gujarat.
