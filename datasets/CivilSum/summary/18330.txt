The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226, and the Rajasthan Non- Government Educational Institutions Act, 1989, Sections 18 and 21, deal with the selection process.
The institute appointed two part-time teachers who also appeared in the interview for the regular basis selection.
However, two other meritorious candidates were chosen for the job and the respondents 2 & 3 were not eligible for the protection under Section 18 of the Rajasthan Act as they had participated in the selection.
