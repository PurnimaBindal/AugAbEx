Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Departmental Enquiry - Legal Assistance - Precedents - While being a 'trained personnel' doesn't automatically entitle a delinquent officer to legal assistance in departmental proceedings, the right to such assistance depends on the individual facts and circumstances of the case.
If the case presents factual and legal complexity that hinders the delinquent officer's ability to defend themselves and they are not academically or psychologically fit to do so on their own, they are entitled to legal assistance.
The ratio of judgments should not be blindly applied, as each case has its own distinct entity and relying solely on reported decisions can lead to unjust outcomes.
