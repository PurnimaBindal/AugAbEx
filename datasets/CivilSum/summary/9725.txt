Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Section 124 - Jammu and Kashmir Subordinate Services Recruitment Rules, 1992, Rules 4 and 5 - Tenure of the member of the Board - The petitioner, who is a member of Administrative Service, has been transferred and posted as a member of Service Selection Board - According to the prescribed two-year tenure, the incumbent can only be transferred or removed - Amendment allows the curtailment of tenure if the incumbent resigns or retires - Since the amendment is prospective, the petitioner's tenure of two years can be curtailed by transferring or removing him from office - The petitioner has the right to continue as a member of the Board for two years despite his superannuation as a Government employee.
