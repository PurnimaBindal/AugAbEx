The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.-II Rule 532A(c) and Rule 3.26(d) of Vol-I - Premature compulsory retirement - The Supreme Court has outlined principles for when a government servant can be compulsorily retired prematurely.
This may occur when they are no longer useful and have outlived their efficiency.
Alternatively, if there is such an adverse entry in their Annual Confidential Reports (ACRs), it may not be in the public interest to allow them to continue in public service, so the competent authority may exercise its discretion.
It is important to note that the Order of premature retirement cannot be treated or passed as a measure of punishment.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.-II Rule 532A(c) and Rule 3.26(d) of Vol-I - Premature compulsory retirement - While considering ACRs, if there is a vigilance enquiry pending, the last ACRs of the employee may not be recorded.
However, once they have been exonerated of the allegations, the ACRs must be recorded and the action taken based on them must be reconsidered in the light of the exoneration.
