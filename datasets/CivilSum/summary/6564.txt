According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, a dependent of a deceased employee can be employed on compassionate grounds for a post that is at least one step lower than the post held by the deceased employee.
If there is a post available, the petitioner has the right to be considered for appointment as an Inspector of Police.
Despite the respondents' claim of non-availability of posts, there are currently four vacant Inspector positions.
The respondents' reasoning for not considering the petitioner is entirely unjustifiable.
Therefore, the court instructs the respondents to review the case and consider appointing the petitioner to the Inspector position.
