The termination of the daily wager clerk by the Director of Lotteries Punjab without notice or compensation after about six years was deemed to be a violation of Section 25-F under the Industrial Dispute Act by both the Labour Court and High Court based on examination of evidence.
[Para 7]
The Directorate of Punjab State Lotteries, which involves systematic activity and profits, is considered an industry under the Industrial Dispute Act.
[Para 7]
After the termination of workmen, at least 10 persons were employed on a daily wage basis for the same job without any communication from the department to the petitioner, which constitutes a clear violation of Section 25H under the Industrial Dispute Act.
The Labour Court's award on the matter stands.
[Para 12]
Due to a delayed reference, the Labour Court limited back wages payment to only 70%, a discretion that was deemed reasonable in the circumstances and thus not interfered with.
