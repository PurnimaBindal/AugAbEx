Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 16 - Allegations of outraging the modesty of a lady and snatching her golden chain and then causing injuries by knife to her husband are of criminal nature - In the absence of the complainant lady and her husband's statement to support the allegations, they cannot be considered proved, especially when they did not support them even in the criminal proceedings that resulted in the accused's acquittal.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 16 - Quantum of punishment - Allegations of absence from duty and outraging the modesty of a lady and snatching her golden chain - The punishment of stoppage of two annual increments with cumulative effect cannot be considered proportionate with the gravity of misconduct since the allegations of outraging the modesty of a lady and snatching her golden chain were not proved - A punishment of censure would suffice to meet the ends of justice.
