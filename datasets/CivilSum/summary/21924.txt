MD University Calender, Volume-III Leave Regulations, Clause 26(ii)(a) to (c) - Extra-ordinary Leave without pay - Increments for the period of Extra-ordinary Leave without pay - To receive increments for the period of Extra-ordinary Leave without pay, an employee must fulfill the conditions of 'invitation' and only apply through their employer to the specified posts or departments.
Independent applications for joining a different department or post will not earn increments for that service.
[Paras 16 & 17]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Maharishi Dayanand University Act, 1975 - MD University Calender, Volume-III Leave Regulations, Clause 26(ii)(a) to (c) - Rule making authority - Delegated legislation - Retrospective operation of Rules - Rules cannot have a retrospective effect that takes away already accrued rights of employees without a specific provision in the Act.
[Paras 13 & 14]

Constitution of India, Article 14 - Discrimination - Article 14 includes a positive concept and cannot be used to perpetuate illegality.
