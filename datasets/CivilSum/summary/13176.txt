Constitution of India, Article 226 and 227 - Writ Jurisdiction - The petitioner requested a conversion of 77 days absence as a period on special duty.
The petitioner was assisting delinquent employees in Departmental Proceedings conducted by Respondent Company, and on certain occasions, was permitted by the respondent itself to treat the period spent on attending enquiries as on special duty.
Although the petitioner has no legal or statutory right, it is a dispute of the nature of an employer-employee.
Therefore, the petitioner should make use of his justifiable and legitimate defence as a defence in the inquiry itself.
The court should not stop domestic inquiry or interfere by exercising its discretionary jurisdiction.
The petition was dismissed being misconceived.
[Paras 4 to 8]
