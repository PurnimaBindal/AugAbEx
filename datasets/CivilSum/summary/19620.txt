According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, male social workers in the Welfare Department have the option to be promoted as Tehsil Welfare Officer or Assistant/Accountant given their clerical cadre position.
The petitioners had chosen to be promoted as Tehsil Welfare Officers, but also accepted promotions as Accountants/Assistants.
However, their promotion categorized them as no more Male Social Workers capable of being promoted as Tehsil Welfare Officers.
Thus, the Department sought to fill the Tehsil Welfare Officer positions through direct recruitment.
The petitioners cannot claim the right to these positions anymore after being promoted to different cadres.
