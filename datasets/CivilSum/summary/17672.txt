According to the Constitution of India, Article 14 and the Regional Rural Banks (Appointment and Promotion of Officers and Other Employees) Rules, 1988, Rule 5, promotion of Field Supervisors is based on both seniority and merit.
However, the petitioner's promotion was affected as he was punished and therefore not considered meritorious for three years.
While seniority is a consideration for promotion, a person's merit must also be evaluated.
One cannot claim promotion based on seniority alone as a matter of right.
