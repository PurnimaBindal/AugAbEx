Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Dispute - Reference - Scope of - The Industrial Tribunal's scope is limited to the terms of reference - A workman admitted to the charges in a charge-sheet - The Industrial Tribunal was to determine if the punishment of downgrading the workman was disproportionate - The Industrial Tribunal exceeded its jurisdiction by investigating the validity of the enquiry - The case was remanded back to the Industrial Tribunal with the direction to confine adjudication within the terms of reference.
