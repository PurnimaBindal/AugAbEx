Letters Patent Appeal, Clause X - Pension - Appointment as Vaid by Zila Parishad - The entire service of the appellant, including their previous service with Zila Parishad, should be taken into account in computing pensionary benefits.
Latest instructions have been issued in supersession of previous instructions.
The appellant is entitled to the benefit of previous service with Zila Parishad in calculating their pension.
It is directed that the pensionary benefits payable to the appellant be computed in accordance with the latest instructions.
[Paras 2 to 6]
