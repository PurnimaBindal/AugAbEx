Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Advertisement of eligibility conditions - Relaxation of - The selection process for public appointments should be fair and in accordance with the eligibility conditions specified.
Relaxation of eligibility conditions after the application cut-off date is not allowed.
Such appointments made by relaxing eligibility conditions are deemed fraudulent to the public and breaches Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution, as it denies the right to compete and consideration to those who found themselves ineligible but would have been eligible if the advertised eligibility conditions were those that came into effect on relaxation.
