In accordance with the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, juniors of the petitioner have been given ante-dated promotions without considering his case, due to the orders of the High Court.
As the cases of promotion of juniors of the petitioner were being considered, it was mandatory that the authority concerned also consider the petitioner's case, as he was senior to those whose cases were being reviewed.
[Paras 5, 6, 7 and 8]

As per the Constitution of India, Article 266, the promotion of the junior was made on September 18, 1990.
The petitioner's cause of action arose when his immediate junior was promoted without considering his case.
The petition was filed in 1990 and is not delayed.
[Para 4]
