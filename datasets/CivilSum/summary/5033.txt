Section 6 of the Evidence Act states that hearsay evidence is admissible in domestic enquiries provided it has a reasonable connection and credibility and is logically probative for a prudent mind.
Hearsay evidence, subject to qualifying (a) and (b), would be sufficient to support a finding in a domestic enquiry.
[Paras 52, 53, and 54]

In a case involving charges of sexual harassment, the complainant must be subjected to cross-examination if the defence of witness is that the complaints were motivated.
However, if the maker of the original complaint is unavailable or cannot be produced, hearsay evidence may be used and be considered good evidence.
[Paras 67 to 70]
