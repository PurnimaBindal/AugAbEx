Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 39(d) - Equal pay for equal work - Governing the pay scale of workers appointed by government and workers assigned by a corporation fully owned by the government on the same project - Making a workers appointed by government to receive a lower pay scale than the worker appointed by the corporation who are discharging similar types of work - is a direct violation of the equality clause in the Constitution of India - There is no flaw in the decision to revise the pay scale of lower paid workers.
