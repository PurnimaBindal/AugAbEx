Constitution of India, Article 226 - Review of answer scripts - Authenticity of certificate - Petitioner appeared as a private candidate for Madhyamik Examination in 1987 under the State Education Board but was unsuccessful.
He later applied for a review and was declared successful.
After pursuing further studies, he obtained a permanent Government job.
However, eight years later, a memo dated 21111995 declared him unsuccessful in the 1987 Examination and questioned the authenticity of his marks sheet, which was found to be genuine by forensic scrutiny.
As a result, the impugned memo dated 21111995 was canceled.
[Paras 8, 9 and 10]
