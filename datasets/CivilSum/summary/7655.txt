As per the Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 311 and Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221, the Superintendent of police has the authority to discharge any constable who is deemed unlikely to become an efficient police officer due to misconduct or absence from duty.
The termination process has two options: (a) punishment by major penalty according to appointment terms and conditions and rules for services with the use of established procedure under Rule 1624 and Article 311 of the Constitution of India, and (b) discharge simpliciter, where no punishment is imposed and only appointment rules are followed, without the need of following the procedure laid down by Rule 1624 or Article 311 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 14-18] B.
In cases of temporary appointment or probationary, termination of employees can happen at any time regardless of satisfactory work and conduct when deemed unlikely to become an efficient police officer due to misconduct or absence from duty.
