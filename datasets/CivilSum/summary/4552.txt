The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 governs appointments, including the approval process.
In this case, a group of teachers from a recognized school filed a writ application as their services had not been approved.
However, the application was dismissed after a report from the D.I.S.
indicated that fraud had been committed.
The teachers argued that they were being victimized by certain individuals in high positions, but the question of fraud can only be decided by a civil court.
The case was sent back to the Director of School Education for further consideration, and disciplinary action was to be taken against all responsible parties.
