Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appointment - The appellant was selected for a post but was not appointed due to an illegal decision by the screening committee.
The High Court reversed the illegal decision and this decision has been finalized.
The appellant has the right to be appointed to the post he was selected for, despite the panel expiring and the post being filled by someone else.
The employer's erroneous decision led to the usurpation of the post, and not due to any fault on the appellant's part.
The respondent is ordered to appoint the appellant to the post for which he was selected.
