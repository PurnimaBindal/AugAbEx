Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 - Transfer - Validity - Argument that promotee officers are not generally transferred as a matter of policy - Numerous examples of individuals being stationed in the same place for many years are cited - The court finds that the petitioner has not demonstrated bad faith and that he has only spent 17 months outside of Delhi during his 27-year career, and therefore there is no basis for interference - Additionally, the petitioner cannot claim a right based on the examples cited.
[Paras 11 and 16]
