Swatantrata Sainik Samman Pension Scheme, 1980 - Pension for Freedom Fighters - The Scheme offers pension only to legitimate freedom fighters - Individuals who did not contribute to the freedom struggle are not eligible for the benefits of the Scheme - The benefit will not be granted unless all the fundamental criteria and prerequisites are met - If necessary documents are not complete, the applicant may reapply.
