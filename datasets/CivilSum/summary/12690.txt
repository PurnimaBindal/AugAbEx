Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 234 - Rajasthan Judicial Services (Recruitment) Rules, 1955, Rule 2 - Judicial Service Examination - A candidate who was found with torn pages on his answer sheet was debarred from future selections for 2 years, but the petitioner did not make any wrongful gain or act fraudulently.
As alleged malpractice was not detected in the examination hall, he could not be held liable.
The order cannot sustain because RPSC acted on the basis of probabilities without complying with the principles of natural justice or holding any inquiry.
