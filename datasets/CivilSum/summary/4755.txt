Service Matter - Enquiry - Dismissal - A department enquiry was conducted against the plaintiff, a Police Constable, on allegations that he consumed alcohol with another police constable at a hotel, broke utensils and misbehaved with the owner.
Upon examination of the relevant material on record, both lower courts upheld the plaintiff's dismissal based on the findings of the enquiry.
[Para 5]
Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 100 - Scope of second appeal - Limited in scope - The High Court cannot interfere with findings of fact made by lower courts, and both courts below found no fault with the dismissal order passed against the plaintiff.
Therefore, the appeal under Section 100 of the Code is dismissed.
