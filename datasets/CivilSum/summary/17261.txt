Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Reservation - Payment/Free seat - A petitioner intended to secure admission to the MBBS/BDS course through the B.C.
(A) category, but was instead granted admission against a payment seat.
Meanwhile, admission was awarded to three other candidates from the Backward class (B) category via a policy decision.
The petitioner is now requesting to increase the reservation percentage from 10% to 20% and to categorise Backward classes B.C.
(A) and B.C.
(B) categories.
The university failed to make sub-categorisation as per instructions, and the petitioner was given a payment seat as a result.
Based on this, the petitioner is entitled to one of three free seats reserved for B.C.
(A) category.
[Paras 5 & 7]
