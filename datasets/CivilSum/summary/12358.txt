Rajasthan Service Rules, Rule 337 - Rajasthan Consumer Protection Rules, 1987, Rule 3(6) - Rajasthan Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1996, Rule 151 - Pay Scale - The petitioner was a member of a Rajasthan Higher Judicial Service (RHJS), appointed as President of the District Forum while in service.
Although RHJS has a superannuation age of 60 years, President of District Forum can hold the position till 65 years or 5 years from date of appointment.
The petitioner continued in the post of President even after his superannuation from RHJS and received a salary to which he was entitled in RHJS but his salary was later fixed at Rs. 3553/- by deducting pension amount from last pay of Rs. 6700/-.
No other allowance was given.
In the appeal by State, it was held that the respondent cannot receive double benefit by adding pension amount to emoluments and is only entitled to his last salary in RHJS minus the amount of pension.
