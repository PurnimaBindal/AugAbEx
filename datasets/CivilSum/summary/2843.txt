Workmen Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Findings of fact - The findings of fact recorded by the Commissioner under the Workmen Compensation Act cannot be challenged in an appeal under Section 30 of the Act - The assessment of the deceased worker's age based on evidence is a question of fact and cannot be challenged on appeal.
[Para 4] B. Workmen Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 3, 4 & 10(1) - Interest - Interest on the compensation amount awarded should be calculated from the date of the accident - The Commissioner has the authority to award interest from the date of filing a claim until the date of the payment - The employer is responsible for paying interest and penalty under Section 4A of the Act according to Section 3 - Section 10(1) only refers to the one-month notice of the accident to the employer to enable them to pay compensation within one month - The filing procedure for a claim is established by Section 10(1) and does not prevent interest from being granted from the date of the accident - Payment of compensation is the only way for the employer to avoid interest.
