Kerala Education Act, (6 of 1959), Section 11 - Kerala Education Rules, 1959, Chapter 14-A, Rule 43 - In order to be promoted to the post of High School Assistant, a teacher must possess efficiency, as stated in Rule 43.
Additionally, in exigencies of service, seniority may be considered for promotion despite the teacher not being qualified in the subject in which the vacancy arose.
However, subject ratio cannot be equated with subject requirement, and the benefit of the proviso can only be given when there are a sufficient number of teachers available in the core subject.
[Paras 10, 11 and 12] B Kerala Education Act (6 of 1959), Section 11 - Kerala Education Rules, 1959, Chapter 14-A, Rule 43 - The respondent contended that since there was no Rule at the time of his initial appointment, his appointment should be approved.
However, the proviso, which has a retrospective effect, cannot take away vested rights.
It was held that a teacher's appointment is not deemed to have vested rights until it has been approved.
