Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - The petitioner was admitted to MA Part-I but missed some lectures, causing her to be ineligible to appear as a regular candidate for the examination.
According to the rules, she was eligible to appear as a private candidate as a woman no later than six days before the examination's start date.
She applied on April 23, 1999, and her case was forwarded to the university on April 24, 1999.
Although the Superintendent of the center allowed her to take one paper on April 26, 1999, she was not issued a Roll Number by the university and was not allowed to take the remaining papers.
Following the High Court's instructions, the petitioner was permitted to take the examination, and it was determined that she was eligible to take the examination as a private candidate.
Therefore, the university was instructed to recognise her candidature for MA Part-I as a private candidate and declare her result.
