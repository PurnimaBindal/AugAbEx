The decision of the Cabinet of the Government of Nagaland regarding the reimbursement of medical expenses of Respondent No. 3 is subject to judicial review under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
The relaxation of existing orders affects the public exchequer, and therefore, the state cannot avoid the scrutiny of judicial review.
[Para 14]  B.
The writ of mandamus cannot be issued in a case where only an individual is involved.
The decision of the government whether or not to prosecute Respondent No. 3 is not a matter of public interest, and therefore, not maintainable as a public interest litigation.
The court will not normally interfere in the due process of law unless there is an indication of public wrong or injury.
The writ petition filed by the petitioner to institute a CBI enquiry against Respondent No. 3 is not maintainable.
