The accused in a murder by conspiracy case were charged with abetment and conspiracy, but were convicted for murder without being charged for it under Sections 302 and 120B of the Indian Penal Code.
They were acquitted due to this illegality, which cannot be cured by the provisions of the Cr.PC The case relied on AIR 1955 SC 216 and AIR 1956 SC 116.
