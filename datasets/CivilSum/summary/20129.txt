Once acquitted on merit, the delinquent is entitled to reinstatement without any blemish on their record and a departmental inquiry is unnecessary.
The material used to deny their promotion was the sole reason for prosecution under the Prevention of Corruption Act, and since it is no longer applicable, it cannot serve as a reason to overlook the promotion during the DPC process.
The appellant should be promoted with full benefits from the date their immediate junior was promoted.
