Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(b) - Police Act, 1861, Section 7 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.1 - Dismissal - Petitioner, a Constable in the Anti-terrorist staff, was dismissed from service without a departmental enquiry due to various complaints against him and the satisfaction of the Senior SP based on objective facts.
The appeal to quash the dismissal order was rejected on the grounds that there was no non-application of mind.
