Constitution of India, Article 226 - UP State Universities Act, 1973, Section 68 - Alternative remedy - Reference - Dispute regarding inter se seniority of teachers of University - Writ petition involving pure question of law admitted five years back - If reference were made to the Chancellor under Section 68 of the Act, any aggrieved party would still have to challenge the decision in the Court - Petitioner cannot be non-suited on the technical ground of not availing the alternative remedy of reference.
[Paras 9 and 10] B UP State Universities Act, 1973, Section 31-A - Seniority - Promotion - Professors - Inter se seniority of promotees and direct recruits - Personal promotion - Provision prescribing length of service for giving personal promotion and prescribing qualifications for that purpose framed and notified only after the date of appointments - Personal promotion has to be given only after the date of notification - Persons appointed by direct recruitment are senior to those given personal promotion which has to be treated after the notification.
