Industrial Disputes Act, Section 6(6) - Correction of award - Only arithmetical or clerical, typing mistakes or omissions or commissions which were accidentally made can be corrected under Section 6(6) of U.P.I.D.
Act - The court cannot rehear the case in order to alter the original relief which was not granted - However, if the court closes the decision without touching the consequential relief, it can be rectified under Section 6(6).
[Paras 8 & 9]

Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 11-A and 25-F - Back wages - The grant of full back wages is no longer an automatic or natural consequence of reinstatement - The court must balance the interests of the workman guilty of misconduct or indiscipline and must ensure that the employer is not unduly burdened - In cases where the employer has been found to have proceeded with the dismissal mala fide, the court may award all consequential benefits - Otherwise, the court must differentiate between the relief of continuity of service and the award of all consequential benefits in each case.
[Paras 10, 11, 15, 17, 19, 20 & 21]

Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 & 25-F - Back wages - The initial burden to assert non-employment during termination is on the workman - Only if the workman asserts this on oath, does it become necessary for the employer to plead and prove his gainful employment.
[Para 18]

Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 11-A and 25-F - Dismissal from service - The mere fact that the labour court found the quantum of punishment excessive is not enough to warrant reinstatement - If one of the charges of serious misconduct is proven, the employer is not automatically required to reinstate the workman even if other charges are not fully proven or if the workman has an earlier long unblemished service.
[Paras 22 to 27]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Failure to implement the labour court's award does not amount to a violation of any orders of the High Court in the absence of a stay - This does not automatically result in an order of reinstatement.
