Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Writ jurisdiction - Dismissal of employees - In a copper mining industry, schools for children of employees were established and managed using grants from the industry.
Due to financial difficulties, the decision was made to close the schools and terminate the teachers' services.
The court cannot issue a writ of mandamus to provide VRS benefits to the teachers at the same level as other industry employees because they are not direct employees of the company.
[Paras 30 to 35]
