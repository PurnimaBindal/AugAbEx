Bombay Provincial Municipal Corporation Act, 1949, Section 67 - Minimum Wages Act 1948, Sections 14 and 22 - Overtime Wages - Respondents are claiming for overtime wages for work performed during holidays - Employees have accepted the terms of the working on holidays through a circular - Therefore, they cannot claim overtime wages under Section 14 of the Act.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C (2) - Overtime wages - The claim for overtime wages is vague, and the existing right has not been established - Hence, the Labor Court does not have the jurisdiction to determine and calculate the claims under Section 33-C(2) of the Act.
