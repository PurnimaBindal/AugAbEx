According to the Civil Procedure Code 1908, Order 6 Rules 14 and 15 and Rule 20(3) of Part F chapter 4 Volume 5 of "The Rules and Orders of the Punjab and Haryana High Court", along with the Constitution of India 1950, Article 226, it is improper for only one petitioner to verify the pleadings instead of all petitioners.
The parties should be bound by their pleadings from the very beginning, and any curable defects should be addressed with appropriate steps.
