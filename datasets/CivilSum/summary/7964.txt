Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 - Appointment - Bonus Marks - A merit list is prepared after awarding five bonus marks to candidates of rural areas and ten to those from a particular district where posts are advertised - A state circular is being questioned in regards to this.
A Single Judge holds the appointments invalid if candidates do not stand in the merit list after deducting the bonus marks awarded to them.
The Petitioners challenged this decision, but they do not stand in merit list even after getting bonus marks.
Persons from the select list were not made a party to this petition.
Thus, no relief can be granted to the Petitioners as they do not stand in merit list even by getting bonus marks, and persons selected have not been impleaded as a party.
Therefore, the appointments are not legally valid, and the appeals were dismissed.
[Paras 6, 10, and 11]
