The Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, Section 32(iv) is not applicable to the Haryana Housing Board as it is not considered a "Local Authority" under this section.
The rule of ejusdem generis is applicable when interpreting the definitions of "Local Authority" contained in the General Clauses Act and the Haryana Housing Board Act, 1971.
However, this rule cannot be applied to Article 12 as the definition of "State" includes several bodies that are heterogeneous in character and therefore, there is no genus in the definition.
Thus, the Payment of Bonus Act is applicable to the employees of the Haryana Housing Board.
