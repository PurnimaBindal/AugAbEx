Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 entitles workmen to apply for arrears of provident fund contributions from their employer.
However, the Regional Provident Fund Commissioner is not considered an employer or workman under the ID Act, as it is a statutory authority established by the Regional Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952.
Therefore, the Labour Court's jurisdiction under Section 33-C(2) cannot be exercised against it.
