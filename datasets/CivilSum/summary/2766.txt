Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 10, 19 and 30(1) - In case of any dispute regarding liability, amount, or duration of compensation under the act, the decision will be determined by the Workmen's Compensation Commissioner.
Such disputes are substantial questions of law under Section 30.
[Para 6]
Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, Sections 167, 149 - Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - A claimant may bring compensation claims under either of the acts.
The decision to invoke Section 149 of the Motor Vehicles Act only arises if the claimant chooses to make a claim under Section 167.
[Para 11]
Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30(1) - Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 - Insurers can only appeal compensation awards under Section 149(2) of the Motor Vehicles Act, not on the grounds of compensation quantum.
[Para 12]
Workmen's Compensation Act, Section 30(1) - A High Court may interfere with a decision of the Commissioner only if there is an appeal involving a question of law.
