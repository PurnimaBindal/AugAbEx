The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 1 Rule 8 - The selection should be challenged with all the selected candidates being impleaded - Failure to do so will result in dismissal of the writ petition.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection - Bias - Estoppel - Practice and procedure - Plea of nepotism and favoritism must be raised during the arguments - Failure to do so and no reasons recorded in the impugned order, it will be deemed as not pressed.
[Para 9] C The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection - Process of selection - Challenge to selection or selection process are two different things - An unsuccessful candidate cannot challenge the selection process after participating and taking a chance in the selection.
[Para 11] D The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Interview marks - Selection - Interview and group discussion marks must not exceed 10% and 5% of the total marks respectively - The Supreme Court directions in Munindra Kumar v. Rajiv Govil, 1991 (2) SCT 304 (SC) must be strictly followed.
[Paras 15 & 16] E. The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Selection on the basis of only interview marks, except in a few cases, is violative of Articles 14, 15 and 16 of the Constitution - Post of Registrar requires academic qualifications, experience, and abilities - Communication and other skills may be judged by the interview, but experience and marks of written test cannot be ignored.
