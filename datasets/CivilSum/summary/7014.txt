DRTA Condition of Appointment and Service Regulations, 1952, Clause 15(2)(vi) - Removal from service - Misconduct - Respondent, a driver in a corporation providing public transport, was charged with being absent from duty without leave for 112 days -Such prolonged absence without leave suggests lack of interest and constitute misconduct - Therefore, termination is justified.
[Para 2] B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-B - Dismissal of workman held justified -Award of the labour court directing reinstatement and backwages is set aside - Granting backwages last drawn under Section 17-B from the date of award is not appropriate - Corporation is required to pay the workman full wages from the time of filing the writ petition to the date of its conclusion.
