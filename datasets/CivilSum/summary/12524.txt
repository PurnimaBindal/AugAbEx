Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Natural justice/Departmental enquiry - Evidence - Cross-examination - Employee charge-sheeted on complaint - Interviewer's Statement recorded in absence of the accused - Lack of opportunity to cross-examine amounts to violation of justice principles.
- It is well-established that every accused official has the right to defend themselves and that all proceedings should follow the principles of natural justice, openness, and fair play.
- The right to cross-examine prosecution witnesses is guaranteed to accused officials.
- The enquiry report and impugned order are set aside with liberty to proceed anew.
[Paras 4 to 6]
