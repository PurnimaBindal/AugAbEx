The Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Promotion - Seniority-cum-Merit - In cases where promotion is based on seniority-cum-merit, seniority must be given preference over merit.
Even if both the candidates meet the minimum merit requirements for the promotion post, the senior candidate, even if deemed less meritorious, is to be preferred over the more meritorious candidate and their comparative merit assessment should not be conducted.
[Paras 3, 4]

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Promotion - Retrospective promotion - Recovery of benefits drawn by the person wrongly promoted superseding his senior - Retirement of - If a person has retired on the date the court declares his promotion as illegal and sets it aside, any benefits received as a result of the wrong promotion should not be recovered or withdrawn, irrespective of whether he was wrongly promoted because of superseding his senior.
[Para 5]
