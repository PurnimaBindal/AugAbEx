Indian Armed Forces Personnel (Reservations of Vacancies in Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) Rules, 1972 - Rules 4 and 5 - Military Service Benefits/Seniority - Rule 5 of 1972 Rules overrides any other rule providing to the contrary, therefore, in fixing seniority under any service rules, benefits under 1972 Rules cannot be ignored - Those entitled to 1972 Rules benefits are also entitled to the benefits in their seniority fixation - The benefit is not limited to the period of emergency only.
JT 1992(5) SC 667 followed.

B. Indian Armed Forces Personnel (Reservations of Vacancies in Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) Rules, 1972, Rules 4 & 5 - Military Service Benefits/Released Army Personnel - 'Demobilization' should have a wider meaning and include those released from military service except on their own request - Such released persons are entitled to military service benefits in the fixation of their seniority in the service.
[Paras 10, 11]  C Indian Armed Forces Personnel (Reservations of Vacancies in Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) Rules, 1972, Rules 4 & 5 - Military service benefit - First appointment - Benefit of the Rules is not limited only to the first appointment in Civil Service - Benefit is granted even on subsequent appointment according to the individual's status - One cannot be denied benefits of the Rules solely because of taking an inferior job until a more suitable job is available.
