Constitution of India, Article 311 - Regularisation of service - Over 1000 substituted/ad hoc teachers of the Education Dept.
have been working since 1981-82, but the State Government has refused to regularize their status and is taking steps to recruit directly instead.
The Supreme Court has instructed the State Government to first consider the regularisation of these teachers before proceeding with direct recruitment.
Additionally, the Court stipulated that any direct recruits can only be appointed if any vacancies exist after the process of regularisation is complete.
[Paras 5, 6]
