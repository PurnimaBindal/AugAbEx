Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Manipur Police Service Rules, 1965, Rules 5(1)(b), 14, 15, 16 and 24 - Seniority - Promotion - Officiating Service - Length of service - The right to seniority based on length of service is acquired and entitles an employee to be considered for further promotion - It is established that service rendered while on probation or in officiating appointments cannot be ignored when determining seniority - Confirmation/approval of appointment relates back to the date of initial appointment and the entire service is computed for reckoning seniority by length of service.
[Paras 12, 13 & 16] It is presumed that compliance with Rules 14(3) & (4) and Rule 15 of MPS Rules is followed before inclusion in the list under Rule 24, which is considered a selection in accordance with Rule 5(1)(b).
