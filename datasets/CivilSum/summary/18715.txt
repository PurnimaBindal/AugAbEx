Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Misconduct - The petitioner committed irregularities resulting in loss to the Government - Government land in long possession of individuals must be sold after determining the market price and recovery of charges of use and occupation - Property was sold on throwaway price without ascertaining possession, determining market price and without recovery use and occupation charges and without holding any public auction - The officer is guilty of misconduct if he does not comply with the rules and procedure dealing with the property of the Government - After an enquiry held in terms of Principles of Natural Justice, the Government is entitled to take action against him if he is found guilty of ignoring the rule of procedure.
[Paras 4, 5, 7 and 8]
