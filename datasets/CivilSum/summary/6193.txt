Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Pension & gratuity - Delay in payment of - Retirement benefits, such as pension and gratuity, are considered the property of a retired employee - Any delay in payment is seen as an infringement of their property rights - If there is undue delay, the employee is entitled to 12% interest and potential penal interest if payment is delayed despite court orders.
