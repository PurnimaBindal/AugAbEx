Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Dismissal - Reinstatement - Back wages - Delinquent driver accused of shortage of palm oil and over consumption of diesel - Labour Court found that workman was not provided assistance in enquiry proceedings and was not fully exonerated - Order of dismissal was set aside and reinstatement with continuity of service was directed but without back wages - Labour Court deemed denial of back wages as adequate punishment and there was no interference.
