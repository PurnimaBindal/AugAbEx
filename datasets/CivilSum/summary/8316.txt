Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(j) and 10(i)(c) - Industry - Definition of - The State did not provide evidence to prove that the project in question did not involve systematic activity with the cooperation of employees for the public's benefit.
Therefore, it is held that the irrigation department falls under the definition of industry.
[Para 4] B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(s) - Workman - Definition of - The determination of whether a person falls within the definition of workman depends on the type of work performed, whether he or she is employed for hire and reward, and whether the person is employed in an industry.
The source of employment, payment of wages, and length of employment do not affect the determination.
