Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 2(i)(h) - Clause XXIX of Schedule II read with Section 2(1)(n)(li) - Workman - Workman employed on a wheat thresher run by power or tractor - Regardless of the number of workers operating a machine run by power, each individual worker must be considered a workman under the Workmen's Compensation Act.
