Under the Rajasthan Compassionate Appointment of Dependants of Deceased Government Servant Rules, 1996, Rule 2(c) and the Rajasthan (Recruitment of Dependents of Government Servants Dying while in Service) Rules, 1975, Rule 2, employment is provided to a dependent of a government servant who dies while in service before anyone else.
This is done to ease the burden on the family of the deceased employee.
The rules also allow for other close relatives to be named by the widow or the guardian of the children, provided that they are approved by the Department of Personnel.
However, someone who is neither a close relative nor a guardian of the children cannot be named or approved for employment on those grounds.
