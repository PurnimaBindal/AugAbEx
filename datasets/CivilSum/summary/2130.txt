According to the Circular dated 15.9.1990, transfer from one institution to another requires approval of the Zilla Parishad as stated in Karnataka Private Educational Institutions (Discipline and Control) Act (10 of 1975), Section 8, and Karnataka Zilla Parishads, Taluk Panchayat Samithis, Mandal Panchayats and Nyaya Panchayats Act (1983), Sections 182, 184.
However, the Commissioner of Department of Public Instructions made such entrustment, which is deemed ultra vires since it should have been made by the State Government and published in the official gazette.
As a result, the Circular holds no legal weight.
