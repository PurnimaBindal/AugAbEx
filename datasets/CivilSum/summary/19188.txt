Constitution of India, Article 311 - Competent Authority - Suspension - Recommendations for suspension - Delinquent employees can be suspended if sufficient material against them exists to initiate disciplinary proceedings - The controlling officer can recommend suspension in the interest of administration and public interest, provided the recommendation is backed by true facts and not baseless allegations - An order of suspension passed by the competent authority, independently considering relevant facts and material during pendency of disciplinary proceedings, cannot be considered illegal or arbitrary.
[Paras 9 and 10]
