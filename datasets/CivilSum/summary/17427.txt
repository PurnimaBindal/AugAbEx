Punjab Food and Supplies Department (State Service, Class III) Rules, 1968, Rule 9 - Promotion - Eligibility - To be eligible for promotion to the post of Assistant, Clerks, Jr.
Auditors at Headquarters, Stenographers and Stenotypists must have five years of experience on the said posts - Promotions can be made from amongst these posts - Petitioner promoted as Junior Auditor while working as Stenotypist - His experience on both posts will be taken into account to calculate his five years of experience - His junior cannot be promoted merely because he was also found eligible for promotion as Junior Auditor.
