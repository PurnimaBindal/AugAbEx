Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admissions/Education - A petitioner seeking admission to Engineering Courses sent his application form by speed post on 2 Sept but the application was received on 11 Sept in the institution and was not entertained due to late receipt of it.
The process of admissions commenced only from 18 Sept A notice was given to the petitioner who later appeared in the Entrance Test and performed well.
The unilateral condition in the admission notice that forms received late will not be entertained cannot be stretched in each and every case.
Respondent has to be fair in its action not arbitrary.
Various students below in merit to the petitioner were admitted, and the College Authority filed a false affidavit with the availability of seat and did not comply with the directions of the court to reserve a seat.
The action of the college authorities was unfair and arbitrary.
The petitioner was entitled to be considered for admission, and directions have been issued accordingly to dispense with one candidate who was the last to make room for the petitioner.
