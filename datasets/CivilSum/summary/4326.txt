Trade Unions Act, 1926, Section 18(i) - Civil Procedure Code, Section 9, Order 39, Rule 1 - Trade Union activities - Leaders and members are permitted to hold meetings and dharnas but an order may be issued restraining them from obstructing the entrance of other workers to the establishment, damaging buildings or holding meetings within 50 metres of the establishment - Such orders are justified as they do not curtail the just right to demonstrate trade union activities - The suit is maintainable --1986 L.I.C.
1543.
