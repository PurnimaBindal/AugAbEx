Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 318 - Kerala Public Service Commission (Composition and Conditions of Service of Members and Staff) Regulations, 1957, Regulation 12 clause (1) - Special pay - Joint Secretaries and Deputy Secretaries in Kerala Public Service Commission are eligible for 'Special Pay' as recommended by Pay Commission due to their special work nature.
This pay would fall under the category of other allowance as allowed under State Rules.
The petitioners are entitled to receive 'Special Pay' from the pay revision date.
