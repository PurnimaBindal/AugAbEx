Jammu & Kashmir Services Regulations Vol.
I, Article 242 - State Govt. Instruction F.D.
Notification SRO-45 dt. 28.1.1980 - Pension and gratuity - There is an absolute bar preventing the checking of past emoluments beyond 24 months preceding retirement - It is not possible to reduce pensions or gratuities by revising past pay fixations.
