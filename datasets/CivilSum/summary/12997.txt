Constitution of India, Articles 311, 16 - Voluntary retirement scheme (V.R.S.)
of State Bank of India (S.B.I.)
- According to Clause (g) of circular dated 25.6.1999, officers of State Bank of India are required to complete the mandatory Rural/Semi-Urban assignment or request to withdraw their promotion before opting for voluntary retirement.
[Paras 19 and 22]
