Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 19 (g) - Indian Penal Code, Section 294 - Moral turpitude - Policy decision of Govt. of India dated 221973, clarified by a further decision dated 17/2631976 - The modification of the policy did not include Section 294 IPC as an offense of moral turpitude - Conviction alone under Section 294 IPC would not lead to the deprivation of the opportunity to serve the State, unless the related facts and circumstances met the policy's requirements - Termination quashed on appeal.
