Industrial Disputes Act, Section 11-A - Dismissal from service - Embezzlement - A bus conductor was dismissed from service due to embezzlement of Rs. 970 - According to the Act, the power of the Labour Court U/s 11-A cannot be abused to reinstate an employee who has proved dishonest and lost employer's trust - An employer cannot be compelled to trust an employee who has shown a tendency to embezzle even a small amount.
