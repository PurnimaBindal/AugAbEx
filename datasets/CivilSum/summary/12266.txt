Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Regularisation - The petitioner's services were refused to be regularized due to not being appointed in a clear vacancy post.
However, a circular states that staff appointed against a permanent sanctioned post should be regularised.
Based on the staff pattern, the petitioner was working in a sanctioned post.
Given the petitioner's considerable tenure in the sanctioned post, their services should not be embargoed in regularising against the vacancy or vacancy that arose during their said school tenure - The DIOS's order is overturned.
