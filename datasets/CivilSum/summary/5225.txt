Articles 16A and 16(4A) of the Constitution of India - Reservation in Promotions - Seniority - Punjab Govt. policy followed by PSEB clearly states that roster point promotees cannot claim seniority in the promoted category from the date of their continuous officiation to general category candidates, who were senior to them in the lower category and were later promoted.
[Para 8] C Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Electrical) Recruitment Regulations of 1965, Regulations 16(a) and 16(b) - Promotion - Seniority - Promotion on reserved point out to turn does not provide any right to claim seniority from the date of promotion itself and the relative date of promotion on the basis of reservation is of no consequence in respect of inter-se-seniority.
