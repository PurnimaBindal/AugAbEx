Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 - Promotion - Challenge to selection - Board not following procedure - Chairman's recommendation of cancellation upheld - It is determined that the Selection Committee can only verify eligibility requirements for a seniority-cum-merit promotion - If a minimum eligibility is not established and selection is based solely on merit, it becomes a merit-cum-seniority promotion and cannot be sustained.
