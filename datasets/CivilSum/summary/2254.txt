Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - U.P.
Procedure for Direct Recruitment for Group `C' Posts (outside the purview of the Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission) Rules, 2001, Rules 6 (8) and (6)(c) - Direct recruitment - Post of Junior Clerk/Typist and Stenographer - Despite being qualified for both categories, the petitioner failed the shorthand and typing tests for the Stenographer position and was not considered for the Junior Clerk-cum-Typist position by the Selection Committee - The court ruled that the Selection Committee was obligated to consider the petitioner's candidature for all vacancies within the categories of posts for which he was eligible and qualified - Consequently, the petition was allowed.
