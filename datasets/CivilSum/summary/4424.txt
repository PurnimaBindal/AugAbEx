The State must consider eligibility of candidates before they are allowed to compete for posts of clerks.
Once candidates are selected and appointed, it would not be fair for the State to declare them ineligible to compete or be appointed.
The Rule of equitable estoppel is fully attracted.
[Para 5]
The State failed to afford petitioners an opportunity to show that they were eligible for their positions.
This failure to grant an opportunity constitutes a violation of the principle of natural justice.
[Para 6]
Reversing individuals higher in the order of merit while the person below them continues would be contrary to the provision of Article 16 of the Constitution.
This scenario taints executive action with an element of unfairness and arbitrariness.
