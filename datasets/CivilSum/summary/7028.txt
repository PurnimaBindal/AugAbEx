Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination of workman - Calculation of 240 days - The period of service that a workman completed prior to the 12 months preceding the date of his or her termination has no bearing on the determination of continuous service - The Labour Court's practice of adding 69 days of Sundays and other holidays to each calendar year as working days is unsustainable because there is no evidence that the workman rendered services or was paid for those days - As the workmen had not completed 240 days of work when their services were terminated, they are not entitled to the protections provided under Section 25-F of the Act.
