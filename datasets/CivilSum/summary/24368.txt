Correction of Date of Birth - Judicial Review - The Court, Tribunal, or High Court should not handle any application for the correction of date of birth unless the applicant provides sufficient evidence within a reasonable time as per the rules - Moreover, the Court or Tribunal should be cautious in granting interim relief or continuation in service unless the applicant produces prima facie evidence of impeccable quality.
[Para 10]
Correction of Date of Birth - Onus of proof - It is the duty of the applicant to prove that the date of birth recorded in the service book is incorrect.
