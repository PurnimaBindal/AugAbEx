Kerala State and Subordinate Services Rules, 1958, Rules 14 and 17A - Reserved castes - Special recruitment - The vacancy position must be seen at the time of making appointments, not at the time of the Review Committee meeting or notification of vacancies to Commission.
Rules permitting such recruitment cannot be used to create an artificial deficit.
[Para 27]  B. Kerala State and Subordinate Services Rules, 1958, Rules 14 and 17A - Constitution of India, Article 16 - Reservation - Reservation for representation of reserved castes cannot exceed the prescribed percentage as it is meant to ensure adequate representation.
The non-obstante clause in Rule 17A cannot be used to exceed the 10% reservation limit.
[Para 28]  C. Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 335 - Reservation - Provisions for - Provisions enabling reservation should be strictly construed and cannot be given a more liberal interpretation.
[Para 31]  D. Necessary parties - Non-impleading of - Effect - When special recruitment is challenged without making the appointed person a party, their appointments are governed by the principle of lis pendens.
[Para 33]  E Special recruitment - Declared illegal - Appointees will be allowed to continue on posts on which they were appointed but not higher posts where promotions will be made in strict order of seniority.
It would be unfair to remove them after 9-10 years of service.
