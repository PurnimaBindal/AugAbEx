Border Security Force Act, 1968, Sections 20(b) and 40 - Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 142 - Court Martial - Departmental Proceedings - Scope of Judicial Review - According to Para 11 and 12, the writ court cannot interfere in judicial review of a summary security force court's trial if the delinquent was found guilty of charges and sentenced to dismissal after following complete procedure and principles of natural justice, unless there is a violation of Rule of law or principles of natural justice or if the sentence is disproportionately high, irrational, or perverse.
However, if the punishment orders are imposed in the exclusive domain of the punishing authority, they are not immune to correction under the jurisdiction of judicial review, and a rational penalty imposed after following complete procedure cannot be interfered by court.
