Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Discrimination - Arbitrariness - The court is obligated to "reach injustice wherever it occurs" even though it does not typically interfere with selections made by duly constituted bodies - The court must ensure that citizens' rights guaranteed by the Constitution are upheld and should strike down any action by the State that violates Article 14 - The High Court cannot remain silent on this matter - Official arbitrariness is more subversive of the Doctrine of Equality than statutory discrimination and it is the court's duty to check it.
