Constitution of India, Article 14 - Special increments/Jurisdiction - The petitioner was granted three advance increments by the Board of Directors at the time of her appointment.
However, the Managing Director revoked this benefit after 11 years and ordered the recovery of the amount paid to the petitioner.
The order was without jurisdiction as the Managing Director had no power to withdraw benefits granted by the Board of Directors.
The court issued directions to prevent any recovery from the petitioner based on the Managing Director's orders and quashed the orders.
