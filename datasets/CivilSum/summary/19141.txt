The Andhra Pradesh State Warehousing Corporation Employees Gratutity Fund Regulations, 1974, Regulation 4 illustrate that Gratutity is not an employer's gift but a right conferred by the provisions of the statute under Section 42(1) of Warehousing Corporation Act, 1962.
There is no maximum limit attributed to payment of gratuity in the Government order, only enhancements to the minimum are being made.
Moreover, the Andhra Pradesh Warehousing Corporation, with the previous sanction of the Government of Andhra Pradesh, had prescribed the maximum limit of gratuity payable according to the regulations of the Act.
These regulations are statutory; thus, Government order does not have the power to overrule them.
