Delhi School Education Act (18 of 1973), Section 8 - Delhi School Education Rules, 1973, Rules 115 and 120 - Termination of appointment of School bus driver - The petitioner concealed the fact of being involved in a road accident in a criminal case, and provided false information upon being questioned by the management - The petitioner's conduct was mala fide, intentional and fraudulent - The school acted within its rights to discontinue services of the petitioner for the safety of young students - The petitioner cannot invoke equity, having played and perpetrated fraud by making repeated misrepresentations.
