Constitution of India, Article 309 - Rajasthan Police Subordinate Service Rules, 1989, Rule 23 - Appointment - Selection - Post of constable - The petitioner was initially deemed unfit for appointment due to flat foot, without explanation.
However, later determined to be fit by a Medical Board.
The petitioner's low marks in the physical test, as well as their low marks in the interview suggest bias on the part of the Selection Board.
Respondents are directed to adjust the petitioner's interview marks to match those of the written test and refer the case for appointment.
