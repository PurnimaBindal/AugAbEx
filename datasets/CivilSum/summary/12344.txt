According to the Constitution of India, under Article 311(2)(b), a Havaldar can be dismissed from service through a departmental inquiry.
The petitioner was dismissed without a regular inquiry due to his involvement in a criminal case, but was later acquitted.
The Disciplinary Authority relied on undisclosed information that the petitioner was engaging in activities harmful to the State, which was not included in the dismissal order.
Hence, the order lacked clear reasons and was set aside, and the petitioner was reinstated.
