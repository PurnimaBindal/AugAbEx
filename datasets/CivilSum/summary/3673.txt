According to Punjab Civil Service Rules, Rule 1.1(a) Vol.
I Part I, Rule 14.30 Volume II Chapter 14, if one fails to deposit benefit and continues to enjoy them during service till six years after retirement, they are not entitled to switch over after the cut off date.
The petitioner in this case was willing to deposit EPF amount to switch over to GPF and subsequently entitled to pension, but since the instructions of Board were applicable from 1966 to 1988 and the petitioner retired in 1991, he missed the cutoff date.
While the Single Judge allowed the claim relying upon a judgment in the case of work-charge employees, the petitioner is a regular employee and the stand of respondents is that no similarly situated employee has been given benefit.
Additionally, a petition by a similar person was dismissed under similar facts.
Therefore, the appeal was allowed.
