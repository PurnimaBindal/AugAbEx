According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, there should be equal pay for equal work.
The conditions of affiliation for Sainik School include pay scale, and the petitioner, who teaches Hindi up to Class X in lower grade Course 'B' in Central Sainik School, is claiming a pay scale equal to the Lecturers in the +2 Schools or Senior Secondary Schools.
However, the service conditions of teachers in Sainik Schools are not governed by the Rules and Conditions of Affiliation to CBSE, but by a set of service rules framed in accordance with the directions issued by the Supreme Court in the All India Sainik Schools case.
The petitioner is not entitled to claim parity with teachers and Lecturers of Punjab Government since the pay scales they receive are not applicable to the petitioner.
The prospective revised pay scales by the fifth pay commission are much higher than those for teachers/lecturers in Punjab Government Schools as well.
