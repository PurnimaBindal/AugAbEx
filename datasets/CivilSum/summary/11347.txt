Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 Appointments/Promotions - Quota Rule/Direct appointments - Promotion to the post of Naib, Tehsildar - The government, via a resolution dated 14963, provided a Quota Rule of 50:50 for promotions and direct appointments.
Direct recruitments were not subject to the Quota Rule for 15 years.
Reversions cannot be made solely for the purpose of allowing direct recruitments, and therefore, reversion orders have been nullified.
