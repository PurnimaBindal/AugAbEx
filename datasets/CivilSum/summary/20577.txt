According to the Himachal Pradesh Forest Service (Class II) Recruitment, promotion and certain conditions of Service Rules, 1966, Rule (4) along with Constitution of India, Articles 309, 16 and 14, an amendment was made to Schedule Column (10) about the seniority of direct recruits in comparison to promotee officers of Himachal Pradesh Forest Service Class (II).
The Himachal Pradesh Administrative Tribunal made a judgment stating that the seniority of direct recruits to the Forest Service Class II will be from the date of joining after training completion and not from the date of joining the training at the Forest Research Institute.
The amendment of 30th April, 1986, stated that selected candidates will be treated as ‘in service’ from the date of joining the Institute.
The Tribunal's interpretation was not acceptable, as the training period of the direct recruits will be counted for seniority in the service provided that the direct recruit successfully completes the training and joins Class II Forest Service.
The Tribunal's impugned judgment and order were dismissed.
