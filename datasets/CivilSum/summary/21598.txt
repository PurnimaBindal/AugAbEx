Gujarat Civil Services Classification and Recruitment (General) Rules, 1985, Rule 11-A, Proviso - Promotion - Reserved posts - In cases where no candidates meet the minimum experience requirements, the authority may relax the condition of experience if necessary for the administration, with reasons recorded.
The main part of the Rule and its proviso should be interpreted harmoniously to fulfill their purpose.
[Para 5] B.
To give effect to the intention of the provision, the proviso and main part of a law must be read together and in harmony.
