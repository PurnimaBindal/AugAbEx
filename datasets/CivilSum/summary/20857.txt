According to Punjab Service of Engineers, Class I, P.W.D.
(Buildings and Roads Branch) Rules, 1960, Rules 9(3)(1), 2(14), 5(6) and 10, the post of Chief Engineer, Buildings and Roads Deptt.
in Chandigarh can be filled by a Superintending Engineer who has completed three years of service.
The eligibility for promotion to this post is not limited to S.E.
(Civil) but also includes those from other branches like Mechanical or Electrical.
The rules do not require that every vacancy of a Chief Engineer in UT Chandigarh be filled by transfer or deputation from a S.E.
of Punjab, but a direct appointee from Chandigarh Administration is also an option.
