The Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 state that it is not permissible for the respondent corporation to use the region-cum-industry formula to deny wage parity between workers in Bihar, Orissa, Assam, UP and Delhi regions and those in Calcutta Port, godowns in the Calcutta city complex, and depots in West Bengal.
