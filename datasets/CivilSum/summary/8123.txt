Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - Natural justice - The Managing Director of a Cooperative Sugar Mill terminated petitioner's services without following rules and principles of natural justice.
It was held that the writ is maintainable.
AIR 1989 SC 1607 followed.
[Paras 7, 15 and 16] B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ - Alternative remedy - Petitioner's services were terminated without following termination of service rules.
It was held that there was no need to relegate the petitioner to alternative remedy and the writ is maintainable.
1989-5 SLR 455(DB) followed.
[Para 8] C Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Petitioner was appointed as Cook (Female) and had experience of cooking.
However, her service as a cook was terminated on the plea that she had no experience as Cook.
It was held that it is difficult to believe that a woman will have no cooking experience.
Requiring a certificate especially applies to the petitioner was held arbitrary.
[Para 13] D. Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 311 and Article 226 - The petitioner's termination was done without giving her an opportunity of hearing.
It was held that the termination is bad in the eyes of the law.
AIR 1996 SC 1921 followed.
