Marathwada University Act (25 of 1974), Section 57 - Appointment - Post of Lecturer - Contention that selection bad due to absence of two members during selection committee formation - Held, committee was properly formed with four members including University expert, and absence of Vice-Chancellor and Director of Higher Education nominees cannot invalidate the selection.
[Para 12] B Marathwada University Act (25 of 1974), Section 57 - Appointment - Post of lecturer - Petitioner not selected - Contention of self-assessment or mala fide cannot be used to challenge selection - Selection committee's opinion must be respected as the best judge.
[Para 13] C Marathwada University Act (25 of 1974), Section 57 - Appointment - Post of lecturer - Reserved Post - Dereserved - Contention by reserved candidate seeking relaxation in qualification - Held, a reserved candidate cannot claim relaxation in qualification when there is a candidate with the prescribed qualifications - To fill a reserved post, it must be advertised as such 5 times in 5 years before the dereservation can occur - In the meantime, a general candidate can be appointed on a temporary or ad hoc basis.
