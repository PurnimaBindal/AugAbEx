According to the Haryana Improvement Trust Services Rules, Rule 11 - Pay scales - all officers working in Improvement Trust shall not be higher than the corresponding posts in State Government offices.
However, this was challenged in court and it was determined that employees of Improvement Trusts were discharging the same duties as their counterparts in Govt.
Departments and were entitled to the same pay scales.
Therefore, Junior Engineers of Improvement Trusts had to be kept at par with counterpart Govt. employees.
Despite this, an artificial distinction was made in the promotional scales given by the Govt. to the J.Es.
working in Govt.
Departments.
