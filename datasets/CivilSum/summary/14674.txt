Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 1(3) and 16(1)(b) (as amended by Amendments Act, 1988) - Non-applicability of Act for period of 3 years for new establishments- The period must be calculated from the establishment date - The date of the amending Act does not matter -Whether the period of three years expired before or after the coming into force of the Amending Act or commences on the setting up of an establishment after the commencement of the Amending Act is irrelevant.
