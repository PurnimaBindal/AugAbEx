Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Section 10 - Order of removal - Petitioner filed writ petition claiming allegations of mala fides - Employer responded with denial of allegations in counter affidavit - Court held that petitioner should seek remedy by raising industrial dispute under 1947 Act and obtaining merit finding from industrial adjudicator - High Court interference deemed unnecessary with equally effective alternate remedy available under statute.
