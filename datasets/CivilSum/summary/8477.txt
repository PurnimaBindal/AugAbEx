Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25-F and G - Termination of services - Unfair Labour Practice - The petitioner- respondent's services were terminated without any notice or compensation, which the Labour Court deemed invalid.
The court ruled for reinstatement with full back wages and continuity of service.
The university contested this ruling by requiring an affidavit from serving persons that they had not been working in any other department of the University.
The Labour Court found this requirement to be an Unfair Labour Practice and no interference is necessary.
