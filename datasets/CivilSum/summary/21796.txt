According to Rajasthan State Electricity Board Officers (Recruitment, Promotion & Seniority) Regulations, 1974, Entry 19, preference must be given to professional experience and qualifications, as this has a direct impact on the administrative efficiency of the service.
The provision requiring higher experience based on differences in educational qualifications for promotion and the quota based on qualifications for promotion to higher posts is not discriminatory and does not violate the right of equality under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
