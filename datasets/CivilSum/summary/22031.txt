Constitution of India, Article 226 - Selection - Judicial Review - Court cannot inquire into the process of selection and comparative merits of the candidates - It is the selecting agency's responsibility to assess specific suitability of officers for particular posts based on qualities, merit, competence, leadership, and flair for policy-making participation.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Proceedings of selection committee - Reasons for selection or non-selection - Non-selection of a candidate does not necessarily mean their name was not considered properly - The selection committee is not obligated to record reasons for selection or non-selection in its minutes - The lack of recorded reasons does not invalidate the selection process.
