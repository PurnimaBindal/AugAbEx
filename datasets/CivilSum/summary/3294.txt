According to Punjab Civil Subordinate Services (Executive Section) Rules, 1944, Rule 9 and the Constitution of India, Article 14, Forest Guards are promoted to Foresters in accordance with their seniority after completing the required training.
However, some junior candidates were sent for the training claiming better merits.
It is clarified that training is just an eligibility criterion and not a part of the promotion process.
Therefore, Forest Guards should be sent for training based solely on their seniority, and their promotion should be considered only after they pass the training course.
In cases where seniority-cum-merit is the criteria, seniority must be given priority unless the candidate is unfit for the higher post.
The court has quashed the order sending juniors first for training and instructed to send the Forest Guards strictly based on their seniority.
The petition has been allowed with costs.
