Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Education/Admission/Supports - Policy and instructions on admissions - Reservation for sportsmen - The policy for reservation of sportsmen must be established and published prior to event tests, in order to establish a grading system for further admission.
Any changes to the policy after event tests and immediately prior to admissions would be unfair to candidates who may miss out on important opportunities to advance their grades and become eligible for admission.
Policies issued after admissions tests should not be considered.
The respondents are asked to admit candidates based on the policy that was in place at the time of the entrance test.
