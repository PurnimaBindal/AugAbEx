As per the policy of the Govt., dependents of work-charged employees are not eligible for compassionate appointment.
However, confirmed work-charged employees are entitled to all retiral benefits under the Terminal Benefits Rules.
Nevertheless, confirmation of work-charged employee does not alter his status as work-charged employee for the sake of compassionate appointment policy.
Dependents of such employees would only receive terminal benefits of the deceased but not appointment benefits.
