Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Compulsory Retirement - Misconduct - Disciplinary proceedings - The appellate authority modified the punishment of dismissal to compulsory retirement for the petitioner's serious lapses while working as a manager in the bank while sanctioning and conducting loan accounts.
However, the petitioner claims that this punishment is disproportionate to the alleged misconduct.
This claim was rejected when the appellate authority considered the material placed on record and documentary evidence supporting the view taken.
The proceedings demonstrate the investigative thoroughness of the appellate authority, and therefore no merit exists in the petition and it is dismissed.
