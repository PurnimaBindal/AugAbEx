Constitution of India Article 226 - Bihar Non-Government Elementary School (Taking Over of Control and Management) Act, 1976, Section 3 - Absorption - The petitioner, who was working as a teacher on a consolidated salary, was demanding payment of scale of salary on the principles of equal pay for equal work.
A condition stipulated in his appointment letter required him to meet certain requirements before receiving payment.
Allowing him to continue on the job did not work as an estoppel against the State Government, as it was not obliged to absorb him into service since he was not appointed by it.
The writ petition was held to be not maintainable.
