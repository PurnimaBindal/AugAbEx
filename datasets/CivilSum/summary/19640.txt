Guru Nanak Dev University Calendar, Volume II, Part B (1991 Edition) Ordinance No. 1 - Examination/Compartment - In the event that a candidate is mistakenly declared as passing and subsequently required to re-appear under Ordinance No. 1, the Vice-Chancellor must exercise their power within a reasonable timeframe in order to allow the candidate to re-appear along with others - If there is an unreasonable delay in notifying the student that they must re-appear and this results in the student losing their chance, the student cannot be expected to suffer - The petitioner is deemed to have passed in the first opportunity available.
[Paras 5 and 6]
