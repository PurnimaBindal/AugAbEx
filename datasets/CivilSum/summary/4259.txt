Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39 - Rajasthan Class IV Service (Recruitment and other Service Conditions) Rules, 1963 - Appointment/Vacancies - Illegal appointments made in violation of the Rules without any vacancies and without calling from the Employment Exchange do not entitle the appointees to any benefits.
The guilty parties of violating the Constitution and the Rules should face consequences, including discharge after receiving notice and an opportunity to show cause- despite existing court orders granting equal pay for equal work.
