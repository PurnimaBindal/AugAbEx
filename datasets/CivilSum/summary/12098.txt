The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226, Police Act 1949, Section 4, and Bihar Police Manual, Rule 663, outline eligibility for the appointment to the post of Constable, including physical health standards.
However, in this case, the height of the petitioner was found to be less at the time of joining than what was recorded at the time of selection.
Despite this discrepancy, the petitioner was issued an appointment letter, and their height was later found to be within the minimum requirements outlined in Rule 663.
Therefore, the petitioner cannot be restrained from joining, as the situation was created by the authorities themselves.
