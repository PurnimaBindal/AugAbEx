According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, the Rule of Seniority for teachers is determined by the date of approval recorded by the Screening Committee.
If two teachers have the same date of joining, seniority is determined by the date of acquiring eligibility qualifications.
If that is the same, age is taken into consideration.
In this case, both the petitioner and the respondent joined on the same date but the respondent acquired eligibility qualifications much later.
The respondent did not claim to be senior in age.
Therefore, the petitioner, who was fully qualified and eligible on the date of joining, must be made senior to the respondent who acquired eligibility qualifications much later.
[Paras 8 to 11]
