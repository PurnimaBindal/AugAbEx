Civil Procedure Code, Section 10 - Second suit - Multiple litigation on one cause of action - Although Section 10 CPC aims to prevent simultaneous trials of two parallel suits related to the same cause of action by courts of concurrent jurisdiction, it only applies to suits filed in civil courts.
The proceedings in the Labor Court cannot be compared to those in a Civil Court as a court of concurrent jurisdiction, and thus Section 10 is not applicable.
