Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Central Civil Service (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965, Rule 5 - Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955, Rule 16 - Termination of services - Natural justice - A temporary Constable in CRPF was terminated due to suppressing information regarding previous arrest and prosecution in a criminal case - The order of termination did not violate natural justice or cast any stigma - The petition was dismissed.
