Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Ad-hoc/Temporary service - Seniority - Fitment in accordance with quota and rota prescribed under the Rules - Promotions made against the vacancies falling to the quota for direct recruitment - Promotees perform officiating duties against the posts of direct quota until direct recruits arrive - Appointments by promotion against the quota for direct recruitment held contrary to the Rules - Promotees cannot benefit from seniority based on their service rendered against the post for direct quota - These promotions and appointments cannot be considered in accordance with the Rules - They are only entitled to fitment in accordance with quota and rota prescribed under the Rules - They cannot demand that the quota rule has broken down and they be put back in seniority from the dates they were officiating against the post meant for direct appointments.
[Paras 1 and 2]
