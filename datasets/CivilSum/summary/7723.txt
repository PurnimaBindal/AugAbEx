Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947), Section 25-F - MP Industrial Relations Act (27 of 1960), Section 31(3) - A workman is not required to work in the same capacity for 240 days for relief under Section 25-F. Any contract basis was merely a tool to bypass the law.
Once the workman has completed 240 days of service under the same employer, they are entitled to the benefits of Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act.
