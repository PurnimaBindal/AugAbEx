Constitution of India, Article 226 - Selection/Appointment - Jurisdiction of the court - Usually, the court should not interfere with the candidate selection process - Selection Committees or Boards must evaluate the candidates' capabilities and the courts should not alter or supplement the individual assessments of selection committee members - This general rule may not always apply - If selection procedures and fairness are completely ignored and questionable methods are used, the courts should not hesitate to challenge such selections or government action.
[Paras 1,15, and 16]
