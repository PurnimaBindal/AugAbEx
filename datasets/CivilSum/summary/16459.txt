State Bank of Patiala (Officers) Service Regulation, 1979 - Voluntary Retirement Scheme, Clause 8 - Scheme provides the bank with the discretion to accept or reject voluntary retirement - Cut-off age for eligibility is 53 years - The fixation of the cut-off age cannot be considered arbitrary or illegal - The petitioner's application for voluntary retirement is being considered by the bank - Petition dismissed.
