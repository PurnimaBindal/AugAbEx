According to Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Companies Act, 1956 and Industries (Development and Regulation) Act, 1951, Section 3(F), there was a dispute regarding the claim of salary absorption by the appellant, who claimed to be an employee of AIDC which is the owner of IPAL.
However, the High Court found that IPAL was a separate and independent company, and the members of the appellant union were not employees of AIDC.
IPAL was not a party in the reference, and the material on record shows that it was promoted by AIDC.
IPAL and AIDC have separate Board of Directors and under these circumstances, the appeal was dismissed.
Nonetheless, employees are still allowed to make claims for what is due to them from IPAL.
