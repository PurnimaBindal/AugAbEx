Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Police Rules, Rule 162 - Dismissal - Absence from duty - Charge against the petitioner is that he was absent from duty for 177 days starting from 12.7.1992 - While the petitioner claims that he was under a mental disorder on 15.7.1992, and he was granted four days leave that day - The inquiry report concluded that a mental disorder definitely existed - The petitioner was found to be a genuine patient of mental disorder and his absence from duty was not a wilful act - The impugned order of dismissal is set aside - The petitioner has been ordered to be reinstated into service without back wages and continuity of service.
