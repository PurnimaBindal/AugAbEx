Constitution of India, Article 226 - Person with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 47 - Central Civil Services Rules, Rule 20(2) - Disability Pension - The petitioner is completely bedridden and unable to work due to medical incapacitation.
The petitioner suffered a disability during service and is covered under Section 47 of the Disabilities Act.
Despite being forced to forgo earned leave due to physical incapacitation, the petitioner is entitled to earned leave under the section.
[Paras 8 and 9]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Maintainability of writ petition - Since the petitioner suffered a disability during service and the respondents failed to comply with the mandatory requirements of Section 47 of the Disabilities Act, his writ petition is maintainable.
