According to the Contract Labour (Abolition and Regulation) Act, 1970, Sweepers within the car parking area of an Airport are covered by the notification under the Act.
The definition of "building" includes appurtenances that form part of it, and an Airport includes not just the areas for aircraft but also passenger facilities, including car parking.
Therefore, it cannot be argued that the car parking area is not a part of the Airport's building.
Further debates on the matter are unnecessary.
