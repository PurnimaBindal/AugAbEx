Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Government National Emergency Concession Rules, 1965, Rules 2 and 4(iii) - Pension Fixation - Petitioner can claim benefits of military service during the first emergency from 261062 to 10168 for computing pension qualifying service, but not for service rendered during the second emergency from 3.12.71 to 3.7.77 - Rule 2 does not include service rendered during the second emergency - Petition was partially accepted, with directions to count the period of the first emergency (5 years 2 months and 15 days) for computing qualifying service, and grant benefits accrued from date of retirement.
