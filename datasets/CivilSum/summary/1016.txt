According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, the termination of services is valid even if the selection of the petitioner was irregular.
In 1994, a petition challenging the termination of services was dismissed.
However, in 2001, the petitioner filed another petition challenging the appointment of some individuals claiming they were junior to him.
The petitioner failed to join those individuals as party respondents and the petition was eventually dismissed.
