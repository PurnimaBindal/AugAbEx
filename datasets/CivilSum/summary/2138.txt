The rules and ordinances of Guru Nanak Dev University do not provide a time limit for re-evaluation of papers, unlike the Punjabi University Ordinance which allows for a 15-day limit.
This loophole has led to hardship for students who are waiting for re-evaluation results while attending their next class.
However, this does not render these rules or ordinances as ultra vires of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
[Para 8] B In a case where a petitioner had already joined the course of MA Part II and had completed the requisite lectures, but the re-evaluation of her marks in two papers was delayed and resulted in wasting her academic year, the University was directed to allow her to appear in these papers in MA Part I in the Annual Exam of April 1993 by relaxing the rules and to declare the result of MA Part II only after the declaration of result of Exam in MA Part I which she had yet to undertake in April 1993.
