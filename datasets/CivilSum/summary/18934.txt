Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pensionary Benefits - Delay - Interest - The respondents have delayed in releasing pensionary benefits to the petitioner without any reasonable explanation, even though more than three years and four months have passed since retirement.
The respondents claim that the delay occurred because the petitioner did not submit a non-employment certificate, but it is unclear under what provisions of law such a certificate is required from a retiree.
The respondents have been directed to issue the final pension payment order within two months of this order and pay interest at a rate of 12% per annum.
