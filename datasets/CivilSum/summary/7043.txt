Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Punjab State Electricity Board Technical Service Class-III Regulations, 1996 - Promotion as SSA from Oil Cleaners - Application under Section 33-C(2) is maintainable - Eligibility for higher pay scale - Entitlement to higher salary of higher post if working on own pay of lower post while waiting for promotion - Recovery of wage difference recoverable through Section 33-C(2) application.
