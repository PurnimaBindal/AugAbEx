The petitioner complained that the HAU violated Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India by appointing the second respondent as District Extension Specialist (Animal Science), despite being lower in the merit list.
The HAU acknowledged the mistake, yet did not rectify it, and the Vice-Chancellor never gave a decision on the allocation of marks to the two respondents.
The order of merit was wrongly ignored, and the appointment of the second respondent was arbitrary and unjustified.
Thus, it was quashed.
[Paras 10 and 11] B The University's decision to appoint eight persons for the two advertised posts, without issuing a fresh advertisement, was totally illegal and deprived other potential candidates of the opportunity to compete.
The post must be readvertised, and the most suitable candidate should be appointed.
The second respondent is directed to vacate the office immediately.
