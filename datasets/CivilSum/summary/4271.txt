Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection - Eligibility/appointment - If a candidate fails to meet the eligibility criteria on the relevant date, they cannot be appointed to the public post.
A court cannot dismiss the challenge against a selection on the basis that the challenger is also ineligible for the appointment, as this would allow for an irregular appointment to continue.
It is necessary for eligibility criteria to be met either on the date of application, on the last date of receipt of applications, or on any other cut-off date specified.
