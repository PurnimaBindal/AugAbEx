Under the Workmen's Compensation Act Section 4 and the National Maritime Board Agreement of 1980, a claimant-respondent who was working as Dock Maintenance and a seaman received a salary of Rs. 1403/- at the time of the accident.
Even though his salary at the time of release was Rs. 2700/- p.m, compensation must be calculated based on the accident date.
Therefore, the claimant-respondent is entitled to Rs. 63,000/- in compensation.
However, they should also receive simple interest at a rate of 6% pa from the date of the accident till the date of the award, and at a rate of 15% pa from the date of the award until the actual payment date.
The appellant failed to deposit this amount without justification and so will be liable to pay a penalty of 50% of the compensation.
