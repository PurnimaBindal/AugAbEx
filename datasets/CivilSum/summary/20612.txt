Constitution of India, Articles 32 and 136 - All India Services Act, 1951, Section 3(1) - Indian Police Service (Cadre) Rules, 1954, Rule 4 - Indian Police Service (Recruitment) Rules, 1954, Rule 9(1) - Indian Police Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations 1955, Regulation 5 - Promotion - Cadre strength - Triennial Review - The appellants seek direction for reconsideration of their promotion to the post of Indian Police Service from an anterior date.
The process of determining cadre strength was initiated in 1989 and finalized in 1991, but no explanation is given for not conducting triennial review within the specified period of 3 years.
The impugned order of the Tribunal is set aside and the Union Government and State Government are directed to reconsider the promotion of State Cadre Officer to Indian Police Service based on the re-determined strength of cadre, treating it as in the year 1989.
If on such re-consideration relief would be available to any of the appellants for promotion to IPS on the basis of quota available to them in the cadre, the same may be given to them.
