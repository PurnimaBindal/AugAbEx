As per Rule 13.8 (2) of Punjab Police Rules, 1934, ad hoc promotion and regular promotion are two distinct types of promotions.
The petitioner was appointed under the sports quota and found suitable in accordance with the rule.
The appointment is considered a regular appointment, even if it was shown to be ad hoc.
The petitioner is entitled to attend the Intermediate School Course along with his equals and juniors, and his seniority will be fixed accordingly on the promotional post of Head Constables.
The respondents are directed to send the petitioner for the course forthwith, and he will be deemed to have gone along with his juniors.
[Paras 4 and 6]

According to Rule 1318 of Punjab Police Rules, 1934, all Head Constables promoted as such are placed under a probation of two years.
If there is no record of misconduct against the promoted Head Constable during two years of probation, and the probation period was not extended, they will be confirmed on the date of completion of probation period.
[Para 5]
