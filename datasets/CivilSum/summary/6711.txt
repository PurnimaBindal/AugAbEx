Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical reimbursement - Medical Treatment received at Private Hospital - The petitioner with unbearable chest pain was immediately taken to a government hospital but failed to get proper response.
Out of anxiety, the family took the petitioner to a nearby private hospital.
It was not shown that any government hospital in the state had efficient emergency facilities for heart operations, including angiography and angioplasty.
The genuineness of the bills submitted by the petitioner for reimbursement cannot be disbelieved, and the petitioner is entitled to 80% of the amount spent on treatment.
There is always a reasonable apprehension in the mind of the patient and family members regarding the best treatment to save the person's life at the relevant time.
