Employees State Insurance Act, 1948, Sections 2(9)(i), 45(A) - Factory Act, 1948, Section 2(k)(iii) - Electricity Board - Sub Station of - Applicability of Act - As employment is connected to the work of the factory, employees at the Sub Station engaged in distribution and maintenance of service lines are covered under the purview of the ESI Act.

Employees State Insurance Act, 1948 - Provisions - Interpretation - In light of the Act being a social legislation intended to provide benefits to workers, interpreting the provisions in a technical and narrow sense would be inappropriate.
The construction of the provisions that assist in achieving the objective of the Act should be preferred.
