The petitioner had appeared in supplementary examination of Plus 1 in English and also in final examination of plus 2 in 1989.
However, the petitioner was shown as late result in the Gazette published in July 1989, and despite various requests to the Board to get his result declared, he received no response, not even to the legal notice.
Therefore, it was deemed unfair to ask the petitioner to avail a special chance to clear the English paper of Plus I now at a belated stage.
As a result, the Board has been directed to declare result of the petitioner showing him pass and also to declare his result of Plus 2 within 2 weeks.
