Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume I, Part I(1984 Edition), Rules 8113 and 821 - According to a Full Bench decision in Surjit Kumar Sharma v. State of Punjab, 1992(3) SCT 485 (P&H) (FB), permanent employees in vacation departments are not entitled to encashment of half-pay leave on retirement, which is only admissible for unutilized earned leave at the credit of the employee.
[Para 2 and 5] B. Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume I, Part I(1984 Edition), Rules 8113, 8119 and 821 - Half pay leave can only be granted for medical reasons or private affairs under Rule 8119, and even then, commuted leave is only granted subject to specific conditions.
The distinction between earned leave and half pay leave is reason for Rule 821, which only permits encashment of earned leave on retirement.
[Para 9] C In a case where a member of the Subordinate Judiciary had his claim for encashment of half pay leave rejected without any reasoning, the court quashed the decision and ordered respondents to consider and pass a well-reasoned order.
However, this decision does not support the claim made by the petitioner and the petition was dismissed.
