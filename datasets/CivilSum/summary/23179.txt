Constitution of India, Articles 136, 226 and 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Public Interest Litigation - Locus standi - In disciplinary proceedings, public interest of general importance is not at stake, as it is a matter between employer and employee - Practising advocate or outsider has no interest in such proceedings - High Court order quashing departmental charge-sheet cannot be challenged by petitioner - Special Leave Petition denied.
[Paras 9 and 10]
