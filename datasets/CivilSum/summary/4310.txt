The petitioner was granted provisional admission for a three year M.D.
(Medicines) Group-II course, completed two years of it, and should receive a one year exemption based on one year of completed house jobs.
Granting this exemption will enable the petitioner to complete the three year course.
In the interest of justice, without considering the merits of the case, the petitioner's result should be declared since the claim has not been contested by the respondents.
