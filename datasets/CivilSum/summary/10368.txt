Constitution of India, Article 226 - Bank of Baroda (Officers) Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 47 - Transfer - Inter-zonal Transfer - The impugned transfer has been challenged as violating the transfer policy by using Regulation 47 without any reasoning - Petitioners who hold a transferable job are obligated to be transferred all across India - According to Regulation 47, every officer is liable for transfer at any location in India, regardless of the transfer policy - The respondent's decision to transfer the petitioner cannot be considered arbitrary, unauthorized, or contrary to the transfer policy.
