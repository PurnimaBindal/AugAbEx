The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226, states that the State is responsible for recognising degrees and diplomas.
Hindi Sahitya Sammelan in Allahabad and Paryag have not been established under any Act nor have they been recognised by any university.
Examination centres run by these schools are fraudulent and their certificates do not qualify individuals for public employment.
The state of Haryana has never recognised these certificates.
Unqualified teachers can cause injury to society and the government has been directed to terminate the employment of such teachers, except for those with three years of experience, and give them an opportunity to acquire the proper qualifications within a set time frame.
Moreover, an appointed committee is to determine the equivalence and recognition of degrees, diplomas and certificates and this list shall be published in two newspapers.
