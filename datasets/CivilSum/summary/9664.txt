The Industrial Disputes Act from 1947, specifically Section 25-F, covers delay and laches.
This becomes relevant in the case of a petitioner whose services were terminated in 1988, with the industrial dispute being raised in 1999.
Additionally, the petitioner was never appointed to a regular post.
Despite the petitioner's claim, the Labour Court's decision to reject their claim was not perverse.
