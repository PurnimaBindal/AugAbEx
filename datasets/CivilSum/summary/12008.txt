Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume 1, Part 1,(as applicable to Haryana), Rule 4.4 - Advance Increment claimed on promotion - Revised pay scale - Pay refixation - Petitioners contend that they were not granted one advance increment on date of their promotion to Headmaster which is illegal - However, since they were already receiving Headmaster pay scale at the time of revision and pay refixation, no relief can be granted as they have already enjoyed a higher pay scale of 2000-3200.
