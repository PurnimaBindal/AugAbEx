Sections 2F, 3, 7 and 19-A of the Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 are discussed in regards to the order of applicability of the Act and its sustainability.
It is noted that no enquiry as per Section 7-A preceded the order, and contractors were not asked to provide names of helpers employed or wages paid.
The order lacks independent determination by authority.
The petitioner employed contractors only between May to November 1976, from 1969 to 1980, and under the circumstances neither contractors nor helpers of contractors can be considered as employees of the petitioner to be counted on the establishment's strength.
It is concluded that the illegality of the order is apparent and the establishment does not have the financial capability and stability to bear the burden under the 1952 Act for the employment of extra persons for short periods due to exigency.
