Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pension - Show cause notice - A retired Principal of Government Dental College was served with a show cause notice asking why a 10% cut shouldn't be imposed on his pension - The notice was challenged as ultra vires Rule 22.
(b) of Pension Rules and against Article 300-A of Constitution - It was held that enquiry proceedings can be continued after retirement of a Civil Servant and, if found guilty, a cut in pension can be imposed - The petitioner was accused of attesting a forged copy of certificate in favour of his son and causing the disappearance of original record pertaining to admission of his son to MDS Course - Show cause notice is meant to give petitioner an opportunity to explain position and respond to proposed action - Petition dismissed as action was in conformity with principles of fair play and natural justice.
