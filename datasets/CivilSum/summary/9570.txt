Indian Air Force Regulations, 1964, Paras 711 and 712 - The Selection Board must not consider the cause or reason of a punishment that has already been awarded by the competent authority when making promotion decisions.
The Board must only consider if the punishment makes the officer unfit for promotion.
If a reproof or censure was not required to be entered in the CR or personal file, and the officer has met all other criteria for promotion, the Board cannot consider the punishment or reasons behind it.
The Board's action in ignoring the petitioner's promotion application based on reproof was deemed illegal and arbitrary, and the Board was directed to reconsider the promotion within two weeks.
[Paras 5 and 8]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - A punishment of 'reproof' that was not reflected in subsequent confidential reports and did not impede the petitioner's promotion to Air Commodore cannot be considered by the Selection Board.
The effect of such reproof, if any, is washed away.
[Paras 4 and 6]
