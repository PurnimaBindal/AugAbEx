Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 12 - Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules, 1957, Rule 9 - Conciliation proceedings - The conciliation officer is not responsible for investigating a matter if the workmen don't appear before them - The workmen have a duty to explain their dispute and its potential resolution to the conciliation officer - Given the lack of appearance by workmen, the conciliation officer rightfully ended proceedings.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 33(i)(a) and 31 - Conditions of service - Change of conditions of service - Transfer - Transfer is not considered a condition of service - Employers retain the right to post employees in different locations as required - Even mala fide transfers are not considered conditions of service - A transfer during the pendency of a dispute under Section 12 does not warrant action under Section 31 of the Act.
