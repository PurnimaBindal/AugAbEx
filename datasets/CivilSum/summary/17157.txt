As per Constitution of India, Article 226 and Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, Regulation 50, a petitioner who joined the Armed Forces Medical College in August 1985 and received commission on July 6, 1990, is not eligible for disability pension as he resigned before the completion of five years and there is no evidence that there were any compelling circumstances.
Additionally, it was not proven that the petitioner didn't intend to retire voluntarily or took this step under the advice of any officer.
