Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulations 136(a), 146 and 183 - Release of service element - The petitioner was enrolled in Army on 8.1.47 and became invalided due to paralysis on 24249.
A disability pension was granted on 25249 and it continued up until 31.1.62.
Despite this, the petitioner was deprived of the service element on the grounds that he had not completed the required 15 years of service.
However, an army man is entitled to the service element if he has put in at least one day of service and becomes disabled, in addition to the disability pension, and the Government cannot argue that the individual has not completed the necessary years of service.
The respondents have been directed to release the service element of the petitioner, which shall be payable to him for life.
