Life Insurance Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1960, Regulation 39 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 9 - Jurisdiction - Dismissal from service - If challenged, the validity of an order passed by a statutory corporation in exercise of powers under Service Regulations must be addressed in civil court if the grievance raised is purely of a civil nature, and the plaintiff is not claiming enforcement of any right or obligation created under Industrial Disputes Act or regulations framed thereunder.
[Paras 15 & 17]  B Life Insurance Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1960, Regulation 39 - Dismissal from service - Natural Justice - The impugned order does not disclose independent application of mind on part of authorities concerned while inflicting penalty; no reasonable opportunity of being heard afforded - Order of reinstatement with all consequential benefits upheld.
[Para 25]  C Life Insurance Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1960, Regulation 39 - Dismissal from service - Reinstatement with Consequential Benefits - If an order of dismissal from service is found to be in violation of principle of nature justice and of relevant Service Regulations, reinstatement in service with consequent benefits is a natural corollary.
Thus, if a dispute has remained pending in courts for last 29 years, laches in no manner can be attributed upon plaintiff to deprive him of legitimate claim to which he is legally entitled.
Additionally, the plaintiff is restrained from working in service of Corporation for no fault on his part.
