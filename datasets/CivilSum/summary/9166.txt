The Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(s) and 33-C(2), the Andhra Pradesh State Road Transport Corpn.
Conduct Regulations, 1963 Clause 2(d), and Classification, Control and Appeal Regulation, Regulation 18 discuss suspension from duty.
In the case of conductor accusations regarding cash and tickets, workers were put off duty without valid suspension orders.
However, after an inquiry, they were eventually removed from service.
Nonetheless, the workmen were entitled to full wages for the period they were put off duty, until the date of the removal order.
Up until that point, they remained in the employment and relationship of employees and employers, which only ceased on that date.
