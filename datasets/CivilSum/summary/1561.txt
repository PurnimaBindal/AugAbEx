West Bengal Primary Education Act, 1973 - Regularisation - Absorption - Petitioner seeking regularisation - Petitioner appointed on a non-sanctioned post - No approval of Managing Committee or District Inspector of Schools given to petitioner - Petitioner's service cannot be regularised since her appointment is in violation of statutory rules - Petitioner must go through the back door despite existing recruitment rules - No regularisation possible even by executive decision and government circular letter.
[Paras 10 and 11] B.
Writ Jurisdiction - Regularisation - Petitioner appointed through back door - No sanction given by Managing Committee or District Inspector of Schools - Petitioner claiming regularisation by way of filing writ petition - Petitioner has no legal right to come to the writ court - Writ petition is not maintainable.
