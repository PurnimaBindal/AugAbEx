Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary action - Appellate Authority - The Appellate Authority in service matters must carefully consider the case and produce an independent order with sufficient reasoning for findings of guilt and punishment.
Failure to do so results in a non-application of mind and can be set aside.
If the original punishment is dismissal, it merges with the appellate order and can only be reinstated with consequential benefits if the order is set aside.
Payment of back wages requires proof of unemployment during that period to the satisfaction of the employer.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Remand - Remand is not necessary when the Appellate order is clearly illegal and lacks proper consideration.
