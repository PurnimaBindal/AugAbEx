Uttari Rajasthan Milk Producers Cooperative Union Employees (Conduct, Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1992, Regulation 13 - Termination - An employee is prohibited from participating in any election, with no exceptions, and as such, the petitioner had no right to run for the position of Gram Panchayat sarpanch.
Permission was merely granted to file a nomination, and his eventual election to the position resulted in the performance of duties for both the respondent and the panchayat.
The order of termination is reasonable and based on regulation 13 of the 1992 regulations.
Regulation 39 does not apply.
