Constitution of India, Articles 311, 16 and 14 - Haryana Education (College Cadre) Group A Service Rules, 1986 - Rule 9(1)(b) - Petitioner has challenged the need for Principals of Government and non-Government Colleges to serve at least 25 years in order to be granted Professor grade of pay - Court has declared this condition to be invalid - as a result, the respondents are tasked with considering the case of the petitioner within 6 months and ensuring that the petitioner is given the same grade as any Principal junior to him who has already been granted Professor's grade.
