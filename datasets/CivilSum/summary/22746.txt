The Central Reserve Police Force Rules, 1955 pertain to the respondent's age of superannuation.
After retiring from military service, the respondent was re-employed by the Indo-Tibetan Border Police, with the terms of employment explicitly stating that CRPF Rules would govern his service.
The respondent claimed a superannuation age of 58, citing certain standing orders issued by the Ministry of Home Affairs.
However, the High Court was incorrect in its conclusion that the standing orders applied to this case as the contract of employment specifically referred to CRPF rules that also cover retirement age.
