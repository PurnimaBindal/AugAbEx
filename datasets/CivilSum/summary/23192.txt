Bihar Private Medical Colleges, (Taking Over) Act, 1977, Sections 3 and 6 - Bihar Pension Rules, Rule 58 - Qualifying service for pension - Private service - Taking over of Private Colleges has implications on the qualifying service for pension under Rule 58.
According to the Rule, government servants qualify for pension only if their service was under the government on a substantive and permanent appointment paid by the government.
Service rendered by teachers in private colleges prior to taking over does not fulfill these conditions and cannot be included for pension calculation.
