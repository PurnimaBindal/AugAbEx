Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Reference/Speaking order - The Industrial Tribunal issued a non-speaking award stating that technical workers in various Spinning Mills in Punjab are entitled to equal pay and pay scales recommended by the Punjab Govt., and did not consider the equivalence of duties or objections regarding maintainability raised by the management.
The award is likely to be overturned.
The case is being remanded to the Tribunal to issue a speaking award that takes into account all parties' arguments.
