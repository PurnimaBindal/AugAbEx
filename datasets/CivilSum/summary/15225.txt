Provident Fund Rules, Rule 14.13 - Release of Provident Fund - The petitioner's application for the release of Provident Fund in connection with the marriage of his son has not yet been decided upon.
However, since the petitioner is entitled to the withdrawal as per the provisions of rules, it is unjustifiable not to sanction the same - The respondents are required to release the amount immediately.
