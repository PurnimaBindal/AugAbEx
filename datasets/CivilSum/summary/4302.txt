For Respondent: LM Lodha, Advocate.
A.
The termination of services of the petitioner by the University without following the basic and elementary procedures is unreasonable and arbitrary and can be reviewed under the writ jurisdiction of the High Court under Articles 311 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 18] B.
The court can examine the unlawful actions of the respondent irrespective of whether or not the terms and conditions of the petitioner's services were expressed as statutory rules and the petitioner was required to sign a contract form since the rule of law demands governance by laws under Articles 226, 14 and t Constitution of India.
[Para 18] C. The act of the University in terminating the services of the petitioner on account of alleged absence without providing an opportunity as mandated by the University Handbook is a clear disregard of the elementary principles of Natural Justice and cannot be accepted under Section 327(5) of Jodhpur University Ordinance and Article 311 of the Constitution of India.
