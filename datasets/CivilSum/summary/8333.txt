Sections 2A(2) and 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, are relevant to Andhra Pradesh Act 32 of 87, which introduces sub-section (2) to section 2A of the ID Act.
This allows a workman to approach the Labour Court directly in case a dispute remains unresolved through amicable resolution or adjudication by a competent court, without requiring a reference from the Government.
The reference under these circumstances is valid and not contradictory to the provisions of section 10 of the Act.
