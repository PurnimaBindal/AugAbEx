According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, promotion can be granted based on seniority-cum-merit or merit-cum-seniority, with grading determined by confidential reports.
The DPC has the authority to reach its own conclusion based on officer review and competence in writing the reports, which the appellant was found lacking in.
Despite no manifest error of law, the court cannot act as an appellate authority over the DPC assessment.
However, the appellant can challenge the order of supersession if she is aggrieved, even after the relevant period.
