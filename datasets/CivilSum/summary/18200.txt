According to Section 3(1) of the Workmen Compensation Act, 1923, the employer has the primary responsibility to pay compensation and interest.
When the insurer is liable to indemnify the employer, there are no justifiable reasons to release the insurer from paying the interest imposed on the employer under Section 4-A(3).
The insurer's liabilities include the principal amount and the interest awarded on the principal amount of compensation.
