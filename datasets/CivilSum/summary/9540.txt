Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 162 - Estoppel - Degrees and Diplomas - Derecognition of degrees and diplomas of one State by another - A native of Punjab, the petitioner took admission in JBT course in Punjab but after passing the same married in Haryana - because Diploma of Punjab is not recognised in Haryana, they are not entitled to seek employment in Haryana based on that diploma.
The doctrine of promissory/equitable estoppel is not applicable as the petitioner never acted on an express or implied promise made by the Govt. of Haryana.
