According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, all employees should receive equal pay for equal work.
This holds true for College Librarians, who were initially placed in the same pay scale and assigned the same non-gazetted Class-II status as PTIs.
However, later on, Colleges of General/Higher Education and those working in Colleges of Engineering, Medical and Education were given different pay scales and status, despite all employees satisfying the same conditions and having the same duties and responsibilities.
This kind of classification between equals infringes the principle of equality and is both unreasonable and unjustified.
