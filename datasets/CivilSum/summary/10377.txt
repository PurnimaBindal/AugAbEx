Constitution of India, 1950, Article 14 - Appointment - Post of Part-Time Sweeper - The applicant for the position of Sweeper was rejected due to age, while alleging that another, who was over the age, was selected - However, the alleged individual was empanelled in 1989 for a role that cannot be compared to the position of the applicant - As the applicant was never empanelled as a part-time Sweeper, she cannot claim to legalize the illegality or irregularity - Article 14 cannot be interpreted to authorize illegal orders - No discrimination.
