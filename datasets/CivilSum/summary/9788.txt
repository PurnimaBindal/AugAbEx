Constitution of India, Article 226 - Central Bank of India (Officers) Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 47 - The decision to merge two regions in Delhi and Calcutta but exclude Mumbai was made on administrative exigencies and is an executive act entirely within the administrative sphere - The court cannot go into the merits of such a decision regarding the policy matters of the Bank.
[Para 21] D Transfer - Policy of posting and transfers - The circular dated 1082000 of the Central Bank of India on posting and transfers is not discriminatory or arbitrary because it was issued in administrative exigencies.
[Para 23] E. Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Central Bank of India (Officers) Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 47 - Transfer on promotion under the policy of posting on transfers - Employees who participate in the promotion process and receive promotion are estopped from challenging the consequential posting and transfer policy.
