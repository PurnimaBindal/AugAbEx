Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Compulsory retirement - Continuity in service - Benefit of Promotion - Petitioner forcibly retired from service due to being involved in criminal case in which he was acquitted - The order of compulsory retirement was nullified by the Apex Court while setting aside the High Court order - Petitioner is entitled to monetary benefits as no adverse remark was made against the petitioner before involvement in criminal case proceedings - Petitioner filed a contempt and was entitled to only increments because of continuity in service awarded - However, promotion cannot be treated as a consequential relief per se - Petitioner is not entitled to interest for delay in making payment within the stipulated period - Petition dismissed.
