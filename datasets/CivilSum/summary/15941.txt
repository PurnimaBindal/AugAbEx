Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1638 - Departmental Enquiry - Enquiry Report - The punishing authority must assign reasons for its dissatisfaction with the enquiry report before ordering enquiry afresh - A fresh enquiry de novo cannot be ordered without any recording of reasons for the defects and disagreement with the report - The objective acting of the punishing authority is obligatory - An order for fresh enquiry cannot be given solely due to the disliking of the enquiry report - The order of fresh enquiry is quashed.
[Paras 10 and 11]
