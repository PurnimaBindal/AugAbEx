Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(4-A) - Dismissal - Reinstatement - Validity - A respondent workman was dismissed for misappropriation/misutilisation of funds by an enquiry committee.
Though labor court found the workman guilty of temporary misappropriation, it ordered 50% backwages to the workman from the date of dismissal till the date of award.
However, the petitioner contends that the labor court granted the award as a premium for grave misconduct, which is an error.
The labor court has erred in granting compensation in lieu of reinstatement despite holding the respondent guilty of misconduct/misappropriation.
The grant of compensation is wholly unsustainable in law.
In such cases, the labor court cannot substitute the penalty imposed by the employer.
The petition is allowed accordingly.
[Paras 7, 8 and 10]
