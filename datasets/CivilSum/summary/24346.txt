The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, Criminal Procedure Code, 1973, Section 24, Legal Remembrancer's Manual, Para 706(3) discuss the termination of Govt. pleaders in the districts, who are considered law officers of the State and paid from the public exchequer.
The expression "may be terminated at any time" in their appointment letter should be interpreted as not being without a cause or reason, and en masse termination without cause and reasons violates Article 14.
Any state action must avoid the vice of arbitrariness to survive.
