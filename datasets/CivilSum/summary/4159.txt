According to the policies for absorption, a holder of a permanent civil post could be permanently absorbed or, failing that, be reverted to their parent department.
The writ petitioner was relieved of their duties as Director of the Company, but the trial Judge set aside the order and directed the appellants to reinstate the petitioner.
The doctrine of estoppel by representation is applicable, and when the petitioner opted for permanent absorption, they did not lose their permanent service under the Government of India.
The principle of promissory estoppel debarred the appellant from taking a stand that the petitioner's service had automatically ended after the initial period of two years elapsed.
The state must conform to the norms laid down by various orders, circulars, and notifications and cannot deviate without a rational basis.
Public employment cannot be made dependent on the whims of authorities, and termination does not depend on their discretion.
Services cannot be terminated without adhering to the conditions of service.
The trial Judge’s judgment was upheld, and the appeal was dismissed.
