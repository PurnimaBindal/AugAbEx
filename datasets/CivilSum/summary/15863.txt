Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Nomination - Death-cum- retirement gratuity - Insurance scheme - An employee may nominate all near relations, except for a wife and sons who are not living with him and not willing to provide financial support - An employee is entitled to exclude such wife and sons - The nominee selection process is akin to drafting a will - The rule aims to ensure an employee's wishes are respected, and benefits are disbursed accordingly - The rule must be interpreted in a manner that advances its objective.
