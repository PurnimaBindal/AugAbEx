Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Pension Regulations for the Army (Part I), Rule 132 - Pension - Disqualification from pension - An army personnel who is absent unauthorisedly creates a deficiency in service, and their claim for pension may be rejected as a result - The competent authority is only able to condone such a defect for up to 180 days - The petitioner in this case remained absent for 473 days, and therefore the rejection of their claim for pension was justified - The action of the authority cannot be considered unjust or unfair if they were not competent to condone the lapse.
