Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-B, 25-F and 2(oo) - Retrenchment - Completing 240 days of work in 12 calender months - After the repeal of Section 2(eee) and introduction of Section 25-B(2), an employee who has worked for 240 days during a period of twelve months will be deemed to have been in continuous service for a year, even if they have not actually worked continuously for one year - As long as they have worked for 240 days in a 12 month period, it is not necessary for them to have worked for an entire year.
This is supported by AIR 1981 SC 422.
