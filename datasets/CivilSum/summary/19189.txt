The Public Works Department (Electricity Branch) Provincial Service Class III (Subordinate Posts) Rules, 1952, Rules 7 and 9 - Rules of departmental accounts examination of ministerial establishment in the Electricity Branch, Rule 13(ii) - Seniority - Probation - Confirmation - Passing of departmental accounts exam.
- After completing the probationary period, an employee can be confirmed under Rule 7 if their work, conduct are satisfactory, and if they have passed the departmental examination.
Seniority is determined at two stages under Rule 9, before confirmation based on the order of appointment dates and finally based on the date of confirmation.
An employee must pass the departmental examination and be confirmed under Rule 7 for a substantial appointment.
Under Rule 9, if a person is superseded due to inefficiency, they lose their seniority.
A harmonious reading of the rules indicates that 'may' in clause (2) to Rule 7 implies 'shall.'
