Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 115 - Termination - Execution - Having two separate enquiries and issuing two separate termination orders is allowed - Even though the earlier order was nullified by the Civil Court, the decision against the first order does not negate the second order's effect.
- The Executing Court can take the second order into account - The petitioner is only entitled to a suspension allowance from the date of the first order to the date of the second order.
