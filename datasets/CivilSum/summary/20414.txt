The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 state pay scales must be revised, and the ICAR restructured departments and cadres, leading to the formation of the Agricultural Research Service (ARS).
The petitioner remained in the basic cadre and did not opt to be allocated to the ARS cadre, even though pay scales were prescribed at the time of revision.
He continued in the same cadre and did not request absorption in the ARS cadre subsequently.
Although there was automatic absorption under the restructured scheme, he insisted on induction and absorption in the S-4 grade.
He cannot claim the benefit of revised higher pay scales of S-3 grade in ARS or other grades later on as he remained in the same cadre which was not available in the cadres in ARS.

The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 state that equal pay for equal work must be given.
The ICAR restructured departments and cadres, but scientists doing the same type of work and remained under the same employer were governed by two systems.
Employees in similar circumstances are entitled to equal pay for equal work and cannot receive different revised pay scales, even if they belong to different groups.
