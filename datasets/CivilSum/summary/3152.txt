According to the Constitution of India, specifically in Articles 14 and 16, it is required that equal work be met with equal pay.
Despite differences in feeder cadres and pay scales for junior Assistants, promotions can still be made on the basis of last pay drawn.
A junior receiving a higher pay than a petitioner would not be considered discriminatory or a violation of Articles 14 and 16.
