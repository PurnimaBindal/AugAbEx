Article 226 of the Constitution of India states that a reserved category individual is eligible to be admitted under the General category based on merit before the seat under the reserved category is filled up.
The Jaskaran Singh and others v. State of Punjab and others 1995(2) SCT 65 decision is followed.
In cases where the petitioner qualifies for admission based on their merit in the general category, it would be unjust and unfair to deny them a seat in the general category.
[Para 7] B Article 226 of the Constitution of India notes that denying a candidate admission based on merit in the General category, despite their application in the Sports category, would be unjust and unfair.
