Section 11 of the Civil Procedure Code deals with res judicata.
In a case where termination of service was found to be illegal and the petitioner was reinstated, the issue of back wages remained unresolved.
While the authorities denied the claim, the petitioner was held entitled to back wages from the date of the suit till reinstatement.
The claim in Civil Writ Petition cannot be barred by res judicata, and even a part of the claim cannot be rejected on grounds of limitation.
The principle of extension applies in such cases.
