The Rajasthan Subordinate Civil Courts Ministerial Establishment Rules, 1986, Rules 28, 30 and 31 and Article 16 of the Indian Constitution govern the recruitment and confirmation of stenographers, and according to these rules, a stenographer cannot be confirmed in service unless they pass the qualifying test conducted by the Commission.
Furthermore, passing the examination for subordinate office under the 1957 Rules would not confer any benefits to the stenographers working in subordinate civil courts.
As a result, they must be treated as working in an extended probationary period.
