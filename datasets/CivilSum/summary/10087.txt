As per the Contempt of Courts Act, the direction to regularize as per seniority had been given.
However, in this case, the first junior was regularized prior to the directions by Division Bench and the second junior was regularized on CAT allowing the application and on dismissal of writ petition.
It was held that the question of respondents acting in breach of mandamus issued by regularizing first junior does not arise.
While the Act may be wrong, it cannot be considered contemptuous.
Furthermore, since the second junior was regularized due to a judicial order and being compelled to do an act under pains of penal consequences, the respondent cannot be said to be in contempt of orders passed.
As a result, the notice of contempt was discharged.
