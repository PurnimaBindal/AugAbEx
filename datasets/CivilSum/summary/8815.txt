According to Sections 2(aa) and 33C(2) of the Apprentice Act, 1961, an apprentice does not have the right to receive wages like regular employees, and they cannot claim stipend due to them under Section 33C(2).
Apprentices can only hope to be absorbed into the establishment later, and Section 33C(2) only applies to workmen who wish to claim an amount due to them.
