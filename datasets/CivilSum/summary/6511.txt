Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Completion of Apprenticeship - The petitioner received appointment based on apprenticeship due to directions from High Court in an earlier petition.
However, the petitioner is now seeking appointment from the date of completion of apprenticeship.
It is held that apprentices do not have a right to be appointed in preference to other applicants and the petitioner should have sought this relief in the previous petition.
