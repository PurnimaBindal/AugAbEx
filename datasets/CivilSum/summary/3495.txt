The Punjab Privately Managed Recognized Schools Employees (Security of Service) Act and Rules, along with the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and Constitution of India's Articles 311 and 226 were all referenced in a case where an employee's services were dismissed.
The director of public instructions upheld the dismissal and the employee later claimed reinstatement due to not receiving a formal order of dismissal and citing an illegal order of approval.
However, the court held that the passing of a dismissal order was a mere formality and not required.
Additionally, employers retain an inherent right to temporarily terminate the relationship with the employee.
[Paras 12, 14, 18 to 20]

The violation of procedure for a writ jurisdiction case under Articles 32 and 226 of the Constitution does not always require interference from the court.
Mere technical violations are not enough to warrant interference.
[Paras 19 and 20]
