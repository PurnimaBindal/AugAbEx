Constitution of India, Article 16 - Medical claim - Reimbursement of - The petitioner, a retired Inspector of Central Excise and Customs, underwent Coronary by-pass surgery at Escorts Hospital, Karnal.
Although he was not a CGHS card-holder, the medical officer referred the petitioner to Escorts Hospital in the absence of any CGHS Hospital in Karnal.
The petitioner incurred nearly 1,78,850 rupees in expenses, but his claim was denied due to lack of CGHS card.
However, in case of serious ailments and in the absence of a CGHS Hospital, certain hospitals like Escorts are recognised by the government for employee treatment.
The petitioner seeks direction for the amount disbursement based on the office memorandum.
