Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Incentive Bonus Scheme - Production incentive - Eligibility - It is not accurate to claim that only the mechanics engaged in repair work are the sole operators of the Division for repair works - The entire staff including mechanical and ministerial staff contributes towards the functioning of the Division and is eligible for the incentive - Even employees who are indirectly involved in the process are qualified to receive production incentive.
