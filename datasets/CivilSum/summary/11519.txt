Rajasthan Education Subordinate Service Rules, 1971, Rule 7 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16(4B), 33, 34, 309, 310, 312A - Recruitment - A recent Corrigendum reserved all 300 posts for the purpose of filling the backlog of reserved categories.
It was contended that as per Rule 7(4), vacancies cannot be carried forward beyond 3 years.
However, it was held that the State can take a policy decision to pay regard to the mandate of the Constitution and therefore, all vacancies can be reserved in view of the mandatory provision of Article 16(4B) of the Constitution.
