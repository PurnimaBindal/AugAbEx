Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Section 142 of the Sikh Gurdwara Act, 1925 state that the Shiromani Gurdwara Prabandhak Committee (SGPC) is a product of a statute.
The Service Rules that the SGPC has framed for its employees using its statutory power have the force of law, and a violation of those rules makes the SGPC amenable to writ jurisdiction.
The writ jurisdiction of the High Court is not limited.
[Para 5]
Section 142 of the Sikh Gurdwara Act does not provide an alternative remedy to an employee of the SGPC who has been terminated or dismissed from their service.
It does not cover those kinds of cases.
