Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Two initially selected candidates appointed following qualifying examinations - 14 additional candidates later appointed through promotion - No fresh examination conducted - Absence of records to confirm legitimacy of these appointments - Concluded that there is a possibility of illegal gratification - Despite this, appointments are not set aside as the appointees were not impleaded as a party, leading their appointments to become final - Therefore, an order is made in accordance.
