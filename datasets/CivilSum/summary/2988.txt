Pharmacy Act (8 of 1948), Sections 26, 46 - Registrar of Council - Appointment by State Government - Services provided on a lending basis to Council - State passed order withdrawing services after specified period - Order challenged - Council argued lack of reasons and non-compliance with natural justice - Held, Council has no right to raise contentions for the employee.
[Paras 9 and 16]  B Pharmacy Act (8 of 1948), Sections 26, 46 - General Clauses Act (10 of 1897), Section 16 - Registrar Appointment and Powers of State Government -Section 46 of the Act empowers state government to make rules and prescribe the qualification, term, and responsibilities of the Registrar - Section 16 of General Clauses Act grants the power of removal to the appointing authority - State has the power to control the State Council; the lack of explicit provision does not mean the power is absent.
