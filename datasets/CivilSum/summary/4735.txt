The Punjab Economic & Statistical Organisation (State Service Class III) Rules, 1963 and Punjab Civil Services (Promotion of Stenographers and Steno-typists) Rules, 1961 govern the promotion of Stenographers and Steno-typists working in the Civil Services of the State to higher posts in the Clerical side.
These rules shall prevail over the Constitution of India, Articles 309, 16 and 14.
Respondent No. 3 was promoted as Senior Scale Stenographer, and his subsequent promotion as Head Assistant was in accordance with Rule 3(1) of the 1961 Rules despite being in the same pay scale as Assistant.
The petitioners' contention that respondent No. 3 being Stenographer could not be promoted as Head Assistant was rejected, and the petition was dismissed.
