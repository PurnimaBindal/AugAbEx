Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Termination - Absence from duty - The petitioner was terminated from his services by a memo dated 781975 due to absence from duty without any enquiry being held.
However, it was held that the memo dated 781975 terminating his service was not justified as on the very day demand notice was given by petitioner.
It was also found that the Labour Court was not correct in giving compensation in lieu of reinstatement.
As a result, the petitioner was ordered to be reinstated with continuity of service and other benefits.
[Paras 2 and 3]
