Constitution of India, Article 226 - United Commercial Bank Officer Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976, Regulations 7(3) and 17(ii) - Penalty - Non-speaking order - The disciplinary authority did not provide a reasoning while concurring with the findings of the enquiry officer - The appellate authority did not address the arguments made by the delinquent - It is mandatory for the appellate authority to consider the contentions raised by the delinquent, and give his own decision with a brief explanation - The order imposing penalty and decision of confirming order of disciplinary authority have been nullified.
