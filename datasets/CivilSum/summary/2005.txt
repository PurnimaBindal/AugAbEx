Punjab Civil Service (Judicial Branch) Rules, 1951, Rule 7 - Qualifying marks - Selection - Candidates who did not secure the minimum marks required for each paper and in aggregate cannot be made eligible for viva-voce test - Rounding off the fraction of marks is not allowed in competitive examinations.
Not giving such benefit is important as every mark counts in such highly competitive exams.
Also, meritorious candidates can be disadvantaged if benefit is given and ineligible candidates become eligible.
The judgments in Asha Mehta's and Raj Deep Singh's cases are not consistent with the correct law.
