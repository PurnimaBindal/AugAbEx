Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Civil Services (Judicial Branch) Rules, 1951, Rules 7 and 8 - Recruitment - Appointment to Haryana Civil Service (Judicial Branch) - The petitioner was not recommended for recruitment to service as a ward of Freedom Fighter, as he did not meet the requirement of securing 55% marks in aggregate - A petition challenging the decision of the Commission up to the Apex Court was dismissed - The petitioner filed another petition seeking the benefit of the amendment made in the rules in 1996, but it was held that the amendment could only apply to pending selections and not those that were already finalized - There was insufficient grounds for directing the respondents to apply the amendment retrospectively.
