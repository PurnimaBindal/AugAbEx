Section 25-F and 25-J of Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and Section 56 of Contract Act are applicable - Chapter VA of ID Act takes precedence over Contract Act - Section 25-F of ID Act operates regardless of Section 56 of Contract Act - Incapacity of workman can only be claimed under Industrial Disputes Act - Workman initially claimed incapacity to work, but later testified in Labour Court that he was able to perform duties after recovering from injuries - His testimony remained unproven during cross-examination - Therefore, Labour Court was right to order reinstatement of workman with full backwages.
