Limitation Act, Section 5 - Condonation of delay - Parties to litigation affected by the judgment- Sufficient cause - Those not party to petition must have no knowledge of the judgment - The delay does not turn bad cases into good ones or allow injustices to prevail - The law is founded in public policy.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Bihar Police Manual Rules, Rules 649, 660 - Seniority - Officiating promotion - Period of ad hoc or officiating promotion can be counted towards seniority only if it was according to the rules - Officiating promotion without seniority until final selection - Clear stipulation that one will not get seniority in the rank until final selection.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Necessary parties - Writ petition should be dismissed if parties likely to be affected by relief prayed for are absent, unless there are specific reasons for non-impleadment.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Discrimination - Equality - Wrong orders contrary to law - Equality under Article 14 is a positive concept - Benefits cannot be claimed by others if illegality was committed for individual or group of individuals - Two wrongs cannot create a right.

Constitution of India, Articles 141 and 226 - Protection in adverse judgment - Court can protect the interest of any person despite an adverse judgment against them.
