Rajasthan Industrial Dispute Rules, 1958, Rules 22-A, 24 and Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Sections 2(b), 17 - An application challenging an ex-parte award can be reviewed by the tribunal after 30 days of its publication.
The review would be a procedural exercise and not a review of merit.
The tribunal is empowered to set aside ex-parte awards and to adjudicate on the application despite the award's finality.
[Paras 8, 11, 14]  B.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17 and Rajasthan Industrial Dispute Rules, 1958, Rule 22-A - The Industrial Disputes Act does not restrict the filing of a challenge to an ex-parte award under rule 22-A of the Rules of 1958.
The filing of an application to set aside an ex-parte award published a month ago cannot be construed in reference to the finality of the award.
[Para 4]  C Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Section 2(b) - The term `award' denotes an interim or final determination of any industrial dispute or any question relating to it by a Labour Court, Industrial Tribunal, or National Industrial Tribunal, and encompasses an arbitration award made under section 10-A An ex-parte order cannot be documented as an award.
