Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary Proceedings/Enquiry agaainst a judicial authority - Initiating disciplinary proceedings against an officer discharging judicial or quasi-judicial functions requires close scrutiny and caution to avoid shaking public confidence in the officer’s independence - However, disciplinary action may be taken when the officer’s action indicates a desire to oblige himself or unduly favour any party with illegal motive.
[Para 8] B Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary Proceedings/Stay of Disciplinary Proceedings/Interim stay - In serious cases of misconduct and misbehaviour by a highly responsible authority, it is not appropriate to grant interim stay order on disciplinary proceedings too lightly - The Tribunal should first consider the defence of the other party and then decide on the interim relief after proper application of mind and recording sufficient reasons - Granting interim stay too lightly could lead to difficulty in punishing wrongdoers.
[Para 6] C. Interim Orders/Observations at intrim stagee - Observations made at an interim stage that touch the merits of the matter are not conclusive and should not inform the final decision which must be based on the material before the court and on merits in accordance with the law.
