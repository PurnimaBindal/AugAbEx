Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 226 - Medical reimbursement - "Heart Surgery" - Work-charge employees - A rule stating that work charged employees are only entitled to medical reimbursement when they suffer a medical problem while on duty, is not legally valid as it goes against the idea of a welfare state that takes care of all employees dependent upon State exchequer.
There cannot be an artificial classification that distinguishes between regular, ad-hoc, and workcharge employees for this purpose.
Once it is established that the employee has suffered a medical problem while in government service, the government must reimburse medical expenses.
