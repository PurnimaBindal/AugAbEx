Punjab District Sainik Welfare Officers (State Service Class-I) Rules, 1986, Rules 8 and 19 - Promotion - Anomaly in the rules - There are no promotional avenues for Class II officers to Class I posts, which causes frustration and affects the efficiency of service - Government is directed to amend the rules to provide reasonable opportunities for promotion.

Punjab District Sainik Welfare Officers (State Service Class-I) Rules, 1986, Rule 17 - Appointment - Promotion - Petitioners are qualified enough to discharge the duties of a Class-I officer and should have been considered for appointment to Class-I post by invoking relaxation clause of Rule 17 - Government suggested accordingly.
