Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 39(d) and 309 - West Bengal Services Rules Part 2, Rule 55(4) - Equal pay for equal work - Petitioners who were appointed as LDC and later promoted as UDC are claiming pay parity with Muharrars who have less qualifications - Rule 55(4) only applies to promotions and not to redesignations like that of Muharrars - Redesignation of posts or merger of cadres is in the domain of State and petitioners have no legal grounds to claim the same pay scale as Muharrars.
