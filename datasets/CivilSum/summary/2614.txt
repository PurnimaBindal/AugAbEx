Constitution of India, Article 30(1) - Minority Institution - Right to administer - Disapproval of selection made by minority institution - Reason being non-compliance with Rule 15, which requires consideration of candidates sponsored by Employment Exchange - Adjudged unconstitutional and wrong as any contractual condition that results in minority surrendering its right to establish and govern an institution of its choice is against Article 30 of Constitution - Thus, rejection is completely unlawful and unjustified.
