Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Medical reimbursement - Treatment received in a non-recognized hospital - Objection cannot be sustained in a case where treatment was taken in an urgency or emergency - Patient cannot be expected to wait for permission to turn to a recognized hospital under such conditions - Provisions of medical assistance should be construed liberally - The authority should not adhere to technicalities while dealing with human problems - Authority directed to reimburse amount equal to AIIMS rates.
