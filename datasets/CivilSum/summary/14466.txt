Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 14 - Public Service Commission Rules of Procedure (Kerala), Rule 18(1) - Civil consequences - Reasonable opportunity - The petitioner was offered a job by the Electricity Board based on the advice of the Public Service Commission, but did not receive the appointment order due to a change in residence.
The cancellation of advice without notice visits the candidate with adverse civil consequences.
It is not legal and incumbent on the Public Service Commission to provide the candidate with an opportunity.
The recruiting agency must ensure that the selected candidates are also given an opportunity to be appointed.
