Judicial review cannot be used to challenge matters adjudicated by a Civil Court, as they do not violate fundamental rights but only decide legal rights.
The only remedy against such orders is appeal, as Article 226 of the Constitution does not allow for writ jurisdiction.
[Para 4] B.
If the execution of a judgment and decree has expired, it cannot be enforced through a writ petition as it has become non-existent.
