Punjab Civil Services Rules, Rule 21 - Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Notice - Inequitable recovery - Emoluments - Petitioners who qualified and were selected for training in various courses in 1967 were ordered to be paid full emoluments during their training period by the State.
However, the order was withdrawn without any notice in 1978 and the trainees were directed to repay the payments they had received.
As the trainees had treated the training period as on duty, it was held that their right could not be withdrawn without any notice.
With regard to petitioners who have retired or are at the end of their tenures, it would be highly unfair to initiate recovery at this stage.
[Paras 1 and 6]
