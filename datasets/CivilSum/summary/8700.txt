The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 specify that ad hoc service cannot be taken into consideration for seniority, except if the appointment is in accordance with the rules.
The service rendered by the petitioner after the ad hoc appointment as a lineman cannot be considered for seniority because the initial appointment was not based on selection by the Staff Selection Committee.
The claim for regularizing the service after 20 years is also rejected as highly belated.
Although the petitioner's claim for seniority has been denied, it does not necessarily imply denial of service benefits.
