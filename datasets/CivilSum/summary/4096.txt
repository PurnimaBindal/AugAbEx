The petitioner could not be found guilty of misconduct for refusing to take charge of furniture unless it was one of their assigned duties.
As such, the order passed by the Appellate Authority and Disciplinary Authority punishing the petitioner with a reduction in pay by three stages was quashed.

The petitioner's contention that the denial of promotion was based on disciplinary proceedings or adverse entries in the ACR failed as there is no automatic promotion to the position of Headmaster.
[Para 17] C. The recording of ACR's was found to have no infirmities, though the petitioner was advised to improve their performance, and the contention that insufficient opportunity was provided to rectify defects was not tenable.
The authorities were not required to write a detailed judgment in the order of punishment.
[Paras 16 and 17] D. The vague allegations against the Secretary and Higher authorities did not constitute mala fide.
[Para 17] E. The petitioner's contention that they were entitled to the senior scale was unfounded as there was no rule showing automatic entitlement upon being found fit for selection grade.
