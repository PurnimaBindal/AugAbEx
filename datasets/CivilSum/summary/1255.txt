According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India regarding appointment, in a case where an interview was held in June 2000 and the panel was valid for one year, and until 2004 no representation was filed before the authorities, the petitioner is seen as not vigilant towards his rights, and therefore the petition is dismissed.

Rule 5 of the Calcutta High Court Rules regarding writ petitions states that disclosing full particulars as to the source of information is essential for verification, as this tests the genuineness and authenticity of the allegations and also makes the deponent responsible for them.
If a petition is verified in a casual manner it is liable to be set aside.
