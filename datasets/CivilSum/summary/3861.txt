According to Section 2(b) of the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, action under the Act is similar to a criminal action and requires proof that the contemptuous act was deliberately committed.
If the contemner was unable to comply with the court order due to compelling circumstances, they may not be punished for disobedience.
Merely not taking up an application for stay along with an appeal and dismissing it as not maintainable does not fall under Section 12 of the Contempt of Courts Act.
