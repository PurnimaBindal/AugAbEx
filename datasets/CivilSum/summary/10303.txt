Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-G - Fixed term appointment - Automatic termination - Malafide - The workman's appointment letter of fixed term was issued after their termination, which violated the provisions of Section 25-G Once violation has been determined, the Act doesn't require a specific plea of malafides.
The award of the Labour Court has been reversed and reinstatement has been directed, but the workman is not entitled to full back wages due to not completing 240 days of service.
They will instead receive 30% back wages.
