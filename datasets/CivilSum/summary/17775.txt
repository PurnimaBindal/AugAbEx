The UP Secondary Education Service Commission and Selection Boards Act, 1982, Sections 18, 19 and 9 and Article 226 of the Constitution of India are relevant to the case.
The petitioner, who was appointed as a teacher after another teacher retired, did not receive approval for their appointment and District Inspector of Schools found there was surplus staff at the school.
However, the petitioner could have sought an alternative remedy by approaching the commission and challenging the correctness of the finding by the District Inspector of Schools.
The writ petition is not maintainable because there is a distinction between giving approval for an appointment and sanction of a post.
