According to Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, a workman must complete 240 days of work during a calendar year in order to satisfy the requirements.
It is not necessary for the workman to have been in continuous service for the entire year.
[Para 5]
When considering back wages under Sections 10 and 11-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, eligibility, qualifications, mode of appointment, and nature and length of service are among the factors to be taken into account.
In a case where a workman has only worked for 240 days within one year on daily wages and on different kinds of jobs, the employer should not be responsible for back wages during the periods the workman did not work.
The direction for reinstatement with 50% back wages was held to be unjustified, and the order for back wages was canceled.
[Paras 6 & 8 to 11]
Undue delay in making a reference by the Government in accordance with Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act prejudices the rights of both parties, and the matter should be resolved promptly by the Government.
