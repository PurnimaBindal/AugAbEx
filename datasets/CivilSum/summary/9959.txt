Constitution of India, Article 226 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 22, Rule 4 - Dismissal from service/Legal representatives - While writ proceedings are not subject to provisions of CPC, the principles similar to Order 22 Rule 4, CPC can be applied in some situations - The widow of a dismissed employee has standing to represent her husband and proceed with the petition against his dismissal.

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Police Act, 1861, Section 29 Punishment/Gravity of misconduct/Dismissal from service - Dismissal from service for unauthorised absence from duty is disproportionately severe - An Enquiry Officer recommended regularisation of the absence to extraordinary leave without pay - Despite the delinquent's effective representation and the Enquiry Officer's recommendation, the Punishing Authority ordered dismissal - The order is illegal and subject to be nullified.
