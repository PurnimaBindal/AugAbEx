Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II Part-I - Enquiry - Delay in initiating inquiry won't be a valid reason to quash charge-sheet and departmental proceedings, if the charges are serious and the delinquent is not affected - The petitioner was charged for serious nature allegations running into loss of lakhs of rupees to the Government, for the duration when he worked as Superintending Engineer - He got promotion to Chief Engineer during the proceedings - The Vigilance investigated 12 counts of allegations - Petition dismissed, as the delay was justified and no reason to stop the proceedings.
[Paras 5, 6 and 8]  B. Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II Part-I, Rule 2.2(b) - The rule that prescribes inquiry initiation limit against retired Govt.
Servants is reasonable, and its absence in the case of current employees is not unconstitutional - An existing employee cannot compare with a retired Govt. servant when being investigated - The two are distinct groups.
