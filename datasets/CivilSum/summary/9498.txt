Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Pepsu Road Transport Corporation (Conditions of Appointment and Service) Regulations, 1981, Rule 20 - Punishment - Imposition of multiple punishments for a single minor misconduct is held unjustified - Rule 20 includes penalties under different heads, including stoppage of increments and withholding of wages for the suspension period - When the charge is not grave and only one penalty is imposed, imposing a second penalty for withholding wages is not justified - Multiple penalties can be allowed in appropriate cases - The order of punishment is rightfully quashed, but the authority can pass a fresh order retaining either of the two punishments - Court leaves it open for the authority to decide.
