According to Regulation 60(1)(b) of the Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, the disciplinary authority must form an opinion about whether or not an enquiry is necessary in a particular case.
However, this does not mean that all cases where an employee disputes their liability must result in a full-fledged enquiry.
[Para 5]
Under Regulation 58 of the Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, it is up to the disciplinary authority to decide whether a regular departmental enquiry, as described in the regulation for imposing major penalties, should be held or not.
This discretion cannot be restricted by an interpretation that contradicts the regulation's language.
[Para 5]
Regulation 58 of the Food Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, 1971, states that the disciplinary authority's decision about holding a regular departmental enquiry is discretionary.
This discretion must be used to consider the facts of each case, and if it is misused or used arbitrarily, judicial review may be necessary.
