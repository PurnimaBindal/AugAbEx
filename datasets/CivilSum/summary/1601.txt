Banking Regulation Act, 1949, Section 45(7) - Amalgamation - Notification as to - An individual who was dismissed from Hindustan Commercial Bank and had an appeal pending challenged the dismissal - During the pendency of the appeal, the bank was amalgamated with Punjab National Bank (PNB) through a scheme notified by the Government of India under Section 45(7) - PNB assumed all liabilities, duties, and obligations of Hindustan Commercial Bank - PNB did not take steps to dispose of the appeal, which was found contrary to principles as no enquiry was conducted even on merits - Therefore, a writ petition against the dismissal is valid.
