Constitution of India, Article 226 - Reinstatement - Notional Increments - Pay fixation - Limitation - In a case where Labour Court has directed reinstatement without break in service, petitioner must receive increment based on that period of service.
The award by the Labour Court, published on 26294, is executable and enforceable within a period of 12 years.
Respondents are directed to refix the salary of the petitioner to include periodical increments that would have been earned during previous employment.
