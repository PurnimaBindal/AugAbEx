As per Constitution of India, Article 309 and Andhra Pradesh Transport Services Rules, Rule 3(a), appointment of Regional Transport Officer from Superintendents in the Office of State Transport Authority as Section Officers in the Secretariat is not against Presidential Order of 1975, Para 5(2).

According to Constitution of India, Articles 141, 215 and 226, a Full Bench reference can be made only in cases where there is per incurium, i.e. omission to consider a binding precedent of same or superior court on same issue or where any statute was not considered while deciding that issue.
Mere request for referring a case to larger Bench is not enough.
A decision by two judges is-binding on another coordinate Bench of two judges unless it is proved that it is not laying down the correct law due to any subsequent changes in law or decision.
