The Central Bank of India Officers Regulations, 1979, Regulation 19 is being challenged by petitioners under articles 14, 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India due to respondents being selected for the position of Assistant Internal Auditor and Assistant Godown Inspector, which constitutes a separate cadre.
The petitioners are claiming promotion and seniority at the same level as the respondents as both at one point belonged to the same clerical staff.
However, the respondents took a calculated risk and joined training for the post of Internal Auditor which proved to be beneficial, while the petitioners refused to join the training voluntarily as they found the job too tasking.
As a result, the bank's refusal to grant their demand cannot be arbitrary, illegal or unconstitutional.
