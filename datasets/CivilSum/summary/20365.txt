Constitution of India, Article 311 - Misconduct/Punishment - Proof of misconduct - The decision on accepting illegal gratification based on preponderance of probabilities and entire evidence on record cannot be deemed baseless - The punishing authority is responsible for determining the appropriate punishment in light of the findings of misconduct - Proven misconduct from earlier service period is not discounted, even if no allegations arise about the employee's conduct - Dismissal from service is a valid punishment in this case.
