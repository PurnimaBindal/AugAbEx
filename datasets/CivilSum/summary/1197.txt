The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - An electricity board allowed its employees to participate in a selection process for a part-time course, but only a limited number of candidates were permitted to join based on a formula dividing 15% of headquarters' strength by four - This led to higher-merit candidates being deprived of admission - The decision was deemed against Article 16 and the reasoning for only considering candidates at headquarters was found to be without justification - All candidates who could attend without jeopardizing their service duties should be allowed to participate, as the course was part-time and scheduled for the evening at 6 pm.
