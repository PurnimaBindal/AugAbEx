Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II - Pension claim denied due to insufficient qualifying service - Service before reinstatement after an award not counted - Only regular service considered - State of Punjab issued a circular dated 31889 and 16788 instructing to include all work charge for retrial benefits after regularization of service - Court held that deduction of period from 19879 to 24495 is illegal and unwarranted according to the Apex Court's decision in Mohan Lal's case - Workman is deemed to be in continuous service after termination is declared invalid - Court ordered to release pension with 6% interest for delayed payment.
