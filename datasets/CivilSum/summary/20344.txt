Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 32 - Indian Police Feeder Services - Notification dated 31.12.97 - Similar benefits to be granted retrospectively to All India Service officers - Petitioners instructed to file detailed representation to the Central Government.
[Para 4] The petitioners are Indian Police Service officers who were promoted from the Delhi and Andaman Nicobar Police Services, which are feeder services to the IPS.
Under Article 32 of the Constitution of India, the petitioners sought to have the Notifications dated 31-12-97 quashed and for similarly situated officers to receive benefits from 29th April, 1985.
The Court had already disposed of a similar writ petition filed by the Tamil Nadu Administrative Services Officers Association.
