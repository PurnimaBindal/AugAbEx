Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and laches - According to the article, a party must approach the Court within six months, or at most one year, from the date of the cause of action; otherwise, the Court must refuse to exercise its powers under Article 226 for relief - Courts should not entertain writ petitions filed after a delay of several years as it unsettles settled matters - A writ petition filed seven years after the cause of action is time barred.
