According to Section 34 of the Specific Relief Act, 1960, a declaratory decree cannot be executed as it does not direct the judgment-debtor to do anything.
It merely declares the status of the parties.
The plaintiff cannot claim arrears of salary without discharging his duties, even if he obtained a declaratory decree that he is assumed to be continuing in service.
Additionally, the plaintiff did not claim any consequential relief in the initial decree.
