Admission/Education - Eligibility clause for admission to a one year B.Ed.
course requires a minimum 40% marks in the qualifying BA examination - The court held that the same clause would apply to admission to the B.Ed.
Correspondence Course - To determine eligibility, fractions of percentage marks like 05 must be disregarded - The petitioner achieved 3954% in BA and was treated as if she had received 40% marks, and thus became eligible for admission to B.Ed.
