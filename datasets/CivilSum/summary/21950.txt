Constitution of India, Article 136 - Appellate order - Writ petition - Maintainability of - A writ petition filed against an order in appeal against interim order in the suit may be heard by the High Court.
The stay of the order under challenge should be continued until the writ petition is heard.
The High Court will decide if a writ petition should have been entertained and also consider the appellant's objection.
After hearing the appellant, the High Court will determine what interim order, if any, should be passed.
