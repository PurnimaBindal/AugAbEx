Rajasthan University Ordinance, Ordinance 278C, Ordinance 278E - Examination - Post Graduate Medical Courses - Must complete three-year training - Postgraduate students taking the exam must complete a mandatory three-year training, and they cannot finish it even if the examination results have not been released.
The University has not given any instructions to revoke the results or degrees of postgraduate students who have not completed the training before the examination commenced.
