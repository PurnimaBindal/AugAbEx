Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Writ jurisdiction/Education - Delaying Post-Graduate Examination - The university holds the power to decide concerning the holding of Post Graduate Examination – The University or its Faculty Council is the sole body authorized to fix the time table for examination - It is a matter that involves academics or academic discipline - The Court may only intervene with those decisions that seem arbitrary or against some statutory provision of law.
[Paras 12, 13, 14 & 18]
