Constitution of India, Article 14 - Displaced employee - Alternate appointment - Eligibility - Technical test - The respondent, as part of a policy to absorb displaced employees, was permitted to take a technical test to determine their eligibility.
However, the respondent failed the test, and did not contest the validity of the test or its outcome.
The HC erroneously directed the Corporation to appoint the respondent to a suitable position, despite their failure in the test.
It was held that, due to the unique circumstances, the Corporation should be provided an opportunity to retake the test.
The appeal was accepted.
