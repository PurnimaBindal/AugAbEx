The Rajasthan Higher Judicial Service Rules, 1969 and Rajasthan Legal (State and Subordinate Service) Rules, 1981, Rules 6(b)(iii) and 41 as well as Articles 14, 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India state that the post of legal remembrancer should be filled by suitable officers from the legal service.
However, since there were no suitable officers available, members of the Rajasthan Higher Judicial Service were appointed as Joint Legal Remembrancers.
These appointments were deemed proper and in compliance with Article 50 of the Constitution.
