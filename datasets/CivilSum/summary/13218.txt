According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, army service may be counted towards the grant of advance increments and pension.
The petitioner in this case had served in the army for over 10 years before being appointed as Assistant Secretary in Zila Sainik Board on November 2nd, 1982, and being promoted to Secretary on January 9th, 1996.
The court had accepted the claim of another person similar to the petitioner, and therefore, the respondent was directed to count the petitioner's army service towards the grant of advance increments and pension.
