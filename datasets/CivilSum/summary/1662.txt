Constitution of India, Article 226 - Appeal - Division Bench ruling - If one writ petition has its order appealed and reversed, it will also reverse the order of the other writ petition with a similar ruling, even if there was no appeal made for that case.
[Para 10] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 215 and 226 - Appeal - Writ jurisdiction - Orders issued under Article 226 cannot be the subject of another petition under the same article and must be appealed instead.
