Constitution of India, Article 226 - Andhra Pradesh (Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and Backward Classes) Regulation of Issue of Community Certificate Act, 1993 Section 9 - Cancellation of caste certificate- The petitioner failed to provide any proof that he belongs to the scheduled tribe 'Mannevarulu'.
He neither provided any evidence nor independent witnesses from his village.
The petitioner obtained his caste certificate from Tehsildar, Secunderabad despite not being a resident or student there.
As a result, he could not prove that he belongs to the scheduled tribe and his certificate is genuine.
The respondent acted properly in canceling his caste certificate.
[Paras 7, 8, 9 and 10]
