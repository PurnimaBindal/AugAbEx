Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Information Brochure of Entrance Examination for BE Course, Clause 131 - Merit - Selection to Engineering Courses - The merit in the Entrance Exam is the sole basis for selection of candidates in the various subjects of the BE Course, whether open or reserved - The allocation of subjects to candidates based on their options must be directly linked to their overall merit in the Entrance Exam - The selection cannot be based on the comparative or inter se merit of the candidate in any particular subject or with respect to any option given - The petition was dismissed.
