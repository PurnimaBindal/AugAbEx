Constitution of India, Article 226 - Retrenchment - Exercise of option - Delay - Appellants who were retrenched after being absorbed in the respondent corporation on giving of option cannot claim parity with other employees who accepted the service conditions.
They had received terminal benefits and cannot maintain their petition after nearly 10 years by taking plea of repeated representations.
The appeal has been dismissed accordingly.
