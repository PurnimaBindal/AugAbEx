Constitution of India, Article 226 - Pensionary Benefits - Revision of - The petitioner, who was appointed as Master on 25662 and retired from service on 31186, was granted selection grade w.e.f.
1472 in an earlier petition filed by the petitioner.
The respondents were directed to release pensionary benefits to the petitioner based on the selection grade and the last pay drawn or deemed to have been drawn by the petitioner.
However, arrears were restricted to only 38 months prior to the date of the petitioner's retirement.
