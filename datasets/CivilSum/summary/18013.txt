Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Limited promotion from class IV to class III - Terminology - Government directions permit promotion from class IV to class III (Clerks etc.) based on qualifications - The use of the phrase "Clerks etc." encompasses different class III positions and not just Clerks - There is no justification for confining the instructions to Clerks alone - Petitioners are eligible to be promoted to any class III position based on their qualification.
