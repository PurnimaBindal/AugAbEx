Respondent Ritu Bahri, AAG, Haryana, violated Article 311 of the Constitution of India by illegally terminating the services of the petitioner.
The petitioner, who was appointed Leader Bhajan Party in the Public Relations Department of Haryana, was entitled to retire at the age of 60 years, but his services were terminated at the age of 58.
As a result, the petitioner is to be considered retired at the age of 60 and is entitled to salary for the two-year period, provided he files an affidavit confirming that he was not gainfully employed during that time.
[Paras 5 and 6]
