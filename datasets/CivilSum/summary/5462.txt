Kurukshetra University Calendar, Volume 1, 1997, Clause 3 of Ordinance XX - Voluntary retirement - Change of date of voluntary retirement - A petition was submitted for voluntary retirement with a proposed date of retirement as 122000, and was accepted - However, a later application was made for a change of date, which was rejected - It was held that a University teacher is generally allowed to withdraw their letter of voluntary retirement, and that the Competent Authority cannot withhold approval without providing a valid reason.
