After promotion from the feeder cadre, seniority in the Assistant cadre is linked with the passing of the Assistant Grade Examination, as per the Punjab State Assistant Grade Examination Rules, 1984.
If an individual passes the test in any of the first five attempts, they keep their seniority in the feeder cadre, and those who pass in attempts six through ten obtain seniority from their appointment as an Assistant.
If a person passes the test in the first five chances and is appointed to an available Assistant position, they will join the cadre with their seniority from their feeder cadre appointment.
If a person passes the test after their first five chances, they will get seniority on their appointment as an Assistant.
Those who pass the test in the first five attempts and are appointed as an Assistant get their seniority from their feeder cadre appointment.
The person appointed by promotion when no posts are available is appointed based on seniority from those who passed the test, regardless of the number of chances they took.
