Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 300 - Pension Regulations, Regulation 132 - Pension - Re-employment - An individual who has completed at least 15 years of service is eligible for pension - There is no provision that excludes the period of service rendered by a sepoy on re-employment only because their enrollment was found to be irregular - The petitioner has served for more than 16 years - The petitioner's entitlement to pension cannot be denied based on the period of service put in by them from the re-employment until their discharge.
