According to Rajasthan Services Rules, 1951, Rule 244(1), voluntary retirement must be allowed without withholding retiral benefits.
Charge-sheets issued after allowing voluntary retirement cannot be used to make the rule ineffective or infringe upon the employee's right.
In situations where the employee is under suspension or departmental proceedings are pending or contemplated, the competent authority may withhold permission to voluntary retirement but cannot withhold retiral benefits once voluntary retirement is allowed.
