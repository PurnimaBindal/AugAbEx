Haryana State Supply and Marketing Corporative Service (Common Cadre) Rules, 1988, Rules 4(a), 23 and 24 - Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Challenge to show cause notice to withdraw promotion - Administrative Committee resolved to promote petitioner as Additional General Manager (Finance and Accounts) but later resolved to obtain ex-post facto approval for relaxation of qualification from Registrar - Registrar issued show cause notice which was subsequently withdrawn - Another show cause notice issued, but since the decision of the Administrative Committee had been approved with withdrawal of show cause notice, promotions could not be tinkered with by any subsequent clarification - Show cause notice was quashed.
