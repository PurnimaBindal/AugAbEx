Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Rule 7 of the Haryana State Electricity Board Employees (Punishment and Appeal) Regulations, 1990 pertains to natural justice in departmental proceedings.
Non-supply of the enquiry report with the show cause notice after enquiry amounts to an infringement of principles of natural justice; however, the provision of compliance of principles of natural justice is inherent even if not specifically mentioned.
While proceedings need not be set aside for mere non-supply of enquiry report, if serious prejudice is shown, the punishment order may be set aside by the court, and parties can proceed afresh after supplying a copy of the enquiry report.
No mechanical action should be taken to set aside proceedings on the mere asking on the grounds of non-supply of the enquiry report.
[Paras 6 & 7]
