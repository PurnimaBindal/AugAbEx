Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 226 - Promotion - Appointments - Seniority - Qualifications - Instructs that the seniority of Masters/Mistresses should be determined based on the date of passing the B.Ed./D.P.Ed.
examination - PTIs who had completed DPE qualifications before joining government service can also be considered for appointment as DPEs - However, these instructions cannot be used to promote someone over a more senior colleague unless the senior is found to be ineligible or not fit for promotion.
