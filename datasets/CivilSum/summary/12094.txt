The Common Coal Cadre Rules, Clauses 4.10.1, 2 and 3, state different promotion provisions for different cadres and the principle of Article 14 is not violated under Articles 14 and 226 of the Indian Constitution.
[Para 6]
When promotion procedure is stated in the Rules, policy decisions cannot ignore the same.
The Court will not interfere unless the decision is in conflict with the rules.
