Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab Service of Engineers Class II PWD (Building and Roads Branch) Rules, 1965, Rule 6(2) - Seniority - Direct recruits - If eligible candidates are not available from source No. (4), which includes Sectional Officers, Draftsmen and Tracers etc., the direct recruits will fill those positions - Therefore, petitioners from source No. (4) cannot claim seniority against those posts.
