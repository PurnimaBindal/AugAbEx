The termination of the Government Pleader's service took place solely on the basis of a report by the District Officer, following an order made in a bail application where the case had to be adjourned several times due to the lack of a post-mortem report.
However, the order was passed without looking at the complete record, which demonstrated that the Government Pleader was not responsible for the issue.
By ignoring the relevant documents on record, the order indicated that the decision-maker did not apply their mind to the matter.
As a result, the impugned order ought to be quashed.
