The appellant, who was working as a Junior Engineer, lost his arm and fingers in a suicide attempt.
His disability was assessed at 100%, but he was allowed to continue in service to complete 25 years of qualifying service for pensionary benefits.
However, his performance was rated as "below average" or "average" during his service, and his premature retirement order after completing 25 years is not illegal.
There is no justification for interfering with the second appeal in this case.
