Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II - Family Pension Scheme, 1964 (as applicable in Haryana), Rule 4 - Family pension - Qualifying service - Dependents of a deceased Government employee are entitled to family pension even if the employee did not complete one year of service, under the condition that they were medically fit at the time of appointment - The rule does not require one year's qualifying service - Smt. Savitri Devi's case in 1996(2) RSJ was used as reference.
