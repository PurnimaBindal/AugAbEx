The scope of interference in the Award of the Labour Court in exercise of writ jurisdiction of certiorary is very limited under Constitution of India, Article 226 and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 25-F.
Unless the award suffers from jurisdictional error or is vitiated in law on the face of the record, there is no scope of interference.
The single judge committed a serious error in quashing the award without returning any such findings.

The workman was reinstated by way of a settlement with continuity of service but again terminated after completing 240 days on reinstatement after earlier termination.
Under Constitution of India, Article 226 and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 25-F, the mere fact that subsequent termination was made before again completing 240 days does not mean he did not complete 240 service so as to disentitle him from the benefits of Section 25-F [Para 12]
