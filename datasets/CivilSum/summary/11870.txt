HSEB Employees (P&A) Regulations, 1980, Regulation 5 - Natural justice - Departmental enquiry - Stoppage of two annual increments with cumulative effect is considered a major penalty that cannot be imposed without following the proper procedure.
Therefore, the punishment order is quashed and can be reissued.
[Para 5]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Natural justice - Appeal - The authority deciding the departmental appeal/representation/review against the punishment order must provide a detailed explanation of the reasons behind its conclusion.
In this case, the appeal and review were dismissed without proper reasoning, leading to the orders being quashed.
[Paras 5 and 6]
