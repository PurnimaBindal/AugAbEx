Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 162 - Government Instructions - Advertised posts meant for general category - Reservation of posts for Ex-servicemen - The Board is required to comply with the instructions of the Government.
If candidates from the Ex-servicemen category were not available, the posts should have been re-advertised.
The appointments made beyond the initially advertised number were bad in law and quashed.
The diversion of posts meant for Ex-servicemen to the general category does not entitle the petitioner to any relief.
The vacancies that have arisen because of the resignation of candidates belonging to the Ex-servicemen category may be offered to candidates from the same category, including petitioner, on the basis of merit.
[Paras 3 and 4]
