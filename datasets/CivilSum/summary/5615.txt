The State Government has the power to relax the teaching experience requirements for appointment as an Assistant Professor in the Eye Department, and is bound to fill at least one vacancy with a person from a reserved category in accordance with the Reservation Act, despite a shortfall of 23 days of teaching experience.
[Para 11]
The Bihar Reservation of Vacancies in Posts and Services Act requires the Government to fill at least one vacancy with a person from a reserved category, even with a shortfall in teaching experience that can be relaxed.
The State Government is authorized to condone a shortfall of 23 days in teaching experience.
