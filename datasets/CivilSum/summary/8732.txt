Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dismissal - Labour Court had reinstated a workman into their service with continuity of service, but awarded stoppage of one increment with cumulative effect and no back wages - Petitioner contended that the Labour Court could not impose two penalties simultaneously under Section 11A of the Industrial Disputes Act - The award of the Labour Court constituted double jeopardy - However, the workman would not be deprived of the increment with cumulative effect as ordered by the Labour Court, and the award of the Labour Court was partially set aside and modified.
