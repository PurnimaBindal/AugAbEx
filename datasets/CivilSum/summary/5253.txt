According to the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 Sections 2(n), 4(6), 22, 23 and 24, if an employee is not at fault for a strike, lock-out, or cessation of work and it does not contravene Sections 22 to 24 of the Act, the workman will be entitled to gratuity for that period.
