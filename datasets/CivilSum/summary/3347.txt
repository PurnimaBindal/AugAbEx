As per Section 30 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 and Rule 20 of the Workmen's Compensation Rules, 1924, a workman who has had an accident while on duty with a truck is entitled to compensation.
The vehicle in question was insured, and the insurance company had taken extra premiums to cover all the liabilities under the Act.
The court held that the insurance company could not refuse to reimburse the penalty amount.
