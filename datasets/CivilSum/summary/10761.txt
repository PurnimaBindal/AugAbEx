Under Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Army Instructions 84/68, Paragraph 3(b), a petitioner was denied arrears of pay and allowances related to a retrospective promotion due to a reliance on Paragraph 3(b) that pay and allowances may only be granted after promotion has been notified in the Gazette of India.
Although the respondents waived off certain requirements for promotion, the petitioner was still denied.
The court held that the petitioner was entitled to both seniority and pay and allowances in the promoted post as there was no fault on the part of the petitioner and the waiver of requirements by the respondents acknowledged their own mistake.
