Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Merit List - Waiting list - Appointment by preference to elder candidate - The preference on the basis of the age of an elder candidate out of the quota of those holding equal merit cannot be considered arbitrary.
However, if one of two equal merit holders is overlooked in favor of the elder candidate for advertised vacancies that are still open, then the other candidate has the right to demand appointment on the basis of equal treatment.
The authorities have been instructed to fill the remaining vacancies by granting appointment to the petitioner.
