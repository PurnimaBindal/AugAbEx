According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, discrimination in the payment of medical allowance to teachers in private primary schools is not permissible.
The respondents in this case, who work in private Government recognised and grant in aid primary schools, were denied payment of medical allowance while teachers in other similar private schools received it.
The appellant argued that the government resolution did not mention payment of medical allowance to teachers in private primary schools.
However, the court held that this was an act of hostile discrimination and directed the issue of a similar government resolution regarding payment of medical allowance to the respondents.
