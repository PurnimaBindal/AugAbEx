Constitution of J&K, Article 124 - CCA Rules, 1956, Rule 31 - J&K Prevention of Corruption Act, 2006, Section 5(2) - Publicmen and Public Servants Declaration of Assets Act, 1983, Sections 12 and 14 - Suspension - Stay - Claim for - The appellant was suspended pending investigation by the vigilance organization regarding allegations of accumulated disproportionate assets - The order was challenged for being in violation of CCA Rule, Rule 31 - Rule 31 itself grants the competent authority the power to place Govt. servants under suspension - The Govt. has given instructions for the guidance of competent authorities while exercising such power provided thereunder - In cases of misconduct for possessing assets disproportionate to known sources of income, the Govt. has issued instructions supplementing Rule 31 of CCA Rules - However, instructions cannot have the same status as statutory provisions, and cannot supplant them as alleged by the appellant - No grounds for staying suspension - Petition dismissed.
