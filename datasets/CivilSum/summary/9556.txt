Constitution of India, Article 226 - Service Rules of Employees of Eastern Coal Fields Ltd, Rule 34.2 - Payment of Gratuity Act, Section 14 - Departmental proceedings - Although an employee may retire due to superannuation, departmental proceedings do not terminate - Rule supplements Section 14 of Gratuity Act - When an employee has already retired, the employer must promptly conclude departmental proceedings.
