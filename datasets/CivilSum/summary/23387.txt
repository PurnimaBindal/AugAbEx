According to Legal Remembrancer's Manual Chapter V, appointing Law Officers on fixed or contractual terms without considering the merit of other candidates is arbitrary.
These appointments made purely based on personal or political connections must not complain if they face an arbitrary termination.
Those who get appointed through backdoor entry should be shown the same door especially when their contract agreement is terminable at any time.
The appointment of Brief Holders/Law Officers on a contractual basis cannot claim equal protection with those appointed based on a fair selection procedure.
[Paras 19 and 20] B Legal profession is a service-oriented job and the relationship between the lawyer and client is of trust and confidence.
The client engages the lawyer for personal reasons, and the lawyer is not an agent but a dignified spokesperson.
The lawyer can choose the facts to be presented but must be fair to ensure justice between the parties and duty towards the court.
[Paras 14 and 15] C. According to Articles 12 and 14 of the Constitution of India, every appointment made by public bodies does not receive public approval; they must select the most meritorious persons with a fair procedure.
