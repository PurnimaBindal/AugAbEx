Constitution of India, Articles 341 and 342 - Constitution (Schedule Caste) Order, 1950 - Claim for - Schedule Caste status - Petitioner's parents belonging to Chamar community in Uttar Pradesh but moved to Gujarat - Petitioner grew up in Gujarat - Rejected for admission on reserved Scheduled caste seat - Article 341 empowers the President to specify the castes to be deemed as Schedule Caste in a particular State - Petitioner's community status in Gujarat not relevant - Permanent residence of parents has special significance at the time of notification of Presidential order - Petition rejected accordingly.
