Constitution of India, Articles 14, 19(1)(c), 19(4), 31(2) and 213 - Bihar Private Educational Institutions (Taking Over) Act, 1987 - Nationalisation of Private Institutions - If nationalisation is done in phases, it is not possible to take over all the institutions simultaneously - Phased nationalisation means that the objective of nationalisation will be taken over gradually - Therefore, the question of singling out a particular institution and treating it as a separate class does not arise, as all private educational institutions defined in Section 2(a) of the Act will be nationalised in phases as per the Act and Ordinances.
