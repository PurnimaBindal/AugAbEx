Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - ACRs - Criminal proceedings and departmental proceedings - Remark support - There exists a difference between criminal and departmental proceedings - The burden of proof required to establish guilt in a criminal trial is far greater than to establish charges in a departmental inquiry - Beyond any reasonable doubt, a criminal charge must be proved, whereas the recording of ACRs only requires the reporting officer's subjective satisfaction.
[Paras 5 & 7]  B Constitution of India, Article 14 - ACRs - Purpose of ACRs - The aim is to bring deficiencies in the official's attention and to require them to be addressed - If the official finds the reporting officer's opinion incorrect or biased, they are given an opportunity to appeal against it.
