The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, especially Sections 2(oo), 25-F, 25-G, and 25-H, deal with the termination of services of an apprentice who could not complete the training within one year.
In such cases, the period of training is usually extended until completion, and upon completion, the apprentice is relieved.
However, such training should not be considered employment, and as such, there is no violation of the provisions of Section 25-A of the ID Act.
