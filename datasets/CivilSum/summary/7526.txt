Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(b) - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 1638 and 161 - Dismissal from service - For dispensing with the holding of a departmental inquiry, the conclusion that an inquiry was not reasonably practicable must be recorded and supported by relevant material on the record.
A vague conclusion is not adequate, and a dismissal order without sufficient material and reasons is a breach of natural justice.
[Paras 6 to 9]
