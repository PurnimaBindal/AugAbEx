According to Section 83 of the Kerala University Act, 1974, Section 83 of the Calicut University Act, 1975, and Section 100 of the Mahatma Gandhi University Act, 1985, teachers of colleges under corporate management that are affiliated to the Kerala University can be transferred within the university.
However, the State Government cannot create statutes during the existence of the University Senate, as there cannot be two parallel authorities making subordinate legislation on the same subject matter.
