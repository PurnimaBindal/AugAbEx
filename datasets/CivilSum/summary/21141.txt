UP University Act, 1973 Section 31(8)(a) says that Executive Council/Appointing authority disagreed with Selection Committee regarding selection to the post of Professor Ancient History.
But the decision of Vice-Chancellor is final.
Vice-Chancellor remitted the matter of appointment to Executive Committee for reconsideration and reconstituted the committee by nomination.
In principle, there is no problem with Chancellor's decision in not deciding but remitting matter to Executive Council.
