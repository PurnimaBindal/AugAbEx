As per the Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Sections 48, 40, 28 and 30; cognizance of an offence can be taken only if a complaint is filed by an affected person or recognised union.
A complaint was filed by a union that was not recognised and hence, the prosecution launched against the appellant should be quashed.
The Labour Court erred in taking cognizance in utter breach of Section 39 of the Act, as provisions of this section are mandatory and the court has no jurisdiction to take cognizance of the offence.
[Paras 15, 16 & 17]
