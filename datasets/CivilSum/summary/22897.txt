Constitution of India, Article 14 - Andhra Pradesh Panchayat Samithis and Zilla Parishads Ministerial Service Rules, 1965 Rule 4, Proviso (as amended) - Seniority - The amended proviso of Rule 4, through G.O.No.
822/22-8-1977, ensures protection only to those who passed the Account Test before 7-8-1967 and were regularised and promoted to higher posts by 22-8-1977 - Those who did not pass the test by the stipulated date, and were not regularised and promoted, will not get seniority over those who did pass - Passing the Account Test does not necessarily mean being regularised as Upper Division Clerks.
[Paras 29, 31 and 32]
