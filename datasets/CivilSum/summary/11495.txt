Punjab Police Rules, Rules 1628, 1638 (as applicable to Haryana Police) - Departmental proceedings - Dismissal - District Magistrate's approval not taken - Service length disregarded - All witnesses testified that no altercation occurred - Decided, according to law from State of Punjab v.
