Punjab State Electricity Board Services (Premature Retirement Regulations) 1982 - Compulsory Retirement/Adverse Confidential Reports - Adverse remarks - The petitioner was promoted as Chief Engineer and seeks to avoid premature retirement on the basis of adverse remarks passed before the promotion - Adverse remarks recorded prior to the promotion cannot be taken into consideration.
[Paras 4 and 5]

Punjab State Electricity Board Services (Premature Retirement Regulation) 1982 - Natural Justice - Opportunity of hearing - Evidence - Proof - Departmental Enquiry - Despite a telephonic conversation being recorded on a video cassette tape containing some incriminating proof about the acceptance of illegal gratification, no departmental enquiry was held nor opportunity given to meet with the allegations - Premature retirement is stigmatic not simpliciter - Such material cannot be considered for the purpose of premature retirement.
[Para 6]
