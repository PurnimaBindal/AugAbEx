According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, equal pay must be provided for equal work.
Therefore, when a Junior Engineer is asked to perform the duties of an Assistant Engineer, they must be paid the pay scale of an Assistant Engineer.
This includes receiving increments in that scale and being eligible for the senior scale after completing the required qualifying service.
In a recent case, the petitioner was ordered to be paid the senior scale of Assistant Engineer (Rs. 3700-5300) from the date they completed 8 years of service and all accompanying increments.
The petition was granted with costs.
