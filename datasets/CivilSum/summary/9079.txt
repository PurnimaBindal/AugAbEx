Civil Procedure Code, Section 34, Order 21, Rules 30 & 58 - Interest - Execution - Executing court - Jurisdiction - The court that executes a decree cannot grant any relief beyond what was granted in the original decree; this principle is supported by AIR 1990 SC 2177.
[Para 4]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 21, Rules 30 and 58 - Retirement/Reinstatement/Back wages - If an employer changes an employee's date of birth in their records to force retirement without providing an opportunity to the employee, and the action is deemed invalid, the employee is entitled to full back wages and benefits for the period in question, regardless of whether or not they worked during that time.
The principle of "no work, no pay" does not apply in this situation.
