State Bank of India Officer Service Rules - Constitution of India, Article 16 - Transfer of Bank employees - It is not legally or rightfully permissible for anyone in public employment to demand a specific posting location - Transfers from one place to another are not only a characteristic of service, but in fact, a condition of service, and transfer orders are necessary for the efficiency of public administration and in the interest of the public - When the bank-employer deems such transfer necessary for the smooth management of the bank, it is not within the rights of the petitioner to question the decision.
