Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion - Employees do not have an inherent right to promotion, but they can make a claim for consideration when their turn comes.
In cases where a promotion committee has considered the employee's case but found them unfit for promotion, they can be superseded by their junior.
If an employee's bad service record was considered as a reason for ignoring his promotion in favor of his junior, they cannot complain.
[Paras 7 and 8]
Efficiency bar - Promotion - The criteria for crossing the efficiency bar differs from the parameters of promotion.
[Para 8]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 226 - Delay and laches - Promotion - The cause of action for promotion cases arises when the junior employee got promoted, considering both, 23 years ago.
The petition can be dismissed on the grounds of delay and laches.
