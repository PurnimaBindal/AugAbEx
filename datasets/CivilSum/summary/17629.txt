Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Admission - Degree course of Bachelor of Architecture/Planning - Petitioner's original application form for admission to the course shows that he provided all necessary information about his qualifying exam - He claims that he included all certificates with the form - Despite the fact that he took the combined entrance test, his application for admission was rejected due to the lack of certified copies of the certificates being attached to the application form - Since other candidates with lower scores have already been admitted, excluding petitioner from consideration is unjust and unfair - Some seats are still available to be filled, and interviews for these seats will be conducted - Respondents have been instructed to allow petitioner to attend the interview, provided he produces the necessary certificates for review by the authorities.
