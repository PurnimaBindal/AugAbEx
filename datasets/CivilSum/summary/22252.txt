Constitution of India, Article 226 - Central Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19 - Jurisdiction of Tribunals/Judicial review/Scope of interference at introlocutory stage - Disciplinary inquiry - The Tribunal or the Court can only interfere in cases where no misconduct or irregularity has been demonstrated on the charges framed, or if the charges themselves are in violation of the law - However, the correctness of the charges cannot be examined by the Tribunal or the Court before the disciplinary proceedings have concluded - Only procedural irregularities can be examined during judicial review, and it is important to note that judicial review is not an appeal - [Paras 4 to 7]
