Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Entitlement to arrears of pay for retrospective promotion - Arrears for period from 1970 to 1979 - Petitioner worked on promoted post without pay from 1970 to 1975 - If employee is denied promotion for no fault of their own and is deprived of pay for the promoted post, arrears cannot be denied on retrospective promotion.
