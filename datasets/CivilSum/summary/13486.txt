Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Civil Services (General & Common Conditions of Service) Rules, 1994, Rule 17 - Selection - Qualifications - Knowledge of Punjabi language as prescribed in the Rule of Matriculation standard or equivalent to that cannot be overstretched as Matriculation examination with Punjabi only - Selection Board cannot reject the candidature only on the ground of requirement of passing Matriculation examination with Punjabi - Knowledge of a candidate must be tested at the time of interview only - Petitioner passed Punjabi Parbodh examination conducted by the Language Department of the State, which must be deemed to be equivalent to matriculation - Board directed to consider her case for appointment in accordance with her merit secured in interview and if services of any less meritorious candidate is required to be dispensed with, it should be in accordance with the rules of natural justice.
