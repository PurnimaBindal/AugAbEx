Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sec. 2 - Employer - Deemed employee - Contract - Labour - Workers engaged through Contractor - The employees engaged by a contractor working for an establishment will be deemed as the establishment's employees even if they are paid by the contractor.
[Para 8]
Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sec. 2A - Establishment - Departments and branches of an establishment - Closure of a branch - Determining functional integrality between two or more units - The survival of the unit/ establishment on closure of a branch or department requires all relevant tests to be considered and applied - The principal dominant test is to determine if the remaining units could survive on the closure of the other units and if the units were simply agents of the remaining establishment.
