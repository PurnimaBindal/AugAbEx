Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 37 and 39(d) - Equal pay for equal work - Regularisation - Part-time Community Employees of Community Service Department engaged for teaching job - Petitioners are being paid a consolidated salary of Rs. 500/- per month while regular community employees performing the same job receive a regular pay scale of Rs. 950-1500, and 1350-2200 for working six hours a day - Court directs to grant petitioners the same pay scale - Further directed to accommodate the petitioners on regular/basis based on their seniority and eligibility.
