The appellant was selected for the post of Deputy Director agriculture and short-listed, but UPSC resorted to a different method of short-listing than what was prescribed in paragraph 31.
The court cannot add words to the advertisement and must read it as it is, so the UPSC cannot use any other method of short-listing.
High Court's judgment is set aside.
[Paras 23 and 24]   B.
When a particular method of short-listing has been mentioned in paragraph 31, the UPSC cannot resort to any other method of short-listing.
[Para 23]   C. Valid short-listing involves two requirements: firstly, it must have a rational and objective basis, and secondly, if a method of short-listing has been prescribed, then it must be followed.
