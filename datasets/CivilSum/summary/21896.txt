Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2-A(2) - Removal from service - Dishonesty in performance of duty - A Bus Conductor was found guilty of issuing tickets of 050 paise denomination instead of Rs. 4/- to 16 passengers - The management proved that it was a case of misconduct and therefore removed him from service - Even though it was not an accidental incident, the fact that he was not caught earlier does not minimize the misconduct - Management's decision was not interfered with.
