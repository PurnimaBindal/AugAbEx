Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Seniority/Correction in seniority/Delay - The Government can correct seniority if a mistake is found, even if there has been some delay in doing so.
If the Government's decision is factually correct, the court cannot interfere with it on the basis of delay alone.
However, a delay may be a bar to granting relief by the court, but not when the Government corrects its own mistake.
[Para 7]

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Seniority/Speaking order - If the factual position regarding seniority is not disputed, the Government is not required to provide a judgment when deciding the seniority or a representation.
