Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 17-B - Regularization of services for daily wagers - Respondent appointed as a daily wager Beldar - Labour Court awards regularization of services for petitioner based on parity with two other workers who were absorbed on a regular basis - However, a daily wager does not hold a post unless appointed as per the rules, and therefore does not have a legal right - Engagement of respondent contractual, and his case falls under Section 2(oo)(bb) of the Act - Any parity with two other persons would mean perpetuating an illegality - Order of the Labour Court has been set aside, yet the respondent is entitled to wages under Section 17 - B of the Industrial Disputes Act.
