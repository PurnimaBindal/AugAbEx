Constitution of India, Article 19(1) - Demonstrations - The right to conduct demonstrations on a highway without obstructing others is a fundamental right, but measures to avoid causing injury or annoyance should be taken - Authorities can put reasonable restrictions on procession and street marches - Citizens and pedestrians also have the right to use the highway.

Constitution of India, Articles 19 and 21 - Processions and Demonstrations - Taking out processions that block the entire road violates the fundamental right of pedestrians - There should be some restrictions and limitations on public processions and demonstrations for public interest - Guidelines have been set.
