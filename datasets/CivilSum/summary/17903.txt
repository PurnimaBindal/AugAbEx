Under the Workmen's Compensation Act 1923, Sections 3 and 4, a farm worker who underwent an amputation of his fingers and thumb on one hand due to being crushed by a toka thresher was granted compensation.
The worker was over the age of 15 and earned Rs. 450/- per month.
The Commissioner found a 70% permanent disability through an appreciation of evidence and awarded the claimant Rs. 20,580/- as compensation.
The award was based on the provisions of the Act, and there was no illegality pointed out.
The quantum of compensation was not interfered with and the appeal was dismissed.
