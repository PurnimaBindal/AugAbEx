Regularisation - The appellant, who was previously employed as a daily wage worker in a scheme, was not eligible for regularisation since his employment was tied to the scheme.
Furthermore, the post he worked in ceased to exist due to a lack of funds.
[Paras 8 and 9] B.
Termination - Although the appellant was appointed as a daily wager in a scheme, his job was co-terminus with the scheme.
Furthermore, the post he held had been eliminated due to a lack of funding, and his termination was not considered retrenchment under Section 2(oo) of the ID Act.
Therefore, he could not request reinstatement under Section 25F if the provision had not been followed.
