Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 14 - Apprenticeship Act, 1961 - Completion of Apprenticeship - Employment as a Draftsman - The petitioner had completed a three-year apprenticeship with PWD (B&R) and sought employment as a Draftsman, which was denied due to the lack of rules or instructions for such appointment.
Lower-merit individuals had been employed as Draftsmen.
The High Court determined that although individuals who have undergone apprenticeship training do not have an inherent right to employment with the employer, when that employer is the State, employment should be based strictly on merit.
Ultimately, the petitioner's writ was allowed and the respondents were ordered to employ the petitioner as a Draftsman (Civil) in any department.
