Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion - Denial of - Based on the ground that the petitioner was censured while holding the post of Constable - The petitioner was directly recruited as Head Constable on participating in a fresh selection - The appointment of the petitioner as Head Constable through competition nullified any censure awarded to him while holding the post of Constable - Previous service is taken into account only for pension benefits - Therefore, the petitioner is eligible for promotion.
