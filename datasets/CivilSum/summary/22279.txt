"The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, and 320, govern the regularisation of services.
Petitioners were former field workers for the Filaria Department of the State Government for approximately 14 years.
The posts were required to be filled through the Public Service Commission (PSC), and the candidates were selected based on the recommendations made by the PSC.
The act of regularisation contradicts Articles 320 and is outside the recruitment rules and the statutory process of selection.
The High Court was correct in rejecting the claim for regularisation; however, the Government directed to relax the condition of age limit for any petitioner who had become overage and still applied for the post.
[Paras 2 to 4]"
