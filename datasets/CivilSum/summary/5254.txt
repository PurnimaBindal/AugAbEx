Rajasthan Police Subordinate Service Rules, 1989, Rules 2(r), 26, 28 and 36 - Promotion - When Petitioner passed the promotion cadre course in February, 1993, his substantive appointment relates back to the point he was promoted, instead of his actual appointment date of 2071992 - Therefore, the petitioner is eligible for promotion as his appointment relates back to the quota of 1991-92, which is the year of his selection.
