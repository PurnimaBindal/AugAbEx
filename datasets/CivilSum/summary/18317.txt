The West Bengal Board of Primary Education (Recognition of Primary Teachers' Training Institute) Regulations, 2002, Regulation 8 states that the decision to grant recognition to Primary Teachers Training Institutes (P.T.T.I.)
is at the discretion of the Board.
However, in this case, the President of the Board rejected the application without consulting the Board, which was deemed to be outside his jurisdiction and subsequently overruled.
The Board has been instructed to provide a fair hearing to the petitioners before deciding on the application for recognition.
[Paras 29 and 32]
