The validity of the proviso to Rule 17 of the Central Civil Services Examination Rules, 1990, which relates to the mobility of candidates in Group `A', is not discriminatory under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India because allowing this mobility could lead to chaos in the administration and the restriction placed on such mobility is neither unreasonable nor arbitrary.
The only condition is that the candidate must resign from the current group before appearing in the next examination.
[Para 8]

Rule 17 of the Central Civil Services Examination Rules, 1990, which permits IPS candidates to move to any service in Group `A', while preventing candidates of other Group `A' services from such mobility, is not discriminatory under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India because the classification made has a rational nexus with the object of improving the efficiency of administration.
Therefore, the rule is valid.
