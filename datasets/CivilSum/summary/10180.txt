Under the Constitution of India, Article 14, transfer of a director from the Power Grid Corporation of India to NHPC does not guarantee a 5-year tenure from the date of joining NHPC if their original tenure in the Grid was to expire earlier.
An OM from 1975 applies only to appointments made to top posts, including Director positions, by transfer and does not extend to cases where the original term expires earlier.
Benefiting from the OM would change the nature of the basic appointment if an extended period is allowed.
Thus, the benefit of OM is not available to such transferees.
