The petitioner in this case has been appointed as a waterman in the SYL Project for more than 5 years on fixed wages, but has not been regularized.
Other persons appointed similarly on various posts have already been regularized by promotion or transfer.
Despite the respondent's contention that there was no regular post of waterman available, the case is covered by the Supreme Court judgment in Piara Singh's case and instruction of the Government.
As a result of this, the petitioner's case must be considered for regularisation or promotion within the next three months.
