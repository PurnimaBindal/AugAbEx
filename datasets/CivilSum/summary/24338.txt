Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Extra Departmental Branch Post Master - To be eligible for appointment, the candidate must have a matriculation certificate and sufficient landed property in their name.
The candidate must provide relevant records to prove property ownership.
Even if the mutation entry could not be effected before the last date of submission, if the candidate became the owner of agricultural land based on a gift-deed before the deadline, it cannot be said that the candidate was ineligible.
Ignoring such cases is illegal.
[Paras 12, 15 and 16]

Constitution of India, Article 16 - Administrative Tribunals Act (13 of 1985), Section 14 - Appointment - A respondent-candidate who was not considered due to ineligibility immediately challenged their appointment.
The matter was pending for a long time before being reviewed by the tribunal.
The tribunal granted relief to the candidate and also made observations to accommodate the appellant incumbent if possible.
The direction by the tribunal, later confirmed by the High Court, is not liable to be interfered with.
[Paras 17 and 18]
