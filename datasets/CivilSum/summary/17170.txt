Constitution of India, Article 226 - Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Sections 20 and 21 - Alternative remedy - Jurisdiction of the Tribunal - As long as the appeal had not been decided within six months and the petition was filed within one year of the expiration of the aforesaid period of six months, the Administrative Tribunal has the power to entertain and decide a petition even if an appeal had been preferred before the competent authority.
Thus, it cannot be considered an illegal act.
