Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ petition - Maintaining it - Disputes over the decision taken by the Central Executive Committee regarding the removal of the petitioner as the General Secretary of the alliance union - Also, the relief sought pertains to the specific implementation of the alliance agreement through a writ - These cannot be treated as statutory or public responsibility that can be acted upon through writ jurisdiction simply by suing respondent No.1 claiming that they accepted the alliance agreement - Therefore, this is not a suitable case to be considered under writ jurisdiction.
