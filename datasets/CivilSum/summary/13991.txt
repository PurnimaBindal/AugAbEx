According to Section 2(13) of the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, contractors are not responsible for E.S.I.
contributions in relation to employees working in canteens that are run for the benefit of workers of Cochin Port Trust.
This is because the canteen is a statutory canteen that is operated by a contractor as an agent of the Port Trust, the principal employer, which is an exempted establishment.
