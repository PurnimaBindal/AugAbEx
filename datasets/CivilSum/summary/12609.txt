Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 - Premature Retirement/Adverse entry/Doubtful integrity - The competent authority ordered premature retirement based on an adverse entry of doubtful integrity, following the Rules and executive instructions of the Government.
The higher authorities considered and upheld the order, deeming it justified, as even a single entry of doubtful integrity can warrant premature retirement.
