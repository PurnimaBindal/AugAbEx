Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Compassionate appointment - A non-speaking order rejecting a request for compassionate appointment without any reason after a period of five years was deemed inexplicable - Delay defeats the objective of providing immediate financial assistance to the dependents for the appointment's purpose - The rejection's manner of the prayer of petitioner was found to be surprising - The competent authority is instructed to reconsider the request for compassionate appointment de novo within a month.
[Paras 1 and 4]
