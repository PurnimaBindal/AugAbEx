According to Section 72(1) of the Himachal Pradesh Co-operative Societies Act, 1969, and Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, (14 of 1947), a workman of a cooperative society can indeed claim benefits under the 1947 Act if their termination of service violates its provisions.
The Industrial Tribunal has the power and jurisdiction to handle such disputes, as confirmed by the judgment of the Apex Court in the Cooperative Central Bank Ltd case.
This decision specified that a dispute related to the service conditions of a cooperative society's employee could not be viewed as a dispute touching the society's business.
Therefore, the question of a workman's termination in violation of 1947 Act provisions should be decided by tribunals established under that Act.
