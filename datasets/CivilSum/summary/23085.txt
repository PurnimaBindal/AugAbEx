According to the UP Basic Education (Teachers) Service Rules, 1981, Rule 8 and the UP Basic Education Act, 1972, equivalency and revocation of equivalency of qualification is an administrative decision and is not within the jurisdiction of the court.
[Para 15]
Equivalency of qualification is at the discretion of the concerned authority, and it is decided by experts.
The court should show judicial restraint and not interfere in this matter.
[Para 15]
When it comes to policy decisions made by the government, the court cannot interfere unless there is a violation of some statutory or constitutional provision.
[Para 14]
The National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE) Act, 1993, Sections 2(m), 14(1), 17(1) and 4 do not relate to ordinary educational institutions, but rather deal with teacher training institutions.
The UP Basic Education Act, 1972, and its rules deal with primary schools in UP and not teachers training institutes.
The NCTE Act does not override the UP Basic Education Act and its rules since they operate in different fields.
