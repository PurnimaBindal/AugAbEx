As per Section 12 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a claim petition can only be submitted to the Labour Court if the employer acknowledges the status of the employee.
If there is a disagreement between the parties, and the employee claims reinstatement while alleging that termination was in violation of natural justice, the District Labour Officer cannot instruct the employee to file a claim petition before the Labour Court.
[Paras 10 and 11]
