According to Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 300 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 18(1) - State Bank of India Employees Pension Fund Rules, Rules 23(2), a petitioner who superannuated on 28.2.1993 and whose pay scale was revised w.e.f.
1.11.1992, is entitled to receive the revised basic pension in terms of the revised basic salary.
[Paras 10 and 15]
According to Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 300 - Pension based on revised scale, and as there was no delay or laches on the part of the petitioner and the delay in filing the writ was due to the respondent's negation of the claim only in May 2001, the writ of mandamus was allowed.
[Para 14]
