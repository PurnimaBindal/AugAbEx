Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Himachal Pradesh Non Government Affiliated Colleges Grant-in-Aid Rules, 1994, Rule 21 - Appointment - According to grant-in-aid rules, employees' appointments must be approved by the Director of Education for the grant to apply, and since the petitioner's appointment as Accountant did not have prior approval, they are not entitled to be either appointed or promoted.
