The `A' Stenographers in CMD have filed a petition for Equal Pay for Equal Work, seeking to be treated the same as `A' Stenographers in the State of West Bengal.
While the first two increments were granted to both cadres, only the Stenographers in West Bengal received the third increment.
The respondents argued that since CMD is an autonomous body, the matter should be referred to the Pay Commission.
However, it was held that since the petitioners had already been promoted to Grade `A' and granted two increments, there was no logical reason to deny them the third increment.
