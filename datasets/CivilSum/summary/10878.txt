Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Rate of Pay - The Labour Court has ordered reinstatement of the petitioner as Mechanic Grade II - The respondent does not deny that before termination the petitioner was being paid Rs. 424/- pm - As all Mechanics Grade II draw a pay scale of Rs. 400-600, the pay of the petitioner cannot be less than Rs. 480/- pm - The pay of the petitioner is to be calculated at the rate of Rs. 480/- pm [Paras 2 and 3]
