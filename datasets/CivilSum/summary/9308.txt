The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Penal Orders/Termination - It is an established fact that if any order with penal nature affecting a Government employee's rights has to be passed, they should be given a complete opportunity, and the correct procedure under rules and law must be followed - Any action of the State concerning its employees must satisfy the criteria of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
