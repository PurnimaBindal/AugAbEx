The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Increments - House Rent - The annual grade increment shall be given to an employee after completion of one year of service, unless an order for withholding is lawfully passed.
The employer is obligated to pay the annual grade increment as well as the house rent allowance due to the employee, unless an order of withholding has been passed lawfully.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 39(d) - Equal pay for equal work - The petitioner is claiming the salary of a Pump Operator while being employed as a helper, on the basis that he was made to work as a Pump Operator.
The principle of "equal pay for equal work" enshrined in Article 39(d) of the Constitution of India has been read as a part of the equality clause in Articles 14 and 16.
In enforcing this principle, the court cannot completely disregard qualifications and seniority.
Unlawful postings of lower cadre employees to higher posts without considering the cases of other senior or equally entitled employees, and without any regard for the qualifications prescribed for the post, do not give the right to claim pay for the higher post on the principle of equal pay for equal work.

The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Representation - Mandamus - A writ of mandamus cannot be issued until a demand has been made for the relief claimed by the employer.
