Constitution of India, Article 226 - Res judicata - Respondent withdrew a previous writ petition without reservation - Decision made in subsequent petition led to Respondent's selection- Claim that Respondent cannot seek the same relief in subsequent petition rejected- because relief sought is different in each petition - First petition claim was for appointment, while subsequent petition was for decision of representation - Respondent had the option to claim available departmental benefits after withdrawing the first petition.
