Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 14 - Punjab Service of Engineers, Class I, P.W.D.
(Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1964, Rules 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 12 - Reservation for Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes in promotion - Petitioners were promoted from Punjab Service of Engineers, Class II (Irrigation Branch) to Executive Engineer, all members of the general category.
They assert seniority to respondent No. 2, a total duty charge against the post of Chief Engineer.
A seniority list is kept in accordance with Rule 12, which stipulates that a person will not be reduced in seniority if he has passed over any promotion.
The rules do not exclude promotion with reservation.
The instructions of the Government of Punjab are binding on the members of the Services in question, and once the posts reserved for being filled up by reserved category candidates are filled up by operation of roster, the reservation objective shall be deemed to have been achieved.
