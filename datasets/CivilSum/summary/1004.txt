Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Eligibility - Entrance examination - An advertisement declares entrance exam is open only to candidates who have passed Masters/BA Degree examination with at least 50% marks in aggregate.
A petition is filed by petitioners with marks lower than the required aggregate - Graduates should have the right to take the exam because LLB is a postgraduate course.
There is no rational basis for denying graduates the opportunity to compete for a position on the merit list of an entrance exam.
The only way to give all graduates an equal opportunity to compete is to open the entrance exam to all graduates.
The requirement of a 50% passing mark in the BA exam as a condition for taking the entrance exam is arbitrary, unreasonable, and violates Articles 14 and 21.
[Para 23 and 52]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Eligibility for entrance exam - Respondents claim it is administratively inconvenient to allow all graduates to take the exam.
However, this claim cannot be used to defeat an individual's right to life, particularly considering the Supreme Court has held the right to education to be a part of the right to life.
This plea is arbitrary, fanciful, and does not relate to any objective that may be sought to be achieved.
[Para 42]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Entrance Examination - Eligibility criteria - A condition that requires graduates to have 50% marks in graduation is restrictive, which is unreasonable and denies a student the right to compete for a seat in a course based on provable merit.
Such a condition is illegal and is struck down.
[Para 52]

Constitution of India, Article 21 - Right to education - It is a fundamental right because it is a part of the right to life.
[Paras 26, 36, 38]
