Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Pension Regulation, 1961, Part - I, Regulation 173 - Disability pension - No opposition to pension claim on grounds of delay and latches.
[Paras 3, 5, and 8]  B.
Army Pension Regulation, 1961, Part-I, Regulations 173 and 186 - Disability pension - If a service member is granted both disability pension and service element pension on the grounds of disability, the service element pension will continue even if the disability pension is discontinued due to recovery, regardless of the length of service.
