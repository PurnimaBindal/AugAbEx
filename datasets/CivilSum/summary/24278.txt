Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - Posting and transfers - Interference by courts - Court guidelines - Posting and transfer of employees is an administrative matter at the discretion of the competent authority for the benefit of public administration - Transfer of an employee is a natural outcome of their appointment unless stated otherwise - Challenges to transfers must be based on mala fide or violation of statutory provisions or authority competence - Allegations of mala fide must be established prima facie to entertain a writ petition - Interference in transfers and postings should be avoided, particularly when disputes in facts are involved.
[Paras 7 to 9]
