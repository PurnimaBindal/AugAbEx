Public Servants (Inquiries) Act, 1977, Section 2 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 311(1) - J & K Civil Services (Classification, Control & Appeal) Rules, 1956, Article 53 - Act of 1977 - Challenge as to - Letter to Petitioner according to Article 53 for review of accusations made against him by Commissioner of Enquiries - Claim that Petitioner already faced departmental enquiry - Held, the usage of two alternative sources of authority does not make the provisions of Act of 1977 discriminatory or ultravires Constitution, notably when safeguards have been provided within it.
