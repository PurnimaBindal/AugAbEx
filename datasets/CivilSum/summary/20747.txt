Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Transfer - Public interest - Joint Director CBI - Transfer from the post of Joint Director CBI to Border Security Force on an equivalent post - The transfer can only involve prejudice to public interest when it is in relation to sensitive and important public officers - Mere suspicion or likelihood of prejudice is insufficient - A strong and unquestionable evidence is needed to prove substantial prejudice to public interest that could vitiate the action - The vitiating element must be approached with caution and circumspection - The plea has been rejected.

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Transfer/Deputation - Tenure Rules for I.P.S.
Officers, Rule 8 - IPS officers serving on the State Cadre can be sent to any organization while on deputation to the Central Police Organizations - The five-year tenure for deputation can be made in multiple organizations.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - The court should normally not interfere in cases of mala fide activities or when the transfer contravenes a professed norm or principle governing the transfer - Matters of public interest should be left to administrative authorities.
