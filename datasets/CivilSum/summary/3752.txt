Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Constitution of India, Article 14 - Disciplinary proceedings - Notice was served two days after retirement and charge-sheet was issued 14 years ago.
With the unexplained delay of 5 years in concluding enquiry after dismissal of SLP, the respondent acted arbitrarily in restarting enquiry after long delay, which was considered a violation of Article 14 of the constitution.
Therefore, the notice was quashed.
[Paras 10 and 14]
