Punjab Civil Services Rules Volume II Chapter VII, Rule 6.17 - Family pension - If a government employee has completed one continuous year of service without a break and dies while in service, their family will receive the pension - After three years of service, every work-charged employee is entitled to become regular and must be confirmed after five years - Based on an award given by the Industrial Tribunal in Punjab, the husband of the petitioner is considered a regular and confirmed employee - Therefore, the widow is eligible to receive the family pension.
