Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 4 - High Court Judges (Salaries and Conditions of Service) Act, 1954, Section 17 (A)(2) and First Schedule Part III Para 2 - Gratuity - Death-cum-retirement gratuity - Petitioners argued that the gratuity limit should be enhanced from Rs. 25 lacs to Rs. 35 lacs.
They claimed that this revised limit should also be made applicable to retired High Court Judge on ground of financial crunch.
The court held that there are hardly one or two Judges who retire every year and the additional burden would be just Rs. 1 or 2 lacs per year.
Therefore, the petition was allowed.
