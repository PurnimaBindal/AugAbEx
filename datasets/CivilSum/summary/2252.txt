Constitution of India, Article 226 - Andhra Pradesh State and Subordinate Service Rules, Rule 4, Rule 37 or Rule 33 - Seniority - Re-allotment - Applicants recruited directly as Co-operative Sub-Registrars - Dispute arose regarding number of vacancies for direct recruits - Revised merit list of Co-op.
Sub-Registrars prepared by APPSC - Applicants requested seniority fixation based on revised list sent by APPSC - Rule 37 covers re-allotment whereby candidates re-allotted under Rule 4 ( c) are assigned seniority below last regular candidate - Special rule, Rule 37, applicable in case of re-allotment and not general Rule 33 of the Rules - Fixation of seniority upheld.
