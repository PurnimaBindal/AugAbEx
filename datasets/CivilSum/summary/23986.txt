Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Ensuring pay parity - Equal pay for equal work - While equal pay for equal work and parity of pay scales are distinct concepts, they should not be confused - When a specialized Committee has made an equation with their expertise, the High Court should not interfere unless there is evidence of malice or extraneous consideration - The determination of pay equivalence should be left to expert bodies like the Pay Commission, who are best equipped to evaluate the nature of duties and responsibilities of different posts.
