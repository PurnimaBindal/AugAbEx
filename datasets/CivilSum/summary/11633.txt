Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 39(d) and 226 - Pay scale - Discrimination - The petitioner was appointed on the basis of the qualifications mentioned in the advertisement.
- Both diploma and non-diploma holders were placed in the same pay scale.
- The classification of one cadre into two categories based on qualifications was not justified because they had common duties to perform and the posts were also interchangeable.
- It was not established that the petitioner, who was a non-diploma holder, was not performing the same duties.
- Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to the pay scale given to other members of the service working in the petitioner's cadre.
[Paras 10, 11 and 17]
