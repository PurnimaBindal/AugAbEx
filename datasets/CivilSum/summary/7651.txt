Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Act, 1950, Sections 63, 80, 116 and 191 - Army Rules, 1950, Rules 34 and 81 - Summary Court Martial - The delinquent held the position of Battle Quarter Master Havildar, a Non-Commissioned rank.
Despite this, an order was passed to reduce their rank to Havildar based on an allegation of ammunition theft.
This order is not proper as the Commandant had no jurisdiction to convene Court-Martial for a non-commissioned officer.
Furthermore, there was a gross violation of Rules 34 and 81 of Army Rules.
Hence, the impugned order should be quashed.
