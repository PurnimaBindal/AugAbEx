The State Government of Punjab needs to regulate the situation of part-time Class IV employees, particularly sweepers and water carriers, who have been working on their respective positions for around 15-20 years.
The existence of work and the need for these positions have been proven, and all part-time employees are in similar situations.
Considering the significant number of part-time employees, the government is directed to create a policy and establish a reasonable scale of pay for the employees to adhere to.
