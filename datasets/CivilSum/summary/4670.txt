Haryana Urban Development Service Regulations Act, 1989, Regulation 8 - Deputation of Sub-Divisional Engineer (Civil) - Recruitment and posting to the post of Sub Divisional Engineers - Eligibility and seniority do not guarantee appointment or promotion to the post.
The regulation allows for filling vacancies by deputation and transfer in the exigencies of service, but such appointments and postings cannot continue beyond three months.
The authority must initiate the process of regular selection within three months.
