Kerala Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1960, Section 15 - Delay and Laches - Disciplinary proceedings - Failure to initiate proceedings in a timely manner, without reasonable justification, is considered a denial of an employee's opportunity to defend themselves.
It is unreasonable to expect an employee to have a photographic memory or meticulously record every detail in anticipation of future consequences.
