The respondent, who was employed in a private educational institution, claimed that he was entitled to continue in service until he turned sixty years old, even after the institution was taken over by the government.
However, the court rejected his claim, stating that there was no provision in the gift deed preserving his retirement age of sixty.
The respondent was retired at the age of 58, and since his service was less than ten years, he was not eligible for pension.
This decision was upheld.
