Constitution of India, Article 226 - Special Examination Rules, Rule 2 - Petitioners took admission in 1995 to Post Graduate Courses - Candidates were required to pass within 4 years - Petitioners were unable to do so - University introduced new rules and Special Examination Rules were framed - Rule 2 mandates that only those candidates who have passed atleast one subject in final and previous year can appear for exam - Court held that the rule is not illegal or arbitrary - Petitioners failed to pass even after being given four opportunities and therefore cannot challenge the legality of the special Examination Rules - Admission and Appearance in examination is subject to Rules and Regulations made by university from time to time - Therefore, there is no reason to interfere.
