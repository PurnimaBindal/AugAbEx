Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25F to 25H - Termination from services - Petitioner was a driver of an official car and was served notice for his acts of omission and commission with its working and maintenance.
Instead of replying to the notice, he refused to accept the same and left the office after throwing the keys on the table of the office superintendent.
The petitioner raised industrial dispute after nine months of termination of his services.
The discipline at the workplace is necessary for the efficient working of the organization, and when an employee breaches such discipline, it is not open to the labor court to interfere with the action taken by the employer.
The workman had a service of 240 days to his credit when his service was terminated, but he is not entitled to any relief as he himself abandoned the job in a most arrogant and undisciplined manner.
[Paras 3 & 4]
