Punjab Homoeopathic Practitioners Act, 1965, Section 21(1) (b) - Punjab Homoeopathic Practitioners Regulations, 1974, Regulations 8, 10, 11 and 22 - Changes made in the Central Regulations are not required to be approved by the State Government under Punjab Regulations - Unless specific provisions are mentioned, it cannot be said that any relaxation was made in the Regulations - Students who were admitted after the Central Regulations cannot claim any benefit under the Punjab Regulations.

Supplementary Examination/Reappear/Result/Admission - After passing lower examination, a student is required to attend higher classes for 1-1/2 years - The result from supplementary exams will apply to annual exams - Therefore, a student cannot be prevented from appearing in higher exams just because they passed the lower exam on the same day as the supplementary.
