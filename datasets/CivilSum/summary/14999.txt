Under Section 15(2) of the Payment of Wages Act, 1936, an employee's pay may be reduced to a minimum stage as punishment following a disciplinary enquiry.
However, if the employee is dissatisfied with the disciplinary orders, they may only challenge them through an appeal, civil suit, or Civil Writ Petition under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, and not before the Authority under the Act.
