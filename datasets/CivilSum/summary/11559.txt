The private Dental Colleges/Institutions violated the fee structure prescribed by the Central Government under Clause 8(b) of the 1994 Regulations and the order dated 781988 issued by the State Government.
The violation of the fee structure was declared illegal based on the decision in "Malvika Mahajan & others v. State of Punjab & others" CWP No. 14249 of 1998.
[Para 10]

The petitioners made their representation against the charging of fee in violation to the notification in 1999 and filed a petition in 2000.
The delay and laches cannot be pleaded as the conduct of the petitioners shows their vigilant attitude about their rights and no estoppel can be pleaded against the mandate of statute.
[Para 11]

Minority institutions cannot mitigate the mandate of law regarding the payment of fee in such institutions.
The fee structure prescribed by notification was violated by private Dental Colleges and minority institutions.
