Constitution of India, Article 226 - Tamil Nadu State and Subordinate Services Rules, Rule 49 - Date of birth - Correction of - Rule 49 allows for the filing of an application to alter the date of birth within 5 years of entering service - The respondent requested alteration of their date of birth after 34 years based on the birth register of the municipality - The tribunal granted the alteration - However, the honorable Apex Court clarified that an aggrieved employee must seek correction of their date of birth at the earliest possible time, and changing it on the eve or before superannuation may lead to fabricated records - Accordingly, the petition was allowed.
