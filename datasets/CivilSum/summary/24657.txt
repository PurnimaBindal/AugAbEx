Constitution of India, Article 16 - Pay Scale - Classification of semi-skilled workers as un-skilled workers - Invalid - Dairy Mates, Junior Plant Operatives and Semi-skilled Operatives petitioned to be placed in the pay scales of 4th Pay Commission, but were categorized as sweepers, chowkidars and malis i.e. unskilled workers - Court ruled that the petitioners should receive pay scales for semi-skilled workers and not be treated on par with unskilled workers - Petition granted.
