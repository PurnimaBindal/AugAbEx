Constitution of India, Article 136 - Posts and Telegraphs Service Rules, Rule 5(1) - Promotion - Seniority - The appellant was directed by the Tribunal to prepare a fresh list for promotion based on the initial date of promotion, excluding the date of confirmation.
The Tribunal did not consider the effect of Rule 5(1) to determine if the crucial date was the date of promotion or confirmation.
The case was remitted to the Tribunal to be reexamined.
