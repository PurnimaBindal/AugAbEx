Constitution of India, Art. 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1)(c) - Equal pay for equal work/Anomaly in emoluments - Petitioners, who have been daily wage beldars or cattle care-takers in the establishment of Director, Punjab Agricultural University, Seed Farm, Ropar for the last decade, have filed a writ petition in the hopes of receiving equal pay for equal work - Respondents contended that petitioners perform unspecialized jobs as daily wage laborers, while regular beldars engage in specialized technical operations - The High Court cannot adjudicate on such matters through a writ petition under Art. 226, as the appropriate remedy is to seek a reference under section 10(1)(c) of Industrial Disputes Act in the Labour Court - There is a serious dispute on the crucial matter.
