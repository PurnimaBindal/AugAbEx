Constitution of Jummu and Kashmir, Article 126 - Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 33 - Jammu and Kashmir Civil Services Rules, Article 128 - Absence from duty - Overstay of leave - Dismissal/Removal on account of absence - Enquiry - An enquiry is required in cases of absence leading to dismissal or removal, but there is no provision for conducting one against an employee who has retired and is no longer a member of the service for any other reasons - Once an employee retires due to superannuation, the punishing authority cannot legally initiate any disciplinary proceedings against them.
