Constitution of India, Article 226 - Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Right and Full Participation) Act, 1995 - Resolution - 3% reservation for visually and orthopaedically handicapped personnel in teaching posts in University and Colleges of Delhi - At least one person must be appointed each academic year - Several advertisements for filling up posts do not even mention of reservation - Contention that posts not earmarked repelled by Government by stating that old Instructions issued for identification of posts still hold good - Brochure on reservation and Concession for Physical Handicapped in Central Government Services issued by Government was available to answer all questions- Respondents show a callous approach and nothing concrete has happened in the last 6 years reservation was established - Order to comply, reserve and fill posts - If posts are unavailable, the option is to either create supernumerary positions or terminate services of those appointed, subject to decision of this petition.
