Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961, Section 26(4) - Punjab Co-operative Societies Rules, 1963, Rule 80(iii) - Competent authority to make a reference to the Government - While the Chairman is authorized to make a reference to the Government when there is a difference of opinion between the nominated members and the members of the Executive Committee of the Society, it does not imply that only the Chairman can refer the matter.
According to Rule, the Registrar must make a reference to the Government on receiving a report from a nominated member if no reference is made within seven days of the meeting.
It is valid for the Government to make decisions based on a report of a nominated member or any other officer of the Society, even if there are irregularities in the reference.
The doctrine of de facto incorporated in Section 29 of the Act will cure any irregularities.
The Executive Committee's decision to make promotions without sanctioned posts was rightly set aside.
