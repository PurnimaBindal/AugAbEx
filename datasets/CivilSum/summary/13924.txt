Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 226 - Compassionate appointment - Rejection of - It was held that compassionate employment cannot be claimed as a right and is only meant to assist families during a sudden crisis - If the retiral benefits granted to the family are equal to the carry home salary of the deceased, then they cannot be considered in financial difficulty and therefore cannot receive compassionate appointment solely on the basis of the death of their family member who held the post.
