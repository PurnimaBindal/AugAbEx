Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Refusal of incentives - Petitioners who were appointed as lay-Inseminators in Integrated Watershed Development Project (Hills) were refused incentives after finishing Phase I of the project - The Deputy Director filed an affidavit stating that no post and provision of work for lay-Inseminators was available as the project was closed - The petitioners argued that they had completed their training and had given a bond for 5-year work - However, the bond period had already ended - The court held that since the project had been completed, the petitioners cannot claim their right for continuation of service.
