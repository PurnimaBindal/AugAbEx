Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Industrial Incentive Code, Industrial Policy Statement of 141979, Rules 43, 217 - Backward Areas declared by the Government - Special Group - Government or State Government can declare an area as backward, even within 10 kilometres from the Municipal limits, forming a special group and an exception to the general group - Units within such an area are in Group IV despite being within 10 kilometers from the Municipal limits - Petitioners are eligible for benefits under the Code and Government policy - Government can't deny benefits that have been consciously granted for a long period.
