Jammu and Kashmir Daily Rated Worker/Work Charged Employees Regulations, Rule 1994 - Minimum wages - Night watchman is entitled to receive the same minimum wages as a daily wager.

Jammu and Kashmir Daily Rated Worker/Work Charged Employees Regulations, Rule 1994 - Advertisement - Public notices must be issued for appointments made to public service in accordance with statutory requirements - Ad hoc and temporary appointments made without public notice would not be extended beyond six months or the term to which the appointments stand extended - State to issue public notice during this period before making appointments.
