Constitution of India, Article 16 - Absorption - Respondent's claim for absorption onto the regular LDC post was denied due to his failure in the Commission's examination - The respondent was not selected for the LDC post - The respondent cannot claim that he was absorbed on the post of LDC, simply because he was considered for regularization and allowed the minimum pay scale for the post of regular LDC.
