According to Clause IV of the Voluntary Retirement Scheme effective from 10101994, acceptance of a request for voluntary retirement must be communicated within three months.
If there's no proof that such communication was made within the stipulated time period, the request is deemed to be rejected, allowing the applicant to continue in service.
[Paras 10 to 16]  B Even though the petitioner applied for voluntary retirement and proceeded on leave, for not receiving communication of the acceptance within the time limit, the court held the petitioner to be still in service and not entitled to any back wages for the intervening period.
However, the period will be considered to determine continuity of service.
