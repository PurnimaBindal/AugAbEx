Haryana Civil Services (Promotion of Group C and D Employees) Rules, 1998, Rules 2 and 3 - Promotion - Petitioner employed as Chowkidar in PWD, Haryana - Requesting promotion from Class D to Class C, as clerk - Competent authority stayed promotion order on the grounds that promotion to the post of clerk is for class IV employee of Ministerial cadre - Petitioner contended that he belongs to Mechanical cadre and is entitled to promotion as water pump operator under statutory rules of service - Court rejected the plea and affirmed that 1998 rules are special rules for appointment to the post of clerk - Any person eligible under these rules must be granted promotion, regardless of their eligibility for water pump operator position - Stay of promotion was quashed.
