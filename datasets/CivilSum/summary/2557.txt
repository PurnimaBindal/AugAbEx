Admission/Choice of subjects and Institutions - The selection of three subjects out of five by candidates for Post Graduate Courses is not arbitrary and is in line with admission being based on merit.
However, restrictions on choice of institutions to only two can be considered arbitrary and may prevent deserving candidates from being admitted to other institutions.
A common merit list based on entrance exams should be prepared first, followed by the choice of institution.
Any provision against the mandate of Article 14 of the Constitution must be invalidated, and a slight delay in admission due to this should not be an issue.
