The Rajasthan Compassionate Appointment of Dependant of Deceased Government Servant Rules, 1996, Rule 5 - Died in harness - Compassionate appointment - The Statutory rules only allow for the appointment in subordinate service, ministerial services, class IV services carrying payscale Nos. 1 to 9 and meant for direct recruitment -Senior Sanitary Inspector's death in-service - The Petitioner was offered a post of Lower Division Clerk - The Petitioner's contention that he should be offered a post considering his Chartered Accountancy qualification cannot be entertained - In subordinate services, the post of Accountant may be filled through direct recruitment but it does not fall within payscale Nos. 1 to 9 - The Petitioner is not entitled to any relief.
[Para 15]
Compassionate Appointment - Appointment on compassionate grounds aims to relieve the grieving family from financial crises, and not to confer status or employment based on qualifications.
