Constitution of India, Article 310 - Orissa Service Code, Rule 71(a) - Pre-mature retirement - Judicial Officers - The government has the discretion to determine pre-mature retirement for judicial officers, and the rise in the age for superannuation was intended to improve the tone and morale of the judicial services.
However, the mandate of the rule is not that the judicial officer must continue until the age of 60 years, as continued service is subject to efficiency and fitness.
Rule 71(a) necessitates review of service record, efficiency and fitness of the judicial officer to continue until the age of 60, which is not ultra vires nor invalid according to the All India Judges Association case.
The government must regulate the services of its employees, and only those capable of rendering useful service should be continued while infirm or those of doubtful integrity, reputation or utility should be removed.
Periodic evaluation of utility is required for better administration and for the removal of dead wood.
