Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion - Sealed Cover promotion - Departmental proceedings - Interim Orders - If an employee is in a situation similar to that of employees eligible for promotion, the mere existence of departmental proceedings against him should not prevent his promotion - However, The Department Promotion Committee is required to keep their recommendations in a sealed cover if the delinquent is deemed fit for promotion, but ultimately not found guilty of the charges against him, he will be promoted immediately - Nevertheless, in proceedings where the validity of a charge sheet is questioned, the court cannot routinely instruct authorities through interim orders to open the sealed cover and promote delinquent individuals subject to the outcome of departmental proceedings.
As a rule, such relief is not typically granted, and only in exceptional circumstances is this rule overlooked.
In the interest of justice, the court may direct the authority to expedite the proceedings if deemed necessary.
[Paras 12, 13, 14, 16 and 24]
