Constitution of India, Articles 21 and 311 - Double Jeopardy - Deparmental Enqury - Criminal case, pendency of - Disciplinary enquiry initiated during pendency of criminal case - The Court needs to determine if the questions of fact and inferences drawn from the facts, are common in both the criminal case and the contemplated disciplinary enquiry.
This is because legal aspects of the case pending before a criminal court may be different from the matter in the departmental proceeding, although the factual basis of both cases is the same.
There is no legal hindrance to concurrent proceedings being pursued against the delinquent employee, against whom disciplinary proceedings were initiated and a criminal case was pending.
[Para 10]
