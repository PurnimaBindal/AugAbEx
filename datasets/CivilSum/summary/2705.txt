Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Regularization of services - Temporary employee - Appointment of petitioner on temporary basis on stop-gap arrangements - Services may be terminated when not required - Due to the petitioner's extended service as temporary employee, she is not eligible for absorption as a regular employee.
[Paras 16 and 17]

Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Selection process - Appointment - As a temporary employee, the petitioner participated in the process for selection for regular employment but was found over the age limit and lacking the required qualifications - The order to terminate her services as a temporary employee was lawful.
[Para 20]

Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Selection - Eligibility criteria - Relaxation - The petitioner participated in the process for selection for a regular post on court directions despite being a temporary employee - However, as the court did not grant relaxation for the upper age limit, the petitioner cannot benefit from such a relaxation.
[Paras 21 and 22]
