The seniority of an IAS member must be determined according to the rules in effect on the date of their appointment regardless of when the vacancy occurred.
Therefore, the year of allotment must be determined based on the seniority rules in effect at the time of appointment.
[Paras 13 and 17] B.
Simply being included on a select list does not guarantee appointment under the Indian Administrative Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations, 1955 and Indian Administrative Service (Regulation of Seniority) Rules, 1987.
