According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, the services of Junior Engineer were placed at the disposal of command area development and he later filed an application before the Tribunal seeking direction to post him under Director of Soil Conservation.
Although an interim order allowed respondent to report to duty before Director of Agriculture, his service was not regularised due to failing to report to duty at his place of posting.
Although twelve others were appointed and later regularised, asking respondent to undergo the procedure would now be inequitable given the three years that have lapsed since his appointment.
As such, the respondent was deemed to have been regularised with effect from the date of the Apex Court's order and is entitled to 50% salary for the period he was not able to work.
