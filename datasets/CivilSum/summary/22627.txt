According to Section 2(oo)(bb) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, delay and laches can be an issue.
A respondent who worked on daily wages for Punjab Roadways was removed from service and waited 13 years before moving the labour court.
The court ultimately ordered reinstatement with back wages, a decision upheld by the High Court and Supreme Court.
However, due to the 13-year delay, the Supreme Court set aside the order for payment of back wages.
