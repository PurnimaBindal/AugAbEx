Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Reference/Jurisdiction of the appropriate Government/Delay - The petitioner's services were terminated 15 years before he was set to retire, and a demand notice was given after this long delay.
The appropriate government declined the reference, stating that it would not be good for industrial peace to reconsider the matter.
However, the government can still review the matter to decide if it is prima facie referable and not frivolous or perverse.
This jurisdiction is limited to this extent.
[Para 4]

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Resignation/Reference - The State government rejected a reference because the workman could not prove that his resignation was taken under duress.
However, this is not an adjudication, and the government was within its rights to decline the reference on these grounds.
[Para 5]
