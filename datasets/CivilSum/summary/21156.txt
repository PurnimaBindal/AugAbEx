Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 323A - Administrative Tribunals - Jurisdiction - Disciplinary proceedings - The authority of the Tribunals is limited to judicial review of administrative action of authorities on grievances related to employee service conditions only - The disciplinary authority has the sole responsibility of examining the evidence and determining if the charge has been proven - Formal rules of evidence do not apply to disciplinary proceedings - If the disciplinary authority has assessed the available information and its findings are supported by it, the Tribunal does not have the power to substitute its own determination on the proof of the charge by re-evaluating the evidence - The Tribunal's order is deemed to lack authority and is set aside if it substitutes its own findings that the charge is not substantiated.
