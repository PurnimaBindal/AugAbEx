Constitution of India, Article 226 - Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1964, Rule 3(1)(iii) - Misconduct - Disciplinary proceedings - The petitioner, an X-ray Technician, was dismissed for misbehaving with a patient in the X-ray room.
The order based on no evidence or in violation of principles of natural justice, even in non-examination of the complainant, doesn't render any error in the Tribunal confirming dismissal.
