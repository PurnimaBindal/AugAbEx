Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 7(6) - Enquiry - Punishment - Despite the enquiry officer absolving the petitioner of the charge levelled against him, the punishing authority issued a show cause notice and imposed punishment without fulfilling the mandatory provision of supplying the delinquent employee with the brief state of ground of disagreement with the report - Thus, the impugned order was quashed for being against the mandate of proviso to Rule 7(6).
