According to the Rajasthan Co-operative Dairy Federation Employees (Non-Workmen) Service Regulations, 1980 and the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 311(2), employees of co-operative institutions are to retire at the age of 60, which was established by a resolution in 1985.
The Circular of 2004 allows the Board of Directors to make decisions regarding retirement age.
The court held that the decision to fix the retirement age is a matter of executive policy that is dependent on the "felt need of administration" and therefore must be left to the consideration of the State Government.
The decision cannot be deemed arbitrary, mala fide, unfair, unreasonable, or irrational.
Additionally, the principles of natural justice do not apply to government policy decisions.
