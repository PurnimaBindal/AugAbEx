According to Rule 12 of MP Regularisation of ad hoc Rules, 1986, a person appointed under Rules is entitled to seniority only from the date of their regular appointment.
The seniority of the respondent whose services were regularised on 441987 and declared as a lecturer on 21.7.1989 had to be considered from the date of regular appointment.
The seniority could not have been given earlier than the order of their regular appointment.
Therefore, the government determined the seniority of the respondent as a lecturer from the date of their regular appointment on an equivalent post.
The tribunal was mistaken in directing that their seniority as a lecturer should be counted from the date they started working as CMO - Lecturer Grade.
