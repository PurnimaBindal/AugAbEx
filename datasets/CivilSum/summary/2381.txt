Misuse of process of law is an abuse of the court and contrary to justice and public policy if a party relitigates an issue already decided against them.
Even if previous orders do not strictly fall under res-judicata, successive frivolous and vexatious amounts to an abuse of the court and law.
Such a litigant is not entitled to relief and their lis must be dismissed with exemplary costs.
[Paras 4 and 8]
Services of the petitioner were terminated in 1973, and they have been litigating unsuccessfully since then.
Their Writ Petition filed against the termination order of 1973 is liable to be dismissed on the grounds of delay and laches.
[Paras 5 and 6]
To approach a Writ Petition without first approaching the tribunal is not maintainable under the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 28.
