Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Appointment - Qualification - Eligibility - The petitioner was found to be lacking the requisite qualification for the post of Multipurpose Health Worker (Male) - The qualifications specified for the position were matric with MPW Diploma and knowledge of Punjabi language upto matric.
- The petitioner had qualified 'General Nursing' instead of the MPW Diploma recognized by the Punjab Government or an equivalent certification.
- This indicated a clear deviation from the required qualifications.- Upon examination of his eligibility, the petitioner's services were correctly dispensed with- His appeal for reinstatement was denied.
