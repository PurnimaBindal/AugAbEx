Karnataka Civil Services (General Recruitment) Rules, 1977, Rule 16-A - Deputation - Repatriation - Petitioners appointed as Reserve Police Constables first and then deployed to printing press.
Later, appointed to a sanctioned post after cadre strength was sanctioned.
Repatriation after 30 years of service in printing press not appropriate.
Director General/Inspector General of Police not prohibited from making appointments to posts sanctioned by State Govt.
Transfer, absorption and promotion granted to petitioners before private respondents were appointed.
No violation of legal rights of private respondents, especially when they did not raise any objection for 14 years.
[Paras 17, 18, 22 and 24]
