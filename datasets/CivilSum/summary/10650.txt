Constitution of India, Article 226 - Petitioner, an AAG from Haryana, had been practicing as an advocate in Chandigarh prior to his appointment - Respondent argued that only AAGs/DAGs who were advocates prior to their appointment were entitled to a 3000/- p.m. House Rent Allowance - The Court held that advocates appointed as law officers while practicing in Chandigarh or other areas must be treated equally with regard to private residential accommodation if they do not own any property in Chandigarh - The petition was granted.
