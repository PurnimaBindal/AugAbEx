The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 38, pertains to the education of blind persons.
The petitioner, a blind individual with an excellent academic track record, was permitted to take the B.Ed.
entrance exam and achieved high merit.
Denying him admission to B.Ed.
courses due to the inability to teach students is unjust.
Depriving him of education based on this ground is a violation of his rights; instead, the state must harness the potential of handicapped individuals to improve their societal status and conditions.

The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 38 mandates reservation for handicapped individuals, specifically the blind.
It is the state's duty to safeguard and support disabled persons in elevating their status and condition in society.
Therefore, the educational pursuits of disabled individuals should be aided systematically by utilizing their inherent abilities to the fullest.
