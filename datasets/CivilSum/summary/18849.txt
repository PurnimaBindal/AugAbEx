Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Dismissal - Misconduct - The petitioner, who was working as a logging officer, was found to have provided false information about the weight of tendu leaves in order to embezzle money - Despite being given ample opportunity to defend himself, he failed to appear before the enquiry officer and avoided service of the charge-sheet - The charge-sheet was eventually posted at his residence and released to the press - The enquiry officer thoroughly investigated the matter and found the charges against the petitioner to be true - The respondent did not violate any procedures or natural justice - There is no reason to interfere with the order of dismissal.
[Paras 13, 14, 15 and 16]
