According to Section 17 - A of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Schedule 3, Item II of the Casual Labour (Grant of Temporary Status and Regularisation) Scheme, Clause 4 states that temporary employment for casual labour may be regularized.
However, if the petitioner is engaged in piecemeal work and has not worked for more than 240 days in a year, they are not covered by this clause.
Despite the petitioner's claim, the Tribunal found the order passed was not illegal.
