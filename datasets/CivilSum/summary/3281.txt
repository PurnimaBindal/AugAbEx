Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 226 - Letters Patent of the High Court of Delhi, Clause 26 - Admission/Difference of opinion/Provisional Admission/Prima facie case - To be eligible for provisional admission, it is required to obtain at least 50% marks in the Graduate/Post Graduate examination of Delhi University - Due to the difference of opinion between two judges of a division bench, it is undetermined which of opinions are correct - A prima facie case exists for the grant of interim relief of provisional admission - The University is directed to grant provisional admission to the petitioner to prevent the loss of an academic year, should the ultimately petitioner succeed.
