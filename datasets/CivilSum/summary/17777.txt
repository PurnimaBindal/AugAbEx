The equivalence of two posts is not determined by their pay scale or nomenclature, but by the nature of their duties, responsibilities, powers, and privileges - Director, Health and Family Welfare has more powers, privileges, and responsibilities than the Director, Health Services - therefore, the post of Director, Health and Family Welfare is considered to be higher than the post of Director, Health Services.
[Para 10]

The determining factors for a higher post are the powers, privileges, and responsibilities of the position - Director, Health and Family Welfare has greater powers, privileges, and responsibilities than Director, Health Services - The recruitment and promotion of Class-III officials under Director, Health Services is made by the Director, Health and Family Welfare, which indicates that the post of Director, Health and Family Welfare is higher than that of Director, Health Services.
[Para 10]

According to the Punjab Civil Medical Service [Class-I] Rules, 1972 as amended in 1979, Rules 9 and 9A, and Articles 14, 16, and 226 of the Indian Constitution, the eligibility criteria for promotion to the post of Director, Health and Family Welfare requires the Additional Director to have one year of experience in this role - The petitioner had the required experience and was posted as Director, Health Services - Respondent No. 2 did not meet the experience requirement of one year as an Additional Director - Although the petitioner was senior to Respondent No. 2, had the right of preferential promotion, and was equal in merit, Respondent No. 2 was promoted/appointed to the higher post of Director, Health and Family Welfare, which violated Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution - The petitioner challenged the promotion/appointment of Respondent No. 2 - The State contended that the post of Director Health and Family Welfare is equivalent to that of Director, Health Services, but this was not sustained - The appointment of Respondent No. 2 was quashed, and a direction was given to consider and promote the petitioner to the post of Director, Health and Family Welfare in preference to Respondent No. 2.
