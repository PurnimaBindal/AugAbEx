Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 39(d) - Equal pay for equal work - Classification within a class - Giving different pay scales or nomenclature based only on mode and source of appointment and not on the work they perform violates the equality provision of the Constitution.
Creating artificial groups within a cadre is also prohibited.
The court does not need to wait for the Government to address pay disparities and anomalies because equal pay for equal work is considered a fundamental right under the Constitution.
