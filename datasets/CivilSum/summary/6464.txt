Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Transfer - Mala-fide transfer - While the writ court typically refrains from getting involved in transfer and posting matters, if a transfer is intended to dislodge an employee near retirement or while under treatment for serious illness, and it is deemed to be malafide, the writ court may intervene to serve justice - In this case, the authorities provided misleading support for such malafide transfer orders - Their actions are subject to be invalidated and may carry associated costs.
