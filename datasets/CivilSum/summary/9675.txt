No. 1327 of 2000: Constitution of India, Article 226 - Domestic enquiry - Misconduct - Dismissal - After evaluating the material on record, the charge of misconduct against employees is proven - The lack of additional evidence from Management is not pertinent to the disproportionate punishment of dismissal - The failure to examine panchas, disregard for the discharge order of the criminal Court, and the imposition of punishment by the Chairman in contravention of Regulation 23 are all unacceptable and unjustifiable.
