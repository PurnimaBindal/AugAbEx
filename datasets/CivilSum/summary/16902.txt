The Constitution of India, 1950 - Articles 14, 15(4) and 226 - Reservation of seat - Admission - A prospectus indicating the reservation of seats for candidates belonging to "Backward area/Physically handicapped/Armed forces, BSF/CRPF = 6%" was provided, but it didn't equate to even one full seat.
Respondent No. 3 was eligible as a Backward area candidate who was higher in merit than the petitioner.
Therefore, the University clubbed the seats and granted admission to respondent No. 3.
The action taken by the University cannot be faulted.
