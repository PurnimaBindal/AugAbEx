Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 20(2) - Criminal Procedure Code, Section 300(1) - Border Security Force Act, Section 75 - Border Security Force Rules, Rules 143(4) and 161(1)(a) - Acquittal - Second trial - Prohibition of second trial under Section 75 - DIG can set aside proceedings where grave irregularity has been committed by the Summary court causing injustice to the accused under Section 161 - Section 161 does not apply to proceedings conducted by a Court having no jurisdiction or competence resulting into no trial in the eye of law - De novo trial is not barred by Section 161, even after acquittal holding the trial to be suffering from grave irregularities - Once personnel is acquitted, holding the trial to be suffering from grave irregularities, no second trial is possible and will be barred by Section 75.
