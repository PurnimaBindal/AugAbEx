Constitution of India, Article 226 - Public Interest Litigation - Locus stand - A working Sub-Inspector of police has filed public interest litigation claiming that IAS and IPS officers have produced false certificates of social status and obtained jobs.
However, the petitioner has not disclosed his specific interest and how the public will benefit from this litigation.
The allegation has been found to be not true after being considered earlier by the appropriate agencies on more than one occasion.
The petition seems to have been filed as a personal vendetta against two officers, and the Learned Single Judge was correct in dismissing the petition.
[Paras 11 and 13]
