To achieve "Permanent Employee" status under the relevant legislation, one must be appointed according to statutory rules by the competent authority for a sanctioned position.
A daily wager does not hold said position unless appointed under the relevant Act and Rules.
Working for over 240 days does not automatically grant the right to regularisation, and appointments made by unauthorized persons are void.
