Examination Bye-Laws framed by CBSE, Rule 26(iii) - Education - Change in subjects - A student who failed in CBSE XII Class examination with Mathematics as one of the papers is not allowed to change the subject according to CBSE rules.
However, the petitioner tried to change to Sanskrit Elective with the class teacher's permission but received an admission card to appear in Mathematics instead of the requested Sanskrit Elective.
Petitioner challenged this action of CBSE and held entitled to appear in the examination with Sanskrit Elective.
