Constitution of India, Article 226 - ROPA Rules, Rules 5(2), 9 - Post of Field Investigator - Qualifications - Estoppel - The petitioner's claim for the pay scale mentioned in the advertisement for the post was rejected without justification.
The High Court ordered the Secretary of Govt. to issue a reasoned order.
The Secretary stated that petitioner was deemed unfit for the job mentioned in the advertised scale but was instead appointed in a lower scale.
The petitioner argued that he was given a revised pay scale which is normally applicable to the post of Field Investigator.
The court found the petitioner's behavior improper, and held that he could not argue that he was entitled to a higher pay scale after accepting the offered pay scale.
The principle of estoppel applies to the petitioner.
