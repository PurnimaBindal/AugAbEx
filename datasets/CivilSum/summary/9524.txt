Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate Appointment - Died-in-harness - Petitioner's mother, an Assistant Teacher, passed away in the line of duty on 121090 - Petitioner filed an application for employment on 29.4.92 - Claim for compassionate appointment rejected on the basis that application for employment by the ward of a teacher who died-in-harness must be submitted within two years of the teacher's death and that the ward must possess the required qualifications on the date of application submission - It was found that the impugned order was not flawed since the petitioner was not qualified for appointment under the rules governing compassionate appointment in the died-in-harness category.
