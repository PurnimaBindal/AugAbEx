Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 309 - Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Electrical) Regulations, 1965, Regulation 10(9) - Amendment of Rules/Promotion - The old rules govern posts that occurred before the amendment of rules, not the new rules - Eligibility for promotion to a post available before rule amendment cannot be taken away by rule amendment.
This ruling follows AIR 1983 SC 852.
