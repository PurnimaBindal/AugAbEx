Industrial Disputes Act 1947, Sections 10, 11 - Territorial Jurisdiction - The petitioner brought up a dispute in Chennai and all communication about their appointment was sent to Chennai.
Meanwhile, the respondent's registered office is in Delhi.
In spite of having worked in Gujarat previously, where the dispute arose, the tribunal at Chennai has jurisdiction over the case since the petitioner resides, works, or the cause of action has arisen in that location.
