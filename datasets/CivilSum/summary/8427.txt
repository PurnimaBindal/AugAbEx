Constitution of India Articles 16 and 226 - Promotion - Seniority - Petitioner, who was junior to second respondent, was placed in charge of the post of Revenue Inspector Grade I much prior to promotion of both - Later petitioner was placed to the next higher post in 1991, and the second respondent was promoted to that post 3 years later in 1994 - Seniority list naturally showed petitioner senior to the second respondent - However, second respondent gave a representation and authorities rectified the anomaly by placing the second respondent above the petitioner - Petitioner challenged this order - Court upheld the Corporation's decision to restore parity and found nothing unfair or irrational about it.
