The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 govern the appointment and selection of teachers based on their teaching experience.
In this case, the petitioner worked as a clerk in a Government School and also taught students, earning a certificate of teaching experience from the District Education Officer.
The advertisement for the post required such experience to be taken into consideration.
Other individuals with similar certificates and experience were appointed based on this criterion, and the petitioner was also eligible.
The respondents were therefore directed to consider the petitioner for appointment as a teacher based on his teaching experience as a clerk.
