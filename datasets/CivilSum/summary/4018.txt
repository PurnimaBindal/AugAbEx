The Appellant was appointed on a probationary basis for two years as a chemist, but their services were terminated due to alleged insubordination, indiscipline, and doubtful integrity without any notice of allegations.
According to clause (ix) of the Office Memorandum, during the deemed extended period of promotion, the shortcomings and deficiencies of the appellant should have been communicated, which did not happen.
As the appellant was on the verge of retirement, compensation of Rs. 3 lacs was awarded.
[Paras 12 & 16]  B.
The contention that the probationer is deemed to be confirmed due to probation not being extended is rejected as a specific order of confirmation is required for confirmation.
