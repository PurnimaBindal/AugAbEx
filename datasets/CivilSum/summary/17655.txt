Respondent A.S. Grewal, AAG, Punjab is involved in disciplinary proceedings where an interim order was issued due to the subject matter being absence from duty without authorized leave.
The passing of the final order was stayed during the admission of the petition.
As the enquiry proceedings have concluded with the report submitted, directions were given to issue the final order and provide all consequential benefits to the petitioner in the event that charges are not proven.
The interim order was vacated accordingly.
