Admission - Eligibility - Denial of admission - The appellants were denied admission in the third semester due to not fulfilling eligibility at the time of admission.
The court held that the appellants should not be deprived of the studies they have undertaken and canceling their admission at this stage would be a grave injustice.
[Paras 6 and 9] B. Misrepresentation - The appellants did not make any misrepresentations or play any fraud to secure admission.
Admission was granted by mistake and the court held that the appellants cannot be penalized for the mistake committed by the respondents.
