Constitution of India, Article 21 - Medical re-imbursement - Room rent of hospital - The petitioner was granted permission and referral for treatment in approved hospitals, including the AIIMS, due to the specialised treatment not being available in the State of Punjab's maintained hospitals.
The expenses for hotel room rent while being an in-patient is considered an integral part of the treatment expenses, and the government has a constitutional obligation to bear or reimburse medical expenses for retired and current government employees.
