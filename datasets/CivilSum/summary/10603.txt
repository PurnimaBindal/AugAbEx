The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 226 and 227, outline the appointment and cancellation of appointments, particularly regarding the concealment of facts or wrong information.
In this case, the petitioner left some columns unfilled, but these columns did not have any material impact and the petitioner did not receive any benefit by not filling them out.
Therefore, it cannot be alleged that the petitioner deliberately concealed any material information or facts, and their appointment has been restored.
[Paras 8 and 10]
