Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Selection - Petitioner applied for appointment as President of District Consumer Redressal Forum - 12 posts advertised - Petitioner interviewed but not selected - The candidates' eligibility for appointment on a public post cannot be confused with their suitability for the position - The Selection Committee is not obligated by law to make recommendations for all advertised posts - If the Committee determines that none of the interviewees are suitable for the position, they may decline to recommend any candidate, and the applicants cannot complain of arbitrariness or mala fides based solely on this decision.
