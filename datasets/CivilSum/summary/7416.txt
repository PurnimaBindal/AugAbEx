According to Section 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, the dismissal of a driver employed by Corporation in a domestic enquiry, for causing death and injury to individuals, does not necessitate reinstatement even if the driver is later acquitted by a court in a criminal case for the same offense.
This is because the employer may have lost confidence in the driver, and the criminal case outcome does not have bearing on the results of the departmental/domestic enquiry, which can hold the workman guilty even if found innocent in the criminal case.
