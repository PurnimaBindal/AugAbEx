Punjab National Bank Officers, Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1977 Regulations 6(3) (5) (iii) and 14 - Charge sheet - Inquiry - The penalty for the petitioner, who served as Junior Management Grade, Scale-I with the bank, was a reduction of salary by three stages in the time scale for acting fraudulently in connivance with the borrower, thereby jeopardising the interest of respondant bank according to Regulations 6(3) (5) (iii) and 14 in Punjab National Bank Officers, Employees (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1977 - Furnishing a list of witnesses by whom the articles of charge are proposed to be substantiated is not necessary to the delinquent official along with the charge-sheet, as in the case of an inquiry held under CCS (CCA) Rules, 1965 - The Inquiry Officer's findings are based on sufficient and relevant material, and the accusations were proved and established - The punishment imposed was neither disproportionate nor too harsh - The petition was dismissed - Constitution of India, Articles 226/227.
