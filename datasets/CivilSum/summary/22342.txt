On the basis of government instructions dated 23.7.1957 and 591979, higher qualifications can lead to a higher pay scale for teachers, but this is not automatic, and it is no longer available after the 1990 government order.
Even those who acquire higher qualifications while holding teaching positions are not entitled to a pay scale of a higher post, and those who held higher qualifications at the time of their appointment to lower posts were not entitled to higher pay scales under any order.
Only JBTs who acquired qualifications during their period of service were able to benefit from the 1970 order, not those who held higher qualifications prior to joining service.
[Paras 5 & 6]
According to Article 142 of the Constitution, even the Supreme Court cannot pass an order that goes against the statute or rules.
Relief is discretionary.
