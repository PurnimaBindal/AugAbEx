Based on Section 76 of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948 and Rule 16 of the Tamil Nadu Employees Insurance Court Rules, 1951, the normal rule is to institute proceedings before a court appointed for the local area in which the insured person was working at the relevant time.
However, there is an exception in Rule 16, which allows the place of suing to be where the cause of action wholly or in part arose.
The impugned order in this case was passed by the respondents within the jurisdiction of the Insurance Court at Chennai, making it the appropriate venue.
