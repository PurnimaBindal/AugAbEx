The validity of the implementation of orders from the Supreme Court regarding a promotional post in accordance with Article 136 of the Constitution of India is being questioned due to service rules being transgressed.
However, the intention of the government was only to implement the orders of the court without causing undue hardships to others.
The petitioner has already been promoted and therefore, there is no need for any interference under Article 136 of the Constitution.
