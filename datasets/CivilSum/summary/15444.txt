Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221 - Absence from duty - Illness - Repeated misconduct - Merely being ill is not a sufficient excuse to avoid reporting for duty or obtaining leave or permission - The offender must demonstrate sufficient reasons, cause, and disability - Once the punishing authority has considered and rejected the explanation as unsatisfactory, there is no reason to interfere with the punishing authority's decision, particularly when the offender's past conduct indicates uncorrectability.
