Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16(4) - The Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995, Section 33 - Appointment against reservation - Post of Lecturer - According to Section 33, there must be three per cent reservation for disabled individuals in every government appointment - The argument is made that this provision applies to Panjab University - However, since no reserved posts have been identified and only one post each for morning and evening sessions are advertised, no relief can be provided - Moreover, if the post is only one, reservation would amount to 100% reservation.
