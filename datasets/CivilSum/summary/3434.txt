Respondent Jagdev Sharma, AAG Haryana, argues that as per the Constitution of India, Article 16 regarding appointment, 125 general category posts were advertised and the petitioner's name was placed at Sr. No. 139 on the merit list.
However, since the advertised posts have been filled and the petitioner is not among the 125 selected candidates, she cannot claim appointment as of right.
The only circumstance under which relief could be granted is if the State had not filled the advertised posts, in which case the petitioner could be considered for vacancies occurring in the future.
