Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Termination - Contractual Appointment - The petitioners were temporary employees of canteen and their services could be dispensed with after giving notice - They were not appointed following the procedure consistent with provision of Articles 14 and 16 - It was held that in absence of any order of regular appointment, the employee cannot be considered to hold the post substantively - The order of termination was found to be legal.
