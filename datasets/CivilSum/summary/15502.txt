Constitution of India, Article 226 - Railway Service Conduct Rules, 1966, Rule 3.1(1)(iii) - Removal from service - Enquiry - Opportunity of hearing - The delinquent was accused of seeking employment with forged documents.
During the enquiry process, the delinquent was not provided with relevant documents nor given the chance to summon the witness he wanted to examine.
This amounted to denying him a reasonable opportunity to defend himself.
Therefore, the punishment of removal has been set aside and Respondent No. 1 is to be reinstated in service without back wages.
The petitioner has also been granted the liberty to initiate a fresh departmental enquiry against Respondent No. 1.
