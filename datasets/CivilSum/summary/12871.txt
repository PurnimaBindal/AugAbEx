Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Education - Reservation of seats for Tsunami victims - The petitioner, who was born and raised in Campbell Bay Island in Andaman and Nicobar and had studied up to 8th class there, was denied admission against the seat meant for Tsunami victims in MBBS course due to her shifting to Punjab on account of the disaster - It is argued that this denial of admission is unfair and not supported by law.
