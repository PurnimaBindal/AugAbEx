According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India and Panjab University Calendar, Vol.
I, 1989 Edition, Rules 22, 23, 24, eligibility for house allotment is based on seniority and pay scale.
Notional pay scale and notional seniority cannot be used to determine eligibility for house allotment, as they are theoretical and not based in reality.
The Committee appointed by the VC cannot make decisions that go against the rules, even for sympathetic reasons.
Therefore, the decision to give benefit of notional appointment as lecturers is quashed and house allotment should be based solely on actual pay drawn and seniority.
