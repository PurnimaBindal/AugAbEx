Constitution of India, Article 226 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1959, Rule 16 - Misconduct - Departmental Enquiry - The petitioner, who worked as a Patwari, committed gross irregularities by transferring land to a person whose name was shown as dead in revenue records to confer illegal benefits to another person.
The Enquiry Officer provided the petitioner with a full opportunity of hearing, and the punishment was stoppage of one annual grade increment with a cumulative effect.
Given the seriousness of the charges, no leniency could have been shown.
