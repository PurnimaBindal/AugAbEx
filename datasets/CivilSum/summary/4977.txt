Under Rajasthan Educational Subordinate Service Rules of 1971, Petitioner was placed on a waiting list for appointment as a Physical Education Teacher.
Although a vacancy occurred, they were not given the position as the select list had lapsed.
The select list's operative period was then extended to May 31st, 1998 and the authority was directed to reconsider the petitioner's case, according to Article 16 of the Constitution of India.
