The Admission to Technical Education, Engineering/Technology Courses criteria, as per Punjab State Board of Technical Education and Industrial Training Act, 1992, Sections 38, 39, and 40, are supplemented by the Examination Rules of the State Board of Technical Education and Industrial Training, Punjab, Rule 26.
The eligibility criteria laid in the prospectus mandating a minimum of 60% aggregate marks in the Diploma in Engineering and Technology in the relevant branch is justifiable.
Rule 26 has to be read as supplementary to the provisions of Section 40 of the Act.
There is a rationale behind the adopted evaluation system as laid down in Rule 26, and cannot be construed as limiting the operation of the prospectus.
