Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2A - Termination - Workman must serve a demand notice in a timely manner - Delay of four years in serving the notice in this case is deemed highly belated - The workman's resignation from service had been accepted by the petitioner society, so there is no explanation for the four-year delay - The workman's claim of undue pressure for submitting the resignation lacks supporting evidence - There is also no evidence on record of the workman's appeal to the Registrar of Societies - The Labour Court's previous award in favour of the workman is not tenable and is therefore set aside.
