Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Discrimination of project employees for regularisation - Unlawful appointments - Daily wage workers' services regularised on regular posts against the rules - No other daily wage workers can claim to be in parity based on such illegal action - Concept of parity being not negative but positive, discrimination is disallowed - Daily wage workers cannot seek regularisation against vacant posts - Public appointments cannot be distributed by engaging daily wage workers and regularising them against the rules without providing opportunity to all eligible candidates openly - Petition denied.
