Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume II, Rule 317-A - Post-retiral Benefits - A petitioner who retired on 15111994 was denied post-retiral benefits due to the fact that their services were regularised after they retired.
However, Rule 317 allows for ad hoc service followed by regular service to be treated as qualifying service if uninterrupted.
The court issued directions to calculate the benefits according to Rule 317.
[Paras 2, 3 & 4]
