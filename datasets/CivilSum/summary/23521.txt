Punjab Civil Services Rules, Rule 532 - Civil Procedure Code, Section 11 - Compulsory retirement - Res judicata - The appellant was compulsorily retired from service under Rule 532 of Punjab Civil Services Rules and his writ against retirement was dismissed by the High Court.
However, later on, the Supreme Court struck down Rule 532 in another case.
A civil suit was filed by the appellant in 1964 to be treated as in service and the suit was not barred by res judicata.
The order of the High Court was deemed void and inoperative and the relief was granted as claimed in the plaint.
