The Labour Court upheld the award reinstating a workman under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F, after the tribunal found that the respondent intentionally withheld work records to avoid providing employment and wages to the workman.
Additionally, the tribunal discovered that the workman had been continuously employed for 240 days through muster rolls, indicating that Section 25-F of the ID Act had not been followed.
