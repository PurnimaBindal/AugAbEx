Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rule 10 - Back wages - Arrears of wages - Review - Jurisdiction of review - The appellate authority under the Punjab Civil Services Rules cannot review or modify its own orders - If an employee is ordered to be reinstated with all consequential benefits, the authority cannot convert the period of back wages into leave without pay - The employee will become entitled to reinstatement and all back wages - Application under Section 33-C(2) is maintainable to recover all such wages including wages for the period he is not allowed to join his duties despite reinstatement orders.
