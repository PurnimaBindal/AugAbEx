Employees Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Sections 1 and 2(e) and 2(f) - Employee and employer - Asharam must meet certain tests - Object of the Asharam is only to provide relief to destitute women and widows on a no profit, no loss basis - Trainees receive nominal payment for their work but are not considered regular employees - They must seek employment elsewhere after completing their training - To apply the provisions of the Act, it is necessary to determine whether a relationship of master and servant exists between the trainees and the institute - The Act is not applicable to this Asharam - Petition allowed and impugned order quashed.
