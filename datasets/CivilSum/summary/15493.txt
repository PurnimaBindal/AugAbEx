Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 230 - Selection - Criteria of selection - Allocation of marks - Public Service Commission has the freedom to adopt any criteria if the recruitment rules do not prescribe any criteria - It is not permissible for candidates who take part in a selection process unsuccessfully to challenge the same criteria afterwards - If there is no written test, allocating 20% marks to higher qualification and the remaining 80% to interview is not unlawful - Allocating up to 50% marks for academic performance and the remaining 50% for interview is fair and reasonable, and does not have any element of arbitrariness.
