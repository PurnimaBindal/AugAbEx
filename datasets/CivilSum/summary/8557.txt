Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Promotion - Protection of pay - In cases of merger or integration of employees from different streams and departments, a fair principle of seniority should be created to balance the interests of all employees.
This should not cause too much detriment to existing employees of the corporation, into which the new employees are absorbed.
For retirement benefits and other service benefits, except promotion, service rendered by the new employees should be considered according to the directives given in GOs.

The new employees should have pay scales similar to or as close as possible to the pay scale of the parent corporation, and should receive no less than their current emoluments plus an increment.
They should also benefit from any pay scale revisions that occur from the date of their absorption.
