Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Regularisation - Punjab Government's instructions dated 22101982 to regularise ad hoc services of employees - Petitioner appointed as Clerk-cum-Typist on temporary basis under an order dated 2241980 - Petitioner applied for regularisation under the aforementioned instructions - Request was denied as petitioner not covered under the instructions - Decision was made to discontinue her services - However, during arguments, petitioner's counsel pointed out that petitioner was regularised on the basis of the instructions - Court held that the petitioner was fully covered by the instructions issued by the Government of Punjab - She fulfilled all the conditions laid down in the instructions - Therefore, she is entitled to be regularised on the basis of the instructions dated 22101982 and to all corresponding benefits.
[Paras 10, 11, 12, and 13]
