Constitution of India, Article 311 - Dismissal - Disciplinary proceedings - The petitioner teacher allegedly pulled the ears of one student in class, resulting in bleeding - No witness or teacher testified in the enquiry - The order of dismissal was disproportionate to the alleged misconduct - The disciplinary authority accepted the Enquiry Officer report without explanation - Dismissal order set aside due to the lack of evidence.
