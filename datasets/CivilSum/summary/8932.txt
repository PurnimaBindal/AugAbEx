Army Pension Regulations, 1961 (Part-1), Regulation 16 (a) - Dismissal from Army - Pension - When an officer is dismissed from Army, the President of India may order the forfeiture of either the whole or part of their pensionary benefits - In cases of minor offenses, the pension may not be forfeited, or only a minor cut may be imposed, but if the offense is severe, all pensionary benefits can be forfeited - The petitioner was dismissed after following the complete procedure - A notice was issued, and after considering their reply, an order of forfeiture was passed - The exercise of discretion was neither arbitrary nor unfair and did not require any interference.
[Paras 7 to 12]

Army Act, Section 71 - Army Pension Regulations, 1961, (Part-I), Regulation 16(a) - Pension - Dismissal from Army - Court Martial - Section 71(h) of the Act and Regulation 16(a) of Pension Regulations operate in distinct fields - Section 71(h) provides the punishments which can be awarded by the Court Martial - Whereas Regulation 16(a) contemplates a stage subsequent to the punishment and there is no inconsistency between the two.
[Para 10]

Army Pension Regulations, 1961 (Part-I), Regulation 16(a) Pension - Gratuity - Under the Regulation Pension includes gratuity also - If pension is forfeited, gratuity is also forfeited.
