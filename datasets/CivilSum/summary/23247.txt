Constitution of India, 1950, Article 14 - Change in service condition of employees - Transfer of employees from a Public Sector undertaking to a private management due to disinvestment - i) Employees cannot be transferred without their consent to another employer.
ii) Government or its instrumentality is not allowed to change the service conditions of its employees.
iii) Any alteration causing prejudice cannot be made without providing the opportunity of a pre-decisional hearing and would be arbitrary and violative of Article 14 of the constitution.
