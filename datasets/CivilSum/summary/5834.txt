Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act and Section 23(6) of the Rajasthan Panchayat Samitis and Zila Parishads Rules, 1959 - Illegal termination of a temporary/Ad hoc employee after 240 days without complying with Section 25-F of the ID Act.
[Paras 6, 7, and 8]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21, 39-A, and 41, Section 2(oo), and Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, and Section 23(6) of the Rajasthan Panchayat Samitis and Zila Parishads Rules, 1959 - Courts must adopt an interpretation of service rules that protects the livelihood of workers and their families.
Rules must reflect the spirit and object of Articles 14, 21, 39-A, and 41 of the Constitution.
The state is expected to provide livelihood to citizens and cannot illegally terminate jobs and violate statutory provisions.
Although the state cannot be compelled to provide jobs, it cannot arbitrarily and illegally take them away.
[Paras 9, 10, 15, 17, and 19 to 22]
Section 2(oo)(bb) and Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act - Non-renewal of contract of service or non-grant of extension in service is not covered under Clause (oo) of Section 2 of the I.D.
Act.
Compliance with Section 25-F is required for termination of services.
[Para 12]
Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Section 114 of the Evidence Act - Termination of services after assurance of regularisation may be considered arbitrary and violative of the principle of estoppel, even against the state.
Such termination may lead to social offenses and crimes.
In cases where services were recommended for regularisation, but not granted nor refused, such services will also be deemed regularised.
