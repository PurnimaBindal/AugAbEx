In accordance with Section 12(2) of the Workmen's Compensation Act, (8 of 1923), a principal employer is obligated to compensate for injuries inflicted on workmen during their employment.
This includes instances where a contracting party is involved, and the clause of indemnification from the contractor is applicable.
In a case where a department of State had installed a water pipeline, and the Assistant Engineer, acting on behalf of the principal, carried out the business activity of digging resulting in injury to workmen during blasting, the workmen are entitled to compensation from both the principal and the contracting party.
This was upheld in an appeal, and the decision was made to dismiss accordingly.
