The Court ordered the respondent to consider the petitioner for regular appointment to the post of LDC in the next available vacancy.
The respondent believed in good faith that the vacancy to which the petitioner was appointed on an ad hoc basis was not the next available vacancy and took steps to comply with the order.
The non-implementation of the Court's order due to a bona fide misinterpretation of the order by the authority concerned does not amount to contempt.
The respondent was not willfully disobedient, so no punishment was required.
The respondent was directed to take immediate steps for regular appointment to the post of LDC, and the case was to be considered along with other eligible candidates.
[Paras 4 and 5] B.
To be punished under the Contempt of Courts Act, disobedience of a Court's order must be willful.
