The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Ensuring equal pay for equal work - Administrative authorities are responsible for setting appropriate pay scales, as there is no set mathematical formula for equal compensation - Unless it is shown to be irrational, baseless or made with mala fide intent, courts should not interfere with the judgment of administrative authorities - Under Article 226 of the Constitution, courts have no jurisdiction to interfere with the factual finding of the Tribunal as long as it is based on material evidence.

Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Reference - Charter of demands - Government responsibility - The Government must consider whether the charter of demands is admissible or not - The management should challenge the reference's maintainability instead of submitting to the Tribunal's jurisdiction for the adjudication of an illegal reference; it is not appropriate to question the reference's non-application of mind by the appropriate Government after the management joins the proceedings before the Tribunal and allows an award to be pronounced.
