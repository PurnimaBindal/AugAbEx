Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Termination - Award - Labour Court found the termination to be illegal but refused relief, citing delay between notice of demand and proceedings before Conciliation Officer - Held, while the Labour Court could not completely deny relief based on this ground, it could have adjusted the relief as needed - Matter sent back for determination of proper relief.
