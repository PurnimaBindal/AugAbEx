According to the Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. 1, Rule 44, when JBT teachers are promoted to Head teachers, they must receive one higher responsibility increment.
This must be given to the promotees, as per Para 2.
In line with the provisions of Articles 21, 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, Rules must be followed when offering compassionate appointments.
Necessary qualification for the post must be insisted upon.
With de hors recruitment in public services, constitutional provisions of Articles 14 and 16 are violated, according to Para 4.
Selection is not an unconditional right for appointment and cannot claim a mandamus for appointment, unless it is not arbitrary.
Consideration is only warranted for a candidate, as per Para 4.
