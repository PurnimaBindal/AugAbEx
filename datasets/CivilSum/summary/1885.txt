Constitution of India, Article 233, 309 - Applicants - Vacancies advertised - It is held that applicants cannot claim a legal right to dispute the number of advertised vacancies or demand additional vacancies, as it is the appointing authority's prerogative to determine the number of appointments to make or posts to advertise.
