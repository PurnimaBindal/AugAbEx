Calicut University First Statutes, 1977, Chapter 2, Statute 36 - Age of Retirement - The implementation of UGC Scheme or directions of UGC cannot impact the alteration of University Statutes or Rules or Regulations made by the Government regarding the age of retirement of teachers of affiliated colleges.
This means that officers like the petitioner have no entitlement to continue in service beyond the age of 55 years as long as the statute governing their service conditions prescribes the age of retirement as 55 years.
