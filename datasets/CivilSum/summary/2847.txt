Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F - Retroactivity of amendment - The amendment to Section 2(oo)(bb) of the Industrial Disputes Act in 1984 does not apply retroactively, and prior retrenchments fall under Sections 2(oo) and 25-F and are not validated if they violated Section 25-F [Paras 9, 13 and 18]  B.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F - Workman - Daily wagers are considered workmen per the Industrial Disputes Act and are entitled to protection under the Act's provisions, including Sections 2(oo) and 25-F, regardless of their day-to-day employment status.
[Paras 15, 19, 21 and 22] C. Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo)(bb) and 25-F - Back wages - Gainful employment - In cases of illegal retrenchment, the burden is on the management to prove gainful employment of the workman during the termination period to deny back wages.
Reinstatement with full back wages is the typical remedy.
