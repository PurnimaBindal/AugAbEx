Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Admission - Sports quota - The applicant and respondent No. 5 had applied for the Elementary Teachers Training Course under the sports quota, with the former having 6250% marks and a `Grade-B' sports certificate and the latter having 6950% marks and a `Grade-C' certificate - R-5 was granted admission.
However, it was established that the grading of the petitioner was higher than that of respondent No. 5 and was therefore entitled to admission - While protecting the admission of R-5, the respondents were instructed to grant admission to the petitioner in the Elementary Teachers Training Course in the upcoming Session.
