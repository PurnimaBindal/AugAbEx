Constitution of India, Articles 12 and 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Public duty - Private industries and companies - Article 226 can only be invoked if the authority or person performs a public function or discharges a public duty - A company engaged in the manufacture and sale of cigarettes cannot be said to discharge any public function to maintain a writ for issuing direction of mandamus - Case law explained.

Factories Act, 1948, Section 46 - Canteen employees - Statutory canteen - As per Section 46 of Factories Act, management has a statutory obligation to set up a canteen for the welfare and health of its workers - When the employer has complete control, the canteen and its workers are deemed to be run by the management.
