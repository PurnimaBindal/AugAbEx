Constitution of India, Article 311 - West Bengal Board of Secondary Education Act, 1963, Section 24-Management of Recognised Non Government Institutions (Aided and Unaided) Rules, 1969, Rule 28 - Disciplinary proceedings - Respondent dismissed from service - Respondent was not provided with copies of the documents nor granted an opportunity to cross-examine the witnesses - The respondent was denied the right to examine witnesses in their favor - Copy of the enquiry report was not given, violating established principles of law and principles of natural justice - Departmental enquiry and the order of dismissal were against established principles of the law.
The appeal was dismissed.
