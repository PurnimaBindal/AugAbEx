Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Compassionate appointment - Rehabilitation Scheme in Orissa for families of non-Government Primary School teachers who suffered permanent incapacity or death - Scheme is only applicable going forward and cannot apply retrospectively - Disability or death needs to have occurred after the scheme took effect - To be appointed, the candidate must fulfil eligibility requirements or acquire necessary qualifications within three years from appointment date.
