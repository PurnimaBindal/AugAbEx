Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission Brochure, Para (C)(ii)(b) - Admission - Reservation - Eligibility - Bachelor of Engineering course - 85% quota reserved for residents of Punjab - The intention of this provision is to provide admission benefits to children of Punjab Government employees who were posted in Chandigarh but had to relocate before the entrance exam on January 1st.
- Despite the fact that the father of petitioner No. 2 was posted at Ferozepur when she appeared for CET-2000, the university was correct in not considering his posting at Chandigarh until January 13, 1998.
- As a result, the university's decision not to grant admission to the petitioner under para (C)(ii)(b) of the admission brochure cannot be criticised.
