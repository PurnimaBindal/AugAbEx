Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Jammu and Kashmir Reservation Rules, 1994, Rule 24-A - Reservation in admissions in Medical Colleges - Candidates from the reserved category cannot be disadvantaged by the right to reservation - If a reserved category candidate has higher merit and is eligible for admission in the general category, they will be considered as a general category candidate for seat allocation - However, if the candidate is better placed against the reserved seats in a preferential discipline/institute, they will also have the option to choose the reserved category for better merit and adjust accordingly - The candidate will have the option to choose a better discipline in the reserved category as well.
