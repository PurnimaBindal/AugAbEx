Benefits accrued under the unamended rules cannot be taken away by way of amendment in the rules later on, as specified in the Constitution of India, Article 14.
[Para 5]
The Demobilised Armed Personnel (Reservation of Vacancies in the Punjab State Non-Technical Services) Rules, 1968, Rules 3(1) and 5(1) state that vested rights accrued to individuals under older versions of the rule cannot be taken away by way of subsequent amendments, as clarified in paragraphs 5 and 6.
