Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Back wages - Reinstatement - The Labour Court/Tribunal U/s 11-A has extensive jurisdiction and discretion - They can determine that the inquiry was just and that misconduct was proven, but the order of dismissal or discharge was unwarranted and reinstate the employee on appropriate terms, even if back wages or part thereof are denied in light of the facts and circumstances of the case.
