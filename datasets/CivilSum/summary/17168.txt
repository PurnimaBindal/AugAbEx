According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, voluntary retirement can be accepted by the employer.
When the employee exercises this option, the acceptance cannot come with any condition for future employment.
[Para 15]

When an employee voluntarily retires and the employer accepts the request, the employer cannot later change their decision based on clarifications received from the government.
In the case of the respondent, the employer did not exercise their power to refuse the request for voluntary retirement.
[Para 16]
