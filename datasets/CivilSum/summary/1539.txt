Constitution of India, Article 311 - Compulsory retirement - Misconduct - The use of compulsory retirement as a short-cut method when a charge-sheet was not issued and suspension pay was not given cannot be sustained.
An order of compulsory retirement after abruptly withdrawing suspension of an employee on allegations of misconduct must be reviewed if no charge-sheet was issued, and no salary for the period of suspension paid.
The power of compulsory retirement cannot be used as a short-cut method.
[Paras 14, 15 and 16]
