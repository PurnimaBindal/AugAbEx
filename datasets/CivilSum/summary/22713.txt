Punjab National Bank Officers (Service) Regulations, Regulation 17 - Promotion to Middle Management Grade Scale II Circular No. 1271 dated 21.3.91 - Promotion - Eligibility - The petitioner, having accepted promotion and joined M.M.G.
Scale II, was required to complete two years of rural service for placement in either list A or B.
However, after not completing two years and subsequently being reverted to J.M.G.
Scale and posted in an urban branch, he becomes ineligible for promotion to M.M.G.
Scale-II for two years due to the bar of two years rural service becoming operative again.
