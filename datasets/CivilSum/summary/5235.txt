According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, monetary benefits should be provided for employees who work on Saturdays and Sundays.
The Supreme Court has already decided in the case of "Municipal Employees Union (Regd.)
Sirhind and others.
Vs. State of Punjab and others" Civil Appeal No. 8434 of 1997 on 1532000 that those who worked on these days are entitled to such benefits.
Therefore, if the petitioners have worked on these days, their claim cannot be denied, and the competent authority must pass appropriate orders in accordance with the judgment.
The petitioners may also invoke the provisions of section 33-C(2) of Industrial Disputes Act.
