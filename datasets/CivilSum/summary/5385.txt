Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Karnataka Selection of Candidates for Admission to Engineering, Medical and Dental Course Rules, 1977, Rule 18(ix) - Admission - Merit - Excluding meritorious candidates for the sake of speedy finalization of selection - Violates Article 14 - Although a swift finalization of the admission process is a legitimate concern, which may allow the Rule making authority to design procedures that facilitate a smooth and early completion of the process, any such measures must not disregard merit, which is the foundation of every selection process.
[Paras 12 and 14]
