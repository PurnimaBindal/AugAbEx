Rule 5(1) of the Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970 stipulates that the imposition of minor penalties must not affect promotion.
The petitioner was denied promotion as XEN on 1.1.1977 even though misconduct that warranted the censure penalty was more than five years old, and was thus inconsistent with the reasoning given by the respondents.
The action taken against the petitioner is therefore discriminatory and arbitrary.
