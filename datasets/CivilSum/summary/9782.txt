Rajasthan Medical and Health Service Rules, 1963, Rule 12 - Probation of Offenders Act, Section 4 - Termination of employment due to conviction under Section 323/147 IPC and release on probation - A conviction under Section 323/147 IPC is not considered moral turpitude and a person cannot be debarred from holding a civil post based on mere conviction - Order of termination is thus invalidated.
[Paras 13 to 15]
