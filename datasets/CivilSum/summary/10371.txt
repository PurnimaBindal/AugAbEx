Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Eligibility marks - Rounding off the marks - If the statutory Rules specify that persons with marks less than a specific number are ineligible, then the rounding off principle cannot be used to increase the marks to make them eligible - No notice is required for dismissal on disapproval of appointment subject to approval of academic committee/expert body unless there is a controversy - If the appointment is not approved, the appointing authority must discontinue the appointment without benefitting from rounding off the percentage of marks - The ruling in Rakesh Kumar's case opposing the Full Bench in Kuldip Singh's case is considered overruled.
