Constitution of India, Articles 226 - Appointment - A writ petition previously led to appointment to the post of Secretariat Assistants by merit list - A petition seeks direction to BPS Commission and its Chairman to recommend names of petitioners for each appointment in light of earlier writ petition - Compliance with High Court's order resulted in last candidate of each category being identified from 308 appointees whose appointments were not affected by revised merit list - Disclosure of facts makes case of petitioners weak - Commission's stand upheld - Petition dismissed.
