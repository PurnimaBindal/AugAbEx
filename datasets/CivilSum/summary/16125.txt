The petitioner, appointed as Govt. Food Inspector, had his reversion stayed by an interim order when his writ petition was admitted.
He was to continue holding the post until his case for regularisation was considered and decided.
Despite only being appointed for a six month period, the petitioner has now been in the role for about six years.
The government's own instructions dictate that he should be regularised, as his uninterrupted tenure and all the requisite qualifications make him suitable.
This regularisation must be considered either by payment or decision.
[Paras 4 and 5]
