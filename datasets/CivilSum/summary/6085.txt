Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 226 - Reimbursement of emergency treatment expenses - In ordinary cases, competent authority must give advance approval of expenses.
Technicality of rules applies.
However, in emergency cases, patient cannot be expected to wait for approval at risk of further harm - even if treatment from unapproved hospital - authority must reimburse amount, at least at approved hospital rate.
[Paras 7 to 10]
