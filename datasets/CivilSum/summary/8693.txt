Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules, 1957, Rule 6 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Civil Procedure Code, Order 9 Rule 7 - Ex-parte proceedings - To set aside ex-parte proceedings, the petitioner must have been served with a notice - In an application made before passing the award, the applicant should be given the chance to support their stand with evidence - The Labour Court must record the statement of the Court peon who served notices - The Labour Court must determine whether services were terminated in violation of the Industrial Disputes Act, even if the management is proceeded ex parte - In this particular case, the award was quashed and the case was remanded to the Labour Court.
