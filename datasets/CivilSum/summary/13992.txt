According to Rule 19(ii) of the Delhi Police (Promotion and Confirmation) Rules 1980, an employee promoted on an out of turn basis will not be considered for seniority if they receive regular promotion in the same year.
Specifically, the respondent was promoted both on an out of turn basis on 5894 and on a regular basis on 18894, so their seniority should be calculated from the later promotion date.
Furthermore, the application filed by the respondent before the Administrative Tribunal more than five years after the promotion is barred by time.
