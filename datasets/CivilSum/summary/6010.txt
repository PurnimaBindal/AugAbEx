Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226, 227 - Haryana Khadi and Village Industries Board (Services) Regulation 1976, Regulations 9(2) and 9(3) - Reversion - Probation - Deemed Completion - Petitioner returned to post of Junior Auditor from Head Accountant - Petitioner argues that reversion order is invalid as probation period is completed - Respondents argue that reversion is allowed per Regulation 9 if probation was not satisfactory - Maximum probation period is three years per Regulation 9 Clause (3) - Decision had to be made before probation period ended - Therefore, employee is deemed confirmed - Petition allowed.
