Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Sections 7 and 12 - Contract labour - If the Principal Employer is not registered under Section 7 and the Contractor does not have a Section 12 license, persons employed by the Principal Employer through the Contractor will not become employees of the Principal Employer - Both the Principal Employer and the Contractor may be liable for prosecution for any Sections 7 and 12 violations - 1991(2) Delhi Lawyer 189 and 1991(1) PLR 1 has been approved, while 1990 FLR 686 and 1985(1) LLJ 492 (Madras) have been overruled.
[Paras 20 and 22]  B.
Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Sections 7, 10, 23 and 25 - The Act regulates the employment of contract labour in certain establishments and provides for its abolition in certain circumstances but does not provide for its total abolition.
The appropriate Government has to abolish the contract labour under Section 10.
