Under Section 21 of the Railway Protection Force Act, 1957, and Rules 57 and 67 of the Railway Protection Force Rules, 1987, a probationary constable's service can be terminated for withholding information.
In this case, the respondent did not disclose the pendency of two cases in their declaration form, leading to termination.
It should be noted that while the respondent was later discharged from these cases, an employer has the right to terminate a probationary employee if information provided during the hiring process is discovered to be incorrect.
In this case, the respondent's lack of integrity in withholding information could not be tolerated in a job requiring a higher level of honesty.
