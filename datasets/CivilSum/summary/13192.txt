Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Pecuniary benefit - Petitioner to receive payment from the date his junior with same qualifications were promoted - Denial of benefit due to petitioner not working on promotional post, despite no fault of his own - Court orders respondents to consider petitioner's case for payment of arrears and all pecuniary benefits of the promotion post for the relevant period.
