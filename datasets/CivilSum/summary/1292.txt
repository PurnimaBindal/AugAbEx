The Army Institute of Law is registered under the Societies Registration Act and is not subject to government control.
As a private body, it is not classified as a company under section 617 of the Companies Act.
Therefore, protection under Section 47 of the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995 is not extended to the petitioner.
The respondents did not violate any statutory rules in the termination of the petitioner.
No intervention is warranted in the termination order.
