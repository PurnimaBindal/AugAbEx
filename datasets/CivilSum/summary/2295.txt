The Enquiry Report under the Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965 – Disagreement by disciplinary authority – Reaching a finding unilaterally by issuing a show-cause notice is unacceptable, as it indicates a biased state of mind and taints the entire proceeding with lack of impartiality – The authority must state reasons for its disagreement and point them out to the delinquent to allow for effective representation, and the disagreement must be tentative.
[Paras 12, 15 and 17] B. Disciplinary Proceedings under the Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965 – Seeking advice from Union Public Service Commission – Legality – UPSC’s recommendation for dismissal is invalid because the disciplinary authority unlawfully presented material to UPSC prior to coming to its decision.
[Para 7] C. The framing of charges under the Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965 – Use of the phrase "delinquent is guilty of charges" – Prejudging the issue – It amounts to pre-deciding the matter, as the disciplinary authority is required to take a final decision after the parties provide evidence and an enquiry report is submitted.
