Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 300-A - Premature Retirement Rules, 1975 - Pension - Gratuity - Qualifying service - Period of leave - The holder of office of Chairman cannot have his pay as Chairman made the basis for calculation of his pension, even if he counts the period for purpose of grant of pension.
The only benefit conferred on the petitioner is limited to counting of period during which a Govt. servant has remained as Chairman or Member in the service for the purpose of calculation of pension.
