According to Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226, Promotion cannot be claimed as a matter of right by an employee, but they can claim the right of consideration for appointment/promotion.
[Para 7]
Constitution of India, Article 16, enshrines equal opportunity of consideration for appointment, and seniority must be considered before promotion.
A senior person cannot be ignored without considering their claim and suitability for appointment.
It's important to avoid a pick and choose method for appointments.
Violation of Article 16 has occurred because the petitioner's case for being appointed as `B' Class Naib Tehsildar candidate from the post of Ziledar wasn't considered.
[Para 7]
Writ Petition was filed in 1981 but was dismissed in default of appearance of the counsel.
After the SLP was filed in 1985 and allowed by SC, the case was remanded back for decision on merits.
No delay occurred in approaching High Court for the relief claimed for.
[Para 8]
The Constitution of India, Article 16, Appointment & Promotion makes it mandatory for the respondents to consider the petitioner's case for appointment as `B' Class Naib Tehsildar candidate from post of Ziledar.
The promotion should date back to the point when Ziledars junior to the petitioner were given the same promotion.
