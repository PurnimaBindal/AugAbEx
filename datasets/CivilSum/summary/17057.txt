According to Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 Sections 12(3) and 18(1)(3), settlements may be reached through two methods - one out of conciliation proceedings and another before the Conciliation Officer.
Settling before the Conciliation Officer binds both parties.
[Paras 6 and 7]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 Section 25-F indicates that a workman must be reinstated with full back wages if there is no proof of gainful employment during the forced unemployment period.
