Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 2(e) - Applicability of - Trainee - The definition of "apprentice" as mentioned in Section 2(e) of the Act does not apply to trainees.
Therefore, a petitioner who was appointed as a trainee cannot be considered as an apprentice since they were not designated to learn any specific trade.

Interpretation of statute - When interpreting provisions of any Act, the court must read them literally in their ordinary, natural, and grammatical meaning used by the legislature.
Additionally, the object of the Act must be taken into account during interpretation.
