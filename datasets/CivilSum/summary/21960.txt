Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Transfer - Joining - Withholding pay for the period between transfer order and joining after prolonged period - If the employer prevents the employee from joining and the employee is not responsible for it, the employee must not be denied wages for the period - Nevertheless, if it is accepted that the employee did not work for that duration, justice will be served by instructing the employer to pay 50% of the salary and allowances for that time.
