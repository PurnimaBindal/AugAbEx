Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Retrospective Promotion - Challenged - The respondent, who was promoted on probation on August 10, 1989, but reverted to the original post due to unsatisfactory work, cannot be placed senior to the petitioner promoted on February 25, 1991, by giving him retrospective promotion w.e.f August 10, 1989.
The court held that the respondent's reversion was good as his work was not up to the mark.
The respondent board cannot overlook the court's order and pass a fresh order giving him retrospective promotion.
Therefore, the fresh order was quashed, and the petition was allowed.
