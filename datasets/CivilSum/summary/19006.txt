Constitution of India, Article 226 - Education - Examination - The petitioner submitted an examination form for Hindi compulsory as a deficient subject of BA I alongside BA III examination.
However, the college did not forward the examination form for Hindi compulsory due to clerical error.
The college admits to its mistake.
When a student submits an application form to the college, the college acts as an agent for collecting the form on behalf of the university.
Since the mistake is of the college, the petitioner cannot be allowed to suffer.
The respondent directed that the petitioner should be permitted to appear in Hindi compulsory as the deficient subject of BA I.
[Paras 5 and 6]
