Civil Procedure Code, Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 - Promotion - Interim stay - The managing director of the bank rejected the petitioner's representation regarding his promotion in 1995 and he was consequently reverted, while another person was promoted - The plaintiff challenged this in a suit - The M.D.
complied with the orders of the High Court in a Writ Petition - The court can grant interim orders only when it is convinced of the three basic ingredients and is satisfied that it is in public interest to do so - Interim orders in matters of appointment, promotion, confirmation, seniority or retirement etc. should be granted with great care because the court rarely stays the operation of government orders solely based on a prima facie case of the petitioner.
