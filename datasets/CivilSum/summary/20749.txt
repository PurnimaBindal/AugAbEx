The cancellation of appointments of course completed Apprentices to rectify a bona fide mistake of the appointing authority and giving them appointments in Group D, as per a decision made by the Railway Administration after discussions with worker unions, was deemed fair and not in violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
The decision, made at the Headquarter level on 27111989, stated that only promotions from Group D to Group C posts would be allowed, with no direct entry to Group C posts.
Direct appointments made after this decision but prior to its communication to the Divisional level were canceled.
[Paras 10 to 14]
