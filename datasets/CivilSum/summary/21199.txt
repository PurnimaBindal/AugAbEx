According to Rajasthan Judicial Service Rules, 1955, Rule 9(3), Munsiff Magistrate appointments should have a 20% reservation for women, but the selection process treated this as a vertical rather than horizontal reservation within the vertical reservation.
This led to impermissible selection of further women from the reserved category, despite 11 women already being selected in the general category.
However, rather than disturbing the selection list, appellants were directed to be accommodated.
It was also held that the selection process adopted by RPSC was contradictory to the reservation policy contained in Rule 9(3) of the Service Rule.
