Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Admission - Reservation - Legitimacy of - The reservation of 150 seats for GMC Amritsar and Patiala and 50 seats for GGSMC at Faridkot is being contested as violative of Article 14 - The rationale behind the reservation was to address staffing shortages and procure qualified staff for the faculty - Additionally, the benefit of reservation was limited to staff who had served for at least five years continuously - The petitioner himself had applied and took the test - Thus, he cannot now challenge the legitimacy of the reservation.
