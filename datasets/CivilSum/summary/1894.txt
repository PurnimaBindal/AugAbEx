As per Constitution of India, Article 226, the competent authority holds the power to issue appropriate directions in furtherance of a brochure and in conformity with the original policy after providing due notice to the candidates.
However, the subsequent instructions must be reasonably declared in advance to the counselling so that all candidates are informed.
(Rajiv Kapoor v. State of Haryana, JT 2000(3) SC 635 relied) [Para 13]
