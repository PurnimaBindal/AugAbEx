The Punjab P.W.D.
(Electricity Branch) Provincial Service Class III (Subordinate Posts) Rules of 1952, Rule 9 state that both graduate and non-graduate UDCs are eligible for promotion and regarded as fit.
However, the quota system prevents a non-graduate UDC from being promoted to a vacancy reserved for graduate UDCs, even if they are more senior and more qualified.
This limits their opportunity for promotion and constitutes a denial of equal opportunity.
[Paras 7 and 8]
The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 prohibit discrimination, and the Punjab P.W.D.
(Electricity Branch) Provincial Service Class III (Subordinate Posts) Rules of 1952, Rule 9's quota system cannot discriminate between graduate and non-graduate UDCs with the same joint seniority list.
Even if the required qualification for promotion is not a BA, both UDCs should be promoted based on the fixed quota.
[Paras 6 and 7]
According to the joint and common seniority list of UDCs in currency from August 31, 1972, to April 1979, the plaintiff was senior to them in the Punjab P.W.D.
(Electricity Branch) Provincial Service Class III (Subordinate Posts) Rules of 1952, Rule 9 seniority system.
Although a fresh seniority list was declared null and void, the suit cannot be held bad for non-joinder as concurrent findings by two courts below are affirmed.
