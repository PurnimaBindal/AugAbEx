Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 226, 14 and 16 - Promotion - Eligibility - Performance evaluation based on "seniority cum merit" - Appellant and respondent both scored 30 marks in the interview, but the respondent received a "very good" rating in the annual performance appraisal for the previous three years and was awarded 32 marks while the appellant only received a "good" rating and was allotted 24 marks - One of the grounds for denying promotion to the respondent was that he lacked the required educational qualification, although this was not made clear at the time - The other ground was that the officer who evaluated his performance in one of the years was not competent, but no malice was alleged - Even if the evaluation for the year in question was excluded, the respondent would still have had a better performance record than the appellant, and educational qualification was not relevant to his promotion - As a result, the appeal was dismissed.
