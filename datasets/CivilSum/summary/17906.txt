Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority/Regularisation of services/Hearing/Natural justice - Duly appointed petitioners through departmental committee must have their services regularised from their joining dates, and their seniority assigned accordingly.
However, their seniority was disturbed without giving them an opportunity to defend themselves against the representations of promotees.
The government must provide an opportunity for petitioners to bring all relevant facts in their favour before taking any action.
The government's decision to disturb seniority without giving the affected persons a hearing violates the principles of natural justice and denies them justice.
Therefore, the order must be quashed due to the violation of principles of natural justice - Punjab PWD (Irrigation Branch) Draftsmen and Tracers State service Class-III Rules, 1960.
