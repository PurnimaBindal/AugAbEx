Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - Reinstatement - Back wages - Respondent No. 1, who had been working as a driver since 1965, was terminated from his services on 3.8.79 without any enquiry being conducted.
He was removed for absence from duty despite notices being issued.
The court held that since no enquiry was held, it was illegal to remove a driver with such a long standing record and the order of termination was quashed.
