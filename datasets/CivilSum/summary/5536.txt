Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(a) and 33-C(2) - Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Recovery of arrears of salary - Quashing of award - Evidence - A teacher who was employed and paid a consolidated salary cannot be considered as a `workman' as teachers in educational institutions are not classified as `industries' - As a result, the Labour Court was unable to accept or decide the employee-teacher's application for money recovery under Section 33-C(2) of the Act because he was not a workman under Section 2(a) - If the Labour Court made an award without authority, this Court is not prohibited from exercising extraordinary jurisdiction - It is not legitimate for the employee-teacher to argue that this Court shall not interfere with the Labour Court's award in writ jurisdiction.
