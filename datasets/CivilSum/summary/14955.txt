According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, Admission for a part-time course in engineering states that the petitioner, who was successful in an entrance examination, was unable to attend college on the date of admission due to illness.
The petitioner failed to provide any explanation as to why he could not inform the respondent about his health condition.
Additionally, there were no vacancies available and therefore, the petitioner is not eligible for admission.
