After being selected and appointed as Scientist Fellow under the Quick Hire Scheme of CDRI, a reduction in pay from Rs. 3737/- to 3400/- was recommended by the selection committee without any valid justification.
The CAT upheld the direction for Scientist Fellows to receive a payment of Rs. 3737/- pm under the Quick Hire Scheme.
[Para 12] B.
The contractual appointment as Fellow Scientist of CDRI under the Quick Hire Scheme is extendable up to three years, and a contract termination is valid with three months' notice.
However, the extension for only six months was considered arbitrary by the court.
The services of the Fellows appointed under the two schemes are not governed by CSIR Service Rules, 1994.
The purpose of the Schemes is to protect the interest of the nation from brain drain, but the CSIR should not give shabby treatment to scientists.
