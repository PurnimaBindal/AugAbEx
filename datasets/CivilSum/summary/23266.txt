Employees of Punjab State Co-operative Agricultural Development Bank Ltd can be prosecuted under Prevention of Corruption Act as they are considered public servants under sections 12(2) and 7 of the act.
The definition of a public servant as stated in section 2(c)(ix) of the same act is also applicable.
This is in accordance with sections 2(d), 2(f), 2(g) and 10 of the Punjab Cooperative Agricultural Development Banks Act, 1957.
