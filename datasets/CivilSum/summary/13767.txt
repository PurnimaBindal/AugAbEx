Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Examination - Shortage of lectures - Medical certificate - Validity - Petitioner suffered from Chicken Pox and was unable to attend all classes, resulting in a shortage of lectures - Roll No. withheld by college for annual exams - The fact that petitioner chose to consult a private medical practitioner for the certificate should not raise doubts about its truthfulness - Respondents ordered to provide the petitioner's internal assessment marks.
