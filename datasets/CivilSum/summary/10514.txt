Article 113 of the Limitation Act pertains to termination and declaration.
The Act stipulates that seeking declaration for null and void termination order has a limitation of three years.
If this time frame lapses, the court is unable to grant the declaration, and the suit is dismissed for being barred by limitation.
This means that Article 113 applies in such cases, and it cannot be claimed that no limitation is prescribed against a null and void termination order.
