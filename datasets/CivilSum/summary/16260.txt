Punjab Transport Department (State Service Class III) Rules 1963, Rules 6(1)(a) and (b) Promotion - The petitioner was promoted to the position of Superintendent and after three years, he was entitled to promotion as Traffic Manager, and after seven years as General Manager.
However, his case for promotion as Traffic Manager was not considered at the due date, and his further claim to be considered for promotion as General Manager was contested on the grounds that he does not belong to the Transport Service.
It was held that after promoting him to the cadre of Superintendent and Traffic Manager, it cannot be argued that he doesn't belong to the cadre.
The court issued directions to consider his case for promotions on all due dates and if found fit, to give him all deemed promotions and consequential benefits.
[Paras 3, 4 and 5] B Deputation/Promotion - In cases where there are no specific rules regarding promotion, an employee cannot claim promotion as a right.
Posts can be filled by taking candidates on deputation, and this does not constitute an illegality or violation of any right.
