The provision under Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical Council Act, 1956, Sections 5, 10 and 33 - Establishment of Medical College - Essentiality Certificate - Denial of - Objection regarding compliance in providing clinical materials and non-availability of required number of patients - is not requiring actual number of patients but hospital making provision for atleast 300 beds with necessary infrastructure facilities.
Single judge's direction for issuing Essentiality Certificate upheld as nothing incriminating stated by Committee in inspection report.
[Paras 8, 9] B State Government has got no say in the matter of establishing a new medical college except verifying qualifying criteria for issuance of essentiality certificate regarding desirability and feasibility of having proposed college at proposed location.
The requirement of obtaining essentiality certificate from State Govt. cannot be equated with permission for establishment of college.
Central Government alone has the authority to decide policy considerations in such matters, and the certificate cannot be withheld by State Government on any policy consideration.
