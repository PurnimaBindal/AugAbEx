Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 Suspension/Domestic Enquiry - Clerk-cum-Cashier in a Bank accused of misappropriation of money - Despite acquittal by the Trial Court, petitioner was kept under suspension during domestic enquiry- Petitioner sought relief of reinstatement through writ petition during the pendency of domestic enquiry - In deserving cases, alternate remedy does not bar the grant of relief.
Respondents ordered to reinstate petitioner and provide consequential reliefs.
