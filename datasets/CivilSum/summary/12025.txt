Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2 - Absence from duty - Serious misconduct - Members of the disciplinary police force must maintain the utmost discipline and devotion to duty.
Unauthorised long absences, especially before completing five years of service, are a serious misconduct.
Even if the termination order does not explicitly state it, the court can infer such misconduct from the facts of the case.
The court does not interfere with such administrative orders.
[Paras 9 to 11]
