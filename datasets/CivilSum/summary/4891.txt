The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 25T, 31, and 34 - Unfair Labour Practice - Filing of Complaint - It is not enough to merely review material, the government must make a determination regarding the propriety of filing a complaint.
[Para 15]
The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 34 - Prosecutor Authorization - Breach of Settlement or Award - The appropriate government needs only consider whether an award or settlement is valid, its terms, and whether compliance has been met.
