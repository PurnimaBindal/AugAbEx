The Labour Court found that the delinquent had engaged in misconduct, including entering the factory while intoxicated and misbehaving with his superiors and security guards, leading to his dismissal under Section 11 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
Evidence clearly established the delinquent's misconduct, and the Labour Court, while declining relief of reinstatement with back wages, provided him with compensation.
According to Section 11 and Schedule 11, Item 3 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the award passed by the Labour Court was proper.
