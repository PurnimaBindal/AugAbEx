"The Railway Protection Force Act (23 of 1957), Sections 9 and 21, along with Rule 44 of the Railway Protection Force Rules (1959), state that employees can be removed from service.
However, in the case of the petitioner who was charged with theft, there was no evidence to link them to the theft and they were not on duty at the time of the offence.
The only evidence against the petitioner was that they obstructed an enquiry by making a witness runaway.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to reinstatement with back wages.
[Paras 12, 18]"
