According to Article 16 of the Constitution of India, transfers must be made only if there is a existing cadre.
The transfer of the petitioners due to compassionate grounds was made on the assumption that their cadre existed, which was incorrect.
The transfer order was then changed to a deputation order.
The courts have held that since the cadre of the petitioners did not exist, it was not illegal for the authorities to treat them as deputees, as long as their seniority in their present cadre was maintained.
