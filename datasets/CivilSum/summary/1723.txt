Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertaking) Act, 1970, Section 19 - Encashment of privilege leave on voluntary retirement - Recovery of - Bank debited account of petitioner for alleged excess payment due to wrong calculation by bank officials - No prior notice or opportunity was given - Court held that such action by bank is illegal and equivalent to criminal breach of trust and misappropriation of funds - Since there was no fraud or misrepresentation by petitioner, bank is not entitled to recover any amount in such manner - Court has directed the bank to refund the amount debited along with interest at the rate of 9% per annum.
