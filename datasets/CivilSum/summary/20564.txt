Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Dismissal from service - Back wages - Labour Court unjustified in refusing back wages and bonus to workers following dismissal without Industrial Tribunal approval - Management's statement before the Tribunal that the impugned order was withdrawn caused workers to be taken back into service from the date they were stopped from working - Management ordered to pay back wages and bonus with 12% interest - The management's statement before the Industrial Tribunal held more weight than any written order that could have been issued.
