The petitioner cannot claim appointment to the post of Nursing Orderly as neither the Advisor nor Dy.
Director Health Service passed any order of appointment and the endorsement of the Advisor was recommendatory in nature.
Furthermore, the government order No. 462-GR of 1990 mandates the constitution of a Recruitment Board for filling up Class IV posts and the Advisor to Governor does not have the power to appoint the petitioner.
