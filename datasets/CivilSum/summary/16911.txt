Constitution of India, Article 311 - Dismissal - Unauthorized absence - After developing a heart disease and being under treatment for 20 years of service, a petitioner was issued with a charge-sheet and removed from service after an inquiry - The petitioner argued that there was no opportunity to produce evidence and that less punishment could have been given in view of their years of service - Medical certificates for the period in question were presented by the petitioner and the period of absence was regularized by authorities by treating it as leave without pay - It was held that the punishment awarded was too harsh and that stoppage of three increments with cumulative effect would be sufficient to meet justice - The petition was disposed of accordingly.
