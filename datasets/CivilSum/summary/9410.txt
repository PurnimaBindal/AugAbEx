Challenge to the sports quota policy for the posts of Physical Training Instructor Grade II and Grade III, on the grounds that it treats national and international level sportsmen the same as those who simply participate and that the government should have separate categories or provide bonus marks for participation.
However, the court held that it is not within its jurisdiction to interfere in government policy decisions regarding bonus marks or conditions for selection under sports quota.
The objective of the policy is to boost participation in sports tournaments and to include a larger pool of candidates.
Therefore, the court cannot mandate the government to increase the limit or provide a separate category.
