Constitution of India, Article 226 - Unauthorised absence - Dismissal - Enquiry held exparte - Enquiry report not served - Appellate and Revisional Authorities mechanically confirmed order of disciplinary authority - The orders demonstrate a lack of consideration and application of mind on the reasons given - In this case, the Tribunal found that the punishment was disproportionate and upheld the observation that the appellate and revisional authorities erred in not giving the respondent an opportunity to be heard.
