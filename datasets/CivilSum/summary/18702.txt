The petitioner was appointed to a regular post as a peon under the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, but was terminated due to being medically unfit.
The petitioner was repeatedly medically examined and found to be fit for the job after every two years of service.
His termination was deemed illegal and without justification, and the authorities were ordered to reinstate him to a regular post with all associated benefits.
