Under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(a)(ii), the term "Appropriate Govt." is similar to that in Sections 2(b)(ii), while under the Minimum Wages Act, 1948, Sections 2(6)(ii) and 5(1)(b), the law only relates to procedure.
In addition, the Constitution of India, Articles 226,227 and 239, and the General Clauses Act, Section 8(6)(iii) all reinforces the fact that the State Government is obligated to propose the revision for minimum wages, and whenever the term "State Govt." is used in connection to a Union Territory, the Central Govt. would represent the State Govt. and the Administrator as an agent or delegatee of the Presidential of India.
