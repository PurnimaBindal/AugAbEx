Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination - Services of the petitioner Assistant Government Pleader terminated - According to the writ petition, the impugned order violates the principles of natural justice because the petitioner was not given a reasonable opportunity before terminating her services and termination may adversely affect her professional reputation - However, the termination of petitioner's services does not result from any disciplinary measure but rather a simple termination of relationship - Consequently, the order does not reflect poorly on the petitioner's capacity and integrity in her function as Assistant Government pleader - It does not cast any negative light on the petitioner - Thus, the order will not hinder the petitioner's future appointments to any public office.
