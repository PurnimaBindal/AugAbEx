Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Army Act, 1950, Sections 164(2) and 179 - Dismissal - A Lance Naik in the Army was dismissed from service by rigorous imprisonment for one year - A petition against the Chief of Army Staff submitted - The case was not decided - A writ stated that the Chief of the Army Staff did not pass any order on the petition under Section 164(2) of the Act - The case was remanded for consideration and decision - The Chief of the Army Staff would keep in mind the fact that the petitioner had to serve the full sentence of one year due to the order of remission likely not being conveyed to jail authorities - If the Chief of the Army Staff/Competent Authority consents to the petitioner's claim upon reviewing the petition, consequential relief derived from the order shall be implemented.
