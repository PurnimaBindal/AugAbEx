Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Pay scale - Workcharged service - A rationalization scheme was adopted by the Management and Workers Union to benefit workcharged service towards seniority, promotion and higher pay scale.
Petitioners, who were in a lower pay scale than their juniors at the time of rationalization, cannot claim equality.
However, they are entitled to include their workcharged service towards seniority and promotion.
