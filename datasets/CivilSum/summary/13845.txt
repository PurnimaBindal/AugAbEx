Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 300 - Pay scale - Revision of pay scales - The plaintiff demanded arrears of revised pay scales and the defendant DRDA initially refused due to financial constraints.
However, the defendant eventually released the arrears during the pendency of the suit.
The plaintiff claimed interest on the delayed payment of arrears.
The court held that the defendant did not intentionally withhold the arrears of pay but did so due to a shortage of funds.
The courts below declined the prayer for interest, and the view was held to be just and fair.
No interference was called for.
[Paras 3 and 4]
