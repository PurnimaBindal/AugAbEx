Constitution of India, Article 226 - Voluntary retirement scheme - A second voluntary retirement scheme was introduced on 2571988, which provided more benefits than the earlier scheme - If an application was not considered before the second scheme came into force, workmen shall be entitled to the benefits of the latter scheme - Terminal benefits under the second scheme, which have been deposited, should be paid to the relevant respondent - As a welfare legislation, the beneficial provision in favour of workmen should be construed liberally in line with the object and intendment of Labour Legislation.
[Paras 2 and 3]
