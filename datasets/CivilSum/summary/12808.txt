Challenge to minimum age requirement for private candidates taking 8th Class examination when no such age is prescribed for regular students, petitioner had been regularly studying for 3 years in a private school and should have been eligible to appear for the 5th Class examination on a particular date so is entitled to appear for the 8th Class examination after 3 years of regular studies - Board should have taken into consideration the cut off date so fixed for 5th Class examination as prescribed by the Punjab Govt - Petitioner wrongly deprived of appearing for 8th Class examination - Board directed to take examination for petitioner by setting out equivalent papers.
[Paras 6, 7, and 8] B.
Standard of examination can be found out by examiner/paper setters who are experts in the line, and should not pose any problem for respondent-Board to set an equivalent paper for petitioner, given that the syllabus for the petitioner and students who appeared in the examination is the same - Constitution of India, Articles 226/227.
