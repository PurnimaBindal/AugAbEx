Back Wages - The petitioner was not at fault for her removal from the rolls and had diligently performed her duties until then.
The removal was arbitrary and there is no evidence that she was employed during her forced idleness.
The learned single judge did not provide a reason for denying 50% backwages, so the petitioner is entitled to full backwages from the date she was illegally kept out of service until payment.

Back wages - Whether an individual is entitled to backwages after an order of termination is declared invalid depends on the specific circumstances of the case.
It cannot be assumed that full backwages are always due.
Reference was made to 2006(1) SCC 479.
