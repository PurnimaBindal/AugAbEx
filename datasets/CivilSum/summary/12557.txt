Provisional arrangements for promotions cannot guarantee any employee an indefeasible right, and employees without the necessary qualifications cannot be considered for regular appointments.
The petitioner's provisional posting cannot be regularised simply because they have worked for more than three years.

The normal line of promotion for T-Mates is through the post of Assistant Lineman to Shift Attendant, so the petitioner's claim for direct promotion to Shift Attendant is not valid.
[Para 10]

Petitioners doing the same job as regular T-Mates are entitled to equal pay for equal work, regardless of the nature of the job.
Even a daily wager is entitled to the same wages as regular employees doing the same job.
