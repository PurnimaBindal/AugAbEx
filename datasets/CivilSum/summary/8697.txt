Under Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, the length of service is a crucial factor in determining gratuity.
In the case at hand, a reference under Section 10(1) determined that the petitioner had 18 years of service and was entitled to all benefits as a regular employee.
Further, the Labour Court was bound by the Award under Section 10(1) and erred in denying the petitioner's entitlement to gratuity based on failure to prove length of service.
