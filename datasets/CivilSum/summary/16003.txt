Regarding Respondent Arun Walia, AAG, Punjab, under the Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Appointment - Qualifications - Experience - Posts of Masters/Mistresses, the grievance is that non-teaching employees of the Education Department lack promotional avenues in their service careers according to statutory rules.
The court will not legislate in this matter to provide promotion avenues, but instead has directed the State Govt. to consider the claims of non-teaching employees of Education Department for appointments to the posts of Masters/Mistresses if they have the necessary qualifications and experience.
The petitioners will make proper representations to the concerned authorities to determine the best way to disposition these representations.
