Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Completion of 240 days service - In the absence of any evidence of loss, the loss of Muster Rolls cannot be accepted as a defense - Since the workman's claim of completing 240 days of service was unrefuted, an increment of higher responsibility must be given to the worker -The court has no choice but to declare retrenchment as illegal due to lack of compliance with laws specified in Section 25-F and G -It is recommended that the authorities maintain proper record in accordance with the law and assist the court in keeping the interest of the state safe.
