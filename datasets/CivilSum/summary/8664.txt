According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, the issue of legality of reference framed on the ground that the demand was not supported by a substantial number of workers nor copy of any resolution passed by the Association placed on record must be addressed.
Even though both parties presented their evidence, the workers failed to offer any evidence on the issue, and the court was still required to record its findings.
The court's decision against the workmen due to non-availability of evidence was not deemed illegal.
