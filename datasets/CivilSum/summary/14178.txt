Constitution of India, Article 226 - Seniority - Final Gradation List - Petitioner was shown senior to respondent in the provisional gradation list, but junior in the final seniority list - Court held that seniority in cadre is determined by date of entry in cadre, even after confirmation - No one can benefit from the seniority of another cadre - Final gradation list was invalidated.
