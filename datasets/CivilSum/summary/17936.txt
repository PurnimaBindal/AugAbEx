Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rules 6.24-C(1) and 244 (as applicable to Haryana) - Pension - The pension calculation of average emoluments for the purpose of pension should be based on monthly pay drawn and not on the notional pay, even if the petitioner is drawing the pay of Sub-Inspector instead of Head Constable at the time of retirement.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to pension based on the pay drawn as Sub-Inspector.
The respondents are required to prepare pension papers of the petitioner and pay arrears of pension with 12% interest within 3 months from the date of order, and the petitioner shall be granted costs at Rs. 1000/-.
