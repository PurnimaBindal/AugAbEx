Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 2(e) - 'Employee' - Meaning of - The Payment of Gratuity Act applies to the date of superannuation - Dismissal of SLP by Supreme Court does not change the date of superannuation - Respondent superannuated on 31.3.1991 - Ceiling for wages for grant of gratuity was Rs. 2500/- pm at that time - Respondent was earning more than Rs. 5,000/- in 1978 - Therefore, the Respondent is not covered under the Payment of Gratuity Act.
