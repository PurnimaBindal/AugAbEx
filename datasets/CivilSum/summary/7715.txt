The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 226 - Promotion - Promotion from Junior Engineers to Assistant Engineers - Diploma holders and graduate engineers holding similar qualifications - The reason for classifying them to provide incentives for diploma holders to acquire higher degrees cannot justify out of turn promotion - Unjust, unfair, and violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.

The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Promotion - Classification - Based on educational qualifications - Is allowed - The classification must achieve a nexus to higher efficiency in the promoted post.
