The Rules of Business of the Government of Kerala, specifically Rules 11 and 12, embody the principle of natural justice that the decision-maker must also be the one who hears the arguments.
The authority who decides on a case must have had the opportunity to hear all arguments, even if a successor takes over the case.
Arguments cannot be considered as having been advanced for someone else to make a decision on them.
[Paras 1 and 5]
