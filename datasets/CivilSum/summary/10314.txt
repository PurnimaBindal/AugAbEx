Constitution of India, Articles 14, 21 and 39(f) and (g) - Delhi School Education Rules, 1973, Rule 37(1)(a)(ii) - Validity of Rule 37(1)(a)(ii) and (4) of Delhi School Education Rules - Public Interest Litigation - Corporal Punishment to students in Schools must be banned- National policy on education - Physical violence is not appropriate for teaching children discipline - Article 21 confers important rights on children, who should live a life of dignity free from violence - Rule 37(1)(a)(ii) and (4) treats the children as a lesser mortal thereby violating Article 21 and Article 14 - Rule 37(1)(a)(ii) and (4) is invalid and must be struck down.
