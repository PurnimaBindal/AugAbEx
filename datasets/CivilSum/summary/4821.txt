Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Selection Process - Interviews - Waiting list - Selection Committee recommending names of seven candidates for appointment and also recommended names of five more candidates for appointment against vacancies if anyone fails to join out of selected list - The appointment of a candidate from the waiting list was made when a selected candidate resigned within one year of appointment - Respondent No. 1 claimed appointment against vacancy caused by such resignation - The Tribunal upheld the appointment of respondent No. 1, who was on top of the waiting list - The order is valid and has no infirmity.
