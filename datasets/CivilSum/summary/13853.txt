Constitution of India, Article 14 - ACRs - An officer posted for a short term can record ACrs - It is necessary for an officer supervising the work and conduct of a subordinate for three months to record his ACRs - The holidays and leaves of subordinates are included - The supervising officer must continue to supervise work and conduct of subordinates even during their vacation, holiday, or leave period.
