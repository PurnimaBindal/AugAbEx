Kendriya Hindi Shikshan Mandal, a government-controlled institution that manages Kendriya Hindi Sansthan, falls within the definition of 'State' under Article 12 of the Constitution, as it is fully regulated by the Government of India.

An employee of the Central Government sent on deputation with a society registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, who is not finally absorbed in the society, remains a Central Government employee and cannot file a writ petition in the jurisdiction of the High Court regarding orders of repatriation, as per the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Sections 3(q), 14(2), 14(n) and 28.
