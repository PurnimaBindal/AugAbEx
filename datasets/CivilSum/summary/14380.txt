According to the Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 and Andhra Pradesh State Handloom Weavers Co-operative Societies Bye-Laws, Bye-Laws No. 7 and 20, the promotion to the next post is decided by the consolidated seniority list which is divided into three wings, namely commercial, administrative, and technical, by the employer.
The petitioner belongs to the technical wing.
The promotion ratio for the cadre of category V to VI is 1:1:1 and 2:1 for the cadre of category IV to III.
Since there is no objection raised in the year 1983 itself, the promotion has been made from approximately 15 years, and the bye-law or seniority list has not been challenged, the court sees no need to interfere with the promotion at this stage.
