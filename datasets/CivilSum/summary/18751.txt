Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Gratuity - Deduction - The petitioner had a sum of Rs. 19,710/- deducted from their gratuity due to receiving Rs. 120/- per month as additional pay.
However, a similarly situated person had already received their full amount, leading to the petitioner being held entitled to the same.
The petitioner can receive the deducted amount upon furnishing an indemnity bond.
[Paras 4 and 6]
