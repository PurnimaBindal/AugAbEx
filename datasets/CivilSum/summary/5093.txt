Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Order of removal - Approval of - Delinquent accused of not issuing tickets - Witnesses extensively cross-examined and evidence of checking staff led before enquiry officer - Since guilt was established in enquiry, findings of enquiry officer cannot be held vitiated - Order of removal upheld.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Disciplinary proceedings - Proceedings before Labour Court - Non-examination of passengers - Charge of not issuing tickets - Production of passengers either in domestic enquiry or before labour court is not at all necessary being highly impracticable as passengers have to be traced out, chased and produced, causing them great inconvenience.
