Constitution of India, Article 226 - Discretion of the court - Academic matters - Unfair means - The court must give due respect to the opinion of experts in academic matters unless it is found to be clearly erroneous or biased - The court cannot substitute its own opinion for that of the experts - Despite the petitioner being found guilty under Regulation 5, the court refrained from setting aside the Single Judge's decision due to the student having passed other exams already and directed the University to issue a new certificate to him.
