Andhara Pradesh Public Service Commission Rules, Rule 6 - Recruitment through Service Selection Commission - Number of notified vacancies is not fixed - Vacancies arising at a later date may be included - Subsequent vacancies are those that arise after selection and recruitment process or the last date set by Commission.
[Para 18] B Andhara Pradesh Public Service Commission Rules, Rules 6 and 22 - Qualifying marks for main examination - State can change criteria through subsequent notification, which candidates cannot challenge - Mere notification of criteria does not give candidates a vested right - State can issue retrospective notifications using powers under Article 309.
[Paras 20, 24] C. Administrative Instructions - Do not have statutory force - Any rule made subsequently by the State will override administrative instructions to the extent it is contradictory - Mere notification of vacancies or selection does not grant a vested or accrued right to appointment - If field of choice is extended by subsequent notification, selection is not vitiated if power is exercised bona fide for good reasons - Court/tribunal will not usually substitute its own criteria in conflict with rules.
