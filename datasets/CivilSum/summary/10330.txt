According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Section 12 of the Special Protection Group Act, the petitioner who joined the Special Protection Group from CRPF by virtue of induction letter is challenging a Repatriation order that repatriated her to her parent organization CRPF.
The terms of the induction letter do not require reasons to be given for repatriation to the parent cadre.
The impugned order does not cast any stigma or adversely affect her career profile.
Since sensitive assignments relating to security are handled by SPG personnel, the Writ court cannot sit in appeal over an administrative order of transfer/reptariation.
Moreover, since there exists an alternate remedy to prefer an appeal, the exercise of writ jurisdiction is barred in the facts and circumstances of the case.
Therefore, there should be no interference in the impugned order.
[Paras 6 and 7]
