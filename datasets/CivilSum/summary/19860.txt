Constitution of India, Article 226 - Leave encashment - The petitioner has the right to receive leave encashment for 240 days, as he has earned 280 days of leave.
Despite the petitioner's earlier benefit of 180 days during previous service with the State Government, there is no valid reason to deny him the benefit.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to the benefit of 240 days of leave encashment on his service with the corporation.
