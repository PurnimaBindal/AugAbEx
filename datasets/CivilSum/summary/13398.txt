According to Section 2(a) of the Essential Service Maintenance Act, 1980 and Clause 6 of Appendix VII, Regulation 23(b) of the Tamil Nadu Agricultural University Regulations, non-teaching staff can be classified as essential services and suspended without following procedure if involved in illegal activities, including strikes.
However, there is no provision in the respondent University Act or regulation or in statutes that empower the authorities to declare non-teaching staff as essential services.
Therefore, the impugned order issued by the Board of Management of the University is being nullified.
