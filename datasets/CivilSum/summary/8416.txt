According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India, an employee can be terminated on notice of one month, but only after being given the opportunity to show cause.
However, in this case, the termination orders for the Junior Scientific Assistant were passed without any show cause notice, and the termination itself was based on allegations of misconduct and inefficiency, as well as carrying a stigma of theft.
As a result, the termination was deemed illegal and against the principles of natural justice, as Article 311 would be attracted.
