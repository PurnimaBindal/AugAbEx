Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Upgradation of post - Brigadier posts were created by upgrading some Lt. Col./Col.
posts - This was done to remove stagnation in higher ranks and to improve career prospects for Army service officers - However, the posts were not upgraded for the incumbents holding them - The order of upgradation clearly stipulated that the criteria for selection to the higher rank of Brigadier needed to be followed - The petitioner was considered for promotion but was deemed unsuitable by the Selection Committee - The petitioner's claim that he should be given an upgraded post automatically due to the upgradation is not valid - The petitioner is not entitled to any relief.
