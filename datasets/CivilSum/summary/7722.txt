Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Settlement between parties - Even if a settlement has been made between parties and then withdrawn by the workman, it is no longer binding and the Labour Court must make its decision based on the merits - An award passed based on a withdrawn settlement is not sustainable.
[Para 5, 8 and 9] B.
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(1) - Reference - If a reference has been made under Section 10(1), it cannot be rescinded or dismissed in default - The Labour Court must adjudicate upon the dispute and determine it on its merits even if a compromise has been withdrawn by the workmen.
[Para 6] C. Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(p) and 18(1) - Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules, 1957, Rule 58 - Settlement - To be a valid settlement, it must be in writing and signed by both parties in the prescribed format.
