Constitution of India, Article 311 - Dismissal from service - Caste Certificate - The appellant, who had applied for the post of Assistant Store Officer, submitted a caste certificate claiming to be a SC candidate, that was later found to be invalid.
However, the selection committee still considered him for the post despite him not meeting the experience eligibility requirements due to his claim of being a SC candidate.
Ultimately, the authority fairly followed a regular procedure for a departmental proceeding and dismissed him from service after it was found that no appointment was made in the eyes of the law.
The termination order was deemed proper and legal.
[Paras 6 and 8]
