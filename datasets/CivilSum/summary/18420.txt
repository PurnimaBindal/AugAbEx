Life Insurance Corporation of India (Agents) Regulations, 1972, Regulations 15, 16 and 19 - Termination of Agency - Petitioner violated rules by receiving premiums and not depositing them, and by giving her own cheques for depositing premiums on behalf of policyholders - Such actions are considered fraudulent and the respondents were justified in invoking regulation 19 and forfeiting the renewal commission payable.
