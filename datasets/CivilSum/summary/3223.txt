According to Article 227 of the Constitution of India, High Court has the power of superintendence over all Courts and Tribunals.
However, this power should only be exercised in exceptional circumstances where there is a risk of miscarriage of justice due to a decision going against the rules of natural justice.
Even though the lower court rejected the employee's suit on the grounds of jurisdiction, provisions in the Civil Procedure Code have been made for appeals in such situations.
The rejection of the plaintiff's claim by the lower court did not result in any miscarriage of justice.
Therefore, a petition under Article 227 is not maintainable.
