Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Retirement - Superannuation age - Co-operative education society aided institution employees - If the service conditions of the employees of an educational institution that receives government aid are regulated by statutory rules established by the authorised body of the society and approved by the Governor as stipulated by the Act and the Rules, and the Rules specify a specific retirement age, the employee cannot be retired before that age unless the competent authority amends the rules specifically.
