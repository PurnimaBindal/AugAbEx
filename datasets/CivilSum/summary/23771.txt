A. Res judicata/Precedents/Dismissal in limine - The Supreme Court's dismissal of a SLP in limine does not prevent consideration of the matter on merits.
[Para 9] B General Clauses Act, 1897, Section 16 - Authority Appointments/Implied powers - Although the rules do not explicitly state it, an authority with appointment powers has implied powers to suspend and terminate an employee.
[Para 9] C General Clauses Act, 1897, Section 16 - Railway Servants (D&A Rules) - Section 16 of the General Clauses Act is subject to the intent of specific rules.
If the concerned rules provide different intent, section 16 of the General Clauses Act shall not apply - Railway Servants (D&A Rules) provide different intention and, therefore, Section 16 does not apply.
[Para 9] D Central Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, Rule 9(1) - Delegation of powers/Competent authority - A competent authority can delegate its powers to some other official, but it can still exercise the same powers.
[Para 14] E Central Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, Rule 2(a) - Constitution of India, Article 311(1) - Railway Servant (D&A Rules), Schedule II - Appointing authority/Delegation of powers - Generally, the authority to whom the powers of the Appointing Authority have been delegated will act as a competent authority, but if the fact appointment was made by a higher authority than the delegate, then the higher authority will be the appointing authority.
