Constitution of India, Article 21 - Medical Service (Medical Attendants) Rules 1940, Rule 8 - Medical reimbursement - Dependent - "Wholly dependent" - The term "wholly dependent" is not to be curtailed to mean dependent only financially or dependent only physically, as it includes both types of dependence - The use of a definition given for a specific purpose cannot be mixed with the definition of a term provided for another purpose - An elderly parent who is receiving a meager pension of Rs. 414/- pm should be considered "wholly dependent" on his son- Disallowing reimbursement for the treatment of his father because he is a pensioner, violates Article 21 and 47 of the Constitution.
