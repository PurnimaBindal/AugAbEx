Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, 226, 300(A) - Pension Regulations for Army, 1961 (Regulation 173) - Disability pension - Medical Board found disability suffered by Petitioner is neither attributable nor aggravated by military service - Claim for disability pension was rejected - Petitioner challenged the order after 10 years - Court held that since Petitioner did not contest the finding given by the Medical Board at the relevant time, and did not seek re-survey, the petition is highly belated and dismissed accordingly.
