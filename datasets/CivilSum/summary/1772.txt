Selection of Police Inspectors - Article 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India - No hard and fast rule can determine the percentage of marks for viva voce test, since it varies from service to service - The Court cannot substitute the opinion of experts who prescribe marks for viva voce tests, given the nature of service - Prescribing 33% marks for the interview, based on the nature of service, is not arbitrary - Awarding interview marks is not arbitrary in any way - Thus, the selection cannot be considered to be arbitrary.
[Paras 8 and 9] B. Re-evaluation of answer sheets of the written test for recruitment - No provision in the rules for re-evaluation, thus it cannot be claimed.
