Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 12.6(2)(c) and Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - Age of eligibility - The age of candidate applying for the post of S.I should not be less than 21 years of age as of 1st February next preceding the date of submission of applications, and the eligibility has to be settled based on the date given in the advertisement.

Punjab Rules, 1934, Rule 12.6(2)(c) and Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - Date of birth - Eligibility - Matriculation certificate - Birth and Death register - A candidate cannot refer to two different date of birth certificates and once a candidate chooses to rely on the matriculation certificate, he cannot switch over to another certificate to take benefit of any one favourable to him.
