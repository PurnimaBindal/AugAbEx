Constitution of India, Article 16 - Mysore State Civil Services (Direct Recruitment to Class III Posts (Special) Rules, 1970, Rule 6 - Karnataka State Civil Services (Direct Recruit to Class III posts (Special) Rules, 1970 Rule 6 - Initial appointment on ad hoc basis - Seniority - The respondent is not entitled to claim seniority from the date of his initial appointment on ad hoc basis, but can only claim seniority from the date of his subsequent appointment a regularisation under the Special Rules of recruitment in 1970.
The respondent can be treated as direct recruit properly made under Special Rules of 1970 from October 26, 1971, and his service rendered prior to the said date was only on the basis of ad hoc employment not made in accordance with the rules of recruitment.
