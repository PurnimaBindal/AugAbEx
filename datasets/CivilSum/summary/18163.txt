Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disciplinary proceedings - Subsequent recovery of documents - Review - Age of superannuation - A writ petition was dismissed due to the fact that the petitioner had reached the age of superannuation (60 years), which made the disciplinary proceedings non-continuable - However, the petitioner sought a review of the order as he claimed that a certain statutory rule would allow him to retire at the age of sixty-two - The institute terminated his services as he was supposed to retire at the age of 60 - The court held that the age of superannuation was not the main issue in the case - The fact that the petitioner was supposed to retire at the age of 62 could not be a valid ground for review - The petitioner could only seek a review after establishing his claim.
