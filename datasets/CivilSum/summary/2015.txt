Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Age limit - Even if the selection process is delayed, candidates who qualified as per the advertised conditions won't become ineligible just because they have crossed the age limit on the date of selection list preparation.
This principle applies irrespective of whether the selection is under Rules framed under Article 309 of the Constitution or not.
The advertised eligibility conditions would remain the same till the finalization of the selection process.
Candidates who were eligible on the date of application should be considered.
