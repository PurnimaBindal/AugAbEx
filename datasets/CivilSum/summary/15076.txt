Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987 - Haryana State Electricity Board Regulations, 1990, Regulation 7 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rule 2.2(b) - Challenge to disciplinary proceedings against a retired employee - A charge-sheet issued after more than six years of retirement is challenged in respect of charges more than four years old - Rule 22(b) states that proceedings can only be instituted in respect of an event that occurred within four years preceding the date of institution of such proceedings - It was held that an enquiry cannot be instituted in respect of an event that took place more than four years before the institution of such proceedings - The order of appointment of enquiry officer and charge-sheet were quashed.
