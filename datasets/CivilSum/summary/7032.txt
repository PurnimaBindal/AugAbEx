Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Probationer - If a driver causing a fatal bus accident is found to have driven negligently and is prosecuted, he may be terminated during his probation if his work and conduct is not satisfactory.
Mere acquittal in criminal proceedings will not entitle him for reinstatement.
A probationer has no right to the post, and if his work and conduct is found unsatisfactory, his termination in accordance with the terms of his appointment cannot be challenged through writ jurisdiction.
