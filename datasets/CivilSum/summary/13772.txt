The West Bengal Schools (Recruitment of Non-teaching Staff) Rules, 2005 under Articles 14, 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India, and Section 3(1)(d) of the Employment Exchanges (Compulsory Notification of Vacancies) Act, 1959, specify Rule 8(5)(a) for Recruitment and Selection.
There has been a challenge to the vires of Rule 8(5)(a), which confines the source of eligible candidates to the employment exchange.
However, it has been held that the rule does not violate Art. 14 and 16 of the Constitution or the provisions of section 3(1)(d) of the Act.
As the state government is entitled to frame different rules for different services in its various departments.
