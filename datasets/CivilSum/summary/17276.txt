Constitution of India, Article 16 - Calcutta Police Gazette dated 471994 - Appointment as Constable - Interview - Procedure of appointment - The addition of an interview component to the rules for future appointments, even if not previously included, is not improper - All appointment procedures must adhere to the existing rules in effect at the time - There is no illegality present in implementing an interview for the appointment process.
[Paras 2 and 3]
