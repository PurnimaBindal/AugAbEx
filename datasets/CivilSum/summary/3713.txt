Government Order No. 548 of 1981 dated 191981 and Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Petitioner's writ petition was dismissed for seeking regularisation and consequential benefits.
However, a similar petition filed by another person was allowed.
The court held that the right to benefits flows from the Government Order/Policy and not from the court's judgment.
Therefore, the state cannot treat both persons differently.
Equals have to be treated equally.
[Paras 8 & 9] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Res judicata - Estoppel.
The rule of res judicata and estoppel provides finality to judicial decisions, but it cannot nullify the fundamental principle of equality enshrined in Article 14 of the Constitution.
The Constitution - the basic law of the land - prevails over all other laws and cannot render the constitutional mandate meaningless.
