Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination of services - Abandonment of service - Continued absence from duty is considered voluntary abandonment of service - The petitioner was absent from duty for several years - Despite unsuccessful challenges to transfer orders in high court three times, the petitioner did not report for duty - Adequate opportunities were provided to explain or return to duty before the abandonment order was issued - The petitioner's termination of services was consequentially declared valid, and no intervention had been made.
