The Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Section 10, along with Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, mandates that private school teachers receive the same service conditions and allowances as government school teachers, which includes receiving House Rent Allowance (HRA).
Private school teachers are subject to the same conditions as government school teachers when claiming HRA.
If one spouse is already receiving HRA as a teacher, the other spouse is not entitled to claim HRA.
Private school teachers cannot be in a better position than their counterparts in government schools.
Any interpretation of Section 106 of the Act that allows for dual HRA payments to a married couple will create an absurd and anomalous situation and is not permitted by law.
The classification under the memo dated 27111965 is justified.
[Paras 6-8] B. Interpreting a statutory provision in a way that leads to confusion or absurdity is not permissible under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
