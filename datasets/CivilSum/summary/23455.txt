"Mines Rules, Rule 72 - Recruitment to statutory posts of Welfare Officers - When selecting internal candidates from a common merit list, they are given priority up to their quota, even if they have lower marks than external candidates.
After that, external candidates are selected.
The current procedure of selecting both internal and external candidates from a common test, without separate tests, is acceptable and beneficial to internal candidates.
The company must also follow the rule of reservation for both internal and external candidates.
Appeal has been allowed.
