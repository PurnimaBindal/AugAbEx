Constitution of India, Article 12 - In order to determine whether a specific Corporation or Body is considered a State or not, several tests must be applied.
One of these tests is to determine whether the body in question is financially, functionally and administratively controlled by the Government.
Such control must be unique to the body in question and must be all-encompassing.
If these conditions are met, the body is considered a State under Article 12.
However, if control is only regulatory, whether under statute or otherwise, it would not suffice to classify the body as a State.
