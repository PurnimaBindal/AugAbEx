The Mumbai Municipal Corporation Service Rules, Rule 205, regarding disciplinary proceedings and compulsory retirement, was already passed by the disciplinary authority prior to the issuance of the order for compulsory retirement.
Thus, the provisions of Rule 205 (2) of the Municipal Corporation Rules are not applicable and are in accordance with Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India.
