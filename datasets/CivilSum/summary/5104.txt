According to Section 2(j) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, in a dispute regarding whether an establishment qualifies as an industry, the burden of proof lies with the workman, and they must provide positive facts to support their claim.
The Labour Court cannot place the onus of this issue on the management, and in a particular case, the Labour Court was wrong in doing so.
The management cannot bear the burden of proof in such cases.
The award of the Labour Court was set aside, and the case was remanded to be decided afresh on the issue.
