Clause X of Letters Patent, Section 19 of Air Force Act, 1950 and Rule 17 of Air Force Rules, 1969 govern removal from service and leave encashment of Sqn.
Leader petitioners.
The government withheld 25% of gratuity and dismissed petition for leave encashment on the basis of Rule 17 which applies to compulsory retirement/discharging from disciplinary grounds.
However, it is wrong according to the court as petitioner gets leave encashment.
[Para 7]
Appellant, who is an Air Force officer, is challenging the Pension Regulations for Air Force, 1961, Regulation 24 that sets minimum qualifying service to be eligible for pension.
Appellant did not complete the minimum qualifying service for pension.
The court stated that the regulation applies only for eligible officers and it causes hardship for the appellant to serve for more than ten years without these benefits.
They grant appellant either pension or gratuity under the circumstances.
