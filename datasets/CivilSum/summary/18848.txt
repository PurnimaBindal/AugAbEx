Rule 39 of the Leave Rules of the University of Kashmir states that three years of service is required for extra-ordinary leave, with the only eligibility criteria being the acceptance of a job outside of India - ad-hoc and substantive nature of service is irrelevant - The petitioner served for three years before accepting a job outside of India and was granted extra-ordinary leave on that basis - Therefore, she was entitled to extend her leave to three years.
[Paras 5 and 6]

As per Article 126 of the Constitution of India, termination of services of a permanent employee cannot be done without holding a regular enquiry as per Section 126 of the Constitution of J&K - Automatic termination notices are not sufficient.
