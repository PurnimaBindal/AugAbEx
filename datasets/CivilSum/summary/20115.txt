Central Government Health Scheme Delhi (Group C) Ayurvedic Recruitment Rules, 1978, Schedule attached to the Rules - Qualifications for the post of Pharmacist - The 'Vaidya Visharad' qualification recognised by Hindi Sahitya Sammelan is not deemed eligible for the post of Pharmacist, even if given a relaxation.
Only 'Vaidya Visharad' awarded by All India Ayurvedic Congress and 'Ayurved Ratna' awarded by Hindi Sahitya Sammelan are recognised.
Therefore, a respondent with a 'Vaidya Visharad' from Hindi Sahitya Sammelan will not be considered qualified for the position of Pharmacist.
