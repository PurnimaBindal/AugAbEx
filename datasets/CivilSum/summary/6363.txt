The petitioner's claim for promotion, made in 1966, was only acted upon after representations were made in 1980 and a petition was filed in 1988, seeking directions.
The claim was eventually decided in 1989, but due to the long period of unexplained silence by the petitioner, the petition was dismissed on the ground of delay.
[Paras 5 and 61]

An official did not challenge a promotion until almost 14 years after its implementation, which the court deemed unfair.
A grant of arrears of salary would not be fair to the state, and thus, a government order granting only benefits of pay fixation and seniority was deemed just.
Financial benefits for the period during which the official didn't perform their higher duties were not given, leading to the dismissal of the writ.
