In absence of any established statutory regulations, previous practice should be followed uniformly in regards to promotion to the post of Chief Engineer under Major Port Trusts Act, 1963, Sections 24, 28, 124 and 125 - Denial of promotion in disregard of previous practice would be considered unfair discrimination and be violative of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
