According to Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227, General Standing Order No. 361 of 1973, and Labour Settlement, employees may receive compassionate appointment benefits.
The petitioner-employer did not offer an alternate job to the father of the respondent, who was discharged due to permanent disability.
The management has a policy of not allowing compassionate appointment if the employee refuses an alternate job, but there is no evidence of this policy.
The petitioner must consider the application of the respondent for compassionate appointment in terms of the General Standing Order and Labour Settlement.
[Paras 5 and 7]
According to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(k), an industrial dispute is any dispute between employer and employees regarding employment or non-employment.
The son of the workman claimed compassionate appointment, which was raised by the trade union.
Thus, there was nothing illegal or incompetent with its reference and its adjudication by the Tribunal.
