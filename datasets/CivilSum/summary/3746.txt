Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - State Bank of India, (Supervising Staff) Service Rules, 1975, Rules 49(h) and 51(1) - Disciplinary proceedings - Dismissal from service on misconduct - The punishing authority must issue a show-cause notice with reasons for disagreement and proposed action and give the delinquent an opportunity to defend themselves when differing with the findings of the enquiry officer who had exonerated the delinquent on all or a few charges - Remitting the case to such an authority to proceed afresh from the stage the defect crept in and take a fresh decision can correct such a procedural defect instead of ordering reinstatement of the delinquent.
