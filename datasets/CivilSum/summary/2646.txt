Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Education - Promotion - Diploma and degrees - Petitioners who received the Certificate of Prathama from Hindi Sahitya Sammellan, Allahabad in a period during which it was recognised as equivalent to Matric, cannot be denied the benefits of the certificate merely because it was later derecognised - The Rajasthan Government must consider the petitioners for promotion based on their Prathama certificates obtained before derecognition.
