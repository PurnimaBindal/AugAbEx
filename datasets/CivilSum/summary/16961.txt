The Provident Funds Act, 1925, Section 5 - MP General Provident Fund Rules, Rules 2(1)(c) and 8 - Applicable in the event of an employee's death - If the nominee is not a member of the family, they have no right to claim the Provident Fund - Nomination of the deceased's brother does not entitle him to the fund - Class-I heirs such as the widow and daughter are entitled to the Provident Fund.
