Constitution of India, Article 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, Schedule II, Item I - Maharashtra Small Scale Industries Development Corporation Employees' Service Regulations, Regulations 31(i) and 68(ii) - Abandonment of service - Absence from duty - Extension of sick leave - The workman was absent without sanctioned leave for 141 days and had no medical certificate supporting his claims for extension of sick leave due to his own sickness and that of his family members.
He even tendered his resignation but was already removed from service by the management on the presumption of voluntary abandonment/desertion after 30 days of absence.
The authority was competent to draw such presumption under rules.
The workman gave a demand notice for reinstatement after 3-1/2 years, but the inference of voluntary abandonment/desertion held justified, even without holding an enquiry for misconduct.
The petition was dismissed.
