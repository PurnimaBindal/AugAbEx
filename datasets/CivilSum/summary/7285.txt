Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 144 - Restitution - Salary - Recovery - Petitioner made deductions based on court order - An ex parte interim injunction order restrained deductions, but the order was suspended when the investment trust challenged it - After a civil revision was dismissed and the decree became final, the employee is liable to have the amount recovered from their salary - The petitioner cannot ignore the outcome of the restitution order.
[Paras 9 and 10]
