Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference - Terms of reference - The Tribunal or parties in a reference cannot exceed the parameters set in the terms of reference - All available material should be considered when framing the reference - Technical objections and scope should be avoided in a positive effort to save the reference and ensure timely resolution - The Court should understand the range of the terms of reference and encompass the entire subject matter of the dispute, including incidental matters to ensure a comprehensive adjudication.
[Paras 4 and 5]
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference - Terms of reference - The Labour Officer cannot decide an issue while framing the reference - The suggestion that a workman was appointed as a Garden Chaudhary by the Competent Authority, when the claim is otherwise, falls outside the officer's jurisdiction - Such findings should be recorded by the Tribunal after adjudication upon the matters.
