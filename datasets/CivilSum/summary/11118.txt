Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 14 - Migration - Order allowing respondents to migrate to Dental College, Rohtak instead of the petitioner, who was higher in merit - Migration cannot be claimed as a right, but if allowed for other persons, it cannot be a selective process - Petitioner was higher in merit and had the same grounds as respondents, so he should have been preferred - Discrimination arises when authorities have unguided discretion - State Government's action was arbitrary and violated Article 14 of the Constitution - Ordered respondents to permit migration of petitioner to Govt. College, Rohtak under MD University.
