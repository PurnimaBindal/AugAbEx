Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 15(2)(b) - Res-judicata - Interlocutory proceedings - The decision made during interlocutory proceedings is not binding and does not fall under the principle of res judicata - The tribunal must decide the dispute afresh on evidence during the merit stage of the proceedings - The industrial tribunal previously held during interlocutory proceedings that respondent No. 2 was not a workman.
