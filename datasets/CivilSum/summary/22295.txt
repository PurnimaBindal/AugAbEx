According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India and various Kerala state rules, inter-se seniority between direct recruits and promotees must be determined.
Direct recruits are given substantive vacancies in the permanent cadre, while recruits by transfer can be adjusted against a permanent or temporary vacancy depending on the vacancy position.
In this case, the appellants were appointed prior to direct recruits, but it was a temporary or stop-gap arrangement, so they cannot claim seniority over regularly appointed direct recruits.
Any appointment or regularization that goes against the rules and causes prejudice to direct recruits cannot be upheld.
