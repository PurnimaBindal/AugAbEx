Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Scope and jurisdiction - Claim of wages for the period after termination of an employee - Section 33-C(2) cannot be invoked to claim wages after termination unless the validity of the termination is established in favour of the workman by the Labour Court under Section 10, or by any other judgment, decree or award in his favour - These proceedings are not for creation of a new right, but only for execution of an existing one.
