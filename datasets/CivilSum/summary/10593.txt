The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Sections 25-F and 25-FF, discusses retrenchment, change of ownership of management, and absorption.
When ownership of management changes and a workman is retrenched from service, the workman may claim absorption in the transferee management.
However, a court cannot direct the new management to absorb the workman in its services if it would be detrimental to their interests.
