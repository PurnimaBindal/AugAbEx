Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination of service - Alleged breach of principles of natural justice - Failure to provide show cause notice or charge-sheet - Claim that Petitioner was dissatisfied working as TGT despite being a PGT, for which position he was deemed unsuitable - Claim that Petitioner voluntarily resigned - Relying on experience certificate obtained by Petitioner - Decision held that since the certificate was issued at Petitioner's behest, Tribunal's finding of service abandonment was correct.
