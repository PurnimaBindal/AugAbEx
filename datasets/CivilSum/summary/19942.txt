Per SB Sinha, J A Constitution of India, Article 16 - Higher Judicial Service - Appointment of District Judges in the State - The Governor has the authority and must consult the High Court to appoint a District Judge in the State.
[Para 5] B Kerala Higher Judicial Rules, Rule 2(b) - Appointment to the post of District and Sessions Judge - Seniority - Seniority is only relevant when merit and ability are approximately equal, and it is not the only criterion.

Naolekar J and Hon'ble SB Sinha, J concurred) C Higher Judicial Service Rules (Kerala), 1961 - Appointment to the post of District and Sessions Judge - The doctrine of acquiescence applies when the appellant accepts a temporary appointment order without objection and cannot later claim a permanent appointment based on changed circumstances.
[Paras 26 and 27] D. Equitable estoppel by acquiescence - The five elements necessary to constitute this type of fraud are explained, including the plaintiff's mistaken belief of his legal rights, expenditure of money or other actions on the faith of this belief, the defendant's knowledge of their inconsistent legal rights, knowledge of the plaintiff's mistaken belief, and encouragement of the plaintiff's actions.
