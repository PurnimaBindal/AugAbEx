Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Disability pension and compensation - The Directorate General, CRPF issued Guidelines of Central Welfare (Risk Premia) Fund Scheme which amended the benefits to individuals who might be invalidated - Petitioner was not given benefits under Para 7(b)(i) of the scheme as amended on 261988 due to his leg being amputated only above the knee and not at hip level - Medical experts found permanent physical impairment and permanent disability of 80% - Petitioner lost his job from CRPF - The case of the petitioner falls under Para 7(b)(i) of the instructions as amended on 261988 - Respondents ordered to provide all benefits accordingly.
