The Labour Court rejected management's request to regard the issue as a preliminary one, however, this decision was not unlawful in view of the authority vested in the Labour Court to decide what is a preliminary issue and what is not, pursuant to Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Civil Procedure Code, Order XIV Rules 1, 3 and 5.
The purpose behind such special Acts, like the Industrial Disputes Act, which provide the Labour Court with exclusive jurisdiction over industrial disputes, is to expedite the resolution of disputes between workers and management.
