The appellant, who had served nearly 8 years in the Army and 17 years in the Central Govt, did not receive pension due to there being no rule for it at the time of his retirement in 1985.
If he were to request pension, it should be considered sympathetically on grounds of hardship, however, his appeal was dismissed.
[Para 6]
The appellant was entitled to the benefit of Rule 10(1) which allowed for gratuity, but not pension.
The writ petition was partly allowed in favor of the petitioner, but the appeal of the government against the grant of gratuity was also dismissed.
