Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination - The workman claimed he worked from 111989 to 111997 when his services were terminated.
The employer stated he worked from November, 1990 till April 1996 and then left of his own accord.
There is no proof that the workman didn't work between April '96 to April '97.
The employer's failure to produce the best evidence will lead to an adverse inference against him.
The workman submitted the demand notice in a reasonable amount of time after the purported termination.
The labor court's decision that the workman's services have been terminated is not erroneous.
[Paras 2 and 3]  B Constitution of India, Article 226 - Backwages - There is no evidence that the workman was gainfully employed during the period of unemployment.
Therefore, the award granting full backwages from the date of the demand notice should not be disturbed.
