According to the UP Intermediate Education Act of 1921, Sections 16-E and 15, as well as the U.P.
High School and Intermediate College (Payment of Salaries to Teachers and other Employees) Act of 1971, Section 3, ad hoc appointments of lecturers are banned by government order.
Any appointments made during this period are not valid, and employees cannot claim salaries for services rendered under such appointments.
However, the selection committee may consider the case of the employee for approval in accordance with the rules set forth by the UP Secondary Education Service Commission (First Amendment) Rules of 1992, Rule 9-A(2), and the UP Basic Education Act of 1982, Section 18.
