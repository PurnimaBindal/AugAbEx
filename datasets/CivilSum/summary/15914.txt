Constitution of India, Articles 14, 15(4) and 16 - Reservation for Government Employees - Admittance to MSc.
Medical Technology Course limited to Government Department/Medical College candidates, while excluding private sector candidates - The classification based solely on employment in Government Departments or its Statutory and Corporate bodies is deemed unreasonable and unfair, rendering the restriction arbitrary and discriminatory in violation of Article 14 of the Constitution - The petitioners working in private institutions and hospitals are deemed eligible for admission into the course.
