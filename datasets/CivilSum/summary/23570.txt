The High Court is not competent to pass termination order of a District Munsiff arrested for corruption according to Andhra Pradesh Civil Services (CCA) Rules 1963 and Constitution of India, Article 235.
The competent authority was the Governor, who must act on the recommendation of the High Court.
The punishment order was quashed and the case was remanded to be processed accordingly.
[Paras 8, 9, 10 and 18]  B.
Rule 21(2) of AP Civil Service (CCA) Rules is in conformity with Constitution of India, Article 235.
The Rule allows the High Court to reconsider its opinion in case of an appeal by a judicial officer who is facing removal from service.
The Governor must still decide on the appeal based on the recommendations of the High Court, but the Rule provides an additional chance for the High Court to reconsider its own opinion.
The Rule is not to be read only because of the limited power of the Governor.
