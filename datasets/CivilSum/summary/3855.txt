Punjab Civil Services Rules Volume-I, Part-I, Rule 4.4(a)(i) - Granting of one increment upon promotion as Head Teacher for discharging higher responsibilities - Pay scale revision not a reason for denial of benefit - The rule must be interpreted in favour of the petitioners who fulfill the requirements of Rule 4.4(a)(i) for the increment, regardless of pay scale changes.
