According to the recruitment rules from Mizoram Education and Human Resources Department for the promotion of Principal, a candidate must have a Master Degree with B.T./B.Ed.
with at least 10 years of teaching experience.
While the petitioners have a professional qualification of M.Ed.
degree, they do not meet the basic academic qualification requirement.
The norms and academic qualifications must be strictly followed for promotions, and although M.Ed.
may be a better qualification than B.Ed., it cannot be equated with a degree of M.A./M.Sc./M.Com.
As a result, the petitioners are not eligible for promotion and there is no violation of Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India.
