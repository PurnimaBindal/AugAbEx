Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 311 - Service conditions - Confirmation - Direct recruits to the post of Accounts Clerk Grade-I must pass the departmental examination within the prescribed time and chances for confirmation, according to Railway Establishment Manual (IREM) Para 167.
If they fail, they may be discharged from service as per the condition of appointment.
A circular that prescribes the condition of the test does not conflict with para 167 of IREM but rather supplements the rule to fill the gap in the warning.
The right to continue in service accrues from passing the examination prescribed for that purpose.
Failure to pass the exam within the prescribed chances can make the incumbents liable to be discharged from service.
[Paras 4 and 5]
