Constitution of India, Article 311(2)(a), (b) & (c) - Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 162 & 1638 - Termination dispensing with the inquiry - Natural justice - Police Officials who allowed a terrorist to escape after several heinous crimes are acquitted by a Criminal Court due to their reluctance to testify against one another - The competent authority was correct in dispensing with the inquiry and exercising its powers under Article 311(2), (b) & (c) and Rules 162 & 1638 of the Punjab Police Rules.
