Correction in date of birth in service record - If there is no prescribed period for filing an application for correction of age, then the application must be submitted within a reasonable time.
The Bihar Financial Rules, Rule 96, state that if a petitioner has to correct their date of birth, the application must be filed within ten years of their entering Government Service - if it's not, they won't be entertained.
It was found that under Rule 96, the petitioner's representation for correction couldn't be entertained because it was made in 2004, which was the fag end of their service.
Article 16 of the Constitution of India, Rule 9 of the Bihar Service Code, 1952, and other relevant regulations were also cited.
