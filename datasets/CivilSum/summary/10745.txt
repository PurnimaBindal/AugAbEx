According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16, Junior Engineers can be eligible for the post of Assistant Engineer in the PSEB Engineering Service if they have been granted the scale of Rs. 2100-3700 after completing 16 years of service.
Upon passing the departmental accounts examination, they can be placed in the scale of Assistant Engineer i.e. 2200-4250 without any benefit of promotional increment.
[Paras 9 and 10]  B The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 allow for correction in pay fixation if an employee's pay was wrongly fixed higher than what they are entitled to, without any misrepresentation or fraud on their part.
Therefore, no recovery can be made from the employee for any excess payment received due to the mistake.
