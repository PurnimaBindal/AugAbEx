The principle of proportionality is a ground for judicial review of an administrative punishment order under Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226, but not sufficient reason to quash the punishment.
[Paras 19 and 20] B.
The punishing authority must consider an employee's past service record when passing a penalty, but cannot rely on undisclosed materials or information without providing the affected person an opportunity to be heard.
[Para 20] C. If the punishing authority has a conflict of interest or is biased, enquiring about an employee's alleged misconduct and then dismissing the employee violates the principles of natural justice and the doctrine of "no one can be a judge of his own cause."
[Paras 25 and 28 to 35] D If there is an apparent breach of the principles of natural justice and bias at the enquiry phase, the court cannot ultimately dismiss a petition for a violation of principles of natural justice and bias during the enquiry phase with the justification of alternative remedy.
