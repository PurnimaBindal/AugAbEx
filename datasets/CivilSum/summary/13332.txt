According to Civil Service Regulations, Article 418, the benefit of previous service can be computed for the purposes of pay and allowances.
The plaintiff/appellant served as a Civilian School Teacher in Army area prior to working in the CDA Department, but there was no break in service.
The appellant's application for the benefit of past service was recommended and forwarded by authorities, implying proper permission as per Article 418(b) of the Regulation.
As a result, the appellant is entitled to the benefit of their previous service for pay and other allowances.
