The petitioner, who served in the Navy from 1967 to 1977, made a representation in 1999 requesting for the grant of Pro Rata Pension.
However, the Regulations of the Navy Pension Scheme do not provide for Pro Rata Pension, and regulation 78 stipulates a minimum of 15 years of service to be eligible for pension.
The notification and CCS (Pension) Rules cannot supplant the Navy Pension Regulations.
The court dismissed the petition due to the unexplained inordinate delay.
