Constitution of India, Articles 309, 14 and 16 - Delhi Administration Subordinate Service Rules, 1967 (as amended on 19.5.1989), Rule 26(3) - Seniority - Length of service - Rota and quota system - Rule 26 establishes the settled principle of service jurisprudence for deciding seniority based on length of service - The principle of `rota' included in sub-clause (b) of Clause 3 of Rule 26 is also valid and falls within the executive domain - Rule 26 is deemed to be valid.
