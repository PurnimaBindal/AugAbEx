Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2A and 10(1)(c) - Stale claim - Workman was retrenched in June, 1992 - Notice of demand was served in February, 2000 - Claim was rejected by the government on grounds of delay - If justifiable?
- No, as the workman did not provide any explanation for the delay/laches from the date of termination of his services till issuance of demand notice - Therefore, the dispute is non-existent and has become a clearly stale one.
