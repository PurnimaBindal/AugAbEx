The Joint Entrance Test is conducted for admission into Ludhiana and Faridkot institutions.
Admissions are granted based on higher merit to Ludhiana and remaining merit to Faridkot.
This resulted in some students with lower merit being unable to secure admission at Ludhiana and not opting for Faridkot.
As a result, some seats remained unfilled.
The authorities were instructed to fill up the seats based on options.
If seats still remained vacant, the petitioner would be adjusted at Ludhiana.
