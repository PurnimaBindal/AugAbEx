In accordance with the provisions of Section 18(3) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 and Article 226 of the Constitution of India, a settlement reached during conciliation proceedings with a recognized majority will be considered binding on all workmen if they are otherwise bona fide and valid.
This applies even if the Petitioner has not personally signed the settlement but it has been signed by an authorized representative.
