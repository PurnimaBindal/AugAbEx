CISF Rules, Rules 25 & 26, A.
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 – Probationers found to have concealed material facts are unfit for public employment, and can be terminated during probation.
This includes those who entered service by furnishing false information.
An order of termination during probation in such a case can't be found fault.
[Paras 9, 10, 11, 18 and 19]

A person who seeks equity must come to the court with clean hands.
Concealment of facts that a probationer is involved in criminal cases can render them unfit for public employment.
One who comes to the court with unclean hands or who is tainted with fraud may not be entitled to any equitable relief.
[Para 12]
