Punjab Co-operative Societies Rules 14, 25 and 26 - Moral Turpitude - While murder is a punishable act, it does not necessarily involve baseness of character.
Whether an offence involves 'moral turpitude' depends on the circumstances in which the offence is committed and how society views the commission of that offence in those circumstances.
If murder is committed with the intent to avenge enmity, it cannot be considered conduct that possesses a morally culpable quality found in offences that involve 'moral turpitude'.
Thus, such an offence does not disqualify a person from being a member of a Co-operative Society.
