Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Judicial review - The High Court writ jurisdiction cannot re-appreciate evidence in law - It does not act as an appellate authority - Its role is to correct errors of law or procedure resulting in manifest miscarriage of justice or violation of principles of natural justice, not for adjudication on merits.
[Paras 9 to 12]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 - Quantum of punishment - Seriousness of charges - The theft of security equipment by the Constable himself while on security duty during the visit of the President of the USA was of the highest grade risk - This misconduct could have resulted in serious security lapses and fatal consequences - A member of the disciplined force engaging in such serious misconduct deserves stringent punishment - Dismissal from service in such cases cannot be termed disproportionate.
