Constitution of India, Articles 309 and 246 - Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, Section 213(4) - Recruitment/Eligibility - Andhra Pradesh Transport Subordinate Service's Assistant Motor Vehicles Inspectors - Qualification - The State Govt. mandated a degree in Engineering as qualification instead of diploma which is the Central Govt. prescribed qualification under Section 213(4) of the Act.
- The State Govt. is permitted to establish qualifications and service conditions if employing its own officers, but it cannot set a qualification lower than the Central Govt. prescribed qualification under Section 213(4) of the Motor Vehicles Act.
