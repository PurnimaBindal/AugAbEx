Under Section 12 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, a company engaged in business and manufacturing of rubber is liable to compensate the death of a worker employed by a contractor for the expansion of the company's building if an accident occurs in the factory premises under their control.
In this case, the expansion of the factory building was a usual requirement incidental for expansion of the business, and the work undertaken by the contractor was in respect of the expansion of the business of the company, hence the company was the principal employer and liable for compensation.
This was concluded by the dismissal of the appeal.
