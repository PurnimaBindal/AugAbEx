The Appellant, who had no control over the workers employed by an outside contractor for packing tea, was entrusted with the work of packing tea under the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, Section 2(9)(i) and (ii) and 2(13).
As per the job specification provided by the principal employer, the Appellant reserved the right to reject any substandard work.
Therefore, it cannot be said that the Appellant was supervising the work under section 2(9)(i) or that the outside contractor was the immediate employer under section 2(13) of the Act.
Accordingly, the Appellant is not obligated to pay the contributions on the packing charges paid to the outside contractor.
