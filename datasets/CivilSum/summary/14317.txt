National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rule 4(iii) - Military service - Military personnel discharged from service - Eligibility to count years of service for pension - It has been held that the benefit under Rule 4(iii) of 1965 Rules is an additional benefit for those who served during emergency and joined civil service within a year of discharge from Army - The petitioner's pension is to be adjusted accordingly.
