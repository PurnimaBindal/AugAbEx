In the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(k), 10, 11 and 18, when there is an industrial dispute and a reference is made to the tribunal, the Industrial Tribunal has the power to direct persons other than the employer and employee to be impleaded as parties.
The Industrial Tribunal also possesses the same powers as a Civil Court in certain respects, and any party necessary for the enforcement of the award can be added as a party to the Industrial Tribunal.
