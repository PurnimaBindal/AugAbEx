Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 311 - Probationer - Termination during probation - Work and conduct during probation - A probationer may be terminated due to unsatisfactory work and conduct, even during an extended probation period.
The termination order must only consider the work expected from the probationer and their conduct.
Any other factors cannot be deemed stigmatic or punitive, and are not considered in the order.
It is important to note that probationary services are always terminable on these grounds.
