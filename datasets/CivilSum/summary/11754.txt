Constitution of India, Article 226 - Ad hoc appointment - The petitioner was appointed on an ad hoc basis for three months or until posts were filled by the competent authority, whichever was earlier.
The initial period of three months was extended from time to time, but no regular selection was made.
The respondent purports to change service conditions of petitioner from an ad hoc appointee to daily wager.
It was held that the petitioner has the right to continue on an ad hoc basis until the post is filled up by the competent authority, and the impugned order changing service conditions was quashed.
