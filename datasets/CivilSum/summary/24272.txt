Orissa Aided Educational Institution (Appointment of Teacher Validation) Act, 1989, Section 3-Ad hoc employees - The petitioner was appointed as a teacher in an Aided School for 89 days, which continued for four years with repeated spells of 89 days.
As per Section 3, the petitioner is entitled to regularization.
It has been rejected that the petitioner could not be regularized as he was appointed subjected to the condition that he would be replaced by a candidate from the select list.
[Paras 8 and 9]
