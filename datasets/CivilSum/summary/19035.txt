The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - PF/Gratuity - Non-payment of - Eligibility as to - Continuity of service - Appellant Bank claims that respondent No. 1 did not complete the required years of service, thus not entitled to PF/Gratuity and other amounts due - Respondent asserts he was absorbed by the appellant Bank with continuity of service - Single Judge rightly concluded that appellant Bank treated this as a case of continuity of service - Denial of benefits to respondent is improper.
