Rule 9.8 of the Common Cadre Rules, 1975 states that the Respondents must follow the instructions issued by the Government regarding the reservation of posts for members of Scheduled Castes.
In particular, 20% of posts in the cadre of Junior Accountants/Executive Officers must be reserved, with at least 10 posts filled by eligible SC members.
The Respondents have not shown that they fulfilled these requirements, nor provided a reason for their failure.
Hence, the Petitioner's grievance regarding the lack of reservation for his claim for promotion to the post of Junior Accountant or Executive Officer is upheld.
[Para 7] B The Petitioner, a Matriculate with three years' experience as Clerk, is eligible for promotion to the post of Junior Accountant/Executive Officer.
He completed the requisite experience on 31st March 1993, becoming eligible for promotion on 1st April 1993.
The Respondents are directed to consider his claim in accordance with the rules and government instructions for reservation of posts.
The Petitioner shall be entitled to be considered from the date he became eligible, subject to the availability of a vacancy for SC members on that date.
If found suitable for promotion, he will receive consequential reliefs.
