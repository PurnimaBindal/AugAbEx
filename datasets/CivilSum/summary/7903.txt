Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume-II, Rule 6.17(b) - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, Rule 54(14)(b)(i) - Family Pension Scheme, 1964, Clauses 4(ii) - `Family' - The exclusion of parents from the definition of `family' has been deemed unconstitutional - Rule 6.17, which excludes parents for the purpose of family pension, has been deemed unsustainable in State of Punjab v. Kharak Singh Kang, 1998(1) SCT 556 - The Supreme Court, in State of HP v. Kedar Nath Sood, 2000(1) SCT 354, has already recommended the amendment of Rule 54(14)(b)(i) of C.C.S.
(Pension) Rules 1972 to include parents in the definition of `family' for the purposes of family pension - The exclusion of elderly parents from an employee's dependents cannot be an absolute rule (see also 2002(1) SCT 953).
