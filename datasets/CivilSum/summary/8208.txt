Constitution of India, Article 226 - Criminal proceedings - Departmental proceedings - Dismissal - Petitioner, a police constable, was dismissed from service in departmental proceedings due to a criminal case against him.
The departmental proceedings were based on the same charges as the criminal case, but the charges were not proved in either.
The Sessions Court acquitted the petitioner, and it was determined that the order of dismissal could not be sustained in law, due to the petitioner's acquittal by a judicial pronouncement.
The petition was allowed.
[Paras 10, 12 and 13]
