Employees' Provident Fund Scheme, 1952, Paragraphs 2(f), 2(f)(ii) & 80(2) - Financial burden on the establishment is not a ground to declare the provision discriminatory, invalid, or unconstitutional.
Employees of the newspaper industry play a dominant role in spreading information, and extending benefits to them is not adverse to the freedom of the press.
The financial burden on the employer cannot be considered "harsh treatment," and it is up to the petitioner to manage their affairs if they intend to continue with their activity as a newspaper establishment.
