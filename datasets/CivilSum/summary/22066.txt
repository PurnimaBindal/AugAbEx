Constitution of India, Article 136 - Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 16(1)(b) - Infancy Benefits - Closure compensation given to all workmen employed after business of old firm closed due to death of managing partner - New firm's partners were different and only the name and some machinery of the old firm were retained - Claim for infancy benefit cannot be denied since business did not continue - No intervention is necessary.
