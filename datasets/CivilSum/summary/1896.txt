Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) Rules, 1930, Rule 25(4) - Pay scales - Revision of pay scales - Mistaken allocation of wrong pay scale on revision - Can be corrected and withdrawn - Haryana Govt. Order dated 1651990 restricts pay scale of Rs. 4100-5300 to HCMS (Class I and II), Engineers and Deputy Superintendents of Police who have completed 12 years of service and are within 20% of posts in the respective cadre - Pay scale wrongly released to all, parity in pay scales not brought out - Omission rectified after complying with necessary procedure and principles of natural justice - Rectification justified.
[Paras 8, 9 and 15(1)]  B.
Determination of pay scales is primarily the function of the Executive and not the judiciary - Court generally does not enter into task of job evaluation done by expert bodies like Pay Commission etc. [Para 10] C. Recovery of over payment - Misrepresentation - Ordinarily, over payment should not be recovered if employees not at fault or involved in misrepresentation - However, if mistake corrected but correction not allowed to be implemented by interim orders at instance of employees, interim orders subject to final decision - Employees liable to refund amount.
[Paras 13 and 15(2)]
