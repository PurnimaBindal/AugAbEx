Constitution of India, Article 309 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1958, Rule 17 - Rajasthan Pension Rules, 1996, Rule 89 - Pension - Department must not withhold pension from petitioner due to failure to make payment on time without plausible explanation - Even if charge-sheet under Rule 17 was pending, department aware about this fact and withholding pension is not justified - Respondent department ordered to pay interest on delayed pension payment.
