Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Qualifications/Appointment - Executive Instructions of the Bank prescribing a maximum limit on qualifications for subordinate cadre posts - The petitioner was disqualified for appointment to the post of subordinate cadre post due to possessing higher qualifications than those prescribed in the instructions - The instructions prescribing a maximum qualification limit are unreasonable and violate Articles 14, 16 and 21 of the Constitution.
