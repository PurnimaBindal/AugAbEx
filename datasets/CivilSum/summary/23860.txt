A.Banking Regulation Act, 1949, Section 45 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 18(1) - Settlement - Amalgamation of Managements - A settlement existing between the transferor bank and union of its employees does not bind the transferee Bank not a party to the settlement.
The settlement contains provisions for compassionate appointments for heirs/LRs of deceased employees of the transferor Bank while in harness.
The transferee Bank takes over all the assets and liabilities of the transferor Bank.
In the light of the Amalgamation Scheme, it cannot be said that no liability could be imposed on the transferee bank qua the compassionate appointments even if no specific provision has been made in the Scheme.

[Para 15] C. Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate appointment - Consideration - The court cannot direct by mandamus to create vacancies for compassionate appointment.
It can direct only for consideration against available vacancies.
However, where after rejecting the claim of the applicants on the ground of non-availability of vacancies, the management itself has appointed such applicants subject to the decision of the appeal, it means they could be fitted wherever they were eligible for appointment.
Defence held unsustainable.
