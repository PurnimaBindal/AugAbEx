The petitioner, a Lt. Col. in the Army, was accused of irregularities and made to relinquish the rank of Lt. Col. and reverted to the rank of Major after a Court of Enquiry.
The Court Martial commenced after a couple of adjournments but was adjourned sine die, and the petitioner was informed that the Court Martial had assembled stood dissolved.
The petitioner applied for the restoration of his rank, but he was issued a show cause notice as to why his services should not be terminated in terms of Sec. 19 of the Army Act.
It was held that the authorities could not take action under Rule 14(2) once the period of limitation for trial by Court Martial had expired, and the statutory bar created by Section 122 of the Act could not be circumvented or nullified by recourse to Administrative action under Rule 14(2).
The court allowed the petition and ordered the restoration of the petitioner's rank as Lt. Col. from the date of reversion, and as the petitioner has since passed away, his legal representatives are entitled to all benefits as per the petitioner's entitlement.
