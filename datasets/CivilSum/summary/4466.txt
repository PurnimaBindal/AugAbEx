Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947) Section 10 - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination of service - Reference - Argument that reference is improper and illegal - The challenge to the legality of reference after 13 years is an unreasonable delay - Therefore, Article 226 must be invoked immediately to challenge the legality of reference - Thus, the management cannot question the legality of reference after such a long time in terms of law or fairness - The award is therefore upheld.
