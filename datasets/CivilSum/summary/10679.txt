Constitution of India, Article 226 - Service matter - Re-fixation of pay - Dispute over stepping up of pay to bring petitioner at par with his junior - Judgment by court attained finality - Respondents stepped up pay of petitioner - Accountant General's act of questioning judgment amounts to contempt - Order for re-fixation of pay and memo by AG set aside as illegal and arbitrary - Respondents directed not to re-fix pay resulting in pension reduction - Petition allowed.
