Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Loss of confidence in the employee - The doctrine of 'loss of confidence' can be invoked if the employee (i) holds a position of trust and confidence (ii) abuses that position resulting in forfeiture of trust and (iii) continuing him in service would be detrimental to the employer, discipline, or security of the establishment.
All three aspects must be present to refuse reinstatement on this ground.
Objective facts establishing apprehension regarding the employee's trustworthiness or reliability need to be alleged and proved.
