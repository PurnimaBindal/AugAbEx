Constitution of India, Articles 188 and 193 - Oath-taking by an elected member of Legislative Assembly - The member is expected to swear in the name of the God he believes in, not necessarily in the name of the God worshipped by his electorate - It is illegal and unconstitutional to force an elected official to take oath in the name of Gods worshipped by his constituents - The term "God" includes Allah, as it is a synonym for the word God.
