The plaintiff in this case worked as a Fieldman on an ad hoc basis and was appointed on a regular basis, albeit temporarily.
The plaintiff was entitled to at least one month's notice of termination, which was in the process of being dispatched.
Despite the plaintiff's temporary appointment, they were still entitled to proper notice before their services could be terminated.
The Addl.
District Judge's approach and conclusion were not illegal or wrong, and the petitioner was reinstated.
