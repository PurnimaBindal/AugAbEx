The Air Force Rules, 1969, Rules 84 and 106, along with Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226, specify the procedure for Court Martial proceedings.
Section 32 of the Evidence Act applies to all General Court Martial proceedings.
The presiding officer of the General Court Martial has the power to stop and restrain the defence counsel if they obstruct the conduct of proceedings.
Rule 106 specifies the obligations of defence counsel and they can be excluded if they do not abide by them.
The exclusion can only be challenged by the accused and not the counsel themselves.
A writ petition is not maintainable before a reference is made by the Court Martial for challenging contempt proceedings.
