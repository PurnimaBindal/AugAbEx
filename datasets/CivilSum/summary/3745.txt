Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sch 2 and Item 6 - Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dismissal of a workman - Enquiry proceedings - The enquiry proceedings held to be not conducted in a fair and non-prejudicial manner when workman was held guilty, due to the reliance on the report of a handwriting expert, who was neither examined nor filed an affidavit, and the copy of the report was not given to the workman, and there was no accedence to the request to send documents to the government expert - Therefore the order is unsustainable.
