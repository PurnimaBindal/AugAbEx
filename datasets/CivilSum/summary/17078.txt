Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 13(1) - Discharge - The appellant, who was enrolled as a constable on 781994, was discharged on 21121995, which was within two years of his initial service.
This was due to the reason that the appellant was deemed unlikely to become an efficient member of the force.
It was also stated that the appellant was given two chances to pass and complete physical and written tests and that there was nothing to suggest that the respondents were exercising their powers maliciously or with any hidden intentions.
It was concluded that holding an inquiry was not necessary before such an order was passed and that the discharge order was not illegal.
