Jammu and Kashmir Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Section 24 - Seniority - Dispute as to - All petitioners and private respondents working on post of Executive Engineer without substantive appointment - Petitioner requested determination of seniority in cadre of Executive Engineers - Single Judge granted petition - However, employer is responsible for determining the seniority of individuals in a class, category, and grade, therefore the adjudication of the dispute by the learned Single Judge was premature and unnecessary - The question raised for determination of seniority was deemed premature and unnecessary, therefore the determination was set aside.
