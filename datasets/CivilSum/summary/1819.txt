Constitution of India, Article 16 - Pensionary benefits - A teacher who resigned in 1977 after completing 24 years of service is not entitled to the grant of benefit of the new Pension Scheme introduced in 1988, as no scheme for pension payment to retiring teachers existed in 1977.

Constitution of India, Articles 16, 14 - Retiral benefits - A cut-off date can be established if it is not arbitrary, irrational, discriminatory, or unreasonable.
To argue that a cut-off date cannot be set would imply that 'the government can never change the conditions of service related to retiral benefits from any date'.
