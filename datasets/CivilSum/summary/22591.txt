Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, Section 10 - Contract labour - Validity of labour contract - Court has the power to discover the true nature of employment and determine the employee and employer relationship - However, this cannot be achieved through writ proceedings or appeals - Workmen must seek industrial adjudication.
