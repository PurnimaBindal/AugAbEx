The Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 2(k), 2(5) and Schedules 7 and 7-A - Civil Procedure Code, Section 9 - Settlement - Jurisdiction of Civil Court - Contractual obligations - The appellant, a workman within the meaning of Section 2(5), seeks the extension of two years of service on the basis of a settlement agreement between management and the employee's union - Because the appellant's claim is based on a settlement agreement, the civil suit is not maintainable - Only the Labour Court has exclusive jurisdiction to entertain such disputes that qualify as industrial disputes - Even if the appellant's claim falls under the general law of contract, the Labour Court must hear it along with the main industrial dispute.
Civil suit is not allowed.
