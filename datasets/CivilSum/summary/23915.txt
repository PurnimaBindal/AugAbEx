The appellants, who were working as Munshis but doing the same work as clerks, were entitled to a higher pay scale and arrears of pay.
The Tribunal found that they deserved the pay scales of clerks and the High Court agreed, ruling that they should receive actual arrears of salary in the higher time scale of pay of clerks from the date of their filing of the writ petition until their respective dates of actual superannuation.
However, arrears of salary were denied because they moved Court after their actual date of superannuation.
