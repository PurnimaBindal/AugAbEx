Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - National Council for Teachers Education Act, 1993, Sections 13, 14 and 17 - Cancellation of affiliation - The University is not authorized to encroach upon the power of NCTE to grant and continue recognition, but rather assesses students and conducts exams - Although the University cancelled the affiliation of the petitioner College for not fulfilling norms for recognition, the stand taken by NCTE that the College fulfilled all norms of recognition holds up - Therefore, the cancellation of affiliation by the University is deemed unjustified, and the order is quashed.
