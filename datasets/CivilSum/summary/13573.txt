Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Transfer - Penal transfer - Mass transfers - If en-mass transfers are carried out, individual cases cannot be examined - En-mass transfers of police officials after an encounter with the men of a particular gang - Such transfers are not considered as penalty, hence no need for a chance of hearing - No evidence to back-up claims of any ill intent - The court cannot intervene.
[Paras 6, 10 and 11]
