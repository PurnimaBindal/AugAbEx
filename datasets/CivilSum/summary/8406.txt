- The charges against the petitioner under Clause 12 of the Bipartite Settlement are serious in nature, however, there is no indication that the employer cannot appoint a legal practitioner as the presenting officer.
Such appointment would not amount to a violation of natural justice or cause prejudice to the petitioner, who has already been allowed to avail the services of a legal practitioner.
[Paras 24 and 25]
- When a domestic enquiry involves complex legal or factual issues, the delinquent can seek the assistance of a lawyer.
In such cases, there is no restriction on the disciplinary authority, i.e., the employer, to also engage the services of a legal practitioner.
