Constitution of India Articles 16 and 311 - Termination - Regularisaction - Petitioner's services were terminated and then later reinstated without back wages - Respondent's appeal was dismissed - Labour Court's award became final - Petitioner claimed entitlement to regularization according to State Govt. instructions - Court directed that petitioner shall be deemed to have been appointed on a regular basis in accordance with instructions and entitled to all consequential benefits related to regularization.
