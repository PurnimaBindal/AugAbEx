Inter-se-seniority between direct recruits and promotees is determined by the J&K Govt. SRO-104/1978, which states that direct recruits should be appointed on slots reserved for them, but if they are not, promotees cannot challenge their seniority from the dates of their appointments.
Direct recruits can only claim seniority from the date of their regular appointment and cannot claim a deemed date of appointment for seniority based on when the vacancy arose.
If the quota rule fails, direct recruits' seniority should be fixed based on their date of appointment.
SRO-104/1978 should be observed to ensure proper inter-se seniority determination, but this can only be done if appointments are made yearly or simultaneously.
