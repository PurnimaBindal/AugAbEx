Constitution of India, Article 311(2) (42nd Amendment) - Central Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1952 (as amended), Rules 14, 15 and 17 - After 42nd amendment of Article 311(2), it is not required to supply the copy of the enquiry report before communicating the punishment order - same is the implication of Rule 17 of CCA Rules.
[Paras 14 and 17]

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary Authority can differ with the findings of Enquiry Officer - But reasons have to be recorded- No necessity to give further hearing as full opportunity was given to the delinquent officer before the Enquiry Officer.
[Paras 20 and 21]

Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965 - Speaking order - Departmental appeal against punishment order - Order of the punishing authority not a speaking order - Case remanded to the appellate authority to pass a speaking order after hearing the appellant.
[Paras 22 and 23]
