Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Termination of workman for unauthorised leave - The Labour Court found that the workman's leave from 111993 to 2711993 was approved by the management.
However, when the workman returned on 2811993, he was prohibited from joining.
The management argued that he lost his job because he had been absent for more than twenty days.
This argument was dismissed, and the Labour Court found no misconduct.
