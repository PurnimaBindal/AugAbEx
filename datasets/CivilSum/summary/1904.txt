Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Retrenchment - Workman not entitled to reinstatement - The workman had remained absent from duty for ten consecutive days without prior leave application.
According to the standing order, this means that the worker stopped himself from duty and cannot be considered as retrenched.
The petitioner's claim that he was on ESI treatment during this period was not supported by evidence.
Therefore, the Labour Court rightly held that the workman is not entitled to reinstatement.
