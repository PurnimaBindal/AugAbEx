Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - All India Institute of Medical Sciences Act, 1956, Section 10 - Appointment - Ad hoc Committee/Selection Committee - Section 10 of the All India Institute of Medical Sciences Act, 1956 requires the formation of ad hoc committees to provide advice on persons to be appointed - The advice of the statutory committee should not be ignored - The advice in the selection of Medical Superintendent may be disregarded, but the advice of the statutory committee is considered binding on the appointing authority.
