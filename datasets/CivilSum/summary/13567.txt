Rajasthan Judicial Service Rules, 1955, Rule 19-A - Re-valuation - Re- totalling - Rajasthan Nyayik Adhikari Nirdeshika was misprinted, using `evolution' instead of `evaluation' - This Rule prohibits re-evaluation, re-checking, and evaluation under the re-totalling of marks - Re-checking to calculate marks given against answers and their total is also not allowed - Re-totalling includes scrutiny, close examination, search, and inspection - The words revaluation and evaluation in Rule 19-A are interchangeable and a harmonious construction should be placed on them.
[Paras 9 & 10]  B Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Natural justice - Re- evaluation - Natural justice principles do not allow examinees to participate in revaluation, rechecking, or re-totalling of answer sheets.
