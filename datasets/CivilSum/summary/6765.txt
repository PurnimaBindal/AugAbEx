According to Constitution of India, Article 226 and Army Regulations, Para 163, the question arises as to whether the petitioner should be entitled to serve as Subedar Major (Clerk) for four years from 11295 or should his tenure be calculated from 271097, the date he physically assumed the charge as Subedar Major (Clerk).
The respondents have said that the petitioner's tenure as Subedar Major should be calculated from 11295, while the petitioner contends that his pay and allowances have been wrongly credited since then.
The petitioner physically assumed the rank of Subedar Major from 271097, and so, he should not suffer curtailment of his tenure for two years through no fault of his own.
Thus, the petitioner is directed to serve as Subedar Major for a full four-year tenure from the date he physically assumed the rank and should not be superannuated effective 1299.
