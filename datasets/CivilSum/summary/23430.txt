Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Bihar Non-Government Secondary Schools (Taking over of Management and Control) Act, 1981, Section 10(7) - Removal of Chairman of School Service Board - Doctrine of pleasure - Chairman can be removed before the expiration of fixed three-year term at the discretion of the government - Provisions within the Act conferring powers on the Government do not violate Article 14 of the Constitution of India - Sufficient material available on record to reach a conclusion that continuation of the Chairman was detrimental to the Board and public interest due to mismanagement - Compliance with principles of natural justice was not required for removal - Removal based on subjective satisfaction of the State Government was valid - Order upheld - Appeal dismissed.
