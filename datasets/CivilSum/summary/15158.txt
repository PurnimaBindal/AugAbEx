Constitution of India, Article 226 - Promotion - Assessment Promotion Scheme - Petitioners seek promotions from date of completion of qualifying service or at least from 1-1-1999 - Revised APS now enables promotions of those whose eligibility fell prior to 1-1-1999 - Clause 12(a) of Scheme states that anniversary date will not apply to those eligible prior to 1-1-1999 - Held that Petitioners are entitled to promotion w.e.f.
1-1-1999 under the rules - Denial of benefit and subsequent appointment on direct recruitment basis to others cannot justify deprivation of the Petitioners' entitlement.
