Only complaint regarding payment of less than prescribed minimum wages is maintainable, not regarding non-payment of wages at all, according to Section 20(2) of the Minimum Wages Act, 1948 (as added in Bihar in 1988).
[Para 9] C In Section 20(5-A) & (6) (as in Bihar added in 1988) of the Minimum Wages Act, 1948, regarding appeals, the original authority requires a 50% deposit of deficit wages.
An appeal is a continuation of the original claim, and the Appellate authority has the power to direct the employer to deposit the required amount.
