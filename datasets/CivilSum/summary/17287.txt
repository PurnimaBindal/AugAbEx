According to Rules 23 and 19(2-A) of the Life Insurance Corporation of India (Staff) Regulations, an employee who resigns forfeits their service and is no longer eligible for pension.
This was determined in a case where an employee claimed that they should be entitled to pension as they could have voluntarily retired instead of resigning, but it was held that voluntary retirement requires three months' notice while resignation requires only one month's notice.
Therefore, the petitioner was rightfully deemed ineligible for pension.
