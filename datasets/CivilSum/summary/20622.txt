Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Railways Act, Section 19(2) – Authorisation of some officers to present a case before the Tribunal will not convert them as a separate cadre of officers - Only a Gazetted Officer of Junior Administrative Grade was appointed as Presenting Officer who have full administrative control over other working under his supervision and guidance - They cannot claim equation of posts or drawing a party in their pay scales with the Presenting Officer merely because they were authorised to practise and appear to act as Presenting Officers before the Claims Tribunals - Law Assistants and Chief Law Assistants cannot be equated to Presenting Officer-cum-Jr Administrative Grade - This matter should be left to the concerned Government as equation of posts and pay scales is purely an administrative function which requires evaluation of duties and responsibilities or the respective posts – Court do not have such expertise.
[Paras 6 to 11]
