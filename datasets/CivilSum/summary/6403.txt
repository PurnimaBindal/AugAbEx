Constitution of India, Article 14 - Seniority - Inter se seniority - Fixation of - There are no existing rules for the promotion and fixing of seniority - The Departmental Promotion Committee lacks authority under the law - Any actions taken by the Committee cannot be accepted as valid for the purpose of determining seniority on promotion.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Seniority - Inter se seniority - Fixation of - Promotion - Discrimination - Petitioners and respondents both come from the same feeder posts - The petitioners have been given the remarks "good" while none of them have been given negative remarks - Respondents have been given higher remarks and promoted ahead of the petitioners despite their seniority - Such differentiation based on subtle differences in remarks is discriminatory and goes against Articles 14 and 16 - The court has directed the respondents to refix the seniority of both parties.
