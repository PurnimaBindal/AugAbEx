Strictures/Disciplinary action - Stay - To avoid harsh or disparaging remarks, it is important not to speak negatively about those whose actions are under consideration in court unless it is necessary for a verdict.
A District Judge expressed strictures against a Land Acquisition Collector for releasing funds to tenants without regard to a stay, but there is no evidence that the Collector was made aware of the stay.
The District Judge's remarks were unnecessary, and therefore have been expunged.
[Para 3]  B.
Stay - The effects of stay - Unless the persons or authorities involved have been notified of a stay, they cannot be faulted for acting against it.
