The entitlement to pro rata pension under Article 226 of the Constitution of India was at issue in this case.
The petitioner sought restoration of the commuted value of his pension 15 years after his retirement, citing prior office orders from the 1980s and 1990s.
The respondent pointed to a different office order from 1990 that had been upheld by the Supreme Court.
The court ultimately held that the petitioner's case fell under the Supreme Court's order and he was entitled to restoration of the commuted value of his pension after 15 years from the date it was credited.
The petition was dismissed.
