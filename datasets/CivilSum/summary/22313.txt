The Committee's recommendation for grant-in-aid under Section 46(2) of the Andhra Pradesh Education Act, 1982 will apply retrospectively, even if the Act does not specifically make it retroactive.
If all conditions are met, a grant-in-aid can be released from the date on which the institution satisfies all the requirements under the act and rules.
[Para 22]
The grant-in-aid right is retrospective, dating back to the years before 1988.
The Committee's compliance recommendations for the various years starting from 1985, though made after 1988, should be considered as existing after different dates after 2771985.
The GO used the word "now," not to indicate that the school identified was entitled to the grant-in-aid for the first time in 1989 but to show that the identification would be linked back to 22.7.1985.
[Paras 23 and 24]
The Andhra Pradesh Education Act, 1995, does not retroactively abolish the legal rights created by the Acts of 1982 and 1988, along with the recommendations made by the Committee formed under GO 326.
It is only retrospective from 17101989 and does not go further back in time.
[Paras 26 and 27]
The legislature cannot pass a law to overturn a judgment, contrary to the Constitution's provisions, unless it takes away the basis of the legal rights on which the order is based, retroactively.
The 1995 Act only retracted rights arising from Government Orders retrospectively.
Therefore, the court judgment must be implemented.
