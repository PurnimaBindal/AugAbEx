The Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited (Determination of Conditions of Service of Employees) Act, 1988, Section 3 - Bharat Petroleum Corporation Ltd Schemes of 1988, 1989 and 1996 - do not violate Article 14 of the Constitution of India, as they provide benefits to both pre and post-nationalisation employees.
[Paras 12, 14, 19, 22, 23, 29, 33 to 36]

The Burmah Shell (Acquisition of Undertakings in India) Act, 1976 - Bharat Petroleum Corporation Ltd (Determination of Conditions of Service of Employees) Act, 1988, Section 3 - Bharat Petroleum Corporation Ltd Schemes of 1988, 1989 and 1996 - Notification dated 24996 to amend the Bharat Petroleum Corporation Ltd (Determination of Conditions of Service of Post Nationalisation Refinery Employees) Scheme, 1989 - Power to frame scheme rightly exercised making service conditions comparable with other public sector employees as per Section 3 - Provisions of Section 3(2) not violative of Article 14-A of the Constitution - Scheme framed under Section 3(1) may provide for the continuance of salary and other benefits received by pre-nationalisation employees - The amended scheme of 1996 gives substantial additional benefits to the employees and is a valid exercise of power.
