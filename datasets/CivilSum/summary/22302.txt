Constitution of India, Article 14 - Tamil Nadu Electricity Board Employees Pension Scheme, 1986 - Scope of - Pension - The 1986 pension scheme does not apply to employees who retired from the Electricity Board before 1-7-86 and were receiving retiral benefits under previous schemes - The Supreme Court has determined that pension schemes that are only prospective do not cover those who have already retired - V. Kasturi v. Managing Director, State Bank of India is a precedent for this rule.
