Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 16, 17, 17-A, 33, 33-A - Conclusion of labour Court/Industrial Tribunal reference - The conclusion of the reference is marked by the writing and signing of an award, which is then submitted to the appropriate Government for publication in the Official Gazette.
After the reference has been concluded in this manner, it should not be regarded as pending and does not fall within the purview of Section 33 or vest any jurisdiction in Labour Court or Tribunal under Section 33-A [Paras 9 and 11]
