The petitioner, a range officer, was transferred and the question was raised if an official can be transferred during an ongoing inquiry to ensure fairness.
The answer seems to be yes, but the transfer cannot be a form of punishment.
The officer must report to the new office for the sake of completing the inquiry.
The intention of the order seems to be removing the petitioner from his present post to prevent him from influencing his subordinates or destroying evidence.
The court found that the transfer order was not without jurisdiction and dismissed the petition.
