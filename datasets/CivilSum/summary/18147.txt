Punjabi University Calandar, 2005, Volume IV, Chapter I - Punjabi University Calandar, 1987, Chapter 18 (Conduct of Examinations), Clause 6 - Supplementary examination - Reappear - Those who have re-appeared in one subject can take the supplementary examination, not those who have failed or didn't appear in several papers - When a specific provision exists to handle a situation, the general provision cannot be applied to it.
