Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Punjab Educational Service (College Cadre) (Class-I) Rules, 1976, Rules 9, 10 and 19 - Qualifications - Eligibility - Equality - A private college has been taken over by the government under a gift deed with conditions for absorbable staff members - The Principal of the College cannot be absorbed as the Senior Lecturer - The respondent seeks absorption as the Principal of the College - The qualification required for appointment as the Principal was MA with First Division or High Second Division (55%) - Respondent, with an MA Second Division and only 52% marks, was unqualified for appointment - Equality clause cannot be invoked under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution - The rule of equality does not allow an unqualified person to claim the appointment.
