Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution grant the right to consideration for promotion.
Additional duties associated with a post account for experience.
The petitioner, a Medical Officer, conducted the role of Deputy Medical Superintendent along with his own duties before being appointed to this role.
A period of 5 years of experience as Deputy Medical Superintendent is required to merit promotion to Joint Medical Superintendent.
The petitioner received a special remuneration for his duties as Deputy MO While considering his promotion to the role of Joint Medical Superintendent, the department cannot engage directly without assessing his qualifications.
