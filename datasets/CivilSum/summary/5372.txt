Industrial Disputes Act (14 of 1947) - Section 25F - Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1961, Clause 2(ii) - Retrenchment - Seasonal Employment - The clause states that an employee who worked for 2/3rd of the duration of a season or 3 months in a clear vacancy can be considered a permanent seasonal employee.
Despite there being no clear vacancy, the workmen worked for not less than three months in a year.
The Labour Court held the retrenchment to be illegal, although stopping the workman from coming to work after work is over does not amount to retrenchment.
The petitioners are obliged to engage these workmen when the season starts in succeeding years in order of seniority.
Moreover, the petitioners are directed to consider the cases of workmen for regularisation under a scheme by the Government and pass orders in four months.
The award has been modified accordingly.
