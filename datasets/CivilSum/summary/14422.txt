Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II, Rule 420 - Pension - Qualifying service - Reinstatement - Reappointment afresh - Rule 420 distinguishes between reinstatement and re-employment afresh, in order to grant service benefits after reinstatement - Re-appointment afresh is not synonymous with reinstatement under this rule - Gopal Singh v. State of Haryana, 1991(3) SLR 651 is not applicable in the case of re-employment afresh - The petitioner, who was reappointed afresh after 6 years, was not entitled to the benefits of Rule 420.
[Paras 4 and 5]
