Article 14 of the Constitution of India states that termination during probation only requires compliance with the terms of the appointment letter and does not require notice.
[Para 3] B The Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-F and Section 2(oo)(bb) state that termination during probation, in accordance with the terms of the appointment, is not considered retrenchment.
Termination without notice and compensation is legal unless it violates the provisions of Section 25-F or is deemed unfair labor practices.
