Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 226 - Departmental proceedings - Quantum of punishment - Removal from service with retiral benefits - A bank employee accused of forgery and causing a loss must be removed from service with retiral benefits.
Even if the actual financial loss is not established, an attempt to misappropriate public funds gives rise to loss of confidence in such an employee and renders them unfit for retention in Bank service.
Even if acquitted by a criminal court, the employee can still face disciplinary action, as long as the punishing authority's decision is not tainted with male fides, violative of principles of natural justice, and prejudicial to the delinquent.
