Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions Act, 1989, Section 29 - Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions (Recognition, Grant-in-Aid and Service Conditions etc.) Rules, 1993, Rule 34 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Revised Pay Scales) Rules, 1989 - Selection Grade - Selection grade means a higher pay scale in the same post and not a promotion to a higher post - Teachers of Non-Government Educational Institutions must receive the same pay scale as teachers of government educational institutions by virtue of statutory provisions.
[Para 7] B Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions Act, 1989, Section 7(3) and 7(4) - Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions (Recognition, Grant-in-Aid and Service Conditions etc.) Rules, 1993, Rule 14(a) - Selection - Grant-in-aid - The state is obliged to provide grant-in-aid for selection - Once the State Government decides to grant aid to Non-Government Educational Institution, it will form a part of grant-in-aid and the State Government will pay the same in accordance with the prescribed percentage.
[Para 20] C Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions Act, 1989, Section 29 - Leave Encashment - Teachers of Non-Government Educational Institutions have the same entitlement to pay scale and allowances, except compensatory allowance, as teachers of government educational institutions - Therefore, teachers of Non-Government Educational Institution have the right to claim leave encashment.
[Para 21] D Rajasthan Non-Government Educational Institutions Act, 1989, Section 2(r) - Word 'Salary' - Definition of - Selection grade - Inclusion of - 'Salary' means the aggregate of emoluments received by a teacher, which includes all allowances, excluding compensatory allowance - The selection grade given to teachers falls within the definition of 'salary'.
