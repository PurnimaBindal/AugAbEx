Article 226 of the Constitution of India outlines the pay scale and grant of salary for Assistant Professors in colleges, as prescribed by the UGC and the State Government.
The minority character of the college does not exempt it from following these guidelines, and the petitioner is entitled to the pay provided by the statute.
[Para 11]  B.
The petitioner, an Assistant Professor, claimed a revised pay scale as per the statute, and impleaded the Chairman and Secretary of the Governing Body of the college as respondents.
The respondents argued that the founder society of the college was not impleaded as a party, but this was not mentioned in their return.
Therefore, the writ petition cannot be rejected on this ground.
