Central Civil Services (Classification Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, Rule 19 - Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti Notification No. F-14-2(93-NVS Vig) dated 20-12-1993 - Teacher - During an enquiry, two Assistant Directors and a Committee found that a complaint of outraging the modesty of a girl student was not disputed although contradictions were found in the manner in which the incident took place - The Commissioner disagreed with the finding, waived the enquiry and terminated the Petitioner's service - The Petitioner submitted letters from colleagues, students and the father of the girl claiming the incident didn't happen - However, the Court held that the letters were stage-managed to cover up the incident - The Petitioner was given ample opportunity to present their defence, and in such cases, a regular enquiry is best avoided as held by the Apex court - Since the pre-conditions for exercising such power were satisfied in this case, the termination was upheld.
