Constitution of India, Articles 16, 20(2) and 226 - Tamil Nadu Electricity Board Standing Orders, Order 19(i) - Double Jeopardy - Recovery of loss caused - Management has the authority to recover loss sustained due to an employee's negligence.
If the charges are substantiated in disciplinary proceedings and the guilty employee has been given an adequate opportunity, recovery proceedings do not constitute double jeopardy.
