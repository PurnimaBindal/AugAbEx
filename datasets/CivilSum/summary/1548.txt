Constitution of India, Article 226 - Advertisement - Cut off date - Validity of - Petitioner challenged the cut-off date condition in the advertisement for granting Commission in the Army.
The application of the Petitioner was rejected on the ground of overage.
The contention was that the alleged condition could only be judged with reference to the date of submitting the application.
The Court held that it is open to the respondent to fix a condition in the advertisement that applies to all candidates.
[Paras 9 and 10]
