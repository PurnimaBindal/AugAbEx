Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, Section 13 - Criminal and Departmental proceedings - Both criminal and departmental proceedings can run simultaneously on the same charges, unless both are based on the same and common set of facts and evidence - While the purpose of the departmental enquiry is to maintain discipline and efficiency of public service, a criminal prosecution is aimed at punishing an offense committed by a public servant - Since the standards of proof differ in criminal and departmental proceedings, the applicability of Evidence Act is excluded in the latter - There is no set formula to determine whether both proceedings can coexist and it purely depends on the factual analysis of each case - Only if the departmental enquiry interferes with the delinquent's defence in the criminal case, it may not continue - The prosecution must establish the known sources of disproportionate assets in the criminal case, and continuing both proceedings simultaneously does not cause any prejudice.
