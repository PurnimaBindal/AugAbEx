According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16, and 226, appointing someone to a post without following its eligible criteria and recruitment procedure, especially for a Group-C post like Water Pump Operator, is not only irregular but illegal and contrary to the Constitution's provisions.
It violates the principles of Article 14 and 16 and renders the appointment void.
Moreover, regularising such an appointment, especially one made without advertising the vacancy beforehand, cannot change its illegality.
