National Airport Authority Act, 1985, Section 13(3) - Deputationist - Request for repatriation - Transfer during pendency - Non-joining at transferred place - Disciplinary proceedings - The petitioner requested repatriation in March 1986, was transferred to Varanasi in July 1986 but did not join, and was relieved in March 1995 before joining under DG Civil Aviation in 1996.
The petitioner could not be automatically deemed to be back in service of DG Civil Aviation until specifically relieved.
It was held that the petitioner should not have absented himself from duties until he was relieved.
[Paras 18, 19]
