Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Advertisement - Posts of Principal - Commission advertised 18 posts - The Commission recommended the names of 30 individuals for appointment - The Commission cannot choose or suggest people beyond the positions advertised - With the exception of recommending two or three candidates more than the advertised number - Allowing the Commission to decide the number could lead to ambiguity and may contradict the guarantees provided under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
