Constitution of India, Article 14 - Karnataka Civil Services (Service and Kannada Language Examinations) Rules, 1974, Rule 4 - Examination/Passing of test/Interpretation of Rules and Statutes/Reversion - Failure to pass test within the stipulated period in the Rule - Promotion during the time employees were required to pass the test - Non-passage of test not necessarily leading to employees being demoted to lower positions, purely on the assumption that the objective of the Rules will be undermined - While the court has a responsibility to decipher Regulations and Statutes with implicit inferences, a deduction that could result in a penalty is not permissible if based solely on the alleged objective or aim of the Statute, Rules or Regulations.
