The Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1972, Sections 44 and 48, deals with revision, maintainability, and the benefits of permanency and other benefits given by the Industrial court to the Petitioner.
The Petitioner complained of a breach of order of the Labour Court, and the Labour Court issued a process.
The employer subsequently gave benefits to the Petitioner, which led to a challenge of the Labor Court's order issuing the process by way of revision.
It was held that the Industrial court had correctly used its revisional jurisdiction by stating that there was no violation of the order to invoke the jurisdiction under Section 48 of the Act that deals with contempt.
As benefits had been granted to the petitioner, the provisions of Section 48 of the Act were not attracted and the petition was dismissed accordingly.
