Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Punjab Civil Service Revised Pay (Second Amendment) Rules 1991 - Stipulated that Junior Auditors are equal to the Assistant-cum-Accountants and considered only for seniority or promotion within their own cadres.
- Petitioners were denied pay scale.
- Equating Junior Auditors with Assistant-cum-Accountants violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
