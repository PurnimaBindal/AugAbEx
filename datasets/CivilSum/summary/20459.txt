Indian Administrative Service (Recruitment) Rules, 1954, Rules 8 and 9 - Indian Administrative Service (Fixation of Cadre Strength) Regulations, as amended by Notifications issued on 151293 and 16.12.93 - The validity of the notifications in question which increased the number of posts only for State Civil Service Officers to be promoted has been challenged - The promotions violate Rules 8 and 9 of the Recruitment Rules and Regulations as there is a change in cadre strength contrary to the ratio of direct recruits and promotees - The increase in the number of posts violates the Regulation and Rule 9 which prohibits any State to recruit under Rule 8 exceeding 33-1/3% of the number of posts against items 1 and 2 of the cadre in relation to the State - Therefore, it can be said that the notification is not valid as it is not in accordance with the Rules.

Interpretation of Rules - Service conditions - Although the courts would try to make a harmonious construction and save the provisions of Rules and Regulations dealing with different aspects of the conditions of service of the employees, in some cases, it is not possible to do so without violating the language used in the notification - In such cases, the court will have no option but to strike down the notification.
