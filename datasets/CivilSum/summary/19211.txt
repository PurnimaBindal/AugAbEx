According to Army Regulations, specifically Regulations 170(d), 170 and 142, discharged individuals seeking re-enrollment must be re-assessed for military character at a standard of "Very Good," rather than just "Good" as required during the initial discharge assessment.
Furthermore, any previous assessment made during the time of discharge is no longer applicable once the re-assessment is made, and individuals who receive a "Very Good" assessment are eligible for re-enrollment without the risk of being later discharged based on their previous assessment as "Good."
