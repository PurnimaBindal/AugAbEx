According to Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 and Punjab Civil Services Rules, Rule 6.17(2), Family Pension Scheme, 1964 and New Family Pension Scheme, 1998 (applicable w.e.f.
1.1.1996), family pension can be granted to parents if they were fully dependent on the deceased at the time of his death, provided there is no widow or child.
However, if the widow of the deceased has already remarried, the deceased cannot be considered a married person and after her remarriage, the parents would be entitled to the family pension.
The decision in State of Punjab & another v. Kharak Singh Kang & another, 1998(1) SCT 556 (P&H) (DB) has been followed.
