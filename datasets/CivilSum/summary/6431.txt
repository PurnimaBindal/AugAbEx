Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Ad-hoc appointment - Last in, first out rule - If employees are appointed on an ad hoc basis for a fixed period, employees who are appointed later must be allowed to complete the period for which they are appointed - In such cases, it cannot be said that no new ad hoc appointment should be made to allow the ad hoc employees appointed earlier to continue beyond the original appointment period.
However, the regular process of selection must be followed as the normal course of recruitment, and any ad hoc appointments must be made only as exceptions, and in exceptional circumstances - Ad hoc appointments cannot be allowed to substitute the regular process of selection, and the exception cannot be allowed to undermine the rule itself.
