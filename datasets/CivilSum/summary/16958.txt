According to Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol. II Rule 2.2(b), an employer cannot sustain a charge sheet due to a delay in issuing it.
Despite the last event made subject matter of charge taking place in 1990, the charge sheet was only issued in September 1999, which violates Rule 2.2(b).
It is not a valid excuse that the employer was informed late by the Vigilance Department.
