Haryana Public Relations (State Service Class-II) Rules, 1975, Rule 13, Punjab Public Relations Department Services Rules, 1958, States Reorganisation Act, 1966 Section 82(6) - Promotion/Seniority-cum-merit/Conditions of service - Amendment of Rules - The amendment of rules defined qualifications and suitability for promotion as conditions of service, hence they could not be changed retrospectively to take away the acquired benefits under existing rules.
The petitioner rendered ineligible for promotion for which they were eligible before the amendment.
The amendment must satisfy the test of Articles 14/16 of the Constitution and was struck down for being violative of Section 82(6) of Punjab Reorganisation Act, 1966.
