Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25F - Certified Standing Orders, Clause 8(k) - Misconduct - Unauthorised absence from duty - Services terminated - Terminating an employee for unauthorised absence from duty without holding enquiry or complying with principles of Natural Justice is void in law - Termination for misconduct without following the principles of natural justice was unjustified - Termination is considered as retrenchment - Management must comply with Section 25-F of the Act - Compensation in lieu of reinstatement and back wages should be paid by the management - Labour Court's award dismissing the reference is quashed.
