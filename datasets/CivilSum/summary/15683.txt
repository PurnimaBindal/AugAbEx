Panjab University Calendar, Vol.
I, 1994 Edition, Regulation 23 - Probationer - Termination during probation - Unless otherwise provided for by the rules, deemed extension of probation occurs when no order of confirmation or extension of probation is made - Maximum probation period cannot be exceeded - The employee will be considered confirmed after the maximum probation period - Two-year probation extension is allowed with advance notice - Failure to provide notice results in the employee being confirmed - Written notice is required but not considered mandatory - If services are terminated prior to probation expiry, rule is still complied with.
[Paras 4, 5 and 6] B. Punjab Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service of Teachers) Act, 1974, Sections 3 & 4 - Probationer - Termination during probation without any negative effects - Termination during probation based on unsatisfactory performance and complaints does not constitute stigmatization - Sections 3 & 4 of the Act do not apply.
