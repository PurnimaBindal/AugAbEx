Under Section 25-F of the Industrial Dispute Act, a writ petition was filed against the award of the Labour Court, which required the respondent to be reinstated and given half of their wages from the date of the demand notice.
The petitioner disputed the relationship between them and the respondent, but the Court upheld the award based on evidence from both parties.
The termination of the respondent's employment without inquiry or payment of retrenchment compensation was deemed illegal.
The writ petition was dismissed.
[Para 12] B.
According to Article 226 of the Constitution of India, writ jurisdiction cannot be used to challenge findings of fact made by an inferior court.
However, if a finding is based on no evidence or regarded as an error of law, then a writ of certiorari can be used to correct it.
The sufficiency of evidence and inferences of fact are the exclusive jurisdiction of the Tribunal and cannot be challenged in a writ court on the grounds of insufficient or inadequate evidence.
