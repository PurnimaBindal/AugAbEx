As per the Punjab State Agricultural Marketing Board (Class II) Service Rules, 1988, Rule 10 under Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, termination during probation is possible if the employee has not completed probation satisfactorily.
Deemed confirmation is not applicable, and the appointing authority must pass a positive confirmation order irrespective of the maximum prescribed probation period.
If an employee receives a show-cause notice or punishment during the probation period, they cannot claim to have completed probation satisfactorily.
[Paras 8 to 12 and 14]
