As per Article 226 of the Constitution of India and the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, in case of termination and disputes arising therein, the management cannot be the sole decision-maker.
In a recent case, the petitioner stated that their resignation was taken by force and the management refused to make a reference.
The court directed the management to refer the dispute and adjudicate on the matter based on evidence presented.
