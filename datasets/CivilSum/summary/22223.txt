The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 and 309 - The government is entitled to formulate and amend its policy decisions - The court has no power to direct the government to change its policy regarding financial responsibility - The posts of General Managers in Indian Railways are filled from a list of Senior Administrative Officers in eight Group ‘A’ Railway Services - Merit-cum-seniority is the basis for selection for the selection post - A policy that places more than six posts for General Managers of one service may be considered prejudiced - The court is prohibited from interfering with government policies.
[Paras 14, 16 to 18, 21 and 22] B.
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Quota Rule/Selection posts - The quota system may not be used for selection posts in senior positions like General Managers on Indian Railways.
