Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Seniority - Ad hoc service - Benefit of - A petition was filed requesting that the petitioner's ad hoc service be counted towards seniority as S.D.O.
and that he be promoted to the post of Executive Engineer - The petitioner was working as a Sectional Officer when he was proposed to be posted as Sectional Officer in his own pay scale against the post of S.D.O.
but was not promoted - He was not given the duties of the higher post - Consequently, there was no promotion that would allow the petitioner to claim a higher status - The representation was rejected in 1977 and the petition filed in 1996 was rightly held to be highly belated.
