Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Disability pension - Petitioner joined CRPF in Feb, 1992 - Petitioner developed Schizophrenia disease on 14.2.1996 - Medical ground being permanent incapacitated, Petitioner was invalidated out from service on account of said disease - However, Petitioner's request for disability pension was rejected - It is held that there is no evidence that the petitioner was suffering from Schizophrenia at the time of his admission into force - Thus, a reasonable inference should be made in favour of the petitioner that the disease he suffered is attributable to his service and not constitutional in nature - Hence, Petitioner is entitled to disability pension.
