According to Section 4 of the AP Civil Services (Disciplinary Proceedings Tribunal) Act, 1960 and Rules 3 and 4 of the A.P Civil Services (Disciplinary Proceedings Tribunal) Rules, 1961 as amended in 1989, disciplinary proceedings apply to non-gazetted officers like the Petitioner who was removed from service.
The Petitioner's contention that the amended rule, which only refers cases of Gazetted Officers to the Tribunal for enquiry, will not apply to him is deemed invalid since the government retained power to decide under unamended rules whether the case be referred to the Court of law, Tribunal, or Departmental Authority.
Therefore, the amended rules will apply to the Petitioner.
