Haryana Co-operative Societies Act, 1984, Section 102 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Industrial dispute - The jurisdiction of the Labour Court under ID Act is not excluded by Section 102 of the Haryana Co-operative Societies Act.
[Para 6]  B Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(oo)(bb) - Abandonment - The onus to prove abandonment rests with management.
Unless there is convincing evidence to support it, abandonment cannot be established based on an unsupported statement from management.
[Para 8]  C Industrial Disputes Act, Sections 10 and 11-B - Back wages - Gainful employment during termination - Casual employment and modest earnings through daily wages do not amount to gainful employment.
Awarding 50% back wages is not an invalid decision.
