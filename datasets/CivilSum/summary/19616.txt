Constitution of India, Article 14 - Fundamental Rules, Rule 22(C) - Indian Railway Establishment Code, Rule 9(1)(a) to (c) - Equal pay for equal work - Petitioners who were directly recruited as Junior Clerks have been found to be senior to the respondent, who is a promotee - Both are discharging same duties and are entitled to equal pay - Third respondent, a junior promotee, earns higher pay due to earned increments in lower post - This amounts to violation of equality under Article 14 of the Constitution, thus the senior is eligible for stepping up of pay at par with junior.
