Kerala Consumer Protection Rules, 1987, Rule 3(1) stipulates that the President of Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum (CDRF) is entitled to receive compensation.
When a retired District Judge is appointed as President of CDRF on a full time basis, he/she is entitled to the salary of a District Judge, including basic pay and allowances.
Even if different pay structures apply to Family Court judges, the President of CDRF cannot be denied the benefits to which he/she is entitled under the Rules.
[Paras 10 to 12]
