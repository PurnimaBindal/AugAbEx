Constitution of India, Article 226/227 - Admission - Petitioner denied admission despite being higher in merit as candidate lower in merit was admitted - Petitioner claimed lack of domicile certificate, but had obtained it by the time counselling was adjourned - University admitted three candidates on 691999, including Respondent 5 who was lower in rank than petitioner - Petitioner argued that just as the University had accommodated candidates with improper documents on 3071999, they should have accommodated him - Court found the admission of Respondent 5 to be illegal, but decided not to disturb it as the course had already been completed - Respondents were directed to admit the petitioner for the year 2000-2001.
