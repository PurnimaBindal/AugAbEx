In regards to admission to the MBBS Course under the Tamil Nadu Backward Classes, Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Reservation of Seats in Educational Institutions and of Appointment or posts in service under the State) Act, 1993, the respondent-writ petitioners claimed they should have been allotted seats in Government Medical Colleges.
However, a clear finding was made by both a learned single Judge and Division Bench that individuals from the open category who received lower marks than the writ petitioners were admitted.
There were also multiple absences among those selected in the open category for the Government Medical College, and the learned single Judge directed for two seats to be kept vacant.
In light of these circumstances, it would be appropriate for the respondent-writ petitioners to be admitted to the Government Medical College.
