Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Suspension - Disciplinary proceedings - The petitioner, who was held guilty of a shortage of stores amounting to Rs. 31,067/-, was suspended and faced criminal charges.
However, as the case was unlikely to result in a conviction, a show cause notice was issued to the petitioner and an order for the recovery of Rs. 31,05765 was passed from their pay.
The respondents did not file a written statement.
The petition was subsequently allowed, and the impugned order was quashed.
