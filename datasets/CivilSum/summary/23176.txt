The Constitution of India, in Articles 14 and 311 discusses probation and termination of services for probationers, including the extension and maximum period of probation as prescribed in the relevant rules or appointment order.
Failure to extend probation does not guarantee automatic confirmation, but rather the presumption is that probation has been extended in the absence of such an order.
However, if an employee has been allowed to continue past the prescribed maximum period of probation, then they carry the presumption of automatic confirmation.
In the case of unsatisfactory work or conduct, a proper charge-sheet must be provided along with an opportunity to rebut.
[Para 5]

Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation Staff Regulations, in Regulation 11(8), detail the completion of the probation period and automatic confirmation.
There is a substantial difference between appointees by promotion and those appointed otherwise in terms of probation period, extension, and consequences upon the expiry of the maximum period.
The use of "probation" and "officiation" implies different methods of handling categories of persons under these regulations, and Clause (8) only requires the completion of the "period of officiation" to pass an order.
Clauses (3) and (4) refer to the deemed completion of the "period of officiation," and Clause (3) allows for termination "within the period of probation" rather than anytime after the completion of the maximum period of probation.
