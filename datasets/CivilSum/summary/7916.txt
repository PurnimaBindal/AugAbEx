The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 25F, and Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India - An ex parte award was given to a workman whose services were terminated after completing 240 days of work without payment of retrenchment compensation.
Despite the petitioner's argument that the workman's services had been rightly terminated as the appointment was made for a specific period, there was no evidence to show compliance of Section 25F.
It was held that when the workman completes 240 days on the renewal of their contract, the provisions of Section 25F are applicable.
[Paras 6, 9 and 10]

The Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, Section 25F, and Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India - The Labour Court gave an ex parte award to reinstate a workman whose termination was contested by the petitioner, alleging that there was no reason for the Labour Court to proceed and pass an ex parte award.
It was found that the petitioner had failed to pursue its remedy before the Labour Court despite being served, and the Labour Court was not moved to set aside the ex parte award.
It was held that since the authorities allowed the Labour Court to pass the award intentionally and with full knowledge, no error could be found in the impugned award.
