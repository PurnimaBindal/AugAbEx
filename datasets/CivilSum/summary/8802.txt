According to the Army Act's Sections 52(a) and 71 and the Army Pension Regulations of 1961, Part (1), Regulation 113(a), dismissal from service results in the forfeit of pension and gratuity except in exceptional cases where the Government deems it appropriate.
A petitioner who received punishment under Section 71(h) was considered under Regulation 113(a) but was not treated as an exception by the Government, and therefore is not eligible for pension and gratuity.
