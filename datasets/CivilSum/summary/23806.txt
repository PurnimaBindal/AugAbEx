The Regional Rural Banks (Appointment and Promotions of Officers and other Employees) Rules, 1988 and Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 specify criteria for promotion to the position of Area Manager/Senior Manager.
Appeals are classified under two groups - Andhra Pradesh Group and Madhya Pradesh Group.
The 'seniority-cum-merit' criterion specifies that even if the senior employees are less meritorious, they would have priority provided that they fulfill the minimum necessary merit criterion.
A comparative assessment of merit is not required to be made for assessing the minimum merit criteria.
The competent authority can lay down the minimum standard for eligibility and mode of assessment of merit through appraisal of performance, service record and interview, and prescribing minimum marks required for promotion based on seniority-cum-merit.
The appeals of the Andhra Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh groups have been disposed of based on this criterion.
