The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Age of superannuation - Classification based on the date of appointment - Those who joined under earlier rules and those who joined after the implementation of new rules - Different age of retirement listed in both sets of rules - Being classified is justified - Rule does not violate Articles 14 and 16.
[Paras 7 & 8] B.
The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Deputation - Absorption of deputationists - Termination of lien from the original department - Age of superannuation - Deputation period is not an appointment - Deputationists remain employees of their parent department unless permanently absorbed and their lien is terminated - They cannot claim to be deemed to be appointed or absorbed from the date of deputation itself.
