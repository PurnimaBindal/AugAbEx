Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Reasonable classification - Discrimination - Apartments allocated by Government for industrial worker - The petitioners did not reveal how they utilized the quarters - They claimed to be deemed tenants due to their prolonged occupation of the apartments - However, they were not regular allottees and had no legal contract with the Government - As such, they belong to a different category than the regular allottees - Charging them a varying rent rate is not discriminatory under Article 14 of the constitution.
