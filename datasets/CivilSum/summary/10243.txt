Punjab Civil Services, Volume II, Rule 616(1) and 6.16(A) - Pension and Gratuity - Qualifying service - pension entitlement - Qualifying Service for pension entitlement is 10 years and not 20 years - An individual is eligible for pension on completion of 10 years of service.
