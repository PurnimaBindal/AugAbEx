Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 14 - Transfer of ownership of vehicle - Failure to inform - Insurance liability - According to Rikhi Ram v. Sukhrania, the aim of Workmen's Compensation Act is to protect the rights of third parties and ensure they don't suffer due to the owner's negligence.
As a result, the insurer's liability towards a third party doesn't terminate as long as the vehicle is covered by insurance, even if the owner or purchaser fails to report the transfer of ownership.

Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 3 - Claim dismissal - Employer-employee relationship - Driver signed trip sheet, and employer accepted ownership - In this circumstance, the claimant was regarded as an employee, and the accident happened during the course of employment.
As a result, the claimant was entitled to compensation.
