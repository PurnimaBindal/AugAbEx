The Rajasthan Educational Service Rules, 1970, Rule 25 provide for promotion from members of Group `E' and Group `F' to the posts of Principal, Higher Secondary School, which are in group `D'.
This provision has been challenged on the grounds of constitutional validity of grouping together Group `E' and Group `F' for the purpose of Group `D'.
However, the policy adopted by the state government of first promoting all persons in Group `E' and then only considering qualified persons in Group `F' for the remaining vacancies is found to be valid and does not violate Article 14 or 16 of the Constitution.
