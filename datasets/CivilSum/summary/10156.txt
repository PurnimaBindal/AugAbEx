Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1970, Rule 8 & 4-A, Part II - When Rule 8 of the Rules charges a person with a major penalty, a minor penalty cannot be imposed unless a proper inquiry is conducted - Rule 4-A, Part II, inserted on January 5, 1993, allows a minor penalty to be imposed despite a charge-sheet being issued for a major penalty - Even though the penalty was imposed on May 28, 1983, and a show cause notice should have been issued much earlier, Rule 4-A Part II does not apply in this instance.
