The Forest Subordinate Service (Executive Section) Rules, 1944, Rule 9 Proviso 1, does not violate Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution.
The Chief Conservator has the power to appoint Forest Guards to the position of Forester, even if they have not obtained a certificate of training from a school.
The mere conferment of discretionary powers does not make the rule ultra vires of the Constitution.
If the discretionary powers have been universally made applicable to all concerned parties, it cannot be considered discriminatory.
Forest Guards were considered for promotion to the rank of Forester based on seniority-cum-merit.
Only those Forest Guards who were found fit on this basis were promoted to the rank of Foresters.
The promotion of the private respondents was not found to be illegal or in violation of any laws or provisions.
The order impugned in the petition does not suffer from any illegality.
