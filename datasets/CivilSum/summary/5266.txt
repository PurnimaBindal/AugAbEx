Society can be subject to a writ when a public interest is at stake, and the institution is catering to that interest, according to the Constitution of India, Article 226.
Therefore, a teacher whose service has been terminated by an institution run by society can seek a remedy under this article.
[Para 7]
According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16, and 311, the termination of a confirmed employee without an enquiry is invalid.
In disciplinary proceedings, the employer is like the plaintiff of a civil suit, and has the burden of proving their case.
Therefore, taking action against an employee without conducting an enquiry is a serious violation of the principles of natural justice.
