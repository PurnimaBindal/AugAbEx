Constitution of India, Article 226 - Dismissal - Absence from duty without authorization as a ground for dismissal - If a period of absence from duty is treated as "leave without pay," can it be considered misconduct for unauthorized absence from duty?
- Termination order is contested - Ruling declares that leave without pay regularization is intended to maintain accurate service records and does not impede or nullify the dismissal order - Termination order is legal.
