Constitution of India, Article 226 - Departmental proceedings - Delayed proceedings - The order punishing reduction in pension for the allegation of missing cash in October 1978 was passed on the directions of the tribunal after an abnormal and inordinate delay of 9 years in initiating proceedings and 7 years in passing the order - This delay is not attributable to the employee - The delay vitiated the disciplinary proceedings - The orders have been set aside.
