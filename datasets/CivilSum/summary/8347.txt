Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(oo)(bb) - Retrenchment - Abandonment of job - Contract of service - Non-renewal of contract of service - Extension of ad hoc service period - If a worker is replaced without an extension of their ad hoc or daily wage service, even if the work and post still exist, it is considered illegal retrenchment and unfair labour practice - Claiming a worker did not report for duty is not acceptable without proof of an extension granted by the employer - If a worker did not report for duty after the expiry of their contract but was recalled and did not report, it indicates continuity of work and post - The petitioner is entitled to be reinstated with continuity of service and may claim back wages under Section 33-C(2) where the parties can prove the worker's employment status.
