Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Reservation - Reservation for the children of Socially-Educationally-Economically Weaker Sections of Society - To be eligible for the scheme, one must meet all three conditions, which are that the parents of such a candidate do not own more than 6-1/4 acres of land, do not receive an income greater than a class IV employee of Haryana State, and are not educated above matric - The aim of the scheme is to improve the status of children or parents who fall under the category of weaker sections, and the criteria is legal and permissible under the Constitution.
