Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Degrees and diplomas - Education - Sanskrit Teacher - Equivalence of degrees and diplomas - Shastri examination - Individuals who have completed the Shastri examination from recognized universities and obtained a Sanskrit B.Ed.
degree are considered eligible for Sanskrit Teacher position - Language Teacher Course (O.T.)
from the Haryana Education Department does not hold as much value as a B.Ed.
Course with Sanskrit from MD University - Candidates who have finished the B.Ed.
course with Sanskrit are qualified for recruitment to the post of Sanskrit teacher.
