Constitution of India, Articles 31-A, 226 and 300-A - Disability Pension - Delay and laches - Petitioner has permanent deafness in his left ear which was established to have occurred during his initial training - Petitioner entitled to disability pension from the date of filing the petition - Petitioner must provide independent medical evidence showing the disability to be at least 20% or higher in order to qualify - Objection regarding delay in filing the petition cannot be used to deny the case on merits at this stage.
