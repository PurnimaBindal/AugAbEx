Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Reservation - Upgradation of posts - Creation of new posts - If existing vacancies have been abolished and posts have been upgraded, this will be considered filling new posts - The authority is obligated to follow the reservation policy in compliance with the Constitution - This constitutional right of weaker sections cannot be taken away through vertical methods - The Supreme Court has held that the right of reservation for the SC/BC/STs is a fundamental constitutional right.
