Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(oo),(c) and 25F - Continuous illness/Retrenchment - The petitioner was given a fitness certificate but could not produce a further certificate of fitness when asked, thus it cannot be presumed that the workman was continuously ill.
The protection of Section 25-F is available.

Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25F - One year regular service/Ad hoc service - The whole working period of the workman has to be counted, whether it be regular or ad hoc, from the day he joined service to calculate the 240 days of service.
