Constitution of India, Article 14 - Rajasthan Transport Subordinate Service Rules, 1963, Rule 9 - Selection - Appointment - Qualifications - Determination of vacancies - The law can change or modify qualifications at any time - Candidates seeking employment do not have a right vested in a particular set of conditions - The vacancies are not determined year-wise - By changing qualifications, the petitioner cannot be considered an aggrieved person to maintain a writ petition.
[Paras 10 and 12]
