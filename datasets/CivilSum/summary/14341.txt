Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10(i) - Reference - The decision to decline a reference on the basis of additional compensation and retiral benefit awarded to a disabled workman was incorrect.
The matter should have been referred to the Labour Court.
It is a settled position of law, and the respondent No. 2 should be directed to make a reference under Section 10(i) of the Industrial Disputes Act.
