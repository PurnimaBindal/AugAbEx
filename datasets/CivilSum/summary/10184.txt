Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Workmen's Compensation Rules, 1924, Rule 4 -Compensation - Occupational disease - Workers employed in Mines for six to ten years- Suffered from occupational diseases during employment- Medical board confirmed the diseases- Based on age and monthly wages, Commissioner assessed damages- Assessment cannot be considered excessive or improper due to evidence and Schedule IV rules.
[Paras 17, 18 and 19] B. Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 10 - Comensation - Occupation diseases - Delay in referring claim -Some workers passed away- Claimants were illiterate- Commissioner has power to condone the delay in such cases-Commissioner correctly condoned delay.
