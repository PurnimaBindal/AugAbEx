Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 20(2) - Double jeopardy - Misconduct - Termination - A terminated employee later found innocent by the Court cannot be terminated again for the same misconduct as it goes against the doctrine of double jeopardy.
[Para 9] B Constitution of India, Article 14 - Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, Standing Order 15 - Termination - Misconduct - Natural justice - Termination orders based on employee misconduct cannot be passed without following the principle of natural justice, as Standing Order 15 is not applicable.
