Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate appointment - Financial Condition - A request for compassionate appointment was denied due to the family's financial condition not warranting the appointment.
If the family is not facing financial difficulties, a compassionate appointment is not necessary.
The family was found to be receiving substantial financial benefits with a regular annual income of Rs. 7200/- and a monthly family pension of Rs. 14,940/-.
The rejection of the request was appropriate, and the Court should not interfere.
[Paras 5 and 6]
