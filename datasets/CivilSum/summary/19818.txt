Punjab Services of Engineers Class II PWD (Buildings and Roads) Branch Rules, 1965, Rule 6(1) - Sources of appointment - Quota rules - The rules allow for ad hoc appointments from other sources as a temporary measure if no suitable candidates are available from a specific source - Promotees appointed under the direct appointee quota cannot claim permanent right to these posts - They will be reverted based on the excess posts they held in the Class II service.
