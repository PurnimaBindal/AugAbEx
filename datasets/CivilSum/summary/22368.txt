According to Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India and Bombay Civil Services Rules, Rule 171, correction in service record for date of birth must be sought within the prescribed limitation.
Even if there is no specific period of limitation, the employee should seek correction within a reasonable time to avoid belated applications.
The court must ensure that there is a plausible explanation and acceptable material for such declaration before granting correction, as belated requests can have consequences for other employees waiting for promotions.
The onus is on the applicant to produce prima facie evidence of unimpeachable character before interim relief of continuation in service can be granted, but there is no irreparable loss to such an employee.
