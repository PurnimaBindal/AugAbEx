In accordance with Section 107A of the Madhya Pradesh Industrial Relations Act, 1962, the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, and Article 226 of the Constitution of India, a Labour Court and High Court may only exercise jurisdiction if a punishment imposed is shockingly disproportionate to the charge proved.
The discretion under Section 11A is limited to circumstances where the punishment is disproportionate to the gravity of the misconduct and requires the reduction of the sentence based on the past conduct of the employee.
Breaching discipline at the workplace is not slavery, but obedience is essential for the prosperity of the organization and its employees.
In the case of assault on a superior officer and unauthorised absence, the Labour Court found the charges to be proved, and reinstatement without back wages was ordered.
The High Court upheld the order of the Labour Court, finding nothing wrong in the enquiry.
