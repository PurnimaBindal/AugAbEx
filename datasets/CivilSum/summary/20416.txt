Constitution of India, Article 16 - Pay/Equal Pay for equal work - A scientist who opted to remain as head of department and outside the Agricultural Research Service accepted the benefit flowing out of the order and enjoyed his headship on a permanent basis until retirement.
Although the subsequent escalation of the pay of the ARS scientist could not have been automatically earned by the scientist, the same employer had two sets of employees, one governed by ARS system of service, the other outside ARS doing the same work.
The doctrine of equal pay for equal work by giving relief of same pay to the respondent Scientist must apply.
