Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Regularisation of services - The petitioner, who has worked for 11-1/2 years as a Carpenter for the respondents, is requesting regularisation of his services - The respondents claim that he was employed as a labourer due to a lack of necessary qualifications for the post of Carpenter - The respondents have been instructed to review the petitioner's case for regularisation of any eligible post in accordance with the State Government's policies.
