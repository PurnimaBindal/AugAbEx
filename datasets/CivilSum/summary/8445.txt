Medical Council Act, Section 20 - Admission - To Post-Graduate Medical courses - A circular issued by Government fixing qualifying marks below 40% for reserved category candidates is being challenged under the act.
AIMS or MCI guidelines fixing 40% minimum qualifying marks for admission of reserved category candidates to its own courses are not applicable to admission rules of Medical Colleges run by States.
It is held that the State has the authority to introduce reasonable and rational minimum standards and fix a qualifying mark of less than 40% for the selection of reserved category candidates.
The impugned circular does not have any defects.
[Paras 12 and 15]
