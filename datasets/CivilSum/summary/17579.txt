Mr. AK Walia, AAG (Pb) represented Respondents No. 2 and 5.
The petitioner joined on the basis of an order of reinstatement, passed in April 1988, which specifically stated that he was not entitled to any payment beyond subsistence allowance.
He received his salary and even made representations for the grant of increments on due dates, but never challenged the order.
The order has become final and was not specifically challenged in the petition.
The pendency of arbitration proceedings has no bearing on the fact that the petitioner did not challenge the adverse order against him and therefore, no relief can be granted.
