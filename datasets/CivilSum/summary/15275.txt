Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 17-B - Award - Payment of minimum last drawn wages - Application for - Workman claimed to not have been employed in any establishment after passing of Award - Petitioner submitted Detective Agency report claiming workman was working as a driver - However, since there is no evidence of workman's employment in said establishment, the report which only has a reference to a car number and no other details cannot be considered reliable - Application approved.
