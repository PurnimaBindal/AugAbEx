Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ jurisdiction - Natural justice - Departmental enquiry - Writ jurisdiction does not involve appreciation or re-appreciation of evidence - If the non-grant of permission for document production or failure to direct prosecution to produce necessary documents is not reported to the relevant authority and the writ petition includes allegations requiring re-assessment of all evidence, the writ is not a suitable remedy.
[Paras 4, 5 and 6]
