Punjab Civil Services (Executive Branch) (Class-I) Rules, 1976, Rules 9, 2(c), 10, 11 and 12 - Reservation - Promotion - The nomination to Registers A-I to A-III, B and C is done based on the provisions outlined in Rules 9 to 11 and 15.
Direct recruitment has a specific process under Register B, where appointments are made through competitive examination, as per Rules 12, 13 and 14.
The petitioner wishes to be appointed based on Registers A-I to A-II and C, where he has been nominated, and does not want to compete for appointment under Register B However, he cannot stake his claim as a direct recruit and should be appointed based on promotion.
Selection for service under Register A-I is neither direct recruitment nor promotion, so reservation for SC/ST candidates is not relevant.
The appointment to service is based on merit and suitability, and even a reserved category candidate may be more suitable than a general one.
Therefore, appointment is based on merit, and not reservation.
