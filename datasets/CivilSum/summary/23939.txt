The termination of services for a workman in terms of Bipartite Settlement deeming voluntary retirement due to long absence without any intimation despite notice is just, fair, and reasonable if the workman does not respond to a show cause notice or report to duty within the given time frame.
Natural justice principles are already complied with in this case.
[Paras 14-18] B.
If the workman refuses to receive the registered notice, and he does not provide a rebuttal, any action taken against him cannot be considered bad for want of notice.
[Para 15] C. Once it is established that the workman was at fault and did not respond to notice and opportunity to join and explain, the Tribunal or High Court cannot interfere with the action taken by relying on the doctrine of natural justice.
