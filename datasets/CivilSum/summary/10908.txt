Constitution of India, Article 226 - Reinstatement - Backwages - The petitioner argued that respondent No. 2's appointment as a driver of Fire Brigade was temporary and not regular because he was not qualified - however, respondent No. 2's service continued until 19493, and the Labour Court found no evidence to support the petitioner's claim that respondent No. 2 voluntarily left the job or lacked the required qualifications - furthermore, the petitioner's written statement contained self-contradictory pleas - since respondent No. 2 completed 240 days of service, there is no reason to interfere with the Labour Court's decision to direct his reinstatement with backwages up to 1793.
