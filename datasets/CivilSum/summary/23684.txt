Constitution of India, Article 226 - Retirement age - Fixation - An employee filed a petition within a month after a decision striking down a rule fixing a different date of retirement for the same class.
The petition was rejected due to filing the petition 4 1/2 years after retirement and deemed improper.

Orissa Education Act, 1969 - Superannuation - Rules 1981 - Retirement of teachers - A rule fixing different dates of retirement at 58 and 60 years for the same class was struck down, and a teacher who accepted retirement at the lower age is not entitled to claim for salary for the idle period of two years.
However, they are entitled to refixation of pension by extending the date of retirement by two years, and arrears of pension must be paid after refixation.
