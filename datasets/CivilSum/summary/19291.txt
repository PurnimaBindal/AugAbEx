Article 226 of the Constitution of India along with Punjab Civil Services (Revised Scales of Pay) Rules, 1979 provides that whenever pay scales are revised, all teachers will receive the same pay regardless of length of service.
Those who join after the pay revision will also receive the same pay.
Those who have been in service for a long period in the unrevised pay scale will be placed in a higher pay scale.
Petitioners started getting more than Rs. 660/- on January 1, 1978 by virtue of the pay that they were drawing in the unrevised pay scale.
Therefore, no discrimination occurred based on length of service.
[Para 7]

Revised scales of pay for Masters/Mistresses and Teachers of C&V Cadre were announced on January 1, 1978, and 1983 instructions increased starting pay from Rs. 620/- to Rs. 660/- for all Masters/Mistresses regardless of precondition.
The instructions aimed to address the disparity between the pay scale of a Master/Mistress and a Sanskrit Teacher belonging to C&V Cadre, whose pay was fixed at Rs. 660/- on January 1, 1978 in pay scale of Rs. 570-1080 by giving the Sanskrit Teacher three increments.
This ultimately brought the pay scale of C&V Teachers to Rs. 660-1200 because their original scale was Rs. 570-1080, which was lower than Master/Mistress cadre.
No discrimination occurred, as the pay revision aimed to reduce the pay scale disparity between the two cadres.
Therefore, the petition was dismissed.
[Paras 7 & 8]
