Constitution of India, Articles 15 and 16(4) Reservation - Reservation for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes - Temporary vacancies occurring due to officiating promotions from State Judicial Services to Higher Judicial Services should not be considered when determining total and reserved vacancies - If vacancies are determined on a uniform basis, there can be no discrimination - Reservation proportions must be maintained in relation to the number of vacancies under Rules - Any other interpretation would result in absurdity.
