Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Dismissal - Ground of unauthorised absence from duty - Charge sheeted - Enquiry - The Disciplinary Authority dismissed the petitioner from service and also ordered that the period of his absence be treated as "leave without pay" - Respondent argued that no penalty could be imposed once the period of absence was regularised - The regularisation of leave without pay is only for purposes of maintaining correct record of service and does not affect the order of dismissal - The order of termination is lawful - The reinstatement order of the Tribunal is overturned.
