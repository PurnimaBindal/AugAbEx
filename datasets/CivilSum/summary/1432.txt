Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Sections 2, 8, 9 and 22 - Workmen's Compensation Rules, 1924, Rule 20 - The claimant used Form F for their application for compensation as a workman, which does not contain details of the nature of dependency required in Form G The insurance company did not challenge the application's competency as dependents of the deceased worker.
Therefore, the order awarding compensation was set aside, and the case was sent back for a new hearing.
