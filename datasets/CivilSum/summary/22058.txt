Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Appointment - Cancellation of Appointment - Natural justice - Appointments of teachers made by the District Superintendent of Schools - The government cancelled the appointments made by the former on the grounds of lack of authority - However, such cancellation cannot be done without affording any opportunity of hearing to the concerned parties - The appellants were entitled to an opportunity of hearing and finding as to whether their appointments were valid or not.
