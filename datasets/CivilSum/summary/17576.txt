Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F and 25-C - Retrenchment compensation - Lay off compensation - Compliance with Section 25-F - Promising retrenchment, one month's salary as notice pay given in addition to compensation - However, lay off compensation deducted from retrenchment compensation as Section 25-C not applicable - Retrenchment notice not invalid - Lay off compensation already paid cannot be set off as second proviso to Section 25-C not applicable - Workmen entitled to deducted amount with 10% interest - Retrenchment upheld.
