The High Court made an error in finding that the order of repatriation was in violation of Rule 14 of the Madhya Pradesh Civil Services Rules, as the 1st respondent had not been promoted to a higher post in the parent department and was instead posted on deputation as an officiating Block Development Officer in the Panchayat and Rural Development Department.
The Court should not annul administrative orders made in the exigencies of administration unless there is evidence of bias.
