According to Articles 14, 16, and 226 of the Constitution of India, ad hoc service periods count towards seniority and promotions if the initial appointment on ad hoc basis was made following the prescribed rules, and services are later regularised - There must be no discrimination between ad hoc and workcharged employees for the purpose of counting the respective service period towards seniority and promotions - Petitioners who were initially appointed as Junior Engineers on ad hoc basis and later given regular appointment as per the rules are entitled to computation of their ad hoc service towards seniority with all consequential benefits including promotion.
