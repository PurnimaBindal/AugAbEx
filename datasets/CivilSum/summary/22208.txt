According to Punjab Labour Department (State Service Class III), Rules 1969, Rule 8(1)(a)(i), ministerial employees are entitled to promotion to the post of Labour Inspector.
However, this does not necessarily mean that a ministerial employee with a higher pay scale than that of a Labour Inspector is entitled to promotion to the post of Labour Inspector.
It should be noted that Superintendents fall under the category of ministerial employees, but their pay scale of 2000-3500 disqualifies them from claiming promotion to the post of Labour Inspector, whose pay scale is lower at 1500-2640.
Essentially, promotion pertains to advancement in both rank and grade.
