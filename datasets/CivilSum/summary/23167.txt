Bank of Cochin Service Code, Chapter VII, Para 22(iv), 22(v), 22(vi) and 22(vii) - Minor misconduct - Gross misconduct - Penalties for minor and gross misconduct - Negligence likely to cause serious loss to the Bank is considered major misconduct under gross misconduct in Para 22(iv)(1) - The High Court's decision on the finding of gross misconduct on the basis of the Manager's proven negligence in granting loans in total violation of Banking Business Laws and Procedures was not incorrect - The High Court's decision was wrong in stating that gross misconduct findings were not called for due to lack of actual loss or malicious intent on respondent's part.
[Paras 15 and 16] B.
Bank of Cochin Service Code, Chapter VII, Para 22 - Punishment - Dismissal from service - Removal from service - The Rule does not provide for the penalty of removal - The appellate authority deemed the penalty of dismissal from service as unwarranted in the case at hand - Penalty for misconduct before the Bank of Cochin's amalgamation into the State Bank of India cannot be governed by SBI Staff Service Rules - The authority can only impose penalties enumerated in the rule applicable at the time of the incident.
[Paras 18 and 19] C. Constitution of India, Articles 226, 136 and 311 - Punishment - Proportionality of punishment - If the rules explicitly fix the gradation of punishments and the punishing authority misjudges one of these mentioned penalties, the court's direction to the punishing authority to award an appropriate penalty from those listed in the rules will not inquire into the proportionality of punishment.
