Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Termination/right to Post/Stay/Directions of the Govt. - The petitioner's employment was terminated due to a stay and orders resulting from a contempt petition filed by some other ad hoc employee.
The court did not direct the termination of regular employees to accommodate a contempt petitioner.
The department still has vacant posts, and is willfully disobeying court orders.
While no one has a vested right to a post, if such a right has been safeguarded by court orders, it cannot be circumvented on unjust grounds.
[Paras 3-4]
