Article 311 of the Constitution guarantees the right to produce a defense for delinquent officials.
This includes the opportunity to inspect records and statements of witnesses examined against them.
Failure to grant permission to inspect these documents denies the appellant the chance to prepare their defense.
Any dismissal order passed without this opportunity is in violation of Article 311 and should be set aside.
[Para 5]
Before taking past records into consideration during termination proceedings, the delinquent official should be given notice to explain their position.
The appellant was not given this opportunity and the final order does not indicate that past records were considered.
This violation of the reasonable opportunity to show cause guaranteed by Article 311 makes the order of termination illegal and null and void.
[Para 7]
Past records are considered in administrative decisions, and if they are not explicitly mentioned in the formal communication to the government servant, it does not invalidate the order.
