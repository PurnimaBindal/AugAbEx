According to Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India, Compassionate appointment should be provided for family members who were dependent upon the deceased's income.
A job deemed suitable for the qualifications of the dependent must be offered to one of them immediately.
For the widow, the appointment should be offered even while relaxing the required qualifications.
Directives call for the appointing for the last grade post forthwith.
[Paras 8, 13 and 14]

An obligation to provide eligible jobs to a deceased employee's spouse can not be denied due to Articles 16, 39, and 226.
An appointment to meet the immediate requirement of the family members needs can be made on compassionate grounds.
Authorities who fail to discharge their obligations resulting in denying a suitable job to a dependent of a deceased employee may become liable, and heavy costs may be compensated to the applicant for harassment.
[Paras 9 and 16]
