Guardians and Wards Act, 1890, Section 7(36) - Adoption - Inter Country and In-country adoptions - The Supreme Court has reiterated the guidelines, including that the child in adoption must be biological, it may not be advisable to provide information about biological parents, and no adoptive parent is allowed to modify the birth certificate of the adopted child in case a declaration is provided by the adopting agency.
(Para 6 and 12)
