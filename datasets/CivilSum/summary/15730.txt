Constitution of India, Article 226 - Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, Section 7-A - Alternative remedy - Challenge to ex-parte order passed under Sec. 7-A of 1952 Act determining amount of contribution payable by employer is barred - Alternative remedy provided in the Act itself as a course of regular appeal is available and seems adequate - Writ jurisdiction cannot decide disputed questions of facts involved in the case - Hence, writ petition is not maintainable.
