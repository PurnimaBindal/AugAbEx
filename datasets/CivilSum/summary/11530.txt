Section 5 of the Limitation Act deals with Condonation of Delay in filing appeals.
An applicant who has not been negligent in filing an appeal can be granted delay condonation up to a period of 16 days.
In examining whether the cause shown for delay is sufficient or not, a liberal approach must be taken in favor of advancing justice.
Ultimately, the course of action that promotes justice must be undertaken.
[Paras 4 and 6]  B Under Order 27 Rules 1 and 2 of the Civil Procedure Code, 1908, an appeal can be filed on behalf of the Government.
In such cases, the Under Secretary is authorized to sign the application and appeal on behalf of the State.
They are also authorized to appoint counsel for the prosecution of the appeal.
