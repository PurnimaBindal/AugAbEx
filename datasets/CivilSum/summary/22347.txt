Constitution of India, Articles 226 & 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 & 11-A - Normally, the Labour Court is the final court of facts with limited jurisdiction for writ court and appellate courts to interfere with findings of fact.
The courts may only interfere if the findings are perverse or based on no evidence, with the possibility of a second opinion not constituting perversity.
If needed, even the Supreme Court can interfere under Article 136.
