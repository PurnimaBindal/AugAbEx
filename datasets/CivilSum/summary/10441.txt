Constitution of India, Articles 16, 226 - Air Force Act, 1950, Section 27 - Promotion - Adverse remarks - Writ Jurisdiction - Promotion to the rank of Air Vice Marshal - The Promotion Board examined the petitioner's case - High Court is not permitted to re-examine the Promotion Board's suggestions - If an officer has a clear ACR, he cannot be considered for promotion to the rank of Air Vice Marshal - The petitioner filed a complaint under Section 27 of the Air Force Act regarding his non-promotion - The competent authority dismissed the complaint after a thorough review, determining that it lacked merit.
