After petitioner No. 1 did not challenge the rejection of her compassionate appointment case under SRO 43 of 1994 for over six years, it cannot be said that the petitioners were facing financial hardship, or else she would have acted sooner.
Therefore, the claim for compassionate appointment cannot be considered at this late stage.
[Paras 6 to 11] B Petitioner No. 2's application for compassionate appointment made seven years after the death of the employee cannot be considered, and the authorities cannot be expected to wait for dependents to become major before considering their claim.
Thus, the petition is dismissed.
[Paras 6, 7, and 9] C. Appointment on compassionate grounds cannot be claimed as a right but is intended to help families cope with sudden financial crises caused by the loss of the breadwinner.
However, such appointments must follow rules and regulations and take into account the financial condition of the family of the deceased who died in service.
[Para 10] D The purpose of compassionate appointments is to alleviate the distress of the family of a deceased government employee, but this cannot apply when an application is made after a long period of time.
