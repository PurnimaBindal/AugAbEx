Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - As per the Regional Rural Banks Act, 1976, Section 29 and Regional Rural Banks (Appointment and Promotion of Officers and Other Employees) Rules, 1998, Rule 7, the promotion of Messenger-cum-Sweeper to the post of clerks is subject to eligibility.
The petitioners in this case were not found eligible for promotion against the 10% interest reserved vacancies.
Their previous posting as officiating clerks prior to reversion to original post does not grant them the right to claim promotion.
