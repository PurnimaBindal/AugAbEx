The Promotion of Respondent No. 1 and others to Assistant Accounts Officers under Punjab State Supply and Marketing Cooperative Services Rules was solely an incentive, as the posts they occupied were abolished and upgraded.
Officials Senior to the petitioner in the cadre of Senior Accounts/Accountants 'A' Grade were also not promoted as Accounts Officer.
The Additional Registrar's direction to promote respondent No. 1 as Accounts Officer was invalid as it ignored the claims of officials who were senior to him.
[Para 5] B.
The direction issued by the Additional Registrar to promote respondent No. 1 as Accounts Officer disregarding the claims of other senior officials, was misleading and cannot be sustained.
