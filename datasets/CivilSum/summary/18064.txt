Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay - Fixation of Pay - Instructions - Anomaly - Special Air Force Instructions dated 26.5.87, Clauses 6(g) and 9 - Pay fixed on the recommendation of revised pay scales - The petitioner's pay was fixed lower than his junior in rank - Representation not accepted - It is a well-accepted principle that the pay of an officer in a higher rank should not be less than the pay of a junior.
Additionally, equal pay for equal work is a necessary requirement.
The Instructions provided in this case ensure that officers of equal rank are not discriminated against, but this principle hasn't been extended to different rank officers.
