The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16, West Bengal School Service Commission Act, 1997's Section 9(2), and Recruitment Rules' Rule 6(E) dictate the appointment to the post of Headmaster; however, the validity period of the selection panel was shortened from one year to 11 days, which is illegal as it neither abides by Section 9(2) nor is in accordance with Recruitment Rules.
The curtailment has negatively impacted the empanelled candidates, and the petitioner, who is the second empanelled candidate, has the right to be appointed on the post since the first candidate did not join.
