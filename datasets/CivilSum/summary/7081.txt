According to Section 13-A of the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, the jurisdiction of the Labour Court is limited to interpreting Standing Orders.
If a Corporation has its own statutory service regulation enacted while exercising powers under Section 45 of Road Transport Corporation Act, but does not have a Certified Standing Order under the Act, then the Labour Court cannot examine the matter under Section 13-A As a result, the order of the Labour Court is set aside.
