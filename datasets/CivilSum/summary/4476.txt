Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - Petitioner, who was initially appointed as a qualified Mechanical Engineer to Marketing Department - served continuously in said Department for 15 years - has been transferred to Mechanical Section - However, functions and duties of both posts are entirely different - Court concluded that Petitioner cannot be transferred from Deputy Manager (Marketing) to Deputy Manager (Mechanical) - Consequently, transfer order is to be invalidated.
[Paras 16, 19 and 27]
