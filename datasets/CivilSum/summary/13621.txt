Limitation Act, 1963, Sections 9 and 15(2) - Civil Procedure Code, 1908, Section 80 - Termination - Suit for declaration - The time for filing suit or application will continue to run once it has exhausted the full prescribed period.
Notice under Section 80 of the Code is given before the expiry of the general limitation period, notice will run concurrently with the general limitation.
The limitation will be extended by the days that would remain in balance after deducting days of the notice period which ran concurrently with the period of general limitation.
The appellant is entitled to the benefit of 40 days under Section 15(2) of the Act, which, when added to 16782, comes to 25.8.1982 - the date on which the suit ought to have been filed.
The suit that was filed on 6982 is barred by the law of limitation by 11 days and is liable to be dismissed.
