Petitioners seeking to be granted the same pay and allowances as workers in other offices of the Transport Department, as per the Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 which call for equal pay for equal work.
Although initially appointed for a particular period, their contracts have been extended.
However, workmen employed on a regular basis in corresponding cadres are earning far more than the petitioners.
Furthermore, counterparts of petitioners appointed on a regular basis have the same duties as the petitioners.
The Respondents are directed to pay petitioners salaries and other benefits as regular workers are eligible for, with arrears limited to a period of three years and two months from date of filing of petition.
[Paras 1 to 4]
