Punjab Land Reforms Act, 1972, Section 11(5) and (7) - Land declared surplus under Pepsu Tenancy and Agricultural Lands Act - Death of landowners in 1970 before the Land Reforms Act came into force - Surplus area remained unused until the landowners' death - It has been established that the authorities under the Punjab Land Reforms Act, 1972 must reassess the surplus area in the possession of the heirs - The case has been sent back.
The decision is based on 1980 PLJ 354.
