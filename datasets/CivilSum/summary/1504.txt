Article 14 of the Constitution of India, Regulations 4(f) and 7 of the Oriental Bank of Commerce Officer Employees (Discipline) Regulations, 1982 - Natural justice - Misconduct - Major punishment - Only minor procedural irregularities have been alleged which did not cause any financial loss to the bank - The officer omitted some formalities to further the interests of the bank - The advice of the Central Vigilance Commission was followed in awarding the penalty, without considering the facts - The CVC's advice is not binding on the punishing authority - It was held that the entire action was in violation of the principles of natural justice.

Articles 14 and 226 of the Constitution of India, Regulations 4(f) and 7 of the Oriental Bank of Commerce Officer Employees (Discipline) Regulations, 1982 - Natural justice - Remand - The disciplinary proceedings were conducted in violation of the principles of natural justice - Despite being advised to drop the proceedings, the delinquent underwent a lot of suffering - If the court finds that the action of the disciplinary authority is in violation of natural justice and excessive punishment, it in normal circumstances remands the case to the disciplinary authority - The case is one where the penalty should be only a warning - Remanding the case to the punishing authority will not be justified - The punishment orders were quashed with all consequential benefits.
