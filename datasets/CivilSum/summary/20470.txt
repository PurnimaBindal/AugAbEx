Ex-Servicemen (Re-employment in Central Civil Services and Posts) Rules, 1979, Rule 6(4) and Note III and Explanation thereto - Qualification - Advertisement for the post of Inspectors of Central Excise, Income Tax etc. - Candidacy requirement - A degree of a recognised University or its equivalent, or passing the degree examination, or acquiring deemed qualification of a graduate in case of an ex-serviceman on completion of 15 years of service in the Armed Forces - The cut off date has no application for acquiring deemed qualification of a graduate - Eligibility for an ex-serviceman who is not a graduate - A candidate who is not a graduate but is an ex-serviceman must complete 15 years of service before the last date of receipt of the application for being eligible for the recruitment to the post of Inspectors in Central Excise and Income Tax.
