The appointments of professors, as per Articles 16 and 226 of the Constitution of India, cannot be adjudicated in a writ on the basis of whether the respondent's research work reflects the necessary qualifications.
Such a decision lies with the Selection Committee.
[Para 8] B Members of a Selection Committee are expected to have some of their prior students as candidates in every selection process.
Hence, Judges cannot assume bias on the grounds that an appointee was a student of the Head of the Selection Committee.
