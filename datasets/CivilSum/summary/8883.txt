The Special Court placed the employee on probation in accordance with Constitution of India, Article 311, Prevention of Corruption Act, Section 5(2) and Indian Penal Code, Sections 161 and 360.
However, even though Rules have been framed by the Municipal Committee, the employee's services were terminated without a show cause notice.
The competent authority failed to provide the employee with an opportunity in accordance with the Rules.
The decision to terminate the employee's services was made without sufficient reason and grounds and was therefore invalid.
Despite the State's appeal, the action was dismissed.
