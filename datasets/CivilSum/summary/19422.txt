Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Seniority - Natural justice - Petitioners' seniority was unfairly altered without being given a chance to object, breaching principles of natural justice.
If an opportunity had been offered, the petitioner could have argued that the respondent's actions were not allowed under the Rules, that daily-wage service by Respondent No. 3-Assistant should not have been considered in determining seniority, and that Store-keepers-cashiers, steno-typists, and Accounts Clerks cannot be granted seniority with the clerks.
Lack of an opportunity led to a significant miscarriage of justice.
The impugned orders have now been invalidated, and the Respondents have been ordered to reconsider the matter after hearing both the petitioners and the respondents.
