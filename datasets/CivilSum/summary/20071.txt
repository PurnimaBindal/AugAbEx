Guidelines for appointment of Facilitators as regular primary school teachers require three years of service and training by 31121990.
The respondent did not fulfill these qualifications, resulting in his rejection.
The Tribunal erred in issuing a direction contrary to the main order, and a review petition could not issue fresh directions.
Since no juniors were appointed, no direction was issued in the respondent's favour.
His case would be considered as and when vacancies arise, and he would be appointed accordingly.
The appeal was allowed.
