According to Section 3 of the Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923, a workman who falls into a well situated within the premises of a factory and dies at the time of drawing water is considered to have had an accident related to their employment if they were on duty and answering a call of nature.
This is because answering the call of nature is considered one of the exigencies of employment and there is a casual connection between the employment and the cause of death.
Therefore, the accident arose out of and in the course of employment.
