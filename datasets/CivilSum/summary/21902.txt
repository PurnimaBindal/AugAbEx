Promotion Policy Settlement, Clause 3.8.2 - Bipartite Settlement, Clause 5(c)(ii) - Banking Service Law - Petitioner, who had been promoted as an Assistant Manager and was on probation for one year, requested reversion to the post of clerk during probation.
The appellant agreed to the reversion on the condition that he would not be eligible for stagnation increments, and that he would be prevented from being promoted beyond the rank of Officer for a period of two years as per Clause 5(c)(ii) of the Bipartite settlement.
The Court found that the condition that appellant would be denied future promotions beyond two years is, therefore, illegal and requires correction.
The order of reversion imposing a condition that the appellant shall forfeit permanently his chances for promotion to the officers' cadre is set aside, and it is directed that he shall forfeit his chances for promotion to the officer cadre only for a period of two years from the date of the order of reversion.
