Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Equal pay for equal work - Claim by Deputy Agricultural Officers for pay equal to Agricultural Officers - Claim granted by the Tribunal - Two groups of employees belong to different cadres with separate qualifications, appointment modes, and seniority lists - Doctrine of equal pay for equal work cannot be applied to disparate parties - Tribunal's order overturned.
