Constitution of India, Article 226 - NCTI Service Rules 1998, Rules 4, 9, 10, 11, 12 - Probation - Appointment - Termination - Executive Director hired the Petitioner as Manager, to serve a probationary period of one year with a termination clause, giving the employer the right to terminate his services without assigning any cause, upon providing one month's notice or pay - Petitioner accepted the offer - Show Cause notices and warnings were issued to the Petitioner, terminating his services after probation period - Petitioner contended that he was deemed confirmed upon completion of probation - The court held that the decision was in accordance with the terms of the appointment letter and the Petitioner was to be considered for confirmation based on his performance - Thus, the decision did not impose a stigma and could not be deemed a punishment - The petition was dismissed accordingly.
[Paras 3, 10 and 11]
