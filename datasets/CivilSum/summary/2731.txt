According to Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, termination of service of a workman may be challenged if the grounds for it are fake appointment orders.
However, in this case, compliance with Section 25-F cannot be expected as one of the conditions of compliance is that the appointment must be genuine.
The award has been misapplied in the violation of principles of natural justice and burden of proof, and should have required the workman to prove the genuineness of the appointment order rather than finding fault with the management's actions.
The applicability of principles of natural justice depends on the specific facts and circumstances of each case and cannot be abused for the opposite purpose.
