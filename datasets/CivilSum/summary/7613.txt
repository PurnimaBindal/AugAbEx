According to the provisions of Section 2(oo)(bb) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, a workman appointed on a contract basis until regular vacancies are filled is liable to have their contract come to an end when a regular candidate is appointed.
In such a case, the protection of Section 25-F would not be available to the workman.
Therefore, the labour court's award was set aside and the respondent- university's order terminating the services of the workman was upheld.
