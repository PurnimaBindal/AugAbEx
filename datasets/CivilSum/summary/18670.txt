Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Retiral benefits - Pepsu Road Transport Corporation Employees Pension/Gratuity Regulations, 1992 - The petitioner opted for the pension scheme on 25111992 and subsequently claims pension upon superannuation on 3131995, which was not granted.
His failure to return benefits received under the Contributory Provident Fund Scheme prevented him from claiming benefits under the 1992 scheme.
The petition is dismissed.
