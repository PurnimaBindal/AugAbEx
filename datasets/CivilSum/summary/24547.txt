The Telegraph Engineering Service Class II Recruitment Rules, 1966 and Post and Telegraph Manual, Vol. IV, Paragraph 206 govern seniority for promotions to Class II cadre.
The concept of seniority changed from the year of passing the examination to the year of recruitment/appointment.
In Parmanand Lal's case and subsequent similar cases, promotions were protected by the court, regardless of whether the case was filed or not.
Such promotions made prior to Parmanand Lal's case are to be protected in all cases.
