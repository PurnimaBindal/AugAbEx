According to the Karnataka Civil Services (Time Bound Advancement) Rules, 1983, Rules 3 and 16, as well as the Karanataka Government Servants (Seniority) Rules, 1957 (as amended in 1976), the appellant was entitled to time bound advancement as long as he completed 10 years of continuous service on the post he holds and is entitled to seniority of that post.
However, as the appellant did not complete 10 years on the post of 1st Divisional Assistant and his service as Junior Laboratory Technician was not countable towards his seniority in that cadre, his request for promotion was rejected.
