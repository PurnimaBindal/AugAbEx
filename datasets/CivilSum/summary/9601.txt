Regarding probation periods, Article 14 of the Indian Constitution states that completion of probation period does not confirm automatic confirmation of employment.
Therefore, the competent authority is entitled to make decisions regarding extension of a probationer's probation period after it has expired.
[Para 10]
The competent authority is responsible for assessing an employee's work and performance during their probationary period.
Punishments given during probation and adverse reports earned are sources for forming an opinion about a probationer's performance.
An order of reversion simpliciter cannot be converted into punishment by referencing disciplinary proceedings against the probationer and adverse reports earned.
[Para 11]
Provisional promotion does not eliminate previous adverse remarks if the promotion is subject to the decision of disciplinary proceedings against the employee.
