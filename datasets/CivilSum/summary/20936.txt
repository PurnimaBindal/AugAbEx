Article 16 of the Constitution of India, Central Administrative Stenographers Services Rules - Rule 5(1), Central Administrative Tribunal Ministerial Staff service Rules and Central Administrative Tribunal Stenographers Services (Group B and C Posts) Recruitment Rules, 1989 are discussed.
The petitioner worked as a section officer in Intelligence Bureau, Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India and came on deputation as private secretary to member CAT on 14/6/1989.
He later opted to be absorbed as a section officer in CAT.
He claims seniority as a section officer in CAT from the date of his promotion in his parent department or alternatively from the date of his deputation to CAT.
However, the Tribunal held that he cannot be given seniority as section officer w.e.f.
the date he held this post in his parent department or date of deputation as he never held the post of section officer in CAT.
The petitioner may either avail of direction of Tribunal for appointment as section officer or get repatriated to the parent department.
