The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) outlines the scope of displaced employees, and the issue of absorption and seniority.
A petitioner was appointed as conductor on 1621989 after a writ proceeding was disposed of, and then filed a subsequent writ petition claiming seniority relief from 1821987.
The relief for seniority from a specific date cannot result in monetary benefits without additional consequential direction.
As such, the labour court is unable to grant any relief to the petitioner.
