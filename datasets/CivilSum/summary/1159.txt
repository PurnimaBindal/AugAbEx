According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India and Rule 2 of the UP Recruitment of Service (Determination of Date of Birth) Rules, 1974, correction of date of birth at the end of service is not allowed.
The employee in question was already aware of the date recorded in his service record, and obtaining an interim order in his favor for continued service while suppressing this fact is deemed inappropriate.
This kind of action is considered criminal contempt by both the involved party and counsel.
