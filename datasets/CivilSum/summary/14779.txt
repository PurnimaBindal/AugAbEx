Haryana Municipal Act, Section 18(3) as amended by Act 9 of 1995 - Tenure of office of Vice-President increased from one year to five years - Members of municipal committee no longer have the right to elect a different Vice-President - Amendment is prospective and not retrospective - At the time of election, Vice-President's term of office is one year - After amendment, term of office will be five years or residue period of membership - Amendment does not apply retroactively - Vice-President's term expires after one year.
