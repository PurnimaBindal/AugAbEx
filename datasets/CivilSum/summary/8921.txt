The Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-H - Re-employment - Employer's Liability - Only the employer who retrenched the workers, not other management who took over the assets and interests, is liable for re-employment under Section 25-H of the Industrial Disputes Act.
Although the successor company took over the company, they did not take over its liabilities.
Enforced liabilities will be against the company taken over and not against the transferee as stated in Section 5 of the Acquisition Act.
[Para 6] B. Maruti Limited (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1980, Sections 5, 6 and 13 - Government Taking Over of Company and Workers - Only those workers in service of the taken-over management on the appointed date were to be taken over as per Section 13 of the Acquisition Act, not those who were retrenched by the previous management.
No awards enforceable against the taken over company existed at the time of retrenchment, and thus, the workers cannot be taken over with the assets of the company.
