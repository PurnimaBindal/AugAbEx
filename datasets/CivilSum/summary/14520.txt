Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10(1) and 12(5) - Reference - Industrial Dispute/Disputed questions - The State Government lacks the power to adjudicate on disputes raised by parties when exercising its powers under the Act.
Although the government can refuse to make a reference for disputes it deems patently frivolous or highly belated, it is the Labour Court/Industrial Tribunals that hold the power to adjudicate and decide on the lis when referred to by the State Government.
As such, the impugned orders declining to refer disputes cannot be upheld, and the State Government has been directed to reconsider the matter in accordance with the law and record its reasons in terms of Section 12(5) if it decides not to refer disputes, while also communicating the same to petitioners.
