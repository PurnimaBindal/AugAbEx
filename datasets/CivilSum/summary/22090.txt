Trade Unions Act, 1926, Section 28(1A) - Industrial dispute - Member of the Union - To invoke Section 28(1A), a person must have been a member of the registered trade union for at least 6 months.
If a person's application for membership has not been considered or accepted, they cannot be considered a member for six months.
This interpretation is in accordance with the Cardinal rule of interpretation that favors a harmonized reading of the provisions.
Disputes between non-members and the Union are not covered by Section 28(1A) nor are they considered an internal dispute of the Union.
Section 28(1A) only bars the jurisdiction of Civil Court in regards to matters that have been referred to an Industrial Court.
Adjudicating whether a person should or should not be admitted as a member is not a dispute falling within Section 28(1A).
Such persons may approach a Civil Court or Industrial Court if permitted to do so under any other provision.
