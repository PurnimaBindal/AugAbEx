For Non-Petitioner No. 1, Rameshwar Dave, Deputy Govt. Advocate, according to Constitution of India, Article 226 and Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10 and 17, an Industrial Dispute case cannot be dismissed due to the non-appearance of the workman in the proceedings before the Labour Court.
The Labour Court is under a duty to proceed to answer the reference and establish the existence of an industrial dispute.
Even if the workman is deceased, the proceedings on a reference cannot abate.
The Labour Court may only state that it was unable to record any findings on the issue, but not that the dispute itself did not exist.
Therefore, this case has been remanded for adjudication.
