Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection - Appointment - Waiting list - Selection alone does not guarantee a right to the post.
However, if a select list and waiting list are created, candidates on the list have a reasonable expectation to be called for appointment as vacancies arise.
Waiting lists may exist for appointment during peak season, suspension of conductors, or leave vacancies.
If the waiting list is disregarded and juniors are appointed instead, candidates on the waiting list should have been offered the position first.
Only if they did not respond should juniors have been appointed.
