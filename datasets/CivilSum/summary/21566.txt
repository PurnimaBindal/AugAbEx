Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Orissa Industries Service Rules, 1985, Rule 7(1) (as amended by the notification dt. 2459191) - Conditions of service - Employers have the prerogative to amend Rules to provide a common cadre of posts and equate them, in order to rectify any unfairness to a group of employees - A mere reduction in chances of promotion is not a change in the conditions of service, unless the horizon of consideration is widened.
