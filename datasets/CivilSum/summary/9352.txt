Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2 para 10 - Jurisdiction of the Labour Court - The terms of reference determine the scope of powers and jurisdiction of the Labour Court - Labour Court does not have jurisdiction to make an enquiry to trace the real employer, which is not a matter of reference nor is referable - Impleading necessary parties is not permitted - Labour Court should not travel beyond the terms of reference.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]
