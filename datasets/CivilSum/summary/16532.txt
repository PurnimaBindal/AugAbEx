According to Punjab State Electricity Board Ministerial Services (Class-III) Regulations 1985, Regulation 4, Appendix-C and Punjab State Electricity Board Employees (Punishment and Appeal) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 4(1), both Superintending Engineer and Additional Superintending Engineer can act as Appointing and Punishing authority for the post of Head Cashier as they share equal powers and status.
Therefore, a valid suspension order can be issued by either of them.
