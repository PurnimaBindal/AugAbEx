Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 311 - Departmental Enquiry - Termination of services - Natural justice - The Enquiry Officer adjourned the enquiry to produce documents that the employer relied on - despite the Petitioner's denial of charges of tampering with documents and prayer for production of the same - but the documents were not submitted - Enquiry Officer submitted his report establishing the charges without waiting for them - Termination based on report - Insufficient evidence on record to prove charges - Proper procedure not followed - Order of termination invalid.
