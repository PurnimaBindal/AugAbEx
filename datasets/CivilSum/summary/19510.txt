According to Article 311 of the Constitution of India, the acceptance of a resignation given by phonogram/telegram with the stipulation that the letter would follow does not necessarily signify immediate resignation or resignation from a prospective date, nor does it imply certain terms and conditions.
In the case at hand, the respondents hastily accepted the petitioner's resignation without waiting for the letter and without allowing a reasonable amount of time to pass.
This acceptance of resignation is completely arbitrary and cannot be legally supported.
