Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14 and 16 - Appointment - Rule of reservation within a cadre - Single post - Out of six posts of Lecturers in the college, one post of Lecturer in History - Posts in different disciplines are not interchangeable - While service, cadre, and post cannot be equated, single isolated posts in various disciplines can exist as a separate cadre - To apply the rule of reservation within a cadre, there must be plurality of posts - In cases where there is no difference in posts in different disciplines, each single post in that particular discipline will be a single post for the purpose of reservation as per Article 16(4) of the Constitution - Application of reservation in the absence of duality of posts will violate the Constitutional prohibition against 100% reservation in accordance with Article 16(1) of the Constitution.
