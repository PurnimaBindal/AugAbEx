Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Recovery of dues from employer - The Labour Court ordered the Vadodara Municipal Corporation to pay the difference in amount due and payable under the Industrial Tribunal's award, effective from 16.9.1986, in the pay scale of Rs. 550-900 by invoking provisions of Section 33(c)(2).
The order passed by the Labour Court is considered just, reasonable and non-perverse.
