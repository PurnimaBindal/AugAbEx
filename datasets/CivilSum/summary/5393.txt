Section 77 of the Employees State Insurance Act, 1948 outlines the accrual of cause of action.
When the Corporation makes a claim to recover contributions from the Principal employer, the cause of action arises.
The proviso stipulates that the Corporation cannot make a claim after 5 years.
However, this period applies only for proceedings before the Employee Insurance Court, and there is no time limit for the recovery of contributions.
[Para ] B The jurisdiction of the Corporation is detailed in the Employees State Insurance Act 1948.
The appellant received a Show Cause Notice after an inspection by the Inspector, which was followed by the allotment of Code Numbers.
During proceedings before the ESI Court, the appellant received an inspection report.
The application of the appellant under Section 75 was dismissed, and the demand upheld.
No further action was taken by the applicant.
The question of jurisdiction is held untenable at the recovery stage; otherwise, the respondents would have proceedings within the jurisdiction.
The Appeals are dismissed.
