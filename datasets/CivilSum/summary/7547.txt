The computation of wages based on Rules, orders or instructions of workmen falls under the jurisdiction of the Labour Court under Section 33-C(2) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
Workmen who did not accept retrenchment compensation are entitled to protection of pay as per order of allocation, and further revisions of pay scales.
This computation of wages is not beyond the scope of Section 33-C(2).
