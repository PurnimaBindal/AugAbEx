Constitution of India Article, 226 - Medical reimbursement - GAIL denied the reimbursement for medical expenses incurred by the patient, due to the fact that the patient underwent surgery on his own insistence.
However, it has been held that in the context of the responsibility that doctors owe to their patients, it would not be reasonable to suggest that a certain course of action was not available to the patient.
Therefore, GAIL's action was deemed arbitrary and unreasonable.
[Paras 24 and 26] B. GAIL Medical Rules, Rules 647 and 610 - Cost of medication - GAIL denied the reimbursement of the cost of medication for the patient due to the absence of supporting documents, vouchers or materials.
However, under Rules 647 and 610, vouchers and supporting documents are mandated to accompany a claim.
Thus, the denial of reimbursement was held to be neither arbitrary nor unreasonable.
