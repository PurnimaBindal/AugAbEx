Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Misconduct - Enquiry - Dismissal from service - The High Court did not act correctly in confirming the Labour Court's order reinstating the respondents with 25% back wages as there was clear evidence of misconduct.
The Labour Court's consideration of good past service did not negate the charges of misappropriation.
In cases of proven misappropriation, past record is not a factor, and the employer has discretion in considering it.
The Labour Court cannot replace the employer's penalty.
The appeal was allowed, and the High Court's order confirming the award of the Labour Court was set aside.
