Admission/Education - Admission to Post Graduate Degree and Diploma Courses in Medicine from amongst Haryana Civil Medical Service (HCMS) candidates for academic session 1997 - The Govt. order dated 21597 did not introduce a new Selection Committee or interview system, but modified the marks allotment pattern.
[Para 11] B Admission/Education - Creation of additional seats - Admission to seats meant for the next academic year is not allowed and additional seats cannot be created.
The seat must be filled based on the merit of candidates in the current year.
Pursuing a PG Diploma course does not entitle a candidate to claim PG Degree in a different discipline.
