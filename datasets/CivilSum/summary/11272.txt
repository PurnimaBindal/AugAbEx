The Posts and Telegraphs Extra Departmental - Agents (Conduct and Service) Rules, 1964, Rule 9 stipulate the recruitment requirements for the post of Extra Departmental Branch Post Master.
However, in this case, the respondent was a provisional appointee without the necessary eligibility criteria.
The Tribunal made the mistake of granting the respondent permanent status, thereby interfering with the regular recruitment process.
The respondent should have gone through the same process as other eligible candidates.
As a result, the order of the Tribunal has been quashed and the petitioner has been permitted to resume the recruitment process as per the rules.
[Paras 8 and 9]
