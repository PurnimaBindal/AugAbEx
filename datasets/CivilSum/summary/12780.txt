Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission - Petitioners were denied admission to postgraduate courses - As some candidates did not join, vacancies were created, which were notified - Only those candidates who had applied for change of discipline were given the option to choose - Vacancies that have arisen in the current year should be open to all eligible candidates - The respondent-university must consider the claim of all candidates who had applied.
