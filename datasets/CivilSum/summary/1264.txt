Industrial Employment (Standing Order) Act, 1946, Section 10-A - Suspension - Standing orders prescribe grounds for suspension when action is contemplated or pending enquiry - Challenges to suspension orders can only be made on the grounds of the competence of authority or if the allegations do not attract any misconduct under the service rules.
[Paras 43 to 45 and 56, 80] C. Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 33, 33(1) & (2)(b) - Industrial dispute - Section 33 explained - The objective of Section 33 is to protect workers from arbitrary actions taken by employers while industrial disputes are pending.
