Termination of delinquent employee violates principles of natural justice because the copy of the enquiry report is not sent to the employee with the show cause notice.
[Para 11]
Employer cannot proceed against an employee for absence from duty due to extraordinary leave that has been granted for that specific period.
[Para 12]
The award of the Labour Court is liable to be quashed if it is not based on any legal evidence and is recorded on surmises and conjectures.
[Para 10]
A termination order cannot be passed on the basis of a confessional statement unless the employee is given the opportunity to dispute it.
[Paras 10 to 13]
A termination order passed on mere confessional statements is not valid and the whole proceeding is vitiated if the employee is not supplied with a list of documents, a list of witnesses and any information about the enquiry officer.
