The petitioner had applied for admission/education/reservation for the wards of defense personnel under the category of wards of ex-servicemen and was initially selected as Sr. No. 18.
However, his Sr. No. changed to 61 after his category was changed to the category for wards of defense personnel, although he had never applied for it.
As a result, he could not get admission, while candidates below his merit were admitted under the general category.
The petitioner argued that, if his category was to be changed, it should have been changed to a more beneficial category, specifically the general category, for which he was eligible.
The court allowed the petition.
