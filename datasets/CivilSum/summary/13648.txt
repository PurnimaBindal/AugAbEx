Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Electrical) Regulations, 1965, Regulations 9(8)(b) - Promotion - Quota - Seniority - The fixed quota for promotion to Executive Engineer from diploma and degree holders is not sustainable.
An Assistant Executive Engineer is eligible for promotion based on their seniority and other relevant considerations.
[Para 5]

Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Electrical) Regulations 1965, Regulation 9(8)(b) - Qualification - Promotion - A petitioner was promoted as an Assistant Engineer Class I based on their Diploma Holder in Electrical Engineering, while a degree holder was also promoted on the same date.
Although the Regulation by the Corporation specifically adopted Diploma holders in the relevant trade, the promotion was denied to the petitioner on the solitary ground that no post was available for this category, as only 10% of posts were to be offered to non-degree holders who would mean Assistant Executive Engineers holding a diploma.
The petitioner was ultimately promoted as an Executive Engineer after the quota rule had been struck down by the Court, and is entitled to emoluments and other benefits from the date their counterpart was promoted as an Executive Engineer.
