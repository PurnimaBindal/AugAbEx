Constitution of India, Article 311 - Andhra Pradesh State Road Transport Employees (Conduct) Regulations, 1963, Regulation 28(xxxi) - Andhra Pradesh State Road Transport Employees (CCA) Regulations, Regulation 12-A - Misconduct - Charge-sheet/Preliminary Enquiry - The requirement to furnish a copy of a report would only arise if the disciplinary authority charges the employee with a misconduct under a particular Conduct Regulation without setting out allegations in the charge-sheet - The necessary allegations that would constitute a misconduct under Regulation 28(xxxi) of Conduct Regulations are furnished in the charge-memo itself - Therefore, there is no necessity for the disciplinary authority to provide a copy of the report to the petitioner.
