Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Enquiry - Evidence - The Labour Court held that the Management failed to prove that the enquiry was fair and proper due to the lack of evidence produced to justify the termination of workman, despite opportunities.
An application to adduce further evidence was allowed but not produced despite various opportunities, and the strike of advocates did not bar the representative of the Management from placing the enquiry file on record.
