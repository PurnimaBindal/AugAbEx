The petitioner was given selection grade with effect from 17281 and his pay was fixed according to the revised scale of the selection grade by order on 23888.
However, as the petitioner failed to seek relief for entitlement to the selection grade with effect from 1479 within a reasonable period, they are not entitled to seek any relief in the writ petition.
Their claim has been rejected on the ground of delay and laches.
