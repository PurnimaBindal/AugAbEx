Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 136 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(j) - Estoppel - The Supreme Court cannot entertain the issue of management not being an `industry' within the meaning of Section 2(j) if it was not argued before the High Court - If the issue is raised in the pleadings but not in the judgment, it is not considered as argued.
[Para 10]  B Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(oo) and 25(F) - Completion of 240 days - The workman must bear the primary burden to prove continuous service - However, once the workman produces some prima facie evidence and calls upon the employer to produce records, the burden shifts to the employer to show that the workman was not in service continuously for 240 days.
[Paras 11 to 16]  C. Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 136 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(s) - Workman - The High Court should not interfere with the findings of fact recorded by the Tribunal.
[Para 16]  D. Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 136 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(s) - Daily wage workers - The state authorities are obligated to maintain proper service records of daily wagers and issue them certificates of their service.
