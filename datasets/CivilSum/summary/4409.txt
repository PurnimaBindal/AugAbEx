Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Admission - Examination - The petitioner appeared for the BAMS entrance test and was placed on the waiting list - He received a letter inviting him to appear at the college on 591998, but due to late delivery he missed his chance and another candidate was admitted in his place - However, since there was no fixed date for the interview or the admission of waitlisted candidates, the date given in the letter could not be considered legally binding - The petition was granted, and the petitioner was to be accommodated in the BAMS course in the next academic year.
