Section 36-A of the Bihar Development of Homeopathy System of Medicine Act, 1953 and Section 2 of the Bihar Homeopathy Medical Educational Institutions (Regulation & Control) Act, 1987 require prior permission of the State Government for recognition of educational institutions of Homoeopathy System of Medicines.
The provision regarding no establishment of educational institution relating to Homoeopathy System of Medicines for conferment of any degree, diploma or certificate in any University came into existence for first time in 1987 Act w.e.f 3131987 and did not exist in the 1953 Act.
As a result, refusal of recognition for lack of prior permission under the 1953 Act based on provisions of the 1987 Act cannot be upheld.
