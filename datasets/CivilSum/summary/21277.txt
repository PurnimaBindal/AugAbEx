Constitution of India, Articles 32 and 136 - Orissa Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, Rule 12 - Suspension - Jurisdiction - Interim orders - The courts typically do not interfere with orders passed by administrative authorities or with interim orders from Tribunals, but they may intervene if the discretion of these bodies is abused or exercised outside its scope without justification.
Where a serious allegation of misconduct leads to an employee's suspension during a pending disciplinary enquiry, the Tribunal has no right to intervene and direct authorities not to suspend the employee without leave of the tribunal.
In such cases, the court may interfere with the exercise of discretion by Tribunals.
