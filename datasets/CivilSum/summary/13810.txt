Constitution of India, Article 14 - Employee under arrest - Show cause notice - Dispensing with enquiry - Termination - Action of Respondents to be tested on reasonableness and fairness - Explanation not given for not following procedure in memorandum and resorting to extreme option of dispensation of inquiry and inflicting penalty of dismissal - Memo was not served to petitioner in jail - Appellant was denied reasonable opportunity of submitting an explanation for his absence - Respondents did not redirect memo to jail - Order refers to fact of appellant being in jail on account of grave charges and is therefore not termination simplicitor but stigmatic order with adverse civil consequences - Corporation's action was unfair, unreasonable, and arbitrary in context of Article 14 of Constitution.
[Paras 10, 12 to 14]
