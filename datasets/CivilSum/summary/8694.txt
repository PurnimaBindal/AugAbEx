Constitution of India, Article 311 - Preliminary Enquiry - Employees subjected to a regular enquiry must be given a reasonable opportunity to defend themselves - If the report of the preliminary enquiry is not used in the regular enquiry, the employee may not be entitled to a copy thereof - However, failure to supply copies of the statements, documents and preliminary enquiry report results in denial of reasonable opportunity when the evidence collected during the enquiry and the report were submitted and were taken on the record of regular enquiry - The prosecution is required to disclose or supply such material to the delinquent - Otherwise, the delinquent cannot avail the opportunity of an effective cross-examination - This is because he cannot be expected to ask for such documents without knowledge of their existence.
[Paras 6 to 10]  B Constitution of India, Article 311 - Punjab State Electricity Board Employees (Punishment and Appeal) Regulations, 1971, Regulation 12 - The delinquent must be supplied with the findings of the enquiry before passing the punishment order and a copy of the report along with the punishment order to enable him to file an appeal, revision or representation - Admittedly, appeal and revision was provided against the punishment order - Failure to supply these materials constitutes a violation of Regulation 12 and the principles of natural justice - This was the requirement even before Mohd.
Ramzan Khan's case.
