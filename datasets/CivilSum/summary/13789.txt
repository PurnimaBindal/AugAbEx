Punjab Privately Managed Recognized Schools Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 6 of Entry No. 11 of Appendix - Art & Craft Teacher - Requirements for Absorption in Govt Service - Eligibility for the post of Art & Craft Teacher for absorption in Govt Service includes a metric qualification with a two-year, recognized diploma in Art & Craft.
Teachers who do not possess these requisite qualifications are not eligible or entitled to be appointed or absorbed in Govt service when the school is taken over by the Govt Only qualified staff are eligible for absorption.
