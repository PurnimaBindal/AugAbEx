Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Paragraph 7(b) - Disability pension - Delay and laches - Attributability of disability to Military service - Though mere delay cannot be the sole basis for denying the benefit of disability pension, the absence of evidence linking the disability to service duty makes it impossible for the court to grant any relief.
