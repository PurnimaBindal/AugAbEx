RP Sethi and HS Bedi, JJ.
have determined that seniority of an employee cannot be determined through ad hoc basis unless the appointment was lawful and against an existing vacancy.
If such appointment was not lawful, the period on ad hoc will not count towards seniority in the cadre.
However, if the appointment was otherwise legal except for certain procedural errors, such errors will be deemed cured with subsequent regularization.
The question required authoritative pronouncement by a larger bench due to conflicting views in prior Division Bench judgments.
This order directs the Additional Registrar to place the matter before the Hon'ble Chief Justice for constitution of a larger Bench.
