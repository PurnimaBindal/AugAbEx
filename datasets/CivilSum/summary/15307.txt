Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ Petition - Laches - A writ petition challenging an award dated 23.9.98 has been filed on 652000 - However, there is no explanation for the delay in filing the writ petition - Furthermore, the workman has already recovered the amount due under the award prior to filing the writ petition - Therefore, the writ petition must be dismissed based on laches.
