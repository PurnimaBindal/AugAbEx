Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 300-A - Pension Regulations for Army 1961, Regulation 173 - Disability Pension - The petitioner was denied disability pension even though he was boarded out due to low medical category, but the Signals Record Office had details of his medical disability discharge.
His record was destroyed as per regulations, but the respondents have directed a medical examination and may consider his claim of disability pension if he fulfills the criteria in Regulation 173.
