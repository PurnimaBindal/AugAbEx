Dismissal from service due to continuous absence must follow natural justice under Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 and Border Security Force Act, 1968, Sections 11 and 62, as well as Border Security Rules, 1969, Rules 22(2), 28-A, 170, 177 and 178.
An ex parte inquiry must be conducted under Section 62 and a show-cause notice issued even if the personnel does not respond or join back, and failure to reply is deemed compliance with the procedure.
The DGP is not required to appoint an enquiry officer according to Rules 21, 48 and 49, as Rule 22(2) and Section 11 can be invoked to determine the delinquent is unfit for retention in service.
