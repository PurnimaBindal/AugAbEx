In regards to education and correction of mark sheets, the Rajasthan Board of Secondary Education's instruction of fixing a 30 day limit for seeking corrections, as outlined in Para 5, does not apply in cases where the school or authority admits to a clerical mistake and requests for correction of two neighboring candidates' marks.
The mistake is not the fault of the student and should be corrected immediately.
[Paras 23, 26, 27] B.
It is the Board's duty to correct clerical mistakes made in the tabulation of marks sent to them by the school, and affected candidates need not litigate with other candidates.
If the Board suspects other candidates may be affected, they can hold an enquiry and provide an opportunity for those candidates to defend themselves.
