Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Necessary parties - A workman from the Comprehensive Rural Health Services, a joint venture of the AIIMS and the Haryana Govt., called to reference - Haryana Govt. not a necessary party to the reference despite the joint venture.
[Para 7]
Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(a) - Appropriate Govt. - Place of work - Place of appointment - Appointment made by a Delhi-based statutory body holds no significance if workmen were terminated from Ballabgarh - Haryana Govt. was the appropriate Govt. for reference, not the Central Government.
[Para 6]
Industrial Disputes Act, Section 2(j) - Industry - Management's nature of activities determines if an industry - Evidence absent before Labour Court about the nature of activities, management cannot contend otherwise before the High Court.
[Para 7]
Industrial Disputes Act, Section 25-F - Workman - Short term need-based appointment - Workmen employed for 8 years not appointed for short term needs - Entitled to benefits of Section 25-F and reinstatement if termination in violation thereof.
