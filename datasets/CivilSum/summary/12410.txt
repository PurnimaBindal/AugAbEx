Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33-C(2) - Suspension - Pay and allowance for suspension period - The competent authority must issue a show cause notice prior to reducing pay and allowances to subsistence allowance only for an employee receiving subsistence allowance during suspension, so that the employee can prove that the suspension order was not justified and that they are entitled to full pay and allowances for the suspension period as if they were on duty.
If the reinstatement order does not follow this procedure or does not show that the suspension order was valid, the employee is entitled to full pay and allowances for the suspension period.
Order allowing the application U/s 33-C(2) of ID Act upheld.
