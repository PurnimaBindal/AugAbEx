The Rules for Management of Recognised Non-Government Institutions (Aided and Unaided), 1969, Rule 28 states that when statutory rules are available, the appointing authority is required to inform the Employment Exchange of the vacancy and consider only those candidates sponsored by the Employment Exchange.

Rule 28 of the Rules for Management of Recognised Non-Government Institutions (Aided and Unaided), 1969, states that the Court cannot direct the managing committee to allow any candidate to appear at the interview in violation with the statutory rules.
