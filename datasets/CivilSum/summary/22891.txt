Constitution of India, Article 16 - Principle of equal pay for equal work - Even if someone employed on a daily wage basis performs the same duties as a regular employee, they are not entitled to equal pay if they have not gone through the selection process that is required of a regular employee.

Constitution of India, Article 16 - Principle of equal pay for equal work - Although a daily wage employee may perform the same duties as a regular employee, they are not entitled to the same pay if they have not undergone the selection process required of regular employees.
The principle of equal pay for equal work requires that there be a complete and total identity between the two persons before granting equal pay for equal work.
The earlier view that if two persons are discharging the same functions they will be entitled to the same wages has now been replaced by the view that there should be complete and total identity between the two.
