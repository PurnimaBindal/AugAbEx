Section 25-F(b) of Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Insufficiency of compensation paid to workman - Waiver raised for first time before Division Bench - Compliance of Section 25-F(b) mandatory before retrenchment of workman - Contrary action is void ab initio.
[Paras 12 and 13]
Section 25-G of Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Rule 77-A of West Bengal Industrial Disputes Rules - Retrenchment - Principle of last come, first go - No seniority list maintained by the company - Creating documentary evidence of junior-most status of workman compulsory compliance of Industrial Disputes Act - Retrenchment without evidence illegal.
[Paras 17 to 19]
Differentiation of Waiver and Estoppel - Waiver is contractual and creates a cause of action - A party knowingly agreed not to assert a right for a consideration - Estoppel is a rule of evidence, not a cause of action.
