Constitution of India, Article 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 10 - Reference - Superannuation - Dispute regarding date of birth - The Central Govt. Labour Tribunal-cum-Labour Court awarded retirement on superannuation to employee and referred the matter to an Age Determination Committee to decide the dispute regarding his date of birth according to rules - The Age Determination Committee held that the date of birth recorded in employee's service record was correct after hearing the employee and examining evidence - The award was challenged in writ petition, but in the meantime, the employee was reinstated and allowed to continue in service after the date of superannuation - Due to the unique circumstances of the case, no relief can be granted to the petitioner employer or employee represented by Union.
[Paras 2 and 5) ]
