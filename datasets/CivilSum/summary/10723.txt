Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16(1) - Regularisation of services - The claim for regularisation to the Helper Motor Mechanic post must be made in accordance with proper procedure in order to be valid - Appointment to public office must follow proper procedure - In this case, the initial appointment was not in accordance with the procedure because the post was not advertised - Such illegalities cannot be remedied by regularisation.
