Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Challenge to seniority list - Counting of ad hoc service - In light of the Supreme Court decision in `State of Haryana v. Haryana Veterinary and AHTS Association', ad hoc service rendered prior to regular appointment cannot be considered for seniority or selection grade benefit - Consequently, the employee's seniority was correctly determined solely on regular service period.

No. 60 - If granted, seniority of employees between S. No. 61 and 118 would be negatively impacted and potential promotion opportunities reduced - Thus, not impleading them proves fatal to the claim for refixation of seniority.
