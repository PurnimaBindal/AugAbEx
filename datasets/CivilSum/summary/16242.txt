Constitution of India, Articles 311 - Punjab Civil Service Rules Volume I Part I - Rule 3.26 Punjab Civil Services Rules Volumes II (as applicable to Haryana State) - Premature retirement - Government Instructions - The petitioner, who was appointed as a teacher in Joint Punjab and allocated to the State of Haryana, was issued a notice to retire after attaining the age of 55 years in public interest.
The impugned action was taken based on the Haryana Government instructions, which were struck down by DB in KK Vaid v. State of Haryana 1990(1) SLR 1 - according to these instructions, government employees who have earned 7% good or better category of report for the past years were to be retained in service after the age of 55.
The record shows petitioner improvement from an average report to a good report and outstanding report.
Premature retirement on the basis of instructions that have already been struck down is not permissible.
The impugned order /notice of prematurely retiring petitioner is quashed.
[Paras 2, 3 and 4]
