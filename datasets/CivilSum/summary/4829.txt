Constitution of India, Article 226 and 311 - Disciplinary Proceedings - Major Penalty- Removal - Misconduct - Acts of omission and Commission during service by petitioner, such as failure to follow loan instructions, irregularities in payment, and allowing overdrawl in excess of overdraft facility.
However, no monetary loss caused to the bank due to these acts.
Penalty of removal was imposed by disciplinary authority and later affirmed by the appellate authority.
It was held that the acts of misconduct were not serious in nature and the penalty was disproportionate.
Therefore, the impugned order was set aside and quashed.
[Paras 26 to 29]
