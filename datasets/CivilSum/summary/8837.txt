Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I, Part I Rule 3.26 - Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Rule 3 - Compulsory retirement - Premature retirement - The intention of the framers of the rule is to maintain integrity in the public service.
The power cannot be used to unjustly target a public servant.
The purpose of exercising this power is to stamp out corruption and incompetence and to ensure efficiency of service.
An employee's conduct and reputation must be such that their continuance in service poses a threat to public service and contrary to public interest to warrant compulsory retirement.
Every omission cannot suggest an ulterior motive or doubtful integrity.
In the present case, the notice of compulsory retirement was issued based solely on an allegation of irregularity relating to a period beyond ten years which was never proven nor an inquiry held.
Such an action cannot be deemed taken in public interest or satisfy the test of judicial scrutiny.
The State Government must review if there was sufficient evidence to take disciplinary action leading to compulsory retirement.
The action is held to be violative of the principles of natural justice, and the notice is quashed.
