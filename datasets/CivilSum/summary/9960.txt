Constitution of India, Article 16 - Qualifications - Appointment to the post of lecturer - Public Service Commission (PSC) advertised the post and also notified qualifications required.
However, PSC could not find candidates with requisite qualifications.
Consequently, PSC selected a candidate having equivalent qualifications.
But the selection got set aside because PSC could not consider any alternate qualifications.
[Paras 6, 7 and 8]
