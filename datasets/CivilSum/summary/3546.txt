Constitution of India, Article 309 - Maharashtra Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1982, Rule 66-A(1) - Pensionary benefits - The petitioner received leave encashment for 43 days after he opted for VRS following 27 years of service.
The petitioner sought 150 extra days of leave encashment under the legal fiction created by Rule 66-A(1), but the court held that this fiction only applies to the calculation of pension benefits, not to leave encashment.
