Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Central Rules, 1946, Rule 2A - Private organization - The right to transfer must come from the contract of employment, service regulations, and category of employment.
The transfer cannot be used as a tool against administration's interest.
[Paras 17, 19, 23, 37, and 40]  B.
Transfer - Implied malice - The transfer order was passed on punitive considerations, and the order smacks of implied malice on the face of the record.
The transfer was not justified as per the service regulations or terms of the appointment letter.
Consequently, the order of transfer cannot hold.
