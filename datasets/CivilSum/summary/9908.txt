According to Article 311(2) of the Constitution of India, in cases where a criminal charge and departmental proceedings for the same offence are ongoing, the departmental authorities are not allowed to proceed with the enquiry.
Even if the Enquiry Officer goes ahead with the proceedings, the court judgment will not be affected.
If the employee is later acquitted in the criminal case, the order of dismissal from service must be issued.
In the case of an employee's death, the widow is entitled to 50% of arrears of salary.
