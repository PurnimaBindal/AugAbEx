Prevention of Corruption Act, Sections 5(2) and 5(i)(e) - Departmental Proceedings - Employees charged with failure to maintain absolute integrity and devotion to duty and possession of pecuniary resources or property disproportionate to the known sources of income - CBI recommends discharge of employee due to insufficient evidence - Departmental proceedings cannot be solely based on CBI's recommendation - Standard of proof and onus in criminal proceedings and departmental proceedings differs.
[Para 13]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Departmental Proceedings - Court will not quash proceedings if the department can provide justifiable reasons for delay - High Court quashed proceedings on the wrong assumption of facts - Order set aside and department directed to complete the enquiry promptly.
