Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and 311 - Confidential Report - Adverse remarks/adverse report - The reporting officer's subjective opinion is used to form the A.C.R., but it must be based on objective appraisal of material.
Assessments will inherently have a degree of subjectivity.
The confidential remarks assess an officer's work, performance, and conduct by their superiors.
If the confidential report is recorded in line with A.C.R.
guidelines, the assessment by reporting/reviewing/accepting authority is not unjustified.
[Paras 12 and 13]
