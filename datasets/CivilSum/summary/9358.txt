Under the Calcutta Municipal Corporation Act 1952, despite being repealed by the CMC Act 1980, regulations created under the former act are still in effect due to section 25 of The Bengal General Clauses Act, 1899.
Section 20 of the CMC Act stipulates that terms and conditions of service must be determined by regulations.
These regulations may be subject to Section 602 and 603 of the Act, but they are not required to be gazetted or notified - a condition that is excluded by Section 608.

The Constitution of India, Article 309 is applicable to the Calcutta Municipal Corporation Act 1980, which requires that service rules and regulations must not have retrospective effect.
When creating Service Rules and Regulations, principles of Natural Justice hold no applicability under Article 309 of the Constitution Regulations changing Service conditions cannot have a retrospective effect.
