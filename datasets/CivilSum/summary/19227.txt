According to the Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974, only the service rules or regulations framed under section 12 will be effective upon government approval under section 13(3A)(a) and (b).
If there are already approved rules in place, any new draft rules will not be applicable unless specifically approved to replace the existing rules.
Any reference to the draft rules before official approval is considered a violation of the current statutory rules and cannot be followed even as guidelines.
