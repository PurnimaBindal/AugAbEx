The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 11, 11-A and 15 - The Tribunal made justified findings on the facts and evidence of the case in its second holding that the charges against the workman were true.
However, the Tribunal failed to exercise its power under section 11-A in determining whether the punishment of dismissal was necessary or not.
Instead of remanding the case back to the Tribunal, the respondent-management was directed to pay the petitioner a sum of Rs. 50,000/- for his rehabilitation as an ex gratia payment.
