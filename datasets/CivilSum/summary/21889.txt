Under the Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971, Sections 26-28 and Schedules II, III, IV, temporary employees can be terminated without it being classified as an unfair labour practice.
Appellants are not entitled to a permanent position simply by working almost regularly since an appointment letter may explicitly define the period of employment and nature of job.
Although the appellant worked for 240 days in many years, termination cannot be seen as an act to deprive the employee of the benefit of permanent status since the respondent was employing temporary workers for various projects.
[Paras 26-28]
Completing 240 days of continuous service during a year does not automatically lead to regularisation.
This concept was introduced so that employers are required to pay compensation in the manner stated in Section 25-F of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
