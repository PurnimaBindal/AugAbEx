Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 21 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(ra) - Regularisation of services of casual daily wagers - Provision of temporary status - The failure to regularise the services of casual daily labourers after granting them even temporary status would be sheer exploitation of labour, which is prohibited under Section 2(ra) of the Industrial Disputes Act, unless their appointments are not de hors the rules and back door entry - It is not a wise policy to employ poor labourers as casual daily wagers or temporary workers for several years and then plead non-availability of regular posts - The government is directed to grant permanent status to such workers and make a decision on the matter pending consideration for creating posts for that purpose.
[Paras 12 and 13]
