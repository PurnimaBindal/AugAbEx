Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Disciplinary proceedings - Charge-sheet - Bias/Malice/Mala fide - It is not within the court's jurisdiction to examine charges at the initial stage in order to quash a charge-sheet - If malice or a mala fide motive is evident, the court may interfere with the issuing of a charge-sheet early on to prevent harassment or humiliation to a public servant.
[Para 37] B.
Bias - Malice - Evidence on record is required to establish malice or bias - General statements are insufficient - Bias counts as a form of malice - There must at least be a substantial possibility of bias to taint an action.
[Paras 5, 6, 9, 10 and 38] C Mala fide - "Mala fide" refers to a deliberate act lacking bona fides with bad motives - Evidence of bias and an absence of bona fides is required - An action being merely not bona fide does not equate to mala fide unless accompanied by other bad motivations.
