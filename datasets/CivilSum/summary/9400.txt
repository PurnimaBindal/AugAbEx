Constitution of India, Article 226 - Border Security Force (Tenure of Posting and Deputation) Rules, 2000, Rules 5 & 7 - Transfer - Mala fide transfers - The transfer of Commandant was made in accordance with due consideration and recommendations of all concerned authorities and prescribed procedures in Transfer and posting Rules.
Therefore, it cannot be claimed to be made for some external reasons.
The transfer matters' judicial review in public service and para-military services for public servants has a restricted scope, and judgments' civilian cases ratio cannot be applied.
