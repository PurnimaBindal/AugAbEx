Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Rules and Regulations, 1985 - Recruitment and Promotions Regulation, Clause 10 - Seniority - Ad hoc service to be counted towards determining seniority - Final seniority list prepared in accordance with Recruitment and Promotion Regulations - Respondents appointed on ad hoc basis after proper selection and consideration of confidential reports - Ad hoc appointments made as no recruitment and promotion rules were in force - Officials appointed on ad hoc basis were eligible and vacancies were available - Petitioners' seniority computed based on date of appointment on temporary basis and same yardstick applied to all - Petitioners have no ground for grievance regarding seniority list - Petition dismissed.
