Constitution of India, Article 226 - Benefit of past service - The petitioner joined PUDA as a Clerk from a different department in 1983 when decision agenda item No. 5325 dated 7.10.80 was in operation, which granted employees joining PUDA the benefit of regular service in their previous government or semi-government job on the same post for further promotion.
A person similar to the petitioner was also given similar benefits.
The subsequent decision in 1986 vide agenda item No. 7920 would only be prospective in nature and cannot affect the vested rights of the petitioner who joined the organization in 1983 and is entitled to the grant of benefit of his past service according to decision vide agenda item No. 5325.
[Paras 9, 12 and 13]
