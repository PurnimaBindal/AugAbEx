Article 16(4) of the Constitution of India - Punjab Service of Engineers Class-I PWD (Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1964, Rule 9 - Reservation for Scheduled Castes/Backward Classes - The reserve points indicated on the roster are to be filled from among reserve category candidates, while candidates belonging to the general category are not eligible to be considered for reserve posts.
Reserve category candidates can, however, compete for non-reserve posts, but their appointment cannot be considered when determining the percentage of reservation.
The percentage of posts reserved for backward classes must be strictly followed, and it cannot be changed simply because members of the backward class have already been appointed or promoted against general seats.
[Para 4]

Article 16(4) of the Constitution of India - Punjab Service of Engineers Class-I PWD (Irrigation Branch) Rules, 1964, Rule 9 - Reservation for Scheduled Castes/Backward Classes - The roster must be maintained in each department in the form of a running account, which operates from year to year until the quota provided under the government's instructions is reached.
Once the prescribed percentage of reserved posts is filled, the roster no longer operates.
Whenever a permanent or temporary vacancy arises, it must be filled from the category to which the post belongs in the roster.
[Paras 5, 6, 7, and 10]

The word "post" refers to an appointment, job, office, employment, or a position to which a person is appointed, and the term "vacancy" means an unoccupied post or office.
The cadre-strength is measured by the number of posts in the cadre, and the percentage of reservation is calculated based on the number of posts that comprise the cadre-strength.
The right to be considered for appointment can only be claimed in respect of a post in a cadre, and the concept of vacancy has no relevance in operating the percentage of reservation.
[Para 6]
