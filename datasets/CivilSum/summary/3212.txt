The respondents' actions in excluding the petitioner from consideration for selection as an Assistant Professor were deemed unjust and invalid due to the fact that the petitioner possessed one of the prescribed qualifications in the advertisement, namely an M.Sc.
in Organic Chemistry.
Furthermore, the petitioner was not estopped from challenging the selection on the grounds of bias even though the objection was not made at the time of the interview.
The court considers the prescription of qualifications for faculty posts in educational institutes to be the domain of experts and are therefore prone to avoiding examinations of such qualifications.
Bias invalidates selection proceedings, but it is hardly grounds for presumption that a guide student would be biased in technical selections.
Ad hoc appointment is bound to the terms of the contract of appointment and rules governing appointment, and the question of filling vacancies or keeping them vacant is up to the competent authority.
