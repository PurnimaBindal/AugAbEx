Indian Medical Council Act, 1956, Section 33 - Graduate Medical Education Regulations, 1997, Regulation 4 - Constitution of India, Article 226- Admission for Medical studies - Minimum age requirement - The challenge to the validity of the criteria for prescribing a minimum age has already been rejected by a Division Bench in the case of Mohit Kumar Arora after discussing various precedents of the Apex Court and High Courts.
The requirement of a minimum and maximum age is mandatory and not directory in professional courses as it is deemed necessary for proper maturity and understanding of the course by the candidate and is based on intelligible differentia.
