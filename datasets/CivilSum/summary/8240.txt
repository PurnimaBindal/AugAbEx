Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 12(5) - Conciliation officer submitted report - Dispute referred to Labour Court - Respondent No. 2 is an NMR employee of petitioner - Accused of removing cable wire - Dismissed from job - Labour Court issued ex parte award - Ordered for full back wages and reinstatement - Award contested - Ruling says ex parte award is valid - Respondent is "workman" as stated under Section 2(9) of Act - Respondent was not given a chance to defend charges - Removing respondent counts as retrenchment - Worker cannot be laid off as punishment - Necessary provisions of Section 25-F not followed - Decision of Labour Court is free of defects.
