Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 226 - Haryana Public Works Department (Public health) Branch Head Quarter Officer Office Ministerial (Group C) Services Rules, 1982, Rule 11 - Seniority in different cadres - The Head Office Cadre and Circle Cadre have different conditions of service and seniority, and seniority is determined by length of continuous service in the respective cadres.
If a person transfers from Circle Cadre to Head Office Cadre and accepts seniority at the bottom on the transfer date, they cannot claim seniority or receive benefits based on their previous service in Circle Cadre.
They cannot be promoted over those who were already in the cadre and senior to them.
