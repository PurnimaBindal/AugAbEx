Constitution of India, Article 226 - Seniority - Refixation of Seniority - Benefit of past services - Petitioner was shifted from one office to another in exigencies of service, with no request for transfer - His initial appointment as clerk was later regularized - Denial of service for computing seniority on clerk and Assistant posts is unreasonable and arbitrary - Respondents must fix petitioner's seniority from his initial date of appointment as a clerk - They must also refix his seniority on higher posts and consider his claim for promotion based on the refixed seniority.
