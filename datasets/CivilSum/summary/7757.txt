Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part I, Rule 7.5(4) - Resignation - Withdrawal of - The concerned authority can accept the request for resignation withdrawal under certain conditions including resignation due to compelling reasons within two months of being relieved, and the employee's conduct during the resignation period was proper and the post still exists.
However, if the application is submitted after two months and the post is not available or there is no change in the compelling reasons for resignation, then the withdrawal request will be denied.
[Paras 9, 10 to 12]

Punjab Civil Services Rules, Vol.
I, Part I, Rule 7.5(4) - Resignation - Retrospective acceptance - The resignation will be deemed to be accepted from the date of resignation, and if an application for withdrawal is not submitted within two months from the deemed date of being relieved, then the employee is not entitled to withdraw the resignation.
The petitioner's failure to apply within the required time leads to the withdrawal request being denied.
[Paras 11 & 13]
