Army Act, 1950 - Army Pension Regulations, 1961, Regulation 173, Appendix II, Paragraph 7(b) - Disability pension/Pension - The petitioner was discharged from service in 1970 due to epileptic fits.
According to paragraph 7(b) of Appendix II, if a disease leading to discharge was not noted at the time of acceptance for military service, it is considered to have arisen in service.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to disability pension, and the petition is allowed.
