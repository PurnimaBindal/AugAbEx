As per the Industrial Dispute Act, 1947 Section 10 and 25F, a workman who was appointed on a fixed term and terminated was reinstated with 25% backwages.
Despite a challenge to the award on the ground of delay, the court held that delay alone is not enough to reject a claim.
The Labour Court was careful and balanced in their approach, as evidenced by the equities presented.
The writ was dismissed, relying on the Ajaib Singh case.
