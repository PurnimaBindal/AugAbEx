Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Adverse reports - Pre-mature retirement - The criteria for promotion, efficiency bar, and pre-mature are based on the employee's service record with a focus on past performance for crossing the efficiency bar and the utility and quality of an employee for continued service for retention.
Whereas, merit plays a vital role in promotion as the person with a higher merit compared to others in the zone of consideration must be selected for promotion.
