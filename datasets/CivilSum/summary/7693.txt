Road Transport Corporation Act, 1950, Section 45 - UP State Road Transport Corporation Employees (other than Officers) Service Regulations, 1981, Regulations 4(1)(iii), 39 - Pension benefits - Argument of claim - Estoppel - Petitioners who were absorbed in a non-pensionable post of the Corporation, contributed to Provident Fund, agreed to the service regulation of the Corporation and received retirement benefits - They have accepted the terms and conditions of employment and thus cannot challenge their rights after retirement.
