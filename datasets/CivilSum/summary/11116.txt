Contempt of Courts Act, 1952, Sections 11, 12 and 19 - Disobedience of the order - Scope of - Petitioner challenged his transfer/reversion order which was set aside - Disciplinary action against petitioner was initiated for not adhering to one of the orders which was not stayed - Contempt petition was disposed of with the observation that no case was made out to initiate contempt proceedings - Directions regarding dropping disciplinary proceedings held to be outside the scope of the contempt proceeding and accordingly nullified.
