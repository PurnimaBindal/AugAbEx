Constitution of India, Article 226 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 - Termination - Reinstatement - The petitioner-employer failed to attend the court of respondent No. 1 even though notice was received - An erroneous belief regarding the non-maintainability of the reference before the Labour Court cannot be a proper or legal ground for remaining absent from court - Additionally, the confession made before a police officer cannot be considered as evidence by the Labour Court without proper legal proof - There is no infirmity in the award of respondent No. 1 directing the reinstatement of respondent No. 2-workman.
