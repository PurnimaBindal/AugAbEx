The Indian Medical Council Act of 1956 and Regulations on Graduate Medical Education of 1997 provide rules for migration from one medical college to another on compassionate grounds.
However, the Medical Council of India has the power to decide if a particular illness, such as Bronchial Asthma, warrants compassion and migration.
In a recent case, the petitioner sought migration due to her illness but there was no evidence to suggest that it caused disability.
As a result, the Medical Council of India decided that compassion was not warranted and their decision cannot be questioned.
