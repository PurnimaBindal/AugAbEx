In accordance with the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14, 16 and 226, a writ of quo warranto/appointment was sought in regards to the promotion of petitioners to posts meant for direct recruits.
It was determined that the petitioners were appointed on an ad hoc basis and were subsequently reverted when direct candidates became available.
Although there was no illegality in the initial appointments or ineligibility of the direct appointees, it was important that ineligible persons not hold public office and that appointments in public offices not be made improperly.
As such, the petitioners appointed ad hoc on posts meant for direct recruits must make way for direct recruits.
As there was no valid ground to quash the appointments of direct recruits, a writ of quo warranto was not issued and the writ was dismissed.
[Paras 9 and 10]
