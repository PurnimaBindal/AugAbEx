As per Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol.
I (Part I), Rule 1010 (as applicable to Haryana Govt. Employees) and Clause 16(a) of Kurukshetra University Employers Foreign Service Rules and Employees Pension Scheme 1995, retirement benefits cannot be denied on the grounds of delayed contribution towards provident fund and pension.
Clause 16(a) stipulates no limitation for payment of contribution.
Therefore, the petitioner is entitled to pensionary benefits without interest despite his laxity in claiming the same.
