Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 10, 11A, 33A, Schedule 3 Item 3 - Industrial Dispute (Central) Rules, 1957, Rule 59 - Award - Removal declared illegal - Reinstatement not ordered - Workman was temporarily reinstated during the pendency of the proceedings - The award was primarily based on the workman's unchallenged testimony - However, it was held that once the labour court confirms the removal to be illegal, it is mandatory to order reinstatement, even if the workman has been temporarily reinstated.
