The Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering and Technology Society employees are not considered employees of a Local Authority under the Representation of the People Act and Punjab General Clauses Act, as they are not managed or elected by the people to carry out governmental functions or civil duties, nor do they have the power to levy taxes or fees.
Therefore, they cannot be ordered to perform election duties.
[Paras 13 and 14] B.
The fact that the petitioners had previously performed election duties does not prevent them from challenging orders to do so now, on the grounds that they are not Local Authority employees under Section 159 of the Peoples Act.
This challenge is not barred by estoppel.
