Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 138(2) - Promotion as Head Constable - Reversion - Petitioner's temporary promotion without training and with the condition of possible reversion at any time, without notice or claim to seniority - Petitioner claiming promotion through sports quota, which authorities denied and couldn't be proven - Petitioner had no right to the post and must make room for eligible and properly selected candidates in accordance with the rules - Reversion decision upheld.
