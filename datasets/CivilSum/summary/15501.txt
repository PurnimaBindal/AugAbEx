Constitution of India, Articles 226/227- Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rules 123(2) and 139 - Passing of Intermediate School Course - Promotion to the post of ASI - Petitioners who have served longer or were confirmed as Head Constables earlier than respondents have seniority - Respondents must follow the service rules and fix petitioners' seniority in the rank of ASI based on their confirmation dates as Head Constables, even if respondents completed the Intermediate School Course later.
