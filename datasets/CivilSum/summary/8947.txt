Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 2(C) - Civil Contempt - Code of Civil Procedure, Order 39 Rule 2-A - Breach of injunction - To promote efficacy, one should opt for the remedies available under the Code of Civil Procedure.
Contempt of Courts Act should only be employed in cases where those remedies are ineffectual.
[Para]

Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, Section 2 - The administration of justice, along with the respect and authority of judicial tribunal, are crucial concerns in matters of contempt.
It is not meant to be used for redressing personal grievances.
For such breaches, one must seek remedies under the ordinary law.
[Para]
