Constitution of India, Article 311 - Compulsory retirement - Disciplinary proceedings - Extensions allowed at 50 and 55 years due to pending proceedings and representation against adverse remarks - Employees may be compulsorily retired nine months before actual superannuation after proceedings conclude - Authority's actions in extending proceedings and ordering retirement deemed just and fair - Suspicion of employee's integrity justifies early retirement - High Court erred in disregarding these points.
