Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability Pension - Petitioner began serving in the Army in 1961 and had no health problems for 20 years.
In 1984, the petitioner was categorized as Shape-1 and developed diabetes and hypertension in 1986.
There is no evidence that the petitioner was suffering from any ailment during his Army enrollment.
Consequently, it can be concluded that the petitioner's disease was caused by military service, and the Board assessed his disability at 30% composite.
As a result, the petitioner is entitled to disability pension.
