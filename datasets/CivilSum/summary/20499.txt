The Court or Tribunal cannot examine the truth or validity of charges if the charge memo is vague and does not specify the misconduct.
The evidence presented during the enquiry by the enquiry officer will be considered at a later stage.
It is important for the Court or Tribunal to examine the statement of facts and charge sheet at the stage of framing charges to determine the nature of the charges and the alleged misconduct.
The Tribunal acted unfairly in examining the charges at an early stage.
The charge memo and material in support thereof disclose the alleged misconduct and therefore the Tribunal was wrong to quash the charge memo.
The impugned order is set aside.
