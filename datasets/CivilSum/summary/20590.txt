The Indian Police Service (Appointment by Promotion) Regulations under Regulation 5(6) and 7(4) state that Select Lists must be reviewed and revised annually to ensure promotion to Indian Police Service is fair.
Applicants who were in the Select List of 1982 cannot automatically be considered for inclusion in the Select List of 1983, as stated by a Full Bench of Tribunal.
[Para 8]

According to the Indian Police Service (Regulation of Seniority) Rules, rule 3(3)(b), the period of continuous officiation in a senior post for the purpose of seniority only counts from the date of inclusion in the Select List.
