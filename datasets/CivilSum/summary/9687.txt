The validity of a reference under Sections 10(1)(d) and 12(5) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, is not affected by a mere reference.
A writ petition cannot be entertained prematurely.
Section 10(3) of the Act applies when the strike or lockout can be prohibited and is connected to a dispute referred to the Labour Court or Tribunal.
B.
A lockout in connection with alleged indiscipline and unjustified activities of workmen does not fall under the purview of Section 10(3) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, as they are not matters connected with the reference to the Tribunal.
