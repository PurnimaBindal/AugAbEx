Section 22(2) of the Apprentices Act, 1961 states that an apprentice must resume training for an initial period of one and a half years.
If the probation period is extended for three months, the workman is not entitled to compensation if he is relieved from service during the extended period.
The Industrial Tribunal has decided to award 60 months pay as compensation in lieu of reinstatement.
There is no automatic confirmation of the workman on completion of the initial training period.
Therefore, the workman is not entitled to compensation.
