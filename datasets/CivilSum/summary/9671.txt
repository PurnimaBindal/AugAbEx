Constitution of India, Article 14 - Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Act, 1979, Section 15 - Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 22-A - Teachers of aided schools - Eligibility for pension - The Retirement Benefit Scheme entitles teachers or employees appointed on or after 511987 to pensionary benefits - Although a proposal was made to amend the pension scheme, executive instructions were passed not to send the case of employees retiring after 162003 - The earlier pension scheme was repealed by the Governor from 162003 - However, since the 1992 scheme applies to all who were in service on 521987 and all employees covered under it form a homogenous class, the petitioners cannot be denied benefit of pension - The respondents' action is violative of Article 14 of Constitution - There is no valid reason to deny retiral benefits to petitioners on the grounds that a new pension scheme is in process - The court directed the respondents to release pensionary benefits.
