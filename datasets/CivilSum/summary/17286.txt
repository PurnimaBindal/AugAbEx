Constitution of India, Article 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11A - Termination - No interference called for in regards to the termination of workmen whose services were terminated after the expiry of the term stipulated by their last appointment letter, as no termination letter had been given to the workmen.
The appointment letters were initially issued for a period of one year and subsequently extended on a year-to-year basis.
