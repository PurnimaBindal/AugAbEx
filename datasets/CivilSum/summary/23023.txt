Entitlement Rules for Casualty Pensions Awards, 1982, Rule 4 - Invalidating from military service - Disability pension - Retirees cannot receive disability element in pension if their Medical category was reduced from the one recorded at the time of entry in service.
This alone cannot be considered as invalidating from service.
[Paras 4 and 5] B.
Army Pension Regulations, Rule 53 - Disability pension - Rule 53 is applicable only when the disability resulting in the discharge from Military service is attributable to service.
It must be proven that the disease is aggravated due to army service.
If the disease is constitutional in nature, the benefit of disability pension cannot be granted.
