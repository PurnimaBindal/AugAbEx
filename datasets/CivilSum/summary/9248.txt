Teachers, Headmasters, Inspectors of Schools or Senior School Inspectors with related titles in the Delhi Municipal Corporation, who have been taken over by the Delhi Administration, are eligible for an increase in their retirement age from 58 to 60 years as per the Constitution of India, Article 311.
The decision in the case of 1989(1) SCC 227 : AIR 1989 SC 356 is being followed.
