Article 324 of the Indian Constitution establishes that the creation, removal and service conditions of Election Commissioners is the responsibility of the Executive (President), and that the service conditions may be altered and Election Commissioners can be removed upon the recommendation of the Chief Election Commissioner, without infringing upon their independence.
Election Commissioners do not have the same status as the Chief Election Commissioner.
[Paras 10, 11, 17, 18, 27, 28, 29 and 30]
Article 324 of the Indian Constitution also establishes that the Election Commissioner's tenure is for five years and that if the post is abolished during this period, the Election Commissioner's services may be terminated.
Such loss is not uncommon in a service career and is an exigency of employment.
The petitioner cannot contest the cutting short of their tenure.
[Para 30]
Article 16 of the Indian Constitution speaks to the appointment of officers for a period of five years.
If the post is abolished during this period, the officer's services may be terminated.
Such loss is not uncommon in a service career and is an exigency of employment.
