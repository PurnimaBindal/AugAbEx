Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Regularisation - Sweeper position - Once an appointee continues to work for a significant period of time after appointment, regularisation cannot be refused on the basis of lack of educational qualification - Petitioner worked for over 20 years and appointment was not stop-gap - It would be illegal to ask petitioner to acquire language proficiency at this stage - Management was previously directed to consider regularisation but failed to comply, leading to impugned proceeding - Corporation ordered to regularise petitioner's service and provide all service benefits including time scale of pay.
