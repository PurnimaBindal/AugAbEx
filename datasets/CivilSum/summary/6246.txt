Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Indian Institute of Technology Act, 1961 - Bachelor of Technology course in Chemical Engineering - Professional courses - Compulsory practical training - Completion of mandatory training period - Application of the principle of "proposive construction" - The provision requiring a particular duration of training aims for the student to undergo at least a certain duration of training.
The provision establishes one duration as directory and another duration as mandatory, like when the training is for a duration of 6 months and when it requires at least 400 working hours - If the trainee has completed the training of 400 hours during working hours, the training is considered to be complete.
