Constitution of India, Article 226 - Army Act - Punishment - Severe displeasure - Promotion - While the Petitioner was working as Colonel in the army, he was selected for promotion to the acting rank of Brigadier.
However, by violating the terms and conditions of the Army Officers Housing Organisation, he deprived himself of the promotion.
It is evident that the petitioner was bound by the terms and conditions of allotment made to him, and without prior consent of the Housing Organisation, he could not affect any sale.
As a responsible officer, he cannot act in flagrant violation of the terms and conditions of allotment.
It is not acceptable for the petitioner to state that the matter is between him and Army Welfare Housing Organisation, and the respondent has no jurisdiction to impose punishment.
The order passed by the fourth respondent, imposing the punishment of severe displeasure, is entirely accurate.
