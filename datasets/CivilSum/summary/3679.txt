The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Reservation - The main duties an employee performs determine his post and not incidental work - The petitioner served as a cook for over eight hours every day - This means he cannot be denied the benefit of the regular pay scale of the post he fulfils, which would violate his fundamental right under Article 14 - The State is responsible for ensuring not just social justice but also economic justice and cannot keep poor workers as casual labour on daily wages.

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(s) and 2(g) - Workman - Industrial Dispute - Employers are responsible for resolving disputes with workmen regarding confirmation of workmen who worked in a higher grade - The existence of the relationship between the workman and employer is a finding of fact - This finding should not be interfered with when under exercise of jurisdiction under Article 227.
