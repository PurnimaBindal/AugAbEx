Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11-A - Dismissal from services - The driver of Roadways caused an accident in which three passengers died and 41 others were injured due to rash and negligent driving, leading to the termination of his services after an enquiry.
The Labour Court's pending proceedings granted reinstatement with a fine, but under Section 11-A of the Industrial Disputes Act, the Labour Court should not have interfered.
Therefore, the termination of services was upheld.
