Constitution of India, Article 226 - Disability pension - Petitioner discharged from Army due to medical reasons after serving for 15 years, 4 months - Diagnosed with Bilateral Multiple Renal Calculi under medical category CEE (P) - Medical board confirms disease as a result of Army Service - Despite offered sheltered employment, petitioner refused and was subsequently discharged - Court orders release of disability pension benefits to petitioner.
