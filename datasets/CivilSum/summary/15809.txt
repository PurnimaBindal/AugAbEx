Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion - Compassionate appointment - Petitioner, who is the senior-most peon, requested to be promoted as Daftri - However, respondent No. 4 was appointed as Daftri on compassionate grounds - As a result, the only promotional avenue available to the petitioner and his colleagues is now closed - Respondent No. 4's appointment cannot be quashed as it was made on compassionate grounds - The petitioner's case is to be considered for promotion in any district in the State as he is willing to be accommodated in any district.
