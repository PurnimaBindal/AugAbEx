Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961, Sections 27, 29, and 55 - Term of the administrator - The Registrar has the jurisdiction to appoint an administrator or supervisor.
The administrator's aggregate period, or any other officer for that matter, cannot exceed five years.
It's important to make arrangements for election of the new committee within the allotted time.
Any administrator's order passed after the five-year term will be invalid - Section 27 is unambiguous in this regard.
[Paras 4 and 6] B. Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961, Sections 30, 55, and 57 - Reference of dispute - Arbitration of disputes - a resolution of the committee must exist for a dispute to be referred to arbitration.
A genuine resolution, not an empty formality, must be passed for the reference of a dispute to arbitration.
[Para 5] C Punjab Co-operative Societies Act, 1961, Sections 27, and 29 - Section 29 of the Act has no application in the case of appointment of arbitrator administrator.
It only applies in the appointment of other officers of the Committee.
