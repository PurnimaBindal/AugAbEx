The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 2(s) and 10(1), was the basis for the case of RS Randhawa, Adv.
As the employee raising the dispute was not deemed a workman within S2 of the Act, the State Govt. declined to make a reference.
The State Govt.’s decision to uphold the order was deemed fine, and the petition was dismissed.
This was in accordance with Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 and followed the precedent of the State of Bombay v. KP Krishnan, AIR 1960 S.C. 1223.
