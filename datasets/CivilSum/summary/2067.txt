Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 309 - Promotion/Reversion/Natural justice - Cancellation of promotion order - Orders made illegally against rules and natural justice are nullity, forged, fabricated and illegal - The affected employee has no right to benefit from such orders - Therefore, the principles of natural justice need not be complied with if the authority wishes to cancel illegal orders after going into the facts.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ Jurisdiction - Writ - The High Court can issue a writ of certiorari at its discretion - Even against illegal orders, the High Court may refuse to issue a writ if it will lead to another illegal order.
