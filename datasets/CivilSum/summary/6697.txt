The petitioner's father, who works in the office of AG (Audit) Punjab since June 1982, is claiming admission to the MBBS course on seats reserved for Punjab residents.
The university requires candidates to have passed 10+1, 10+2 or its equivalent exams as regular candidates from institutes in Punjab.
In Meenal Sharma's case, the court held that the condition of qualifying exams from an institute in the state is not discriminatory and has a rational object to stop people from filing bogus domicile certificates.
However, the petitioner's father did not reside in Punjab for the past three years and only visited during tours in the state when he paid TA/DA, making the petitioner ineligible to be considered a resident of Punjab.
