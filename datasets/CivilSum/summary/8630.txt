Constitution of India, Article 226 - Letter Patent Appeal, Clause X - Transfer - Due to exigency of service, the transfer and posting of Respondent is not liable to be interfered with in writ jurisdiction, as transfer and postings are an incidence of service - Order of single judge set aside - Appeal allowed.
[Paras 20 and 21] B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Transfer - Jurisdiction - It is not within the jurisdiction of this court to question the decision on transfers and posting, as it falls exclusively within the domain of concerned authorities - This court will not interfere unless the decision is vitiated by mala fides or on totally extraneous consideration.
