Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Punjab Agricultural Service (Class I) Rules, 1974 - Single vacancy - Policy of reservation - Unreserved vacancy - Petitioner claims that he has a right to be considered for the post of Joint Director Agricultural as a member of Scheduled Caste - Appointment by mode of selection from amongst Agricultural Engineers - Benefit of reservation admissible only in a year when at least two vacancies have arisen - Only one vacancy had arisen, so it would go to a general candidate - Petitioner cannot claim the single vacancy to be reserved for the Scheduled Caste.
