Constitution of India, Articles 226, 309 - Kerala Service Rules Part- III, Rule 90(6-A) - Family Pension - Eligibility of family pension - Deceased survived by his mother and only married daughter - Rule 90 does not terminate eligibility for family pension upon marriage of deceased's daughter - Mother and married daughter eligible for family pension as mother has no other means of support - Petition granted and costs awarded.
