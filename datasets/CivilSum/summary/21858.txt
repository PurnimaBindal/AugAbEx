The ID Act's Section 2(g) and 25-B, and the U.P.
ID Act's Section 4(k) and 6N, address retrenchment for employees who complete one year of service and 240 days of work.
If an employee fulfills this requirement in less than 12 calendar months, they can receive benefits under sections 25B and 25F The word "preceding" in Section 25-B does not appear in Section 2(g), and interpretation of the latter should not impede the legal benefits workers deserve.
The rule requiring an inquiry into 240 days worked during the 12 months preceding retrenchment does not mandate that the employee worked for 240 days during their entire term of service.
However, the appellant's interpretation of this rule is untenable, according to Ramakrishana Ramnath v. The PO, LC 1970(3) SCC 67.
