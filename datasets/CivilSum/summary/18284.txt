Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of the Deceased Government Employees Rules, 2003, Rules 18 and 3(k) - Orphans requiring compassionate appointment under Rule 18 must receive an exception to the rules for underage individuals ("shall"), with offers remaining available until the child reaches majority or service entry age.
Claims from orphans must be considered under relaxed rules.
[Para 5] B Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of the Deceased Government Employees Rules, 2003, Rules 18 and 3(k) - Rule 3(k) defines orphans as children who have lost at least one parent at any time, without mandating the death of both at the same time.
