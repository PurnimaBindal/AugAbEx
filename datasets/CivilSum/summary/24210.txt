Indian Medical Council Act, 1956, Sections 16, 20 and 33 - Post Graduate Medical Education Regulations 2000, Regulation 9 - Admission to Postgraduate Medical Courses - Minimum percentage of marks for direct admissions and in-service candidates - States or Universities cannot prescribe lesser qualifying marks for in-service candidates contrary to MCI Regulations.
Full Bench of Rajasthan High Court's decision shows inconsistency with the Supreme Court's view in Dr. Preeti Srivastava's case that MCI regulations are mandatory and universities are bound to comply with them.
MCI Regulations have statutory force and are mandatory.
Common Entrance Examination under MCI Regulations for post-graduate medical education is not a mere screening test.
The requirement of minimum qualifying marks cannot be lowered or relaxed contrary to the MCI regulations.
The in-service candidates must be assessed as eligible for entrance in PG There can be only one common entrance test for determining eligibility for post graduation for both in-service candidates and those not in service.
The Full Bench of the High Court could not have relied upon the dissenting opinion of one learned Judge overlooking the majority opinion of a Constitution Bench.
