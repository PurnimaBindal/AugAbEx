Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Jammu and Kashmir Constitution, Section 126 - Appointment - Appointment orders were issued without any public notice - Such appointments are in utter breach of constitutional provisions and cannot be considered valid - No writ can enforce an order that is contrary to the law - Petitioners, however, are entitled to receive salary for the duration of their actual work.
[Paras 14 and 16]
