According to Punjab Police Rules 1934, Rules 162 (1), police officers who are absent from duty while consuming alcohol are committing the gravest misconduct, which cannot be overlooked even with mercy.
Even a single act of such misconduct will invite the wrath of Rule 161 (1) and constitute the gravest misconduct under it, making the officer liable to punishment, including dismissal from service.
Since police constables are part of the law-enforcing agency, they are expected to maintain discipline and exert self-restraint.
Consequently, the petition was dismissed, and the court followed JT 1992 (4) SC 253.
