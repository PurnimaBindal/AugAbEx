The Constitution of India, Articles 14, 19, and 21 - Municipal Employees demand ownership of built houses based on a resolution passed by the Corporation - The resolution creates a settlement between the Corporation and the fire brigade workers' union - The resolution only indicates that an effort will be made to provide land for building houses - Articles 14, 19, and 21 are not relevant - There is no fundamental right to claim built houses - The demand for ownership is not valid.
[Paragraphs 7 to 9] B.
The Madhya Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1956, Sections 80 and 242 - Rules dictate that a transfer of property can only be done after the state government's permission - The absence of government approval means that the claim based on a corporation resolution is invalid.
[Paragraphs 14 to 15] C. Municipal Property - Transfer of - Property meant for accommodation for fire-brigade staff - Property located near the fire brigade station - The employee's claim for transfer of such property at ownership to the occupants (employees) is not valid - The property built in the fire-brigade vicinity is meant for municipal staff belonging to the fire brigade only and cannot be transferred on an ownership basis.
[Paragraphs 16 and 17] D. The Constitution of India, Article 226 - Claim for allotment of houses - Disputed question of fact, Article 226 is not the correct remedy.
