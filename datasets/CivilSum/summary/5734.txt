The Haryana Education Code, 1978, Article 153 and the Constitution of India's Articles 14, 16, and 226 - Special Certificate - Qualifications - Acquiring eligibility qualifications for a Special Certificate for untrained teachers - The petitioner had met all eligibility requirements for the Special Certificate but was denied it because of the cut off date - The purpose of the cut off date was to encourage employees to apply in time for the certificate and not to deprive those who became eligible after that date - The State authorities cannot deny equal treatment in service on technical grounds - Fixing cut off dates for such benefits is arbitrary and discriminatory.
[Paras 16 and 18]
The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 16 - Service record - Benefit under the policy - It is the responsibility of the authorities to ensure that the benefit of a particular policy is given to each eligible employee - The benefit cannot be denied on the grounds that the employee did not make the service record available to the authorities.
