Constitution of India, Article 311 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 11 - Termination - Appointment in leave vacancy - A resolution by the Municipal Committee to appoint the petitioner on a regular basis was suspended, and the petitioner was not allowed to work in the position.
However, it was determined that an employee remains in service until a termination order is passed and conveyed, meaning that there was no termination of services in this case.
