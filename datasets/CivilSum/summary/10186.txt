Canara Bank Officers Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 46(1) - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Gratuity - Discriminatory payment - Based on retirement date - In view of the law laid down by the Supreme Court in DS Nakara's case, it is impermissible to deny additional benefit available to the same class of pensioners by adopting a different formula regarding the dates of retirement - Article 14 of the Constitution disallows class legislation.
[Paras 14, 15, 18, and 20]  B.
Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 - Gratuity - Nature of right - It is held that gratuity is a statutory right earned by long and continuous service as a retiral benefit - If it has been accrued, it cannot be taken away by an agreement between parties.
[Para 12]  C Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 - Gratuity - Agreement - Inconsistent with provisions of the Act - Sustainability - It is held that a party is entitled to challenge that portion of an agreement which deprives it of a statutory right after taking advantage of the remaining terms - Any term that is inconsistent with the 1972 Act is illegal, void ab initio, and unenforceable.
