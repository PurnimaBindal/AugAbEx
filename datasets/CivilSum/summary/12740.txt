Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - West Bengal Board of Secondary Education Recruitment Rules, Rules 3(d) and 28(7) - Absorption - The petitioner was appointed in a leave vacancy that was temporary and clearly specified to last for six months or until the return of the incumbent, whichever was earlier.
As per Recruitment Rules, the petitioner has no legal right to the post after its vacancy and is not entitled to be absorbed into it.
