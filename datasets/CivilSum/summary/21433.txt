The age of superannuation is set at sixty years old, and an employee born on 171943 would reach sixty years old on 3062003.
The court determined that in calculating a person's age, the day of their birth is counted as a whole day, and they reach the designated age on the day before their birthday.
This decision was based on LR (1918)1 Ch.
263 and AIR 1986 SC 1948.
