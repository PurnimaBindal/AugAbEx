Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Punishment/Removal from service - Personal hearing/Principles of Natural justice/adequacy/inadequacy or severity of punishment/Procedure/Findings on evidence/Appeal - The appellate authority must examine whether the procedure as per rules has been followed and whether the order of the punishing authority was based on the evidence on the file before considering the adequacy/inadequacy or severity of punishment.
If a hearing is necessary, it must be provided.
The order dismissing the appeal on a technicality is null and void, ultra vires and illegal.
As a result, the authority is directed to reconsider the appeal.

When a hearing is required, it must be provided.
Dismissing an appeal on a technicality is not acceptable.
The appeal should not have been treated as a review or a revision.
