Haryana Urban Development Authority Act, 1977, Sections 17(1) and 42 - Haryana Urban Development (Disposal of Land and Buildings) Regulations, 1978, Regulation 14 - Surrender of plot - Refund of entire amount deposited with interest - Petitioner who surrendered plot on 25383 is entitled to entire amount paid with simple interest at the rate of 9% pa from 25385 to date of actual payment, as guidelines for levy of penalty were framed on 12285 and were not in place at the time of plot surrender.
