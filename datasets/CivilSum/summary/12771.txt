Constitution of India, Article 226 - UP Cane Co-operative Service Regulations, 1975, Regulation 27 - Disciplinary proceedings - An employee was accused on 8 July 1997, a mere seven days before the end of the crushing season, which was 15 July 1997.
After an investigation, the employee was punished on 9 October 1998.
If the proceedings were not completed before the end of the crushing season due to any fault, they would be considered as dropped automatically.
Therefore, the punishment order that was delivered after the crushing season based on the disciplinary proceedings has been invalidated.
