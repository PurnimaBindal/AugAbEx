Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Adhoc appointment/Regularisation of service - The petitioner was appointed in the Bank on a temporary basis and completed more than 257 days of service in a year.
Despite the existence of a vacancy with the Bank, the petitioner's services have not been regularized.
It is settled law that the petitioner's services must be regularized.
