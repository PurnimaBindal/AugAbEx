Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Competent Authority - Rajasthan Civil Services (Special Selection and Special Conditions of Service of the Vikas Adhikaris) Rules, 1982, Schedule I - Suspension - District Collector, Rajsamand, as the head of the department, is authorized to conduct an inquiry and impose minor punishments on the Vikas Adhikaris - He can suspend a Vikas Adhikari before the inquiry if there are serious allegations that would make it undesirable for the employee to continue in their position - A single judge’s quashing of the order that only the government was the competent authority was set aside.
