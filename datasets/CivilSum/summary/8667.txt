Central Civil Services (CCA) Rules, 1965, Rule 3(1) - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Accusations of bias and prejudice aimed at delinquent - Punishment for charge of disobedience of superiors and higher authorities - Subsistence allowance reduced - Enquiry report not furnished with show-cause notice - Enquiry officer appointed to harass delinquent, and report not produced even in court despite directions - Order of removal from service overturned, and authorities instructed to reinstate petitioner with full back pay and benefits.
[Paras 24 to 27, 33 and 34]
