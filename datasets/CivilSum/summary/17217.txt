Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Authorisation - A petitioner serving as a clerk was authorized on a temporary stop-gap basis to discharge the functions of a Cadre Secretary - However, a regular Cadre Secretary was appointed, and the authorisation was recalled - The order of appointment made it clear that the authorisation would end with the appointment of the Cadre Secretary - The appointment does not necessarily mean through direct recruitment but can also happen by transfer or posting of a regular Cadre Secretary - Thus, the petitioner has no legal right to continue on the post.
[Paras 4 and 5]
