Constitution of India, Articles 16, 139(d) - Equal pay for equal work - Discrimination - Post of Gram Sahayak - Assignments related to post are capable of being executed with equal proficiency by both graduates and undergraduates - Allocation of different pay scales for this post based on level of educational attainment - Is discriminatory because it amounts to treating equals unequally.
