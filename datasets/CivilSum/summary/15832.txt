Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Abolition of post - Surplus - Absorption - Pay scale - Following the abolition of supervisor posts, certain employees were absorbed on pay scales of Rs. 1800-3200 while others were absorbed on pay scales of Rs. 2000-3500.
The petitioners assert that they should have been absorbed on higher-paying positions and their claim is valid.
Respondents contend that the petitioners were never promoted to supervisor positions and have since been relegated to Junior Field Investigator posts.
However, this argument has no merit and the petitioners should be considered for absorption on suitable posts that correspond to their qualifications and either pay scale of Rs. 2000-3500 or Rs. 1800-3200.
Petition granted.
