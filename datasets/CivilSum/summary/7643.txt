The Rajasthan Universities Teachers & Officers (Selection for Appointment) Amendment Act, 1974, Section 3(3), which allows for ad hoc appointments, potentially violates Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India when used for an extended period.
The Supreme Court has directed that individuals employed under such policies for long periods must be absorbed into service.
However, those appointed temporarily on an ad hoc basis typically have no right to the post or to be absorbed into service.
Rattanlal v. State of Haryana AIR 1987 SC 478 backs up these points.
[Paras 17 & 18]
The Rajasthan Universities Teachers & Officers (Selection for Appointment) Amendment Act, 2003, removed Sub-Section 3 of Section 3, which permitted ad hoc appointments.
However, this deletion of the rule does not affect the rights of employees who were already appointed under the Act of 1974.
[Paras 19 to 21]
