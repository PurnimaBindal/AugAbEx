Constitution of India, Article 226 - Medical Reimbursement - The petitioner incurred expenses for a Kidney transplant and claims entitlement to reimbursement of his medical bills as a chronic patient.
The State government had formulated a policy for reimbursement of medical bills for chronic patients from 192000.
However, the petitioner suffered from a disease that required constant treatment and care and it would not be right for the State to deny reimbursement of expenses incurred by the petitioner from 111998 to 3182000.
It was held that the petitioner was entitled to reimbursement of bills from 111998 onwards, with the policy decision dated 192000 given retrospective effect.
