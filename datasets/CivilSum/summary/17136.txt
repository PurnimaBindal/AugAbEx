Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Petitioners are seeking admission into grant-in-aid posts of Record Assistants - The College has denied their claim due to their work in the Stores Section, which it considers a business organization - However, a previous petition challenges the College's claim that it is not involved in the college's administration - The Court finds that the College's rejection of the Petitioners' claim is unlawful - The College runs the Stores, and uses the profits from book sales for the benefit of students - As such, the Stores is one of the College's departments for the students' benefit - The impugned order is the result of non-application of mind, and therefore set aside.
