Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985, Section 19 - Enquiry - Disciplinary action - Administrative Tribunals have the power to interfere with the orders of the punishing authority if the punishment order is based on an enquiry report that is contradictory, ignores material evidence, and has been found to be biased.
In such cases, the Tribunal can set aside both the punishment order and the enquiry report.
