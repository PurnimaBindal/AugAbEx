Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume II, Rules 22(c)(i) and 2.2.
(b) - In a case where a criminal case under Prevention of Corruption Act is registered against a petitioner, but challan is not presented, and the petitioner has retired from service before the presentation of challan, the gratuity of the petitioner cannot be withheld as criminal proceedings are only deemed to be instituted on the presentation of challan.
Therefore, no criminal proceedings on the date of retirement of the petitioner were pending and the mere registration of an F.I.R. against the petitioner would not be a valid reason for withholding the amount of gratuity.
The decision in 1991(3) SCT 317 (SC) is relied upon.
