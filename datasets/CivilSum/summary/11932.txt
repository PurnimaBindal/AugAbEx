Constitution of India, Article 16 - Cadre and Recruitment Regulations of Service Personnel of the University - Experience - Qualification - In order to qualify, candidates must have held an administrative position in the Govt. or Statutory Body for at least 5 years - This means that holding an administrative position for this length of time is essential - Even if a person performs administrative work, such as a Stenographer, for five years, they do not necessarily gain the same level of experience as someone who has held an administrative position for the same duration.
