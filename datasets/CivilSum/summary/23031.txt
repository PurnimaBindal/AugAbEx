Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Andhra Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation Employees (Pay and Allowances) Regulations, 1964, Regulation 13 - Absence from duty - Leave without pay - Extra-ordinary leave - Increments for non-duty period - Service Rules do not allow for increments during non-duty period of unauthorised absence - Unauthorised absence cannot be treated better than authorised leave without pay or extra-ordinary leave, which does not count towards service for increments - An employee who is punished with denied wages for unauthorised absence cannot claim increments for that period.
