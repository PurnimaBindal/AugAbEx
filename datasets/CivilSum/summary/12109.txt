Constitution of India, Article 16 - Appointment - Permanent employee - A petitioner was appointed as a lecturer on an ad hoc basis, and later applied for a part-time permanent post advertised.
The appointment was made by a duly constituted selection committee, and referred to in a memorandum as being for one year.
The petitioner fulfilled the duties of permanent appointees and therefore held entitled to the regular scale.
The petitioner has to be treated as a permanent employee.
[Paras 13 to 17]
