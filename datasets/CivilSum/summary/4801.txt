Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F - Termination of service - Workman - Labour Court rejected a claim of a workman due to delay in sending the demand notice after 3-1/2 years and failure to explain the delay - Even if a workman completes 240 days and there is non-compliance of Section 25F, he cannot be ordered to be reinstated, as per authority in Mehboob Deepak's case - Only compensation can be claimed - Procedural laws apply even to proceedings under the 1947 Act - A stale claim was rightly rejected.
