Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Recovery - Stoppage of benefits - The petitioner, a Constable in Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) was found ineligible for financial up-gradation resulting in the stoppage of benefits of the ACP scheme.
Directions were given for the recovery of already paid amounts.
The petitioner argued that he was not at fault for the wrongful grant of benefits, but the court held that he continued to enjoy the benefits that were wrongfully given to him.
Therefore, the petitioner is liable to refund the payment made to him wrongfully.
[Paras 5 to 7]
