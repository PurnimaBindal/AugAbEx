The inquiry officer conducted exparte proceedings despite orders from the competent authority that no inquiry was necessary, resulting in no evidence being presented to implicate or hold the delinquent responsible.
Therefore, an order of dismissal based on this report cannot be upheld and is without jurisdiction due to bias.
[Paras 19, 20 & 22]

Dismissing an employee from service based on a charge which was never presented to them in the charge-sheet is a violation of natural justice under Articles 14 and 311 of the Constitution of India.
