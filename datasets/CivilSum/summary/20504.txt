Madhya Pradesh Medical Education (Gazetted) Service Recruitment Rules, 1987, Rules 6(1)(b), 14(1) & (2) and 15 - Promotion to the post of Reader - Seniority - Promotion from one discipline to another - The promotion of lecturers to Readers must be from the same discipline; those in Prosthetics may be promoted as Reader in Prosthetics, while those in Periodontics may be promoted as Reader in Periodontia.
Oral Diagnosis lecturers may become Readers in Oral Diagnosis and Pedodontia lecturers may become Readers in Pedodontia.
Failure to consider the senior-most eligible candidate for promotion in a corresponding discipline infringes on their constitutional right to consideration as per Article 16 of the Constitution.
The Rules do not prohibit the appointment of two Readers from the same discipline in a Dental College.
Promotion must be based on merit and seniority, with a select list of eligible persons being prepared within the consideration zone.
The fifth post of Reader can be filled up by a lecturer of any discipline as per the Govt.'s instructions and practice being followed.
