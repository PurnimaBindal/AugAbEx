MP Civil Services (Pension) Rules of 1976, Sections 9(4) and 64 - Pension withheld without fixing provisional pension - If a departmental enquiry is not completed, pension benefits cannot be withheld for more than two years - Even though the enquiry has already been dropped, the employee is entitled to interest for delayed payment of their pension at a rate of 12%.
