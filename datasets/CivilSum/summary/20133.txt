Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Selection process - Arbitrariness - Cut-off date - Appointment to post of Extra Departmental Branch postmaster - The date of registration of the last candidate is considered the cut-off date for determining seniority - Names of 9 eligible candidates forwarded by employment exchange in order of seniority depending on the date of registration - First respondent registered later than the 9 candidates whose names were forwarded, therefore his name not forwarded - No arbitrariness.
