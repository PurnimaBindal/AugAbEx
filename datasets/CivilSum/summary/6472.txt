According to Section 3 of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1936, if a workman dies during the course of his employment, he is eligible for compensation.
However, in a case where a workman leaves the premises during duty hours and meets with an accident, and there is no evidence to suggest that the workman was sent out for any official duty when he met with the accident, then it cannot be said that he died in the course of his employment, as per Paras 7, 8, 9 and 13.
