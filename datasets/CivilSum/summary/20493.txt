The Indian Forest Service (Regulation of Seniority) Rules, 1968, Rules 3 and 9 dictate how seniority is determined and how officiating appointments are counted.
In order for an officer's officiating service to count towards seniority, two conditions must be met: the officer must receive an officiating appointment to a senior post and their name must be included in the select list.
Both of these conditions must continue to be met throughout the entire period of officiation.
However, the appellant's appointment was not to a post in the Service's cadre, but rather to the lower-paying post of OSD in the State Forest Service.
As a result, the requirement of Rule 9 was not fulfilled, and the non-inclusion of the appellant's name in the select list means that they are not eligible to benefit from officiating service under Rule 3(2)(c).
Therefore, the tribunal was justified in denying the appellants the benefit of such officiation.
