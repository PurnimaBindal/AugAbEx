Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay scales - Equating pay scales - Equal pay for equal work - If posts have been equated, there is no reason to stop the equation when revising pay scales - The duties and qualifications of Mortar Mate and Work Munshi are the same and interchangeable - Giving different pay scales during pay revisions is against Articles 14 to 16 of the Constitution.
