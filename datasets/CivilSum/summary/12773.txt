Constitution of India, Article 14 - Pension - Voluntary premature Retirement - Non-production of record - The petitioner was refused pension after 15 years on the grounds of leaving service without requesting for his premature retirement.
However, reference of his representation was found in the documents.
On direction of the Cout, even the record not produced by respondents, an adverse inference must be drawn against the respondent and the petitioner must be released with 6% interest on the arrears and costs of Rs. 15000/- must be paid by the person responsible for delay from the Government.
