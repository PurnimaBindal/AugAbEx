Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Date of Birth - Jurisdiction of the Industrial Tribunals - Correction of date of birth - Upon absorption of employees from a statutory body into another state body, the employees' dates of birth should be recorded in their service books according to medical examination instructions.
- In this case, the date of birth of the workman was recorded as 1932 instead of 1936 without notice or opportunity, leading to their service being terminated incorrectly on the wrong date of superannuation.
- The Labour Court/Tribunal has the jurisdiction to determine that the date of birth was wrongly recorded and hold the management accountable accordingly.
- Consequently, the management's petition was dismissed.
