The petitioner had worked as a peon/daftri from 1978 to 1997 before being promoted to Restorer.
During his tenure, he had consistently received a B+ rating except for two reports which were satisfactory.
However, his last four reports from 1998 to 2001 were consistently good.
He was only subjected to two minor penalties of stoppage of increment without cumulative effect and censure in his 25 years of service, which do not justify the order of retirement.
It would not be fair to dub the petitioner as a "piece of dead wood which ought to be checked out."
Thus, the court has issued directions to reinstate the petitioner with all consequent benefits.
