Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Removal - Natural Justice - Right of hearing - It is not permissible to pass an order of removal from service based solely on the report of an Enquiry Officer without providing the delinquent Officer an opportunity to be heard - The enquiry report was not shared with the delinquent Officer, and he was not given a chance to be heard before the final decision of the Disciplinary Authority - As a result, the order of removal is in violation of the principle of natural justice, and has been set aside.
