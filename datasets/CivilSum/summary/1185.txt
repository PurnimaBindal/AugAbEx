Constitution of India, Article 16 - Promotion - Candidates for top managerial posts must be selected based on seniority/seniority-cum-merit/seniority-sum-fitness/consideration of merit.
Seniority-cum-fitness should be used for lower positions involving more manual labor, while seniority-cum-merit should be used for higher/managerial posts requiring intellectual effort.
However, for promotion to the post of General Manager, merits were not considered and the selection was deemed bad.
[Paras 37 and 38]
