Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Termination - A Labour Court ruled that termination was illegal and ordered reinstatement with back wages up to 50% - Petitioner argued that the appointment was temporary and the workman had been working on his agricultural land, making him ineligible for reinstatement with back wages - The court held that the workman's work on his agricultural land did not mean he earned the same as his salary - The Labour Court's decision was upheld.
