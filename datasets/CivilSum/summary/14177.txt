Under Section 45(2) of the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, employers must maintain account books from the date of their coverage under the Act.
If an employer is found to have had illegal suppression of material regarding employment strength prior to coverage, the ESI Corporation can investigate and make the establishment coverable under the Act from the date it becomes eligible for coverage.

According to Section 85(g) of the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, failure to produce records prior to the date of coverage does not constitute a contravention under Section 85(g).
However, if there is additional independent material to show suppression of material, it could be an offence punishable under other provisions of Section 85 of the Act.
