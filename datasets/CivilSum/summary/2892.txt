Under Rule 16.2 of the Punjab Police Rules, 1934, absence from duty can be considered as the gravest misconduct and can lead to dismissal from police services only if this misconduct is proved to be the gravest with incorrigibility and complete unfitness for the job.
A single instance of absence can be regarded as a severe misconduct in the police service.
If a police employee has displayed a habit of remaining absent despite minor punishments time and again, it reveals his incorrigibility and complete unfitness for the police service.
In such situations, even if this employee has not completed 10 years qualifying service, the authority may award a lesser pension so as to preserve his retiral benefits.
