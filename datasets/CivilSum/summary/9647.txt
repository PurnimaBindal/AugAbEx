In WP(C) No. 3938/2004, Ms. Barkha Babbar and Col. T Prashad with Major SS Pandey represented Union of India.
Pension Regulations for the Army, Regulation 132 - Qualifying service - Pro-rata pension - The Fourth Pay Commission's recommendations merely involve continuing the current pension payment system where such a system exists - They do not propose a new system or a reduction of the qualifying service from 15 to 10 years - According to Regulation 132 of Pension Regulations for Army, as long as the qualifying period remains at 15 years and there is no provision for pro-rata pension, the petitioners cannot claim such payment - The petition was dismissed.
