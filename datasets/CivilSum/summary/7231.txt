Constitution of India, Articles 226, 341, 342 - Selection - HCS Judicial Branch - Reservation - Scheduled caste category - According to Rule, a member of a scheduled caste from one Union Territory is not eligible to benefit from being a member of a scheduled caste in another State.
This is why the petitioner is not entitled to reap the benefits of being an Ad-dharami of Chandigarh, even if the Ad-dharami caste is a scheduled caste in Haryana as well.
[Paras 8 and 11]  B Constitution of India, Articles 226, 341 and 342 - Reservation - The capital of Haryana being Chandigarh does not make Chandigarh a part of the state.
The petitioner, who is a resident of Chandigarh, cannot be considered a resident of Haryana.
