The policy of the government has changed regarding the benefit of higher grade of pay in Pay Scales.
Appellants who have not obtained B.T./B.Ed before 9390 will not be able to claim the benefit of higher grade of pay automatically.
Those who have earned B.T./B.Ed before 9390 are eligible for the benefit of para 2 of the Punjab Government's letter dated 23.7.1957, while those who have earned B.T./B.Ed later will be governed by the changed policy of the Haryana Government dated 931990.
