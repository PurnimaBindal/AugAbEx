Andhra Pradesh Charitable and Hindu Religious Institutions and Endowments Act, 1987, Sections 142 and 144 - Constitution of India, Articles 25 and 26 - The committee recommended pay scale rationalization and staff classification based on their status and qualifications in temples and religious endowments.
Govt. accepted the recommendations.
It was upheld that the Archakas, Potu workers, and other religious staff are entitled to payment of honorarium.
The appointment of Ekangies and Adhyapakas, determination of cadre strength, and grant of payscales and creation of posts are subject to prior approval of the Govt., and are upheld to maintain customs and usages.
Moreover, customary entitlements of Mirasidars are protected under Section 144 of the Act as they had prior to abolition of the Act.
It was also upheld that appointments by competitive recruitment maintaining customs and usage are warranted to protect the sanctity of religious rituals, and payment of honorarium proposed for posts like Shri Pedda Jeeyangar, Sri Chinna Jeeyangar are subject to revision by the TTD authorities yearly as accepted by the Govt.
