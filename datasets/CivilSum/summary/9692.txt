Workmen's Compensation Act (VIII of 1923), Section 3 - Death of workman who was sent to work on a specific ship but found dead on another - Dock Board responsible for compensation - The burden of proving death due to employment-related accidents is on the claimants and does not require direct evidence.
[Paras 18 and 20]
Workmen's Compensation Act (VIII of 1923), Section 3 - Death of workman arising from employment - The phrase "arising out of employment" applies to the employment as a whole, including its nature, conditions, obligations, and incidents - If any of these factors expose the workman to special danger, injuries arising from such danger are covered by the Act - Compensation claims will be successful unless the workman recklessly endangered himself.
