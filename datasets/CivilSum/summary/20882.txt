The respondents No. 1 to 4 were not included in the promotion list to Deputy Tehsildar due to the minor punishment of stoppage of increment imposed on them in a disciplinary inquiry.
However, the tribunal ordered that their case be considered without influence from the punishment.
This was deemed improper as the denial of promotion does not constitute penalty or double jeopardy.
The authorities have the right to consider past punishments when making promotion decisions.
Therefore, the direction given by the tribunal was overturned.
