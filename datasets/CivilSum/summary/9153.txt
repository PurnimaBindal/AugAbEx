Petitioner BP Srivastava, Advocate argues that the termination of services, after being appointed as daily wager for ten years and then on ad hoc basis for one year, without giving a hearing opportunity and for want of a vacancy, is a violation of Constitution of India, Article 311 - Termination/Natural justice.
According to the Petitioner, the termination is arbitrary, and the State must prove the absence of arbitrariness.
