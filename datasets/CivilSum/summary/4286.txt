Constitution of India, Article 14 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Indian Penal Code, Section 304-A - Rash driving - Disciplinary proceedings and criminal proceedings - Each case has to be decided on its peculiar facts - Even if the employee is acquitted in the criminal case, the departmental proceedings can be initiated against him and he may still be found guilty - The disciplinary proceedings and the criminal proceedings are grounded on the same facts - Disciplinary proceedings stayed till the decision of criminal case directing the respondents to co-operate and produce the witness before the criminal court without any delay.
[Paras 11 & 12]
