Constitution of India, Article 16 - Bengal (Rural) Primary Education Act, 1930 - Bengal (Rural) Primary Education Rules, Rule 3D - Primary Education Act, 1973, Section 105(4) - West Bengal Primary Education Rules, Rule 14 - Compassionate Appointment - Die-in-harnes - Extension in service - The petitioner's case for employment must be considered under the died-in-harness category, as during the extension period after completion of service, their father passed away while on duty.
The term extension simply means continuation of service and those working during the extended period shall receive all the benefits available to teachers under the age of 60.
