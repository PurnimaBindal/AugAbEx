The inter se order of merit of recommendees to the post of doctors for appointment to PCMS-I against direct quota has not been determined by the commission, and the state government fixed the same with the petitioner listed third.
The selection order was quashed by the civil court, which directed the state government to fix the inter se seniority only after hearing the parties.
However, while changing their inter se seniority, the state government has given no reason, making the order silent in regard to the basis on which inter-se seniority of doctors was fixed.
This order has been set aside as it's like an award of arbitrator containing no reason.
[Para 5]

Seniority is a matter that seriously affects the service prospects of a government servant.
The seniority of doctors for posts of PCMS-I against direct quota cannot be determined in an arbitrary manner.
The order passed by the government must contain reasons for any change and the process of reasoning that forms the basis of the changed seniority.
As a result, the order has been set aside and the State Government has been directed to pass a reasoned order fixing/refixing the inter se seniority of doctors.
