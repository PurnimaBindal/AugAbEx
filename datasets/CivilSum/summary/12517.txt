Army Pension Regulations, Regulation 16(a) allows the President of India to forfeit pension and other retiral benefits of officers who have been cashiered, dismissed, or removed from service after having put in the minimum period of service required.
The officer must be given an opportunity to show cause before the order can be passed, as per the law.
In this case, the petitioner did not present any explanation in response to the show-cause notice, and therefore, the court cannot interfere with the order of forfeiture.
[Para 4]

The petitioner filed a writ petition 14 years after the order of forfeiture of his pensionary benefits was passed on his dismissal from service.
The court finds the delay and laches of the petitioner and dismisses the petition.
