Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Punjab Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1952, Rules 7 and 8 - Natural justice - Employee accused of producing a forged certificate of date of birth at the time of appointment - Dismissal without proper investigation deemed a violation of natural justice - Punishment order overturned with instruction to conduct a thorough enquiry and allow petitioner full opportunity for defense.
