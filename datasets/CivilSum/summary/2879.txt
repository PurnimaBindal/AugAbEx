Punjab Civil Services Rules, Volume I Part I, Rules 75, 76 - Haryana Civil Services (Punishment and Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rule 4A - Suspension - Challenge as to - It is not necessary to issue a charge sheet before suspending an employee when disciplinary action is proposed or contemplated against the employee.
[Para 4]
Judicial review - Scope of - When challenging a suspension, the jurisdiction exercised is solely to review or correct errors of law or procedural defects that led to manifest injustice or violation of principles of natural justice, and it is not akin to that of Appellate Authority.
[Para 3]
