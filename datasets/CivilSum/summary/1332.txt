According to Rule 56 of the Fundamental Rules and Rule 2 of the UP Judicial Officers (Retirement on Superannuation) Rules, 1992, a Judicial Officer may be compulsorily retired in public interest before the age of 60 years, which does apply to judicial officers.
[Paras 17 and 19] B.
The petitioner, a Judicial Officer, was compulsorily retired based on the judgment of acquittal he gave in a murder case despite being transferred from that place and not working with the expected cautious approach that a Judicial Officer should exhibit.
It was deemed that public interest was involved and the order to retire was not incorrect.
