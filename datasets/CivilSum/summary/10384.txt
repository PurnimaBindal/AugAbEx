Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Quashment - Order of promotion - Found that no chance was given to petitioner before promoting respondent - It does not appear that any opportunity was given to the petitioners who have been previously promoted to justify why seniority should not be revised - The order granting retrospective promotions cannot be upheld without affording any opportunity to the petitioners before disturbing their seniority - The impugned order is quashed.
[Paras 13 & 14]
