Constitution of India, Article 311 - Bihar Service code, 1979, Rule 74(B)(i) and (ii) - Compulsory retirement - Natural Justice - The order for compulsory retirement must comply with principles of natural justice even if couched in innocuous language, and if challenged, the court can examine its basis regardless of its form.
The order in question was actually punitive in nature due to financial irregularities committed by the officer that caused financial loss, making it a violation of Article 311 and principles of natural justice.
