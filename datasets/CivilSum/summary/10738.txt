Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Compensation - A driver drove the truck and the claimant was injured - The claimant cannot drive due to permanent disability of one leg - This results in the claimant losing his entire earning capacity - The awarded compensation of Rs. 94,780/- is appropriate.
[Para 7] B. Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Penalty - The insurer who is under contractual obligation to indemnify the employer is not responsible for paying the penalty amount for the claim.
[Paras 8 and 10] C. Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923, Section 30 - Compensation - Penalty - The awarded compensation already includes interest from the date of application to the date of judgment - Granting interest on the total sum of compensation, penalty and interest from the date of application again is not valid - If the compensation money is not deposited within 30 days of judgment, interest will be paid from the date of judgment to the date of payment.
