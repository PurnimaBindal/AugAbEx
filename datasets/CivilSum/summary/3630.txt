Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regional Rural Banks Act, 1976, Sections 18 and 29 - Regional Rural Banks (Appointment and Promotion of Officers and Other Employees) Rules, 1988, Rule 4 - Dismissal - Misconduct - The dismissed petitioner had extended loans to borrowers and violated bank norms and practices by doing so - The petitioner was grossly negligent in discharging bank duties and all charges except one were proved in the enquiry - Although there was no misappropriation of bank money and the petitioner had no past adverse record, the dismissal was held to be a harsh penalty for the charge of gross negligence - The petitioner was reinstated but not entitled to back wages - Pay was to be fixed notionally as on the date of dismissal.
[Paras 4, 14 and 33]
