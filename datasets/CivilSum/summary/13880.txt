Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Recovery of overpayment of salary - Misrepresentation and fraud - In cases where an employee's pay was wrongly fixed by the authority without any misrepresentation or fraud on the part of the employee, the authority may correct the mistake at any time but cannot recover arrears of overpayment from the employee.
The recovered amount must be refunded.
[Paras 8 & 9]
