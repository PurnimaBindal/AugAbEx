The Andhra Pradesh Fundamental Rules, Rule 12 state that for deputation/repatriation procedures, the competent authority should issue an official order for repatriation to the deputationist's parent department and adequate time should be given for joining.
In absence of this order, there is no automatic repatriation and the deputationist cannot be considered as having been repatriated.
Likewise, even if a deputationist is suspended by the competent authority of their parent department, they are still entitled to join their previous post after the revocation of their suspension.
Mere suspension does not equal repatriation.
Lastly, the deputationist in question drew salary from the Board, including their deputation allowance, and cannot claim otherwise due to the principles of estoppel under Section 115 of the Evidence Act, 1872.
