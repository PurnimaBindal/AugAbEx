Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Madhya Pradesh Judicial Service (Classification, Recruitment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1955, Rule 24 - Probation - Confirmation - Deemed confirmation - Competent authority needs to specifically order confirmation of a probationer in service on satisfactory completion of probation period, or the service Rules themselves must restrict extension of probation period beyond a particular time period - Unless Rules explicitly state so, status of deemed confirmation should not be granted - Supreme Court in Dayaram Dayal's contradicts Seven Judges Bench Judgment in Sukhbans Singh and GS Ramaswamy and Akhar Ali Khan - Sub Rule (3) of Rule 24 can be used as a special provision to negate inference of deemed confirmation after probation period - Full Court of the High Court found Subordinate Judges unsuitable, recommending their termination but deferred their cases for re-consideration after giving further opportunity to improve - Continuation of probationers does not confer status of deemed confirmation just because maximum probation period has expired - Case law discussed.
