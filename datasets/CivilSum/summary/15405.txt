Jammu & Kashmir Constitution Section 124 - India Constitution Articles 15(4) and 16(4) - J&K Reservation Rules (SRO (126), 1994, Rule 7) - Residents of areas adjoining Line of Actual Control - Non-renewal of eligibility certificate does not make residents ineligible for reserved category - Certificate renewal's purpose is to avoid applications from those who have shifted, not to render permanent residents ineligible - Rules serve to benefit residents, not ban them - Denial of benefit due to non-renewal defeats purpose of rules.
