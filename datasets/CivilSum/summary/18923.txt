According to the Workman's Compensation Act of 1923, Section 12, the principal employer is liable for compensation when injuries occur during employment.
In this case, the appellant, who entrusted the work to the fifth respondent, is considered the principal employer while the fifth respondent is the immediate employer.
As such, the appellant is required to pay compensation for the deceased's injuries.
[Paras 8 and 9] B.
According to the Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923, Section 2(1)(n), and Schedule II (A1), a worker engaged on a contract basis is considered a workman.
In this case, the deceased falls under the definition of workman as he was employed to deepen an open well on a contract basis.
This is admitted by the appellant in the reply notice.
[Para 10] C. The Workmen's Compensation Act of 1923, Section 30, requires an appropriate multiplier to be used based on the deceased's age.
With the age of the deceased being 30 years, a multiplier of 30 is appropriate instead of 40.
As a result, the amount awarded is reduced from Rs. 29,949/- to Rs. 22,46180.
