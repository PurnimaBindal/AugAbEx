Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(2)(b) - Workman - Removal from service - Application for approval - Allegation of unauthorized absence from duty - Workman failed to show evidence of sanctioned leave - Industrial Tribunal and Single judge of High Court dismissed application and appeal for approval of removal - It is concluded that by remaining absent from duty workman committed misconduct, therefore Tribunal should have granted approval - However, in view of Full Bench judgment of this court in case of Jagdish Chander, workman will be deemed to be continuing in service till dismissal of writ petition and consequent wages.
