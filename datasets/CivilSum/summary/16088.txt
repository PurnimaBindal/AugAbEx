Regulations 185 and 185-A of the Pension Regulations Act of Army 1961, pertaining to disability pension, require regular medical checkups to ascertain improvement when disability is capable of improvement.
Regulation 185-A requires an award of disability pension that is proportionate to the degree of disability.
In this case, the Medical Board has assessed the petitioner's disability to be less than 20%, which is insufficient to grant pension.
Therefore, discontinuing the temporary disability pension is justified under the rules.
[Para 5]
Regulation 185-A of the Pension Regulations Act of Army 1961 is not in violation of the Constitution of India, as per Articles 226/227.
[Para 6]
Although the petitioner left the Army at a young age and may have had insufficient funds for survival, this is not adequate grounds to grant disability pension when the Medical Board has assessed the petitioner to have less than 20% disability.
As per the regulations, the petitioner ceased to be entitled to the pension.
Therefore, the petition is dismissed under Articles 226/227 of the Indian Constitution.
