The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Sections 25-F, 25-G, and 25-H deal with termination of services.
The court has found that there is no evidence on record to support the Tribunal's finding that the workman completed 240 days of continuous service.
Additionally, the plea that his juniors are retained in service cannot be entertained without a finding on the matter by courts below.
The Tribunal's decision to hold that the termination of services of the workman is illegal and that he is entitled to be reinstated with continuity of service and backwages is incorrect.
