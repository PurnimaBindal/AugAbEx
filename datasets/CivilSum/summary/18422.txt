Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, 1957, Section 90 - Pay scale and Promotion - Petitioners who hold Class IV posts under MCD and have performed duties of toll tax collectors, a Class III post, are claiming promotion - They have all fulfilled the necessary qualifications for a higher post, with the recruitment rules providing for a 10% quota for promotion as LDC - It is held that all Class IV employees with necessary qualifications are eligible for promotion according to recruitment regulations - However, the eligibility for promotion does not automatically equate to regularization as LDCs - The petition is dismissed.
