Constitution of India, Article 14 - Medical Council Act, 1956, Section 33 - MP Rules for Selection and Appointment as House Officers in Medical Colleges, 1984, Rules 3 and 4 - Reservation - Appointment as House Job Officer in Medical College - Rules allowing cent per cent reservation in favor of institutional candidates - No discernible basis for classification through wholesale reservation - Rules considered discriminatory - Unless a minimum of 25 percent of the seats are reserved for outsiders, the rules cannot be allowed to continue.
AIR 1971 SC 1762 and AIR 1984 SC 1420 considered.
