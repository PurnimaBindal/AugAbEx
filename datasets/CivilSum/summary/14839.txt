The Constitution of India, Article 19(1)(c) and (4) specifies the formation of Students Union.
However, students do not possess any fundamental right under Article 19(1)(c) to compel Universities to provide for elections and in turn establish student union.
It should be the responsibility of the appropriate bodies to determine the worth of holding such elections.
