Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - UCO Bank (Employees) Pension Regulations, 1995, Regulation 22(1) - Estoppel - Employees Gratuity Fund Rules, Rule 10 - Removal from service on account of misconduct - The punishing authority has discretion regarding forfeiture of retiral benefits - If the punishing authority decides that the delinquent should receive his retiral benefits while being removed from service and the employer does not contest this decision, the employer cannot oppose implementation of the order in the employee's claim petition.
