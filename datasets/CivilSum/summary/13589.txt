The termination of the respondent, who was employed by Surat Municipal Corporation under the Bombay Provincial Corporation Act, 1949 and was not a government servant, is not protected by Article 311 of the Constitution However, if the termination is by way of punishment, Section 56 of the Act requires a reasonable opportunity for the official to be heard before imposing the punishment.
The single judge made the correct decision in concluding that the principles of natural justice should have been followed and the order of termination was quashed.
