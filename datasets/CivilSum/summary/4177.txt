Sections 10 and 11 of the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971 - Disobedience of court order - The petitioner claimed pension and it was rejected due to the cut off date established for such retiral benefits, based on the date of implementation of revised pay scales - This date was not determined arbitrarily and was not mala fide to disobey court orders - The definition of civil contempt includes wilful disobedience of the court's orders - Only wilful disobedience will amount to civil contempt, not any errors made in following the judgment by the contemner.
