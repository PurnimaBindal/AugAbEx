Constitution of India, Article 21 - Fast Track Courts - Supreme Court directed Union of India to continue the Scheme for one month and utilise unspent funds of previous Scheme - Accused entitled to speedy justice - Speedy justice is State's obligation and cannot be denied on financial grounds - Maintenance of Fast Track Courts is the States' responsibility and they must provide funds - Scheme must not be disbanded and States should continue and make necessary arrangements.
[Paras 6, 8, 9 and 10].
