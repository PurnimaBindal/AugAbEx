J&K Civil Service (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1956, Rule 14 - Regularisation - The petitioners were appointed as Medical Assistants on a stop-gap basis for 30-day periods.
These appointments were made by the Chief Medical Officer who was not authorized to make such appointments and are therefore void ab initio, providing no rights to the petitioners.
The petitioners cannot be granted regularisation.
[Para 2]
J&K Subordinate Service Recruitment Rules, 1992 - Appointment - The petitioners challenged the appointment of respondents No. 6 to 10, claiming that the interview process was a sham.
However, the Service Selection Board duly selected respondents No. 6 to 10.
The petitioner appeared for the interview but was not successful, and failed to include the Service Selection Board or any member of the interview committee as a party.
As a result, the petitioner cannot challenge the appointment of respondents No. 6 to 10.
[Paras 3, 4, and 6]
