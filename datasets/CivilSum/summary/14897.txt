As created under the 1961 Act, Panjabi University is a statutory autonomous body that falls under the definition of "State" in Art. 12 of the Constitution of India.
Therefore, any instructions of the State Government are not binding on the University and the University had granted Special Allowance to its Senior Desk Assistants through a resolution of its syndicate.
The Government has no jurisdiction to interfere with its affairs.
A subsequent resolution passed by the syndicate to withdraw the special allowance based on Government instructions is illegal and an abdication of its authority to the Govt.
The resolution is unsustainable and the petitioners are entitled to special allowance as per the earlier resolution.
