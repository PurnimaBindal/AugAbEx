Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Punjab Govt. National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965 - Military service benefit during second emergency - Benefits of annual increments granted to petitioners for military services rendered during second emergency revoked by respondents - Petitioners not considered entitled to the second emergency benefit - Order granting benefits passed without any fraud or misrepresentation by petitioners - Order for recovery of funds already paid to petitioners is quashed.
