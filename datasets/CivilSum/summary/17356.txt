The petitioner, who was seeking a government job, had their application for a "Resident of Backward Area" certificate rejected by the Tehsildar due to their family income.
However, the court held that the income of the petitioner's brother could not be considered while determining the family income, which only includes the petitioner and their father.
As a result, the decisions of the Tehsildar and Deputy Commissioner were overturned.
