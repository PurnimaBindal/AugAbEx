Constitution of India, Article 14 - Departmental proceedings - The writ Court cannot review the sufficiency or insufficiency of evidence in domestic enquiries - Strict rules of procedure and Evidence Act do not apply to such proceedings - The Court will not interfere with the punishing authority's decision if some relevant evidence has been presented - Conductor found guilty of pocketing fare money - Inspector's statement used as evidence, but passengers not examined - Failure to examine passengers does not invalidate proceedings.
[Para 9] B Constitution of India, Article 14 - Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - Res judicata - If a writ petition has been dismissed, even with one word "Dismissed", it will function as res judicata in subsequent proceedings before the Labour Court.
