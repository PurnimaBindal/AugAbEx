Letters Patent Appeal, Clause X - Appointment on additional posts - Sustainability - The appellants were appointed on posts that became available due to retirement, promotion or death of incumbents, in addition to the advertised posts.
The number of posts could vary as per the advertisement and there is no evidence to suggest that the selection process was tainted by mala fide or fraud.
The respondents are not among the meritorious candidates who would be benefited.
The Single Judge's direction to quash all appointments is nullified since the appellants have been working continuously for almost a decade.
[Paras 11, 13, 14, 16, 17]
