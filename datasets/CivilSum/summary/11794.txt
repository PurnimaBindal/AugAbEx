Constitution of India, Article 16(4) - Reservation in admissions - Admission/Education - Admission to Elementary Teachers Training (ETT) and JBT 1996-98 in Punjab by competitive entrance examination - Admissions must follow the quota of 50:50 for boys and girls - The Admission Brochure has the force of law and the 50% quota must not be exceeded for any category, including reserve and general - If candidates from reserved categories are not available, seats may be filled by general category candidates - The 50:50 quota must be maintained, and even fractions of seats may not be filled in violation of the quota.
[Para 4] B.
Admission - Education - Admission made in excess of the 50% quota in violation of admission brochure - Students in 1st semester and 2nd semester may not have their admission canceled at this stage - However, those denied admission due to the fault of the respondents may not be deprived of their right - 2 special seats will be created for them, they will receive special coaching for the 1st semester and will be allowed to attend classes for the 2nd semester - The request to sit for 2nd semester examination without studying 1st semester has been declined.
