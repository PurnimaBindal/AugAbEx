Constitution of India, Articles 14 & 16 - Central Government Policy for ex gratia dt. 22.9.1998, Category (c) - Ex gratia amount - When a deceased falls under Category (c) and not Categories (a) & (b) of the policy, the family of the deceased is entitled to an ex gratia amount of Rs. 75 lacs - It has been previously decided in CWP No. 18469 of 2001, Dr. SS Bali v. Union of India, whether or not the case of the deceased falls under category (c).
