Bank of India Officer Employees' (Discipline and Appeal) Regulations, 1976, Regulation 19 - Voluntary retirement - Notice of voluntary retirement - The permission of competent authority must be sought for notice of voluntary retirement to be effective.
It was deemed not acceptable to interpret the word "May" in the regulation as "Shall."
