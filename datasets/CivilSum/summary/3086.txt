The Punjab National Bank (Officers) Service Regulations, 1979, Regulation 17 outlines the criteria for promotion from Scale IV to Scale V Officers.
The criteria includes an assessment of job and general knowledge, personal characteristics, potential for higher responsibilities, and performance.
An interview is conducted to evaluate these factors as well as performance over the preceding two years.
Eligibility requires three years of satisfactory service as Sr.
Manager Grade Scale IV and different marks are assigned for Very Good, Good, and Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory service records.
The promotion policy is not arbitrary and the two years of performance are relevant in determining merit.
[Paras 18 and 19]

The Directors of Promotion Committee may ratify promotions without the availability of records before them.
While hearing an appeal or revision, it is not necessary to examine the records or hear the aggrieved party, but the authority may do so if necessary.
[Paras 23, 24 and 27]
