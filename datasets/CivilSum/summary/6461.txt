The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, Rules 24 & 41(1) - Pension after dismissal from service - Dismissal from service results in the forfeiture of pension and gratuity - The pensionary rights of the person are governed by Rule 24 in conjunction with Rule 41 once the dismissal order is final.
[Paras 5 and 6]

The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, Rules 24 &41(1)(i) read with Rules 28 and 32 - Pension after dismissal from service - Dismissal from service results in the forfeiture of entire pension and gratuity, except if the punishing authority decides to grant compassionate allowance - Once the dismissal order becomes final, it cannot be brought up again in subsequent claims for pension - Rules 28 and 32 do not apply if the person was dismissed through disciplinary proceedings.
[Paras 8 and 9]
