Constitution of India, Article 226 - Seniority - Petitioner requests a proper seniority position in the Cadre of Associate Professor - Both parties were interviewed, recommended, and joined together - Appointment letter contains a clause requiring submission of a medical certificate and compliance - Respondent submitted joining report before Petitioner after medical examination, and has an earlier date of birth - Petitioner relies on a circular for seniority determination, but respondent has its own rules and guidelines - The seniority position has been validly fixed and requires no interference, and the petition is dismissed.
