Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Reversion - Sustainability - The ground for reversion was that the post belonged to a reserved category and the record and roster register were not produced.
It was contended that another vacancy occurred on promotion of an officer, which was not rebutted.
The court held that under the circumstances, the petitioner should have been promoted to the vacancy which became available, and hence the petitioner would be deemed to be holding the post with all consequential benefits from the date of joining.
[Paras 7, 8]

Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 - Reversion - Principles of natural justice - Compliance of - The representation of the respondent was accepted at the back of the petitioner.
The court held that once promotion was granted, it could not be snatched away without giving an opportunity of hearing as it would result in a reduction of salary, status and involve civil consequences.
Hence, the order was set aside.
[Para 7]
