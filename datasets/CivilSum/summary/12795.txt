As per the Constitution of India, Article 226 and the Administrative Tribunals Act of 1985, Section 14(3), a Central Government employee who retired from the National Institute of Sports is entitled to pension computed based on their service in NIS.
However, a petitioner in 1984 filed a writ petition to claim the benefit of their service in Pepsu State Service towards their qualifying service for computing their pension.
The petition was transferred to the Central Administrative Tribunal after the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 came into operation, barring the jurisdiction of the High Court.
