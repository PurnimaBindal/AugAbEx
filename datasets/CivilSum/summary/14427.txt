According to the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25(O), the Government must determine if an establishment seeking permission for closure is genuinely unable to function in current conditions.
Even if the establishment can secure loans, a request cannot be refused if the business is suffering losses due to a lack of buyers or orders.
In one case, the Government's denial of closure was deemed illegal and arbitrary as the establishment had been suffering significant losses and a decline in orders.
The Government cannot act as an advisory body and force the business to continue to suffer further losses.
