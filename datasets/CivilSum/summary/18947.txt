Constitution of J&K, Section 126 - Reservation Rules, 1994, Rule 11 - Appointment - Reservation - Posts of Agriculture Extension Officer Agriculture Assistants - A petitioner, who was the son of an ex-serviceman, was seeking a direction to be considered under the category of ex-serviceman - The children of defense personnel are entitled to concessions only while their parent is in service, after retirement, they alone are entitled to such concessions as ex-servicemen - Therefore, the petitioner was rightly not considered for the post.
