Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - National Council for Teacher Education ('NCTE'), 1993, Section 14(3)(a) - Affiliation of colleges - Once a recognition has been granted by NCTE, the University is obligated to grant affiliation of such an institution.

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Admission - Common Entrance Test - Single window procedure can be used by private institutions in absence of any regulatory measure - State must either provide a procedure for holding single entrance test or allow private institutions to hold entrance test for admissions subject to merit, non-commercialisation, and transparency - A schedule specifying the duration of the course, date of commencement of course and last date of admission must be provided - In absence of such schedule, State must conduct entrance test without wasting the infrastructure of a recognised institution.
