The policy dated 331980 for compassionate appointment of work charge employees requires completion of 5 years of work charge service before death.
However, the period of daily wage service is not counted, and a deceased employee who did not complete 5 years of work charge service is not eligible.
Moreover, policy dated 24.4.1986, specifically for riot victims, is not general.

A delay in providing compassionate appointment after the death of the petitioner's husband resulted in her filing a writ in 1993, which was finally heard in May 2002.
The compassionate appointment is intended to provide financial relief to families impacted by untimely death.
Since the petitioner survived for 12 years during the litigation process, there is no need to provide compassionate appointment as a bounty.
