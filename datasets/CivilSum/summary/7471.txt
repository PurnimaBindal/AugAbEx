Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Recovery - Natural justice - If an employee receives a benefit wrongly through no fault of their own, it would not be fair to recover the benefit from them - Doing so would violate principles of natural justice.
[Paras 20 to 22]

Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Selection grade - Ad hoc service - In determining an employee's selection grade, their ad hoc service from the date of their initial appointment must be taken into account.
