According to Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, termination can be referred to the Labour Court, who may decline such a reference.
However, the High Court may reverse the findings of the Labour Court in a writ petition if there is a patent error of facts or if the writ petition is directly against the order of the Labour Court.
The jurisdiction of the writ court differs from those routed through various courts or authorities, and findings of fact may be interfered with under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
In this case, the finding of facts was properly reversed.
[Para 6] B.
In cases of termination under Section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act of 1947, back wages may be awarded by a single Judge.
However, if there has been a delay of five years in issuing a demand notice, the amount awarded may be reduced from 75% to 50%.
