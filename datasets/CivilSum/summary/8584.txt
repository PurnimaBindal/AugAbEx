Industrial Disputes Act, Section 10 - The Labour Court alone has the authority to determine whether an inquiry was fair and if the termination order issued to the petitioner was valid or not, and the government is not permitted to interfere in the dispute's merits.
[Para 9] Relevant cases referenced are: 1.
The M.P. Irrigation Karamchari Sangh v. State of M.P. and another, A.I.R. 1985 SC 860.
2.
Workmen of Syndicate Bank, Madras v. Govt. of India and another, A.I.R 1985 SC 1667.
3.
Telco Convoy Drivers Mazdoor Sangh and another v. State of Bihar and others, A.I.R. 1989 SC 1565.
4.
The Rajasthan State Road Transport Corporation and another etc. etc. v. Krishna Kant etc. etc., A.I.R 1995 Supreme Court 1715.
