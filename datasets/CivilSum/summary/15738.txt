As per Article 16 of the Constitution of India, a petitioner was selected for the position of Constable in 1988.
A report on the petitioner's character was requested and it was found that they were involved in a criminal case.
However, the appointing authority received a letter from the District Magistrate stating that there were no legal obstacles to appointing the petitioner per the Government order.
Despite this, no action has been taken yet.
The respondents have been directed to start taking action immediately.
