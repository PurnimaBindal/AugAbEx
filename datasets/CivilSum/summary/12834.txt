According to the Haryana Affiliated Colleges (Security of Service) Act, 1979 and Article 311 of the Constitution of India, termination of a teacher's service requires prior approval of the Director.
The petitioner's service was terminated by the Governing Body of College without such approval, but the error was rectified by a subsequent order after approval was obtained.
The petitioner's claim that the initial termination order was invalid was dismissed.
