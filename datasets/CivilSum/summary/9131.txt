Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 33(C)(2) - Industrial Disputes Rules, 1985, Rule 20-D - Industrial dispute - Additional issues - The principle of framing additional issues, as provided under Civil Procedure Code, can be applied in the adjudication of a matter before the Tribunal or Labour Court.
If there is no reference by the appropriate government, the Labour Court must consider objections and/or additional objections filed by the petitioner-company in making a just decision for the company.
[Paras 7 & 8].
