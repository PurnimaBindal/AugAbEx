The transfer order of an employee was cancelled after 27 years of stay at the same place, even though the order was made in a routine manner along with others.
The authority did not provide any reason as to why the routine transfer order was cancelled and there was no evidence to show that the petitioner was indispensable at their previous posting.
The court quashed the order of cancellation of transfer, citing Director Rajya Krishi v. Sri Nathi Lal (FB) as precedent.
[Paras 13, 15, 16 & 17]

An order of transfer was made in a routine manner but was later cancelled without any valid reason.
The court determined that the cancellation order was not valid, as there was no evidence presented to justify the exception to the rule of transfer.
The court stated that there should have been reasons justifying the exception.
[Paras 7 and 10]
