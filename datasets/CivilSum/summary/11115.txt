Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Embezzlement - Suspension - Enquiry proceedings - Termination - The petitioner was issued a notice by the Enquiry Officer, which he did not attend, and did not respond to a second show cause notice from the disciplinary authority.
The disciplinary authority even attempted to grant a personal hearing, but the petitioner did not appear.
Due to the enquiry report, the disciplinary authority terminated the petitioner's services, and the order should not be invalidated merely on the grounds of not supplying the report to the delinquent official.
The petition was dismissed.
[Paras 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8]
