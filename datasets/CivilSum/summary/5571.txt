Amicus Curiae: Mr. Annupam Gupta, Advocate.
According to the Constitution of India, Articles 14,16, and 41, reservation in admissions for education is recognized on the basis of territorial reservation.
Reservation in professional courses based on the residence/domicile of the state running the institutions is also recognized.
However, it cannot compromise merit by making a total reservation.
At least 15% of seats must be kept open to be filled by open merit.
The Information Brochure issued by the Director Technical Education Haryana for admission to various institutions in the State of Haryana in various professional diploma courses including Institute of Hotel Management, Catering, and Nutrition at Panipat has been quashed for making reservations beyond the 85% limit.
