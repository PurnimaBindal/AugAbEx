Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Widow and son of deceased challenge termination order - Son claims compassionate appointment - 6-year delay in filing writ petition deemed misconceived - Petition challenging dismissal order should have been filed during deceased's lifetime - Legal Representatives could have pursued if deceased died during writ petition.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate appointment claim by son 6 years after termination - Criteria - Deceased was not earning anything for over six years - Family members were not dependent on income of deceased - Appointment on compassionate basis not a right - Its purpose is to enable deceased's family to sustain during financial crisis arising from employee's sudden death.
