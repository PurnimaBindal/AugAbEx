Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compulsory retirement - Government's decision to retire petitioner at age 55 based on service record and policy of having 70% or more good or above entries is subjective and court cannot interfere unless decision is arbitrary or contrary to record.

Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compulsory retirement - Uncommunicated adverse remarks can be considered by Government while making decision based on entire service record of employee.
Court cannot act as court of appeal over administrative authorities' decision.
[Para 5]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compulsory retirement - Adverse entries in confidential reports not nullified by employee being permitted to cross-efficiency bar.
[Para 6]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compulsory retirement - Promotion after adverse entries were made is not sufficient ground to argue against compulsory retirement.
