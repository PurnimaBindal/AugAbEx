Constitution of India, Article 311 - Departmental enquiry/Preliminary enquiry report - The Enquiry Officer based their conclusions not only on their exhibited report, but also relied on it.
Since the accused were not provided with a copy of the preliminary enquiry report, their defence is clearly at a disadvantage.
Additionally, the Enquiry Officer gathered evidence by visiting the scene, without involving the accused officers.
This evidence cannot be used in the enquiry without being presented to the accused first.
Such a course of action violates the principles of natural justice.
