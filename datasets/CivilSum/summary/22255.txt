Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Extension in service - Natural justice - To grant an extension in service, a reviewing committee recommended by the competent authority must be consulted - It would be unfair for a member of the reviewing committee to issue an order or refusal who has been accused of misconduct by the employee concerned - Such an order would violate the rules and guidelines outlined for granting an extension in service, and would be passed by someone who is not authorized to do so - The order was thus quashed and remitted to the competent authority for reconsideration.
