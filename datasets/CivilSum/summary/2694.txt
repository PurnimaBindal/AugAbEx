Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Claim of Special Family Pension - Employee died in accident while going for duty in Delhi - Objection as to jurisdiction - The Chief of Army Staff may be sued anywhere in the country - In this case, the permanent residence of the petitioner being in the State of Haryana, Punjab and Haryana High Court has jurisdiction to resolve the controversy.
[Para 5] B Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Claim of Special Family Pension - Claim rejected on ground that death is not attributable to or aggravated by Air Force Service - In a similar case, the First Appellate Committee found cause of death as attributable to Air Force Service and awarded Special Family Pension - The respondents are directed to consider cause of death of petitioner's husband as attributable to Air Force Service and award special family pension to petitioner within two months and if failed, the petitioner is entitled to receive interest @ 9% per annum.
