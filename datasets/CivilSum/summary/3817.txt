Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1980, Rule 55(1) - Disciplinary enquiry - Charge of embezzlement - The charge is supported by documentary evidence, and the delinquent official does not deny the genuineness of the documents- The enquiry is not flawed by the lack of oral evidence brought by the employer, since Rule 55(1) does not require oral evidence to establish the charge.
[Paras 9, 10, 11 and 22] B Dismissal - An official faced dismissal since charges relating to embezzlement were raised, along with other charges such as disobedience of orders, failing to hand over charge of office after transfer, making alterations to government records, and not making entries of rent receipts in relevant registers- The dismissal order was upheld.
