Pension Regulations for Army, 1961 - Disability Pension - Claim of - Petitioner invalided out of service on medical grounds - Diagnosis of Schizophrenia - CDA (P) denied claim as disability not attributable to or aggravated by army service - No evidence of pre-existing ailment at time of entry into service - Determined that Schizophrenia was a result of stress and strain from army service and therefore attributable to and aggravated by service - Petition granted.
