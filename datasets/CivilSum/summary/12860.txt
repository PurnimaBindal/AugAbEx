Punjab Civil Services (Premature Retirement) Rules, 1975, Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol. 1, Rule 3.36(a) and (d) - Premature retirement/ Adverse reports/ Average reports/ Uncommunicated reports - A Head Master was prematurely retired on the basis of uncommunicated adverse remarks or conveyed at a belated stage despite having 70% good reports - The record of the last five years of service was taken into consideration, where no adverse reports were conveyed to the petitioner - Average and uncommunicated reports are not reliable.
