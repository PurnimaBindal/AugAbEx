The Petitioner challenged the selection for the MD/MS Course after the Government changed allocations of marks for "Rural service."
The Petitioner was later admitted due to a provisional order but had already completed the course.
The Respondents failed to file a reply or vacate the order.
It was decided that it was fair and proper to regularize the Petitioner's admission given the totality of facts and circumstances.
