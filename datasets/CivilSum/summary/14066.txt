Central Civil Services (Fixation of Pay of Re-employed Pensioners) Orders, 1986, Clause 4 - Re-employment - Pay protection - The scale of pay for re-employed persons prior to retirement is not protected, only the total emoluments are protected in case of any shortfall and the pay has to be fixed at the same stage as the last pay drawn before retirement.
For those retiring before 55 years of age, their total pension is not ignored but only to the extent provided in Sub-clause (d), and their pay is fixed as per Sub-clause (b)(ii).
