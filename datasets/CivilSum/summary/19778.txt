The decision of DPC can transfer a petitioner under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226/Transfer/Competent - Authority, but a transfer by the Managing Committee of the Bank is illegal and has been stayed.
[Para 9]
Under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311, a petitioner working as a Bank Officer in Grade V cannot be arbitrarily transferred to a post carrying Grade IV, and the transfer has been stayed.
[Para 9]
Rules of the Bank state that a Commissioner of the Central Vigilance Commission can hold an enquiry against an officer of the Bank under the Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311/Competent Authority/Enquiry Officer/ Departmental Enquiry.
The Chief Vigilance Officer is not competent to hold an enquiry, so the Bank has been directed to ask the Vigilance Commission to hold an enquiry through one of its Commissioners.
