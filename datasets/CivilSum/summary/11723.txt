Punjab Courts Act, Section 41 - Punishment - Dismissal - The petitioner was charged with charging late fees instead of the publication fee of Rs 20/- required for renewal of permit.
However, the charge was not deemed serious and the charge-sheet did not indicate any loss caused to the State.
Additionally, a similar case with the same charges resulted in the appellant authority revoking the order of removal from service and imposing punishment of reduction in pay scale.
The penalty imposed in the present case was found to be disproportionate to the gravity of the charges and the decree and judgment of the courts below were set aside.
The respondent was ordered to pass a fresh order of punishment within 30 days based on the records.
