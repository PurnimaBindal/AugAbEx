Constitution of India, Articles 226 and 227 - Swatantrata Sainani Samman Pension Scheme - The petitioner sought Freedom Fighters Pension due to his one-year sentence under Section 37 of the Hyderabad Defence Act after participating in the Hyderabad Freedom Struggle.
Although his request initially declined for a lack of jail certificate, it was ultimately approved for pension grant based on the petitioner's evidence and absence of opposing evidence.
