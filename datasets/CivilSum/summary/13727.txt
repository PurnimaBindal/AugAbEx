Constitution of India, 1950, Articles 14 and 6 - Grant of Selection Grade - Senior Scale - Revision of payscale - The petitioner, who served as Librarian in a privately added college, is entitled to senior scale and selection grade as per the Punjab Government's notification, which was adopted by the institution.
Throughout his service career, the petitioner was treated in the same manner as other Librarians posted in the Colleges of Punjab and Public Libraries.
There is no justification for the institution to treat the petitioner differently at the time of granting benefits after the revision of the payscale.
The writ petition has been allowed.
