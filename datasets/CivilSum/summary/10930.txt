Constitution of India, Article 226 - Compassionate Appointment - Despite being included on the list of employees to be regularised, the petitioner's husband, who had been employed as a badli worker since 1982, died before receiving the benefits of regularisation - The petitioner's claim for compassionate appointment was opposed solely on the grounds that her husband had not been regularised prior to his passing - Policies adopted by the State must result in tangible benefits to those for whom they were enacted - Technicalities cannot be allowed to defeat welfare policies - Respondents instructed to review petitioner's compassionate appointment application.
