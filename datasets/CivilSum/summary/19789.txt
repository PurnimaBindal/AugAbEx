According to Fundamental Rules, Rule 56(a), (b), (e), workmen/Artisans/Age of retirement, the petitioner who was appointed as chowkidar in Municipal Corporation Delhi after 1969 and later designated as a security guard, has no right to claim retirement at 60 years of age.
The Rules dictate workmen appointed after 15969 to retire at 58 years of age.
The petition was dismissed.
