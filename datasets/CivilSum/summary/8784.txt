Principles of natural justice - Violation of - A grant of leave was sanctioned for the plaintiff, but subsequent applications were rejected by the Department - The Department terminated the plaintiff's services by issuing an order of discharge without issuing a show cause notice or holding a regular inquiry - Violation of principles of natural justice - The impugned order is set aside.
[Para 11]
Discharge order/Termination - Absence of inquiry - Plaintiff was a regular employee, services could not be dispensed with by passing a simple order of discharge - Impugned order quashed.
[Para 12]
Back wages - Termination order illegal, void and against principles of natural justice, set aside - Full back wages not awarded to the appellant - He is only entitled to back wages for 38 months which will be worked out on the basis of his last pay drawn in the department.
