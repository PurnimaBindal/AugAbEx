The petitioner, who is senior to the private respondent, was not promoted.
However, it is undisputed that the petitioner was drawing a higher salary.
The trial court found that the petitioner was senior and receiving a higher salary, but was not considered for promotion and was unjustly bypassed by juniors.
This is a violation of Article 14 of the Constitution of India.

The appellant argued that a writ cannot be filed against a company.
However, no material was produced to support this contention.
As a result, the appellants cannot make such an argument at this time.
Merely raising a dispute without providing any supporting evidence does not remove the matter from the purview of the writ jurisdiction.
