According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, equal pay must be given for equal work.
Any appointments that are made illegally, such as those without advertising vacancies or without calling candidates through employment exchanges, are in violation of these articles.
Those appointed in such a way, including contractual employees and daily wagers, cannot receive a writ of mandamus to fix their pay in the regular scale based on the principle of equal pay for equal work, even if they are working in a vacant post.
