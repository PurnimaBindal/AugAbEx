According to the Constitution of India and the Apprentices Act of 1961, apprentices are given preference, including exemption from tests and interviews, but they are not exempt from these for regular appointments.
However, if things are equal, apprentices are entitled to the benefits of preference, including sponsorship from employment exchange, age relaxation, and priority based on seniority in training.
This is laid down in Transport Corporation v. Parivahan Nigam Shikshak Berozgar Sangh, as per entries (i) to (iv).
