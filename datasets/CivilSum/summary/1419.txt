The pension available to retired employees is considered a property under the Constitution of India's Articles 19 and 31.
Thus any action that deprives a person of their pension right can be challenged in writ jurisdiction.
[Paras 7 and 9]
The principle of no work no pay does not apply when dismissed employees are reinstated with various conditions after 15 years without back wages.
The petitioners cannot be denied back wages as no speaking order was passed in regard to the Principle of no work no pay.
[Paras 10 and 12]
If the pay and allowances of the petitioners are not considered properly, the court can re-examine the record and the orders.
[Paras 11 and 12]
The petitioners may have claimed interest at the rate of 18% on arrears of salary.
Still, the grant of 18% interest would be harsh, so the court has only permitted 12% interest.
