The petitioner was granted family pension starting on January 1, 1990, but it was discontinued in an August 7, 1991 order on the grounds that the petitioner was the second wife of the late employee.
The petitioner's claim of restoration with arrears rests on the fact that she is actually the legally wedded wife of the late employee, which was established in an enquiry.
The pension certificate also clearly indicates that the petitioner was receiving family pension at the time of her husband's death.
Therefore, there is no justification for discontinuing the pension, which has not been revoked or withdrawn and for which no show-cause notice has been issued.
The respondents are directed to restore the family pension from three years preceding the date of filing the petition.
