Article 226 of the Constitution of India and Section 17-B of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, cover the scenario where an industrial tribunal has awarded reinstatement to a worker, but the employer contests the award in a High Court proceeding or before the Hon'ble Supreme Court.
During the pendency of the appeal, the worker must not be employed by any establishment, and a signed affidavit to this effect must be filed by the worker.
