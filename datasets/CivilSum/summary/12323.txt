Delhi School Education Act, 1973, Section 8 - Removal - An advertisement was released for Junior Teacher, Grade V vacancies in the pay scale of Rs. 1260-2500, where 12 were appointed as JT, Gr.
V in pay scale of 1260-2500 and one was appointed as JT, Gr.
IV, in pay scale of Rs. 1480-3200.
Respondent No. 1's appointment was on a consolidated salary of Rs. 2500/- pm for one year, which was a leave vacancy.
On the expiry of one year, R-1 was removed - An appeal was made under Section 8 of Delhi School Education Act which resulted in reinstatement with all consequential benefits.
The decision was made on the belief that it was a case of "removal" from service as the procedure for imposing major penalty under the Delhi School Act, 1973 had been violated.
The Delhi School Tribunal held that there was nothing wrong with appointing respondent No. 1 on an ad hoc basis for a specified period, considering the circumstances.
The rule was made absolute and respondent No. 1's appeal was dismissed.
