Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Schools Employees (Security of Service) Rules, 1981, Rule 22A - Retiral benefits - Pension - Petitioners retired from Punjab Privately Managed Recognised Aided Schools upon reaching superannuation age - Petitioners opted to adopt 1992 Pension Scheme - Privately recognised aided school retirees entitled to receipt of pension, DA at par with Government employees through subsequent notification - Petitioners entitled to pension release at Government employee level and concurrent dearness pay benefit - Respondents directed to release pension without any deduction.
