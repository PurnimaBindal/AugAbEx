Constitution of India, Articles 226, 12 and 14 - Punjab Service (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1940 - Reimbursement of Medical bill - Outdoor patient - If outdoor treatment follows indoor treatment, an employee is entitled to reimbursement of the expenditure incurred by him as an outdoor patient - The petitioner is entitled to reimbursement of his medical bill - Writ allowed with special costs of Rs. 10,000/-.
