Arbitration and Reconciliation Act, 1996, Sections 5 and 11 - Arbitration agreement - Judicial review - An agreement between the parties existed which contained an arbitration clause for resolving disputes - The court should not intervene in the dispute between parties if there is an arbitration agreement - The power of judicial review possessed by superior courts should not be exercised in the presence of an arbitration clause - Disputes governed by the arbitration clause should be referred to arbitration.
[Paras 15, 18 and 23]
