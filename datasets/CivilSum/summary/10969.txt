Under Articles 14 and 16 of the Indian Constitution, a petitioner was declined candidacy for the Post of Extra Departmental Branch Post Master due to the list received after 30 days of sending the requisition.
The instruction for selection against one post does not specify a maximum number of candidates whose names can be considered for selection.
Furthermore, the respondents did not claim that the petitioner was excluded from consideration by short-listing.
The Tribunal's decision to reject the application was an error of law on the face of the record and must be set aside.
