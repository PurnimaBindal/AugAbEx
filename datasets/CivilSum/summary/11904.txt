The transfer of an employee from one place to another is generally the authority's responsibility and is an administrative order.
The court, in exercising its writ jurisdiction, normally does not meddle with transfer or posting orders, except in rare cases where an administrative power has been used unfairly or in breach of statutory laws (not simply guidelines provided by the government for transferring workers).
The remedy may be sought by way of judicial review.
[Para 3]

The rules framed by the court for filing writs allow the petitioner to verify the contents of the petition, and it is not mandatory to file a separate affidavit.
The petitioner must verify the contents of the petition to ensure that no false statements are made, even if the provisions of Order 19 Rule 3 of the Code are not applicable.
