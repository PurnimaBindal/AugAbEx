According to the Constitution of India, Articles 21, 309 and 311, as well as the Indian Penal Code, Section 196, and the Criminal Procedure Code, Sections 4, 190 and 195, an employer has the right to pursue both disciplinary and criminal proceedings against an employee who commits a criminal offense resulting in misconduct in their employment.
The constitutional right of protection of life under Article 21 cannot be overridden by any Service Rules or government instructions.
If an employee illegally gheraos their officer, the officer can file a private complaint directly with the Magistrate, in addition to pursuing disciplinary proceedings.
