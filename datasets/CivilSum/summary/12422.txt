According to Constitution of India Articles 14 and 226, Departmental proceedings and Criminal proceedings differ on charges in crimes of misusing bank property and misconduct in employment, respectively, and cannot be assumed to be founded on the same facts.
Therefore, charge-sheet cannot be dismissed even if the evidence in the enquiry or criminal proceedings is already concluded.
[Paras 8 and 9] B.
A charge-sheet that was issued six years ago cannot be challenged due to pending criminal proceedings.
Petitioners may have their petition dismissed due to delay in the process.
