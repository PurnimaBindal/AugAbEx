Constitution of India, Article 16 - Transfer - Locus standi - A petitioner, who was an Assistant Executive Engineer transferred and posted in Jammu Municipality as against the available vacancy, had argued that the transfer order has blocked his chances of promotion.
The court held that the person aggrieved is the Jammu Municipality and not the petitioner and that the petitioner has no locus standi in this matter.
Mere loss of chance of promotion, which is a purely contingent right, cannot be enforced at the instance of the petitioner.
Hence, the petition is not maintainable.
[Paras 4 to 6]
