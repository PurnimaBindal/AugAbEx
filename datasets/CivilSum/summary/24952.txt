Retirement - If a government employee is facing disciplinary action for misconduct and retires before the proceedings are completed, the state government can deduct pension if the allegations are proven.
If the charges are not proven during the proceedings or the proceedings are quashed, the government cannot deduct the pension on the same allegations.
However, if serious allegations are established and could have affected the employee's service, the government can still take action for pension deduction in accordance with the rules.
