The appellant, who joined the ICAR in 1972, was promoted to S-2 scientist in 1980.
However, due to a decision by the Delhi High Court, she and other scientists were given promotion as S-2 scientists from 1976.
The Board called for assessment proformas for S-2 scientists in 1992, and promotion to the next higher grade for ICAR scientists is based on the assessment of their own work during a 5-year period in one grade, not on comparative assessment.
The procedure adopted by the Board was deemed neither arbitrary nor in breach of the rules.
Hence, the tribunal's order directing respondents to give promotion to the appellant as S-3 scientist was set aside.
