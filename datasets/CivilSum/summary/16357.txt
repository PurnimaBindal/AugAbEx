As per the Rajasthan Educational Subordinate Service Rules, 1971, Section F, Column 4 of Entry No. 8 to the Schedule appended thereto - Rajasthan Educational Service Rules, 1970, Rule 2(g), the eligibility for the post of "Senior Teacher" is defined.
There was a query regarding whether the term "schools" used in entry No. 8 column 4 of Section F of the Schedule to 1971 Rules is confined to the schools imparting education up to the secondary level or the senior higher secondary schools are inclusive to it.
The Court has held that all types of schools that are part of the education system in the State of Rajasthan come within the purview of the term "Schools."
Therefore, a senior teacher is entitled to teach in senior secondary schools also.
The intention was to include all kinds of schools, including Senior Secondary Schools.
Confining the term "schools" up to secondary schools is adding something with a statute that was not intended by the legislature.
It was held that rejecting the candidature for the appointment as Senior Teacher on the ground that they do not possess two subjects taught in secondary schools with a degree of Graduation is illegal.
