Constitution of India, Article 300-A - Guru Nanak Dev University, Calendar Volume IV, Ordinance 18 - Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, Section 2(e) - Retiral benefits - For the petitioner who retired as a Lecturer from Hindu College, Amritsar after 33 years of service, the provisions of Guru Nanak Dev University are applicable - Even though teachers are not categorized as skilled, semi-skilled or unskilled, manual, supervisory, technical or clerical employees, the employer must still disburse the amount of gratuity to the petitioner in accordance with Ordinance 18 from the Guru Nanak Dev University, Calendar.
