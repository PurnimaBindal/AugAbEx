Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Tamil Nadu Recognized Private Schools (Regulation) Act, 1973, Section 56 - Tamil Nadu Recognized Private Schools (Regulation) Rules, 1974, Rule 15(4) (1) - Appointment - Approval of appointment - Withdrawal of approval - The order of withdrawal of approval granted is only valid if the appellant's name was not sponsored by the employment exchange.
Even though the rule specifies recruitment from candidates sponsored by the Employment Exchange, it is acceptable to invite applications from the general public.
The impugned order has been eliminated.
