Section 33 of the Industrial Disputes Act states that in order to claim a contravention of the section, the workman must be involved in a dispute that is currently pending.
The alteration that caused the dispute must have also occurred during the pendency of said dispute in order for an authority listed in Section 33 to have jurisdiction to address the contravention.
[Para 6]
According to Section 2(k) of the Industrial Disputes Act, an industrial dispute pertains to a disagreement between employers and workmen, between employers themselves, or between workmen that is associated with employment or the conditions of labor.
The dispute can be related to non-employment or to the terms of employment of an individual.
