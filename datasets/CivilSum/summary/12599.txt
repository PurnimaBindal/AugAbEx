According to Section 63(c) of the Delhi Cooperative Societies Act, 1972, a delegate of one society sitting on the committee of another society must vacate their seat if their society become defunct or a defaulter for a continuous period of three months.
However, if a member of the Board was not a defaulter themselves and their society was not defunct on the date they became a member of the Board or participated in a meeting of the Board of Directors, they are not disqualified from participating in proceedings.
Additionally, a member has the ability to remove their society's default within the prescribed three month period, and if the default is removed before this period ends, there is no disqualification that can vitiate the proceedings of the Board's meeting.
