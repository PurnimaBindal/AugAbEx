Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion - Seniority - Deemed date of joining - If an employee has been given a deemed date of joining, then their seniority from that date cannot be ignored, regardless of whether monetary benefits were given during that period - For the purpose of promotion, the deemed period cannot be disregarded.
[Paras 16 and 18] B.
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Delay and latches - Seniority - Date of knowledge - If a petitioner only became aware of the illegality in the fixation of seniority when their name was not considered for promotion, and they immediately filed a writ petition, it cannot be rejected as belated.
