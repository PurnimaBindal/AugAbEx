Constitution of India, Article 226 - Regularisation - The petitioner was originally recruited as a Beldar on a daily wage basis, but his services were regularized in that position.
He argues that he should instead be regularized as a clerk-cum-typist, as he claims to have been performing those duties.
However, the petitioner was never officially appointed in that role, and only worked there with the informal permission of an officer.
This unauthorized action cannot confer any right on the petitioner, and therefore there are no grounds to support his claim for regularisation on a class III post.
[Paras 3, 5 and 6]
