Constitution of India, Article 226 - Award - Findings by Labour Court - High Courts should refrain from interfering with Industrial Tribunal awards on technicalities - Orders can only be interfered with in cases of jurisdictional error, breach of natural justice or apparent error of law - Contrary findings cannot be based on reappraisal of evidence - Award is confirmed since there is adequate evidence and no legal or jurisdictional errors.
[Paras 12 and 13]
