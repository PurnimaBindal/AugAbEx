The Constitution of India lays out several articles related to ad hoc appointments and the regularisation of services.
Article 14 is the umbrella article, while Article 16 ensures equality of opportunity in public employment.
Appointment to Class-III and Class-IV posts on an ad hoc basis and regularisation with artificial breaks violates Articles 14 and 16, leading to corruption and disappointing those waiting for employment.
The Court cannot direct the regularisation of employees disregarding government policy.
[Paras 1 and 2]
Article 226 of the Constitution and the doctrine of equality in Article 16 relate to the regulation of service conditions for government employees.
The government has the right to make its own policies about the conditions of service, including for daily wagers.
The Court cannot enforce the regularisation of employees on Class-III posts which is illegal.
The Court must not perpetuate the illegality by directing on Article 226 of the Constitution.
[Paras 9 to 12]
