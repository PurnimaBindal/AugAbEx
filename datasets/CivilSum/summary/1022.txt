Regulation 425 of the Regulations for the Indian Air Force states that if a Muslim member of the Armed Forces had a beard at the time of joining the service, they are allowed to keep it.
However, the policy instructions dated 2422003 and 1.4.2003 are considered legal and valid.
The court found that sporting a beard is not an integral part of the Islamic religious tenet and is not protected under Article 25 of the Constitution of India.
According to Mohammedan Law, growing a beard does not fall under any of the fundamental commandments that a devout Muslim is obligated to observe.
Facial identity of every member of the service is important, particularly while in uniform.
Therefore, a Muslim personnel in the Armed Forces cannot insist upon sporting a beard merely because they desire to do so.
