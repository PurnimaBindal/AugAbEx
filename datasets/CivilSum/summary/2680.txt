According to Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1638, it is the authority competent to appoint and promote to a post that is responsible for such actions, not the approving authority of the promotions and appointments.
Rule 138(2) makes it clear that promotions as Head Constables shall be made by the Superintendent of Police who appoints them.
The DIG Police's role is only to scrutinize the list and approve it at the time of the annual inspection.
Therefore, the Superintendent of Police, not the DIG, is the appointing and punishing authority of Head Constables.
Just because the DIG approved the promotions or list C-1, it does not make the DIG the appointing authority.
[Paras 8, 13 to 16]

Under Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1638, the sanction of the district magistrate is not required when disciplinary proceedings are initiated under the Police Rules.
Even if the inquiry reveals the involvement of the delinquent in criminal cases, it will not make the sanction of the magistrate necessary.
It cannot be said that the procedure under Rule 1638 was not adopted.
