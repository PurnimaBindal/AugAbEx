Constitution of India, Article 226 - Seniority fixation - Petitioner contests appointments of Respondents 2, 3 as officer effective from 28.8.1986 and those of Respondent 5, 6 effective from 1.10.1996 - Petitioner is also contesting seniority fixation relative to petitioner, who was appointed as computer officers on 741988 - Court ruled that petitioner cannot contest appointments of respondents under pretext of challenging their placement in seniority list - Respondent No. 1 acted in compliance with Recruitment Rules - Employer can have appointments from various sources of recruitment - Once petitioner's case was considered, they cannot have any lawful complaint - No violation of recruitment rules by respondent No. 1 - Petition dismissed.
