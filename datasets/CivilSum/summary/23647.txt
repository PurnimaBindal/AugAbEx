Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 32 - Seniority - Promotion - When the cadre of Telex Operators merged with the existing cadre of AG II, their seniority had to be fixed in accordance with paragraph 1 (viii) of Executive Instructions, read with clause H(iii) of the principles of seniority.
The benefit of promotion to regular employees in AG II could be extended to AG I, with effect from the date of promotion of Telex Operators, without being seen as unreasonable or arbitrary.
Clause B(iii)(b) pertains to the fixation of seniority in the case of departmental promotees.
Regular employees in AG II grade who were senior to the Telex Operators were denied promotion while their juniors were promoted as AG I Therefore, it is incorrect to view that regular employees in AG II cadre could not be considered for promotion as they did not meet the criterion of 18 years of service.
The decision of the Commission that cadres up to AG I should be maintained on a regional level, is not arbitrary, and the order of the High Court is set aside.
