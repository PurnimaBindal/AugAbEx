Constitution of India, Article 311 - Central Civil Services (Temporary Services) Rules 1965, Rule 5(1) - Termination - Whether punitive or simplicitor - The respondent's work was never satisfactory during the probation period, and the service was terminated without an inquiry.
The petitioner's right to terminate as contract of service and service rules were not denied.
The order of termination is legal, valid, and not punitive as it does not cast any stigma on the employee and is not based on misconduct.
