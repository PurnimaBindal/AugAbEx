Rajasthan Subordinate Officers Ministerial Staff Rules, 1957, Rule 25(7) - Regularisation - Eligibility for consideration - Though Rule 25(7) is not mandatory, authority to consider eligible persons for regularisation must do so in accordance with the Rule and benefit eligible persons - If authority acts arbitrarily, employees can approach court for violation of their rights.
[Paras 3, 6-8, 16, 18, and 19]

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 2(ra), Entry 10 to Schedule V - Unfair labor practice - Regularisation - Long continuance of any person in temporary status to deny him benefit of permanent status is considered unfair labor practice by Parliament and violates Articles 14 and 16 - Workman has vested right to seek enforcement of his right by invoking Writ jurisdiction of the High Court.
[Paras 13-15]

Rajasthan Subordinate Officers Ministerial Staff Rules, 1957, Rule 25(7) - Regularisation - Rule 25(7) protects the right of eligible employees to be considered for regularisation and effectuates the obligation of the State to act fairly in employment.
[Para 16]

Rajasthan Subordinate Officers Ministerial Staff Rules, 1957, Rule 25(7) - Regularisation - Casual labor - Appointment of casual labor on work charged establishment is not irregular since rules provide a mechanism for conferring the status of semi-permanent or permanent to the casual labor - Object of the Rule is equal protection of law and enforceable right as a constitutional guarantee.
[Paras 17-18]
