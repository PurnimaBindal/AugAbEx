Education - Examination - Additional subject - The petitioner was never informed before acceptance of her form that she was not eligible to appear in the examination of the additional subject.
However, her form was accepted which must have been scrutinized in the office.
The petitioner did not conceal anything from the University at the time of submission of her examination form.
When the University accepted the petitioner's form, it cannot now say that petitioner was not eligible to appear in the examination, especially when the petitioner has already undertaken it.
Respondents are directed to declare the petitioner's result of the examination of the additional subject.
However, this order is passed only in peculiar circumstances and shall not be binding for the respondent University.
