According to Section 5 of the Punjab Affiliated Colleges Act (Security of Service of Teachers), 1974, amended in January 1983, a lecturer's service can be terminated, but if they are reinstated by the DPI(Colleges) and the order is set aside on appeal, an appeal may be made before the Additional District Judge.
However, the power of the District Judge to hear the appeal was withdrawn and was vested in the College Tribunal by the Amendment Act of 1983.
As a result, the order was without jurisdiction, and the College has been given the freedom to file an appeal with the College Tribunal.
