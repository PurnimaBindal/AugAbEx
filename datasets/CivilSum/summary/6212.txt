The employee of the Hyderabad Municipal Corporation who has been dismissed from service is subject to the same rules as Government servants in the State, and thus the High Court does not have the power to hear petitions under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
[Para 9] B.
An employee of the Hyderabad Municipal Corporation who has been dismissed from service has the right to appeal to the Standing Committee, but the State Government is empowered to cancel the resolution of the Committee without giving notice to the employee, as long as there is no violation of Section 679-A of the Hyderabad Municipal Corporation Act, 1955.
