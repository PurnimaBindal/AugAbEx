Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Despite the claim of the petitioner, juniors were promoted to the position of Superintendent - Despite the argument of the respondent authorities that the promotions were erroneous and the juniors will be reverted, the petitioner has the right to be considered for promotion prior to any of his juniors - Had he been found qualified, he would have received all the perks of his juniors - The authority is required to examine his case from the date of his juniors' promotion, and if he meets the eligibility criteria, he should be promoted with all ancillary benefits within two months.
[Paras 2 and 3]
