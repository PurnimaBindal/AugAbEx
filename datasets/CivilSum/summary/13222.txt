Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Pay scales - Equality in pay scales - Haryana Agricultural University should have granted the pay scale of Rs. 950-1400 for technical posts to holders of III certificate/diplomas (who are non-matric) - Matriculate holders of III certificates should receive the pay scale of Rs. 1200-2040, as given to their counterparts under the Haryana Government - Granting equal pay for equal work is a goal enshrined in our constitution - Regardless of whether or not the plaintiffs appealed to the District Judge, Hissar, this judgment benefits all of them.
