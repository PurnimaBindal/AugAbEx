According to Central Reserved Police Force Rules, 1955, Rule 8(b) and Border Security Force Rules, 1969, Rule 14(2), the determination of seniority of Emergency Commissioned Officers (ECOs) and Short Service Commissioned Officers (SSCOs) who were recruited as Assistant Commandants in the Border Security Force after their release from the Army depends on whether they are entitled to count the service rendered by them in the Army for calculating their seniority in the Border Security Force.
However, the seniority of SSCOs absorbed/appointed as Assistant Commandants in BSF on selection by the Special Selection Board during 1974-78 is not governed by Rule 8(b) of CRPF Rules and are not entitled to count Military Service period towards their service.
Even if Rule 8(b) of CRPF Rules applied, they were not entitled to that benefit as there was no provision in Rule 8(b) enabling an Army Officer to count his Army service for the purpose of seniority in CRPF.
Consequently, the judgment of the High Court was set aside, and the appeal was allowed.
