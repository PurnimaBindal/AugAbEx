Punishment and Appeal Rules, 1952, Rule 7 - Recalling of order - Lower authority does not have jurisdiction to reopen a case - An official cannot be forced to face another inquiry for the same charge.
[Para 6] B.
Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226/227 - Double Jeopardy - After undergoing a departmental procedure, a delinquent officer cannot be compelled to face another inquiry on the same charge - The order is invalidated.
