The Government is authorized to make appointments under Article 162 of the Constitution even in the absence of appointment rules, but once statutory rules are framed, appointments should be made only in accordance with the rules.
The executive power can only be exercised to fill gaps.
Instructions can supplement the rules but cannot supplant them.
Ad hoc employees should be replaced as soon as possible by direct recruits when there are appointment rules for regular appointments.
[Para 7]

A little need for ad hoc appointments due to emergent exigencies does not give the Government the power to relax the appointment rules and to regularize such appointments made by relaxing the rules that are not in accordance with the rules.
[Para 7]

Appointments in Civil Services must be made by selection and consultation with the Public Service Commission (PSC).
Regular appointments cannot be made under the Rules without the selection made by the Public Service Commission.
The Commission is established as an independent body to guarantee the selection of the best available persons for appointment to a post to avoid arbitrariness and nepotism.
[Paras 8 to 13]
