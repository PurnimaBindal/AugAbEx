The Constitution of India, Article 16, saw the Circular dated 1751994 challenged, clarified and amended by Notification dated 17.12.1994, resulting in Reservation for Medical Colleges that was violative of Article 16 due to the Reservation of 65% which reduced the general category to 35%.
This was rectified in the amended circular by reserving 30% for ladies, confined to a paragraph of the amended circular.
The amended circular made vertical reservation 50% for the general category and 50% for scheduled caste, scheduled tribes and backward classes, while the Reservation of 15% for various categories mentioned in the earlier circular was made horizontal in the amended circular.
This extended it to all seats, and Reservation was now more in the general category.
The amended circular divides all seats in CPMT into two categories - general and reserved, both allocated 50%.
The interim order passed by the Court staying declaration of results is discharged, and in view of the circular issued by the Government on 17.12.1994, the grievance cannot be said to survive.
