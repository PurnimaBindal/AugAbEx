Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Age of superannuation of work-charge and regular employees - Petitioner benefited from 60 years age of superannuation as work-charge employee.
However, his claim for regularisation under the policy, if considered, would have caused him to retire at the age of 58 years as a regular employee.
Unfortunately, the particular date of consideration for regularisation occurred after the petitioner's superannuation at 58 years, hence leading to the rejection of his claim for regularisation.
