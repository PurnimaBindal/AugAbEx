High Court Establishment (Appointment and Conditions of Service) Rules, 1973, Rule 19(4)(ii) - Restorer vying for promotion as Clerk - Exempted from typewriting test - Medical Board declared that petitioner was incapable of efficient type work - However, exemption was rejected on the grounds that Doctor never certified petitioner incapable of typewriting - While similarly situated individuals were exempted and promoted - Court held that medical opinions of others did not indicate their incapability for typewriting, thus only way to achieve equality is to grant same exemption to petitioner - Petitioner deserves exemption for promotion.
