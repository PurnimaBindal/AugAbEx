Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Appointment - Waiting list - If a vacancy arises due to non-joining of selected candidates, waiting list candidates may be appointed - A State Government's request to send names from the waiting list was denied because the one-year recommendation period had passed - Vacancies resulting from the cancellation of candidates were not included in subsequent examinations - The Public Service Commission was instructed to send names from the waiting list of the previous examination.
