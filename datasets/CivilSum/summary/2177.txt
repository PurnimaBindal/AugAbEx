The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, Section 25-F states that there is no limitation to raise an industrial dispute, and the Hon'ble Apex Court has been considerate in condoning even long delays.
Every case has to be taken on its facts and circumstances.
However, the rationale of all these judgments is that there must be justification or explanation given.
In the present case, mere exchange of some correspondence or writing letters cannot be considered a good ground to condone delay.
The Tribunal rightly denied relief on the ground of long and inordinate delay.
