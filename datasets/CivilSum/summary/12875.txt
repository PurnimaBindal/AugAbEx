Minimum Wages Act, 1948, Section 22-C - Offences by a company under the Minimum Wages Act - Prosecution of one of the directors responsible for the conduct of the business of the company - The company is not made an accused, and the prosecution of the director is quashed - The company must be accused before the director can be charged.
2005(4) RCR(Criminal) 141 : 2005(3) Apex Criminal 229 (SC) relied.
