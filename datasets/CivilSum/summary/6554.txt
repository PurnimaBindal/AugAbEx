Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - Relationship of employer and employee - If an employer ceases its relationship with an employee, it cannot initiate enquiry proceedings against that erstwhile employee - The petitioner, an erstwhile employee of State Roadways merged into Government Roadways Corporation, had its employer-employee relationship with the government ceased - Consequently, government was no longer competent to initiate enquiry proceedings against the petitioner - The action taken by the corporation on the enquiry report sent by the government without affording petitioner any opportunity held illegal.
[Paras 15 and 16]
