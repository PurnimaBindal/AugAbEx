Life Insurance Corporation Act (31 of 1956), Section 48 - Life Insurance Corporation of India (Employees) Pension Rules, 1995, Rule 3 - Pension scheme - Cut off date to be prescribed - Distinction between retired and serving employees is not arbitrary - Article 14 requires explanation for differential treatment of those retiring before and after a specified date - Classification of former LIC employees for pension entitlement based on cut-off date is objective and reasonable - Capacity of LIC to grant pension need not be addressed.
