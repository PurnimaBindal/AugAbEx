The Punjab Medical Education Service Class I (1st Amendment) Rules, 1985, Rules 4 and 7(2), in accordance with the Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226/227, state that promotion should be based on qualification.
The department of Cardiology requires a minimum of three years' experience as a Senior Lecturer, gained in Cardiology.
However, the promotion of Respondent No. 3 to the position of Assistant Professor Cardiology is being challenged as they do not possess the required experience.
The Petitioner, who has the requisite qualifications and experience, is being overlooked.
The Court has directed Respondents to consider the petitioner's case for promotion to the post of Assistant Professor Cardiology, and has declared the promotion of Respondent No. 3 as illegal and non est, liable to be quashed.
