The petitioner sought voluntary retirement under the assumption that Fundamental Rule 56(k) was in force and that he was eligible as he was not under suspension at the time of submitting his request.
However, the authorities refused the request as the exercise of the right of voluntary retirement is determined by the conditions of service, rules, and bye-laws.
Since there was no such rule applicable, the petitioner was not entitled to voluntary retirement.
