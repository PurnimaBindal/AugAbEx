Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Promotion from retrospective date - If an official's promotion was withheld due to a mistake, they cannot be deprived of promotion and arrears of salary - Writ Court has jurisdiction to mold the relief in the facts of the case and interest of justice - The petitioner is entitled to arrears of pay and allowance from the dates of their promotions, and not just from the dates of their actual joining the promotion post.
