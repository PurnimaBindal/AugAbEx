Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Dismissal - Reinstatement - The petitioner's claim for annual increments should be accepted - Reinstating the petitioner will give him the same rights as if his services had not been terminated - The petitioner is entitled to the increments that he missed during his absence due to dismissal - Additionally, recomputation of salary based on the annual increments is due to the petitioner.
