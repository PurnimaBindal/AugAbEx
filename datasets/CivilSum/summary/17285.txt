West Bengal Board of Secondary Education Act, 1963, Section 24 - Disciplinary enquiry - Suspension - Natural justice - The Headmistress faced charges from the Management Committee and was placed under suspension.
The impugned order was an administrative function and did not fall under the purview of natural justice.
The petitioners were given the opportunity of hearing before the order was passed.
When a function is purely administrative, only the right to make a representation before final action may be called for.
[Paras 8, 13, 14 and 18]
