Constitution of India, Articles 226, 227 - Compensation in lieu of reinstatement - Sustenance - If a termination is found illegal, the usual rule is to grant reinstatement unless there are special exceptional circumstances, such as a delay of more than 10 years between termination and reference.
In the absence of exceptional circumstances, the petitioner is entitled to reinstatement, as ruled by the Labour Court.
[Paras 9 to 12]
