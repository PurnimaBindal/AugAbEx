Constitution of India, Article 226 - Termination order - Unsustainable due to complete non-application of mind by Appellate authority related to charges against employee - First charge was proved by Disciplinary authority without discussion, whereas second charge was only partially proved - Appellate authority held both charges prove without proper consideration [Para 4]
Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punishment - Proportionality - Punishment of termination for taking a vehicle off authorized route is disproportionate to nature of offense - Authorities must impose a proportionate punishment [Para 6]
