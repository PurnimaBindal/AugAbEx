Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Delhi School Education Rules, 1973, Rule 98(3) - Promotion - Qualifications - Eligibility - Petitioner, a Language Teacher with a Masters Degree in Economics, was selected for the post of PGT (Economics) from the Cadre of TGT (Language) but denied promotion by the Director due to lack of teaching experience in the subject.
However, there is no Rule requiring teaching experience in the same subject and even a general candidate with teaching experience in any subject can be considered for the promotion.
Thus, there is no logic in refusing approval to the promotion of the petitioner.
[Paras 5 and 6]
