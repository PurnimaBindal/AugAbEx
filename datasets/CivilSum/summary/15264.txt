Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Payment of salary and pension - Sanction of Competent Authority - Natural justice - The disbursing officer cannot take any unauthorized action to withhold, deduct or reduce the payment of an employee or pensioner once the payment is sanctioned and released by the Competent Authority.
Any curtailment of the employee/pensioner's right to payment without affording him an opportunity in compliance with the principles of natural justice is not permissible.
