The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Promotion/Delay and laches - Promotions granted during the period of 1975 to 1981 - The petitioner's representation was declined in 1981, and a legal notice was sent in 1990, followed by a petition filed in 1990 - The petitioner has not justified the long delay - The claim is considered overly delayed, and a suit would be barred after three years - A writ petition cannot be entertained where a suit would be time-barred.

The Constitution of India, Article 14 - Promotion/Merit - The petitioner received a satisfactory grading while his juniors were graded as outstanding or very good - The petitioner was considered, but his juniors were found to be more meritorious and therefore promoted - The petitioner cannot raise any grievance on this matter.
