Constitution of India, Article 226 - Age of superannuation - The petitioner requested an increase in the retirement age from 58 to 60 years, in accordance with a memorandum issued by the Central Government - An interim order was passed to prevent the petitioner from being retired until the next hearing - However, due to a previous decision in CWP No. 6152/98 regarding the same issue, the petitioner's application has been dismissed and the interim order has been lifted.
