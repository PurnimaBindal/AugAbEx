Constitution of India, Articles 14, 226 and 311 - Departmental proceedings - Judicial review - Scope of judicial review - The role of courts in reviewing orders given by authorities in disciplinary proceedings is limited to checking if the accused official was given the chance to defend themselves in accordance with the rules of natural justice.
The court cannot question the sufficiency of evidence supporting the punishment.
However, if there is no evidence supporting the allegations, the court can consider the plea of no evidence.
The allegations of the Constable consuming liquor while on duty were not proven, and it was not even confirmed that the Constable was on duty.
The Doctor's opinion suggested that it may have been the effect of medicine as claimed by the accused.
Punishments that are not based on evidence cannot be allowed to stand.
