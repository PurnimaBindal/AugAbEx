Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 16.2 - Discharge from service - Dismissal from service - Absence from duty - Gravest misconduct - Discipline is required to be maintained in the disciplinary forces - Despite the necessity of discipline, absence is sometimes unavoidable - Employee was absent for only 23 hours and 5 minutes - State instructed to reinstate respondent without back pay but with the caveat that if he is absent without prior permission for two consecutive days within a year, he may be dismissed from service - Although, he may still receive pension if he is eligible at the time of retirement under the applicable rules.
