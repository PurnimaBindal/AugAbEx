Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Correction of the date of birth - Refusal to correct - Relevant materials - The relevant materials for correcting the date of birth include the employee's documents, forms filled in for giving information, and the documents that have been relied upon and supplied.
The employee must verify that the date has been wrongly recorded due to the negligence of a third party.
[Paras 11 and 12]
Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 226 - Correction of the date of birth - Natural justice - Enquiry report - Non-supply of - The employee must be granted full opportunity to justify his claim for the correction of the date of birth.
Even if the findings of the investigating officer is not supplied before the rejection of the report, there is no ground to seek relief from the court.
The decision should be based on good and sufficient material.
[Paras 17 and 18]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Correction of the date of birth - Delay - The High Court should not entertain claims for the correction of the date of birth at the end of the petitioner's service.
[Para 18]
Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 226 - Correction of the date of birth - Interim relief - Interim relief should not be provided if the opinion of the competent authority and the enquiry report are based on good and sufficient material.
The employee does not suffer any irreparable loss or injury if the interim relief is not granted.
[Para 19]
