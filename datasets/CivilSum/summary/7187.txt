The petitioner, who works in the army, was charged with theft of ammunition and received a punishment of reduction in rank by a summary court martial under Army Act, 1950, Sections 52(a) and 63.
The order was challenged on the grounds that the punishment was disproportionate to the offence, but this contention was found to have no force.
The punishment was actually considered to be lenient.
[Paras 13 & 15]
