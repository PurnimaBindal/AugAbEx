Army Pension Regulations, Part I, 1961, Regulation 173 - Disability pension - Claim of - The Medical Board found that the disability was not attributable to military service and this opinion was not challenged.
The Petitioner relied on a different opinion of disability being 30%.
However, the finding and opinion of the Medical Board are subject to experts and cannot be disbelieved without sufficient material.
Therefore, the Petitioner is not entitled to disability pension.
