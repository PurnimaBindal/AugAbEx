Indian Veterinary Council Act, 1984, Sections 21(1)(b), 22 and 66(1) - Admissions to Veterinary Colleges - The power to regulate education in veterinary science falls under the Veterinary Council.
Therefore, the Veterinary Council is responsible for conducting the all India Entrance Examination for filling up of 15% of seats of All India Quota and not the Indian Council of Agricultural Research.
