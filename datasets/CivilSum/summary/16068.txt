Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Vol. II, Rule 16.2(1) - Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311 - Dismissal from service/Misconduct/Taking liquor - The petitioner was dismissed from service due to a single case of taking liquor, without any proof that the alleged misconduct reflected a permanent character flaw of the petitioner.
Furthermore, the petitioner had already completed enough service to qualify for pension, but this was not taken into account.
The order of dismissal from service was arbitrary and illegal, and must be quashed.
