Punjab Police Rules - Punishment of Censure - Sustainability - The petitioner was given punishment for leaving guard duty after sunset for 1-1/2 hours.
However, the petitioner submitted a reply to notice stating to have gone to a doctor and recorded a DDR after coming back.
The court held that the respondent acted irrationally without considering the petitioner's explanation and no case was made out of gross misconduct for awarding punishment, so the order was set aside.
[Paras 9 and 10]   B. Punjab Police Rules - Punishment of Censure - Allegation of malice - The High Court held that it is not inclined to go into statements of malice made by the petitioner, as it is not a court of facts nor having appropriate machinery to adjudicate upon disputed questions of fact.
