Constitution of India, Articles 311 and 226 - Misconduct - Gross indiscipline - Dismissal - Unauthorised absence from duty - Appellant working as Food Inspector and transferred to another place - Appellant remaining absent from duty without leave for a considerable long time - Appellant indulged in highly deplorable practice to get himself transferred at a place of his choice - Dismissing appellant was due to gross indiscipline - Courts cannot interfere with such an order in Letters Patent Appeal.
