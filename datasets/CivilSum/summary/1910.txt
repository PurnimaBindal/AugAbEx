Civil Procedure Code, Section 92 - Permission of the Court to institute a suit against a trust or trustee - Prior permission is required before filing a suit - If the defendant was issued notice of the suit along with the application for permission and he files reply to the application also and the Court decides to grant permission, the validity of institution of suit cannot be challenged - Objection cannot be raised on the ground of validity of permission at any later stage.
[Paras 12 to 15] B.
Civil Register - Order granting permission under Section 92 to institute the suit found recorded in the Civil Register but missing from the file of the suit - It can be assumed that the order was not available on the file but found recorded in the Civil Register which carries a presumption of truth and is maintained under the directions of the High Court.
