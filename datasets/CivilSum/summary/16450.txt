Central Services (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1944 - Post heart surgery medical expenses - A denial of reimbursement after the death of a spouse is not permitted by law, according to the ratio of Ravi Kant's case.
Provision of medical assistance to the sick and disabled is an integral part of the state's obligation to improve public health, and the petitioner is entitled to medical reimbursement based on the law, logic, rationale, or compassion.
Any order or instructions that may prevent this should be ignored.
The benefit given during the petitioner's husband's lifetime cannot be denied after his death.
Follow-up treatment is an integral part of indoor treatment, so expenses incurred on outdoor treatment should be reimbursed as indoor treatment.
