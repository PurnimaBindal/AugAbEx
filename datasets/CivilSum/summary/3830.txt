Constitution of J & K, Section 124 - Constitution of India, Articles 14, 16 and 21 - Compassionate appointment - Its purpose is to provide immediate financial assistance to the family of a deceased sole breadwinner - It cannot be claimed as a right and is an exception to the Rule of equality - The concession cannot be kept alive for a long time - Petitioner cannot insist upon appointment to a particular post of his choice based on earlier instructions which are superseded and not provide requisite information, indicating that his need was not immediate.
