Constitution of India, Article 226 - Voluntary Retirement Scheme - VRS introduced by circular dated 1.12.97 - Applications submitted on 16.12.97 - Petitioners relieved from duties on 31.12.97 - Petitioners claim that their applications for Voluntary Retirement were given under coercion - However, they did not inform higher authorities or the police of this alleged threat - Furthermore, they received their dues without protest and are therefore precluded from filing this petition - Petitioners are not entitled to reinstatement.
