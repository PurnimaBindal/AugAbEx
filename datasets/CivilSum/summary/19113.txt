Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punjab Civil Service Rules, Volume II, Rule 3.17-A(f)(i) - Pension - Petitioner requested full period of service to be considered for his pension computation - Respondent argued for half of service being counted as petitioner's salary was paid from contingency fund - Court held that all service time where petitioner's pay was drawn from contingencies will count towards qualifying service for pension calculation.
1998(1) SCT 795 was referenced.
