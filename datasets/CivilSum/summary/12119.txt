As per Industrial Dispute Act, 1947, Section 33-C sub-section (1), recovery of money due from an employer is possible, but it must be an established right and cannot be used to establish a right.
The application cannot be used to adjudicate a seriously disputed claim for lay-off compensation.
Also, the appropriate government cannot adjudicate a disputed claim under the guise of power.
Additionally, the claim need not always be for a predetermined sum.
[Paras 5 and 6]
