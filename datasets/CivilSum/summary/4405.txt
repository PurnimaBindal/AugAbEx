"According to the Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, and the Punjab Educational Service, Class III, School Cadre, Rules of 1955, Rules 7 and 9(b) and (d), the seniority and absorption by transfer of the petitioners into the Education Department of the State of Haryana must be determined by state rules and transfer conditions.
As such, the petitioners should receive the benefit of their total length of service in accordance with Rule 7(b) and 9(b) and (d), entitling them to seniority.
