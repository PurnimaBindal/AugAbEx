According to the Constitution of India, while reserving a single post, the reservation formula specified in the 40 Point Roster cannot be used and the reservation of a single post would be unconstitutional and impermissible as it would violate the principle of reservation.
The constitutional guarantee of equal opportunity would be violated, making the reservation of a single post impermissible under Article 16(1) and an exception to Articles 16(1) and 16(2).
An advertisement made reserving a single post for scheduled caste quashed.
