Factories Act, 1948, Section 2(n) proviso (ii), 92 & 101 - Occupier of the factory - If a company owns a factory, only a single director can be designated as the occupier for purposes of the Act, and the company cannot nominate any other employee as such.
If the company fails to nominate a director, the Inspector of Factories may proceed against any director for prosecution and punishment in the event of a violation of the Act.
