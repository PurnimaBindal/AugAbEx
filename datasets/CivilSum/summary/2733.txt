Civil Procedure Code, Section 80(1) & (2) - Notice of filing suit against the State - Purpose of - Waiver or exemption of notice - The aim is to inform the authority of the grievance so that they can provide a solution without involving the litigant and State in unnecessary litigation.
- In the case of urgency or other reasons, the court can exempt the issuance of notice if it deems it ineffective - If the defendant does not object to prior notice, it will be considered waived and the authority will be barred from raising an objection later - Once the trial court grants the plaintiff exemption to sue without notice, failure to comply with Section 80 notice requirements cannot be used to dismiss the suit or appeal.
