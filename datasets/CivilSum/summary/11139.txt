Madhya Pradesh Ashaskiya Shiksha Sanstha (Adhyapakon Tatha Anya Karmchariyon Ke Vetano Ka Sandaya) Adhiniyam 1978 - The Managing Committee of an aided institution governed by the Act is considered to be 'State' under Article 12 of the Constitution, and is accountable to follow principles of natural justice - Violation of such principles occurred when the petitioner's status as a teacher was terminated by the society, which receives grant-in-ad from the government - Even if there were no rules or procedure prescribed under the Act at the relevant time for taking disciplinary action, the Act obliges the Managing Committee to adhere to principles of natural justice.
Petitioner's termination deserves to be quashed.

Shiksha Sanstha (Adhyapakon Tatha Anya Karmchariyon Ke Vetano Ka Sandaya) Adhiniyam, 1978, Section 6 - Article 226 of the Constitution - Re-instatement - Backwages - Although the government cannot be held responsible for paying arrears of salary to a teacher who was wrongfully terminated by the Management, the employee can claim back wages for a limited period of time - In this case, the petitioner was granted partial relief of back wages for the past three years at the rate of salary per month last drawn by her, along with re-instatement.
