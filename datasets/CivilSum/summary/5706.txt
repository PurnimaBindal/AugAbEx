Constitution of India, Article 226 - Writ Jurisdiction - Alternative remedy - The Division Bench admitted the writ petition despite the availability of alternative remedy and did not feel the need to relegate petitioners to the remedy of Civil Suit.
It was held that writ cannot be dismissed on the ground of availability of alternative remedy and the claim made by petitioner violates fundamental rights.
