The plaintiff, who held the position of Patwari, was convicted and sentenced to life imprisonment for their involvement in a murder case.
The disciplinary authority dismissed them from service after considering the severity of the offence and the plaintiff's conduct.
The lower courts made an error in accepting the plaintiff's lawsuit, as the disciplinary authority had considered all circumstances.
The judgments of both courts were overturned.
