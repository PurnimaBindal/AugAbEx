Constitution of India, Article 16 - Daily wage worker - Regularization - If there is no provision or policy to deem regularization, and the competent authority has not passed an order deeming the service of an employee as automatically regularized, then an employee's service cannot be treated as regularized unless based on the terms of their appointment.
[Paras 10, 11 and 28]

Constitution of India, Articles 226, 309 -- Delhi State Industrial Development Corporation (Staff) Service Rules (1978), Rule 44(2) - Daily wage worker - Dismissal from service - Even though the right of enquiry and hearing is available to a regular employee, a discharge simplicitor with no enquiry does not count as punitive.
A plea of non-compliance of Section 25F of the Industrial Disputes Act can only be raised through the Industrial Disputes Act and not directly through a writ petition.
[Paras 12 to 16, 19, 27 and 28]
