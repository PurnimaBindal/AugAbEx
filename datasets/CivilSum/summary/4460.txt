According to Section 124 of the Constitution of Jammu and Kashmir, Rule 14(1)(2) of the Jammu and Kashmir Employees (Conduct) Rules, 1971 do not prohibit a teacher from engaging in political activity.
A university resolution also permits teachers to participate in political activity.
As such, if the employer-University has no objections to the teacher's involvement in political activities then no one else can force them to be barred or subjected to any action.
