Constitution of India, Article 311(2) - Enquiry - Disciplinary proceedings - Natural justice - Although the petitioner requested legal assistance, he was not given permission to have a lawyer present during the proceedings.
The petitioner did not seek assistance from another coworker or officer, and his request for inspection of documents was granted.
Despite conducting the proceedings himself, the petitioner was not placed in a disadvantaged position, and no violation of the rules of natural justice occurred.
Therefore, the enquiry proceedings were not invalid.
