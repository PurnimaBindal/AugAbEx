The Constitution of India, Articles 16 and 226 - Seniority - Reserved candidate - The use of Roster Point No. 1 to grant accelerated seniority to respondent No. 3 over the petitioner is not valid, as Roster points are only meant for reservation quantum calculations - Seniority must be granted based on merit determined by the selecting agency - As the petitioner had a higher merit, he should be considered senior - Therefore, the impugned order to grant seniority to respondent No. 3 over the petitioner was quashed as illegal.
