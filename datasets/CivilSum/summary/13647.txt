Constitution of India, Article 16 - Punjab Government National Emergency (Concession) Rules, 1965, Rules 2 and 4 - Army service benefit - The petitioner, who served as a Sepoy in the Indian Army from July 20th, 1959 to November 9th, 1977, and was appointed as a clerk on January 9th, 1980, is entitled to increments for the period of service rendered during the proclamation of emergency.
This is because such service falls within the scope of "Military Service."
