Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, Part-I, Regulation 3 - Reduction in pension - The competent authority can reduce the pension under Regulation 3 of the Pension Regulations for Army, but cannot confiscate the entire pension.
Regulation 3 only allows for reduction, not confiscation or total reduction.
[Paras 6 and 7]

Pension Regulations for Army, 1961, Part-I, Regulation 3 - Satisfactory record of service - If a petitioner is convicted and confesses guilt under Section 5(1)(e) of the Prevention of Corruption Act with a sentence "till the rising of the court" and a fine of Rs. 500/-, their record is deemed stigmatic and unsatisfactory.
In this case, Regulation 3 of the Pension Regulations will apply.
[Para 6]
