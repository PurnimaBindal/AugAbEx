According to UP Intermediate Education Act (1921), Section 16G(3) and Regulations under the Act, Chapter III Reg.
32(3), a disciplinary enquiry can be conducted against a Head Master which can result in dismissal from service.
However, in a case where charges proved were not serious enough to warrant dismissal, the DIOS cannot award such a punishment.
In a particular case, a Head Master was reduced in rank instead of being dismissed, but the petitioner who was directly recruited to the post of Head Master cannot be reduced to the lower post of Assistant Teacher LT grade.
The order of reduction in grade was quashed.
The High Court cannot substitute its discretion for that of the competent disciplinary authority in determining the quantum of punishment.
[Paras 9 and 10]
