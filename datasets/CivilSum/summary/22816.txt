Constitution of India, Article 20(2) - Haryana Civil Services (Punishment & Appeal) Rules, 1987, Rules 7(1) & 7(2) - Double jeopardy - Imposition of punishment on the basis of MACT award for reduction to original time scale U/Rule 7(1) - Conviction in criminal charge U/s 304A by Criminal Court enables competent authority to terminate without further enquiry U/Rule 7(2) - Different situations are covered by both provisions - Thus, it cannot be considered as double jeopardy.
