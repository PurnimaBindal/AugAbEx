Constitution of India, Article 311 - Disciplinary proceedings - substitution of punishment - Effective date - Removal from service - Disciplinary proceedings initiated and punishment of removal from service imposed upon the appellant - Division Bench directed disciplinary authority to consider for a lesser penalty - Authority awarding penalty of compulsory retirement - The penalty of compulsory retirement shall be effective from the date the original penalty of removal from service was imposed - It is held that when a lesser penalty is imposed in lieu of the original penalty, the effective date shall be the date on which the original penalty was imposed.
[Paras 5, 6 and 7]
