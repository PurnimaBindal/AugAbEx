As per Rule 15 of the Industrial Disputes (Central) Rules, 1957, the employer sought to produce additional evidence, i.e., documents such as a photocopy of a cheque given to the workman as retrenchment compensation, a postal acknowledgement receipt of registered A.D. sent to the workman, a copy of the judgment, and a notification circular issued by the petitioner.
Moreover, allowing the employer to produce these documents would cause no prejudice to the workman.
Thus, the employer was permitted to produce the aforementioned documents as evidence.
