The Staff Selection Commission engaged the Respondents on daily wages to handle and process applications for various examinations.
However, their services were discontinued and given to TEXCO on a contractual basis.
The Tribunal directed that the Respondents be reinstated.
It is the responsibility of the appellant to determine whether such manual work should be done by casual workers or TEXCO.
If the decision is made in good faith to increase efficiency, it is not open to scrutiny.
Since there were no regular work opportunities available for the positions, the Tribunal could not have issued an order for reinstatement.
