Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 21 - Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of the Deceased Govt. Employees Rules, 2003 - Haryana Compassionate Assistance to the Dependents of the Deceased Govt. Employees Rules, 2006 - Compassionate appointment - Compassionate appointment can only be given within a reasonable time frame provided by the rules - Posts cannot be reserved for minors until they reach the age of eligibility - The offer of Compassionate appointment must align with the rules existing at the time of eligibility - Compassion cannot be used as a recruitment alternative or to offer posts - Under the 2006 Rules, Compassionate appointment cannot be offered - The petitioner is only entitled to financial assistance according to the rules.
