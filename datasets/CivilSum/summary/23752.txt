The Constitution of India, specifically Articles 14 and 16, outline promotion from Major General to Lieutenant General in the Army.
The Special Selection Board did not recommend the respondent for promotion due to QRs for promotion to Lt. General in the "Staff Stream" and comparative merit.
The High Court ruled against the given reason and noted the respondent's high performance, but the QRs only allow for a Staff appointment outside of the Corps.
The respondent's previous appointments were also Corps appointments and did not meet the requirements of Clause (b) of QRs.
Therefore, the High Court's decision to interfere was incorrect, and the Central Government's decision to not promote the respondent was upheld.
