Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Necessary party - An ex parte award issued by a Labour Court directed for the respondent No. 2's reinstatement into work - The Block Development and Panchayat Officer challenged the order - The Labour Court refused to dismiss the application for setting aside the ex-parte order - BD&PO and Panchayat Samiti were not made parties to the Labour Court proceedings and were not properly and necessary party to the case - The award could not be executed against BD&PO or Panchayat Samiti since they were not impleaded - Resultantly, the award is not binding on them - Therefore, the award was set aside and remanded the matter to the Labour Court for fresh disposal in accordance with the law while showing the respondent as Panchayat Samiti, represented by BD&PO.
