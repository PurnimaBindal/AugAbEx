Government Servants Family Benefit Fund Schemes 1974 and 1985 - Option - Paras 3, 3(d) and 4(2) of 1985 Scheme - Mere fact that the employee has not commenced contributing to the revised subscription or had not chosen the 1985 scheme does not imply that he chose the 1974 scheme and is not eligible to receive the benefit of 1985 scheme - While reading para 3 of the 1985 Scheme with para 4(2), it cannot be inferred that the 1985 Scheme is not applicable to employees who were over 50 years old on June 1, 1985 - The query regarding para 3(d) of the notification being inconsistent with para 3 of the 1985 scheme was not raised before the High Court and cannot be brought up for the first time before the Supreme Court - However, this matter has been left open.
