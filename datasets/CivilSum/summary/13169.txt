Constitution of India, Article 16 - Family Pension - Refusal to grant - The petitioner was denied family pension because her husband retired before a Government order providing for pension was issued.
However, family pension is payable to family members of the deceased even if the deceased had retired before the order was issued.
Classification based on a cut-off date is not allowed as it creates a class within the homogeneous group of family pension holders.
Therefore, the denial of pension is arbitrary and discriminatory.
The petition is allowed accordingly.
