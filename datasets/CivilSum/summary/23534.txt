The Administrative Tribunals Act 1985, Sections 29, 29-A and 33-C and Payment of Wages Act, Sections 17 and 15 are discussed.
Transfer of appeals to Administrative Tribunals has not impacted the jurisdiction of the authorities under the Payment of Wages Act to entertain and decide an application and appeal under sections 15 and 17 of the Act.
An appeal under Section 17 of the Act before the Distt.
Judge is not an appeal U/s 96 CPC.
The two-tier judicial system constituted under the "Corresponding Law", like the Payment of Wages Act, are not affected by the constitution of the Tribunals.
In case of a decision under Section 15 of the Act, the appeal shall lie U/s 17 before the Distt.
Judge, not the Tribunal.
