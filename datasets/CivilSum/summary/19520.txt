According to the Employee's State Insurance Act, 1948, Sections 1(5) and 2(1), the Appropriate Government has control over the employees of establishments.
However, in the case of Punjab Roadways, Chandigarh, they are not under the control of the Central Government.
The Appropriate Government in relation to the establishment is the State Government of Punjab, which has the authority to apply the provisions of the ESI act.
Currently, the Chandigarh Administration does not have control over these employees, and no notification has been issued by the Punjab Government indicating that the Act is applicable to them.
Therefore, the act does not apply to the employees of Punjab Roadways, Chandigarh.
