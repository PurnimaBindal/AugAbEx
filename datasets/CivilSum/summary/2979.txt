The Punjab National Bank (Employees) Voluntary Retirement Scheme, 2001 allows employees to withdraw their option of voluntary retirement at any time before its acceptance.
This right cannot be taken away by any mandatory clause in the scheme or by an employer unless there is a legal, constitutional, or contractual bar.
Furthermore, this general principle is applicable to every government servant and other agencies and instrumentalities of the government within the meaning of Article 12 of the Constitution.
Punjab National Bank is considered an agency and instrumentality of the government under Article 12.
Additionally, an agreement is not always void in its entirety if severable distinct promises are made for one and the same lawful consideration, and the law will only enforce those that are lawful.
Finally, the Scheme under which employees were given the temptation to voluntarily retire was not accepted, approved, and finalized as it is.
No consent was taken from the optees under the revised Scheme.
Acceptance of options made prior to amendment of Scheme without the consent of the optees were deemed illegal and not binding upon them.
