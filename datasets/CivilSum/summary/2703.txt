According to Schedule 2, Item 6 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, daily wagers may be eligible for regularisation of services.
If a petitioner has completed ten years of continuous service and possesses the minimum educational qualifications required for the post of Clerk, they are entitled to regularisation under the Regularisation Scheme of 1995.
This regularisation will be effective from the date of enforcement of the scheme.
