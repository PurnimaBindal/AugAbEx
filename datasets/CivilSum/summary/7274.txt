According to Articles 14 and 16 of the Constitution of India, a bus driver who has been convicted of a serious crime due to rash and negligent driving is not eligible for reinstatement after serving their sentence, even if the crime is not morally reprehensible.
As the custodian of both public property and passengers, the role of a state roadways bus driver is of great responsibility, and therefore the same vehicle cannot be entrusted to the driver again.
However, the State must establish a uniform policy and avoid a selective approach.
