Constitution of India, Articles 226/227 - Pay Scale - The Petitioner, a Laboratory Assistant, was appointed in Respondent School in 1964, and was subsequently given various grades of pay up to Rs. 1200-2040 w.e.f.
111986.
After the Respondent authorities withdrew a grade of Rs. 290-500, Petitioner challenged the withdrawal in court and was successful.
Despite this, he claims that he is entitled to the grade of Rs. 1200-2040 w.e.f.
111986.
The court finds that there is no justification to deny the petitioner this grade when Laboratory Assistants in Sainik School were given the same pay scale as those in Kendriya Vidyalayas.
Petition allowed.
[Paras 2 and 9]
