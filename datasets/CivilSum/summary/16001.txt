Punjab Cooperative Societies Act, 1961, Section 69 - Revision - Appeal - Punishment - The remedy of revision under Section 69 is not barred if appeal against the punishment order is maintainable before the Registrar.
Both the Government and the Registrar have the power to review any decision or order passed by a society.
The society cannot avoid the jurisdiction of the authorities under the Act just by framing their own bye-laws.
The revision petition is maintainable against the order passed by the Registrar in appeal.
