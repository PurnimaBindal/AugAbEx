Universities Act, 1973 (as amended by Act 15 of 1980), Section 28(J) - Education/Admission - Admission in post graduate courses for admission to medical colleges - General candidates must have a 50% cut off mark and Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes candidates must have 40%.
There is a 165% weightage of total marks and the criteria is valid.
