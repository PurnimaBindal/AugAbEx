Army Rules, 1954, Rule 37(1) & (3) - Court Martial - Prior to convening the Court Martial, the Officer in charge must ensure that there is sufficient evidence to support the alleged offence and that a prima facie case is established - The decision to proceed with trial by court-martial is a grave one, and Rule 37 requires the competent authority to comprehend the nature of the offence and review the evidence - However, the officer convening the court-martial is not necessarily responsible for this - As long as the competent authority has satisfied themselves of the facts and evidence, they may proceed with convening the court-martial.
