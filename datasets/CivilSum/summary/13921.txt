Punjab Civil Services Rules Vol. II Part I, Rule 6.17 - Family pension - Prior to 1671998, parents of an employee dying in harness were not considered part of the 'family' under the unamended Rule 6.17 and hence not eligible for family pension.
The amendment made on 1671998, which included parents in the definition of 'family', is effective from 111996 and does not have a retrospective effect.
[Paras 9 and 12]
