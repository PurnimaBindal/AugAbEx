Constitution of India, Article 16 - Rajasthan Civil Services (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1970, Rule 3 - Reimbursement of medical expenses - The petitioner paid Rs. 1,40,000/- for a dual chamber pace maker implanted in AIIMS New Delhi but received reimbursement of only Rs. 60,000/- as there was no provision for reimbursement for a pace maker.
The court held that the cost of an artificial part of the body can be reimbursed for the purpose of saving life and curing any disease with the concurrence of the Medical and Finance Departments under Rule 3(2), and the petitioner is therefore entitled to full reimbursement.
The writ petition was allowed.
