Constitution of India, Article 226 - Admission - Migration - Pre-medical test is not required for admission by way of migration of students from one medical college to another, unless otherwise specified in the rules.
Any deviation from non-statutory guidelines will not invalidate an otherwise permissible action under the rules.
The court should also consider subsequent events when adjudicating issues that may affect students' career.
