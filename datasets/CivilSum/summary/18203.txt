Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Promotion - Seniority - If seniority is the basis for promotion, then the senior or higher paid employee has the right to be promoted first - Promoting Junior Lecturer Assistants over Junior Technicians Grade-I and Store Keepers in higher pay scales to the position of Technical Assistants Grade-I is considered illegal - Those in higher pay scales must be considered previously promoted and senior.
