Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 16 - Regular appointment - Probation - Ad hoc appointment after probation - After a satisfactory completion of probation period, employees are entitled to regularisation upon appointment on a regular basis.
Post completion of probation, employees cannot be appointed as ad hoc appointees, as they are deemed to have been regularised.
Particularly when the position still exists and the need for the person continues.
