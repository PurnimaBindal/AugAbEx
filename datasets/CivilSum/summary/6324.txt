The Constitution of India's Articles 14 and 311 are aligned with Punjab Police Rules, 1934, Rule 1221-D - In cases where a person benefits from a forged document which violates the Rules in their appointment/admission, even if an order of termination/discharge casts a stigma, such an action cannot be termed vitiated or violative of principles of natural justice if no prejudice is caused to the individual, the position would have remained the same even after issuance of a show cause notice and the Rule provides for taking such action.
Furthermore, if there is no provision under the Rules to issue a show cause notice, and the individual not only produced a forged certificate but also concealed material information from the Education Board while applying for a duplicate certificate with their date of birth modified and subsequently secured enrolment in the police on the basis of such certificate, then there is no defence available to said individual, irrespective of whether show cause notice would have been issued.
The Order of discharge is upheld.
[Paras 9, 10 and 14]
