According to Section 2(J) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, an agency that runs tubewells whether by itself or through the government can be considered an industry.

Section 25F(c) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, requires that Retrenchment Notice be sent to the labor department and employment exchange in the prescribed Form 'P.'
Notice can be sent by personal delivery through a peon, and an entry must be made and receipt acknowledged in the Peon Book Service of Notice through registered post is only directory and not mandatory.
[Para 10]  C Section 25F(b) of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, specifies that the amount of retrenchment compensation must be sent by bank drafts in the names of each retrenched employee and must be delivered through sub-offices concerned, well in advance of the expiry of the notice period.
This constitutes valid payment of retrenchment compensation.
[Para 9]  D. Section 25FF of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, deals with transfer/re-employment and continuity of service.
If the work of running the tubewell is transferred from the government irrigation department to a government-owned corporation, the staff retrenched by the government irrigation department may be reappointed in the Corporation.
Although retrenched employees do not have the right to claim both retrenchment compensation and reemployment with continuity of service in the successor undertaking, the court can review the action to ensure that the service conditions imposed ensure fairness.
Employees who do not accept retrenchment compensation can be granted continuity in service for determining retrenchment benefits, computation of salary, and length of service in lieu of retrenchment compensation.
