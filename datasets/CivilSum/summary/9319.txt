Transfer - Jurisdiction - Public interest - While a Transfer is an administrative order that should generally not be interfered with, the Court may certainly interfere if the order was made for a reason other than public interest.
In this case, the Transfer was made based on the protected workman's union activities and behavior towards the employer, which is not in the public interest.
Therefore, the Court has quashed the Transfer and ordered the Petitioner to be moved back to Chandigarh.
[Para 6]  B Civil Procedure Code, Order 39, Rules 1 and 2 - Temporary injunction - Jurisdiction - The Appellate Court cannot interfere with a trial Court's decision to grant or refuse a temporary injunction unless there is a very strong reason to do so.
In this case, the Appellate Court did not adequately address the reasoning provided by the trial Court for granting the temporary injunction.
As a result, the decision of the Appellate Court has been overturned.
