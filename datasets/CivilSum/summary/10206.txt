The order for appointment at Sringar Medical College with a condition to join is seen as potentially violating an employee's rights under Articles 16 and 21 of the Constitution, as well as creating another category of migrants and burdening the State exchequer.
The petitioner, who is already working in the Health Department at Jammu, has expressed concern for her safety under the State Jammu and Kashmir Migrants Act of 1997.
Therefore, the authorities have been directed to consider adjusting the petitioner's job location to Jammu in order to address these concerns.
