Constitution of India, Article 226 - All India Council for Technical Education Act, 1987, Sections 23(1) and 10(o) - Education - Admission - Despite the government's approval for extra seats in degree courses for diploma holders, some respondents refused to admit students in those seats - The Council recognized that avenues to higher qualifications should be available to deserving diploma holders - The notification of guidelines dated June 15th, 1992 carried the weight of law and was binding to respondent Nos. 2 and 3 - The court issued directions to respondents to follow the notification.
