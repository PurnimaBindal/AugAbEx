Constitution of India, Article 20(2) - Army Act, 1950, Sections 22 and 39(a) - Army Rules, 1954, Rule 13(3) Item 4 - Double Jeopardy - Despite imposing punishment of rigorous imprisonment and discharge under Army Act and Rules, the principle of double jeopardy is not applicable since punishment for an offence and discharge of recruit are two separate proceedings - The proceedings under Court martial addresses penal aspect of misconduct while under Central Rules, tackles disciplinary aspect.
