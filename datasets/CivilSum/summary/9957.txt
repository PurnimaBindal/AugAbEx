According to Articles 14 and 311 of the Constitution of India, in cases of punishment/natural justice, the punishing authority must grant a hearing.
If the punishing authority differs from the findings of the enquiry report while punishing the delinquent, another competent authority should provide the hearing.
If the penalty of stoppage of three annual increments with cumulative effect is imposed without granting an opportunity of hearing to the delinquent, the order of punishment is vitiated on account of violation of the rule of natural justice/audi alterm partem.
It was necessary for the successor-in-office to grant an opportunity of hearing to the delinquent as he never heard him.
[Para 4] B The Constitution of India, Articles 14 and 311, state that in cases of punishment/charge-sheet, the punishing authority travelling beyond the scope of the charges incorporated into the charge-sheet served upon the delinquent and taking into consideration material not brought to his notice for his explanation would vitiate the order of punishment.
The burden of proof lies upon the prosecution to establish the charges and cannot say that delinquent has failed to explain.
