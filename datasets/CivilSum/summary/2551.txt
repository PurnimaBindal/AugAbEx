Police Regulations Calcutta, Regulation 5 and Constitution of India, Article 311 - When a constable in the police force is convicted of an offence such as unlawful assembly and causing hurt, they are not fit to continue as a member of the disciplined force.
[Para 25]  B Constitution of India, Article 311 - Moral turpitude - Dismissal - Moral turpitude should be determined based on the facts and circumstances of the case, and not by a narrow or pedantic approach.
Conduct which does not benefit a person in their present employment is considered moral turpitude.
[Para 15]  C Constitution of India, Article 226 - Punishment - Jurisdiction of Court - A constable in the police was dismissed following convictions under Sections 147 and 323/149 IPC, and unauthorised absence from duties.
The Trial Civil Court set aside the dismissal order and ordered a deduction of one month's basis salary from their back wages for their unauthorised absence from duty.
The writ Court cannot take on the role of inflicting punishment or usurping the power of the concerned authority, and the finding of the Trial Judge cannot be sustained.
