Constitution of India Article 226 - Border Security Force Act 1968, Section 11 - Dismissal from services - Unauthorized absence - Absence due to collapse of house due to heavy rains and serious illness of mother - The delinquent submitted relevant certificates issued by the sarpanch and doctor - The punishment of dismissal is shockingly disproportionate - If authenticity of the certificates were doubted, an opportunity could have been given to the delinquent - The impugned order of dismissal is set aside.
[Paras 12 and 14]
